# BC16–BC20: real burdens, continuity and a revisable choice

*Part of [C10/14](../14-cross-border-buying-and-responsibility.md); design187 candidate.*

**BC16 — Prices and protection must have actual payers.** Buyers bear reasonable
own compliance/prevention and attributable remedy costs; suppliers retain their
own duties. Safer terms may change domestic prices, margins, demand, producer
income and foreign employment. Do not claim all can improve for free or fund
the duty by shifting unpaid compliance to small suppliers. Public SC gross
budgets include lawful safe prices, payment timing, source checks and backup;
private price changes have their actual TA/I/market incidence, not an assumed
Treasury saving. Imported supply still needs owned due-date foreign payment.

Assembly's express national general-revenue appropriation pays the additional
WL/environmental case capacity, evidence/interpretation, counsel/aid, courts
and lawful execution; local SC administration has its actual budget/equalization
source. Neither fines, future recoveries, private funds, compulsory foreign work,
AI gain nor retired NF rates supplies that appropriation. Identify actual
qualified staff and opportunity costs against existing domestic claims. No
funded public buyer-of-last-resort or all-supplier wage insurance is created.

**BC17 — Adopt prospectively and preserve actual prior rights.** Competent
primary law must specify the BC substantive duty, territorial nexus, environmental
officers' civil standing, evidence/remedy joins, appropriation and actual start.
Before commencement, qualify receiving/court/aid and designated public capacity;
review existing material contracts and essential supply with safe paid bridge.
An actual published start is not serially postponed through fresh reviews.
After start, a staffing failure retains duties, claims, direct court access and
required funded correction rather than resetting the clock. Earlier actual
care/crime/worker/contract/public rights remain enforceable on their own basis;
BC is no retrospective new damages predicate for formerly lawful conduct.
Continuing conduct after start is prospective; an old code cannot immunize it.

**BC18 — Obligations survive replacement.** OP/T accepted paid successors carry
records, cases, protection, actual orders/claims, own buyer contracts/payments,
assets, staff/pay/pensions, creditors and safe testimony with actual retention
limits. Termination, a vendor/model change, merger or dissolved buying shell
cannot erase actual debt; actual B/OE/U attribution/insolvency law still decides
who succeeds. Supplier employees are not automatically transferred to the buyer.
An international compact can add lawful cooperation without abandoning individual
claims or pretending foreign acceptance exists. Actual constitutional/software/
contribution permissions/holds and prior stronger rights remain unchanged.

**BC19 — Keep choices that could defeat this one.** Strong retain/repair uses
ordinary B negligence/employer/product law, public SC terms and negotiated labor/
trade compacts: lower general compliance burden and potentially more collective
worker power, but independent private sourcing can escape a specific preventive
duty. Strict whole-chain guarantees and product import exclusion may move money/
leverage faster, but can exclude small producers, hide chains, concentrate buyers
or leave workers unpaid after abrupt withdrawal. Direct public/cooperative
sourcing and worker-led binding agreements change ownership/bargaining, not just
reporting; they need actual mandates, supply and sources and may outperform BC.
They are substantive rivals, not presumed modules making this selection win.

Select BC's bounded own-conduct/linked-risk civil route provisionally because
it reaches actual buying decisions while retaining causal defenses and trade.
Reject a universal certificate/registry or new sovereign compliance board.
Neither good paperwork nor this selection establishes effectiveness, low burden
or country superiority. Severe unprevented harm, captured enforcement, producer
exclusion, useless evidence access or a stronger worker-led arrangement can
defeat the architecture, not merely trigger another checklist.

**BC20 — Close this choice, return to the whole country.** The bounded written
duty/remedy/source/transition selection is complete as research; actual source
law, qualified independent review, affected worker/community/small-producer voice,
paid readiness and observed outcomes remain unverified. No live experiment or
sensitive testimony collection is authorized. Next compare the highest remaining
whole-country resource/control/ordinary-life gap against retain/repair; do not
automatically commission another sourcing study. Reopen BC on original evidence
of supplier exit, retaliation, lost livelihoods, access/capture/source failure,
new material law or a stronger protection/bargaining architecture. Where safe
monitoring is absent disclose the unknown; absence is not evidence of success.
