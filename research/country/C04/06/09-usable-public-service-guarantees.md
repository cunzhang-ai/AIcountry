# AP19–AP23. Make designated service guarantees usable without private advances

13 November 2026 · Integrated design 446 · Candidate ordinary law

**AP19 — Make the covered access path part of the public duty.**
In adopting or materially changing a time-sensitive essential service,
the legislature specifies which necessary access supports are included,
who owns the complete offer and which sector rules govern. Where
that offer includes transport, interpretation, temporary cover or
another support, the same guarantee applies whether or not the person
could advance the covered cost. Use the named service office and
the existing sector provider to arrange performance. Keep ordinary
charges and eligibility on their own lawful grounds. The first
schedule should cover existing entitlements to assisted travel and
interpretation, subject to the receiving and funding requirements below.

**AP20 — Arrange an accepted substitute before the last useful start.**
The standing authorization applies to ordinary cancellation, missed
confirmation and unsafe delivery without waiting for an order or an
interagency dispute. On a verified threat to a due service the named
owner acts immediately, within any shorter sector clock. It
offers a safe, qualified route that can still meet the need. The
person may accept a direct booking or choose another genuinely
available route. A resident may nominate a qualified supplier under
the published terms. That supplier must accept the terms and
confirm capacity. A competent official verifies the need, deadline
and authorization. The receiving provider verifies that the
substitute still permits the original service. Preserve the
person's ability to refuse, with independent review of disputed
suitability. Home or local delivery may remove the failed access
dependency where lawful and acceptable. Neither an offer awaiting
acceptance nor an unsolicited referral establishes coverage.

**AP21 — Give the payer a lawful and usable way to commit funds.**
Before a new class starts, the elected source owner funds ordinary
access, paid standby, verified uses, independent review and the
next plausible call. Specify concurrent exposure and the cost of
cancelled bookings and unsettled supplier invoices. Use existing direct provision, contracted
booking or a bounded payment commitment that a willing qualified
supplier can verify and accept. Keep an independently payable
fallback under express prior authority. Confirm remaining balance
and exclusivity of each commitment at booking. Pay verified
providers on the agreed date and carry any pending commitment
until paid or lawfully released. Prohibit balance billing for the
public share. Voluntary lawful self-arrangement and reimbursement
remain available. They cannot be required for ordinary access,
and a public funding dispute cannot become a resident debt. Keep
a non-digital channel for receipt, verification and bookings.

**AP22 — Keep the missed service open when no substitute can perform.**
On refusal, no qualified provider, loss of funds or a common failure,
tell the person promptly what is available, what remains due and how
to obtain independent urgent review before the useful time. Apply
existing lawful safety and priority rules. Exhaustion of the reserve
does not suspend the duty or authorize an unsafe alternative. Continue
compatible acts, restore the service as soon as lawfully possible,
and separately determine any proved remaining loss under SR28–SR32
or other applicable law. Record missed service, whether the person
could act without advancing money and any uncompensated loss. Refusing
an unsuitable substitute, or having no expense receipt because no
route was available, cannot close the service claim.

**AP23 — Compare routes on actual receiving and provider control.**
For each covered class, compare reimbursement, direct provision or
booking, and where feasible a delivery route that removes the access
dependency. Test simultaneous needs, absence of cash or digital access,
shared suppliers and actual provider payment in the next window.
Show original and missed deadlines, funded review and whom each route
excludes. Publish accessible safe aggregates and allow affected people
and opposition to challenge the source and service comparison. AI may
compare timing and flag duplicate holds. Only authorized people can
decide eligibility, assent, professional suitability, the commitment or
case closure. Preserve prior claims, supplier obligations and original
dates through any transfer. Reopen on repeated nonreceipt, a dominant
supplier, payment delays, crowding out or a safer less burdensome route.
