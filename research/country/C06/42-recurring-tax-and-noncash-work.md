# C06. Recurring taxes, local resources and noncash work

*Integrated design156 scope · Proposed NF01–NF40 · English research, not enacted law.*

**Numerical selection revised:** [TN17–TN20](59/05-country-package-and-source-continuity.md)
keeps NF's explicit mixed-source units/bases/remedies and benchmark formulas,
but withdraws this numerical package's default replacement preference. Actual
lawful retrofit sources continue until express prospective joined replacement;
new founding needs positive source law. No rate/date/right changes by indexing.

Select explicit company, consumption, site-value and received-transfer tax bases
alongside progressive personal income; make noncash work's pension contribution
and employer-funded tax cost explicit. No gross assessment or statutory claim
is counted as received cash, and no source is declared sufficient for the country.
