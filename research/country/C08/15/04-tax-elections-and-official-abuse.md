# Public integrity without criminalizing disagreement

*K22–K24; part of [C08/15](../15-special-crimes-and-bounded-sanctions.md).*

**K22 — Deliberate tax evasion.** Intentionally submit a materially false
return/record or conceal a material taxable fact under a specific lawful
disclosure duty, knowing tax is legally due and intending to evade it:
maximum five years. Deliberate nonfiling qualifies only after usable notice
of the particular duty and opportunity/actual ability to comply, with the
same proved knowledge and evasion purpose. An honest classification dispute,
mistake, inability to pay, inaccessible filing system or failed business is
insufficient. A tax authority's assessment is contestable evidence, not guilt.
The offense cannot create a tax, reverse criminal proof or seize essential
resources by a collection algorithm. Tax collection, civil penalties,
unlawful gains and criminal sanction retain their distinct legal bases.

Misappropriation of public money, corrupt bargains, fraudulent procurement
and deliberate destruction of audit evidence retain K07/K08/K09/K12's own
elements. Poor policy, a missed economic forecast or an unfunded duty is
not automatically a theft or fraud. No new general misconduct catchall is
created to fill an unpopular outcome.

**K23 — Electoral coercion and falsification.** Intentionally use unlawful
force or a credible threat of serious harm or unlawful loss of an existing
right to control another's vote, candidacy or peaceful electoral activity:
maximum five years. Knowingly offer or pay a personal benefit conditioned
on a specified vote or abstention: three. Ordinary universal policy promises,
lawful campaign support, advocacy and an elector's poverty are insufficient;
this limb does not criminalize the elector merely for receiving a benefit.

Intentionally alter, add or destroy cast ballots, eligibility records or
official counting returns with the purpose of unlawfully changing eligibility,
the count or lawful handover: maximum seven years. Prove the material act
and purpose; a good-faith correction, security test or contested legal
interpretation is insufficient. An uncompleted scheme needs P03's specific
intent/substantial step. False political speech, peaceful protest or
challenging a count is not this crime. Immediate electoral correction and
public campaign access use C03's independent routes, not guilt by injunction.

**K24 — Knowing coercive abuse of office.** Knowingly exercise nonexistent,
expired or otherwise clearly unlawful coercive power to impose a material
deprivation of liberty, bodily integrity, home privacy or equal political
standing: maximum five years. Prove the actor's knowledge of the missing/
unlawful power, personal act/duty and material deprivation. Mere office,
negligent procedure, a later-reversed judgment, good-faith legal disagreement
or an allegation is insufficient. K05/K11/K12 retain their more specific
confinement, torture and obstruction elements/ceilings; no relabelling evades
them or doubles punishment.

Outside-command investigators/prosecutors and independently allocated courts
handle alleged official abuse, including protectors accused of retaliation.
The implicated minister, commander, vendor or judge cannot choose their own
reviewer, retain sole evidence custody or decide that a report merits no
external receipt. Existing recusal and alternate-assignment law supplies the
route; no new commission with self-granted powers is introduced.

Support, urgent liberty review and civil correction continue regardless of
whether knowledge can be proved criminally. Acquittal does not validate an
unlawful public act; a civil finding does not convict its official. Funding,
information and execution independence must be real, not nominal. Parliament
may revise prospective general law but cannot order conviction or dismissal
of a named opponent's case. AI assistance cannot choose criminal targets.
