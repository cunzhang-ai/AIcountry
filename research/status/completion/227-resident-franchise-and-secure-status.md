# Resident franchise and secure status

Design319 · Scoped result for [country completion](../../DESIGN-COMPLETENESS.md)

[RFV01–RFV05](../../country/C02/03/01-resident-franchise-and-secure-status.md)
selects one continuous year for the future national resident vote
and candidacy. Local voting, four-year secure residence and voluntary
naturalization retain their own rules. Existing elected terms and
protected immediate succession use predecessor law.

The [comparison](../../settlement/resident-franchise/01-compare-residence-and-voting.md)
tests the authored eligibility, real return and initiative boundaries.
The jurisdictional examples do not establish an optimal duration.
Actual legitimacy, independent registration, costs and observed
public control remain open.
