The [design-28 predecessor snapshot](../2026-10-external-responsibility/predecessor-design-28/README.md)
retains the full unsplit C03, C05, C07, C09, C10, C11 and C13 files with
checksums. Relocation changed headings, navigation and relative link depth; it
did not itself select new governing rules. The exact-text check can reconstruct
each of the six later-split chapter bodies from its topic parts after reversing
link rebasing. The C04 and C06 long-topic bodies reconstruct the same way; C14
has a small substantive design-29 opening-inventory addition. C10's new
substantive paragraphs and the aligned changes to C02,
C06, C07, C14, the overview and status are design-29 research changes, not part
of that structural equivalence claim.

Large `COUNTRY-DESIGN-design-N.md` files in dated directories are immutable
earlier editions. They remain at their existing paths so source links and
provenance are preserved. They should not be edited as current law or consulted
as a prerequisite for a focused contribution. Navigation does not enact a
research method, constitution or service.
