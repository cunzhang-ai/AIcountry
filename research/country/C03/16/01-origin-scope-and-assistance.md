# NI01–NI04. A public proposal does not need a political patron

*Part of [national initiatives](../16-public-national-initiatives.md).*

**NI01 — Implement A2 public origination.** A person/collective may bring a
coherent ordinary national legislative or policy proposal without an Assembly
member, party, minister, corporate registration, private donor or platform
sponsor. At least one actual nationally eligible elector accepts the formal
organiser/contact responsibility, with a safe contact route and supported
replacement. Other residents, children and actually affected outsiders can
contribute to framing, evidence and hearings; this creates no extra franchise.
NI's qualifying human support is governed by NI05, not account/AI counts.

A qualified initiative earns an actual hearing and recorded Assembly decision,
not a favorable vote. National statutes/tax/appropriation still need the actual
A2/DB/primary-law route and lawful votes. Constitutional amendment, territorial
status, local law, individual eligibility/court relief and an already called
vote keep A9/RR/TS/LR/sector procedures. NI creates no statutory referendum,
criminal prosecution, judgment appeal, new sovereign tier or AI lawmaker.
A person needs no signatures to seek an existing right, urgent relief or report
ordinary harm. No public initiative delays E19's seven-day shortfall response.

**NI02 — Usable assistance and a bounded scope decision.** Existing Assembly
Clerk receives written/oral-assisted/accessible proposals, preserves first receipt
and gives necessary help/next steps within five calendar days. Public opening/
competence decisions use AP30-working-day ordinary decision/one bounded extension,
proportionate proof, fair reply and outside review. No completeness reset or
policy-alignment/cost-benefit screening. The proposer need not draft a legal
code, source a national budget or obtain a lawyer to open collection.

Give specific reasons for an indeterminate request or one outside ordinary
national competence, actual help/referral and a chance to clarify. Do not reject
because a proposal is unpopular, critical of the country, opposed to government,
similar to an opponent's initiative or financially disputed. Serious rights/
constitutional objections stay visible and reviewable; ordinary NI cannot
perform a reserved protected change by relabelling it. Lawful advocacy remains
possible even when a different formal route is required. Clerk controls process,
not political merit or an Assembly vote. Independent courts correct routing,
source/help and exclusion within actual AP/OJ/CJ jurisdiction, not policy choice.

**NI03 — Fix what a supporter is authorizing.** Before first qualifying support,
publish openingO, the coherent proposal/objectives, organiser/controllers, actual
scope and alternatives/known costs or unknowns, E0/threshold and exact collection
closeC. Plain language suffices. A broad linked package may state its integrated
choices; unrelated burdens must not hide behind an innocent title. Proposers
approve their public formulation, including translated/future-medium meaning;
no moderator silently deletes an unfamiliar concept or chooses their position.

The stable substantive version is the support object. A material change of
purpose, burden, affected scope or requested act needs a new collection/version;
old support does not transfer. Safe nonmaterial corrections retain both versions,
reasons and usable supporter notice. A later legislative draft may differ only
with explicit differences and fresh elected responsibility under NI11: its
support count never becomes approval of those changes or the resulting law.

**NI04 — Organisers are accountable, not owners of a mandate.** Publish safe
responsible organiser/controller and material sponsorship information; do not
publish refuge/home addresses, ordinary supporters or private political dossiers.
Organisers can explain the supported question, not sell its public status,
claim every signatory's position on later amendments or direct legislators.
Before closing they may withdraw collection with actual notice; after verified
qualification they may cease representing it but cannot privately erase the
public consideration duty. Clerk provides an accessible replacement/input route
without inventing consenting representatives. Preserve objections and dissent;
merged hearings do not merge support, controllers, choices or decisions.
