# Design390. Extend safe drinking-water duties beyond the public network

[WS26–WS32](../../country/C09/16/06-small-water-supplies-and-safe-receipt.md)
identifies suppliers and actual users, assigns independent safety review
and gives the local Water Service Director a residual duty with a national
fallback. Track alternate receipt through the next necessary use and keep
it funded through an independently approved return.

The [worked comparison](../../settlement/small-water-supplies-and-safe-receipt/01-two-supplies-and-one-backup.md)
tests shared transport and a second day of supply before recovery. Actual
coverage, cost, participation, operational readiness and outcomes remain open.
