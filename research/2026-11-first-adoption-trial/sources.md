# Design506 evidence and scope

- [Danish NemKonto Act 696/2025, sections 13(3), 14 and 31–32](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2025/696/pdf) sets an alternative payment duty, separate delay responsibility and the limited waiting-account transition. The [Danish Ombudsman's 2023 cash assistance case](https://www.ombudsmanden.dk/find-viden/udtalelser/2023/2023-40) concerns the earlier legal framework and illustrates why an account prerequisite can obstruct access. Neither source supplies a candidate roster or cost.
- The [Copenhagen Finance Committee decision of 25 November 2025](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/%C3%98konomiudvalget/m%C3%B8de-25112025/referat/punkt-6) and its [administration memo, page 3](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/agenda/00b61cf6-43bb-45ae-b1d1-334bc5f7881b/2a8c061f-6525-42d5-8727-0dfb521e09c3-bilag-1.pdf#page=3) supply an actual incumbent service and investigation request. The frequency is an administrative report about use rather than measured receipt or denominator. No independent replacement quote, payer agreement or trial enrollment is shown.
- The Danish Agency for Digital Government's [waiting-account guidance](https://www.nemkonto.dk/en/citizen/ventekonto/)
  and [payment-specific account guidance](https://www.nemkonto.dk/en/citizen/nemkonto-for-citizens/payment-specific-account/)
  describe different routes and their conditions. A waiting-account
  balance or administrative dispatch establishes no particular timely
  usable receipt. The current waiting-account payer scope is limited.
  Neither route authorizes a trial of a new payment instrument elsewhere.
- [Finkelstein and Notowidigdo's randomized study](https://economics.mit.edu/sites/default/files/2022-08/aaFinkelstein_Noto_QJE_August_2019%20%281%29.pdf)
  compares outreach and assisted SNAP application among likely eligible
  older people. Enrollment is a different outcome from timely control
  of established cash. The [prior take-up comparison](../settlement/effective-take-up/01-observed-take-up-and-assistance.md)
  preserves the other published contexts and their separate scopes.
- [Local trial and expansion rules](../country/C14/03/01-parallel-local-trials-and-shared-obligations.md),
  [payment access](../country/C04/12/04-independent-payment-and-receipt.md)
  and [same-person outcome comparison](../country/C14/04/07-better-lives-and-country-choices.md)
  define project choices. The short evidence example and its payment
  counts are authored. No actual vote, enrolment or payment was observed.
