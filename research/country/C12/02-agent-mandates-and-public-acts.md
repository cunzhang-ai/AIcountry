# C12. Agent mandates and consequential public acts

*Part of [C12 future collaboration](../C12-future-collaboration.md); integrated design 62 research, not enacted law.*

Personal or collective agents require a revocable mandate stating principal, scope,
duration, resource bounds, reserved decisions and permitted subdelegation. Their
copies do not multiply standing. A signature authenticates an act under a method;
it does not establish understanding or consent. Shared technical standards carry
the legal effect of an authorized act without forcing every form of expression
into one data schema. People can challenge mediator framing and retain an effective
direct or different channel. Critical vendor or AI failure requires a practiced
alternate operator or assisted route that can receive a claim, issue a valid
receipt, protect urgency and reconcile records afterward; shutdown creates no
new lawmaking or coercive power.

A proposal may originate through speech, drawing, embodied demonstration or future
media. Before it becomes binding law, its operative terms and translations must be
accessible, authenticated and open to challenge for material loss of meaning. The
Assembly's exact enacted instrument remains the legal rule, including its
[lawfully designated authentic versions](04-authentic-versions-and-public-law.md).
Neither a mediator's summary nor agreement among agents supplies a vote. People can dispute the framing
and replace the communication method without erasing valid commitments.

Consultation on a delegated rule accepts spoken, written, assisted, collective
or future forms of contribution with an attributable record of interpretation
and material loss. Agents may submit within their principals' mandates;
replication does not multiply votes or establish consent. The issuing office
must answer the selected substantive portfolio in accessible terms and disclose
material objections not examined under [FA](03-finite-attention-and-lawful-closure.md).
Separate legally required review remains due; elected scrutiny and independent
challenge stay open when a machine summary masks a real disagreement.

## Revocation and shared commitments across disconnection

The legal act record separates the principal's authority, the agent's current
mandate, the agent's output, receipt by the other party, a competent public
decision and any resulting obligation. A technical credential or signature
can help prove source and scope; it cannot prove that a person understood the
terms or that an office had power to bind the state. A public or private body
must check the applicable mandate and legal rule before relying on a
consequential act. A chain of subagents shares its principal's total spending,
disclosure and decision bounds; copying the chain never resets them.

The principal can revoke prospectively through a reachable direct or assisted
channel, with an authenticated receipt and an independent correction route if
a provider withholds notice. Law must define when notice takes effect against
the agent and a relying party, and what happens when either is disconnected.
Mandates therefore expire, set an offline resource ceiling and reserve specified
acts for a current human or competent institutional check. A public vote,
constitutional consent, use of force, major public spending or irreversible
rights waiver cannot be inferred from an offline agent's silence or earlier
general permission. A valid obligation already made within authority is not
erased by deleting a message or revoking the agent; disputes over attribution,
unfair terms, error and remedies follow the applicable law.

During a network partition, local inquiry and service may continue within
prior lawful allocations, but two agent groups cannot both spend the same
shared worker, cash balance or essential resource. The authority responsible
for that commitment needs a source-backed reservation or another prior-law
nonoverlap rule before decentralized execution. On reconnection, custodians
reconcile events, duplicate claims, revocation notices and pending obligations
with an accessible independent challenge; a last-arriving message does not
decide who loses an accrued right. No claimed emergency expands an agent's
mandate or lets a platform create sovereign power.

For a reciprocal public payment, distinguish a proposed match,
each competent payer's assent, its legal discharge and the actual
residual receipt. Authenticated original reliance evidence must
remain available to the two payers and independent review. Agent
copies, convergence or restoration of a feed cannot supply a
missing legal event. Under TPE03–TPE06 continue independently
assigned work and avoid replay of an unresolved instruction.
Share only the scope, legal terms and event evidence needed for
reconciliation, keeping resident service records separate.

Public bodies must practice a provider exit and offline service route that
preserves lawful claim dates, pending decisions, receipts and source records.
The same legal standing must be reachable without an AI agent. New media can
represent intentions and situations differently, so interoperability requires
preserving operative meaning, dissent, authority and continuing duties for a
declared purpose, not forcing every human relationship into a universal schema.

[Civil B02–B03](../C08/13/01-persons-agreement-and-agents.md) now supplies
private assent, actual/apparent agency, accessible notice and reliance rules.
Routine lawful delegated contracts need no per-transaction human click;
public powers, protected waivers and reserved acts retain competent authority.
Replication never multiplies a shared resource ceiling or a mandate.

[B41–B52/P21–P24](../C08/17-jurisdiction-and-legal-cooperation.md) now keeps
server/agent location separate from lawful connection, applicable law and
execution. Cross-border records preserve original meaning/authority, safe
source custody and unrecognized semantics; a shared platform creates no court.

[G01–G14](../C08/18-international-crimes-and-accountability.md) follows actual
control, material information and feasible action through AI-mediated command;
replicas/title do not multiply a mandate or establish guilt. Current human
responsibility leaves future nonhuman rights/personhood separately reviewable.

[TS05/TS14](../C08/22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md) now applies
this mandate distinction to consequential automated operation: actual domain,
controller, feasible fallback and changed evidence need scoped review. Future
media/teams can supply proven equivalent methods; copying an agent, converging
on a model answer or naming a powerless human multiplies no authority or safety.

[PD16](../C02/07/04-security-records-and-future-media.md) separates an agent
mandate from lawful data reuse and treats selective proofs/local processing as
optional methods, preserving meaning, offline receipt and actual legal authority.

[AP10](../C04/06/03-paid-operation-media-and-succession.md) applies this separation
to ordinary public requests: help, filing, proof, consent, decision and delivery
remain distinct across media. A receipt survives provider change; an agent's
summary or silence cannot withdraw a claim, waive reply or open all records.

[DP08/DP12](../C11/09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md) applies these
rules to actual shared-service changes/incident restoration: preserve source
meaning, prior lawful acts/first receipt and nonoverlapping offline commitments.
A restored database/last arriving event does not settle title, guilt, consent,
resource conflict or whether money was received. Fresh scoped review follows
actual relevant dependencies and material objections; unsupported semantics
stay available for interpretation instead of being silently erased.

[DF service/command](../C10/12/03-voluntary-service-rights-and-handover.md)
requires actual legal authority and freely accepted finite service terms.
An agent copy, simulated consensus or new medium supplies neither enlistment
consent nor military command. Authenticated interpretation/source/record duties
can use different lawful media; no text-only ontology or new force power follows.

[AH05–AH08](../C04/07/02-access-mandates-and-working-space.md) applies these
mandates to free public help: private deliberation, authorised filing and an
agency decision remain distinct. Actual client-selected content and source
receipt/notice govern, not an assistant summary or a whole-life data transfer.

[DP38–DP42](../C11/09/09-shared-dependencies-and-safe-changes.md)
keeps shared update and recovery control separate from mandates.
Preserve received acts and earlier rights while affected advice
or uncertain provenance goes to the competent ordinary route.

[DPA01–DPA08](../C03/19/07-deferred-public-acts-and-lawful-handover.md) applies
this separation across a change of office. Preserve original legal effects,
limited mandates and independently authorized execution.
