# Leadership aggression and proved personal contribution

*G05–G06; part of [C08/18](../18-international-crimes-and-accountability.md).*

**G05 — Aggression is a leadership crime.** A person actually able to control
or direct a state's political/military action intentionally plans, prepares,
initiates or executes a state act of armed aggression that by its character,
gravity and scale manifestly violates the UN Charter. Prove leadership
capacity, own material act, required state armed-force act, manifest threshold
and knowledge of material circumstances. A policy critic, voter, ordinary
soldier, civil servant or programmer is not guilty by membership/office.

The state-act categories are invasion/attack/occupation or forceful annexation;
bombardment/use of weapons against another state's territory; blockade of
ports/coasts; attack on another state's armed forces or fleets; use of
consensually stationed forces contrary to the agreement or remaining beyond
its end; making territory available for another state's aggression against
a third; or sending armed bands/irregulars/mercenaries, or substantial state
involvement, carrying out armed acts of equivalent gravity. A declaration
of war is not required. Identify the actual Charter violation and threshold,
not merely an unfriendly act, cyber label, electoral mandate or prediction.

The completed offense requires the state act to occur; specific intentional
attempt uses G06's substantial-step rule. P01 preserves the prosecution's
burden to disprove a properly raised lawful ground; a cabinet or Assembly vote
does not legalize unlawful external force. No minister's finding determines
another state's guilt. G09 deliberately limits domestic aggression reach;
actual ICC aggression jurisdiction has additional distinct treaty conditions.

**G06 — Own act, attempt and direct incitement.** A natural person who
intentionally orders, commits or substantially assists a specified G offense,
with knowledge of the relevant acts/context and the offense's required
purpose, answers for that proved contribution. A supplier's ordinary product,
investment, criticism, neutral service or shared AI vendor alone is insufficient.
Identify control, actual assistance, knowledge and its material contribution;
do not combine unrelated workers' information into fictional intention.
Aggression participation retains G05's actual leadership limitation.

Intentional attempt needs a substantial step directed to the particular
offense beyond preparation, the required context/fault and G11's lower
ceiling; effective voluntary abandonment preventing completion excludes
attempt liability but not separately completed harms. Agreement alone is
not a general new conspiracy offense.

Directly and publicly inciting genocide is an express separate offense:
intend to induce the specific G02 destruction, communicate an unmistakable
call in its actual linguistic/social context to commit it to a public audience,
and prove source/meaning/intention. Genocide need not subsequently occur.
Quotation, journalism, historical inquiry, unpopular advocacy or an ambiguous
model-generated phrase alone is insufficient. AI amplification does not
prove its operator's knowledge/purpose. No hate/truth-score tribunal or new
censorship/search power is created. Imminent protection retains separate law.
