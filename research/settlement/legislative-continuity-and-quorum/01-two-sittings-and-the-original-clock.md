# Two sittings and the original fiscal clock

Design359 · Constructed comparison under
[LCQ01–LCQ06](../../country/C03/05/01-legislative-continuity-and-quorum.md).

A 100-seat Assembly has a 51-member quorum. The proposed
appropriation also requires 51 affirmative votes. A regional
transport interruption leaves 43 lawful members able to attend
the ordinary chamber. Ten further members can participate through
a paid, supervised alternative with the same text, debate and
tally. The budget deadline remains the same in each case.

| Stated conditions | Verified present | Affirmative votes | Legal result |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Ordinary room only | 43 | 41 | No quorum; no appropriation |
| Alternate reaches nine more members | 52 | 49 | Quorum restored; the appropriation fails |
| Alternate reaches all ten and 51 members assent | 53 | 51 | Appropriation passes if all other requirements are met |
| One of the 51 affirmative votes is materially disputed | 52 undisputed | 50 undisputed | Hold certification pending timely review |

The third row assumes a different pattern of votes, not simply
one more member present. A deliberate absence without any access
failure uses ordinary political procedure; it cannot appoint a
substitute. Separately, assume 51 seats become legally vacant.
With four eligible and willing nominees from the frozen succession
pools, 53 seats can again be occupied. That count is an upper bound
on possible attendance and does not show that any vote occurs.
Exhausted pools follow EL19–EL20 and the remaining full seat
count is still 100.

If no lawful successor budget takes effect by the cutoff, 8
units of covered standing duties and 2 units of eligible bridge
work can be funded from their separate usable sources in the
declared example. At the start of day 91 the bridge cannot
authorize new operations. The 2-unit source does not become
discretionary money. No settlement reserve is assumed here.
Outstanding eligible debts keep their original claims. A possible
supplementary poll requires its own source; this case does not
finance it from standing or bridge balances.

A provisional lower denominator could pass business with the
43 available seats if the threshold were reset to 22. That gives
a substantially smaller group authority to bind the country.
This comparison selects restored participation and the original
thresholds, accepting that an unresolved blockade can leave
policy decisions unmade. It does not demonstrate actual
legislative, emergency or electoral capacity.
