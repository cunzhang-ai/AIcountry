# Who controls money, banks and public shortfalls?

3 October 2026 · Supporting decision analysis for C06/C14 · Research proposal, not enacted procedure

## The bounded national decision

The [country design](../COUNTRY-DESIGN.md), integrated design 7 of 2 October 2026, already selects a **conditional external-currency reference**, a plural mixed economy and an essential-service/income floor. Its [status register](../DESIGN-STATUS.md) correctly says that no actual currency access, bank liabilities, settlement route or operating capacity has been demonstrated. The earlier [political-economy review](../2026-10-political-economy-review/analysis.md) explains why a public budget is not a national production or payments system. This review asks a narrower question: **which public actor may respond when a bank, payment intermediary or the Treasury cannot meet a due obligation, and who may expose taxpayers or reduce a statutory guarantee?**

The reviewed [authority package](../AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION.md) assigns legislation and appropriation to the Assembly, execution to Treasury and legal review to courts. It gives a standing essential-obligations schedule legal continuity after a budget lapse. It does not specify the bank-failure and tax/payment rules needed to prevent a supervisor, receiver, correspondent bank, payment vendor or AI tool from making the country's fiscal choices in the first hours of a crisis. This is an authority gap, not proof that the selected economy is impossible.

**Values:** preserve usable subsistence and payments, equal political standing, limited public power, contractual and property rights, and democratic control of taxes and losses. **Design hypothesis:** legal separation plus advance funding and tested alternatives can reduce arbitrary crisis decisions. **Empirical/legal unknowns:** bank asset quality, deposit distribution, clearing rights, creditor ranking, debt denomination, external-currency stocks and contracts, fiscal room and actual providers. None is supplied by the synthetic 100,000-resident ledger. The [Basel Committee's 2024 Core Principles](https://www.bis.org/committees/bcbs/basel-framework/standard/bcp/02/inforce/2024-04-25/published/2024-04-25) treat supervision as a minimum framework, not an assurance that no bank fails. The [FSB's resolution standard](https://www.fsb.org/work-of-the-fsb/market-and-institutional-resilience/post-2008-financial-crisis-reforms/effective-resolution-regimes-and-policies/key-attributes-of-effective-resolution-regimes-for-financial-institutions/) separates continuity of critical functions from taxpayer solvency support. These sources inform comparisons; they do not enact AIcountry law.

## Coherent rival settlements

| Choice | Monetary, fiscal and social arrangement | What it cannot promise; who bears the conflict |
|---|---|---|
| **R0. Retain design 7 without further allocation** | Assembly sets taxes and budgets; Treasury pays; a future statute is expected to provide bank resolution and funded payment continuity. This accurately leaves a real jurisdiction to choose its own scheme. | The reference offers no lawful first-hour decision rule. A receiver may preserve one depositor's access at another's expense, a Treasury bank may freeze taxes and benefits, or an executive may claim an implicit bailout. Retain only if the country design explicitly labels this operating chain unfinished. |
| **R1. Statutory external-currency operating settlement — selected conditionally** | The Assembly pre-enacts tax-payment finality, bank-entry and loss-allocation rules, bounded deposit protection, an industry-funded resolution/insurance facility, Treasury cash custody, alternative payment access, existing-claim priorities and a route to vote on extraordinary public support. Bank supervisors and a separate resolution authority execute those rules; neither can create new taxes, guarantees or state credit. The social floor remains a legal claim, with delivery and funding failures disclosed. | No actor can issue the external currency; an industry fund and reserve can run out, correspondent access can fail, and a lawful claim can remain unpaid. The statutory coverage and ranking distribute losses among owners, creditors, depositors, taxpayers and service users. Their levels require public choice and real balance sheets, not an AI optimum. |
| **R2. Domestic currency with public monetary authority** | A legitimately adopted currency law creates monetary issuance, bank settlement and bounded liquidity support under a public mandate, with separate fiscal authorization, prudential supervision, resolution and social-floor law. Government may have more domestic-currency liquidity during a run. | It still cannot print trained workers, imported medicine or foreign exchange; inflation, exchange rates and emergency credit distribute losses and can protect connected firms. Independence of operations cannot give monetary officials a veto over elected fiscal policy, nor can elected officials secretly compel unlimited rescue. A constitutional and monetary-law transition, conversion of existing claims and cross-border agreements would be needed. |
| **R3. External currency with a smaller prospective social floor** | The Assembly could reduce *new* service promises and prefund a narrower set of obligations, lowering apparent fiscal exposure while keeping lawful prior claims and constitutional basic protection. | Less legal coverage can leave people without actual care, income or housing, and may shift costs to unpaid family labor or localities. It does not resolve bank failure, a frozen payment rail or a foreign-settlement cut-off. Retrenchment is a political value choice requiring affected-public authority, not a technical consequence of a short cash forecast. |

Select **R1 as the provisional reference** because it completes the design's existing external-currency premise with a bounded legal decision chain. R2 remains a serious alternative for a real adopting public; R3 is not a substitute for operating rules. This research selection neither ratifies R1 nor proves it preferable in any jurisdiction. If the lawful funds and operators required for R1 cannot exist, the reference cannot honestly claim an operating social floor; a public may choose another monetary/social settlement rather than a fictional reserve.

The IMF's [full-dollarization analysis](https://www.imf.org/external/pubs/ft/issues/issues24/index.htm) explains why a state using another issuer's currency has a constrained lender-of-last-resort function. That general mechanism supports this comparison; it establishes neither AIcountry's foreign-currency access nor any particular deposit-insurance amount.

## The proposed authority chain and crisis defaults

| Stage | Actor and preauthorized action | Boundary, evidence and remedy |
|---|---|---|
| **Legislate and fund before a crisis** | The elected Assembly defines taxes, payment finality, bank non-viability triggers, insured deposit coverage, creditor hierarchy, fund levies, resolution powers, Treasury custody, public debt ceilings, the standing social obligations schedule and any narrow temporary liquidity facility. Cabinet must budget the transition, alternate payment route and contingent exposure. | Exact scope, duration, beneficiaries and maximum taxpayer risk are public. A technical standard, contract or agency rule cannot authorize a new public guarantee. Opposition can propose alternatives and audit the fund; courts review rights and legality. |
| **Collect taxes and establish finality** | A revenue authority assesses under statute and accepts payment through legally designated routes. The law states when a taxpayer's obligation is discharged and which party bears intermediary failure before or after final settlement. Treasury records *usable received funds* separately from assessments and bank claims. | A bank failure must not let the state collect the same confirmed tax twice or deem an unreceived bank promise spendable cash. Taxpayers obtain correction and appeal; protected data are audited without a single compulsory AI account. The exact finality rule requires lawful local payment-system design. |
| **Supervise and trigger** | A prudential supervisor examines institutions and, on pre-existing non-viability criteria, refers a failing bank for resolution; another statutorily designated authority makes the resolution decision with written, later reviewable reasons. | A bank's liquidity problem, insolvency and a payment-rail outage are distinct diagnoses. Supervisory discretion does not confer a policy veto or power to appropriate Treasury funds. Appointments, budget, bank data and enforcement must be checked for common capture by the incumbent or owner. |
| **Resolve a bank and continue payments** | Under prior law, the resolution authority can transfer insured transaction accounts and critical functions to a qualified receiver, bridge or solvent provider, or wind down the bank. Equity and creditors absorb losses in the enacted order, with due-process and compensation safeguards. The deposit insurer pays or transfers **only covered claims within its lawful, funded capacity**; the payment operator activates a tested alternate channel for essential transactions. | No automatic guarantee of uninsured balances, bank shareholders, government deposits or a whole payment network. Resolution funding and service-provider capacity are reported separately from Treasury's service budget; one reserve cannot be counted twice. The [IADI 2025 Core Principles](https://www.iadi.org/uploads/Core-Principles-September-2025.pdf) emphasize coordination of deposit insurance, supervision and resolution; the [CPMI-IOSCO payment principles](https://www.bis.org/committees/cpmi/pfmi/overview) require a legal basis and operational continuity planning. Their standards do not establish this project's actual fund or alternate operator. |
| **If a lawful bank resolution exceeds the fund** | The receiver records residual claims, available assets, loss allocation and critical functions at risk. Cabinet submits quantified alternatives to the Assembly, including private recapitalization, industry recovery, a bounded publicly funded bridge or refusal of additional taxpayer support. | A receiver or minister may use only an already enacted limited facility. Any *new* taxpayer rescue, guarantee, loss transfer or change to social spending requires the Assembly's affirmative lawful decision, risk disclosure, rights review and later audit. Legislative delay does not silently authorize bailout. Court review can correct unlawful taking or discrimination; it cannot create external currency. |
| **If Treasury itself lacks cash** | Treasury follows the previously enacted essential-obligations and due-date rules, uses only legally available cash and financing, reports every unmet payment and accrued claim, and alerts cabinet, Assembly, auditor and affected people before interruption where possible. Cabinet presents tax, borrowing, negotiation, reallocation and service-continuity choices; Assembly decides changes under rights and prior obligations. | Bank-resolution powers cannot pay a sovereign bill, and a fiscal shortfall does not permit Treasury to erase eligibility or favor political allies. A pre-enacted temporary sequence must identify which due payments may legally wait; it cannot override debt contracts, court orders or basic rights by a software flag. If incompatible nondeferrable claims exceed usable cash, the conflict and arrears remain explicit. No AI, central banker or receiver may invent a priority hierarchy. |
| **If an external clearing route fails** | The responsible payment authority invokes contracted alternatives and lawful offline or assisted access, preserving transaction records and the original due time. Treasury and service agencies disclose which payments and imported inputs are still impossible. | A domestic mandate cannot force an external correspondent, foreign issuer or supplier to cooperate. A failed fallback becomes a public shortfall requiring Assembly action and remedies, not a fiction that a completed screen equals receipt. |

The [FSB standard](https://www.fsb.org/work-of-the-fsb/market-and-institutional-resilience/post-2008-financial-crisis-reforms/effective-resolution-regimes-and-policies/key-attributes-of-effective-resolution-regimes-for-financial-institutions/) gives a useful comparison for rapid bank action, continuity, loss bearing and judicial redress. Its permission for fast resolution does not authorize a democratic state to hide the fiscal incidence of that action. In this proposal, politically consequential choices about coverage and taxpayer exposure belong to the Assembly *in advance* where possible and to an explicit later vote when new costs arise. The fast actor executes the pre-enacted rule; it does not secretly select the social settlement.

## Migration, continuity and the two proposed main-design changes

An adopting jurisdiction would inventory every deposit class, public cash account, bank guarantee, loan currency, tax intermediary, accrued benefit, supplier and payment contract. It would determine which earlier claims and creditor ranks are protected by existing law; no retroactive loss waterfall, forced currency conversion or extinguished pension follows from this research. Migration needs legal reconciliation, supervisory and resolution staff, an industry fund or lawful committed credit, protected Treasury cash custody, another usable payment operator, tested transfer of records and credentials, accessible non-digital access, supplier agreements, overlapping service contracts, and compensation or remedy funds where required. These are concurrent costs alongside the social floor and electoral safeguards. A captured authority, missing foreign settlement or unfunded insurer can defeat the scheme despite a correct chart.

Only two changes are recommended for the main design:

1. **C06:** Name the separate tax/revenue, Treasury, prudential-supervision, resolution, deposit-insurance and payment-operation roles, with a pre-enacted bank-loss and public-shortfall rule. State that supervisors and receivers cannot create taxpayer exposure; new bailouts and any change to statutory guarantees require the elected Assembly, and external-currency liquidity cannot be assumed.
2. **C14 (and the status register):** Make commencement of operating payment and deposit-continuity claims conditional on the chosen regime's lawful clearing access, disclosed coverage/loss hierarchy, funded facility, staffed receiver, safeguarded Treasury accounts and practiced alternate route. If these are missing, preserve existing rights and disclose failure; do not report the social floor as operational. A real affected public must still choose or revise the regime through its legitimate route.

**Evidence, authority and next bounded question:** The selected chain is reasoned research informed by the linked primary BIS, FSB and IADI standards and local design texts inspected on 3 October 2026. No real balance sheet, existing-jurisdiction statute, stakeholder testimony, qualified public review, operational drill or observed outcome was obtained. The [framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [handoff template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md) require those categories to remain separate. The next bounded question for a real jurisdiction is whether one failed bank plus an interrupted external correspondent can preserve insured essential transactions and tax/benefit payment finality *within already authorized assets and powers*, while publishing who is unpaid and who bears loss. A desk walkthrough can expose missing legal authority; only real institutions and affected people can test readiness and decide adoption. Reopen this provisional choice if a lawful domestic-currency alternative, actual liabilities, noncooperating provider or affected-party objection changes the tradeoff.
