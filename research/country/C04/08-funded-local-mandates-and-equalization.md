# C04. Common rights with funded local responsibility

*Design533 · MF01–MF49 · Proposed national and local fiscal law.*

**The national legislature guarantees funding for the common rights it
assigns. Local elected bodies choose lawful ways to deliver them.** Reconcile
every material locally delivered common obligation to its funder,
receiving authority, useful time and remedy. Preserve national
responsibility if the assigned local path cannot deliver.

The rule uses the country's current local source law. The existing
[NF23–NF24 benchmark](../C06/42/06-home-deferral-revenue-share-and-equalization.md)
remains a comparator under [TN](../C06/59-tax-sources-and-country-operation.md).
No new tax, equalization percentage, regional tier or standing source is
selected. [FC](../C07/23-funded-local-early-care.md) remains the specific
early-care duty and fallback, with shared funding reconciled once.
