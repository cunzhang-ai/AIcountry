# What election costing and a minority programme can establish

## Canada. Optional requests and separately public estimates

Section 79.21 of the Parliament of Canada Act provides financial estimates
on request during a defined election period. It covers representatives of
recognized parties and members who were outside those parties on the
specified date. It regulates confidentiality, withdrawal, ministerial
assistance and public release after notice that a proposal was announced.
The statute also distinguishes unfinished estimates. The Parliamentary
Budget Officer's 2025–26 report records 92 requests costed and 60 published
in the 2025 federal campaign, with a 35-day costing period. Those counts
do not show which unpublished requests were withheld and why, how voters
used the analysis or whether the service could cover all new contestants.
[Statutory scope](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-1/section-79.21.html?txthl=public)
· [PBO activities report](https://www.pbo-dpb.ca/en/publications/ADM-2627-001-S--202526-report-activities-office-parliamentary-budget-officer--compte-rendu-activites-2025-2026-bureau-directrice-parlementaire-budget)

Retain the value of voluntary confidential analysis. The proposed country
uses the Assembly's existing career budget staff and its electoral access
rules. It expressly includes new and independent contestants. It links a
public claim of official costing to the matched version and limits, while
preserving confidential exploratory work. It does not import Canada's
specific eligibility rules, its clock or the release rule tied to announcement.

## Netherlands. A minority programme and a later budget proposal

| Stage | What the official record establishes |
|---|---|
| Published 2026–2030 coalition agreement | D66, VVD and CDA set policy and budget terms. The agreement recognizes the need for wider parliamentary support. Its appendix describes a balanced package of changes and an end-of-term deficit reference of 2.1% of GDP using the stated baseline. |
| 27 March 2026 Spring and Initial Memoranda | The government announced its multi-year rules, spending ceilings and revenue frameworks and a projected 2.1% deficit in 2030, using a newer forecast and including known adverse items. |
| 15 September 2026 budget presentation | The government presented the 2027 Budget Memorandum and Tax Plan, reporting a 2.9% projected deficit in 2027 and 2.2% in 2030. Its finance minister said the proposal accommodated some opposition requests, including relief from the burden of the freedom contribution, and changed or postponed measures to allow alternative reforms. |

· [Budget day figures](https://www.government.nl/latest/news/2026/09/15/budget-day-2026-a-renewed-drive-to-safeguard-prosperity)
· [Finance minister's presentation](https://www.government.nl/documents/2026/09/15/speech-by-the-minister-of-finance-on-the-2027-budget-memorandum)

The agreement's change table and a deficit forecast are different quantities.
The spring and autumn 2030 figures differ by 0.1 percentage points at the
displayed precision. This is a difference between published forecasts.
Without matched baseline vintages and measure-level reconciliation, it
cannot be assigned to one party, tax measure, economic event or programme
failure. The September documents describe a proposal. No final tax law,
appropriation, approval of the entire programme or realized effect follows
from those presentation records.

A political programme can therefore name material commitments and still
need another public choice. GP requires a versioned reconciliation through
the ordinary budget and sector processes. Existing law, lawful funding and
actual useful service remain separate. The comparison does not import Dutch
fiscal targets, formation law or parliamentary composition.
