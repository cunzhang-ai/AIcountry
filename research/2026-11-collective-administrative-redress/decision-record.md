# Design342 decision on proactive redress after common administrative error

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design341, LX01–LX10, AP, TU, RLW, SCN, SR and the working framework.
Existing law in the design already requires finding affected cases and
following remedies to receipt. Add an operational duty with named owners,
dated scope and release, independent examination of excluded records and
a continuing route for returned payments and unresolved cases.

Select proactive correction without a fresh application for established
rights. The decision officer holds the merits and release duty. Existing
independent reviewers verify and correct process within lawful powers.
The Assembly and other competent elected bodies fund the work. Courts
retain jurisdiction over legal orders and remedies.

## Alternatives and objections

Individual complaints protect context while missing people who never
learned of the error. A central automatic refund can be fast for
verified cases, with identity, duplicate payment and wrong-recipient
risk. Select proactive search, separately released verified amounts and
individual human review of exceptions. Keep a simpler screened review
where the affected set can actually be shown complete.

The [primary comparisons](sources.md) illustrate large remedial searches,
independent estimates and payment details that remain unresolved after a
refund exercise. The two-, ten- and five-working-day standards are design
choices. An overly broad search can intrude, and more process can divert
staff from current services. Bound purpose, protect normal capacity,
test actual reach and publish residual uncertainty without personal data.

## Migration and reopening

Keep existing limitation, finality, accrued claims, privacy and sector
rules. Fund competent teams, independent review and actual receiving.
This is an assistant-authored research choice. Affected participation,
qualified independent review, enactment, costs and actual outcomes are open.

Test one full redress cycle with consenting current and former recipients,
a payment operator, independent reviewers and people outside ordinary
application channels. Measure omitted people, partial and returned
payments, time to receipt and unresolved cases. Reopen on overbroad
matching, concealment, repeat failed payments, unlawful offsets or a
simpler route with equivalent reach and protection.
