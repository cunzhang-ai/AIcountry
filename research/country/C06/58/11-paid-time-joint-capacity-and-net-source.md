# WF49–WF52. Paid time, service capacity and a source used once

*Design171 ·4November2026 · Part of [country frontier](../58-country-source-and-capacity-frontier.md); proposed research, not a funding certificate.*

**WF49 — Retain the real time choice and fund the whole staged offer.** Retain
TW's36/32 retained-pay proposal, rest/leave/learning, essential services and Y's
actual earned/basic/credit protections. TW18 already requires an elected funded
calendar and qualified paid cover; research never activates it. Unknown country
sources or a partial expense proxy alone do not prove that32hours is worse or
justify quietly substituting a weaker promise. A36-only/repair-first or separate
later32Act remains a genuine rival with less time release and different risks,
not a free way to declare the existing offer funded.

For a proposed Act choosing both stages, show BOTH dated stages and the continuing
whole offer: retained/extra public and private work, qualified peaks/replacements,
training, source/pay/price/tax/I05/Y/old claims, capital/foreign inputs and paid
transition. First-stage cash or a future model gain cannot finance the second
stage by implication. This clarifies TW18, adds no separate readiness approval
or second-Act requirement, and changes no enacted date/more protective right.
Actual source failure remains E/NP/DB/RF protection and elected correction.

**WF50 — Allocate one real task gain among time and services once.** For a
matched affected segment, let a(h) be actual qualified task availability per
paid equivalent, d its genuine extra same-quality task demand, and g its net
validated same-quality/safe-workload gain PER qualified task hour after actual
review/fallback. Absence, break, learning or retention changes already in a
cannot also enter g; a per-paid-worker/day gain needs actual re-expression,
not multiplication into the changed availability a second time.
Let u=Dold/(Nold*a(old)) be baseline lawful task demand relative to actual
qualified staff capacity, including unmet/overdue need rather than assuming
current service is adequate. Required equivalents relative to original staff are
`r=u*a(old)*(1+d)/(a(new)*(1+g))`. At retained pay/no layoffs, conditional extra
compensation is `C*p*max(0,r-1)`. Separate old unmet-need repair
`C*p*max(0,u-1)` from the joint change; existing headroom is no universal reserve.
The older matched reference explicitly assumes u=1. Spare task capacity is not negative
payroll or a new tax receipt. The same g cannot independently fund shorter
hours, the full new service volume and the full cash saving. Incompatible
skills/shifts and public/worker/user time still need actual separate receiving.

The matched44-week/fixed-break/learning/absence reference with u=1 remains authored,
not observed Danish public capacity. With its37→32 calendar, an assumed20%gain
leaves only about1.8%same-staff extra task room after the hours change; adding
10%task demand still requires more equivalents. No20%gain is forecast. Larger
pace, lost breaks/consent, hidden unpaid work, worse learning/health or fewer
safe received services cannot count as a qualified gain. A worker's time release
and a patient's extra task may be differently valuable; a ratio decides neither.

**WF51 — A new public payslip is not an independent national source.** Separate
compensation expense from gross personal wages: employer-only contributions/
nonwage components are not an identical personal income-tax base. For a declared
fully paid illustration let x be the named affected segment's G-funded gross
additional compensation (not already-netted whole-G payroll), w=alpha*x its qualified
personal gross-wage portion, Tnew actual compatible net G tax attributable to it,
Tlost the actual prior-work G receipts displaced, B the matched NET source-
compatible G benefit cost avoided (negative if new G cost), and K separately
identified other NET G requirements: other uses/foregone receipts less actual
compatible extra receipts. Never use S/private/foreign benefit savings as B.
Benefit tax/credits already netted in B cannot also enter Tnew/Tlost or K; record
a nonoverlapping source/period allocation. No promised job implies benefit savings.
K is not a balancing residual or permission to assume a positive tax yield.
The partial G requirement is `x-Tnew+Tlost-B`; only with identified K can the
scoped total be `x-Tnew+Tlost-B+K`. Unknown terms are not zero or a balancing tax.
Public-to-public redeployment records BOTH new-segment wages and actual old-
segment counterpart. An actual lawful released old expense can reduce K; mere
relabeling or an old service needing replacement cannot. Prior/new G wage taxes
match separately, with no duplicate old pay credit, hidden loss or free reserve.
Public payroll tax reduces the matched gross cost; do not book it once there
and again as an outside reform yield. Exact source/payment dates still matter.

Prior private/public/foreign work can lose output, taxes, safe services or FX;
recruitment is no free reserve. A wage-weighted prior-work sensitivity is not
observed recruitment. New S contributions stay restricted and need comparison
with displaced old S receipts, earned/credit/legacy claims and G-to-S backing;
they are not unrestricted G tax. Internal transfers/netting create no new
national source. I05, pension-altered income, tax credits and actual remittance
must match once; old TN fixed-income yields do not transfer automatically.
The model's compensation-tax/old-job shares are authored, not actual rates,
recruits or direct application of tax rates to the whole OFF3 expense.
