# Design334. Constitutional counterproposals

[CRV06–CRV10](../../country/C14/01/05-constitutional-counterproposals.md)
protects the initiative's exact text and lawful date. Give each
alternative its own approval and fund a conditional preference,
review and any lawfully needed repeat. Carry original approvals and
predecessor safeguards only within their applicable scope.

The [decision](../../2026-11-constitutional-counterproposals/decision-record.md)
retains raw totals and initiative priority as real alternatives.
Actual adoption, paid independent capacity and public receipt remain open.
