# NM05–NM08. Receipt, calendar and independent relief

[Contestable nominations](../23-contestable-list-nominations.md)

**NM05 — Finish the first selection with a real repair interval.** For
the ordinary national and municipal EL calendar, publish usable selection
notice by V−58, allow proposals at least through V−54 and make the final
selector material available by V−51. Allow secret voting for at least
48 elapsed hours after that material is usable, ending by V−48. Deliver
the reasoned outcome by V−47. Earlier whole schedules are allowed when
the actual call and effective notice permit them. Times are fixed under
the common prospective election calendar. V remains the first lawful
casting or return of a public ballot. On a V−60 call the same latest
dates apply. Shorter or special contests require their own prospectively
compatible law. Keep the existing V−40 freeze, V−35 decision, V−30 proof
and V−21 production dates. No late notice or review restarts the clock.

**NM06 — Secure receipt against the body under challenge.** A group names
a tally officer and a separately controlled record custodian before
selection notice. The group provides a reachable private route to propose,
correct roster facts and cast a secret choice. The custodian can receive
and preserve originals without a political officer's permission. Publish
the selector basis, timetable, rules, declared result, consent status
and certifiable order. Keep selector identities, linkable counts and
ballots from public disclosure. Use protected original access with
purpose limits and minimum retention through applicable challenge.
Allow a paper and in-person route that does not depend on the challenged
operator's credentials. The electoral career officer receives the
certified procedure and outcome through the ordinary filing. That officer
does not collect routine membership or ballot files.

**NM07 — Reach an independent court in useful time.** A candidate,
selector or list with a concrete statutory injury may use the existing
direct EA16/ER06 court route. Internal review remains available where
useful. Exhaustion, a leader's permission and disclosure of private
preferences are unnecessary. Provide protected original access, prompt
notice, defense, legal aid where needed and an independently credentialed
executor. Apply the existing 24-hour consideration and earlier useful
deadline. The court may preserve originals, restore a wrongly denied
proposal or vote before lawful close, order an expedited lawful rerun
before V−40 or correct the filing to the result proved on the original
record. A rerun preserves at least 48 elapsed hours of usable secret
voting and enough notice and candidate access to repair the injury. It may not replace selectors, infer how anyone would have voted
or appoint a preferred candidate. A complaint alone does not stop the
selection or block the list.

**NM08 — Keep the protected route usable under withholding.** Name the
authorized neutral receiver and paid backup before call. Where a court
finds a valid result or orders a funded lawful rerun, the original
custodian or qualified preassigned substitute can deliver the resulting
attestation to the existing career officer. If a shared vendor, access
key or controller blocks both receivers, report actual failure and
preserve first receipt, available evidence and the outstanding claim.
A court order counts as delivered only when the needed act reaches the
responsible official in time. The finance investigator, executive and
party leadership have no role in granting the substitute's permission.
