# Design230 decision record. Public rules and verifiable implementation

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned design 229](baseline.json)

## Decision and limits

A1 and A3 already identify authoritative law and authenticated
publication. DP already tests safe reliance, change and supplier
exit. EV already governs material uses of corrected evidence.
These protections can coexist with manual instructions or a
second service that applies the same wrong legal interpretation,
period or evidence transform. Select
[LX01–LX10](../country/C11/12-public-rules-and-verifiable-implementation.md)
as ordinary implementation for materially consequential rules.
It requires independent testing of legal meaning, a trace to
each actual case and scoped correction that reaches non-complainants.
Use the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md). Actual legal hierarchy,
constitutional text, dates and authority remain governed by their
valid processes.

The values are lawful equal administration, accessible challenge,
privacy and replaceability. Eligible facts and proof are determined
by law and competent adjudication. Technical completeness and
verified capacity remain empirical. Timing, contextual meaning
and clinical or legal judgment must not be filled by a default.
The [worked replay](../settlement/public-rules/02-two-periods-and-a-failed-release.md)
demonstrates a narrow error and cash distinction. It does not
validate all rules or staff and is not production code.

## Competing arrangements

| Alternative | Strong case | Material objection |
|---|---|---|
| Use authenticated human law, ordinary procedure and better staffing | Keeps discretion, nuance and existing safeguards. Prefer for low-volume or deeply contextual decisions. | A common written manual can still misapply law at scale. Original version and affected-case repair remain necessary. |
| Share machine-consumable legal rules as a single controlling implementation | Makes standardized calculations easier to reuse and inspect. | Interpretation can be centralized into an unchallengeable default. A common error or captured maintainer can propagate across services and alternate providers. |
| Authenticate law and verify replaceable implementations against it | Supports diverse tools and human access while preserving traceable reasoning and independent correction. | Requires paid legal and operational competence, carefully bounded custody and an actual ordinary route. Published examples cannot exhaust the law. |

Select the third route. Use a common reference and reusable
tests where they lower burdens. Give them their declared scope
and authority and retain an independently reasoned alternative
for relevant failures. This choice applies to deterministic and
manual processes as well as AI. It does not license a minister,
vendor or evaluator to change legal effect by changing a release.

## Authority, custody and migration

The lawful publication authority supplies exact texts and public
history. The existing sector owner authorizes its implementation
and owns decisions and repair. Ordinary courts and lawful
reviewers retain jurisdiction and independent source access.
The payer executes lawfully established dues. Budget, appointment,
conflict, source access and execution must survive the reviewed
owner and provider. Record rejected interpretations without
exposing protected records. Qualified human decision and aid
remain available through a separate practical route.

Preserve original case dates, then-operative law, payment and
proof history, ongoing orders, rights, employment and privacy.
Map current and new tools to funded actual owners under DP and
FT/OP/T. Recheck the scope of court decisions and finality before
reconsidering materially affected cases. No technical rollback,
incomplete replay or unqualified alternate settles an individual
claim. No new law, appropriation, code mandate or publication
permission follows from this research decision.

## Next bounded comparison

Compare one legally consequential routine under a competent
ordinary route and a verified implementation. Examine two legal
versions, one missing evidence case, protected review and an
executed correction on the same timetable. Seek actual affected
people and independent qualified legal and service reviewers.
Reopen after material error, unnoticed case loss, inappropriate
data retention, false transparency or a simpler same-rights route.
Actual participation, enactment, funded independent operation and
better outcomes remain unestablished.
