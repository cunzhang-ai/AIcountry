# C12. Plural communication and mediator power

*Part of [C12 future collaboration](../C12-future-collaboration.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

People can cooperate through conversation, shared activity, demonstrations, supported
communication, personal agents or future media. These channels do not have to collapse
into text forms or one national platform. Local and professional communities can maintain
different practices while honoring common rights and shared obligations.
In the [C14 national-form test](../C14/05-scale-and-national-form.md), remote
deliberation may reduce distance and translation cost but must be tested
against actual voting, opposition, legal receipt and remedy. Faster agent
coordination cannot choose a constituency, multiply a mandate or settle a
shared resource claim.

Future civic channels must be tested for who owns their identity, discovery,
translation, payment and record layers. Apparent competing agents may all depend on
one operator or funder. Public bodies must retain tested ways to publish, receive,
authenticate and challenge consequential civic acts across replaceable providers and
offline routes. Interoperability and exit rights must protect privacy and security;
an export file alone is not a usable exit if relationships, mandates or public records
cannot lawfully continue. No provider may condition participation in lawful politics
on accepting its preferred mediator.

Shared translation, scheduling, records exchange and other support can make
separate institutions easier to reach, but their operators can also control
which needs are recognized and which objections move forward. People and the
authorized public institutions must be able to contest that framing, choose
and replace the coordination method, and use a staffed alternate path when a
shared system fails. A common receipt or matching data does not transfer a
person's consent, a professional judgment or an elected body's authority.

Keep expression, interpretation, delegation and consent distinct. A model's summary is
challengeable; a prediction is not permission; a simulated consensus is not a public
decision. People can replace intermediaries and act without them. No universal life log,
social-credit score or public archive of sensitive testimony is required.

Separate local experimentation from changes to common rights, shared resources and
national authority. Experiments need lawful scope, meaningful participation, exit and
continuity for people who cannot leave. Forking a proposal cannot duplicate a budget,
erase a tenancy or divide sovereignty. Both informal stability and institutional change
are legitimate choices; redesign speed is not a national success measure.

## Civic attention when agents can speak without limit

The formal public agenda and consultation process cannot infer consent,
independence or representativeness from message volume. A wealthy sponsor
may deploy many agents with different names, and a state agency may summarize
them through one favored model. The responsible office preserves the bounded
accepted issue/source record and material disagreements within the declared
[FA finite process](03-finite-attention-and-lawful-closure.md), discloses organized
sponsorship/grouping and permits correction of lost meaning. It cannot promise
substantive review or storage of unlimited genuinely novel submissions. Copies
need not consume repeated hearing time; a lone novel claim deserves accessible
receipt/selection and challenge, not automatic substantive examination.

The office allocates limited formal response and hearing capacity by affected
scope, legal stakes, urgency and distinct reasons under published, reviewable
rules. It maintains direct, assisted, collective and privacy-protecting routes
for people without agents or stable connectivity, including people who never
entered an existing service. Lawful speech outside the formal docket remains
free; triage determines a public institution's duty to examine and answer,
not which opinion may exist. A person need not publish a universal identity
or private life log merely to criticize policy. Where legal standing or a
binding instruction matters, the authorized decision-maker verifies that
specific mandate through a proportionate, challengeable method.

No official or model gets final power to decide that a disagreement is only
duplicate wording. The responsible office publishes the material claims it
grouped, why it treated sources as shared, whose interests were not heard and
what remains disputed; privacy and safe testimony limit disclosure. An
independent route can require reconsideration before an irreversible public
decision when a material objection was suppressed. The Assembly or competent
local elected body retains policy authority. More tokens, agents, fluent
speech or faster negotiation may improve inquiry, but do not expand a
principal's vote, allocate more public resources or shorten other people's
lawful response time.

**Design146 public-media join.** [PM17–PM18](../C11/08/03-funding-reach-and-future-media.md)
protects plural accessible news representations, disputed meanings, source versus
reconstruction and a nonprofiled/direct route. An assistant's selected context is
neither shared understanding nor popular/editorial consent; private alternatives
and lawful criticism remain without a public-newsroom licence or universal schema.

[LX17–LX23](../C11/12/05-public-standards-and-replaceable-implementation.md)
protects unfamiliar meaning and usable equivalent evidence when a public
interface binds. The person retains a direct human route and choice of medium.

[DP70–DP77](../C11/09/15-assisted-communication-and-public-receipt.md)
implements protected receipt and qualified meaning in public services,
with actual correction at the lawful receiving office.
