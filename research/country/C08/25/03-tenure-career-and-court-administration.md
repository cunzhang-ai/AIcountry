# OJ11–OJ15: protect a judge from both political and internal retaliation

*Part of [C08/25](../25-ordinary-justice-and-public-prosecution.md); design120 candidate.*

**OJ11 — Secure ordinary career, bounded office.** New ordinary appointments are
secure to statutory judicial retirement at 75, resignation, death or lawful OJ17
removal/incapacity. No renewable probationary judicial term or Cabinet confidence
vote controls judgments. The age is a prospective administrative hypothesis,
not evidence that age/diagnosis determines competence; preserve stronger existing
personal tenure/retirement/accrued rights through OJ36. Retirement ends this post,
not a person's civic worth or a separate lawful CJ term; no return as a renamed
acting ordinary judge to defeat the limit. General Y/RF actual pension rights
apply, not a new privileged unfunded judicial pension or honorary vote.

**OJ12 — Career decisions are reviewable, not merit by verdict.** Promotion uses
OJ04/OJ07 public competition, not a presider's favor or automatic seniority.
Qualified reasoned evaluation considers actual competence, fairness, diligence
with available resources, accessibility and paid learning; it cannot count
conviction rates, government wins, reversal alone, lawful dissent or speedy
closure as its target. Give evidence/reply and OJ16 review. Training is paid,
plural and source-inspectable; no ideology test or exclusive vendor credential.
Repeated resource failure must be remedied as source/caseload, not blamed on a
judge who lacked records/clerks. Misconduct retains actual proof, not score guilt.

**OJ13 — Consent to transfer and no secret case withdrawal.** A judge's permanent
court/grade transfer requires actual consent and lawful post/source, except
competently enacted general restructuring or a proved lawful disciplinary order,
with reasons, protected interests and direct review. A president cannot exile a
judge or borrow an ordinary/visiting judge for an unwanted named case. Temporary
accepted deployment has exact tasks/dates, paid travel/cover and actual release
from other duties. No general compelled-deployment allowance or serial transfer
resets these protections. Case reassignment follows OJ15's actual rules.

**OJ14 — Administration is not adjudicative command.** Judges lawfully serving
in each court elect a presiding administrator by more than half of their full
serving roster for two years, nonconsecutive; one actual person supplies one vote,
not a vacancy/agent. No agreement leaves the next eligible unconflicted judge in
prepublished rotation carrying funded routine administration only. Presider has
no casting vote, named-case direction, judicial appointment/discipline power or
unlimited budget. The appellate presiding administrator appoints a protected-career
Court Service Registrar through C04 open qualified competition/shortlist/reasons;
the Registrar appoints qualified clerks/administrative staff under C04 and their
own assigned statutory acts. Independent OJ16/B courts hear its own staff/status
or access disputes, not the Registrar as final reviewer. A lawful C04 deputy's
routine management expires after 180 elapsed days without serial reset/new
policy/discretionary senior appointment; existing officers keep own duties.

**OJ15 — Assignment before the outcome is known.** Registrar executes published
lawful specialty/territory/availability/conflict rotation or auditable draw with
preserved original order. Presiders/selectors/parties cannot choose a friendly
judge or secretly change availability to exclude one. Necessary exceptions have
original facts, reasons, reply and direct unimplicated review; correcting a random
assignment error is not consent to rerun until a desired judge wins. Protect
physical/evidence access and funded duty shifts. Visiting/nonresident ordinary
judges require actual prior OJ appointment, competence, permission/contract,
privacy/foreign-law fit and paid release, not an external name to fill a quorum.
