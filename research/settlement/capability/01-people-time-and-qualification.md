# A reserve needs teachers, time and qualified receiving

*Part of [capability comparison](README.md); all quantities are authored.*

The design-64 reserve target added clinical 295, education 178, network 62
and technical 3 to the old staged 420-person additions. It did not establish
where trained people came from. The new reference gives that reserve a
declared preparation path; it does not certify real professions or licensing.

| Track | Paid intake | Dedicated instructors | Course years | Qualified at the declared 85% completion floor |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Clinical, prior annual pay 60,000 | 209 | 21 | 6 | 177 |
| Clinical, prior annual pay 30,000 | 139 | 14 | 6 | 118 |
| Education, 30,000 | 210 | 21 | 4 | 178 |
| Network, 30,000 | 73 | 8 | 3 | 62 |
| Technical, 30,000 | 4 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| Total | 635 | 65 | Different lead times | 538 |

Instructors have a maximum ten trainees each in this fiction; clinical
instructors receive 60,000 and other instructors 30,000. Actual specialty,
teaching workload, qualification standards, applicants and capacity are
unknown. These durations/ratios are not professional standards. All trainees
retain the declared prior wage composition rather than receiving a cheap
uniform stipend. Trainees and instructors are not also frontline service.

With the old 420 paid preparation places, the first year's public paid
work uses 11,210 person-years from the declared 55,000-person workforce.
The remaining 43,790 people are **not proof of private employment or output**;
prior private jobs/tasks are displaced. By normal year 8, 11,048 public
positions reproduce the earlier stated post-commission staffing target.
Non-qualifiers and instructors have paid return bridges; a bridge and the
end of a course do not guarantee a private job or erase continuing claims.

New technical/network/education/clinical graduates become available only in
years 3/4/5/7 respectively, after their complete courses. With old additions
also assumed qualified, all seven stated role minima survive the reference
10% absence only from year 7. Earlier differences against this postproject
target remain visible; they do not prove that every future project duty was
already legally due. Ordinary existing protections continue under prior law.

A 10% loss of instructor capacity admits only 580 of 635 candidates on time,
yielding 491 rather than 538 graduates. At the final 10% frontline absence,
gaps remain clinical 24.7, education 16 and network 0.1 FTE. An unavailable
training facility produces no new reserve graduates. Unplaced people are
not failed students or free waiting capacity: their contract/support and
later completion costs remain unresolved. Normal finance is not a revised
failure budget or authorization to put unqualified people into service.

The [WHO workload method](https://www.who.int/tools/wisn) uses workload and
activity-time standards; it is context for counting real time, not evidence
for the fictional intake, instructor ratio or national staffing minimum.
Future retirement, attrition and replacement cohorts need a continuing
training plan. One accepted cohort cannot maintain the country forever.
