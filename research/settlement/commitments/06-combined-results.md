# Combined reference: cash, care and institutional capacity

13 October 2026 · Authored annual arithmetic, not a national forecast

The [preserved September inputs](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-09-national-settlement/inputs.json)
assume 100,000 residents, six service envelopes, separate income support,
debt/cash limits and three years. The new [assumptions](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-commitment-reconciliation/assumptions.json)
combine in year 2 the previously separate 10% revenue loss, 20 million CU
extra income-support need and 10% loss of available care staff. They also
assume a 10% loss of available staff in each of the gross justice/safety/defense
and democracy/administration/science/culture envelopes. That last loss is
newly authored, not an observed probability or the closure of a named court.
Costs and staffing allocations remain unchanged; no new duty is priced as zero.

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Year-2 unfunded cash, million fictional CU | 12 | 52 | 84.5 |

| Year-2 care shortfall against authored need, FTE | 200 | 155 | 200 |
| Year-2 gross justice/safety/defense shortfall, FTE | 82 | 82 | 82 |

| Full current-country cost and feasibility | Unknown | Unknown | Unknown |

The two institutional envelopes each fall from 1,020 available FTE to 918
against an authored allocation/need of 1,000. They contain non-interchangeable
roles; adding their gaps into one substitute workforce would be misleading.
The care shortfall is a separate physical constraint. B/U already leave some
growing workload unallocated; their 200-FTE care gap is not all caused by
the loss. B clearing its annual arrears later does not erase year-2 unmet
due claims or prove that a person got timely protection.

No package passes even this limited compound test in year 2. S remains the
conditional social planning reference, with failed delivery plainly stated;
the arithmetic provides no sufficient basis to rank people's lives or reject
a differently funded cash/private-supply alternative. U's private-supply,
price and bargaining effects are still unestimated. A fiscal ledger cannot
settle that empirical or value disagreement.

Postponing S's extra 20 million of capital would leave at least 32 million
of the immediate cash gap before capacity effects. Additional 52 million
receipts could close the immediate arithmetic gap, but would not restore
care, institutional shifts or an independent payment/records route. No such
tax, borrowing, deferral or allocation is selected by this calculation.
Maintenance needed to sustain existing claims cannot be declared expendable
merely to balance the year. Benefit cuts and unpaid family labor are not
implicit balancing items.

## Institutional failure remains a separate unresolved test

An authored additional condition asks whether the claim desk, independent
review and proposed substitute share a failed payment operator or record key.
The annual ledger has no corresponding controller map, shift roster, due-time
claim queue or restored access evidence. Therefore this condition is **not
quantified or passed**. The [C13 joint failure path](../../country/C13/21-compound-shortfall-and-public-control.md)
specifies whom to follow and how actual receipt would be checked.
Missing numbers cannot certify either institutional independence or collapse.

## Selected design consequence and reproducibility

Require the [joint schedule](../../country/C06/18-joint-commitment-and-delivery-schedule.md):
attribute baseline inclusion and increments, reconcile simultaneous standby
use, and preserve lawful democratic/remedy continuity beside services.
Use genuine new-project phasing as one elected option; existing duties
retain their force and missed due claims remain visible. This is a research
selection about the country design, not adoption of a governing method.

The [model](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-commitment-reconciliation/model.py),

[validation](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-commitment-reconciliation/validation.json)
pin historical sources, reproduce the original results and check separate
cash/debt identities, carried arrears, staffing gaps and preserved unknowns.
The [decision record](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-commitment-reconciliation/decision-record.md)
records alternatives, objections and migration. Neither numerical consistency
nor document coverage establishes lawful authority, adequate benefit levels,
subannual payment, safe shifts, import access, ecological feasibility,
independent control, actual delivery or observed wellbeing.
