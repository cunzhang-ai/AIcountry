# Scientific possibility is not consent or permission to reproduce

*RP13–RP14; part of [C08/20](../20-reproductive-law-and-parentage.md).*

**RP13 — Define the research/clinical boundary prospectively.** Necessary
somatic treatment and clinical trials keep ordinary consent, S02 coverage,
safety and independent ethical/scientific review. A research approval,
clinic license, private contract or model update cannot authorize a birth
experiment. The candidate excludes clinical reproductive use of heritable
genome editing, reproductive cloning or lab-derived gametes until a fresh
specific Act establishes actual safety evidence, consent, future-person
protection, origin/parentage, monitoring/exit, funded care and lawful public
choice. This is an independently authored hold, not a claim that international
law or all scientists prescribe the identical scope indefinitely.

Human embryo research requires a specific statutory license, actual separate
material consent, proportionate necessity and independent oversight. No
research embryo/construct may be transferred for reproduction. Default no
culture beyond 14 days of active development after fertilization, excluding
frozen time, or appearance of the primitive streak, whichever comes first.
Fourteen is a contested national boundary, not evidence that earlier work
is safe or that a simulated entity has human status. An agency cannot extend
it through a favorable risk score; changed scope needs prior public law.

Do not license clinical selection/marketing for racial purity, social rank,
predicted productivity or nonmedical sex preference. Clinically indicated
serious-disease assessment requires actual evidence, uncertainty and free
choice; no compulsory testing, transfer, termination or disability hierarchy.
A person born retains equal rights regardless of genotype/selection or an
adult's unlawful conduct. Private belief or a patient's pregnancy outcome
is not itself an offense under this provider-side rule.

**RP14 — Preserve unfamiliar cases and actual rights.** An embryo-like
construct with plausible organized human-development potential needs a
reasoned independent classification before new culture/reproductive use;
no experiment is exempt simply because it lacks a fertilization label.
Equivalent developmental progression and disputed categories require an
express lawful assessment, not a new unbounded AI definition. Ordinary
software/simulations are not biological embryos merely by resemblance.
Use the more protective no-transfer/hold route while competence is unresolved,
with review and continuity of unrelated lawful care/research.

Entirely external human gestation or other novel reproductive methods need
a new specific clinical Act and defined bodily/source/parentage and future-
person protections before use. No mandate to extract a pregnancy, experiment
on a child or compel a technology as a condition of healthcare follows.
If an actual human is already born or independently needs child protection
in a case outside familiar definitions, provide provisional identity, care,
safe receiving and independent representation immediately; a missing named
gestator or disputed technique cannot create a rights-free person. C02's
separate personhood/future-sentience questions remain open, not settled by
replicating an agent or applying ordinary kinship to a digital model.
