The baseline circuit has 150u public net borrowing, 120u domestic private net
lending and 30u rest-of-world net lending. Selling 30f publicly owned foreign assets
leaves120u new domestic public claims. A 100u bond offer fails; money with an
expressly hypothetical adequate mandate can settle the first period but cannot
renew itself. Repetition beyond the first period is an FX-only conditional test,
not a funded path with actual future lenders, authority, interest or due dates.

With 100f public opening/10f floor, 30f sales fail in period 4. An export-receipt
shock brings failure to period 3 even while the domestic financing figure falls.
More private export receipts do not transfer title: voluntary offers can conserve
public FX while increasing required domestic claim issuance. Direct FX repricing
can improve public cash numbers while lowering private resources. Those facts
warn against a one-number national success test; they predict no national event.

Format `133-conditional-sector-circuit-v1` keeps new independent u/f units and
no capital formation, foreign income/transfers, revaluation flows or statistical
discrepancies. It is not a complete SNA system, observed economy, inflation model,
legal spending power, retail payment design or new public deposit account. The
unchanged100,000-person current reference/all 37 function actual-null interfaces
remain separate; its source/cash peaks are not inserted as sector net borrowing.
