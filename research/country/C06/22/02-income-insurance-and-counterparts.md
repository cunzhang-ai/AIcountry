# Keep households, insurance, production and public finance together

*Part of [C06 country book](../22-country-operating-resource-book.md); design 68.*

The operating fixture retains I05's actual-resource needs test, individual
filing and existing progressive wage-tax calculation. All private support
is transferred once between persons; a shared bill, listed service or
partner's wealth is not automatically usable cash. Use received basic,
earned and survivor payments in their proper needs-test branches. Nobody
is made to borrow or consume an arbitrary residual to validate a chosen GDP.

The 225-million earned-pension fixture represents a different hypothetical
40-year/30,000-pay history for the 15,000 older people. It neither erases nor
classifies old 45-million claims. Survivor payments 12 and credited-period
transfers 6 are explicit new allocation fixtures, not observed eligibility.
Unknown actual inherited and newly accrued `L(t)` remains paired in household
assets/insurance liabilities, never set to zero or declared fully financed.

Restricted contributions 228, bond interest 5, credit transfer 6 and public
backing 1 fund earned/survivor payments 237 and administration funding 3.
The 3 is paid from insurance to general government to finance a component
already inside public resources; do not add another administration bill.
The public backing is a visible fiscal burden, not permission to confiscate
another protected service or silently cut pensions. Future liabilities,
aging and full administration/credit/survivor costs still need original data.

The market account separates operating surplus in GDP from taxable profit:
bank-held sovereign coupons are property income; foreign credit costs are
expenses. Neither is new output. Dividends use after-tax profit and valid
ownership. Full payout, 25% public equity and the numerical corporation/
capital/product-tax choices remain authored hypotheses, not a command to
firms or proof of public control. Acquisitions and foregone private uses
need real title, compensation/source and fresh distributional review.

Normal cash closure is a conditional consistency result, not the country's
objective. Productive borrowing, retained profit, household saving and
funded future security can be legitimate with actual counterpart claims,
risk and willingness. A changed saving/payout/accrual/ownership schedule
requires a new whole comparison; do not transfer this result to it.
Unknown pension-accrual changes also prevent interpreting these cash balances
as a complete accrued-saving, net-lending or actuarial-solvency account.

Required output 4,266.89 is derived from modeled available cash, public
services, paid replacement and net exports at fixed prices. Real production,
prices, work/time, imports and agency must substantiate it. A nominal GDP
input, a bank loan or predicted AI productivity does not build that supply,
deliver safe services or create a democratic mandate. Lived distribution,
external harms and future limits remain independent tests of the design.

The 25% ownership description above belongs to preserved book68. [SF](../25-source-finance-and-payment-continuity.md)
changes the bank share to 12.5% while producer ownership stays distinct. The
[CP bridge](../28/01-production-and-evidence.md) corrects the GVA denominator/
product-tax split without altering frozen GDP, payouts or flows. Neither a
proposed full payout nor zero saving is a national operating requirement.
