# Two users and a common supplier failure

This constructed comparison exercises
[AEC01–AEC05](../../country/C07/05/05-assistive-equipment-continuity.md).
At hour 0 a supplier closes. Its service and remote support both fail. One
person loses a covered mobility device, with indoor mobility needed by
hour 8 and accessible community travel by hour 18. Another loses their
covered communication device and needs private usable communication by
hour 4. Their individual needs and lawful support are already established.
A named regional team owns the two cases. Both receive qualified responses
before their first useful windows.

| Response | Indoor mobility by hour 8 | Community mobility by hour 18 | Private communication by hour 4 | Gross paid provision |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Two nominal loans using the failed platform | Unmet | Unmet | Unmet | Existing commitment |
| Independent fitted indoor loan and communication replacement | Hour 6 | Unmet | Hour 3 | 700 |
| Independent full-function loan and communication replacement | Hour 6 | Hour 16 | Hour 3 | 900 |

The independent indoor loan costs 250. A fitted full-function loan costs
450 and can serve indoor needs by hour 6 and community travel by hour 16.
The independent communication replacement costs 350 and uses authorized
locally held material for setup. Separate paid delivery and fitting for
the two people cost 100 in either package. The figures stipulate two named
workers with compatible schedules and lawful scope, actual safe fit, and
usable travel arrangements. A stock count supplies none of those inputs.
The independent offers share neither the failed platform nor the failed
supplier. The users can decline or correct any proposal.

The regional operating source can release 700 by hour 0. The public
purchaser has a valid 500 claim against the insolvent supplier with zero
available receipts in this window. Fund and deploy the 700 response now.
Record the remaining community function and its lost activities and
seek an authorized timely 200 top-up and the qualified receiver. If the
top-up and receiver become available before release, the 900 option covers
all three functions on the stated dates. A late top-up does not erase
earlier loss. Keep any right to a separately accessible essential trip.

If the proposed communications replacement secretly relies on the failed
platform, remove it from the receiving plan and record the hour 4 gap.
An independent qualified replacement at hour 6 may restore communication
and still leaves two hours of delay past the useful window. A relay
person or a generic device counts only to the extent the user can privately
and voluntarily use it for the required function. See the
[comparison check](verify-assistive-equipment.py). Actual user experience,
costs, qualified capacity, product safety and independent review are open.
