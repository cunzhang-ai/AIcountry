# Protection, supported family life and stable care

*B31–B32; part of [C08/16](../16-family-agency-property-and-estates.md).*

**B31 — Removal is not a poverty response.** A child-protection service
receives a concern without a complete dossier, safeguards contact and
offers actual family/material/clinical help. Before nonemergency compulsory
removal, the service proves clear and convincing evidence of specific serious
harm/risk that available less restrictive support cannot safely address.
The child and affected carers have independent advice, evidence access,
usable participation and a court decision. Diagnosis, belief, migration,
household form, school disagreement or poverty alone is insufficient.
The state may not first withhold owed help and then use the resulting shortage
as the reason for removal.

For a particularized imminent serious danger, emergency action is minimum
necessary, recorded and immediately referred to a duty child judge. A
supported judicial hearing must occur within 24 continuous hours; initial
order at most 14 days, renewed only on fresh reasons for at most 30 days.
No agency self-extension, custody-clock reset or automatic parental waiver.
Children have funded independent representation; parent counsel is separate
where interests conflict. Protect confidential source/child evidence while
preserving fair challenge and an outside-command route for official abuse.

Every compulsory arrangement names an actual accepting safe place, qualified
carer, school/clinical continuity, safe contact and a reunification or stable-
care plan. The first receiver retains protection duties until safe handoff;
siblings/relationships are preserved where safe. No locked punishment or
compelled treatment follows a care label. A separate deprivation of liberty
needs its own strict lawful grounds; under-16 conduct cannot become a penal
placement through protection. Inspect independently and hear confidential
complaints; the placing agency cannot certify its own safe performance.

If timely authority/receiving fails, there is no permission for an unlawful
hold, abandonment or forced dangerous return. The duty service must obtain
urgent independent court access and pursue safe consensual alternatives;
missed review is an actionable failure, not consent or extended power.
An actual inability to provide a lawful safe option defeats readiness and
requires immediate independent remedy and public capacity correction. A
paper timeout cannot solve that physical/authority failure or hide its harm.

**B32 — Stable care and adoption.** Prefer willing safe kin/chosen-family
care with real support where consistent with the child's wishes and needs;
kinship is not proof of safety or a compulsory unpaid assignment. A long-term
arrangement identifies financial/care duties, records, contact and independent
review. A child need not lose all prior lawful relationships to obtain secure
care; adding/revising legal parents requires explicit judicial scope.

Adoption requires child-centred independent findings, informed freely given
adult consent where required, and supported child voice. A birth parent's
consent is only post-birth, after independent advice and without material
pressure; it is revocable for 30 days after usable receipt, before a final
order. No prenatal binding surrender, sale, reward per placement, orphanage
supply target or contract of the child's future labor. Necessary actual
professional costs need disclosure and lawful funding, never a purchase price.

Without required parental consent, prove clear and convincing persistent
serious abuse/abandonment, actual available support, and why a less restrictive
safe stable option cannot protect this child. A parent's disability/poverty,
an agency backlog or a forecast alone is insufficient. Give independent
counsel, reasons and appeal before irreversible change except separately
necessary temporary protection; privacy cannot conceal origin/history from
the person's lawful future access. Actual foreign/adoption and donor-record
compatibility remains unfinished; no overseas order is automatically erased
or enforced. Lawful existing child status/services continue during migration.

[RP07–RP08](../20/04-parentage-and-support.md) applies these post-birth
consent/revocation, safe-care and judicial protections to pregnancy assistance,
while intended support commitments have separate grounds. Prenatal payment
or a change in clinic software cannot supply a parental surrender or order.

[YC stable child care](../../C07/27-stable-child-care-and-adult-transition.md)
specifies ordinary receiving, funding, sibling and school continuity,
and support after a child order. It retains B31's removal grounds and
clocks and B32's consent, relationship and adoption rules.
