# FR20–FR25. Protect residents when home insurance becomes unusable

*Design373 · Part of [disaster recovery and return](../07-disaster-recovery-and-return.md) · Proposed research law.*

**FR20 — Treat loss of usable cover as an early warning.** Under
prospective law, give the policyholder usable notice at least 60
calendar days before a planned material nonrenewal, new exclusion or
deductible increase at ordinary renewal, with reasons, effective date,
alternatives and direct challenge. Preserve stronger rules and the
contract already in force. Where mandatory prudential or safety action
cannot wait, give prompt notice and activate only existing lawful
protection. Notice failure supplies a remedy under adopting law and
does not create unfunded coverage. For a material area withdrawal,
the insurance supervisor verifies the coverage actually offered,
common insurers and reinsurers, and affected classes. Local housing
offices give occupants a safe route to help without releasing the
policyholder's private financial information.

**FR21 — Separate coverage, borrowing and a safe place to live.**
Before relying on a replacement, verify the named hazard, deductible,
exclusions, claim dates, affordable premium and household resources
still usable after essential costs. Give the owner and lawful
occupants independent information suited to their different rights.
Where law permits creditor-arranged cover, disclose its actual
beneficiary, coverage, cost, alternatives and challenge before a
charge. Such cover cannot count as protection of the occupant's
contents, housing or repair unless its terms actually supply that
protection. A missing policy creates no expedited possession ground
or safety determination. Ordinary security, tenancy and safety
rights keep their own court procedures. Rescue, emergency housing
and established personal support remain available under their laws.

**FR22 — Compare a paid bridge with durable risk reduction.** Competent
elected bodies compare ordinary renewal and independent assistance,
a bounded household premium or deductible grant, paid mitigation or
voluntary safer receiving, and any expressly authorized catastrophe
backing. Disclose full prudent risk costs, household contribution,
public payer, coverage dates and who receives each benefit. Any
transitional subsidy has an enacted ceiling, date, actual source and
review. Prioritize continued safe residence or a supported voluntary
move over subsidizing a property's former price. Price community and
receiving capacity, repair work, ecology, and risks displaced to other
places. Do not extend eligibility to new avoidable exposure merely
because existing residents need transition. No insurance decision
authorizes compulsory relocation or clearance.

**FR23 — Test correlated claims before voting public exposure.** Under
the existing disaster finance and NB laws, price private retention,
collectible reinsurance, contingency and a bounded public layer on
the same adverse events and claim dates. Publish the public ceiling,
loss order, counterparty concentration, actual cash source, restricted
balances and resulting debt or repayment. Do not count an expected
claim recovery before useful settlement or the same backing across
simultaneous promises. Test liquidity with a lost common reinsurer
or settlement route. Any public layer requires express elected
authorization and funded transition. It creates no blanket guarantee
or permission for insolvent underwriting. Preserve prior insurance
and secured claims under their original terms and insolvency order.

**FR24 — Verify protection at renewal and again at the next risk date.**
The insurance supervisor checks coverage and collectible claims.
The housing authority verifies safety, direct occupant protection and
a genuine accepting place or qualified repair. Fund the decision,
review, valid bridge and the following coverage or safe receiving
period separately. Count risk reduction only when work is accepted
and changes the relevant safety or actual policy terms. If support
expires before a route is usable, show the affected claims and loss,
continue the underlying duties and bring a lawful source or changed
offer to elected decision. Keep access to direct court review of
wrongful loss, misclassification and execution. A subsidy or new
policy signature cannot certify an available home.

**FR25 — Keep exit contestable and carry the original duties.** Publish
area-level measures of usable cover, unaffordable deductibles,
protected tenancies, displaced people, actual safety, fiscal costs and
delivered remedies. Safely disclose missing residents and unsupported
risk assumptions. Fund independent supervision and review outside the
provider's and paying authority's sole control. An AI may compare
contract terms, inspect dates and calculate stated losses. It cannot
decide a claim, safe occupancy, property rights or an appropriation.
Preserve existing benefits, claims, leases, secured priorities, court
orders and review dates through a change of insurer or government.
Reopen when uninsured harm grows, a support layer becomes recurrent,
a transition moves risk elsewhere, or a better funded option qualifies.
