# Four duties and two failures in the first budget

[Joint continuity](README.md) · Authored comparison

At H0 a district loses its normal feeder until H6. In the second
scenario the feeder outage also disables the shared alternate hub
through H6, for example through its common site control. Treat both
failures as declared stresses. They carry no probability estimate.
Intervals include the start hour and exclude the end hour.

| Duty | Protected window | Load | Other necessary capacity assumed for this case |
|---|---|---|---|
| C · clinic | H1–H4 | 4 kW × 3 h | Qualified clinic crew, records, stock and receipt. Dedicated 4 kW / 12 kWh source. |
| W · safe water | H1–H5 | 3 kW × 4 h | Accepted operator and receiving. Committed 3 kW / 12 kWh reserve with no displaced protected donor duty in this window. |
| J · urgent court remedy | H1–H3 | 2 kW × 2 h | Independent judge, clerk, original evidence, access for parties and an authorized executor able to act by H3. |
| E · election evidence | H2–H4 | 2 kW × 2 h | Independent election officer and custodian, secure original intake and preservation of recorded receipt by H4. |

J represents the practical protection of a person with a time-sensitive
claim. E represents original receipt, including evidence an opposition
contestant needs to challenge a decision. The local custodian keeps
the original time and chain of custody under existing rules. National
certification, poll timing and case dispositions retain their own law.
Operators can connect and accept the routes by the respective starts.
For the split package the independent sites also have independent
access and control, receiving staff and qualified means for these tasks
through the declared hub failure. The staffing check below changes
one of those assumptions.

| Package | Added capital / recurring | Feeder only | Feeder and hub |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Retain shared hub, 3 kW / 8 kWh | 0 / 0 | C and W work. J and E cannot both finish. | C and W work. J and E fail. |
| Widen hub to 4 kW / 8 kWh | 2 / 1 | All four work. | C and W work. J and E fail. |
| Move E to independent local node, 2 kW / 4 kWh | 2 / 1 | All four work. | C, W and E work. J fails. |
| Move J to independent court route, 2 kW / 4 kWh | 2 / 2 | All four work. | C, W and J work. E fails. |
| Both independent routes | 4 / 3 | All four work. | All four work. |

The shared hub has the 8 kWh required by J and E. During H2–H3
they need 4 kW at once. The existing hub supplies 3 kW. Either one
can finish alone under feeder loss. Delaying either beyond the
protected window would lose part of the corresponding duty. The
schedule exposes the conflict. The applicable authorities retain their
own decision and remedy rules. C and W keep their committed paths.

Capital is available by installation and commissioning. Recurring
units are annual net source after continuing commitments, including
staff and asset upkeep under the declared scope. These are budget
units, independent of kW and kWh. With 4 capital and 3 recurring,
select both independent routes. With 4 capital and 2 recurring, no
listed package satisfies both stresses and the budget. Moving only E
and moving only J each protects a different duty on hub loss. Present
that difference, any lawful source or genuine repair, and each exposed
claim to the competent elected bodies. The case establishes no general
priority between the court and election duties.

Even with two independent sources, a shared intake clerk for J and E
would be double-booked in H2–H3. Recheck qualified staffing and safe
data access as well as installed power. These results leave actual
acceptance, cash, exercise performance, incident delivery, replenishment
after H6 and the next event open. They supply no authority to divert
clinical or water power or to treat nonreceipt as timely completion.
