# Choose a deliverable country, not an accounting color

*CP09–CP10; part of [national commitments](../28-national-commitments-and-residual-scarcity.md).*

**CP09 — Compare coherent whole alternatives.** Retain I's individual needs
protection, Y accrued rights, S essential service purposes and plural production
as conditional choices, not a proven simultaneous operating bundle. Reject the
interpretation that corrected national accounts, larger bank equity or all-positive
function bids select an affordable launch. Do not force 100% dividends, zero
saving, unlimited work or nonexistent AI productivity to make the country balance.

Compare at least: retaining/repairing actual provision; dated plural expansion;
a comprehensive guarantee with explicit redistribution and qualified recruitment;
and public direct provision/shared capacity where it materially changes control,
maintenance and entry. Any production-first growth route needs its real capital,
time, ecological/import and distribution mechanism; any lower-growth route needs
its actual consumption/services, investment, work/time and burden incidence. No
label, smaller GDP or voluntary-saving assumption alone settles which is better.

[NP](../33-protection-production-and-national-expansion.md) now selects protection-
first paid receiving, explicit progressive burdens, actual supply/use and dated
public improvement choice alongside the CP05–CP08 residual rule, now extended
only by [CP13–CP16](07-committed-logistics-and-continuing-claims.md)'s scheduled
public-logistics slice. It still limits unproved blanket commitments/executive
power and leaves protected critical conflicts unresolved. Uncommitted-only
retain/repair, broader public/private requisition and paid redundant capacity
remain real alternatives with different reach, coercion, source and capture risk. Preserve these value/security/distribution
objections; original evidence and legitimate public choice can displace selection.

**CP10 — The evidence compiler does not govern.** Sector/local operators prepare
actual needs, production, dates and alternatives; Treasury reconciles cash/debt/
foreign claims and material burdens; separately controlled source custody preserves
originals; the Assembly authorizes general priorities, tax and money. Qualified
professional judgments, clinical consent, opposition, courts and actual independent
review retain separate powers under C03/C04/B/P. A missing estimate permits no
technical policy veto or executive tax, confiscation or eligibility decree.

Follow appointment/removal, funding, source/vendor access and execution together.
Protected ordinary funds and lawful deputies must remain usable when challenged
actors refuse or a cabinet loses office. Same-controller AI reviews are analysis,
not independent verification or a public mandate. Present affected participation,
expert qualifications and actual source independence honestly; do not equate a
multi-agent desk audit with lived accounts or qualified legal/economic review.

No success score can offset suppression of opponents, arbitrary coercion or an
unreceived remedy. Report household safety/agency/time, ordinary relationships,
productive/ecological burdens, observed dissent and actual political replacement
separately. A lower account residual or more rules is not evidence of better lives.
