# Recurring taxes: a company owes on its own evidenced net profit

*NF01–NF04; part of [C06/42](../42-recurring-tax-and-noncash-work.md).
Design105 research, not enacted law.*

**NF01 — An explicit mixed source benchmark.** Specify25% company profit tax,
10% destination VAT with specified zero rates,1–2% local site-value tax and
progressive recipient gift/inheritance tax. PA10's benchmark personal20/35/45/55%
and Y10% actual labor remain. This is not a yield/affordability finding. No
general net-wealth, turnover, financial-transaction, robot or carbon tax is
created by this package; NF37 states the environmental/source boundary.
Assembly primary law owns bases, rates, thresholds, exemptions and new powers;
local councils vary only the delegated site rate. A tax form/AI cannot enact it.

[Design156 TN17](../59/05-country-package-and-source-continuity.md) withdraws
the whole numerical package's default replacement preference. Retain these
specified units/base/rate formulas as benchmarks and actual lawful retrofit
sources until express prospective replacement; NF39 notice/dates and old
claims persist. Numerical or source-law approval does not transfer by indexing.

**NF02 — Company, not controller guilt.** Each limited-liability trading body,
commercial cooperative or commercial public corporation is a separate annual
1January–31December taxpayer:25% of positive net profit, zero on a loss. Sole
traders/genuine unincorporated partners retain their TA personal units. No group
consolidation/loss transfer or automatic director/shareholder debt is selected.
Domestic controlled transactions remain actual transactions with NF06 correction,
not two deductions for one outside cost. Noncommercial gifts/statutory grants
to genuine public-purpose charities are not trading receipts; actual commercial
profit is taxable equally. Public-body tax within consolidated government is
an internal transfer, not extra national resources. Custodied client property
and an actual restricted fund are not a custodian's own company profit.

**NF03 — Defined residence and source.** Resident means incorporated here or
actually centrally managed here by the people making strategic decisions.
Resident bodies include worldwide profit; dual residence/foreign relief uses
actual treaty/source proof. A nonresident owes only net profit attributable
to a domestic fixed business place, a domestically active person habitually
concluding its contracts, or domestic land/building rent/disposal. Functions,
assets, risks, actual receipts/costs determine attribution, not merely a server,
customer, listing or foreign parent. No domestic statute invents foreign tax
collection, global permanent establishment or forced foreign evidence access.

**NF04 — Profit is neither turnover nor Treasury cash.** Recognize revenue
when performance is delivered and an enforceable consideration right exists;
recognize corresponding actual received inputs/services and enforceable costs.
Deduct once genuine pay including NF30 employer-funded remuneration, consumed
inputs/inventory on sale, rent, energy, repair/access, ordinary insurance and
business services. Exclude distributions, private consumption, income tax,
punitive fines, principal and fictional invoices. VAT due to Treasury/refundable
deposits/client assets are not revenue; recoverable input VAT is not a second
cost. Unrecoverable VAT enters a genuine cost once. Proved uncollectible debts
get one deduction, actual later recovery one inclusion. Accrual may precede
collection: lawful installment/insolvency protection applies, no invented cash.

[OE19/36](../../C05/13-enterprise-and-association-constitutions.md) now clarifies
genuine non-distributing public-purpose charity, preserving this actual receipt/
commercial classification. Registrar entry does not award tax relief/donor deduction.
Consolidated reports do not pool legal-unit losses, private title or Treasury cash;
no new company rate or cooperative/public-label commercial exemption follows.

[FP17–FP19](../56/05-published-sources-and-common-risks.md) retains mixed sources
without independent-upside assumptions. A foreign pension-return tax creates
no NF levy, public investment income or permission to take beneficiary assets.
Private client/member principal remains outside the manager's profit; actual
source law, genuine returns, loss and receipt keep their separate treatment.

[PF01–PF08](../57-private-retirement-tax-and-personal-capital.md) distinguishes
actual member-owned paid contributions/fiduciary custody from sponsor capital,
NF02 trading profit and NF28 unallocated accumulation. No pension licence grants
an entity exemption. Deduct genuine gross remuneration once under actual NF04;
net private saving/withholding inside it is not another employer expense.
