# AS05–AS08: identify the employer before transferring risk

*Part of [C07/18](../18-person-directed-assistance.md); design 116 candidate.*

**AS05 — Actual employer and nondelegable public duty.** For the public employed
route, the named public/local care entity employs and pays its staff. For an
agency/cooperative route, its actual legal employer contracts and bears employment
duties; a recipient is not that employer merely by directing their own support,
choosing staff, joining a cooperative or signing a service plan. For actual direct
employment, the choosing recipient or separately willing lawful employer accepts
that role with funded support and an understood contract. No deemed election
from cash receipt, silence or a relative's/AI's instruction.

Substance, actual control and C05/B law defeat sham self-employment, payroll-only
labels or a false nominee; an agreement cannot waive a worker's floor. Identify
employer, funder, day-to-day director, payroll custodian and actual backup separately,
including disputed scope. Actual joint liability needs its own grounds, not an
assumed all-purpose joint employer. The local care authority retains S/E's
nondelegable receiving duty under every route; contracting out does not transfer
that duty to the person or turn them into a guarantor of provider solvency.

**AS06 — Service money has a purpose and a custodian.** A direct allocation is
restricted assistance funding, not a gift, ordinary taxable benefit, general liquid
wealth or a provider's free working capital. Identify actual title, authorized
payments, accessible statements and lawful custody/segregation under the applicable
public/M/NB rules. This clause creates no new bank/client-fund guarantee or license.
For new allocations, identifiable unspent AS funds are legally segregated for
the person's assistance and covered dues, outside unrelated recipient/employer/
custodian creditor estates. Disputed tracing uses ordinary courts; no priority
over a bank's own assets or retroactive relabeling of old private security follows.
Use a managed public payment route where chosen; no compulsory personal app,
commercial bank product or single vendor. Preserve funded direct control and
proportionate spending evidence; do not inspect unrelated purchases/relationships.

A payroll agent only has its authorized task; employing a helper or paying an
invoice does not authorize it to change hours, transfer the mandate, keep unused
funds or discipline staff. Separate recipient assistance, employment dues,
withholding/remittance and administrator fees. Gross wages already include
employee deductions: do not pay them again as an employer contribution. Reconcile
actual provider/payment overlap once; no duplicate invoice, recovery or self-credit.
A blocked account is not received support and cannot finance another public duty.

**AS07 — Budget the actual support and employment package.** Start with actual
individual time/windows, simultaneous workers, travel, handovers, preparation,
records, night constraints and accessible equipment/maintenance. Add actual gross
pay/leave, employer contributions, insurance, recruitment/training/payroll/legal
help, chosen management/interpretation, independent advocacy and paid reliable
backup. Use TW hours/rest, safe qualified practice and separate public/source
calendar; an hourly contact tariff alone cannot discharge these obligations.

Do not add the same worker's leave, break or learning twice, count one person
as two concurrent assistants, or fund all choice by unpaid household time. A
continuous observation/sleep-in constraint is not free off-duty time by label;
actual restrictions decide. Whole-night and two-person tasks need actual safe
cover, not only an annual FTE denominator. Grouping has to meet each person's
need and free agreement, not reduce the assessed budget automatically.

A wage/price change or new need prompts earlier useful-time provisional source
correction and a review, not a hidden hours cut. The recipient need not top up
recognized essential care from ordinary cash, employment income or their family.
Additional voluntarily chosen nonessential purchases remain separate. No fixed
reference tariff is selected as adequate by this research.

**AS08 — Flexible use and cost correction.** Permit lawful substitutions within
purpose, actual safety and the person's chosen outcomes, including different
communication/support methods. Novel meaning remains open for explanation and
review, not automatically prohibited or credited as equivalent. Changes affecting
worker duties, clinical permissions, legal scope or continuing contracts need
their competent source, understanding and actual assent.

Underspend can mean postponed needs, unavailable workers or a lawful reserve;
it is not proof of overassessment or abandonment. Retain accepted commitments
and contingency provision before returning a genuine uncommitted balance through
a reasoned review. No automatic sweep at a month end, hospital admission, move or
death. Unreceived care remains an unmet claim, not an accounting saving or an
ordinary-cash overpayment. Assisted error correction precedes a fraud inference;
proved intentional misuse uses its own fair process and independent recovery.
