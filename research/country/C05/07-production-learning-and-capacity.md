# C05. Build productive capability before spending its expected gains

15 October 2026 · Design 66 · Candidate N01–N12, not enacted law

Choose **plural production with a bounded, elected public-capability
portfolio and paid continuing learning**. Firms/cooperatives retain ordinary
commercial choice and risk; public service capacity, common knowledge and
essential infrastructure have explicit mandates and funding. Neither a
central AI production plan nor a forecast growth dividend is the default.

A capability is a usable combination of people, knowledge, rights, tools,
sites, materials, maintenance and actual demand. Money, an AI forecast, an
approved post, a degree or a completed building alone supplies none of that
combination. Different skills, local meanings and organizational methods
must remain visible. C06 accounts trace finance; they do not choose careers,
override clinical/professional judgment or appropriate future private output.

The [joined preparation example](../../settlement/capability/README.md)
replaces an instantaneous 538-person reserve assumption with 635 paid
trainees and 65 dedicated instructors from declared prior market roles.
It preserves prior wage composition and adds facilities, maintenance,
replacement and paid return bridges. Combined with the old bounded enabling
programme and attributed operating replacement, bills are 525.725 million;
conditional prior source 535.725 retains buffer 10. No actual source or
national affordability exists; replacement must not be counted twice.

The stated reserve becomes available in year 7 only if all qualifications
and receiving conditions hold. A 10% instructor-capacity loss defeats the
final loss target; a two-year clinical delay needs a comparable ten-year
envelope, not a shorter cheap snapshot. This gives a costed route to a
conditional capability, not evidence that private productivity rose or the
whole country can operate. Future replacement cohorts remain unpriced.

Next make a compatible whole-duty production/service/transition choice,
including actual demand, time, pensions and prior sources. A lower-growth,
repair-first or purchased-capacity model can displace the example. Reopen
on bottleneck, source, qualification, controller, cost, workload or delivery
evidence; successful arithmetic does not establish better lives.
