# C04. Correct the decisions that relied on a wrong public record

*Design402 · RUC01–RUC08 · Prospective ordinary administrative law.*

[PD10](../../C02/07/03-access-correction-retention-and-objection.md) requires
source correction and downstream action. Give that duty a usable path from a
wrong fact to every materially affected administrative decision, its executing
party and any due remedy. The [EV19 acknowledgment and disposition clocks](../../C11/11/01-material-use-and-review.md)
and earlier applicable duties continue. Each authority remains accountable
for its own act.

**RUC01 — Keep a link only where the fact matters.** When an office obtains a
fact from another body and materially relies on it for a covered decision or
instruction, retain in the case the issuer, assertion and revision reference,
lawful purpose, relevant fact period, date received and official responsible.
The issuer keeps the minimum lawful means to reach each known material recipient.
A recipient that lawfully forwards the assertion or a conclusion derived from it
becomes responsible for its own downstream links. Preserve the relationship
through calculations, extracts and decision handoffs. Use pairwise references
and purpose-specific retention; a central resident profile or bulk collection
is unnecessary. When a material use cannot be traced or reviewed, suspend that
use for new adverse action and use authorized alternative evidence.

**RUC02 — Specify what changed and when it was true.** A competent source
distinguishes a historical correction, a change effective from a stated time,
an unresolved dispute, and a restriction or erasure with its own legal effect.
Record separately the fact period, issue date and assertions superseded, with
a protected reason and review route. A later-arriving message cannot restore a
superseded assertion. Concurrent inconsistent notices go to human resolution.
Use an authenticated, minimum-scope alert for each lawful recipient. Preserve
usable paper or assisted channels and original dates. An alert can restrict
reliance without revealing the corrected detail to someone who lacks authority
to receive it.

**RUC03 — Protect the person while facts or receipt are uncertain.** At credible
notice of a material error, the receiving officer checks the scope and stops
unqualified adverse reliance before the next avoidable loss. Continue existing
lawful benefits and unaffected duties. A genuine urgent exception requires
independent evidence, existing statutory authority, reasons, time limits and
expedited challenge. Do not infer a replacement fact, fresh entitlement or
consent. Seek verification directly or use other lawful evidence. A confirmed
urgent claim uses existing relief, including the applicable funded AP or sector
route. Keep original filing, response and due dates. An outage or failed lookup
does not shift the public burden onto the person.

**RUC04 — Assign unresolved delivery to a named office.** The issuer tracks
acknowledgment and routes an unanswered material alert to the recipient's
designated continuity officer before the relevant harmful act or deadline.
The receiving office searches live, executed and derivative uses of the
affected assertion, including work held by contractors. Record confirmed
dependence, independent grounds and unresolved links. A delivery confirmation
proves delivery of the notice only. Where a lost or unauthorized link makes
complete tracing impossible, narrow the exposed use, search lawful alternative
records, give safe assisted challenge and record remaining exposure for
independent review. Never disclose the fact through a blanket broadcast.

**RUC05 — Decide each legal effect and preserve direct challenge.** A named
qualified officer reviews a materially affected open or completed administrative
decision under the law and evidence for its original period. Record whether
the correction changes the act or independent grounds sustain it. Give usable
notice and fair reply where required. In covered prospective schemes, require
an own-motion disposition of an adverse act materially affected by a confirmed
historical error. Give the ordinary review period from usable notice of that
new disposition for the corrected issue. Preserve earlier or broader routes
under their own law. A source correction supplies no power to reopen a court
judgment, rewrite third-party rights, impose retroactive liability or claw back
a payment without independent lawful grounds and process.

**RUC06 — Execute the remedy and keep missed windows visible.** Trace changed
instructions through the actual provider, registry or payer. Preserve separately
the revised decision, restoration due, date and amount or service actually
received. Avoid double payment or delivery of a single obligation and keep
distinct duties separate. If restoration in time is no longer possible, the
competent officer records the missed window and hands off any lawful substitute,
backpay or compensation claim. Retain the original relief where still possible.
A fixed record, notification or sent transfer cannot discharge an undelivered
remedy. Keep that obligation and its owner open under [LX](../../C11/12/04-proactive-redress-and-missing-cases.md)
or the applicable law.

**RUC07 — Keep correction contestable and appropriately private.** People may
submit contrary evidence or challenge suppression, misuse or omission at a
reachable office without taking the message to each agency. The data supervisor
can examine lawful use and trace failures. A competent administrative reviewer
or court controls the merits and enforceable remedy within its jurisdiction.
Allow protected review of originals and lawful audit without republishing the
wrong record. Apply PD retention, erasure and legal holds to case links, copies
and backups. Test restored systems for stale adverse instructions before use.
AI may identify possible links and omissions. A model inference or match
cannot establish a disputed fact, bind a review or certify delivery.

**RUC08 — Fund and test the whole duty before commencing a class.** Elected
lawmakers specify covered transfers, scope, authorities, effective dates and
appropriations. Assign paid correction, receiving, review and execution owners,
including successors through vendor exit or local reorganization. Test delayed
or conflicting notices, stale backups, privacy exposure, offline receipt,
concurrent staff demand and actual remedy deadlines. Compare with direct
verification and simpler evidence reuse. Hold a new use if its material errors
cannot be reversed in practice. Keep existing protective duties, receipts,
accrued claims and independent remedies under their governing law.
