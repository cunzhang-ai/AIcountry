# Sixteen adverse school-control arguments and substantive alternatives

Design161 · Written reasoning, not field tests, schooling outcomes or paid drills

| Adverse situation | Selected response / actual remaining failure |
|---|---|
| Mayor demands a principal's party loyalty | Actual local public employer/C04 qualified appointment, protected career and external challenge; no lawful purge, but actual compliant remedy/source unknown |
| Teacher uses professional freedom to hide abuse | Actual duty/evidence/fair employment action, child safety/PO/OJ independent; professional peers have no final criminal immunity |
| Principal chooses the school's final inspector | State outside-provider career function and conflict-free case allocation; names alone do not establish independent controllers |
| Complaint concerns the national Inspector | Chief/implicated team cannot finally decide it; protected receipt, OJ court-selected qualified outside assistance/direct review and paid competent receiving, actual availability unproved |
| Parent faction demands censorship or exclusion of another child | Lawful content/professional judgments and individual rights constrain majority; own child voice/direct help remains |
| Head controls which pupils may speak; AI copies flood feedback | Independent safe channels/chosen representation/other workable methods and reasons; no head-appointed voice or account-count mandate |
| Funded school screens donors, faith or disability by interview | ED equal publicly funded entry, lawful need/continuity/travel rules and fair residual draw; additional cost/support/actual alternative unknown |
| Inspector treats unfamiliar home/future learning as neglect | Proportionate equivalent learning/safeguarding with supported interpretation and review; no home-entry/testing power or state ontology monopoly |
| Immediate grave violence risk at a school site | Head at most2calendar-day narrow site restriction, safe arrangements/notice, separate actual force/clinical law; no child detention or automatic diagnosis guilt |
| Head changes the order label or term to restart a suspension | Initial clock cannot reset; independent at-most10calendar-day continuation/fresh reasons and direct useful-time court access; expiry creates no staff |
| Prolonged safety need, no actual accepting place | Inspector/local allocation must arrange actual safe qualified learning from missed session; non-delivery recorded, no unpaid parent/device or fictional receiver cures it |
| Unsafe household or teacher complaint, defense needs evidence | Direct private child route, chosen aid/protected PD/OJ evidence and meaningful reply, no compulsory parent/principal-first/public testimony |
| AI platform quietly revises curriculum/credential ranking | DR/LR/actual law for binding rule, retained human professional assessment/HC and appeal; credentials/data copies are no mandate or understanding |
| Care/clinical/transport reserve also counted as teacher backup | One paid qualified peak/calendar account, NC shared periods once, separate health/care needs; actual workforce/FX/full cost remains unknown |
| National/local source freeze defeats lawful school/inspection orders | Existing funded guarantee/fallback/source challenge/direct courts; no Treasury merits veto or self-funding promise, actual non-delivery can persist |
| Method start coincides with closure/private-provider insolvency | Exact ED start and OP/T case/employer/record/worker/pension/contract/title successors, no forced private asset seizure or changed existing due rights |

| Genuine institutional rival | Real tradeoff and reason for current selection |
|---|---|
| Strong local retain/repair | Less transition/inspection overhead and stable relationships; may work better where existing independence/access is real. Still requires actual remedy/source and no public exclusion. |
| Parent-choice/voucher | More individual route choice and plural initiative; risks selection/transport/information/resource dependence. Publicly funded equal access and paid residual capacity cannot be assumed from a voucher. |
| Autonomous community/worker-governed schools | Stronger shared responsibility/innovation and potentially less bureaucratic power; group closure, unpaid participation and child dissent remain. Preserve as an actual alternative, not just a label inside ED. |
| Direct national public schools | Common authority/source may reduce local exclusion; central political/curriculum/career capture and distance may grow. No universal winner without actual scale and governance. |
| Stronger pupil self-government | Greater practical learner authority could improve agency; safeguarding, minority rights, developing capacities and paid adult duties remain. Student voice is no compulsory unpaid administration or universal substitute consent. |
