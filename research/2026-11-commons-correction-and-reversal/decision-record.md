# Design510 decision record

**Baseline and selection.** Use the [Design509 baseline](baseline.json)
under the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md). Existing PCO04 and
PCO11–PCO16 make regression and review reach a political decision.
SR and the source-correction rules give remedies and scoped evidence
correction. Make their material implementation separable before reliance,
so an authorized stop or later elected change can reach the actual
controllers while independently lawful duties continue. Select the
[funded exit and verification](../country/C14/04/08-reverse-a-harmful-public-measure.md)
for a major practice with linked adverse effects or retirement of
essential ordinary capacity.

**Objections and alternatives.** Duplication can delay benefits and
consume already scarce capacity. Use a narrower staffed fallback where
it covers the full shared failure and next call. A model flag or lawful
policy's poor performance should not allow judicial substitution of an
elected choice. Maintain distinct legal and political tracks. Where
evidence cannot determine scope, seek the proper narrow relief and
continue qualified independently supported work. Preserve due claims,
contractual rights and protected data. An unresolved individual case
does not imply every act in the programme was defective.

**Handoff and reconsideration.** Identify affected custodians, service
owners, lawful executors and funding windows before a scoped live test.
Use a constructed or suitably authorized rehearsal with no induced loss
of services. Rate both false stops and missed stops, correction through
the last receiver, and the next independent duty. Reopen if replacement
losses, reports, independent verification or a common funding dependency
undermine the scope. The agents' review and the [sources](sources.md)
establish no affected-party assent, enactment or actual restoration.
