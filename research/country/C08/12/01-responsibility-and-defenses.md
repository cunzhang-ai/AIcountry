# Personal responsibility, defined fault and supported defense

*P01–P05; part of [C08 criminal core](../12-criminal-core-and-procedure.md).*

**P01 — Legality and the prosecution burden.** Only a sufficiently precise,
published, prospectively enacted offense can support punishment. Its statute
states conduct, relevant circumstances, fault, harm/risk, defenses and maximum
sanction. No analogy, administrative label, model update or local order can
create a crime or raise a maximum. Prosecution proves every element and
disproves a properly raised justification or excuse beyond reasonable doubt.
The accused need only identify a credible evidential basis for a defense;
silence or inability to finance an expert does not shift that burden. Genuine
remaining doubt benefits the accused. A later lawful decriminalization or
more lenient penalty benefits pending and existing cases; administration
cannot invent an exception. Prior lawful procedural protections survive.

**P02 — Fault, causation and actual ability.** Intention means a purpose to
perform the conduct or bring about the result, or knowledge that the result
is practically certain. Recklessness means conscious disregard of a
substantial, unjustifiable, specific risk. Criminal gross negligence applies
only where expressly stated: a legally defined duty, actual ability to act,
serious foreseeable risk, a gross departure from reasonable care in those
actual circumstances and the required causal result. A bad outcome, model
score, hindsight or unavailable ideal staffing does not prove fault. Result
liability requires a substantial causal contribution within the risk/conduct
prohibited; a remote association is insufficient. An omission needs a prior
legal duty, practical capacity and the specified fault. A genuine resource
shortage retains civil/public correction duties without automatically proving
an official's crime. Disputed science needs adversarial evidence, not a new
court-made offense.

**P03 — Participation, attempt and institutional responsibility.** A person
who intentionally directs or substantially assists a specified offense is
liable only on proof of their own act, knowledge and required purpose.
Membership, rank, kinship, platform operation or employment alone is not
complicity. Intentional attempt requires a substantial step directed to the
particular crime beyond preparation; its maximum is half the completed
maximum. Effective voluntary abandonment that prevents completion excludes
attempt liability, but not a separately completed harm. Agreement or
prediction alone is not an additional conspiracy offense under this core.

Under the current human-responsibility design, an AI output is neither an
independent offender nor a defense. This does not settle future sentience or
legal personhood; any revision needs separate rights, attribution and lawful
migration review. Replication cannot multiply a political mandate. Identify who
actually controlled deployment, authorized the relevant act and had the duty,
knowledge, ability and fault proved. Do not automatically blame the operator,
programmer, user or director. A legal person's criminal attribution requires
an expressly enacted offense/sanction schedule and proven authorized policy
or attributable culpable act; unrelated workers' knowledge cannot be pooled
to fabricate intent. [Q07–Q09](../15/06-organizations-and-continuity.md) now supplies a proposed
body-attribution/fine/correction schedule, with fresh review required; civil,
regulatory, repair and illicit-proceeds routes retain their own grounds. Natural persons remain prosecutable on their own proved conduct.

**P04 — Defenses without compulsory treatment.** Necessary, proportionate
force against an actual or reasonably perceived imminent unlawful attack is
a defense, including protection of another. A mere property loss does not
justify intentionally lethal force. Necessity needs imminent serious harm,
no reasonably available lawful alternative and a proportionate response;
it cannot justify torture, slavery or deliberate killing of an uninvolved
person. Serious coercion can excuse conduct under the same constrained
necessity/proportionality assessment. Honest factual error negates required
knowledge or intention; reasonableness remains relevant to an expressly
reckless/negligent offense. The state disproves a properly raised defense.

If, despite support, a person could not understand the relevant nature or
wrongfulness of conduct or exercise the control required by a defined excuse,
criminal liability cannot be presumed from diagnosis or age alone. Examine
actual capacity at the act with independent assistance. This does not remove
civil legal capacity or create a treatment/custody order. Voluntary intoxication
is not a blanket excuse or proof of intent; liability still needs enacted
fault. Superior orders cannot excuse torture or other manifestly unlawful
harm. Clinical need uses the same separate consent/rescue laws as for anyone.

**P05 — Children and proof of age.** No person is criminally convicted for
conduct before 16; no seriousness exception lowers that age. For conduct at
16–17 use specialized child proceedings, independent counsel and supported
voice, with no adult-court transfer and an aggregate custodial maximum of five
years. Age is assessed at the act, not charging or trial; unresolved reasonable
age doubt uses the protected younger category. No maturity score lowers the
threshold. Voluntary restorative/support routes and immediate lawful safety
remain available below it. Compulsory child placement needs its separate
specific protection grounds, least restrictive safe care, prompt independent
review and actual delivery. It cannot impose a penal term, confession bargain
or indefinite locked treatment for conduct outside criminal responsibility.
Adults' civil capacity, young people's medical consent and child sexual-
protection ages are different questions; this clause settles none of them.

The [LF public-force ceiling](../../C08/24-lawful-force-and-civilian-response.md) adds a separate means test for an already authorized public act. P04 private defense, B18 claims, original clocks and court jurisdiction remain distinct; a valid order does not itself authorize force.
