# Household protection and usable temporary receiving

*Design287 · TH13–TH17 · Proposed application of TH02 and TH09–TH12.*

**TH13 — Establish the household's safe routes and latest starts.**
When unsafe indoor conditions threaten a resident's ability to remain
or receive an essential service, the existing TH02 officer works with
the resident, the building operator and the relevant care authority.
Record each coupled need and its own deadline, the period to cover
and the last time to switch before harm. Compare a safe stay with
repair, accessible temporary receiving and a voluntary secure move
where the condition will persist. Give due weight to the resident's
ordinary care, commitments and preferences. Use simple arrangements
when a safe, directly verifiable repair or equivalent service suffices.
Offer accessible human contact and accept a report before a complete
assessment. Provide independently safe aid as it becomes available.

**TH14 — Verify that each essential component works at the receiving
place.** To commit to a route, obtain the actual receiver's acceptance
for the person, place, time and period. For a temporary move, that
includes suitable accommodation, a journey the person can take and
any essential care or equipment that must work at the destination.
Check what must happen en route, what can remain separate and how
the person may return. Reserve the necessary distinct slots. The
arranging officer keeps the coordination duty through actual receipt
and the original repair remains assigned. Relevant operators retain
clinical, safety and delivery responsibility. A room, referral or
separate green service status counts only for its proved contribution.

**TH15 — Fund the interruption through the next safe decision.**
Before choosing a route, verify the payer's actual available funds by
each due date, the paid service and any bridge or continuation option.
Check common failures, existing commitments, donor protection and
replacement capacity. Keep future refunds separate from spendable
funds. Elected bodies determine eligible sources and scope. Use only
authorized expenditures and existing urgent powers. If the combined
route fails, deliver any safe component that can stand alone, seek an
available lawful alternative and expose the unresolved protection gap
to the competent officer and urgent independent reviewer. Preserve
original claims, clocks, ordinary payments and remedies.

**TH16 — Protect the resident's choice and verify what happened.**
Explain available routes, known limits, costs and consequences in
accessible terms. Support a person's voluntary decision, including
refusal of a site or agreement to an independently safe part. A
referral or silence supplies no consent. Refusal leaves the underlying
need and remaining lawful duties visible. Share only task-specific
information with recipients who need it. The resident can correct
the record and reach existing urgent review directly. Verify actual
receiving and any gap with the resident and service providers as
appropriate. AI may help identify conflicts or omissions. A competent
human retains the decision and operators retain delivery obligations.

**TH17 — Decide before the temporary route runs out.** Recheck
before the last feasible reservation or dispatch time. Keep a paid,
accepted extension or other qualified route available where return
conditions remain uncertain. Confirm safe indoor conditions, practical
access and any necessary resumed service before authorizing supported
return, with the resident's participation and applicable consent.
Repair completion and the end of a booking are separate events from
safe return. Preserve the underlying tenancy, service eligibility,
continuing repair and original breach record. Record verified return,
funded continuation and any uncovered interval separately.
