The question is how the country's **existing C04–C07 service guarantees and
local discretion** receive lawful, collectable, reviewable revenue without
making a technocratic model the taxing authority or letting a rich locality
buy out of the national rights floor. Design 18 named broad tax types and a
single national commitment account but left the fiscal incidence, tax-base
assignment, local rate power, equalization and taxpayer remedy chain thin.
This is a substantive country-design dependency, not a request to choose
fictional universal rates.

**Values:** effective public services, equal political standing, local
self-government, fair distribution across people and places, civic
independence from concentrated wealth, due process and ecological repair.
**Constraints:** only enacted law can tax; lawful accrued claims and
contracts survive transition; cash, labor and imports cannot be inferred
from a rate; government levels share one consolidated account. **Empirical
claims untested here:** expected yield, evasion, incidence, behavior,
land valuations and whether a transfer arrives on time. **Design
hypothesis:** assigned diverse bases plus national equalization can support
common rights while leaving local choices, if collection and appeal are
usable. Administrative capture, informal incomes or a collapsed tax base
could defeat that mechanism.

## Packages compared without an invented tax base

| Package | Public-power and financing effect | Important failure |
|---|---|---|
| Retain design 18's list of tax types and general equalization | Minimizes new procedure; future law chooses details. | Leaves a service mandate unfunded or a local tax power unclear; consumption relief can arrive after hardship; valuation and tax exemptions can become patronage. |
| Direct income and corporate taxes with national grants, little consumption or land taxation | Explicit ability-to-pay focus and a simpler resident-facing system in some contexts. | Volatile or mobile bases may make local services hostage to central grants; untaxed land and intergenerational transfers can entrench advantage. It may be appropriate where valuation or consumption-relief capacity is absent. |
| Consumption and land taxation with wide local rate freedom, minimal central redistribution | More visible local fiscal choice and potentially broad routine bases. | Essential needs differ from local tax capacity; a poor place may have to tax heavily for weaker services. Regressivity, land-title disputes and local tax competition can undermine equal standing. |
| **Selected: assigned plural bases, elected rates and rights-backed equalization** | National law defines bases and rights; local councils vary delegated land rates and service choices; national taxes and grants cover the common floor; household relief and independent challenges accompany collection. | More institutional capacity is required. Several modest taxes can still produce high combined burdens; transfer and valuation errors may harm people before an appeal. |

The selected package is a **working design**, not an optimal tax theorem.
It does not imply that every listed tax must commence on the same day or
that the earlier synthetic revenue amounts are attainable. The direct-tax
package remains a genuine rival if local valuation, consumption relief or
corporate assessment cannot be made fair and reliable. A government may
choose a lawful narrower base, but must show how it finances existing
guarantees and what people lose or gain.

## The selected revenue and local-finance chain

The Assembly must enact the taxable event, base, payer, rate structure,
threshold, relief, filing duty, information powers, commencement and
challenge route. A regulator, Treasury formula, revenue-agency model or
AI system cannot amend those elements. Personal taxation is individual
by default, with progressive rates on labor and realized capital income.
Child, disability and care support is arranged through transparent credits
and benefits rather than assuming a spouse provides unpaid work. Final
assessment corrects withholding and allows appeals. Corporate tax reaches
genuine net profits, with enacted treatment of losses, investment and
related parties; the personal/corporate interaction is published so neither
the same gain nor a disguised wage slips invisibly between bases. Large
gifts and inheritances use a progressive transfer route with notice and
lawful liquidity relief, without declaring all private inheritance invalid.

The consumption tax has a broad statutory base with explicit household
relief paid **before or alongside** the burden to people otherwise unable
to afford essentials. The design does not presume a bank account, stable
address, digital ID or perfect benefit register. Where that relief cannot
arrive reliably, the Assembly must retain a narrower essential-goods
protection or choose a different tax mix. It must publish the joint effect
of tax, benefit withdrawal and service fees, including people outside
formal employment. A user fee cannot be used to repeal the guaranteed
health, education or emergency floor by stealth. Tax credits and reduced
rates are counted as public costs, not free generosity.

National law defines a recurrent **land-value** base and independent
valuation/appeal cycle. Local councils set a rate within legislated bands
and retain an enacted share; the remainder, if any, enters the national
account under exact law. Deferral for a cash-poor owner is bounded, recorded
as a later claim and accompanied by protection against unjust eviction or
opaque liens. Tenants and historic land claimants have a route to challenge
misattribution; land title is not made legitimate by a tax map. Valuation
requires evidence independent of a local permit patron. Pollution charges
and public natural-resource rents name measured harms or lawful extraction
rights, disclose costs passed to households and firms, and fund protection
and transition where imposed. Revenue that shrinks as harm falls is not
treated as a growing permanent base.

The national government sets the basic service floor and finances the
portion local capacity cannot meet. The representative equalization
formula compares **standardized service need** with **potential own-source
revenue at a common reference rate**, rather than actual discretionary
spending or the rate a council chose. Elected local bodies retain lawful
rate and provider choices, and keep a defined marginal benefit from a
stronger local base; an automatic full clawback would hollow out that
discretion. Population, age-related need, accessibility and geography may
enter the formula only with justified, reviewable measures. Formula
versions, missing data, distribution and transition caps are public;
personal records stay with their custodians. Affected localities and
residents can challenge classification, but one wealthy locality has no
veto over the common floor. If a locality cannot meet a lawful guarantee,
the national fallback in C04 still applies and must be funded; a grant
dispute does not extinguish the resident's claim.

The revenue authority applies law through a taxpayer charter: usable
notice and assistance, proportionate information requests, confidentiality,
reasoned assessments, prompt correction and refund, internal reconsideration,
an independent tribunal and ordinary court review. Collection and appeals
need protected staff and funds; one vendor, minister or dominant company
cannot control both the evidence and final judgment. Enforcement must
distinguish mistake, inability to pay, civil noncompliance and proven fraud.
No AI risk score is evidence enough for an assessment or seizure; criminal
penalties need normal proof, defense and proportionality. The state may
publish aggregate exemptions, settlements, arrears and enforcement patterns
without a permanent public dossier on each taxpayer. Opposition and audit
have lawful access to detect selective enforcement against political rivals.

The [OECD analysis of taxation and inequality](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2024/07/taxation-and-inequality_b7cf450c/8dbf9a62-en.pdf)
compares VAT incidence under different denominators and the relative
targeting of cash relief; this record does not transfer its country results
to an unknown population. The [OECD fiscal-equalization comparison](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/fiscal-federalism-2022_201c75b6-en/full-report/component-5.html)
supports distinguishing standard need from actual spending. The
[OECD land-tax review](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2015/04/reforming-the-tax-on-immovable-property_g17a2631/5js30tw0n7kg-en.pdf)
informs local tax assignment and valuation limits. The
[OECD cross-border tax overview](https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/policy-issues/cross-border-and-international-tax.html)
explains profit-shifting problems but does not grant unilateral taxing
rights. The [IMF domestic-tax-dispute note](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/tnm/issues/2026/08/18/good-practices-for-tax-administration-management-of-domestic-tax-disputes-576524)
informs the appeal chain, while the [IMF fiscal-transparency code](https://www.imf.org/external/np/fad/trans/code.htm)
supports showing tax expenditures and fiscal risks in the budget. These

## Compatibility, delay, objections and migration

This selection revises C06 and specifies C04 equalization; it affects C05
income/care incentives, C07 service funding, C08 administrative justice,
C09 land/ecology, C11 fiscal evidence, C13 cross-border tax agreements,
C14 founding, A5 and founding phase F. It is compatible with design 18's
conditional domestic currency and complete external-currency alternative:
neither currency choice creates a tax base, real purchasing power or foreign
exchange. The three-year social/fiscal example retains its authored receipts;
design 19 does **not** relabel them as taxes yielded by this package.
Individual rate, land-value and transfer-tax choices remain statutory,
while constitutional equal standing, property process and the rights floor
constrain them. No prior analysis automatically approves this new package.

An adopting jurisdiction must inventory current tax laws, assessed debts,
refunds, credits, treaties, records, land titles, local grants, staff,
appeals and privacy obligations. New rates start under published prospective
rules with notice; pending audits and cases retain a lawful successor and
appeal. First-year collection needs trained assessors, accessible counters,
valuation, payment and refund channels, independent adjudication, audit
and retained old-system operation during transfer. These use cash, people
and technology in the same C06 commitment account as care, elections and
housing. A new service cannot be declared funded by projected corporate,
estate, land or carbon receipts that have not been legally or operationally
established. When receipts fall, Treasury discloses cash and claims; cabinet
presents tax, borrowing, lawful reprioritization and service-continuity
options to the Assembly. It cannot suspend equalization or a taxpayer
remedy by administrative delay. If the Assembly fails to act, prior
obligations and limited budget bridge continue only within their lawful
and available resources; a tax forecast supplies no emergency authority.

Objections remain material. Consumption tax plus failed relief can harm
low-income households; land levies can displace people with little cash;
corporate rules may shift investment or be evaded; inheritance taxation
may burden family enterprises or be circumvented by the wealthy; multiple
bases impose compliance costs; and central equalization can become a
political weapon or reward manipulated need data. A nationally standardized
formula may miss a locality's real costs, while local autonomy can reproduce
spatial inequality. Neither revenue yield nor a favorable average net
incidence resolves these conflicts for an affected person. Alternative
tax mixes and local-finance assignments remain open to lawful public choice.

**Evidence qualification:** the linked primary comparative sources and
local design texts were reviewed, but no original population, tax returns,
titles, local budgets or affected-party evidence was obtained. **Research
selection:** the plural-base, rights-backed local-finance package.
**Legal authorization:** none. **Operational readiness:** no collection,
refund, valuation or appeal capacity shown. **Observed outcomes:** none;
no claim of higher welfare or superiority to a real country is established.

The next bounded question is whether the package can fund an existing
essential-service floor through one revenue shock **without pushing a low-
income household or a low-capacity locality below that floor**. A real
adopting public would need an audited opening tax/base inventory, anonymized
household and place incidence, old claims, actual collection/refund dates,
valuations, appeal capacity and service costs. The deliverable is a side-by-
side account for the selected package and the direct-tax rival, showing
gross receipts, collection costs, net household burdens, local grants,
arrears and remedies, with participant objections. The research project
has no lawful data mandate or field budget. Reopen design 19 if actual
incidence, administrative capture, title conflict, failed relief, external
tax law or affected-public opposition changes the tradeoff; if monitoring
is absent, do not claim the system is funded or fair.
