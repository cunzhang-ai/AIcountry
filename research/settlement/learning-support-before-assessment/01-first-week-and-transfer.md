# Support in the first school week

Design336 · Constructed comparison for [ED34–ED38](../../country/C07/21/08-learning-support-before-assessment.md)

Two learners need four qualified support-hours in the first week. A
transferring learner needs two under a continuing plan for earlier
classes. A newly referred learner needs two before later classes
that are materially inaccessible on credible educational evidence.
The second learner has no diagnosis. The planned independent
assessment will issue a decision on day 25. The school already has
three suitable hours in the exact windows. An independent provider
can deliver the fourth hour before the later class for two cash
units. Assessment preparation and review need a separate one unit
and a qualified examiner. Three units are available for these
incremental costs. All evidence, staffing and money are stipulated.

| Arrangement | First-week result |
|---|---|
| Wait for final assessment or transfer clearance | The existing timetable contains neither verified comparable transfer support nor the new interim support. Four hours remain unreceived in the stipulated case. |
| Prioritize the two earlier due hours and use ordinary capacity only | Deliver both transfer hours and one new hour. One later hour remains uncovered. The unused cash alone cannot supply it. |
| Use three ordinary hours and buy the accepted fourth hour | Both receive two qualified hours. Two units pay the independent provider, and one pays the separately qualified assessment path. |

Select the third arrangement. The transfer school confirms actual
comparable support at A's first relevant class. The local owner
provides B an interim disposition before the first useful time,
within the proposed two-teaching-day outer limit. The assessment
on day 25 can establish continuing support, or modify it with
reasoned notice and review. Neither the assessment nor a
later extra session is credited as the first week's support.

If the outside provider shares a crew already committed elsewhere,
its offered hour cannot qualify. One support-hour remains unmet
even if the two cash units remain available. If the original
assessment later slips to day 34, it requires a valid reasoned
extension. Without it, the day-30 decision duty is overdue.
The education owner retains due support and the outside route.
A transfer on day 20 does not move that date.
