# C13. Drought, price shock and an unsafe reserve

*Part of [C13 lived experience](../C13-lived-experience.md); integrated design 55 authored stress, not an observed event.*

Two dry seasons reduce a region's harvest. An import route becomes costly,
one large buyer cuts farm-gate prices while retail prices rise, and a local
water plan protects an already damaged downstream ecosystem. The food
authority proposes reserve release, but one stored lot has a credible
contamination signal. A low-income household cannot reach a cheaper shop;
a school reports that meals are arriving late.

C09's food-security authority publishes the original harvest, water,
stock, route and price observations with uncertainty and proposes more than
one package. C08's food-safety inspector verifies the lot and uses only its
lawful hold or recall power; no official counts the suspect lot as edible
capacity to improve a shortage figure. C05 examines the buyer's control
and the farmers' real alternatives with fair defense. C10 tests replacement
imports and foreign-payment limits rather than promising a foreign seller's
consent. C07 follows the household and school to food actually received,
with safe interim support and a direct complaint route.

C06 shows whether reserve replacement, household support, farmers' lost
income, ecological water duties, delivery workers and foreign currency fit
one account. Cabinet may execute prior-law measures; a new material
priority or fiscal burden returns to an Assembly that can sit and hear
affected farmers, residents, workers and downstream communities. No
minister, buyer, food model or inspector can acquire rationing, tax or
ecosystem-waiver power by delaying a vote. If lawful supplies still fall
short, the unmet need remains visible and subject to remedy.

The exercise fails if stock on paper replaces safe food, a household cannot
use its benefit, a school meal is counted on dispatch, one buyer captures
the response, or ecological loss is shifted silently to the next season.
It proves only that responsibility and conflict routes are named. Actual
geography, people, law, prices, stocks, contracts and observed outcomes are
missing.
