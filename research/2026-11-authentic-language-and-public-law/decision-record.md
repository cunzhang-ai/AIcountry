# Design315 decision on authentic legal versions

## Baseline and decision

Pin Design314, A1–A3, DR05–DR08, LX01–LX03, C12 and the working method.
Select how a competent elected body can adopt an exact set of co-authentic
versions where the governing law permits them. Keep public evidence,
elected approval, authentic publication, legal interpretation and
execution distinct. The project assistant authors the selection.
Existing legal powers, publication requirements and holds govern.

## Alternatives and material objections

A single binding language simplifies proof but can make speakers of
another recognized language dependent on a derivative version.
Co-authentic texts with a bounded number of designated languages
support parity where legitimately chosen. They cost real time,
drafting and access to qualified interpretation and court relief.
Treating every media representation as equally authoritative would
make consent and later correction hard to verify. Keep open
the choice of lawful official languages and authorized annex forms.
Distinguish a publication discrepancy from a disagreement actually
inside the legislation. The Canadian sources support feasibility
of the legal model. The case isolates the consequences of two
different filing triggers and does not borrow a Canadian result.

## Selection, continuing duties and reopening

Select ML01–ML05. Preserve equivalent participation across media and
equal authority only where legally adopted. Require a fresh exact
choice on any unresolved material difference before the affected
vote. Publish and correct authentic versions under prior law. Keep
original receipts, lawful claims, archived versions, existing language
and collective rights, qualified independent review and funded
successors. The proposal neither adopts an official language nor
changes existing project or legal permissions.

Test a funded legislative packet with actual multilingual readers,
a material discrepancy and a later legal claim with a close deadline.
Verify full receipt, service correction and effects on people who did
not complain. Reopen on systematic translation loss, delayed remedy,
unreachable publication or a better protective alternative. Actual
participation, legitimate authority, operational readiness and
observed outcomes remain open.
