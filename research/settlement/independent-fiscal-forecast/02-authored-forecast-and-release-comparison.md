# Test a first budget when a projected source and staffing gain are disputed

The amounts, people and times are authored. By day 30 protected
cash of 80 units is due, comprising 38 for care and safe water,
24 for household protection and 18 for the independent court and
election duties. Those require 40 separately qualified and paid
shift units. Assume ordinary protected operators have accepted
the work, with continued capacity thereafter. Continuing cash dues
through day 90 total 92. All figures are cumulative through their
date unless expressly identified as a shift during the window.

Treasury claims 93 in cash by day 30, including a six-unit
conditional sale receipt. The independent record verifies 87.
The sale is due no earlier than day 60 and remains conditional.
Verified cumulative cash by day 90 is 100 without the sale.
Treasury also expects five additional qualified shifts from
automation. The independent schedule verifies only 42 total shifts
in the early window. Two remain for optional work after protecting
the 40. A separately restricted balance cannot fund any of these
increments. The model assumes accepted authorization and distinct
qualified staff for the quantities verified in each row.

| Option | Additional cash by day 30 / day 90 | Additional early shifts | Disposition |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Keep existing due duties only | 0 / 0 | 0 | Within verified cash and shifts. No new work. |
| Full optional programme | 9 / 13 | 5 | Early cash is short by 2 and qualified shifts by 3. Without the sale, day 90 is short by 5. |
| Independent preparation tranche | 5 / 6 | 2 | Within verified cash and shifts. Requires an actual authorized tranche and independent receiving. |
| Smaller fallback tranche | 3 / 4 | 1 | Within verified cash and shifts, including the stated common loss below. |

If the sale actually arrives on day 60 it makes day 90 cash 106,
enough for the total 105 that the full programme would need.
It cannot pay a day 30 obligation. The additional shifts cannot be
booked before verified competence, release from other duties and
actual acceptance. The continued 92 of protected cash remains in
all alternatives. These sums do not assess the benefit of the
new programme or treat later funds as early borrowing authority.

Under a second case, three of the verified cash units arrive
after day 30 but still by day 90, and one of the optional shifts
is unavailable. Verified early cash is then 84 and one spare
shift remains. The preparation tranche requires 85 and two spare
shifts. A prior-authorized smaller alternative with early cash
83 and one spare shift can qualify if all receiving facts hold.
If the full programme was already legally bound, the continuing
account retains its claims. Report the true gap and seek a lawful
repair. The alternative cannot silently replace a binding order.

The comparison favors independent preparation or the smaller
approved fallback under the declared conditions. Retention is
viable in the same fixture. A fully qualified funding and staffing
plan for the larger proposal can change the disposition. The
figures do not price the country or show resident-level outcomes.
