# Design417. Keep conflicting appointments from defeating public rights

[PSW01–PSW06](../../country/C04/06/08-compatible-public-service-windows.md)
makes reported conflicts answerable by existing service owners. Require
a usable combined sequence or an explicit unmet duty. Keep adverse
attendance findings subject to notice, proof and independent review.

[Worked morning](../../settlement/compatible-public-service-windows/01-two-valid-appointments-one-morning.md)
tests elapsed time, accepted capacity and the limits of one funded
alternate. Actual relief, administrative costs and time saved remain open.
