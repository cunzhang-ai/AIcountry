# Verify the first mandate as one country

*Design531 · Application of existing country choices for a prospective adopter*

Start with repair of an existing lawful polity, the first-round selection.
Use the same inherited duties and rights for ordinary repair, the selected
parliamentary reform and any structural rival. The prior constitutional
authority decides the lawful electorate, territory and operative texts. A
local service test can start on its existing statutory authority before that
decision where it is genuinely independent of new institutions and finance.
Its results cannot establish the national polity's mandate.

## Three exhibits before relying on the opening

| Exhibit | Who prepares it and what someone else can check | Decision it supports |
|---|---|---|
| Lawful public control | The competent prior authority supplies exact constitutional and contest instruments and their effective dates. Electoral, opposition and judicial offices demonstrate usable originals, competing motions, a timely executed remedy and legal transfer on separate authority. | Which protections and electoral rules can be put to the competent public, and what must be funded or corrected first. Each owner signs for its actual scope. Independent budget staff and opposition can reconstruct a different feasible budget from the original evidence. | Standing supply, strongest repaired baseline and exact separable additions. Decide commitments only where funded sources, staffing and receiving agree. [Resource account](../first-country-resource-account/01-the-first-budget-on-one-perimeter.md) · [Joint first mandate](../../country/C06/65/09-feasible-first-mandate.md) |
| One lived comparison | Residents and independent reviewers verify timely controlled cash, actual service, a way to refuse or contest, and whether relief reaches the person. Service owners price both the normal route and the next call after loss of a common dependency. Include people missing from normal intake and the work displaced elsewhere. | Repair, continue a qualified pilot, or propose a separately authorized replacement. Report lived effects separately from lawful authority and use only comparable observations. [Payment trial](../first-adoption-trial/01-test-payment-access-before-expansion.md) · [Public conditions](../../country/C14/04/07-better-lives-and-country-choices.md) |

Keep all three exhibits and their original custody. A combined public summary
shows the evidence that applies to each decision. Do not join individual
files merely to populate it. Test overlapping dates and shared resources
even when separate bodies reported different obstacles. If an input is disputed,
retain its original owner and deadline. The reviewer verifies evidence and
the elected body decides the exact next motion.

Use the [fourteen-chapter adoption register](../founding-and-first-mandate/01-adoption-register-and-first-mandate.md)
for the full set of responsible institutions. In this exercise, C01–C04
and C08 supply the authority, membership and remedy boundary. C05–C07 and
C09–C10 supply the labor, financial, social, ecological and external
conditions. C11–C12 govern AI, source custody and participation. C13–C14
join lived receipt to transition and later review. The
[full objective audit](../../status/completion/01-objective-and-proof.md)
sets out the wider claims still to be tested. A missing chapter input
remains open even when the narrower example succeeds.

## Set a lawful order of commencement

Consider a protection that ends a minister's power to remove an independent
referee. On the same date the first contest still needs a staffed officer
and a court that can deliver a remedy. Compare a single cutover with all
appointments ready, an expressly authorized interim custodian who can serve
until a separately appointed successor accepts, and holding the proposed
dependent change before its effective date while separately lawful repair
advances. The selected staged route needs an interim appointment under
competent prior law, funds, independent originals, actual receipt and a
fixed handback. Use the [EA09 inaugural selection route](../../country/C03/09/03-founding-voting-and-failure.md)
where it applies and the existing independent court for review. A records
custodian can receive an original complaint and preserve its date only
within existing powers. A binding ruling needs its own competent office.
The incoming Assembly cannot supply authority for the referee of its
initial election. If the protection already binds and the interim
appointment fails, keep the protection in force, disclose the uncovered
duty and use the competent prior remedy. A vacancy does not revive the
minister's removed power. A separable existing-law payment repair still
has its own operating and review route.

## Reconcile results before putting them in the first budget

Run a constructed exercise with an ordinary delivery week, an early election
and the next compulsory service call. This example assumes each participant
has the requisite authority for its illustrative exercise. Nothing below is
a real appointment or appropriation.

| Time and event | Use of the common schedule | Result or necessary disposition |
|---|---|---|
| First week, established local payments | On separate dates, ordinary repair and an optional alternate payout both demonstrate that the same class of recipient obtains usable money. Prior protections and evidence are preserved. | This can support a scoped local decision. It demonstrates neither an ordinary election route nor a national fiscal effect. |
| Early election week, shared workbench and ordinary payment channel lost | A binding electoral correction needs a qualified independent interpreter from hour 2 to hour 4. A proposed optional expansion needs the same person from hour 3 to hour 5 for its accessible release exercise. The existing payment and challenge route has its own alternate and interpreter in this window. Each stand-alone exercise used the contested person successfully. | The two exercises cannot jointly support the optional expansion. Keep the independent ordinary route and the electoral correction. Hold the unbound expansion unless separate accepted cover can perform its exercise. |
| Next compulsory call, the same resource is needed by two standing duties | The later loss also removes the ordinary local interpreter. Both original rights now depend on the overlapping alternate. | Holding an optional act no longer fixes the conflict. The election and payment owners must finance accepted independent cover, or use separately qualified routes and existing relief. If either duty is missed, record the original date, harm, reviewer and funded correction. |

For the next call assume the same clock offsets and that the two standing
receivers now need the same interpreter. Their tasks require four worker-hours,
including two workers during hour 3 to hour 4. A single interpreter can supply
only three worker-hours in the combined window. If the second worker accepts
hours 3 to 5, takes neither work away from another due roster nor depends on
the failed controller, both assignments fit. Verify actual notice, privacy
and interpretation with the two receivers, and test the next paid shift.
A pay line, alternate account and each isolated
success can all be genuine while simultaneous operation still fails. On the
stated facts, fund and witness the distinct protected work first. Proceed with
separable local repair and carry any shortfall on its original clock. Keep
constitutional adoption and new reliance subject to their own authority.

## Use a decision route appropriate to the evidence

| Finding | Next disposition |
|---|---|
| Prior authority or protected contest arrangements lack a timely lawful route | Use the applicable prior remedy or a lawfully revised proposal. Separable service repair can continue on existing law. A test cannot authorize a contest or change its calendar. |
| Standalone results are favorable and the common schedule does not fit | Continue independent due work. Bring the costed protected repair and any separable optional motion to their competent bodies. Name the people still waiting. |
| Protected functions, sources and receiving fit on the first and next call | Submit the exact instruments to the competent public and elected bodies, with the strongest feasible repaired predecessor and the independent objections. A favorable rehearsal qualifies only its tested scope. |
| Actual lawful delivery follows | Record what reached people on time, who missed out, costs, remedies and outcomes. Return to the applicable review and renewal decisions before expanding. |

HM Treasury's evaluation guidance distinguishes early practical testing from
evaluation of effects at wider scale. This case applies the distinction to
shared protected functions and lawful authority. The guidance establishes
an evaluation practice. It supplies no evidence that this country has adopted
or operated its model. [HM Treasury, Test and Learn](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-magenta-book/test-and-learn-html)
