# PQ04–PQ06. Recognize demonstrated scope and keep decisions reachable

*Part of [fair licensing](../32-fair-licensing-and-portable-competence.md).*

**PQ04 — Give the same proved scope the same effect.** The sector
regulator records the exact act, operative standards, restrictions,
validity and accountable issuer. A valid authorization under the
same national standard carries across localities without a second
licence. A separate site duty, a material local risk under prior law,
and a subsisting lawful restriction still apply. For other domestic
or foreign evidence, accept equivalent competence for the scope
proved, including verifiable prior work and a practical test where
a familiar diploma or issuer is unavailable. An applicable treaty
may supply a lawful recognition route. Publish any material local
difference and ask only for proof of the actual gap, such as a
necessary task-specific language or legal competency. Preserve
existing safety duties, employment and immigration decisions under
their own laws. Permit proportionate verification without a
universal identity or access to an applicant's full history.

**PQ05 — Make the decision and its conditions usable.** Offer full
recognition for an established match. For a safe and separable subset,
issue a limited authorization with its scope intelligible to clients
and employers. Where supervision is necessary, the permission
specifies accountable acts, the supervisor's acceptance, paid time
and escalation. The act may start only within that coverage.
Otherwise give a reasoned deferral or denial and state which focused
assessment or bridge could establish the gap. A supervised bridge
does not waive wage, equality or safety obligations. Give an
alternative assessor when a competitor, employer or school controls
the first route. Qualified public case officers hold decision power
under the named sector Act, with conflict reassignment and protected
V10 succession. AI may compare standards or translate with disclosed
uncertainty. An AI score, issuer label or absent reply cannot decide
a person's authority to work.

**PQ06 — Keep the original clock and finance safe receiving.** Use
V02, B16 and AP for protected first receipt, the applicable ordinary
30-working-day decision clock, one authorized reasoned extension,
adverse reply and direct court review of delay or merits. Appropriation
funds conflict-free assessment, accessible verification, independent
review and qualified service receiving separately. Treasury fees
remain bounded and include a hardship route funded from an enacted
source. When proof or an assessor fails, use a genuinely independent
lawful route within the existing clock. Preserve the scope already
proved or held under a valid permission, subject to actual safety
orders. If there is no qualified alternate, report the uncompleted
decision, preserve the remedy and use a funded authorized service
alternative where it exists. Silence or a staffing shortage cannot
authorize the unproved act. Keep evidence, scope, first receipt and
pending challenges through a regulatory or technology change.
