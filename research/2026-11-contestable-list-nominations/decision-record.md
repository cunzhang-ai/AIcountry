# Design233 decision record. Contestable list nominations

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned Design232](baseline.json)

## Decision

Select [NM01–NM12](../country/C03/23-contestable-list-nominations.md)
for ordinary national and municipal collective list nominations. The
existing open-list preference and same-gate independent entry protect
voters after the pool is known. EN02 requires disclosure of the
nomination procedure, and EN04 protects the frozen pool. An accurately
disclosed executive veto can still prevent a genuine selector choice.
Requiring proposal access and binding selection under prior law closes
that gap for this public electoral act. Use the
[working method](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

## Real choices and objections

| Alternative | Strong reason to choose it | Material risk or tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Keep charter disclosure, voter preferences and independent entry | Respects association, keeps entry inexpensive and permits coherent slates. | The public cannot choose an absent candidate, and switching lists near the freeze loses access to existing endorsements and organizing. |
| Binding selection by members or their elected delegates with protected proposals and remedy | Members or lawful selectors control nomination and order. Small civic groups retain a simpler collective route. | Factions can capture membership and meetings. Paid access, independent custody and effective relief cost time and money. Delegate systems can distance ordinary members. |
| State-administered open primaries for all eligible electors | Broadens selection beyond party members and can weaken leadership patronage. | Rival voters can influence another association's candidates. It adds a major public ballot, eligibility and funding burden and can privilege high-visibility candidates. |
| Eliminate group nominations and select all people in a common first round | Removes exclusive party control over one ballot slot. | Changes the proportional party/civic bargain and creates separate agenda, organization and threshold problems. This remains a genuine whole-election rival. |

Select the second rule. Keep individual entry and political association.
A group can choose its purpose, lawful standing criteria and a coherent
platform. It cannot make a statutory collective choice conditional on a
leader's assent. Founding members may still form narrow groups. This
proposal does not require every group to become an all-elector party.
It also cannot solve unequal visibility or coercion simply by permitting
a primary ballot. The pre-call terms and eligibility limits need real
affected input and qualified rights review.

## Authority and succession

The Assembly or competent local law fixes prospective rules and funds
the minimum within its scope. Selectors, through their chosen method,
decide the actual candidates. An existing career officer verifies
objective process and records. A court applying prior law orders
appropriate repair. No party, court, government office or AI can
substitute preferences for missing acts. Separate appointment,
appropriation, original custody and independent execution. Treat
party-controlled membership exclusion and state-controlled denial of
custody or relief as distinct, possibly cumulative failures.

The [timed case](../settlement/list-nominations/02-withholding-in-the-final-week.md)
tests old proof, missing acts and useful execution. It assumes qualified
receivers and positive capacity where stated. NM clarifies the scope
of historical proof at V−30. It leaves the V−40 legal cutoff, support
gate, personal consent, proportional count, vacancy and lawful contest
clocks in place. The additional service and review workload needs
lawful recurring funds. Applicable RR holds and preexisting EN/ER/PC
rights, filings, privacy and paid FT/OP/T successors retain their
source. No actual procedure is adopted here.

## Reopen on actual access and independent operation

Compare the selected procedure with a real charter-only route for a
small civic list and a large organization. Rehearse one early call,
a local intake, private original access, a withheld filing and paid
review before V−40. Record cost, effective inclusion, security and lost
opportunities. Use protected testimony only where safe and authorized.
Reopen on impossible timing, serious faction exclusion, unacceptable
state leverage, weak comparative access or a better complete rival.
Observed comparators, selection, actual participation, enactment,
paid readiness and better representation remain distinct.
