# Design362. Reserve shared budget room and test actual recurring delivery

[FB42–FB47](../../country/C06/62/08-forward-budget-and-shared-commitments.md)
charges earlier and proposed uses to one elected path, leaves an explicit
planning margin and checks source, staffing and actual receiving separately.
A forecast is neither an appropriation nor a paid reserve.

The [comparison](../forward-budget-and-shared-commitments/01-two-reforms-one-forward-budget.md)
distinguishes a lost planning margin, an already bound shortfall and an early
payroll failure despite the annual pass. Actual sources and capacities remain open.
