  but misleads present readers about the current country. Reject for active
  consumers; preserve the exact dated artifacts/predecessor instead.
- Create a central reconciliation/readiness authority: could simplify routing,
  but concentrates framing/source/veto power without supplying people or money.
  Reject new power; retain existing competent owners, direct review and current
  source/consent boundaries. Ordinary problem reports need no technical dossier.

  reject that conclusion. Child/person/worker agency, care quality, source and
  actual receiving are distinct and can defeat a mathematically fitting option.
- Reopen every institutional choice at once: may expose a deeper alternative,
  but can displace stable adequate rules and whole-country material integration.
  Retain genuine I05/U, plural/direct-public, regional/joint-service and paid
  retrofit/new-settlement rivals; choose the current-account task next with
  new harm/evidence/objections able to reprioritize.
