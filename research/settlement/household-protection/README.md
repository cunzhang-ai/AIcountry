# Household protection through an interrupted service

*Design287 · Constructed unit and route comparison.*

Use a single household, the same original need and protected ordinary
capacity for every route. The key relationship is between the location
and timing of the tasks. Where useful protection requires both
components, coverage is the time and place where both can actually
be used. Cash receipt, physical acceptance and permission to move
each need separate evidence.

| Route | Cash by hour 6 / through hour 36 | First useful receiving |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Safe stay and repair | 3 / 3 | Valid if independently powered heat and the home care visit are verified. Fails in the assumed shared outage. |
| Ordinary room and original care roster | 2 / 4 | Room and journey are available. Care is still scheduled at home and no route joins both needs. |
| Temporary room with accepted care | 5 / 8 | Room and journey by hour 7, destination care by hour 9. Reserve the necessary continuation and return. |
| Secure move | Outside this envelope | Retain as a voluntary long-run choice. The assumed later unit cannot meet immediate deadlines. |

The five early units must be available by hour 6, before dispatch.
They cover the eligible commitment for the accepted
room, travel and changed care. The three remaining units cover the
extension with care and return. Cash amounts are cumulative. Staff and
vehicle allocations already due elsewhere remain protected. A reference
payer has five early and eight total. A weaker payer has four early and
eight total. A lawful one-unit advance received by the early deadline
and reconciled against the later settlement restores the reference timing
without raising the total. This comparison assumes an independent
temporary site, route and roster with availability lasting until the
decision. Changing those assumptions can change the answer even when
cash is unchanged.

Test initial delivery and continuous receiving separately. At hour 20,
secure the funded extension through hour 36 if the return cannot be
verified by hour 24. A repair at hour 26 and verified handback at hour
28 work through the extension. Without it, record the original hour 24
gap through actual restoration and preserve remedy. Keep the case open
if a provider's claimed completion conflicts with resident experience or
critical facts remain unknown. The exercise uses a single shift need at
the start, an accepted care plan through any extension, and a specific
confirmation of resumed care at handback. It is neither a national
cash estimate nor a care or temperature standard.
