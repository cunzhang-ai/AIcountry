# FB48–FB54. Decide expiring support before its costs reach households

*Design393 · Proposed ordinary budget and service duties within [fiscal continuity](../62-lasting-services-and-fiscal-shocks.md).*

**FB48 — Record what ends and what remains due.** Treasury and each
competent service and revenue owner give the Assembly one dated account
of material expiries. Include temporary benefits, contracts, capital
support, funding streams, tax changes and conditional tranches with a
common material effect. Identify the legal end, last eligible period,
notice date, last usable payment, source restrictions, operator and
continuing contract, remedy or personal claim. A funding line, a power,
an individual's entitlement and actual service capacity can end on
different dates. Preserve independently due rights and appeals. A
public forecast neither renews a measure nor changes the source law.

**FB49 — Publish distinct baselines on the same dates.** Independent
budget analysts show the enacted expiry path and a specified continuing
policy path with prices, populations and institutional scope aligned.
Show the whole fiscal and operating effects of continuing or replacing
the support, including related tax expiry, delayed collections, local
cost transfers, arrears, workers and paid remedy. An assumed extension
stays marked as an assumption. An appropriation that ends releases only
cash and capacity actually available for another lawful use. Show
recurring costs and the finite settlement and transition schedule under
FB and DB. Opposition and affected people can challenge the evidence in
useful time. The analysts may expose an unknown without acquiring a veto.

**FB50 — Trace simultaneous effects on people and providers.** Compare
the same households, providers and places in the same windows. Check
overlap across schemes and distinguish people, entitlements and actual
resources. Show the burden of linked losses for people unable to switch
provider or pay in advance, and where an apparent saving shifts cost
onto local budgets, patients or unpaid carers. Preserve service and
personal privacy through limited lawful joining, independent safe
samples and accessible assisted reporting. Check actual operating
capacity and uncovered needs separately. A smaller caseload or a lower
average payment cannot prove that the standing floor is usable.

**FB51 — Put exact alternatives to elected decision in useful time.**
The Clerk dockets a material known expiry by 90 calendar days before
its first affected window or by the earlier last safe decision date
if funding, notice or service preparation needs it. A later discovered
expiry receives the next lawful protected decision opportunity with
the lost time and effects recorded. Compare expiry with verified
ordinary receiving, exact renewal and a funded staged or permanent
replacement where feasible. Show distribution, foregone uses, gross
settlement and next-window capacity under weaker assumptions. Use
ordinary amendment, minority access and exact votes. Record approval,
rejection or the absence of a timely disposition. Silence creates no
authority to extend, cut a standing duty or suspend a vote.

**FB52 — Separate a funded bridge from a permanent offer.** The Assembly
may authorize a bounded transition on specified eligibility, duration,
source, amount and actual receiving. A recurring offer needs a verified
recurring net source and accepted delivery. Apply existing independent
release, appropriation and treasury controls. A later tax receipt cannot
settle an earlier bill. Do not pledge already restricted funds or assume
that a temporary transfer, hoped-for savings or unreleased crew can pay
the next period. Price usable alternatives before binding them and
retain earlier valid commitments. A bridge neither renews the old
programme by default nor delegates authority to change protected floors.

**FB53 — Protect accrued rights and timely replacement.** The actual
service or revenue owner gives usable direct notice normally at least
30 calendar days before a material change or promptly where earlier
notice is impossible. State the last eligible period, payable arrears,
applicable continuing rights, qualified alternative and independent
review. Carry existing valid claims, staffing, records and all relevant
original clocks into the successor. Verify delivery before relying on
an alternate. If it fails, use any prior lawful funded protection and
refer the remaining choice. Record unmet needs and unpaid duties
separately, including where the temporary lawfully expired measure
cannot be extended. A budget vote does not close a pending case.

**FB54 — Report what changed and keep the comparison reproducible.**
After the first affected window and again when recurring funding comes
due, reconcile vote, source, actual receipt, uncovered people and
remaining liabilities. Identify capacity lost or redeployed and direct
unresolved cases to named lawful receivers. AI may reconcile dated
accounts, identify possible overlaps and replay transparent assumptions.
It cannot assign rights, start a tax, choose whose service ends or
certify a handoff. Enact prospectively with named sources and protected
review. Reopen on earlier or jointly material expiry, omitted groups,
late collection, unavailable receiving or a better qualified alternative.
