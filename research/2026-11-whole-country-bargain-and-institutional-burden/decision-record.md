## Models, constraints and substantive choice

Compare strong retain/repair, current service-first plural, territorial direct-
public services, rights-preserving U+C2 and decentralized mutual/common provision.
All carry fourteen chapters,37 original functions/later duties, protected rights,
old claims and actual independent political/legal control. They differ in needs
assessment, ownership, provider dependence, local knowledge and management.
Their legal/material specification is incomplete, not a reason to declare rivals
inferior. Technical/AI assistance is a method available to each; AI-exclusive
sovereignty cannot meet the explicit democratic objective.

Retain current parliamentary/rights/service-first/plural/public-responsibility
country and real rivals. Revise the response to a detected failure: a consequential
new compulsory step must identify lawful authority, purpose, simpler/repair
alternatives, burden/control and paid continuity through existing public-law
routes. Prefer a workable simpler route where rights are protected. No new
simplification office or preliminary approval; individual case proof does not
require a national study. Necessary independent judgment/warrants/reply/consent
retain actual law. Elected bodies may choose new institutions with reasons and
proper resources; no expert/model veto or guaranteed optimization is created.

CS17 makes actual paid retained human/other-provider capacity explicit; expected
digital savings cannot be its sole source. A vendor update cannot authorize
workforce/rights changes. This is a proposed future-country refinement, not a
new common research procedure or enactment approving itself.

## Evidence, uncertainty, values and dissent

[Current publisher summaries](sources.md) qualify access and assumed redeployment,
not a national outcome or whole-regime winner. Different publisher reports can
share observations. Prior study findings retain historical scope; no complete
source/cost or empirical advantage is inferred from dated country examples.

The preference prioritizes actual needs, plural exit, public responsibility and
revocable public power under uncertainty. Means assessment can intrude/exclude;
U can protect independence even without immediate poverty. Contracting can
fragment accountability; direct public provision can dominate; mutual life can
support knowledge or constrain dissent. Fewer steps can make proof easy or remove
protection. No score resolves these value conflicts. Removing a needless form
and removing independent adjudication are different choices.

Material objection: CS16 can itself produce paperwork or delay. Limit it to
consequential generally binding burdens and use existing explanation/scrutiny;
no separate gate or per-request dossier. Record uncertainty and proportionate
reasons, not universal scientific proof. More direct services or supported
automatic enrolment may displace complex access where actual law/privacy permit.
No compulsory identity graph or unconsented household disclosure follows.
