# Design437. Verify what a transfer delivered

Design436 is the pinned predecessor. T61–T66 reconcile the two
payer accounts. T67–T72 specify the evidence from scheduled
settlement to the person or service, including netted transfers
and loss of useful time. The revision used AI assistance and
parallel research.

Use the Copenhagen midyear adjustment for a bounded comparison.
The [source record](sources.md) establishes the local decision,
the legally scheduled mechanism, and which budget values can
be arithmetically reconciled. Actual bank transactions, usable
cash, payroll and recipient outcomes remain unverified.
There is no independent qualified review, affected-party
participation or legal adoption of the candidate.

Periodic audit alone is lighter and remains suitable for lower-risk
settlements where there is time for ordinary correction. Targeted
checks catch a payment deadline or missing service earlier. Tracing
every underlying transaction and resident can increase both privacy
risk and administrative burden. Select targeted tracing on material
programme dependencies, with batched evidence when the actual use
and challenge remain visible. Keep a funded alternate or advance
available only within competent law and appropriation. Independent
review can challenge the sender and receiver without gaining
authority to direct ordinary local services.

Use existing elected appropriation, public payment and audit
authorities. Preserve continuing duties, exact legal dates, loss
responsibility and protected claims on migration. Compare one real
material adjustment using the original payment advice, matching
restricted ledger extracts and the receiving service's payroll,
invoice or service evidence as applicable. Check the next payment
window and provide direct challenge. Reopen on a genuine missed
duty, unreliable records or a simpler adequate proof path.
