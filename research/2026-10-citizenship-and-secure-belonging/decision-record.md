## Diagnosis, values and alternatives

A settled-resident vote is weak if government or an employer can make the voter
removable, withhold their sole identity credential or condition care on status.
Birth/origin gaps also create inherited exclusion. Stable shared home, equal
political standing, child agency, liberty and actual receiving are values;
residence thresholds and institution design are fallible means. Material supply,
work/property control, social prejudice and foreign law may defeat formal rules.
The [rival comparison](../settlement/belonging/02-rivals-and-reopening.md) retains
descent/citizen-only, guaranteed naturalization with citizen-only voting,
immediate/cumulative residence, renewable permits and narrow fraud-revocation
models. They offer real continuity, entry-control or correction benefits with
exclusion/retaliation costs. No aggregate score or AI agreement settles this choice.

## Selected complete conditional reference

[MB01–MB20](../country/C02/06-citizenship-and-secure-belonging.md) selects citizenship
for every human child born alive here, regardless of parent status/method, and
actual national vessel/aircraft births. Foundlings have a presumption with stable
recognized citizenship. Overseas legal-parent descent requires twelve cumulative
prior habitual months, waived for otherwise-stateless children; deceased parent
status at death can qualify. Donors/genetics/support contracts do not themselves
create parentage. Later proof of a birth parent differs from a later adoption.

Later qualifying parentage/adoption or parental naturalization supplies a minor's
immediate protected application; acquisition takes effect at actual conferral,
not a fictitious birth. Sixteen-year-olds apply with their own supported assent;
younger children have a carer/independent-child route. Otherwise-stateless children
habitually here acquire immediately on reliable recognition without parental veto.
Actual location governs future birth methods; unsupported meanings receive a
determination route, not erasure. RP's safety/consent holds stay separate.

After four continuous habitual years, secure residence vests, including asylum,
irregular/disputed status and childhood. Temporary/coerced absence is protected;
a genuine pre-vesting break starts a new period. Cumulative time is a real rival.
Vested secure home/work/return survives job/divorce/crime/card expiry/absence.
Adult naturalization is free, voluntary and entitlement-based at the same point,
including later application abroad; no income, language, loyalty, health or
good-character gate. Ordinary criminal accountability still applies, not banishment.

Existing adult resident national vote/candidacy remains independent of citizenship.
Local eligibility now has six national habitual months and actual municipal home;
a genuine later move changes constituency without another waiting period.
Citizen diaspora voting remains different from noncitizen current-resident voting;
this asymmetry and four-year exclusion are retained costs. Founder/electorate
legitimacy cannot be manufactured by these proposed rules or a database.

No involuntary citizenship deprivation, including an applicant's own fraud after
a genuine official grant/recognition. Protect valid automatic acquisition too.
An office's competence concerns its kind of authority, not perfect eligibility;
prior audit/procedural error cannot retroactively cancel an actual operative act.
Counterfeit credentials or use of a victim's record without acquisition/grant
are different. A civil court, not executive label, resolves identity with clear
and convincing state proof to displace established status. Missing archives or
biometric failure alone is insufficient. Separate B/P/K accountability needs
actual proof; no new offense is silently invented.

Irreversible corrupt formal grants can change the electorate. Closed entitlements,
no investment/honorary waiver, separate assessment/conferral, source audit before
effect and criminal accountability reduce, not eliminate, that risk. Narrow
judicial fraud revocation without statelessness remains an expressly rejected-
default rival. No second-class naturalized franchise secretly cures the tradeoff.

Only an adult personally/informedly renounces, with actual other nationality,
thirty-day withdrawal and no tax/debt/military clearance. A foreign promise is
insufficient; conditional cooperation may fail against incompatible foreign law.
Wrongful/coerced loss has correction; any voluntary former citizen can recover
on supported identity/application without a new residence/wealth test. Previous
obligations and the child's independent status continue.

Stateless determination uses relevant-country links and shared reasonable proof,
not impossible worldwide certificates or unsafe consular contact. Recognition
immediately gives secure residence and a two-habitual-year citizenship application
route; later foreign nationality does not cancel vested status. Factual nationality,
treaty-benefit exclusions and non-return risk are distinct. No safe accepting
destination means no removal; protected stay/work continues with reasoned review.

Assembly ordinary-admission categories/places expose actual funded capacity and
alternatives; return/family/protection have separate rights routes. No new annual
schedule supplies no new discretionary places outside still-valid authority;
that restrictive default has a migration cost. In-country sponsor change needs
no new entry place. Leaving a job/partner gives an independent180-day bridge and
pending review protection, actual payment/housing advice and no abuse-report gate.

Removal before secure status needs actual individual grounds, thirty-day filing/
appeal opportunities and suspensive effective review, accepting safe destination,
child/family assessment and execute-time facts. No routine migration prison.
Prior-judge adult execution restraint is at most six continuous/twelve rolling
thirty-day hours through actual national/carrier control. Long routes may be
unavailable; no unsafe abandonment, paper handoff or silent midflight extension.
P/G/CD/B separate transfer grounds/clocks remain; no child execution custody.
