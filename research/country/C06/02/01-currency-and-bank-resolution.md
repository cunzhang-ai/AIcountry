# C06. Money, credit and payments: Currency, public liquidity and bank resolution

*Part of [C06. Money, credit and payments](../02-money-credit-and-payments.md); integrated design 104 research, not enacted law.*

## Money, public liquidity and bank resolution

The **conditional domestic-currency package** is the working monetary reference
for a future state able to establish it lawfully. It is selected to test whether
democratic fiscal choice can coexist with reliable local payments, a bounded
monetary authority and honest external constraints; it is not proof that issuing
money improves welfare or makes any budget affordable. The earlier
[external-currency package](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-operating-settlement/economic-control.md)
remains a coherent alternative and stress branch, especially where founding
capacity or public confidence in a new unit is absent. For the first budget,
prefer continuation of a lawful predecessor package where it can operate.
Compare it with an expressly adopted external arrangement and a bounded
own-currency start. An elected motion for a different regime must specify
currency law, obligations, eligible settlement assets and operators,
convertibility terms, continuing sources, public risk, supervision,
resolution and independent enforceable remedies. Give opposition usable
alternatives, price paid overlap and set an elected review deadline for
any concurrent regimes. Where predecessor authority or acceptance is
missing, name uncovered lines and the competent decision. Research neither assigns a currency nor carries over
an asset or institutional membership.

Under the domestic reference, the Assembly creates the unit of account and legal tender,
enacts the monetary authority's joint price-stability and voluntary-employment mandate,
sets its powers and risk limits, and retains tax, spending, debt and guarantee
decisions. The authority operates settlement and monetary instruments within that
law without case-by-case cabinet orders. [MC01–MC32](../41-money-and-financial-public-control.md) specifies five fixed
five-year seats/three actual votes, open Clerk recruitment and full-N three-
fifths appointments, finite reserves and independent ordinary-court removal.
Audited accounts, decisions, votes, risk exposures and transactions with Treasury
are published with narrowly justified delays. The Assembly may change the
general mandate prospectively through law but cannot order an undisclosed loan
to allies or treat operational independence as a veto over elected budgets.

Treasury has no automatic overdraft or unlimited direct monetary financing.
Any temporary central-bank credit to government needs a prior statute specifying
purpose, ceiling, maturity, pricing, loss bearer and Assembly reporting; a new
facility or extension needs another public vote. The monetary authority may
provide time-limited, collateralized liquidity to banks meeting statutory
solvency criteria, with published eligibility and an exposure ceiling. A bank
whose solvency is not established cannot receive that facility pending prompt
assessment; a bank found nonviable enters the separate resolution route. A
central-bank loan cannot disguise a grant to shareholders or a taxpayer bailout.
Fiscal indemnity or recapitalization beyond the enacted ceiling needs Assembly
authorization. These are proposed boundaries, not assurances against inflation,
political pressure, capture or a mistaken solvency assessment.

[M01–M16](../24-financial-law-and-loss-allocation.md) supplies a proposed defined
coverage, loss order, monetary mandate and public-risk package. Its new duties
are unadopted and unpriced; no old financial reference is thereby compliant.
The Assembly must enact tax-payment finality, deposit coverage, bank-loss order,
a bounded resolution and insurance fund, Treasury cash custody, payment continuity
and the maximum public risk *before* a failure. The revenue authority assesses
and collects under reviewable law and records when
a taxpayer's obligation is discharged; a failed intermediary cannot make the
state collect a legally settled tax twice. Treasury distinguishes assessments,
bank claims and usable received cash. A prudential supervisor detects a bank's
nonviability under statutory criteria; a separate resolution authority gives
written, reviewable reasons and executes the enacted transfer, bridge or wind-down.
Owners and creditors bear losses in the lawful order; covered depositors receive
only the protection actually authorized and credibly financed. Payment operators
maintain tested alternate and assisted routes for essential transactions.
Supervisors, receivers, insurers, monetary officials and vendors cannot create
a new public guarantee or tax. The [monetary-architecture decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-monetary-architecture/decision-record.md)
compares the two complete packages and identifies their migration conditions.
The [debt-distress route](../06-sovereign-debt-and-distress.md) counts any
sovereign-debt loss transmitted through banks, pensions or central-bank collateral
as part of an elected fiscal package rather than a hidden monetary rescue.

[SF01–SF10](../25-source-finance-and-payment-continuity.md) now selects a
conditional safeguarded public-payment alternative with genuine bank equity
conversion and bounded source terms. A monetary purchase is distinct from
Treasury spending; no particular trade or bank subscription is authorized here.

[BF/RA/BR](../39-bank-failure-and-protected-payment.md) now separates M07 insured unavailability from M09 critical-function entry and fixes the panel/career/court/source/execution chain. Neither a refused resolution nor a bank/public source shortage cancels due insurance or creates a new monetary/Treasury mandate.

MC07's expressly enacted own-rate/transaction exception is narrower than
banking rulemaking; MC17's finite continuing charter has annual audit/hearings,
not automatic annual expiry. MC21–26 supplies actual bank-case/settlement owners,
gross commitment/solvency limits and honest losses/remittance; no negative-equity
fiction grants Treasury spending or cancels existing liability payments.

[SF11–SF17](../25/06-cash-access-and-essential-payment-continuity.md)
extends the proposed domestic tender reference to ordinary essential
retail acceptance and requires a funded non-digital route through
covered payment failure.

## Carry the opening budget in its executable currencies

Use [TPE01–TPE06](../60/11-public-cash-and-independent-execution.md) for each payment class.
The competent elected authorities specify the currency for each eligible
class, the legal discharge event, the payer, the dated usable source,
recipient acceptance and a funded alternate. Separate the monetary
instrument and the appropriation. The competent operators establish
readiness on independently reviewable evidence, including same-currency
settlement, qualified backups, financial loss allocation and access
after bank failure. Under expressly authorized conditions, hold only
unbound new commitments that rely on a failed step. Keep binding
claims and their original denomination, date and remedy. Actual access
and finality need separate findings. Apply [SF05](../25/03-safe-continuity-and-funding.md)
to verified unpaid public obligations. Deposit insurance, where applicable,
uses its own original trigger, covered beneficiary, funding and payout
denomination. It does not insure Treasury payroll cash or redenominate
the underlying bank debt. Use the [essential payment route](../25/06-cash-access-and-essential-payment-continuity.md)
for independently due food, utilities or other protected essentials.
Carry foreign debts and essential imports on their own usable dated
foreign sources under applicable law.
Do not use the same reserve for domestic continuity and foreign supply.
