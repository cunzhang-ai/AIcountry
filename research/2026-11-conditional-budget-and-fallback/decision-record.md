# Design309 decision on conditional commencement and budget fallback

## Baseline and request

Pin Design308, BCV01–BCV15, DB06–DB10, BLC and T13–T17. Apply the
existing research method. The project assistant proposes a prospective
rule for an optional schedule that needs a separate competent decision.
Legitimate elected institutions and applicable higher law control adoption.

## Situation and competing choices

Design308 protects order and enacted preference but the sequence could
close on a national yes even where the optional schedule cannot take
effect without an exact local act. The resident sees the lost service
and the opposition loses a still viable choice. Pre-clear other approvals
where useful and lawful. A single omnibus vote would obscure a genuine
local veto. An automatic substitute would remove elected reconsideration
after new information. A finite reservation with a protected fallback
puts known costs and deadline risk before the first vote. The authored
case tests that mechanism. The ACT drafting source supports the narrower
possibility of linking legal commencement to another provision.

## Selection, objections and continuing duties

Select BCV16–BCV20 for scope-specific conditional schedules. Set the
decision deadline and reserve time for a separate fallback vote. Cover
the direct waiting cost and verify what funds and care staff are still
available. A local no remains fully effective. Withheld verification,
no quorum, an unaffordable fallback or missed useful time can still
lose the opportunity. A late failure after commencement preserves
earlier binding claims. Do not attach unrelated law or repeat the same
unexpired decision by renaming it. The design does not adopt a new
constitutional or project procedure. A competent prospective rule and
lawful paid handover are required. Keep prior votes, elected terms, tax
effects, accrued service rights, remedies, records and protected sources
under their applicable law.

## Remaining decision

Check a paid calendar with two independent elected bodies and one
actual locally controlled budget. Ask whether the fallback vote and
receiving can occur after disagreement without postponing a protected
hearing or blocking viable prior approvals. Reopen on actual deadline
loss, failed reserve, duplicate legal authority, a superior simpler
choice or previously excluded affected perspectives. The worked
comparison establishes distinct outcomes under authored assumptions.
Actual affected participation, independent review, authorization, paid
readiness and observed improvements are open.
