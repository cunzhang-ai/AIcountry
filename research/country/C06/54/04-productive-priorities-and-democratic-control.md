# NS13–NS16: the productive program serves capabilities

**NS13 — Select five connected priorities, not a national catalog of private jobs.**

| Priority | Country-design use / what is still required |
|---|---|
| Safe essential inputs | Clinical supplies, ordinary food/water/power and sanitation need qualified products, recurring sources, stock/lead times, diverse routes and paid receiving; do not assume local manufacture is always safest. The six clinical budget rows are not a medicines strategy. |
| Homes and useful infrastructure | Repair and safe access before optional expansion; separate tenure/title, materials, capital/working cash, grid/transport reliability and paid temporary receiving. Retained property is not free of old claims. |
| Human capability and time | Qualified care, learning, courts, public administration and independent evidence with paid instruction/cover; protect rest, voluntary relationships and unpaid care without drafting them into spare labor. |
| Diverse ordinary and traded production | Fair entry/credit access, safe work, competition, cooperative/private/public choices and actual buyer/foreign acceptance; no compulsory sector, IP-rent assumption or export-employment quota. |
| Replaceable technical capability | Interoperable secure operations, accessible offline continuity, lawful knowledge access and plural research/AI with observed net gains; do not found the country on an unbuilt model or single vendor. |

**NS14 — Use existing accountable investment and buying institutions.** Cabinet
and sector/local executives propose the mix and sources; Assembly/elected
councils choose within competence. IC limits gross cash/commitment/loan/equity/
guarantee risk; SC/OE/WL/MP govern buying, control, work and competition. Existing
qualified career officers administer, independent staff verify actual receipt,
Treasury pays lawful dues, AA audits, and courts/executors correct wrongs. EV
publishes answerable evidence without a policy or truth veto. No new industrial
sovereign, compulsory planner, target-volume bonus or hidden evergreen fund.
Market failure can justify paid public capacity but does not prove its efficiency;
public failure can justify alternatives but does not cancel due universal access.

**NS15 — Distribute useful gains without presuming them.** Compare who gets
security, disposable resources after actual housing/work/care cost, time, work
power and political voice. C2 reduces one stacked tax burden but needs more
source and favors higher receipts; I05 can stigmatize/nonreach; full U can
compete with services. SG136 now prefers continuing individual I05/SD05 plus
services/earned rights. Retain rights-preserving U+C2, C1/C0 and paid provider/
ownership rivals, not the superseded assumed U destination. No national growth
rate or investment return decides that value conflict. AI gains require net
output/quality/time evidence including oversight/error/security/energy costs;
choose lower cost, more service, wages, rest or shared returns through lawful
bargaining and elected source decisions, not a tax on imaginary automation.

**NS16 — Handle demand weakness and bottlenecks differently.** An order shortage,
unaffordable access, scarce nurse/part, monopoly price, foreign refusal and failed
bank are not one financial problem. Use ordinary budget/social protection for
lawfully chosen stabilization; qualified learning/repair/input investment for
capacity; MP for proved market power; MC/BF for their actual financial mandates;
and NP/CP for lawful scarcity/protection. Some diagnoses remain disputed. General
issue cannot recruit a qualified worker or settle a value dispute; tightening
cash can worsen access without curing a physical bottleneck. No automatic model-
triggered tax/cut/price control, coercive rationing or monetary back door is added.

[NR finite-resource regime](../../C09/15-finite-resources-and-mineral-rights.md)
now supplies the actual public-right/plural grant/no-extraction/closure/finite-
receipt choices missing behind NS02's lawful-return exception. This adds no
assumed deposit, compulsory mining/AI output, generic royalty tax or funding
of the service-first country. Original private/common/retain-repair rivals persist.

[EB portable buffer](../../C05/16-portable-earnings-and-work-transition.md)
now makes one positive person-level earnings-transition choice beside existing
I05/Y/RF/MC shock protection. It is no employer viability certificate, job/
training guarantee, whole demand/inflation policy or proven employment gain;
actual service/input/work/source bottlenecks still require their own response.

[MS01–MS16 stabilization](../61-employment-prices-and-household-security.md)
now selects a joint monetary price/voluntary-employment objective, person-side
automatic protection and separately elected demand/capacity/source responses.
It fills that national priority choice, not a guaranteed job, calibrated macro
model, additional instrument or funded countercyclical programme. NS16's
diagnosis and real-input distinctions remain; neither cash nor a mandate supplies
scarce work, imports or universal compensation for a real supply loss.
