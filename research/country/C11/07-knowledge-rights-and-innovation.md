# C11. Knowledge rights, paid discovery and practical use

1 November 2026 · Design 95 · KR01–KR32 research selection, not enacted law

People may learn, criticize, create, repair and develop competing ideas without
a government innovation score. Select **limited private/collective rights plus
paid public access and plural research**, rather than a state knowledge monopoly
or a promise that free files pay for discovery and deliver essential services.

Patent 20 years and copyright life-plus-50 are treaty-aware reference choices,
not proven optimum incentives or proof of actual treaty compliance. Fully paid
nonsensitive technical results default to defined reusable open permissions;
creative commissions purchase an agreed paid scope. Commercial copying for AI
training needs actual rights or a valid specific exception; autonomous output
creates no automatic new exclusive title or political mandate.

A free licence, an injunction or an escrow is not a qualified factory, safe
medicine, maintained software or received care. [Authored cases](../../settlement/knowledge-rights/README.md)
expose payment, foreign-input and replacement-capacity gaps. Inherited rights,
privacy, worker choice, safety and actual external obligations survive migration.
Actual authority, sources, qualified participation/operation and better lives
remain unproved; a stronger open/public or private alternative can displace this.
