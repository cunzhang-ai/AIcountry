# XR16–XR20. Give portable essential support a recurring payer

*Design520 · Proposed domestic continuity law*

**XR16 — Put a defined portable minimum into ordinary supply.** Begin with
existing essential home support that can safely be provided at an actual
destination, including repeat stays without changing ordinary residence.
The responsible Assembly and local councils compare strong repair of
ordinary reimbursement, a fully funded interlocal compact and separate
national funding for the verified residual. The latter is selected where
ordinary agreements cannot reliably cover qualified host costs or where a
qualifying person has no lawful origin payer. Before commencement, enacted
sector law specifies covered service and eligible intervals, responsible
offices, recurring receipts, appropriated host budgets, limits, challenge and
the next receiving window. Expand to another sector only by its own exact
decision. Cash benefits and pensions retain their existing award, source and
payment dates. School placement, treatment, tenancy and safe contact retain
their separate rights and decision-makers.

**XR17 — Pay readiness, delivery and exceptional costs through distinct
lines.** The origin funds its lawful contribution for the covered episode
once. The host or authorized compact receives eligible dated funds for its
accepted qualified capacity, including safe travel, local access and necessary
qualified overlap. Deduct the portion already financed for that same capacity
under ordinary equalization or another commitment before allocating new
funds. Include reliably predictable volume in ordinary equalization or the
standing compact where those sources can meet the actual cutoffs. Fund the
verified incremental shared block under a recurring national sector
appropriation if ordinary sources cannot cover it in time. Use the
existing Treasury and sector authorities. Where several origins share one
block, price verified availability once for the eligible catchment. Set an
ex ante cost cap with audited access, workforce and site assumptions. Pay
actual eligible use on its own terms. Reconcile returned, idle, irrecoverably
committed and reused capacity separately. The host may choose any qualified
lawful provider. No charge requires a person to advance public funds
or accept unpaid family work.

**XR18 — Name the first payer and settle the final bill later.** Use the
authorized ordinary payer wherever its funds and delivery reach in time.
For a defined due service with no reachable payer, use the already enacted
national sector appropriation and the separate statutory Treasury payer to
settle eligible action at the accepting host or provider before the last
useful cutoff. A disputed ordinary contribution can be advanced under XR06
within its actual cap. Recover once from a public body only where lawful
liability for that component is established. Keep unrelated grants payable
under their own terms. If review confirms no liable
origin, the named national appropriation bears the verified residual as a
final expense within its enacted coverage and limit. Update the next budget
for unresolved balances and verified final charges. Review keeps lawful local
duties and receipts distinct from the temporary payer. An undelivered act
or a proposal to replenish funds cannot be booked as capacity or cash. Use
AP13–AP18 where a responsibility or merits dispute remains.

**XR19 — Protect ordinary local needs and the next visit.** Check the same
home and host households, already due services, qualified shifts, adapted
premises, wages, travel and independent backups under ordinary and simultaneous
loss. In a common shortage, uphold existing binding reservations and use the
applicable urgency and MF27 rules for available alternatives. Neither local
address nor expected public reimbursement changes clinical need. Pay only
for a usable qualified package or lawful useful partial relief. Record any
uncovered service and keep its deadline and independent remedy. An interim
budget expiry or a fresh arrival cannot renew a standing fiscal bridge.
Repeated inability to deliver requires a recurring budget correction or a
qualified shared sector route, with existing duties and lawful local choices
preserved.

**XR20 — Verify receipt and keep the person in control.** Preserve first
contact, an established award and the direct review route. Confirm each
accepting service and any change of provider with the applicable consent.
Understand safe local conditions before counting equivalent care. Record an
accepted booking, delivered service and later payer settlement separately.
Use a scoped service reference and validate the same cost once. Keep personal
addresses and protected evidence with their proper custodian, and offer direct
assisted or offline contact. AI can propose feasible schedules, flag duplicate
claims and reveal exposure in either region. Qualified people determine safety,
entitlement and payment, and confirm actual receipt through the next call.
Reopen on residence coercion, origin cost dumping, unfunded host duties,
excluded residents, lower-cost reliable repair or a better shared route.
