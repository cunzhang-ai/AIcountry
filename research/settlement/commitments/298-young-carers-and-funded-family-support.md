# Design385 commitment. Pay for replacement care and lost learning

[YCR01–YCR07](../../country/C07/07/01-young-carers-and-funded-family-support.md)
requires separate sources, accepting providers and remedy for the
care recipient and the child. Count each shift once. Preserve
choice, original dates, care duties and support across adulthood.
Never count an under-18 helper as guaranteed delivery.
