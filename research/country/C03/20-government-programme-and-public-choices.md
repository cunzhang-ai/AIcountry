# From an electoral choice to a government programme the public can test

Design207 · GP01–GP08 · Proposed country rules

**Select optional independent campaign costing and a public governing
programme that can change through parliamentary choice.** People can see
what a governing group retained, changed, deferred or abandoned, who pays
and which services can actually start. Election, investiture, expenditure
authority, continuing rights and actual results remain distinct.

| Part | Rule |
|---|---|
| [GP01–GP04. Estimates, formation and the first programme](20/01-estimates-formation-and-programme.md) | Fair support before polling, disclosed support terms and a matched programme after appointment |
| [GP05–GP08. Change, challenge and continuing authority](20/02-change-challenge-and-continuity.md) | Votes on actual measures, independent challenge, protected continuity and prospective adoption |

Use the existing [formation and caretaker rules](06-government-formation-and-caretaker.md),
[budget and amendment rules](../C06/60-democratic-budget-and-execution.md),
and [actual handover](19-lawful-handover-and-actual-control.md). No new office,
certification veto or automatic confidence vote is selected. The [comparisons
and worked first budget](../../settlement/governing-programme/README.md) show
where comparable analysis ends and public choice begins.

[FB13–FB20](../C06/62-lasting-services-and-fiscal-shocks.md) applies GP to
the first country budget with separate voted and continuation baselines,
funded starts and protected continuing duties. GP votes and deadlines remain.

[T13–T17](../C14/06/04-linked-choices-and-lawful-start.md) puts the
effective texts, money, continuing rights and independent protection
on the same first-programme schedule after separate lawful decisions.

[HT13–HT17](19/04-caretaker-commitments-and-public-choice.md)
carries enforceable predecessor commitments and the price of lawful
exit into each exact successor programme and first budget.

The [matched first government programme](../C14/06/05-integrated-first-government-programme.md)
uses exact narrower alternatives when local acceptance, dated
cash or common independent capacity changes.

For eligible competing whole budgets, [BCV11–BCV15](../C06/60/08-majority-comparison-and-final-budget.md)
orders the first vote by explicit full-seat preference over each rival
where demonstrated. Final budget votes, source laws and confidence
remain separate.

[PCO11–PCO16](../C14/04/05-review-major-laws-in-operation.md) assigns major
primary measures a protected first review date and elected disposition.
Carry open reviews, evidence and continuing obligations into successor
programmes alongside the ordinary GP and handover rules.
