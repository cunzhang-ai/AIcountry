# Belonging: transition, rivals and reopening

*Canonical MB18–MB20; part of [C02/06](../06-citizenship-and-secure-belonging.md).*

**MB18 — Count actual receiving and public control.** Nationality, residence,
protection and electoral officers have lawful merit appointment, fair discipline,
separate source access and independent review. The Assembly controls exact scope
and appropriations; officers cannot change entitlements by scoring applicants.
Status clerks do not become a new sovereign branch. Ordinary C04 career and CJ/C08
judicial protections apply with their own lawful funding and conflict rules.

[C06/30](../../C06/30-membership-and-reception-capacity.md) attributes specialized
casework, public advice, interpreters, ordinary judges, safe local places, clinical/
learning/child needs, payment, actual travel and received correction once. National
admission cannot hand an unfunded duty to a local council or assume a new citizen
creates a home. Existing restricted sources and the single SF old-scope standby
retain their purposes; new adopted dues need recomputation and actual source.

**MB19 — Prior law, exact dates and continuing people.** This is a research
selection, not actual nationality, sovereign recognition or treaty membership.
A legitimate affected public and competent existing procedure must choose the
founding membership and exact prospective law. The newly proposed electorate
cannot approve its own founding; neither authors nor models certify a population.
Actual source nationality, immigration, election, parentage, privacy and treaty
law continue until authorized compatible migration. C14 structural-effect and
anti-self-dealing rules remain; its rights-restoring exception is still pending.

The enacted schedule maps existing citizenship/residence, prior habitual time,
birth/parentage, applications, refusals, wrongful loss, cards, pending claims/
removal orders, wages/benefits, staff, records and contracts to lawful successors.
Preserve existing/accrued rights and count proven prior residence rather than
reset everyone to zero. MB02 automatic birth applies to births from the exact
lawful commencement. Minors then habitually resident here who were born in the
territory acquire citizenship at commencement, not a fabricated earlier birth
date. The exact adopted cohort receives protected individual notice and proof;
no past election is recomputed. Other earlier people keep established nationality
and direct recognition/restoration, otherwise-stateless-child and four-year routes.
Any wider retroactive grant needs explicit public choice, not a hidden sweep.
No transfer erases a child or overdue claim, restarts custody or fabricates consent.

**MB20 — Why choose this and when it can change.** Equal civic standing and
stable actual home support territorial birth, voluntary naturalization, a resident
vote independent of citizenship and no involuntary banishment. These are value
choices; four years, twelve parental months and processing/execution ceilings
are revisable normative parameters, not observed best values or proof of success.

Retain citizenship-only/descent-oriented membership, narrow judicial fraud
revocation without statelessness, and permit-renewal residence as genuine rivals.
They may protect inherited bond, entry control or correction of corrupt grants;
they risk permanent outsider status, administrative electorate control and unequal
naturalized citizenship. The selected package can induce strategic residence,
birth travel, fiscal pressure and irreversible corrupt conferral; disclosure,
closed entitlements and actual resources mitigate without disproving those costs.

Reopen on exclusion, coercion, unsafe return, corrupt grants, loss of public
control, impossible reception or a better complete alternative. Independent
qualified review and actual affected participation remain absent. Design82 UA now selects necessary agency; next rights-restoring amendment/consultation closure
and the joined material/transition comparison. Do not automatically add another
citizenship sub-study or treat unknown legal adoption as an undefined design choice.
