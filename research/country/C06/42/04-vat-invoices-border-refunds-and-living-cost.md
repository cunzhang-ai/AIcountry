# Recurring taxes: input credit, borders, refunds and actual living costs

*NF13–NF16; part of [C06/42](../42-recurring-tax-and-noncash-work.md).*

**NF13 — Matched invoices and paid consideration.** Output VAT arises when
actual consideration is received, including an identified future-supply advance;
an unpaid invoice alone is not Treasury cash. A registered buyer credits VAT
on a genuine invoice when actually paid for taxable/zero-rated business use.
No exempt/private-use or duplicate input credit. The buyer retains genuine
paid supplier VAT credit if that supplier defaults; claim the supplier, not a
second buyer payment. Private employee-consumption inputs are not business VAT
credits: delivered NF29 value uses tax-inclusive comparable/private-use cost.
Ordinary paper/accessibly electronic invoices state legal supplier/supply/net
price/rate/VAT/date; consumers get receipts without compulsory identity. A
cancelled advance/returned price uses matching credit note/reversal once.

**NF14 — Destination, not an assumed global collector.** Actual foreign
export delivery/use is zero-rated with evidence; a foreign account with domestic
delivery is not. Customs import VAT uses actual transaction value plus lawful
duty/transport to first domestic delivery, same rate/zero category. Pay at
lawful release; a registered importer credits that actually paid tax once,
not a second border/quarter charge. Name the importer of record: a seller-
importer credits its border payment and charges the later domestic sale once;
a consumer-importer pays border VAT once and that foreign pre-import sale is
not a second domestic output supply. Credit seller-precollected import VAT to that same customs obligation only
on proved lawful collector/customs receipt or authorized accepted-debit TX02
finality; supplier default then retains its actual public receivable, not a
second importer tax. A foreign seller's unsupported VAT invoice is no customs
discharge: preserve the actual import debt and buyer's restitution/urgent-access
route, no second domestic output or fictional Treasury cash.
Imported business services reverse-charge
output and simultaneous eligible input; unregistered/exempt domestic businesses
owe the otherwise applicable import VAT through a limited remittance route,
without an invented full input credit. Private consumers have no new duty to
self-file on foreign digital services: nonresident suppliers above the same2M
domestic-consumption threshold use accessible direct/representative remittance.
Small/noncooperating foreign supplies leave a disclosed enforcement gap, not
consumer guilt or a global platform mandate. Foreign recognition/FX/source
requires real law/acceptance; invoices do not create imports or owned currency.

**NF15 — Quarter and refund are actual dates.** Quarters end31March/30June/
30September/31December. File/pay net positive output less eligible input VAT
30calendar days after quarter-end. Customs payments are credited, not paid
twice. Proved excess input is a statutory refund under TX/RG, paid by the
Treasury career refund payer within10working days of first sufficient return/
held evidence; disputed remainder keeps full merits/direct court and actual
payment after binding correction. No automatic forced carryforward loan to
Treasury or whole-refund freeze from an AI flag. Each quarterly due event
anchors TX assessment/collection/interest; invoice/office changes do not reset it.

**NF16 — Protect tax-inclusive needs without a fictitious rebate.** I02/U/I05
price actual final consumer baskets, including nonrecoverable VAT once. Before
the new tax date, the coordinated Act funds/pays the matching gross-price
ordinary advance and provision for actual extra needs, using the existing
I07/E accepted payer/receiver/urgent court route. Provisional first-period
protection uses the existing priced basket plus its enacted tax effect; later
observed prices correct it without duplicate reimbursement or invented cash.
Pass-through need not equal10%; show both price/margin possibilities and actual
receipts. Zero-core protection remains even if payment fails. A missed person's
advance is an unpaid claim/urgent receiving failure, not authority for a cashier,
minister or AI to suspend nationwide VAT. No new systemic zero-rate fallback
is selected; such a change needs actual elected law. Preserve actual old net/
allowance/essential claims and safe delivery, no lost support to force reform.
Quarterly future tax cannot finance a January advance before actual receipt;
Treasury must supply actual seed/refund/overlap cash. No fiscal readiness or
better-lives claim is made when that source/delivery is absent.

[SD25–SD32/114](../43-country-source-and-delivery.md) now tests paid within-chain credits, physical border tax, simultaneous reverse-charge service entries and zero-export refund cash separately. Gross producer outputVAT plus border tax is not national yield; changed retail supply also requires needs/price/source recalculation, not a blanket10% public-budget levy.
