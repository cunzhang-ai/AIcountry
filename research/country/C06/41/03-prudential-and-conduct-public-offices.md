# Finance: separate supervision and consumer protection with named owners

*MC09–MC12; part of [C06/41](../41-money-and-financial-public-control.md).*

**MC09 — Separate chiefs, exact appointments.** The Prudential Superintendent
and Financial Conduct Commissioner are distinct from the monetary Governor,
competition chief, bank-resolution panel and deposit insurer. Each has one
nonrenewable six-year fixed seat and separately approved consenting same-expiry
reserve. Publicly draw inaugural3/6-year slots before nominations; thereafter
their expiries remain three years apart. Use MC03 Clerk recruitment, relevant
supervisory/conduct competence, full-N three-fifths vote, notice and no acting
reset; MC04 conflicts/for-cause ordinary-court removal apply. One person cannot
occupy two financial sources. Shared Assembly selection can capture all offices;
separate titles are no proof of controller independence.

**MC10 — Careers survive a chief's absence.** Each chief is C04 employer of
their career secretary/deputy, examiners, licensing/assessment/hearing, records
and customer-remedy staff, with open selection, prior conflicts/rotation,
proved discipline/reply and direct courts. Cabinet, monetary Governor and bank
cannot select a case investigator or final appeal reviewer. Preassigned qualified
case officers have their own M/V powers; no head signature gate. An established
absence first uses the actual preapproved reserve within its fixed seat term.
Without either, a predesignated qualified career Deputy has only180calendar
days routine funded management from the original vacancy, then own statutory
duties only. No general policy, senior appointment or stability-council vote
passes to that Deputy. Open original-source recruitment within14days. Express
first-establishment Cabinet/C04 appointment can fill only qualified funded
nonmanager statutory case roles, not an unapproved policy head/reserve.

**MC11 — One bank, distinct lawful findings.** Prudential officers license
M01 deposit-taking and assess M02/M03 capital/liquidity/exposure, source values,
condition and reasoned V/M correction. Conduct officers license actual M01
nonbank lending, brokerage/custody/payment activity and enforce M04–M06 bank/
nonbank terms, client property, payment correction and credit-data access.
Bank license/solvency and customer conduct are different questions; a bank
need not obtain an invented duplicate nonbank license. Ratio breach is not
fraud or automatic nonviability. Monetary credit, insurer M07 and panel M09
entry remain their actual separate duties, sources and clocks.

Officers may require relevant regulated-activity accounts/contracts, commitments,
common-control exposure, funding, collateral and source valuations only under
actual M/V03/PD powers, purpose, confidentiality, retention and privilege. No
home/device/message/foreign-server access by analogy. A source-linked joint
inspection needs both legal bases, not a merged universal dossier. The first
receiver retains triage until a competent receiver accepts; no head or platform
veto on filing. Ordinary courts resolve perimeter and authority conflicts;
no license by silence or unchallengeable bank/source/AI solvency certificate.

**MC12 — Enforcement is not a new punishment power.** Verify a breach, state
particular risk/less restrictive alternative and use V05 correction with B16
reply/reasons. V04 imminent serious-harm case protection retains its actual
scope and independent72-hour hearing; it cannot become a general system rule.
Only scheduled VD duties/actual fault, ability and V06 court process permit
punishment. No new fine/crime by analogy with a council ratio or AI warning;
V08 same-course limits stop duplicate prudential/conduct punishment. K law
needs its own elements/P process. Lawful repayment-risk credit refusal remains;
foreclosure, client restitution and property seizure need their actual B/U/M
route, not a supervisor's private bailiff. Individual users/banks/providers and
qualified representatives retain direct independent challenge and useful-time
protection, without a secret model identifier or futile internal exhaustion.

[NB01–NB36](../44-nonbank-finance-and-client-protection.md) expressly extends these actual career roles: conduct licenses custody/payment, manager/advice/broker, nonbank lending and venue; prudential additionally licenses insurer, funded pension and CCP promise/funding risk. The same fixed chief/source, separate case authority, direct courts and actual skill/pay requirements persist. A bank license never certifies these distinct technical obligations.
