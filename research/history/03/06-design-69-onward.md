# Design history: designs 69–76

*Part of [Design history](../../DESIGN-HISTORY.md); research, not enacted law.*

70. **Integrated design 69** compares three same-service income/ownership
    architectures in [C05/08](../../country/C05/08-income-ownership-and-country-choice.md):
    existing I05, a universal personal base with changed taxes, and I05 with
    voluntary mutual equity acquisition paid through a seller loan. The
    [conditional accounts/incidence](../../settlement/country-models/README.md)
    separate required output from common-production gains/losses and actual
    supply. Lower required output is not a productive or welfare gain. A
    book-price fund purchase is not free wealth; a price sensitivity fails
    debt service. Retain I05 conditionally, hold the substantive rivals and
    select no ready operating package. Preserve the
    [317-artifact design-68 predecessor](../../2026-10-country-model-choices/predecessor-design-68/SNAPSHOT.md)
    and [decision record](../../2026-10-country-model-choices/decision-record.md).
    Actual public choice, source finance, full workload and transition,
    independent review and superiority remain unestablished. Next draft
    grouped ordinary-law schedules; reopen economics on discriminating
    evidence or different material terms rather than another invented input.

71. **Integrated design 70** selects proposed
    [K13–K24/Q01–Q12](../../country/C08/15-special-crimes-and-bounded-sanctions.md):
    child/exploitation, privacy, physical/sector and public-power harms;
    usable needs-protecting fines, finite chosen community measures, proved
    legal-body attribution, bounded corporate orders, proceeds, sealing and
    prosecution periods. Preserve P proof/defense/child/liberty limits and
    independent support/repair. [Twenty-four boundary cases and workload](../../settlement/special-crimes/README.md)
    expose threshold cliffs, hidden debt/work coercion, shared control and
    a staffing gap; these are authored examples, not judgments or safety data.
    The [325-artifact predecessor](../../2026-10-special-crimes-and-sanctions/predecessor-design-69/SNAPSHOT.md)
    and [decision](../../2026-10-special-crimes-and-sanctions/decision-record.md)
    preserve scope, objection and migration. New numbers are uncalibrated,
    source law/software unchanged; sector predicates, family/civil,
    international jurisdiction, actual costs/receivers and superiority remain
    open. Next draft family/civil agency and remaining grouped law, then
    re-audit ordinary coverage rather than claim a complete national code.

72. **Integrated design 71** selects proposed
    [B21–B40](../../country/C08/16-family-agency-property-and-estates.md):
    civil majority/young-person acts, supported adult agency, scoped plural
    compacts/no-fault exit, real shared-property/care claims, child status,
    maintenance/protection, wills/estates, missing persons, civil periods and
    a public payer for proved official-certification loss. [C06/23](../../country/C06/23-family-claims-and-registry-risk.md)
    separates receiving, private support/estate money, Y's single pot and
    unpriced public risk. [Twenty-four journeys](../../settlement/family-civil/README.md)
    expose duplicated assets, support loss, caregiver invisibility and court
    capacity gaps; no real participation, verdicts or independent review.
    Preserve the [337-artifact predecessor](../../2026-10-family-civil-and-estates/predecessor-design-70/SNAPSHOT.md)
    and [decision](../../2026-10-family-civil-and-estates/decision-record.md).
    Plural status, young-person autonomy, ages/shares/clocks and new fiscal
    liability remain disputed hypotheses, not adopted law or safe capacity.
    Next draft remaining reproductive, sector and jurisdiction/international
    boundaries, then re-audit ordinary law and simultaneous national delivery.

73. **Integrated design 72** selects
    [B41–B52/P21–P24](../../country/C08/17-jurisdiction-and-legal-cooperation.md)
    and [C10/06](../../country/C10/06-legal-cooperation-and-protected-transfer.md):
    connected forum/law, recognition, family/property continuity, parallel
    cases/insolvency, actual safe notice/evidence, criminal reach/finality
    and protected transfer. Foreign alerts, servers, copied agents and
    requests create no self-executing force. Compare treaty-first and broader
    actor-reach rivals. [Twenty-four journeys](../../settlement/jurisdiction/README.md)
    expose duplicate recovery, custody reset, unsafe evidence/transfer and
    staffing gaps; no actual foreign authority or received remedy is supplied.
    Preserve the [350-artifact predecessor](../../2026-10-jurisdiction-and-legal-cooperation/predecessor-design-71/SNAPSHOT.md)
    and [decision](../../2026-10-jurisdiction-and-legal-cooperation/decision-record.md).
    The inspected Rome Article 80 does not alone prescribe national 30/life;
    ordinary caps stay, while full international-crime law is still unfinished.
    Actual source/treaty status, independent capacity, costs and better lives
    remain unverified. Next finish grouped remaining statutes, then re-audit
    ordinary coverage and simultaneous whole-country delivery/resources.

74. **Integrated design 73** selects [G01–G14/closed W01–W29](../../country/C08/18-international-crimes-and-accountability.md)
    and [C10/07](../../country/C10/07-atrocity-prevention-and-legal-control.md):
    defined domestic genocide/humanity/war/aggression, actual participation/
    command, differentiated fault, adult G/child protection, bounded presence
    reach and foreign-sham exception, finite penalties and fair cooperation.
    Sources are explicitly dated UN/official comparative law, not a latest-ICC
    incorporation; deliberate domestic variations and retrieval limits remain.
    [Twenty-four reasoned cases](../../settlement/international-crimes/README.md),
    [363-artifact predecessor](../../2026-10-international-crimes-and-accountability/predecessor-design-72/SNAPSHOT.md)
    and [decision](../../2026-10-international-crimes-and-accountability/decision-record.md)
    preserve reasons and objections. Main 25, command 25/15/10, incitement 7,
    reporting/recruitment 5 and G age 18 are normative, uncalibrated choices.
    No actual foreign competence, qualified review, staff/cash, source-law
    compatibility or superior outcome is supplied. Next finish sector/monetary,
    reproductive and conflict-detention law, then audit ordinary coverage and
    the full country's simultaneous obligations, resources and public control.

75. **Integrated design 74** selects [V01–V12/D01–D09](../../country/C08/19-regulation-and-bounded-enforcement.md)
    and [M01–M16](../../country/C06/24-financial-law-and-loss-allocation.md):
    bounded regulation, full punitive proof, finite money, actual repair, financial
    perimeter/capital/liquidity/exposures, client property, limited insurance,
    bank loss order, compensation and elected public risk. New parameters are
    uncalibrated national hypotheses, informed by dated BCBS/FSB/IADI references
    without claiming conformity. Retain M03 and reject the old book bank
    sovereign position 250/E100 as compliant finance; excess 150 needs a real
    source, not an exemption or fictional investor. [Cases/alternatives](../../settlement/regulatory-finance/README.md),
    [379-artifact predecessor](../../2026-10-regulatory-and-financial-law/predecessor-design-73/SNAPSHOT.md)
    and [decision](../../2026-10-regulatory-and-financial-law/decision-record.md)
    preserve the changed dependency and structural narrow-payment rival.
    No actual financing, legal adoption, independent review, staffed delivery
    or better-life outcome is established. Next repair source finance and
    finish reproductive/conflict-detention and technical sector instruments,
    then audit simultaneous country obligations and effective public control.

76. **Integrated design 75** selects [SF01–SF10](../../country/C06/25-source-finance-and-payment-continuity.md):
    a conditional independent public-payment standby with paid bank equity,
    drawn working credit and explicit source/ownership/legacy flows. New
    borrowing 175 replaces 250; a voluntary conversion of existing H100 bonds
    to bank shares gives E200, public share 12.5% and separately recalculated
    dividends. Custody179.695833 is one gross reference public period, not
    another resource or proven delivery. Domestic known claims remain 2485.
    M03 now maps omitted funding categories; old E100/250 remains rejected.
    [Eight comparisons](../../settlement/source-finance/README.md) include a
    full-reserve/equity-credit rival, failed missing-market/withdrawal/duration
    branches and matched principal/monetary liability changes. Preserve the
    [400-artifact predecessor](../../2026-10-source-finance-and-payment-architecture/predecessor-design-74/SNAPSHOT.md)
    and [decision](../../2026-10-source-finance-and-payment-architecture/decision-record.md).
    No actual investor/source, price, full cost/capacity, consent, external
    conformity or better-life outcome. Next finish remaining statutes/technical
    instruments and audit the whole country; reopen finance on discriminating
    evidence, not automatically another invented macro scenario.

77. **Integrated design 76** selects [RP01–RP16/RPO01–RPO03](../../country/C08/20-reproductive-law-and-parentage.md):
    bodily/material consent, prospective donor/origin rules, compensated
    voluntary pregnancy assistance without prenatal surrender, pre-use intended
    support and post-birth judicial status, storage/posthumous estate limits,
    severable foreign child protection, research/future-method holds and
    bounded wrongs. [Ten authored cases](../../settlement/reproductive-law/README.md)
    preserve distinct claims, receiving and dissent; no actual participation
    or clinical safety is supplied. Preserve the [410-artifact predecessor](../../2026-10-reproductive-law-and-parentage/predecessor-design-75/SNAPSHOT.md)
    and [decision](../../2026-10-reproductive-law-and-parentage/decision-record.md).
    WHO/HFEA/HCCH notes have explicit version/retrieval limits; no global
    recognition convention or external-law conformity is assumed. Age/limits,
    compensation formula, storage/estate/research windows are uncalibrated
    choices. Clinical/court/origin/storage staffing, sources, costs, actual
    adoption and better lives remain unestablished. Next finish conflict-
    detention/technical sector law, then audit the full national settlement.
