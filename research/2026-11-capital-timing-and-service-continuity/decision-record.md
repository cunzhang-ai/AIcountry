# Design205 decision. Let the public see what moves when a budget moves

9 November 2026 · Research selection from Design204

## Evidence and decision

Use one observed municipality and year instead of transferring financial
assumptions across jurisdictions. Copenhagen's 2026 budget records separate
a fiscal retiming, the capital framework, an internal execution target,
monitored delays, adopted project options, a subsequent release and reported
completion. Published alternatives trade greater school coverage for addressing
capacity at fewer schools. Care and school accounts share the same municipality
and year, while grants and restricted uses have different fiscal boundaries.

Retain the adopted modernization baseline in this comparison. There is no
verified local source, common crew constraint or access evidence justifying
a switch to another complete programme. Keeping a repair decision is a
substantive choice. It leaves the documented capacity question and any
present service deficiencies visible. Do not treat the later rejection of
one amendment as informed consent by every affected pupil.

## Alternatives and power

The observed arrangement already monitors execution separately and makes
public budget and amendment decisions. Retain it as a strong rival. A
separate universal approval process would add scrutiny but could delay
useful work and concentrate a veto. A blanket ban on any planning budget
above forecast spend would simplify the numbers while discouraging genuinely
contingent preparation. Approve real concurrent exposure within authority
and preserve a usable service path using existing forums. Do not infer
expected slippage from a prior portfolio without checking common risks,
contract terms and actual usable capacity.

The strongest objection is that agencies may relabel a service delay as a
technical change or drown urgent exceptions in new documents. Require a
short joint record from the payer and service owner for material cases, and
give existing outside review access to source versions and complaints. Treat
time for an appeal as part of actual access. Assembly and local elected budget
powers, opposition and distinct audit, courts, safety and professional
functions retain their appointment and funding rules. If the same controller
can block every route, record that failure. No extra office, expert veto
or AI approval follows. AI can help reconcile schedules and identify conflicting
premises. Research assistance supplied no actual affected participation or
independent review of the selected design.

## Transition and bounded follow-through

Under DB, FB, MF, IV, sector, evidence and rights law, preserve authorized
purposes, restricted funds, old contracts, pay, due dates, lawful access,
pending claims and paid successors. A change of budget year confers no
service postponement authority. Any adopted threshold is prospective.

Next use this joined calendar on an actual funded bundle with independent
service and procurement evidence. Carry one education/care route, ongoing
facility service, local finances and a separately owned critical national
or regional input. Keep privately produced inputs, labour, participation,
ecological exposure and independent remedy explicit. Require actual payment
dates, work windows, service receipt, displacement and comparison with
ordinary repair. Retain, revise or reject according to what the evidence
can establish. If the necessary information is missing, keep uncovered scope
and unmet duties explicit. Reopen on a verified access deficiency, failed
interim route, correlated delay, new funded alternative or harmful process burden.
