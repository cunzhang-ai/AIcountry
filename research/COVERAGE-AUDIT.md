# Functional coverage audit of the country design

15 October 2026 · Integrated design 68 research audit; not a second country design

This audit checks whether the current research assigns a first-order authority,
delivery or resource path, challenge and continuity rule to each identified
major state function. **Specified** means such a route is described, not that
it is enacted, complete, staffed or successful. **Partial** identifies a
material choice that remains unassigned. The [UN functions-of-government
classification](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/cofog/revision)
supplies an [external expenditure-function cross-check](2026-10-end-of-life/function-crosswalk.md),
not proof that every right or life transition works. A broad category can
hide a failed handoff.

| Knowledge, AI and change | Evidence, cultural and digital power, evaluation and cyber continuity | [3 functions](coverage/04-knowledge-ai-and-change.md) |

Each function has one finding in the linked file. The [integrated country
design](COUNTRY-DESIGN.md) and [C01–C14 chapters](country/README.md) own the
proposed institutions; this audit only checks coverage. The [design
history](DESIGN-HISTORY.md) and [support map](SUPPORT-MAP.md) retain why these
functions were added and which studies bear on them. No chronology is
maintained inside this audit.

The [commitment inventory](settlement/commitments/README.md) connects all
37 named functions and 61 selections to proposed owners, resources and
remedy executors. It exposes unknown quantities and cost attribution;
this denominator measures mapped research coverage, not feasibility.

The list is bounded. Context-specific air, sea, rail and specialized product
law requires a real jurisdiction and valid authority. A complete clause
inventory, affected-public choice, funded delivery and comparison with
existing countries remain separate. Reopen this audit when a missing state
function, excluded person or failed cross-system duty is identified. Moving
findings between files does not change a country-design selection or create
legal authority.

The [full-objective completion assessment](DESIGN-COMPLETENESS.md) separates
chapter/role coverage from missing rules, coherent resource choices, legal
adoption and observed outcomes. It does not close this functional denominator
or treat classification/index changes as adopted governance.

[Design167 NR](country/C09/15-finite-resources-and-mineral-rights.md) adds explicit
ordinary mineral ownership/concession/title-neutral operation/closure/depletion
scope beyond the initial37-row census. The preserved rows/financial objects are
not a closed national-function universe and did not already price this work.
[Resource-scope handoff](coverage/03-resources-and-external-dependence.md) keeps
that extension separate from unchanged historical row identities/source limits.
