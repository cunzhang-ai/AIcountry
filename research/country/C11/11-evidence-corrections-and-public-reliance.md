# When public decisions depend on evidence later found wrong

Design203 and Design433 · EV17–EV30 · Proposed country rules

An evidence custodian can establish that a publication was wrong. The authority
that used it still owes the people affected a decision under the law that governs
their case. A published correction, a changed legal decision and relief actually
received are separate events.

**Select a duty on existing public decision owners to trace material reliance,
decide what follows and verify delivery.** Reserve money, new obligations and
changes in law remain subject to their own democratic authorizations. People
can reach independent review even if the public body fails to acknowledge a warning.
