# Design425. Keep food available through the next shortage window

## Decision and baseline

Design424 selects a rotating food buffer, independent safety and
funded local receiving. Existing rules distinguish a warehouse
release from an actual meal. The remaining decision is how the
national reserve separates routine rotation, protected delivery
and a general market intervention when two useful delivery windows
compete for the same lot. This revision was prepared with AI
assistance. An elected budget and existing statutory authority
control any real release.

## Alternatives and choice

Ordinary income protection and commercial supply preserve choice
and avoid avoidable public storage. They work where available food,
price and access support actual receiving. A general market release
can increase available supply and possibly affect prices, but can
reach other buyers before the intended recipients and draw down
the next window. Direct distribution can reach people excluded
from the market. It adds work, cost and the risk of inferior or
stigmatizing offers. Different funded commercial, cooperative and
local routes remain possible.

Choose limited protected release with a paid route for a physical
access gap and retain ordinary income protection. Give any broader
market intervention its own law and source and test it on the same
remaining physical balance. Separately cost storage, rotation and
replenishment. The comparison selects a timed release for a stated
quantity and delivery path. It does not set a national stock target.
A future window's exposure can justify retaining some stock. A
genuine earlier urgent duty still needs a timely disposition under
its own lawful authority. Publish who bears the loss when both
cannot be served. Holding food until it spoils is also a failure.

## Evidence, authority and migration

[Primary comparators](sources.md) document stockholding and reserve
arrangements at different scales. They do not establish which food
this country needs or the dates, costs or delivery capacity. The
comparison uses stipulated food, receipts and service paths. No
producer, recipient, supplier or independent reviewer participated.

Apply prospectively to authorized stocks and future releases.
Preserve legal ownership, reserved lots, protected beneficiaries,
signed obligations, wages and independent review. The Assembly
sets purposes and source. The designated food authority issues
a scoped release within them. The independent safety authority
qualifies lots or holds them. A paid receiver delivers the lawful
food and Treasury pays under its own authority. Ordinary courts
and sector reviewers keep the power to correct unlawful withholding
or exclusion. Audit checks cash, proof and common controllers
outside the reserve operator. Carry claims, records, paid access
and replenishment through any transfer. No wider emergency or
rationing power follows.

## Scoped result and bounded next test

Documentary comparators and the constructed balance qualify a
research choice only. Actual safety, suitable food, budget,
delivery and better outcomes remain open.

For one item and receiving area, the competent owners and a
qualified independent reviewer would test the same two deadlines
with observed stock, shelf life, lawful holding, alternative
providers and one shared loss. Complete when they can reconcile
actual dispatch, recipient receipt, missed access, committed
later deliveries and replenishment costs. Include people unable
to cook or travel. There is no committed exercise budget.
Reopen on prolonged demand, concentration, repeated spoilage,
missing receipts or a stronger ordinary option.
