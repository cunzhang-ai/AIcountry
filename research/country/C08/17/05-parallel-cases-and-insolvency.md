# Parallel proceedings, sovereign interests and a finite estate

*B49–B50; part of [C08/17](../17-jurisdiction-and-legal-cooperation.md).*

**B49 — No automatic race or double recovery.** Compare pending cases by
actual competence, parties/issues, meaningful notice, protection, fair access,
evidence, timely enforceability and existing rights. First filing is relevant,
not a universal mandate. A court may stay in favor of a genuinely available
more appropriate forum only on evidence of accepted access and a usable
remedy, preserving domestic dates and urgent protection; do not dismiss
on a proposed court/tribunal that has not accepted the case. Review the stay
on refusal, delay, danger or changed facts and resume if its basis fails.
Never assume foreign limitation will be tolled by a domestic order.

Credit actual compensatory payment/returned property against the same harm
across courts, settlements, insurer recovery and enforcement. Distinguish
promised, reserved, recovered and received amounts, currency/date, priority
and who has legal recourse after paying. No defendant pays the same loss
twice and no victim loses a valid balance merely because another office
reported collection. Legitimate distinct harms are not averaged away.

This country's own public body has no general immunity from its statutory
rights/operational-liability court route. A foreign state/international
organization may have actual jurisdictional or execution immunity; determine
the exact source, act and asset independently, including separate treatment
of commercial activity. An embassy/reserve is not ordinary attachable capital.
Do not assume every state-owned firm is either immune or the state's debtor.
Valid immunity can leave a real remedy gap: preserve lawful diplomatic/
contractual alternatives and report unmet harm, not pretend domestic courts
can order a foreign sovereign or waive its protections unilaterally.

**B50 — Cross-border private insolvency.** A foreign representative may seek
recognition of an authentic collective proceeding with independent court
supervision and fair creditor/debtor access. Determine the debtor's genuine
center of main interests from stable actual administration transparent to
ordinary counterparties; a recent paper move or vendor domicile is not
conclusive. Identify actual local operations/assets for any secondary route.
Prove authority, standing, procedure, interests and material foreign orders.

Recognition may coordinate records and lawful bounded preservation. A
foreign stay, priority, discharge or restructuring does not automatically
override U's exemptions, client property, support/intentional-harm exceptions,
secured interests or confirmation protections. Give affected parties notice,
source accounts, equivalent participation and independent reasons for a
specific compatible order. Foreign creditors are not demoted by nationality.
Several registers/representatives cannot each distribute the same asset.

Require a joined asset/claim/priority and payment account, lawful source
finance and actual receiving before recognizing a plan as executable. A
haircut is a creditor loss, not output or cash. Restricted pensions, bank
resolution and sovereign distress retain C06's separate regime; no general
private discharge spends public/insurance assets. Where recognition cannot
safely supply a remedy, state the unmet obligation and available domestic
route rather than invent a foreign guarantee. Actual treaty/specific sector
conflicts need lawful reconciliation before adoption or a scaling claim.

[RCR01–RCR06](../13/06-representative-claims-and-delivered-redress.md) supplies
the domestic coordination and same-loss distribution route for opt-in civil
claims. B49's jurisdiction and actual receipt tests and B50's separate
insolvency authority continue.
