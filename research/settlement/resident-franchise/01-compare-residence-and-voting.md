# A national vote before secure residence or naturalization

*Design319 · Legal comparators and authored election examples.*

New Zealand's Electoral Act and Electoral Commission guidance
provide national voting for qualifying resident noncitizens with
a continuous year of residence at some time, subject to other
rules. The electoral resident category generally requires lawful
presence with no obligation to leave at a specified date. New
Zealand's national candidacy rules retain citizenship. AIcountry's
candidate treats a current habitual home as the electoral basis
independently of those status filters and keeps equal candidacy.
[Electoral Act, section 74](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2025/82/en/latest/sections/LMS1460080/DLM308827)

The UK Electoral Commission lists British, Irish and qualifying
Commonwealth citizens among eligible national electors under other
applicable conditions. It separately lists the corresponding
nationality qualifications for candidacy. This supplies a different
comparison based on selected nationalities and qualifying status.
Neither comparator identifies the best residence threshold or
measures the effects of the AIcountry proposal.

## The same residents under competing rules

All cases are authored. The adults are noncitizens unless otherwise
stated. Where a new ordinary contest is mentioned, lawful adoption
and useful-time verified eligibility are assumed.

| Person or event | Local vote | National vote and candidacy | Secure residence or voluntary naturalization |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Eight habitual months and an actual local home | Yes | No under either threshold | Four-year basis still unfulfilled |
| Eighteen continuous habitual months, with pending status and no card | Yes with actual local home | Yes under the new rule, no under the earlier four-year rule | Four-year basis still unfulfilled |
| Eighteen months including a proved temporary absence with a continuing home | Yes with actual local home | Yes under the new rule | Four-year basis still unfulfilled |
| Eighteen calendar months since arrival, but only seven since a genuine prior break and return | Yes with six cumulative months and actual local home | No new qualification without an earlier vested period | Four-year continuous basis still unfulfilled |
| Previously qualified, left permanently, then actually reestablished habitual residence | Apply the cumulative local rule and actual home | Restored on genuine return under the new rule. No resident national vote during permanent absence | Determine independent MB residence and return rights |
| Eighteen-month resident at the protected immediate succession after a later amendment | Prior local law | Apply the predecessor rule through its lawful repeats | MB scope remains independent |

Under the old ordinary reference a person whose first national
poll falls 18 months after arrival can miss it, and may wait
through the next full election interval. The new threshold
would permit participation at that first poll if operative.
It reduces the statutory residence threshold by three years.
It does not guarantee when the next poll takes place.

At the next valid NI opening, recompute the lawful electorate.
For illustration, an old opening of 100,000 and a new opening
of 103,000 imply thresholds of 100 and 103 under the existing
one-per-thousand rule. An initiative that opened under the old
law retains its lawful opening denominator. Individual support
uses actual eligibility at the date of the act and the applicable
transition law. Recalculate election staffing, campaign help and independent
verification for the future scope rather than assigning
eligibility from the example or predicted political preference.

Immediate actual-residence voting would remove the remaining
wait, but makes genuinely new and disputed home connections a
larger issue for verification. A citizenship gate gives more
weight to formal membership and more control to status processes.
The one-year option gives real earlier power while preserving a
test of sustained current home. Whether that balance works
depends on independent evidence, safe access and a legitimate
prospective decision.
