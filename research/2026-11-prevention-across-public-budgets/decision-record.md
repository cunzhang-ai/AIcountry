# Design279. Prevention across public budgets

10 November 2026 · Research selection · No actual enactment

## Baseline and decision

[Design278](baseline.json) already protects due housing and clinical
services, requires qualified recurring sources and recognizes differences
between released staff time and cash. It does not give spending bodies a
positive route for a joint optional start whose benefits cross portfolios.
Use the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md). Select a financing route and
compare first-budget offers for the same group without transferring legal
authority. Codex assisted research and drafting. No affected participants,
external reviewers, delivery authorities or public payers made a decision.

## Diagnosis and rival institutions

Better housing can matter directly to residents and can change crisis
demand. The first payer might still be unable to pay on time even if use
of other services falls. The opposite diagnosis is also credible. The
block could be scarce suitable homes, rejected conditions, lack of support
or poor intake. A shared budget cannot supply those missing elements.

- **Ordinary funded repair or single-payer delivery.** Improve access to
  existing services or fund the full offer from one qualified budget. This
  avoids cross-body approvals and has clear accountability. An authority
  may still lack headroom or leave people waiting for an available home.
- **Select an expressly voted joint programme.** Existing bodies vote
  capped gross contributions, retain their delivery and correction duties,
  and bear the cost even if offsets are zero. A separate smaller proposal
  can use one qualified source where lawful. Preserve the elected choice
  over other priorities and compare actual receipt. With only one funder
  needed, prefer the administratively simpler lawful route if it carries
  the same protection and coverage.
- **Centralize funding or pay a share of estimated savings.** A common
  executive budget can reduce bargaining and direct support more quickly.
  It concentrates discretion and may displace elected local choices.
  Outcome-contingent payments may transfer some delivery risk but create
  measurement, renewal and selection incentives. Neither is a condition
  of help. Reopen the funding model if fragmented budgets repeatedly block
  lawful, better supported options, after testing the actual bottleneck.

## Support, control and continuity

The [Canadian study](sources.md) supports a possible benefit for the
examined offer and population, with positive estimated net cost. Its
estimates are neither independent new confirmation of the same trial's
earlier summary nor local cost or budget data. The [authored comparison](../settlement/prevention-across-budgets/02-authored-housing-choice.md)
checks early cash, the recurring source, paired housing/support capacity
and the timing of any later payment release. It cannot test actual
suitability, sustained access, causal benefit here or political assent.

The competent elected bodies choose the contribution and displaced use,
the named lead records actual commitments, housing and support operators
deliver through distinct authority, and ordinary independent review and
audit examine payment and receipt. Treasury and independent legislative
analysts reconcile totals using the original custodians' records. Even
this chain can fail if the executive controls all feasible providers or a
payer withholds its already due contribution. Preserve FB and MF ordinary
repair and payment routes. Existing HL claims, case dates, lawful tenancies,
voluntary support, worker claims, and national and local duties carry
forward. No provider may convert access into a performance contract with
the resident. Review any new allocation or changed scope prospectively
and fund the handover before retiring an ordinary route.

## Next bounded action

Before pricing broader national coverage, obtain one competent steward's
matched offer schedule for ordinary intake repair and a bounded secure
tenancy cohort. The steward and payers would need actual dated units,
support workers, usable backup, source and displaced use, cost at zero
offsets and the rights carried after the last entry. Verify the terms
with affected people and independent reviewers before any first vote.
Do not claim a real programme, service benefit or cash release until
that scope is supported. Reopen on a missing contribution, unsuitable
homes, lost coverage, displaced queues or a stronger ordinary option.
