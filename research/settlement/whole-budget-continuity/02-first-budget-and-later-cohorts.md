# Compare a whole first budget and later starts in the same setting

This is an authored decision exercise. Money units are invented.
Existing and accrued claims, safe access, bodily autonomy and democratic
independence are common conditions. The same starting state, geography and
qualified resources apply to each option. All currently budgeted due work
and restricted funds are reserved before these increments. Unmet duties
discovered under the common baseline still need action. The exercise
quantifies only the stated margins and selected measures.

## What is available after existing commitments

Annual money is unrestricted fiscal room in the scenario. It is not
necessarily cash available before the first payment. Skill slots are
qualified, staffed and usable at the relevant common peak, outside previously
assigned work. Assume the work and locations make each slot usable for the
specified tasks. The weaker path combines lower receipts with fewer accepted
qualified slots. It does not count projected recruits, future AI savings or
voluntary family support.

## What members actually choose

The slots below cover home-support delivery or independent service assurance,
not interchangeable judges or nurses. The fictional budgets include all
stated scope and costs for the chosen measures. Judicial powers, old claims
and independent budget control remain separate.

| Measure | Annual money | Years 1–2 cash before first receipt | Care slots | Independent assurance slots |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A. Repair verified missed home care and independent follow-through | 5 | 3 | 3 | 2 |
| B. Paid training, backfill and qualification support, years 1–2 only | 3 | 2 | 2 | 1 |
| C. New home-support coverage, continuing | 4 | 3 | 3 | 1 |
| D. New assisted administrative access with tested non-AI alternatives, continuing | 5 | 3 | 0 | 3 |

The payer has seven usable money units before the first additional
payments in each of the first two years. Assume the rest of each annual
source arrives before the later scheduled payments in those years. Other technical and administrative workers for D are assumed
separately available for the case. B's finite cost includes backfill and
closeout. It buys instruction and supervision. It does not supply future
qualified slots automatically. A is the same binding repair in all lawful
options. Its affected recipients retain S, AP, independent challenge and
execution protection. B, C and D are proposed here, and have yet to become
existing entitlements.

| Option | First year under central assumptions | Same-scope result |
|---|---|---|
| Strong retain and repair, A | 5 annual, 3 early cash, 3 care, 2 assurance | Fits under both paths. No paid preparation or selected later expansion |
| Repair and paid preparation, A+B | 8 annual, 5 early cash, 5 care, 3 assurance | Fits under both paths. Choose this under the exercise assumptions. Pay the two-year preparation and measure later actual qualification and deployment |
| Immediate new coverage and access, A+C+D | 14 annual, 9 early cash, 6 care, 6 assurance | Fails annual money, early cash and assurance even on the central path |
| New coverage alongside preparation, A+B+C | 12 annual, 8 early cash, 8 care, 4 assurance | Fits central annual money and assurance. It fails central early cash and care, and the weaker annual path as well |

Under the weaker path, A+B+C in Year 2 fits the annual ceiling of 12 but
still needs eight care slots against seven and eight early money units
against seven. In Year 3, with B completed,
A+C+D fits the central annual and skill margins, but needs 14 against 13
money and six against five assurance slots in the weaker path. If verified
funds and actual due-date capacity permit, A+C alone fits those Year 3
weaker annual and skill margins at nine money, six care and three assurance.
If access failures instead dominate, A+D is a real competing vote at ten
money, three care and five assurance. Neither passes on unknown early cash,
actual acceptance or later obligations. In Year 4 the joint proposal fits
both sets of displayed margins. Fresh authority and paid future capacity
are still required. An actual resource loss goes to the elected correction
process with existing duties protected, not an automatic model ranking.

This result orders a decision under explicit assumptions. It does not price
the other rows, qualify a national programme or choose a future budget on
behalf of the public. Compare baseline exclusions and the cost of waiting
before preferring the same sequence in a real setting.
