# Design344 decision on employment-linked housing

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design343, H secure tenure, RT anti-evasion, MB12 portable status,
TW07 cash pay, HL housing loss and the working framework. Existing
anti-evasion and migration protections leave genuine service occupancy
and its last day of usable housing underspecified. Select ordinary
tenure for ordinary use, a narrow demonstrable on-site exception, an
independent possession route and a funded safe handover. Work, housing
and status keep their separate legal owners.

## Alternatives and objections

Ordinary tenure for all employer-linked homes offers strong security
and may make some essential on-site posts harder to cover. A notice-only
exception is easy to administer and can leave an occupant homeless.
Employer housing could be discouraged through independent leasing and
funded regional stock, with transition costs and real supply constraints.
Select ordinary tenure plus the narrow exception, and keep independent
provision a live alternative. Account for scarce employer and public
stock and the costs of actual continued housing.

The [primary comparators](sources.md) illustrate worker housing standards
and possession protections. The 30-day initial period, 14-day hold
review and proof of suitable receiving are candidate choices. Delayed
repossession, weak public bridges and financing costs could limit
lawful housing and jobs. Report those losses and preserve actual
receiving and review rather than letting shortfalls determine rights.

## Authority, transition and reopening

The competent elected bodies set scope and pay applicable public duties.
Employers or operators fund their lawful transition, the local authority
owns housing receiving and an ordinary court decides possession. The
assistant authored this research selection. Adoption, independent review,
affected participation, costs and effects remain open.

Test a genuine on-site closure and a disguised ordinary letting with
tenants, local housing officers, employers and independent counsel.
Compare safe receiving, the timing of lawful possession, retained work
rights, total funding and suitable alternatives. Reopen on coerced
moves, repeated funded-source failures, exclusion at entry or a better
feasible independent housing arrangement.
