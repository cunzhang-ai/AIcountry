# Design369 commitment. Pay for the people who can carry the next duty

[HC05–HC10](../../country/C11/05/01-ai-and-succession-of-public-competence.md)
requires the operator and lawful budget owner to show qualified decision,
review, instruction, learner pay and substitute cover over the expected
replacement interval. Separate approved funding from a willing qualified
receiver and accepted placement. Count existing duties, origin capacity and
independent review on the same calendar. A shortfall leaves original
claims and funded authority unchanged.

The [worked staffing comparison](../ai-and-succession-of-public-competence/01-two-cohorts-and-a-duty-roster.md)
uses illustrative paid units. No real hiring, saving or qualified future
workforce is established. Protect existing qualifications, wages, learning
rights and duty dates through transfer and tool change.
