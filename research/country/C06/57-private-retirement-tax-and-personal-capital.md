# C06. Private retirement saving: one contribution, one basis, one recovery

3 November 2026 · Design 142 · PF01–PF16 research candidate, not enacted tax law

[Design155 TN](59-tax-sources-and-country-operation.md) retains PF's private
source/basis/cash-choice rules and exact benchmark arithmetic. PA/SD's numerical
default replacement preference is withdrawn; actual lawful retrofit source
periods continue pending express replacement. Statistical pension labels and
net income do not establish PF/TA bases or an actual national tax yield.

**Select ordinary post-source private saving: no new contribution deduction,
asset-yield levy or blanket withdrawal exemption.** New work-sponsored funding
passes through actual ordinary remuneration with a usable cash choice. Recover
proved own capital once; tax actual income under PA/TA. Private capital remains
separate from public Y solidarity rights and I05 safely usable resources.

The selection rejects default new EET contribution relief, a TEE-style blanket
return exemption and a separate annual pension-asset tax. They remain genuine
legislative alternatives with distribution, timing, privacy and source costs.
It also rejects taxing an entire payment just because its label is pension.
This is a value/design choice within service-first public protection, not
proof of optimal private saving, equal incidence or national revenue gain.

DC means owned investment units; their disposal retains TA06's real-gain and
price-indexed basis. A genuine nonmarketable periodic promise uses nominal
capital-first recovery without a new tax mortality table. This deliberately
defers taxable annuity income and can favor wealthier savers' timing; the
pro-rata rival remains material. Prudential NB valuation still needs qualified
actuarial evidence. Neither method supplies safe assets, guarantees or staff.

PF follows FP19 and CS18's identified positive-rule gap. It proposes new
coordinated source treatment, not a licence-created privilege or retroactive
reinterpretation. Earlier private-plan law/relief/net rights, all Y units,
actual claims and pending remedies retain paid OP/T successors. Actual software,
constitution, contribution permissions, holds and common method stay unchanged.
