# IA05–IA08: protected careers, own duties and finite vacancy

*Part of [C10/10](../10-civilian-assessment-and-intelligence-limits.md); design102 research selection, not enacted law.*

**IA05 — Director selected and replaced through C04.** Cabinet is the actual appointing and disciplinary owner of NAU's civilian career Director, through an accessible open C04 competition and reasoned qualified public shortlist. Require five years of relevant analytic, diplomatic, public-risk, civilian defense/cyber, research or accountable administration work **or demonstrated equivalent competence**, with demonstrated evidence evaluation, rights/confidentiality and public administration ability. No proprietary clearance guild, military rank, wealth, party agreement or AI score is a qualification. Publish qualification reasons/material interests with protected personal details. This is protected career employment, not a copied six-year independent-chief term: a new government can change lawful questions and resource policy, but cannot purge a director for the inconvenient answer, protected disclosure, lawful vote or service under its predecessor. Cabinet may discipline/reassign/remove for proved misconduct, inability after reasonable accommodation or job-related performance failure, or carry out a genuine prospective lawful reorganization with continuing claim/records/worker protection; give usable grounds, response, reasoned decision and direct ordinary-court review. A complaint or political disagreement does not prove misconduct. The appointing Cabinet cannot finally decide an unlawful-retaliation claim against itself.

**IA06 — Staff employer, qualification and own duties.** The Director recruits/assigns/disciplines the Deputy, analysts, lawful liaison/translation and records/security staff through C04 open protected careers, job-related competence, documented conflict/rotation and direct ordinary review. Staff are civilians in this unit, not an embedded party/military secret command. The analyst with a prior published assignment has their own statutory competence to prepare/preserve a source-linked finding, deliver scheduled assessments and send a lawful urgent warning/referral; the records officer has own custody/access/preservation duties. Director/minister cannot require a personal case signature to suppress these duties, alter original findings or reassign an investigator/reviewer of the unit's own abuse. Real workload allocation remains lawful management: protection of an assigned finding does not give each analyst an unbounded policy program or publication of private sources. Staff with concrete conflicts use prequalified prior substitutes, not ad hoc ministerial selection for a named opponent. Court, audit and PD own-case routes remain external; NAU is not a prosecutor or adjudicator.

The Act expressly assigns the qualified Assessment Officer function in IL05 to an appropriately trained, preassigned career analyst appointed/assigned/disciplined through this C04 chain; it is a case competence, not another insulated office or a Director’s mandatory personal signature. Ordinary paid expert analysis/translation, licensed public reports and properly receipted source-safe access expenses are possible under the actual task/procurement law. Paying for secret person files, clandestine recruitment, collection volume or findings the minister prefers is not an expert-analysis contract.

**IA07 — Finite vacancy and first staffing.** A predesignated qualified Deputy may perform routine administration during a vacancy/inability for no more than180calendar days from the original vacancy, with no serial acting title/renamed deputy reset. Existing lawful warning/referral, scheduled analysis, records/access, pay and ordinary staff protection continue through their own case-role powers after that period; an acting title is not required for an officer's already enacted duty. The temporary deputy may operate existing appropriations/contracts and fill ordinary staff posts within a published plan, but cannot assume the permanent director appointment, make discretionary senior appointments, create a new program/collection method, expand foreign access or change a material policy. Cabinet must open permanent C04 recruitment within14calendar days; no appointment by timeout. If no deputy or director exists, only each actual appointed officer's own competence continues: no analyst inherits a missing managerial power. For the inaugural unit Cabinet may use the same C04 process to appoint the qualified Deputy and nonmanager analyst/records posts directly under the new Act, before a director, without deeming them a permanent chief or bypassing that competition. Unknown actual qualified people are an operational failure, not a reason to invent a roster or delay a due court/notice duty. Election caretaker limits still forbid a discretionary senior appointment; first staffing must have a lawful appointing source/date rather than a caretaker exception.

**IA08 — No agency-owned clearance veto or political purge.** Director/staff need role-specific safe access and confidentiality training, not a service-controlled political allegiance licence. The C04 employer may inspect only relevant evidence of an actual conflict, compromise, misconduct or source-safety risk using lawful data; disclose an answerable ground/gist, minimize third-party material and allow direct ordinary-court review/urgent protection. Citizenship/foreign family/peaceful belief or an allegation alone is not disqualification; particular competing foreign obligations or demonstrated compromise need evidence and proportionate response. No unit, vendor or foreign partner can veto the committee's lawful appointments or finally deny its statutory access. Specific disputed disclosure modes are decided under IA12, never a blanket clearance exemption. Protected disclosures to competent committee, auditor, PD supervisor, prosecutor or court do not become misconduct merely because they embarrass Cabinet; actual harmful unnecessary source disclosure retains applicable law and fair process. This draft creates no new secrecy crime, immunity or mandatory compelled testimony power.

Ordinary ministerial accountability makes the executive easier to replace and avoids a second permanent security policymaker. It also leaves Cabinet significant recruitment, budget, framing and performance leverage. A fixed-term insulated chief or separate service could resist one Cabinet but accumulate long-lived capture, obstruct an elected change or block through its own clearance process. A self-perpetuating expert board is rejected. These are competing control costs, not evidence that ordinary careers are actually independent. Direct courts and separate parliamentary custody can also be jointly captured or unavailable.
