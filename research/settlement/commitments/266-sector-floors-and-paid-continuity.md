# Design353. Finance real wages and care through a sector-floor change

[WL41–WL46](../../country/C05/12/08-sector-floors-and-paid-continuity.md)
joins pay for covered work, ordinary payroll charges, the exact public rate
and a separate qualified backup. Budget for participation, inspections,
independent review and the continuing service, including direct-hire support.
A failed payment preserves earned wage claims and original care deadlines.

The [comparison](../home-care-wage-and-service-compact/01-payroll-and-visits.md)
uses dated payroll and reserve cash without assuming recruitment or cheaper
retention. Actual prices, staffing, sources and authorization remain open.
