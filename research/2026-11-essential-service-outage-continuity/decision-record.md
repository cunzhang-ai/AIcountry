## Alternatives and public control

Grid repair and mobile mutual aid can be cheaper where a verified path
beats the service deadline. A protected site resource can maintain a
short-deadline task while a common road or network fails, but costs
upkeep and usable capacity. Direct alternate service may avoid extra
power equipment, but a safe staffed site, water distribution, accessible
journey or offline receiver needs real acceptance. No device mandate
applies across unlike places. Select performance at the receiving
service and only the necessary reserve. The last safe start reflects
actual travel, hookup and process recovery. One year is a provisional
maximum for rechecking credited readiness. It is not a universal
battery duration, staffing clock or judicial deadline.

A strong objection is that a readiness floor could overbuy capacity,
raise bills, expose vulnerable people through data collection or give
operators a hidden veto. Assembly and councils set funding and scope
through prior law. Independent technical and sector acceptance apply
only to their respective facts. Require attributed cost, protected
evidence, actual remedy, lower-cost alternatives and useful partial
delivery. Failed tests limit reliability credit and preserve due rights.
An urgent act already permitted under law is not gated by a second
signature. Service and legal authority do not follow a moved device or
an AI alert. An inaccessible alternate court or poll remains a public
control failure. No affected person or qualified independent reviewer
participated in this edition.

## Continuation and reopening

Keep EG/WS/RC/TS, PS21–PS32, CP/RX, separate clinical consent and actual
poll and court law. Carry debts, personnel, meter and source records,
privacy, permits, existing service duties, budgets and assets through
lawful paid OP/T succession. No standard transfers a commercial contract
or renewable entitlement by itself. Actual lawful adoption, network
engineering, funded provision and better lives remain separate and
unproved. Evidence is scoped and the authored comparison is a desk
check, not site commissioning.

Reopen on verified interruption, changes to equipment or access, a
different due deadline, loss of independent control or a cheaper
equivalent safe route. Use a common locality and period to compare
attributable capacity, benefits and continuing fiscal sources across
the whole offer, including who loses when several services call at
once. A failed or unresolved finding should trigger a practical
repair or a changed decision where justified.
