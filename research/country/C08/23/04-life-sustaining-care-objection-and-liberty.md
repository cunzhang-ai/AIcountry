# Necessary care: life support, objection and liberty

*Canonical UA10–UA12; part of [C08/23](../23-necessary-care-and-unknown-will.md).*

**UA10 — Explicit life-sustaining decisions.** Known applicable choice/refusal
uses UA03 and C08/08, not this residual standard. For genuinely unknown will,
starting, withholding or withdrawing effective life-sustaining treatment is a
separate high-stakes UA07/08 question, never an ordinary basic-care checklist.
Prior judicial review, two qualified opinions including independent relevant
palliative input, funded advocacy and a usable appeal opportunity are required.

For starting, identify a substantial clinical benefit proportionate to burdens,
with actual monitoring and a defined trial/reassessment where uncertainty warrants.
Failure of the positive test for a new proposed intervention means no authority
to start it, not a duty to initiate every effective option. Existing lawful
support continues within its grounds; no-new-intrusion under uncertainty differs
from removing an already relied-on necessary function. Withholding a function
already being supplied, like withdrawal, uses the stricter following test;
compare adequate alternative ways to supply it, not a mandatory particular device.
For such withholding/withdrawal, prove either inability to achieve the actual clinical
purpose under disclosed reviewable standards, or severe refractory bodily suffering/
serious physical harm caused by the intervention whose burdens clearly outweigh
its remaining benefit despite adequate less harmful/relieving alternatives.
The purpose is the intervention's stated bodily therapeutic function, not
restored cognition, communication, independence or normality. Effective gas
exchange support is not ineffective merely because it cannot cure the disease
or restore consciousness. Both limbs obey the same exclusions below. Document
dissent, prognosis and uncertainty; no blanket futility or disability label.

Inability to communicate, dependency, cognitive prognosis, lack of conventional
productivity, a disliked way of life, costs, missing beds or benefit to heirs/
donation recipients cannot establish this test. Continued dependence alone does
not mean a trial failed. Uncertainty favors the least intrusive safe continuity
and reassessment, not automatic new invasive escalation or automatic withdrawal.
This can leave effective treatment continuing without knowledge of the person's
actual preference; the opposite decision can also contradict that unknown wish.

No withdrawal of ordinary food/drink, hygiene, comfort, presence or other lawful
claims as a cost or death instrument. Clinically administered nutrition/hydration
is assessed as the actual medical intervention, not hidden in ordinary food nor
automatically removed with another treatment. Proportionate symptom relief needs
its own clinical purpose, lawful prescribing and monitoring; no covert death goal.
The current reference retains no assisted-dying authorization or exception to
otherwise applicable P/K law. That retain/no-change choice does not bar future
lawful public reconsideration; refusal, lawful withdrawal and relief are distinct.

**UA11 — Personal acts and coercive shortcuts stay excluded.** UA cannot choose
sexual/intimate consent, marriage, a vote, a will, nationality renunciation,
research participation, organ/gamete donation, elective reproductive use or general
legal agency. It creates no diagnosis-specific forced-treatment/commitment power.
Any unknown-will proposal, including mental-health care, must meet identical UA
grounds; no treatment over credible opposition, behavior-normalizing medication,
covert dosing, closed placement, general restraint or forced return is authorized.
Treatment availability, relatives' interests and model confidence supply no mandate.

**UA12 — Objection is information and a stopping condition.** Words, gestures,
withdrawal, distress and nonverbal resistance require pause, support, relief and
assessment. A credible indication of opposition bars proceeding under UA; it
cannot be discarded as irrational or symptomatic. A clinically evidenced reflex
is not automatically a personal refusal, but uncertainty is investigated rather
than converted into consent. Newly ascertainable actual choice ends residual power.

Stop/modify at the earliest clinically safe point, minimizing harm from a procedure
already underway; this is not permission to complete a course over refusal.
Immediate rescue has its separate actual general-law necessity, force/review and
known-refusal protections. No missing court, new label or deliberate delay creates
an emergency, resets a liberty clock or expands this nonemergency permission.

**Design147 HP distinction.** [HP10/HP13](../../C10/11-ordinary-health-and-hazard-protection.md)
keeps a public-health separation/nonclinical physical-zone departure distinct from
clinical consent or unknown will. Its express environmental evacuation source
may require leaving an immediately dangerous place, never hospital admission,
medical examination/treatment or a changed personal will. UA's actual refusal,
source conditions, dependent continuity and no diagnosis-specific force remain;
HP cannot be used as a psychiatric/self-harm or life-support shortcut.
