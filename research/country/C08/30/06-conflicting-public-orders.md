# SR23–SR27. Resolve conflicting public orders without withholding relief

*Design301 · Proposed ordinary civil and public-law execution procedure.*

**SR23 — Identify the exact incompatibility and preserve ordinary action.**
Before issuing or executing a resource-specific public order, check for
known overlapping commitments in useful time without disclosing unrelated
cases. Keep both original orders, their authority and scope, statutory
deadlines, evidence, affected people and first useful starts. A genuine
conflict exists if obeying a specified operative requirement precludes
obeying another in its lawful window. Common parties, limited budgets
or a vendor's assertion do not alone establish this. Check the actual
orders and safer compatible performance, including an independent
alternate already permitted under either order. Continue all compatible
acts. The executor promptly sends both authenticated orders and the
verified conflict to both courts and affected parties through protected
intake. Either claimant, a directly affected third party or either
court can also raise it. Maintain counsel, accessible participation
and usable channels that do not depend on the public respondent.

**SR24 — Make a narrow conflict decision through existing courts.**
Each originating court may promptly clarify or vary its own order
where it already has power and both can be made compatible in useful
time. Under express prospective legislation, if that route cannot
timely resolve a genuine conflict among executable first-instance

of Appeal. Use the existing OJ02 three qualified judges and two
affirmative votes, a prepublished assignment and recusal rotation, and
lawful relief capacity. Neither originating judge joins that formation.
The respondent cannot choose the forum or gain extra time by serving
one court late. Constitutional, criminal, liberty, electoral and other
specially assigned matters keep their own jurisdiction and clocks.
Orders outside the defined scope go to their actual competent routes.

**SR25 — Change only the means needed to make protection usable.**
The conflict formation considers the request by the earlier useful
start under B17 and any earlier applicable duty. Give all materially

strict conditions and prompt review. Verify genuine conflict, less

qualified staff and actual acceptance. Check other orders or duties
that the revised means would displace. On those findings it may vary
the smallest necessary operative means or sequence within the original
lawful deadlines. A change requiring acceptance by another competent
court or operator is effective only with their timely acceptance.
Keep both underlying merits and claims, access and protections.
The order names precise scope, receiving, review and expiry. A separate
stay still needs its own actual grounds and authority. Neither
convenience nor an allocation model can choose which right lapses.
The formation cannot create staff or money, merge cases automatically,
rewrite statutory deadlines or establish a general service priority.

**SR26 — Expose a missed protection without giving the executive a stay.**
Filing, missing quorum, inaccessible evidence or absent money does not
stay either order. If lawful compatibility cannot be achieved in time,
continue every compatible step and apply only existing, expressly
applicable safety or scarcity authority within the orders and governing
law. Holding the resource does not confer priority. No first-come,
ministerial or AI selection decides between remaining requirements.
Preserve the actual missing capacity, steps taken, denial, deadlines,
any lawful mitigation and residual claims. Return to the competent
court for enforcement, factual correction, compensation where owed
or other relief within its power. Determine actual inability and
strategic withholding separately before any personal consequence.
Give each affected person a safe record and usable challenge. A late
variation or service never records earlier lost protection as received.

**SR27 — Budget for a working response and keep responsibility local.**

and accessible communication with an actual alternate route. The elected

equipment, paid handoff and the next call. Where conflicts recur,
compare accepted ordinary alternatives, duplication and redesigned orders

pronounce a conflict, decide priority, issue a variation or verify its own
receipt. Commence prospectively with original cases, appeal routes,
records and accrued claims carried to their lawful receivers. Reopen
