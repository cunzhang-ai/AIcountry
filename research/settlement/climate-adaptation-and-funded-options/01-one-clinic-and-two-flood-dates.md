# One clinic, two flood dates and a decision that cannot wait for flooding

*Design399 · Constructed comparison. Years and cash units are illustrative.*

A flood-prone district must keep a clinic and its accessible route
working. A central scenario places the first material failure at
the start of year 8. A faster plausible scenario places it at the
start of year 5. These are authored planning dates, without a
probability assigned. Earlier independent monitoring can distinguish
the branches by the start of year 2. A policy that waits for the
ordinary major review would decide in year 3. There are three
settled units for new work in year 0. Maintenance costs two of them
and protects the existing useful capacity up to the applicable
failure date in the stated scenario.

| Path | Conditions and dated resources | Latest decision in fast / central case |
|---|---|---|
| Maintain and choose regional flood storage | Preserve land access and designs now for one unit through the year 2 decision. A separately elected construction and receiving budget of six units must be settled when required. End-to-end lead time is three years. | Year 2 / year 5, subject to an actually renewed option in the later case |
| Maintain and protect the clinic and access in place | An alternative under the stated hazard only if its separate inspection, ecological floor and receiving pass. Lead time is two years. Implementation costs eight and only six can settle at the start of year 3. | Year 3 / year 6, with the additional two actually funded for the faster branch |
| Move the clinical service to a different site | A separately lawful new location and staffed access plan take four years and cost ten, including the handover. No site or funding is committed in the base case. Existing households retain their own rights. | Year 1 / year 4 |

In the fast branch, waiting until year 3 makes the regional path
one year late even if its six units later arrive. The in-place path
could still meet the date, but needs two more settled units and
qualified approval. In the central branch, the early reservation
can expire if it is no longer worthwhile. Any retained regional
option must be independently renewed or lawfully requalified by
its later decision date. Neither outcome follows from sunk cost.

Select maintenance and purchase of the limited regional option in
year 0. Retain an elected choice in year 2. This qualifies
preparation and buys a possible switch. It does not appropriate
the six construction units. If a credible faster risk appears
earlier, bring the useful decision date forward and expose any
options already lost. If neither permanent path can qualify in
time, an already authorized alternative receiving site can cover
only its first season at two separately protected units, assuming
accepted staff and travel. Its cost and subsequent duty remain
separate. A year 5 permanent failure still exists where the
alternate cannot receive or when the finite window runs out.

The case compares dates and funding. It does not estimate actual
hazards, design a flood structure or establish local readiness.
