# Design495 decision record

**Decision and basis.** Continue from the [Design494 baseline](baseline.json)
under the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md). Require independently
executable routes for protected work that depends on a common channel
before migrating that work. Prefer the least burdensome eligible ordinary
or repaired route, followed by directly scoped, funded independence.
Reject a mandatory common approval switch where its outage defeats the
actual protected acts. Real democratic authority and the resources to
enforce it are kept separate.

**Comparison and review.** Apply the [joined scenario](../settlement/opening-resilience-and-public-choice/01-govern-and-deliver-without-the-common-channel.md)
to a strong predecessor repair, independently routed repair and common
standby. Review agents analyzed handover, the financial boundary and
lived outcome comparisons using partly shared sources. This qualifies a
research choice and constructed sensitivity result. It cannot stand in
for affected participation, independent outside review or observed
operational proof. See [sources and limits](sources.md).

**Objection and compatibility.** Independently controlled routes can
duplicate scarce staff, impair privacy or bypass a useful ordinary chain.
Use proportionate, funded alternates and test the receiving end and next
call. The design adds no office, no emergency authority and no new
appropriation. It preserves existing law, files, earned claims, originally
due acts and the lawful route for disputed appointment and signed orders.
A failure to migrate does not erase an existing service failure.

**Next decision.** A competent adopter can submit dated appropriation,
authority, records and accepted staffing for one actual protected load.
Require the matched scope, paid overlap and verified next-window capacity
before relying on it. Reopen when ordinary repair meets the same gate,
when the alternate shares a controller, spills costs into other duties,
fails to protect hard-to-reach people, or causes significant new harm.
Real adoption, independent receipt and better lives remain unverified.
