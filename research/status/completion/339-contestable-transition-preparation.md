# Design431. Give opposition and new entrants a way to prepare

[HT32–HT38](../../country/C03/19/08-contestable-transition-preparation.md)
provides bounded preparation through lawful contestant eligibility,
common factual updates, protected negotiation and timely independent
review. It preserves statutory work and the constitutional cutover.

[Worked comparison](../../settlement/contestable-transition-preparation/01-three-contestants-and-a-late-correction.md)
tests a late entrant, separated staff and a delayed correction.
Actual staffing, electoral effects and authorization remain open.
