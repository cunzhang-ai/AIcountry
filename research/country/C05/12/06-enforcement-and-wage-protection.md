# WL31–WL35: there must be a real claim, payer and independent judge

*Part of [C05/12](../12-work-contracts-and-collective-power.md); design 117 candidate.*

**WL31 — Protected careers, elected policy.** Cabinet separately appoints a
Work Standards Director and Work Relations Registrar through C04 open, qualified
public shortlists, usable objections/reasons/conflicts and direct ordinary review.
Require actual labor/evidence/fair-hearing and accountable-management competence,
with equivalent practical experience, no sole guild, party or AI gate. Employer/
union executive, minister/legislator or conflicted paid advocate cannot serve
simultaneously. These are protected career posts, not Cabinet pleasure, new
sovereign boards or automatically expiring election mandates. Cabinet is the
actual disciplinary owner: proved misconduct, persistent material failure or
inability after reasonable accommodation, usable notice/reply/reasons and B16/B17
independent challenge. An adverse case, lawful dissent or change of government
is no removal ground; policy direction cannot dictate a named case or ballot.

Each head recruits its own qualified career staff under C04 and disciplines
with the same safeguards. Standards officers investigate/correct labor duties;
Registrar officers determine lawful units/process/recognition and administer
neutral ballots/dispute help. Each has own statutory signatures and protected
source access, conflict allocation and predesignated qualified substitutes.
No examiner decides their own contested case or selects their reviewer; disputed
merits/punishment go to independent ordinary courts, not a director's final veto.
A head's own conflict goes directly to unconflicted courts. Each deputy may do
routine funded management for at most180 elapsed days, no serial acting reset,
new general policy or discretionary senior appointments. Existing statutory case/
payer officers retain own functions after expiry; no missing management power is
invented. Cabinet opens unexpected vacancy recruitment within14 days, planned
succession beforehand. First establishment permits funded qualified case officers
through existing C04 recruitment before heads, without bypassing head competition.
This completes V10 for these two work offices only; other appointments stay intact.

**WL32 — Precise bounded enforcement.** Existing V03 warrants/access and V04
specific imminent-harm stop/expiry apply, with PD protected evidence and actual
control/ability. A worker's ordinary report receives assistance, not dossier
rejection or compulsory employer consent. V05 correction and direct TW/B relief
are separate from punishment. Apply only actually enacted VD03 wage/hour/rest/
collective/safety predicates with V06/V07/P fault, notice/correction, defenses and
V08 one-course/maxima. No new VD14, fine base, prison or inspector-imposed final
penalty is created. Technical contract-form defects, a disputed good-faith status
or union dissent are not automatic offenses; no analogy extends the schedule.
Fines fund no pursuing office and never substitute for paid wages/restored work.

**WL33 — Reachable work courts and claims.** Existing independent civilian
courts hear status, contracts, recognition, protection, exit/pay and action cases
on actual full merits, with qualified assigned labor capacity/aid and B17 earlier
useful-time relief. No new employer/union-appointed final tribunal, mandatory
arbitration, internal exhaustion or preclaim fee. B37's six-year wage-installment
rule and B15/B37 discovery/tolling govern; no new short dismissal deadline destroys
blocked claims. A usable timely claim to either statutory work office preserves
its date for assisted competent court transfer. Parties get safe evidence/reply,
representation, lawful appeal and no punitive costs merely for losing a good-faith
claim. B16's public appointment/administrative-annulment clock and constitutional
office procedures retain actual scope/tolling; a civil work claim does not reset
them or grant appointment authority. Decisions trace actual employer/host/state debtor, amounts/acts, dates,
source and actual enforcement/receipt. A court cannot invent a job or appropriation.

**WL34 — A limited general wage advance, explicitly selected.** After actually
due wages/paid leave or due notice pay remains unpaid for seven calendar days,
Treasury's named protection payer must provide the undisputed established eligible
advance within seven calendar days of a usable assisted request, with I/E earlier
hardship protection. Formal employer insolvency is not a prerequisite. Eligible
amount is unpaid accrued ordinary wages/paid leave and due notice pay, not alleged
damages, redundancy severance or every future salary. At each claim cap it at the
smaller of eight representative ordinary-pay weeks and sixteen normal-stage weekly
minimum cash wages (`16 × enacted normal weekly hours × lawful hourly minimum`),
less actual general advances received in the previous365 days across employers.
The receipt window is the365 elapsed days ending at the claim, excluding a
receipt exactly365 days before it and including prior receipts on the claim day.
Preserve any established larger advance right or applicable higher prior-period
cap for this same claim until matched actual receipt; late administration or
a TW stage change cannot cut that protection. An established unpaid award is
not reduced again by earlier receipts already reconciled when it was decided.
Match subsequent actual same-item receipts once; no award exceeds its real
unpaid protected claim merely by renaming a cap. No negative award or retroactive
debt if a later cap falls. Counts follow actual
people/receipts, not accounts, agents or split jobs. Missing original pay/rate/status
needs assisted provisional review, not an AI denial or an invented statutory rate.
The whole employer debt remains; the cap is only on this new general advance.
Full state-employer duties and AS12's higher specific backing are not capped by it.

**WL35 — Source and recovery do not pay themselves.** National general revenue
funds WL34 gross advances, independent administration/aid/courts and actual early
cash under an express Assembly appropriation, not a guessed levy or recoveries.
Coordinate TA/Y/I actual wage substitution/withholding, period and irregular receipt;
no wage or tax paid twice, assistance as its own resource, or phantom past receipt.
Treat the gross advance as payment on behalf of the employer of the matched wage/
leave/notice claim, retaining its original TA/Y classification/period; Treasury
withholds/remits the lawfully due employee amounts inside gross. Uncovered employer
contribution duties remain separately owed, not silently guaranteed by this scheme.
For I05 the actually received net labor-payment advance enters E with its existing
disregard/withdrawal, not O/A. The I05 award itself is never its own resource.
For I07 count only actual safely usable net resources in the applicable receipt
month and its existing irregular-receipt treatment, never unpaid historic wages
or the same employer repayment to Treasury as a second worker receipt.
An actually received same-item advance matches an AS/public/employer payment once;
I07's actual usable resources and distinct assistance treatment retain source law.

Treasury receives only actual matched subrogation; it cannot automatically seize
an innocent worker/recipient's floor. In prospective U distributions its advance
recovery is ordinary unsecured, after remaining worker-priority claims, with old
security/title/earlier source rights intact. Expected recovery is not available
cash. B18 handles actual private debt, and an express coordinated Act places
established WL34/state pay judgments in adopted B19 payer/appropriation/executor
scope. A defendant minister cannot block a binding due payment; cash/worker shortage
retains unmet claims and requires competent elected sources, not new bailiff tax,
protected-fund/FX sweep, general damages/firm guarantee or compulsory work.

[BC14](../../C10/14/03-claims-and-bounded-enforcement.md) expressly proposes

Director/officers, including actual covered home-buyer delegation abroad.
Qualified assigned expertise/paid source and unconflicted direct courts are
additional. This is no overseas raid, employer-status presumption, new VD crime
or extension of WL34 advances to all supplier wages. Original WL duties remain.
