# MW01–MW08. Deliver municipal waste service through verified receiving

*Design394 · Proposed ordinary law within [sanitation and waste](../06-sanitation-and-waste.md).*

**MW01 — Give residents an accessible collection floor.** The elected
local council assigns a named Solid-Waste Steward and funds published
service by area and stream. Offer covered households a safe route for
food waste, dry recyclables and ordinary residual waste. As proposed
starting standards, collect food waste at least weekly and residual
waste at least every two weeks. Use earlier collection where health
or storage requires it. An equivalent communal or other local route
needs independent health and access review and actual daily
availability where relied on. Include flats, informal or temporary
homes, people without formal addresses and residents who cannot carry
bins. Publish staffed routes for bulky and separately controlled
household materials. Clinical, industrial and other regulated streams
retain their applicable handling and payment duties.

**MW02 — Fund the ordinary floor before using price incentives.**
Use voted local revenue and national equalization for the common floor.
Reconcile any adopted producer payment against its actual attributable
service cost. Provide the ordinary covered household collection
without a point-of-use charge or debt cutoff. A council may vote a
prospective charge on measurable extra residual service beyond an
adequate baseline only after providing usable alternatives, an
inability-to-pay adjustment and a no-penalty allowance for unavoidable
extra waste from care, disability or child needs. Keep an accessible
route for requests, corrections and ordinary billing review. Test
exclusion, dumping and unrecorded transfers before expansion. Shared
bins do not establish individual fault. Preserve sanitation during
collection disputes and use existing proportionate civil enforcement
with proof and independent challenge.

**MW03 — Separate collection, custody, recovery and disposal.** The
operator records streams, source scope, weights, dates, lawful
custodians, transport and an accepting licensed destination. Reconcile
starting stock and receipts with verified reuse or material recovery,
separately authorized energy recovery, safe disposal, rejected or
returned material, documented processing losses, closing stock and
unresolved differences. Check units, measurement uncertainty and
reporting periods. Keep each mass balance at its own perimeter and
deduplicate transfers. An output with an unverified next receiver
remains open. Verify actual material recovery with independent sample
or receiving evidence. A dispatch to a sorting site or an export does
not itself qualify. Apply LCY to product responsibility and keep the
same tonnes and proceeds from being claimed twice.

**MW04 — Give host communities a real decision and remedy.** The
competent planning authority and independent environmental permit
body apply their distinct existing powers. Before binding a material
site or transfer route, disclose cumulative local exposure, transport
burdens, capacity, rejected alternatives and full closure costs.
People living or working near a site or on its route receive usable
notice, participation and direct review, including affected residents
across local boundaries. An elected authority states its reasons and
actual scope. Regional pooling needs lawful joint agreement and actual
receiving. A host payment cannot purchase permission to exceed health
or environmental limits. No facility receives a guaranteed waste
volume that overrides lawful prevention and repair duties.

**MW05 — Buy and test an alternate before accepting dependence.**
The Steward books finite compatible capacity for collection, temporary
lawful containment, transport and destination receiving. Test loss
of a major facility or operator alongside a shared loss of power,
vehicles or payment. Name the hazard-based latest safe collection,
storage and treatment windows under applicable standards. Count
all prior users and the next call before replenishment. Include
contracts, actual funds, safe staffing, notice, interim access and
worker protection. A site with free nominal tonnage cannot take a
stream outside its permit, safety limit or accepted schedule.

**MW06 — Keep the public receiving duty through failure.** On a material
loss, the Steward activates the already funded alternate, verifies
each receiving step and tells residents and workers where safe access
continues. The competent health or environmental officer can exercise
existing lawful stop or protection powers independently. If local
capacity fails, the designated national sector owner must take up
the unresolved essential duty through existing lawful emergency or
equalization sources and accepted capacity. Record any remaining
missed collection, unsafe accumulation or unaccepted stock. Use
existing priority law where capacity conflicts. No export, unfunded
order or improvised dumping satisfies the duty. An operator dispute
or insolvency does not close the resident's claim.

**MW07 — Secure closure and continuing protection separately.** A
covered new or materially enlarged disposal or treatment site must
provide enforceable, independently collectible assurance for defined
closure, maintenance, monitoring and corrective obligations on a
third-party cost basis. Review after material scope or risk change
and at least annually. Public ownership requires an equally
inspectable paid provision, with lawful alternatives to a private
bond. The independent permit body verifies control of funds, actual
draw conditions, ownership transfer and continuing duties. Keep
closure provision distinct from routine operations and alternate
receiving. Use CLN and ordinary remedies for releases, historic
liabilities and insolvent successors. An estate or title transfer
does not release security until an independent review establishes
an equivalent lawful successor or complete performance.

**MW08 — Verify receipt, correct failure and migrate lawfully.** Offer
direct accessible reports and time-bounded correction under AP and
sector law. Publish safe results for missed service, stocks, verified
destinations, closure provisions and host effects. The inspector
can independently check a municipal or contracted operator, and
courts retain their own review and execution. AI can assist routing,
compare records and flag missing evidence. It cannot assign household
fault, authorize a transfer, certify recovery or lift a safety stop.
Adopt prospectively with paid operator and records succession,
separate sanitation and producer duties, and all existing claims
and deadlines preserved. Reopen on repeated missed service, unsafe
host exposure, unverified destinations or a stronger funded alternative.
