# Belonging: durable residence and voluntary citizenship

*Canonical MB04–MB06; part of [C02/06](../06-citizenship-and-secure-belonging.md).*

**MB04 — Four years creates a stable home.** After four continuous years of
actual habitual residence, secure residence vests by law: indefinite permission
to remain, work, leave and return, independent of employer/partner, with no
renewal purchase, income or language test. Refugee, asylum, disputed/irregular
status, childhood and lawful custody time count where actual habitual residence
is established. A clerk declares this entitlement; delay cannot reset it.
Wrongful state removal or coerced absence does not interrupt qualifying time.

Habitual residence is a real sustained home/life connection, assessed from
actual evidence rather than nationality, tenure, home ownership or one exclusive
global center. Nomadic, shared-home and cross-border lives need individual proof.
Absences with none exceeding six consecutive months and at most twelve aggregate
months during the four-year
period presumptively preserve residence where the home connection continues;
longer absence can do so on proved continuing home, care, study, displacement
or medical circumstances. A genuine break before vesting starts a new four-year
period, rather than pausing accumulated months; protected temporary/coerced absence
is not that break. This continuity choice retains a cost to mobile people and
the cumulative-residence rival. Already vested secure status survives. Record reasons
and independent challenge. The separate [national electoral qualification](../03/01-resident-franchise-and-secure-status.md)
uses one continuous year and preserves eligibility on actual habitual return
for people previously qualified. Protected electorate changes obey C14.

Secure status does not expire by card expiry, job loss, divorce, criminal
conviction, political dissent or later overseas residence. Crime has the ordinary
P/K process; banishment is not another sentence. A secure resident can voluntarily
relinquish this permission under MB08 safeguards. Return rights do not pay an
income abroad or confer a nonresident noncitizen vote; actual-residence/service
and political rules retain their own predicates. Different citizen diaspora
rights remain an explicit asymmetry, mitigated by accessible voluntary citizenship.

**MB05 — Naturalization is an entitlement, not a favor.** An adult who attained
MB04's residence period may apply and obtain citizenship through a reasoned,
non-discretionary determination, including from abroad after secure status vested.
Naturalization is free. No wealth purchase, employer nomination, military service, political oath,
language/culture exam, health test or criminal-good-character veto is added.
Provide accessible legal information and supported personal choice. Prove identity
and residence with MB09/MB16 assistance; neither an inaccessible foreign certificate
nor a model score can be the exclusive gate. Existing residence/franchise rights
continue while citizenship is decided. There is no ministerial exceptional-grant
or group-naturalization power outside these enacted entitlement grounds.

**MB06 — Children do not wait unnecessarily.** A minor obtaining at least one
lawful citizen parent by adoption or genuinely later parentage has an immediate
protected application entitlement under MB03's parental residence/otherwise-
stateless connection, without a four-year child wait. A minor whose legal parent
naturalizes also qualifies immediately. Citizenship takes effect at actual
operative conferral/judgment, not a fictitious birth or adoption date. At sixteen
the young person gives their own supported application/assent; below sixteen
use the protected carer/independent-child route below. Hear concrete interests,
disclose material foreign-status consequences and preserve other nationality
where this country controls the rule. No forced surrender or automatic later loss.

A child who completed four habitual years can apply independently at sixteen;
below sixteen, use supported application with a lawful carer or an independent
child representative where the carer is absent, conflicted or obstructs protection.
The child court resolves actual contested authority/interests under B, not presumed
family ownership. An otherwise-stateless child habitually living here acquires
citizenship upon reliable recognition of that condition, without a residence
waiting period or parental veto. Provisional identity/care continue during proof.
