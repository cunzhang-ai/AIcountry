# One discharge and one urgent bed

Design364 · Constructed comparison under
[HDC01–HDC06](../../country/C07/05/06-hospital-to-community-and-safe-receiving.md).

A is clinically ready to leave inpatient care at hour 10, needs a
qualified first act by hour 14 and would prefer to return home.
The ordinary home route can accept essential care only at hour 26.
A relative is unavailable and has accepted no duty. B needs an
independently assessed acute bed by hour 18. The ward can admit B
by hour 17 if the existing bed is released and remains staffed. An
alternative acute slot has its own staff, can accept by hour 16
and requires 4 cash units by hour 8.

| Route in the declared case | Dated additional cost | A's receiving | B's receiving |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Book an ordinary ride home | 2 by hour 8 | Home care unavailable until hour 26, twelve hours late | Existing staffed ward can accept |
| Accepted temporary recovery place | 5 by hour 8 | Arrive by hour 11; accepted care by hour 13 | Existing staffed ward can accept |
| Voluntary staffed ward stay and separate acute placement | 2 + 4 by hour 8 | Ward can provide the required act in time if A accepts the stay | Separate funded acute slot can accept |

The temporary place is clinically appropriate and acceptable to A
in this example. It has confirmed transport and an independently
staffed team. With 5 early units available, select that path. The
follow-up contract is priced in the same 5 and covers the declared
window until the home route accepts. The named transition lead
verifies actual acceptance at hour 26 and retains the case if it
fails. No vacant bed or completed ride is counted as a received
service until it meets the relevant need.

With only 4 early units, the temporary place cannot be dispatched.
A voluntary ward stay costs 2, leaving only 2 for the 4-unit
separate acute placement. Use existing urgent pathways and disclose
B's unresolved need unless a separately authorized source and actual
alternate can be found. The example assumes no other qualified
route is ready. A late transfer or unused restricted source cannot
fill the hour-8 gap. If A lawfully chooses to leave, do not count the
stay. Keep reachable support and original claims. A blocked transfer
gives no authority to detain A or treat B as no longer due.

These values show the consequences of simultaneous duties and one
funding loss. They give no real clinical recommendation, bed norm,
appropriation, transport plan or cost estimate.
