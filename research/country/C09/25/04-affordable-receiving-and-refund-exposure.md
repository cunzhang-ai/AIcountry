# HL13–HL17. Open housing offers that residents can afford to keep

*Design282 · Proposed extension to [housing loss and secure receiving](../25-housing-loss-and-secure-receiving.md).*

The first housing budget should reserve usable homes and the means to
keep them, alongside the existing safe-night duty. A funded support
programme can run below plan when suitable homes are unavailable.
A finance plan based on reimbursed activity must carry that exposure.

**HL13 — Count a net suitable offer against the right obligation.** The
local housing steward checks actual lawful control, access date,
condition, accessibility, rent terms, location and willingness to accept
among the residents to whom an offer could be made. Use the existing
lawful allocation order and private records. Keep protected independent
review of eligibility and matching. Count the unit once after checking
existing tenants, required rehousing, shared reserves and displaced
allocations. A commitment to nominate, a funded reservation and an
accepted offer are different stages. Before an exact cohort opens,
identify the provider and usable dates without requiring residents to
pre-commit. A resident may refuse an unsuitable offer without losing
ordinary priority or safe receiving.

**HL14 — Qualify affordability through the first loss of support.** The
steward and income office identify full lawful housing charges, deposit,
utilities and material access costs. Use actual eligible and usable
resources and the [income protection](../../C05/06-income-floor-and-safe-resources.md)
and housing rules. Check resources left for ordinary needs over the
offer term and at the first known expiry or reduction of assistance.
Assume no income rise without evidence. Show the authorized protection
that will reach the resident if a known gap remains. Compare a lower
rent offer, an expressly funded transition and durable rent or income
support with their actual incidence. A future shortfall triggers an
offer repair and continued ordinary help. It does not remove the person
from the lawful housing queue or require proof of future self-sufficiency.
Check affordability with the resident and a usable route to correction.
Keep support voluntary and separate from tenure.

**HL15 — Price lower delivery and a lost reimbursement together.** The
national funder and local payer present gross first payments, recurring
net cost, the statutory claim base, actual receipt dates and the cost
of a bridge. Use [MF09–MF13](../../C04/08/03-reimbursement-and-resident-receipt.md)
and [FB31–FB35](../../C06/62/06-prevention-across-public-budgets.md).
Test fewer suitable offers, lower eligible activity, continuing interim
work and a delayed or denied claim together. An underspend tied to a
blocked service stays visible under FB09. Keep any restricted balances
and continuing claims separate. A bounded advance or own-source bridge
can protect the first due payment if authorized and actually available.
Reconcile it against the final public settlement once. A later loss of
reimbursement cannot reverse a valid lease, earned pay or support due.

**HL16 — Choose between a quick match and durable supply.** Compare
using qualified existing homes, negotiating limited new allocation rights
or rent terms, and a separate acquisition, conversion or construction
motion where the market cannot supply suitable homes. Price actual
displacement, durable rent, land, upkeep, delivery time and rights. The
first programme can pay for bounded preparation and verified reservations
where those have an independent lawful source and value. Pay only the
named contractual scope. State the cap, exit and duty to restore a
failed route. Fund the exact cohort and support before binding. Bring
a smaller or changed offer back under T unless its exact terms already
have effective authority. Keep first safe-night receiving, prevention
and existing awards on their original sources and clocks.

**HL17 — Reconcile the result from allocation to retained housing.**
The housing steward reports the stages separately, from proposed and
returned units to offers, accepted moves, support actually received and
retained housing after the first funding change. Show missing coverage,
refusals, unsuitable offers, displaced residents, costs and executed
remedies. Independent reviewers can inspect safely held source evidence.
AI may reconcile offer dates and forecast scenarios with source references.
It may neither classify someone as unworthy of a home nor authorize a
lease or fund transfer. Review at the first funding change and earlier on
a material loss of income, funds, supply or safe access.
