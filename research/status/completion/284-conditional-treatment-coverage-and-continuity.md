# Design376. Fund conditional treatment through review and exit

[CTC01–CTC07](../../country/C07/14/01-conditional-treatment-coverage-and-continuity.md)
adds a bounded coverage decision, independent evidence appraisal and
a funded, individually safe transition before admissions begin.

The [official activity and worked case](../../settlement/conditional-treatment-coverage-and-continuity/01-two-patients-and-a-funded-exit.md)
distinguishes notifications, drug costs, actual treatment and
reappraisal. A later cash or receiving gap defeats an otherwise
fundable start. Adoption, staffing, individual participation and
actual outcomes remain open.
