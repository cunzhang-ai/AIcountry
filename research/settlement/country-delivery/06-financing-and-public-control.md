## Compare paired transactions before claiming savings

All cases buy one50-CU service package and pay50 cash support. The producer pays
30 wages and20 foreign units (FU) for inputs at1CU/FU. Five cases collect30 tax
and finance70; the tax-only case collects100 and intentionally changes household
burdens. Opening200 of household deposits, bank reserves and separately owned
central-bank FX claims are explicit matched hypotheses, not free new wealth.
A willing authorized CB sale supplies20FU; Treasury cannot seize those reserves.
Nonfinancial assets and foreign input production costs are outside this example.

| Regime | Final household claims / change from opening | Consolidated G+CB primary financial change | Marginal70 interest at authored5% |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Households buy G term bonds |180 deposits+70 bonds /+50 |−70 |3.5 to households |
| Bank buys G term bonds |250 deposits /+50 |−70 |3.5 to bank |
| Fiscal issue, zero reserve rate |250 deposits /+50 |−70 |0 under changed operating conditions |
| Fiscal issue, paid reserves |250 deposits /+50 |−70 |3.5 to bank |
| Fiscal issue, then CB sells70 bills |250 deposits /+50 |−70 |3.5 to bank |
| Tax100, no new financing |180 deposits /−20 |0 |0, with70 more household tax |

The first five cases leave domestic private net financial positions +50 and
the foreign supplier +20. G+CB takes−70. Bonds change asset composition and
liquidity; they do not subtract70 from private net financial wealth. This is
net financial accumulation, not proof of gross saving, consumption, voluntary
portfolio demand or productive capacity. Aggregation does not establish an
acceptable distribution among people. The tax-only row is not socially equivalent.

Paid-reserve issue and interest-bearing bills do not automatically remove the
interest burden. Including180 of remaining common reserves, the full end-stock
5% sensitivities are12.5 for both bond cases and both paid-issue cases; tax-only
is9. Zero-rate issue gives0 but changes remuneration on existing as well as new
reserves; it does not establish implementation of a positive policy rate.
Interest is accrued/not financed or paid in the marginal ledger, never recycled
as free revenue. Bank income is not customer income without actual pass-through.
The new G grant improves G's standalone account while CB's financial position
falls70; consolidation cannot conceal that exposure or confer spending authority.

Bank-created deposits are not simply relending earlier savings
([Bank of England, 2014](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/quarterly-bulletin/2014/q1/money-creation-in-the-modern-economy)).
Central-bank negative equity does not by itself prevent operations
([BIS Bulletin68, 2023](https://www.bis.org/publications/bulletin-68-why-are-central-banks-reporting-losses-does-it-matter)).
Neither proposition establishes this proposed country's safe fiscal scale.
A conventional monetary-finance model reports contingent stimulus and credibility/
inflation risks, not a universal outcome
([Federal Reserve research, 2017](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/feds/money-financed-fiscal-programs-a-cautionary-tale.htm)).

## Real and foreign constraints survive every financing label

If the foreign supplier requires20FU and CU depreciates from1 to1.5CU/FU,
unchanged inputs cost30CU. The old50-CU producer invoice then loses10; full cost
pass-through makes the service invoice60 and gross G use110, still for one
package. At unchanged30 tax the nominal gap is80. A lender/loss absorber or
lawfully paid reprice must bear the difference; none appears by revaluation.
These are authored cost sensitivities, not exchange predictions or a complete
revaluation ledger. With zero available owned/willing FX, all six cases fail
this import payment unless a real accepted alternative is found. No CU issue
forces conversion, a private exporter or a foreign supplier to consent.

Qualified labor, equipment, land/ecology and safe receiving remain separate.
Inflation can affect necessary baskets, rents, wages, debts and tax bands
unequally. Recalculate indexed I05/Y and actual essential bills with their
lawful source rules; do not call a fixed nominal transfer constant protection.
No output-gap estimate, aggregate price index or tax receipt proves that each
person receives essentials. Demand/investment, saving and exchange responses
remain uncalibrated; this ledger cannot rank the regimes by wellbeing.

## A substantive fiscal-money rival, with powers kept separate

The alternative would require **new express primary legislation**. It is a
12-month maximum *new-principal authorization*, with an enacted numerical gross
issue ceiling, eligible appropriated G uses, named settlement accounts and
published timing, reserve/bill-interest exposure and elected loss/financing plan.
Renewal needs fresh primary law; no index, model, tax return, rollover or missed
vote renews the ceiling. Repayment or tax does not reset gross authority unless
that law expressly chooses and explains a different net limit. These bounds are
research hypotheses, not an empirical optimal amount or current legal permission.

The Assembly decides fiscal recipients and burdens through ordinary appropriated
law and opposition scrutiny; Treasury requests verified authorized payments.
Existing MC career operators execute within the separately enacted mandate;
CB cannot select political beneficiaries, subsidize an affiliate or create an
indemnity. Monetary rate/operations judgment remains with MC under its own law.
A rate change cannot secretly become a new fiscal grant or cancel an old promise.
Appropriation alone is not issuance authority; issuance alone is not an appropriation.

Publish paired G/CB accounts, sources, actual settlement and disaggregated harms,
with protected original records, independent audit, funded opposition access and
ordinary full review/execution of legal limits. Courts can order a determined
lawful administrative payment/correction under actual law, not set policy rates,
invent FX or grant new principal. Technical disagreement must be published to
the elected body; it is neither a fiscal-policy veto nor automatic permission
to exceed the law. The incumbent must face the same caps, audit and effective
peaceful replacement as a challenger would.

Expiry stops new issue, not deposits, valid contracts, accrued interest, basic
rights or elections. Existing liabilities retain their sources and execution;
MC25–28 loss disclosure/continuity remains separate from recapitalization.
A failure triggers lawful interim protection and elected source/capacity choices,
not compulsory conversion, austerity by CB, renewed emergency power or arrears
renamed financing. Positive inflation/FX warnings trigger scrutiny and a public
choice under existing law, not an unelected office's new power to cut claims.
