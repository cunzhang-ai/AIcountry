# PD13–PD16: secure adequate records and replaceable future media

*Design 96 ordinary-data research candidate; not enacted law, funded readiness or
demonstrated superiority.*

**PD13 — Security and incident protection.** Apply role-limited access, encryption where
proportionate, tested backup/recovery and vendor separation. Report a breach presenting
material risk to people’s rights or safety to the information-rights supervisor within
72 hours of reasonable awareness; where personal harm is likely material, notify
affected people without undue delay and within 72 elapsed hours of reasonable awareness,
with concrete account/claim protection and earlier useful-time action. A brief specific
independent delay may protect an active lawful investigation, never conceal an agency's
embarrassment. C10 retains separate cyber recovery and no new surveillance power follows
a breach. Notify in phases if scope is still being established; uncertainty is reported,
not hidden until perfect forensics. Protect contact details and other subjects rather
than mass publishing victims. A processor informs affected controllers without undue
delay; each retains its notices/protection duty. A justified notice delay needs the
specific court-reviewed ground and expiry in PD25, not controller embarrassment. C10
shares one bounded technical core; controllers separately protect their own users.

**PD14 — High-risk and small controllers.** Before large-scale sensitive processing,
biometric/neural identification, cross-context profiling or consequential automated
decisions, the controller records necessity, alternatives, disparate harm, security,
retained human capacity, supplier control, affected access and how to stop/repair; a
protected public summary and independent challenge are available. This assessment is no
permission to breach PD04–PD08 and no AI office grants a general licence. A small
low-risk provider may use a simple purpose/recipient/retention notice and reasonable
safeguards rather than an enterprise dossier, but must still answer an affected person
and correct harm. Off-the-shelf software or a trade-secret clause cannot hide a legally
consequential source. Where residual risk cannot be brought within the enacted limits and safeguards,
do not start that new processing. Seek specific advice/review for a lawful redesign
or challenge; supervisor advice never waives the base, protection or sector law; assessment is
not self-approval, a broad technology licence or an extension of existing due services.
Existing sector permissions remain separate.

**PD15 — Minimal *adequate* records.** Collect the least identifying material that can
still prove a claim, preserve safe care, correct a mistake and execute a remedy. The
sector source custodian keeps the original fact, provenance, necessary limitation and
correction history under a published retention schedule. A consequential decision record
keeps applicable rule/model version, source references, disclosure recipients,
claim/receipt and review status for the lawful challenge period. Permitted routine
positive/reversible automation records its human-authorized rule/version and machine
trace; adverse or individually reviewed decisions require an attributed case-specific
human reason. No fresh case reviewer is invented for every uncontested payment. It does not
replicate every source record into one state directory. The public evidence service
receives only its lawful purpose-limited access and aggregates, not a duplicate power to
decide eligibility. A justified legal hold protects relevant evidence with restricted
access; indefinite universal storage is no substitute for a claim. Multiple model
reports derived from one source family are one source of fact, not independent
corroboration.

**PD16 — Future media, proof and mediation.** Allow local processing and selective
attribute proofs for a defined fact when an issuer, verifier, revocation method, expiry,
evidence quality and accessible fallback are known. A proof of age, residency or income
band is not proof of clinical need, citizenship, current consent, legal mandate or
payment receipt. Avoid a stable cross-sector identifier where a purpose-specific receipt
suffices; keep identified source and payee records where a claim/earned right requires
them. Speech, gesture, shared activity and agent messages may carry an intention; an
accountable human/institution still interprets operative meaning. A personal agent gets
a separate scoped revocable mandate and a separate lawful data-use ground; no copied
agent or generic training checkbox may expand either. Direct, assisted and offline
channels have equivalent legal standing. Public interoperability preserves meaning,
objections, continuing obligations and data protections, not one compulsory national
app. Selective proofs/local computation remain optional methods, not the legal
architecture or a compulsory wallet. Prove issuer/source, current scoped meaning and
recipient authority separately; a valid signature establishes neither true facts nor
authorization. Evaluate correlation through metadata, shared issuers and repeated use;
fewer disclosed fields do not establish anonymity. A new sensory or collaborative medium
preserves interpretation, dissent, consent and continuing commitments instead of forcing
every relationship into a single schema.
