# A minority government has to choose a feasible first offer

This exercise is authored. The legislature, sources, staff and service
options illustrate the proposed country. They do not describe Canadian
or Dutch elections or a financed national offer.

## First choose who holds office

The Assembly has 100 statutory seats. Assume the prior-law majority
formation phase closed without a winner and the 47-seat coalition's
candidate has the highest qualifying support under the existing fallback.
On the separate tolerance vote, 47 support, 45 oppose and eight abstain.
The candidate takes office under the assumed valid formation law.
The same votes would not pass the first budget under DB04, which needs
at least 51 actual yes votes. Five other elected members later support
the specific repair budget, giving it 52 yes votes. Their support is
for that measure. It neither guarantees another vote nor settles a
later confidence question.
