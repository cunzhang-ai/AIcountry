# Design234 decision record. Stable child care and adult transition

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned Design233](baseline.json)

## Decision

Select [YC01–YC12](../country/C07/27-stable-child-care-and-adult-transition.md).
The predecessor B31–B32 already sets a high bar for removal, safe
acceptance, review and stable family care. C07 and NP supply a general
paid residual, XR protects cross-local services and B21 preserves adult
agency. The new decision specifies protected placement availability,
who continues delivery and payment through moves, and the voluntary
support due when child protection authority ends. Use the
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

## Compare real arrangements

| Option | Strong reason to select it | Material objection |
|---|---|---|
| Retain existing child and general adult duties, improve referral and procurement | Less administrative overhead. Existing effective provision or safe family help can avoid a costly new place. | A directory cannot supply a staffed place. The existing general adult offer need not have an accepted successor on the birthday. |
| Paid local public residual plus plural qualified care and voluntary adult continuation | Responsible local service, stable relationships and paid fallback. Different people can choose different modes. | Standby costs and recruitment can be substantial. Local loyalty can hide unsafe care or concentrate placement power. Protect outside access and independently inspect public operators. |
| Territorial or national direct public provision | Pools scarce specialist capability, allows durable planning and reduces some purchasing dependence. | Long routes, monopoly and failed exits remain possible. A qualified direct-public or joint route may be the best local capacity. |
| Adult cash allowance and a private housing search | Supports autonomy and can work where ordinary supply and advisers are already accessible. | A cash offer can leave someone without housing on the due date. Keep payment and actual accepted housing distinct. |

Select the second arrangement. Prefer safe continuity and the child's
actual voice to throughput targets. A genuine safe family return can
displace a placement when its own evidentiary and authority tests are
satisfied. The age 21 continuation and age 25 transition boundaries
are authored proposals. Their usefulness, cost and possible cliff
effects require separate qualification. General adult rights and
accrued claims retain their own dates.

## Authority, evidence and migration

The Assembly appropriates the common floor, local authorities and
qualified providers deliver, and the courts control the existing
compulsory powers and resolve contested cases. The independent care
inspection chain applies the new bounded sector remit only after
proper enactment. Staff need separate appointment, records and
protected finance. The child's voice and the parent's procedural
rights remain distinct. AI may help with schedule feasibility,
access and evidence comparison. It cannot provide consent, findings
of harm, an individual placement or a legally sufficient remedy.

The [two-cohort example](../settlement/stable-child-care/02-two-cohorts-and-a-first-budget.md)
separates a nominal place, lawful accepted receiving, next use and
cash available through actual replacement. The comparators support
testing these distinctions. They give no local availability, age
effect or universal case preference. Preserve FT/OP/T successors,
source and rights with original dates and safe records. Preserve
B31 hearings, the B32 relationship and adoption tests, B21 adult
agency, and ordinary S/AS, housing, health and XR authority. No live
law, budget or placements change.

## Reopen on actual receiving evidence

Use one local and one host service to test a protected sibling
placement and an actual voluntary transition near age 18. Compare
retain/repair, a paid local pair and a qualified out-of-area route
with school, contact, travel, night cover and the next case. Obtain
safe affected input and original cost and receiving evidence before
asserting independent execution. Reopen on blocked voice, unsafe
continuity, poor actual access at an age boundary, funding failure,
or a better complete alternative. Actual affected authorization,
qualified readiness and better lived outcomes remain open.
