# 210. Local funding and population change

[Design302](../../2026-11-local-funding-and-population-change/decision-record.md)
selects a common-floor cost and representative capacity method with
a distinct date-based bridge. [MF14–MF18](../../country/C04/08/04-funding-through-population-change.md)
specifies assessment, revision, protected installments, caps and
independent correction. Local delivery authority and earlier valid
claims continue.

The [two-locality comparison](../../settlement/local-funding-and-population-change/README.md)
shows that a qualified annual allocation can still miss a service
deadline. A capped advance requires its own authority and actual
receipt. Local and national aggregate estimates, participation,
funding and comparative delivery remain unqualified.
