# PB05–PB08: actual co-residence is neither a wallet nor a mandate

*Part of [C05/14](../14-ordinary-life-and-household-production.md); design 124.*

**PB05 — Choose an explicit occupancy case, not a national household rule.**
For the next authored reference use 20,000 single-adult homes,20,000 two-adult homes
and 10,000 two-adult/two-child homes. Age/type rows reconcile 65,000 working-age,
15,000 older and 20,000 children, preserving 123's population; no employment,
caregiving, biological family, political electorate or joint-wallet mandate is
inferred. Living alone, chosen community, changing relationships and unfamiliar
forms remain genuine alternatives. This case's 50,000 homes is not a cap or an
instruction to relocate actual people. Reopen it if preferences/needs differ.

**PB06 — Keep all retained structures and title obligations.** Match 30,000
owner-occupied,16,000 private-rental and 4,000 public-rental homes. Of the old 75,000
structures, the other 25,000 are explicitly unoccupied private units in this
hypothesis;71,000 private/4,000 public counts reconcile. Unoccupied is not free,
safe, available, legally transferable or suitable for another person. Owner
repair/renewal/insurance/site obligations continue. Actual tenancy/mortgage,
worker/creditor, public-use and foreign claims are preserved, not reset by a case.

**PB07 — Reprice shared resources from households and people separately.**
The declared household recipe is 1,800 kWh/home plus 400 kWh/person, giving 130GWh,
and 12 m 3/home plus 36 m 3/person, giving 4.2 millionm 3. Purchaser quotes imply 21.450 m
power,8.400 m potable water and 15.000 m home broadband; these are not safe thermal/
water standards, actual tariffs or qualified PS dispatch. Shared home connection
is separate from personal mobile. Sanitation, supply losses, public/commercial/
clinical demand, peak/backup and source capacity retain their actual scopes.
This is a replaced household block, not a claimed net national power saving.

**PB08 — Shared cost is allocated once under actual liability and receipt.**
The quoted owner 162 m repair/renewal/insurance/site, private-rent 96 m, public-rent
26.6 m and unoccupied-owner 80 m burdens have different recipients/purposes. Owner
renewal is not already bought housing, rent is not another repair invoice, and
vacant-investment costs are not automatically personal necessary housing or G
subsidy. A flat 800 site comparison is no NF valuation/rate/exemption finding.
Keep I/H actual occupancy/affordability/privacy and landlord/creditor claims;
no cohabitant's income is presumed available or compulsory pooling authorized.
