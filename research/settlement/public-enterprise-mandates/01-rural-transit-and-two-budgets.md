# Rural transit, a public enterprise and one shared reserve

This constructed comparison exercises
[OE41–OE46](../../country/C05/13/09-funded-public-enterprise-mandates.md).
A competent council commissions a rural bus route at a protected fare.
Efficient attributable costs for the period total 120, including 12 of
maintenance and 8 of shared depot cost. The public mandate accounts for
only its own depot share. The operator receives 25 in passenger fares and
5 in a dedicated external grant. Its separately authorized net council
compensation is 90. Only 70 is paid on time. Twenty of dated costs are
therefore uncovered even though the full expense was authorized.
The operator also has 30 in unrelated commercial losses.

A separately enacted common reserve contains 25. Under its prior law,
a second essential service has a verified priority call of 15 by day 3.
Transit needs the missing 20 by day 4. Both operators' staffed and
maintained service paths are ready if paid. The calls require 35 in total.

| Response | Dated fiscal result | Service consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Treat the full council award as cash | Records the 20 arrear as if already paid. | The route lacks its actual 20. |
| Promise the same reserve separately to both services | Reports two affordable uses but promises 35 from 25. | At least 10 remains uncovered. |
| Apply the reserve's prior priority and expose the remainder | Pays 15 to the earlier duty and 10 to transit. | The bus service still needs 10 by day 4. Preserve claims and seek an enacted source or qualified interim service. |
| Authorize a timely 10 top-up | Funds both service calls using 35 in total. | Verify actual transit and second-service receipt. Keep commercial losses separate. |

The transit enterprise can ask the council to correct the shortfall or
arrange a lawful alternative. It cannot take 10 from required maintenance
and claim the original safe service was fully funded. A uniform surcharge
that might raise 10 would require a separately lawful decision and evidence
about access and collections before the deadline. The council's vote
cannot make hypothetical future receipts available on day 4. The
unrelated commercial loss of 30 remains with its lawful obligor and
distress procedure. Any valid recovery or rescue has its own authority.

Count the 90 council transfer as a use in the council account and a source
in the operator account. At consolidated public-sector level it is one
internal transfer. The real 120 service cost and outside receipts still
matter. Record how much transit and second-service provision people
actually receive. The [arithmetic check](verify-public-enterprise-mandates.py)
covers compensation, reserve use, maintenance and the commercial loss.
Actual prices, delivery, lawful funding and distribution remain open.
