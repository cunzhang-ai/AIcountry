# CP13–CP16. A narrow public-logistics diversion, with continuing claims

Design162 · Proposed revision of CP05's previously uncommitted-only pool

**CP13 — Permit a defined emergency use, not a change of entitlement.** Retain
CP05's uncommitted public/voluntary/preparedness pool. Add only an expressly
scheduled category of actually public-controlled ordinary passenger vehicles,
common movable logistics equipment and their incident fuel/qualified paid
operation, temporarily committed to a **less urgent public service**. Actual
owner/dispatch law and accepted contract terms must permit the use; a public
label creates no power over a private contractor, client/pension money or
another authority's assets. The committed resource stays recorded as committed;
this is an explicit limited diversion, never reclassification as spare stock.

Every condition must hold on credible original facts: imminent irreversible
serious bodily harm; a feasible protective effect; no actually available less
burdensome route; minimum sufficient resource/time; qualified lawful operation
and accepted safe receiving; and no equal or greater grave downstream harm.
A clinician decides whether ordinary transport is safe where clinical judgment
is needed. Do not turn a school bus into a staffed ambulance by declaration.
No predicted social worth, employer status, wealth, political loyalty or AI
ranking determines whose life matters. Disclose uncertainty: qualified urgent
judgment is not a certificate of a risk-free outcome. Unknown critical safety/source facts
are not affirmative proof of a usable resource.

Exclude stock/time actually needed for life-sustaining care, basic food/water/
shelter, dependent personal safety, ecological safety or independent election,
legislative, court/remedy/source-record functioning absent a verified lawful
safe substitute actually accepted first. Schoolchildren already entrusted to
transport/care must have actual safe supervision/receiving, not a stranded
roadside or compulsory unpaid parent. This grants no forced private transfer,
worker conscription/strike restriction, new job/shift beyond lawful employment,
clinical coercion, search, tax/debt, military command or new general rationing.

**CP14 — Use existing authority and the original clock.** The existing legally
assigned CP local civilian lead makes the reasoned order under the expressly
enacted resource schedule and source. The actual public dispatcher/owner and
qualified sector receivers verify control, feasible safety, other commitments
and return; they cannot invent another's consent or authority. Existing narrow
safety powers remain distinct. CP07's local24elapsed-hour and affirmative
Cabinet-before-expiry/absolute72-hour limits run from the **earliest related
allocation**, including prior uncommitted-stock acts. Minimum rescue time and
prompt safe return govern within those outer limits, not a right to keep a bus
for three days. No new label/term/owner/office or foreseeable recurring shortage
restarts a materially continuous episode. Official fault never cancels available
urgent protection; it supplies no extension. Durable/repeated material priority
needs ordinary competent elected law and paid capacity, not permanent emergency.

**CP15 — The displaced people remain owed actual provision.** Record protected
sources, resource/time, lawful actor, who loses what, child/patient choice and
safety, actual receiver, expense/source payer, return and received result.
Give immediate safe notice to affected service owners/people, opposition,
independent review and Assembly under CP07, without public intimate testimony.
Preserve direct CP08/ordinary court review of authority, facts, equality,
proportionality, ongoing necessity and actual relief; no mandatory internal
exhaustion or final reviewer chosen by the alleged abuser. A logistics label
cannot conceal actual personal restraint/site exclusion: applicable ED/HP/LF
protections and their earlier clocks remain.

Original education/care/work/other claims are not fulfilled or waived because
this use is lawful. The competent original service owner arranges actual safe
interim support, alternative travel and catch-up/repair where possible under
existing rights; necessary paid cover cannot default to a relative. Dispatching
and service owners identify actual lawful cost/restoration sources and required
elected adjustment, no assumed reimbursement, free standby or balancing tax.
Record lost learning/work/time and legally owed wages/claims/compensation;
this creates no blanket unpriced damages promise or permission for retroactive
benefit recovery. Actual unavailability remains an unmet duty. Expiry ends new
exceptional allocations, not a safe ongoing rescue or accrued source/worker/
claim obligations. Ordinary criminal responsibility still needs its own proof.

**CP16 — Select the limited slice; keep broader alternatives distinct.**
Replace the uncommitted-only default with this limited public-logistics slice,
retaining the excluded critical pool and all CP06/07/08 protections. Reject
an inference of general executive requisition or a universal welfare allocator.
A clinic/election generator conflict without safe substitution still falls
outside; new competent elected priority would be needed. The old narrow rival
better limits discretion but may leave avoidable serious harm; broader public/
private requisition may reach more resources but increases coercion/capture.
Actually paid redundancy/ordinary sharing can reduce such conflicts, at real
staff/capital/maintenance cost. None is a universally demonstrated winner.

New committed-logistics authority starts only under a coordinated Act stating
its actual method date: first1January at least12calendar months after enactment,
with exact resource/actor/old-case/source/worker/contract/property/record and
OP/T successor scope. Existing due rights/source/court/force/ED/NC/TW/Y/RF/
electoral clocks remain; no emergency declaration or readiness certificate
adopts or delays the new rule. Preserve161 and the original79 reasoning;
no old review transfers to the changed scope. Actual safe capacity/authority/
source and better lives are unknown. Reopen on actual downstream harm/capture,
qualified resource/source evidence, affected objection or a better whole choice.
