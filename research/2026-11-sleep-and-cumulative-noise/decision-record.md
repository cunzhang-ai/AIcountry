# Design343 decision on sleep and cumulative environmental noise

## Baseline and choice

design supplies a substantial-interference remedy. It lacks an ambient

Select an enacted ambient floor with separate metrics, a local duty with

## Alternatives and evidence

Source-by-source permits preserve clear operator scope while missing
combined exposure. Private insulation can deliver protection rapidly
in a specific home, while leaving outdoor exposure and people in unsuitable
buildings. A universal night ban is simple, with disproportionate effects
on essential services and workers. Select measurable combined duties and
separate event protection, with a defined route for genuine urgent uses.
Keep stronger source control and targeted building measures in the
same comparison.

The [primary sources](sources.md) supply health evidence and examples of
strategic maps and action plans. They do not determine this country's
numerical limits, which require affected participation and prospective
law. The five- and 20-working-day choices need paid capacity and
assessment. Measuring indoors can expose private life. Keep measurements
proportionate and publish only safe evidence.

## Transition and reopening

Fund measurement, source changes, independent evidence and necessary
temporary protection. Retain existing permits, hearings, compensation
claims and stricter requirements under their actual legal scopes during
migration. An early noise reduction does not buy permission to transfer
harm elsewhere.

Compare a full ordinary monitoring period with residents and operators
on both routes, including renters, night workers and a genuine emergency
service. Measure total and peak exposure, ventilation and actual mobility.
Reopen on shifted risk, missed event patterns, unserved duties, excessive
burden or a better feasible approach. Actual qualified and affected
review, adoption, costs and health outcomes remain open.
