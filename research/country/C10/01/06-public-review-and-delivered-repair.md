# RX22–RX26. Review emergency decisions and deliver their correction

*Design524 · Proposed ordinary accountability for exceptional action*

**RX22 — Open a review the executive cannot withhold.** At the first
authenticated operation of an RX declaration, the Assembly's career Clerk
opens a protected review record and notifies the existing independent audit
and Ombuds authorities. The record remains open through change of Cabinet,
dissolution, expiry or revocation. At expiry or revocation of the declaration,
the preassigned independent reviewers begin the substantive after-action
comparison under their existing audit and Ombuds mandates or the exact
enacted review instrument. Name their respective sources and duties before
activation, with a funded conflict substitute outside the affected command.
A separately lawful sector challenge can start sooner. Open the same type of
record from the first operation of a broad ordinary restriction under RX08,
including where no declaration exists. Where restrictions materially continue
across Acts, maintain a cumulative account with each source, territory and
expiry identifiable. The declaration's end starts its own review clocks.
Final cessation of a separately continuing ordinary restriction starts its own
review clocks without resetting the earlier ones. Review cannot extend
expired power or reopen a lawfully concluded
case without the ordinary grounds.

**RX23 — Make the record complete enough to challenge.** Preserve original
authority, dated orders, evidence, forecast assumptions, alternatives,
dissent, other lawful emergency actions and affected downstream decisions.
Collect ordinary operational evidence and independently seek the accounts of
people who could not use the official route. Verify actual consequences:
restriction received, notice usable, service delivered, relief executed and
harm transferred to another place or group. Use proportionate protected
custody, independent access and fair reply. Public reasons and safe findings
remain available without disclosing unnecessary private testimony. A contested
model output, technical log or reconstructed summary is evidence of what was
recorded or inferred. Its conclusions require review against original
authority and independent observations.

**RX24 — Give findings an elected decision on the original timeline.** By
30 calendar days after expiry or revocation, publish a safe preliminary
account of live risks, gaps, evidence and named remedies. By 120 days publish
independent findings, disagreements and scoped recommendations or a missing
finding with its reason and retained responsibility. Schedule a recorded
Assembly disposition by day 150 on the findings available and the exact
ongoing recommendation or funded response. The Assembly considers the
continuation of ordinary services, individual and systemic repair and
changes to future powers through their own instruments. Compare a bounded
recovery offer for documented losses from lawful measures with ordinary
restoration and no additional offer. Show who missed work, education or care
and who would bear the cost of recovery. An offer needs an exact elected
authority and funds. Opposition can
place an exact costed alternative within the existing agenda rules. Existing
faster court, service and budget clocks continue. If a lawful sitting or
a qualified review fails, publish the missed act and use the earliest
lawful correction with the original date retained. A missed deadline creates
no power, political assent or presumption about liability. A later finding
receives its own protected disposition where it changes the standing response.
Before a proposed further segment under RX08, table independently checked
current necessity, cumulative burdens, affected accounts, usable alternatives
and paid continuity. Use the existing Assembly procedure for separate recorded
choices on materially separable effects and fresh primary law. A missed
finding is a disclosed procedural failure. Existing expiry, urgent relief and
the competent forum's own judgment continue under their applicable rules.

**RX25 — Follow each valid correction to a person or receiving office.**
Keep the three results distinct: a finding about public operation, a lawful
individual decision or remedial order, and verified receipt. Promptly route
proved or plausibly exposed cases to the existing competent owner and direct
independent challenge. A lawful systemic correction can reach missed people
without a duplicate application. Where consequences depend on a common
classification, stop unsupported downstream reliance under the applicable
law, verify authentic replacements and prove that material recipients ceased
using the expired authority. Preserve separately supported acts. Follow
needed restoration, repayment or other enforceable relief through actual
execution and the next due call under PCO27–PCO31 and applicable source law.
No public review report decides individual criminal guilt, creates
compensation or appropriates money.

**RX26 — Pay for review and independent human judgment.** Use an express
standing line or other exact elected appropriation to fund independent
review, affected participation and separately lawful redress execution.
Price ordinary capacity, the loss of the emergency platform and the next
due service. The Assembly can fund a wider inquiry on separately authorized
terms when the existing bodies cannot reach the affected system. Compare
the narrower baseline with a temporary independent inquiry and ordinary
requirements for audit or court review. AI may link lawful records, expose
missing groups or test a proposed reconstruction. Keep source access and
the ability to verify or receive a complaint without the disputed system.
Protect a separately paid ordinary service and review route through accumulated
claims and the next service window. An independent custody route must survive
loss of the platform used for the original measure and its appeal.
AI cannot clear its own earlier output, verify lawful authority or decide
a remedy. Reopen on missing evidence, repeat harm, ineffective correction or
a qualified simpler procedure with equivalent public control.
