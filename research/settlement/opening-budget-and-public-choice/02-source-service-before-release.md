# A reserve that can take away another public service

*Design470 · An additional branch of the two-year opening decision.
Cash and operation dates in this example are constructed.*

## The same national opening, with the source service included

Use the [four-payer opening and exact competing motions](01-two-year-budget-and-decision.md).
Keep the same 1-unit early rephasing, 3-unit standby with its one-unit
fee if drawn, and the cabinet's proposed 4-unit later backstop. Compare
continuing repair (R), finite home support (P) and the opposition's
water preparedness motion (W). The accelerated motion still fails
at the first cutoff. No outcome, lawful appropriation or opening
balance for Denmark is inferred from this fixture.

Before commitment, an independent site check and affected people's
challenge establish that 2 of the 4 proposed refit units pay for the
only accessible intake and assessment room during an existing site
closure. That use is protected through the same late payment window.
The room uses a qualified team assigned within ordinary service.
No substitute has verified access, a staffed receiving time or an
independent challenge route by the original appointment deadline.
The affected assessment users are outside the list of people who
would receive the additional home visits or water offers. The
other two refits are genuinely discretionary in the fixture and
can be reassigned only through an exact competent vote.

The [observed hospital estates comparison](../maintenance-continuity/01-reported-estate-backlog-and-investment.md)
illustrates why spending and a backlog total cannot establish when
an actual task can be deferred. Its sites, amounts and trends do
not price the assessment room. The [reference opening motions](../integrated-first-programme/13-reference-opening-motions.md)
still have no qualified common real-world cash and delivery ledger.

## Joint cash and service test before commitment

The late balance without a backstop reflects repayment of the
standby and its fee. The protected minimum is one. 'Room retained'
means the two source units stay committed to their original use.
Assigning a budget reserve without drawing it does not itself
remove the room. The problem is having to draw the same two units
in the adverse branch when intake needs them.

| Exact motion and evidence available before commitment | Early cash after any draw | Late cash if three are delayed and never clear | Standing intake service and decision |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| P or W relying on the original four with no competing use | 1 | 1 with all four | The original favorable fixture remains valid on its own stated assumption. |
| P or W retaining the intake room | 1 | −1 with the two eligible units | Both fail the same test before binding. The gap is two including the minimum. A protected vote for W retains its own scrutiny. |
| R with the room and one of the two eligible units expressly authorized for the ordinary shortfall | 1 | 1 | The original intake capacity and ordinary dues remain covered on these assumptions. Report the reserve shortfall separately if the correction is unavailable. |
| P or W with the room and two additional unrestricted units settled by the late cutoff | 1 | 1 | The source conflict closes. Only the exact voted option can proceed, with its own receiving and qualified review. |

If the delayed amount clears in time, one backstop unit suffices for
P or W in that path. This does not qualify either option before an
irreversible commitment when timely clearance and failure are still
indistinguishable. A newly settled independent source or an independently
accepted lower-cost substitute room changes the comparison only when it
actually closes the same adverse path and preserves useful access.

In the identified branch, choose R and bring the exact scoped ordinary
shortfall to the competent budget decision. Keep the room and its original
appointments, accessible intake and independent remedy. Show the two
genuinely discretionary refits displaced by any choice. Keep the care and
water offers available, including a narrower funded offer with fresh
scrutiny where its terms change. Report deferred improvements for their
intended recipients as well as preserved assessment access.
When reliable evidence distinguishes the later paths before binding,
reopen the exact option using that evidence and the available vote window.

If a conflict is discovered after an offer binds, preserve actual supplier,
worker and resident claims and intake. Record gross obligations, any
available cash, original deadlines and uncovered service. A lawful funded
correction or changed contract follows its own process. Previously published
estimates do not make the conflicting commitment available twice.
