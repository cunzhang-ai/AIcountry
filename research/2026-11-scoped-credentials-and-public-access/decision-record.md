# Design238 decision record. Scoped credentials and public access

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned Design237](baseline.json)

## Decision

Select [CRD01–CRD12](../country/C11/13-scoped-credentials-and-public-access.md).
PD and VR already require bounded data and evidence use and a usable
nonexclusive route. DP and HT keep sector authority and scoped recovery.
The added choice defines sector acceptance, shared proof dependency,
a risk-bounded offline rule and separately funded verification through
a compromised issuer. Retain public, private and ordinary assisted
routes only at qualified scopes. Use the
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

## Real alternatives

| Alternative | Strong reason | Material objection |
|---|---|---|
| Continue direct and paper verification only | Limits cross-service metadata and national vendor exposure. Can be the best qualified local option. | Can make repeat visits, remote use and accessible choice harder. Paper can still share a custodian or a closed counter. |
| Common optional wallet and one national verification authority | Simpler initial operation and interoperability. Can lower repeat proof and staffing demands. | A common root or abuse can exclude people across services. Alternative access must be real. A single authority could accumulate metadata and control. |
| Sector-scoped proof and independent funded receiving | Preserves replaceable tools, usable sharing and independent actual decisions. | Duplicate skills and recovery cost money. Multiple logos can still share the same data or signing path. Different rules may confuse people. |
| Sector-only incompatible accounts | Limits some cross-service failures. | Increases repeated proof, lock-in and burden on people who need several services. Does not address vendor correlation. |

Select the third choice. Shared standards and optional tools can
reduce repeat proof where actual evidence supports them. Pay for
independent personnel and source custody before relying on the
alternate. The 24-hour ceiling for authorized offline cached
status is a proposed maximum. It cannot validate a reserved act
or force a new data system. Consider earlier limits and ordinary
direct evidence where risk or access calls for it.

## Power, evidence and transition

Assembly sets the lawful purposes and floor. Source custodians
attest facts, relying services own decisions and DP supplies
limited exchange and recovery support. Courts and authorized
electoral review retain actual independent challenge. No vendor,
credential issuer, model or protector obtains general authority.
Maintain usable routes against captured supervisors and common
source or funding loss. Protect actual appointments, funding,
records and enforcement in each reviewed institution.

The [authored case](../settlement/scoped-credentials/02-two-deadlines-and-a-compromised-issuer.md)
tests timely proof separately from decisions, settled payments
and staffing. Preserve VR, PD, AP, DP, JC, HT, B03 and sector
powers and original duties. Fund migration and usable verification
under FT/OP/T. No actual identity, electoral, payment, source or
technical permission changes. Comparators show different legal
and technical choices; they do not prove efficacy or real uptake.

## Reopening and the next useful test

Use a real benefit and election evidence route with two different
lawful relying bodies. Compare direct access with an optional
shared tool in an authorized outage exercise. Obtain actual staff
and safe receipt, original independent evidence, privacy exposure,
elapsed timing, effective review and recurring costs. Include
people without devices and those affected by unsafe household
control. Reopen on repeated delay, correlation, common failure,
unsupported security or a stronger complete alternative.
Actual affected participation, authorization, qualified readiness
and better lives remain open.
