# Design488 decision record

**Scope and baseline.** Compare legal closure and usable succession
against Design487 with the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[baseline hashes](baseline.json). Separate public duties, private
liabilities, payer, operational receiver, records and independent
review. The worked events and costs are constructed.

**Selection and objections.** Prefer a funded stand-ready handoff
or an equally effective narrower public fallback. A bulk export
cannot confirm legal authority, live service or sufficient review.
Paid overlap may duplicate cost, entrench an incumbent, or depend
on the same staff. Price it separately, rehearse loss of incumbent
cooperation and end it by the lawful decision after independent
receipt and exposure review. Preserve protected ordinary duties
where an optional start fails.

**Continuity.** Keep original dates, the open appeal and payroll,
safe updates to receiving authority and each valid obligation.
Confirmed return, a verified payee and exclusive release authority
qualify one lawful replacement payment. An uncertain or final transfer uses its own reconciliation
or remedy. Revoked authority stays revoked. A missed legal gate
does not create new powers, revive the predecessor or cancel
unserved work. A legally fixed date and a discretionary release
need distinct consequences.

**Evidence and reopening.** The [comparison](../settlement/remedy-through-closure/01-one-closure-four-surviving-duties.md)
tests scoped dependencies and authored timing. [Sources](sources.md)
record context and limits. Reopen on failed access, actual new
legal authority, unacceptable duplication or a distinct better
receiver. The remaining institutional choice is how to preserve
independent receiving and review across repeated provider failures
without converting a temporary public fallback into a permanent
monopoly. Actual authority, participation and outcomes are unverified.
