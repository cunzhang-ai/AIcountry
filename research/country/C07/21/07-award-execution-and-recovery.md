# ED29–ED33. Turn an education award into usable support

*Design270 · Part of [ED public learning](../21-public-learning-and-school-power.md).*

**ED29 — Commission at the award and keep one public owner.** When a
binding education plan or independent order specifies support, the ED22
local owner opens execution when it receives notice, preserving the
effective date and first protected opportunity. Continue any existing
lawful support while scheduling a change. The school and qualified
provider confirm the people, paid time, preparation, place, necessary
aids and transport, and who can act if they fail. Confirm access through
a safe, accessible channel with the learner and the people they choose
to involve. Record known gaps and expected dates at issue or handoff.
The owner can delegate tasks and retains responsibility for seeing that
the award reaches the learner. Administrative acceptance neither changes
the plan nor resets its dates. No new family request is needed.

**ED30 — Give the owner a paid way to act before a missed session.**
Use qualified ordinary provision first when suitable. Give the owner a
bounded, appropriated ability to book an already qualified local or pooled
alternate at the first forecast failure that threatens a due service.
The applicable source, latest booking time, release condition, reserved
hours and suitable timetable must be visible to budget and independent
review. Count paid preparation, access, transport and rest. A shared
alternate is available only to the extent that its other obligations
leave time. Follow published lawful priorities for competing due work,
give reasons and preserve unmet claims and urgent review. The authority
cannot quietly move an occupied slot from another learner. Apply
[FB26–FB30](../../C06/62/05-deficit-settlement-and-recurring-services.md)
when the same shortage needs recurring finance or permanent staffing.

**ED31 — Carry execution and review in parallel.** A dispute over the
award's content goes to the competent body. The owner continues the
operative lawful provision and the portions that can safely be performed,
subject to a lawful stay or change. An outside inspector can check missed
or imminently unusable provision and trigger existing execution and remedy
routes without relitigating the merits. Provide direct access when a
useful deadline is at risk. Keep the original notice and unresolved
part when routing a matter, tell the learner who can act, and retain
existing court authority and earlier rights. A transfer requires a paid,
accepted successor and continuity. The current owner keeps responsibility
until lawful acceptance. Use existing access protections for any private
information the successor needs.

**ED32 — Keep present receipt, past loss and remedy separate.** Verify
delivery proportionately through ordinary records and safe learner input
where wanted, with independent sampling and a route to challenge. Record
delivered usable support, unavailable support, unresolved receipt and a
freely chosen suitable adjustment separately. A generic attendance record,
school place or invoice cannot establish receipt of the awarded component.
An imposed timetable reduction still follows ED23. Use qualified judgment
and learner participation to choose feasible recovery. Extra sessions can
displace rest or other learning and may be unable to recover a lost window.
Keep actual recovery, financial remedy and remaining loss separate from
current delivery. Lack of a family response leaves an evidentiary gap
and cannot close the case. Avoid coercion and preserve chosen lawful routes.

**ED33 — Make shortages visible to the people who can repair them.**
Use the existing first-budget calendar to compare recurrent paid hours,
the reserved alternate and earlier needed cash against actual due work.
Publish aggregate unfilled work, repeated use of the reserve and separate
continuing and remedial delivery without exposing a learner's case.
The elected local body and Assembly at their own levels choose sources
and structural capacity. The inspector and court retain their separate
routes and funding. AI can flag a double booking, a missing accepted
handoff or an unexplained shortfall. Qualified people decide suitability,
entitlement, priority and whether the remedy was carried out. Accessible
human contact and ordinary offline records remain usable.
