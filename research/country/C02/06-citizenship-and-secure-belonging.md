# C02. Citizenship and secure belonging

*Integrated design 81 · MB01–MB20 · English working reference, not enacted law.*

Belonging is a durable right, not a sponsor's property or a reward for agreement
with government. Select territorial birth citizenship, bounded overseas descent,
a four-year voluntary naturalization entitlement and secure residence after the
same factual residence period. The [adult resident vote](03/01-resident-franchise-and-secure-status.md)
has its own one-year threshold. Naturalization and secure residence do not gate it. No person becomes a citizen through an AI account.

The selected no-deprivation rule deliberately goes beyond the exceptions allowed
by the 1961 statelessness treaty. It retains even an actual official grant based
on the applicant's own fraud, with separately proved accountability. Counterfeit
credentials or theft of someone else's record are not an actual grant. This
protects equal standing but risks irreversible corrupt grants; audit and separate
pre-effect verification reduce that risk without pretending to eliminate it.

A four-year period, one-year overseas parental connection and processing/custody
bounds are normative hypotheses, not demonstrated optima. Stable residence is
not unlimited new entry, automatic service receipt or an external state's consent.
