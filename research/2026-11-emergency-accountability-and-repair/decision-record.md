# Design524 decision record

*14 November 2026 · Post-emergency public accountability*

## Baseline and selection

Use the [Design523 predecessor](baseline.json), [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md),
RX01–RX21 and the existing Ombuds, audit, court and public-conditions rules.
Select [RX22–RX26](../country/C10/01/06-public-review-and-delivered-repair.md).
Open a protected review record on first operation, use funded standing
independent review at expiry and require an elected response and actual
execution of relief. Individual and statutory remedies retain their earlier
deadlines. The [first-round frozen design](../2026-11-first-round-stable/README.md)
remains the original baseline.

## Alternatives and objections

The [comparison](../settlement/emergency-expiry/03-review-and-delivered-repair.md)
distinguishes strong existing ordinary review, the funded preassigned route
and a temporary wider inquiry. Its primary comparator sources establish
actual review requirements in Canada and an inquiry and reporting process in
Australia. These establish institutional comparators. They provide no
estimate of the effect of the proposed clocks, a qualified adopter or evidence
of individual recovery here.

Standing reviewers can act without a decision by the Cabinet whose actions
are under review. They can also lack independence, scope or paid capacity in
practice. The enacted design therefore names a lawful conflict substitute,
evidence access, funded review and actual receiving. A separate inquiry may
be justified for a wider system failure. Compare the opportunity cost of
duplicated investigation and the cost of requiring residents to testify again.
Keep protected originals, fair reply, ordinary justice and previously valid
independent acts. Trace any ending restriction to the actual recipients
using it. A new AI rendering is neither a lawful source nor a recovery.
The government's response can choose a wider policy only through the
applicable elected law and paid delivery.

## Review and migration

An actual adopter identifies the Clerk, unconflicted reviewers, original
custodians, relevant ordinary authority, exact protected review funding and
usable resident remedies. The worked comparison uses authored times and
known and unknown outcomes. It distinguishes timely and late execution.
Reopen on suppression, missed repair, wider material impact, broken source
access or an ordinary procedure that achieves equivalent results. The
bounded next decision is an exact scoped review and funded receiving
exercise against a competent ordinary alternative. Real authority, actual
respondents, delivery and outcomes remain open. No project procedure or
existing legal permission changes.
