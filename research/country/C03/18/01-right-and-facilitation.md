# AX01–AX05: people may assemble without buying permission

*Part of [C03/18](../18-peaceful-assembly-and-public-dissent.md); design 189 candidate.*

**AX01 — Equal peaceful participation across forms.** Persons under C02's scope
may organize, join, leave, observe and report peaceful assemblies regardless of
nationality, voting eligibility, wealth, beliefs or approved AI use. No forced
participation, loyalty pledge or promise to forgo future dissent. Physical,
indoor, moving, remote and future-mediated collective expression receive the
applicable protections; a private meeting, solitary expression or unpopular
idea retains its own rights too. Material event risks depend on actual conduct,
not politics or an AI's predicted future opposition. A few violent actors do
not make everyone violent; LF/P individual responsibility remains.

**AX02 — Ordinary public forums and others' rights.** Streets, squares, parks
and other ordinarily accessible public forums admit peaceful nonexclusive
assembly subject to actual AX limits. A minister cannot turn the target's whole
surrounding area into a speech-free zone. Facilitate a meaningful place within
sight/hearing or the effective future-media equivalent of the intended audience
where practicable; distant token space is no automatic substitute.

This grants no forcible entry into homes, court deliberation rooms, clinical
spaces, secured critical installations or private property. Actual neutral
facility/equipment/vehicle/fire/environment law remains, with RX justification
and direct review; a protest label neither waives safety nor supplies an extra
licence. Public land/space plans and disposals must preserve practical accessible
civic forums and actual public-access rights. LA/PT/LU law and paid source
govern any acquisition/change; no free private title or universal mall access.
Selective application or an assembly-specific prohibition disguised
as a general booking/safety rule remains subject to AX. No right to unlimited
exclusive occupation, harassment or erasure of others' usable public access.

**AX03 — Voluntary notice, no missing-organizer veto.** People may request
traffic/access/protection help by any reachable human, phone, paper, assisted
or future channel, as early as practical. Notice is voluntary, free and never
an application for permission. Accept approximate place/time/access needs and
an optional safe contact; no required participant list, incorporated organizer,
party registration, identity scan, compulsory steward or insurance bond.
Anonymous, spontaneous, urgent and leaderless assemblies remain protected.
Actual facilitation cannot depend on submitting perfect information or receiving
a reply. Absence of notice is neither an offence nor AX grounds for restriction.

**AX04 — Use existing paid public roles.** The responsible elected local
public-space executive designates qualified C04 career receiving/facilitation
officers with their own statutory duties/signatures and protected discipline.
They coordinate actual road/facility/medical/police services within each one's
law; they do not grant assembly permission or command police cases. National
owners of relevant public forums provide the same receiving duty through their
existing qualified staff, with accepted local coordination rather than a new
sovereign tier. First receiver retains help/urgency until a competent successor
accepts; no routing loop can consume the event's purpose.

PO's qualified civilian territorial/incident commanders retain lawful deployment;
ministers and the people criticized cannot dictate a named opponent's restriction.
Actual personal/office conflicts need unconflicted assignment/direct courts;
criticizing public policy does not disqualify every public officer. Offer usable
coordination promptly and before the opportunity is lost, preserve safe reasons
and actual unmet need. No committee, mayor or AI acquires a discretionary veto.

**AX05 — Competing users and hostile audiences.** Try proportionate shared
space, time separation or access lanes with actual participants and other users.
An earlier booking is relevant evidence, not permanent political ownership;
no fabricated first-booker or compulsory agreement. Counterassemblies have equal
rights, with practical protection against intimidation and mutual obstruction.
Target credible violent actors and protect threatened participants before
restricting the people threatened. Hostility alone cannot create a veto over
unpopular speech. No state guarantee that all groups get identical exclusive
space at the same instant; an unavoidable material collision uses AX07–AX08
with actual evidence, fair voice and the least restrictive lawful solution.
