The problem is more than choosing a respected judge: the appointment, promotion,
transfer, evidence, pay, assignment, charge/override and own-disciplinary chains
can be captured together. Conversely, a career body can entrench itself or make
ordinary justice unaffordable. Keep public accountability, professional judgment,
defense and actual receiving distinct; a case manager or funding office is no
new sovereign. Political equality does not mean a popularity contest determines
guilt, and judicial independence is protection for people, not immunity for judges.

New ordinary judges have secure prospective tenure to75, reviewable merit careers,
consent to transfer except lawful restructuring/proved sanction, and no conviction/
government-win quota. The number/age/experience rules are authored hypotheses,
not scientific or biological optima. Ordinary trial/appeal defaults are1/3;
status uses distinct3/5 formations, while CJ12's closed7-person exception stays.
Misconduct/incapacity has actual closed grounds/proof; finite paid suspension
cannot become permanent removal through delay. Existing stronger rights remain.

DPP open qualification and Assembly3/5fullN plus1/3frozenO supply a different
leadership source from ordinary judicial appointments. Seven-year nonrenewable
personal term, protected career case powers, written instructions/own signed
overrides and safe original dissent constrain the chief. A vacancy does not
silence all prosecutors or create an indefinite acting leader. Three separately
preselected external case prosecutors have independent six-year staggered seats,
neutral activation and judicial accountability for implicated-command cases.
No one may privately appoint a friendly prosecutor after the incident. Failure
of every qualified route remains failure, not immunity or a new police power.

A joint inaugural source exception needs express legitimate founding/constitutional
law, actual consenting prior judicial peers and exact open criteria/elections;
first judges cannot adopt their own appointer. Ordinary new law starts180calendar
days after competent enactment, preserving actual cases, stronger tenure/rights,
source dates and paid succession. Appointment notice21days cannot cut B16's
ordinary20-working-day adverse reply; material new grounds need fresh review.
