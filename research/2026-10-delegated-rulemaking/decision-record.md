# Decision record: democratic control of delegated rules

4 October 2026 · integrated-design-27 · Research selection, not enacted law

## Baseline, question and authority

The predecessor is the [design-26 snapshot](predecessor-design-26/README.md):
four cross-cutting documents and fourteen country chapters, copied before this
revision with SHA-256 checksums. The [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md) guide this research comparison;
they do not supply legal adoption. The current [country design](../COUNTRY-DESIGN.md)
and [status](../DESIGN-STATUS.md) hold the resulting reference selection.

One AI-assisted project author/controller prepared this record. There was no
legislator, resident, local council, worker, regulated party, judge, original
case file, affected-public deliberation or legitimate adopting jurisdiction.
The only observations checked are the local design texts and the original
external institutional materials below. No operating regulation was tested.

**Bounded question:** C03 gives the elected Assembly general lawmaking power,
while C04 and the proposed legal hierarchy allow regulations within statute.
The design does not yet specify who may issue a generally binding rule,
how the Assembly reserves important choices, what scrutiny follows different
effects, or when a technical standard, form, guidance or AI parameter has
in practice changed a right. Without this chain, a cabinet can keep a
democratic statute's title while altering its operation through delegated
texts. Conversely, voting on every technical update can overload the Assembly
and delay safety work. This is a cross-country-design authority gap, not a
request for another central review office.

**Values:** equal political standing, open opposition, local self-government,
lawful expertise, timeliness, safety, intelligibility and accessible remedies.
**Constraints:** prior constitutional and statutory rights, due claims, scarce
Assembly and professional time, separate judicial function and no self-enacting
research procedure. **Empirical unknowns:** actual volume and impact of rules,
Assembly capacity, affected participation, source-law delegation, consultation
capture, court and archival capacity. **Hypothesis:** explicit enabling scope,
effect-sensitive scrutiny, authenticated publication and direct challenge can
keep adaptation possible without permitting an executive shadow legislature.

## Different institutional packages

| Package | Mechanism and possible gain | Failure or tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Retain design 26's general hierarchy | Existing statute, court and election checks remain; no new procedure or workload. | “Within statute” does not tell a court or Assembly whether a general eligibility model or binding guidance crossed the line. |
| Assembly votes on every general administrative rule | Direct elected control of each text. | A flood of technical detail consumes legislative time, invites opaque bundles and makes essential updates wait; formal votes may be uninformed. |
| Broad executive delegation with consultation and judicial review | Agencies adapt using specialist knowledge and public input. | Cabinet can redefine basic rights and obligations before litigation is possible; organized interests dominate access; courts become the only backstop. |
| Independent technical council certifies all rules | Could expose bad evidence and preserve expertise. | A new unremovable gate can block elected choices, share vendors with agencies and treat technical risk ratings as political authority. |
| **Selected: bounded delegation with effect-sensitive legislative control** | Assembly statutes reserve foundational choices, name delegable scope and scrutiny; ordinary technical rules use published notice and a callable Assembly route; material delegated instruments need affirmative elected approval; urgent rules are narrow and temporary. | Classification and consultation can be manipulated; Assembly time, independent review and updated public records cost real capacity. |

The selected package is a working constitutional/statutory design hypothesis.
It is not evidence that one of the comparator states has solved executive
overreach or that the proposed country will perform better. A later affected
public could retain another package with reasons.

## Reference rule and chain of responsibility

1. **Keep essential choices in enacted law.** Constitution protects the
   democratic and rights functions already selected. Assembly statute fixes
   the purpose, persons and territory affected, eligible decision-maker,
   permissible instruments, content and outer scope of a delegation, any
   allowed subdelegation, material-impact triggers, duration, publication,
   scrutiny, funding and remedy. Crime and maximum sanction, tax bases and
   national rates, protected franchise and election count, core benefit
   eligibility, general force and surveillance powers, public debt or
   guarantees, and transfer of elected competence cannot originate in a
   regulation or vendor contract. Local elected rate and service choices
   remain possible within a genuinely enacted national delegation.
2. **Classify by effect, not label.** A generally applicable instruction,
   form, tariff, professional standard, model threshold or data rule that
   materially changes who receives a service, pays, may act, is inspected,
   loses liberty or can challenge government is treated as a rule regardless
   of whether it is called guidance, code, update or AI output. A professional
   judgment about one person's case remains an individual decision with its
   own reasons and appeal. Nonbinding advice may help but cannot become a
   hidden condition of a legal claim. Referencing a private or foreign
   standard does not give its later updates automatic domestic legal force.
3. **Use a review tier that law fixes in advance.** For a routine, reversible
   rule within a narrow mandate, the agency publishes an exact draft, source
   clause, reasons, evidence, distribution and implementation cost; it offers
   accessible input and answers material objections before authenticated
   publication and a prospective effective date. The Assembly and opposition
   can call it for debate or annulment under an enacted period; an independent
   court can review authority and rights. A rule materially changing a
   delegated service standard, physical exposure, serious obligation or
   cross-local burden is laid with alternatives and requires an affirmative
   Assembly vote before effect. A proposed rule outside the enabling law
   returns for a statute rather than being relabelled a technical update.
   Effects and cumulation, not drafting length or agency preference, control
   the tier. Classification reasons and challenge are public.
4. **Let affected people speak without making consultation a veto.** Notice
   reaches residents, workers, small organizations, local authorities and
   people who use supported or future communication methods, not only
   registered lobbyists. Record original submissions, common funding and
   source families, changed definitions, reasons for accepting or declining
   material points and dissent, with safe custody for sensitive testimony.
   AI agents may explain or draft under mandate; copies do not multiply a
   person's standing. Consultation supplies evidence and challenge, not
   consent or a substitute for the Assembly's authorized vote.
5. **Constrain urgent rules.** A statute may preauthorize a narrowly scoped,
   immediately necessary temporary instrument for a specified danger when
   ordinary timing would defeat protection. The officer publishes the trigger,
   evidence available, alternatives, rights effects, start and fixed expiry,
   with prompt independent challenge and Assembly scrutiny. Continued general
   effect needs a fresh elected authorization under the proper tier; repeated
   replacement cannot evade that requirement. Expiry does not cancel accrued
   rights, lawful prior duties or individual remedies. Silence is not consent,
   and an emergency rule cannot amend the charter, create a crime or tax, or
   suppress opposition outside valid prior law.
6. **Make the rule knowable and executable.** One publicly authenticated
   register links enabling text, exact proposal and final rule, prior versions,
   effective and expiry dates, scope, votes, reasons, source standards,
   machine-executed criteria and review orders. A person has a usable human
   route to know and contest the operative rule. Agencies budget training,
   accessible forms, inspectors, remedies and transition for pending cases;
   a published rule is not a staffed service. Technical operators, archives
   and AI vendors may maintain records, not choose legal effect. A successor
   must preserve original records and due claims when a rule is replaced.

The elected Assembly sets delegation and its scrutiny categories by law,
controls the relevant committee's standing resources and can amend or revoke
the enabling statute prospectively. Opposition has access to the register,
agenda and a usable call-in route. Local councils legislate within their
protected sphere and national floor; national agencies cannot use a model
update to seize local competence. Courts decide legal scope, procedure and
rights with an executable remedy, but cannot write the missing policy or
convert an agency error into sovereign court rulemaking. The C06 account
includes agency, legislative, archival, court and participant time; an
unfunded scrutiny timetable is a paper safeguard.

## Evidence, objections, compatibility and next test

The [German Basic Law, Article 80](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/englisch_gg/englisch_gg.html)
is a primary example of specifying content, purpose and scope of delegated
authority in law. The [UK Parliament's procedural guide](https://guidetoprocedure.parliament.uk/collections/lA7IUL12/delegated-legislation-and-statutory-instruments)
shows different legislative scrutiny routes, including affirmative and
annulment routes, and that some instruments receive no parliamentary step.
The [OECD regulatory-policy recommendation](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/recommendation-of-the-council-on-regulatory-policy-and-governance_9789264209022-en.html)
supports accessible public participation, assessment and review as a policy
comparator. These are separate normative/legal source families, not measured
AIcountry outcomes. None selects this package's thresholds, proves adequate
scrutiny, or authorizes a rule for this project.

The strongest objection is classification capture: an agency could split a
material change into minor updates, hide binding criteria in software, call
an emergency ordinary administration, or place broad discretion in the
enabling statute. Review therefore examines cumulative effects and the
actual decision pipeline, with access to operative model versions and
procurement terms. Another objection is delay: material safety updates may
need expert speed; narrow urgent authority preserves an immediate path but
its trigger, deadline and consequences require legitimate local law. A
third is unequal voice: well-funded regulated firms can dominate comments,
while affected non-users lack time and safe access. Supported outreach and
reasoned dispositions help, but only real participation can test that claim.
Courts and legislative committees may themselves share appointments,
funding, information or vendors with the issuing executive. The older
democratic and judicial independence tests still apply to them.

This revision affects C03's elected lawmaking, C04's administration, C06's
institutional workload, C08's challenges, C11's public record, C12's agent
mandates, C14's legal hierarchy and future change, proposed A4/A13 legal
wording, and F2/F4/F5 stress review. Sector laws, local competences, current
permissions, pending claims and old manuscripts do not change automatically.
The applicable prior constitutional route must authorize any real transition;
a delegated rule cannot authorize its own delegation or make this research
selection binding.

**Evidence qualification:** comparative primary legal and institutional texts
and an identified gap in current local design. **Research selection:** the
bounded, effect-sensitive delegated-rule chain above. **Legal authorization:**
none. **Operational readiness:** no legislative committee, register, agency,
source-law inventory, court, budget or real affected-user route established.
**Observed outcomes:** none; superiority and social benefit remain unproved.

The bounded next test for a real potential adopter is to map three live
instruments under its actual source law: one service eligibility rule, one
safety/physical standard and one urgent or AI-mediated rule. Preserve the
original rule, source statute, real effects, affected and non-user accounts,
Assembly capacity, review path and pending claims. Compare design 26's broad
hierarchy, this tiered route and a meaningful local alternative; determine
whether each would permit correction before harm without disabling democratic
control. No jurisdiction or field budget is available to this project.
Reopen the selection if independent challenge is inaccessible, the Assembly
cannot scrutinize material instruments, technical updates change rights, or
the adopted alternative protects rights and adaptability with lower burden.
