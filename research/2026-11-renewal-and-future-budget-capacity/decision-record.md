# Design324 decision on renewal and future budget capacity

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design323, IV01–IV27, DB, FB, MF, JC and the working framework.
The prior country design already reconciles maintenance, protects due
service and compares material deferral. Add a durable cross-budget
renewal schedule and a separate elected displacement decision before
a new commitment takes an existing work window. The project assistant
authors the proposal. Actual adoption and appropriations belong to
the competent elected institutions.

## Alternatives and evidence

Compare the current annual review and project approvals, a fixed
maintenance share, and a rolling renewal schedule with protected
actual work and a separate recorded displacement vote. Annual
approval can preserve discretion and still miss a later collision
with binding continuation. A minimum percentage is visible but can
misallocate funds between assets, overlook a crew bottleneck, or
reward changes in accounting labels. Select actual protected windows
and service obligations, with evidence-qualified retiming and an
open political choice over lawful substitutes. This costs appraisal
and can delay a valuable start. The amended schedule needs a route
to release surplus capacity and simplify immaterial work.

The [primary sources](sources.md) document observed measurement and
funding problems and an established life-cycle governance approach.
They do not demonstrate this design's comparative benefit. The
worked water and school example stipulates a two-budget choice and
tests later money separately from crew availability.

## Authority, migration and reopening

Owners maintain original condition, liability and service evidence.
Budget offices reconcile material schedules once. Elected authorities
choose funding and lawful service tradeoffs. Qualified staff and
independent review control safety, receipt and timely lawful relief.
Introduce the rule prospectively with no new power to transfer
restricted funds, remove a liability or make a future vote binding.
Carry existing contracts and accepted services through any change of
government, provider or scope. Existing safety authority applies
immediately on its own grounds.

Reproduce a material portfolio for one real shared works crew. Obtain
actual condition and receiving evidence, dated ownership of funds,
employee availability and opposition and affected-user input. Compare
safe deferral, separate crews and simpler planning on the same
obligations, including disability and rural access. Reopen on
repeated unsafe rollovers, earlier loss, funding gaps, paper compliance
or an equally protective simpler route. Actual lawful authority, paid
capacity and comparative outcomes remain open.
