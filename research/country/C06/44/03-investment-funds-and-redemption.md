# NB09–NB12: a fund cannot promise liquid money against immovable assets

*Part of [C06/44](../44-nonbank-finance-and-client-protection.md); design113 candidate.*

**NB09 — Real units, real valuation, accountable managers.** A fund's assets,
units, mandate, fees and custody are legally separate from its manager and each
other fund. Independent valuation/custody functions have direct source access;
common control/conflicts and uncertain prices are visible, not presumed absent.
Use independently supportable current realizable values, accrued costs and
liabilities; publish missing/unreliable valuations and a reasoned pricing route,
not invented stable NAV or a zero asset. Manager's own eligible paid loss capital
must cover the larger of6months' evidenced fixed operating costs or paid
wind-down cost, without counting client assets, reciprocal equity or callable
support. This is operational protection, not capital insuring market returns.

**NB10 — Select constrained ordinary retail funds, preserve other choices.**
Ordinary broadly offered retail funds may invest under disclosed diversified
mandates, with no more than10% NAV exposure to one common-control issuer group
(except genuinely safeguarded cash); report sovereign concentration too. Gross
asset/committed bounded-loss exposure may not exceed110% current NAV; borrowing
is only temporary settlement/redemption liquidity for at most30calendar days,
not rolling leveraged investment. Include derivatives' maximum additional loss,
undrawn guarantees and related vehicles once, with no cross-counterparty netting
or omitted unknown loss. Loss-driven breaches require prompt source assessment,
no distributions/new risk and a lawful correction/run-off plan, not automatic
forced sale or criminal guilt. Closed-ended and specifically opted-in separately
licensed risk funds can use other disclosed bounded leverage; no deposit-like
marketing, presumed retail suitability or public rescue. A ban on all such funds
and broader ordinary leverage remain genuine rival choices with access costs.

**NB11 — Redemption terms must fit the assets.** Constant-par on-demand money
belongs to M01/M05, not fund branding. A daily dealing fund needs independently
verified executable same-currency liquidity covering actual30-day contractual
outflows plus a20% NAV redemption stress, net of no hoped-for subscriptions or
uncommitted rescue. Do not double-count a due redemption in both terms.20% is
an authored starting stress, not evidence that a larger run is safe; publish
larger-run and combined market/credit cases. Less liquid assets require actually
supportable dealing/notice, at least90days for an illiquid-asset interval fund,
or a closed-ended transferable unit without fund redemption.90days alone proves
no buyer. Terms, valuation/cutoff and maximum ordinary gates are fixed before
investment; material deterioration cannot secretly cancel already due claims.

At a common dealing cutoff, valid requests receive the same unit price and
available pro-rata fulfillment within the same legal class. Actual independently
verifiable transaction/market-impact costs caused by redemption are charged to
that dealing flow, not secretly transferred to remaining holders or insiders.
No invented surcharge fixes insolvency. Saleability, authorization, settlement
and safe actual receipt are distinct facts.

**NB12 — Exceptional suspension is not indefinite managerial power.** A specific
serious inability to price/settle permits a published narrowly scoped protective
halt for at most72elapsed hours from first related interference, notifying the
conduct office immediately and affected holders through usable media. No renamed
fund/vendor/agent reset. Independent V04/B17 review by72hours/earlier useful time;
ordinary contractual redemption terms need no fabricated emergency. Before expiry,
resume lawful dealing, obtain a reasoned ordinary court run-off/reorganization
order under NB29, or use an actually completed lawful prospective change. A
manager cannot invent another emergency or reset overdue obligations by a vote.
Court run-off has actual realizable assets, creditor/title protection, accepted
administrator, monthly public aggregate accounts and reviewable distribution;
no forced sale at a fictitious price or assertion all clients are paid.
