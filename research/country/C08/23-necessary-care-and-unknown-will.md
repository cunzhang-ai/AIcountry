# C08. Necessary care when a person's will remains unknown

*Integrated design82 · UA01–UA20 · English working reference, not enacted law.*

Select a narrow statutory care/protection route when an adult's relevant will
cannot genuinely be ascertained after serious support. This is authorization
without ascertainable consent, not invented consent, a capacity forfeiture or a
general guardian. Current choice, applicable advance directions and defensible
personal interpretation come first. Unknown will cannot mean disagreement with it.

The fallback permits some necessary interventions contrary to a person's wishes
that remain unknown. Strict nonintervention can instead allow preventable harm.
Neither harm disappears through terminology. The CRPD Committee's interpretation
opposes substitute consent; retaining formal capacity, a court or single-act scope
does not establish compatibility. This is a contested normative candidate,
requiring actual source-law/qualified/affected review before any lawful adoption.
