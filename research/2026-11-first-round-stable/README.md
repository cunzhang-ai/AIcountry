# AIcountry first-round stable edition

*Design520 · Frozen research baseline · 14 November 2026*

This edition is the complete first-round working country design for public
review. Start with the settlement, then use the fourteen chapters for the
operative proposal and the operating blueprint for its first decisions.
The dated archive preserves the documents as reviewed. The research map in
the workspace remains the entry to active development.

| Decision status | Design520 boundary |
|---|---|
| Selected national model | Replaceable parliamentary democracy, elected local autonomy, independent courts and practical opposition, essential services and personal protection, plural production and bounded public AI. The destination's election rules apply at their lawfully effective contest. |
| Selected first programme | Lawful retrofit, protected contest and handover, standing duties and targeted repair first. Existing lawful finance continues while elected bodies compare durable alternatives. A finite works-draft AI evaluation is the initial optional candidate. |
| Separate prospective decisions | A limited public confirmation vote on withdrawal of independent essential access requires its own constitutional authority. Funded portable home support requires exact sector authorization and ordinary appropriations. Changes to tax, work time, currency, new nationwide entitlements and additional public AI scope use their own laws and release conditions. |
| Policy alternatives kept for review | Strong lawful repair, territorial direct-public services, a rights-preserving unconditional cash floor and decentralized mutual provision. Compare on the same duties, costs and funded next window. |
| Actual adopter and results | Competent public, jurisdiction and calendar, source and staffing, independent affected-party review, authorization and receipt remain open. The chosen design and the empirical question of improved lives have separate evidence. |

The archive contains the current research tree, its supporting files and
the research structure checker. It includes the decision records and evidence
from Designs518–520, with older dated evidence and predecessor snapshots
linked to the project research library. The outer release folder supplies
its archive and manifest. The archive omits site software and credentials.

To verify an extracted copy, use the SHA-256 for the archive and the
record of individual relative paths in the manifest. Treat a material change
as a new design edition. Use the existing decision record for scope, affected
duties, objections and review. The frozen edition stays available for
comparison under its original terms.
