# A full political voice without an invented electoral home

2 November2026 · Design111 working choice; no enacted count or ready election

**Select one nationwide proportional-list vote, with an optional person preference,
and a separate actual-local council vote.** Independents can enter as one-person
civic lists. Replace the mixed national territorial-first/independent-seat rule
rather than keep adding repairs to it. [EL01–EL24](../../country/C03/13-equal-franchise-and-usable-elections.md)
is canonical; [the exact package](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-equal-franchise-and-usable-elections/README.md)
contains arithmetic, limits, preserved predecessors and a prepared user protocol.

## Why change the structural choice?

The old rule reserved independent territorial winners' I seats, then allocated
N−I list seats. In a12-seat/4-territory authored example, second votes A240/B160
give A7/B5 if all territorial winners are list-backed. Four independent winners
instead leave A5/B3 plus four personal seats; if those people are assumed aligned
with A, the bloc is9/3. This illustrates why classification/control evidence can
materially change representation. It alleges no real fraud and does not predict
how people would vote with a single ballot. Genuine independent/local mandates
were a benefit of the old model, not an illegitimate preference.

The new count applies the same capped Sainte-Laguë rule to every positive-vote
list, whether party, civic or one person. It removes that personal-seat exception,
not wealth, popularity, party control or rounding incentives. A counterexample
in the same package splits A240 into eight30-vote one-person lists: their bloc
can gain8 seats/B4 instead of A7/B5. That retained flaw requires honest comparison
with other whole systems, not a claim that the new design solved political power.

Losses: a concentrated local independent may need more nationwide organizing;
there is no guaranteed territorial-person national mandate. Whole-local PR loses
ward guarantees. Closed lists, regional/STV and the former mixed system remain
real rivals. The selection prioritizes a common national seat rule and removes
a sensitive count classification/foreign-home requirement; it is not an empirical
ranking of governments or proof of universally better lives.

## The person, home and political act are different facts

| Situation under existing C02 substance | Proposed EL route |
|---|---|
| Adult citizen permanently abroad/never domestically resident | Same national ballot; no artificial district, ancestral address or fictitious local credit |
| Adult noncitizen with four protected habitual years and resident/vested-return connection | Same national ballot, plus actual local home if locally eligible |
| Adult resident with six cumulative national months but not national franchise | Local vote/credit only; no national source rights by party transfer |
| Homeless, mobile, displaced, in care or custody | Actual/protected single local connection, safe alternative proof and assisted private access; no landlord/account/diagnosis gate |

[EL01–04](../../country/C03/13/01-persons-homes-and-correction.md) keeps one local
home per contest, actual relocation updates before first accepted vote and no
second vote after moving. A forced move does not manufacture an electorate.
Use protected originals, alternative proof and direct useful-time courts; no
universal public voter/participation/medical graph. A chosen assistant can convey
a person's expressed will; caregiver or AI cannot infer a vote for them.

## Count, entry, ballots and local government are specified together

At call, freeze positive E_call and N. Each list needs
s=min(ceil(E_call/100),ceil(E_call/(4N))) active endorsements with at most two per
person. The old national1% gate never rises under this formula; the field maximum
is floor(2R/s), not200. Each actual person appears once per body; total nominees
are bounded by min(2N×lists,K), not fictitious duplicate candidate pools. K needs
independent P-eligible candidate evidence, not an assumption that it equals E_call.

All valid list votes count, no legal percentage threshold; exact quotient,
capacity and prepublished ties fix the seat trace. Preferences determine persons
inside the awarded list seats, not another vote. A clear list choice survives
invalid/cross-list/multiple preferences. Clear candidate-only indications can
imply their list under EL14. All names are available through a neutral full field,
paper booklet and private chosen-list directory; no personalized machine shortlist.

The illustrative72,000-elector/N40 field can reach320 lists,25,600 nominee names:
14 primary list pages at an **authored24 rows/page**, versus1,067 inline-name
pages. At N400 the same formula can reach3,200 lists and134 primary pages; actual
candidate supply caps an otherwise fictitious2.56m names at the assumed78,000.
These page arithmetic results establish no legibility or acceptable workload.
The large-field readiness claim is explicitly rejected pending a real whole-
package comparison, not repaired by tiny fonts or secretly excluding candidates.
[The protocol](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-equal-franchise-and-usable-elections/usability-protocol.md)
prepares actual affected-user tasks; no participants or comprehension results exist.

Local councils use one whole-municipality PR track. The initial charter schedule
is9/15/25 seats at≤10,000/≤100,000/larger reference electorate, capped by the
positive actual reference people in tiny places. Freeze n before call; four-year
terms, public chair election/minority tolerance, full-n dismissal, ordinary quorum,
material budget votes and bounded caretaker/repeat rules are in EL09–12.
Protected territorial rights and actual map/adoption remain separate. No ministry,
clerk or AI draws a binding convenient electorate or supplies missing mandates.

## A vote and a remedy must actually arrive

In-person/mobile/absent paper and paid accessible alternatives are the reference;
foreign cooperation must be real. A timely disputed voter can seal a conditional
ballot; absent inner choices remain unopened until P, allowing a verified personal
cast to replace a pending remote envelope. Eligibility is unlinked before mixed
counting. A spoofed prior-vote record is not an automatic veto; unresolved fraud
and result effect remain a real contest problem, not perfect integrity by database.

P+7 ordinary source review/P+10 proved certification is a proposed outer schedule,
not a timer-elected member. No choice is inferred from missing records or AI
confidence. Frozen unused nominees fill vacancies; exhaustion needs a whole-
electorate supplemental poll within90days, winners serving only the remaining
term. This can change a minority's old share; it does not appoint a loyalist or
rerun sitting members. Full N/n quorum/majorities never shrink with vacant seats.

Called national cash becomes one E v credit track: in the B1,200/E72,000 example,
432,000CU rather than864,000. Annual/local/ref credits remain. More lists, source/
private booth work, actual receiving, counts and court capacity may offset that
quote difference; it is **not** an actual budget saving or available clinical staff.
Old107/108 public election prices do not certify this changed package.
