# Design415. Preserve surviving obligations after public closure

## Decision and baseline

The Design414 predecessor carries linked awards into a new scheme, preserves
source-law rights, and exposes opening liabilities by date and payer.
It leaves a specific transition choice open. An organization can close
after active cases transfer while residents still need someone with the
power, records and funds to receive a valid later claim. The elected
legislature controls the closure law and budget. This revision selects a
candidate design for review. The decision was drafted with AI assistance.

## Alternatives and selection

Keeping the existing office through the remaining claims period protects
specialized staff and records. It can be the best feasible choice when the
successor cannot yet make a decision or execute a remedy. A clean transfer
with rapid release of residual assets can free resources for current
services if the exposure has been independently resolved or already has a
lawfully usable source. Treating unfiled claims as released assets can
instead conceal losses. The candidate uses a competent ordinary successor
with separately funded casework, restricted cash, audit and an independent
remedy. The constructed pension case shows why this choice can preserve
rights with lower ongoing costs. It does not estimate national savings.

The relevant value choice is that changing the operator cannot remove an
existing claim or impair a practical remedy. The need for specialist
overlap, likely claims and actual receiving costs are empirical questions.
There is a material objection to tying up funds for weakly evidenced claims
when money is needed for immediate services. Use dated reassessment,
credible exposure bounds and independently verified release conditions.
The actual size and period of reserves remain contestable. A statute must
identify who can fund and order a remedy and how it is executed even if
the sponsor or receiving office opposes payment.

## Evidence and migration

[Primary comparators](sources.md) establish examples of lawful liability
transfer and arrangements for people missed in qualifying private pension
closures. They do not establish an appropriate reserve size, legal fit or
successor capacity for AIcountry. No claimant, affected staff member,
independent reviewer or democratic authority participated in this revision.

Add LPC01–LPC08 alongside the existing opening-liability, linked-award,
handover and pension clauses. Preserve filing dates, eligibility, applicable
finality, separate budgets and privacy constraints. Carry a case and its
proof under the original rules through the named destination. Use paid
overlap for functions without a qualified receiver. Hold a discretionary
release if its legal or operating gates fail. Keep an enacted dissolution
on its lawful timetable and expose and remedy any breach through ordinary
authority. The new references change the research design and preserve the
predecessor. This record confers no governing or contribution authority.

## Status and bounded next action

Evidence qualification is limited to documentary comparators and a
constructed comparison. Research selection is a competent funded
successor with a separately controlled reserve release. Legal authority,
operational readiness and observed outcomes have not been established.

For one proposed scheme closure, the accountable successor and an
independent reviewer would trace a known payment, a contested filing and
an unidentified beneficiary through the enacted mandate, evidence
retrieval, accessible casework, appeal, treasury settlement and receipt.
The test ends with a dated cost and exposure statement, a paid test of
each route and a disposition of every failure. No real test budget has
been established. Keep a discretionary release on hold if authorization,
monitoring or receipt evidence is absent. Reopen the design if the case
files cannot be transferred safely, higher claims cluster or independent
remedies remain unexecuted.
