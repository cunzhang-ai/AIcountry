## What was checked and what failed

Normal planned five-sector cash closes under fixed prices/full voluntary
payout and existing I05/tax calculations. Required GDP4,266.89 implies market
value added per remaining worker 52.33% above the old reference. No productive
gain or realistic demand/price response was observed. Operating profit in
GDP differs from taxable profit after bank coupon and foreign credit costs;
property income is not additional output. Unknown pension accrual prevents
calling cash balances complete accrued saving/net lending or actuarial finance.

The candidate fails its water-loss condition. Private foreign settlement
first fails in month9 under 10% gross export loss; unreceived imports cannot
count as delivered consumption. Coincident debt principal breaches the
Treasury intramonth floor despite an apparently adequate year-end balance.
The domestic bank facility is only a collateral/capacity comparison; actual
solvency, eligibility, draw costs and common-payment failure remain unknown.

Retained objections: cost shares can hide unpriced workloads and false
precision; the larger public workforce can displace valued private work;
continued earnings insurance preserves income hierarchy; concentrated
capital returns/full payout may be unsuitable; public reliance on one bank
can capture essential services and opposition finance. These may justify
rejecting the candidate. They are author-identified objections, not a public
consultation, qualified dissent or proof of a superior competing package.

## Authority, support and continuity

Assembly law/appropriation chooses actual burdens, debt and material
coverage; sector/local operators, qualified professionals, source custody,
audit, courts, opposition and affected challenge retain separate powers.
Follow appointment, money, information and actual execution together. The
book compiler cannot turn allocation shares or missing evidence into a
sovereign mandate, eligibility cut, policy veto or executive-term extension.
Current law/software and accrued claims remain unchanged; pending obligations
need funded lawful successors and received remedies under any future change.

was searched for fiscal-sector, stock and risk coverage, not for calibration,
compliance or endorsement. No full handbook/statistical-standard audit is
claimed. Old numeric families supply only their pinned fictional scopes;
the precursor design-67 inventory supplies current function/owner identities.
No sensitive testimony or real financial account was collected.

[Validation](validation.json) reconciles unique allocations, payroll/cohort
bounds, GDP/cash identities, insurance, opening books, separate FX, source

authority, full requirement estimates, qualified capacity, receiving and
observed better-life outcomes remain unestablished. All-function allocation
does not qualify the candidate as an operating country.
