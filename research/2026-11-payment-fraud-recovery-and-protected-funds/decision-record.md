# Design251 decision record. Payment scam recovery and protected funds

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned Design250](baseline.json)

## Decision and diagnosis

Select [PFR01–PFR05](../country/C06/67-payment-scam-recovery-and-protected-funds.md).
Separate a customer-approved instruction, material deception, temporary
preservation, final title and a consumer reimbursement claim. Protect
the first report and a real inter-provider response without making an
innocent holder bear an indefinite freeze. Apply the
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

Existing banking and civil law protects accounts, title and access
and allows court-controlled preservation. It does not provide a joined
service for fast payment scams. The consumer may have approved the
transfer and yet acted because of fraud. The victim's useful time is
short, while the holder may need the money for rent or payroll.
The design places the initial qualifying payout with the sending
provider and shares the regulated loss with the first receiving
provider. No criminal conviction or collection from the holder is
required for that payment. The four-hour temporary power is a new,
express, narrow candidate power, not implied by ordinary fraud review.

## Compare coherent alternatives

| Choice | Strong reason | Material exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Leave loss to ordinary civil claims and criminal recovery | Protects settled title, requires an actual independent determination and limits shared premiums. | Funds can leave quickly and a consumer may lack a viable defendant or the liquidity to wait. |
| Stop any flagged payment or freeze the whole receiving account | Can slow onward dissipation under a broad preventive mandate. | Punishes innocent holders, rewards bad alerts and can deny essential payments or become a tool of abuse. |
| Use bounded preservation, direct review and funded sender-first reimbursement with shared regulated loss | Preserves actual recoverable funds and assigns a prompt payer without treating restraint as title. | Requires always reachable people and courts, liquid provider funds, accurate amount tracing and independent challenge. Costs can affect smaller providers, access or prices. |
| Require a court order for every post-settlement restraint, but keep provider reimbursement | Gives stronger independent protection against abusive recipient restrictions. | Timely evidence and a paid judge can still be unavailable in the short window. Payout restores some loss without recovering the original funds. |

Select the third as a research candidate, retaining the fourth where
a funded court can reliably act in time. Four elapsed hours, five
working days, a total 20-working-day limit and equal provider sharing
are proposed parameters. The domestic consumer scope and one statutory
incident ceiling are explicit boundaries. The ceiling amount and
adequate provider liquidity remain uncalibrated. Compare loss shifting,
fees, exclusion, claim fraud and false restrictions before adoption.

## Evidence, authority and transition

The [UK primary sources](sources.md) demonstrate a sender-first
regulatory model and distinct pre-settlement authority. Their dashboard
reports scoped consumer outcomes. It does not establish the optimal
candidate hold, loss split or causal effect on all fraud. The
[authored case](../settlement/payment-scam-recovery/01-one-loss-two-payers-and-a-timed-hold.md)
reproduces hold, release, earlier and later recovery, and a lower
ceiling. A false match requires actual release and authorized correction.

A competent law must assign scope, verified notices, funded liquidity,
independent rapid court and conduct access, and actual transaction,
remedy and fraud evidence boundaries. Preserve M/MC, B17, PT, criminal
and PD standards, prior funds and claims. Make any new contribution
prospective. An insolvent provider uses existing source and resolution
law. No research adoption or provider model creates public backing.
Actual affected participation, qualified review, law, capacity and
observed outcomes remain open.
