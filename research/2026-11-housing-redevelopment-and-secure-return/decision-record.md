# Design424. Secure real return before replacing occupied homes

## Decision and baseline

Design423 protects secure tenancy, compares adequate repair and
replacement and requires temporary housing and return for works.
H21–H25 separately measures preservation and new entry. The remaining
choice is the practical chain for planned permanent redevelopment,
especially when a project removes occupied affordable homes before
the new ones are available. This revision was prepared with AI
assistance. It needs competent planning, housing and budget authority.

## Alternatives and selection

Adequate repair preserves relationships and existing access where
safe and durable. An immediate rebuild can add the most homes sooner
where suitable interim capacity, affordable return and cash are
already available. Moving to a suitable permanent home can be a
better voluntary choice for a household, while using stock otherwise
available to someone waiting. Build-first can reduce forced moves
and double handling, but costs earlier land, construction and overlap.
The project must keep occupied buildings safe through that period.

Select an enforceable, funded receiving and return requirement and
compare the exact sequence before any irreversible action. In the
constructed case, build-first is feasible on stipulated conditions
while immediate redevelopment leaves households without receiving.
This is a choice for the stated site, not a countrywide rule that
every redevelopment has a suitable first parcel. Allow independent
review of a denial or delay. A tenant's protected choice does not
replace ordinary public planning, and a planning permit alone does
not terminate a tenancy. A restrictive replacement term can increase
project cost or reduce development. The elected authority must show
the consequences against repair, different sites and legitimate
alternatives before it binds the project.

## Evidence, authority and migration

[Primary comparators](sources.md) illustrate public programmes and
individual decisions with relocation, rent and return protections.
They provide no local housing cost, acceptance estimate or causal
evidence for the build-first choice. The constructed case stipulates
safety, consent, capacity and money. No tenant, applicant, worker or
independent reviewer participated in this revision.

Apply prospectively to the covered redevelopment phase. Preserve
original tenure, prior claims, secured rights and ordinary source-law
powers. The elected authority sets the plan and actual source. The
project operator arranges moves and qualified receiving. Independent
inspectors verify safety, and competent courts determine possession
and remedies. Verify usable financial assurance and the alternate
payer independently of the proponent. Keep public budget, workforce
and protected service routes on the same dates. Lawful title or
operator succession carries accepted leases, return claims, deposit
rights, paid obligations and review records.

## Scoped result and next test

The comparison separates physical addition, protected occupancy and
net entrant offers. Actual project authorization, tenure receipt,
resident preferences and improvement in lives remain open.

For one potential site, the local housing authority and an independent
reviewer would compare retention, immediate works and build-first.
Complete when a protected move and occupancy schedule shows accepted
or unresolved options, lawful rent, travel and care, cash and claims
for existing residents and net entrants. Report the result if a
first parcel, funding source or provider fails. No site or test
budget has been committed. Reopen on lost tenure, paid-cost overruns,
unavailable receiving or a better supported housing option.
