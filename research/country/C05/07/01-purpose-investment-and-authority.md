# N01–N04: investment serves lives and remains contestable

*Part of [C05 capability candidate](../07-production-learning-and-capacity.md); design 66.*

**N01 — Specify a useful capability and its actual constraint.** The
responsible sector/local authority identifies the service or productive use,
affected people, existing duties, demand, safe quality and missing inputs.
Distinguish lack of cash, qualified time, materials, lawful access, maintenance,
market power and an unsuitable institutional model. More technology or
faster communication need not solve the problem. Include people whose
private time, care, livelihood or environment would bear the intervention.
Do not turn every informal relationship into a measured government service.

**N02 — Compare complete productive choices.** Cabinet presents retain/repair,
purchase/partnership and build/learn alternatives where material, including
public, cooperative and private arrangements. Show prior sources, reliable
inputs, worker choice, opportunity cost, imports/harms, distribution, failure
and life-cycle renewal. A national self-sufficiency label is not resilience;
a cheap purchase needs an actual partner and continuing access. An ownership
transfer requires lawful title, price and control review, not free returns.
Production need not grow when better maintenance, less waste, safer work or
more discretionary time can meet the chosen purpose. A value conflict is
not resolved by maximizing GDP or an expert's composite score.

**N03 — Use the existing democratic chain.** Assembly law/appropriation sets
the public purpose, period, maximum exposure, permitted instruments, source
and loss-bearing boundary. Cabinet's accountable sector agencies/local
authorities propose and execute within that mandate; C04 merit appointments,
procurement, audit, reviewable removal and local competence apply. Public
enterprises separate commercial accounts from paid noncommercial duties.
Ordinary firms/cooperatives choose commercial investment under general law.
Neither a pension fund, bank, vendor, minister's informal instruction nor AI
may allocate a sovereign mandate or make a commercial failure a public debt.

Opposition, workers, affected residents and local bodies can introduce
alternatives and obtain source scrutiny through the existing Assembly
process. Follow appointment, money, information and execution controllers
together; public ownership does not make a party-controlled monopoly safe.
No new guardian office or mandatory central coordination platform is created.

**N04 — Make discovery possible without granting unchecked risk.** The
bounded portfolio can fund maintenance, training, research, open technical
infrastructure, demonstrators and supplier diversity. Use published access
and reasoned selection, suitable staged contracts and independent appeal,
with competition and anti-retaliation protections. Competent negative findings
within an honest authorized experiment are useful results, not fraud or a
reason to falsify success. Actual human interventions need their separate
consent, safety and lawful review; research approval is not deployment authority.
Do not force private firms into a national production list or punish a lawful
refusal to adopt AI. Public knowledge/access terms apply to financed work
under contract, not an automatic seizure of unrelated private knowledge.

[IC08–IC12](../09/03-public-investment-charter.md) now specifies N03 window
limits, recruitment, award/verification separation, instrument/title/return
accounts and expiry with a funded successor; no sovereign investment fund.

[NS national priorities](../../C06/54/04-productive-priorities-and-democratic-control.md)
now joins essential/home/human/ordinary-traded/technical capability under these
existing N/IC/SC roles. It creates no job quota, free training, automatic AI
productivity or new industrial authority. Actual safe receipt remains necessary.
