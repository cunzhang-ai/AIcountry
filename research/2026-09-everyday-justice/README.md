# Everyday justice: protection, fair process and recovery

23 September 2026 · Candidate research package · Not enacted or operational

This revision follows three situations through the justice system: domestic violence,
fraud and violent assault. The aim is less harm, accessible protection, fair adjudication
and recovery. More arrests or convictions are not themselves evidence of success.

- [Candidate provisions](candidate-provisions.md): exact evidence-rule corrections
  and a proposed public-safety and justice-delivery instrument.
- [Three case pathways](case-pathways.md): actors, thresholds, handoffs, failures,
  remedies and rival prevention approaches.
- [Capacity and evaluation](capacity-and-evaluation.md): shared staff and accommodation
  constraints, a reproducible synthetic calculation, and limits on outcome claims.
- [Decision record](decision-record.md): baseline, objections, compatibility and next action.
- [Sources](sources.md), [source manifest](source-manifest.json) and
  [artifact validation](validation.json).

The baseline is the local September 16 source-document snapshot, including criminal
code revision `0bf12b0a-4d87-4db1-b1bc-87f17bae7605:justice.criminal-code` and criminal
procedure revision `206f51b8-fed7-4777-b67d-22ef2089d3d8:justice.criminal-procedure`.
Existing manuscripts are preserved. This package neither creates current coercive
powers nor claims that its institutions exist. Current hosted law remains unverified.

The common research method remains [FRAMEWORK](../FRAMEWORK.md). This is a contextual
justice proposal, not a new universal social architecture or a completed criminal code.
