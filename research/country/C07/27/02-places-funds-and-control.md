# YC05–YC08. Suitable places, funds and independent control

[Stable child care](../27-stable-child-care-and-adult-transition.md)

**YC05 — Commission availability that a child can actually use.**
The local duty service maintains a paid public residual, alone or with
lawful participating localities, and can buy qualified plural capacity
under the public procurement rules. Count places by actual staffing,
skill, accessibility, lawful use, safe sibling capacity, accepting
operator and time. Keep each worker's ordinary, reserve and relief
shift separate. Include transport, clinical and education counterparts
and next-use readiness. In the elected budget, compare family support,
local retained capacity and matched cooperative or contracted routes.
Test simultaneous placement, provider failure and host duties under
JC. Use private restricted records for individual matching. AI can help
find feasible options and audit double booking. It cannot rank a
child's worth, decide removal, select the placement or assign consent.

**YC06 — Finance care and safe change without buying an outcome.**
Pay willing qualified carers' agreed care, expenses, respite and support.
Public and independent providers receive defined payment for actual
qualified provision or verified reserved availability. Pay no finder
fee, placement bounty, surrender payment, occupancy target bonus or
incentive to delay a safe return. The same receiving shift is counted
once. Preserve worker pay and continuing care through provider exit.
Prohibit use of the child's property to finance publicly owed care.
National MF finance supports high-need and sparse-area costs. Price
separately the costs of reserved capacity, actual care and transition,
and test weak sources in the regular elected budget.

**YC07 — Keep care in place while institutions settle payment.**
Use XR when placement, schooling or adult support crosses a boundary.
The host gives urgent receiving within its lawful scope. The placing
authority continues its existing case and payment duties until a lawful
accepted transfer. When responsibility or payment is genuinely disputed
or inaccessible, use the existing XR interim advance on its own terms
and settle final liability separately. No host reservation is spare
if an earlier protected task would be displaced. Give any affected
child a timely alternative or disclose actual failed receiving and
seek useful independent correction. A place cannot be ended merely
to move liability or meet a budget target.

**YC08 — Inspect the public operator as well as the contractor.**
Assign this sector function by primary law to the existing national
[ED04–ED05 inspection chain](../21/01-authority-employers-and-review.md).
Fund a distinct child safeguarding scope, competent protected staff and
independent evidence access. ED05’s own-office review applies. The
placing service, purchaser and provider cannot direct the inspector or
choose the final reviewer. The inspector can check staff, visit
operating places and hear children privately under bounded authority,
and require a reasoned safety correction under applicable law. Review
a disputed order in the ordinary court. Arrange qualified receiving
through any intake restriction or closure. Existing urgent safety powers
retain their own terms. Missing alternatives do not authorize unsafe
intake or abandonment. The placing service and provider remain answerable. Independent investigation of
alleged abuse keeps its own powers. Publish aggregate unmet placement,
disruption, useful remedies and staffing without identifying children.
The elected bodies control budgets and general law. They do not direct
findings or assignments for a named child.
