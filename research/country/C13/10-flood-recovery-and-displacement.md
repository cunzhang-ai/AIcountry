# C13. Flood recovery with local fiscal collapse

*Part of [C13 lived experience](../C13-lived-experience.md); integrated design 55 authored stress, not an observed event.*

A flood destroys rental blocks, a school, a substation, a land-record office
and the main local tax base. Some owners have valid insurance, renters have
none, an insurer disputes coverage, a customary land claim is missing from
the registry, and a downstream locality can receive evacuees only if its
water and classrooms are funded. The local council cannot finance its usual
services. A national minister proposes a fast redevelopment contract; an AI
hazard map marks the area permanently unsafe.

C10's local incident lead orders only lawful immediate rescue and temporary
exclusion. C07 receives each person for safe accommodation, income, care and
school without waiting for title or insurance. C11 preserves original and
reconstructed records; officials issue receipts and accept plural evidence.
The local elected council, with displaced residents and the receiving place,
compares safe repair, altered land use and supported movement. The Assembly
must decide any national reconstruction money, cross-boundary water works,
new borrowing and unresolved material priority. The minister cannot convert
a temporary exclusion into permanent clearance or offer a secret contract.

C06 shows what the reserve can pay now, when insurance might pay, the local
revenue gap, loan terms and the workers, sites, transformers and water pipes
actually available. A household's emergency help, the insurer's contractual
claim, compensation for any public wrong and the elected rebuilding grant
are recorded separately. A court reviews a false hazard class, a denied
insurance claim or unlawful taking; it cannot manufacture a replacement
school or levy a national tax. Until a lawful permanent choice, existing
service and property claims persist and a funded temporary receiver must
operate essential local functions. Displaced people keep practical access to
political participation and remedies.

The test fails if a signed grant cannot buy materials, an insurer's balance
is counted before it pays, the receiving locality is overloaded, renters
are erased, a map silently decides land rights, or emergency administration
becomes permanent rule. The route is specified, not funded or demonstrated.

[The FR household and place exercise](../../settlement/disaster-housing-and-recovery/02-household-and-place-case.md)
adds a late transfer and an accessible receiving place. It keeps the original
stress case and traces the distinct claims to actual safe housing.
