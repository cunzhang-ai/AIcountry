# C02. Private life, personal data and public control of information power

1 November 2026 · Design 96 · PD01–PD32 research selection, not enacted law

People need care, identity, proof, private relationships and public criticism
without selling a universal life dossier. Select **purpose-limited plural custody**:
public/private bodies answer for their actual uses, vendor chains and correction.
Consent, a contract, bodily choice, agent delegation, technical proof and a legal
decision remain different acts. No general transferable ownership of every fact.

Optional tracking can be refused without losing essential access. Necessary
care/payment/election records need their actual lawful purpose and limited scope;
a privacy request cannot erase a debt, defence, lawful judgment or corruption.
The person can challenge misuse without an app, proof wallet or the supervisor’s
permission. Source correction must reach consequential decisions under their law;
deleting a chat or training row does not prove a model has forgotten the person.

All ages, terms and clocks are normative candidates, not proven optimum or
actual treaty compliance. Stronger current/sector duties, lawful records and
pending claims survive. [Authored cases](../../settlement/personal-data/README.md)
expose access, correction, vendor, proof and notice-capacity failures. No actual
jurisdiction, source funding, safe staff/custody or observed better lives exists.
