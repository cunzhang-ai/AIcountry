# Public assistance is directed by the person seeking it

Design175 · [AH01–AH16](../../country/C04/07-person-directed-public-assistance.md)

**A free core service helps a person understand options and act on their own
aims before/across public requests, including when an agency is challenged.**
Existing AP/B13/C07/DP help remains; AH specifies the person-side public debtor,
actual provider/outside source and continuity relationship.

| Choice | Practical boundary |
|---|---|
| Person-side core | Explanation, supplied documents, chosen requests, communication and initial complaint/navigation; no unlimited private product |
| Actual public responsibility | Existing local receiving management, national funded floor/residual and qualified plural/outside providers |
| Genuine choice | No income/party/AI gate or coerced life assessment; a challenge needs no proof of agency wrongdoing to seek outside help |
| Private work and lawful filing | Confidential deliberation differs from an actual authorised filing/receipt and official decision |
| Optional AI | Competent source-based support with human/direct alternatives; no universal subscription, life graph or professional licence |
| Paid receiving/remedy | Qualified professionals, source, outside correction and accepted continuity, not a directory or a renamed chatbot |

[Examples and real rivals](01-scope-capacity-and-rivals.md) compares stronger
existing agency help, this bounded person-side service and a broader personal-
agent utility. [Decision/evidence](../../2026-11-person-directed-public-assistance/decision-record.md)
qualifies actual scope: no full national source, measured local benefit,
qualified independence, legitimate adoption or better lives is established.
