# Family models, primary context and unresolved consequences

*Part of [family/civil journeys](README.md); consulted 15 October 2026.*

**Household/status control** can simplify defaults and preserve familiar ties
but can trap individuals under a spouse/guardian and exclude informal or
plural care. **Contract-only ordering** can recognize diverse arrangements
but leaves coercion, children, incapable communication, public receiving and
third parties poorly protected. **Individual floor with scoped ties** is
selected: optional plural compacts with safe unilateral exit, genuine common
property/care claims, independent child law and bounded state interventions.
Informal cooperation remains voluntary; registration is not proof of better life.

The CRPD Committee's 2014
[General Comment 1](https://docstore.ohchr.org/SelfServices/FilesHandler.ashx?enc=Bp6OrEzlKfwJGS8ZwwExP67dRavke6dQwMqf20yoV3xROfBfxBaYFWNXry0kbocROXDjnUTGjWX8kNEJdhC5FQ%3D%3D)
distinguishes legal capacity from decision skill and calls for support rather
than substitute decision-making. It uses the person's will/preferences when
communication is difficult. This informs scrutiny of B23; it does not certify
our asset-preservation procedure or prescribe its 48-hour/14-day/90-day figures.
Whether the procedure avoids disguised substitution needs fresh independent
disabled-person and legal scrutiny, not a claim of automatic treaty compliance.

The [1996 HCCH child-protection convention](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/full-text/?cid=70)
provides a primary example of distinct jurisdiction, urgent protection,
recognition and safe cooperation. Article 4 excludes adoption decisions;
its existence does not give an imaginary country membership or make our
family/parentage/property defaults internationally recognized. Actual treaty
status, territory, foreign orders and rights-compatible transfer are open.

These sources do not choose plural compacts, genetic/intended parentage,
young-person clinical autonomy, estate shares, support age 21, periods or a
registry compensation guarantee. Those are explicit proposed values and
unvalidated parameters. C06's progressive large-transfer tax route continues;
the examples assume zero actual tax only for bounded arithmetic, not repeal.

Material objections include power inside multiparty compacts, concealed
commitments, difficult child bodily choices, dangerous removal deadlines,
unprovided care, inherited inequality, new fiscal liability, stale claims
and privacy cost. The draft proposes scoped commitments, actual authority,
prosecution/civil burden distinctions, independent advice, needs/ownership
accounting, received remedies and visible missing capacity. It has not
shown these measures sufficient, independent or better in practice.

Source-law, authority and actual affected participation are absent. Twenty-
four authored cases and arithmetic checks do not replace people, legal
review or observed outcomes. Preserve pending family/customary/property/
benefit rights during migration; no automatic compulsory reclassification.
Donor/gestational and cross-border law, sector powers/penalties and full
national resources remain unfinished. Next draft those remaining boundaries,
then audit ordinary-law coverage and simultaneous country delivery; reopen
on harmed agency, safety, independence, unfair loss or credible better models.
