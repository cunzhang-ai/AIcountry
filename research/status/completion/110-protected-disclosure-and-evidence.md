# Scoped finding. Protect a report before the next adverse act

Design202 adds a common civil route through independent channels
for serious wrongdoing. It distinguishes protected reports from
proof of the underlying allegation, enables B17 relief, tests
the same lawful decision defense and requires actual delivery of
remedies and a verified correction through lawful decision owners.
[Canonical clauses](../../country/C08/31-protected-disclosure-and-evidence.md)

Scoped European and U.S. sources supply actual alternatives.
An authored case separates reported service, corroborated
receipt, protected payroll and facts still in dispute. Actual
staffing, independence, funding, adoption and effects remain open.
