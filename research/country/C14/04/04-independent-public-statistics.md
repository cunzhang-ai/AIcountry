# PCO06–PCO10. Keep public statistics independent, usable and corrigible

*Design300 · Proposed national statistics and lawful reliance.*

**PCO06 — Give statistical production a distinct public duty.**
An adopting Act assigns a National Statistical Service to publish
an open work programme and independently produce core population,
work, prices, services and environmental series with separately
responsible source agencies and local producers. The Assembly
sets purposes and transparent appropriations. It hears proposals
from local bodies, opposition, workers and residents about what
is missing. Professional statisticians decide methods, quality
and publication. Sector agencies keep their own lawful records and
may publish distinct series with disclosed scope. Journalists,
researchers, communities and opposition may publish competing
accounts without permission. Official status gives no exclusive
power to define welfare, entitlement or truth. Record where the
state's categories miss a population or an important distinction.

**PCO07 — Separate appointment, money, data custody and release.**
The reference Act selects a professionally qualified director
by open merit examination, public hearing and three fifths of
the full Assembly plus one third of its frozen non-government
membership, rounded up, with a nonempty second group. The director
serves one six-year term. Appointment and vacancy are reviewable
in ordinary court under the adopting Act. One fifth of the full
Assembly may petition there for removal. Only the court may order
it on proved serious misconduct, incapacity or breach of a
specified statutory duty, with notice, reply and appeal. Neither a
minister nor the director can stop a lawful release for political
or commercial convenience.
A prequalified career deputy on the published rotation may act
on existing work for at most 180 days during vacancy or recusal.
Standing career publication duties continue after that limit.
The deputy cannot enlarge collection powers or reset the term.
Give the Service a distinct voted operating line and career staff,
with reasons and a protected hearing for material proposed cuts.
The actual Treasury payer executes due lawful funds. Source
agencies retain custody where sufficient and produce the exact
lawfully required outputs. Assign separately credentialed local
collection and publication routes where a shared controller could
block them. The external auditor checks resource use and release
obstruction within its mandate. It cannot select the estimate.

**PCO08 — Publish quality and failure on the same clock as results.**
Preannounce release times and publish to everyone simultaneously
without privileged political access. At the scheduled time provide
the result and scope or a dated quality or safety notice, with
reasons, what can still be used, the next update and a responsible
officer. Preserve the original and revised series, definitions,
coverage, disclosure limits, material breaks and recoverable method
records. When feasible, run an old and proposed method together and
show the difference in time for actual use. Publish qualified partial
counts or bounds with their population and unresolved coverage. A
substitute series earns suitability for each decision by evidence.
A blocked, experimental, delayed or partial estimate stays visibly
limited. An independent reviewer needs safe source inspection and
power to publish their own view. Affected people may challenge a
failure to collect, disclose a safe result or label a material limit.
A court can enforce the exact process and order a lawful release or
review. It cannot choose a number or compel unsafe disclosure.

**PCO09 — Keep being counted separate from being governed by a record.**
Use the least intrusive method that can answer the question. Compare
existing aggregate sources, voluntary sampling and direct local
coverage work before asking for new personal data. Any mandatory
collection or identifying linkage needs its own specific prior law,
necessity, limits and independent review. It is not created here.
Keep any lawful linkage keys separated from analytical access.
Limit retention, independently test disclosure across repeated
queries, and support protected replication with checked outputs.
Statistical copies of confidential records and their linkage keys
cannot be used for policing, immigration enforcement, benefits,
taxation, individual scoring, advertising or AI model training.
Safely published aggregates remain available for lawful public use.
Ordinary lawful access to original source records follows its own
authority. No one loses an individual service or protection because
they decline a voluntary survey or are absent from a statistical
frame. Communities can correct a missing category or unsafe
disclosure without supplying identifiable testimony to the public.
Handle breach and remedy under the relevant privacy law. Preserve
safe aggregate releases and correction history within retention
limits. AI may help find anomalies and compare methods. Staff
verify source fit and decide releases. Do not fill missing people
with simulated respondents and report them as observations.

**PCO10 — Decide what a changed series can lawfully change.**
For each material statutory or budget use, the competent decision
owner records the applicable series, population, reference date,
vintage and lawfully available contingency before relying on it.
When a series breaks or is revised, publish which decisions cannot
rely on it and review earlier material uses under EV17–EV24.
Apply the actual preauthorized fallback, if any. Where law leaves
a gap, seek an express decision with funded continuity. An office
cannot substitute a favorable number or count hypothetical savings
from a pending discretionary change. Preserve individual claims,
local transfers already due, accepted work and independent remedies
under their own source. A new prospective basis needs competent
authorization and qualified effects by group and place. Retain
pending challenges, staffed release duties and safe evidence when
transferring a series. Reopen on credible exclusion, common control,
unexplained delays, exposure through linkage or a more reliable
ordinary alternative.
