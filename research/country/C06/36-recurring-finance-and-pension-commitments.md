# C06. Pay for continuing protection without borrowing its future twice

11 November 2026 · Design 89 core and Design313 joint finance · RF01–RF21 candidate, not enacted law

**Select recurring taxes and pooled social insurance as the ordinary financing
of personal protection, services, maintenance and earned pensions. Retain
bounded, elected borrowing for investment, shocks and expressly chosen temporary
exceptions.** Reject permanent residual borrowing, compulsory personal pension
prefunding and unreceived future investment gains as default balancing devices.
There is no constitutional annual balanced-budget rule or technical veto.

**For future pension service only, select a benefit-base ceiling of one median
full-time annual wage `W`, replacing the old two-`W` candidate ceiling.** Retain
1.25% annual accrual, best 48, credited care, the uncapped 10% solidarity levy,
ordinary protection and every old earned unit/index/survivor claim. The choice
prioritizes ordinary replacement over larger future earnings replacement. It
does not save today's pension bill or establish an affordable national launch.

[SG](../C05/15-service-first-personal-security.md) now prefers the continuing
individual guarantee with services/earned rights; U remains a substantive rival,
no presumed destination. [FP](56-national-fiscal-settlement-and-source-boundaries.md)
retires130/131's selected national financial-reference status, retaining diagnostic
interfaces and all actual claims. No preference compels an unfunded new launch
or permits an office to cancel existing rights. Competent elected law chooses new burdens/coverage
and exact dates; existing E/NP/CP rights and shortage powers retain their scope.

The [new reference](../../settlement/recurring-finance/README.md) separates
gross cash, nonfinancial primary balance, recurring replacement, new assets,
financial subscriptions, coupons, principal, opening cash and conditional loans.
Even an extreme82%-all-income-band case that passes the old annual offered-loan
test retains a recurring deficit. A further84% stress shifts losses to nonbank
producers. These experiments select no tax rate and predict no behavior.

The insurance cash seed needed before a due payment can exceed its annual
subsidy. A 75-year summary cannot replace due-date liquidity, active workers'
past units, changing cohorts and a remaining-claims tail. Original histories,
complete costs, actual sources, public choice and operational independence remain
unknown. A synthetic comparison is not qualified actuarial or fiscal evidence.

No fiscal guardian, automatic tax/cut, public investment sovereign or new local
government tier is created. AI may expose dependencies, simulate alternatives
and assist accessible explanation; it cannot choose rates, recipients, clinical
priorities, private careers or political authority. Research method, contribution
permissions, software and constitutional holds remain unchanged.
