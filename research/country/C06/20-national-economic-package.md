# C06. One national production, distribution and financing package

*Integrated design 64, 15 October 2026; research selection, not enacted law.*

The country retains plural production and person-level protection, but a
proposed operating guarantee must now have a joined **production, income,
financial, ownership, external and physical account**. A GDP figure, public
asset label, income formula or successful fiscal check cannot establish
delivery. The [new reference](../../settlement/national-economy/README.md)
is the first matched five-sector/GDP/stock/resource comparison, not a
complete estimate of this country's 37 functions or a social-progress score.

| Canonical part | Current selected rule |
|---|---|
| [Production and counterpart accounts](20/01-production-and-counterpart-accounts.md) | GDP, wages, taxes, capital consumption, firm profit and planned/received flows remain distinct |
| [Tax, income and owned returns](20/02-tax-income-and-owned-returns.md) | Compare full schedules and price ownership/maintenance; no free dividend or automatic set-off |
| [Physical and external capacity](20/03-physical-and-external-capacity.md) | National private/public demand, ecological/standby limits, qualified labor and currency constrain the same package |
| [Transition and public decision](20/04-transition-and-public-decision.md) | Finance and commission enabling capacity before relying on later output; preserve claims, opposition and choice |

The selected change is this cross-account requirement and its hold on
unqualified launches. Preserve the existing institutional division and
income I01–I12 as a **conditional candidate**; the 50% taper is no longer
the only live income/tax hypothesis. An integrated refundable-credit rival
is investigated, not selected as law or presumed superior. Its reference
retains 25% additional gross work earnings after contribution through a
different joint charge/credit schedule, which raises material equity,
incentive, privacy and timing questions requiring fresh public comparison.

The ordinary-size full-flow case remains unfunded. A larger-economy/credit
case has planned cash surplus but lacks declared energy, water and export-
shock capacity, with care/justice staffing losses and transition still open.
No case qualifies an operating country. Tax rates, portfolio shares,
larger GDP and resource quantities remain authored assumptions; they cannot
be optimized into a governing mandate or substituted for original evidence.

[C06/21](21-demand-consistent-operating-reference.md) adds demand derived
from disposable income, paired producer/bank/monetary opening books, paid
replacement and monthly finance. Some new conditional comparators close
normal accounts; that does not revise the six old cases or establish actual
production, pensions or whole-country costs. The protected transition is
specified in C14, with prior funding still unverified. Next choose an
attainable whole income/pension/production package against retain/repair
and different ownership models. Account progress is not better lives.
