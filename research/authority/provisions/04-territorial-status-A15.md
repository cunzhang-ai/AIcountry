# Candidate A15. Cultural rights and territorial-status process

*Design 143 · [A10–A15 entrance](../05-candidate-provisions-A10-A15.md) · Proposed charter text, not enacted law.*

Persons may sustain language, culture and religion in community, with usable
public access, internal dissent and nonmembership protected. Distinct collective
rights, including applicable Indigenous land, governance and treaty claims,
retain independent sources and remedies. No office, group agent or AI assigns
a person's identity or dissolves a rights-holder by drawing a boundary.

Ordinary service/competence/boundary changes retain their valid statutory route.
Creation or loss of protected self-government, or national-sovereignty change,
uses the legitimately adopted charter route. Affected persons, groups, councils
and actual rights-holder representatives can seek accessible reviewable scoping
without a complete technical dossier. Enacted objective map criteria, competing
claims, independent process review and safe records precede judging support.

For that protected-status route, a petition supported by five percent of the
positive affected-resident electorate obliges a funded, bounded public hearing
and negotiation, not a first permission ballot or a sovereign transfer. Area
standing is age18, six cumulative national habitual months and a genuine area
electoral home, with existing forced-displacement protection. Citizens abroad
without that home retain national standing. D, first presentation seeking
qualification, fixes the area denominator subject to genuine corrections;
signers qualify when signing and on D. At least 12 calendar months are available
to collect support. The separate national A9 petition and RR06 denominator/date
rules are unchanged. Agents or account replication add no political standing.

A valid national A9 initiative needs no area-petition trigger or completed talks;
its lawful final-text certification also calls the required separate area question
under prior adopted law, without a new Cabinet veto.

Framework law supplies scope/qualification/negotiation clocks and resources,
limited EA administration, ordinary independent review, protected opposition,
plural advice and replaceable optional facilitation. No minister, majority leader,
facilitator or AI may indefinitely withhold an owed hearing or declare consent
by silence. Courts enforce owed process/rights, not an agreement or political map.

The exact protected-status settlement needs the existing national A9 approval
route **and a separately counted majority of valid yes/no affected-area votes**;
a tie or zero valid votes gives no approval, with no turnout quorum. Actual
ballot-day franchise applies under the already valid source charter. Proposed
new franchise cannot ratify itself. Each applicable individual/collective right
and foreign legal condition retains its actual scope. A national or area majority
cannot extinguish protected rights. Material changed terms need fresh necessary
mandates and approvals; ordinary electoral succession protections cannot be
bypassed by territorial labels or boundary changes.

Final text states operative conditions/dates, powers, sources and paid successors
for services, workers, claims, pensions, debt, title, courts and foreign duties.
Existing law and duties continue pending lawful effect; no vote automatically
cancels nationality, third-party debts, treaties or rights. Peaceful advocacy is
protected against service cutoff, strategic relocation and emergency retaliation.
Failure to agree requires reasons and leaves later peaceful proposals and lawful
remedies open. This route cannot extinguish applicable international rights or
invent legitimate whole-order founding authority.

ordinary implementation candidate, including exact prospective clocks and the

be legitimately adopted before use; no research decision approves itself.
