# An authorized source and an honest funding contract

*SF01–SF02; part of [C06/25](../25-source-finance-and-payment-continuity.md).*

**SF01 — Select a whole source package, not a balancing loan.** Before a
new public commitment, Treasury and the competent elected budget body expose
actual owned cash/FX, due obligations, prices, counterparties, existing claims,
protected continuity cash and independent operating capacity. Choose borrowing,
tax, funded capital or a different service/provision package through C03/C06,
with actual rights and affected burdens. A lender, monetary official, AI or
reserve custodian cannot make that fiscal choice. Existing due claims survive
failure; missing finance never makes a new entitlement or guarantee disappear.

In the working reference, preexisting G325/S25 cash is a preserved hypothetical
opening source, not independently established money. New bank subscription
175 to sovereign paper and the already selected G-to-S transfer 75 produce
G425/S100. This replaces the old additional borrowing 250/G500. The original
assets, bank capitalization, source transactions and lawful titles still need
verification; the revision does not certify the inherited opening.

Select actual amounts only within all joint cash, capital, concentration,
stable funding, currency and safe-receiving constraints. A low-water cash
floor is not a second spending pot. Keep safeguarded standby cash distinct
from unrestricted normal cash; demonstrate both remaining funded after due
payments, maturities and missing remittances. Unpriced new work stays unknown.

**SF02 — Match the permanent public-liquidity use openly.** The proposed new
sovereign instrument is a voluntarily purchased, transferable fixed-coupon
perpetual note: no holder right to demand principal or accelerate merely
because its market price falls. Interest is due monthly at one twelfth of
the annual fixed rate chosen through a lawful transparent issue/auction;
5% is only this reference's price assumption. Starting after year ten the
issuer may redeem at par on at least 60 days' notice with separately enacted
available funds. No automatic rollover, conversion guarantee, variable penalty
rate or hidden tax exemption. Existing fixed-maturity debt is not converted.

This deliberate contract removes a fabricated short new principal maturity,
not the debt or continuing coupon burden. Holders bear market/duration and
sovereign credit risk, with actual informed assent and independent pricing.
No presumed buyer, par valuation or ready resale market. A rate increase can
sharply reduce both bank equity and usable liquidity. Legislative authorization
under M15 must specify exposure, purpose, risk, terms and paid consequences;
a research issuer-call option cannot rewrite actual current borrowing law.
