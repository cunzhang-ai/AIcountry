# JS01–JS05. Give shared services a bounded democratic mandate

*Design312 · Proposed rules within [national and local authority](../01-national-local-authority.md).*

**JS01 — Agree the service and its actual area before delegating.**
Each participating council approves the same public compact by
recorded vote under prior enabling law. Define the function, service
area, affected people, current duties, participating councils, duration,
already funded capacity, contribution formula, ceilings, ordinary
delivery floor and exit obligations. Identify material effects beyond
the boundary. Compare an existing workable contract and a staffed
elected alternative. Functional areas can differ between transport,
clinical care and catchments. The compact carries only specified powers
the councils can lawfully exercise together. It supplies no tax,
general ordinance, taking, territorial status, new local contribution
or authority over a nonmember. Scope and source changes require the
actual approvals that authorize those changes.

**JS02 — Make decisions inside the compact by two recorded majorities.**
Each council publicly selects and may replace one voting councillor
and an alternate from its elected membership by its ordinary recorded
decision. They remain answerable to their council and can serve only
while holding their lawful elected office. For a material operating
choice within the funded scope, require affirmative votes from more
than half of all participating councils, representing more than half
of the compact's combined resident population. The charter fixes
auditable population data and the update schedule in advance. Absence,
vacancy, abstention and a tie do not reduce either denominator.
Only an authorized alternate can fill an absent or recused seat.
Publish the exact proposal, legal and fiscal scope, individual votes
and both totals. A chair has no casting vote. Routine dispatch stays
with qualified operators under the agreed rules. The two majorities
are a bounded operating rule for a delegated body. Elections retain
their separate equal-franchise rules.

**JS03 — Give opposition and affected people access before commitment.**
Fund a record custodian and lawful alternate presider outside the sole
control of a provider, chair or largest council. Give member councils
and their opposition representatives usable source access. Any member
council can bring an exact in-scope alternative to a decision by the
published useful date. Residents and other affected people can raise
a material effect, receive a reasoned reply and use direct independent
review of competence, rights and execution. Protect nonmembers,
minorities, and applicable collective or downstream rights through
their own law. A rule that appears within the map can still impose
an unauthorized outside burden. An office may enforce lawful delivery
and access; it cannot replace the political vote. Electors can replace
their councillors through the ordinary election, and councils can
change their delegates without changing residents' claims.

**JS04 — Keep the old service working when the vote fails.** If a
proposal fails either threshold, record why and retain lawful
current commitments and actual available funding. Use narrower agreed
options, paid ordinary repair or the existing competent route for a
material residual. The compact cannot declare assent after a missed
deadline, a refused meeting or a municipality's unpaid share. Test
whether a large member can block every useful change or small members
can repeatedly stall a due allocation. Protect individual orders,
continued service and any lawful urgent action under their own source.
Where repeated failure or durable wider powers justify another form,
compare a directly elected region with its complete jurisdiction,
budget, opposition, independent review and funded transfer. Adopt a
different form only through the competent prospective public decision.

**JS05 — Fund entry, review, replacement and exit.** The adopting
compact names the operator, lawful payer, independent review and paid
successors. Keep shared staff, cash, assets, contracts and obligations
on one actual schedule. Reserve the source needed for accepted
continuity on expiry, withdrawal or insolvency. Approve a successor
with actual rights, resources and acceptance before relying on it.
Publish safely aggregated delivery, costs, outside burdens and repeated
deadlock at each annual review and on material failure. AI may
compare maps, forecasts, receipts and tally records. It cannot
choose the jurisdiction, vote, authorize funding or confirm receipt
without competent evidence. Reopen on cumulative scope expansion,
persistent exclusion, material service loss, or a simpler or more
directly accountable arrangement with better usable results.
