# Design373 decision on home insurance exit and safe transition

## Baseline and selection

Pin Design372, the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md), NB13–NB16,
FR01–FR19, HL and disaster risk finance. These preserve valid cover,
claims, early public protection and safe post-loss repair. They leave
a gap when renewal, exclusions, premiums or deductibles make protection
unusable before a disaster.

[FR20–FR25](../country/C09/07/05-home-insurance-exit-and-safe-transition.md)
selects prospective notice and challenge, separate resident safety,
bounded transitional comparison and an explicit collective liquidity
check. The proposed 60 calendar days is a working notice period to
test, not an established cost or optimum. Existing contract, housing,
court, regulatory and elected source owners retain their powers.

## Alternatives and objections

Ordinary risk-reflective renewal can reveal actual costs but can also
leave people without a safe paid path. A targeted premium or deductible
bridge can protect current residents while genuine works or voluntary
receiving is prepared. It can also subsidize repeated exposure or
property value without improving safety. A public risk pool can spread
some risks and can also concentrate losses in the budget precisely
when disaster weakens revenues. Funded mitigation or relocation has
its own physical, cultural, property, host and transition costs.
Compare all routes with the same original residents, dated protection
and remaining losses. Do not price an unfunded public guarantee.

An enforced notice period can slow an insurer's lawful response to
material insolvency. The proposal preserves urgent prudential action
and actual claims. A creditor can have a real collateral interest;
its own cover and remedies remain on their terms with independent
challenge. Affected tenants and other occupants have direct safety
access regardless of a loan, policyholder or private adviser.
No insurer or AI gains authority over residence or relocation.

## Evidence, migration and next action

[Published records](sources.md) distinguish premium, coverage
availability, transition and public exposure. The
[matched case](../settlement/home-insurance-exit-and-safe-transition/01-renewal-deductible-and-two-claims.md)
checks the early premium, later deductible and collective claim
liquidity separately. Actual participation, qualified review,
adoption, funded delivery and superior outcomes remain unknown.

Preserve valid policies and pending claims, accrued priority, leases,
public duties, dates and protected evidence. Review existing
renewal and mortgage terms before prospective commencement.
A bounded next exercise would verify one cohort of actual eligible
renewal offers, private and public settlement dates and funded
housing alternatives without using identifiable public records.
Stop at the coverage and receiving comparison and priced unresolved
losses. Reopen if the bridge repeatedly expires without a successor,
a common insurer fails, actual households cannot use cover, or
durable mitigation or receiving better protects the same people.
