# A correction must reach someone and the regulator must be replaceable

*V09–V10; part of [C08/19](../19-regulation-and-bounded-enforcement.md).*

**V09 — Repair, claims and safe receiving are distinct.** A notice/judgment
identifies actual recipients, item/service, accountable executor, assets or
payer, useful due time and verified receipt. Refund, replacement, wage arrears,
clinical protection and environmental restoration are different obligations;
a fine does not discharge them. Direct B13/B17 relief remains available
without waiting for regulator investigation or successful punishment. Advice,
translation and independent evidence access depend on stakes and access,
not account credentials or ability to write a technical report.

A disputed cost-recovery bill proves necessary reasonable actual cost,
responsibility, source power and no double recovery, with review; it is not
a profit stream or uncapped penal daily charge. Emergency public assistance
needs its own authorization and recovery ground, not automatic personal debt.
Recall/closure names actual worker, client-property, payment, transport and
essential-service successors. No receiver can compel informal unpaid work
or book future fines, rescued assets or promised AI savings as available cash.
If the chain fails, preserve claims, protect people through existing due law
and expose the capacity/funding conflict to the Assembly and courts.

[PD17–PD28](../../C02/07-private-life-and-personal-data.md) now specifies the
information-rights Commissioner. Its single nonrenewable-chief selection
expressly replaces the generic staggered description below for that office
only; other sector panels and common V safeguards retain their scope.

[MP/MA/MR](../../C05/11-competition-and-economic-power.md) likewise replaces the generic staggered-panel wording for the markets office only, with a six-year chief, protected career case powers and direct ordinary courts. No other sector body is reselected.

**V10 — Regulated power cannot own its judge.** Retain C05/C06 sector bodies,
qualified public appointments, opposition-accessible scrutiny, staggered terms,
recorded confirmation, for-cause removal and independent legality review.
No new universal regulator, executive appeals court or industry veto is created.
Predesignated qualified deputies replace conflicts/vacancies; a minister cannot
pick a substitute for a named case. The existing elected appropriation supplies
staff, defense, independent sources, review and enforcement. Industry fees
follow enacted bounded schedules; fines never fund the pursuing body or
produce an investigator's quota. Budget restraint cannot become a secret
order to drop an ally's case; reasons and missing capacity stay inspectable.

Publish lawful aggregate enforcement, delays, false findings, corrected harm,
distribution and unresolved obligations. Protect private source accounts and
defense access; neither unlimited secrecy nor permanent public testimony is
required. Cross-sector referral preserves first receipt and accepted custody;
common controllers, vendors, funders and evidence sources are disclosed rather
than counted as independent checks. C03's popular policy control and C08's
court control survive replacement or defeat of the cabinet. AI can assist
analysis/access but cannot issue final contested guilt or multiply a mandate.

[BF/RA/BR](../../C06/39-bank-failure-and-protected-payment.md) now completes M09's three-seat panel and separate career deposit insurer/own-case powers. Its fixed votes/finite reserves and direct ordinary courts displace no other supervisor, monetary body or CJ jurisdiction.

[TA/RG/TX](../../C06/40-tax-law-and-accountable-revenue.md) now selects ordinary protected career Revenue/Review heads with own statutory case powers and direct full merits. This office-only rule supersedes any generic staggered-supervisor implication for revenue; other V/PD/MA/RA/EA/monetary bodies remain in their actual scope.

[WL31–WL35](../../C05/12-work-contracts-and-collective-power.md) now specifies
separate protected career Work Standards Director/Work Relations Registrar,
case/ballot/court powers and limited wage-advance payer. This replaces generic
V10 staggered leadership only for those work offices. VD03/V06 predicates/proof/
maxima remain; no new VD14, imprisonment or fine-financed labor government.

[OE26](../../C05/13/06-registry-courts-and-enforcement.md) now specifies a C04
protected-career Enterprise Registrar, Cabinet qualified appointment/discipline,
actual own-case officers/recusal, direct courts and a finite180-day routine deputy.
This replaces generic V10 leadership only for that office. Work Relations Registrar
is separate and gets only expressly enacted OE workforce-ballot scope. Parliament
must fund both; a renamed body/AI account is no new independent reviewer.

[GA07–GA12](../../C11/10/02-office-evidence-and-decisions.md) expressly selects a
separate General AI Safety Office, five-year nonrenewable qualified Cabinet-
appointed Director, protected own-case officers and direct full-merits courts.
It replaces generic V10 leadership only for this new subject; DP/other bodies
retain their powers. GA18 adds civil protective/compliance orders, no VD offence
or punitive fine. No actual office/power exists by this research selection.
