# Opening wealth, replacement and continuing claims

*Part of [C06 operating reference](../21-demand-consistent-operating-reference.md); design 64.*

Declare opening tangible assets, usable cash, bonds, deposits, equity,
collateral and liabilities with two sides. A public share is a legal claim,
not free acquisition, public control or cash. Unknown housing/other assets,
market values and accrued pension claims remain unknown. The reference
includes a producer, bank and monetary subledger so consolidation cannot
hide deposit liabilities or invent opening net wealth. Its Treasury/monetary
aggregation is an accounting comparator; their legal powers and usable
reserves remain separate. Monetary foreign assets are not automatically
Treasury spending capacity or a private bank bailout source.

The opening uses paid public assets 588, market assets 1,920, bank equity
100, bank reserves 175 and deposits 350. General-government gross bonds
830 include 100 held by the monetary unit; net market bonds 730 are held
425 by households, 100 by insurance and 205 abroad. Public/private market
book equity is 436.25/1,308.75. All these are authored opening assumptions,
not verified titles or market valuations. Prior working-cash lending 100,
bank capitalization 100, equity/bond placement 75 and enabling capital 108
need lawful independent source/transaction review. No automatic Treasury
credit or compulsory monetary purchase follows. Treasury cash 325 is not
future tax revenue; actual initial finance and the full training/overlap
programme are still unestablished.

Gross replacement 29.4 public and 96 market equals their declared annual
5% wear. It is paid even when there is no net expansion. Replacement,
ordinary maintenance 16 and noncash wear are different entries. Larger
investment needs real sources and expected uses; this steady comparison
neither prohibits growth nor proves all infrastructure can last forever.
All 37 current function costs/quantities remain unverified beyond the
bounded old envelope; an extra 100 expenditure sensitivity is not their cost.

The [Y01–Y16 candidate](../../C07/04-retirement-security.md) retains ongoing
accrual and restricted finance; an old 188 receipt margin does not price
future rights. The following closed-legacy comparator is a structural rival.

The closed-legacy rival collects 40 plus existing bond interest 5 to pay
the scheduled earned pension 45, while public basic pension/needs protection
continues. It assumes no new earned accrual, which is a consequential
alternative to the old 10% contribution/funded-accrual model. It cannot
cancel vested, contractual or other promised rights. Actual accrued claims
are a paired symbol `L(t)` in household assets and insurance liabilities;
never set them or their changes to zero. Insurance solvency, lifetime aging,
unknown other payouts and transition remain open. A smaller contribution
cannot be selected merely to balance one year's cash.

Retained profit or insurance accumulation needs an asset/counterparty and
purpose. In the declared comparison those savings finance public deficits,
subject to law, risk and actual willing contracts; the model supplies no
such commitments. Productive borrowing and funded future rights may be
legitimate. Zero net debt alone is not superiority. Map future claim holders,
losses and sources before an operating or intergenerational-security claim.
