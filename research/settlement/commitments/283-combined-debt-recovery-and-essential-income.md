# Design370 commitment. Preserve the same essential income once

[DCR01–DCR06](../../country/C08/13/07-combined-debt-recovery-and-essential-income.md)
requires the executing authority to reserve only a verified collectible
balance, preserve protected and third-party funds and record actual
payments. Each channel has a finite instruction and verified cancellation.
Give the resident, claimant and support recipient independent review and
actual return or support delivery. Account separately for bridge cash,
delayed collection and unrecovered claims.

The [matched case](../combined-debt-recovery-and-essential-income/01-one-pay-cycle-and-three-claims.md)
uses illustrative figures. It supplies no new source, authority or
calibrated recovery rates. Preserve original claims and dates on migration.
