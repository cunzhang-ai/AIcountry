# PB17–PB20: useful lives, market output and public cash are distinct

*Part of [C05/14](../14-ordinary-life-and-household-production.md); design 124.*

**PB17 — Keep household/private producer/public records as counterparts.**
One purchase may be a household use, seller receipt and taxable event, not three
national uses. Public provision changes the payer of an individual service; it
can reduce a matching private bill without creating free service or permitting
an automatic Q deduction. Maintain national G, local, restricted S/Y/client/
insurer/standby, private supplier/household and owned FX separately. Unreceived
sale, assessed tax, alleged recovered loss or private asset equity is not money
available to pay a current State duty. Actual original claims retain their law.

**PB18 — Do not invent cash from statistical output or unpaid work.** Owner-
occupied housing services may be imputed in national accounts; this is not actual
rent received, personal tax income under TA, usable I05 cash, an invoice or a
Treasury source. Keep actual repairs/insurance/utilities and old creditor flows
separate without claiming the real service is worthless. Private unpaid cooking,
care, travel, relationships and voluntary work matter to lives even outside core
market output; valuing their time does not make a compulsory labor pool or cash.
More market turnover or GDP alone cannot establish a better national settlement.

**PB19 — Match tax to actual paid work and its legal source year.** With the
supplied M30,000, full-source-year 10%worked Y and single.4 M allowance, 18,000 gross
means 1,800 restricted Y/840 PA and 15,360 net if received;30,000 means 3,000/3,000/
24,000. These are qualifying one-source worker examples, not actual withholding/
collection or country yield. A changed firm/job/pay must change person receipts,
PA/I05/Y with actual dates/resources/old pension units; do not retain wages of
removed prospective jobs or tax an unreceived wage as current spendable cash.
Firms' economic residual, taxable profit and next-April cash remain distinct.

**PB20 — Keep all national duties and legacy obligations in the open total.**
The 19 ordinary quote rows, shared bills, optional services and replacement invoices
are neither a full current household budget nor G cost. Old 37 bids/123 clinical/
care/housing/source work retain their scopes; education, independent democracy/
justice/audit/evidence, physical/ecological/foreign/source and all new current
obligations remain. Preserve actual old mortgages, private title, pension/index/
actuarial/guarantee and worker/creditor tails;600 m old loan and 9.15 bn housing-equity
lenses do not vanish or become cash because occupancy changed. No GDP/profit/
export residual, client/S sweep, compulsory shopping or free AI closes this account.
