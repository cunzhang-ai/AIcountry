# MF14–MF18. Fund the local floor through population and revenue change

*Design302 · Proposed fiscal allocation and service continuity rules.*

**MF14 — Use the cost of accessible provision and a representative
own-source share.** For the common-floor scope, the adopting budget and
law identify the comparable efficient cost, the share of representative
lawful own-source capacity allocated to it and other eligible recurring
receipts already assigned to it. The prospective residual national
allocation is the greater of zero and the cost less those two sources.
Cost fixed minimum networks, staff and access separately from the
marginal work that changes with need. A falling population does not
prove that a school, water route or staffed access point can be retired.
Count actual service populations, including safe estimates of excluded
eligible people and host burdens, and only the tasks for which the locality
has responsibility. Include a pool or national service once. Deduct a
receipt already netted in the estimate only there. Capacity uses the
actual adopted tax bases, standard effort, legal restrictions and
allocation rules under TN and MF02. A council's voluntary lower collection
does not increase this entitlement. An involuntary loss of base can
change assessed capacity. Cash receipt and an assessed share remain
different. This method creates no tax, automatic draw or authority to
use a restricted balance.

**MF15 — Revise on a public cycle with a distinct early correction.**
Use the annual budget cycle. In the reference procedure Treasury
publishes comparable old and proposed schedules at least 60 calendar
days before the funded period, with the source vintages, affected scope,
dependence on fixed sites and a meaningful challenge route before
application. Elected bodies set the method, statutory sources and
dates. The calculation officer applies the adopted method and gives
answerable reasons. The independent producer or qualified verifier
reports coverage and source limits under PCO. If a series breaks,
use the source law's actual contingency. Prefer a preauthorized
last qualified allocation for undisputed installments, plus a separately
funded verified-needs route under MF16. A local report of credible
material demand or involuntary revenue loss can trigger that review
without waiting for the next census. An unreconciled forecast or
political preference cannot replace an adopted input. Reconcile
actual amounts and preserve the duty clock even when a release is late.

**MF16 — Advance bounded money where the floor will otherwise fail.**
The adopting Act and appropriation name eligible common-floor work,
payer, usable source, ceiling, review date and the amount reserved for
concurrent local calls. Verify the cost and last useful date from
independent service and finance evidence. Pay the undisputed amount
first. Use a separately authorized advance only up to the lesser of
the verified uncovered cost of continuity until the next lawful
settlement and the remaining authorized applicable amount. A reference
advance period is one reconciliation period and at most 90 calendar
days from first covered risk. New labels and repeat filings do not
restart it. Preserve each timely payroll or recipient duty and each
actual acceptance. Compare an advance against an existing authorized
installment retiming, funded local bridge, shared provision or paid
national fallback. When more than one eligible locality needs the
same reserve, apply only prior enacted priorities. Report unmet work
and seek an elected funded choice where the reserve or lawful rule
cannot meet it. An automatic percentage cut, opaque contest or
unfunded promise supplies no residual allocation.

**MF17 — Reconcile structural change and the transfer's actual effect.**
Update durable shifts with qualified evidence and a voted prospective
source, scope and date. Price the least burdensome lawful access path
with affected councils and people, including consolidation or shared
staffing where practicable. Reduce fixed cost only after the old work
can lawfully and actually end or a funded qualified receiver takes it.
Show old and new gross need, standard effort, available cash and
displaced duties. No flat protection of all prior grants or use of a
deferred local saving as present national cash. Attribute an advance
once to the same final liability where the law permits. If it exceeds
the corresponding installment, recognize any lawful recovery or later
credit on its own date only after protecting subsequent duties. A
transfer correction does not undo lawful individual receipt. Failure
to renew an advance does not terminate the underlying service or claim.

**MF18 — Keep local choice, independent challenge and payment separate.**
The local elected body retains delivery choices, optional budgets and
lawful common-service partnerships. It may dispute both cost and
revenue capacity without seeking the paying minister's permission.
Residents can contest lost access under their ordinary route. Keep
independent review, underlying evidence and court enforcement funded
through the same period. The Treasury payer executes due amounts and
the local provider confirms actual use and receiving. The funder cannot
condition a correction on political agreement or a supplier it favors.
Publish safe aggregates and the effects on other localities. AI can
reconcile calendars, flag a weak denominator and compare stated
alternatives. It cannot choose fiscal weights, restrict a grant,
establish resident eligibility or certify receipt. Adopt prospectively
with paid succession for prior grants, original claims, data custody and
accepted contracts. Reopen on persistent bridging, strategic undercollection,
missed people, stranded fixed capacity or a stronger shared-service path.
