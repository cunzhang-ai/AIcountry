# C07. Interpersonal safety and survivor support

*Part of [C07 social floor](../C07-social-floor.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

A person exposed to intimate-partner, family or sexual violence, stalking or
coercive control can enter through a clinic, independent advocate, social
service, school where appropriate, safe communications channel or police.
Every first receiver must provide a private, accessible response, explain
confidentiality and its narrow legal limits, assess immediate needs with the
person, and stay accountable until a named service actually accepts the case.
Help does not depend on a police report, criminal charge, separation,
residence document or a prediction score. The person may choose a supporter
and decline an offered referral without losing urgent care or subsistence.

The health network supplies time-sensitive examination, treatment after
sexual assault, reproductive and other urgent care, and continuing physical
and mental-health support with informed consent. A clinical visit is not an
automatic forensic examination. Where enacted law allows, an independent
evidence-preservation option explains storage, access and expiry even if the
person is not ready to make a police report. The clinician treats the person;
police and prosecutors make their separate lawful decisions. Children and
adolescents receive age-appropriate clinical and safeguarding support with
their own voice and privacy under C08's child rule.

The local housing authority maintains an actual safe short-stay option and
a route to stable housing, including accessible places and safe travel. A
bed that requires the person to abandon a child, disability support or a
needed animal without a safe arrangement is not an accepted placement.
The benefits and payments offices offer a direct, confidential claim and
protected receipt when a partner controls the household account; an earlier
joint filing does not make the controller the only payee. Schools, care and
employers make lawful continuity arrangements for children, work, paid leave
and communication without revealing a protected location. The person retains
tenancy, property, maintenance and debt claims for later lawful decision; an
urgent safety transfer does not silently forfeit them.

With the person's safe consent, a trained coordinator may make one plan across
health, housing, income, care and legal assistance. Each sector keeps its own
legal duty and independent remedy. Information exchange is limited by purpose,
recipient, retention and review; one combined case file must not expose a
location to a respondent, contractor or unrelated agency. A non-digital and
private contact route matters when a partner controls the phone, account or
transport. If a person cannot safely receive a callback, the receiver agrees
another safe contact method rather than closing the case as unreachable.

Support continues through an uncharged case, failed prosecution, order expiry,
reconciliation attempt or move across local boundaries when need and enacted
eligibility continue. A person can return after a prior refusal or separation
attempt without being blamed or put at the end of a new queue. Services do not
promise that every private relationship can be made safe, and staff do not
coerce someone to leave as a condition of help. Independent advocates and
reviewers test delays, denied places, unsafe disclosures and the actual
execution of remedies. [C08](../C08/10-interpersonal-violence-protection-and-fair-process.md)
governs force, protection orders, criminal process and the respondent's rights.
No real service map, affected participation, staff, budget or outcomes exist.

[WL54–WL60](../C05/12/10-paid-safety-leave-and-confidential-receipt.md)
protects paid work time and safe payroll while a worker uses necessary
safety services. Service entitlement, urgent cash and consent retain
their own authority and earlier duties.

**Protect the first safe night through actual receiving.** Match lawful reservations for a suitable place, safe travel and usable private payment. Keep the first receiver responsible until acceptance. A criminal case outcome changes none of those duties by itself. If payment status is uncertain, protect the principal and use only independently authorized urgent relief.
