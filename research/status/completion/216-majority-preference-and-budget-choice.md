# Majority preference and the final budget

Design308 · Scoped result for [country completion](../../DESIGN-COMPLETENESS.md)

The [canonical budget change](../../country/C06/60/08-majority-comparison-and-final-budget.md)
orders an eligible text first if a full-seat majority explicitly prefers
it to each alternative. If such a preference is absent or the first
text fails its binding vote, the earlier sequence supplies the remaining
votes. Under a changed eligible docket, fresh comparisons and scrutiny
protect the remaining choice.

The [worked first-budget comparison](../../settlement/integrated-first-programme/09-majority-preference-and-budget-choice.md)
reproduces different choices over the same assumed feasible options.
It also reproduces abstention, cycles, protected fallback, failure to
enact and loss of quorum. The votes and amounts are authored. The
budget retains actual funding and delivery requirements after approval.
No legitimate adoption, paid parliamentary rehearsal, independent
affected review or improved lived outcome has been demonstrated.
