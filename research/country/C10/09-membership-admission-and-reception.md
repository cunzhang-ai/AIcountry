# C10. Membership, admission and actual reception

*Integrated design 81 · Consumer of [MB01–MB20](../C02/06-citizenship-and-secure-belonging.md); research, not enacted law.*

MB is the sole canonical owner of citizenship, secure residence, ordinary admission
classes and removal-reference rules. C10's public protection office receives claims
at borders, sea and controlled/contractor sites. Ordinary admission has a prospective
Assembly capacity/category schedule; citizen/secure return, family entitlement
and protected claims retain separate routes. R11 customs clears goods and does
not decide nationality or a person's protection. No carrier, sponsor or AI has
the final status/return veto. Existing treaty duties are not cancelled by MB.

The state receives credible protection/nationality-undetermined claims with
express provisional stay/work under MB, safe intake, counsel and review; E01/I01
alone creates no migration power. Nationality and statelessness inquiry remain
separate from non-return assessment. Do not contact a possibly persecuting consul
before protection review, or assume a foreign nationality/receiver from a paper
promise. An adverse decision cannot outrun effective independent challenge.

Actual ordinary residents continue their I/S claims during status dispute.
The responsible national office funds receiving, while a named local authority
accepts safe nonclosed housing, clinical/learning and child/care handoff. One
immigration referral does not end responsibility or prove that a place exists.
Employment/family exit has MB12's independent bridge without a criminal-report
gate. Permission, actual wages, tenancy, service receipt and political eligibility
are different legal/evidential states; no private sponsor owns them together.

Citizens/secure residents are not banished for crime; P/K accountability remains.
Before that status, removal needs MB13's actual grounds, accepting safe destination
and suspensive court review. MB14 permits only tightly bounded adult execution
restraint on prior judicial authorization; no routine migration prison or child
execution custody. All actual custody grounds/clocks remain distinct; aliases,
contractors, transport delays or new case labels never restart limits. Failed
execution returns the person to safe liberty and any applicable MB10 protection.

[C06 capacity](../C06/30-membership-and-reception-capacity.md) exposes independent
caseworkers, interpreters, source custodians, ordinary courts, receivers, travel,
actual cash and correction duties. [Adversarial journeys](../../settlement/belonging/01-adversarial-journeys.md)
are authored, not observed safe border operations. No diplomatic agreement,
external document acceptance, actual trained staff, funding, adoption or improved
life outcome has been established. Lawful commencement needs exact source/claim
migration; secure belonging cannot self-authorize a new territory or electorate.

[WF111–WF116](../C06/58/25-ethical-health-workforce-recruitment.md)
governs funded recruitment campaigns separately from lawful individual
mobility, admission, recognition and sponsor exit.
