# C07. Health service, clinical judgment and patient control

*Part of [C07 social floor](../C07-social-floor.md); design164 HG01–HG26 research candidate, not enacted law.*

**Select an integrated National Clinical Service (NCS) public employer/backbone,
internal purchasing/access and outside clinical-rights review, retaining qualified
plural additional routes.** No routine internal purchaser/provider market,
compulsory new board or patient consent by management follows.

The national Assembly enacts an essential health entitlement and its revision
procedure. It identifies prevention, primary and urgent care, acute and
chronic treatment, mental health, rehabilitation, essential medicines,
disability-related support and palliative care, with a lawful route for
sexual and reproductive services. Exact interventions, response standards
and contested value choices require legitimate public law, clinical evidence
and affected-person participation; an expert list or insurer algorithm
cannot silently redraw the entitlement. No one loses essential coverage
because employment, residence paperwork or a payment platform changes.
The [reproductive and early-life route](09-reproductive-health-and-early-life.md)
specifies a continuous service and its C08 consent and parentage boundary
without transferring clinical judgment to a registrar.
The [population-health route](08-population-health-and-prevention.md) assigns
ordinary monitoring, outreach and cross-place prevention separately; a
clinical patient's record is not a general public-health command or data pool.
The [serious-illness route](10-serious-illness-and-end-of-life-care.md)
specifies relief, patient choice, accepting care and verification near death;
a palliative label cannot become an unreviewable coverage or cost gate.
The [mental-health and substance-use route](11-mental-health-substance-use-and-crisis-care.md)
funds direct voluntary community and crisis access; a referral or diagnostic
code does not count as accepted treatment.
The [interpersonal-safety route](12-interpersonal-safety-and-survivor-support.md)
requires time-sensitive, confidential clinical care after violence, distinct
from forensic evidence collection or a police-report decision.

National pooled finance pays NCS's needs-adjusted public clinical backbone and
qualified contracted additional providers under S/SC/HN; accreditation alone
creates no automatic contract or public-funding right. Its **clinical regions** follow patient
journeys and specialist capacity; they are service networks, not elected
political regions or a transfer of C04 authority. NCS's purchasing/access
function maintains reachable first-contact and referral routes, while each network
names facilities, qualified teams, emergency transport, after-hours cover,
medical supply, records and a provider of last resort. A local front door
gives a receipt and accountable referral; a completed referral is not a
completed consultation. Fixed-capacity and high-need costs are funded rather
than rewarded only when easy visits occur.

The patient receives a timely clinical assessment, understandable options,
informed consent or a narrowly lawful emergency exception, a usable record
and a way to correct it. A clinician owes independent professional judgment
within law and must disclose a material conflict; the purchaser may set the
public package but not secretly order an unsafe individual denial. Patient
privacy, a second opinion for material irreversible decisions, and access to
an independent safety/rights reviewer protect against both state and private
provider power. The [C08 crisis rule](../C08/09-mental-health-crisis-liberty-and-consent.md)
rejects diagnosis-specific civil detention and forced treatment in this
reference. Immediate rescue and ordinary medical emergency care have
distinct general-law grounds, minimum duration and independent review;
a clinical label or missing appointment does not enlarge them.
AI may support documentation and options, never
make an unattended final denial or coercive decision.

Scarce appointments, beds, medicines or organs are allocated under published
clinically relevant criteria and applicable rights, not wealth, political
standing, employment or a model's social-worth score. Elected law controls
the coverage boundary and public budget; qualified clinicians apply
case-specific safety and urgency; an independent route corrects mistake or
discrimination. A shortage is recorded as an unmet claim with interim safe
care where possible, not disguised as a change of eligibility. New
technology and medicines receive plural evidence review, explicit cost and
opportunity-cost analysis, affected-party challenge and an elected decision
on material general coverage. A clinical reviewer does not enact taxes.

A provider's closure, epidemic, cyber outage or transport failure requires
the responsible network and purchaser to name a funded receiver, preserve
ongoing treatment and transfer lawful records and workers with patient
notice. Independent safety inspection, incident learning and compensation
or legal redress remain separate from purchasing and professional licensing.
Track avoidable delays, harm, patient time and actual recovery by place and
need, without reducing health to one target that encourages case selection.
C06 must count trained staff, shifts, medicines, laboratories, power, water,
sanitation, imports and substitute capacity once. Clinical teams separate
hazardous waste at source; the [C09 steward](../C09/06-sanitation-and-waste.md)
remains accountable for safe transport, treatment and final disposal.
No plan may book the same
clinician or vehicle for every failing service. The [decision
record](../../2026-10-life-course-services/decision-record.md) compares this
public-backbone choice with coherent public-only and insurance-led rivals.

[S01–S04](13/01-health-and-coverage.md) now specifies the candidate necessary-care boundary and complete-path timing; material exclusions still require elected law, clinical safety and independent challenge.

[C08 B21–B23](../C08/16/01-age-decisions-and-support.md) now proposes supported
young-person clinical choice and narrow asset preservation without general
guardianship. A family compact or custody of property does not authorize
treatment or override a valid bodily refusal. New ages/strong autonomy are
research choices requiring fresh clinical/affected review, not adopted law.

[TD09–TD10](../C08/22/11-water-food-and-clinical-duties.md) and
[C07/14](14-medicines-devices-and-safe-methods.md) now separate product/evidence,
competence, actual clinical choice, coverage and received care. Foreign evidence
recognition is scoped; no technical approval waives consent or proves access.

[HN01–HN12](19-clinical-networks-and-specialist-access.md) now adds actual
local/visiting/regional/external receiving, clinical responsibility and necessary
journey direct payment/advance, with existing public residual/consent/review
duties. It uses this purchaser/network, not a new sovereign referral agency;
all actual source/clinical/foreign capacity and costs remain unqualified.

HG's public employer/management/review method needs coordinated prospective
law and paid OP/T successors. Existing S/HN care/consent/journey/source duties
and earlier clinical/court/work/election clocks persist. [The comparison](../../settlement/clinical-governance/README.md)
retains separated-purchaser/common/retained rivals and actual source/readiness
unknowns; integration is no proof of care quality, independence or better lives.

[MQ04–MQ09](26-critical-medicines-and-safe-continuity.md) assigns shortage
receiving to the NCS clinical route, with separate product release, consent,
rights review and continuing care through the next treatment window.

[AEC01–AEC05](05/05-assistive-equipment-continuity.md) assigns responsibility
for covered essential mobility, positioning and communication equipment.
Count useful fit and actual function through repair, recall or supplier exit.

[HDC01–HDC06](05/06-hospital-to-community-and-safe-receiving.md) sets the
handoff to an actual destination and accepting care. It preserves patient
choice and tests the next urgent bed, cash and service independently.

[SNR01–SNR08](../C04/08/07-essential-service-network-reconfiguration.md) applies when a material network change
affects access. Qualified clinical decisions, care continuity and consent retain
their sector authority.
