# Design292. Opening liabilities and protected budget choices

10 November 2026 · Research selection · No actual enactment

## Baseline and decision

[Design291](baseline.json) retains the opening inventory, WF's three
cash and finance views, HT's commitment disclosure, and independent
review of ordinary procurement and claims. The gap is how a late
or contested opening exposure changes exact choices while already
due service continues. Apply the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md)
and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md). Codex assisted
the comparison. Actual claimant participation, elected adoption
and independent professional qualification remain open.

## Whole alternatives and selection

- **Retain the forecast plus narrative disclosure.** This is simpler
  and can inform ordinary choices where a risk cannot affect their
  funding or useful time. In the authored case a fully disclosed
  call still defeats the larger release if cash is not tested by date.
- **Pair the opening inventory with owner-specific payment paths and
  exact alternatives.** Select this disposition. Preserve separately
  verified dues, disputed claims and unbounded exposures. Use a funded
  contingency for a plausible call, without recognizing the claim
  by doing so. At eleven units the preparation choice fits both
  priced paths, while the retrofit needs twelve in the adverse path.
  Actual release requires its own lawful authority and receipt.
- **Use wider national underwriting or a central funding backstop.**
  A sufficiently funded, lawfully priced and bounded guarantee
  can help a local authority keep service through a shock. It moves
  risk to the national source and may weaken creditors' and public
  bodies' incentives to establish authority. Compare it as a separate
  elected choice with an actual ceiling, cost, funding, loss and
  recovery schedule. Avoid an inferred central promise from the
  consolidated accounts.

## Authority, objection and migration

A reserve can unnecessarily displace work, an optimistic opening
claim can hide risk, and public disclosure can prejudice a pending
dispute. Give competent independent bodies controlled access
to originals and publish a safe account of aggregate exposure,
provenance and material omissions. Preserve the originally valid
contract, wage, care, pension, election and remedy dates. Allow
an affected creditor or resident to challenge unsupported delay.
AI may help compare references, amount and timing but cannot
determine whether an obligation is valid, erase a disputed claim
or authorize spending. Cash exposure in a scenario is separate
from accounting recognition and actual settlement.

Use the current or separately authorized bridge if service would
otherwise fail. A successor receives only lawful custody, rights,
duties and funded execution. Existing accounting, adjudication,
creditor and independent review competence persists until lawful
change. Preserve all prior versions and the identity of any
unresolved action. A budget choice or fiscal revision cannot alter
the result of an election or extend an office.

## Next bounded decision

In one consenting lawful setting, reconcile one public-enterprise
return, one potential guarantor and one affected local service.
Obtain dated original authority and exposure with protected review.
Test known due cash, a jointly plausible call, a later recovery and
an actual service bridge. Compare the same narrower and larger
motions on usable balances and protected receipt. Reopen if a
new source changes a choice, scope is contested or the reserve
displaces an existing duty. No legal or operational readiness
or observed improvement is established.
