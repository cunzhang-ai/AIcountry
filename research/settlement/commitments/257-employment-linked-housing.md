# Design344. Pay for a safe housing transition when a job ends

[RT26–RT31](../../country/C09/18/06-employment-linked-housing.md) separates
the employer's lawful transition cost, public support, qualified receiving
and existing wages and tenure. Count shared housing and funds only once.

The [decision](../../2026-11-employment-linked-housing/decision-record.md)
retains independent provision and ordinary tenure as real alternatives.
Actual funding, safe placement and independent review remain open.
