# Copenhagen 2026. Align dates without treating different records as one incident

Statistics Denmark's monthly cash-benefit table KY26 gives the
following administrative totals for Copenhagen Municipality,
code 101, and the published total social assistance category.

| Benefit month | Affected persons | Reported amount, DKK million |
|---|---:|---:|
| February 2026 | 8,880 | 94.056 |
| March 2026 | 8,901 | 93.847 |
| April 2026 | 8,916 | 93.051 |

In March the four subcategories contain 8,914 category memberships,
while the official total counts 8,901 affected persons. The published
method removes duplicate persons in the total. The difference is not
a count of additional households or necessarily the number of people
with more than one category. The four March amounts sum to the total
at the published precision. Neither the monthly count nor the amount
identifies the ordinary payment run, each recipient's actual date and
usable balance, or anyone excluded or unable to claim.
[Statistics Denmark KY26](https://m.statbank.dk/TableInfo/KY26)
· [Statistical definitions](https://www.dst.dk/en/Statistik/dokumentation/documentationofstatistics/cash-benefits/statistical-presentation)

The [election record](../election-cycle-first-budget/01-copenhagen-election-and-ordinary-duties.md)
dates the call to 26 February and the poll to 24 March. The published
benefit schedule implies an ordinary reference payment date of
31 March. The council's later 30 April transfer was an internal
authorization, with DKK 34.951m shown on both sides of the vote's
supporting tables. This is a forecast and approved purpose transfer.
The transfer and the monthly benefit amounts are different fiscal
scopes. They cannot be used as one pool of spendable cash or proof
of when any authority could first pay a worker or recipient.
[Copenhagen council decision](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Borgerrepr%C3%A6sentationen/m%C3%B8de-30042026/referat/punkt-24)

| Protected scope | Published record now available | Still needed to establish joined operation |
|---|---|---|
| Household payment | City-month affected persons and administrative amounts, plus ordinary schedule | Authorized March batch, settled amount and dated usable receipt or correction |
| Election access and evidence | Call, held poll, cost estimate and later council authority | First usable source, staffed and accessible receiving, and actual correction or evidence availability during loss |
| Home care | Same-year cost and organizational evidence and reported transfer scope | Actual March paid staff, accepted qualified visits, site and common dependency |
| Court remedy | Independent due-time and execution rules in the proposed comparator | A real matched case or safe case series, original local time and verified execution during the same window |

The [city service budget](../service-headroom-and-usable-budget/README.md)
and [home care transition](../home-nursing-and-protected-demand/README.md)
supply same-year context without filling the March care or court cells.
There is no identified real common failure in these records. Keep the
observed calendar and the [authored loss](02-authored-operating-alternatives.md)
separate. The remaining decision is whether an exact paid local and
national package can protect the same duties at the same time and
through a second call.
