# Change a local intake route after a joint budget vote

*Design492 · Constructed comparison of competence, access and release.*

Continue the [school, care and scrutiny budget](../education-care-and-scrutiny/01-three-commitments-one-opening-calendar.md).
The school window now has a separately effective national grant and
a local receiving decision. Assume the grant names the supported
cohort, funds 4 units for the same two optional school sessions and
requires usable receiving evidence. It leaves the compliant choice
of intake medium to the council. The council already owes a
separate individualized accommodation to another pupil. The
independent hearing and already binding care continue on their
own cash, staff and authority. All amounts and cases are authored.

| Change proposed by the council | Competent action and consequence |
|---|---|
| Move intake from a public counter to a private staffed room at the same school | The same paid worker, protected records, two eligible pupils, dates and funding carry through. A lawful accessible change that fits the delegated terms needs logged local authorization and an independently checkable release decision. The competent local authority applies any material network procedure. The council must retain the authority. If its own instrument expressly binds the room, change that instrument under its own law. The unchanged national grant needs no new assent. |
| Replace human intake with a mandatory tool that takes only typed answers | One pupil can use it. The other cannot give their communication in that medium. The responsible authority can qualify it as an optional support route if lawful. It cannot rely on the mandatory design for the affected release. Preserve the original contact date, ordinary assisted intake, a separately due accommodation and direct appeal. |
| Move funds to a different cohort or remove the local obligation required as a condition of the national grant | The change exceeds operational authority. If the instruments tie an uncommenced grant tranche to a specified instrument or cohort, re-examine that exact affected scope under BCV16–BCV20. Obtain any necessary distinct votes before reliance. An already effective grant, local duty or signed commitment follows its legal terms. |

## Four units and two actual pupil journeys

In the qualified room plan, the two sessions plus staffing,
interpretation and protected intake cost 4 in total. A typed-only
vendor plan also costs 4 after support and transition. It confirms
two invitations but only one pupil can complete intake. The second
pupil uses supported speech to request the session and reject only
the vendor's proposed account. The vendor generates a refusal of
the service itself. Keep that rendition disputed and preserve the
original receipt and actual meaning through DP70–DP77. If the
account-only gate rejected the attempt before recording it, use
ordinary proof to establish its original date. No new application
or premature adverse clock follows from the mistake.

If an optional tool plus a paid human route also costs 4 and both
pupils can use it on time, the council can choose between the
qualified alternatives. A human label alone gives no assurance.
If the accessible room loses its trained worker, offers only a
phone number the pupil cannot use, or transfers the same interpreter
away from the independent hearing, the room plan also fails. Two usable intakes still fail the release
test if the challenged vendor controls the only route for appeal.
Verify actual support, timely start, scope, confidentiality and an
independent route for correcting what was heard.

A fifth unit promised next week cannot fund a human route needed
before tomorrow's cutoff. If the council already has a separately
funded lawful path for one pupil, release only that separately
authorized scope on its verified delivery cost if the enacted terms permit it. Otherwise hold the
dependent optional start and record the missed opportunity. An
already due accommodation continues through its own lawful route.
If that route fails, record nonreceipt and use the applicable
independent remedy; the optional grant supplies no replacement
appropriation or added power. Apparent savings do not support another
service until they are actually verified, usable and separately authorized.

## Make the change contestable and reversible

The council publishes a brief comparison of exact changes, people
affected, implications for the national agreement, deadlines and
paid alternatives. Protect private originals, support recipient
correction, and record each legal effect and delivered result
separately. Give the competent elected bodies and opposition
usable notice where a material choice changes. Keep the old or
another lawful accessible route where useful and funded until
verified receiving. Ask for fresh authority only where the changed
act needs it. A material legal replacement cannot inherit earlier
assent. AI can compare scopes, produce optional drafts and flag
missing access. It cannot decide consent or give an old vote a new
meaning.
