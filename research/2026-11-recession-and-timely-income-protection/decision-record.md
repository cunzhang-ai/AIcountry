# Design422. Fund the actual path from job loss to timely protection

## Decision and baseline

Design421 keeps standing personal income and finite earnings protection
available through economic shocks. It treats borrowing and monetary
decisions separately. The institutional gap is between a lawful claim and
a paid, reachable route when demand rises and revenue falls at the same
time. This revision proposes an operating schedule for the existing
income and hardship duties. It was prepared with AI assistance. Only
competent elected law and budget decisions can authorize its resources.

## Alternatives and working selection

Ordinary staffing and a flexible appropriation are economical when
the case mix and usable capacity are stable. A cash-only reserve
protects payment capacity but leaves claims unexamined when trained
work is scarce. A larger permanent staffing baseline may be preferable
for a durable increase in demand. Its recurring cost and effect on
other duties must be counted. An extraordinary universal or employer
payment could reach some households quickly but has different
coverage, source and work effects and cannot replace continuing
individual claims. Do not infer it from a cash-shortfall trigger.

Select a separately priced joint operating reserve for a time-limited
surge, with a smaller activation where it suffices. Keep ordinary
benefits and urgent protection on their own legal grounds. Show the
first and next windows, including exhaustion. The authority can
decline an optional staffing commitment when cash is short. It
cannot reduce lawful benefits through an allocation limit. The
constructed case demonstrates two independent constraints. It does
not set a national reserve size or prove that the ordinary route can
handle the next downturn.

## Evidence, authority and migration

[Primary audit evidence](sources.md) documents claim surges and
delays involving staffing, access and processing in other programmes.
It does not calibrate the candidate costs or establish that borrowing,
staff redeployment or any particular intake method will be available.
The example is constructed. No claimants, sector workers, Assembly
or independent review authority participated.

Apply prospectively to new operating budgets. Preserve existing
eligibility, original claim and payment dates, available hardship
protection and independent correction. The Assembly sets the source,
activation limits and expiration. The service owner operates within
the funded staffing mandate and Treasury executes lawful payments.
Independent fiscal and judicial review retain their own access,
funding and powers. A court may enforce an existing obligation within
jurisdiction. It cannot supply staff or an appropriation. Record the
failure if none of the available routes can deliver in time. Transfer
open work, earned pay, payment evidence and unresolved remedies to
named funded successors.

## Scoped result and next test

Documentary evidence and the constructed comparison support a research
selection. Actual finance, qualified alternate staffing, legal authority
and resident receipt remain open.

For one income-protection authority, replay a representative sudden
demand increase and a following payment window using protected
aggregate and source records. Compare ordinary staffing, a larger
permanent baseline and temporary joint capacity with the same
eligibility and due dates. Complete when independently confirmed
staff time, lawful cash, actual payment paths, unresolved claims
and effects on displaced work reconcile in both windows. No exercise
budget or operating authority has been committed. Reopen on prolonged
demand, excluded people, missing remedies, false staffing assumptions
or a stronger ordinary route.
