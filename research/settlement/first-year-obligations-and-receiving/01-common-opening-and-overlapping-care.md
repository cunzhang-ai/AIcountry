## What to do on the actual transition dates

Carry both the city transfer and the separate January regional
change. The city transfer estimate is 721.4 in 2026 and 714.2 from
2030. The forecast pressure of 24.1 concerns the transferred scope
and is a separate forecast, without a verified incremental source.
The early February estimate that priced municipal acute nursing
cannot be used as the price of the later regional choice. Keep
the stated payer, task, counterparty and scope for each item.

The city budget annex gives a matched projection for this same
regional reform. In 2027, its signed funding entries in DKK
million are 469.832 for the task-related grant cut, 3,065.000
for lost grant and cofinancing, −181.681 for transition
compensation, −2,567.000 for the abolished cofinancing pool,
47.000 for an expired adjustment, −454.222 for the adjusted
health expense and 21.327 for the other expense adjustment.
The sum is a projected funding deterioration of 400.256
before equalization. The published 2028 deterioration is
406.319. The same annex records 52.973 more unused room under
the service ceiling in 2027. The funding and ceiling effects
move in opposite directions. Neither establishes settlement,
an actual shortfall after equalization or new receiving capacity.
[Budget annex, tables 17–18](../../2026-11-first-year-obligations-and-receiving/original-sources/copenhagen-budget-annex.pdf).

| First motion | Decision on the inspected evidence |
|---|---|
| Continue the ordinary mixed arrangement and repair failures | Keep the existing city teams, the regional and municipal operating assignments, funded protection and individual remedies. A missed shift or delivery stays visible. Select this reference path. |
| Add standalone preparation or continuity protection | Bring forward a priced care backfill or drinking-water receiving offer only if its own funds, operator and affected sites qualify. Seek a defined funded approval for each. |
| Accelerate both transitions with a new service cohort | Keep open for elected comparison. The published amounts do not establish separate accepted staff, simultaneous delivery or available finance. |

At one care facility and one assisted home route, test safe water,
sanitation, a rested driver, a separately available care worker and
actual receiving under both the January and May changeovers.
Use the previously constructed hour-72 target and hour-90 delay
as stress timings. These timings give no evidence of an outage
or real receipt. Keep the next call and replenishment on their
own dates. If the same nurse appears in the sending and receiving
rosters in the same window, count the shift once and expose the
remaining duty. If a scope-specific balance cannot pay drinking
water, keep the lawful uses separate and seek a funded response.
