# LCY09–LCY12. Public choices, remedies and transition

[Product repair and material responsibility](../26-product-repair-and-material-responsibility.md)

**LCY09 — Buy the useful service over time.** For material public
purchases of covered goods compare repair, refurbishment and
replacement at common tested safety, useful time and expected
service. Price parts, worker time, maintenance, energy, support
and handback, including a funded exit for essential use. Accept
safe equivalents and life extension where they meet the actual
requirement. A low sticker price or nominal recycled share cannot
decide a procurement. Where no safe repair is available by an
earlier essential deadline, use a paid qualified substitute under
the applicable service authority. Procurement powers stay under
SC and lawful budget control.

**LCY10 — Separate the payer, inspector and remedy.** The existing
waste steward owns actual collection and continuity, and the
consumer and technical authorities decide their respective
matters under applicable C08 law. The Assembly enacts scope,
source, assurance, release and independent review. Elected
local bodies choose operating arrangements within that scope.
A buyer, repairer, worker or affected resident can reach the
relevant independent court or sector remedy on their actual
standing. Check correction against the original due time and
actual receiving. Inspectors preserve qualification, independent
source and safe reporting. Neither a producer scheme, purchasing
office nor AI closes its own case.

**LCY11 — Treat older products and new classes prospectively.**
Keep stronger warranties, defect claims, existing repair rights,
lawful permits, worker and creditor obligations and already
received deposits. For legacy goods with no accountable solvent
payer, price a distinct paid public safe-receiving route. Preserve
actual recovery where law allows, without a retroactive placement
charge or an assumed new priority. Use FT/OP/T for current
operators, paid staff, custody, funds and accepted successors.
Set a dated start only under actual competent law and prior
procurement and budget authority. Compare genuine alternative
treatment and support before extending the classes.

**LCY12 — Revise on actual access, harm and capacity.** Publish
repair acceptance and turnaround, unresolved material, real
consumer cost, entry barriers and unsafe work with safe aggregates.
Compare whole alternatives at the same delivered service. Reopen
if independent repair is blocked, security or safety worsens,
assurance fails, burdens shift abroad or a better complete rival
appears. A higher collection rate alone establishes no useful
recovery or better life. Actual rights, adequate funds and
qualified receivers remain separate from the research choice.
