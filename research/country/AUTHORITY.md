# Who decides across the country

*Design545 · Working allocation of competence for the [national settlement](../NATIONAL-SETTLEMENT.md).*

Each office acts within powers conferred by law. People and voluntary groups
choose their own lawful arrangements. Elected local authorities control
local methods and improvements within enforceable common rights. The
Assembly funds what it assigns and legislates for matters that need a common
decision. Courts enforce the boundary under their jurisdiction. Sovereign
decisions require the competent public institution. AI may help people
understand or prepare a decision within a scoped mandate.

| Chapter | Common decision and accountable institution | Decision that remains with people or the competent narrower body |
|---|---|---|
| [C01 Principles](C01-first-principles.md) | The charter protects equal standing and lawful remedy. Electors and representatives choose among lawful rival programmes. | People choose their purposes and hold different views of a better life. A public comparison preserves those differences. |
| [C02 Rights and membership](C02-membership-and-rights.md) | Common law protects status, equal rights and access. Competent civil and status offices determine covered public claims with independent review. | People choose lawful relationships and associations. A request for one public protection supplies only the authority and disclosure needed for that act. |
| [C03 Democratic government](C03-democratic-government.md) | Electors choose representatives. The Assembly makes national law, votes supply and can replace the executive. Independent election officials and courts perform separately granted functions. | Local electors choose local governments. Voters, opposition and civic groups form their own views, campaigns and organizations. |
| [C04 Administration](C04-public-administration.md) | The Assembly sets and finances justified common duties. A named agency remedies failure only under a defined authority and funded backstop. | Councils choose lawful delivery, providers and local improvements. Temporary national relief returns control on its lawful terms. |
| [C05 Work and production](C05-economy-and-work.md) | General law protects free bargaining, fair dealing, workers, creditors, users and competition. Competent institutions enforce exact public-service obligations. | Firms, workers and members organize through lawful charters and bargaining. Nationally prescribed ordinary board proportions require a separate specific case. |
| [C06 Public finance](C06-public-finance.md) | The Assembly authorizes national taxes, commitments, debt and spending. Separate payers, oversight and elections keep their operative sources. | Councils vote within lawful local sources and powers. People control their lawful assets and income, including the funds they receive as an individual cash entitlement. |
| [C07 Social floor](C07-social-floor.md) | National law finances defined essential protection and enforceable income and earned claims. Named owners answer for failed delivery. | Residents choose among lawful options with usable consent and refusal. Local providers and qualified professionals control delivery and individual professional judgments within their scope. |
| [C08 Justice](C08-everyday-law-and-justice.md) | Law defines offenses, evidence, liability, penalties and remedies. Independent courts decide contested cases. Police, prosecution, defense and corrections have distinct authority. | People make lawful agreements, choose advice or mediation and retain direct rights of defense and court access. |
| [C09 Land and ecology](C09-land-and-ecology.md) | National or jointly authorized authorities address cumulative or cross-boundary harm and shared infrastructure needs with defined, reviewable limits. | Councils choose local land and service methods within those limits and protected territorial rights. Owners choose lawful uses and owe any actual duties their use creates. |
| [C10 Safety and external relations](C10-safety-and-international-relations.md) | Competent national institutions maintain lawful external commitments and address threats beyond local reach, under civilian and elected control. Emergency acts have defined authority, expiry and review. | Local actors carry ordinary safety and response within their powers. Individual protections apply to emergency action and to each affected case. |
| [C11 Knowledge, culture and AI](C11-knowledge-culture-and-ai.md) | Public bodies protect independent evidence, open public access and narrowly authorized AI assistance. The lawful duty owner retains responsibility and a usable alternate. | People, researchers and cultural groups choose their own tools and inquiry. People can use a staffed route for protected acts and contest consequential errors. |
| [C12 Collaboration](C12-future-collaboration.md) | Competent institutions define the limited requirements for lawful receipt, authority, consent, recorded effect and correction. | Participants choose media, language or mediator and may revoke an individual agent's mandate on its terms. |
| [C13 Lived experience](C13-lived-experience.md) | Service owners and independent reviewers verify receipt and fix actionable failures. Protected testimony and complaints have their own routes. | Residents decide what help to accept and what voluntary testimony to share. A person's circumstances or service case does not give a coordinator authority over unrelated decisions. |
| [C14 Establishment and improvement](C14-establishment-and-improvement.md) | Competent publics and elected bodies adopt, finance, review and revise the exact instruments. Existing rights, ordinary obligations and remedies carry on their terms. | Local variation and voluntary experiments can proceed within valid authority and funded protection. Human and AI contributors can propose changes under the common review method. |

For a disputed boundary, ask what exact harm or public duty requires the
proposed authority, who already has lawful power to respond, and whether a
smaller route can deliver the same protection. A binding national outcome
normally allows equivalent lawful local methods. An elected residual decision covers only the unresolved effect. A common method, permanent
transfer or new material restriction needs its own authority, demonstrable
case and funded execution. Use the existing local competence, compulsory
burden and sector rules, including independent relief and protected consent.
