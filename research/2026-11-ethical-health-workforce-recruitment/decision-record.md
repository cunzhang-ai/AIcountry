# Design421. Account for both health systems when funding recruitment

## Decision and baseline

Design420 separates recruitment, training, qualification, willing
deployment and retained work. WF87 requires examining origin effects
without restricting workers. Membership law protects admission and
sponsor exit through its own route. Portable competence law protects
workers and patients through independent assessment. The open
institutional choice is how the state can actively finance recruitment
where origin services may already be stretched. This decision was
prepared with AI assistance and needs competent law and appropriations.

## Alternatives and working selection

Immediate active recruitment can fill a real destination vacancy
quickly but can move the shortage to a community with fewer
alternatives. A blanket origin-based restriction on individual
applications would remove worker choice and would mistake national
averages for individual and local facts. Retention, better work
conditions, supported return and domestic training can be the best
ordinary response. They take paid effort and may take longer than
the next service deadline.

Select funded domestic cover where it works and allow a targeted
public programme in an exposed service only with separately authorized,
verified origin safeguards and destination receiving. Keep personal
mobility, work choice and independent applications under their own
law. A joint programme can create new capability. Its paperwork and
payments can also conceal delayed replacement or harm shifted to
another location. Require dated delivery, transparent scope,
independent review and lawful suspension of new activity. Preserve
worker pay, status and remedy when a public programme fails.

The constructed example selects staged recruitment only because a
finite domestic bridge and genuinely additional source cover are
both available. A sustainable domestic package or a different lawful
source could win on another comparison. Source support cannot be
priced as the purchase of a person's consent, labor or right to move.

## Evidence, public authority and migration

The [primary comparators](sources.md) establish international
voluntary recruitment guidance, recent provisions on care and
co-investment, and recruitment-cost protections. They do not establish
that a particular region has capacity to spare. The example stipulates
service, costs and willing acceptance. No source authority, migrant
worker, patient group or independent reviewer participated.

The Assembly authorizes the national scope and gross exposure.
The competent foreign authority decides its own commitments through
applicable treaty and budget law. Local receiving owners choose the
lawful method within their authority and fund oversight, onboarding
and paid work. Independent review can inspect original evidence and
publish findings outside the buyer. Employment, immigration and
licensing institutions retain separate decisions. Lawful alternate
routes protect fee recovery, source support, pay and case review.
Apply prospectively to new public campaigns, renewals and contracts.
Carry accepted jobs, accrued wages, existing admissions and protected
source commitments through the change.

## Scoped result and bounded next test

The documentary evidence and constructed case qualify a research
choice. No working agreement, payroll, assessed qualifications,
lawful receiving or better outcomes have been established.

For one proposed occupation and origin region, the responsible
sector authorities and independent reviewers would compare the
first two service windows against the same domestic alternative.
Complete when they can trace the verified origin task, paid bridge,
worker choice and costs, accepted qualified destination work and
actual care or failure on each side. Publish safe unresolved
effects and keep individual remedies usable. There is no committed
exercise budget. Reopen on late replacement, hidden fees, a broken
complaint route, worsened origin access or a stronger domestic option.
