# T143–T147. Limit the national programme to what needs public power

*Design543 · Proposed national settlement and opening law under [staged capacity and protection](../06-staged-capacity-and-protection.md).*

**T143 — Begin with free action and accountable government.** People may
choose how to live, communicate, work, associate, organize a lawful enterprise
and cooperate by mutual agreement. General law protects others' rights,
consent and fair dealing. A person requires public permission only where
applicable law gives a competent authority an exact power for a justified
purpose. Access to a public service does not confer authority over unrelated
private choices. A provider cannot demand assent to AI advice, unrelated
record sharing or a preferred living arrangement as the price of a due
service. Public office can act only within granted powers, subject
to independent remedies and effective democratic replacement.

**T144 — Put each decision at the lowest level that can carry it.** Let
individuals and voluntary groups settle matters they can lawfully control.
Let elected local bodies choose how to meet a funded common floor and
respond to local needs. Use voluntary and elected interlocal cooperation
where it can reach those affected and carry the cost. National law secures
equal political and legal standing, enforceable basic protection and fair
fiscal capacity, and handles material common or cross-boundary problems
that narrower arrangements cannot deal with in useful time. National
responsibility for a service floor may coexist with local, private,
cooperative or public delivery. Preserve independent rights, remedies and
the real choice of affected people at every level.

**T145 — Justify the burden of each new material power.** In the applicable
ordinary legislative or delegated process, the sponsor identifies the right,
protected duty, substantial harm, bottleneck or common resource at issue.
Show who cannot act alone, why ordinary law, repair, voluntary cooperation
or a narrower level falls short, and which exact intervention can work.
Show affected groups, costs, least burdensome feasible scope, safeguards,
review and a lawful route to narrow or end the power. Assess concentrated
private power as well as state power. A regulator, analyst or model gains
no additional approval role. This rule uses the existing justification
process under [CS16–CS18](../../C01/03/07-compulsory-burdens-and-country-disposition.md).
Examine material inherited high-risk powers through T138–T142 and carry
ordinary compatible law through the transition. Preserve vested claims and
lawful rights when removing a rule.

**T146 — Choose three opening instruments.** The competent prior public
process establishes the rights, contest, opposition, transfer and independent
remedy that make the government replaceable. A competent Assembly votes
continuity supply for existing lawful duties, actual fiscal sources and
critical upkeep. It votes separately on a short list of demonstrated
failures in basic services or democratic operation, with named responsible
people, money, original deadlines and a funded second call. Apply ordinary
repair first when competent law already permits it. Use the narrowest
new power where repair cannot work. Keep extraordinary support, sector
formulas, labour schedules, new tax or currency instruments and new AI
deployments subject to distinct later decisions unless a specific opening
dependency requires a lawful, funded measure now. Existing legal duties
and rights continue by their own authority. An unfunded due duty remains a
claim and an obligation for the competent owner, with its remedy.

**T147 — Use AI for bounded help and keep the opening decision finite.**
Allow authorized, proportionate assistance with analysis, drafting and
checking under existing safeguards. The initial optional public-works
evaluation remains available for separate consideration against competent
staff and ordinary tools. Opening supply and constitutional operation do
not depend on its approval, results or expected savings. Keep ordinary
human receiving and the accountable decision-maker. Close national design
review on the exact core choice and opening instruments. Carry detailed
mechanisms through their applicable later decision routes. Reopen a core
choice where it fails a protected duty, depends on unqualified resources
or loses to an executable less burdensome rival on the same duties and
dates.
