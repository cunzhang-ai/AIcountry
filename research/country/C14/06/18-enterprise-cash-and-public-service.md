# T79–T84. Pay for essential service without absorbing every enterprise loss

13 November 2026 · Integrated design 439 · Candidate first-budget rules

**T79 — Keep the enterprise and government accounts distinct.**
Name the asset owner, contract sponsor, operator, creditors and
service recipients. Distinguish fare or commercial revenue, public
service compensation, dividends, equity, loans, guarantees and
protected customer balances. Match transfers on both books.
Eliminate an internal flow once within the applicable consolidated
scope, without erasing either payer's due-date cash requirement.

**T80 — Qualify the money and obligations by date.** Identify owned
cash, unencumbered investments and genuinely drawable facilities
separately. Record commitment status, draw conditions, settlement,
expiry, purpose, repayment and the counterparty. Trace gross debt,
interest, collateral and accepted capital commitments. Keep the
first 90 days and second-year tail. A revenue entry, accounting
profit, guarantee or advertised credit limit supplies no paid service.

**T81 — Make the sponsor pay for the exact mandate.** Under
[OE41–OE46](../../C05/13/09-funded-public-enterprise-mandates.md),
identify the required route or service, payment calendar, lawful
compensation and independently verified receiving. Protect wages,
maintained assets and required accessibility. Disputed invoices
retain their remedies and do not authorize unilateral service cuts
or access to unrelated company funds. Fund the next due period
before relying on an optional new mandate.

**T82 — Compare continuity and corporate support separately.**
Compare ordinary funded contracting, a timely contract payment or
lawful bridge, an independently funded alternate operator, and
corporate recapitalization where credible. Identify how much
actually protects the service and who bears enterprise, lender
and fiscal losses. Test a simultaneous fare loss, late public
payment, unavailable credit and a valid guarantee call. Public
ownership does not guarantee every liability. Record each legally
binding call separately under
[DB27–DB32](../../C06/60/10-public-guarantees-and-common-fiscal-risk.md).

**T83 — Keep democratic choice and independent execution.**
Existing elected bodies authorize appropriations and guarantees.
The enterprise board retains its lawful decisions and regulator
and courts keep independent jurisdiction. Any provisional funding
has a specified purpose, date, ceiling and repayment or closeout
rule. Keep dividends and optional expansion conditional on their
own authority and protected obligations. AI may reconcile funding
and flag correlated exposure. It cannot declare credit drawable,
trigger public backing or decide whether a company survives.

**T84 — Carry the public service through failure and succession.**
Protect accrued obligations, staff rights, access and public
records with an actual paid receiver. Preserve original due
dates and creditor claims under applicable law. Verify receipt
and independently test the alternate's next service window.
Reopen on an unpriced guarantee, missing route capacity, a
material source revision or a better funded continuity option.
