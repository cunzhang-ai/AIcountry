# One service and a rejected budget

Constructed comparison. The Assembly has 100 statutory seats. The
ordinary budget and continuation each require at least 51 actual yes
votes, with lawful presence, exact text, notice and procedures.
On lapse day 60 the full annual proposal receives 44 yes votes.
Its disputed optional building project remains independently separable.

A protected water service needs four units for a short maintenance
contract. The supplier can accept through day 85 and delivers on day
92. The expired authority will not cover work after day 90. The
separate settlement authority for work already earned during the
bridge cannot authorize this contract. A standing care payment of six
units is due separately and has its own settled restricted source.
Five unrestricted units remain legally available for the short
period. Assume actual qualified maintenance and independent execution
can be secured within the period if the Assembly authorizes it.

| Exact choice | Binding result |
| --- | --- |
| Keep the annual package as the only vote | Its 44 votes fail. The care payment retains its standing authority. The water contract still needs authority. |
| Pass a separate motion limited to the four-unit water contract | A hypothetical 55 yes votes authorize the exact source and scope. Treasury and the provider still have to complete the lawful contract and the actual work. One unrestricted unit remains. |
| Add the unrelated three-unit building project to that motion | The two commitments would need seven unrestricted units. The standing care balance is unavailable for them. Keep the project for an independently funded decision. |

In the separate-vote branch the contract is authorized on day 60
and can be accepted by day 85. The caretaker and the next annual
budget remain on their own procedures. If the required sitting fails
or the supplier can no longer accept before its useful time, record
the missing authority or capacity and pursue the available lawful
route. A late supplier agreement cannot be counted as timely without a
newly verified receiving path. On the remaining one unrestricted unit, a later budget that
still proposes the three-unit project must identify two additional
usable units or change the proposal. A valid water obligation keeps
its original payment and remedy after any cutover.
