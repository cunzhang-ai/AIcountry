# Primary records for insurance exit and safe transition

- [United States Treasury Federal Insurance Office, January 2025 market report](https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/311/Analyses_of_US_Homeowners_Insurance_Markets_2018-2022_Climate-Related_Risks_and_Other_Factors_0.pdf)
  supplies the 2018–2022 averages in Figure 14, printed page 26.
  Its scope discussion describes excluded markets, policy forms and
  flooding. The [observation file](observations.json) transcribes only
  the premium and nonrenewal cells used in the worked case.
- [United States Government Accountability Office, GAO-23-105977](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-105977)
  examines the public flood insurance programme's change toward
  risk-related premiums and distinguishes transparent budgeted
  affordability assistance from general rate discounts. Its proposed
  reforms are policy comparators, not observed effects of adoption.
- [Flood Re, 2024 transition report](https://www.floodre.co.uk/ara-2024/transition/)
  describes its planned 2039 exit and the dependence of a future
  market on reduced risk, reduced damage and effective insurance.
  A planned date does not establish an affordable successor.

The official observations concern different markets and legal
structures. No rates, transition periods or hypothetical claim
figures were transferred into the proposed country from these sources.
