# Design430. Complete the proposed small-claims route

[SCL01–SCL06](../../country/C08/13/09-accessible-small-civil-claims.md)
defines affordable filing, a fair answer, reasoned adjudication,
predictable transfer and verified receipt within the ordinary court.

[Worked claim](../../settlement/accessible-small-civil-claims/01-a-small-award-and-actual-payment.md)
tests failed notice and two payments. The local threshold, source,
staffing, participation and real performance remain open.
