# NS01–NS04: choose the economy's direction without inventing its success

**NS01 — Keep the question about lives and power.** Retain the existing-community
retrofit context, real prior rights and comparison with paid greenfield/retain
alternatives. No actual territory, consent or sovereignty is selected. A viable
economy must sustain useful service, time, security, relationships and public
control within ecological/external limits. Output, jobs, exports or budget balance
alone can improve while those lives worsen. New law must not buy financial success
with cheap migrant labor, suppressed opposition, unpaid care or exported harm.

**NS02 — Compare substantially different national paths.** These are mechanisms
and conditional diagnoses, not claims that existing countries fit pure types.

| Path | Potential support for the same guarantees | Material failure / present disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Resource/land/financial-rent enclave | Genuine paid public title and durable external receipts can fund common provision | No such endowment is established; depletion, outside loss, ownership concentration and exclusion can undermine rights. Reject as assumed founding source; retain actual lawful returns if evidenced. |
| Export-first competitive hub | Paid outside demand can support imports, learning and useful production | Demand can fail; a jobs/exports target can subordinate care, wages, ecology and democracy. Reject maximization as the national objective; retain qualified voluntary tradables. |
| Service-first diversified retrofit | Repair, essential services, paid skills and plural production combine domestic capabilities with diverse accepted trade | Needs actual tax/distribution, investment, skills and foreign counterparties; no automatic productivity gain. Select as primary conditional strategy. |
| Broad self-sufficiency/public command | More domestic control of genuinely critical inputs may reduce specific exposures | Autarky can worsen quality, ecology, labor freedom and investment burden. Reject all-sector self-sufficiency or assigned private careers; retain justified paid resilience projects. |
| Negotiated shared productive/fiscal provision | A larger pool can share rare skills, infrastructure, supply and shocks | No partner mandate, common source, representation or exit settlement is established. Retain as a structural rival, not an assumed foreign rescue. |

**NS03 — Preserve conditions that could displace the selection.** A verified
resource base, strong existing public capability, scarce small-country skills,
accepted regional arrangements or credible outside demand can favor another
mix. Do not absorb every critique into a larger coordination office. Service-first
can mean inefficient monopoly, exclusion or dependence if users/workers cannot
challenge and replace controllers. Competing public/cooperative/private routes,
MP competition, OE/WL power and enforceable service rights remain substantive.

**NS04 — Do not mix monetary jurisdictions.** Own-unit managed-float MC/RF remains
the current candidate. Shared/foreign-currency membership changes who can issue,
settle, borrow and bear losses; it needs actual treaty authority, representation,
source, transfer/exit and continuing-claim terms under C10/C14. The Assembly
cannot issue another polity's money by naming it a national resource. Conversely,
an own-unit state is not financially identical to a household. Keep a distinct
finite sovereign fiscal-issue rival requiring its own primary mandate; neither
this strategy nor a macro identity creates that mandate or actual foreign acceptance.
