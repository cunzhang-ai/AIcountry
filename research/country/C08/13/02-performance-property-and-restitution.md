# Performance, protected property and return of retained benefit

*B05–B08; part of [C08 civil core](../13-civil-obligations-and-executed-remedies.md).*

**B05 — Performance and proportionate breach remedy.** Perform lawful
agreed obligations in good faith. A material breach permits proportionate
repair, replacement, price reduction, termination, restitution or compensation
for proved foreseeable loss. Credit actual delivery and prevent duplicate
recovery for the same item. Specific performance cannot compel personal labor
or an unsafe/unlawful act. Mitigation means reasonable feasible steps, not
unpaid family care, dangerous travel, new debt or abandoning protected rights.
Actual loss of income, reasonable substitute costs and delay can be claimed
on proof; punishment needs separate prior law. An extraordinary outside-control
event can excuse genuinely impossible performance where law/contract fairly
allocates that risk. It does not excuse avoidable delay, notice, protection,
refund of unjustly retained benefit or a separately owed essential-service
continuity duty. Less profit or a vendor's AI mistake is not automatically
impossibility. Mere inability to pay is not fraud or authority to detain.

**B06 — Defective assent and unfair exploitation.** Fraud, material factual
mistake, unlawful duress or knowing coercive exploitation of actual dependence
can justify avoidance or adjustment after fair hearing. Identify the act,
materiality, choice constraint and causal effect. Poverty/diagnosis alone
neither invalidates a person's agreement nor proves fair choice. Protect
innocent third parties and accrued nonwaivable rights; distinguish divisible
unfair terms from a whole agreement that cannot fairly stand. A party cannot
keep an informed favorable result while opportunistically denying the same
lawful authority only after loss. Actual unfairness, agent scope, error and
restitution require distinct findings. A court may repair within enacted law,
not impose its own general wage, rent or tax policy.

**B07 — Property, possession and records.** Distinguish possession, title,
leases, security, easements and collective/downstream claims. Transfers need
actual lawful authority and the relevant prospective form/registration rules.
A record change, model label or missing credential cannot extinguish an
unheard person's rights. Registry correction gives notice, accessible proof,
interim preservation, reasons and independent review to affected parties.
A good-faith buyer and original holder both need a lawful loss-allocation
route; do not automatically sacrifice the less digitally visible person.
Court-ordered restoration and separate compensation against a responsible
wrongdoer/state custodian require actual grounds and a payer. [B38](../16/06-civil-periods-and-registry-loss.md) now assigns prospective
official-certification loss to a public payer; no actual reserve/adequacy is established. No blanket perfect-title
promise or appropriation arises from this clause.

Public acquisition needs competent elected authority, genuine lawful purpose,
proportionate scope, applicable compensation and an executable independent
appeal; do not disguise it as a software correction. Existing national/local,
Indigenous/collective, tenant and ecological duties retain their distinct law.
Provisional essential housing/food access follows need without finally deciding
a contested title. No eviction, asset sale or boundary change can be justified
by an unsettled database match alone.

**B08 — Unjust enrichment and mistaken transfer.** Return an actual benefit
received without a lawful basis or after that basis fails. Identify recipient,
amount, continuing entitlement and legitimate good-faith change of position;
no fictional receipt of inaccessible money or unused proposed service is
counted. Restitution is not punishment or automatic liability for every
intermediary. A transfer to the wrong account requires source/payment custody,
correction and a dispute route; a person should not have to sue an unknown
recipient before challenging their responsible provider. Fraud and coercion
retain separate criminal/civil tests. Recovery preserves subsistence,
third-party rights and the lawful claim; essential interim support must not
vanish because the liability hearing is unfinished.

**Design145 B07 completion.** [LA01–LA20](../../C09/13-public-acquisition-and-secure-relocation.md)
now supplies the positive public-land/right acquisition candidate: exact elected
mandate, alternatives, independent compensation, separate holders/debt, actual
safe receiving/payment and ordinary-court possession. B07 still supplies common
title/proof/restoration; LA grants no current power, emergency seizure, personal
labor, automatic all-property nationalization or new court.

**Design148 B07 ordinary completion.** [PT01–PT24](../26-property-transfers-and-secured-credit.md)
now supplies authorized identified-goods/claims and recorded land transfer,
shared-property limits, functional security, continuous notice/possession rank,
ordinary goods buyer protection and court-controlled realization. PT13 frees
qualifying buyers only from seller-created security, not the true holder's
ownership. B38 restoration and separate official-certification payer remain;
no registry/token cures a void transfer, grants an old creditor new rank or
supplies safe receiving/actual compensation cash.

[PFR01–PFR05](../../C06/67-payment-scam-recovery-and-protected-funds.md)
separates a temporary restraint and regulated reimbursement from B08
restitution. Keep title, good-faith protection and actual recovery
under their own lawful decisions. Credit the same loss once.
