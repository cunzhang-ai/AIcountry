# Time, care and the ability to live and participate

3 October 2026 · Supporting choice for C05–C07/C13–C14 · English research proposal, not enacted law

## Decision and baseline

The [country design](../COUNTRY-DESIGN.md), integrated design 9, says automation
should expand discretionary time and that services must be usable alongside
paid work and unpaid care. It does not yet assign an enforceable working-time,
rest, scheduling and care-leave settlement. A person may receive a nominal
clinic slot, care offer and political franchise while unpredictable shifts and
unpaid responsibility consume the hours needed to use them. That is a design
gap, not a measured failure in a real AIcountry.

**Select a layered reference:** an enacted minimum of rest, bounded hours,
advance schedule information, protected leave and nonretaliation; collective
bargaining and sector agreements may improve and adapt the floor; portable,
partly pooled care-leave income and actual care services protect people whose
employer or household cannot supply time. Parameters remain for legitimate
public choice and actual capacity calibration. This choice protects free time
as a condition for health, relationships and political agency, without assuming
that less paid work automatically produces enough nurses or a viable small firm.

The pinned September 16 [labor manuscript](../2026-09-readiness/source-documents.json)
already proposes specific ordinary/overtime hours, weekly rest and annual leave
in `work.labor-protection` Article 2. Those numbers are explicitly provisional
research choices, not observed national optima or enacted rights. The present
analysis retains their *functions* for comparison and does not silently ratify
or translate the numeric schedule. The [health, education and care analysis](../2026-10-core-institutions/health-education-care.md)
requires safe staffing and noncompulsory family labor; the [production/work
analysis](../2026-10-material-and-resilience/production-work.md) protects worker
bargaining and paid learning. Neither by itself assigns the time floor.

## Different explanations and coherent packages

Some time poverty comes from a firm controlling unpredictable schedules; some
from too little income to refuse hours; some from missing care workers, long
travel or household dependence. A working-time rule can change the first two
without creating care capacity or transport. Conversely, better services may
free time but leave an employer's compulsory availability intact. These are
different mechanisms and should not be collapsed into an average leisure score.

| Package | Mechanism and attraction | Major failure or value conflict |
|---|---|---|
| Retain bargaining and sector discretion | Workers, firms and public providers negotiate hours, on-call duties and leave around their own needs, under existing safety and anti-retaliation law. | Where union entry is blocked or workers need income urgently, formal consent can be coerced. Self-employed and unpaid carers have no collective employer to bargain with; geographic care shortages persist. |
| Uniform maximum hours and employer-paid leave | One simple national rule protects employees and shifts costs to the organization setting work. | A single schedule may be unsafe or impossible for emergency services without funded replacement staff; employer-only leave costs can discourage hiring care-constrained workers or small-firm entry. A statutory number does not address unpredictable hours within the maximum. |
| **Selected layered floor plus pooled care support** | Assembly enacts a justiciable floor; worker organizations negotiate better or sector-specific patterns; portable insurance and general revenue finance defined leave income; care services and income support remain separate entitlements. | Needs tax/insurance administration, inspectors, replacements and transition cash. A nominal floor can be evaded through contractor labels or on-call work. Paid leave can displace work onto underpaid carers unless staffing is funded. |

This is a conditional research choice. A jurisdiction with strong universal
bargaining and equivalent coverage may prefer the simpler first package. A more
uniform law may better protect workers where bargaining capacity cannot be
built. These alternatives have different power and cost distributions and must
be judged by affected workers, carers, recipients and firms, not by an AI
productivity estimate.

## The proposed chain from right to lived time

1. **The Assembly sets the floor and funding.** Labor law defines ordinary and
   maximum hours, recurring rest, paid annual and medical leave, advance notice
   for material schedule changes, limits on on-call availability, fair
   compensation for required exceptions, and protection against retaliation.
   It defines a gender-inclusive, portable care-leave benefit for childbirth,
   child and dependent care, and serious family need without making relatives
   the default service provider. The benefit schedule identifies replacement
   income, duration, contributions, general-revenue support, job protection,
   qualifying evidence, appeal and funds by due date. Numbers must be chosen
   by a legitimate legislature after capacity and distribution review. Neither
   an employer policy nor an AI schedule silently changes a statutory right.
2. **Collective and sector choice operate above the floor.** Independent worker
   organizations and employers negotiate patterns, rest rotation, training
   time and additional leave. A sector may adapt shifts for continuous clinical,
   utility or safety coverage only under enacted safeguards: worker voice,
   documented necessity, bounded period, recovery time and review. Emergency
   exceptions cannot become the ordinary roster by renewal. Public and private
   employers face the same safety, nonretaliation and actual-hour records;
   professional judgment does not give managers a labor-law veto.
3. **Portable protection reaches precarious work.** Employment status follows
   actual control and dependence, as the predecessor labor candidate proposes,
   not a platform's contract label. Contributions from covered employers and
   workers, with law-defined public support for low incomes, finance portable
   leave across job changes. Self-employed people can receive a portable
   income benefit under a separately specified contribution/public-funding
   rule, though an absent employer cannot promise reinstatement. People
   outside paid work retain care services and the income floor; calling those
   services “leave” would obscure their separate claim. Care recipients retain
   their own autonomy and may refuse a plan that relies on unpaid relatives.
4. **Delivery and remedy are not a payroll checkbox.** The responsible labor
   authority inspects actual hours, scheduling changes, withheld leave and
   retaliation with protected records; independent tribunals can give timely
   interim relief, wages/benefit arrears, reinstatement or compensation where
   law warrants. Benefit agencies make reasoned, appealable payments. Workers
   need confidential access without employer permission, and employers receive
   fair process for genuine operational disputes. Service authorities report
   whether leave-takers and caregivers could actually obtain replacement
   care, transport, clinic hours and safe return to work.

The common C06 account must price coverage by period, not merely describe the
right. It includes employer staffing and predictable-shift costs, insurance
contributions and public payments, small-firm effects, qualified replacement
workers, care places, supervision, travel, training, taxes and imported inputs.
A nurse, teacher or care worker on leave cannot still be counted on the service
roster; repeated overtime is not a reserve workforce. Nor can an unpaid partner
be drafted to make the timetable balance. If a public service lacks safe
replacement, its authority discloses the shortfall and seeks an enacted, funded
solution while protecting the worker and recipient's existing claims. The
Assembly, not the employer, insurer or scheduler alone, decides any general
change to the floor.

An authored stress case connects this to democracy. A precarious worker with
night shifts cares for a parent and seeks to stand for local office. They may
have an accessible clinic and formal candidacy but no predictable time to attend
care, meetings or vote. The selected package gives a labor remedy for unlawful
hours or retaliation, portable leave income, actual care support and the C03
candidate/campaign route. None of those remedies creates a missing care worker,
buys political influence or guarantees election. If they compete with hospital
night coverage, the public account must show the replacement or the unmet duty,
not assign the same hours to both. This is a logical test, not an observed life.

## Evidence, objections, compatibility and status

The [ILO working-time resources](https://www.ilo.org/resource/other/ilo-helpdesk-tools-and-resources-business-working-time)
identify international standards on hours, rest, leave, night work and part-time
work. The [ILO balanced-working-time guide](https://www.ilo.org/publications/guide-developing-balanced-working-time-arrangements-epub)
addresses scheduling choices for workers and enterprises. [ILO Convention No.
156](https://normlex.ilo.org/dyn/nrmlx_en/f?p=NORMLEXPUB%3A12100%3A0%3A%3ANO%3A%3AP12100_ILO_CODE%3AC156)
sets a comparison principle for workers with family responsibilities; the
[ILO care-at-work report](https://researchrepository.ilo.org/esploro/outputs/report/Care-at-work-investing-in-care/995219571002676)
examines gaps affecting self-employed, informal, migrant and other workers.
These are primary institutional sources inspected on 3 October 2026. They
inform the questions and norms; they neither bind AIcountry without actual
legal circumstances nor prove this package's costs or outcomes. Their data
and sponsorship may overlap. No affected worker or independent expert reviewed
this AI-authored selection.

The main objection is that a floor plus pooled benefit can raise costs and
reduce flexible entry or essential night coverage. That is a reason to measure
replacement capacity, small-firm incidence and service quality, not to presume
workers can supply limitless emergency hours. Another objection is that care
leave, especially if unequally used, can entrench gendered unpaid work; benefit
design and public services must support voluntary shared care rather than
substitute for it. A government could use working-time or leave eligibility
to exclude migrants or contractors; C02 rights, actual-work classification and
independent remedies constrain that. A rigid cutoff could punish workers who
voluntarily prefer a different pattern; within safety and rights limits,
negotiated variation remains possible. Exact hours, pay rates and leave
duration require public value choices as well as evidence.

If selected, the main design should add (1) a C05 layered time/rest/scheduling
and paid-care-leave choice with real bargaining and precarious-worker coverage,
and (2) C06/C07/C13 compatibility with the workforce, service and household
time account. C14 and status should identify legal migration from the earlier
provisional numeric labor candidate, pensions/insurance, existing contracts
and service rosters. Existing accrued leave, pay and pension claims continue
through lawful change; no new rights begin merely because this note was written.

**Evidence qualification:** comparison of project texts and cited ILO materials,
without time-use or firm/service data for AIcountry. **Research selection:**
layered time floor and pooled care support recommended conditionally.
**Legal authorization:** none. **Operational readiness:** no population,
workforce, contribution base, insurer, inspector or funded replacement capacity
verified. **Observed outcomes:** none. A lower nominal workweek, AI efficiency
or more leave on paper is not proof of more free time or better care.

The next bounded real-world question is whether an affected adopting community
can map actual paid and unpaid hours, shifts, travel, care demand, leave use,
firm size, inspector capacity and replacement workers under this package and
the bargaining-only rival. The first deliverable is a joint time-and-workforce
account with worker, recipient, employer and household objections, plus a
funded sector transition path. The design should be reopened if a simpler
bargaining system provides equal practical control over time, if the selected
floor causes unfilled life-sustaining service shifts without a lawful remedy,
or if pooled leave worsens care inequality or household coercion. Missing
monitoring is not evidence of success.
