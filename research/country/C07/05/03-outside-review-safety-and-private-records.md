# HG11–HG15. Review outside the public provider and purchaser

*Part of [public clinical service](../05-health-service-and-patient-control.md).* 

**HG11 — Name the existing outside clinical-review head.** The state employs
a Chief Clinical Reviewer to head the existing independent clinical safety/
coverage-rights review function, outside NCS's command and purchasing budget.
Cabinet appoints through C04's open qualified shortlist, disclosed relevant
interests, protected career/case judgment and direct review. The Chief assigns
actual conflict-free clinical/rights reviewers with relevant competence, evidence
access and paid protected time under published allocation rules. NCS may not
appoint/reassign its final reviewers, suppress findings or withhold their source.
One state/payroll or different job labels proves no actual independence; expose
shared patrons/controllers and fund genuinely usable external-case capability.

**HG12 — Give the review an actual bounded remedy.** Under the coordinated
Act, a person/representative can directly obtain clinically qualified assessment
of indicated need/safety/urgency and reasoned coverage/access correction within
actual S/HN law, without NCS/insurer/AI permission or a fee at the clinical-review
door. The function may issue binding NCS access/payment/safe-receiving correction
orders within that law; B19/OJ ordinary courts and lawful executors retain actual
independent enforcement. It cannot enact general coverage/tax, compel a named
practitioner to perform an unsafe/nonconsensual act or create a missing bed/
medicine. Treatment/release/data/choice authority remains separately required.
An order for covered care is not consent, a shipment or completed treatment.

**HG13 — The reviewer and the service can both be challenged.** Direct ordinary
OJ merits/rights review remains without universal serial appeals, and urgent
interim help must be usable before the safe time. Complaints implicating the
Chief Reviewer/assigned review team cannot be finally decided by them: an
unimplicated receiver preserves first receipt and routes to competent OJ court,
which selects actual qualified conflict-free outside evidence help/adjudicates
review. State-employer discipline follows C04 fair process; allegation alone
removes no one. Separate Assembly-funded review/court/aid/source custody,
safe report publication and AA spending scrutiny need actual people/payment/
records/accepted execution. No provider charge, fine bounty or claimed NCS
saving finances independence by assumption. Acting Chief Reviewer limit180days
from original vacancy, no reset, earlier limits and ordinary case duties retained.

**HG14 — Keep licensing, safety inquiry and punitive judgment separate.** HG
review supplies targeted clinical-rights/quality correction, not a new market
approval, professional licence, search/custody/forced-examination or penal power.
TD09/10 regulators, TS09 independent serious-safety investigation and PO/OJ/V
retain their own actual lawful scopes. A review office that participated in a
failed decision cannot certify its own later investigation independent. The coordinated Act authorizes only targeted necessary case/quality record
inspection for NCS/publicly funded covered-route duties, with PD minimization,
protected purpose/source/privilege and direct court control. It grants no
universal health/family-record access or jurisdiction over all private health
activity; separate ordinary/TD law governs that scope. A public operator faces the same safety law, no self-
certification privilege. A safety finding neither convicts nor settles all harm.

**HG15 — Protect private meaning while preserving usable reasons.** PD/clinical
custodians retain purpose-limited source/retention/correction; share only actual
lawfully necessary case/payment/continuity facts with protected notice/controls.
No universal medical/family graph, public intimate-testimony archive or automatic
model-training/research release. Safe complainants and meaningful defense both
need independent lawful evidence handling. Children, disabled people and
unfamiliar communication get actual supported access; a parent has no blanket
clinical-record veto/disclosure right. AI/remote/future media may help under
HC/DP/TD. Network heads may use lawfully configured tools for translation,
draft records, medicine-list reconciliation and referral/capacity support with
competent verification, original meaning and measured net benefit. They never
provide consent, professional competence, new coverage or sovereign case authority. Pay retained human/non-AI interpretation and care/records fallback;
a model or host update cannot close a standing claim or erase prior dissent.
