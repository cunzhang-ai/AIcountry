# Independent remedies, transition and proof

*Part of [AQ clean air](../24-clean-air-and-cumulative-exposure.md).*

**AQ08 — Give affected people a direct route to correction.** A
resident, worker or group acting for affected people can report
an exposure, missing monitor, unlawful approval, unexecuted plan
or shifted harm without proving personal injury first. Use accessible
first receipt, reasons and an outside reviewer with protected
evidence. Ordinary courts can order an existing legal duty, lawful
interim protection or a reasoned compliant plan on a useful
timetable. The court does not select the transport policy or
create an appropriation. A penalty or damages claim retains
its own evidence, fault, causation and defense requirements.
Identify the accepting executor and lawful payer. Show actual
protection, correction and remaining claims separately.

**AQ09 — Keep evidence and enforcement independent in practice.**
Trace the environmental and public-health officers, monitor
operators, budget and remedy executors through appointment,
funding, source access, conflicts and succession. Protect an
outside reporting and inspection route when the largest emitter,
local body or ministry owns the instruments or controls the
records. Fund qualified monitoring, verification, public review,
transitional alternatives and remedy once across shared tasks.
AI can help compare source estimates and surface patterns for
qualified review. It cannot declare compliance, infer individual
fault, choose a displaced community or waive a legal floor.
Publish actual observations, changes in burden and any unprotected
population separately from forecasts.

**AQ10 — Protect existing claims through phased change.** Before
commencement and on transfer, map actual permits, operating
conditions, worker and property rights, monitoring records and
pending claims to competent paid successors under FT/OP/T.
Apply new conditions prospectively with legal notice, funded
alternatives and independent challenge. Preserve existing
safety, compensation and restoration duties during disputes or
insolvency. Verify correction over the applicable measurement
window and at materially affected receiving places before
closing the plan. Reopen after persistent exposure, a failed
substitute, a changed transport or climate pattern, evidence
loss or a better feasible means of protection. A plan's expiry
cannot close an unmet duty.
