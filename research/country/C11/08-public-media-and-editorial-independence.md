# C11. Public media with an independent editorial mandate

*Design146 · PM01–PM24 · [C11 knowledge/culture/AI](../C11-knowledge-culture-and-ai.md) · Candidate ordinary law, not adopted authority.*

Select **one noncommercial statutory Public Media Service within a plural private,
community and public media environment**. It supplies a free public-interest news/
access floor with accountable resources, independent journalism and public correction.
It neither licenses other speakers nor becomes the state's truth office. This
replaces C11's unspecified public-media appointment/funding promise with a positive
institutional choice; no actual institution or budget is created by research.

Six seats and the terms/thresholds are contestable hypotheses, not proof of independence.
Always-half worker seats satisfy the OE reference at its largest threshold without
changing board size as employment changes. The sector statute expressly replaces
OE20's usual ministerial owner-side appointment for this body; it does not create a
public/cooperative exception to worker rights or alter ordinary enterprises.

The selected public service is not a grant allocator for all journalism. Existing
lawful independent/private/community media, KR grants and MP/PD rights remain.
No-central-public-outlet plus direct support and distributed community/common
provision remain real [rival architectures](../../2026-11-public-media-and-editorial-independence/decision-record.md).
This choice accepts concentration, occupational capture, cost and deadlock risks;
public control means an enforceable purpose and lawful replacement of the method,
not an incumbent's power to commission flattering stories or censor criticism.

Actual public, source law, need/languages, paid staff/receiving, independent affected
review and measured better lives remain unknown. Earlier rights, workers, contracts,
archives and current software/constitutional/contribution boundaries continue.
