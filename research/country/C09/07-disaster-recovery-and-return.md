# C09. Disaster recovery, return and reconstruction

*Part of [C09 land and ecology](../C09-land-and-ecology.md); FR01–FR33 proposed recovery duties.*

Disaster recovery is more than the end of an incident. The selected route
joins immediate safe shelter and essential services to a lawful decision on
homes, land, work, public infrastructure and future hazard exposure. A flood,
fire, earthquake, epidemic or technological failure does not cancel a person's
vote, tenancy, property, cultural claim, court case, school place or basic
protection. Nor does it oblige the state to replace every privately owned
asset at its former value. **Emergency help, insured loss, compensation for a
legal wrong, and publicly chosen reconstruction are distinct claims.**

The recovery route keeps individual claims, shared facilities and host capacity on separate decisions and accepted timelines.

## Prepare the choice before a disaster

Local elected authorities maintain place-specific hazard and asset records;
national stewards publish shared hazards, cross-boundary systems and source
uncertainty. Planning law requires inspection, maintenance, warning, accessible
shelter and safer construction where feasible. Risk maps disclose their method,
missing observations and people whose homes, work, language or customary
rights are absent. A model's risk class alone cannot extinguish a title,
tenancy, insurance contract or service claim. Law specifies prospectively
when a hazardous use may be restricted, how owners and occupants receive
notice and challenge, and which existing rights and liabilities continue.
Inaction before known danger, and a late hazard reclassification used to avoid
a lawful duty, remain reviewable.

The [C06 recovery-finance rule](../C06/07-disaster-risk-finance.md) prearranges
limited cash sources. Local and national authorities identify usable sites,
workers, materials, transport, records, power and water as well as money.
Insurance pays only its valid contract, when collectible; a contingent loan
is debt; a reserve booked for several simultaneous hazards is not several
reserves. The [C06 common account](../C06/03-commitment-account.md) exposes
maintenance and reconstruction obligations before new projects compete for
them. Preparation must include a manual claims route if records or networks
fail and an independent way to challenge a wrong hazard or damage finding.

## Protect people while a permanent choice is made

The [C10 incident lead](../C10/01-public-safety-and-emergencies.md) coordinates
rescue under existing law. The C07 housing and service authorities give a
receipt, safe temporary place, income and care route, and an accepted handoff
without requiring a property title, insurer approval or working phone.
Tenants, informal occupants, people with disabilities, separated children and
people unable to return receive person-level protection. Temporary safety
exclusion is reasoned, limited and reviewable; it cannot become an unvoted
permanent clearance. Source records of tenure, mortgage, insurance, utility
accounts, benefit eligibility and damage are preserved or reconstructed from
plural evidence, with correction and privacy safeguards. A destroyed registry
is neither proof that a claimant owns everything nor grounds to erase a claim.

The elected local council leads a public recovery plan for its area. It must
hear displaced residents, people who stayed, renters, workers, rights-holders
and the locality receiving evacuees through usable channels, including when
some cannot attend a physical meeting. National rights, ecological floors,
finance and cross-boundary infrastructure remain Assembly matters; the cabinet
executes only an enacted mandate. The local council may not externalize flood,
pollution or debt costs to another place. If local government itself fails, a
preauthorized funded service receiver maintains rights and records while a
lawful elected successor is restored; an emergency board gains no permanent
land, tax or franchise power. Delay leaves temporary care and claims in force;
silence is not consent to relocation or redevelopment.

## Choose a durable place and account for every loss

For each affected place, the competent elected body compares **safe repair in
place, adaptation with changed use, and supported voluntary movement** as
whole packages. Show realistic hazard and ecological exposure, home and
livelihood access, school and care continuity, cultural and collective land
ties, receiving-place capacity, public works, private loss, travel, time,
maintenance, future obligations and who pays. A promise to “build back better”
is not authority to clear a poor neighborhood for private redevelopment.
Neither a current titleholder nor a risk model alone decides for tenants,
customary users or the receiving community. Protected collective rights and
any territorial-status process keep their separate rules.

Where remaining is unsafe, law can set a necessary, evidence-based and
reviewable land-use limit. State-directed relocation that displaces people
is a last resort after feasible risk reduction and less disruptive alternatives
are compared; a person's voluntary move need not wait for that finding.
It requires the applicable property and housing process, informed affected
participation, an actual safe destination, continuity of income, services and
political access, protection of family and cultural ties, and compensation
where law requires it. A person may challenge the safety finding, valuation,
site or unequal offer; urgent danger permits only the lawful temporary act.
The state must also be honest when it cannot promise safe return. A right to
contest permanent loss is not a right to compel others to operate a known
unsafe water or power system.

The claims account separates public emergency assistance, insurer obligations,
landlord and tenant claims, mortgages, public-asset loss, public wrongdoing,
environmental liability and rebuilding grants or loans. It prevents duplicate
payment for the same proved loss with notice and appeal, without using a
fraud allegation to suspend basic protection. Courts decide legal liability
and compensation under applicable law; the Assembly chooses the prospective
public aid and infrastructure package. Local officers cannot give connected
owners priority over excluded renters or covertly transfer damaged public
land. Procurement, land acquisition and contractor performance remain
publicly auditable, with independent inspection and a funded receiver for
failed work.

Report actual safe occupancy, restored school, care, utilities, livelihoods,
legal claims resolved and people still displaced by place and group. Funds
announced, homes started and insurance claims filed are separate from receipt
and durable recovery. Recovery powers, exceptional contracts and data access
expire or return to ordinary law; original evidence and corrections survive.
The [C13 exercise](../C13/10-flood-recovery-and-displacement.md) tests a local
fiscal collapse and conflicting return claims. No real place, public, hazard,
contract, legal authority or recovery outcome has validated this route.

[ADP01–ADP08](08/01-climate-adaptation-and-funded-options.md) sets
earlier preparation and a funded route through long-run change.
Actual shelter, safe return and voluntary relocation retain their
separate authorities and duties.
