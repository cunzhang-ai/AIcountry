# IC08–IC12: useful public investment without an unaccountable national owner

*Part of [C05/09](../09-income-capital-and-democratic-investment.md); design88 candidate.*

**IC08 — Elected purposes, separate cash and risk limits.** The competent
executive proposes windows through existing sector/local authorities, with
retain/repair, purchase and new-build rivals. Assembly law sets the national
framework; elected councils decide local windows within C03's assigned scope.
National funding conditions cannot transfer unrelated local appointment or
budget powers. The competent elected body authorizes a maximum
five-year authority for new commitments, purpose, eligible instruments, annual
gross cash, total multiyear commitments, maximum outstanding loans/equity/
guarantees, actual source and funded closure. Count a whole committed project,
not just its first tranche. Guarantees need a nominal exposure ceiling and
loss provision; a low expected loss never licenses unlimited nominal risk.

No complete funded annex means no new discretionary awards. Already due
services, wages, contracts, pensions and remedies continue under their own
law; a blank investment annex is no indefinite suspension. NP protection
and maintenance precede optional expansion. Calling a project essential cannot
uncap it. This charter creates neither a sovereign fund nor general borrowing
power. The competent elected body can reject or replace its production method.

**IC09 — Trace appointments and prevent recipient capture.** The responsible
authority's existing civil-service recruitment office applies C04 open qualified
recruitment, conflict, pay and reviewable-removal rules. Its accountable national
or local executive appoints the responsible director through that process
with published reasons and conflicts. No recipient, party adviser or AI vendor
chooses the evaluators. Existing lawful terms apply; serial acting appointments
cannot bypass them. No award-volume bonus, recipient fee or carried interest.
The minister directs published prospective policy within law, not secret
named recipients, jobs, rescues or credit. A named exceptional public project
requires explicit authorization by the competent elected body and ordinary
safeguards; national commitments still require the Assembly's national authority.

Before calls, publish eligibility, evaluation, instrument terms and correction
routes with usable time, assistance and offline/new-concept entry. For each
consequential award, existing qualified sector, finance and delivery evaluators
give attributed reasons; at least one is outside the buying team and none has
a recipient conflict. The responsible recruitment/assignment office assigns
substitutes under published prior conflict/rotation rules consistent with C04. The director
decides and explains material departures. No AI-only ranking, exclusion or
award; absence of competent evaluators prevents a new award, not existing care.
Cost and staff for this work are an explicit window charge, never free overhead.

**IC10 — Use an instrument that matches the purpose.** Procurement buys
defined outputs; public construction creates a public asset; a grant purchases
a specified public benefit/access or knowledge obligation; a loan needs a
credible repayment basis; equity purchases paid title, voting, return and
exit terms. Do not disguise a grant as a recoverable loan, or require care,
prevention and common knowledge to pass a portfolio-wide profit hurdle.
Public-enterprise commercial and compensated noncommercial accounts remain
separate, with minority and service-user protections.

Title and financial returns belong to the actual lawful owner, not the
director or a presumed national mutual. Gross repayments, interest, dividends
and disposal proceeds enter the public account; reinvestment needs applicable
appropriation. No off-budget evergreen spending or dividend promised twice.
Construction invoices, equity subscriptions and acquisition payments are
different claims; only actual capital formation counts as new production.

**IC11 — Qualified acceptance, correction and public scrutiny.** Tranches
require named lawful work and contract conditions; delivery verification comes
from a qualified person outside the award decision. Treasury checks source,
payee and lawful authority, without a new industrial-policy veto. Consent,
safety, land and environmental permission remain separate. Ordinary procurement
challenge, urgent direct C08 courts and protected complaints apply without
requiring internal reconsideration first. A court can correct unlawful process,
discrimination or conflicts and enforce lawful dues; it cannot invent an
appropriation or choose an unfunded industrial policy. A complaint alone
neither proves guilt nor stops essential services.

Publish quarterly commitments, cash, controllers, returns, losses and defined
benefits; Assembly reviews annually. Existing independent audit uses risk and
random source/delivery checks, with protected records access for opposition
and courts and privacy for personal evidence. AI summaries retain inspectable
sources and accountable human reasons. Political dissent or lawful refusal of
AI cannot disqualify an applicant. Honest authorized failure is not fraud.

**IC12 — Stop expansion without abandoning liabilities.** At a commitment or
exposure limit, stop new commitments that would exceed it. Separately recognize
supported impairment/loss when it occurs, even below a limit, and disclose who
bears it. A write-down cannot replenish gross commitment authority or conceal
nominal guarantee exposure. A performing portfolio can reach its cap. No
automatic rescue, recapitalization or hidden guarantee. Additional public risk
requires a new appropriation showing displaced protected uses. Lawful private
creditors/owners bear their agreed risk; due workers, pension claims and victims
retain their legal priority and remedies, rather than being erased by closure.

Expiry ends new awards only. Each window names in advance an existing lawful
successor for records, contracts, loan collection, servicing, audits, remedies
and funded safe closure. Elections or renewal cannot cancel those obligations.
Renewal compares the incumbent with repair, ordinary procurement and credible
private/cooperative supply; continued existence is no presumption of success.

The published [2020 English KfW law](https://www.kfw.de/PDF/Download-Center/Law-Concerning-KfW/KfW-Gesetz-DE-EN.pdf)
illustrates specified mandates and the importance of tracing guarantees and
retained profit. This proposal does not import that law's federal guarantee,
bank status or reserve powers, or claim that its model proves these controls work.

[SC ordinary buying](../../C04/05-public-buying-and-continuous-supply.md) now details
procurement entry/award/standstill/direct court and continuity; it does not alter
IC investment caps, grant/equity/title rules or old due rights. New work is unpriced.
