# T129–T132. Verify an essential input through commissioning and replenishment

*Design467 · Part of [staged capacity and protection](../06-staged-capacity-and-protection.md).*

**T129 — Work backward from the first useful service.** For a critical
imported or domestic input in a material programme, record the original
service and next call. Identify the last useful operating start,
functional acceptance, installation and acceptance staff, site, arrival,
clearance and settlement. Identify the competent owner at each step.
Test the last useful times under the same declared interruption.
A contract acceptance, border release or installed component establishes
only its own event. Apply NS21–NS30 and the competent sector and safety
rules to the actual input.

**T130 — Reconcile each independent resource in the elected comparison.**
Compare ordinary procurement with a usable retained or repaired route
and a genuinely independent supply, retrofit or mutual-aid alternative.
Keep owned currency, gross lawful commitments, payment to the original
supplier, compatible inventory, paid commissioning and the source of
replenishment separate. Price alternate operators and displaced origin
work, including independent evidence and court execution. Count each
part, qualified shift and service slot only within its accepted scope.
A later refund, late shipment or unqualified substitution cannot fill
an earlier window. Put live isolation, switchback and safe restart
on the same calendar as existing maintenance and service. Verify
actual coverage at the affected sites and homes through the cutover.
Compare in-place work, a separately accepted parallel supply and
a later or narrower isolated start. Before binding an affected
stage, price the alternate source, operators, safety checks and
restoration, with its own next-call coverage. An accepted part and
paid installer cannot qualify an outage of a prior protected service.

**T131 — Give conditional obligations precise dates and scope.** An
optional start relying on an indispensable input waits where its own
lawful release condition is missing. Use a separately authorized
continuity route where qualified. Separate an unbound new order from
already binding supplier, worker and resident claims. Keep each actual
due act and review route. If acceptance cannot occur by the useful time,
report the service that will be exposed and return the exact scope and
source choice to the competent bodies. Apply relevant timely remedies
without treating a technical result as a new authority or general stay.

**T132 — Check the next call and actual receiving before claiming continuity.**
The named service owner verifies independently qualified replenishment
or a lawful paid substitute at the next useful date. Treasury and
auditors verify their own parts of the chain. Preserve actual use,
the lost window, affected people and any later correction separately.
Keep original records restricted to necessary scope. AI can compare
milestones and flag common dependencies and impossible calendars.
It cannot certify quality, settle a payment, permit a shipment
or decide whose protection can lapse. Reopen on delayed approval,
failed commissioning, exhausted reserve or an equally effective
qualified route with lower costs or burdens.
