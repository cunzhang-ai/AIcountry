# Design340. Pay for scrutiny and continuity when material powers expire

[PWR01–PWR06](../../country/C03/01/01-review-and-renewal-of-material-powers.md)
assigns separate funding for the legislative agenda, evidence, affected
participation, service transition and ongoing obligations. A capacity
shortfall does not authorize renewal.

The [decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-public-power-renewal/decision-record.md)
retains selective repeal and review without expiry as alternatives.
Actual costs, overlap capacity and independent scrutiny remain open.
