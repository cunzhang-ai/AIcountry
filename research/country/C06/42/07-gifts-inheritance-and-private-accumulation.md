# Recurring taxes: received transfers, source years and private accumulation

*NF25–NF28; part of [C06/42](../42-recurring-tax-and-noncash-work.md).*

[Design156 TN17](../59/05-country-package-and-source-continuity.md) retains
the explicit recipient/base/rate/threshold benchmark and protected old periods,
withdrawing the whole NF numerical default replacement preference. Actual lawful
sources continue until express prospective replacement; no deemed receipt/yield.

**NF25 — Recipient and actual beneficial receipt.** A TA-resident natural person
receiving gifts/estate/trust property owes on actual new beneficial control,
worldwide subject to proved treaty/foreign relief. A nonresident owes only on
received domestic immovable-property interests, including the attributable
fraction of actual shares/rights in a body deriving at least50% of gross value
from domestic immovable property at any time in365days before transfer. Prove
that actual ratio/source and treaty limit; do not tax all worldwide share value. A disputed will, unreceived/
blocked foreign asset or bare custodian title is not receipt; loans are not
gifts unless genuinely forgiven/transferred. Actual joint ownership/shares and
prior debts are respected. No automatic spouse exemption, donor-count threshold
or controller/AI copy multiplies one person's allowance. Necessary dependant
support, genuine shared-cost reimbursement and actual court-owed support are
excluded, not a renamed capital gift or whole private-family wealth file.

**NF26 — Prospective cumulative10/20% with small-gift relief.** Ordinary gifts
up to.1M per recipient per calendar year in aggregate are excluded; not.1M per
donor/account. Inheritance is not in this small-gift exclusion. Record only
necessary bounded sources; ordinary small presents need no universal life log.
Other post-commencement transfers accumulate in units of the lawful M at each
receipt. With z prior units and new taxable V/M, charge **M×[F(z+V/M)−F(z)]**,
where F has0% on first5units,10% on next15 and20% above20. No tax on historical
pre-commencement gifts or invented missing history; source-period thresholds
remain fixed, later M changes affect new receipts only. z includes only prior
post-start transfers inside this country's NF25 nexus when received, including
its zero lifetime bands; exclude annual small-gift relief and foreign-only
nonresident gifts. Immigration does not tax earlier foreign receipt, emigration/
return does not reset genuine prior domestic z, and no re-entry retaxes owned
assets. Genuine foreign-only natural-person gifts may remain outside this base;
no universal donor/citizenship/exit charge is silently created. Keep a protected minimal z/source-period/M/final-correction record for this
continuing liability, not permanently public family testimony or unrelated raw
bank history; PD purpose/access/retention and actual claim-finality rules apply.
Report/pay by30April
following receipt year with supported assistance/TX source calendars. Recipient
loss of a genuine gift before usable receipt is not fictitious positive income.

**NF27 — One transfer base, continuing gains and liquidity.** A gift/inheritance
is not also PA11 ordinary income; genuine pay/dividends cannot disguise themselves
as gifts. Carry giver's documented adjusted acquisition basis for later TA06
realized gains; no automatic market step-up or new deemed sale at death. Actually
paid transfer-tax principal attributable to that asset adds to basis once,
not interest/penalties; deferred unpaid
tax is no paid basis. Missing original basis uses actual protected source/
review, not a punitive invented zero. Land/transfer/later gains are distinct
cumulative burdens, not described as one tax. Credit actual same-transfer foreign
tax only up to domestic liability, no refund or fabricated foreign receipt.

An illiquid primary home or continuing operating business may elect up to ten
equal annual installments,2% simple carrying interest on outstanding principal,
secured without priming earlier rights; first is due30April after receipt.
Each installment has its own original due event/TX outer, no indefinite renewal.
Carrying interest covers only not-yet-due principal; after maturity use actual
TX neutral late-interest rules, never both charges on the same time/principal.
Means/essential occupancy, worker and client protection stays under TX/B/H;
no automatic possession or family status decides it. Other gifts use ordinary
TX need-based plans. Deferred liability is no cash for current public services.

**NF28 — No unallocated private-foundation escape.** An irrevocable private
accumulation trust/foundation with no identifiable currently receiving taxable
natural beneficiary owes20% of net gift value it actually receives, with no
multiplyable personal5M exemption. This applies only to an actual gift/beneficial
transfer to the separate private vehicle. A resident vehicle uses NF03 residence
and worldwide receipt; a nonresident vehicle is charged on the domestic land/
land-rich fraction above. A resident natural donor's actual funding of a foreign
unallocated private vehicle instead incurs the same20% donor-side charge, once,
with same-asset actual foreign entry-tax credit capped at that liability. No
charge on an ordinary foreign natural recipient is implied. Distribution cannot
reset a vehicle's actual source period or grant a world enforcement power;
 nominee custody, genuine restricted
campaign/public grants and ordinary trading capital subscriptions are different.
A genuine irrevocable public-purpose charity for health/care, education,
science/culture, environment or equal rights, with no donor/family private-use
right, is outside entry tax regardless of lawful advocacy/political viewpoint; commercial profit and diversion keep their
actual NF/TA/ordinary enforcement. Revenue must prove the actual arrangement,
not infer it from an unfamiliar name, software or an unrecognized social form.

Each such private accumulation vehicle is also an NF02–NF08 taxpayer on net
investment/trading returns regardless of commercial label: interest, rents and
realized gains use actual corporate source/cost rules, gift principal is not
income, and domestic company dividends retain their one-company-layer treatment.
Actual income/earnings distributions to natural beneficiaries are PA11 income,
not NF26 gifts; distribute earnings first, then traced gift principal. No
low transfer band converts untaxed investment returns into exempt gifts.

Actual entry-taxed principal distributions are excluded from a second NF26
charge only to the extent traced to that already taxed principal net of entry
tax, with source ledger; untaxed accumulations/new gifts retain their own rules.
No tax-credit cash refund or exclusion beyond actual remaining taxed principal.
Do not release gift principal without settling/reserving its due entry tax;
unlawful distributions retain actual B/U creditor recovery/defense, not automatic
controller guilt. If unpaid entry principal also attracts recipient NF26, actual
collection on that same principal credits the remaining entry claim, no duplicate
public recovery beyond the greater applicable charge. Default/foreign refusal
remains a possible public loss, not an assumed receipt.
A private-use benefit to an actual person is valued/attributed under its actual
work/dividend/gift character; no double recovery on the same transferred value.
Entity entry tax uses annual30April filing/TX due/review; no default personal-
home deferral for a vehicle or new seizure priority. Independent court decides
contested classification; actual unreceived assets/unknown meanings stay disclosed.

[PF05/PF12](../57-private-retirement-tax-and-personal-capital.md) preserves
these actual gift/vehicle distinctions. A paid subscription for an identified
member's own pension claim is not itself an unallocated gift; a disguised NF28
vehicle retains this entry/returns/earnings-first law. Actual inherited units
keep NF27 carryover basis/paid attributable tax once, not a step-up or allowance
per account. A contractual survivor's own payment is separately classified.
