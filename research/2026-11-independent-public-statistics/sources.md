# Original evidence for independent public statistics

| Primary source | What it establishes | Design use and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [UN Statistics Division, Fundamental Principles handbook](https://unstats.un.org/capacity-development/handbook/html/Handbook/C3/UN_Fundamental_Principles_of_Official_Statistics.htm) | The official handbook identifies professional method and release decisions, advance calendars, equal access, correction and statistical-purpose confidentiality. It also distinguishes coordination from production across official sources. | An established institutional standard, not evidence that a particular director's term, vote or appropriation is sufficient. The candidate grants no general mandatory survey power. |
| [Statistics Canada, 2021 Census coverage estimates](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/230927/dq230927f-eng.htm) | The official release reports 3.1% national net undercoverage for the 2021 census. Separate coverage studies feed demographic estimates without replacing the published enumeration. It cautions about comparison as methods change. | Different versions and uses can be legitimate. The reported rate provides no estimate of local service coverage or need in the constructed budget. |
| [Office for Statistics Regulation, 2023 Labour Market Statistics rapid review](https://osr.statisticsauthority.gov.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Review_of_experimental_labour_market_statistics_produced_by_the_office_for_national_statistics.pdf) | The regulator examined an experimental alternative after the survey-based release became unusable in its then-current form. It recorded problems for users who lost regional and group breakdowns and competing resource needs for ordinary production and transformation. | A replacement headline can lack the detail its consumers depend on. The report does not establish that the candidate's institutional split would avoid disruption. |

The [coverage comparison](../settlement/independent-public-statistics/README.md)
uses authored dates, range and price assumptions. None of these sources
qualifies the proposed country's actual staff, funding or outcomes.
