# PF09–PF12: private capital is neither public insurance nor automatically cash

*Part of [private retirement tax](../57-private-retirement-tax-and-personal-capital.md); prospective resource/source clarification.*

**PF09 — Preserve Y's different promise.** Public Y contributions pool risk;
they are not a private personal capital account or new PF cost basis. Public
basic/earned/credited/survivor units, original indexation and stronger source
law remain. New actual work remuneration still bears its Y source/contribution
and applicable service-year ceiling, even when the person saves its net amount.
Later private capital/pension receipt is not worked wages taxed to Y again.
Proved unpaid work and cash-independent qualified interruption credits keep
their own law; a private contribution alone creates no public earned unit.

**PF10 — Voluntary saving does not subtract ordinary received resources.**
I05 E uses actual net work remuneration before the person's voluntary private
contribution. PF03's net wages actually paid to a chosen plan are not unpaid
wages or an expense added to N. An unfunded promise is neither current E nor
a received private asset. Locked/unavailable plan balances and another person's
fund remain unavailable; tax recognition/withholding credit alone is not food,
rent money or received ordinary in-kind support.

Use existing I04/I06 evidence-based asset-draw/diversion rules, protected buffer,
actual safe need and direct review; do not invent collectible cash, compel
harmful liquidation or deny emergency necessities to punish private saving.
No compulsory spending ledger or employer approval governs ordinary lawful use.

**PF11 — Keep a resource-capital account distinct from indexed tax basis.**
Tax gain/exclusion does not determine I05 income or safely usable wealth. Trace
actual cash, nominal own capital, source/period and amounts already counted as
E/O/A. A redemption of assets already included in I04 A moves existing wealth;
it cannot also become new O solely because a tax gain is recognized. Adjust
the lawful asset account for actual payments/costs, without repeating consumed
stock or counting the same incoming income in A and O for the same period.

For previously unavailable private capital or a genuine periodic promise,
identify the actual nominal capital returned separately from new income.
Use the PF06 proportional rule on its unrecovered nominal resource cost for
DC; use capital-first recovery of nominal own resource cost for PF07 promises.
An inflation tax adjustment creates no extra nominal capital or cash. Deduct
only actual appropriately attributed tax once; keep the rest of received
income in O and safely usable capital under I04's buffer/draw, never exempt
all money because tax excluded part. Corrections preserve actual receipt dates.

For new PF payments, select the existing bounded contributory-pension disregard
only for that net income component at standard Y02 age, an actually qualified
Y04 early stage, or a separately lawful disability/survivor pension contingency.
It is one aggregate 20%-of-income/up-to-20%-of-B limit across qualifying public
and private receipts, not per account, and never an exemption for capital draw.
Ordinary earlier cash-outs remain ordinary resource receipts without this new
retirement qualification. This source-category extension needs express law;
it changes no basic eligibility, rate or stronger old disregard by inference.

**PF12 — Death changes people, not the amount of remaining basis.** Inherited
owned units/property follow actual civil beneficiary/title and NF25–NF27
transfer law, carryover tax basis and attributable transfer-tax principal
actually paid once; no market-value step-up or basis per heir. A contractual
survivor's own earned/annuity right is not automatically an inheritance of
the deceased's fund capital. Allocate a single contract's remaining cost among
actual successors under traced lawful shares; no double primary/survivor recovery.
Paid transfer tax adds a separate basis lot dated when actually paid, not an
inflation adjustment reaching back before that payment.

I05 separately examines the actual new beneficiary's owned/available assets,
income and support under source law, not an automatic copied resource exclusion
from the deceased's tax ledger. Unreceived/disputed estate assets are not cash;
necessary support and urgent services continue through status disputes. If no
estate/survivor claim survives, unreturned capital is not invented family wealth
or state compensation. Safe minimum basis/title records survive the provider,
without making sensitive family testimony permanently public.

[WF19](../58/05-observed-population-income-and-retirement.md) demonstrates the
classification boundary: reference statistical private pensions include public
servants' pensions/ATP, not automatically personal PF capital. Trace actual
source/ownership/period before any tax-basis, Y/I05 or replacement classification;
no statutory claim or safely usable cash follows from an aggregate label.
