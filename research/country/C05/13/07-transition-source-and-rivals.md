# OE31–OE35: a new constitution must have a lawful transition and real alternatives

*Part of [C05/13](../13-enterprise-and-association-constitutions.md); design118 candidate with Design544 disposition.*

Design544 selects ordinary lawful governance choice. OE31's board clock
applies only if a later competent Act selects that alternative and adopts its
transition. Review independently affected parties, costs and narrower
protection before making that decision.

**OE31 — Coordinated prospective law.** Competent OE/B/U/WL/TA/NF/PD/V/registry/
Treasury law starts new ordinary duties180 calendar days after enactment. Mandatory
OE worker-board composition takes effect12 calendar months after enactment;
complete the initial funded election before that date for already qualifying
undertakings, without an extra90-day extension. Actual stronger prior board/work
rights keep earlier dates. Fund recruitment, training,
records, ballot/court capacity and exact company/person/contract/source succession.
No restart of TW/WL/AS schedules or readiness veto over due claims/elections.

Preserve actual paid capital/title, membership, stronger work/board/contract,
pension, creditor/security, custody/charitable lock, tax, privacy/foreign and pending
claim rights. For existing supervotes/board classes or incompatible articles,
require notified competent-law migration with independent review and compensation
where source law requires it; no filing clerk confiscates vested property or
revives barred debt. Protected worker seats do not themselves convey private equity
or cancel capital return. New certification-risk/VD14 applies prospectively, with
no retrospective criminalization or guarantee of all old market loss. Express
coordinated law places established OE27 public compensation/state orders within
adopted B19 payer/appropriation/executor scope. Navigation and proposed review
methods do not enact themselves; actual constitution/software/permissions remain.

**OE32 — Price the whole dependence.** L04 production/labor, D10 civil entity/
contract/property, D07 career/integrity, D11 courts/aid/execution, R04 finance/tax,
K01 evidence/AI and affected service/worker owners share actual time/resources.
Original37/61 objects/bids/actual nulls remain original mapping, not a current
ceiling or new appropriation. Price board/representative/auditor/advice and covered
work replacement, registry/source/custody/ballot/court and official-risk exposure,
capital/working cash/exit and public noncommercial mandates once. Notice working
wages and the same replacement or retained profit are not paid twice.

Separate owner value, receipts, actual productive formation, G/local source,
restricted S/Y/client/insurer/pension/CCP and owned foreign settlement. No free AI,
assumed volunteer, fine, unreceived recovery, registry fee or coerced saving pays
these duties. Accounting surplus/paid subscription and reliable immediate cash
are distinct. Actual public loss/source schedules and independent professionals
remain unqualified; no new blank public guarantee or self-levy.

**OE33 — Structural rivals can displace this selection.** Select limited liability
with actual source, duty and return controls, plural forms, minority rights,
worker bargaining and representation, and elected public ownership. Board
composition follows the ordinary choice above. Investor-only boards may decide faster
and attract capital, but concentrate dependence. Full worker ownership/parity at
all sizes can deepen voice but concentrates worker financial/job risk and may
exclude users/entrants. Larger public/common ownership can protect access or
concentrate party/vendor control. Strong sector bargaining can coexist with a
different board model. Voluntary association purpose freedom is different from
public/enterprise power, not an exemption from worker rights.

Retain all as real whole arrangements with actual price/control/exit/continuity
comparisons; no claim that50/1000, six seats or four years is optimal. The alternative's
parity has no capital chair second vote: deadlock, capital cost, threshold gaming
and delayed change are actual risks, not proof of deeper democracy or better lives.
A justified retain/no change or different model remains valid.

**OE34 — Future cooperation is not exhausted by incorporation.** Shared situations,
transient teams, evolving understandings, commons and future media may warrant
new organizational forms. Preserve unfamiliar meaning and actual scoped commitments,
not automatic conversion into today's share company/member database. Expression,
interpretation, delegation, ownership, employment, consent and public authority
are separate. A replicated AI cannot supply new votes, capital, workers or mandate.
Keep mediator/controller replacement, safe revocation, offline authority and actual
continuing obligations; a converged ledger alone settles neither resource conflict
nor the right to direct others. No general license over informal human relationships.

**OE35 — Evidence and final review stay distinct.** Primary normative/comparative
sources can reveal options, not affected consent or a demonstrated national optimum.
Inspect actual control/elections/pay/transactions, public/private funding, excluded
voices, failure, privacy and source families, including protectors' capture. Root
analysis and same-controller model outputs are not independent professional or
worker/member/creditor/user review. New events/objections require fresh scoped
review before support reuse even with unchanged bytes. Keep safe reasons/corrections
without public permanent testimony. Legal selection, legitimate authority, paid
operational readiness and observed lives remain separate, presently unverified.
