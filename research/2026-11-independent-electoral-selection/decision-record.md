# Design288. Independent selection for the electoral legal source

10 November 2026 · Research selection · No actual enactment

## Baseline and decision

[Design287](baseline.json) retains the Design260 separation of ordinary
judicial appointments. CJ06 still supplies both constitutional judges
and two commissioners. One missing lawful voting category can delay both.
A different failure is common control of recruitment, funds, evidence
or review, which additional selectors alone cannot remedy. Apply the
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and the
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md). Codex assisted analysis
and drafting. Affected participants and an independent reviewer have yet
to assess the selection.

## Whole alternatives and choice

- **Retain shared selection and separate its operations.** Lower
  recurring professional and lay obligations. Choose this if delay is
  attributable to a shared administrative path and lawful categories
  remain available. A standing absence still blocks both appointments.
- **Separate the legal-source election selection and its usable
  operating path.** Select this prospective arrangement. It limits
  the effect of a category failure and retains three different
  appointment methods on the commission. It costs nine additional
  principal and nine additional alternate places at full coverage,
  paid review, access and actual cover for other duties. It still
  needs independent recruitment and protected rights.
- **Fill both seats through separately drawn public panels.** This
  removes the standing election legal formation and may make public
  control more direct. It gives that selection method five of the
  seven seats over time. Compare diversity of access, mediator and
  recruitment dependence and paid review before preferring it.
  Retain the distinct cabinet/ministry and chief alternatives
  already considered under EA18.

The [matched case](../settlement/independent-electoral-selection/README.md)
chooses the separate source under a declared loss, conditional on
an actual independent path and the priced court capacity. A legal
appointment on time restores the ability to convene in the example.
It supplies neither four votes on the commission's eventual act nor
authority to change a poll or declare a winner. The historical
[evidence](sources.md) qualifies mechanism distinctions only.

## Authority and transition

A competent democratic process must lawfully fund and fix a published
prospective boundary. Current officeholders, reserves, claims, ballots,
qualification rules, due hearings and governing mandates retain their
own law. New authority is limited to future process openings under
EA25. The prior process retains its record and remedies. Independent
courts hear ordinary selection challenges under EA16 and constitutional
dispositions retain their existing owner. Shared appointing interests
may still affect ordinary reviewing judges, so prior conflict and
assignment rules and an actual timely execution route matter. An
operating failure has the existing lawful disposition, including
unresolved status when necessary. It creates no appointment power.
