# Design405 decision. Preserve a real public choice before material execution

## Baseline and scope

This AI-assisted research selection uses [Design404](predecessor-design-404/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md),
the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and the [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).
IV and PL require return for material investment or disposal decisions.
SC already supplies procurement challenge. PI and RR give review routes
for their own grounds. An executable gap remains where a material public
choice is legally authorized or still under review but early release of funds,
control, assets or work leaves no practical alternative. The affected parties
include residents, workers, current service recipients, elected opposition,
counterparties and offices responsible for review and execution.

## Rivals and selection

Retain sector review alone. This avoids repeating procedures but can miss a
foreclosing act split across contracts, payments and operations. Require
independent preapproval of all material public releases. This may improve
discovery but makes a reviewer a general veto holder and increases the cost
of lawful service. Select a prospective statutory schedule of material acts,
complete notice, a limited concurrent interval and narrow independent relief.
Permit genuinely reversible preparation and retain due payments and urgent
protective acts under their own law.

The main objections are delay to needed delivery, abusive claims, strategic
fragmentation, and publishing too little because a transaction involves
commercial information. Limit coverage by actual effect and prior law.
Keep original sector intervals where equivalent, require fair reply and
protected original evidence and count the cost of delay. Test the ten
working days as a hypothesis. No independent reviewer or affected party
was secured for the selection.

## Control, timing and continuity

The competent elected body retains the merits and appropriations. The sponsor
identifies authority and practical exposure. Independent reviewers and courts
apply the governing law and decide any lawful interim relief. The actual
payment, registration or site officer must receive and execute it. Give
opposition and affected people usable access to source evidence and challenge.
The sponsor cannot approve an omission, and the court cannot choose a budget.
Before adoption, verify staffing, custody, review, payment and execution
independent of the sponsor's ability to obstruct. Appointment and replacement
remain under the applicable source law.

Valid contracts, accrued rights, prior orders, protected data and ordinary
services retain their source, dates and successors. No sunset revives a
different contract or forgives premature action. Return, invalidity and
good-faith claims remain separate legal determinations. Existing court powers
and tighter sector rules continue. Finance, procurement, service and site
execution must carry original deadlines across personnel and system changes.
AI can surface linked commitments, and accountable officers decide and act.

## Evidence, limits and continuation

[Primary procurement comparators](sources.md) inform the institutional
comparison. The [constructed lease case](../settlement/public-commitments-before-effective-review/01-the-depot-lease-and-the-review-window.md)
checks usable notice, concurrent periods, actual execution and protected pay.
[PCR01–PCR08](../country/C03/01/02-public-commitments-before-effective-review.md)
records the selected proposal. Lawful adoption, independent practical capacity,
actual service and observed outcomes remain unresolved.

The next bounded check is a legally inspectable material public release.
Reconstruct its last real alternative, final notice, original commitments,
hearing and operative receipt. Compare the time and cost of its sector
process against PCR, including interruptions and the effect on ordinary
duties. A competent public auditor and affected groups could examine this
with authorized records and funded independent access, neither yet secured.
Reopen on unusable notice, delays that regularly consume the remedy, project
fragmentation or a simpler effective sector process.
