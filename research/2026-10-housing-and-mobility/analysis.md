# Housing and everyday mobility as one lived settlement

3 October 2026 · Bounded national-design analysis for C04/C06–C07/C09/C13–C14 · Same-controller AI research recommendation, not enacted law

## The unresolved national choice

The [integrated country design](../COUNTRY-DESIGN.md) connects housing, transport,
work, care and services in a person's day. C04 assigns elected local access plans
and an Assembly decision for material unresolved cross-local allocations. C07
promises secure tenure, accessible housing supply and usable connections. C09
governs land, infrastructure, water and ecological limits. Yet no comparable
housing-and-mobility chain says who maintains adequate dwellings and everyday
routes, who pays for them, or who must act when a landlord, builder or transit
operator fails. Water, electricity and food have named stewards and operators in
C09. A permit, benefit or travel plan cannot by itself deliver a safe home or a
journey to school, care, work, a court or a polling place.

The problem has at least two explanations. Supply can fail because land, water,
grid, finance, builders and vehicles are physically scarce. Access can fail even
where aggregate stock exists because prices, location, inaccessible routes,
discrimination, tenancy insecurity or service hours separate people from daily
life. The first calls for coordinated capacity and investment; the second calls
for distribution, standards and enforceable remedies. Both can occur together.
No population, territory, stock, demand, commute pattern or original testimony
is available to determine their relative weight for AIcountry. This note
compares institutional responses without claiming such evidence.

## Coherent alternatives

| Model | Mechanism and attraction | Material failure and power risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Retain current design** | Keep C04/C07/C09 plans, ordinary markets, tenure law, targeted assistance and general national fallback. Avoid another administrative layer. | No named local capacity purchaser or transit commissioner must reconcile homes with routes and utilities. A housing voucher can chase absent stock; separate bus, road and housing agencies can each satisfy a paper duty while a person cannot reach an essential service. |
| **Predominantly direct national provision** | A national public body builds and owns much housing and operates major passenger transport, moving assets and funding across places. This could secure visible capacity and continuity where markets are thin. | Central control of jobs, tenancies and travel can become political leverage. National schedules may miss local geography and preferences. Acquisition, construction, workforce, debt and maintenance require a very large transition; state ownership does not create spare homes or vehicles. |
| **Regulated plural provision without a public backbone** | Private, cooperative and public developers and transport operators supply under land plans, safety/access rules and contracts; households receive targeted support. This can preserve initiative and ownership diversity. | Providers may favor profitable locations or users. A service contract, competition rule or insolvency claim does not supply a receiving home, driver, accessible vehicle or depot during exit. The public remains legally responsible without residual capability. |
| **Selected conditional reference: elected local public responsibility with plural production and residual capacity** | The Assembly sets an enforceable national floor and funding; elected local authorities commission housing capacity and an accessible daily mobility network, including maintained public or publicly controlled fallback assets. Private, cooperative and public providers build or operate under reviewable duties. Cross-boundary choices use C04's elected route. | Requires capable local institutions, a real public operating reserve, fiscal transfers and independent safety review. A local public owner may exclude newcomers, protect incumbents or become a patronage landlord; public residual capacity must be demonstrated rather than named. |

The selected model is conditional, not a claim that mixed ownership is optimal.
It retains private property, lawful enterprise, cooperatives and local choice
while rejecting the idea that a public guarantee can rely only on a contract
or cash transfer. A stronger direct-public model could prevail if actual
evidence shows that plural contracting fragments service or drains the public
workforce. Retaining the current arrangement could be justified if an existing
jurisdiction already has independently accountable delivery and receiving
capacity; none has been shown for this project.

## Proposed authority, money and daily operation

**Rule and territorial choice.** The Assembly would enact the housing safety,
tenure, nondiscrimination, accessibility and remedy floor; the scope of any
urgent shelter or mobility claim; interlocal competence; and the funding and
review rules. It would not promise an immediate preferred dwelling or universal
door-to-door service where capacity is absent. Elected local councils would
decide site and service organization within that floor and C09's land, water,
energy and ecological duties. The C04 interlocal forum would prepare material
cross-boundary housing-and-commuting options; the Assembly would make the
reserved final distributive choice when localities cannot, specifying payer,
implementer and conditions. Planners, developers and transport engineers would
not obtain a political allocation power from a model or deadline.

**Accountable delivery.** Each local authority would name a housing-capacity
authority answerable for an inventory, safe public or controlled residual
stock, lawful acquisition/commissioning, repair, accessible units and an
interim route for people whose present home is unusable. It would name a
mobility authority answerable for roads and safe walking/wheeling connections,
the minimum accessible passenger network, service hours and cross-provider
connections. A connected service can use scheduled routes, community transport
or other modes according to geography; a uniform mode is not the right. School,
health and care authorities retain their separate service obligations and
must reconcile their hours and specialist transport with the common network.
Private owners and operators keep their lawful property and commercial roles;
the authorities secure public duties rather than owning every home or vehicle.

**Independent checks and usable claims.** Building and transport safety
inspectors need information, staff and reviewable powers separate from the
authority or operator they inspect. Tenants, owners, riders, disabled people,
workers and displaced people need accessible intake, interim protection and
independent review. A provider cannot close a claim merely by offering an
unreachable unit or route. Review should examine actual cost, travel time,
barriers, safety and continuity, not only nominal vacancy or a scheduled trip.
The right to an accessible complaint and lawful political activity cannot depend
on a landlord, employer, fare account, phone or AI intermediary. Courts can
enforce a lawful duty and require disclosure; they cannot create dwellings,
drivers, cash or a water connection.

**One capital and operating account.** C06's joint schedule would distinguish
private investment risk from public capital appropriation, land servicing,
construction loans and guarantees, debt service, building renewal, insurance,
repair, route operation, vehicles, depots, maintenance and replacement workers.
National equalization would support the enacted floor where local need exceeds
fiscal capacity; local revenues, rents, fares and targeted household support
would have explicit roles. Low rent or fare cannot be financed by invisible
asset decay, coercive unpaid care or an unfunded local mandate. Subsidies,
concessional credit and public guarantees require an Assembly purpose, cap,
loss account and review under C05–C06. The same land, road space, building crew,
power connection, accessible vehicle and external currency cannot be booked
simultaneously for unrelated promises. Affordability, maintenance and ecological
costs must be compared together rather than maximizing construction or ridership
alone.

**Failure and delay.** Before a licensed transport operator exits, the authority
needs a lawful step-in or receiver path, protected route and ticket records,
accessible vehicles, depots, trained staff, parts and payment authority. A
landlord's insolvency or unsafe building requires a distinct due-process route
that preserves tenants' occupancy or safe interim accommodation, ownership and
creditor claims, accrued rent and deposit rights, and funded repair or transfer.
Neither failure authorizes automatic nationalization. A paper receiver without
assets and workers does not meet a continuing claim. During planning deadlock,
existing tenancy, safety, maintenance, service and ecological duties continue;
silence neither grants a project permit nor removes a person's claim. If the
fallback cannot operate, the authority publishes who lacks a safe home or route,
arranges lawful interim protection with available resources, and presents the
unfunded choice to elected institutions under C06. It may not redefine an
unusable offer as delivery.

## A compound shortage that could defeat the model

Assume an authored, unobserved region loses safe dwellings to flood damage while
a major transit operator fails and a grid connection for replacement homes is
delayed. A low-income carer must still reach work, a clinic and an older person's
home; a tenant cannot accept a replacement dwelling that destroys those links.
The local housing authority must identify safe interim accommodation, preserve
tenancy and property process, and disclose actual accessible stock. The mobility
authority must attempt lawful alternate routes with qualified staff and vehicles;
schools, care and health authorities arrange their own urgent accommodations.
The C09 water, grid and land stewards disclose what replacement sites can be
serviced and what ecological limits apply. Cabinet joins cash, labor, transport
and imported materials in C06's account. If the shortage redistributes material
burdens across localities, the C04 process prepares options and the Assembly
takes its reserved decision. Courts can review rights and prevent arbitrary
eviction or exclusion without claiming that an unavailable home exists.

This test fails the selected model if the supposed public fallback shares the
failed operator's controller, fleet, landlord, insurer or lender; if local
authorities conceal a politically disfavored group's unmet need; or if the
Assembly's choice cannot be paid for or implemented before people are exposed.
Relevant comparison is not a single housing or transit score: report safe
occupancy, displacement, time and cost to essential destinations, access for
different abilities, maintenance, distribution of public cost and ecological
loss, remedy completion and effective political contestability separately.

## Integration, objections and next test

For **C04**, name local housing and mobility authorities and use the existing
national fallback only for a prior-law essential-floor failure, with local
review and a route back to local control. For **C06**, join capital, finance,
operation, maintenance, targeted affordability, alternate operator and external
inputs to the same cash and physical schedule. For **C07**, define effective
housing and mobility access with scope, timing, safe interim provision and a
remedy, without implying unlimited preferred locations. For **C09**, connect
approved homes and routes to serviced land, water, grid, hazard and ecological
capacity. For **C13**, use the compound failure to test a person's actual day,
including political participation. For **C14**, inventory existing titles,
tenancies, deposits, buildings, vehicles, routes, concessions, workers, debt,
repair backlogs, hazard exposure and pending claims; name lawful successors
before changing delivery. C10 disaster logistics and C11–C12 offline access
must remain compatible, but neither supplies ordinary mobility authority.

Material objections remain unresolved. More public residual stock can improve
continuity yet concentrate control of homes and jobs; private production can add
capacity yet select profitable locations; stricter tenure rules can protect
residents while affecting future supply; lower fares can improve access while
starving maintenance; rapid construction can worsen water, climate or cultural
harms; local vetoes and national overrides can each disenfranchise affected
people. Owners, tenants, builders, transport workers, riders, disabled people,
caregivers, adjacent communities and future residents have different stakes.
Their participation and disagreement cannot be supplied by this AI-authored text.

**Evidence qualification:** this is a conceptual comparison based on the linked
working design and its internal premises, not observations from a jurisdiction.
**Research selection:** conditional local public responsibility with plural
production and demonstrable residual capacity. **Legal authorization:** none.
**Operational readiness:** no verified land, population, provider, workforce,
budget or independent receiver. **Observed outcomes:** none. This is
same-controller AI assistance, not independent review or affected-party consent.
The [status register](../DESIGN-STATUS.md) should retain those distinctions if
the main design selects this proposal.

The next bounded question is whether, for one actual connected housing-and-travel
area, the selected package can deliver a safe dwelling and reachable essential
services through a simultaneous building and operator loss at a lawful cost.
The deliverable would be a joined stock/route/rights/cash map and comparison with
the direct-public and regulated-plural models, using original local observations
and affected-party challenge. Completion requires named authorities, existing
obligations, provider and worker capacity, transfer powers, a subannual payment
schedule, ecological constraints and a witnessed remedy path. No study budget or
jurisdiction is available now. Evidence of capture, unusable access or inability
to staff the fallback would reopen the conditional selection; absent that evidence,
neither desk agreement nor another hypothetical scenario establishes success.
