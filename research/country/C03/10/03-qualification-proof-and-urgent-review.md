# ER01–ER09: career decisions, answerable proof and usable review

*Part of [C03/10](../10-democratic-entry-and-campaign-resources.md); design101 research, not enacted law.*

**ER01 — One early-voting-safe calendar.** In this new national-contest package, **V is the earliest lawful casting/return of any ballot, including postal, absent, assisted, institutional or early voting; P is final poll closing**. Select the explicit reference interval **Δ=14calendar days**, so V=P−14; only competent prospective law before the affected contest may revise Δ after actual absent/access capacity comparison. The new repeat package requires Δ≤30, not a postjudgment choice. Prior Assembly law fixes final P and therefore V, the full EL ballot/count/N and actual local map, source roll and lawful call before the contest. Ordinary call is at least90calendar days before V; an independently authorized early/repeat call gives at least60calendar days before V. All EN/CF/ER entry/support offsets use V, never the later P. Formal nomination/active-endorsement freeze is V−40, historical-proof cure V−30, normal production V−21, credit assignment V−14 and payment≤48elapsed hours thereafter; no actual vote may precede V by a provider's administrative direction. The law must price accessible absent/foreign distribution and safe timely return for its actual V/P interval. This does not call an early election, postpone P/V, extend an incumbent or change the count.

The common decision deadline is V−35. CF02 guide/directory availability and executable provider assignment are due48hours after qualification; the actual fair public presentation/Q&A is due no later than V−21, earlier when useful opportunity requires. This is an **express staged baseline amendment for these new national contests**, not a claim that a future booking delivered the old E16 whole48-hour promise. Every part retains its own actual receipt/arrear, and a late correction cannot erase lost earlier reach. Last-day common qualification may give only about33days before first voting; that is a real cost to newcomers versus longer preparation. Existing-called polls retain their prior law. **ER02 — Actual filing and accountable career decider.** An accessible paper, in-person, supported phone or secure digital nomination reaches the lawful local C04 career nomination officer for an actual local list and the preassigned national EA02 career registration officer for a national party/civic/one-person list. E02 first credible attempt at the lawful receiver fixes receipt; the officer acknowledges it with exact contested items and assistance within the reachable interaction or recovery. The election commission sets lawful general published directions under EA10 but need not cast four votes to approve each objective individual filing; the Act directly grants these career officers that statutory case competence. A finance investigator, commissioner, incumbent or vendor cannot veto or select the officer for a named opponent. Published prior assignment, conflict rotation, qualified substitute and direct court relief apply. A commission quorum failure leaves statutory career filing/qualification duties intact, but creates no commission certification vote or new power.

**ER03 — One substantive entry rule.** Apply EN01–EN08: actual C02 candidacy, party/civic-list and independent routes,2N pool ceiling, two active personal list endorsements, EL06’s min(ceil(E_call/100),ceil(E_call/(4N))) threshold and honest corrected-person field bound. No second cash-eligibility signature, wealth/deposit, party-history, ideological or account gate. Finance/support CF rules do not change C03 count or create an independent-seat exception for a controlled nominee. The ballot officer gives answerable reasons and separate original-source proof; an allegation alone cannot override this gate.

**ER04 — Specific verification and privacy.** The officer verifies identity/eligibility and actual assent against separately held roll and source records, with a documented risk/random sample and proportionate full check where a particular item could change the threshold or two-active-slot rule. An authenticated attestation, accessible assisted statement or other lawful original can prove support where an official ID/roll field is missing; state-held record failure cannot shift a technical dossier burden to the entrant. Publish counts, rule, exclusion reasons and material sponsor/control of the filing, not signer names, refuge location, small supporter identity, vote intention or complete personal files. Give the contestant a masked answerable discrepancy and protected source/court access; affected signer may correct mistaken attribution. No AI score, copied database, rival allegation or shared family name proves forgery. A person may revoke/reassign until V−40; proof later may establish the historical cutoff state but cannot change the choice retroactively. Proven forgery/coercion keeps its exact separate remedy.

**ER05 — Preliminary help, common final decision and useful correction.** Within 24 elapsed hours after E02 first receipt, give specific missing-item/help notice, including items held by public custodians; give a reasoned **preliminary eligibility and source-status response** within five calendar days of first filing. It is not ballot qualification or grant entitlement. At common V−40 close, freeze manifests and actual active endorsements; the assigned career officer serves final reasoned qualification/denial for **every** filing no later than V−35, earlier if needed for useful print/access. For an on-time filing, proof correction through V−30 may establish signer eligibility/active assent and candidate personal nomination assent actually existing by V−40, or current cutoff facts objectively dating the candidate’s lawful eligibility by final P under EN01, not create a nominee/new endorser/new choice after cutoff; give corrected reasoned disposition within 48 elapsed hours of sufficient proof and earlier useful time if needed. An office request, outage, missing state-held field or finance allegation does not reset the V−35 duty or V−40 first-receipt protection. A favorable correction activates the same E16 48-hour or earlier useful-time baseline; the late remedy's lost earlier reach remains recorded. Silence neither qualifies nor disqualifies.

**ER06 — Direct independent challenge.** Applicant, affected signer, competing contestant or voter with a concrete ballot/access claim may use EA16's unconflicted ordinary court directly, without internal exhaustion, party sponsor or finance-office permission. The court considers an urgent request under E13/B17 within 24 elapsed hours and sooner than the last useful print/access opportunity; the Act funds an assigned duty judge, counsel/interpretation, original roll/assent access and serving executor. The court can decide proven statutory eligibility/assent facts, correct a denial, order the actual career officer to enter a qualified filing, compel a missing public record or limited preservation, and protect affected voters and existing candidates. It cannot invent an assent, appoint a preferred commissioner, decide political truth, lower the threshold for one party, choose a winner or postpone P. Ex parte action obeys E14's 72-hour hearing plus one necessary 72-hour extension and is as reversible as possible. A contesting claim alone does not automatically suspend an opponent's entry or baseline support.

**ER07 — Last-minute facts and print.** If the office's lost record, blocked ID route or late wrongful denial threatens V−21 print, preserve the timely E02 filing and obtain an immediate evidence-based court disposition before the useful print lock. The court may order precise reprint/correction and official voter information when executable with actual cash, staff and safe distribution; it cannot treat an unverified claimant as automatically qualified by silence. A narrowly conditioned provisional ballot inclusion is possible only under an expressly enacted strong-likelihood standard, notice/defense, a technically tested ballot/custody method and **two separately auditable conditional counts** (with/without that candidature) that both obey EL07’s entire fixed-N capped proportional list/person allocation; the court must determine actual qualification before any affected certification. A mere label or segregated stack cannot claim a workable conditional count. If this is not safely possible, record the practical exclusion and preserve the postpoll claim rather than silently extending P or certifying an invented result. Wrongful late identity/support objections are treated by evidence, not accusation timing. Protected signer data stay under PD.

**ER08 — Finance and criminal boundaries.** The EA13 finance head may investigate actual donations, control or coordination using specific law, but an unresolved allegation does not hold an objectively qualified ballot filing, E16 baseline or voter-assigned support. Only a competent court applying exact enacted grounds may impose disqualification or interim restriction, after useful-time defense and proportionality; K23/K24/P and any new VD12 retain distinct fault/proof, not guilt by candidate status. Malicious reports do not give the office a censorship or counter-accusation shortcut: preserve original report/source and refer only proved separate offenses, with reporter protection for good faith and fair defense for the subject.

**ER09 — Old contests and limits.** The new rule and its **explicit E16 national-contest amendment** apply only after competent prospective enactment with trained local/national officers, call-roll originals, funded worst-case/growth baseline capacity, print/provider/court time and actual support appropriation; already called polls, filed nominees, donor promises, votes, privacy and pending challenges remain under lawful source/migration terms. Publish missing appointment, records, paper access or cash as failure, not automatic entry, delayed poll, election result or approval of the research. Reopen the V−40/V−30/V−21 sequence, two-active/EL06 support gate or baseline mode on observed exclusion, manipulation, inaccessible first receipt, signer coercion, capacity failure or a stronger more-open entry route.
