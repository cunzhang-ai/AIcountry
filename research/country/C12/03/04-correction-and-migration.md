# Public attention: correction, migration and rivals

*Canonical FA10–FA12; part of [C12/03](../03-finite-attention-and-lawful-closure.md).*

**FA10 — Correction without an endless argument veto.** Any person can report
lost meaning, source distortion, concealed control, unequal access, unlawful
selection or new consequential information in ordinary language. Within two
calendar days the team acknowledges and safely routes an accepted report; within
ten calendar days or earlier useful time it gives the reasoned process/routing
disposition. After the cycle the named public issuer owns this duty, not a
dissolved contractor. Missed time is failure, not approval or loss of court access.

A real procedural complaint or individual legal injury retains its applicable
consideration/remedy duty; staff cannot relabel it discretionary policy input to
avoid FA02 or courts. A request based on a new policy argument, value or evidence
is not thereby entitled to exhaustive merits review within ten days. Apply the
declared finite inspection/selection and safe intake bounds, say whether it was
examined, selected for bounded further work, referred to the ordinary elected
agenda or remains unexamined, and explain that status. A routing answer is not
substantive refutation. Calling endless new arguments “complaints” neither buys
infinite analysis nor proves that the original process was unlawful.

New original evidence, material events, suppressed reasoning, translation loss,
legal/dependency error or a newly exposed affected group may justify reopening,
a narrower decision or a stay under existing law. A changed value judgment can
also warrant elected reconsideration; it need not pretend to be a new fact.
Novelty alone does not compel a complete restart or stay. Where a material request
is actually examined, an issuer declining reopening states relevance, consequence
and remaining uncertainty; it cannot call a contribution false merely because it
missed the old intake window. A credible urgent legal/safety injury goes to its
own timely receiver rather than waiting for discretionary agenda space.

Court review concerns legal duties, adequate access, rational reasons, fair
process and actual rights, not the judge's favorite thirty policy arguments.
A court may require correction, quash an unlawful act or protect against unlawful
irreversible action under its existing powers. Mere filing is not an automatic
stay; useful-time individual/urgent relief remains governed by its own rules.
A defective consultation is not cured by an expert certificate, deadline or
majority pleasure. A procedurally complete consultation can still produce an
unlawful substantive decision. Preserve that distinction in the closure record.

**FA11 — Migration preserves scope and obligations.** General lawful adoption
specifies which future consultations use this protocol and dates/owners/funds.
Pending ones keep their valid rules unless an expressly lawful, fair migration
preserves accepted submissions, required consideration, claims, clocks and pay.
No new finite rule retroactively calls a promised review complete. Records and
safe custody survive contractor exit; public history does not require permanent
publication of sensitive testimony. Distributed/local forums may use different
lawful methods; a common record does not grant them another community's budget
or override its consent. Existing informal cooperation can remain informal.

AIcountry research may compare and criticize this model using its existing
permissions and proposed common method. FA is not an adopted software submission
cap, public-review gate or procedure for approving itself. The method's scoped
attention clarification has its own preserved predecessor and no adopting power.
Agent copies do not create independent review, popular participation or mandates.

**FA12 — Real rivals and a test that can fail.** Exhaustive substantive review
before closure gives each distinct reason more assured attention but permits
indefinite obstruction under novel generation. Elected representative scrutiny
without this extra portfolio is cheaper/simpler and may protect informal
cooperation, but concentrates framing in members and staff. Decentralized
situation-based affected forums can understand lived context better, while
shared national burdens still require a legitimate common choice. These are
real different institutions, not steps inevitably absorbed into FA.

Choose this bounded reference provisionally for major national consultation;
retain simpler ordinary representative decision where no such process is legally
required. Reopen on inaccessible intake, manipulated sampling, systematic lost
meanings, controlled mediators, excessive burden/delay, unresolved grave risks,
failed court correction, changed evidence or a better supported rival. Measure
actual understanding, influence, exclusion, burden and correction, not tokens or
agreement. No affected public, independent qualified process evaluation or
comparative outcome evidence has yet validated the design or its numeric bounds.
