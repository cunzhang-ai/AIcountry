# C09. Land, ecology, infrastructure and resilience: Essential systems and ecological limits

*Part of [C09. Land, ecology, infrastructure and resilience](../C09-land-and-ecology.md); integrated design 59 research, not enacted law.*

The selected essential-systems arrangement names public stewards for water, power
and food security without imposing one ownership form. A water steward accounts
for catchment limits, safe supply and the national floor while elected local
authorities arrange distribution through public or licensed utilities. A power
steward secures adequate network and generation capacity; the grid/dispatch
operator has nondiscriminatory access and published reliability duties, separate
from sellers' preferences. A food-security authority monitors physical availability,
safe procurement, reserve rotation and last-mile delivery while farms, processors
and shops may be private, cooperative or public. Cash nutrition support remains
with the social-protection authority. Independent water, grid and food-safety
reviewers inspect the operators, including public ones; no operator judges its
own serious failure. The distinct [sanitation and waste duties](06-sanitation-and-waste.md)
cover unsafe effluent, on-site systems and solid-waste routes that safe water
supply alone does not deliver. Every license or public charter names renewal,
spare parts, staff, records, outage notice, compensation and a funded successor or temporary
receiver. A lawful safety isolation or recall is temporary, reasoned and
reviewable; it does not create a permanent resource allocation or new tax.
The fuller [ordinary food-system route](09-food-systems-and-nutrition.md)
connects physical stewardship to household access, dietary adequacy,
food safety, producers, imports and ecological limits without merging
their separate decisions or presuming a state diet.

Before a shortage, the Assembly enacts essential-access and ecological duties,
the method for temporary scarcity priorities, tariff/assistance rules and
cross-boundary competence. Life-sustaining water, food, care, sanitation and
the ecological conditions of future supply cannot be counted as available
twice. Operators report actual stocks, network condition, dependencies and
losses; Cabinet reconciles pumping, cooling, transport, fuel, imports, repair
workers and cash across systems for public Assembly decisions. A technical
forecast constrains feasible choices but does not allocate political priority.
If rights and physical supply cannot all be met, responsible authorities disclose
unmet duties, deliver lawful interim protection and seek a funded elected
decision rather than redefining an outage as completed service.
When several sectors need the same reserve, C06's [staged scarcity
authority](../C06/05-cross-system-scarcity.md) defines bounded first-hour
acts, common-stock records and return to elected choice. A water, grid or
food operator cannot turn technical safety competence into a general
priority over another sector or locality.

Ordinary health, utility, building and civil-protection law should fund hazard
surveillance, maintenance, heat/flood planning, accessible warnings, mutual aid,
safe shelters, backup power and trained repair and clinical reserves before a
disaster. Locations, stocks, transport and payment paths matter as much as stock
targets. Climate and ecological limits constrain production and construction;
local measurement must establish actual budgets. Carbon accounting alone does
not represent biodiversity, water security or all harms. The
[C09 transition rule](08-climate-and-ecological-transition.md) assigns
an elected emissions path, distinct ecological floors and a financed
reliable-capacity sequence without giving a grid operator climate lawmaking.
The [disaster-health comparison](../../2026-10-material-and-resilience/disaster-health.md)
keeps ordinary prevention and response distinct from extraordinary coercive
authority under C10.

Local water, land, ecological and network authorities keep original place-specific
observations and their correction histories. National public statistics disclose
how shared resource estimates were built, where observation is missing, and how
method changes affect earlier comparisons. Neither a minister's improved metric
nor an AI reconstruction can erase a downstream community's original account
or certify that an unmet ecological duty has been delivered.

Economy-wide material accounts should connect household, private and public demand
to land, water, energy, imports, waste and ecosystem effects. A service ministry's
energy line or a carbon price alone cannot establish enough physical capacity for
the whole country. Where imported inputs shift harm abroad, the external account and
affected-community evidence must show that cost rather than calling it domestic
success.

Future-generation and environmental representation should have access to information,
legislative hearings and standing for legal review, not an unaccountable power to govern.
The [future-options rule](04-future-options-and-irreversibility.md) requires an
explicit elected choice before material hard-to-reverse loss, including cumulative
small approvals and a credible restoration path.
Resource extraction and imports must disclose harms exported abroad. “Green” national
figures cannot conceal displaced pollution or uncompensated losses elsewhere.

The [joined national package](../C06/20-national-economic-package.md) now
compares public and private energy/water requirements, protected ecological
and standby uses, and foreign supply together. Its larger-economy cases
fail those authored constraints despite annual Treasury success; actual
sources, geography, permits and delivered remedy remain unverified.

[TS/TD](../C08/22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md) now specifies
entry/operating, source/evidence and actual repair duties. [C09/11](11-safe-infrastructure-and-mobility.md)
connects safer design, maintenance and usable alternatives; neither technical
approval nor a missing inspector displaces lawful access/continuity duties.

[PS01–PS20](../C06/37-physical-supply-and-paid-trade.md) now selects resource-fit
mixed domestic/trade supply, accepted repair/reuse and public/common control
of new publicly financed core network assets, with plural accountable operators.
Existing title/concessions require lawful paid migration. Captured returns,
quality/season/stock, firm MW/response and paid foreign sources are distinct;
the joined reference does not establish actual source or national feasibility.

[Design127 physical portfolio](../C06/50-country-physical-portfolio.md) now supplies a scoped replacement working reference: literal PB/private/institutional products, monthly source/reuse, matched meters, bounded crop/trade and reinforced power rivals. Old example scopes remain historical; no actual nutrition, quality, engineering, title, source or tariff is adopted. Existing duties and stronger rights persist.

[PS21–PS32 critical public backup power](../C06/37/06-critical-public-power-and-actual-priority.md)
now supplies a separately enacted standing allocation for scheduled temporary
public generators/batteries/fuel, not the entire grid. Task-specific bodily
priority can leave real political/record loss; qualified safe operation, actual
clinical/UA10 law, RX/RR holds and unmet claims remain. No free redundancy,
new CP resource, general rationing power or full-source feasibility follows.

[NR mineral rights](15-finite-resources-and-mineral-rights.md) concerns finite
geological title/grants and title-neutral operating/closure duties, not automatic
ownership from a publicly financed grid. Critical-input demand, a clean-energy
label or mineral rent does not waive ecological/source duties or supply actual
mined inputs. No deposit or royalty is assumed in this country's sources.

[WS01–WS20](16-water-allocation-and-service.md) supplies the ordinary water institution/allocation/service-cost settlement: actual state Steward/local Director/outside Reviewer, direct public default for new public/residual systems, finite covered connected grants and elected attributable tariffs. Existing public/plural/collective rights, TD07 safety, essential nonpayment protection and paid successors remain; power is unchanged.

[EG01–EG12](19-electricity-service-and-grid-access.md) specifies the electricity
operators and ordinary access, charges and service continuity. Keep the
actual engineering, power rights, PS backup scope, prior-law emergencies and
existing utility and customer obligations distinct.

[AQ clean air](24-clean-air-and-cumulative-exposure.md) gives a place
with harmful combined exposure an accountable monitoring, correction and
receiving route. Source permits and ecological and service duties remain
separate.

[Ecological replacement](04/01-ecological-loss-and-replacement.md)
verifies additional function within the affected system and exposure window
before dependent irreversible work. Separate flood protection, native
habitat and public services in the common stock and budget.
