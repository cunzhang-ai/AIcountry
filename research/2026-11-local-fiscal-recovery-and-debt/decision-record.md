# Design208 decision. Protect local democracy and public duties through debt distress

9 November 2026 · Research selection from Design207

## Gap and selected response

MF protects due transfers and common rights. FB governs temporary and
persistent financing gaps. C06 public debt requires a lawful perimeter
and keeps local claims distinct, while disaster law protects affected
local continuity. The ordinary municipal distress path lacks an exact
boundary between the urgent payment, continuing public funding, local
choices and a creditor adjustment that can bind a holdout.

Select LD01–LD08. First identify the problem and protect actual service.
Correct undisputed source obligations, use a finite voluntary bridge only
against a real repayment path and take a permanent mismatch to a lawful
source and service choice. Prefer voluntary debt agreement. Add a court-
supervised composition only for expressly eligible future domestic
unsecured finance or existing holders who actually opt in. Require
recorded council initiation and plan, class assent and a rights- and
service-compatible feasibility test. No national general guarantee,
political receiver, creditor self-government or appointment power follows.

## Original evidence and competing choices

Danish same-year accounts show municipal taxes, national grant allocation,
conditional lines and actual adopted budget figures with distinct scopes.
They also distinguish a special grant from a capital or refinance loan.
The Peterborough direction shows permission to capitalize specified costs
and the consequences for later revenue provision. U.S. federal law shows
a separate public debt adjustment with limits on judicial control. These
are institutional and source observations, not actual AIcountry outcomes.
[Evidence](sources.md)
· [Observed budget and legal comparisons](../settlement/local-fiscal-recovery/01-observed-funding-and-distress.md)

Retain normal grants and negotiation where sufficient. A national
guarantee can lower some borrowing risk but transfers exposure and may
reward misuse. Central financial control may restore a payment function
yet can capture local policy and elections. A broader restructuring can
reach more liabilities with larger losses for workers, suppliers and
rights. Choose a narrower route to test whether eligible debt can move
while rights and local policy stay with their owners. It will leave
secured, foreign or otherwise excluded debts unresolved in some cases.
There is no claim this choice minimizes credit cost. Reopen if credit
becomes inaccessible or the route fails to prevent material service loss.

## Cumulative control, creditor loss and service delivery

A creditor or national official might use delayed payment to acquire a
veto over a council. A local executive might use a hardship claim to hide
diversion, related creditors or permanently unfunded choices. Separate
career evidence custody, grant calculation, legal payer, council vote,
service receiving and independent courts. Check beneficial ownership,
actual voting amounts, affected claims and a realistic lawful no-plan
alternative. A dissenter may suffer a reduced entitlement only within
prospective law and after the specified protections. Hear workers,
residents and suppliers through usable existing routes. The financing
authority cannot be the only source custodian or reviewer. AI assists
analysis with auditable provenance and an available manual route.

The seven-day preliminary account, 30/90-day stay and two-thirds class
support are proposed bounds. The court can refuse an order, never fill
an Assembly budget or assume resource capacity. Existing elections,
rights, debt, wage, pension, contract and service rules continue.
Adoption needs lawful financing, credible independent staff, explicit
future debt terms and paid successors. This AI-assisted decision record
obtained no actual affected-party participation, independent qualified
review, enactment, funded readiness or evidence of better lives.

independent court and election paths, supplier terms and the national
source of any interim payment. Check whether another locality claims

choice. Reopen on concealed debt, capture, unaffordable service, an
