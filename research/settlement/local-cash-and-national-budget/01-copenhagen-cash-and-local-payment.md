# Copenhagen's 2026 local cash and a separate transfer test

Match the City of Copenhagen to the same Danish opening year as
the [central-government comparison](../opening-debt-and-cash-window/01-denmark-opening-quarter-and-release.md).
The two governments' accounts remain separate. Amounts in the
observed comparison are DKK million.

| Published local measure | Amount | Scope |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 2025 Q4 reported liquidity | 20,700 | Trailing 12-month average |
| 2026 Q1 reported liquidity | 20,949 | Trailing 12-month average, excludes deposited restricted funds as defined by the source |
| Budget-management cash after 2025–26 carryover | 1,000 | Municipality's measure after existing decisions, assessed in the April note |
| Approved capital project budgets, 2026–2037 | 24,548 | Multi-year scheduled expenditure, not a same-day cash deduction |

The reported trailing average rose by 249. The April note's
budget-management figure matches the stated first-half target.
These different measures cannot be subtracted to estimate earmarked
cash or prove actual money available to a specific payer that day.

In a separate August 2026 budget assessment, the city reports a
move in budget-management cash from 1,135.5 after its May forecast
to 710.8. It discusses proposed additional expenditure of 467.3
for displaced people from Ukraine, with 111.5 of national finance
and 355.8 expected to fall on the city's budget-management cash.
It also reports an allocation of 110.4 for past part-time staff
additional work following a judgment and a 34.0 net reduction
from other midyear changes to grants and equalization. Other
highlighted increases are 66.1 for prior-year reimbursement,
8.4 for a local pay allocation and 2.0 for separate cases.
Together these displayed movements imply 711.8, leaving a −1.0
residual against the reported 710.8. Leave it unexplained.
The 10.8 above the second-half target of 700 was identified
as a potential budget source subject to final assessment.

In October 2025 the council chose the state-guaranteed tax and
grant basis for 2026. The alternative local forecast projected
126 more at the selected tax rate before the shared adjustment,
with any final settlement due in 2029. Keep that alternative out
of the opening cash account and retain its potential later
effects as a separate comparison. The guarantee choice did not
prevent the August midyear budget changes. These records show
budget and liquidity measures with distinct timing and scope.
They do not show the day the state or individual payee received
or sent each amount. [Sources](../../2026-11-local-cash-and-national-budget/sources.md).

## Constructed payment dates

A local body starts with 4 usable units and 5 more earmarked
for separate capital work. It owes 9 for a national duty and
has an approved reimbursement of 6 due later. It needs 5
before the service deadline. The central payer has separately
authorized and usable capacity to advance 5 now. Select that
advance in this case, if it reaches the local payer in time
and the service actually receives the money. The later net
settlement of the same liability is 1 if 6 remains allowed.
The consolidated account removes the internal transfer while
retaining the 9 of actual service cost once. If the advance
fails to settle, the shortage stays at 5 unless another lawful,
funded payer takes the duty. A forecast or loan without lawful
availability supplies no cover. A reduced final award would
use its own review and loss rules.
