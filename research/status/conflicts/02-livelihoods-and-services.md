# Legacy conflicts: livelihoods and services

*Unresolved older-law and transition issues in integrated design 69; research, not enacted migration.*

| Earlier conflict or gap | Current working resolution | Continuing law, evidence and transition dependency |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary income labels versus controlled resources; universal cash or collective assets can conceal support/claim offsets and acquisition costs | Emergency resource access stays distinct; [IC](../../country/C05/09-income-capital-and-democratic-investment.md) prefers universal ordinary provision plus extra needs while retaining I05 through funded lawful replacement; plural paid ownership replaces a blanket leveraged mutual default | Actual income/tax, accrued basic/earned/survivor, support, property/credit and data law need claim-level matching, sources and authorized transition; no automatic rewrite, sale or guarantee |
| Sector service rules can each count an offer as delivered even when the combined schedule is unusable | Effective access and voluntary joint resolution selected; sector authorities retain duties | [Social-provision review](../../2026-10-social-provision-review/analysis.md) is research; entitlements, provider terms, local/national budgets and privacy rules require joint legal migration |
| Health, school and care manuscripts specify sector rights without an assured provider-of-last-resort capacity or separated purchaser/provider/reviewer chain | Public sector-specific delivery backbone and regulated additional providers selected conditionally | [Sector institution review](../../2026-10-core-institutions/health-education-care.md) is research; financing, employment, provider closure, records and actual independent safety and rights review require legal and operational migration |
| A general health/education/care guarantee left content and resource quantities undefined; labels can double-book shared work or conceal due-period gaps | [S01–S16](../../country/C07/13-public-service-entitlements.md) specifies candidate content/offers and preserves distinct receivers, agency and review; joint outcomes count actual resources once, with skill/period checks | [Service decision](../../2026-10-service-entitlements-and-workload/decision-record.md) is research: new ages/hours/clocks and earlier benefit/professional/education/child/privacy law require explicit authorized migration, stronger-right continuity, safe standards and actual sources. Old gross headcount reviews do not approve this schedule |
| Palliative care is named as a health benefit while patient wishes, medicine access, death verification, body care and a survivor's first support remain disconnected | C07 gives an accepting relief route; C08 separates present and advance choices, clinical death, registry, investigation, rites and interim survivor claims; C06 funds the handoffs without a new assisted-dying power | [End-of-life decision](../../2026-10-end-of-life/decision-record.md) is research; current consent, controlled-medicine, disability, death, donation, body-care, estate, pension and child-support law plus actual staff, sites, records and affected choice require reconciliation |
| Clinical prevention language and emergency restriction law leave ordinary population monitoring, local outreach, warning, privacy and funding without one accountable noncoercive path | C07 assigns local prevention and national technical support, C06 funds routine and surge capacity, while C10 retains any compulsory powers under separate law | [Public-health decision](../../2026-10-public-health/decision-record.md) is research; older health-act isolation clauses, patient and source-data rights, local and sector law, actual international duties, staff, supplies and trustworthy access require lawful reconciliation |
| The older C07 health clause permits individualized mental-health restriction or treatment without consent but does not establish a voluntary crisis receiver, substance-use and overdose pathway, or a boundary against diagnosis-based detention | C07 specifies voluntary receiving care and C08's working reference removes diagnosis-specific civil commitment and forced treatment; only a general, conduct-specific immediate rescue route survives with strict necessity and review | [Mental-health decision](../../2026-10-mental-health-and-crisis/decision-record.md) is research, not a release or statutory amendment; existing orders, medical consent, disability, child, drug, police, emergency, housing and privacy law, plus staff and independent remedies require prospective lawful reconciliation |
| C08/01 named confidential help and temporary protection for domestic harm but left night access, clinical/forensic choice, accepted shelter and payments, order execution and respondent review disconnected | C07 supplies private first receipt and continuing material support; C08 separates short civil protection, criminal guilt and child or status cases; C06 funds the receiving and enforcement chain | [Interpersonal-safety decision](../../2026-10-interpersonal-safety/decision-record.md) is research; existing criminal, family, child, housing, employment, evidence, data and migration law, orders, cases, staff and accrued claims need lawful reconciliation |
| C06 resolves bank failure and public liquidity but leaves ordinary credit, linked nonbanks, household over-indebtedness and financial gatekeeping without one authority/rights path | Regulated plural intermediation, distinct payment claims, cross-sector risk tools, consumer conduct review and lawful debt workout chosen; public investment and extraordinary guarantees remain elected fiscal choices | [Credit-and-finance decision](../../2026-10-credit-and-finance/decision-record.md) is research; existing finance law, balance sheets, supervisors, borrower/creditor claims, court capacity, controller independence and real outcomes are unknown |
| Mixed-economy and automation promises lacked an explicit productive path; later reserve posts omitted complete instruction, private displacement and renewal | [N01–N12](../../country/C05/07-production-learning-and-capacity.md) retains plural enterprise and bounded public mandates with paid learners/instructors, actual net gains, acceptance and funded continuity; no central AI production mandate | [Capability decision](../../2026-10-productive-capability/decision-record.md) is research; labor/credit/procurement/qualification/source law, real people, workload, prices, physical supply, private displacement, future renewal and affected choice need reconciliation. Old fiscal reviews do not approve the changed state |
| Older labor schedule proposes numeric hours/rest/leave, while current social-floor design did not choose how people gain usable time or care leave | Layered enacted hours/rest/scheduling floor, independent bargaining and portable pooled care-leave income selected without ratifying old numbers | [Time/care review](../../2026-10-democracy-and-time/time-and-care.md) is research; work.labor-protection Article 2, insurance and pension clauses, small firms, self-employment, care capacity and actual replacement staff need fresh legal/cost review |
| Old retirement architecture left parameters and claim boundaries unspecified; a later closed-legacy cash rival stops new accrual and could be mistaken for savings under that architecture | [Y01–Y16](../../country/C07/04-retirement-security.md) retains ongoing pooled accrual and individual basic protection, now with explicit candidate age/base/credit/survivor/reserve and prospective rules; closed-legacy remains a materially different alternative | [Retirement decision](../../2026-10-retirement-claims-and-finance/decision-record.md) preserves prior law and accrued rights; normative parameters need fresh legitimacy/calibration, actual histories, legacy/survivor costs, reliable price/survival sources and funded migration. Old financial reviews do not transfer |
| Housing support and land/transport plans do not name daily capacity commissioners, funded maintenance or a lawful receiver when homes or routes fail | Local elected housing and mobility responsibility under a national floor, with plural provision and demonstrated public residual capacity | [Housing/mobility comparison](../../2026-10-housing-and-mobility/analysis.md) is research; titles, tenancies, deposits, safety and service clauses, local/national funding, transport concessions, workers and actual assets require legal and operational reconciliation |
| A growing set of separate protectors can consume the same staff and resident time, while a single intake or shared vendor can control every remedy | Accountable receipt with several direct routes; independent conflicting decisions; proportionate review; shareable support with alternate operation; institutional load counted in C06 | [Institutional-usability decision](../../2026-10-institutional-load/decision-record.md) is a research selection, not law; claimant journeys, non-users, actual staff and case volumes, source records, independent controllers, privacy and lawful transition remain unverified |
| C05's labor protections, C06's broad taxes and C07's pensions could each assume the other maintains income or contributions during a large AI-driven shift | Employment-independent basic protection and one observed income, contribution, tax, service and ownership account with an elected comparison of coherent responses; no automatic AI tax or benefit cut | [Automation-settlement decision](../../2026-10-automation-settlement/decision-record.md) is research; actual adoption, firms, prices, tax collection, workers, nonworkers, public returns and rights-preserving transition are unknown |
| Plural ownership and generic consumer rights can coexist with one controller of jobs, housing, payment and civic reach, while scattered safety notices fail to remove harmful goods | A statutory markets case route examines real exit and common control; sector product inspectors and courts connect traceable recall to buyer repair under a common floor | [Market-power decision](../../2026-10-market-power/decision-record.md) is research; existing merger, product, contract and public-enterprise law, agency appointments, pending cases, suppliers, workers and funded successors require lawful reconciliation |
| Earlier private-credit/civil routes name insolvency without exact estate/exemption, priority, repayment/discharge or enterprise-confirmation rules | Candidate U01–U12 specifies actual needs/estate, fair notice/stay, priorities, bounded ordinary fresh start, exceptional support/harm claims, guarantors, viable enterprise rescue and independent delivery | [Completion/insolvency decision](../../2026-10-completion-and-private-insolvency/decision-record.md) is research; inherited collateral/claim migration, credit/victim/worker effects, court/officer funding, cross-border law and actual recovery require legitimate review; no write-off is new cash |

| Blanket new S/Y start dates are unsupported by the staged productive/service references | [CP03–CP04](../../country/C06/28/02-dated-commitments-and-equality.md) replaces them with exact elected cohorts, source/interim/equality protection and contribution/accrual matching | Actual adopting dates, full costs and qualified receiving remain unknown; no ministerial readiness veto, erasure of existing dates or reclassification of overdue claims |
