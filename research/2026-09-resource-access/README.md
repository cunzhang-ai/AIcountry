# Emergency help while facts are uncertain

Drafted 27 September; checked 28 September 2026 · Proposed research · Not enacted

The central choice is **who bears uncertainty while an essential need is due**.
A reimbursement promise cannot finance a purchase for someone unable to advance
the money. Provisional assistance can address that barrier but exposes public funds
to mistakes. An unconditional payment changes the entitlement itself and may avoid
intrusive means tests; its coverage and resource demands need their own assessment.

This continuation of [housing continuity](../2026-09-housing-continuity/README.md)
compares those choices without assuming that better administration is the only answer.

- [Candidate rules](access-candidate.md): resource access, provisional payment,
  duplicate claims, correction, subsistence and democratic control.
- [Comparison and error account](comparison.md): three packages, four invented
  applicants, adverse cases and an explicit shared appropriation.
- [Decision record](decision-record.md): research selection, dissent, compatibility,
  source limits and the next bounded inquiry.
- [Arithmetic](comparison-inputs.json), [source manifest](source-manifest.json)
  and [validation](validation.json).

The narrow research conclusion rejects reimbursement as the sole emergency route
for people unable to advance funds. It does not establish provisional means testing
as superior to an unconditional floor. No real payments, participation, independent
review or operational readiness are claimed.
