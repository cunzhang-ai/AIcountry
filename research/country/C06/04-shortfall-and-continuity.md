# C06. Shortfall, continuity and legal limits

*Part of [C06 public finance](../C06-public-finance.md); integrated design 56 research, not enacted law.*

## Shortfall, continuity and legal limits

If a shortfall threatens delivery, responsible agencies disclose it before interruption
and use only existing lawful contingency powers. An ordinary cabinet must present
revenue, borrowing and reallocation alternatives with their distribution and
service effects. During [government formation](../C03/06-government-formation-and-caretaker.md),
a caretaker supplies factual options while elected members can introduce
proposals; the Assembly
decides, subject to rights and independent remedies. Treasury executes lawful decisions
and cannot quietly rewrite eligibility. New expansion depends on funded delivery
capacity, while existing claims survive shortage and institutional change. A missing
resource cannot be created by a court order, and does not extinguish the right or excuse
arbitrary denial. Neither uniform cuts nor compulsory family labor is the default.
An enacted but future-start service must name its start condition, interim protection,
responsible authority and public review date. A minister cannot hold it indefinitely
under an undefined “readiness” label; a missed legal due date is reported as an
unfulfilled duty unless valid law changes it. A new discretionary project may be
prepared in a bounded phase when full delivery capacity is absent, but must not
be reported as an operating guarantee. Existing rights, court orders and accrued
claims retain their separate legal force regardless of a favorable forecast.

Use the [joint schedule](18-joint-commitment-and-delivery-schedule.md) to
attribute each duty to an actual baseline or increment and test all claim
routes in the same shortage. Election, opposition, legal-aid, court, evidence
and order-execution continuity need qualified capacity alongside essential
services. Neither an annual cash balance nor a repeated generic reserve
establishes that these protections survive the same failure.

The proposed [legal settlement](../../AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION.md) resolves the **day-91
authority gap** by separating a preadopted standing schedule of essential obligations
from a temporary 90-day bridge for other existing operations. At expiry, standing
rights and their defined funding authority continue; the bridge does not. The
Assembly can amend the standing schedule through normal law, subject to rights.
This is a candidate legal mechanism. It does not establish available cash, staffing
or an actual day-91 service path in a real jurisdiction; unpaid claims remain visible.

A cash shortfall differs from a simultaneous **physical** shortage. The
[cross-system scarcity rule](05-cross-system-scarcity.md) bounds first-hour
allocation under prior law and returns an unprovided material choice to the
Assembly. Neither the temporary budget bridge nor a bank-liquidity power
authorizes rationing water, clinical care or food by executive preference.
If debt service itself threatens due claims and essential imports, the
[public-debt rule](06-sovereign-debt-and-distress.md) requires a separate
diagnosis and elected choice; a routine cash bridge does not settle or erase
creditor claims.
For a [hard-to-reverse commitment](../C09/04-future-options-and-irreversibility.md),
the common account also carries maintenance, restoration and failure exposure
beyond the first budget term. A distant due date does not make a real future
obligation free, and a promised but uncollectible private bond is not funding.
The [communications and logistics account](../C09/05-communications-and-logistics.md)
also prices basic access, spare paths, postal notices, cold-chain delivery,
drivers, fuel, power and actual provider-of-last-resort capacity. One alternate
generator or carrier cannot back several simultaneous guarantees at once.

[BLC01–BLC05](66-budget-lapse-and-paid-continuity.md) gives the proposed
day-91 schedule a dated payment and independent execution test. An express
separate closeout can pay eligible accrued bridge debts from their named
source. The 90-day operating authority still ends.
