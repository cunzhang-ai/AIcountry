# Four years of protected service and a real revenue choice

*Design460 · Danish accounting observation and a separate constructed decision.*

## What the observed Danish accounts show

The December 2025 Danish Economic Survey listed pension yield taxation
at DKK 12.9 billion in 2023 and 43.1 billion in 2024. Its 2025 figure
was 36.0 billion in that vintage. Statistics Denmark's June 2026
general government release reported DKK 47.0 billion for 2025.
That later release reported net lending of 90.9 billion in 2025
and 123.5 billion in 2024. Preserve the two vintages. Do not treat
the difference in the tax figures as a verified same-scope cash
forecast error without reconciling their basis and dates. The
national accounting figures identify neither an available balance
nor a guarantee of recurring collection. No amount is imported
into the example. [Economic Survey, table B.19](https://en.oem.dk/media/44tfpuug/economic-survey_december-2025_web.pdf)
· [Statistics Denmark, June release](https://www.dst.dk/da/Statistik/udgivelser/NytHtml?cid=52178).

## A common four-year schedule

Amounts are authored units. The persistent general-account gap is
6 each year. It covers 3 in necessary local care, 2 in a lawful
general transfer backing earned payments, and 1 in independently
operated elections, courts and execution. Other costs and receipts
are assumed unchanged and paid for this scope. The earned payer
has its own qualified source for the rest of its due payments.
Consolidation removes both sides of the 2-unit transfer, while
the due pension payment remains external. No earned assets or
independent institution's budget supplies new financing.

In year one, all durable instruments take effect prospectively
from year two. One lawfully approved and settled 6-unit bridge
covers year one, including 4 needed before the first deadline and
2 at the later deadline. Its terms require 3 principal plus 1
interest in each of years two and three. A separate verified
finite source of 3 per year in those windows is dedicated to
principal. In this fixture it comes from separately appropriating the settled
receipts already assigned to an unbound finite capital project
with no accrued claims, after that project is cancelled. It competes with that
project and ends with it. An unauthorized deferral or a cancelled
invoice does not establish the source.

| Gap in standing ordinary service and earned backing | 6 | 6 | 6 | 6 |

| Principal due | 0 | 3 | 3 | 0 |

## Exact packages on the same protection and deadlines

| Source choice from year two | Central / weaker recurring net receipts | Result after bridge interest in years 2 and 3 | Result in year 4 |
|---|---:|---|---|
| R. Existing sources and the finite bridge | 0 / 0 | Short 7 in either path | Short 6 |
| V. One volatile owner-income source | 8 / 4 | Central room 1; weaker short 3 | Central room 2; weaker short 2 |
| M. Mixed realized owner-income reform and progressive ordinary source | 8 / 7 | Central room 1; weaker balance 0 | Central room 2; weaker room 1 |
| P. Broader progressive ordinary personal source alone | 8 / 7 | Central room 1; weaker balance 0 | Central room 2; weaker room 1 |
| H. Smaller ordinary increase and qualified spending reprioritization | 8 / 7 | Same cash result if the stated release occurs | Same cash result if the saving lasts |

In M, the proposed owner-income component nets 5 centrally from
7 gross collections less 1 of refunds and 1 of administration.
The weaker collection of 6 nets 4 with the same deductions.
The ordinary progressive component nets 3 in either case after
its own deductions. P collects 10 centrally and 9 in the weaker
path, less 2 for relief and administration. The baseline company
tax stays outside the incremental personal layer. The constructed
scope of M is after company tax and applies only to realized
owner income. The ordinary source changes upper-range personal
income liability prospectively and keeps the declared low-income
and essential-consumption protection. Incidence may extend to
other people through wages, prices, behavior or investment.
The fixture does not estimate those effects. H combines 6 central
or 5 weaker net collections with 2 recurring units from ending a
discretionary unbound office-services purchase. In the fixture the
two units are separate from the capital project and all payroll,
ordinary services and independent review remain fully staffed. H
would limit tax increases but removes a genuine service improvement
for public staff. It qualifies only while the actual net release
and unchanged protected coverage are verified.

M and P are real alternatives on these assumptions. In the
constructed elected choice, select M to spread the increase
across differently exposed source bases and limit the direct
increment on ordinary personal income. The assumed weaker path
already lowers owner-income collection while holding the ordinary
increment. A common deterioration would change the result.
P and H remain eligible for separate votes. P could be preferable
with stronger collection evidence and H if that spending is less
valuable than the forgone tax relief. If the common downturn removes
1 additional recurring unit from M, the weaker years two and three
again fall short by 1. That persistent gap needs a fresh source or
lawful reprioritization. A timing reserve could pay one deadline
but cannot finance both years. V is insufficient
under its stated downside. R protects only the bridge year unless
a qualified additional source or a lawful funded repair is adopted.

## A surplus can still miss the first payment

In the weaker M year two, 4 of the 7 net receipts are needed by
the early deadline for continuing work, earned backing and
independent execution. An on-time path supplies 4 early and 3
later. If 1 moves later, the early shortfall is 1, even if all 7
arrive before year end. The dedicated principal funds cannot be
used. A separately appropriated 1-unit timing reserve can protect
the first call. If the delayed unit arrives, restore the reserve
after verified settlement. If it never arrives, the reserve covers
the current outlay but remains depleted. A second such failure
before replenishment exposes a 1-unit gap and returns to elected
correction. A reserve draw cannot replace the recurring source.

The illustrative votes therefore address the first bridge, the
recurring source, the separate principal schedule and the exact
continuity reserve. Keep a lawful narrower release and ordinary
repair available. If actual care staffing, independent execution
or receipt fails, keep the original duties and remedies and
report the affected deadline. None of the constructed revenues
qualifies a national tax rate, a full country budget or better lives.

## Separate debt-term comparison

For a separate constructed portfolio, assume 20 of opening debt
refinances at par in year one, with 3 of opening cash and a minimum
of 1. The recurring room after protected duties and other costs is 2
in year one and falls to 1 in year two. A one-year floating instrument
has a first-year coupon of 0.8. The staggered fixed option costs 1.2,
with 10 due in year three and 10 in year four. This is a separate
portfolio comparison. Its amounts do not enter the source table above.

In year two, the short coupon resets to 2 before maturity and only
15 of the 20 principal can be refinanced. Cash falls from 4.2 to −1.8,
a gap of 2.8 including the minimum. The staggered option falls from
3.8 to 3.6 without a principal call. Its recurring interest still
exceeds the new recurring room by 0.2. If none of its first 10
refinances in year three, and the same recurring conditions hold,
cash falls to −6.6. Prefer the staggered option in this bounded test
with a separately qualified later source. The short option saves 0.4
in year one and remains a rival with independently funded rollover
protection. Compare actual cost, terms and concentrations before
committing. [IMF and World Bank analytical manual](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/tnm/issues/2019/05/14/medium-term-debt-management-strategy-analytical-tool-manual-46897).
