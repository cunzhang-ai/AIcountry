# Prevention across public budgets

[FB31–FB35](../../country/C06/62/06-prevention-across-public-budgets.md)
gives elected authorities a way to pay for a defined preventive offer
when the benefits occur in several budgets. It keeps usable capacity and
lawful cash separate and maintains the relevant service and review duties.

- [Observed Housing First analysis](../../2026-11-prevention-across-public-budgets/sources.md)
  explains where the estimated cost offsets arose and what they can support.
- [Authored first-budget comparison](02-authored-housing-choice.md)
  tests funded ordinary repair, a narrower cohort and a larger cohort with
  the same duties and a missing contribution.
- [Decision and migration](../../2026-11-prevention-across-public-budgets/decision-record.md)
  states the institutional choice, alternatives and open qualifications.

This is a country-design selection. Actual authority, allocation, access
and improved outcomes remain separate.
