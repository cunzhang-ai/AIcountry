# Design311 decision on recurring places and common services

## Baseline and requested choice

Pin Design310, XR01–XR10, MF09–MF13 and the working method. A person
can divide ordinary time across local boundaries without making a
permanent move. Select how to keep common care and access usable
over repeated windows and a late change. The project assistant
authors this proposal. Competent legislatures and service authorities
hold adoption, funding and operational powers.

## Comparison and objections

A single-address default is administratively clear and can be
adequate where actual service stays local. It can leave predictable
needs uncovered across areas. The separate temporary service
comparator shows why clinical receipt and transport require distinct
acceptance. One-off booking may be better for infrequent changes.
A standing schedule with funded host acceptance better fits the
specified repeated pattern. A national direct provider is a genuine
rival where consolidation reduces coordination burdens and preserves
resident control. National funding cannot replace local staffing or
professional judgment.

Costs include retaining routes, overlap and changes. A reserved
provider may deserve payment even without delivery. Count the
payment, service and any actual breach separately. A standing plan
can become a travel gate or intrusive record. Retain safe contacts,
non-digital changes, actual consent and access for people without
an address or a standing plan. Evidence of one service does not
establish receipt of another.

## Selection, continuity and next decision

Select XR11–XR15 and keep separate legal owners for each award.
Existing rights, contracts, ordinary local services, original dates
and appeals transfer only under their own law with funded successors.
Apply prospectively where the actual source and receiving powers
are enacted. No new funding, approval or constitutional procedure
follows from this record. Retest with one locally accepted repeating
care and travel schedule, including a late change and another
person's simultaneous protected booking. Reopen on actual delivery
loss, unlawful location demands, disputed payment, capacity failure
or a simpler equally usable choice. Actual affected participation,
independent qualified review, lawful adoption, paid readiness and
better lives remain unverified.
