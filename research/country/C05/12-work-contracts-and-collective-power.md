# C05. Work contracts, fair exit and collective power

2 November 2026 · Design 117 · WL01–WL60 research candidate, not enacted law

**Select enforceable work relations based on actual dependence and control,
valid-reason dismissal from entry, independent collective organization and
bounded public wage protection.** Business adjustment remains possible with
actual reasons, fair procedure, accrued claims and safe service continuity.
Neither a private contract, public employer nor AI may erase the common floor.

TW's hours/pay/leave/rest and actual separate source dates continue. AS's higher
28-day assistance notice floor, full defined dues backing, consent and public
receiving survive; WL's general advance is neither a cap on them nor general
insurance of all wages, severance or business losses. Constitutional office
appointment/removal, police/defense command and current software remain distinct.

Numbers are contestable authored choices: three-month probation; 24-month/two-
renewal ordinary fixed-term boundary with narrowly proved exceptions; ordinary
employer notice14/28/56/84 days by service; redundancy two weeks per service year,
up to26; majority recognition without turnout veto; councils from20 workers;
and a limited general-revenue wage advance. No primary source establishes them
as optimal or this package as superior.

The [current comparison](../../settlement/work-law/README.md) and
[decision](../../2026-11-work-contracts-and-collective-power/decision-record.md)
expose employer cost/closure, collective capture, restriction/legacy, source and
execution risks. New public wage advances, inspection/ballots/courts, paid
representation and old/new service overlap need fresh source/workload estimates.
A right, calculation or file check is no accepted job, delivered remedy or good life.
