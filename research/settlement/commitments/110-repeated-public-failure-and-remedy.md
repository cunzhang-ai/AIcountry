# Commitment 110. Enforce the order and address a proved recurring public breach

[SR01–SR12](../../country/C08/30-repeated-public-failure-and-remedy.md)
requires accountable actual delivery, disaggregated shortfall and, where
a material common breach is proved, a finite supervised public repair.
Keep urgent personal relief, notice, distinct authority and original
dates. Verify actual receipt and a working next cycle before reporting
full success.

Prior-law source, fair hearing, elected choices, scope-limited substitute
execution and paid OP/T continue. Actual funded capacity, participation
and improved outcomes remain unresolved. [Comparison](../public-remedy/README.md)
