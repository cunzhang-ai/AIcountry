# Design388 decision. Keep shared public standards replaceable

## Baseline and selection

Design387's DP25–DP28 tests contracted exit, DP38–DP42 limits common
release dependence and LX preserves legal meaning and decisions.
The gap is who can mandate a common interface and how one version
or certification regime can gain practical control across sectors.
Even several suppliers may share one licence, interpreter or approval
channel. File conversion can lose a legal claim without a visible error.

Select [LX17–LX23](../country/C11/12/05-public-standards-and-replaceable-implementation.md).
Require competent adoption, a minimal implementable profile, plural
access and direct independent challenge. Keep original and disputed
meaning, funded ordinary judgment and source permissions. No single
test suite or issuer controls admission to a public right.

## Alternatives and objections

Retain independent bilateral arrangements. They support local fit but
can duplicate costs and obstruct receiving across institutions.
A mandatory central format and implementation can lower integration
costs but concentrates certification and meaning. Select a shared
minimal boundary with qualified equivalent and ordinary routes.
The added transition and fallback cost is real. A narrower ordinary
route can be superior to a second nominally compliant stack.

Open terms can be confused with open data, and old versions can
prolong unsafe dependence. Keep lawful scoped records, finite support
and funded succession without destroying older usable evidence.
Narrow exceptions for actual licensing needs retain expiry and
challenge. Do not force small operators or residents into unnecessary
common systems. Test how a replacement handles unfamiliar meaning.

## Evidence, migration and next action

The [primary comparators](sources.md) describe public interoperability
assessment and open standards policy. The [constructed transfer](../settlement/public-standards-and-replaceable-implementation/01-one-interface-two-real-routes.md)
tests one payment and one dispute across a version change. No
qualified local testing, affected participation or legal review occurred.

Adopt prospectively through the competent rule, procurement or service
authority, as applicable. Fund distinct ordinary receiving and
independent challenge. Preserve initial dates, existing duties, earlier
payments, licence rights, records, pending cases and lawful non-digital
access. A new profile supplies neither substantive legal change nor
permission to activate the current project software.

Next qualify a real interchange between two independently controlled
public services. Include an original record unavailable in the new
format, a current payment, an appeal and loss of a shared dependency.
Price overlap and safe human receiving and hear an affected user.
Reopen on common control, excessive burdens, missing meanings or a
simpler qualified route. Actual authority and results remain open.
