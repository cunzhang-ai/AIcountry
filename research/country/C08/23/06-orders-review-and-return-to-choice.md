# Necessary care: finite authority and return to direct choice

*Canonical UA15–UA16; part of [C08/23](../23-necessary-care-and-unknown-will.md).*

**UA15 — A bounded operative order, not personal management.** State legal
predicates and uncertainties, act/purpose, permitted procedural anesthesia where
necessary, named qualified operator, actual receiving site/shifts, lawful payer,
monitoring, response to opposition, review, endpoint and evidence of received care.
Use 'court-authorized without ascertainable consent', not 'patient consented'.
An order never delegates unspecified later surgery, medication or living decisions.

An initial order lasts at most fourteen elapsed days from first related operative
authorization, or completion/stated earlier end, whichever first. A discrete act
has a named performance window; completion consumes that permission, not a reusable
license. A defined continuing course/arrangement may renew only after fresh
specific review for at most ninety elapsed days per order, before expiry. Check new
signals/events/objections even when previous evidence remains valid in scope.
Related case labels/operators cannot reset the initial window or conceal renewal.

A new invasive act or material change of indication, risk, irreversible consequence,
location, provider conflict or proposed restriction needs fresh prior authorization.
Ordinary clinically equivalent delivery adjustments stay within actual ordered
scope and monitoring; no court form dictates unsafe practice. The professional
must not perform a clinically unjustified intervention simply because permitted.

Before a planned irreversible act or life-support withdrawal, provide the reasoned
order and protected evidence to independent counsel at least twenty-four elapsed
hours before action, unless a separate competent ordinary appellate formation has
actually reviewed the earlier timing and substantive protection before that act,
with counsel's real opportunity to challenge evidence; a scheduling check is insufficient.
The person's/advocate's timely challenge suspends that planned irreversible act
until the appeal court's useful-time consideration and reasoned stay/disposition.
No silence is a waiver. Other appeals do not automatically stay all basic care;
B/E shorter hearing/protection limits remain and UA cannot extend them.

**UA16 — Choice, expiry and actual continuity.** Reassess at every care encounter
and material change; actual supported choice or applicable refusal immediately
ends/narrows the unknown-will premise without provider permission. Stop or adapt
at the earliest clinically safe point, with reasons for any unavoidable delay,
not a right to finish an unwanted course. Offer correction, explanation and
accounting; no retroactive consent or agreement is manufactured.

Expiry or failed renewal ends UA order-based intrusion, subject only to the
express dependent-continuity source below, not statutory basic care,
other actual consent/authority or genuinely separate emergency duties. No silent
renewal, label change or temporary family 'consent' fills a missing court. Arrange
safe nonintrusive continuity before expiry and report an unmet essential claim;
do not abruptly withdraw another independently lawful treatment merely because
an administrative form lapsed. State exactly which authority/duty continues.
For lawfully begun treatment whose interruption at expiry would itself create
immediate risk of death/serious injury, the statute separately requires the
minimum existing function-preserving continuation, while will remains unknown
and no applicable refusal/credible opposition bars it. This includes a necessary
safe taper/recovery, not renewed elective treatment, new escalation, surgery,
restraint or an open-ended course. It is not consent or renewal of the old order.
Seek renewal before expiry; on failure request urgent court protection immediately
and within six elapsed hours, sooner if needed. Independent consideration is due
within twenty-four elapsed hours and operative resolution within seventy-two,
both from first actual resort to this separate dependent-continuity source,
with earlier useful time and E13's receipt-based limit still controlling.
Record failure, actual support and independent clinical/advocate checks at least
daily; pursue a lawful alternate court/receiver. After missed review deadlines,
only this minimum independently justified continuity persists until lawful review,
actual choice or safe nondependent exit; no wider authority appears. There is no
expiry-triggered unplugging or unsafe abandonment. This deliberate no-cliff rule
can prolong unconsented intervention if review fails; it is a serious retained
cost and visible breach, not timely adjudication or proven protection. Repeated
finite orders remain challengeable acts, never permanent incapacity.
