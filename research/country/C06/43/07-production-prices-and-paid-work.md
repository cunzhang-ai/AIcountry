# SD25–SD28: the same production choice changes prices, work and protection

*Part of [C06/43](../43-country-source-and-delivery.md); design114 research selection.*

**SD25 — A national source needs its actual counterpart.** Retain plural voluntary
production under IC/TW/MP/NB, not administrative jobs or a compulsory consumption
plan. A producer record names quantity/quality/time, actual customer/consideration,
workers and usable paid hours, current/imported inputs and their suppliers,
capital/title/renewal, financing, taxes, delivery and receipt separately. Public
procurement needs prior public payment; it is not an independent foreign source.
A wage is income when actually received; its unreceived contractual claim persists.
Gross sales through a supply chain are not all final demand/value added. Unknown
buyer, skill, tariff, currency or legal meaning remains a named unresolved item,
not a balancing exporter, zero expense or automatic model permission.

The [114 comparison](../../../settlement/country-delivery/07-production-prices-and-financial-claims.md)
now crosswalks all24 named108 domestic producer offers, three traded books and
A/C1 person/public obligations. It retains37 original function/22-package quotes
at their actual earlier scope,108 replacement assets and later unpriced current
duties. It does not price all current PC/EL/LR/NB increments or create a national
GDP/deficit. The108 full-cohort assignment is not complete workforce closure:
external QA/distribution/premises costs need their own qualified paid suppliers;
a purchase amount or an already assigned general professional is not extra work.

**SD26 — Compare a production alternative on both sides of the household.**
The same2,447,500 retail items may use108's local garment/device assembly plus
imports, or retain the original finished-import quantity. The import-retaining
branch forgoes2,300 prospective maker jobs/51m pay and18m proposed new assets,
increases covered recurring foreign quotes10.6m and removes6.55GWh gross maker
meter load. It neither cancels an actual old contract/asset/debt nor proves a
net national demand/grid change. Lower prices and more jobs are distinct gains;
no forced work, buy-local rule or free-income replacement is selected.

The same actual person ordinals/tenure are preserved. Recompute received wages,
PA/Y, ordinary needs/I05/U and payer cash when a changed supply mix changes them;
no cheap-import comparison retaining wages from the removed producer, and no
job-creation comparison holding obsolete consumer prices. Existing earned wages,
pensions, support, creditor/worker and useful-time duties survive a prospective
comparison. A different real transition needs actual paid receiving/retraining/
settlement, not a retrospective hypothetical unemployment finding.

**SD27 — A price basket is evidence, not a sovereign score.** Keep the lawful
EV/Y/I price methods, original components, quantities/access/quality, distribution,
replacements and uncertainty. Actual necessary housing, public received food and
other provided components count once. Retail prices do not by themselves define
the whole ordinary basket, general CPI or each person's additional needs. Missing
composition/indexation is an unquantified duty, not zero inflation or a lower
claim. Pay undisputed/provisional protection and useful-time remedy under I/E;
competent elected law alone changes statutory bases/protections.

114 explicitly studies an import-reference13248-CU adult basket and a matched
retail-price addition spread over92,000 adult equivalents (child factor.6).
That is an authored distribution assumption, not discovered old basket contents
or a new benefit/index law. Its January pension sensitivity explicitly assumes
general CPI equals the studied B ratio and retains Y03's nominal floor; actual
CPI/old cohorts remain unverified. Do not present a frozen earned-pension bill
as price-consistent S finance. Compare same-bundle remaining cash separately
from nominal cash, employment, agency and wider lived outcomes; no one scalar
establishes superiority or justifies weaker democratic control.

**SD28 — Tax flows need the whole valid invoice scope.** Apply NF to each actual
legal unit/performance, paid output, paid eligible input, border and due event.
Within a chain, supplier output and buyer input credit cancel only once; a
registered importer's actual border VAT credits, and eligible zero-rate export
inputs can create an early Treasury refund. Imported business-service reverse
charge output/input is not physical-border cash twice. Retained company profit
is not a shareholder distribution, current assessment is not next-April receipt,
and unrelated profitable firms do not fund a loss firm by aggregation.

Do not carry108's all-water-zero prototype into a yield or invoice: NF10's
unflavored drinking-water predicate does not automatically cover every reclaimed,
farm/industrial supply. Actual product/delivery/credit/registration evidence
controls; missing allocation means unknown, no backdated levy or automatic user
charge. No blanket10% VAT on all public purchasing, tax on own occupation or
company profit inferred from a public budget. Revenue/Customs careers, TX full
merits/refund payer and ordinary courts retain their exact powers and dates;
a study's register neither adopts a rule nor proves collection.
