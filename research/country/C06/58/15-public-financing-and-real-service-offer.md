# WF65–WF68. Free-at-use financing and additional services are different changes

Design181 ·4November2026 · Whole-offer accounting interpretation, no new law

**WF65 — Identify the existing service before adding a production shock.**
Retain the selected service-first/plural country, optional care, clinical and
ordinary support, TW36/32, CL/Y and democratic/legal duties. For a free-at-use
change distinguish who finances actual existing episodes from new quantity,
quality, access, staff/space and paid transition. A higher government final-
consumption entry alone is no new physical service. A cheaper household bill
alone is neither unchanged access nor a funded provider. This is a scoped
interpretation of existing source duties, not a new certificate or budget veto.

The fixed-service diagnostic holds the same actual episode/provider/quality,
input recipe and purchaser-price/tax treatment. For an eligible nonmarket
public service with household payment F, retain public output and its costs,
reduce public sales F, increase government consumption F and reduce household
final expenditure F. Fiscal revenue/net lending fall F absent a compatible
source; fiscal expenditure does not rise merely from this reclassification.
For a market provider's same existing episode now bought by government and
supplied to the household in kind, public market-purchase expense and government
consumption rise F, household expenditure falls F; public output/sales need not
change. Provider ownership/output do not change because the payer does. These are
S.13 nonmarket versus purchased-market accounting routes, not public/private
ownership camps; a public corporation outside S.13 can supply market services.

These are conditional same-episode mechanisms, not findings that real fee
abolition has zero administrative cost, demand response or new qualified cover.
Price/VAT/duty, provider classification, contracts, private/foreign title and
actual scope need separate matching. Do not call all public sales household
fees, convert municipal sales into an identified household bill, or manufacture
matched private multipliers from aggregate import shares/metadata labels.

**WF66 — Preserve the whole country and count the fiscal loss once.** The
[finite comparison](../../../settlement/financing-production/README.md) retains
all seven original Denmark2023 final-use classes, fiscal/production boundaries
and old obligations. An authored1bnDKK example gives government consumption+1
and household consumption−1, total final use unchanged. Either route reduces
net lending1 absent other changes, through different revenue/expense entries.
No new direct/upstream worker or import requirement follows solely from the
fixed-service financing switch. That zero is an assumption about this margin,
not proof of spare capacity or a real zero-cost national programme.

An expressly authored matched personal-tax counterpart can restore that partial
fiscal position; it is no chosen rate, observed receipt, balancing levy or cash
source. Fee relief minus actual tax and any actual compatible I05/other-support
change determines household incidence. Same-payer aggregate neutrality says
nothing about gains/losses among users, nonusers, employers or future people.
I05 matches actual avoided expense/received support only within its real ordinary
component/credit law; a clinical/school price creates no ordinary credit. Preserve unrelated
needs and unreceived claims; no automatic gross-fee deduction or backpay clawback.
Cash/title/currency/due dates still require actual source and paid receiving.

**WF67 — New use, wage/time cost and replacement need their own work.** The
unchanged177 hospital/care/nonlevel-learning/new-software5bn vector remains
an authored additional-production comparison, not a price for every publicly
financed service. Its modelled6,233 direct-plus-upstream wage-FTE equivalents
and0.819bn import-content lens cannot be attached to fee abolition or paid CL
cash as if a new real-output vector. Conversely, unchanged existing episodes
cannot discharge genuinely new patients, unmet needs, longer access, safer
quality, staff learning, shorter hours or CL replacement. Their actual tasks,
qualified peaks, prices, capital/foreign inputs and foregone work remain due.

Direct cash replacement, public purchase of existing market service, reduced
public fee revenue, additional service production and capital acquisition are
different changes. Actual provider/regulatory/administrative/appeal work also
needs source. Do not add an upstream invoice twice, infer available people
from model jobs, or count a higher cash wage as proportionally more real care.
CL/TW bank/cover, Y backing, S/NC/AS and paid independent remedies retain their
actual grounds; no forced family work, assumed nursery saving or AI dividend.

**WF68 — Retain guarantees, compare real financing rivals.** Retain selected
free-at-use essential access within its actual scope, plural qualified delivery
and actual stronger rights. Compare retained fees with targeted help, same-
service public financing and additional/differently organized public provision
with their equity/privacy/uptake/source/ownership costs. None is a universally
equal substitute for protected time, bodily agency or clinical need. No genuine
whole rival must copy every unadopted parameter; actual common fundamental
protections and due/accrued claims continue under CS14 and valid law.
