15 October 2026 · Design86 · QE01–QE12 research selection, not a budget or law

choice**. Replace reliance on historical bid shares with the first joined,
authored event/task/input/asset benchmark. Do not call it a full qualified
estimate: all37 functions have named costs, but important known duties and
supplier chains remain unpriced. Arithmetic cannot supply a country.

workers, missing supplier capital, cash transfers and impossible peaks. Actual
funding, skill, ecology, patient safety, independent review, public authority,
receipt and superiority remain unknown. Existing rights, original procedures,
source-law conflicts, frozen research editions and the single old-scope SF
standby survive; no old fund or bid is requalified by this estimate.
