# EV05–EV08: continuity without an acting ruler

**EV05 — Failed appointment stays failed.** Repeat a public ballot at least every
30days while a competent Assembly can sit; reopen applications after two failed
ballots under the same rules. No simple-majority fallback, executive appointment or
renewed incumbent term follows. Ordinary courts can correct unlawful exclusion or
withholding and grant bounded interim relief, not pick a preferred chief or deem
qualification/appointment from silence. Inability to decide remains public failure.

**EV06 — Bounded career continuity.** The chief appoints a qualified career deputy
through C04 open competition run by Assembly recruitment staff before any vacancy.
During vacancy/judicial suspension the deputy has at most180elapsed days for
statutory preservation, existing-series release, necessary error correction
within the registered existing method and routine funded maintenance only.
From day one, no new material policy/method, enlarged powers or discretionary
senior appointments; relabeling the same absence cannot reset the limit.
Thereafter no one holds acting-chief powers. Designated career custodians/
statisticians retain only their own lawful routine duties under the same limits.
If no qualified unconflicted deputy exists, including first creation/death
before appointment, those officers continue their own duties immediately; no
acting chief is invented. Chief-reserved functions remain unperformed, reported
with their consequences; Assembly appointment duties continue. No alternate
may claim chief powers by renaming an office or treating an absence as assent.

**EV07 — Removal has legal grounds and external judgment.** One fifth of the full
Assembly, an existing competent integrity investigator, or a person with ordinary
standing may seek removal in ordinary court for proved serious misconduct,
falsification/destruction, persistent material statutory breach, or inability after
reasonable accommodation. Unwelcome results/lawful dissent are no ground. Notice,
defense, independent assignment/recusal and appeal apply; urgent judicial suspension
uses B17, not a minister's allegation. The court removes only for lawfully proved
grounds, never appoints a replacement or chooses the desired statistical result.
Removal takes effect after the appeal period expires unused or one ordinary appeal
is decided; competent further review/stays remain. Interim source protection uses
EV06. Prepare succession without creating a second chief during a pending removal.

**EV08 — Fund duties, not political obedience.** Assembly appropriates staff,
fieldwork, privacy, accessible releases, archives and independent correction, with
a bounded standing line for indispensable scheduled duties under NP. Treasury pays
lawful dues; neither the chief nor a court appropriates unlimited funds. Cuts need
public incidence/reasons and lawful prospective treatment of duties. Donor/vendor
money cannot buy official methods/releases. Missing capacity is disclosed; it grants
no tax, secrecy, election delay or power to cancel an existing entitlement.
