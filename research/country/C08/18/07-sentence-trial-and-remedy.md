# Finite punishment, meaningful defense and received restoration

*G11–G12; part of [C08/18](../18-international-crimes-and-accountability.md).*

**G11 — Defined national ceilings.** Completed G02–G05 offenses have a
25-year adult custodial ceiling. G07 causal command liability has at most 25
where actual knowledge is proved; 15 for a civilian's conscious disregard;
ten where military knowledge rests only on the express should-have-known
standard. Distinguish those proved fault levels; no higher branch by rank.
Direct public genocide incitement has seven; the separate reporting breach and
G08 national 15–17 recruitment offense five. Intentional attempt at a main
offense has at most half its completed maximum. These are limits, not
mandatory terms; compare least restrictive proportionate lawful responses
under P16, with harm/fault and actual alternatives, not wealth or political demand.

One factual course/campaign, including overlapping K/G labels, cannot exceed
25 years; deliberately splitting known related counts cannot reset it.
Genuinely later independent crimes require fresh lawful proof; prediction,
diagnosis, refusal of optional treatment or absent housing never extends a
finished sentence. Credit all same-case actual custody, relabelled holds,
foreign restrictions and payments, without invented money-to-prison conversion.
No death, torture, corporal punishment, irreducible life or indefinite
preventive detention. P18 humane custody/inspection and P19 half-term release
review under RL, actual expiry/reception and Q11 record protections apply
to national G sentences. Actual foreign and tribunal judgments have their
own enforcement instruments. [RL01](../28/01-review-and-release.md)

Main G02–G06 offenses and causal command responsibility have no prosecution
time bar; still prove the historically applicable offense fairly. The separate
five-year duty/recruitment offenses use Q12's ordinary six-year candidate
period and applicable tolling. New national extensions are prospective;
no research edition revives an expired lawful bar. A new account cannot
erase source law or pretend every historic case is now lawfully chargeable.

G crimes here concern natural persons. Existing K corporate liability,
civil restoration, lawful asset recovery and specific correction remain;
no automatic confiscation of families, a corporation or all state resources.
Victim restitution and support are not fines, mandatory reconciliation or
proof that the same assets can be recovered twice. No sanction/recovery
forecast pays the institution pursuing it.

**G12 — Ordinary independent civilian justice, with actual specialization.**
Use the existing human civilian criminal court, published allocation/recusal,
independent prosecution and funded defense, with qualified specialist teams
where necessary. No victor's commission, special executive judge, military
command or AI issues guilt/sentence. Outside-command investigation, alternate
conflict assignment and source access apply even to the supposed protectors.
Appointment, removal, budget, custody, vendors and enforcement need actual
independence, not merely different institutional names.

Preserve original orders/logs, sites/material evidence, lawful clinical/
forensic accounts, transformations, uncertainties and exculpatory sources.
No battlefield estimate, imagery classification, mass pattern or several
AI summaries alone proves an accused's act, context or intent. P11–P14
meaningful disclosure, independent expertise, torture/derivative exclusion,
fair confrontation and no-secret-guilt rules remain during war/secrecy.
Support, privacy and safe participation for affected people do not remove
the accused's defense or turn testimony into indefinitely public training data.

Victims can obtain information, safe voice and civil/compensation review
without controlling a conviction, waiving rights or undergoing forced
restoration. Clinical care, shelter, income, child safety and factual record
correction have their distinct receivers. A judgment names an executor,
assets/payer, due useful time and accepted route; actual receipt is checked.
Missing foreign assets, immunity, cash or safe personnel leaves an unmet
claim requiring lawful action, not a fiction of completed national healing.
