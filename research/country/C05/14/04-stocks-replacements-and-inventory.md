# PB13–PB16: a replacement quote must reach its owner in the due period

*Part of [C05/14](../14-ordinary-life-and-household-production.md); design 124.*

**PB13 — Give durable goods a physical stock and an actual due event.** The
case hypothesizes 50,000 shared home kits/10-year uniform vintage and 90,000 personal
devices/4-year vintage:5,000 kits and 22,500 devices are replaced and retired in
the reference year. Closing units remain 50,000/90,000. These are consumer goods,
not automatic public or national-accounts fixed capital. Actual title/vintage,
need/quality, repair/reuse and paid acceptance remain unknown. New quote stocks
replace this reference's old generic lens; do not add them to old 92.5 m durables
or treat their full valuation as this year's purchase, private cash or G wealth.

**PB14 — Derive purchaser price from the named producer scope.** Kit/device
quotes begin with 1,700/300FU imports, actual declared retail task/pay/input/
renewal/energy, a 6%margin on retail service only and 10%standard-rate VAT in this
qualified fiction. They yield about 2,028.75/352.51CU per replacement at 1CU/FU.
No second margin is applied to the pass-through import value. Named annual
household purchases are about 10.143743 m/7.931523 m, not the entire quote stock.
Actual customs, invoicing/registration, upstream credits, premises/finance,
foreign prices/acceptance and other costs remain unqualified, not zero law.

**PB15 — Finance the gross stock before later customer/tax receipt.** Thirty-
day stock including borderVAT needs about 1.378767 m CU for the two named goods
before buyer receipts. The example charges 4%annual interest on that whole gross
stock throughout the year; actual average debt/input-credit timing may differ.
A bank loan needs its own M/MC capital/liquidity/source/contract, NB lending actual
funds; neither creates kits or owned FX. Annual 15.25 m FU named import quotes
are not national FX need or Treasury property. At 1.25CU/FU both prices and seed
change; no exchange forecast, CPI or automatic pension/benefit reset follows.

**PB16 — Keep replacement need and received funding once.** Under I16, a
kit's current derived quote less 500 actually available same-purpose cash enters
additional N in its actual due period. A promised 400 public contribution does
not reduce that need; actual matching 400 receipt does. Annual renewal allocation,
CFC, a booked stock or a future tax refund is not another purchase or current
receipt. Preserve ordinary private cash/asset protection and apply I05 resources/
disregards/taper afterward. No unconditional appliance grant, receipt classifier,
forced loan or permission to consume another person's protected buffer is created.
