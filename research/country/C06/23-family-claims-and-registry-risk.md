# C06. Family receiving and official registry risk

15 October 2026 · Design 71 · Proposed responsibility, not a funded increment

[B21–B40](../C08/16-family-agency-property-and-estates.md) adds civil/family
and estate duties to the national account. The Assembly controls general
law and appropriations; independent courts determine contested claims;
named services/executors deliver. These are distinct roles, not a new
family ministry with authority over every person's life.

| Obligation | Resource/claim treatment and accountable receiving |
|---|---|
| Safe young-person/adult agency | Existing legal-aid and clinical/care authorities fund independent advice, interpretation, accessible communication and actual direct access; no unpaid relative or AI capacity score substitutes |
| Child protection and court clocks | Child service funds accepted safe carers/places/transport and support; judiciary funds duty judges, child representatives and separate parent counsel. Annual pooled hours cannot prove 24-hour hearing coverage |
| Private support/property | Actual private obligor/estate assets fund valid duties; limited ability and third-party interests stay visible. Shared property is excluded before estate distribution; same assets/support are never promised twice |
| Urgent food, housing and care | I/S and existing continuity receivers pay or provide legally due protection while status/maintenance/probate is disputed; a claim on a relative is not cash received and does not excuse public receiving |
| Pension survivors | Y09's one lost-support pot remains separate insurance, capped and costed once. More ties or an estate distribution do not multiply it |
| Probate and missing people | Court/registrar and qualified administrator need inventories, safe notices, source records, interim support and review; administration costs need lawful reasonableness and actual estate/public sources |
| Official title certification loss | B38's independent adjudication establishes a prospective public statutory claim for proved net reliance loss caused by incorrect official certification. Treasury pays through a separately visible public-claims account, not the pursuing office's fines or a fictional assurance fund |

The public-claims account distinguishes opening recognized claims, new
adjudicated obligations, actual due payments/recoveries and unpaid balances.
Preserve contingent exposures before judgment without calling them zero;
publish safe aggregates and reasons, not private claimant records. Recovery
from a wrongdoer/insurer reduces the same loss once and is recorded only
when received; it cannot finance today's due payment by assumption.

B38 assigns public operational risk to the state rather than forcing an
innocent buyer to absorb the registry's official error. It is **not a blanket
guarantee of all private fraud, perfect titles or investment returns**.
Price scope, transaction exposure, uncertainty and a credible appropriation/
reserve before operational adoption. If cash fails, the judgment and urgent
needs remain; E continuity, direct independent review and elected funding
action apply. A clerk cannot haircut rights, a court cannot enact a tax,
and a technical rollback cannot reverse an eviction or a spent estate.

The existing 1,053.05-million operating bid and 37-function allocations do
not contain a qualified full increment for B21–B40. Staff/period needs and
contingent compensation remain unknown, with no new hypothetical tax yield
or safe reserve credited. Avoid counting paid care, informal cooperation,
survivor pots, private estate assets or shared court staff as separate funds
for each claimant. Qualified quantity/cost, lawful sources, transition and
actual received remedies are still required; no country readiness is claimed.

**Design148 explicit notice-risk addition.** [PT21](../C08/26-property-transfers-and-secured-credit.md)
adds prospective no-personal-fault Treasury responsibility for proved net direct
reasonable reliance loss from materially wrong official security-index search/
effective-time certification or office-attributable publication-order failure.
It does not insure false private content, correctly disclosed outage, accurately
unperfected security or every fraud. Join this new unpriced contingent/due scope
with B38/OE27 in the same visible public-claims account; actual old rights,
independent adjudication, due compensation and protected essential receiving
continue, without double recovery or an assumed transaction-fee reserve.

[Design149 CB](../C09/14-shared-buildings-and-neighbor-rights.md) joins actual
common-building legal/technical/access/repair and qualified I03 receipt work.
It creates no new Treasury all-owner repair guarantee, public loan/lien or
official association-dues certificate assurance. Existing lawful public need
and CB public orders require coordinated B19 source/execution; private
association debts, actual contingent state claims and paid repair stay distinct.
Cost shared old roles/incremental work once; source/staff adequacy is unknown.

[YC stable child care](../C07/27-stable-child-care-and-adult-transition.md)
adds qualified placement availability, a host continuity account and
accepted voluntary adult housing and support. Keep existing B31 court
and service costs separate. Test actual dated replacement cash even
when the failed provider may later owe a refund.
