# Design319. Resident franchise and secure status

[RFV01–RFV05](../../country/C02/03/01-resident-franchise-and-secure-status.md)
changes the prospective national electoral period, keeping legal
status and affected participation separate. Preserve existing vested
rights, original evidence, lawful amendment and protected succession
under their own instruments. Price actual independent registration,
assistance, source custody and useful-time electoral remedies.

The [decision record](../../2026-11-resident-franchise-and-secure-status/decision-record.md)
preserves the longer, immediate and status-based alternatives.
Actual lawful adoption and operation remain unverified.
