# Two services and one usable route

*Design471 · Constructed comparison for a national renewal decision.*

The [household example](../../country/C13/23-household-protection-and-usable-receiving.md)
shows how a timely warm room and timely care at the old address can
leave a resident without either usable arrangement. In this example
a national programme compares two packages for ten residents each
needing both warmth and care in the same window. The population,
bookings, records, costs and times are constructed.

Both exact motions use six cash units and four paid peak blocks.
Ordinary protection, independent review and already due services
have separate accepted funding in the fixture. The records below
compare offers and verified use of the added arrangements. If they
expose an actual breach of a duty, that duty keeps its separate
remedy and original clock. Assess safety, meaningful choice and
private access on equally demanding terms in both proposals.

| Proposed continuation | Timely warmth and care in separate provider records | Residents with both entries | Confirmed usable combinations, and remaining possible |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Retain separate bookings at provider-chosen locations | 8 each | 6 | 5 confirmed. For the sixth, the warm placement and care record refer to different locations with no accepted transfer. The other four have at least one independently confirmed unavailable component. |
| B. Offer a resident-directed warm place or home route with matched care and an accepted travel plan | 7 each | 7 | 7 confirmed. The other three have at least one independently confirmed unavailable component. |

A leads on each provider count and B reaches two more people with
both services usable. A route can join different places when the
person accepts it and travel, handoff and timing are qualified.
Record refusal, missing evidence and confirmed incompatibility
separately. Refusal does not establish failed agency or waive an
owed remedy. Report the people still missing each service for B.

With A's marginal counts alone, at least six and at most eight
people have both timely entries, because the counts refer to the
same ten residents and window. That arithmetic says nothing about
whether even one pair can be used together. Where the cohorts or
time windows differ, do not apply that bound. Where residents'
route records cannot be matched in time, a qualified reviewer
can use lawful local evidence or safe sampling and report the
scope and limits. If just the sixth pair in A is unresolved rather
than incompatible, A's usable result is five to six. If only six
of B's seven pairs are verified, B is six to seven. Those ranges
touch and do not establish a strictly higher B result. Sample
uncertainty is a separate issue from these bounds.

## Disposition before the next commitment

On the verified rows, select B for the exact incremental renewal
in this scenario, subject to a fresh elected vote and authorized
receiving on the next call. The qualified reason is usable combined
access under the same dated budget, with agency and independent
challenge retained. The constructed observations do not establish
causation or wider country outcomes. Keep A and ordinary repair on
record, including A's separate service advantages.

Suppose a resident reports the mismatch in plain language shortly
before renewal, and a provider maintains that its own task was timely.
Independent review can confirm both accounts while disputing the
combined claim. If a shared tool has normalized 'booked' and 'used'
to the same category in the two agencies' reports, inspect the
original bookings, dispatch records and protected recipient accounts
before counting either feed as proof of receipt. Correct the dependent release premise and put the
exact affected motion before the competent body. Publish a safe
reason and preserve the resident's earlier claim and protection.
When the revised premise changes eligibility under an enacted
condition, only the affected, unbound release waits. Where the
vote must still take place on an incomplete comparison, record
unknowns and limit any finding to the supported scope.
An already binding renewal retains its actual commitments and remedies.
Until the competent decision occurs, carry independently authorized
standing work and any existing qualified fallback.

Use a resident-held or locally held itinerary where sufficient.
Check only the necessary original locations, timing, support and
acceptance under lawful restricted access. A national report uses
safe counts or bounds rather than identifiable itineraries. A person
can challenge using oral, assisted or offline evidence. Human or AI
summaries may point to the same discrepancy. Multiple retellings
do not create new observations. Preserve the original evidence,
material dissent and any corrected dependent decisions.
