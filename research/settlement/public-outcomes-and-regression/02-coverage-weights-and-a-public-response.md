# Coverage, weights and a public response

This authored case compares access to the same time-sensitive service
before and after changing the intake route. “Received” means actual
useful-time service. Two reported groups differ in access needs.
People using the ordinary route and those needing assistance both
retain their existing rights. The table contains only measured cases.

| Period | Ordinary group, received / observed eligible | Assisted group, received / observed eligible | Combined |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Earlier | 72 / 80 | 10 / 20 | 82 / 100 |
| Later | 81 / 90 | 4 / 10 | 85 / 100 |

The published observed aggregate rises from 82% to 85%. The ordinary
group remains at 90%, while the observed assisted group falls from
50% to 40%. At the earlier 80/20 composition, the later group rates
give 80%, down from 82%. This is a fixed-weight comparison of the
observed groups. It does not show the same individuals' change.

A separate lawful check identifies five additional eligible people in
the later assisted group who are outside both measured rows. Assume
no comparable known gap in the earlier scope. Their receiving outcomes
are unknown. The later combined rate lies between 85/105 and 90/105,
or approximately 81.0% to 85.7%. The assisted rate now has a range of
4/15 to 9/15, or 26.7% to 60.0%. Neither the whole-population change nor
the full assisted-group change has a settled sign on those counts alone.

If later original evidence confirms that all five missed service,
the combined rate is about 81.0% and assisted receipt about 26.7%.
Keep the earlier public claim and show the correction. Even then
the comparison does not establish that the new intake caused the loss.

In the authored case, independent review separately verifies that
the paid assisted route was withdrawn with a material access gap.
That finding can trigger the protected response without waiting
for the five outstanding observations or a causal study. Preserve
individual review and act on urgent losses at their actual deadline.
Bring the restored ordinary route, funded assistance and a genuinely
accessible alternative to the elected decision. Do not announce a
national improvement or award a fiscal saving while the comparison
is unresolved. The report, later vote and actual restored service
are separate outcomes.

If the Clerk receives the independently qualified finding on day 0,
the lead response is due by day 30 and recorded elected disposition by
day 60. A copy on day 10 leaves both deadlines intact. An individual
protection that is due on day 2 still needs actual action by day 2.
If the Assembly cannot lawfully vote on day 60, report the unheld
decision, keep the case on the lawful route and preserve the individual
remedy. Neither elapsed time nor statistical correction supplies funds.
