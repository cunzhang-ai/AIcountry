# Proof and voluntary access comparators

[Scoped credentials](README.md)

NIST's SP 800-63-4 separates identity proofing, authentication
and federation. SP 800-63C-4 discusses pairwise pseudonymous
identifiers and describes why other shared information can
still enable correlation. These are useful technical distinctions.
They do not determine public legal authority or establish that
a particular system cannot link people. [NIST guidelines](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/63/4/final)
· [NIST federation and assertions](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63c.html)

The European Digital Identity Framework provides for voluntary
wallet use and continued access through other means. Its wallet
provisions address selective disclosure and correlation controls
under defined scope. They supply an actual legal comparator
without proving every alternate route is timely or that every
common dependency is controlled.
[Regulation (EU) 2024/1183, inserted Article 5a](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1183/oj)

The December 2024 Australian compilation contains a section 74
voluntariness rule for participating services. It addresses
reasonable accessibility and substantially less favourable terms
and includes express exceptions. The design uses it as a
published legal comparator at that date. It establishes no
actual performance or law for this proposed country.
[Australian Digital ID Act 2024, section 74, dated compilation](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2024A00025/2024-12-11/2024-12-11/text/original/pdf)

The 24-hour cap, divided operational roles and staffing in this
design are selected hypotheses. Verify real people, protected
source evidence, elapsed deadlines, capture resistance and
alternative costs before asserting readiness.
