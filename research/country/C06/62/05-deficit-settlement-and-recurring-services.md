# FB26–FB30. Settle old deficits and fund continuing services separately

*Design269 · Proposed national and local fiscal duties.*

**FB26 — Keep the historical balance and recurring service in separate
accounts.** When a material recurring gap or repeated standby use
threatens the same service, show the historical stock, its legal status
and accrued claims, annual ordinary costs and authorized recurring net
sources. Separately report due-date cash, restrictions, applicable
spending limits, qualified staff and verified receiving. An accounting
override may alter a balance presentation or a statutory test within
its own law. It cannot pay a supplier, remove a person's claim or
provide recurring source. A historical shortfall may contain disputed,
estimated or already financed amounts. Reconcile those before treating
it as a payable debt. Apply the existing earlier useful-time and DB
notice rules when a protected duty is exposed.

**FB27 — Put settlement, interim protection and the new ordinary
package to explicit decisions.** The competent elected bodies vote
on the historical settlement and its residual, finite paid protection
through the change and the ordinary scope and recurring source.
Where decisions depend on one another, give the exact linked texts
and the consequence if any fails. For each grant or other source
show restricted purpose, approvals, actual receipt, effective
application date, residual exposure and later obligation. Count a
transfer once on consolidation. A grant committed to an old deficit
does not finance today's service unless its law expressly leaves
a verified amount available for that purpose. Include the repayment
and end date of any bridge. Preserve actual local budget and delivery
authority under [MF](../../C04/08-funded-local-mandates-and-equalization.md)
and [LD](../../C04/10-local-fiscal-recovery-and-debt.md).

**FB28 — Compare real continuing delivery before adopting the source
reset.** On the same population and dates, compare a repaired ordinary
route, a bounded continuing source or qualified delivery change, and
any credible temporary alternative with an exit plan. Price qualifications,
paid staff release, interim provision, independent remedies, maintenance
and later cohorts. Use FB01's annual and adverse paths, and show who
loses any discretionary allocation that funds a reallocation. A
new national grant cannot substitute for a qualified local worker.
An accounting saving, shorter schedule or cancelled referral needs
evidence of what the recipient actually lost or received. For
learners, apply [ED](../../C07/21-public-learning-and-school-power.md)
to the actual usable place, support, transport and independent correction.
Protected provision and prior legal claims retain their own deadlines.

**FB29 — Protect service through pending approval, expiry and change.**
Before relying on a new ordinary route, verify the original staff,
sites and cases that will carry through and the paid accepted substitute
for any loss. Do not end an existing service or spend conditional cash
on a forecast of approval. If a finite response must end before a
qualified ordinary route begins, name the lawful interim protection,
outstanding gap and actual competent decision. An inability to settle
the old balance supplies no new rationing power and cannot automatically
terminate a resident's service. An extension requires its own bounded
elected authority and a real source. Continuing claims and review remain.

**FB30 — Test whether the new baseline actually lasts.** Independent
budget analysis matches the chosen option with the original need,
fiscal scope and actual work, and separately reports settled historic
items, continuing revenue, paid usable service and unresolved loss.
Give local representatives, opposition, recipients and workers safe
access and useful-time challenge. Publish the original forecast,
revisions, conditions, staff and affected scope without a universal
case dossier. AI may reconcile duplicate sources and show when a
reserve has become routine. It cannot alter a claim, impose a local
programme, certify receipt or appropriate funds. Reopen on a new
recurring gap, expired support, missed paid work or a better qualified
whole option.
