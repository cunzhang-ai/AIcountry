# Pay for a choice that can still be made

*Design528 · Constructed budget with separate observed comparators.*

## An actual choice with limited information

Two localities share a first-budget envelope. In this exercise, standing
service, safety, court and electoral duties have their own paid rosters and
lawful funds. They do not move between options. The legislature can vote 10
additional units for above-floor work on the same 12 residents. Six in the
town are candidates for an extra month of home-energy price relief. Six in the
outlying area are candidates for an above-floor care follow-up. Each person's
existing protections and access to urgent care, safe heat and review continue,
including while any evidence is collected. All amounts, dates, cohorts and
results in the exercise are assumed.

By day 2 the Assembly must choose whether to buy the ordinary legal repair,
commit to one additional improvement or pay to keep two alternatives open.
The two improvement offers would otherwise lapse on day 2. A comparison
involving both localities investigates an unresolved classification of prices,
travel and service availability. A qualified independent finding can reach
the authorized decision office on day 5, and the last useful release is day 6
for delivery by day 10. Retaining both offers is possible under exact contracts.
The same financial fact can be observed regardless of which provider bids.
Residents' participation is voluntary, and earlier duties remain enforceable.

| Potential finding | Price relief in town | Accepted assisted journey and care in outlying area |
|---|---|---|
| Town price pressure | Six can actually use the extra relief on the identified dates. | The transport offer would bring two extra residents to completed follow-ups. The remaining four have a different obstacle. |
| Outlying route gap | Two can use the extra relief on the identified dates. The others need a different form of aid or have no eligible additional amount. | Six can consent to a timely accessible trip and be seen by a separately staffed receiving service. |
| Missing, conflicting or late finding | No prospectively voted release based on the missing distinction. Use a separately qualified common improvement only if its dates and prior authority still fit. | Any standing need, original complaint or lawfully accrued claim still follows its own receiving and remedy rule. |

The alternatives produce different outcomes. The number receiving extra
price relief and the number completing follow-up do not imply comparable
health, liberty or welfare gains. In this example the Assembly votes the
conditions for the two exact branches in advance. It keeps live the option
of serving a smaller group with common repair, and gives reasons for its
distributional choice. A new conflict about values needs an elected decision.

For the common improvement, assume accepted paid workers in each locality
can resolve two previously stalled matters per place by day 10. They make
direct assisted contact and verify the residents' next usable service route.
These four receiving corrections have their own endpoint. A resident may
also qualify for one of the optional offers and appears once per outcome.
The common improvement takes a separate roster and is compatible with either
early option. Its offered price and assigned staff expire on day 2.

| Motion | Voted uses of the 10 units | What the motion can deliver under its own assumptions |
|---|---|---|
| Commit to the defined town price relief | Six for town price relief and four for a qualified receiving and navigation improvement usable in both places. Fixed commitments take effect on day 2. | Six or two timely price-relief receipts, depending on the cause. The common improvement proceeds. The optional care offer lapses. |
| Commit to a qualified trip and service package | Six for the complete accessible outlying route with accepted service, plus the same four for the common improvement. Fixed commitments take effect on day 2. | Two or six timely care receipts, depending on the cause. The common improvement proceeds. The optional price-relief offer lapses. |
| Fund early independent evidence and reserve both exact choices | Two for independent accessible verification and one to hold each offer, leaving six for one exact release. Exercise either option by day 6. | If the favorable finding arrives on day 5 and the necessary route qualifies, six timely receipts of the relevant type. The four-unit common improvement is forgone. Where no release qualifies, the four remain spent and the six stay uncommitted. |
| Commit to common receiving improvement only | Four for lawful accessible contact and receiving improvement in both places. Six remain uncommitted under ordinary budget law. | Delivers two receiving corrections per locality by day 10 and keeps funds for a later actual decision. Neither optional offer is still available on day 6. |

The first and second rows are credible commitments if the relevant barrier is
already established. The six-unit care option includes accepting professionals,
accessible travel and return, and paid backup where needed. More appointments
without travel would be a supply-only rival. In the outlying route branch it
does not address the six affected people's barrier. If the live observation
instead finds no qualified staff to receive the referred person, suspend the
transport branch under the voted terms and compare qualified clinical staffing
at a fresh decision. Where both findings qualify, apply the expressly voted
priority if one exists. Here neither outranks the other by prior law, so require
a timely elected disposition or exercise neither. One underlying resource can
support alternatives only under a binding mutually exclusive dispatch and
cancellation agreement that protects its other commitments.
Conversely, a resident who can reach care and needs a
protected price correction follows the ordinary remedy immediately.

## When paying for evidence loses

If day 5 evidence arrives on day 7 or the qualified care roster becomes
unavailable before release, preserve any funded ordinary response. In the
contingent motion, the four-unit common improvement was already forgone and
cannot be recovered from the same four paid units. The six remain uncommitted
and return to lawful budget choice. The cost of spending, waiting and lost
receiving capacity explains why a better diagnostic alone does not win. If
the initially missed people cannot safely participate, do not interpret their
absence as low need. An observed mismatch or changed authority reopens the
affected part. Closing the accounts does not close original claims.

Select the funded common improvement for the present case and retain the six
unbound units. The comparison establishes no credible probability that the
diagnostic will identify a use valuable enough to justify foregoing known
work in both places. Even timely information adds at most four timely receipts
of an optional type over a poorly matched immediate option in these branches.
It also forgoes the common improvement and can delay help. A later proposal
can choose the reserved option on exact elected reasons and independently
qualified evidence and offers that can meet the deadline.

## Recognize the scope of actual evidence

In Oregon, a lottery offered uninsured low-income adults the opportunity to
apply for Medicaid. The first-year analysis found greater health-care use and
lower out-of-pocket expense and medical debt. Insurance combined several
financial protections. The results do not isolate a price-only intervention or
tell another country what service capacity it needs. [Oregon first-year study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3535298/)

In a West Philadelphia trial at two practices, missed appointments were
144 of 394 among those offered a complimentary ride and 144 of 392 among
controls. Only 57 of the 288 people in the offer group who answered the phone
used the ride. This estimates an offer on that particular channel in an urban
sample. It does not estimate a separately accepted trip-and-receiving route in
a remote area. Use the journal's corrected results. [Trial and correction notice](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamainternalmedicine/fullarticle/2671405)
