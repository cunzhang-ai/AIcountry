# S09–S12: support that preserves the person's life and choice

*Part of [C07 service candidate](../13-public-service-entitlements.md); design 67.*

**S09 — Functional needs with personal agency.** National finance and local
receiving capacity cover necessary support for eating, hygiene, movement,
communication, daily safety, chosen community participation and other
assessed essential activities. Eligibility follows actual support need and
the person's will, without a diagnosis, marriage, employment or unpaid-family
condition. Housing, treatment, income and supported decision-making keep
their separate grounds. A care assessment cannot remove legal capacity.

Provide an accessible needs assessment within 14 calendar days; urgent
protection follows E's earlier useful-time rule with provisional paid support.
The plan states tasks, safe frequency/duration, qualified receiving shifts,
travel, personal choice and independent review. Review on material change or
request, with a normal offered review at least every six months; stable
needs do not require repeated intrusive proof. No fixed average-hours cap
or lack of relatives replaces individualized support. Consent may be
supported; it is neither assumed from silence nor overridden by a care label.

**S10 — Childhood care without a work test.** Offer optional usable childcare
from infancy through age 12, with a reference baseline of up to 40 weekly
hours over 48 weeks and assessed additional support for disability, safety
or unavailable safe family coverage. Weeks outside the baseline are not
presumed unpaid family time. Families need not exhaust relatives or prove formal
employment. Safe staffing, food, accessible travel and age-appropriate care
are part of the offer; no duty to use all hours or surrender family/cultural
life is created. Coordinate local hours with real work/care patterns.
School/early-learning episodes may meet part of this care offer only when
actual safe supervision is provided. Before/after-school and holiday needs
require real receiving arrangements; a closed classroom is no care place.

S10's 40-hour/48-week offer is a contestable authored reference, not a proven
optimum or expected usage. Evaluate child health/development, agency, relationships,
quality and worker conditions separately from attendance or parental employment.
An accepted place is necessary receiving evidence, not proof that care improved
a child's life. Adverse findings require safe correction/alternative provision
with actual source/rights continuity, not automatic withdrawal of optional care.

**S11 — Choose provision, not abandonment.** Fund home, community or
residential support as appropriate with recipient choice and outside
safeguarding. A person may choose a direct budget or a willing paid relative
only where adequate lawful provision can actually be bought, with employment,
safety, records and independent review. A below-cost cash offer cannot
discharge care or force hidden family labor. Voluntary informal help is
respected and may be withdrawn; it is counted as available only with actual
consent and a continuity plan. Payment, personal agency and safeguarding
remain distinct decisions, with several direct complaint routes.

**S12 — A real receiving handoff.** A care worker, facility or payment failure
requires funded safe receiving and timely review, preserving the person's
preferences, records and continuing claims. Neither a waiting-list entry,
an unsupported AI-companion offer nor a pension discharges required assistance.
A tool-supported task may qualify on demonstrated adequacy, consent, safety
and viable fallback; future methods are judged on actual merits, not labels.
Actual human/social support is not reduced to task completion; privacy,
relationships, autonomy and unwanted institutionalization are assessed too.
Care workers keep safe hours, pay, training and voice. Family members,
children, recipients and workers have separate rights, not one household
interest that a coordinator can resolve by an aggregate benefit score.

[UA](../../C08/23-necessary-care-and-unknown-will.md) now selects narrowly bounded
basic-care continuity and prior-court consequential arrangements without invented
consent or general guardianship. It cannot create a private signature, compulsory
family labor, closed placement or an objection override. S entitlement alone
still grants none of those powers; accepted paid receiving remains required.

Design115 [I17–I20](../../C05/06/06-received-services-time-and-replacement.md)
reconciles actual care/work/commute/holiday timing and received costs, not weekly
slots alone. It does not reduce S09's individualized hours or S10's additional
cover, credit a clinical/school duty as ordinary cash, or presume free family
work. Existing net reference costs cannot lose the same public support twice.

[AS01–AS24](../18-person-directed-assistance.md) now makes S11/S12's assistance
choices specific. Covered support is free at use; the person need not become
an employer to direct it. Employer, payroll, personal control and public receiving
are separate; paid worker dues/notice and actual backup continue on a change.
AS does not cap S09 hours, reduce S10 or create new guardianship/clinical powers.

[NC01–NC20](../20-early-care-relationships-and-staffing.md) now specifies the
organized publicly funded under6 group candidate: ratios3/4/6 and normal
stable groups6/8/12 at under2/2–3/3–6, actual competent presence, relationships,
paid cover and independent correction. These do not prove optimality, price
all care/6–12, mandate attendance, replace individual additional support or
license private family life. All earlier S/source dates and needs continue.

[CL chosen care time](../../C05/17-care-leave-and-return.md) adds actual portable
income/absence/return, not a family duty, custody or clinical power. Paid leave
and services can be complementary; no automatic place cancellation, nursery
saving, recipient fee or free qualified cover. Six-week temporary-leave expiry
cannot discharge S09/S10/AS long-term receiving or require unpaid relatives.

[WF65–WF68](../../C06/58/15-public-financing-and-real-service-offer.md) separates
publicly financed existing service episodes from additional actual S/NC/AS
quantity/quality/access. A changed payer cannot supply a new place/worker or
automatically nationalize a provider. Fee removal needs actual source and may
change use; fixed-episode arithmetic does not discharge unmet/new needs.

[CW usable early care](../24-usable-care-time-and-shift-continuity.md)
specifies short and irregular under-six windows within S10, without
waiving age-six-to-twelve, earlier urgent or additional assessed duties.

[HS01–HS08](../25-adaptive-home-support.md) gives adults at home a protected
individual plan, with supported consent for ordinary adaptations and public
review of material reductions. S09 and E protect reassessment and relief.
