# Stalking, intimate privacy and deliberate damage

*K15–K16; part of [C08/15](../15-special-crimes-and-bounded-sanctions.md).*

**K15 — Stalking and intimate-image abuse.** Knowingly pursue at least two
unwanted acts of targeted surveillance, following or contact, without lawful
justification, knowing or consciously disregarding that the course causes
reasonable fear of violence or substantial disruption of ordinary liberty.
Prove the acts, course, fault and effect; an unpleasant message, dislike,
peaceful protest or refused friendship alone is insufficient. Maximum three
years; five only for proved resulting serious bodily injury with at least
recklessness about that injury. No prior warning/order is required to prove
the offense, and an order alone does not prove it.

Intentionally share an identifiable person's private sexual image or a
realistic fabricated equivalent, knowing lack of permission for that sharing,
without a lawful justified purpose, is a distinct three-year-ceiling offense.
Consent to creation or one recipient is not consent to onward distribution.
For this limb, prove privacy, identifiability, sexual content and the fault;
satire merely referring to someone is insufficient. Actual K14 exploitation
has its own elements. A fabricated record is never evidence that the
depicted person performed the act.

Neither limb criminalizes proportionate necessary evidence/reporting,
lawful public-interest inquiry or court/clinical use with protected access.
Public-interest necessity is tested independently, not by the publisher's
assertion; sensational curiosity alone is insufficient. A court may order
specific preservation, removal or protection under separate lawful grounds,
with scope, expiry, notice/challenge and safeguards for contrary evidence.
No general censorship filter, blanket identity collection or secret
surveillance authority arises. Immediate support retains C08/10's own route.

**K16 — Property destruction and physical sabotage.** Intentionally destroy
or materially damage another's property without entitlement or lawful
justification: maximum two years. A good-faith title dispute, necessary rescue,
ordinary wear or accidental damage without another expressly stated fault
does not satisfy it. Prove ownership/interests and the actual material effect.

The ceiling is five years where the actor knowingly disables an essential
physical supply or safety system and thereby materially interrupts a
necessary service; ten where the actor consciously creates a specific,
substantial unjustifiable danger of death through fire, explosion, flood
or deliberate disabling of a safety barrier. A political motive, a critical-
infrastructure label or a service operator's complaint cannot replace those
elements. Culpable death/injury retains K01/K02 and the highest applicable
single-course ceiling, without double punishment.

Lawful strikes, safe disconnection under valid safety duties, criticism,
good-faith repair and protected disclosure do not become sabotage merely
because a service is interrupted. Digital interference uses K10's additional
boundaries where applicable. Repair/continuity and affected people's
receiving services do not wait for guilt, nor does a protection or repair
finding prove guilt. No cyber or emergency coordinator may create the crime.
