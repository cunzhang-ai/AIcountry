# ED01–ED05. Name the employer and the outside reviewer

*Part of [ED public learning](../21-public-learning-and-school-power.md).* 

**ED01 — Keep the existing public offer.** S05 foundational ages6–18,
39-week/30-hour offered capacity, S06 early/catch-up and S07 first pathway
remain. ED's school-operation rules cover public and publicly funded
foundational schools; home/community/future routes retain ED09 and separate
proportionate checks. NC owns under6 group/care standards. Higher-learning
academic governance, professional licensing and every requested advanced
course are not silently determined here. Civil majority remains18; voting,
clinical/work and other ages keep their own law.

**ED02 — A public entity owes the place and employs the staff.** The elected
local authority is the public foundational-school employer and service debtor,
through its education administration, unless an express lawful inter-local
public arrangement identifies the actual employer, paid source and successor.
A school name, contractor or joint portal cannot erase that responsibility or
create a sovereign tier. National education administration retains C04's
lawful funded guarantee/fallback; no automatic seizure of local power. An
additional provider names its actual employing legal entity; public funding
conditions do not nationalize its assets or transfer private worker claims.

**ED03 — Qualified local leadership, no patron principal.** The local elected
executive appoints its Director of Education from C04's open qualified shortlist
with reasons/conflicts and protected career status. The director appoints each
public school head after open job-related competition, independent qualified
assessment and accessible learner/staff/family input. Input informs reasons;
it is no loyalty vote, parent veto or employment referendum. Delegations name
actual lawful deputies; assignment changes, discipline, dismissal and worker
rights follow C04/WL and direct review, not a purge after an election. A head
cannot hire/fire through a vendor score or conceal the public employer.

**ED04 — Use the existing national inspection function.** Name a National
Education Inspector as its career head, employed by the state in national
education administration, Cabinet-appointed from C04's open qualified shortlist.
Relevant independent qualification, conflicts, protected career and review
apply. It is the outside-provider education/NC inspection function already
required, with actual discipline-specific staff, not a new sovereign regulator
or one person's universal clinical/care licence. National/local politicians,
purchasers, school heads and vendors cannot direct individual findings, suppress
lawful reports or choose the final reviewer of their own disputed conduct.
The head assigns actual conflict-free inspectors under published competence/
allocation rules and preserves their protected professional judgments.

**ED05 — A protector is reviewable.** Inspector decisions have reasons,
protected evidence and direct ordinary OJ court access; AA audits sources and
spending without replacing educational merits. Complaints about the chief or
inspection itself cannot be finally decided by that chief or implicated team;
an unimplicated receiving officer protects first receipt and routes the own-
office complaint to the competent ordinary OJ court, without mandatory internal
exhaustion. The court selects actual qualified conflict-free outside evidence
assistance under ordinary law and adjudicates review; the chief cannot select
or direct its final review. Employment discipline stays with the competent
state employer/C04 fair process, subject to courts; allegation alone removes
no one. Absence of a qualified independent receiver is recorded as failure
needing lawful paid response, not cured by another account name.
Acting director/head/Inspector assignments last at most180elapsed calendar days
from the original vacancy, no serial reset; earlier source-law limits prevail.
Existing competent officers' own lawful powers/case duties persist, not a proxy
extension of the vacant office. No expiry manufactures an appointed successor.

[AL public advanced learning](../22-lifelong-learning-and-renewal.md) now
selects the previously excluded adult/advanced institutional scope; foundational
schools keep these ED powers and S05–S08 rights. Existing qualified inspection
may serve its competent AL scope, not certify every profession or direct grades.
