## Decide how to combine a shared floor and local improvements

Use two localities with one due episode per resident and 100 residents each, the same cost of
10 units per episode, and equally qualified delivery on the dates unless a
scenario says otherwise. Initially people in A pay 9 because A funds a one-unit
local reduction off the common charge. In B they pay 10. The national reform
limits the charge to 8 and authorizes 400 units at each call for the two local
providers. A's 100-unit local authority and next-call source remain lawful for
its separate reduction. Eligible people who could not previously take up the
offer remain in a separate evidence account. No take-up change or delivery gain
is assumed by the price calculation.

The legal form can change the result before anyone votes again. A's active
one-unit relative reduction yields a price of 7 under the new maximum of 8.
If the local rule instead capped the final charge at 9, the new common price
of 8 already satisfies that rule. If the local instrument promises an
independent fixed award, apply that award's own terms and reconcile any
express same-item offset. The same prior bill of 9 does not establish which
of these commitments exists. Never infer a recurring funding release solely
from a charge calculation or extinguish an adjustment already owed.

| Choice | Price to residents in A / B | National / local finance for 200 episodes per call | What must be decided |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common maximum of 8 with a lawful local reduction | 7 / 8 | 400 / 100 | Preserve the distinct sources while A's reduction is in force. B may choose lawful local relief, or the Assembly may improve equivalent practical protection through common law and equalization. |
| Common maximum of 8 with local replacement after lawful expiry or separate prospective decision | 8 / 8 | 400 / 0 | A residents lose one unit per episode against the first option. A's 100 can support other local priorities if lawfully released. The national reform cannot decide that local tradeoff by implication. |
| Uniform higher protection with an effective price of 7 | 7 / 7 | 600 / 0 on a fully national source | The published 400 national source is 200 short. Explicit coordinated finance can cover a lawful local share if authorized. Preserve accrued local claims and reconcile overlap once. |

Even if A lawfully joins its 100 to the 400 common budget, a uniform price of
7 requires another 100. On that pooled 500 envelope, a uniform price of 7.5
fits the arithmetic with unchanged delivery costs. It lowers B's charge by
0.5 and raises A's by 0.5 relative to the first option. That tradeoff requires
authorization and comparison with keeping the existing local terms. The
pooled comparison does not assume new authority to use A's money in B.

For the second call assume national cash of 200 owed to A has not arrived.
B has received its own 200. A's local 100 is already committed to the
supplement. A can meet the common increment only if an independently usable
ordinary source or separately authorized bridge provides another 200 before
service. A bridge funded for just one call stays outstanding after use. If
reimbursement slips again, a third call must have its own timely money.
Settlement of the delayed 200 repays a valid advance once. An estimate or a
net reduction elsewhere in the grant is neither that payment nor a resident
debt. Preserve the applicable price and remedy. If the provider cannot continue,
report exact missed protection and use only lawful funded fallback.

If twenty B residents cannot reach the receiving site, A's extra reduction
neither causes nor resolves B's lack of common access. The next funded B
decision compares qualified receiving or transport repair and an alternate
provider on actual receipt. A lower common price could still help some people.
It has to demonstrate that effect. Separately compare whether A should retain
the lower price or vote a better use of its 100. A national option can win if
the two places' unequal effective protection persists and a higher floor with
usable delivery fits an expressly chosen recurring source. The comparison
selects separate authority and continuity at the existing terms. It leaves
elected officials free to choose the later lawful disposition.
