# I21–I24: source claims stay challengeable and a new floor does not fund itself

*Part of [C05/06](../06-income-floor-and-safe-resources.md); design115 candidate.*

**I21 — Use a register to expose gaps, not approve them.** For each material
component trace original observation/price estimate/retail offer/wholesale quote/
accepted contract, edition, unit/currency, custody/controller, actual supplier/
qualification/capacity, public subsidy, cash/stock/maintenance and due receipt.
These statuses are different. A household budget is not a provider invoice;
a subsidized ticket is not a network's full operating/renewal cost. Check new
facts/objections before reuse, even when data bytes are unchanged. A shared
agreement, platform or AI output is no independent second observation or final
power over the actual supplier. Preserve and correct mistaken extractions;
do not invent a continuing conflict from a stale search snippet.

The115 external comparison is urban-UK April2026 research, with separately dated
Leicester retail-ticket information, not a selected territory, local public or
complete source book. Original item lists and annual category prices have
different editions. Retain their difference, source rounding residuals and
unresolved classification. No automatic GBP/CU conversion, UK gross-earnings
requirement as M, household budget as personal B, or benchmark group's consent
as this country's authority. Unknown meanings remain visible until resolved.

**I22 — Who decides, pays and corrects.** Existing I/C04 merit career receivers
and eligibility/correction officers apply enacted source law; EV supplies
protected evidence within its fixed independent appointment/pay/publication
chain. Treasury's named protection/refund payer and C07/H/MO actual provider
receivers deliver their scoped obligations. Assembly controls the joint budget,
source/criteria and public provider choice; no new lifestyle authority, fiscal
veto or self-financing levy. No fine, client/S/pension/FX raid, unaccepted
volunteer or free AI staffs this duty. Price people/time, translation/care,
private access, actual replacements/stock, vendor exit and independent courts
once with all other37-function and later obligations.

I08/B ordinary full review, urgent E/useful-time relief, protected evidence/aid,
actual Treasury payment and bounded B18/B19 external determined-act execution
retain their actual sources, competence and earlier clocks. Outside correction
cannot invent a new basket, missing appropriation or physical receiver. Verify
actual accessible cash/goods/care at the receiving end. An empty account, absent
provider, wrong classification and unlawful refusal require different remedies;
a court paper or corrected database is not restored life. Due arrears and
source/financing duties remain if the alternative executor also fails.

**I23 — Retain genuine social choices.** Select component-profile adequacy,
individual control,20% ordinary cash-need minimum, strictly matched voluntary
in-kind credits and due-period essential replacement/care/time assessment.
Keep cash-first/U-with-services, extensive voluntary public provision, safer
actual pooling/cooperation and different context/ownership choices alive.
0% cash may concentrate dependence;50/100% can protect more discretion at
additional source/overlap cost. No aggregate budget, purported optimization or
newer concept proves a superior life. Actual exclusion, high burden or a better
whole rival can displace this design; the value conflict is not missing evidence.

The context analysis is qualified external research, not project participation,
legitimate adoption, real local supplier acceptance or national affordability.
I05 remains the detailed operating mechanism; IC/C2 remains conditional, with C1 a rival, with
larger existing net/source claims preserved.20% is an N parameter, never added
to an old payment outside the formula or used to cancel a larger cash right.
B/tax/W/CPI and age/content changes still require their competent source law.

**I24 — Prospective law and a bounded next country question.** Coordinated
Income/IC/PA/C07/H/MO/source law commences these additions180calendar days after
competent enactment, with paid preparation and exact component/receiver/source
succession. Use the prior lawful basket/source fallback where new schedules are
unfinished; no zero need, readiness veto over commenced rights or election delay.
Actual old claims, due dates, tax/pension/index/worker/tenancy/creditor/privacy/
foreign obligations and safe case custody survive. The new method cannot approve
itself; actual constitution/software/contribution permissions remain unchanged.

Reopen on inaccessible/private-choice cash, unsupported deductions, harmful shared
allocation, missed care/rest/replacement, classification/source errors, coercion/
capture or a stronger whole rival. Missing monitoring means uncertainty, not
consent. Next examine whether the country's ordinary-services design actually
supports independent living with substantial functional support needs. S09–S12
already protects chosen care; specify person-directed assistance employer and
provider control, worker/recipient liability, payroll/intermediary alternatives
and actual public fallback. A healthy-household benchmark is no ceiling. Compare
loss, source, practical replacement and executed remedy. This is a country-design
priority, not permission to defer the still-missing context supplier/working-cash,
actuarial/price/FX/legacy book or invent affected testimony. A larger contradiction
or urgent actual harm can reprioritize; root prepares research, not absent
institutional authority, professional/participant budget or actual life outcomes.
