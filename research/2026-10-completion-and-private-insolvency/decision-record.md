## Coherent competing debt models and selection

Compare retaining/funding collection and negotiated workouts, creditor-led
workout without public discharge, blanket debt relief/public rescue and a
court-controlled protected estate/bounded ordinary fresh start. Retain/repair
may suffice where creditors agree and courts actually deliver; it can also
leave a dependent person in endless collection. Private workout can be cheap
but fragmented creditors, guarantors and powerful patrons can block exit.
Blanket relief reduces debtor burden but can erase worker/victim/secured
claims and shift loss to taxpayers without authority or funds. Select the
bounded court route with actual control/resources, fair notice, source-backed
priority, independent case actors and a declared creditor loss. Ownership,
cooperative rescue and no-change alternatives remain live public choices.

U01–U04 specifies no-asset access, court stay, owned assets rather than presumed
family wealth, needs/exemptions, avoidance and explicit collateral/unencumbered
priority. Ordinary tax arrears share ordinary unsecured ranking; insider
subordination requires actual control/instrument, not family identity.
U05–U08 uses safely available excess, a candidate half-excess contribution,
36-month ordinary plan with prompt no-means discharge, bounded fraud extension,
protected exceptional claims, separate guarantors and corrected credit records.
U09–U12 permits genuine viable-firm rescue with committed sources, class/
liquidation protection, worker/receiver duties, neutral officers and lawful
closure; bank/sovereign resolution and public bailout remain separate powers.

The 3-month needs buffer/wage cap, 12-month lookback, 36-month plan/extension/
flag, half-excess share, two-thirds class value, 90-day no-means decision
and 90+90-day firm-plan periods
are candidate value choices, not calibrated optima. Exceptions for accrued
support and proved intentional violence/fraud can survive ordinary discharge;
actual-ability/half-excess protection limits collection but victims may remain
unpaid. This disagreement is explicit. Ongoing family duties and criminal
accountability do not vanish. No prison, forced work, civic ban or presumed
victim fund resolves insolvency. Write-off changes counterpart claims; it
creates neither new output, cash nor a national funded floor.

## Evidence, comparators and objections

[Eight cases](../settlement/private-insolvency/README.md) ask about actual
estate/control, priority, victims, guarantors, forecast finance and biased
officers. They are authored hypotheses, not verdicts or delivered payments.
The September civil and current credit/civil/income families remain shared
project sources; macro results are retained only within their old scopes.
[Checks](validation.json) establish preservation, source identity and current
organization, not legality, independent capacity or improved life.

The official [UNCITRAL MSE recommendations](https://uncitral.un.org/en/lrimse)
and [concordance](https://uncitral.un.org/en/insolvency_table_of_concordance_part_5)
were checked through indexed material on 14 October; direct retrieval failed.
They address simple access, stays, affected creditors/workers and discharge,
not our selected priorities/numbers. The [World Bank natural-person report](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/668381468331807627/pdf/771700WP0WB0In00Box377289B00PUBLIC0.pdf)
was retrieved and relevant introductory/policy passages inspected. It frames
competing context-sensitive options and expressly does not identify one set
of best practices. It is methodological support, not endorsement, current
local credit data or independent original evidence of this country's outcome.
No complete reading of all 147 pages or treaty/foreign-law compatibility is claimed.

Material objections: discharge/exemptions may raise rates or exclude small
borrowers; no access gain is asserted and actual pricing/provisions must be
assessed. Secured priority can leave workers/victims unpaid; needs/receiving
and wage priority expose rather than erase that loss. Exceptional claims can
undermine fresh start; bounded collection/no coercion remains, while the
value disagreement needs actual affected participation. A trustee can be
captured; rotation, conflicts, neutral pay and direct review require real
independent sources/funding. Forecast rescue can hide unpaid wages; sources
and physical operation, not plan labels, control continuation. These are
reasoned objections, not resolved preferences or public assent.
