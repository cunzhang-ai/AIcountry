# Continue foundational learning when the usual place fails

9 November 2026 · Design217 · [ED21–ED28](../../country/C07/21-public-learning-and-school-power.md)

A named local education owner maintains usable learning when a learner's
normal arrangement cannot deliver it. Schools, health workers and outside
reviewers retain their distinct functions. Measure loss and receipt with
respect for chosen learning routes and private life.
