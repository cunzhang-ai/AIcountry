# H06–H10: permanent provision without a patronage landlord

*Part of [C09/12](../12-secure-housing-and-repair.md); design91 research selection.*

**H06 — A permanent option, not universal public ownership.** Existing local
housing authorities shall commission or own a retained cost-rental portfolio
through municipal ownership or an accountable nonprofit/community landholder.
New grants and acquisition terms protect continued affordable rental use and
public value against insider sale; lawful redevelopment carries the obligation
and tenants to a funded successor. Landholding, operating and tenant membership
need not be identical. No new sovereign housing council or central AI allocator
is created. Buy/lease/build through authorized paid contracts, voluntary valid
contribution or separately justified compensated public acquisition. Existing
stock, mortgages and rights do not become public by a model or declaration.

**H07 — One reachable application and reasoned allocation.** The local housing
authority accepts assisted, offline and protected applications within I01 scope;
it names an officer and gives a reasoned assessment. First priority is imminent
serious danger, violence or lack of any safe home; next is serious unsuitable
access/overcrowding or loss of an essential journey; then other unmet adequate-
housing need. Within a band use duration of the verified unmet need, with an
auditable draw for materially equivalent claims and urgent reassessment when
facts change. Necessary access features must match the actual unit. Application
speed, party/employer affiliation, citizenship franchise, account ownership or
previous local residence cannot displace the ordinary-residence/urgent floor.

Publish criteria, decisions' safe reasons and anonymized allocation patterns;
never refuge addresses or identifiable violence testimony. A reasonable refusal
of an unsuitable offer does not erase priority. A materially suitable declined
offer permits review of present urgency, not presumed consent or loss of I03.
The queue gives no permission to leave urgent needs unreceived. General council
policy cannot reserve homes for incumbents or ban otherwise qualified newcomers.

**H08 — Resident voice with bounded powers.** Residents elect and replace a
tenant committee, receive paid/accessible participation and independent advice,
and can choose common-space rules and management among qualified funded offers.
Use one adult-resident vote each and supported child voice; nonparticipation
forfeits no service. Shared-household consent differs from allocation of other units.
Ordinary decisions need published notice, reasons, challenge and dissent rights;
affected minorities retain rights against majority pressure. The committee
cannot allocate outsiders' homes, compel social care, authorize eviction, waive
repair or private space, privatize public equity or make tax/sovereign law.
Nonmembers, future applicants, workers and neighbors use public consultation,
elected scrutiny and courts rather than fictive representation by current tenants.

**H09 — Follow the whole control chain.** Elected councils approve sites,
appropriations and operator mandates within national rights/C03–C04 competence.
Professional appointments/procurement follow public integrity rules, disclosed
interests and removable fixed mandates; landlord management and benefit/safety
adjudication have separate case decisions and review. National elected finance
funds the national floor through an explicit intergovernmental schedule, not
unfunded local duties. Independent inspection, opposition access to safe accounts,
protected organizing and direct ordinary courts constrain the owner too.
An alternative operator must actually have staff, title/access, money and records;
changing a logo cannot count as exit from a common controlling landlord/vendor.

**H10 — Reachable correction before exclusion.** A housing authority must give
written safe reasons and a reachable independent ordinary administrative/civil
court route. B17 urgent relief and earlier useful-time protection remain; a
queue appeal is no substitute for a dwelling. Preserve the disputed allocation
opportunity or a suitable equivalent where possible without displacing an
innocent occupant. The lawful public housing steward executes receiving and
court-ordered substitute repair under existing power; courts cannot conjure
stock or new appropriation. A failed substitute leaves named landlord/public
breaches, monetary losses and the elected scarcity decision visible. No housing
office may postpone elections, punish opposition or require mediation first.
Provider acceptance, tenant consent, suitability and court authority are separate;
declining a lawful adequate alternative is no universal address veto or waiver
of urgent necessities, and never authorizes forced cohabitation or restraint.

[CB01–CB24](../14-shared-buildings-and-neighbor-rights.md) supplies the proposed
ordinary shared-building owner/resident decision and finance/repair boundary.
H08 one-adult voice and stronger funded qualified-management choice remain in
H's operator scope. A private common-asset association has no power to allocate
public homes, overrule private-unit title, waive H safety or impose a tax.
CB actual protected repair/participation costs share existing roles, not free
resident labor or a new sovereign neighborhood body.

[LU01–LU05](../17/01-elected-plans-and-permission.md) now bounds ordinary
planning: elected rules and enforceable conforming decisions, no incumbent
neighbor veto. These H need/allocation/resident powers stay distinct; neither
planning permission nor owner finance allocates a protected tenancy.

[HL housing loss](../25-housing-loss-and-secure-receiving.md) assigns
actual emergency receiving and a reasoned durable route. H07 ordinary
allocation, suitability and H10 independent remedy retain their scope.
A temporary allocation does not rewrite the permanent queue.

[RT21–RT25](../18/05-rental-entry-and-lawful-income.md) supports
timely private entry with verified lawful payment. H07 need-based
cost-rental allocation and HL urgent receiving keep their own scopes.
