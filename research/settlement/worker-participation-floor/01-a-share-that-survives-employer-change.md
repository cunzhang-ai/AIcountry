# A worker's participation follows the work already performed

*Design521 · Institutional comparator and constructed accounting*

The [selected candidate](../../country/C05/18/08-worker-participation-in-realized-surplus.md)
combines a cash participation floor and room to bargain for a better
allocation. It applies by the economic result and control of work. This lets
a worker contest exclusion even when no one can reliably attribute individual
dollars of surplus to AI.

## Give existing institutions full credit

France already requires participation for qualifying firms, allows
group arrangements and maintains information for eligible former employees.
Its official guidance describes a five-month payment deadline and a
statutory default where a required agreement is missing. Its formula is
based on profits, equity, wages and value added. The proposed distinction
is a direct share in a specified lead group's result for labor it actually
controls through a separate employer and a readily usable cash option.
Agency work under existing French arrangements must receive its full
credit in a real comparison. [French public guidance](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2141).

Nimier-David, Sraer and Thesmar use an earlier French change in the firm-size
threshold. Their revised working paper estimates a 1.8 percentage-point
increase in labor's share and a 1.4-point reduction in the profit share.
Its worker analysis shows differences by occupation, including wage
substitution for some higher-skill workers. The worker dataset does not
directly report individual participation amounts. The authors impute them
using firm accounts. The study does not measure AI attribution or the later
candidate's net effects. [Working paper, sections 5–6](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w31804/w31804.pdf).

Germany's arrangements for specified establishment changes supply an
independent route to determine a social compensation plan in covered cases.
Treat that as a strong means of addressing losses, with different eligibility
and benefit from sharing gains. [Works Constitution Act sections 111–112](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/englisch_betrvg/englisch_betrvg.html).

French employer-funded insolvency guarantees can cover participation in
specified proceedings and within applicable limits. A separately priced
guarantee or protected custody is therefore a genuine stronger comparator.
[French guarantee guide](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F31409).

## Join group surplus, actual payment and household change

Use authored units throughout. The audited positive result is 30 after
company tax and ordinary accrued costs, before participation. An enacted
normal-return deduction of 10 leaves 20. The illustrative participation
rate of 10% owes 2, with no loss carry in this branch. Allocate using 200
covered and consistently measured work units, including any qualified
credited hours within their original cohorts.

| Workers in the covered period | Work units | Gross participation due |
|---|---:|---:|
| Current direct workers | 140 | 1.40 |
| Direct workers who left before payment | 20 | 0.20 |
| Workers supplied by a separate agency and directed by the lead | 40 | 0.40 |

A different formal employer or a cancelled access account does not remove
the last two rows. Suppose the lead changes agencies during those 40 work
units. A person who worked under both and leaves before close counts their
verified units once, including if a prior agency cannot supply payroll.
If an agency has already paid 0.15 to the same workers
from the same lead-group liability and period, the lead owes the remaining
0.25 for those workers. An award from the agency's own independent result or
another period supplies no offset. Suppose total actual receipts by the due
date are 1.60 against 2 owed. Keep 0.40 due unless the forum grants a
lawful bounded deferral. Any lawful deferral preserves its dated cash
requirement. A negative result next year creates no further award and
leaves that balance payable. In a separate insolvency branch assume five
units remain in the estate after security and process costs. Four go to
prior-ranked wage claims. If unpaid participation and other unsecured
claims are two units each, the remaining unit yields 0.50 for participation.
The award is therefore two and its recovered amount is 0.50. A lawfully
funded separate guarantee would need its own premium, coverage, receiving
and creditor-law comparison.

For an individual who receives 0.40 in a separate illustrative distribution,
deduct 0.05 incremental tax and 0.10 loss of an existing benefit to obtain a
0.25 net gain, assuming no other change. If their lawful base pay fell by
0.25 on comparable work, this gain disappears. If the 0.40 was never
accessible when assessed, that cash cannot close the household's budget.
An offset correction or benefit averaging rule has its own legal and
budget decision. No changes to social-benefit bases are presumed.

## Make the rival capable of winning

- **Strong bargaining with a lead present.** If lawful arrangements already
  reach all three groups with the same usable proceeds, paid time or better
  terms and preserve claims across departure, prefer the simpler route. If
  only direct employees gain, compare the unserved cohorts and costs explicitly.
- **Statutory participation with a cash remedy.** Purchases from independent
  suppliers stay outside unless actual control is established. Check wages,
  total net household income, paid time, staffing and work reclassification
  through two periods. A majority agreement that delivers more in total
  while excluding supplied workers cannot meet the stated floor.
- **Broad tax and social protection.** This can reach people outside
  profitable firms and diversify exposure to the current employer. Match
  actual revenue dates and qualified receiving. These funds remain a rival
  public allocation and an independent source for standing protection.

Only select the trial's start where affected people have been heard and the
adopter has enacted scope, financing, competent review and payment routes.
Prefer continuation only if workers can actually receive a material gain
without weaker wages, protections or displaced service. Publish the costs,
tradeoffs and uncertainty of each branch. The worked values set no national
profit rate, group threshold, employer charge or expected outcome.
