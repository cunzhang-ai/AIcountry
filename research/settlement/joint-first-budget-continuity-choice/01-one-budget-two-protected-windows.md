# One first budget and two protected windows

Constructed comparison. Cash is in units. Each new verified income
claim costs one. A food package counts only upon safe receipt.
Keep 10 units of standing general duties in each window, including
independently staffed review, care and existing payments. The three
following pressure points are additional to that baseline. General
cash is settled at 24 and 18 respectively. The second figure is a
newly available amount and assumes zero carryover from the first
selected window. A restricted food reserve starts with ten suitable
packages and ten settled delivery units, with no timely replenishment.
Each reserve delivery costs one and each window has accepted capacity
for six. Ordinary food deliveries supply two and one respectively,
from their already financed source.

The full budget fails in the stipulated vote. The exact water
continuation receives a separate valid majority and can legally bind
by the supplier's deadline. Its first cost is four. Two more are
needed under a separate confirmed successor authority in window two.
The ordinary case team can handle four new claims per window after
standing work. A valid income appropriation can buy four distinct
slots for two in window one and one slot for one in window two.
These staff do not supply the standing or independent review shifts.
Income eligibility is unchanged. Eight and five claims are due.

Deliver six reserve packages plus one ordinary package for seven of seven total. |

Select the third response for this case. General headroom is zero
in both windows. The first uses four restricted delivery units and
four reserve packages. Six of each remain for window two. The pilot
needs a separately funded and qualified choice. The comparison makes
no finding about the pilot's value on another budget.

If window two general cash is 17, the selected package is one unit
short. Keeping the specified water and standing duties leaves at
most four of five timely income receipts with the available choices.
If no separately qualified slot arrives, the result is also at most
four even with 18 cash units. A two-package safety hold leaves five
of seven food receipts even when the full general budget settles.
If water has no operative authority, its unspent cash is not proof
that water is delivered or that another use can bind. Keep each
original date, the actual unmet duty and the remedy. Do not treat
this example as an allocation priority in a real conflict.

This is an incremental comparison with no inherited fiscal estimate
from the separate food, lapse or recession cases. Actual national
baselines, cash, prices, staffing and all other sector obligations
require their own common-source qualification.
