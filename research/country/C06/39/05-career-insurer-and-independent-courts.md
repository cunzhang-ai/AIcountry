# A career insurer and independent courts

Design 99 · Proposed research, not enacted law or funded bank protection.

**RA07 — A professional insurer with a named employer.** A separate statutory deposit insurer
administers M07/M08, not general insurance business. One protected-career executive is appointed by
a reasoned Cabinet decision from C04 open job-related competition and a published qualified public
shortlist, with usable objections, conflicts and direct ordinary-court challenge. This is ordinary
career tenure under C04, not another six-year policy chief/board or ministerial pleasure
appointment. Require demonstrated depositor-rights, protected records, accountable claims/payment
administration and public-fund competence, with equivalent relevant experience permitted and no
guild/vendor/party veto. No simultaneous bank management/control, resolution-panel vote, prudential
finding, monetary decision or Treasury release role. Actual financial/prior-case conflicts require
disclosure, recusal/divestment and independent review.

Cabinet is the executive's appointing/disciplinary authority, only for proved C04 misconduct,
persistent material job-related/statutory failure or inability after accommodation, with
notice/reply, protected career tenure, reasoned decision and direct ordinary-court interim/appeal
protection; inconvenient due payouts, lawful dissent, cabinet change or refusal of an illegal
instruction is no ground. No minister may remove/reassign for a particular depositor/bank case.
Executive recruits/assigns/disciplines insurer staff under C04, with prior
qualification/rotation/conflict rules and separate statutory claims decision versus payment
execution; no premium-paying bank owns the applicant pool or claims appeal. No personal officer
quota rewards denied claims or small public draw.

**RA08 — Insurer career powers, even with no executive or panel.** The enacted M07 rule assigns
deposit-unavailability finding, individual beneficiary/coverage determination and authorization of
actually due M07 payments directly to preassigned qualified statutory claims officers, with
predesignated consenting qualified case substitutes for actual conflicts/inability; not to a
panel/head/Treasury permission or a discretionary bailout vote. A distinct payout officer verifies
payee/available lawful source, executes and checks credit, never redecides entitlement or demands
new head approval. Investigation/coverage dispute preserves undisputed portions, safe source access
and direct court relief. Prudential/resolution/monetary/bank original facts are evidence, not a veto
on receiving the report or insurer decision. An unsupported suspicion, identity app failure,
industry pressure, unopened panel or no-M09 critical-function case does not stop the statutory
claims route.

The financial selection's separate M07 rule must state the precise trigger/T0, short decision and
payment clocks: proposed qualified officer reasons within6actual hours of a credible recorded
referral/own evidence, essential advance of min(one B, undisputed covered balance) within24actual
hours from T0, credited once toward the remaining covered amount due≤seven working days from T0. A
court may independently determine the objective unavailability date/omission, without pretending M09
approval. Backdated T0 exposes overdue payment; it never fictionalizes on-time delivery, silently
resets a clock or treats a mere technical outage as bank inability. B, ordinary statutory coverage
and actual M08 funding retain their separate law. Six-hour on-call decision requires costed paid
qualified coverage/source/counsel, not an actual roster inferred from this duty. Useful protection
is earlier where law requires; no six-hour permission to wait through known urgent harm.

Before executive vacancy, a C04 career deputy is designated through open competition. At
vacancy/suspension deputy has≤180elapsed days routine funded management only, no new policy or
discretionary senior appointments, then no acting executive power; relabelling cannot reset it. Own
statutory claims/payment duties continue throughout and immediately if no qualified deputy exists.
First funded nonmanagerial insurer case/payout appointments use an express prior Act's existing C04
Cabinet/career recruitment channel before executive entry; they cannot appoint a permanent executive
by silence or change coverage. Actual lawful appointing decision, qualified staff, consent and pay
remain unknown. Absence of personnel means unperformed duties/direct courts and retained claims, not
invented staffing or delayed statutory dates.

**RA09 — Independent court finding is not a resolution vote.** Depositors/beneficiaries may apply
directly to an unconflicted ordinary court against omitted/delayed M07 finding, covered share or
insurer/public-payer nonexecution without panel permission or futile internal exhaustion. Court
independently determines the specific statutory insurance ground/date/entitlement on answerable
protected sources and orders the named existing insurer/claims/payer officers to act within actual
law, with B17 urgent consideration≤24hours/earlier useful time and paid advice/interpretation. It
neither requires M09's critical-function test nor creates a structural bridge, write-down, levy, new
guarantee or foreign-currency delivery right. No actual case record or available competent judge
means unmet remedy, not presumed unavailability or a fictitious panel quorum. Operational outage
without insurer financial trigger still retains M06/SF and other actually lawful protection, not a
broadened insured-bank failure by analogy.

**RA10 — M09 interference, valuation and own-office review.** Affected owners, creditors, clients,
workers and depositors receive M11 direct ordinary review of competence, entry, critical-function
necessity, source valuation, treatment and actual remedy. Panel gives immediate notice/court access
and meaningful judicial hearing within72actual hours, no serial reset or weaker administrative
rehearing. Prior-law narrow urgency never creates immunity, suppresses defense or renews a
termination stay. Court may stop unlawful acts, require fresh lawful decision, order feasible
correction or M11 compensation, with third-party safety/rights; irreversible harm does not convert
compensation into automatic immunity or promise mechanical reversal of every accepted transfer.

For contested M11 liquidation-counterfactual assessment, an unconflicted ordinary court appoints an
actually consenting qualified valuer from its prospectively published open competence/conflict
rotation, with job-related equivalent practice, disclosed remuneration/sponsors, protected original
sources and party challenge/counterevidence. The panel/receiver/insurer supplies records but cannot
choose the final compensation valuer or suppress their unfavorable report. A real paid roster,
available advisers and relevant jurisdictional/technical competence must exist; no imported expert
or next name is presumed available. If qualification/conflicts exhaust it, disclose missing evidence
and seek a lawful qualified appointment with due process, not a self-selected AI valuation. Court
finally determines liability/quantum; M10 source hierarchy and M11 no-extraordinary-rescue
comparison remain. Expert cost and compensation-source cash are separate claims.

**RA11 — No protector self-approval or borrowed judicial formation.** Resolution-panel
appointments/removal/recusal, its own funding/procurement/data and insurer executive/claims/records
conduct have direct unconflicted ordinary-court review under B16's ordinary60working-day
usable-notice/blocked-access protection, B17 urgency and distinct ordinary appeal; concealed
fraud/material conflict remains hearable. An office does not finally adjudicate its own wrongdoing
or control sole records/keys/intake. Existing independent audit, ombud, integrity and competent
prosecution routes preserve their different lawful powers; neither allegation nor model agreement
proves fault. Actual judicial assignment/recusal excludes personal pay/participation/sponsor
conflicts; diffuse public fiscal interest is not universal disqualification. CJ retains its
constitutional component under actual scope; no CJ12 special own-court chamber, EA electoral
authority or markets chief becomes a financial tribunal. Ordinary court insurance/U powers are
explicit; parallel administrative M09 property entry by a judge is not selected. If all actual
forums/sources are captured or conflicted, publish safe unmet-remedy reasons, not invented
independence.
