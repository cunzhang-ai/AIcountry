# Same people and services, different income arrangements

*Design 58 one-year partial launch reference; all prices, wages and social arrangements are authored.*

The fixture retains 100,000 residents: 20,000 children, 65,000 working-age
adults and 15,000 assumed eligible older residents. It does not select a
pension age or create a founding electorate. Working-age monthly gross
earnings are zero for 10,000; 1,000 for 15,000; 2,500 for 20,000; 5,000
for 15,000; and 10,000 for 5,000. The represented wage total is 2,280 million
CU annually, including public/private wages without a second public wage bill.

Twenty thousand declared parent/child pairs have support received of zero
for 6,000 children, 400 for 6,000 and 800 for 8,000. The 105.6-million annual
private support flow is debited to parents and received by children, not
added as GDP or a public receipt. Five thousand further declared couples
pair a high earner with an adult receiving no controlled resources. Other
people are singly assessed in the fixture. Five thousand adults receive
250 monthly cost compensation against the same recognized access cost.
These counts are not survey observations or assumptions about real families.

Compare A, a pooled needs assessment; B, the selected individual-resource
assessment; and C, a universal personal base plus targeted expense support.
All retain services, full basic older payments, the same tax/contribution
assumptions and actual support. A real safely pooled system could add
individual escape protection; its costs and disclosure burden need fresh
assessment. This pooled test instead exposes the effect of deeming a
partner's income available when the individual has not received it.

| High-price reference arrangement | Annual general transfers, million CU | Partial launch cash gap, million CU |
|---|---:|---:|
| A pooled | 752.7 | 686.7 |
| B individual | 830.7 | 764.7 |
| C universal base | 1,230.6 | 1,164.6 |

The 78-million difference between A/B is exactly 5,000 adults' independent
1,300-monthly protection. It is an authored value/resource conflict, not
proof every partner controls money or that pooling is always cheaper.
A pooled arrangement with genuinely usable person-level protection remains
a competitor, as does a differently taxed universal arrangement.

## What the fiscal substitution actually says

Policy begins in old reference year 2 with cash 150, debt 630 and interest
31.5 million CU. Preserve service envelopes 774 and capital 60. The new
represented wage-tax schedule yields 316.5; other tax receipts remain an
assumed 505, giving partial receipts 821.5. The historical 410 personal-tax
line is replaced, not also retained. Borrowing 60 and usable cash draw 50
are the old assumed limits; refinancing 100 cancels matching maturity.
Thus B outlays 1,696.2 against receipts plus financing 931.5, leaving 764.7.
The substituted wage-tax line is not a causal reform estimate or a complete
capital/land/corporate/VAT incidence account. No behavioral or price forecast
updates the other receipts. General administration beyond the envelope
and all missing national costs stay unknown, not zero.

Employee contributions total 228 but are restricted to insurance. They are
not additional general floor funds. The included branch assumes old 45
basic pension, 15 cost compensation and 40 needs support fit inside the new
payments; this requires source/person/period proof in a real transition.
An unclassified 40 retained separately raises B's gap to 804.7. A separate
branch treats 45 as earned pensions, preserves them, and separately accounts
for received pension and restricted insurance funds; unknown remaining
insurance liabilities do not become a free surplus.

Under 10% loss of represented/general receipts plus 20 extra support, B's
gap becomes 866.85. Earlier care and institutional staffing losses remain
distinct physical constraints; this new ledger does not re-price or cure them.
A lower **unqualified** personal basket 500 and housing 200 still leaves
B short by 263.85. Lowering an unmeasured standard is not proof of adequate
life or a rights-preserving adjustment. No arrangement is established as
affordable, operable or superior by this exercise.
