# T91–T94. Requalify the first programme through the next budget

13 November 2026 · Integrated design 441 · Candidate first-budget rules

Apply [T85–T90](19-joined-opening-budget-and-release.md) and the
[forward budget](../../C06/62/08-forward-budget-and-shared-commitments.md)
to a common closing account and renewal choice.

**T91 — Start the next budget from the qualified closing position.**
Join first-year outturn to the next year's sources and obligations
by legal payer and due date. Preserve restricted funds, arrears,
still-binding guarantees, repayment schedules, earned rights and
open remedies. Where outturn is pending, label the assumed closing
position and show alternatives for material deviations. Separate
remaining transition work, recovery backlog and recurring service.
Any saving enters only when its qualified capacity or cash is
available for lawful use. Keep the applicable vote and expiry rules.

**T92 — Show the first date on which a recurring offer fails.**
Price ordinary work, independent judicial and electoral functions,
repayment, staffing and qualified receiving in the same windows.
Compare exact starts against early and late settlements, weak
recurring income and overlap with the old route. Give separately
visible gaps for current payments and for restoration of the
lawful minimum. A year-end surplus cannot cure an earlier failure.
Check the next independent service window before binding a
discretionary early stage. A later budget retains its lawful choice.

**T93 — Price a timed solution and its repayment on the same path.**
Compare ordinary timely receipts, lawful prefinancing or reserve,
a changed maturity schedule, and a funded smaller or deferred
option. Identify who owns each source, its carrying cost, any
new burden and the repayment date. Recheck the original service
and successor duties after the draw, after repayment and under
a repeated delay. Temporary liquidity supports an exact window.
A lasting service needs a recurring source and paid delivery.
Preserve valid claims even when no proposed new option qualifies.

**T94 — Let elected renewal reflect actual receiving.** Show
both costs of waiting and measured outcomes of earlier work.
Provide affected people and opposition a usable comparison
and independent correction. Retain the separate Treasury,
operator and review roles. AI can identify a crossing of dates
or double-counted funds. It cannot renew an appropriation,
defer a lawful payment or certify a service. Reopen on missing
resources, repeated bridging, accumulating arrears, or evidence
that another lawful package performs better.
