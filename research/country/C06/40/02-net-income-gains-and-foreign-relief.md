# TA05–TA07: genuine net income, realized gains and foreign relief

*Part of [C06/40](../40-tax-law-and-accountable-revenue.md); design100 research candidate, not enacted law.*

**TA05 — Net personal business and capital cost.** A natural person's business includes actually received sales/fees and deducts evidenced costs **actually paid for goods/services used in current or earlier taxable activity** (a prepayment for future use carries to its use period; the same cost is deducted once), including worker pay, purchased inputs, rent, energy and necessary accessibility; mixed personal use receives a reasoned allocable share. Ordinary consumption, gifts, income tax itself, debt principal, punitive fines and fictitious invoices are not business costs. A necessary work expense counted in personal-business net income is not also entered a second time as an I05 necessary-cost increase; true separately reimbursed costs are matched to the original expense once. A capital asset's purchase is not also an operating expense: acquire its documented basis and deduct depreciable equipment/software in five equal annual portions from first actual business use, buildings in 25 equal annual portions, and no land depreciation; on sale include actual proceeds and deduct only unrecovered adjusted basis, recapturing any duplicated deduction. This five/25-year scheme is an explicit **normative personal-business hypothesis** requiring capital-intensive/small-firm incidence review, not a corporate deduction schedule or proof of a true physical life. Y05 separately determines the actually worked personal-labor component of a sole trader's net receipts and its 10% restricted contribution with evidence/review; deduct only that lawful paid contribution before PA10, never 10% of automatically assumed capital return or a second employer levy. A person's net business loss is never a negative PA10 payment; carry the evidenced remainder against that person's positive net business income for the next five tax years, oldest first, with no wage, U, pension or unrelated-person offset. No transfer to a company/spouse or retrospective reopening to manufacture a source.

**TA06 — Realized personal gains with preserved basis.** For nonbusiness personal property, tax only a completed sale/exchange with actually received enforceable consideration: net gain is received proceeds/value less documented acquisition basis, capital improvements and actual sale cost, with the basis multiplied by the published general CPI ratio from acquisition to disposal **not below the original nominal basis**. Convert actual foreign acquisition/disposal amounts at their respective dated published rates; never claim foreign cash is available from a paper domestic conversion. A genuine gift or inheritance is not a PA11 receipt for the giver/recipient merely on transfer; documented carryover basis follows the asset, and any transfer/inheritance tax requires its separate enacted base. A disguised sale, labor payment or controlling-party extraction is classified by proved substance with independent challenge, not label alone. Realized capital losses offset that person's realized positive gains in the same year, then carry forward five years against gains only, not wages or pension; no refundable loss credit or mark-to-market tax. This reference selects no special primary-home gain exclusion: an actually realized inflation-adjusted gain is taxable, a material housing/mobility objection that should be tested with H and land-value policy before adoption. Missing basis is an evidence/correction question, not automatic zero or full stated price.

**TA07 — Cross-border relief and protected unknowns.** Credit **actually paid** foreign income tax imposed on the same resident-period income, source country and tax year, at most the domestic PA10 tax attributable to that foreign net income by its share of total positive annual taxable income (fraction capped at one, and aggregate credits never above assessed domestic tax); no negative/refundable/transferable credit and no credit for a foreign tax merely assessed but unpaid. One foreign payment is credited once. Later actual qualifying foreign payment can produce relief after the ordinary domestic assessment bar: claim within two years of that foreign payment, with court-controlled B15 blocked-access/diligence toll. Relief is only return of domestic tax **actually paid on the same identified income**, capped by remaining domestic attributable tax and qualifying foreign tax paid; no unrelated base/rate/Y/I05 reopening or time-barred state increase. No absolute longstop is selected for genuinely later foreign payment; disclose this bounded-amount but long-tail contingent refund cost. Preserve lawful minimum original payment/source proof with purpose-limited custody, not an eternal whole-life dossier. No interest accrues on this newly matured relief before its TX08 due date.

A later **actual foreign refund** must be reported within30calendar days and creates only a new matching overcredit-restitution duty, at most the original domestic credit attributable to that refund. Serve its usable final assessment within TX04's four years from that30-day report due date, court-proved concealment actual toll≤two/absolute six; review, principal due and collection follow TX03/TX07/TX12. It cannot reopen the old income/rate/unrelated tax. A paper foreign credit is not received money; original domestic-credit valuation, actual foreign records and treaty rights control the matched amount. No pre-refund interest on this new restitution event; any later lawful interest uses TX09 and the new report due date, not the old income year. A treaty may instead require exemption, different source allocation or another credit method; actual treaty text and foreign tax evidence govern. Distinguish a tax credit from an actual G receipt and an import FX asset. The selected source/period/cost rules require accessible records and paid cross-border help; unknown countries, treaties, rates, taxable ownership, foreign enforcement and resulting yield remain unknown. Nonresident gain/royalty/source exceptions and home-mobility burden are substantive points to reopen, not permission for an officer to tax by analogy.

nonmarketable claim instead uses the expressly selected nominal capital-first

not a tax-free rollover; genuine custody-only transfer carries old basis.

**Design145 acquisition join.** [LA09/LA16](../../C09/13/02-people-valuation-and-replacement.md)
leaves TA06 actual sale/exchange/indexed-basis rules intact: no special home gain
exclusion, tax-free title rollover or double old/new basis. The project pays actual
attributable compulsory-transaction tax cost through its own replacement package,
including any taxable gross-up under the ordinary schedule. Actual paid credit/
remittance, blocked deposits and personal resource capital retain separate meanings;
this is neither a new tax exemption nor new national real revenue.
