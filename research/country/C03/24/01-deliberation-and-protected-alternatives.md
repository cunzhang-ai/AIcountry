# C03. Give a resident panel's competing proposal an elected decision

*Design411 · RDA01–RDA08 · Proposed addition to the existing RD procedure.*

Recording disagreement preserves evidence. An independently reviewable
alternative can also deserve its own elected disposition before a sponsor
combines or replaces the available choices. Give one such alternative
protected time within the existing commission and preserve dissent outside it.

**RDA01 — Reserve one additional disposition inside the original commission.**
The commissioning Assembly records the authorized panel seats, the shared
comparison baseline, the dates, floor slot and funded drafting, evidence and
verification capacity for the main recommendation and one competing text.
Keep RD's authority, question and original outer deadlines. Existing
consultations, initiatives, member amendments and individual rights retain
their own routes. No resident panel replaces the elected legislature or
decides the individual rights of people outside it.

**RDA02 — Qualify an exact, materially different proposal.** An alternative
must answer the commissioned choice within scope and explain what would
change, for whom, on what legal path, by when and with what funding or
explicit financing decision still required. Specify its treatment of
continuing rights and its actual dependencies and losses. Test on the same
baseline as the main recommendation. A genuine uncertain cost or a separate
required appropriation can be stated openly. A promise that conceals its
necessary authority, unresolved protected duty or missing implementing act
is incomplete. A panel majority, sponsor or analyst cannot exclude a text
because it rejects their policy preference.

**RDA03 — Count support for consideration by actual people.** By the
published cutoff within RD's report period, an alternative needs explicit
support from at least one quarter of the commissioned resident seats,
rounded up. Freeze the denominator at commissioning; vacancies and
abstentions are not support. Each sitting resident may designate one exact
alternative for this route after assisted access and a meaningful chance
to inspect it. A timely replacement takes the same seat and supplies their
own verified expression. Support requests a disposition and does not bind
a policy preference. Do not count agent copies, unsolicited signatures
or document edits as renewed support. Preserve confidential verification
and published safe totals. If several qualify, the highest verified
support receives the one protected slot; a prepublished auditable lot
resolves a verified tie. Keep other material reasons and text visible.

**RDA04 — Give competing authors useful and independent support.** Allocate
paid drafting, interpretation, accessible timing, source access and a real
right to correct each rendering. The Assembly service gives common legal,
budget and service evidence with the same dates and uncertainty standards.
Give each author group a usable response to objections and access to
independent correction. A sponsor's refusal, inaccessible evidence or
a failed facilitator cannot be treated as a weak substantive case.
Use the applicable RD and FA remedies before the original deadline.
Keep out-of-panel affected accounts and smaller-group harms on the record
under their own protections, including when they lack threshold support.

**RDA05 — Dispose of each exact text in the protected floor process.** Within
the RD04 deadline the Assembly records a distinct vote to take forward or
reject the main recommendation and then the qualified competing text, using
the reserved sitting and any lawful resumption. Members may
support either, both, or neither. Publish exact texts, named votes, reasons
and comparison before recording a disposition on a materially revised
substitute for the commissioned choice. A take-note motion, a sponsor's
summary or a vote on a merged text cannot satisfy either exact disposition.
If both texts advance and conflict, a later binding act must resolve the
conflict through ordinary elected law, budget and sector authority.
Neither disposition enacts a law, moves a payment or grants a permit.

**RDA06 — Repair omission without acquiring a veto.** Preserve source records
and provide a direct timely correction request to the Assembly service,
a lawfully preassigned alternate presider and the existing RD05 review
route. Check actual receipt and execution of a correction. A material
alternative text change needs fresh support for that version within the
original process. If qualification or the vote cannot be lawfully completed,
record the specific failure and any available remedy. Preserve the main
recommendation and its ordinary protected disposition. A missed procedural
duty neither counts as a vote nor gives a panel or reviewer authority over
the elected decision. Preserve urgent prior-law action, continuing rights
and the original dates. Report an actual missed opportunity.

**RDA07 — Follow the elected response into law and receiving.** Publish what
the Assembly took forward, what later changed, any enacted authority or
appropriation, who delivers, and the actual receipt or unresolved failure.
Keep ordinary representative, affected-person and independent challenge
rights. AI may render participant accounts with their correction or compare
stated costs. It cannot identify genuine assent, choose the alternative,
collapse materially different reasons or certify a public mandate.

**RDA08 — Keep the rule bounded and replaceable.** Pay for the added
disposition and support within the commission's stated scope. Compare a
published dissent and response, one protected alternative and ordinary
member sponsorship on actual agenda time, public influence, excluded
voices and execution. Adopt prospectively and preserve prior earned pay,
live notices, custody and outstanding remedies. Reopen on systematic
fragmentation, inaccessible thresholds, tactical delay, displaced ordinary
hearings or a simpler route giving equivalent influence and timely decisions.
