# ED16–ED20. Pay the school, protect the transition, keep alternatives real

*Part of [ED public learning](../21-public-learning-and-school-power.md).* 

**ED16 — Inspect proportionately, not continuously.** Existing education/NC
inspectors check actual present competence, learning/access/safety, exclusions,
protected staff/learner voice and paid receiving. Use safe relevant observation,
records and confidential complaints; disclose missing groups and uncertainty.
No universal camera, compulsory AI profile, public child trace or ED private-
home entry/medical examination/data-merge power. Actual police/child-protection/
clinical grounds retain independent law. Staff receive fair notice/reply and
review; safe complainants need no unsafe parent/principal confrontation. Do
not import an old recommendation's medical or complaint gate as domestic power.

**ED17 — A public source funds independence and actual time.** Assembly source/
national need-adjusted grants and elected local budgets fund qualified teachers,
heads, support/inspection staff and outside-review access, sparse/high-need
capacity, languages/disability, buildings, materials/meals, actual travel,
non-AI tools, safeguarding, preparation/assessment/learning/voice time and paid
substitution/overlap. Offered pupil hours are team capacity, not an individual
teacher contact quota. TW breaks/rest/leave/learning persist;30 pupil hours do
not exhaust paid preparation or supply32 free task hours. NC
joint periods/inspection count once with actual competence. HN/AS/public safety
cannot lose a worker merely because a school needs cover. The151/159 paid-time
bill,37 original functions and old bids are not prices for these new duties.
All actual included/incremental amounts, simultaneous qualified people, source
cash/FX, independent receiving and outcomes remain unknown, not zero.

**ED18 — Preserve existing entitlements and lawful competence.** S05–S08/NC,
B21–B23/UA, clinical/child/PD, C04/E/AP, WL/TW, local DR/LR, source-law and
stronger school protections continue. This creates no new attendance crime,
compulsory treatment, general parental incapacity, political-age change or
professional licence. An inspector may correct a lawful access violation;
it cannot appropriate tax, rewrite binding curriculum or take over unrelated
local powers. Treasury does not certify educational merits or extend a due date.
Actual shortage/noncompliance/unlawful refusal/conflicted authority are distinct;
report failed delivery without a fictional receiver or automatic emergency rule.

**ED19 — Prospective organization with paid successors.** A coordinated Act
states the actual new employer/appointment/inspection/ED site-order method
start: first1January at least12calendar months after enactment. Exact pending
cases/new orders/old workforce/records/source/counsel/executor successors and
already stronger rules are named; no employer changes by a website notice.
Maintain service through the actual school/alternative calendar; no mass
reappointment or lesson-calendar reset is implied. Existing
claims/term dates and NC180-day quality, TW36/32, Y/RF, election/court/force/
source clocks are not advanced, paused or reset. OP/T pays employer/worker/
pension/contract/creditor/property/privacy and record overlap until lawful
accepted handover; no forced private transfer or ministerial readiness veto.
A missed enacted date is a failure requiring lawful remedy/elected response,
not automatic cancellation or authority for an unqualified substitute.

**ED20 — This form is provisional.** Compare strong existing local repair,
parent choice/voucher, autonomous community/worker-governed schools, direct
national schools and deeper learner self-government with the same rights,
public costs, staff freedom, child voice and practical alternatives. A lighter
arrangement may serve a small place better. Retain the local public backbone/
plural methods, concrete career/inspection chain and no mandatory extra board;
reject loyalist control and exclusion of the entitlement. Central capture,
professional closure, parent domination, weak inspection, expensive due process,
insufficient staff and ineffective learning remain objections. Normative sources
and written adverse paths prove no learning/wellbeing/civic improvement. Reopen
on actual learner/teacher experience, independently traceable outcomes/costs,
rights failure or a better whole-country choice. Next integrated ordinary/
adverse paid operation and substantive rival comparison, not an automatic
school-rule or university-detail chain; no study grants itself authority.
