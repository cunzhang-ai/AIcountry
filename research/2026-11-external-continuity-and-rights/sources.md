# Design504 sources and scope

- The United Nations treaty text of the [Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties](https://legal.un.org/ilc/texts/instruments/english/conventions/1_1_1969.pdf),
  notably articles 26, 27 and 70, distinguishes performance,
  internal law and the effect of termination where applicable.
  Article 73 leaves succession questions outside this Convention's
  prejudgment. This record reaches no jurisdiction-specific result.
- The [European Commission's EU–UK Withdrawal Agreement overview](https://commission.europa.eu/strategy-and-policy/relations-united-kingdom/eu-uk-withdrawal-agreement_en)
  and [citizens' rights explanation](https://commission.europa.eu/strategy-and-policy/relations-united-kingdom/eu-uk-withdrawal-agreement/citizens-rights-0_en)
  document an actual negotiated transition, defined individual rights
  and country-specific monitoring and legal remedies. That example
  neither sets the candidate's rights nor proves that every person
  promptly received the intended protection.
- The [cross-border pension case](../settlement/cross-border-pension-rights/01-two-careers-and-one-delayed-payment.md),
  [treaty comparison](../settlement/democratic-treaties/README.md)
  and [opening payment case](../settlement/opening-payment-access/01-fund-receipt-before-changing-currency.md)
  support distinct legal and operating boundaries. The claims,
  dates, budgets and receipts in Design504 are constructed.
