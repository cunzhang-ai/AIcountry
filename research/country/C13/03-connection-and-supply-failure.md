# C13. Communications and supply failure in one week

*Part of [C13 lived experience](../C13-lived-experience.md); integrated design 55 research, not an observed outcome.*

An invented storm disables a regional mobile backhaul and grid feed while a
bridge to the only cold store is closed. A clinic needs medicine, a polling
place must send count evidence, and people waiting for benefit and court
notices cannot use the usual online route. One alternate generator and a
small number of drivers are also claimed by water pumping and food delivery.

C09's connectivity steward must show which physical paths, fuels, spare
parts and trained crews remain. Election offices keep authenticated local
ballots and their prior-law reconciliation route; a network operator cannot
certify a winner or erase a vote because transmission failed. The clinic's
service authority finds a lawful supplier and receiving site, and the
transport steward tests a road, rail or local carrier route using actual
vehicles, cold-chain capacity and workers. Postal or in-person legal notice
follows enacted continuity rules; a missing electronic receipt does not
extinguish an individual claim.

If the same driver, generator or fuel has already been assigned twice, C06's
prior-law scarcity plan permits only bounded first-hour safety action. The
competent elected authority decides any new material cross-sector priority
with unmet duties, affected people and review visible. C10's emergency
coordination may aid communication and movement but cannot create a new
surveillance power, tax or party preference. Operators record actual
arrival, not a modelled route or a dispatched vehicle as delivery.

This exercise does not establish the number of needed towers, trucks or
people, the legal priority in a real emergency, or resilience of any actual
country. A real test must inspect original assets and contracts, alternative
routes, rights-holders, staff and fuel, lawful authority and received goods
and notices. Reopen the design if a supposedly independent fallback shares
its backhaul, bridge, vendor or controller with the failed primary route.

[OC continuity](../C09/20-essential-service-outage-continuity.md) now
starts the funded alternate before the last safe start and tests its
usable reserve, staff and access against the same failure. It treats
actual water, care and public remedy as separate delivery results.

[The Design223 medical supply case](../../settlement/democratic-treaties/02-medical-supply-renewal-and-exit.md)
compares notice, renewal, actual foreign payment and a safe domestic
receiving path. A legal end date and first available supply are
separate conditions.

[The Design224 shared-core case](../../settlement/communications-access/02-locality-and-shared-failure.md)
counts household access and simultaneous emergency receipt separately. It
leaves the uncovered last-mile demand and budget explicit.
