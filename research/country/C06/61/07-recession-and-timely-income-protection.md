# Make income protection payable when claims rise and revenue falls

*MS27–MS32. Candidate operating rules for standing income protection in a downturn.*

**MS27 — Keep personal rights and operating triggers separate.** The
ordinary income and earned-support authorities receive, decide and pay
under existing source law, including the earlier hardship protection.
No national recession declaration or staffing alert starts a person's
right or changes its amount. The adopting budget sets operational
activation rules for funded surge capacity. Use dated evidence of
attempted intake, new needs, pending cases, due payments, cash
and usable staff. Include blocked intake and abrupt local or sector
losses that an aggregate indicator can miss. Keep a direct route
where official statistics or payroll evidence arrive late.

**MS28 — Preauthorize cash and casework on one calendar.** Before the
ordinary budget, Treasury and the service owner compare continuing
operations, a bounded cash-only response and a funded joint reserve.
State which payments and work are additional, the gross source
and actual due-date liquidity, authority ceilings and the last
useful hiring, release and settlement times. Price qualified intake,
disputed evidence, adjudication, safe payment, accommodation,
independent correction and second-window receiving. Reserve a named
payer and accepted staff time with lawful origin cover. Keep old
claims, restricted assets and other essential obligations separate.
A future loan or a clinician, clerk or reviewer already needed
elsewhere supplies no second resource.

**MS29 — Activate the smallest complete response in time.** Under an
enacted schedule the named service owner activates exact trained
coverage and Treasury releases the authorized cash against the same
scope and deadline. Preserve established payments. For unresolved
claims, use the applicable provisional protection and urgency rules
on individual evidence. Keep a staffed and reachable non-digital
route. If only part of the package is available, preserve lawful
partial delivery, record the specific unpaid or undecided duty and
seek urgent budget or ordinary correction. Capacity ceilings limit
discretionary commitments. They do not cap statutory eligibility or
create a new queue priority. Statutory dates and independent challenge
remain reachable.

**MS30 — Keep speed, accuracy and receipt distinct.** Use verified
information already lawfully held, proportionate alternative proof
and assisted intake. Track attempted and completed intake,
first lawful decisions, first safe receipt, unresolved claims,
inaccurate payments, corrections and useful-time review separately.
Include residents who cannot use employer records, a digital account
or ordinary payment credentials. The competent official decides
disputed scope. No shared error flag or AI estimate can deny,
rank or close a claim by itself. Correct mistakes under E08 with
notice, review and protected subsistence. A predicted recovery
cannot fund the first payment.

**MS31 — Make the next funding decision before the reserve expires.**
Reconcile actual tax and contribution receipt, continuing benefit
cost, gross borrowing settlement, remaining cash and released staff
on the next due dates. Show the margin for a renewed shock. Compare a
separately financed minimum continuation, ordinary recurring correction
and other enacted sources. The Assembly votes on any new authority.
Do not renew an unchanged emergency package merely because the first
worked. Temporary staffing ends under its own terms with funded
successors, accepted work and a completion date. Expiry does not
remove due benefits, unpaid awards or access to review.

**MS32 — Give legislators and residents an independent account.**
Publish safe dates, forecast ranges, actual first receipt, people
unserved and other work displaced. Independent fiscal review checks
the source and repeated exposure. Ordinary reviewers and courts
protect existing rights and follow orders through payment and
receipt. Preserve original dates, decisions, staff obligations and
lawful successors through a budget or government change. AI may
compare demand and roster scenarios with inspectable assumptions.
It cannot declare a recession for legal purposes, appropriate cash,
determine eligibility or settle a claim. Reopen if demand lasts
longer, the joint reserve displaces another protected duty, or a
simpler programme can pay the same people with less burden.
