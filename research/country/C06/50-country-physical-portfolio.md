# C06. A country needs paid food, water and power in the same place and season

2 November 2026 · Design 127 · Conditional research design, not authorized works

Retain plural resource-fit production, public/common control of new core networks,
independent safety review and democratic choice of burdens. **Displace the fixed
mixed domestic-share expansion as the default numerical example.** In this authored
context, use a water-constrained new-production reference with paid imports;
keep import-heavy and larger domestic-production packages as genuine alternatives.
No national crop quota, official diet or technical rationing power is enacted.
