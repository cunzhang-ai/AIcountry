# Design370 decision on combined debt recovery and essential income

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design369 and the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md). Use B17–B20,
U03/U05–U07, B30, I and HL for protected means and separate creditor,
support and resident duties. TX09–TX12 keeps valid stays, recovery and
outer periods. These rules address parts of recovery. A wage deduction,
a bank instruction and a public offset can still rely on the same money.

[DCR01–DCR06](../country/C08/13/07-combined-debt-recovery-and-essential-income.md)
selects a common protected balance for compulsory recovery from a person's
covered usable income. A civil execution service verifies and reserves
limited instructions. The court retains contested facts, rights and
urgent remedies. Current support has the first claim on available excess,
with an independent source needed for any lawful public bridge. The
Assembly retains the needs schedule, old-claim ranking and actual source.

## Alternatives and objections

Independent channel limits are cheaper to administer and share less data.
They can over-collect across channels, even with correct local execution.
A common limit needs protected evidence and funded clerks but can preserve
both an actual living floor and continuing rights. A structural route
would prohibit compulsory collection from recurrent protected sources
more broadly and pursue other assets or insolvency. It can simplify
administration and reduce exposure, with lower creditor recovery and
potential changes in credit access. Where recurring excess is inadequate,
the existing bounded insolvency route remains a real alternative.

A fragile common account may suppress valid support or invite surveillance.
Limit it to a specific live period and claim purpose. Give affected
people a direct court route with protected evidence and actual execution.
Do not force partners to pool property or collect from a child's funds.
The choice protects each resident's necessities while leaving shortfalls
for a support recipient visible. Current support, delayed public income,
civil awards and tax recovery each affect distinct people and budgets.
No current public bridge or affordable credit effect has been established.

## Evidence, migration and next action

The [primary comparators](sources.md) document narrower caps, priority
and deposit protection. The [worked case](../settlement/combined-debt-recovery-and-essential-income/01-one-pay-cycle-and-three-claims.md)
tests shared income, a lower second wage, a stayed claim and a late
return. These results support a scoped research selection. Actual
qualification, participation, consent, funding and delivery are unknown.

Migrate existing orders prospectively after notice, record matching and
verification of accepted replacements. Keep amounts already lawfully
received, rights, rank, appeals, applicable limitation dates and stronger
exemptions. A new instruction gives no second recovery. Review the law
governing each legacy instruction before disabling its prior route.

The next bounded exercise would compare two accepted payment channels,
one child-support claim and a protected deposit using synthetic records.
Civil execution, independent judges and payment owners would check
duplicate prevention, accessible challenge and delivered return by the
bill deadline. End with actual timed observations and attributed costs
before any live commencement. Reopen on unaffordable enforcement, missed
support, discriminatory effects, repeated overcollection or simpler
equivalent protection.
