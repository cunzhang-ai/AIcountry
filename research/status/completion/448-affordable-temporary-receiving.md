# Design540 completion finding

The common housing design now protects residents from the uncovered charge
in a publicly arranged placement that depends on unsettled income support.
The housing payer may contract for that amount within exact enacted limits.
Actual received income, safe arrival, resident choice, accepting care and
the next affordable period remain separately verifiable.
[Canonical rule](../../country/C09/25/06-affordable-temporary-receiving.md)

The constructed case distinguishes one period of funded protection from a
second unsupported promise. It also distinguishes a funding limit from
the loss of the last safe room. Actual rates, public authority, provider
enforceability, participatory review and observed outcomes remain open.
