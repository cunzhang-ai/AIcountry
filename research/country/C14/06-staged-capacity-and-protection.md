# C14. Build capacity without suspending rights or democracy

T01–T36 · Proposed transition rules, not enacted law

The country needs an attainable route from inherited institutions to its
chosen design. A future end state cannot pay today's construction bill,
qualify a worker, receive an import or justify postponing an election.
Select **protected, reversible stages under ordinary elected authority**.
Retain the existing income-floor research choice; this package neither
adopts new tax rates nor creates a ministerial power to delay lawful rights.

The [enabling reference](../../settlement/transition/README.md) follows six
construction years and two operating years. It exposes omitted salaries,
maintenance, unavailable foreign payments and delay beyond the accounting
horizon. Its amounts are illustrative; no timeline, engineering project,
grant, tax source or qualification has been verified. A funded narrow
project does not establish the whole country's affordability or better lives.

Compare retaining lawful arrangements with repairs, staged plural supply,
and a state acquisition/direct-provider alternative. These differ in
ownership, incentives, displacement and public power, not just speed.
Reject unconditional simultaneous launch and dependence on later output
as a current funding source. No change remains legitimate where a proposed
transition would worsen security, freedom, time or effective public control.
