## Constructed delayed-transfer case

The earned scheme starts with 9 units legally usable for payment.
Contributions of 3 clear before a 10-unit pension comes due.
Authorized backing of 5, expected in the same window, arrives
in the next. An adopted reserve test in this constructed example
requires 6 after payment. The pension can still be paid. After
payment the account has 2, so the reserve needs 4 of separate
lawful cover if it must be restored before the late transfer.

The care payer separately has 6 units and owes 6. Taking 4 would
leave a 4-unit care shortfall. Choose the protected pension
payment and, if authorized and available, the separate 4-unit
bridge. When backing of 5 settles, repay 4 only where lawful and
where the next due payment and reserve remain covered. The next
payment is 4. With no other flows, repaying all 4 would leave only 3,
so it must wait for a lawful funded repayment schedule. Compare
early appropriated backing against a separately financed standby,
including cost and the next call. If neither qualifies, keep
the reserve shortfall visible while paying the due pension.
Amounts are constructed and do not price RF08 or the country.
