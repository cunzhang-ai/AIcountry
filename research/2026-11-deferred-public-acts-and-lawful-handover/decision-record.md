# Design413 decision. Verify the authority for each deferred public act

## Baseline and affected scope

This AI-assisted selection uses [Design412](predecessor-design-412/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md),
the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and the [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).
HT05 already withdraws expired access and HT13 identifies bindings and
options. C12 protects finite mandates and TPE governs payment uncertainty.
The remaining operating choice is how an action spanning the boundary is
classified at each material stage, especially where an earlier signature
exists, the decisive approval has not occurred or an ordinary payer is
unavailable. Affected parties include recipients, counterparties, civil
servants, independent institutions and elected officeholders.

## Competing arrangements and selected rule

Stop every scheduled public action at handover. This reduces stale
authorization but can interrupt independently owed benefits and wages.
Preserve every previously queued instruction. This avoids some interruption
but can let a prior officeholder continue discretionary government.
Select classification under each source law, current authority for each
new reserved act and separately funded ordinary performance of established
obligations. A dormant or disconnected process cannot create the missing
approval. Preserve scope and original evidence while a material conflict
goes to an independent decision. Use simpler ordinary execution where
it preserves the same control and receipt.

The main objections are unclear timing, qualified record access, split
authority in offline operation and protected third-party reliance. Use
independently held originals, explicit prior nonoverlap and ordinary
remedies. Hold only the affected unsupported step and report actual
service loss. No independent reviewer or actual affected party was secured.

## Public control, compatibility and operation

Lawful predecessor and successor authority follows the constitution and
source law. A service owner preserves the original action and due scope,
qualified operators execute only their own roles, and courts and independent
bodies retain their own review powers. Opposition and affected people can
challenge a lost record, expired authority or failed receiving. Test
appointment, funding, custody and actual execution against obstructing
officeholders or providers. No digital custodian can recognize a government
or turn its own record into authority.

Existing mandates with independent legal bases, valid obligations, court
orders, original due dates, data limits and protected claims carry through
funded succession. Where later authority is required, a new decision takes
effect on its own terms. TPE controls uncertain settlement and reissue.
A signed draft, legal commencement and actual receipt remain distinct.
AI can check sequences, with authority and remedies kept in lawful hands.
