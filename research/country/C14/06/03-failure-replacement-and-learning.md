# Failure response, democratic replacement and correction

*Candidate T09–T12; part of [C14 protected stages](../06-staged-capacity-and-protection.md).*

**T09 — Shortfall is a public burden decision.** Operators promptly report
late cash, unsafe work, missing qualified shifts, unavailable imports and
controlled evidence through accessible channels. Treasury distinguishes cash,
solvency, foreign payment and physical shortages under C06. Use only prior
lawful reserves and substitutes; pause new optional commitments only within
authority and contractual terms. Identify cancellation/defect claims and
continuing wages, benefits, urgent care and court execution. A pause cannot
cancel an accrued claim, fund itself from pensions or silently shift paid
care onto relatives. Cabinet brings coherent protection/funding/sequence
alternatives to an alternate-capable Assembly. New taxes, debt, rationing and
changes in entitlement follow their separate competent routes. If every
fallback fails, the unmet duty, immediate harm and responsible receiver stay
visible; a truthful failure notice is not a delivered remedy.

**T10 — Delay does not hide beyond the horizon.** Report exact missed legal
and physical dates, remaining costs and liabilities through comparable
completion and operating periods. A delayed project may look cheaper within
a fixed eight-year window because maintenance has merely moved into year
nine. Keep its training salaries, completion bills and later operations in
view. A statutory promise missed in transition retains T03–T04 remedies;
permit/safety delay also needs reasons, source evidence and appeal, rather
than automatic safe approval. Where neither route can deliver in useful time,
fund a lawful existing or alternative receiver and expose displaced duties.
An arbitrary six-year timetable is not selected national law.

**T11 — Handover during unfinished work.** Ordinary elected successors can
change future policy within rights and law. The outgoing cabinet transfers
Treasury authority, source records, invoices, procurement powers and civilian
service responsibility through C03–C04; it may not bind later voters through
undisclosed guarantees or project-completion emergency extensions. Record
which contracts legally bind successors and disclose cancellation costs.
Opposition can challenge sponsor control, supplier favoritism or failed
protection through funded political access and independent courts. A reviewer
who refuses, is captured or lacks capacity triggers the lawful alternate
route, not a new self-appointed protector. Technical plans cannot extend an
ended mandate.

**T12 — Correction and practical life.** Publish understandable aggregate
cost, delay, receiving, correction and control findings; protect identifiable
claimants, workers and sensitive records. Preserve source reasoning and
material dissent. Compare actual safety, usable income, time, relationships,
freedom and public power against retain/repair and other social models. Do not
average away exclusion or infer superiority from a completed asset. New
proposals pin dependencies, reopen changed support and receive fresh final
review; a procedure cannot approve itself. Communication can use meetings,
voice, simulations, agents or future media, with interpretation, delegation
and consent kept distinct, usable offline routes and replaceable mediators.
No verified monitoring means no confirmed success. A reasoned no-change or
abandonment of a harmful new project remains a valid result, with lawful
continuity of claims.
