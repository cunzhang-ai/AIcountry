# Inspected financial definitions and the limits of the country book

3 November 2026 · Research interpretation, not fiscal certification or foreign law

[ABS 2015, Statement of sources and uses of cash, 12.3/Table 12.1](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/detailed-methodology-information/concepts-sources-methods/australian-system-government-finance-statistics-concepts-sources-and-methods/2015/12-statement-sources-and-uses-cash/part-b-statement-sources-and-uses-cash),
primary methodology read directly. It separates operating cash, nonfinancial
assets and financial/financing flows; total cash change is not the cash
surplus/deficit measure. Noncash depreciation and in-kind flows are outside
that cash statement. The reference period is 2015, not a current country budget.

[ABS 2015, Consolidation, 3.95–3.103](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/detailed-methodology-information/concepts-sources-methods/australian-system-government-finance-statistics-concepts-sources-and-methods/2015/3-flows-stock-positions-and-accounting-rules/part-h-consolidation),
primary methodology read, including its employer/social-scheme rerouting caveat.
Matched internal elimination leaves a given reporting group's balancing item
unchanged. Sector/unit membership and source/valuation differences matter;
some apparently internal rerouted transactions are not eliminated in GFS.

These are two sections of **one conceptual source family**, not independent
observations or actual qualification of AIcountry. Our account-set exercise
does not implement full GFS institutional boundaries, rerouting, accrual,
expense/capital/asset classification or balance sheets. No statistical label
or ownership permission is inferred from an account name.

Direct IMF GFSM 2014 chapter/manual/about pages returned 403; full IMF text was
not inspected in this turn. Search excerpts helped locate the original ABS
sections but are not claimed as a full manual review. These dated definitions
are used only for the explicit conceptual distinctions above, not called the
latest exhaustive standard or imported law. New events/source meaning require
fresh review; no automatic transfer of earlier final reviews.
