# Information people can choose and contest

Keep the entire lawful field neutrally reachable through paper, accessible local
receiving and usable digital routes. Existing CF/EL already separate primary
list choice from optional person preference and provide the hundred-word guide,
full nominee directory and actual seven-minute presentation/Q&A. A video file
or directory entry does not deliver the forum. Court-proved common control has
its existing exact rules; an allegation or related names cannot merge opponents.

Select plural optional comparison alongside those duties. Voters may choose
their own questions, interpreters, civic publications, discussions or assistants,
request full source-linked answers and compare conflicting accounts. No official
AI decides a shortlist or a mandatory issue taxonomy. A neutral complete index
and direct contestant materials stay available outside any recommendation path;
random/rotating exposure can assist discovery without becoming an admission rule.
Different claims need not be averaged into an approved position.

Shared situations, deliberation and simulations can expose consequences of
policy beyond a text submission. Label observations, authored simulations,
contested interpretations and value choices separately. Preserve a contestant's
answer and unresolved objection when a mediator compresses it. Permit correction,
alternative explanation and a different provider; source convergence is not consent.
No political identity, compulsory query history, ideology profile or agent account
is required to browse, get help, change an assistant or vote in secret.

Public baseline providers remain subject to the existing equal-access, source,
procurement, sponsor-disclosure and review rules. Any extra publicly funded
comparison service needs a lawful published appropriation and open plural access
terms; this research grants neither a ministry monopoly nor private providers
new cash. Disclose material ownership/funding and summary scope; political
views themselves are not official misinformation adjudications. Defamation,
coercion, paid persuasion and data misuse retain their separate actual legal tests.

A twenty-row page and five-hundred-word guide page are costing hypotheses,
not tested accessible layouts. Request segmentation is a sensitivity, not a cap
on free full access or a claimed saving. The all-request printing envelope is
retained. Existing twenty-two librarians keep their ordinary duties; added
helpers need paid training, release, privacy and language/access capability.

Actual testing must let affected people choose questions and reject the frame:
include first-time entrants, noncitizen electors, displaced/absent voters,
minorities and people with different access/language needs, without invented
participation. Examine mistaken choices, discoverability, meaningful disagreement,
source correction, time and free choice; compare full paper/no assistant and
plural methods. Neither page availability, clicks nor a quiz alone proves
understanding, legitimate preference or the ability to replace a government.
