# IL11–IL16: protected review, correction and actual state payment

*Part of [C10/10](../10-civilian-assessment-and-intelligence-limits.md); design102 research selection, not enacted law.*

**IL11 — Classification is an access format.** The Director may mark a precise source identity, method, diplomatic confidence or live protective detail for restricted access only on recorded concrete harm and a least-restrictive alternative. Classification does not establish IL01 grounds, legalize collection, exempt PD inspections, override a court, or make an entire allegation permanently secret. Reassess a particular restriction at least every 90 calendar days while active and annually for archived source protection. A safe summary of mandate, annual task categories, number of tasks, source types, disclosures, corrections, complaints and unfulfilled remedies is published; operational specifics and protected witnesses are withheld only under actual reasoned law. The Assembly committee's protected original access and nongovernment members follow IA, not the Director's discretionary clearance.

**IL12 — Subject notice and access.** PD09 ordinary notice/access/correction applies where NAU retains identifiable material. A specific ongoing lawful operation or person's safety can justify only PD25's particular court-reviewed delay/redaction: order expiry no later than 30 calendar days, fresh necessity for renewal, lawful remainder within seven calendar days after ground ends. IL11 classification alone, an indefinite partner promise or a future possible task is not a delay ground. Safe protected notice may omit a source's identity while giving the person a usable allegation gist and challenge route. PD13 breach notice remains applicable. No claim of permanent “neither confirm nor deny” by NAU label.

**IL13 — Confidential challenge.** The person materially affected by a NAU file, warning or downstream decision, a representative, and a source or worker alleging illegal collection may file confidentially with the PD supervisor or directly with the competent unconflicted ordinary court under B13/B17. A requester identifies a credible connection, not a classified file number; the State must identify its legal task, source category, authorization and actual dissemination to the reviewer. The supervisor can inspect protected originals under PD25; disputed access/conflict goes to court. An inability to reveal all detail publicly does not permit an unchallengeable secret adverse finding under B14. Court procedure supplies a safe gist, protected counsel or other answerable method where needed; if no fair method exists, the State may not use the secret allegation for an adverse individual act.

**IL14 — Orders and execution.** A court may stop unlawful processing or onward disclosure, preserve specific evidence, require corrected warnings and recipient reconsideration, order deletion subject to a narrowly held abuse record, require PD25 notice, and award proved material or nonmaterial loss under the actual B/PD grounds. B17 urgent protection starts in useful time and its 24-hour consideration/72-hour respondent safeguards apply. An actual affected decision remains reviewable in its own forum; NAU cannot immunize its recipient by classification. Judgment names the preassigned NAU records/correction officer for original custody, correction and actual transmission, each recipient for its own reconsideration, and the lawful Treasury payer for compensation, with dates and verification; the Director remains answerable for managerial obstruction but their vacancy cannot stay officer performance. B18/B19 execution and source finance apply within their enacted scope; no court order itself creates a new Treasury overdraft, proxy search power or foreign enforcement. If cash/staff/safe counsel is missing, preserve the claim and disclose the shortfall rather than call the remedy delivered.

For this adopting NAU Act, expressly extend B19’s narrow record/payment execution scope: preassigned qualified B18 court civil-enforcement officers, recruited/assigned/disciplined under the ordinary court service’s protected C04 careers and prior conflict rotation, may execute the court’s **already determined** records/correction/notice/verified-payment duty with separately held source/payment credentials and bounded standing pay. This creates no contested-data search, foreign command, new eligibility, appropriation or political conclusion. It is a new scoped statutory selection, not approval inherited from campaign/tax execution.

The State is the primary payer of its own proved NAU/committee wrong within actual B/PD attribution, fault/causation and compensation law; the claimant need not first collect from an insolvent officer. A named qualified Treasury C04 career judgment payer implements the final award from the expressly enacted bounded state-judgment source within30working days, or an earlier actual useful-time right; Treasury administration is its protected appointing/disciplinary owner, with notice/reply/direct court and preassigned conflicts substitutes. Source shortage is an arrear, not cancelled liability, automatic personal guilt or a new tax/overdraft. Recovery from a culpable official/partner is a separate actual claim. No general safety guarantee or compensation merely because a lawful forecast proved wrong is created.

A source/court/PD correction establishing a material error triggers the preassigned records officer’s actionable correction to every known material recipient in useful time and within24elapsed hours, earlier if harm requires; actual recipient acknowledgement/reconsideration and missed receiving are recorded. Unauthorized recipients also invoke PD13 breach/protective recovery, not a ‘lawful recipients only’ escape. New contrary evidence while facts remain disputed is marked and reviewed promptly; it is not falsely declared a proved correction or ignored until a minister confirms. Each recipient retains its own actual judgment/appeal authority—NAU cannot automatically cancel a ballot, tax or court act by changing a record.


**IL15 — Fault boundary.** Illegality supports civil correction without proving a crime. K24 applies only to its own knowledge, clearly unlawful coercive act and material deprivation elements; other K/VD08 offenses apply only to their expressly defined acts and fault. These clauses add no crime, civil penalty, punishment by analogy or exemption for ministers, analysts or partners. P07's criminal warrant remains a police/prosecutor instrument, not a NAU warrant. LF limits any separately lawful physical act but gives NAU no force authority.
