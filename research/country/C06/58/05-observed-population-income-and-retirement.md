# WF17–WF22. Match people, cash and pension claims before netting costs

3November2026 · Design152 · Part of [WF](../58-country-source-and-capacity-frontier.md)
· A published-reference bridge, not candidate entitlement or source certification

**WF17 — Include the people a source leaves out.** FOLK1A's1January2023
Denmark snapshot counts5,932,654 people, including947,458 aged0–14,
1,213,329 aged65+ and134,115 aged65–66. INDKP201's2023 income population is
4,938,895: persons15+ at year-end registered for the entire year, excluding
in-year migration/deaths. These are different populations and time measures.
Do not equate their difference with one identified missing cohort or use the
income population as the entire country. Candidate children, new residents,
people who die/move, disputed status and protected foreign/old claims retain
their actual source-law needs, periods, tax and successors; a statistical
absence cancels no claim. Quarterly snapshots are not annual recipient-years.

**WF18 — Separate cash from imputation and shared-family indicators.** In
INDKP201, published mean times common population gives approximate2023
proxies: wages1,263.9bnDKK, public transfers328.4, public pensions210.9,
statistically private pensions126.4, income/labor-market taxes641.1 and
disposable income1,380.9. Imputed owner rent143.7 is included in that last
proxy; it is not received cash. Published rounded means do not reveal exact
totals, individual joint records, tax bases or safe resource access.
Equivalised income's family-sharing convention does not prove personal control.
Use I04–I06/PF's actual cash, safe capital and separately matched housing;
do not assess rent that an owner has not received as O or hypothetical cash.

**WF19 — Preserve source meaning across pension labels.** The source's
public-pension category includes disability/old-age and early-retirement
payments; it is not only Y basic. Its statistically private category includes
public servants' pensions and ATP as well as labor-market/private annuities.
Neither label decides public/private legal obligor, ownership, accrued rights,
PF tax basis or access. The328.4bn public-transfer proxy also differs in scope,
population, rounding and accounting from OFF3 household current transfers.
Their difference is unresolved reconciliation, not a new source or duplicate
bill to delete. Preserve genuine old guarantees and actually restricted assets.

**WF20 — Price basic protection gross, net replacement separately.** At the
January snapshot, each assumed1,000DKK of monthly ordinary B would require
1.213329bnDKK gross per month for65+, including0.134115bn for65–66. This is
a conditional snapshot scale, not an adequate B, actual eligibility count,
annual pension budget or net cost of lowering another country's pension age.
Match actual basic/earned/disability/housing payments, tax, period and lawful
replacement before any subtraction; statistical pensions cannot be swept into
a general replacement pool. Y's age65, no forced work exit, price protection,
earned units/credits and old claims stay unchanged. Increased gross coverage
can be worth its source burden; it is not a free relabeling of existing money.

**WF21 — Do not tax or assess an average person.** Under the unchanged
SD05/PA/I05/Y rules, an authored annual B120,000, M500,000 and necessary
housing60,000 yield different totals for two wage distributions totaling
1,000,000DKK. Wages0/1,000,000 yield tax227,500 and I05180,000;
500,000/500,000 yield tax100,000 and I050. The reference B/M are not measured
adequacy or W. No welfare ranking, behavioral response or candidate national
yield follows. Joint needs/earnings/other-income/asset/actual-service receipt,
periods and public-source histories matter. Use safe disaggregated ranges
where valid; do not demand a public universal linked personal dossier.

**WF22 — Keep unpaid basic and earned rights alive.** A conditional older
person with B120,000, housing60,000 and received contributory60,000 has
I0512,000 with received basic120,000, or I05132,000 when basic is unpaid.
The unpaid basic claim120,000 remains before any lawful source-period
correction. Equal immediate G outlay is no saving or cancellation. Separate
G basic/top-up, S earned payment, tax, arrears and lawful protected overlap
correction; no double credit, automatic recovery or forced delayed subsistence.
Provider receipt reduces ordinary need only under I17's actual lawful matching;
cash reduction is not net public saving without actual provider/transition cost.
