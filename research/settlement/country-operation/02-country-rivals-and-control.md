# The whole-country choice behind a public-resource exception

Design162 · [Canonical CP13–CP16](../../country/C06/28/07-committed-logistics-and-continuing-claims.md)

The hard choice is allocation of real burdens, not whether a coordinator can
read every record. A due service/resource commitment can protect people from
arbitrary interference yet prevent grave-harm relief when nothing is formally
spare. Retain the existing democratic/service-first/plural country; revise its
uncommitted-only residual preference for one bounded public-logistics category.
Existing privacy, consent, evidence, public control and paid duties constrain
that change. Broader control is not automatically a better life.

|---|---|---|

Retain as a substantive rival; actual prior law may already offer a better valid route. |

effectiveness or justification for CP13. Exact XML/selected text and limits

no score averages away incompatible rights or ownership choices.

## Finite result and the next consequential uncertainty

Select the limited CP13 slice, not the broader architecture. Source evidence is
normative/comparative text and authored reasoning, not an actual independent
clinical/transport/education evaluation. The12 joined written cases include
failed/unresolved paths. No real jurisdiction, affect-party participation,
legitimate adoption, paid workforce/cost, actual simultaneous receiving or
better-lives result was obtained. Actual unavailability is no successful remedy.

Next compare the whole country's **ordinary paid service and independent-control
architecture** against strong retain/repair and a genuine direct/common-provider
alternative using the current duties, actual feasible sources where available
and explicit unknowns. Deliver a reasoned retain/revise/reject/unresolved choice;
do not automatically add more diversion categories, school rules or macro tables.
A new actual harm or qualified source/affected objection can reopen this result.
Research/file consistency checks cannot adopt a governing rule or prove superiority.
