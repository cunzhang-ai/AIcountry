# LF09–LF12: command can prevent force, not legalize it

*Design 97 research selection; no enacted power or proven readiness.*

**LF09 — Plan and assign a lawful operation.** For foreseeable arrest, protective-order
execution, custody transfer, public-event response or lawful civil enforcement with material
force risk, the competent civilian service names a responsible commander, underlying authority,
trained workers, objective, place, time, accessible warning, de-escalation/withdrawal options,
bystander protection, aid receiver, source records and independent contact before action where
time allows. A plan is bounded to the actual event, not a rolling permission to seize or use
weapons. Frontline staff retain a duty to apply LF02–LF04 to actual facts and may refuse a
manifestly unlawful command through a protected route. A commander must stop an unsafe method
and cannot command the judge, prosecutor, journalist or clinical receiver. A sudden immediate
life threat permits the minimum lawful response without a written plan; it triggers prompt
record and outside review, not a permanent plan exception. Executive policy may allocate lawful
resources, not authorize an individual use of lethal force or a named political arrest.

**LF10 — Controlled equipment and human decisions.** Elected law sets the permitted classes and
prohibited modes of force equipment; lawful published technical rules specify issuance, custody,
maintenance, independent risk testing, qualification and renewal. A name such as “less lethal,”
a vendor manual or an imported approval is no legal or safety finding. A person may carry/use a
force device only with current individual competence for that device and assigned role.
Untrained deployment, crowd-indiscriminate use, automated person selection/engagement and a
system that cannot be stopped or audited are forbidden. A device suggestion is evidence to
question, not an officer's ground or defense. New body sensors, cameras, acoustic systems and
analytics require their distinct PD/P data/search basis, retention and worker terms; equipment
procurement is not a surveillance warrant. Publish the legal criteria, safety risks and safe
aggregate deployment, allowing narrow operational-security redaction with independent
access/review.

**LF11 — Care and contemporaneous record.** The actor stops force when its ground ends, calls
appropriate medical aid immediately when needed and protects the affected person's bodily
choices, counsel, safe contact and support under the applicable law. Give first aid as actually
qualified and arrange a competent accepting clinician; neither an officer nor a camera certifies
treatment receipt. Preserve relevant original time, order, persons, warnings, alternatives,
device issuance/use, injuries, custody/transport, clinical referral and bystander evidence
without exposing private lives in a public log. Record every intentional physical contact beyond
consensual assistance or ordinary noncoercive guidance, every restraint/device use, weapon
display or discharge, and any force causing injury or a complaint of pain/distress. Use a
proportionate short entry for minor contact; it is not exempted by calling it immaterial. Give a
safe affected-person receipt/access route; protect justified witness identity and defense
rights. Complete the record by end of duty or within **12 elapsed hours**, whichever is earlier,
with corrected addenda rather than retroactive overwrite. Serious injury, death, a firearm
discharge toward a person or comparably life-threatening act needs immediate aid, scene/evidence
preservation and LF12 notice, not a 12-hour delay. Missing recording equipment does not prove
innocence or guilt; deliberate unlawful destruction has its separate K/P predicates. The 12-hour
outer clock is an uncalibrated proposal.

**LF12 — Outside-command serious-event custody.** C08/04's existing external investigator and
separate prosecutor receive direct notice and necessary protected access without an implicated
command credibility gate after a death, serious injury, missing restrained person or any LF04
potentially lethal act even if nobody was hit; no implicated-unit credibility gate applies. The
first responder protects life and originals, while the independent custodian obtains safe
copies, source/vendor logs, relevant officer and bystander accounts, medical evidence and
exculpatory material through enacted access or urgent court order. The implicated unit cannot be
sole owner of a key, scene, witness route or closure decision. Courts control any new
search/warrant, protected disclosure, liberty and remedies; prosecutor, command and judge remain
separate. A less serious force complaint receives first receipt, reasons, protected evidence and
direct court/ombud access without a criminal proof gate. Preserve privacy, defense and source
safety; records are not an indefinite public testimony or training pool.
