# AT01–AT04. Triggers and original evidence

[Technology transition](../18-technology-transition-and-shared-gains.md)

**AT01 — Watch effects across sources and affected people.** Each
quarter the existing Treasury, Revenue, work and service authorities
maintain the joint account under C05/04. Publish safe compatible
national, labour-market and sector breakdowns fixed by prior
published rules. Show real earnings, employment and involuntary
hours loss, actual contribution and tax receipts, accrued claims,
service need, qualified shifts and available ownership and provider
dependencies. Use original series, versions and uncertainty. Do
not impute cause from a forecast, exposure score or the equipment
used. The independent evidence service preserves methods and
corrections. Ordinary receipt, appeal and duties continue.

**AT02 — Trigger a public response on verified deterioration.** Open
the response on either of these proposed reference tests. First,
national real covered earnings are at least 5% below the comparable
year-earlier quarter in each of two consecutive quarters. Second,
real covered earnings in a predeclared labour-market area or sector
are at least 10% below the comparable year-earlier quarter in each
of two consecutive quarters and original records show material
involuntary hours or employment loss there. Publish the denominator,
coverage, price adjustment, missing groups and revisions. Keep
changes of eligibility and reporting scope separate from changes
in earnings. A paid reduction in hours on unchanged real earnings
does not meet either earnings test by itself.

**AT03 — Act before a series can confirm an imminent failure.** A
credible current risk to an identified due benefit or protected
service, or independently evidenced loss of an essential operating
or oversight route through common provider control, opens a scoped
response without waiting for a second quarter. Workers, employers,
local bodies, service users and Assembly members can submit original
evidence of a material overlooked group or dependency. Treasury
receives or accepts a protected handoff and gives a reasoned trigger
disposition within ten calendar days of first receipt, sooner at the
actual useful time. The responsible sector remains answerable for its
underlying duties. The
thresholds govern escalation. They do not change eligibility, delay
a WL17 duty or discharge ordinary contingency duties. Missing data
require a public finding of uncertainty and a dated correction,
with direct access to existing oversight and review for suppression.

**AT04 — Discriminate among mechanisms before choosing burdens.**
Compare actual production, qualified task time and demand with
credible alternatives. Test complementarity and better work, local
job displacement, changing trade or demand, concentrated rents and
loss of essential capacity. Several can coexist. Separate private
revenues and profits, taxable domestic base, assessed liabilities,
paid collections and restricted contributions. Keep constant-price
output separate from dated cash. Compare gains and losses by
affected group, including people outside formal employment and
places hidden by a national average. Protect personal and firm
originals under applicable law, with answerable outside access.
