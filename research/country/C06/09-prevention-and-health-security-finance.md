# C06. Financing prevention and health security

*Part of [C06 public finance](../C06-public-finance.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

The Assembly separately authorizes the core public-health service,
needs-adjusted local teams, laboratories, lawful data custody, outreach,
preventive supplies, communications and bounded surge reserves. These
functions are not assumed to be covered by payments for clinical visits or
by C10's disaster budget. A primary-care network can deliver an immunization
or screening service under contract, but it does not thereby acquire the
population surveillance, public warning or restriction mandate. The
appropriation names the lawful payer, operator, independent reviewer and
receiving service if a laboratory, supplier or local team fails.

The national account compares prevention options with expected benefit and
harm, uneven access, individual and worker time, cold chain, qualified
staff, imported supplies, privacy and remedy costs, and competing clinical
needs. Estimates do not become a guaranteed saving in hospitals or a reason
to cancel due treatment. National equalization uses measured local need and
capacity, not a reward for reporting few cases; a locality must not gain by
suppressing a signal or excluding difficult-to-reach residents. A surge
plan states which ordinary service loses staff, vehicles or laboratory
capacity and how it continues. The same reserve, clinician or cold store
cannot be booked twice.

A materially new compulsory programme, tax, procurement guarantee or
cross-sector priority requires valid law and an Assembly funding choice.
Routine voluntary programmes and first-hour health safety acts continue
only within enacted bounds and rights. Contracted vendors do not own the
public-health data, determine programme eligibility or declare an epidemic.
Treasury reports appropriation, actual cash, physical stock, people reached,
protection delivered and unmet duties separately. The [C07 public-health
route](../C07/08-population-health-and-prevention.md) sets substantive
boundaries, while [C10](../C10/01-public-safety-and-emergencies.md) controls
restrictions. No real baseline, incidence, supply contracts or staffed
review route is established for AIcountry.

**Design147 HP source.** Public owners must fund actual support/sites/transport,
protected daily source reassessment,24-hour judges/counsel, repeated ordinary and
escalated appellate review/counter-expertise, source data/retention and received
release/correction. HP actual-work-loss replacement and10% absence-credit backing
are new G duties, not Y levy/cash or existing free sick-pay capacity. Same-hour
claims/stock/staff cannot be duplicated across illness, health and disaster work.
No actual national cost/source or qualified operation is established.

[HG/NCS](../C07/05-health-service-and-patient-control.md) integrates the public
clinical service, not this separate population-health/source/monitoring mandate.
Clinical preventive delivery grants no surveillance/restriction power or right
to cancel local public-health budgets or merge private records.
