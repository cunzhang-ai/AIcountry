# Design485 decision record

**Scope and baseline.** Apply the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md)
to the Design484 [baseline](baseline.json). Compare a material source
and a continuing protection offer together at the actual cutoffs.
All model amounts and affected people in the local fixture are authored.

**Selection and objections.** Carry ordinary duties and compare the
unchanged baseline with a retailer and utility instrument and owner-side
preference reform. In the adverse price branch the service charge fails
the specified protective purpose despite collecting sufficient revenue.
Select the owner-side instrument only for the scoped assumptions,
including verified collection at cutoff, household incidence and the
reconciled recurring margin where needed. Less pass-through or a
different funding design could justify a different choice. Closing a
nonessential preference can have real incidence, including time, income
and lost investment opportunities. No observed distribution is inferred.

**Authority and continuing claims.** Submit independently reviewable
source, outlay and fallback terms to the competent elected route. Retain
protected opposition and nonrecipient challenge, original dates and
applicable remedies. Cost a legally applicable benefit adjustment once
on its real schedule. A funding gap holds only a dependent prospective
step under its own law. A late forecast, analyst opinion or AI output
does not change an effective claim or supply a vote.

**Evidence and reopening.** The [comparison](../settlement/funding-and-household-incidence/01-when-the-source-undoes-the-benefit.md)
reproduces arithmetic by person and cutoff. [Sources](sources.md)
qualify mechanisms and limits. Reopen on changed receipts, pass-through,
actual wage, rent or utility effects, displaced services or stronger
opposition alternatives. The next bounded design question is whether
the independent relief route remains funded and timely when multiple
sectors discover the same incidence error and all require correction
before the next essential bills. Actual participation, enactment,
delivery and better lives remain unverified.
