# Real custody alternatives and the costs of narrower power

*Part of [conflict custody](README.md); design selection, not enacted law.*

| Model | Reason to consider it | Present selection and material objection |
|---|---|---|
| Leave conflict custody to command and general humane-treatment promises | Fewer formal interfaces and operational discretion. | Reject: treatment promises do not establish who may hold whom, when a judge intervenes, how opposition can inspect or how release actually occurs. |
| Ordinary criminal law for every person and no nonpenal status custody | A clear prosecution-or-release boundary avoids preventive detention expansion. | Retain as a serious liberty-oriented rival, but reject as the complete reference for now: actual POW and neutrality obligations/return-to-hostilities questions are not all criminal guilt. Its security, safe-release and real international compatibility require a concrete context. |
| Narrow POW and actual neutrality grounds, independent civilian review, no general civilian/NIAC internment | Separate protected status, narrowly justified restraint, criminal culpability and actual return. | Select CD01–CD16 conditionally. It needs greater investigative, judicial, paid care and safe-receiving capacity; no claim of fewer attacks or lower total harm is demonstrated. |
| Individually necessary civilian-security internment under a defined exceptional law | May address a serious armed threat which criminal proof cannot yet establish; actual IHL contemplates specific civilian internment categories. | Do not select. Broadening the executive's reachable population risks opposition suppression, discriminatory error and rolling emergency. Keep the rival distinct; actual threats, feasible alternatives, rights, legal duties and error/remedy evidence could reopen the choice through competent law. |

The selected model does not prove the rival immoral in every imaginable
context or displace actual nonwaivable duties. It deliberately declines powers
that some treaty regimes permit. A specific incompatible actual obligation
requires a lawful reconciliation before adoption or deployment, not a claim
that protective wording silently solved it. Qualified legal analysis, affected
participation and legitimate authority are separate missing steps.

## One resource chain, without a detention economy

C06/26 requires paid custody/care, independent judges/counsel, language and
child competence, safe places, records, visits and actual release routes.
Account by shift, skill, location, controller and due event; a clinician or
judge already assigned to ordinary care, elections or a trial is not spare
because another file calls them a reserve. Shared buildings and transport
need simultaneous availability, safe conditions and replacement costs.

No compulsory captive labor, confiscation, expected foreign reimbursement,
future convictions or assumed AI savings finance present duties. No actual
increment is priced or funded by the old country-book bid or SF cash example.
A mission must assess custody and release capacity before relying on capture;
immediate lawful defense does not erase humane obligations to people actually
controlled. Missing capacity remains a failure needing correction, never an
excuse for new force, longer custody or a false readiness label.

Retain obligations to missing people, survivors, injured persons and actual
receiving services after release or a political/territorial handover. A
communication platform, new status taxonomy or faster AI agreement cannot
change someone's liberty without actual authority and independent hearing.
Reopen on a concrete excluded category, unsafe duty, failed execution, new
original evidence or a demonstrably better institutional alternative.
