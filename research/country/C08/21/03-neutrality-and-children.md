# Actual neutral military duties, ordinary restraint and child protection

*CD05–CD06; part of [C08/21](../21-conflict-detention-and-release.md).*

**CD05 — Neutrality custody requires an actual legal obligation.** When this
country is genuinely neutral in the identified interstate conflict and actual
applicable neutrality law requires it, the candidate permits only the relevant
foreign belligerent military custody on its territory, with CD04 review and
proof of actual category/necessity. Record treaty applicability, customary
basis if asserted, neutrality facts, individual role, medical needs and safe
less restrictive alternatives. A claim of neutrality is not proof, and one
alliance label cannot decide every simultaneous conflict.

The inspected Hague V Articles 11–14 distinguish entering military forces,
escaped POWs and wounded/sick cases. Do not intern an escaped prisoner or
asylum-seeking civilian merely because they came from an army or a country
at war; no generic foreign-national/ideological class. A needed actual
military-neutrality measure does not confer criminal guilt, conscripted
work or a right to return someone to prohibited harm. Apply humane national
protections and review even where treaty POW provisions have different scope.
No silent assumption that all belligerents are parties or custom is identical.

This Act does not select occupation-based former-force internment or retention
of medical/religious personnel simply to fill our own staffing deficit.
Offer safe lawful return/release and actual voluntary assistance instead;
provide our own paid staff and protect patients through an accepted handoff.
A protected clinician/chaplain is not automatically an ordinary POW. Any
actual nonwaivable competing obligation needs explicit lawful reconciliation,
not coercion or an assertion that a medical need made authority appear.

**CD06 — No alternative route for ordinary civilians or recruited children.**
Outside CD03/CD05, use P's actual published-offense arrest/remand or only
minimum restraint strictly necessary to stop an actual ongoing/imminent
armed attack and obtain safe immediate control. It ends as soon as that
immediate ground ends, with CD04 review no later than 24 hours. This is no
24-hour intelligence/screening entitlement. New criminal custody needs its
own P grounds and original clocks; no NIAC/terror label substitutes. Ordinary
clinical rescue, voluntary safe shelter and child protection keep their own
consent, strict grounds and independent courts, not a military-care disguise.

Children receive protected status benefits where actually applicable, never
an adult-camp exception or guilt from recruitment. A continuing actual IAC/
neutrality restriction needs a child-competent civilian judge and specific
clear/convincing necessity, safe alternatives and an order at most seven days,
renewed only on fresh child-specific grounds. Prioritize safe release/family
or chosen supported care; do not use poverty, absent papers or a care vacancy
as military necessity. No forced labor, adult criminal transfer or punitive
placement under a welfare label. P05 still bars conviction below 16 and caps
16–17 criminal custody; B31/B32 controls independent care/removal, not a new
status-prison sentence. Actual current lawful grounds, not nominal age/status,
determine any continuing restriction; missing safe receiving defeats readiness.
