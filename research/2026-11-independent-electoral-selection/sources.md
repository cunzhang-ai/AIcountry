# Evidence for separate electoral selection

| Primary source | Observation and scope | Design use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [UK Speaker's Committee, 2023 appointment report](https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/cm5803/cmselect/cmspeak/1766/report.html) | The published appointment protocol places shortlisting and interviews with a panel, a recommendation decision with the committee and formal appointment steps elsewhere. Its political nomination route can begin with names submitted by qualifying party leaders. | Examine the actual power to admit candidates, recommend and appoint separately. An independently labelled panel does not decide the complete chain. |
| [Mexico Electoral Tribunal, SUP-JE-1172/2023](https://www.te.gob.mx/sentenciasHTML/convertir/expediente/SUP-JE-1172-2023-) | The tribunal confirmed two appointments made through a statutory draw by the Chamber of Deputies after agreement was not reached. The judgment describes the technical evaluation committee's prior lists of five. | A fallback draw can reach an appointment during deadlock. Its prior list controls whom it can select, so timing, conflicts and useful review of entry still matter. |
| [Design260's account of Spanish judicial appointment interruption](../settlement/appointment-independence/01-spain-appointment-interruption.md) | The historical legislative restriction and later appointments support examining the separate effects of blocked renewal and restrictions on an outgoing body. | Preserve operating duties on their own authority. The historical record does not identify the effect of a CJ/EA split. |

These records document mechanisms. The proposed electoral formation,
staffing arithmetic and concurrent case are authored. The evidence does
not show that the three-formation design improves real election outcomes
or that any named country would have benefited from adopting it.
