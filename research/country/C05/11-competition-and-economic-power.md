# C05. Competition law and accountable economic power

1 November 2026 · Integrated design 98 · Working selection, not enacted law

Select a finite cartel/exclusion/merger statute, a replaceable markets chief,
protected career case functions and direct ordinary courts. Private, cooperative
and public enterprise receive the same substantive test. Successful scale,
low prices, public ownership, lawful collaboration and unpopular views are not
violations. Economic law serves usable exit and bargaining; it does not give
an official ownership of the economy or the right to decide political speech.

voluntary terms; contested prohibition/adverse merger conditions and material
property transfers require an independent court. A filed case alone extends no
hold. Courts and staff still need actual paid independent capacity. MR05/VD11
