# LCQ01–LCQ06. Restore an Assembly without transferring its power

*Design359 · Part of [legislative capacity](../05-assembly-capacity-and-scope.md).*

**LCQ01 — Preserve the legal denominator and distinguish the failure.**
Keep the statutory full seat count and each act's quorum, assent and
notice requirements. At a failed sitting distinguish verified vacancy,
disputed mandate, practical exclusion, incapacity and nonattendance.
Protect private reasons and report lawful aggregate status. An empty seat,
temporary absence or abstention cannot be counted as an assent, lower
the denominator or transfer the vote to a party or agent. Recognize
vacancies only under EL and applicable court orders. A boycott does
not vacate a seat or trigger a new election. Allow attendance and
ordinary political challenge without compelling a vote.

**LCQ02 — Restore practical access through a prior lawful route.** Before
the term, fund and test an alternate accessible physical chamber and a
separately controlled route joining supervised public meeting sites.
The adopting law specifies which sittings may use these routes and the
ordered presiders. On a documented access failure, the presider or
predesignated deputy publishes the deficiency within 24 hours and sets
the earliest lawful substitute sitting within the next 48 hours where
possible. Publish the blocked duty, cause and next date if that cannot be
met. Keep minimum notice, debate and amendment periods. Neither Cabinet
nor the affected service vendor controls activation. Publish scope and
reassess the necessity of the alternate at least every seven calendar days.
Return to ordinary sitting when effective access is restored.

**LCQ03 — Require deliberation and actual lawful votes.** Verify the
member, authority, usable presence, access to the exact text and
opposition's ability to speak and amend. At linked sites, confirm the
same live debate and authenticated personal ballot, with no double vote
and an independent durable tally. Provide accessible transport, evidence,
communication support and safe participation. A chat acknowledgement,
proxy, AI agent or saved ballot cannot supply a vote for this route.
If a material access or tally dispute could affect lawful presence,
assent or fair participation, use the applicable pause, review and repeat
route before certifying the result. A valid record distinguishes the two
thresholds, abstentions and actual votes. No official may cure an
unlawful process by adjusting the count after the fact.

**LCQ04 — Keep the work visible when binding votes cannot occur.** Use
permitted opposition statements, evidence preservation and preparation
of alternatives with the original decision docket. Existing committees
can act only within their own lawful authority and quorum. Keep
publication and ordinary independent court, audit and election work
funded. A deliberation without quorum cannot enact law, appoint
officials, validate confidence or make appropriations. Trigger timely
legal review of exclusion, obstruction or credentials through existing
courts. A court may restore lawful process within its jurisdiction.
It cannot cast a missing vote.

**LCQ05 — Restore actual seats and protect continuing obligations.** Use
the frozen succession pool and then the existing EL19–EL20 triggers
and supplementary polls for verified vacancies. Pending mandate
disputes keep their own remedy. Use ordinary formation, replacement
and expiry law on its own trigger. Budget lapse keeps the independent
standing schedule, eligible bridge and fixed closeout under BLC.
Preserve due rights, fund lawful alternates, record uncovered work and
use previously authorized urgent measures on their own grounds.
A blocked sitting does not extend the bridge, renew an office, give
Cabinet new discretion or cancel a claim. If both sitting routes fail,
record unmade decisions and retain their remedies.

**LCQ06 — Pay and test the complete recovery chain.** The Assembly's
protected budget identifies alternate sites, presiders, secure record
custody, transport, accessibility, independent review and the next
needed sitting. Price these beside any lawful election and standing
service payments. Record common controllers and simultaneous exposure.
Give opposition and members the usable evidence and route to challenge
activation and refusal. Preserve pending votes, exact texts and original
dates through handover. AI may reconcile schedules and discrepancies.
It cannot certify mandates, participation or assent. Reopen on
selective access, prolonged deadlock, unsustainable alternate cost
or a better equally democratic route.
