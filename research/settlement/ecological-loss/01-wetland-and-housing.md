# A wetland and a housing obligation in the same budget

Design320 · Constructed comparison for [ER01–ER05](../../country/C09/04/01-ecological-loss-and-replacement.md)

Hold fixed 100 households needing lawful housing, including 30 due suitable
interim accommodation this season, and a public capital ceiling of 100
illustrative cost units. Interim provision needs a separate 6 operating
units and verified receiving capacity for those 30. The lower supported
catchment estimate is 8 flood-attenuation units in the design season. An
authored legal floor is 6. An existing native movement corridor has separate
place-specific protection. The relevant window is before the next exposure;
the figures are neither real hydrological values nor proposed national floors.

| Option | Housing offer and capital | Ecological result before release |
|---|---|---|
| Full wetland footprint, credits purchased | 100 permanent homes, 85 including credits | 8 − 3 = 5. Three promised future units elsewhere supply zero in this window. Protected corridor also severed. Hold. |
| Reduced footprint plus serviced infill | 70 permanent homes at the reduced site and 30 at infill, 70 + 30 = 100 | 8 − 1 = 7 and corridor retained. Infill relies on its own approved capacity. |
| All infill | 100 permanent homes, 115 | 8 and corridor retained. Exceeds capital authority by 15. |
| Reduced footprint only | 70 permanent homes, 70 | 7 and corridor retained. Thirty permanent places remain unfunded. |

In the authored facts, phase the reduced footprint and serviced infill
under the existing 100-unit ceiling and pay the separate 6 for interim
housing. Stage releases still require actual tenure, site services, housing
receipts and ecological checks. If the 30 infill places fail, maintain the
funded interim route, disclose the permanent shortfall and return the choice
to the elected authority. If the receiving places themselves fail, the
housing authority still owes the underlying duty and must invoke its lawful
fallback and report unmet need. An ecological hold settles no housing claim.

Challenge the result in three ways. A corrected baseline of 6 makes the
reduced footprint leave 5, so the release stops and the 15-unit additional
appropriation for all-infill becomes a live choice. If the corridor can be
avoided under a revised full design, one independently observed additional
unit in the same scope and season could restore the flood floor from 5 to 6,
subject to legal protection, persistence and cumulative commitments. A unit
already supporting another permit or arriving the following season leaves 5.
A verified flood unit supplies no corridor protection.

The [decision record](../../2026-11-ecological-loss-and-replacement/decision-record.md)
retains the alternatives and reopening test. The accompanying check exercises
authored arithmetic and release predicates. Actual housing and ecological
outcomes require field observation and independent receiving evidence.
