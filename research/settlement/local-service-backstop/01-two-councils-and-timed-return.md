# Two councils, one national reserve and the return of delivery

Design325 · Constructed comparison for [LD14–LD18](../../country/C04/10/04-local-service-backstop.md)

Two councils have binding home-support duties and retain their own elected
budgets, workers and open claims. In A, a cash interruption leaves two
qualified visits uncovered by hour 12, at 4 illustrative units each.
A separate local team continues the already funded 4-unit work. In B,
one qualified visit costing 6 is due by hour 18, with no timely ordinary
team. Assume each original visit is indivisible. A has a 6-unit lawful
undisputed ordinary transfer due on day 3. The transfer is too late for
the first visits and has its original purpose. It is neither extinguished
nor usable in the first window.

The national vote has scheduled a separate 14-unit reserve for these
home-support failures. Two distinct providers accept the relevant shifts
and terms. The national sector officer has safe access to independently
held minimum source records. A statutory safeguard prioritizes the
earlier otherwise equivalent deadline in a conflict. The required
verification, on-call court and paid handback work are funded separately.

| Arrangement | Result in the first window |
|---|---|
| Ordinary grant and voluntary recovery alone | A's ordinary transfer is due on day 3. The three visits miss their original windows on the stipulated facts. |
| Broad financial administration of the councils | Could commission the same visits if it had the same funds and providers. Appointment, unrelated decisions and resident political control would also become exposed. |
| Scheduled national service backstop | Two visits in A cost 8 and one visit in B costs 6. Both are received on time if the accepted routes perform. The 14 is counted once and neither council loses its remaining powers. |

An urgent activation before the first deadline is reviewed at hour 48,
within the 72-hour limit. In A the lawfully received ordinary transfer and
an independently verified local route enable handback on day 5 after the
first accepted local service. In B the ordinary route is still unfunded
on day 25. The national and local elected authorities must act on ordinary
finance or delivery before expiry. An extension would need the existing
legal grounds, remaining cash and accepted capacity; the initial reserve
was fully used. A day-30 expiry does not make the next visit payable from
the former reserve or erase B's duty.

If only 10 of the reserve is actually usable, A's two visits consume 8.
The remaining 2 cannot purchase B's indivisible 6-unit visit. The funding
gap is 4 and the entire visit is still missed. Report both, seek a funded
alternate and retain B's original claim. If a provider belongs to a shared
crew already committed elsewhere, even the full 14 fails to supply that
visit. If review does not lawfully confirm continued activation by hour 72,
the exceptional authority ends; any separate ordinary duty or authorized
urgent route carries on in its own scope.

The [decision record](../../2026-11-local-service-backstop/decision-record.md)
retains the alternatives and the first field check. All capacities, deadlines
and amounts are authored. No municipality or actual operation is described.
