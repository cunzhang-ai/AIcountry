# Compare the same country through freedom, refusal and renewal

Design 141 · [Current country case](../../country/C01/03-country-case-and-competing-designs.md)

All alternatives carry the same protected needs and old obligations, including
politics/justice, ordinary private production, services, networks/ecology,
safety/external affairs, knowledge/culture and future revision. Only their
institutional form differs at this comparison level; actual special law,
resources and later commitments require explicit reconciliation, not equal
imagined costs. The [five cards](../../country/C01/03/06-current-country-and-strong-alternatives.md)
do not establish that every alternative is fully specified or feasible.

| Choice | Ordinary freedom and access | Government/private abuse | Paid transition and failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strong retain/repair | Preserve working relationships/services and informal routes; remove located barriers | Retain real ordinary justice/opposition; inherited exclusions/controllers require repair | Keep actual assets/staff/claims and price necessary correction, not zero change |
| Service-first plural | Individual actual-resource I05, S/Y and voluntary provider choice; assistance/means intrusion/nonclaim remain | Public residual responsibility plus independent review; contractors/public bodies can share controllers | Pay fallback, contracts/title, staffing, supply and source dates; failed handoff never cancels dues |
| Territorial direct-public service | Fewer contracting interfaces may help; care/clinical choice and ordinary enterprise remain free | Public monopoly/management can dominate; direct delivery cannot judge its own failures | Paid acquisition/construction/recruitment and safe continuity; no free avoided margin or assumed larger payroll |
| Rights-preserving U+C2 | Ordinary cash has no means assessment; actual additional needs/services/earned rights remain | Cash independence may help exit, but housing/platform power and residual proof persist | Complete tax/source/price/distribution and overlap; no cancelled claims or presumed government financing |
| Mutual/common provision | Situated worker/user/community knowledge and voluntary cooperation; refusal/exit remain usable | Internal hierarchies and unequal wealth matter; national rights and distinct public control remain | Lawful equalization/common sources, accepting successors and external duties; no rich-place opt-out from shared claims |

A parent, child and older relative retain different will, service, cash and
privacy claims in each option. A cheaper household total cannot compel unpaid
care, unsafe work, unwanted treatment or surrender of the child's education.
Necessary travel/time, assistance and actual usable services matter alongside
income; political participation need not be a compulsory civic lifestyle.

Under a hostile incumbent, trace appointment/removal, funding, source access,
independent adjudication and executed relief for each arrangement, including
private/public protectors. Fewer boards may reduce work or concentrate control;
more selectors may share a patron. Neither number settles actual independence.
If all lawful routes fail, record failed protection, not a fictional guardian.

All can use competent AI, simpler software, personal agents, shared situations,
collective methods or offline human practice within authority. Technical
administration is a cross-cutting method, not necessarily a separate country.
AI-exclusive sovereignty fails the user's democratic requirement; bounded
technical delegation remains a genuine option inside a democratic alternative.
Data convergence never allocates conflicting resources or multiplies a vote.

The retained choice prioritizes actual needs, public responsibility and plural
exit under uncertainty. It is a stated value/design judgment, not an empirical
finding that A is cheaper than U or public provision outperforms other forms.
CS16–CS17 permits simpler arrangements to displace a compulsory layer with
proper law and preserved rights; existing independent judgment is not a spare
resource. New evidence or legitimate contrary priorities can displace this bargain.

Next complete the identified voluntary funded-pension tax interface, not an
indefinite national-model conversation. Its source/basis choice must preserve
all these due rights and actual law. Complete country cost/source/incidence and
observed wellbeing remain separate missing evidence, not inferred achievements.
