# Design325 decision on the local common-service backstop

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design324, LD01–LD13, MF, FT, JC, SR and the working framework.
The prior design separates transfers, debt and received service and
permits bounded delivery support. Specify a prospective activation and
expiry for a nationally financed, pre-scheduled task where an imminent
common service will otherwise fail. Preserve the ordinary elected
local authority. The project assistant authors the proposal. Legitimate
lawmakers choose authority, funding and continuing accountability.

## Alternatives and evidence

Compare ordinary grants and voluntary support, broader fiscal
administration, and the selected statutory backstop for named services.
Ordinary support is less intrusive and remains the first workable
option. It can leave residents exposed when money, local assent or
provider capacity arrives after the deadline. A fiscal administrator
could address causes together, but would give a distant appointee
power over elected priorities, employment and unrelated spending.
Select narrow national delivery within its own money and actual
accepted capacity. Repeated failures still require a durable elected
source and delivery choice. Avoiding takeover can leave a difficult
coordination problem unresolved by the initial activation.

The [primary comparators](sources.md) establish different legal
allocations of power. Their institutions and results do not transfer.
The [constructed case](../settlement/local-service-backstop/01-two-councils-and-timed-return.md)
isolates the first reserve, concurrent receivers, existing transfers,
independent review and timed return.

## Authority, migration and reopening

Enact covered tasks, qualifications, protected sources, priority
criteria, rights, limits and successor rules before use. The responsible
sector career officer activates the covered service. The court reviews
legality and necessity and can enforce an existing duty. Keep local
taxes, budgets, appointments, opposition, elections and laws with their
ordinary authority. Settle lawful worker, provider and creditor claims
separately. Migrate prospectively and keep original service dates,
open cases, evidence and complaint routes live.

Test a real local continuity plan across two affected localities with
qualified providers, residents, opposition, a court or independent
review exercise and a national payer. Price actual staffing and the
next service after expiry. Compare political bias, delay and service
receipt with repair of ordinary transfer and qualified local support.
Reopen on concentrated intervention, repeated extensions, diversion,
late review, loss of independent remedy or a simpler working route.
Actual adoption, lawful delivery, costs and outcomes remain open.
