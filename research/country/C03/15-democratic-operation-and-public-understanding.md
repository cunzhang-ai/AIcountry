# Democratic operation and public understanding

*Part of [C03](../C03-democratic-government.md); design128 working selection,
not enacted law, an actual election plan or a demonstrated democratic outcome.*

**Retain national proportional representation with an optional person preference,
open entry and existing deadlines. Select a proposed operating arrangement that
separates reusable factual support, fresh qualification, independent adjudication
and privately chosen plural information.** A lawful large field is a duty to
serve, not a reason to select acceptable opponents by budget or AI ranking.

[Paid capacity and scope correction](../C06/51-democratic-operation-and-paid-capacity.md)
counts national, local and question duties together; annual money does not create
qualified people available at the same time. [Comparison and source record](../../settlement/democratic-operation/README.md)
retains dissent, counterexamples and exact dependencies. This selection neither
creates an office nor gives a research model authority over candidates or votes.

**Design146 public-information chain.** [PM01–PM24](../C11/08-public-media-and-editorial-independence.md)
now provides the public-media governance/source/editorial/correction reference,
without claiming a public outlet replaces independent/community speech or actual
understanding. No sovereign truth referee, election-date change or unseen audience
mandate follows from a model's summary; real contrary information/paid reach remains.

[EDA01–EDA06](15/07-electoral-discovery-and-direct-access.md) adds a bounded direct
route where independent evidence shows an electoral discovery bottleneck.
