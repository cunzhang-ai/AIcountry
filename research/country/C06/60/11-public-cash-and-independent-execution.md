# C06. Keep authorized public payments executable through a shared failure

*Design410 · TPE01–TPE08 · Proposed ordinary public-finance and payment duties.*

A consolidated balance can be useful for public cash management. To meet
a due obligation the government also needs lawful payment authority,
accessible funds, an independent way to execute and actual receipt.
Protect those conditions together across public programmes.

**TPE01 — Identify the obligation and its actual last useful time.** For
scheduled classes of essential payments, record the legal duty, payer,
amount and currency, eligible source, due time, accepting recipient or
service, and the consequence of delay. Separate standing benefits,
authorized payroll and suppliers, independent public functions, court
execution and other covered duties by their own laws. Distinguish a
source or authority shortage, blocked access to owned cash, uncertain
settlement and unusable receipt. Give affected people and offices a direct
assisted way to report loss without navigating the failing channel.

**TPE02 — Reconcile cash centrally and qualify execution independently.**
The elected budget sets classes, aggregate limits, sources, expiry,
appointments under existing law and review. Treasury retains a consolidated
account by legal owner, restrictions and currency. Before reliance on a
backup, assign actual funds, paid staff, independent evidence access and a
qualified route before the deadline, including the next likely payment call.
Compare ordinary central repair, protected independently executable balances
and lawful sector or local routes. Price idle liquidity, reconciliation,
custody and service costs separately. A ledger subaccount, a second bank
name or an uncollected transfer is insufficient where the same failure can
prevent both releases. Protected balances remain in the public accounts and
under their enacted purposes. No private, custodial, earmarked or foreign
balance becomes fungible by consolidation.

**TPE03 — Give each payable amount one release authority.** Before a
shared failure, identify any reserved alternate by the obligation or
nonoverlapping tranche, maximum amount, permitted source, authorized paying
officer and objective activation and end. Confirm the alternate can act
on independently available originals without a failing controller's
permission. Preserve legally protected local and independent powers.
Transfer release only with verifiable exclusion of any competing instruction
or under a preauthorized exclusive assignment. Confirm actual authority,
cash, acceptance and payment scope before release. An alternate can pay
covered dues. It cannot create a new award, appropriate money, change the
priority or place an unrelated discretionary order.

**TPE04 — Resolve uncertain settlement without a blanket freeze.** Check
whether the original transfer was not sent, remains revocable, was canceled
or returned, became final, or cannot yet be determined. Continue verified
unaffected amounts and correct the unusable part through its own route.
Never replay an uncertain or final transfer as outstanding principal. Seek
independent original evidence; a sent instruction or missing reply alone
cannot resolve finality. If an existing duty and statute permit a separate
funded hardship advance, use it within its own limit and source without
treating the original payment as settled. Keep distinct any recipient's
actual access failure after final interbank settlement.

Apply the same evidence and exclusivity rule to an authorized
reciprocal public discharge under MF12. Distinguish confirmed
non-discharge, uncertain effect and final matched discharge with a
separate residual payment. A lack of confirmation does not mean
either party can replay the two old instructions. Keep independently
verified unaffected payments and funded urgent protection running.
Check whether the actual deadline, protected cash and residual still
fit before release. Give review and funded reconciliation an
independent route outside the service that prepares the match.

**TPE05 — Protect the next due window and expose concurrent shortfalls.**
Keep due principal, separate advances, operating costs and accessible cash
on one dated schedule. Count the same balance or paying team once.
Where more obligations are due than can lawfully be met, apply only the
existing applicable priority and urgency rules. Identify the uncovered
amount, affected rights and lawful correction for each case. Do not extend
a deadline, move earmarked money or prioritize a political ally through a
recovery queue. Seek a competent elected source decision in useful time.
An emergency substitute service has its own acceptance, authority and cost.

**TPE06 — Reconcile actual delivery after recovery.** Match each instruction,
settlement and usable receipt to the lawful obligation and period.
Distinguish genuinely unpaid amounts, returned transfers, resolved principal,
and advances already delivered. Offset an advance only where law permits
and only against the same obligation to the extent actually received.
Any duplicate recovery, loss allocation or correction needs ordinary lawful
process with notice and review. Do not take money automatically from an
unrelated payment or charge the recipient for correcting a public failure.
Restore ordinary release and replenish reserves only after valid receipt,
pending transfers, protections and the next call are reconciled. Preserve
original dates and actual losses even if an amount later settles.

**TPE07 — Keep control and remedies independent.** The Assembly funds and
reviews the scheme, including independent payment and review functions.
Treasury reconciles but cannot create a political withholding power.
The designated official can refuse an unlawful instruction with reasons
and direct review. Courts and lawful independent payers retain the
applicable execution authority. Verify actual source access, availability
of qualified alternates and the ability to enforce relief against a
failing or obstructing controller. Publish protected aggregate misses,
payment age and unused or trapped liquidity. AI can compare schedules
and identify discrepancies. It cannot establish finality, choose a
recipient, authorize a transfer or close a claim.

**TPE08 — Adopt prospectively and test the whole chain.** Price the next
useful deadline, independent verification, concurrent claims and usable
receipt before activating a new class. Exercise a common failure and
loss of the alternate route. Preserve existing claims, source restrictions,
lawful custody and independent remedies through a change of government,
bank or service. Adopt named funded successors and retain older laws
until competent revision. Reopen on duplicate payments, stranded cash,
repeated late funding or a simpler equally protective route.
