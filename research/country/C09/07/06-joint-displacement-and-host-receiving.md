# FR26–FR33. Receive displaced people without losing existing local protections

*Design400 · Proposed ordinary recovery and finance duties within [disaster recovery](../07-disaster-recovery-and-return.md).*

**FR26 — Use one dated receiving account across overlapping incidents.**
When several evacuations or continuing displacements draw on the same
area, the responsible host recovery lead under enacted law joins the
relevant housing, care, learning, health and transport accounts.
Retain each competent owner, source, legal scope and original deadline.
Record displaced people already present or expected, ordinary local
need, prior commitments and missing coverage. Include people outside
formal shelters and those with protected contact needs. Do not turn
registration, proof of title, local residence or participation in a
group move into a new condition for due urgent protection.

**FR27 — Check the dwelling and the other necessary services together.**
For an actual person or household, check safe accessible occupation,
lawful terms, travel, essential care, needed school access and the
next usable dates. Name an accepting owner for each component and
what can safely proceed independently. Use the person's supported
choice and only necessary lawful evidence. A suitable dwelling can
start when the person chooses it and necessary protection is actually
available. A referral, vacant room, service list or financing award
does not establish acceptance. A material unresolved component
remains open with interim protection and a named owner. Avoid
separating households or moving people again by administrative default.

**FR28 — Protect current occupants and avoid creating new displacement.**
Count a unit as an addition only when it is safely and lawfully
available without canceling a valid tenancy or already binding
placement. Emergency purchases verify vacancy, term, legal control
and actual access. Exclude a supplier's proposed unlawful removal
or coercion and refer credible abuse to independent enforcement.
Show shifts from ordinary rental and service supply and protect
people facing displacement in the host area under the same legal
rights. Compare converting genuinely available stock, ordinary
leasing, supported staying or safe return and a new qualified site.
Neither a high emergency rent nor a large gross booking count
establishes additional capacity.

**FR29 — Use lawful priorities across compatible whole offers.** Keep
binding commitments and earlier safety duties on their own law.
For competing new claims use enacted sector priorities on need,
urgency, safety and suitable receiving, with a neutral authorized
tie rule where warranted. A former address, arrival channel or
ability to pay upfront does not itself outrank an equivalent
claim. Apply JC to common emergency resources and use lawful
cross-local escalation where authority differs. Each competent
officer decides within their jurisdiction. The host lead may
assemble and explain feasible choices and cannot override another
sector's judgment, compel relocation or invent a national rank.
Offer a usable direct route to correct a mistaken assumption.

**FR30 — Pay immediate receiving and settle final liability separately.**
Before a planned commitment use actual owner funding and compatible
contracts. For eligible urgent receiving use authorized XR and MF
advances with named payer, limited purpose, available amount,
approved ceilings and an accepting provider. Protect ordinary host
services and prior protected claims under their existing sources.
Check transport, staff, facilities and cash for the next interval
before counting a repeated offer. Reconcile the national outflow,
local receipt, actual payment and any later recovery once. A
disputed payer, expected insurance recovery or conditional
reimbursement supplies neither current settlement nor a worker.

**FR31 — Qualify a national alternate before local capacity is exhausted.**
An adopting Act names the national housing and relevant sector owners,
eligible service packages, payment and operator agreements, bounded
capacity and dates. Maintain a separately usable alternate for
declared concurrent losses where the elected plan depends on it.
If local services cannot accept in useful time, activate the actual
lawful alternate. Count the receiving area's prior commitments and
independent transport and staff. Where capacity or lawful funding
is still insufficient, use prior funded protection and seek the
competent elected correction. Record the missed component, residual
harm and original claims. A national referral does not by itself
move the person or discharge the duty.

**FR32 — Do not end temporary receiving at the accounting date.**
Before a temporary period expires, check actual safe return, accepted
ordinary tenure or another chosen suitable receiving arrangement
through the following period. Tell each person the applicable terms,
limits and independent remedy. Extend support only through lawful
funded authority or use an independently funded successor. If no
route qualifies, apply existing urgent protection and report the
unmet need. Preserve claims arising from an earlier missed day.
An emergency booking, provider invoice or lapse of a grant cannot
certify permanent housing, lawful return or loss of ordinary rights.

**FR33 — Verify delivery and carry the public choice forward.**
Elected bodies decide exact scope and sources, while independent
review tests exclusion, unlawful removal, compatibility, actual
receipt and money. Publish safe counts for offered, accepted,
received, late and unmatched components. Do not disclose protected
locations. AI may test schedules and flag conflicts under bounded
review. It cannot determine priority, consent, eligibility or
receipt. Adopt prospectively with named paid owners and continued
earlier duties and claims. Reopen on missing people, displaced
host residents, shared resource loss, repeat shortfalls or a
stronger qualified arrangement.
