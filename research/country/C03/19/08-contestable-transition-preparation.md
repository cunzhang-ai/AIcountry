# HT32–HT38. Make preparation for a change of government contestable

12 November 2026 · Integrated design 431 · Candidate ordinary law

Give qualified contestants a usable way to understand how government
works before a contest. Give participants in lawful formation enough
facts to negotiate feasible support terms. Keep administration,
campaign activity, parliamentary evidence, independent costing and
lawful investiture under their existing authorities.

**HT32 — Open access by rule, with a common public starting point.**
The Assembly's nonpartisan service maintains accessible public
portfolio packs throughout the term. They cover existing duties,
organization, decision powers, material deadlines, commitments,
known operational constraints and the common fiscal baseline under
HT18–HT23. During the published preparation window, every lawfully
qualified national contestant, including a new group or independent,
can request a bounded factual briefing. Use existing lawful contestant
recognition and an urgent independent route for errors. Size extra
work by actual portfolio question, materiality, useful date and
necessary support. Polls, seat history, ministerial favor and multiple
accounts do not decide access. Reserve a useful first pass for
qualified later entrants and use the standing service for short-notice
contests. A briefing never determines ballot access or its calendar.

**HT33 — Separate statutory business from campaign and formation
requests.** Career departments continue to advise serving ministers
on lawful current duties, including during emergencies. Record the
office purpose and scope of a material election-period request.
Send a party's preparation request through the same nonpartisan
route for incumbents and challengers. The service can explain
implementation steps and consequences of a stated option using
verified facts. Campaign strategy, political drafting and advocacy
remain with contestants. Under [GP01–GP02](../20/01-estimates-formation-and-programme.md),
the Assembly's analysts provide the distinct optional costing
service. Agencies receive only the scope needed to answer.

**HT34 — Protect questions and release common facts.** Keep a
contestant's exploratory questions, private proposal and confidential
negotiating position under independent restricted custody. Where a
request exposes an omitted or materially changed current-law fact,
verify it and update the shared portfolio pack on the common timetable.
Remove links to the requester and private question. Where publication
could reveal a protected question or record, arrange lawful safe
aggregation, redaction or a common release through the independent
reviewer, with dated reasons and continued review of the restriction.
A material public correction cannot be withheld at the requester’s
or minister's discretion. Give protected originals only to authorized
custodians and independent reviewers within their scope. AI may
assist with navigation and reconciliation in authorized custody.
A provider, model or shared account cannot decide access, infer
eligibility or reuse protected work for another client.

**HT35 — Fund first access and a useful alternative.** The Assembly
votes a dated source for its service, translation and independent
review. Each career department provides identified competent staff
and a named alternate evidence custodian with lawful access before
the window opens. Protect existing statutory and urgent service
work, counting each qualified hour once. Publish the request window,
typical workload and prioritization rule. Allocate a usable first
pass before discretionary additional work for any one contestant.
Where a bottleneck remains, provide an independently verified
partial answer, identify missing evidence and the next available
slot. Allocate follow-up by the same published criteria. Trace any
access loss or work displaced, including across shared suppliers.

**HT36 — Remedy exclusion before the relevant decision.** Give
requesters a receipt, usable decision date and specific reasons for
refusal, delay or partial delivery. An independent reviewer with
lawful access to original records and an unimplicated clerk hears
urgent challenges. Set the review date from the affected ballot or
formation decision and the time needed to use the answer. The
reviewer can direct release through a competent alternate, a
common factual correction or reassignment of a protected slot.
Where authority or protected material is contested, use the existing
court and parliamentary routes. Verify actual receipt in useful time.
Publish safe totals and systemic failures. A missed briefing remains
a recorded loss and does not delay an election, enlarge the caretaker's
authority or transfer political office.

**HT37 — Change scope at formation and appointment.** After the
ballot, parties or members actually participating in lawful formation
may request factual implementation comparisons for a declared
negotiation purpose. The Assembly Clerk records procedural
participation under existing law using minimum necessary disclosure.
Eligibility requires neither the largest seat count nor an incumbent's
consent. Protect competing negotiations, apply the same capacity
and review rules, and end special access when the participant exits
formation or the relevant office is lawfully filled. Statutory
briefings, protected working records and scoped production authority
pass to the actual appointee at their lawful points under HT02,
HT05 and HT21. Preparation provides no command or right to act.
The parties keep responsibility for bargaining and elected decisions.

**HT38 — Test a hostile incumbent and the handover tail.** Compare a
public pack alone, confidential discretionary access and the
bounded common route on one workload and election timetable.
Check late entrants, split formation talks, suppression of shared
facts, office requests repurposed for a campaign, protected records
and the effect of a denied request before the deadline. Budget
ordinary work, independent review and successor receiving together.
Preserve lawful in-flight requests, published corrections and
evidence for review when staff or systems change. Close access and
dispose of protected drafts under lawful custody and retention,
preserving material under a competent hold. Reopen on unequal
usable access, chilling disclosures, displaced duties or a simpler
way to protect practical replacement.
