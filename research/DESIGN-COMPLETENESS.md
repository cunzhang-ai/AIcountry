# Design coverage and evidence

The [Edition 1 design](edition/README.md) specifies a constitutional
parliamentary democracy with protected local choices, common guarantees,
and accountable AI. The first governing programme covers democratic
authority, continuity of lawful obligations, and repairs for documented
failures. Each prospective act needs its applicable authority and resources.
