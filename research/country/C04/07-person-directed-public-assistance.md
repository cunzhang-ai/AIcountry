# C04. Public assistance works for the person seeking help

Design175 ·4November2026 · AH01–AH16 proposed service, not an adopted entitlement

**Choose a free, person-directed core assistance service before and across
public-service requests, with a funded independent route where interests conflict.**
The person can understand options/letters, prepare a chosen act and coordinate
specified services with qualified help. Optional AI may support the work; human
help, private working space and direct access remain available.

C11/DP already supplies useful AI tasks/shared funding; AP already requires
agency assistance; C07 has first receivers/joint plans; B13 already funds advice/
representation by need. AH concretizes a distinct person-side public debtor,
provider/source and continuity relationship. It does not claim those duties
were absent or offer an unlimited personal AI/compute subscription.

[The joined country choice](../../settlement/public-assistance/README.md)
compares repair of current agency help, this limited person-side service and a
broader personal-agent utility. Actual independent providers, benefit, source,
simultaneous capacity, legal adoption and better lives remain unproved. A helpful
conversation is not an application, professional licence, government decision,
medical consent or a political mandate.

[TU effective take-up](12-effective-take-up-and-early-benefit-delivery.md)
assigns the programme's own early-delivery duty. AH remains the person's
independent support route, including when the agency's proof or payment fails.

[FRE01–FRE05](07/05-essential-services-and-free-refusal.md)
protects separately due services through refusal, exit or complaint.
The person's AH support does not replace independently controlled
payment, review and service receiving.
