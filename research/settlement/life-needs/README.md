The reference keeps the authored 100,000-person case and source scopes. It does
not revive the old 55,000-job/wage distribution, convert GBP/CU or assume a local
population, supplier, lender or foreign partner exists. Counts/prices are design
hypotheses; no clinical standard or actual public budget follows from this table.

WHO's [WISN overview](https://www.who.int/tools/wisn) motivates workload/activity
and availability distinctions; our declared arithmetic is not a qualified WISN
assessment. WHO's [Pacific health-system discussion](https://www.who.int/westernpacific/activities/strengthening-pacific-health-systems)
identifies specialist/transport constraints. The [NI referral page](https://online.hscni.net/our-work/travelfortreatment/transfer-for-treatment-by-the-strategic-planning-and-performance-group/)
provides a real operational example, including its no-travel-advance rule.
HN consciously chooses direct booking/advance access instead; it does not import
NI eligibility, panels or exceptionality. These sources do not prove project
partner availability, safe capacity or measured comparative advantage.

No person must become an employer, accept institutional care, supply unpaid family
work or finance necessary public travel to satisfy a model. Actual legal authority,
professional/affected judgment, complete source/legacy/FX, safe receiving and
better lives remain unknown. The next work is the whole ordinary-basket/occupancy/
producer/source account, not an automatic extension of the newest clinical detail.
