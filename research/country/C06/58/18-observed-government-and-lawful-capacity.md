# WF77–WF80. Start from what an existing country actually has to carry

*Design213 · Part of [the national source comparison](../58-country-source-and-capacity-frontier.md).*

**WF77 — Use an observed year to anchor the comparison.** Select Denmark
2025 as a historical reference for the first-budget comparison. Carry
the full public purpose and whole-economy use accounts. Reconcile the
disjoint central, social insurance, regional and municipal accounts,
eliminating matched internal transfers only for the consolidated view.
Retain each transfer's payer and receiver for budget and continuity.
Keep each source's definition and release date. No appropriation,
country selection or new financial adequacy follows. The 2023 production
and work model stays at its own vintage and cannot be scaled up as an
observed 2025 resource map.

**WF78 — Separate amounts, actual law and usable service.** The existing
public owners supply the financial, authority and continuity baselines
required by FB13–FB16. Attach actual source authority, payment dates,
restricted balances and independent budgets at their appropriate scope.
An accounting total is a reference for comparison. It cannot fill
missing law, cash, accepted skills, readiness or actual receipt.
Quarterly full-time equivalents by purpose and by subsector inform
different questions. Keep them as separate partitions. Test paid rest,
training, peak workloads, simultaneous supply and actual receiving
under T and the applicable sector law. Use only protected necessary
evidence. Expenditure per reported worker cannot serve as a wage,
staffing cap or cross-service efficiency score.

**WF79 — Correct known unsafe dependencies before relying on them.**
Where a prospective change depends on work or a recovery route that
credible evidence shows to be unlawful or unavailable, the sector
owner removes that assumption from the continuation and proposal
capacity. Keep the due service, wage and remedy obligation in the
account. Distinguish earlier loss, a subsequent lawful change and
what the new route actually delivers. Assign and pay for actual safe
staffing or an accepted fallback under existing law. The Assembly
gets the separate recurring cost, finite correction, unmet duty and
effect on proposed starts. Independently controlled services retain
their own funds, records and correction decisions. An AI may trace
contradictions or compare schedules. It cannot legalize a shift,
decide a case, or certify the only route by which its result is reviewed.

**WF80 — Choose a funded, reversible comparison before a larger start.**
For the tested staffing and recovery examples, prefer repair of the
verified dependency and a separately paid independent test before any
new reliance on it. Preserve a competent established corrective route
where current evidence qualifies it. Compare ordinary staffing and
repair, lawful negotiated alternate arrangements with actual rest
and receipt, and replacement providers or system paths. The last two
can be less disruptive or more resilient when separately funded, but
cannot transfer judicial power or justify coercion. If a failing route
can only be maintained temporarily, use the bounded existing
continuity law and report outstanding obligations. No new power to
detain, waive rest, cancel hearing dates, or suspend enacted rights is
selected. Reopen on verified compliance, changed authority or staffing,
and actual outside review of useful-time service.
