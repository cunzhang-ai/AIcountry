## Fictional one-day capacity collision

A previously scheduled selection hearing, urgent campaign-access case and an
unrelated force incident overlap. These role-hours are authored assumptions,
not a proposed three-person panel, actual rates or a complete national cost.

The public source has a drawn 36-person panel with a three-hour sitting planned; only 26 eligible members can attend the same sitting. Twenty-four affirmative votes still fail its 27-present quorum. The full plan lacks ten members; the quorum alone lacks one. Substitution requires the pre-drawn lawful order and actual acceptance, not added staff hours or AI proxies.

The 164-hour plan includes the full panel, not a legal requirement that all 36 attend. The quorum-only panel subset is 27 × 3 = 81h; with support it is 137 planned vs 115 available, a 22-hour role gap (3 panel plus 19 support), conditional on actual lawful replacement, concurrent attendance and 24 votes. Neither a spare hour nor a court order supplies a consenting eligible person.

The 36-member panel time is included above, but actual pay, care/travel/standby, released ordinary
work, training, equipment, accepted receiving, taxes, prices and owned sources
are additional unknowns, not included free inputs. A judge cannot supply both
hearings at once; CJ is not the ordinary bench; a clinician is not an investigator.
No spare aggregate hours, SF payment stock or old country bid fills a skill gap.

Preserve first receipt/source evidence and seek specific lawful relief; an order
cannot invent eligibility, campaign money, judges, delivery or a winner. Immediate
aid and outside-command preservation do not wait for a charge. A failed P09
hearing requires actual release under its rule, not renamed custody. Failed
execution and lost pre-vote opportunity remain harm even after later compensation.
Due clinical, election and civil claims, worker rights and creditor/FX ownership
survive the collision. These invented fixtures support no real-world verdict.
