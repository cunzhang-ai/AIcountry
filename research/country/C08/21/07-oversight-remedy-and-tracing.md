# Public control of the protector and an executable liberty remedy

*CD13–CD14; part of [C08/21](../21-conflict-detention-and-release.md).*

**CD13 — Separate command, original records, inspection and judgment.**
The elected Assembly authorizes general law and money; cabinet/command issues
only lawful mission/custody instructions. Civilian judges decide liberty;
qualified health teams care; independent civilian record/inspection functions
preserve tracing and inspect actual conditions. Apply existing merit/public
appointment, opposition-accessible scrutiny, qualified conflict deputies,
reviewable removal, ordinary funding and outside-command case allocation.
No new universal war guardian or final intelligence appeals office is created.

Keep an actual national custody/information register under civilian justice
administration, securely linked to original first-restraint/handoff records,
accessible to counsel/courts and competent protective bodies. No captive's
family, raw medical file, exact vulnerable location or interview is public
by default. Lawful audit/Assembly oversight receives usable protected source
access; a minister/vendor cannot own the only copy or block a lawful successor.
Accountability/correction history survives war's end without permanent public
exposure of sensitive testimony or automatic model training.

Facilitate actual treaty-required Protecting Power/ICRC access and national
independent visits, private interviews and original-condition inspection.
Neutral custody has its own treaty scope; national additional protections
are not a false claim that every POW article automatically applies. A specific
immediate physical access danger requires recorded narrow arrangements and
prompt court/visitor alternative; no open-ended secrecy veto. An invitation
is not an accepted staffed visit, and inspection cannot replace direct court
relief. Freely chosen prisoner representatives can bring collective concerns,
without being selected by guards or waiving another person's rights.

Opposition-inclusive Assembly scrutiny follows C10/03, with actual qualified
legal/technical help and protected access to grounds, full costs, injuries,
complaints, review failures and release receipts. It cannot adjudicate named
guilt, dictate custody in a case or use withheld budget/data to protect allies.
Shared sponsors, command, contractors, source systems and vendors are assessed
as cumulative power, not counted independent because their labels differ.

**CD14 — The court's order needs a real executor.** A restrained person,
chosen helper/counsel or protective representative can reach independent
liberty review directly without an account, perfect identity or complete
technical dossier. Provide safe communication, advice, interpreter and original
records. Emergency/foreign-policy secrecy cannot suspend this access. Actual
coercion/torture/illegal custody opens B state/individual remedies and distinct
K/G review; no strict criminal guilt merely because a clock or system failed.

A release/correction order names responsible commander/civilian executor,
actual place, useful due time, protected person/records and receiving support.
Verify compliance outside implicated command, with court enforcement and
lawful replacement of obstructing officials under existing law. Missing money
or an ally's refusal does not mark the judgment paid. Missing/dead persons
retain independent investigation, safe tracing/family notice, clinical death
verification/body care and actual claims; a civil death/status entry cannot
erase disappearance or transfer liability into a statistical count.
