# Decision: bound regulatory power and specify financial losses

15 October 2026 · Design 74 · Same AI author/controller; no independent reviewers

## Requested design choice and baseline

The full objective is a better future country with meaningful democracy,
complete national functions and accountable AI assistance. Current design 73
already assigns bank, conduct, monetary, resolution and court roles but leaves
many operative thresholds, insured claims, claim ranking and regulatory
penalty powers unspecified. The bounded deliverable is an explicit national
candidate for those missing rules and its consequential source-finance audit.

The exact 378 current Markdown artifacts and commitment inventory are preserved
in [379 predecessor artifacts](predecessor-design-73/SNAPSHOT.md). Current
software, source constitution/manuscripts and publication permissions remain
unchanged. No real affected public, enacted national code, bank, staffing or
national budget exists. This research selection cannot authorize any of them.

## Diagnosis and coherent alternatives

One diagnosis is weak enforceability: protection duties without a defined
inspection, correction and monetary process encourage selective informal power.
Another is dependence: the regulator, public borrower, bank, platform and
service provider can control each other's funding and evidence. A third is
resource scarcity: a penalty or a new agency cannot create a receiver, equity,
usable cash, original evidence or additional trained people. All three matter;
no universal coordination platform cures them by recording more events.

Compare the existing principles-only design, limited common process plus
specified plural finance, and a structural public-safe-payment/narrow-credit
system. A discretion-heavy super-regulator is not selected: its framing,
budget and enforcement could become a new sovereign economic controller.
Pure contract/private litigation leaves dependent residents without timely
protection. Public provision can remain preferable where actual access fails.

Select V's closed duties and bounded powers, with independent P-strength proof
before punitive monetary orders; actual nonpunitive correction keeps its own
purpose/proof. Select M as a conditional regulated-plural candidate, with
client-property separation, limited insurance and explicit public losses.
Retain the structural narrow-payment alternative rather than absorbing it
as merely another feature of the selected model. No option is shown superior.

## Consequential findings and values

V defines prior law, fair entry, lawful evidence, 72-hour urgent hearing,
finite fine bands, actual attribution, needs-protected collection, direct
review and receiving. Its statutory schedule is closed; omitted sector
thresholds remain gaps. A changed model or regulatory label cannot create law.
M defines perimeter, capital, concentration/liquidity, price/authorization,
client custody, coverage, loss priority, compensation, stability tools,
monetary control, elected risk and migration. Nationally chosen numbers are
contestable hypotheses, not borrowed proof of an optimum or treaty conformity.

M03 permits domestic-sovereign exposure no greater than eligible equity.
The old current book explicitly states bank E100 and sovereign paper 250.
That position exceeds the proposed ceiling by 150 in its own fictional units.
Retain the boundary and reject this financing as M-compliant. Replacing 150
needs a genuine funded holder or at least 150 new eligible equity plus changed
counterpart accounts, not a shell, guessed AI gain or public exception.
This new incompatibility reopens the national material/source choice now.

M07's twelve-basket insurance is not twelve months of every actual living
cost. M08's 0.4% premium/2% target does not fund every systemic failure;
its full covered-liability Treasury line is a real contingent public choice,
with actual funding required before promising capacity. M11 adds a separate
compensation exposure. None is new available cash or a free central-bank loan.
The old 37-function bids do not qualify new staff, insurance or compensation.

## Objections, source evidence and authority

[Official sources](sources.json) were inspected via the web tool: dated BCBS
LEV20, FSB April 2024 and IADI September 2025. These are linked publications,
not outcome/participant observations. No complete immutable publisher download
is claimed. Source version and source family remain explicit; one AI's synthesis
and arithmetic are not independent reviews or multiple original observations.

Material objections remain: criminal-style monetary proof can make enforcement
costly/slow; ordinary negligent reporting fines may chill small providers;
8% gross equity, broad commitments and capped derivatives may constrain useful
credit/hedging; liquidity weights can fail or become procyclical; sovereign
concentration limits may impede financing essential services; insurance and
compensation socialize residual risk. 2% inflation and all other numbers need
qualified and affected review. No empirical calibration resolves these values.

Reasons for conditional selection: bounded proof/needs rules constrain abuse;
explicit claims/losses expose who pays; holding our own sovereign to a limit
prevents declaring an exception merely to pass. Keep the narrow-credit/public-
payment rival and allow it to displace M on whole-package evidence. No outcome
score can replace practical opposition, popular fiscal control or enforceable
remedies. No new cross-agency super-appeal or self-approving rule is created.

Appointments, deputies, for-cause removal, funding, direct sources, court
allocation and enforcement follow existing qualified/Assembly/independent
routes; no reviewer is appointed in fact. Common sponsor/vendor dependence
can defeat every named safeguard. Public/private providers receive the same
substantive duties, with actual authority and case conflicts independently
reviewed. The legislature chooses general policy and risk; case adjudicators
do not set taxes, allocate homes, exclude opponents or waive a friend's debt.
