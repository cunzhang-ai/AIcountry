# Keep future capacity and present protection in the same decision

*Design512 · Proposed rule within [future options and hard-to-reverse choices](../04-future-options-and-irreversibility.md).*

**Preserve a physical and legal account beside the economic appraisal.** For a material decision whose effects reach beyond its present budget or term, publish dated rights, safe service, physical stocks, ecological thresholds, qualified work, owned cash, debt, restoration and retained alternatives. Use the evidenced life of the asset, obligation or plausible loss, including what happens after scheduled closure. For genuinely commensurable effects, also publish dated real amounts, discounted comparisons, assumptions and the rate or condition at which the recommendation switches. A discount calculation cannot replace a due payment or a physical limit. Show cohorts, territories, actual recipients and unknowns separately. Compare action, repaired retention and a credible structural alternative under the same need and concurrent adverse paths. Demonstrate separately that each indispensable reserve, crew, site and external supplier can meet all dates for which it is counted.

**Keep future protection usable under a changed government or asset owner.** Before relying on the investment or transfer, identify the first date at which delay forecloses a repair, the earliest material concurrent peak, and the latest useful decision before each. The source instrument names observable thresholds, the last useful elected choice, procurement or mobilization date, expected exposure, funded observation and the competent office even when the risk comes from inaction. A missed monitoring date never shifts the exposure. The instrument names lawful owners, verified funds, safe receiving, review and handback. Check for falling use and fixed costs where successful exit can strand remaining users or shift load to a shared network. Compare ring-fenced assurance with ordinary recurring public provision and a physically viable smaller or different design. Test insolvency, revenue loss and overlapping civil, service and ecological demands. Any loss of the incumbent obligor activates only the applicable existing legal duties and funded fallback. Keep restoration claims open when assurance is unavailable. Preserve a safe essential route through the next inspection and subsequent exposure, not merely until the successor signs.

**Give the elected opposition a real long-horizon choice.** Use existing independently funded evidence, audit and fiscal offices. Before the first binding decision, publish an independent challenge to material assumptions and a source-qualified alternative where available. Give residents, younger people, relevant rights holders and people bearing external effects accessible evidence, confidentiality and review on their proper standing. The sponsor cannot appoint or defund the case reviewer. Secure timely source rights and qualified interpretation through measurement and vendor change. Maintain independently repeatable observations or a qualified overlap with the outgoing method before retiring it. A noncomparable series leaves the relevant release unresolved and preserves the last valid legal baseline, continuing duties and evidence challenge. Put the exact source, scope, costs and effects of any opposition alternative to the applicable elected process. Where a legal floor binds, enforce it under its own law. Within the lawful set elected bodies can choose differently with recorded reasons. Reviewers or models do not represent future people or determine the elected result.

**Carry denied or deferred decisions forward without entrenching a project.** A credible warning, changed control, lapse of physical capacity or material method revision reopens the affected future use before irreversible work under existing release and review law. Preserve independently lawful services, legal duties, claims, original deadlines and contestable observations. Record what is a current obligation, later contingent exposure, feasible option or expired assumption. Show which low-cost option remains realistically available after each stage and whose ability to use it changes. A missed public choice leaves its continuing consequences and remedy visible. Budget approval, forecast success or silence does not release a dependent project. AI can compare scenarios and trace shared dependencies. Qualified people determine safety and actual receiving; the relevant elected body controls the public choice.
