## Coherent alternatives and selection

| Option | Benefit / loss / disposition |
|---|---|
| Retain sector/ex-post law only | Less upstream concentration and entry burden; potentially too late for irreversible/uncompensable harm. Strong live rival, not selected for the identified gap |
| Shared voluntary evaluation/insurance | Plural learning and price signals; participation, shared-source independence and uninsurable nonuser harm remain. Useful supporting choice, inadequate sole duty |
| Narrow evidence/pathway pre-exposure regime | Targets actual severe external acts, keeps contained inquiry and ordinary use free; still risks capture, delay and insider barriers. Selected working bargain |
| Broad frontier training/release licensing | More advance visibility/control; high research/entry/centralization cost and unreliable size boundary. Not selected absent stronger necessity evidence |
| Exceptional prohibition/moratorium | Can avoid more irreversible exposure; sacrifices beneficial science/access and concentrates permission power. Not selected as a general default; actual particular law would need its own democratic case |
