# National choices and evidence for the first decision

*Design548 · Evidence for the [national settlement](../../NATIONAL-SETTLEMENT.md). The [authority table](../../country/AUTHORITY.md) assigns competence.*

Denmark and Copenhagen are the common accounting and institutional reference
for testing a lawful retrofit. Compare the actual incumbent after competent
repair with the selected country. Keep other jurisdictions' findings on their
own dates, populations and authority. They can test a mechanism or suggest a

constructed exercises distinct. No entry grants public authority.

## Choose the first empirical comparison

Use one municipal subsistence payer, one defined class of already established
awards and two consecutive complete due cycles. Begin with the strongest
lawful ordinary payout and correction under the existing obligation to pay.
Compare ordinary assisted receipt with an independently callable route
if the ordinary operator or branch cannot serve. Include the next roster,
source and independent challenge. Keep the relevant costs, qualified staff
and remedy on the actual same dates. Use the [paired opening choices](../../settlement/first-round-mandate/11-first-evidence-and-budget-choice.md).

This comparison can choose a narrower repair or qualify a bounded alternate.
It leaves national adoption, joint democratic operation, full public finance,
ecological limits and wider lived outcomes open to their own evidence.
