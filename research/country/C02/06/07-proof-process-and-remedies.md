# Belonging: proof, finite processing and received remedies

*Canonical MB15–MB17; part of [C02/06](../06-citizenship-and-secure-belonging.md).*

**MB15 — Separate public duties and finite clocks.** A designated career
nationality officer determines citizenship under enacted entitlements; a different
residence/protection officer determines entry/status. The civil registrar records
lawful events and corrections; the electoral service separately verifies C02/03
eligibility. Police, carrier, employer, consul and vendor do not determine them.
For a constitutive citizenship grant, separate evidence assessment from final
conferral, preserve authenticated authority/source and audit lawful predicates
before effect. An operative conferral/recognition is the designated officer's
authenticated signed final act expressly identifying the person and operative
status, evidenced by actual issue or register entry. Drafts are not final acts;
failed registration/notice cannot undo or postpone an actually issued final act.
Protected recording and usable notice remain duties. Once that act occurs, a prior audit/procedure defect cannot retrospectively
make it no grant. A pending assessment, fake signature or copied credential is
different. Automatic acquisition remains independent of an entry or notice.
No secret mass discretionary grant or automatic database sweep.

The first authorized status/protection receiver gives receipt in the reachable
interaction, preserves the earliest credible attempted filing and retains routing
responsibility until the competent decision office accepts. Border referral,
reclassification and missing evidence do not restart that processing origin. Give urgent provisional proof/access within twenty-four elapsed hours,
earlier when useful protection requires it. Within seven calendar days provide
one consolidated missing-evidence request and actual assistance. State-held or
unsafe foreign evidence needs official inquiry/safe alternatives; serial requests
cannot restart clocks. Decide routine extracts/replacements within thirty days,
ordinary residence/renewal within ninety, all nationality/protection merits within
180, with earlier routine
resolution. One extension of at most ninety days needs independent court approval before
expiry for a specified material barrier; backlog or foreign silence alone is
insufficient. Silence is a breached duty, neither citizenship nor refusal.

Timely renewal/review preserves prior permission and lawful work/access incidents
until usable decision and effective challenge. A genuine pending protection or
nationality-undetermined claim has provisional stay/work while it is actually
resolved; an unsupported duplicate filing cannot restart a decided claim.
A simple assisted request or reasonable indication triggers low-threshold inquiry;
no technical dossier is required. Triage identifies material facts potentially engaging
citizenship, statelessness or non-return for a person under actual national control.
The designated status officer must give evidence/reasons for refusal or alleged
duplication, with direct urgent suspensive challenge; an intake clerk or AI cannot
refuse to register a claimed risk. Initial stay protects assessment/review.
Recognized pending inquiry has provisional stay/work, including an actual disputed
citizenship claim meeting that threshold; an overseas application creates no
foreign admission or provisional vote. No unsupported duplicate resets a final
determination; genuinely new risk still receives effective pre-return assessment.
The competent ordinary court decides established legal entitlement, not just
refers indefinitely. A statutory citizenship-entitlement judgment becomes itself
constitutive once ordinary merits appeal opportunities are exhausted or validly
waived and it is operative; the registrar then records it. The losing officer
has no conferral-signature veto; E15 acquires no new nationality authority. Constitutional referral
under CJ does not disable urgent individual protection or extend another clock.

**MB16 — Proof belongs to a person, not a platform.** Use relevant records,
school/work/tenancy history, credible testimony and independently checked
attestations in combination. No exclusive passport, address, biometric or account
requirement; no forced full clinical/family file when a limited attestation works.
A compromised card may be cancelled for security only with usable replacement
proof; it does not cancel underlying status. Keep disputed identities separate
until lawful reconciliation, original meaning/provenance, safe correction history
and purpose-limited access/retention. Safe foreign inquiry is not universal disclosure.

Paper, supported oral, remote and future communication can authenticate a legal
act without making a model's interpretation the person's consent. Preserve
unfamiliar concepts and disclose uncertain translation. A mandate to assist an
application is not a mandate to renounce, settle, disclose or vote. AI may assist
search/translation, never secretly decide nationality, dangerousness or entitlement.

**MB17 — An order must reach the person.** Independent ordinary civil/admin
courts hear fact/law, urgent stay, entitlement and unlawful delay with funded
necessary assistance. Give reasons, proportionate appeal, evidence access and
actual accepted handoff. Status denial/loss or removal has suspensive review;
no filing fee consumes a useful protection opportunity. Correction orders name
responsible officer, exact status/record/payment act, lawful source, deadline,
executor and verification of usable receipt. Existing B liability/compensation
and public remedies require their own proof; nationality does not promise every
foreign loss. E15 alternates execute existing due record/payment acts, not grant
nationality, invent admission places or appropriate cash. Real unpaid duties,
missing staff and refused foreign cooperation remain visible with lawful redress.

[AP01–AP12](../../C04/06-reachable-administration-and-finite-decisions.md)
provides common receipt/proof/omission safeguards. MB's explicit special source
clocks, acquisition/conferral distinction, status protection and suspensive
review remain; AP's ordinary 30-working-day default does not replace them.
Neither an expired clock nor unavailable foreign proof creates nationality.

[VR civil registration](../../C08/29-civil-registration-and-identity.md)
gives the civil registrar actual event-proof and correction functions.
MB's distinct officers, merits clocks, grants and stays continue. A
new civil extract does not replace an operative nationality act.
