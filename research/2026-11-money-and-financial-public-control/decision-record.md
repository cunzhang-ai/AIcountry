## Public risk, source and actual protection

The charter requires finite typed class/group/commitment/collateral/sovereign/
intraday bounds and separate deliverable-currency FX stock/floor/annual gross
outflow limits. Unknown fields permit no new risk, not unlimited discretion or
a zero assumption. Initial amounts are legitimate context calibration fields:
the Assembly chooses on disclosed original stocks/stress/incidence/rights and
joint C06 sources before operations; no universal nominal amount is invented.
Annual audit/report/hearings and a protected June30 full-N reasoned response
may retain, propose valid amendment or commission funded comparison; silence
neither expands risk nor expires currency. A resolution is no substitute Act.

Gross drawn/binding undrawn common-control exposure is counted once; collateral,
expected recoveries and private/insurer/Treasury FX are not free headroom.
Prudential facts, monetary credit/title decision and actual settlement are
separate qualified roles, with2h help/6h sufficient-fact/24h original-receipt
status and2h settlement outers, no promise that solvency is known in6h. An
exceptional bank episode has90+one90 original-day limits; a normal repo label
cannot refinance insolvency. M07/M09/SF/customer/worker/court clocks continue.

Losses and own reserve liabilities are paired, not Treasury revenue. Recognize
negative monetary equity without an automatic commercial-bank insolvency or
service cut; first verified interim/audit finding starts30-day plan, not late
annual acknowledgment. Fiscal recap/indemnity still needs separate M15 law/source.
Paid oversight and ordinary outside review/MC-scoped B19/B18 correction/payment
need actual independent staff, credentials, cash and accepted receiving; courts
cannot choose policy rates or make up a bank loan or appropriation.

Public bank/risk/price/loss disclosure is finite, with minimum safe publication
by180days and narrowly proved granular source protection under annual court
review/full protected access. Natural-person PD rights retain their own route.
Both run risk and concealment/capture remain; no confidentiality label settles
that value choice. Future communication preserves actual meaning, source,
scoped mandate, dissent and new-event review without universal identity/AI gate.
