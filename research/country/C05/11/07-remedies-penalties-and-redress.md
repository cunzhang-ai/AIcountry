# Lawful correction, court penalties and received redress

Design 98 · Proposed research, not enacted law or verified operation.

**MR01 — Contestable public correction.** After B16 notice, evidence, ordinarily 20 working days to
answer an adverse proposal, and reasons, the markets authority may issue a prospective nonpunitive
V05 order to stop a proved cartel or exclusion, remove a specified barrier, give proportionate
access or interoperability, correct a contractual term, return a specifically identifiable unlawful
payment, or publish an accurate notice. Contested distributed damages, gain calculations and
disputed third-party ownership go to court. The order must identify the exact MP duty, facts,
affected market/persons, necessary scope, safe executor, due date, review and exit condition. Actual
legal and technical feasibility, KR knowledge rights, PD privacy, security and safety remain
constraints; a complaint is not a compulsory data-sharing license. An order is reviewed at least
every six months and ends when the proved barrier and necessity end. It cannot impose new prices or
general service duties that the Assembly did not enact, seize an IP right, dissolve a body, or
replace bank M09–M11 resolution.

**MR02 — Urgent protection.** On V04's documented credible imminent serious harm, a competent
official may order a narrowly necessary temporary stop, preservation or warning within an existing
enumerated power. Preservation may protect an established lawful access status quo; V04 creates no
new positive access entitlement. A new or disputed temporary access order requires the express MP08
substantive ground and an independent B17 court finding of likely breach, useful-time necessity,
least restrictive safe scope, actual accepting capacity and fair paid terms; KR/PD and other
permissions remain. No access label renews an expired MP15 merger-only hold. Give immediate usable
notice unless a court authorizes limited delay, B17 independent hearing within 72 actual hours and
only its one necessary 72-hour extension, then reasoned bounded expiry and review. A lost client
route, wage payment, clinical supply or essential service has a named receiving executor and lawful
funding route; urgency does not create assets or an automatic provider of last resort. A model
concentration score, market share or untested prediction alone is insufficient. Ordinary contested
market restructuring uses court, not repeated emergency notices.

**MR03 — Structural change and successors.** Only an independent court may prohibit a transaction
after contested merits or order divestiture, separation, forced access involving a material property
transfer, or compulsory sale. The enacted MP predicate must be proved; assess whether a narrower
stop, nondiscriminatory access or public option can remedy actual harm. A forced sale requires
specified assets/rights, fair contestable valuation and payment route, viable and independent
accepting successor, worker and pension obligations, secured/client/creditor claims, tax and
public-contract continuity, confidential data transfer limits, lawful licenses and foreign claims. A
common funder, cloud or control arrangement cannot masquerade as an independent buyer. Give owners
and affected workers/users/creditors proportional notice, a B17 interim route and a B18 execution
plan. A court may refuse an unworkable transfer while granting narrower relief and recording
unremedied dependence. The markets authority never appropriates sale proceeds or funds its own
operation from a fine.

**MR04 — Private redress.** Preventive MP/B17 standing includes a worker, user, supplier or entrant
with credible evidence of likely material effect from the challenged conduct/concentration; already
suffered monetary loss is not required to request lawful interim prevention. This is distinct from
the proof of actual loss/causation required for damages below. A person or eligible representative
with a credible factual showing of actual economic or essential-access harm may sue directly under
B13–B15. B14 permits necessary protected discovery and a reasoned adverse civil inference after
unjustified nonproduction; there is no automatic cartel-harm or pass-through presumption, reversal
of criminal burden, treble damages, or liability from share alone. Recover proved actual loss,
restoration of an unlawfully taken payment or an appropriately traced net gain only to the extent
source law authorizes and without duplicate recovery. Attributable causation, mitigation actually
available, lawful costs and independent appellate review matter. B20 governs representative cases,
notice, settlement, distribution and conflicts; inability to fund a bond cannot defeat essential
relief. A fine is paid to Treasury and does not substitute for compensation. Unpaid judgment remains
visible under B18/U rather than fictional receipt.

**MR05 — Narrow VD11 class B candidate.** If and only if the Assembly prospectively adds a precise
VD11 schedule and amends V06/V11, an independent civilian court may impose V's class B fine for
either: (a) intentional implementation of a naked agreement among actual competitors to fix sale or
purchase prices, restrict output, allocate customers/territory, or rig a bid, where the prosecution
proves no integral lawful production or collective-bargaining arrangement explains the restraint; or
(b) knowing or reckless consummation of an objectively reportable statutory merger during the actual
live MP15 standstill including its one MP15 court-extended hold, by a person with control and
ability over that consummation. A merely proposed agreement, unilateral high price, mere dominance,
good-faith boundary dispute, below-gate acquisition, authority forecast, late response or ordinary
exclusion is outside VD11. No class A general reporting/record offense is added; exact preexisting
K/VD falsification or obstruction predicates apply only if independently proved. This schedule
creates no custody, community work, fee surcharge or punitive license exclusion by implication. VD11
does not punish breach of a below-gate MP16 court order by analogy; only that order's independently
enacted enforcement route and actual fault/process can apply. The listed cartel acts are exhaustive
for punishment; MP04's wider civil boundary is not silently imported.

**MR06 — Penalty constraints.** Apply V06's maximum 180 Q02 means-based units for a responsible
natural person and Q08's middle body maximum (larger of 5% verified defendant prior-year revenue or
twice proved net illicit gain), all subject to zero minimum, proportionality, actual
control/fault/ability, P counsel/proof/exclusion/appeal, Q03 poverty and collection limits, V08
same-course anti-stacking and V07/Q07 attribution. A parent is not charged on fictional whole-group
revenue; a director is not guilty from office. File the V class B usable charge within six years of
completed conduct with only Q12's proved concealment/evasion toll of at most three extra years. The
MP17 merger challenge period is separate from this punitive clock and B15's ordinary civil claims.
No penalty for a pre-commencement act or a newly classified pending case.

**MR07 — Delivery and lawful succession.** Each order names its accountable official, payer/asset,
actual recipient, lawful executor, deadline and proof of receipt. B18 civil enforcement applies to
private judgments; B19 to a state duty, with LF's separate force ceiling. Restitution and redress
rank as actual claims under B/U/M; fines do not jump secured, client or worker rights. Before
commencement the Assembly maps old competition powers, pending reviews/cases, standstill dates,
confidentiality undertakings, evidence, rights, paid investigators, defense counsel, specialist
judges, interpreters and receivers to named lawful successors under V12; no retroactive new
predicate or automatic approval. Fund independent expertise and court capacity from ordinary
appropriation, not expected penalties. If these cannot operate, preserve lawful existing protections
and expose the shortage rather than asserting readiness. MP20 fixes 180-calendar-day commencement
and pending-deal treatment; actual staffing/readiness cannot move that date.

*Part of [the competition statute](../11-competition-and-economic-power.md); normative thresholds and institutions, no authority from publication.*
