# Coverage, choice and elected authority

*Part of [PL public concessions](../64-public-concessions-and-future-budget-control.md).*

**PL01 — Follow the public burden through the legal form.** Apply
this schedule to a material long-term service concession,
privately financed public asset, availability contract, lease
with equivalent financing, or linked arrangement involving
public payments, material guarantee, exclusive public-service
rights or public step-in exposure. Examine the whole network of
agreements, including public companies and controlled vehicles,
subcontracts, security and side instruments. Count related
transactions together. Materiality limits are published in
law by size relative to the actual payer's budget, duration,
and control of an essential service. A guarantee or exclusive
essential bottleneck grant always requires the applicable
express elected authority. Routine divisible purchases within
their existing mandate use SC and DB. Public accounting keeps
actual ownership and legal liability intact.

**PL02 — Compare the service before choosing the financing.** The
sector owner specifies the same required access, safe quality,
timing, rights and terminal asset condition for each option.
Compare viable retained repair, public financing with separable
competitive contracts, and the proposed long-term bundle.
Include a user, cooperative or local option where feasible.
Compare real borrower and counterparty capacity, initial money,
whole-life resource and cash paths, user charges, risk and
public management. Show how any claimed earlier start or
transferred risk produces actual service value. Publish common
assumptions, monetary basis, discount rates and sensitivity,
with annual cash and full legal exposure separately. A cheaper
bid for weaker maintenance or later access is a different offer.
The chosen method must have a reason beyond fitting an annual
capital ceiling or an accounting boundary.

**PL03 — Reserve the long commitment for a recorded elected vote.**
Before a material commitment, the competent Assembly or elected
council approves a named ceiling, term, covered service, payment
schedule, currency, tariff bounds, maximum legally payable
guarantees, permitted termination compensation and the planned
paid successor. National and local funding and legal decisions
are separate where both are required. Preserve the actual DB,
RF, LR and SC powers. For a material negotiated award, publish
a usable final comparison and protected evidence for elected
scrutiny at least 20 calendar days before final authorization.
SC bid challenges and PE information routes retain their own
clocks. Publish undisclosed material changes and return them
for a fresh decision on the changed terms. Officers may execute
only within actual delegated authority. A rejected or late
decision authorizes no award. Bounded urgent continuity under
prior law can protect users without granting the long term.

**PL04 — Reserve future law and safe public access.** A concession
may grant a defined time-limited use, operating or fee right
under law. A grant cannot make a private operator the final
authority over public eligibility, statutory tariffs, safety,
competition, land, labor or ecological rights. No prospective
clause grants a private veto, freezes general public law or
requires automatic compensation for general lawful regulation.
A valid payment for a proved breach, discriminatory act,
specific termination or lawful acquisition follows its own
competent law and review. Require the ordinary appeals, lawful
emergency access and alternative service routes to survive.
A large penalty that would effectively prevent their use
fails the award test. Actual old contracts, domestic legal
remedies and foreign obligations require separate lawful
resolution through the competent authorities.
