# AA01–AA05: public audit protects public control without governing the country

*Part of [C04/04](../04-public-audit-and-accountable-oversight.md); design121 candidate.*

**AA01 — Reconstitute the external public-audit function.** Establish its actual
Auditor General (AG), responsible statutory audit/report staff and separate
account/source duties under this coordinated Act, not a second copy of every
internal inspector or an AI sovereign. Audit public receipts, spending, assets,
liabilities/guarantees, public procurement and controlled entities at actual
national/local and external-source scope. Internal management/audit, Revenue,
prudential/competition/electoral authorities, prosecutors and courts retain
separate powers. A lawful policy choice is not corruption merely because an
auditor prefers another objective; audit can expose legality, implementation,
resource/outcome evidence and uncertainty without vetoing elected policy.

**AA02 — Follow public resources, preserve others' title.** Public grants,
contracts, public-service compensation and equity risk remain auditable at the
actual transaction/recipient/undertaking scope, including split/related courses
and public operations outside a general-budget line. Minority public investment
alone does not turn all private affairs into government. Private/member/donor/
client property, bank/pension/S/insurer funds and foreign claims do not become
Treasury assets through a consolidated audit. Private counterparty information
needs the precise necessary public-resource nexus/law or actual lawful contract,
not a general license over belief, family, civil cooperation or private wealth.
OE ordinary audit and M/MC/NB supervision keep their own mandates.

**AA03 — Legal, financial and performance findings are different.** Examine
original accounts, compliance and economy/efficiency/effectiveness with actual
scope, comparators and rights, not a universal monetary value for dignity.
Distinguish error/uncertainty, control weakness, suspected illegality, lawful
political disagreement and alleged criminal conduct. A finding is not a judgment,
asset recovery, ban, guilt or public mandate. No number of AI flags/agreeing
reports proves source independence or makes an official punishable. Public
bodies keep their actual responsibility for choosing and delivering lawful acts.

**AA04 — No auditor's coercive or fiscal government.** AG cannot make law/tax,
appropriate/borrow, choose a public policy, veto a budget/payment, direct a
named prosecution, remove an elected body or award a contract by audit score.
Recommendations and ordinary reporting are nonbinding as to policy; precise
record/retention duties under AA16–AA20 are separate enacted obligations.
No search/arrest/custody, compulsory criminal testimony, financial freeze or
private seizure follows an audit mandate. Civil protection/correction and P/K
criminal inquiry use their actual independent law; no new VD15 or fine band.

**AA05 — Courts and sensitive operations are auditable in their actual scope.**
Court/Assembly/monetary/defense accounts, public pay and buying are not exempt
because an office is independent or a contract classified. Audit cannot direct
a judgment, disclose protected judicial deliberation, choose a monetary rate,
reopen lawful case proof or obtain unrelated private records. Safe annex/court
access, necessary lawful confidentiality and public coverage limits preserve
PD, defense, source and security rights. A blanket secrecy assertion is not a
veto; contested privilege/access/redaction goes to an unimplicated ordinary
court, with CJ reserved constitutional competence intact.

[PI13](../../C03/17/03-public-access-audit-and-effective-remedies.md) expressly
proposes a coordinated statutory extension for compliance with lawfully required
public-office interest/contact/draft/disposition records, including Assembly/
local records, beyond the prior public-resource nexus alone. AA04 and AA16–AA20
limits/privilege/reply/courts remain; no general advocate/client/private search,
criminal, policy, vote/seat, payment or promulgation veto follows. Actual prior
mandates stay until valid prospective adoption; no source/review transfer.
