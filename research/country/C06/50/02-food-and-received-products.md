# Food products are not interchangeable tonnes or an official diet

Map PB's six food components explicitly into nine products. The staples,
fruit/vegetable and pulse/nut splits are authored assumptions. Preserve purchased
protein alternatives, dairy alternatives and oils/condiments as composite finished
products; do not silently replace them by meat, cow milk, pure oil or a plant diet.
Unknown ingredients, nutrition, cultural fit, animal welfare and foreign harms
stay visible. A new product or unfamiliar meaning requires interpretation.

The private reference is 51,597,000 kg plus 9,555,000 litres annually. The liquid
transport factor 1.02 kg/litre is an authored logistics conversion only, never a
nutritional equivalence. Add an explicit recipe for 5,585,840 institutional meals:
2,513,628 kg plus 558,584 litres. School/care/custody quantities are inherited
workload hypotheses, not actual meal receipt or clinical/menu adequacy.

No private grocery substitution is assumed in this additive stress. Public food
is a subset of the new combined total, not another addition to that total. If
actual public meals replace private purchases, recompute the matched demand;
no ordinary I05 credit or reduced cash right follows automatically. Public kitchen
work remains separate from farm packing, freight and retail. Existing meal-budget
quotes cannot be replaced by a cheaper ingredient illustration without matching
quality, purpose, tax, receiving and actual costs.

Domestic harvest separates retained seed, packing input, side streams and final
distribution loss. Seed retention is not free feed or new annual cash; first
compatible seed stock needs a source. Packing side streams and unrecovered food
have no assumed ecological credit or safe disposal. Imported and domestic inputs
retain their separate routes; crop output is not required GDP or export finance.

Ninety-day selected dry-product stocks require initial purchase and storage.
Annual spoilage replacement is additional once; ordinary rotation is already in
ordinary supply. Dry stocks are not ninety days of adequate diets, fresh produce
or every clinical substitute. A thirty-percent harvest loss adds import need;
a thirty-day import interruption still exposes perishables and route failures.
No emergency diet, unsafe recall exemption or compulsory buying is selected.

Paid field, packing, freight, retail, seasonal relief and instruction are counted.
The bounded extended-firm case adds 1,746 core people and 372 seasonal people beyond
the 126 reference, including nine new instructors across food/utilities. This
is only a headcount screen; skills, rest, consent, compatible locations, actual
contracts and finance are unknown. Shopping, cooking and voluntary care are not
claimed as free balancing labor or assigned by an AI household plan.
