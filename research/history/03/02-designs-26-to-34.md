# Designs 26–34

27. **Integrated design 26: institutional usability and cumulative load selected,
    operation open.** The [institutional-usability decision](../../2026-10-institutional-load/decision-record.md)
    compares retaining sector paths, adding a separate protector for each risk,
    central case management, ministerial consolidation and the selected plural
    receiving duty with independent decisions and shareable support. C06 now
    counts claimant and staff time, case handoffs and common-control costs; A4
    remains a proposed statutory/legal handoff, not an enacted new claim date.
    The F1–F10 exercises now ask whether the same scarce people or vendor can
    carry remedies across simultaneous failures. No original case journeys,
    affected-party participation, staffing, lawful adoption or observed gain
    establishes that this package improves real lives.
28. **Integrated design 27: bounded delegated rulemaking selected, authority and
    operation open.** The [delegated-rule decision](../../2026-10-delegated-rulemaking/decision-record.md)
    compares a vague hierarchy, Assembly votes on all rules, broad executive
    rulemaking, technical-council certification and the selected effect-sensitive
    delegation. The Assembly reserves foundational choices and defines each
    delegation; material instruments need affirmative approval, routine ones
    have an elected call-in, and urgent instruments expire under prior law.
    General AI criteria and external standards cannot change duties unseen.
    F2/F4/F5 now probe shadow lawmaking during budget, digital and emergency
    failure. No actual source-law inventory, public participation, staffed
    scrutiny or observed protection establishes this as superior.
29. **Integrated design 28: plural self-government and territorial-status route
    selected, authority and delivery open.** The
    [territorial self-government decision](../../2026-10-territorial-self-government/decision-record.md)
    distinguishes portable language/cultural rights, distinct collective claims,
    local autonomy, constitutional status and sovereignty. It compares ad hoc
    allocation, uniform individual protection, automatic local exit, fixed
    identity-based federation and the selected layered negotiation route.
    A15 is candidate wording, and F11 stresses disputed water, services,
    minority rights and rival national/regional assertions. No actual affected
    people, rights-holder representation, territory, law, treaty, asset account
    or observed outcome establishes the route as just or workable. Central
    obstruction and local exclusion remain serious objections.
30. **Integrated design 29: external responsibility made operational as a
    research choice, authority and delivery open.** The
    [external-responsibility decision](../../2026-10-external-responsibility/decision-record.md)
    adds prior elected control of material provisional application and lawful
    withdrawal to treaty entry; an accountable pre-return protection path;
    funded reception and visible critical-import dependencies; and a bounded
    complaint and remedy route for serious externally imposed harm where this
    country's authority reaches. The C03, C05, C07, C09, C10, C11 and C13
    chapter splits are navigation only. No treaty, partner, border office,
    receiving locality, foreign claimant or actual jurisdiction has been
    verified. No research selection enacts an international or domestic duty.
31. **Integrated design 30: retirement security and care distinguished,
    financing and democratic adjustment open.** The
    [lifetime-security decision](../../2026-10-lifetime-security/decision-record.md)
    selects an individual basic old-age payment and capped earnings-related
    public insurance with care/interruption credits. C06 counts cohort claims
    and actual care capacity; C13 tests record error, inflation and provider
    closure together. Elected law, not an actuary, fund manager or AI system,
    changes material parameters. The prior design-29 files are preserved.
    No real population, age, amounts, contribution rates, legal authority,
    actuarial valuation, staffed care or observed outcomes verify adequacy or
    sustainability. Means-tested, flat and funded-account rivals remain open.
32. **Integrated design 31: public conditions and comparative answerability
    selected, real-world evidence open.** The
    [public-conditions decision](../../2026-10-public-conditions/decision-record.md)
    rejects a single binding wellbeing score or an expert certificate of
    superiority. After a governing term, source-backed, distributional
    results, missing people, externally imposed harm and future capacity
    receive plural comparison and an opposition-accessible hearing before
    elected response. The founding election has an opening baseline instead
    of invented performance. The design-30 predecessor is preserved; no
    population, comparator, safe testimony, independent evaluator, statute,
    budget or observed improvement verifies the proposed mechanism.
33. **Integrated design 32: agentic civic attention and disconnected commitments
    selected, legal operation open.** The
    [agentic-democracy decision](../../2026-10-agentic-democracy/decision-record.md)
    compares unlimited agent participation, one-agent-per-person gating,
    central AI mediation and the selected plural attention and bounded-act
    route. Material reasons and affected stakes, not message count, guide
    formal response; principal mandates, expiry, offline limits and prior
    resource allocations constrain agents. Delayed revocation does not erase
    valid earlier duties. C12 was split by topic without an additional policy
    choice. No real civic docket, principal, counterparty, staff, law,
    independent reviewer or observed benefit validates this package.
34. **Integrated design 33: public conditions for voluntary social life
    selected, actual access and effects open.** The
    [public-life decision](../../2026-10-public-life/decision-record.md) compares
    private provision, official cohesion, vouchers and a locally chosen
    shared-place network under a statutory fair-access floor. C11 names the
    access and maintenance duty without assigning friendships or official
    community identity; C04, C05, C06, C09, C13 and C14 connect delivery,
    usable time, resources, land, household experience and assessment.
    The design-32 predecessor is preserved after the structural navigation
    cleanup. No actual locality, affected participation, lawful budget,
    staffed remedy or observed improvement verifies the selection.
35. **Integrated design 34: cross-system physical scarcity under prior law,
    first-hour capacity and elected choice still unverified.** The
    [scarcity decision](../../2026-10-cross-system-scarcity/decision-record.md)
    compares market, equal-share, technical and broad-executive allocation
    with a staged statutory route. C06 names the responsible first-hour
    actors, common-stock limits and elected decision for a new material
    priority; C07, C09, C10 and C14 keep individual claims, sector safety,
    civilian action and commencement aligned. A8 remains candidate wording.
    The design-33 predecessor is preserved; no actual stock, Assembly route,
    affected public, reviewer or delivered remedy validates the package.
