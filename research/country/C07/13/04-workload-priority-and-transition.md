# S13–S16: joint outcomes, real workload and accountable revision

*Part of [C07 service candidate](../13-public-service-entitlements.md); design 79.*

**S13 — Count joint production without double booking.** One valid preschool
episode can provide learning and safe childcare when the actual staffing,
competence, access and quality satisfy both duties. Report the distinct
rights/outcomes, but count the physical worker, room and child-time once.
Do not add two labels into twice the wellbeing, twice the budget or a spare
team. Conversely, clinical consent, learning, care and electoral authority
cannot merge merely because they share a building, worker or record vendor.
Do not presume any two activities compatible without their actual demands.
The same test applies to supervised learning, clinical encounters and future
mixed services; a shared label is not proof of compatible work.

**S14 — Derive capacity from the service, not the other way round.** Start
with people/events, delivered frequency/duration, grouping, travel and
individual support. Add preparation, documentation, supervision, review,
absence, maintenance and standby without counting the same allowance twice.
Then identify qualified role/shift/place and actual usable time. Account for
client/resident waiting and participation, not only paid workers. Test
ordinary time and simultaneous high-demand periods; unused annual hours
cannot be transported into an election or care crisis that is due now.
An aggregate FTE pass does not establish a specific skill, patient shift or
accessible place. Keep original evidence, uncertainty and unfamiliar needs;
a workload schema is not the complete definition of a good life.

**S15 — One accountable choice, several independent powers.** Sector/local
owners prepare entitlement, workload, safe receiving, cost attribution and
continuity; Treasury reconciles sources; the evidence service preserves
methods and original observations; Assembly law/appropriation sets material
coverage and burdens. Clinical/professional judgment, safety review, courts,
opposition and affected challenge retain their separate authority. Publish
safe aggregates, not linked family/clinical/electoral dossiers. Shared time
does not authorize shared personal data or common-controller final review.

On shortage expose real options: additional qualified capacity, safe repair,
different provision or a prospective public coverage change. Do not treat
unpaid relatives, undocumented AI savings, lower measured take-up or a
hidden queue as the balancing item. Existing due claims survive; prior-law
scarcity criteria and urgent remedies govern actual unavoidable failures.
The [bounded workload example](../../../settlement/service-floor/README.md)
prices neither all services nor the other currently audited country functions.

**S16 — Exact transition and replaceable methods.** [CP03–CP04](../../C06/28/02-dated-commitments-and-equality.md)
replaces the blanket common start with an express Assembly-enacted schedule
for each new service/coverage cohort: exact date/event, receiving owner, source,
paid preparation/overlap, interim access and remedy. Readiness evidence informs
that choice; no provider/Treasury/AI certificate can activate indefinite delay.
Preserve stronger current rights, pending/overdue claims and source-law dates.
State need/place/disability incidence and lawful interim protection, rather
than select only easy rich-area expansion. Identify included/new/shared/unknown
costs, qualified places/people and actual reserve sources. A missed enacted
date remains non-delivery, not an eligibility cut or reset to a future cohort.
Future changes need fresh public reasons, rights review and affected
participation; technical list/model updates cannot enact them. Compare
genuinely different social/service models and retain/no change. New media
and forms of learning/care may qualify on their actual merits and agency,
with disclosed unsupported meanings rather than forced translation into
one platform. Actual law/software remains unchanged by this research.
These service-age boundaries do not set civil, criminal or political majority
or remove stronger education, child-support and disability rights.

[NP](../../C06/33-protection-production-and-national-expansion.md) now selects
paid public residual or accepted prepaid capacity before reliance, task-bounded
personal/democratic continuity and explicit source/displacement. Ordinary work,
teaching, reserve and independent review are separate; use same-scenario supply
and concurrent demand, not additive mutually exclusive disasters. The actual
new requirement/source is unqualified, not absorbed in the old 23-component bid.

[RF10–RF12](../../C06/36/03-local-access-and-shared-capability.md) now selects
local first contact with shared specialist/back-office capability only where
the full access/quality/task comparison supports it. Existing service offers,
consent, paid travel, accepted receiving and independent remedies continue;
no numerical saving or mandatory online gate is inferred.

[TW14–TW17](../../C05/10/04-service-capacity-and-time.md) now keeps the children’s
40-hour offer while comparing32/40-hour paid-worker calendars, breaks, learning
and actual replacements. Route/day, review, task and roster tests remain
separate; no generic staffing cut, unpaid-family cover or annual-hours saving.

[NC/WF153](../../C06/58/07-living-bundles-and-early-care.md) now makes one care
candidate's integer room/day/source costs explicit. Annual eight-equivalent
fit is not the three-absence day; paid actual backup/skills and complete
provider costs remain necessary. Parent fees already net of subsidies are
no full provider price. S13/S14 joint work and S16 exact source-law dates persist.
