# IC05–IC07: ownership has a price, a constituency and a loss bearer

*Part of [C05/09](../09-income-capital-and-democratic-investment.md); design88 candidate.*

**IC05 — Retain plural ownership and contest dependence.** People may create,
join, leave and finance lawful private firms, worker/user cooperatives and
associations under C05 competition, labor and C08 civil/insolvency law. Public
ownership serves specified public functions. Neither employee ownership nor
public ownership automatically gives affected users, future residents,
nonmembers or workers on temporary contracts meaningful control.

For an actual undertaking, separate participation, ownership votes, management,
public regulation and dependence. State the franchise, appointment/removal,
pay, member entry/exit, minority protection, reinvestment, disclosure, loss and
insolvency terms before subscriptions. Workers retain bargaining and labor
rights against their own cooperative or public employer. A public service
must have accessible alternatives/remedies even when its owner is democratic.
No common AI broker or single national fund becomes the exclusive route to
jobs, credit, productive tools or civic participation.

**IC06 — Broaden future ownership through actual contributions.** Paid new
equity, voluntary conversions, employee/user subscriptions, succession sales
and public procurement with lawful access/knowledge terms are permitted paths,
not automatic tax-funded entitlements. Compare keeping an effective current
provider with each conversion, including valuation, seller receipt, debt,
worker choice, customer continuity and the cost of alternatives. Grants may
support a stated public benefit through IC08–IC12; public money does not
become insiders' private equity without explicit consideration and authority.

New project returns first meet contracts, wages, maintenance, taxes, creditor
terms and authorized reserves; only actual distributable receipts reach the
specified owners. A hoped-for automation dividend is not income protection.
Public equity belongs to its lawful public owner and is recorded at risk;
an equal social cash payment needs its own appropriation, not a second claim
on the same retained profit. Do not force pension savers, households or
private businesses to purchase national debt or new project shares.

**IC07 — Reject the blanket leveraged mutual as a current funding source.**
In the authored selective-tax case, even assigning *all* F dividends of
157.961 million CU to a purchaser of only the 2,000 million nonhousing book
stock would miss first-year service of 166.667 million on a 5% seller loan
with 30 equal principal payments. The 8.706 million shortfall excludes
administration, reserves, acquisition taxes and target-specific restrictions.
The optimistic zero-extra-cost price ceiling is 1,895.528 million.

Book equipment cost is not a company's equity price. The actual target
portfolio's dividends are unknown and smaller scope need not retain all F
receipts. Housing, land, creditors, taxes and minority claims cannot disappear
to improve this bound. This rejects an immediate cash-source claim, not
cooperatives, redistribution or every fair acquisition at different terms.
Do not quietly change to interest-only debt, full profit payout, free shares
or uncompensated title transfer to declare success.

The [earlier mutual comparison](../08/02-mutual-ownership-and-real-acquisition.md)
is preserved as a different frozen fixture; its assumed price and receipts
cannot be carried into the new macro case. An acquisition needs fresh
lawful title, valuation, liquidity, control, funding and continuity review.

[H01–H20](../../C09/12-secure-housing-and-repair.md) now selects a retained
public/community cost-rental option with paid title and binding new tenancy/
security terms. It does not mandate blanket conversion or treat acquisition as
new supply, free income or authority beyond the IC owner/window scope.

[OE](../13-enterprise-and-association-constitutions.md) now specifies ordinary
forms, contributed capital, actual director/member/worker authority and succession.
Its compatible public owners follow elected funded mandates/open appointments,
not a new sovereign fund or IC borrowing/acquisition power. Book stock, equity
price and actual productive formation remain different; no free assets result.
