# Design289. Voluntary care and paid continuity

10 November 2026 · Research selection · No actual enactment

## Baseline and decision

[Design288](baseline.json) continues the service-first design.
S09, NP, HS and AS already require assessed need, willing help
and public fallback. The open question is what the budget must
buy when chosen help ends at the same time for multiple people.
Apply the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md). Codex assisted
the comparison. No affected party or independent professional
reviewed the proposed arrangement.

## Alternatives and selection

- **Keep ordinary paid shifts and mobilize after a failure.** This
  uses fewer resources while family help is available. It works
  only where qualified substitute receiving is independently
  reachable at the necessary times. In the declared case neither
  withdrawal can be covered on that basis.
- **Protect ordinary care and respite, with paid shared coverage.**
  Select it where it has qualified staff, dated funds and timely
  independent coverage for the priced failure. At twelve units
  the constructed choice covers both withdrawals. At nine units
  the separately feasible one-reserve option covers one and leaves
  the other unmet. A reserve commitment may displace another use,
  even if its cash activation never occurs.
- **Pay ordinary staffed coverage for the affected windows.** In
  the reference comparison this costs the maximum every time.
  It could outperform standby where calls recur, journeys are
  long or standby degrades the rest of the roster. It remains
  optional for the recipient under existing choice and consent
  rules and can coexist with personal relationships.

[HS14–HS18](../country/C07/25/04-voluntary-care-and-paid-continuity.md)
selects the service mechanism.
[WF100–WF104](../country/C06/58/23-voluntary-care-and-national-capacity.md)
puts the exposure, household time and actual receipt on the same
first-budget comparison. The [primary evidence](sources.md)
qualifies the unsupported-assumption mechanism and the broad
scope of unpaid work. The demand, call pattern, prices and
worker matches are authored.

## Authority, objection and migration

Reconfirm willingness and ability only when a public delivery
decision relies on informal help. An administrative record can
itself pressure families or intrude into household life. Preserve
independent confidential choice, only necessary information and
the original eligibility and appeal clocks. A supported recipient
can prefer paid workers for intimate tasks. A willing helper can
stop without acquiring a public service or employment obligation.
Protect ordinary service, planned respite and any earned wages.
No count of relatives, unpaid hours or forecast fiscal gain
reduces a statutory floor or gives a payer authority over care.

Local and national elected authorities must price the relevant
own-payer and equalization shares. Authorized care officers
arrange receiving and the competent independent route checks
actual relief. Preserve all other statutory services and courts
on their own clocks. Apply current powers and prior terms until
a valid transition is authorized. Compare added paid coverage
against a real existing roster so that the same worker, pay or
reserve is never counted twice.

## Next bounded decision

For one willing local service team, obtain safe aggregate records
of booked respite, time-critical planned help, changed or refused
arrangements and concurrent withdrawals. Match the records to
qualified shifts, travel, actual reserve costs and original due
dates. Price one funded direct-roster rival and the first and
second calls. Reopen if reserve reliance harms ordinary access,
excludes sparse communities or coerces families, or if ordinary
staffed care provides more dependable protection. Actual delivery,
authorization, costs and improvement remain unverified.
