## Compare and select coherent packages

| Package | Causal benefit | Main loss |
|---|---|---|
| Retain general hierarchy | Less added procedural work | Opposition promise/hidden criteria remain hard to enforce. |
| Fewer rules, decentralized practice, direct statutes for remaining floors | Reduce capture/compliance/docket dependence | Private gates or unsafe/inaccessible baselines may replace public protection. |
| Direct Assembly statute/vote on every update | Elected responsibility is clear | Technical volume and opaque bundles can exhaust meaningful judgment. |
| Broad executive delegation, consultation and later litigation | Faster specialist adaptation | Burdens precede public control; affected people bear litigation and capture cost. |
| New independent technical certification veto | Expertise exposes some errors | Entrenched framing/source/funding power without electoral replacement. |
| **Specific delegation plus effect-sensitive control** | Reserved statute choices, ordinary detail, any-member conversion, material affirmative vote and direct courts | Minority freeze, majority starvation, stale old rules and unsafe delay may persist. |

Selected [DR01–DR24](../country/C03/11-delegated-rules-and-effective-public-control.md)
is conditional national research. Cumulative materiality includes a1% enacted-
programme fiscal limb and rights/physical/political tests regardless of sums.
Every ordinary draft gets30days input; major national changes retain FA30/10/
90(+one30), then final L30/45. Any elected member may call a routine proposal
through day30. Material/called votes need floor(N/2)+1 yes/present on days31–45;
Clerk listing,7day notice and reserved day45 sitting cannot invent quorum.
Every ordinary proposal needs actual issuer making by45 or lapses. A proposed
repeal has no effect before lawful replacement. A seven-day ordinary urgent
exception needs an exact existing source, cannot reset and cannot skip ordinary
requirements for continued effect; valid primary law remains an alternative.

The one-member trigger is deliberately chosen over a party-size threshold;
it can freeze every routine update. No cap, default approval or expert veto
solves that democratic/resource choice. Unchanged old lawful rules persist and
any-member annulment has protected time but no automatic lapse. This favors
continuity over universal legacy expiry and can preserve an unjust baseline.
Those are retained substantive objections, not “resolved” by extra prose.

## Information, review, remedies and transition

Source/cumulative reasons, exact freely knowable standards/model versions,
consultation meaning, safe originals, dissent and unknowns travel to the public,
Assembly and courts. Future media/assisted delivery do not multiply mandates
or equate interpretation with consent. Neither clerk nor vendor selects policy.
Direct ordinary challenge can precede harm; an interim stay needs actual B17
grounds. Courts decide source/process/rights, not missing technical/tax policy
or Assembly votes. Known recipients receive actual correction, Treasury pays
finally proved judgments, and expressly scoped B19/B18 execution has its own
credentials/source. Empty accounts remain unpaid claims, not personal guilt.
Past transactions/good-faith benefits do not automatically collapse on invalidity.

A competent compatible Act starts at180calendar days, without readiness delay.
Any operative post-commencement amendment, routine or material, follows its tier;
old unmade drafts complete the process. Unchanged old lawful rules, actual due
rights, wages, contracts, judgments and source/foreign duties retain their dates
and lawful successors. An Assembly's legal end, not an election call, lapses
unfinished proposals; a new body gets a fresh complete window. Caretaker limits,
C10/D9, local competence and current software/constitutional holds stay stronger.
This proposal cannot retroactively legalize a legacy wrong or enact itself.

including the initial sector-material consultation gap, routine legacy-amendment
