# Design326. Income shifts and public finance

[TN21–TN25](../../country/C06/59/06-income-shift-and-public-finance.md)
carries general and restricted budgets through an income-source
shift. Separate legal backing, finite reserve and recurring net tax
yield. Preserve existing earned rights, assessment periods, remedies
and actual pension and service payments.

The [decision](../../2026-11-income-shift-and-public-finance/decision-record.md)
retains ordinary-source continuity, tax alternatives and public returns.
Actual net collection, appropriations, incidence and operation remain open.
