# Paid execution, retained rivals and four failures

Design 98 · Proposed research, not enacted law or verified operation.

**MA12 — Elected funds and inspectable source control.** Assembly appropriates bounded
recurrent/case-peak investigators, qualified economists/technical advisers, hearing officers,
defense, protected records, independent courts, source audit, actual remedy execution and lawful
receiving. Necessary due operations use C06/NP's enacted bounded standing-source rules, not the
chief setting an unlimited constitutional budget. Treasury executes lawful scheduled dues without
choosing favored cases. Industry fees, if lawfully bounded/enacted, enter elected accounts; no
penalty, recovery commission, donor/vendorship or forecast AI saving funds the pursuing office.
Publish budget incidence, source families, shared employment/funders/vendors, complaint/sample
selection, waits, dispositions, reversals, actual switching/access and unreceived claims, with safe
sources for opposition/courts and no permanent public testimony. Existing C04 independent audit
checks source/delivered remedies through funded risk and random work; it does not adjudicate guilt
or appoint new economic protectors. Cases/records survive lawful change of government/provider.

**MA13 — Trace who actually executes.** Every order names duty owner, actual recipient, qualified
executor, asset/payer, due time and received verification. The firm performs its lawful correction;
B18's authorized civilian service executes only actual court scope against private property and
B19's expressly adopted substitute handles bounded state refusal. No new force unit, universal
server key, personal know-how compulsion, unpaid worker, tax or unlimited Treasury guarantee arises.
Public enterprises face the same applicable law; internal state fines create no new national
resources and do not repair users. Distinguish inability from deliberate noncompliance; personal
consequences need prior duty, actual ability, fault and independent process. If an order cannot be
delivered, preserve the claim, existing safe service and legal duties, expose the shortage/capture
and seek the elected lawful source/receiving choice. Reporting a referral, fine or company breakup
is not proof of real exit or received compensation.

**MA14 — Lawful migration, no authority from replacement prose.** An adopting Act explicitly
replaces C05/05's generic markets panel and only this office's V10 staggered-panel description with
MA01–MA13, defines the office/career powers and substantive code, exact source-law start/transition
dates and paid initial staffing. Preserve lawful old cases/appointments/terms, contracts, market
access, evidence, client/worker/victim claims and appeal dates until competent compatible revision
and named successors accept them. Neither chief/panel nor a new selector approves its own founding
power. A narrower mandatory permission or new offence starts only prospectively through its actual
law; no default merger approval, cartel amnesty, presumed old data licence or revived expired
penalty case. Missing inventory/readiness cannot postpone an already enacted duty or erase a claim;
actual old jurisdictions, operators, people, source cash and international reach remain unverified.
Preparing the limited initial C04 case staff does not appoint a chief or a substitute judge.
Existing EA/CJ/PD/KR/M/NP and constitutional/software/contribution holds remain; case support must
be freshly reviewed after changed substantive predicates, rather than transferred by index.
