# Let the Assembly protect existing services in a separate vote

*BCV21–BCV26. Candidate rules implementing the limited continuation under BLC02.*

**BCV21 — Identify the part that can lawfully continue on its own.** During
a lapse, or when the published calendar shows a material imminent lapse
risk, a member may propose a separate appropriation to protect specified
existing public duties that would otherwise face interruption. Identify
the source law, due service, least sufficient scope, latest useful
decision and delivery dates, competent payer, legal expiry and affected
people. List the standing authority and bridge amounts already available.
Separate any lawfully necessary companion source from unrelated policy,
new expansion and confidence. A proposed service or source genuinely
dependent on a separate law remains conditional on that law under
BCV16–BCV20. Neither label nor committee routing makes it independent.

**BCV22 — Put the exact motion within reach of a vote.** Include
eligible continuity proposals in the BLC day-30 account as available.
Schedule the protected decision by the existing day-60 milestone, or
earlier where existing sitting rules and a documented useful time allow.
Use DB02–DB03 assistance and notice and existing lawful urgency rules.
A disputed estimate stays visible and does not bar a lawful proposal.
For incompatible qualified continuity alternatives, apply existing BCV
designation, floor recovery and ordering within that scope. Preserve
the ordinary hearing of other proposals. Record the continuation vote
separately if no operative successor budget already funds the exact
duties in time. Use DB04's actual presence and full-seat majority
threshold. A continuation vote leaves the whole budget's independent
agenda in place. A missed or inquorate sitting triggers existing
restoration and review, with no inferred assent.

**BCV23 — Reconcile scope, cash and later claims before each decision.**
An eligible motion identifies the exact new authority, named payer,
settled source or verified condition on settlement, dates, ceilings,
staff, accepting providers and independent remedy. Disclose unresolved
qualifications and keep binding commitments subject to actual law,
source and receiving. Compare affordable
narrower options and show omitted duties, including those without a
powerful sponsor. Preserve prior law, restricted sources, standing
services and already bound obligations. Apply one national cash and
capacity account. Before each later competing or whole-budget vote,
deduct already committed uses and show any legally substitutable
balance. A material change receives fresh scrutiny. A continuation
appropriation cannot spend another body's cash or use forecast savings
to pay a due bill.

**BCV24 — Give the temporary law an actual end.** An enacted continuation
names its last date and any earlier successor cutover, eligible tasks,
commitment and payment ceilings and funded handover. Cover only the
stated protection period and attached obligations. The existing
90-day bridge and its 91–120 settlement authority keep their own
scope and dates. A separate appropriation is required for new authorized
work outside that bridge. Repeated continuations cannot rely on the
earlier vote. Any further limited motion needs a separate timely
recorded decision on remaining duties, actual finance and why ordinary
budget agreement has failed. Report recurring reliance for an elected
source decision. Expiry preserves valid accrued obligations and remedy.

**BCV25 — Keep funding and government confidence separate.** An enacted
continuation carries only its exact appropriation. It neither adopts a
programme outside its scope nor grants confidence or enlarges caretaker
power. A rejected or unheld vote cannot suspend a duty or displace a
standing payment. Courts and other competent reviewers may enforce
specific notice, sitting, record or payment duties within actual powers.
They cannot record votes members did not cast or create a budget.
Name the qualified alternate payer and receiving route. If authority,
cash, staff or receipt fails, preserve the original duty and available
remedy and identify the actual loss in time for scrutiny.

**BCV26 — Keep the record usable through replacement.** Publish the
texts, linked dependencies, competing uses, votes, actual authority
and independent checks on cash, work and receipt. Give opposition
members, service users and affected workers useful access and protect
private evidence. Preserve accrued claims, valid votes, expired
authority, actual deadlines and paid successors across elections or a
change of payer. AI may compare exact text, detect duplicate uses and
explain the choice. It cannot decide parliamentary scope, cast a vote
or authorize spending. Reopen on routine subdivision, delayed whole
votes, displaced protected duties or a better way to preserve both
continuity and elected choice.
