# T67–T72. Verify the transfer through the final service

13 November 2026 · Integrated design 437 · Candidate first-budget rules

**T67 — State which cash event has been verified.** For a material
grant on the first-programme path, retain the original authorization,
recipient, covered duty and adjustment terms. Report the scheduled
settlement, actual sending debit, actual receiving credit and date of
legal availability separately. Identify the evidence and accountable
office for each. Use a reconciled batch for routine flows where it
preserves individual challenge and traceability. Record missing
evidence as unknown and preserve a disputed entry for review.

**T68 — Show the gross components and the net remittance.** Match
offsets, other grants, recoveries and separately restricted items to
their own legal bases before reconciling a combined payment.
A positive component of a negative adjustment supplies no extra cash
over the unadjusted schedule. State the net change relative to the
applicable baseline and distinguish it from the whole remittance.
Where a new duty needs timely funds despite an offset, name and fund
its own usable route under T61–T66. Reconcile each component once.

**T69 — Make the last useful payment date control the fallback.**
The local service and payer identify when wages, supplies or direct
support must reach the recipient and the lead time needed after
receiving the grant. If lawful scheduled funds can arrive too late,
compare funded local cover, a separately authorized advance and a
lawful alternate payer. Before the last useful time, secure a named
receiver and funding for the uncovered amount within the adopted
ceiling. If no alternative qualifies, record the uncovered duty,
preserve its original date and open the applicable remedy. A missed
receipt never licenses duplicate payment.

**T70 — Follow payment and performance separately.** Reconcile the
outgoing wage, supplier or beneficiary payment with usable evidence
of receipt. Verify the actual access or service outcome that the
programme required. A staff payment may support a service without
proving that a resident was served. Use proportionate records and
direct or representative checks as appropriate. Disclose the
denominator, coverage and unresolved cases. Keep ordinary proof and
challenge reachable through assisted and non-digital routes.

**T71 — Put the correction outside the failing desk.** The national
sender and local receiver maintain distinct records. Give competent
independent reviewers lawful access to source records and the means
to seek actual correction. Record returns, pending settlement and
duplicates distinctly. Verify exclusivity and finality before retry
under [TPE](../../C06/60/11-public-cash-and-independent-execution.md).
Keep limited safe public status and protect personal and commercially
sensitive data. AI may reconcile versions and flag unmatched items.
It cannot deem an account usable or close a recipient's claim.

**T72 — Adopt prospectively and preserve prior obligations.** Budget
for reconciliation, independent review and alternate payment through
the next call. Retain open disputes, original due dates, lawful
loss allocation and accrued worker or resident claims on handover.
Compare targeted checks with ordinary audit and broader tracing on
actual delay, uncertainty and administrative burden. Reopen on
persistent mismatches, missed recipients or a simpler reliable
proof path. An evidence failure remains visible in programme review
while substantive duties retain their own law.
