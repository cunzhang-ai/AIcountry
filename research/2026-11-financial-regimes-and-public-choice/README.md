2 November 2026 · Proposed research, no monetary/fiscal authority

[Current country judgment](../settlement/country-delivery/06-financing-and-public-control.md)
retains ordinary RF tax/insurance/bounded debt and specifies a finite, distinct,
unadopted fiscal-money rival. The [canonical SD source clause](../country/C06/43/03-sources-ownership-and-cash.md),
[RF clarification](../country/C06/36/01-recurring-sources-and-borrowing.md) and

- [Decision](decision-record.md): actual baseline, alternatives, selection, objections,
  compatible reuse, missing review and bounded next action.
- [Model](model.py) and [results](results.json): six exactly paired claim/payment
  examples at the same gross real/G use, different tax, owner, instrument and
  interest exposure; three exchange-cost sensitivities are not predictions.
- [Primary source scope](sources.json): BoE deposit creation, BIS negative equity,
  Federal Reserve model credibility/inflation. No country calibration or original
  observation is inferred. Sources were reopened, not reused as fresh authority.
- [Scope](scope-record.json), [exact pins](source-manifest.json), [checks](validate.py)
  and [result](validation.json): identity/accounting/continuity only.
- [Exact108 baseline](baseline.json):787 artifacts, including786 current Markdown

Run `python3 model.py` then `python3 validate.py` from this directory.
Existing dated108/107 and earlier sources are immutable. Imported108 fields
show that household G is not all expenditure and private/company assessments
are conditional, never a calculated complete national issue or bond requirement.
All original37 function and61 selection objects remain unchanged.

The ledger fixes one delivered service package; it does not model whether that
package can be produced, who consents, price/demand/portfolio response or welfare.
Opening deposits/reserves/FX claims are paired hypotheses and require actual
owned title/availability before any real payment. No actual private records or
sensitive testimony were gathered; no independent/affected review occurred.

The next overall design priority is continuous political finance, including
pre-call, local/referendum and coordinated AI/third-party spending. The source/
VAT/credit/production/price/FX account remains open, not silently completed.
A larger urgent harm, actual demand/source evidence or legitimate public choice
can reopen RF/issue and A/C1. Accounting success is not social superiority.
