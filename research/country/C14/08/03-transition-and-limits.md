# Democratic repair: transition and limits

*Canonical RR08–RR10; part of [C14/08](../08-democratic-repair-without-self-authorization.md).*

**RR08 — The exception cannot authorize itself.** At legitimate first founding,
the actual affected public may choose this package as part of its original
charter. Later introduction of RR into a system without it obeys that system's
actual amendment and delayed-effect rules. RR cannot approve its own earlier
commencement, replacement court, new denominator or supporting procedure.
Research selection revises no present national law, software permissions,
constitutional hold or AIcountry contribution gate. No model receives a mandate.

The preserved design 82 C14 blanket and C02 exception were inconsistent. RR is
an explicit new research reconciliation. The earlier design 17 anti-incumbent
reasoning is historical support for comparison, not authority to rewrite what
design 82 actually said. Old judgments, pending disputes, signature validity,
claim dates, orders and accrued rights survive under their valid source law;
the enacted migration names custodians, legal successors and operative dates.

**RR09 — A determination must reach people.** The publication office records the
exact approved text, old-law determination, effective provision/cohort/date and
legal challenge route. Electoral administration performs registration, notices,
accessible ballots and actual correction under its lawful mandate. Treasury pays
only authorized commitments; legal aid and ordinary courts keep their own sources
and timely shifts. A judicial order or website publication is not actual repaired
access. Record refused/late execution and the responsible receiver. E15 can
perform an exact existing record/payment duty, not choose the electorate,
certify a repair, appoint a judge or enact constitutional text.

**RR10 — Reasons, rejected defaults and reopening.** Retain delay as the default
because the authority to call a reform remedial is itself valuable political
power. Choose the narrow exception because forcing an already entitled excluded
person through another contest is also a democratic injury. Refuse a broad
“beneficial reform” exception: better court design, emergency checks or a new
franchise may deserve adoption without becoming incumbent powers immediately.
Blanket delay remains a real rival with lower classification discretion and
higher restoration cost. Broader constituent repair may be necessary in some
captured systems but is not legitimate simply because an incumbent demands it.

This selection cannot guarantee a fair court, timely capacity or repair of an
already captured order. Simultaneous capture, strategic case assignment, weak
funding and an exclusionary predecessor electorate can defeat the mechanism.
Reopen on actual denied restoration, disguised incumbent benefit, harmful delay,
unusable implementation, a new material objection or a better complete model.
Compare actual rights restoration and replacement with baseline and rivals,
including burdens on excluded people; documentary guidance is not outcome data.
The next integrated task is the joined material/transition alternative and then
the objective-wide comparison, not an indefinite series of repair exceptions.
