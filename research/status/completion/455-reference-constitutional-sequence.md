be pursued using the comparator's existing powers and which target
choices require constitutional change. The [reference sequence](../../settlement/first-round-mandate/12-the-existing-constitution-governs-the-change.md)

condition and exact commencement.

legal determination, adoption or executed reform. Existing rights
and separately authorized ordinary repair retain their own authority.
