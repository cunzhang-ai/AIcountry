# An ending ground, a real receiving path and no proxy disappearance

*CD11–CD12; part of [C08/21](../21-conflict-detention-and-release.md).*

**CD11 — Release/repatriation is an actual obligation, not a negotiation chip.**
End restriction promptly when the actual individual ground fails. For POWs,
cessation of active hostilities in the identified conflict triggers release/
repatriation without delay; no final peace signature, reciprocal concession,
confession, prosecution quota or unrelated future war is a prerequisite.
The court hears actual conflict facts and contrary evidence, not merely the
executive's permanent-war declaration. Necessary prior wounded/sick release/
neutral accommodation is assessed promptly by independent qualified clinicians
and competent protective bodies under actual law, with consent/safety review.
No sick person is kept to obtain information, unpaid work or a budget payment.

Neutral military custody ends with the actual legal duty/necessity, not a
minister's new label. No former POW becomes an immigration/security detainee
merely because documents, an onward state or a house are missing. Give
provisional identity/protection review, safe nonclosed accommodation where
chosen, clinical continuity and actual transport/receiving. A lack of safe
foreign destination cannot perpetuate the ended military ground. Actual
non-refoulement/safe-return review may change destination, not create prison.

Never force return to a persisting prohibited risk or treat refusal to go
there as continued enemy allegiance. Actual other lawful restrictions need
their own defined grounds/review. Inform the person, preserve their belongings,
due wages/claims and safe source copies. Release is verified by restored
ability to leave and accepted support, not an office closing the record or
a convoy/clinic flag changing. If safe handoff fails, urgently obtain actual
independent remedy and funded noncustodial protection; no unsafe abandonment,
indefinite medical hold or disappearance is authorized by a missed clock.

**CD12 — A foreign receiver needs law, ability and an effective remedy.**
POW transfer has the actual applicable GCIII Article 12 receiving-party/willingness/
ability requirements, source record, clinical/age/identity continuity and
independent prior rights assessment. Ordinary extradition/deportation and
international-court surrender retain their different P/G/C02 routes; a war
transfer cannot evade their actual protections or an operative release order.
No informal memorandum, vendor change or partner's assurance alone is a power.

A credible irreversible risk of torture, prohibited treatment, execution,
political persecution or applicable grave fair-trial denial receives effective
independent review/appeal before transfer, with actual enforceable safeguards.
No alleged security benefit balances away a prohibited risk. Child voice and
safe family/care review remain. Record actual accepting custody, continuing
monitoring/contact and lawful corrective/return measures if the receiving
state materially fails its obligations. Do not pretend we can compel a foreign
state or commence new military force; refusal/missing consent remains a real
failed route requiring lawful alternatives, not a claim of completed safety.
Our own past/control-based duties and remedy do not disappear on dispatch.
