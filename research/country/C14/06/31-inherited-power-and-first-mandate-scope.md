# T138–T142. Carry obligations and decide which powers continue

*Design538 · Proposed transition law under [staged capacity and protection](../06-staged-capacity-and-protection.md).*

**T138 — Separate an inherited claim, the law that governs it and the
means used to administer it.** Continue valid rights, debts, titles, awards,
employment protections, pending cases and binding public commitments on
their applicable terms. State separately what coercive, emergency, delegated
or otherwise exceptional authority would carry forward, who could use it and
which rights or political controls it could impair. An inventory entry gives
no power. An announcement does not establish a claim or an appropriation.
An inherited power may have existing lawful effect until competent law or
review changes it. Use the operative remedies for unlawful exercise now.

**T139 — Put each material high-risk power to an express transition
decision.** Through the competent prior-law process, identify powers that can
materially impair opposition, liberty, timely essential receipt or the
execution of remedies. Review the original legal source and actual operation,
including shared evidence, funds, credentials and receiving offices. The
transition instrument names each power, use, effective date, decision-maker
and independent forum. Select repeal, narrower replacement or express
continuation. If limited overlap is needed, specify the covered acts, an
outside expiry date, payable duties and a qualified ordinary substitute.
Give the elected public a distinct choice on material powers and any
renewal. Expiry of a power leaves continuing claims and remedies on their
applicable terms. Keep general continuity for ordinary compatible laws.
If an apparent dangerous power was omitted from the schedule, refer it
promptly to competent review and elected correction under operative law.
Omission itself neither extends nor terminates a power.

**T140 — Let an early warning reach an independent authority.** The
opening implementation schedule identifies who forwards a protected warning
under existing [Ombuds and systemic review](../../C04/13-public-ombuds-and-systemic-correction.md)
and [implementation and redress](../../C11/12-public-rules-and-verifiable-implementation.md).
Require a review registry or public correction unit that records a serious,
identifiable common defect in an inherited recurring decision route to notify
the competent independent reviewer directly. Send the rule, proof or practice
at issue, foreseeable next use and enough protected evidence to assess it.
If the next adverse act is imminent, use the existing urgent route immediately.
Use minimum necessary disclosure and preserve confidential individual relief.
Closure, repayment or settlement of one case leaves the referral duty in
place. The recipient acknowledges in time to assess the next material act and
can invoke existing lawful inquiry, stay or redress routes through a named
executor. A warning alone changes no entitlement, collection or spending
authority. Keep favorable payments and independently supported lawful acts
running. Record an unconfirmed or missed stop on its original clock.

**T141 — Limit the first legislative package to national functions.**
Prioritize the charter and peaceful succession, independent review and
execution, continuity of standing duties, and exact funded repairs for
material nonreceipt. Then decide structural changes where a specific barrier
survives ordinary repair. Sector or enterprise detail enters this package only
to the extent that a precisely identified shared right, serious harm to
others, concentrated public or private power, or continuity of a binding
essential obligation requires an opening decision. Compare ordinary law,
targeted repair and the actual cost of requiring a uniform rule. Where
existing law is compatible, carry it and leave lawful internal arrangements
to owners, workers and members within their respective rights. Put general
board formulas, ownership preferences and specialized entity filings to
separate later decisions if a public case is established. Preserve operative
participation rights, contracts and remedies under their applicable law.
The public handover of an essential service may need a specific duty for its
operator without deciding board design for every enterprise.

**T142 — Finish the opening design in one elected comparison.** Present a
standing-rights and repair motion, an express disposition of material
inherited powers and the strongest executable narrower or structural rival.
Use the same people, continuing claims, resources, legal effect dates and
deadlines. Include the first harmful use that an independent order could
prevent and the next funded call after the handover. Historical evidence
identifies a failure mechanism and the conditions under which it was observed.
Price ordinary repair and the proposed protection against that mechanism,
including where simpler repair covers it. Record research choice, affected
participation, actual authority, paid readiness and outcomes separately.
Use the existing elected and judicial routes. An unfinished sector study
does not hold the opening package unless an explicit legal, funding or
delivery dependency remains unresolved.
