# KR17–KR20: essential knowledge through lawful safe delivery

*Design 95 research candidate; not enacted law, a funded offer or demonstrated
superiority.*

**KR17 — Map a usable dependency, not a license alone.** For an essential medicine,
device, water or energy repair, or public service software, the responsible service
authority records owner/territory/expiry of rights, technical files, tacit know-how,
validated process, skilled people, materials and imported inputs, equipment, test labs,
regulator, cybersecurity/privacy where relevant, power, foreign payment, maintenance and
the receiving operator. C07 independently decides clinical quality, consent, coverage
and care; C08 controls product conformity and lawful conduct. Access to a document or
source code is not qualified manufacture, a safe repair, a stocked clinic or permission
from a foreign supplier.

**KR18 — Purchase continuity before dependence.** A public award or supply contract
that creates or maintains an essential-service operational dependency must either obtain paid nonexclusive public-purpose access, validated
documentation, lawful escrow/transfer triggers, training, spares/ingredient and quality
records, interoperable data and a tested alternate qualified operator or explicitly
choose, within lawful service guarantees, a funded alternative and disclose the
remaining dependence. Other basic-research, arts or exploratory awards use KR15’s
actual foreground/access obligations, not an invented spares/alternate-operator duty.
The contract names who can activate a step-in, the objective
trigger, scope/duration, remuneration, records and safe handback or expiry, with notice
and independent challenge. An escrow without maintained build tools, people, rights to
dependent interfaces and an accepting producer is not an alternative. If no capable
successor can be paid, disclose the single-source risk rather than call the contract
resilient. The named service authority may activate only an objectively triggered
pre-agreed licence/deposit route; independent courts hear wrongful activation and actual
receivers protect users. A contractual step-in is not a new statutory seizure. Separate
current performance from future optional procurement; a blank new contract cannot cancel
existing claims.

**KR19 — Prior-law patent intervention.** Select an ordinary-court statutory
nonexclusive patent licence only for a specified medicine, repair, accessibility or
other legally identified essential service where a particular blocking claim materially
prevents timely lawful provision, usable alternatives cannot meet the protected need,
and proportionate voluntary terms were genuinely sought without timely agreement. The
court tests essentiality, causal obstruction, alternatives, a credible paid
preparation/delivery plan with scheduled qualified acceptance, public/third-party harm
and exact rights; a cheaper quoted price alone is insufficient, but proved
unaffordability may be part of actual essential obstruction. Ordinary application must
document 30 calendar days of negotiations unless refusal or the useful protection time
makes that period futile. Imminent grave harm may justify dispensing with negotiation,
with recorded reasons. This is a proposed exception needing actual domestic/treaty
reconciliation, not a statement of current international permission. A plan may receive
enabling permission before capacity is complete; actual manufacture/use still needs all
operative safety, rights and receiving conditions. The licence cannot certify a paper
supplier. Semiconductor technology use is limited to public noncommercial use or a
separately proved competition remedy, with its relevant conditions; it is not covered
merely by an essential label.

**KR20 — Limits, timing and payer.** The order names claims/product, authorized
operator, acts, quantity, territory, purpose, start, reporting, remuneration and a
definite expiry no later than the underlying right. Review at least annually and on
material changed conditions or a grounded termination request; renewal or extension
needs fresh findings, notice and reasons. When the obstruction ends and is unlikely to
recur, end the special use with lawful protection of legitimate licensed investment and
safe receiving/handback. Annual review is not automatic yearly relitigation or a power
to cancel a lawful clinical claim. Domestic manufacture/use/import is allowed only to
the exact extent lawfully specified; no general export authorization. Any pharmaceutical
export/import route must separately meet the actual applicable
bilateral/Article 31bis system conditions and foreign permissions; domestic paper cannot
command an exporter. Notice and hearing precede ordinary grant. B17 controls any urgent
interim order, including its short hearing/expiry safeguards; a final licence still
needs full adjudication. No industry or minister appoints that judge. Filing does not
itself authorize use or stay existing lawful care.

The named licensed operator owes reasonable remuneration from the first authorized use;
a public procurer owes it only where its valid purchase/funding instrument assumes that
duty. A public funding promise does not release the operator without valid
creditor-approved or statutory novation. One royalty is credited once across
overlapping payers, not recovered twice. The court publishes valuation reasons considering licensed use, public
contribution, reasonable investment/reward, comparable terms and proven circumstances,
not hypothetical monopoly revenue as automatic damages. Undisputed sums fall due on the
stated schedule; contested sums use a lawful secured/deposit route only where
proportionate, with public funding needed for any public subsidy. No automatic Treasury
guarantee, upfront bond that defeats urgent rights protection, fictional zero royalty or
foreign settlement assumed paid. Remuneration/cash disputes retain enforceable claims
and urgent review; licence is not proof of manufactured supply or a balanced budget.
This expressly rejects a blanket 12-month final-licence cap: repeating full trials may
undermine paid manufacturing and continuity. Urgent B17 interim powers keep their short
independent hearing/expiry limits; they cannot renew themselves into a final licence. No
uncertainty about remuneration deems it zero or grants an automatic Treasury/FX
guarantee.

KR19 authorizes only the named patent acts: it does not override copyright, secrets,
data rights, professional licensing, clinical consent, land permission, material
shortages or foreign law. Separate voluntary licences or a specific independently
justified enacted exception are required where they actually block delivery. Courts may
prevent proved abusive rights enforcement under precisely defined law, rather than treat
every thicket as anticompetitive. Copyright claims cannot suppress lawful criticism,
political quotation or access to operative law under their actual exceptions; no
automatic takedown or global service shutdown on an untested accusation. B courts
enforce/refuse tailored remedies, C04 authorities arrange accepted delivery, TS controls
safety and C07 protects people. If safe paid capacity remains absent, record unmet
claims and invoke lawful receiving/funding choices; do not order compulsory worker
know-how or claim licence equals remedy received.
