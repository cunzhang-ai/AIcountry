# C11. Knowledge, culture and AI as public capability: AI as a public capability

*Part of [C11. Knowledge, culture and AI as public capability](../C11-knowledge-culture-and-ai.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

[KR](07-knowledge-rights-and-innovation.md) now separates actual human IP,
autonomous outputs, lawful commercial-training copies, data/privacy and bought
public audit/continuity rights. An output label grants neither title nor permission.

AI may help people learn, communicate, model alternatives, administer services and draft
law. Public authority stays with accountable democratic institutions. No provider may
control eligibility, political information, execution and the only appeal mechanism.
Public procurement must preserve lawful switching, usable data portability, explanation
of consequential decisions and continuity during outages. Critical non-AI routes must
be maintained, with their real costs included.

The selected [public AI capability portfolio](../../2026-10-ai-public-capability/decision-record.md)
gives AI useful but task-limited work across the country. Residents may use
revocable personal agents for explanation, translation and applications without
making an AI account the entrance to rights. Public authorities may compare
budget, workforce, land and ecological options using models that expose source
facts, assumptions, alternatives, uncertainty and dissent. Clinicians, teachers,
care workers, inspectors and legal advisers may use appropriate tools to find
options and reduce paperwork while retaining their professional duties.
Utilities and hazard services may use forecasts for maintenance and warnings;
resource allocation, coercive restrictions and force stay with the legally
competent human authorities. Researchers and communities may use plural tools
for discovery and expression without a government model certifying conclusions.

Each deployment has an enacted purpose, named public owner, permitted data and
actions, impact class, resource ceiling, expiry, incident rule, challenge and
funded non-AI or other-provider route. Exploration with no legal effect,
reversible bounded assistance, and consequential effects require different
authorization. An unattended AI cannot decide guilt, political standing,
essential-service deprivation, an election, a new tax or use of force. A
responsible officer's review of a high-stakes recommendation must include
time, competence, source access and power to refuse it. Neither a ceremonial
click nor splitting one harmful process into low-risk tasks changes its
actual effect. Automation within a narrow prior rule remains possible where
lawful, reversible and monitored; calling a system a pilot creates no power.

An agency must compare an AI use with a workable non-AI or simpler-software
method at comparable quality before scaling it. Report delivered access,
people's time, errors and reversals, excluded groups, privacy and dignity,
worker burden, cash and physical inputs, and who controls the information and
appeal channels. Model or vendor updates preserve exact laws, pending claims,
source records and correction history; they do not self-approve wider use.
The shared public layer supports replaceable standards, evaluation and lawful
interoperability, while sector authorities own decisions and plural providers
can challenge or replace one another. More model use is not itself progress.
Public AI procurement also enters the [C05 distribution and power
account](../C05/04-automation-income-and-power.md): actual public asset
returns, worker effects, supplier control and tax-base changes must be
shown before a claimed productivity gain funds another promise.

[HC01–HC04](05-retained-human-capability.md) now makes retained learning,
error detection and independent human review/fallback explicit where the
deployment relies on them. Assisted throughput/assignment scores alone are
insufficient. Safe assessment preserves accommodations, professional authority,
participant protections and existing rights; it creates no general private-AI ban.

[CS16–CS17](../C01/03/07-compulsory-burdens-and-country-disposition.md) requires
actual paid retained assistance/other-provider capacity; projected digital
savings cannot be its sole source. A vendor upgrade does not itself retire a
human route, authorize workforce change or add compulsory disclosure/approval.
Use the existing competent rule/source/transition decision, with real gains,
displacement and independent capability measured separately.

[Design150 DP01–DP16](09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md) now supplies
the common operating source behind this portfolio: actual career support/
technical coordinator, optional useful shared tasks, sector-owned admission,
whole relevant dependence, paid simultaneous alternatives and explicit stop/
restore scope. Routine lawful favorable/reversible automation remains possible
under PD07; a model, central host or ceremonial click supplies no protected
adverse judgment. DP chooses no added compulsory cross-sector command or
universal manual duplication; actual gains/source/received outcomes remain unknown.

[Y20 public equality](../C07/04/05-common-factors-and-equal-standing.md) prohibits
public authorities/AI from using predicted lifetime fiscal worth, care cost or
lifespan to rank political standing or deny ordinary subsistence/basic age/
necessary care/remedy. Safe aggregate planning and actual proportionate statutory
claim proof remain possible; personal explanation is no consent to a life dossier.

[AH person-directed public assistance](../C04/07-person-directed-public-assistance.md)
now chooses a bounded free core service with actual local/national debtor,
provider and independent conflict receiving. AI is optional support under these
existing task/DP/HC limits, not an unlimited personal model or a new case authority.

[GA upstream capability](10-general-ai-capability-and-public-control.md) separately
covers severe-risk general-model exposure by public/private/community controllers.
DP/portfolio use does not confer that power or waive that duty; GA permission
supplies no sector-use authority, benefit proof or sovereign AI mandate.

[DP25–DP32](09/06-procure-a-usable-exit.md) requires a priced and witnessed
exit at material reliance. Keep independently usable public decisions, safe
records, ordinary intake and actual review through a supplier change.

[DP89–DP94](09/19-public-ai-portfolio-and-common-inputs.md) checks common capacity
and the effect of easier access on actual public work.

[DP95–DP99](09/20-shared-advice-and-policy-feedback.md) tests shared public inference when later outcomes are affected by several decisions using the same advice. Fund useful independent evidence and retain lawful ownership of each act.
