# An opening liability changes the first budget

*Design292 · Constructed first-budget cash comparison.*

An opening local account has eleven units usable by day 5. Two
material services share that window, with three units due for
protected care and pay and two for verified supplier work.
Independent review and execution of these ordinary lines are
assumed funded in their respective costs. A proposed three-unit
retrofit is optional. A separately effective one-unit inspection
and repair-preparation option can stand alone.

Two sources describe the same disputed guarantee. One is a
public enterprise's contract file and one is the public payer's
contingency record. They point to the same possible four-unit
external call on day 5. The example counts one cash exposure
while keeping the enterprise and potential guarantor's legal
roles separate. There is no decision to pay the guarantee.
Compare the elected choices against both the no-call and the
declared early-call paths.

| Exact choice | Cash required by day 5 if no call | Cash required under the four-unit call | Disposition with eleven units |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Existing duties only | 5 | 9 | Both priced paths fit. |
| Duties and one-unit preparation | 6 | 10 | Both fit if the preparation has separate lawful authority and usable receiving. |
| Duties and three-unit retrofit | 8 | 12 | Fits only without the early call. Hold if the adverse path is still material and unresolved. |

The four-unit path is a declared amount and date. It is neither
proof of liability nor a bound on every undiscovered claim. If
an additional material exposure cannot be bounded, the apparent
two-unit residual under the early-call path cannot qualify an
affected new commitment. Retain safe prior work, obtain the
missing evidence or a genuinely independent source and return
the affected choice for decision. Where a guarantee is later
held unenforceable, independently verify the revised exposure,
actual release authority and continued service before any new start.

With only nine usable units, existing duties and the early call
fit in the stated model but neither optional purchase does.
A forecast three-unit recovery on day 20 cannot cover day 5.
A grant limited to other uses also provides no usable cover.
A lawful and actually received early unrestricted unit would
change the priced exposure to the ten-unit envelope and might
make the narrower choice feasible. If a second genuine guarantee
of two units is also callable, count it and reconsider even if
the first guarantee appears twice in the records. Valid due
payments and actual useful service remain separate evidence.
