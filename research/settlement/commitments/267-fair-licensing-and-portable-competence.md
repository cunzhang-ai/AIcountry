# Design354. Pay for open entry and safe recognition

[PQ01–PQ06](../../country/C08/32-fair-licensing-and-portable-competence.md)
assigns separately funded rule appraisal, qualified case assessment, independent
review and safe receiving. A backup assessor and a backup worker are different
resources. A portable credential or an issued licence buys neither.

The [inspection comparison](../fair-licensing/01-inspections-and-independent-decisions.md)
shows remaining work after two paid assessment slots and their fallback fail.
Keep original decision dates and budget lawful substitute service separately.
Actual resource quantities and accepted operators remain unverified.
