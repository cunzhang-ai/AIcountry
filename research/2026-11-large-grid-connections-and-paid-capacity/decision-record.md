# Design374 decision on large grid connections and paid capacity

## Baseline and selection

Pin Design373, the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md), EG01–EG12,
WS, IV, CBG and the qualified public AI sourcing rules. EG already
sets protected access, milestone-based reservations, limited supply
and attributable tariffs. A large applicant's delayed start,
parallel site requests or departure can still expose households
and public investors to the costs of premature construction.

[EG13–EG18](../country/C09/19/04-large-grid-connections-and-paid-capacity.md)
selects a staged material-load comparison, chargeable and funded
exit exposure, independent review and verification at each phase.
Elected bodies set lawful scope and public risk. System, network,
land and environmental owners keep separate powers.

## Alternatives and objections

Wait for a full ordinary firm connection with its proven funding
and forgone benefits. Begin a separately funded smaller phase
where actual capacity and fair entry permit. Offer later voluntary
flexibility when water, safe interruption and recovery work. A
qualified private onsite option or durable common expansion is a
substantive alternative, with actual backup, pollution, cost and
public-control consequences. Compare these routes without reserving
the same physical capacity or shared contribution twice.

Heavy security could exclude smaller legitimate or community
applicants and subsidize those able to tie up cash. Use only
proportionate verified exposure, alternative evidence and timely
return of surplus. Large politically connected firms cannot buy
a higher protected priority. A network delay cannot be relabeled
as applicant breach. Existing review and courts can order an
actual correction on the original dates. A public benefit may
justify explicitly voted shared infrastructure without assuming
the proposed user's promised jobs or consumption will occur.

## Evidence, migration and next action

[Primary records](sources.md) support the queue and cost-risk
distinctions. The [worked case](../settlement/large-grid-connections-and-paid-capacity/01-two-load-windows-and-an-exit.md)
reproduces a published difference and tests separate resource
windows and exit terms. It establishes no actual safe capacity,
authority, affected participation, affordability or delivered gain.

Keep valid connection rights, pending applications, approved works,
prior recovery and remedies. Reconcile existing deposits, duties and
dates before applying new terms prospectively. An authorized next
exercise would use a synthetic large-load proposal, one contested
milestone and independent grid, water and finance records. Price
actual preparation, waiting and fallback, then stop at the exact
qualified phase and its first exposed later duty. Reopen with a
changed common dependency, usable lower-water or lower-cost route,
invalid security or evidence of displacement.
