# National learning and institutional change: integration analysis

1 October 2026 · Assistant-authored analysis · No enacted procedure

## The design gap

In [design 4](COUNTRY-DESIGN-design-4.md), C14 tells the AIcountry research project
how to keep a current design. It does not give the proposed *country* an equally
clear path from a resident's problem or a new communication method to an authorized
law, bounded trial, observed correction or institutional retirement. C03 gives
citizens proposal rights and an elected Assembly, while C12 keeps experimental
cooperation separate from common obligations. A country could meet both statements
yet let a ministry or platform select every question, run every trial and interpret
all results. This is a textual gap, not evidence of actual abuse.

## Three arrangements

| Choice | How it learns | Who decides and what can fail |
|---|---|---|
| Continue without a national learning route | Ordinary elections, ministries, courts and local governments can change policy. | Simple and familiar, but agenda and evidence may remain with incumbents; a published report can have no route to a decision. |
| Central expert renewal commission | A protected body selects trials, evaluates policy and recommends or triggers updates. | Expertise and continuity may improve, but one body can control problem framing, data and the definition of success. Automatic law changes or vetoes would displace democratic choice. |
| Distributed inquiry with democratic settlement | Citizens, residents, local governments, workers and service users can raise problems through several media; public bodies commission rival inquiry, operational feedback and qualified evaluation; the elected Assembly or competent local body accepts, repairs, retains or rejects a proposed change under law. | Requires money, staff, data protections and a usable agenda route. Power can still concentrate in the funder, data custodian or mediator, so those dependencies must be reviewable and replaceable. |

Select the third as a **reference national arrangement**, not as an adopted method.
It is more consistent with C03's public lawmaking and C12's plural communication than
automatic expert revision. The choice is not proof that it yields better lives than
ordinary accountable government. Political disagreement over values remains for
representatives and the public; inquiry can narrow factual uncertainty without
turning a model into a sovereign judge.

## Candidate national rule and operating sequence

1. **Agenda:** a person may report a problem in ordinary language or another
   accessible medium, alone or through an agent. Civil associations, opposition,
   local bodies and public workers can also propose a change. A public office records
   the issue and identifies who can act; it does not require a technical dossier or
   an AI-generated consensus. An independent route challenges refusal to docket a
   lawful proposal. Bounded agenda slots and response duties need legislation and
   resources, with special attention to people who cannot afford to campaign.
2. **Inquiry:** the responsible institution distinguishes observed harm, disputed
   meanings, rival causal explanations, value conflict and actual constraints. It
   discloses sponsors and common data sources, lets affected people challenge
   interpretation, and protects private testimony. A parliamentary committee and
   independent audit can demand reasons and records. Neither inquiry nor a citizen
   panel automatically enacts its preferred solution.
3. **Trial and operation:** a policy pilot needs prior legal authority, bounded
   affected scope, budget, baseline duties, meaningful participation, adversarial
   signals, a stop rule and a route for harms that cannot be undone. A trial may not
   suspend constitutional rights or outsource coercion to an AI. Exiting a trial
   must preserve pending benefits, contracts, cases and ecological duties. Where
   consent or fair comparison is impossible, use less intrusive observation or
   public reasoning instead of pretending an experiment is neutral.
4. **Decision and review:** a qualified evaluation reports distribution, resource
   costs, people's ability to object and observed outcomes as separate dimensions.
   The elected Assembly or competent local body decides under its existing mandate,
   with public reasons for retain, repair, expand, replace or reject. A successful
   trial does not authorize nationwide scaling. Constitutional changes follow the
   valid constitutional route, never the trial's own rules. Every replacement names
   the successor for each obligation and funds transition. Later events can reopen
   the decision without making every service permanently provisional.

## Support and limits

The [OECD evaluation review](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/improving-governance-with-policy-evaluation_89b1577d-en.html)
describes evaluation as a route to accountability and learning while noting
capability and use gaps across countries. Its [guidelines for representative
deliberation](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/evaluation-guidelines-for-representative-deliberative-processes_10ccbfcb-en.html)
distinguish process integrity, participant experience and pathways to impact.
These institutional analyses support the questions asked, not the effectiveness
of this exact arrangement. The earlier [institutional-renewal proposal](../2026-09-institutional-renewal/framework-v3.1.md)
supplies the exploration/operation/renewal distinction but is a research hypothesis,
not a governing mandate. No affected party, actual policymaker or independent
evaluator took part here. Legal adoption, budget, competent staff and observed
results are all absent.

This selection changes the proposed *national* C14 and touches C03 and C12. It
does not replace the research project's [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md), grant
new publication rights or make evaluation reports binding law. Migration in a real
jurisdiction would have to map existing petition rights, administrative procedures,
research ethics, public records, procurement, local powers, constitutional amendments
and continuing liabilities. The next real test requires a specified lawful setting
and affected public; a further invented pilot would not establish readiness.
