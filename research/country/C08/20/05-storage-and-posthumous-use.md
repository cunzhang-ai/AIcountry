# Stored material, loss of contact, death and bounded estate claims

*RP09–RP10; part of [C08/20](../20-reproductive-law-and-parentage.md).*

**RP09 — Store under actual instructions, not inherited ownership.** A
licensed custodian records material/source, current separate storage/use
consents, scope, expiry, fees/public coverage, withdrawal, serious incidents
and accepted closure transfer. An adult storage consent may run for up to
ten years and be affirmatively renewed; no age/fertility score requires use
or disposal. Medically indicated childhood preservation defaults through 20,
with the person's own supported choice/renewal at majority and actual funding
review. Parents cannot donate a child's future gametes to someone else.

A joint embryo requires the continuing required reproductive consent of both
material contributors until transfer. Relationship exit, one person's strong
wish, sunk cost or a stored invoice does not authorize use against withdrawal.
A court can preserve/dispose lawfully with affected parties heard; it cannot
invent consent to pregnancy or research. Death/incapacity does not transfer
material into the ordinary estate or an agent's unrestricted control.

Give safe usable renewal/expiry notice with help and a12-month protected
hold before contested/unsupported disposal. Nonresponse is not consent to
reproduction, research or donation. If contact cannot safely be achieved,
a court may approve proportionate substituted notice and an outcome on
actual evidence, source instructions, rights and resources; no silent clinic
abandonment rule. Preserve materially disputed holdings until timely lawful
review and an actual funded safe receiver; an evidence hold is not authority
to store indefinitely without accounting or to bill impossible penalties.

Provider failure triggers actual material/record inventory, viable transfer,
patient notice, confidentiality and funded receiving under V/M where relevant.
Separate necessary publicly covered preservation from discretionary private
storage; unpaid fees never transfer a body/material-use mandate to a creditor.
Destruction/loss retains independent civil/sector claims and original evidence;
no automatic homicide fiction, compulsory retrieval or embryo public auction.

**RP10 — Posthumous use needs explicit prior reproductive and parenting will.**
No retrieval/use after death solely on kinship, a will's general inheritance
clause or a partner's wishes. Require the deceased's proved prior specific
posthumous authorization, actual named/scoped recipient and parent/support
intention, surviving person's current bodily consent and lawful clinical safety.
A nonparent donation remains nonparent; missing consent stops new use without
erasing any person already born or lawful existing claim.

A child already conceived before death retains ordinary B34 provision. For
new posthumous conception with a proved intended legal-parent commitment,
select default estate-descendant eligibility only if born within 36 months of
death and a credible notice reaches the estate within six months. Reserve
only court-reasoned likely claims, not every estate for three years. An
actually blocked claimant has B37/B08 restoration/reliance safeguards; no
blanket clawback of innocent essential support. Explicit lawful contingent
will/trust gifts may provide later benefits within actual assets/source law.

These time bounds serve estate closure, not lesser human status. Identity,
care, origin and independent I/S rights persist regardless; no new Y survivor
pot, citizenship or unlimited support is inferred from genes. The court must
reconcile actual B34/B35 creditors, dependants, residue and existing rights.
The 36/six-month choice is contested and uncalibrated, with qualified/affected
review required; no new rule silently cuts an inherited source-law right.
