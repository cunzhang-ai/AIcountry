# C03. Public decisions, outside influence and particular private interests

Design179 ·4November2026 · PI01–PI20 proposed coordinated constitutional/ordinary law

**Public decisions must have a usable record of material outside influence and
specific financial conflicts. No public office may become a private paid access
service, and disclosure alone cannot authorize particular self-benefit.** This
adds common duties for legislators and other public decision owners beyond the
existing PC/NI campaign boundary and C04 integrity principles. It creates no
lobbyist contact licence, new commission, political truth office or actual law.

places the narrow anti-self-benefit constraint at constitutional rank. Ordinary
records rules cannot alone defeat an express later primary Act; nationwide
statutory constitutional validity stays with CJ. No auditor/Clerk can cancel a
vote, change N, manufacture a vacancy or veto forming/replacing government.
Actual constitutional/software/contribution permissions and holds stay unchanged
until their own legitimate revision; this package cannot approve itself.

The [country comparison](../../settlement/public-decision-integrity/README.md)

and ownership/redistribution alternatives. Transparent lobbying can remain
unequal; actual paid countervoice, independent records and usable correction
are essential and still unproved. No source, causal reduction of capture,
legitimate affected assent or better lives is established by these drafts.
