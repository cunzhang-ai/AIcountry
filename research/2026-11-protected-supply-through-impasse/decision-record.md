## Baseline and decision

Use the [Design521 edited-file baseline](baseline.json), frozen
[Design520](../2026-11-first-round-stable/README.md) and the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md).
The opening already provides standing essential authority, a 90-day
indispensable operations bridge, an accrued-only closeout and a separate
elected continuation vote. A competent Assembly can still vote supply
without investiture. This review uses those powers as the baseline.

Select [BLC11–BLC15](../country/C06/66/02-protected-supply-through-an-impasse.md).
An actual prior legislature may authorize and fund an exact limited
supplement for independently verified loss of a legally competent
supply forum, with no timely equivalent authority. Full legislative
annulment is the constructed example. An Assembly that remains
legally competent retains its ordinary legislative and budget decisions
even when a motion loses or it cannot produce quorum. Give a qualified
standing schedule the same credit as a separate instrument.

## Evidence and comparison

dependencies and receipt of service and civic protection. They used a
shared research corpus. They do not supply actual consent, independent
empirical confirmation or a participating adopter.

The [combined comparison](../settlement/first-round-mandate/03-protected-supply-through-impasse.md)

alternatives for the distribution of spending authority. Their own scope
and limits apply. Neither demonstrates operation under the combined
failure. The constructed calendar uses the existing judgment-based
repeat rule, unchanged bridge and separate final appropriation date.
The fiscal branch distinguishes pooled receipts from usable independent
cash on each due date. The lived comparison distinguishes component
results, usable combinations and unobserved receipt.

## Continuing duties and review

Review authority, reserve cash, independent payers, legal challenge,
local capacity and actual receipt through expiry and the next call.
No scope transfers to a caretaker, AI or court simply because supply
is absent. An existing claim survives with its real deadline and remedies.
A finite payment source cannot finance a permanent duty and an existing
restricted balance cannot pay a different task merely because both are
public duties. Questions about the company's profit share remain in the
separate Design521 proposal.

A real adoption would need a competent legislature, actual elections,
priced choices and direct affected participation. Prepare the exact
continuity schedule, independently executable sources and earliest
lawful successor date for one affected scope. Compare against strongest
ordinary repair and a wider law with equal rights, recipients and due
times. The legislature sets actual fiscal limits. Reopen on legal
misclassification of an impasse, competing funded uses, unobserved
receiving, dependency between proposed independent routes, or a simpler
equally protective choice. The settlement and original archive remain
available as the first-round baseline.
