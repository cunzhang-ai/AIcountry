# Design358. Pay for source access, judgment and timely alternatives

[DP64–DP69](../../country/C11/09/14-independent-judgment-before-adverse-action.md)
joins original evidence, qualified first consideration, verification of material
differences and independent challenge to the existing deadline. A second
signature on a common summary buys no independent fact. Keep existing
awards, paid urgent routes and unresolved cases when a review fails.

The [comparison](../independent-judgment-in-ai-assisted-decisions/01-one-summary-two-signatures.md)
finds the competent ordinary route cheaper than the first-view assisted
route under the stated assumptions. No actual staffing saving is released.
