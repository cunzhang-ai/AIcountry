# CBG01–CBG04. Budget and physical account

[Climate budgets](../27-climate-budgets-and-essential-continuity.md)

**CBG01 — Set the period and cumulative ceilings by law.** The Assembly
enacts successive five-calendar-year gross domestic CO2 ceilings and
a cumulative ceiling schedule, initially covering at least two
periods. Before the first of two remaining enacted periods ends,
set a further five-year period with actual evidence and due
participation.
Publish annual delivery paths and longer asset implications.
The law states covered sources, inventory boundary, units, starting
date, any sector ceilings and the treatment of material land fluxes.
Choose amounts using scientific ranges, actual capability, historical
and external burdens and a publicly justified national responsibility.
A global carbon budget does not allocate a national share. No
numeric national ceiling or present permit follows from this proposal.

**CBG02 — Keep measures and legal consequences distinct.** Record
gross CO2 releases and independently verified removals separately,
and publish the physical net account and uncertainty. Under the
initial gross-cap proposal, no removal, purchased unit or avoided
emission increases legal gross headroom. Set separately accountable
rules for methane and other greenhouse gases with stated metrics.
Publish territorial emissions and the distinct embodied and
transboundary account. Imported goods do not become a domestic
emission or justify ignoring displaced harm. Apply actual treaty
accounting in its own scope. AQ air, water, habitat and other
lawful floors cannot be exchanged for a climate unit.

**CBG03 — Measure on original evidence and preserve revisions.**
The competent inventory office publishes methods, observations,
coverage, uncertainty and comparable vintages, with a quarterly
provisional path and an annual consolidated account. Independent
evidence and audit staff examine material source gaps, overlapping
claims and revisions. Record actual power dispatch and other
operating emissions. Verify any removal's quantity, durability,
loss risk, lawful title and counterpart accounting for its separate
use. A reversal must be recorded at its actual time. Preserve
both the prior reported finding and a reasoned factual correction.
A forecast or scope change cannot remove an observed emission.

**CBG04 — Use both ceilings when determining remaining headroom.**
Usable ordinary headroom is the smaller of the remaining
period ceiling and the remaining cumulative ceiling through
that period, using all measured in-scope emissions since the
respective start. If either is exhausted, show the shortfall
and the required lawful correction. In the joint public account,
show expected use by existing operations and concurrent material
commitments, plus any contingency reserved under law. Assess proposed
uses together before granting authority that relies on the same
remaining space. A planning estimate confers no permit or property.
An overrun already counted against the cumulative ceiling is not
deducted a second time.
Unused period space does not enlarge a later period ceiling.
No ordinary advance on a future period is selected. Report
past noncompliance under its governing law and observed evidence,
even if a later period brings the cumulative account back within
its ceiling. Factual corrections follow CBG03.
