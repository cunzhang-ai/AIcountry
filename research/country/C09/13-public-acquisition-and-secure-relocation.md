# C09. Public acquisition and secure relocation

*Design145 · LA01–LA20 · [C09 land and ecology](../C09-land-and-ecology.md) · Candidate ordinary law, not an actual taking power.*

Select a **bounded public-acquisition route with replacement, livelihood and
independent-remedy duties**. Retain ordinary purchase, repair, route changes,
leases and easements as actual alternatives. Existing B07/C09 public-purpose,
necessity and compensation principles now have a positive procedure; a planning
approval or essential-service promise still does not transfer property.

| Canonical part | Selected rule |
|---|---|
| [LA01–LA05: purpose and authority](13/01-purpose-authority-and-alternatives.md) | Narrow public purposes, competent elected vote, least necessary right, no new generic emergency seizure |
| [LA06–LA10: people and compensation](13/02-people-valuation-and-replacement.md) | Distinct title/tenure/livelihood claims, actual replacement and access, tax/debt interfaces without double recovery |
| [LA11–LA15: notice, payment and possession](13/03-notice-payment-and-independent-possession.md) | Real notice/hearing, finite notice life, cash/secure disputed deposit, independent possession order and no homelessness |
| [LA16–LA20: continuity and correction](13/04-continuity-misuse-and-paid-transition.md) | Property/personal-capital source, unused-land process, actual paid successors and lawful prospective migration |

No private developer, minister, valuer, registry, project AI or inspector approves
all steps. Elected local/national decisions, ordinary independent courts, actual
payer and safe receivers have distinct roles. Public benefit is not established
by larger GDP, sale price, a single cost-benefit ratio or the proponent's forecast.
Applicable collective consent and nonreplaceable rights remain separately necessary.
An economically attractive project may therefore fail lawful authorization or
actual replacement/source requirements; this retained cost is not hidden.

The [finite country-gap review](../../settlement/country-gaps/README.md) selects
this omission over revisiting already specified educational ages or another
emergency study. [Sources and rival institutions](../../2026-11-country-gaps-and-public-acquisition/decision-record.md)
are scoped comparisons, not public consent or proof of superior outcomes.
No actual parcel, holder, project, title, legal competence, valuation, budget,
accepting replacement or independent affected-party review is established.
