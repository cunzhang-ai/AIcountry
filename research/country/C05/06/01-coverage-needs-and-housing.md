# Income clauses I01–I03: coverage, needs and housing

*Design 58 proposed clauses; [income package](../06-income-floor-and-safe-resources.md).*

**I01 — Person-level protection.** Every person ordinarily living in the
country shall have a direct ordinary needs claim, without a citizenship,
political-franchise waiting period, employment, sponsor, marriage or AI
account condition. Actual ordinary residence is a factual center of life,
not possession of one document or an assumption about language or descent.
Use accessible proof, provisional treatment and independent correction
during dispute. Temporary persons in urgent need retain continuity hardship
and essential protection; existing portable/foreign claims and obligations
under the country's external authority remain governed by their source laws.
This Act shall not decide admission, citizenship or the vote. A child's
claim is independent of parental fault or status; name a safe lawful receiver
and independent safeguarding without withholding necessities during a dispute.

**I02 — Legislated needs and autonomous use.** The Assembly shall specify
a publicly priced basket of adequate nutrition, clothing, hygiene,
communications, ordinary mobility and participation, including a minimum
person-controlled cash component. Age and functional needs inform goods
and access, not human worth. Housing, necessary work costs and disability
access are separately reconciled; essential public health, school and care
shall not be converted into a compulsory cash substitute. Price actual
available goods and reachable access, including tax and place differences;
a free listed service counts only where received for that same component.
An independently governed evidence service preserves prices, original
observations and uncertainty; affected people and plural outside inquiry
can challenge an unusable or stigmatizing basket. Experts publish evidence;
the Assembly chooses content and material revisions.

Law shall fix an accessible monthly price-update formula and source fallback,
with a basket-content review at least every two years. Routine calculation
cannot reduce nominal personal-basket protection without prospective law
and rights/transition review. Missing price data retains the prior lawful
value and triggers source correction, not a zero benefit. Earlier safe-time
hardship duties apply during a price surge. General CPI alone cannot prove
that the poor or a remote resident can buy the specified necessities.

**I03 — Actual housing and one shared bill.** Recognize a person's actual
necessary share of safe rent, essential utilities or lawful owner-occupier
running cost. The same bill shall be allocated once across its occupants;
publish the share rule and permit a challenge. A reference decent-unit cost
informs planned provision, not automatic eviction or a hidden benefit cap.
An above-reference unavoidable cost needs housing-authority response and
an actually usable safer/affordable alternative before ordinary support
is reduced on that ground. Review within three months; a missed review or
unavailable alternative shall not itself extinguish protection. Voluntary
luxury expansion need not be subsidized; record the specific distinction
and preserve subsistence, tenancy rights and direct remedy.

Shared residence shall not prove shared cash or consent. Actual housing
provided safely by another person may credit that component, but cannot
erase the independent personal cash component, a separate claim or safe
exit. Preserve location privacy. Count restoration, supply, inspection,
receiving-place and transport costs in C06; money alone creates no dwelling.

[H11–H15 cost rent](../../C09/12/03-cost-rent-and-finance.md) now adds a permanent
public/community option and actual-bill/arrears/repair reconciliation. It changes
neither I03's necessary-share floor nor the received-support rule. A capital grant
is no second tenant payment, and low modeled cost rent is no affordability finding.

**Design108 housing clarification.** Apply I03 to actual necessary payer costs,
not a uniform model bill or imputed rent. Where rent excludes essential utilities,
recognize the separate due component; where already included/received, count it
once. Owner status does not create a free cash claim for economic depreciation,
mortgage principal or general wealth accumulation. A specific legally due,
necessary protected safety/renewal payment needs its actual purpose, amount,
restricted use and nonduplicate cost record; unsupported CFC is not that proof.
Do not deny an existing recognized claim because new records are missing: retain
source-law protection, pay undisputed needs/provisional urgent relief and allow
assisted review. No valuation experiment authorizes eviction or a hidden cap.

Design115 [I13–I16](05-component-standards-and-cash-control.md) specifies
component profiles and at least 20% of personal ordinary B as ordinary cash
need within N, preserving stronger old rights. Actual private needs may require
more. The provisional .6 child factor is no adequacy or additional-needs cap;
no household estimate or GBP benchmark automatically sets a personal B.

[CB13–CB18](../../C09/14-shared-buildings-and-neighbor-rights.md) applies this
existing necessary-need rule to an actual legally due primary-home common
safety/renewal share with verified purpose, current I05 resources and protected
receipt. No new all-owner capital subsidy/loan/lien or general depreciation
allowance. Pay only the matched qualifying item once across owner, association
reserve, public direct receipt and actual insurer/developer recovery; expected
recovery is no present cash. No tenant inheritance of an owner's capital bill
or reduction of unrelated person-controlled cash through a fictitious receipt.

[I36–I41](10-essential-cost-shocks-and-usable-protection.md) applies I02
through an early price or availability loss. Keep the last lawful nominal
floor and separate necessary rent, actual cash receipt and physical access.
