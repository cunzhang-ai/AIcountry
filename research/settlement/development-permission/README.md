# Development follows public rules and reaches a real serviced place

Design172 · [LU01–LU20](../../country/C09/17-development-permission-and-serviced-land.md)

**A conforming applicant should receive the planning component under public
rules, with actual title, infrastructure and safe occupation resolved separately.**
Elected councils decide plans; existing qualified officers apply them. Neither
an incumbent neighbor nor a minister sells an individual political exception.

| Joined choice | Practical consequence |
|---|---|
| Public plan | Contextual uses/intensity/risks and necessary access; no invented universal density |
| Applying entitlement | Proved conformity gives a reviewable right to the planning component; no approval by silence |
| Parking/access | No mandatory general residential parking minimum; independent accessible/emergency/servicing needs persist |
| Actual serviced phases | Plans, security and promises are not commissioned water, safe buildings or occupied homes |
| Attributable costs | Lawful project/share costs and compatible same-cost credits once; public backlog and operating rights retain their sources |
| Predictable change | Identifiable first-receipt future plan regime, AP finite proof/decision, material-change rules and actual old reliance |
| Continuing duties | Finite new commencement term, maintenance/abandonment and paid lawful successors, without old-grant expiry |
