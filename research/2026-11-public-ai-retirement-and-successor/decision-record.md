# Design443. Preserve protected service through upstream retirement

Design442 is the pinned predecessor. DP38–DP42 already govern
shared changes. DP25–DP32 and SC34–SC35 already govern exit and
handover. The added choice is enforceable dated notice, actual
version evidence and joint service qualification when upstream
support ends before the public contract. This revision used AI
assistance and parallel review.

Compare independently safe temporary continuation, early migration
and paid ordinary operation. Extension may preserve staff time but
depends on lawful support, distinct control and actual scope.
Migration may work sooner when the successor can be independently
evaluated. Ordinary receiving can protect the same rights but
needs actual qualified staff and review. Select the ordinary bridge
in the constructed case. Record which rights would miss their
deadline if paid cover fails. Avoid open-ended duplicate operation.

The [sources](sources.md) establish lifecycle and risk-management
mechanisms. They do not verify a particular contract, model,
service clock, affected party or independent approval. Existing
procurement, legal powers and review remain operative. Preserve
original claims, protected records, lawful payments and continuing
duties through any supplier change.

Next test one real shared supplier, two service owners and an
independent fallback roster against a dated lifecycle notice,
including late notice, a changed model alias and partial alternate
cover. Compare receipts and burden after the first deadline and
the next call. Reopen on an earlier cutoff, exhausted staff,
changed scope or a better independently verified choice.
