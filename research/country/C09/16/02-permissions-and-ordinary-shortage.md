# WS06–WS10. Conditional new use and ordinary seasonal government

*Part of [water allocation and service](../16-water-allocation-and-service.md).*

**WS06 — Finite new covered permissions.** For genuinely new/enlarged
commercial or material noncommercial self-supply surface/groundwater abstraction
connected to a shared basin/aquifer
under the coordinated WS/TD Act and actual national territorial jurisdiction,
the Steward names user/collective, source/intake, gross seasonal quantity/rate,
separate authorized seasonal depletion amount and return conditions,
place/purpose, reliability/shortage class, monitoring, review and final date.
Select a maximum ten-year new grant, shorter where evidence/source warrants,
with at least annual seasonal allocation; no perpetual or automatic renewal.
Coverage does not depend on public/private/collective land/source/operator title;
actual existing rights, required consent, LA and territorial/foreign limits remain.
No coverage of isolated private storage by mere assertion of connectivity.
Ten years is a contestable policy ceiling, not optimal hydrology/investment.
Longer lawful land/reservoir/construction concessions cannot promise water beyond
a valid grant. No new ordinary household-collection licence, extraction tax or
offence. Actual prior minor-use exemptions and collective/subsistence scope
must be carried expressly into the adopting Act; no retrospective reclassification.
That Act must define material/cumulative connected self-supply and genuinely minor
domestic collection through exact source-specific thresholds/conditions before
application; fragmented wells or a household label cannot hide material depletion.
No-licence minor use still follows expressly enacted proportionate source/shortage
duties, not an inferred home-surveillance or cutoff power. Preserve essential
access and actual collective consent/authority; no officer invents a threshold.

**WS07 — Public reasons before first possession or highest bid.** Reserve actual
essential personal/domestic supply, essential-service/food-security/subsistence
and protected ecosystem/dependence requirements before discretionary expansion.
Rank feasible competing new uses under enacted need, net source impact, reliable
capacity, distribution, whole public burden and lower-use alternatives, disclosing
source/conflicts and objections. Historic take, jobs/GDP label, bid or political
connection buys no priority. Essential food is not every export crop; essential
power is not every industrial cooling project. Existing rights retain WS04/09.

Publish qualified competing applications/intended award/reasons accessibly,
protecting private/security evidence. Give ten working days before an ordinary
new grant operates for objections/direct interim review; a material source/
quantity/recipient change restarts that proposal's notice. Retain AP final/reply/
omission clocks, earlier rights and actual stays; silence confers no assent/title.
Equal-ranked indivisible proposals use a publicly specified fair tie method
under prior law, not secret discretion or volume-weighted voting. No-grant is
real. An over-allocated pool admits no extra net pressure by paper headroom;
equivalent release must have actual compatible source/legal effect.

**WS08 — Fresh renewal, controlled transfer, usable collective practice.**
Renewal is a reasoned source/rights/need decision before expiry, with no automatic
sunk-investment priority or use-it-or-lose-it waste. Planned nonuse within the
actual conditions is not illicit hoarding. New permission is not freely tradable
source ownership or unreviewed collateral. Change of controller/use/intake/time/
return/depletion needs actual approval and fresh adverse-effect/priority review,
with notice where material. A sale/record update cannot expand use or erase old
repair/closure/creditor claims. Older actual transferable/longer rights retain
source law until lawful transition; no retrospective security confiscation.

An actually authorized collective permission may use fair internal access,
assisted low-cost proof and proportionate shared monitoring without forcing a
community into a corporation or silencing outsiders. Its representative cannot
waive another's rights or multiply a source by delegation/agent copies. Compare
monitoring burden and informal cooperation with an individual permit regime.

**WS09 — Ordinary seasonal sharing enacted before shortage.** The Act/plan sets
observable quantity/quality triggers, decision-maker, essential/ecological
reservations, usable notice, class reductions, restoration and review. Steward
applies these rules with observed conditions, uncertainty, permit-specific
amounts and reasons; an AI cannot invent priority. After protected reservations
and continuing actual prior rights, new grants in the same shortage class share
the remaining compatible depletion envelope proportionately to their separately
authorized same-unit seasonal depletion allotments, capped at each allotment.
Gross diversion/rate/conveyance caps, evidenced return obligations and joint
location/quality/time/lag constraints must also hold. No conversion from a gross
allotment to a net share without actual qualified return assumptions. A zero-
depletion use still has physical/gross constraints, not unlimited withdrawal.
Where another constraint binds, reduce/reassess within the enacted compatible
pool; do not reallocate fictitious released water across incompatible sources.
Lower-priority discretionary classes
reduce before higher classes. Location/quality/time are not fungible. Older
rights use their own shortage/variation law, not this pro-rata pool by inference.

No blanket farm/industry shutdown, full lawn/pool domestic exemption or favored
firm exception. Basic domestic/clinical/care/sanitation, actual food-subsistence
and animal-welfare duties need a delivered route; a reservation is not received
water. If actual supply cannot meet all floors, disclose breach/alternatives,
retain NP/E/constitutional/ecological law and seek bounded elected correction.
No general life-over-ecology exception. A shortage itself grants no extra
power over private or foreign sources.
CP's actual public pool/local24/absolute72 clocks are not seasonal drought law;
PS21–PS32 scheduled public backup-power priority is unchanged.

**WS10 — Lawful correction without new coercion.** Actual TS/TD/AP compliance
uses proportionate lawful records/inspection, reply/correction and review.
Public office/AI detection is not an offence's proof; existing fault, penalty,
LF entry/force and court law apply. No new home-entry, asset seizure, water police
or punitive cutoff. Imminent source hazards use existing competent safety powers
and safe alternatives. Material long-term restriction outside lawful grant
conditions needs actual elected/source-law authority, compensation where due
and paid household/farm/worker transition. A damages claim is not water, an
automatic safety stay or a cancellation of an actual stay. Expiry never ends
old injury, worker, creditor, restoration or accrued public-service claims.

[WS21–WS25](05-water-power-and-catchment-limits.md) coordinates qualifying
electricity with this water allocation in the same dry and hot interval.
It carries applicable nonwaivable limits, independent environmental
approval, original priorities and actual downstream receiving.
