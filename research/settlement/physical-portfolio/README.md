# Country physical portfolio: food, seasonal water and reliable power

Design 127 · [Canonical choice](../../country/C06/50-country-physical-portfolio.md)

Five physical profiles cross production and source choices; each has retained,
six-hour and extended twenty-four-hour firm/storage power arrangements. The conditional bounded-crop
reference replaces fixed mixed-share expansion as the next numerical example.
Its quarter-scale is a hypothesis, not a quota or safe national food standard.
