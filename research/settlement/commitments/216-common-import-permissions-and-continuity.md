# 216. Common import permissions and continuity

| Owner | Bounded obligation | Receipt or failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public buyer and applicable regulators | Trace indispensable upstream permission, dates, actual authority and alternative quality | A separate seller counts only through a truly available path. |
| Assembly and authorized Treasury payer | Fund gross commitments and qualified backup on owned currency and the domestic calendar | Keep already paid money, current legal capacity, returned funds and affected budgets separate. |
| Trade officers and competent elected authorities | Maintain separate factual assessment, lawful negotiation and exact approval of material terms | Preserve review and lawful obligations. A supply demand grants no waiver. |
| Clinical and other receiving operators | Confirm accepted local delivery and treatment or use by both deadlines | Report missing people and dates even when cash or cargo arrives later. |
