# Court orders need a receiver, lawful means and actual delivery

*B17–B20; part of [C08 civil core](../13-civil-obligations-and-executed-remedies.md).*

**B17 — Timely independent relief.** Accessible courts use published lawful
allocation/recusal, competent paid judges, clerks, counsel and records.
Urgent consideration occurs before useful protection is lost; for this
candidate, a lawfully assigned duty judge must consider the request within 24 hours
at the latest, with shorter liberty/clinical rules prevailing. That is not
a guaranteed favorable decision, lawful waiting allowance or finding of
actual capacity. Interim orders need specific grounds, minimum effective
scope, notice, evidence and fair respondent/third-party protection. Ex parte
relief gets a prompt meaningful hearing opportunity no later than 72 hours, with at
most one further 72-hour necessity extension; no serial reset. Underlying
claims/duties survive lapse. These clocks explicitly propose broader civil
application; earlier E13–E15 review is not silently transferred to it.

A court may preserve specified assets/evidence or prevent an imminent
irreparable wrong, but cannot freeze every worker's subsistence or an
unrelated person's property on a model match. Security/costs cannot exclude
an impecunious person from essential protection. Later compensation for a
wrongful measure needs fair grounds and hearing; losing on the merits does
not automatically prove a malicious application. Appeal and any stay need
reasons; absent a lawful stay, the obligated receiver still acts.

**B18 — Private execution without private coercion.** A qualified statutory
civil enforcement service carries court-authorized acts, with notice,
proportionate asset inquiry, third-party claims and direct judicial review.
It has no commission/reward tied to seizures or recovered amounts. A claimant,
landlord, platform or collector cannot acquire sovereign force, secret
surveillance, lockout or detention by contract. Secured interests need their
actual priority, notice and lawful enforcement route, not a self-executing
software foreclosure. Subsistence cash/goods, disability needs, dependants
and proportionate work tools are protected under an explicit exemption
schedule; high-value surplus is not universally immune. Essential housing
loss requires a lawful court route and separate C07 receiving/protection,
not disappearance of a valid creditor claim. [U01–U12](../14-private-insolvency-and-debt-recovery.md) supplies candidate
exemption/priority/insolvency rules; actual law, jurisdiction and loss finance
remain unverified. No new power fills an unadopted or missing source.

A debtor's inability to pay is not civil imprisonment or criminal contempt.
Deliberate obstruction needs a separately enacted ground, personal fault,
fair hearing and proportionate remedy. Verify actual transfer/return/repair;
sending a letter, placing a lien or auctioning an unavailable asset is not
received compensation. Unpaid claims and third-party rights remain visible.

**B19 — Government execution and refusal.** A state judgment names the duty
owner, lawful Treasury channel, amount/act, date, receiver and verification.
The litigating minister cannot selectively block source records or lawful
due payment. Prior law supplies a bounded continuing appropriation and
narrow substitute record/payment executor through audited rotation/recusal;
this extends only within expressly adopted B19 scope, not by reusing E15's
approval. No new tax, rationing policy, sovereign debt or worker can arise
from a bailiff's instruction. Distinguish willful refusal from cash/physical
shortage. Personal consequences require actual legal duty/ability, fault,
notice and independent review; a clerk cannot jail a minister for an empty
account. Real shortage retains the person's judgment and requires competent
elected finance/receiving alternatives under C06/T01–T12. If all channels
are controlled or fail, publish safe reasons for the unmet remedy and maintain
direct independent challenge; an order alone has not restored public control.

**B20 — Collective claims, continuity and code boundary.** Courts permit
qualified representative claims where common harms otherwise go unremedied.
Check representative/sponsor conflicts, common issues, actual notice,
accessible participation and distribution. Opt-in is the baseline. A narrowly
enacted opt-out route may cover uniform monetary claims only with genuinely
usable notice/exit and adequate representation; silence after an unread
platform message is not consent to waive bodily, family, status or individualized
rights. Review settlements, material dissent, fees and actual distributions.
Unclaimed compensation cannot simply become a sponsor's profit or budget
receipt. Independent inquiry/consumer services can assist and expose patterns;
they do not judge their own disputed enforcement. Protect private testimony.

Pending cases, agreed lawful settlements, wages, deposits, judgments and
records move to named lawful successors. Preserve earlier dates and actual
obligations during statute/provider/local-boundary change. Article/clock
completion and digital payment success do not prove a received remedy.
Cost qualified intake, counsel, adjudication, records, enforcement, receivers
and lawful loss finance once in C06; actual quantities/costs remain unknown.
Candidate [private insolvency](../14-private-insolvency-and-debt-recovery.md)
now specifies U01–U12; [B21–B40](../16-family-agency-property-and-estates.md)
adds family/estate, periods and a registry-loss payer. Actual source finance,
[B41–B52](../17-jurisdiction-and-legal-cooperation.md) now supplies a private-
international/cooperation candidate; actual source finance, special reproductive/
sector/international-crime law and safe capacity remain incomplete.
Future contributions retain changed dependencies, material dissent and
reopening conditions. Reassess the whole country's missing design choices
before automatically adding another local procedure.

The [LF public-force ceiling](../../C08/24-lawful-force-and-civilian-response.md) adds a separate means test for an already authorized public act. P04 private defense, B18 claims, original clocks and court jurisdiction remain distinct; a valid order does not itself authorize force.
