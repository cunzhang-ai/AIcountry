# C14. Territorial status and shared constitutional authority

*Design 143 · [Founding and constitutional change](../01-founding-and-constitutional-change.md) · Candidate, not adopted law.*

[A15](../../../authority/provisions/04-territorial-status-A15.md) now selects a
protected territorial-status route rather than deferring its electorate and
thresholds to an unspecified later choice. [C04 TS01–TS16](../../C04/02-territorial-self-government.md)
contains the canonical implementation: accessible reviewable scoping; a five-
percent area petition under existing local-standing/home rules; a protected
collection period; funded, finite negotiation; exact public terms; the prior
valid national A9 route plus separate area majority. No preliminary permission
ballot is required. Minority/collective rights and ordinary service remedies
retain independent sources; neither public can average away the other's consent.

The protected charter must be legitimately adopted before the first case.
Ordinary framework clocks do not adopt their own governing charter. Actual
predecessor law remains the national franchise/denominator source under RR06;
TS's area presentation-date denominator does not rewrite the national initiative-
opening rule. Constitutional/status votes use actual ballot-day standing, distinct
from ordinary EL P-date anticipation. TS expressly joins PC14's exact final-text
calendar and separate paid campaign questions without retroactive poll changes.

The final text must specify operative dates/conditions, jurisdiction, sources and
paid successors, not leave a Cabinet readiness veto. A domestic vote does not
supply foreign consent/resources or cancel debts, title, nationality, pensions,
workers or earlier due rights. Protected current terms/immediate succession,
actual collective protections and applicable international routes remain.
Courts can compel owed process and rights, not decree independence after delay.

Shared national authorization is selected over unilateral area majority, retaining
the objection that national rejection may indefinitely frustrate a justified
regional wish. Negotiation and review do not resolve that value conflict. No
actual map, affected public, source-law qualification, paid operation or superior
outcome has been established. This candidate provides a concrete comparison for
legitimate choice; it does not bind an actual people's self-determination claim.
