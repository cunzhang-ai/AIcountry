# C09. Private rents, practical tenure and housing entry

*RT01–RT31 · Proposed ordinary law, not an actual rent order.*

Select **freely agreed genuine initial private rents, with a nonwaivable annual
CPI ceiling during the continuing tenancy**, lawful succession and no sham
reset. Actual rent can be lower; the ceiling grants no automatic increase.
Signed index changes can require a reduction. Allow tightly justified improvement
and temporary essential-cost relief through independent ordinary courts.

This fills an actual H gap: secure indefinite tenancy did not specify which
originally agreed private escalation/market-review terms could operate. B
already prevents arbitrary changes without valid terms; it supplies no general
rent policy. H11–H13's audited public/community cost-rental schedule stays
separate. RT does not convert private landlords into cost-rental agencies.

Genuine full turnover can reset to a newly agreed rent. This tenancy-linked
choice protects stayers more than entrants and can encourage conversion,
withdrawal or pressure to leave. H02/LU/H06–10 reduce some mechanisms but do not
prove supply or outsider access. Keep dwelling-linked controls, market review
with actual assistance/supply, and funded public rent insurance as strong rivals.
No new landlord licence, universal rent register, public guarantee of profit,
blanket price freeze or assumed new dwelling/source is selected. [Decision](../../2026-11-private-rents-tenure-and-housing-entry/decision-record.md)
retains the distributional disagreement; no actual law or superiority follows.
