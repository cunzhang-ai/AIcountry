# C03. Democratic government and the ability to remove it: Civic independence and concentrated power

*Part of [C03. Democratic government and the ability to remove it](../C03-democratic-government.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

Competition also depends on power outside government. A person must be able to join
an opposition movement, report abuse and seek office without a dominant employer,
landlord, lender or communications gatekeeper making ordinary life untenable. Law
protects lawful political association against coercive retaliation while preserving
other people's expression, property, contractual rights and fair adjudication of
genuine misconduct. The country therefore treats political finance, media reach,
work and housing dependence, payment access and digital gatekeeping as one cumulative
democratic-control question, not separate sector statistics.

Provide neutral, law-defined public campaign support and usable opposition access;
disclose significant funding, coordinated spending and the effective controllers of
political advertisers, with safeguards for private individuals at risk. Electoral
administrators investigate unequal treatment under reviewable rules. They cannot
decide which lawful political opinion may be expressed. A challenger needs a timely
remedy that restores an actual opportunity to compete, rather than damages awarded
after an election. Exact finance limits, eligibility and privacy protections require
lawful adoption in a real jurisdiction; the [democratic candidate](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-09-democratic-control/democratic-constitution.md)
supplies a starting specification, not proof of equal competition.
The dedicated [political-finance route](07-political-finance-and-campaign-access.md)
assigns baseline access for new contestants, timely disclosure of organized
support and a pre-vote remedy against incumbent use of state resources,
while preserving small-donor privacy and independent speech.

Before a law, merger or major public contract materially changes control of civic
communication or essential economic gateways, the responsible elected and regulatory
bodies should publish a combined dependence assessment. Affected workers, residents,
users and political challengers can contest the finding before an independent court
under deadlines suited to the harm. Review must examine shared owners, funders and
operators, as well as whether a lawful alternative can actually take over. It cannot
turn a regulator's political preference into a veto over ordinary competition or
speech; its scope and remedies require prior legislation and their own review.
The [MP competition statute](../C05/11-competition-and-economic-power.md) supplies economic predicates and finite merger/court routes, not an extra civic-political veto. The [C05 market-power authority](../C05/05-market-power-and-real-exit.md)
examines the economic case; electoral bodies and courts retain their distinct
roles in protecting a challenger's practical political access.

Agent-rich sponsors cannot buy formal public attention merely by multiplying
messages. The [C12 attention rule](../C12/01-plural-communication-and-mediation.md)
requires review of distinct reasons, affected scope and shared sponsorship,
with a challenge when a mediator suppresses a material objection. Lawful
outside speech stays free; a public office's response allocation cannot
exclude a poor or unassisted opponent from a usable agenda route.

The Assembly's enacted vote, the exact published statute and its commencement are
separate events. A publication clerk authenticates the record and can be challenged
for withholding it; neither that clerk nor an AI assurance gate decides policy.
Courts can annul unlawful action and enforce rights but cannot legislate a replacement
tax, appoint themselves, or decide an election from a missed administrative deadline.
The earlier proposed [authority chain](../../AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION.md) names proposers,
lawmakers, delivery authorities, treasury, reviewers and remedy executors.

[AX public dissent](18-peaceful-assembly-and-public-dissent.md) now supplies
ordinary non-electoral assembly/public-space rules: voluntary facilitation,
spontaneous/leaderless participation, practical forum access, prior-court material
planned restrictions and narrow actual on-scene authority. Existing PC/CF
regulated paid electoral acts remain distinct; ordinary peaceful issue advocacy
and constitutional criticism gain no permission or truth-approval requirement.

[CI political freedom and livelihoods](04/01-political-freedom-and-livelihood-protection.md)
implements a specific civil wrong, protected entry and timely delivery for
coercion through work, housing, payment or essential services. It compares
credible emergency relief with standing independent access and structural
separation. The scope reaches public and private controllers on the same
terms, while leaving voting, safety and liability with their lawful owners.
