# Design476 continuing commitments

Carry accepted local and cross-local services on their original dates
and sources. [Portable receiving](../portable-services-and-host-capacity/01-pay-for-receiving-before-visits.md)
charges staffed availability and actual visits separately. Protect
earlier residents, payment and ordinary review through delayed
reimbursement or exceptional expiry. No service claim changes a
resident's status, electoral standing or ordinary local authority.
