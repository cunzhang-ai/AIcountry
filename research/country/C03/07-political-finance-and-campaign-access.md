# C03. Political finance and a contest people can actually enter

*Part of [C03 democratic government](../C03-democratic-government.md); integrated design 101 research, not enacted law.*

Equal votes are weak if a governing party can use public money, jobs, data
and distribution channels to drown out or punish challengers. This design
protects lawful political speech and association while making material
campaign resources, sponsorship and misuse contestable before an election
is over. No office may decide which platform is politically true or use
finance enforcement to remove an opponent without law and independent
review. Electoral, fiscal, media and market powers remain distinct.

## A usable entry route before the vote

The Assembly enacts prospective political-finance and campaign-access law
before nominations: definitions of candidate, party, civic list,
coordinated third-party spending and in-kind value; public-support
eligibility; permissible sources and limits; reporting; enforcement;
privacy; and remedies. Changes that would favor current officeholders
cannot take effect for their own contest through a late amendment or
emergency order. A qualifying contestant gets a modest equal baseline of
usable public campaign access before voting, including an accessible
official voter guide, reasonable public venues, lawful postal or offline
reach and a proportionate public funding route. Qualification uses
objective, reviewable filing and genuine support evidence rather than
incumbent consent or seats won at the last election. The [EN/CF/ER candidate](10-democratic-entry-and-campaign-resources.md) now selects
explicit thresholds, common qualification and paid access/credit/limit formulas;
a legitimate public still must choose/adopt a costed complete ballot package.
Referendum and territorial-status campaigns need parallel prospective
rules for lawful committees, public information, material sponsorship,
state-resource misuse and timely remedy. An incumbent cannot make the
official view the only funded or distributed view; the law must protect
direct citizen advocacy without pretending every referendum has parties
or candidate seats.

Additional support may be allocated by a capped, published voter-support
formula that leaves genuine new parties, civic lists and independents
viable. Treasury pays under enacted formula; the election office verifies
ballot qualification, not ideological merit. The formula, transfer dates,
appeal and fallback must be known before campaign costs are incurred.
Missing cash or a withheld venue is a remediable loss of opportunity, not
something a post-election report can declare equal. Public funds cannot
buy private loyalty or replace the independent operation of opposition
parties and media. A legally authorized unpaid volunteer remains free to
speak without becoming a state-registered campaign supplier.

Private financial and in-kind support remains possible within enacted,
equal rules that cover beneficial controllers, loans, discounts, vendor
services and materially coordinated spending. Major donors, third-party
campaigners and paid political advertising disclose effective sponsorship
and value in time for voters and rivals to use the information. Small
individual supporters and people at risk retain privacy through a
proportionate public threshold and protected confidential audit trail;
ballot secrecy and ordinary anonymous political expression remain intact.
Law may restrict foreign state finance, use of public bodies' funds and
high-risk contractor contributions through specific, reviewable grounds,
without treating every cross-border opinion or association as unlawful.
A sponsor cannot bypass a limit by splitting payments among controlled
entities or copied agents; shared control is proved, not presumed from
similar views.

## Separate public office from party advantage

Public vehicles, civil-service time, procurement, benefit lists, school or
hospital channels, official data and public media cannot be converted into
party campaign resources. Necessary government information, disaster
warnings and lawful ministerial advocacy continue under published
boundaries and source records; labeling an ordinary service notice as
campaigning may itself become an incumbent's censorship tactic. Career
workers need safe refusal and reporting routes. The public office records
official and campaign expenditure separately, with independent access to
contracts, payroll, advertising purchases and beneficial controllers.
Appointments, grants, land, licenses or state advertising cannot be
offered or withheld to buy campaign support. C04 procurement, C05 market
power and C08 official-abuse remedies retain their own legal cases.

Paid political messages in dominant media and digital channels disclose
their sponsor, price or material value, audience-selection criteria and
distribution period in a timely, searchable archive with privacy limits.
Public media have protected editorial independence, fair legally defined
campaign opportunities and a complaint route; a platform does not gain a
license to rank lawful opposition out of reach by secret party agreement.
Unpaid journalism, satire and personal speech are not campaign purchases.
Materially deceptive impersonation can receive a narrow, evidence-based
correction or legal remedy without an official truth test for disputed
policy claims. [ER21–ER25](10/10-electoral-instructions-and-timely-correction.md)
scopes timely relief to material risks to voting access. AI agents can produce arguments, but copies neither create
many distinct donors nor multiply a principal's legal campaign mandate.

## Inspect and repair without a finance police veto

An electoral finance office receives reports and audits material flows
under an enacted mandate, published selection criteria and protected
source access. Its appointment, funding and data providers cannot be
controlled solely by the governing party; investigators disclose
conflicts and keep a source trail. It can require records and refer a
reasoned case, but serious penalties, disqualification and an election
result's validity go to an independent court with defense rights and
review. The ballot administrator, finance investigator, public-media
governor and court are distinct; multiple offices using one vendor or
party patron do not make an independent chain.

Opponents, voters, candidates, journalists and workers have a usable
route to report misuse and obtain protected evidence. Before polling, an
urgent court can order lawful access, preservation, correction, return of
misused resources or a proportionate stop to proven conduct where delay
would destroy a fair chance to compete. It cannot appoint a favored
candidate or cancel speech merely because an incumbent or regulator calls
it misinformation. After polling, any fine, restitution, seat or contest
remedy follows enacted elements, proof, causal relevance and affected
voters' rights; a technical reporting mistake does not automatically
discard valid ballots. Unresolved wrongdoing remains visible through
handover, with protected records and enforcement officers.

C14's public-conditions account reports candidate and voter access,
money and in-kind support, concentration of sponsors and distributors,
late disclosures, state-resource misuse, timely orders and actual
compliance. A clean filing database is not proof of practical opposition.
This is a research route with no electorate, media market, campaign cost,
staff, law or observed contest. Reopen it if public support entrenches old
parties, disclosure endangers vulnerable speakers, enforcement targets
challengers, or a rival package better protects expression and replacement.

[EA13–EA16](09/04-funding-finance-and-review.md) now assigns the finance head and staff through protected career selection, separate case allocation, bounded evidence and direct ordinary courts. It supplies no office-level opponent ban; [EN/CF/ER](10-democratic-entry-and-campaign-resources.md) now provides the distinct national Assembly entry/support candidate.

[Candidate E16](../C06/19/04-remedy-and-political-continuity.md) adds a
five-day outer qualification-decision clock and 48-hour baseline-support
clock after qualification, both shortened when an effective opportunity
would otherwise disappear. The nomination calendar, formula and remedy
must fit together before a contest; silence grants neither qualification
nor a winner. For new national contests within [EN10/ER01/CF02](10-democratic-entry-and-campaign-resources.md), explicit prospective common-close qualification and staged guide/forum duties replace this rolling clock; V is first voting and P final close. Old-called contests retain source law. All remain research hypotheses, not live election law.


[PC01–PC24 continuous finance](12-continuous-political-finance.md) now supplies
rolling sources/persuasion, annual organizing credits, actual-local/ref support,
AI/service attribution and express restrained enforcement. Ordinary policy
advocacy/unpaid speech stays distinct, and investigators cannot freeze due access.
Its new public duties and VD12 scope require coordinated prospective source law;
no earlier called contest, actual claim, appointment or contribution permission
is amended by this research. Whole-country funding and actual public control
remain unqualified.
