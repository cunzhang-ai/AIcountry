# NI13–NI16. Pay for public origination and keep its powers bounded

*Part of [national initiatives](../16-public-national-initiatives.md).*

**NI13 — Issue advocacy remains distinct from an electoral campaign.** NI support
collection and parliamentary-policy advocacy do not themselves become PC01
'electoral persuasion' or an identified public ballot. Retain ordinary issue
advocacy's actual PC01/16 sponsor/value disclosure and PC02–04 actual controller,
paid AI/service value and public-resource boundaries. If an act actually makes
a covered electoral appeal, its existing PC/EN limits also apply on proved facts;
a petition label cannot hide it. NI creates no new private-donation/spend cap,
party credit, ballot-side subsidy or imputed fee for ordinary unpaid speech.

Ordinary discussion, lawful independent media and people declining support remain
free. No benefit/school/job is conditioned on endorsement. Required material
sponsor/controller disclosures and actual purpose accounts retain protected
small-member/supporter identity. Wealth can still purchase issue reach; disclose
that unresolved risk against broader initiative-specific limits, with ordinary
courts/actual source law, not an election regulator's general permission for dissent.

**NI14 — An actual parliamentary and source workload.** Assembly's own career
administration funds intake, drafting/cost assistance, protected agenda, public
information, qualified witnesses, translation, participant/care/access support,
hearings/votes and follow-through. EA's new career verification/custody, actual
independent courts/aid/execution and paid worker/source/record successors need
express appropriations/source under DB/NP/RF/OP-T, with proper independent access.
No new initiative office, general guardian, automatic cash grant or free expert.

C03's forward docket counts actual overlapping initiatives, bills, budgets,
consultations, urgent rights, elections and common-source personnel. Same experts,
records and supported travel count once, with conflicting judgments distinct.
Do not price every AI copy as a new person or assume common genuine claims cost
nothing. Overload needs public funded/elected repair and actual missed-duty relief;
it neither authorizes executive approval nor silently retires qualified initiatives.
Original37functions/old political bids price no complete NI increment; census is
open and full national public/private/foreign/legacy/transition source is unknown.

member/opposition/panel origination can use paid expertise and limit extra
parliamentary load but keeps an intermediary agenda gate. NI lowers that gate
while exposing sponsor capture, paid reach and repeated/overlapping work. A
binding statutory popular initiative/referendum gives more direct final control
but needs separately founded authority, actual electorate/choice/source/minority
and succession protections. Broader civic assemblies or affected nonvoter
qualification offer different standing/capacity choices, not added mandates here.
