# Essential services and free refusal

Design323 · Scoped result for [country completion](../../DESIGN-COMPLETENESS.md)

[FRE01–FRE05](../../country/C04/07/05-essential-services-and-free-refusal.md)
protects independently due benefits and services from leverage over
optional consent, institutional participation or provider choice.
Require service-specific grounds, funded independent execution and
practical relief when shared control blocks access.

The [matched comparison](../../settlement/essential-services-and-free-refusal/01-integrated-provider-and-free-exit.md)
checks deadlines, actual alternate control and gross cost including binding
prior claims. Actual enforceability, independent delivery, source and
effects remain open.
