# Industrial closure, contamination and funded restoration

Design221 · CLN01–CLN14 · Proposed country rules

A covered industrial site must remain safe through shutdown, sale
or insolvency. Protect exposed people when the hazard occurs. Allocate
the cost through enforceable security, a disclosed prospective site
charge and a separately funded residual route.

| Part | Country choice |
|---|---|
| [CLN01–CLN04. Cover the risk and protect people](21/01-assurance-transfer-and-immediate-protection.md) | Defined activities, collectible assurance, safe transfer and actual public receiving |
| [CLN05–CLN08. Allocate failure costs and verify closure](21/02-insolvency-residual-source-and-closure.md) | Narrow estate priority, residual risk finance, independent acceptance and paid transition |
| [CLN09–CLN14. Legacy contamination and long-term protection](21/03-legacy-contamination-and-long-term-protection.md) | Assisted reporting, distinct budget, affected-area protection and paid monitoring |
