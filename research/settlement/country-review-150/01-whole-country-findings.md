## Priorities and what completing each would establish

1. **Positive shared-operating gap, repaired now:** DP actual source role, lawful
   useful automation, joint dependency/capacity, material incident report,
   noncommand coordinator, sector stop/restore and due-right receipt. This matters
   across democracy, care, finance and AI, not merely one technical office.
2. **Current whole-country feasibility gap, next:** compare one published complete
   reference account with explicit candidate changes, quantities/coverage/source
   ranges and resource/transition losses. Reconcile current taxes/services/Y,
   source owners, physical labor/capital/FX, access and oversight once. A plausible
   context/forecast can be hypothetical; transferred foreign GDP/tax is not
   candidate revenue or actual foreign consent. Do not invent residual buyers,
   profits, available staff or exact prices to make a plan pass.
3. **Remaining authority/actual-context/observed-result gaps:** keep legitimate
   public, jurisdiction, source rights, independent affected judgment, real
   accepted workers/records/receivers and better-life observations separately
   unverified. They matter for actual implementation/outcomes, not a demand that
   a hypothetical design already be an enacted state.
4. **Value and structural rivals:** retain service assessment/U, private/public/
   cooperative supply, owner/resident control, privacy/learning/failure tradeoffs
   and simpler/decentralized digital operation. New evidence can displace the
   country architecture; more detail is not itself a reason to retain it.

No closed census of all future functions, missing major annexes or all legal
conflicts is claimed. Qualified original comparisons, rejection cases and
feasible quantitative boundaries are still needed for the user goal; its
completion remains unproved. Actual urgent harm/larger contradictions can
reprioritize without waiting for another research edition.
