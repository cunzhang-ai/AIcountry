# Choose who decides a recurring cross-local allocation

*Design481 · Constructed comparison of national, joint and regional authority.*

The [catchment case](../cross-local-effects/01-three-councils-and-one-catchment.md)
tests compatible water allocations among North, Central and South.
Use those same three affected places and the same protected four-unit
ecological flow. No arrangement changes existing allocations or
creates water. Add two recurring material decisions about prospective
allocations that need action on the same calendar. One concerns
a use whose effects are inside the three-place area. The other also
affects a fourth area outside it. Existing rights and lawful urgent
service continue on the same funded terms in every arrangement.

## Compare authority and time together

Each decision needs one complete block of hearing, elected
deliberation and a recorded vote before its original deadline.
The baseline ordinary national docket leaves one usable national
block in that window after protected business. In this exercise the
Assembly assigns it to the outward-facing decision under the
applicable lawful calendar. Hearings and transfer of independent
evidence are in the priced blocks where stated. The assumed workload,
demand and budget units are authored.

| Arrangement | Operating implication |
|---|---|
| Local origin decisions with national residual | Preserve each council's local powers. Both material decisions reach the national scope on the stated facts. One original opportunity is missed under the assumed peak. Buying a second national block is a real rival if actual members, staff, independent review and notice fit. |
| Voluntary service compact | It could discharge existing funded operations. In this example at least one necessary council declines the exact new allocation mandate. The compact cannot impose that mandate, allocate a nonmember's rights or choose the outside case. The national workload remains two. |
| Directly elected, limited regional authority | If constituted by legitimate prior law, assign only the specified within-area decision to the region. Give it a separate staffed block and independently funded review. The outside case stays with the national route. The region gains no constitutional status, general taxing power or authority over the fourth area. |

Assume the extra national block would cost two units per cycle
and that available recurring finance is two. With one additional
qualified national block, that route meets both deadlines and
avoids forming a new institution. It is the preferred repair while
qualified. In the recurring strong comparison, the required national
members are unavailable in that window even with money. A limited
region has a separately eligible roster, financed public access
and independent review costing two per cycle. Before it can operate,
a separately accepted one-time founding and overlap cost of three
is due. The proposed national budget has only two one-time units
available. Under that cap the regional option cannot start.
Neither a recurring surplus nor a nominal regional delegation
pays the missing first unit.

If a separate lawful founding source supplies the additional unit
and the protected election and hearing routes actually qualify,
the limited directly elected regional option can resolve the
recurring within-area gap. Select it prospectively on those scoped
assumptions. A feasible ordinary national repair or later compact
remains available for reconsideration. If the regional operation has
the same unavailable reviewer or staff as the national route, the
two blocks do not coexist. If every disputed decision crosses the
proposed boundary, the added body does not solve this bottleneck.
Keep affected nonresident users' direct hearing and review under
ordinary law. Where their protected consent is required it remains
separate.

While the decision is open, protect all earlier lawful uses and
remedies. A missed disposition is a reviewable delay. It confers no
new authority and cannot make an optional draw lawful. Preserve
the original docket, opposing proposals, funding evidence and
paid case receiving at any successor. The first vote that changes
regional competence must be a legitimate prospective choice.
An AI can compare scope, workload and timetables without deciding
the final jurisdiction, source, allocation or legal consent.
