# C05. Service-first personal security: a guarantee, not a predetermined cash destination

3 November 2026 · Design 136 core and Design450 receipt comparison · SG01–SG16 research selection, not enacted law

**Select an individual income guarantee, universal essential services and
continuing earned rights as the country's preferred material-security design.**
Retain I05's individual actual-resource formula and SD05's one personal tax
allowance. Remove the presumption that this architecture must eventually be
replaced by full universal ordinary cash. A rights-preserving U+C2 package
remains a substantive rival, open to future democratic selection.

This revises design 88/131/135's preferred *destination*, not people's rights,
the existing tax/benefit formula, pension qualifications or any actual law.
Ordinary survival is not conditional on employment, job search, marriage,
political agreement or AI use. Individual means assessment is nevertheless
a real intrusion and possible source of exclusion; it requires paid assistance,
safe evidence, timely payment, independent remedies and continuing challenge.
Neither the word “universal” nor the word “targeted” proves adequate receipt.

The [two-layer comparison](../../settlement/service-first-security/README.md)
uses published Finnish model-household work/childcare cases and separately
audits unchanged country A/U and plural/direct-provider examples. These are
different contexts and units, not a calibrated Finnish AIcountry. Foreign
benefit substitutions, family means and tax changes do not transfer here.
Existing older-person claims are held in the country comparison; no matched
observed lifetime/service-quality result or full country source is supplied.

The reason for the revision is a stated value choice under uncertainty:
prioritize actually met needs, useful services and political/personal agency
over a predetermined transfer form. Extra universal cash may be worth its
burden, but that is an open package choice rather than the project's assumed
finish line. The comparison supports rejecting shortcuts, not a claim that
I05 is empirically optimal, affordable, nonintrusive or immune to capture.

IC05–IC14 paid plural ownership/investment, IC04/IC15 transition protections,
IC17–IC20 cash-independent qualified credits, Y, TW, WL, H, S/MO, NS and the
37-function account continue. If U is already lawful or commenced, pay it
and preserve its arrears/source-period rights until lawfully changed. Research
reprioritization creates no recovery, canceled entitlement or Treasury veto.
Actual authority, source, qualified receiving and better lives remain unverified.
