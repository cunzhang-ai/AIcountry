# Design353 decision on sector floors and paid care continuity

## Baseline and authority

Pin Design352 and the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md). WL25 permits
voluntary sector agreements and reserves any binding extension to
competent law. Existing HS duties cover actual assistance and interim
receipt. The project assistant proposes the initial sector and a
two-year statutory schedule for comparison. The elected Assembly
chooses exact terms and dates under ordinary law. Private representatives
negotiate for their verified principals. The work inspector, care
commissioner and courts retain distinct powers. No actual sector was
consulted and no authority to start a schedule has been established.

## Alternatives and objections

Enforce general minima and enable voluntary agreements. This costs less to
start and preserves local control over terms; well-organized firms may
still face competition from lower pay or unpaid travel. A statutory
minimum offers equal protection across comparable work, with lawful
local bargaining above it. It may raise private prices or make a thin
route unviable, especially if public purchasing remains on old rates.
Direct public provision can sustain an isolated route where providers
leave; it requires an accepting team, sources and continued user choice.
Use an actual market and dependence account before choosing a rate.

Choose the legislation-and-funding route as the design candidate.
Defer a statutory start if the matched source, labor and backup are
missing. No incumbents' accord creates outsider consent. A worker with
no bargaining representative has an independent hearing and remedy.
Protect private worker and recipient evidence. Record separate bargaining
mandates, direct affected submissions and actual published reasons.
Legislative review can revise or end the schedule prospectively. Fund
the successor procedures, open cases and care throughout the transition.

## Evidence, result and next decision

The [sources](sources.md) support the institutional distinctions and a
real succession failure. The [authored case](../settlement/home-care-wage-and-service-compact/01-payroll-and-visits.md)
checks payroll liability, funded coverage and continued care after late
cash. It establishes no price elasticity, improved retention, legal
adoption, operational readiness or better lives.

The next bounded real comparison needs a willing care commissioner and
independent worker and service-user participation. Compare one payroll
and care roster over the same first due-pay window, including a small
or remote route and one direct-hire arrangement. Collect verified covered
hours, pay, qualified replacements and actual available funds within the
commissioner's authorized budget. Stop before any operative change.
Reopen the design if price/route loss dominates gains, ordinary enforcement
can deliver the same improvement, or independent hearing and relief fail.
