# XR01–XR05. From a protected request to real service

*Part of [cross-local service continuity](../09-cross-local-service-continuity.md).*

**XR01 — Preserve standing across a domestic boundary.** Apply this
route where a planned move, temporary stay, sudden displacement or
disputed service area materially affects a common service. Existing
lawful decisions, due claims, worker and provider commitments and
independent challenges keep their applicable scope. Assess the actual
period and need. Temporary presence can trigger interim receiving
without deciding citizenship, ordinary residence, voting, tax,
immigration or permanent assignment. Optional local benefits and
foreign provision have their own law. An office cannot classify
people as moved to discard their claims or invoke residence to
manufacture a second claim for the same episode.

**XR02 — Open a route at either competent door.** Preserve the earliest
lawful contact, including a request by an authorized supporter and
evidence of attempted contact under AP. For a planned move, contact
the receiving authority and seek an actual arrangement before arrival
or the first due service. Help the person know the choices, named
receivers and remaining risks. There is no requirement to wait for
public approval to move. In a sudden or unsafe move, start competent
interim action as soon as the need is known through a lawful route.
Do not require a prior notice period, new address certificate, police
report, original provider's consent or agreement between councils
before the applicable protection. Assess relevant facts and legitimate
safety and entitlement limits without restarting an existing clock.

**XR03 — Separate the host's action and the origin's continuing duty.**
The first receiver preserves contact, deadline and routing until the
competent sector accepts. The origin retains its actual ongoing
obligation, lawful commitments and paid interim responsibility until
its applicable duty lawfully ends or the funded successor actually
receives it. Where local action is needed at the destination, the
competent host must arrange available lawful interim receiving or
activate the named sector fallback within the applicable useful time.
An origin worker may deliver by accepted arrangement. The host retains
the interim duty within actual source law. This is also the entry
where no origin or ordinary residence is established. A host may
report missing capacity and seek funded escalation. It cannot record
performance from an unanswered referral or direct the person to return
to an unsafe place. The national sector remains responsible for its
common floor and authorized fallback.

**XR04 — Keep the actual service and the recipient's choice in view.**
For ongoing assistance, use the current lawful needs and support plan
as provisional evidence, with prompt competent review of change.
Keep covered tasks, equipment and paid worker commitments with the
right person. A plan does not replace consent, a qualified shift or
equipment fit. Clinicians secure safe medication, results and follow-up
under HN and S/E. Housing and survivor teams arrange an accessible
safe interim place without requiring disclosure of its address to an
unsafe person or a transfer of tenancy. Keep prior housing, clinical,
income and safeguarding claims on their own terms.

For a displaced child within S05, protect the established school
where it is safe, chosen and usable with actual transport and support.
Otherwise the competent receiving school authority arranges a safe
provisional school place or equivalent lawful teaching with access
support from the first scheduled school day following protected
receipt, or sooner where existing duties require. A missing address
or record alone cannot prevent provisional receiving. Use appropriate
safeguarding, the child's supported voice and genuine receipt.
Provisional participation does not decide custody, force a school
transfer, override safety restrictions or close the independent
review. An unserved school day remains a failed duty.

**XR05 — Transfer only the duty actually accepted.** Confirm scope, time,
qualified provider, actual funded access, consent where required,
safe travel or transport and the fallback. Distinguish administrative
assignment, provider acceptance, usable access and first actual receipt.
The new assignment does not settle arrears or discharge a missed
episode. Where the law makes the host the responsible authority on
arrival, its duties begin then; the origin's remaining duties and
any paid overlap keep their source-law scope. Keep usable reasons,
the next date and a direct challenge. For a short stay or a return,
use lawful continuity without a compulsory permanent move.

[FR09–FR13](../../C09/07/03-community-relocation-and-host-capacity.md)
governs an optional joint recovery project. A receiving project's
approval is separate from the original XR obligation to accept eligible
urgent or continuing duties. Do not charge a shared project twice or
make an individual XR route depend on group participation.

[ED34–ED38](../../C07/21/08-learning-support-before-assessment.md)
keeps comparable safe learning support from the first affected teaching
opportunity and carries the original assessment date. Record the
accepting school and actual qualified receiving.
