# What implementation failures and disclosure rules establish

## An available total can be the wrong fact

Services Australia describes Robodebts raised from averaged
tax-office income information in its 2015–2019 Income Compliance
Program. The Australian Royal Commission's 2023 report recommended
a clear review path, plain-language information and independent
scrutiny of business rules and algorithms where automated
decision-making is used. Its further recommendation discussed
technical audit, fairness and client usability. The commission
and the agency describe the same program and are not independent
replications. The facts support testing lawful time and evidence
semantics and follow-up to actual receipt. They do not establish
which general software or decision structure is best.
[Services Australia description](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/information-about-robodebt?context=60271).
[Royal Commission report, Chapter 17 recommendations](https://robodebt.royalcommission.gov.au/publications/report).

## An explanation can describe the actual use

The French Code of Relations between the Public and Administration,
Article L311-3-1, requires notice for covered individual decisions
based on algorithmic processing and provides information on request,
subject to its legal limits. Article R311-3-1-2 describes the
contribution, data and sources, parameters applied and operations
among the information that can be made available. These are
specific disclosure provisions. They do not demonstrate that
recipients can successfully challenge a decision or are paid
a correction. LX joins information to independent review and
actual remedies.
[Article L311-3-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000033205535/2024-07-02).
[Article R311-3-1-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000034195881/2023-06-30).

## A common machine-readable rule is a design choice

The OECD's 2020 *Cracking the Code* paper presents official
machine-consumable rules as a possible approach and examines
limitations. A shared representation could make comparison and
implementation easier. It could also make one mistaken reading
common to several services. LX selects authenticated law, public
verification and replaceable implementations. That is a design
judgment. The OECD paper supplies no performance finding about
the proposed country.
[OECD working paper](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/cracking-the-code_3afe6ba5-en.html).
