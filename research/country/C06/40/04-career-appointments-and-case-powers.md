# RG01–RG05: elected policy and protected professional careers

*Part of [C06/40](../40-tax-law-and-accountable-revenue.md); design100 research candidate, not enacted law.*

**RG01 — Public tax law, lawful elected priorities.** Assembly alone sets national taxable events, base, nexus, rates, thresholds/reliefs, withholding/discharge, information powers, penalties, interest, limitation and effective dates; elected councils vary only lawfully delegated local rates. PA10's chosen schedule and legal M/ordinary-U treatment remain their existing hypotheses and commencement rules. Cabinet/minister may propose law, set published lawful prospective service/audit priorities and obtain aggregate performance explanations, not direct a named person's audit, assessment, review finding, settlement, collection or refund. No minister, officer, EV statistic, AI model, donor, tax software or platform account owns tax truth. Statutory tax rules stay exact/public; technical forms/guidance and changing W/CPI evidence cannot silently revise rates, M, eligibility or source-period law. General reorganization requires actual competent law, published reasons and protected staff/pending-claim succession, never abolition of review or a transfer to punish one case.

**RG02 — Two open career appointments, not an insulated tax government.** Cabinet separately appoints a Revenue Director and Tax Merits Review Head by reasoned decision from C04 open, accessible, job-related competition and a published qualified public shortlist with conflicts, usable objections and direct ordinary review. Existing public civil-service recruitment staff run the competitions with disclosed qualified advice; the director neither owns the review-head shortlist nor nominates/appoints their own reviewer. Require demonstrated fair tax administration/accounting/evidence competence for the director and tax-law/merits/fair-hearing competence for the review head, with protected-data, accountable management and supported language/access ability; equivalent relevant practical work may qualify, not a sole guild, proprietary credential, party loyalty or AI score. Current minister/legislator, party executive/campaign manager, tax-farm proprietor or paid representative of a materially conflicted taxpayer cannot simultaneously serve. Disclose actual material financial/family/sponsor/prior-case conflicts; use recusal/divestment and direct court review, not ideology tests or automatic incapacity by allegation.

Both are protected professional **career posts under C04**, with no new fixed political/supermajority term, automatic expiration at election or self-perpetuating board. Tenure ends by actual lawful resignation/retirement/reorganization/removal under governing employment law, not Cabinet pleasure; no extra retirement age is invented here. Cabinet is the actual appointing/disciplinary owner for each: only proved misconduct, persistent material job-related/statutory performance failure or inability after reasonable accommodation, usable notice/reply, reasoned decision and ordinary-court interim/appeal protection. Honest reversal, lawful dissent, lost revenue forecast, an inconvenient refund or a changed government is no ground. A minister cannot personally purge/reassign either head for a named case. Lawful prospective elected policy and actual professional discipline remain possible; careers gain no policy veto or immunity from proved failure. B16's ordinary60working-day appointment/exclusion/discipline challenge runs from usable notice/publication with blocked-access protection, and B17 urgent relief is earlier.

**RG03 — Divide employment power and case powers.** Revenue Director appoints through C04 the operational heads/staff for assistance/records, investigation/assessment, collection and refund authorization; director is their protected disciplinary owner, with proved grounds/notice/reply/direct ordinary review. Team heads recruit/assign/discipline their ordinary staff under the same framework and published qualification, rotation, conflict and exceptional-reassignment rules. Statute specifies which qualified officer signs an assessment, collection step, overpayment/refund determination or court application; no director's mandatory personal approval. A collection officer cannot redecide assessed liability or reject a lawful stay, and a refund officer cannot relitigate a binding favorable review/court result. No person checks/executes their own disputed decision as if independent.

The separately appointed Review Head recruits/assigns/disciplines merits reviewers and their protected source/clerical support through C04; director, assessment manager and collection manager cannot remove/reassign them, choose a favorable reviewer or make their sources available only at their discretion. Cabinet's general employer role cannot be used to dictate one merits result. General necessary reassignment needs attributable lawful reasons, preserved dates/rights, no retaliation/pretext and direct review. A reviewer cannot have examined, investigated, assessed, collected or advised the disputed case, represent a materially interested person or choose themselves on a direct conflict. Predesignated qualified next-case substitutes follow prior rules; a head's own contested direct conflict/employment wrongdoing goes to unconflicted ordinary courts, not a handpicked loyal deputy. Hearing officers are statutory administrative reviewers, never self-appointed judges.

**RG04 — Vacancies preserve actual duties.** Each head appoints a qualified career deputy through open C04 competition before vacancy. Deputy handles at most180elapsed days of routine funded management, with no new material general policy, enlarged competence or discretionary senior appointments from day1, and no reset by acting title/absence relabelling. Thereafter no acting-head powers. Existing assessment, review, collection, refund, records and payer officers continue only their own enacted functions throughout; if no qualified unconflicted deputy exists, they do so immediately. Missing reserved management decisions are disclosed, not manufactured by office labels. No new major lawful case/merits correction/refund needs an absent head's permission; no employee acquires a missing legal power or political portfolio.

Cabinet must open an unexpected head competition within14calendar days; an actual planned departure is prepared180days beforehand where known. Failure does not appoint anyone or reset a taxpayer's clock. For first establishment the adopting Act expressly permits Cabinet, through existing C04 recruitment staff, to appoint funded qualified nonmanagerial statutory assessment, review, collection and refund officers before head entry, with published roles/reasons/conflicts and direct challenge. This cannot bypass either head's open professional selection, grant case powers beyond law, appoint a judge or let one examiner review their own case. Actual appointers, qualified willing people and source funds are unverified; absent staff means unmet duties/direct courts and retained claims, not fictitious continuity.

**RG05 — Office-only compatibility.** This ordinary career revenue model expressly completes/replaces any unspecified revenue-authority leadership description and any V10 generic staggered-board implication for **this revenue service only**. It leaves PD/MA/RA/EA panels/chiefs, monetary/prudential roles and CJ jurisdictions unchanged. Neither a Cabinet appointment, tax official nor procedure approves its own legal foundation. Actual prior appointments/employment/claims persist until their compatible competent transition, not a research-text purge. No inferred parliamentary/O approval, paid tax farm or special judicial formation is introduced.
