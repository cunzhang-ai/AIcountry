# PA13–PA16: received resources, residual needs and useful-time correction

*Part of [C06/38](../38-work-pay-prices-and-country-income.md); design 93 candidate.*

**PA13 — Recompute the residual; do not recycle a frozen award.** Let N retain
the personal basket, necessary housing/work/support costs and only matching
actually received in-kind deductions. Let U be safely received ordinary cash,
E net work/replacement, O other actually received resources after lawful net-
pension disregard, and A the protected actual asset draw. Apply
`R = max(0, N − U − O − A − 0.5×max(0,E−d))`, with d=20% of the age-appropriate
personal basket. U does not also enter O. Recognized access compensation remains
separate, exempt and matched to its own cost. Do not tax R or feed it back to
erase its award. Credits, gifts, support, ordinary services and reimbursements
retain their actual meanings and I03–I06 once-only matching.

U and Y basic supply the same ordinary component once; earned/care/survivor
payments and source indexation remain distinct. A pension's new tax treatment
cannot recap past units or silently erase a protected net claim. Changes to tax,
prices or receipt require fresh calculation, not `old I05 − U` copied from a
different scenario. Shared residence still proves neither received support nor
consent. A price reference cannot become a hidden housing/benefit cap.

**PA14 — Show the whole budget constraint.** Publish gross wages, employee/
employer costs, contribution, tax, received U, residual/access payments, rent,
actual work/care/travel expense, services, product tax and prices. For work-income-only receipts with fixed
facts,10% Y,55% ordinary marginal tax and active 50% residual withdrawal, a worked
gross wage unit leaves 20.25%, not 50%. That is a highest-band formula example,
not a real work-incentive guarantee or maximum retention. Replacement cash,
nonwork income, changed needs, costs, VAT, eligibility and producer responses
can differ. Neither low earners nor employers gain by a nominal relabeling alone.

**PA15 — Pay and collect through different accountable duties.** Existing
ordinary-payment authority pays on I07's advance calendar, without tax-return,
employer or AI-account permission. Revenue administers provisional withholding
and one annual assessment under the taxpayer charter; benefit officers determine
actual residual needs. Protected source certificates, assisted/offline access,
funded independent adjudication and ordinary courts connect them without a
common private-life dossier. No final automatic denial or partner liability.
Publish safe aggregate incidence and opposition/audit access; protect testimony.

**PA16 — Late cash is not a resource or a second tax opportunity.** Foundation
arrears retain one exclusive source-period exclusion; they do not shield unrelated
wages during nonpayment or become taxable year-bunched ordinary earnings later.
If interim R already paid that original component, reconcile only the proved
same-person/period overlap once under IC04, with notice, review and subsistence
protection. Past arrears do not automatically cancel the current floor; unpaid
original U/R is never deemed received. Tax refunds need matching to their source
and actual receipt, not repeated I05 credits or phantom cash. Good-faith correction
and recovery retain I08 protections. A projected refund cannot replace urgent
provisional protection or excuse the public payer's missed due date.

Design 131 [PW01–PW04](../53/01-foundation-tax-and-work.md) replaces preferred
conditional C1 with C2's untapered allowance. PA14's20.25% highest-band active-
I05 example remains possible. C1's 63% personal marginal rate/16.65% gross-work
retention and8,000 earlier fixed-case individual losses versus A stay scoped
comparators. No child gain cancels an individual's due right. Actual net/legacy
continuity and price/financing effects need their own source/receipt review.
