# Public conditions: comparison, control and response

*Part of [C14 public conditions](../04-public-conditions.md); prior duties plus scoped design85 outcome corrections.*
The Assembly enacts a bounded core publication duty and funds it. C11's public
evidence service produces reproducible statistics from original custodians,
with protected methods, privacy and source access. It does not declare which
society is best. A plural evaluation process, including independent outside
inquiry and affected people's accounts, tests rival explanations; it is not
controlled by the government being assessed or by one chosen AI model.
Official data, causal interpretation, individual legal claims and elected
policy choice remain different acts. Opposition and local bodies have timely
lawful access to the same nonsecret evidence and a route to challenge
unjustified withholding. Source records and correction history survive a
minister, vendor or administration change.

Each term begins with published definitions, coverage, proposed comparator
jurisdictions and reasons for choosing them. A comparison distinguishes
within-country change, differences from other countries, and a justified
claim about what a policy caused. It shows relevant differences in population,
law, geography, starting resources and shocks; where comparability or causal
inference fails, it says so rather than awarding a rank. Material method and
population-boundary changes retain the old series and an explained bridge.
No ministry may improve its reported result by dropping an unfunded duty,
excluded group, death, emigrant, refused applicant or externally imposed harm.

The account shows distributions and lived paths, not only an average: people
with low income, care needs, disabilities, language barriers, distinct local
conditions and contested political positions may experience a gain or loss
different from the national mean. Disaggregation is limited where it would
identify or endanger people. Confidential testimony can challenge an official
category without becoming public property. New communication methods and
community questions can be added without letting a mediator silently change
the meaning of an old commitment. AI may translate, link sources and model
scenarios, but its multiple outputs sharing one source are one evidence family.
For shared civic places, report reachable access, opening hours, charges,
maintenance, exclusions and voluntary accounts separately from subjective
connection. A new venue or rising participation count is not proof that
friendship improved, and nobody is obliged to supply relationship data.
For interpersonal safety, compare private access, accepted protection,
order execution, repeat harm and wrongful restriction without exposing
survivors, children, respondents or safe locations. A change in reporting
may reflect trust or fear rather than a change in violence.
For equal standing, compare rejected applicants and nonusers, timely
accommodation and executed repair alongside provider constraints and
wrongful findings; demographic parity alone proves neither fairness nor bias.

## A response that does not turn a score into government

The Assembly holds an opposition-accessible hearing on the account before the
electoral choice, including contested omissions and resource costs. Cabinet
states which findings it accepts, disputes or cannot resolve and proposes a
funded response; opposition, local bodies and affected people can put
alternatives on record. The Assembly gives reasons for retain, repair, trial,
replace or no change under existing rights, law and budget powers. A missed
publication or a favorable score neither extends a cabinet's term nor enacts
an appropriation. Courts can protect original evidence, participation and
legal claims; they do not choose a national wellbeing score or write the
replacement policy. Urgent harm and individual remedies are acted on when
known, not held until the next election report.

Success is not a single index. A better average cannot compensate for loss of
effective opposition, arbitrary detention, destroyed ecological options or
unremedied serious harm. A separate failure in any dimension remains visible
even when another dimension improves. The same C06 account funds the staff,
survey burden, original field observations, privacy protection, outside
scrutiny and remedy execution. Public reporting without capacity to correct
a discovered failure is not delivery.

Design 85's [CS comparison](../../C01/03-country-case-and-competing-designs.md)
requires actual audit findings' reach, safe challenge, corrective action and
received remedy beside audit cost/displacement/substitution. Incumbent defeat
is no mandatory success measure. For I/S/Y, distinguish nonclaimants/nonusers,
refusal, proof/access failure and unknown need; more take-up can miss the neediest.
For care/learning, report child/recipient health/development/agency/relationships,
worker conditions and retained/transfer capability separately from attendance,
parental employment and AI-assisted output. Use existing bounded publication/
privacy duties, not an extra national scoring office or compelled testimony.

[PCO01–PCO05](03-comparable-outcomes-and-hidden-regression.md) compares
coverage, fixed composition and missing outcomes separately. A qualified
serious finding has a protected elected response between ordinary reports.
Earlier legal duties and individual remedies continue.
