# Document organization and preserved predecessors

5 October 2026 · Navigation history; no new country-design selection

The current research has one integrated national entry, a status register and
fourteen stable C01–C14 chapter entrances. Topic files under each C-number
hold the detailed current design. Cross-chapter operating maps and the
functional audit help find dependencies; dated studies and predecessor
snapshots remain evidence and history, not rival live editions.

substantive selections and objections for each integrated edition are in the

method or give an AI system political authority.
