*Integrated design 81 · Consumer of [MB01–MB20](../C02/06-citizenship-and-secure-belonging.md); research, not enacted law.*

## Separate decisions, one received protection chain

The Assembly enacts entitlements, exact admission categories/capacity and bounded
funding. A merit-based nationality officer applies nationality law; separate
evidence assessment and final conferral prevent one unchecked signature pipeline.
Residence/protection officers decide their own lawful scopes, registrars record
legal events and electoral staff apply independent franchise predicates. No office
can erase an actual right by withholding the only document. Courts decide fact/
law and effective correction independently; police do not determine membership.

Attribute caseworkers, birth/foreign-law expertise, public advice, translators,
ordinary and appeal judges, paper/remote proof, protected testimony, actual places,
clinical/child receiving, transport and correction liabilities alongside existing
duties once. A person cannot occupy simultaneous nationality, independent appeal,
removal escort and ordinary liberty-review shifts. A border envelope, total staff
or a national fiscal residual is not qualified safe reception.

Treasury pays enacted dues through separate commitment, verification, source
custody and payment; actual receipt/continuing service is checked independently.
An E15 alternate executes exact existing due payment/record duties with usable
distinct credentials, not nationality, admission quotas or a general allocation.
All incremental quantity/cost/source, actual competent staffing, physical receiving,
foreign cooperation, safe travel and remedy receipt remain unknown. Preserve
old bid amounts/components and actual nulls; no blanket support transfer to MB.

The existing SF stock covers only its old declared protected-period scope.
Recompute any additional adopted dues and demonstrate an actual lawful source;
do not divert pension/client/insurance property or count standby twice. A new
citizen or work permit neither creates housing nor guarantees labor income.
Entry limits cannot extinguish protected/non-return and existing due obligations;
expose shortfalls and lawful elected capacity choices rather than abandon people.

habitual time, dues, pending removal/stays, source data, contracts and paid
successors. Exact adopted starts respect C14, CP cohorts and already due rights;
no agency's readiness certificate postpones an enacted protection indefinitely.

separates reference design, lawful authorization, qualified operation and outcomes.

actual later duties in the national basic/continuity and cohort schedule. No
new independent shift or payer source is supplied by an old payroll share.
The core is task-bounded, not every institutional invoice; existing rights,
professional safety and source-law payment priority remain distinct.
