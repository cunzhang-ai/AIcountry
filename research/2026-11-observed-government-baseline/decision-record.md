# Design213. Observe the country's starting accounts and correct fragile capacity

## Baseline and requested choice

Design212 is the pinned baseline. FB separates existing law and voted
funds from maintaining actual services. WF already has a 2023 reference
and an industry model. MF assigns actual payers and local duties. T
separates safe acceptance and legal dates. Select a more recent
observed reference with matched financial and public employment
perspectives and test the first-budget preference against known
failures of essential national functions.

## Evidence, rivals and selection

Select WF77–WF80. Use Denmark 2025 as a historical reference with
actual expenditure, transfers, employment indicators and final uses.
Keep full public purpose coverage and compare owner and job counts
within their defined scope. The transfer reconciliation closes to
source residuals and removes duplicated national totals, but cannot
supply a recipient's actual settlement. The fiscal and workforce
partitions cannot replace a roster. The original and amended central budget and the state outturn
and net cash bridge anchor a separate state perimeter. The
comparison still lacks the full enacted legal and dated paid
baseline for every function and payer.

The strong repair rival has an existing independent audit function,
regular publication and concrete corrections already reported.
The same-year custody and court findings show that reliable
financial accounts can coexist with deficiencies in practical
capacity. The later response partly changes the comparison and
must stay attached. Compare existing repair and negotiated staffing
with qualified alternate providers or recovery and paid overlap.
Select correcting the demonstrated dependency before adding new
reliance. Give the actual correction credit only for its qualified
scope. A broad replacement or reorganization is not justified from
these cases. Stronger shared preparedness might also be worthwhile
where it independently improves the real receiving path.

## Material objections and existing authority

Protect worker rights, custody safety, judicial independence and
ordinary review. Maintaining old operations may cost more than the
outturn suggests, but audit arithmetic cannot price that difference.
Uncritical exclusion of later lawful shifts can exaggerate a gap.
Conversely, relying on an approval without checking rest and actual
service can preserve harm. Preserve both source periods, qualify paid
shifts and safe restoration, and keep people and their original
remedies in view. Only enacted law and elected appropriations change
public powers. Actual sector authorities own recovery and staffing.
AI can compare authorized scenarios and trace missing dependencies
under DP without replacing judgment or independent evidence.

## Compatibility and continuation

Preserve FP's retirement of old full-country finance, WF's 2023
vintage, FB and MF sources, T commencement, corrections and court
authority. Actual participation, independent qualification,
enactment, readiness and observed improvement in the proposed
country remain unestablished. Next, a named owner would need to
price one disclosed repair using actual establishment and skill
coverage, accepted recovery or relief, lawful outlays by due date
and observed service. That requires paid review and authorized
data access. Reopen on a completed independent test, changed law,
withdrawn staff or funding, or an ordinary repair that meets the
same conditions with less burden.
