# Design335 decision on whole-council local recall

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design334, EL, PC, ER, local authority, RR and the working
framework. The country already provides regular local elections,
council replacement of its chair and independent remedies for
wrongdoing. Add a prospective popular route to end an ordinary
proportional council mandate once in a fixed midterm window. Choose
separate recall and conditional replacement ballots. The project
assistant authors the proposal. Existing legitimate institutions
must authorize it prospectively.

## Alternatives and evidence

Compare regular elections and confidence alone, individual recall,
and whole-council recall. Regular elections protect stability and
leave fewer remedies when the governing majority shields itself.
Individual recall may better target a grievance and can distort
the original proportional choice. The whole-council route risks
displacing capable opposition members and costs a second contest.
Select it with a common petition window, independently verified
support, a meaningful affirmative floor, and renewed proportional
choice for the original remainder. The floor can preserve an
incumbent despite a strong majority of votes cast. The thresholds,
timing and fiscal cost require actual review.

The [primary sources](sources.md) show distinct recall and replacement
mechanisms. They do not establish an appropriate threshold or the
case for a proportional local council in this candidate.

## Authority, migration and reopening

The prospective instrument defines the covered elected councils,
original term, window, eligible roll, votes, finance, review and
continuing status. Preserve ordinary elections, protected succession,
independent court jurisdiction and formal wrongdoing remedies.
Fund the legal call, accessible ballot, protected challenges and
staffed handover. Incumbents and their opponents retain equal
access and current lawful obligations. No replacement or renewed
term arises from a failed or unverifiable vote.

Test a real-sized mock council ballot with consenting local voters,
minority lists, original access needs and qualified independent
staff. Compare the fixed window and the existing stability baseline.
Rehearse delayed certification, simultaneous demand and failure
of one count. Reopen on intimidation, disproportionate minority loss,
excessive public cost or a stronger accountable alternative.
Actual legal authority, costs, access and outcomes remain open.
