# A first-budget choice with two currencies and two receiving dates

## Match the obligations and the bottlenecks

This authored case uses currency A, currency B and domestic budget
units with no assumed conversion. Each route is assessed against
the same due service. One accepted refrigerated medicine consignment
and four units of essential fuel-equivalent service are needed by
day 8. The fuel-equivalent requirement covers named water, clinical
and independent public sites within the fixture. Staff, storage,
quality, access and protected court and electoral alternatives
still require their own valid arrangements. There is also a binding
two-unit A debt payment on day 9. Its original legal source and
date continue in every option.

| Source, price or route | Timely capacity and restrictions |
|---|---|

| Regional facility | Up to four B for eligible fuel from the regional counterparty. The medicine seller accepts only A. Record the actual draw and its later repayment. |

| Regional fuel route | The partner can transport up to four. Seven fuel units are available and four are reserved for the partner's own verified floor. The present safe offer is therefore at most three. Each accepted unit costs one B. Its independent terminal is open. |
| Domestic alternative | A scoped repair and dispatch plan can supply two fuel-equivalent units by day 8. It uses three domestic operating units and distinct qualified shifts already authorized for this work. |

Assume port P is unavailable through day 8. Both A and B
sources are lawfully distinct and cannot be reclassified. Faster
clearance does not waive safety. The domestic repair counts only
if it supplies the same usable service at the affected facilities.
Protect the neighboring reserve and the day-9 creditor. Do not
move other people's lawful resources into the comparison.

Select the third path for this window only, under the stated
actual-source and receiving assumptions. The domestic payment and
foreign draw have different sources. The supplier cannot sell the
same lot twice and the national receiving officer verifies each
leg separately. An optional import or construction programme
cannot use the creditor's amount or the donor's reserve. Scope
and actual authority decide how spare funds may be used.

## Test a second call and an additional failure

If the regional terminal also fails, the domestic path supplies
two and two fuel-equivalent units remain exposed. Holding B
cannot replace that terminal. If the domestic work has no
qualified cover, the second option still leaves at least one
unit exposed. Escalate the exact shortfall and use the prior-law
protection for the affected services, with independent review.
The arithmetic supplies no rule for ranking individual patients
or suspending democratic access.

On day 20, assume the domestic route has qualified only one
unit during a necessary maintenance window. Three regional
fuel units are then needed. Assume the partner verifies a fresh
three-unit physical offer after replenishment and donor
protection. The original facility has just two B left and no
replenishment is verified. One B is missing on day 20, even
though day 8 passed. Reopen the regional and domestic options
under their actual law and source. The original A balance,
private day-10 offer and apparent unshipped donor stock cannot
close this particular gap without an accepted lawful transaction.
