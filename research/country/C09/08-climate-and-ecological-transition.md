# C09. Climate and ecological transition under public control

*Part of [C09 land and ecology](../C09-land-and-ecology.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

A durable country must reduce the damage it causes while keeping people able
to live, work and govern during the change. Climate mitigation, adaptation,
habitat, clean water and local pollution are related but **not one fungible
score**. A tonne estimate cannot buy the loss of a protected habitat or erase
a downstream water claim. Nor does declaring a clean-energy target create a
reliable grid, trained workers, homes or imported components. The founding
public must first establish the actual territory, source emissions, ecosystems,
existing claims, treaty duties and feasible transition paths. This working
design selects an authority and correction route, not invented national
quantities or an exemption from applicable international law.

## Set bounded public commitments from original evidence

[CBG climate budgets](27-climate-budgets-and-essential-continuity.md)
specifies the proposed period and cumulative gross CO2 ceilings,
separate measures for removals and other gases, and a timed correction. It separately enacts habitat, water, air and
restoration floors with their own units, affected places and safeguards.
Scientific bodies and local source custodians publish observed conditions,
model assumptions, uncertainty and gaps; C11 preserves original observations
and prior inventory vintages. Opposition, affected communities and outside
inquiry can challenge an omission or method change. An AI forecast or
ministerial reclassification cannot change the legal budget, erase a past
breach or claim that an offset restored an unmeasured ecosystem. Imported
embodied harms are reported separately from direct domestic inventory so
neither disappears nor is counted twice as a domestic cut.

The Cabinet proposes a **whole transition package** for Assembly choice:
electricity generation, grid, storage and firm backup; buildings and heat;
transport and freight; industry and agriculture; land and ecological repair.
Each sector's operator remains responsible for its legal duty. The package
shows the sequence, intermediate capacity, likely emissions, reliability,
household prices, worker and local revenue effects, land/water/mineral demands,
imports, debt, maintenance and displaced harms abroad. Compare credible
lower-demand, technology, public-investment, regulated-market and local siting
routes without assuming that one price or technology solves every constraint.
The [C06 transition account](../C06/08-ecological-transition-finance.md) counts
shared workers and receipts once. Grid and essential-service stewards must
show a usable replacement before a retirement or electrification date is
reported as operational. Safety standards and ecological floors remain in
force while elected bodies resolve a real collision; a forecast cannot
quietly shut off clinical care or set a new national exception.

## Allocate authority, burdens and remedies

The Assembly enacts the national path, material sector standards, pollution
charges, public investment and any legal change to a floor. Competent elected
local councils decide ordinary siting and service forms within rights and
shared ecological limits; material unresolved cross-boundary effects return
to the Assembly under C04's scope rule. Environmental and sector inspectors
measure and enforce permits, cessation and restoration only under prior law,
with published reasons and independent challenge. A utility dispatcher cannot
choose national climate priority; a minister cannot make a permit override
by renaming it an emergency. Courts correct unlawful acts and enforce floors,
not select the preferred power mix or tax rate. Genuine uncertainty is stated;
failed alternatives remain publicly reviewable rather than being averaged
into one “optimal” AI plan.

Affected workers have information, bargaining, protection of accrued pay and
pensions, paid time and viable learning or job-search routes. Residents facing
higher energy or transport prices need timely household protection and actual
service alternatives; localities losing a major employer or tax base need a
law-bound transition account. Aid is not an automatic indemnity to owners of
polluting assets, and a training enrollment is not a replacement livelihood.
The public comparison names who bears pollution now and who would bear new
mineral, land or electricity burdens. Collective, customary and downstream
rights retain their separate procedures; a “green” project has no blanket
permit or compulsory acquisition power. The [C05 work route](../C05/02-work-time-and-care.md)
and [C10 external account](../C10/02-external-relations-and-migration.md)
carry these effects beyond the environmental agency.

Polluting operators owe prevention and restoration where law and evidence
assign responsibility. Permits name measurable limits, monitoring, security
for closure and a lawful receiver; a refundable restoration bond is not
ordinary tax revenue. Charges, permits and civil liability have distinct
legal bases and fair defenses. Any credit or offset used toward an enacted
emissions path requires independent evidence of additionality, permanence,
reversal risk and no double counting, and cannot automatically discharge a
local habitat or water obligation. Where proof is inadequate, the claimed
credit is not counted as an achieved cut; the underlying legal duty remains.

If an inventory or sector path misses the enacted budget, the responsible
offices report the cause, actual harms and available corrective packages.
The Assembly must publicly decide a lawful correction or an exact prospective
revision with rights, treaty and future-option effects stated. A missed number
does not give AI, an inspector or Cabinet new tax, rationing, plant-closure or
entitlement power. Conversely, a delayed vote does not certify compliance or
make an illegal permit valid. Courts can enforce the required account and
existing law; the political choice within those bounds stays elected.
Annual public conditions distinguish emissions, ecological condition,
reliability, real household burdens, jobs and delivered restoration rather
than awarding one transition score. No actual baseline, infrastructure,
workforce, law, affected-public choice or observed improvement validates this
reference. Reopen it if distinct ecological harms vanish into carbon totals,
if essential services cannot remain reliable, if costs exclude poorer people
or if another package offers better actual protection and public control.

[PS power/trade](../C06/37-physical-supply-and-paid-trade.md) now links food and
water to electricity, fuel imports, emissions and time-specific failures. New
thermal/other firm capacity needs accepted sources and the prior ecological
path; a passing static source-loss example is no permit or zero-carbon result.

[BC02/BC14](../C10/14-cross-border-buying-and-responsibility.md) now gives
connected commercial sourcing an explicit grave community/ecological floor.
The responsible environment executive designates qualified protected C04 case
officers with express BC civil-prevention application standing/duty and direct
ordinary review, including conflicts against public buyers. No new sovereign
board, foreign permit power, public seizure or general import embargo follows.
Actual paid expertise/source and domestic ecological duties remain distinct.

[TH05–TH08 safe indoor service](23/02-retrofit-energy-and-source.md)
joins building measures, usable active heating or cooling, actual peak supply,
elected sources and paid transition. Compare the alternatives on the same
indoor outcome and separately retain the climate and ecological accounts.

[AQ clean air](24-clean-air-and-cumulative-exposure.md) separately measures
local ambient exposure, possible displacement and corrected outcomes.
An emissions inventory or climate offset cannot settle an air exceedance.

[ADP01–ADP08](08/01-climate-adaptation-and-funded-options.md) sets
place-specific preparation, a latest useful decision, separately
funded implementation and real service through a missed switch.
