# Design367. Finance the first independent act and the next protected receipt

[JC33–JC38](../../country/C06/65/08-joint-restoration-and-independent-start.md)
counts starting capacity, ordinary service, independent review and the next
replenishment on their actual sources and dates. A circular contingency
cannot be booked as recovered capacity before its first accepted input.

The [comparison](../joint-restoration-and-independent-start/01-two-services-and-a-first-independent-act.md)
keeps the early deliveries and hearing funded even if recovery fails.
Actual availability, accepted operation and later receipt remain open.
