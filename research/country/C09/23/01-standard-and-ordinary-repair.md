# A usable indoor standard and a remedy

*Part of [TH safe home temperatures](../23-safe-home-temperatures-and-continuity.md).*

**TH01 — Enact a health-protective indoor floor.** The Assembly
defines the covered ordinary residential duty, the funded route to
immediate protection and the scope of technical delegation. Existing
public-health and building authorities jointly propose locally
applicable, measurable schedules for cold and heat. State the
protected occupied rooms and sleep periods, relevant temperature,
duration, humidity or radiant exposure, design weather, measurement
conditions, and when an individual's evidence warrants a tighter
limit or faster help. Use local observation and health evidence
with public reasons and independent challenge. Include rental and
other occupied homes in the applicable delivery routes, with
practical safeguards for people without housing. The enacted
schedule sets ordinary inspection and repair clocks and the
last safe times for interim protection. A design-weather limit
bounds permanent building requirements. Emergency protection still
applies beyond that limit. Changing a protected entitlement,
compulsory power or source requires its competent legal decision.

**TH02 — Assign the failure to its actual controller.** Landlords
maintain a safe building and installed systems under H04. Developers
and building controllers meet the prospective standards under LU
and technical law. Common-property associations perform shared repair
under CB without extinguishing the landlord's duty to occupants.
Heat network operators, electricity operators and energy sellers
carry their applicable supply, safety, billing and restoration
duties. Private owners have access to the enacted assistance and
urgent protection route. The local housing authority is the named
case owner for residential reports and for arranging interim
receiving. It takes the first report, identifies the responsible
actors and preserves the original clock through referrals. The
contested final cause or payer is resolved separately from the
resident's immediate safety.

**TH03 — Admit useful evidence without monitoring private life.**
Accept tenant and other resident reports, condition records, time
series from safe voluntary measurements and qualified inspections.
Give residents accessible ways to supply and dispute evidence,
including an offline route. Qualified reviewers assess sensor
location, uncertainty, time of day and changing weather. A
single reading after a repair or during a visit does not dispose
of credible recurrent exposure. Offer an independent inspection
with a useful-time deadline and evidence preserved for review.
Follow existing entry and privacy law. Optional sensors must
allow refusal and use the least data needed. No benefit, tenancy
or rescue depends on installing a device, logging activities,
proving an immigration status or sharing a complete medical file.
Public reporting uses protected aggregates and retains visible
gaps in coverage.

**TH04 — Act before the safe window closes.** On reasonable
evidence of imminent harm the duty officer arranges a lawful
temporary repair, safe in-home protection or an accepted
accessible temporary place in time. Follow the shorter safe
window under H04 and OC. The original claim, occupancy, rent
protections and lawful repair rights continue. Use existing
inspectors for specific repair and safe-use orders and independent
courts for urgent review and execution. The competent officer
names the actual repair or receiving provider and funded
interim payer. The public fallback pays eligible urgent delivery
from a voted source, with only lawful later recovery from a
responsible owner or operator. The resident does not have to
advance the cost to qualify. Coercive entry, evacuation and
clinical treatment retain their separate necessity, authority,
consent and review requirements.
