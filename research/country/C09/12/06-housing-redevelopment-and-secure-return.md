# Add homes through redevelopment while preserving actual residents' rights

*H26–H31. Candidate rules for planned redevelopment of occupied affordable rental homes.*

**H26 — Decide on retention and replacement before irreversible work.**
For a material proposal, the elected local authority compares adequate
repair, a staged build-first option and other lawful replacement on the
same safety, tenure, affordability, access and service requirements.
State who lives there, who may be displaced and which homes and services
are already committed. Use proportionate protected evidence of lawful
occupancy and an independent route for omitted or contested claims.
A lawful development permission is distinct from possession. Apply
H02, LU and any acquisition law to the exact proposed act. Immediate
safety evacuation retains its own power and protections.

**H27 — Secure real options before a planned move.** The owner and
receiving authority name the lawful households, signed and suitable
temporary or permanent offers, affordability, access, school, work
and care journeys, accommodation and moving costs. Offer independent
assistance and reasonable time to respond. A resident can choose a
permanent alternative with clear terms, without waiving other
claims by silence or temporary departure. Preserve lawful separate
tenancies, succession, deposits and safe contact. No affordability,
access or family arrangement may be presumed from a household label.
The affected stage may start only after qualified receiving and
valid consent or a lawful independent order, as required.

**H28 — Make return affordable and enforceable.** Before removal, the
project binds a named successor to an equivalent secure, suitable
and accessible return or direct replacement offer for each eligible
household. Preserve the applicable lawful rent basis, tenure and
deposits, with any permitted changes stated and independently
reviewable. Publish the replacement scope and protection term.
Do not add a new entrant screen for a returning lawful tenant.
An approved voluntary permanent alternative can discharge the return
commitment when actually accepted and received on the disclosed terms.
Where return is physically impossible, use the existing necessity,
compensation and independent possession route with actual suitable
replacement. No unbuilt unit, purchase allowance or budget entry
alone discharges a continuing housing duty.

**H29 — Pay for overlap and delay without weakening control.** At each
stage identify the actual operator, construction and receiving
dependencies, dated funding, maintenance and independent enforcement.
Provide a lawfully usable reserve, bond or separately appropriated
source sufficient for the tested overlap and delay. Specify who may
release it and the alternate if the project owner, lender or operator
fails. Verify cash and accepting homes rather than the nominal value
of security. Hold a discretionary move or demolition if a gate
fails. Where a stage is already binding, preserve housing, wages,
creditor and resident claims and seek the available lawful correction.
No planning authority gains a general power to seize assets or force
a resident to advance project costs.

**H30 — Count supply after reserving replacement homes and other uses.**
At each independently accepted stage show physical dwellings,
returning households, secure affordable tenures and net suitable
entrant offers. Deduct replacements and other prior commitments from
project offers. Also show displaced offers in the surrounding housing
system against the same no-project baseline. A voluntary off-site move
can preserve a tenancy while using a home otherwise available to a new
entrant. Count each home once per result and separate temporarily
unavailable stock from permanent removal. Give elected bodies and
waiting households safe evidence of both delivery and opportunity cost.

**H31 — Keep the remedies and operator after a title or budget change.**
Residents, applicants and workers can challenge a material loss or
false supply claim through the existing independent routes. Verify
actual occupancy, lawful charges, accessible services, protected
payment and the next maintenance period. Keep interim housing
available on its authorized terms until lawful receiving or an
identified failure and remedy. Preserve current claims, records and
paid successors through sale, insolvency, retiming or an election.
AI may reconcile unit counts and service schedules. It cannot
decide consent, issue a possession order, value a claim or certify
occupation. Reopen on hidden displacement, repeated delay, a failed
receiving route or a stronger rights-preserving housing option.
