# VR09–VR12. Verify a fact without connecting a person's whole life

*Part of [civil registration](../29-civil-registration-and-identity.md).*

**VR09 — Keep custody scoped.** The registrar holds the necessary
enduring civil event and correction history under a specific legal
purpose. Clinical cause and consent, child origin and protective records
keep their own custodians and governed access. Keep routine duplicate
submissions, help-channel material, verification logs and evidence
copies only for justified terms under PD. Secure original timestamps,
purpose and lawful authority. A register is not a publicly searchable
directory. Durable evidence is not a reason to retain every intimate
account or make it available for later training. Hold only what existing
law and the actual dispute require. Keep child access to lawful origin
evidence through RP and protected assistance.

**VR10 — Verify the smallest fact under an actual mandate.** A person
can present a limited extract or authorize a case-specific verification.
A compelled query or status notification needs specific law, necessary
scope, named recipient, audit and usable challenge. Existing protective
notice exceptions retain their limits. No blanket transfer to employers,
landlords, police or immigration agencies. Use scoped internal references
that authenticate neither the holder nor a universal account. No common
compulsory cross-service number, routine biometric collection, public
lookup by former name or default live tracking of verification. Where
identity assurance is material, accept proportionate lawful routes
without an exclusive digital platform. Issuer signatures or other
qualified future media can assist offline proof, with usable handling of
revocation and uncertainty. Official statistics receive only lawfully
necessary protected inputs and release disclosure-controlled aggregates
under EV. A status office cannot repurpose research data as eligibility.

**VR11 — Prepare for loss of the service and test the remedy.** Fund a
usable paper/offline issue and intake path, independent records custody
and verification through C04/DP and paid OP/T. Keep local protected
receipts when connectivity fails, and reconcile securely without
changing the original date or inventing a central approval. Check
correction, revocation and lawful deletion before restored records can
drive decisions. A compromised credential can be replaced without
cancelling the underlying person or lawful status. Publish safe counts
of missed registration, proof not received, unresolved corrections and
actual downstream restoration. Audit delay and exclusion, including
children born outside facilities. The Assembly funds counters, outreach,
records security, review, direct help and actual remedy execution under
DB/FB. Fees and model savings do not fill an unpriced gap.

**VR12 — Keep interpretation, mandate and power separate.** An AI may
help transcribe, translate, or flag a specific conflicting record in a
bounded audited task. It must preserve the original and uncertainty and
allow human correction and a usable non-AI path. No model creates a civil
fact, decides identity, issues a final certificate, merges records or
receives an automatic mandate to disclose. Accept future communication
and proof methods only where authority, accessible meaning and ability to
contest can be verified. Preserve actual existing status, paper rights,
contracts, claims and OP/T receivers through any lawful migration.
Reopen on missed people, mistaken links, unsafe disclosure, capture or
a more usable and less intrusive route with equivalent protection.
