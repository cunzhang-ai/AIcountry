# N05–N08: usable time, paid learning and credible technological gains

*Part of [C05 capability candidate](../07-production-learning-and-capacity.md); design 66.*

**N05 — Count skills and time, not posts alone.** A delivery plan names
distinct tasks, qualified role/shift, supervision, safe workload, location,
source and receiving capacity. Separate frontline service, teaching, training,
leave, repair, standby and recovery. A safe joint activity may serve several outcomes, but its hour is counted
once, never as independent capacity for incompatible tasks or several
reserves. Pooled budgets do not pool all professions. An instructor
released from service needs a replacement or an exposed lawful tradeoff.
Dedicated instructors still displace other work and require prior pay.
An approved role, course admission or account label is not operational FTE.
Supervised learning may produce real service only with demonstrated safe
competence, consent, oversight and net delivered work, counted once. The
bounded preparation example credits none of that unverified contribution.

**N06 — Learning has a protected contract and a real receiver.** Voluntary
paid training specifies prior income/benefit continuity, duration, qualified
instruction, facilities, assessment, recognition, limits, complaint and
receiving job/task. Pay, collective voice, safety, rest, care access and
earned pension rights continue under C05/Y; paid earnings are not also a
second care credit. Budget actual teaching, recruitment, support and expected
non-completion, not only successful graduates. No coerced career allocation,
unpaid family substitute or automatic private-job guarantee is implied.

Course delay, inaccessible instruction, provider failure and non-qualification
are distinct events. Preserve safe assessment and independent challenge;
do not license unqualified people merely because the contract clock ended.
The candidate preparation reference supplies at least six months of paid
return/learning bridge after the stated term for non-qualifiers and instructors;
that minimum does not discharge unfinished training or statutory claims.
Identify actual follow-on learning, employment and ordinary income/care
receivers; an empty referral or an expired stipend is not a completed remedy.
Actual terms and costs need lawful source/contract and affected participation.

**N07 — AI must produce a demonstrated net capability.** Under C11's
revocable mandates, tools may assist research, design, scheduling, translation,
training, repair and routine work. Before claiming usable gains, deduct
verification, rework, supervision, learning, compute, incidents and continued
non-AI access; identify who has the qualified released time and a compatible
receiving task. A benchmark or simulated hour saving is not a delivered care
hour, productive sale, wage, tax receipt or political mandate. Shared model
evidence/controllers do not become independent through multiple agents.

Compare a bounded deployment with actual alternatives on safety, access,
quality, worker/resident time, distribution and autonomy. Harmful or ineffective
use can be rejected. Workers and service users retain applicable refusal,
consent, representation and challenge routes; AI assistance cannot become a
condition for basic rights. Keep interoperable records, lawful access and a
funded exit/offline successor so technical replacement does not erase claims.

**N08 — Renew capability across generations.** Maintain role-specific
retirement/turnover, demand, training lead-time, instructor and replacement
plans with paid overlap before predictable exits. A one-time reserve cohort
does not maintain a country indefinitely. Reconcile new entrants with actual
education/care capacity and other work; births, migration and future AI gains
are not guaranteed inputs. Publish safe aggregates with contestable original
methods; do not expose personal rosters or compel reproductive/labor choices.

[HC](../../C11/05-retained-human-capability.md) now requires proportionate safe
retained/transfer/fallback competence evidence where learning or independent
human review matters, alongside assisted output. Budget practice, qualified
review and source access within N/NP once. Preserve accommodations and use
lawful safe assessment; no dangerous live tool withdrawal or assumed universal
deskilling effect. A measured chat gain is no national productivity coefficient.

[WF49–WF52](../../C06/58/11-paid-time-joint-capacity-and-net-source.md) now
joins paid time and services through actual baseline need/per-task-hour gain
and prior-source matching. Existing learning/break/absence changes count once;
more pace, unpaid work or a displaced private service is no free productivity.

[AL adult renewal](../../C07/22-lifelong-learning-and-renewal.md) supplies a
distinct person-initiated public learning route outside these N contracts.
Existing N income/receiving/bridge/worker terms continue; neither route changes
qualification or makes teaching, paid release, safe cover or support free.

[WF85–WF89](../../C06/58/20-staffing-formation-and-first-service-capacity.md)
applies N to the first budget. Protect mentor and origin coverage and
count separately accepted, qualified and retained paid deployment.
Smaller paid cohorts can remain eligible under an explicitly voted release.

[HC05–HC10](../../C11/05/01-ai-and-succession-of-public-competence.md)
applies the capacity account where AI, outsourcing or reorganization
changes the work that forms future public decision-makers. Count safe
practice, pay, mentor cover and independently qualified assignment.

[AL21–AL27](../../C07/22/05-portable-apprenticeships-and-paid-continuity.md)
preserves demonstrable progress and funded receiving when the
employer or placement changes. Old wage and new training sources
remain separately accounted for.
