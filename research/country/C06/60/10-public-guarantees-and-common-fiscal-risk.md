# DB26–DB32. Put public guarantees on one dated fiscal-risk account

*Design389 · Proposed ordinary law within [democratic budgets](../60-democratic-budget-and-execution.md).*

**DB26 — Record the substance before classifying the promise.** Treasury
maintains a reconciled account of material guarantee, indemnity,
counter-guarantee and other conditional payment exposure across the
covered public bodies. Each issuer attests its own original instruments.
Identify support in contracts, letters, concessions and controlled
entities by actual legal effect, not by label or accounting perimeter.
Separate binding conditional obligations, amounts already due, disputed
authority and prospective rescue without a promise. Public ownership,
consolidation and expected political intervention create no guarantee.
Preserve separate liable bodies, restricted assets and valid claims.
The account does not change bank, insurance or concession authority.

**DB27 — Vote bounded authority and the public purpose.** Before new
exposure binds, use the competent elected authority and DB06–DB10.
State the instrument and issuer, beneficiary and effective controller,
covered claim, currency, verifiable trigger, exclusions, maximum
outstanding and aggregate payments, applicable period, expiry and
source. Compare a narrower guarantee, a direct grant or purchase and
no new backing on comparable service terms and risk. Material
extension, wider scope or changed controller needs its applicable
authorization and review. No general reserve, public ownership or
standing service obligation authorizes a different guarantee.

**DB28 — Price the protection and expose any subsidy.** Independently
estimate eligible exposure, expected cost, administration, loss under
adverse conditions, and uncertainty. Charge an appropriate lawful
risk fee where the guarantee is meant to be commercially priced.
An elected subsidy discloses the difference, who gains, actual
appropriation and the counterfactual. Neither a fee estimate nor an
expected loss is a legal cap or a timely cash source. Count lawful
received fees once and assign their use under their source terms.
Show how the beneficiary, lender and any guarantor still bear loss.
Collateral and private recourse count only on their actual terms.

**DB29 — Test common calls in each payer's currency and date.** Maintain
an independent adverse path for joint calls from common lenders,
owners, markets, currency and physical interruptions. Show plausible
common losses, an adverse settlement or refinancing failure, and
unknown or disputed tail exposure. Use legal maxima for authority
and compatible calls for each stated stress. Preserve mutually
exclusive paths without adding them as concurrent. Count the same
reserve, borrowing permission and counter-guarantee cash once.
When one body's payout precedes another's reimbursement, show
gross obligations on each legal calendar before consolidated netting.
Keep protected service, worker, pension and remedy claims visible.
No uncollected recovery funds a call. An optional issue holds if its
actual authorized source or joint cash path fails to qualify.

**DB30 — Verify the trigger, then settle independently.** The
authorized verifier checks the event, legal scope, amount,
prior payments and operative deadline from original evidence.
Separate certification, payment and review. Pay valid amounts
under their own law and report shortages before the first threatened
date. A dispute about a portion does not erase an undisputed due
amount. Use timely ordinary court and audit access and keep the
borrower's and creditor's lawful challenges. Verify actual receipt,
then record lawful subrogation, collateral, repayment and loss
separately. If a promise lacks authority, obtain competent review
of enforceability, third-party rights and remedies. Neither payment
by analogy nor repudiation by register entry follows.

**DB31 — Decide any new rescue openly.** For a failure without a
valid guarantee, compare ordinary insolvency, direct continuity
of a protected service and any proposed finite support on actual
loss and sources. Use existing sector and emergency authority only
within its bounds. A new rescue requires its own competent decision,
amount, expiry and source. Do not rely on expected rescue to declare
a new contract financeable. A creditor's loss and a resident's
need retain distinct law and receiving. The elected body hears
wider risk and the independent court enforces actual rights.
No counterparty or analyst acquires authority over ordinary budgets.

**DB32 — Keep the public account usable through handover.** Publish
material terms, authorized bounds, covered dates, aggregate stresses
and actual calls, disputes and recoveries. Protect narrowly necessary
confidential material and give independent analysts, opposition,
audit and courts lawful access to original instruments and reasons.
Update the account on material changes and before new reliance.
Fund scrutiny and actual receipt. AI may reconcile versions and
flag shared exposures. It cannot guarantee, value a claim
authoritatively, approve a call or release funds. Adopt prospectively
and retain existing lawful commitments, sources and claims. Reopen
on hidden exposure, weak stress, delayed payment or a better
qualified alternative.
