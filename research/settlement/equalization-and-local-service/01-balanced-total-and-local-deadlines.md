## Common comparison

In a constructed example, nationwide reform reallocates 12 units.
Locality A has a verified retained protected duty costing 10 in each
of two periods, with 6 usable ordinary units per period. Locality B
has a separate 2-unit deadline in the first period and can otherwise
cover its work. The receiving authority has a distinct 12-unit funded
budget for transferred work. Its staff and service acceptance are
tested separately. A has a proposed first-period transfer of 4. The
second period starts after that transfer expires. The common backstop
has 4 accessible units, with earlier protected claims for B and a
complete A service feasible only if all 4 additional units are available.
No allocation of the same 4 can fund both complete packages. A funded
operator can use 2 as a separately verified partial package that serves
some residents while leaving others with active claims. The assumed
priority follows verified urgency and original deadlines under MF27.
The prior appropriation separately permits a recoverable timing
advance and a finite protective payment where no receivable exists.

| First-period path | Result at the first cutoff | Result at the second cutoff |
|---|---|---|
| A receives its separately voted 4 in time | A uses 6 + 4 and B uses 2 from the common backstop. | A needs a recurring 4 or a funded lawful redesign. |
| A's voted 4 arrives after the cutoff | B uses 2 and A uses 2 from the backstop. A has 8 and a 2-unit protection shortfall. | Reconcile the advance only when cash settles. Late payment leaves the missed period visible. The recurring 4 is still open. |
| A's proposed 4 fails to take effect | Carry independently lawful prior sources. The test supplies 6 ordinary units for A and 2 in a separately authorized protective backstop after B. A is short 2. | No claim against the draft transfer. Seek a recurring source or funded redesign. |

If A's partial package cannot deliver, hold those 2 in reserve and
report A's 4-unit shortfall. If the receiving authority's roster fails,
its 12 units stay distinct and do not establish any delivered service.
Under all three paths, a structural 4 remains without a replacement
source. Show the recurring motion separately from the original
handover. If reform has yet to bind, preserve qualified ordinary
repair and hold optional integration until the schedule and receiving
qualify. If it already binds, keep the duties enforceable and report
each missed deadline and the route to correction.
