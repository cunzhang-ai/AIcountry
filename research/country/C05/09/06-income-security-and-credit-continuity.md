# IC17–IC20: independent security, continuous earned rights

3 November 2026 · Design 135 · [IC country selection](../09-income-capital-and-democratic-investment.md), not enacted law

**IC17 — Separate four promises.** Ordinary spendable protection, additional
needs/services, earned retirement units and actual pension-fund cash are distinct.
A person may receive unconditional ordinary U while needing an assessed housing
addition, an accessible service and a separately qualified interruption credit.
Conversely, a person without a current I05 cash entitlement can still qualify
for Y06 care, illness, disability or unemployment credit. Simplifying one payment
does not abolish the other duties, their costs or their protected records.

[Design136 SG](../15-service-first-personal-security.md) now selects service-first
individual A/I05 protection as preferred continuing architecture. Conditional
U+C2 remains a substantive rival, no presumed destination. Both retain the
**same services, additional needs, credit eligibility and prior rights**;
neither comparator imposes a harsh work condition on ordinary protection. Compare actual receipt, control, time and total
tax/source burdens, including people financing the package. Neither lower
administrative counts nor a larger unconditional payment establishes superiority.

**IC18 — Cash status neither grants nor erases a credit.** Apply Y06's actual
qualifying situation and period under either ordinary-payment architecture.
Do not require renewal of an otherwise unnecessary means-tested cash claim to
maintain independently established credit eligibility. Offer a separate assisted
and offline credit route through existing retirement administration; no duplicate
benefit office, public claimant graph or automatic cross-agency data sharing.
Necessary evidence may still be required proportionately, with reasoned notice,
protected originals, correction and direct independent review. A credit dispute
does not suspend ordinary subsistence, disability/care protection or due pensions.

U receipt alone creates no Y06 eligibility. Safe suitable-work/accommodation/
protected-refusal rules still bound unemployment credit; qualified care is not
an instruction to provide unpaid family work. Low earnings alone remain no
credit. Neither a model's worker label nor a payment-account count proves a
qualifying period. Do not manufacture retrospective unemployment classifications.

**IC19 — Match work, cash and periods correctly.** Deduct already covered
earnings for the *same eligible period* from the credit target, within the
applicable source-year annual ceiling. Proved covered work retains its accrual
when wages or employer remittances are unpaid; zero received cash is not zero
covered earnings. Preserve the wage/remittance claim separately, without double
credit or collection. Work outside an eligible period uses annual headroom but
does not consume that period's target. Overlapping eligibility counts time once.

Keep four entries distinct: actual ordinary cash received; covered service base;
required versus actually received G-to-S credit funding; and the new annual
pension unit with its continuing liability. U is not worked wages for the Y levy.
Y06's 10% credit transfer and Y07's 1.25% accrual remain unchanged hypotheses;
neither is an actuarial price. Preserve stronger source-law credits, old earned
units, indexation, survivor claims, pending cases and actual due dates.

**IC20 — Revise the evidence claim, not people's rights.** The
[original-evidence review](../../../2026-11-income-security-and-credit-continuity/sources.md)
qualifies the old preliminary Finnish citation and distinguishes income,
conditionality, employment, survey experience and recorded health. It does not
identify a universal national financing, retirement or service-delivery effect.
Do not book employment, health-budget or administrative savings as a source.
This correction gives no foreign trial authority or permission for involuntary
experiments, benefit withdrawal, private-data access or automatic national rollout.

Existing I/Y officers maintain separate claims and safe transfer to paid OP/T
successors; Treasury pays legally due G obligations into restricted S, whose
administration records actual receipt. Existing independent review/courts can
correct and enforce; AA examines spending and EV evidence without choosing
entitlements. No AI score supplies final eligibility or public consent. Fund
additional assistance, records, staff, overlap and remedies; actual incremental
costs, lawful source, qualified receiving and affected approval remain unknown.

The [bounded comparison](../../../settlement/income-security/README.md) tests
these interfaces, not lifetime sufficiency. Next resolve the complete matched
income/service/provider choice on existing published household evidence and
source-law rights, including losers and paid transition. Reopen on qualifying
evidence, actual exclusion, different burdens, stronger rivals or new objections.
Without follow-up observation, retain uncertainty rather than claim success.
