# C10. Preventing atrocities and controlling our own force

15 October 2026 · Design 73 · Proposed responsibility, not an operational mandate

[C08 G01–G14](../C08/18-international-crimes-and-accountability.md) defines
the national crime candidate. Criminal trial is not the entire prevention
system: lawful force limits, democratic replacement, independent original
records, safe reporting, civilian service continuity and an actual ability
to stop unlawful conduct matter before harm. Prevention is no new warrant,
target list, emergency power or immunity from review.

Cabinet/Assembly retain C10/03's prior mandate, purpose, duration, budget and
lawful handover. Commanders have recorded responsibility and actual control,
not power to define enemies through political/ethnic/model profiles. Orders,
weapons/software legal review, material warnings, precautions, detention/
transfer records and contrary findings need safe independent custody and
access during mobilization and after defeat. Ministers/vendors cannot make
the implicated command the sole historian or choose a favorable prosecutor.

Personnel receive usable IHL/rights advice, confidential outside-command
reporting and a protected route to refuse manifestly unlawful orders. Actual
knowledge/control/ability determines criminal responsibility under G07;
rank, an alert or an unavailable ideal response is not guilt. The country
chooses 18 as minimum armed-force/reserve service, without a learning/training
label that recruits children into hostilities. Child victims need safe voluntary
disengagement and receiving, not adult criminal transfer or locked punishment.

The existing prohibition on autonomous selection/engagement of people without
a human decision on the particular target remains. A human click alone is
not sufficient: the decision-maker needs usable material information,
understanding of lawful limits, feasible stop/control and preserved original
reasoning. Independent legal/reliability review precedes new means/material
software/context change; no deployment authority or technical weapon design
is supplied by this topic. An unreviewable system is not fielded.

Immediate civilian protection, clinical/food/water access, safe movement,
child receiving and lawful investigative preservation keep their own grounds;
they do not await a tribunal verdict or certify guilt. No aid mandate or
atrocity allegation alone authorizes foreign military intervention. Independent
evidence can change an assessment, including claims against our own allies,
officials or supposed protectors. No conviction/target/body-count quota funds
or measures institutional success.

C06 counts the distinct people, transport, communications, forensic/clinical
teams, safe facilities, custody, handover and received remedies alongside
ordinary elections/courts/services. International assistance and future
recovered property are conditional, not allocated current resources. Actual
territory, conflict needs, treaty competence, safe staff and full costs remain
unknown. [CD01–CD16](../C08/21-conflict-detention-and-release.md) now supplies
a separate narrow custody/release candidate, with [C10 control](08-conflict-custody-and-civilian-control.md);
G offense definitions create no hidden detention power or actual capacity.

[DF](12-civilian-defense-and-service.md) now names the public command/employer,
ordinary criminal-court endpoint and actual PO/OJ outside-command case/source
assignment. Military operational learning cannot replace an independent abuse
case. Existing G/P/LF/CD grounds, individual proof, child receiving and safe
original custody remain; no Chief-controlled tribunal or new punitive power.
