# DF16–DF20: pay the civilian-controlled institution and preserve whole alternatives

*Part of [DF](../12-civilian-defense-and-service.md); no weapons design or operational mission plan.*

**DF16 — Include the actual whole burden.** Count qualified command/personnel,
training/leave/rest/replacements, transport/maintenance/energy/sites/imports,
owned FX, safe records, counsel/courts/outside inquiry, clinical/injury/veteran/
survivor care, civilian shelters and safe demobilization. Add SC/TS legal review
of equipment/material software and bounded human-controlled AI support with
paid independent fallback. One reserve worker/specialist cannot simultaneously
fill incompatible military, clinic, police, election or technical shifts.
Defense business income, uncommitted allies, captive labor and future AI are
no assumed free source. A GDP ratio or reference defense purpose prices none
of this country's actual threat/rights/legacy obligations.

**DF17 — Move economic control, not just a signboard.** A retrofit Act must
identify actual predecessor businesses/contracts/assets/debts/workers and
conflicts, their lawful civilian successors or closure, exact disposition
dates and paid continuity before ending military control. Military command
cannot keep beneficial control through a foundation, proxy director or vendor.
Legitimate critical capability can remain civilian public property; no automatic
privatization, forced sale or private-share confiscation. LA/title/creditor/
worker/pension/environmental and foreign duties survive. A new founding has
no military commercial estate to presume; actual retained assets are not free.

**DF18 — Start prospectively without resetting another duty.** The coordinated
Act starts the new DF organization/service method on the first1January at least
12calendar months after enactment, naming the date, exact old/new obligors and
paid record/appointment/commercial/case successors. It must expressly resolve
incompatible prior military forums prospectively without erasing pending
charges, defense, finality or actual jurisdiction. Earlier/stronger current
rights, notice/exit terms and due claims remain; no readiness extension.
New anti-control rules govern from their express dates, not an indefinite
commercial transition plan chosen by the Chief. C10/03 attack/24-hour/seven-day/
90-day mission bounds, RX/LF/CD/P and source/TW/electoral/court dates persist.
No expanded war/service/force power follows from publication or unfilled posts.

**DF19 — Keep substantively different security choices.** Prefer civilian-
controlled professional/voluntary defense provisionally, not a specified size,
GDP share, alliance or global winner. A smaller/demilitarized country with civil
protection/diplomacy and actual accepted regional guarantees may be better;
missing foreign consent cannot be treated as protection delivered. A voluntary
citizen/reserve model can distribute capacity but risks local capture, command
fragmentation and unpaid care/work displacement. A conscript model can expand
staff while imposing coercion/unequal burdens and requires its own legitimate
source/rights choice. A civilian public supplier can preserve capability without
a military commercial estate. Simpler command and different lawful exit terms
remain genuine rivals; more formal review is not inherently safer or better.

**DF20 — Close the positive repair, retain the country-source question.** This
specifies civilian command/economic/justice/personnel boundaries within existing
force law. It proves no actual coup resistance, threat adequacy, qualified
independent appointments or received remedy. Next prepare the joined national
paid-operation/source/control comparison across current democratic, legal,
service/TW/NC/Y, material/foreign and defense duties under ordinary and adverse
handover, with source-preserving retrofit and genuine whole rivals. Distinguish
positive omissions, value choices, bounded feasibility and actual authority/
readiness/outcomes; do not demand an already enacted country for design or
automatically add military details. Reopen on actual threat/source/control/
workforce evidence, harm/exclusion, old-claim changes or a better whole option.
