# Finite mineral rights: public inheritance, plural operation and real exit

Design167 · [NR01–NR16](../../country/C09/15-finite-resources-and-mineral-rights.md)

**Select legitimate public/common underlying mineral rights with finite plural
concessions and no compulsory extraction.** Private/collective interests remain
under their actual sources; discovery is not ownerlessness or acquisition.
Exploration/access, a finite mineral right and independent operating permission
are different. New operating/phase/closure safeguards apply regardless of title.

| Country choice | Boundary |
|---|---|
| Whether to deplete | Existing elected scope/future-options law compares no project, smaller/slower, reuse/trade and ownership; money cannot buy a prohibited ecological/collective loss |
| Public rights award | Existing C04 qualified owner officer, published competitive whole-life terms/actual controllers, no-award possible and10working-day award-intent standstill; no universal royalty/term or perpetual finder entitlement |
| Plural/small operation | Actual objective accessible permission/paid support, no auction-only cartel, minor-use criminalization or worker/ecological exemption |
| Closure/source | Independent TS11/TD11 phase condition covers incurred+proposed exposure, actual available security and receiver; no duplicated collateral/retroactive creditor preference or bond as liability cap |
| Who receives finite returns | Public-owner consideration differs from tax/security/GDP/settledFX; actual lawful NP/source core precedes default replacement capital, with explicit bounded further current-use budget/Act exception |
| Continuing obligations | Exact old title/grant/permit/worker/pension/contract/creditor/record/foreign claims and paid OP/T successors; no new fund/sovereign/force/tax or actual enactment |
