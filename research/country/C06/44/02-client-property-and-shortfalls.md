# NB05–NB08: return the asset that exists, preserve the missing claim

*Part of [C06/44](../44-nonbank-finance-and-client-protection.md); design113 candidate.*

**NB05 — Ownership survives an intermediary.** Client assets have enforceable
separate title, identified class/beneficiary/units/currency/location and custody
chain, independent daily third-party reconciliation and separate usable copies.
They are outside the operator's general estate. No cross-client/affiliate pledge,
set-off of an operator debt, or use to meet operating capital. M05's fully
safeguarded payment balances retain100% accessible protected cash/reserves and
objective reserve-account access; NB does not substitute an investment fund,
uncommitted credit line or bank deposit for that required reserve custody.
Investment settlement cash may use disclosed separately titled bank accounts
only where actual M07 eligible look-through rights apply, no invented duplicate
coverage or insurance of fund units. Preserve
both securities units and value: a lawful market fall is not missing custody.

**NB06 — A mandate changes risk only by an actual separate act.** No retail
waiver of basic segregation or blanket permission to rehypothecate. A separate
optional securities-lending/investment mandate specifies property transferred,
counterparty, collateral/margin, duration, recall, costs and loss; it cannot be
a condition of basic custody/payment. Actual B/PD/agent authority is needed;
a click, copied agent, simulation or prediction of consent proves none. Clearly
mark transferred title and the replacement claim, never simultaneously report
the same instrument as freely available safeguarded property. Sophisticated
users do not waive fraud, meaningful reasons or court access by wealth alone.

**NB07 — Partial return must not await every disputed account.** On failure,
conduct officers seek the ordinary court's independent custody administrator
under NB29, not the bank-resolution panel. Record actual units and separate
pools; return proved identifiable property in kind. Within7working days of
verified lawful available ownership, instruct an undisputed distributable part
to the accepted chosen route, earlier useful-time rights prevailing. Courts
can establish the actual first verification date; an
administrator's acknowledgment cannot postpone that event. Reserve
only the genuinely disputed ownership amount with court reasons and reconsideration;
missing one customer's record does not freeze all clients. Equal interests in
one actually short fungible pool receive the same pro-rata available fraction,
not first-click priority. Different property/currency/classes do not pool by
software convenience; an actual reserve for proved competing title is explicit.

**NB08 — A shortfall is neither zero nor an insured investment.** The operator
owes restoration of property it was bound to safeguard, unless it proves the
loss was an expressly lawful separately assumed investment/market risk rather
than a custody shortage. Fault/causation for additional consequential damages
remain B law; do not make a client prove a thief's identity before recording
the primary missing-property debt. Actual recovered property goes to its pool,
with paid amounts/recoveries/assigned insurer claims reconciled once. General
restitution deficiencies rank under U04 in the operator estate, not ahead of
workers by an invented preference. No new public investor-compensation guarantee
is selected; compare one explicitly if actual losses justify its taxes/levies.
Court-approved administration is paid from operator capital/estate and actual
separate public no-asset court funds, not an automatic raid on client title.
Absent cash/staff delays actual return and leaves claims, not paper success.
