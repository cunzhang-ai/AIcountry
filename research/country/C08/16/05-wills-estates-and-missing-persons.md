# Wishes, dependants, debts and missing people

*B33–B36; part of [C08/16](../16-family-agency-property-and-estates.md).*

**B33 — Authentic wills and chosen representatives.** From 18, a person may
make/revoke a will with supported understanding and voluntary communicated
intent identifying the person, property disposition and operative version.
Use a signed/witnessed or equivalently reliable authenticated record, with
two disinterested human witnesses or an independent authorized recording
professional. Accessible speech, marks and future media qualify when meaning,
identity, free choice and version can be independently proved. No general
medical capacity certificate, digital account or central registration is
mandatory. A beneficiary/controller cannot act as the sole authenticator.

An executor is a fiduciary, not an heir with unlimited authority; conflicts
need notice, accounts and independent replacement. AI may assist drafting/
search but cannot infer a binding will from messages or rewrite it after
death. Ordinary agents cannot create/revoke a person's will. A later
authenticated voluntary choice prevails to its proved scope; a court can
resolve authenticity/coercion and safe interpretation, not invent an ideal
family or general redistribution policy. C08/08 bodily/rite wishes and Y
survivor rights are distinct from an estate disposition.

**B34 — Dependants and default distribution.** Determine actual ownership,
debts and statutory child/dependant provision before gifts or intestacy.
For children, provision considers safe reasonable support to 18 (B30's
learning extension where applicable); actual adult dependants may receive
up to a 12-month lost-support bridge, with continuing contractual/compensatory
claims assessed separately. Deduct reliable independent resources/services,
not hypothetical means-tested aid made necessary by leaving them unprovided.
Limit all awards to actual net estate, disclose losses and preserve public
I/S/Y receiving independently; an estate cannot provide nonexistent wealth.

After those claims and applicable C06 transfer tax, a valid will governs.
Without one, half the residue goes equally to surviving participants in an
express shared-life partnership or persons proving an equivalent committed
shared-life undertaking; a limited care/property compact alone is insufficient. Half
goes to descendants by branch, with a deceased child's branch shared among
their descendants. If only one class exists, it receives the whole residue.
For this default, descendants/ancestors count in their kin branch rather
than the partner pool; duplicate ties never add a second partner share.
No compulsory sexual/intimacy proof. Ordinary friendship or recent claims
without an actual undertaking are insufficient; hear disputed standing safely.

If neither class exists, surviving parents share; a deceased parent's share
passes to that parent's descendants by branch. If no member of those classes
survives, residue goes to the public Treasury under elected law. No tie or
agent copy multiplies a person's share. These defaults do not grant an
unlimited inherited-wealth exemption: C06's progressive estate/large-gift
route remains, with actual rates/source compatibility still uncalibrated.
Strict dynastic succession, stronger social inheritance and broader testamentary
freedom remain substantive alternatives, not settled empirical questions.

**B35 — Administration and insolvent estates.** Appoint a willing competent
executor/independent administrator with an inventory, protected records,
creditor/dependant notice, accounts and court challenge. Exclude others'
joint-property shares before distributing. Preserve reasonable essential
care/housing provision and lawful funeral/administration costs, not lavish
rites ahead of valid creditors. Valid security attaches only to its actual
collateral; insolvent estates follow U's published priorities and proportional
ordinary recovery. No heir inherits personal debt merely by family status;
liability for received estate assets or their own wrongdoing needs proof.

Reserve disputed/foreseeable claims before distribution; give safe individual
notice and an ordinary six-month creditor filing opportunity. That window
does not erase an unnotified/latent protected claim under B37. Return
mistaken distributions under B08 with defenses, subsistence and no duplicate
recovery. Statutory survivors' pension is not an estate asset or refund.
Advance subsistence and receiving do not wait for probate, death debt or taxes.

**B36 — Missing persons and return.** Preserve assets and due support through
B23's narrow reviewable custody, without declaring death to unlock money.
A final civil presumption of death needs clear and convincing evidence,
reasonable independently documented search, notice and a court: ordinarily
seven years since reliable life evidence, or one year after a specifically
proved likely-fatal event. Neither a model's probability nor elapsed time
alone suffices. Exit from a partnership never requires that declaration.

On return, promptly restore live identity and access, review remaining
assets/distributions under B08/B38, and protect innocent reliance and actual
child safety through a hearing. No automatic dispossession, guilt or clawback
of good-faith essential support. Missing/detention investigations and state
accountability survive a civil death record; it cannot erase an unlawful death
or disappearance. New times/shares are hypotheses, not operating guarantees.

[RP10](../20/05-storage-and-posthumous-use.md) now adds specific posthumous
reproductive consent and prospective after-death-conception estate windows.
Already conceived children keep this ordinary route; actual existing rights,
source-law transition and separate Y/I/S claims are not silently rewritten.
