# DP05–DP08: rely on a service path, not a collection of backup logos

*Part of [C11/09](../09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md); scoped reliance conditions.*

**DP05 — Trace the whole relevant dependence.** Before new material reliance,
the task owner records actual model/update/controller, software, identity/keys,
hosting/network/power, records/payment/permission, source expertise, delivery and
review/receiver dependencies. Include foreign jurisdiction/licence, common
finance and supplier control relevant to failure. A private or public common
root can defeat many nominal alternates. Sensitive maps stay protected with
competent independent/court access; publish safe substantive limits, not exploitable
keys or universal user graphs. Unknown dependency is unresolved, not independent.
Do not require a map of all society to provide an ordinary person's due service.

**DP06 — Preserve the actual required service during a declared failure.**
For each critical duty, identify the lawful due/protection window, minimum safe
output, accepting independent route, original receipt/authority and paid skill/
site/cash/material capacity at that same peak. Qualification is relative to the
failure/control being resisted; a path may use safe independent technology,
local bounded automation or human work. HC/TS assistive access remains: this
is not a requirement to manually reproduce every obsolete task or remove aids.
Direct human/legal challenge and protected judgment remain actually reachable.

Test common update, host, key/identity, source-record, payment and hostile-owner
failure where material, including simultaneous essential demand. An alternate
relying on the failed/captured root is not available in that scenario. One
qualified worker/standby balance cannot be reserved twice for simultaneous
fallbacks. Security/reviewer independence is additional to technical uptime;
no different model name proves it. Scope exclusions and remaining shared power/
physical risks are explicit, with current source/capacity evidence, not a claim
of surviving every conceivable catastrophe. No generic72-hour/90-day target
extends a faster actual clinical, custody, election, hardship or receipt duty.

**DP07 — Buy usable exit and pay retained competence.** SC/KR/PD contracts
specify lawful original records/keys/configuration, necessary audit/operation
permissions, actual alternative hosting/operator, trained paid workers and
accepted transition. Source/model ownership or a ZIP export alone is no usable
exit. No assumed right to copy a foreign model/data or seize a vendor's assets;
choose a lawfully usable simpler/other-provider path or do not expand reliance.
Count compute/hardware/energy/water, security, HC practice/learning, error repair,
interfaces, local/offline receiving and staff cover once in OP/T. A promised
AI gain cannot be the sole source for the people who must catch its failure.
Actual demonstrated gains may fund lawful future choices after this accounting;
neither blanket manual duplication nor unconditional AI expansion is selected.

**DP08 — Review real change without restarting society.** Pin law, task,
source, model/configuration, meanings, operating/evaluation scope, dependencies
and actual owner's permissions. A changed relevant source/controller/medium,
new material adverse signal or lost alternate invalidates only affected support,
but propagation follows the complete relevant dependence, not merely an unchanged
file name. Keep material objections and safe correction history. Supporting
results can be reused within valid scope after fresh event/objection checks;
final consequential decisions need actual fresh review. Unsupported future
semantics are retained for interpretation, not silently normalized away.
No vendor release, copied review or new index approves changed authority for
itself. Limit new reliance/restore accepted capability when a required route
fails; old claims and actual employment/source obligations do not expire with
the model. Qualified retain/no-AI/simpler outcomes are valid country choices.
