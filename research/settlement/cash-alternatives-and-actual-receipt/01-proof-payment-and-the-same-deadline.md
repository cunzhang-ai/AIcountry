# The same cash floor through different proof and payment failures

This constructed case compares twelve eligible adults. Four would
receive 6 units of ordinary support under the means-tested route
if their need could be established in time. Under the universal
alternative all twelve qualify for 4 without ordinary means proof.
The four with higher need retain a separately assessed 2-unit
top-up. These are illustrative amounts, outside the national
U+C2 price models. Existing services, other needs, earned awards
and old arrears continue on their separate sources.

Two of the four can prove the assessed need and have a working
route. The third can prove need but the common payment route fails.
The fourth qualifies for the universal base and has a working
payment route. Their assessed need cannot be established until the
next window. The ordinary lawful provisional award, if applicable
and funded, could pay 4 pending that determination. For this case
its separate proof is sufficient. A qualified staffed fallback
reaches the third recipient in either system. Assume the other eight
can use their payments by the original date.

| Exact offer in the first window | Cash actually usable by the date in this case | Four higher-need adults receiving at least 6 on time | Four receiving at least 4 on time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Unrepaired ordinary route | 12 | 2 | 2 |
| Strong repair and qualified provisional award | 22 | 3 | 4 |
| Universal base with the same repair of payment and residual assessment | 54 | 3 | 4 |
| Universal base relying on the failed payment route, with no accepted fallback | 48 | 2 | 3 |

Under repaired assessment the fourth resident gets 4 provisionally.
Under the universal offer they get 4 under the base entitlement,
without needing to prove shortfall for that amount. Both still need
a decision on the remaining 2. If the provisional route has no
lawful proof or cannot receive, repaired assessment delivers 18
and reaches only three of the four with 4 or more. The funded
universal offer can still deliver its base to the fourth.
That difference matters independently of the payment to the
eight people outside the targeted cohort. The universal offer
can also be preferred for predictable income and privacy even
when today's amounts match for those with higher needs.

Reserve 24 or 56 for the prospective award and correction
exposure respectively, including the unresolved 2 under the
assumptions. Stipulate respective operating and independently
funded review costs of 5 and 4. The two complete first-window
commitments cost 29 and 60. With 40 available, only the repaired
option fits. A later 20-unit receipt cannot fund the earlier
difference. The 6 blocked by the failed payment route remains due
under the universal offer and cannot be booked as a saving. If the universal option has 60 by the useful date,
funding no longer distinguishes feasibility on the stated inputs.
The authorized body still weighs burden, reliable access, who pays
and which other uses lose funds. Neither package can quietly use
accrued earnings or service funds. The operating numbers and
funding windows are stipulated, with no assumed real saving.

For the actual comparison, follow people outside the ordinary
claim route. Measure the proof tasks avoided, safe personal receipt,
source failures, outstanding needs and restoration of missed dates.
Record the wider resource and tax distribution even where the
immediate cash outcome is equal. These are comparison requirements,
without a finding about countrywide take-up or living standards.
