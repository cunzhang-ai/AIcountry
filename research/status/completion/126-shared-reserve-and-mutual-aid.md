# Design218. Shared reserves and mutual aid

Test protected donor service, exclusive lawful release, staffed receiving
and safe return against concurrent needs. Fund a genuinely separate
route when the shared route cannot deliver the agreed protection.
Keep scarce resources, money and receipt distinct.
