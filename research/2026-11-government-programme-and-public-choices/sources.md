# Original observations for Design207

Inspected 9 November 2026. The Canadian law and its office's activity report
are evidence about design and operation in one jurisdiction. The Dutch
agreement, spring announcement and September presentation are successive
documents from another government's policy process. They are not independent
measurements of outcomes. Preserve the publications' original languages.

| Record | Exact use |
|---|---|
| [Parliament of Canada Act, section 79.21](original-sources/canada-parliament-act-79-21.html) | Subsections 1–3, 5–9, 11–17 establish request, period, confidentiality, disclosure, withdrawal, unfinished requests and definitions. Scope is statutory, not all candidates. |
| [Canada PBO activities report 2025–26](original-sources/canada-pbo-2025-26-activities.html) | Election costing section reports 35 days, 92 requests costed and 60 published. No inferred explanation for every unpublished estimate, electoral effect or shared resources for this country. |
| [Netherlands 2026–2030 coalition agreement](original-sources/netherlands-2026-2030-coalition-agreement.pdf) | Official English publication, cooperation section and budget appendix. The coalition describes need for broader support and identifies an end-of-term reference deficit and assumptions. The agreement is a political programme. |
| [27 March 2026 spring framework](original-sources/netherlands-2026-03-27-spring-framework.html) | Official announcement of Initial Memorandum frameworks and 2.1% 2030 deficit projection, with new economic information and specified pressures. |
| [15 September 2026 budget day](original-sources/netherlands-2026-09-15-budget-day.html) | Official account of presentation of the 2027 proposals and projected 2.9% in 2027 and 2.2% in 2030. No final parliamentary disposition claimed. |
| [15 September finance minister's speech](original-sources/netherlands-2026-09-15-budget-speech.html) | Describes seeking opposition input and changed or postponed proposals. It is an account by the proposing minister, not independent verification of motive, attribution or results. |
