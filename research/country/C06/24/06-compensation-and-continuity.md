# Independent compensation and an actual receiver for payments

*M11–M12; part of [C06/24](../24-financial-law-and-loss-allocation.md).*

**M11 — Review actual interference and liquidation losses.** Affected owners,
creditors, workers, depositors and clients can challenge competence, entry,
valuation, treatment and remedy directly before an independent civilian court.
No minister or resolution official is final judge of its own transaction.
Fund advice/expertise proportionate to complexity and access; confidential
source accounts need usable adversarial disclosure, not unchallengeable secrets.
Urgent review under M09 can stop an unlawful act where necessary; reversal
is not promised where it would destroy third-party lawful protection. The
court orders feasible correction, fresh lawful decision or compensation with
actual due execution. A compensation route is no immunity from review.

Commission an independent valuation of what each claimant would actually have
received in ordinary lawful bank liquidation at the entry date, using M10's
hierarchy, same information reasonably available, no extraordinary public rescue
and explicit costs/uncertainty. Compare actual resolution value/payments and
timing, not face value or a perfect future sale. Any proved shortfall is a
compensation claim against a separately appropriated public resolution-claims
account, with ordinary public execution and appeal; Treasury bears the net
loss unless a separate lawful recovery is proved. No fictional industry fund
or insolvency priority makes compensation paid. A receiver's self-selected
AI valuation cannot settle it. This fiscal risk is additional to M08 and
must not be netted/double-counted against the same public commitment.

**M12 — Preserve the function without preserving its old owners.** Name
accepted licensed receivers for actual client assets, deposit access, due
wages, benefits, tax settlement, essential merchants and political payments.
Provide assisted routes and usable identity/evidence correction, including
people without a smartphone, fixed address or stable network. Publish access
failures/arrears and protected complaint contacts. A copied ledger alone is
not receipt, liquidity or the legal assumption of obligations.

M07 payments, M06 transaction correction and E/I due support retain their
own amounts/clocks; one pot cannot fund three full payments to the same
person for the same claim. Keep any valid distinct entitlement/compensation.
Due support may have to use a pre-funded public payment alternative while
bank ownership is resolved; it is not an emergency loan to the shareholder.
Existing shortage and court routes expose missing cash/receivers and require
lawful elected action. Elections, opposition access and source evidence do
not pause because a bank or its shared cloud/provider failed.

C06 counts paid bank/insurer resolution teams, independent valuation, courts,
defense, alternative systems, cash/transport, bridge capital, compensation and
contingent public finance once. No assumed fines, asset recoveries, unpaid
community work or model savings finance them. Actual costs and source/market
capacity are unknown; the prior country-book allocation is not qualification.

[RA10/BR08–BR12](../39-bank-failure-and-protected-payment.md) now specifies a court-appointed independent compensation valuer, protected original-title/receipt ledger and an awarded M11 payment date≤30working days, earlier useful-time rights intact. M11 public claims remain additional to M08 and bridge finance. No new irreversible implementation after missed mandatory72h review until lawful reasoned court continuation; completed lawful third-party payments are not automatically reversed.
