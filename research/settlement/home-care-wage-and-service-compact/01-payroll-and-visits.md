# One payroll, one weekly route and two service outcomes

Design353 · Constructed case under [WL41–WL46](../../country/C05/12/08-sector-floors-and-paid-continuity.md).

One commissioner owes 100 weekly one-hour visits. The incumbent's
voluntary agreement pays 12 CU per hour and covers its 60 visits.
An independent smaller provider covers 40 visits and pays 10 CU.
All visit hours in this simplified case are payable. Ordinary travel
and other costs are already financed and unchanged. A real account
would price actual covered travel and other paid work separately.
One assumed quarter-rate payroll charge applies to the increment.
The public rate passes through verified additional payroll cost.
The separately contracted backup has ten compatible shifts and a
net incremental activation cost of 40 CU after an authorized
redeployment of existing payment for those visits. The backup
team is already paid at or above 12 CU. Its existing standby cost
stays in the baseline. The 40 CU pays for activation only.

| Same duties and work | Smaller provider's lawful hourly floor | New spending and usable cash by payroll | Visits actually received in stated case |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Enforce the existing baseline; voluntary bargaining stays open | 10 | 0 new; existing pay/source on time | 100 |
| Enact a 12 floor with the matched rate and backup | 12 | 80 wages + 20 payroll charges + 40 restricted backup available | 100 |
| Same enacted 12 floor; small provider stops 20 shifts and all additional cash arrives late | 12 | 140 voted; 0 available in the required window | 80 |

In the delayed case the small provider completes 20 hours. Its
incremental wage liability for those worked hours is 40 CU, plus
10 in charges. The worker's ordinary pay and any separate contract
claims continue. The exercise assumes the cancelled 20 visits were
unworked and gives no result on cancellation pay under real contracts.
It also assumes no lawful borrowing or other ready backup. If the
10 reserve shifts and their 40 CU are independently available, the
same drop leaves 90 visits, with 10 still due. Later money may pay
arrears, fund restoration and recover provider losses under their
own rules. It cannot restore the original receipt dates.

With no credible date-matched finance, retain and enforce existing
terms and prepare the better-funded route before setting a new
commencement date. With funding and the assumed coverage, the
candidate can extend pay protection to 40 worked hours and keep
100 visits in this case. No incremental recruitment or retention
is assumed. A structural alternative is direct public delivery of
the isolated route. It cannot supply those 40 hours in this case
because no qualified team has accepted them. With a real team,
price travel, payroll, supervision and independent complaints
against the two provider arrangements before choosing.
