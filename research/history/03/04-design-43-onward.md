# Design history: design 43 onward

*Part of [Design history](../../DESIGN-HISTORY.md); historical research selections, not enacted law.*

44. **Integrated design 43: health, education and care gain distinct delivery
    and remedy chains.** The [life-course services
    decision](../../2026-10-life-course-services/decision-record.md) keeps
    national effective access and public residual capacity but assigns
    separate clinical, school and care decisions, independent review, paid
    fallback and a voluntary cross-service interface. C04, C06–C08, C13
    and C14 align; the design-42 predecessor is preserved. No actual public,
    professionals, assets, financing, lawful authorization or observed result
    validates these arrangements.

45. **Integrated design 44: sovereign debt distress gains a public burden-sharing
    route.** The [sovereign-debt decision](../../2026-10-sovereign-debt/decision-record.md)
    distinguishes timing, solvency, foreign-payment and physical shortages;
    requires an elected negotiation mandate and exact binding vote; and carries
    bank, pension, creditor and service effects together. C06, C10, C13 and C14
    align; the design-43 predecessor is preserved. The integrated overview's
    operating questions were relocated into four system maps for navigation,
    without changing those prior substantive answers. No original contracts,
    real public, lawful authority, creditor consent or observed result validates
    the route.

46. **Integrated design 45: disaster recovery gains a durable democratic and
    financial route.** The [recovery decision](../../2026-10-disaster-recovery/decision-record.md)
    separates immediate protection, insurer claims, legal compensation and
    public rebuilding. Local elected planning compares safe repair, adaptation
    and supported movement; the Assembly authorizes national finance and
    cross-boundary burdens. C03–C04, C06–C07, C09–C10, C13 and C14 align;
    the design-44 predecessor is preserved. No real hazard, affected public,
    insurer, fiscal capacity, lawful authority or observed result validates
    the route.

47. **Integrated design 46: climate and nature transition gain a democratic
    whole-system route.** The [ecological-transition decision](../../2026-10-ecological-transition/decision-record.md)
    places a measured multiyear emissions path and distinct habitat/water
    floors under elected law, joins sector reliability and transition finance,
    and preserves worker, local and external burdens. C05–C06, C09–C10, C13
    and C14 align; the design-45 predecessor is preserved. No actual
    territory, baseline, treaty status, replacement capacity, public choice
    or observed outcome validates this research selection.

48. **Integrated design 47: ordinary population health gains a noncoercive
    operating chain.** The [public-health decision](../../2026-10-public-health/decision-record.md)
    assigns elected local prevention, national technical warning, separate
    clinical care and C10 compulsory authority, protected data and explicit
    finance. C04, C06–C07, C10–C11, C13 and C14 align; the design-46
    predecessor is preserved. No actual affected public, lawful data power,
    teams, laboratories, supplies, trust or observed health result validates
    this research selection.

49. **Integrated design 48: ordinary food security gains a whole-system
    choice.** The [food-system decision](../../2026-10-food-systems/decision-record.md)
    joins safe and adequate household food to production, labor, market
    power, land/water, imports, reserves and separate safety enforcement.
    C05–C10, C13 and C14 align; the design-47 predecessor is preserved.
    No real population, jurisdiction, diet, stock, law, public choice,
    budget, staff or observed outcome validates this research selection.

50. **Integrated design 49: political competition gains a financed,
    enforceable pre-vote route.** The [political-competition decision](../../2026-10-political-competition/decision-record.md)
    gives lawful new contestants baseline access, makes major sponsorship
    and paid reach inspectable, limits incumbent use of state resources
    and places urgent repair with independent courts. C03–C06, C11,
    C13 and C14 align; the design-48 predecessor is preserved. No real
    electorate, political-finance law, independent enforcement, usable
    campaign resources or observed replacement validates this selection.

51. **Integrated design 50: reproductive life gains a rights, service and
    child-status route.** The [reproductive-life decision](../../2026-10-reproductive-life/decision-record.md)
    protects bodily choice, names accepting reproductive and early-life
    clinical care, preserves a child's identity during adult status disputes
    and costs the necessary delivery and review. C02, C06–C08, C13–C14
    align; the design-49 predecessor is preserved. No real affected public,
    law, clinical workforce, court, budget or observed outcome validates
    the selection, and emerging reproduction choices remain contested.

52. **Integrated design 51: animal welfare becomes a distinct public
    responsibility.** The [animal-welfare decision](../../2026-10-animal-welfare/decision-record.md)
    distinguishes suffering from food safety, conservation and human health;
    assigns elected scope, independent inspection, safe care and costed
    transition across C01, C06, C08–C10 and C13–C14. The design-50
    predecessor is preserved. No jurisdiction, affected-public choice,
    species observation, lawful power, staff or observed result validates it.

53. **Integrated design 52: serious illness, death and survivor support
    gain a continuous but legally separated route.** The [end-of-life
    decision](../../2026-10-end-of-life/decision-record.md) connects relief,
    supported choice, verified death, body care, estate and a dependant's
    first support across C06–C08 and C13–C14. The design-51 predecessor is
    preserved. No patient, law, medicine supply, registrar, public choice
    or observed outcome validates this selection. Assisted dying remains
    a distinct prospective democratic question.

54. **Integrated design 53: voluntary mental-health and substance-use care
    gains a separate liberty boundary.** The [mental-health and crisis
    decision](../../2026-10-mental-health-and-crisis/decision-record.md)
    assigns direct receiving care, overdose response, a diagnosis-neutral
    immediate rescue rule, independent review and funded transition across
    C02, C06–C08, C10–C11 and C13–C14. The design-52 predecessor is
    preserved. No real affected public, law, service capacity, review or
    observed outcome validates the selection; disguised detention remains
    a live objection.

55. **Integrated design 54: interpersonal safety gains a complete receiving
    and fair-process chain.** The [interpersonal-safety
    decision](../../2026-10-interpersonal-safety/decision-record.md) connects
    private first contact, health, housing, payment, temporary protection,
    prosecution and child decisions across C02, C06–C08, C10 and C13–C14.
    The design-53 predecessor is preserved. No real affected public, law,
    after-hours receiver, enforced order, respondent hearing or observed
    outcome validates this research selection.

56. **Integrated design 55: equal standing gains a common enforcement
    route.** The [equal-standing decision](../../2026-10-equal-standing/decision-record.md)
    joins a civil equality floor, individualized accommodation and lawful
    distinctions to sector correction, independent pattern inquiry, direct
    courts and actual remedy capacity across C02, C05–C08, C11 and C13–C14.
    The design-54 predecessor is preserved. No affected public, enacted law,
    independent appointments, accessible capacity or observed equality
    outcome validates the selection.
    The integration review also withdraws an obsolete completion claim and
    distinguishes current commitment reconciliation from adoption gates.

57. **Integrated design 56: national obligations gain a populated joint
    schedule and compound test.** The [commitment-reconciliation decision](../../2026-10-commitment-reconciliation/decision-record.md)
    maps 37 audited functions and 54 selections to proposed owners, resources
    and executors, while keeping all current quantities unverified. C06
    requires baseline/incremental/overlap attribution and simultaneous
    service, democratic and remedy capacity; C03, C07–C08 and C13–C14 align.
    The design-55 predecessor is preserved. Reproduced historical envelopes
    fail a combined cash/care/institutional staffing test; they do not cost
    the full country or establish authority, operating control or wellbeing.
    The next bounded package reconciles due income, urgent care, independent
    review and election continuity rather than extending an isolated service.
