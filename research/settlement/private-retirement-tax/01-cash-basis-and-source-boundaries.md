An owner-preserving custodian change is not a purchase of a different asset.
Death/beneficiary changes retain actual title, source-period cost and NF27's
paid attributable transfer tax once; a survivor's own contractual claim is
not automatically an inherited owned fund. No basis per account/heir or market
step-up is invented. Foreign relief/nexus needs actual qualified law and records.

New PF private income uses one existing bounded pension disregard only on its
net income component at qualified Y02/Y04 or lawful disability/survivor scope.
Early ordinary cash-outs obtain no new retirement status, tax-free return or
capital-draw exclusion by label. Earlier/stronger actual rights remain.
