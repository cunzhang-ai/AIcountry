# AT05–AT08. Protect due rights and decide in public

**AT05 — Maintain the person-side floor during uncertainty.** Employers
and public services keep original pay, benefit, earned claim,
care and correction dates. Activate existing I05, EB, Y, E and
sector continuity where applicable, including actual offline
receipt. Preserve WL consultation and bargaining before a covered
reduction. Show general cash and restricted earned funds separately.
Use standing Treasury liquidity only within its enacted purpose
and supported availability. A lawful public backing payment enters
the earned fund once as a transfer. Future tax, restricted assets
and an unreceived return cannot pay the current general bill.

**AT06 — Prepare an executable choice before the first gap.** For a
qualifying response, Cabinet and Treasury present the joint account
and real alternatives within 30 calendar days of first qualifying
scheduled publication or protected receipt of evidence meeting AT02 or AT03,
and sooner where a due obligation is threatened. If confirmation
takes time, keep the original received date and protect actual dues
in parallel. Show what can actually be paid and staffed on each
date. Identify the earliest uncovered service or payment and submit
a needed lawful supplement before that deadline. State missing
facts, who owns them and how they can change the decision. Include
one feasible retain/repair route where available and preserve an
unresolved shortfall where no lawful route works.

**AT07 — Put compatible member alternatives before the Assembly.**
The Clerk uses existing DB, PE and protected agenda routes to
register exact member alternatives, protect minority access to usable
costing and arrange a reasoned Assembly disposition within 45
calendar days of the same trigger origin. Apply earlier valid
deadlines. This is a proposed agenda duty, subject to lawful
sitting, appropriation and vote rules. Record votes, material
unresolved objections and named follow-up, including a decision
to retain or revise existing measures. A missed sitting is a
public control failure and leaves lawful continuity and remedies
available. It never approves a tax, benefit cut or new borrowing.
Cabinet, an auditor, central bank and AI gain no substitute vote.

**AT08 — Pay for transition without treating relief as a permanent
source.** Compare actual existing protection, short-time or
redeployment agreements, paid voluntary learning, durable receipts,
public investment and a fully costed unconditional payment where
proposed. Include small employers, entrants, carers and informal
work in the incidence. Use only authorized FB/RF finite finance
with an explicit cap, repayment or replenishment rule where
applicable and a dated end. Compare its successor under weak
sources. No employer subsidy replaces wages or viable work by
assumption. No temporary instrument counts as permanent funding
or cancels a continuing obligation at expiry.
