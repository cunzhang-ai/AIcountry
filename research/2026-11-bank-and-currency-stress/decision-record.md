# Design483 decision record

**Decision and scope.** Apply the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md)
to the Design482 [baseline](baseline.json). Compare three separately
authorized regimes against a constructed month of common bank failure,
exchange shock and loss of a settlement route. Hold legal claims,
protected service and physical endowments constant. Price different
institutional arrangements as distinct choices.

**Selection and material objections.** Retain the conditional lawful
predecessor for opening. Prefer truly independent settlement and funded
reserve service where they deliver protected outcomes on the same
elected budget. This may favor an external settlement operator in
a particular context. An own-currency rail may better serve a new
domestic cohort with lawful acceptance and real available supply.
The model holds one legacy price path fixed; actual price and confidence
effects could favor a different whole package. No aggregate score
overrides a missed treatment or a binding debt.

**Migration.** Preserve prior claims and payout clocks. Separate legal
headroom, cash actually received, outstanding draws, nominal credits,
affordability and delivered goods. An alternate sitting or enacted
bounded rule can handle a scarce public resource. A failed venue
cannot silently transfer the vote to an operator or AI. Continue
the responsible payer, source, protected records and direct review
when a new regime or receiver is prepared.

**Evidence and next question.** The [month case](../settlement/bank-and-currency-stress/01-one-month-under-three-regimes.md)
checks dates and delivery branches, based on the [sources](sources.md).
Legal adoption, macroeconomic effects and actual affected participation
remain unverified. Reopen on original jurisdictional evidence, correlated
capacity or a stronger policy rival. The next operating comparison should
follow a legally due protection under sustained higher import prices
after financial access returns, testing an explicit elected supplement,
a paid physical substitution and ordinary nominal continuation under
the same recurring budget and resident protections.
