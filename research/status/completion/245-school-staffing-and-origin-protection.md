# Qualified teaching through recurring staffing losses

Design337 · Scoped finding for [country completion](../../DESIGN-COMPLETENESS.md)

[ED39–ED43](../../country/C07/21/09-school-staffing-and-origin-protection.md)
makes repeated losses trigger a local disposition and a funded correction or
an exposed gap. It preserves earlier pupil remedies, worker choice and
qualified teaching at both ends of a shared appointment.

The [two-school comparison](../../settlement/school-staffing-and-origin-protection/01-two-schools-and-a-shared-teacher.md)
checks origin loss, actual coverage, cash, common failure and early triggers.
Actual funded delivery, participation, authority and improved learning remain open.
