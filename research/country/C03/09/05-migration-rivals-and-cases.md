# EA17–EA20: compatibility, real alternatives and four finite tests

**EA17 — Prospective adoption, current duties.** The competent prior-law/C14 public process must
authorize this exact source/term/quorum/mandate change, funding, first effective dates,
inaugural or ordinary route, officeholders and named source/contract/case successors. Preserve
existing lawful appointments/seat expiries, rights, ballot/count rules, already-called polls,
pending filings/appeals, pay, records, contracts, PD privacy and continuing service duties
unless a specific compatible amendment lawfully changes them. No source-selector can approve
this procedure's own authority, research indexing appoints no one and future favourable timing
cannot evade C14/RR. A funded preparation schedule identifies actual roll custodians,
draw/recruitment staff, judges, panel/commissioner acceptance, safe records and local sites.
Missing certification/readiness is disclosed failure, never authority to postpone an already
fixed poll, extend a caretaker/old mandate, cancel essential claims or transfer sovereign choice
to the founder. Unknown actual people/territory/source authority does not prevent a coherent
reference; it prevents an operational/legitimacy claim.

**EA18 — Why retain seven, and what could displace it.** Retain/complete D4's seven staggered
commissioners to avoid making one permanent administrator the sole national
governance/certification choke point and to preserve its existing multi-source hypothesis. This
is a normative preference for distributed judgment, not evidence that seven or three sources is
optimal. One merit/parliamentary-appointed nonrenewable chief plus career local officers/direct
courts is simpler and may cost less, but concentrates capture/blocking on one person. Ordinary
cabinet/C04 election administration with transparent counts/courts is easiest to replace and
staff, but the competing incumbent can control rolls, money and evidence before remedy arrives.
Seven members add selector costs, bargaining/cartel risk, recusals and quorum failures;
opposition seats can have a common patron, public selection can be mediated and the electoral
legal-source formation may share upstream controllers with CJ06 or OJ06. Inaugural public concentration, 3/5 O blocking, category veto
and no 4 vote disposition can preserve a harmful status quo. Courts, Treasury, recruits, media and
vendors may be captured together. Reopen on a stronger whole alternative, not another formal
independence label or an AI review count.
