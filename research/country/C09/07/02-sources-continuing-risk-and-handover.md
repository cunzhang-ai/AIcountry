# FR05–FR08. Keep money, land and continuing obligations in view

*Design216 · Part of [C09 disaster recovery](../07-disaster-recovery-and-return.md).*

**FR05 — Fund the early person-side route and the whole place plan.**
Use the existing Assembly reserve, local duties and national residual
authority under disaster finance, MF, XR and FB. Record funded temporary
housing, legal help and actual host services separately from prevention,
property payments, rebuild and long-run maintenance. In advance, identify
a standing appropriation and a lawful urgent payment route at the level
needed for due protection. Use qualified evidence about actual sites,
workers, available resources and correlated failures. Distinguish maximum
grant, conditional or requested payment, received cash, provision, loan
and recurring source. Any material move of money from risk reduction to
acquisition or the reverse shows risks shifted, receiving needs, changed
cost shares and remaining commitments. Report those to the competent
elected bodies and test actual independent receiving before reliance.
A fixed national cap can leave local costs and hazards unresolved.

**FR06 — Pay distinct losses and reconcile actual same-loss receipt.**
Keep immediate human support, safe temporary tenure, loss or damage to
a legal interest, insurance and project or wrong-based compensation
as distinct. A lawful offset applies only to the same proved loss and
actual legally attributable receipt, with reasons and challenge. Pay or
deliver lawful undisputed help while ownership, insurance or mortgage
allocation is contested. Respect secured and lease interests and protect
an affected person's own aid from being silently credited to another
claimant. Count an insurer's disputed payment as unresolved. No blanket
public guarantee of historic value or future rebuilding is selected.
Plan recurring risk reduction and private insurance on their own terms,
with affordable protections for people unable to leave safely.

**FR07 — Assign stewardship of the land that remains.** Before a plan
assumes acquisition, removal or changed use, name the competent lawful
owner, necessary restrictions, funded maintenance, deconstruction and
any safe compatible reuse. Record continuing tenants, neighbors, utilities,
ecological and cultural duties and access rights. Avoid recreating the
same dangerous occupancy by quietly releasing land or lifting a use
condition. A materially different later use needs its own evidence,
affected process and lawful authority. Keep the independent recovery
and compensation routes open. An emergency receiver does not acquire
permanent land-use or sale power by taking temporary custody of records.

**FR08 — End extraordinary administration with accepted successors.**
Use existing FT, T and sector routes. At a recovery office's planned
end, name the ongoing officer, funded provider, accessible intake and
secure record custodian for open cases, payment dates, inspections,
land stewardship and appeals. Preserve first receipt, notices and
outstanding duties, including where government-level settlement is late.
Tell affected residents who has accepted each task. Temporary assistance
continues under its governing need and legal terms. Its administration
must not extend emergency powers. Publish independently reviewable
evidence of safe occupancy, stable access, outstanding displacement and
actual remedies. AI may compare physical options and identify missing
links under DP. It cannot declare that people have moved, approve
compulsion or be the only way to dispute a decision. Reopen after new
risk, loss of a receiver, unfair exclusion or better verified mitigation.
