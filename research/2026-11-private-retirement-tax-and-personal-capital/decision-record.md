## Competing choices, values and selected mechanism

Post-source own saving avoids a new deduction/exemption subsidy and protects
personal control; new employer current-service funding is ordinary actual pay
with a genuine cash choice, applicable Y/PA and actual voluntary net remittance.
It may increase current employer/member burden or reduce occupational saving.
Only actual own funds establish basis; old untaxed promises are not own capital.
Sponsor guarantee/recovery/capital and current-service pay stay separate.

EET contribution relief, TEE-style exempt returns, an annual asset-yield levy and
pro-rata annuity allocation remain genuine alternatives, with redistribution,
timing, record/privacy and source costs. [Primary comparator pages](sources.md)
show their distinctions, not PF's optimality or national revenue gain. Gross
inclusion is legitimate for a proven all-pre-tax/no-cost claim, not a missing-
records default. A plan licence or foreign tax rule grants no domestic relief.

TA06's CPI floor/index matters for owned DC disposals; it cannot be silently
replaced with nominal gain. Aggregate source-qualified same-person DC accounts,
recover proportionally, keep original lot dates and gains-only loss rules.
A genuine nonmarketable annuity/DB promise instead recovers proved nominal cost
first, then taxes actual income. This intentionally defers annuity income and
can favor wealthier savers' timing; no claim of equal incidence or superiority.
It avoids a new tax mortality table while retaining all NB actuarial funding
requirements. Redeemable wrappers remain actual assets, not a tax-name election.

A true same-owner custody transfer preserves history; purchase/exchange of a
different annuity is ordinary disposal/purchase with actual consideration and
a tax-payment route, not a tax-free cross-form rollover. An initial draft
would have carried latent indexed gain into that new contract; review rejected
that hidden deferral before finalizing. Only actual own new purchase cost
becomes its basis. Final unused cost is a gains-only capital loss, not state
compensation or a new negative pension-tax payment.
