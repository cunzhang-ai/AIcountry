# Design history: design 57 onward

*Part of [Design history](../../DESIGN-HISTORY.md); research selections, not enacted law.*

58. **Integrated design 57: continuity gains proposed ordinary-law clauses
    and due-time delivery tests.** The [continuity-law decision](../../2026-10-continuity-law/decision-record.md)
    selects E01–E20 for due income, hardship protection, urgent receiving,
    independent review, narrow substitute execution and political access.
    Administrative clocks cannot authorize late actual protection; bounded
    ex parte hearings also constrain protectors. Cost attribution preserves
    the old six envelopes and exposes residual work, while sample shift and
    common-controller failures remain visible. C03, C05–C08 and C13–C14
    align. The design-56 predecessor contains 216 Markdown files and its
    structured inventory; all 217 artifacts are preserved. A national direct
    provider/reserve rival remains open. No benefit/clinical/contest parameters,
    legitimate enactment, actual independent capacity or observed wellbeing
    is established. The next integrated priority is income-floor content
    and its combined incidence and resource consequences.

59. **Integrated design 58: income protection receives person-level rules
    and a joint tax/launch account.** The [income-floor decision](../../2026-10-income-floor/decision-record.md)
    specifies candidate I01–I12 for needs, safe resources, withdrawal,
    distinct compensation, payment and legacy transition. A common 100,000-
    person fixture exposes private control, expense timing and unfunded
    starts; no basket amount or general funding is inferred from the old
    100-million line or restricted contributions. C02, C05–C07 and C14
    align; 228 design-57 artifacts are preserved. Pooled and universal
    alternatives remain live, actual costs/quality/authority/outcomes remain
    unknown, and whole national financing/production now takes priority.

60. **Integrated design 59: national feasibility gains matched production,
    financial and resource accounts.** The [national economic decision](../../2026-10-national-economic-package/decision-record.md)
    joins GDP approaches, five counterpart sectors, capital/tax/owned-return
    classification, private finance, repeated cash and physical/FX checks.
    Six declared cases include a live integrated-credit rival without
    silently replacing I05. Planned sector balance and annual public cash
    success do not establish received protection; larger cases fail private,
    physical or recurring conditions. The 236 design-58 artifacts remain
    intact. C05–C06/C09/C14 align and all cases are held from readiness;
    attainable multiyear transitions now take priority. No authority,
    actual capability or better human outcome is established.

61. **Integrated design 60: protected stages replace an unexplained leap
    to the future economy.** The [transition decision](../../2026-10-staged-transition/decision-record.md)
    selects candidate T01–T12 for continuing rights, exact legal starts,
    elected choice, source availability, paid overlap, safe acceptance,
    failure and ordinary replacement. Eight-year reference cases expose
    salary/maintenance omissions, FX failure and delayed post-horizon costs.
    Numerical projects, prices, six-year timing and grants are not selected.
    No national floor is financed or actual readiness/outcome established.
    All 244 design-59 predecessor artifacts remain unchanged. Ordinary-law
    enforceability is the next bounded national design task.

62. **Integrated design 61: criminal law gains concrete elements and
    a bounded defended route.** The [criminal-core decision](../../2026-10-criminal-core/decision-record.md)
    selects P01–P20/K01–K12 as candidate responsibility, harm, investigation,
    proof, finite sanction and correction rules. Criminal age 16 and a hard
    24-hour first hearing explicitly differ from the older 14 and extension;
    no numeric choice is empirical optimality or adopted law. Thirteen reasoned
    cases expose blame, custody reset, poverty and hidden-care punishment.
    Core digital access is narrowed to harmful purpose, with protected testing.
    All 251 design-60 artifacts remain intact. Specialized criminal schedules,
    actual capacity and observed outcomes remain open; the civil/remedy core
    is next. Existing software and constitutional boundaries are untouched.

63. **Integrated design 62: civil obligations connect to actual restoration.**
    The [civil-core decision](../../2026-10-civil-core/decision-record.md) selects
    B01–B20 for equal persons, real assent, aggregate agent authority, breach,
    property/wrongs, accountable defendants, proof, public challenge and
    execution. Twelve journeys expose duplicated authority, controlled records,
    insolvent employees/defendants and nominal settlements. New defect,
    institutional and contribution rules are proposals, not legal adoption.
    Broader civil urgency/substitute rules require fresh review rather than
    inheriting E13–E15 approval. All 260 design-61 artifacts remain intact.
    Specialized statutes/finance and actual capacity remain open; the next
    task audits missing design choices across the whole country.

64. **Integrated design 63: full-objective audit exposes material closure
    gaps and private debt receives a fair failure route.** The [completion/
    insolvency decision](../../2026-10-completion-and-private-insolvency/decision-record.md)
    preserves all 269 design-62 artifacts. Chapter/function coverage is not
    completion, and missing actual authority is separate from a missing rule.
    U01–U12 specifies needs/estate, priority, ordinary fresh start, exceptional
    claims, enterprise rescue, neutral officers and actual loss allocation.
    Parameters are hypotheses; no discharge is a new source of output/cash.
    The joined material/economic package remains the binding design gap;
    specialized law, real capacity and superiority remain unestablished.

65. **Integrated design 64: required demand, maintained stocks and timely finance.**
    The [demand/stock decision](../../2026-10-demand-and-steady-stocks/decision-record.md)
    preserves 278 design-63 artifacts and adds ten conditional comparisons.
    Some normal five-sector accounts close with paid replacement; a further
    538-role reserve target has annual pay 21.45 and stated loss coverage if
    qualified. Required output/productivity, prior sources, full pension/duty
    costs, cash/FX timing and bank liquidity remain separate. No new tax,
    earned-accrual, payout or ownership law is selected. One-year closure is
    no evidence of better lives; full-duty attribution and the coherent
    income/pension/production choice remain the next integrated task.

66. **Integrated design 65: continued pension accrual becomes explicit rights.**
    The [retirement decision](../../2026-10-retirement-claims-and-finance/decision-record.md)
    preserves all 285 design-64 artifacts. Y01–Y16 specifies individual basic,
    age/choice, capped career bases with uncapped solidarity contribution,
    care/interruption credits, survivors, records, reserve and elected change.
    Closed-legacy zero-balance comparators are not the selected baseline.
    Authored histories show why a current 188 margin can shrink to 8 before
    omitted costs, with a larger due-date cash requirement. Parameters are
    normative hypotheses, not calibrated affordability or adopted law. Full
    production/service costs, original claims and prior finance remain open.

67. **Integrated design 66: productive capability has a paid path.**
    The [capability decision](../../2026-10-productive-capability/decision-record.md)
    preserves 292 design-65 artifacts. N01–N12 specifies plural productive
    choice, bounded public mandates, paid instruction, net AI gains, accepted
    capability and renewal. A joined preparation reference requires 635
    trainees/65 instructors for the nominal 538 reserve, with prior wages,
    bridges, facilities and replacement. Ordinary eight-year bills are 525.725;
    conditional source 535.725 retains buffer 10. Instructor loss fails the
    target; clinical delay requires a ten-year comparison. No real source,
    productivity gain or full-country cost/readiness is established.

68. **Integrated design 67: the service floor has content and actual workload.**
    The [service decision](../../2026-10-service-entitlements-and-workload/decision-record.md)
    preserves all 299 design-66 artifacts. S01–S16 specifies candidate health,
    free flexible learning and chosen functional/childcare support, with
    distinct receiving, rights and transition. It distinguishes genuine joint
    outcomes from double-booked resources and annual totals from due-period
    capacity. Partial care/skill/clerical cases fail even where gross counts
    pass; repairs are hypothetical, not recruited staff or a funded country.
    Ages, hours and clocks remain normative hypotheses; all actual standards,
    full-duty costs, source finance and observed outcomes remain unverified.

69. **Integrated design 68: one all-function operating bid book.**
    The [country-book decision](../../2026-10-country-operating-book/decision-record.md)
    preserves 307 design-67 artifacts. C06/22 allocates 23 unique components
    across all 37 current functions, matching payroll/purchases, I05, ongoing
    insurance/backing, opening claims and separately owned foreign settlement.
    Normal planned cash closes at required GDP 4,266.89; market value added
    per remaining worker must be 52.33% above old reference. This is no supply
    forecast. Water loss, private FX and maturity fail; actual full needs,
    source finance and qualified transition remain unknown. Bid coverage is
    not complete law, adequate workload or an operating country.
