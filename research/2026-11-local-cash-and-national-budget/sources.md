# Design436. Copenhagen local finance evidence

- The city's 28 April 2026 liquidity note distinguishes the
  twelve-month average, the city's budget-management cash and
  the capital programme. [City liquidity note](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/agenda/fd9924c9-582a-4659-8fd1-e098cd4c1b8f/a783fc97-a240-408a-af08-0386038e5c03-bilag-4_0.pdf).
- The August report describes a later budget revision, named
  components and a possible budget source subject to final
  assessment. [Council minutes, August budget proposal](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Borgerrepr%C3%A6sentationen/m%C3%B8de-27082026/referat/punkt-3).
- The 2 October 2025 minutes record the council's choice and
  discuss the 2029 timing of the alternative adjustment.
  [Council minutes, guarantee choice](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Borgerrepr%C3%A6sentationen/m%C3%B8de-02102025/referat/punkt-4).

Sources checked on 13 November 2026. The [transcribed comparison
and constructed test](../settlement/local-cash-and-national-budget/01-copenhagen-cash-and-local-payment.md)

establish actual transaction timing or freedom to spend the balances.
