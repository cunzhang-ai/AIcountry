# Sources arrive to particular owners on particular dates

*Design 106 proposed operating discipline, not a new fiscal regulator or law.*

National G, local 80% site shares/equalization, S/Y, insurer/client/bank/SF and
private sources retain owners. Taxes from public wages/contracts require gross
payment first; actual withholding can discharge a worker before Treasury receipt.
A quote, expected profit/loan/export or asset valuation cannot make January cash.

If the hypothetical Act is2 November 2026, NF12-month new bases first start
1 January 2028; site/grants 18-month and RF24-month future-service cap first start
1 January 2029. Existing claims arrive earlier. January support, pension seed,
gross-price protection, local advances, supplier/public payroll and urgent
receiving/refunds require opening owned source or authorized accepted bridge.
Quarterly/next-year tax settlement cannot be relabelled earlier receipt.

One matched A/B/C/D/E comparison remains unfinished: access-first mixed repair;
same social package/import-heavy-bulk; full U; targeted/prepaid essential capacity
with explicit reduced ordinary provision and private burdens; and paid direct-
public provision. Carry the same old rights and need/exposure facts, while showing
actual policy differences in tax, use, labor, ownership, autonomy and access.
No rival can win by deleting pension tails, compulsory household labor, missing
supplier inputs, independent remedies or actual foreign/ecological harm.

A successful authored comparison would support a research choice only. Actual
qualified evidence, affected public choice, authority, financed safe readiness,
received remedies and observed comparative lives remain separate unverified facts.
Elected law can choose a prospective expansion/date or reject it. Failure after
commencement leaves a due claim and actual interim relief, not a Treasury veto,
cancelled vote, new rationing power or perpetual emergency.

The [design 107 current comparison](04-matched-country-comparison.md) now revises
the preferred U tax counterpart to C1, prices current duties/paid physical repair,
and retains this earlier subset only within its declared scope. These figures
are not transferred to changed tax/source/receiver assumptions.
