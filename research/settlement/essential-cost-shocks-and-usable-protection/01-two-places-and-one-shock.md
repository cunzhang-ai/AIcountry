# Two places, one cost shock and the next delivery

*Design380 · Constructed comparison with separate [UK price evidence](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-essential-cost-shocks-and-usable-protection/sources.md).*

Two people each have 100 units of usable funds for a seven-day
ordinary basket. Food costs 40, necessary mobility 20 and other
ordinary components 40. Existing rent and care are funded separately
and cannot be moved into this response. The next ordinary adjustment
can pay on day 10, after food and travel are needed on day 4.
Both people have otherwise matched resources for the case.

In A, suitable food and travel remain accessible but prices rise
to 46 and 22. A needs eight additional units before day 4.
In B, a nominal 40-unit food listing remains online but no suitable
retail offer can reach the person before day 4. Mobility costs 22.
A food service has an accepted batch covered by its ordinary goods
source. An incremental 12 pays qualified preparation and delivery
without using the ordinary rent, care or delivery shift. B needs
two cash units for travel and the separately delivered food. This
example assumes lawful voluntary receipt and does not reduce B's
ordinary cash. A future deduction needs its own lawful matched basis.

| Comparison | Result at day 4 |
|---|---|
| Wait for ordinary monthly adjustment | A still lacks eight units. B still lacks two units and usable food. |
| Uniform interim supplement of eight each | A can meet the basket. B can pay for travel but has no reachable food offer. |
| Matched bridge and supply with 22 settled units | Ten reaches the two people, and twelve funds actual preparation and delivery. Both are protected if cash and food arrive in time. |
| Twenty settled units with the ten paid first | The incremental delivery has only ten of its required twelve. Do not count B's food; record the gap and seek an authorized workable alternative. |
| Full cash, late or unaccepted delivery | A's cash and B's travel can qualify. B's food need remains open. |

If the loss persists to the next protected window, compare a
restored retail route with another funded preparation and delivery.
With the same facts and only the first 22 units, the second window
does not qualify. An unchanged need can survive a bridge ending.
The figures describe particular recipients, costs and dates.
They are neither estimates of national savings nor a delivery rule
that selects A over B when supply is short.
