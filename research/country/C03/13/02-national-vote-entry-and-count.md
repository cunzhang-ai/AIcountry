# EL05–EL08: one national choice and an exact proportional mandate

*Part of [C03/13](../13-equal-franchise-and-usable-elections.md); design111 candidate.*

**EL05 — One national constituency, no personal-seat carve-out.** Every national
elector has the same nationwide list ballot, including lawful citizens abroad.
There is no first territorial plurality vote, separate overseas seat quota, N−I
list reduction or list-backed/independent classification changing the count.
An independent may nominate alone as a civic list; one person/one list and actual
personal consent remain. No list receives an external seat through an affiliated
nominee. No legal vote-percentage threshold; finite N and candidate capacity
still create an effective threshold and rounded outcomes, not perfect proportionality.
Local and protected territorial self-government are not national legislative seats.

**EL06 — Entry follows a prospective seat-related gate.** At call, fix positive
E_call (known-P electorate ascertainable at call) and N; require s=min(ceil(E_call/100),ceil(E_call/(4N))) active endorsements
for each list at V−40. Each eligible person may authorize/revoke at most two
active list endorsements until that cutoff. Candidate pool1…2N, one-list/person,
actual consents, disclosed nomination/fallback and neutral preliminary directory
remain EN02/04; no seats, deposits, party incorporation or ideological test.
Use P-known eligible endorsers under EL01, with original evidence/cure to V−30.
At actual cutoff R, at most floor(2R/s) lists can meet this gate. This is not a
universal200; no source officer may cap qualified rivals at a convenient number.
The formula never raises the old national1% threshold, but former territorial
independents may face a larger national organizing burden. One-person and party
lists meet the same rule. Quarter-Hare/1% bounds are hypotheses, not optimality.

Keep EN/ER ordinary V−90/early V−60 call, V−40 freeze, V−35 decision, guide48h
normally V−33, actual7-minute presentation/Q&A by V−21 and useful-time remedy.
No new postcutoff signatures or nominees masquerade as correction. N/list count,
actual distinct candidate supply and lawful growth must price the full field.
A growing roll changes exposure/rights, not an incumbent's hidden threshold.

**EL07 — Count all positive valid list votes by capped Sainte-Laguë.** Let v_i
be each qualified list's valid primary votes, k_i initially0 and c_i its frozen
eligible pool capacity at the actual count scope. Repeatedly award the next of N
seats to the eligible positive-vote list with highest v_i/(2k_i+1), then increase
k_i by1. Skip k_i=c_i; zero-vote lists receive no mandate. Compare quotients as
exact fractions; a tie uses greater total v_i, then the prepublished neutral
public-draw list order fixed before V. Publish the full quotient/seat trace and
candidate capacity, blank/invalid/conditional counts and corrections.

If positive-vote capacity runs out, leave the actual shortfall unfilled and use
EL19's supplementary route; no zero-vote appointee, seat multiplication or change
of full N. This identical rule also counts whole-municipality lists with n seats.
A recount uses preserved ballots/rules, not a new tie draw or inferred vote.

**EL08 — The optional person preference does not buy another vote.** Within
each successful list, rank eligible nominees by actual recorded preference totals,
then its prepublished frozen order for ties/zero preferences; select its k_i.
No party can reorder names after V−40, insert a loyalist after polling or vacate
a member solely by expulsion. A person occupies one seat in this body; actual affiliations/
controllers remain disclosed under PC for finance, not an independent-seat reward.
EL14 preserves a clear list vote despite an invalid/absent personal preference.
EL19 freezes the unused preference/fallback succession and handles later refusal,
death, genuine ineligibility, exhausted capacity and vacancies. Candidate/executive
conflict law remains; a party or office does not adjudicate an opponent's truth.

[NM contestable nominations](../23-contestable-list-nominations.md)
sets the pre-freeze procedure by which collective lists establish
their consenting pool and fallback. The existing entry gate, cap,
public choice and allocation keep their own terms.
