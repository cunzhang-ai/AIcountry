# A portable finite buffer for earned-income transitions

Design168 · [EB01–EB16](../../country/C05/16-portable-earnings-and-work-transition.md)

**Select person-paid transition support alongside existing I05/services.** It
follows genuine job loss/short-time into new work without employer application,
compulsory training or an invented public job. Annual actual capped labor
history limits the insured base; old wages are not current I05 usable cash.
This adds smoothing above/alongside the needs floor, not its first shock rule.
