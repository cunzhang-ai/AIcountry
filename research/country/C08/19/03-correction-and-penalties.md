# Restoring lawful service and proving a monetary penalty

*V05–V06; part of [C08/19](../19-regulation-and-bounded-enforcement.md).*

**V05 — Correction is limited by its actual purpose.** After usable notice,
reply, evidence and reasons under B16, a sector authority may direct cessation,
accurate disclosure, safe recall, compliance repair or restoration of a proved
existing duty within its enumerated power. Ordinary disputed nonpunitive
facts use B14; necessity, proportionality and source law remain reviewable.
No general confiscation, compulsory sale, political-payment ban or transfer
of elected policy follows from a correction notice. A material divestiture,
contested property transfer or dissolution needs an independent court and
specific substantive law, affected-party process and accepted successors.

A prospective condition lasts only while necessary to meet the actual
activity requirement, reviewed at least every six months and on material
change; it cannot become a punitive occupational ban. Q09's punitive
monitor/restriction maxima remain separate. Financial resolution has M09–M11's
distinct grounds and compensation, not a shortcut through a recall power.
When urgency is absent, use practicable staged correction with paid capacity
and a due date; no obligation to await harm before ordinary inspection.

**V06 — Punishment receives full protections despite its label.** V penalties
apply only to VD01–VD14's closed duties, applicable at the act. A regulator
investigates/proposes; an independent civilian court decides contested guilt,
fault and amount with P proof, counsel, disclosure, exclusion, defenses and
appeal. No administrative tariff, computer demand or unpaid bill is already
a conviction. A voluntary advised settlement requires actual informed choice,
judicial confirmation and reasons; it cannot buy immunity for separate serious
harm, bar others' remedies or compel a secrecy/rights waiver.

| Proved duty breach | Natural-person maximum | Body maximum |
|---|---|---|
| A: ordinary negligent failure of a precise reporting/record duty, after usable compliance notice and at least 20 working days to correct | 60 Q02 means-based units | Q08 lower band: larger of 2% verified defendant revenue or twice proved net illicit gain |
| B: knowing/reckless material breach of a scheduled substantive duty, or deliberate material falsification/obstruction of a lawful record duty | 180 Q02 means-based units | Q08 middle band: larger of 5% verified defendant revenue or twice proved net illicit gain |

These are upper limits, not mandatory fines. Breach needs actual ability,
relevant control and the exact fault; unavoidable outage, unsupported risk
classification or a missing public receiver is not strict guilt.
For A, ordinary negligence means failing to take reasonable care that the
person could actually take in those circumstances after the required notice;
the prosecution proves it, not merely that a return was late. No V custody,
community work or fee surcharge; child conduct retains P05 and Q child caps.
A serious K crime needs its own elements and process, not automatic promotion
of a low-level breach. V08 prevents stacked punishment for the same course.

[VD12/ER18–ER19](../../C03/10/08-precise-fines-and-continuing-duties.md) is a new closed national-campaign B-only predicate. For this duty alone the body ceiling expressly includes twice proved unlawful campaign value as another alternative to existing revenue/net-gain bases; individual180-unit/zero-minimum, attribution/defenses/proportionality/same-course and court-only proof persist. No implicit extension to other V/Q cases or automatic electoral ban.

[NB32/VD13](../../C06/44/08-failure-remedy-and-finite-enforcement.md) expressly adds only its closed nonbank B predicates through prospective primary law. Existing individual180-unit/body Q08 middle-band, zero minimum, proof/means/ability/defenses and same-course safeguards remain; no classA or general punishment of a bad outcome.

[OE29/VD14](../../C05/13/06-registry-courts-and-enforcement.md) adds only its
closed prospective enterprise B predicates. V06/Q money/proof/means/ability,
correction/defenses and V08 one-course rules remain unchanged; no custody,
new gain formula or general late-filing/business-loss offense.
