# TW10–TW13: time off requires income and accepted cover

*Part of [C05/10](../10-work-rest-and-paid-capability.md); design90 candidate.*

**TW10 — Paid annual and public/flexible rest.** Select six normal working
weeks of paid annual leave, accruing from the first covered day, plus two
normal working weeks of paid public/flexible holidays. Use the person's normal
hours for part-time and rotating patterns, not a fictional five-day job. Assembly
law names common dates and equivalent time for workers whose schedules differ;
protected cultural/religious arrangements remain. Public holidays do not consume
annual leave; sickness is not automatically renamed vacation. Charge leave
only against otherwise scheduled work, never ordinary daily/weekly rest.

Leave planning respects actual worker requests and safe replacement; no infinite
deferral because a manager refuses to hire. Permit at least two consecutive
normal working weeks when requested. Under the candidate rule, genuinely
deferred leave carries into the next 18 months without erasing employer-caused
nonreceipt; independent remedy preserves claims where illness/retaliation or
unlawful refusal prevented use. Pay in lieu is normally limited to employment
ending, not a purchased waiver of recurring rest. Better contracts continue.

**TW11 — Specify medical absence and income.** Medically necessary absence
protects supported recovery, job accommodation and confidentiality; a diagnosis
alone creates no liberty or treatment power. Employer pays usual regular pay
for the first absence hours equal to two of the person’s normal working weeks
in a rolling 52
weeks for that employment, with common-control employers aggregated. Thereafter the
existing social-protection authority administers an 80% usual-gross-pay replacement
from explicit general revenue, with the public benefit base capped at one median
full-time annual wage W as independently defined in Y05 for that year, per
person across jobs/self-employment, multiplied by
min(1,total covered normal hours/the enacted normal week), for absence up to 26 normal-week equivalents
in that rolling 52-week period, counting the initial employer-covered period, once per person across concurrent jobs
and accounts. For duration, each calendar week counts only the fraction of the
person’s combined usual covered hours actually lost, at most one week;
part-time schedules do not multiply the clock. Pay only actual covered loss;
neither jobs nor replicated agents
multiply the public cap or reset the rolling period.
Prorate partial incapacity and avoid claiming paid work and replacement for the
same hours. Irregular/self-employed people need evidenced normal earnings and
the corresponding public route, not a fictional reinstatement job. The public
route covers their first two normal-week equivalents at 100% of the capped
base, then 80%; only actual covered normal earnings lost through incapacity
qualify. Prior income without current work/loss does not create replacement;
ordinary I05/IC protection remains. Self-employment uses personal labor earnings before personal tax and after
genuine business expenses, not passive capital receipts. The annual W cap and
median-hourly minimum are distinct measures; match their populations/hours
rather than assuming a fixed conversion.

Use 13 preceding paid weeks for the reference regular-pay basis, excluding
exceptional overtime; short service uses actual agreed normal pay, not zero.
Short absences up to three scheduled days allow protected self-certification;
longer/disputed incapacity uses accessible competent assessment without a diagnosis
dossier for the employer. Provisional undisputed payment, safe challenge and
ordinary anti-fraud proof apply. A benefits model cannot certify recovery.

The public component is a new G-funded requirement, not Y's restricted 10%
contribution or an unpriced employer levy. Employer insolvency triggers a public
advance of due first-period protection with a separately recorded recovery claim;
unreceived recovery is no source. Where an employer actually continues salary,
match only the due same-person/period component through lawful reimbursement;
the worker does not receive it twice. Better contractual top-ups remain additional.

After the bounded wage replacement, I05, disability/care, clinical support,
accommodation and applicable employment claims continue; there is no automatic
dismissal or forced unpaid family care. Actual credited earnings/leave and Y06
care/illness additions count once. New public replacement is ordinary taxable personal income but not itself worked
wages for the 10% Y levy. Actual covered salary and Y06 illness credits match
once. For I05 only, classify net actually received TW wage replacement as
earnings-replacement E under its existing disregard/taper; it creates neither
worked hours nor a second benefit. The prospective coordinated Act must state
these dates and preserve past units; current software/law remains unchanged.

**TW12 — Care and civic time keep their own duties.** [CL01–CL24](../17-care-leave-and-return.md)
now specifies the deferred prospective child/chosen-person care-leave schedule.
Actual existing stronger leave and paid participation/release remain distinct from annual vacation,
illness and the ordinary foundation. Do not consume a clinical patient's or
child's service offer merely because a worker has a shorter week. Paid domestic
care, relatives' chosen support and the recipient's agency remain separate.
Unpaid private care and relationships are not a balancing labor reserve.

**TW13 — Paid learning is not free capability.** Select 64 protected paid learning-activity hours, exclusive of statutory breaks,
per normal full-time year, proportionate to covered time, without reducing
professional qualifications or stronger negotiated learning rights. Employees
and independent representatives shape useful choices; it is not compulsory AI
adoption or an ideological income test. The employer/operator funds time and
ordinary instruction; public training contracts need their own appropriations,
qualified instructors, placements and replacement cover under N.

One actual activity can discharge learning and another valid duty only with
matched people, purpose and hours. Deduct the worker's time once. Retain distinct
teaching, independent review and resident services. Teacher preparation or care
documentation is not automatically 64 hours of learning. Portable records preserve
what was actually learned and its limits, not a promised job or a model score.

**Design147 protective absence.** [HP20](../../C10/11/04-paid-compliance-claims-and-transition.md)
adds a G-funded100%-of-actual-unreplaced-normal-gross-loss component, capped by
W times actual lost normal hours/year once, for able workers' source-qualified
protective absence. It uses TW's13-week/new-worker/labor-income proof, not an
illness certificate or26-week illness-clock consumption. Clinical inability
retains TW11, no same-hour duplicate. Actual advanced salary may be reimbursed
only to the due component; better rights remain. HP cash is ordinary taxable
replacement, not worked-wage Y levy, and net actually received is I05 E.

[EB09–EB12](../16/03-overlaps-tax-credits-and-correction.md) coordinates the
separate job-loss buffer with actual same-person/period replacement. Gross EB
cannot subtract merely net TW cash: qualified net plus actual lawful withholding/
credit discharge defines comparable gross M, not an unpaid award. Original TW
source/duration/caps and medical permission remain; neither benefit is work Y.

[AL11](../../C07/22/03-time-support-and-continuity.md) distinguishes public
adult instruction from these TW13 employment learning hours. Compatible actual
voluntary episodes may count once, with distinct payer/qualified cover and
retained employer duties; no automatic128 hours, extra paid leave or public wage.

[WL54–WL60](../12/10-paid-safety-leave-and-confidential-receipt.md) gives
covered workers separate paid safety leave and confidential receipt.
Keep ordinary rest, health and care leave and their separate sources.
