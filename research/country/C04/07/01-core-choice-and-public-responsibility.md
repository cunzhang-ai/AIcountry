# AH01–AH04. The resident chooses the task and has a real public debtor

*Part of [person-directed assistance](../07-person-directed-public-assistance.md).*

**AH01 — A free core before a formal dispute.** A natural person seeking to
understand/exercise a covered domestic public-service, personal legal or civic
right can request initial explanation/navigation and necessary practical help,
including before applying or complaining and across chosen services. Actual
C02/source connection matters, not nationality, income, employment, party,
AI use or proof of entitlement to the underlying benefit. Urgent/provisional
access retains actual law. Supported representatives/children keep B/UA scope.
No subscription, private legal dossier or upfront charge is required for the core.

The core covers understanding official letters/current rules/options and useful
dates; organising supplied evidence; preparing a chosen request/correction;
communication/translation/access support; chosen cross-service planning; and
initial complaint/route identification. Work, learning, care, housing or debt
papers can be considered where needed for the person's actual issue. This is
not unlimited private research, business compliance/tax planning, a premium
personal companion or professional representation in every dispute. B13/P/sector
funded legal/clinical/financial help and stronger support remain separately due.

Civic help includes neutral procedural/source/accessibility assistance and
permitted drafting of lawful NI/electoral submissions in the person's own words
and political position. It is not publicly purchased partisan advertising,
voter targeting, fundraising or campaign strategy. Actual PC/CF/NI political-
support sources and limits apply, with the same public work funded once. No
viewpoint screening or 'neutralisation' of lawful criticism is permitted.

**AH02 — The person's aim governs assistance.** Agree the issue, tasks, limits,
urgency, sources and next steps in accessible terms; no compulsory preliminary
life assessment, preferred career or government fiscal-worth score. People can
change helper, decline suggestions, seek an alternative interpretation or act
directly without losing their separate rights. Explain materially different
lawful options, obligations and tradeoffs; do not steer political choices,
pressure settlement, maximise denial/withdrawal or sell referrals. Refuse only
actual unlawful acts, lack of competence or genuinely out-of-scope extras with
reasons and usable qualified alternatives where the core requires them. A refusal
of fraud is no ideological test or automatic report about private thoughts.

**AH03 — Public responsibility uses existing local/national roles.** The local
authority is the statutory core service debtor and funds direct public receiving
or actual qualified public/community/cooperative/additional providers. Its existing
C04 receiving administration names the employing authority and qualified career
manager through normal protected appointment/assignment, with published remit,
conflicts and direct review. A referral contract does not outsource the guarantee.
No new national advice commission, universal case officer or AI sovereign.

The competent national public-administration portfolio funds the enacted need-
adjusted floor and arranges an accepting residual/outside route where local
capacity or independence cannot suffice, within actual C04 authority. Existing
national career commissioning/source officers and Treasury grant/payment roles
execute lawful funded agreements under SC/DB/NF, not personal ministerial
permission for each claimant. A locality may provide more from its lawful source;
a reduced grant does not erase the national floor or impose unfunded local work.

**AH04 — An actual independent route, not a renamed agency chatbot.** The person
may choose an available qualified core provider and request outside help where
a receiving agency, local debtor, adviser/provider or its material controller
is implicated. They need not first prove wrongdoing or obtain that agency's
permission. Local/national standing contracts must provide an actually accepting
qualified provider outside the implicated advice/case-control chain, with funded
source/records access and useful-time remote/in-person support as actually usable.
No guaranteed named private provider, forced travel or nominal second account.

Check who selects/removes advisers, directs the advice, sees private files,
withholds named-case payment and decides complaints. Public funding alone is
not proof of independence or its absence. Source contracts pay actual competent
availability/work, prohibit case-direction/retaliation and supply outside remedy;
not fees tied to benefit cuts, client settlement, referrals or recovery quotas.
If the funder/manager itself is challenged, use the standing national outside
receiving route and unimplicated ordinary court/source review, not its own final
permission. Missing actual independence is failed delivery requiring remedy.
