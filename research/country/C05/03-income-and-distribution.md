# C05. Production, ownership, work and the gains from automation: Income security and distribution

*Part of [C05. Production, ownership, work and the gains from automation](../C05-economy-and-work.md); integrated design 69 research, not enacted law.*

The reference package prioritizes universal essential services and an income-security
floor, including timely emergency support. A legislated needs basket and coordinated
tax/benefit schedules must address housing, disability, household composition and
usable resources without assuming compulsory family support. Preserve portable
insurance/pension claims and accessible correction of benefit errors. Combined tax
and benefit withdrawal must be assessed together rather than hiding new income cliffs.

[SG01–SG12](15-service-first-personal-security.md) now selects service-first
individual I05/SD05 protection as preferred continuing architecture; full U is
a rights-preserving rival, not a presumed destination. Gross expenditure, net
incidence, services, coverage, assessment burden and agency remain distinct. It cannot automatically replace
disability support or create missing care capacity. The
[national social and fiscal settlement](../../SOCIAL-FISCAL-SETTLEMENT.md) selects services
plus an income floor as the conditional planning reference, and compares retention
and an added universal payment within the same three-year account. It does not prove
one income architecture universally superior.
The [automation settlement](04-automation-income-and-power.md) keeps basic
protection independent of an employer when paid work changes and requires a
joint test of household income, contributions, tax yield and service capacity.

Retirement income follows C07's [two-layer reference](../C07/04-retirement-security.md):
an individual basic payment from general revenue and a capped earnings-related
public-insurance benefit with publicly financed care and interruption credits.
The basic payment is not financed by pretending that every older person had a
continuous paid career; the earnings layer does not replace the needs-based
floor, health care or long-term support. Combined tax, contribution, pension
and work incentives need a person-level assessment, including self-employment,
disability and later-life work.

[Candidate clauses E05–E08](../C06/19/02-income-and-provisional-protection.md)
preserve due payments, define a seven-day actual-resource hardship bridge,
require a reasoned initial decision and bound recovery. The needs basket,
eligible scope, update rule and tax/benefit interaction still require one
complete income statute; the old transfer envelope cannot supply them.

The [design-58 income package](06-income-floor-and-safe-resources.md) supplies
I01–I12: individual protection, measured goods/access, one housing bill,
actual resources, smooth work withdrawal, cost compensation and advance
payment. Its [reference incidence/launch](../../settlement/income-floor/README.md)
exposes dependence and unfunded amounts, not adequate prices or proven superiority.

[C05/08](08-income-ownership-and-country-choice.md) now compares I05 with a
universal personal base/tax change and real-cost mutual acquisition under
the same service bids. Common-production incidence and ownership debt are
explicit. [Design88 IC](09-income-capital-and-democratic-investment.md) supersedes
that destination preference; I05 remains through funded lawful replacement. Neither architecture establishes
better lives, full affordability or a funded transition.

[TW pay/medical income](10-work-rest-and-paid-capability.md) now selects an
individual hourly minimum separate from public protection, retained regular
pay during shorter-week change and a bounded G-funded sickness replacement.
Actual net received replacement enters I05’s E disregard/taper only under the
prospective coordinated law; no worked hours, pension levy or double payment
is inferred from that resource classification.

[EB01–EB16](16-portable-earnings-and-work-transition.md) now selects a finite
person-paid earned-income transition buffer alongside existing SG/I05/services,
not the first shock protection or a new U destination. Historical capped reference
measures this insured gap, never actual I05 cash; tax/source, old rights and
received essentials remain distinct. Insider/newcomer and net-I05 tradeoffs persist.
