# NB13–NB16: price a promise, hold loss capacity and pay the undisputed claim

*Part of [C06/44](../44-nonbank-finance-and-client-protection.md); design113 candidate.*

**NB13 — Insurance is a specified contingent debt.** Insurers publish covered
risks, trigger/evidence, exclusions, limits/deductibles, premiums/renewal/cancel,
beneficiary and claim receiver in usable terms. No post-loss underwriting or
an undisclosed AI exclusion. A false answer affects cover only on proved material
misrepresentation, actual source-law fault/reliance and proportionate remedy;
not automatic forfeiture for an unrelated clerical error. Mandatory liability
coverage or public premium support needs its separate elected scope/source;
basic treatment, rescue, food and I/Y rights cannot depend on buying a policy.
Brokers disclose insurer/commission/conflicts; the insurer remains the debtor.

**NB14 — Insurer capital is not the bank8% formula.** Value assets by lawful
realizable amount and liabilities by prudent discounted expected full runoff
cashflows plus explicit uncertainty/risk margin and operating/claims costs;
no future new-business profit offsets existing promises. Eligible own funds
exclude goodwill, circular capital and inaccessible/encumbered property.
Select capital sufficient for a one-year99.5th-percentile adverse change in
net assets, plus risks not captured by that estimate, without counting the
same risk twice. This is an authored policy target, not a claim every IAIS
regime uses it or a model proves the tail. Maintain independently validated
mortality/morbidity/lapse/catastrophe, asset, credit, duration/FX, operational
and correlated scenarios, valuation uncertainty and reverse stresses. An
unsupported tail estimate is unknown, not zero required capital or a license.

Primary law fixes this protection target and capital eligibility. DR-approved
published line-specific valuation/scenario/capital schedules define assumptions,
method, aggregation, missing-data conservatism and review; reasoned individual
model approval cannot invent a lower target or secretly change the method.
Local calibration, specialist scrutiny and original data remain unperformed,
so this is a selected design method, not a complete launch-ready insurance table.
Same-currency usable liquidity must separately cover30-day stressed due claims/
costs without hoped-for premiums/reinsurance not actually available in time.

**NB15 — React before promises become fictitious.** A breach gets immediate
prudential notice, no dividend/buyback/new inadequately backed underwriting and
an evidenced restoration plan within30calendar days; serious inability needs
sooner protection/ordinary court entry, not30days' delay. Existing coverage,
claims/defenses and due payments continue, no automatic rescission. Reinsurance
counts only its legally enforceable net collectible amount/currency/time with
counterparty/collateral/concentration and correlated disaster failure; gross
ceded claims are not cash. Separate statutory long-term/nonlife backing pools
from owner assets and each other; do not pledge them for unrelated debts.

**NB16 — Claims have a payer and a loss order.** Insurer receives assisted/private
claims, identifies necessary missing evidence and gives reasoned decision within
15working days, one justified extension to35total; pay known undisputed due
amounts within5working days of determination or an earlier contractual/right
date. No extension delays that part or urgent support under other laws. A
rejection/paperless claimant goes directly to independent ordinary courts/aid;
no binding company-selected final arbitrator. On failure NB29 court supervises
runoff or a genuinely funded accepted portfolio transfer. Preserve valid existing
security; necessary pool-preservation costs then policy debts share the relevant
backing pool proportionately to allowed liability, with residual to its lawful
owner. Deficits remain insurer claims under U04, not publicY/S debt. No automatic
insurer-guarantee scheme or Treasury indemnity; catastrophic protection/rebuilding
retains separate C06/C09 duties and elected new financing.

[FR14–FR19](../../C09/07/04-insurance-proceeds-and-safe-housing-repair.md)
follows received building proceeds through lawful custody and actual
repair. Insurer determination and payment clocks retain their own scope.

[FR20–FR25](../../C09/07/05-home-insurance-exit-and-safe-transition.md)
adds a prospective renewal notice and separate resident protection,
without displacing prudential action, valid coverage, claims or
statutory loss order. Verify any catastrophe backing at its due date.
