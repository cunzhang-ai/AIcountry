# Decision record for Design469

**Baseline and procedure.** Use Design468 and the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md).
[Baseline hashes](baseline.json) preserve the exact earlier scope.
Existing judicial, fiscal and elected jurisdiction governs.

**Selection.** Require distinct available judges for the actual
concurrent formations and separately test execution and opposition
receipt. In the constructed case choose a previously eligible and
independently paid reserve where its source and origin cover qualify.
Prefer ordinary scheduling where all useful times can be preserved.
No new forum or appointment authority is created.
[Worked comparison](../settlement/independent-courts-and-concurrent-review/01-three-cases-and-one-roster.md).

**Reasons and objection.** Three formally valid assignments can
compete for one judge. Paying for coverage uses shared resources and
may not resolve any conflict of interest. A separately funded reserve
is useful only when the roster, ordinary cover and actual assignment
qualify. Losing an opposition receipt does not itself block a court
that has an independent original. A missed court or receipt date
retains its remedy and original clock.

**Compatibility and other corrections.** Clarify the separate CJ13
disciplinary first stage and preserve valid CJ12 jurisdiction. Keep
RR's narrow lawful urgent relief and prohibition on term extension.
Separate procurement and corporate audit thresholds from a later
tax choice. Preserve any valid predecessor thresholds and keep the
NF rate conditional on its own adopting Act. Later legislation names
the opening scale, dates and migration of pending awards and annual
accounts through ordinary authority. No fallback creates a tax.

**Limits and reopening.** Authored simultaneous review and resource
limits establish neither actual staffing nor appropriation. Affected
participation, actual enactment and received outcomes remain open.
Reopen on a conflict, verified roster change, missing legal scale,
missed original protection or better qualified alternative.
