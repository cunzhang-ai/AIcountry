# OC09–OC13. Restore the grid without interrupting essential service twice

*Design259 · Proposed national and local continuity duties.*

The national plan shall name how a de-energized area can start, reconnect,
and return to ordinary service. Fund a protected local bridge wherever the
common network cannot credibly recover before a service deadline. Compare
that bridge with direct sector alternatives such as water already safe to
distribute, a separate communications route or an accepted care destination.
Use [OC01–OC08](../20-essential-service-outage-continuity.md) for the underlying
duties and [EG](../19-electricity-service-and-grid-access.md) for grid control.

**OC09 — Require a safe path from a dead grid to usable load.** The licensed
system and area operators agree in advance who controls each stage and
boundary. Verify source start without normal grid supply, the availability of
its starting energy, local control and communications, safe isolation,
protection, voltage and frequency, real and reactive capability, and the
startup and sustained demand of the proposed block. Use approved limits,
including allowance for unusually high demand when disconnected equipment
restarts together. Record how a failed start or interrupted transfer can be
isolated and retried and which crew and supply remain. Where telemetry fails,
use an approved independently verifiable local path or hold the electrical
step and activate the affected service's safe alternate before its deadline.
This is a requirement to qualify local plans under EG, with equipment
settings and switching procedures controlled by the licensed operator.

**OC10 — Separate electrical safety, public priorities and service receipt.**
The elected assembly sets service classes and lawful priority criteria in
advance, with funded local implementation and independent review. Apply
service deadlines, harm from interruption, alternate access and the people
who would otherwise remain unserved, including dispersed homes, rather than
the size of a customer or its political influence. The electrical operator
determines the safe order within the authorized priorities and records
constraints that make the sequence differ from them. If two duties cannot
both be met, apply the pre-enacted conflict rule and record the exposed
people and the alternate response. A safety hold keeps the original
deadline visible and starts the applicable sector response.

Track three distinct states for each protected route.

| State | Person or body responsible for evidence |
|---|---|
| Network section energized and stable within approved bounds | Licensed system or area operator |
| Facility can operate on the restored connection under the required load | Facility operator and responsible electricity operator |
| The named people or public function can actually use the service at the required quality | Competent sector operator and designated receiver or accessible independent verification route |

Receiving tests include safe water at the service boundary, an available
clinical route and a usable communication path from the affected place.
An election or court office records whether its lawful function can be
used through its own procedure. Grid restoration grants no authority over
ballot access, certification, hearings or legal time. AI may reconcile
records, expose dependency conflicts and flag delayed confirmation.
Responsible people control dispatch, service acceptance and remedies.

**OC11 — Compare local bridges with national restoration under the same loss.**
Price bulk-first restoration, autonomous local delivery and a combined
route for each territory's protected duties. Bulk-first can reach more
load with fewer local starts where timely qualified routes exist. Local
routes can serve the earliest duties and remote recipients but have finite
running time and may depend on the same crews, controls, communications
or fuel. Use both only where each contributes to a due window and the
handoff is feasible. A sector alternate may be safer or cheaper than
restoring a feeder for one service. Apply the donor protection and actual
receiving rules of [RC](../../C10/13/05-shared-reserve-and-conditional-release.md)
to shared sources. Count an external tie only within its accepted electrical,
contractual and donor scope.

**OC12 — Protect the handoff and the next call.** Keep the local bridge
available until a qualified transfer and a receiving check cover the same
people, duty and period. Use each source once in the dated dispatch and
reserve account. Include the transfer window, the next plausible trip
before recharge, each route's physical exhaustion and the funded time to
replenish it. Releasing temporary capacity requires a named successor
and an accepted restoration and replenishment schedule. Preserve worker
coverage, paid standby, donor limits, maintenance and existing obligations.
Use [JC](../../C06/65/04-common-failure-and-paid-public-control.md) to show
the cost of the base path, independent alternate and replenishment alongside
ordinary services and public control. Use [NS](../../C06/54/06-essential-imports-and-usable-settlement.md)
where replenishment requires imports. Declare any unfunded or unaccepted
interval with its real deadline and prior-law correction route.

**OC13 — Make a contested order reviewable during loss of the network.**
The public guarantor provides an offline report and challenge route.
Record why each material exception occurred, whose service was exposed,
when it was observed, what substitute was delivered, and who carries the
remaining duty. Protect medical and security-sensitive detail in publication.
The independent reviewer can verify duty priority, unequal exposure,
delay and the usability of remedies under existing law, with technical
advice and access to the original records. Technical disputes go to a
qualified operator for safe correction. Preserve original claim dates,
paid receiving capacity and the ordinary route to legislative funding or
sector repair. Trigger fresh review when a start source changes, a
substitute proves inaccessible, common dependencies change, or delay
exceeds funded endurance. A later restoration clears only the confirmed
current interruption and leaves earlier injuries and remedies traceable.
