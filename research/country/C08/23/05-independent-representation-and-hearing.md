# Necessary care: independent representation and a useful-time hearing

*Canonical UA13–UA14; part of [C08/23](../23-necessary-care-and-unknown-will.md).*

**UA13 — A person has an advocate, not an invented consenting proxy.** The person,
chosen helper, clinician, worker or concerned party can report need through simple
human/offline/remote intake. The responsible clinical/local care authority obtains
support and initiates a necessary application; relatives need not pay or prepare
a technical dossier. The first receiver preserves date and remains responsible
until an actual competent accepting handoff; referral cannot restart useful time.

Use the person's chosen independent advocate where ascertainable and safe;
otherwise an independent legal-aid office assigns a qualified paid advocate and
necessary counsel through published duty rotation/recusal. The proposing provider,
payer, heir, property custodian or implicated relative cannot select/remove the
case advocate or control their only records/payment. C04 career, C08 and CJ
evidence/appointment/funding protections apply to actual roles, not a new guardian.
Familiar contacts remain evidence contributors, not presumptive final authorities.

The advocate tests communication, prior evidence, conflicting interpretations,
less intrusive options, burdens and resources, represents the person in review,
and records uncertainty rather than signing fictional consent. A clinical assessor
is not simultaneously that person's independent counsel. Shared public funding
alone does not prove capture or independence; disclose actual control/conflicts
and provide another lawful assigned route. If none exists, record that failure.

**UA14 — Hearing before the act, not after its consequences.** The ordinary court
uses preassigned accessible jurisdiction with supported participation, protected
notice, evidence disclosure, relevant clinical/functional expertise, the advocate
and affected respondents. Unknown will alone is not an ex parte ground. New
consequential treatment requires this adversarial scrutiny; no clerk/AI grants it.

Determine the application within fourteen calendar days of first receiving it,
but always before the actual useful clinical/care time. E13 consideration within
twenty-four elapsed hours applies when its essential-access/agency scope is met,
earlier if needed; it is not a treatment permission or permission to wait. Existing
basic care, lawful actual authority and genuinely separate emergency powers retain
their own scope. Deliberate delay to manufacture emergency authority is reviewable
wrongdoing under actual applicable law, not a new offense inferred from UA.

Missing an assessor, advocate or judge creates no replacement consent/power.
Seek a qualified independent alternate and preserve lawful essentials. A court
may identify unmet duty and require lawful funded reconsideration; it cannot
create staff, prescribe an unsafe method or ration a whole nation through one case.
