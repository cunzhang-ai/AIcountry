# C03. Delegated rules and effective public control

*Integrated design103 · Proposed DR01–DR24, English working design, not enacted law.*

A democracy needs adaptable administration without a second legislature hidden
in forms, contracts, technical standards or AI thresholds. This selects specific
statutory delegation, effect-sensitive elected control and direct courts.
It makes the earlier general call-in promise concrete while accepting that a
single member may force a vote and delay useful adaptation. No new guardian
or generic emergency power is created.

1. [Authority, materiality and cumulative effects](11/01-authority-and-real-effects.md): DR01–DR04.
2. [Consultation, final publication and paid custody](11/02-consultation-and-final-publication.md): DR05–DR08.
3. [Member call-in, fixed votes and protected agenda](11/03-members-votes-and-protected-agenda.md): DR09–DR12.
4. [Urgency, existing rules and fixed transition](11/04-urgency-old-rules-and-transition.md): DR13–DR16.
5. [Direct courts and received correction](11/05-direct-courts-and-actual-correction.md): DR17–DR20.
6. [Rivals, shared resources and future change](11/06-rivals-resources-and-future-change.md): DR21–DR24.

After mandatory consultation, complete final publication L starts a fresh
30-calendar-day call-in window. Material or called national rules need a real
full-membership majority on days31–45; otherwise the proposal lapses. Routine
uncalled detail still needs the named issuer's lawful act after day30.
Missing votes never create approval. Old lawful duties continue on their own
source, not a generic revival of every earlier regulation.

[Decision, received objections and source limits](../../2026-11-delegated-rules-and-public-control/decision-record.md)
retain the predecessor and competing packages. [Paid dependencies](../../settlement/commitments/19-delegated-rules-and-public-control.md)
separate scrutiny from its actual staff/cash/FX. The [remaining-country audit](../../status/completion/14-delegated-law-and-country-control.md)
distinguishes completed rule design from authority, readiness and lived outcomes.


[LR01–LR24 local law](14-local-law-and-public-control.md) now selects a distinct
full-n local general-rule/input/agenda/urgency/payer procedure. National DR is
not automatically imported into a council or made its policy supervisor. New
local source/rights and actual adoption/readiness remain independently unproved.

[LX public rules](../C11/12-public-rules-and-verifiable-implementation.md)
qualifies consequential implementations against the exact operative text.
An implementation change has no separate lawmaking or commencement power.

[PWR01–PWR06](01/01-review-and-renewal-of-material-powers.md) governs
the outer term and renewal of new material delegations. DR making,
consultation, call-in, review and shorter expiry rules continue within it.
