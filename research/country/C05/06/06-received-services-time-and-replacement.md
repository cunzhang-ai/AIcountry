# I17–I20: a service only replaces the cost and freedom it actually covers

*Part of [C05/06](../06-income-floor-and-safe-resources.md); design115 candidate.*

**I17 — Match the actual component, not the provider's label.** An ordinary
in-kind credit needs an applicable legal basis, same person/purpose/period, compatible
quality, usable location/time, actual safe receipt and voluntary suitable use.
It cannot exceed the priced necessary component and expense actually avoided.
Partial delivery receives only its proved matching share; a ticket does not
replace every journey, and a clinical appointment does not replace all personal
goods/services. No credit for another person's receipt, duplicate invoice,
statutory availability, inaccessible place, changed undisclosed method or an
unknown quantity/price. Preserve the unmatched need and assistance, not a zero.
A distinct MO, clinical or school duty gains no ordinary-credit status merely
from a provider price; MO10's independent cash protection remains.

When the actual purchased component is standby/contingent protection, distinguish
received usable paid cover of that precise contingency from a mere promise or
a visit in another period. Missing reliable scope/capacity/fallback does not
erase a necessary reserve. Failed receipt triggers earlier I/E/C07 correction,
not duplicate recovery or a new entitlement ceiling. Ordinary cash alternatives
remain practically usable when provision is unsuitable or declined, under IC02;
no compulsory meal or indirect school/AI-account attendance condition. This is
not a cash-out right to a clinical or school entitlement's whole procurement
value: essential services retain their separate C07 duties and safe alternatives.

**I18 — Care and paid time are material inputs.** Compare actual work hours,
commuting/drop-off, chosen reliable care, holiday/illness cover, unpaid household
work and protected rest together. A nominal free-hours number cannot erase
necessary paid cover outside the usable window. TW's32-hour staged week does
not itself make a parent, partner or neighbor available; voluntary care needs
actual safe consent/time. Childcare and school are different services. Do not
subtract public support already reflected in a reference net cost again, or
classify a price averaged across jurisdictions as one eligible local tariff.
C07/C04 careers arrange paid qualified reception/correction; the claimant need
not negotiate a whole staffing plan before obtaining existing urgent protection.

**I19 — A replacement reserve is not a received appliance.** Ordinary budgets
may include realistic replacement/repair provisions. Keep actual accessible
stock, remaining useful life, owner/maintainer, cash reserve and timely necessary
replacement separate. On an essential failure or safe household separation,
recognize the verified necessary repair/replacement capital cost in that due
period, less only actually available matching-purpose funds/cover once, keeping
other protected essential resources under I04. Add that uncovered cost to N
and apply I05's actual resource test; this is not a universal extra capital grant
or forced private loan. Actual previous same-purpose payment/receipt is matched,
not assumed from economic CFC or a past hypothetical basket amount.
Funds already used in this matching cannot also be deducted as O/A; an unused
excess retains its separately lawful actual-resource treatment. Distinct due
disability/access compensation retains I05's own independent source rule.

Use I07/E's earlier useful-time advance/provisional receiving and actual lawful
payer/source, with freely chosen safe lawful supply and assisted evidence. A
recognized cost or payment instruction is not delivery. Routine future renewal
for the new asset is not another initial purchase; public/private title, future
reserve and transition spending need explicit accounts. No automatic debt,
food deduction or whole-benefit fraud finding follows from a genuine loss,
missing old stock, unusable shared assets or prior necessary hardship spending.
Independently proved misrepresentation retains I08/B/P defenses and process.

**I20 — Future communication cannot silently redefine human participation.**
Nutrition, relationships, privacy, mobility, learning, care, culture and democratic
opposition are capabilities, not only app subscriptions or message counts.
New media, shared situations, agents and remote presence can help; a model's
cheaper representation is no proof of understanding, adequate relationships,
consent or fulfilled physical need. A person may choose an actual alternative
without surrendering public standing or ordinary privacy. Record what is lost
or unknown, actual delegated authority and revocation, mediator/sponsor power
and practical offline/other-provider access. Replicated agents do not multiply
a mandate. Assembly's contested basket choice, source investigation, personal
consent and actual delivery remain distinct, revisable acts.

[AS05–AS12](../../C07/18-person-directed-assistance.md) now separates restricted
assistance allocations from ordinary cash/resources, actual employment dues and
care received. A personal service account is not I05 O/A, a worker salary is not
a received care visit, and public backing is no second payment for the same due.
It does not convert separate support services into ordinary in-kind credits.

[WF29–WF36/NC](../../C06/58/07-living-bundles-and-early-care.md) now keeps full
published household cost blocks, already subsidized parent fees, actual task/
peak staffing and provider costs separate. No household-person conversion,
subsidy deducted twice, unmatched fee/payroll netting or automatic ordinary
credit follows; independent cash/extra needs and original dates remain.

[WF65–WF68](../../C06/58/15-public-financing-and-real-service-offer.md) separates
an existing service's payer change from new production. Fee relief needs actual
same-person/purpose/period avoided-cost and received-support matching under
I17/I05, not automatic gross-fee deduction, duplicate ordinary credit or an
assumed household gain ignoring actual taxes/benefit changes. Unrelated needs,
unreceived cash/rights and earlier remedies remain; no new I05 formula is selected.
