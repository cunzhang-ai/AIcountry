# C06. Fast scam recovery with protected funds and independent review

*Part of [public finance](../C06-public-finance.md). Design251 research selection.*

**PFR01 — Protect the first report and distinguish the claims.** Provide
an always reachable route at the sending provider for a payment induced
by deception, including accessible assisted and non-digital contact.
Keep the original receipt, reliable instruction, amount, chronology,
safe contact and exact facts available. Distinguish unauthorized payment,
a payment the customer approved under material deception, and an
ordinary performance or billing dispute. A successful authentication
check is evidence about the credentials used. It cannot conclusively
establish the payer's actual assent or freedom from deception.
Preserve separate civil, criminal and account-correction routes.
A person need not find the recipient, file a criminal charge or
surrender unrelated personal records to seek assistance. Licensed
providers offer accessible recipient checks where lawful, voluntary
transfer limits and a safe call-back route for unexpected requests.
A failed name match or warning prompts clarification without
establishing fraud or barring an otherwise lawful payment by itself.

**PFR02 — Preserve a specific sum for a short useful time.** The adopting
Act would permit a qualified provider officer to pause execution of
a specified unsettled instruction, or the receiving provider to hold
an identifiable available sum after settlement. Either action requires
reasonable particularized grounds of material deception, actual urgency,
necessity and the least restrictive lawful scope. For a post-settlement
hold, require a validated referral or original evidence linking the
amount to the report and an imminent risk of dissipation. Use the
lower of the attributable amount and the reported unpaid loss, with
other people's proven property and applicable protected funds excluded.
A model alert or a payment to an unusual recipient cannot supply the
grounds. A payer's unverified assertion alone cannot freeze someone
else's funds. A provider may use a narrower voluntary lawful step.

The proposed common outer limit is **four elapsed hours** from the
first actual restriction under this rule for the same identified amount
and transaction. End earlier when the grounds cease. Routing the
case to another provider, payment rail or officer cannot restart the
clock. A continuing restriction requires a distinct timely B17 order
or another specifically applicable existing power on its own grounds.
Seek the necessary order promptly. Without it, restore actual access
by expiry and report any failure. A technical hold changes neither
legal title nor settled payment finality. It cannot block unrelated
balances, incoming wages or payments from funds outside its lawful scope.

**PFR03 — Protect both sides and keep review independent.** Give the
payer and affected holder safe reasons, amount, expiry and a usable
direct challenge. A delay of notice requires specific prior authority,
recorded grounds and a protected prompt review. Check emergency living
needs and lawful use of unrestrained funds. The provider bears proved
direct charges and interest caused by its temporary restriction.
Other proven loss follows applicable civil law. Use an independent
conduct remedy and an ordinary court with access to original records.
Provider, police and reviewers retain distinct roles. No risk flag
or reimbursement decision supplies guilt, surveillance authority or
a right to debit an innocent holder. Preserve minimum necessary
evidence in the original form. AI may check routing and reconcile
timers. A responsible officer checks facts and decides action.

**PFR04 — Pay a qualifying loss independently of criminal recovery.**
For the candidate's covered domestic consumer transfers, the sending
provider is the first payer of eligible net loss up to a published
incident ceiling set by law. Eligible loss requires evidence of
material fraud causing the payment. Proven collusion, already returned
amounts and actual scope limits apply. Ordinary inattention or
clicking a warning does not by itself bar payment. Give a reasoned
decision and pay the verified undisputed part within five working days
of protected receipt. A materially disputed remainder can use one
reasoned review period within a total of 20 working days, with early
payment and hardship routes as applicable. Missing either deadline
does not approve or cancel a claim. Preserve direct conduct and court
remedies, original receipt and independent challenge. The receiving
provider reimburses one half of the qualifying net loss under the
adopting law. A dispute, criminal investigation or collection delay
between firms cannot delay a verified consumer payment.

**PFR05 — Reconcile recovery, fund real delivery and keep scope finite.**
Separate suspected loss, legally restrained funds, actual return,
provider payment and final recovery. Amounts actually returned before
reimbursement reduce the same qualifying loss once. Later lawful
recoveries fill the consumer's outstanding loss first, then credit
providers up to their actual net outlays, without extra recovery of
the same loss. Preserve lawful third-party title and objections.
Provide funded 24-hour response, independent review, held liquidity
and secure alternate communications within ordinary conduct and
prudential mandates. Provider liability uses its lawful own resources,
with any additional resolution or guarantee only under its own prior
law. No routine Treasury, deposit-insurance, monetary or foreign
enforcement authority follows. Publish safe results on loss, timing,
false restrictions, relief and unrecovered amounts. Reopen for missed
expiry, displaced fraud, unequal burdens, weak funding or a safer
simpler route. Preserve applicable M, B, PT, P, PD and OP/T rights.
