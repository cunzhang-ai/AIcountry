# C04. Redesign essential service networks without losing access

*Design403 · SNR01–SNR08 · Proposed common rules for material network changes.*

The public guarantees usable services. A network may need to add capacity,
move a site, change its hours or share facilities across localities as needs
change. Treat the receiving service, the journey, transition costs and the
capacity to recover from failure as part of the decision to change the network.

**SNR01 — Test the actual change in access.** Apply this rule to a planned
change of an essential public route where location, hours, capacity, staffing,
language or delivery medium could materially change whether people receive a
protected service. Assess linked steps together against the last lawfully
assessed configuration so several small reductions cannot evade scrutiny.
Use published sector materiality criteria with independent challenge. Routine
scheduling within the lawful scope stays delegated. An urgent unsafe site may
close under its own powers, with earlier safety and continuity duties applied.
Temporary safety action does not by itself authorize permanent reconfiguration.

**SNR02 — Compare networks on the same people and duties.** Compare retain
and repair, a distributed or shared option and consolidation where credible.
Measure qualified actual slots, usable round trips, waiting, care and work
time, accessibility, affordability, emergency coverage and affected worker
conditions. Include those already excluded, demand suppressed by prior cuts,
and materially affected neighbors and receiving sites. Use ordinary and
credible adverse service periods. An average travel time, facility count or
total seat count cannot settle a material loss for a group. Use the applicable
sector rights and service floor. For repeated losses of providers,
compare temporary backstop, a voluntary independent network and
an ordinary public or cooperative service on the same duties.
Test a second loss before recovery and count shared staff,
control, transport, records and paying routes once. Include the
sending place's live duties and likely concurrent need. Explain remaining burdens even where the
floor is met, and protect small groups' identities.

**SNR03 — Leave the choice with answerable public institutions.** The elected
body with lawful authority votes the defined material plan and resources or
a bounded mandate after affected communities, workers, users and opposition
can examine alternatives and propose their own. Use [CLX](../01/02-cross-local-effects-and-residual-decisions.md)
for material unresolved cross-local effects. Qualified sector authorities retain
their lawful safety, clinical or educational judgments. A funder, provider or
asset buyer cannot dictate closure through a grant model or contract.
An independent reviewer must be able to hear a challenge and order useful
interim or final relief within its powers before an irreversible act. No local
veto can extinguish a protected neighboring service or force unsafe operation.

**SNR04 — Fund the complete replacement and the overlap.** Allocate the old
route's continuing costs, the successor's staff and assets, travel assistance,
transition, independent review and any temporary return path by real source
and date. Check each dependent service against its own protected duties.
A nearby hub needs both accepting staff and a journey people can actually make.
A shared site, worker, vehicle or cash receipt is counted once. Forecast
savings, sale proceeds and unaccepted routes cannot pay for earlier obligations.
Any adjustment to equalization uses [MF14–MF18](04-funding-through-population-change.md)
and the actual date a fixed obligation lawfully ends.

**SNR05 — Move in stages whose conditions are knowable in advance.** A lawful
plan identifies the first transfers, the applicable access and safety criteria,
the time and evidence needed to verify them, and the people responsible for
action when they fail. Begin with qualified capacity and preserve a funded
continuing or return route until the successor has demonstrated receipt and
can handle the next protected window. Use representative lawful tests and
actual early receiving where feasible. A person cannot be required to lose a
protected service to test an alternative. Keep accrued cases and accepting
staff, access accommodations and records together under ordinary transfer law.

**SNR06 — Keep irreversible commitments conditional.** A sale, unconditional
lease termination, demolition or permanent surrender of key capability needs
its own legal authority under [PL](../../C06/64/04-public-asset-disposal-and-control.md)
or applicable law. It cannot take effect before the independent access check,
the adopted transition conditions and any required relief opportunity have
been satisfied. Where the old site itself cannot safely reopen, identify a
qualified other route and its source before counting continuity. Do not use
an asset sale deadline as a reason to approve an unsupported replacement.

**SNR07 — Act on failure while the person can still be served.** When
material access fails, pause further transfers under the adopted plan and
use the qualified, lawful continuing or return path by the relevant deadline.
Preserve existing entitlements, funding restrictions, open cases, worker
rights and direct independent challenge. If no path can deliver, report the
precise uncovered duties and invoke the applicable MF, LD, AP or sector
route only within its authority and source. A lapse ends exceptional authority
under its own law. Return persistent need to ordinary elected service and
budget decisions. Repeated provider loss triggers an ordinary
comparison of paid repair, independent joint delivery and a
bounded standing provider. Record whether each leaves a practical
alternative, independent complaint and lawful future replacement.
A standing provider needs its own recurring source, exact scope
and elected authorization. Keep the standing operator separate from
custody and final review of its own disputes. Give affected residents
and opposition usable time to contest the comparison and offer a
qualified successor. Report missed care or lost access
even if later repaired.

**SNR08 — Review actual effects and allow revision.** The implementing law
names owners for assessment, authorization, payment, site operations, review
and successor receiving. Publish protected results across access needs and
sites, including total cost, missed services and unresolved claims. Retest
on material population, provider, transport or fiscal changes. An AI model
may compare lawful routes and expose omitted demand. A forecast cannot supply
consent, clinical judgment, legal authority or proof of use. Adopt prospectively
with paid handover and preserve existing duties. Reopen if the fallback is
consumed, costs shift onto households, or a simpler option protects access
more reliably.
