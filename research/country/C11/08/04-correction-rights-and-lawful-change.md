# PM19–PM24. Correction, opposition and change remain usable

*Design146 · [Public media](../08-public-media-and-editorial-independence.md) · No general press regulator or proof of actual independence.*

**PM19 — A reachable complaint with a finite answer.** Receive reports through human,
assisted, paper, phone and accessible/future channels without a complete dossier
or AI account. Protect source meaning, actual first receipt and privacy. Provide
help within five working days, an independent complaints-editor response within
20working days, one notified reasoned extension of at most20working days before
expiry for actual necessary work. Record failure; neither silence nor a metric
says the story is true, complaint rejected or relief delivered. Actually verified
material error/safety correction cannot wait for that outer clock.

These are internal editorial-response duties, not substituted administrative merits,
required court exhaustion or a shortened B16 adverse reply. Genuine public-law
access/funding/rights disputes keep AP/B/E and their earlier actual useful-time
clocks. Prioritize immediate harm with actual competent source/protection; an
urgent report does not itself establish falsity. Group identical copies safely
without dropping genuinely different underlying claims or required rights review.

**PM20 — Publish findings without an official truth monopoly.** Complaints editor
publishes the reasoned finding, evidence limits and material dissent with a linked
chief/editor response within five working days of disposition, safely redacting
sources/personal harm under contested lawful limits. The Service cannot bury the
finding because it is embarrassing. An established material correction is linked
prominently to the original and propagated through actually controlled channels;
independent republishers retain their actual duties, not a new global deletion order.
The chief can disagree in a distinct response. The role can enforce that publication
process within the statute, not make a political interpretation incontestable,
ban coverage, penalize outside media or decide a person's compensation/guilt.

**PM21 — Outside judgment and actual execution.** Affected people retain direct
ordinary B/PD/WL/PC/MP rights, protected evidence/counsel and useful-time B17 relief.
Existing ordinary courts decide actual legality, privacy, injury and source-law
correction/damages, with normal proof/defense, not desired political balance.
Prior restraint requires its actual narrow legal grounds and urgency safeguards;
criticism, ordinary factual error or refusal to praise government creates no new
“false news” crime or broad injunction. Public journalism grants no immunity for
separately proved unlawful acts. State agencies cannot use investigation to silence
sources or transfer official-abuse evidence into the implicated command.

Name actual officer, source/payer, due correction/payment/access and independent
proof of receipt under B/E/OP/T. AA audits public money, EV authenticates statistics,
competent courts adjudicate rights; none chooses editorial truth. A complaints
editor's own misconduct uses actual outside ordinary law/PM06, not self-review.
One fifth Assembly can seek existing lawful scrutiny, with public individual
standing unaffected; a parliamentary committee cannot command a named story.

**PM22 — Evaluate more than audiences or government's satisfaction.** Report actual
reach, excluded groups, independently supported original reporting/correction,
source uncertainty, privacy, workplace/fallback competence, cost and executed
remedies; include people who never used the service. Ratings, clicks, AI throughput,
formal balance and an improved democracy score are not public consent or proof of
better lives. Preserve actual participation separately from analytic review, and
material disagreements rather than force one consensus narrative. A user need
not share intimate beliefs to criticize framing or choose another outlet.

**PM23 — Dates, assets and continuing people.** The compatible implementing reference
commences180calendar days after a valid Act, or a later explicit A9/RR-compatible
component date where a protected emergency/oversight/electoral source is affected.
Preparation hiring/roll/draw/source mandates need their own actual authority and
funds beforehand; they do not certify their own selectors. Publish exact component/
service dates, offices, appointment sources and notice, actual assets/IP contracts,
workers/pay/pensions, sources/safe records, old liabilities and accepted successors.
No readiness certificate postpones an already fixed/due service or extends an
incumbent. Failed paid preparation means non-delivery/failed launch, not a new
Cabinet/founder/editor AI mandate. Actual original software/law/permissions/holds
remain until competent revision; no research rule adopts itself.

**PM24 — A real alternative and reopening.** At four-year statutory review compare
retain/repair, no central public outlet with direct general support, and distributed
community/common provision using the same access/rights, journalism, worker,
source/capacity and paid transition requirements. A legitimately chosen alternative
may replace this form; protected plural public information is not an eternal
corporation or a court's editorial preference. Workers, private rights, archives,
contracts and due claims keep named funded accepting successors. A renamed/recreated
body or transferred newsroom cannot evade actual protected terms/cause; genuine
replacement needs the competent prospective route and applicable RR dates. Reopen on qualified
capture, failed reach/verification/correction, unaffordable/source/court load,
workplace domination or a better actual rival. Full common-controller capture or
missing independent receivers remains failed control, not an AI's rescue mandate.
Next complete the ranked ordinary health restriction/power choice from the country
review, unless urgent evidence or a larger national omission changes priority.

[AX15–AX17](../../C03/18-peaceful-assembly-and-public-dissent.md) now supplies
common public-dissent protection: precise actual substantive source, ordinary
court, and heightened grave-harm proof for advance/provisional suppression,
including already-published material pending final merits. PM21's criticism/
error/no false-news protection remains; final particular remedies need substantive
proof/defenses and useful appeal. No general editorial-truth or network veto.
