## Diagnosis and substantive alternatives

The design-64 reserve target instantly adds 538 qualified people at annual
pay 21.45. Its own limits did not establish training or actual qualification.
A bottleneck can be missing skilled time, instruction, access, materials,
maintenance, demand or institutional power, not merely money. A favorable
GDP/financial identity supplies none of these by itself.

Compare retain/repair, filling posts from an existing qualified pool, paid
domestic learning with bounded public capability, and purchase/partnership.
Retain plural ordinary enterprise and **select N01–N12** as the public
capability path: elected purpose/risk, protected paid learning, independent
acceptance, demonstrated net technological gains and continuing renewal.
It extends the existing economic/time selection, not the number of sovereign
offices. AI planning and forecast savings are rejected as current source or
acceptance evidence, not as possibilities for safe future useful tools.

Public capability may support repair, research, common knowledge, supplier
diversity and training; commercial firms/cooperatives retain ordinary choices
under general law. Central direction could sometimes coordinate resources
but also concentrates framing and access; pure market purchase can be quicker
but needs actual willing supply/continuity and may externalize harm. Neither
is excluded by an ownership label. Public authority, consent and received
capability are distinct from expert project selection and bookkeeping.

## Objections, authority and continuity

This architecture can be slow, bureaucratic, capture-prone and harmful to
useful private work; public training can produce wrong skills or depend on
an irreplaceable credential gatekeeper. The fixed wage/qualification/ratio
choices are normative/authored, not professional standards or an optimal
calibration. Purchased capacity or repair may be better. Public discovery
can fail honestly; it must not conceal harm or turn failure into corruption
charges merely to preserve a success metric. No actual participant dissent
has been obtained; these are author-identified objections, not participation.

Assembly law/appropriation defines public purpose and exposure; existing
cabinet/sector/local duties, professional safety and independent courts
retain separate powers. Review appointment, money, source custody, vendors
and execution together. N creates no central AI mandate or guardian veto.
The public can challenge the model and method through existing routes;
newer prose or a funding milestone cannot adopt its own authority.

E/T/I/Y/U and actual source-law/software boundaries continue. A missed
training/receiving start is a duty/failure, not authority to delay rights,
license unsafe staff, seize pensions or extend executive tenure. Map wages,
contracts, prior jobs, records, review and legal successors through change.
Future turnover and pension/service demands require ongoing qualified
replacement; the known annual programme components 60.5 are not the full
steady-state cost. Actual renewal, private output, physical/FX and all
37-function quantities/costs/remedies remain unknown.
