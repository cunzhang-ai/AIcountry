# C06. Democratic budgets that govern actual public commitments

*Design309 · DB01–DB36 and BCV01–BCV26 · Proposed democratic budget duties.*

**Choose an amendable Assembly budget with explicit programme, commitment and
payment authority, limited execution discretion and public correction.** Cabinet
prepares a whole offer; any actual member can propose alternatives. Existing
Treasury/Assembly staff, committees, AA and courts perform distinct duties.
No fiscal guardian, mandatory balanced budget, new tax rate or automatic source.

Assembly appropriation, member proposals, protected opposition, four payment
controls, standing essential authority and the separate90-day bridge already
exist. The preserved manuscript also has120/60-day preparation, monthly/quarterly
reporting and explicit carry-forward. DB reconciles those predecessors and fills
ordinary amendment/authorization/execution gaps, rather than inventing them all.

Budget law cannot quietly amend an entitlement, postpone an election, expand
emergency power, lend restricted pension cash or authorize another polity's
currency. An appropriation is not money, a safe worker or delivered protection.
Actual constitutions, software, permissions and holds remain until lawful change.

[Country selection and cases](../../settlement/budget-government/README.md)
retains Cabinet-envelope, more detailed legislative and participatory rivals.
Actual complete costs/receipts/skills, independent execution, affected authority
and better lives remain unverified; a positive founding source package remains
unselected within the distinct new-settlement alternative.
