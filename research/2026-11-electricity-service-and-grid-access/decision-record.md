[Inspection](sources.md) · [Worked comparison](../settlement/electricity-service/README.md)

Repair of existing institutions could reduce transition burden and preserve
useful organization. A vertically integrated public utility could remove a
layer of counterparty failure while concentrating owner and operational
authority. A market-led plan may use price and flexible service more widely,
but needs an honest common network account, a priced essential floor and
strong review. The selection favors separate system control and practical
service continuity within the current plural design. Readiness criteria
can favor better-resourced applicants. Support accessible proofs and
protected dates, publish the criteria, provide meaningful challenge and
price real capacity instead of accepting paper assurances. Separate
dispatch, seller and evaluator, with independently funded oversight
and original safe evidence. These safeguards cannot by themselves resolve
capture, utility underinvestment or the cost of paid fallback.

Power disruption can leave real public control or bodily protection unmet.
Prior elected classes and engineering limits govern the conduct within
scope. Existing election officers, courts and other actual authorities
retain their duties and the power to respond within their own law.
Neither rapid restoration nor an aggregate reliability metric settles
exclusion or validates an election.
