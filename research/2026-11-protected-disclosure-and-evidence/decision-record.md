# Design202 decision. Give a serious report a route past its subject

*9 November 2026 · Research selection · Exact design201 predecessor.*

## Gap and selection

EV and AA protect original sources and outside challenge, and
WL, PD, GA and other sector rules refer to protected reporting.
The common enforceable case was underspecified when a report,
its subject, the worker's contract and the source system fall
under different bodies. Select WB01–WB12 as a civil common floor
for serious wrongdoing. Allow an external route without internal
exhaustion and preserve anonymous entry, evidence and a tested
defense. Use existing lawful sector investigation and independent
courts for retaliation and effective relief. No new general
regulator, criminal offense or truth monopoly is created.
[Canonical choice](../country/C08/31-protected-disclosure-and-evidence.md)
· [Exact predecessor](baseline.json)

Select reasonable grounds at the time of the report, proof of
a contributing protected factor on the balance of probabilities,
and a clear and convincing same-action defense for that measure.
Civil and criminal standards remain distinct. A covered public
disclosure is proportionate to the interest and actual risks.
The seven-day acknowledgement, 30-day status and 60-day update
are design hypotheses. They do not shift B17, AP, safety or
other earlier duties, mandate a final investigation date or
guarantee actual receiving capacity.

## Original evidence, alternatives and objections

The European Union directive supplies scoped external and public
routes, confidentiality, a retaliation presumption and protection
of defense. The U.S. federal personnel statute supplies the
contributing-factor and same-decision alternative. The official
OSC account documents a provisional stay and does not establish
final relief or pay. In Halet the Grand Chamber weighed the
public interest, competing harms and proportionality of the
sanction in one case. The proposed common scope and civil test
are new choices, without a claim of imported law or superiority.

· [Matched and adverse cases](../settlement/protected-disclosure/02-care-pay-and-evidence.md)

Prefer strong retain/repair when existing lawful channels actually
correct evidence and protect people. A single national receiver
can clarify entry but would concentrate identifiers and intake.
Public disclosure by default can defeat a captured process and
expose unrelated people's private lives. Choose multiple existing
receivers, direct timely relief, a narrow public disclosure test
and independently controlled records. The objections include
strategic allegations, suppressed exculpatory evidence, retained
private material and cost. Apply fair hearing, specific evidence,
source limits, proportionate triage and review of the receiver.
A correction must reach its lawful decision-maker without
granting a veto to a court, audit office, evidence service or AI.

## Continuing authority and reopening

Preserve existing WL, B17–B20, P/K, SR, EV, AA, PD, GA, media,
classified information, privileges and actual remedies and
ongoing duties. Protect confidential sources without preventing
an effective defense. Adopt with lawful funding and named
independent actual payment and evidence routes under paid OP/T
and HT. A report supplies neither official permission, factual
proof, public funding nor immunity for unrelated misconduct.

No actual affected-party participation, qualified independent
review, legal adoption or funded receiving was established.
Test one real protected report through initial evidence custody,
an unimplicated investigation, effective order or source
correction and actual pay or protection, with continuing service
for affected people. Reopen on credible chilling, record abuse,
missed relief or an equally effective less burdensome alternative.
