# Country case: resources, care and access beyond applicants

*Canonical CS05–CS07; part of [C01/03](../03-country-case-and-competing-designs.md).*

**CS05 — Cash supports agency; its financing and supply still matter.** Received
personal resources can reduce hardship and patron dependence while preserving
choice. In the studied rural cash setting, consumption/assets increased with little
measured inflation and no detected overall labor-supply reduction; this does
not establish zero effects elsewhere. An externally
financed one-off rural transfer is not a national recurrent tax/FX programme,
care shift, income taper estimate or fiscal multiplier to spend in advance.
I05's assessment/taper and NP's progressive financing remain conditional design
choices, not identified by that treatment. Compare universal receipt and other
real alternatives if assessment exclusion/burden outweighs the chosen savings.

Housing, food, work costs, controlled accounts and missing support can defeat a
cash award's purpose. Inspect actual resources/receipt, price/supply, distribution,
voluntary work/saving and burdens rather than force household spending to balance
GDP. Income, independent status and usable services can complement each other;
their full combination has not been empirically validated here.

**CS06 — Services can protect one dimension without proving every benefit.**
Insurance evidence can show financial protection/access and mental-health effects
alongside increased spending and no detected short-run improvement in selected
physical outcomes. A null estimate is not proof of zero benefit; neither an access
increase nor a favorable biomarker is a complete measure of life. Medical spending
is a resource cost, not automatically a saving or complete social cost.

Care/learning offers need actual quality, consent, suitable hours, relationships,
child/recipient development, health and worker conditions. Attendance, parental
employment, an accepted place or payroll is intermediate evidence. Adverse
childcare policy evidence challenges an automatic child-benefit assumption; it
does not justify withdrawing optional childcare or claim every provider harms
children. S10's 40-hour/48-week offer remains an authored contestable reference,
not a proven optimum or desired usage. The public/prepaid ownership choice needs
its own comparison: no read study identifies NP's ownership mix as best.

**CS07 — A remedy that nobody reaches is not effective access.** Information
and application help can raise take-up while leaving important unmet needs and
changing who enrolls. A successful-application count cannot establish that the
neediest people were reached. Waiting for a denied claim/appeal misses people
who never apply or stop midway. Existing programme owners therefore fund safe
outreach/help and examine eligible nonclaimants/nonusers, without presuming
eligibility or forcing uptake.

Distinguish voluntary refusal, inaccessible intake, missing proof, unavailable
service and unknown need. Nonresponse is not refusal, lack of need or fraud.
Use lawful minimum data and voluntary safe contact; no universal dossier or
unsafe disclosure to a controlling family member. Sampled/qualitative findings
retain uncertainty/missing groups; no promise to observe everyone. Courts and
individual claims keep their legal duties regardless of what a survey finds.

Retain complementary personal cash and actual essential receiving conditionally.
Reopen I/S/Y on hidden exclusion, repeated proof/travel, unwanted care, adverse
child/recipient outcomes, harmful tax/rent incidence or a better funded rival.
Actual affected accounts and current full quantities/sources are still missing.
