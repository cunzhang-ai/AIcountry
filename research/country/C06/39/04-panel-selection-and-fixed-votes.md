# Three panel members, fixed votes and finite reserves

Design 99 · Proposed research, not enacted law or funded bank protection.

**RA01 — Three fixed seats and exact property competence.** The distinct statutory Bank Resolution
Authority exercises only M09's actual/next30day inability or net-realizable-insolvency finding, no
credible timely cure, and necessity over ordinary liquidation to preserve critical lawful functions;
a ratio/AI/ministerial alarm is insufficient. It may use only enacted M09/M10 transfer, bounded
bridge, eligible write-down/conversion and narrow termination stay, with M11 compensation/review.
M07 insured-deposit unavailability is a different trigger and does not require that
critical-functions test. Prudential supervisor supplies original accounts; monetary authority
decides only its own lawful solvent-bank facility; Treasury executes only authorized sources. No
panel sets new coverage, levies, taxes, guarantees, debt, FX conversion, bank licensing law, general
loan policy or election/payment eligibility.

Three paid members have fixed six-year **seat** terms, one expiry every two years. Label
legal-insolvency/rights, valuation/balance-sheet risk and payment/operational continuity seats.
Before inaugural nominations, publicly draw these labels onto initial two/four/six-year slots and
publish the common seat-calendar start/expiry dates. Delayed appointment consumes the fixed slot,
never restarts six years; a midterm replacement serves only the remaining term. All primary holders,
including short inaugural terms, are nonrenewable. No holdover/cabinet acting member. The seat
labels require each holder's particular demonstrated competence; every member must also understand
M09/M10/M11 grounds, basic financial sources, fair process and public accountability. Require ten
years relevant financial law, insolvency, valuation/accounting, banking risk, payment operations or
equivalent demonstrated work, safe language/access accommodation and practical availability; no sole
guild/degree/bank nomination list or AI score owns qualification.

No current minister/legislator, senior party executive/campaign manager, paid lobbyist, bank
director/senior manager or actual bank controller serves; require four years since those roles. No
simultaneous prudential decision, monetary/Treasury management, insurer executive/claims decision,
bank representation or receiver role. Disclose financial/family/sponsor/prior-case interests and use
actual recusal/divestment; ordinary advocacy/party membership is not loyalty proof.
Expertise/cooling/role labels may reduce the willing pool and are contestable, not evidence of
staffed independence.

**RA02 — Open pool, recorded public appointment.** Existing Assembly recruitment staff advertise
each primary/reserve place at least30calendar days, accept direct consenting applications and verify
published job criteria using disclosed qualified advice. Publish all eligible nominees, reasoned
exclusions and safe conflicts at least14calendar days before the decisive vote; any Assembly member
may nominate an eligible applicant, and public hearing/usable objections/reasoned response precede a
single-name recorded vote. Each primary/reserve requires ceil(2N/3) affirmative votes of full
statutory Assembly membership including vacancies, independently for each place; no
absence/abstention lowers it. If several pass for one place, a further single-choice ballot still
needs that fixed threshold. Publish proposed appointment/qualification at least21calendar days
before entry. Ordinary B16 appointment/exclusion challenge is60working days from usable
publication/notice with blocked-access/unfairness and concealed-fraud/material-conflict protection;
useful-time B17 relief is earlier, no automatic stay or term extension.

Open scheduled processes180days before seat expiry, report deficiencies by90days and failure/hearing
by30days; unexpected vacancy within14days. First ballot within30days after eligible list, repeat at
least every30days while a competent Assembly can sit, reopen applications after two failed ballots.
No plurality, executive emergency appointment, source transfer or silence fallback. No legitimate
Assembly/qualified appointees/funded administration means actual formation fails; research/founder
or the panel itself cannot bootstrap a sovereign vote. Exact actual sources and start dates require
C14/competent adoption, not a claim that existing people already consented.

**RA03 — Fixed two votes; one finite reserve each.** Every M09 entry, chosen
transfer/bridge/write-down, material amendment, extension within existing law, official termination
stay and panel administrative appointment requires at least two lawful members actually present and
**two affirmative votes of the fixed three** on the exact finding and disposition. Safe remote
participation needs actual simultaneous access/understanding/authentication, not an unexamined
proxy/AI vote. Chair has no casting vote or private assignment veto. Choose a presiding
administrator for a two-year term, with no consecutive reappointment and never beyond the holder's
own seat expiry by the same vote; if none, published seat rotation supplies necessary meeting
administration only. Source material/reasons and dissent are recorded; two incompatible positions
cannot be combined by a clerk.

One named consenting reserve per seat, separately selected by RA02 with the same competence and
expiry, may serve only after established temporary inability/recusal or permanent vacancy, never
simultaneously with its primary. Permanent replacement consumes that remaining seat term; no new six
years. After exercising panel authority a person cannot receive another primary/reserve seat term;
succession within their own designated remaining term is allowed. Recheck actual eligibility,
conflicts, paid standby/ordinary work released and availability every six months and before
activation. Unserved reserve has no future entitlement. If primary/reserve cannot serve, seat
unavailable; replenishment uses ordinary full process for remaining term, never an ad hoc
pending-case substitute. No deputy/career officer, inspector, judge, minister or bank representative
supplies a missing panel vote.

**RA04 — Recusal, removal and external judgment.** A member does not adjudicate their own contested
direct conflict; two unchallenged lawful members can determine it by the fixed rule, otherwise a
competent unconflicted ordinary court decides with useful-time relief. Allegation alone, naming all
members, diffuse fiscal interest or disagreement does not disqualify everyone. On ordinary-standing
claimant,1/5full Assembly or competent integrity-investigator petition, ordinary courts may remove
for proved serious misconduct, persistent material statutory breach or inability after reasonable
accommodation, with notice, defense, safe source disclosure, reasoned B17 judicial suspension and
ordinary appeal. Unwelcome lawful losses, honest error/reversal, dissent or a new cabinet is no
ground. Removal takes effect after unused appeal time or one ordinary appeal decision, subject to
competent further review/stays; natural seat expiry remains fixed despite pending removal. Actual
inability activates only the finite lawful reserve. No new appointee double-votes during
suspension/review.

**RA05 — Professional staff prepare and execute; they do not become a panel.** Panel appoints a
career operational secretary by two votes through C04 open merit/qualified shortlist; panel is that
secretary's C04 appointing/disciplinary authority with proved grounds, usable notice/reply and
direct ordinary review. Cabinet cannot remove/reassign the secretary for a case. Secretary
recruits/assigns/disciplines qualified investigation, valuation-source, legal, records and
operational staff under C04 and prior published competence/rotation/conflict rules; no outcome-based
quotas or ad hoc ministerial team. Staff prepare alternatives, preserve sources, initiate competent
court applications and execute only actual lawful decisions. A single investigator cannot certify
their own valuation as independent. Panel can demand lawful general progress/quality, not a
preferred fact or retaliatory reassignment. Direct conflicts use predesignated qualified career
substitutes and ordinary courts.

Secretary designates a qualified career administrative deputy through C04 before vacancy. On
secretary vacancy, deputy has≤180elapsed days routine funded management, no new policy/wider
competence/senior appointment; thereafter own enacted career functions only. If absent/conflicted
from day1, existing officers immediately retain only their own duties; missing reserved management
work is disclosed. For first establishment, an express prior Act may assign Assembly recruitment
staff/Career Clerk C04 open competitions and reasoned appointments of funded nonmanagerial statutory
case staff only, not panel, secretary or an emergency property decider. Actual qualified
staff/pay/courts are unknown, and designation is not availability.

**RA06 — No-quorum failure, not authority by necessity.** Below two lawful members or two agreeing
votes, no M09 panel decision exists. Publish safely which grounds/actions remain unresolved, source
evidence, ordinary appointment duty and next lawful consideration. Staff may not transfer/write
down/create a bridge under an old ratio warning or invented emergency delegation. Ordinary courts
retain their adopted U liquidation, preservation, M07 entitlement/omission and actual B rights
powers; they do not manufacture two votes or acquire parallel structural M09 entry without a
separately chosen prior Act. Preserve M06/M07, SF accepted payment routes and actual due claims; no
missed vote extends a panel term, creates money or permits a deposit holiday. This can leave
critical functions insufficiently protected; record the failure rather than create an unaccountable
temporary banker-state.
