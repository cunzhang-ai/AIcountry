# C14. Democratic repair without self-authorization

*Integrated design 83 · Proposed RR01–RR21; not enacted law.*

The country needs both reliable limits on incumbent rule changes and an ability
to restore rights before another exclusionary contest. A universal delay can
protect the very exclusion it should prevent. A broad “democracy repair” power
can instead legitimate court packing. This reference retains structural delay
with a closed exception; it gives neither ministers nor AI a repair mandate.

2. [Prior-law review, electorate and election timing](08/02-review-electorate-and-time.md): RR05–RR07.
3. [Continuing obligations, migration and alternatives](08/03-transition-and-limits.md): RR08–RR10.
4. [Cumulative impairment of democratic control](08/04-cumulative-impairment-of-democratic-control.md): RR11–RR17.
5. [Shared budget safeguards](08/05-democratic-safeguards-in-a-shared-budget.md): RR18–RR21.

The ordinary [public amendment route](01/02-public-constitutional-change.md)
retains its proposal/signature/voting thresholds and at least 90 days on the
exact final text. [CJ17](../C03/08/09-transition-and-reconsideration.md) governs
creation and transfer of courts; a new court cannot approve itself in advance.
[Finite consultation](../C12/03-finite-attention-and-lawful-closure.md) cannot
replace ratification, mandatory hearings or timely individual rights relief.

[Cases and real rivals](../../settlement/democratic-revision/README.md) retain
blanket delay and broader constituent repair as different choices, including
the cost of refusing the latter when an old system is already captured.
[C06 capacity](../C06/32-democratic-repair-and-public-attention-capacity.md)
traces appointment, records, money, opposition, review and execution. Actual
independent competence, source finance, adoption and better lives are unknown.
