# Design231 decision record. Clean air and cumulative exposure

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned design 230](baseline.json)

## Decision and scope

The existing country requires an air floor and cumulative permitting
but does not fully assign a place-specific duty when current source
conditions coexist with unhealthy ambient air. Select
[AQ01–AQ10](../country/C09/24-clean-air-and-cumulative-exposure.md)
to enact an enforceable ambient floor, protect receiving locations
at new permissions, and require a funded correction of existing
exceedance. Keep acute exposure and its actual protection visible
through the separate lawful response. Use the
[working method](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md). Actual enactment and
budget remain competent elected decisions.

Equal practical protection and the inability to export a prohibited
burden are design commitments. Measurements, attribution, feasible
alternatives and the cost of moving toward stricter limits are
empirical. Set exact pollutant thresholds and verification rules
through the applicable law using real place evidence. The proposed
15- and 90-day ordinary response periods need separately qualified
capacity. They never extend an earlier safety duty.

## Alternatives and objections

| Alternative | Strong case | Material objection |
|---|---|---|
| Improve monitoring and enforce existing source permits | Keeps existing legal tools, manageable investigation and clear individual responsibility. | Several lawful sources and a background contribution can leave a local breach without an adequate regional response. |
| Use broad emissions prices or uniform controls | Can reduce large amounts with fewer source-by-source bargains. | Can leave or displace serious local harm. Revenue or aggregate improvement cannot establish attainment or fair costs. |
| Enforce a place-specific floor with shared correction and local delivery | Assigns responsibility for cumulative and displaced harm. Allows different local means within the same protection. | Requires independent measurements, trained staff, useful review and paid clean alternatives. Local objections can delay real improvements if scope is unclear. |
| Centralize all pollution, transport and heating decisions | A central body could coordinate a coherent response across boundaries. | Concentrates authority and can ignore local exposure, worker conditions or alternatives. Shared evidence and bounded correction can join the work without transferring unrelated powers. |

Select the third option. Retain sound existing source controls and
use a broad price or national programme only where it works with
the receiving-place rules. The
[two-neighbourhood case](../settlement/clean-air/02-two-neighbourhoods-and-a-shared-limit.md)
compares the same pollutant, service and cash scope and leaves
uncertain attainment visible. It supplies no national engineering
or environmental effect estimate.

## Public control and continuing claims

The Assembly sets the standard, jurisdiction and appropriations.
Existing environmental authorities own monitoring and correction,
local authorities own their actual service routes, and public
health and rescue own the immediate receiving response within law.
Independent custody, conflict-tested review, protected finance and
court execution must work against a captured operator or ministry.
Residents and workers can report absent evidence or harmful transfer
through actual direct routes. Evidence, source responsibility,
individual liability and actual receipt are distinct.

Preserve valid permits, restrictions, worker and property rights,
records and pending claims through funded FT/OP/T succession.
Use existing safe housing, electricity, health, transport and
remedy protections through any phase-in. Courts can enforce an
existing duty and useful timetable without choosing an unrelated
budget or operating means. New permits and material restrictions
need their own lawful authority and actual review.
