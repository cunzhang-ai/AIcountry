# Design329 decision on concentrated job loss and local recovery

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design328, AT, MF, LD, portable benefits, housing, industrial
closure and the working framework. Existing duties already respond
to income loss, early local finance gaps and employer liabilities.
Add a joint, place-specific choice that protects personal claims and
viable staying and moving on the same actual work and budget calendar.
Preserve local elected power over delivery. The project assistant
authors the choice. Legitimate elected institutions and affected people
retain their respective decisions.

## Alternatives and evidence

Compare portable assistance alone, retaining the anchor through a
targeted rescue, and preserving basic services while supporting optional
mobility and qualified diversification. Mobility can reach better work
but can transfer housing and care needs to an unready host. A rescue can
protect existing work and assets but can deepen dependence and divert
money needed for current rights. Local diversification can retain
community ties but requires real accepted work and finance. Select
first-period service and personal protection, then a separately decided
and evidenced recovery portfolio. Do not guarantee an individual employer
or permanent operation of every facility.

The [primary comparators](sources.md) document place-based transition
and the need to measure outcomes of worker assistance. They do not
establish local causal effects or verify the authored comparison.

## Authority, migration and reopening

Use existing AT clocks, local and national appropriations and ordinary
rights. Local authorities select lawful provision. A host authority
accepts actual incremental work and receives its legal source. The
person controls an optional move. Existing wages, social protection,
tenure, appeals, creditor and site duties keep their separate law.
Use elected recurring finance to replace an enduring service gap and
protect national and local budget authority. No extra unfunded task
arises from this research selection.

Exercise one real closure or contraction using original local receipts,
worker and supplier coverage, actual host and staying options, service
operators, local opposition and consenting residents. Track actual
suitable work, income, care and service receipt among people who stay
and leave. Price two periods and verify any promised source independently.
Reopen on coerced movement, recurring unfunded duties, lost capacity,
a concentrated successor or a better working alternative. Actual
adoption, financing, affected choices and outcomes remain open.
