# TX07–TX12: independent merits, refund and finite collection

*Part of [C06/40](../40-tax-law-and-accountable-revenue.md); design100 research candidate, not enacted law.*

**TX07 — Two real review routes.** A taxpayer or directly affected owner/payee may request an optional internal full-facts-and-law review within B16's ordinary 60 working days of usable final notice. A preassigned qualified reviewer employed/assigned by the separately protected Tax Merits Review Head, not the assessing Revenue Director, can cancel, decrease or increase only after fresh notice and the same TX03 answer, correct a credit, order a refund and bind the Revenue Service/Treasury within enacted law. The reviewer decides within B16's 30 working days of protected first receipt of the review request, with one necessary, timely notified and reasoned extension of at most 30 working days under AP05; a missed date preserves immediate direct court delay/merits relief and cannot become a default affirmation. The assessor cannot review their own case, choose their reviewer, withhold protected original evidence, cancel the review budget or veto a correction. The taxpayer may instead go directly to an unconflicted ordinary court for full tax **merits**, not legality alone, within the same 60 working days, with B16 blocked-access/unfairness protection and B17 useful-time relief; no internal exhaustion, filing bond, disputed-tax prepayment or official case permission. A timely internal filing preserves direct court access for60working days from usable review decision, without a serial process demanded by the state. The disputed-increment collection stay also covers that entire court-filing window; a timely court filing continues it through final ordinary merits and applicable timely ordinary-appeal filing/review windows. No new compulsory collection during those windows merely because an administrative decision says final; if no timely next ordinary challenge is filed, the stay ends on expiry. This does not reset TX12’s original outer bar. The court can correct amount/credits/refund, order source disclosure and due execution; it cannot enact a rate, tax base or Treasury overdraft.

**TX08 — Refund finding, payment and interest.** On an ordinary return/refund request, give an exact essential missing-item notice within five working days. A statutory claim sufficiently proved by the return, lawful withholding credits and official/payer-held records, with no specific evidenced counterfact, is **undisputed**: the qualified claims officer authorizes and the named Treasury career payer pays it as soon as possible and no later than ten working days from first sufficient receipt, including a proved partial amount while another item remains disputed. A lawful versioned calculation may assist and execute this reversible positive payment; an AI risk flag or missing record already held by a public/payer custodian cannot hold every refund. An adverse or genuinely disputed remainder receives B16's reasoned decision within 30 working days of protected first receipt of the request, with one timely notified and documented material-need extension of at most 30 working days under AP05, and B13/B17 direct review. A favorable reviewer/court correction is paid within ten working days of the decision or earlier useful-time right. Silence is no deemed zero refund; unavailable Treasury cash is an arrear, not cancellation. The Act sets simple noncompounding refund interest at an annual rate of **min(5%, max(0, the prior calendar year's independently published average domestic sovereign borrowing yield for maturities at most one year))**, fixed for that tax year. The initial Act fixes 2% until the first valid prior-year series; if a later series is missing, carry the last lawful rate. For an ordinary excess annual withholding/credit, interest starts at the applicable ten-working-day undisputed due date or the disputed 30(+one30)-decision-plus-ten-payment deadline, whichever applies to that proved amount, even if the office remains silent. For tax later proved unlawfully exacted, interest starts on its actual debit/receipt date. Interest ends only on actual refund receipt; accrue principal × the applicable calendar year’s annual rate × actual unpaid days /365(or366 for a leap year), split at year boundaries, without interest on interest, and is an appropriated public cost, not a penalty, source of finance or inferred bank overdraft. Additional proved actual loss needs its own B/M source and no duplicate interest recovery.

**TX09 — Disputed principal and neutral late interest.** A timely, adequately identified TX07 merits challenge or evidenced correction of a mistaken self-assessment automatically stays coercive collection of the **actually disputed principal**, including a genuine corrected self-declared amount or an additional assessment, through final ordinary merits disposition **and the applicable TX07 ordinary filing windows**, without extending TX12’s outer bar; it does not delay the separately undisputed due balance or ordinary withheld amounts already lawfully owed. No pay-first percentage, security bond or automatic bank freeze is a condition for being heard. The court can narrow an abusive claim to its actually disputed amount on reasons and preserve specific nonexempt property under TX10. On an undisputed overdue tax sum, use the same TX08 simple rate from its statutory due date to actual payment. For an additional finally upheld assessment, neutral interest on only that upheld principal runs from the original statutory due date to usable service of the proposed assessment, except for periods caused solely by the authority's own error, blocked taxpayer access or reasonable reliance on then-published competent official guidance; the independent reviewer/court tests disputed reliance on the original text and facts, not the agency's later label. This time-value accrual is **not an earlier principal due date**. For an actually timely good-faith challenge, the interest pause includes the ordinary proposal/reply period and **all linked TX07 ordinary filing windows**, even when the person files on a permitted later day; no interim filing-window charge survives that timely challenge. If no next ordinary challenge is timely filed, interest resumes from the applicable already-set30-working-day post-decision payment date, without a new TX12 trigger. Through actual timely ordinary merits it remains paused and resumes only after the30-working-day post-final payment date if unpaid; a court may restore neutral time-value interest for a specifically proved deliberate dilatory abuse, never merely because the taxpayer loses. No surcharge, compound interest, administrative fine or punitive daily charge arises by analogy to V; K22/Q rules need their own precise acts/proof. A taxpayer's actual inability, inaccessible payment channel or state-caused error receives reasoned installment/correction relief and protected U03 minimum needs, not debt custody.

**TX10 — Specific court preservation, not a collection shortcut.** Pending a timely merits case, the authority may ask a B17 duty court to preserve identified nonexempt assets only on original evidence of a substantial likely lawful tax claim and a concrete imminent intentional dissipation risk that ordinary later collection cannot remedy. The court hears the taxpayer and affected third parties, protects B14 sources, U03 subsistence buffer, valid security, client property, employee wages, pension assets belonging to others and due G/S/social public funds; no gross-bank-balance or one-click all-account freeze. A truly necessary ex parte order has B17's hearing within 72 hours and one necessary 72-hour extension; preservation expires within 90 calendar days unless a court, on fresh proof, extends once for at most 90 further days. No renamed case/asset/vendor restarts this total; the court may shorten, substitute a genuinely affordable security arrangement or release when risk ends. It does not transfer money to Treasury, decide guilt or authorize home entry, seizure or travel control. A completed final tax claim uses TX11 rather than serial preservation.

**TX11 — Paid collection and property boundary.** For a final or undisputed due tax, give a plain account of principal, lawful simple interest, valid payments/credits, actual payer and review. For a newly changed/estimated incremental assessment, **no compulsory collection before the60-working-day TX07 challenge window expires**, even though voluntary payment/principal due may be30working days after notice; a timely challenge keeps the actual disputed increment stayed through ordinary merits. During that window use only TX10's specifically proved court preservation, without transfer to Treasury. Do not treat silence at day30 as waiver/admission; the rule leaves genuinely admitted self-assessed/withheld debt due. Allow at least20working days of usable payment/plan opportunity and do not begin compulsory collection before both the applicable due date and this review protection; the authority may negotiate a documented means-based installment plan and receive voluntary payment, with no seizure quota or recovered-amount commission. A same-person tax refund may be offset only against a separately final undisputed tax debt under an exact statute with notice, first preserving the Tax Act's expressly adopted U03 three months of verified personal needs and independently due work/child/social claims; no set-off against someone else's account, M05 client property, restricted G/S funds, pension assets belonging to others or a contested amount. Nonconsensual bank garnishment, receivable diversion, lien execution, physical seizure or sale requires an independent B18 court order identifying final claim, actual debtor/title, item, limited amount, priority and affected third parties, with fair valuation, notice and direct challenge. A residence needs C07 safe receiving; necessary disability goods, three-month personal buffer and proportionate work tools are exempt under U03/B18. Corporate assets needed for already due worker/client obligations require their actual priority and safe receiver; ordinary tax arrears do not jump U04/M10 ranks. No algorithmic sweep, private bailiff coercion, travel ban, custody, forced labor or LF force without separate prior law. Necessary reasonable actual collection costs need court review and no percentage bounty.

**TX12 — A single collection endpoint and lawful migration.** Compulsory tax collection,**including execution of any tax judgment or preservation order**, ends six calendar years after the first usable lawful quantification and payment-due event for the same principal: the statutory due date for an admitted self-assessed/remitted amount, or 30 working days after the first usable final administrative assessment of a later additional amount. A timely merits challenge stays disputed collection but does not restart or automatically suspend this outer period; a later court judgment, amended calculation of the same obligation, part payment, reminder, installment, new collector, bank order or renamed claim also does not restart it. A court may exclude only time actually proved by the authority to involve the debtor's deliberate evasion of usable lawful notice or specifically ordered collection, up to two extra years and never beyond eight years from the original event. Good-faith review, inability to pay, authority/court backlog, change of office or ordinary foreign delay is not evasion. At expiry, no new or continued coercive recovery, set-off, interest or fee may be imposed for that principal; release any unexecuted freeze/garnishment. Credit value already **actually received** through lawful pre-expiry execution, preserve innocent completed transfers and return any excess. An installment schedule cannot silently waive the outer bar or authorize post-bar compulsory collection; any voluntary payment after expiry has no enforced-tax status and requires a separate lawful treatment. A timely assessed claim can be corrected or refunded under its own law; the collection bar creates no new tax or K22 guilt. Map valid legacy limitation/judgment interests prospectively rather than extinguishing them by research text.

For a corrected self-assessment, the ultimately upheld original admitted principal keeps its original statutory due event; a genuinely new additional amount uses its first lawful administrative assessment plus30working days. Invalid demands do not create liability or refresh the same obligation. This **tax-specific outer bar replaces B37 judgment/renewal stacking only for this prospective tax debt**; B37 for other civil judgments remains. A ten-year single outer bar is a live revenue/cross-border rival. Under the selected six/eight-year rule, ordinary court backlog can consume the period and lose public recovery: fund prompt courts and disclose write-offs/incidence rather than shift this risk into perpetual liens. Refund/correction, K22/Q12 and valid U discharge retain their separate law.
