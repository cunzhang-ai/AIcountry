# PI01–PI05. Trace the public decision without licensing the person

**PI01 — Common duties, distinct public owners.** Apply this proposed scheme
to national/local elected members, ministers, career and public-enterprise
actors when exercising public decision functions. Cover identifiable laws,
budgets, general rules, public appointments, grants, contracts and licences.
Existing PC/CF/NI electoral/issue-speech boundaries, SC buying, C04 integrity,
PD private life and competent case procedures remain. A lawful interest or
unpopular argument is not misconduct. No new crime or general private registry.

**PI02 — The office records material receipt and use.** Each Assembly/local/
department public records function keeps its own protected originals and a
public decision record; shared publication may be federated. The Assembly
Clerk administers Assembly records, not Cabinet/local/private case custody.
Record organized representation or materially resourced advocacy received in
connection with the identified decision, including direct informal approaches,
commissioned research, outside drafting and consequential supplied models.
Include the actual requested change, date/participants' public roles, known
represented interest/principal/payer/controller and mandate, supplied material/
version and material use or modification in the decision. An official cannot
hide pre-draft influence by using a private meeting, device or intermediary.

Actual organized representation by a business, union, charity or other group
can be relevant whether compensated or self-interested; salary, association,
shared views or incidental personal AI use alone proves no purchased act,
control or improper bargain. Seek proportionate clarification of a claimed
mandate; mark unknown/disputed origin instead of inventing a donor, consent
or complete history. This is the office's duty, not a pre-contact register,
fee, compulsory representative, viewpoint screen or a requirement that ordinary
people prepare a lobbying dossier before being heard.

**PI03 — Timely useful records, no automatic policy veto.** Enter the material
record promptly and ordinarily within14 calendar days of actual receipt/use;
include it with the actual decision/input packet earlier where existing notice
or useful contrary participation requires. Outside drafts/material changes get
source/version identification, not a claim that the author owns public policy.
Existing legislative/DR/LR/DB/appointment/procurement notice and reply remain.
A later correction cannot restore already lost public opportunity by assertion.
Valid urgent action keeps its own source/expiry; record its grounds and known
influence as soon as practicable, with initial protected record/usable public
gist within24hours and completed ordinary record within14days. These targets
are hypotheses, not source-law clock extensions or emergency powers.

Missing records are a breach subject to correction/actual relief, not automatic
legislation lapse, a Clerk's approval gate or an auditor's power to stop care.
Record substantive dispositions and material reasons, including disagreement;
no verbatim transcript of every conversation, mandatory recording of private
life or exhaustive reconstruction of every idea/model training datum.

**PI04 — Protect help without creating a commercial-case loophole.** Ordinary
constituency help, benefit/clinical/legal representation, protected complaints,
private association, journalism and personal unpaid advocacy need no public
case or relationship log. Lawful case files and necessary source records stay
protected under actual PD/B/P/sector duties. Particular commercial permits,
tax settlements and official intervention in individual adjudication still
have actual conflict, equal-treatment and fair-hearing duties; calling them
private cannot hide preferential access. Use necessary confidential case-file
records and reviewable public substance where lawful, not public client names.
An unpaid owner/director or person speaking 'as a resident' does not thereby
exempt actual organizational representation or a particular commercial benefit
request from PI02 and protected case integrity. Personal civic views alone remain
protected. Political parties' public decision contacts do not gain a covert-access exemption;
ordinary party speech/association retain PC and privacy protections.

**PI05 — Meaning and safe evidence survive new media.** Speech, documents,
agent negotiation, simulations and future media can carry input. Keep actual
attribution/meaning, material assumptions, translation/interpretation loss and
contested versions where lawfully available. A summary/model convergence is
not original assent, a million mandates or independent sources. Sensitive
identity, privilege, confidential business details and source safety need
necessary limited custody, redaction and a meaningful public gist with reasons;
contested limits go to competent unimplicated courts. Actual lawful legislative/
Cabinet deliberation and legal privilege remain; an external paid contribution
does not become privileged merely by entering those deliberations. Neither blanket secrecy
nor automatic public testimony is selected. No universal personal graph or
compelled access to all prompts/weights/private relationships follows.
