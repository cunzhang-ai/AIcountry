# NR13–NR16. A finite stock can become assets, not an imaginary permanent income

*Part of [mineral rights](../15-finite-resources-and-mineral-rights.md).*

**NR13 — Keep the public price, tax and restoration custody separate.** The
identified public finite right has actual contractual consideration: fixed/
quantity/value/price-linked payment terms and risk allocation are stated before
award, with verified extraction/sales/related-party valuation and actual due
currency/payee. No universal royalty rate or new general private-mineral/rent
levy is inferred from ownership; NF37's no-generic-levy choice remains. Existing
TA/NF tax bases/cost/capital/collection law continues without automatic credit,
exemption or spending authority. Actual owner receipts, company profit/tax,
public-enterprise transfers, expenses/claims, closure-purpose security and local
transfers need separate gross records/counterparties; payments never count twice.
Declare actual lawful tax treatment rather than assume gross concession price
is equal net national revenue. Public royalties are not received FX until
actually owned/settled; an in-situ valuation or export promise is no cash source.

**NR14 — Select a replacement-capital default with an explicit present-need choice.**
Show finite stock depletion, uncertainty/irreversibility, received owner cash,
closure/tail liabilities and actual asset/capability replacement separately.
Keep original public-account/statistical classifications; a policy earmark does
not relabel recorded rent as national capital formation or new output. Compute
actual net public consideration for depletion of the identified finite right
under an enacted itemized net/cost/claim rule, with no second deduction of tax
or security already counted separately. Netting is limited to actual resource-
attributable lawful costs and superior
source claims, while showing gross receipts/expenses; restoration/client/
restricted custody remains separate. Before discretionary capital commitment,
apply the actually adopted source-compatible NP11–NP12 payable useful-time
basic/democratic priority and other actual prior source priorities. Record that
current use once as consumption of finite wealth and foregone replacement;
existing authorized priority needs no new discretionary exception. It cannot
sweep pension/bond/third-party restricted assets or turn every ministry bill
into a resource cost/core task. Only the remaining lawfully available owner cash
enters the Treasury replacement-capital account; it may be zero. An insufficient
core remains an unmet duty, not delivered protection or new money.
Default use requires ordinary lawful appropriations for actually justified
public/common durable capability or actual diversified financial claims under
existing mandates, not an autonomous sovereign fund, automatic dividend or
minister's discretionary estate. A capital label, security holding or purchase
of an existing asset does not establish greater real wealth or production.

Beyond those actual already authorized priorities, an express full-statutory-
Assembly-majority primary Act, including an express qualifying annual budget/
appropriation clause, may instead allocate a
bounded amount/period to current essential protection or paid transition, naming
foregone future capacity/asset stock, actual beneficiaries/incidence, end and
credible replacement path or admitted irreversible loss. No permanent office veto
over that lawful value choice, forced saving, automatic principal raid or pledge
of imagined deposits. Ordinary general tax receipts are not swept into this
owner-account rule. This default has a real opportunity cost for present people;
ordinary budget use with scrutiny is a substantive alternative. Finite/volatile
rent never automatically backs permanently growing duties. NS/RF/NP/FP/TN/I05/Y/
TW/service/source law and every due claim retain their own continuing basis.
