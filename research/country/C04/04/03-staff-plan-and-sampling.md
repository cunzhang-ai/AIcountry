# AA11–AA15: audit priorities can be challenged without selecting a friend's case

*Part of [C04/04](../04-public-audit-and-accountable-oversight.md); design121 candidate.*

**AA11 — Statutory staff and exact case responsibility.** AG appoints audit,
quality/records/report and support staff through C04 open competence/merit,
actual conflicts, protected reasoned career process and direct B/OJ review in
its express scope. Commission qualified natural-person statutory audit leads
and a Reports Officer with their own enacted duties, not personal permission
from the chief for every record/publication. Assign teams/quality review by
prepublished skill/availability/conflict rotation; a minister or audited client
cannot choose or remove the named case auditor. General management cannot
retaliate for preserved contrary evidence, protected reporting or lawful dissent.
Actual paid training/time/independent access matters, not separate job labels.

**AA12 — A public funded plan, not universal certification.** Before each annual
period publish lawful mandate, field/financial/risk/complaint work, genuinely
random component, criteria, sample frame/units/probabilities and actual staff/
source/expected coverage in safe form. Mandatory urgent/safety/source-law work
continues rather than waiting for a draw. Parliament funds scope; AG chooses
lawful audit issues, without a minister's named-case instructions or a committee's
private target list. Source shortage is an exposed unperformed duty/component,
not a zero-risk certificate, hidden extra power or an excuse to audit opponents
only. One narrow annual plan is not permission for unlimited staffing/funding.

**AA13 — Public reasons for selection and changes.** People, workers, local bodies,
opposition and human/AI/joint contributors may report issues without a full
technical dossier. Preserve actual accounts and common source/controller links;
account/model counts are not independent witnesses. Give an ordinary request
usable acknowledgment/triage within five working days and selection/referral
reasons within 30 working days, preserving earlier actual urgent duties. A
nonselection is not proof the conduct is lawful or a bar to courts/other reports.
Changes/cancellation of material work state resources, source and displaced
scope; preserve completed findings/evidence and appropriate safe public reasons.
New allegations cannot silently replace the originally sampled awkward case.

**AA14 — A genuine random component.** Commit to a lawfully complete in-scope
frame and declared grouping before an auditable unpredictable draw; preserve
original order and safe ex-post verification. Do not omit unpopular jurisdictions,
closed contracts, AI vendors or split courses to improve the result. Protect
private identifiers/secrets; no universal public transaction/person ledger.
No redraw because of a disliked outcome. A lost/unavailable unit requires
recorded nonresponse and separately justified additional work under the original
method, never silently swapping it for an easy case. Actual future forms and
meaning must be preserved/qualified, not deleted because a schema cannot encode
them. A clerk's committed hash is not proof that the frame was complete.

**AA15 — Do not estimate the country from targeted cases.** Risk/complaint-directed
findings apply to their actual examined scope. Any prevalence/value extrapolation
states original frame coverage, selection probabilities/weights, unit/source
uncertainty, actual nonresponse and non-sampling limitations. Do not average
targeted cases into a representative national corruption rate or count recoverable
book loss as cash. Sampling can miss important harm; institutional discretion,
framing and mediator power remain contestable. Private/company audits or other
inspectors' work can support only their valid complete original scope, not
uninspected shared-source reviews or final decisions on changed facts.
