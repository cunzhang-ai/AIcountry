# WS21–WS25. Keep power and safe water deliverable in the same dry period

*Design262 · Proposed coordinated water, electricity and budget duties.*

**WS21 — Qualify capacity against the same catchment conditions.**
Before contracting firm electricity or approving an operating plan,
the grid operator, plant operator and water steward exchange a
dated, independently checkable operating envelope. Record actual
power and duration at the receiving network, safe startup and
shutdown, gross withdrawal and net depletion, returns by place and
time, downstream flow, temperature, quality and ecological exposure.
Add concurrent water treatment, household cooling, hospital, farm
and other essential needs. Rainfall or an annual water entitlement
does not prove usable water at an intake. The same water can be
physically present and unavailable for another use because of heat,
location or timing. Preserve both plant safety and connected watershed
limits. A change in drought conditions, control or measurements reopens
the affected capacity, dispatch and future contract assumptions.

**WS22 — Keep the test of electrical necessity and the environmental
decision separate.** Under prior law, the competent grid operator
identifies the useful deadline, the protected loads at risk, the smallest
needed increment and actual alternatives. The independent competent
water and environmental authority verifies the receiving reach and
affected rights before making any decision within its own permit scope.
Existing plant safety approval remains separate. An electricity request
alone cannot vary a limit. Establish in advance which protections are
nonwaivable and whether any operating bands are lawfully adjustable.
A shortage, model recommendation or elected spending decision creates
no waiver. If a valid pre-existing procedure allows a narrowly scoped
adjustment, verify both the need and the receiving conditions,
including downstream and cumulative exposure. Select the least
burdensome qualifying duration and amount. If evidence or applicable
authority is absent, follow the existing safe operating limit and
activate the qualified service alternative.

**WS23 — Compare concrete portfolios for the same useful interval.**
Prefer funded efficiency and lawful, actually usable flexibility,
independent electricity supply and direct service continuity where they
avoid concentrating heat and depletion in one catchment. Compare that
portfolio with an ordinary reduction in generation and a limited
operating adjustment where lawful. Include who bears compensated
deferral and whether the postponed work is still useful, actual
imports and donor impacts, alternate water service, storage endurance,
worker and fuel availability and next-period replenishment. Count any
emissions or ecological burden shifted elsewhere under its proper
account. No protected water, care or political function can be credited
as discretionary demand. Apply [EG](../19-electricity-service-and-grid-access.md)
and [OC](../20-essential-service-outage-continuity.md) for safe
dispatch and receipt, and [JC](../../C06/65/04-common-failure-and-paid-public-control.md)
for the common budget and its alternative routes.

**WS24 — Stop on observed limits and preserve the next interval.**
For each material adjustment retain the legal authority, locations,
operating conditions, measured use and reasons, protected downstream
limits and a binding time end. The plant and grid operator prearrange
a safe reduction and accepted service fallback before relying on the
increment. Test the first binding physical or legal limit, including a
hotter return, lower flow, loss of an instrument or qualified receiver,
repeated demand before recovery and the separate duration of exposure.
If evidence fails, use only a separately approved and independently
verifiable fallback. Stop the exceptional increment when its conditions
end and route protected duties to their accepted alternatives. A later
permission cannot erase an earlier exposure or remedy. Recurrent
reliance triggers an ordinary capital, demand and permit review under
elected budget law.

**WS25 — Make displaced harm and restoration reviewable.** Retain
separate records for an authorized envelope, actual use, receiving
quality, interruption and observed harm. Give downstream users and
the affected communities useful notice and access to original evidence
through lawful privacy and safety protections. Keep an independent,
paid inspection and urgent challenge route with authority for applicable
correction. Publish material uncertainty and missing monitoring, whose
service was exposed, who owns remaining repair and what people received.
Assembly scrutiny compares proposed sources and can fund or amend future
policy under the applicable law. It cannot certify a plant or a river's
condition. AI may compare forecasts, reveal double counting and flag
conflicts. It supplies neither dispatch, environmental approval nor a
verdict about harm.
