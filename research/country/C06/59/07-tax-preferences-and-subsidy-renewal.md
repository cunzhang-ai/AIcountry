# Put targeted tax preferences and subsidies to an elected renewal choice

*Design327 · TN26–TN30 · Proposed fiscal and public-accountability rules.*

**TN26 — Show all support for the same public purpose on one account.**
Before adopting material discretionary business support, the competent
elected body identifies the ordinary tax baseline, the purpose and
affected people, eligible acts, funding and actual administering authority.
Count a credit, exemption, grant, deferral and contingent guarantee by
its own legal and cash effect, then reconcile overlaps by recipient
group, activity and period. Report expenditure, revenue forgone,
timing cost, collectible recovery and maximum contingent exposure
separately. Compare joint repeal or replacement as a package where
measures interact. Maintain independently reviewable criteria for
which ordinary tax rules and rights protections belong to the baseline.
A new label, entity or delivery instrument cannot avoid scrutiny.

**TN27 — Vote exposure, eligibility and expiry before inducing reliance.**
For new targeted business support, select an express elected expiry
after no more than four applicable tax years or four years from grant
commencement. A successor scheme requires a fresh competent Act or
appropriation at its actual legal rank. The adopting instrument sets
the funded or fiscal ceiling, cohort, published allocation rules,
material milestones, data access, independent evaluation, exit and
treatment of valid prior acts. If a capped programme needs reservation,
allocate it transparently before a person undertakes a qualifying act.
An exhausted ceiling does not cancel a vested credit, refund or
signed obligation. General progressive allowances, basic protections
and existing accrued rights keep their own law. An open-ended existing
scheme needs an express lawful review and change before this expiry
can apply.

**TN28 — Check what the support added and who received it.** Before
launch, select a proportionate comparison with no award, direct
purchase or a differently targeted instrument. Identify the useful
result, accepted quality, costs and timing, and the original evidence
that could establish additionality. At review distinguish supported
activity, activity that would have occurred anyway, effects displaced
elsewhere and genuinely additional received benefit. Examine workers,
households, competing providers and local effects separately, including
access for small applicants and unintended transfers to owners.
Where causal estimates are weak, publish the rival explanations and
uncertainty. Keep actual fraud or a broken contractual term on its
own proof and recovery route. Disappointing outcomes alone supply
no repayment claim.

**TN29 — Require a distinct decision before renewal, and fund the exit.**
Submit the independent comparison early enough for the existing budget
and member scrutiny calendar. Give affected people and opposition access
to safe evidence, alternative text and a recorded vote. The competent
elected body can renew, narrow, replace or let the support expire.
A review delay supplies no continuation. If the evidence does not
support the stated benefit, any continuation requires an express
reasoned and bounded decision, with uncertainty, costs and rival
choices visible. Finish valid claims, refund, worker and contracted
service duties after expiry under their own law. Count any release of
tax or spending capacity only when lawful, net and usable by date.
A foregone-revenue estimate does not establish the cash from repeal.

**TN30 — Keep award, evaluation and payment independent.** Protect
taxpayer confidentiality, trade evidence and safe testimony while
publishing purposes, criteria, beneficiaries where law permits, total
exposure, actual effects and the precise elected disposition. Give
reviewers independent evidence access, and provide funded challenge
for erroneous exclusion or unlawful recovery. AI may compare
scenarios or flag overlapping group claims. It cannot establish
additionality, grant a preference, terminate rights or appropriate
claimed savings. Adopt prospectively with named assessment, claims,
service and paid successor routes. Reopen on opaque stacking, fiscal
leakage, weaker access, systematic displacement or a better funded
instrument.

tests the choice and exit. Actual counterfactual, fiscal yield and
