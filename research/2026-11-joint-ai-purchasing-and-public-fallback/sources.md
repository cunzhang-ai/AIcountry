# Evidence for joint AI purchasing and fallback capacity

| Primary source | Observed mechanism | Applicability and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [U.S. Government Accountability Office, GAO-26-107530, June 2026](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-107530/index.html) | In a study of 24 selected federal agencies, 17 reported continuing cloud-cost tracking challenges and 11 reported difficulties with multi-vendor procurements. GAO reports that a single procurement code per contract limited data precision. It describes negotiated central purchasing discounts and operational barriers to using multiple providers. | Track cost, underlying capability and usable alternatives separately. The studied agencies and reported experiences do not establish national AI procurement prices or that multiple vendors always protect service. |
| [NHS England, Synnovis clinical impact update, 8 August 2024](https://www.england.nhs.uk/london/2024/08/08/update-on-cyber-incident-clinical-impact-in-south-east-london-thursday-8-august/) | After a shared pathology provider was attacked, NHS England reported 1,680 elective procedures and 10,001 acute outpatient appointments postponed across two trusts by the end of the ninth week. Its report separately described mutual aid that restored access to non-routine testing for GP practices. | Distinguish a useful recovered segment from the full residual service failure. This was a pathology and IT incident. It supplies no causal estimate for the proposed AI or contract choices. |
