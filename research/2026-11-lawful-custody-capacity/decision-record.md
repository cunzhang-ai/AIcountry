# Design214. Who acts when lawful custody needs exceed staffed places

## Baseline and decision

Design213 is the pinned baseline. Use the current framework and decision
template. P and RL already give remand, sentences, release and recall
their respective judicial grounds. PO assigns corrections staff and
actual execution. WF requires removal of unsafe staffing assumptions.
Select PO25–PO32 so elected lawmakers see the demand their proposals
create and operators act before a local shortfall. Distinguish
disclosure, actual authorization, safe receiving and individual remedy.

## Observations and live choices

The observed 2025 corrections account shows a small favorable net
variance alongside increased gross expense. Reported occupancy and
the audit show why this cannot establish released safe capacity.
The multi-year political agreement and its legal implementation
are a strong actual rival combining staffing, construction, revised
sentences and options outside custody. The receipts, reserves and
funding profiles do not establish qualified relief. A subsequent
reported change in approved rostering belongs in its own time and
may change the actual operating comparison when independently tested.

Select verified ordinary staffing, reserves and repair, with actual
court, clinical and local receiving. Reject a capacity plan based only
on nominal places, national average occupancy or a funded programme.
Keep larger suitable facilities and paid recruitment as genuine options
where demand and outcomes justify them. Lawful negotiated shifts can
reduce disruption when rest is verified. Qualified community provision
can avoid some custody and also consume staff and local services or
widen monitoring. Courts keep a no-supervision comparator. Neither
resource account chooses the moral sentence or proves deterrence.

## Allocation of power and objections

The Assembly owns policy and sources with member and opposition
access under DB and GP. The career corrections authority owns sites,
staff and lawful transfers. The judiciary owns case orders and
meaningful review. Independent inspector, counsel, worker, clinical
and victim routes remain usable and separately funded. A forecast can
overstate demand or crowd out prevention and reintegration. Test
alternative legal policies at a common scope. A strict operational
response can raise costs or produce longer travel. Keep harm, useful
time, family contacts and lawful individual relief visible. No data
tool or official can trade off those limits by a score.

## Compatibility and bounded next action

Preserve P, Q, CD, RL, DP, FT and T and continuing wage, liberty,
health and remedy claims. No enacted date or actual powers change.
To compare a real repair, the corrections authority would need
authorized dated budget and payroll evidence, affected worker and
prisoner participation, a local legal-demand and qualified roster
baseline, an accepted relief route and independent execution evidence.
Reviewers would need paid capacity and protected access. Reopen on
actual compliance or deterioration, loss of relief, demand change,
or a better ordinary arrangement. The two money bridges here are
reported or authored. Actual affected-party participation, independent
qualification for AIcountry, enactment, paid readiness and better
lives remain open.
