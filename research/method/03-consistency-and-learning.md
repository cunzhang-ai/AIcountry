# Consistency, protection and learning

## 6. Maintain consistency without freezing the framework

Keep stable identities and exact revisions for material claims, evidence, definitions,
packages, decisions and procedures. Record why dependencies exist. Distinguish a
source's observations from summaries and reanalyses of the same evidence family.
Independent controllers alone do not prove independent evidence.

On a changed source, definition, method, context or material incident:

1. Preserve the event and old reasoning safely.
2. Identify potentially affected conclusions through declared dependencies and a
   check for omitted relationships. Disclose incomplete scope.
3. Reassess, revise, narrow qualification, retire or retain with reasons. Affected
   does not automatically mean false.
4. Reuse only supporting results still valid for their exact scope, context, method,
   procedure and event coverage. Final decisions and material-objection review renew.

A new objection matters even if input fingerprints are unchanged. Critical unresolved
questions limit the claim being made; they do not prohibit honestly labelled inquiry.
Declare the actual finite inspection scope, received objections, source families,
coverage gaps and material known issues not substantively examined; do not quietly
restrict “known” to one retrieved batch or claim that all important reasons were
reviewed. Unlimited novel generation does not create unlimited analysis capacity.
A consequential qualification needs fresh review sufficient for its stated claim;
unexamined potentially defeating objections require narrower qualification,
additional bounded work, deferral or an expressly reasoned provisional selection,
never a claim of resolved objection. Preserve dissent, explanations and reopening.
This is a proposed scoped research-method clarification with an exact [predecessor](../2026-10-democratic-repair-and-finite-attention/predecessor-design-82/method/03-consistency-and-learning.md).
It does not adopt national FA as a project gate, waive independently required
review, change publication permissions or let a procedure approve itself.
Actual legal claims and mandatory hearings retain their own due consideration
and appeal; research attention limits cannot close them.

Reviewers identify task competence, qualification source, controllers and conflicts,
and exactly what they inspected. Shared sponsorship, instructions or evidence can
limit independence. Unavailable qualified review remains a stated limit; repeated
self-review must not be presented as independent assurance.

Replacement, division, merger and retirement map old duties, pending cases, resource
claims and remedies to explicit successors. Technical rollback cannot undo every
real consequence. Ending a discretionary mandate is distinct from ending a service.
Use actual continuity arrangements; do not invent them to pass a research gate.

A proposed procedure cannot approve itself. Use the valid predecessor route for
adoption and explicit treatment of pending work. Current constitutional holds and
higher review classes still apply to actual and cumulative power changes. Research
branches can question those rules without pretending they have replaced them.

## 7. Protect people and preserve learning

Keep public reasoning and correction history, with separately justified custody
for sensitive evidence: purpose, minimum data, access, retention and complaint route.
If suitable custody is unavailable, do not collect private cases into the research
register. Restriction or removal needs a safe event record and reassessment of claims
that can no longer be checked. A later revision does not erase prior disclosures.

A real intervention requires actual competent authorization, appropriate participant
and ethics arrangements, continuity, adverse-signal response and resources. Simulations,
shadow assessments and real service changes are different activities. Examine people
affected without joining, including those unable to leave; do not manufacture consent.

Every consequential result leaves a learning obligation: what could change it,
whose observation is needed, a responsible role, resources, an appropriate trigger
and the next bounded action. Unassigned roles and missing monitoring remain explicit.
Failure to observe harm is weak support when observation does not reach affected people.

Review the method's own burden and concentrated control. A simpler method may be
better. Institution retention can be a successful result. Neither a longer framework,
more reviews, new version numbers nor passed synthetic checks establish progress.

## 8. Continue without rebuilding the history

Use [one decision record](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md), adding linked technical or contextual
material only where needed. Ordinary-language reports are valid entry points; the
research steward develops the deeper assessment before requesting a consequential
qualification. Minor editorial changes can use a short record when unchanged meaning
and the applicable procedure justify it.
