# C03. Lawful handover and actual public control

*HT01–HT56 · Proposed handover duties.*

**Office passes under constitutional law. The working means of governing
pass through named and separately accountable receivers.** Prepare before
a contest, withdraw expired political access at the lawful boundary and
keep already owed services working through an obstructed handover.
The new executive receives its actual scope. Parliament, courts,
elections, audit, public media and local authorities keep theirs.
