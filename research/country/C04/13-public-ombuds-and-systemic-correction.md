# C04. An independent public Ombuds office with enforceable follow-through

*Design392 · OM01–OM12 · Proposed ordinary law.*

Select an independent, free route for people to bring administrative
failures to light. The public Ombuds can investigate a recurring problem
on its own initiative, require a reasoned response and test implementation.
The responsible authority delivers any due remedy. Courts retain binding
judgment and execution. The elected Assembly and local councils decide
policy and funding in public under their own law.
