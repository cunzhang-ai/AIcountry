# Two offices, one independent roster

Continue the stressed repaired path of the [joined budget](../joined-opening-budget-and-release/01-four-payers-two-windows.md).
The published example ends day 90 with Treasury at its 1-unit
minimum. The proposed AI work below uses a new constructed
comparison. No discretionary Treasury cash is available from
that margin.

Two agencies would each release 4 front-office hours in normal
operation if both deploy. A bounded start uses one agency, so it
can redeploy at most 4 hours. This is paid time for qualified
backlog work, with no payroll saving. A common failure consumes
all hours released in the affected deployment and leaves none
of them for the optional backlog. The standing independently funded review roster has
4 hours, of which 2 are due for ordinary appeals in each
window. The extra evaluation needed by each agency consumes
the other 2 hours. Broad rollout needs 6 hours in total,
including appeals. The ordinary staff cannot undertake those
independent reviews. During common failure, even the bounded
rollout needs another 2 independent hours for affected-case
correction in the next window.

| Same protected service and deadline | Ordinary review hours | Extra AI review hours | Qualified hours needed |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Strong ordinary service | 2 | 0 | 2 |
| Bounded deployment, ordinary operation | 2 | 2 | 4 |
| Bounded deployment, next window after common failure | 2 | 4 | 6 |
| Broad rollout, ordinary operation | 2 | 4 | 6 |

Assume a separate, authorized fund can pay 2 units for an
independently qualified 2-hour alternate that actually accepts
the next window. If available, bounded rollout can protect the
stated review and delivery in both windows. Its ordinary
redeployment is conditional on actual backlog receiving and is
recalled to due work during failure. If the alternate is funded
solely by an assumed 2-unit future saving, that money is absent
in the adverse case and two review hours remain uncovered.
Then retain the strong ordinary route. A broad start tied to
reducing the review roster fails even in ordinary operation.
Keep the existing workers' pay and independent reviews in all
cases. These are constructed conditions, without a claim of
actual productivity, appropriation or authority.
