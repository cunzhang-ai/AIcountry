# Safe notice, lawful evidence and a real cooperation service

*B51–B52; part of [C08/17](../17-jurisdiction-and-legal-cooperation.md).*

**B51 — Service is actual opportunity, not platform delivery.** Serve material
claims/orders with usable content, language/support, relevant grounds and
enough actual time to defend, through a lawful channel under the applicable
service regime. A courier mark, agent inbox, biometric match or synchronized
receipt alone proves only its limited fact. No deemed confession/jurisdiction
by silence. Substitute service needs independently reasoned real efforts,
safe notice design and a meaningful setting-aside route for missed defense;
it cannot publish a survivor's hiding place or defeat compulsory higher law.

A domestic evidence order needs actual statutory/judicial authority and
proportional scope. A foreign request is not a domestic warrant, and a
domestic warrant is not permission for a foreign search, hack or covert
extraction. Use actually available lawful assistance/service channels.
For a locally accountable custodian's cross-border data, identify real
control and conflicting laws, hear objections and require a competent
reasoned resolution/cooperation route; physical server location alone
neither supplies worldwide power nor automatically defeats a lawful claim.
Consent from a witness/vendor does not authorize every sovereign intrusion.

Trace source, transformations, collection authority, uncertainty, contrary
evidence and shared upstream sources. Several agreeing AI reports are not
independent corroboration; translation/reconstruction is not the original.
Keep scope, purpose, lawful retention, onward sharing, access and correction
explicit; safe testimony is not permanently public training material.
Exclude unusably secret/tainted criminal proof under P11–P14; civil evidence
uses its own actual fairness/burden rules, not a machine probability.

**B52 — Cooperation has an owner without a supreme mediator.** An accountable
justice cooperation service receives/transmits requests, preserves lawful
dates/source records, arranges translation/advice and reports actual response.
It is a career administrative channel under C04, not an exclusive claimant
gate or a power to rule on guilt, status, immunity, deportation or execution.
Courts retain accessible direct/urgent routes and competent independence.
Foreign-affairs officials address diplomacy/treaty competence under C10;
the Assembly authorizes material commitments and general implementing law.

Publish authenticated applicable texts, dates, scope, reservations, amendments,
termination and domestic authority through the existing legal register.
A new treaty/model-law version, foreign standard or AI update does not
approve itself or silently amend national rights. Before lawful change,
map pending cases, accrued claims, custody/restrictions, evidence custody,
payments, assets and successors; repeal or a local boundary change cannot
make a foreign obligation disappear. Existing lawful protection continues.

Budget qualified clerks, independent judges/counsel, interpreters, safe data
custody, alternate/offline routes, actual transfer/reception and court-order
execution once in C06. Other states' cooperation and unpaid family/legal
work are not committed resources. The existing operating bids have no
qualified full increment. Appointment, funding, vendors, evidence and
enforcement require cumulative-control scrutiny; copied agents add no mandate.
Report request, accepted task, lawful decision, actual handover/payment and
received remedy separately, preserving unfamiliar meanings and material dissent.
