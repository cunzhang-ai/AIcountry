# C07. Paid receiving for personal essentials

*Integrated design 84 · Consumer of [NP](../C06/33-protection-production-and-national-expansion.md),
not an additional service entitlement or funded headcount.*

The local/sector public authority must plan dependable useful-time receiving
before relying on the C07 guarantee. Where ordinary plural providers cannot
supply that receiving, NP08 selects paid public/local residual capacity or an
actually accepted prepaid substitute. It changes prior allocation/retainer and
handover, not the name of an existing care payroll or last-resort duty.

Clinical providers keep clinical judgment and consent; care authorities organize
chosen home/community/nonclosed alternatives, learning authorities keep real
educational access, and housing/network owners keep their separate duties.
The receiving package links staff/shift/place, safe transport, communication,
personal payment and independent review without merging private records or powers.
No public residual placement creates guardianship, restraint or compulsory care.

A retainer pays for actual reserved availability, skill, staffing, maintenance,
replacement and accepted access, with ordinary care/learning work and standby
attributed once. Examine nights, holidays, sparse places, exceptional needs,
contractor/control failure and relatives' voluntary withdrawal. A nominal empty
bed, paid vacancy, list or penalty clause does not qualify as safe receiving.
If existing staff are reassigned, show the tasks displaced and lawful replacement.

I05 cash, Y pension, a voucher and a direct support budget remain money; record
what service was actually bought and received separately. Missing care cannot
be reconciled away as voluntary family work or an in-kind component already
credited against the basket. Extra housing cash also needs reachable supply,
not a disguised rent cap on the person's unmet need. Existing individual service
claims/assessment/appeal remain under I/S/Y and professional law, with assistance.

The adopted national schedule states the included share, incremental/overlap
cost, actual source, qualifications and paid alternate. National equalization
covers relevant high-need/place costs; it cannot silently transfer an unfunded
duty to a poorer locality. Clinical/care legal help is independent of the provider
controlling delivery or treatment. A funded invoice/referral is not an accepted
next shift, received payment or delivered remedy.

No actual new safe places, workers, contracts or full-cost source is verified.
The former23-component bids remain historical comparisons, not a cap or free
incremental appropriation. [S14–S16](13/04-workload-priority-and-transition.md)
and [T](../C14/06-staged-capacity-and-protection.md) preserve actual rights,
missed-date correction and safe continuity during any lawful migration.

[QE's named reference](../C06/34-reference-costs-and-delivery-conflicts.md) now
prices full optional offers separately from actual take-up and exposes missing
peak places/compatible operator replacement. Guaranteed availability cannot be
financed by presuming refusal or unpaid relatives; only an applicable legal
basis and same-component actual receipt can support I05 credit, subject to
I17 and independent MO/S scope. Annual paid role-equivalents are not accepted
people/skills, and the reference neither changes entitlement nor stops due clocks.

[FD public receiving](../C06/35/03-courts-receiving-and-continuity.md) now selects
a costed reference of eight public125-place sites,496paid receiving roles,
qualified floating/mobile care and distinct transport.720aggregate places after
two whole-site losses do not clear local distribution, consent or family/privacy
conflicts. Voluntary preaccepted deployment is paid, never duplicated L07 care.
These are unqualified requirements; actual old/new receivers and due rights
continue. Building/referral lists or a national total cannot qualify a placement.

Design116 [AS](18-person-directed-assistance.md) now adds employment/control,
restricted payroll/custody, ordinary-dues backing and actual next-shift duties.
Its new public payment exposure, advice/independent time and backup cannot be
absorbed as free work in old FD/NP bids. The corrected credit sentence above
repairs an overbroad earlier inference;115 checks did not inspect every such
sentence or approve credits for distinct protected services.
