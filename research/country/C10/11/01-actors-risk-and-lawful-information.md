# HP01–HP06. Public health facts do not themselves confer power

*Design147 · [Ordinary protection](../11-ordinary-health-and-hazard-protection.md) · Statutory hypotheses; no actual diagnosis/order.*

**HP01 — Distinct ordinary responsibilities.** Prior Act authorizes designated
qualified senior local public-health officers within existing C07/C04 career
services to investigate a grave communicable/transferable contamination risk,
recommend supported protection and apply to ordinary courts for specified orders.
National public-health professionals supply reference methods/qualified mutual aid;
the accountable health minister proposes national general rules, not individual
judgments. Local civilian incident leads handle physical hazards under HP13.
Published job competence, actual qualification/conflict review, named duty/relief
rota and C04 merit/discipline apply; a manager's designation is no exemption from
clinical licensing or a free new staff member. No new health board or AI commander.

**HP02 — A serious risk to actual others.** A compulsory health measure needs specific
current evidence of a transmissible infection/contamination capable of death,
serious physical injury or clinically grave illness, a material risk of transmission
from this person/setting to others, and why adequate supported less intrusive means
cannot protect in useful time. Record actual mode/context, expected harm, uncertainty,
alternatives and unequal burdens; not any remote theoretical possibility or ordinary
incidental exposure. Clinical diagnosis or disability alone is insufficient. Political
view, nationality, neighborhood label, poverty, harmless behavior or test refusal is
not a substitute for the causal evidence. Economic inconvenience alone is no ground.

**HP03 — Offer protection before coercion.** Provide accessible explanation, actual
safe voluntary separation/testing/treatment offers, support, ventilation/PPE and
reasonable work/contact adjustment where effective. Do not demand futile trials
before imminent danger is addressed, but explain why an available narrower option
fails. Reassess when circumstances change and at least daily for individual liberty
orders; end or narrow earlier when grounds fail. An authority may not create an
unsafe crowded facility then cite its own exposure to reset a person's limits.
First support/urgent duties follow need/actual receipt, not a complete file.

**HP04 — A bounded notification duty, not a national dossier.** Actual treating
clinicians/laboratories must notify the competent local public-health receiver
without avoidable delay of an evidenced suspected/established HP02 grave transmissible
hazard, with only identity/contact, relevant observations/results and necessary
exposure facts. Explain that lawful limited use where safely feasible. This is an
express public-health source under PD, not voluntary consent manufactured from care.
No blanket report of every minor test, belief or clinical history. Aggregate public
methods/thresholds that materially change required reporting use the actual DR route,
not a private model update. A novel hazard must still satisfy HP02, not wait for an
ancestor's list or become a secret exempt category.

Purpose-limit access/corrections and recipients; no routine employer, immigration,
party, insurer or criminal dossier. A genuinely distinct disclosure/investigation
requires its own actual law and rights review; HP creates no new criminal warrant.
People may supply voluntary contact accounts without an AI account or compelled
self-incrimination. This Act supplies no general power to compel personal answers,
continuous geolocation, home sensors or population biological credentials.

**HP05 — Original evidence and actual consent.** Distinguish original observation,
clinical inference, exposure estimate, public rule, individual order and clinical
permission. Preserve contrary facts, shared sources, safe source identity and
correction; repeated AI reports are not independent corroboration. Future media,
nonverbal choice and supported interpreters can communicate a real decision, not
prove consent through a signature/model's certainty. A source gap is uncertainty,
not zero risk, verified danger or automatic power. Courts can require disclosed
qualified evidence and funded counter-expertise where materially needed.

**HP06 — A real independent burden of proof.** For continued compulsory separation,
the public applicant must establish clear and convincing specific evidence of HP02
current grave transmission risk and necessity of the least restrictive adequate
safe measure. Reliable exposure can justify temporary quarantine under HP08 without
claiming infection proved; longer infection-based confinement needs established
current transmissibility, not merely old positive material or a refused test.
No universal numeric infection cutoff or AI risk score is enacted. Actual competent
clinical/public-health reports, contested evidence and effective independent counsel
are required; general scientific standards inform but do not decide the person.
Actual unknown pathogen facts need qualified original review, not a model certificate.

The same actual domestic grounds/protections apply at ports and to noncitizens;
HP grants no nationality-based detention, removal or new entry/refoulement ban.
MB/protection/foreign-jurisdiction duties and actual treaty competence remain.

[RC01–RC05](../13/01-public-employer-and-bounded-leadership.md) names the
physical-hazard civilian lead's public employer, qualified Director/deputy and
assignment/relief chain. HP public-health officers keep their separate mandate;
no RC appointment adds HP power, clinical qualification or a fresh clock.
