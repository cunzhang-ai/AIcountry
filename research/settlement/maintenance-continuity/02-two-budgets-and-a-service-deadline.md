# Two budgets and a service deadline

An authored clinic and a court use the same site. The urgent service
scope is 12 visits and eight hearings per window. The competent owner
can use the original site in the early window. Unless the affected
building is repaired, replaced or safely vacated by the later window,
its protected capacity cannot be relied on. The early incremental cash
limit is eight and the later limit ten. One qualified construction crew
can work in each window. The costs cover the stated work and receiving
only. Unrelated service and other estate costs stay outside the margin.

| Portfolio | Early cash | Later cash | Visits / hearings received in the later declared loss |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Begin a discretionary expansion and delay the required repair | 4 | 12 requested | 9 / 6 through authorized interim capacity only |
| Repair on time and fund continuity | 6 | 2 | 12 / 8 |
| Replace the affected site immediately | 9 | 2 | 12 / 8 only if the additional early source and usable route were provided |
| Fund a phased exit and accept users elsewhere | 7 | 4 | 12 / 8 |

The delayed option requests nine later units for the repair and three
for interim service. The repair cannot be committed under the displayed
later limit. The independently usable three-unit interim route is
already lawful and paid in the declared case, but only covers nine
visits and six hearings in time. The earlier four-unit expansion
remains a real cost. The missed service stays in the account even if
repair eventually occurs. Early cash of four rather than six cannot
be recorded as a two-unit permanent saving.

The chosen targeted repair costs five early plus one for protected
cover. Its later two-unit planned renewal stays in the next budget.
The combined approach of early repair and early expansion would need
ten cash units and two crews in the same window. Keep expansion
unstarted. If the ordinary room is unavailable, the paid alternate
can accept the original 12 visits and eight hearings in the defined
loss. If that alternate also fails, publish the loss and use the
existing sector and court routes. The funded phased exit is a real
rival if deterioration makes the site unsuitable. It uses more money
and adds four aggregate hours of travel in this authored case.

## The backlog roll-forward is a different account

An opening estimate of ten becomes eleven on a common scope and basis:
add three newly overdue, two earlier defects newly found and one of
price revision. Remove four of independently verified work and one of
lawfully closed asset scope with service and residual obligations
explicitly assigned. The move from ten to eleven does not say the
assets physically deteriorated by one unit. The verified four are a
reduction in estimated remaining work. They are not the contractor's
invoice or proof that a patient arrived. The planned renewal of two
has not yet fallen overdue in this example. Moving an unfinished task
to another operator cannot reduce the consolidated total.

Use these figures to test a budget disposition, not to forecast an
actual clinic, court, contractor or construction market.
