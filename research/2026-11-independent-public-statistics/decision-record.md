# Design300. Independent public statistics and lawful reliance

10 November 2026 · Working selection · No actual enactment

## Situation and baseline

[Design299](baseline.json) already protects evidence and corrections,
compares lived outcomes and requires response to qualified regression.
It leaves the production, release and continuity chain for common
national series underspecified. A department can report a plausible
number that omits people and then use it to remove the route they
depend on. Declining coverage, delayed release and deliberate
interference need different remedies. Apply the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md)
and [decision record](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md). Codex assisted.
No resident, producer or external reviewer participated in this work.

## Alternatives and research choice

- **Retain departmental production and strengthen independent audit.**
  Lowest duplication if coverage remains stable and timely audit is
  available. A qualified audit can correct misuse. Without an independent
  release route it may arrive after a budget vote and cannot recover
  people who have disappeared from all available frames.
- **A statutory statistical service with plural source and scrutiny.**
  Select this architecture for recurring common series. Separate method,
  funding, custody, release and policy application. Give safe public access,
  open challenge and real operating cost. The coverage case supports a
  labeled partial result and funded continuity under a weak budget.
  With a qualified independent check the proposed closure fails on
  evidence. A genuinely qualified improvement can reopen the choice.
- **One central administrative file with compulsory linkage.**
  May reduce some duplicate reporting and improve timeliness where the
  same people are covered. A common frame can reproduce the same exclusions
  and exposes respondents to repurposing. Reject this as the default.
  Retain separately justified, lawful and purpose-limited sources.

The proposed vote, term and acting limit are design choices. A separate
parliamentary source could still be captured, or both political blocs
could leave the job vacant. A professional incumbent could also suppress
bad results or exclude contested populations. Preserve scheduled duty
through career succession, a publication status when quality fails,
independent replication and enforceable process. Preserve legitimate
reasons for withholding unsafe or unreliable material. Audit power does
not become statistical management or policy control. The source payer,
review and alternate publication route still need funded independence.

## Compatibility and qualification

[PCO06–PCO10](../country/C14/04/04-independent-public-statistics.md)
owns the proposed statutory model. [Primary sources](sources.md)
document working distinctions and real coverage and release failures.
The [budget exercise](../settlement/independent-public-statistics/README.md)
illustrates their possible importance using declared assumptions.
Preserve current authorities, original lawful vintages, privacy
and retention limits, due claims, staff obligations and pending
appeals. A revision cannot by itself alter a legal payment formula.
The actual decision owner must carry a funded lawful contingency
or seek competent disposition before changing the policy basis.

## Next bounded test

Use one independently funded, consented and safely sampled
service-area comparison with a real planned decision deadline.
Price an existing departmental release and an independently
controlled coverage check on the same lawful privacy scope.
Record achieved coverage, time, safe publication and receipt of
any resulting correction. Reopen on systematic nonresponse,
unreproducible estimates, selective suppression, shared control,
unsafe linkage or a lower-burden method that reaches the same
population. The candidate has no verified public mandate,
actual operating appropriation or demonstrated welfare result.
