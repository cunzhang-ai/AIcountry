# Test whether people can use an established payment on time

*Design506 · Proposed first adoption trial and decision protocol.*

**Offer one municipal payer a time-limited trial of independently usable receiving for established cash assistance.** Keep the amounts, eligibility, dates, legal discharge rules and ordinary access of the existing scheme. Fund an assisted, no-account route only where the governing law permits that payment method, and maintain a separately authorized urgent response where it does not. This is a first test of the country's claim that a public entitlement should reach a person in usable form. It leaves constitutional adoption and the terms of new entitlements to their own decisions.

## Put an exact proposal before the competent bodies

| Instrument or commitment | Who decides and what must be in place |
|---|---|
| Payer authorization and trial budget | The elected body with spending competence approves a named payer, population, permitted instruments, gross cost cap, two complete payment cycles plus closeout, funded emergency route and any necessary local source. Identify paying authority for each tranche. Separate lawful principal, cash float, staff, transport, losses and appeal. A municipal vote cannot change a national payer's powers. Obtain any required source, interlocal or protected data authority from its actual owner before release. Leave new powers to a separate law. |
| Delivery and finality | The public payer and its accepting local partners verify safe contact, the person's choice, proof, funding, legal exclusivity, settlement and a route the person can actually use. No account or phone requirement for trial participation. If an original instruction is uncertain, keep the claim and use separately lawful urgent relief. A trial instruction cannot authorize duplicate principal. |
| Comparison and independent review | Use the existing competent oversight route with funds insulated from operator or supplier results. Confirm access to minimum necessary source records, practical applicant challenge and access to the competent court. Include direct input from people with no usable bank access, those who decline the offer and frontline staff. An analyst or AI may summarize evidence. Neither approves claims or outcomes. |
| Expiry and paid handback | The original payer continues all rights and ordinary access. Agree an accepted owner for unresolved cases, disbursement, accounting, record retention and independent review after expiry. No enrollee has to reopen the same claim or prove need again because the trial ends. |

Before changing any live payment, execute an offline rehearsal of the payer and receiving roles, including a shared-provider failure and a second call. Fund and test a same-day response where the actual deadline requires it. If current law allows only information, booking or assisted account designation, run that narrower comparison or a rehearsal. Do not present those results as evidence of live no-account payment. If an exact legal effect or spending source is missing, submit a separate lawful instrument before relying on it.

## Design a comparison the repaired incumbent can win

- **Ordinary repair** offers competent human help, earlier safe notice, accessible identity proof, and timely use of all existing lawful alternate paths. **Independent receiving** includes these protections and adds the separately funded no-account collection or delivery path, with a known exclusive release owner and receiver. Record full cost, including work currently hidden in family help, staffing, oversight and fallback. Give the same minimum protection and access to urgent correction to people in both routes.
- Before comparing outcomes, freeze the eligible cohort, payment cycles, needed minimum effect worth the additional cost, burdens, known exclusions and rules for protected record access. Agree the maximum additional cost and ordinary staffing burden before comparing outcomes. The denominator includes all established awards in the scoped payer and calendar, with separately reported outcomes for those known to lack a usable account. Treat missing status as unknown rather than excluding it. Summarize false holds and harms for people outside the paid sample. Count original due dates, actual control of the full due sum, time and expense, wrong or duplicate releases, remediated and unremediated losses, and independent complaint receipt. A cashpoint collection or reconciliation counts only when it is usable and attributable to that award.
- If equally eligible groups can be served independently and ordinary receipt is protected, stagger optional offers in a public predeclared order. Use random order only where law, consent requirements and equal protection permit, and only at the level of independently controlled clusters to avoid sharing staff or custody between arms. Report by offered route first, even on refusal or crossover. If comparability cannot be sustained, keep a paired observational comparison and label the remaining confounding. For a bank failure that cannot ethically be induced, use a separately identified witnessed rehearsal.

## Decide before changing scope

| Evidence at the second cycle and adverse rehearsal | Disposition |
|---|---|
| Repaired ordinary service reaches the same residents by their deadlines with no greater burden or full cost | Retain ordinary repair and renew only what its real source and mandate allow. |
| Independent receiving shows a predeclared material gain in timely usable payment and no added rights or safeguard failure, including across adversely affected groups | Bring the same scoped authority and gross ordinary costs to a separate renewal vote. Before expansion, verify source and receiving sites, displaced work, funding and independently usable next-call capacity in each new location. |
| Missing records make either result compatible with the other, or apparently faster payments shift work to families or other protected recipients | Retain immediately safe duties and test the reason. Keep the trial finite. Reassess the exact route and source before relying on a gain. |
| Evidence confirms unauthorized payment, compromised contact, lost independent challenge or a trial-caused missed protected deadline | Stop using the affected release path, preserve and remedy the original claim, and pay any independently verified ordinary duties. Escalate through the applicable legal route. Restart the affected scope only with a newly qualified lawful decision. |

A signed appropriation, published protocol, submitted file or bounded AI experiment establishes no measured receipt. In the authored example, 20 eligible awards in a cycle are split into comparable groups of ten. Strong repair has six verified timely and usable receipts and two unknown. Independent receiving has eight verified and one unknown. With missing cases retained in the denominator these imply six to eight and eight to nine. There is no guaranteed strict advantage. If evidence later verifies that the two ordinary unknowns missed the cutoff, its six is below the eight already verified for independent receiving. For this authored example, a prospective renewal screen could require a lower difference bound of two per ten, compliance with the cost cap and no attributable safeguard failure. Confirming the ordinary misses would meet the numerical screen. The permitted trial scope, outcome uncertainty, actual full price and rights still require independent review and the elected renewal decision. Do not apply these hypothetical figures to any real institution.

## What adoption could establish

Denmark provides a stronger ordinary comparator than referral to a bank. NemKonto Act section 13(3) obliges a public payer to ensure payment another way when no account is designated or the system reports a failed payment. Section 14 gives distinct rules on responsibility for delay. The legacy waiting account is not the ordinary fallback for new municipal payments. An earlier Ombudsman case involving Copenhagen cash assistance identifies a concrete public payer and access failure under the former legal framework. It supplies no current municipal failure estimate or participating sponsor. Copenhagen’s October 2025 memo describes a caseworker withdrawing cash at Danske Bank in Brønshøj and handing it to the recipient. The administration reported one or two uses per quarter, without payment-by-payment deadlines or coverage. The Finance Committee subsequently requested investigation of alternative cash providers. Compare actual staffed cash handover with a separately callable lawful alternative when that same branch or bank is unavailable. Start with witnessed and retrospective comparison if two cycles do not yield enough comparable cases. Two cycles are an operating check, never a substitute for evidentiary sufficiency. These show why dispatch and actual control require separate measures, and why a no-account service needs legal authority separate from a payout exception. They do not supply a partner, local dataset, approved scope or cost for this trial. An earlier randomized study of outreach and application help supplies a reason to compare competent assistance, not a cash-delivery effect. [Evidence and limitations](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-first-adoption-trial/sources.md)

For a parallel constitutional exercise, verify actual opposition drafting and filing, lawful independent review and executed relief during a shared workbench loss. Count accepted successor control separately. A drill tests the scoped dependency. It supplies no governing authority or substitute for actual affected participation. An independently reproduced improvement can support a local service decision and may later qualify comparable places. It cannot test national democratic replacement, recurring economy-wide work, environmental limits or foreign receiving. Retain the first national resource and governance comparisons for those claims. Publish by scope, with failures and dissent accessible to residents and opposition.
