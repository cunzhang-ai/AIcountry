# C06. Disaster risk finance and the recovery budget

*Part of [C06 public finance](../C06-public-finance.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

Prior Assembly law states which immediate relief, local fiscal continuity,
public-asset repair and household rebuilding costs the public will bear;
which are conditional grants or loans; and which losses remain with valid
private contracts. The C07 minimum safety and essential-service duties do
not depend on buying insurance. The budget records historical public
liabilities, credible hazard ranges, correlated events, the exposed people
and places, expected cash dates and physical reconstruction capacity. A
private policy may pay slowly, exclude the event or leave an insurer insolvent;
its headline coverage is not usable Treasury cash or a delivered home.

The reference finance package combines an annually appropriated reserve for
bounded recurrent costs with prior-law contingent finance for larger losses
and optional, transparently purchased risk transfer when its premium,
exclusions, trigger, currency, counterparty and payout delay compare
favorably with retaining the risk. It does not require any one instrument.
Premium subsidy or a public insurance backstop is an explicit distribution
and public-risk choice, subject to an Assembly ceiling and equal-treatment
review. A loan remains debt under the [sovereign-debt rule](06-sovereign-debt-and-distress.md);
a catastrophe trigger does not grant automatic unlimited borrowing, a central-
bank overdraft or a new tax. Foreign-currency proceeds still require actual
settlement and imports still require suppliers and routes.

After a disaster, Treasury publishes a common ledger: opening reserve and
valid payouts, relief already due, local revenue loss, public works and
maintenance, grants, loans, insured claims, guarantees, bank/mortgage and
pension exposures, debt service, currency needs and unfinanced gaps. The
Assembly compares a complete package with tax incidence, service losses,
future debt and physical bottlenecks. It may lawfully authorize a bounded
supplement and formula-based local transfers; cabinet cannot favor loyal
localities by withholding ordinary aid. If no new appropriation passes,
continuing legal claims and the prior-law essential-obligations schedule
survive, but an unfunded capital project is not reported as delivered.
A local fiscal collapse does not turn an unelected national receiver into a
permanent regional government.

Insurance contract enforcement and public assistance eligibility remain
separate reviewable routes. Prior premiums, policy exclusions, title and
secured-creditor claims cannot be rewritten by an AI damage model or a
minister's press release. Prospective public support may target need,
repeated exposure and affordable risk reduction without declaring all
private property losses equal or rewarding politically connected owners.
The recovery plan must fund people and materials, not merely record a
transfer. Reopen the finance mix when actual hazard distribution, insurer
capacity, public debt, local tax base or affected people's rights materially
differ from the assumptions. The [recovery rule](../C09/07-disaster-recovery-and-return.md)
sets who chooses reconstruction and protects return or movement claims.

[NB13–NB16](44-nonbank-finance-and-client-protection.md) adds private insurer promise/capital/liquidity/claims and court runoff, not a national disaster guarantee. Uninsured or insolvent-insurer losses cannot condition existing rescue, safe housing or support; new public reconstruction finance keeps its elected source and incidence.

[FR05–FR08](../C09/07/02-sources-continuing-risk-and-handover.md) separates
early usable cash from property, prevention and long-run commitments. Any
material reallocation shows local cost shares, risks shifted, funding dates
and the obligations that stay with a paid successor.

[FR09–FR13](../C09/07/03-community-relocation-and-host-capacity.md)
separates collective facilities, individually accepted moves and new
host capacity. Fund reversible preparation, any valid commitments and
a continuing safe alternate on their own schedules. Disclose changes
in the funded minimum phase before releasing optional work.

[FR20–FR25](../C09/07/05-home-insurance-exit-and-safe-transition.md)
compares capped renewal relief, durable safety and separately authorized
catastrophe backing through simultaneous claims. Keep actual covered
loss, public source, deductible and resident protection separate.

[FR26–FR33](../C09/07/06-joint-displacement-and-host-receiving.md)
requires actual compatible housing and support, with the later
receiving window and any reimbursement priced separately.
