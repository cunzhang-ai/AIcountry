# Design504 decision record

**Basis.** Continue from the [Design503 baseline](baseline.json)
under the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md). Select a first
foreign-relations package that preserves affected people's claims
and physical receipt when partner status or permission changes.

**Selection.** Carry inherited obligations within actual authority
and independently finance domestic dues and essential cover. Seek
a narrow lawful extension or independently viable continuity
arrangement against wider early recognition. Give material legal
changes their own exact elected acts and protect original effects,
records and paid routes. In the [constructed comparison](../settlement/external-continuity-and-rights/01-carry-rights-and-supply-across-a-change.md),
fund independently replenishable local supply while trying to
resolve separate foreign claims and permission. Use cheaper
ordinary repair where it actually protects both receiving windows.

**Objection and proof.** A narrow interim instrument can add new
interfaces and delay valuable reciprocal protection. Domestic
stock can consume scarce workers, spoil or cause displacement.
Keeping established law may preserve unequal access that requires
repair. The [sources](sources.md) supply separate legal contexts
and a bounded empirical comparison. Parallel analysis has common
dependencies and cannot supply independent final review or
affected-party participation.

**Compatibility and reopening.** Preserve original domestic and
foreign legal effects, amounts, deadlines, notice, scope and
remedies. No research selection compels partner consent or creates
a publicly funded international promise. Before reliance the
foreign affairs and sector owners need independently verifiable
external scope, finance, notice, receiving and onward protection.
Reopen on changed recognition, missed claims, withdrawal,
shared dependencies or stronger funded cooperation.
