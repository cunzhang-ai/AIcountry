# Authored case. A decision before the restoration season

All days and units below are constructed. No person participated in this
exercise and no allocation was made. The responsible authority is assumed
competent for the two discretionary options. Keep the existing two units
of funded habitat maintenance outside the discretionary comparison.

| Choice | Discretionary cash | Delivery requirement |
|---|---:|---|
| Ordinary proposal A, acquire a parcel | 5 | One qualified inspection and an accepting land operator |
| Panel proposal B, restore a different reach and provide safe passage | 4 | One qualified inspection and an accepting restoration operator |

The example budget has five discretionary units and one usable inspection.
The choices are alternatives. Their two totals cannot be added under that
budget. The comparison assumes both options can meet the same rights and
environmental limits. It predicts neither ecological nor household gains.
The chair of an ordinary hearing might obtain the same information with
fewer resident hours.

The separately funded process has two units for basic work and two for
translation, paid time, care and replacement. Four night-shift residents and
eight others receive invitations. A previously declared material coverage rule
requires at least two actual seats from the night-shift group on a panel of
eight. With the supports three accept and two are drawn. Six are drawn from
seven other willing people. At three cash units the support package cannot
run and only one night-shift resident can attend. The evidence can still be
heard, with the gap disclosed. A missing stratum is never filled by counting
an AI rendering or claiming that a daytime participant speaks for it.

The panel opens on day 0, delivers B on day 80, and records a minority
preference for A. The elected vote on day 130 is within 60 days and before
an independent day-135 decision deadline. A delayed vote on day 140 meets
the 60-day clock alone and misses that independent deadline. If the coverage
defect cannot be repaired, the same slot records the deficiency and allows a
separate lawful decision within the original calendar.

In the positive branch the Assembly records taking B forward. Separate
appropriation and an accepting operator allow B to start. Completion and
independently checked outcomes still require evidence. If the operator is
unavailable, the decision and appropriation remain recorded and B has
no delivery. Existing maintenance appears on both ledgers as pre-existing
work and does not become an achievement of the panel.
