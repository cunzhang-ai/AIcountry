# C05. Protect competition through successive acquisitions

*Design404 · SCM01–SCM08 · Proposed prospective additions to the competition statute.*

A sequence of small transfers can remove the last independent customer, worker
or supplier alternatives. Apply a bounded filing rule to the new transaction,
and assess its actual effects in the market the previous transactions created.

**SCM01 — Identify effective control and the activity that overlaps.** Apply
MP11–MP13 to domestic acquisitions of independent control, including through
subsidiaries, asset businesses and durable joint control. Use evidence of who
can direct relevant decisions, including coordinated control established on
facts and rights, rather than names, brands or share percentages alone.
For the numerical series gate, require the same or substitutable domestic
goods, services or labor-facing business activity with an actual common area
of competition. Work on the same skill and hiring catchment where buyers of
labor compete. Establish each target's overlap in the same specific activity
and catchment before adding it. A broad industry label, common passive investor
or ordinary supply contract alone cannot establish control or actual overlap. Vertical
or emerging-entry harm still uses the ordinary MP11 and MP16 routes.

**SCM02 — Measure the new transaction against a bounded series.** Freeze the
test at the new transaction's MP12 first binding event. In addition to MP12
and MP14, require filing when its MP12 combined domestic group turnover is
at least 10,000T and the current target and at least one qualifying earlier
target together have overlapping domestic turnover of at least 500T. Use the
preceding 36 calendar months, measured back from that event, including the
opening date. Earlier targets must have transferred effective control in
that interval and still be controlled by the same acquiring group, or be
subject to its still-operative binding commitments made in that interval.
Include genuinely simultaneous commitments in the test for each new deal.
A later agreement cannot retroactively make an earlier step reportable.
An earlier commitment or holding relinquished to effective independent
control before the test is excluded.

**SCM03 — Count the overlapping business once.** Measure each target's
attributable overlapping activity under MP12–MP13 using its own frozen
benchmark and completed turnover period. Add the resulting multiples of T.
For the combined group test use only the current MP12 total, with no added
historical turnover. Identify successive dealings in the same business,
connected transactions and genuinely independent dispositions on their
actual evidence. Count underlying business activity once, including where
an acquired bundle contains earlier targets. Preserve ordinary MP12 treatment
of one binding plan. Unknown turnover, dates or control remain contestable
facts. Publish calculation examples and use the existing prompt court route
for disputes. The adopting law fixes the gate and any revision prospectively.

**SCM04 — Keep filing and reliance finite.** Use one MP15 filing per reportable
transaction even if several gates apply. A shared dossier for simultaneous
deals may avoid repeat proof while preserving each decision, date and defense.
Use the existing completeness, reply, standstill, court and expiry rules.
No fresh hold follows from a revised theory, a later deal or an investigation
of the same transaction under another label. Cases outside the numerical gate
keep MP16's evidence-based call-in and limited court relief. Under SCM alone,
use prospective civil enforcement through existing courts. A new SCM-only
filing duty does not extend MR05 or VD11 punishment.

**SCM05 — Prove what the new deal would change.** Under MP11, compare the
likely future with this deal against the likely future without it, taking
the actual prior market and continuing lawful alternatives as given.
Evidence may include ownership of genuine alternatives, switching costs,
worker mobility, bidding rivalry, potential entry and foreseeable cumulative
effects of operative commitments. Test verifiable transaction-specific
benefits, independent purchase, repair, and documented failure of the target
under the same service and timing assumptions. The filing arithmetic or
historical pattern alone does not prove harm. Preserve separate effects on
workers, users and suppliers rather than offsetting one group's loss silently
against another's gain.

**SCM06 — Preserve finality while addressing proven new harm.** Earlier
disclosed and cleared deals can supply evidence of actual market structure.
Use the original MP17 deadlines and defenses for a claim against any earlier
transaction. An order against the new deal cannot use series review to unwind
an expired or validly cleared earlier deal. Preserve separate MP17 concealment
and independently proved ongoing-conduct remedies. A contested prohibition,
structural order or forced transfer requires court authority under MP and MR.
Any proposed remedy names the actual independent operator, funding, qualified
workers, client assets, accrued rights and protected service receiving needed
to carry it out. Keep voluntary, narrower or prospective alternatives open.

**SCM07 — Keep decisions independent and evidence accessible.** Use the MA
separation of investigator, career decider and independent court. Budget
verification and defense under ordinary appropriations. Give workers,
customers, competitors and suppliers a protected route to submit contrary
evidence and seek existing relief. Publish the scope, reasons, delays and
effects in usable form while protecting sensitive records. AI may reconcile
entities and flag missing history. It cannot establish common control from
a match, choose the market or impose a hold. A model, shared investor or
procurement official cannot determine its own independence.

**SCM08 — Commence prospectively and test the actual burden.** The adopting
Act sets a prospective date, legal benchmark and funded case and court
capacity. Count for mandatory filing only events first binding on or after
that date, including qualifying earlier events within the rolling window.
Preserve already applicable filings, claims and ongoing conduct law. Test
expected docket, false inclusions, missed local labor and service effects,
disputed ownership, actual alternatives and delivered remedies. Reopen on
repeated harmful below-gate deals, disproportionate costs to independent
exits or a simpler test with equivalent protection. The authority cannot
change the gate, clear its own related transaction or self-fund from cases.
