# TW06–TW09: wages are individual cash claims

*Part of [C05/10](../10-work-rest-and-paid-capability.md); design90 candidate.*

**TW06 — Select an hourly cash floor.** The candidate national minimum is 60%
of independently estimated median ordinary gross hourly employee earnings.
The Assembly enacts the method, coverage, exact starting rate, dates and index;
the evidence service publishes source, hours/pay definitions and uncertainty.
Workers, employers, irregular/part-time workers and unrepresented people receive
usable information and actual opportunities to challenge the measure before
the elected decision. Consultation neither creates agreement nor gives an
incumbent employer association a veto. Courts review lawful method/liability.

Use ordinary cash pay and actual paid normal-hour equivalents; do not use
task-output scores, unpaid overtime, misleading annual-income ranks or a shared
partner's income. Show how part-time, variable schedules, paid leave and platform
work enter. A fall in the median or deflation cannot automatically reduce the
nominal floor; the statutory price-protection rule and a reasoned prospective
legislative revision remain separate. The candidate annual 1 January update is max(previous nominal floor, previous
floor × verified general CPI ratio,0.60 × verified median ordinary hourly pay).
Publish exact source periods and the statutory provisional-index/correction
route; an unsupported observation does not become zero or a new agency-set
rate. Delayed qualification preserves prior dues, immediate I05 protection
and any source-law adjustment claim, not an indefinite wage-index veto.

Sixty percent is a disclosed distributional hypothesis, not an adequate-life
certificate. I05/IC extra needs and services remain independent. In the example,
median annual 30,000 at 32 normal paid hours implies a 10.817 CU hourly floor:
18,000 for a full normal year,9,000 at half-time. A person earning 12,000 may
need a raise if full-time and already exceed the floor if half-time. The old
55,000 employed-person count therefore cannot be assumed to mean 55,000 FTE.

**TW07 — Separate wages from public benefits and expenses.** Pay through the
worker's safe chosen route on the agreed regular cycle, at least monthly, with
an understandable hours/rate/deduction statement. No recruitment debt, required
equipment, ordinary business expense, employer housing, public income, tips or
partner support discharges the cash minimum. Genuine worker-chosen extras and
lawful deductions retain their separate contract, tax and civil treatment.
No rent, care or political-retaliation threat can compel an offset.

An employer must preserve actual hours/pay records, pay the undisputed amount
and permit protected challenge. Recoverable losses require applicable civil law,
not an unexplained AI payroll deduction. Wage arrears, insurance contributions,
worker insolvency priority and private debt remain visible under C08; a failed
firm's expected recovery is not current cash for the worker.

**TW08 — Preserve regular pay through the shorter-week transition.** Reducing
the statutory normal week cannot itself proportionally cut an existing regular
weekly/annual salary. New hires and part-time conversion obey the hourly floor
and nondiscrimination; employers cannot replace a covered worker with nominally
independent accounts to evade it. Independent bargaining may change lawful
contracts genuinely and prospectively with ordinary protections. No model can
infer worker assent from continued work or a representative's copied mandate.

Retained pay raises the employer's hourly cost; recruitment, prices, investment,
informality and closure can respond. The country does not guarantee unchanged
profit or every job. It funds public commitments and lawful income/transition
protection explicitly; it does not reimburse private owners automatically.
Collective bargaining, credible competition, job search/learning and actual safe
receiving matter alongside the statutory number.

**TW09 — Make organizing and refusal usable.** Workers choose independent
representatives and can bargain across common-control employers without an
employer/party/vendor selecting their voice. Protect temporary, domestic,
migrant and platform workers through accessible assistance, confidential
reporting, interim relief and actual execution against retaliation. Ordinary
employer defenses and genuine misconduct/performance process remain.
An essential-service labor dispute needs funded qualified continuity and lawful
negotiation, not blanket strike criminalization or permanent compelled overtime.

[PA01–PA04](../../C06/38/01-jobs-hours-and-paid-claims.md) now resolves the old
12,000/full-time ambiguity with explicit financed-uplift and consented part-time
rivals. Paid FTE and task availability differ with breaks/learning. No old55,000
headcount, wage residual or hypothetical tax receipt establishes actual labor/source.

[TW23](06-usable-time-and-pay-at-commencement.md) expressly applies ordinary
weekly pay protection to existing hourly and other regular pay methods at
the stage change. Use WL06 where its guarantee arises and keep optional
overtime and actual prospective contract changes distinct.

[RT26–RT31](../../C09/18/06-employment-linked-housing.md) protects the
ordinary home and separates lawful housing charges, wages and paid
housing transition when a worker leaves a job.
