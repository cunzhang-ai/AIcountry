# NI05–NI08. Support is an actual person's scoped act

*Part of [national initiatives](../16-public-national-initiatives.md).*

**NI05 — A modest national agenda threshold, no geographic veto.** For a positive
lawful opening national electorateE0, select k=ceil(E0/1000), at least1 actual
person, with no district, property, wealth or prior-local-residence quota. E0 is
the number entitled to the national franchise atO under actual C02/EL law,
including its noncitizen and absent-elector scope, not a citizen-only or merely
registered-user count. EA's actual source custodians publish safe denominator,
method, uncertainty and correction before opening; no fictional zero, research
population or conveniently smaller list can establish the gate. Missing source
requires assisted correction/review, not automatic qualification or exclusion.

A supporter must actually hold national franchise at their support act's date;
a person becoming eligible during collection can support. Death/later movement
alone does not retroactively invalidate an authentic eligible act. Freeze the
lawful opening baseline against later partisan changes; material error uses
reasoned independent correction and protection for timely authentic support,
not a silent mid-campaign threshold switch. One person may support distinct
initiatives, but contributes at most one live support to each pinned version.

**NI06 —180 days with equal real channels.** Publish C=O+180calendar days and
its exact lawful closing timestamp/calendar before collection. Organisers may
request an earlier published close with seven calendar days' usable notice;
no surprise early cut-off to suppress withdrawals. Original lawfully received
support byC counts subject to proof, even if official processing occurs later.
A privately fabricated earlier timestamp is no timely act. Official channel
failure gets source preservation, useful-time review and an actual rights-
compatible correction, not universal presumed signatures or an executive reset.

Paper, accessible human-assisted and plural safe electronic/future-media routes
can establish identity, eligible principal, pinned meaning and authentic assent.
No sole government/vendor platform, mandatory AI or owned-device test. A bounded
agent may transmit an actual specific revocable act within its mandate; it may
not infer political support from browsing, conversation, work or an old vote.
Actual principal/evidence matter, not messages, model names or account numbers.
Support can be withdrawn through equally usable safe channels untilC; retain
protected correction evidence. Signature payment, threats or service/employment
conditions cannot purchase a valid endorsement; ordinary paid organising under
WL is distinct. Applicable actual fault/proof/sanction law remains, no new crime.

**NI07 — Count through expressly assigned career roles.** The coordinated NI Act
adds support verification/qualification to EA's existing career secretariat,
under its operational secretary's protected C04 assignment/conflict rules. This
is an express new ordinary-petition duty, not EA's A9/TS authority by analogy or
a new commission appointment/ballot. The NI career team issues a reasoned count/
qualification decision within AP30working days of closeC, with its single
notified bounded extension and required fair reply/usable notice. No EA board
vote is required for this assigned NI career count; Commission directions cannot
change k, scope or merits. Count lawfully received original support, deduplicate
actual principals and match withdrawals/versions once, preserving disputed items.

Give organisers safe count/source reasons and people a private way to verify or
correct their own support. Missing state-held proof needs actual assistance,
not blanket invalidation. Known defects/fraud use individual evidence/proportionate
correction and notice/reply; an allegation or percentage estimate cannot erase
valid unrelated support. Define Q as usable notice of actual qualification;
Clerk receives it directly, without an extra sponsor transmission/fee gate.
Silence or an unverified claimed count is no qualification.

**NI08 — Safe evidence and useful independent correction.** Support identities
are not a public petition graph or a political loyalty register. Apply PD purpose,
access, retention and lawful live-hold limits; preserve safe aggregate reasons
and necessary correction/audit history, not permanently public personal evidence.
EA/Clerk/organiser custody and independent copies have actual restricted owners,
qualified paid receivers and successor/exit duties. Future media must disclose
unresolved interpretation or authentication rather than fabricate agreement.

A person/organiser can reach actual AP/OJ ordinary courts without commission or
MP permission; own-office cases require unimplicated outside judgment. Urgent
B17/E relief remains earlier than ordinary deadlines. Courts may correct lawful
counts, require actual receiving/notice/listing and preserve timely participation,
not invent support, a national electorate or policy consent. Existing orders,
qualifying support and individual rights survive official/vendor failure. Missing
independent judge/custody/executor remains failed protection with a paid remedy,
not another controller account or automatic transfer of decision authority.

Expressly give the competent ordinary court a bounded corrective-collection
power for proved material official E0/threshold error or official access/channel
failure that impaired timely support. After fair hearing, it may order one
necessary period of no more than90calendar days for the same pinned initiative,
with actual published start/close and seven days' usable prior notice. Use the
correct lawful E0 at ORIGINAL O, never a new partisan or current population.
Preserve authentic prior support; allow equally usable withdrawals through the
corrective close and newly eligible people's actual dated support under NI05.
No executive extension, serial same-error reset or presumed endorsements.
Use corrective collection only where official failure materially defeated the
opportunity to qualify; correction of an otherwise valid earned qualification
needs no reopened support contest and does not remove its agenda right.

Court states source/help/verification duties, the status of disputed Q and pending
agenda steps. Correction does not reset a still-valid qualification's clocks;
if actual qualification first arises only after corrective support, usable newQ
starts NI09–11 while earlier official breaches remain actionable. No correction
automatically nullifies an independently lawfully enacted statute. Missing actual
source, timely public notice or receiving remains failure, not a fictional period.
