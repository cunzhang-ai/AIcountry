# MP21–MP26. Make changing an essential service provider usable in practice

*Part of [competition and economic power](../11-competition-and-economic-power.md). Design350 research selection.*

**MP21 — Set a prospective sector duty with a clear owner.** The Assembly
may designate covered household services by law where a failed switch
could interrupt an essential function and sector evidence supports a
common transfer duty. Name the covered providers, exact service scope,
minimum transferable items, continuity obligations, complaint route
and funding. Apply the same substantive duty to public, private and
cooperative providers within scope. The existing sector authority sets
bounded operational standards. The competition authority addresses
persistent exclusion under its own powers. Individual protection within
a designated service does not require a new finding of market dominance.
Preserve stronger sector rules, including CN switching.

**MP22 — Transfer only what the person authorized.** At the person's or
lawfully mandated representative's request, the receiving provider
coordinates the switch. Confirm receipt within one working day and
give the proposed scope, actual start, end and charges in usable form.
Provide assisted and offline access and proportionate alternative
proof. Keep cancellation without a replacement available, with clear
consequences and any independently owed protections. Give a safe
confirmation and route to revoke a pending mandate. Authorization to
move a service or identifier supplies no consent to move unrelated
accounts, grant marketing access or waive claims. Transfer the
minimum lawful service records or identifiers, with scoped use and
correction. Where other protected people are affected, observe their
actual rights and any required separate authority.

**MP23 — Verify delivery before ending the old route.** Name the actual
receiving provider, safe test, agreed cutover and independently usable
fallback for the specific functions at risk. Check dependent services
the person identifies within lawful information limits. For a switch,
verify that the intended new service works in the person's setting
before the old provider ends its covered service, unless the person
separately chooses a lawful earlier end. Where the old route fails
or is unsafe, the responsible parties activate their prearranged
lawful fallback within the useful window. Record unmet functions and
seek immediate sector relief when neither route works. A data export,
order acceptance or platform status does not prove usable transfer.
The operator retains responsibility for actual receipt.

**MP24 — Separate valid old debts from the price of exit.** Give an
itemized final account and promptly release lawful undisputed refunds,
credits or deposits under applicable rules. An unrelated disputed
balance cannot hold a lawful transfer. Preserve agreed charges for
service actually due and separately reviewable equipment or fixed
term obligations that meet consumer law. A provider-caused delay or
required technical overlap cannot generate duplicate subscription
charges for the same covered function and period. The sector schedule
allocates attributable overlap and remedy costs and identifies any
permitted charge. Optional genuinely distinct parallel services need
separate agreement. Handle disputed allocation or compensation
through the competent independent route. A refund or fee correction
cannot establish restored access.

**MP25 — Reverse errors without shifting the burden to the resident.**
On credible evidence of an unauthorized or failed switch, the named
receiving coordinator and lawful incumbent preserve evidence and
restore the prior safe route or an agreed safe alternative, with
urgent independent review where necessary. Isolate the affected act
without unrelated account suspension or additional data sharing.
Record notice, original request, outage, actual receiving and
financial correction. Separate remediation, negligence and valid
contractual claims. A provider, broker or AI cannot certify its own
dispute, act without current mandate or insist that the resident
restart an already received request.

**MP26 — Verify the transfer duty through real cases and exit.** Fund
ordinary verification, independent review, paid fallback and lawful
migration through the enacted sector schedule. Price the burden on
smaller providers and give them interoperable, proportionate means
to comply without a single mandatory intermediary. Test where the
providers share infrastructure and where independent fallback is
actually needed. Where only one provider is practically available,
record that limitation, preserve choices within service where
feasible, and maintain independent challenge, original records
and a qualified way to replace the operator. Test the ability
to switch after the next failure. Do not require a resident to
move to demonstrate competition. Publish safe measures of unauthorized switches,
failed handoffs, dual charges and restoration. Preserve existing
contracts, lawful claims, safe evidence and stronger protections.
AI may flag conflicts and compare dates. Reopen on common failure,
unaffordable exit, exclusion of smaller providers or a simpler route
that protects the same practical choices.
