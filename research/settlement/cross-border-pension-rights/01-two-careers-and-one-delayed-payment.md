# Two careers and one delayed pension payment

This constructed comparison exercises
[Y30–Y35](../../country/C07/04/08-cross-border-pension-rights.md).
A pension holder at standard age has four full domestic service years
at a verified revalued annual base of 24,000, within the applicable
domestic ceiling. The holder has moved their ordinary residence abroad.
The domestic formula gives an annual earned pension of 1,200, payable
as 100 domestic units per month. The ordinary residence-based basic
payment is separate.

The domestic record covers months 1 through 48. A foreign provisional
record lists months 43 through 120. The six overlapping months reflect
duplicate reporting of the same work in this case. After review, the
competent authorities attribute those months to domestic work and
recognize foreign months 49 through 120. The established combined
qualification record therefore has 120 months. The hypothetical partner
requires 120 recognized months for qualification. The stipulated
agreement counts unique time toward that test, and the partner pays two
foreign currency units per month for each of its 72 credited months,
or 144 foreign currency units per pension payment. The two source
currencies and payers remain separate.

| Response | Domestic earned monthly amount | Partner amount |
|---|---:|---|
| Count only the standalone records | 100 domestic units | Below the stipulated standalone threshold |
| Apply the operative agreement with confirmed records | 100 domestic units | 144 foreign units, subject to the partner decision and payment |
| Agreement applies and partner records are delayed | 100 domestic units remain due | Pending verification and decision |
| No applicable agreement | 100 domestic units remain due | Assist a separate claim under partner law. Do not assume qualification. |

In the delayed case the domestic amount is independently established.
Its initial cross-border payment instruction fails on the monthly due
date. The domestic payer confirms nonsettlement and reroutes to the
holder's chosen lawful domestic receiving account. The holder receives
the same 100 on day 3 after the due date. Record lateness and
any separately available remedy. Keep the original due date and
do not add a second 100. The foreign award, expected payment and
its conversion remain outside domestic receipt. A later approved
foreign payment still needs separate verification.

No institution converts the four domestic years into six or adds
foreign wages to domestic Y. The [arithmetic check](verify-cross-border-pension.py)
tests the dates, coverage and receipt. The age, formula, domestic bases,
foreign terms and successful second route are inputs to this case.
Actual partner acceptance, administration, exchange costs and delivery
are open.
