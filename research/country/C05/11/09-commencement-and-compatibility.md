# Commencement, compatibility and one canonical owner

Design 98 · Proposed research, not enacted law or verified operation.

**MP18 — Separate correction, recovery and punishment.**
[MR01–MR07](07-remedies-penalties-and-redress.md) is the single detailed remedy/punishment schedule
for this Act. MP04/08/09 proved conduct may receive proportionate nonpunitive correction; private
recovery needs actual causation/quantum. Court alone orders contested merger prohibition, material
transfer and punitive VD11 guilt. VD11 is limited to intentional implemented naked cartel and
knowing/reckless completion of an objectively reportable deal during its live MP15 standstill/court
extension; it does not punish exclusionary economic judgments or below-gate transactions. There is
no MP-only custody, class-A general reply offense or automatic cartel damages. Neither remedy nor
fine is received delivery or new resources.

**MP19 — One bounded owner, direct remedies.** [MA01–MA16](05-chief-and-statutory-careers.md)
completes the markets authority's leadership, career, case, court, funding and execution chain.
MA06–MA11 supplies source/decision/review and MA12–MA16 resources/failure; none creates a second
merger gate or clock. Ordinary courts retain their actual jurisdiction, CJ only its constitutional
component. Initial source law and actual people, authority, paid capacity, independent records and
received remedies remain unknown. This chief/career choice expressly displaces the old unspecified
C05 panel and V10's generic staggered description for this office alone.

adopting Act**, prospectively for conduct after that date; existing stronger lawful rules and due
claims operate meanwhile. Commencement is not delayed by readiness, a missed price update or an
office vacancy: map unready functions to lawful existing protection and disclose the unmet duty,
while the initial Act must fix B before the numerical merger gate can apply. Map pending old
mergers/cases, authorizations, contracts, buyer and worker claims, wages, pensions, data, IP,
essential supply, customer funds, creditor priority and foreign law to named successors; preserve
U/Y/H/TW/NP/CP, M banking and C03 civic access. No fine funds the authority; no breakup without
owned cash/FX, qualified independent operator, necessary tech/IP permissions and safe receiving. If
a regulator misses a clock, reveal capacity and allow lawful court action within MP17; no perpetual
standstill, approval by silence, transfer of sovereign policy to the chief or automatic public
bailout. An Assembly-funded public/cooperative alternative can be chosen separately when rivalry
cannot deliver a due essential floor. Reopen if actual entry, labor bargaining, prices, rights,
source evidence, court execution or capture shows this package inferior to the live rivals. The Act
fixes initial B in its domestic unit before the 180-day numerical gate commences; later missing

earlier signed but uncompleted concentration is tested using commencement as MP12's first benchmark

before that day. Deals completed before commencement keep old law and pending old proceedings; new
