# Continuing learning and renewal belong to the learner

Design173 · [AL01–AL20](../../country/C07/22-lifelong-learning-and-renewal.md)

**An adult can initiate meaningful public learning without an employer or a
selected production project choosing their direction.** Preserve existing
foundational catch-up, first qualifying pathways, paid N contracts and TW13.
Add a continuing offer and a separately assessed qualifying renewal route.

| Public commitment | Actual boundary |
|---|---|
|64 supported adult learning hours | Voluntary offered capacity, not paid leave, private-study meter or a full-path ceiling |
| Meaningful qualifying renewal | Practical capability need and proportionate suitable support; no employer or lifetime fiscal-score gate |
| Chosen direction | Alternatives serve the accepted aim or a freely accepted revised aim; no imposed career to close a dashboard |
| Usable first step | Necessary preparation/accommodation inside the12-month proposed limit, with actual accepted later continuation |
| Public advanced institutions | Staff/learner/public governing roles, large-workforce parity, academic senate and outside remedy |
| Paid participation | Actual instruction/materials/access/care, living-income rights, qualified cover and continuity; no free instructor or automatic full wage |

[Examples and genuine alternatives](01-offers-capacity-and-rivals.md) compares
person-directed public services with smaller continuing offers plus N-funded
projects, portable money accounts and unrestricted repeated-degree provision.
The [decision](../../2026-11-person-directed-learning-and-renewal/decision-record.md)
qualifies original sources and fresh actual corrections. None demonstrates
complete national funding, legitimate adoption or improved lives. Public learning
funds no political loyalty, coerced AI use or unproved professional licence.
