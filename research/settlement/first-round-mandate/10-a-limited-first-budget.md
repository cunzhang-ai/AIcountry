# A first budget that stops at the national responsibility

*Design543 · Constructed opening decision under the national settlement.*

Use the same lawful inherited polity, residents, service rights, liability
and qualified roles throughout. Existing basic sources, valid payments,
staff and courts continue. A legally competent authority has repealed
an inherited power through the proper process. An independent substitute
must now receive a correction before an electoral deadline. At the same
time two residents are due housing, care or an established cash payment.
Ordinary provider routes exist. One resident's accepted care requires a
last-mile connection that a timely schedule change already supplies under
existing law. A separate overdue benefit needs an accepting corrected
payment channel. All figures are assumptions.

| Assignment | Eligible funds first / next | What the example permits |
|---|---|---|
| Independent authority and execution | Reserved extra cost 2 / 2, on its own source | Use the distinct staff, lawful remedy and receiving officer on both calls. The service payer cannot draw this source. |
| Service repair | Eligible cash 4 / 3 on the service payer | Correct the payment channel for 2 / 2. Confirm accepted ordinary scheduling on its own existing terms. The repair leaves 2 / 1 uncommitted. |
| New staffed connection schedule | A further 2 / 2 when run in both windows | It duplicates the covered ordinary connection here and the later service balance is short by one. Defer. |
| AI works evaluation | Separately proposed 2 / 2 for evaluation, plus accepted independent review | The first budget leaves no funded two-window source on these facts. Defer. Expected gains pay none of the above. |

**Select the core instruments and ordinary repair.** The legislature votes
its exact continuation and required repair lines. Neither proposed optional
programme begins. Existing ordinary housing and care officers keep their
duties. No general national provider model, business formula or resident
registration is required for this result.

If the ordinary schedule fails and the owner still owes a due connection,
use the owner's existing lawful correction or an already authorized
bridge. If no sector law assigns the connecting task and the
[limited schedule](../../country/C04/06/11-funded-cross-service-connections.md)
can supply the missing link, put its exact cost, extra staffing, consent,
displaced work and second-call funding to the competent bodies. A usable
first-window balance alone cannot fund recurring work. If an urgent publicly
arranged room would otherwise create unaffordable debt, compare an affordable
ordinary place, timely verified support and a direct public undertaking on
the [applicable housing rule](../../country/C09/25/06-affordable-temporary-receiving.md).
The safe feasible choice earns its own decision. Preserve original duties,
lawful emergency options and access to remedy if no route qualifies.

An opposition motion can choose a lower-cost ordinary channel or better
local delivery on the same schedule and duties. Only the competent body
can bind either route. Protect the successor's real options and do not
count future bonuses, disputed recoveries or hoped-for AI savings as
current funds. Reopen the common programme when a right cannot be
delivered, national coordination is indispensable, or ordinary repair
demonstrates equal durable protection at lower burden.
