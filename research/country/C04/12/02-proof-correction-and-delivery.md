# Evidence, correction and useful receipt

*Part of [TU effective take-up](../12-effective-take-up-and-early-benefit-delivery.md).*

**TU05 — Reuse the fact within its lawful scope.** Each source
retains custody. The programme receives the minimum verifiable
assertion for its named purpose, with origin, date, scope
and dispute status. A birth registration, for example, may
establish birth. It does not establish present care, parental
authority, actual income or the safe payee. Use an enacted
basis or a genuinely voluntary authorization consistent with
PD. Offer lawful alternative proof and protect third parties.
Prohibit general life dossiers, bulk scanning without distinct
authority and routine forwarding to unrelated enforcement.
A data interface is not a new legal purpose. Preserve the
source and safe correction trail without unnecessary copies.

**TU06 — Give a qualified official responsibility for each decision.**
Allow a named official to authorize a reviewed batch of
established dues using an inspectable deterministic calculation
under a fixed version of the law, facts and controls. Record the
authorization and per-recipient evidence, maintain safe exception
and duplication checks, and keep audit and independent challenge.
The official owns the result and can halt a faulty release.
AI may help explain a rule, flag a discrepancy or prepare
alternatives. A model, match or batch process cannot make an
adverse determination, decide disputed facts, choose on behalf
of a person, reduce an existing due payment or refer suspected
wrongdoing as a finding. A conflict routes to a qualified
human. Preserve prior due dates and urgent ordinary service
where the failure scope permits.

**TU07 — Review a wrong, missed or unsafe payment in useful time.**
Give a useful notice of the legal basis, material inputs, amount,
recipient and due date through a safe route. Keep direct assisted
and independent review for missed cases as well as wrong awards.
Use AP, PD, EV and the programme's substantive remedies. Correct
a bad source with the original custodian and trace the material
uses that need correction. Do not make the claimant carry a
public record correction between offices. If a verified due
payment is blocked or misdirected, the payer and case owner
preserve the original entitlement, use any lawful interim route
and arrange safe restoration by the earlier useful time. Backpay
and compensation follow the actual effective-date, fault and
remedy law. A loss does not disappear when the record is fixed.
Any reduction or recovery needs separate reasoned authority,
protection of the lawful floor, fair hearing and correction.
No clawback or account block follows solely from a duplicate alert.

**TU08 — Count delivery only when the person can use it.** Record
separately the indication, contact, accepted request, award,
payer instruction, usable cash or actual service, and remaining
loss. Confirm practical access without monitoring spending or
using a universal account. Offer a lawful payment alternative
for a closed, unsafe or inaccessible bank route and a staffed
alternate if a credential or vendor fails. Clinical and care
consent and actual staffed places stay with the relevant
providers. An eligibility notice, successful referral or posted
transfer does not by itself establish usable receipt. Preserve
other public or private claims and recognize resources only
under the controlling received-support rules.

[TU13–TU17](04-independent-payment-and-receipt.md) names the payer,
first-contact options and exclusive release rules for established cash.
The underlying entitlement and clock continue through channel failure.
