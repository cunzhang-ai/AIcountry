# WB01–WB04. Make protected reporting usable

*Part of [protected disclosure](../31-protected-disclosure-and-evidence.md).*

**WB01 — Protect reports on reasonable grounds.** A person may report
information they reasonably believe, in the circumstances then known,
shows serious wrongdoing. Covered matters include material breaches of
law, grave risks to people or ecosystems, serious abuse of entrusted
power or public resources, material falsification of evidence relied on
for public duties, and deliberate concealment of those matters. A person
may explain uncertainty and correct their account. Protection does not
depend on later substantiation, a prosecution, the reporter's status,
or proof of pure motives. Mere policy disagreement keeps its ordinary
speech and democratic routes. A grievance may also contain a covered
disclosure. Deliberate material fabrication has ordinary fair-process
consequences. A mistake, unsafe silence or withdrawal supplies no
automatic adverse inference.

Protect current, former and prospective workers, trainees, contractors,
suppliers' workers, volunteers and people materially dependent on an
implicated service or public authority. Protect a person targeted because
the retaliating actor believed or suspected they were a reporter,
and a witness, supporter or associated person against consequential
targeting. No political sponsor, payment, employment category, model or
nationality determines the protection within actual jurisdiction.

**WB02 — Give direct access outside the implicated institution.** A person
can use a safe internal channel, the competent external inspector or
statutory receiver, independent advice or direct court protection within
existing powers. No internal complaint, manager consent or group
mediation is a prerequisite. Use labour inspection for covered workplace
conduct, the appropriate sector for the underlying issue, AA for public
audit within its remit, EV for evidence correction and P/K for suspected
crime within those authorities. Preserve ordinary legal advice and source
protection. Those roles cannot finally judge their own disputed conduct.
The common route adds protection and safe referral. It gives no receiver
a new search, audit, prosecution or compulsory disclosure competence.

If a receiver lacks jurisdiction or is implicated, protect the first
receipt and route only to an authorized unimplicated receiver. Do not
return the report to the subject of it for clearance. Direct B17 court
protection and existing conflict assignment remain reachable. If no
lawful independent investigator is available, disclose the safe scope
of the failure and seek a competent remedy. A new label cannot supply
independence.

**WB03 — Protect contact and necessary evidence.** Accept safe assisted,
oral, written and anonymous reports. Where reply is possible, provide
a protected route. Establish the available evidence and keep the original
account, its interpretation and the date distinct. An anonymous tip
may justify proportionate inquiry, while an adverse merits finding
requires independently testable material and a fair chance to answer.
Use separately controlled original evidence and identifiers, minimal
lawful retention and direct PD correction. Do not route source identity
or derived identifiers into personnel, platform or programme systems.
Give usable notice and a chance to seek protection before a compelled
identity disclosure unless a court authorizes a specific necessary
delay with protective conditions and a defined end. The judge must
consider safe summaries, controlled access and necessary defense.
Where evidence cannot be fairly tested, do not rely on it to impose
an adverse merits result.

**WB04 — Respond and preserve evidence before it is lost.** Under this
proposed common procedure, give a safe acknowledgement within seven
calendar days of protected first receipt where safe contact exists.
Give a reasoned status, named receiving role and next review within
30 calendar days, and an updated reason at least every 60 calendar
days thereafter while the report remains active. Use earlier mandatory
duties and the actual useful time for safety, evidence and court relief.
Acknowledgement, plan and final determination are different. Document
actual transfer, accepted responsibility, scoped preservation and any
delay, unknown or closure reason. Protected source access and report
correction must remain usable during staff or system change. No
deadline licenses a disclosure, suppresses a report or requires an
unfounded final determination.
