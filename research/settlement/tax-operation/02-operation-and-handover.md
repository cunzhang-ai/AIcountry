# Paid operation: normal days and an adverse change of power

Design155 · Written design arguments, not tested events or staffed fallbacks

Annual net lending, received cash, qualified work, lawful power and observed
protection are different facts. TN does not infer the last four from the first.

| Situation | Required source/control/receiving | Failure and continuing duty |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary personal protection | Actual I05/Y payer, usable individual receipt, due period and distinct restricted S | A promised tax refund/basic pension is no cash; safe advance/correction and original arrears survive |
| Ordinary essential and NC care | Actual accepted competent contact/backup, paid leave/training/site/inputs and concurrent supply | Annual staff-equivalents can fail the day; family/AI is no missing caregiver and cost remains unknown |
| Ordinary justice/enforcement | Separate competent police/inquiry/prosecution/court/counsel/custody/clinical roles, actual paid independent case execution | One employee cannot supply simultaneous incompatible independence; PO adds no free capacity or force |
| Election and peaceful replacement | Actual standing election/opposition/source/information/court access and sector execution | Budget dispute or outgoing Cabinet cannot reset ballot/remedy dates; aggregate public surplus is no independent source control |
| Source-law transition | Exact prospective tax/source periods, old collectable duties, protected net promises and actual new receipts | A lower rate is not source-neutral by name; avoid tax-gap double spending, no retroactive residual levy |
| Restricted contribution | Correct personal labor qualification, actual remittance, Y accrual and protected solidarity liabilities | Consolidation grants no G use; employer default cannot erase proved work/old claim |
| Hostile outgoing police/vendor | Lawful incoming authority, actual records/pay/keys and accepted independent court/source execution | PO finite command/DP sector restoration apply; no holdover by password, shared patron or invented outside investigator |
| Own-director abuse during shortfall | Actual unimplicated qualified accepted outside-command allocation and paid prosecutor/court/remedy | Different label/account/AI is no independence; if all actual routes fail, report failure and preserve claims, not fictitious successful redress |
| Annual surplus but early payroll due | Source owner/currency, opening unrestricted cash and accepted actual finite bridge before due date | Future tax/annual lending cannot pay an earlier missing balance; liability and urgent due-time remedies persist |
| Old/new operator overlap | OP identified old obligor/successor, lawful work/asset/record terms, separate paid tasks and exact dates | No universal reset, duplicate saving or transfer of assets without debts/pension/contract/case tails |
| Foreign/private source assumed | Actual owner/acceptance, currency, import logistics and restricted purposes | Reference residents' saving or other states' workers/assets are not national resources available by research selection |
| Both receiver and fallback unavailable | Existing liable sector/elected source duties, independent urgent review and actual accepted alternatives where available | No research text supplies a worker, cash or consent; continuing claim, harm and actual unresolved delivery remain visible |

These12 paths compare the same current service-first democratic country with
source-preserving retain/repair and an explicitly replaced mixed-source offer.
Direct public/common/contracted provision and smaller independent digital
routes retain their own source, competence, ownership, consent and transition
costs. A simpler arrangement can win; adding offices/review is not progress.
No real receivers, affected participants or accepted financing were obtained.
The tax experiment improves the source boundary but does not complete the
whole country year or certify safe handover. The next bounded TN12 comparison
must resolve base matching and largest paid increments rather than adding
another nominal institution or assuming actual authority is needed for design.
