# C06. Public debt, distress and democratic burden sharing

*Part of [C06 public finance](../C06-public-finance.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

Lawfully incurred public debt is a claim on future public resources, not an extra
resource and not a reason to erase existing service rights by accounting
entry. The Assembly authorizes each borrowing power, ceiling, permitted
instrument, guarantee and debt-manager mandate through prior law. Treasury
issues and services only within it; a public firm, locality, central bank or
minister cannot pledge national tax, land, mineral, port or future service
revenue by an undisclosed side letter. The common account publishes stocks,
holders where lawfully knowable, currency, governing law, collateral,
maturities, interest, guarantees, contingent liabilities and likely rollover
needs, with protected commercially sensitive detail under opposition/audit
access. A guarantee not yet called is still exposure, not free headroom.

## Distinguish the failure before choosing the remedy

Treasury and original source custodians report rolling cash and foreign-
currency due dates, collectable taxes, domestic settlement, physical imports,
bank and pension exposures, and accrued service and creditor claims.
Where secured borrowing or a hedge can create a material collateral call,
record the gross cash call by currency, payer, custodian and settlement
cutoff alongside original creditors and essential imports. Reconcile
each relevant haircut, prior encumbrance, accepted substitution and
actual release. Reserve or count the same collateral only once. Book an
expected return separately until it is received, available and usable.
Test optional commitments on the resulting due-day balance under the
same adverse path. Verify the accepted instrument and settlement route
independently. A later shortfall preserves binding contractual claims,
the applicable payment law and standing service remedies.
Independent audit and plural analysis test four different diagnoses:
**temporary cash timing**, **structural debt unsustainability**, **foreign-
currency/payment access failure**, and **physical supply shortage**. They
may overlap, but a favorable growth or AI forecast cannot turn one into
another. Publish uncertainty, competing assumptions and effects on people
who do not hold bonds. A technical risk grade prompts an Assembly hearing;
it cannot declare default, cut an entitlement or order the central bank to
finance Treasury.

Before any missed payment, the elected Assembly compares coherent packages:
lawful tax and collection changes with incidence; adjustment or delay of
future discretionary commitments with rights and contract protection;
temporary borrowing if terms and actual lenders exist; negotiated maturity
or rate changes; and, if unavoidable, a legally grounded debt workout.
Show each package's due-date cash, debt path, prices, domestic-bank capital,
deposit and pension claims, imports, local budgets, workforce and basic
service delivery. Do not call a tax increase available cash before it can
be collected, or count nominal debt relief twice as pension and health
funding. Protect a prior-law essential-obligations schedule and immediate
individual remedies within actual capacity; this does not give a cabinet
authority to cancel creditor law or compel foreign medicine shipments.

## A bounded public workout, not an executive side bargain

If the Assembly finds normal finance cannot meet lawful obligations without
unacceptable rights or material-life losses, it may enact a **negotiation
mandate** stating the debt perimeter, lawful negotiators, disclosed objective,
maximum new public risk, time limit, protected records and reporting dates.
Affected service users, workers, pensioners, depositors, local governments,
creditors and external partners need meaningful, appropriately protected
routes to challenge the incidence and alternatives. Negotiators may keep a
narrow time-limited bargaining position confidential, but material terms,
collateral, policy conditions and distributional consequences must be
published before an exact binding Assembly decision. No lender may gain
an undisclosed policy veto; no creditor holding alone confers a vote in the
Assembly. Courts review authorization, contractual claims, fairness and
rights under applicable law, not the political choice of a fiscal package.

Domestic-law claims, foreign-law claims, local debts, public-enterprise
liabilities and pensions have different obligors and enforcement routes.
Their renegotiation requires consent or a valid, reviewable law that can
actually govern the claim; AIcountry cannot bind foreign creditors, a treaty
partner or a court abroad by national declaration. A payment pause, exchange,
haircut, index change or redenomination is **not automatic** when an
indicator turns red. If lawful payments cannot be made, Treasury records
the missed claim, reasons, interest and creditor remedy, while the Assembly
chooses a publicly justified next package. Arrears remain liabilities until
lawfully settled. No artificial priority for political donors, state-owned
firms or connected lenders may be hidden in payment operations.

A domestic workout can damage banks, insured deposits, payments, public
pensions and the monetary authority's collateral at the same time. The
Assembly sees these losses and any proposed recapitalization or guarantee
as one package with explicit ceilings; a pension fund cannot be silently
cut to make a headline debt ratio look better. Domestic-currency issuance
may move losses through prices and exchange rates and cannot settle an
unconsenting foreign-currency claim. The [C06 monetary rule](02/01-currency-and-bank-resolution.md)
and [external-payment account](02/03-foreign-payments.md) keep their distinct
authorities. Debt terms may not secretly surrender constitutional tax,
ecological or service decisions. If no lawful agreement is reached, the
state reports the continuing creditor, import, credit and service harms;
there is no promised costless default or judicially invented money.

The [decision record](../../2026-10-sovereign-debt/decision-record.md) compares
retention, narrow liquidity support, austerity, negotiated workout and
monetary escape claims. Its thresholds and distribution choices require a
real founding/public decision, original contracts and capacities. No debt
crisis, creditor consent or superior outcome has been observed for AIcountry.
