# A1–A15: bounded current-country coherence review

Design143 · Rank/power/date comparison, not all laws, contracts or outcomes

Compared current candidate core with relevant C02–C06/C10/C14 interfaces and the
integrated C01–C14 design. This review locates a major positive-rule gap; it does
not claim every downstream topic was freshly substantively revalidated.

| Clause | Current consumer and scoped result |
|---|---|

| A2 | C03/C04/C14 elected parliamentary competence/residual allocation: retained; conditional map/form must precede first contest |
| A3 | C11/C12 records/AI assistance: retained; authentication is not sovereign approval |

| A8 | C10 emergencies versus ordinary restrictions and nonderogable rights: no new conclusion; precise limitations/derogation reconciliation is the next bounded review |
| A9 | C14 RR/PC source-law route, no self-adoption, protected succession: retained; national petition denominator remains initiative-opening, final voters ballot-day |

A15 was chosen over another private-tax vignette because territorial democracy
otherwise depended on a future discretion the framework explicitly claimed to
bound. Actual map, collective sources, treaties and founding authority cannot be
filled by invented research facts. Those qualification gaps survive the positive
candidate. A8 is a remaining interface question, not a finding that ordinary
protection is forbidden or a claim this legal spine is finished.
