# Protect ordinary duties after loss of the legal supply forum

*Design522 · Scoped national continuity proposal*

[BLC11–BLC15](../../country/C06/66/02-protected-supply-through-an-impasse.md)
specify early appropriation, independently established trigger and useful
time, distinct custodians and payers, a fixed end and continued claims.
A rejected budget or obstructed sitting in a legally competent legislature
uses its ordinary routes. The proposal does not extend the government or
reassign budget authority after a loss of quorum.

The [compound case](../../settlement/first-round-mandate/03-protected-supply-through-impasse.md)
checks a full legislative annulment, fiscal expiry, the next valid supply
opportunity, shared funding risk and a lived combination of services,
independent correction and civic access. Its constructed results support
a conditional design selection. They do not establish lawful enactment,
paid readiness, actual results or superiority.
