# Delegated law: expiry is real, existing rights still have owners

*DR13–DR16; part of [C03/11](../11-delegated-rules-and-effective-public-control.md).*

**DR13 — No general emergency is created.** An immediately necessary temporary
ordinary rule can bypass the normal timetable only where a prior enabling Act
already names issuer, observable grave danger, precise scope and urgent means.
Show why existing law or a lawful individual protective act cannot suffice,
minimum effects, actual source/receiver and original start. Publish immediately
in usable channels, notify the Assembly/Clerk within24elapsed hours or earlier useful time, and provide
direct B17 useful-time review. Outer expiry is the **earlier of its own source
expiry and seven calendar days from first effect**. For this date-based rule,
the first-effect date is day0 and effect ends at start of day7; any exact
shorter source-hour limit wins. No renaming, related replacement or partner/
software update resets it. No reserved choice, new crime/tax/force/surveillance
power or missing appropriation arises. This ordinary-rule exception neither
extends nor replaces C10/D9, P/LF or sector-specific individual powers.

An affirmative vote alone does not dispense with mandatory ordinary consultation
or the final-text window. Continued ordinary delegated effect needs the completed
normal route; the Assembly can instead enact a necessary bounded primary statute
through its valid procedure. If this cannot be done before expiry, the temporary
rule ends. Existing lawful rescue/service powers remain, but cannot impersonate
the expired general norm. Short safe adaptation may therefore be lost: a real
tradeoff, not a claim that seven days guarantees adequate protection.

**DR14 — Define the default, not just the deadline.** A proposed new rule that
is called, unapproved, withdrawn or lapsed has no effect. Its proposed repeal
of an old rule also has none: the independently valid existing law continues
until its own lawful replacement/expiry. A rule that already lawfully replaced
an earlier one does not automatically revive that predecessor when later
annulled/expired; DR18 and the enabling Act identify lawful residual duties.
No deadline cancels due care, benefits, worker pay, pending claims, individual
orders or court remedies. Missing money/staff is an unmet duty, not emergency
authority, suspended elections or a right to cut an entitlement by guidance.

**DR15 — Revisit existing law without fabricating a retroactive mandate.**
Lawful pre-commencement rules and completed rules validly made after any required approval continue
on their own source law; this Act does not cure an unlawful legacy rule.
Any member may require a separately reasoned annulment/repeal motion to be
listed within7calendar days and considered at a protected sitting within30days
of receipt, with7days motion notice and the DR10 full-N vote to annul under
this special route. No vote leaves the existing rule, not a claim of public
approval. Failure is visible/challengeable. No single member's renewed motion
can suspend an existing rule outside its initial call window; court interim
relief and primary legislation remain. Distinct material new effects use DR12.
This preserves continuity at a cost: a hostile majority can keep an old lawful
but harmful policy. Compare broader automatic legacy expiry with actual sector
capacity rather than conceal that distributional choice.

**DR16 — Fixed prospective commencement and genuine succession.** A competent
adopting Act starts this procedure **180calendar days** after its enactment;
no readiness certificate extends it. Before then the Clerk and each issuer
publish an inventory of actual sources, existing versions/sunsets, pending
proposals/cases, funds, staff, licenses and named custodial/pay successors.
Every new rule and every operative amendment after commencement uses this route
at its proper tier, including routine amendments to legacy rules; only DR12
nonoperative clerical corrections use its logged exception.
unmade old drafts complete its final window and any missing consultation.
No retrospective criminal/fiscal burden or extinguished earlier claim follows.
An Assembly's actual legal end lapses its unfinished proposals, including approved but still unmade proposals; approval
alone cannot preserve an unmade rule through that legal end. The new Assembly
requires fresh L and full30/45. An election call alone does not end the existing
Assembly under C03/06. Caretaker limits remain; no lapse revives an outgoing
cabinet's policy mandate. Source law/constitutional conflict needs competent
resolution, not a researcher asserting repeal of current law/software holds.

**Design144 RX join.** A declared emergency's14elapsed days cannot extend DR13's
earlier source/day7 expiry; a three-fifths declaration-renewal vote is not DR10
approval or a primary Act. [RX08](../../C10/01/02-declaration-expiry-and-ordinary-law.md)
adds the prospective ordinary broad movement/assembly/participation sunset and
fresh-primary-law continuation requirement; normal delegated procedure still
applies where used. Neither route permits domestic constitutional derogation,
new underlying powers, live-case self-amendment or a reset of actual old clocks.
