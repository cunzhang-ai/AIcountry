# Design198 decision. Preserve claims and qualify real budget releases

*9 November 2026 · Research selection · Exact design197 predecessor.*

## Gap and disposition

DB11–DB14 already protect lawful duties, distinguish expenditure from
payment and control carry-forward. FB01–FB08 already require a recurring
source for a lasting promise. The unresolved joint issue is how to qualify
an asserted efficiency or favourable variance when the same change may
hide a lost service, delayed obligation or household burden. Select
FB09–FB12 to distinguish actual releases, approve lawful uses at the
right authority and correct a failed release. Use existing budget,
audit, court, service and remedy roles. Do not add a fiscal guardian.
[Canonical clauses](../country/C06/62/01-underspending-and-released-capacity.md)
· [Predecessor](baseline.json)

A finance officer can show lower outlays without knowing why. The public
operator can show who received care without knowing what cost was
removed from the budget. Join both with original scope and dates. For an
efficiency, compare ordinary and difficult receiving with strong repair
under the previous structure and account for whole recurring public
costs. Keep shifted burdens, lawful policy reduction, hours redeployed
and finite implementation separate. Require a relevant completed cycle
for qualification. Do not make cycle length a new statutory service
deadline. An equivalent simpler path may win. The Assembly decides the
new funded offer and retains express prospective lawmaking.

## Evidence, rivals and strongest objections

Copenhagen's 2025 ordinary operations and gross unused authority
moved in opposite directions. The October 2026 forecasts and staffing
follow-up show that changed enrolment, preservation of existing staff,
hiring and a separately identified earmark have different meanings.
The extended-hours committee record gives a concrete retain/repair
route with an actual decision to look further. Low inquiries can reflect
lower need, unsuitable hours or access barriers. The observed sources
do not resolve which explains unmet care for excluded families. Scope the
local disposition to evidence and assisted repair first. A different
employer or national architecture needs additional causal evidence and
paid transition. The national proposal remains service-first plural.
[Source inspection](sources.md)

Uniform sweeps offer predictable cash and simpler control but can punish
honest reports and interrupt projects or transfer costs to families.
Automatic retention protects local discretion but can preserve idle or
captured funds and block elected changes. Qualification can itself delay
legitimate savings or lock in incumbents. Apply it to material permanent
sources, use existing records and safe targeted observations, allow finite
authorized trials with separate finance and protect earlier useful-time
duties. Use the existing decision process for finite verified balances.
Independent audit and court review require actual paid capacity.
The design does not guarantee clean data, ready staff or sufficient money.

## Continuing duties and next test

Preserve DB owner/purpose/permission, FB four-year and permanent-source
rules, FC local receiving, NC qualified staffing, AP accessible intake,
SR actual individual relief, OC reserves, HT effective powers and OP/T
paid succession. Transfer no earlier legal authorization or final review.
An AI may assist scoped checks, but cannot approve its own case, allocate
resources or substitute for source or accepting people.

The inspected public records supply bounded evidence for competing
explanations. There was no actual affected-party participation in this
research, independent final review, country adoption or qualified
operation. For the next decision compare the same extended-hours family
journey and provider window under accessible retain/repair and a paid
targeted extension. Reopen on evidence of suppressed need, a released
source with degraded care, a failed payment or a better less burdensome
design. A local finding cannot establish a fully sourced country.
