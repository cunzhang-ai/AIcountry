# C03. People can put an ordinary national proposal before the Assembly

Design174 ·4November2026 · NI01–NI22 proposed statutes and reserved constitutional option, not an adopted procedure

**Choose a public national agenda initiative without a member sponsor.**
Enough actual electors supporting a stable ordinary national proposal earn
paid preparation, a protected hearing and a recorded Assembly disposition.
They do not enact law, appropriate money or call a referendum. The Assembly
may reject the proposal, with public reasons and accountable votes.

A2/C03 already recognise public origination. Opposition/member agenda, [resident
panels](24-resident-deliberation-and-elected-response.md), local LR petitions, constitutional A9/RR and territorial TS routes
already exist. NI supplies the missing ordinary national implementation,
without borrowing their threshold, electorate, finance or prior approval.
Existing E19/NP/B19/B20 failure-correction institutions are retained.

[NI17–NI22](16/06-public-confirmation-of-service-withdrawal.md) select a separate
prospective constitutional option for elector confirmation of one defined
withdrawal after ordinary enactment. The opening agenda initiative confers no
right to call that poll.

Thresholds and calendars are contestable normative hypotheses. A meaningful
minority can require consideration; it cannot cast Assembly votes or claim
that support for an agenda is consent to a materially different final bill.
Human/AI assistance and future media preserve actual principal, scope,
revocation and meaning, with practical non-digital participation.
