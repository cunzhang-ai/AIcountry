# National package: rivals and conditions for displacement

*Canonical NP19–NP20; part of [C06/33](../33-protection-production-and-national-expansion.md).*

**NP19 — Keep institutionally different packages.** Compare the same current
obligations, affected people and preparation/completion/renewal horizon, not the
same artificially fixed bids, payout assumptions or chosen GDP.

| Package | Mechanism and attraction | Real cost/risk and disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Retain and repair | Preserve working institutions/relationships; fix maintenance, exclusion and remedies without a whole new service regime | Can entrench inherited inequality and dependence; a live lower-transition rival, never permission to ignore existing duties. |
| Protection-first mixed provision | Reserve personal/democratic capabilities first, pay public residual or accepted precontracted capacity, progressive burdens and enacted additional cohorts | Working reference. Standby costs, public patronage, means-assessment power and delayed improvements remain; not proven globally best. |
| Comprehensive earlier guarantee | Enact more coverage sooner through actual recruitment/partners, redistribution and all necessary protection | Can remove severe exclusion earlier; larger simultaneous tax/workforce/import/transition need and immediately overdue claims if missed. A model's shortage does not establish it inferior. |
| Comprehensive direct provision | Public/shared ownership, paid workforce and stocks give direct responsibility and change bargaining/control | Acquisition/compensation, management, overlap, recruitment, maintenance and public monopoly/capture costs; a real structural alternative rather than free ownership. |
| Prepaid regulated plural capacity | Pay accepted private/cooperative capacity and enforce equal access/exit without a comprehensive public workforce | May use actual diverse supply well; provider rents, common controllers, thin markets and unusable step-in can defeat it. |

The reference can choose a bounded prepaid alternative for a specific service
without assuming its national structural rival has been adopted. A political
value choice cannot be settled by averaging incompatible ownership or time paths.
More monitoring/reviews are costs as well as possible protections.

**NP20 — What this selection does and does not complete.** NP specifies the
national allocation, source/production/household rules, paid receiving choice,
shortage treatment and duty to decide/prepare. The current37-function requirements
map locates known duty families and later increments without treating them as
complete law or calibrated estimates. Actual values are unknown, not zero.
Context-specific quantities require the NP02/NP09 task/useful-time selection rule,
Assembly-bounded source/commitment, professional safety and independent challenge;
no unspecified official may fill them with unlimited expenditure or eligibility cuts.

The numerical examples test bounded cash-class and accepted-capacity assumptions;
they do not cost all 37 functions or prove national supply. The full parameterized
material package remains unqualified until all required roles/resources/periods
are actually estimated and compared. This is a limitation of claimed feasibility,
not permission to leave national priorities or institutional authority undefined.

Reopen on unmet essentials, actual worker/household/affected accounts, harmful tax
incidence, private investment/saving responses, controlled backup/review, ecological
or import limits, missing duties, changed source events or a better complete model.
The next integrated task is the objective-wide causal/distributional comparison,
carrying this unqualified material feasibility honestly. If actual quantities or
another contradiction become available first, address them rather than follow a
fixed study chain. Superiority, legitimate adoption and received wellbeing remain
separate unverified claims; no ledger can establish them by construction.

Design 85 [CS](../../C01/03-country-case-and-competing-designs.md) now supplies
the written objective-wide comparison with scoped positive/adverse studies.
Accepted capacity, take-up and parental employment are intermediate results,
not proof of child/recipient wellbeing. Actual source/quality, hidden nonusers,
retained competence and cumulative burden can defeat NP. The new audit, outreach
and HC increments need full requirement/source estimates; no old bid or study
coefficient qualifies them. Next full-duty material/transition comparison.
