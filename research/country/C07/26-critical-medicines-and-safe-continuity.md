# C07. Critical medicines and safe continuity

*Design228 · MQ01–MQ10 · Proposed clinical supply and receiving duties.*

**Protect the treatment window when a medicine cannot be supplied.**
Use separate authority for product release, physical dispatch, individual
treatment and independent remedy. Buy independently reachable stock and
capacity against the actual treatment deadline. When the prescribed
product is unavailable, follow the patient to safe receipt or record
the unmet clinical duty and its next correction.

1. [Define the risk, buy capacity and require early warning](26/01-criticality-buying-and-warning.md)
   assigns product scope, exposure, purchasing and evidence.
2. [Activate a lawful response and protect each patient](26/02-release-substitution-and-receiving.md)
   governs dispatch, quality, consent and individual delivery.
3. [Keep independent oversight and restore capacity](26/03-review-replenishment-and-migration.md)
   follows unmet needs, correction, replenishment and lawful change.
