# C06. Employment, prices and household security

*Design184 core, Design280 energy shock and Design284 receiving · MS01–MS32 · Proposed national policy, not a rate decision or appropriation.*

Select **medium-term price stability and the highest sustainable voluntary
employment as joint monetary objectives**, alongside existing automatic personal
protection and elected fiscal/capacity choices. Employment is no longer merely
secondary to price stability in this candidate. Neither objective automatically
overrides the other; neither authorizes sacrificing basic rights, compulsory work
or a Treasury overdraft. Monetary instruments cannot supply missing real inputs.

This expressly revises M14/MC01's objective hierarchy, retaining the2% consumer-
price reference,1–3% reporting band, actual MC seats/votes/charter/instruments,
elected fiscal power and all actual prior-law boundaries. It promises neither
zero unemployment nor a particular inflation result. There is no new agency,
universal public-job entitlement, employer bailout or automatic stimulus formula.

The strongest retain/repair rival is the prior flexible inflation objective:
it already permits shock-sensitive adjustment and rejects automatic austerity.
A joint mandate can enlarge discretion or weaken price/FX credibility without
creating capacity. Voluntary public employment, employer retention and stronger
fiscal/monetary regimes are different whole choices with real costs and control.
[The decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-employment-prices-and-democratic-stabilization/decision-record.md)
selects an explicit value balance under uncertainty, not a claim of originality,
empirical superiority, funded operation or better lives already observed.

[AT technology transition](../C05/18-technology-transition-and-shared-gains.md)
adds a timed fiscal and service disposition for material earnings and
control loss. It does not change monetary instruments, standing support
eligibility or the existing lawful vote.
