# Completion finding. A binding remedy and a recurring failure have working paths

Design197 specifies partial receipt, the cause of non-execution, a
bounded agency repair for proven recurring breach, independent verification
and evidence for closure. The Assembly retains fiscal choice and the
individual keeps direct relief. [Canonical rules](../../country/C08/30-repeated-public-failure-and-remedy.md)

Court records and an administrative audit distinguish continuing grants,
financial verification, payments and final cases. The authored receipt
example preserves late and unresolved obligations. Actual authority,
funded execution and outcomes remain open.
