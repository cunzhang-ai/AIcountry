# C08. Fair recovery and a bounded way to start again

14 October 2026 · Design 63 · Candidate U01–U12, not enacted insolvency law

Select a court-controlled private insolvency route that preserves basic
life, assigns genuine losses and allows a bounded fresh start. It applies
to natural persons and ordinary enterprises, with simplified access for
small firms. Bank, safeguarded-client-asset and sovereign resolution retain
their separate C06 law; no household procedure creates bailout authority.

| Part | Candidate provisions |
|---|---|
| [Access, stay and protected estate](14/01-access-stay-and-estate.md) | U01–U04: eligibility, notice, asset/control evidence, subsistence and priority |
| [Personal repayment and discharge](14/02-personal-repayment-and-discharge.md) | U05–U08: actual resources, bounded duration, protected exceptional claims, guarantors and records |
| [Enterprise, delivery and transition](14/03-enterprise-delivery-and-transition.md) | U09–U12: viable restructuring, loss/closure, neutral officers, bank/public and migration boundaries |

The country cannot make a poor person's debt permanent forced labor or
imprisonment; nor can it declare every creditor, worker or victim paid by
writing off a liability. Exemptions, costs, security, ordinary priority,
consenting/nonconsenting claims and unreceived support need explicit rules.
A borrowed balance is not safely received income, and a forecast surplus
is not a committed refinancing source.

The candidate uses a 36-month ordinary personal plan, at most one 36-month
extension for defined material fraud, half of safely available excess income,
a three-month needs cash buffer, a 12-month avoidance lookback and bounded
small-firm plan periods. These are openly provisional normative values,
not empirical best durations, enacted promises or proof of affordable credit.
Some proved maintenance/intentional-harm claims survive ordinary discharge
and can remain unrecovered; this exception/value conflict is explicit, not
a universal fresh-start promise. Existing source rights/cases need lawful migration.

[Cases](../../settlement/private-insolvency/README.md) preserve employee,
child-support, harmed-person, secured-creditor and small-business concerns.
No participant, court, trustee, insurer, borrower or actual lender agreement
is available. Discharge exposes a creditor loss; it does not fund the nation
or prove that people are better off. Retain/repair collection, creditor-led
workouts and public/blanket relief remain substantive alternatives.
