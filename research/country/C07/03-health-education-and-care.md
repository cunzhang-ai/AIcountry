# C07. Health, education and care: one guarantee, three delivery duties

*Part of [C07 social floor](../C07-social-floor.md); integrated design 56 research, not enacted law.*

The national social floor requires effective access under enacted need,
quality, time and accessibility standards. It is financed with real cash,
qualified people, facilities and remedies. A budgeted slot, referral or
digital appointment is not service delivered. The [C07 access
rule](01-effective-access.md) preserves the person's first claim date and
allows a voluntary joint plan without merging distinct entitlements or
forcing one data account on a family.

| Service | Accountable public chain | Independent correction |
|---|---|---|
| [Health](05-health-service-and-patient-control.md) | National pooled finance/state NCS employer/Chief, functional networks/internal purchasing and qualified additional providers; actual receiving on failure | [HG outside Chief Clinical Reviewer/ordinary courts](05-health-service-and-patient-control.md), clinical judgment/licensing/serious inquiry separate |
| [Education](06-education-and-learning-power.md) | National rights/finance floor; elected local school authority guarantees a place, support and a receiving school | [ED outside-provider career inspector](21-public-learning-and-school-power.md), protected learner voice, bounded site order and direct ordinary court relief |
| [Care](07-care-and-support-through-life.md) | National financing and equalization; locally organized public residual and regulated additional providers | Independent safeguarding, eligibility and delivered-hours review with a direct route outside the provider |

These chains deliberately differ. Clinical judgment cannot be replaced by a
school admissions rule; a school curriculum cannot decide medical coverage;
and care assessment cannot remove a person's legal agency. National law sets
each entitlement, payer and provider-of-last-resort duty, while applicable
local democracy and professional judgment specify delivery within rights.
The [population-health duty](08-population-health-and-prevention.md) is a
separate ordinary function within health: it funds prevention, local outreach
and source-backed warnings without taking over individual clinical decisions
or C10's distinct compulsory powers.
The **clinical** service region is not an elected regional government.
Public, nonprofit and private providers retain their own safety, employment
and contractual duties; commissioning never transfers the state's residual
responsibility to an unreachable contractor.

A person with linked needs can ask a first receiver to convene a time-limited
joint access meeting. The person chooses whether to share information, while
each sector names its own decision, interim service, handoff date and appeal.
Refusing the joint route forfeits no separate right. A family member is not
booked as an unpaid worker or made the default consent-holder. During a
provider closure, status dispute, supply failure or cyber outage, an actual
accepting service must be identified and funded; the common case record may
coordinate but cannot mark all duties discharged by sending a referral.

C06 publishes each sector's legal entitlement, appropriation, workforce,
physical supply, imported inputs, payment route, waiting people, delivered
service and executed remedy
separately. One worker, room or vehicle cannot be booked as independent capacity
for several simultaneous contingency plans. A genuinely shared activity
may satisfy several outcomes with its actual resources counted once. The elected Assembly must
make visible material tradeoffs under rights and lawful shortfall rules;
neither a purchaser, inspector, court nor AI model can quietly rewrite the
general package. The [joint commitment schedule](../C06/18-joint-commitment-and-delivery-schedule.md)
requires explicit baseline inclusion and allocation across reproductive,
crisis, palliative and general clinical work, paid care, prevention and
independent review; these are neither automatically new bills nor unlimited
capacity inside a health heading. The [life-course service decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-life-course-services/decision-record.md)
compares the whole delivery arrangement and its limits.

[S01–S16](13-public-service-entitlements.md) now gives this guarantee explicit
candidate service content, learning/care offers, assessment clocks and
workload/transition rules. These hypotheses do not enact a package or make
it affordable; existing stronger rights and separate chains continue.
