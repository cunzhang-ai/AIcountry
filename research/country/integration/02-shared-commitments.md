# Shared commitments across time and borders

The same tax receipts, workers, homes, energy, water, land and reviewer hours cannot
be promised twice. Budget and physical-capacity accounts expose collisions to elected
decision-makers while preserving existing legal claims and allowing independent
review. AI can help people understand options and help staff perform bounded tasks;
it has no exclusive sovereign vote, no compulsory claim gate and no power to turn
resource scarcity into a hidden technical decision.

The [C06 joint schedule](../C06/18-joint-commitment-and-delivery-schedule.md)
now connects those promises to a [populated function/selection inventory](../../settlement/commitments/README.md).
Baseline inclusion, incremental expense and overlapping resources require
attribution; unknowns remain visible. A joint failure tests lawful service,
democratic scrutiny and independent remedy capacity at the same time.

The [candidate continuity Act](../C06/19-continuity-law-and-delivery-package.md)
now specifies a bounded due-income, urgent-care, independent-remedy and
political-continuity package. Included reference core shares leave other
claims short; their displacement requires explicit elected reasons and
cannot be hidden by naming the core protected.

The [C06 public-debt route](../C06/06-sovereign-debt-and-distress.md) keeps
lawful creditor claims, service rights, pension and bank exposures, foreign
payments and real imports in one elected comparison. Treasury can report a
shortfall and negotiate within an enacted mandate, but cannot distribute
sovereign losses by payment order or by an undisclosed lender condition.

The [physical communications and logistics rule](../C09/05-communications-and-logistics.md)
names the paths by which legal notices, election evidence, medicine, food and
repair parts reach people. It counts shared backhaul, fuel, drivers and cold
stores once; an online transaction or dispatched parcel is not actual receipt.
The [sanitation chain](../C09/06-sanitation-and-waste.md) needs actual treatment
and safe disposal. [Cyber recovery](../C10/04-cyber-incident-and-recovery.md)
needs trustworthy records and offline claims. [Customs clearance](../C10/05-customs-and-border-clearance.md)
needs a lawful release, independent appeal and a route for perishable goods.
These share workers, fuel, records and inspection capacity with other services.

Some commitments remove options from people who cannot yet vote. The
[C09 future-options duty](../C09/04-future-options-and-irreversibility.md)
requires a prior-law trigger, original stock evidence, less damaging feasible
alternatives, elected reasons and a funded repair path before a material
hard-to-reverse choice. Courts protect existing floors and process; neither
an AI forecast nor a future-generation guardian gets a general policy veto.

If several essential systems need one physical reserve, the [C06 scarcity
rule](../C06/05-cross-system-scarcity.md) bounds first-hour safety and
preassigned allocations under prior law. A new material priority returns to
the Assembly; missing stock and displaced duties stay visible, and no
operator's technical competence becomes a national rationing mandate.

The [C09 ecological path](../C09/08-climate-and-ecological-transition.md)
links climate mitigation, adaptation and noncarbon ecological duties to an
elected sector timetable. Its [C06 finance account](../C06/08-ecological-transition-finance.md)
checks power reliability, household and worker burdens, land, water, imports
and restoration together; a target or payment does not create physical supply.

The [C09 food-system route](../C09/09-food-systems-and-nutrition.md)
also shares land, water, workers, cold storage, freight and foreign currency
with other services. C06 counts the capacity once; C07 follows a person to
safe food actually received, while C08 retains independent safety action.
Neither a stock number nor purchasing power alone establishes adequate meals.
The [animal-welfare responsibility](../C09/10-animal-welfare-and-one-health.md)
adds living animals, veterinary care, inspection, safe custody and production
transition to the shared account. Welfare, food safety, human health and
ecological survival remain separate judgments, with any new burden publicly
chosen rather than hidden in a sector forecast.

The [C09 recovery rule](../C09/07-disaster-recovery-and-return.md) joins
temporary protection, insurance and property claims, local tax loss, actual
rebuilding capacity and an elected durable-place choice. Its [C06 finance
route](../C06/07-disaster-risk-finance.md) counts reserves, contingent debt
and insurance proceeds only when usable, and exposes displaced costs to
receiving places and future budgets.

Retirement security combines an individual basic old-age payment from general
revenue with a portable, capped earnings-related pooled public-insurance benefit.
Publicly funded credits cover specified care and interrupted-work periods;
health and long-term care are separate claims requiring people and places, not
only pension cash. An independent actuarial service publishes cohort scenarios,
but material contribution, benefit or retirement-age changes require elected
law and review of accrued rights. No AI model or reserve manager automatically
reduces a due payment or shifts care to relatives.
The [C05 automation account](../C05/04-automation-income-and-power.md) tests
paid-work and contribution changes alongside tax yield, household income,
ownership control and actual service workers. A modelled productivity gain
cannot be spent once as wages and again as public revenue.

External commitments use the same chain. Cabinet may negotiate, but material
ratification, provisional application, withdrawal, guarantees and security
commitments require prior elected authorization with their exact terms, continuing
duties and fiscal and physical exposure. A domestic vote cannot by itself cancel
another party's lawful claim. A public protection office receives border claims
under this country's authority; contractors and AI cannot issue an unreviewable
return decision. Local reception, critical import continuity and remedies for
foreseeable serious harm abroad require named law, capacity and review rather than
being counted as costless national gains.

The [protected-stage transition](../C14/06-staged-capacity-and-protection.md)
keeps existing claims and elected replacement in force during construction.
Available pre-growth sources, paid training/overlap, safe acceptance and
post-horizon operations enter the joined account; no unreceived export or
future output pays a present obligation.

[Current119 resource findings](../../settlement/country-integration/02-shared-resource-findings.md)
keep constitutional annual slack, ordinary/external courts, paid representative
cover, common-loss actual receipts and source dates distinct. Old prices/bids
are scoped subsets; no aggregate new national cost/deficit or free current
capacity is established. Existing lawful triage/claims and elected burdens remain.
