# Conditional budget and fallback

Design309 · Scoped result for [country completion](../../DESIGN-COMPLETENESS.md)

[BCV16–BCV20](../../country/C06/60/09-conditional-budgets-and-protected-fallback.md)
keeps independent elected approvals distinct and reserves only dated
uncommitted national resources for an optional conditional schedule.
Failure before commencement restores a protected final vote. Failure
after commencement follows ordinary continuity and claims law.

The [first-programme exercise](../../settlement/integrated-first-programme/10-conditional-housing-and-fallback.md)
compares timely approval, rejection, expiry, no quorum and later
payment failure. The assumptions include paid review and a scheduled
fallback. No actual local approval, real fiscal or staffing source,
paid parliamentary readiness or delivered result is established.
