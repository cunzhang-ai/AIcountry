# C09. Land, ecology, infrastructure and resilience: Housing land, transport and relocation

*Part of [C09. Land, ecology, infrastructure and resilience](../C09-land-and-ecology.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

Housing approvals and mobility routes must be assessed together with serviced
land, water, grid, hazard exposure, safe walking access, maintenance and the
time and cost of reaching work, care, school, courts and political participation.
A new transit line that displaces lower-income residents or a housing project
without a usable journey can fail the effective-access floor despite adding
units or route kilometers. Plans disclose who gains, who moves, which sites
and paths remain usable during construction, and who funds continuing service.
Local permitting still follows law and property process; an unmet national
guarantee is not a blanket project approval.

Acquisition and relocation require a lawful public purpose, necessity against
feasible alternatives, fair review, compensation where required and practical
rehousing before removal except immediate danger. Protect access to work, school,
care and community ties, not just the property's money value. Historic injustice
and reliance interests remain contestable; neither ownership nor a new plan erases
existing claims. Restoration and decommissioning costs belong to the approving
decision. Drought, contamination and grid scarcity use preadopted, reviewable
continuity priorities for life-sustaining access and ecological needs; temporary
official restrictions cannot invent a permanent allocation bargain.
The [disaster-recovery route](07-disaster-recovery-and-return.md) applies this
boundary when damage, hazard and local revenue collapse together.

[H01–H20 secure housing](12-secure-housing-and-repair.md) now selects the
ordinary tenure, permanent public/community option, allocation, finance and
repair/return chain. It does not infer occupancy from income groups or create
supply by buying existing titles. This planning/relocation boundary continues.

**Design145 acquisition completion.** [LA01–LA20](13-public-acquisition-and-secure-relocation.md)
replaces the formerly unspecified compensation/possession interface above with a
positive ordinary-law candidate. Planning permission alone transfers no right;
actual replacement, tax/debt/tenure, court/deposit and safe housing/access duties
must be met before taking. No actual title, payment or receiving is qualified.

[LU development permission](17-development-permission-and-serviced-land.md)
adds ordinary conforming-project decisions, no general residential parking
minimum, real serviced phases and lawful attributable infrastructure costs.
H/LA/CB/WS and independently necessary accessible journeys/old rights remain;
a permission, grant or higher site value is no completed affordable home.

[IV23–IV27](../C06/63/06-transport-housing-and-land-receipts.md)
applies these protections to transport investments and station-area
changes. Keep original residents, later entrants, usable access and
actual land receipts visible in their separate scopes.
