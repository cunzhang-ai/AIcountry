# PB09–PB12: changed life patterns change work, price and risk together

*Part of [C05/14](../14-ordinary-life-and-household-production.md); design 124.*

**PB09 — Compare the same voluntary take-up rate.** Old 108 home-help scope
was 60,000 buyers among 75,000 hypothetical homes,80 hours each. The new 50,000-home
case at the same 80%take-up has 40,000 buyers/3.2 millionhours, not the old 4.8 million.
This is ordinary optional private help, distinct from essential S/AS care. Do
not force spending or unpaid family labor to preserve GDP, jobs or tax forecasts.
Private cooking/cleaning/care/time still matters when no purchase occurs.

**PB10 — Retain the wage meaning actually in the source.** The old 5,000 low-
wage roles are full annual roles at 18,000CU, not.6 part-time posts. Old capacity
was not proved wrong merely because another cohort earned 30,000. Actual TW
hourly/weekly pay protections, availability, rest, qualifications and contracts
still need their own source. With retained 90 m salary plus 3 m inputs/.5 m renewal,
this reference keeps 93.5 m named cost. No lower volume silently dismisses workers,
erases wages or supplies free staff to another task.

**PB11 — Carry repricing and loss branches as well as new employment.** At
same 6%cost margin/10%VAT, retained staff/3.2 m hours require 34.0690625CU/customer
hour. Keeping the old 22.7127083 price produces a 27.426667 m loss before financing/
unknown costs; actual sales and wage financing remain conditional. A prospective
2,882 whole-post/30,000 salary recipe has 86.46 m payroll and 32.779175 unit price,
not a free transition for 2,118 displaced incumbents. Lawful retention, bargain,
paid reassignment, severance, I/TW protection, closure or supported reorganization
have distinct owners/source. Do not declare a winner from price or job count alone.

**PB12 — Preserve private plural organization and public safeguards.** Direct
employment, qualified firms/cooperatives, shared neighborhood purchasing and
self-provision can replace the commercial recipe, with actual wages/capital/
quality/control and voluntary participation. A chosen collective does not make
all residents liable or transfer political rights. Existing WL/OE/MP, consumer
and private insolvency law govern work/power/exit; ordinary help is not a public
unlimited-profit or job guarantee. Actual claims survive failure; lenders,
public receivers and courts do not create missing buyers, money or useful time.
