**RG12 — Refund entitlement, authorization and receipt are different.** A named qualified career refund determination/authorization officer gives the actual overpayment/refund decision under the remedies code or implements a binding reviewer/court determination, records claimant, source tax period, credited withholding/received amount, lawful offsets if any, due date and protected payer instruction. A separately assigned **Treasury career refund payer**, appointed/disciplined under Treasury's C04 administration rather than assessment control, verifies actual authorized payee/amount/source/duplicate-payment history, executes the transfer and confirms irrevocable accepted credit. It cannot reopen tax merits or hold a political opponent's payment for a minister's clearance. Corrected-source periods and interest follow that selected code; neither budget verification nor an unrelated new audit resets those clocks. No new offset, creditor rank, premium, tax rate or interest policy is created here.

A fiscal shortage does not erase or fictionally pay a refund. Publish actual paid/overdue amounts, remaining cash and adverse incidence safely; seek immediate B19/elected funding/NP source treatment within actual law. Existing standing/refund appropriation and narrow substitute execution must have actual adopted scope/payer/access, not generic E approval transferred by citation.
