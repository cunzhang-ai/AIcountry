# MF46–MF49. Assign elective revenue risk before a council takes it

*Design533 · Part of [funded local mandates](../08-funded-local-mandates-and-equalization.md) · Proposed ordinary law.*

**MF46 — Use the common financing basis for the common duty.** Assess
common-floor needs and representative capacity on the enacted uniform terms.
Where national law permits a council to elect a provisional financing basis
using its own tax or population forecast, make the common basis available
without requiring that election. Identify the difference between ordinary
changes in real population or capacity and the incremental fiscal exposure
from choosing a different advance and reconciliation schedule. Keep the
national residual and ordinary funding, review and receipt duties under MF.
The common basis does not guarantee every local receipt or expenditure.

**MF47 — Give the deciding council the result of its choice.** Before a
competent council opts in, publish the common and elective schedules, same
baseline assumptions, actual gross sources, settlement dates, grant effects
and downside limits. Set the election cutoff and pin the source vintage
and lawful reconciliation terms before election. Keep common realized inputs
consistent when attributing the difference. Report disputed attribution
separately. Match the result to the original election once, with no later
switch of basis. Assign the elective net result between the choosing council
and its authorized counterparty by default. Fund potential settlement calls
on each paying side before assuming the election is viable. Compare the opportunity cost,
the disposition of any unused reserve and at least one joint shock. Set the
source, binding exposure and responsibility for any residual in advance.
Where exposure cannot be bounded by the law or its funded terms, prefer the
common basis. Forecast gains confer no capacity to start recurring services.

**MF48 — Make any transfer of risk an express public choice.** An expressly
authorized national guarantee or pool may bear part of the elective risk.
Name its eligible members, payer, funded limits, allocation of gains as
well as losses, settlement and review. A competent legislature may choose
broader sharing through exact law with disclosed incidence and funding.
Present changes affecting nonparticipating councils as separate gross
amounts with proof that their ordinary rights and duly funded obligations
can still be met. An elective variance does not by itself change ordinary
equalization or authorize a deduction from another council's due payment.
General changes in assessed need or capacity use the same ordinary rules
for participants and nonparticipants. Do not classify those changes as
elective risk to evade national responsibility.

**MF49 — Correct a failed forecast without charging the resident.** At
settlement, verify the exact lawful payment or recovery and affected payer
before adjusting available resources. Apply the enacted terms and check
continued coverage. If an adverse tail threatens an existing service, use
available lawful local correction and the applicable common-service
backstop. Keep its cost and any recovery separate from the elected risk,
and preserve the resident's claim and deadline. Provide independent review
of attribution, withheld funds and delivered remedy. AI can reproduce
the defined comparison and flag duplicate settlements. It cannot elect a
budget basis, assign a disputed loss or certify receipt.
