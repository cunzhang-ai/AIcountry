# Protect due work before using the first-programme remainder

*Design294 · Constructed extension of the same matched first budget.*

The [previous release comparison](04-first-release-register.md)
prices K, an independent reserve for the optional energy bridge E.
It begins after protecting continuing duties. This case adds a
distinct loss in previously assumed ordinary cover at the shared
peak. The existing duties still stand. The constructed correction R
buys two qualified home-support slots, one independent ordinary
review slot and a separately accepted food delivery and replenishment.
It needs national cash 1 early and 2 total, local own cash 2 early
and 3 total, with no additional recurring or foreign line. The
food delivery uses a qualified route separate from the utility
logistics slots. Standing care, cash, court and election work
already paid in the baseline remains on its original schedule.
R repairs different exposed work from K. No food, home-care
or earlier liability-case units are imported as prices.

In this case Q costs one further national unit by day 90.
A qualified independent reviewer accepts those payment terms
and has an actually usable slot at the earlier peak. Verify the
slot before release. A court clerk, election reviewer, future
trainee or the already booked ordinary reviewer cannot be
credited as Q. With no usable Q the weaker case selects R and
ordinary repair. With independently funded Q and an exact vote,
the weaker case may select E with K and Q. The preference assumes
the additional energy protection is worthwhile against retaining
the reserve and ordinary repair. No partial approval of E + P
authorizes E alone. The cost of waiting for training and housing
remains visible. If P or H were chosen alone in the reference,
their omitted energy benefit must be shown too.

In a separate branch H bound before the loss. In the reference
it uses both remaining care slots and one review slot, so the
selected E + P start can no longer be treated as available.
In the weaker path H retains its lease and support but is one
early local own cash unit and two care slots short even with its
advance. A missing early advance widens the local payment gap.
Use a lawful funded bridge or record actual nonreceipt. Later
payment cannot backdate delivery.

If R cannot actually be funded, staffed or received, its
qualified part and the uncovered duty remain distinct. The
affected optional starts cannot rely on an assumed completed
correction. The numerical results are specific to this setup,
with no proof of national balance, contingency costs or delivery.
