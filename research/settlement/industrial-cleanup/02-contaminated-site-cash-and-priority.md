# A failed industrial operator and a contaminated water source

This is an authored case. All figures are fictional money units.
The local water provider can supply safe interim water, and the
environmental authority has an accepting contractor and lawful
access. The exposure requires the following work.

| When | Work | Cost | Usable source if the assumptions hold |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Day 2 | Safe water, sampling and immediate controls | 3 | Dedicated closure cash |
| Day 15 | Contain the source and secure treatment | 6 | Four in collectible surety and two in a voted emergency advance |
| Day 90 | Remediation and lawful disposal | 8 | Two in net site proceeds and six in the eligible residual account |
| Following year | Verify continued safety and monitor residual exposure | 2 | Separate reserved balance from the residual account |

The guarantee's headline is six units. Only four are payable by the
required date and under the applicable terms for this work. The
public advance of two is a real separate payment by day 15. The
later sale cannot protect water on day 2 or pay for the early
containment. Its proceeds can lawfully recover the advance if the
adopting Act and the court order so provide.

On day 60 the covered site realizes eight units gross. One unit
covers necessary realization costs. A valid earlier perfected lien
gets two. Of the remaining five, the prospective registered charge
has a ceiling of four and the court finds that at least four of
eligible work is uncovered or recoverable within its terms. Two
repay the same public advance and two pay for remediation. One
goes to the post-registration secured creditor, who is owed five.
Its four-unit deficiency retains ordinary U04 treatment. Separate
unencumbered estate money of two meets protected wage claims under
U04 in this example. The site charge draws on none of that money.
Actual ordinary unsecured distributions beyond the displayed scope
are unknown. Household damages and any contested insurance are
separate claims.

The four works cost 19. Dedicated cash and collectible surety supply
seven. The site contributes four net including repayment of the
advance. The residual account supplies eight, of which two are for
monitoring. The emergency advance is not counted again as a final
source. Without the registered charge, the same creditor receives
five from the post-legacy site proceeds. With the other assumptions
held constant, the protection package then needs four further units
of external funding, including the unrecovered advance. A broader
charge could collect more against other assets and impose wider
credit and distribution costs. Fully secured up-front closure
could reduce that exposure while raising the price of entry.

If no lawful advance arrives by day 15, two containment units are
unfunded despite the later plan. Continue available safe water and
protection, preserve independent relief and identify the actual
uncovered work. If the sale fails, the advance remains unrecovered
and two remediation units lose their source. If the residual
account is also unavailable, report the remaining funded capacity
and a lawful elected choice. No accounting rank, insurance limit
or estimate of ecological benefit pays an invoice by itself.
