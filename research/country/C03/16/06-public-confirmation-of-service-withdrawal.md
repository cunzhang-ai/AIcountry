# NI17–NI22. Let electors decide a contested withdrawal of essential access

*Design519 · Proposed constitutional option and implementing law*

**NI17 — Keep the Assembly's lawmaking and reserve one direct decision.**
Select prospective public confirmation for an ordinary national statute that
would retire the last independently usable route protecting a defined population's
access to a standing essential public service or payment during failure of the
ordinary channel. Include a formally replaced route if the proposed alternate
depends on the same controller, sole payer, receiving staff or custody and
therefore would fail with the ordinary channel. Include a statute that
requires such a withdrawal through a later order. A separately discretionary
administrative decision retains its own continuity duties and review. An accepted
independently usable substitute at the same effective time avoids this trigger. Use the same affected
population and useful deadline under MF37–MF40 and DP continuity rules. The
Assembly originates, debates, funds and passes the exact law. The public can
require and decide its confirmation under the rules below. The NI01–NI16
agenda initiative retains its separate hearing and vote. It cannot call this poll.

**NI18 — Establish the power and exact scope in advance.** This mechanism
requires its own legitimate constitutional authorization, an implementing
statute and an independently funded electoral and review route. It takes effect
prospectively under the applicable amendment and succession protections.
It is outside first supply and the first contest. The competent independent
forum resolves disputes about coverage, separability, original effect and
a proposed substitute under existing review safeguards. Publish the
authenticated withdrawal clause and any provisions that must rise or fall
with it as one indivisible question. Identify old rules, affected residents,
independent routes, receiving dates and costs of continuing them during review.
Preserve other expressly severable and independently lawful parts.
The procedure cannot vote away a constitutional protection, determine a
person's case, enact a substitute, appropriate money or delay ordinary supply,
regular elections or a protected remedy. An ordinary supply bill cannot
retire a protected route by implication. A temporary urgent response still
uses its separate source-law power, duration and review.

**NI19 — Open a reviewable collection period.** Day zero is publication of
the covered enactment and its authenticated exact question, once both are
lawfully available. No withdrawal takes effect on publication. Give 60
calendar days for valid requests from at least one percent of nationally
eligible electors on the opening denominator, rounded up. Apply the
NI05–NI08 principle of actual eligibility at the act, one qualifying
person and protected verification, using the electoral authority with
direct assisted and offline routes. A support request asks for a poll
and has no effect as a vote. The electoral authority publishes a reviewed
qualification determination by day 75 or records the breached duty.
A late or missing determination cannot be treated as proof of insufficient
support. Allow ten calendar days after usable notice for an ordinary
qualification challenge, with earlier urgent relief and protected late
challenge under applicable law. A timely scope or count challenge keeps
the affected clause held while it is decided. A lawful determination of
insufficient support becomes final when its ordinary challenge period
ends without a challenge or competent review resolves a timely challenge.
The clause can then take effect at the later of that date and its ordinary
commencement date. No new public mandate is inferred.

**NI20 — Decide the exact withdrawal and fund the waiting period.** For a
verified qualifying request, hold the independently administered poll by day
165 from publication. The question offers confirmation of the exact
withdrawal or retention of the prior protection. Confirmation requires more
valid votes for than against. Rejection or a tie leaves the prior protection
in force. Voting-time support, public materials including both positions,
meaning correction, cognizable campaign rules, secret ballots, source
custody, recount and independent judicial execution follow prospectively
specified law. Give affected people without a national vote a safe
opportunity to submit evidence before the vote. Underlying rights and
funded continuity remain independently enforceable during the process.
If neither a final qualification allowing ordinary commencement nor a
final lawful ballot result is established by day 180, the uncommenced
withdrawal clause lapses for this reference, subject to any separate
constitutionally authorized court remedy. Missing records, failed supply
or disrupted polling cannot bring it into force. Any replacement measure
requires its own exact decision and cannot bypass the procedure by changing
the name of the same functional withdrawal. Publish lawful effect and
notify each material receiver.

**NI21 — Keep the whole thing operable under political pressure.** Before
activating this power, fund publication, verification, campaign access,
separate qualified court and voting staff on one dated schedule, actual
execution, the old route and its next due call. If contest dates collide,
keep ordinary elections and urgent reviews on their existing calendars.
Use preauthorized compatible scheduling with distinct resources or let
the optional withdrawal lapse under the exact rule. A vote that confirms
a withdrawal supplies neither permission to breach higher law nor evidence
of an appropriation, a lawful substitute or actual receipt. Confirmed
law retains its other commencement conditions. If the old service becomes
unusable during the process, its responsible body pursues lawful funded
repair and individual relief on the original deadlines.

**NI22 — Retain strong alternatives and exact conditions for revision.**
Compare a strong initiative and independently reviewed elected reconsideration,
this binding confirmation, and a broader ordinary referendum. The agenda
and reconsideration route uses less ballot capacity but can leave lawful
closure with the same unwilling majority. A broader referendum gives more
direct control but exposes more budgets, people and implementation dates
to delay and campaign power. The first candidate confines binding effect
to the specified withdrawal. The numerical schedule is a design hypothesis
requiring actual costing and inclusive constitutional choice. Review real
opportunity to ask for a poll, whether concentrated sponsors dominate,
whose access was preserved, actual delays, displaced rights and independent
capacity. Reopen if simpler review protects the same access with equivalent
public control, strategically altered source law defeats coverage, or a
protected vote repeatedly displaces timely election or essential service.
