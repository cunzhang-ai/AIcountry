# C14. Choose a national form that people can actually govern

*Part of [C14 establishment](../C14-establishment-and-improvement.md); integrated design 129 research, not enacted law.*

The country-design reference is a constitutional parliamentary democracy with
an elected national Assembly and elected local governments. Its exact
territory, population, founding electorate, Assembly size **N**, local charter/
jurisdiction map and interlocal workload are unknown. The current EL reference
has one national PRtrack; it does not leave a territorial-seat count D to fill. Equal political
standing and replaceable rulers cannot be inferred from the word
"parliamentary" or from a formally proportional formula. A design that works
for a compact polity may leave remote residents unable to vote, meet,
obtain a court order or influence a national agenda. A dense metropolis may
instead waste authority on artificial regional tiers. The national form is a
founding choice with tested conditions, not a software default.

## What must be chosen before the first election

The legitimate founding process identifies inhabitants, affected peoples and
existing claims without letting an incumbent define away opponents. It
publishes original, challengeable evidence on settlement and travel patterns,
languages, disability access, communications and outage routes, historic and
collective rights, work and care time, fiscal bases, ecological catchments,
service networks and cross-boundary duties. Missing data and people who cannot
safely participate remain visible. It then compares at least three coherent
forms: (a) the current national Assembly/local-council model with interlocal
preparation; (b) a directly elected regional tier with specified law,
finance and review; and (c) issue-scoped, democratically controlled joint
services without a general regional legislature. Where peoples already hold
or seek constitutionally protected autonomy, that separate C04/C02 claim
cannot be collapsed into a mere efficiency option.

For each form, the public record assigns **who votes**, who can introduce an
alternative, which body decides rights, tax, service standards and material
shared allocations, who pays, which court reviews, who executes an order,
and how a defeated government hands over. It maps one continuous journey
through a service, a dispute and an election for remote, low-income,
linguistically excluded and high-dependency residents. The same tax base,
qualified staff, hearing time, transport, alternate communications and
reviewer capacity cannot be counted for every level simultaneously. A region
without its own reachable electorate, lawful duties and money is an
administrative office, not democratic self-government.

The founding authority must enact actual N, local charter sizes/jurisdictions,
roll/entry/ballot/meaning/count, recount, vacancies, repeats and transfer rules
before the first contest. [EL's current single national PRreference](../C03/13-equal-franchise-and-usable-elections.md)
replaces the older two-vote/D-ceiling candidate, which remains a historical
rival rather than unfinished current parameters. Test equal vote weight,
actual representation, viable opposition, comprehension, travel/offline access,
source custody, independent courts and useful-time received remedy with the
actual context. A differently elected regional/territorial form needs its own
complete legitimate prospective choice; an election office cannot add territorial
seats or redesign allocation after votes are known. Regional benefits and the
national reference's territorial-organizing loss remain material tradeoffs.
Future communication may improve understanding and participation, but a fast
mediator or copied agent cannot create mandates, settle borders or make a
remote poll equal political power by itself.

## Conditional reference and lawful continuation

The current Assembly-reserved final vote on unresolved material interlocal
allocations is retained as a **conditional reference**. Before its use, a
reconciled docket must show that the national Assembly can hear ordinary law,
budget, oversight, treaty, crisis and recurrent interlocal choices with
protected opposition and affected-public access, qualified staff and source
records. Test a plausible simultaneous service shortage, contested map and
noncooperating incumbent. If capacity or representation fails, the founding
public must choose a remedy and test its delivery before relying on it. The
same institutional form remains eligible if a funded repair can meet those
tests. Any change of authority requires a lawful allocation and transfer.
Existing institutions may continue within their authority while it decides.

Before proposing a new tier, compare qualified extra national capacity,
a voluntary compact and a directly elected limited area on the same
unresolved docket, original deadline and affected people. Distinguish
decisions wholly within the proposed jurisdiction from those that still
need national disposition. Price one-time electoral and institutional
start, paid overlap and continuing independent work. Show a potential
failure of any shared electoral, opposition, evidentiary or review route.
Select ordinary national repair where it supplies protected capacity
through the recurring window. Where it repeatedly fails and a separately
constituted region can resolve a defined recurring class, put that exact
regional authority and its funded transfer to the legitimate prior
public decision. An occasional delay or unavailable interim cash
does not itself confer jurisdiction.
Failure never gives cabinet, an interlocal forum, AI or a timeout the missing
legislative vote. Existing rights, local authority and due claims continue
under their applicable law until a valid successor is in place.

A chosen region needs a defined affected electorate, directly elected body,
limited competences, revenue/equalization, staff, records, court path and
cross-border duty map. Its foundation cannot be inferred from repeated
national delay. Joint service bodies retain member and affected-person
scrutiny, direct remedies and elected finality for material new burdens;
contractual cooperation cannot tax nonmembers or erase downstream claims.
Changes later require a public review of delivered results and lawful
prospective amendment, not an administrative redrawing that favors incumbents.

The [decision record](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-national-form/decision-record.md) compares
these institutional packages. No real founding public, map, budget or
participation exists here. Reopen the reference if observed geography,
representation, Assembly capacity or common resource obligations defeat
practical democratic control, or if affected people prefer a legitimate
alternative within enforceable rights.
