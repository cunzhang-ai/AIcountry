# OJ26–OJ30: hierarchy can be challenged and the chief cannot own their investigation

*Part of [C08/25](../25-ordinary-justice-and-public-prosecution.md); design120 candidate.*

**OJ26 — Allocation and reasoned case decisions.** Use prepublished lawful
specialty/territory/availability/conflict rotation, preserving original assignment
and exception reasons. The assigned prosecutor records lawful charge/noncharge/
diversion with safe usable reasons and actual source; P06 independent closure
review and direct court legality/fairness review remain. Neither complainants
nor courts can demand conviction, and public budget priorities cannot erase a
serious-abuse investigative duty or conceal unperformed work. Report real shortage,
not a fictitious favorable merits finding. Victim/support and defense routes remain.

**OJ27 — Written supervision, protected dissent, signed responsibility.** Lawful
supervisors may request evidence/review and give reasoned written instructions
within P13. Keep the original, author, actual source, contrary material and
prosecutor's protected written objection; a person need not sign a false account.
DPP can replace a case disposition only by an own signed reasoned decision in
the case record and lawful conflict-tested reassignment, with notice/defense/
review appropriate to the stage. No secret oral political order, deletion of
old reasons, post hoc phantom assent or punishment for lawful objection.
Material instruction/override is disclosed to the parties with necessary witness/
investigation privacy, not an unchallengeable secret. An unlawful instruction
is not obeyed; immediate independent legality/protection review is available.

**OJ28 — Review without a judicial prosecution service.** An uninvolved prosecutor
reviews a contested closure/override; OJ29 supplies the outside-command route
where DPP, the reviewing line or relevant selectors are implicated. Direct
ordinary courts can protect evidence/liberty, set aside unlawful procedure/
retaliation and require actual independent lawful reconsideration. They cannot
invent an offense, privately choose a prosecutor, order guilt or require a charge
unsupported by P13. Confidential reasons receive safe adversarial court scrutiny;
blank secrecy is no reason to deny effective review. Do not require serial
internal steps when useful-time independent protection would be lost.

**OJ29 — Three preselected external case prosecutors.** Assembly separately
appoints three consenting qualified external case prosecutors through OJ22/23
open notice and exact dual votes, each with ten relevant criminal-justice legal
years and actual competence, not employment/discipline under the DPP. A DPP or DPP-appointed senior command
role within the prior four years bars entry; prior case involvement/conflicts
still bar particular activation regardless of elapsed time. Six-year
nonrenewable seats expire one every two years; inaugural two/four/six-year terms
are drawn before nominations, replacements fill only the remaining term.
Prior independent legal practice may continue only with actual conflicts/time
separated; no simultaneous judge/ordinary prosecutor/operational party role or
multiple roster seats. Pay actual standby/training/source access and release
from other work. Expiry ends the officer's authority over new and ongoing acts;
a lawful successor accepts the records, original dates and duties through the
standing general assignment, never a personal case-completion holdover.
These are case officers, not a new lawmaking or general
investigation council, and cannot select their own successors/remuneration.

On serious official-abuse/obstruction grounds that implicate the prosecution
command or the ordinary outside-command reviewer, an unimplicated ordinary court
activates the next actually eligible consenting officer in a prepublished neutral
rotation/draw order, with reasons and challenge; no post-incident friendly nominee.
Existing automatic preservation/referral triggers and earlier P clocks continue;
the implicated chief cannot screen out the complaint. Exact mandate names incident/
scope/source-law power and reporting, never carte blanche. Preassigned qualified
outside-command investigators retain P07 warrants/limits. Case officer's statutory
case directions cannot be blocked by the DPP; accused actors cannot control
sole evidence/pay credentials. This does not create new police powers or actual
staff. If all three are conflicted/unavailable, no fourth is invented: preserve
reports/urgent rights, disclose the failure and use only actual pre-existing
lawful routes or prospective properly approved replacements, never a targeted
emergency appointment. No one obtains immunity from that failure.

**OJ30 — Review the external prosecutor too.** Each case officer's own status,
misconduct/removal and finite suspension uses OJ16–OJ18's independent process;
separate criminal inquiry goes to the next unimplicated preselected officer and
unimplicated investigators, never the accused. OJ16 ordinary courts, funded
defense and P safeguards constrain its decisions; private/Cabinet funding or
case-driven fees cannot buy priority. An allegation alone does not suspend/remove
any officer or reset the case. Assembly may scrutinize systemic reports/source
without directing a case. Same appointment patrons/records/Treasury can still
capture all routes; three names or AI copies cannot prove independent justice.

[PO09–PO12](../27/03-independent-investigation-and-own-abuse.md) now concretizes
outside-command investigators' public employer/director and own-director
allocation/source conditions. Ordinary unimplicated prosecutors remain competent;
OJ29 activates only on its actual grounds, not every police complaint. No
investigator/director inherits charging/judicial powers or an unstaffed roster.

[PX neutral enforcement](../../C03/22-open-contests-and-neutral-enforcement.md)
adds useful-time scrutiny of materially restrictive timing. OJ26–OJ30,
ordinary proof and case direction retain their own scope. A campaign
or complaint cannot activate an external prosecutor on its own.

[OJ49–OJ54](11-report-to-investigation-and-review.md) makes P06 review
reachable for material inaction before a formal closure. OJ28–OJ29
retain the outside-command and court routes. Evidence preservation and
reconsideration need actual lawful powers and paid execution.
