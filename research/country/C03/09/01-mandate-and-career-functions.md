# EA01–EA04: limited acts and qualified paid work

**EA01 — Election law, not commission preferences.** Assembly law fixes franchise, membership
proof, count, candidate/list entry, poll/calendar, finance/access formula, disclosure, recount,
ordinary vacancy and challenge/remedy grounds before competition. Within that law the commission
administers registration, nomination procedure, polling, count reconciliation and certification;
it publishes exact administrative directions and preserves accessible non-digital participation.
Its directions cannot amend ballot eligibility/count, disqualify lawful political views, create
new spending limits or fines, postpone a poll, extend any mandate or decide constitutional
validity. Applicable affirmative approval, publication and C14/RR anti-self-dealing rules bind
material changes.

**EA02 — Actual sources and career competence.** Local career election officers keep
authenticated original roll changes, nominations, ballots, tally/reconciliation records and
custody logs, with privacy-preserving access for voters, contestants, observers and courts.
Public count-level evidence and separately controlled lawful copies/communications survive
national/vendor refusal. A national career secretariat compiles only accepted originals,
publishes missing/disputed items and executes lawful directions; it cannot turn a provisional
total into an invented final mandate. Every alteration has source, actor, authority, prior
version and correction route. PD applies without publishing a person's secret vote, refuge
location or vulnerable small donor.

The commission appoints an operational secretary from C04's accessible open merit competition
and reasoned qualified public shortlist, by EA10's fixed vote. The secretary appoints, assigns
and disciplines operational staff through C04 protected career procedures, using published
job-related qualification and assignment/rotation/conflict rules. Local lawful recruitment
appoints local officers under the common national floor; a minister cannot replace them for one
contest. No ballot worker or vendor controls the only original and only complaint. Predesignated
qualified career deputies/officers retain their own enacted duties during vacancies; no
commission/secretary vacancy appoints a new chief or creates a missing power. Career
pay/discipline, judicial review and funded training remain C04; no private sponsor or AI rank
controls the employment pool.

By EA10 vote, the commission is the appointing and disciplinary authority for the secretary and
finance head, under C04 usable notice/reply, proved job-related grounds, protected career tenure
and direct ordinary-court review. Cabinet cannot remove or reassign either head. Direct head
conflicts use predeclared qualified career substitutes and unconflicted ordinary courts;
inability to form a lawful commission decision creates no ministerial removal power. Existing
pay, actual statutory duties, pending lawful proceedings and source preservation continue.

**EA03 — Common open commissioner qualification.** Every consenting applicant may apply directly
to any source; a public receiving register timestamps and supplies applications but owns no
exclusive shortlist. Require five years of relevant election administration, law, rights
protection, public logistics, accessible civic work, finance/audit or secure records work **or
demonstrated equivalent competence**, and demonstrated fair-process,
source-evaluation/confidentiality and accountable public-administration ability with lawful
language accommodation. Every source publishes reasoned application of these common criteria,
material interests and practical availability. No wealth, proprietary qualification, party
agreement, AI score or unpopular lawful view is a test. A commissioner cannot simultaneously be
a minister, legislator, judge, candidate, senior party executive/campaign manager, paid
political lobbyist, election operational officer or finance investigator; require four years
since minister/legislator/candidate/party-executive/campaign-manager/lobbyist roles. Ordinary
advocacy/party membership is not executive campaigning. Recusal/divestment addresses actual
financial/family/sponsor conflicts. The qualification/cooling periods are hypotheses, not proved
independence.

**EA04 — Seven seats, finite seat terms.** Seven paid commissioners hold seven-year nonrenewable
seat terms, with one scheduled seat expiry each year. Label two legal-source seats L1/L2, two
opposition-source seats O1/O2 and three public-source seats P1/P2/P3. A public draw before
inaugural nominations assigns these labels to initial one-through-seven-year term slots; the
recorded label/annual-expiry cycle remains fixed. At scheduled expiry the same seat/source
receives seven years; an interim permanent replacement receives only that seat's remaining term.
No holdover or executive acting commissioner. An initial short-term holder is also nonrenewable.
After exercising commission authority, a person cannot receive another primary/reserve seat
term; succession within their own designated remaining term is permitted. One person holds at
most one primary/reserve role and never two votes. Paid availability/other duties and actual
released work must be costed; seven names are not seven full-time free workers.

**Design174 ordinary-initiative assignment.** [NI05–NI08](../16/02-support-verification-and-privacy.md)
expressly proposes a new career-secretariat verification/qualification duty
under the existing operational secretary, with actual source/assistance/recusal
and ordinary outside review. It is not A9/TS scope by analogy or an EA10 board
certification vote. Actual enactment/source/qualified staff are required; old
electoral powers, formations, timelines and appointments remain unchanged.

[RC29–RC33](../../C10/13/07-accessible-evacuation-and-protected-transport.md)
protects a separately qualified civic transport path where access depends
on it. The electoral owner preserves original access and poll duties.
Transport dispatch supplies no decision about validity or results.
