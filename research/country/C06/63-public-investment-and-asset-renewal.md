# Choose public investments together and keep the assets working

Design324 · IV01–IV38 · Proposed country rules

A legal appropriation permits spending. People benefit when an asset supports
usable service through its operating life. Two individually worthwhile projects
can conflict over a qualified crew, a site, a shutdown window or future money.

**Select an elected investment portfolio with separate preparation, commitment
and service acceptance decisions.** Give repair and replacement a real place
beside expansion. Reserve project and budget decisions to the Assembly or the
competent elected local body. Use existing sector, procurement, audit and review
roles to test plans, contracts, actual work and rights.
