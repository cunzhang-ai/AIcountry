# IC01–IC04: ordinary security and additional needs

*Part of [C05/09](../09-income-capital-and-democratic-investment.md); retained design88 U rival, qualified by design136 [SG](../15-service-first-personal-security.md). Ordinary cash is no longer the presumed destination; continuing autonomy/old-right protections remain.*

**IC01 — A personal foundation, independent of employment or a sponsor.**
Every ordinarily resident person in I01's covered scope has an age-appropriate
ordinary personal foundation. Citizenship, voting eligibility, an employer,
marriage, willingness to train, approved AI use and political agreement are not
conditions. Do not import a citizenship waiting period into residence-based
protection. MB admission/residence rules and immediate protection outside the
ordinary-resident scope remain separate; an eligibility dispute cannot erase
urgent existing needs. Covered children have their own claim, with B-family
safe administration, growing agency and protection against adult diversion.

The retained U alternative covers the ordinary personal basket, not an identical
sum for all ages or every housing/disability situation. The elected basket
must specify nutrition, clothing, ordinary personal use, cash discretion and
price protection publicly. A vendor, benefit office or AI cannot redefine it.
No adequacy level is established by the fictional CU reference.

**IC02 — Preserve extra needs and each person's control.** Housing, utilities,
disability/access costs, necessary care, actual support duties and extraordinary
needs retain I01–I24 receiving, advance payment, safe-access and remedy routes.
The ordinary base is paid without assessing earnings/assets. The *residual*
assesses only lawfully relevant, actually usable resources under I05; a rich
partner's resources, unpaid dividend, expected estate or inaccessible assets
are not cash. Universal services are not privately cashable income. Child
support is one payer/recipient transfer, not another public funding source.

The reference uses `U + max(0, old I05 top-up − U)` for nonolder people at
the same prices/receipts, plus separately due access compensation. This is a
transparent continuity comparator, not a complete future tax-benefit Act.
The existing individual Y basic supplies the matching ordinary component for
older people once; earned/care, survivor and accrued price rights remain
additional. Do not confiscate those rights through a renamed matching exercise.

Credit an in-kind ordinary component only for actual, appropriate receipt in
the same person/period, subject to I02's cash-discretion floor. An available
place or nominal free meal is insufficient. A lawful cash alternative must
remain practical when provision is unsuitable or declined; no compelled meal,
care arrangement or pooled household spending. Receipt never becomes a school
attendance condition or a new intrusive child-surveillance requirement. Use
protected reconciliation and full cash when receipt is not established.
No duplicate recovery after
an operator fails to deliver the credited service.

**IC03 — Assess the whole tax and payment change.** Universality can remove
earnings reporting from the ordinary payment while leaving tax/residence and
additional-needs administration. No administrative savings or work response is
assumed. Publish personal incidence after taxes, contributions, withdrawal,
actual prices, support and accrued claims, including children, nonowners,
unpaid partners and people outside usual data. Keep a fixed-receipt comparison
separate from changed production/profit incidence and actual outcomes.

The selective-tax reference adds 30.960 million CU for a base of 20% of
the after-food ordinary cash component and
338.053 million for the full base at fixed receipts; 12,000 and 48,000 people
respectively gain cash in that authored perfect-receipt model. Many poorer
people instead obtain the same amount with less ordinary assessment. This
does not measure their independence gain, show zero real exclusion, or prove
these are net gains after financing. A small base does not remove assessment
of the uncovered ordinary basket; it is not a demonstrated cheap equivalent.
The comparator leaves the base untaxed and uses a fixed reference price index;
it selects neither an exemption nor actual inflation, work or investment behavior.
Paying the full basket in cash *with received food additional* is a distinct
live rival: its fixed-receipt extra cost is 345.277 million, 7.224 million more.
The selected component-matching approach preserves the cash alternative above;
it does not treat those two personal-provision bargains as equivalent.

**IC04 — Payment must precede replacement.** Assembly law sets exact coverage,
claim matching, price rules, taxes and commencement dates with paid overlap.
For each person, keep the old due claim until the lawful replacement is
actually received and the matching amount is reconciled. Safe accounts,
assisted/offline access, provisional disputes and direct court execution remain.
No automatic household clawback, debt or service reduction finances overlap.
Missing funds defeat a *new launch claim*; they do not authorize an office to
leave existing rights unpaid or grant a Treasury veto over enacted deadlines.
