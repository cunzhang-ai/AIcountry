# Design391 decision. Keep essential purchases possible through payment failure

## Baseline and choice

[Design390](predecessor-design-390/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md) recognizes domestic
legal tender, bank loss and payment finality, and a safeguarded standby for
public payments. These mechanisms leave ordinary cash acceptance and
the last step from a failed retail payment to an actual prescription or
meal under-specified.

Select [SF11–SF17](../country/C06/25/06-cash-access-and-essential-payment-continuity.md)
under the [working research method](../FRAMEWORK.md). Protect an ordinary
cash circuit for covered essentials, qualify independent alternate payment
paths, and give existing sector owners a funded path to actual provision
when timely purchase fails. Merchant settlement, finality, patient choice,
privacy and worker safety remain distinct constraints.

## Alternatives and objections

Retain general continuity duties with voluntary merchant acceptance.
This leaves more room for local arrangements and fewer recurring cash costs.
Households and merchants can each reasonably decline a rarely used
option, leaving no maintained local circuit when one is needed.
A regulated offline-card-first system can cover many ordinary outages
without holding as much cash, but can exclude people without an eligible
instrument or available balance and depends on safe power and settlement.
A universal public retail or public-account monopoly could standardize
access, with higher transition costs and a common point of political or
operational failure. Retain plural provision and require public fallback
where necessary. Compare staffed shared, mobile and direct alternatives
on coverage, travel time, safety and real running cost.

A blanket cash duty could disproportionately burden small shops or put
staff at risk. Allow a narrow independent safety exception, funded
replacement and prospective proportionate limits. The conduct supervisor
must explain refusal or exception decisions and make review reachable
without the blocked payment channel. The monetary authority publishes
operating requirements, with independent audit and court review. The
Assembly can change the mandate and sources prospectively, and local
service owners must keep giving reasons and arranging receiving.
An essential purchase commitment needs explicit eligibility, merchant
consent and actual funds. It cannot become open-ended public credit.

## Evidence, transition and next action

[Primary records](sources.md) show bounded ordinary cash acceptance and
offline settlement designs in other jurisdictions. The [constructed
comparison](../settlement/cash-access-and-essential-payment-continuity/01-one-outage-and-three-needs.md)
checks a second-day shortfall across separately owned sources. No
independent affected-party review, adopted funding, operating coverage
or observed outcomes are established for AIcountry.

Apply prospectively under new elected authority, with independent
qualification of the covered routes, dated cash-service contracts,
an actual protected payer and funded sector fallback. Preserve lawful
preexisting settlement events, client property, accepted obligations and
open claims. Maintain the original service deadline through a provider
change, and open a separately funded correction if receipt fails.

Next trace an actual district's staffed shops, pharmacies and cash
handlers through one data interruption and a second-day power loss.
Measure who reaches an accepting provider and when merchants receive
funds, including users without accounts, workers and small merchants.
Reopen on repeated exclusion, safety costs, common dependencies, late
settlement or a better qualified service architecture.
