15 October 2026 · Decision 77 · Research selection, not adopted procedure

civilian war authorization and humane-treatment duties. It leaves actual

The country has no verified founding public, territory, conflict, treaty-party

Select a bounded national law candidate serving C08 liberty, C10 civilian

subject to actual constitutional and international obligations. This record

## Diagnosis, real alternatives and selection

The gap is the difference between condemning abuse and limiting the institution
that controls bodies, evidence, money and release. General criminal law alone
also does not decide every genuine POW/neutrality situation. [Alternatives](../settlement/conflict-custody/02-choices-and-capacity.md)
compare unchanged command discretion, criminal-only custody, the narrow selected
model and exceptional civilian-security internment as a distinct live rival.

Select [CD01–CD16](../country/C08/21-conflict-detention-and-release.md): actual
POW/identified neutrality grounds, no general involuntary civilian-security
or NIAC internment, no occupation/former-force power or medical-personnel
retention imported by implication. Protection in doubt is not endless hold
authority; affiliation, an AI score or opposition is not guilt or targetability.
Actual obligations which cannot lawfully be declined need explicit reconciliation.

A civilian judge hears first restraint within 24 continuous hours; a qualifying
provisional status case reaches a full hearing within seven calendar days.
The state proves status/necessity clearly and convincingly, with actual defense
and answerable material sources. Adult orders last at most 30 days and child
orders seven on fresh grounds; earlier direct challenge remains. These national
choices are uncalibrated, not universal treaty rules or demonstrated safety.

Humane care, agency, counsel and protected records survive captivity. No forced
productive labor, interrogation without advice, indefinite isolation or status
extension as discipline. A proved intentional safety/order breach can restrict
one optional organized leisure activity for seven days, with full defense,
state proof beyond reasonable doubt, impartial hearing and court challenge;
no criminal conviction follows. The narrower labor/discipline choice costs
paid staff and may reduce command flexibility; these effects remain unmeasured.

Own military personnel and protected captives use the same independent ordinary
civilian criminal courts/procedure. Actual applicable combatant privilege,
POW protection, personal criminal proof and jurisdiction stay distinct. Real
criminal-use remand has the shortest P aggregate, 90 actual days or three
calendar months; independent status-only custody is separately reviewed.
No case-purpose concealment, relabelled reset or omitted sentence credit.

End a failing ground promptly and POW custody without delay at cessation of
relevant active hostilities, without awaiting a peace treaty or reciprocity.
Actual safe receiving, non-refoulement, usable interim identity, nonclosed
accommodation and effective transfer challenge join the order to freedom.
A foreign assurance, missing transport or housing vacancy creates no new hold.
Missing capacity never licenses killing, unsafe abandonment or false completion.

## Support, objection and the protector's power

[Three documentary families](sources.json) were inspected: ICRC treaty text,
HRC General Comment 35 and Hague V primary text reproduced by Yale. They
inform distinctions; they do not prove our whole model compatible or superior.
[Ten authored cases](../settlement/conflict-custody/01-cases.md) expose ambiguity,
not actual outcomes or independent evidence. No complete publisher-byte archive,
current-party/custom survey, protocol/case-law census or qualified final review
is claimed. Earlier macro passes retain their complete original limited scopes.

The broader internment rival could address a serious unchargeable armed threat;
we decline it because error, indefinite emergency and political capture may
reach a much larger population. That is a value/security tradeoff, not a proven
causal ranking. Criminal-only remains a rival if actual obligations/security
and safe alternatives permit it. Real affected accounts and discriminating
original evidence could displace this selection rather than expand it silently.

Assembly law/funds, cabinet missions, civilian judges, care, original-record
custody and independent visits retain distinct mandates. Existing C03/C04
appointment, removal, conflict-deputy, opposition and ordinary-budget routes
apply; no self-appointing wartime guardian is added. Cumulative command/vendor/
source/funding control still needs actual scrutiny. Protected oversight access
is not public exposure of families, clinical testimony or vulnerable locations.
Courts name actual executors and verify receipt outside implicated command;
a record, report or inspector invitation does not equal executed relief.

or assumed AI gain funds this increment. All actual quantity, cost, adoption,
qualified capacity, received remedy and outcomes remain unknown. A failed
fallback stays an unmet duty/violation requiring correction, not self-extension.
