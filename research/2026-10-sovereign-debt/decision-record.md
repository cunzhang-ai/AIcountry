# Decision record: public debt distress without a hidden sovereign

5 October 2026 · Integrated design 44 research selection; not enacted law

## Baseline, people affected and the bounded question

The [design-43 predecessor](predecessor-design-43/README.md) is preserved.
C06 records stocks, cash, due dates, foreign-currency constraints and the
Assembly's fiscal authority. It does not yet specify an authorized route
when debt service, essential claims and external payments cannot all be
met. That omission can let Treasury choose favored creditors, a lender
acquire policy power through a side agreement, a central bank finance the
state by default, or a cabinet announce a unilateral debt “solution” that
cannot bind anyone abroad. It affects patients, workers, pensioners,
depositors, taxpayers, local governments, creditors and people outside the
country. None have participated in AIcountry's decision.

The diagnosis might be a short cash mismatch, structurally unsustainable
debt, a foreign-currency constraint, bank/pension losses, actual import
shortage, concealed guarantees or an avoidable distributional choice. These
require different remedies. The values in conflict are faithful treatment
of lawful claims, essential life and rights, future capacity, fair loss
sharing, access to credit and democratic control. Neither a creditor's
contract nor a service aggregate alone chooses the whole burden. Original
contracts, public accounts, import records and affected people's accounts
are necessary; a synthetic debt ratio cannot do it.

## Coherent choices

| Package | Potential benefit | Material loss or objection |
|---|---|---|
| Retain C06's general shortfall route | Simple; ordinary budget authority remains | No specified negotiation mandate, creditor treatment or domestic-bank and pension transmission during debt failure |
| Temporary lawful liquidity, tax collection and delayed discretionary starts | May solve a timing gap without changing contracts | Fails if debt is structurally unpayable or external currency/physical supply is absent; delayed projects can destroy future capacity |
| **Selected: Assembly-controlled diagnosis and bounded negotiated workout only when needed** | Exposes claims and service effects, preserves legal process and gives the public a choice of burdens | Negotiation can be slow, creditors may refuse, domestic financial losses can offset relief, and political actors may hide terms or target opponents |
| Automatic debt moratorium or domestic monetary finance | May release immediate cash or avoid a negotiation veto | A national announcement cannot bind foreign claims; arrears, inflation, exchange depreciation, capital loss and import disruption may move harms onto residents |
| Across-the-board rapid austerity to pay all creditors | May preserve contractual payment and lender confidence | Can breach protected services and shift losses to people least able to bear them; it can also fail to restore solvency |

The selected [C06 rule](../country/C06/06-sovereign-debt-and-distress.md)
does **not** assert that restructuring is always preferable. It requires
a public diagnosis, real cash and legal scope, alternative whole packages,
an elected negotiating mandate if justified, recorded creditor and service
incidence, and a fresh exact Assembly decision before binding terms. A
liquidity case may be resolved without restructuring; a genuine physical
shortage cannot be refinanced away. The [C13 combined
exercise](../country/C13/09-debt-medicine-and-pension-crisis.md) tests the
authority and delivery chain, not a country's actual solvency.

[IMF's sovereign-debt overview](https://www.imf.org/en/topics/sovereign-debt)
states that the country chooses whether to negotiate restructuring. Its
[domestic-debt policy paper](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/policy-papers/issues/2021/11/30/issues-in-restructuring-of-domestic-sovereign-debt-510371)
describes banking and pension effects. The [World Bank's fiscal-risk
materials](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/debt-toolkit/fiscal-risk)
identify public-enterprise and local contingent exposures. [UNCTAD's
responsible lending principles](https://unctad.org/publication/principles-promoting-responsible-sovereign-lending-and-borrowing)
address borrower and lender responsibility while expressly not creating
new international-law obligations. These are primary comparators and
diagnostic support, not a debt contract, applicable jurisdiction or proof
that AIcountry's package would be accepted by creditors.

## Legal and democratic chain

The Assembly authorizes borrowing and any public guarantee beforehand.
Treasury records and executes claims; original lenders, banks, pension
funds, localities and suppliers keep source evidence. Independent audit and
plural analysis examine alternative forecasts and concealed exposures.
Opposition has timely protected access to the material evidence; affected
people can contest distribution and who frames the options. The Assembly
alone decides whether to mandate negotiations, which claims and public
resources may be discussed, and whether to accept exact terms. Cabinet
negotiators answer within that mandate. The central bank maintains only
its enacted monetary and payment functions; an AI tool can model scenarios
but cannot declare insolvency, bind a creditor or allocate claims.

Applicable domestic law, contract terms, foreign law, treaty duties and
court orders remain distinct. A sovereign law cannot erase every
foreign-law claim. A missed payment is recorded as arrears with remedies,
not relabeled a saving. Any standstill, exchange, redenomination or loss
allocation needs valid authority and a review route for affected holders.
The process must protect confidential personal data and narrow bargaining
positions while disclosing material terms before the vote; secret side
collateral or policy control is not a legitimate price of consent.
Domestic banks, deposit insurance, payments, central-bank collateral and
public pensions enter the same loss account. A haircut that requires a
public recapitalization is not its headline gross relief. Essential service
rights retain their applicable legal force; actual cash and physical goods
may still fail, and unmet claims remain visible.

Important objections are unresolved. Publishing a mandate can weaken
bargaining, while secrecy can hide capture. Relief for small domestic
holders can be fair but shift more losses to outsiders; foreign creditors
may retaliate through litigation or supply withdrawal. Protecting basic
service spending can protect residents but cannot be announced as an
unreviewable super-priority over every valid claim. Repeated emergency
borrowing can postpone a necessary correction; premature restructuring can
destroy credit. A coalition of lenders, banks, media and cabinet may
capture the whole process despite formal roles. These conflicts cannot be
settled by averaging a debt ratio and a health score.

Migration requires original borrowing statutes, instrument terms, guarantees,
collateral, public-enterprise and local liabilities, bank/pension claims,
trade contracts, treaties, court jurisdiction, reserves, tax capacity,
service duties and budget law. Accrued claims continue until lawfully
changed; no website research selection amends them. Funded Treasury,
legal, audit, court, negotiation, payment and public-communication capacity
must be shown before a real government could use this route. Existing
reviews of the older C06 text cannot authorize a materially different
workout proposal.

**Evidence:** official comparators and authored logic, without any
AIcountry debt instrument or creditor/resident participation. **Research
selection:** an Assembly-controlled diagnosis and conditional negotiated
workout with separate cash, external, banking and service accounts.
**Legal authorization:** none. **Operational readiness:** no jurisdiction,
cash, contracts, foreign counterparties, counsel, staff or reserves.
**Observed outcomes:** none; no superiority over current countries follows.

The next bounded test would take one real possible jurisdiction's original
debt contracts, cash calendar, import needs, bank/pension holdings and
service obligations, then compare timing support, negotiated terms and
adjustment under actual law. Its affected public and creditors should
challenge the framed options and simulated delivery. Reopen this selection
if domestic financial losses erase relief, if creditor/nonresident claims
cannot be lawfully addressed, if essential services remain undeliverable,
or if a rival yields better practical public control and lower harm.
