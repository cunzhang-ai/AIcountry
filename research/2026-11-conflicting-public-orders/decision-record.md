case. An authority can also overstate an incompatibility to avoid

## Alternatives and disposition

- **Keep originating courts and use ordinary independent capacity.**
  Prefer this where already lawful and feasible. It preserves
  the original proceeding and avoids consuming appellate time. In the
  authored four-unit case both orders can be met on original terms.
  Ordinary court clarification also suffices where it is available
  and can reach both cases by the useful time.
- **Give an existing appellate formation a narrow coordination duty.**
  Select this residual design for an expressly enacted case where
  ordinary compatibility fails. It can adjust only the means of
  joint execution, with real receiving and safeguards. At two units
  the scenario supports the accessible hearing route if the participants,
  hearing court and independent capacity actually qualify.
- **Consolidate all merits or give the agency a dispatch priority.**
  A wider common proceeding may help repeat failures but can consume the
  only useful interval and merge distinct claims. The executive needs
  any existing scarcity power in its exact lawful scope. Reject a new
  discretionary agency stay or general priority.

An appellate route can itself become a bottleneck or favor a well
represented claimant. The formation needs prior-law independent
assignment, paid aid, separate evidence access and timely alternate
capacity. No one may use a conflict referral to pause both cases.
The scope excludes reserved jurisdictions. A genuine need for new
shared capacity requires elected source action. Preserve a clean
ordinary remedy and record lawful limits rather than counting
unavailable service as delivered.
