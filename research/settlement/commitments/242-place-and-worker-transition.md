# Design329. Place and worker transition

[AT23–AT27](../../country/C05/18/06-place-and-worker-transition.md)
requires matched household, town and host receiving during concentrated
job loss. Pay ordinary claims and the common floor and keep optional
mobility voluntary. Compare separate employer support and
diversification without presuming new net tax or a cleanup release.

The [decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-place-and-worker-transition/decision-record.md)
retains alternatives and a two-period field check. Actual authority,
funding, resident choices and service outcomes remain open.
