# Public investment and realized technology returns

*Design298 · AT13–AT17 · Proposed application of AT, IC and public purchasing.*

**AT13 — Establish what discretionary public support would buy.**
For a material investment in a technology transition, the competent
public buyer identifies the useful additional output, open access,
safer work, independently maintainable service or other public
benefit sought. Compare ordinary private provision, repair, purchase,
a grant with defined results and a paid ownership or sharing option.
Include what a firm would deliver without the support and what
households, workers, competing providers and public services would
give up. Elected bodies set the authority and gross exposure.
A claimed productivity gain or an AI label earns no support by itself.
Existing worker and resident rights keep their own sources.

**AT14 — Choose enforceable public terms before making the award.**
State the actual public deliverable, milestones, source, maximum
loss, access and exit, treatment of intellectual property and
who verifies actual performance. A commercial return option needs
explicit audit access, period, threshold, calculation base, cap,
settlement dates, qualified independent review and consequences of a
change of control. Trace related-party payments where material to
the agreed base. Compare the cost and possible exclusion of small
providers with a simpler purchase or open grant. A financial
return cannot replace accessible public service or worker
protection. An open licence cannot by itself replace the
paid maintainer. Keep actual scopes distinct.

**AT15 — Keep the different gains and claims in their own accounts.**
Identify actual lower prices, paid work and benefits, operating
costs, legal tax liabilities, cash receipts, retained investment and
distributable owner returns. Where elected terms authorize a public
share of a realized gain, reconcile its agreed base with actual
worker and tax obligations and any other public return. Count each
receipt once. Distinguish forecast surplus, audited obligation,
received cash and lawfully available funds. An unpaid return, share
valuation, estimated recovery or promised tax cannot fund the first
advance or a recurring benefit. Allocate received public returns
through ordinary budget authority. Protect agreed worker rights,
independent earned funds and lawful creditor priority.

**AT16 — Release only the exact priced and accepted scope.**
Compare current usable public cash, later gross exposure, actual
alternative supply and the qualified nonincumbent route before
binding. Keep separately authorized ordinary repair available
where a conditional offer fails. Material changes to control,
expected benefit, terms, safety or an accepting operator require
fresh competent review and any new lawful decision under SC and
IC. Preserve valid obligations through that review. Keep the
qualified service and data route usable when the funded firm or
its model stops. Honest failure under a lawfully accepted risk
does not prove fraud or create automatic repayment. Any recovery
or protection uses its actual terms and law.

**AT17 — Protect public choice, independent audit and handback.**
Publish safe scope, terms, gross costs, expected and realized
benefits, losses and usable returns. Give elected opposition,
affected workers and independent audit access to material
original evidence within legal privacy and trade protections.
Keep the buyer, technical acceptance, payer and reviewer distinct.
AI may check arithmetic or detect possible overlap. It cannot
choose the award, certify the gain or appropriate the return.
Adopt prospectively with funded workers, contracts, data and
service successors. Reopen if returns displace actual access,
audit is controlled by the recipient, or a better ordinary or
open route protects the same outcomes.
