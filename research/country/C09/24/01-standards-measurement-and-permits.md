# Standards, measurement and permitting

*Part of [AQ clean air](../24-clean-air-and-cumulative-exposure.md).*

**AQ01 — Legislate an enforceable ambient floor and distinct alert
duties.** The Assembly adopts a pollutant-specific schedule of
concentration units, averaging periods, exceedance rules, covered
places, compliance dates, response triggers and independent review.
Use qualified health and ecological evidence, publicly compare
health guidance and feasible phased protection, and state any
unresolved exposure. Distinguish a binding ambient limit, a public
health guideline, a short-term warning threshold and an individual
source's emission limit. A national mean cannot cancel a local
breach. An ambient breach creates a public duty to protect and
correct under this law. It does not alone establish an operator's
fault, an individual's causal injury or a new coercive power.
Changing the legal floor or deferring a binding date needs the
competent elected law and its own public reasons and review.

**AQ02 — Observe where people and ecosystems are exposed.** The
existing environmental authority and independent qualified custodians
maintain a publicly justified network covering ordinary background,
known hot spots and plausible missed exposure, including schools,
housing, outdoor work, traffic and relevant ecosystems. Publish
methods, representative periods, uncertainty, missing coverage,
site changes and the separate status of measurements and model
estimates. Keep original evidence outside any implicated operator's
control. Resident reports and calibrated indicative sensors can
trigger verification without transferring the regulatory proof burden
to residents. Prove comparability before changing a site or applying
a method retrospectively. No national average, absence of a monitor
or AI estimate can certify a place as safe. Protect sensitive
household and worker data.

**AQ03 — Check combined exposure before granting additional burden.**
Under TD11 and LU, the competent environmental decision-maker
considers existing and committed emissions, recurring background,
seasonal and foreseeable combined conditions, related small projects
and effects in receiving places. Assess each covered pollutant and
time window. A new permission or material increase cannot authorize
a new exceedance or materially worsen one already present. Show
the qualified basis and any residual uncertainty. If evidence is
insufficient on a material risk, withhold the affected new permission
or use a lawful, independently reviewable limit with verification
before harmful reliance. Count a reduction only where it is legally
secured, in place by the needed time, and demonstrably protects the
same affected places and windows. Carbon offsets, a charge or an
unfunded later reduction cannot waive the floor. Existing lawful
operations and genuine urgent protection keep their separate rules.
