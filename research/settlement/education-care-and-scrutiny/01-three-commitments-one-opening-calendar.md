# Decide care and school expansion on one opening calendar

*Design491 · Constructed first-budget comparison with protected independent access.*

The new care route can add two home visits per period. The new

Both are optional additions. A pupil's existing required support,
a resident's existing care, worker pay and all urgent routes remain
due on their own dates and funding. This test assumes those duties

## Shared access without shared professional judgment

Both optional starts and a due independent accessibility hearing
require a qualified interpreter from 09:00 to 10:00 on the same
morning, including protected setup and handoff. Their respective
receiving cutoffs do not allow the blocks to be sequenced in the
base case. If safe sequencing can reach all original cutoffs, cost
that as a separate rival. Care and school staff are distinct and cannot substitute
for one another. Interpretation enables access. It does not decide
clinical suitability, educational placement or the hearing. The
hearing also has its own reviewer, record and executor.
Assume distinct paid pre-vote opposition evidence assistance under DB
and PE. The morning hearing is post-vote and cannot replace that earlier
work. Test a late amendment proposing school intake preparation
on the separately booked pre-vote assistance slot. Preparation
requires its own prior authority. If no funded timely alternative
exists, the amended timetable fails even if the later hearing and
cash still qualify. An absent estimate does not veto a lawful vote. Two purchased
interpretation blocks are genuinely usable. One is reserved for that
hearing. It cannot be lent to either expansion. A second agency
offering the same worker creates no third block.

The first window has 8 usable incremental units for the displayed
choices. Pre-vote assistance is paid separately in the common baseline. The hearing's
protected block and support cost 2. Each expansion costs 4 to
start, including its interpretation. Preparation for a deferred
option costs 1 and uses neither of the morning blocks. Accelerating
both needs an independent third block, with a premium of 1 above
the ordinary interpretation already priced in the starts.
All costs and receipt assumptions are constructed. For a recurring
decision verify operations separately from startup.

| Exact first-window motion | Cash and block demand | Result if recipients accept and the original work remains covered |
|---|---|---|
| Protect standing functions and prepare both | 4 units and one block | Hold both starts. Keep the hearing and prepare both routes. Two potential visits and two potential sessions remain outside the added offer. |
| Start care and prepare school | 7 units and two blocks | Preserve the hearing. Two extra visits can reach residents. The proposed new school sessions wait. |
| Start school and prepare care | 7 units and two blocks | Preserve the hearing. Two extra school sessions can be received. The proposed new visits wait. |
| Start both | 11 units and three independent blocks | The motion cannot start on 8 and two. Losing the hearing would not qualify the package. |

In this fixture two new care visits have the earlier useful time
and no comparable interim offer. The additional school sessions
can begin in the next window within their proposed timetable.
Select the care-first stage on those facts, while preserving a
timely school-first amendment. If the school recipients face
earlier irreversible loss or an independent ordinary care route
becomes available, reopen the choice. A forecast cannot rank
school and care on a single value scale. At 11 usable units and
only two blocks the simultaneous start still fails. With 11 and
a distinct third block it can qualify if the sector and receiving
conditions also pass. Existing duties come
before either additional service.

## Make the next vote and receipt a real option

The next window has 8 usable units after other existing commitments,
before subtracting the displayed continuation and independent access.
Under either staged branch, the new operation already started
costs 2 to continue, school or care startup costs 4, and protected
independent access costs 2. Continuing visits or sessions use a
different time and do not add to the two overlapping access blocks.
The deferred start can qualify only when the new receiver, origin
cover and hearing access fit. If the second-window source settles
at 7, preserve the 4 for binding continuation and independent access.
The still-unbound 4-unit start waits unless the Assembly separately
authorizes and qualifies an exact smaller useful service. A
council's receiving decision retains its own authority. An eventual
late receipt does not repair that cutoff.

If the interpreter for an optional start becomes unavailable,
test a separately authorized, usable alternative with the actual
person. Keep the independent hearing's funded own route. If that
route itself fails, use the applicable substitute and report any
missed hearing. Do not declare the protected function disposable.
A funded slot for a person who cannot safely receive the service
counts as no receipt. Put lost first-window benefits, continued
cost and original remedies in the comparison.

Give all members useful access to the same assumptions, private
evidence under lawful safeguards, independently funded costing and
a real opportunity to choose either sequence. A late change to a
material source, protected access or receiver needs fresh scrutiny.
Preserve each binding duty after a failed prospective condition.
AI can flag common scheduling and custody dependencies. It does
not allocate a protected hearing or authorize a new service.
