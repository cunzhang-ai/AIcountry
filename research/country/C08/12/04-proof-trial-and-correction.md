# Meaningful defense, reliable proof and actual correction

*P11–P15; part of [C08 criminal core](../12-criminal-core-and-procedure.md).*

**P11 — Independent defense and usable disclosure.** Defense receives funded
counsel, interpretation, accommodation, source access and necessary technical
expertise under a reasoned court route outside investigative control. Disclose
inculpatory and exculpatory evidence, provenance, collection/testing errors,
contrary findings, relevant model limitations and acquisition history early
enough to prepare an effective challenge; supplement when new material arrives.
A vendor's secrecy or a witness-protection label does not exempt evidence.
The court chooses safe disclosure, excludes unusably secret material or grants
sufficient fair-trial relief. Material that cannot be meaningfully challenged
cannot support guilt **even alongside other evidence**. Defense delays do not
create additional detention power. Names of distinct offices do not prove
independent personnel, source custody or control of money.

**P12 — Coercion and derivative evidence.** Torture-derived statements are
excluded in any proceeding except against an alleged torturer to establish
that the statement was made, not its factual truth. Other involuntary statements
extracted by coercion are excluded against any accused under this broader
candidate. On a credible issue, a separate admissibility hearing protects
medical/custody evidence and defense. Prosecution proves lawful voluntary
acquisition beyond reasonable doubt before admission against an accused;
material unresolved doubt requires exclusion. A misconduct conviction is not
a prerequisite for suppression. Independent medical help and an outside-
command complaint route remain available.

Derivative evidence also needs a disclosed history and a genuinely independent
lawful source proved beyond reasonable doubt; otherwise exclude it under the
selected broader candidate. A new investigator, edited output or agreeing model
does not cleanse the original source. Legitimate preservation of tainted material
for an abuse inquiry is distinct from using it to prove the accused's guilt.
The broader derivative rule is our value choice, beyond CAT Article 15, and
needs comparative/practitioner scrutiny; deterrent effectiveness is unproven.

**P13 — Charge, diversion and trial.** Independent prosecutors decide sufficiency
and public-interest charge/noncharge/diversion under published law and reasons,
with conflict-based alternate assignment. Cabinet and Assembly cannot direct a
named charge or acquittal. A proposed diversion agreement needs independent
advice, informed voluntary choice, no increased punishment for refusal and
bounded obligations. It cannot remove serious-abuse inquiry, compel victim
reconciliation or punish someone who otherwise faces no warranted sanction.
Refusal, withdrawal and admissions made only for an uncompleted restorative
route cannot prove the underlying charge. Independently lawful evidence or a
separately proved new threat/harm retains its ordinary route. A blanket waiver
cannot turn optional restoration into an evidence trap. A guilty admission
needs independent advice, actual understanding, voluntary choice and a judicial
check of each element on sufficient lawful facts. Prosecutors cannot threaten
charge multiplication or extra punishment solely for exercising trial rights;
no admission removes defense, review or the separate proof/authority questions.

An independent legally accountable human court conducts a fair public hearing,
with narrow reasoned privacy limits. Accused persons can remain silent, contest
material proof and witnesses and present their own evidence. Accommodations
support actual understanding and participation. If effective defense is impossible
despite support, pause with reasons and review; diagnosis alone proves neither
unfitness nor guilt. Remand clocks continue, and incapacity gives no locked-
treatment power. A judgment states every element, relevant defense and reasons
on each contested source; case allocation/recusal follow published C08 rotation.

**P14 — AI and proof standards remain distinct.** Synthetic reconstructions
are labelled; they cannot become original recordings. Preserve observations,
transformation and uncertainty so an independent expert can reproduce/challenge
the relevant analysis. Shared training/source material across models is not
independent corroboration. Statistical association alone cannot prove a person's
act, fault or guilt. AI may assist search, translation and scheduling but cannot
choose a secret judge, issue a conviction or determine sentence. Criminal proof
is beyond reasonable doubt. A support decision follows need; an interim protection
order uses its distinct statutory grounds; an ordinary civil finding follows its
own burden. None can be silently imported as criminal proof.

**P15 — Appeal, innocence and repair.** A conviction or sentence may be appealed
to a different qualified formation within 30 working days of usable judgment
notice. Missing records/counsel, defective notice or serious unfairness permits
extension; urgent liberty review has no wait for that window. A lawful appeal
is not automatically a stay; reasons and urgent relief address irreparable harm.
Material new evidence of innocence or unreliable proof permits reopening despite
finality. Acquittal is final against repeated same-episode prosecution; fraud in
the original adjudication requires a distinct narrowly enacted reopening rule,
not executive dissatisfaction. Record correction, release and lawful compensation
have named recipients, payers and executors. The affected person need not first
convict an investigator to challenge wrongful detention or secure civil repair.
Fair process also protects accused officials. No court outcome guarantees a
received payment, safe home or restored public reputation; trace actual delivery.

[OJ01–OJ40](../25-ordinary-justice-and-public-prosecution.md) now specifies ordinary
personnel/formations and prosecution selection/case control. The default single
trial/three-person appeal and special status formations apply only through the
coordinated prospective Act, preserving stronger/different actual source rules.
P13 no named political charge, sufficiency, fair defense/guilt and P15 appeal/
finality remain. A written DPP override is reviewable, not an instruction to a
judge or a substitute for proof; implicated command uses preselected OJ29 officers.
