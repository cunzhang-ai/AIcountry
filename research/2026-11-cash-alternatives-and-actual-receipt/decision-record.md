# Design450. Compare timely income and the power to control it

**Baseline and choice.** Design449 retains the service-first system
and the universal cash rival. SG05–SG12 and SD already compare
annual incidence, funding, and old claims. TU and AP cover
independent receipt and provisional protection. This revision
compares simultaneous proof and payment failures on the same
useful date. AI-assisted drafting and parallel review contributed.

**Alternatives.** Keep the ordinary route as a priced counterfactual,
repair it with lawful provisional assistance and actual receiving,
and specify a universal base with residual needs protection on the
same clock. Select the financed repair in the constructed constrained
window. Keep the universal offer available to elected choice when its
source and protection qualify. Equal immediate amounts for some
people leave meaningful differences in recurring proof, predictability
and independence. Full national funding, distribution and behavior
remain unresolved. Neither a late tax receipt nor an unspent earmark
creates early authority.

**Objections and authority.** A provisional route can fail the people
who have the least evidence. A universal base can still be inaccessible
through a broken common payment route, and funding it can displace
other useful work. Keep actual required remedies, supplier and
payment obligations, lawful accrued claims and original deadlines.
Give opposition and affected residents access to safe independent
comparison. Keep each qualified repair, burden and actual launch
within the existing elected and judicial powers. No project or legal
procedure is adopted by this record.

**Next evidence.** Compare actual receipt for one defined cohort of
verified, provisionally eligible, unrecorded and payment-blocked
people. Use protected sampling and original payment evidence, with
a shared reference period and actual payer and review cost. Keep
foreign and resident evidence distinct. Reopen when missed receipt,
proof burden, a stronger universal design or a qualified source
changes the choice. Actual participation, funding, legal authority
and outcomes remain unverified.
