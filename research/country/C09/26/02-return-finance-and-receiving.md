# LCY05–LCY08. Return, finance and actual receiving

[Product repair and material responsibility](../26-product-repair-and-material-responsibility.md)

**LCY05 — Fund accessible safe return before selling.** The responsible
operator finances the net cost of qualified take-back, sorting,
reuse preparation, recovery and safe residual treatment for its
covered cohorts under prior law. A person may return a covered unit
through a reachable route without paying a gate fee or buying another
unit. Provide suitable collection for bulky, hazardous or inaccessible
items. Use product classes and independently verified costs and risk
to differentiate charges. Count actual used return capacity and net
receipts once. Do not price projected resale as paid cash or use an
unvalidated eco-score as a binding fee. Public tariffs or assistance
keep their own enacted scope. Publish estimated incidence on buyers
and producers.

**LCY06 — Secure due work against supplier and scheme failure.** Before
covered placement require collectible financial assurance for the
defined producer-funded duty, sized to actual outstanding cohorts and
stress costs. A pool can serve small producers on open terms with
independent governance, protected assets and tested alternate delivery.
It cannot select who may repair or fix retail prices. The appointed
steward verifies separately controlled callable security and a qualified
receiver. Draw only within actual law and purpose when the covered
obligation fails. A finite elected residual bridge may protect urgent
collection or separately owed essential access when assurance is late
or insufficient. State the cap, named payer and recovery claim. Retain
consumer, worker and creditor claims and existing insolvency priorities.
A possible later recovery is not current money.

**LCY07 — Verify each material destination.** Keep a batch-level balance
of remaining use, safe reuse, accepted treatment, recovered usable output,
authorized residue or measured loss and unresolved material. Transfer,
collection, preparation for reuse, actual reuse and secondary material
use are distinct events. Compare at equivalent service, with paid work,
transport, toxicity, energy and environmental effects. No mass may be
counted twice. Independent technical and environmental inspection checks
the original weigh, test and downstream records, including public and
producer-funded facilities. Records can be maintained without identifying
a household. Do not make an unneeded product passport, model result
or tracking account a condition of ordinary repair or return.

**LCY08 — Follow the receiving end across borders.** Apply actual waste
classification, permits and cross-border consent where required.
Declare testing, real receiving and intended lawful reuse before
using a second-hand classification. If a shipment's disposition
cannot be verified, preserve it as unresolved and pursue lawful
correction, recovery or return through the responsible authorities.
A dispatch, foreign invoice or claimed certificate alone does not
prove recovery. Do not credit an export by moving pollution, unsafe
work or remaining liability outside the accounting boundary. Customs,
technical safety and external responsibility retain distinct powers.
