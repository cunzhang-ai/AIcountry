# AP09–AP12: received decisions, plural media and continuing claims

*Part of [reachable administration](../06-reachable-administration-and-finite-decisions.md); design 140 research selection.*

**AP09 — Use accountable existing roles and pay for their work.** The C04
career receiving/sector case officer owns intake, assistance and a reasoned
determination within enacted competence. The lawful manager can arrange
qualified cover/reassignment, with recorded conflicts and no clock reset; no
central AI or new universal case officer inherits every decision. Elected
national/local bodies choose law/burdens; record custodians disclose only with
authority; qualified professionals, courts, Treasury and B19 executors retain
distinct tasks. A complainant can go directly to independent review without
the manager's permission. AA/EV assess their lawful scopes, not final eligibility.

OP/T/C06 must count assistance, lawful evidence retrieval, interpretation,
decision/reply, interim receiving, court/counsel, payment/execution and correction,
including simultaneous ordinary demand and transition. Existing annual roles,
shared support or standby are not extra free people. A missing lawful source
or competent officer is an unmet duty/capacity problem, not compliance or a new
power. No modelled saving pays these obligations before matched qualification.

**AP10 — Preserve meaning across future communication.** Speech, supported
gesture, collective assistance, simulations and future agents can carry a
request or evidence. Preserve operative meaning and disputed interpretation
without forcing all experience into one schema. Distinguish conversation,
authorized filing, agency receipt, proof, decision, assent and delivery. An
assistant needs the relevant mandate; copies neither multiply a claim nor
establish reviewer independence. A permission to seek help is not consent to
disclose every record, waive a hearing, withdraw or accept an adverse decision.

Maintain usable direct/offline alternatives, limited source provenance and safe
correction. A vendor outage, inaccessible authentication or model version cannot
reset receipt, rewrite law or become final evidence. Lawful reconciliation across
disconnection preserves earlier acts, uncertainty and objections, not last-write
priority or a compulsory identity graph. Retain sensitive records only under
their lawful purpose/period, not permanently public because they supported review.

**AP11 — Report delay without rewarding exclusion.** Existing sector and
public-conditions accounts retain first receipt, help, evidence requests,
extension grounds, disposition, usable notice and actual execution separately.
Show pending age, unresolved/withdrawn/adverse cases, review/corrections and
known nonclaimant/third-party/worker burdens with safe uncertainty. No public
personal dossier or ranking by raw closure speed. A revised category cannot
erase old pending time. Examine denial/withdrawal spikes, inaccessible proof
and displaced ordinary work before calling a shorter queue an improvement.

**AP12 — Adopt and migrate prospectively, without a new readiness veto.**
An actual competent Act must reconcile scope, calendars, special source laws,
funding and exact start. Keep pending first dates and original evidence. For
pending cases the new ordinary ceiling would be 30 working days from that
Act's start, with the same single at-most-30-day extension; an earlier existing
due date/overdue breach, urgent right or stronger claim is never postponed or
forgiven. Preserve notice-based appeal periods and already pending courts.
Do not reopen final cases, create retrospective sanctions or recover benefits
merely because research changed the process. Paid OP/T successors preserve
staff, custody, records, contracts, earned rights and received remedies.

Actual software/constitution/permissions/holds and common method remain unchanged.
Final adoption needs fresh qualified and affected review. Reopen for rushed
denials, suppressed meaning, burdens exceeding gains, capture or a stronger
substantive simplification; actual readiness and better lives remain unverified.

[AH person-side public assistance](../07-person-directed-public-assistance.md)
adds a free core debtor/provider/outside route before/across requests. AH06
expressly proposes covered AP receiving status only for an actual chosen filing
under coordinated law; private advice remains distinct. These AP dates/proof/
source duties continue, with no automatic special-court/election/NI receipt.
