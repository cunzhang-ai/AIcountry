# PS01–PS04: food, plural production and resource fit

*Part of [C06/37](../37-physical-supply-and-paid-trade.md); design92 research.*

**PS01 — Count products and uses rather than a nominal residual.** For a
selected supply path identify units, quality, timing, location, raw inputs,
work, assets, losses/byproducts, imports and actual receiving. A tonne of mixed
food is not a nutrient, a warehouse release is not a meal, and a reserve is not
annual production. Compare actual voluntary/private demand and protected public
needs without inventing purchases to make GDP balance. Retain unfamiliar
products/processes with disclosed unsupported semantics until their actual
outputs/dependencies can be assessed; do not drop them into a zero residual.

**PS02 — Resource-fit selective production.** Preserve lawful private,
cooperative, community and public producers, bargaining and practical exit.
Favor accepted repair, appropriate rainfed crops, diversified horticulture and
local processing where their whole land/water/work/ecological burden is preferable.
Import other foods/materials through diversified paid routes rather than impose
self-sufficiency. Neither domestic share nor trade openness is an overriding
target. Compare retaining effective producers, import-heavy supply and alternative
domestic technologies at comparable human needs, rights and full transition cost.
Paid public procurement can build dependable demand within enacted purposes;
it cannot commandeer farms, select a compulsory diet or waive downstream rights.

**PS03 — Food choice, safe production and a rotating buffer.** The C09 food
steward proposes production/import/rotation plans; independent food safety,
nutrition evidence, animal welfare, land/water and market rules remain distinct.
The initial research plan tests90 days of selected dry goods, not90 days of
adequate diets or every person's safe substitute. Actual plans need age/clinical/
cultural alternatives, seasonality, receiving kitchens and usable retail routes.
All-animal-product imports in one numerical branch are neither a ban nor a
source-welfare exemption: feed, fishing, disease, care and external harms remain.

The11-commodity500kg/person reference is **ingredient receipt**, not eaten food
or a clinically validated national diet. Domestic packing side-streams and
distribution losses have distinct destinations; neither becomes free feed or
an automatic ecological credit. A higher-yield irrigated rival can need less
land/work and more water than the rainfed-fit hypothesis. Actual context can
reject either; synthetic/novel foods need their own safe recipes, energy,
nutrients, paid expertise and freely usable alternatives rather than an AI label.

**PS04 — Access, meals and personal time.** I/S food access remains personal:
cash must buy actual suitable food, with an accepting delivery/meal alternative
when cooking, travel or storage is unusable. No compulsory household food app,
family-head allocation or political food score. Public school/care/custody meals
are subsets of national food receipt, with their own safe catering work and
clinical duties. Do not add their ingredients twice or delete kitchen workers
because upstream packing is modeled. Household shopping, preparation/cleanup,
unpaid care, equipment and inability to cook remain real burdens; a voluntary
relative is no balancing workforce. Actual received-food credits, I05/IC and
Y indices change only under their lawful matched-component rules.

[FM01–FM05](../../C09/09/01-usable-food-and-local-meal-continuity.md)
compares accepted ingredients with staffed local preparation and
suitable direct meals. Price each route through the next window
without consuming water or paid shifts owed to another service.
