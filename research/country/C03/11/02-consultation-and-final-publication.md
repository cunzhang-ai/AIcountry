# Delegated law: hear people, then publish the exact final rule

*DR05–DR08; part of [C03/11](../11-delegated-rules-and-effective-public-control.md).*

**DR05 — Government assembles its case.** The named issuer publishes its source
clause, lawful baseline, exact draft and operational criteria, affected people
including nonusers/neighbors, retain/no-change and substantive alternatives,
original evidence and uncertainty, materiality reasons, distribution, costs,
funded receiver and transition. A person reporting an effect needs no technical
dossier. Models may assist source interpretation; agreeing copies are not
independent observations, public consent or power to issue the rule.

**DR06 — Consultation is a separate first stage.** Every ordinary delegated draft
receives at least **30 calendar days** of accessible input; reasoned disposition
follows within14calendar days of its close. Major national policy changes use
[FA01–FA12](../../C12/03-finite-attention-and-lawful-closure.md): at least30days
intake,10days framing correction, supported examination/closure within90days,
at most one elected30-day extension. Longer mandatory sector processes prevail.
These are distinct from the subsequent parliamentary30/45-day clock; neither
shortens the other. Failure of mandatory consultation prevents ordinary effect.

Offer paid assisted, oral, private, collective and future-media routes and
outreach to affected workers, small operators, people with disabilities and
localities. Preserve contested meanings, translation losses, original sources,
material objections, selected/unexamined reasons and dissent. Existing legal
claims go immediately to their own competent receiver/useful-time protection,
not into a consultation queue. Input/closure supplies no referendum mandate.

**DR07 — L is actual complete final publication.** After mandatory consultation,
the issuer sends the signed final packet to the Assembly Clerk and free public
register: enforceable text/versions, source law, classification, reasons,
changes since draft, required hearing/objection dispositions, funds/receivers,
proposed future effect and transition. **L** is the actual calendar date when
both the Clerk has received it and the complete packet is publicly accessible;
no backdated signature, later committee acknowledgment or vendor certification
can replace this event. Issue protected receipts independently of the issuer.
A claimant/member's timely first attempt blocked by the responsible office keeps
its actual receipt; official obstruction cannot convert it to a late call.

Missing nonoperative source testimony may use protected custody, but the whole
enforceable condition must be freely knowable and answerable. No license,
paywall, broken dynamic link or private model substitutes for an exact binding
version. If meaningful disclosure is impossible, narrow/withdraw or obtain
proper primary law, not enforce an unknowable rule. The issuer's assertion of
completeness remains court-reviewable; no clerk obtains a policy approval veto.

**DR08 — Paid public custody, not a monopoly platform.** The Assembly Clerk
employs a preassigned career rule registrar/counsel under C04, with separate
readable Assembly appropriation/payment. The registrar authenticates originals,
L, calls, actual votes, issuer making, start/expiry, superseded versions and
court corrections; it cannot decide lawful policy or invent a vote. Agency
and Assembly maintain separately usable original receipts/copies, offline/
paper access and accepted archival succession under C11/PD, not two unlimited
personal databases. Machine criteria and physical standards have exact units,
version and interpretation; unknown future semantics require disclosure and
challenge before binding use. Foreign updates acquire no automatic legal effect.

[ML01–ML05](../../C12/04-authentic-versions-and-public-law.md)
applies DR07 to all required authentic versions and binding annexes.
Preserve actual receipt, publication time, applicable independent
review and the distinction between a copy error and enacted divergence.
