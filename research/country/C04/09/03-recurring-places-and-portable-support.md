# XR11–XR15. Keep common services usable across recurring places

*Design311 · Proposed domestic continuity rules.*

**XR11 — Plan for the places and times a person actually uses.**
A person with an existing common service can ask a competent
provider or local authority to arrange recurring delivery in two
or more areas. Relevant patterns include shared care, study, work,
temporary lodging and regular support of another person. Decide
the applicable need, service and window under sector law. Ask
only for the service locations and facts necessary for that plan.
Ordinary residence can determine funding or a separate right.
A common service needs its own useful receiving route without
a forced declaration of permanent move. Keep the ordinary urgent
route open to people without a stable plan or address.

**XR12 — Keep one answerable public owner for each service.** Under
its source law the current authority remains answerable for the
active award and unresolved gaps until a lawful successor accepts.
It gives the person an accessible contact that can arrange or escalate
any uncovered episode. The competent host accepts and arranges
delivery for the stated dates within its own authority. Separate
care, treatment, travel, education and associated consent or safety
decisions. Use one agreed set of repeat windows or a flexible bounded
arrangement if the person and actual providers can use it. Reassess
only a materially changed decision and give reasons and direct review.
A person may use an existing voluntary provider arrangement when
it meets the same lawful terms. Keep other actual public owners,
deadlines and competent remedies reachable.

**XR13 — Fund the regular pattern and the bounded change.** Use actual
interlocal agreements, sector payments and the scoped XR06 advance.
Agree who pays for each task, accepted interval, ordinary travel,
protected fallback and any binding standby or cancellation amount.
Verify any named fallback with its own qualified worker, capacity
and owned cash. Where predictable shared demand cannot reliably
be served from ordinary funded capacity, compare payment per
accepted episode, a separately financed availability block plus
usage payments and voluntary shared delivery. Name who funds
the minimum accessible shift, facility or vehicle before it
must be booked, and who pays usage and any genuine cancellation.
Check low use, peak use and the next funded call. A fixed payment
requires verified available capacity. It supplies no proof of an
individual visit. Protect original host and origin services and
count each funded availability cost once with MF37–MF40. Recognize when a named vehicle, worker or payer
is already booked for someone else. Count each delivered episode
once. Record a lawful payment for unused but committed work as such.
Keep separate lawful tasks on the same day separately payable.
Never count household labour, an unfunded later recovery or the
same person's two records as extra capacity. Preserve freedom
to change plans, travel and decline an offered provider. A genuine
limit calls for an actual alternative or a recorded gap with remedy.

**XR14 — Change only the affected assignment and verify each receipt.**
On a late location change keep prior claims and unaffected bookings.
Confirm the new provider and associated access, record consent and
release old bookings under their terms when safe. Open the competent
urgent route where the ordinary handoff cannot finish in time.
An invoice for standby does not establish a visit. A successful
visit does not establish a separate journey or treatment. When
capacity or funds fail, preserve the original relevant clock, record
the missed task and use independent correction and actual funded
sector remedies. Report repeated failures to the competent elected
bodies for review of ordinary capacity and finance. A service failure
or dispute does not itself change residence, an award or political standing.

**XR15 — Preserve control of evidence and choice of channel.** Give the
person a plain copy of the agreed plan and separate decisions, with
safe and assisted ways to change or challenge them. Providers receive
the necessary scope and a safe contact or service location under
existing data rules. Use a protected service reference where lawful
to reconcile the same episode across authorities. Maintain a usable
non-digital route. No universal location or household history is
needed. AI may help prepare schedules, find duplicated bookings
and flag unsupported transfers. The person and responsible
professionals keep consent, safety and actual acceptance; lawful
public owners retain payment and disposition. Reopen on persistent
unserved routes, unfair residence proxies, privacy harm or a
simpler comparably safe arrangement.
