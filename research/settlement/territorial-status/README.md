# Territorial status and constitutional coherence

Design143 · Research selection, not enactment or operation

[TS01–TS16](../../country/C04/02-territorial-self-government.md) is the canonical
country procedure. [A15](../../authority/provisions/04-territorial-status-A15.md)
sets its proposed protected charter content; C14 states the founding/rank bridge.
The [15-clause review](01-legal-spine-review.md) distinguishes retained scoped
rules, repaired gaps and next work. It is not a full enacted-code certificate.

The change replaces a future-threshold promise with a five-percent area trigger,
finite paid negotiation, national A9 plus separate area consent, explicit franchise/
calendar joins and continuing obligations. It adds no permanent office, first
permission ballot, forced mediator or self-executing sovereignty. National veto
and exclusion/administrative burden objections remain material.

The [decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-territorial-status-and-constitutional-coherence/decision-record.md)
compares no-change, ordinary repair, discretionary talks and unilateral-area
alternatives. Original comparator sources are limited legal examples, not an
empirical finding that this procedure outperforms others. Model checks use
invented counts and assumed predicates; they authenticate no person or approval.
Actual affected participation, legal authority, map, qualified source/capacity
and observed better lives remain unknown. Next reconcile A8 rights restrictions/
derogations against ordinary safety, clinical and liberty powers.
