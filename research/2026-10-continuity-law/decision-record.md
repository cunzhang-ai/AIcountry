## Selected meaning and bounded findings

E02 preserves a credible first claim date and an accepted handoff without
forcing a dossier or AI gate. E04 separates standing spending authority
from actual cash and the separate temporary budget bridge. E05–E08 preserve
due income and define a seven-day actual-resource hardship formula, timely
determination, fair recovery and explicit treatment of accrued claims.
Its eligible scope, basket value and update rule still belong to a complete
income statute; the old transfer envelope does not supply those parameters.

E09–E12 distinguish administrative routing from the earlier safe time,
actual qualified care and separate clinical/liberty/child/scarcity powers.
E13–E16 supply direct urgent review, no filing/appeal fee for this protection,
fair interim process and a prior-law narrow payment/record substitute.
The fresh review bounds ex parte hearing delay to 72 hours plus at most
one justified period of 72; shorter source-law limits prevail and serial
orders cannot reset the clock. The substitute uses published merit roles,
assignment/recusal, standing pay and separate audit; a challenged office
cannot choose its named-case replacement or control every credential.
E16 bounds qualification and baseline support without making silence an
electoral qualification, postponement, winner or new caretaker mandate.
E17–E20 expose displaced work, breach, an elected shortfall response and
continuing duties during commencement or later change.

Equal standing, care without compulsory relatives, enforceable protection
and public replacement are values. Clock origins, legal scope and accounting
identities are definitions. Chosen clocks and core shares are hypotheses.
The model's five adverse cases satisfy their outer administrative clock
but miss the actual useful time; all retain their claims. Five further
cases test bounded interim-order expiry, a justified extension, attempted
serial extension, nonattendance after opportunity and a shorter source limit.

## Evidence, objections and qualification

[Pinned inputs](inputs.json) identify the historical numerical source family;
[results](results.json) and [validation](validation.json) check conservation,
non-erasure, clock/order counterexamples and path failure. Current canonical

Old numbers are reused only as unchanged authored arithmetic; the previous
whole-inventory review cannot transfer to revised legal clauses. No new

survey establishes safe capacity or any country's current performance.

As normative comparators, [ICCPR Articles 2(3), 14 and 25](https://2covenants.ohchr.org/About-ICCPR.html)
connect effective/enforced remedies, fair independent hearing and genuine
periodic political participation. [ILO Recommendation 202, paragraph 7](https://normlex.ilo.org/dyn/nrmlx_en/f?p=NORMLEXPUB%3A12100%3A0%3A%3ANO%3A%3AP12100_INSTRUMENT_ID%3A3065524)
supports defined statutory protection and accessible complaint/appeal routes.
OHCHR text was inspected during drafting and its official source rechecked
on 14 October; the ILO paragraph was available through official indexed
text, but direct full-page retrieval failed. Neither source supplies these
time limits, resource shares or applicable AIcountry law. They are not
independent empirical support for a service or governance outcome.

Material objections remain: outer limits may become slower service targets;
earlier useful time therefore prevails. Courts and substitutes may become
unaccountable protectors; hearing bounds, assignment, controlled scope,
funding/source separation and existing independent discipline remain required.
Standing spending can bind later majorities; lawful prospective revision
with accrued-right transition remains available. A protected core can hide
deprivation elsewhere; publish the displaced duty and require elected grounds.
More forms and hearings consume staff/resident time; ordinary reports and
voluntary cooperation survive, and costs cannot be declared free. These
are authored objections and dispositions, not evidence of affected-party consent.
