# Two valid orders, one accessible vehicle and an earlier decision time

*Design301 · Constructed comparison. Costs, receipt and timing are assumptions.*

At 08:00 the county receives evidence that the ordinary backup lift is
unavailable. Two enforceable orders in different ordinary civil courts
now rely on the same staffed accessible vehicle. Order A requires
receiving four residents at a staffed safe site by 09:40. The only
qualified shared vehicle must depart by 08:50, reaching it at 09:30.
Order B requires timely transport of two people to a housing hearing.
The accepted shared vehicle must also depart at 08:50 to reach the court
by 09:20. The hearing begins at 09:30. Both movements are lawful and
accepted by the people involved. The two routes and the accessible
boarding procedures cannot be combined in this interval.

Court B's order already accepts an independently qualified substitute
vehicle. Reserving one requires four units and commitment by 08:35.
A separate accessible hearing at the participants' site costs two
units and must be set up by 08:45 to admit them at 09:20. It needs
a narrowly lawful change to the means specified in order B and actual
acceptance by the hearing court and participants, including privacy,
counsel and communication. Court B retains the hearing decision.
One originating judge is unavailable in the relevant time. The
example assumes the limited appellate competence is enacted and
a different qualified formation and its intake are already funded.

At four units, retain the separately available vehicle on its
ordinary terms in this case. It avoids urgent variation and
preserves the chosen hearing mode. At two units, the independent
hearing route can achieve both protections. A later court decision,
two judges without the third required member, unsafe communication,
refused alternative or a hearing channel that shares the failed
access controller defeats that route. An accessible invitation
does not establish entry to the hearing. Both routes in the
comparison are assumed to reach their authorized receivers.

If neither route qualifies, use the shared vehicle for A only if
existing authority specifically permits that deployment under the
actual facts and orders. This is a separate input in the failure
test. Without it, the comparison reports no lawful joint or
single dispatch through the conflicting assignment. It leaves all
other lawful actions available. Even if A is served, B's missed
original hearing protection and claims remain open. No docket
flag, later arrival or court filing amounts to a stay or
successful performance. A later ordinary remedy is separately
assessed and recorded.
