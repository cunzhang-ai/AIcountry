# EB09–EB12. Match the loss without cancelling a different earned right

*Part of [earnings transition](../16-portable-earnings-and-work-transition.md).*

**EB09 — Wages, notice and severance have different purposes.** Full earned
salary/scheduled paid leave, guaranteed hours and notice/pay-in-lieu retain WL source/debtor/
ordinary remedies and actual protected wage periods. Unpaid old pay is not usable
cash, a valid contract reduction or a limited EB claim; WL34's own advance/cap
and earlier I/E remedies remain. Cash-out of accrued unused leave at termination is an old earned-rest claim,
not automatically new future wage-covered weeks or E at cash receipt. Identify
actual source/period/substance as with notice and severance. EB starts only for
actual post-coverage or valid prospectively reduced future remuneration. No automatic EB reimbursement to an
employer or assignment of all worker wage/creditor claims follows.

WL16 redundancy severance compensates service/job loss, not fictional paid wage
weeks. Preserve it and separate actual damages/old claims; no automatic severance-
as-26wages offset against EB. Existing actual TA/I05 receipt/resource law still
classifies the severance safely; do not claim nonassessment under I05 merely
because EB does not offset it. A true same-period wage/replacement component
must be identified by substance/source, not hidden as a top-up or severance.

**EB10 — Actual replacement once, preserve stronger old sources.** TW medical,
old unemployment/foreign insurance, private same-risk replacement and EB need
same-person/period/component matching. M uses only the qualified gross discharged
component corresponding to actual usable net receipt and actual lawful withheld-
tax/credit discharge, never a merely owed gross benefit. An unreceived award
is not cash or a funded recovery. Old stronger awards/periods/top-ups remain, with exact actual
priority and no double paid same component. Necessary care/cost compensation,
Y earned rights and genuine additional contractual top-ups are not silently
absorbed into an insurance limit. EB creates no TW medical entitlement or
clinical/care consent; concurrent absence needs its own real sources. If actual
source semantics conflict, obtain assisted qualified interpretation/correction,
not a model-imposed denial or cancellation of another benefit.

**EB11 — Taxable replacement, no invented work or pension credit.** New EB is
ordinary taxable personal replacement income under coordinated TA/NF source
law, with net actually received EB entering I05's earnings-replacement E once
under its existing single aggregate disregard/taper. No second0.2B allowance,
family pooling or historical wage treated as current I05 cash. Current basket,
necessary housing, additional needs/service credits and safe actual receipt keep
their own law. Actual tax/gross/net and later corrections must be shown, not
assumed from the withdrawn numerical TN defaults or a frozen salary case.

EB is not worked-wage income for Y's10% levy or automatic earned units. Y06
care/illness/involuntary-unemployment qualification remains independent, including
safe suitable work/accommodation/protected refusal, proved unpaid covered wages,
same-period top-up and separately required G-to-S funding. EB's choice of no
extra job-search quota neither grants nor cancels that credit. Paid real new
work/learning retains its actual wage/contribution/accrual source once; receipt
of EB, an AI output or duplicate account creates none of those facts.

**EB12 — Correct entitlement, distinguish collection and actual receipt.** Newly
proved previously omitted actual work/legally earned pay changes correct E and
the award even if that wage is unpaid. Later receipt of E already counted cannot
reduce it a second time. Newly received comparable replacement changes gross-
discharged M only on its qualified receipt/source facts. State the original
period/item, amount correction, quota-day correction and any restitution claim
separately; a corrected entitlement is not immediate collection or usable cash.

Reconcile actual gross/net/withholding, prior EB/I05 payments and tax/Y/source
credits before any recovery; never recover gross from only net receipt, an
already corrected component twice, or unrelated severance/damages. A good-faith
backpay-only reclassification waits for actual matched usable duplicate receipt
for collection, under I08/E subsistence-safe rules; a bare court award is no
money. Proved fraudulent hidden work may use its ordinary lawful restitution/
fault route, not that deferral as immunity. Employer wrongdoing alone proves no
worker fraud. Actual reply/review/limitation/finality applies; preserve independent
I05 actual-resource protection. This is no new unlimited wage guarantee or
WL35 automatic subrogation; full employer debt survives actual unmatched claims.
Only received recovery is a fiscal source. Restore actual wage/Y records for
hidden work, with proper fault proof, no AI blacklist or private account seizure.

[CL17](../17/03-portable-pay-and-overlaps.md) adds direct care replacement to
comparable received M, not worked labor E, paid-employment history or13 recovery-
work weeks. No182day spell reset. Actual continued salary retains its real source;
match actual usable net/withholding/gross discharge once, without changing EB.
