# KR09–KR12: learning, repair, AI and protected collective meaning

*Design 95 research candidate; not enacted law, a funded offer or demonstrated
superiority.*

**KR09 — Exceptions, exhaustion and contracts.** Quote lawfully available works for
criticism/reporting with fair scope/source, use justified illustration in teaching,
preserve deteriorating holdings through qualified archives, and make accessible-format
copies for eligible users. Lawful owners/users may make necessary temporary copies and
inspect or decompile only what is needed for security testing, repair, accessibility or
interoperable independent products, without redistributing protected expression or
bypassing safety/privacy law. Genuinely noncommercial research on lawfully accessed
works may computationally analyze them with secure purpose-limited copies, no expressive
substitute or onward corpus release; commercial training that reproduces protected works
needs a license or another specifically applicable defense. Each exception has a defined
purpose and is tested against normal exploitation and legitimate holder interests; a
contract or technical lock cannot nullify an enacted lawful exception, but assistance
tools remain bounded. Research status follows the actual use, not a sponsor’s label; the
exception supplies no commercial corpus resale or public expressive substitute. It does
not give copyright over learned facts or require permission for independent ideas.
Noncommercial experimental use of an invention and genuinely necessary regulatory tests
are limited exceptions, not a right to market the patented product. Reasonable
accessible-format copying is for the actual access need, not unrestricted commercial
reissue. Assisted/offline access, lawful libraries and practical means of exercising
these rights need funding; a technical lock cannot make a statutory defence unusable.

**KR10 — Authorized items and exhaustion.** International exhaustion follows an actual
rights-holder-authorized first sale anywhere for resale, import and repair of that
particular item; it does not authorize reproduction, new manufacture, misleading marks
or unsafe product use. Actual treaty/bilateral limits, who authorized sale, and
separated software-license rather than sold-copy cases need source review; border
measures require express law and due process.

**KR11 — Human contribution and AI.** AI instances acquire neither authorship/title nor
additional political mandates merely through generation or copying. Protect proven
qualifying human contributions under KR02/KR05; separately identify
employer/commission/contract transfers and lawful ownership of materials. No copyright
in facts, general ideas, styles or an unedited machine output merely because a user paid
for generation; no inference that unlawful source copying or confidential-data misuse
becomes lawful because an output lacks copyright. Mere prompting/model ownership does
not establish inventorship; require the selected law's demonstrable qualifying
contribution. Retain provenance with proportionate privacy, not a universal prompt or
identity log.

For commercial training that performs a reproduction covered by another's actual right,
require licence or a specific enacted exception; KR09 supplies the separate bounded
lawful-access research exception. Neither blanket all-training freedom nor ownership of
facts/style follows. [PD ordinary data law](../../C02/07-private-life-and-personal-data.md) owns those
separate bases/rights; KR permission cannot substitute. Human consent for personal/confidential material and research
participation remains independent of copyright permission; data-subject consent is not
always the only lawful basis, and forced consent cannot cure forbidden processing. A
deployed public-law decision must expose its exact operative legal rule, relevant
reasons and challengeable source basis; it does not require publishing every private
training record or convert proprietary output into sovereign law. Purely autonomous
output gets no new copyright/patent merely from a machine’s production under this
candidate. Existing rights in incorporated expression, a process, data or lawful
confidential inputs survive. A human’s real contribution may qualify; routine prompting
or owning the model alone does not prove it. This allocation does not settle every
future claim about morally significant nonhuman entities.

**KR12 — Collective and unfamiliar knowledge.** Traditional, oral, situated, collective
or evolving knowledge is not presumed abandoned because it lacks a single author,
registry entry or Western property vocabulary. Receive protected original
testimony/demonstration, identify contested custodians/affected users and separate
disclosure permission, grant of licence and any lawful collective authority. No
self-appointed representative, majority account count, museum, scientist or AI can
consent for everyone. Existing public-domain material is not newly enclosed as one
speaker's private property; neither does public availability erase privacy, confidence,
valid source restrictions or a proved communal right. Community submissions need not
disclose sacred/sensitive details publicly to obtain help; courts test legally relevant
title/validity with answerable protected evidence. Unknown meaning/authority may limit
the asserted right or proposed use rather than create a new cultural veto by analogy.
