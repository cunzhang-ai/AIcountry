# RC06–RC10. A call must reach capable help

*Part of [civil rescue](../13-civil-rescue-and-incident-command.md).*

**RC06 — Public continuous emergency intake.** The local Civil Rescue Service
arranges free, continuously staffed emergency intake/dispatch, with an actual
responsible shift officer, reachable alternative during outage and qualified
sector receiving. Shared dispatch with other localities/sectors is possible;
no compulsory new call-center corporation or invented universal telephone number.
Provide usable voice/text, supported sign/interpretation, accessible in-person/
relay and future compatible methods; speech, literacy, a device, account, digital
ID or ability to describe a technical incident is no precondition. An emergency
report needs enough to seek safety, not a complete AP dossier or payment check.
Give a usable acknowledgement, safety advice within competence and actual handover;
a forwarded ticket is not a reached responder. Do not promise an unsupported
universal arrival minute or conceal a missed useful-time duty behind averages.

**RC07 — Dispatch by actual need and lawful competence.** Qualified triage links
fire/physical rescue to rescue teams, clinical urgency to an accepted HG/NCS
clinical dispatcher/team, social/mental-health needs to actual competent care,
and actual crime/force grounds to PO police. No routine police deployment to
every distress, disability or welfare call; a credible current bodily danger
still receives lawful immediate protection. The intake officer is not a clinician
by job title, and a clinical dispatcher is not a police commander. Explain unsafe
or declined handovers, preserve first receipt/earlier E/sector dates and keep a
responsible receiving officer pursuing available safe help. Bad connectivity,
private-provider failure or a disputed address cannot make the person an ownerless
case. Later investigation/charges need their own grounds and due process.

**RC08 — Everyday preparedness is paid work.** Director maintains actual risk-
and travel-sensitive stations, shift/relief competence, maintained protective and
rescue equipment, usable communications/water/access routes and qualified practice.
Publish safe aggregate coverage/known gaps; protect exploitable details and homes.
Compare prevention, safer buildings/land use and access with extra response units
through existing sector issuers/SC/TD/HP, not a new Director general inspection,
demolition or permit power. Disability, age, language and isolated places need
usable plans and paid help; do not assume families can evacuate people unaided.
Practice must not invent a new compulsory drill, enter a home, remove a pupil or
bill an unwilling resident. Local people/workers can challenge the plan/source
through ordinary council/LR/AP/court routes without joining a rescue association.

**RC09 — Real professional, community and voluntary cooperation.** The public
backbone carries due continuity/standby, not a promise that unpaid neighbors will
fill mandatory rosters. Communities, cooperatives and volunteers may contribute
local warning, knowledge and safe help without a credential to express concern.
Ordinary lawful private rescue/necessity remains subject to existing P/B grounds;
RC gives no private general force permission. Any public responder designation
needs actual consent, competence, training, supervision, insurance/protection,
lawful status and paid expenses/time where assigned obligations require it. Do
not treat volunteering as consent to an indefinite shift, compelled labor or
professional practice beyond qualification. Pay actual release/cover; recognize
informal help without forcing it through one national platform or registry.

**RC10 — Accepted mutual aid with a safe home service.** Director requests and
accepts interlocal aid under prior source law/agreements naming employer, actual
available crew/equipment/time, deployment command/task, liability, safe release,
return, cost and receiving conditions. Coordinator assists real cross-area/foreign
requests through lawful external channels; neither a map nor aid offer supplies
foreign consent, import/clinical licence or jurisdiction. Do not remove donor
life-safety/election/court-critical capacity without a verified lawfully accepted
safe substitute. A shared crew cannot simultaneously satisfy two shift plans or
HG/NC/DF duties. Owner disagreement, absent competence or failed receiving is
reported and reviewed; actual CP may resolve only its expressly permitted pool.
A promise of future reimbursement is not cash, equipment or an accepted crew.
