## Diagnosis, evidence and values

Income dependence and unequal control can make formal consent/exit unreal.
At-will loss, sham contracting, changing schedules and provider failure can
undermine free association and political standing. A rigid rule can also exclude
entrants, raise prices, destroy viable jobs or concentrate union/state power.
Ownership, bargaining, public income/services and real labor capacity are distinct
causes/choices; a coordinator, AI review score or more consultation alone is not
an answer. Values: dignity, agency, equal standing, voluntary enterprise, independent
worker/employer organization, privacy, safe continuity and democratic revision.

## Whole choices and selection

| Arrangement | Disposition / retained reasons and losses |
|---|---|
| TW/MP/AS principles alone | Keep their actual rights/source scope, reject as sufficient definition of ordinary employment/contracts/exit/collective relations |
| At-will contracts with a strong universal foundation | Can ease adjustment and reduce some income dependence; reject as substitute for protection against employer retaliation/coercion. Full U remains a real income/ownership rival, not already financed |
| Individual floors plus voluntary bargaining only | Protects some rights with less workplace veto cost, but weak groups may have little practical influence; retained as a narrower rival |
| Actual status/floors, fair exit, independent collective/action and scoped council consent | Selected current architecture, with explicit private/public source, strong-right continuity and real closure possible; may increase hiring/administration/entry/deadlock costs |
| Strong sector extension, worker board co-determination/ownership | Structural rivals capable of displacing the selection, not silently absorbed as another council label; need the next enterprise-constitution choice |
| Priority-only wage recovery / wider funded wage guarantee | Lower public cost can leave assetless workers unpaid; universal guarantee has much larger risk/moral hazard. Select a bounded general advance while keeping higher AS/state duties and full employer claims |
| Symmetric unpaid lockout | Retained rejected-default power bargain. Select pay for willing guaranteed-work staff locked out for pressure; actual unsafe/unviable operations can lawfully close with obligations |
