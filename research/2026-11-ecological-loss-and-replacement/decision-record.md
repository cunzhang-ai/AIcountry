# Design320 decision on ecological loss and verified replacement

## Baseline and decision

Pin Design319, C09 future options and ecological limits, LU permission,
H and HL housing, C06 funded choices, and the current framework. Choose
function-by-function floor compliance through every irreversible stage.
The project assistant authors the selection. Legitimate elected
lawmakers and participating affected communities control actual adoption.

## Alternatives and evidence

Compare aggregate tradable credits, prohibition of any substitution, and
verified functional substitution under prior-law floors. Credits can finance
restoration and pool monitoring; purchase and forecast can also transfer a
real gap to another place or season. A general ban is easier to state and
protects against weak evidence; it can foreclose useful restoration and
needed construction where real substitution is feasible. Select verified
additional capacity before damage where equivalence is supported. Preserve
irreplaceable functions under their own protections and retain separately
reported above-floor residual losses. This allocates timing and proof risk
to the proponent and may increase initial costs or delay development.

England's irreplaceable-habitat rules demonstrate an existing distinction
between standardized gain accounting and compensation. The EU judgment
illustrates cumulative assessment and a demanding evidence threshold in
its particular legal regime. The hydrology paper supports the importance
of local hydrologic timing for wetlands. These sources do not identify a
universal floor, proof rule or project result. See [sources](sources.md).

## Authority, migration and reopening

A competent legislature sets floors prospectively. Existing designated
officers apply them with independent review. The elected budget authority
must choose and finance interim and permanent provision through its
own instruments. Adopt ER01–ER05 only with prospective legal effect,
existing rights and remedies, and funded evidence, independent assessment
and successor capacity. Preserve earlier permissions and claims under
their governing instruments. A release check proves ecological eligibility
for that stage only. Planning permission, funds and actual housing remain
separately reviewable.

For one real catchment, publish matched observations, hydrologic and habitat
dependencies, combined permissions, tenure, independently inspected service
capacity and paid housing alternatives. Obtain participation by affected
communities and record dissent and material uncertainty. Reopen if
verified protection fails, another option protects the same functions
with lower exclusion, or delivery capacity makes essential service
obligations impossible under the chosen path. No observed national
benefit, public assent, adoption or operational readiness is established.
