# H01–H05: a home and an enforceable tenancy

*Part of [C09/12](../12-secure-housing-and-repair.md); design91 proposed law.*

**H01 — People, homes and privacy are different units.** Assess actual safe
occupancy, private sleeping/living space, sanitation, accessibility, climate,
care and essential journeys. Income-assessment groups, kinship and AI accounts
establish none of these. New public offers default to self-contained homes;
chosen shared facilities require meaningful consent and a usable alternative.
Protect a private secure sleeping space for each adult who does not choose to
share, suitable child/dependant space and informal family forms. Do not require
one dwelling per independent cash claimant, presume pooled money or force
separation/cohabitation. State each adult's occupancy, payment share and exit;
another adult's debt is not presumed joint. Publish measurable building/occupancy
standards under technical law; a national model's unit count is no adequacy test.

**H02 — Secure covered residential use.** Under the prospective H19 regime,
ordinary residential tenancies are indefinite from possession. Contract expiry,
sale, insolvency, higher income, criticism or refusal of optional AI alone is
no possession ground. An independent ordinary court may order possession for
proved serious persistent breach, actual abandonment of primary-home use,
actual unresolved arrears under H14, necessary
owner occupation, or lawful permanent redevelopment when adequate repair is
infeasible. Give reasons, notice, defense, proportionality and effective appeal.
No administrative allocation office or landlord decides its own eviction.
Protected work, study, illness, care or detention absence alone is not abandonment;
prove actual loss of primary-home use, not a consumption score or missed visit.

Private owner occupation needs six months' notice, actual personal need with
no reasonably usable owned alternative, and intended occupation for at least
12 months; sham use permits reinstatement where practicable and proved loss.
A public/community body cannot invoke personal occupation. Redevelopment needs
the separate lawful planning/property process and H18 receiving; a maintenance
excuse cannot end a tenancy. Serious immediate danger uses bounded safety
evacuation/protection, not a presumption of guilt or permanent forfeiture.

**H03 — Ordinary landlord and resident duties.** The landlord supplies safe
structure, essential systems and repair; tenants pay lawful actual rent, avoid
proved damage and respect others' safe use. No lockout, document seizure,
utility disconnection or automated score removes possession. Entry normally
needs agreed timing and48 hours' notice; actual emergency allows only necessary
entry with a record and challenge. No political, intimate-life or household-head
monitoring. New deposits are capped at one month's lawful rent, held separately
from landlord operating money, and returned within14 days of surrender except
an itemized genuinely disputed amount. A dispute does not freeze the balance.

**H04 — Repair with a receiver.** Address imminent serious danger immediately
and arrange safe receiving before useful protection time expires;24 hours is
an outer response bound, not permission to wait. For ordinary defects acknowledge
within two working days, provide a dated reasoned plan within seven calendar
days and complete necessary repair within30 calendar days unless a specifically
justified safe extension is independently challengeable. Inspectors under C08
technical law may order repair/restricted use; independent courts enforce it.
Fund inspection and the actual repair/receiving contractor. Authorized escrow,
abatement and repair-and-deduct require evidence and prompt independent review;
do not compel tenant labor or leave emergency work waiting for reimbursement.

**H05 — Leave, separation and death.** A covered tenant may leave on28 days'
notice without a public-option exit charge; violence protection can shorten it.
One joint tenant's notice cannot surrender others' homes: sever only separable
obligations, otherwise obtain prompt judicial allocation without forcing an
unsafe tie or releasing third-party debts. B26 occupancy does not transfer title.
Existing co-tenants retain their own rights on death, without requalification.
For a new successor require actual primary-home occupancy for12 months, except
birth, recent dependent care or recognition wrongfully denied. Recognize notified
residence with the affected private household's consent; a landlord may object
only on specific lawful safety/occupancy/third-party grounds, with direct court
challenge. Paper recognition is not a household-head or landlord selection power.
Several qualifying residents succeed jointly/separably as actual space permits;
B-family/courts resolve incompatible occupancy with actual alternatives, not
committee favoritism. Courts decide disputed exceptions; no outsider buys a
queue place. Children receive safe continuity and a lawful
receiver, not inherited debt or ownership by inference. A necessary size/access
transfer needs an actually usable alternative and independent challenge.

[Design149 CB](../14-shared-buildings-and-neighbor-rights.md) makes common-
property association repair a separate actual duty using H04's earlier response/
plan/repair clocks. Residents can report directly; manager/landlord forwarding
cannot reset receipt. Landlords keep H responsibility despite a failed vote,
common fund or contractor. CB planned-unit entry needs actual consent or its
specific independent access order; ordinary48-hour notice alone is no forced
entry, surveillance or eviction permission. Rent/deposit/receiving rights persist.

[RT private pricing](../18-private-rents-tenure-and-housing-entry.md) now fills
the H02 indefinite-tenure price interface: prior market-review/escalator terms
are constrained prospectively by its actual ceiling/migration. Original B
agreement law already bars arbitrary repricing; H possession/repair/deposit/
succession remains. A rent order is no completed dwelling or new source.

[TH01–TH04 safe home temperatures](../23/01-standard-and-ordinary-repair.md)
specifies the applicable indoor outcome and first receiver for heat and cold.
A disputed building, bill or supply cause cannot reset H04's clock.
Actual interim protection preserves tenancy, rent and continuing claims.
