# T95–T99. Keep review funded when public AI fails its test

13 November 2026 · Integrated design 444 · Candidate first-budget rules

**T95 — Put the review source outside the result it must judge.**
Fund core independent evaluation and applicable appeal capacity
from standing lawful sources that remain available if the programme
fails evaluation, pauses or yields no savings. In the first and
next budget windows show actual payer, purpose, settlement and
separate authority. The reviewed programme cannot condition
payment on a favorable result or call an independent reviewer
into service recovery. Ordinary funded common support is available
within its approved scope and independent control.

**T96 — Separate distinct work even when qualifications overlap.**
Under [DP33–DP37](../../C11/09/08-verified-gains-and-public-service-receipt.md)
and [DP78–DP83](../../C11/09/17-independent-public-ai-evaluation.md),
record ordinary delivery, case correction and appeals, evaluation,
and the trained successor by useful time and actual role. A
shared specialist may fill distinct compatible windows, subject
to conflict and authority rules. Count a simultaneous hour once.
Protect existing cases and the next review window before using
genuinely released time. An authorized operational fallback needs
its own qualified, accepting capacity.

**T97 — Decide material reductions explicitly.** A proposal to
reduce the independent review or standby budget to release an AI
saving needs its own affirmative disposition by the competent
budget authority, within existing budget rules. Provide the
actual avoidable cash or qualified time, legal scope, receiving
task and evidence that residual capacity survives common loss.
Use DP37's existing observation and adverse-test requirements.
Routine lawful staffing within an existing mandate retains its
usual owner. A draft budget or a service report cannot withdraw
a source or promise the same release to another body.

**T98 — Compare on ordinary and adverse deadlines.** Compare a
strong ordinary route, bounded AI with independently funded
oversight and broader reliance with any proposed reduction.
Test both the loss of forecast savings and the extra work caused
by a common failure in the same window. Include arrears,
appeals, case handback and paid succession in the next window.
Hold only an unsupported optional start or reduction. Carry due
services and remedies, report the remaining shortfall and use
the ordinary elected budget and correction routes.

**T99 — Verify both work and authority through renewal.** Publish
safe results, remaining claims, actual fiscal effects and
material dependency gaps. Give affected people and opposition
a usable opportunity to challenge claimed savings and independent
capacity. AI can reconcile calendars and test assumptions.
It cannot certify its own review, release an appropriation or
transfer a legal duty. Preserve original claims and protected
records during changes in supplier, staff or administration.
Reopen on a review conflict, exhausted fallback, missed due
date or better funded ordinary service.
