# C06. Whole-country commitment account

*Part of [C06 public finance](../C06-public-finance.md); integrated design 56 research, not enacted law.*

## Account scope and the [joint delivery schedule](18-joint-commitment-and-delivery-schedule.md)

For each existing duty, proposed expansion, maintenance obligation and contingent exposure, source custodians record legal standing, affected people and territory, cash by due date and currency, national/local financing, actual qualified labor, land, water, energy, materials, imports, ecological limits, provider and remedy capacity, lifecycle cost and uncertainty. Internal transfers, refinancing, public guarantees and existing liabilities are reconciled; the same worker, site, reserve or cash receipt cannot finance two simultaneous promises. The schedule also includes sanitation treatment and disposal, waste collection, cyber recovery and manual service routes, border inspection and perishable custody; their common vendors, fuel, staff and alternate sites cannot be counted twice. The account
distinguishes **lawfully owed, authorized, cash available, physically deliverable, actually received and
remedied**. An unknown input is not zero, and an unpaid obligation is not imaginary borrowing. Treasury
assembles cash, sector and local authorities attest delivery, C11 preserves source/method history, and
audit and opposition can challenge omissions. Neither those offices nor AI acquires a policy veto: the
Assembly votes on the exposed tradeoffs under rights and law.

Retirement and care liabilities are not one annual transfer line. Record basic
old-age payments, accrued earnings benefits, contribution receipts, publicly
financed care and interruption credits, survivor and dependant claims, legacy
promises, administrative expense, reserve risk and long-term care workers and
sites by cohort and due date. Publish projections under alternative longevity,
birth, migration, employment, wage, care and price paths. A pension reserve,
future tax forecast or assumed unpaid relative cannot be spent twice. The
Assembly compares prospective adjustments with current claims and other
services; the actuarial office exposes risk but cannot change a pension rule.

A contemplated territorial-status change requires a paired current-and-successor
account, not a line on a map. Show which level would collect each tax and pay
each debt, pension, wage, court order and service; which people can still reach
water, health care, payments, schools and transport across the line; and which
assets, workers, ecological duties, records and foreign obligations cannot be
divided without agreement or adjudication. The account must show costs of
overlap and a failed negotiation. A forecast of regional revenue cannot certify
constitutional consent or discharge an existing national obligation.

The schedule also tests whether services can be used together in a person's day:
travel, waiting, forms, paid work and unpaid care consume time. Local access and
spatial plans identify shared sites, water, grid connections, construction crews,
transport and maintenance before counting several promises against one capacity.
Coordination, civil legal help, independent enforcement, safe reception and
cross-boundary planning require staff and appropriations in this same account;
they cannot be booked as free consequences of better information.

The account also counts the institutions' own load. A proposed office, mandatory
step or new oversight power states what existing work it displaces or why added
capacity is necessary; expected cases, qualified staff and reviewer hours,
training lead times, clerical and evidence work, accessible sites, translation,
travel, resident and carer time, backlogs, maintenance and an alternate route
all enter the comparison in their own units and distribution, not an invented
single money value. The same professional, vendor or shared credential
cannot be counted as independent capacity for several conflicting duties.
The Assembly compares repair of an existing body, shared support with distinct
decisions, a new body and no change. Estimates inform an elected decision; they
cannot cancel an existing claim or become a technical veto over legal protection.
The [institutional-usability decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-institutional-load/decision-record.md)
records the competing packages, common-controller objections and real-world test.

Future civic mediation also consumes hearing time, mandate checks, safe
non-agent access, network-failure capacity and independent correction. Agent
replication cannot multiply the public attention, spending envelope or worker
capacity assigned to one principal. Delayed revocation and disputed prior
acts enter the same legal and resource account; a synchronized copy is not
proof that a shared commitment was available twice.

Delegated rules consume agency analysis, consultation, Assembly time, publication,
training, inspection, legal aid and court capacity. A package of small rules can
impose one material burden or disable a remedy; an agency cannot choose its own
lower scrutiny tier through a fiscal estimate. The [Assembly capacity account](../C03/05-assembly-capacity-and-scope.md)
uses the same staff and hearing inputs across budgets, rules, treaties, territorial
requests, crises and investigations. It shows actual queues and missed duties.
More committee work or another elected tier has startup and transition costs here.

Housing and daily mobility have separate but linked capital and operating lines:
land servicing, construction and public guarantees; buildings, repair, insurance
and renewal; routes, vehicles, depots, staff, accessible stops and maintenance;
rents, fares, targeted support and national/local transfers. Private investment
risk is not a public appropriation; a public loan or guarantee needs a capped
loss account. Low rent or fare funded by deterioration is an unpaid future cost.
The same serviced site, road space, builder, driver, grid connection or external
currency cannot deliver several housing and transport promises at once. The
Assembly authorizes any new general subsidy or cross-local loss allocation,
while accountable local authorities commission and report actual access.

The time settlement adds actual paid/unpaid hours, irregular or on-call duty,
leave contributions and benefits, employer and small-firm costs, care-service
capacity, replacement staff and training lead times to that account. A worker
on protected leave cannot also be counted on a hospital, school or utility
roster. More predictable time can help people use services and join public
life, but it neither automatically funds leave nor supplies a replacement
clinician. The Assembly must face an explicit shortfall rather than making
unpaid family labor or unlimited overtime the balancing item.

The public-capability portfolio and essential-system account also identify each
water and power asset's renewal, safe operation and repair costs; food stocks'
location, rotation and transport; household tariff relief and nutrition support;
and imported parts, fuel and settlement currency. A low regulated tariff cannot
be financed by invisible pipe or grid deterioration. One worker, water flow,
backup generator, cold store or foreign-currency reserve cannot secure several
promises at once. Public, cooperative and private operators disclose common
control and critical subproviders; financial and physical accounts remain
separate. The Assembly decides lawful subsidies and scarcity rules while
independent inspectors test safety and responsible operators report delivery.

The same workforce account must show regional health teams, teachers, paid care
workers, defenders, investigators, courts, safe custody and community supervision,
including training lead times, travel, supervision and overlap during provider
failure. It must compare prevention, urgent protection, prosecution and custody
rather than funding the visible criminal case while leaving safety services empty.
Digital and non-digital public access, separate oversight, an alternate operator
and tested record transfer also consume funds. Neither a trainee nor a nominal
paper route counts as immediately available replacement capacity.
Public AI capability adds compute, energy, water, imported equipment, skilled
staff, source-data care, translation, independent evaluation, appeal capacity,
training, security and funded manual or alternate-provider operation. Put those
costs beside the nurses, teachers, care workers and maintenance crews the same
budget could fund; claimed automation savings do not count unpaid resident
work, hidden queues or a removed remedy as public gains.

The same account must fund democratic contestability: election administration,
independent investigation and courts, safe public information channels, and lawful
continuity when a critical private or public provider fails. A procurement exit clause
without a staffed, financed alternative is a paper safeguard. These costs cannot be
hidden inside an assumed efficiency gain or taken from essential services without a
visible legislative choice and rights review.

Budget review must connect public accounts to household and firm production,
investment and credit, bank liabilities, debt rollover and trade, while separately
tracking physical resources and ecological limits. An appropriated payment is not
available cash; a public cash balance is not the country's foreign currency reserve.
Treasury reports prospective payment failures, but the Assembly decides lawful
priorities and adjustment packages under rights and existing obligations. No
technical operator or central bank acquires authority to cancel an entitlement.

External borrowing, guarantees, critical imports, transport and payment routes
belong in that account. A treaty or cabinet side agreement cannot create concealed
debt, surrender taxing authority or declare a social guarantee funded when its
foreign-currency and material path is unavailable. Strategic reserves, local
substitution and supplier diversification compete with present services in the
same public budget; no unilateral promise secures a foreign partner's cooperation.
Material provisional treaty application and withdrawal also enter the account
before elected authorization: identify continuing liabilities, claims held by
people outside the country, exit costs, replacement supply and legal duties that
survive a domestic vote. The account separates lawful authority to decide from
cash, currency, skilled staff and physical ability to deliver. It does not assume
that a counterparty will accept new terms or that a national saving offsets harm
abroad.

[Design177 production/source bridge](58/13-public-offer-production-and-source.md) joins public production,
consumption, fiscal spending and one declared service/capital change. Direct
and upstream requirements are not extra invoices or fiscal sources; same-
service TW wage/time cost is not proportionally more real service. Own-account
R&D adjustments need paired capital/revenue treatment, not a cash inference.
Original import/duty/ownership boundaries and unpriced actual sources persist.
