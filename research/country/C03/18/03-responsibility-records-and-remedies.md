# AX11–AX15: accessible opposition, evidence and useful-time correction

*Part of [C03/18](../18-peaceful-assembly-and-public-dissent.md); design 189 candidate.*

**AX11 — Own acts and real mandates.** Actual organizers/participants retain B/P
liability only on proved personal acts/duties, fault, causal contribution and
fair defenses; no strict guarantee of everyone else's behavior or policing costs.
Leadership, attendance, a shared channel or failure to police strangers is not
guilt. Genuine control/contribution still matters. No mandatory steward force,
protester insurance or coerced undertaking to prevent future dissent.

Future agents may help communicate within actual revocable mandates. Account or
agent replication neither multiplies people/mandates nor makes one contacted
person the crowd's representative. Count actual physical load for safety without
turning it into a political authority score. Preserve unfamiliar meanings and
translation loss; a simulation, poll or negotiated route is not consent to a ban.
A civil restriction does not establish criminal guilt or bind absent people by
invented assent; actual court service/scope and individual response still matter.

**AX12 — Record public power, protect private people.** Officials preserve actual
instructions, original grounds, contrary evidence, clocks and force/medical events
in protected source custody under PD/LF, including independent own-abuse access.
Participants, journalists and observers may lawfully record/report public acts
without state accreditation. A recording is not a criminal target; no seizure or
device-unlock power follows. Safe bystander/victim identities and fair defense
retain their particular privacy/notice/retention law. Public occurrence does not
make a permanent public attendance graph lawful.

Masks/anonymity, refusal to use an official app or political association alone
justify no face scan or identity demand. Actual P/PD/IA powers require their own
specific predicate, necessity, retention and independent challenge; AX adds no
mass participant-identification or opinion-risk list. No secret sole archive keys
for the implicated command, no indefinite sensitive testimony publication.

**AX13 — Relief before the opportunity is lost.** Actual participants, prospective
participants on credible facts and affected others may reach ordinary B13/B17
courts directly without organizer consent, prior administrative exhaustion, visa,
paid licence or compulsory AI intake. Usable interpretation/help, protected
identity/representation and accessible remote evidence follow actual stakes and
fair-defense rules. A named official can be required to explain an unlawful
omission, barrier, informal restriction or threatened public suppression.

Hear planned restrictions before the event loses its purpose; accept urgent
requests against on-scene action immediately, without waiting six hours. Interim
orders need actual law/grounds/necessity, not automatic stay or guilt. An AX
assembly restriction or AX16 expression-restraint order made without usable
notice/opportunity to answer requires particular reasons why those
could not safely occur first, an immediate usable challenge and hearing as soon
as needed, at most 24 elapsed hours from the first such order, with earlier
liberty/clinical/expressive
rights prevailing. It lapses at that limit without an actual independent merits
hearing after usable safe notice/opportunity to answer; no serial order reset.
A fairly notified person may decline participation without consenting or vetoing
review; do not invent a representative or substitute a paper invitation for
accessible review. This AX-specific ceiling replaces B17's longer ex-parte
period only for these orders, not ordinary unrelated civil cases. It permits
no suppression of a one-hour event pending a 24-hour hearing. Ordinary appeal must remain capable of useful-time correction.

**AX14 — Correction must reach its recipient.** Courts can annul an unlawful
restriction, order actual restoration/protection/access, preserve/disclose lawful
evidence and award proved B relief with accountable debtor/source/execution.
B18/B19 actual executors, PO outside-command investigation/OJ prosecution and
PD/AA scopes remain distinct. Ministers/implicated officers cannot choose the
only receiver or withhold the sole source record; own-court conflicts use actual
unconflicted assignment/appeal. If an event's opportunity is irreversibly lost,
acknowledge non-delivery and remedy rather than call later damages full restoration.
No invented appropriation, available officer or retrospective forced assembly.

**AX15 — Criticism does not require state truth approval.** Holding opinions,
peaceful political/religious disagreement, satirical/offensive criticism of
rulers/institutions, ordinary factual error and peaceful advocacy of political,
social or constitutional change require
no official licence or ideological balance. A proposal need not match the
current constitutional preference; actual changes still need valid authority. No AX false-news, insult, blasphemy,
notice/disobedience offence or punitive fine is created. Actually applicable
specific unlawful threats, exploitation, privacy harms, violence/assistance or
other defined wrongs retain their own elements, source law and defenses; an
injunction/risk warning is not criminal proof. State offices cannot use funding,
public access or an AI's confidence in a narrative to suppress lawful dissent.
AX16 narrows prior restraint without choosing editorial or historical truth.

[APS01–APS07](../../C08/13/08-public-participation-and-abusive-civil-claims.md)
adds early civil review where the litigation burden threatens lawful
participation. Keep the stronger AX restraint, privacy and actual
useful-time safeguards on their own authority.
