# RT06–RT10: a predictable review and a bounded improvement exception

*Part of [private rent/entry](../18-private-rents-tenure-and-housing-entry.md).*

**RT06 — One fixed annual review window.** Review on the anniversary of lawful
possession, at most once per12months; a contract's longer/lower protection stays.
For an increase give at least90calendar days' itemized notice before that date,
with old lawful base, index values/months, factor, permitted allowance and new
amount. Late/missing upward notice forfeits that year's ordinary increase;
it does not change the anniversary or create catch-up. No tenant application
is required to enforce a due lower ceiling. Use accessible non-AI receipt and
B/AP/H help; tenant silence is no assent to new charges or waiver.

**RT07 — Specify the signed index.** Use EV's independently published general
national all-items consumer-price index, not local asking rents, dwelling value,
a landlord-selected series or a personal surveillance score. For review monthM,
use the index for calendar monthM−6 divided by the index forM−18. Fix those
reference months at the annual anniversary; use the first officially published
values for them. The six-month lag leaves time for publication and90-day notice;
it is a proposed timing choice, not an empirically optimal lag. Apply the same
series/basis; an official neutral rebasing needs a published conversion.

The ceiling follows the signed factor: if lawful base1000 and the factor0.95,
next base must be at most950, even without an upward review request. If the
factor1.04, the maximum is1040, but RT05 still requires a lawful review source/
notice for a rise. Round the final ceiling down to the currency's smallest
lawful payment unit; actual agreed rent can be lower. Do not treat inflation
as a mandatory rent award or use a floor that silently eliminates deflation.

**RT08 — Missing or corrected data create no private policy power.** If a
required official value/valid conversion is unavailable, no ordinary upward
change becomes payable that year until a later lawful annual review; no rent
backlog or substitute asking-rent index is created. Retain the last otherwise
lawful base and actual separate allowances pending usable values. Correct a
proved downward ceiling from its scheduled date once the required values become
available, with tenant credit/refund and ordinary fair proof; actual shortage
and RT11 relief remain separate. Statistical revisions alone do not reopen a
completed calculation using its prescribed first-published values; proved
fraud/arithmetic/series error and neutral rebasing retain correction/remedy.
EV cannot choose an economic rent policy by altering a particular case's index.

**RT09 — An improvement is not neglected maintenance.** Ordinary repair,
previously due safety/access work, renewal already financed in rent/reserves,
cosmetics, acquisition/refinancing and land-price gain do not justify a rent
rise beyond the ordinary cap. A genuinely additional durable improvement or
new statutory upgrade beyond the previous duty can be proposed with actual
scope, works/receiving, net costs and alternative funding. Tenant-specific optional enhancements and their proposed payment need prior
informed tenant agreement. Lawful common-building works under CB and new mandatory
statutory upgrades keep their own decision/access protections; RT grants no
individual veto over others' lawful works and no automatic capital pass-through.
Any beyond-cap common-work allowance still needs RT10 proof of actual material
safe/useful service to occupants, not only owner asset gain or an optional
luxury. Agreement to works is distinct from allowance; consent cannot waive RT.
H04 urgent repair/access/receiving continues while price is disputed.

**RT10 — Exceptional improvement charges need actual independent permission.**
Before charging beyond the cap, the landlord applies to the existing ordinary
housing/civil court with notice, tenant response and proportionate qualified
evidence. This additional step is limited to the exception, not each lease or
routine index change; the owner cannot approve its own charge. Qualifying
monthly allowance cannot exceed `Knet/(12L) + Fqual/12`: actual reasonable net
new capitalK after received grants/other matched funding, over verified accepted
service lifeL, plus actual reasonable annual finance cost allocated only to
that component, only while useful service and actual unrecovered qualifying
capital remain. Acquisition-wide or refinanced owner debt is not such a cost.
No simultaneous principal/depreciation double recovery, imputed
land/equity yield or allowance for a grant-funded share. Missing life/cost/source
proof supplies no presumed allowance; ordinary due repair cannot await it.

The order fixes amount/schedule, useful start after received works, funding
credits, review and end. Recover no more capital thanKnet; stop the capital
part when recovered/life ends and the finance part at the earliest of proved
cost ending, qualifying capital recovered or useful component service ending.
Keep each component outside the ordinary indexed base, reconcile later grants/
refunds and show residual owner obligations. Actual I03 eligibility and landlord
finance/public subsidy remain separate decisions; the court creates neither
appropriation nor a public guarantee. RT11/15 governs real failure/affordability.

On genuine full vacancy/new RT02 letting the old separate allowance ends; the
new agreed base may reflect the home's condition but cannot also carry the old
component order as another charge. H05 succession/protected co-tenant continuity
keeps only the actual remaining lawful schedule and recovery/funding records.
