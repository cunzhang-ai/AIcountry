# One false instruction and several useful voting deadlines

*Design299 · Constructed comparison of correction and execution.*

A message falsely claims that lawful voting access has closed.
It is reported at 17:00. The ordinary national account has been
compromised, but the current operative schedule is independently
verified at local electoral offices. Two people have already lost
the only practical route available to them before the report.
Seven more still have an opportunity to use an available lawful
route if they receive a correction in time.

Four of these seven depend on a telephone channel and need the
verified message by 18:00. The other three can receive accessible
local information by 19:00. Polling closes at 20:00. The earlier
times account for practical journey and access in the example.
They are not new polling deadlines. The groups, prices, clocks
and receipts below are assumptions.

| Choice | Cost units | Timely corrective receipt among seven |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Online publication and ordinary account recovery | 1 | 0 under the declared loss and access conditions |
| Independent public and local correction | 2 | 3 through a staffed local route |
| Local correction plus a qualified separate telephone relay | 5 | 7 if both routes deliver before their useful times |

The telephone route uses a distinct authorized sender and
staffed delivery through an existing lawful arrangement in the
comparison. If voluntary or preauthorized delivery cannot work,
an actual court order could require the same narrowly specified
notice only under the proposed competent law and evidentiary
conditions. A private channel or voter list does not become
available because a court identifies the harm. The assumed full
route reaches the four at 17:45 and the other three at 18:30.
At three units the lawful local route reaches three and leaves
four exposed. If the telephone route shares the compromised
credential or arrives at 18:15, it still supplies no timely
receipt for those four.

Keep the two already lost opportunities in every comparison.
An original deadline or disallowed official record must be
reviewed under the applicable law. The counts describe timely
receipt of a correction, not votes cast, preferences, outcome
change or proof that all people understood the notice. A
publisher's failure or a correction order does not settle the
validity of the contest. Protect any remaining lawful path.
