# Design239 decision record. Climate budgets and essential continuity

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned Design238](baseline.json)

## Decision

Select [CBG01–CBG12](../country/C09/27-climate-budgets-and-essential-continuity.md).
C09/08 already calls for an enacted emissions budget and funded
transition. EG, PS and JC protect actual delivery and C13/11
exposes a grid delay. The added choice sets period and cumulative
headroom, separate gross and net accounts and an actual elected
correction tied to a dated service risk. Use the
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

The global relationship is physical. A national allocation, gross
cap, non-banking rule, period and 30/60-day duties are design
choices. Real authorised emission limits and an actual national
fair share are still open. Give the effect of separately lawful
emergency operation its full place in the emissions account.

## Real alternatives

| Alternative | Strong reason | Material exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Rely on annual targets, sector standards and the ordinary budget | Simple, adjustable and compatible with real operator expertise. | A later headline can hide accumulated loss. Different operators can each assume the same unused space. |
| Use multiyear net budgets with limited carry and verified removals | Offers flexibility for difficult sectors and weather or construction shocks. Rewarding durable real removal can make sense. | Accounting scope, reversals and offset dependence can hide gross delay. Banking can defer otherwise necessary change. A carefully qualified net regime remains a rival. |
| Gross period and cumulative ceilings with separate removal and service accounts | Exposes actual emissions, finite headroom and what a timely alternative can do. | Less flexibility and potentially higher transition cost. If lawful near-term supply is absent, essential duties and environmental risk must be addressed in public. |
| Set a rigid automatic operating stop at exhaustion | Makes a physical limit visible and limits pressure for delay. | A mechanical stop can interrupt urgent protection without another source or lawful allocation. It cannot replace sector authority, funded prevention or public correction. |

Select the third rule. Keep real removal and non-CO2 measures
visible and contestable. Preserve actual applicable floors and
narrow separately lawful urgent action. A stronger net framework
with an independently verified gross safeguard and complete
service and equity package could displace this preference. The
[delayed-grid comparison](../settlement/climate-budget-continuity/02-two-budgets-and-a-grid-delay.md)
tests money, accepted service and physical headroom independently.

## Authority and migration

Assembly sets the law and budget. Existing environmental,
inventory and sector authorities keep their own roles. Local
bodies retain elected siting and service powers. Existing
courts and scrutiny bodies review scope, source and execution
without deciding the power mix. Give the opposition and affected
people original evidence and practical challenge. No scientific
body, controller or AI receives a substantive legislative vote.

Keep EG/PS/OC/JC, AQ, water and habitat authority, actual
treaty obligations, ordinary rights and existing authorizations.
Any future special operation requires its own express prior
legal ground. Preserve workers, contracts, cleanup, affected
claims, original vintages and paid FT/OP/T successors. A later
Act or account cannot remove emitted tonnes. No actual budget,
permission, source, retirement or existing law changes here.
