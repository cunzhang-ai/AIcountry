Accessed 3 November 2026. This is a fictional-country research comparison, not
advice about a current jurisdiction's taxes or a market/economic forecast.

[OECD, Taxing Wages 2026, Methodology and limitations](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/2026/04/taxing-wages-2026_d1f39986/full-report/methodology-and-limitations_f25b8cbc.html),
sections “The calculation of marginal tax rates” and “Limitations,” was opened
and read. It supports examining small earnings changes with taxes/contributions/
benefits together and warns that narrow formal-incidence cases cannot establish
overall welfare or final economic incidence. It supplies no AIcountry rates,
recipe, labor elasticity, fiscal yield or comparative superiority. The present
individual/unsafe-household commitments are AIcountry's choices, not OECD's
limited illustrative family types adopted as a social ideal.
