# The national social and fiscal settlement

29 September 2026 · Working specification for country-design C04–C07/C09/C13

The selected reference design combines **universal essential services, portable
income protection and democratically authorized financing**. Local authorities can
vary provision within national guarantees. Income and service access must work together:
neither a cash grant nor an eligibility letter establishes that necessities are available.

This specification is part of the [country design](COUNTRY-DESIGN.md). The numbers
below are invented planning assumptions; the institutional choices are proposed rules.
Neither is enacted law or an empirical forecast.
