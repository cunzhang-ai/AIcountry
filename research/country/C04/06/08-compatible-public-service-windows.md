# Make overlapping public service obligations usable

These candidate rules implement effective access for designated ordinary
health, care, education and benefits interfaces. The adopting law lists
covered duties, service owners and funded receiving routes. Professional
judgments, individual consent and special statutory timetables retain
their own authority.

**PSW01 — Remove avoidable attendance before coordinating it.** For a
covered compulsory contact, the service owner identifies the legal
purpose and offers the least burdensome usable means permitted by
source law. Compare lawful reuse of already verified facts, a written
or assisted reply, a home or local option and attendance. Keep a
separate hearing, inspection or in-person assessment where it serves
a distinct necessary purpose. The person can choose a staffed route
and challenge an unnecessary requirement. No shared appointment system
can waive a required act or substitute for clinical consent.

**PSW02 — Give the first report an owner and a deadline.** A person or
authorized helper can report a material conflict to either covered
service or the local convenor. The first receiver records the original
contact and sends the minimum necessary timing and access information
to the named scheduling officers, with the person's permission where
required. Keep the matter until the convenor acknowledges receipt or
a competent service repairs it. Treat the earliest useful decision time,
including travel, preparation and a protected care handoff, as the
deadline for arranging a response. Act immediately where an ordinary
urgent duty applies. A person can report a conflict without disclosing
their employer's full schedule, diagnosis or other appointments.

**PSW03 — Offer one feasible sequence with separate acceptances.** Each
authorized provider states the exact window, duration, location or
channel, accessibility and required support, plus any last safe time
and confirmation limit. Check elapsed travel, waiting, known uncertainty
and support with the person. Budget each necessary worker, vehicle,
interpreter and room once. Retain the original slot until the proposed
replacement and its dependencies are accepted within their own mandates,
or the person chooses to release it. Each service can use a short,
explicitly expiring reservation from its authorized capacity. Include
reserved time in the queue and release unusable holds promptly. An
unanswered offer, partial reservation or AI suggestion does not bind a
person or a provider. Expiry releases the hold and preserves the claim
and the evidence of delay.

**PSW04 — Keep a scheduling failure from becoming a loss of rights.**
Before an adverse attendance finding after a reported material conflict,
the service proposing it must establish usable notice, a feasible
option within the person's relevant circumstances, and a fair chance
to respond. Record an absent or unusable offer as an unresolved access
failure. Keep original filing dates, existing lawful payments and
entitlements under their own law. An inability to attend does not by
itself establish refusal, ineligibility or a new award. Continue
necessary care and other severable duties. A reviewer may correct
attendance records and order timely reconsideration, an available
alternate or other authorized relief. A revised slot cannot silently
move a source-law deadline.

**PSW05 — Fund the resolution and expose shared shortages.** Elected
local authorities assign and fund convening through the existing access
or assistance service. Each statutory service owner pays for its own
participation, accepted delivery and legally assigned support. An
adopting schedule names the national sector fallback and the bounded
budget for exceptional support. Apply existing interim-payer rules
where responsibility itself is disputed. For unavailable capacity,
preserve existing commitments and priority rules, report which duty
and date are affected, and give the person a direct outside review
route. An unfunded duty needs an elected source or lawful correction.
A convenience budget cannot displace a previously committed resource
or create paid leave authority over an employer.

**PSW06 — Measure time returned and rights delivered.** Record actual
receipt, time spent travelling, waiting and repeating contacts, missed
duties, paid capacity and unresolved conflicts with safe aggregate
disclosure. Keep the denominator of people who reported a conflict
including those without a final booking. Compare ordinary scheduling
repair and removal of unnecessary appearances with a joined service
window. Route recurring conflict patterns to the elected authority for
changes to hours, locations or evidentiary requirements. Give reviewers
safe access to original events and authority to trace correction.
Humans and AI can propose or translate sequences using scoped, revocable
mandates. Acceptances, rights and qualified judgments stay with the
lawful actors. Keep a direct human route and avoid a common personal
calendar. Reopen when reservations displace timely care, residents
cannot use the reporting route or a simpler arrangement does better.
