# Belonging: a child's birth and legal connection

*Canonical MB01–MB03; part of [C02/06](../06-citizenship-and-secure-belonging.md).*

**MB01 — Distinct rights and statuses.** Legal personhood, civil identity,
nationality, admission, residence permission, habitual residence, political
eligibility and actual service receipt are separate findings. Universal rights,
child protection, I01 ordinary-resident needs and urgent protection do not wait
for citizenship. Citizenship includes an unconditional right to enter/return,
remain, obtain usable nationality proof and seek lawful consular assistance;
no foreign state's admission, document recognition or rescue capacity is promised.
The adult vote and candidacy follow [C02/03](../03-political-membership.md):
citizen from eighteen, or noncitizen from eighteen after one continuous
year of actual habitual residence and a current habitual home. From eighteen, local eligibility requires
six cumulative months of national habitual residence and actual current municipal residence;
a genuine move then changes constituency without another six-month wait.
One person has one vote in each domestic contest; another state's lawful vote
is not automatically fraud. No account or agent copy adds a person/vote.
These nationality routes concern humans; a future nonhuman personhood claim needs
express lawful public consideration, not automatic rejection of its meaning or
recognition by a platform. Existing software/constitutional holds remain unchanged.

**MB02 — Territorial birth and foundlings.** Every human child born alive in the
country's lawful territory acquires citizenship at birth, without a parental
status, marriage, wealth, detention, method-of-birth or registration condition.
There is no diplomat-child exception in this national reference; dual nationality
is permitted and parents' immunity/admission remain separate. A birth on a ship
actually flying this country's lawful flag, or an aircraft actually registered
here at birth, has the same national acquisition rule. This does not change
foreign territory, port authority or another state's nationality law.

A child found in the territory with unknown parentage/place is presumed locally
born and a citizen. Investigate origins safely; later foreign origin/parentage
discovery does not strip acquired or officially recognized citizenship under MB07.
Registration declares the entitlement and preserves clinical/source facts; a
missing entry cannot make a born child nonexistent or suspend care.

**MB03 — Overseas descent and future birth circumstances.** At least one legal
parent who was a citizen at birth transmits citizenship if that parent had at
least twelve cumulative months of actual habitual residence here before the
birth; childhood residence counts. A deceased lawful parent qualifies by
citizenship at death and that residence history; RP's actual parentage/use rules,
not estate deadlines or genetics alone, determine the connection. A legal
citizen parent also transmits where
the child would otherwise be stateless, without that residence condition.
Do not assume foreign citizenship from a safe opportunity to apply, an unsafe
consular step, a discretionary promise, genetics or an intended-support contract.
Assess actual foreign law and its operation with shared proof under MB09.

B28/RP07 determine legal parentage, including more than two lawful parents;
a valid nonparent donor's nationality alone is irrelevant. Later proof of a
parent existing at birth recognizes qualifying descent from birth; adoption or
a new parentage event uses MB06 instead. Citizenship already acquired survives
later parentage correction, adoption or loss/change of a parent's nationality.

For remotely operated artificial gestation, use the physical child's actual
birth location, not server, account or operator location. If a future case
cannot be classified, preserve original facts, identity, immediate protection
and direct determination rather than discard the child or unsupported meaning.
No human-birth rule recognizes a copied AI persona as a human birth or removes
RP future-method safety/authorization holds; the child's rights do not license
an unlawful method or manufacture past consent or foreign recognition.
