# Scoped finding. A fiscal balance must survive a delivery check

Design198 adds FB09–FB12 to the existing budget and durable-source rules.
It qualifies a recurring release only after its realized payment scope,
public costs, continuing duties, affected access and lawful disposition
are clear. Retain/repair is preferred first in the bounded Copenhagen
comparison. [Canonical addition](../../country/C06/62/01-underspending-and-released-capacity.md)

Official records distinguish the 2025 variance, October 2026 forecast,
staffing plans and a separate extended-hours earmark. The local committee
asked for better demand evidence before proceeding with a new
recommendation. This supports a distinction between low uptake and low
need and supplies no estimate of actual suppressed demand.
[Evidence](../../settlement/released-capacity/01-copenhagen-service-and-budget.md)
