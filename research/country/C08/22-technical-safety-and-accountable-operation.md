# C08. Technical safety, open entry and accountable operation

15 October 2026 · Design 78 · TS01–TS16/TD01–TD12 research, not enacted law

Select enforceable sector duties with graded entry: ordinary low-risk activity
remains free; defined consequential activities need evidenced competence,
safe operation and independent challenge. Protect people through design,
maintenance and usable alternatives before blaming the last worker or user.
No universal technology license, private standard monopoly or AI risk sovereign.

The common statute and domain predicates are now proposed explicitly. Missing
site/chemical/engineering annexes, actual jurisdiction/treaties and qualified
clinical/technical evidence remain real limits, not universally safe defaults.
No threshold inferred from a foreign document, model or absent annex can
support a penalty. No all-sector operational completeness is claimed.
Next audit the whole national design and address its most consequential
incompatibility; do not automatically start another narrow technical study.

**Clause-family scope.** These TS clauses concern **C08 technical safety**.
C04's territorial-status TS clauses have a different canonical source and meaning;
chapter/path-qualified reference prevents accidental review or power transfer.
No historical code, current permission or substantive technical duty is renamed.
