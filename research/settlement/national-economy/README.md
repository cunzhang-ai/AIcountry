14 October 2026 · Integrated design 59 · Authored planned accounts, not an operating country

The [canonical national package](../../country/C06/20-national-economic-package.md)
joins production, distribution, finance, owned returns, external payments
and physical limits. It retains practical democracy and person-level
protection while holding all tested packages from a readiness claim.

flows: an unpaid obligation is not a received benefit, wage, export or
lendable saving. Larger GDP, public ownership and a tax coefficient are
assumptions, not a source of authority or proven productive capacity.

No case has a jointly verified attainable, funded, physically supplied
and democratically controlled transition. The [protected-stage package](../transition/README.md)
now specifies pre-growth finance, paid overlap, acceptance, failure and
handover; retain/repair and different ownership alternatives remain live. Do not choose
one green annual metric as a substitute for human lives or public power.
