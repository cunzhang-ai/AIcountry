# Work law must constrain arbitrary power and permit real change

2 November 2026 · Design117 · Country-design candidate, not enacted labor law

[WL01–WL40](../../country/C05/12-work-contracts-and-collective-power.md) adds the
ordinary employment settlement missing behind TW's time/pay/leave principles.
It selects real work status, stable terms, valid-reason exit from entry,
independent collective power, limited workplace consent, protected enforcement
and bounded public wage advances. No claim that the whole country is funded.

## The concrete settlement

| Question | Proposed answer and retained boundary |
|---|---|
| Who is an employee? | Actual paid personal work/control/dependence raises a rebuttable presumption. Genuine independent enterprise can rebut; a platform/account/contract label cannot decide. A care recipient directing support is not automatically employer. |
| Can terms change? | Actual agreement or a competent collective mechanism, no deemed consent from hunger/silence/agent copies or dismissal solely to rehire on worse terms. Stable ordinary hours/indefinite employment are defaults; real temporary/seasonal purpose and worker-chosen intermittency remain possible. |
| Can a job end? | Yes, for proved conduct/capacity or real operational grounds with fair process from entry, actual notice/accrued rights and independent remedy. No permanent post or guaranteed profit is invented. |
| Can people negotiate together? | Independent plural unions, fair majority recognition without a turnout veto, real information/bargaining and members-only voice if no majority. Employer/minister cannot appoint worker representatives. |
| Is consultation enough? | Elected councils from20 workers also have scoped consent over new general rating/discipline, intrusive monitoring and ordinary roster rules. No waiver of individual/privacy/work floors. Deadlock can delay useful change; broader board co-determination/worker ownership remain distinct rivals. |
| Can workers strike? | Peaceful labor/economic-social action has a bounded mandate/notice path and no government license; imminent safety refusal does not wait. Narrow court-reviewed minimum provision needs actual willing/precommitted lawful people, pay and safe means, not a whole-sector ban or conscription. |
| What about unpaid wages? | Employer/host duties remain; a new limited Treasury advance can protect part of actual unpaid ordinary wage/leave/notice. It does not cover every redundancy/damages claim or cap higher AS/state obligations. |

The authored ordinary notice floors are14/28/56/84 calendar days at below1,
1–below4,4–below10 and10+ completed service years. Redundancy from one completed
year adds two weekly pays per service year, proportionately including a fraction,
up to26 under this new minimum. Stronger existing terms survive. These are value
choices, not research-proven optima or an extension of AS's28-day floor to everyone.
A paid working notice period is reconciled once, not also booked as extra pay.
Actual leap/anniversary dates matter; a365-day shortcut can create a false year.

Collective reduction consults at least30 days,45 for100 proposed in90 days,
triggered by10 in30 days or at least max(10,ceil10% of original workforce) in90.
Reason, selection, actual alternative jobs, worker/creditor/source and service
receiving remain necessary. Consultation is not a promise that no one loses work.

## Collective control has exact scope and actual people

Council seats are1/3/5/7 at20/50/200/1000 workers. Highest averages1,3,5… with
actual candidate capacity, person preference and a prior neutral tie lot gives
minority access; an individual list cannot win more seats than candidates. More
than half the full statutory seats must affirm, even during vacancies. Union
agreements need a valid worker mandate and secret ratification. Neither a council,
union, arbitrator nor AI makes sovereign law or waives a person's nonwaivable
rights. Paid representative work/actual cover costs employers and cannot disappear
into spare time or be counted twice with the same care/production hours.

Ordinary protected action needs a live majority-valid-vote mandate within six
months and seven days' notice, with genuine urgent safety exceptions. Ministerial
labels and economic loss alone do not establish an essential minimum. B17 process,
actual clinical/task evidence and fresh bounded court review remain; the court
cannot produce staff, compel intimate work or claim a missing service delivered.
Ready workers locked out for bargaining pressure retain guaranteed-work pay;
actually unworked strike hours need not be paid absent a better rule. This
asymmetric risk choice may raise closure/cost and has a real bilateral-lockout
rival. I05 actual needs/resources remain, with no new strike-pay guarantee or
political eligibility test. Actual coercive police/defense command retains its
separate valid law and independent voice/review, not a ban on all public workers.

## Public wage protection has a finite claim and a real cost

After due ordinary wages/paid leave/notice remain unpaid seven days, WL34 proposes
an undisputed eligible advance within seven days of usable request, earlier I/E
hardship rights intact. Formal insolvency is unnecessary. Ordinary current cap
is min(eight representative weekly pays,16×enacted normal-week hours×hourly
minimum), less actual general advances in the prior365 elapsed days. Preserve
higher established same-claim caps and unpaid awards; processing delay or a TW
stage change cannot cut them or deduct earlier reconciled receipts twice.
Employer debt, AS12's specific full backing and actual state-employer dues remain.

In one invented CU fixture, weekly pay960 and minimum18 at32 hours give cap7,680.
Actual recent general receipts2,500 leave5,180; a5,000 receipt exactly365 days
old and an unreceived4,000 instruction do not reduce this window. Expected5,000
recovery finances nothing. Only a subsequent actual matched2,000 lowers this
advance's outlay to3,180; the remaining employer wage claim is6,820, not canceled.
High-pay eight-week16,000 instead meets the9,216 minimum-linked cap. Original
pay/rate/status and source are hypothetical, not legal/market calibration.

Treasury pays gross, with original-source employee withholding inside it and
actual remittance, not another tax added to gross. Original TA/Y treatment and
I07's actual usable receipt month apply; net labor advance enters I05 E, not its
own I05 award as a resource. Employer repayment to Treasury is not second worker
income. Uncovered employer contributions/severance/damages retain actual defendants.
Public recovery is prospective ordinary unsecured after remaining priority worker
claims; it creates no assets, old security override or automatic innocent-worker
debt. New gross advances, credit losses, independent work offices/aid/ballots/
courts and early cash need express general-revenue appropriation and source.
Fines, assumed recoveries, restricted funds and free AI do not pay them.

## Keep authority, evidence and readiness distinct

The Work Standards Director and Work Relations Registrar are separately selected
protected C04 careers with own case officers, finite deputies, independent courts
and no named-case ministerial direction. Existing VD03/V punishment limits remain;
there is no new VD14 or inspector's final penal court. B16 office-review dates and
constitutional appointment/removal stay separate from ordinary work claims.
Coordinated law would start new WL duties180 days after competent enactment,
without restarting TW/AS, canceling accrued claims or a readiness election delay.
