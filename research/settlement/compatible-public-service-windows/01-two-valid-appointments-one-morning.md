# Two valid appointments and one morning

Constructed comparison. The resident has an essential clinic visit from
09:00 to 09:25. The clinic has no other suitable time that morning.
An ordinary benefits authority offers an in-person evidence appointment
from 09:30 to 09:45 at another site. Qualified travel and entry take
25 minutes after leaving the clinic. The resident has arranged care
cover until 10:45 at the benefits site. A decision is due at 11:30.
The competent officer needs a further 30 minutes after the evidence
appointment to check the record and issue the decision.
An unresolved fact must be discussed with an authorized caseworker.
Assume written reply and a remote hearing cannot lawfully and practically
resolve this particular fact by 11:30. Retain those routes where they work.

| Choice on the same facts | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Keep both offers and apply the ordinary absence flag | The resident can reach the second site at 09:50. At least one offer is unusable. A lack of attendance is insufficient for an adverse finding. |
| Transfer scheduling and merits to one central office | A common calendar can reveal the clash. Moving the decision would add a transfer of power and would still require a qualified clinic, caseworker and paid opening. |
| Keep the clinical time and confirm a 10:10–10:25 evidence appointment | The resident can reach both appointments and has 20 minutes of cover after the second. The agencies confirm their own parts. Protect the 11:30 decision date. |

The extra caseworker time costs four units and a staffed coordination
contact costs one. Six settled units are available for those purposes.
One unit remains after this change. The ordinary clinic and casework
costs were already committed and are outside this comparison. No
claim of saved public money follows from the absence of a missed visit.
The additional appointment and contact must be funded and accepted.
The evidence conversation is not the merits decision. A separately staffed ordinary decision can finish at 10:55. The
case owner must still issue it by 11:30.

If the only 10:10 slot is already promised to another claimant, the
resident has no usable pair on these facts, even with six cash units.
If the funded amount falls to four, a vacant slot alone does not finance
this arrangement. The receiving body checks for a separately lawful
alternative and retains the original claim and applicable protection.
Any missed decision is reported with its original date and ordinary
review route. The plan earns credit only after actual clinic attendance,
actual evidence receiving and the decision are separately verified.
