# Design390 commitment. Pay for safe water through qualified return

[WS26–WS32](../../country/C09/16/06-small-water-supplies-and-safe-receipt.md)
pairs accountable small suppliers with independent review and a public
fallback. Fund qualified testing, actual transport, accessible delivery
and repeated service while return remains unsafe. Record delayed
recoveries, competing uses and independently verified reopening separately.
