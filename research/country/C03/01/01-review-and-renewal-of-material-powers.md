# PWR01–PWR06. Put material executive powers back before the Assembly

*Part of [Assembly and delegated law](../01-legislature-and-delegation.md). Design340 research selection.*

**PWR01 — Separate exceptional discretion from continuing law.** A national
Act that creates or materially enlarges a delegation to impose compulsory
general restrictions, recurring disclosure or certification, or discretionary
exclusion from a statutory service must identify that delegation separately.
Assess cumulative and practical effect, including instructions implemented
through contractors, platforms or automated systems. Apply this procedure
to the material power to impose the burden. Continue underlying rights,
primary statutory guarantees and routine administration within law. The
authorizing Act identifies the issuer, exact power, commencement, expiry,
affected groups, public purpose, testable outcomes, independent evidence
custodian and funded default on expiry. The reference maximum is four
calendar years from the delegation's first legal effect or the earlier
source-law date. Emergency and other shorter safeguards govern their scopes.
A related rule, a change of provider or a technical amendment retains
the same outer date.

**PWR02 — Open scrutiny on time, without executive permission.** The
Assembly Clerk opens review 180 calendar days before expiry. Publish the
responsible body's account and independent evidence 120 days before expiry.
The existing subject committee holds an accessible hearing by day minus 75.
The Clerk secures a separate public decision on the exact disposition by
day minus 45. Publish fixed dates before commencement. For powers shorter
than 180 days, use an expressly set shorter calendar with meaningful scrutiny
before renewal. Existing emergency expiry and approval routes remain primary
in their scopes. Open an earlier review within 30 calendar days after one
tenth of full Assembly seats request it with specific reasons or the Auditor
reports a substantiated material failure within its mandate. The committee
sets a dated hearing and disposition. An early review leaves the outer
expiry intact. Residents can submit concerns directly and use ordinary
petitions or initiatives. Earlier rights and useful-time remedies continue.

**PWR03 — Examine the record outside the operator's control.** The operator
publishes actual uses, outcomes, denials, effects on people missing from its
records, costs and continuing claims. Use the independent audit, statistics
and parliamentary evidence routes to check originals and common sources.
Give opposition members, people with affected rights, frontline workers and
responsible local authorities practical access to the record and a chance
to present contrary evidence. Fund interpretation, privacy-preserving
participation and review staff. State missing evidence and major rival
explanations. AI can reconcile versions, analyze evidence and identify
missing groups with reproducible sources and qualified correction.
Synthetic testimony, repeated outputs and a predicted benefit cannot stand
for affected participation or establish necessity. A withheld report triggers
the existing evidence and remedy routes and stays visible on the docket.
It neither delays expiry nor establishes that the power is justified.

**PWR04 — Require express primary legislation for renewed power.** A recorded
disposition can let the power end, narrow it, renew it for a new fixed period
or replace it. Give qualified member alternatives usable consideration under
ordinary Assembly procedure. Keep the decision on a separable renewal distinct
from an appropriation or service guarantee. Only an express primary Act
enacted and in effect by expiry continues the delegation. A committee report,
a support vote, a general funding measure or a caretaker instruction confers
no continuation. A late Act can create a prospective power within ordinary
law. It cannot retroactively validate exercises in the gap. If it authorizes
delegated rules, they still require their lawful making, publication and
applicable DR approval. An election or withheld evidence neither moves the
expiry nor transfers powers to the outgoing executive. Publish any missed
agenda duty and use existing process remedies. A court can enforce limits
and procedure within its jurisdiction. The Assembly decides policy.

**PWR05 — Stop using the power and keep serving people.** Before effect,
publish a paid plan for expiry or replacement with the lawful service owner,
source, actual alternate route, pending decisions and review access. At
expiry the operator disables the expired authority in every delivery medium,
withdraws corresponding forms and automated checks and informs staff,
contractors and affected users. Keep valid prior acts and accrued claims
subject to their own law. Stop new adverse steps that depend on the expired
power. Check material downstream uses of a marker, derived
status or verification condition even where the stored fact is accurate.
Distinguish an operative prior act, an unmade or merely queued act,
and independently due service. Use EV25–EV30 to correct the legal
use at each receiver. Preserve lawful history and terminate new
adverse uses that depended on the expired power. Verify receipt
and the next protected batch through independently usable routes.
Maintain independently due payments, assistance, hearings, remedies,
staff obligations and secure records. Where an existing process needs
replacement, use only separately authorized lawful means and report the
real shortfall. A missing successor does not revive either an expired power
or a predecessor it previously displaced.

**PWR06 — Introduce the method prospectively and test for avoidance.** Publish
a ranked inventory of existing material delegations with sources, dates,
actual effects and existing review routes. Assign priority scrutiny to
high-burden powers. The inventory cannot shorten their existing lawful
terms. A fresh material enlargement uses PWR; a disputed classification
is open to existing independent legal review. Pay Clerk, audit, evidence
access, deliberation and service transition from distinct lawful budgets.
When deadlines coincide, expose the review shortfall and assign elected
priority without extending a power by default. Revisit on weak evidence,
disruption of essential services, abuse by the review process or a simpler
route that secures equivalent democratic control. Any future Assembly
changing the procedure does so expressly through competent law.
