# What the proposed retirement formula actually does

*Part of [retirement comparison](README.md); fictional annual pay, not real claims.*

Use reference full-time-equivalent pay `W = 30,000`, a benefit-base ceiling
`2W`, worker contribution 10% of uncapped actual labor earnings, accrual
1.25% and the best 48 price-revalued calendar bases. Partial-period amounts
and ceilings are prorated; a partial calendar year still occupies one base
slot. These redistributive choices remain open to substantive challenge.

| Declared history | Annual earned pension at 65 | What is exposed |
|---|---:|---|
| One full year at 30,000 | 375 | No minimum-career cliff; this is an annual claim, not owned cash |
| Forty years at 30,000 | 15,000 | 1,250 monthly insurance, plus separate basic protection |
| Forty years at 120,000 | 30,000 | Benefit base capped at 60,000; contributions above that are solidarity, not personal units |
| Twenty years at 30,000, twenty eligible full care years | 11,250 | Credit base 15,000 in each otherwise unpaid year; general-revenue credit payments and lifetime cost differ |
| One eligible care year with actual pay 10,000 | 187.50 | Credit adds 5,000 rather than another 15,000; public transfer 500 |
| One paid-leave year at 30,000 with eligibility recorded | 375 | No second care accrual or credit transfer for the same covered pay |
| Fifty years at 30,000 | 18,000 | Only best 48 calendar bases count; later same-pay years do not promise more |
| Forty-eight years at 30,000, then one at 60,000 | 18,375 | Higher later base replaces one lower base, preserving prior amount |
| Six months work at annualized 30,000, then six eligible care months | 281.25 | Work and care are period-matched, not offset against unrelated months |
| Six months at annualized 120,000 only | 375 | The six-month benefit ceiling is 30,000; the full uncapped received 60,000 still bears contribution |

Price revaluation doubles a single-year 375 entitlement to 750 under the
authored price-ratio 2. A later deflation does not reverse nominal accrued
rights. No financial investment return is assumed or inferred.

For annual earned pension 15,000, the maximum survivor pot is 7,500.
Eligible lost support 4,000/8,000 produces shares 2,500/5,000. If total loss
is only 1,500, pay that loss, not the unused maximum; no proven loss gives
zero under this earned-survivor formula, while individual needs and urgent
child protection remain separate. Actual eligibility/receipt is not modeled.

Toy survival certainty through 84 gives early-60/ordinary-65/deferred-70
factors 0.8/1/1.3333 at zero real discount. A separate declining-survival
fixture checks survival to deferred start from the *same* valuation age.
These deliberately finite toys are not human life tables or valid quotes.
Missing real survival or unbounded-tail evidence yields **unknown**, not a
factor 1, denial of the ordinary pension or a forced deferral.

Authored boundaries also reject duplicated/overlapping service records.
They do not validate real care evidence, behavior, pricing, privacy, lawful
adjudication or payment. A credible lifetime valuation needs original data.

[Design89 RF](../../country/C06/36/02-pension-units-cash-and-generations.md)
now replaces only future-service two-W with one-W after statutory notice.
All prior earned units, indexation, survivor and age protections remain.
The old examples above preserve their frozen rule/price scope, not new rights.
