# Can continuing services survive weaker fiscal years?

*Design191 · Supporting comparison for [FB01–FB08](../../country/C06/62-lasting-services-and-fiscal-shocks.md).*

The proposed public offer includes continuing duties and paid work-time
changes. A favorable source year cannot show that those duties survive a
weak year. The earlier [WF06 matched-calendar case](../../country/C06/58/02-time-capacity-and-conditional-frontier.md)
leaves 26.800 billion DKK in the 2023 comparison. Apply that same pay
assumption to the same-year accounts for 2015–2025 before selecting a fiscal
rule. The observations are eleven paired historical contexts. They supply
neither projected frequency nor comparable real-price amounts for pooling.

## A fixed pay comparison with two source paths

For each year use released general-government net lending and compensation
of employees. Multiply compensation by `1308.6 / 1110.6 − 1`. This is the
WF06 matched-calendar 37-to-32 comparison with all public compensation in
scope, unchanged annual pay and output, and no net task gain. It includes
neither a separate weekly time case nor the whole-economy pay case. Subtract
this conditional addition from the observed balance. A second path adds a
hypothetical new net receipt equal in size to 10% of the sum of two disjoint
personal income tax lines, central and municipal. Both observed lines are
already part of the reference's tax system. The calculation adds only the
declared hypothetical increment. It does not derive a rate, statutory base,
net yield or incidence.

Money columns use same-year nominal billion DKK. Zero is a comparison point.
There was one observed year below zero, five after the conditional pay
change, and three after the hypothetical added receipt. The 2015 added
amount to zero is 19.312% of the two observed tax lines in size. This
quantity does not estimate the legal rate, permanent gap or actual funding
needed. The distinct pension-yield tax line ranges from 11.158 to 63.413
billion DKK across the period. Its variation illustrates the need to assess
the durability of a source. It does not classify the tax as one-off or
identify how much of the change in net lending that line caused.

Holding other changes outside this exercise isolates the conditional pay
addition. Actual service costs, productivity, public staffing mix, net
tax behavior, prices, social insurance, private resources, debt and cash
are still open. Neither path is a forecast, a lower bound or a required
annual balance. Nonnegative balances do not show usable cash or staffed
service. No amount is added to the earlier WF reference as a second cost.

## Which fiscal rule belongs in the country?

| Choice | What it offers | Failure to resolve |
|---|---|---|
| Retain or repair the actual service and source system | Avoids unpriced replacement, preserves working capacities and permits targeted repairs | Needs a direct comparison of access, paid time, public control and lost improvements |
| Continuing source plus finite shock finance, selected | Matches the duration of promises to sources and preserves elected action through temporary losses | Durable tax or real reallocation remains to be demonstrated; weaker forecasts could be used to defer worthwhile changes |
| Borrowing-led finite launch | Can bridge timing or acquire productive capacity without requiring immediate full receipts | Needs accepted credit, a finite authorized commitment, recurring debt service and upkeep, and a real exit if expected yield fails |
| Annual balance constraint | Makes a visible numerical check simple | Can amplify temporary losses, force bad timing, or invite off-budget obligations; the matched observations do not warrant it |

The selection requires amendable source and service choices in the same
budget process and a finite ordinary response to shocks. It deliberately
keeps the Assembly free to select a viable source or narrower prospective
offer without withholding existing rights. An unfunded receiving duty is
a failure even when all its forecast columns balance.
