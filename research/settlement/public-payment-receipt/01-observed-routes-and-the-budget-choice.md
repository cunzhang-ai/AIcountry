# A valid award can leave a person without money

## What Copenhagen observed

In December 2022 Copenhagen suspended a resident's established social
assistance after he lost his designated payment account. It denied a
later request for a sleeping bag on the grounds that assistance was
held for him, although he could not use it. The parliamentary
ombudsman found that the authority had to arrange another method,
including cash where suitable, without compelling a third-party account.
The December 2023 public account describes about a year without
payment. [Parliamentary ombudsman's decision](https://www.ombudsmanden.dk/find-viden/udtalelser/2023/2023-40)
· [Dated public statement](https://www.ombudsmanden.dk/find-viden/nyheder/2023/kommune-kunne-ikke-stille-krav-om-nemkonto-til-udbetaling-af-kontanthjaelp)

A municipal February 2024 note records a cash payment collected with a
staff member at a bank. It did not verify an eventual ongoing bank
route or the full historical amount restored. The note estimated around
200 possible cash payments per month if the city introduced cash
payments, based on other municipalities. That was a planning estimate.
[Municipal note, 20 February 2024, pp. 2–3](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-public-payment-and-independent-receipt/original-sources/copenhagen-february-2024.pdf)

The October 2024 municipal adviser examined notices and the revised
internal workflow. Guidance still prioritized opening an account or
using someone else's account and could delay or omit information about
cash. The administration reported **12 people received written
guidance** from 1 November 2023 to 13 May 2024. That count does not
measure the number needing, denied or receiving cash. The adviser also
recounted a separate reported case where payment continued to a former
partner after the recipient said it should stop. The letter does not
independently establish all facts of that case or the sum lost.
[Municipal adviser, 2 October 2024, pp. 7–9](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-public-payment-and-independent-receipt/original-sources/copenhagen-october-2024.pdf)

These observations favor an early, protected direct choice and actual
receipt audit. They also support keeping the ability to revoke a
representative in the paying office, where it can affect release.
Ordinary account access reform would help many recipients. It leaves
people with no timely or safe account exposed unless the benefit
payer has an accepted alternate. A strong municipal repair that pays
on time could meet the proposed outcome.

## Same duties, different institutional choices

| Arrangement | Strength | What needs qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Account-first with case-by-case municipal rescue | Uses an existing skilled payer and bank network with low added ordinary burden | A manager or bank may control access and time. Verify early choice, direct safe cash and independent review before relying on it. |
| National cash-out network through shops or post offices | The UK offers cash collection for qualifying people without accounts, with wide physical reach and no need to build a counter in every locality. | Requires an accepted document and a stocked point. Verify reach for people without documents or mobility and trace common redemption, cash and supplier failure. |
| Statutory direct receipt with plural points and funded national fallback | Preserves useful everyday accounts and places the residual duty on a named public payer. The person can use an accessible no-account channel and direct review. | Costs paid staff, secure cash and a separate backup. Test actual authority and delivery at both ends. |
| Public transaction account as the ordinary universal offer | Could reduce commercial bank gatekeeping and support normal bills. | Adds a public custodian with privacy, seizure and common-root risk. It still needs no-account receiving and independent correction. |

The UK DWP August 2026 award notice for a future cash-out service
listed **£12,997,124 excluding VAT** and an estimated March 2027 to
February 2030 contract period. The published status was pending.
Treat this as a quoted public procurement benchmark, not spent
money, the amount of benefits, unit cost or proof of delivery.
The public service guidance allows cash collection subject to identity,
voucher and cash availability. The existing guide's 90-day collection
limit leads to a reissue request if missed. The proposed country keeps
the underlying claim live and pays earlier when the existing useful
deadline requires it. [DWP payment exception guide](https://www.gov.uk/payment-exception-service)
· [Official UK6 notice, OCID ocds-h6vhtk-056964](https://www.sell2wales.gov.wales/search/show/search_view.aspx?ID=AUG650432&catID=)

## First-budget decision

Retain ordinary cash awards and useful account settlement. Protect
an individually chosen no-account route from the first payment.
Buy existing staffed reach where it passes a practical check. Budget
a separately controlled receiving route for cases that the same
network or treasury access failure would otherwise exclude. Compare
procurement and direct public operation on the same needed coverage,
pay dates and authority. Keep the household principal, necessary float,
channel costs and urgent advances separately sourced. Never net the
protection against unpaid awards. No historical Copenhagen estimate or
UK price is imported into the 2025 whole-country reference.

Use one actual benefit due run as the payment line in the
[joint first-budget case](../first-budget-common-failure/README.md).
Require redacted evidence of authorization, funds at each date, staff,
stopped old instructions, lawful receiving and independent review.
Match that window to local essential service, executable court relief
and election access. If a route has no qualified price, working date
or receiver, preserve the duty and show the exposure explicitly.
