# Put the democratic change on the predecessor's lawful calendar

*Design547 · Application of the [transition rules](../02-transition-and-commencement.md) to one constitutional reference.*

**Keep the democratic destination and split its legal starts.** For a
lawful retrofit, preserve the predecessor's competent electoral and
appointment authorities until a valid replacement takes effect. Fund
ordinary access, review and service repair on their own applicable authority.
Use ordinary law for a compatible measure and the protected constitutional
route for one that displaces an operative constitutional rule. Review the
exact text and any genuinely disputed interpretation in the predecessor's
competent process. A research classification supplies no ruling.

**Protect the referendum and the intervening election independently.**
Set the prior electorate, rules, funds, original record control and
available timely remedy before reliance. Only lawful electors under the
prior regime decide a proposed enlargement of that electorate. Give
affected people without a vote usable scrutiny, testimony and access to
applicable legal challenge. Record actual territorial effects, including
separate representation and any consent required by applicable law.
Research support, political agreement and an individual consultation convey
different authority.

**Make compatible protections useful even when a constitutional change
fails.** Keep lawfully adopted, independently useful repair on its own
scope. An optional power that depends on the change stays unstarted.
Preserve binding duties, ordinary opposition and lawful succession.
Apply the original review and commencement rules to a late, altered or
rejected instrument. The prior process cannot borrow a future court or
electorate to validate itself.

For Denmark, the selected national resident franchise and final election
adjudication cross explicit constitutional lines. Separate them from an
ordinary package of effective opposition, transparent handover and actual
service receipt under unchanged legal competence. Keep current ministerial
appointment and confidence rules until any binding replacement takes
effect. Put the exact next choice against that stronger ordinary package.
