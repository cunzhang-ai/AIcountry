## Alternatives and finite disposition

1. **Retain135:** keep A operation and a presumed eventual U destination. This
   preserves an autonomy priority, but does not resolve the country material
   choice; reject the presumption, not the underlying independence objection.
2. **Select service-first individual security:** retain I05/SD05, universal
   essential services, actual-person resources, no ordinary work test, earned
   rights, paid assistance/access and independent remedies. Select this preferred
   continuing architecture under explicit material-priority values.
3. **Rights-preserving U+C2:** retain same services, extra needs, old net rights,
   pensions/credits, cash alternatives and protected transition; identify all
   additional sources and losers. Retain as a substantive rival, not a doomed
   or deliberately underfunded comparison. It can displace the selection.
4. **Direct-public/common-provision reorganization:** compare against OP's
   plural public backbone for actual duty/quality/control/title/source/worker
   scope. Retain genuine provider rivals; reject selection by margin arithmetic.

The research preference follows a value judgment: organize around actually met
needs and agency, without predetermining their transfer form. The calculations
support rejecting shortcuts; they do not establish targeting's optimality,
affordability or superiority. Repeated assessment, privacy loss, nonclaim and
dependence are unresolved objections. Universal cash can improve independence
without raising the poorest person's amount. Added U expense can also compete
with services and remedies. No public has chosen between these complete bargains.
