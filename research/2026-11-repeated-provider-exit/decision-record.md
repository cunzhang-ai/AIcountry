# Design489 decision record

**Scope and baseline.** Compare the Design488 [baseline](baseline.json)
under the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md). Keep the same protected
visits and original rights across temporary cover, independent mutual
aid and a bounded ordinary service. This is a constructed comparison.

**Selection and objections.** Prefer independently qualified reserve
plus voluntary joint capacity where it protects both places on the
next call. If sending places repeatedly share the same shortfall,
prepare an ordinary specialist service with an exact elected scope
and separately funded startup and recurring costs. Preserve other
qualified routes and an enforceable choice process. A public provider
can also reproduce incumbent control or displace a scarce independent
worker. A private or cooperative team may depend on the same broker.
Actual accepted rosters and separate review determine availability.

**Authority and continuity.** The statutory owner stays answerable.
Keep common and distinct causes explicit. Same unresolved failure
does not restart exceptional clocks. A new ordinary mandate needs
its own elected vote. Preserve original rights and each person's
choices and remedies. Release neither final funds nor protected
assets on a nominal receiver or forecast. Account for costs at both
origin and destination.

**Evidence and reopening.** The [comparison](../settlement/repeated-provider-exit/01-two-exits-and-an-ordinary-service-choice.md)
and [sources](sources.md) test conditional costs and receipt.
Reopen on changed staffing, safer provider choice, inaccessible
review or evidence that no option meets the joint floor. Next
test the assignment of trainees and experienced crew needed for an
ordinary specialist service against the same workers' existing
care duties, with actual replenishment and resident choice.
Actual law, budgets, participation and outcomes remain unverified.
