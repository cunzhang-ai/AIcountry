# C08. Personal agency, family, property and estates

15 October 2026 · Design 71 · Proposed B21–B40, not enacted law

Select a common personal-rights floor with **scoped, optional family and
property ties**. People retain identity, bodily agency, income and political
standing individually. Relationships can create actual care/property duties;
they do not turn a household representative, relative, registrar or AI into
the legal person. Children have their own voice, protection and resources.

This chooses neither compulsory traditional marriage/household control nor
a purely private-contract model. Public law protects children, bodily agency,
dependants, creditors and safe exit; adult care/property compacts may involve
more than two people without duplicating assets or forcing every informal
relationship into registration. A new tie cannot dilute already vested rights.
Private intimacy, care, property, parentage and sovereign authority remain distinct.

Majority is proposed at 18, with supported young-person decisions; this does
not alter P05's criminal age 16, K13's protection rules, C02's voting rules
or Y's pension age. New ages, shares, periods and review clocks are value
hypotheses, not scientifically optimal or adopted numbers.

The [boundary journeys](../../settlement/family-civil/README.md) expose conflicts,
shared-property/support arithmetic and missing court capacity. They are not
observed families, verdicts, independent legal review or a funded service.
No registry error, death entry, family label or agent replication can create
extra ownership, parentage, insurance money or political mandates.

Cross-border recognition, third-party gestation/donor identity, sector
delegations and monetary penalties, treaty/international-crime law and full
costs still need reconciliation. This is an extension of the ordinary draft,
not a complete code. Current software, source law, constitutional boundaries
and accrued claims remain until authorized revision. Next complete the
remaining jurisdiction/sector boundaries, then audit ordinary-law coverage
and the whole country's simultaneous resource and delivery dependencies.
