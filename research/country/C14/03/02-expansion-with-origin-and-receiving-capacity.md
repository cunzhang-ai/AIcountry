# LT06–LT10. Carry the conditions of success into each new cohort

*Design310 · Proposed national expansion rules.*

**LT06 — Trace what actually made the first service possible.**
Before proposing material expansion, identify the operator, qualified
roles, paid supervision, local relationships, travel, source, remedies
and response times that made the observed service usable. Check the
ordinary improvement comparator and remaining exclusions. Track extra
work and resources supplied by outside teams, households or workers.
Identify which conditions exist in the next areas and which must be
built, replaced or tested. A provider change, different population or
larger recruitment market can change the mechanism even under the same
written contract. Carry the source, effect estimate, uncertainty and
material dissent together. The later operator needs its own evidence.

**LT07 — Count the service at the place it leaves and the place it reaches.**
Compare the starting offer, continuing trial obligations, later cohorts,
origins and shared inputs on one dated resource and service account.
For each material borrowed or relocated input show accepted use,
protected origin work, actual qualified replacement and later
replenishment. State which apparently spare capacity is already booked
or held for another simultaneous call. Pay retention, backfill, travel
and overlap. Workers retain free movement. A public expansion budget
cannot assume that another provider's unfilled duty or an unpaid
household shift supplies its staff. Where dependencies span jurisdictions,
retain each body's authority and the ordinary interlocal resolution
route. Test a foreseeable loss without counting the same alternate twice.
Where several expansions share a scarce enabling input, count it by
task and useful time across all offers and existing independent
functions. Protect hearing, opposition and remedy access on their
own source and staffing. Make the remaining joint limit visible in
the elected comparison, including which people wait and the
second-window cost of an earlier start.

**LT08 — Give people waiting for expansion a funded choice now.**
For each materially excluded place or group, state whose existing
claims are due, which future improvement they lack, and what they can
actually use during the wait. Give severe and avoidable exclusion
weight in choosing the next cohort. Compare immediate ordinary repair,
a need-prioritized and adapted start, and a funded earlier common offer
with independent capacity. Compare their losses across existing
recipients, origin populations and waiting households. A route that
fits only people with transport, unpaid helpers or working credentials
does not cover the same need. Publish the selected interim service,
payer, deadline, remaining exclusion and a usable challenge. If
no safe funded interim improvement fits, record that fact and give
the competent elected body a real funding or repair choice.

**LT09 — Vote the actual schedule and protect existing rights.**
The competent elected bodies choose funded preparation and exact
prospective cohort dates under CP and NP. Reassess the whole remaining
choice after a material change to personnel, provider, sources or need.
A trial result, verifier or minister cannot start or move an enacted
right. Before any new discretionary commitment, verify accepted
people, facilities, independent review and dated owned funds under
ordinary release law. Once a duty or contract binds, keep its
original date and remedy through failed delivery. Do not treat loss
of a common reserve as a reason to reopen an already lawful worker's
contract or to cancel a continuing personal claim.

**LT10 — Evaluate actual access across the full affected area.**
Observe pilot and nonpilot places, origin and receiving work, people
who never entered the service, and transition and continuing costs.
Publish comparable safe totals, denominator changes and service
losses as well as gains. Maintain accessible original testimony and
challenges without a central private-life register. AI can check
dependency reuse, calculate competing scopes and help people give
evidence in their own medium. Responsible professionals verify
capacity and elected bodies decide timing and burden. Reopen on
material origin harm, repeated exclusion, workforce or vendor loss,
or a more effective funded ordinary alternative.
