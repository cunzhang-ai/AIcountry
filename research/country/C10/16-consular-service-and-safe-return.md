# C10. Consular service, travel documents and safe return

Design452 · Proposed ordinary law

**CSL01 — Assign the duty and protect access.** The foreign affairs
ministry provides ordinary consular assistance to nationals and to
others within the express scope of domestic law or an applicable
agreement. A continuously staffed home duty office takes protected requests directly
and through authorized posts or partner missions. Provide free urgent
intake, accessible communication and a safe way to give evidence
without an account or contact with an unsafe authority.
For detention, apply the relevant notification and access duties.
Respect an express refusal of consular action where applicable and
keep access to qualified local counsel separate. Arrange
consular communication, information on local legal and medical help,
and contact with a chosen person as permitted by host law and with
the person's agreement or separate lawful authority. A consul
cannot decide the foreign case, exercise host police powers or
compel the person to leave.

**CSL02 — Keep nationality and document issuance distinct.** The
national document office decides ordinary passport issuance and
renewal. For a verified national or a person with an independently established
right of return and express document eligibility, a qualified officer
at a designated post, or the authorized
home duty office, may issue a narrowly scoped emergency travel
document under enacted terms. Verify identity and right to travel
using reliable existing records or independently checked alternative
evidence. Refer genuine status disputes to the competent status
office. Keep urgent protective help open on its separate grounds.
State permitted travel, expiry and who must accept the document.
Use child protection and independent review when guardians conflict.
Give reasons and timely independent review for delay or refusal,
before the last useful departure where possible. An issued document
does not establish admission by a carrier or foreign authority.
An inability to pay cannot block the covered urgent route. Ordinary
fees and any lawful recovery have separate terms and review.

**CSL03 — Fund voluntary assistance through actual arrival.** Check
host and transit permissions, the person's agreement, qualified
transport, care or accessibility requirements and accepted receiving
before committing a departure. Use local lawful assistance or an
agreed partner where these are usable. For covered urgent travel or
temporary protection, the elected budget names the paying office,
settlement and limits. Advance payment or a capped public purchase
may be authorized without assuming a family's funds. Verify arrival
and lawful local care or support handover. No travel document, loan
or declaration creates a route where permission, safe receiving or
physical transport is absent. Record the gap and maintain lawful
assistance. Keep non-return and independent status protections.

**CSL04 — Make failure reviewable and preserve the next call.**
Provide a separately reachable home supervisor and independent
urgent review under ordinary law. Track request, determination,
actual document delivery, acceptance, departure, arrival and the
next due protection separately. Budget ordinary staff, interpreters,
partner terms, emergency funds and renewal without double use.
Compare a resident post, a staffed home office with partners, and
local assistance without travel. Keep original claims and protected
records through transfer. AI can assist translation and scheduling.
It cannot decide nationality, issue documents, infer assent or
certify return. Reopen on missed departure, unsafe disclosure,
unavailable partner or stronger lawful receiving.
