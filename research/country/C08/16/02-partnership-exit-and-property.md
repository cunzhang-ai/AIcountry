# Partnerships, unilateral exit and fair property settlement

*B24–B27; part of [C08/16](../16-family-agency-property-and-estates.md).*

**B24 — Optional scoped partnership.** Two or more consenting adults may
register a civil care/property compact on equal terms, regardless of sex,
gender, belief or family form. No under-18 exception, representative consent
or compelled ceremony. Identify participants, voluntarily assumed duties,
property scopes, contribution/default shares, notice and exit; provide
accessible independent advice for material unequal terms. No household head.
Record expressly whether this is shared-life partnership or only limited
care/property commitments; limited support alone does not choose an heir.
This is not automatically parental responsibility, medical authority, a
joint tax unit, immigration status or a waiver of personal rights.

An individual may retain several distinct lawful support/property ties.
New commitments require actual authority over the promised resources and
cannot dilute existing vested shares or children's claims without applicable
rights/consent. Inform affected rights holders safely when their commitments
would change; a partner has no veto over another's bodily/private life.
Religious or cultural practice remains voluntary within the common floor.
Informal life need not be registered, and courts can recognize proved common
property/contribution duties without policing intimacy or counting AI accounts.

**B25 — No-fault exit.** A participant may end their own status prospectively
by an authenticated, usable declaration to the civil-status service; no fault,
partner permission, mediation, reconciliation, child surrender or property
settlement is required. Provide a manual/accessible alternate receipt during
outage. Record the effective declaration and give safe notice within seven
working days; an unsafe recipient route needs court-controlled substituted
notice, not publication of a refuge address. Fraud/identity/meaning disputes
receive independent challenge; no administrative delay creates a partner veto.

Other willing participants' separable ties may continue; none is silently
rewritten. Existing assets, debts, support arrears and child duties survive
for lawful settlement. Directly revoke relevant agent mandates under B03;
status exit does not retroactively cancel third-party rights or automatically
give a co-owner every joint account. Immediate safety uses C08/10 separately.

**B26 — Property and care contributions.** Keep prior separate property,
personal gifts and inheritance separate unless validly contributed. For a
proved common acquisition undertaking, start with equal net shares among
its actual participants unless a voluntary informed agreement or evidence
justifies another allocation. Unpaid care, foregone earnings and maintained
common work count as real contributions; sole title or cash wages do not
conclusively erase them. Living together or sharing a utility bill alone is
not proof of a whole-property undertaking. Identify each asset, ownership,
valid debt and contribution once; relationship graphs do not multiply shares.

Adjust for proved coercion, concealed assets and inequitable exploitation
under B06, with independent evidence and reasons, not moral blame for leaving.
Protect children, innocent co-owners, secured parties and collective/tenant
rights. Specific temporary occupancy needs safety/child/housing grounds,
expiry and challenge; it does not transfer title. Avoid a forced unsafe sale
where a lawful funded buyout/other settlement is available, but do not invent
unlimited public buyout money or make one partner an involuntary lender.

**B27 — Maintenance and debts.** Courts may order a needs/ability-based
transition payment for proved loss of actual support or care-related earning
capacity: normally up to 24 months, reviewed at six-month intervals. A longer
compensatory award for proved durable care-related loss requires specific
evidence, a finite amount/duration and appeal, not lifelong personal control.
Use actual usable means after protected own/dependant needs; no compulsory
cohabitation, care labor, sex or treatment. Public I/S/Y rights remain personal;
unpaid maintenance is not received income. Credible involuntary inability
permits prompt prospective reassessment without erasing due arrears by an
algorithm. Intentional concealment needs actual proof, not presumed earning power.

Only an authorized borrower/guarantor or proved joint undertaking incurs its
debt; family status alone does not bind other adults. Separation allocation
cannot release a creditor or transfer collateral without lawful authority
and process. U insolvency and exceptional support claims retain their scope.
The new plural default and 24-month norm require public review; they are not
adopted law or proof that formalization improves informal cooperation.
