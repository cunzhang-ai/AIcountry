# Design389 decision. Put conditional promises and cash on the same public calendar

## Baseline and selected mechanism

Design388 DB07 controls gross commitments, DB21–DB25 provides
independent fiscal scrutiny, FB42–FB47 compares forward budgets
and PL06 addresses concession guarantees. The unresolved
application is a consistent whole-portfolio register and execution
test across issuers, including separate counter-guarantees, doubtful
authority and expected rescue without a legal promise.

Select [DB26–DB32](../country/C06/60/10-public-guarantees-and-common-fiscal-risk.md).
Identify the promise, competent authorization, actual coverage,
currency, liability, fees, legal cap, date and payer. Expose common
calls and protected duties before new reliance. Verify the first
lawful payment and keep repayment, loss and service receipt separate.

## Alternatives and objections

Retain separate issuer limits and forecast expected costs. That is
simpler and can preserve institutional autonomy, but can spend one
shared reserve in several adverse accounts. A broad ban on new
guarantees would avoid new conditional support, but can forego
useful financing or shift the same burden into direct purchase or
public debt. Select narrow, explicit and contestable guarantees
with shared stress and retained alternatives.

Large published totals can mislead when conditions are mutually
exclusive or when internal flows are duplicated. Retain both gross
cash and consolidated views, with source, scope and timing. A strict
reserve can tie up money that could fund an essential service.
Compare actual adverse timing, lawful financing and a smaller or
different instrument, with no invented common percentage. Rescue
expectations may remain. Keep their service consequences visible
and return any new backing to the elected authority.

## Evidence, migration and next action

[Primary records](sources.md) describe risk pricing and portfolio
review within Sweden's own legal framework. The [constructed calls](../settlement/public-guarantees-and-common-fiscal-risk/01-two-guarantees-and-one-due-date.md)
test a shared adverse event and a late cash recovery. Actual
independent review, affected deliberation and local capacity remain
unverified.

Adopt prospectively under DB and applicable sector law. Assign
issuers, Treasury, separate verification and payment, independent
analysts, courts and actual service owners. Preserve prior lawful
instruments, restrictions, due claims, official misconduct review
and lawful third-party remedies. An unexplained or disputed old
instrument needs a competent determination. No register entry
ratifies it or cancels it. This edition exercises no fiscal authority.

Next trace two real conditional instruments and a counter-guarantee
from their original text to cash, including a common shock and an
ordinary service deadline. Compare the exact narrower, direct and
no-new-backing options under the same evidence. Reopen on omitted
terms, fiscal concealment, repeated shortfalls, unmet service or a
stronger less costly alternative.
