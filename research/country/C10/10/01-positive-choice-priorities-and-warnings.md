# IA01–IA04: assessment, elected priorities and actual warning

*Part of [C10/10](../10-civilian-assessment-and-intelligence-limits.md); design102 research selection, not enacted law.*

**IA01 — Select assessment without a separate secret domestic service.** Establish a civilian **National Assessment Unit (NAU)** within the Cabinet Office's ordinary C04 administration. Select lawful strategic/open-source/diplomatic assessment, safe receipt of voluntary reports and referral to competent ordinary services. Do not establish a distinct covert domestic service or grant a new noncriminal search, interception, compulsory disclosure, covert infiltration, recruited/paid clandestine source, covert political operation, detention or force power. Police/prosecutors retain actual P/K jurisdiction and particular lawful grounds; ordinary protection, cyber recovery, defense and clinicians retain their own tasks. An intelligence warning, a foreign partner's label, PD25 delay order, emergency or procurement cannot supply a missing power. This is a substantive selected limit, not indefinite postponement of a presumed necessary agency. Actual valid predecessor powers require an express lawful migration decision; this proposal does not repeal them by manuscript.

A is selected because this country assigns coercive intrusion to contestable specific legal grounds and chooses to spend scarce qualified capacity on prevention, resilience, ordinary investigation and credible warning before adding a broad secret preventive task. Assessment can still fail, be politicized or miss grave harm. B/C can outperform A in particular safety cases; comparative safety and liberty cannot be collapsed into a single promised score. The selection rests on a disclosed value preference for constrained observable public power, not on missing territory alone or an unsupported claim of empirical superiority. A purely dispersed no-unit arrangement is a leaner retain/no-change rival: it saves central staff and reduces agenda framing, but can leave cross-sector/foreign dependencies without a named analytic receiver. NAU is chosen for that bounded integration task, not as a national truth or command center.

**IA02 — Positive assessment purposes.** NAU prepares source-linked assessments of (i) external armed attack/coercion and civilian exposure; (ii) grave cross-border cyber/service/supply disruption; and (iii) material interaction of those risks with civilian physical hazards and the country's ability to protect people and lawful institutions. It compares uncertainty, contrary explanations, source families and practical defensive/resilience options. Sector owners retain health, utility, climate, market, cyber and defense judgments; NAU may not turn ordinary unemployment, criticism, lawful strikes, migration, religion, lawful autonomy advocacy, democratic opposition or an unpopular social model into a security threat. Particular evidence of an independently lawful grave harmful act may be referred without assigning guilt to that community. No general population loyalty, association or person-risk register, election certification, party vetting or political-truth ranking.

**IA03 — Elected priorities, not ministerial conclusions.** Cabinet collectively names the responsible civilian minister publicly, approves an annual work/resource plan with the budget and publishes lawful analytic questions, recipients and expected outputs. A new lawful cabinet publishes its priorities within30calendar days; missing publication does not stop existing warning/custody duties. A protected annex may identify genuinely sensitive source detail under the companion secrecy rule, never conceal the legal purpose, total appropriation or existence of a program. The minister may request a lawful question and set general resource priorities within the Act/appropriation, but cannot require a preferred conclusion, fabricate confidence, omit contrary evidence or task a person for party advantage. Record all ministerial and major partner tasking with legal ground, version and recipient; unlawful tasking is refused and preserved for external review, not silently executed then called analysis. Material new task or method outside IA01/IA02 returns to prospective Assembly law, with C14 competition protections; a directive/contract cannot amend the Act.

**IA04 — Delivered outputs and referrals.** Deliver each calendar-quarter source-linked assessment to Cabinet and the statutory committee within15calendar days of quarter end, plus an annual public National Risk Outlook by1March following the calendar year with safely releasable reasons/uncertainty and dissent. Keep the original analytic text and any later ministerial policy response distinct; Cabinet may disagree publicly rather than rewrite what analysts found. Source-identifiable/classified parts use the exact lawful protection and contested-redaction route, not indefinite suppression of the entire scheduled output. A credible grave urgent warning is sent without awaiting a director/minister signature to the competent existing civilian/defense receiver and the committee's protected receiver; actual emergency facts use immediate lawful notification, not the next quarterly deadline. The first receiver retains triage until a competent successor accepts under P06/C04. An alert grants no new coercive power or presumption of guilt. Necessary safe public warning is issued by the competent sector authority under its own duty; NAU preserves the referral/acceptance/failure and may communicate lawful nonidentifying safety facts, not take over operational command. Committee requests for existing records follow IA12; a new analytic request gets a reasoned Cabinet work/resource disposition within20working days, with urgency addressed separately, not an unlimited unpriced parliamentary task queue.

The first reports cover only the actual operating portion of a year, not invented predecessor observations. Each scheduled report releases a safe public summary of the actual mandate/method/source limitations, budget/receipt and material dissent; restricted details use IL11–IL13, not suppression of the entire output. Any person may report a missed mandatory publication or seek its ordinary lawful review without a classified identifier; a source-safe court order can compel the existing statutory output, never prescribe a political conclusion. A common factual report may support several outputs only within its complete valid dependency scope; outputs are not several independent observations.
