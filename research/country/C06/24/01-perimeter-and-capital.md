# Classify the claim and make the owner bear real risk

*M01–M02; part of [C06/24](../24-financial-law-and-loss-allocation.md).*

**M01 — Activity, not a label, defines the perimeter.** Commercially taking
public funds as repayable payment/savings balances, on demand, notice or term,
requires a prudential bank license and M membership, except a separately authorized
fully safeguarded payment custodian under M05. Investment risk cannot be
marketed as an insured deposit. Commercial lending, investment brokerage/
custody and payment transmission require the distinct conduct license for
the actual activity. An issuer's disclosed transferable bond/equity offering
is not deposit-taking unless actually offered as a cash-management balance;
securities issuance keeps its own law and cannot masquerade as insured money.
Incidental supplier payment terms retain sale/consumer duties; they are not
a bank or financial-lending business merely because payment is deferred.
Ordinary gifts, mutual informal assistance, a person's
own investment, open software or an agent copy alone do not.

Publish the legal debtor, governing law, denomination, maturity, withdrawal
right, fees, loss ranking and actual insurance before acceptance. Tokens,
platform wrappers, off-balance affiliates, public ownership and automated
interfaces cannot remove the actual activity. The issuer/controller needs
actual attribution; owning a tool is not operating a financial intermediary.
The prudential supervisor licenses banks; the separate conduct supervisor
licenses nonbank conduct, with V02 reasons, proportional entry and appeal.
[NB01–NB36](../44-nonbank-finance-and-client-protection.md) now specifies the distinct
insurer/private-pension/fund/CCP candidate and its prudential/conduct roles.
Neither this bank schedule nor NB's method proves finished actuarial tables,
funded capacity or lawful new operation.

**M02 — Capital is paid loss capacity.** For this national candidate require
eligible equity E of at least 8% of gross exposure X, daily measured and
monthly reported. E is paid common equity plus retained earnings, minus
losses, goodwill/intangibles, deferred-tax assets dependent on future profit,
and the bank's own/reciprocal capital holdings; no callable public promise,
revalued software prediction or its own loan funding circular equity counts.
Report uncertain realizations and independently contested valuations.

X counts gross assets before netting against deposits, plus legally committed
undrawn lending/guarantees at full amount, and derivatives' positive current
replacement exposure plus their remaining maximum contractual additional
loss. Do not count an already booked exposure twice. No cross-counterparty
netting, zero-risk sovereign exclusion or unbounded derivative exposure is
assumed; a contract with no determinable bounded loss is not allowed inside
this deposit-taking candidate. State this restrictive product choice openly:
it can raise credit/hedging cost, and is not Basel's leverage calculation.
A separate risk-bearing vehicle has no deposit protection or implicit rescue.

The supervisor can require a reasoned institution-specific equity ratio up
to 12% for evidenced unaddressed loss/operational risk, with independent
challenge; it cannot lower 8% or guarantee safety at 12%. If risk cannot be
controlled within lawful scope, restrict the actual risky activity or seek
new legislation rather than inventing an unlimited capital order. Falling
below a ratio triggers assessment/correction, not automatic fraud, closure
or M09 nonviability. No distribution/buyback may leave E below required E;
new shareholder money must have an independent lawful source.
