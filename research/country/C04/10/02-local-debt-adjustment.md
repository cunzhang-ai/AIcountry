# LD05–LD08. Adjust local debt within law

**LD05 — Start with consent and limit any binding composition to a known
debt class.** An elected council may seek voluntary changes under ordinary
law. If credible accounts show it cannot meet eligible financing and
continuing lawful duties, it may seek a court-supervised plan after good
faith negotiations, or show why a meaningful attempt was impracticable.
Only the council's recorded lawful vote can initiate this route. It applies
only to domestic-law unsecured municipal financing expressly subject to
the enacted terms at issuance or subsequently opted in by actual consent.
It cannot impair a separate guarantee, secured revenue, supplier claims,
wages, pensions, deposits, trust property, protected service rights or
foreign-law obligations. Each keeps its own source and remedies. Report
creditor, supplier, resident and worker exposure. An unresolved claim
remains visible. No local entity, court or minister acquires national debt
authority from the petition.

**LD06 — Pause enforcement only by a finite judicial order.** On evidence
of a material risk to protected services or an orderly eligible workout,
the competent court may stay specified collection steps for eligible
financing if necessary and proportionate. Give affected creditors notice
and a useful hearing. Where delay itself risks immediate serious loss, an
interim order needs prompt review and the earliest practicable adversarial
hearing. Protect creditors against avoidable deterioration and preserve
ordinary statutory and service remedies. The proposed outer limit is 30
calendar days initially and one reasoned extension, ending no later than
90 calendar days from the first order in the same distress. No renamed
petition or appeal automatically restarts it. A stay changes specified
enforcement only. It creates neither a payment, a new priority nor
permission to ignore a claim. Interest and arrears follow their applicable
law unless lawfully changed. Use existing interim public finance only where
actually authorized.

**LD07 — Require disinterested class support and an executable elected plan.**
A council votes on the exact plan, source and continuing services. An
independent court can confirm it against a dissenter only after each affected
class supports it by at least two thirds of verified eligible outstanding
principal held by independent creditors in that class. Abstentions do
not count as assent. Common control, affiliate holdings, undisclosed
side payments, artificial claim splitting and manipulated classes cannot
manufacture support. Disputed voting entitlements need an independent
determination under prior law. The court checks classes by materially
similar legal rights, equal treatment absent consent, feasibility over
the whole affected payment and service path, proportionality and at least
the expected value under the feasible lawful no-plan alternative, without
an assumed liquidation or forced service cut. Require applicable approvals
and funding before relying on them. Give dissenters, affected people and
workers a practical challenge route. A whole dissenting class cannot be
bound. If any condition fails, use ordinary law, continue service protection
and disclose unadjusted liabilities.

**LD08 — Keep government elected and plan for a second failure.** The court
may confirm or reject the financial plan and enforce it within its
jurisdiction. It cannot choose taxes, officeholders, electoral boundaries
or the service mix. Keep elections, local opposition and existing national
and sector protections running. No receiver becomes the government.
Publish lawful payment changes, actual debt, service receipt, later
financing risks and recovery end dates. A failed plan returns to ordinary
lawful correction, with no renewed stay by implication. Adopt prospectively,
retain existing creditor and worker terms and fund actual review and
receiving through handover. AI may reconcile schedules and scenarios with
source provenance, without declaring default or validating creditor votes.
The stay and class thresholds are proposed choices. Reopen on evasion,
costlier access to credit, blocked claims, delayed essential services or a
simpler locally accountable repair.
