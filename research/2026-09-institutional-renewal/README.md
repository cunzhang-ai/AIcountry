# Institutional formation and renewal: proposal 3.1

[Framework 3.1](framework-v3.1.md) extends the
[3.0 collaboration architecture](../2026-09-collaboration-foundations/framework-v3.md)
by explaining how institutions form, select coordination methods, share authority,
learn, divide and yield power. It treats the practical ability to change institutions
as an explicit research question alongside present outcomes.

The [worked mobility and care case](worked-case.md) compares an integrated provider,
separate local networks and a shared allocation arrangement with diverse local
assistance. The proposed revision changes scope and control; it does not claim
empirical superiority or establish a real allocation authority.

[Iteration record](iteration-record.md) · [Validation](validation.json)

Status: local research proposal, with no adoption or deployed capability. Prior
frameworks remain preserved. New material is English and continues the framework-only
scope. The next useful step is a bounded comparison with actual participants and
independent assessment, whose ownership, access and budget remain unassigned.
