## Diagnosis, values, evidence and competing explanations

Personalization can leave care recipients with control on paper but an unchosen
employment business, unpaid liabilities, unaffordable payroll help or no actual
replacement. A provider-led public service can instead control routines, access
and intimate life. Worker rights can be lost under either model. Individual cash,
collective bargaining, material capacity and accessible independent support are
substantive causes/choices; a better digital coordinator alone cannot resolve them.

Values: supported personal agency, equality, privacy, voluntary relationships,
worker dignity, effective democratic control and continuing necessary support.
Constraints: actual skill/time/source, employment and prior contracts, consent
and lawful authority. Hypothesis: a real person-directed non-employer option plus
optional supported direct employment can preserve control with less unchosen
risk. Public backing/administration may cost more or produce new capture.
No ratio, newer concept or court label establishes social superiority.
