# Knowledge, AI and future change

*Integrated design 66 research map; the linked topic files contain the current detailed choices. This map does not enact law.*

| Public question | Selected operating answer | Detailed design |
|---|---|---|
| What work should AI do? | Optional replaceable tools help with access, planning, professional work, hazards and inquiry under revocable mandates. Claimed productive gains deduct review, rework, learning and operating effort and need a qualified receiving task; competent people remain answerable for legal effects and actual outcomes. | [public AI capability](../C11/02-public-ai-capability.md) · [net capability](../C05/07/02-people-learning-and-ai.md) |
| Can future agents enlarge political power or spend shared resources by copying themselves? | Formal civic response follows distinct material reasons and affected stakes rather than message volume; every agent act stays inside one principal's mandate, expiry, resource envelope and reviewable legal effect, including during disconnection. | [agent mandates](../C12/02-agent-mandates-and-public-acts.md) |
| How can knowledge and institutions change? | Protected source records, contestable public statistics, independent inquiry, plural communication and replaceable AI support human judgment; lawful public decisions and bounded trials preserve rights while allowing revision. | [public knowledge](../C11/01-public-knowledge-and-culture.md) · [institutional learning](../C14/03-learning-and-amendment.md) |
| How would the public know whether lives actually improved? | A term-end public-conditions account compares multiple lived outcomes, distribution, political control, external harms and future capacity with inherited conditions and justified alternatives; opposition and affected people challenge it before an elected response. | [public conditions](../C14/04-public-conditions.md) |

restoration, full public amendment, finite policy attention, individual remedies
and actual enactment. Unexamined arguments remain unexamined; a closed docket
is no public mandate, policy truth or legality certificate.

[GA upstream AI safety](../C11/10-general-ai-capability-and-public-control.md)

exposure decisions. Civilian technical judgment, direct courts and elected scope
apply across public/private/community models; DP/sector/mandate duties continue.
