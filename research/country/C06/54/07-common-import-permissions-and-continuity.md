# NS26–NS30. Preserve essential supply when a shared permission fails

*Design303 · Proposed public purchasing, continuity and democratic control.*

**NS26 — Count actual independent paths through the original deadline.**
For a designated critical purchase, the responsible public buyer uses
the existing NS and sector account to verify product, upstream inputs,
authorized production and release, export and transit permissions,
settlement and insurance, and licensed local receiving. Map which
actual controller can stop each path and at what date. Two sellers,
plants, flags or banks can depend on one upstream licence, site or
intermediary. Test loss of that common condition. A proposed backup
with unverified dependence remains unqualified for that loss.
Require proportionate source and material-change evidence under actual
contract or law, with protected independent verification. Apply the
same evidence burden to a domestic producer that needs an imported
input. Country of origin alone neither qualifies a route nor proves
misconduct. Publish the affected service, relevant dependency class
and exposed windows with protected details and a challenge route.

**NS27 — Structure prospective terms to preserve usable options.**
Use the ordinary procurement route to compare accepted milestone
payments, advance security with a genuinely independent issuer, rotating
stock, paid independent capacity, regional agreements and qualified
domestic provision. Before committing, name verified prerequisites
for release, binding dates, protected payment and quality criteria,
what may be stopped or recovered, and who bears the cost if export
permission changes. A small supplier may reasonably need an advance.
Price that and separately price any payment guarantee or substitute
without assuming either enforceable or independent. Use paid,
tested receiving through the next protected call. Explain any
single-source choice against the same requirements, continuity cost,
ecological effects, workers and affected people abroad. New terms
apply prospectively. Preserve existing lawful invoices, supplier
rights and change procedures.

**NS28 — Finance the bridge gross and on its own clock.** When a
permission is credibly unavailable, the source owner gives timely
notice to the service lead, Treasury and the applicable regulatory
and review offices. Confirm the actual condition before concluding
breach. Withhold only an unearned or specifically stoppable payment
under existing law and exact terms. Keep any escrowed, already paid
or still owed amount, foreign currency floors, recoveries and usable
new cash distinct. A disputed claim, guarantee, expected refund or
insurance payout finances no replacement until actually released and
available for that use. A lawful payment may still be due for an
order that cannot meet the service deadline. Use only an expressly
authorized and funded alternate, including lawful reassignment of
money that is truly free. Verify domestic spending and owned usable
foreign currency independently. Name the residual uncovered people
and duties before optional commitments. Apply MQ or the competent
sector rules to any substitution, quality, choice and actual receipt.

**NS29 — Keep foreign leverage out of operational discretion.**
Distinguish lawful regulatory requirements, ordinary negotiated
conditions and a demand for an unrelated public concession. Preserve
the original evidence without presuming intent. Trade and diplomatic
officers may seek written clarification and lawful cooperation.
If a condition materially alters rights, public expenditure, regulatory
independence, public assets or binding external commitments, the
competent elected authority decides on the actual terms under the
applicable law, including TR where relevant. The purchasing officer
cannot waive an audit, conclude a side arrangement, retaliate or
bypass the applicable licence to count the shipment as available.
Provide protected evidence to independent review and lawful opposition
on the actual timetable. No concession can displace applicable
constitutional or treaty duties. Preserve lawful third-party claims
and avoid exporting the shortfall to a donor locality under simultaneous
need. Record a refused, legally unavailable or unaccepted arrangement
as unavailable capacity.

**NS30 — Verify receiving and account for the loss separately.**
The Assembly votes the actual risk and sources. The existing public
buyer and Treasury execute within their authority. The relevant
regulator and receiving operator determine safety, licensed use and
actual delivery. Audit the contract, available fallback and useful
receipt separately. The competent court hears withheld payments,
unlawful exclusions and protected claims within its jurisdiction.
AI may compare source records, shared dependencies and alternate
schedules. It cannot verify its own sources, permit export, authorize
payment, select patients or accept a political condition. Preserve
original duties, claims, lawful notice, provider records and paid
handover across any successor. Reopen after repeated joint failure,
late recoveries, changed licence terms, unmet second calls or a
better independently qualified ordinary route.
