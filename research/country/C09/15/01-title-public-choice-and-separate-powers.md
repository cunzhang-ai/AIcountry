# NR01–NR04. A finite endowment is not an automatic operating mandate

*Design167 · Part of [mineral rights](../15-finite-resources-and-mineral-rights.md).*

**NR01 — Choose the underlying-right model explicitly.** The state holds the
country's legitimately public or actually ungranted in-situ mineral/nonrenewable
geological rights in public/common custody for present and future people. This
is a selected future ownership model, not a declaration that all subsoil is
already state property. Lawfully extracted products and operator equipment may
be privately/cooperatively/publicly owned under the actual grant and ordinary
law. No public operator monopoly or extraction/export target follows. Geological
minerals, construction stone/sand and any covered fossil stock need their actual
legal resource definition; water/brines, land, waste/recovered materials, energy,
nuclear/exceptional activity and foreign/seabed rights keep separate sources.
A mineral right supplies no water withdrawal, home entry or offshore sovereignty.
The coordinated Act separately imposes independent site/activity/closure duties
on new commercial mining, materially disturbing exploration and new/enlarged
phases regardless of public/private/collective mineral title. Existing title
needs no public concession merely to remain private; operating permission is
a distinct actual TD11/NR requirement, not a title transfer or new taking.

**NR02 — Establish title before a grant.** Surface and mineral title, customary/
collective interests, leases/security, ecological rights, land access and actual
operating permission are different. A newly discovered deposit is not necessarily
ownerless; absence from an incomplete registry is not proof of ungranted title.
Protected source/evidence inquiry with AP help and ordinary review must resolve
material conflict before granting the disputed exclusive right. Existing private/
collective rights retain their actual source; any public acquisition/exclusive
appropriation uses B/LA actual competence, paid/secured compensation and safe
receiving, not a discovery/cadastre/software label. No blanket retrospective
nationalization, revocation or public price over privately held ore is selected.
Ordinary lawful safety regulation is no universal guarantee of private profit.

**NR03 — Decide whether to deplete before selling permission.** The competent
elected Assembly/council under C03/C04 actual scope chooses material public
resource programmes and hard-to-reverse depletion within rights/ecological law.
State exact source area/stock uncertainty, lawful purpose/quantity/rate corridor,
public owner, affected powers, resource/source/closure limits and review. Compare
no project, smaller/slower extraction, repair/reuse/import, different technology
and direct-public/plural operation on the same rights/duties. Cumulative small
approvals cannot evade the future-options duty. No positive bid, projected jobs,
royalty, carbon credit or imagined future repair buys a prohibited water/habitat/
health loss or substitutes for actual required collective consent. Consultation
is not consent, and a geological survey is not that elected decision. Ordinary
nonmaterial grants within an actual prior programme need no new national vote
for every truckload. A dispute uses existing C03 scope/local/interlocal/court
routes, not an owner/forum veto or approval by delay.

**NR04 — Use existing accountable public functions with separate powers.** The
coordinated Act assigns the public-owner function to national land/resource
administration under the responsible Cabinet minister, with a named C04 career
Mineral Rights Officer and actually qualified conflict/relief/source staff.
Lawful specific local delegation names territory, rights and funding; it creates
no new sovereign region or tax. Officer issues grants only within the elected
programme and prior award law; minister directs lawful policy, not secret party
awards or an opponent's treatment. Public enterprises operate under OE/SC/WL;
their owner/promotion command cannot certify their own technical compliance.
TS/TD environmental, water, plant, worker and exceptional safety officers retain
actual distinct competence, qualified access and independent ordinary review.
AP/V/OJ courts and AA source scrutiny need actual separate receiving/pay/records;
public labels/common payroll do not prove independence. No new mining board,
resource sovereign, private police or AI permit/mandate is selected. Public
careers/status/assignment review and existing constitutional holds remain.
