# One country operating reference, not another independent institution

3November2026 · Design163 · Whole provider/control research selection, not a funded launch

[OP01–OP24](../../country/C06/45-country-operation-and-handover.md) chooses an
existing public delivery backbone with usable plural routes, shared compatible
support and distinct independent powers. It rejects an all-at-once administrative
handover. It preserves all enacted dates, actual old rights, elections, appointments,
source law and meaningful alternatives. No new national guardian or procedure.

| Whole arrangement | Potential advantage | Material burden or failure |
|---|---|---|
| Selected access-first plural/public backbone | Existing service continuity, voluntary routes and funded fallback, separate public control | Contract/choice complexity, nonclaim and common-vendor capture; real public standby remains paid |
| Territorial direct-public provider | Fewer purchasing interfaces, collective service management | Paid acquisition/new formation, management/cover, monopoly failure and practical challenge; not automatically expensive or coercive |
| Residual cash/contract | Personal purchasing and narrower provider administration | Actual private fees/premiums, information, shortage, provider exit and public fallback; rights reductions cannot be hidden as administrative savings |
| Conditional full U+C2 (C1 rival) | Ordinary independence, less repeated income testing | Full paired tax/payment/needs/source and entrant overlap, service/physical/public-control capacity still required |
| Paid greenfield | Can change spatial/capital constraints | Land, homes, networks, opening stocks, lawful authority and continuing old rights are separately paid, not free retrofit assets |

These packages are not averaged into a metric winner. Direct-public can displace
plural organization without abandoning consent/democracy; full U may be chosen
without pretending source evidence exists. Actual affected choices are absent.
