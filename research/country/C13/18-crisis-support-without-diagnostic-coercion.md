# C13. Crisis support without diagnostic coercion

*Part of [C13 lived experience](../C13-lived-experience.md); integrated design 55 authored stress, not an observed event.*

A tenant facing eviction asks for help during a severe crisis. An app calls
them high risk, an employer sees an old diagnosis, and a neighbor calls
police. The nearest mental-health office has a three-week wait, while a
community team has no after-hours staff. In a second branch a person has
an opioid overdose; a witness hesitates to call for help because the
scene may be treated as a criminal investigation. Neither diagnosis nor
an algorithm tells the state which legal power it may use.

C07's funded first receiver must arrange an accepting civilian response,
safe communication, a place to talk, clinical assessment and voluntary
care, with housing and income services addressing the eviction separately.
If there is an observable imminent serious danger, C08's general safety
rule permits only the necessary short protective act with reasons,
medical attention, prompt independent review and an actual exit. Police
may address an independently grounded threat or offense; they cannot
convert a failed appointment into indefinite mental-health custody.
The person can challenge employer disclosure and a mistaken record. In
the overdose branch, trained witnesses and responders can use lawfully
available naloxone and emergency care, while any criminal inquiry keeps
its own elements, evidence and safeguards. Help-seeking is not guilt.

C06 must count the same mobile team, ambulance, clinician, pharmacy,
short-stay place, advocate and night worker once. If a voluntary place
is unavailable, the gap is a public failure requiring safe interim
support and elected repair, not a reason to redefine detention as care.
C14 would compare actual accepted help, distress, injury, police force,
privacy harm, housing, re-entry and executed remedies, including those
who stopped seeking care after a coercive encounter.

The exercise fails if a score triggers force, a diagnosis cancels a vote
or tenancy, a child or adult is detained for lack of a bed, overdose help
is avoided through fear, or a court order has no staffed receiver. No
actual person, law, staff, distance, source record or outcome was observed.
