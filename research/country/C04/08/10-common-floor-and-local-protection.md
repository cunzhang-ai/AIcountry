# MF41–MF45. Raise common protection while preserving lawful local choice

*Design527 · Part of [funded local mandates](../08-funded-local-mandates-and-equalization.md) · Proposed ordinary law.*

**MF41 — Establish a common floor and identify effective local protection.**
When national law improves a standing service, support or charge limit,
the competent national and local authorities publish the effect on the
same people at the same dates. Identify the existing common obligation,
the new common increment and lawful local improvements. Distinguish an
accrued reduction or award, a binding prospective supplement, a discretionary
programme with no existing award, and a planned improvement. Include people
with equivalent needs who lack a local supplement or have already lost
access. Territorial rights and portable services retain their own sources.

**MF42 — Follow the actual terms of the local decision.** Preserve accrued
obligations and keep valid prospective local terms effective until their
lawful expiry or express lawful replacement. A separately voted reduction
relative to a common charge follows that reference if the operative terms
provide for it. A fixed local amount keeps its own terms. Apply an existing
same-item offset when the applicable law or award requires it. Resolve an
unclear relationship through the ordinary competent interpretation and
review route with any lawfully available interim protection. A rise in the
common floor gives no implicit power to cancel a provision, impose a new
supplement or charge twice for one duty. A local council may change an unaccrued
supplement prospectively within its authority, with a recorded decision,
applicable notice and protection of continuing duties. Any national override
requires express lawful authority and reasons, including effects on local
self-government and affected residents. A change to protected elected local
competence follows its own constitutional route under [C04](../01/04-protected-local-competence.md).

**MF43 — Attribute cash, staffing and residual risk at the right level.**
Under MF09–MF13 and MF37–MF40, the common funding schedule identifies the
funder, person-side payer, approved local share, dates, paid capacity and
bearer of delay or denial for the new duty. Local improvements keep their
own source. Assess equalization against the cost and practical reach of the
common duty and standardized revenue capacity under the applicable formula.
A locality's voluntary extra spending alone neither reduces its
ordinary entitlement nor creates a national reimbursement claim. Compare need,
revenue capacity and actual access under the applicable common rules. Preserve
the ordinary resident payment or price on its original clock. Use a lawful
dated advance or a separately authorized and adequate local bridge, including
the second call, before treating a new prospective start as funded. If there
is a shortfall after a duty has commenced, record it and pursue the matched
ordinary correction and any available lawful backstop. A pending or lower
upstream settlement does not itself authorize a higher charge, delayed delivery
or resident clawback. Disputed reimbursement and resident review stay separate.

**MF44 — Verify what residents can actually use.** The local delivery owner
verifies the actual bill, payment or service and any funded correction or
refund. Compare original records and independent evidence of access, including
people unable to use the offer. Use direct accessible and assisted notice,
complaint and review. Publish safe totals and known gaps by need and place,
including where transport, unsafe waiting or a lost provider defeats a nominal
improvement. The public funder separately verifies settlement and how a paid
bridge was reconciled. Credit each funding line once. AI can flag inconsistent
charges and match dated authorizations under existing custody and purpose rules.
Competent officers decide the obligation and verify both receipts. No AI account
or case linkage beyond lawful authority is required for correction.

**MF45 — Put material geographic inequalities to a public decision.** When
comparison shows that a place cannot actually reach the common floor, make
ordinary funded correction available through MF and LD. Where everyone can
reach the floor but material differences remain, elected bodies compare
retaining local choice, improving equalization or the common floor, and a
uniform higher guarantee with a real national source. Price each on the same
population, amounts, dates and accepted capacity, including the strongest
lawful alternative use of local funds. Local experimentation remains subject
to equal standing and enforceable common rights. Neither the initial reform
nor its forecast authorizes new order of priority or appropriation.
