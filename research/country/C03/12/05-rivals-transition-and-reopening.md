# PC21–PC24: costs, incompatible rivals and honest transition

*Part of [C03/12](../12-continuous-political-finance.md); design110 candidate.*

**PC21 — Compare whole political economies, not a disclosure checklist.** Retain
national CF paid baseline/credits and add PC rolling source/persuasion controls,
annual organizing credits and plural local/referendum access. This reduces the
post-call loophole but adds privacy, verification and public-cash burdens. It
cannot equalize fame, relationships, prior wealth, unpaid editorial influence or
independent issue framing. Strong caps can entrench people with free official
visibility.1% committee baseline qualification can exclude a small minority;
voter-directed cash without that gate mitigates but does not erase that loss.

Rivals remain substantive: disclosure-only private funding avoids cap decisions
but preserves wealth purchase; broad all-issue caps close more framing loopholes
but threaten ordinary advocacy and regulator capture; equal cash per group
avoids popularity dependence but needs a different shell/field/source solution;
seat/vote-based annual subsidy is simpler but entrenches prior winners; public
noncash-only support reduces donations but concentrates agenda/provider power.
None is disproved by these authored arithmetic probes. PC's selection is conditional
on real expression/access/cost and independent affected review, not an adopted rule.

**PC22 — An additional due liability is not an old bid.** Annual public credit
planning needs actual national/local rolls and v times their distinct entitlements,
not number of parties. Separately price the actual possibly E-sized funded annual
receiving directory and actual filing/turnover, staff/help/custody, quarterly reports, protected donation
records, value/control audits, legal aid, civil execution, translation/care and
alternate vendors. Ballot-qualified guides/forums still depend on actual entrants,
not credits; national/local/ref and repeat duties can coincide. An annual private
cost cap is no public service price or permission to dilute equal access.

The [authored probes](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-continuous-political-finance/README.md)
show cash formulas, normalized windows, tiny tracks, shell/AI attribution and
simultaneous forum time. They do not establish media prices, source cash, paid
independent staff, safe custody, a functioning donation cross-check or whole
national marginal cost. Original37/61 objects and actual nulls remain; allocate
the new duty to its real owner/source/executor and retain uncertainty. No presumed
AI labor saving, future fine, S/client/FX pooling or election delay funds it.

**PC23 — Prospective source law and obligations survive.** Coordinated PC/CF/EA/
E/B/VD12/C04/PD/Treasury/C14 legislation must expressly reconcile the new scope.
Use at least180days preparation. The first annual PC credit year begins on a
1January at least12months after enactment, subject to C14/RR's protection against
incumbent-benefiting changes for its own next contest. New private source/
persuasion windows count only actual postcommencement acts; no earlier lawful
receipt becomes an offence. Start the rolling history prospectively, disclose
the first365day build-up and inherited advantage. Freeze each act's known index/
roll/ceiling; old assets, contracts, vendors, grants and membership/privacy rights
need named lawful custody/successors, not confiscation or automatic permission
to use newly prohibited funds. Provide actual pre-use segregation/return help.

The first local/referendum scope likewise applies only to a contest called at
least12months after coordinated enactment and after lawful franchise, exact
question/nomination/continuity/calendar/source reconciliation, with RR protection.
This does not certify a ready poll or let an office move one. Already called
contests, old E16 due support, fine elements, contracts, unpaid wages, due grants,
petition and useful-time remedy claims retain their source law. New funding is
not a retroactive cure for lost opportunity. Current constitution/software and
contribution permissions remain unchanged; no new procedure approves itself.

**PC24 — Reopen on actual power and deprivation.** Report who can enter and
organize, views actually reachable before first voting, withheld/delivered public
resources, donor/distributor concentration, independent coordination findings,
false accusations, unsafe disclosure, court correction and actual execution.
Separate actual original observations from model output/AI summaries. Safe
confidential evidence, protected correction history and dissent need custody;
do not collect sensitive testimony simply to fill the map. If monitoring fails,
state uncertainty and preserve lawful claims, not presumed equality or consent.

Reconsider caps, source privacy, public-credit/baseline tradeoff or PC itself on
credible exclusion, captured investigators/payers/vendors, coercion, unpaid
source/capacity, agenda domination or a better whole rival. This turn supplies
positive continuous finance rules, not legitimate adoption or independent living
proof. The next overall country question is the complete franchise/map/ballot
usability and lawful local nomination/continuity join. Test national/territorial/
local/diaspora routes and actual-sized candidate pools with protected accessible
alternatives; do not let a residence/account record silently lose a track.
[NB](../../C06/44-nonbank-finance-and-client-protection.md) and
[LR](../14-local-law-and-public-control.md) now supply those positive nonbank/local
rules; technical actuarial calibration, source/credit/VAT/production/price/FX and
actual participation/capacity remain unfinished.
