# Design370. Preserve essential income across compulsory collection

[DCR01–DCR06](../../country/C08/13/07-combined-debt-recovery-and-essential-income.md)
selects one protected limit across wage, account and public recovery
channels, priority for current support and a direct remedy for wrongful
recovery. Separate statutory claims, stays, assets and limits continue.

The [worked case](../../settlement/combined-debt-recovery-and-essential-income/01-one-pay-cycle-and-three-claims.md)
tests whether lawful isolated claims can exceed jointly available income.
It checks a delayed payment, a stay, priority, duplicate execution and
actual timely return. Lawful source, affected participation, execution
capacity and outcomes remain open.
