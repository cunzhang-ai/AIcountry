# Move a public function without losing the people it serves

Design209 · FT01–FT08 · Proposed country rules

**Select a funded transfer by task, with one accountable legal owner and a
named service receiver at every lawful date.** A change in who must provide
a service may leave its operator, employer, premises or records where they
are. Compare each of those choices and keep existing rights and remedies
usable throughout.

| Part | Rule |
|---|---|
| [FT01–FT04. Allocate the duty and pay for the change](11/01-assign-and-fund-the-transfer.md) | Lawful scope, distinct operating routes, funding and workforce or asset succession |
| [FT05–FT08. Keep each service working](11/02-accept-live-duties-and-remedies.md) | Protected case acceptance, exact cutover, independent correction and valid exit |

Use existing [territorial authority](01-national-local-authority.md),
[funded local duties](08-funded-local-mandates-and-equalization.md),
[clinical responsibility](../C07/19-clinical-networks-and-specialist-access.md)
and [transition rules](../C14/02-transition-and-commencement.md). No new
political region or general takeover power is selected. The [observed comparison
and worked case](../../settlement/public-service-transfer/README.md) test
the distinction between new legal ownership and actual service.
