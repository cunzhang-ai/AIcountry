# Commitment 105. Fund the common early-care floor where it is due

[FC01–FC08](../../country/C07/23-funded-local-early-care.md) assigns the
ordinary care duty to local authorities and the assessed finance gap and
limited fallback to national authorities. The voted schedule separates
ordinary local sources, national recurring advances, finite transition
and separately funded standby. Reconcile every quarter and protect
undisputed due amounts, independent remedies and paid successors.

A fee reduction, public transfer and actual staffed care remain distinct.
The Copenhagen 2026 allocation reconciles at its published scope.
The proposal's actual net source, independent paid workforce,
accepting fallback and household effects remain unresolved.
