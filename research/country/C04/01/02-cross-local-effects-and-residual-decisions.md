# CLX01–CLX06. Decide material effects that cross local boundaries

*Design357 · Part of [national and local authority](../01-national-local-authority.md).*

**CLX01 — Follow the effect and keep the local default.** The adopting
statute fixes sector materiality criteria before a dispute. A new or
materially changed decision needs the joint route where related acts
could materially affect protected rights, shared resource limits,
durable service access or duties in another locality, and prior local
or compact authority cannot dispose of the effect. Assess actual and
plausibly cumulative effects, including related phases and separately
named permits. Define the affected area by the reach of the effect,
with reasons for exclusion and uncertainty. Use the ordinary local
route for effects within lawful local authority. A scope referral
cannot decide a permit, change a boundary or dissolve existing duties.

**CLX02 — Give affected people a direct route and an original date.** A
materially affected person, representative body or council may file
with an affected council or the Assembly Clerk. A short statement of
the place, proposal and plausible shared effect is enough to open an
assisted scope inquiry. Pay for accessible receipt and secure transfer.
Keep the first proved receipt and identify the qualified receiving
officer. Within ten working days of that date, a conflict-free officer
predesignated under the adopting statute gives a reasoned preliminary
scope decision, following usable notice and opportunity for reply.
Where the facts or adequate notice are missing, keep the scope
provisional and report what is needed. Ordinary merits and fuller
reply periods retain their own operation. A missed decision
remains challengeable and does not reset the date or determine scope.
Give applicants and affected people direct independent review of
scope, notice, lawful limits and delay. Where prior law permits it,
a court may order a narrow, useful-time hold of the disputed
irreversible act. A filing alone stops no lawful ordinary work.

**CLX03 — Hear the affected area before the choice is fixed.** For a
material referral, the existing interlocal forum is convened under
the adopting statute. A predesignated conflict-free convenor uses
nationally funded administration and necessary local assistance.
Send usable notice and source summaries to affected places before
alternatives close. Residents, workers, service users and affected
right holders outside compact membership get the same opportunity to
inspect, answer and submit alternatives as similarly affected members.
Include applicable collective rights and direct representation under
their own law. Protect identifying or sensitive evidence and enable
qualified independent inspection. Hear dissent, including a council's
objection to the proposal or to the affected-area map. The forum
prepares options, including refusal, separate smaller stages and
any lawful joint agreement. It cannot allocate the disputed resource.

**CLX04 — Make the responsible elected body decide.** Within 45
calendar days of first receipt the forum files its advice and any
missing evidence, or records why it could not. A voluntary arrangement
can proceed only within actual powers, consents and rights. The
Assembly hears each material residual except a class prospectively
assigned by law to a competent elected region. The Assembly retains
any material allocation outside the region's jurisdiction. The
competent elected body gives a recorded disposition within 90 calendar
days of first receipt, subject to lawful notice, sitting and voting.
Referral or succession preserves the original date. Each body gives
protected consideration to a qualified opposition alternative under
its agenda rules. The competent body can refuse or select an exact
lawful allocation, or order dated further assessment with reasons
and continuing duties. An assessment order neither meets an applicable
allocation deadline nor starts a new clock. Record any unmet deadline
and preserve direct review and due protection. Identify the payer,
service owner, enforceable conditions, useful dates and unresolved harms. A later material
addition requires new affected input and its own review without
extinguishing the original docket. A council's absence or delay cannot
consent for its residents. Separate protected consents remain necessary.

**CLX05 — Protect people while the allocation is undecided.** Keep
existing lawful rights, services, ecological duties, permits and
independent remedies under their original authority. No disputed
material allocation starts before its competent decision and any
separate permit, source and receiving conditions. Use applicable
earlier sector deadlines and paid urgent provision when due. Put
funding, accepting providers and enforceable conditions on the same
calendar as the proposed decision. Any cost transferred to another
locality needs lawful authority and a usable source. If a hearing is
missed, publish the unresolved issues and use the earliest lawful
replacement route. Silence supplies neither approval nor a new power.
If a binding commitment cannot be delivered, keep its original claim
and lawful remedy rather than deeming it optional.

**CLX06 — Carry the docket through political and physical change.**
Reconcile the forum, affected-party access, opposition, independent
review and delivery with the common budget and legislative calendar.
Share a hearing only where separate claims and proposed decisions
remain discernible. Publish reasons, covered territory and safe
original evidence, including whether remedies were actually received.
AI can trace geographic and resource overlaps and compare alternatives.
A model cannot decide standing, scope, consent or actual harm. Preserve
original dates, rights, custody and resources through changed councils
or operators. Reopen on changed physical limits, an omitted group,
unfunded delivery or recurrent inability to secure a timely national
decision. Compare a properly founded elected region for durable
dockets under C14, with defined powers, finance and opposition safeguards. Test whether
the region would actually own the recurring scope or leave the material
residual with the Assembly. Compare ordinary added national capacity
and adequate voluntary arrangements before choosing a different
form. Keep the prior accepted and protected allocation until a
competent successor receives it.
