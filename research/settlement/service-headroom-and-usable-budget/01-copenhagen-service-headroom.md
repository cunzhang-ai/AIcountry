# Copenhagen 2026. Three different answers to how much room remains

Copenhagen's Finance Committee accepted its first citywide forecast
on 16 June 2026. Table 2 projects service expenditure of DKK 35,677.5
million in the stated perimeter, excluding the separately reported
costs for displaced Ukrainians. It gives these distinct comparisons.

| Forecast comparison, DKK million | Published amount or scoped reconciliation |
|---|---:|
| Corrected committee budgets, excluding the central lines | 35,420.8 |
| Expected committee expenditure on that scope | 35,566.0 |
| Difference at committee scope | 145.2 over |
| Central unforeseen and other funded budget lines | 8.7 + 119.9 |
| Expected spending on those central lines | 111.5 |
| Forecast expenditure over the corrected funded reference after those central lines | 128.1 over |
| Unfunded service-target room included in the target comparison | 58.4 |
| Forecast expenditure over the target that includes that room | 69.7 over |

The last two comparisons refer to the same DKK 35,677.5m forecast.
The funded reference is DKK 35,549.4m and the target including
unfunded room is DKK 35,607.8m. The budget-reference difference is
not a cash deficit. The meeting paper separately describes DKK
1,134.5m as the city's real treasury balance under its reporting
classification. That aggregate does not identify payable cash for a
particular service, appropriation or date. The same forecast reports
DKK 144.1m pressure in the Social Committee and DKK 20m favorable
variance in the Health and Care Committee. Each is a department
estimate. Neither figure alone identifies a transferable service,
funding source or free worker.
[Finance Committee, Table 2 and treasury account](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/%C3%98konomiudvalget/m%C3%B8de-16062026/referat/punkt-4)

On 26 August the Social Committee received an updated forecast of
DKK 175–225m pressure on ordinary operations in its stated scope.
It rejected the proposed qualified hiring restriction by four votes
in favor and seven against. It separately approved beginning talks
about an internal loan. Discussion authority was distinct from
approval of a loan, a payment or a service recovery. The August
ordinary operations figure has a different perimeter from the June
consolidated comparison and must not be subtracted from it as a
like-for-like change.
[Social Committee recommendation and votes](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Socialudvalget/m%C3%B8de-26082026/referat/punkt-2)

An October committee paper estimated a DKK 42m reduction in paid
parking receipts. It expected an equally lower state offset against
money reserved in the municipal treasury, leaving no net financial
effect at city scope. It nevertheless showed the lower net revenue
budget consuming service-target room. This documents the proposed
budget mechanism, with actual final cash, resulting allowance and
resident service requiring their own evidence.
[Climate, Environment and Technical Committee, parking adjustment](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Klima-,%20Milj%C3%B8-%20og%20Teknikudvalget/m%C3%B8de-26102026/referat/punkt-11)

within the same municipal year. They give real forecast perimeters
and committee choices. They do not fill the missing March award
run, payroll and receipt, court execution or common failure records.
