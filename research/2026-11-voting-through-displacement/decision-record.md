# Design321 decision on voting through displacement

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design320, EL01–EL04 and EL13–EL18, ER16–ER17, RR, FR, JC and RC.
The country already protects electoral residence, permits absent ballots
and prices concurrent rescue and civic transport. Choose a preauthorized
host jurisdiction as a full lawful electoral receiver, with a separately
staffed mobile or alternate route where the same window requires one.
The project assistant authors the choice. Actual lawmaking and electoral
authorities retain their separate responsibilities for adoption and operation.

## Alternatives and evidence

Keeping only the ordinary home counter and transport is simpler to
administer, but can make local franchise unusable when a road or crew
is lost. A centrally managed remote ballot could reach people across
the country, but requires separately demonstrated independence, secrecy,
access and custody. A bespoke emergency directive can adapt to facts
but leaves too much of the original access window dependent on a new
decision and can favour or burden particular districts. Select standing
host receipt on neutral criteria plus the relevant distinct paid route.
Accept the cost of additional trained staff and secure distribution of
multiple local ballot sets. Independent review must be able to correct a
failure to activate in time.

Australian enrolment guidance, New Zealand's special-vote process
and North Carolina's 2024 Helene response establish different workable
elements, with scope limits documented in [sources](sources.md). None
establishes comparative effects, local costs or the feasibility of this
specific combination. The common cash and time comparison is authored.

## Authority, transfer and reopening

Prospective election law fixes competence, receipt, P and source custody.
The electoral authority assigns independent qualified career officers and
substitutes. Courts keep direct independent review and existing narrow
materiality and mandate consequences. The elected budget prices host
demand, shelter, rescue, assistance, custody and review on the same dates.
Publish safe route and aggregate evidence. Preserve the original contest
and records through migration, with RR protection for immediate succession.
Keep actual shelter and rescue duties payable even if voting access fails.

For a real pilot, select two lawful adjoining jurisdictions. Exercise the
same closure with authorized mock ballots, affected residents including
people outside shelters, competing contestants, a funded mobile route,
rescue personnel and independent court or review observers. Record lost
access and original receiving, test whether common control can block both
routes and compare with a real ordinary route on the same demand. Reopen
on lost or coercive access, unrecoverable ballots, weak independence or a
better rival. Actual participation, authority, readiness and outcomes remain open.
