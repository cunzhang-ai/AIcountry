## Problem and competing explanations

Design 3 protected formal opposition and mentioned campaign finance, media plurality,
collective bargaining and vendor exit. It did not join those into a test of whether
a person can survive and communicate after opposing a wealthy governing patron.
This is a finding about the text, not an observation about any actual country.

Three rival diagnoses matter. **Procedural capture:** fair election rules and finance
oversight are missing or unenforced. **Material dependence:** jobs, housing, credit,
services and time make formal dissent costly even with fair ballots. **Information
gatekeeping:** a shared controller can restrict discovery or distribution while
ordinary speech rights remain on paper. These can coexist, but each implies a
different first intervention. Evidence to distinguish them would include real
control and funding records, accounts of retaliation and exit, campaign reach,
service dependencies, and whether remedies are executed before a contest ends.
No such AIcountry observations exist. Privacy and safety may limit publication of
individual testimony.

Values: political equality, lawful expression, fair process, secure livelihoods and
the ability to form independent associations. Constraints: scarce staff and money,
property and contractual rights, privacy, legal authority, actual supplier capacity.
Hypothesis: a plural-power arrangement may make opposition more usable than formal
electoral protections alone, without assigning all economic and information control
to the state. This is not an empirical result or a universal constitutional theorem.

## Coherent alternatives and selection

| Package | Mechanism and appeal | Main cost or failure |
|---|---|---|
| Retain design 3's limited provisions | Elections, disclosure, public-media access and ordinary labor/competition law address known channels. Existing judicial and electoral remedies remain. | A controller spanning work, housing, finance and communications can make lawful opposition materially impossible without violating a single isolated market test. |
| Consolidate strategic assets in public ownership | Government directly provides major employment, payments and information infrastructure; elected institutions could direct continuity and access. | A governing party could control all three, and acquisition, staffing, investment and independent editorial governance have costs. Public ownership does not itself create civic independence. |
| Selected plural-power arrangement | Combine rights against political retaliation, independent organizing, transparent significant influence, plural media, contestable common control, tested exit and lawful structural remedies. Parliament sets rules and appropriations; independent, challengeable bodies apply them; providers retain fair process. | Cumulative oversight may be expensive, chilled speech is possible if disclosure is excessive, and fragmented providers can still share funders or fail together. Requires actual alternative capacity and protection against regulator capture. |

The selection changes C03, C05, C06, C11, C12, C13 and C14 of the main design. It does
not mandate breaking up every large enterprise or public takeover. Remedies range
from nondiscriminatory access and portable claims to procurement separation and
structural change, proportionate to demonstrated control and available alternatives.
Legislative authority remains with the elected Assembly; a new economic or media
regulator would apply adopted law, not invent sovereign rules. Existing independent
court, treasury and local-service duties remain. Corporate owners, employees,
residents, journalists, parties, taxpayers, users and contracted providers all have
interests; none were represented in this authored selection.

## Support, objection and a compound failure

The [OECD policy-capture report](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/preventing-policy-capture_9789264065239-en.html)
supports examining several influence mechanisms together; [International IDEA's
political-finance brief](https://www.idea.int/publications/catalogue/political-finance-transparency?lang=en)
supports transparency and a fairer electoral field. [UNESCO's media-pluralism
account](https://www.unesco.org/en/media-pluralism-diversity/media-pluralism?hub=747)
identifies public, private and community media as distinct contributors to choice.
The [ILO statement of fundamental workplace rights](https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/fundamental-principles-and-rights-work)
includes association and collective bargaining. The [OECD analysis of portability
and interoperability](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/data-portability-interoperability-and-competition_73a083a9-en.html)
describes possible competition effects, not guaranteed usable civic exit. These are
primary institutional analyses, not observations of AIcountry or independent tests
of this combined package. They were inspected on 1 October 2026. The earlier
democratic candidate already specified campaign finance; this revision makes its
connection to material and information dependence explicit.

Authored failure: a firm owns a major employer, funds a party, distributes local
news and operates a public payment gateway. It threatens dissenting workers and
withholds opposition reach and payment service after a lawful defeat. A functioning
response requires timely worker protection, funding/control evidence, a regulator
that can obtain records, an independent court, actual alternative media and payment
capacity, lawful enforcement and money for continuity. A declaration of competition
or a court order alone does not supply any of these. If the public fallback is under
the outgoing cabinet's exclusive command, it may reproduce the same obstruction.

Material objections remain. Transparency can expose dissidents or small donors;
the proposed duty focuses on significant organized influence and protects people
at risk, with thresholds to be set in law. Antitrust or access orders can undermine
investment or speech; proceedings need defined standards, hearing, proportionality
and appeal, and ownership remedies need transition and compensation where required.
Public media can become incumbent media; its appointment, funding and editorial
chain need a genuinely independent and enforceable design. Worker and tenant
protections can be misused to shield unrelated misconduct; fair adjudication must
separate motive from legitimate grounds. None of these objections is settled by the
authored case or citations. Opponents of the package retain standing to challenge it.

## Qualification, migration and next bounded question

**Evidence:** the text gap and source positions were checked; ownership, causal
effects and civic outcomes were not measured. **Research selection:** design 4 now
includes a cross-system civic-independence requirement. **Legal authorization:** none;
current source clauses and publication permissions remain. **Operational readiness:**
unknown; no actual regulator, court, alternate operator, qualified staff or budget
has been verified. **Observed outcomes:** none. The added authored stress case is
diagnostic, not a sixth demonstrated protection.

Consumers of the change include election law, competition and labor law, housing,
public procurement, media and communications rules, payment infrastructure, the
national budget and future collaboration methods. Existing rights, contracts,
protected claims and current procedures continue unless lawfully changed. A real
transition must map who controls each dependency, preserve service and record
continuity, fund alternate capacity, assign remedies and provide appeals. The
current [authority package](../AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION.md) and [social/fiscal
settlement](../SOCIAL-FISCAL-SETTLEMENT.md) remain conditional; their budgets do not
include a measured cost for this correction. Their final adoption needs fresh review.

The next bounded question is whether, in a **specified consenting jurisdiction**,
lawful opposition can organize, communicate, obtain payment and keep essential
livelihoods under actual linked controllers, and which limited remedies can work
without creating a public monopoly. The deliverable would be an affected-party
control/dependency map, independent legal and capacity review, and a costed
comparison of the three packages above, using that jurisdiction's law and data.
There is no jurisdiction, participant or budget here, so this work is a real-world
gate rather than another invented national estimate. Evidence of practical
independence under the current design could support retaining it; evidence of harm
or capture under the selected approach would reopen or reject this correction.
