# Public power and territorial choice

The electorate can choose and remove the Assembly. The Assembly sets general law,
taxes, appropriations and material shared-resource decisions. A cabinet with a
professional civil service executes those choices through accountable local and
sector authorities. Independent records, auditors, courts, opposition and public
organizing test decisions; named officers and funded services carry remedies into
effect. A safeguard that shares its appointment, funding, evidence and enforcement
controller with the power it checks is only nominally independent.
The [C03 campaign route](../C03/07-political-finance-and-campaign-access.md)
adds a funded path for a new contestant, material sponsor and public-asset
disclosure, and a pre-vote judicial remedy. Treasury, election officials,
finance investigators, public media and courts have different powers; a
shared incumbent controller or late order can still defeat replacement.

The [C03 formation rule](../C03/06-government-formation-and-caretaker.md)
separates an Assembly election from a cabinet mandate. Members first seek a
majority prime minister, can tolerate a reviewable minority cabinet, and may
dismiss any cabinet by a full majority. An outgoing cabinet is caretaker
during the election period; failed formation or a repeat poll never expands
its authority. The Assembly, treasury and service officers keep their own
lawful budget and delivery duties during the gap.

General rules remain with elected law unless an enacted statute bounds their
delegation. The statute names purpose, issuer, scope and scrutiny. Material
administrative rules require an affirmative Assembly decision; narrower
technical rules receive public reasons, accessible input and an elected
call-in route. Urgent rules have a prior-law trigger and expiry. Neither
cabinet guidance, a vendor standard nor an AI update can quietly change
the rights or burdens set by law.

Legislative power also needs usable time and staff. Prior law assigns choices
to accountable local, interlocal or national scope, while the Assembly keeps
general law and unresolved material common choices. Its public capacity
account exposes the combined budget, rule, territorial, treaty and oversight
queue. Harmful delay calls for an elected response; it cannot silently grant
cabinet or AI the missing vote. The national/local arrangement is conditional
on the [founding form test](../C14/05-scale-and-national-form.md) at actual
population and geography. A legitimate public must compare a directly elected
regional tier and issue-scoped joint services before the first election; any
region requires its own lawful electorate, finance, successors and review.

People may seek stronger self-government or a different territorial status
without surrendering ordinary rights and services. The proposal opens a
protected process for affected residents and rights-holders to contest maps,
compare nonterritorial and territorial arrangements, and negotiate. Ordinary
service boundaries follow enacted law; protected autonomy or sovereign change
requires the prior constitutional and affected-area authorization. A vote’s
legal effect is fixed before polling, and land, water, debts, citizenship,
services and remedies need lawful funded successors before a change starts.
No AI system identifies a people, certifies consent or draws a binding border.
