# LU06–LU10. Permission and money must reach an actual serviced place

*Part of [development permission](../17-development-permission-and-serviced-land.md).*

**LU06 — Record real capacity and lawful milestones.** Relevant water/sanitation/
power/mobility/service owners identify actual location/time/quality/rate capacity,
existing committed/protected needs and compatible expansion, with source and
accepting responsible receiver. Use minimum actual bottlenecks, not an annual
average or a housing target. A planning grant alone does not allocate scarce
network capacity or override an older source right; actual WS/PS/CP/service and
priority law governs. Do not reserve the same headroom for several paper projects.

A planning component can be approved with exact lawful infrastructure/rights/
TD05 milestones before specified work/affected occupation. Identify conditions,
payers, accepted means, dates and what is unresolved; do not claim a promised
plant is commissioned or a bond is water. Where proposed use cannot meet lawful
site/source constraints even through a credible authorized route, state actual
reasons/refusal and alternatives under AP. Missing public finance cannot be hidden
as an unrelated developer payment or a cancellation of existing essential access.

**LU07 — Conditions require authority, purpose and a real connection.** Impose
only precise prior-law conditions necessary and proportionate to the proposal's
actual land-use/source/physical effects, within published rules. Link each to
its ground, quantity/quality/milestone, responsible person, receiver and review.
No unrelated donation, public-works wish list, political undertaking or payment
for a discretionary exception. A condition cannot purchase ecological/collective
consent, title, demolition or safe occupation. Standard repair/mitigation and
actual shared-capacity terms may differ on evidence, not patronage. A new general
burden requires its competent source/LR/DR law, not a case manual or AI score.

**LU08 — Attribute development works and finance explicitly.** Applicant funds
necessary on-site works and actual connection/mitigation attributable to the
project under prior law; shared trunk/road/service capital uses an express
statutory allocation method with actual causal capacity/benefit shares. Publish
eligible cost, source owner, existing deficit/renewal/general public benefit,
new demand, timing and credits. Do not assign an entire old network backlog or
the national housing/care/school guarantee to one project by calling it growth.
Existing users likewise do not automatically finance optional private expansion.

Select charges limited to lawful attributable net eligible capital/connection/
mitigation cost under an elected schedule, not an ad hoc share of predicted
land-value gain or a new general development tax. Distinguish valuation/tax NF,
WS tariffs, H cost rent, actual land/easement purchase and public grants. New
ordinary operating services use their actual budgets/tariffs/rights law; no
per-child residency tax or fee creates/denies school, care or safe-water rights.
Any express broader public subsidy/cross-subsidy needs its own source/budget
choice and incidence. A lower fee does not make the missing capacity free.

**LU09 — Credit once and keep custody/source distinct.** Credit compatible
public grants/provided resources, actual connection payments and accepted in-kind
works against the SAME funded cost once. Show the remaining project/shared cost,
amount due, payee/legal source and refund/adjustment for actual overpayment or
unbuilt purpose. No duplicate WS connection/tariff, NF charge or public-resource
recovery. Different purpose/period receipts are not fungible credits; a promise,
security, future tax or restricted custody balance is not received construction
money. Valid existing creditor/property/custody ranks survive new security terms.

Where lawful advance/security is required, specify actual ownership/custody,
permitted draw, qualified executor, coverage, release and safe failure/refund
under source law. Release only after independently verified reduction in remaining
secured exposure or actual valid compatible replacement security preserves that
coverage and prior claims. A promise being called secured, an unperformed
contractor bond or an opening ceremony is not accepted work or reduced exposure;
the same contractor cannot supply its own independent verification.
No independent government guarantee or automatic priority over older secured
creditors/U law. Price/date/quantity changes need lawful reviewed reconciliation;
an arbitrary later invoice cannot reopen a settled item or create capacity.

**LU10 — Maintain and finish the route, including abandonment.** Before reliance,
name actual asset owner/operator, lawful land rights, inspection/commissioning,
ongoing maintenance/renewal payer, records and accepted public/private successor.
A developer handover transfers only actual accepted title/work/source, not all
unpaid duties to a municipality. Guarantee/maintenance liabilities, wages,
contracts/creditors/tenant and restoration claims survive sale/insolvency/expiry.
Qualified safe temporary access/containment/repair and actual funded receiving
are needed if a site or contractor fails; TS/LA/H/CB and ordinary courts retain
their powers. No forced worker/family/neighbor reserve, new seizure or extra
CP command. Absent source/receiver stays an unmet obligation, not a safe site.

[IV23–IV27](../../C06/63/06-transport-housing-and-land-receipts.md)
separates housing and transport finance from LU08 connection and
mitigation charges. Any broader source requires its own law.
