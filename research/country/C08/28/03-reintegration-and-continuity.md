# RL11–RL15. Release must reach an ordinary life

*Design 190 candidate. Part of [C08/28](../28-sentence-review-and-release.md).*

**RL11 — Keep the person connected to ordinary rights.** Corrections prepares
documents and a safe way to leave. Named local receiving services arrange
immediate shelter where needed, clinical continuity with consent, medication
and accessible support, and usable I05/other income applications within their
own eligibility and urgent access duties. Identify actual payers, consent,
accepted handoffs, arrival and fallback. Release proceeds on the due date
even if a bed, clinic, identification record or receiving worker is missing.
Record the unmet duty, activate existing urgent help and give an executable
claim against the responsible office. Prison is unavailable as a housing or
care substitute.

**RL12 — Protect people affected by the offense.** Provide safe, timely and
proportionate information about a release decision and relevant protection
to victims who choose it, with a named protection contact and an actual
response path. Protect private addresses and identifying data on both sides.
Existing violence, child protection and civil remedies retain their own
thresholds. Release, restitution, criminal guilt, clinical help and
restoration remain distinct decisions. No compelled meeting, forgiveness,
testimony or return to a shared home is required. Apply Q11 from its actual
earlier release or lawful expiry event. Recall does not restart that clock.

**RL13 — Fund the real receivers and separate their powers.** Existing ordinary
courts decide liberty. The PO Civilian Corrections Service assigns qualified
execution and community officers under protected career rules. Health and
local service authorities retain their own duties. Fund defense, interpretation,
review, outreach, victim safety, transfer and community services through
lawful general revenue and local/equalization sources. Include paid transition
and simultaneous court and care demand. A freed cell or model estimate
supplies no automatic cash, housing or qualified worker.

PD protects case and clinical information. AI may assist lawful scheduling,
translation and source organization with a usable human alternative. It has
no authority to determine risk, eligibility, conditions or return to custody.
Direct courts and outside-command abuse review remain available. Preserve
actual custody and release evidence in reachable independent custody. Track
actual shelter, safety, claims and reoffending with scope and privacy limits.
Reporting targets cannot govern an individual decision.

**RL14 — Adopt prospectively and reconcile actual sentences.** A competent
Act establishes the scope, halfway deadline, powers, source and start.
Review existing sentences individually under lawful leniency and source
law, including valid old earlier release and protection. Where adoption
covers a person already past the halfway point, provide the hearing by the
operative start and execute the actual new or stronger old limit.
For an existing conditional release, count from the original ordered or
actual release, whichever was earlier. Preserve any earlier discharge. A delayed
migration does not restart a sentence. Reconcile national G and actual
foreign/tribunal obligations before relying on domestic review. Preserve
pending cases, protection, accrued remedies and paid accepted OP/T successors.

**RL15 — Retain real alternatives and reopen on evidence.** Select automatic
halfway review with a presumption of release, individualized exceptions and
a finite recall window. Compare automatic fixed-fraction release with funded
support, which gives more predictable liberty but fewer individualized
exceptions. Compare full finite sentences with discretionary or humanitarian
review, which preserve more of the original punishment but risk avoidable
custody and arbitrary discretion. The selected design accepts a real safety
tradeoff at the recall endpoint. Victim safety then depends on ordinary
protection and lawful new cases. Its burden, fairness, safety and paid
readiness require qualified comparison and affected participation.
