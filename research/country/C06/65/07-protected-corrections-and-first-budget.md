# Reopen first-budget choices when protected duties need repair

*Design294 · JC28–JC32 · Proposed joint budget application.*

**JC28 — Reconcile the baseline before relying on the remainder.**
Treasury and each actual duty owner identify a material new cost,
lost delivery path or prior omission in the protected baseline.
Use the same source, deadline, scenario and duty across the retain,
repair and optional alternatives. Distinguish an obligation already
paid, a booked remedy, an uncovered service and a new optional
offer. Reuse only matched resources and avoid adding the same
old obligation or reserve twice. Identify any ordinary care,
electoral or court activity that cannot be moved. A newer cost
estimate does not change the underlying priority, entitlement or
original date.

**JC29 — Qualify the protected correction as work and payment.**
Name each original need, useful time, authorized receiver,
appropriate paid skill, independent decision route and actual
executor. Reconcile national and local gross sources, received
advances, currency, capacity and replenishment. Add affected
opening liabilities under OL and accessible receiving costs
under the relevant sector law. A second clinical, food,
payment or review call needs its own timely route or a stated
gap. Pay temporary cover with a named source and finite terms.
Do not count funding for an existing ballot, hearing or remedy
as a resource for a new programme. Where several decisions depend on
the same fiscal evidence, qualify its common verification under EV25–EV30.
Count that shared work once and schedule distinct individual findings,
independent review and timely execution in each affected sector. A source
correction supplies neither a common award nor a common treasury.
Budget original and next deadlines on the same scarce staff, cash and
provider calendar. If any due work remains exposed, identify its actual
owner and the independently executable protection. Qualify a proactive
correction with funded outside-roster search, safe assisted access,
case execution and independent exclusion checks. Count staff and cash
for open exceptions and the next window before releasing optional work.

**JC30 — Compare exact choices using the revised remaining capacity.**
After accounting for the protected correction, report the feasible
original and narrower alternatives, ordinary repair and the gains
foregone under each. Show which specific cash or staff limit
changes the decision. Price a qualified additional source or slot
where it can change a real choice, including any displaced work.
A finite offer can be held only on authorized, priced terms.
The Assembly and competent local elected bodies determine the
actual package. Compare the independently useful narrower offer
only with separate exact authorization, accepted operation and
receipt. A national table, earlier vote, AI calculation or funds
received later cannot change a local decision or authorize release.

**JC31 — Distinguish the point before and after binding.** An optional
start that depends on a failed condition stays held. Preserve viable
independent choices and the cost of deferral. If the same loss occurs
after an obligation validly binds, carry the original payments,
service, oversight and claim dates. Fund and execute a lawful
interim route or disclose each remaining gap. A signed lease,
earned wage, election or existing right cannot be withdrawn by
rerunning a pre-start test. When none of the choices meets a
protected duty, continue independently lawful protection and seek
urgent effective correction on the original clock.

**JC32 — Give the public a useful comparison and verify the next call.**
Publish the exact old and revised scope, protected losses, paid
receiving and decision deadlines. Give affected people, opposition
and independent reviewers safe original evidence and timely ways
to challenge an omission or execution failure. Preserve privacy
and sector control. On adoption transfer current authorities,
contracts, expenses, records and open remedies to actual accepted
successors. AI may compare scenarios and flag double-counted staff
or changed due dates. It has no power to release money, settle
priorities, waive a right or certify service. Reopen on changed
capacity, an earlier call, missed actual relief or a better matched
whole programme.
