# PS05–PS08: water, source choice and physical continuity

*Part of [C06/37](../37-physical-supply-and-paid-trade.md); design92 research.*

**PS05 — Repair and accepted reuse before additional withdrawal.** Public water
stewardship joins catchment/downstream rights, household/service/farm customers,
network condition, sanitation, treatment and compatible receiving uses. Reduce
actual avoidable losses and use safely accepted nonpotable streams where their
whole costs/risks are preferable. Recovery is not automatically reuse: constrain
it by quality, customer need, season, conveyance and storage. No food-washing or
potable substitution is inferred from a large treated-water volume.

The reference separates farm20% hypothetical captured return from other80% return and limits reuse to
2m m³ farm plus1m industry, subject to actual compatible demand. Import-heavy
farming reduces the usable farm sink; keeping3m reuse would fabricate supply.
Freshwater, sewage, treatment loss, final discharge, reuse and network loss have
separate balances. Leakage, brine and discharge are no guaranteed ecological flow.

**PS06 — Compare real additional sources.** Domestic desalination, paid bulk
imports, accepted conservation and changed voluntary production remain rivals.
Each needs source title/contract, quality, treatment, energy, capacity, staff,
imports, ecological effects, commissioned access and due cash. Desalination
requires intake/outfall and brine treatment/permits; imports require actual
foreign source, shipping/pipeline, receiving terminal and payment. A smaller
domestic electricity bill does not erase foreign water/energy harm.

In the mixed reference,3.823m m³ extra annual source is required after repair/
reuse, against an authored6.5m available catchment. Three5,000m³/day trains at90%
availability supply4.928m annually; two cannot meet normal demand. Two8,000m³/day
bulk suppliers at90% supply5.256m. Neither number proves seasonal reliability,
independent routes, safety or readiness. The irrigated food rival defeats both
normal source envelopes. A20% catchment fall defeats the mixed desalination case
without waiving the ecological/standby exclusions. No plant gets automatic priority.
The unity raw-to-header yield is an authored subset assumption, not potable
quality or free treatment; actual backwash/loss, treatment and energy need review.

**PS07 — Fill and protect a stock with an explicit exposure.** The research
plan tests14 days' interruption of the additional source while other sources,
reuse and routing continue. Four50,000m³ tanks at90% usable give180,000m³;
the mixed reference needs146,624m³ for that exposure. Losing an area tank leaves
135,000m³ and fails. All-fresh-source loss needs395,939m³ and fails. No protected
claim disappears because one narrower test passed. Compound outages, unsafe
quality, locality and replenishment need their own accepted plan.

Initial tank filling200,000m³ is an extra one-time water/energy/cash requirement,
not already received reserve. The reuse project's20,000m³ nominal buffer lasts
only about2.19 days at3m annual reuse. The old0.5m m³ annual standby-flow exclusion
is not a tank, an appropriated purchase or renewable emergency permission.

**PS08 — Accountable utility operation and paid assets.** New publicly funded
essential trunk/reuse/storage assets retain public/common control and nondiscriminatory
access; accountable public or licensed operators may provide the service. Existing
private title/concessions need lawful paid migration, not retrospective conversion.
Land purchase, actual capital formation, current repair, depreciation, financing,
renewal cash and delivered tariff are different accounts. Cost-selling public
utilities are not automatically general-government nonmarket production.
Inspectors/courts remain outside the failing operator's final control; the public
steward owes an accepting paid successor/alternative under prior law. No safe
receiver or unpriced land is assumed present to make a reference pass.

[WS allocation and service](../../C09/16-water-allocation-and-service.md) retains this physical/source/ownership choice while supplying actual public roles, compatible gross/depletion/return/lag grants and attributable elected tariffs. A reused stream is not also its original downstream return; these example volumes do not become legal permits, safe water or selected customer prices.
