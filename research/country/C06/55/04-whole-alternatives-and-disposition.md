# OB13–OB16: a strong existing alternative can defeat the proposal

**OB13 — Compare complete arrangements, not a fictional weak incumbent.**

| Arrangement | What is retained / what cannot be assumed |
|---|---|
| Observed retain-and-repair | Preserve actual services, production, homes, work, trade and lawful institutions; accounts alone do not prove access, democratic control or absence of needed repair. |
| A/I05 plus protected essential services | Proposed receipt/additional-need and delivery rules face actual people, tax/source law, staff and prior obligations; no country CU cost transfers into Denmark. |
| Conditional full U+C2 with the same services | Price gross U, actual same-person displaced cash, tax/source-year effects, pension/extra needs, prices/work/care and paid transition; do not count all public services as benefits available to cancel. |
| Paid direct-public/provider alternative | Retain the same offer and due rights; real title/acquisition, recruitment, management, maintenance, overlap and public/user control are burdens, not supplier margin assumed saved. |

All actual matched incremental DKK costs/sources remain unknown. This comparison
does not rank the packages by an invented common welfare score. Actual families,
workers, users, outsiders and future people can disagree; household averages
cannot cancel an individual's rights. No affected public was consulted here.

**OB14 — Make a reasoned present disposition.** Retain NS's service-first
diversified retrofit direction and the existing income/provider/ownership rivals.
Observed coexistence of consumption, investment, trade and public provision
supports using a whole-economy baseline; it does not validate this proposed
package. Prefer preserving functioning capacity and repairing identified defects
over an unpriced general replacement. Reject per-head GDP funding, consumption-
as-total-budget, gross-surplus dividends, automatic statistical in-kind credits,
wealth-growth income and a transplanted currency environment. Select no new
tax, benefit or labor parameter from these aggregates. Simpler retain/repair
can still outperform the candidate. Better lives and meaningful democracy need
independent outcomes/control evidence, not national accounts alone.

**OB15 — Preserve scope, law and succession.** Original 37 complete function
objects/61 selections/73 inherited references, dated sources and the independent
133 u/f circuit remain exact. Actual constitution/software/contribution permissions,
holds and common method stay unchanged. Changes to actual law or resources need
competent authority and paid OP/T source/claim/record successors. Prior services,
wages/pensions/earned units, homes/title, deposits/creditors, contracts/licenses,
cases and foreign duties continue; no model or missing baseline postpones them
or an election. Public source records do not require permanent public testimony.

**OB16 — Next test the incremental choice at a usable distributional scope.**
Build one matched published household/service/earned-retirement comparison
against this macro baseline or an explicitly different price/year context. Price
one consequential A-versus-U/shared-service/provider choice, with actual coverage,
cash/receipt, gross/net tax, housing/work/care/time, legacy and transition scope;
preserve unpriced pieces and material dissent. Do not invent people, substitute
a mean for a median, pool unsafe households, or claim new authority/affected
consent from aggregate data. Finish a bounded retain/revise/reject disposition;
unsupported calibration remains unknown rather than another residual output.
Existing researchers can prepare it; actual staffing/budget/mandate are absent.
Urgent harm or larger contradiction can reprioritize. Reopen on new original
revision, distribution/access/source/control evidence or a stronger whole rival;
missing monitoring means uncertainty, not success or consent.
