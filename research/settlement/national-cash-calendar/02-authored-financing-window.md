# The same year-end balance can conceal a missed first deadline

This authored fixture compares one early and one later payment window.
It is independent of Denmark's figures. The jurisdiction uses one
eligible central cash account with an opening balance of 6 and an
adopted minimum of 2 for remaining immediate claims. The table assumes
listed receipts settle before the earliest matching payment in their
own window. All other accounts and legal entitlements remain separate.
Qualified service, court and electoral operators are assumed for the
protected case only. This test does not establish those rosters.

| Cash input or protected use | Early | Later |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ordinary receipts | 8 | 12 |
| Already authorized refinancing settlement | 4 | 0 |
| Due services, payroll, election and remedy | 10 | 8 |
| Maturing principal | 5 | 0 |

In the ordinary path, balance after due uses is 3 early and 7 later.
The budget separately identifies 1 in recurring net sources beyond
the protected base for the proposed improvements. Narrowed repair and
preparation costs 1 early, 1 later and 1 in following annual periods.
Accelerated coverage and its independent support costs 3 early,
1 later and 3 recurring. Both are prospective. The wider path
needs two further early units to preserve the cash minimum and
two additional recurring units after the transition. Moving two
later receipts earlier, if lawful and feasible, would solve only
the first gap. Changing the date does not create annual revenue.

| Test on the same due duties | Early balance | Later balance | Consequence |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Ordinary protected base | 3 | 7 | Both windows pass. |
| Narrowed repair in ordinary case | 2 | 5 | Passes cash and recurring source. |
| Accelerated coverage in ordinary case | 0 | 3 | Fails minimum early and recurring source. |
| Protected base, refinancing delayed until later | −1 | 7 | Early projected shortfall despite identical later balance. |
| Delayed refinancing, separately preauthorized early bridge of 3 | 2 | 6 | Protected base passes. Later balance includes payment of 3 principal and 1 financing cost. |

A negative projected balance is a funding gap, not an authorized
overdraft. With a bridge of 2 the projected balance is 1 early,
which misses the stated minimum even if current invoices can be
covered. The 3-unit bridge is a conditional scenario with an
independent payer and lawful repayment source. It needs its own
authorization and actually usable cash. If it fails, show the
specific exposure and preserve original claims.

Select the protected base and lawful contingent bridge. Permit the
narrowed addition only if its binding costs are also protected under
the declared stress. This bridge alone buys no early room for it
in that stress. An earlier larger prefinanced reserve or longer
debt maturity is a real structural rival. Price the holding or
interest cost and displaced uses, and keep recurring service sources
distinct. The larger offer remains valuable to those who would
otherwise wait, and needs a complete new source and capacity decision.
