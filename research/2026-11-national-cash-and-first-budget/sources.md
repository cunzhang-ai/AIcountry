# Design274 evidence and custody

- [Danmarks Nationalbank, Central government borrowing and debt 2025](https://www.nationalbanken.dk/media/q5cf5y2l/central-government-borrowing-and-debt-2025.pdf),
  pages 6 and 12 and Appendix A1–A2, supplies the account definition,
  year-end amounts and the distinct published financing figures.
  [Archived original](original-sources/dnb-government-debt-2025.pdf)
- The Danish bank's [January monthly statement](https://www.nationalbanken.dk/da/viden-og-nyheder/presse/arkiv/2025/valuta-og-likviditet-samt-maanedsbalance-januar-2025-04-02-2025)
  supplies the state deposit row for December 2024 and January 2025.
  [Archived original](original-sources/january-2025.html)
- The [March monthly statement](https://www.nationalbanken.dk/da/viden-og-nyheder/presse/arkiv/2025/valuta-og-likviditet-samt-maanedsbalance-marts-2025-02-04-2025)
  supplies February and March values.
  [Archived original](original-sources/march-2025.html)
- The [December statement and 16 January correction](https://www.nationalbanken.dk/da/viden-og-nyheder/presse/arkiv/2026/valuta-og-likviditet-samt-maanedsbalance-december-2025-05-01-2026)
  supplies November and December values and revised financing
  aggregates. Its corrected table and old narrative differ.
  [Archived original](original-sources/december-2025.html)
- The [preserved 2025 public account record](../2026-11-observed-government-baseline/sources.md)
  supplies the separate consolidated national accounts and state
  budget and cash perimeters, with exact source vintages.

The annual and monthly bank sources are one reporting family.
[Observed values](observations.json) preserve distinct reported
figures and the missing bridge. The check reads the selected
monthly balance rows from archived HTML, tests the displayed
arithmetic, and separately reproduces the authored cash case.
The sources do not supply daily unrestricted balance by payer,
actual future funding or operational receipt in the proposed country.
