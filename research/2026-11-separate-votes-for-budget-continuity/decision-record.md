# Design426. Separate a funded continuity decision from a budget impasse

## Decision and baseline

Design425 protects standing duties and a finite lapse bridge. BLC02
requires a real decision on a successor budget or separate limited
continuation by its existing milestone. BCV already protects whole
budget alternatives and separates unrelated law in principle. The
open choice is whether and how a member can put a genuinely independent
continuity appropriation to a separate decision while the full budget
is disputed. This revision was prepared with AI assistance. It does
not exercise any current parliamentary or fiscal power.

## Alternatives and selection

Keeping the whole annual package as the only decision protects
fiscal coherence and limits serial line-item bargaining. It can make
an existing service dependent on unrelated concessions. Broader
standing authority can stabilize service and reduce repeated votes.
It can also place a larger share of the budget beyond timely elected
revision and may still lack actual cash. A narrow separately approved
continuation can keep a specific service working while the elected
body remains free to reject the rest of the package.

Choose a protected exact continuation decision where there is no
operative whole budget financing the service in time. Use the actual
majority, notice, alternatives, and paid sitting and correction rules.
Keep a bounded purpose, separate source and expiry, and deduct
already committed money and work from later options. A lawful linked
source keeps its own vote. More than one vote may be needed by the
existing milestone. The Assembly must budget that work. Serial
temporary proposals can consume floor time and obscure long-run
cost. A repeat requires fresh authority, cost and reasons. Retain
the ordinary annual decision and allow the simpler route where it
provides real timely protection.

## Evidence, compatibility and authority

[Primary comparators](sources.md) illustrate interim expenditure
authority and legislative separation of expenditure limits and
appropriations. They do not establish the candidate procedure or
its political results. The worked comparison stipulates votes,
lawful presence, cash and operating deadlines. No actual voters,
workers, recipients or independent reviewers participated.

Adopt prospectively with paid members' support, protected docket
and independent execution. Preserve all existing BLC dates, prior
standing scope and settlement law. The legislature alone authorizes
the separate appropriation. Its Clerk and lawful alternate preserve
agenda access, text and actual vote records. Treasury and the service
owner verify and execute the funded scope. Courts enforce specific
duties under their own law. No presider, court, vendor or AI supplies
a missing vote. The government's confidence, legal authority and
replacement retain their own procedures. Preserve prior commitments,
accrued claims, authorized records and paid successors at cutover.
