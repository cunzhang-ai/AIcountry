# HP07–HP12. Liberty has its own source, actual clock and bodily limits

*Design147 · [Ordinary protection](../11-ordinary-health-and-hazard-protection.md) · New civil permissions are not criminal arrest or medical consent.*

**HP07 — Court first; a narrow emergency exception.** Planned nonconsensual separation
requires an ordinary civil judge's reasoned person-specific order after effective
notice, funded counsel, accessible hearing and HP06 proof. Where an actual imminent
grave transmission risk cannot await prior judicial consideration, a qualified HP01
officer may direct minimum safe separation for **at most24continuous hours** from
first actual restraint, promptly seek court and no later than six elapsed hours,
and obtain an actual meaningful independent hearing/operative order no later
than that24-hour outer. Missing the six-hour filing duty is independently
reviewable and supplies no clock reset or approval.
Earlier useful-time or stronger law wins. Filing, clinician opinion or court silence
is not an order. If no timely lawful order exists, **end compulsory separation**;
continue voluntary care/support and separately lawful protection, no new hold.

The clock begins when the person is actually not free to leave, including compelled
home stay, transport or a locked nonhospital room; changing actor/site/label cannot
restart it. A direction is not authority to use unspecified force, interrogate or
force admission for clinical treatment. Safe chosen home where workable, appropriate
noncustodial accommodation and voluntary measures precede a closed facility; no jail,
unsafe institution or abusive household as the default. Explain ground/duration,
contacts, counsel, source and remedy immediately in a usable form. No secret detention.

**HP08 — Exposure is not an indefinite diagnosis.** The initial judicial period
expires no later than **168elapsed hours from original first restraint**, earlier
on grounds/source expiry. Each subsequent judicial period ends at most168elapsed
hours after its actual fresh decision, with current evidence and supported narrower
alternatives; no future time bank. A fresh prospective decision after a gap cannot
cure the unlawful interval or reset the original actual-restraint ledger.
Exposure-only quarantine for the same episode has an absolute **336aggregate
operative-hour** ceiling (14×24), or the shorter scientifically supported actual
risk window measured from the last verified relevant exposure, not late intake.
Administrative waiting or refusal of a test cannot extend it.
New clinical evidence of established current grave transmissibility may support
the infection-based source, counting original related restraint toward HP09; it
is not a new clean slate. Actual distinct later exposure needs independent factual
proof, not renamed speculation, custodial cohorting or another model prediction.

**HP09 — Prolonged infection-based separation has an escalated burden.** Before any
compulsory separation beyond28elapsed days from the original related first restraint (gaps do not move that date),
and for each later continuation, use OJ's **three-qualified-judge ordinary appellate
formation with two actual assents of the fixed three**, independent of the
challenged issuing judge/custodial chain. Its lawful assignment may recur; each
judgment needs fresh actual review, not transferred approval or three new fictional
judges every week.
An ordinary single judge cannot authorize effect beyond that28-day boundary.
Every escalated order ends at most168elapsed hours after its actual decision, with earlier release whenever
risk/necessity fails. Obtain current qualified opinions including an actual expert
outside the issuing/custodial control, effective patient-directed counsel/counter-
expertise, meaningful direct hearing and reasons why less intrusive protection
still fails. Preserve disagreements; two AI outputs or nominally separate employers
do not prove independent source. Old diagnosis/failed access or medicine refusal
cannot bear the authority's current burden.

Exposure-only limits count all related actual compelled health-restraint hours,
including an invalid interval, not only hours the officer chooses to call quarantine.
No universal biological maximum duration is assumed for established ongoing grave
transmissibility. Continuing weekly restrictive orders may nevertheless last a long
time: a material loss of liberty and capture risk, not proof of humane control.
A hard aggregate limit is a genuine rival with possible grave transmission after
release. The selected court route does not license psychiatric/behavioral detention,
force a cure or detain someone until agreeing to a test. Missed renewal/escalation
ends this compulsory source; no judicial vacancy/appeal target extends it.

**HP10 — A bodily procedure remains a different act.** Offer diagnostic examination,
samples, vaccination, prophylaxis, decontamination and treatment under ordinary
informed supported clinical consent, RP/child/UA safeguards and applicable refusal.
This Act supplies **no physically forced examination/sampling, vaccination or
treatment over actual objection**, even if a court permits separation. Refusal alone
is neither proof of infection nor an offense. Preserve a real non-test route to
challenge/release through current evidence, narrower protection and the actual
risk/outer window. Isolation must not become “until you consent”; even lawful
alternatives may pressure choice, an explicit retained coercion risk.
Genuinely unknown-will clinical care
uses its separate actual narrow source; HP is not a UA override or a clinical trial.
Noncontact/future methods still require actual lawful purpose/privacy/meaning and
consent where applicable; “noninvasive” does not automatically mean unregulated.

**HP11 — Clinical choice and life continue inside protection.** Provide safe appropriate
conditions, independent clinical care, communication/private counsel, chosen-person
contact, food/water/sanitation, disability/child support, actual outside review and
care that is not conditioned on agreeing with the health authority. A family member
is not automatic representative; children have their own voice/representation.
Separate infection protection, indicated hospital care, adult choice, public-health
law and actual criminal custody. No double isolation bill, automatic migration
case or earned-status loss. Restrictive conditions count in the original actual
record, including general measures that function as person-specific confinement.

**HP12 — Independent appeal and release.** The person/authorized supporter can seek
immediate ordinary liberty/rights review at any time without internal exhaustion;
ordinary OJ appeal and B17 urgency apply, no30-day waiting allowance. Hearing must
be meaningful with confidential counsel and injury/conditions inquiry; safe remote
participation needs actual equivalence, not a failed connection. No automatic stay
prolongs detention or waives the public burden. Independent source/custodian records
show actual start, order, next review, release and accepted voluntary/clinical
handoff. Release means actual freedom from the expired source, not transfer into
another undeclared locked place or abandonment without due protection/support.
