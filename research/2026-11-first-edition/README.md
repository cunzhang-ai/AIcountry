# AIcountry · Edition 1

*Design548 · Frozen English research edition · 14 November 2026*

AIcountry is a limited constitutional parliamentary democracy with
enforceable rights, funded common guarantees, elected local self-government
and a plural economy. AI assists under accountable public authority.
People retain ordinary choices about their lives and associations.
