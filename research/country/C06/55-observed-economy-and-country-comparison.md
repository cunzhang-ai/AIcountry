# An observed economy and a fair country comparison

*Part of [C06](../C06-public-finance.md); design 134, 3 November 2026.
OB01–OB21 research selection; no foreign law, mandate or entitlement transplanted.*

**Retain service-first diversified retrofit, now tested against an observed
whole-economy accounting baseline.** Denmark 2023 shows public/private consumption,
investment, outside trade, redistribution and financial positions together.
It does not demonstrate this proposal's affordability, service quality or
superiority. Retain-and-repair remains a strong live alternative, with its actual
institutions and obligations rather than a deliberately weak comparator.

and disclose unsuccessful archived-financial retrieval. The core June 2026
production/income tables and separate fiscal/financial companions are not silently
declared one identical vintage. No new fictional producer or residual source.

interfaces, later obligations, actual rights and common research method survive.
