# AL11–AL15. Learning must fit a life and survive failure

*Part of [lifelong learning](../22-lifelong-learning-and-renewal.md).*

**AL11 — Instruction, protected work time and living income differ.** AL's64-hour
offer creates no automatic extra64 paid-leave hours or full previous-wage
stipend. TW13 retains its actual employer/operator-funded64 proportionate paid
learning hours and ordinary instruction duties. An episode can satisfy both
only within genuine voluntary compatible purposes, people, dates and hours:
count the learner/teacher/replacement once and record actual funding shares.
Do not silently shift an employer's required instruction cost to the public
payer or substitute employer compliance training for person-chosen learning.

Use flexible/part-time/evening/accessible modes where useful, not an assumed
free evening or coerced carer. I05/SD actually usable income and separately due
housing/care/disability/clinical/journey rights continue under their own law;
student status does not remove ordinary personal protection. N training salary,
TW leave, EB replacement and Y credits keep exact eligibility/tax/receipt and
same-period rules, no invented full wage, double stipend or worked credit.
Longer protected release/replacement funding needs actual WL/N contract or
competent prospective law, not employer permission to decide public eligibility.

**AL12 — Necessary learning costs are not a tuition debt trap.** Public accepted
first/renewal plans and the AL02 floor fund necessary ordinary tuition, standard
materials/equipment access, qualified teaching/assessment, required placement
supervision, language/disability support and actual necessary learning journeys/
care access not already paid for that same component. Pay actual provider/support
costs through lawful source, with safe advance/direct receiving where needed.
A person is not required to borrow to exercise the included public offer.
Optional premium amenities/private extras and rejected duplicate qualifications
are not automatically public costs; give reasons and a real usable alternative.

Do not deduct tuition, a nominal free place, unreceived transport or someone
else's grant from personal cash as if received living resources. I05 same-
component lawful credits and actual custody remain; AL creates no new household
means test or mandatory family contribution. Required productive apprentice/
placement work keeps actual WL pay/safety/voice; a 'learner' label cannot supply
free service labor. Demonstrated supervised output counts net once under N05.

**AL13 — Future learning methods stay possible.** Shared practice, workplace/
community projects, remote or embodied simulation, unfamiliar media and optional
AI can serve learning where actual competence, access, understanding and safety
justify them. No mandatory single mediator or personal capability graph. HC
retained/transfer/fallback competence checks remain proportionate and safe;
assisted task completion is no independent mastery. Do not withdraw necessary
assistive tools or test dangerous live work to prove independence. A course
record authorizes no agent to consent, change career or perform a reserved act.

**AL14 — Interruption, transfer and provider failure retain a receiver.** A plan
names paid continuity/teach-out, recognised transferable work, safe records,
actual alternative and review. Illness, care, disability, inaccessible delivery
or supplier failure do not automatically consume first/renewal rights or force
repayment of properly received ordinary teaching. Voluntary switch/withdrawal
uses supported revision and real recognised learning; it supplies no new parallel
full plan or automatic repeated public purchase. Genuine disputed progress/
misrepresentation uses fair evidence/reply and proportionate correction, not a
benefit sanction. Academic non-qualification remains distinct from poverty,
unlawful refusal and unsafe provider failure. Necessary licensing standards stand.

**AL15 — Reach actual outside correction.** People can challenge public eligibility,
suitability, time, inaccessible provision, assessment, exclusion, paid support
and non-delivery through actual AP/education-inspection/OJ jurisdictions with
safe evidence and competent independent academic assistance. The implicated
provider/funder/board is not final judge of its own conduct. Inspector own-office
complaints retain ED05's actual outside court route; no universal inspector can
certify every specialty. Ordinary courts remedy rights/procedure within actual
power, not award unproved qualifications or appropriate unknown budgets.
Identify paid counsel, executor, refund/source and accepting learning/support
receiver. A successful decision without useful received correction is still
failed protection; no new custody, attendance offence or coercive training power.
