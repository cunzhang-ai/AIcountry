# IL01–IL04: lawful information for a narrow situation, no secret-service mandate

*Part of [C10/10](../10-civilian-assessment-and-intelligence-limits.md); design102 research selection, not enacted law.*

**IL01 — Narrow positive task.** The Cabinet's National Assessment Unit (NAU) may produce civilian assessments and warnings for the Cabinet, affected lawful sector authorities and the Assembly security committee about (a) credible external armed attack or coercion and civilian exposure, (b) grave cross-border cyber, service or supply disruption, and (c) the material interaction of those risks with civilian physical hazards and the country's ability to protect people and lawful institutions. It may analyze whether a threat exists, uncertainty, dependencies and lawful protective options. It does not decide individual guilt, eligibility, service access, policing, force, military action or public speech. Opposition, criticism, migration, labor organizing and unpopular belief are not threat predicates. “Security”, “foreign influence” and a minister's request alone are insufficient task grounds.

**IL02 — Defined public task, not an open source licence.** Subject to actual enactment, IL01 plus IL03–IL10 is NAU's PD04(d) public-task basis for the enumerated categories, purposes, recipients, limits and review; it creates no P07 search warrant. An analyst may use authenticated public institutional reports, lawfully published information relevant to a specific IL01 situation, diplomatic information both lawfully held and lawfully disclosed for this task, and narrowly volunteered first-party observations. A public webpage, overseas server, foreign identity, vendor contract or statement that a source volunteered is not permission to build person dossiers. Where an observation contains a third person's data, NAU records its provenance and tests its own lawful necessity and any source's lawful disclosure basis before using it. Ordinary, data and source-specific restrictions continue.

**IL03 — Necessary identifiable data.** The presumption is an aggregate situation, institution or official-role assessment. Record a person's identity in an ordinary task only on checked original factual grounds making that identity necessary to understand a specified IL01 operation or warn a competent lawful receiver, and include contrary material and uncertainty. A public official's acts in official capacity can be evaluated; unrelated family, health, location, associations and private life cannot be imported by that status. PD06 sensitive or proxy data (including political affiliation, religion, biometrics, precise location or inferred mental state) needs a separately stated, specific and strictly necessary protective ground for that item; mere source availability, party loyalty, broad risk analytics or a model score is insufficient. Do not rank citizens, journalists, dissidents or applicants by political/social worth.

**IL04 — Authorized collection ceiling.** NAU may request a scoped voluntary institutional or first-party report and may receive a transfer another public body can lawfully make under its own purpose, minimization and recipient rules. No person is compelled by NAU to answer. NAU has no authority to subpoena private records; buy or broker private dossiers; deploy covert identities, paid informants or domestic infiltration; intercept communications; enter homes, devices or networks; collect persistent location/biometrics; conduct mass scraping or person-level profiling; or direct a platform or partner to do any of those. An offer of employment, benefit, contract or safe passage cannot be conditioned on “voluntary” intelligence disclosure. Ordinary C10 cyber incident coordination may handle technical recovery within its own law but supplies no NAU collection power.

For the narrow IA04/IL05/IL06 **credible imminent grave-risk referral**, a minimum identifying fact may be recorded provisionally on IL08/PD04(e)’s genuine vital-interest basis when consent/verification cannot safely be awaited, including a single-origin report. Mark the identity/claim unverified, state the actual credibility/necessity reason and any separate PD06 sensitive-item ground, and transmit only the necessary fact to the named independently lawful emergency receiver. Complete the source check or mark unresolved with reasons within24actual hours under IL05; IL09’s original receipt/purpose/disposal remains. This is no ordinary person file, covert collection, corroboration by model agreement, warrant/crime predicate or adverse decision. A protected identity/belief or unsupported danger score alone cannot trigger it.

The ordinary identity gate requires substantive checking of original facts, source authenticity and the identity’s relevance to a justified IL01 situation, not a checklist saying that someone made the allegation. A sufficiently verified single original may establish an official-role fact; two witnesses are not an automatic condition, and repeated paraphrases do not verify it. A disputed unverified identity allegation is handled only by the narrow sealed-source/referral/clarification route, not retained as an ordinary person assessment. The distinct provisional imminent-life exception above does not relax this ordinary gate.
