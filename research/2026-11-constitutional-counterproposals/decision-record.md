# Design334 decision on initiative counterproposals

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design333, CRV01–CRV05, A9, RR and the actual referendum-law
candidate. The prior design separates independent changes, protects
the exact public initiative and leaves collisions to other applicable
law. Choose a narrow paired procedure for one independently qualified
Assembly alternative that genuinely conflicts with a direct initiative.
Use two independent approvals and a conditional public preference.
The project assistant authors the choice. Only legitimate predecessor
adoption can confer authority.

## Alternatives and evidence

Compare raw affirmative totals, default initiative priority whenever
both pass, and a separate conditional preference. Raw totals combine
different question participation and approval routes. Initiative
priority protects public agenda setting but can prevent electors
choosing a better Assembly text after both qualify. Select separate
approval and a conditional preference, with initiative priority for
a verified tie or no valid preference votes. This adds voter and
administrative effort. Protect the initiative's date against late or
strategic alternatives, and distinguish a lost count from abstention.

The [primary comparators](sources.md) show a three-question ballot
and different statutory conflict rules. They do not establish the
candidate's outcomes or workability.

## Authority, migration and reopening

The prospective amendment authorizes limited pairing, protected
deadlines, independent review and the narrow repeat. Each text
needs its own prior-law authorization, and each operative date
keeps RR and any affected-public assent. Fund equal opposition,
accessible ballots, record custody and useful-time review.
An ordinary review of a count supplies no amendment or executive
postponement. Live proposals stay governed by their own authority.

Test accessible paired and separate instructions with consenting
electors, including voters who reject both or leave a question blank.
Rehearse a late rival, a tied preference and a lost source with
independent reviewers and a funded repeat. Compare costs, comprehension
and unequal campaign access. Reopen on delay manipulation, a blocked
public initiative, misread dependencies or a better lawful rival.
Actual adoption, voter participation, court work and outcomes remain open.
