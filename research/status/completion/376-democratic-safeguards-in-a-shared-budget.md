# Design468 completion finding

The [shared budget case](../../settlement/democratic-safeguards-in-a-shared-budget/01-funded-on-paper-and-blocked-in-use.md)
compares full separation, common dependence and shared routine
support with independently controlled evidence, execution and next
payroll. A court can rule while its order remains unexecuted. A
competent staffing loss is distinct from deliberate withholding.

Working provisions and bounded arithmetic are checked. Actual
independence, paid authority, oversight and better life outcomes
remain open.
