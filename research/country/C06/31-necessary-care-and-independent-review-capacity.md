# C06. Necessary care and genuinely independent review capacity

*Integrated design 82 · Consumer of [UA01–UA20](../C08/23-necessary-care-and-unknown-will.md); research, not a funded increment.*

The Assembly enacts exact residual authority, public obligations and bounded
appropriations. Clinical/local care owners retain delivery; an ordinary court
applies the specific law, not coverage/tax policy. Existing E/I/S payment and
receiving duties do not create consent, while B23's asset authority remains separate.

| Existing owner | Required actual role/resource, attributed once |
|---|---|
| L07 clinical/care | Real communication support, proposing assessment, independent clinical/functional review, qualified treatment/care and monitoring shifts |
| D11 justice/legal help | Independently assigned advocate/counsel, expert evidence, ordinary and appeal hearings, review and received remedy |
| D10 civil/agency | Scoped personal orders and separately justified B23 custody, no general guardianship |
| D02/D03 | Actual supported-agency proof/accommodations, no diagnostic incapacity designation |
| L06/L10 | Safe direct payment, accepted home/community place, transport/utilities and continuity |
| L08/L02 when actually needed | Palliative/serious-illness and mental-health expertise; unknown will activates no diagnosis-specific coercion |
| R04/D07/K01 | Treasury/source custody, merit/procurement/qualified alternates, protected original records and vendor exit |
| D12 only separate lawful acts | Actual rescue/safety/criminal authority with its own grounds/clocks; police are not default care providers |

Count distinct proposer, independent assessor, advocate/counsel, ordinary/appeal
judge, actual receiver, care shifts and source/payment verification. A prescribing
hour is not simultaneously independent assessment. A CJ reserve is not an
available ordinary judge when already assigned; MB status/protection and P09
liberty duties keep their own paid coverage and shorter clocks. Different app
labels, signatures or common-model restatements do not establish independence.

A named legal-aid authority assigns advocates through published rotation/recusal,
with lawful career/discipline protection, direct record access, bounded standing
pay and independent challenge outside a provider's control. Clinical independence
needs actual relevant competence, source access and material-control review.
If an independent role cannot be staffed, use a qualified lawful alternate;
a relative or the interested provider cannot certify its own independence.

Public clinical/care authorities contract and pay under their own scope; do not
invent the person's agreement to private debt or compulsory asset liquidation.
Separate commitment, verification, Treasury payment, custody and receipt under
existing C06 rules. E15 performs an exact due record/payment act, not treatment
consent, judicial selection or a new appropriation. An order, referral or paid
invoice is not actual safe care or accepted next-shift handoff.

All incremental quantity/cost/source, qualified simultaneous coverage, safe places,
clinical protocols, actual acceptance and received remedy remain unknown. Existing
37-function bid amounts/components stay unchanged and unqualified for these new
duties. SF remains its single old-scope stock; recompute any additional adopted
protected-period dues and demonstrate an actual source, without diverting client,
pension/insurance property or counting standby twice. No hypothetical AI saving,
future liability recovery or family labor supplies that source.

If review is missed, UA16's separate minimum dependent-treatment continuity
prevents expiry-triggered serious harm, with immediate application/daily checks
and no new escalation. Missed24/72-hour review is a breach, not timely protection
or wider permission; prolonged unconsented continuity is a retained cost.
Statutory basic care stays within its own narrow grounds;
new consequential authority does not appear. Before an order expires arrange safe
lawful continuity and name the still responsible receiver. Missing staff/cash,
refusal, actual bodily risk and lawful lack of authority are different failures.
Report unmet claims and lawful elected resource choices; do not solve the budget
by restraint, unreviewed life-support withdrawal or declaring a dependent life
less valuable. [Transition](../C08/23/08-transition-rivals-and-reopening.md)
preserves old claims and actual law until compatible authorized migration.

[NP](33-protection-production-and-national-expansion.md) now includes these
actual later duties in the national basic/continuity and cohort schedule. No
new independent shift or payer source is supplied by an old payroll share.
The core is task-bounded, not every institutional invoice; existing rights,
professional safety and source-law payment priority remain distinct.
