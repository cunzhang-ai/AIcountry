# Finite resources, mineral rights and the public inheritance

Design167 · NR01–NR16 · [C09 land/ecology](../C09-land-and-ecology.md)

**Select public/common custody of legitimately public or genuinely ungranted
in-situ geological resources, with finite plural extraction rights and a real
no-extraction option.** Existing property, water/environment, tax and safety law
does not itself select mineral title, access/award, depletion returns or mine-
closure succession. This adds those positive choices, not an assumed deposit,
resource-rich country, new resource tax or ready source of national welfare.

keep inherited private rights plus regulation/tax, direct public production,
repair/reuse/trade and leaving deposits in place as substantive alternatives.
Public title cannot prove impartial management or buy ecological/collective
rights; a mine closure bond is neither restored land nor a liability ceiling.
Actual deposits, titles, costs/receipts, affected authority, safe operation and
superior lives remain unknown. All new publication is English research, not law.
