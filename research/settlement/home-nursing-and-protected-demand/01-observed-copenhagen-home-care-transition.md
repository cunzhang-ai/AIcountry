# Copenhagen 2026. Home care responsibility, spending and teams moved on different clocks

The Copenhagen care administration reports that home care, home nursing
and the specified assessment and aids work transferred into the Health
and Care Administration on 1 June 2026. It reports roughly 3,900
additional residents needing home care, nursing or both, and describes
additional assistive-device responsibility separately. Approximately
700 employees and managers transferred, including hourly workers and
trainees. The headcount is neither full-time equivalents nor a count
of staff qualified and available for a particular visit. The former
Social Administration teams were reported to continue their work
through the operating integration planned for 3 May 2027. Children's
home care remains with the Social Administration under the stated
boundary. The planned operating date is 336 elapsed days after the
reported transfer. It is a target rather than an observed completion.
[Care administration's transfer and scope report](https://sundudvalg.kk.dk/om-forvaltningen/hjemmepleje-og-sygepleje/ressortaendringer-der-vedroerer-suf)

The 22 April implementation record estimated a budget transfer of
DKK 721.4m in 2026 and DKK 714.2m from 2030, a DKK 7.2m difference
attributed to expiring appropriations. It excludes certain corrections
planned for a separate amendment. The committee took note of the
implementation point and recommended the budget point. The figures
are estimates in that transfer scope, without observed payments or
verified first-day shifts.
[Social Committee implementation and economic section](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Socialudvalget/m%C3%B8de-22042026/referat/punkt-6)

The May-based forecast was before the transfer in its scope and
was considered on 3 June. It reported DKK 120.0m projected pressure
on ordinary operations and a separate DKK 24.1m on the transferred
scope. The latter is DKK 18.5m for social home care, mainly pressure
in nursing, and DKK 5.6m for assistive devices. The department
reported roughly 8% more residents needing nursing services and
greater time per resident with more complex work. The committee
text gives no matched cohort denominator or individual visit record.
The DKK 24.1m is an expected variance, distinct from the planned
transfer envelope. The two figures do not constitute a revised
appropriation or a paid source.
[Social Committee forecast, service and aid lines](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Socialudvalget/m%C3%B8de-03062026/referat/punkt-2)

These records give a more detailed service-side reference for the
[same-year city budget](../service-headroom-and-usable-budget/README.md).
They add a real transfer scope, staffing count and documented
demand pressure. They do not provide the simultaneous March shifts,
specific care receipt, or performance through a shared failure.
They concern a change inside the municipality, separate from
[the planned 2027 transfer to the region](../public-service-transfer/01-copenhagen-health-transition.md).
The strong comparator is the reported staged local arrangement,
whose actual protection remains to be assessed.

[First-year obligations](../first-year-obligations-and-receiving/01-common-opening-and-overlapping-care.md)
keeps the municipal integration and separate regional duties on one
calendar, with independent receiving through a common outage.
