# 220. Copenhagen emergency funding and usable restoration

| Owner | Continuing or new duty | Acceptance or failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Treasury, city payer and regional operator | Trace the same lawful funding from grant through actual payment | Record original purpose, overlapping entries and operator receipt |
| Utility and affected elected bodies | Establish the exact network objective and operating commitments | Confirm authority and real restoration at receiving locations |
| Care and local delivery owners | Maintain drinking water, sanitation and paid care before restoration | Verify actual receiving, replenishment and the delayed case |
| Independent review | Check accepted scope and unmet needs | Keep actual missed windows visible and challenge accessible |
