The question is how the C08/C10 democratic state deals with **credible serious
wrongdoing by its own protectors**, including police, prison, border,
intelligence, emergency and contracted custodial actors. Design 20 said
investigators and charging decisions must be outside the implicated command.
It did not yet assign original evidence custody, conflict substitution,
medical care, noncharge review and actual remedy execution through the first
hours of a hostile or politically sensitive case. Without those, a ban on
torture or unlawful detention may remain a statement rather than an
enforceable restriction on state power.

**Values:** bodily safety, liberty, equal protection, peaceful opposition,
trustworthy public service, fair treatment of accused staff and actual
redress. **Constraints:** no retroactive crime; warrants, evidence, defense,
privacy, labor rights and public claims continue; independent staff and
cash cannot be invented by a trigger. **Empirical claims unknown here:**
frequency and causes of abuse, reliability of incident reports, whether
external investigators are independent, and whether the route reduces
harm without disabling lawful protection. **Design hypothesis:** preassigned
external custody, investigation and prosecution plus separate service/civil
remedies make suppression and impunity harder. Shared controllers, hidden
records or no receiving capacity could defeat it.

## Competing diagnoses and institutional packages

| Package | Mechanism | Serious failure or cost |
|---|---|---|
| Retain current outside-command wording with internal professional standards | A well-led agency can learn quickly, preserve operational expertise and discipline staff; ordinary prosecutors handle serious offenses. | The alleged actors may control scene, colleagues, logs and access before outside review begins. Internal clearance cannot determine criminal guilt or substitute for independent remedy. |
| One powerful independent protector investigates, prosecutes, suspends and compensates | Concentrated authority may act quickly against a captured police hierarchy. | If its appointments, funds or case filters are captured, every remedy fails together. It can also target opposition or freeze lawful safety work through accusations. Calling it independent does not prove it. |
| Direct elected-executive control of each serious investigation | Voters can remove leaders who tolerate abuse and set public-safety priorities. | Cabinet can protect loyal officers or prosecute critics; electoral victory does not supply impartial case proof. A new executive could reverse a pending case after an election. |
| **Selected: plural, preassigned case chain under elected general law** | Multiple safe reporting routes, prompt evidence preservation, external investigation, conflict-tested prosecution, courts, independent medicine/defense, civil relief and public learning hold separate mandates. | Requires specialized people, protected records and money. The same patron may still control formally separate offices; delay or excessive interim restrictions can harm both public and officers. |

This selection does not make all complaints criminal cases. Lower-level
mistakes may need correction, compensation or discipline rather than
prosecution; credible allegations of grave harm demand a route capable of
criminal investigation. The chosen model could lose to simpler independent
ordinary prosecution where actual institutions already have distinct
controllers and usable capacity. A later affected public must decide the
legal and distributive tradeoff, not an AI-generated score.

## Proposed authority and evidence path

1. **Safety and entry.** Any person may seek immediate medical, legal or safe
   contact through a public service, court, ombud or prosecutor without
   applying to the implicated command. A death or serious injury in custody
   or after force, or a missing detainee triggers a mandatory incident record
   and notice to the law-assigned external investigator. A person can report
   torture, retaliation or evidence destruction through any independent route;
   the implicated command cannot filter the account by labeling it incredible.
   Hospitals, courts and custodial inspectors can give independent notice.
   An initial legal hold on relevant evidence is distinct from an intrusive
   search requiring its own lawful grounds and court control. Triggering
   investigation is not a finding of guilt.
2. **Preservation and access.** First responders protect life, secure the
   scene and record actual custody start, force, personnel, weapons and
   medical transfers. Original video, logs, devices, communications,
   dispatch records and conflicting accounts receive time-stamped, audited
   custody under prior law. The implicated command and vendor cannot be the
   only custodians or erase an unfavorable source. The external service has
   direct statutory access and an urgent judicial route to compel preservation
   or production, with obstruction and irretrievable loss recorded even if
   no named officer can yet be blamed. Independent forensic and clinical
   personnel can examine evidence with privacy and chain-of-custody
   safeguards. The accused retains meaningful defense access to material
   evidence; witness protection cannot make guilt rest on unchallengeable
   material. Missing evidence is itself examined, not automatically treated
   as proof that a named officer committed the underlying harm.
3. **Assignment and conflict.** Statute specifies the external investigative
   service's jurisdiction and a prosecutor outside the implicated command.
   A documented conflict activates a predesignated alternate region or
   office, with source records transferred under audit; cabinet cannot
   select a convenient substitute after seeing the allegation. The assignment
   or denial of recusal is challengeable before a court, and accusations
   against investigative or prosecutorial staff follow the same external
   chain rather than being closed by their own office. Prosecutors
   decide charges and give reviewable reasons for noncharge, diversion or
   delay. Courts decide warrants, detention, admissibility, guilt and legal
   remedies; an appeal can correct a failure to investigate or apply law
   but cannot compel conviction. If all available offices share effective
   control, this is an unresolved constitutional failure, not a case won
   by a paper transfer.
   Prior law also allocates judges, tests recusal, supplies a substitute and
   appeal, and separates a judicial-corruption investigation from fair
   professional discipline. Neither cabinet nor prosecutor may replace a
   judge in a named case, and discipline cannot secretly revise a judgment.
4. **Interim powers and continuing duties.** An employer or court may
   temporarily restrict weapons, custody, case records or witness contact
   under enacted grounds, written reasons, narrow duration and prompt
   independent review. Replacement staff keep the public service lawful.
   The accused keeps pay and employment process as law provides; allegations
   alone do not establish guilt, justify indefinite suspension or make
   political speech criminal. Victims receive care, protection and counsel
   according to need, independent of willingness to testify or a final
   charge. Protecting a witness cannot suppress exculpatory evidence.
5. **Judgment, payment and learning.** Criminal and disciplinary processes
   use their own standards and appeals. A civil or administrative order for
   release, correction, treatment or compensation names the responsible
   state/body, payer, executor, due time and proof of actual receipt. The
   state cannot force an injured person to collect solely from an insolvent
   officer; lawful recovery from a wrongdoer is a separate matter. An
   adverse budget or cabinet order cannot close the claim by changing a
   case status. Aggregate force, death, complaint, charge, acquittal,
   disciplinary and remedy data can inform Assembly policy and public
   scrutiny with protected identities and small-group reidentification checks.
   Neither case count nor conviction rate alone measures safety or fair justice.

The case chain requires pre-enacted appointment, removal, budget, source-data,
forensic, court, payment and appeal rules. Investigators need authority to
reach police and contractor evidence; prosecutors need protection against
case-specific ministerial instructions and a lawful substitute if conflicted;
defense and harmed people need independent access; courts need an executable
order. An elected Assembly can revise general policy, scrutinize aggregate
failure and fund the institutions but cannot command the result of an
individual case. An ordinary officer's lawful response to immediate danger
remains possible within enacted necessity and proportionality limits.

## Candidate offense boundary, not an enacted code

The [historical core criminal draft](../2026-09-readiness/source-documents.json)
already proposes general killing, injury, sexual violence, confinement,
property, fraud, bribery and digital offenses with **provisional numeric
maxima**. Design 21 neither ratifies those numbers nor silently rewrites
prior cases. The current C08 penal model still requires a complete code.
For serious public-power abuse, the legislative drafting package must
distinguish at least the following without criminalizing a mere policy error:

| Candidate category | Conduct and fault that law must settle | Safeguard against overreach |
|---|---|---|
| Torture and grave official ill-treatment | Intentional severe pain or suffering by, at the instigation of, or with the consent/acquiescence of a public actor for a prohibited purpose, with the exact protected scope and proof elements enacted. | No emergency or superior instruction justifies torture; an accusation still requires lawful proof. Less severe misconduct needs a separate proportionate route. |
| Unlawful official force, killing or detention | Existing general violence/homicide law applies; any official-specific offense defines knowing or reckless excess of lawful power, causation and harm. Detention must have an enacted basis and recorded start. | Necessary and proportionate lawful force is not made criminal by an adverse outcome alone. Mistake, negligence, recklessness and intent are distinguished. |
| Evidence obstruction and retaliation | Intentional material falsification, destruction or concealment to impede a lawful investigation, or targeted retaliation for protected reporting, defense or civic participation. | Good-faith correction, protected confidentiality and ordinary supervision are not labeled obstruction by a hostile prosecutor. |
| Corruption and diversion | An official or entrusted contractor solicits or accepts an improper benefit for misuse, or deliberately diverts public assets or a contract; the old bribery proposal is a starting point. | Conflict disclosure or an unpopular lawful contract is not itself guilt; beneficial control and material exchange need proof. |
| Participation and command | Ordering, assisting, concealing or culpably failing to perform a specific legal duty to stop a known offense under enacted elements. | Rank, association, institutional failure or AI prediction alone is insufficient for personal criminal liability. |

For each category the Assembly must publish conduct, mental state,
participation, causation, lawful defenses, maximum sanction, jurisdiction,
limitation and transition before prosecution. The prosecution bears the
burden of proof beyond reasonable doubt and must disclose exculpatory
material. Civil correction and employment discipline remain available on
their own lawful standards when criminal proof is absent; they cannot
secretly impose a criminal punishment without trial. Serious offenses by
non-officials receive equal protection and fair process; focusing this
decision on state actors does not make other harms unimportant.

The [Convention against Torture](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-196818/)
informs the prohibition, criminalization and prompt impartial investigation
of torture. The [ICCPR](https://2covenants.ohchr.org/About-ICCPR.html)
states effective remedies even for official acts, liberty and fair-trial
requirements. The [Minnesota Protocol](https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/Documents/Publications/MinnesotaProtocol.pdf)
is primary guidance for potentially unlawful-death investigation and
source custody. The [Istanbul Protocol](https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/documents/publications/2022-06-29/Istanbul-Protocol_Rev2_EN.pdf)
informs independent medical documentation. The [UN prosecutor guidelines](https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/prosecutors.pdf)
and [force principles](https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/Documents/ProfessionalInterest/firearms.pdf)
inform separation, impartiality and lawful-force review. The
[Bangalore Principles](https://www.unodc.org/pdf/crime/corruption/judicial_group/Bangalore_principles.pdf)
inform the judicial-conflict and integrity question. Their legal
status and applicability in any founding jurisdiction require actual
review; none proves this proposed chain operational or supplies a sentence.

## Compatibility, objections and transition

This choice develops C08/C10, proposed A7 and founding exercise F6. It
depends on C03's independent courts and peaceful opposition, C04's career
service and local/national allocation, C06's funded remedy account, C07's
care, C11's protected source records, C12's constrained AI, and C14's
handover. The old coercive-evidence exclusion and custody-clock protections
remain; no review of them transfers automatically to these new offense or
institutional proposals. Existing crimes, lawful convictions, investigations,
sentences, personnel rights and compensation claims survive until lawful
successors and transition provisions are enacted. New offenses cannot be
retroactively charged or used to lengthen punishment. A new investigator
cannot absorb a pending case without preserving custody, defense access and
competence review.

The first real jurisdictional account must identify all force-using bodies,
detention sites, contractors, prosecutors, doctors, forensic labs, evidence
stores, courts, defense providers, witnesses, reporting routes and public
payment operators. It must cost an out-of-command on-call team, independent
medical examination, safe transport, replacement staff, protected evidence
copies, legal aid, victim support, appeals and lawful compensation. A unit
cannot promise ordinary crime investigation and external police review with
the same unavailable investigator. The chain must work during a hostile
cabinet, vendor outage or election handover, not only after a cooperative
superior voluntarily shares records.

Material objections remain. An external unit can be captured by the same
funders, use misconduct claims to harass officers or opposition, or lose
knowledge of a local incident; mandatory notices may expose victims or
overload scarce capacity; protected secrecy can hide exculpatory material;
multiple institutions may delay urgent protection. Officers, harmed people
and the public may reasonably disagree about risk, privacy, punishment
and review. The proposal limits automatic triggers to serious events,
separates interim duty restrictions from guilt, preserves court/defense
access and protects sources. These written limits do not establish real
independence or consent. A simpler arrangement may be preferable if the
actual institutional comparison proves it can reliably investigate its own
force without capture.

**Evidence qualification:** the cited primary international texts and
local predecessor files were checked conceptually; no original AIcountry
incident, budget, affected-party testimony or independent field review.
**Research selection:** preassigned plural external case chain and candidate
official-abuse offense boundaries. **Legal authorization:** none.
**Operational readiness:** no real investigator, prosecutor, court, records,
funded medical service or alternate jurisdiction demonstrated. **Observed
outcomes:** none; a sixth authored stress case is not proof of resilience.

The next bounded test for an adopting public is one **hostile official-force
case during a disputed election**, using actual command, staffing, record,
funding and legal dependencies. Compare this route with internal review and
a single external protector. The deliverable is a timed account of the first
life-saving act, independent evidence acquisition, conflict substitution,
charging review, defense disclosure, interim staffing and civil payment,
including failures and dissent from harmed people, officers, counsel,
opposition and forensic staff. This project has no affected-party mandate
or field budget. Reopen design 21 if evidence access, independence, safety,
fair process, resources or a credible rival changes the choice; if incidents
cannot be observed independently, make no claim of accountability.
