# Design374. Qualify large electricity connections and their exit costs

[EG13–EG18](../../country/C09/19/04-large-grid-connections-and-paid-capacity.md)
prices actual load, grid and water capacity, delayed use and cancellation.
It preserves protected services, fair review and a separate elected
decision on any public share.

The [published data and worked comparison](../../settlement/large-grid-connections-and-paid-capacity/01-two-load-windows-and-an-exit.md)
distinguishes offers from metered demand, then tests a staged first phase,
a recovery window and an unfunded exit. Actual engineering, source,
affected participation, lawful adoption and delivered results remain open.
