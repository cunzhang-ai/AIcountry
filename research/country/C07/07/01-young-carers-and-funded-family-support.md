# YCR01–YCR07. Let children stop carrying essential unpaid care

*Design385 · Proposed ordinary law under [care and support through life](../07-care-and-support-through-life.md).*

**YCR01 — Give the child a direct route to help.** A person under 18
who is providing or expected to provide material care can ask for
support directly or through a safe trusted person. The local care
authority also acts on a credible concern about a concrete burden.
Schools, clinics and other authorized entry points protect first
receipt and seek accepted onward contact. No parent account, household
disclosure, diagnosis, minimum hours, absentee record or formal
identity as a carer is required for initial help. Offer an accessible
private channel and explain confidentiality and its lawful limits.
A child can choose safe support for their participation.

**YCR02 — Distinguish ordinary help from reliance on a child.** The
assessment records tasks, timing, risks, effects on sleep, education,
relationships and private time, and the child's own wishes and
support needs. Ask what would happen if they stopped today. Essential
services cannot count a child as guaranteed capacity, including a
substitute for a sibling, interpreter or worker. Do not make money,
a place or another person's care contingent on the child's labor.
Ordinary voluntary and age-appropriate help can continue where it
is safe, actually wanted and can stop without an unmet essential
duty. Family structure, disability, ordinary chores and poverty
alone do not establish neglect or unsafe care.

**YCR03 — Assign the replacement to the service that owes it.** The
local care authority coordinates, and the competent health, adult
care or child service owner supplies each task within its own remit.
Make an interim response before the earliest applicable useful time.
Follow the earlier urgent duty where safety or essential care is at
risk. For a material nonurgent case give a reasoned initial plan
within five working days of protected receipt and a separate
reviewable assessment on the applicable AP clock. Include the
responsible owner, interim help, next date and actual gaps. Preserve
ED35's earlier teaching timetable and other stronger deadlines.
A payer dispute uses a qualified AP13–AP18 route. No dispute,
assessment or failed referral compels another shift by the child.

**YCR04 — Keep two people and two sets of choices in view.** Arrange
suitable paid replacement, training for paid workers, respite or
other accepted support. The person receiving care keeps consent,
choice of lawful provider and independent review. The child keeps
their own confidential advice, safe relationships and support.
Combine assessments or plans only with appropriate separate lawful
agreement. Share the minimum needed to arrange each act. A declined
offer does not authorize forced care or make the child a provider.
Seek a feasible alternative and disclose the remaining gap. Use B31
only on its distinct grounds and safeguards. The state cannot use
a failure to supply owed care as a reason to remove a child.

**YCR05 — Restore learning and ordinary time separately.** The school
and responsible education authority give usable learning access and
any needed recovery under ED, and the care owner confirms the
protected sleep, departure or activity window freed by actual
replacement. Do not use flexible teaching or excused absence to
normalize a continuing care burden. Preserve the child's original
learning rights, safe private contact and independent complaint.
Provide eligible support to the child even if an adult declines
optional joint planning. No cash award, alternate timetable or
meeting establishes that either person's needs have been met.

**YCR06 — Fund real shifts and the next service window.** Budget the
earliest and later tasks together, including skill, travel, safe
rest, repeated calls, qualified backups, learning recovery and
independent review. Use the actual accepting provider and settled
lawful source. Check that support is received at both ends. Do not
count already allocated workers twice or move the burden to a
sibling or another household. If no safe funded route works, expose
the unmet duty, deliver independently safe help and use the urgent
independent and elected correction routes on the original dates.
An adult's plan and the child's support need their own remedies.

**YCR07 — Preserve rights through transfer and adulthood.** Offer a
paid handover before a change in school, location or age status.
Keep adult care due through the child's birthday and distinguish
continuing education and adult carer support from expiring child
measures. No adulthood threshold supplies consent or cheap labor.
Preserve pending cases, original dates and privacy. Publish safe
aggregate evidence about late or missed help and hidden coverage.
AI may flag schedule gaps or aid translation with review. It cannot
infer caregiving or consent from family, school or device data,
decide removal or certify that a child is free to stop. Adopt with
qualified local authority and funds. Reopen on stigma, coercion,
missed care, education loss or a better independently usable route.
