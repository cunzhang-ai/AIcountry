# GA01–GA06: distinguish inquiry, training, testing and exposure

*Part of [general AI capability](../10-general-ai-capability-and-public-control.md).*

**GA01 — Reach the responsible activity.** The proposed General AI Safety Act
covers public, commercial, nonprofit and community developers/controllers of a
model usable across materially different tasks, within actual domestic territory,
person or operational control. The same GA02/GA04 severe-risk gate also covers
specialized AI where no actual sector pre-exposure regime addresses that pathway
and act; narrow purpose is not a catastrophic-capability exemption. A specific
sector gate covering both takes priority for that scope, with an explicit public
allocation and equivalent evidence/review, not exemption by mere regulation.
A name change, agent swarm or split run does not evade actual control.
Contribution alone is not control.
An AI's apparent wishes, capability or copied accounts confer no public mandate.

**GA02 — Three severe-harm grounds, with a credible pathway.** Before the GA04
exposure acts, the controller must seek GA12 permission where it knows or has
reasonable documented grounds to expect that the actual configuration materially
increases practical capacity for (a) mass-casualty biological, chemical or physical
harm; (b) widespread, sustained disruption of essential life-supporting systems;
or (c) autonomous unauthorized acquisition/persistence/control of external
resources capable of producing (a) or (b). Require specific capability evidence,
practical barriers and a credible exposure/causal pathway, including coordinated
misuse, not an impressive score, speculation about intelligence or mere scale.
Political disagreement, criticism, ordinary inaccuracy, lost market share and
lawful organization are not these grounds. Other serious harms retain their laws.

Before GA04 external exposure, controllers undertake a reasonable proportionate
capability/pathway inquiry using available original results, configuration and
credible signals. Declining to document or inspect known warning evidence is no
escape; this is not a compulsory full hazard battery for every ordinary model.
For classification, the Office may require targeted relevant original evidence
on recorded plausible GA02 grounds, with scope/cost/custody challenge under GA12;
this creates no covert search, universal inspection or presumption of danger.

The Office may issue a reasoned individual classification on those same grounds,
with evidence, response and direct full-merits court challenge. It bears the
burden to justify scope and restrictions; an operator need not prove universal
safety. Evidence of new facts matters even below a compute proxy. Grounded good-
faith reliance on a written exclusion protects reliance on the disclosed facts,
not a concealed or materially changed capability. A label is not guilt.

**GA03 — Notice is a screening aid, not a training licence.** Notify planned
training expected to exceed 10^25 floating-point operations, or later discovery
that it did, within ten working days of reasonable knowledge and before GA04
external exposure. Count arithmetic training operations, not parameter count or
inference tokens; aggregate pretraining and capability-changing post-training
for the same model lineage under common control, including coordinated split
runs. Disclose known inherited training and uncertainty; do not invent unknown
predecessor compute. Independent projects are not pooled merely for sharing
research or equipment. Unknown compute is not proof of crossing the threshold.

The Office screens within30 elapsed days from protected first receipt. Its
silence does not classify a model, license it or prohibit otherwise lawful
research. GA02 facts still govern. The proxy borrows a foreign screening scale,
not a demonstrated danger discontinuity; Assembly law must change it materially.
Algorithmic efficiency and below-threshold hazards require substantive evidence,
not secret administrative expansion or counting hardware purchases as training.

**GA04 — Exposure is an act, not only a product sale.** Covered acts are providing
external model/API/tool access, publishing executable weights sufficient for
material capability, or testing/operating with access to nonconsenting persons,
live networks, physical systems or external resources. Noncommercial supply and
an internal test with live external permissions can qualify. Evaluate exact
access, tools, permissions, supervision and distribution, not just base weights.
A controllable API and freely copyable weights are distinct exposure choices.
Transfer solely to a genuinely contained evaluator under enforceable safe custody
is not, by itself, external exposure; actual external permissions still count.

**GA05 — Contained inquiry remains possible.** Lawful training, theory, ordinary
publication and genuinely contained evaluation need no GA permission merely for
size or topic. Containment must actually block the relevant external pathways,
including test-agent tools and shared infrastructure; an operator's declaration
alone does not establish it. GA02/GA04 apply before intentionally opening those
paths. Breach or threatened escape invokes GA15–GA17, not automatic confiscation
of all research. PD/KR/work/physical-safety duties apply during contained work.

**GA06 — Liability follows ability and contribution.** Identify the legal person,
actual controller, relevant suppliers, resource authority and accountable human
officer; disclose material common control. A wrapper/integrator is responsible
for its actual capability/access change, not every unknowable upstream defect.
Neither a disclaimer nor an agent mandate waives others' rights. Domestic
import/distribution/control can be governed on these grounds, not by nationality
or a claim of worldwide inspection. Ordinary users acquire no filing duty simply
by using a general assistant. Actual foreign cooperation and enforcement remain
limited; a domestic permit neither governs the world nor guarantees safety.
