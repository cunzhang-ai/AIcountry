# C08. Civil registration and proof that belongs to the person

*Design195 · VR01–VR12 · Proposed ordinary civil-status law.*

**Record life events locally under common law, issue usable proof at public
expense, and repair a wrong record through every affected service.** The
Civil Registrar records evidence and legally operative events. Courts,
clinicians, status officers and election officers retain their separate
decisions. Registration, proof, and entitlement to a service are distinct.

| Canonical part | Selected duty |
|---|---|
| [VR01–VR04: event and proof](29/01-event-and-proof.md) | Accountable registrar and local intake, immediate and late birth recording, usable proof and name/status changes |
| [VR05–VR08: death, dispute and repair](29/02-death-and-correction.md) | Keep unconfirmed deaths separate. Preserve access, repair mistaken links and trace receipt downstream. |
| [VR09–VR12: data, continuity and public control](29/03-custody-and-continuity.md) | Minimum disclosure, bounded verification, paid offline continuity and lawful transition |

The design favors one public responsibility with plural limited records,
human and paper routes, and case-specific verification. It selects neither
a universal public identifier nor routine biometric enrollment. Existing
MB, B, RP, AP and PD rights, earlier clocks and actual eligibility remain.
