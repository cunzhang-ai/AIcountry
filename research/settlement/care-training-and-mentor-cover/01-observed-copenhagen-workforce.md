# Copenhagen. Training funds and actual staffing are different records

## Recurring funding and the first-line provider

The September 2024 Health and Care Committee approved the six-part
training plan subject to a staff cooperation discussion. The municipal
record describes DKK 28.6 million per year in 2025 prices and pay levels
for the department's share of the continuing programme. The funding
route shifts from a national Finance Act project in 2021–2024 to block
grant funding incorporated in the department's budget from 2025.

The annex states a DKK 16.9 million local training budget line,
comprising DKK 11.0 million annually from the block grant and an
existing annual allocation of DKK 5.9 million stated at 2024 prices
and pay levels. This is the committee's stated composition, without
a fresh inflation adjustment in this comparison. Do not add the DKK
11.0 million to the DKK 28.6 million. The existing allocation is
distinct from the added grant share. The annex describes the line as
a contribution to the sites' existing training responsibilities, not a
complete cost or a dated mentor rota. [Committee decision and annex](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Sundheds-%20og%20Omsorgsudvalget/m%C3%B8de-19092024/referat/punkt-8)

## The output count, completion rate and employment have different bases

The December 2025 workforce status reports the following measures for
municipal social and health care education. It links the higher annual
completion count to larger intake. It separately describes using higher
intake planning to accommodate dropout.

| Reported measure | Earlier comparison | Reported 2024 status |
|---|---:|---:|
| Graduates | 376 per year, 2020–2022 mean | 426 |
| Share of training courses completed | 65%, 2019 entry and expected end in 2020 or 2022 | 46%, 2021 entry and expected end in 2022 or 2024 |
| Share hired by Copenhagen after completion | 39%, 2020–2022 | 49% |

The completion count is 50 higher than the earlier annual mean and the
completion measure is 19 percentage points below the restated baseline.
The annex uses course records, with more than one record possible for
one learner. It documents a method change from a 41% to a 65% baseline
for completion and from 55% to 39% for first municipal jobs. Use the
restated values for this comparison. The municipal hiring rate is a later transition for graduates. Do not
use the mixed periods and rounded shares to infer intake, net new paid
hours or staffing at a specific site. A first municipal job also gives
no proof of additional care received or continued employment. The report
does not establish that the new 2025 funding arrangement caused results
reported for 2024. [Annual workforce status](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Sundheds-%20og%20Omsorgsudvalget/m%C3%B8de-02122025/referat/punkt-6)

The comparison supports a separate budget for teaching sites and
workforce stages. It leaves matched mentor time, competent cover,
actual payments and service by shift open. The reported intake
strategy does not establish that the sites relied on dropout to cover
binding places. WF97 is a prospective design choice about that risk.
