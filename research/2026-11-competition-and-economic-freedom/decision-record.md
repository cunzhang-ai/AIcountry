## Diagnosis, values and coherent rivals

Usable exit, worker bargaining, plural supply, lawful cooperation, property,
speech and equal political standing are values. The design97 text explicitly
lacked operative competition thresholds/acts despite naming a regulator. That
is an observation about research text, not measured monopoly or a prediction
that every large firm harms people. Power may stem from resource scarcity,
ownership, networks, public dependence or unlawful conduct; more competition
litigation does not automatically solve all of them.

| Whole choice | Mechanism and unresolved tradeoff |
|---|---|
| Retain ordinary courts/private claims without a new positive competition rule | Limits agency discretion/filing burden, but existing B cannot invent cartel/merger elements; injured people need evidence, aid and useful time. |
| Finite statute, protected chief/career inquiry and courts | Selected: exact acts/gates, direct cases, nonpunitive ordinary correction, court-only contested merger/structural/punitive orders and bounded clocks. Paid sources/skills and capture risks remain. |

| Essential-gateway public/cooperative provision as principal architecture | May deliver a due service directly; needs paid assets/workers/rights/maintenance/loss finance and effective public replacement, and can itself coerce dissent. |

## Operative choices and their limits

MP04 defines naked rival agreements; MP05 protects genuine ventures and worker/
economically dependent solo bargaining. Similar prices/common AI are not proof.
MP07–10 needs usable substitutes, durable power, specified exclusion and real
capacity/privacy/IP defenses. Prospective12month tests do not force waiting for
harm. Public and cooperative bodies have the same applicable conduct limits.

B is M07/I02 monthly ordinary adult essential cash basket, not median pay/extras/
housing; T=12B. An Act fixes initialB; missing later publication carries the
prior lawful value and first-year events use initialB. Combined10,000T plus
500Ttarget domestic turnover is a chosen gate, not calibrated optimum. Actual
financial income excludes deposits/client flows. Prior essential-route designation
is a separate exact gate, effective90days after notice for12months with six-month
review. Binding-event/initial/deal scope is fixed prospectively; genuinely later
acquisitions do not retrospectively create gun-jump. This can miss smaller or
parked deals; specific harm and court routes remain, not universal notifications.

A career officer clears/accepts narrow voluntary terms; an independent court
alone imposes contested merger prohibition/adverse conditions and material
transfer. First complete filing, one5workingday omission list, phase30/90working
days and one30workingday court hold constrain delay. Filed litigation alone
extends no hold; relabelled V04/B17 forecast cannot renew it. Lapse permits closing
without deemed merits clearance. Merger-specific structural filing is12months
postclose, with court-proved deliberate concealment allowing12from discovery
capped36postclose. A shorter18month-cap rival favors finality but exposes hidden
control; the selected36cap knowingly increases uncertain unwind cost. Separate
proved loss and actual new/continuing cartel/exclusion keep their own periods;
continued ownership alone cannot reset merger law. Timely ordinary clearance
appeal, safe notice, reply and blocked-access protections remain.

VD11 is narrower than civil MP: intentional implemented listed naked cartel,
or knowing/reckless consummation of an objectively reportable live-held deal.
No exclusion fine, class-A general records offense, below-gate analogy or custody.
V/P/Q court proof, fault, ability, attribution, maxima/periods and anti-stacking
remain. Actual loss/causation is distinct from prevented harm and Treasury fine.
