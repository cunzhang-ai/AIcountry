# I13–I16: living standards protect agency, not just a survival total

*Part of [C05/06](../06-income-floor-and-safe-resources.md); design115 candidate.*

**I13 — Choose a component standard, not a portable headline.** Under I02's
Assembly-owned method, name ordinary private needs, shared ordinary costs,
housing/utilities, necessary work/care, disability/access and extraordinary
replacement separately. Specify quantity, usable quality, place/season/time,
actual liability, price/tax/unit/currency/edition, delivery alternative and
receiver. Preserve omitted/unfamiliar needs and interpretation disputes;
components support reasoning, not an exhaustive list of possible human lives.
Public services are separate legal duties, not cashable resources by default.

Use age and functional circumstances, safe actual sharing and needs assessment;
marriage or a shared address proves neither cost sharing nor controlled cash.
The old.6 child factor is at most a provisional reference, never a ceiling on
proved ordinary/additional child needs or a substitute for paid care. Preserve
larger continuing lawful claims pending authorized revision. Infants, older
people, disabled people, nonusers and unusual households are not made equivalent
by dividing one household mean. Additional need does not require statistical
membership or a claimant's complete technical dossier.

**I14 — Select a20% minimum ordinary cash-need floor.** Let B be the lawfully
priced age/context personal ordinary basket before ordinary in-kind credit,
and Q only I17's eligible same-component credit. Recognized ordinary cash need
is at least `max(B − Q, 0.20 × B)`, plus separately recognized unmet housing/
work/care/access/extraordinary costs. This supplies I02's formerly unspecified
minimum person-controlled cash component;20% is an authored normative choice,
not a social-science optimum or a conclusion of the external budget research.
More necessary noncreditable private components and stronger old rights prevail.

The amount enters N in the existing I05 formula; it is NOT an extra20% payment
to everyone. Actual usable earnings/nonwork resources/asset draw and the work
retention rules still apply once. For IC's conditional universal destination,
the ordinary cash foundation observes the same floor and is credited once in
the residual, never again in O. No grant is its own resource. Housing or actual
cost compensation is not swept into this ordinary floor, and the floor never
caps additional need. Children retain their own safe lawful receiving/growing
agency, not unaccountable parental or AI control. This does not enact U or
alter actual current software/constitutional/contribution boundaries.

Public provision of most goods may be welcome without reducing a person's
whole private life to a voucher, state-approved preference or permission to
join others. Do not demand item-by-item spending approval to use ordinary cash.
Existing lawful protection against coercion/fraud/child diversion retains its
own source and fair process; no new account monitoring or moral consumption test.
Compare0/20/50/100% cash-need floors and cash-plus-services as substantive rivals;
more unrestricted cash costs resources, and20% may still be inadequate.

**I15 — People and evidence have different authority.** EV preserves independent
source access, period/unit/basket bridges, coverage and uncertainty; existing
Assembly law chooses content and material changes. I02's monthly price method,
two-year content review, nominal protection/fallback and earlier hardship clocks
remain. Hear affected people through paid accessible oral/private/collective/
experiential and future media, with interpretation loss, missing perspectives,
reasons and dissent, not one central AI's ideal lifestyle. A source-country
focus group or copied account is neither this public's participation nor a
mandate. Preserve safe reasons without requiring permanently public testimony.

**I16 — Shared goods need a single actual cost and separate people.** Reconcile
one bill/item/period once, allocating actual necessary shares and actual safe
receipt without presuming a common wallet. No universal family map, signatures
from every cohabitant or private contract requirement for informal cooperation.
Assist ordinary declarations; a blocked payer or disputed share retains timely
independent review and provisional support. Preserve private choice/exit when
an asset stays with another person; inaccessible property is no supplied cooker,
phone, bed or safe home. New shares do not confiscate existing title or require
an abused person to return for proof. Separate capital, renewal cash, ownership,
maintenance and actual use before reporting household or national savings.
