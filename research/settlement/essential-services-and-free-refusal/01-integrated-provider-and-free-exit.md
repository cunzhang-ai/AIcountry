# Leaving an optional programme without losing income or care

Design323 · Constructed comparison for [FRE01–FRE05](../../country/C04/07/05-essential-services-and-free-refusal.md)

An adult has a valid 100 income award due at hour 4 and needs
a qualified home-care visit by hour 6. The integrated provider
has access to both delivery channels and also runs a voluntary
employment programme with optional AI activity monitoring.
The adult declines employment participation and monitoring, and
still requests the visit. A provider flag holds the income instruction
and marks the care request as cancelled. The case has a protected
gross budget of 156 illustrative units. Payroll and care qualifications
are separately established. No worker is compelled to accept a shift.

| Arrangement | Same-window result after refusal | Capacity to fund |
|---|---|---:|
| Integrated provider and one common gate | Its ordinary package costs 140. Under the stipulated hold the person receives neither due payment nor care in time. | 140 |
| Common intake with independent execution | An authorized independent payer settles 100 at hour 3. A qualified separate team delivers care at hour 5 for 48. Accessible intake, confirmation and independent review cost 8. | 156 |
| Separate permanent intake and delivery | The independent payer and care team can meet the same duties. The separate ordinary administration, review and notice cost 16 in this case. | 164 |

Select the second arrangement for the authored case. The independent payer
verifies nonsettlement before release under existing payment law. The 48
care amount is the gross cost of the accepted alternate in this window. The original
40 visit was uncommitted at the point of choice. If the original provider
has a valid outstanding 40 claim, gross need rises to 196. A lawful
settlement could reduce the claim, and a separately available prior
source could finance it. An anticipated refund supplies no usable cash. The staff and payer
need independent execution control; sharing the same release officer or
queue that imposed the hold defeats the alternate even with 156 in cash.
If the separate team cannot arrive by hour 6, the duty owner still pays
the 100 on time and reports the unmet care, pursues lawful urgent
receiving and seeks an elected capacity decision.

For a service-specific contrast, a nurse can require enough lawful
information to deliver a requested medication safely. If a resident
refuses the only safe available way to obtain that information, the
nurse may withhold that particular unsafe administration under existing
clinical law, explain safer alternatives and preserve earlier safety
duties. This supplies no basis to hold the person's income payment or
an unrelated care task. An optional support or monitoring refusal
never supplies that clinical evidence.
