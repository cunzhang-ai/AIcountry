# An express duty schedule and a lawful successor

*V11–V12; part of [C08/19](../19-regulation-and-bounded-enforcement.md).*

**V11 — Closed application, no generic public-interest offense.** Only the
[VD01–VD14 schedule](07-duty-schedule.md) supplies V's predicates. A specifies
only its listed records/reporting conduct and requires notice/correction;
B requires knowing/reckless material substantive breach or deliberately
false/obstructed lawful records. Identify actual duty, person, activity,
version, competent owner, evidence and fault; no unpublished limit applies.
A sector cannot use V to create a free-standing duty of ideological loyalty,
truthfulness on all subjects, maximum social efficiency or obedience to AI.

A technical threshold not yet lawfully specified is a real gap. The Assembly
or bounded lawful delegate must choose it through V01/C03 before applying
that threshold; no foreign standard, certification body or future model fills
it by reference alone. The schedule covers selected ordinary duties, not all
medicines, buildings, transport, professional practice or environmental doses.
A published statute-to-standard register maps their owners and unresolved
instruments. Its publication does not itself enact omitted law or justify
closing a service which has not actually breached an existing duty.

**V12 — Continuity and commencement.** Before each lawful commencement,
map old powers, authorizations, duty/standard versions, pending cases,
compelled records, fines, time credit, private property, guarantees, workers
and due services to named lawful successors. Preserve stronger applicable
protections and source-law rights pending authorized revision. New punitive
predicates/maxima are prospective; lawful leniency applies and expired bars
are not revived. Reorganization or an invalid old act does not erase accrued
claims or turn a new authority's file into a prior valid review.

Fund actual independent adjudication, lawful source custody, receiving,
protected access and transition before claiming operation. Missed readiness
does not suspend existing remedies, automatically continue an urgent power
or let a technical inspector write a new fiscal priority. If a safe new regime
cannot operate, retain lawful current protections and expose the missing
legislation/capacity; a new research design cannot renew an incumbent's mandate.

RP/CD now supply scoped reproductive/conflict-custody candidates; SF replaces
the rejected old financing reference conditionally. [TS/TD](../22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md)
now adds a common technical statute and VD10 predicate with explicit missing
local annexes. Next audit the whole national settlement. Preserve disagreement over
criminal-style monetary enforcement, compliance cost and alternative public
provision; legal detail and successful file checks do not prove better lives.

[NB32/VD13](../../C06/44/08-failure-remedy-and-finite-enforcement.md) is expressly
B-only and prospective, not a general extension of bank or reporting penalties.
Missing operative actuarial/technical schedules create no new predicate by analogy.
