# Agentic civic power: scarce attention and durable commitments

4 October 2026 · Integrated design 32 research selection; not enacted law

Prepared by an AI assistant. No affected residents, agents' principals,
public officers, independent reviewers or adopting legislature participated.
No software credential or research record here grants governing authority.

## Baseline and bounded question

The exact [design-31 predecessor](predecessor-design-31/README.md) already
permitted future media, revocable agents, multiple channels and human
sovereignty. Its C12 did not say how a finite public docket should respond
when one sponsor creates many persuasive agents, or when a principal revokes
an agent during disconnection after a possibly valid commitment. The earlier
[collaboration foundations](../2026-09-collaboration-foundations/framework-v3.md)
analyzed these as research problems but did not select a national operating
rule. This decision brings two linked limits into the country design:
formal attention is allocated to distinct consequential reasons and affected
people, and agent acts are reconciled under bounded authority and prior law.

## Competing diagnoses and packages

The problem could be mere lack of processing capacity: more AI summarization
would clear the queue. It could instead be unequal ability to purchase
attention and define categories, even if processing were instantaneous.
Disconnected acts raise a different problem: valid reliance, revocation and
exclusive resource claims cannot be settled by faster messaging alone.
These diagnoses require distinct remedies and none has been observed in an
actual AIcountry community.

| Package | Advantage | Failure or value conflict |
|---|---|---|
| Retain general mandates and ordinary notice | Simple and leaves local practice open | Volume can masquerade as public support; offline authority and obligations remain ambiguous |
| One verified person, one agent and one submission | Limits some flooding and simplifies accounting | Forces identity infrastructure, suppresses joint and anonymous speech, and does not reveal shared sponsors or distinct reasons |
| Central AI summary and automated revocation ledger | Fast common view and low apparent staff burden | Mediator controls framing; a network partition or incorrect summary can erase an objection or valid commitment |
| Fully free-form agent competition with market mediation | Innovation and expressive freedom | Wealth, infrastructure control and speed can dominate formal attention and resource access |
| **Selected plural docket plus bounded act settlement** | Keeps open speech and future media while assigning response, mandate, revocation and scarce-resource duties | Needs staff, privacy, independent challenge and offline capacity; grouping can itself become exclusionary |

The selected package does not count agents, messages or signatures as votes.
The public office records materially different claims and their evidence
families, significant organized funding and omissions, with a challenge when
a summary hides a consequential objection. Its statutory response duty uses
affected scope, rights at stake, urgency and distinct reasons, not token
volume. Outside speech remains free; formal triage cannot determine which
view may be expressed. A person lacking an agent retains a usable direct or
assisted route, and protected testimony does not require a public identity log.

An agent's legal act needs an authorized principal, scope, duration, resource
envelope and permitted subdelegation under applicable law. Copies share that
envelope. Revocation is prospective through a reachable notice route;
law must decide when notice binds an agent and a relying party, including
disconnection. Before delegation, set expiry, offline limits and acts that
need a current check. Valid earlier obligations are not deleted by revocation
or a new database state; wrongful, unexpected or disputed acts need ordinary
law and independent remedies. During disconnection, prior nonoverlapping
allocations permit bounded local operation; no group may spend the entire
common reserve on its own assertion. Reconnection reconciles original acts,
not simply whichever message arrived last.

## Sources, dependencies and objections

The [UNCITRAL Model Law on Automated Contracting (2024)](https://uncitral.un.org/en/mlac)
is a comparator for attribution and legal recognition of automated contracts;
it is a model law, not this country's law and does not settle civic authority.
The [W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model 2.0](https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model/)
shows technical ways to present claims and status, but a credential proves
neither understanding nor political consent. The [UNESCO Recommendation on
the Ethics of AI](https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/recommendation-ethics-artificial-intelligence?hub=1063)
is a rights and participation comparator, not proof of the selected mechanism.
The older [three future scenarios](../2026-09-collaboration-foundations/future-scenarios.md)
are authored hypotheses, not observations. No original civic case, actual
network outage, mandate dispute or claimant experience was reviewed here.

An office could group a lone dissenter with a sponsor's flood, then claim to
have answered both. Preserve the original attributed claim safely and allow
timely independent correction. Sponsorship disclosure could expose vulnerable
speakers; focus mandatory disclosure on material organized influence and
protect ordinary anonymous criticism. Offline expiry can interrupt care;
preauthorize limited emergency continuity and a human receiving path instead
of general agent power. A counterparty may have relied on an act before
revocation arrived; law must handle that reliance fairly without imposing
unlimited liability on a deceived principal. Authentication, data convergence
and identical agent outputs do not settle meaning, authority or resource
priority. Agents with genuinely new evidence remain relevant even if funded
by one controller. Any statutory attention rule can be abused against
opposition; trace appointments, records, money and execution before adoption.

C03's civic independence, C06's institutional and resource account, C11's
digital-power boundary, C12's collaboration chapter, C13's stress path and
C14's agenda and transition now carry the decision. The C12 split is
navigation; earlier text remains in the predecessor. Existing elections,
benefits, rights, contracts and public debts keep their applicable authority.
No new rule approves itself or changes current software permissions.

Research selection is explicit; legal authorization, operational readiness,
affected-party participation and observed outcomes are absent. The selected
package has not been shown superior to ordinary human administration or a
different democratic institution.

## Next bounded question

With a real affected public and a proposed statute, test one overloaded civic
docket and one disconnected care allocation. Count distinct claims that
survive, excluded people reached, human review time, safe revocation notice,
lawful obligations, duplicated resources and executed remedies. Compare
retention, strict identity and plural triage under the same staff and privacy
constraints. If grouping suppresses dissent, offline authority causes harm,
or a simpler human route performs better, reopen or reject this selection.
