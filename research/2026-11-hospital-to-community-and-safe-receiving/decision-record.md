# Design364 decision on hospital-to-community receiving

## Baseline, proposed choice and authority

Pin Design363, HG, HS, AEC, local housing and care, MF and the
[working method](../FRAMEWORK.md). Existing clinical and housing
rules require actual receiving, but do not specify their sequence
at the end of inpatient care while a new acute need arrives.
Select a named lead, separate qualified acceptance, voluntary
alternatives, funding and the next receiving check. The project
assistant proposes the 24-hour reassessment parameter while a
person remains voluntarily. Actual clinical, local, court and
elected authorities retain their roles. No new office, payment,
detention, discharge or service is authorized here.

## Alternatives and objections

A hospital can optimize departure time by arranging transport
to a nominal destination. That can leave support unavailable.
A voluntary ward stay preserves the current qualified place but
uses scarce acute capacity. A funded community route can release
usable capacity if it receives the person in time and can cover
the continuing needs. Compare accepted direct home support,
temporary recovery and a ward or independently funded alternate.
Give the resident a practical choice and support a lawful decision
to leave. Never use family status as unpaid standby.

The coordination role can grow into a gatekeeper, and a temporary
destination can become permanent without consent. Keep source-law
rights, each owner's decision and direct independent remedies.
Name the next acceptance and costs before treating a temporary
arrangement as complete. Hospital management must see pressure on
the next person, while the receiving services can refuse an unsafe
assignment. Cost repeat contact and protected evidence separately.

## Scoped result, compatibility and next action

The [primary material](sources.md) supports planning and role
separation. The [constructed comparison](../settlement/hospital-to-community-and-safe-receiving/01-one-discharge-and-one-urgent-bed.md)
joins two people's deadlines, accepted capacity and early cash.
It establishes no clinical effect, real staffing, affected consent,
adoption, funding or recovery. Existing benefit, case, care and
housing dates survive an actual transfer of location or payer.

Use an authorized service research budget and consent-based
protected review of one clinical-community handoff and one
concurrent urgent admission. Compare the actual receiving,
costs and staffing against the ordinary path and the best
voluntary alternative. Stop at a qualified comparison without
changing live care. Reopen on an unsafe move, coerced stay,
unmet ward demand, failed community receiving or a better route.
