# EV01–EV04: a professional mandate and a public appointment

**EV01 — Limited service, dispersed originals.** Assembly law specifies official
statistical/public-archive duties, publication, lawful access and bounded funds.
Sector/local authorities retain original operational records and their decisions.
The service may compile, preserve and explain; it cannot tax, determine eligibility,
license outside inquiry, control journalism or certify political truth. No universal
private-life register or prior permission for every private job is required.

**EV02 — One chief, no self-selecting board.** One chief has a six-year,
nonrenewable personal term from lawful assumption, with no holdover or return as
acting chief. The chief manages professional staff under C04, but cannot choose
their permanent successor or enlarge statutory powers. Archives, statistical production and
commissioned evaluation have separate responsible staff/review; sharing facilities
does not let one producer finally adjudicate its own source or privacy complaint.

**EV03 — An open qualification pool.** Existing Assembly civil-service recruitment
staff advertise for at least30days and verify published job-related competence in
statistics, records/privacy and accountable administration, using disclosed qualified
advice. Demonstrated equivalent work may qualify; no closed degree/guild, recipient,
vendor, party loyalty or AI score owns eligibility. Publish all eligible names and
reasoned exclusions with protected evidence and direct ordinary-court challenge.

**EV04 — Assembly selection.** Open a scheduled process180days before expiry,
or within14days of unexpected vacancy. Any Assembly member may nominate an eligible
person. Give public notice, conflicts and an accessible hearing before a single-name
recorded ballot; appointment requires three fifths of full statutory membership.
First ballot is due within30days of the eligible list. Vacancies/abstentions do not
reduce the denominator; no failed vote, court delay or clock appoints anyone.
