# Design513 decision record

**Basis and choice.** Continue the [Design512 baseline](baseline.json) under
the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md). Extend independent judgment beyond
a single adverse case. Assess materially shared advice across independent
public authorities where their decisions can reinforce the same mistaken
inference. Distinguish a fixed observation, the response that changes it,
and what can support future reliance. Prefer source-qualified, staffed common
review and accountable sector decisions over centralized policy command.

**Alternatives and objections.** A central national service could reconcile
all data efficiently, with material privacy and control costs. Vendor
diversity protects some failures and leaves shared derivations possible.
Competent ordinary casework, deterministic scheduling and independent
resident evidence can be cheaper or stronger. The selected rule uses
proportionate shared assessment only on material cross-sector reliance.
It does not require a central case record, independent retraining for every
use, equal support for unsupported claims, or keeping redundant services
once a qualified better choice is made. The substantive service and budget
effects require ordinary review and elected or sector decisions.

**Handoff.** The [canonical choice](../country/C11/09/20-shared-advice-and-policy-feedback.md)
and [comparison](../settlement/independent-public-judgment/01-when-services-teach-the-model.md)
protect original receipt, existing entitlements, review, elections, lawful
provider changes and case custody. Notify only necessary affected owners.
Trace any completed legal effect and actual remedy to its receiver. The
[sources](sources.md) and parallel analysis are neither a public mandate
nor verified actual delivery.

**Next decision.** In an actual adopting setting, select two decisions on
one common service chain, verify lawful independently controlled counts,
cost competent ordinary work and test whether a model adds a useful result.
Use nonbinding rehearsal for comparisons that could otherwise deny people
rights. Reopen on a new common dependency, inaccessible evidence, missing
receivers, a credible alternative diagnosis or a simpler reliable route.
