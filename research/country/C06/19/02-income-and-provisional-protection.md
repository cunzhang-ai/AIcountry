# Candidate Act E05–E08: due income and provisional protection

*Design 57 proposed clauses; [Act entrance](../19-continuity-law-and-delivery-package.md).*

**E05 — Due payments and safe receipt.** An established income or survivor
claim shall be paid on its program's enacted due date, without repeated
reapplication because government, budget, vendor or local boundary changes.
The benefits authority shall distinguish entitlement, authorization,
released cash and safe actual receipt. A failed household account shall
have a lawful alternate individual route, including an accessible non-digital
route; a controlling partner or employer shall not be the compulsory receiver.
Reasoned suspension for a legally material eligibility change remains
challengeable, with notice, evidence and necessary provisional protection.
An unpaid instruction or promised transfer is not a received benefit.

**E06 — Seven-day hardship protection.** On credible evidence that a person
cannot obtain essential food, shelter, utilities or disability-related
necessities before ordinary determination, a benefits officer shall decide
provisional protection within 24 elapsed hours of E02 receipt and act sooner
where the loss would otherwise occur. The person need not supply records
held by the state or an inaccessible controller before this decision.
The provisional amount shall equal the positive difference between the
applicable enacted seven-day needs basket and resources actually available
for those same needs and days, excluding compulsory unpaid help and assets
unavailable without a harmful forced sale. Overlapping benefits count only
to the extent received and usable; a due but unpaid benefit is not cash.
The Assembly shall define the basket, eligible scope and update rule in the
income statute; an officer cannot invent its value or narrow that scope.
Offer cash or a genuinely usable safe alternative chosen with the person;
in-kind provision shall not disguise unpaid entitlement or compel custody.

**E07 — Initial decision and continuing protection.** The benefits authority
shall give an initial reasoned ordinary decision within 14 calendar days
from E02 receipt. A request for further evidence shall not restart that
clock. The decision shall state eligibility, amount, covered period,
material facts, unresolved evidence and review; an acknowledgment or new
evidence request shall not count as determination. If unresolved, it shall
report the missed duty and reconsider the
seven-day protection before its end under the same actual-need test.
Renewal is not automatic final eligibility and delay grants no new adverse
power. Established claims keep their own due dates. The person can seek
E13 review immediately where denial or delay threatens effective protection;
no serial administrative appeal is mandatory before urgent court access.

**E08 — Fair correction and recovery.** A good-faith provisional recipient
shall not face a penalty solely because later evidence changes the amount.
The authority shall distinguish its error, changed circumstances and proved
misrepresentation, give notice and reasons, and permit independent challenge
before compulsory recovery. It shall not recover by taking the essential
subsistence floor or another person's benefit; installment and hardship
rules require enacted law. Fraud prosecution retains ordinary proof and
defense, separate from administrative reassessment. A corrected award
shall preserve earlier lawful claims, safe records and an actual payment
executor; arrears remain owed under the applicable source statute. A credit
between programs requires an express prospective same-period rule and
independent correction; it shall not cancel earned or accrued pension
rights by relabeling emergency assistance as their replacement. Where
support is genuinely additional, report its additional cost rather than
claiming baseline inclusion through an accounting label.

The 100-million-CU historical transfer envelope does not price this formula,
its adequacy, administration or take-up. Existing and provisional payments
are reconciled once; their labels do not create two grants for one expense.

[Candidate I01–I12](../../C05/06-income-floor-and-safe-resources.md) supplies
the substantive ordinary income architecture behind this bridge. The
measured basket still needs original price/access evidence and a coherent
funded launch; the old envelope cannot establish adequacy or operating rights.

[HL housing loss](../../C09/25-housing-loss-and-secure-receiving.md)
separately requires a real place at the housing deadline when money
cannot secure one. Reconcile same-cost, same-period receipts under
applicable credit rules and preserve the E06 hardship claim.

[I25–I29](../../C05/06/08-income-timing-and-usable-resources.md)
adds a narrow ordinary calendar correction. E06 continues to assess
actual safe resources for the relevant short window. An attribution
does not supply cash, postpone a bridge or authorize a second payment
for the same due need.

[I36–I41](../../C05/06/10-essential-cost-shocks-and-usable-protection.md)
separates a necessary price change from unavailable goods before the
next ordinary payment. E06 and E07 retain their original clocks and
rights; no separate temporary schedule is effective without lawful source.

[MS27–MS32](../61/07-recession-and-timely-income-protection.md)
adds funded surge operations while preserving original hardship,
provisional protection and decision clocks.
