# C13. Exclusion across work, housing and care

*Part of [C13 lived experience](../C13-lived-experience.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

A resident is rejected by an employer and a landlord and receives a late
clinical appointment. All three use a shared identity or scoring vendor.
The resident suspects a disability, language or ancestry proxy, but the
rejections might also reflect genuine job requirements, scarce housing and
clinical urgency. One aggregate gap or one model output proves neither
discrimination nor innocence. The first receiving office preserves dates,
separate decisions and original reasons; each sector provides an accessible
interim route and independent challenge suited to the harm.

The equality inquiry service may compare selection rules and shared vendor
control across sectors and assist a lawful case. It cannot override a
clinician, allocate a home or order an arrest. The employer, landlord and
clinic disclose relevant evidence under court protection. The resident can
seek urgent accommodation or access; each respondent can present legitimate
reasons and challenge an overbroad order. A sector reviewer cannot call
the person ineligible merely because another sector already rejected them.

Test whether a person without a digital account can file, whether the
vendor's proxy and source records remain inspectable, whether privacy
protects uninvolved applicants, whether a court can restore the lost
opportunity before it disappears, and whether the order is executed. Then
test capture: the same party funds the equality office, vendor, employer
and a political campaign. Alternate claimant, court and association routes
must still work. Compare groups, places and nonusers without publishing
small-group identities. This invented journey is a design test, not evidence
that discrimination occurred or that the proposed system has improved lives.

[FRE01–FRE05](../C04/07/05-essential-services-and-free-refusal.md)
adds the distinct case of a common provider using one due service to
obtain consent for another purpose. Verify each actual duty and the
funded independent route under the same deadlines.

[ASI01–ASI08](../C11/09/16-public-scoring-and-purpose-limits.md) governs the public-service
part of this problem, including an accurate fact used outside its lawful purpose.
