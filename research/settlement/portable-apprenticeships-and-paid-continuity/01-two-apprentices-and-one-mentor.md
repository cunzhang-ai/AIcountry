# Two apprentices and one available mentor

*Design396 · Constructed comparison. Cash and mentor blocks are abstract units.*

A's employer closes. B moves to another district for family reasons
and asks to continue the same qualification. A and B each have
independently verified credits and each needs one qualified practical
block in the same next useful window. The destination and sending
providers retain existing commitments to other learners. After those
commitments the shared pool has one compatible mentor block.
A second independently qualified workshop can accept one more
through a paid standby agreement. Both apprentices freely accept
the qualified interim routes in this example.

| Route | Window 1 result |
|---|---|
| Require the old employer or a fresh start | The former employer cannot deliver A's training. Starting over loses verified work for either learner without creating qualified capacity. |
| Transfer records and match jobs only | Verified credits carry. There is still only one usable compatible block before the two deadlines. |
| Fund independent receiving and interim support | Ordinary qualified receiving costs 3 and the independent workshop costs 2. Necessary travel and protected learning support cost 2 more. Seven settled units and two actual acceptances meet both practical needs. |
| Borrow a mentor already assigned elsewhere | A proposed second place displaces a protected learner. It adds no net compatible capacity and cannot qualify the transfer. |

The construction treats the two practical blocks as supervised
instruction without productive work. If a host instead uses a
learner for productive work, a lawful employer, actual wages and
protection must be present before that work begins. A has one
unit of unpaid old wages. That claim is separate from the seven
units for new receiving and is still open at the first deadline.
B retains separate accrued employment rights through the move.

In window 2, no permanent new employer has yet accepted A.
The two learners still need one compatible practical block each.
Assume two qualified receivers are available at an authorized
total of six units, but only five have settled. Delayed recovery
of the old wage claim belongs to A and cannot finance the
programme. One more lawful unit is needed. If the workshop
also loses its mentor, the money alone cannot cover the missing
block. Maintain available instruction and independently review
any unmet duty. Neither the calculation nor a matching tool
sets priority between the learners.
