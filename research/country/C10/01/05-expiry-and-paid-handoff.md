# RX17–RX21. End exceptional authority and maintain lawful care

*Design272 · Proposed emergency exit and continuity rules.*

**RX17 — Prepare to exit from the moment authority is activated.**
The activating instrument must name the office that owns its exit
plan and the offices that retain ordinary duties. For each material
measure record the actual source, current actor, first effect,
controlling expiry and work likely to remain at that expiry. Separate
acts that end, tasks that can run on independently valid authority,
and accrued claims that still need payment or adjudication. For every
continuing task name the competent ordinary receiver, lawful scope,
source and cash date, staff, safe handoff and accessible challenge.
Assembly and opposition can inspect safe versions before deciding
whether any narrower prospective law is needed. A missing plan
triggers urgent correction without moving a legal clock.

**RX18 — End the source in the field as well as in the register.**
At the earliest controlling expiry, stop issuance and execution of
new exceptional instructions and end any continuing exercise of
that authority. A restriction already in place needs its own valid
grounds for every period it remains in force. The duty owner gives
accessible public notice and authenticated instructions to local
offices, responders, courts, contractors and system operators.
Disable expired signing and command scope, including delegated
agents, local cached rules and paper authorizations. Give offline
operators the controlling expiry and the ordinary fallback in
advance. Failed notice, delayed revocation or a stale queue cannot
validate a later command. Preserve original evidence under
independent lawful custody. Protect valid separately sourced acts
from an overbroad shutdown.

**RX19 — Move necessary work through an accepted ordinary route.**
Prearrange lawful rescue, voluntary shelter and treatment, public
works safety, payroll and payment routes within their own laws.
A receiver accepts the exact task, remaining work, custody, payer
and earliest applicable deadline while the outgoing office can
still hand it over. For work that cannot physically stop safely,
schedule a staffed lawful takeover or a technically safe stop
before the boundary. Use independently authorized stabilization
only within its own scope. Work in progress cannot extend a
restriction. If neither route is available, identify the exposed
people and original duties, seek existing timely relief and use
only genuinely lawful measures. Ordinary aid and reentry support
remain available without consent to a continuing restriction.

**RX20 — Keep accrued claims, elections and independent remedies live.**
The duty owner and payer separate completed lawful work, accrued
wages and compensation, contested instructions and future work.
Use applicable [DB budget authority](../../C06/60-democratic-budget-and-execution.md)
and standing payment law, including independently authorized
continuity if the ordinary source is unavailable. Payment of a
valid claim confers no permission for another act. Expiry neither
decides liability for a past act nor stops pending review, correction
or lawful compensation. Preserve opposition, public records,
ordinary independent courts, remedy execution and election deadlines
through an adverse handoff. Incoming or outgoing command cannot
certify its own contested compliance. Use RX11–RX12 and existing
safety, liberty and election routes where relief is time-sensitive.

**RX21 — Test exit without counting on renewal.** In the first-budget
exercise, compare exact expiry with no renewal, blocked access or
payment, and a necessary ordinary task whose acceptance comes too
late. Verify actual cessation, qualified receiving, continuing
claims and access to review in each case. Apply
[JC23–JC27](../../C06/65/06-domestic-mutual-aid-and-concurrent-need.md)
when cross-region assets are involved, preserving independently
valid earlier or later assignments. The elected budget funds the
handoff, review and safe shutdown as separate work. Reopen the
exit design on recurring unsafe gaps or a stronger rights-compatible
ordinary route. AI may identify conflicting clocks, missing successors
and stale instructions. Competent officers and independent courts
retain actual authority and review.
