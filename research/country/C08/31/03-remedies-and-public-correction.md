# WB09–WB12. Deliver protection and correct the underlying record

*Part of [protected disclosure](../31-protected-disclosure-and-evidence.md).*

**WB09 — Correct the public act through its own decision-maker.** Keep
the allegation, evidence preservation, investigation, finding, civil
retaliation claim and underlying decision distinct. A regulator can
use already authorized immediate safety powers on qualified evidence.
A civil claim can protect the reporter before the underlying issue
is determined. A material verified correction goes to the competent
evidence and decision owners under EV and applicable budget, service,
electoral, oversight or court rules. Preserve uncertainty and material
objections for authorized recipients. Notify relying offices within
useful time, with protected records and a usable public correction
where law permits. A report, fact revision or AI summary does not
cancel a vote, replace a lawful threshold or give the investigator
authority to set policy.

**WB10 — Execute individual relief and account for each loss.** A
competent court may order a specific stop, record correction, pay,
safe reinstatement if sought and practicable, equivalent relief,
compensation and defined costs under ordinary law. Verify the
actual receiver, due date and delivery under B18/B19 and SR.
Reconcile provisional pay against the same period and loss only
after actual receipt. Keep separate proven expenses, other harm
and ongoing support visible. An employer or platform cannot
discharge relief through an unreceived transfer or a reinstated
account with no work or access. Qualify any shared repair under
B20/SR. No automatic immunity for future misconduct or new
criminal offense follows. An insolvency or state source shortfall
preserves actual claims and prior-law priority and execution.

**WB11 — Protect against the protector and abusive complaints.**
No implicated manager, auditor, vendor or ministry may control
the only intake, records, funding, investigation or order
execution. Fund existing outside receiving, secure evidence,
independent review and legal assistance separately through
ordinary elected source law. Material public reporting uses safe
aggregates, including delays, reprisal outcomes and unexecuted
relief, without creating an identity inference. Report weak data
or systemic exclusion. Preserve fair response and actual evidence
limits for accused people. Early proportionate triage may close
a genuinely repetitive or unsupported issue with reasons and
reopening on material evidence. It cannot silence a protected
report, erase its date or resolve an independent claim by secrecy.

**WB12 — Preserve rights through system, law and provider change.**
Adopt prospectively with named funded roles, conflict arrangements,
qualified workers, record custodians and independent executors.
Keep existing stronger protections, lawful older claims, pay,
legal review and source duties through OP/T and HT. AI may
assist with expression, translation, deidentification or checking
provenance with a paid human route and retained original meaning.
Multiple summaries of the same source do not corroborate it.
An AI cannot deny protection, infer source identity for personnel
use, decide merits or certify that a remedy arrived. Reopen on
proven chilling, unsafe disclosure, displaced informal resolution,
independence failure or a simpler effective alternative.

[EV17–EV24](../../C11/11-evidence-corrections-and-public-reliance.md) assigns the receiving office’s review and actual follow-through. Preserve safe source custody and independent relief when the report is still disputed.
