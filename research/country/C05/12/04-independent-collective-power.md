# WL21–WL25: people who work must be able to act together

*Part of [C05/12](../12-work-contracts-and-collective-power.md); design 117 candidate.*

**WL21 — Independent association.** Workers may form/join/leave plural unions
and employers their own associations without employer/ministry permission.
No compulsory union membership, employer-selected union, blacklisting or dismissal
for lawful organization. Unions choose accountable officers by their own democratic
rules with usable accounts, member challenge and independent civil remedies;
executive cancellation is no answer to dissent. A union does not speak for every
worker or acquire sovereign lawmaking. Separate voluntary political contributions
and actual source/control under PC/CF; workplace membership/dues are no inferred
electoral donation or mandate. True dependent solo workers keep MP05 bargaining
protection; this is no business cartel exception for downstream bid fixing.

**WL22 — Recognition with actual workers.** On a request supported by at least
10% of the proposed unit or ten actual workers, whichever number is lower,
Registrar offers assisted confidential verification and a secret recognition
ballot. Disputed units follow actual community of work/interests and employer
control with independent ordinary-court merits review; management cannot draw
an opponent-free unit. Qualifying actual workers, including part-time/temporary
staff with real continuing ties and disputed retaliatory dismissals provisionally,
have one vote per unit, not per account/agent, independent of political franchise.
Actual employer negotiating principals cannot vote on both sides.

A majority of valid votes chooses the representative or no recognition; no
turnout quorum rewards abstention. Use a runoff between the leading options
if a multi-option first ballot has no majority. Rejection leaves members-only
voice, organizing and a fresh lawful challenge; no sole union permission gate.
Ordinary repeat recognition/decertification ballots are at least12 months apart,
except proved coercion/material defects or a real changed unit needing correction.
Recognition covers the unit with fair representation, minority voice and no
membership compulsion. A tied runoff certifies no new mandate; existing lawful
rights/representation continue, with members-only voice and independent correction.
Registrar normally determines process within30 calendar
days of a usable request; records/authority disputes retain interim protection,
reasons and direct courts, not automatic recognition by timeout.

**WL23 — Bargain, do not manufacture agreement.** Recognized employer/worker
representatives meet within14 calendar days of a usable request, exchange
necessary protected information and negotiate in good faith on pay, time, safety,
work rules and consequential change. No duty to accept the other's proposal,
waive individual floors or preserve every business. Give protected reasons for
withholding genuinely confidential data and an independent route to inspect what
is necessary. An unlawful refusal has direct restorative relief; no compulsory
AI settlement or minister's final wage arbitration. A unit agreement needs a
majority of valid secret ratification votes, no turnout veto, and defined scope,
dates, signatories, amendment/exit and accrued-claim survival. Minority/free choice
and stronger individual rights remain. Ordinary collective peace commitments
need exact scope; they cannot waive immediate safety refusal or enforce new work
by force. A different actual mandate can be chosen through lawful procedures.

**WL24 — Elected workplace councils with limited binding power.** From20 actual
workers offer a council elected by all eligible unit workers; smaller units can
choose a representative voluntarily. Seats: one for20–49, three for50–199, five
for200–999, seven from1000; two-year terms. Use one secret list/individual vote,
one optional person preference on the chosen list and highest averages1,3,5…
across lists, skipping lists with no remaining willing eligible candidate. A
quotient tie uses higher original list votes, then a disclosed prior neutral
lot. Within-list seats go by person preferences, then prior neutral lot, not
management's slate order. Actual proportional
minority access matters; no state party list or copied mandate. More than half of the full
statutory seats must affirm; vacancies do not lower that denominator.
Vacancies go to the next willing eligible candidate on the original list;
if none, an assisted by-election fills the remainder of the term. No candidate
is compelled to serve and an unfilled body gains no fictitious quorum. Recall
needs20% petition and majority valid secret votes; ordinary recall at most once
per six months, without blocking urgent independent correction of abuse.

Councils get timely information/consultation and consent power over new general
rating/disciplinary procedures, intrusive monitoring proposals and collective
ordinary roster rules within actual competence. They cannot waive PD/TW/equality,
clinical judgment, individual contracts or determine sovereign policy. Binding
pay agreements use WL23; business investment/closure remains lawful with WL13–18.
On deadlock retain lawful arrangements, negotiate/mediate or use jointly accepted
arbitration, never an employer/council self-approved waiver or invented agreement.
A court can correct unlawful existing rules; no perpetual permission for a harmful
legacy follows. This veto can delay useful change and is a retained value/cost
tradeoff. Statutory emergency safety powers remain their own narrow sources.

Provide at least two paid ordinary-duty hours per representative per week and
reasonable necessary additional access/time where proved, with qualified task
cover; no unpaid administrative reserve or enlargement of TW hours. Employer
funds the minimum and relevant actual replacement, not a union-selection fee.
Union bargaining and council scope are distinct; neither replaces the other's
mandate. Actual overlapping tasks/time are counted once.

**WL25 — Wider bargaining and ownership remain real choices.** Permit voluntary
sector/multi-employer bargaining and worker cooperatives with free independent
representation. Binding extension to outsiders needs competent general law with
actual coverage, source/incidence, small/new-enterprise/worker input and DR or
Assembly approval as applicable; a cartel label or minister's informal signature
cannot enact it. This candidate does not introduce compulsory corporate-board
worker seats or decide all enterprise forms. Worker board co-determination,
worker ownership and more extensive sector bargaining remain structural rivals
for the next company/association design, not presumed equivalent to consultation.

Design118 [OE11–OE20](../13-enterprise-and-association-constitutions.md) now
supplies the separate enterprise-board/member/public-owner choice. WL council
consent/terms remain; a four-year OE board mandate is not the two-year council
mandate. Work Relations Registrar gains board-ballot powers only by the express
OE Act. No worker seat transfers private equity, political franchise or an
association's doctrine/member authority. Existing stronger terms continue.

[WL41–WL46](08-sector-floors-and-paid-continuity.md) supplies the later candidate
for an enacted minimum in in-home personal assistance. It preserves voluntary
agreements and gives affected outsiders a paid, independently reachable hearing.
