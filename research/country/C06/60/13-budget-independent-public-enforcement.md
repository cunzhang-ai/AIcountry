# DB33–DB36. Keep enforcement lawful when the budget expects a saving

*Design537 · Proposed ordinary budget and execution rules.*

**DB33 — Name the legal and evidentiary basis before claiming the saving.**
For a material budget proposal relying on recoveries, denial, withdrawal or
reduced awards, identify the exact source law, evidence needed for the
individual act and any amendment needed for lawful implementation. Distinguish
program appropriations from legal power over a person's claim. Price credible
receipts net of refunds, assistance, independent review, ordinary delivery
and remedy. Present the unchanged lawful baseline and an adverse path with
no usable incremental receipts. Give independent budget and legal reviewers
access to original assumptions, source evidence and material contrary legal
advice through protected lawful custody. A forecast or budget vote cannot
settle a disputed interpretation or establish an individual debt.

**DB34 — Keep the power to correct independent of collection.** Fund the
case decision, staffed ordinary payment, independent review and remedy
execution through sources usable if the estimated recovery is zero.
Do not attach payments or worker targets to the number or amount of adverse
decisions, disputed assessments, complaints rejected or awards stopped.
Apply the same rule to staff, contractors and model objectives. Track
lawfully established recoveries separately from initial claims, cleared
receipts, refunds and actual resolved errors. A verified lawful fiscal
improvement may return for ordinary elected disposition once its scope,
continuing costs and usable dates qualify under FB09–FB12.

**DB35 — Keep stopping the practice a funded option.** Where evidence
credible under LX06 calls for suspension, use the applicable legal stop
and review. On the same original dates Treasury shows the commitments that
depend on the affected amount and the existing ordinary and corrective
sources. Hold any unbound dependent release that lacks its own qualified
source under DB24. Preserve valid binding commitments, unaffected awards and
earlier remedies. If correction and lawful claims outstrip available money,
record the missing payment, its owner and original date and bring an exact
funded correction to the competent budget body. A budget gap supplies no
power to continue the affected adverse practice.

**DB36 — Test the general case and the individual harm separately.** Under
existing evidence and oversight powers, give the independent reviewer a
protected record of material contrary rulings, agency withdrawals and
repeated reversals that concern the same budget assumption. A decision
binding only in one case can still justify review of a shared mechanism.
Settlement or withdrawal leaves that evidence available under applicable
privacy and retention law. Apply LX11–LX16 to an established common error.
Follow corrections to original
recipients including people who did not complain. Give opponents and affected
persons usable independent review, original evidence within lawful scope and
an executed remedy. Test the same arrangements for manual and assisted
procedures. AI may identify uncertain evidence and reproduce competing
estimates. It cannot find liability, count a disputed assessment as settled
finance or decide whose benefit ends. Use existing elected fiscal and
judicial authority. Keep accrued rights, applicable stronger rules and
original deadlines.
