# Design526 decision record

*14 November 2026 · A national democratic choice after a failed formation repeat*

## Decision and baseline

Pin [Design525](baseline.json) and the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md).
[Existing formation](../country/C03/06-government-formation-and-caretaker.md)
allows a first automatic repeat. After that repeat fails, it protects limited
caretaking, opposition, ordinary supply and the regular election. It requires
separate authority for any further poll. Select [GF01–GF05](../country/C03/06/01-national-recovery-after-a-failed-repeat.md)
as a prospective option for that missing authority. It requires its own protected
constitutional decision and implementing law. Retain the first-round timetable
and earlier authorization conditions. Current enactment, software and participation
rules continue on their own terms.

The selected rule gives eligible electors one route per original term after
independently verified lawful failure by a competent repeat Assembly. It pairs
a binding public question with a separate proportional ballot and seats a new
Assembly only on two final results and actual lawful convening. Its threshold,
clocks and budget are authored hypotheses. There is no assumption that a poll
produces an effective cabinet. This addresses a national political choice and
retains the competence to vote supply throughout the pending period.

## Alternatives, control and dependencies

The [matched case](../settlement/national-formation-deadlock/01-restore-choice-without-creating-a-vacancy.md)
compares a fixed Assembly, a voter-triggered choice and an automatic second
repeat. It uses Swedish and Spanish constitutional texts as examples of
automatic electoral return. These texts establish institutional alternatives.
Actual national response, distribution of participation, staffing and costs
remain open. Direct executive election and a collegial executive remain whole
constitutional rivals with different succession and removal consequences.

The voters' route can cost more than a second repeat and can reward strategic
nomination refusal or organized groups with easy access to petition channels.
Protected assisted entry, independent verification, recorded failed votes and
timely judicial relief constrain those risks. An independently funded neutral
clerk and the electoral authority perform bounded acts under prior law.
Preserve separate court appointment, source custody, review and actual payment
and execution. Disqualification of the ordinary custodian uses the preassigned
alternate, with urgent review and reasons. A party, model or executive does not
control the electorate, ballot question or result. A vote supports a real
alternative with prior known rights and responsibilities. Count petition and
ballot harms, suppressed entry, opposition capacity and spent public resources
against the strongest lawful fixed-term alternative.

The project’s local recall rules permit separate replacement. GF applies only
at the national deadlock and keeps the supply body through lawful handover.
Existing election validity and annulment remain separate. Apply the ordinary
budget and limited bridge within their exact authority. Design522's post-bridge
schedule requires actual legal loss of the competent Assembly. Design525's
ordinary independent operation supplies a model for funded receipt and review.
Preserve legal dates, pay, commitments, court orders and actual claims through
every branch. Keep any additional contest outside the first round and never
let it alter a contest underway or extend the current term.

## Review and re-opening

distinction between national aggregate and actual local costs. They supplied
no independent affected-party participation or legal authority. The next
decision is whether a legitimate adopting public should enact this option on an
exact calendar and budget. Compare proven independent petition receipt, lawful
record access, an executed judicial remedy, ordinary ballots, court and service
work across the first and second deadline. Price paid leave, information access,
aid, scarce staff and the strongest alternative use of additional funds. Bring
affected voters, opposition, delivery workers and residents without a vote into
their appropriate review routes. Reopen for exclusion, systematic delay,
unauthorized use, costs that threaten other duties, or a better whole rival.
Authority, actual operation, safety through contested handover and comparative
