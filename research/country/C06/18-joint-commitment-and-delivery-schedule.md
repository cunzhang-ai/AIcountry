# C06. A joint commitment and delivery schedule

*Design119 scope/priority clarification,2 November2026; retains the design67 research schedule, not enacted law.*

The [whole-country account](03-commitment-account.md) becomes a joint
schedule rather than a set of sector assurances. The [first populated
inventory](../../settlement/commitments/README.md) connects all 37 currently
audited original functions and61 original selections, with later current
extensions, to proposed owners, shared resources
and remedy executors. Those are navigation denominators, not a census of
every legal obligation or a measure of national readiness. All current
function-level quantities and executed remedies remain unverified.

## Attribute costs before adding or reusing them

For each enforceable duty, delivery component and proposed new start, the
responsible sector and Treasury identify its scope, legal source or missing
law, eligible people, event and due time, payer, receiving operator, reviewer
and actual remedy executor. A component may serve several duties without
merging their eligibility, confidentiality, consent or challenge rules.
The accountable first receiver assists a person with an ordinary report;
the government assembles this account, rather than requiring a dossier
from the claimant.

Each cost or capacity item is marked **included, incremental, overlapping
or unknown** relative to an identified baseline line. Included requires an
explicit workload and resource allocation; a broad heading such as health
or justice is insufficient. Incremental means the additional resources
beyond that baseline. Overlapping requires an allocation that counts the
resource once while preserving each duty. Unknown stays unpriced, with a
responsible estimator and uncertainty; it is neither free nor automatically
an additional bill. National/local transfers, service wages, pension reserves,
refinancing, guarantees and household relief retain their distinct accounting.

The preserved September model has six service envelopes and a cash-support
envelope. It contains justice and democratic administration in aggregate;
neither their omission nor their sufficiency can be inferred for later
functions. A replacement package must reconcile the old envelopes and new
allocations, not add every later chapter on top or declare all of them covered.

## A reserve must survive the same event as its users

Capacity entries identify the worker or asset, skill, controller, location,
usable hours or stock, dates, replenishment and mobilization delay. Publish
safe aggregates; protect personal rosters, clinical records and locations.
Separate normal duty, maintenance, leave, training, standby and surge.
A shared substitute can be retained if a simultaneous-use test shows how
conflicts are resolved under prior law. Otherwise its uses remain competing
claims, not several available reserves. Common fuel, supplier, credential,
floodplain or supervisor can defeat nominally separate alternatives.

The joint scenario tests ordinary care, protection and repair alongside
election access, lawful Assembly sitting, source custody, legal aid, review
and order execution. Their minimum lawful continuity cannot be cancelled
by calling them administrative overhead. A protected cash line without
qualified people and a usable independent executor is not a protected remedy.
The Assembly enacts the bounded continuity allocations and acceptable
contingencies; clinicians, courts and operators keep their separate powers.
Neither Treasury nor an AI optimizer supplies a national priority order.

## What happens when the package cannot deliver

Report legal authority, cash due, qualified capacity, receipt and executed
remedy independently for every material duty. The [combined reference
exercise](../../settlement/commitments/06-combined-results.md) exposes cash,
care and institutional staffing failures in the same year; no package
has demonstrated full current-country feasibility.

Cabinet presents coherent alternatives; opposition and affected local bodies
can propose competing packages. Assembly law and appropriation determine
revenue, borrowing, permitted reallocation, prospective standards and which
genuinely discretionary new work is deferred. Show displaced beneficiaries,
maintenance, later cost and effects on public control. Capital required for
existing rights is not discretionary merely because it is capital.
Direct courts and the [C06 shortfall route](04-shortfall-and-continuity.md)
remain available; lawful clinical urgency is not replaced by claim arrival.

Existing claims, accrued liabilities and protected rights survive missing
estimates or resources. A new future-start provision needs an exact public
start rule, interim protection and elected review date. Missing evidence
does not authorize a technical veto, indefinite ministerial delay, coercive
family care or a lower hidden standard. A missed legal due time is an unmet
duty, even if lawfully authorized cash has not arrived. An elected decision
to proceed under uncertainty exposes that risk; it does not establish delivery.

Treasury compiles; sectors and local operators attest actual capacity and
receipt; the separately governed evidence service preserves methods and
protected originals. Audit, courts, opposition and affected people contest
omissions through their existing routes. This selection creates no new
super-office or governing procedure. Reopen on a new duty, changed source,
controller, hazard, price or failed handoff. Later changes must reconcile
continuing obligations and obtain fresh final review.
