# Two reported hospital sites and two different maintenance movements

England's annual Estates Returns Information Collection includes
estimated backlog work and maintenance-related investment. Inspect
Addenbrooke's Hospital and Royal Cornwall Hospital as illustrative
same-code general acute hospital sites, with the same reported formal
survey method and 81–100% survey band in both selected returns. The
pair shows contrasting movements. It is not a representative sample.
[2023/24 return and site file](https://digital.nhs.uk/data-and-information/publications/statistical/estates-returns-information-collection/summary-page-and-dataset-for-eric-2023-24)
· [2024/25 return and site file](https://digital.nhs.uk/data-and-information/publications/statistical/estates-returns-information-collection/summary-page-and-dataset-for-eric-2024-25)

Backlog below sums the high, significant, moderate and low risk
fields. Investment sums the two reported investment fields for
critical and non-critical infrastructure risk. Figures are reported
pounds rounded here to three decimal places in millions.

| Site | 2023/24 backlog | 2024/25 backlog | Difference | 2024/25 reported investment |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Addenbrooke's Hospital, RGT01 | 208.559 | 234.752 | +26.192 | 18.000 |
| Royal Cornwall Hospital, REF12 | 51.613 | 45.425 | −6.188 | 6.188 |

The second site's similarity between the magnitude of its backlog
decline and its recorded investment is not a completed-work
reconciliation. The first site's return records investment even as
the estimated backlog rises. In both cases the annual return lacks a task-by-task
bridge across newly overdue work, prior defects found, changes in
valuation or scope and work independently completed. A common survey
band does not guarantee a common building scope or constant prices.
No causal effect or current safety assessment follows.

The January 2025 National Audit Office report estimates at least
£49 billion for the scoped government estate as of October 2024,
with incomplete and heterogeneous information. It also reports about
5,400 annual NHS clinical service incidents associated with property
and infrastructure failures on average over 2019/20–2023/24.
These different measures establish why the service effect deserves
its own account. They do not price either site in this pair or
represent all government infrastructure.
