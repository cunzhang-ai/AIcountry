# Design544 decision record

*14 November 2026 · Apply the limited-government settlement across all chapters*

Pin [Design543](baseline.json), the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md),
[T143–T147](../country/C14/06/32-limited-government-and-first-supply.md)
and [compulsory-burden rules](../country/C01/03/07-compulsory-burdens-and-country-disposition.md).
The [country authority table](../country/AUTHORITY.md) applies the existing
national, local and personal boundary across fourteen chapters. It identifies
the competent scope and guides readers to the controlling proposals.

The old C05 ordinary-board rule had selected fixed national proportions
with a prescribed transition. Replace that default with choice within
compatible law, charters and valid collective agreements. Preserve existing
board, bargaining, safety, representation, minority and remedy rights.
Retain the numerical board formula and its dependent machinery as a
specified alternative. Any future Act adopting part of it must authorize
that exact scope, decide its boundary and fund an independent remedy and
transition. A binding public service mandate follows its own law.

Shared governance may improve voice and productive investment where
bargaining or safe exit cannot address dependence. A quasi-experimental
German study of a specific one-third rule in stock corporations examined
wages and investment. It did not identify a wage effect and found evidence
of higher capital formation in that scope. It cannot settle this country's
parity rule, size thresholds or what different arrangements would deliver.
[Original study](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjaa038)

Reopen on credible evidence of an identified power failure that ordinary
bargaining, worker representation, safe mobility and existing sector
protection cannot repair, and a funded narrower or broader alternative
with a better outcome for affected workers and others. Use directly
affected participation and report operating costs, transition and dissent.
The frozen edition and past evidence retain their scope. The selected
change concerns the working proposal and requires actual competent adoption.
