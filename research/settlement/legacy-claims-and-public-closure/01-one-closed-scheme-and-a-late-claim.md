# One closed scheme and a later survivor claim

This is a constructed comparison in cash units across two payment windows.
The scheme stops admitting new members. Assume the elected law protects
source-law rights and funds an ordinary pension office with a separate
review route. Assume the source law permits partial payment of an award
without settling the unpaid balance. The old scheme has 12 settled units restricted to its
obligations, case access and remedies. No new revenue is assumed. Six units
are payable to verified claimants in window one. Casework and retrieval
cost two units in window two. Pre-closure records support an allowance of
four for a possible survivor award, with no active filing.

| Choice on the same opening evidence | Window one | Window two if three units are verified |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keep the old body through window two | Pay 6 and carry 6. | Standalone operation costs 3. Pay 3 to the survivor. No cash remains. |
| Absorb duties and release funds for cases with no active filing | Pay 6, release 4 and carry 2. | Fund access and casework for 2. No cash remains to pay the verified award. Show 3 due. |
| Transfer to the ordinary office and carry the restricted allowance | Pay 6 and carry 6. | Fund access for 2 and pay 3. The remaining 1 stays restricted until lawful reassessment. |

The selected constructed choice is the third. The extra cost of the old
office is stipulated. If it alone can retrieve the old evidence or protect
a hearing, retain funded overlap until the successor passes the receiving
test. If admissible evidence before closure resolves the exposure and the
law permits an earlier release, immediate absorption can be reasonable.
At the opening decision the possibility of a claim is known, while its
actual amount and outcome are unresolved.

Test two failures on the selected path. If the amount verified in window
two is five, available money for the award is four. The uncovered one
remains due and the ordinary payment or budget remedy is needed. If the
paid two-unit records route fails, possession of the six units gives no
basis for certifying that the claim was decided or paid. Preserve the
claim date and activate the staffed alternative. A booked transfer is not
receipt. Verify receipt through the lawful payment route and follow any
returned payment through correction.
