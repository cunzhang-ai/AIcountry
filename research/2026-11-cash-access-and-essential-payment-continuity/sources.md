# Primary comparators for essential payment continuity

- Norway's Ministry of Justice and Public Security announced that the
  revised consumer cash-payment rules would take effect on 1 October
  2024. The official national inquiry describes the coverage of
  staffed sales premises, exceptions and the statutory amount limit.
  The comparison concerns ordinary consumer access and its defined
  exceptions, without transferring those thresholds to AIcountry.
  [Ministry announcement](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/aktuelt/1.-oktober-styrkes-forbrukernes-rett-til-a-betale-med-kontanter/id3044260/)
  · [National inquiry, chapter 4](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/dokumenter/nou-2024-21/id3074368/?ch=4).
- Sveriges Riksbank describes an agreement effective 1 July 2026 for
  eligible physical card and PIN purchases of essential goods through
  data-communication outages lasting up to seven days. The agreement
  covers participating issuers and merchants. The Riksbank separately
  describes bank obligations for withdrawal, deposit and merchant cash
  services. These official descriptions establish design and scope.
  They do not demonstrate receipt in a combined power and telecom failure.
  [Offline payments](https://www.riksbank.se/en-gb/payments--cash/payment-preparedness/offline-payments/)
  · [Cash service obligations](https://www.riksbank.se/en-gb/payments--cash/notes--coins/obligation-for-major-banks-to-provide-certain-cash-services/).

The local worked case uses constructed prices, coverage and events.
