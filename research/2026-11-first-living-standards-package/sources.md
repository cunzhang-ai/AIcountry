# Design496 evidence and boundaries

- The pinned [2025 work account](../settlement/matched-national-work/01-observed-2025-and-boundaries.md)
  joins official Denmark national work tables and general-government accounts.
  The [compensation and weaker-year sensitivities](../settlement/matched-national-work/03-who-pays-for-the-shorter-week.md)
  apply authored work-calendar and payroll assumptions to the observed
  account. They establish neither a new tax base nor local prices, cash,
  privately supplied costs or qualified rosters. The local and regional
  percentage concerns observed compensation rather than all hours or costs.
- The pinned [2024 EU-SILC housing and warmth source record](../2026-11-housing-warmth-and-budget-coverage/sources.md)
  and [reconstructed overlap bounds](../settlement/housing-warmth-and-budget-coverage/01-evidence-and-first-budget-choice.md)
  compare common private-household indicator denominators. Indicator reach
  is a mechanical entry result rather than an estimate of programme
  exclusions or actual eligibility. The joint interval allows unknown overlap.
- [Fan, Schor, Kelly and Gu, 2025](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10636509)
  report well-being improvements in six-month income-preserving four-day
  week trials with pre-trial reorganization, using 2,896 individuals
  in 141 participating organizations and 12 comparison companies.
  Scope and participation limit generalization. The study supplies
  neither reimbursable public pay rates nor service-wide replacement,
  household-care or fiscal effects for the candidate country.
- The [opening holds and remedies](../settlement/opening-resilience-and-public-choice/01-govern-and-deliver-without-the-common-channel.md)
  and [three commitments](../settlement/education-care-and-scrutiny/01-three-commitments-one-opening-calendar.md)
  supply separate constructed tests of common dependencies. The two-window
  figures in Design496 are authored and have no common price unit with
  the Danish accounts.
