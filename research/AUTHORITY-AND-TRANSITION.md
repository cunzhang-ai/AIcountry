3 November 2026 · Candidate A8 rights/emergency boundary reconciled in144; A4/A15 and A10 date scope in143; A9/A10 core in129; other parts retain their scoped revision labels

This is the legal operating design for [AIcountry's country proposal](COUNTRY-DESIGN.md).
It allocates public authority through a complete decision chain. It also specifies
what must happen when power changes hands. A9 now carries the full RR protected-succession/closed-restoration scope; A10
now describes the selected EL single national PR reference instead of the
superseded two-track count. This repairs current candidate consumers, not an
adopted constitution or a renewed review of every older provision.
The earlier manuscript is a research
edition with AI-exclusive drafting permissions, not an operating constitution for
a known territory. These clauses are replacement candidates, not a change to that
edition, the software's permissions or any real person's legal rights.
The later [legal-hierarchy decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-legal-hierarchy/decision-record.md)
maps the integrated country's subsequent choices to distinct legal ranks.
It preserves the [pre-hierarchy wording](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-legal-hierarchy/AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION-pre-hierarchy.md)
