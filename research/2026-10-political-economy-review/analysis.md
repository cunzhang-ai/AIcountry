# Political economy review: can the country produce and pay for its guarantees?

2 October 2026 · Supporting analysis for country-design C05–C07/C09/C13–C14 · Research only

## Decision to which this review contributes

The [country design](../COUNTRY-DESIGN.md), integrated design 4 of 1 October 2026, selects a democratic mixed economy, essential services plus income security, and an **external settlement currency as a conservative planning baseline**. It leaves the final monetary regime to a real founding decision. This review asks whether that combination is a coherent *reference national settlement*, rather than merely a government expenditure plan. It does not claim a territory, bank charter, currency access, affected-public consent or power to enact a rule.

The reviewed [national settlement](../SOCIAL-FISCAL-SETTLEMENT.md) openly says that its 100,000 residents, GDP, tax receipts, staff, energy and financing are authored assumptions. Its model is useful for demonstrating that money, staff and energy are not interchangeable. It is not defective arithmetic. Its boundaries, however, omit the private economy and external account that would supply taxable income, workers, imports, bank credit and settlement liquidity. The [founding plan](../FOUNDING-AND-STRESS.md) correctly makes usable payments and an audited opening balance conditions for launch; it does not choose or finance an operating monetary system. The [status register](../DESIGN-STATUS.md) lists this as an open dependency while calling the reference country proposal coherent and complete. The latter description needs qualification: institutional authority is drafted; the production, banking and external-payment architecture is not yet a complete national reference.

This is a design judgment, not an empirical claim about any actual population. In the current fictional service package S, year-three revenue is 930 million CU, primary outlays 968 million CU, interest 34.025 million CU, and the model assumes 100 million CU of maturing principal can be refinanced and 60 million CU of new debt issued. It closes with 137.975 million CU of public liquid assets. If refinancing of that 100 alone disappears while all other assumptions implausibly remain favorable, year-three closing cash becomes 37.975 million CU, 62.025 below the *model's* 100 buffer. This sensitivity is an arithmetic deduction from the published inputs, not a crisis forecast. It shows why the financing and payment mechanism cannot be treated as a minor later parameter.

## Competing diagnoses and coherent options

One diagnosis is mainly **fiscal discipline**: the selected package can work if Parliament imposes credible borrowing limits and adjusts spending. Another is **productive and external dependence**: a balanced public budget can still face unavailable care workers, inaccessible bank deposits, missing medicines, energy, housing materials or foreign settlement. A third is **monetary governance**: domestic issuance may relax nominal liquidity constraints but creates a different inflation, exchange-rate and political-control problem. These explanations can coexist; simply adding money or state ownership does not settle their conflicts.

| Reference alternative | Operating arrangement and possible gain | Failure and distribution question |
|---|---|---|
| **0. Retain design 4 wording only** | Keep the external-currency assumption and public cash/staff model; require real-world calibration at founding. Low immediate research burden and no premature currency choice. | No accountable operator or rule for deposit failure, imported essentials, settlement access or a lost refinancing auction. “Funded” may mean appropriated but unpayable. The burden of silent interruption falls on claimants with the least ability to wait. Retention is acceptable only if the completeness claim is narrowed accordingly. |
| **1. Specify an external-currency reference system** | Lawful access to the settlement currency and clearing network; public receipts/payments and debt in that unit; an audited public liquidity schedule; supervised, resolvable banks; a limited, explicitly funded deposit/payment-continuity arrangement; publicly scrutinized contracts or inventories for critical imports; and due-date priority rules enacted by the Assembly subject to rights and appeal. This is the smallest completion of the *chosen planning baseline*. | The state cannot issue the settlement currency and has no assumed unlimited lender of last resort. Reserves, insurance funds and credit lines have costs and finite coverage. Bank losses, capital flight or import-price shocks can force visible tax, borrowing, rationing or spending choices; low-income people, remote places and people dependent on continuous treatment may bear more harm. Preferential payment queues can become political patronage unless transparent and reviewable. |
| **2. Select a domestic-currency system by legitimate founding choice** | A publicly mandated monetary authority, payment settlement, prudential supervision, bank resolution and a fiscal/monetary division are adopted together. Domestic-currency liquidity and countercyclical policy become possible, with legislative scrutiny and independent operational review. | Issuance cannot create nurses, energy or imported medicines. Foreign-currency debt and imports still require foreign exchange. Exchange-rate changes, inflation and asset-price effects redistribute burdens among workers, savers, pensioners, debtors and import-dependent patients. Executive control of the monetary authority or emergency credit may also finance incumbents and weaken opposition. This is a coherent rival, not a free financing add-on to option 1. |

The research selection is **option 1 as the provisional reference architecture**, conditional on real lawful access, rather than a claim that it is best for every real country. It honors the chosen external-currency planning premise and makes its hard limits explicit. A real affected public might prefer option 2 or another lawful arrangement after comparing actual banking, trade, debt denomination and distribution. The choice of a monetary regime is political and constitutional; an AI-authored research record cannot decide it for them. This selection must not alter current law or research-platform permissions. If the project declines to specify option 1's operating requirements, it should instead retain option 0 and stop calling the production/monetary side complete.

## Necessary reconciliation across the country

The proposed planning test is a **linked set of accounts and decision paths**, not one technocratic score. The [UN System of National Accounts](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/Nationalaccount/sna.asp) describes production, distribution, consumption, saving and investment across households, business, government and the rest of the world. The [IMF balance-of-payments manual](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/manuals-guides/issues/2016/12/31/balance-of-payments-manual-sixth-edition-22588) supplies an external-account framework. The [UN SEEA Central Framework](https://seea.un.org/en/methodology/seea-central-framework) supplies separate physical/environmental accounting. These are measurement frameworks, not evidence that any AIcountry proposal works.

1. **Production and distribution.** Trace essential outputs and their inputs through households, firms, cooperatives, public providers and imports. Identify where the same skilled worker, dwelling, land parcel, construction crew or unit of energy appears in several promised programs. Account for private investment and jobs whose tax base and living standards may change when resources shift to public service; include unpaid care without assuming families will absorb a public shortage. Do not infer adequate service from GDP or public staffing alone.
2. **Financial stocks, flows and control.** Reconcile public cash, debt maturities, contingent guarantees, deposits, bank balance sheets and private credit; disclose who owns the settlement accounts and who can interrupt a payment. A treasury cash balance is not the economy's foreign assets. An insured deposit is a potential public liability requiring a funded legal scheme, not automatically part of the existing 600 million CU debt. Bank resolution must preserve essential payment access without guaranteeing every investor or permitting the incumbent to seize opponents' funds. The [IMF's analysis of full dollarization](https://www.imf.org/external/pubs/ft/issues/issues24/index.htm) describes the limited lender-of-last-resort capacity where the state cannot issue the settlement currency; it does not select a regime for AIcountry.
3. **External and physical dependence.** For critical medicines, food, energy equipment and payment technology, distinguish local capacity, contracted imports, currency needed when due, supplier concentration, shipping access and substitutable stocks. Inspect exports or lawful financing that would earn or supply external currency. Separately track water, land, energy, material throughput, emissions and ecosystem damage; a public-service GWh line is not an economy-wide energy or ecological budget. Use local evidence and affected communities to set limits; never invent “green” capacity from a price or a national average.
4. **Who decides a shortfall.** Treasury and an independent auditor expose cash, import and production gaps before interruption. Service authorities report who cannot receive care, shelter, food, power or a payment on time. Cabinet presents packages showing tax, debt, reserve use, procurement, import substitution, demand reduction and distributional effects. The Assembly chooses within rights and external obligations, with genuine opposition access. Courts and remedies protect priority against discrimination and unlawful denial, but cannot create a foreign currency balance or a supplier. Emergency orders cannot silently rewrite the social floor, transfer private assets or grant an AI sovereign allocation power.

This accounting is compatible with a plural economy. It does not require central planning of every firm, a single state data lake, constant household surveillance or an exact annual material prediction. Aggregate and protected confidential information can reveal dependencies while privacy, trade-secret and due-process limits remain contestable. The core obligation is to expose which claimed guarantees depend on which supply and payment paths, and who has authority when they fail.

## Recommended changes to the main design

- **C05:** Retain plural ownership, but add a publicly reviewable *essential-capability strategy*: workforce training/retention, maintenance, supplier diversity and domestic or external sourcing for critical services. Distinguish effective production from fiscal transfers; safeguard workers and ecosystems in transition. Avoid a blanket nationalization or self-sufficiency mandate.
- **C06:** Make the external-currency baseline an explicit provisional operating package: lawful clearing/payment access, public liquidity by due date, debt/refinancing terms, bank supervision and resolution, bounded funded depositor/payment continuity, and an external trade/foreign-currency account. Do not describe the modeled 60 million CU borrowing or refinancing as available credit. Publish limits and distributional implications of any rescue, deposit coverage or creditor priority. Domestic currency remains a legitimate alternative for public choice, but not a silent backstop.
- **C07/C09:** Link each essential guarantee to critical imported inputs, payment and physical replacement paths and to land/water/energy conditions; distinguish legal entitlement, appropriated amount, safe supply, actual access and delivered remedy. Rights survive shortage; an unprovided item must be reported as a breach rather than counted as access.
- **C13/C14 and status:** Add the rollover failure as a bounded authored sensitivity, or a more useful real-world failure chain, and qualify “complete reference design” as complete in *institutional outline* but conditional in monetary/production architecture. Founding phase E must require an adopted monetary/bank-resolution package plus evidenced settlement and essential-import access. No public vote or legal text itself supplies these capacities.

## Objections, limits and next bounded test

Option 1 may tie the country to a foreign monetary cycle or unsympathetic payment gate; option 2 may better absorb a domestic demand shock. Conversely, domestic issuance may permit inflationary hidden taxation and political credit allocation; reserve rules can protect creditors while depriving vulnerable residents. These are value and distribution conflicts as well as empirical questions. No numeric reserve, deposit-insurance or import-stock threshold is justified here. A different territory's trade structure, law, bank deposits, debt currency and available suppliers may overturn the provisional selection. People dependent on imported care, bank deposits, unpaid family care, export jobs or affected land need participation, not merely modeled representation.

The next bounded research action is a **single linked balance-sheet and essential-input exercise** for a specified lawful jurisdiction, *if one is brought forward by people with standing*: opening public and bank liabilities; currency and clearing rights; deposit and refinancing exposures; critical-service input/import map; energy/land/water limits; and one combined refinancing/payment/import disruption with distribution and remedy delivery. Completion means every claimed essential payment and service has a documented accountable operator, lawful authority, due-date resource path, fallback and explicitly recorded remaining failure. It does not mean all shortages can be eliminated. Until a real jurisdiction exists, the project can amend the reference design to state this architecture and its limits, but cannot certify the actual capacity or obtain consent.

It used no affected-party testimony, original economic observations, independent operational assessment or legitimate enactment.
