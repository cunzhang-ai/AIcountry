# TX01–TX06: exact liability, evidence and assessment finality

*Part of [C06/40](../40-tax-law-and-accountable-revenue.md); design100 research candidate, not enacted law.*

**TX01 — Exact law and a usable account.** The Assembly names each taxable person, event, period, base, rate, relief, withholding and remittance duty, relevant territorial/source rule, legally due date, prospective version and competent national/local recipient. A Revenue Service form, model classification, interpretive note, employer file or foreign schedule is no tax law. Personal PA09–PA12 uses one individual and its actual annual receipts, with the lawful ordinary foundation excluded once, no household pooling or account multiplication. The selected annual calendar year is 1 January–31 December and the ordinary annual reconciliation and remaining undisputed personal-tax payment is due 30 April next year; a lawfully designated payer's provisional withholding is credited to the person on proved deduction even if that payer defaults, with payer remittance due the 15th of the next month. Other tax bases require their own actually enacted due calendars. Give assisted/offline filing, language and disability support, named help and an acknowledgement of first receipt; a person need not file a technical legal dossier to report an error.

**TX02 — Correct payment finality.** A taxpayer's obligation is discharged to the amount of a valid tax-channel instruction accepted irrevocably under its published M06 rule **and actually debited from the taxpayer**, or another expressly enacted final cash-payment event. Preserve the payer's distinct unpaid withholding/remittance duty, protected wage credit, M05 client ownership, tax-currency and foreign-law limits, and M06 correction of erroneous or unauthorized payment. Record who owns cash at each point and when Treasury actually receives it; discharge is not proof of Treasury cash.

**TX03 — Notice, reply and final assessment.** A proposed changed/estimated assessment identifies tax law/version, person, period, original taxable facts, computation by source, relief/withholding/settled-payment credits, disagreement, due amount or refund, intended evidence request and appeal route. The competent assessment officer gives the taxpayer the relied-on material and ordinarily at least B16's 20 working days for answer, with counsel/interpretation and protected third-party data. The officer then gives reasoned final item-by-item assessment, actual credits and due date within B16's 30 working days of protected first receipt of the taxpayer's request or response, with only one timely notified, documented and necessary extension of at most 30 working days under AP05. A valid previously enacted self-assessment/withholding due obligation remains distinct from a new disputed incremental assessment.

**TX04 — Civil assessment period, independent of crime.** Serve a new or increased ordinary tax assessment with usable notice within four years after the statutory return/remittance due date for that tax period, whether the return is late, absent or amended; no software version, changed officer, renewed request or continuing effect restarts it. Only a court finding on original evidence that deliberate material concealment of a legally required taxable fact prevented reasonable discovery excludes the actual proved concealed time, capped at two additional years and at six years total from that due date. An honest classification error, employer failure, missing office record, ordinary complexity or a bare K22 allegation does not toll. Serve the usable **final administrative assessment** within the bar; a proposal, inquiry, unfinished computation or K22 referral cannot reserve more time. Allow TX03 reply/decision inside it. A valid timely final assessment retains only its assessed scope during review; the law cannot increase an expired amount by relabelling it a penalty, collection charge or a later year. K22's intentional crime and Q12 charge clock are separate and cannot create an omitted tax base or revive a barred civil assessment. Taxpayer refund/correction claims retain B15's actual discovery/access rules; no new bulk extinguishment of legacy claims occurs through this proposal.

**TX05 — Necessary records, not a financial panopticon.** Each audit or third-party demand identifies the enacted tax, person, period, discrepancy, precise records, custodian, purpose, retention and protected challenge. Routine payer withholding/remittance reports, including a bank's exact enacted interest/withholding return if selected, need their own prospective law and minimum fields; they do not authorize all-bank transaction feeds, movement data, social graphs or AI training. Compelled nonroutine third-party bank account histories/details, nonconsensual business entry, home entry, search, seizure, messages or surveillance require specific independent judicial authorization under P/PD and actual source law; a tax audit label or V03 analogy supplies no warrant exception. No foreign server/account compulsion or treaty information exchange exists without its actual law/consent and safeguards. Keep confidential source custody and access logs; separate public aggregate tax accounts from private family/clinical records.

**TX06 — Proof and a rebuttable estimate.** The authority bears the burden under B14 ordinary civil proof of the actual taxable event, legal person, period, base, correct amount and why a proposed credit is unavailable; it must identify contrary source evidence. A taxpayer claiming a deduction, relief or payment within their own control must give reasonably available supporting facts, with B13 assistance and third-party source routes where the state/provider holds the proof. An estimate is permitted only after a specified necessary record was not reasonably obtainable despite lawful notice/opportunity, or an evidenced material discrepancy makes the actual amount unavailable; state source method, comparable observations, uncertainty and exclusions. A model score, unexplained industry average, asset ownership or failure to use a smartphone does not prove income, intentional concealment or a penalty. The person may rebut the estimate on evidence; the final authority/court decides actual law and facts anew. Criminal K22 keeps its own intent and beyond-reasonable-doubt burden.
