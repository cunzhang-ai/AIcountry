# What US wildland mobilization records show

The 2025 National Multi-Agency Coordinating Group operating plan
describes a delegated group that sets priorities and can direct
reallocation across geographic areas. It names protection of the
public and firefighter lives as its overriding consideration and
also considers retaining initial response capacity. This is evidence
of an operating rule and institutional division of responsibility.
The source does not validate a general national power across services.
[2025 NMAC operating plan, printed pages 1–4](https://www.nifc.gov/sites/default/files/NICC/5-NMAC/Ops%20Plan/Current%20NMAC%20Ops%20Plan.pdf)

The National Interagency Coordination Center publishes tactical
request status for the reported US system. The crew-mobilization
sections give the following totals.

| Fire year | Requests | Filled | Canceled | Unable to fill |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2024 | 3,350 | 1,839 | 636 | 875 |
| 2025 | 2,126 | 1,530 | 264 | 332 |

Among requests that were not canceled, the unable-to-fill proportion
was about 32.2 percent in 2024 and 17.8 percent in 2025. Keeping all
requests in the denominator gives 26.1 percent and 15.6 percent.
Each denominator answers a different question. The 2025 report also
lists 50 days at preparedness level 4 and none at level 5. Those
calendar totals cannot locate unfilled requests by day. [2024 NICC
annual report, printed page 24](https://www.nifc.gov/sites/default/files/NICC/2-Predictive%20Services/Intelligence/Annual%20Reports/2024/annual_report_2025.pdf)
· [2025 NICC annual report, printed pages 17 and 25](https://www.nifc.gov/sites/default/files/NICC/2-Predictive%20Services/Intelligence/Annual%20Reports/2025/annual_report_2025_1.pdf)

These are orders, with cancellations kept distinct. A filled order
does not establish safe, timely local operation. The aggregate does
not count unique incidents or crews, unmet need that was never
requested, harm or the causal effect of priority rules. The two years
are one reporting family. They show why a national account should
expose unfilled demand and use a separate receiving check.
