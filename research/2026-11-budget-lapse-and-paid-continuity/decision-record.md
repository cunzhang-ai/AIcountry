# Design242 decision record. Budget lapse and paid continuity

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned Design241](baseline.json)

## Decision and affected people

Choose [BLC01–BLC05](../country/C06/66-budget-lapse-and-paid-continuity.md)
for the lapse portion of the country design. Keep standing rights separate
from the 90-day operational bridge. In the candidate, an Act enacted before
lapse can reserve a named source and ceiling for a separate 30-day closeout
of debts earned under the bridge. Price settlement as part of the first
budget, including overlap with ordinary obligations. Apply existing due dates,
funding rules, direct remedies and protections against transfer of purpose.

Households with little liquidity, small suppliers and workers bear more
cost from an identical delay. A lost water service or election appeal can
cause harm that later cash cannot reverse. A lapse also risks allowing the
incumbent to select which challengers and essential recipients can function.
The relevant protections are timely receiving and enforceable political
competition, with the elected Assembly retaining control of new spending.

## Compare distinct designs

| Choice | Strong reason | Material exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Retain standing duties and end the bridge without a separate settlement window | Simple expiry. Affected suppliers and oversight can see the full extent of exhausted annual power. | Earned claims may survive with money in a restricted account and no lawful means to settle. Suppliers bear involuntary delay, including when services already operated within the bridge. |
| Roll forward the last annual appropriations automatically | Protects ongoing operation against legislative obstruction. A clear rule can reduce cliff-edge pressure. | The old policy mix can persist through strategic deadlock. Past allocations can include discontinued work, exclude new need and carry a larger executive mandate. Restricted money, staffing and independent delivery can still fail. |
| Continue a legislated protected schedule and expire operational authority while settling eligible accrued bridge debts | Protects rights, opposition and already earned payment while preserving a real vote over new activity. | Requires an intelligible standing schedule and genuinely separate execution. An arbitrary ceiling or cutoff can shift costs to people with slow evidence or disputes. |
| Lapse all annual operations and rely on explicit interim votes | Requires elected authorization of each additional public commitment. | A blocker could extract concessions by interrupting independent review or essential services. Every new appropriation still depends on actual source and receiving. |

Select the third choice. The candidate requires a verified purpose-specific
ceiling and residual source rule for the closeout. Existing DB and B19 powers
stay within their enacted limits. A failed day-60 vote cannot create funding,
continue old office or prevent a later lawful Assembly vote. Lawmakers can
choose a longer or shorter settlement window prospectively on evidence,
including unequal effects of acceptance or appeal delays. Protect independent
review from the litigating executive and keep the watchdog's authority bounded
by purpose, amount and actual duty. Its failure triggers ordinary independent
challenge and a reported unmet obligation.

## Evidence, authority and transition

The [official comparators](sources.md) show distinct legal mechanisms for
continuing expenditure and paying previously incurred obligations. They
support the feasibility of distinguishing permissions in law. They do not
calibrate the candidate's 30 days or show that this institutional mix
preserves democracy or improves people's lives.

The [authored case](../settlement/budget-lapse/01-due-money-and-independent-receipt.md)
reproduces cash and payment outcomes with a qualified provider and named
source assumed. It shows how an unpayable claim differs from an unfunded one
and how a common payment rail can defeat nominally independent execution.
It provides no observed recipient or service outcome.

Any enactment needs the Assembly's lawful procedure, rights review and the
budget and source instruments. Assign Treasury, independent payers, records
custodians and receiving parties in those instruments. Preserve actual law,
earned claims, payment restrictions, open reviews and succession. In an
adopted migration, retain the existing rule for obligations already incurred
unless valid transitional law supplies an equivalent lawful settlement path.
Funding, actual affected-party participation, independent qualified review,
legitimate enactment and operational readiness remain open.

## Next bounded decision

At the first elected budget's repair and contingency vote, ask the fiscal and
service owners to produce one payable schedule for an actual dated benefit,
one urgent election or court remedy, one essential supplier and one bridge
debt. Within the voted administrative budget, compare the declared alternate
and a common-rail loss across days 90–93. Inspect legal authority, actual
restricted balances, signed access, receiving deadlines and the owner of each
unmet duty. Complete the check only when dates and receipt can be reconciled,
or record each open gap for an elected remedy. Reopen the closeout period if
real acceptance times, appeals, insolvency or affected people show systematic
exclusion. A disrupted audit or unavailable custody suspends assurance and
preserves the claims and direct challenge.
