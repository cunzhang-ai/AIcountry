# C06. Financing, production and useful-time delivery

16 October 2026 · Design87 · FD01–FD12 proposed country selection, not enacted law

The country needs a credible way to finance and supply its guarantees. Join
individual resources, businesses, public services, insurance and external trade
instead of treating tax receipts, output and cash as interchangeable. The
[dated six-sector bridge](../../2026-10-finance-and-delivery-bridge/README.md)
now computes planned counterpart transactions and same-measure production tests.
It replaces none of the actual unknowns with verified capacity.

**Reject all four tested tax/source combinations as complete launch plans.**
Their planned annual accounts balance as identities but lack sufficient disclosed
finance and independent production. Keep NP's protection-first mixed provision
conditional. No selected tax rates, forced saving, seized pension reserve or
AI productivity gain supplies the missing resources. Broader current legal and
material obligations remain unpriced, so even a passing subset would not qualify
an operating country. Existing lawful claims and useful-time remedies continue.
