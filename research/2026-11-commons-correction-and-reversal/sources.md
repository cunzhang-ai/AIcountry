# Design510 sources and scope

- The [Royal Commission report on Australia's Robodebt Scheme](https://robodebt.royalcommission.gov.au/publications/report)
  examined the public debt programme and recommended transparency and
  independent scrutiny for automation in public services, better opportunities
  to challenge a proposed debt before recovery and clearer routes for raising
  legality concerns. The findings concern the Australian scheme and authorities.
- [Services Australia's 2023–24 annual report](https://www.transparency.gov.au/publications/services-australia/services-australia/services-australia-annual-report-2023-24/part-8%3A-management-and-accountability/8.1-royal-commission-into-robodebt-scheme)
  describes the agency's recommendation-review processes and independent
  assurance. Administrative implementation reporting does not establish the
  timing or completeness of each individual's remedy.
- The [worked comparison](../settlement/commons-correction-and-reversal/01-stop-the-practice-restore-the-right.md)
  uses invented quantities and a hypothetical court order. Neither the
  identities nor the outcomes are claims about the Australian case.
