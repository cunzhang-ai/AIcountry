# FB17–FB20. Turn the budget comparison into an elected sequence

*Design212 · Part of [C06 fiscal durability](../62-lasting-services-and-fiscal-shocks.md).*

**FB17 — Give first-year priority to enforceable protection and a viable
path to further delivery.** The first budget under a retrofit or newly
constituted country identifies and finances continuing lawful obligations,
including independent administration, electoral and remedy duties.
If shortages prevent full performance, invoke existing protective and
correction routes and expose the missing capacity. Separately choose
preparation that can relieve a demonstrated bottleneck, such as paid
training, relief staffing or repair, where it fits without displacing
a protected duty. A competent ordinary route and evidenced low-risk
improvements can continue. The Assembly can select faster reform when
its lawful sources and capacity hold together. It must see the cost of
delaying gains and choose any new burdens through ordinary law.
The research preference under unresolved common constraints is to start
with funded repair and enabling capacity. It leaves prospective benefits
and their future elected choice explicit.

**FB18 — Vote on first starts, later decisions and the consequences of loss.**
Under DB, FB and T, state what is authorized now, what remains an option and
which enacted dates and accrued claims already bind. For new cohorts, show
the actual dated cash, appropriate workers and supplies, accepted operators,
independent remedies and continuing commitments under both paths. Specify
who covers urgent risk during any lawful interval. Later decisions may
change the form or date of a still unadopted addition. Existing entitlements
and enacted commencement are governed by their own law. A signed funding
agreement, a model or an analyst's report cannot start or suspend a right.
No omnibus approval of a budget forecast and no additional Treasury veto.

**FB19 — Return material conflicts for a choice with usable alternatives.**
When a loss would threaten actual pay, service or remedy, the responsible
sector and Treasury identify the earliest consequence and report it under
FB05–FB07. Use the already authorized protection and scarcity routes where
applicable. Bring feasible choices for elected correction in useful time,
including lawful extra source, slower still-unadopted starts, different
provision or an expressly chosen loss of a discretionary benefit. State
who carries each cost. No hidden diversion from protected independent
budgets, unpaid family transfer, withheld wages, or use of reserves
already promised elsewhere. Where even the retained plan lacks adequate
capacity, show the outstanding claim, correction and actual recipient.
Keep decision-making accessible through lawful government replacement.

**FB20 — Revisit the comparison after a material change.** Reconcile actual
receipt, missed services, staffing and cost to the original paired
baselines at budget review and after a material change. Preserve the
original forecast and decision. A later cohort needs fresh qualified
support for changed duties, sources and capacity, and ordinary lawful
authority. Keep restrictive custody and actual implementation claims
separate. Publish who gains time, who loses income or access, which
unmet duties persist and whether new routes can be reversed safely.
The existing FB09–FB12 test governs actual released resources. No
retroactive validation of an early start through improved later evidence.
