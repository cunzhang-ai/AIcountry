# Due-time and independent-path counterexamples

*Design 57 authored failure cases; none is an observed patient's or contestant's account.*

The candidate Act makes the earlier useful protection time prevail over
an administrative outer clock. A response may arrive on the administrative
deadline and still fail the duty. The [model](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-continuity-law/model.py)
tests that distinction using five deliberately adverse cases:

| Duty | Candidate outer limit | Authored latest useful time | Completed action | Finding |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| New hardship protection | 24 hours | 8 hours | 18 hours | Outer clock met; essential protection late |
| Urgent clinical receiving | 2 hours | 1 hour | 2 hours | Routing clock met; safe time missed |
| Interrupted paid care | 6 hours | 3 hours | 5 hours | Interim clock met; essential task late |
| Independent interim consideration | 24 hours | 10 hours | 20 hours | Court clock met; effective opportunity lost |
| Qualified contestant's baseline access | 48 hours | 36 hours | 40 hours | Support clock met; campaign opportunity lost |

These times are invented counterexamples, not recommended medical waiting
periods. All five claims survive failure. No administrative timer licenses
delay, detention, coercive treatment, a changed vote or an unqualified judge.
E14 requires an opposing-party hearing opportunity within 72 hours and
permits only one further period of at most 72 hours on fresh necessity
findings. Serial orders cannot restart it; proper notice and a meaningful
opportunity permit a hearing despite nonattendance. Five further model cases
check expiry without extension, a justified extension, attempted serial
renewal, nonattendance and a shorter six-hour source-law limit. Underlying
statutory claims survive the temporary order's expiry. Qualification needs a reasoned
decision within the candidate five-day outer limit and sooner where the
election opportunity requires it; waiting for certification cannot hide a
timely filing or disable direct court review.

## A staffed annual core is not a full night roster

A separate 24-hour sample has four consecutive six-hour blocks with
different staff for each block; rest, replacements and qualification are
assumed, not established. Authored productive hours inside each block
leave paid care short by 12 hours, judicial work by one and separate counsel
by four. Across the four blocks those gaps are 48, four and 16 hours.
Clinical, clerical, execution, benefits and contest processing meet their
authored hour totals only; that does not establish safe location, case
allocation, supplies, actual pay or useful response time. The sample is a
bounded subset, not a conversion of all national FTE into local shifts.

Adding overtime, unpaid relatives or one shared professional is not an
implicit solution. A substitute must actually have the right skill and
time; diversion from another care visit or case must be recorded. The
annual cost allocation remains a separate conditional account.

## The same controller can disable both doors

The path test fails the primary record key `R1` and payment/execution key
`P1`. Benefit, contest-support and remedy-execution routes each receive
two hypothetical variants. Two app/office routes that both depend on
those failed nodes have **no complete surviving path**. Preassigned
alternates using separate `R2`/`P2` and distinct competent verifier,
qualification evidence, filing and judge nodes have a surviving graph
path in this sample. That establishes no actual legal competence, safe
record, cash, staff or independent controller; delivered outcomes remain
unknown in both variants. Courts may still hear matters even where a
downstream executor cannot act.

The [E13–E16 rules](../../country/C06/19/04-remedy-and-political-continuity.md)
therefore define a narrow lawful substitute and preserved direct review,
not a new general succession office. A live test would follow original
records and actual receiver/executor availability before the useful time,
with both cooperating and withholding officials. No such trial occurred.
