# Design379 decision. Review cumulative impairment through existing forums

## Baseline and diagnosis

Design378 protects immediate succession under RR01–RR10 and has a
cumulative test for delegated rules under DR. A9 already states that
different statutes cannot jointly disable protected democratic functions.
The unresolved mechanism is how to bring ordinary laws, delegated acts,
finance, records and implementation omissions into one causal record
when none individually changes a constitutional rule or defeats access.

Select [RR11–RR17](../country/C14/08/04-cumulative-impairment-of-democratic-control.md)
as charter protection and implementing duties. Judge actual functioning,
with concrete standing and a material causal threshold. The proposer
discloses the whole necessary route. Challenged authorities produce
original facts within their control. Existing courts apply the rule and
existing executive or civil actors execute limited lawful relief.
There is no new approval body or permission to spend.

## Choices and objections

Retain isolated act review and specific sector safeguards. It preserves
simple responsibility but misses the case in which three controls
exhaust a common deadline. A mandatory cross-government independent
preclearance body could find more links. It would add a veto, another
appointment and appropriation chain and opportunities for its own capture.
Select joined effects review with existing forums, an evidence duty and
tailored orders. Retain sector-level process and ordinary elected choices.

Judges or opposition could stretch an effects test into policy control,
and disclosure or litigation can consume limited review time. Require
a concrete affected opportunity and evidence, no automatic suspension,
narrow relief and prior-law jurisdiction. Give timely reasons and a
fair chance to answer. An effective, lawful, less impairing substitute
permits reorganization. Material shortages remain visible without
imputing intent, pretending resources exist or merging distinct rights.

## Evidence, migration and next check

The [original public records](sources.md) distinguish joined procedural
impairment, composition and an executed remedy. The first two overlap
as evidence. The [worked comparison](../settlement/cumulative-impairment-of-democratic-control/01-three-changes-before-a-contest.md)
uses authored tasks and verifies its specific deadlines. Actual affected
consultation, independent legal review and capability were not obtained.

Apply existing A9, RR and T to any lawful adoption. Identify the lawfully
competent forum and actors, active orders, usable dates, record custodians,
payer and executor before commencement. Preserve accrued claims, current
office terms, pending cases and valid final judgments. Prospective review
can address continuing effects; retrospective invalidity and liability
need their own grounds. DR review and CJ12's closed own-court jurisdiction
remain. This edition exercises no public authority.

Next reconcile one protected electoral or court deadline from original
records across one budget, one records change and one implementation act.
Have independent counsel and actual executors check intake, separate
authority and receipt before that deadline, with the implicated
institution and affected people heard. Include a genuine efficiency
repair and a blocked-execution path. Reopen on strategic docket flooding,
ordinary reform delayed without material harm, a jointly missed deadline,
or a simpler path showing independent and timely execution.
