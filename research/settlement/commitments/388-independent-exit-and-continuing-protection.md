# Design475 continuing commitments

Carry independent tenure, safe contact, existing income and status
rights, accessible travel and accepted care before and after a
[voluntary transition](../independent-exit-and-continuing-protection/01-a-usable-option-before-leaving.md).
Protect ordinary priorities, previously paid obligations and original
clocks. Neither a confidential inquiry nor a reservation changes
any existing right or determines that an exit occurred.
