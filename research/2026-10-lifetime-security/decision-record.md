# Lifetime security: retirement income, care and democratic adjustment

4 October 2026 · Integrated design 30 research selection; not enacted law

Prepared by an AI assistant for the project's working design. No independent
reviewer, affected claimant, worker, carer or adopting public participated in
this selection. The assistant has no legislative or fiduciary authority.

## Identity, baseline and bounded decision

The exact [design-29 predecessor](predecessor-design-29/README.md) is pinned by
checksums. Its C05, C06 and C07 preserved pension claims and offered an income
floor and long-term care, but did not select a national retirement-income
architecture, its financing, record and appeal chain, or its response to
demographic change. The preserved [source manuscript](../2026-09-readiness/source-documents.json)
contains `work.labor-protection` Article 5, which
required a pension schedule and actuarial balance, but its numerical content
and authority were not adopted into the working country. This record selects
a conditional architecture for the research design, not benefit amounts,
ratification or actual operation.

## Situation, values and rival explanations

People need income when paid work ends or becomes unsafe, and many have
interrupted earnings because of care, disability, unemployment or exclusion.
Older people also need actual health and long-term care, which a money transfer
cannot produce. A payment promise can harm younger cohorts if it hides future
tax and worker demands. Conversely, an actuarially balanced formula can impose
poverty if it silently reduces individual benefits. These are design risks,
not observations about a real adopting population.

| Coherent option | Attraction | Material failure or tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Retain design 29's undefined pension reference | No premature rate or age choice | Administration, financing and cohort burden remain unresolved |
| Means-tested older-person assistance only | Targets scarce cash on low-income people | Repeated eligibility control, asset cliffs, weak independent claims and possible stigma |
| Flat universal pension only | Simple individual floor and broad coverage | Ignores accrued earnings claims and may need a higher general tax than the public accepts |
| Compulsory individual funded accounts | Explicit personal balance and some asset accumulation | Investment, inflation and longevity risk; care and low-wage histories accumulate less; transition liabilities remain |
| **Selected basic payment plus public earnings insurance** | Individual poverty floor, portable earned supplement and visible intergenerational account | Contribution burden, administration, record errors, future fiscal pressure and possible political capture |

The selected combination has two distinct functions. A general-revenue basic
payment protects each qualifying older resident independent of a spouse or
complete earnings history. A capped mandatory earnings-related public
insurance benefit rewards covered work while publicly financed credits
recognize bounded care and interruption periods. Other income support remains
available before qualification, and health and care claims remain separate.
The earnings tier's working finance is pooled current contributions for
current benefits with a bounded liquidity reserve and explicit public backing;
it does not pretend each contribution is an individually invested asset.
The exact pension age, amount, accrual rate, credits, indexation and residence
conditions require law and an actual population, budget and public choice.

## Authority, resources and continuity

The Assembly enacts eligibility, financing, benefit formula, record rights,
appeals and each material adjustment. Cabinet proposes an integrated budget.
Revenue collects lawful contributions; a social-protection authority records
and pays individual claims; treasury provides cash; an independent public
actuarial service reports scenarios; courts and independent appeals correct
denials and records. Their appointments, original evidence, operating funds
and remedy execution must be assessed against shared-controller capture.
Neither actuaries, reserve managers nor AI decide the rule or automatically
raise retirement age. A reserve is a risky asset in the same C06 perimeter,
not an extra nurse, imported medicine or tax receipt.

The C06 account must show due payments, accrued liabilities, contributions,
public care credits, legacy pensions, reserves and actual care capacity by
cohort and due date. Scenarios vary longevity, births, migration, employment,
wages, prices, care demand and productivity. They are inputs to an elected
decision, not facts about this proposed country. A shortage triggers a
published current-claim and future-accrual comparison of contributions,
taxes, timing, care investment and other spending under rights and law.
Retirement reform cannot quietly make relatives the unpaid care workforce,
stop a due payment or erase accrued records. Prospectivity and lawful review
protect people who planned under earlier rules while allowing future policy
to be revised through democratic choice.

C05 now connects work and interrupted careers to retirement claims; C07
specifies income, care and payment; C06 counts cohort obligations; C14 inventories
and tests lawful transition; C13 tests one overlapping income/care failure.
The integrated overview, status, social/fiscal settlement and chapter map
are affected consumers. Existing pensions and care claims continue under
their applicable law until a lawful, funded successor accepts them.

## Evidence, objections and limits

The [ILO Social Protection Floors Recommendation No. 202](https://www.ilo.org/resource/ilo-social-protection-floors-recommendation-2012-no-202)
supports basic income security in old age and accountable, sustainable
administration; it is a recommendation, not enacted AIcountry law. The
[OECD 2025 pension-system taxonomy](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/pensions-at-a-glance-2025_e40274c1-en/full-report/component-7.html)
documents basic, targeted and earnings-related architectures, while its
[gender pension-gap analysis](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/2025/11/pensions-at-a-glance-2025_76510fe4/full-report/gender-pension-gap_90ed13b5.html)
examines the effect of unequal earnings and care histories. The
[WHO long-term-care package](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240086555)
supports treating care capacity as a service system. These sources are
different evidence families but do not identify an optimal formula for a
nonexistent population. No original pension records, income distribution,
life tables, workforce data or affected-person testimony were reviewed.

Material objections remain. A universal basic payment may direct public
funds to wealthy retirees; tax incidence and whether a targeted top-up would
protect low-income people better require comparison. The earnings tier can
reproduce wage inequality even with credits; a more redistributive formula
may be warranted. Public insurance can be captured by short-term benefit
promises or austerity; a published actuarial office can be captured too, and
there is no independent authority without staff, funds, records and enforceable
review. Contribution rates can depress wages or formal work; migration and
unpaid care can shift the burden between groups. Later retirement may be
unfair for hazardous workers or those with shorter healthy lives. A basic
payment could be too small, or crowd out care if both are counted as one
success. None is resolved by declaring the model fiscally balanced.

The result is a **research selection only**. There was no affected-party
participation, legal authority, approved contribution, actuarial valuation,
staffed payment operator, care-place inventory or observed wellbeing outcome.
An AI-generated stress case is not independent evidence. The decision needs
fresh final review with real people and applicable law before adoption.

## Next bounded question

For a real affected jurisdiction, compare this two-layer package with a
means-tested floor, flat pension and funded accounts using original records
and a common 30-year cohort, cash, workforce and care-capacity account.
Report distribution, poverty, tax and contribution incidence, record error,
partial-retirement behavior and continuity under inflation and low employment.
Actual costs, claimant objections, evidence of unaffordable promises or
demonstrated care displacement reopen this selection. If such evidence is
missing, retain the design as conditional rather than claiming readiness.
