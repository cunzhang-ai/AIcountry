# Design435. Danish central-government opening liquidity

- The December 2025 strategy, table 1, provides the forecast as
  available before 2026. It reports separately the projected account,
  financing need and proposed instruments.
  [Danmarks Nationalbank, strategy for 2026](https://www.nationalbanken.dk/media/q03btq5i/central-government-borrowing-strategy-2026.pdf).
- Table 2 and the central-government balance-sheet liability give
  the stated period's settlement flows and closing deposits. The
  January release also reports the December 2025 opening reference.
  Retain the March release's published quarter separately from the
  sum of the monthly issues.
  [January release](https://www.nationalbanken.dk/en/news-and-knowledge/press/archive/2026/foreign-exchange-and-liquidity-and-monthly-balance-sheet-january-2026-03-02-2026),
  [February release](https://www.nationalbanken.dk/en/news-and-knowledge/press/archive/2026/foreign-exchange-and-liquidity-and-monthly-balance-sheet-february-2026-03-03-2026),
  [March release](https://www.nationalbanken.dk/en/news-and-knowledge/press/archive/2026/foreign-exchange-and-liquidity-and-monthly-balance-sheet-march-2026-07-04-2026).

The [transcription and reproduction](../settlement/opening-debt-and-cash-window/01-denmark-opening-quarter-and-release.md)
retain the selected figures from these sources checked on 13 November
2026. The Python check reproduces the arithmetic of transcribed values.
The observed quarter does not provide a full legal or daily cash account.
