# Design322. Public record loss and essential rights

[RLW01–RLW05](../../country/C04/06/04-public-record-loss-and-rights.md)
protects a credible procedural date subject to rebuttal and a lawful
continuing award subject to actual settlement. Fund interim essential
need, review and execution separately. Preserve prior claims, evidence
scope and the lawful handover. Keep the interim period, final entitlement
and recovery distinct.

The [decision](../../2026-11-public-record-loss-and-rights/decision-record.md)
retains ordinary reconstruction and full claimed payment as alternatives.
Actual paid receiving and independent review remain unverified.
