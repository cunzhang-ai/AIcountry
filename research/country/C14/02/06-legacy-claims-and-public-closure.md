# Closing a public institution while its obligations continue

These candidate clauses apply to the lawful closure of a public scheme or
body with surviving obligations. The pension example tests the handover of a
retirement scheme. Application to a different duty requires its own legal
schedule, affected people, funding and receiving test.

**LPC01 — Authorize each closure step separately.** The elected legislature
sets the scope, lawful dates and continuing duties in the governing law.
A closure schedule names the last intake for new business, the handover of
existing duties, dissolution, and the authority and conditions for releasing
any remaining assets. These may occur at different times. The schedule
identifies where each power, duty, restriction and pending case goes. The
ordinary law governing a claim determines its accrual, expiry and finality.
Closure, silence and absence from the transfer register confer no new power
to extinguish it. Renewed policy for future cohorts is a separate choice.
For a contracted operator, distinguish expiry of delegated authority,
continuation of the public owner's duties and separate disposition of
restricted assets and records. Contract closeout supplies no assignment
of liability or authority to release funds.

**LPC02 — Assign one answerable successor for each class of claim.** The
published schedule identifies the office with legal power and a duty to
receive, decide, pay and implement remedies, plus the funded lawful
alternative if that office cannot receive the case. A separate custodian or
paying agent has a named service obligation and access deadline. A person
can start through a single accessible entry point. Wrong-door referrals
preserve the original filing date and stay owned until accepted. Before a
discretionary closure, the successor must demonstrate that it can retrieve
a sample file, continue a pending case, make an authorized payment and
execute an independent remedy. Assign four roles explicitly where
they differ: lawful duty owner, service receiver, payment operator
and protected records holder. Keep original review outside the
operating chain. Test an undisputed award, an uncertain instruction
and a pending appeal concurrently, then remove the outgoing
provider's access and cooperation. Cash, enough paid qualified
people and independently usable evidence are separate requirements. Use paid overlap where knowledge or records
cannot safely move at once.

**LPC03 — Account for duties by date and source.** Reconcile known due
amounts, unsettled awards, live disputes, deferred rights and plausible
unidentified claims against original records and other lawful evidence.
Record uncertainty, expected call dates, common causes of undercounting,
custody and review costs, restrictions and liquidity by payer. Show a
credible higher-call case and its funding route or gap. Preserve standing
service budgets and ownership. Recoveries count when lawfully usable.
Credit prior payment only against the same obligation after verification.
Preserve release exclusivity, prior finality and current safe receiving
under TPE03–TPE06 and LRT04. Lost provider access is no reason for
duplicate principal. Carry each instruction's last independently
verified status and exclusive remaining payment authority. Reconcile
a change or revocation received before closure against the actual time
of release. Where a payment may have relied on an invalid mandate,
retain a disputed claim and use the ordinary liability and remedy route.
Qualify continuity advances on their own source.
Document the scope of any continuing public duty separately from private
debts, wages and surviving insolvency claims.
Use the [opening liability account](05-opening-liabilities-and-protected-budget.md)
and the [public cash controls](../../C06/60/11-public-cash-and-independent-execution.md).

**LPC04 — Keep claims practically usable after dissolution.** Carry valid
awards, original filing dates, limitation protections, proof and case status
with the duty. Keep an accessible claim route for surviving beneficiaries,
lawful representatives and people whose old identifiers fail. Publicize the
route and use proportionate, purpose-limited tracing and safe notice for
plausibly affected people. Use the applicable
[record loss and proof rules](../../C04/06/04-public-record-loss-and-rights.md)
where records are missing. Unknown evidence remains uncertain, and ordinary
finality and reopening rules apply to decided claims. Retain only records
needed for lawful operation, review and specific holds, with access limits
and disposition dates. Publish cohort totals with disclosure controls.

**LPC05 — Protect reserves independently of closure savings.** At each
proposed release, show cash that is settled, lawfully accessible on the
required dates and uncommitted after protected payments, a reasoned allowance
for unresolved and later claims, and staffed administration and remedy.
Preserve asset restrictions unless a competent authority changes them
lawfully. The funding ledger distinguishes releasable money from unused
authority, restricted residuals and continuing exposure. An allowance sets
the funding provision. Claimant entitlement follows applicable law. If a
higher valid claim arrives, identify the lawful source and date, show any
shortfall, and keep the claim and remedy active.

**LPC06 — Keep correction independent of the office saving money.** The
elected body can approve or revise the scope and appropriation, with reasons
and an opportunity for residents and opposition to offer alternatives.
Under ordinary safeguards the payer answers the case, an independent
review body can require disclosure and order a remedy, and an independent
audit function verifies the funding and handover. Provide each function
lawful access to needed records and its own funded operating capacity.
Neither the outgoing sponsor nor the receiving payer can veto disclosure
to reviewers, appeals, or a lawful remedy by withholding operational
access or a discretionary transfer. Preserve a usable route to order
interim protection and payment under applicable law.

**LPC07 — Give each failed gate a lawful consequence.** If the proposed
closure or asset release is conditional and a gate fails, the empowered
office holds that action and publishes the unresolved duty, owner, next
decision date and funding gap. A dissolution already fixed by law proceeds
under the enacted timetable unless the competent authority lawfully
changes it. Activate the assigned fallback and seek the required lawful
budget or corrective order. Where the fallback also fails, log the missed
service or payment as an outstanding breach with its original due date.
No official or AI acquires a power to extend office, rewrite eligibility or
take restricted funds because the transition failed.

**LPC08 — Close work and release funds on distinct evidence.** Review
successor capacity and remaining exposure on stated dates. End duplicate
operating costs after acceptance and observed delivery. Retain, scale down
or lawfully transfer the specialist work and restricted balances that are
still needed. Record each correction through an enforceable disposition and
verified receipt. Reopen the funding assessment when omissions, missed
payments, failure to retrieve evidence or concentration of calls undermine
the assumptions. Keep aggregated findings available for democratic scrutiny
and preserve protected access for a valid individual claim.
