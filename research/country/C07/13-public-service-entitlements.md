# C07. What the public service guarantee actually provides

15 October 2026 · Design 79 · Candidate S01–S16, not enacted law

Specify the **public health, learning and care floor** rather than leaving
its content to an insurer, contractor or later undefined list. Retain the
[three distinct delivery chains](03-health-education-and-care.md), personal
agency, national finance and local/professional judgment. A common service
plan creates neither one family dossier nor one authority over every right.

| Candidate part | Public commitment |
|---|---|
| [S01–S04: health and coverage](13/01-health-and-coverage.md) | Necessary care, transparent boundaries, qualified assessment and continuing treatment |
| [S05–S08: learning and educational freedom](13/02-learning-and-educational-freedom.md) | Free foundational education, early learning, flexible routes and independent learning |
| [S09–S12: care, childhood and agency](13/03-care-childhood-and-agency.md) | Needed functional support, usable childcare, voluntary paid options and safe receiving |
| [S13–S16: workload, priority and transition](13/04-workload-priority-and-transition.md) | Valid joint delivery, distinct time/skills, due-period capacity and lawful change |

Reference choices are a seven-day routine first clinical assessment; free
foundational education from 6 to 18 with continued completion access;
optional early learning from 3; a public place offering 39 weeks with 20/30
weekly early/foundational supported hours; and optional childcare through
age 12 with a 40-hour/48-week baseline plus individually assessed support.
These are normative hypotheses and public **offers**, not compulsory seat
time, proven adequate standards, adopted law or an affordable launch.
Earlier useful-time E duties and existing stronger protections continue.

The [workload comparison](../../settlement/service-floor/README.md) derives
service hours from declared people, needs and grouped delivery. One valid
preschool episode can meet learning and care duties together; count the
actual workers once. Conversely, annual clerical hours can balance while
an election-period peak fails. Both errors can hide behind gross headcounts.

The fictional early-child/adult-care workload alone exceeds the earlier
2,250-FTE public-care reference; qualified additional supply and its funding
remain unknown. Other service functions, actual prices, shifts, localities,
private delivery, renewal and full-country cost are not filled with zeros.
No staffing minimum or arithmetic pass proves an adequate national package.

Next reconcile these quantities with the whole-duty production/finance
choice and actual safe workload evidence. A different service mix or
institutional model may be better. Reopen on excluded needs, unsafe hours,
source/price change, distributional objection or failed receipt; actual
participation, authority, readiness and observed improvement remain absent.
