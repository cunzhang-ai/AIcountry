# C09. Animal welfare, living systems and shared health

*Part of [C09 land and ecology](../C09-land-and-ecology.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

The working country treats avoidable serious suffering of plausibly sentient
animals as a public concern in its own right. Human health, food production,
livelihoods, culture and ecology matter too, but a benefit in one account does
not erase harm in another. Species conservation concerns populations and
habitats; welfare concerns the experiences of individual animals. Neither
measure substitutes for the other. This does not declare every animal a legal
person or resolve the different moral status of species by assertion.
Where sentience or a suitable measure is uncertain, law requires an explicit,
revisable evidential classification and protection against plausible severe
harm, rather than silent exclusion.

## Separate decisions in one accountable chain

The Assembly enacts the [C08 animal-protection floor](../C08/07-animal-protection-and-remedies.md):
covered animals and uses, minimum duties, justified exceptions, inspection,
enforcement, review and funding. C09's competent veterinary and animal-welfare
service maintains disease and welfare capacity, species-specific guidance and
original observations. An enforcement inspector is institutionally separated
from ministries or commercial bodies promoting production and cannot be paid
only from fines. C07 population health owns human clinical and prevention
decisions; C09 food policy owns supply; C08 owns food safety and legal process;
C09 ecological authorities own species and habitat duties. A shared One Health
assessment identifies interactions without giving any coordinator authority to
rewrite these distinct laws. AI may detect patterns and explain options, but
cannot license, seize, cull or define sentience on its own.

Farm and aquaculture rules cover breeding, housing, feed and water, handling,
transport, disease care, killing and the conditions of fish as well as land
animals. Standards use animal-based observations where feasible, with
independently checked conditions and methods where direct measures are weak.
Inspectors sample actual sites, worker reports and supply chains; a clean
certification file is not proof that animals were well. The Assembly weighs
adequate food access, farmer and fishery livelihoods, worker safety, import
dependence, land and water use, emissions and welfare together. It may choose
phased changes and assistance, but must record who bears suffering and who
receives the transition support. It cannot meet a national welfare target by
moving harmful production abroad without examining foreseeable imported harm
and lawful trade constraints.

People responsible for companion, working or dependent animals owe suitable
care, safe housing, veterinary access and non-abandonment under prospective
law. Local authorities arrange an accepting rescue and temporary-care route
for cruelty, neglect, disaster or an owner's incapacity; a seizure without a
safe place is not a completed remedy. Stray-animal management must examine
public safety, disease, shelter capacity and humane population measures,
not treat killing as a data-cleansing operation. Poverty alone is not cruelty:
reachable advice, veterinary help and safe alternatives should precede a
punitive response where no immediate serious harm requires intervention.

For wildlife, C09 conservation law protects habitat and ecological functions,
while the welfare floor also governs human handling, trade, capture, conflict
response and killing. A disease, invasive-species or safety intervention needs
a specific lawful purpose, evidence of necessity, feasible less harmful
alternatives, competent methods, public reasons where safe and prompt review.
It must account for effects on Indigenous and local claims, livelihoods and
other species; it cannot turn a conservation label into unlimited removal.
In a disaster, human rescue retains its priority, while C09 and C10 plan
realistic animal evacuation, shelter, feed, veterinary care or humane triage
without silently diverting the same transport, water or workers twice.

Research and education using animals need a prospective statutory purpose,
licensed facilities, independent ethical and scientific review, replacement
where possible, fewer animals where valid, and reduced suffering throughout
life. The same research institution cannot be its sole reviewer. Valuable
human or animal knowledge does not itself authorize any method. Emergency
disease control and veterinary antimicrobial use remain governed by distinct
public-health, professional and rights rules, with transparent welfare effects.

C14 reports observed welfare by relevant species and setting, inspection
coverage, actual corrections, disease and resistance signals, household food
access, worker and producer burdens, ecosystem condition and imported effects
separately. It records missing animals and uncertain measures. No territory,
animal population, production map, legal inventory, inspector workforce,
animal outcome or affected-public choice exists for AIcountry. Revisit this
selection if protection excludes major sentient groups, inspectors become
dependent on producers, food access worsens without funded repair, harmful
production moves abroad or a different democratic settlement better protects
humans, animals and living systems.
