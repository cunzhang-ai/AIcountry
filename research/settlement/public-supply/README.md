# Public inputs, procurement and continuous supply

Design 132 · [SC01–SC20 canonical candidate](../../country/C04/05-public-buying-and-continuous-supply.md)

The new candidate supplies entry, award, standstill, direct court, contract-change,
payment and essential-continuity rules through existing C04/OJ/AA/sector bodies.
It adds no new central award authority or general private-market permission.

The current firm/plural/A purchase subset is 123.986m fictional CU; 29.804m links
to current partial utility/food/housing interfaces, 94.182m lacks a matched current
supplier recipe. Six clinical budget rows total 43.52m. No linked row is qualified
actual supply, and none of these numbers is full national cost or a lower bound.
PB's unassigned choice envelope creates no output/tax base; the garment offer
remains a separate partial hypothesis. More fabricated producers are not evidence.

[Commitment continuity](../commitments/45-public-supply-and-procurement.md) and
[country disposition](../../status/completion/40-public-inputs-and-procurement.md)
retain all 37 function interfaces, old rights and unpriced new work. A/I05,
conditional U+C2/C1/C0, provider/ownership and RF/MC/finite-issue alternatives
keep their separate scopes. No ready/funded whole country is established.

Interface `132-input-coverage-and-proposed-clocks-v1` is a coverage account, not
a replacement of 130/131 case semantics. New concepts or changed source scope
need a qualified adapter; an unsupported exception cannot become procurement
authority. Human/AI/joint inputs face the same reasoning, privacy and mandate
requirements; a platform or copied agent is no independent reviewer or vote.
