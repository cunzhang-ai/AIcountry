# PF13–PF16: named public roles, cross-border source and paid succession

*Part of [private retirement tax](../57-private-retirement-tax-and-personal-capital.md); no actual levy, exemption or permission is enacted.*

**PF13 — Use existing accountable tax and financial roles.** RG career Revenue
officers determine tax/source/basis under TA/TX and full ordinary judicial
merits. NB trustees/custodians owe actual plan/title/payment records; prudential
permission does not decide income tax or I05. The income service determines
its separate resource claim. Courts and named Treasury/B19 executors retain
their scopes, not a new pension-tax board or private final adjudication.

Publish an annual accessible statement of actual contributions, prior relief,
tax basis/DC dates, nominal resource-capital history, payments/recovery, tax
actually withheld/remitted and remaining claims. Only necessary records pass
under PD lawful purposes; no all-family file or employer access to unrelated
income. Private data requests retain PD09's clock, public cases their TA/TX/
AP routes and earlier useful-time rights; none restarts by provider transfer.

An expressly authorized domestic PF payer provisionally uses the ordinary
annual PA function on the known taxable payment component, not all principal
or a second allowance. Reconcile one person's actual annual income once;
direct DC disposals retain TA06/TA08 filing where no payer is authorized.
TA09/TA10's 15th-next-month remittance, proved withholding credit and separate
payer debt apply. A credit is not Treasury cash; no actual deduction means no
invented credit. Existing refunds, interest, finality and court execution keep
their original TA/TX/RG due events, not a new pension clock or punitive fine.

**PF14 — Select a nexus, preserve actual treaties and prior relief.** Residents
include actual income received in their TA resident period; no tax on already
owned capital or a deemed arrival sale. Nonresidents' owned-unit gains retain
TA03 land/business nexus, not a universal domestic-manager tax. For a genuine
private guaranteed income contract, expressly select the actual domestic legal
debtor's yield component as domestic source, with capital excluded; an offshore
server or payment bank is not the debtor. Legacy untaxed employment-pension
income needs actual service-source attribution and applicable treaty, not
new classification of already recognized PF03 wages as future labor income.

Actually applicable treaties/foreign source rights override these candidates.
Prior foreign deductions/exclusions and recognized cost require a qualified
source-period bridge; no duplicated basis or automatic zero for missing proof.
TA07 credit requires actual qualifying foreign tax on the same included income,
with its caps/correction. A foreign annual asset tax or loss carry is neither
automatically that credit nor cash owned by G/S. No unilateral foreign records,
collection, compensation or settlement is promised.

**PF15 — State exact prospective source law and paid transition.** Propose the
first 1 January at least 24 months after a coordinated adopting Act, with the
actual date, covered new service/contribution cohorts and PA/M/CPI vintages
fixed in that Act. Reconcile its relation to existing PA/TA/Y commencement;
this notice does not defer an earlier due benefit, refund, election or remedy.
Map old contributions/relief, basis, guaranteed net promises, accrued DB/DC,
future contractual commitments, withholding, losses, pending cases and actual
foreign law to paid lawful successors. New future terms need lawful notice/
employment/contract change; no forced conversion, retrospective levy, cancelled
old credit or surprise tax on a transfer merely to open the new ledger.

Where old source treatment is unresolved, preserve its stronger actual rights,
undisputed payment and protected correction while the bridge is reviewed; a
record gap cannot approve a new exemption or extinguish a claim. Pay payroll,
member help, valuation, custodial/basis reconciliation, receiving, Revenue/court/
Treasury correction and overlap once under OP/T. No assumed private-plan fee,
public tax gain, new AI efficiency or shared standby finances this work.

establishes national affordability, saving behavior or better lives.
