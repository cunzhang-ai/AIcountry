# C05. Enterprise and association constitutions

Design544 · OE01–OE46 research proposals and specified alternatives

**Select plural legal forms with actual title/liability, worker participation in
enterprise decisions, protected voluntary association and public ownership under
elected control.** A company, cooperative or foundation label is neither public
consent nor immunity. No single private/public/AI institution owns everyone's
work, credit, communication or social ties.

Ordinary enterprises choose their board structure through compatible law,
charters and collective agreements. Protect existing representation, bargaining,
safety and minority rights. A competent legislature can address a specific
power failure or essential-service duty with a proportionate rule. The
[limited opening](../C14/06/32-limited-government-and-first-supply.md)
requires an evidenced case, funded enforcement and transition for any such
change.

The specified alternative has at least one-third workforce-elected
seats from50 covered workers and parity from1000, using the13-week actual
headcount method. Standard covered board: six, respectively2/4 or3/3 worker/
owner seats; charter multiples of six preserve the fraction. No chair casting
vote manufactures parity. A worker cooperative must demonstrate equal actual
workforce franchise to substitute its own elected board; a label is insufficient.
Voluntary association purpose/member control and sovereign/public-office powers
remain distinct from workplace/economic management.

Numbers and institutional forms are authored hypotheses, not research-proven
optima. New board time, registry, audit, court, limited official-certificate
liability and transition need real source/capacity. Financial licensing/capital,
actual NF tax units, WL/AS rights, B/U priorities, PD and public IC mandates remain.
No automatic state guarantee, corporate tax consolidation, free acquisition or
new rights from AI copies. [The comparison](../../settlement/enterprise/README.md)
and [decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-enterprise-and-association-constitutions/decision-record.md)
retain investor-only, worker-owned, shared-control and public alternatives.
