# C07. Serious illness, relief and end-of-life care

*Part of [C07 social floor](../C07-social-floor.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

Serious-illness care aims to reduce avoidable suffering while preserving a
person's agency, relationships and ordinary life. Palliative care is available
on clinical need at any age and may accompany treatment intended to cure or
prolong life; it is not a forced transfer to a terminal category. National
law specifies an essential package and review, while C07's health purchaser
funds a named first-contact team, after-hours advice, medicines, symptom
relief, specialist referral, mental-health and social support, and reachable
home, community or facility options. A remote patient needs an accepting
clinician and transport or home visit, not only a referral number. Children
receive age-appropriate care and supported voice; families may receive help
without becoming unpaid mandatory staff. Local care services retain their
separate paid-support and housing duties.

The patient receives understandable information about likely benefits,
burdens and uncertainty of reasonable options. They may accept or refuse a
proposed intervention through supported, informed choice, including when
the choice may shorten life. A disability, age, poverty, prognosis label or
anticipated cost does not cancel legal capacity or justify withholding
ordinary care. A clinician is not required to offer an intervention judged
clinically ineffective under a published, reviewable standard; that judgment
needs reasons, a timely second opinion and urgent independent correction
where delay is irreversible. Scarcity is a separate C06 public decision and
cannot be concealed as an individualized finding of futility.

The [C08 advance-choice rule](../C08/08-end-of-life-decisions-and-survivors.md)
lets a person state values, name limited support and make a decision-specific
advance refusal under prospective law. Clinicians seek the person's current
will first, provide communication support and check any recorded choice's
scope, currency and authenticity. A household head, payer, care home or AI
cannot silently replace the person. If preferences remain unknowable during
a true emergency, lawful temporary care protects life and comfort while an
independent route tests the next irreversible step; it does not create a
general guardian. Treatment withdrawal never withdraws pain relief, hygiene,
human presence or the person's other lawful claims.

Clinical teams assess pain, breathlessness, distress and other burdens,
offer appropriate medicines, and review effects and adverse events. The
medicine authority and purchaser must make essential controlled symptom
medicines safely reachable under enacted prescribing and diversion controls;
a legal prescription with no stocked pharmacy is non-delivery. When symptoms
remain refractory, proportionate sedation requires an enacted clinical
standard, documented purpose, consent or lawful preference route, review
and continued care. It is not a covert method for a government to hasten
death. The reference design supplies no new authority for assisted dying;
the current reference retains that no-new-authority boundary, with its moral
cost and possible future lawful reconsideration. It is not inferred from refusal
or relief. [UA10/16](../C08/23/04-life-sustaining-care-objection-and-liberty.md) now
specifies unknown-will life-support/continuity, not fictional patient consent.

Clinicians who determine death follow legally specified clinical criteria,
training, verification and record rules; a registry or AI estimate cannot
declare a person dead. Consent to organ or tissue donation has its own
lawful route. The team deciding death and the team seeking donation must
have separated responsibilities and auditable records; no donation target
may affect treatment or death determination. Suspected violence or neglect
goes to a distinct lawful investigation without leaving relatives without
basic notice and care. C08 owns registration, body care, estates and
survivor claims after clinical confirmation.

The purchaser reports timely symptom relief, unwanted treatment,
refusals honored, missed visits, medicine availability, place of care,
patient and family complaints and executed repair by age, place and need.
It protects intimate records and accounts from people who could not access
the service. No actual patients, clinicians, pharmacy stock, home teams,
law or observed outcomes exist for AIcountry. Reopen if a pathway becomes
a cost-driven denial, substitutes family labor, loses a person's voice or
cannot deliver relief before death.
