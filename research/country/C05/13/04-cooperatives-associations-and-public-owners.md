# OE16–OE20: different ownership forms require different constitutions

*Part of [C05/13](../13-enterprise-and-association-constitutions.md); design118 candidate.*

**OE16 — Cooperative membership.** A primary cooperative gives each actual
consenting member one vote, not votes per contribution or copied identity. Legal
entity members in genuine federations have one disclosed actual member vote;
nominee/control splits cannot fabricate independent membership. Publish lawful
purpose/entry criteria, actual contributions, work/use ties, elected organs,
account access, expulsion/reply/review, withdrawal and continuing obligations.
Membership is voluntary, with no automatic eligibility from work, address or AI
participation. No prohibitive artificial subscription to strip an entitled member's
voice or force unpaid work. Fair actual role/purpose criteria retain association
freedom and civil equality. Under18 assets/debt use actual B21–B23 protection.

**OE17 — Labor and capital in a cooperative.** Actual employment keeps WL/TW
pay/exit/union rights even for a member; a dividend is no unpaid wage substitute.
Nonvoting external capital and limited disclosed return can finance operations
without buying member control; genuine loans/securities retain M/NB licensing
and investor risk. Distribute only lawful actual surplus after OE10 and accepted
reserves, using published member-use/work/capital terms and source tax rules.
Withdrawal value/time follows real contribution/rights/liquidity, not a promise
of immediate cash or free national acquisition. A board-election method may
satisfy the OE12 alternative, if enacted, only where all covered workers actually have equal workforce votes,
required seats/eligibility and nonretaliatory exit, including nonmembers. A
worker-cooperative label or member majority alone is no exemption.

**OE18 — Non-distributing association.** Informal civic/religious/cultural/union
association remains lawful without registration. A registered body has its own
assets/obligations and a membership constitution: one actual member/one vote,
accountable elected organs, regular accessible meetings at least annually,
reasonable admission/exit, notice/reply and independent review for actual civil
rights/wrongful expulsion. Private lawful doctrine/affiliation is not decided by
Registrar; no forced membership or state truth test. Ordinary valid-vote majorities
decide, while changing purpose, distributing entitlement or dissolution needs two-
thirds of all entitled members and applicable asset lock/affected rights. Actual
roll/change reasons, safe participation and minority remedy prevent convenient
expulsions manufacturing a majority. Founders/members cannot divide restricted
or donated purpose assets by renaming them personal profit; legitimate pay and
real expenses are distinct. On closure the remaining locked assets go to a lawful
compatible purpose after actual debts, not to a minister or insiders automatically.

**OE19 — Purpose foundation and actual public benefit.** An asset-locked foundation
has at least three responsible trustees, chartered purpose/appointment/removal/
succession, four-year renewable terms and at least one actually independent of
controlling founders/donors/managers. Charter names the lawful selector, conflict
challenge and successor; no empty independence label. Beneficiaries/qualified
purpose advocates can seek direct civil protection without fictional votes from
animals, future people or AI copies. Trustee power does not own the beneficiary's
private decisions. A private foundation is not a democratically elected government;
concentration/self-perpetuation risks remain subject to purpose, work, competition,
political-finance and ordinary-court controls, not claimed solved by a quota.

For NF02, genuine public-purpose charity means actual non-distributing public
benefit, e.g. relief, care, learning, science, rights, culture, religion/community
or ecology, with no disguised private extraction. The examples are not a closed
list or an ideological permit. Revenue decides actual receipt/trading classification
under TA/NF independent review; Registrar's form entry alone grants no exemption,
donor deduction, campaign privilege or waiver of commercial tax. PC's narrow
ordinary-advocacy/electoral distinction remains, without a general speech license.

**OE20 — Public enterprise is an elected ownership mandate.** Assembly or competent
elected council approves the lawful public purpose, owner, gross capital/risk,
funded noncommercial duties and account/exit. Accountable Cabinet minister or local
executive appoints owner-side directors through C04 open qualified shortlists,
reasons/conflicts and direct review; four-year staggered terms permit reasoned
lawful mandate-change replacement or proved misconduct/inability after reply,
not party jobs, named-case retaliation or secret appropriation. OE worker seats
apply to compatible enterprise organs; public audit and opposition access survive.
Professional staff/manager rights and competent sector law remain.

Separate commercial cash/risk from public-service compensation. Public ownership
is not every debt's Treasury guarantee, monopoly license or citizen consent;
actual state/nondelegable duties cannot be outsourced away. Reclassification or
corporate transfer cannot silently turn an already protected public employee or
claim into a capped private claim. IC procurement/grant/loan/equity limits and
actual lawful title/paid acquisition remain; no free assets or automatic rescues.
