# C14. Transition and service commencement: Opening inventory and budget

*Part of [C14. Transition and service commencement](../02-transition-and-commencement.md); integrated design 56 research, not enacted law.*

## Transition inventory and opening budget

Use the [C06 joint schedule](../../C06/18-joint-commitment-and-delivery-schedule.md)
and [current responsibility inventory](../../../settlement/commitments/README.md)
as a starting map, not an opening balance sheet. Reconcile each continuing
claim and new start to source law, due time, baseline cost inclusion, qualified
capacity and remedy executor. Preserve unknown items and confidential source
records; a named function or edition does not establish inherited funding.

The opening inventory must include ordinary civil claims and pending cases, land and
water rights, permits, ecological and restoration liabilities, cross-boundary plans,
external contracts, debts, treaty duties and protection claims. It must also cover
provisional treaty application, lawful exit routes and continuing duties, foreign
guarantees, critical suppliers and currency needs, border contractors, pending
protection appeals, reception places and claims by people harmed abroad. It also covers
patients, school places, care plans, worker agreements, criminal cases and custody,
reproductive-care appointments and continuing treatment, consent and donor
records, birth and parentage claims, newborn access and pending family orders,
advance care wishes, palliative treatment and controlled-medicine access,
death certificates, donor records, body-care sites, wills and survivor claims,
pending official-abuse reports, use-of-force and detention logs, forensic custody,
interpersonal protection orders, pending violence cases, safe housing,
clinical and advocate contracts, child-contact orders and confidential
location records,
pending discrimination and accommodation claims, sector evidence rules,
safe claimant records and lawful provider duties,
independent investigation and prosecution assignments, digital credentials,
keys, vendor contracts, legal mandates and protected records;
existing party and candidate funds, donor confidentiality, public-media
assets, political advertising contracts, pending campaign complaints and
the lawful source records needed to review them;
productive firms and public-enterprise obligations, utility licenses and asset
condition, food stocks, critical suppliers, workforce agreements and actual
cross-system fuel, water, power, transport and foreign-payment dependencies.
The [food-system inventory](../../C09/09-food-systems-and-nutrition.md)
also identifies production and import routes, safe stock quality and expiry,
farm and worker claims, buyer control, local retail reach, household access,
food-safety cases and land/water limits. A warehouse count cannot establish
edible food, an affordable diet or a funded last-mile service.
The [animal-welfare inventory](../../C09/10-animal-welfare-and-one-health.md)
separately identifies covered animals, lawful keeping and research, existing
permits and claims, disease and welfare records, veterinarians, inspectors,
safe receiving care, farms and fisheries, imports and pending protection cases.
Living animals cannot be left without care while an agency changes name.
The [C09 connectivity and freight inventory](../../C09/05-communications-and-logistics.md)
also maps spectrum and network licenses, shared backhaul, towers, postal
notice duties, routes, depots, cold stores, emergency roaming, repair crews,
fuel and alternate providers before claiming an opening service is ready.
The opening inventory also maps sanitation and solid-waste permits, on-site
systems, treatment capacity, hazardous-waste liabilities, downstream claims,
cyber backups and incident records, pending customs declarations, held goods,
appeal clocks, inspection powers and lawful fallback receipts. Each continuing
duty needs a funded successor before a transfer is called complete.
The [population-health inventory](../../C07/08-population-health-and-prevention.md)
separates lawful clinical and surveillance records, correction and retention
claims, laboratories, local outreach teams, vaccines and other supplies,
cold-chain and translation capacity, and any actual international reporting
duty. A technical warning is not an inherited license to share person-level
data or impose a new compulsory measure.
The [mental-health and crisis inventory](../../C07/11-mental-health-substance-use-and-crisis-care.md)
identifies current patients, lawful liberty orders and custody time,
medicine and overdose supplies, facilities and contracts, paid and peer
workers, community places, housing, privacy claims, pending challenges
and actual after-hours responders. No person is discharged into a
nonexistent service because an old institution closes on paper.
It also identifies existing competition cases, merger conditions, product
hazard notices, recalls, buyer claims, warranties, responsible suppliers,
testing capacity and viable alternate distributors. A new market authority
cannot retroactively invent an offense or leave pending remedies ownerless.
The financial inventory separates insured deposits, safeguarded client balances,
investment risks, loans and collateral, household and small-firm arrears,
bank/nonbank leverage and maturity mismatch, cross-border claims, payment
rails, insolvency cases and contingent public guarantees. A balance cannot
be classified as insured by its app label or a sponsor's assurance.
The [disaster-recovery inventory](../../C09/07-disaster-recovery-and-return.md)
also maps hazard and exposure evidence, emergency shelter and receiving sites,
insurance contracts and exclusions, public reserves and contingent loans,
land/tenure claims, local tax dependencies, damaged-record backups and
unresolved reconstruction or relocation duties. An untested payout is not cash.
The [ecological-transition opening account](../../C09/08-climate-and-ecological-transition.md)
needs source emissions and ecosystem observations, actual energy and grid
assets, permits and restoration duties, workers and local revenues, imported
dependencies and any applicable treaty commitments before a numerical path
can be claimed ready.
The [public-debt inventory](../../C06/06-sovereign-debt-and-distress.md)
separately identifies each public obligor, creditor, maturity, currency,
governing law, collateral, side condition and guarantee, including exposures
held by banks and pensions and disputed or unauthorized claims.
The social-insurance inventory separates pensions already due, accrued worker
and care credits, survivor and dependant rights, disputed earnings records,
contributions owed, public subsidies, reserves and their ownership, and
projected future accrual. It maps health and long-term care places and staff
separately from cash benefits; a pension fund balance cannot certify a care
service or discharge an inherited benefit by changing its scheme name.
It must identify lawful source custodians, prior public-statistics vintages,
research records, restricted testimony, archive keys, retention duties,
record-correction claims and suppliers controlling backup copies. Neither
an outgoing cabinet nor a replacement vendor may erase evidence needed for
continuing rights, public accounts or independent inquiry.
New institutions need named successors for both the benefits and burdens of earlier
decisions. A plan to rebuild housing, a court system or a reception service must
preserve safe continuity while its staff, sites, funds and lawful authority are
assembled. Publication alone does not commence a service. The first
administration must map essential posts, vacancies, payroll, supplier
beneficial control, live tenders, amendments, performance evidence, disputed
awards, source records, subcontractors, exit rights and receiving capacity.
No incoming cabinet inherits a right to purge the career service, and no
outgoing official or contractor may hide a binding obligation or destroy a
complaint. Essential procurement can continue under valid prior law during
transition, with disclosed exceptions and review; a new integrity body is
not assumed operational because a charter names one.
The first lawful budget starts from that opening inventory and the C06 commitment
account, not from zero. Each service launch states which protections operate
immediately, which inherited claims continue, which new standard starts later,
and which actual residents received it. Any geographic sequence needs public
reasons, interim support, a review date and challenge; high-need places cannot
be relegated to the end merely because easy places improve the headline rate.
A missed milestone requires a fresh elected decision, while due claims remain
visible and enforceable within actual lawful remedies.

If a lawfully authorized territorial-status change is contemplated, the opening
and transfer accounts also identify each proposed successor for residents,
workers, public assets and debts, tax collection, deposits and payment access,
land and water rights, shared networks, courts, custody, pensions, existing
contracts, essential services, external obligations and their remedies. Both
governments must show the cash, personnel, records and lawful cross-border
arrangements that carry those duties through the effective date. A political
agreement or referendum count is not evidence that people can receive care,
water, wages or a judgment the next morning. Unresolved liabilities and lawful
claims remain assigned under prior law until competent law and fair process
name a funded successor; there is no automatic clean slate.

[OL01–OL05](05-opening-liabilities-and-protected-budget.md) carries
late, contested and unbounded exposure into each exact first-budget
choice. It separates verification, reserve, lawful recognition and
actual payment while retaining the original service and claim dates.
