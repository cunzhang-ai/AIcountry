# T31–T36. Budget for the work left after a service reopens

*Part of [staged capacity and protection](../06-staged-capacity-and-protection.md). Design346 research selection.*

**T31 — Separate returning service from clearing unmet duties.** When a
material disruption leaves continuing claims, the finance officers and
each duty owner carry its recovery into the same budget and calendar as
current services, elections, remedies and proposed starts. Name the due
scope, original deadline, legal owner, actual receiver and status.
Distinguish restoring a route, current ordinary delivery, repairing past
loss and any distinct compensation. A person may receive service now and
still have a claim arising from the interruption. The original clock,
emergency expiry and existing rights continue.

**T32 — Reconcile the accumulated work and the flow of new need.** At each
useful operating interval, show the opening unresolved stock, new due
work, newly identified earlier cases, actual receiving and separately
lawful exits. Deduplicate only against verified obligations. Keep
unscheduled, unrecorded and disputed need visible with a safe assisted
reporting route. Count a referral, transfer or canceled appointment as
its actual event. Verify delivery or the legal reason an item no longer
requires it before closing a duty. Include the support work, travel,
assessment, maintenance and other skills each task needs. A lower queue
count can result from restricting entry. Publish coverage and reasons
as well as the count.

**T33 — Reserve the next ordinary work and price real recovery capacity.**
Estimate ordinary arrivals and their useful deadlines under a stated
range. Protect urgent and new due work on their own law. Carry missed
duties and review deadlines alongside it. Apply the sector's lawful
urgency and priority rules within the service. The budget cannot decide
who should receive clinical care or a judicial hearing first. Identify
paid, qualified, accepting capacity available for missed work after
continuing obligations. Test how it responds if arrivals rise or a second
failure occurs. Pay overlap, origin cover, safe rest and independent
review where needed. Work available in another week or profession
does not fill a missing slot now.

**T34 — Give elected bodies a dated recovery choice.** On finding a
material accumulation after an interruption, the duty owner reports
promptly and submits a funded disposition within ten working days, or
sooner where existing useful-time duties require it. Show ordinary
sources, temporary cover with expiry, earlier missed dates, the next
budget decision and all remaining gaps. Compare retention and repair,
additional paid capacity and retimed or separately authorized smaller
starts. Use the same due services and adverse scenarios across options.
A source shortfall goes to the competent elected body and the
independent remedy route. Hold an optional release when it would
displace due work or depend on unverified recovery. Carry any already
binding undertaking on its own date and seek a lawful remedy.

**T35 — End temporary cover through an actual handoff.** Before an
ordinary or exceptional bridge ends, name the staff, funding and
receivers for the next complete service cycle. Show what work will
remain and who must answer for it. Exceptional powers expire on their
own terms. Continuing work transfers only under a separately lawful
source and authority. Verify actual completion and continuing capacity
with the relevant independent reviewers and affected people. Close
resolved portions while preserving open cases, original losses,
review and lawful compensation. A one-time queue reduction is not
evidence of durable operating capacity.

**T36 — Keep evidence and public control through succession.** Fund
safe evidence access, independent verification, elected scrutiny
and practical challenges outside the interrupted provider. AI may
reconcile flows and detect calendar conflicts. Responsible officers
verify facts, qualified providers accept tasks and elected bodies decide
budgets. Adopt prospectively with paid handover and original dates.
Reopen on an unexpected arrival surge, missing groups, displaced
ordinary service, shared staff failure or a better feasible schedule.
