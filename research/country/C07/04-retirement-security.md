# C07. Retirement rights, continued accrual and actual payment

Pooled architecture · Candidate Y01–Y41, not enacted law

Retain **individual basic old-age protection plus ongoing pooled earnings
insurance**, with care/interruption credits and separate health/care rights.
Make the rights and adjustment route concrete. Stop treating a closed-legacy
cash comparison as the default pension architecture. A different architecture
can be proposed openly; it cannot inherit this selection or erase old claims.

The research default is standard age 65, basic payment equal to the adult
ordinary basket, worker contribution 10% of personal labor earnings, annual
accrual 1.25% and best48 revalued annual units. [RF05–RF09](../C06/36/02-pension-units-cash-and-generations.md) now selects a one-W ceiling for new service after notice, replacing two-W only prospectively; all earlier units/indexation/survivor claims remain intact.
These are **explicit normative hypotheses**, not optimal estimates, current
law or an established affordable package. They require actual public choice,
source-law reconciliation, histories, prices, actuarial work and funding.

In a fictional 30,000 real annual-pay career, 40 full years earn 15,000 annual
insurance pension, alongside the basic layer. For 15,000 such recipients,
insurance payments are 225 million, rather than the old scheduled 45 million.
Against old receipts 228/interest 5 the apparent margin falls from 188 to 8,
before administration, survivors, credits and other unknown obligations.
Those are alternative history fixtures, not observed cohorts or a forecast.
The [formula/finance comparison](../../settlement/retirement/README.md)
also exposes the larger due-date cash reserve. No actuarial solvency follows.

Basic pension is credited once in I05's separate needs test; earned payments
keep its bounded disregard. Neither a spouse nor a hypothetical household
asset is presumed available. Care, disability, housing and safe work remain
separate duties. Contributions pool risk rather than buy a personal account;
all valid claims require actual payers and lawful successors.
[IC17–IC20](../C05/09/06-income-security-and-credit-continuity.md) now separates
ordinary cash status from qualified interruption credit and covered work from
received wages. No new credit rate, eligibility class or funding is inferred.

Next close the full-duty production/income/transition choice with these
continuing claims. Reopen pension design on original cohort/health/earnings
evidence, unequal effects, cost or a better social model. No new AI, Treasury
or actuarial power can cut rights, choose sovereign burdens or postpone elections.

[QE's joined estimate](../C06/34/02-households-funding-and-assets.md) now uses
180m basic alongside225m earned and3.75m authored survivor payments, with I05
receipt/needs reconciliation and restricted S cash. These are one history/overlap
branch, not new pension law or a full current-survivor/actuarial valuation. The
old45m basic or closed-legacy amount cannot silently price this current Y design.

[FD](../C06/35/01-prices-tax-and-personal-resources.md) now separates the
personal-basket/basic adjustment from the general earned/survivor index and
joint personal tax, explicitly backing current S shortfalls. Its alternative
price baseline is not observed inflation or a reset of actual accrued rights.
Lifetime accrual, histories, source/reserve and full actuarial tails stay unknown.

[IC](../C05/09/01-personal-foundation-and-continuity.md) credits the existing
Y basic once as the older person’s ordinary foundation; earned/care, survivor,
price, arrears and accrued rights remain separate through lawful paid transition.

[NB17–NB20](../C06/44-nonbank-finance-and-client-protection.md) governs voluntary additional funded private DC/DB plans and their member governance, real funding and fair transfer. It does not privatize public Y/S, compel private saving, change accrued units or guarantee private investment/insurance losses.

[Design126dated country reference](../C06/49-country-source-and-claims.md) now joins conditional monthly needs/receipt/tax, prior pension/basic/credits, bills and owner-specific sources. PBsite800 and owner renewal are comparison provisions, not automatically paid NFtax or ordinary needs. Larger actual/source-law rights and unpriced private resources/legacy/index/actuarial tails remain; no new rate or source authority.

[Design139 FP18–FP19](../C06/56/06-owned-principal-and-country-disposition.md)
keeps public Y solidarity rights separate from private funded principal and a
foreign pension-asset tax. No personal Y capital withdrawal/deduction, imported
levy or public guarantee of private investment losses follows. Private basis
and I05 usable resources retain distinct matching; all earlier units survive.

[PF01–PF16](../C06/57-private-retirement-tax-and-personal-capital.md) now selects
the written voluntary private-saving tax/source interface. Public Y solidarity
contributions/units are not personal PF basis; private capital/payouts are not
worked earnings a second time or a new guarantee from S. All old earned/credit/
price/survivor rights and actual source-year law remain, with paid successors.

[WF17–WF22](../C06/58/05-observed-population-income-and-retirement.md) now anchors
published age/income scope and separate gross-basic/earned/arrears examples.
Statistical public/private pension labels do not establish Y basic or PF basis;
missing basic remains a due claim alongside actual interim needs protection.
No pension-age, rate, accrual, source-period or qualified-liability change.

[WF45–WF48 paired bridge](../C06/58/10-paired-net-source-and-legacy-bridge.md)
clarifies Y15 same-basic continuity/stronger eligible old rights, prospective
units and actual remittance/tax/I05. Ten authored cases qualify no actual
national source; all old units/source conditions and commencement dates persist.

[TU02 and TU06](../C04/12-effective-take-up-and-early-benefit-delivery.md)
supports release of already established installments without a new
application. The pension holder's start choice and source law determine
the date. Wrong records, actual payment and unresolved eligibility keep
their independent human routes.

[RF17–RF21](../C06/36/05-ageing-finance-and-care-capacity.md) compares
the earned pension source and ordinary care on the same dates.
Existing units, notices, payment dates and separate legal sources continue.
