## One person and three separate responsibilities

where they can receive ongoing home support. The competent housing,
care and transport offices and their payers are known. The room
provider requires confirmation of the arrival shift. The care
provider requires confirmation of the address. The transport payer
requires both to release payment. Each actor is ready within their
ordinary scope, but each awaits another. Prior law in this fixture
permits exact conditional bookings. The resident can refuse or seek
the continuing lawful arrangement. The authorized providers retain
