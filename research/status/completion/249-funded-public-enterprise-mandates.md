# Funded public enterprise service mandates

Design341 · Scoped finding for [country completion](../../DESIGN-COMPLETENESS.md)

[OE41–OE46](../../country/C05/13/09-funded-public-enterprise-mandates.md)
separates elected service commissions, due compensation, actual cash,
commercial losses and the person's usable service. It requires a dated
public correction when funding is insufficient.

The [transit comparison](../../settlement/public-enterprise-mandates/01-rural-transit-and-two-budgets.md)
checks net cost and two calls on one reserve. Actual funding, local costs,
independent review, service delivery and household effects remain open.
