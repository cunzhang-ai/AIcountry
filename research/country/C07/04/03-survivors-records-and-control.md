# Y09–Y12: dependants, received payment and public control

*Part of [C07 retirement candidate](../04-retirement-security.md); design 65.*

**Y09 — Survivor protection follows loss, not marriage privilege.** A
deceased insured person's survivor pot is 50% of the earned annual pension
already in payment or the accrued age-65 annual pension if not yet claimed.
This is an additional insurance contingency with its own cost, not an estate
account or a refund of personal contributions. Eligible actual dependants
are children under 18; adults with continuing significant functional support
needs who depended on the deceased; and other actual adult dependants for
a 12-month bridge. Review continuing adult need with supported agency and
appeal, without compelling family care or treating a diagnosis as incapacity.
This benefit's age boundary does not set civil majority or cancel other
parental-support, education or disability rights.

Allocate the pot in proportion to proven lost safe support, capped by each
loss and the total pot; preserve separate child custody/property protections.
Concurrent own pension or another dependant's account is not automatically
confiscated. I05 separately assesses actual received resources and needs.
Unpaid minors/disputed dependants receive urgent provisional subsistence
under the death-and-survivor route while records are resolved; no estate or
parentage dispute can hold their food hostage. An allegation alone does not
cancel a survivor claim; any intentional-killing exclusion needs its separate
criminal/civil law and a competent final decision. After a survivor term ends,
ordinary individual protection and services continue under their own law.

**Y10 — Advance and actual receipt.** Pay pension on the first calendar day
of each month in advance to the person's safe chosen route, with a prorated
first period and staffed/non-digital access. Record entitlement, instruction,
cash settlement and safe receipt separately. A bank or employer failure
triggers continuity's useful-time hardship and independent remedy routes;
neither a final ledger nor the next monthly batch is protection delivered.
Known undisputed amounts continue during a record challenge. A missed lawful
payment remains an owed claim with a named payer and received-remedy check.
Carry any smallest-unit rounding remainder to the person's next installment;
an account calculation is not itself a settlement instruction or receipt.

**Y11 — Records and independent remedy.** The social-protection authority
administers claims through accountable career officials under C04; the revenue
office collects contributions, separate custodians hold restricted assets,
the evidence service commissions qualified actuarial work, and independent
eligibility/record adjudication and courts decide contested rights. Ministers
answer to the replaceable cabinet/Assembly; existing merit appointments,
reasoned removal, audit and opposition source-access routes apply. None
may change statutory parameters by contract, AI instruction or unpublished
investment policy. The state funds necessary assistance and timely execution.
An employer ledger or AI inference is evidence open to challenge, not final law.

**Y12 — Check the whole control chain.** Publish safe aggregates of claims,
payments, administration, investments, controllers and errors. Protect personal
health/care/earnings evidence by purpose and access; tax collection receives
the necessary credit certificate, not unrestricted clinical files. Separate
appointment, funding, source custody, procurement, review and execution in
the actual operating account. Shared sponsor/vendor control can defeat named
separation; report that failure and fund an available independent route.
Affected workers, carers, older and younger people and opposition can propose
and contest alternatives through the existing Assembly process. Neither a
fund manager nor an actuarial office receives a governing veto.

[Family/estate B21–B40](../../C08/16-family-agency-property-and-estates.md)
does not multiply Y09's one lost-support pot, convert it to estate property
or assume private inheritance received. Plural ties need actual eligible
losses, not account/relationship counts; the design-65 benefit parameters
and unknown original costs remain unchanged by this compatibility note.

[Effective access](../01-effective-access.md) now includes safe practical help
for potential Y nonclaimants/nonusers and disrupted survivors, distinguishing
refusal, proof/access/service failure and unknown need. No forced uptake,
unsafe data merge or inferred eligibility; actual credits, claims and due-date
protection retain their own source law. Fund the additional receiving work.

[NB17–NB20](../../C06/44-nonbank-finance-and-client-protection.md) private funded-plan trustees do not replace Y11 public careers or Y09 survivor law. Public restricted S assets and accrued/statutory payments retain their actual source, not a private member-account balance or backing for a failed plan.
