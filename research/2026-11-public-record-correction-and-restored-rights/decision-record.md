# Design402 decision. Make correction effective in every materially affected decision

## Baseline and requested change

Compare [Design401](predecessor-design-401/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md) using the
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).
This AI-assisted selection concerns cross-office evidence used for consequential
ordinary administrative decisions. It affects residents, fact issuers, local and
national decision offices, service providers, payers and reviewers.
PD10 already requires correction, notification and checking downstream effects.
AP protects original dates and access, TU tracks benefit delivery, and LX
covers proactive redress. EV17–EV24 already requires decision traces, bounded
review and remedy delivery across public bodies. RUC applies those clocks and
specifies the narrower rule for shared facts. The remaining design choice is how to turn these
separate obligations into an executable case when the first receiver is absent,
the fact is valid for only one period, or the resident already lost an entitlement.

## Rival choices and residual objections

Retain the current general duty with local interpretation. This minimizes
formal trace work but leaves the minimum evidence link and response to missed
handoff underspecified. Require direct re-verification before each consequential
use. This reduces stale extracts but can load the source and interrupt decisions
during outages. A central register with automatic rewrites simplifies broad
distribution but concentrates visibility and lets a fact issuer change decisions
outside its competence. Select scoped, pairwise traceability with independent
recipient decisions and an enforceable escalation path. Retain direct checking
where simpler and reliable, and other admissible evidence when a link fails.

The strongest objections are that tracing grows into surveillance, recurrent
false alerts block decisions and paid staff will miss unrecorded uses. Limit
links to material purposes, use existing PD restrictions and short useful-time
response, and count unresolved links and delayed relief explicitly. Review the
necessity of retaining each link when laws, offices or suppliers change. No
independent reviewer or affected participant was secured for this selection.

## Control, compatibility and execution

The elected lawmaker decides covered classes and funds the public duty. The
issuer identifies or restricts the factual assertion. Receiving officers remain
answerable for their own acts, including work delegated to vendors. The data
supervisor and ordinary merits reviewer keep separate existing jurisdictions.
Use independent interim orders and merits remedies with named executors.
Elected scrutiny can examine safe aggregate evidence and amend the scope.
Protected access to originals and realistic paid reviewer capacity remain
conditions for commencement. No authority follows from a shared AI assistant.

Preserve source law, current awards and filings, holds, privacy and stronger
remedies. Prospectively assign named successors and keep the trace between
old facts and still-live decisions through a change of medium. Protect lawful
third-party rights, judgment finality and current appeals. Each downstream
decision changes through its own legal process. The proposed review period on a
new disposition applies only to the corrected issue and does not extinguish
other rights. There is no new data-sharing purpose.

## Evidence, limits and next decision

[Primary law](sources.md) establishes institutional comparators.
The [constructed case](../settlement/public-record-correction-and-restored-rights/01-one-fact-two-public-decisions.md)
tests a common error across housing and essential travel. It does not
estimate how often the mechanism fails or pays on time. Research selection
is recorded in [RUC01–RUC08](../country/C04/06/07-public-record-correction-and-restored-rights.md).
Independent review, lawful adoption, paid operation and observed outcomes
remain unverified.

A competent data supervisor and two service authorities could reconstruct one
de-identified, lawfully inspectable cross-office correction, including failed
delivery and the resident's own account where safely and voluntarily available.
Completion would require the actual original fact period, decisions, handoff
times, paid reviewer effort, missed windows and verified remedies. Staff, budget
and lawful access are unresolved. Reopen the selected mechanism on systematic
missed links, intrusive trace retention, continued harmful stale decisions or a
simpler route delivering equivalent access and relief.
