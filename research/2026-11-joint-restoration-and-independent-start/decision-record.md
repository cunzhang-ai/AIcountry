# Design367 decision on joint restoration and an independent start

## Baseline, choice and authority

Pin Design366, JC01–JC32, T31–T36, current scarcity and emergency rules,
legislative continuity and the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md). Existing
design tests simultaneous receipt, second calls and common failure.
Select an explicit proof that at least one act can start recovery when
the proposed alternates depend on each other. The project assistant
proposes a separate test and paid exercise. Elected bodies set lawful
sources, and existing sector officers retain their decisions. No
general coordinator or new emergency authority is selected.

## Alternatives and objections

A separate backup list can be cheap to maintain but miss a circular
dependency. Independently staffed temporary protection can meet early
duties without restoring a wider service. A separate starting route
can restore both services if every prerequisite is actually usable.
Changing the ordinary architecture to remove one standing dependency
may be more reliable and more expensive. Compare continuing costs,
shared failure, timely rights and retained independence on the same
dates. Preserve first deadlines even when the eventual benefit is large.

A restoration priority can invite diversion from urgent protection or
undermine independent scrutiny. Keep separate authorized sources and
rights review. Include lost records, alternate courts and elected
proceedings where relevant. Do not assume a paid reservation is independent
or that a sector must accept an unsafe input. Publish useful aggregate
evidence and keep operational and personal details in protected custody.

## Scoped result, transition and next action

The [primary evidence](sources.md) documents real interdependence.
The [constructed comparison](../settlement/joint-restoration-and-independent-start/01-two-services-and-a-first-independent-act.md)
tests a blocked first act, prior funded protection and a missed next
call. It establishes no actual infrastructure readiness, authorization,
consent, spending or service improvement. Carry existing duties, records,
deadlines, receipts and actual pending claims through every transfer.

Within an authorized joint exercise budget, compare a real pair of
mutually dependent services and a required independent remedy.
Use accepted operators, qualified reviewers and verified available sources.
Stop at an observed sequence and a priced, explicitly unresolved failure
case without changing live priorities. Reopen on a changed controller,
new dependence, failed refill or a stronger standing design.
