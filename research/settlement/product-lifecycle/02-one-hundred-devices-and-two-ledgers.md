# One hundred devices through supplier failure

[Product lifecycle](README.md) · Authored comparison

One hundred users need equivalent functioning communication devices
by day 14. Each returns or presents a failed original device weighing
1 kg under the declared measurement scope. Compare two years of
equivalent accepted function with the same required safety and
ongoing support. The fixture assumes the new and restored devices
meet that requirement, that 60 original devices can safely be
repaired by the deadline and that each nonrestored device enters
qualified treatment. For material accounting assume the restored
devices retain the measured original mass and follow only the 100
original devices. New devices have their own
lifecycle duties. Any expected repair and parts materials outside
the declared measured outputs need a separate real-world comparison.

| Package | Actual service by day 14 if capacity is available | Cost units |
|---|---|---:|
| Replacement and treatment only | 100 new devices. All 100 originals treated. | 100 × (5 + 1) = 600 |
| Independent repair and replacement for the rest | 60 safe repairs and 40 new devices. Remaining 40 originals treated. | 60 × 2 + 40 × (5 + 1) = 360 |
| Sole supplier repair with an apparently separate reseller | Same nominal rates while the vendor performs. | 360 under the same capacity. If the shared pairing route fails and only 50 new units are available, 50 users remain without service. |

Under a separate loss of parts capacity, the independent route
can safely repair only 35 and can access only 50 replacements.
Actual service covers 85. It leaves 15 people without qualified
devices, even with adequate cash. A substitute counts only on
safe acceptance by the due time. This is a physical limit,
independent of the cost or financial assurance.

## Who can pay when the producer fails

In the passing independent package, assume owners fund 40
out-of-warranty repairs at 80 and the existing owners or
applicable essential-service payers fund 40 replacements at 200.
The defined producer obligation includes 20 due warranty repairs
at 40 and treatment of 40 originals at 40. This scope is assumed
for the fixture. It leaves other warranty or civil claims separate.
If the producer fails, verified assurance provides 60 of the
80 by the actual payment dates. A separately authorized residual
bridge of 20 covers the remaining due work. If only 15 is usable,
the producer-side shortfall is 5. A future creditor recovery
or an assurance issuer controlled by the failed producer cannot
fund the gap. The assurance is a source for the same 80, not
an extra cost or a public payment of the 200 replacement bill.

## Verify the material destinations

In the passing independent case, 60 kg remains in use, 24 kg
is documented recovered output accepted for use, 12 kg is
lawfully treated residue and 4 kg is measured authorized process
loss. This accounts for the 100 kg once. If 20 of the 40 kg sent
for treatment is instead dispatched abroad with no receiving
proof, record 20 kg unresolved. Under the declared composition
of the 20 kg still treated domestically, verified recovery
then falls to 12 kg, with 6 kg residue and 2 kg loss. A dispatch
certificate cannot restore the higher credited recovery.

Select independent repair with paid receiving under the passing
assumptions. Replacement remains a real option where service,
safety or environmental comparison changes. The 240-unit difference is a direct cost margin. If independent
qualification, tools, standby and support add 100 units net of
the replacement route, the comparison becomes 460 against 600.
At an additional net cost of 240 the two tie. Higher incremental
costs reverse the direct comparison. Actual lifetimes, energy,
rebound, work, parts and public or household cost remain open.
