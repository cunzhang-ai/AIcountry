# CLN05–CLN08. Allocate real costs and verify that a place is safe again

*Design221 · Part of [industrial closure](../21-industrial-cleanup-and-insolvency.md).*

**CLN05 — Preserve dedicated security and disclose a narrow site charge.**
The coordinated insolvency and permit Act keeps duly segregated closure
assets in their lawful restricted custody. Recognize only actual non-estate
property. The court applies a prospective public charge to specified site
interests and their net sale proceeds, after necessary realization costs
and preserved earlier perfected rights. Record the affected interests,
purpose and maximum charge in the public register before it can bind
later rights. The charge ranks ahead of consensual security created on
those interests after valid registration. Use the ordinary pre-charge
allocation to identify the part otherwise available to the unencumbered
estate. Preserve U04's necessary administration, protected support and
capped wages in that pool before applying the charge. Count those
protected amounts once across the whole estate. Other property,
non-estate assets, protected client funds and inherited secured ranks
remain governed by their own law.

The court limits a draw to verified necessary containment, remediation
and monitoring, including an expressly assigned like-for-like recovery
of an actual eligible public advance. Deduct overlapping coverage and
payments. Release excess to its lawful owner and apply U04 to the
remaining distribution. No diversion to unrelated spending, penalties
or general damages. Preserve the underlying liability under its own
law; the ceiling bounds the priority, not the duty. An uncollected
residual stays a loss or continuing obligation, rather than cash.
Trustees have paid safe-custody duties within actual authority and
estate or enacted sources. Personal liability requires its own grounds.
Give operators, affected people, workers, creditors and guarantors
notice, useful-time review and a meaningful dispute route.

**CLN06 — Put residual and historic costs in separate voted accounts.**
Select a prospective, bounded sector-financed residual account for
scheduled operator failure after collectible instruments and available
proceeds. The Assembly defines eligible work, covered classes, verified
residual exposure base, risk differentiation, collections, reserves and
release conditions. Review common downturn, actual ability to pay,
entry, price incidence and future source. Funded interim public
advances have their own cap, authority and dated liquidity. Credit only
actual like-for-like recoveries and never spend them twice. Legacy sites
without an available operator, valid instrument or eligible source
receive a separate elected protection and cleanup budget based on
hazard, duration and a competent receiver. No automatic retrospective
levy or open-ended public guarantee. Keep low-cash emergencies and
long-term upkeep visible. If an account is exhausted, use available
lawful protection and report the unmet exposure for an elected choice.
Existing individual claims and independent review remain usable.

**CLN07 — Release only verified performance.** The qualified inspector
outside the operator and site-promoter conflict verifies containment,
treatment, lawful disposal, exposure at receiving locations and actual
restoration under published criteria. Follow the required monitoring
window and future receiving obligations. Keep funds for uncertain
residual duties until the stated release tests are met. Separate
decommissioning, risk reduction, certification, spend and actual
conditions. A closure certificate does not erase a resident's proved
loss or a continuing source duty. Publish safe status and independent
reasons with usable challenge. AI may compare estimates, surface
inconsistent monitoring and assist communication. It cannot certify
site safety, determine liability or authorize a release.

**CLN08 — Adopt prospectively and test both kinds of failure.** Prior
law sets adoption, covered thresholds, registration and transition.
Preserve earlier priorities and lawful contract, site, employment,
wage, support, collective and compensation claims. Use funded OP/T
succession and existing officials, independent audit and courts.
Compare phase-secured provision with stronger funded pooling,
ordinary public cleanup and secured-creditor-first alternatives on
the same protection. A fair phase-in and competitively available
security matter for smaller operators. Reopen on failed collection,
hidden pollution, unreliable closure estimates, unlawful transfer,
credit exclusion or stronger verified prevention. No new crime,
general takeover, recovery guarantee or foreign authority follows.
