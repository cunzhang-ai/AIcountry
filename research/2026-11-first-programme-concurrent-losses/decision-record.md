The [Design304 baseline](baseline.json) contains priced individual
failure tests and separate exact approvals. The same optional programme
can fit after either of two failures and fail when both consume the
same budget. A signed housing lease also changes the starting position.
Apply the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

## Exact choices and tradeoffs

- **Protect and repair ordinary provision.** This is the live
  retain-and-repair comparator. It leaves incremental heating and
  training benefits open. If a continuing staffing gap exists even
  here, show the duty, missed deadline and correction rather than
  claiming success through the absence of optional work.
- **Start the finite energy bridge with independently accepted
  replenishment and review.** Select this exact option in the
  authored unbound reference, conditional on harm in the useful
  window, actual source and a separate lawful approval. Protect
  ordinary water and care and record the training cohort and
  new tenancies that wait.
- **Start paid preparation or bring a different housing or
  delivery proposal.** Paid preparation can fit on its own in
  the reference. A narrower or direct-public housing route needs
  a specific accepted alternative, physical capacity and its own
  paid transition. Ownership does not create a spare worker.

The selected priorities are working judgments. Staffing evidence
can fail or a different harm can dominate. Keep separate elections,
remedies, lawful approvals and local control. A new cost cannot be
used to conceal an existing service cut or reallocate a court slot.
Once a lease binds, preserve it and find lawful funded support.
One accepted provider can cover the authored gap. The national
housing advance and local receiving remain distinct.

## Compatibility and qualification

[Transition rules](../country/C14/06/07-first-programme-under-concurrent-losses.md)
carry the choice into the first programme. Apply the original
case's units, reserve, authorities and protected duties only in
their specified scope. The [original evidence](sources.md) informs
the exposure and verification questions. The [combined exercise](../settlement/integrated-first-programme/06-concurrent-losses-and-binding-duties.md)
does not price a real launch. Actual appropriations, lawful action,
participation, readiness and outcomes remain unverified.

Test one candidate operating context with real water receiving,
home-care and protected financial schedules, the first two calls,
and housing handoff. Compare the same affordable choices after
recorded acceptance and actual reserve costs. Reopen on changed
authority, a missed original duty, overlapping staffing, or a better
funded route for residents.
