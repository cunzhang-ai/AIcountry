# Design360. Trace a local service floor through the actual catchment

[MF19–MF24](../../country/C04/08/05-neighborhood-access-and-local-allocation.md)
joins access evidence, lawful local allocation, independently usable remedy and
continuing deadlines. Show a recurring shortfall separately from a lawful
reallocation or temporary backstop. Count shared routes once across budgets.

The [comparison](../local-funding-and-neighborhood-access/01-one-council-two-neighborhoods.md)
separates a resource assignment that can change from a binding contract
and from the staffed fallback. Actual funds and service receipt remain open.
