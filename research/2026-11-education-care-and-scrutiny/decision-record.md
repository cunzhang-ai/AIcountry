# Design491 decision record

**Scope and baseline.** Apply the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md)
and Design490 [baseline](baseline.json) to the first-budget
choice. Both optional starts share a constrained access input
with a due independent hearing. Costs, dates and accepted receipt
in this exercise are constructed.

**Selection and dissent.** Select the care-first stage on the assumed
useful deadlines while retaining an exact school-first rival. Either
could qualify in a different context. Full acceleration depends on a
real third access block and gross cash. Preparing both preserves
flexibility while residents wait. Report that burden. Keep independent
review and original care and education outside discretionary tradeoffs.

**Authority and continuity.** Members see the same dated source and
capacity evidence with protected originals and usable independent
costing. Any material alteration needs a fresh useful opportunity for
scrutiny. National and local acts retain separate authority. Preserve
binding continuation and direct remedies when a later optional stage
fails. No calculation, forecast or staffing document executes a vote.

**Evidence and reopening.** The [matched case](../settlement/education-care-and-scrutiny/01-three-commitments-one-opening-calendar.md)
and [sources](sources.md) support a conditional cross-sector choice.
Reopen on actual qualified access, receipt, different urgent needs
or a better sourced common offer. Next compare the same budget when
a local council changes one intake or its delivery medium after the
vote, with protected resident access and recorded effective acts.
Actual participation, authority, readiness and better lives remain unverified.
