# Design348 decision on meaningful choice and assent in public services

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design347, DP01–DP57, AP, PD, TU, free refusal, LX and the working
framework. Existing rules protect staffed intake, fallback and remedies.
Specify how a public operator must present choices, prove a legally
operative assent and correct prior steering or misrecording across media.

Select a proportionate duty for material choice effects, a usable refusal
path and an express act where assent is required. Preserve statutory
automatic due payments and lawful accessibility support. The project
assistant authored the choice. Service owners operate and fund the routes.
Elected bodies set authority and burdens. Independent reviewers verify
evidence and remedy within existing jurisdiction.

## Alternatives and objections

A notice-only regime is inexpensive but can leave an unequal path
intact. A blanket ban on recommendations can remove useful help and
accessible reminders. Select bounded transparent assistance with
effective refusal, act-specific proof and a prompt correction route.
Keep ordinary staffed assistance and simpler forms as live alternatives.

The [official sources](sources.md) examine administrative burden and
consumer choice design. They inform what to test and do not establish the
candidate's public law. Evidence gathering can itself burden people or
expose them. Use safe samples, actual lawful choice records, and
independent review with funded ordinary access.

## Migration and reopening

Carry pending requests, lawful mandates, current benefits, prior consents
and original dates. Pay staffed receiving and correction through any
channel change. Actual independent review, affected participation, adoption,
costs and experience remain open.

Test an ordinary service and a future agent-mediated version with willing
participants, including people who decline optional use. Compare actual
comprehension, refusal cost, timely receipt and erroneous recorded
permissions. Reopen on pressure, unexplained gaps, broad evidence collection,
continued misuse after revocation or a simpler effective route.
