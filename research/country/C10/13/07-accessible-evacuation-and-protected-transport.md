# Accessible evacuation and protected public transport

*Design290 · RC29–RC33 · Proposed ordinary readiness and reserve rules.*

**RC29 — Buy an entire safe route before counting a place.** Local
rescue and transport authorities jointly identify foreseeable need
for accessible evacuation and other protected transport, including
people without private vehicles or a prior registry entry. Assess
routes, actual wheelchair or stretcher positions, a compatible
vehicle and crew, transport time, necessary support and a staffed
accepting destination. Include transfers between vehicles and onward
care. Compare supported safe stay, local transport, and paid external
or regional alternatives. Purchase distinct routes where duties can
coincide, including lawful accessible election and remedy access.
Set civic availability and route criteria prospectively on actual
access barriers. Keep ridership details protected from campaign use.
Share reserve capacity only where its time, control, staff, routes
and destination can actually accommodate the combined calls or where
the residual risk is expressly identified as uncovered.

**RC30 — Prove the immediate need and the route's useful time.**
The existing local rescue lead verifies imminent danger and safe
options. Clinical staff determine the required care in transit.
The competent electoral or service officer identifies the original
access duty and deadline. The authorized public dispatcher confirms
owner release, vehicle and crew, accessibility, necessary companion
or equipment, pickup, safe travel, accepted receiving and next
availability. Keep a usable offline dispatch and pickup route.
Accept direct requests, local observations and reports through
trusted intermediaries. Neither an unreachable person nor a late
request loses priority for that reason alone. A referral is neither
consent nor actual receipt. Keep consent and existing narrow
HP/LF grounds for protective action distinct.

**RC31 — Decide only within the scheduled uncommitted reserve.**
A competent ordinary Act may schedule specific public-controlled
reserve vehicles for these joint purposes, with lawful ownership,
release terms, spending and a named dispatch duty officer. Give
independent civic access its own protected transport commitment
where identified under RC29. Retain established protected bookings.
For the uncommitted common reserve, first check whether safe partial,
sequential or alternate trips actually fit the deadlines. If a
genuine incompatibility remains, select the feasible prevention
of imminent death or irreversible grave bodily harm first.
Then prevent the imminent irreversible loss of lawful civic access
before a recoverable service interruption. Preserve the reasons
where a partial trip could separate a person from necessary support.
For comparable bodily claims use applicable clinical criteria or
CP06's equal-claim rule with recorded reasons and actual feasible
benefit. Preserve every feasible access route. The source rule
creates no power over committed protected transport, private
vehicles, workers, clinical consent or the result of an election.
CP13's exclusions and original CP/RX clocks govern any action
that relies on those powers.

**RC32 — Keep the missed trip and next call on their original clocks.**
Notify the affected service owner and people through safe accessible
channels. Protect original filing, poll or care times and records.
A viable substitute can be used only under its own law. The
election or court owner keeps its decision and independent review
route, including an applicable physical-failure remedy under ER.
Transport officials cannot count a lost vote, certify a result,
move a poll or treat later service as timely original access.
Return the resource under RC26 only after safe receiving,
travel, reset, qualified crew, fuel and rest. Price the next
accepted call or disclose the still-uncovered interval.

**RC33 — Make the actual tradeoff reviewable and correctable.**
Publish the paid availability, declared common failures, actual
use and aggregate losses without revealing private health, identity
or ballot information. Provide original route and commitment
evidence to competent independent review. Seek useful-time
correction for a false exclusion, induced shortage or protected
vehicle diverted without authority. Maintain affected workers'
pay and outstanding service remedies. The Assembly and relevant
elected authorities choose and finance the actual resource schedule
prospectively with staff, accessible intake, non-AI fallback,
accepted destinations and independent execution. AI may compare
route feasibility or flag conflicts. An accountable human issues
the dispatch order. Reopen after missed access, repeated emergency
reliance, a failed second call or a stronger separately staffed
rival. A live transfer preserves all prior bookings, source terms
and protected clocks through a named successor.
