**CRD09 — Fund and test simultaneous receiving.** Before relying on a
material proof route, the service names the people, actual sources,
lawful sites, transport where needed and independent reviewing
operators that make the ordinary and alternate routes work.
Test shared issuer, hosting, time, network, power, custody,
recovery and staffing failure with simultaneous actual deadlines.
Use JC and the ordinary budget. Count the same verifier's shift
once. Distinguish demand, timely intake, accepted proof, legal
decision, actual receipt and unresolved cases. Missing funding
requires lawful correction with current claims visible.

**CRD10 — Keep admission, review and remedy contestable.** The
Assembly sets the general floor and elected bodies fund it within
their competence. Source issuers, sector acceptors and operators
act within their stated mandates. Existing data and audit bodies
may examine their respective source and governance duties.
Ordinary courts and the actual electoral review route hear
contested exclusion or abuse with protected source access.
Independent reviewers need separately controlled records,
qualified appointments, funding and an actual execution route.
A provider cannot certify its own decisive error, a regulator
cannot allocate service through an accreditation list, and DP
cannot take over the protected institutions.

**CRD11 — Keep portability and exit meaningful.** For any shared
operator, procure lawful and usable handback of format, custody,
verification and protected pending cases. Test a separate route
with genuine independence. Keep old verification material
where necessary to examine valid old acts and apply the actual
retention and safety rules. Revoke obsolete access and preserve
records, not the old operator's power. Use FT/OP/T and DP for
paid succession. A new format or interpretation must preserve
scope, consent, original dates and contestability. Foreign proof
depends on applicable law and qualified source. Recognition of
a format gives neither foreign jurisdiction nor automatic status.

**CRD12 — Review lived access and future means.** Report missing
proof, adverse delay, unnecessary disclosure, actual recovery
and false acceptance under safe aggregates. Include people who
never reached intake. Compare retained direct proof, optional
shared routes and independently sourced alternatives on the
same tasks, costs and rights. Do not choose by enrolment alone.
Reopen on common failure, exclusion, coercion, unpayable
operations or a better complete alternative. Adopt only
prospectively under actual authority, with dated funded receivers
and stronger duties preserved. No common credential or platform
is adopted by this research.
