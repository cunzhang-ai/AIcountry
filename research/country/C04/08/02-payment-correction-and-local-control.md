# MF05–MF08. Pay on time and protect local public choice

*Part of [funded local mandates](../08-funded-local-mandates-and-equalization.md).*

**MF05 — Name the payer and pay the undisputed amount.** Keep the due dates
and payment authorities under actual source law, including existing
benchmark and sector advance rules where applicable. Profile cash at the
first payroll, supplier or service at risk. The national calculation
officer gives the locality usable reasons and a protected view of its
inputs. A distinct authorized Treasury payer delivers lawful undisputed
amounts by their own deadlines. The local receiver confirms accessible
cash and responsibility for the named service. A correction in one line,
dispute with one council or change of government cannot hold unrelated
amounts. Lawful recovery is scoped and reviewable. An unresolved claim
retains its original due date and independent correction under DB/B19.

**MF06 — Diagnose and correct the shortfall without charging residents.**
Distinguish a wrong allocation, late transfer, local evasion or misuse,
unexpected need, lawful revenue loss and actual unavailable staff or sites.
A finite correction must identify the legal source, usable money,
receiving operator, restoration and end date. Pay a lawful alternate only
under existing authority. Keep the lawful debtor and any valid recovery
or advance separate, without paying the same debt twice. Neither a loan
nor a standing rescue fund follows from an unmet duty. If existing
available authority cannot finance a remedy, report the first threatened
payment and service and bring the actual source or scope choice through
DB and FB. Use sector and prior-law interim duties and independent
review in useful time. Report the still-unserved duty if cash, capacity
or lawful authority remains missing.

Deliberate nonpayment and breaches retain existing accountability.
Any applicable fiscal discipline is directed at the proper public entity
and actual cause with independent challenge. Do not deduct a collective
penalty from the protected funding required for continuing common duties
because another locality exceeded its budget. Reconcile unlawful
diversions, recovery, unused balances and lawful optional expenditure
through their existing routes. No discretionary loyalty condition,
local election delay or ministerial dissolution follows from payment.

**MF07 — Pay for shared services while retaining elected responsibility.**
Local councils retain choice over methods and optional enhancements
within LR and actual competence. A pooled service or cross-local
compact names the legal payer for the fixed standby and the actual
accepted work, including safe travel and displaced duties. Explain how
it allocates resident and nonresident use within lawful sector criteria.
Use a prior elected compact or competent allocation for binding
contributions. A temporary visit, moving address or disputed payer does
not end a qualifying person's existing useful-time protection. An
accepted handover and statutory fiscal settlement are distinct acts.
The first receiver helps route and preserve the date under AP, while
the actual debtor retains responsibility. No automatic debt claim
against the other locality or transfer of political jurisdiction arises.

**MF08 — Make the calculation contestable and protect successor capacity.**
Publish the aggregate need, capacity, grants, expiry, local uses and
remaining delivery gaps with original data scope and uncertainty.
Give members, opposition and affected councils the same timely access
to public methods and usable alternatives. Give residents a safe route
to challenge eligibility, lost service or a false receiving claim.
Use AA audit, competent independent review and SR execution under
their existing mandates. Retain records, accrued claims, actual payroll,
contracts and independently usable payment controls through OP/T and HT.
There is no new fiscal guardian or national control of local elections.

explain the account, with original source provenance and a paid manual
route. The elected authority changes the standard. A competent officer
decides a fact. The authorized payer transfers money. Actual providers
deliver. Model agreement cannot validate a contested need, impose a
grant condition or prove receipt. Reopen on measured inaccessible care,
a structural local revenue shock, common substitute failure, wrongly
