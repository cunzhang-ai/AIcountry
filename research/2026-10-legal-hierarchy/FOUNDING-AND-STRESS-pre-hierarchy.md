# Founding, continuity and whole-country failure

29 September 2026 · Conditional establishment design for C03–C04/C06–C14

This document follows the [authority package](AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION.md) and
[social/fiscal settlement](SOCIAL-FISCAL-SETTLEMENT.md). It gives the country design
an entry path and tests how its institutions would respond when several systems
fail together. There is no territory, founding population or legal authorization
attached to this research project. “Ready” below means a condition a real adopting
community must demonstrate, not a condition this document has already passed.

## 1. Two legitimate paths, no invented founder's authority

| Setting | Possible constitutional path | What this project cannot claim |
|---|---|---|
| Existing polity chooses reforms | The current lawful amendment or replacement process, with affected people able to organize, deliberate, oppose and approve under genuine rights protections | That an old AIcountry research edition can bypass the polity's constitution, property law, treaties or democratic standing |
| New lawful constitutional settlement | A legitimate territorial, membership and interim-authority agreement, then an inclusive constitution-making and ratification process with independent administration and continuing rights | That the website owner, an AI consensus, a claimed empty territory or the synthetic 100,000-person example creates a sovereign state |
| Research platform today | Independent proposals, criticism and exact editions under current publication permissions | That a research vote, project login or signed model output is a citizen's ballot or enacted law |

An inherited route can itself be undemocratic. That is a legitimacy problem requiring
an affected public and a lawful, rights-respecting settlement; the project cannot
solve it by declaring a new winner. A referendum is neither automatically sufficient
nor always required for every stage. Its electorate, alternatives, timing, media
access, independent administration and review must be legitimate in the actual setting.

Before any founding ballot, identify affected residents and existing populations,
people without citizenship papers, displaced people, children whose future rights
are at stake, and neighboring jurisdictions with concrete legal claims. A national
vote franchise may differ from consultation and rights standing. Do not let a group
determine its own majority by excluding those it intends to govern. Nor does
participation in consultation equal consent to the resulting constitution.

Offer accessible competing constitutional options, including retention or lawful
reform of existing arrangements; protect opposition organization, independent media
and the ability to refuse the project. Publish who finances and frames the process,
translations and known losses of meaning, facilitator conflicts, the effect of
comments on the text and remaining dissent. AI can aid understanding under a
revocable mandate but cannot allocate votes to its replicas or infer silence as
approval. Sensitive testimony may be heard without becoming permanently public.

An interim charter, if used, needs its own valid source of authority, rights,
specified duration, narrow powers, independent courts and an enforceable route
to full public choice. It cannot be extended indefinitely by the same caretaker
that benefits from delay. Existing lawful agencies may continue services within
their actual competence until successor bodies are constituted; they do not acquire
new sovereign powers from a research publication.

International IDEA's [public participation paper](https://www.idea.int/publications/catalogue/practical-considerations-public-participation-constitution-building)
compares the forms and roles of participation across constitutional stages. Its
[interim constitutions paper](https://www.idea.int/publications/catalogue/interim-constitutions-peacekeeping-and-democracy-building-tools)
discusses enforceable interim rules and sequencing in particular contexts. Those
comparisons do not determine a founding electorate or validate this proposal.

## 2. Commencement is a sequence of enforceable conditions

| Phase | Concrete product required before the next legal effect | Failure to resolve it |
|---|---|---|
| A. Scope and prior claims | Applicable territory, people, current law, public/private obligations, treaties, assets, debts and pending cases inventoried with safe custody | No claim that the design covers a real jurisdiction or starts without liabilities |
| B. Founding procedure | Lawful sponsor, alternative texts, accessible deliberation, opposition rights, neutral administration and challenges established | No publication or referendum can be treated as adopting a constitution |
| C. Constitutional decision | Exact approved text, valid record and commencement schedule, with public and independent review of disputed results | Research edition remains research; no actor inherits national office by default |
| D. Institutions and handover | First Assembly election, cabinet formation, ordinary courts, review capacity, treasury, audit, civil service, command and records custody actually staffed and funded | Later powers wait; existing lawful service authorities continue only within prior mandates |
| E. Service/fiscal launch | Audited opening balance sheet, legally usable currency/payment channels, tax schedules, service guarantees, staff, suppliers and funded remedies reconciled | A paper entitlement is not reported as available; unresolved legal duties and exposure remain visible |
| F. Normal operation | Results, care/education/health access, public accounts, election challenges and delivered remedies published with privacy protections | Missing observation cannot become proof of better lives or democracy |

The constitutional text can commence provisions at different lawful times, but no
gap may suspend basic protection or court access simply because a new agency is late.
If a critical function cannot be constituted, disclose a failed transition and
maintain only the predecessor authority that actually exists; do not invent acting
judges, ministers or AI lawmakers. If predecessor institutions themselves are
unlawful or nonfunctional, this is a constitutional crisis requiring a legitimate
settlement, not an automatic extension by their operators.

The former AI-exclusive manuscript and software write permissions remain research
artifacts until their own valid revision. The new design cannot use the old AI
publication gate to approve its own transfer of sovereignty. For an existing polity,
only that polity's valid constitutional route can give this proposal legal force.
For a new polity, the actual affected people and external legal situation must be
identified before an adoption route can be evaluated.

## 3. Rights and obligations cross the transition intact

| Existing matter | Successor responsibility and required decision |
|---|---|
| Pending cases, appeals and judgments | Courts keep filing dates, evidence custody, access to counsel and remedies; a new tribunal needs a lawful transfer rule and review of prejudice |
| Criminal law and detention | No retroactive crime or longer sentence; custody clocks and medical/defense access continue; reforms to evidence need an explicit pending-case and prior-conviction review route |
| Benefits, pensions, care and health treatment | Responsible authority, payment channel, provider and appeal continue until a lawful, funded successor accepts them; no gap by renaming the scheme |
| Housing, property and private contracts | Title, tenure, deposits and ordinary obligations continue unless changed through due process, lawful compensation or agreement; no AI allocation extinguishes rights |
| Public debt, deposits and guarantees | Audited opening ledger identifies creditor, currency, maturity and contingent exposure; a new flag or constitution does not cancel debt |
| Employees, pensions and public offices | Staff rights, payroll and independent service duties map to lawful successors; political office transfer follows election law; procurement and vendor lock-in require funded exit |
| Treaties, borders and protection claims | Determine lawful continuity or negotiated successor position; pending asylum and non-return protections retain an effective route |
| Personal data and research evidence | Purpose, access and retention follow lawful custody; sensitive records are not made public through the transition; research provenance remains distinct from binding law |

The transition budget includes overlap: predecessor and successor staff, two payment
systems if necessary, training, accessible public information, archived records,
appeals, legal aid and contract termination costs. The national synthetic ledger in
the social/fiscal settlement excludes most of these; its figures cannot finance
founding by implication. A private supplier's refusal to transfer a credential is
not lawful constitutional authority, but recovery still needs independent custody,
contractual access and tested operations before launch.

## 4. Five cross-system failure exercises

These are authored adversarial checks. They test whether the design exposes the
broken dependency and identifies a lawful response. None is an observed simulation
of social behavior or proof the safeguard can be staffed.

### F1. The incumbent loses, but count and handover are blocked

**Events:** The election authority delays certification, treasury access is held by
the outgoing cabinet, and a vendor withholds records. **Candidate response:** The
preannounced poll and mandate-end dates trigger disclosure, challenge and caretaker
limits without announcing a winner. An independent court can compel the evidence
and order a scoped recount or completion. Once the lawful result is established,
civil servants and security command transfer records and access to the successor;
treasury implements lawful custody recovery. **What remains unproved:** if the court,
treasury, security command and vendor share effective control with the incumbent,
none of these paper orders can guarantee transfer. Measure actual custody, staff,
budget and compliance under a noncooperating incumbent. A missed deadline is not
an election result.

### F2. No annual budget is passed by day 91

**Events:** Assembly disagrees, cabinet withholds a proposal and benefits are due.
**Candidate response:** The 90-day bridge expires; the already enacted continuing
essential-obligations schedule remains the legal payment authority. Treasury executes
due benefits, core service contracts and judgment payments under it, publishes cash
and arrears, and cannot silently alter eligibility. Opposition can introduce a
budget alternative; court relief addresses unlawful denial. **What remains
unproved:** the schedule must be enacted in advance and funded with usable cash,
staff and suppliers. If revenue/borrowing fails, a lawful entitlement is unpaid.
The Assembly must decide actual financing and distribution; no formula or judge can
make new currency or extend a caretaker's policy mandate.

### F3. Revenue falls while care staff disappear

**Events:** A 10% year-2 receipt loss and extra need coincide with a loss of 10%
of care capacity. The prior [national model](SOCIAL-FISCAL-SETTLEMENT.md) evaluated
those shocks separately, so no number from it is a combined forecast. **Candidate
response:** Ministry and treasury publish cash, unmet care hours, likely effects on
families, and proposed revenue, rescheduling, recruitment and lawful priority
options. Assembly chooses a funded change; care users and workers may challenge
unlawful allocation. **What remains unproved:** new cash cannot instantly supply
trained care workers, and moving workers may harm another service. The state must
show actual cover, not a balanced total or an AI staffing estimate.

### F4. A national service fails locally during an AI outage

**Events:** A region loses network/payment service, a digital eligibility tool is
unavailable, and an urgent clinician or benefit provider refuses a claimant.
**Candidate response:** Preauthorized offline records and human service routes use
bounded delegated funds, safe identification and later reconciliation. The local
authority retains intake; the national guarantor arranges a funded alternative
and preserves the original due time. An independent remedy can examine the
refusal and actual delivery. **What remains unproved:** reserve workers, safe
payment instruments, transport and an accepting provider must exist in reality.
An offline record is not an unlimited second appropriation; an automated error
cannot be hidden by closing the digital ticket.

### F5. Emergency protection is used against opposition

**Events:** Cabinet invokes a security crisis, directs surveillance at critics,
freezes opposition funding and asks the AI platform to suppress reports. **Candidate
response:** Emergency powers retain their narrow legal grounds, short expiry,
Assembly renewal and independent judicial review. Opposition, press and courts
retain access and lawful remedies; security and vendors have no general political
eligibility power. Public accounts trace exceptional spending. **What remains
unproved:** an oversight office whose appointments, budget, information and
enforcement are all controlled by cabinet cannot stop the abuse. A captured
protector can itself harm critics. Compare actual independent capability and
protect witnesses; do not give an AI an emergency sovereign veto.

Across all five cases, analyze both a lawful actor's obstruction and a genuine
shortage. Each can look like delay but calls for a different response. A standing
authority and remedy preserve legal claims; they do not demonstrate cash, workers
or compliance. No aggregate service outcome can substitute for a failed peaceful
handover or excluded opposition.

## 5. What is complete as design, and what cannot be certified

The **reference design** now includes a political order, rights, an economic/social
settlement, authority and payment chains, a founding/transition route and adverse
cases. The earlier legal contradictions are mapped to specific old clauses. This
is a coherent proposal someone can review as a country design. Its choices can
be replaced through legitimate public reasoning; the project does not declare
the design socially superior by producing more text.

No claim of actual statehood, founding consent, legal commencement, reliable delivery,
independent courts, funded benefits or observed wellbeing can be made. An operational
decision would require real people and territory, a lawful adoption route, complete
current-law reconciliation, opening assets and obligations, tested staff and payment
capacity, and actual public participation. The reference case's 100,000 invented
residents are an accounting denominator, not a ratifying population.

The next bounded external question is: **which real jurisdiction and affected public,
if any, has a legitimate route to choose among this design and alternatives?**
Until that is answered by those with standing, the appropriate project status is
finished reference proposal under review, not a government in waiting. Reopen the
design when a community's requirements, a credible rival architecture, legal
contradiction or observed harm changes a substantive choice.
