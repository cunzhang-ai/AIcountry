# Public standards without a protected incumbent's permission gate

*TS03–TS04; part of [technical safety](../22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md).*

**TS03 — A binding technical instrument has exact lawful scope.** C03/C04/V01
require named source power, issuer, affected activities, operative version,
prospective dates, transition, inspection powers, remedy and sanction boundary.
Publish the complete enforceable text free of charge in usable forms. A private
paywall, hidden classifier, vendor manual or changing foreign web page cannot
supply law. Mere guidance and internal procurement specifications are distinct.

Each numeric limit identifies quantity/unit, population/site, exposure period,
measurement method, detection/uncertainty rule and consequence of an exceedance.
A design target, monitoring trigger and enforceable maximum are not interchangeable.
For an equivalent method, require evidence addressing the same actual hazards and
people, not an incumbent's proprietary tool. Publish material uncertainty and
reasons for rejecting alternatives without exposing protected testimony.

Assembly affirmation applies to material changes of protected access, reachable
activities, substantial burdens or accepted risk; lawful bounded delegates can
update reversible technical detail within that mandate. Neither an unelected
panel nor a standard-setting association can enlarge its own powers. Changes
remain challengeable for evidence, rights and actual delegated scope.

**TS04 — Graded entry must name the regulated act.** TD entry requirements are
exclusive under this statute. Use ordinary duties/notification where sufficient;
reserve prior permission for listed consequential activity. License the operator,
site, product or actual reserved competence separately as required, not every
buyer or software user. Publish criteria, tests, fee/cost and necessary items;
V02/B16 hearing, accessible help, decision clocks and direct court relief apply.

No silence grants a hazardous-operation approval; no rolling information request
creates an unreviewable veto. A court can require an actual lawful decision or
interim protection, not invent unproved competence. Qualifications test practical
ability with reasonable accommodation and demonstrated equivalent learning;
no hereditary guild, mandatory incumbent sponsorship or nationality/AI-score ban.
Fees enter the ordinary Treasury and remain bounded by actual authorized cost.

Required technical annexes must be adopted before a new annex-dependent activity
can be licensed under the new regime. Their absence creates no punitive numerical
breach or retrospective ban on an existing lawful activity. Existing protections
continue under TS15; material newly proposed risks may need a specific prior Act.
A classification disagreement has reasoned independent review, not automatic
entry into the most restrictive class. Serious imminent harm retains TS09's own
actual grounds; missing paperwork alone does not establish that danger.
