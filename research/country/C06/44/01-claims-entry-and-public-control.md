# NB01–NB04: classify the promise, then assign the actual authority

*Part of [C06/44](../44-nonbank-finance-and-client-protection.md); design113 candidate.*

**NB01 — Different promises require different protection.** Apply to actual
commercial intermediation, including public/cooperative, foreign-facing and
agent-operated firms: payment custody; securities custody/brokerage/advice;
pooled investment; nondeposit lending; insurance; funded private pensions;
trading, clearing and settlement. Separate the legal issuer, asset owner,
operator/controller, currency, governing law, maturity, redemption/guarantee,
fees, loss bearer and actual protection before acceptance. A token, agent,
affiliate, synthetic exposure or future communication medium cannot erase an
obligation. A customer's own investing, ordinary mutual help, open software,
political organization or supplier's incidental terms are not licensed finance
merely by involving money. Unsupported novel semantics require an answerable
scope determination, not disappearance or automatic authorization.

Demand/notice/term cash-management repayable public balances remain M01 bank
activity unless fully safeguarded M05 payment custody. A disclosed ordinary
bond is the issuer's risk-bearing security, not an insured balance. An
investment unit participates in actual assets/losses; an insurance/DB promise
is the issuer's obligation, not ownership of every backing asset. Public Y/S
social insurance is statutory solidarity, not a private funded pension or a
promise that an individual owns their contribution history as cash.

**NB02 — Entry has a specified owner.** Conduct career licensing officers under
MC09–12 license the actual custody/payment, brokerage/advice, investment manager,
nonbank lender and trading-venue activity. Prudential career officers additionally
license insurers, funded pension arrangements and central counterparties (CCPs)
for their distinct promise/funding risks. One coordinated filing, distinct
findings, no duplicate bank conduct license; a license for one activity never
licenses the others. Prospectuses register disclosure, not expected returns.
V02 supplies evidence, reasons, proportionate conditions, public fees and appeal;
no chief signature gate, silence approval or general permission to manage private
life. Actual career competence/accepted custody and source must exist before
new commercial operation; missing staff is accountable failure, not informal
regulatory discretion to waive the protection.

**NB03 — Protectors remain replaceable and reviewable.** Extend MC chiefs'
existing fixed appointments/reserves, independent case careers, for-cause courts,
separate readable operating pay and protected source records expressly to these
NB duties. No new sovereign AI or sector-appointed policy chief. Each office
employs qualified actuarial, custody, market, credit and forensic staff through
C04; an industry consultant is disclosed assistance, not the final case owner.
Same sponsor/vendor/source families do not establish independent assessment.
One joint inspection needs each actual M/V/PD legal basis. Ordinary courts hear
scope, license, conflict and correction disputes directly; no merged customer
health/political/financial dossier or home/device search by analogy.

**NB04 — General rules do not arise from a model release.** NB's bases, floors,
priorities, public guarantees and customer rights require primary law. Within
express bounded authority, general valuation/operating schedules use national
DR consultation/scrutiny and exact public versions; individual officers apply
those criteria with reasons. A new coefficient, source, fallback or learned
behavior is a changed criterion even at the same current output. Stability
council MC13–16 retains only enumerated M14 tools; NB does not create broad
nonbank leverage bans, bailouts or a central AI portfolio mandate. Actual public
adoption, original evidence and policy authority remain distinct.
