# Protected stages: an enabling programme is not a whole country

14 October 2026 · Integrated design 60 · Authored reference, not a funded launch

The [C14 candidate T01–T12](../../country/C14/06-staged-capacity-and-protection.md)
turns the national transition requirement into duties for elected decision,
continuing rights, finance, paid workers, safe acceptance, failure and handover.
It selects protected stages, not the numerical projects below.

| Part | Bounded supporting question |

| [Schedule and obligations](01-schedule-and-obligations.md) | What must be paid before usable capacity and who is displaced? |
| [Failure cases and alternatives](02-failure-cases-and-alternatives.md) | Does cash hide missing FX, salaries, delay or post-horizon obligations? |

[checks](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-staged-transition/validation.json) preserve the exact
scope. There is no actual funding agreement, jurisdiction, qualified roster,
engineering design, supplier, safe receipt, independent public participation
or observed outcome. The [prior national cases](../national-economy/README.md)
remain held; a project envelope cannot erase their income/private-finance gaps.

The next national design task is the enforceable ordinary-law core and its
links to direct, reachable remedy. Actual whole-country finance and public
adoption remain necessary before operating claims, not reasons to keep
producing ever more optimistic growth scenarios.
