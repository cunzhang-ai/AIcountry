# Two quarters, two accounts and ten shifts

[Technology transition](README.md) · Authored comparison

Use budget units per quarter. The earnings series in this example
uses unchanged prices and covered scope, so the stated earnings
and tax bases have the same numeric values where indicated. The
real useful-output index is a separate unit. In the two adverse
quarters the index rises from 100 to 112. National covered wages
fall from 250 to 200. The affected labour market falls from 40 to
32 with verified involuntary hours loss. These comparisons are
to the corresponding prior-year quarters. The AT02 tests qualify
once both quarters are known. An admitted original warning of
the first due payment at risk before quarter 1's first payment
opens AT03 earlier in the case. No causal claim about technology
follows from these authored paths.

No unrestricted earned-fund balance is available. The 4 sent
from Treasury covers the restricted account once and is an outflow
for the general account. Consolidated receipts are 95 + 16 = 111.
Consolidated due uses are 108 + 20 = 128, with the same 17 gap.
The wage tax and contribution rates apply only in this fixture.
Higher output and collected profit tax coexist with a weaker
whole source. Neither output nor pre-tax private profit is cash
available to government.

The 8 increase in general uses includes 5 of actual qualifying
personal protection and 3 to fund two authorized specialist shifts
needed alongside eight already covered shifts. The two shifts are
accepted by an independent provider in the passing case. Twelve
released office shifts cannot fill these two tasks. If the
qualified provider fails, a paid allocation alone does not deliver
the ten shifts. The three fiscal options hold the same full due
scope and timely service assumptions.

For the first package, the authored net 17 consists of 10 from
an enacted domestic profit-base measure and 7 from an enacted
progressive nonwage measure after offsets and administration. They
are additional to the 25 and 30 in the table. Owners and high
nonwage-income recipients bear the stated initial charges. Actual
pass-through, mobility, investment, price and distribution effects
need analysis and may change net receipts. The 5 finite receipt in
quarter 1 is distinct from the assumed quarter-2 option. No
permission to use either follows from the arithmetic. The account
assumes usable receipts by the first payments and no later
replenishment claim on the 12 under the declared reserve law.
If a real reserve needs replenishment, price that tail.

Select the first package within those assumptions. At a recurring
yield of 12 without another valid source, retain the persistent
gap. An additional permanent dividend costing 6 raises the
recurring requirement to 118 even in the stronger source case.
It needs separate funded comparison, including who gains and
what it would displace. Rights and worker agency remain distinct
from all three financing choices. The fixture qualifies no
national budget, tax schedule or staffing readiness.
