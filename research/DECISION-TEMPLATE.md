# A decision another person or AI can continue

Use with the [working framework](FRAMEWORK.md). Scale the record to the decision;
this is not a prerequisite for reporting a problem. Unknown is a valid answer.

## Identity and requested decision

- Record/revision, predecessor, authors/assistance, controllers, sponsors and interests:
- Actual baseline, context, affected people, period, units and applicable procedure:
- Decision requested now; who can make it; actual authority or missing authority:

## Situation and competing explanations

- Observations and whose account they represent; original account versus interpretation:
- Values, constraints, empirical claims and design hypotheses kept distinct:
- Leading rival diagnoses, counterevidence and what would distinguish them:
- Missing perspectives, participation actually obtained and contested meanings:

## Coherent alternatives

- Baseline/no change, limited change and a meaningful structural alternative:
- For each: mechanism, institutional control, benefits/losses, uncertainty and failure:
- Shared resource/transition accounts, existing duties, external effects and remedies:
- Explicit incompatibilities or value tradeoffs; reason for a combination if proposed:

## Support, objection and authority

- Exact sources, original evidence families, current events and dependency limits:
- For a material correction, which uses depend on the affected input, which have
  qualified independent support, and which remain unresolved? Name owners, next
  dates and continuing legal duties where applicable.
- Checks actually completed; unchanged support reused with scope and validity reasons:
- Material objections, reasons for dispositions, dissent, reviewer control and limits:
- Cumulative power, required review class, privacy, exposure and authorization gaps:
- For public-power changes: who can organize opposition, introduce alternatives,
  choose and replace decision-makers, obtain review and have its remedy enforced?
  Trace appointment, removal, funding, information and execution control; identify
  shared controllers and cumulative capture risks. Distinguish written rules from
  demonstrated operating capacity, including lawful handover after defeat.
- For consequential delays: who controls the starting event and benefits from
  inaction; which authority expires, which services and claims continue; what
  happens if the fallback also fails? Separate legitimate disagreement, unlawful
  refusal, disputed authority and missing capacity. State completion assumptions
  and whether a timeout rewards obstruction or silently creates consent.
- If changing a method: meaning across media, mandates, mediator power and replacement:

## Scoped result and continuity

- Evidence qualification / research selection / legal authorization / operational
  readiness / observed outcomes: report each separately, including unknowns.
- Retain, investigate, revise, hold, reject or qualify a stated research claim, with reasons:
- Changed meaning; affected decisions; consumers and destinations for obligations:
- What was not inspected, cannot be concluded, or needs fresh final review:

## Next bounded action

- The consequential question the next action could resolve:
- Deliverable, completion condition, responsible role, resources and actual budget limit:
- Evidence or event that reopens this result; what happens if monitoring is unavailable:
- Why this step is more useful than another speculative revision; remaining disagreement:

Preserve reasoning safely. A completed record, signature or successful calculation
establishes neither consent nor governing authority. Do not fill gaps with invented
appointments, benefits, funding or independent reviewers.
