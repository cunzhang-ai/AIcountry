# H11–H15: cost rent, individual affordability and actual payment

*Part of [C09/12](../12-secure-housing-and-repair.md); design91 proposed package.*

**H11 — Show the whole price.** Each cost-rental cohort has audited acquisition
and title, development, management, maintenance, utilities, tax, vacancy/loss,
finance, prudent contingency and renewal accounts. Rent follows a published
long-term cost schedule, net of actual grants/receipts, with no speculative
land-price reset on tenant turnover. New public/community stock has no private
distribution of windfall appreciation. Lawful contracted finance has its stated
price and risk; public land and equity are not costless simply because no
market rent is charged. Explain location/size/access allocation of common costs.
Keep operating surpluses for stated duties or an explicit accountable rent
adjustment; do not raid renewal money for a general fiscal shortfall.

**H12 — Capital cost and capital cash are different.** Economic cost includes
consumption of the asset, not loan principal as a second consumption expense.
The cash schedule separately pays acquisition, debt principal/interest, actual
replacement and deposits/withdrawals of a renewal reserve. Borrowing is a
liability and a grant has an appropriated source. Principal repayment and saving
for future replacement can both require cash, but are not both depreciation.
A reserve balance is not a second dwelling, GDP or an already made replacement.
Do not also charge the same renewal invoice against a retained reserve and
current cash as two independent uses. Name opening stocks, all future finance
terms and the payer of any mismatch before offering a rent as sustainable.
Debt outliving accepted remaining asset service needs a funded accepted renewal
and lender/security plan; one year's positive rent cover does not supply it.

**H13 — Rent is not an affordability finding.** I03 covers the actual necessary
housing share once across actual occupants; IC personal cash and Y rights remain
distinct. An audited cost rent may still be unaffordable. Explicit assistance
or capital subsidy, with a real source, bridges that difference; an assumed
rent discount does not. A rise in income may change lawful personal assistance,
not terminate the secure lease or force private money pooling. For H cost rental, rent review is
at most annual with90 days' notice, the audited calculation and independent
challenge before an unlawful increase is enforced. No automatic market reset.
If lawful costs rise beyond affordable receipts, the elected owner must choose
funded subsidy, finance/operating revision or a lawful supported transition;
an executive cannot silently cut repair, seize deposits or reset old rights.

**H14 — Correct arrears before possession.** Reconcile actual bills, credited
receipts/deposits, defects, lawful abatements, assistance due and each person's
usable resources. Offer a workable repayment plan after protected needs and
correct delayed public payments; inability is not fraud or a criminal offense.
Advances to a lawful landlord count as the same housing receipt for the
specified bill, with a separate public recovery claim. Expected recovery is no
cash source. Court-ordered escrow/setoff uses actual matched amounts and records;
do not pay the landlord twice or impute a disputed transfer as tenant income.

A court can ultimately order possession for unresolved arrears after these
steps and funded receiving/support efforts, subject to proportionality and
appeal. Neither a tenant nor a private owner must become the state's indefinite
unpaid shelter source. During an actual funding/stock failure, use bounded
judicial interim protection and the prior elected scarcity route, name both
the person's unmet housing claim and the owner's uncompensated loss, and retain
debt/remedy claims. Missing capacity does not fabricate payment or possession.

**H15 — Landlord failure does not erase a home or a creditor.** New public
funding/security contracts preserve the H06 rental-use obligation, lawful
tenancies and operational step-in terms before execution. No new lender may
price an unrestricted vacant sale and later call the restriction unexpected.
Older secured priority is not retrospectively subordinated by this research.
Before any new full-coverage lease over an older mortgage, obtain binding
lender/non-disturbance terms or an expressly lawful funded priority migration.
Do not market/authorize unsupported full coverage. A breach does not void the
occupant's tenancy or nonwaivable rights: enforce against the grantor, correct
representations and fund actual receiving/remedy; neither disclosure nor tenant
assent reprioritizes the old lender or waives mandatory protection.
The competent court under applicable public-debt or U insolvency law maps
title, creditor/deposit claims, repairs and
pending orders; the public steward funds an actually accepting operator or
suitable alternative under existing authority. Worker pay, debt, tenant rights
and public subsidy remain separate claims. Disclose a failed handover and keep
direct relief open; a receiver's appointment is not delivered housing.

[NF17–NF24](../../C06/42-recurring-tax-and-noncash-work.md) now specifies the
site-value levy, actual public-use exclusion and local/national receipt/grant
rules. H11 uses the actual lawful bill or explicit paid subsidy, not a blanket
cost-rental exemption, guessed zero or twice-counted grant. NF20 site valuation
excludes structures/repair and respects existing tenancy/use restrictions.
H13 affordability/notice and H15 prior security/receiving duties remain; a new
levy cannot silently become a tenant pass-through or maintenance cut.

Design108's [payer comparison](../../../settlement/country-delivery/05-production-title-and-receipts.md)
corrects a fixture in which owner bills included utilities while tenant rents
did not. Separate essential utility costs cannot disappear behind equal nominal
rent. Restricted renewal, tax, actual repair and loan principal are different
flows; a funding contribution is not an already made repair or a second GDP use.
Opening public/private title and lender claims need actual paid history or new
accepted finance. Public-site NF18 exclusion/owner NF21 deferral require their
own facts; none is granted simply by this model or housing-provider label.

[PT05/PT17/PT23](../../C08/26-property-transfers-and-secured-credit.md) supplies
ordinary recorded land/mortgage priority and court realization. Prospective
later lending takes existing valid H tenure; the H15 old-mortgage/source-law
non-disturbance migration still applies. Neither a movable security filing nor
auction can promise vacant title by erasing a protected tenancy or another
owner's share. Actual receiving/payment remains a separate duty.

[CB13–CB18](../14-shared-buildings-and-neighbor-rights.md) joins actual common-
property operation/repair/renewal contributions and restricted reserves to the
same cost/cash/receiving account. A landlord/public owner owes its actual share,
without an automatic tenant capital pass-through, levy lien or old-lender
reprioritization. I03 qualifying need support is the existing source rule;
funded invoices/reserve deposits, realized repairs and hoped recoveries differ.

[RT01–RT20 private rent](../18-private-rents-tenure-and-housing-entry.md)
separately selects genuine initial market agreement, CPI-bounded continuing
base, signed deflation, limited court-authorized exceptions and actual remedy.
H11–H13's audited public/community cost schedule is not a private acquisition/
loan pass-through; I03 and actual due rights apply in their actual scope.
