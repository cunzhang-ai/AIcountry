# Belonging: accountable admission and independent status

*Canonical MB11–MB12; part of [C02/06](../06-citizenship-and-secure-belonging.md).*

**MB11 — Entry rules do not decide every right.** Citizens and secure residents
have return rights; protected entry/claims and actual non-return duties have
individual routes, not an ordinary admission quota. A minor dependent child,
a person's genuine voluntary lawful partner, or a necessary dependent carer/
care-recipient of a citizen/secure resident has a family-admission entitlement
on proved relationship/dependence, individual safety and actual child interests.
Recognize lawful plural relationships under B without a two-person ceiling or
compulsory genetics. No minimum income, citizenship purchase or sponsor-control
condition applies; support/receiving duties remain separately funded. A claimed
family link requires fair proof; an immigration officer does not invent parentage.

The Assembly sets a prospective annual envelope and published categories for
additional ordinary work, learning and visitor admissions, with actual reception,
service/teacher/clinical/housing capacity, labor rights, equality, fiscal/physical
limits and effects abroad exposed. It must publish competing lower/higher-entry
and capacity-investment options and reasons; a minister or AI cannot change the
envelope or conceal a nationality-based exclusion through ranking. Within work
places, verified lawful work or a self-directed actual lawful activity qualifies on evidenced resources and
sector permissions, with reasoned review rather than an AI earnings forecast; learning
places require a genuine accepted course and funded receiving. Visitors have a
stated purpose, end date and fair extension route. No wealth purchase category.

If genuinely equivalent eligible work/learning claims exceed the enacted places,
use a public auditable draw with protected personal data, not employer bidding,
AI social worth or a discretionary loyalty list. Unused places are not owned by
a recruiter. No new annual law means no new discretionary places beyond an
existing still-valid schedule's unused authority; return/family/protection and
existing permissions continue. This restrictive default can obstruct useful
migration and is an explicit cost, not a solution to missing capacity. No actual
number is invented: before operation the elected schedule selects within the
published paid/physical capacity account and protected duties, with challenge.

**MB12 — Leave a sponsor without losing personhood.** Ordinary work permission
is portable across lawful work/activity categories and employers; no former
employer's consent, passport retention or private immigration veto. A work or
family-status holder who leaves a job/relationship gets 180 days of independent
stay/work to establish another lawful route, without first proving abuse.
A timely application/review preserves that bridge until effective determination.
Admission planning accounts for possible in-country conversions and their actual
duration/service costs. A lawful in-country category conversion or portable job change consumes no new
entry-quota place; applicable category conditions still receive review. Absence of a new
admission envelope cannot silently cancel an already admitted person's bridge.
Already vested secure residence needs no bridge and cannot be cancelled by exit.

Provide individual payment access, safe housing/transport acceptance, advice and
private communication where needed. The state may inspect actual work, contracts,
recruiter fees and accommodation under labor law; the bridge does not promise a
job, seize the former employer's assets or prove anyone's guilt. Existing wages,
child support, tenancy, civil and protection claims retain their own remedies.
Decision-makers cannot convert protection reporting into an employer-controlled
status alarm or require agreement with an AI's account of a private relationship.

[RT26–RT31](../../C09/18/06-employment-linked-housing.md) separates
housing tenure and safe transition from employer control. MB12 keeps
its own work and residence decisions and original clocks.
