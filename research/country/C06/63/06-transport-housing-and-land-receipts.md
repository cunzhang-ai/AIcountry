# IV23–IV27. Keep public investment usable as land and rents change

*Design316 · Proposed transport, housing and investment rules.*

**IV23 — Follow the people who are meant to gain.** Before a material
transport or station-area commitment, compare existing housing, rents,
tenure, accessible journeys and service receipt for affected people.
Use the earliest credible and lawfully documented project baseline
and show any anticipatory changes. Track the original population
and later occupants separately, with privacy-preserving samples
and voluntary evidence. Include people priced out, moved for works,
unable to travel and previously excluded. Compare actual housing
and journey cost, travel time and access to work, care and learning
for households at both ends of a move. A changed average can reflect
replacement of residents or destinations. Disclose attribution
limits, missing coverage and unserved needs.

**IV24 — Pay the housing and access obligations before relying on the
project.** Compare repair and a smaller network change, phased development
on existing public or community land, and a larger route with a funded
housing package. Protect prior tenancies, lawful acquisition, safe return
and direct remedy. Where project-induced exposure is material, choose
actual, funded measures within ordinary housing and land law. These may
include preservation of secure tenancies, permanently affordable homes
on lawfully held land, interim transport or direct relocation and return
support. Give each promised protection an exact scope, duty holder,
appropriation, receiving operator and useful date. Carry transition
and maintenance together. Stage only a separable lawful part if a
material protection is unready. Existing rights and obligations continue.

**IV25 — Treat land income and tax as distinct sources.** Disclose public
title, actual sale or lease terms, payment windows, cost, risk, existing
encumbrances and permitted uses. Compare retaining land on affordable
terms with selling it. Show the recurring public value and commitments
each path gives up. Estimate any land-value change against a credible
no-project comparison, keeping uncertainty and other causes visible.
Estimates, revaluation and expected sale proceeds do not fund a due
payment. Count actual collected receipts and authorized borrowing
separately, with lender, gross debt, repayment source and loss owner.
LU attributable connection charges keep their existing limits. Any
broader tax or development levy requires its own elected law, published
incidence, objective terms and independent challenge. It cannot be
negotiated as a condition for an otherwise lawful permit.

**IV26 — Keep renewal, revenues and affordable access on one schedule.**
The elected bodies approve exact transport, housing and finance decisions
within their lawful scopes, including weak property receipts, cost
overrun and a delayed opening. Reconcile contributions, restricted
revenues and asset income once. Protect the original housing and
service promises before using surplus for optional expansion.
When only later receipts are expected, fund any bridge and its failure
exposure under actual debt and appropriation law, or choose a feasible
smaller stage. Keep recurring operations on an identified recurring
source or a lawfully funded finite contract with real exit. A public
company, grant announcement or AI forecast cannot create an implicit
guarantee or bind another elected body.

**IV27 — Report stable access and the losses separately.** Verify actual
occupied affordable homes, their continuing price and tenure, useful
journeys and actual service receipt. Publish results for affected
original residents, new entrants and people who left, with known
coverage and the lawful action for remaining harms. Give affected
people, local bodies and opposition usable access to evidence and
direct challenge. AI can compare costs, routes and valuation ranges.
Competent authorities decide commitments and independent reviewers
verify sources and actual receipt. Reopen when net affordable
capacity, source, travel or funded protection fails, or a more
effective retained option becomes available. Preserve original
contracts, dates and remedies through a changed project.
