# KR01–KR04: scope, authors and creative livelihoods

*Design 95 research candidate; not enacted law, a funded offer or demonstrated
superiority.*

**KR01 — Legislative and territorial boundary.** The elected Assembly alone sets
national intangible-right categories, eligibility, terms, exceptions, claims and
remedies; local bodies may fund creation and set their own lawful procurement terms but
cannot invent exclusive rights. A domestic grant controls only acts within its lawful
territorial reach. Registration abroad, a server address, a platform term or an AI
attribution does not itself create domestic title or foreign enforcement. Apply
[C08/17](../../C08/17-jurisdiction-and-legal-cooperation.md) to forum, applicable law
and recognition; honor actual treaty and old-law duties after source reconciliation. For each
exclusive IP right, validity, scope, term, exceptions and infringement follow
the law of the territory for which protection is claimed; segment multi-territory
acts rather than choosing one worldwide IP law from a server or claimant address.
B41 forum connection, proof of foreign right/law and executable reach remain
separate; B43 governs contractual royalties and B43/B44 govern connected
confidentiality/unfair-competition claims.

**KR02 — Copyright and software.** Human original expression, including source/object
code, photographs, film and original selection or arrangement of data, is protected
automatically; facts, procedures, interface facts, ideas and underlying data are not
privately owned merely by compilation. The human author initially owns economic rights
and attribution/integrity rights; a clear assignment or employment rule may transfer
economic rights, with statutory protection against coercive blanket waivers. Rights
cover reproduction, translation/adaptation, public communication/distribution and
applicable rental. For every identified natural author, including a film or photograph
author, economic term ends **50 years after death** (last surviving author for joint
work). An anonymous/pseudonymous human work whose author stays unidentifiable, or a work
whose valid term is otherwise not measured by a natural life, ends **50 years from
authorized publication, or from creation if no authorized publication within 50 years**,
computed from the following calendar year; identification within that term shifts to
life-plus-50. Attribution and protection against reputation-harming distortion last
through the corresponding economic term, without a post-term veto over criticism. No
registration or mark is a condition of protection; actual source/treaty rules may
require a longer term or different treatment at commencement. Official statutes, binding
general rules and authoritative judgments have no exclusive domestic copyright in their
legally operative text; privacy/redaction and third-party material remain separate.
Incorporating a private standard must secure lawful freely usable access to its
operative requirements, not declare every underlying private work confiscated.

**KR03 — Make creator contracts inspectable.** A commission or employment agreement
names the human/organizational right holders, uses, territory, duration, attribution,
payment due dates, audit access, onward transfer and lawful exit; it distinguishes
original work from licensed background material. Creators and performers may organize,
bargain and use lawful collective management without a sponsor forcing one collecting
society. A court can correct abusive withholding, unpaid royalties, false attribution or
unlawful appropriation under prospective enacted law. A public arts grant pays specified
creation and access work; it does not silently purchase every future use, the person's
likeness or their unrelated work. Private distribution may continue within the agreed
retained rights. Initially the human author/inventor holds the relevant right; only a
clear lawful scoped prospective work/assignment contract transfers economic rights.
Commission, employment, publication, an AI subscription or a grant alone is no unbounded
assignment of unrelated future work. Where a promised new use was not purchased and no
valid exception applies, obtain permission and price it.

**KR04 — Related rights.** Performers may control unauthorized recording and qualifying
communication of live performance and copying of recorded performance; phonogram
producers control reproduction; broadcasters control protected fixation/rebroadcast
acts, all without displacing authors' rights. Candidate term: **50 years from the end of
the performance/fixation year** for performers/phonogram producers and **20 years from
the end of broadcast year** for broadcasters. Contract terms must name each layer; one
platform license does not silently erase a performer or author claim. Limited statutory
uses and remunerated licenses are separately specified, not inferred from a user's
subscription.
