# C05. Production, ownership, work and the gains from automation: Work, bargaining, time and care leave

*Part of [C05. Production, ownership, work and the gains from automation](../C05-economy-and-work.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

Work policy combines safety, worker organization, portable protection and support for
changing jobs or learning. Unpaid care belongs in service and time budgets. Automation
should expand people's capabilities and discretionary time, with gains shared through
wages and bargaining, taxation, services and returns on actual public assets. Neither
permanent full employment nor a post-work abundance economy is assumed.
Usable time also allows voluntary friendship, cultural practice, association
and quiet life; a nominally open public place cannot serve people whose
schedule, care duties or travel make it unreachable. This connection informs
local hours and transport planning without making private social activity a
condition of leave or income protection.

Workers receive enforceable information, consultation and independent bargaining
over pay, conditions, safety and consequential automation or closure, with a route
to negotiate paid learning and redeployment. Employers retain lawful investment
decisions and fair process; a worker's voice is not selected by management, a
public owner or AI. Small, temporary and care-constrained workers need practical
representation and interim relief against retaliation. Training requires time,
qualified teachers, replacement care and funds; a new credential is not evidence
that a displaced worker obtained a viable job. Bankruptcy, a public-enterprise
change or provider failure carries accrued wages, pensions, contracts and service
claims to lawful successors rather than silently charging the social floor.
The [ecological transition route](../C09/08-climate-and-ecological-transition.md)
also treats a plant closure or new green industry as a work and local-revenue
change: bargaining, accrued claims, paid learning time and a viable next
livelihood are distinct from enrolling people in a training course.

The selected [time-and-care settlement](../../2026-10-democracy-and-time/time-and-care.md)
adds an enforceable national floor for bounded work hours, recurring rest,
advance notice of material schedule changes, limits on compulsory availability,
paid annual and medical leave, and protection from retaliation for using them.
Independent bargaining and sector agreements can improve the floor and arrange
different safe patterns; essential shift coverage needs planned qualified
replacements and recovery time, not serial emergency exceptions. [TW01–TW20](10-work-rest-and-paid-capability.md) now selects32 normal paid hours
after a36-hour stage, six annual-leave and two public/flexible working weeks,
paid breaks, distinct daily/weekly rest, protected learning and a median-linked
hourly cash floor. These are normative research choices, not inferred from an
AI denominator. The September numeric draft remains historical and actual
software/canonical law unchanged until valid revision. No safe national roster
or affordable full operating bundle is established.

[CL01–CL24](17-care-leave-and-return.md) now selects the portable care-leave
promise formerly deferred here: actual child-arrival/temporary chosen-person care,
protected loss handoff, job-protected time/return and public capped replacement.
The existing social-protection authority pays its defined component from explicit
G source law; the former unspecified contribution scheme is not that source.
Actual stronger salary/top-ups and job duties retain their employers. Self-employed
people have evidenced labor-loss income, not fictional reinstatement, and people
outside paid work retain I05/care/credit/agency rather than invented lost wages.

Medical recovery, voluntary chosen care, public services, employment/time rights,
replacement cash and Y06 credits remain distinct; no forced family substitute or
presumed nursery saving. Qualified paid cover, public/private displaced work,
actual source, tax/I05/EB/Y and legacy transition join the whole country once.
Independent useful-time remedies and actual return are necessary; neither leave
on paper nor its public payer establishes funded operation or better lives.

[AS09–AS16](../C07/18-person-directed-assistance.md) now applies TW to assistance
work, separates intimate-contact refusal from employment termination, selects
funded fair28-day notice with stronger rights retained, and defines scoped public
backing for covered ordinary dues. It does not insure all business/tort losses or
complete ordinary labor law; classification/contracts/dismissal/collective power
need a joined national settlement beyond TW and MP's bargaining boundary.

[WL01–WL40](12-work-contracts-and-collective-power.md) now supplies the ordinary
employment-contract/exit/collective-action settlement formerly missing here.
It preserves TW and higher AS protections, and adds limited general wage advances
with explicit new source exposure. The public/worker choice is not a guarantee
of every job or proof that the complete package is funded or improves lives.
