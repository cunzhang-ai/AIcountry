# WS11–WS15. Supply a service and explain who pays

*Part of [water allocation and service](../16-water-allocation-and-service.md).*

**WS11 — An accepting operator, not an account gate.** Local Director is the
public receiving point for ordinary safe domestic water, including unconnected
homes and failed licensed suppliers. Protected first receipt starts AP/earlier E
and proportionate proof/help: decide a feasible connection/repair or actual safe
interim delivery. Deposit, citizenship, landlord arrears or AI identity is no
gate to essential water. A connection confers no title, unsafe-occupation licence
or unlimited right to remote development at others' expense. Published lawful
conditions price actual necessary works and less-burden alternatives with I03/
needs/housing protection; no unpayable demand before considering essential
receiving. No fictional universal installation deadline; existing useful-time
interim-water duties continue even while construction remains unresolved.

**WS12 — Attributable allowed revenue and actual cash.** Each public service
publishes an independently reviewed annual/lifecycle revenue requirement:
qualified staff, source/treatment, power, parts, safety sampling, actual outside
review, maintenance, accessible billing/help, lawful financing, planned renewal
and accepted reliability/alternate supply. Identify asset lives, losses and cash
dates. The all-customer tariff requirement deducts actual compatible public
payments/grants and provided resources once. Farm/industry/public-user receipts
meet their allocated shares; subtract them only when deriving the remaining
household requirement, never both from the all-customer total and again from
that remainder. Assessed bills, claims on late grants and actual due cash remain
distinct. Restricted money is not generic tariff relief; an unreceived grant
is not safe cash. Public ownership does
not make staff, depreciation, inherited pipes or owned foreign inputs free.

Separate economic depreciation/capital cost from cash formation, interest,
principal and renewal provisions. Full renewal reserve alongside debt repayment
is a distinct disclosed cash policy, not twice an asset's production cost.
Never charge old R05/public power/sewage lines or the same engineer twice.
Actual legacy private concession returns/compensation are separate contracts,
not a return guarantee for every requested new public asset. No automatic G
dividend or monopoly profit; actual compatible residual remains in voted
service/renewal or is returned through lawful tariff choice.

**WS13 — Elected tariffs, explicit shared costs.** Council adopts ordinary local
public-service tariffs under LR after outside Reviewer findings, accessible
user/worker/unconnected-person representations and reasons. Assembly chooses
national/source charges, equalization and material cross-local burdens. Neither
Director, Reviewer nor software alone sets a new tax/tariff/common priority.
Use a disclosed two-part schedule: attributable readiness/capacity cost and
actual metered delivered-use/treatment cost, with proportionate nonmetered proof
when metering is unavailable/inappropriate. An estimate is not proved actual
use; show units, reading dates, correction and safe dispute route.

Attribute domestic, farm, industrial and public-service costs to actual caused
capacity, source/conveyance, quality/return duties, service and received inputs;
explain shared allocation/uncertainty. Do not recover optional marginal farm/
industrial expansion through household essential charges by default. Any chosen
cross-subsidy/common resilience investment needs elected purpose, beneficiaries,
actual source, amount/method and review in the budget, not a hidden pooled-litre
price. General ecology/public-health/equalization needs explicit compatible G/
local backing. No national uniform price, scarcity auction, uncosted free block
or assumed bill/revenue/FX yield. Lawful licensed tariffs retain actual contract/
statute until prospective change; a worksheet creates no new charge.

**WS14 — Affordability must reach the user and operator.** Preserve C07's
existing inability-to-pay protection, I02 actual basket, I03 necessary housing
and additional need/source law. Fund person-level help and express service/
equalization grants, reconciled once: cash and tariff/in-kind credit cannot each
fund the same bill. Operator receives actual authorized support, with earlier
provisional/interim receiving if assessment fails. Treasury promise/benefit label/
internal offset is neither delivered water nor general tax cash.

Essential quantity/quality/physical access follows lawful source-specific health,
domestic, household-size, disability/care/climate and sanitation needs, not a
universal litre cap or per-meter small-family norm. Extra need is not presumed
waste. No debt-collection cutoff/flow limiter may deprive any occupant of
essential safe water. Actual acute contamination/engineering isolation retains
TD07 safe alternate duties. Leak disputes require actual attribution/repair,
not charges for fictitious consumption or removal of protected supply.

**WS15 — Debt, restrictions and billing disputes differ.** Usable bills include
correction/help, actual notice/reasons and direct AP/V interim challenge. Collect
only lawful debtor/period/amount through ordinary civil remedies, preserving
I08/E; no inherited occupier debt, landlord cutoff, collective punishment,
new lien or private bank seizure. Prepayment of the contested amount cannot gate
review. Restricting nonessential use needs express prior lawful conditions,
proved facts, necessary scope, accommodation and actual preserved essential
access; WS grants no new general cutoff power. Seasonal WS09 allocation, health
isolation and debt collection cannot evade each other's process by relabelling.
Trace reconnection/correction/alternative to an accepting paid person, not a ticket.

[LU06–LU10 development costs](../17/02-capacity-conditions-and-costs.md)
credits actual compatible SAME-cost connection/resources once, with separate
source/period/payer/custody. Planning charges do not buy water allocation,
change these WS customer/operating duties or commission unbuilt infrastructure.

[WS26–WS32](06-small-water-supplies-and-safe-receipt.md) applies the
residual water duty to users of small off-network supplies, with
funded alternate receipt through independent safety review.
