# HN01–HN04: keep responsibility through the whole clinical path

*Part of [C07/19](../19-clinical-networks-and-specialist-access.md); design 123 candidate.*

**HN01 — Organize by useful clinical reach, not sovereign self-sufficiency.**
The existing nationally financed clinical networks maintain accessible first
contact, ordinary continuing care, qualified urgent response/stabilization and
safe ongoing support under S. Compare local, visiting/shared specialist and
external provision against actual population, geography, skills, safe volumes,
transport and contingency. No fixed population-to-doctor ratio proves quality;
no local hospital label promises every rare specialty. Public, cooperative,
nonprofit/private and cross-border arrangements retain their actual law and
clinical duties. Do not close a needed local function solely to book a saving.

**HN02 — Name the responsible clinical team and receiving service.** Following
assessment, identify the indicated next step, appropriate destination, clinically
safe time, patient choices, who maintains care while waiting and who receives
results/aftercare. Match a referral to the actual receiving team's acceptance,
capacity and booked resources, not a generic institution directory. The referring
network keeps its defined interim responsibility; clinical handover states when
and what the receiving provider assumes. S/E's original request/useful time and
consent protections continue; no new referral-stage clock resets them.

**HN03 — Buy specified capacity without buying sovereign discretion.** The
health purchaser can prepare lawful domestic/shared/foreign contracts for named
clinical scope, qualified staff, medicines/devices, reserved versus per-case
capacity, payment/currency, aftercare, audit/privacy and a workable dispute/
failure route. Actual acceptance, foreign permissions and exportable records
are necessary; a model or treaty aspiration supplies none. Avoid double-booking
the same specialist/bed across countries' standby plans. Visiting practice,
training and source-country replacement require paid legitimate arrangements,
not assumed free imported labor or remote supervision of an unavailable team.

**HN04 — Referral failure remains a public care failure.** Maintain lawful
interim care, relief, clinical reassessment, alternate receiving and direct
S/E/clinical/court remedies on refusal, delay, border closure or provider failure.
The State's existing residual responsibility is not discharged by funding approval
or a contractor's insolvency. No official guarantees a nonexistent treatment,
forces travel, removes consent or rewrites general scarcity criteria. A patient
may decline an unsuitable journey without losing standing access/continuity;
this does not entitle anyone to every requested destination or unproven treatment.
Record the unmet need and safe response without falsely marking care received.

[SC21–SC32](../../C04/05/06-need-and-upstream-capability.md) now requires
critical medicine/device/input paths, qualified usable buffers and paid
maintenance/fallback; a treatment reservation and a stock record are not
actual clinical receiving. Joint foreign supply requires accepted source,
title, release, exit and continuing care, not assumed pool membership.

[FT01–FT08](../../C04/11-public-service-transfer-and-continuity.md) governs a legal transfer of the service itself. HN continues to control clinical referral, consent, interim receiving and useful time.
