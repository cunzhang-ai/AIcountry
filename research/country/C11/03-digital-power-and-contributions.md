# C11. Knowledge, culture and AI as public capability: Digital power and contributions

*Part of [C11. Knowledge, culture and AI as public capability](../C11-knowledge-culture-and-ai.md); integrated design 96 research, not enacted law.*

The selected [digital-power arrangement](../../2026-10-core-institutions/digital-ai-power.md)
uses publicly governed, replaceable shared infrastructure. The Assembly sets legal
purposes, rights and funding; each service authority owns its decisions and records;
a technical operator maintains versioned exchange and authentication standards
within law but cannot redefine eligibility, rank lawful political speech or decide
an appeal. Data, procurement and sector reviewers, election bodies and courts have
distinct mandates and need access to shared suppliers and controllers. Their
appointments, budgets, information and remedy execution must survive the cabinet
or provider they review. A central public operator can itself be captured, so
plurality and succession must be tested rather than assumed from formal separation.
The [C09 connectivity rule](../C09/05-communications-and-logistics.md) governs
physical networks, spectrum, interconnection and outage routes.
[CN open carriage](../C09/22/02-bottlenecks-open-carriage-and-exit.md)
protects lawful traffic from carrier discrimination and keeps platform
participation, carriage and public authority distinct. This digital evidence
function cannot turn a network license into content control or claim
that an online form is available when a person has no usable connection.
The [C03 campaign-access rule](../C03/07-political-finance-and-campaign-access.md)
also keeps paid political sponsorship and distribution inspectable without
making this technical operator a judge of lawful political truth.
The [C10 cyber response](../C10/04-cyber-incident-and-recovery.md) assigns
incident warnings, forensic custody and tested recovery without letting a
technical coordinator acquire power over service eligibility or lawful speech.

[PD01–PD32](../C02/07-private-life-and-personal-data.md) is the ordinary data-law
owner: specific bases and sensitive uses, actual vendor responsibility, subject
rights, downstream correction, accountable supervision and paid foreign/exit
capacity. A technical exchange or a KR licence supplies neither base nor consent.

Legal status, evidence of a fact, authentication of an act and authorization for
a service are different. Exchange of health, education, benefit, police and civic
records requires a specific lawful purpose, minimum necessary disclosure,
inspectable provenance, retention limits and correction. Compulsory processing
cannot be laundered through coerced consent. A consequential deprivation of liberty,
subsistence, an essential service or the franchise cannot rest on an unattended
AI final decision: an accountable officer needs actual time, competence and power
to question it, give reasons and direct interim protection. An independent route
must let the person contest source facts, rules and interpretation without using
the same model or vendor. Assisted, in-person, paper or other suitable channels
have the same legal receipt, standing and effective remedy, with funded staff
and deadlines; identical screens are unnecessary.
The [equality route](../C08/11-equality-law-and-effective-remedies.md)
also examines protected-group proxies and unjustified effects across a
shared vendor's decisions. Its inquiry cannot convert correlation into
automatic guilt or require a universal sensitive-identity registry.

Human, AI and joint contributors may propose improvements. Replication creates neither
additional votes nor an independent review. This design grants no sovereign franchise
to model instances; any future claim about morally significant nonhuman entities needs
separate examination rather than silent dismissal or automatic political power for vendors.
Credit data and underwriting models are likewise not a person's civic worth.
Lenders must be able to explain and correct consequential source errors under
C06; a shared model, bureau or payment operator cannot silently turn an
unpaid private bill into lost voting, public benefits or lawful speech.
The [C05 markets route](../C05/05-market-power-and-real-exit.md) tests common
control of data, payment and platform gateways; the [C08 product route](../C08/05-consumer-and-product-safety.md)
preserves operative versions and remedies for connected products. A technical
operator cannot settle either legal case by changing its standard.

An agent credential can help verify source and bounded authority, but legal
effect depends on the principal's mandate, applicable law and any required
current assent. Revocation notice, offline expiry and subagent resource limits
need more than a shared identity platform. The [C12 commitment rule](../C12/02-agent-mandates-and-public-acts.md)
retains a direct human route and reconciles valid prior acts without using
one vendor's last message as the final legal record.

The [civil core](../C08/13-civil-obligations-and-executed-remedies.md)
distinguishes private actual/apparent authority, principal representation,
notice, revoked/expired mandates, delivered benefits and defect/control
liability. Shared credentials or a vendor update cannot decide those cases.

[GA01–GA24](10-general-ai-capability-and-public-control.md) now specifies a distinct
upstream severe-risk testing/release regime across public/private/community
controllers. Ordinary AI use/research remains free under its actual laws; GA
adds neither model votes nor a general speech/model licence.

[CRD scoped credentials](13-scoped-credentials-and-public-access.md)
makes proof acceptance sector-specific and alternate access funded.
A shared standard, directory or credential is no transfer of authority.

[EDA01–EDA06](../C03/15/07-electoral-discovery-and-direct-access.md) addresses
bounded election discovery and independent correction.
