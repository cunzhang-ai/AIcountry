# Design382 decision. Resolve public responsibility without suspending service

## Baseline and selected rule

Design381 AP protects first receipt, E supplies urgent source-specific
duties, XR covers interlocal disputes and HDC covers arranged discharge.
Effective access requires follow-through but gives no default authority
or payer across sectors when both deny ownership. A routing duty alone
cannot make an unassigned service available by its deadline.

Select [AP13–AP18](../country/C04/06/06-essential-service-duty-while-responsibility-is-disputed.md)
for designated essential interfaces, including a health and home
assistance seam in the worked case. The elected schedule fixes
accountability in advance. Preserve an established provider's legal
duties, use a local coordinator for covered unowned receiving and a
named national fallback with a separate lawful payer. Each sector
decides its own scope. Settle durable assignment and payer liability
in parallel and make both omissions and unsafe arrangements reviewable.

## Alternatives and objections

Retain existing channels and strengthen referrals. This preserves local
discretion and avoids new budget exposure but leaves the unresolved
interim power in a same-place cross-sector dispute. A single integrated
public provider could avoid some assignment costs. It concentrates
control and still needs independent review and paid substitute staff.
The selected limited route keeps substantive boundaries and makes the
temporary exposure explicit. Integration remains a real rival where it
can establish the same rights, expertise, access and failure capacity.

An advance can invite cost shifting or indefinite provisional status.
Require named scope, expiry, verified receipt, ordinary decision clocks
and independent resolution. The resident keeps direct challenge and
need not fund the dispute or accept informal work. No reimbursement
funds initial action before actual receipt. Coordination itself consumes
paid time and cannot supply unavailable clinical staff or a suitable place.

## Evidence, migration and next action

The [official framework and one investigated case](sources.md)
distinguish funding responsibility, appropriate care and the actual
effects of administrative delay. The [constructed comparison](../settlement/essential-service-duty-while-responsibility-is-disputed/01-two-duties-and-a-disputed-payer.md)
tests several concurrent calls without assuming that authority or
cash creates staffing. No qualified local evidence, affected participation
or independent legal review has established the proposed route.

Use an expressly enacted schedule with authority, cap, payer, fallback,
records and lawful operational succession. Retain applicable XR, E, HDC,
AP and ordinary review. Preserve prior terms, accrual, consent, local
democratic competence and source-specific deadlines. A newly funded
interim power does not change final eligibility or confer general
authority on the coordinator. No actual statute, software or spending
permission changes here.

Next trace a same-place health and assistance interface through actual
dates and paid capacity, including people with no established provider.
Use existing independent case and budget reviewers, and seek separately
safe accounts from affected people and workers. Reopen on routine
fallback use, delayed adjudication, cost dumping, displaced care or
better qualified integration. Actual readiness and outcomes remain open.
