# MS09–MS12: protect people and keep fiscal choice elected

*Part of [national stabilization](../61-employment-prices-and-household-security.md).*

**MS09 — Retain person-side automatic protection as the default stabilizer.**
Actual I05/SD current-need rules, finite EB earnings support and existing due
services/earned Y continue through lawful eligibility and timely payment;
people do not wait for a recession declaration, employer rescue or new aggregate
model verdict. Their existing ordinary standing/payment authority is retained,
not newly invented here. No cap by application order, annual programme line or
secret macro target can redraw statutory claims. Their real costs, tax offsets,
coincident revenue loss, expiry and excluded people must be visible. This is
not automatic full wage insurance, compensation for every price rise or an
extension of EB duration. M, indexation and earnings/coverage changes retain
their actual source-law selectors; no new general inflation-indexation formula
is created. I05/S and actual additional-needs routes remain after
EB ends; no assumption that everyone then has a job.

**MS10 — Recovery is a public package, not a new monetary benefit.** Cabinet
prepares and Assembly can amend a coherent protection/recovery/capacity package
through DB/RF/TN: personal support, qualified current services, useful repair/
learning/investment, prospective burdens, accepted borrowing and continuing
claims. Prefer person-side portability over a default firm subsidy; a justified
retention scheme must name actual worker protection, viable task, public risk,
entry/exit and no double replacement. Voluntary paid public work is a meaningful
rival requiring useful tasks, supervisors, wage conditions, materials, receiving
and source, not a costless entitlement inferred from unemployment. No such new
scheme, rate, appropriation or sovereign finance power is adopted here.

**MS11 — Financing and employment conditions can deteriorate together.** Keep
RF's explicit elected borrowing purpose/currency/exposure/duration/servicing,
recurring-source choice, due-date liquidity and actual counterparty settlement.
An existing bounded annual authority can be used only within its terms; this
adds no automatic rollover or invented lender. A current-year deficit can be
justified for stabilization without a balanced-budget rule. A fiscal change
must carry its demand, capacity, distribution, price/FX and debt consequences;
an automatic stabilizer is not proof of available cash or adequate supply.
Source failure preserves E/NP/DB protection, unmet claims and elected correction,
not a Treasury veto or unlawful borrowing to make the model green.

**MS12 — Coordinate evidence without merging authority.** Treasury, service/
production owners, independent evidence service and MC share lawful original
assumptions and alternative paths with protected sources. Cabinet/Assembly
chooses tax/spending/debt under law; MC independently chooses only its lawful
instruments under the joint mandate. Publish material disagreements and actual
policy assumptions; no secret bargain to waive rights, rate veto by a minister,
central-bank veto of the budget or fiscal surrender to a single model.
Neither side can force the other to make its forecast come true. Existing
appointments, opposition scrutiny, courts and ordinary democratic replacement
continue during recession, inflation or disagreement. Coordination creates no
new council, emergency authority or final AI decision maker.

[MF25–MF30](../../C04/08/06-local-fiscal-shocks-and-service-reserves.md)
adds a dated common-service reserve where local revenue losses coincide.
Keep it separate from person-side protection and test national revenue
and qualified service capacity through the next call.

[MS27–MS32](07-recession-and-timely-income-protection.md) tests
when standing stabilizers can actually reach people. Keep existing
eligibility, monetary authority and elected fiscal limits.
