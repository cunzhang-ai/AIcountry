# Design298. Public investment and realized technology returns

10 November 2026 · Research selection · No actual enactment

## Baseline and decision

[Design297](baseline.json) keeps general lawful sources, worker
protection, plural ownership and limited public investment. The
earlier transition case separates falling wage receipts from rising
profit. This decision concerns what the public buys when it
chooses to finance an additional commercial capability. Apply the
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md). Codex assisted
the analysis. Actual affected people and independent specialists
have not qualified the proposal.

## Alternatives and selection

- **Keep existing service and decline the discretionary award.**
  This is the appropriate choice where ordinary repair works or
  the proposed benefit, independence or additionality is unproved.
  It leaves the opportunity to build the new capability open.
- **Buy the open operating route.** This obtains a defined service
  without creating a commercial return claim. It can be better
  where an additional financial term makes participation harder,
  raises price or relies on unreviewable company information.
- **Authorize staged support with a bounded public return.** Select
  this option in the declared case where actual public service,
  competition and gross costs match. It needs a separate enforceable
  agreement, independent verification, accepted exit and a later
  received return. It supplies no early funds. A paid ownership
  option remains a stronger structural rival where actual governance
  and operating capacity justify its different cost and risk.

[AT13–AT17](../country/C05/18/04-public-investment-and-realized-technology-returns.md)
selects public terms, realized receipts and the loss boundary.
The [worked comparison](../settlement/public-technology-returns/README.md)

It does not support a forecast, new tax or source of current payment.

## Authority, objection and migration

Elected authorities set the purpose, gross commitment, losses and
source. The competent buyer conducts the award, independent staff
verify service and terms, and the appropriate payer and auditor
confirm actual receipts. Revenue, competition, intellectual property,
worker rights and procurement retain their existing competence.
A complex financial term could favor incumbents or encourage
shifting the return base. Compare its actual additional cost and
preserve independently accessible original evidence. Do not
infer a failure is fraud or impose new recovery retrospectively.

Existing worker and resident rights continue on their original
sources. New terms are prospective. Preserve accepted service,
original pay and dispute dates, lawful private rights and
public obligations through an actual funded successor. A return
received later enters its own account and cannot repair a missed
early payment date.

## Next bounded decision

Obtain comparable lawful offers from willing providers for one
public capability. Compare an ordinary open purchase and a staged
return with accepted nonincumbent maintenance, actual user outcomes,
independent audit and loss exposure. Reopen if access is exclusive,
the base cannot be checked, the price or transition changes, or
ordinary repair supplies the benefit. No award or national fiscal
source is qualified.
