# AIcountry: working framework for institutional research

Revision: Design433 · 13 November 2026 · Proposed research method clarification.
[Immediate predecessor](2026-11-scoped-evidence-correction-across-decisions/predecessor-design-432/FRAMEWORK.md)
· [Earlier Design83 record](2026-10-democratic-repair-and-finite-attention/decision-record.md).
No governing or contribution procedure is adopted by this revision.

**Mission:** design institutions that improve people's lives and their
practical ability to shape those institutions within material and ecological
limits. Claims of superiority need actual comparison; no country has adopted
this proposal. The [country design](COUNTRY-DESIGN.md) is the project output;
[design status](DESIGN-STATUS.md) names choices and conflicts. This page is a
self-contained working method for examining changes. Use the [decision
record](DECISION-TEMPLATE.md) proportionately; an ordinary problem report does
not require a full dossier. The method is proposed research guidance. Existing
publication permissions, constitutional holds and lawful authority remain
unchanged.

## 1. Democratic boundary and authority

Equal political standing, effective opposition, public control, enforceable
remedies and peaceful replacement are requirements of the intended country. AI
may assist but cannot hold exclusive sovereign lawmaking power. For any
public-power proposal, trace initiation, appointment, funding, source
information, review, enforcement and the possibility of a lawful handover under
a hostile incumbent. Check cumulative capture, including abuse by protectors.
During delay, distinguish disagreement, unlawful refusal, disputed power and
absent capacity; identify who controls the trigger, which duties continue and
what happens if a fallback fails. Silence, a clock or a research selection does
not create consent or new authority. [Detailed democratic and diagnostic
reasoning](method/01-democracy-and-diagnosis.md).

## 2. Diagnose before choosing an institution

Keep the common method, competing social models and contextual decisions
distinct. The coordination-centered model can be rejected; material provision,
ownership, bargaining, relationships and ecology may explain a problem better.
Record the situation, affected people, missing perspectives and rival
explanations. Separate values, lawful or physical constraints, empirical
observations and design hypotheses; trace important claims to original evidence
and do not count AI restatements as independent observations. Preserve
unfamiliar meanings and what would discriminate among explanations. A genuine
value conflict cannot be settled by a metric. [Diagnosis
details](method/01-democracy-and-diagnosis.md).

## 3. Compare whole choices and their transitions

For a consequential choice, compare the current arrangement, a limited change
and a meaningful structural rival where feasible. Each option needs a causal
mechanism, responsible people and institutions, affected winners and losers,
rights, resource and transition requirements, external and future effects,
correction and failure paths. Reconcile shared commitments once; name legal
claims and operational successors. A technically attractive end state can fail
in transition. Do not average away incompatible arrangements or treat a named
reviewer as staffed independence. Actual affected-party participation and
legitimate authority remain distinct from qualified analysis. [Package
comparison details](method/02-comparison-and-collaboration.md).

## 4. Select the next useful action

Choose work by consequential uncertainty, affected people, dependencies and
cost. Competing diagnoses call for discriminating evidence; disputed meanings
for interpretation; unknown feasibility for a bounded resource assessment;
absent authority for a prepared public choice; untested mechanisms for a safe
informative assessment. A reasoned retain/no-change decision is valid. Name a
bounded output, completion condition and resource limit. Report evidence
qualification, research selection, legal authorization, operational readiness
and observed outcomes independently. State what was checked, not checked,
objected to and could reopen the result. [Action-selection
details](method/02-comparison-and-collaboration.md).

## 5. Keep future communication replaceable

Expression, interpretation, negotiation, delegation, authorization and
execution are distinct acts. Text, speech, direct meetings, simulations and
future media may assist different acts. Preserve source meaning, attribution,
known translation loss, affected scope and challenge routes without forcing
private life into a public schema. A mandate has scope, duration, resource
bounds, reserved powers and revocation; agent copies do not multiply it.
Framing, ranking, sponsorship and mediator replacement are questions of power.
Decentralized inquiry does not settle competing commitments; offline action
needs prior nonoverlapping limits and cannot assume instant revocation. Judge
methods by understanding, agency, inclusion, correction and effort, not message
volume. [Collaboration details](method/02-comparison-and-collaboration.md).

## 6. Maintain valid support and continuing duties

Pin the baseline, procedure, source revisions, definitions, dependencies and
material events. On change, preserve earlier reasoning safely; map affected
claims and omitted dependencies; reassess, narrow, retire or retain with
reasons. Reuse support only within its complete valid scope; new events and
objections require fresh scoped review even with unchanged inputs. Final decisions
need fresh review; disclose received/unexamined material and do not claim exhaustive
coverage. An unresolved issue may narrow, defer or defeat the claimed conclusion.
The detailed method distinguishes bounded research from actual legal review. Reviewers disclose competence, controllers, shared evidence and
actual inspection. When institutions change, map pending cases, obligations,
resources and remedies to lawful successors; technical rollback cannot reverse
every consequence. A new procedure cannot approve itself. For each material use record whether
support is necessary, independently sufficient or contextual. When a correction
arrives, identify confirmed and reasonably suspected uses, explain their distinct
dispositions, and retain unresolved scope with a named owner and next decision.
Shared provenance supplies no authority over another decision.
[Worked use](settlement/scoped-evidence-correction-across-decisions/01-one-source-and-four-decisions.md). [Consistency
details](method/03-consistency-and-learning.md).

## 7. Protect people and learn from results

Preserve public reasons and correction history while limiting sensitive
evidence by purpose, access, retention and complaint route. If safe custody is
absent, do not collect testimony merely to fill a research register. Real
interventions require competent authorization, participant protections,
resources, adverse-signal response and continuity. Check people who cannot
participate or exit. Leave a bounded next question, responsible role, evidence
trigger and condition for revisiting the decision; missing capacity stays
explicit. More formalization, review volume or new prose is not proof of better
lives. [Protection and continuation
details](method/03-consistency-and-learning.md).
