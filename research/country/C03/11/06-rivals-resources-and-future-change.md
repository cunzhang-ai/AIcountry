# Delegated law: a revisable choice, not an automatic improvement

*DR21–DR24; part of [C03/11](../11-delegated-rules-and-effective-public-control.md).*

**DR21 — Compare fundamentally different choices.** Retaining broad hierarchy
costs less new procedure but leaves shadow criteria and unusable call-in. Fewer
binding rules, decentralized practice and direct statutes for the remaining
floors may reduce capture/delay; private gatekeepers may instead acquire power.
A statute/vote on every update gives direct responsibility but can bury judgment
in technical bundles. Broad executive discretion adapts quickly but shifts
burdens and litigation costs before the public can intervene. A new independent
technical veto is not selected: expertise does not confer permanent political
power. The selected middle is specific delegation, routine notice, material/
called affirmative control and ordinary courts; a simpler package may displace it.

One-member conversion can be used to call every proposal. This is accepted
rather than making a small opposition dependent on party size/majority assent.
It does not guarantee timely safety updates. A majority can pass a properly
scrutinized proposal, enact narrower law, resource more sittings, or propose a
prospective lawful procedural change. It cannot declare the queue an emergency,
infer consent or cancel old rights. Neither a filled docket nor fewer rules is
itself evidence of better lives. Actual harmful backlog/inequality can reject
this architecture; more committees are not the automatic response.

**DR22 — Pay the whole chain once.** Price issuer/source and cumulative-effects
work, ordinary/FA consultation, affected time/access/care, opposition/counsel,
separate Clerk/register/offline archives, model/standards licenses, inspectors/
providers, local implementation, legal aid, ordinary judges and actual execution.
Keep simultaneous budget/election/court/service duties and paid alternate sitting
visible. One worker, venue, controller or vendor is not several safeguards.
Assembly appropriation/payer is separate from the challenged issuer, not a new
self-taxing office. Treasury G/S, insurer F, SF standby, client/worker/pension
property and separately owned FX retain their own obligations. No expected fine,
AI productivity, free testimony or private exporter currency funds new work.

The [dependency note](../../../settlement/commitments/19-delegated-rules-and-public-control.md)
and [dated fixtures](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-delegated-rules-and-public-control/results.json)
expose elected-hour, cash, owned-FX and qualified legal-shift shortfalls. They
are invented quantities, not national cost forecasts. An annual surplus cannot
supply the same interpreter or member in simultaneous hearings. If safe exact
foreign-source access cannot be purchased, choose a lawful accessible alternative
or leave the new rule unmade; an inaccessible reference is no shortcut.

**DR23 — Future collaboration may change the method.** Speech, private small
meetings, collective experience, demonstrations, simulations and future media
can communicate effects; text/account submissions are not the only evidence.
Interpretation, delegated delivery, political support and actual consent remain
different acts. Preserve unfamiliar meaning/known loss, source custody and
challenge; a human source/controller does not become independent through agents.
Public versions state which source/method/scope changed, affected consumers,
continuing duties and what support still holds. Reuse complete unchanged
support only in scope; new events/objections require fresh review. An AI model
update cannot authorize a rule or rewrite the procedure that judges it.

**DR24 — Bounded conclusion and next national decision.** This selects a
positive rulemaking framework, not an adopted procedure. Missing real public/
legal authority, original effects, qualified independent review, actual funded
capacity, received remedies and better lives remain distinct unknowns. Reopen
on shadow criteria, cumulative evasion, inaccessible versions, opposition or
court starvation, repeated harmful backlog, unsafe expiry or a substantively
better less-rule/direct-statute alternative. Review the first actual annual
rule docket with affected people before claiming operational adequacy.

[LR local-law selection](../14-local-law-and-public-control.md) now specifies
local general-rule approval, input, agenda, urgency/continuity and direct paid
execution; it does not revise this national DR procedure. MC/NF/PC/EL later
choices have their own scope and are no retrospective revalidation here.
Current whole-country priorities are in [DESIGN-STATUS](../../../DESIGN-STATUS.md): nonbank financial law,
full sources/production/credit/VAT/price/FX/legacy and actual qualified public/
operational evidence remain. Reprioritize actual urgent harm/larger contradictions;
no claim that every future national need is exhausted.
