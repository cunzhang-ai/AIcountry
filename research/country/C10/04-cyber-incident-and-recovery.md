# C10. Cyber incidents without a standing digital emergency ruler

*Part of [C10 safety and international responsibility](../C10-safety-and-international-relations.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

A breach can interrupt elections, courts, care, payments, water or freight
without granting the executive a general power over those institutions.
Each public service authority remains responsible for its own systems,
procurement, lawful data, continuity and user remedies. Prior Assembly law
requires asset and common-vendor inventories, tested backup integrity,
least-privilege access, incident drills, manual service routes, reporting
thresholds and funded recovery. Critical private operators carry compatible
duties under their sector charters. The technical duty follows the service
and its users, not the location of a cloud server.

[DP01–DP16](../C11/09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md) now assigns
this bounded technical coordinator to the career C04 Public Digital Operations
Director, with qualified appointment/tenure, actual G source, external own-wrong
review and independent sector support. It receives material reports, verifies
relevant dependencies, warns and coordinates voluntary/preauthorized nonoverlap
aid. No added compulsory cross-sector direction is selected. Sector owners
isolate only actual own unsafe tasks within their lawful scope and authorize
qualified restoration; coordinator cannot redefine a benefit, cancel a ballot,
suspend a court deadline, censor speech, order broad network shutdowns or
create surveillance access. Intrusive investigation, compulsory disclosure,
search/interception, wider closure or coercion need their separate lawful
C08/C10 authority and challenge. Emergency labels bypass no constitutional limit.

An incident record preserves source logs, changes, custody, uncertainty and
who authorized each isolation or restore step without publishing exploitable
secrets or private testimony. Affected people receive timely, accessible
notice of material risks and a route to correct records, protect accounts,
restore claims and seek legal remedy. Notice may be briefly delayed only on
specific lawful grounds with independent review; indefinite secrecy cannot
hide an official failure. Investigators of serious public-office misconduct
are independent of the implicated operator and command. Audit, opposition and
courts obtain protected evidence through law, not through a central dashboard
controlled by the institution under review.

Recovery must verify clean backups, legal records and actual service before
announcing success. Essential offices keep equivalent in-person, paper or
other nonnetwork receipts while systems are unavailable. C09's physical
communications and freight duties, C06's shared scarcity account and C03's
election continuity remain distinct. A common cyber vendor outage is counted
once across all dependent sectors, including its staff, power and replacement
capacity. If restoration priorities impose a new material loss on another
service or locality, the competent elected authority decides under prior law;
incident staff can take bounded immediate safety actions only.

Actual systems, threat actors, contracts, lawful security classifications
and workforce are unknown. The [decision record](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-service-boundaries/decision-record.md)
compares sector ownership plus bounded coordination with a central cyber
commander and uncoordinated sector response. Reopen if a tested shared
failure cannot be contained in time, the coordinator accumulates political
control, or manual remedies are unusable.

[PD13/PD25](../C02/07-private-life-and-personal-data.md) supplies ordinary data
breach notice and bounded independently reviewed delay, not new cyber command,
search or surveillance powers. Controllers retain protected notice/receipt duties.

[IA/IL/IE civilian assessment](10-civilian-assessment-and-intelligence-limits.md) may receive a necessary lawful sector summary and warn a competent receiver. A cyber label, shared vendor or partner cannot create NAU private-network access, bulk data, device search or covert surveillance; source quality and corrections remain separate from each operator’s actual recovery/decision authority.

[CN09–CN11 communications](../C09/22/03-independent-fallback-and-control.md)
sets independently witnessed receiving and the last safe alternate start for
each communications-dependent duty. DP retains bounded technical assistance
and each sector retains its existing delivery and rights powers.

[CRD scoped credentials](../C11/13-scoped-credentials-and-public-access.md)

Preserve independent sector control and ordinary receipt during recovery.
