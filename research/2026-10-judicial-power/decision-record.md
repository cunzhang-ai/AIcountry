The bounded question is **how a person can obtain an impartial ruling and see it
performed when the government, a court leader, a powerful litigant or a common
vendor controls appointments, funds, files or enforcement**. C03 and C08 already
promise independent courts, urgent relief and executed judgments. D5 proposes
a constitutional bench; the continuity correction warns that vacant seats and
unavailable reserves cannot be cured by deadlines. The remaining gap is one
operating chain connecting court competence, judge selection, case assignment,
discipline, funding, evidence, appeal and compliance. Otherwise a constitutional
right can survive on paper while the claimant never gets a hearing or the losing
state keeps its credentials and money.

**Values:** equal legal standing, fair process, public control of general law,
independent adjudication, protection against judicial corruption and timely
effective remedies. **Constraints:** applicable constitutional and ordinary law,
valid existing judgments, finite judges and cash, adverse-party participation,
and no judicial tax or executive appointment by default. **Unknown empirical
claims:** real case mix, delay, professional independence, common-controller
exposure, staff and vendor capacity, and which court arrangement people trust.
**Design hypothesis:** divided selection, protected case jurisdiction and data,
reviewable judicial conduct, bounded funding and named compliance duties can
make legality review usable without making judges a rival legislature. It can
fail through coordinated capture, judicial corporatism, systematic refusal
to obey, or shortages that no court can order into existence.

## Competing institutional packages

| Package | Why it might help | Failure and transition cost |
|---|---|---|
| Retain C03/C08 and enforce existing protections | Uses existing general duties and avoids another body. A real jurisdiction with independently functioning courts might need no structural change. | Appointment, docket, payroll, records and execution can still converge in the challenged office; vague independence does not name a receiving forum after vacancy. |
| Elected judges with short renewable terms | Creates a direct electoral replacement route for abusive judges. | Funders and partisan media can reward favorable rulings; campaign and reelection pressure can make a judge dependent on litigants or the cabinet. Poorly informed contests can miss misconduct. |
| Judges alone select, discipline and budget the judiciary | Insulates cases from partisan branches and can protect technical competence. | A closed profession can protect its members, ration access, conceal discipline and become an unaccountable policy veto; it also cannot lawfully appropriate itself unlimited public funds. |
| A single powerful constitutional guardian with exclusive emergency jurisdiction | Uniform constitutional answers and a visible opponent to executive overreach. | One vacancy, captured appointment chain or closed filing channel disables all relief; exclusive jurisdiction can extinguish older ordinary-court remedies. |
| **Selected: ordinary courts plus bounded constitutional review, plural selection and separate compliance** | Keeps everyday claims and urgent rights review distributed, while a designated constitutional forum resolves general constitutional questions; divided personnel, data, budget and execution limit one veto. | More trained staff, appellate coordination, confidentiality, conflicting interim orders and public money; plural labels do not prevent one patron from controlling the selectors or executor. |

The selected arrangement does not establish the D5 court's size or terms as a
fact. The adopting public must compare a separate constitutional court with
constitutional review in a supreme ordinary court under its legal history,
case volume, territorial structure and affected preferences. A mixed body
may be preferable to elected judges in one context and worse where the legal
profession and citizen-selection infrastructure share a sponsor. This is an
architectural selection conditional on independent capacity, not an empirical
ranking of judicial systems.

## Selected authority and remedy chain

1. **Assign jurisdiction before a dispute.** Assembly law specifies ordinary
   first-instance and appellate forums for civil, administrative, criminal
   and local-law claims; identifies who can seek constitutional relief and
   which constitutional questions reach the designated final forum. Existing
   ordinary-court rights and urgent interim powers are not erased by creating
   a specialist bench. A public competence and appeal rule handles overlapping
   proceedings without sending the claimant between offices until time expires.
   An independent court decides law and remedy in a case; it cannot set a tax,
   invent a general service standard or choose a government because it prefers
   its program. Elected bodies remain free to legislate prospectively within
   protected rights and final judgments.
2. **Select qualified judges without a single appointing owner.** A judicial
   appointments body has staggered, disclosed sources: judges selected by
   peers, legal practitioners selected through contestable professional
   processes, and paid lay members drawn by an independently audited public
   process. No one class, ministry or sitting court leadership controls its
   shortlist, conflicts, budget and final vote. It publishes eligibility,
   work-related evidence, conflicts and reasons while protecting private
   applicants. Ordinary judges are selected through this process under
   prospective law and judicial challenge. The constitutional forum has
   plural appointment sources including elected and non-government Assembly
   support, qualified legal selection and a paid public route; a governing
   majority or judicial guild cannot choose all seats in one term. The D5
   nine-seat, twelve-year, three-source formula remains a provisional option
   needing separate founding approval and quorum testing. Political loyalty,
   wealth, model scores and agreement with a party are not qualifications.
3. **Keep vacancies from rewriting the rules.** Start a scheduled vacancy
   process before expiry; publish unresolved seats, actual candidate and
   reserve availability, and the lawful quorum. A preselected eligible reserve
   may serve only within the original seat source, term and qualification
   rules. Vacancy, delay or an AI prediction cannot lower a voting threshold,
   extend a nonrenewable term or create a cabinet acting judge. If the final
   constitutional forum cannot sit, preauthorized ordinary courts may grant
   narrow, appealable individual-rights protection and preserve records and
   claims; they do not issue a replacement final nationwide constitutional
   judgment. General constitutional questions await a lawfully competent
   forum. The public record identifies rights left without a final remedy;
   an unfilled bench is a readiness or constitutional failure, not consent.
4. **Make assignment and judicial accountability reviewable.** Court law
   prepublishes jurisdiction, specialization, auditable rotation or random
   docket assignment and transfer rules; a court leader cannot hand-pick a
   named political case. Any exception has a recorded legal reason and challenge.
   Parties can seek recusal on disclosed conflicts before a differently controlled
   judge or panel. Hearings and operative orders are public except for
   legally justified protection, with reasons and proportionate appeal.
   A distinct complaints and disciplinary route investigates corruption,
   serious misconduct or incapacity under specified grounds, independent
   evidence, fair hearing and appeal; it cannot discipline a judge merely
   for an adverse lawful ruling. A judge does not select their own reviewer.
   An external criminal investigator and prosecutor handle suspected crime,
   with case allocation and accused-person rights preserved. Court leaders
   cannot privately change a docket, suppress dissent or become unremovable.
5. **Protect a usable court, not an unlimited self-budget.** Prior law gives
   core filing, legal-aid, court, archive and judgment-execution operations a
   bounded continuing-obligations schedule under A5–A6. Treasury pays lawful
   due amounts on an audited route that the challenged minister cannot
   selectively stop. The judiciary proposes a need and risk statement; the
   elected Assembly chooses general funding with public reasons, protected
   rights review and limits on retaliatory in-year cuts or discriminatory
   withholding. Judges cannot appropriate their own pay or programs. Staffing,
   interpretation, access, counsel, safe records, backup premises and security
   are recurrent costs in C06, not free consequences of constitutional text.
6. **Trace compliance after the judgment.** Every order names the lawful
   defendant, required act or payment, due date, information needed, first
   executor and independent evidence of delivery. A civil enforcement service
   can act against private parties within property and subsistence safeguards.
   For a public body, prior law assigns the named office and treasury channel
   a duty to comply; a minister cannot treat an appeal or budget slogan as an
   automatic stay. If an official refuses, the court can order production,
   correct the record and impose only prospective statutory, individually
   reviewed consequences. A preauthorized substitute may perform a ministerial
   records release or due payment; it cannot legislate a policy, tax, hire
   nonexistent staff or seize an essential network. Where lawful cash or
   physical capacity is genuinely absent, record the unmet judgment and
   responsible elected funding decision without erasing the claimant's right.
   Police enforce only a specific lawful order with proportionate powers;
   the military is not a court's default collection service. If executive,
   treasury, police and records all refuse, the country has a constitutional
   failure that a paper order has not solved.
7. **Keep AI subordinate to the case record.** AI can help with translation,
   search, scheduling and option analysis under a named judicial owner; it
   may not assign a judge secretly, create evidence, determine guilt, set a
   sentence or write a binding judgment. Parties can inspect material AI
   inputs and contest outputs subject to lawful privacy and secrecy rules.
   A workable human/offline route and record export survive vendor failure.
   Publication of a model's explanation is not a court's reasoned finding.

The [UN Basic Principles on the Independence of the Judiciary](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/611397/files/455c71de2.pdf)
are a primary institutional comparator for protected jurisdiction, selection,
tenure and discipline. The Venice Commission's [Rule of Law Checklist](https://www.venice.coe.int/images/SITE%20IMAGES/Publications/Rule_of_Law_Check_List.pdf)
tests access to justice, judicial independence and implementation of judicial
decisions. [ICCPR Article 14](https://treaties.un.org/doc/Publication/CTC/Ch_IV_4.pdf)
states a fair hearing by a competent, independent and impartial tribunal for
the determination of rights and obligations in a suit at law and criminal
charges. These primary sources inform checks; none selects AIcountry's court
size, appointment mix, automatic reserve, budget, domestic legal force or
proof that the package works. A real polity must identify its applicable
treaties and inherited judicial rules before lawful change.

## Dependencies, objections and transition

This choice revises the working interpretation of C03/C04/C06/C08/C11/C14,
proposed A7 and the standing-obligations schedule. It gives the appointment,
funding, information and execution chain different decision points without
claiming they are actually independent. The judiciary must retain ordinary
accountability: published performance and delay data with case privacy,
qualified audits, appellate correction, lawful discipline, and public ability
to amend general procedural law prospectively. Courts protect constitutional
rights and review abuse; neither court leaders nor a self-appointed judicial
council may govern general social policy. Prosecutors and defenders keep their
own roles and budgets. The bar or an AI supplier cannot be the only gateway
for a poor or politically unpopular claimant.

The source manuscript's `governance.constitutional-institutions` still assigns
general legislation to AI, and its judicial appointment schedule cannot be
silently adopted under that old authority. Earlier D5 and continuity proposals
remain separate predecessor artifacts, not automatically accepted by this
research selection. Existing cases, filings, limitation periods, judgments,
lawful judge tenure, pay, pensions, counsel and evidence must survive any
transition. A real adopting jurisdiction needs constitutional authority,
lawful appointment/discipline statutes, an actual bench and reserve inventory,
court-worker agreements, accessible premises, records custody, due judgment
liabilities, appropriation and tested treasury/security compliance before
commencement. An automatic purge or blanket appointment rollover may destroy
either independence or fair process.

Material objections remain. Mixed appointment sources may share donors, parties,
families or professional patrons; a paid citizen route may be manipulated by
its qualification pool; lawyer participation may protect guild interests.
Ordinary-court fallback may fragment constitutional law or prolong disputes.
Continuing funding may entrench an inefficient court, while Assembly budget
control can starve an unpopular one. Enforcement against a government can
become judicial fiscal rule or intimidation of public servants. Transparency
can expose vulnerable litigants; secrecy can hide favoritism. Each objection
requires original controller, case-flow, cost and remedy evidence, not another
independence label or model agreement. A reasoned retain/no-change outcome is
valid if the adopting public's existing courts already meet the same duties.

**Evidence qualification:** historical local proposals and the cited primary
standards, without real case files, workload, budgets, appointments or affected
testimony. **Research selection:** the seven-part court and compliance chain,
conditional on a legitimate choice of constitutional forum. **Legal
authorization:** none. **Operational readiness:** no judges, clerks, counsel,
secure archives, reserve seats, direct treasury channel or compliant officers
verified. **Observed outcomes:** none; F10 is an authored stress case.

The next bounded adopting-public test is **a court vacancy plus executive refusal
to obey an urgent order during a budget lapse**. In a real jurisdiction, map
the original filing and bench, appointment and quorum rules, case assignment,
legal-aid payment, vendor-held evidence, treasury authority, named executor,
appeal, available substitute and actual receipt. Include litigants, judges,
court workers, ministers, opposition and affected service users; compare the
selected route with existing protections and a more self-governing judiciary.
The deliverable is a timed lawful-authority, money, evidence, staff and remedy
account with dissent. This project has no jurisdiction or field budget. Reopen
the selection if independent courts are already effective or if the proposed
divisions generate capture, arbitrary judgments, unpayable duties or material
loss of democratic control. If evidence is unavailable, do not claim that
judicial independence or successful remedies have been demonstrated.
