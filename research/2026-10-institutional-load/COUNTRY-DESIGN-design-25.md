# AIcountry: the country we propose to build

4 October 2026 · Integrated design 25 · English working design, not enacted law

**AIcountry is proposed as a constitutional parliamentary democracy with a plural
economy, universal essential services, meaningful local self-government and AI under
revocable public control.** Its purpose is to give people security, freedom, time,
relationships and the practical power to shape their society, within ecological limits.
It is neither an AI sovereign nor a state that directs every aspect of private life.

This is the main country-design document. It makes a coherent set of choices that can
be criticized and revised. [Design status](DESIGN-STATUS.md) identifies conflicts,
unfinished work and supporting material. [The framework](FRAMEWORK.md) explains how
to evaluate changes; it is not a substitute for designing the country.

The country is designed as one connected settlement:

| Public question | Selected operating answer | Where it is developed |
|---|---|---|
| Who can rule, and who can remove them? | An elected Assembly controls law, tax and spending; a replaceable cabinet governs within law; opposition, courts and distributed election evidence protect a peaceful transfer. | C02–C04 |
| How can people live and work freely? | Plural production, worker bargaining and usable time sit alongside health, education, care, income, safe housing and reachable daily mobility, each with a responsible delivery authority and remedy. | C05–C08 |
| What limits promises? | One elected budget and separate cash, labor, land, water, energy, ecological and external-payment accounts expose tradeoffs and maintain physical systems. | C06/C09 |
| Who may use force or decide a crisis? | Prospective law, separate civilian institutions and independent review govern ordinary justice and protection; elected and constitutional limits constrain exceptional power. | C08/C10 |
| What work should AI do? | Optional resident agents and replaceable task tools help with access, planning, professional work, hazards and inquiry; competent people remain answerable for legal effects and real outcomes. | C11–C12 |
| How can knowledge and institutions change? | Protected source records, contestable public statistics, independent inquiry, plural communication and replaceable AI support human judgment; lawful public decisions and bounded trials preserve rights while allowing revision. | C11–C14 |

The [authority and legal package](AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION.md) and
[founding and whole-country failure assessment](FOUNDING-AND-STRESS.md) supply
proposed transition and stress checks. The [decision register](DESIGN-STATUS.md)
tracks edition history, conflicts and evidence limits. Actual ratification,
funding and operation require real people and a lawful jurisdiction.

“Selected” below means selected for this working design, not ratified by a population.
No territory, founding electorate, operating government or national budget has been
established. Institutional hypotheses are not claims of superiority over existing states.

## C01. First principles and the social settlement

People have different conceptions of a good life. Knowledge is dispersed and fallible;
power can protect or dominate; material resources and human attention are limited.
Institutions must therefore distribute authority, support real alternatives, expose
their mistakes and preserve the ability to change course. These are design premises,
not scientific proofs of a uniquely correct constitution.

The proposed settlement has five connected commitments:

1. Everyone has enforceable basic rights and access to essentials, including people
   without political membership or complete documents.
2. Members of the national electorate have equal political standing, effective
   opposition and peaceful replacement of rulers. Good service statistics cannot
   substitute for public control; the electorate's boundary must itself be contestable.
3. People may build businesses, cooperatives, associations, families and different ways
   of living. Neither state permission nor compulsory data collection governs ordinary
   lawful relationships.
4. Those exercising power or imposing costs must answer to affected people through
   law, democratic institutions and usable remedies.
5. The present generation must maintain ecological and institutional conditions that
   leave meaningful choices to future generations and people affected abroad.

Conflicts between these commitments require public reasons and legitimate decisions.
No national happiness score, intelligence ranking or optimization system settles them.

## C02. Membership, rights and belonging

All persons have bodily autonomy, due process, privacy, expression, association,
religious freedom, protection against discrimination and standing to challenge harm.
Children have independent interests and supported voice. Disability and language
differences require access, not lower civic status. Care and dependency do not reduce
political worth. Families and voluntary communities may take different lawful forms.

Legal identity belongs to the person, not a household representative or digital
platform. Birth and later status changes need accessible registration, evidence,
privacy, correction and independent review. Missing or disputed papers must not
bar urgent safety, health, basic education or subsistence; a provisional access
route preserves the claim while nationality, parentage and electoral eligibility
are decided under their distinct laws. Children retain their own identity and
voice even when adults disagree. Chosen supporters may assist an adult with
specified decisions, but support does not silently transfer the person's legal
capacity, property or vote. The [personal-status decision](2026-10-personal-status-and-care/decision-record.md)
records the competing family and state-power risks and the unresolved lawful
adoption questions.

The reference ordinary national franchise gives one vote and eligibility to stand
to adult citizens from 18 and to adult noncitizens after one ordinary four-year
Assembly term of habitual residence. This residence period is a provisional
democratic choice, not a proven optimum. Local voting and candidacy begin after a
shorter, law-defined period of actual residence. Time as a refugee, asylum seeker or
under disputed administrative status counts when habitual residence can be shown;
eligibility is not controlled by an employer, landlord or AI account. Citizenship
remains accessible under transparent, reviewable rules but is not the only route to
national political standing. Citizens abroad retain a usable ballot under adopted
registration and duplicate-vote controls. All eligibility and exclusion decisions
need timely independent remedies and safe alternatives to a single document or
biometric system. No one can lose a vote through arbitrary denaturalization or
politically motivated removal. The [membership comparison](2026-10-membership-review/analysis.md)
records the rival citizenship-only model and the unresolved value tradeoffs.
Core franchise and candidacy rights belong in the protected constitutional settlement;
registration law may administer them but cannot narrow the electorate for the
incumbent's benefit. Changes to electoral eligibility cannot govern the current
officeholders' next contest, apart from a narrowly reviewed rights-restoring rule.

This ordinary electorate does not decide its own founding authority. A legitimate
founding population and ratification process must be established beforehand with
the affected people; this project's author cannot choose it. Actual residence,
citizenship and diaspora rules would need lawful adoption in a real jurisdiction.

The state must not make essential protection conditional on ideological loyalty,
employment, a digital identity or acceptance of an AI intermediary. Residence and
citizenship serve different purposes and cannot silently determine every entitlement.

## C03. Democratic government and the ability to remove it

An elected Assembly legislates and controls taxation and appropriations. Elections
use the four-year cycle and the fixed-size, list-vote-backed compensatory reference
specified below.
The Assembly selects a prime minister; a majority can replace the cabinet by selecting
a successor. Courts adjudicate legality and rights. Ordinary policy remains contestable
through elections and legislation, not permanently delegated to experts.

The selected [candidate-entry arrangement](2026-10-democracy-and-time/candidate-gate.md)
uses two votes to keep proportional representation while reducing party control
over viable names. The [count-rule decision](2026-10-election-rule/decision-record.md)
now fixes what those votes mean in the reference design. The first vote chooses a
named territorial candidate; a plurality-winning independent takes a personal
seat. A plurality-winning list-backed candidate takes a seat only if their list
has enough second-vote seats. The second vote chooses a party or qualifying
nonparty civic list and may indicate one candidate on it. All valid list votes,
whether or not they carry a preference, determine that list's seat share;
preferences influence which unelected nominees fill its remaining seats. The
ballot must state plainly that a list-backed territorial plurality is not a
guaranteed seat. This limits Assembly growth but can leave a district without
its first-place candidate as a member; residents retain local elected councils
and access to every Assembly member, not an invented replacement winner.

The reference count starts with a fixed Assembly size **N** and a smaller number
of single-member territories **D**, both lawfully fixed with the map before the
contest; D may not exceed one third of N in this reference. Independent
territorial winners occupy **I** seats. Allocate the remaining **N - I** seats
among all eligible party and civic lists by the highest Sainte-Laguë quotients
of their list votes, without the old candidate's proposed legal percentage
threshold. A list cannot receive more seats than its prepublished eligible
candidate pool. For each list, territorial plurality winners take its seats in
descending order of their share of valid first votes in their respective
territories, up to its entitlement. Remaining seats go to not-yet-elected pool
members by recorded preference total, with the prepublished list order deciding
ties and zero-preference places. A person may appear for one list and at most
one territory; the same person can occupy only one seat. An independent may not
also be on a list. A party's later affiliation changes neither the count nor
the vacancy route. The complete count, ties, candidate exhaustion, recounts and
vacancies must be enacted before polling, with original ballot evidence and
court review; no official fills an uncovered territorial seat by discretion.
The N/D ratio, absence of a legal threshold and independent-seat exception are
research hypotheses, not claims of optimal representation.

Personal-seat status is not a label a list can lend to a proxy. A candidate's
accepted formal list endorsement, list direction or materially coordinated
campaign support must be disclosed before polling and makes that candidate
list-backed under reviewable rules; hidden common control requires an
evidence-based court remedy. Shared policy views, ordinary individual speech
or a later coalition do not by themselves establish fraud. An incumbent could
abuse this boundary to exclude opponents, so certification, evidence access and
appeal must be independent and timely. Covert coordination remains a material
limitation of the selected model.

Parties and civic lists choose their own platforms and lawful nomination pools
under published rules; the state cannot appoint opponents or test ideological
fitness. Electoral law sets objective filing, disclosure, cutoff and vacancy
rules, equal access to reasonable campaign support, and rapid independent relief
when exclusion would make candidacy useless. Lists cannot privately reorder
viable names after the legal cutoff; party expulsion does not alone remove an
elected member. Disclose material control of nominations and organized funding
without exposing vulnerable small supporters. Voter preference can itself reward
wealth and celebrity, and optional preferences may give a small group influence
over rank; ballot usability, representation and sponsor control need actual
public comparison with closed and flexible-list alternatives.

List and list-backed territorial vacancies draw from the election's frozen
preference/fallback order of unused nominees, with urgent review of eligibility;
an independent personal-seat vacancy uses a prior-law territorial by-election
open to all lawful candidates and does not recalculate the past national list
vote. This limited departure from proportionality, and the possibility that the
new winner affiliates with a list, must be disclosed before adoption. No party
may insert a new loyalist into an already counted list after polling.

Opposition receives practical agenda access, public information, questioning rights,
fair campaigning conditions and leadership of public-accounts scrutiny. Residents can
originate proposals; compensated resident panels can investigate and demand a reasoned
response. Neither panels nor referendums bypass individual rights or become an incumbent's
device for eliminating future competition. Public and independent media remain plural.
The constitutional future is also contestable: the [protected change route](2026-10-constitutional-change/decision-record.md)
lets both Assembly members and electors initiate exact amendments, requires a
genuine public final choice, and delays changes that would benefit current rulers.
It does not give Cabinet a plebiscite power or let a court reject lawful policy
because it dislikes the proposal.

Electoral administration, judicial review and audit require staggered appointments,
protected removal rules, independent information access and funding that the challenged
office cannot withhold. Appointment plurality alone is not independence. The detailed
[democratic candidate](2026-09-democratic-control/democratic-constitution.md) supplies
starting rules; its [continuity correction](2026-09-democratic-continuity/continuity-candidate.md)
limits claims about deadlock, reserves and handover. The still-unselected N,
district map and chosen one-third D ceiling are hypotheses, not demonstrated optima.

The [judicial-power decision](2026-10-judicial-power/decision-record.md) connects
those protections to an operating court system. Ordinary courts retain defined
civil, administrative, criminal and urgent individual-rights jurisdiction with
published appeal and conflict rules. A designated final constitutional forum
resolves general constitutional questions under prior law; whether it is a
separate court or a supreme ordinary-court bench requires legitimate selection.
The earlier nine-judge, twelve-year, three-source candidate remains a hypothesis,
not an enacted bench. A mixed-source appointments process tests qualifications,
conflicts and public reasons without letting cabinet, a governing majority or
judicial guild own the whole pipeline. Law fixes case assignment before a dispute,
allows challenge to conflicts and provides independent, fair discipline for
misconduct rather than punishment for an adverse lawful ruling. A vacancy cannot
lower an appointment threshold, extend a nonrenewable term or create an executive
acting judge. Preauthorized ordinary courts can preserve narrow individual
rights while a final forum lacks quorum, but cannot invent its final nationwide
authority. The gap remains public until a lawful court can decide it.

The reference democracy also needs a preauthorized, distributed route from
electoral evidence to an executable remedy. Local election offices retain
authenticated original poll evidence and a lawful reconciliation route; candidates,
voters, observers and courts receive timely, rights-protecting access. Ordinary
courts have specified urgent individual-rights competence if a specialist bench
is vacant, with appeal and conflict rules set before the dispute. Core election,
court, legal-aid and audit operations have bounded standing funding and record
custody independent of a challenged cabinet or its principal digital provider.
After a lawful result or order, named civil and security officers perform transfer
duties; neither local officers, courts, vendors nor a missed deadline invent a
winner. This [democratic-continuity choice](2026-10-operating-settlement/democratic-institutions.md)
distributes evidence, access and execution, not sovereign power to a new guardian.
Its independence depends on actual staff, usable cash, distinct controllers and
obedience to lawful orders; labels and duplicate AI reviews do not establish it.

Election records and public accounts have named lawful source custodians and
protected archival successors under C11. Candidates, opposition and residents
can contest suppression or material error through timely, privacy-protecting
access to original evidence. A changed statistical definition cannot alter
eligibility, erase a valid ballot or certify a preferred winner. An official
archive preserves the enacted rule and previous record version, while election
administrators and courts retain their own decision and remedy duties.

A valid electoral defeat must transfer command, records, treasury access and public
office. A losing executive has no veto. Peaceful opposition to the design itself is
protected; democratic safeguards must not become an excuse to suppress critics.
Command transfer includes armories, security authorizations, communications
credentials and custody of investigative records under lawful succession rules;
neither a commander nor a vendor certifies its preferred election result.

Competition also depends on power outside government. A person must be able to join
an opposition movement, report abuse and seek office without a dominant employer,
landlord, lender or communications gatekeeper making ordinary life untenable. Law
protects lawful political association against coercive retaliation while preserving
other people's expression, property, contractual rights and fair adjudication of
genuine misconduct. The country therefore treats political finance, media reach,
work and housing dependence, payment access and digital gatekeeping as one cumulative
democratic-control question, not separate sector statistics.

Provide neutral, law-defined public campaign support and usable opposition access;
disclose significant funding, coordinated spending and the effective controllers of
political advertisers, with safeguards for private individuals at risk. Electoral
administrators investigate unequal treatment under reviewable rules. They cannot
decide which lawful political opinion may be expressed. A challenger needs a timely
remedy that restores an actual opportunity to compete, rather than damages awarded
after an election. Exact finance limits, eligibility and privacy protections require
lawful adoption in a real jurisdiction; the [democratic candidate](2026-09-democratic-control/democratic-constitution.md)
supplies a starting specification, not proof of equal competition.

Before a law, merger or major public contract materially changes control of civic
communication or essential economic gateways, the responsible elected and regulatory
bodies should publish a combined dependence assessment. Affected workers, residents,
users and political challengers can contest the finding before an independent court
under deadlines suited to the harm. Review must examine shared owners, funders and
operators, as well as whether a lawful alternative can actually take over. It cannot
turn a regulator's political preference into a veto over ordinary competition or
speech; its scope and remedies require prior legislation and their own review.

The Assembly's enacted vote, the exact published statute and its commencement are
separate events. A publication clerk authenticates the record and can be challenged
for withholding it; neither that clerk nor an AI assurance gate decides policy.
Courts can annul unlawful action and enforce rights but cannot legislate a replacement
tax, appoint themselves, or decide an election from a missed administrative deadline.
The earlier proposed [authority chain](AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION.md) names proposers,
lawmakers, delivery authorities, treasury, reviewers and remedy executors.

## C04. National guarantees, local power and competent administration

The reference allocation is decentralized government within common constitutional
rights. National institutions handle the rights floor, macroeconomic framework,
interregional redistribution, major networks and cross-border obligations. Elected
local governments shape land use and deliver or commission community services.
Regional arrangements follow geography and scale; no arbitrary map is assumed.

Local discretion cannot remove national rights. National government cannot require
local delivery while hiding its cost. Equalization uses published standardized
service need and own-source revenue capacity, not a locality's discretionary
underspending or tax effort; the formula, protected data and changes receive
appeal and audit. Cross-boundary effects need joint arrangements
with clear responsibility; creating a committee does not dissolve anyone's obligation.

Local councils choose land use and service organization where effects remain local.
Shared catchments, housing and commuting areas, ecological systems and major networks
need legally scoped joint plans prepared with affected local governments and people.
The Assembly sets common rights, resource duties and conflict rules. For a material
unresolved allocation among localities, a statutory interlocal forum prepares
alternatives with affected people and councils but cannot tax or bind nonmembers.
The elected Assembly is the default final authority for the residual common
choice, voting its precise allocation, conditions, payer and implementing duties
with public reasons and independent rights review. Ordinary permits and valid
local plans proceed within prior law; a minor cross-boundary effect does not
require a national vote. An executive, technical forum or resource owner gains
no project approval or veto through delay. A directly elected regional authority
remains an alternative only after a lawful founding decision defines a durable
area, its affected electorate, powers, financing and review. The selected
[territorial comparison](2026-10-operating-settlement/territorial-authority.md)
does not presume a permanent regional map or local unanimity as the country's
allocation rule.

Adopt national guarantees and local funding responsibilities together. The national
authority remains responsible for arranging a lawful response when a locality cannot
meet an essential guarantee. A grant shortfall cannot silently remove the person's
claim or force another locality to absorb it without resources. The local authority
retains its duties and an accessible route to contest the funding decision.

An essential-service fallback requires prior law, not a cabinet declaration that
local government has failed. The statute sets observable non-delivery and danger
triggers, notice and a chance to cure except where urgent protection cannot wait,
and prompt independent review available to residents and the locality. A named
sector authority may temporarily use only the staff, records, facilities and
preauthorized funds needed to keep the guarantee running. Its powers have a
published scope and time limit, respect worker rights, privacy and property due
process, and require public accounting and a route back to local control. The
authority reports whether the cause was unlawful refusal, missing staff or
inadequate national funding;
intervention cannot manufacture capacity or displace a council's unrelated powers.
Professional clinical and teaching judgments remain governed by their own law.

A professional civil service works for lawful institutions rather than a party or
vendor. Cabinet directs lawful policy and may appoint a limited, disclosed set of
political advisers; ordinary and senior operational posts use open, job-related
competition, accessible entry, reasoned selection and appeal. Cabinet may choose
a senior manager from a qualified public shortlist for a defined mandate, with
published reasons and conflicts. Acting appointments expire under law and cannot
be serially renewed to bypass the competition. Career protection does not prevent
proportionate discipline, reassignment or dismissal for proved misconduct or
performance failure with due process. Professional staff can give candid advice,
then must execute a lawful elected decision; they cannot acquire a policy veto by
controlling staffing, files or implementation dates. A new cabinet cannot purge
career staff for their vote, lawful dissent or service under its predecessor.

Each essential service has one publicly identifiable authority responsible for
getting a remedy delivered, even when several providers participate. That
authority owns the workforce, supplier, maintenance, replacement and complaint
plan and reports whether people actually receive the service. It cannot call
an underfunded vacancy a productivity gain or use a contractor to conceal
staffing obligations. Staff need lawful pay, training, workable caseloads,
safe reporting channels and time to correct mistakes. Professional clinical,
teaching, legal and inspection judgments retain their own duties. Informal
cooperation remains possible without turning every interaction into a state
workflow.

Public buying begins with an elected or lawfully delegated service purpose,
not a vendor's proposed system. The commissioning authority compares public,
cooperative, private and mixed delivery where appropriate, including workforce,
full-life cost, maintenance, market concentration, rights, failure reception and
exit. Competitive, proportionate tendering is the default. Bid criteria and
weights, material beneficial ownership, political/official conflicts, award
reasons, changes, payments and delivery results are recorded and disclosed with
justified protection for trade secrets, privacy and security. Requirements must
allow capable new entrants; splitting a contract or altering it after award
cannot evade competition. Sole-source or emergency purchase needs a previously
enacted ground, a narrow term and ceiling, recorded alternatives and prompt
independent review. Urgency does not authorize a new permanent surveillance,
force or eligibility power.

For a material purchase, the body defining need, evaluators, contract manager,
delivery verifier, Treasury payer and auditor have distinguishable duties and
source records. Shared services can supply a small locality with professional
procurement capacity, but cannot choose its policy or become the only supplier.
No bidder or sponsor verifies its own performance or controls the only complaint
channel. Workers, residents and losing bidders have appropriate routes to
challenge a breached standard, unlawful exclusion, corruption or non-delivery;
the remedy must name who can suspend, repair, recompete or provide the service.
AI may flag anomalies and compare options, but an automated score cannot award,
blacklist, certify delivery or dismiss a complaint without accountable human
reasons and review. The [state-capability decision](2026-10-state-capability/decision-record.md)
compares this operating chain with patronage, central gatekeeping and outsourcing.

Contracts for essential delivery specify records custody, safe access, staff and
claim continuity, transferable data and keys, paid transition capacity and a
lawful receiving operator. The public authority retains the funded ability to
take over or recommission within a realistic period; a paper step-in clause is
not capacity. A contractor may perform authorized work but cannot gain sovereign
tax, arrest, detention, general eligibility, protest-control or lawmaking power
through procurement. Public enterprises and mixed operators face the same
service, conflict, audit and exit tests. The Assembly directs a public enterprise
through its lawful mandate, funded noncommercial duties and disclosed risk limits;
ministers cannot secretly demand preferential lending, employment or contracts.

Integrity protection covers ministers, career staff, local officials, public
enterprises, bidders and major subcontractors. Material interests and gifts
are disclosed to a statutory reviewer separate from the appointing or buying
office; recusal, reassignment, divestment or a bounded post-office restriction
follows the actual conflict, with privacy and proportionate appeal. A secure
independent reporting route
protects staff, suppliers and residents against retaliation and can preserve
evidence during a handover. Reports can reach audit, an ombud, a competent
prosecutor or a court within their different mandates; the implicated manager
does not control every intake. Audit, investigation, prosecution and adjudication
remain distinct, with appointment, funding and information safeguards against
both cabinet suppression and an abusive integrity office. A complaint is not
proof of corruption; inquiry protects defense, labor rights and confidential
sources. An allegation alone neither blacklists a rival nor halts an essential
service; narrow interim preservation or suspension needs reasons, time limits,
review and a receiving provider. If a procurement breach or public-enterprise
loss is proved, courts and competent authorities can order lawful correction,
recovery, compensation and discipline while the essential service continues
through a funded route.

Elected local governments also publish place-based access plans connecting housing,
clinics, schools, care, transport and service hours, with workforce and funding.
A voluntary cross-service convenor can resolve conflicting schedules or referrals
and escalate missing capacity, but cannot cancel a sector entitlement or override
clinical, educational or judicial judgment. The national authority funds its floor
and equalization and remains responsible for a lawful fallback when joint access
fails. The [social-provision comparison](2026-10-social-provision-review/analysis.md)
rejects both navigation without authority and one compulsory welfare agency.

Under the selected [housing-and-mobility arrangement](2026-10-housing-and-mobility/analysis.md),
each elected local authority names a housing-capacity authority responsible for
safe stock, lawful commissioning and repair, accessible homes and an interim
route when a dwelling becomes unusable. It names a mobility authority responsible
for safe walking and wheeling connections, roads and a minimum accessible daily
passenger network with usable hours and links to essential destinations. Public,
cooperative and private builders and operators may supply capacity, but the
authority retains a funded public or publicly controlled fallback and a lawful
receiving route when a provider exits. School, health and care authorities retain
their own transport and access duties; a common network does not cancel them.
Cross-boundary housing and travel burdens use the same interlocal preparation
and Assembly-reserved material allocation, not a separate technocratic veto.
National standards and equalization prevent a locality from shifting an
essential-floor failure onto poorer residents or neighbors; C04's prior-law
fallback and return to local control still govern national intervention.

Service law separates the elected body's rule and budget, the authority that
secures capacity, the provider's professional work, safety regulation and the
individual rights reviewer. The same office must not be the final judge of its
own service failure. Public health, school and care capacity must remain usable
when a contractor exits; the public authority needs lawful staff, sites, records,
funding and a transfer plan, not merely a contractual right to demand continuity.
The [sector-institution comparison](2026-10-core-institutions/health-education-care.md)
selects a public delivery backbone with regulated additional providers as a
working hypothesis, subject to its workforce, capture and cost objections.

Ordinary civilian protection is locally accountable within a national rights and
training floor. A national civilian service has defined cross-boundary and serious
official-abuse competence, plus a narrow, reviewable duty to protect people when a
local authority refuses or cannot do so. It must bring staff, evidence access and
safe services, not just issue a directive. Intervention does not automatically
dissolve elected local government; affected people can challenge both abandonment
and overreach. The [security review](2026-10-security-review/analysis.md) compares
this divided arrangement with central command and local autonomy.

## C05. Production, ownership, work and the gains from automation

The selected economic model is a plural mixed economy: competitive private enterprise,
cooperatives, public provision and commons where their purposes fit. The state protects
entry, contract, consumer rights and collective bargaining while constraining monopoly,
fraud and the conversion of concentrated wealth into political control. Bankruptcy
provides a lawful route out of unpayable debt; poverty is not a crime.

The selected [production arrangement](2026-10-material-and-resilience/production-work.md)
gives firms and cooperatives ordinary investment decisions and commercial risk within
labor, consumer, competition and ecological law. The Assembly separately funds a
bounded public-capability portfolio for essential networks, service capacity,
training, maintenance and supplier diversity. Cabinet proposes costed alternatives;
responsible agencies and public enterprises execute explicit mandates. Each public
enterprise reports its commercial account separately from funded noncommercial
duties, with professional management, worker voice, public audit and independent
challenge. Its board receives an open, competency-based appointment route,
published ownership mandate and reviewable removal; the appointing minister
cannot use informal instructions to turn its assets into party finance.
Neither an optimistic return forecast nor a lender's preference is an
appropriation. Concessional credit, equity or guarantees need a prior legislative
purpose, cap, loss account and review under C06; the bank-resolution fund cannot
finance industrial policy. More direct public production and more market-led
provision remain genuine alternatives, conditional on actual capability and power.

Ordinary credit is a private or cooperative risk decision under general law,
not a substitute for a social entitlement or a ministerial allocation list.
The [credit-and-finance decision](2026-10-credit-and-finance/decision-record.md)
selects regulated plural intermediation for the working design, with public
investment confined to separately appropriated mandates. It compares that
choice with lighter regulation, directed public credit and narrow public
payments. No amount of lending alone establishes productive investment or
better lives; asset-price inflation and household debt can disguise a loss
of real access.

Plural ownership matters only if people can actually refuse domination. Competition
and sector authorities should examine common beneficial control across essential
employment, land, credit, payments, public contracts and communications, including
contractual dependencies that a simple market-share measure misses. Lawful responses
can include open entry, collective bargaining, tenant security, nondiscriminatory
access, interoperable infrastructure, procurement separation, public options and,
where justified, structural remedies. Each response needs published reasons,
independent challenge, compensation where law requires it, a transition plan and
evidence that an alternative can operate. The state cannot call an incumbent public
monopoly a solution if the governing party then controls its jobs, housing or data.

Workers and residents need enforceable protection for organizing, whistleblowing
and lawful political activity, including routes to interim relief when dismissal,
eviction or exclusion would make later vindication useless. Employers and owners
retain fair process and defenses for genuinely unrelated decisions. Public services,
portable social protection and secure tenure reduce the cost of losing a job or
political patron; they are part of democratic independence as well as welfare.

Essential networks require public ownership or enforceable public-interest regulation,
transparent access and continuity obligations. This design selects the guarantee and
accountability, not one ownership form for every industry. Public enterprises need
accounts, professional management and democratic oversight; public ownership alone
does not prevent patronage or inefficiency.

An essential-capability strategy links critical services to trained workers, maintained
facilities, supplier diversity and actual domestic or importable inputs. It compares
production and replacement capacity with fiscal transfers: paying more cannot supply
an unavailable medicine, care worker or power component. The strategy protects
workers and ecosystems during change; it does not presume national self-sufficiency
or place all production under one public planner.

Work policy combines safety, worker organization, portable protection and support for
changing jobs or learning. Unpaid care belongs in service and time budgets. Automation
should expand people's capabilities and discretionary time, with gains shared through
wages and bargaining, taxation, services and returns on actual public assets. Neither
permanent full employment nor a post-work abundance economy is assumed.

Workers receive enforceable information, consultation and independent bargaining
over pay, conditions, safety and consequential automation or closure, with a route
to negotiate paid learning and redeployment. Employers retain lawful investment
decisions and fair process; a worker's voice is not selected by management, a
public owner or AI. Small, temporary and care-constrained workers need practical
representation and interim relief against retaliation. Training requires time,
qualified teachers, replacement care and funds; a new credential is not evidence
that a displaced worker obtained a viable job. Bankruptcy, a public-enterprise
change or provider failure carries accrued wages, pensions, contracts and service
claims to lawful successors rather than silently charging the social floor.

The selected [time-and-care settlement](2026-10-democracy-and-time/time-and-care.md)
adds an enforceable national floor for bounded work hours, recurring rest,
advance notice of material schedule changes, limits on compulsory availability,
paid annual and medical leave, and protection from retaliation for using them.
Independent bargaining and sector agreements can improve the floor and arrange
different safe patterns; essential shift coverage needs planned qualified
replacements and recovery time, not serial emergency exceptions. No universal
hour limit or leave amount is inferred from an AI model. The September labor
candidate's numeric hours, rest and annual-leave provisions remain provisional
predecessor proposals requiring fresh legal and capacity review.

Portable care-leave income covers enacted child and dependent-care circumstances
without assuming that a relative must provide unpaid care. A lawful combination
of employer/worker insurance contributions and explicit public support finances
the benefit; employers protect eligible jobs and maintain fair process, while
self-employed people need a separate portable income route rather than a fictive
reinstatement right. People outside paid work retain distinct care and income
claims. Care recipients, paid care workers and relatives keep separate autonomy
and rights. Inspectors and independent adjudicators must remedy actual hours,
denied leave, underpayment and retaliation in time to preserve a job or care
arrangement. A nominally shorter workweek or more leave on paper is not evidence
that people gained safe discretionary time.

The reference package prioritizes universal essential services and an income-security
floor, including timely emergency support. A legislated needs basket and coordinated
tax/benefit schedules must address housing, disability, household composition and
usable resources without assuming compulsory family support. Preserve portable
insurance/pension claims and accessible correction of benefit errors. Combined tax
and benefit withdrawal must be assessed together rather than hiding new income cliffs.

An unconditional cash component remains open, with gross expenditure, net incidence,
service effects and coverage assessed separately. It cannot automatically replace
disability support or create missing care capacity. The
[national social and fiscal settlement](SOCIAL-FISCAL-SETTLEMENT.md) selects services
plus an income floor as the conditional planning reference, and compares retention
and an added universal payment within the same three-year account. It does not prove
one income architecture universally superior.

## C06. Taxes, money and a budget that connects the country

The reference revenue architecture combines progressive **individual** taxation
of labor and realized capital income; coordinated corporate-profit taxation;
a broad consumption tax paired with timely household relief; a recurrent
land-value levy; and pollution and scarce-resource charges. Inheritance and
large gifts enter a progressive transfer-tax route to reduce the ability to
escape the personal base by changing legal form. The Assembly sets
taxable events, base, rates, thresholds, relief, information powers and
effective dates in public law. It publishes expected gross yield, distribution
by income and place, interaction with benefits, administrative cost and
uncertainty; no rate or revenue amount is inferred from the older synthetic
budget. Existing lawful liabilities and private rights receive explicit
transition rules rather than a retrospective software recalculation.

Personal taxation uses individual filing so a person's work is not made
dependent on a spouse's income, with accessible credits or benefits for
disability, children and care rather than an assumed unpaid household worker.
Withholding is provisional; an independently challengeable final assessment
reconciles self-employment, cross-border income and realized gains. Corporate
tax applies to genuine net profit with published treatment of losses, debt,
capital costs and related-party transactions. Its interaction with personal
capital tax is examined to avoid both artificial double charging and a
zero-tax conversion of labor income into dividends. Cross-border cooperation
and information exchange require lawful treaties and privacy safeguards;
an AI risk score cannot itself prove evasion or impose a penalty.

Consumption-tax relief reaches eligible households **before or alongside**
the tax burden through a funded, accessible payment route. If reliable cash
delivery cannot be shown, the legislature retains narrow essential-goods
relief or another lawful protection rather than assuming a future rebate has
arrived. The Assembly publishes the combined tax, transfer and service
incidence, including people outside formal payroll or digital accounts;
exemptions and reduced rates have their own fiscal cost. Essential health,
education and emergency access cannot depend on a user fee imposed as a
substitute for an unfunded national guarantee.

National law defines the land-value base, independent valuation cycle,
notice, evidence access and appeal. Elected local councils set a rate within
statutory bounds and keep an enacted share for local duties; national grants
and equalization fund the common rights floor. A cash-poor owner may obtain
a bounded deferral secured against later transfer rather than immediate loss
of a home, with tenant protections and published fiscal exposure. Planning
decisions, public infrastructure and ecological restrictions can change land
value, so valuation and exemptions cannot be left to the same local patron
who awards permits. The levy does not prove that dwellings are built, rents
fall or historic land claims are settled. Pollution charges and public
resource rents disclose ecological effects and who bears the price; essential
users and affected workers need funded protection and transition. Declining
emissions revenue is not a permanently growing funding base. Asset sales,
unrealized gains and speculative AI savings are not recurring cash.

The national legislature assigns each tax base and shared-revenue rule;
local councils may vary only delegated rates under recorded local votes.
Equalization compares standardized service need with **potential** own-source
capacity under a representative rate, avoiding a grant reward for deliberate
under-collection while preserving room for local choices. Its formula includes
population, access geography and objectively evidenced service costs, with
privacy-protected source checks, appeal, regular revision and a transition
against abrupt grant loss. A locality keeps a defined marginal share of
additional lawful revenue; no hidden full clawback removes the incentive to
improve its tax base. National government remains responsible for financing
its enacted minimum service duties when local capacity fails. A revenue shock
triggers disclosed temporary stabilization and an Assembly funding decision,
not automatic local service cuts or unlimited national credit.

The revenue authority administers taxes under a taxpayer charter: clear
notice, accessible filing and assistance, proportionate information demands,
confidentiality, reasoned assessments, correction, independent appeal and
timely refund. Enforcement targets legal liability, not political dissent;
criminal fraud requires ordinary proof and defense. Tax expenditures,
settlements, arrears, refunds, collection costs and material beneficial-owner
risks enter audited public accounts without exposing protected individual
records. The authority's appointment, budget, data access and audit are
designed so cabinet, a dominant firm or one vendor cannot both select targets
and finally adjudicate its own assessments. The [tax and local-finance decision](2026-10-tax-and-fiscal-federalism/decision-record.md)
records the rival packages, rights limits and unresolved incidence.

The elected Assembly authorizes the consolidated budget. Treasury executes it; separate
audit and legislative scrutiny examine actual payment and delivery. Central, local and
controlled public entities share one account of taxes, transfers, expenditure, liabilities,
guarantees and public assets. Internal transfers must not be counted as new national funds.

The account keeps source transactions with the responsible revenue, Treasury,
bank, service and local custodians. A separate C11 public evidence service makes
official cash, workforce and physical series comparable, publishes methods,
uncertainty and missing coverage, and preserves earlier versions. It cannot
turn an estimate into spendable cash or change a legal entitlement. Audit,
opposition and affected people need lawful access to challenge both source
suppression and misleading aggregation; protected evidence access and the
service's routine publication require real staff and bounded standing funds.

Cash, qualified labor, land, energy, materials and ecological capacity are separate
constraints. More money does not instantly produce a nurse, home or power connection.
Each commitment must identify an entitlement, delivery authority, workforce, capital,
maintenance, due dates and remedy funding. Borrowing and automatic stabilizers are
available only under adopted fiscal rules and actual financing conditions.

The **conditional domestic-currency package** is the working monetary reference
for a future state able to establish it lawfully. It is selected to test whether
democratic fiscal choice can coexist with reliable local payments, a bounded
monetary authority and honest external constraints; it is not proof that issuing
money improves welfare or makes any budget affordable. The earlier
[external-currency package](2026-10-operating-settlement/economic-control.md)
remains a coherent alternative and stress branch, especially where founding
capacity or public confidence in a new unit is absent. Neither package is
enacted, and a real adopting public must choose before claiming readiness.

Under this reference, the Assembly creates the unit of account and legal tender,
enacts the monetary authority's price-stability and employment-support mandate,
sets its powers and risk limits, and retains tax, spending, debt and guarantee
decisions. The authority operates settlement and monetary instruments within that
law without case-by-case cabinet orders. Its staggered leadership is selected
through public qualifications, cross-party scrutiny and recorded Assembly
confirmation; removal is limited to stated cause with independent review.
Audited accounts, decisions, votes, risk exposures and transactions with Treasury
are published with narrowly justified delays. The Assembly may change the
general mandate prospectively through law but cannot order an undisclosed loan
to allies or treat operational independence as a veto over elected budgets.

Treasury has no automatic overdraft or unlimited direct monetary financing.
Any temporary central-bank credit to government needs a prior statute specifying
purpose, ceiling, maturity, pricing, loss bearer and Assembly reporting; a new
facility or extension needs another public vote. The monetary authority may
provide time-limited, collateralized liquidity to banks meeting statutory
solvency criteria, with published eligibility and an exposure ceiling. A bank
whose solvency is not established cannot receive that facility pending prompt
assessment; a bank found nonviable enters the separate resolution route. A
central-bank loan cannot disguise a grant to shareholders or a taxpayer bailout.
Fiscal indemnity or recapitalization beyond the enacted ceiling needs Assembly
authorization. These are proposed boundaries, not assurances against inflation,
political pressure, capture or a mistaken solvency assessment.

The Assembly must enact tax-payment finality, deposit coverage, bank-loss order,
a bounded resolution and insurance fund, Treasury cash custody, payment continuity
and the maximum public risk *before* a failure. The revenue authority assesses
and collects under reviewable law and records when
a taxpayer's obligation is discharged; a failed intermediary cannot make the
state collect a legally settled tax twice. Treasury distinguishes assessments,
bank claims and usable received cash. A prudential supervisor detects a bank's
nonviability under statutory criteria; a separate resolution authority gives
written, reviewable reasons and executes the enacted transfer, bridge or wind-down.
Owners and creditors bear losses in the lawful order; covered depositors receive
only the protection actually authorized and credibly financed. Payment operators
maintain tested alternate and assisted routes for essential transactions.
Supervisors, receivers, insurers, monetary officials and vendors cannot create
a new public guarantee or tax. The [monetary-architecture decision](2026-10-monetary-architecture/decision-record.md)
compares the two complete packages and identifies their migration conditions.

The selected [credit-and-finance architecture](2026-10-credit-and-finance/decision-record.md)
links normal lending to that failure regime. Payments, savings, investment and
credit carry different claims and risks; a resident must know whether a balance
is an insured deposit, safeguarded client asset, investment at risk or debt.
Basic lawful payment access needs a usable public or regulated provider route
separate from acceptance of a loan, political profiling or an AI account.
Credit is not a general entitlement: lenders may decline a loan for evidenced
legitimate reasons including repayment risk, while discriminatory exclusion,
deceptive terms and a provider's control of the only payment channel remain
reviewable. Public development loans, guarantees and preferential rates
require the C05/C06 legislative mandate,
subsidy and expected-loss account; the deposit insurer and central-bank
liquidity window cannot be used to conceal them.

Banks bear prior-law capital, liquidity, governance, concentration and
connected-party limits proportionate to their activities. Nonbank lenders,
funds, insurers and platform intermediaries are supervised for the risks they
actually create, especially leverage, short funding of long assets, promises
of redemption, bank exposures and common controllers. Moving a loan to an
affiliate or labeling it a token does not remove a material risk from the
perimeter. Supervisors can obtain lawful source evidence, including exposures
that are not visible on a bank's balance sheet, with privacy and independent
challenge. They publish aggregate credit, arrears, leverage, concentration,
housing exposure and contingent public support; uncertainty and unobserved
nonbank activity remain explicit. One asset-price model or AI score is neither
a solvency finding nor a command to approve or deny an individual's credit.

The Assembly establishes a financial-stability mandate and enumerates the
permitted system-wide tools, bounds, decision rule, review and expiry. A
statutory financial-stability council with one voting member from each of
the monetary authority, prudential supervisor and financial markets and conduct supervisor
may, by recorded majority, activate a specified, time-bounded capital,
liquidity, exposure or borrower-risk tool
for a stated systemic risk; the competent supervisor applies it to regulated
entities. Prior law supplies conflict and vacancy substitutes; when the
council cannot lawfully decide, sector supervisors retain only their own
existing powers. Treasury supplies fiscal facts but has no council vote or
veto over publication or prudential action and cannot authorize a bailout
through the council. The council cannot set taxes, direct a named loan,
assign homes, change deposit coverage or suspend political payments.
Its ex officio voters hold qualified, staggered, publicly scrutinized posts
with reviewable for-cause removal; conflicted voters use predesignated
deputies, not a minister's case-specific replacement. A bounded statutory
budget and direct lawful source access cannot depend on one regulated firm;
industry fees and exceptions are audited. The Assembly may revise the
general tool mandate prospectively, not pick a named winner or borrower.
Council decisions show evidence, expected distributional and credit-supply
effects, dissent and renewal conditions, with Assembly
scrutiny, affected-borrower and small-firm hearing where time permits, and
judicial legality review. An urgent rule receives prompt later hearing;
representative challengers can contest its lawful scope without selecting
individual borrowers. A rule's emergency label does not make it permanent.
Sector supervisors retain ordinary licensing and enforcement; the council
does not become their universal appeal judge.

A financial markets and conduct supervisor distinct from the prudential bank
supervisor enforces clear total price,
fees, currency and variable-rate exposure, proportionate assessment of a
borrower's ability to repay, fair credit-data access/correction and
non-harassing collection across banks, brokers and digital lenders. Its
complaint determinations remain subject to an independent ombud or court
route. AI-based underwriting and intermediaries remain
answerable to the licensed provider; a score cannot replace a reasoned adverse
decision and independent challenge. Foreclosure and seizure follow notice,
court-controlled due process, subsistence protection and safe housing duties
where law provides them. Accessible restructuring and personal or small-firm
insolvency can discharge qualifying unpayable debt under prospective law while
respecting secured claims, workers, other creditors and fraud findings. Neither
a blanket debtor rescue nor automatic repossession solves a systemic shock.
Conduct review, prudential supervision, resolution and courts keep distinct
mandates, evidence and appeals; the same financial sponsor must not control
all four. Exact capital ratios, borrower limits, debt relief and protection
funding remain for a real legislature and affected public to choose.

Bank insolvency, payment-system outage, inflation or exchange-rate shock, and
sovereign cash shortfall trigger different duties. Bank resolution uses the
preauthorized fund and loss hierarchy; any new taxpayer bailout or change to
statutory guarantees requires an affirmative
Assembly decision. A payment outage activates lawful alternate and assisted
channels while preserving transaction evidence and due claims. If Treasury lacks
cash, it follows enacted due-date and essential-obligation rules with only lawful
funds, publishes arrears and who bears interruption, and presents lawful tax,
borrowing, reprioritization or specifically authorized monetary-financing options
to the Assembly. A receiver cannot spend the social budget, and Treasury cannot
use a bank rule or AI priority score to cancel an entitlement. If incompatible
nondeferrable claims exceed lawful finance or actual deliverable capacity, the
conflict remains public and legally challengeable; a court cannot create nurses,
imports or foreign exchange by ordering domestic issuance.

The default exchange-rate reference is a managed float without a guaranteed
conversion rate. Intervention uses only lawfully held, uncommitted reserves under
published objectives and audited limits. The external account separately traces
foreign-currency debts, import bills, correspondent access and reserve drains by
due date; domestic issuance cannot settle a foreign-currency obligation unless
the creditor lawfully agrees. Capital-flow limits or exchange
allocation need explicit rights-reviewed legislation, finite duration and public
incidence reporting, not a hidden payment-operator decision. A faster domestic
payment does not establish imported medicine or fuel delivery. Prior deposits,
wages, contracts and debt retain their lawful denomination until changed by
consent or valid, reviewable law with specified remedies; no research text
silently redenominates them.

Each budget and material midyear or framework-law change uses the selected
[whole-country commitment account](2026-10-whole-country-resources/decision-record.md).
For each existing duty, proposed expansion, maintenance obligation and contingent
exposure, source custodians record legal standing, affected people and territory,
cash by due date and currency, national/local financing, actual qualified labor,
land, water, energy, materials, imports, ecological limits, provider and remedy
capacity, lifecycle cost and uncertainty. Internal transfers, refinancing, public
guarantees and existing liabilities are reconciled; the same worker, site,
reserve or cash receipt cannot finance two simultaneous promises. The schedule
distinguishes **lawfully owed, authorized, cash available, physically deliverable,
actually received and remedied**. An unknown input is not zero, and an unpaid
obligation is not imaginary borrowing. Treasury assembles cash, sector and local
authorities attest delivery, C11 preserves source/method history, and audit and
opposition can challenge omissions. Neither those offices nor AI acquires a
policy veto: the Assembly votes on the exposed tradeoffs under rights and law.

The schedule also tests whether services can be used together in a person's day:
travel, waiting, forms, paid work and unpaid care consume time. Local access and
spatial plans identify shared sites, water, grid connections, construction crews,
transport and maintenance before counting several promises against one capacity.
Coordination, civil legal help, independent enforcement, safe reception and
cross-boundary planning require staff and appropriations in this same account;
they cannot be booked as free consequences of better information.

Housing and daily mobility have separate but linked capital and operating lines:
land servicing, construction and public guarantees; buildings, repair, insurance
and renewal; routes, vehicles, depots, staff, accessible stops and maintenance;
rents, fares, targeted support and national/local transfers. Private investment
risk is not a public appropriation; a public loan or guarantee needs a capped
loss account. Low rent or fare funded by deterioration is an unpaid future cost.
The same serviced site, road space, builder, driver, grid connection or external
currency cannot deliver several housing and transport promises at once. The
Assembly authorizes any new general subsidy or cross-local loss allocation,
while accountable local authorities commission and report actual access.

The time settlement adds actual paid/unpaid hours, irregular or on-call duty,
leave contributions and benefits, employer and small-firm costs, care-service
capacity, replacement staff and training lead times to that account. A worker
on protected leave cannot also be counted on a hospital, school or utility
roster. More predictable time can help people use services and join public
life, but it neither automatically funds leave nor supplies a replacement
clinician. The Assembly must face an explicit shortfall rather than making
unpaid family labor or unlimited overtime the balancing item.

The public-capability portfolio and essential-system account also identify each
water and power asset's renewal, safe operation and repair costs; food stocks'
location, rotation and transport; household tariff relief and nutrition support;
and imported parts, fuel and settlement currency. A low regulated tariff cannot
be financed by invisible pipe or grid deterioration. One worker, water flow,
backup generator, cold store or foreign-currency reserve cannot secure several
promises at once. Public, cooperative and private operators disclose common
control and critical subproviders; financial and physical accounts remain
separate. The Assembly decides lawful subsidies and scarcity rules while
independent inspectors test safety and responsible operators report delivery.

The same workforce account must show regional health teams, teachers, paid care
workers, defenders, investigators, courts, safe custody and community supervision,
including training lead times, travel, supervision and overlap during provider
failure. It must compare prevention, urgent protection, prosecution and custody
rather than funding the visible criminal case while leaving safety services empty.
Digital and non-digital public access, separate oversight, an alternate operator
and tested record transfer also consume funds. Neither a trainee nor a nominal
paper route counts as immediately available replacement capacity.
Public AI capability adds compute, energy, water, imported equipment, skilled
staff, source-data care, translation, independent evaluation, appeal capacity,
training, security and funded manual or alternate-provider operation. Put those
costs beside the nurses, teachers, care workers and maintenance crews the same
budget could fund; claimed automation savings do not count unpaid resident
work, hidden queues or a removed remedy as public gains.

The same account must fund democratic contestability: election administration,
independent investigation and courts, safe public information channels, and lawful
continuity when a critical private or public provider fails. A procurement exit clause
without a staffed, financed alternative is a paper safeguard. These costs cannot be
hidden inside an assumed efficiency gain or taken from essential services without a
visible legislative choice and rights review.

Budget review must connect public accounts to household and firm production,
investment and credit, bank liabilities, debt rollover and trade, while separately
tracking physical resources and ecological limits. An appropriated payment is not
available cash; a public cash balance is not the country's foreign currency reserve.
Treasury reports prospective payment failures, but the Assembly decides lawful
priorities and adjustment packages under rights and existing obligations. No
technical operator or central bank acquires authority to cancel an entitlement.

External borrowing, guarantees, critical imports, transport and payment routes
belong in that account. A treaty or cabinet side agreement cannot create concealed
debt, surrender taxing authority or declare a social guarantee funded when its
foreign-currency and material path is unavailable. Strategic reserves, local
substitution and supplier diversification compete with present services in the
same public budget; no unilateral promise secures a foreign partner's cooperation.

If a shortfall threatens delivery, responsible agencies disclose it before interruption
and use only existing lawful contingency powers. Cabinet must present revenue, borrowing
and reallocation alternatives with their distribution and service effects; Assembly
decides, subject to rights and independent remedies. Treasury executes lawful decisions
and cannot quietly rewrite eligibility. New expansion depends on funded delivery
capacity, while existing claims survive shortage and institutional change. A missing
resource cannot be created by a court order, and does not extinguish the right or excuse
arbitrary denial. Neither uniform cuts nor compulsory family labor is the default.
An enacted but future-start service must name its start condition, interim protection,
responsible authority and public review date. A minister cannot hold it indefinitely
under an undefined “readiness” label; a missed legal due date is reported as an
unfulfilled duty unless valid law changes it. A new discretionary project may be
prepared in a bounded phase when full delivery capacity is absent, but must not
be reported as an operating guarantee. Existing rights, court orders and accrued
claims retain their separate legal force regardless of a favorable forecast.

The proposed [legal settlement](AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION.md) resolves the **day-91
authority gap** by separating a preadopted standing schedule of essential obligations
from a temporary 90-day bridge for other existing operations. At expiry, standing
rights and their defined funding authority continue; the bridge does not. The
Assembly can amend the standing schedule through normal law, subject to rights.
This is a candidate legal mechanism. It does not establish available cash, staffing
or an actual day-91 service path in a real jurisdiction; unpaid claims remain visible.

## C07. A social floor that can actually be used

Guarantees belong to people; provision can differ by locality within national rights,
quality and accessibility rules. The state retains responsibility when it commissions
another provider. A nominal entitlement without a usable route and remedy is incomplete.

The selected national guarantee includes **effective access** under legislated need,
quality and response standards: reachable place and hours, accessible transport and
communication, affordable use, required support and safe continuity. An agency may
not count an offer that a person cannot practically use as completed delivery without
reasons and a challenge route. This does not promise every preferred provider or
instant capacity. When several valid offers conflict, the person may request a
voluntary joint plan naming sector duties, an interim arrangement and a deadline.
Refusing the plan does not forfeit any separate entitlement. Shared information is
limited to what lawful coordination needs; no universal family dossier is required.

| System | Selected design | Connection that must hold |
|---|---|---|
| Health | Universal resident essential-care package, urgent stabilization regardless of ability to pay, accountable clinical decisions | Staffing, medicine supply and waiting-time remedies are funded with the package |
| Education | Free accessible basic education; plural inquiry, teacher responsibility and pathways back into learning | Missing documents, disability or criticism cannot remove access; AI tutors retain human alternatives |
| Housing | Secure tenure, accessible housing supply, transport-linked planning and targeted affordability support | Cash assistance cannot substitute for building capacity; protection does not erase property rights or rent obligations |
| Care | Supported autonomy, child protection and dependable disability/older-person support | Care recipients and carers retain separate rights; unpaid labor cannot silently fill staffing gaps |
| Income | Portable social protection, assistance for uncovered essential needs and provisional emergency help | Assess usable resources, safe receipt, appeals and subsistence before recovery |
| Food, water and mobility | Safe essential supplies and accessible connections to daily life | Maintenance, reserves, local access and environmental costs enter the same budget |

For housing and mobility, the Assembly must enact the scope, quality, time,
affordability, nondiscrimination and accessible-remedy standards for the
national floor. A safe interim dwelling and a reachable essential journey may
be urgent claims under that law; neither creates an unlimited right to a
preferred address or door-to-door service. Local housing and mobility
authorities must show actual safe stock, travel time, usable hours, barriers,
cost and continuity before counting an offer as delivered. Independent building
and transport safety reviewers inspect public and private operators alike.
A landlord's insolvency or unsafe building needs tenancy, deposit, property,
creditor and safe-accommodation process; a transit operator's exit needs lawful
step-in, route and ticket records, accessible vehicles, depots, trained workers
and payment authority. Paper receivers cannot carry residents or house them.
The [housing-and-mobility comparison](2026-10-housing-and-mobility/analysis.md)
retains direct-public and regulated-plural alternatives and the risk that a
local public provider becomes a patronage landlord or excludes newcomers.

Water and electricity law protects an essential, safe level of access against
termination solely for inability to pay, with a fair billing dispute route and
an explicit funded tariff or assistance schedule. This does not cancel lawful
debts or promise unlimited use. Food access combines income protection with
physical supply and emergency in-kind delivery when money cannot buy food at
an accessible place. The public steward remains answerable for a safe alternative
after a licensed operator's failure; a completed transfer notice is not delivered
water, power or food. The [essential-systems comparison](2026-10-material-and-resilience/essential-systems.md)
preserves different operational forms for networks and food production under
one national guarantee and ecological account.

The housing and emergency-access studies now serve this common social-floor design.
Their case rules are supporting specifications, not separate national architectures.
Education, healthcare and care are equally central; the volume of recent housing
analysis must not determine the country's actual priorities.

The national schedule must specify coverage, need, quality, accessibility and enforceable
response standards together with its delivery budget. High needs require differentiated
resources. One authority remains responsible for making a remedy usable when a contracted
provider fails. Maintain accessible intake and independent challenge without requiring
an AI account; benefits and review cannot depend on political loyalty or employment.

Public, nonprofit and private providers retain professional obligations. Contracts
and budgets must account for high-need people, reachable hours, continuity and
people who cannot use an offered slot, rather than rewarding only easy cases or
completed referrals. Care recipients choose among lawful supports; relatives retain
their own time, income and autonomy and are not a compulsory workforce. Paid carers
need lawful pay, training, safe staffing and collective voice. A joint failure may
be reviewed across agencies, but the reviewer cannot excuse an individual sector
or create absent staff; elected institutions must fund or lawfully revise general
rules while unmet claims remain visible.

The selected [operating model](2026-10-core-institutions/health-education-care.md)
adds three different delivery chains under that common guarantee. Nationally pooled
public financing covers the essential health package without employer-linked loss
of cover; a national authority purchases and maintains capacity through regional
public clinical networks, with accredited additional providers under equal-access
terms. Independent clinical-safety review and patient rights review do not answer
to the purchaser. National need-adjusted funding and local elected education
authorities maintain public school places, accessible transport, buildings and
teacher development; other publicly funded schools follow equal-access and
curriculum-floor rules. National care financing supports locally organized public
and regulated additional long-term care; recipients retain supported choice and
relatives are not compelled to supply unpaid labor. Professional judgment remains
within law, while patients, students, care recipients, families and workers can
contest failures without taking over elected budget authority.

Payment rules must account for complex needs, continuity and fixed local capacity,
not simply visit counts, enrolment or completed referrals. A failed provider's
authority arranges interim service and transfer of staff, records and lawful funds;
safety inspectors and rights reviewers remain distinct. A closure cannot erase an
ongoing treatment, school or care claim. Whether this public backbone is preferable
to more direct public provision depends on actual worker, cost, access and capture
evidence; the reference choice supplies no operating capacity by itself.

Family support cannot become a hidden eligibility or labor test. Each person has
a direct, safe route to their own health, care and income claims, including when a
partner, parent or supporter controls the household account. A local child-and-family
service can provide housing help, respite and accessible care before a material
shortage is treated as parental failure. A child in immediate danger needs an
actual safe placement and competent carer; a protective order alone does not
supply either. A disabled or older adult can choose support and contest a carer
or provider without losing legal agency. Shelter, interpreters, legal assistance,
independent payment and substitute care enter the same funded service schedule;
the family's willingness to work unpaid cannot be booked as public capacity.

For each guarantee, the delivery schedule also identifies critical imported inputs,
payment channels, qualified suppliers and physical replacement options. It reports
legal entitlement, appropriation, safe supply, actual access and remedy delivered
separately. A shortage remains a visible unfulfilled duty, not an administrative
redefinition of who was entitled.

## C08. Everyday law, protection and justice

Civil law governs contracts, property, family obligations, consumers and remedies.
Administrative law requires public decisions to have authority, reasons, fair procedures
and independent challenge. Criminal law defines prohibited conduct and culpability in
advance; prosecution must prove guilt. Dissent, poverty, identity and predicted risk
are not crimes. The existing core criminal schedule is incomplete, not a full code.
Retain the constitutional prohibition of torture, slavery, secret detention and capital
punishment; integration does not weaken those protections.

Financial contracts also need a usable civil route: a borrower can challenge
materially false terms, data and collection conduct, while a creditor can
enforce a valid claim with notice, evidence and protection for subsistence
and third parties. Personal and small-firm insolvency require their own
prospective debt-ranking and discharge rules, not an AI affordability score
or a bank receiver's decision about a person's home. C06 assigns financial
supervision and public-risk limits; ordinary courts remain responsible for
property and debt judgments.

The selected ordinary-law architecture has a national civil and procedure floor:
legal capacity and supported decisions; contract and nonwaivable terms; property
and records correction; family and child protection; baseline work and consumer
rights; civil wrongs; insolvency, evidence, limitation and remedies. Elected local
law can add land-use, tenancy, work or consumer protections only within express
constitutional or statutory competence. A published conflict rule determines the
law and forum for cross-boundary conduct; an agency cannot send a claimant between
localities until a deadline expires. Courts resolve competence disputes with
reasons, not a power to write general policy. The [ordinary-law comparison](2026-10-civil-law-review/analysis.md)
preserves the existing manuscript's useful civil and administrative provisions as
predecessors requiring reconciliation.

The [personal-status and care decision](2026-10-personal-status-and-care/decision-record.md)
specifies how that common floor treats identity and family power. A civil-status
service records birth, death and lawful relationship changes with accessible,
privacy-protecting correction and independent review. Its record is evidence,
not an unchallengeable decision about disputed parentage, citizenship, custody
or inheritance. Missing records trigger provisional access to immediate services
and a route to establish facts, not exclusion or a fabricated legal status.
Consenting adults can form and dissolve lawful partnerships on equal terms;
the law specifies property, maintenance and child duties without making
marriage or a household head the gate to every person's vote, care or income.
Chosen support ties can help with visits and decisions under limited mandates;
they do not silently convey guardianship, property or parental powers.

Parents and lawful carers owe care and safety, while children retain their own
identity, privacy and supported voice in decisions affecting them. Practical
support and less restrictive alternatives come before compulsory separation
where safe. Serious specific harm or immediate danger, rather than poverty,
disability, migration status, family form or belief alone, grounds state removal.
An urgent protective action needs written reasons, the minimum necessary scope,
actual safe care and prompt independent court review with the child and affected
adults heard through suitable assistance. Continuing arrangements require review,
safe contact, a reunification or stable-care plan, and an executable remedy for
wrongful intervention or neglect. No agency may judge and audit its own placement.

Adults retain equal legal capacity, with accessible information, chosen supporters
and safeguards against coercion. Disability, diagnosis, age or residence in care
does not remove legal capacity, even for a single decision. A chosen supporter
or agent acts within a limited, revocable mandate and accounts for conflicts.
If a person cannot communicate a current choice despite support, prior will and
preferences guide any narrowly lawful protective act. Emergency medical necessity
uses safeguards applicable to anyone; temporary property custody preserves assets
without creating a general substitute decision-maker. Each act has a legal ground,
minimum scope, independent review and a way back to direct control. A person can
challenge their supporter and reach a bank, provider or court through safe help.
Relatives are not an unlimited, compulsory care workforce.

Domestic or intimate harm has a separate immediate route to confidential help,
safe housing, health care, protected payment and a temporary protective order
on stated risk grounds. The respondent receives timely reasons and a meaningful
challenge with safe disclosure. Such an order is not a criminal conviction and
does not decide custody, property or immigration status by itself; each has its
own evidence and remedy. After death, a lawful administrator accounts for the
estate, valid claims, dependants, creditors and pending proceedings under court
review. Neither an AI suggestion nor a registry edit distributes property.

A person or small organization can enter through accessible human or remote intake;
the first receiving service preserves the claim date and completes referral only
when another competent body accepts it. Advice, interpretation, accommodation and
civil representation are funded in proportion to stakes, complexity and power
imbalance, not reserved for criminal defendants. Voluntary settlement and
specialist review remain available, but dependence on an employer, landlord or
authority cannot force waiver of protected rights or a private forum. Courts
provide fair hearing, proportionate appeal and temporary relief when delay would
make a later remedy useless, with notice and protection for defendants and third
parties. Repeated harms may support representative claims only under defined
standing and affected-party safeguards.

A final order names the obligated party, act, date, lawful funding or asset source
and executor; actual receipt is verified. Enforcement against private property
protects subsistence and third-party rights. For an order against government,
treasury and the service authority must distinguish refusal from missing cash or
physical capacity and report incomplete delivery. An independent enforcement route
cannot create a tax or worker, while the person's claim does not disappear in a bad
budget. Pending cases, accrued rights, limitation periods and judgments receive
named successors when laws, local boundaries or providers change.

Court independence includes usable filing, counsel, interpreters, clerks,
case evidence and payment, not only protected judges. The Assembly enacts
ordinary jurisdiction, qualifications and a bounded continuing schedule for
core court, legal-aid, archive and enforcement operations. Treasury pays due
amounts on an audited route that the litigating minister cannot selectively
block; the judiciary cannot appropriate its own unlimited budget. A case is
allocated by a published, auditable rotation or draw after lawful specialization.
Exceptions and recusal have recorded reasons and review. Judgments give reasons
and permit proportionate appeal. Serious judicial misconduct has
a separate evidence and disciplinary process with fair hearing, while suspected
crime uses an investigator and prosecutor outside the implicated court. Neither
cabinet nor a court president may replace a judge in a named case for the
substance of a lawful ruling.

For a public defendant, a judgment identifies the officer and treasury channel
that must act, the due date, the evidence of receipt and the review route.
A lawful appeal is not an automatic stay unless the governing rule or court
provides one. Refusal can trigger only enacted, individually reviewable
consequences; a preauthorized substitute may release records or a due payment
but cannot choose policy, tax or staff in place of elected bodies. Genuine
cash or physical shortage remains a visible unmet judgment and elected
funding question, not compliance by assertion. If the executive, treasury,
records custodian and enforcement staff all refuse, an order alone has not
secured constitutional control. AI may assist translation, search or scheduling
with contestable inputs and human/offline alternatives; it cannot secretly
assign a judge, determine guilt or issue a binding judgment.

The selected [penal model](2026-10-core-institutions/crime-justice.md) gives prevention
and immediate safety their own lawful grounds; neither depends on conviction.
The Assembly defines prospective offenses, culpability, defenses and maximum
sanctions for serious harm or risk where civil, regulatory and service responses
are insufficient. Police investigate both inculpatory and contrary evidence under
defined powers; independent prosecutors give reviewable reasons for charge,
noncharge or lawful diversion; independent courts determine guilt; defense has
practical access to counsel and evidence. Torture and coerced evidence are excluded
under the stronger everyday-justice candidate. Custody clocks cannot reset through
transfers or a new administrative label. A report, risk prediction or failure to
prove an allegation is not itself proof of a crime by anyone.

After guilt is proved, the court compares a legally available no-sanction outcome,
restitution, a subsistence-protecting fine, bounded community measures and custody.
It chooses an adequate, proportionate response with reasons and independent appeal.
An alternative must not place someone under supervision who otherwise would have
received no sanction; inability to pay cannot alone turn a fine into imprisonment.
For grave culpable harm, including serious violence, lawful custody remains possible
when a less restrictive adequate response will not protect people or satisfy
proportionate accountability. It needs humane conditions, independent health care,
defense and family access where safe, external inspection and actual release planning.
Sentence length cannot depend on an opaque future-risk score or on treatment,
homelessness or administrative delay. Basic services and lawful supervision after
release need named providers; a completed prison program is not reintegration.

An enacted civilian corrections authority, separate from police and prosecution,
executes custody and lawful community supervision under court orders. Regional
operation answers to national rights and budgets; courts control sentence and
release legality, while an independent inspector can enter facilities, protect
complaints and require a court-enforceable correction. The health authority remains
responsible for clinical care in custody through protected professional judgment.
Before release, corrections and the receiving local service authorities agree a
funded, reviewable handoff for documents, housing, treatment and support; a local
shortfall cannot extend a lawful sentence. Neither a prison commander nor a
software record may postpone a release order. Whether a more local corrections
model could meet these duties with less concentration remains open.

Restorative processes are optional under enacted safeguards and cannot replace
investigation of serious or official abuse. Participants receive independent advice,
safe refusal and withdrawal; coercive dependence, threats and unequal local power
must be assessed. Refusal cannot increase punishment or remove support. Victim
protection, a protective restriction and child or property arrangements have their
own grounds, expiry and review, separate from guilt. A false or mistaken allegation
does not justify automatic punishment of the reporter; an accused person can contest
unlawful detention, overbroad restrictions and data disclosure promptly.

The same enacted rights floor follows coercive power across police, intelligence,
border control, detention, emergency units and contractors. Search, surveillance,
detention, force, movement restrictions, coercive denial of essential resources and
compelled disclosure each require a specific lawful purpose, authorizer, record,
limit, independent review and remedy. Changing a uniform, software system or
contract cannot create a new power. Contractors cannot receive sovereign
detention, protest-control or lethal-force authority by procurement. Serious
abuse by protectors requires investigators and charging decisions
outside their command, lawful access to evidence, protected witnesses and an executor
able to carry a court's order into effect. Fair process protects accused officials.

The [public-power abuse decision](2026-10-public-power-abuse/decision-record.md)
makes that requirement an operating chain. A person can report to an accessible
service, a court, an ombud or a prosecutor without first obtaining the implicated
commander's permission. A death or serious injury in custody or after official
force, or a missing detainee, triggers prompt preservation and notice to an
investigator outside the implicated command under prior law. A report of
torture, retaliation or evidence obstruction can be lodged through several
independent routes; the implicated unit cannot decide whether it is credible
enough to reach them. An initial evidence hold is distinct from a coercive
search, which requires its own legal grounds and court control. The first
custodian must protect life and preserve the scene, original custody time,
logs, body or facility recordings, weapons, communications and medical
evidence; it cannot retain sole control of the inquiry. The external
investigator has enacted direct-access and urgent court-order routes if the
unit or vendor refuses handover, with a recorded failure if evidence is lost.
Independent medical and forensic access, safe witness contact and defense
evidence access are funded. Privacy protection does not allow the accused
office or its vendor to erase contradictory material.

A case-allocation law gives the external investigator and a prosecutor outside
the implicated command timely authority over evidence and charging. Conflict
checks and a predesignated alternate jurisdiction apply if either is implicated;
the assignment and claimed conflict can be challenged before a court. The
same outside-command rules apply when an investigator or prosecutor is accused;
a minister cannot choose a friendlier investigator after an incident. A
reviewable noncharge decision gives reasons without promising a conviction.
Courts control warrants, liberty, admissibility, guilt and remedies; prosecutors
remain separate from judges. Prior law assigns judges and provides recusal,
reassignment and appeal for a judicial conflict; an allegation of judicial
corruption or evidence suppression uses an external criminal route and a
separate fair disciplinary process. Neither a prosecutor nor cabinet can
replace a judge in a named case or overturn a judgment through discipline.
An officer may be temporarily removed from weapons, custody or evidence
access on stated safety and integrity grounds,
with prompt review and replacement staffing, without treating an allegation
as guilt. The Assembly sets general law and funding and can examine systemic
failure, but cannot direct a named prosecution or acquittal.

The enacted criminal code must distinguish torture, intentional or reckless
unlawful official force or detention, deliberate evidence destruction and
retaliation for protected reporting, and corrupt diversion of public power
or assets from mistake and ordinary professional disagreement. It must state
conduct, fault, causation where relevant, lawful defenses, participation,
command responsibility and maximum sanctions before use. Rank alone is not
criminal guilt; a superior's liability needs a defined act, order, assistance
or culpable failure under an actual duty. Existing general offenses apply to
officials and contractors too. No emergency, superior order, outsourced
service or AI classification silently creates an offense or a defense to
torture. The older core code's numeric maxima remain provisional and are not
adopted by this design revision.

Immediate care, protection, record correction, lawful compensation and a
delivered civil remedy do not wait for criminal conviction; nor does a civil
finding erase the accused person's criminal presumption of innocence. A final
order names the liable state or person, payer, executor, due date and evidence
of actual delivery. Lawful recovery from a wrongdoer is separate from making
the injured person chase an insolvent officer. Audit and protected public
reporting disclose deaths, serious-force events, complaints, independent
investigation, charging, case delay and remedies by relevant group with
small-group reidentification safeguards, without turning sensitive testimony
into a permanent public file. Shared political,
financial or technical control over investigator, prosecutor, forensic source,
court and payment is a critical failure even when the offices have different
names.

Justice funding includes prevention, defense, courts, accessible reporting, safe
accommodation, victim and witness support, forensics, custody, community supervision,
reintegration and enforcement of judgments. A court order or case closure is not
evidence that a person received the remedy. Specialized offenses, sentence ranges,
civil gaps and service standards need reconciliation before any complete-code claim.

## C09. Land, ecology, infrastructure and resilience

Public planning connects housing, work, schools, care, transport and ecosystems.
Communities participate in siting and redevelopment; property and displacement remedies
remain enforceable. Existing owners cannot privately decide every national infrastructure
need, and national planners cannot treat affected communities as expendable.

Plans distinguish land on a map from safe, serviced and reachable land; water
withdrawals from ecological flows and quality; electricity generation from grid
and backup capacity. They record titles, tenancies, customary or collective claims
where applicable, existing permits, prior displacement, flood risk and uncertainty.
Affected tenants, workers, farmers and neighboring communities can challenge
alternatives while siting and scale can still change. The planning body publishes
cumulative effects, gain and loss, maintenance, mitigation, dissent and the capacity
each option actually needs. If councils cannot agree on a material shared allocation,
the Assembly takes the reserved decision under C04 with reasons and rights review. A court can
stop an unlawful plan; it does not choose the country's preferred density or water
use. No approval follows merely from missed time limits.

During a shared-area deadlock, existing lawful permits, property claims, service
and ecological duties remain with their named authorities. No new irreversible
allocation follows from inaction; an unmet housing or life-sustaining water duty
still requires lawful interim delivery, funding disclosure and an effective
challenge. The interlocal forum's map must include downstream and other materially
affected people beyond its member councils, with safe evidence access and dissent.
The Assembly's reserved decision cannot erase historic titles, tenancy, customary
claims or restoration liabilities without the applicable process. Repeated
national backlog or excluded communities is a reason to reconsider the territorial
model, including a properly founded elected region, rather than silently handing
the decision to a planner, landowner or first permit holder.

Housing approvals and mobility routes must be assessed together with serviced
land, water, grid, hazard exposure, safe walking access, maintenance and the
time and cost of reaching work, care, school, courts and political participation.
A new transit line that displaces lower-income residents or a housing project
without a usable journey can fail the effective-access floor despite adding
units or route kilometers. Plans disclose who gains, who moves, which sites
and paths remain usable during construction, and who funds continuing service.
Local permitting still follows law and property process; an unmet national
guarantee is not a blanket project approval.

Acquisition and relocation require a lawful public purpose, necessity against
feasible alternatives, fair review, compensation where required and practical
rehousing before removal except immediate danger. Protect access to work, school,
care and community ties, not just the property's money value. Historic injustice
and reliance interests remain contestable; neither ownership nor a new plan erases
existing claims. Restoration and decommissioning costs belong to the approving
decision. Drought, contamination and grid scarcity use preadopted, reviewable
continuity priorities for life-sustaining access and ecological needs; temporary
official restrictions cannot invent a permanent allocation bargain.

The selected essential-systems arrangement names public stewards for water, power
and food security without imposing one ownership form. A water steward accounts
for catchment limits, safe supply and the national floor while elected local
authorities arrange distribution through public or licensed utilities. A power
steward secures adequate network and generation capacity; the grid/dispatch
operator has nondiscriminatory access and published reliability duties, separate
from sellers' preferences. A food-security authority monitors physical availability,
safe procurement, reserve rotation and last-mile delivery while farms, processors
and shops may be private, cooperative or public. Cash nutrition support remains
with the social-protection authority. Independent water, grid and food-safety
reviewers inspect the operators, including public ones; no operator judges its
own serious failure. Every license or public charter names renewal, spare parts,
staff, records, outage notice, compensation and a funded successor or temporary
receiver. A lawful safety isolation or recall is temporary, reasoned and
reviewable; it does not create a permanent resource allocation or new tax.

Before a shortage, the Assembly enacts essential-access and ecological duties,
the method for temporary scarcity priorities, tariff/assistance rules and
cross-boundary competence. Life-sustaining water, food, care, sanitation and
the ecological conditions of future supply cannot be counted as available
twice. Operators report actual stocks, network condition, dependencies and
losses; Cabinet reconciles pumping, cooling, transport, fuel, imports, repair
workers and cash across systems for public Assembly decisions. A technical
forecast constrains feasible choices but does not allocate political priority.
If rights and physical supply cannot all be met, responsible authorities disclose
unmet duties, deliver lawful interim protection and seek a funded elected
decision rather than redefining an outage as completed service.

Ordinary health, utility, building and civil-protection law should fund hazard
surveillance, maintenance, heat/flood planning, accessible warnings, mutual aid,
safe shelters, backup power and trained repair and clinical reserves before a
disaster. Locations, stocks, transport and payment paths matter as much as stock
targets. Climate and ecological limits constrain production and construction;
local measurement must establish actual budgets. Carbon accounting alone does
not represent biodiversity, water security or all harms. The
[disaster-health comparison](2026-10-material-and-resilience/disaster-health.md)
keeps ordinary prevention and response distinct from extraordinary coercive
authority under C10.

Local water, land, ecological and network authorities keep original place-specific
observations and their correction histories. National public statistics disclose
how shared resource estimates were built, where observation is missing, and how
method changes affect earlier comparisons. Neither a minister's improved metric
nor an AI reconstruction can erase a downstream community's original account
or certify that an unmet ecological duty has been delivered.

Economy-wide material accounts should connect household, private and public demand
to land, water, energy, imports, waste and ecosystem effects. A service ministry's
energy line or a carbon price alone cannot establish enough physical capacity for
the whole country. Where imported inputs shift harm abroad, the external account and
affected-community evidence must show that cost rather than calling it domestic
success.

Future-generation and environmental representation should have access to information,
legislative hearings and standing for legal review, not an unaccountable power to govern.
Resource extraction and imports must disclose harms exported abroad. “Green” national
figures cannot conceal displaced pollution or uncompensated losses elsewhere.

## C10. Safety, defense and international responsibility

Public safety, disaster response and any defense force remain under civilian democratic
control, lawful command and independent investigation of abuses. Intelligence powers
require defined purposes, authorization, oversight and remedies. Secret agencies cannot
set the country's political membership or ordinary law. AI cannot independently authorize
coercion or lethal force; accountable human judgment and lawful constraints remain.

Separate ordinary civilian protection, independent prosecution, civilian
corrections, intelligence and external defense. Elected ministers set lawful
security policy and answer to the Assembly; professional commanders direct
specific operations within law. Neither ministers nor commanders may direct
a named prosecution or decide an election. Intelligence has no routine
public-order command. Military disaster logistics stay under a named civilian lead;
domestic policing by defense personnel is exceptional and requires separate law and
prompt review. A single cabinet command over all force is not treated as efficient
democratic protection when it can also defeat every remedy.
No emergency may transfer investigation of a death, torture claim or serious
official violence back to the implicated command or conceal its evidence;
staff and funds for independent review and urgent care remain part of the
security budget, including during a lawful handover.

For ordinary hazards, a named local civilian incident lead coordinates rescue,
shelter, transport and communications under prior law. Health authorities make
clinical and communicable-disease decisions within enacted powers; utility
operators isolate unsafe water or equipment under their technical licenses;
the national health and civil-protection network supplies bounded mutual aid
and nationally held stocks under published rules. Clinicians apply reviewable
clinical standards to patients, not a minister's preference. Police enforce
only a specific lawful protective order; defense personnel may supply limited
logistics under civilian command, not routine public-health or protest policing.
An expert hazard finding or AI forecast informs action but grants no new cabinet,
police or operator power. Material redistribution beyond preadopted rules returns
to the Assembly, subject to immediate lawful protection and C04's territorial
route. The same worker, shelter, generator or water reserve cannot be promised
to both affected and supplying localities without showing the loss.

Compulsory isolation, evacuation, access restriction and closure each require
their own enacted ground, necessity, affected scope, duration, accessible reasons,
reassessment and independent remedy. The responsible authority must also arrange
the food, water, medicine, income, childcare, communication and safe housing
needed to comply; an abusive home is not an acceptable default refuge. Workers
retain protection, lawful pay and a safe way to report failures. Restrictive
effects count across serially renamed orders, and measures end when their
justification ends even before an outer sunset. Narrow, hazard-specific and
rights-consistent measures can operate under ordinary law with independent
review. Broad restrictions that persist without derogating from protected rights
require explicit continuing elected authorization and review under an enacted
schedule. If a measure or series effectively suspends protected democratic
participation or seeks to derogate from constitutional rights, its actual effect,
not its ordinary-law label, invokes the constitutional emergency limits and
review route if that effect is permissible at all. The executive cannot reset
the cumulative clock by changing the sector, territory or name of an order;
non-derogable rights, lawful opposition and electoral succession remain protected.
Difficulty or multiple concurrent duties alone confer no exceptional power.
After the event, exceptional surveillance, procurement and
movement controls terminate under review, while claims, care, compensation
where law requires it, repair and independent public learning continue.

Use the democratic candidate's emergency architecture: a short initial executive period,
Assembly renewal, independent review and cumulative limits. Its proposed 14-day initial
period conflicts with older manuscript wording; the old blanket 30-day formulation is
not silently carried forward. Emergency authority never supplies a new electoral mandate.

Foreign policy favors peaceful dispute resolution and transparent commitments. Treaties,
defense commitments and guarantees require democratic legislative authorization and
disclosure of fiscal and rights effects. Refuge claims receive individual assessment
and effective remedies against prohibited return. Migration combines fair rules,
integration resources and protection against arbitrary exclusion.

The selected [external-relations approach](2026-10-life-systems-integration/external-relations-analysis.md)
is accountable interdependence. Cabinet negotiates material trade, borrowing,
security, resource and infrastructure commitments within a lawful mandate. The
Assembly authorizes commitments that bind future budgets, rights or critical public
capacity; ordinary cooperation proceeds within prior statute and audit. The public
account exposes contingent guarantees, external-currency needs, important suppliers,
foreign labor and ecological effects, lawful exits and continuity. Limited negotiating
confidentiality cannot hide taxing or coercive authority. A domestic gain purchased
through foreseeable harm abroad requires prevention, mitigation and a competent
claim or diplomatic remedy where this country controls the actor or transaction.

Protection claims need safe intake, translation, competent assessment, independent
appeal and funded local reception. A state contractor or third-country arrangement
cannot be used to evade prohibited return or basic rights. Temporary visitors and
new arrivals have protection and legal standing before they gain the ordinary vote;
long-term residence follows the C02 membership route. Neither cooperation nor
strategic reserves guarantee supply from an uncommitted foreign partner.

The [defense and war-power decision](2026-10-defense-and-war/decision-record.md)
selects a **provisional** division between standing defense and an expanded
campaign. Prior law may authorize immediately necessary defense against an actual
armed attack and protection of forces; the cabinet records the legal ground,
orders, area, expected civilian risk and resources and notifies a protected
Assembly channel within a proposed 24 hours. An urgent cross-border action to
stop an ongoing attack requires its own legal assessment. Repeated actions pursuing
one foreign campaign cannot continue beyond a proposed seven days without an
affirmative Assembly mandate. That public mandate states purpose, theater,
force classes, duration, fiscal ceiling, civilian duties, reporting and an exit
rule, with a protected classified annex. It expires within a proposed 90 days
unless affirmatively renewed. Silence or failure to convene is not consent:
standing territorial defense and safe withdrawal remain available under prior
law, but the executive cannot enlarge the campaign or renew its own mandate.
These clocks are research hypotheses, separate from the proposed 14-day
constitutional emergency period. Neither an emergency declaration nor a
domestic vote makes an unlawful external use of force lawful.

The elected cabinet sets lawful defense objectives; a minister issues a recorded
mission order; professional commanders direct operations within it. They serve
constitutional offices through an election, not a party, and cannot certify
results or veto a successor. Service members need legal advice, protected
reporting and fair discipline, including a route to refuse manifestly unlawful
orders under enacted law. Civilian courts retain criminal and rights jurisdiction.
Intelligence has defined statutory purposes and collection limits; intrusive
domestic surveillance generally needs prior independent authorization, with
only narrow urgent exceptions and prompt review. A proportionally constituted
Assembly security committee including opposition has independent legal and
technical support, protected access to classified grounds and spending, and
a court route to challenge unjustified withholding. It scrutinizes but does not
authorize war or direct operations. Outside-command investigation of serious
official abuse survives secrecy, mobilization and a cabinet change.

Applicable humanitarian law governs targeting, detention and civilian protection.
New weapons and material software changes receive documented legal review before
use and when context changes. AI may assist logistics and analysis, but a model
profile is not a lawful target decision. This reference prohibits autonomous
selection and engagement of persons without a human decision on the particular
target. A narrow defensive interception of defined weapon objects requires a
separate bounded legal and reliability review, a named human controller, feasible
stop controls and preserved logs. An unreviewable system is not fielded.

The reference uses a professional volunteer force and voluntary reserves. Any
compulsory service needs separate prospective legislation, finite necessity,
fair selection and nonpunitive alternative service for conscientious objectors.
The C06 account must include training, maintenance, medical and veterans' duties,
independent investigation, fuel, imports, foreign currency and civilian shelters;
one worker or reserve cannot cover both military and civilian plans unnoticed.
Major security assistance and continuing defense commitments still require
Assembly authorization and fiscal and rights analysis. Security procurement,
records, armories and command credentials transfer to lawful successors with
independent custody and access arrangements that a defeated cabinet or vendor
cannot block. Existing treaty, worker, creditor and harmed-civilian claims survive
mobilization and demobilization.

Territory, neighboring states, defense needs, treaty access and founding sovereignty
remain unspecified. This design neither claims land nor promises security guarantees
that no other party has made.

The executive cannot turn an emergency declaration into a new tax, a postponed
election or a renewed mandate. Assembly renewal, independent necessity review,
opposition oversight and a real expiry are part of the same proposed rule. Treaty
review and signature follow an elected mandate; AI can analyze but cannot ratify.

## C11. Knowledge, culture and AI as public capability

Protect independent science, arts, journalism, open criticism and culturally diverse
public spaces. Public research funding uses transparent purposes and contestable
selection without requiring ideological agreement. Schools and media must enable people
to question the state, including the country's founding design.

The selected [public-evidence arrangement](2026-10-public-evidence/analysis.md)
separates four acts: recording an event, constructing an official statistic,
interpreting causes, and making a binding legal decision. Election, revenue,
service, court, utility and ecological authorities keep the original records
needed for their own duties, with named custodians, lawful retention, visible
correction histories and successors. A separate statutory public evidence
service makes cross-sector statistics comparable, preserves authenticated
public law, budget, method and statistical vintages, and coordinates plural
evaluation. It receives only purpose-limited lawful access, not a central
personal dossier. Sector authorities remain responsible for the underlying
service and decisions; the evidence service cannot change eligibility, law
or budget allocations. Independent universities, professional bodies,
communities and media may investigate and disagree without its license.

The Assembly enacts record and publication duties, access and privacy limits,
remedies and bounded operating funds. Evidence-service leaders need public
merit criteria, open hearings, staggered terms, for-cause removal and judicial
review; protected professional staff and a narrow qualified acting succession
keep routine publication possible during appointment deadlock. These rules
do not themselves create independence. Review the shared controllers of
nomination, source staff, pay, servers, archive keys, courts and execution.
An opposition and affected-party route to inspect unjustified withholding,
with protected access for sensitive material, must be usable before a later
report loses its value. A separate operator or lawful protected copy must
make preservation and access orders executable through vendor failure.

Official methods and release dates are published in advance; material changes
state their reasons, uncertain coverage, missing groups and relationship to
earlier series. Corrections append provenance rather than silently replacing
an unfavorable result. People can challenge errors in their own consequential
records with interim protection; researchers and communities can contest
methods or suppression. Safe testimony and personal records are not made
permanently public merely to preserve an audit trail. National comparability
must leave room for local meanings and observations that a common category
does not represent. An AI summary records shared source families and its
interpretive role; many models repeating one altered source do not corroborate
it. A statistician, archivist or evaluator cannot become the final judge of
political truth or legally bind the public through a changed metric.

A plural information environment needs independent private, public and community
media, with visible control and material funding relationships for influential
outlets. Public media funding and appointments must resist incumbent retaliation;
their editorial choices must remain independent. Media concentration review and
fair access to dominant distribution channels protect opportunities to reach others,
not a state power to certify truth or demand ideological balance from each speaker.
Narrow rules against threats, fraud or unlawful coercion require reasons and timely
independent review. People retain private conversation and anonymous political
expression where consistent with others' rights; transparency duties focus on
institutions and significant organized influence rather than universal identity logs.

AI may help people learn, communicate, model alternatives, administer services and draft
law. Public authority stays with accountable democratic institutions. No provider may
control eligibility, political information, execution and the only appeal mechanism.
Public procurement must preserve lawful switching, usable data portability, explanation
of consequential decisions and continuity during outages. Critical non-AI routes must
be maintained, with their real costs included.

The selected [public AI capability portfolio](2026-10-ai-public-capability/decision-record.md)
gives AI useful but task-limited work across the country. Residents may use
revocable personal agents for explanation, translation and applications without
making an AI account the entrance to rights. Public authorities may compare
budget, workforce, land and ecological options using models that expose source
facts, assumptions, alternatives, uncertainty and dissent. Clinicians, teachers,
care workers, inspectors and legal advisers may use appropriate tools to find
options and reduce paperwork while retaining their professional duties.
Utilities and hazard services may use forecasts for maintenance and warnings;
resource allocation, coercive restrictions and force stay with the legally
competent human authorities. Researchers and communities may use plural tools
for discovery and expression without a government model certifying conclusions.

Each deployment has an enacted purpose, named public owner, permitted data and
actions, impact class, resource ceiling, expiry, incident rule, challenge and
funded non-AI or other-provider route. Exploration with no legal effect,
reversible bounded assistance, and consequential effects require different
authorization. An unattended AI cannot decide guilt, political standing,
essential-service deprivation, an election, a new tax or use of force. A
responsible officer's review of a high-stakes recommendation must include
time, competence, source access and power to refuse it. Neither a ceremonial
click nor splitting one harmful process into low-risk tasks changes its
actual effect. Automation within a narrow prior rule remains possible where
lawful, reversible and monitored; calling a system a pilot creates no power.

An agency must compare an AI use with a workable non-AI or simpler-software
method at comparable quality before scaling it. Report delivered access,
people's time, errors and reversals, excluded groups, privacy and dignity,
worker burden, cash and physical inputs, and who controls the information and
appeal channels. Model or vendor updates preserve exact laws, pending claims,
source records and correction history; they do not self-approve wider use.
The shared public layer supports replaceable standards, evaluation and lawful
interoperability, while sector authorities own decisions and plural providers
can challenge or replace one another. More model use is not itself progress.

The selected [digital-power arrangement](2026-10-core-institutions/digital-ai-power.md)
uses publicly governed, replaceable shared infrastructure. The Assembly sets legal
purposes, rights and funding; each service authority owns its decisions and records;
a technical operator maintains versioned exchange and authentication standards
within law but cannot redefine eligibility, rank lawful political speech or decide
an appeal. Data, procurement and sector reviewers, election bodies and courts have
distinct mandates and need access to shared suppliers and controllers. Their
appointments, budgets, information and remedy execution must survive the cabinet
or provider they review. A central public operator can itself be captured, so
plurality and succession must be tested rather than assumed from formal separation.

Legal status, evidence of a fact, authentication of an act and authorization for
a service are different. Exchange of health, education, benefit, police and civic
records requires a specific lawful purpose, minimum necessary disclosure,
inspectable provenance, retention limits and correction. Compulsory processing
cannot be laundered through coerced consent. A consequential deprivation of liberty,
subsistence, an essential service or the franchise cannot rest on an unattended
AI final decision: an accountable officer needs actual time, competence and power
to question it, give reasons and direct interim protection. An independent route
must let the person contest source facts, rules and interpretation without using
the same model or vendor. Assisted, in-person, paper or other suitable channels
have the same legal receipt, standing and effective remedy, with funded staff
and deadlines; identical screens are unnecessary.

Human, AI and joint contributors may propose improvements. Replication creates neither
additional votes nor an independent review. This design grants no sovereign franchise
to model instances; any future claim about morally significant nonhuman entities needs
separate examination rather than silent dismissal or automatic political power for vendors.
Credit data and underwriting models are likewise not a person's civic worth.
Lenders must be able to explain and correct consequential source errors under
C06; a shared model, bureau or payment operator cannot silently turn an
unpaid private bill into lost voting, public benefits or lawful speech.

## C12. Future collaboration without compulsory central mediation

People can cooperate through conversation, shared activity, demonstrations, supported
communication, personal agents or future media. These channels do not have to collapse
into text forms or one national platform. Local and professional communities can maintain
different practices while honoring common rights and shared obligations.

Future civic channels must be tested for who owns their identity, discovery,
translation, payment and record layers. Apparent competing agents may all depend on
one operator or funder. Public bodies must retain tested ways to publish, receive,
authenticate and challenge consequential civic acts across replaceable providers and
offline routes. Interoperability and exit rights must protect privacy and security;
an export file alone is not a usable exit if relationships, mandates or public records
cannot lawfully continue. No provider may condition participation in lawful politics
on accepting its preferred mediator.

Keep expression, interpretation, delegation and consent distinct. A model's summary is
challengeable; a prediction is not permission; a simulated consensus is not a public
decision. People can replace intermediaries and act without them. No universal life log,
social-credit score or public archive of sensitive testimony is required.

Separate local experimentation from changes to common rights, shared resources and
national authority. Experiments need lawful scope, meaningful participation, exit and
continuity for people who cannot leave. Forking a proposal cannot duplicate a budget,
erase a tenancy or divide sovereignty. Both informal stability and institutional change
are legitimate choices; redesign speed is not a national success measure.

Personal or collective agents require a revocable mandate stating principal, scope,
duration, resource bounds, reserved decisions and permitted subdelegation. Their
copies do not multiply standing. A signature authenticates an act under a method;
it does not establish understanding or consent. Shared technical standards carry
the legal effect of an authorized act without forcing every form of expression
into one data schema. People can challenge mediator framing and retain an effective
direct or different channel. Critical vendor or AI failure requires a practiced
alternate operator or assisted route that can receive a claim, issue a valid
receipt, protect urgency and reconcile records afterward; shutdown creates no
new lawmaking or coercive power.

A proposal may originate through speech, drawing, embodied demonstration or future
media. Before it becomes binding law, its operative terms and translations must be
accessible, authenticated and open to challenge for material loss of meaning. The
Assembly's exact enacted text remains the legal rule; neither a mediator's summary
nor apparent agreement among agents supplies a vote. People can dispute the framing
and replace the communication method without erasing valid commitments.

## C13. How the whole design works in a person's life

Consider a worker whose job changes through automation while they care for a child
and an older relative. Employment protections and collective bargaining govern the
job change; portable income support and education enable a transition. Care services
and protected leave make time to learn, rest, care or work usable. Predictable
schedules and safe limits on required availability matter alongside income.
Housing and transport preserve access to opportunities.
Healthcare remains available independently of their employer. Each decision has a
reasoned remedy, and the person can organize politically to change the underlying rules.
They may ask a personal AI agent to explain options and coordinate appointments
within a revocable mandate. The benefit, clinic and employer still issue their
own legally accountable decisions; a wrong summary can be corrected without
losing the claim date. The worker can instead use a staffed direct channel.

The combination matters: a school place, clinic appointment, care visit and housing
offer can each satisfy its agency's form yet be unusable together because of travel,
hours, accessibility or paid work. The person may seek a voluntary joint access
resolution without losing any separate claim. If local housing expansion needs the
same water, grid crew or flood-safe land as other obligations, an elected shared-scope
decision must choose and fund a feasible package. If an employer withholds wages or
a provider denies service, legal help, interim relief and actual judgment enforcement
must remain usable across local boundaries. None of those institutional routes can
manufacture a missing worker, dwelling, water flow or payment.

Now suppose the contracted care provider closes during a shared identity-system
outage, and a person reports violence at home. The local care authority must
maintain or obtain a lawful worker and safe contact route; the digital operator
cannot mark the claim closed or make a single credential mandatory. Health care,
safe accommodation and legal advice arise from assessed need, while investigation,
protective restrictions and any criminal charge follow their separate legal tests.
The same staff and budget cannot be counted simultaneously as replacement care,
hospital cover, victim support and prison health capacity. An individual remedy
requires delivery by named people and funds, while the elected Assembly must
decide any general shortfall without cancelling pending claims by software change.

Now suppose tax receipts fall while care demand rises. The same qualified workers,
buildings and funds cannot be promised independently by employment, education, health
and care agencies. The consolidated budget exposes the conflict. Elected institutions
choose lawful tradeoffs, independent courts protect rights, treasury executes actual
payments and service authorities deliver continuity. AI compares consequences; it does
not secretly choose whose claim disappears. This is the central integration problem
for the country, not another isolated emergency scenario.

If a flood then makes homes unsafe while a transit operator fails and a grid
connection for replacement housing is delayed, a carer cannot be offered a
nominally available home that cuts off work, care and a clinic. The local
housing authority arranges lawful safe interim accommodation and protects
tenancy claims; the mobility authority attempts accessible alternate journeys
with actual vehicles and staff. Land, water and grid stewards identify serviceable
sites, and the Assembly decides a material cross-local distribution after C04's
preparation. Courts protect rights and expose unmet duties without inventing
homes or drivers. This is a stress hypothesis, not a demonstrated fallback.

If the same household has a mortgage or small-business loan, lost income and
a payment outage must not turn a disputed AI credit record into eviction,
loss of the vote or disappearance of a benefit. The lender can prove and
enforce a valid debt; the person can correct facts, seek a lawful workout and
contest foreclosure before a court while housing protection follows its own
rule. A troubled lender's shareholders and creditors face the enacted loss
order applicable to that institution; any wider public rescue is an Assembly
choice with a visible cost.
The availability of a loan did not itself provide a secure home or viable job.

The first national planning comparison now uses one invented population and three-year
budget for retained capacity, service expansion and an unconditional cash supplement.
Under its assumptions, all three fit the reference financing limits, but retention
and the cash supplement leave growing service-workload gaps. The service expansion
also fails a revenue/need shock and a separate care-workforce shock. These findings
support explicit shortfall duties and qualified expansion within that earlier
scenario. They do not cost the later electoral, monetary, production, essential
systems, disaster, time/leave, housing/mobility or public-evidence commitments
together, so they do not certify the present design's affordability, staffing,
adequacy or superiority. Exact assumptions and results belong to the
[shared settlement](SOCIAL-FISCAL-SETTLEMENT.md); the consolidated current
account remains a required lawful-adoption and readiness test.

The same model assumes debt refinancing, bank payments and access to imported inputs
without demonstrating them. Losing a refinancing rollover can leave its service
package below the model's cash buffer even if the annual appropriation is unchanged.
The [political-economy review](2026-10-political-economy-review/analysis.md) therefore
adds bank, external-payment, production and physical-resource paths to the reference
design; it does not certify that any real country can operate them.

The integrated [failure assessment](FOUNDING-AND-STRESS.md) then connects delayed
election certification, a day-91 budget lapse, simultaneous fiscal and workforce
loss, a local service/AI outage, an emergency used against opposition, official
violence with evidence obstruction, and joint housing-credit/payment failure. Each
case distinguishes lawful authority, actual capacity and the point where common
control defeats the safeguard. The cases reveal failure routes; they do not
demonstrate that operating institutions will withstand them.

The eighth case joins armed attack, a contested election and a payment outage.
Standing defense continues under prior law, but neither disputed succession nor
an ally's request authorizes a continuing foreign campaign. The Assembly's
alternate sitting, civilian shelter, fuel, classified evidence, independent
investigation and lawful transfer of command must work together; an AI target
profile and a reserve entry on paper supply neither legality nor capacity.

The ninth case joins a disputed child-identity record, movement across localities
and intimate violence. It tests whether the child and carer can reach safe housing,
school, health care and money before a registry or parentage dispute is settled,
while an independent court promptly reviews protection and any removal. Preserving
the first claim date matters, but no database entry supplies a safe placement.

The tenth case joins a court vacancy, a budget lapse and executive refusal to
obey urgent relief. It asks whether an ordinary court still has lawful limited
competence, whether a claimant can obtain counsel and original records, and
whether treasury and a named civil officer perform the order. No missed
appointment deadline creates a cabinet judge, and no court can turn a genuine
shortage into staff or cash by calling its order self-executing.

Another cross-system failure is a dominant private controller that employs residents,
finances a governing party, distributes most local news and operates the public
payment interface. After an electoral defeat it withholds opposition reach and
threatens workers and service continuity. The design's response is cumulative
disclosure and contestable political-finance enforcement, protected organizing,
plural information routes, enforceable procurement exit and a funded public-service
continuity path. A court may order access or stop retaliation, but success requires
independent investigators, practical alternative channels, payment operators and
staff able to carry the order out. A public takeover without those capacities could
merely move the bottleneck to the incumbent. This is an authored stress case, not an
observed outcome or a warrant for emergency censorship or confiscation.

These examples explain institutional connections, not observed outcomes. Success must
be assessed through security, freedom, usable services, discretionary time, distribution,
ecological conditions and effective public control, including harms and missing voices.

## C14. Establishment and continual improvement

For a real country, establishment would require a legitimate territorial and membership
settlement, participatory constitutional ratification, an audited opening balance sheet,
lawful transition of obligations and functioning service/remedy institutions. Existing
inhabitants, creditors, workers and neighboring communities do not disappear because a
new design is published. A gradual lawful adoption by existing communities is also a
possible use; a wholly new sovereign state is not the only path to learning from this work.

The selected [legal hierarchy](2026-10-legal-hierarchy/decision-record.md)
protects the country's democratic and rights functions without freezing every
untested policy. A legitimately founded constitution fixes the electorate's
protected boundary, equal vote and viable opposition, lawful replacement,
rights and executable review, elected local government's existence, the
basic essential-access and ecological duties, and limits on emergency and AI
power. The elected Assembly specifies ballot mechanics, service entitlements,
provider and funding duties, tax and monetary rules, public evidence, ordinary
justice and local/shared competences through amendable framework statutes.
Annual budgets, local enactments, regulations and contracts act only within
those laws. A research edition or official statistic has no lawmaking rank.
The [proposed legal provisions A1–A14](AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION.md) express
the division for future legitimate review; none is current law.
The first Assembly cannot enact the election law under which it was chosen;
its exact initial ballot, count, dispute and handover rules need a legitimate
founding or predecessor authorization before polling. Later Assemblies may
revise future elections within the protected competition and timing limits.
For the C03 reference this includes N, D, a reviewable district map, nominee
pool and list-cap disclosure, the no-threshold quotient count, original-evidence
recounts, vacancy and repeat-poll rules. An uncovered list-backed territorial
plurality, tied count, exhausted pool or independent by-election must be
explained and tested before the first ballot; an election authority cannot
complete those choices after seeing votes.
The [founding sequence](FOUNDING-AND-STRESS.md) separates that first lawful
contest from later framework statutes and service commencement.

After legitimate founding, ordinary constitutional amendments may be proposed
by one third of the full Assembly or by an elector petition. A 1% petition
opens an accessible public drafting/hearing route; a final exact text signed
by 5% of the C02 electorate reaches a referendum without an Assembly veto.
The Assembly route needs two thirds of its full membership and a majority of
valid yes/no referendum votes. The direct petition route, which lacks the
Assembly supermajority, needs three fifths of valid yes/no votes; this guards
against an incumbent using a sponsored petition to bypass the Assembly
threshold, but may also obstruct a deserved reform. Both routes need at
least 90 days of public consideration of the final text, a clear ballot and
balanced, independently challengeable information. The reference puts the
ballot within 180 days of lawful certification, subject to a narrow
court-reviewed postponement. The Assembly has 60 days to publish its view
of a citizen text. There is no turnout quorum in this reference. These
numbers are provisional hypotheses, not enacted thresholds or proof that
ordinary people can meet them.
The charter itself must protect the petition thresholds, at least a one-year
collection period, objective verification and a court remedy; later election
law supplies accessible methods but cannot make a live initiative impossible.
The Assembly may publish an alternative, not replace the petitioners' exact
ballot text or use a simultaneous conflicting vote to cancel it.

The exact amendment states its effects on rights, offices, public resources,
existing claims and commencement. A court reviews procedure and whether the
change would defeat equal political standing, practical opposition, periodic
competition, executable remedies or peaceful replacement; it publishes
reasons and cannot treat policy disagreement as constitutional invalidity.
Changes to elections, tenure, appointments, review, emergency power or the
amendment process cannot govern current officeholders or the contest choosing
their immediate successors. The old procedure judges its own replacement;
a cabinet cannot call a self-authorizing referendum. A later system may alter
institutional forms while preserving those democratic functions. A genuine
constituent replacement is a separate affected-public and legal question,
not a research edition's or incumbent's automatic power. Petition,
referendum, review and handover need protected staff, cash, original records
and usable opposition channels; a paper right to amend is not public control.

Constitutional protection of essential access is a duty to establish, fund,
deliver and review a basic floor, with equal treatment and feasible urgent
help, not an assertion that a charter creates absent homes, clinicians or
foreign currency. Statute must name concrete coverage, standards, payer,
provider, interim route and remedy. A court can correct unlawful exclusion,
preserve accrued claims, order available lawful protection and expose an
unfunded duty; it cannot set a new tax or choose the nation's preferred
allocation. Elected institutions must respond publicly to shortages and may
revise future statutory guarantees through express prospective law within
constitutional rights. Appropriations, administrative categories and AI
outputs cannot repeal a claim silently. Material election and oversight
changes need prior publication, independent review and prospective effect
that does not reward the current officeholders' next contest; cumulative
changes cannot disable protected democracy by crossing several statutes.

The opening inventory must include ordinary civil claims and pending cases, land and
water rights, permits, ecological and restoration liabilities, cross-boundary plans,
external contracts, debts, treaty duties and protection claims. It must also cover
patients, school places, care plans, worker agreements, criminal cases and custody,
pending official-abuse reports, use-of-force and detention logs, forensic custody,
independent investigation and prosecution assignments, digital credentials,
keys, vendor contracts, legal mandates and protected records;
productive firms and public-enterprise obligations, utility licenses and asset
condition, food stocks, critical suppliers, workforce agreements and actual
cross-system fuel, water, power, transport and foreign-payment dependencies.
The financial inventory separates insured deposits, safeguarded client balances,
investment risks, loans and collateral, household and small-firm arrears,
bank/nonbank leverage and maturity mismatch, cross-border claims, payment
rails, insolvency cases and contingent public guarantees. A balance cannot
be classified as insured by its app label or a sponsor's assurance.
It must identify lawful source custodians, prior public-statistics vintages,
research records, restricted testimony, archive keys, retention duties,
record-correction claims and suppliers controlling backup copies. Neither
an outgoing cabinet nor a replacement vendor may erase evidence needed for
continuing rights, public accounts or independent inquiry.
New institutions need named successors for both the benefits and burdens of earlier
decisions. A plan to rebuild housing, a court system or a reception service must
preserve safe continuity while its staff, sites, funds and lawful authority are
assembled. Publication alone does not commence a service. The first
administration must map essential posts, vacancies, payroll, supplier
beneficial control, live tenders, amendments, performance evidence, disputed
awards, source records, subcontractors, exit rights and receiving capacity.
No incoming cabinet inherits a right to purge the career service, and no
outgoing official or contractor may hide a binding obligation or destroy a
complaint. Essential procurement can continue under valid prior law during
transition, with disclosed exceptions and review; a new integrity body is

The first lawful budget starts from that opening inventory and the C06 commitment
account, not from zero. Each service launch states which protections operate
immediately, which inherited claims continue, which new standard starts later,
and which actual residents received it. Any geographic sequence needs public
reasons, interim support, a review date and challenge; high-need places cannot
be relegated to the end merely because easy places improve the headline rate.
A missed milestone requires a fresh elected decision, while due claims remain
visible and enforceable within actual lawful remedies.

Before the proposed social floor can commence as an operating claim, a legitimate
public decision must choose a monetary and bank-resolution package, verify actual

valid currency and central-bank law, a staffed and audited settlement operator,
lawful Treasury accounts, defined monetary and fiscal exposures, known deposit
coverage and loss order, a funded and staffed resolution facility, and a practiced
alternate way to receive taxes and deliver essential payments. The opening

against the powers and resources actually available. If those conditions cannot
be met, the public may choose the complete external-currency package through its
lawful route and disclose its distinct reserve and correspondent risks. A legal

of existing personnel and contractors,
protected evidence and cases, and funded overlap of protective capacity; neither

The credit architecture needs its own lawful commencement: classify inherited

The opening assessment must also map effective control of land, major employers,
