Design 13 has substantial limits on AI power and digital concentration. It
does not yet tell a future government or resident *which useful AI capabilities*
to build first, who owns each task, when a model can execute rather than advise,
what resource it consumes, and what observed gain would justify keeping it.
The decision is a countrywide operating portfolio, not a universal model or a
technical procurement specification. The value premise is that AI should expand
people's usable time, understanding and collective problem-solving while public
authority remains democratic. Whether any task achieves that is empirical.

## Alternatives and selected institutional package

| Package | Benefit | Material loss or power risk |
|---|---|---|
| Retain manual/sector tools and use AI only in optional research | Minimizes model dependence and public compute cost; professional and civic practice remain familiar. | Can leave translation, complex service navigation, maintenance analysis and public option comparison costly or inaccessible where carefully bounded AI could help. |
| One integrated national AI administrator for policy, services and public information | Could share infrastructure and rapidly coordinate data and model investment. | Combines data, framing, eligibility, spending advice and appeals under one operator; a cabinet or supplier could become the effective governor despite formal Assembly votes. |
| **Selected: plural task-mandated public AI capability** | Individuals may use revocable personal agents; sector authorities own lawful decisions; shared public standards, evaluation and replaceable compute support multiple providers; independent oversight and offline channels remain usable. | Distributed responsibility can become confusing, expensive and insecure; shared cloud, data, identity or funding can still create one hidden controller. Adoption may consume resources that would serve people better as staff or facilities. |

The state would fund interoperable, replaceable public capabilities and allow
private, cooperative, academic and community tools within lawful roles. It would
not grant a single foundation model a public franchise, policy veto, case-law
authority or monopoly on civic communication. Each deployment needs an enacted
purpose, named public owner, allowed inputs/actions, risk boundary, expiry,
appeal and provider-exit route. The owner compares the proposed AI service with
a feasible non-AI or simpler software alternative at equivalent quality. A
government's AI adoption rate is not a measure of better lives.

The reference portfolio spans the country, but each use is separately scoped:

| Public purpose | Possible AI contribution | Decision owner and nondelegable boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Resident access and communication | Explain enacted rules, translate, help complete a claim, coordinate a person's chosen services or agent mandates. | The person controls optional assistance; the receiving authority owns the legal receipt and case; no account or model is required for essentials or voting. |
| Budget and democratic choice | Present multiple fiscal, workforce and material scenarios with assumptions, dissent and missing evidence; explain laws and proposals in accessible forms. | Assembly chooses law and appropriations; Treasury attests actual cash; election bodies protect neutral access; an AI forecast or personalized political ranking cannot decide votes or spending. |
| Health, education, care and work | Support scheduling, records review, learning aids and professional option search. | Clinician, teacher, care professional, labor inspector or lawful officer remains competent; no unattended adverse decision on treatment, schooling, wages or subsistence. |
| Land, utilities and hazards | Forecast maintenance and shortages, detect anomalies and compare lawful site or response options. | Operator may take only previously authorized, reversible safety actions; elected/sector authorities allocate resources; coercive restriction and force need their own human legal authorizer and review. |
| Justice and public evidence | Aid legal research, translation, discovery and source-tracing, including contrary material. | Defense, prosecutor, judge and evidence custodian retain their separate duties; a prediction is not guilt, credibility, sentence or permission to surveil. |
| Research and cultural inquiry | Help explore hypotheses and expressive forms with visible attribution, provenance and plural tools. | Researchers, artists, communities and funders retain freedom to challenge or reject a model's framing; one generated consensus does not establish consent. |

The portfolio has three operational classes, assigned to *uses* rather than
model brands. Exploration with no legal effect may proceed under ordinary
research and privacy rules. Reversible, low-stakes assistance may execute within
an explicit delegated ceiling with logs, sampling and quick correction.
Consequential recommendations or actions affecting liberty, franchise,
subsistence, essential services, substantial property, coercion or public
obligations require a responsible competent officer's genuine prior judgment
and an independent appeal; some coercive or political acts cannot be delegated
to AI at all. A minister cannot call a high-impact deployment “pilot” or split
it among small tasks to avoid the actual-effect category. An officer needs time,
evidence, skill and power to refuse the system; a ceremonial click is not review.

Before beginning, the owning authority publishes the purpose, legal basis,
affected scope, controller map, data origin, tested alternatives, expected time
or access gain, rights and distribution risks, cost and resource envelope,
human capacity, incident stop rule and expiry. Different controllers can test
the same task, but labels or model replicas do not supply independent evidence.
The public evidence service preserves consequential input/output provenance and
method vintages without retaining a universal private life log. Affected people
can correct source facts and challenge a model's interpretation through another
person, provider or medium. Privacy and security may require protected inspection
rather than exposing confidential records or code to everyone.

During operation, publish comparable delivery results: actual time to a useful
service, errors and reversals, missed groups, dignity and privacy harms, staff
workload, cash and energy/water use, dependence on imports and common suppliers,
and whether opposition and residents can still reach independent information.
The same public account pays for compute, evaluation, appeals, training,
translation, field staff, alternate operators and non-AI routes. An apparent
AI saving that moves unpaid work onto residents, hides a long queue or counts
missing staff twice is not a saving. Workers and users need a real part in
redesign; collective bargaining and professional judgment continue. Expansion
needs fresh elected or delegated approval within law and actual results; a
successful test cannot scale itself.

Model, schema or vendor change preserves the exact enacted rule, pending claims,
source records, prior decisions, mandates and correction history. Critical
operators test a funded manual or different-provider route able to issue a
valid receipt and urgent remedy during outage. Shutdown stops the AI task, not
the underlying entitlement. If a shared provider also controls public identity,
payment, civic discovery and appeals, the plural portfolio fails the C03/C11
democratic independence test despite multiple interface brands. A public
operator can be the same capture point; exit and external challenge matter.

## Evidence, objections, continuity and status

The [OECD's 2025 government-AI review](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/2025/06/governing-with-artificial-intelligence_398fa287/full-report.html)
compares public-sector functions and implementation risks. The
[NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)
provides a voluntary lifecycle risk-management vocabulary; NIST notes that
version 1.0 is under revision. The
[UNESCO AI ethics recommendation](https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/recommendation-ethics-artificial-intelligence?hub=1063)
frames rights, human oversight and the prior question of whether AI use is
appropriate. These are primary institutional comparisons, not endorsements
of this portfolio or observations of AIcountry. Claims about productivity,
accuracy or social benefit for any proposed use remain unqualified without
original local observations, current methods, affected-party challenge and
comparison with non-AI delivery.

Material objections remain: more AI may deskill officials, narrow human
relationships, consume scarce power and water, make civic knowledge depend on
model framing, favor people with better devices or language, and relocate
decisions to vendor staff. Plurality can increase security risk or hide common
ownership. Manual alternatives can be underfunded while nominally existing.
Conversely, excluding AI from useful reversible tasks could waste scarce human
time or obstruct access. No aggregate efficiency figure overrides a failed
right, excluded group or political replacement. A real public may choose a
smaller portfolio or different ownership after comparison; the selected
package is a working hypothesis, not an automatic accumulation of use cases.

Affected consumers include C03–C14, the public evidence service, sector
professionals, labor and procurement rules, local authorities, digital and
ordinary-law candidates and future agents. Existing contracts, worker rights,
records, claims and non-AI channels need named successors before any deployment
change. No earlier AI review transfers to a changed task or model. A model
update is not a legal amendment. The current research site and its publication
credentials do not gain a new AI operating mandate from this selection.

**Evidence qualification:** comparative primary institutional guidance and
internal stress logic only. **Research selection:** plural task-mandated AI
capability enters the country design. **Legal authorization:** none beyond
whatever existing rules already permit in the project; no state exists.
**Operational readiness:** unknown for data, staff, energy, compute, independent
review, alternative providers, manual routes and actual budgets. **Observed
outcomes:** none. Same-controller AI drafting is not independent public review.

The next bounded action for a legitimate interested jurisdiction is to select
one service task, document a usable non-AI baseline, original affected-user and
worker needs, lawful decision rights, compute and staff costs, then conduct a
time-limited, independently observed comparison with an alternate operator and
delivered remedy test. Its elected or otherwise competent authority must set
scope, budget and consent/ethics arrangements. If gains vanish when full costs,
errors or excluded people are counted—or if a common controller can veto a
lawful challenge—retain the non-AI method or redesign the task. This project
has no mandate or budget to run such an intervention.
