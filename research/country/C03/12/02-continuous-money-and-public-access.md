# PC05–PC09: predictable continuous limits and usable organizing resources

*Part of [C03/12](../12-continuous-political-finance.md); design110 candidate.*

**PC05 — A private budget cannot reset at call or New Year.** For each covered
private contribution delivered on date t, lock its amount divided by the lawful
I02 monthly ordinary-cash basket B(t) then in force; use the last lawful value
if updating fails, never an invented zero. Include actual dues, gifts, earmarked
purchases, self-finance and attributable in-kind benefit; public entitlements and
ordinary unpaid work are not private gifts. In the half-open rolling interval
(t−365days,t], one natural person's total contribution units across all covered
party/candidate/independent political accounts shall not exceed1; to one effective
principal not more than .5. A later basket or controller finding does not revalue
a past lawful amount or retrospectively establish punitive fault. Boundaries and
receipts are dated in elapsed days; no January or poll-call reset.

A donor may fund only a national/local/vote purpose for which they have C02
political eligibility at receipt. A local-only resident may support their local
competition, not national advertising through a party transfer. Mixed accounts
segregate purpose/source; eligibility is not inferred from passport or account.
Retain CF07/08's separate called-contest caps as additional constraints; one cost
entry may face both tests but is not two expenditures. Entity/foreign-state/
public-body donations and political loans/guarantees/forgiveness/>30-day vendor
credit remain forbidden in this covered scope. Genuine commercial invoices are
not automatically gifts; discounts, disguised patron purchases and actual use
need source proof. Valid old assets, ordinary arms-length sales, actual refunds
and asset returns stay disclosed distinct flows; no reset makes them new donations.
Their continuing wealth advantage remains a transition objection, not free cash.

No central prior permission to make a gift is required. Supply a free simple
receipt/cap worksheet and assisted alternative. Recipients take an attributable
eligibility/source and cumulative-total attestation with reasonably available
proof; no unrelated political dossier. A donor's concealed other gifts do not
automatically make a reasonable recipient guilty. Give five calendar days after
usable specific notice to segregate/return an unused excess, with source help;
actual use and knowing concealment retain their own consequences. Due equal
access/credit is not withheld on a source allegation.

**PC06 — Bound paid persuasion over the full cycle.** Fix each year's E0 from
the actually lawful1January roll for the relevant electorate; missing initial
roll requires an enacted reference before operation. A national/list principal's
annual reference ceiling is B0(6+E0/200); an individual territorial/local candidate's
is B0(1+E0/200); an independent electoral advertiser's is max(B0,E0 B0/1000).
B0 is that year's lawful1January basket. For each delivered paid electoral item
lock cost/reference-ceiling as a fraction. Sum these fractions in every rolling
365-day window: at most1 per effective principal/relevant scope. This normalized
limit prevents calendar/call resets and retroactive roll/index revaluation.
New lawful roll correction preserves rights; a frozen small ceiling cannot be
silently increased by an incumbent. Publish any resulting unused credit/cost.

Called contests additionally keep their fixed CF or PC contest ceiling. Shared
production, standing ads, subscription compute and staff are allocated by actual
use/delivery time and purpose; early prepayment does not move later delivered
benefit out of either window. No guessed impressions or prorated future services
are actual delivery. Ordinary nonpersuasion organizing/administrative costs are
reported but not forced into this persuasion cap. Strong limits can favor free
incumbent visibility and restrict challengers; actual costs can displace them.

**PC07 — Annual public organizing credits without incumbent seats.** Each person
may assign once per calendar year one national credit if C02-nationally eligible,
and one credit in their actual lawful local home if locally eligible. Each is
v=q floor(B0/(200q)), fixed on1January with currency minor unit q; zero v requires
prospective repair, not a fictional grant. Initial verified rolls plan source;
eligibility at a timely accepted instruction, including a protected corrected
first attempt, retains the full credit. A person first becoming eligible after
the30November ordinary close can use the next annual round; this timing loss is
explicit and does not delay their franchise or contest credit. No automatic renewal,
agent copy, party membership, prior vote or ballot creates another entitlement.
Accept assignments through30November; valid corrections of a timely request may
run to15December, with adverse notice within24h and payment within48h/earlier useful
time. A later new preference creates no fresh year-end grant. Blocked timely
requests retain due remedies; actual money is not duplicated without recovery
or separately authorized relief. Death/status change does not undo a lawful payment.

Cap one principal's annual public receipts at v max(1,floor(.4E0)) in the positive national track
and v max(1,floor(.4E0,d)) in each positive local track; aggregate controlled names, preserve
restricted local-only purposes. Source needs v times the actual distinct eligible
national-plus-local entitlements, plus separately priced administration/custody/
access/review. Annual credit is ADDITIONAL to called-contest credits. Unassigned,
capped and unspent cash stays/returns public; valid vendor/wage claims survive.
Unused annual cash cannot be banked as a new next-year public gift. Amount spent
is costed once under all applicable caps; normal contracted organizing/care/access
work is eligible, private consumption or loyalty payments are not.

**PC08 — Receiving is not permission to exist.** A person/collective can file an
annual receiving account with actual eligible responsible natural person(s),
effective controllers, safe contact, political-purpose scope and a lawful restricted
M05 or optional CF03 public fiscal-agent account. No1% signatures, prior votes,
seats, ideology certificate or discretionary party recognition is required for
this voter-directed-only money. Reasoned objective decision within five calendar
days; no serial-document reset, direct EA16 review, silence is not qualification.
The funded annual name/contact/purpose directory has one entry per principal
with an actually reserved/paid first credit instruction, not per unsupported logo.
A person may direct that first credit to themselves/another lawful collective;
no minimum private donation or incumbent approval. A free pre-receiving filing/
assisted lookup route permits that instruction before public listing. Entries
with current reserved/paid credits are bounded by actual credit entitlements;
price turnover and pending/corrected filings separately, not as200 entrants.
No automatic cash or broadcast slot per logo.
Association and lawful unpaid advocacy need no such receiving registration.
PC local/referendum receiving reuses this objective financial test with its
actual vote purpose/eligibility; it never requires a prior annual-credit
assignment or signature qualification for voter-directed cash.

**PC09 — Actual disposal and private choice.** PC07 credits use CF03/ER11–14's
separate protected preference custody, real segregated beneficial cash, alternative
paper/assisted instructions,24h fiscal-agent supplier payment, direct career payer
and court-verified substitute. Extend those powers expressly to annual/local/
referendum scope; do not inherit them by analogy. Principal/source totals may
be public; voter preferences and small-member identity are not. Price actual
receiving and the possibly E-sized annual directory/help workload plus actual turnover, not200 groups
or free volunteers. A booked transfer, vendor promise or unsafe monopoly platform
is not usable political independence. No tax/pension/service file gets this choice.
