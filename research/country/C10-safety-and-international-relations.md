7. [Atrocity prevention and control of our own force](C10/07-atrocity-prevention-and-legal-control.md), with actual command records, independent source custody, child receiving, human target decisions and no hidden detention authority.

10. [Civilian assessment and intelligence limits](C10/10-civilian-assessment-and-intelligence-limits.md): IA/IL/IE positive warning/data/source, protected careers/committee/courts and an explicit prospective exit from standalone noncriminal covert powers.

11. [Ordinary health and hazard protection](C10/11-ordinary-health-and-hazard-protection.md): HP01–HP24 targeted source/court/liberty, voluntary bodily procedures, specific place/general-rule/force boundaries, actual paid support and transition.

12. [Civilian defense and service](C10/12-civilian-defense-and-service.md): DF01–DF20 actual public employer/Chief, civilian appointment and source, no military commercial estate/command criminal forum, finite voluntary duties/exit and paid lawful succession; no new force/war power.

13. [Civil rescue and incident command](C10/13-civil-rescue-and-incident-command.md): RC01–RC33 local public rescue, accessible dispatch, bounded command, paid shared reserves, protected donor service and safe receiving.

14. [Commercial buying and responsibility across borders](C10/14-cross-border-buying-and-responsibility.md): BC01–BC20 own-conduct prevention/causal repair, reasonable linked-risk response, safe direct claims, express existing-officer civil functions and responsible exit; no blanket chain guarantee/import ban/new crime.

15. [Democratic treaty commitments](C10/15-democratic-treaty-commitments.md): TR01–TR12 exact elected approval, controlled provisional use and amendment, lawful external notice and funded continuity.

16. [Consular service and safe return](C10/16-consular-service-and-safe-return.md): CSL01–CSL04 accessible assistance, ordinary and urgent documents, host-country limits and funded return.
