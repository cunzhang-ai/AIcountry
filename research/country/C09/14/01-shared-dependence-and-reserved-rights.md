# CB01–CB06: ownership, residence and the shared duty are different

*Part of [C09/14](../14-shared-buildings-and-neighbor-rights.md); prospective ordinary law.*

**CB01 — A limited common-property association.** At a new lawful unit-title
registration with at least two separately owned principal units sharing a
structure or essential installation, constitute one limited statutory legal
person for that actual common property. Each actual unit owner belongs for
that limited purpose. It can contract, hold purpose-bound money, sue/be sued
and perform the specified duties; it owns no private unit by membership. Keep
actual common title/shares in PT's source records; an association is not an
automatic transfer of land or collective rights. Existing buildings enter only
through CB23's competent migration. Single-owner rental buildings keep the
landlord's own structural duties, with CB04 resident-use voice for shared
residential areas; creating a shell association does not outsource H liability.
Unshared neighboring houses need no association; CB19–CB22 still protect use.
Voluntary clubs/cooperatives may do more only through genuine lawful mandates.

**CB02 — Identify what is actually shared.** The authenticated plan/source
schedule identifies private boundaries, structural support, roof/exterior,
fire egress, shared pipes/cables/lifts, access rights and actual service groups.
A system serving several units is not privately disowned because it runs
inside one unit. Actual engineering function and title evidence are distinct;
court correction hears affected owners/occupiers, without a manager redrawing
title. Disputed responsibility does not postpone immediate safe H/technical
protection; preserve a provisional source account and later fair contribution.
An entry in a tax/AI/building model establishes neither title nor who paid.

**CB03 — Owner obligations and financial scope.** Owners owe their lawful
CB13 share of necessary common operation, safety/repair and qualified renewal,
including unsold/vacant units and public/commercial owners. A developer cannot
shift unsold-unit costs to first purchasers by its own initial rules. Liability
for a levy is the actual owner's several share, not every member's unlimited
guarantee of the association or another owner's debt. Actual new financing
needs CB08/PT and actual contractual liability. H landlords keep their own
nondelegable tenant duties; a failed association is no defense to a tenant.
Original fault/defect and actual indemnity claims remain B/TS, not presumed
solvent merely because a developer/insurer exists.

**CB04 — Actual resident voice.** Each adult18+ with their actual primary
home in the building on the published ballot day has one resident vote,
regardless of citizenship, ownership, rent/debt status, household position or
optional AI use. Use safe factual residence evidence and correction; no landlord
permission or minimum residence period. Joint homes are not one patriarch's
vote, and agent copies/multiple units do not multiply a person's vote. Support
children's voice and their separate rights; no parent consent to waive them.
Nonresident owners, shop workers, visitors and neighbors retain affected input,
source rights and direct claims, without being represented fictively as residents.
No resident roll becomes a migration, political or intimate-life surveillance list.

**CB05 — Everyday decisions within real means.** Residents choose fair
booking/access times, ordinary noise/waste/common-space practice and accessible
management instructions within law, actual existing shared rights and the
funded ordinary operating envelope. CB09 supplies the ballot. This does not
create new owner levies, buy assets or let residents cancel needed maintenance.
Owners decide actual asset/financial choices under CB07/CB08; publish resident
proposals/reasons and costed alternatives before discretionary budget decisions.
Refusing a luxury amenity is possible; selective defunding to suppress lawful
opposition or discriminate is challengeable under B/equality/H. H08's stronger
funded tenant choice of qualified management persists in its own operator scope;
it does not convey another unit's common-asset vote to that tenant committee.

**CB06 — Powers a majority never acquires.** Neither constituency may
authorize eviction, sell/pledge a private unit, transfer shares, waive fire/access/
repair, force domestic labor, ban lawful identities/criticism/association, expose
refuge addresses or subject daily movement to permission. Privacy/quiet/safety
rules require concrete reciprocal grounds; dislike of children, disability,
lawful family form, culture or political organizing is insufficient. Reasonable
access accommodation and practical alternatives remain duties. Essential
entrance/lift/water/power cannot be denied for arrears or a dissenting vote.
Actual limited temporary safety restrictions need their own technical/HP source
and receiving, not a manager's punishment. Common-area cameras/data need PD
necessity/source/access/retention and safer alternatives; no face/behavior score,
continuous home observation or new compulsory personal questions by by-law.

[LU04–LU05](../17/01-elected-plans-and-permission.md) preserves actual shared
property, covenant, nuisance, tenant and access claims. Planning suitability
is no collective assent, demolition/entry authority or resident veto over
outsiders; existing CB reserved rights and lawful change routes remain.
