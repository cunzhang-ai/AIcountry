# DP09–DP12: stop unsafe computation without stopping people's rights

*Part of [C11/09](../09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md); express narrow scope.*

**DP09 — A reachable material incident report.** A covered operator aware of
an actual or credibly imminent digital/AI failure materially threatening a due
essential, political, liberty, safety or protected-record function promptly
alerts its competent sector authority and the DP02 coordinator, no later than
four elapsed hours after that awareness; useful-time warning/action is earlier
where needed. Report original awareness/uncertainty, affected functions/common
roots, protective action, accepted receiving and update contact, without waiting
for a perfect root-cause report. Missing facts do not restart the first time.
Preserve unavailable-channel attempts and use actual direct/offline alternatives;
do not claim receipt by a failed endpoint. Users/workers may directly report to
sector, aid, AA/PD or court without the alleged failed operator's permission.
Separate PD breach/affected-person notices, TS safety and bank/election clocks
remain due; this4-hour report neither replaces nor extends them. No new cyber
crime, V penalty class or disclosure/search power is created by this duty.

**DP10 — Specific protective actors, no national kill switch.** An assigned
qualified operator may pause/disconnect its own unsafe task/update/interface
within its actual preauthorized operating domain/contract and minimum necessary
scope, recording source, reason, time and actual receiving. Independently
qualified minimum-risk physical automation may continue under TS05; an unsafe
human takeover is not required. Actual wider safety/closure, force, search,
network restriction or liberty needs its own sector/HP/LF/P/constitutional source.
Entitlement, receipt, pending claim and elected office continue even when unsafe
operation must stop before a substitute is available; disclose unmet protection.

Director coordinates technical warnings, original dependency summaries, voluntary
assistance and already authorized nonoverlapping mutual aid. No additional
cross-sector compulsory direction is selected: cannot shut the country's network,
revoke everyone's credentials, order a private device search, freeze payments,
choose a resource loser, suppress speech or replace an elected/legal decision.
An advisory severity label creates no emergency authority. Resource conflict
outside a valid preassigned scope returns to actual CP/elected law, not model
convergence. Sector owners keep responsibility and can challenge a false warning.

**DP11 — Restoration is a sector decision with actual proof.** The task's
competent operating owner authorizes scoped restoration on qualified original
evidence of safe source/configuration/records, accepted service, repaired cause/
containment and valid permissions. High-consequence operation gets the actual
independent sector review required by TS/PD/HC law outside the failed vendor/
implicated command; lawful predesignated qualified substitutes handle conflicts.
Neither Director's dashboard nor supplier's fix certifies every sector. Partial
unaffected restoration is possible on proof without waiting for a national
green light. No automatic restoration after a report deadline or unsafe forced
service because money/skill is missing. Actual courts may review unlawful delay/
scope within jurisdiction, not waive safety or appoint an AI sovereign.

**DP12 — Reconcile events, not just databases.** Keep original first receipt,
versioned law/mandate, separate data/interpretation, actual decisions, reserved
offline resources, paid/failed payment events and unreceived obligations.
Do not replay a legal decision/payment twice, silently reset a clock, reverse
an executed right by technical rollback or use last-message arrival as law.
Conflicting claims need accessible source-grounded independent determination;
a synchronization checksum is no consent/title/guilt proof. Existing lawful
priority, sector payment finality and C12 offline nonoverlap govern their exact
scope. Preserve safe proof of omissions/wrongful restrictions and actual funded
correction/receiver; a restored server is not restored care, access or public control.

[DP22–DP23](05-screening-queues-and-actual-decisions.md) applies these duties
to queue failures and follows the correction through to actual treatment.
