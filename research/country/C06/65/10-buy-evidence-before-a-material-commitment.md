# JC44–JC47. Pay for evidence only when it can change a funded choice

*Design528 · Part of [joint continuity and first-budget commitments](../65-joint-continuity-and-first-budget.md) · Proposed ordinary law.*

**JC44 — Compare immediate work with a bounded decision after evidence.**
When plausible findings about an access barrier favor materially different
uses of the same discretionary budget, compare an immediate lawful option,
an immediately useful common repair where available, and acquiring the
missing evidence while preserving an executable later choice. Describe who
faces each barrier, what the evidence would change, and whose burden waiting
or moving funds would increase. Compare the exact budgets on the original
population, caseload, legal protections and actual decision dates. Use existing
sector, local and national correction routes for due services. Neither a new
comparison nor a reserve may postpone an enforceable duty or remedy.

**JC45 — Fund the observation and the ability to act on it.** The responsible
elected body identifies the lawful limits and bearer of the costs of the test,
its independent supervision and each retained option. Price accessible
participation, necessary booking and standby, release and restoration, and the
resident cost of waiting. Protect existing rights and already committed work.
Use original recipient and provider evidence, including direct evidence from
people outside normal intake. A paid and safe exercise can compare full
receiving paths. It cannot withhold due protection to create a comparison.
Require a practical later start, a latest useful finding and an expiry before
making the reservation. Account for evidence error, selective reporting,
shared observers and common upstream losses. When safe evidence comes too
late, compare prompt repair or useful reversible work on the narrower scope.

**JC46 — Resolve exact options under the budget already voted.** Specify the
evidence and independent receiving authority for each mutually exclusive
branch. Use JC09–JC12 for an exact authorized release and BCV16–BCV20 for
any further elected act and its fallback. Publish the actual finding and
what held the other option back. Before release, verify actual authority, cash,
staff, recipient choice where required and the accepting operation. Set a
lawful default for a missing, conflicting or late finding, including an unspent
balance where no exact route remains qualified. A different scope needs fresh
assent. Resolve materially contested value choices by ordinary elected decision.
The reviewer determines evidence within their mandate. A model can compare
dates and constraints, but neither reviewer nor model sets political priorities
or changes an individual's entitlement through this procedure.

**JC47 — Keep later harm and new chances for choice visible.** Verify timely
receipt, household expense and time, paid work displaced and coverage of
people facing a different barrier. Before renewal, show whether the early
choice committed recurring costs or left an alternative executable, including
in a weaker budget. Publish delays and unserved cases on their original dates
and route actual remedies through the ordinary owners. Preserve valid contracts,
accrued claims and original observation scope if another option wins later.
Reopen on missed dates, biased observation, repeatedly unused reservations,
qualified ordinary repair, or a stronger rival with actual access. An allowed
reserve never establishes a right to spend beyond its voted scope or timing.
