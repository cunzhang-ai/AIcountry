# One country through ordinary life, abuse and transition

*Part of [C13](../C13-lived-experience.md); design 129 current authored paths,
not observed households, actual participation or a demonstration of readiness.*

The current country is a parliamentary democracy with plural production,
individual essential-access rights and revocable AI assistance. People can
work, organize, care, learn or decline optional activities without an employer,
family member, provider or AI owning every entrance to rights. These paths
join the present choices rather than treating an old scenario as current law.

| Path | What the design must actually join |
|---|---|
| [Ordinary life and usable time](22/01-ordinary-life-and-time.md) | Work, care, school, clinical routes, housing, money, privacy and person-directed help |
| [Adverse government and replacement](22/02-adverse-government-and-replacement.md) | Political entry, lawful evidence, separate courts, source/execution and handover alongside ordinary essentials |
| [Paid transition and competing countries](22/03-paid-transition-and-whole-rivals.md) | Retrofit, real provider/ownership choice, continuous claims, source dates, physical assets and future revision |

Use the same underlying people, obligations and resources when comparing options.
The hypothetical parent, child and older relative are not one legal interest:
each retains separate will, privacy, services, safety and review. A joint plan
is voluntary and cannot settle them by an aggregate household-benefit score.
No universal person dossier or compulsory agent is introduced by these examples.
