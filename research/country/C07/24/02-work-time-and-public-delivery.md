# CW05–CW08. Allocate the responsibility for incompatible schedules

*Part of [usable early care](../24-usable-care-time-and-shift-continuity.md).*

**CW05 — Repair avoidable time conflicts where they originate.** A public
employer arranging a predictable shift compares an agreed change or
swap, paid relief and a compatible service window under TW/WL and
actual collective arrangements. It identifies the staffing and access
consequence for the original service and any other workers. An
avoidable conflict should not be purchased away with a child's
unnecessary additional institutional time. No worker has to accept
an unlawful, unsafe or involuntary swap. The competent public body
scheduling an appointment or mandatory appearance must give a
compatible slot or usable alternative where its sector authority and
underlying duty allow it, or explain promptly why that is unavailable.
Earlier claims, review deadlines, election opportunities and urgent
protection retain their priority. Existing refusal, accommodation, notice,
bargaining, leave and expense rights apply. This rule gives
a provider no scheduling or pay power over an employer, and creates
no universal wage guarantee.

**CW06 — Resolve sudden changes before the safe handoff where possible.**
Keep the original request, the change and the earliest time action
is needed separately. For a foreseeable request use the existing
care and ordinary decision clocks. If timely supervision or safe
handover is at risk, contact the named receiver and start lawful
fallback immediately under FC and E. Work, transport and care actors
can offer compatible alternatives within their own mandates. If a
material employer change was made on short notice, the applicable
TW/WL agreement, refusal, urgency, pay or correction path still
governs. A care request cannot validate the change. A genuine urgent
change, late transport or unexpected provider failure is handled
without requiring the family to settle fault first. Interim
supervision, definitive cover, lost wages and damages stay separate.
If no safe and lawful option works, give an actionable explanation
and accessible independent review in useful time where possible,
retain unmet hours and downstream claims, and record actual delivery
or failure. Review or referral does not discharge the care duty.

**CW07 — Budget the actual window and its backup.** The local authority
uses existing open budget and care processes to compare ordinary
schedule repair, targeted extension, shared provision and home-based
alternatives for the same family needs. Consult children as appropriate,
families including nonusers, workers and supplying or neighboring
areas. Use same-period need, qualified presence, safe travel, rest,
price, backup and unresolved harms. The national gap includes the
funded eligible scope under FC and FB. For any change in public
contribution name the lawful payer, actual advance and accepted
provider before claiming it works. Keep late-coverage and standby
commitments separate where required; a shared substitute cannot
cover simultaneous uses. Apply DB choices and FB09–FB12 when
redirecting funds or claiming a lasting saving. Elected authorities
can choose the provider pattern within the duty. Missing staff and
late transfers remain visible under FC07, with existing review and
affected-party remedies. Keep wages, contracts, child relationships
and paid OP/T succession through any cutover.

**CW08 — Share intervals, with separate acceptance and authority.** People
may use calls, direct agreement, assisted tools or future shared
calendars to communicate relevant time and support needs. Employer,
provider, transport operator and family each confirm only their
own undertaking. The first receiver follows up on gaps and
revocations. Preserve consent, the reason for the version change
and protected evidence as needed. Do not distribute full family,
clinical or employment calendars, permit continuous location
tracking or make automated coordination mandatory. AI may find
candidate slots, explain reasons and detect overlaps. It cannot
admit a child, direct a worker, authorize a payment, sign for an
absent participant or serve as qualified presence. Only actually
received care counts toward delivery. Review complaints, missed
windows, child wellbeing, informal burdens and worker conditions
without penalizing lower optional use.
