## Coherent alternatives and retained losses

A: I05/0.4M allowance, essential-service guarantee, paid plural/public-residual
capacity and mixed repair. B: same social package, import-heavy/bulk. C: full U,
extra needs, first-unit other-income tax and equivalent duties. D: targeted
cash/prepaid plural essential capacity with more ordinary private provision,
including its uncovered/private/unpaid burden rather than free savings. E:
direct-public capacity with paid assets/management/renewal. Retain/no-change
and paid alternative context remain legitimate. Same old rights, needs and
adverse scopes stay common; changed policy effects are explicitly repriced.

I05 risks stigma, nonclaim and dependence; U has tax/service opportunity costs;
private prepayment risks thin-area failure/rents; direct ownership can concentrate
power and neglect maintenance. Annual full 0.4M allowance even for partial-source
periods chooses simplicity/entry-cliff protection over proration and may cost
revenue. A first midyear U eligibility receives U immediately and retains A for
that first year: price the deliberate transition overlap. No existing U waits
for a tax switch. None of these losses is erased by the priority selection.

## Received objections and actual dispositions

1. Root initially considered demoting the U destination after failed partial
   finance. Fiscal and material reviewers challenged that inference: IC already
   prohibits unfunded launch and I05 is not proven funded either. **Reject that
   inference and retain the preferred conditional U goal**; choose access-first
   next execution as an explicit priority, not a technical value verdict.
2. Reviewers identified PA/TA first-unit tax as dependent on actual U. **Revise**:
   SD05 selects T(max(0,Q−0.4M)) for I05, SD06 retains T(Q) for full U; align
   PA09/19, TA01/15, IC and NF source-year gross-up, overview/status and CX.
   Old-net/source rights survive; no full-U yield transfers to the I05 case.
3. The fiscal close found ambiguous midyear coverage/proration. **Revise**:
   one full annual allowance, explicit first-midyear-U overlap, no eligibility
   delay and next-calendar-year full-U tax transition under express enacted law.
   Existing nonreceipt uses actual interim protection, not fictional income.
   The final fiscal reread requires costing this as recurring future entrant
   exposure, not just founding overlap; the new annual examples expose that
   liability without claiming a complete within-year benefit/source forecast.
4. Delivery review challenged aggregate staffs/extra offices and shared protectors.
   **Retain** item/shift/controller allocation, known replacement and null costs;
   no new fiscal guardian or technical readiness veto over rights/elections.
5. Physical review challenged choosing imports by a narrow water pass. **Retain**
   both whole branches, paid external source/route/harm and grid/receiver tests;
   neither branch is certified and both may be displaced by better original facts.

These are actual same-controller AI rereads, not independent judgments, votes,
