# FP17–FP18: observed fiscal composition, shared risks and retained guarantees

3 November 2026 · Design139 · [National fiscal settlement](../56-national-fiscal-settlement-and-source-boundaries.md), not enacted law

**FP17 — Retain mixed sources, qualify their common dependence.** Keep NF/PA's
contestable personal/company/consumption/site/received-transfer sources with
actual lawful public returns, and Y's separately owned contribution/credit
funding. Reject personal-tax-only or assumed public-investment-return financing
as the default *complete source account*. This is a research choice about
burdens/capacity, not an instruction to collect an unmeasured yield or create
a new tax. Simpler consumption-heavy, rent-heavy, broader-tax insurance and
different ownership/monetary alternatives remain substantive rivals.

The [published comparison](../../../settlement/published-fiscal/README.md) supplies
one pinned Denmark2023 fiscal/source context with2021–25 companion series.
It distinguishes top-level taxes from their components, accounting revenue from
cash, expenditure purposes from transfer types and source vintages. A statistical
tax label is not this country's tax base, rate, payer, owner or receipt date.
The comparison neither finances the candidate per head nor selects an optimal
rate or a cheaper/superior national package.

Different revenue names need not diversify economic risk: wages, profits,
consumption, property and financial returns can depend on shared activity,
prices, institutions and external conditions. Assess joint changes with benefit/
service demands, refunds, private resources and supply; do not add independent
upside forecasts. A short observed series supplies no causal response or future
joint-shock distribution. Fund any proposed stabilization/contingency capacity
with actual lawful source and ownership, not a named but empty reserve.

**FP18 — Do not substitute fiscal categories for pension or service promises.**
Public Y basic, earned/credited units, current payment, restricted S assets and
private funded pensions retain distinct rights and sources. A foreign tax on
pension-asset returns is neither our S investment income nor permission to take
members' assets. Small social-contribution receipts in another statistical
system do not demonstrate a contribution-free retirement system or price this
country's existing earned obligations. Public Y/RF's prospective rules and
all earlier units/indexation/survivors/source laws continue.

An ordinary public guarantee is not a private fund's market-value promise.
Do not make due Y/I05/service protection contingent on achieving an investment
target or reserve valuation. Private DC losses and funded DB/employer remedies
retain NB law; this is no new public guarantee of every private investment.
Tax/source weakness requires the existing elected fiscal response and useful-
time protection, not an AI-imposed pension cut, forced withdrawal or new levy.

COFOG spending purposes, household transfers, public consumption and pension-
labelled groups overlap or cover different things. Do not add them as separate
bills, subtract private/family care as free supply, or map a foreign pension
label directly to Y eligibility. Treasury/sector planning must trace actual
people, periods, due claims and delivery; accounting totals alone establish
neither adequate care nor independent public control.
