# Democratic repair: ordinary delay and restoration

*Canonical RR01–RR04; part of [C14/08](../08-democratic-repair-without-self-authorization.md).*

**RR01 — A protected succession, not a disposable first poll.** Ordinary changes
to elections, tenure, appointment, constitutional review, emergency power or the
amendment procedure do not govern current officeholders or the contest choosing
their immediate successors. For an elected office, that protected succession
includes its nomination, ballot, count, litigation, recount, lawful rerun and any
formation-failure repeat poll needed to constitute that successor government.
A failed poll does not consume the protection. For a nonelected fixed seat,
current tenure/appointment and selection of its immediate successor stay under
the old rule. A later lawful new term is not a right to keep an obsolete rule
forever; related reforms and staggered offices disclose their separate dates.

An amendment states exact dates and affected cohorts rather than “after the
next election.” Unaffected prospective provisions may start separately only
where the approved text expressly makes them severable. No executive decree,
readiness opinion or mediator may decide to accelerate a protected provision.

**RR02 — A closed early-effect test.** Earlier effect is possible only after the
RR05 forum finds all of the following on the actual approved provision:

- A concrete barrier impairs equal political standing, practical opposition,
  meaningful public control, enforceable remedy or peaceful replacement already
  protected by the applicable predecessor constitution.
- Earlier effect is necessary to prevent identifiable exclusion or irreparable
  loss of that function before ordinary delayed effect; an adequate narrower
  existing-law remedy is unavailable.
- The provision removes that barrier using the least structurally disruptive
  effective repair among identified alternatives, including existing remedies.
- Everyone in the same protected situation receives equivalent access; the
  criterion is independent of expected political preference or party allegiance.
- Its effects, dependent rules, implementation time and actual receiving are
  sufficiently defined to avoid a new exclusion. Remaining unknowns are stated.
- It satisfies RR03–RR07 and the unchanged full public amendment procedure.

A new desirable policy, general efficiency, claimed AI wisdom or a majority's
prediction of better outcomes does not establish restoration of an existing
protected democratic function. A newly proposed franchise expansion can be
adopted prospectively without disguising that value choice as existing law.

**RR03 — No institutional replacement hidden in repair.** The exception cannot
extend current terms, reset term limits, add/remove court seats, alter current
appointment sources or voting thresholds, install/dismiss reviewers, grant
incumbent immunity, validate unlawful past acts or erase accrued remedies,
  narrow opposition access, alter seat conversion or district
boundaries, or change the amendment/certification procedure approving itself.
Such provisions take the ordinary delayed route even when packaged with a
restoration. A sponsor cannot compel an immediate mixed package by making it
nonseverable; it must obtain a new lawful text/authorization or accept delay.
Ordinary proved individual misconduct remains governed by its separate old law.

**RR04 — Access may change political outcomes.** A remedy such as accessible
registration, a usable independent challenge or restoration of an unlawfully
excluded class may incidentally help a party. That is not a veto: do not predict
how restored people will vote. Show actual legal entitlement and neutral scope,
not polling advantage or a sponsor's “pro-democracy” label. Administrative repair
under existing powers remains possible without pretending an amendment is needed.
An old court's error or hostility does not empower the executive to replace it.
