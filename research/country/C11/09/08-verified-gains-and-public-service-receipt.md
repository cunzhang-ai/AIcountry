# DP33–DP37. Turn verified AI gains into usable public service

*Part of [public digital operation](../09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md).
Design247 research selection.*

**DP33 — Evaluate a public task against a strong ordinary alternative.**
Before a materially relied-on AI use, name the statutory task, lawful
priorities, benefit expected by people, affected groups, paid operator and
evaluation budget. Compare current service and a competent simpler route
such as clearer forms, trained assistance or fixed-rule automation.
Measure from first attempted contact through useful receiving and any
correction. Agree comparable case mix, demand, due dates, resources and
observation periods before looking at the results. Where safe, lawful
and useful, vary the timing of optional assistance while keeping equal
rights, paid receiving and direct review. Otherwise use a defensible
matched comparison with known limitations. Record spillover between teams
and changes to law, staffing or incoming demand. Protect urgent cases and
people who decline the tool. Participation and evaluation do not waive any
duty or authorize retaining unnecessary personal data.

**DP34 — Count the entire shift, the person's time and actual resources.**
Separate routine staff work, checking and rework, qualification and support,
appeals and delivered remedies, protected standby, and shared operations.
Count each paid interval once and attribute its actual skill, place and
useful window. Keep resident waiting and active effort separate from each
other and from staff hours, including repeat contact, unpaid assistance,
unmet need, inaccessible channels and cases that disappear from the queue.
A rejected or abandoned request is not completed work. Record actual
technology, contract, infrastructure and exit costs separately from hours.
A count of model uses, office tasks finished or survey-reported time saved
cannot supply a funded shift or fiscal saving by itself.

**DP35 — Qualify by service and by who bears the loss.** Set the sector's
service, safety and access criteria and thresholds in advance under its
own authority. Compare timely, lawful and useful outcomes in the whole
eligible population, including urgent, assisted and non-digital routes.
Inspect material concentrated harm and missed or wrongly closed cases
even if the overall rate improves. Keep underlying cases reviewable,
publish safe denominators and missing evidence, and preserve original
receipt dates. Service users and workers can challenge the choice of
comparison and omitted burden without surrendering protected testimony.
Where independence, alternate capacity or protected receiving fails,
limit or stop the affected reliance under DP09–DP12. Repair the harm and
keep prior duties working. A reversible bounded use can remain where
evidence supports that scope. Uncertainty does not justify irreversible
cuts or a presumption of adequate access.

**DP36 — Give verified gains an accountable destination.** A material
public deployment budget identifies the intended use of any released time.
First protect due service, assistance, remedy and fallback. Within the
existing authorized mandate, reserve verified compatible time to repair
missed duties or improve actual access where the service owner can
demonstrate a need. Identify the receiving task, qualified staff, dates and
capacity lost elsewhere. Reconcile each use once. A worker's paid time is
not a cash saving. Worker hours, wages, rest and bargaining retain their
existing law. A material redirection, recurring budget reduction or labor
change needs its own competent elected or employment decision under DB,
JC and C05. Book cash only when actual avoidable costs and obligations,
including overlap, vendor exit and the retained route, support it.
Agencies cannot pre-spend a forecast saving, and suppliers gain no automatic
claim on released resources.

**DP37 — Recheck before reducing the operating reserve.** For a material
reduction in funded fallback or a recurring saving attributed to an AI
change, require at least two comparable completed service cycles with
qualified review outside the deployment and supplier's control, plus a
witnessed joint adverse test under the proposed remaining staffing.
A two-cycle minimum does not override longer evidence or service needs.
If the remaining route fails, retain or restore funded protection and
return uncovered requirements to the competent budget process. Pin the
version, lawful task, comparators, labor and data scope and dependencies.
Reopen when any change affects the evidence, including a new model,
material workload, erosion of skill, source or reviewer loss. Publish
safe findings and the actual disposition. Preserve accessible non-AI
routes, old claims and paid successors through migration.
