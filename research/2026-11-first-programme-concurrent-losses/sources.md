It supplies neither local service costs nor a first-budget preference. |
| [US Government Accountability Office, Emergency Responder Workforce, GAO-20-525](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-20-525) | The 2020 report identifies critical skills, multiple concurrent events, at-risk people and availability of other responders as factors that the workforce target omitted. The implementation record describes a later capability assessment involving two sequential hurricane events. | Establishes a reason to count usable capability and recovery between calls. Neither a national staffing target nor this audit supplies an available care shift. |
