# Design376 commitment. Carry conditional treatment through the next decision

[CTC01–CTC07](../../country/C07/14/01-conditional-treatment-coverage-and-continuity.md)
requires an authorized offer, qualified early and later receivers,
paid evidence and review, timely funds and an individualized transition.
Protect existing care and deadlines. Account for each clinical shift
and source once. A delayed rebate or revised label does not fund a
treatment or change a duty.

The [matched comparison](../conditional-treatment-coverage-and-continuity/01-two-patients-and-a-funded-exit.md)
establishes no clinical recommendation or actual spending authority.
