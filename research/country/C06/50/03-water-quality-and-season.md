# Water must arrive safely in the dry month

Retain the same household 4.2 million m³ and public 1.2 million m³. Add explicit
food-process potable demand and a separate compatible nonfood industrial sink;
retire the unmatched old commercial aggregate. Month-specific farm irrigation,
catchment, sewer, treatment, reuse, fresh header and losses have separate balances.
A recovered stream is reusable only up to an actually compatible receiving sink.
Food washing and potable products do not become reuse sinks by arithmetic.

The reference protects 2 million m³ ecological flow and a separate 0.5 million m³
unallocated flow exclusion; neither is a tank or spending authority. Drought
reduces raw catchment by 20% before preserving those exclusions. A declared 95%
catchment treatment yield exposes backwash/unrecovered flow; neither it nor a
positive treated volume proves drinking-water safety. Intake, barriers, monitoring,
independent sampling, safe distribution and received correction remain unqualified.

Under the fixed mixed profile, the new monthly stress needs about 48,796 m³/day
supplemental source at its peak. Five desal trains do not meet that stress after
one is lost. The sizing illustration needs twelve trains and twenty-one tanks;
this is an expansion hypothesis to compare, not evidence that expansion is best.

In the selected bounded-crop example, five trains leave 18,000 m³/day after one
loss, against about 16,574 m³/day peak supplemental need. Eleven 50,000 m³ tanks
leave 450,000 usable m³ after one tank is lost, enough for the declared fourteen-
day peak fresh-and-reuse-loss volume test. This is a finite volume comparison,
not an adopted engineering standard, accepted quality or geographic independence.
Different duration, climate, intake, routing or demand can overturn it.

Initial gross tank filling is an extra water, process-energy and source request;
a stock target is not water received. Storage does not pump itself. The extended
power failure remains unresolved; no combined safe fourteen-day water/power
service follows from separate tests. Desal intake/brine and foreign bulk source,
shipping, terminals, ecology and actual contracts need their own qualification.

Existing stewards, operators, councils, inspectors and courts keep their distinct
powers. No water model grants a crop priority, tariff, compulsory farm reduction,
withdrawal permit or waiver of downstream rights. Applicable CP/NP scarcity law,
existing claims, actual interim protection and elected burden choices continue.
