# Source inspection for repeated public failure and remedy

*Design197 · Inspected 9 November 2026.*

| Primary source | Inspected scope and limitation |
|---|---|
| South African Constitutional Court, *Black Sash Trust v Minister of Social Development*, [2017] ZACC 8 | Publisher-hosted judgment, operative order paragraphs 4–12. The court's own legal power and a one-year payment arrangement with privacy, financial audit and quarterly reporting belong to its jurisdiction. Local download failed. |
| South African Constitutional Court, *Freedom Under Law v Minister of Social Development*, [2021] ZACC 5 | Judgment introduction and operative order. The document-access and updated-verification order does not settle final profits or show receipt of individual grants. The local copy request failed. |
| U.K. NAO, *Investigation into the Windrush Compensation Scheme*, 2021 | Official PDF and publisher overview. Summary paragraphs 10–13 and 16, and compensation discussion. Scoped 2,163 claims, 414 at payment stage, 633 people receiving money and 824 unreconciled internal final decisions. Figures use different stages and should not be joined as a cohort of fully paid cases. |

The two court decisions form one litigation history, not independent
replications. The NAO report describes administration and does not show
the effect of a structural court order. The 100-benefit example and
the 14/90-day design periods are authored. No foreign remedial power,
payment price, staffing level or actual approval is imported.
