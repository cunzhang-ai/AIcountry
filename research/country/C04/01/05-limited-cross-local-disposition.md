# CLX07–CLX11. Decide the shared effect and return local choice

*Design545 · Proposed implementing law for [national and local authority](../01-national-local-authority.md).*

**CLX07 — Put only the unresolved effect before the wider body.**
Under CLX01–CLX06 the convenor states the exact remaining allocation,
enforceable safeguard or shared cost that a competent compact or prior
sector rule cannot settle. Record separately the affected places,
independently lawful local acts, common limits, and operations whose
cumulative effect remains unresolved. A credible threat can use existing
urgent review before the full comparison is complete. A forum cannot
turn delay, local disagreement or a single unresolved permit into
jurisdiction over a whole field. Pending scope or allocation does not
reset existing permits, deadlines, rights or review.

**CLX08 — Use an equivalent local method where it works.** The
competent compact or elected residual body sets the exact shared
result, each party's assigned exposure and funding, the dates,
independent verification and enforceable correction. Assess proposed
local methods against the same affected people, physical limits,
actual deadlines and effects in other places or seasons. An
equivalent lawful method remains available under the relevant sector
approval route. Its proponent supplies comparable evidence. A ministry
cannot make preference for its own provider or platform a condition.
Where genuinely incompatible effects leave no qualified local method,
any required common method needs the distinct authority and
necessity finding under [protected local competence](04-protected-local-competence.md).

**CLX09 — Give a genuinely separable part its own disposition.** The
competent authority can resolve an exact partial proposal when it
has independent authority, funding and receiving, protects the common
floor and does not make a later lawful alternative materially infeasible.
Verify cumulative loss and any irreversible commitment before release.
First use an existing lawful local or sector decision where it covers
that part. Use the competent elected route for the actual residual.
Publish the change's exact scope and what remains undecided, including
who can challenge it and by when. A partial disposition leaves the
original docket and all protected consents in operation. Silence or a
missed date supplies no approval. Grant nothing in excess of a
physical floor or unassigned protected capacity.

**CLX10 — End exceptional direction on its own terms.** Any temporary
direction over local operations names its lawful basis, exact task,
scope, payer, lawful end and accepting ordinary authority. Bring an
ordinary funded disposition to its competent body before expiry if the
material effect will persist. Review linked conditions, joint procurements,
grant terms and operational instructions by their combined effect.
Once local or compact arrangements demonstrably meet the binding conditions,
return the affected method or operation at the earliest lawful accepted
handoff. The enforceable shared limit and original remedies continue.
An authority opposing handback states the exact unmet binding condition
in time for independent review. Preserve separately owed funds, source
records and access while the dispute is heard. Under existing powers
the reviewer may enforce source access or a qualified handoff.
An omitted decision remains challengeable on its original applicable clock.
An objection alone establishes neither failure nor readiness.
Unready handback remains a delivery failure under its own law. It supplies
no general continuation or permanent transfer of power. Apply
[LD14–LD18](../10/04-local-service-backstop.md) only within its own authority.

**CLX11 — Keep actual receipt and jurisdiction contestable.** Affected
people, councils and rights holders retain direct independent review
under CLX and the sector rules. Verify actual receiver, physical protection,
use of funds and downstream effects. If a measure moves harm across a
boundary, report it and use the earliest applicable remedy. AI may
compare scope and resource evidence and identify possible spillovers.
It cannot establish consent, allocate a shared resource or authorize
intervention. Adopt prospectively with existing obligations, original
dates, independent evidence and lawful local operation carried through.
Reopen where local equivalence is refused without grounds, a partial
release forecloses a real option, or a handback exposes a protected duty.
