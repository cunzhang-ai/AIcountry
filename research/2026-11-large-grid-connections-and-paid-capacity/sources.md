# Primary comparators for large grid connections

- [Ofgem, Demand Connections Reform Call for Input, February 2026](https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2026-02/2026-02-12-Demand-Connections-Call-for-Input.pdf),
  section 2.5, reports contracted demand offers of 41 GW and 125 GW.
  The [observation file](observations.json) transcribes these published
  totals. The rounded transmission and distribution components are
  not recombined to replace the published totals.
- [Ofgem, Proposed data centre connection reforms, July 2026](https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/consultation/proposed-data-centre-connection-reforms)
  documents proposed commitment security and readiness milestones.
  The consultation is used as a proposal, without attributing to it
  enacted terms or an observed impact.
- [Ohio Power Company, October 2025 tariff book, Schedule DCT](https://www.aepohio.com/lib/docs/ratesandtariffs/Ohio/October_2025_AEP_Ohio_Tariff_Book.pdf)
  and the [Ohio regulator's order announcement](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/OHPUC/bulletins/3e8bb79)
  document a distinct 2025 response to underuse and cancellation
  exposure. The tariff specifies demand billing and departure
  conditions. The [utility's explanation](https://www.aepohio.com/company/about/rates/data-center-tariff/)
  describes proof of collateral and the possibility of reallocating
  capacity. No Ohio percentages, thresholds or terms are selected here.

These records distinguish applications, signed offers, capacity
charges and physical service. The worked example's power, water and
financial values are constructed. The sources do not quantify the
candidate's protected margin, payable network costs or causal effects.
