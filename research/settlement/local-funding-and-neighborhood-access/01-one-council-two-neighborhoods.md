# One council budget and two neighborhoods

Design360 · Constructed comparison under
[MF19–MF24](../../country/C04/08/05-neighborhood-access-and-local-allocation.md).

A council owes 50 timely accessible journeys in each of two
neighborhoods for an already assigned service. People need the
journeys to reach existing health, learning or support services.
The journey has one local payer and is counted once. Under the
declared task, travel and staffing assumptions, a central journey
costs 1 unit and an outer-neighborhood journey costs 2. Service
owners still verify arrival in time and the underlying appointment
or instruction separately.

The usable local envelope for this example is 150 units from
ordinary authorized sources. This is enough for the complete
150-unit floor under the declared conditions. In the baseline,
the council allocates 60 to the center, including 10 for an
optional enhancement, and 90 to the outer neighborhood.

| Alternative | Central / outer core spending | Timely central / outer receipt |
|---|---:|---:|
| Report only municipal totals, keep the baseline | 50 / 90 | 50 / 45 |
| Reserve 75 for each neighborhood with no transfers | 50 / 75 | 50 / 37 |
| Keep a common floor and redirect the unbound 10 by lawful vote | 50 / 100 | 50 / 50 |

The first row reports 95 of 100 overall, but five journeys
remain overdue in the outer neighborhood. The uniform ring fence
cannot use the center's 25 left over and funds only 37 whole
outer journeys. The matched correction reaches all 100 if the
enhancement is unbound and an additional five qualified slots
are accepted in time. It changes a local discretionary choice
within its authority, without revising the national grant.

If the central enhancement is already lawfully bound, the
council cannot silently move that 10. Under a separate authorized
continuity route, a reserve of 10 could cover all five journeys.
If only four compatible slots are available, four are delivered
at 8 units, one remains due, and the unspent 2 remains restricted.
Neither the separate reserve nor the approved budget creates
a driver. Without the lawful source, the fallback is unfinanced.
Actual missed dates and remedies remain visible. No conclusion
on an actual council, travel cost or clinical outcome follows.
