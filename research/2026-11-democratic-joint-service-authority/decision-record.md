# Design312 decision on joint service authority

## Baseline and request

Pin Design311, the current country method, C03 decision scope, C04
local authority, C14 national form and existing interlocal remedies.
Select how councils can exercise already authorized powers together
without every operating decision returning to the Assembly. The
project assistant authors the research. Actual legislators and elected
councils retain the power to create and oversee any compact.

## Compare institutional choices

Repeated separate council approvals protect local consent but can leave
already funded connected services waiting. A joint compact can choose
within the scope and money that councils have expressly authorized.
Population-only voting can allow one large council to control the
shared choice. Equal council voting can permit a small share of residents
to control it. The two thresholds limit each form of unilateral action.
They can increase deadlock and leave the largest council with an
effective veto. The rule governs delegated public operations. It is
not an electoral weight rule. A directly elected region would permit
direct replacement and could better fit sustained wider work. Compare
its actual authority, map, staff and burdens, including networks
that do not share its boundaries.

## Selection, continuity and next question

Select JS01–JS05 for material choices within a limited joint service.
Preserve national residual authority, individual review and any separate
status or collective right. Bind councils only through the enabling law,
their own exact approvals and the competent operating vote. Continue due
work, contracts, assets, worker rights, independent review and original
claims through withdrawal and change. The proposal changes neither a
current constitution nor a research governance rule. Adoption requires
lawful authority and a paid transition.

Price one actual decision calendar with an outside effect, hostile
agenda control and qualified alternate receiving. Include the residents
and affected people whose interests cannot be inferred from council
votes. Reopen on chronic deadlock, outside burdens, lost independent
review or a better direct or contractual arrangement. Current evidence
is a legal comparison and a constructed example. Actual assent,
independent review, enactment, paid readiness and lived outcomes remain
open.
