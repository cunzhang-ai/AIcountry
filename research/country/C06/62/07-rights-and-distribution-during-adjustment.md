# FB36–FB41. Protect minimum rights and show who bears fiscal adjustment

*Part of [lasting services and fiscal shocks](../62-lasting-services-and-fiscal-shocks.md). Design352 research selection.*

**FB36 — Look at the effect of the whole adjustment.** Apply this review
to a material proposed national or local fiscal adjustment that may
reduce practical access to essentials, independent remedies or
democratic functions. Combine related changes in spending, fees,
eligibility, travel, staffing and administration across public bodies
and periods. Use existing law to identify protected minimum duties.
Distinguish a proposed lawful change above those minima, failure to
fund an operative duty and a physical shortage. An equal percentage
or an unchanged headline appropriation does not establish equal or
continuing protection.

**FB37 — Compare actual burdens and workable alternatives.** The proponent
publishes the unchanged-law baseline and exact alternatives on the same
dates, populations and service scope. Compare lawful revenue and
reallocation, timing repairs, verified efficiency and deferral of
unbound work before reducing essential access. Show effects on people
with low controlled resources, disability and care needs, children,
different places and other materially affected groups. Separate money,
staff, useful receipt, time and foreseeable displacement. Assess related
changes together and show who disappears from administrative data.
Protect personal information and give affected people, opposition and
independent analysts usable evidence and time to contest it.

**FB38 — Reserve material reductions for an express elected choice.**
A proposal that would materially reduce access must identify the
exact duty, scope and period, who loses, governing authority,
mitigation, source, review and restoration. Give separable reductions
their own recorded decision under the applicable elected procedure.
The proponent must show why the selected burden is necessary and
proportionate given credible less harmful alternatives. No ordinary
adjustment may authorize violation of a protected minimum or
discriminatory withdrawal. A lawful prospective permanent change
uses competent legislation and separately addresses existing claims.
A temporary reduction under this adjustment route expires within
90 calendar days unless earlier law sets a shorter period. Continuation
requires a fresh competent decision on current evidence and the same
rights tests. A new label or successive executive direction cannot
renew it.

**FB39 — Carry the unserved duty and preserve lawful relief.** If none
of the lawful funded paths can meet the whole protected set in useful
time, the responsible authority uses existing sector priorities and
earliest independently lawful alternatives. Record the specific duties
still unmet, people affected, due dates, and actual need for cash,
staff or supply. Name who can correct them and request an elected
decision before the next threatened protected deadline where feasible.
Retain original claims, accessible notice, independent review and
ordinary enforcement. A court may enforce rights, evidence access,
applicable procedure and authorized relief within its jurisdiction.
It cannot enact a tax, supply a missing worker or substitute its
preferred budget.

**FB40 — Verify restoration through the next ordinary cycle.** The
responsible service owner and independent reviewer test actual
receiving, claimant access and the next budget before treating
mitigation as delivered. Publish safe group and place results,
displacement, unpaid costs and remedy performance. A saving reported
by one body is assessed with costs moved to residents and other
payers. Keep expiry real and preserve unresolved cases, lawful claims
and accepting successors.

**FB41 — Fund scrutiny and migrate prospectively.** Use existing elected
budget, sector and equality institutions with paid review, lawful
evidence custody and accessible participation. Preserve stronger
rights, existing appropriations, exact source limits and original
clocks. AI can reconcile cases and model ranges using traceable
inputs. It cannot certify necessity, set a priority or make an adverse
decision. Reopen on concentrated losses, hidden cuts, unequal access,
missed restoration or a simpler effective lawful adjustment.
