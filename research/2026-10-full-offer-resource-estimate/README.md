# Design86: the named-resource country benchmark

15 October 2026 · Fictional100,000-person scenario; research, not a country budget

The country decision is [QE01–QE12](../country/C06/34-reference-costs-and-delivery-conflicts.md).
This package converts named duties across all37 audited functions into workload,
input, asset, transition and household funding allocations, keeping important
unpriced families and actual qualification unknown. It does not complete every
function, prove superior institutions or enact an appropriation.

- [Context, household and scenarios](inputs.json).
- [Task pools, shifts, role floors and omissions](workloads.json).
- [Quantity-price purchases/assets and maintenance exclusions](catalog.json).
- [Reproducible calculation](model.py) and [results](results.json).
- [Decision, objections, revisions and next step](decision-record.md).
- [Source scope](sources.json), [dependency manifest](source-manifest.json),
  [scope record](scope-record.json) and [bounded checks](validate.py).
- [564 exact predecessor artifacts](predecessor-design-85/SNAPSHOT.md).

Run this model and its validator only for this edition's declared inputs.
The validator checks arithmetic, sensitivity, attribution and byte identity;
it does not establish professional safety, original empirical replication,
current actual capacity, public authority or actual better lives. Prior dated
validators are frozen and are not rerun against changed current-country text.
