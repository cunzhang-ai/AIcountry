# A closed war-crime schedule with conflict and protection proof

*G04; part of [C08/18](../18-international-crimes-and-accountability.md).*

**G04 — Conflict, nexus, act and fault.** Prove an international armed
conflict (including applicable occupation), or a non-international armed
conflict of sufficient organization/intensity, and the particular act's
actual nexus to it. Riots, isolated/sporadic violence and peaceful dissent
do not become war by ministerial/AI designation. No knowledge of the legal
label is required, but prove awareness of the material conflict/protection
facts and G08's applicable fault. Ordinary harms retain their own elements.

The closed schedules are [protected people/relief W01–W14](03/01-people-and-relief.md)
and [methods/property W15–W29](03/02-methods-property-and-means.md).
Each row applies only to its actual protected person/object, prohibited
conduct/result and express fault. General intent/knowledge governs except
an explicit narrower/different condition; do not impose strict outcome
liability. An IAC-only row cannot be imported into NIAC by analogy.

The candidate deliberately applies specified common protections across IAC
and NIAC, including starvation, shields, proportionality, environmental harm
and listed weapons. This is an express domestic choice supported in part by
the preserved comparative code, **not a claim that all versions/parties of
the Rome Statute have identical coverage**. G09's international-basis filter
still applies to presence-only cases; actual treaty/custom review is needed.
Some later Rome amendments may differ from the old UN source inspected.

Protected persons include civilians not directly participating, wounded/
detained/surrendered people and others actually entitled to the applicable
IHL protection. Protected status can be contested on real facts, not a whole
population profile. Incidental lawful civilian loss is not automatically a
war crime, nor does a claimed military objective eliminate precautions,
proportionality or protection. A plan/policy or large scale is not a new
required element for every individual war act in this domestic candidate.

Lawful combatant privilege and applicable IHL exceptions require specific
facts/law and P01's defense burden; an enemy label is insufficient. No
punishment for merely holding a political view, seeking care, reporting
harm or participating in a protected civilian role. Failure to prove war
context does not erase independently proved ordinary criminal/civil harm.

This schedule creates **no new battlefield internment, interrogation, search,
targeting or emergency power**. C10/03 still requires recorded human decisions
on particular person-targets and prior weapon/software legal review; legal
and reliable operation must be demonstrated independently. A casualty report,
strike authorization or command's self-audit cannot determine criminal guilt.
Conflict-specific detention/status/release legislation remains a named
separate gap, not a power supplied by silence in this criminal schedule.
