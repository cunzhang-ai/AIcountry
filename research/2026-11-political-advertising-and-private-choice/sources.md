# Primary comparator on paid political distribution

1. Regulation (EU) 2024/900, articles 11–13 and 18–19. The
   regulation provides for political advertising labels, associated
   transparency notices and a repository for online advertisements.
   Article 18 sets conditions on personal-data targeting or delivery,
   including direct collection and separate explicit consent, and
   excludes profiling using special categories of data. Article 19
   provides additional transparency duties. The EU definition and
   covered service boundaries differ from this project's PC01 rule.
   [Official regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/900/oj).
2. European Commission, October 2025 implementation guidelines
   for Regulation 2024/900, sections on scope, targeting and records.
   The original guidance distinguishes private opinions and
   editorial content from political advertising services under
   its own rules. It is part of the same institutional evidence
   family as the regulation, without independent outcome evidence.
   [Official guidelines](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A52025XC05514).

The sources establish actual comparator design, not its measured
effects on privacy, reach, speech, election outcomes or concentration.
AIcountry's limits on proxies, review and early delivery are authored.
