# Design354 decision on fair licensing and portable competence

## Baseline and institutional choice

Pin Design353, V01–V02/V10–V12, TS03–TS04, B16/AP, AL09 and CRD, under the
[working method](../FRAMEWORK.md). Current law design already allows equivalent
competence where lawful, but leaves the case for retaining entry restrictions,
domestic portability, partial practice and financed alternate assessment
underdefined. Select exact reserved acts and recognition by proved scope,
with a five-year maximum between completed reviews and 90 days
for an elected disposition after the due date. Both are parameters
proposed by the project assistant. The Assembly decides restrictions and appropriations. A conflict-free
statutory panel advises on rules, sector officers decide cases, and ordinary
courts hear independent challenges. None of these roles has been appointed
or authorized by this record.

## Comparisons and objections

An occupation-wide local licence is simple to describe, but ties safe entry
to institution, occupation and location. Automatic recognition by title
can admit unproved higher-risk acts. Recognizing individual scope reduces
both problems, while increasing assessment work and making boundaries
harder for clients to understand. Show scope in plain language at the
point of service. Keep a structural rival that licenses the operator and
relies on effective inspection, liability and simple entry where independent
verification can prevent the relevant harm. Preserve lawful occupational
self-organization and stronger safety obligations.

A review panel can reproduce incumbent control through appointments, common
training providers or evidence. Use conflicted-member exclusion, qualified
alternates and direct challenge. Assessors who share a principal or unavailable
specialist are one dependency. Regulator failure can block entry and local
service simultaneously. Preserve both clocks and pay for actual alternatives.
Protect applicant and service-user evidence and disclose aggregate effects.

## Qualified result, migration and next action

The [primary sources](sources.md) support legal mechanisms, and the
[constructed inspection comparison](../settlement/fair-licensing/01-inspections-and-independent-decisions.md)
checks a local gate, unbounded recognition and scoped admission under lost
assessment capacity. There is no actual participation, independent expert
judgment, enactment, cost estimate, staffing commitment or observed outcome.
Legacy licences, first-receipt dates and pending appeals survive through the
actual lawful transition. A learning record remains evidence of competence
within its proven scope. A separate regulatory decision supplies authorization.

For one willing sector, map a single contested reserved act and its closest
separable lower-risk task. Compare a source decision, a portable licence
and a limited assessment against the same harm, actual applicants, clients,
waits and cost. Stay within the responsible sector's authorized analysis
budget and stop before any operative change. Reopen if separation shifts
unverifiable risk to clients, the alternate is controlled by the first
assessor, or ordinary inspection would provide equal protection at lower burden.
