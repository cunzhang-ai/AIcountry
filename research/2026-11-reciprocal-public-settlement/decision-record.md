# Design478 decision record

**Baseline and method.** Use Design477 and the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md).
The [baseline hashes](baseline.json) preserve the prior edition.

**Selection.** Allow exact prospective bilateral discharge of mature
undisputed public obligations with compatible legal purposes, only
under competent authorizing law and actual agreement. Compare gross
payments, sequential settlement with qualified interim cash and
reciprocal discharge with the residual separately funded. Preserve
ordinary authorized payments and separately paid urgent services.

**Reasons and objections.** Synchronized discharge can reduce cash
turnover and bridge financing without adding resources. It can also
obscure original claims, enable withholding or become costly to
verify when a common service fails. Preserve gross budgets,
protected cash, independent original evidence and a usable ordinary
route. No party has to match to receive its independently due payment.
A verified discharge and legal authority precede any accounting relief.
A record failure leaves the affected status unknown until verified.

**Compatibility and reopening.** No rights or clocks move because of
the match. Affected residents keep their own contact, claims and
review without filing again. Preserve existing legal terms,
arrears and remedies through a payer or technology change. Reopen on
shared dependency, potential double release, recurring cash failure,
resident harm or an equally protective simpler route.
[Source limits](sources.md) distinguish historical evidence and
the constructed mechanism. Actual enactment, participation,
independent operational proof and better lives remain open.
