# Whole-country requirements and fourteen scoped findings

Design145 · Source: actual current144 entrances and named canonical dependencies

The full goal requires: R1 equal personhood/membership; R2 real democratic control;
R3 ordinary law and executed remedies; R4 health/learning/care/homes/personal income;
R5 production/work/ownership/tax/money; R6 land/networks/ecology; R7 safety/foreign
responsibility; R8 knowledge/culture/private association; R9 useful accountable AI;
R10 revisable human/AI collaboration; R11 lawful paid transition/operation; R12
credible comparison of better lives without sacrificing public control.

These requirements are derived from the user objective and AGENTS, not retrofitted
to the completed files. “Retain” below means a scoped positive candidate exists,
not evidence it is adopted, affordable, complete in every detail or superior.
