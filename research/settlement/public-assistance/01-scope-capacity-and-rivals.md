# A useful assistant helps without taking over the case or the person

Design175 · [Country choice](README.md)

Someone comparing a benefit letter, a new work offer and an adult-learning plan
can seek help with those three documents. They need not open a national life
record or let a government agent choose their occupation. The helper explains
source/options, prepares chosen communications and accepts only bounded actual
authority. Medical treatment, court representation, financial licensing and
actual entitlement decisions remain with competent professionals/institutions.

If the benefit agency is challenged, a new chatbot controlled by that same
agency is not the outside advice route. Actual qualified providers and source/
records access must be available beyond the implicated control chain. Payment
cannot depend on reducing benefit awards or persuading the client to settle.
Public funds buy qualified availability/help, not the client's political loyalty.

On day0 the person discusses options privately. On day3 they actually ask an
expressly authorised AH receiver to file a covered AP request; the agency gets
it on day5. The protected administrative date is day3, not the private discussion
or delayed agency transfer. A special court/ballot/NI filing has its own actual
receiver; an assistant's promise cannot fabricate that receipt or jurisdiction.

An AI draft taking ten minutes is not necessarily ten minutes of complete help.
For an authored case,20 minutes preparing/checking client facts plus15 minutes
source review and5 minutes actual handoff is40 adviser minutes, even if a model
writes instantly.100such cases require66.67 qualified hours before general
supervision, urgent cover, private custody, infrastructure and leave. No national
saving or staffing adequacy is inferred from a short response timer.

Retain/repair current AP/sector help plus optional DP tools can be simpler and
cheaper but leave early cross-agency assistance dependent on the agency task/
controller. A broader publicly funded personal-agent utility may aid more private
purposes but adds resource, framing and surveillance power. Community/worker/
professional arrangements have different access/funding/continuity needs. AH
selects a bounded person-side public core with actual paid independent receiving;
it can be displaced by a stronger complete paid, rights-compatible alternative.
