# Design296. Usable time and public participation

10 November 2026 · Research selection · No actual enactment

## Baseline and decision

[Design295](baseline.json) retains a service-first parliamentary
country, time and care protections, reachable remedies, paid public
participation and CS16's obligation to justify new public burdens.
The issue is whether separate provisions actually leave people time
to use the services and public rights they value. Use the
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md). Codex assisted
analysis and drafting. No actual affected participant or independent
professional assessed these options.

## Alternatives and selection

- **Use common staffed appointments and ordinary live participation.**
  This is a real low-cost choice when people's usable windows align.
  It retains timely review in the example. It leaves the resident
  with fragmented time unable to join the live deliberation.
- **Keep independent staffed review and offer flexible participation.**
  Select this compatible arrangement in the declared four-unit
  case. A paid facilitator and genuinely useful replies allow the
  resident with shorter windows to contribute. The method can lose
  live exchange, create framing power or fail through a common
  coordinator. Keep the direct option and independent correction.
- **Fund relief or redesign the underlying care and work schedule.**
  A usable paid relief hour protects the live option in this case.
  It requires actual staff, consent and a separate source. A durable
  change in care provision or paid work can be better where the
  collision recurs. Compare its whole labor and source consequences.
  Cash alone can support choice where usable help exists and does
  not establish a qualified available worker.

[CS19–CS23](../country/C01/03/08-usable-time-and-public-participation.md)
selects the cross-government rule. The
[authored case](../settlement/usable-time/README.md) distinguishes
equal hours, concurrent duties and actual opportunity.
[Original evidence](sources.md) supports distinguishing forms of
time and burden. It does not demonstrate a national result.

## Compatibility, objection and migration

Use existing authorities, separate review and independently funded
access. Any new noncooperation safeguard needs explicit ordinary
law. Preserve first receipt, original appeal and electoral clocks,
current benefits, booked care and the recipient's choice.
Prospective changes name their paying and receiving authorities.
No policy owner can determine which optional engagement a person
should prefer or require disclosure of a complete private calendar.
A time comparison cannot authorize changing a poll, compelling
care or financing a programme from assumed household work.
A joint calendar is evidence rather than a transfer of power.

## Next bounded decision

Compare actual appointments and a lawful participation process
with consenting residents, including people who could not attend.
Use safe voluntary evidence on notice, paid and unpaid burden,
cared-for people's choices, interpretation, actual reply and
resourced independent review. Reopen if asynchronous exchange
loses material influence, staffing or consent fails, or a simpler
or more structural route restores meaningful time more reliably.
Actual authority, operating readiness and outcomes remain open.
