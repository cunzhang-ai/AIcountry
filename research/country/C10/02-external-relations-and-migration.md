# C10. External relations and migration

*Part of [C10 safety and international responsibility](../C10-safety-and-international-relations.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

Foreign policy favors peaceful dispute resolution and transparent commitments. Treaties,
defense commitments and guarantees require democratic legislative authorization and
disclosure of fiscal and rights effects. Refuge claims receive individual assessment
and effective remedies against prohibited return. Migration combines fair rules,
integration resources and protection against arbitrary exclusion.

The selected [external-relations approach](../../2026-10-life-systems-integration/external-relations-analysis.md)
is accountable interdependence. Cabinet negotiates material trade, borrowing,
security, resource and infrastructure commitments within a lawful mandate. The
Assembly authorizes commitments that bind future budgets, rights or critical public
capacity; ordinary cooperation proceeds within prior statute and audit. The public
account exposes contingent guarantees, external-currency needs, important suppliers,
foreign labor and ecological effects, lawful exits and continuity. Limited negotiating
confidentiality cannot hide taxing or coercive authority. A domestic gain purchased
through foreseeable harm abroad requires prevention, mitigation and a competent
claim or diplomatic remedy where this country controls the actor or transaction.
The [public-debt route](../C06/06-sovereign-debt-and-distress.md) applies when
external borrowing or guarantees become unsustainable; treaty and foreign-law
claims retain their applicable terms and lawful remedies.

Protection claims need safe intake, translation, competent assessment, independent
appeal and funded local reception. A state contractor or third-country arrangement
cannot be used to evade prohibited return or basic rights. Temporary visitors and
new arrivals have protection and legal standing before they gain the ordinary vote;
long-term residence follows [MB](../C02/06-citizenship-and-secure-belonging.md), with [actual reception](09-membership-admission-and-reception.md). Neither cooperation nor
strategic reserves guarantee supply from an uncommitted foreign partner.

## Who can bind and release the country

[TR01–TR12](15-democratic-treaty-commitments.md) specifies the exact process. The Assembly enacts the classes of external act that require its prior assent:
ratification, material provisional application and withdrawal from a treaty,
public guarantees or foreign borrowing, security commitments, and arrangements
that materially change rights, critical infrastructure, tax authority or durable
public expenditure. Cabinet may explore terms and conduct ordinary diplomacy
within law. It submits an exact final text, authentic translations, expected
domestic implementing law, affected people, fiscal and physical exposures,
compatible existing duties, confidential annex grounds and exit consequences.
The Assembly can accept, reject or require renegotiation; it cannot compel a
foreign partner to agree. A prior statute may permit low-impact technical
cooperation with published limits and later audit, without requiring a national
vote on every working exchange.

Provisional application can create real external and domestic exposure before
formal ratification. A cabinet may not use an emergency, side letter, public
enterprise or lender contract to begin a material obligation that the Assembly
has not authorized. The same legislative and rights account precedes a material
withdrawal: identify what exit the instrument and applicable law permit, who
would lose protection or supply, continuing liabilities and the funded bridge.
A domestic repeal or withdrawal vote does not alone erase an international
obligation or someone else's accrued claim. The authenticated public register
links the mandate, signed text, entry into force, implementing law, reservations
if lawful, later changes and actual termination. Genuine negotiating secrecy
may delay a narrow part of disclosure under review, not conceal who has power
to tax, use force, return a person or pledge public assets. A court can review
competence and protected rights; it does not conduct diplomacy or invent the
other party's consent.

## Protection, entry and membership

At a border, at sea or in a contractor-run control point under this country's
authority, an identifiable public protection office must be able to receive a
claim and prevent an irreversible return while a credible protection risk is
competently assessed. A rejection states facts, law and reasons; the person
receives interpretation, access to relevant evidence, needed counsel and an
independent challenge capable of acting before a prohibited return. Emergency
care and humane conditions apply during review. Detention needs its own lawful,
necessary and reviewable basis; lack of documents or refusal to use an AI tool
is not by itself proof of danger. Identity, family links and child interests are
examined individually. A carrier, neighboring state or digital screening
contract cannot be made the final unreviewable decision-maker.

Ordinary admission, work and family-movement rules require a published statute,
equal treatment within its lawful categories, reasons and appeal. The state can
set prospective capacity and eligibility rules within rights and treaty duties;
it cannot promise unlimited reception from missing homes, schools and clinical
staff. Local receiving authorities need national funding for actual places and
services, not an unfunded transfer of a border decision. Work status should not
make one employer the only lawful route to wages, housing, complaint or continued
residence: a portable, reviewable change-of-employer path reduces coercion while
recognizing legitimate labor-market rules. Labor recruiters and state purchasers
must disclose fees, custody of documents and workers' remedies, including harms
to source communities. Long-term residents follow C02's political-membership
route; a protection decision is not an automatic national vote, and disputed
membership does not extinguish basic rights.
The [interpersonal-safety route](../C08/10-interpersonal-violence-protection-and-fair-process.md)
requires confidential immediate help and competent status advice when a
partner or sponsor controls documents or residence. A help request does not
itself decide a protection claim or removal.

## Trade, dependency and people affected abroad

Each critical import and export dependency has a named domestic steward, source
and route inventory, payment currency, contract and treaty constraints,
replaceable supplier or repair/substitution option, reserve and release rule,
and the time until essential service fails. [NS26–NS30](../C06/54/07-common-import-permissions-and-continuity.md) checks shared permissions and brings material external conditions through the competent public decision. The Assembly chooses public risk
and stock costs in the same C06 budget as present care and maintenance. A low
price is not resilience if one port, lender, fuel source or foreign contractor
can interrupt the service. Conversely, compulsory self-sufficiency can consume
workers, land and energy needed elsewhere; the public account compares both.
The [C09 food-system route](../C09/09-food-systems-and-nutrition.md) applies
that comparison to food imports and production, including foreign land,
water, labor and payment exposure; a domestic reserve cannot erase them.
The [C09 logistics rule](../C09/05-communications-and-logistics.md) names the
domestic corridor, cold chain and alternate-carrier duty; neither that steward
nor a domestic vote commands an overseas port or supplier.
The [customs rule](05-customs-and-border-clearance.md) assigns domestic
declaration, inspection, release and appeal without letting a trade hold decide
a person's protection claim.
During an interruption, prior-law rationing, lawful procurement and service
continuity apply; a minister cannot impose a new tax, seize private supply or
breach a treaty merely by declaring a forecast emergency.

For state-directed, publicly financed or domestic-jurisdiction commercial
activity with foreseeable serious harm abroad, the responsible body identifies
affected workers and communities, adverse evidence, prevention, mitigation,
monitoring and a lawful remedy before material commitments. [BC01–BC20](14-cross-border-buying-and-responsibility.md) specifies connected commercial buyer duties, proportionate inquiry and direct remedies.
People harmed by that country's own public project or a regulated actor need
an accessible complaint and evidence route, with independent legal review
where jurisdiction and standing exist. Claims require proof and fair defenses;
an official warning is not automatic liability, and this candidate does not
pretend that domestic courts control every foreign company or event.
Where the country cannot adjudicate, it must use lawful diplomatic,
contractual or cooperative routes and disclose the remaining harm rather than
count it as a national gain. Source records and safe testimony must survive a
supplier change; trade secrecy and security need proportionate protection,
not a blanket shield for public guarantees or foreseeable damage.
The [ecological transition account](../C09/08-climate-and-ecological-transition.md)
reports harms in imported minerals, energy and goods separately from domestic
emissions reductions; a clean national inventory does not release this
country from its actual treaty or jurisdictional duties abroad.

AI can compare treaty versions, translate claims and expose supply scenarios
with source references and uncertainty. A named official remains answerable
for an external commitment, border act, procurement decision or remedy;
no model can sign, withdraw, reject protection or decide which foreign lives
count in a national welfare calculation. Foreign publics do not automatically
vote in this country's Assembly, but people who bear a material imposed cost
receive the applicable notice, evidence and remedy rather than disappearing
from the public account.
