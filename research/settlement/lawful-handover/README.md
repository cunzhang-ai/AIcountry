## Observed practice and separate costs

The U.S. GAO reports that the General Services Administration received a
9.9 million USD appropriation for 2020–2021 post-election transition
support. Its Table 2 reports outlays of 6,272,820 USD for incoming
administration support, 2,248,847 USD for outgoing support and 984,418 USD
for appointee orientation. The lines sum to the reported 9,506,085 USD.
The report separately records pre-election outlays of 6,069,425 USD.
These are agency-reported outlays for specified assistance, not the
whole cost of changing governments, an independent payment audit or a
price for this parliamentary country. The broader GSA estimate was
about 34.6 million USD including additional funds and expenses. The
appropriation-specific lines are within that broader scope. They show why preparation,
ordinary office work and paid transfer need distinct allocations.
[GAO report, Tables 1–2](https://www.gao.gov/assets/880/871856.pdf)

The same report describes the 2022 U.S. law that permits transition
services for multiple apparent successful candidates, with equal
information access requirements and reports to Congress. Its 2025
budget discussion illustrates that access rules and usable funds can
diverge during uncertainty. The reported budget options were proposals,
not findings of a funded outcome. The proposal here uses parliamentary
formation and no presidential trigger. The United Kingdom's 2024
guidance illustrates continued public custody of official papers and
continued essential business during an election. It is a different
constitutional setting. The NIST control catalogue distinguishes
revoking former access, keeping access to organizational information,
separating duties and limiting privileged roles. It establishes no
democratic mandate or validation of this handover.
[U.K. election guidance, sections F–G](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/election-guidance-for-civil-servants/general-election-guidance-2024-guidance-for-civil-servants-html)
· [NIST catalogue, AC-5, AC-6 and PS-4](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final)

## Follow one valid change under obstruction

The following is a desk exercise. At hour zero a recorded investiture
takes effect under existing law, without a stay. The outgoing political
role ends and a due benefit payment under earlier law remains due in
two hours. The vendor refuses its ordinary login and the main archive
cannot be reached. A preassigned alternate holds an authenticated
operative act and protected copies. The two-hour payment time is a case
assumption, not a new legal deadline.

| Scope | Attempt and proper result | What still counts as failure |
|---|---|---|
| Legal authority | The authorized receiver verifies the operative investiture from the lawful alternate. No former minister or technical vendor signs off. | No authenticated source available to the particular receiver. Keep independently lawful duties and seek confirmation without reviving old powers. |
| Outgoing and successor access | Disable the old political accounts, live sessions, pending unsigned commands and expired agent delegations. Use independent sector recovery for the lawful successor's scoped access. | A disconnected device or copied token can still issue an effective order. Mark the scope unsafe and isolate the route lawfully where possible. |
| Ordinary payment | An authorized career payer and its lawful alternate preserve prior payment history and sufficient usable source. The beneficiary receives payment before the original due time. | The alternate uses the same failed operator, money is unavailable or payment never arrives. Record the unmet duty. A second route cannot duplicate payment. |
| Independent remedy | A lawful court or independent case receiver has actual source records, staff and a usable challenge route. | The same missing archive, support worker, provider or funding defeats review. A nominal backup adds no real remedy. |
| Public control | The incoming executive receives its own scope and courts, election authorities, local government and protected media retain theirs. | New ministers demand their own credentials to edit ballot evidence or protected journalism. Refuse and preserve challenge. |

A timely report could truthfully read: office changed, ordinary
benefit received, protected records available, the former signing
path still exposed and one specific successor command unproved.
Different scopes remain open even if citizens receive ordinary
services. Conversely, closed outgoing access cannot stand in for
working lawful successor access.

In a rival branch no valid investiture exists, or its actual effect is
stayed. Preparation can continue and any lawful caretaker and standing
duties retain their limits. No operational credential grants full
government. If an operative source conflict affects one particular
direction, send that decision to the competent review route and keep
unrelated lawful duties. In a third branch the independent operators
or civilian executors are themselves captured. The paper plan fails.
Use actual lawful protection and disclose which duties cannot be performed.

## Compare arrangements

| Arrangement | Benefit | What may fail |
|---|---|---|
| Repair ordinary departmental handover | Keeps familiar staff, systems and record duties, with lower transition overhead | Can still rely on one outgoing signer, vendor, archive or authority channel |
| One national administrative override | Can restore many systems quickly under a reliable controller | A common bypass can put independent institutions under executive control and become the target of the obstruction itself |
| Separate scoped recovery and common reporting, selected | Preserves institutional powers while testing actual outgoing exclusion, successor access and ordinary service | Independent personnel and contracts cost money. Multiple paths can fragment or share an unseen dependency. Urgent exceptional access may still be unavailable. |

A protected, bounded recovery path cannot decide an election or spend
money. Test restoration at the actual service, command and remedy
boundaries, including a non-destructive rejection of stale access and
a verified lawful receiving act. No actual country, government transition,
permission system, funded alternate or observed outcomes are established.
