# DP13–DP16: make the human and democratic parts operational obligations

*Part of [C11/09](../09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md); candidate continuity.*

**DP13 — A direct remedy cannot share the defendant's only gate.** AP first
receipt/help/decision, B aid/urgent court/execution and PD/sector actual source
review remain reachable through independently held records/credentials and
assisted/offline routes. Care, paid support, ballots, legal defense and safe
housing need no optional AI subscription, vendor identity or consent to training.
Actual qualified human review before protected final adverse effects includes
time, original evidence and power to change the outcome. Independent autonomous
support can help, but cannot manufacture that legal authority or defeat a
person's meaningful challenge. If every route fails, record failed control and
continuing claims; naming a new guardian or another account is no remedy.

**DP14 — Safe evidence, political replacement and independent inquiry.** Keep
necessary original incident/task/authority/change records under PD and relevant
unexpired claim/hold periods, with protected source/defense/independent inspection.
An oversight record is not a permanent public archive of private testimony;
logs cannot be repurposed into social worth, political loyalty or personal
training data without actual independent lawful basis. Publish safe service/
dependence/spend/incident/recovery findings and material unexamined objections.
AA/OJ/PD and actual sector investigation address their own duties, including
Director/vendor misconduct, outside the implicated chain. Assembly/opposition
can examine source/funding/control without becoming the public's model editor.
Lawful government replacement transfers actual public contracts/credentials/
records to competent successors, not a vendor or outgoing minister's continued
political veto. This is no authority for a technician to decide who won office.

**DP15 — Fund the public job and continuing duties.** The existing public
digital-support function needs a separately visible G appropriation for shared operation/technical
coordination; each agency/sector pays its actual delegated task, independent
judgment and accepted alternatives under its own lawful source. Shared agreements
attribute real included/incremental work once, preserving autonomous budget/
source/records of independent institutions under OP06/OP07. Actual purchased
capacity, capital, standby, upkeep, drills, outside experts, staff cover and
claim/remedy tails require appropriated cash/real inputs. No levy, fine quota,
data sale, forecast AI saving or private restricted asset funds the office by
default. Public-need/state-order payment requires express coordinated inclusion
within adopted B19 source/narrow executor scope; no Treasury guarantee of every
private cyber loss or power to create a new tax. Actual old public claims,
private contract/fault liability and separately adopted assurances persist.

**DP16 — Prospective migration and revisable choice.** A coordinated Act sets
a fixed component date at least180calendar days after enactment, later actual
RR/sector dates where required, with exact source powers, paid staff/contract/
record/claim successors and stronger continuing law. Current laws/software/
contribution permissions stay unchanged until competent revision. Do not cancel
old service, rights, wages, contracts or claim dates because a new reliance
condition is unready; no official can extend due duties by a readiness label.
Map unsafe legacy dependence to an actual lawful corrective/receiving plan;
there is no overnight claim that an independent whole country already exists.

Keep a smaller task portfolio, independent sector-only support, negotiated common
services and a more integrated public operator as real rivals, each with their
cost/control/failure/transition. Public ownership and more nodes are no proof of
independence; full decentralization may add price, coordination and attack costs.
Reopen on common failure/capture, lost human agency, excluded groups, unfunded
alternates, unsupported gain or a better whole arrangement. Future media/teams
must preserve actual meaning, authority and dissent without one universal
schema. Next address the current whole-country source/capacity feasibility
frontier against a published reference and explicit assumptions, while preserving
all major functions/rights and distinct actual adoption/outcome states.

[PO20](../../C08/27/05-remedies-information-source-and-handover.md) now joins
lawful civilian-enforcement succession and outside-command source access to
this actual key/record/paid-receiving boundary. No police chief/DP operator gets
a master credential or case sovereignty; original rights and authority expire
on their own law despite a surviving account, with actual qualified execution still needed.
