# C10. Conflict custody under replaceable civilian authority

15 October 2026 · Design 77 · Proposed control, not an operational war mandate

[C08 CD01–CD16](../C08/21-conflict-detention-and-release.md) owns legal
custody/status/review, humane treatment, criminal clocks and actual release.
C10/03 owns force authorization; neither this topic nor IHL treatment rules
create new external force, intelligence, tax, border or emergency powers.

Command records actual first control, person/age/needs, custodian, legal basis,
source and every transfer. A named commander is responsible for lawful humane
execution; civilian justice retains independent register/court/inspection
access, including when the implicated force or vendor controls the original.
Actual hospitals, contractors, ships, joint sites and data aliases cannot
hide custody or restart a timer. Protection does not depend on reciprocal
conduct, a confession or the legality of a prisoner's political views.

The selected national rule has no general involuntary civilian-security or
NIAC-specific internment power. IAC POW/actual neutral military grounds are
narrow, judicially reviewable and distinct from guilt; ordinary criminal
capture and strict immediate defense retain their own requirements. Lawful
combatant privilege is not impunity for war crimes. A protected civilian,
child, journalist or medical worker does not become targetable by receiving
POW benefits or appearing in a military source database.

The Assembly authorizes prior capacity/risk and scrutinizes protected facts,
with opposition support and independent legal/source access. It cannot vote
a named person guilty or make an unlawful external force lawful. Courts can
order and enforce actual release; clinical and child services have real safe
receivers. Inspector, adviser, visitor and court names are not four independent
checks if the same command, sponsor or vendor controls all of them.

Before planned capture-dependent missions, fund/verify qualified courts,
defense, interpretation, humane sites, clinical access, protected records,
neutral cooperation, communications and release paths without displacing
civilian elections, clinics and remedies. No captive labor pays that bill.
Actual staffing, transport/foreign access and whole costs remain unknown;
a mission label or completed checklist is not operational readiness.

On mandate expiry/election/withdrawal, stop unauthorized expansion, preserve
independent evidence and lawfully hand over continuing protection. A detention
obligation does not self-renew the military campaign, and a campaign expiry
does not erase a person's status, claims or safe release duty. Refused transfer,
unsafe return or missing documents use actual court/receiving alternatives,
not secret rendition, indefinite closed care or an automatic national-status
change. Source-law/treaty competence still requires qualified case assessment.

[DF09–DF10](12/02-political-neutrality-money-and-ordinary-justice.md) excludes
confinement through professional discipline and names ordinary justice/outside
inquiry. CD/P actual custody/release grounds and earlier clocks continue;
new military organization/source labels cannot detain a person or reset review.
