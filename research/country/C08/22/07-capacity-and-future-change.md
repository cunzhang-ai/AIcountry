# Qualified capacity and changes that do not silently move the safety boundary

*TS13–TS14; part of [technical safety](../22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md).*

**TS13 — Count laboratories, original sources and ordinary life together.**
[C06/27](../../C06/27-technical-safety-and-repair-capacity.md) requires actual
qualified assessors, metrology/calibration, inspectors, independent investigators,
counsel/courts, accessible explanations, repair stock, safe receiving and retained
records by field, shift, location and controller. Do not add sector labels and
assume the same engineer, clinician, sample or vehicle is available repeatedly.
A valid test depends on actual sampling, instrument, uncertainty and custody;
an AI estimate or copy of another laboratory's output is not a new observation.

Public technical assistance and lawful shared/regional laboratories can reduce
avoidable entry costs; operators still prove the actual relevant result. Foreign
recognition needs TS06's source/configuration scope and actual accepted cooperation.
No treaty, external test slot or specialist reserve is assumed to exist. The old
37-function bid book/SF cash does not qualify these incremental duties. Subsidies,
fees and insurance have distinct actual payers and household/worker incidence.

Before commencement, show a paid functioning chain or retain actual current lawful
protections and disclose the gap under TS15. Do not close every unreviewed essential
service because a new assessor is absent; nor certify an actual serious hazard as
safe to improve access statistics. If a stop and alternate delivery both fail,
record the conflict, protect people under existing law and seek actual funded
correction; no template turns impossible simultaneous duties into readiness.

**TS14 — Review changed meaning, domain and hazards, not every line of code.**
Each approval states the configuration/domain, material assumptions, maintenance,
monitoring and predefined permitted-change envelope. Changes to intended use,
affected population, critical barrier, control/learning method, operating domain
or evidence on which safety depended need prior scoped reassessment before the
expanded use. Reuse supporting results only where their complete dependencies
remain valid, including new incidents/objections even when input files match.

Routine maintenance and validated equivalent corrections within the approved
envelope need protected records and continuing verification, not automatic full
relicensing. Urgent narrowly protective patches can follow a previously approved
safe-change plan with immediate useful warning and TS07 reporting; a vendor cannot
claim urgency to expand capability, remove safeguards or erase prior evidence.
If no validated safe continuation exists, restrict/stop the affected function and
arrange actual continuity rather than force a dangerous rollback.

Future media, shared situations or delegated agents may alter understanding and
teamwork. Demonstrate preserved consent, attribution, uncertainty, challenge,
revocation and safe action at the useful time; communication convergence alone
cannot settle external risks, resources or authority. A novel coordination method
can replace the present one through competent law without losing ongoing claims.
