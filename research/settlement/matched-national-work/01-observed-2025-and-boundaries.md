# Observe the same 2025 economy from both sides of public employment

[Matched national work](README.md)

Use Denmark in calendar 2025 at the stated vintages. NABB69 and
NABP69 are both from the 30 June 2026 release. The 69-industry
tables provide 2025 and include a general-government memo. The
117-industry tables used by the earlier comparison stop at 2023.
The memo is included in the total. Subtract it once. Non-government
includes activity outside general government such as public
corporations. It is not a census of private ownership.

The leaves of the employment table sum to 2,000 fewer employee
hours than the published total. The compensation leaves sum to
DKK 1 million more. Use the published total and keep the residuals
visible. These source-defined hours are not a paid roster or TW
coverage. Employee compensation includes employer contributions,
including imputed contributions. It is not a cash wage, public
appropriation, per-worker quote or tax yield. Employment counts
also differ from public-job FTE in OBESK. Other worked hours do
not measure all unpaid household work or available voluntary care.

The general-government compensation amount matches the 2025
OFF3 amount of DKK 442.275 billion at the retained release.
The same [2025 fiscal reference](../observed-government-baseline/01-accounts-transfers-and-work.md)
shows consolidated net lending of DKK 90.924 billion, with a
separately scoped state cash outturn of −13.0 billion before debt
issuance and redemption. Neither measure identifies an available
recurring appropriation or an owner's cash at a due date. Matching
one compensation item does not create a bridge between those
different fiscal measures. The same-year accounts keep their
separate scopes. [Statistics Denmark public accounts](https://www.statbank.dk/OFF3)

The [custody observations](../lawful-custody-capacity/01-danish-appropriations-and-capacity.md)
and [court observations](../court-capacity-and-timely-remedy/01-danish-case-flow-and-source.md)
show why a signed variance or a falling backlog cannot certify
lawful rest, staffing or actual remedy. Preserve the continuing
obligations and repair needs in both alternatives. The
[existing adaptive care example](../adaptive-home-support/01-denmark-and-copenhagen.md)
gives retain/repair a real service architecture. Its transition
grant and actual recurring cost have different scopes.

Keep the actual [wider uses and trade flows](../observed-government-baseline/01-accounts-transfers-and-work.md)
in the baseline. These aggregates do not identify all household
work, legal coverage or public foreign settlement. The 2023
detailed industry and demand models remain separate. The matched
2025 reference changes the basis for comparison without proving
a national start, any savings or changed service outcomes.
