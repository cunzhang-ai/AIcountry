9 November 2026 · Research selection from Design208

## Gap and selection

OP/T covers founding and paid succession. MF and LD cover national-local
funding and distress. XR covers a person moving between localities.
HN assigns the accepting team for a referral. The ordinary case of a
whole function moving between already operating authorities needs a common
choice about legal owner, actual operator, paying authority and retained
dependencies. Select FT01–FT08. Preserve the legal date and prepare a
funded fallback that preserves actual service without holding the new
authority hostage to a certificate or the old institution's consent.

Keeping the current accountable operator and funds can preserve service
relationships and avoid expensive migration. It can leave persistent
fragmentation or local capacity gaps. Direct successor operation can join
resources but create simultaneous labour, access and information changes.
Retained delegated delivery can work with clear legal responsibilities and
whole costs. A separately financed overlap costs more and can draw resources
from retained work. Choose by task using actual need, qualified staffing,
public power and useful-time protection. A common schedule reveals the
displaced work and the difference between a fiscal transfer and a higher
transition cost. No diagnosis selects an elected regional tier.

## Power, risk and continuing duties

Elected institutions choose legal responsibility and public resources.
Statutory owners retain public accountability even when an operator
delivers the task. Employers, qualified service providers, fiscal payers,
independent courts and review keep their legal powers. Test a hostile
outgoing office, a national funder that withholds a transfer and a backup
dependent on the same staff or vendor. A controller can hide a cost in
retained buildings or deny access by asking for repeated proof. Keep
itemized evidence, source access and remedy usable through the original
due time. Keep confidential records protected. AI can reconcile options
and route contacts, without accepting a case or moving rights.

The proposed 30-day continuity account has real workload. A simpler
handover is preferable if it protects actual people and lawful authority.
An adopting law supplies authority, exact dates, paid overlap and
successors. Preserve existing wages, collective rights, pensions,
contracts, assets, orders, privacy, complaints and the original case
clock. If authority takes effect before full delivery, the successor
retains the duty and its failure. The predecessor may act only within
express authority. No general takeover, national-loan power, forced
staff assignment or readiness veto follows. This AI-assisted decision
has no actual assent, qualified independent review, adoption or measured
better lives.
