to verify in a matched context. Their money and staff remain on their

with one declared common failure. They do not price a real launch.

## Add the declared shared-system loss to the authored budget

The ordinary approved E route assumed usable second-call and independent
review arrangements. A newly examined scenario exposes one common
controller across the ordinary energy contact and its apparent backup.
Under this scenario, a separately funded reserve arrangement K supplies
independent contact, staffing and useful-time review for E. Assume the
alternate owner accepts it and its evidence is valid for the scheduled
window. K needs one extra early national cash unit, included in the
cumulative total, one additional local unit by day 90 and one extra
independent administrative review slot. K is incremental to the earlier
illustration. It does not repeat the previously priced ordinary E
reserve. It has no annual recurring or foreign cash increment in this
case. Care, court and election funds and qualified staff stay outside K.

K is a binding cost once contracted, even if unused. The example
assumes an effective voted reserve option before the new route is used.
K can be committed once for the selected package. Pricing it in two
mutually exclusive alternatives gives neither two backups nor an
authority to exercise both. The same local, national, foreign and work
ceilings from the earlier exercise still apply.

|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| E + P + H with K | 6 / 10 / 2 | 4 / 9 / 3 | 1 and 3 / 3 / 2 | Fits the reference envelope, if separately authorized and accepted. |

If K is unavailable, the conditional energy options that depend on it
stay held. Bring a genuinely independent energy route, an independently
authorized housing or training choice, or ordinary funded repair to the
competent decision. Holding E does not erase a resident's standing
energy protection. If only the earlier reference motion passed, the
officer cannot release E + P merely because it fits. If the exact
narrower option and reserve were approved and are usable, the weaker
scenario selects E + P with K. This spends one real review slot that
cannot also hear another case. It leaves the opportunity for H visible.

## If the loss occurs after binding, carry the duty

In a separate branch H is lawfully bound before an account outage on
the first payment day. Local early own cash is two, and four is due.
The national advance is inaccessible at that time and becomes usable
later. The payment is still two short on the original date. Keep the
lease, support, accrued claims and continuing cash need in the budget.
The local payer and national funder use the existing lawful interim
route if it can deliver, with independent correction and the remaining
gap reported. A later transfer can settle the account without satisfying
the original deadline. No reserve or authority in this comparison
permits a protected payment to be silently moved or a lease rescinded.
