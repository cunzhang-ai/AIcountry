# A reachable forum, usable evidence and bounded delay

*B13–B16; part of [C08 civil core](../13-civil-obligations-and-executed-remedies.md).*

**B13 — One accepted request and a competent forum.** Ordinary intake accepts
an account of the problem by reachable human, phone, assisted, remote or future
channel. Preserve first receipt and any urgency while helping clarify parties,
requested relief and evidence; no full legal dossier/credential is required.
The first office retains lawful triage until a competent receiver accepts.
A published conflict rule identifies national/local/sector forum and applicable
law; courts resolve competence with reasons and preserved dates, not claimant
ping-pong. Basic civil/administrative protection cannot be exported by a
business's unilateral terms. Cross-border compulsory reach needs actual law/
treaty, not an assertion about a foreign server.

Fund advice, interpretation, accessible participation and representation by
stakes, complexity and dependence. Essential/liberty/equality and destitute
claimants cannot lose usable access through an upfront fee or cost-security
bond. Costs are proportionate, reviewable and not a rich party's retaliation
weapon. A simplified ordinary-money route may reduce forms when issues are
suitable; disputed identity, bodily care, home loss, status and fundamental
rights are not small merely because damages are low. Preserve full fair
hearing and proportionate appeal. Voluntary settlement must permit safe refusal;
serial compulsory mediation/internal appeals cannot block direct urgent court
access. Bad-faith procedural abuse needs a narrow reasoned court response,
not an agency's unilateral claim ban.

**B14 — Civil proof and controlled information.** Ordinary disputed civil
facts use the balance of probabilities unless a specific rights/protection
law requires another standard; a machine percentage is not that standard.
Give the respondent a real chance to answer. A claimant identifies a credible
factual basis and relevant respondent-held records; courts can order necessary
proportionate preservation/disclosure with privilege, privacy and witness
safeguards. Unjustified failure after notice can support a reasoned adverse
civil inference, not automatic liability, punitive sanctions or criminal guilt.
Protected self-incrimination/privilege cannot become guilt by silence. The
special equality test retains its explicit initial showing and defense burden.

Unchallengeable secret evidence cannot decide an adverse civil finding.
Original observations, transformed/synthetic material and shared AI sources
remain distinct; agreeing agents are not independent proof of assent or harm.
Torture-derived statements stay excluded under P12 even outside criminal
cases; disputed provenance receives a fair separate inquiry without requiring
a prior torture conviction. Broader coercion/derivative exclusion retains
its specific candidate safeguards, not an implied private right to suppress
contrary facts. Safe disclosure cannot force sensitive testimony into an
indefinitely public file.

**B15 — Limitation and actual opportunity.** Retain the source's candidate
three-year ordinary civil period from knowledge, or reasonably obtainable
knowledge, of material harm/facts and the responsible party. Concealment,
minority, serious threats or an actual inability to access a competent route
can toll it under a reasoned court decision; diagnosis alone neither removes
capacity nor decides tolling. Missing notice, lost records or an office's
failed referral cannot consume a period while making filing impossible.
Continuing wrongs and interim protection have their applicable separate rules;
do not fictionally reset an old completed harm or waive latent-injury rights.
[B37](../16/06-civil-periods-and-registry-loss.md) now provides candidate
land, estate, work, latent-harm and judgment periods, with fresh migration/review. No bulk revival/extinguishment
of old claims occurs through this research; actual stale-evidence and third-
party fairness must be assessed under lawful transition.

**B16 — Administrative reasons and direct review.** Public decisions identify
lawful power, facts, proposed result and usable challenge before an adverse
act, with meaningful reply and disclosure. The source's ordinary proposed
schedule is retained: usable requirements/help within five working days,
ordinary decision with usable lawful notice within 30 working days of **protected first receipt**, one
notified reasoned extension at most 30 working days before expiry. Design140
[AP01–AP12](../../C04/06-reachable-administration-and-finite-decisions.md)
replaces the earlier completeness-controlled start: proof requests, routing,
AI queues or renamed files cannot stop/reset it. Mere closure as incomplete
is no merits decision; missing evidence is assessed fairly under actual law,
not automatically eligibility, ineligibility or fraud. Give at least 20 working days
for ordinary adverse reply unless a documented imminent threat warrants a
shorter lawful route. Existing due duties and urgent safe times prevail;
these numbers never allow delaying care, liberty or already due payment.

Direct judicial review includes unlawful omission, proof demands and delay
without waiting for adverse notice or exhausting useless internal steps; the source's 60-working-day filing candidate runs from usable notice,
with extension for blocked access/unfairness. A court can stop unlawful effect,
order source disclosure, annul and require lawful reconsideration or owed
restoration; it cannot write a new benefit, tax or general policy. Silence is
not approval of a dangerous activity, new power or consent. These uncalibrated
outer clocks and actual court capacity require fresh public/professional review.

[OJ03/OJ08–OJ09/OJ16–OJ18](../25-ordinary-justice-and-public-prosecution.md)
expressly proposes special ordinary judicial appointment/status and appellate
routes. Judicial appointment challenge30calendar days and status removal appeal
seven-calendar-day opportunity are specified exceptions, with actual blocked-
access extensions; the ordinary60-working-day B16 administrative filing route
is not generally shortened. Civil/administrative appeal30working days is distinct
from first judicial review. B16 ordinary20-working-day adverse reply survives
OJ's21-day appointment notice; exclusions need source/reply/direct review.
No shorter source/urgent liberty/clinical right or pending actual period is lost.

[AA16–AA30](../../C04/04-public-audit-and-accountable-oversight.md) now proposes
necessary audit records/preservation, fair material findings/reply and direct
ordinary challenge, plus explicit AA09/29 special AG status jurisdiction. B16
reply/access/source protections remain; mere audit delay, error or allegation
creates no offense, guilt, mandatory private disclosure or policy veto.

AP preserves fair reply when new material arrives late; a missed decision due
date remains visible while fair process continues. Silence neither grants nor
denies a claim. Notice-based filing cannot silently run out during an ongoing
unnotified omission. Existing special/earlier clocks and law remain; AP12 names
prospective pending-case migration without forgiving old delay or resetting rights.

[AH01–AH16](../../C04/07-person-directed-public-assistance.md) now adds a
free initial/practical person-side public service, actual outside conflict
receiving and paid source/continuity. This B13 advice/representation by stakes/
complexity/dependence remains distinct; AH is no blanket professional licence,
full private litigation subsidy or reset of court/claim/source dates.

[PI14/A2](../../C03/17/03-public-access-audit-and-effective-remedies.md)
now proposes a narrow public-interest legality claim for identified particular
self-benefit on credible facts, without a separate personal denial/competing
bid or one-fifth Assembly support. Ordinary case assistance/reply/protection
and necessary CJ reference retain their actual source; no universal policy
merits action, automatic stay or broader B19 guarantee is inferred.

[SCN03–SCN04](../../C04/06/05-safe-public-contact-and-notice.md) separates
a relay's receipt from usable notice and requires case-specific judicial
direction for civil service when ordinary proof is missing. Preserve
the filing party's original date, urgent relief and stronger procedures.

[UCL01–UCL08](../25/13-urgent-civil-legal-assistance.md) implements
urgent civil advice and scoped representation under B13. Original filing,
proof, hearing and applicable remedy rules continue.

[SCL01–SCL06](09-accessible-small-civil-claims.md) specifies allocation,
usable service, proof and affordable participation for suitable ordinary
money claims under B13–B16.
