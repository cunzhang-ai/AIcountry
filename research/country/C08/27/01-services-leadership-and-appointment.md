# PO01–PO04. Elected policy and protected professional leadership

*Part of [PO](../27-civilian-enforcement-and-public-control.md); a concrete
ordinary public employer/command candidate, not an enlarged force statute.*

**PO01 — Name the public institutions and their limits.** The Civilian Police
Service supplies ordinary lawful investigation, protection and court-process
execution through territorial commands under one national civilian employer.
An accountable human Police Chief manages it. The Civilian Investigation Service is a separate
public employer led by its own accountable human Director, concretizing C08/04's existing
outside-command investigation function. Prosecution/OJ, courts, civilian
corrections, clinical/care/peer response, border, NAU and defense keep separate
source-law powers. Neither chief is a general public-safety sovereign.
National administration does not transfer local government or service policy,
nor grant ordinary police every regulator's or clinician's powers.

**PO02 — Open qualification, not a command-owned succession.** Existing
protected Assembly recruitment staff receive consenting applications with
at least30 calendar days, public job criteria/qualified names/reasons/conflicts
and B16 reply/direct ordinary review of exclusions. Require ten relevant years
or demonstrated equivalent competence in civilian policing/investigation/
justice/public safety and actual rights/evidence/operations/management ability.
The investigation director needs actual serious-investigation/source-integrity
competence. Military rank, wealth, party agreement, proprietary clearance and
an AI ranking are no qualification. No simultaneous minister/legislator/senior
party or campaign leadership, private coercive-provider ownership or conflicted
case role; lawful civic views and earlier lawful public service are protected.
Qualification determinations are reviewable, not an incumbent's closed guild.

**PO03 — Distinct nominations and actual fixed votes.** Cabinet nominates
one qualified Police Chief applicant. After at least21 calendar days' usable
public hearing/notice, a named ballot needs `floor(N/2)+1` of full statutory
Assembly N and `ceil(O/3)` protected non-government approval. For the Civilian
Investigation Director, any member may nominate a qualified applicant and
approval needs `ceil(3N/5)` and `ceil(O/3)`. Separate votes; no bundled slate,
automatic appointment or lowered vacancy/absence denominator. Expressly use
CJ05's closed governing-cohort definition, frozen seat/denominator/successor
rules and fresh vote after new government/agreement; O0 cannot appoint.
PO disputes go to unimplicated ordinary courts, not CJ12's reserved own-court
forum. Stronger adverse-reply rights are not shortened by21-day notice.

Chief term five personal years and Director term six from lawful assumption,
nonrenewable with no acting/case-completion holdover. Each vacancy starts a
successor's own term, not extension of the predecessor. Open recruitment180
days before expiry, within14 after unexpected vacancy. Cross-party approval
can deadlock or be jointly captured; votes establish neither independent
people nor source. Actual office begins only under competent adopted law.

**PO04 — Neither partisan purge nor untouchable chief.** Expressly extend
OJ16's independent three-person status/five-person appeal and OJ18's paid,
finite suspension safeguards to these two heads, excluding selectors/case
participants/conflicts. Removal requires proved serious corruption/office
abuse, deliberate material qualification fraud, continuing incompatibility,
or enduring actual-duty inability after accommodation; serious persistent
failure of lawful duties after supported correction also qualifies here.
Punitive misconduct needs OJ17's proof; functional inability needs its actual
clear-and-convincing basis. Lawful political views, source disclosure, refusal
of illegal force or a lawful inconvenient case decision are no grounds.
An elected lawful general policy must be executed; the chief has no policy
veto. Existing timely criminal inquiry, urgent safety/access protection and
fair defense remain distinct. No head appoints or funds its own final reviewer.
