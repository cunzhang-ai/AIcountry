# When home support needs change faster than a public contract

9 November 2026 · Design211 · [Canonical choice](../../country/C07/25-adaptive-home-support.md)

Select a guaranteed personal floor with a known team, paid discretion and an
external decision on any material reduction. The strongest comparator already
uses flexible care and public responsibility. The decision is whether to add
specific safeguards and wider need-based coverage to that kind of arrangement.

| Arrangement | Gain | Risk and disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Good specified tasks and accountable visit times | Clear and independently checkable duties, predictable staffing | Slow amendments can miss real changes. Keep where preferred or necessary, and retain for essential tasks and useful windows |
| Flexible care with bounded public authority | The person and team can change delivery without a new authorization for each visit | Unbounded provider discretion can hide cuts. Select with individual floor, independent reductions and funded changes |
| Supported individual budgets | Person controls more of the routine and relationships | The person can inherit supplier, employment and availability risks. Keep AS paid assistance and public alternatives |
| Direct public team provision | Reduces contract interfaces and permits paid shared staffing | Common employer concentration or local monopoly may weaken exit. Compare full staffing and actual alternatives |

[Observed law, budgets and evaluation](01-denmark-and-copenhagen.md) and
[the household and public source case](02-coverage-shifts-and-expiry.md)
qualify the decision. Reopen if the protected plan adds administration without
better practical agency or if backup, high-need access or recurring funding
fail. The choice neither imports Danish eligibility nor books future savings.
