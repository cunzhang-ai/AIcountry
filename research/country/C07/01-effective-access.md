# C07. A social floor that can actually be used: Effective access and whole-person delivery

*Part of [C07. A social floor that can actually be used](../C07-social-floor.md); integrated design 58 research, not enacted law.*

Guarantees belong to people; provision can differ by locality within national rights,
quality and accessibility rules. The state retains responsibility when it commissions
another provider. A nominal entitlement without a usable route and remedy is incomplete.

The selected national guarantee includes **effective access** under legislated need,
quality and response standards: reachable place and hours, accessible transport and
communication in a language the person can use for a consequential decision,
affordable use, required support and safe continuity. An agency may
not count an offer that a person cannot practically use as completed delivery without
reasons and a challenge route. This does not promise every preferred provider or
instant capacity. When several valid offers conflict, the person may request a
voluntary joint plan naming sector duties, an interim arrangement and a deadline.
The [equal-standing rule](../C02/05-equal-standing-and-differentiated-needs.md)
tests both explicit refusal and a formally identical process whose effects
exclude people without justification. Accommodation and alternate access
need an owner, not an automated exception label.
The [health, education and care contract](03-health-education-and-care.md)
assigns separate sector owners, receiving services and independent review;
coordination does not merge a child's school claim with another person's
medical consent or care decision.
Refusing the plan does not forfeit any separate entitlement. Shared information is
limited to what lawful coordination needs; no universal family dossier is required.

People may enter through several accessible human, professional, local or digital
channels. A first authorized receiver gives a usable receipt, preserves any lawful
claim date and follows a referral until a competent authority accepts it; the
person can still contact that authority or an independent reviewer directly.
The receiver may help assemble a voluntary joint plan but cannot decide every
sector's entitlement, compel an AI intermediary or require a single shared
personal record. A short queue at the first door is not access if later handoffs,
travel, repeated testimony or review delay leave the service undelivered.
During a territorial-status dispute or transition, the current national and local
providers remain responsible under prior law until funded lawful successors
accept each service. A resident's language, community affiliation or position
on the status question cannot be a condition for school, care, safe water or
urgent protection. The C06 account must include interpreters, cross-border
journeys and duplicate operation during any change.

During a genuine cross-system shortage, the lawful temporary priority and
actual delivery are recorded separately from a person's standing claim.
Inaccessible queues, wealth, political allegiance or a compulsory digital
channel cannot decide an essential allocation. The person retains reasons,
interim protection where physically available and independent correction of
an unlawful or factually mistaken denial; a shortage does not become a
completed service entry.

The [candidate continuity Act](../C06/19-continuity-law-and-delivery-package.md)
specifies a first-date rule, seven-day hardship protection and urgent
clinical/paid-care receiving duties. Its administrative outer clocks never
permit waiting beyond the earlier safe time, and a timely referral alone
is not delivery. These proposed clauses need source-law and capacity
reconciliation; they do not commence a new operating entitlement here.

The [person-level income floor](../C05/06-income-floor-and-safe-resources.md)
counts actual cash and matching received support; a wealthy partner, listed
service or blocked balance is not usable money. Cash does not replace paid
care or housing, and a prompt needs update must also be financed and received.

Design 85 adds an accountable access duty before successful application: the
existing I/S/Y programme/local owners fund proportionate safe outreach and
practical help for potentially eligible nonclaimants, nonusers and people who
stopped midway. Use lawful minimum information and voluntary safe contact;
no automatic eligibility, compelled uptake, unsafe family disclosure or broad
record merge. Distinguish voluntary refusal, inaccessible intake, missing proof,
unavailable service and unknown need. Nonresponse establishes none of them.

Check coverage/need using safe original sampled/qualitative information with
uncertainty and missing groups, including people outside administrative contact
frames. Do not claim to locate everyone or infer that higher take-up reaches
the neediest. Actual claim-date/eligibility/payment rules remain under source
law; outreach alone backdates no award and grants no denial. An individual
legal route does not wait for a population study. NP includes staff/access/
privacy/independent correction costs rather than treating outreach as free.

[Design140 AP](../C04/06-reachable-administration-and-finite-decisions.md)
adds ordinary first-receipt/proof/omission safeguards without replacing useful-
time clinical/care access, E06/E07, due payments or special source clocks.
The office keeps lawful proof responsibility; unavailable records do not erase
a claim, establish eligibility or make a nominal service actually received.

[AH core public assistance](../C04/07-person-directed-public-assistance.md)
now concretizes a client-directed local/national debtor/provider/source and
independent conflict route before/across public requests. Existing first receivers,
sector/joint-plan duties and AS paid personal support remain; no compulsory
new access gate, merged life dossier or universal case owner.

[XR](../C04/09-cross-local-service-continuity.md) preserves effective
access after a move or displacement. Temporary receiving, ordinary
residence, lawful funding and actual continuing service are separate.

[TU01–TU12](../C04/12-effective-take-up-and-early-benefit-delivery.md)
makes covered established dues payable without duplicate applications and
offers assisted confirmation where facts or choices remain. Outreach,
proof, consent, independent decision and usable receipt remain distinct.

[AP13–AP18](../C04/06/06-essential-service-duty-while-responsibility-is-disputed.md)
assigns an interim owner and lawful payer for a designated cross-sector
responsibility dispute. Keep each service decision, consent and actual
receiving separately enforceable under its own authority.

[PSW01–PSW06](../C04/06/08-compatible-public-service-windows.md) makes
a reported clash answerable through a feasible option or an unmet duty.
An attendance record alone does not establish refusal.

[HG21–HG26](05/07-health-access-bottlenecks-and-first-budget.md)
applies effective access to the whole care path from attempted contact
through completed receiving and independent remedy.
