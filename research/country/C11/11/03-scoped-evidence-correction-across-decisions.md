# EV25–EV30. Carry a material correction to each decision that depends on it

13 November 2026 · Integrated design 433 · Candidate ordinary law

**EV25 — Record the material use with its meaning.** Under EV17 the
decision owner records the original observation family, revision,
affected population, period, units and transformation where material.
Identify what the input supports: a causal claim, a cost bound, an
operating condition or another decision premise. Keep controlling
law, budgets, value choices and actual authorization separately
identifiable. Record whether the conclusion needs the input, has
genuine alternative support, or merely cites it as context. An
unresolved or undocumented use remains open. Record it at the
smallest scope that allows meaningful review and reach, including
through a batch description where cases can still be found.
Preserve the basis in usable custody across tools and media.

**EV26 — Route scope, urgency and uncertainty.** A custodian gives
the original and corrected versions or a safe description, changed
scope and its earliest plausible consequence. The original warning
and earlier useful-time duties under EV18–EV19 continue. Send a
material notice to known decision owners and through safe public
and assisted channels for omitted or disconnected users. Each
receiver acknowledges acceptance or identifies a competent receiver
that actually accepts. Preserve original discovery and due dates.
Deduplicate repeated alerts while recording new meaning and new
affected uses. The custodian can correct its own release. Authority
for a dependent decision stays with its competent owner.

**EV27 — Reassess dependence, including omissions and alternatives.**
The owner checks shared source families across people, agencies and
AI outputs. Two analyses of one observation can test a method and
still depend on the same original measurement. Check uses reached
through translations, derived estimates, manual copies and shared
resource plans. A missing link warrants a targeted search and an
explicit unknown, particularly where a plausible omission could
change an outcome. Give priority to the nearest irreversible act,
protected due duty or person who needs timely relief. Compare
corrected and original results in the actual scope, and test
alternative support and material contrary evidence on their own
merits. A copied claim or unchanged checksum cannot resolve a new
objection or establish controller independence.

**EV28 — Give each affected use an accountable disposition.** Keep
a material result under its competent review when the corrected input
could change it. Narrow, retain or replace a conclusion with reasons
and qualified review of the exact remaining support. Keep a result
under a stated uncertainty when material scope cannot yet be
determined and name the next review date and any lawful temporary
protection. Record immaterial uses and reasons so unrelated work can
continue. Where a lawful release condition can no longer be met,
withhold only the affected optional release and take the applicable
review route. Use EV20 and EV21 for adopted rules, lawful vintages,
accrued claims and reliance. A notice or shared record has no power
to undo a vote, reopen a judgment or cancel pay.

**EV29 — Keep the route usable through disagreement or tool loss.**
Accept plain reports and protected oral or offline evidence. Use
restricted records or safe descriptions where appropriate, with
independent inspection within lawful authority. An interface that
cannot carry an original qualifier marks the gap and refers it
for clarification. The source custodian, affected decision owners
and independent reviewers retain separate responsibilities. A
regional or community steward can route a discrepancy without
having power to settle it. Give affected people direct ordinary
challenge if an owner ignores an alert, controls the only copy
or treats a routing receipt as a decision. Verify ordered relief
through actual receipt under EV22.

**EV30 — Fund the narrow duty and test wider effects.** The adopting
authority names paid decision owners, evidence custodians, lawful
alternates and independent review for material uses. A materially common
fact may be verified once for independent inspection. Preserve distinct
local evidence, governing rules, protected custody and review. Compare
a repeated check with shared verification on the same dated staffing,
cash and execution schedule. A shared finding leaves each dependent
decision with its competent owner and usable-time remedy. Track
acceptance, decision and effective remedy by use. A shared status
cannot close another owner's decision or an unserved person's claim. Preserve
ongoing duties, protection and records with accepted successors.
Test a shared-source defect, genuinely independent support, a
missing consumer, contested meaning and an imminent obligation.
Report avoidable restarts, time to useful correction, people missed,
custody harms and displacement of existing work. Keep retention
proportionate and avoid any compulsory record of private inquiry
or routine reading. Reopen on systematic omissions, arbitrary
holds, unacceptable burden or a simpler effective route.
