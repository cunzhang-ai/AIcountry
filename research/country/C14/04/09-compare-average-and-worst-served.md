# PCO32–PCO35. Test the national bargain where gains and losses diverge

*Design516 · Part of [public conditions](../04-public-conditions.md).*

**PCO32 — Preserve the population and reason for each result.** For a national comparative claim publish the observed population, eligibility or need-conditioned denominator, relevant endpoints, scope and coverage. Keep the actual lived burden separate from the explanation of why it occurred. Where repeated independently held observations show an apparently improved average, inspect the selected worse-served groups and relevant barriers under the same definitions. Report changes in group composition, selection and coverage uncertainty. Preserve usable direct case evidence and existing challenges. A survey value, particularly a reported zero at display precision, cannot dispose of an individual remedy.

**PCO33 — Give rivals the same chance to meet the observed need.** Before choosing a material budget response, state the causal mechanism each rival would change. Compare ordinary repair, a delivery or ownership rival, and income, time or access support where plausible. Use the same floor, recipients, original dates, full resources and adverse windows, while letting each rival use the method it actually proposes. Distinguish a lead about waiting, unaffordability or geography from a confirmed local bottleneck. Test credible shared alternatives where assistance can only work alongside a staffed receiving route. Repeat the comparison where demand, geography or the needs of the next cohort change which option can reach people and whether fixed costs are affordable. Give an exact narrower choice its own disposition. Do not credit an option for work, paid capacity or cooperation it assumes but does not carry.

**PCO34 — Give an explicit disposition on a mixed result.** Report the improvement, the loss, its affected people, uncertainty and prior duties. If an option misses a protected duty, use the applicable remedy and require a qualified protection or a narrower prospective choice. Where the remaining tradeoff lies inside lawful bounds, give the competent elected body the exact feasible motions, distribution and independent objections. It may select a critical intervention, retain and repair, or seek focused evidence before changing an ordinary duty. Give the unresolved comparator its own date and responsible owner. A narrowing gap can occur because the better-served group worsened, and a widening gap can occur while both groups improve. Neither gap orders the choice by itself.

**PCO35 — Test gains in receipt and later living conditions separately.** Where participation in a new route makes outcomes more visible, keep both the original eligible population and the observed coverage visible through review. Price verification and support, include people outside the ordinary channel through a lawful proportionate method, and check that paid effort did not shift harm to another service. AI may reproduce subgroup figures and compare alternative mechanisms. Accountable owners establish interpretation, qualified operation and a lawful vote. Reopen when a result changes under a corrected denominator, an affected group is missing or a qualified rival shows a larger usable improvement.
