# Design373. Preserve housing protection before insurance loss

[FR20–FR25](../../country/C09/07/05-home-insurance-exit-and-safe-transition.md)
adds usable notice and review, affordable effective cover, protection
independent of a lender and separate funded transition or risk reduction.

The [market evidence and worked case](../../settlement/home-insurance-exit-and-safe-transition/01-renewal-deductible-and-two-claims.md)
separates premiums, deductibles and timely cash for correlated
claims. Its figures qualify a scoped proposal. Actual source,
insurer acceptance, safe receiving, authorization and lived results
remain open.
