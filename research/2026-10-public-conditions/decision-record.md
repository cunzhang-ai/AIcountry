# Public conditions: compare lives without governing by a score

4 October 2026 · Integrated design 31 research selection; not enacted law

Prepared by an AI assistant without affected-party participation, independent
review or any authority to impose a publication duty on a real state.

## Baseline and question

The [design-30 predecessor](predecessor-design-30/README.md) protected original
records, plural inquiry and lawful policy trials. It did not require one
recurring, whole-country account to place a government's term beside
inherited conditions and plausible contemporary alternatives, then route
material findings to an elected public response before replacement is decided.
Sector success reports could therefore be individually accurate yet hide
people outside their denominators, a lost freedom or costs shifted abroad.
This is a logical failure route, not an observed AIcountry outcome.

The bounded choice is how a future public can ask whether lives improved
without turning a metric, expert body or AI system into a substitute for
democratic judgment. Values conflict: material security, freedom, relationships,
equal political standing, ecological options and fair treatment of outsiders
cannot be reduced to a common unit accepted by everyone.

## Coherent alternatives

| Option | Mechanism and benefit | Failure and tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Retain dispersed sector reports | Low new institutional burden; domain expertise stays close to services | Denominators, methods and effects across work, care, safety and ecology may never be joined |
| One national wellbeing score with automatic targets | Simple headline and strong managerial focus | Weights embed contested values, invite gaming and could let good averages erase rights or authorize policy automatically |
| Expert or AI commission certifies superior national performance | Comparative continuity and technical skill | Framing, sources and interpretation concentrate with a body the public cannot peacefully replace |
| Public testimony alone | Finds harms that indicators miss and preserves local meaning | Hard to trace scale, trends, causes and silent nonparticipants; powerful speakers may dominate |
| **Selected plural conditions account and elected response** | Reproducible dimensions, protected accounts of exclusion, rival comparisons, public hearing and reasoned elected choice | Needs staff, safe data, outside inquiry and opposition access; a formal report can still be captured or ignored |

The selected account is tied to the ordinary election cycle. C11's statutory
evidence service publishes source-backed, distributional series under a
prepublished core; plural independent and affected evaluators contest causes
and omissions; cabinet and opposition offer alternatives; the Assembly must
hold a hearing and give reasons. It may choose retain or no change. Scores
neither extend office nor enact law. Individual claims and urgent harms remain
on their ordinary, faster routes.

## Compatibility, authority and resources

The Assembly would enact the publication duty, scope, lawful data access,
privacy, schedule, minimum response and bounded funding. The government
being assessed cannot alone choose the comparator, delete an earlier series
or suppress independent criticism. The C11 service preserves original
custody, method bridges, coverage and uncertainty; it does not certify the
best social model. Opposition needs practical evidence and hearing access,
and courts need power to enforce access and record protection without writing
policy. Public accounts include outside investigators, safe testimony,
survey and field costs, translation, missing populations and remedy staff.
Appointment, vendor, funding and execution control must be tested as one chain.

The account compares within-country change, other jurisdictions and claims
of policy effect separately. It discloses context, data definitions, shocks,
uncertainty and missing evidence. It retains harms experienced by people who
left, never entered a service or live abroad where this country imposed costs;
it does not assign those people a national vote by statistical inclusion.
Personal data and sensitive testimony remain protected. No favorable average
offsets an unlawful detention, suppressed opposition or an unremedied claim.

This changes C03, C06, C11, C13 and C14, the integrated overview and status.
It reuses the design-30 [public-evidence analysis](../2026-10-public-evidence/analysis.md)
only for source custody and statistical safeguards; that study did not itself
select a term-end comparative mandate or prove any outcome. The new duty
requires its own legal authorization and resource account. Existing rights,
service claims and review routes do not wait for the report.

## Evidence and serious objections

The [UN Fundamental Principles of Official Statistics](https://unstats.un.org/fpos)
support impartial, transparent and accessible official data. The
[OECD How's Life? 2024](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/2024/11/how-s-life-2024_bdcf2f9f.html)
shows a multidimensional comparison of current outcomes, inequalities and
future resources. The [OECD policy-evaluation framework](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/policy-framework-on-sound-public-governance_c03e01b3-en/full-report/component-11.html)
distinguishes outcomes and policy learning. These are comparators for method,
not evidence that this proposed institution would improve a real country.
No original resident accounts, comparator data, administrative costs or
independent affected-person judgments were reviewed here.

An official core can exclude what matters most; people need to add and
challenge categories without letting one mediator speak for them. A term-end
report may arrive too late; ongoing individual remedies and immediate hazard
duties remain separate. Surveys and linked records can threaten privacy and
burden residents; use purpose-limited data, safe participation and reviewable
methods, and fund them openly. International comparisons can reward a rich
starting position or a favorable denominator; prepublish peers and differences,
and mark unsupported causal claims. Legislators can still ignore findings;
opposition access, public reasons and elections provide accountability but
do not guarantee a wise decision. A captured evidence service or court can
make formal access meaningless; alternate source custody and practical
enforcement need testing. The account may crowd out care workers and housing
maintenance if its own cost is hidden; C06 must compare those uses.

The result is a research selection only. Legal authorization, funded operation,
independent evidence, public acceptance and observed improvement are all
absent. More dimensions or a green link check do not prove superiority.

## Next bounded question

In a real potential adopting jurisdiction, can people who use, avoid and are
excluded by services test a trial account against original records and at
least two plausible comparator choices, while preserving privacy and a
timely opposition response? The test is complete only if it exposes an
omission, supports a justified no-change or change choice, and costs the
staff, field work and remedy path. New evidence of metric capture, unsafe
disclosure, inability to reach nonparticipants, or a better ordinary
parliamentary route reopens this design. If no credible comparison is
possible, report uncertainty and do not claim to outperform another state.
