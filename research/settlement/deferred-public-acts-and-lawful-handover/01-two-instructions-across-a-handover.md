# Two instructions across a lawful handover

This constructed case tests [DPA01–DPA08](../../country/C03/19/07-deferred-public-acts-and-lawful-handover.md).
At hour 0 a new government lawfully takes office. Two public actions are
scheduled for hour 2. Assume separate usable appropriations of four and
six units respectively. A delayed vendor message still shows the former
officeholder as active. The operative public record is independently available.

| Scheduled action | Status at hour 0 | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Four-unit housing award | Binding before handover, verified unpaid, ordinary payer independently authorized | The payer can settle the four units and verify receipt by hour 2 |
| Six-unit optional expansion | Signed preparatory instrument, but source law requires a distinct competent approval before commitment | Hold commitment under the old instruction |

The rule governing the optional expansion, rather than the signature's
format, is what makes the later approval necessary. If the successor
affirmatively approves on sufficient current authority and all lawful
conditions, the expansion can proceed from that decision's lawful date.
No acceptance can be inferred from replay of the former instruction.
The housing award does not depend on that choice.

If the ordinary payer loses its vendor, a funded independent payer with
an exclusive assignment and verified nonsettlement can still deliver the
award. If both routes depend on the same failure, report the four-unit
gap. If the original payment has already settled or its settlement is
unknown, the alternate does not replay it as principal. A settled amount
that the resident cannot use still has a receiving remedy. Preserve the
original receipt date and use any separately lawful interim support.

The figures and legal conditions are assumptions. This checks authority,
conditional commitment, exclusive payment and received protection. It
supplies no actual authorization or finding on any real transaction.
