# Proceeds, records, finality and lawful commencement

*Q10–Q12; part of [C08/15](../15-special-crimes-and-bounded-sanctions.md).*

**Q10 — Confiscation with ownership proof.** After conviction, a court may
recover proved net unlawful benefit or its traceable substitute under
published law. Identify the source, amount, defendant's interest and liens;
return traceable property to its lawful owner before Treasury proceeds.
No double recovery of the same benefit through restitution and confiscation.
An instrument may be forfeited only on proved offender ownership, intentional
use for a serious offense and proportionate necessity, with an innocent
owner/secured claimant's independent notice and challenge. A household,
business group or shared server is not automatically the offender's estate.
These powers do not authorize a separate no-conviction forfeiture regime.
Prior temporary asset preservation requires specific court grounds, narrow
scope, expiry and review; no general freeze follows an AI risk flag.

**Q11 — Records and usable reintegration.** On acquittal, noncharge or
termination without conviction, cease routine adverse disclosure and correct
state-held inaccurate labels promptly; pending-case preservation uses its
separate lawful purpose. Routine conviction disclosure ends two years after
the final noncustodial judgment, or five years after the earlier of actual
release and lawful custody expiry. Child conduct uses one year at the same
respective starting events. Poverty, unpaid fine residue or unlawful delayed
release cannot postpone these clocks. Apply stronger child protection. Later
convictions have their own clocks; they do not reset every old record.

Sealing prevents routine employment, housing, credit and civic screening;
it does not erase a lawful public judgment, journalistic history, safe victim
record or correction trail. Exceptional court/safeguarding access needs a
specific prospective role, relevant risk, necessary limited disclosure,
reasons, time limit and challenge; no unlimited public blacklist. Record
management has purpose-limited custody, retention and access review. Protect
children's identities and test whether notices actually reach downstream
users; a corrected database alone is not restored housing or employment.

**Q12 — Prosecution periods and jurisdiction boundary.** For K01–K24, a
proper charge and usable notice normally must occur within three years for
offense maxima up to two years, six above two and up to five, and twelve
above five. The period starts at the completed conduct/result; continuing
acts end when the proved prohibited conduct ends, not when effects cease.
No time limit applies to intentional killing, K11 torture, K06 slavery or
servitude, K13 child sexual acts/authority abuse, or K14 production of actual
child exploitation material. Other offenses against a child start their
period when that child turns 18. Proof of guilt and a fair trial remain
mandatory however much time has passed; delay never supplies corroboration.

An independent court may exclude only time proved beyond reasonable doubt
to involve deliberate concealment preventing reasonable discovery, or
deliberate evasion of usable lawful case notice, to a combined maximum of
three extra years. Neither a police file, complaint, routine secrecy nor
official inaction pauses the clock. Remand/trial fairness has its separate
shorter clocks; timely charging is no license for indefinite proceedings.
Acquittal finality remains P15, not a new prosecutor's opportunity to relabel
the same episode. The narrowly defined P15 fraud exception remains unavailable
without its distinct enacted rule; this schedule does not create it.

[P21–P24](../17/07-criminal-reach-and-transfer.md) now specifies territorial/
limited personal reach, foreign finality, custody credit and protected transfer;
[B41–B52](../17-jurisdiction-and-legal-cooperation.md) supplies civil forum/law,
recognition, insolvency and evidence. Mere transit/criticism creates no power.
Actual territory/treaty competence and full international-crime law remain
separate; applicable actual obligations are neither discharged nor waived.

Before lawful commencement, map each old offense/sanction/period, pending
case, custody time, paid/remaining fine, property, record and affected claim
to a competent successor. New crimes/longer maxima apply only prospectively;
lawful leniency benefits old cases, and this candidate does not revive an
expired prosecution bar. Independent counsel/courts review contested mapping;
no bulk deletion, new notice fiction or automatic release/collection by this
research artifact. Continuing support and safe receivers cannot wait for
the criminal process. All new periods are uncalibrated hypotheses; actual
source law, staffing, price/docket evidence and adoption remain unknown.

For RL cases, apply Q11 from the actual earlier release or lawful custody
expiry. Carry that date through recall and subsequent release for the same
conviction. [RL12](../28/03-reintegration-and-continuity.md)
