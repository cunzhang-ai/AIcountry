# Finance and build enabling capacity before relying on it

*Part of [C06 national package](../20-national-economic-package.md); design 60 research.*

An economic transition must state opening assets/obligations, source law,
authorities, affected people, exact prospective changes and successors for
claims, data, work, contracts and remedies. Show acquisition price, building,
training, overlap, maintenance, imports, commissioning, risk and who funds
them before later output or public return arrives. Do not finance the
pre-growth phase with revenues that require the unfinished project.

Compare maintaining the prior package, repaired provision, different
tax/income/ownership choices and a materially different social model.
Separate necessary investment from genuinely deferrable new work; merely
calling a service or capital line discretionary does not cancel a right.
A staged new start has an exact public rule, interim protection and review
date; a missed stage remains a duty/failure requiring elected response.
Neither lower hypothetical needs prices nor indefinite readiness delay
can convert a shortage into fulfilled protection.

Cabinet prepares coherent packages; affected people, workers, local bodies
and opposition can introduce alternatives through the existing public route.
The Assembly chooses general taxes, public debt, investment, source duties
and material allocation under rights. Treasury executes; sector operators
attest delivery; independent evidence, audit and courts challenge suppression,
unlawful action and nonexecution. Providers or technical reviewers cannot
veto national policy, extend their own powers or report an unpassed package
as operating. Clinical/safety case judgments retain their separate lawful scope.

Appointment, removal, funding, source access, court execution and transfer
must be assessed across the **combined** state, banks, firms, housing and
information system. A stronger public balance sheet does not compensate
for a captured election, private coercion or an irremovable guardian.
Protect informal cooperation and voluntary pooling; more compulsory
reporting or faster centralized coordination is not itself better life.

The current design holds every reference case from a full operating claim.
Research selection is the joined account and transition constraint; the
credit alternative, portfolio expansion and larger-GDP case remain rival
hypotheses, not selected law. Existing I05 and actual source-law boundaries
remain until an explicit authorized revision. Assess outcomes and rights
with original observations and actual affected participation before superiority
claims. Reopen on a changed tax base, controlled source, liability, price,
private/public capacity, harmful work/agency effect or failed transition.

The [C14 protected-stage rules T01–T12](../../C14/06-staged-capacity-and-protection.md)
now assign exact commencement, paid overlap, source-available commitments,
acceptance, shortfall and lawful replacement. The [bounded enabling envelope](../../../settlement/transition/README.md)
exposes missing salary/maintenance, unreceived FX and costs displaced beyond
the closing year. It finances no national floor and establishes no actual
source; existing tax, ownership and income alternatives remain open.

[C05 N01–N12](../../C05/07-production-learning-and-capacity.md) adds paid
instruction, credible net gains and continuing renewal. The [joined preparation](../../../settlement/capability/README.md)
prices intakes, instructors, bridges, facilities and attributed replacement.
Old national/transition results keep their original scope; new facility stock,
maintenance and wear require fresh whole-account reconciliation.
