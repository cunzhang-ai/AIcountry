# Design451. Make changes of public authority usable by third parties

**Baseline and choice.** Design450 preserves HT01–HT38 and DPA.
HT already authenticates the operative act and requires testing
at the accepting resource. DPA preserves lawful prior orders and
distinguishes restitution. The new issue is an external receiver's
access to a usable check, and its proved loss after the government
misstates an expired mandate. Drafting and parallel scope review
used AI assistance. The research choice is scoped direct verification,
with a separate claim where reasonable reliance on a designated
public error caused proved loss. Existing claims keep their own grounds.

**Rivals and objections.** A blanket stop protects against some new
errors but interrupts valid services. Trusting every apparently
official direction shifts political power to whoever controls a
notice. A broad indemnity can subsidize carelessness or collusion.
The candidate requires an attributable public error, reasonable
reliance, actual loss and independent proof. Preserve ordinary
competition and avoid automatic ratification. A supplier's due
payment under a valid earlier order keeps its own source and clock.
The separate claim needs prior law and a separately payable budget.

**Authority and continuity.** Constitutional law controls legal
effect. Career custodians issue scoped evidence. Affected residents
and suppliers have direct protected intake, and opposition retains
safe scrutiny. Independent adjudication and actual execution need
their own evidence and funds. Carry affected orders, contracts,
records and accrued claims to their paid successors. No research
entry authorizes actual procurement or changes an officeholder.

**Next evidence.** Exercise a tabletop with separately controlled
notice and payer routes, one genuine prior order, an erroneous notice,
and a resident with an approaching reply deadline. Verify original
intake, accepted service, independent adjudication and funds by
useful time. Reopen on disproportionate cost, a shared controller,
repeated misleading confirmations or a simpler qualified design.
Actual legal adoption, staff, affected participation and receipt
remain unverified.
