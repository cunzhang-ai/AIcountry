**NF17 — Land rights, not buildings or speculative permission.** Tax lawful
unimproved site/location value, including vacant sites, excluding structures,
repair/productive improvements. Respect actual permitted use/development,
protected tenancies, contamination, hazards, ecological restrictions and binding
obligations; no invented upzoning or settlement of historic title by a tax bill.
Strata/condominium interests use actual parcel share once. For separable long-
lease land interests deduct the same assessed interest from the lessor, so
combined rights do not exceed the one site base. A building levy, public ground
rent and no new land tax remain alternatives with different repair/title costs.

**NF18 — Named liability, no occupation tax.** Actual holder owes for owned
days; protected evidence establishes genuine nominee/beneficial arrangement,
not an AI family graph. Entity land is entity liability, not automatic personal
director debt. Residential tenants owe no owner levy by occupation or separate
pass-through defeating H rent security; economic rent incidence can still shift.
Exclude only actual noncommercial statutory essential-public-use sites. Commercial
public/charity/cooperative land has equal rules; internal public payments are
not outside national revenue. Cost rental recognizes actual tax/explicit subsidy
in its audited affordability/cost schedule, not a raid on maintenance or tenure.
