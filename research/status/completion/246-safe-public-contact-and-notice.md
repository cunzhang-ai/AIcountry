# Safe public contact and notice people can use

Design338 · Scoped finding for [country completion](../../DESIGN-COMPLETENESS.md)

[SCN01–SCN05](../../country/C04/06/05-safe-public-contact-and-notice.md)
selects a free route for people with unstable or confidential addresses.
It distinguishes routing, usable notice and service by judicial direction,
and preserves both parties' claims and ordinary urgent protections.

The [closed-counter comparison](../../settlement/safe-public-contact/01-closed-counter-and-hearing.md)
checks safe delivery, a standing payment, court directions and funded fallback.
Participation, privacy in use, lawful adoption and actual receipt remain open.
