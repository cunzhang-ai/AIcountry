# Physical capacity and payments outside the country

*Part of [C06 national package](../20-national-economic-package.md); design 59 research.*

Test the same package against public and private workforce/skills, sites,
land, water, firm power, materials, food, maintenance, logistics, imports
and ecosystems. Do not extrapolate a ministry's energy line into national
adequacy. Regular service, leave/training, standby and surge are separate
uses; distinct professions cannot be replaced by one generic FTE. Nominal
capacity requires physical locations, lawful operators and real availability.

The [reference vector](../../../settlement/national-economy/02-packages-and-limits.md)
preserves ecological and standby allocations rather than counting them
as routine supply. A larger economic/wage case that improves finance also
raises authored private demand and fails the energy/water check. Those
figures are hypotheses, not measured grids, catchments or emissions.
The original care and institutional staffing losses still need actual
trained receivers, independent sources/executors and paid replacement.

Keep foreign-currency obligations and conversion/payment paths separate
from domestic cash. Export receipts require real delivered sale and usable
settlement; a partner need not buy this country's planned output. Imports,
foreign interest and reserve use have due dates, currencies, suppliers,
safety, custody and continuity. A managed float or domestic issue is not
an agreed foreign payment. Productive inputs, food and medicine cannot
reuse one reserve, transport vehicle or correspondent balance simultaneously.

Compare a supplier, export, currency and power/water shock together with
care, courts, communication and lawful Assembly sitting. Explain the duties
and localities displaced by each contingency. Use the existing C06 scarcity,
C10 external and C09 ecological routes; a technical shortfall supplies no
new confiscation, conscription, treaty, return, vote or rationing authority.
Preserve unmet claims and actual received remedy even where no available
stock can meet them. Fiscal balance and lawful allocation are different facts.

To claim a plausible future productive gain, identify the actual mechanism:
trained people, safe technology, capital and maintenance, access, reliable
energy/material supply, worker/time distribution and external effects.
AI may help those bounded tasks, but estimated automation value does not
itself supply taxes, medicine, clinical judgment or political mandate.
Demand, pricing, substitution and supply assumptions must be checked together;
a changed GDP input is not an investment plan or observed progress.
