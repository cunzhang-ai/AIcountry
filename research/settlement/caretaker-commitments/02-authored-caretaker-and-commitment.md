# Protect a winter service without binding a ten-year policy

The following is an authored comparison. During caretaker an
essential heating route would fail before the next Assembly
decision unless covered. Ordinary due benefits and accrued
supplier or worker payments cost 4 before the handover. Available
and eligible current cash is 6. Standing law allows a qualified
short service bridge at 2, through the relevant hearing and first
lawful successor decision. The bridge is nonrenewable under this
fixture. Staff and the short service are assumed accepted only in
the branch that specifies them. Existing independent review and
court enforcement are assumed paid separately.

The outgoing cabinet has previously announced an optional ten-year
operating plan. Its unmade contract would cost 2 early and create
a further 10 of binding future payments. The announcement was made
before caretaker and no lawful contract had been concluded. Staging
it through a public company or choosing a later operative date
would leave its real future effects unchanged.

| Route | Early due payments and heating | New tail at handover | Result |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Freeze every new action | 4 paid, urgent heating uncovered | 0 | Fails the safety need. |
| Attempt to commit the whole plan in caretaker | Early payments could fit if authorized | 10 | No competence to enter this optional long contract in the fixture. |
| Pay due work and use the authorized short bridge | 6 paid with real heating cover | 0 | Preserves the service and the later elected choice. |
| Same bridge after a separate prior contract's valid first installment of 1 is found | 7 required before handover, 6 available | Prior tail priced separately | One unit exposed. Earlier liability survives and needs actual correction. |

The actual power to order short supply and the ability to deliver
it are distinct. If no supplier accepts before commitment, report
uncovered heating and use any independently lawful alternative.
A later failure after a valid commitment leaves the governing
contract and any termination costs to resolve. Do not claim the long contract becomes
authorized by that shortage. A valid pre-caretaker commitment is
different from the announced proposal. Its earned payments and
termination costs enter both alternatives before the next decision.
If prior law gives the competent Assembly a real decision before
the winter deadline, a complete longer-term statutory option is
a rival. A new government may choose it after independent review
of costs, legal scope, safety and other existing duties.

The selected bridge holds only on its declared source and staffing.
It is not a verdict on actual heating, public finance or caretaker
performance.
