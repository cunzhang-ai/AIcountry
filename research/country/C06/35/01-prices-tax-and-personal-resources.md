# FD01–FD03: tax, prices and personal resources

*Part of [C06 joined bridge](../35-finance-production-and-delivery-bridge.md); design87.*

**FD01 — Tax actual resources once before applying I05.** Compare progressive
individual tax on wages after contributions, earned/survivor pensions and
actually paid dividends/interest. Basic Y, I05, matching access refunds and
child support remain exempt in these candidates. Corporate tax applies to
identified positive net taxable profit after allowed capital consumption;
retention is not the shareholder's cash. F is a synthetic consolidated tax group;
actual firmwise losses/grouping and collections still require separate estimation. The model uses 25% corporate tax and
40% voluntary payout, not an assumed 800m profit base or universal full payout.

For this attribution hypothesis, compute work tax T(W), pension tax
T(W+P)−T(W), then other-capital tax T(W+P+D+I)−T(W+P). I05 receives net work
in E and actual net nonwork in O; its bounded pension disregard applies to
**net** pension receipt. Prorata attribution is a legitimate rival with different
benefit incidence, not an algebraic fact to conceal. The selected I05 taper,
protected cash, no presumed partner wealth and actual support-cost recognition
survive. Private child support 105.6m is paid/received once, not GDP or tax revenue.
A dividend owner's partner receives no assumed access. Taxing a pension requires
prospective lawful authority; the gross earned claim and actual correction remain.

**FD02 — Finance price relief and pension indexing together.** One comparator
has no consumption tax; another taxes half the personal basket at15%, with food
relief; another taxes it at20%. Utilities face the corresponding levy; rent
and access costs are exempt here. Business/public-input relief prevents a
Treasury self-payment becoming net financing. These are accounting experiments,
not a complete VAT code or selected rates. Actual collection costs remain unknown.

The selective/broad branches raise the adult personal basket to1,075/1,200 and
basic Y to193.5m/216m. School-food actual-receipt credits become13.71m/16.452m.
Their fixed-quantity general consumer-index proxies are1.078834/1.155994,
**different** from the personal-basket factor. Earned/survivor pensions therefore
become246.783m/264.434m in the fully indexed annual comparison. Timely I/Y relief,
Y03's actual price trigger and useful-time payment remain legal duties; no
annual average authorizes a first-year delay. The same basket linkage updates
RP's private gestational support example, without making escrow public revenue.

Housing here is explicitly costed, not capped at the old300: private structure
replacement266.25m + upkeep106.5m +56.8m passed-through site levy gives429.55m
private rent; public cost rent21m is separate. Utilities add60m before tax.
Land-levy pass-through, maintenance, tenure and dwelling choice are authored,
not observed rent incidence or proof that actual need is adequate. This is an
alternative price baseline, not an observed price change since design86;
actual accrued pension/index history cannot be reset to these assumptions. I03 continues
to recognize actual necessary shares and safe alternatives. These bill changes
raise I05 costs rather than being hidden outside the national comparison.

**FD03 — Judge net funding, not gross tax yield.** The moderate marginal rates
are0/15/30/45%; the higher-source branch0/25/50/70%, at real personal-basket-
indexed thresholds. All five source components are matched to their payers.
Households choose to consume80% of positive cash above necessities and save20%;
company payout, saving, exports and renewal have separate sensitivity cases.
No compulsory consumption or debt purchase is introduced.
