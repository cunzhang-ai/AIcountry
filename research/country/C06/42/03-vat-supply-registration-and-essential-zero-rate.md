# Recurring taxes: consumption VAT with a knowable essential floor

*NF09–NF12; part of [C06/42](../42-recurring-tax-and-noncash-work.md).*

[Design156 TN17](../59/05-country-package-and-source-continuity.md) retains
these explicit VAT/base/zero-list/threshold formulas as comparative benchmarks,
withdrawing default numerical replacement preference. Actual lawful retrofit
taxes continue until express prospective replacement; NF39/old rights persist.

**NF09 —10% on defined domestic consideration.** A registered supplier owes
10% of tax-exclusive consideration for a domestic taxable good/service, unless
NF10 zero-rates it or NF12 excludes it. The supplier is remittance debtor;
contract/pricing law remains B, not compulsory household enrollment. Domestic
goods are delivered here; services/intangibles use actual domestic consumption,
with proportionate contract/billing evidence, not continuous location tracking.
An actual barter exchanges consideration; an ordinary genuine gift does not.
Public interagency transfers/grants without a purchased supply are outside VAT.
No common source assumes everyone uses an AI wallet or pays by a platform.

**NF10 — A specified zero rate, with input credit.** Zero-rate these foods for
human consumption: grains/flour/plain bread, legumes, tubers, nuts, unprepared
meat/fish, fresh/frozen fruit/vegetables, eggs, unsweetened milk and unflavored
drinking water. Prepared restaurant/catering service, confectionery, sweetened
drinks, alcohol and supplements remain standard-rated; C07 essential food-
assistance supplies retain their statutory service protection. Zero-rate
prescribed medicines/medically necessary devices for patient use and clinical
treatment, school instruction and essential care meeting C07 definitions,
regardless of public/private provider. No permission to charge for an otherwise
free guarantee. Zero-rated is not exempt: legitimate taxable inputs remain
creditable/refundable. Actual exported goods/services are also zero-rated under
NF14. Rich consumers may benefit and borderline classifications cost work;
these distributional losses remain, rather than claiming perfect targeting.

**NF11 —2M registration with prospective notice.** M is the published TA01/PA10
annual scale, not GDP. When one actual business's taxable/zero-rated delivered
supplies (excluding one-off capital disposal) exceed2M in a rolling12months,
notify/register within30calendar days; status and tax on subsequent supplies
start on day31 after that original crossing, even if office entry is late.
No retrospective VAT on earlier supplies. A known above-threshold existing
business registers before commencement. Voluntary entry commits for two years;
below-threshold nonregistrants charge no VAT/claim no credit. Aggregate only
proved artificial fragmentation of one actually jointly controlled operating
business after reasons/full merits; shared family, premises or tools alone
are insufficient. No extra allowance per company/account/replicated agent.
The crossing date uses its then-lawful M; later reindexing does not erase a
trigger already reached. Mandatory exit requires12consecutive months below the
then-applicable2M and a prospective election at least30days before a quarter
start; voluntary entry also completes its two-year term. Actual lawful status
is not indefinitely withheld by clerical delay; TX reasoned decision/direct
review preserves the correct event, no backdated tax cancellation.
On lawful exit, reverse only actually obtained input credit attributable to
remaining private/exempt stock/use, never more than the traced prior credit;
no new capital charge merely because the register changes. Ordinary finality/
need/property protections apply. This threshold favors some small sellers and
can discourage growth; the higher-threshold/no-threshold rivals remain real.

**NF12 — Exemption/exclusion has a cost.** Existing residential rent and resale
of an existing occupied dwelling are exempt, without input credit solely for
that activity. A new dwelling sale/construction and commercial rent are standard-
rated if supplied by a registrant. Own occupation creates no invented sale.
Loan/deposit principal and genuine intermediation interest/margin are outside
VAT; separately priced advisory/brokerage/explicit service fees are ordinary
supplies. Mixed inputs follow documented actual taxable/zero/exempt/private use,
with answerable allocation and no full credit on exempt finance/housing. This
can leave embedded VAT and favor some financial services; a financial-activity
levy is not silently created. NF10's guaranteed clinical/education/care supply
is zero-rated, not an inconsistent exemption.
