# Legal bodies: attribution, finite penalties and surviving obligations

*Q07–Q09; part of [C08/15](../15-special-crimes-and-bounded-sanctions.md).*

**Q07 — Express attribution.** K01–K24 also apply to a legal body only where
their material acts can be performed through its authorized human conduct.
Prove a governing policy or an act/omission of a person actually delegated
control of the relevant activity, within that function, with the particular
offense's required fault. A nominal prohibition does not excuse a proved
contrary authorized practice. An employee's wholly private act, group
membership, ownership or an aggregate of unrelated people's knowledge is
insufficient. Prosecution bears all burdens; the body does not prove its
innocence by buying certification or producing a compliance plan.

For an offense expressly allowing gross negligence, organizational breach
requires a prior specific duty, actual control/ability, a gross systemic
departure and the required causal injury/death. Identify the responsible
decision structure and available safer action. No fiction of collective
intent fills a gap. An individual conviction is not indispensable to proved
body liability, and body guilt does not automatically convict any director,
worker, programmer or user. AI remains a tool under P03, with no punishment
or extra mandate assigned by copying agents.

**Q08 — Corporate fine.** The court chooses a proportionate fine between
zero and the larger of the band revenue cap or twice proved net illicit gain.
Revenue caps are 2% for offense maxima up to two years, 5% above two and up
to five, and 10% above five, using the responsible defendant's verified
preceding-year revenue. New bodies use actual revenue to date without a
forecast multiplier. No fictional future sales or automatic whole-group
turnover. A controlling
parent needs its own proved attribution; corporate division is not a defense
to actual policy/asset-transfer evidence. The same-course aggregate uses the
highest applicable band, not stacked percentages/gain multipliers.

Prove revenue/gain and contest accounts; take gravity, available assets,
repair obligations, worker/user effects and proportionality into account.
Fines use ordinary Treasury receipts and never reward the pursuing office.
They are not forecast operating revenue. Essential public firms are not
immune, but an internal public-sector transfer creates no national resources
and cannot discharge duties to users. A fine/repair shortfall goes through
the lawful B/U loss/receiving route; no automatic public or pension guarantee.

**Q09 — Correction, exclusion and continuity.** A court may impose specific
correction/reporting and an independently qualified monitor for at most
two years, reviewed at least every six months. A narrowly relevant activity
restriction or procurement exclusion lasts at most three years with the
same review; personal occupational disqualification needs the individual's
own proved conduct and a separate reasoned necessity finding, at most five
years. No general ban on voting, candidacy, organizing or lawful criticism.
These orders address the proved activity; they cannot commandeer the whole
economy or grant a monitor general rulemaking powers.

Dissolution is exceptional: prove the body was created or predominantly
operated for serious offenses, no adequate less restrictive correction and
a lawful asset/claim/receiving plan. Protect worker pay, deposits, client
property, valid creditors, evidence and essential service users through
named, accepted successors before stopping a necessary service. Immediate
danger can require a narrower urgent stop with separate emergency grounds,
safe receiving and prompt review; continuity is no immunity from stopping
harm. A receiver cannot invent money or compel informal unpaid cooperation.

Defendant, affected workers/users/creditors and innocent owners have timely
notice and independent challenge proportionate to their interests. Conflicted
regulators/monitors need alternate assignment and protected original records.
No firm purchases immunity by paying; serious personal offenses retain their
own prosecution. Correction, fine, restitution and forfeiture must be assessed
together for proportionate total burden and no duplicate recovery. Actual
monitor, court, source finance and successor capacity remain unverified.

[VD12/ER19](../../C03/10/08-precise-fines-and-continuing-duties.md) now proposes an explicit national-campaign-only additional body upper-ceiling basis: twice proved unlawful campaign value, with one flow/course once and full V/P/Q proportionality. This is a prospective law change for that exact duty, not an assertion that Q08 already valued electoral advantage or a larger maximum for other offenses.
