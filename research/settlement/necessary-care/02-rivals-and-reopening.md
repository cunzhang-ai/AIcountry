# Necessary care: incompatible models and an explicit choice

*Design82; normative comparison, not proof of a superior clinical/legal system.*

The problem includes inaccessible communication, absent individual evidence,
paternalism, private control and missing material care. Better coordination alone
cannot settle whose values govern bodily interference. An institution's intention
to help does not establish permission; formal nonintervention can also abandon
people who never had a usable opportunity to record directions.

| Whole choice | Protection/mechanism | Cost and current disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Previous B23 asset-only rule plus ordinary consent/emergency | Preserves agency and refuses broad guardianship; no new residual bodily authority | Does not answer necessary nonemergency personal care and may encourage waiting for emergency. Not retained as the complete endpoint |
| Strict no act without actual consent/applicable advance authority | Strongest protection from public/personal substitution | Can deny indispensable care and favor people with previously recorded choices. Retain as a real rival, not proof that later emergency harm was chosen |
| Best interpretation only | Uses personal history, responses and relationships, consistent with the Committee's central preference | Can generate fictional will where relevant evidence is genuinely absent. Use real interpretation first, but do not claim it always answers |
| Selected act-specific necessity | Explicit basic-care permission and heightened court authority for consequential acts prevent some abandonment without a general guardian | Still permits nonconsensual acts, actual unknown contrary wishes, normalization/capture and serious CRPD compatibility objection |
| Broad best-interests substitution | Allows a proxy/court to resolve many continuing choices, with possible continuity and efficiency | Transfers power to define a worthwhile life, can override refusal and normalize permanent dependency control. Not selected |

Irreversible therapy is not excluded merely to make the reference easier to
validate. It has actual serious-condition/alternative/independent evidence and
prior judicial predicates. Reproductive loss is a real consequence, not erased
by a therapeutic label. Personal reproductive use, research, donation, political
acts and actual opposition remain excluded. Actual competent clinical judgment
and safety standards are not supplied by these legal clauses.

For effective life support, the selected strict direct-bodily-burden/actual-function
rule rejects cognitive/social/fiscal grounds. A more autonomy-absolutist model
would refuse unknown-consent continuation; a life-at-all-costs model would refuse
withdrawal even where treatment inflicts severe refractory bodily harm. Both
remain moral rivals. The current no-assisted-dying authorization is retained;
no new scheme/exception is selected here. That can deny a desired voluntary
exit; palliative care and lawful refusal do not erase the disagreement.

Order expiry cannot safely be a medical withdrawal criterion. UA16's separate
minimum continuity source avoids an expiry cliff, with urgent24/72-hour review,
no escalation/objection override and visible breach. If independent review fails,
this can prolong unconsented intervention; it is not meaningful control merely
because a statute names a deadline. More severe automatic cessation has its own
life/bodily harm. No wording removes this structural conflict or staffs a court.

At adoption map actual directions, agents, orders, ongoing treatment/dependence,
claims, source privacy, staff and received care to funded lawful successors.
Do not bulk-convert disabled people into an unknown-will class or erase actual
consent/refusal. Independent qualified legal/clinical review, actual disability/
patient/worker participation and applicable treaty reconciliation remain missing.

Reopen on hidden capacity tests, ignored refusal, inaccessible support, unnecessary
therapy, scope drift, indefinite control, failed review/receiving or a better
complete rival. RR/FA now selects revision/attention; next joined material/transition and objective-wide comparison,
then full-objective review; do not automatically extend this local study.
