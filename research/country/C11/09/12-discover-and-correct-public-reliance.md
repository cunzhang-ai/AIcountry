# DP53–DP57. Find the AI the public service actually depends on

*Design314 · Proposed oversight of real public-service use.*

**DP53 — Start with the duty and how it is performed.** Each responsible
public or contracted duty owner records material digital and AI reliance
by service. Include built-in features, subcontracted work, shared advice
and informal outputs when their adoption changes receipt, priority,
evidence, a decision or usable fallback. Count actual cumulative effects.
Keep each different use of a common tool and its accountable owner
visible. Distinguish research, testing, operation, temporary suspension
and retirement. A listing supplies neither authorization nor a measured
gain. Ordinary drafting with no material effect needs no separate
entry for each worker or document. A person's private use of assistance
does not place them in a government inventory.

**DP54 — Check the record against actual work.** At each annual budget
and before material new reliance or renewals, reconcile the register
with scoped service walks, relevant contract features, current operator
practice and representative lawful case traces. Check credible reports,
new upstream control and affected receipts when they arise. Include
qualified independent inspection under existing AA and sector powers.
Staff and residents can raise an omission through a direct assisted
route and protected reporting under WB where it applies. Record the
checked sample, uninspected dependencies and accountable next action.
Use original records and proportionate evidence in protected custody.
Do not collect a national transcript of staff prompts or resident
activity. Public summaries describe material service effects and
available correction with lawful confidentiality.

**DP55 — Respond to an unrecorded dependency at its actual scope.**
The duty owner verifies which acts depend on it and records the
first affected date, known limits and unresolved downstream use.
A credible material risk triggers the relevant DP and sector response
in useful time. Suspend unqualified material reliance and keep
lawful ordinary intake, existing dates, urgent protection and
independent review operating. Compare independent receiving, scoped
correction and retirement before resuming that use. Preserve
unaffected tools and prior decisions unless their own lawful review
requires change. An omitted entry does not settle any person's
entitlement, invalidate every decision or establish fault. Disputed
effect and misconduct follow their separate lawful procedures.

**DP56 — Price simultaneous reliance as soon as it is found.** Reconcile
the additional use with common operators, updates, credentials, evidence
sources, staff and standby on the same useful deadlines. A purchased
product can conceal reliance beyond the office that bought it.
The common support office compares scope and publishes safe aggregate
exposure. Sector bodies retain responsibility for decisions and delivery.
If the independently staffed route cannot carry all original duties,
hold new optional exposure, fund a qualified repair or record the
uncovered work and seek elected correction. A delayed review or new
register entry does not move the due date.

**DP57 — Restore service and verify closure.** Keep the correction,
actual received service, continuing loss and paid new or ordinary
arrangement distinct. Qualified oversight reviews sampled cases and
limitations independently of the vendor or service team. Report
material unresolved exposure to elected scrutiny through existing
source and confidentiality law. AI may suggest omitted dependencies
and compare case and contract traces under scoped authority. It
cannot decide the scope of its own public authority, erase an
omission, confirm receipt or dispense with independent judgment.
Reopen on silent scope growth, retaliation, repeated omissions,
blocked source access or a less intrusive equally effective review.
