# EA09–EA12: legitimate bootstrap, fixed votes and explicit failure

**EA09 — Inauguration without fictitious predecessors.** Where a legitimate existing Assembly
and lawfully constituted electoral legal-source formation can exercise their expressly
authorized mandates, use EA05–EA07 for the seven drawn inaugural term slots. Where no such
predecessor Assembly and legal-source formation exist,
the lawful C14 founding public may instead **expressly authorize all seven inaugural
primary/reserve appointments through one paid36-person public panel**, under
EA03/EA07's27present/24fixed-vote and separate approval rules for each of14places.
Initial1–7year seat terms and long-run L/O/P labels are publicly drawn before nominations.
Within an inaugural seat term the selecting source remains this public route, including its
same-source reserve/replenishment; at scheduled expiry its recorded long-run L/O/P source takes
over. Creation of a later Assembly cannot reselect all initial commissioners early. The
inaugural panel expires after180days; failed places require a fresh panel with no approval
transfer, not a founder's acting appointee.

Before that draw, the legitimate founding instrument fixes and funds a **provisional founding
participation roll** under the actual C14 public/territorial process, its
eligibility/evidence/challenge rule and cutoff; this is not the election commission certifying
its own selectors. It names temporary paid roll/recruitment/draw custodians chosen under that
publicly authorized instrument, disclosed controllers, open application/verification and
auditable random draw. Original roll evidence, exclusions and source applications have protected
access and a competent predesignated ordinary judicial challenge route. No project-owner list,
website accounts, future commission, AI score or invented opposition creates that
public/authority. If lawful public mandate, usable roll, competent independent judges,
consenting qualified panel/candidates or actual funded custodians do not exist, those are real
founding failures; no selection or operational legitimacy is claimed. Preparation failure cannot
postpone an already lawfully fixed poll or extend an old mandate. One inaugural panel
concentrates fourteen choices and may share a founder's sponsors; disclose that risk against
divided lawful-predecessor selection, rather than label it three-source independence.

**EA10 — Five present, four affirmative.** A valid commission decision requires at least **five
lawful members present and four affirmative votes of the fixed seven**, including ordinary
administrative certification, public directions, secretary/finance-head appointment and its
chosen remedy within statute. Vacancies, abstention, recusals or being on remote call do not
lower either requirement. No source-presence condition gives one source a separate boycott veto.
Record actual joined disposition/reasons and dissent; four incompatible preferences are not four
votes. Elect a presiding administrator by the same vote for a two-year term, with no consecutive reappointment and never beyond their own commissioner seat term; absent
agreement, next lawful commissioner by published seat rotation performs necessary meeting
administration only. No casting vote, private assignment, general reserve activation or
ballot-alteration power. No formal commission majority can change statutory franchise/count or
replace authenticated evidence with estimates.

**EA11 — Conflicts/removal and exhausted membership.** Disclose direct
financial/family/sponsor/prior-participation interests and evidenced bias. Neither an allegation
alone nor naming all commissioners as defendants activates reserves; diffuse public
institutional interest or disagreement alone is insufficient. A challenged commissioner does not
decide their own disputed recusal. Unchallenged members may decide by EA10 if its quorum/votes
remain; otherwise an unconflicted ordinary court decides with B17 urgency where needed. Only a
source-preselected lawful reserve replaces an established inability/conflict. No recusal
loophole supplies eight seats, a lower vote or a new party-selected temporary voter.

An ordinary independent court may remove a commissioner on petition by a person with ordinary
standing, one fifth of full Assembly membership or an existing competent integrity investigator
for proved serious misconduct, persistent material statutory breach or inability after
reasonable accommodation. Notice, defense, protected source disclosure, direct-conflict recusal,
reasoned judicial suspension, appeal and applicable B/P process prevail. Lawful dissent, an
inconvenient count or changed government is no ground; unsupported complaint causes no
suspension. Removal takes effect after unused appeal time or one ordinary appeal is decided,
subject to competent further review/stays. The commission appoints neither the judges nor their
replacement and cannot deem misconduct to make its own quorum.

**EA12 — No vote is no commission decision.** Below5lawful members, or below4agreeing votes,
publish no valid decision, exact missing/disputed matters, continuing prior-law effects and next
lawful step. Already appointed local/secretariat officers continue only their own statutory
roll, filing, preservation, polling, mechanical count/reconciliation, public provisional-result
and funding duties; they neither claim commission powers nor infer a winner from a deadline.
Candidates/voters may directly obtain urgent ordinary-court factual/statutory/rights relief and
correction/enforcement against actual actors. Within express pre-enacted ordinary electoral
competence, a court can determine proved count/eligibility facts and the specific
certification/publication duty and require its named existing career executor to act; an order
does not manufacture four commission votes, settle an unresolved fact without proof, change
count law or acquire CJ's national constitutional jurisdiction. If neither a lawful commission
decision nor a competent judicial determination can be reached, national disposition remains
unresolved; current poll/caretaker/formation limits and continuing claims still apply. No
executive/vendor/commander/AI, poll estimate, source defect or missed deadline creates a winning
mandate.
