# C05. Market power, dependence and real exit

*Part of [C05 economy and work](../C05-economy-and-work.md); integrated design 98 research, not enacted law.*

A legal right to choose another employer, landlord, lender or supplier is weak
if the same controller owns the practical alternatives. Price and market share
matter, but the public question is also whether a person can refuse an
employer, platform or official patron without losing work, housing, payment,
communication or a route to complain. Scale can fund useful networks; a large
firm is not unlawful merely because it is large. Public monopoly can also
create dependence. The Assembly therefore sets prospective competition,
anti-coercion and essential-access law under rights and democratic control.
It cannot delegate the meaning of political loyalty or lawful speech to a
competition regulator.

A statutory markets authority investigates cartels, exclusionary conduct,
material mergers, common beneficial control and vertical bottlenecks across
private, cooperative and public enterprises. It maps actual switching cost,
entry barriers, worker and supplier bargaining, usable alternatives, shared
vendors and the ability of a dominant firm to turn economic dependence into
political retaliation. Local bodies, workers, consumers, competitors and
public-service users may present original evidence; confidentiality protects
legitimate trade secrets and vulnerable complainants. Neither a count of
company names nor an AI concentration score establishes independent control.

[MP/MA/MR](11-competition-and-economic-power.md) now supplies the one detailed
statute: exact agreement/exclusion/merger grounds, turnover and prior essential-
route gates, finite review, one court hold and bounded post-close challenge.
MA expressly replaces this office's unspecified staggered panel with one
six-year chief, protected career investigator/decision functions and ordinary
courts. Appointment needs three-fifths of full Assembly and protected opposition
support; no head vacancy creates a case-signature veto. A career decision
officer can clear/accept voluntary terms, but contested merger prohibition,
punishment and material property transfer require an independent court.
Ordinary lawful staff and sources still need paid, actual independence.

Remedies must restore usable freedom or lawful service, not merely produce a
fine. Depending on proved harm, competent bodies can order entry and
interoperability, fair access to an essential facility, cessation of exclusion,
contract correction, restitution, or structural separation when a workable
receiver and proportionate transition exist. Workers, pensions, suppliers,
users, data and public contracts continue under named legal successors.
A divestiture that leaves the same funder, cloud, key workforce or distributor
in control is not a real alternative. A public option or cooperative route
may be funded by the Assembly where competition cannot provide an essential
floor; it is accountable under the same access, audit and failure duties.

C03's civic-independence review separately protects election and opposition
access. C04 examines public procurement dependence; C06 protects payment and
credit access; C09 governs physical networks; C11 preserves lawful digital
access and evidence. A markets case cannot suspend a vote, ration food, set
tax or decide asylum. The common C06 account includes investigators, lawyers,
judges, source inspection, transition capital and any displaced service.
Actual market maps, firms, controllers, costs, legal powers and effects are
unknown. The [decision record](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-market-power/decision-record.md)
compares this layered route with private litigation, a central economic
controller and broad public ownership. Reopen if enforcement cannot prevent
cross-system dependence, its own panel is captured, or a rival architecture
provides more freedom and reliable access at an acceptable cost.

[V01–V12](../C08/19-regulation-and-bounded-enforcement.md) remains the common
process floor. MP supplies the previously missing competition predicates;
MR05 and the closed VD11 now select only narrow prospective cartel/gun-jump
punishment. Exclusion is civil/corrective; share, economic judgment, a below-
gate deal or late reply alone is no fine. Keep actual P/B/KR/PD/M powers,
property rights, workers and accepted successor/receiving requirements.
