# Copenhagen and East Denmark. A changed public duty can have several operators

These published records describe transition preparation in 2025–26 and
the planned legal transfer on 1 January 2027. They do not observe the
completed transfer, actual late-night coverage or health outcomes.

## Law, operator and staff are different decisions

Denmark's Act 1761 of 29 December 2025 gives the legal route for moving
specified functions to regions and for horizontal operating agreements.
Section 118d allows operation and, in the specified link to delivery,
allocation of the health service to be assigned by contract. Other
responsibility remains with the legal authority. The Act separately
amends transitional rules for certain time-bounded requests for continued
municipal operation. It does not turn an operating agreement into a
general transfer of political powers.
[Act 1761, sections 1, 2 and 14](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2025/1761/pdf)

The Copenhagen City Council's 26 February record approved a basis for
agreements with reservations, including a conditional acute-nursing
agreement only if municipal operation were selected. The record concerns
acute nursing, health and care places (70% of temporary places under
the local description), patient-focused prevention and specialized
rehabilitation including parts of advanced rehabilitation.

The region's 18 March announcement reports a mixed result. Under the
Capital Health Council, acute nursing will be operated by the region in
cooperation with municipalities. Copenhagen and Frederiksberg will
operate health and care places in their areas, while Bornholm's hospital
operates on Bornholm. The announcement identifies continued municipal
patient-focused prevention with a Bornholm exception and Copenhagen's
proposed role in a common regional specialized-rehabilitation model.
Do not transfer the city council's February conditional assumption about
acute nursing to the later selected arrangement.
[City Council record](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Borgerrepr%C3%A6sentationen/m%C3%B8de-26022026/referat/punkt-6)
· [Regional announcement and operating choices](https://www.regionoest.dk/nyheder/2026/flyt-af-sundhedsopgaver-er-nu-aftalt-i-hele-region-oestdanmark-1)

The regional announcement names division of staff, buildings, equipment,
debt and contracts as a further task and describes deadlines and a
separate dispute route for that division. Neither the operating choice nor
the legal date proves a completed division or a staffed service.

**Retain the chosen mixed operating allocation as the comparison baseline.**
The regional and city records distinguish authority, funding, operation
and asset division. They do not establish a better operator for every
service. Under the proposed country, require a complete cost and staffed
handover for each task and any shared local service. Preserve actual
care, assistance, original cases and independent remedy through a paid
interim route where needed. Use separate lawful approvals. Do not import
the Danish boundary, deadlines, health-council structure or amounts as
defaults for AIcountry.
