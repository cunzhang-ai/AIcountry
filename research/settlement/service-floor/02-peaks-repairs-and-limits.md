# Adequate annual totals can still fail at the due time

*Part of [service-floor comparison](README.md); candidate repairs are not adopted headcounts.*

Thirty shared clerical FTE at 1,200 hours/year give 36,000 hours. Ordinary
health records need 30,000 and an authored election operation needs 6,000
in months 9–10. Annual balance is exact, yet each election month needs
5,500 hours against 3,000: the two-period gap is 5,000 hours. Ten-percent
additional absence raises it to 5,600. Unused earlier hours do not preserve
a vote or a contemporaneous clinical record that is due later.

Sharing this clerical pool is a hypothetical capacity test, not authority
to mix health/election files, electoral judgment or independent appeal.
Actual competence, lawful appointments, privacy, vendor and reviewer control
remain unverified. People retain direct sector/independent routes.

All marginal pay, qualifications and usable time are hypothetical. The
repairs face the same absence, so adding only the unadjusted gap is inadequate.
They are whole-person rounded sources, not a claim that the necessary
people are available. They need N's paid preparation, willing source work,
actual receiving shifts/sites and a public funding decision. Other original
work displaced, training, supervision, contributions/needs/pension interaction,
capital, imports, travel, renewal and full national costs remain unknown.
No actual tax, appropriation, 39.70 budget or recruitment has been selected.

Alternatives remain live: actual additional public/regulated supply, a safe
different grouping or service organization, valid joint delivery, and a
prospective public coverage choice. A smaller hidden need estimate, unpaid
family labor, a lower measured take-up or assumed AI saving cannot repair
existing claims. An AI tool must show a safe net gain in the relevant role,
period and receiving task under N07, not a generic annual productivity number.

The [WHO benefit-package principles](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240020689)
provide context for transparent evidence-informed choices, and
[UNESCO's education-rights explanation](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/what-you-need-know-about-right-education)
for access, quality, freedom and correction. Neither supplies this design's
ages, hours, caseload, ratios, wages or superiority evidence. No full treaty,
clinical, pedagogical or staffing assessment was performed here.

The example tests neither the seven/fourteen-day legal access clocks nor
every health/learning/care duty, much less the country's other functions.
It exposes inconsistency between a nominal staffing minimum and explicit
partial service demands. Preserve the old tests in their original scopes;
do not transfer their pass to a new service schedule or these new repairs.
