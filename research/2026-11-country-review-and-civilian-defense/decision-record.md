## Diagnosis and different whole choices

Security needs, civilian legitimacy, individual rights and material/skill
limits can conflict. A command with sole pay, business, legal forum and records
could defeat a formally democratic appointing body; this is a causal design
argument, not project field evidence. Rights/readiness numbers are hypotheses.
Original OSCE1994 normative provisions and limited DCAF2002 abstract context
support the importance of civilian source/scrutiny/service rights; they do not
mandate our structure or establish safety/outcomes. Underlying empirical
country performance was not investigated. Common sources/models are no
independent reviewers and a completed artifact cannot grant authority.

Retain generic professional defense is a simpler real rival but leaves actual
power/source/exit discretion unclear. Select state/Defense Ministry employer,
civilian Minister/human Chief, C04 open qualified appointment and Cabinet-
replaceable command assignment with protected status, no military estate,
ordinary criminal justice and actual PO/OJ outside inquiry. Do not create a
protected-term military guardian or extra judge/committee to select tactical
targets. Lawful civilian policy must be peacefully replaceable; partisan purge,
retaliation and manufactured guilt remain forbidden/reviewable.

Smaller/demilitarized civil protection/diplomacy with actually accepted external
security, voluntary citizen/reserve, conscription/long service, civilian public
industrial capability and special military criminal forum are different
alternatives, not labels absorbed into DF. Smaller force may reduce control/
cost harm yet leave an actual threat uncovered. Conscription/long terms may
raise staff/readiness but impose coercion and unequal household burdens. A
special forum can supply expertise/speed but concentrate command/source power.
Commercial force autonomy can preserve some cash while defeating legislative
control; prefer actual civilian source/capability, not a free asset transfer.
Actual public/territory/threats/treaties/workforce/source could displace DF.

14-day ordinary/at-most90-day freely accepted command exit is a contestable
compromise, with stronger earlier terms and immediate unsafe/coercive-exit
grounds preserved.90 notice is not the90 war mandate; new deployment/title
cannot reset either. Voluntary terms cannot become indefinite emergency
conscription. Potential turnover, loss of specialized readiness and paid
replacement costs are material objections, not assumed away by AI or allies.
