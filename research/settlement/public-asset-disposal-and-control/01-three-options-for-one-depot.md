# Three options for one public depot

*Design397 · Constructed comparison in abstract fiscal units.*

A depot supports a continuing transport service and also earns
commercial income. The same ordinary public service floor and
three units of already due environmental work apply under every
alternative. The host and workers retain their rights. Comparable
tax flows and unrelated ordinary costs are outside the example.
Assume service is independently accepted in the central comparison.
Count the public fiscal position, not a social welfare score.

| Choice | At decision and closing | Years 1 and 2 | End of year 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retain and repair | Pay 3 for existing environmental work and 2 for renewal | Net commercial receipts of 6 after maintenance, less 2 for the public service, leave 4 each year | Publicly owned depot with estimated net residual value of 12 |
| Grant a finite concession | Receive 7. Pay the same 3 and fund 2 for transition. Usable net cash is 2 | Rent of 2 less separate public service cost of 1 leaves 1 each year | Depot returns with estimated net residual value of 8 under accepted handback terms |
| Sell on the offered terms | Headline consideration of 17 includes 3 contingent in year 2. From 14 actually paid, deduct 1 transaction cost, the same 3 and 4 for the funded handover. Usable net cash is 6 | Pay 2 each year for continuing public service | No publicly owned depot. The 3 contingent receipt is shown only in the branch where it actually settles |

Using an authored 5 percent annual comparison rate, the central
two-year fiscal values with residual assets are about 13.32 for
retention, 11.12 for concession and 2.28 for sale before the contingent
receipt. If the contingent 3 is received in year 2 the sale rises to
about 5.00. The 6 in closing cash is neither a recurring source nor
a measure of the asset given up. The residual values are estimates of
assets held at the end of the comparison, not available cash at close.

Select retain and repair in this central case, assuming the voted
source can pay the opening 5 and the service can actually continue.
If annual retained net receipts fall to 1 and residual value to 8
while concession terms and acceptance remain qualified, retention
falls to about 4.12. The finite concession then leads fiscally.
If the highest sale bidder cannot prove the required handover,
that offer cannot close under the proposed authority even when its
cash price is higher. Reconsider the exact funded alternatives with
the relevant lawful owners. The fiscal ranking leaves service,
competition, democratic control and affected outcomes for their own
evidence and decision.
