# Primary records for conditional coverage and protected transition

- [NHS England Cancer Drugs Fund activity update, Q2 2024–25](https://www.england.nhs.uk/long-read/cancer-drugs-fund-activity-update-q2-2024-25/)
  supplies the monthly notifications in Table 1, the reported drug
  cost in Table 2 and the cumulative reappraisal paragraph. The
  [observation file](observations.json) transcribes those fields.
  The publication expressly distinguishes notified and treated
  patients. The cost totals do not identify pathway or exit costs.
- [NICE managed access](https://www.nice.org.uk/what-nice-does/patient-access-schemes-and-pricing-agreements/managed-access)
  and the [NICE appraisal recommendations manual](https://www.nice.org.uk/process/pmg36/chapter/committee-recommendations-2)
  distinguish a finite evidence plan, commercial terms and a later
  routine-use decision. They supply a procedural comparator and no
  cost-effectiveness threshold for the proposed country.
- [NHS England, Appraisal and Funding of Cancer Drugs from July 2016](https://www.england.nhs.uk/statistics/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/appraisal-and-funding-of-cancer-drugs-from-july-2016.pdf)
  gives the historical funding, expenditure control and exit terms.
  Its model agreements are evidence of institutional design, not
  evidence that an actual patient received continuing care.
- [European Medicines Agency, conditional marketing authorisation](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/human-regulatory-overview/marketing-authorisation/conditional-marketing-authorisation)
  describes a distinct authorization with regulatory obligations and
  reassessment. It does not decide national public reimbursement.

The reported groups have distinct denominators and periods. No
observed clinical effect, current cost for an individual treatment,
funding source, or comparative patient outcome is established here.
