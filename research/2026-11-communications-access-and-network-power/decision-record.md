# Design224 decision record. Communications access and network power

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned design 223](baseline.json)

## Choice and authority

The prior country had a general connectivity floor, sector regulation,
continuity and alternative public routes. It lacked a usable service
claim tied to an actual carrier, household price, wholesale remedy and
independent emergency acceptance. CN01–CN12 fills those gaps. The
[working method](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision record](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md)
are the proposed research procedure. The decision owner here is the
research project. Enactment requires the lawful Assembly and applicable
local and rights authorities. Operators, officials and AI have no
independent power to commence the package.

Equal civic access, open carriage, lawful choice and freedom from
surveillance are design commitments. The content of a usable offer is
contextual and measurable. General affordability, sufficient standalone
operator entry, demand, technology and readiness remain empirical
questions. Portable identifiers apply where interoperable and lawfully
assigned. Physical substitution preserves only the named timely task.

## Real alternatives

| Alternative | Mechanism and gain | Failure or condition |
|---|---|---|
| Retain and repair | Use actual existing network, targeted household finance and existing competition duties. Keep viable operators, staff and contracts. | Prefer where it delivers the floor and verifiable fallback. A sole shared core and discriminatory access can survive an extra grant or resale brand. |
| One publicly owned and operated carrier | Unify renewal and procure against elected priorities. In a small or dispersed place, one accountable network operator may cost less. | Requires ownership transfer, lifecycle finance, independent political checks, paid operating successors and demonstrated alternative control and routing. National integration alone can concentrate political and outage power. |
| Shared essential bottlenecks with plural provision where feasible | Enforce basic access, an arm's-length wholesale offer and timely switching. Separately contract independent emergency delivery. | Foreclosure may shift to queues and repair, and regulation can itself be captured. A small place may have no viable second carrier. An authorized residual provider and timely external remedies must be funded. |

Select the last architecture at demonstrated bottlenecks and retain
effective ordinary routes elsewhere. Compare direct public, cooperative
or private operation for each actual local mandate. Do not divide viable
routes merely to create more operators. The [same-scope case](../settlement/communications-access/02-locality-and-shared-failure.md)
leaves both capital and recurring gaps visible, and distinguishes a
partial emergency fallback from ordinary household continuity.

## Compatibility, custody and transition

The C09 regulator and an existing civil portfolio carry the technical and
guarantor roles. Prior competition, private-life, court, election, cyber,
emergency, electricity and shared-reserve powers retain their scopes.
Review cumulative control over nominations, budget, evidence, restoration
credentials, interim relief and actual execution. An operator's source
records cannot be the only channel for reporting its own failure. Compare
dependent providers by control and actual resources, including AI tools
and vendors. Keep protected claimant evidence purpose-limited. Preserve
public reasons and continuing claims without copying private testimony
into a public record.

Set the prospective standard, audit actual existing rights and publish
the financed receiving schedule before any start. Map old contracts,
workers, licenses, credits, port requests, disputed bills and pending
outages to accepting owners. If transfer or payment fails, keep valid
prior duties and maintain the separately lawful temporary route. Neither
an expired award nor an insolvent license shifts a duty by itself. Use
the lawful funded successor and record actual handback.

## Bounded next check

Inspect one actual hard-to-serve district, one proposed network dependency
pair and one independently staffed emergency receiver. Compare retained
repair with the shared-bottleneck option against the same task and
budget. A lawful independent team should obtain voluntary and accessible
affected-party input, a wholesale order and repair test, direct
measurement of ordinary receipt and a safe simultaneous failover
exercise. Reopen for material exclusion, foreclosure, recurring
underfunding, adverse outage results, practical capture or a better
feasible alternative. Evidence inspected is documentary and the failure
case is authored. Actual affected-party participation, independent local
review, enactment, paid execution and lived outcomes remain unestablished.
