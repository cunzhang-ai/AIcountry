# Three cases and one judicial roster

*Design469 · Constructed test of the existing judicial and budget design.*

The [shared budget comparison](../democratic-safeguards-in-a-shared-budget/01-funded-on-paper-and-blocked-in-use.md)
tested control over records and payment. This case tests a different
failure. The records and money are accessible, but different courts
need the same qualified people during one short window. The cases,
prices, rosters and dates are constructed.

| Pending matter | Competent route within the fixture | People needed in an overlapping window |
|---|---|---:|
| Constitutional court challenges a decision about its own institutional funding | CJ12 external chamber with seven seats, quorum of five and four affirmative votes for a disposition. | 5 |
| Ordinary electoral appeal under EA16 already before a competent ordinary appellate formation | The separate ordinary court sits under applicable electoral and ordinary court rules. This appeal uses the OJ02 three-judge formation. | 3 |
| Urgent judicial review of an individual's custody | Designated unconflicted ordinary judge under the applicable custody clock. Existing legal aid and protection continue. | 1 |

The last useful times in this fixture do not permit compatible
sequential use. All eight available judges are assumed eligible for
their stated assignments under the published rotations. The CJ12
roster contains seven named seats. Only five eligible assigned
members can actually sit in the window. A separate three-person
first stage belongs to CJ13 discipline. It is not required for this
CJ12 budget case. Notice, records, counsel, signed dispositions and
execution are separate work. Opposition evidence is due through
the Assembly's own authorized route and can still arrive when an
individual court case lacks a judge.

| Response | Result |
|---|---|
| Count each formation in isolation | Each matter appears to fit. Together they need nine distinct judges and have eight. Report the exposed act under existing assignment, urgent relief and scheduling rules. The count itself establishes no priority. |
| Draw a ninth from the already eligible, unconflicted reserve | If prior law permits the assignment and the judge accepts before the deadline, reserve one funded additional block and protect their origin docket. All three formations can sit. The elected budget buys paid availability and cover, not a ruling. |
| Use a reserve with a direct personal interest in the funding claim, or move ordinary appellate jurisdiction into CJ12 | Neither solves the lawful shortage. Any independently qualified other assignment must follow the prior published rules and remain on its original clock. |
| Both forums share an independent payroll but the executor loses the only usable record | The affected hearing or ruling may still occur. The order cannot reach the recipient through that route. Use an authorized distinct executor or preserve the unexecuted order and original remedy. |

The authored reserve costs two units including paid origin cover.
The standing margin of one unit is insufficient. A separately
voted additional unit can fund this modeled window. A cheaper
rival can schedule distinct compatible times if actual deadlines
permit. In this fixture they do not. Select the qualified paid
reserve only if its legal roster, source and availability each
qualify. A court cannot reassign unrestricted spending or enlarge
its jurisdiction to fill the gap. If an affected judge must
recuse, two more seats on paper are no assurance of a lawful
replacement. A valid vote still requires four affirmative votes
in CJ12 and the applicable thresholds in the other courts.

## Execution and a later conflict

If the opposition cannot receive its copy on time but the court
has a lawfully protected original, the court's work can proceed.
If the court signs an enforceable order but its executor cannot
serve it on time, the finding remains distinct from delivery.
The second event does not erase the original service, case or
election clock. Preserve protective acts already authorized and
refer any unfunded correction to the competent elected body.

If the same reserve judge is needed again in an overlapping
ordinary matter, verify the next paid assignment and keep the
ordinary case on its own date. A distinct alternate, actual lawful
sequence or additional authority may repair the shortage. No
automatic renewal, special executive jurisdiction or AI allocation
follows from a failed result. Publish safe distinctions among
unheld hearing, no disposition, signed but unexecuted order and
timely receipt. Verify actual loss with the person affected.
