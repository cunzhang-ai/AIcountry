# Three investment choices and one conditional public return

*Design298 · Constructed supplement to the technology transition.*

The comparison concerns optional public support for a reusable
digital tool used by essential service offices. The agreed public
output is an accessible interface and an independently maintained
operating route with qualified staff and a tested handoff. The
same offices keep due work, wages, resident protection and
independent review on their prior sources. The optional envelope
has three early and six cumulative units after those duties.

| Exact offer | Early / cumulative gross public cash | Public outcome and exposure |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Ordinary repair only | 0 / 0 incremental | Maintain the existing service and leave the added capability open. |
| Purchase the defined open operating route | 3 / 6 | Pay for accepted scope and independent maintenance. No financial return is assumed. |
| Staged support with the same public scope and a bounded return | 3 / 6 | Pay for the same access, handoff and accepted operating capacity, with a separately enforceable future return. The costs include the independent audit and closure work. |
| Paid public equity and direct operating capability | 4 / 7 | Buy defined title, governance and an actual operating route. A future sale or dividend is uncertain. It exceeds the stated envelope. |

Prefer the staged choice in this declared tie only when the
public delivery terms, competition, gross cash and independent
verification actually match. If the return is opaque, costly or
requires exclusive access, compare the simpler open purchase on its
own terms. A qualified direct public or cooperative route can win
with different verified costs. Any award needs separate authorization
and actual acceptance. Neither financial interest nor a grant
justifies a broader AI reliance or replacing a due human route.

The staged offer's illustrative return is half the annual qualifying
distributable cash above three units, capped at two. Its agreed
base is after actual operating costs and the already applicable
worker and tax obligations. At two units the payment is zero.
At five it is one, leaving four in the company's distributable
account. At eight the cap gives two. This is a contract
hypothesis, not a tax schedule. Uncollected obligations and later
ownership valuations add zero to the early envelope. Count a
received return only on its own date and lawful source.

If only two early units are usable, none of the additional
offers can bind on these terms even when the future return
reaches its cap. A failed acceptance after a binding award
requires safe continuation and lawful contract treatment,
including any earned payment. Preserve prior services and
worker claims. Compare on actual burdens, useful work and
independence before claiming a benefit. No current price,
adoption or actual future return is assumed.
