# Design307. Emergency funding and the interval before restored service

11 November 2026 · Working decision for the first programme

## Baseline and new evidence

The [Design306 baseline](baseline.json) selects ordinary repair and
preparation on public accounts and earlier decisions. Later records
establish a released regional appropriation and an identified older
people's share. The network recovery proposal makes the separate
financing of institution-side continuity explicit. Apply the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md)
and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md). Codex assisted.
This assessment used public records. Actual affected participation
and qualified independent review remain open.

## Choices and disposition

- **Use ordinary service and carry approved preparedness.** Retain
  the existing public obligations and actual appropriations. The
  inspected records establish authority for specified purposes and
  leave operator-side payment, procurement and safe delivery open.
- **Prepare one priced local reserve with a funded shared alternative.**
  Select this decision for the water and care interface. Verify the
  facility and the assisted home route at the same dates. A funded
  extension protects people when the network objective slips. Shared
  capacity also needs donor cover, an accepting operator and an actual
  date for the next replenishment.
- **Rely on network restoration and private preparedness.** Household
  stocks can help where people can safely acquire and use them.
  Price the exposed home routes and public facilities. The recovery
  objective alone leaves the extended window uncovered in the exercise.
  The consequences may fall hardest on people already needing care.

Duplicate funding can make either local or shared provision appear
affordable. Reconcile the same grant through both sides and show the
true purpose of any different programme. Keep fire protection,
drinking-water and sanitation duties on their actual sources.
Regional coordination must preserve service choice, funded local
duties, qualified work and independent review.

## Compatibility and next verification

[Proposed rules](../country/C14/06/09-reserve-funding-and-usable-restoration.md)
carry the observed appropriations and use the water proposal
conditionally. Preserve tariffs, source rights, contracts and existing
public duties. The [primary records](sources.md) and [exercise](../settlement/integrated-first-programme/08-copenhagen-emergency-reserve.md)
establish distinct evidence. No actual site acceptance or outcome has
been verified.

Choose a consenting facility and a linked assisted home route.
Obtain the exact lawful source, operator receipt, priced delivery
and paid staffing through two calls and delayed restoration.
Compare a paid shared reserve with ordinary local provision at the
same deadlines. Reopen on an effective water decision, an actual
contract, delayed receipt, unmet household need or a stronger
qualified route.
