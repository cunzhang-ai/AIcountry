# TR09–TR12. Make exit lawful without breaking people's lives

*Design223 · Part of [treaty commitments](../15-democratic-treaty-commitments.md).*

**TR09 — Put renewal and exit on the public calendar early.** The
competent foreign affairs officer tracks notice, external receipt,
automatic renewal, cessation, review and later claims. Put options
before the Assembly with enough time for its actual scrutiny and any
necessary external response. Present retain, amend, negotiate extension
and lawful suspension or exit with costs and reliance. Material
withdrawal and suspension use TR04 with exact terms and dates.
The vote authorizes only the stated legal act. The lawful external
actor sends the permitted notice and tracks the actual response and
effect. A missed notice may leave prior obligations in place under
their terms. Expose that outcome, its owner and available lawful
correction. Neither inaction nor a domestic repeal independently
changes the actual external terms.

**TR10 — Protect people through the first useful date after change.**
Before relying on entry or exit, identify pending protection, earned
pension and worker claims, supply and service dependencies, guarantees,
lawfully continuing liabilities and protected record custody. Name
actual owners, sites, qualified staff, lawful currency and dates,
needed permissions and accepting receivers. Price temporary cover
and actual overlap under FB, DB, FT and OP/T. Give people timely safe
notice and a direct independent route. Apply nonreturn, citizenship,
care and accrued rights under their own law. A foreign partner's
promise or expected receipt cannot fill a gap. Protect available
duties under prior law, expose remaining failures and bring a
material source or transition decision to elected authorities.

**TR11 — Address noncompliance without inventing a foreign result.**
Courts review domestic competence, disclosure and protected rights
within their own jurisdiction. They may order preservation, timely
domestic protection and correction by lawful receivers. Review an
unauthorized or disputed commitment separately under the instrument
and applicable international law. The actual external legal position,
including any available objection, notice or dispute route, is recorded
by competent officials and independently contestable at home. Do
not infer that a domestic defect voids external effect. The executive
can seek lawful accommodation or correction within authority. Foreign
acceptance, tribunal consequences and domestic rights keep their own
grounds. No court or official can supply another party's consent.

**TR12 — Fund open scrutiny and continuity through government change.**
Use existing foreign affairs, sector, Treasury, Assembly Clerk and
independent court roles. Fund qualified counsel, authentic translation,
record custody, minority scrutiny and execution separately. Preserve
notice and consent records and hand open obligations to actual lawful
successors. Publish safe status, deadlines, known failures and an
opportunity for review before renewal. No new diplomatic court,
master data service, voting threshold for ordinary legislation or
military power follows. Reopen for missed notice, a shifted burden,
abuse of confidentiality, changed legal effects or better verified
ordinary cooperation.
