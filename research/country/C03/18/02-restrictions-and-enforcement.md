# AX06–AX10: inconvenience is not a licence to silence a crowd

*Part of [C03/18](../18-peaceful-assembly-and-public-dissent.md); design 189 candidate.*

**AX06 — Tolerate ordinary democratic disruption.** Temporary traffic/pedestrian
delay, audible criticism, ordinary commercial loss, inconvenience, offense,
embarrassment, an unpopular cause or missing notice do not by themselves justify
restriction/dispersal. Nonviolent civil disobedience does not become violence
by label; separately proved unlawful acts retain actual P/B elements/defenses.
Public safety/health/security are purposes requiring specific prior law and
facts, not self-executing grounds. Genuine clinical/safety/access duties continue.

**AX07 — Courts decide planned material restrictions, not whether to allow
assembly.** Before imposing a material nonconsensual assembly-specific place,
time, manner or prohibition condition, the public body must obtain an ordinary
court order under AX08. It presents actual facts, targeted alternatives,
participants'/other users' burdens and proposed exact scope. Routine voluntary
coordination/neutral lawful facility operation needs no court application.
No ruling is needed merely to assemble; an application or missing judge does
not create a planned restriction or approval by silence. Actual hazard-specific
HP/RC/P powers retain their own scope, with no relabeling to evade AX.

Affected participants/authorized representatives and actually affected others
receive usable notice/evidence and a meaningful chance to answer, including
safe remote/assisted participation. No invented organizer binds a leaderless
crowd. Use B17 urgent receiving and relevant expertise; courts adjudicate
necessity, not political merit or preferred slogans. An ordinary administrative
reply clock cannot postpone useful-time review. AX09 alone supplies the narrow
new on-scene exception; actual earlier/stronger source protections survive.

**AX08 — Concrete grounds, least necessary scope.** An assembly-specific
restriction needs a proved serious present or clearly imminent threat to life,
bodily safety or protected rights/access, causally connected to actual event
conditions and not adequately addressed by feasible less intrusive protection.
For a restriction relying on disruption rather than imminent bodily danger,
identify serious AND sustained actual or clearly imminent impairment of others'
protected access/rights, such as sole necessary clinical access, shelter or
essential supply. Concrete event facts must support prevention, not speculation
or a zero-disruption standard; ordinary delay/commercial loss is insufficient.
Assess actual severity, duration, alternatives, unequal burdens and protected
political opportunity, not a score or demand for zero disruption.

A whole-event prohibition/dispersal is last resort: targeted violence response,
partial access, limited relocation/timing or protection must be inadequate.
Officials cannot create a ground by withholding reasonable facilitation or
allowing hostile actors to dictate it. A necessary life-protecting response to
actual danger remains possible despite prior official failure; that failure
still has its own remedy. Every assembly-restriction order names authority, facts, event/place/actions,
shortest term and challenge. It expires when grounds end, its specified event/
term ends or seven elapsed days pass, whichever is earlier. Further restriction
needs fresh court grounds/hearing, no automatic rolling future-protest ban.

**AX09 — Strictly limited immediate civilian command.** If AX08 serious present
harm or clearly imminent grave physical danger, supported by specific actual
facts, cannot safely await court protection,
PO's predesignated qualified on-duty civilian incident/territorial commander
may direct the minimum necessary temporary access/particular-place change or,
as last resort, whole-event dispersal. Name the actual commander/service identity,
facts, alternatives, scope and first material restriction time. Seek the duty
court immediately, earlier than loss of the actual expressive opportunity.

This new unilateral event authority ends at the earliest end of grounds/event/
specified term or SIX elapsed hours from first material restriction without an
independent court order. Six hours is an outer maximum, never a waiting right.
No reset through renamed traffic/health/security grounds, new commander, route
or overlapping instructions concerning the same underlying event/harm. It cannot
replace/extend an expired or refused court restriction on materially the same
facts. Existing
distinct urgent individual protection/rescue powers retain their actual predicates;
they cannot be used serially to recreate an expired whole-event dispersal.
A superior's preference, resource shortage or an emergency declaration adds none.

**AX10 — Explicit physical-task authority with the separate LF ceiling.** The
proposed AX Act authorizes sworn civilian police only to use minimum necessary
physical escort/removal to implement a valid AX07–AX09 specific access/relocation/
dispersal act, when safe workable nonforce measures are insufficient. Give usable
warnings, genuine opportunity to comply, accessible help and safe exits, with
continuing medical aid and reassessment. A command/court order alone is not the
second LF02–LF04 necessity/life test; an officer must refuse an unlawful/unsafe act.

No new weapon, general search, seizure, identity demand, arrest, detention,
surveillance or collective-containment power. A cordon/move that actually prevents
leaving needs its distinct lawful liberty grounds/P clocks; AX's six-hour event
clock grants no six-hour crowd custody. LF07's individual targeting/device bans
and LF04's life threshold remain. Basic refusal/nonviolence/missing notice alone creates
no new crime or force entitlement. Contractors, marshals, models and defense
staff gain no AX physical power through procurement or a facilitation request.
