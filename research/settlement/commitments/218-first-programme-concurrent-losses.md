# 218. The first programme under concurrent losses

| Owner | Continuing or contingent work | Acceptance or failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Treasury and local payer | Reconcile all affected duties and exact optional choices on original dates | Verify owned cash, actual advance, distinct reserve and subsequent payments. |
| Water, care and housing operators | Preserve continuing work and accepted obligations through the next call | Qualify actual receiving, staffing and substitute. Keep unmet support and payment visible. |
| Elected bodies and independent reviewers | Decide exact remaining scope with a real retain-and-repair alternative | Preserve prior authority, affected challenge and separate democratic capacity. |
