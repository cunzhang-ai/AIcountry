# Direct your assistance without being required to run an employer business

2 November 2026 · Design 116 · English country-design selection, not enacted law

[AS01–AS24](../../country/C07/18-person-directed-assistance.md) makes the existing
S09–S12 care rights more specific. The country selects actual control of support,
a usable non-employer route, optional supported direct employment, protected
workers and continuing paid public receiving. Additional needs are not capped
by a healthy-household budget or an average number of hours.

## The important distinction

A recipient should be able to choose who helps with intimate daily life, what
support is needed and how it fits their routines. That does not require them
to become an employer, accountant, insurer or manager of employment disputes.
Different people may choose different degrees of administration and delegation.
None of these routes is a compulsory social model:

| Route | Who employs? | What the recipient controls / what remains owed |
|---|---|---|
| Person-directed public service | Named public/local care entity | Personal support, suitable workers and routine; public employer pays, handles work/safety and lawful changes |
| Accepted private/cooperative provider | Actual provider employer | Personal support and provider/assignment choice; cooperative membership is not by itself employer status |
| Supported direct employment | Actual willing lawful direct employer, after informed election | Greater direct recruitment/employment control, with funded payroll/advice/insurance, due obligations and public receiving |
| Payroll agent alone | Depends on actual source/control; the agent is not automatically employer | Delegation has limits; payment administration does not silently transfer all employment liability |

Direct employment and service purchasing may be combined safely by scope/time.
No care cut, diagnosis ban or management-aptitude test forces a route. Independent
advice and actual accepting alternatives matter more than names on a website.
Supported communication can use future media; expression, understanding, personal
consent and legal authority stay distinct. Copied agents do not multiply mandates.

## Two people's freedoms, two different decisions

The recipient can refuse unwanted contact or home access immediately and request
actual replacement. That is neither a finding that the worker did wrong nor an
automatic employment termination. No court remedy forces intimate admission or
care labor. Civil equality and proven wrongdoing retain their separate rules.

The employer handles reassignment, agreement or fair termination, with reasons,
reply and independent challenge. The proposed assistance floor is at least 28
calendar days of ordinary-pay notice or pay instead, retaining stronger old
terms; proved serious misconduct has its own fair grounds, and accrued wages
remain. The person need not retain unwanted contact while a work dispute runs.
Nor may the worker be made to waive pay to keep the care budget intact.

TW work/pay/leave/rest and actual worker consent apply in private homes too.
Risk assessment, paid training/equipment and qualified clinical permissions are
separate from control of private life. Unsafe-work refusal triggers safe public
receiving and correction, not retaliation, abandonment or new compulsory powers.

## Pay is not a provider allocation, and a budget is not care

AS12 explicitly proposes Treasury as co-obligor for defined lawful ordinary
wages, leave, contributions and accepted notice/termination dues when the approved
employer/custodian fails. It covers accrual from commencement; earlier debt needs
express funded acceptance. This is **new gross public payment exposure**. It is
not insurance of every provider loss, tort award, optional extra or permanent job.
Actual source, insurance and expected claim costs have not been qualified.

A worker can claim directly, with assisted evidence, without first bankrupting
the person or provider. Pay the same due once. In the authored established-dues
fixture,8,960CU consists of gross wages/notice8,000 (including employee deductions)
and employer contributions960. Actual earlier net pay/remittance totals1,000;
remaining public advance is7,960. Sending6,000 to the failed payroll custodian did
not pay the worker; a blocked5,000 balance and expected5,600 recovery are not cash
available for that advance. Only an actual later matched3,000 recovery lowers
that advance's net outlay to4,960. These are invented docket amounts, not actual
tax law, debt judgments or a fiscal forecast. Recovery cannot be assumed against
an innocent recipient merely for lawfully choosing direct employment.

Restricted care funds are not ordinary income or free provider assets. Protect
accepted wages/notice/supplier/contingency commitments before a reasoned return
of truly unused funds. Hospital admission, a move, changed need or death cannot
automatically sweep the account or erase contracts. Existing clinical, estate,
source-law tax and creditor rights remain distinct, with no duplicate recovery.

## What the staffing checks do and do not establish

One authored 4-hour daily support case needs 1,460 annual person-hours. Half an
hour daily needing a second worker adds182.5 worker-hours; travel/handover/records
add365. Total task requirement is2,007.5. At the declared TW five-day calendar,
1,664 annual gross paid hours include paid leave; working time before break/
learning is1,408 and stressed task availability1,110.6. Two full-time annual
positions plus a separately paid .25 standby allocation are only a planning
bound. The nominal30CU/hour and all nonpay/oncost quotes are authored, not a
qualified pay floor, insurance tariff, actual accepting shift or complete budget.

A **separate continuous-post case** has42 weekly four-hour core shifts, each
with .25 paid incoming handover. Seven hypothetical part-time people give168
core hours and178.5 duty hours in a repeated week, with explicit rest/limits.
Removing one primary leaves24 uncovered hours; a reserve helps only when actually
qualified, willing, available and accepted by the recipient. A reserve already
promised elsewhere or declined does not fill the gap. Annual leave/learning/loss
raises the same-pattern count to at least10 people, without proving a full-year
absence/night roster. Six handovers daily may worsen continuity/privacy; this
pattern is not the universal service choice. Clinical compatibility remains unknown.
The 365-day duty need is 9,307.5 hours;52 repeated weeks give only9,282. Keep the
additional25.5 duty hours rather than silently omit the remaining calendar day.

inspection, TW/B/S/I/E/PD and lawful external execution retain their separate
