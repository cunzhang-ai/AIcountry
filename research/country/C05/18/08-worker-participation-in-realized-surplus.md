# AT34–AT39. Give workers a claim on a defined share of realized surplus

*Design521 · Proposed prospective ordinary law · First test in a defined sector*

**AT34 — Set a participation floor before the accounting period.** Select a
statutory cash floor for a bounded first trial of sufficiently large controlling
enterprise groups in a named domestic sector. A separately enacted schedule
defines covered activities, size and phase-in, participating group entities,
accounting period, rate and normal return. Use paid full-time-equivalent work
across the controlled scope when assessing size, including work supplied under
AT36. Use at least two completed periods for the size test. Carry the
attributable operating history of continuing activity through restructuring,
with a disclosed allocation if only part of a business transfers. Publish a
graduated entry schedule to limit a single hiring cliff. The law identifies the
lead obligor, an independent labor forum and staffing and funding for review.
Use existing agreements and ordinary protection where they already give
equivalent access. Technology adoption, a claimed AI gain and consent to tool
use confer no participation, deployment permission or rate.

**AT35 — Calculate the amount independently of cash timing.** Start with the
audited realized result for covered operations after computing ordinary company
tax without the participation charge. Add back the charge if it was booked
as an expense. Verify actual attributable costs, accrued wages, ordinary
maintenance and enforceable obligations under the stated accounting basis.
Remove unrealized revaluations, returned capital and duplicated intragroup
sales or costs. Apply the enacted normal return on eligible operating capital
and any lawful carried loss once. A carried loss covers only unused eligible
loss from a preceding period, with its source, expiry and use recorded.
Multiply positive remaining surplus by the enacted participation rate. A
nonpositive result produces no new award. Set the company's participation
tax treatment separately and reconcile the chosen base once. A paid offset
under AT37 affects the amount still due, with no second deduction from the
calculation base for that same cost. Disputed adjustments and cash availability
retain separate decisions. Keep valuable investment and solvency in the
comparison. No later loss can offset a worker's earlier accrued claim.

**AT36 — Follow the worker and the actual control of the work.** Include covered
direct and fixed-term workers and people supplied through another firm where
the lead or controlled group materially directs individual work or staffing
and uses those workers as part of the covered operation. Evidence includes
who sets shifts or assignments and who can remove the person from the work
at the time the covered work occurs. If the payroll record is unavailable,
the independent forum may verify relevant assignment and payment records
and worker accounts under the ordinary evidence rules.
Merely buying independent goods or setting an agreed product specification
does not establish that control. The lead discloses relevant facts and gives
both direct and eligible supplied workers usable representation and challenge.
Ordinary wage and safety duties retain their own legal obligors. Allocate
the pool proportionally to covered paid work and lawfully credited protected
absence at the person's normal covered hours, with each period admitted once.
Use the worked fraction for a person who leaves, transfers or is dismissed
before settlement. The covered work establishes a contingent legal interest
at the time it is performed, with the amount determined at the audited close
and correction through ordinary review. A new payroll entity or the end of
employment leaves the eligible period in place. Give the person an accessible statement and safe
payment instructions through a route available after departure.

**AT37 — Recognize agreements that actually deliver comparable protection.**
Use the cash floor where no verified equivalent arrangement applies. A
lawfully negotiated agreement may choose extra cash or a worker's freely
chosen paid time at unchanged ordinary pay. The independent forum checks
each worker's floor, actual usable payment or release, and equivalent access
for covered supplied workers and leavers. For opted-in time, credit only
additional paid hours the worker actually controls beyond already owed leave
or release, at the lower of regular paid value
and independently verified value under the enacted equivalence terms. Require
qualified service cover under AT20 and a cash balance by the deadline for
undelivered time. Preserve a cash option, ordinary wages, due contributions,
paid leave and existing remedies. Company shares, an inaccessible account or
a promise of training supplies no cash credit. Credit only an authenticated
distribution for the same person, liable group surplus and period, once.
If another employer administers the transfer, the named lead remains
answerable for the shortfall.

**AT38 — Make earned participation enforceable through loss or sale.** The
reference trial uses a statement by day 120 and settlement by day 150 after
close, with day zero at the accounting close. Pay undisputed amounts on time,
even if other amounts are contested. A competent forum may grant one
time-limited liquidity deferral of up to 90 days on verified evidence with
worker participation, dated cure and an independently enforceable plan.
Apply the enacted bar on discretionary owner distributions and buybacks
while a deferred undisputed balance remains. Apply existing insolvency law
with the separate participation balance in the ordinary unsecured class
unless an independently authorized rank or protected custody actually applies.
No existing wage advance guarantees this amount. Neither a
liquidity finding nor a group restructuring waives principal. Preserve
separate wage, tax, pension and creditor rules. Before a prospective transfer,
require notice and a reviewable allocation of covered work, receipts, costs,
capital and losses to the appropriate obligor and period. Preserve the former
obligor within its legal scope until an authorized successor accepts, subject
to applicable transfer and creditor law. An ordinary sale between independent
firms does not consolidate their separate gains.
Existing labor courts or competent statutory forums enforce disclosure
and payment, with actual payroll or independent receipt checked before
closure. Underlying benefit and tax law governs funds when genuinely
available. Report net household effects and identify any separately costed
correction for a proved cliff.

**AT39 — Decide continuation on observed results.** Price strong bargaining
with actual lead participation, the statutory floor and broad tax-funded or
cross-employer protection on the same people and resources. Check base pay,
work and household time, involuntary departure, supplied-worker exposure,
enterprise survival, investment and qualified service through a second
paid window. Keep people outside profitable enterprises protected through
standing income, pension and service sources. Publicly report net receipt
and material dissent with protected evidence. AI can compare ledgers and
flag omissions. A qualified auditor and the competent labor forum determine
coverage, lawful amounts and correction. Continue or expand the exact scope
by a separate elected choice after independent evaluation. Reopen on wage
substitution, apparent divisions of a controlling group, delayed cash, service
loss or a simpler equivalent arrangement. Extending or ending the trial
preserves accrued claims and paying successors.
