# Keep the basin limit and let councils choose how to meet it

*Design545 · Constructed extension of the [catchment exercise](01-three-councils-and-one-catchment.md).*

The basin can reliably supply 14 units. Four are reserved for ecosystem
flow. Existing lawful uses take five, including two for a downstream
council outside the compact. Two upstream councils seek three new
units each. The full requests would use 11 of the ten allocable units.
The compact can bind its members on their own acts but cannot affect the
downstream rights or dispose of an unassigned residual on its own.
Allocation units in this exercise reflect one season and mutually
compatible physical receiving. All quantities and costs are assumptions.

Before acceptance, a new independent measurement puts gross supply at 12.
With the same ecological floor of four and the five continuing uses, only
three allocable units remain. Assume both unbound projects would use two
and have equal legal rank at this stage. A first application that can start
tomorrow uses two, leaving one. Its immediate approval would resolve which
project can use the remaining window before the joint choice is heard.
Hold that allocation for the lawful common choice. A separately lawful
repair to an existing pipe can proceed if it preserves the same scope and
does not consume the contested water or fund both proposals with the same
crew. If an independent lawful redesign actually uses one unit with a
qualified receiver, return the two-part 5 + 2 + 1 package to its competent
decision. None of the new drawings acquires a right from existing works.
