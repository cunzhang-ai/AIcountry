# FR14–FR19. Make disaster repair funds usable while protecting residents

*Part of [disaster recovery and return](../07-disaster-recovery-and-return.md). Design349 research selection.*

**FR14 — Keep shelter, insurance and the secured claim separate.** A displaced
occupant reaches temporary housing, essential services and safe contact through
FR and HL without title, insurance, a lender's permission or a new debt.
The housing office names a receiver and keeps the original date. The
insurer decides coverage and pays undisputed amounts under NB16. Any
lender or servicer handling proceeds identifies its authority, receipts
and restrictions. The creditor's balance and enforcement have their own
law. A contractor's estimate or insurance approval does not prove that
repair money has arrived or that anyone can safely return.

**FR15 — Safeguard and release actual repair proceeds.** For a covered
primary home with a lawful safe repair plan and an accepting qualified
repairer, the person or lawful representative may request staged release
of received building proceeds. A servicer lawfully holding them keeps
a separate protected account, with the owner, claimant, legal terms,
stage, paid amount and outstanding balance visible to authorized people.
Use the received proceeds for the lawful repair stages within actual
security and policy terms as reconciled by prospective law. Give a
decision and pay the lawful undisputed stage within five working days
of verified evidence and usable funds, or earlier when the applicable
duty requires. Request specific missing evidence promptly and give
reasons for any held amount. Offer reasonable independent inspection
and direct contractor or resident payment under the authorized plan.
A disaster-related arrear alone cannot authorize diverting earmarked
repair money into debt service. A genuinely disputed allocation goes
to independent review and the competent court, with useful-time
relief for proved preservation work within existing authority.

**FR16 — Protect a short decision window on possession.** For a scheduled
disaster affecting the borrower's primary home, usable notice to the
creditor of a supported loss starts a 30-calendar-day bar on new or executed
compulsory possession based solely on disaster-caused payment or
occupancy default. Any prior order retains its legal status and uses
its lawful stay and review route. Give usable notice and a named
independent challenge route. Either party may seek tailored relief.
One further period of up to 30 calendar days needs a reasoned court
decision before expiry, with notice, necessity, proportionality and a
specific repair or safe receiving path. Preserve lawful principal,
ordinary interest, creditor priority and separately due amounts.
Disclose repayment and interest effects. Charge no additional default
fees solely for use of the protection. Any forgiveness or financing
uses its own source. Expiry gives no automatic right to evict or sell.
Independent ordinary possession and safety rules remain applicable.

**FR17 — Reconcile the purpose, person and actual receipt.** Temporary
accommodation and immediate needs are distinct from building repair,
contents loss, debt reduction and compensation. Under FR06, allow a
credit or lawful recovery only for the same attributable loss and
actual legally relevant receipt. Keep a separate account of any prior-law
public advance, where it went and what remains collectible. Neither a
disputed insurer amount nor a creditor's balance is usable recovery cash.
When lawful safe repair is unavailable or the household freely chooses a
lawful move, obtain a reasoned insurance and security allocation under
the applicable law. Preserve notice, tenure, title, valid priority and
independent remedies. No insurer insolvency, home equity or unpaid
settlement becomes a general public guarantee.

**FR18 — Follow the repair and the household separately.** The local
recovery owner verifies the next safe place, tenure, services and useful
transport. The repair owner verifies staged payment, actual work,
inspection and safe acceptance under FR. Keep disputed work, partial
repairs and unserved households visible. Tenants and other occupants
may challenge unsafe or late receiving without seeing unrelated
financial information. An AI may reconcile dates, declared purposes
and receipt evidence. It cannot decide coverage, title, creditor
priority, habitability or compulsory possession. Fund independent
inspection and legal aid within their actual scopes.

**FR19 — Migrate with lawful finance and review.** Elected bodies enact
the prospective scope, funding and exact application to security and
policy terms. Preserve accrued claims, existing priority, original
dates, stronger protections and valid final orders. Pay for staffed
receiving, proof, inspection, review and public advances separately.
Publish safe results on delays, repair stages, temporary housing and
unresolved claims. Reopen on withheld funds, deteriorating safety,
repeated debt pressure, unaffordable exposure or a simpler workable
funded route.
