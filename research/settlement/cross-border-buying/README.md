# Connected buying without exporting serious harm

Design187 · [BC01–BC20](../../country/C10/14-cross-border-buying-and-responsibility.md)

The existing C10 principle, B ordinary care/causation, equal foreign standing
and public SC safeguards were real design commitments. Missing was the explicit
independent commercial buyer duty, especially when the product itself is safe
but upstream labor/community conditions are gravely harmful. This choice
specifies that interface rather than claiming foreign harm had no protection.

| Question | Working choice | Material unresolved cost |
|---|---|---|
| Which buyer? | Actual connected home/local commercial/public buyer; own controlled delegation abroad remains covered | Attribution/foreign cooperation, not all affiliates or households |
| What harm? | Specified grave work/coercion/child/organizing/community/ecological floor; ordinary stronger laws remain | Contested seriousness, local source law and expert/affected interpretations |
| What action? | Reasonable own prevention/cessation and feasible linked-risk response affecting price/time/orders/payment | Real prices/margins, supplier livelihood and weak-buyer capacity |
| What repair? | Direct safe B forum, targeted records and causal damages; B11 indivisible loss retained | Qualified paid counsel/courts/executors and actual recovery |
| Who enforces? | Existing Work Standards and designated protected environment case officers with express civil functions | New expertise/workload, public-buyer conflicts, ministerial budget capture |
| What exit? | Stop own contribution promptly; reasoned finite response/withdrawal with actual due payments and feasible mitigation | No invented supplier consent, replacement livelihood or essential stock |

[Adverse cases](../../2026-11-cross-border-buying-and-shared-responsibility/adverse-cases.md)
challenge the clauses; these are authored scenarios, not actual worker testimony.
[Decision](../../2026-11-cross-border-buying-and-shared-responsibility/decision-record.md)
compares retain/repair and worker-led structural alternatives, plus stronger
strict-chain/import-exclusion regimes. [Source inspection](../../2026-11-cross-border-buying-and-shared-responsibility/sources.md)
distinguishes captured UN guidance, a same-family handbook, OECD browser-only
metadata and empirical evidence not obtained. More guidance is not an efficacy
result or a new legal mandate.

[Continuing commitments](../commitments/100-cross-border-buying-and-responsibility.md)
retain actual rights, sources, source-preserving transition, paid OP/T successors
and the open37-function census. The whole country still needs actual matched
resource/control/life comparison; this finite choice does not achieve the goal.
