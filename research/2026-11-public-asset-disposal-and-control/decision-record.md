# Design397 decision. Put material public asset transfers to a full elected choice

## Baseline and selected mechanism

[Design396](predecessor-design-396/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md) governs public
enterprise mandates, public acquisition, asset renewal, concessions
and common fiscal risk. An outright sale or control transfer can
still be presented as a cash receipt without the rights, income,
future options and liabilities that move or remain.

Select [PL13–PL20](../country/C06/64/04-public-asset-disposal-and-control.md)
under the [working research method](../FRAMEWORK.md). Give the
elected owner a dated comparison of retention, alternate use and
transfer on the same service terms. Keep competition, regulation,
settlement and service acceptance distinct. Use capital receipts on
voted capital or debt purposes, with an explicit lawful finite
transition exception and separate recurring finance.

## Alternatives and objections

Retain ordinary asset management and judge transfers through
competitive market price. It can simplify surplus sales, but the
winning price can hide a continuing subsidy or weakened public
control. Require permanent public ownership of every material
asset instead. That preserves future ownership options but can
trap capital and protect poor management. A bounded concession can
change operation while retaining title. Its future payments, risks,
actual control and handback cost need separate scrutiny. Prefer an
explicit comparative choice and keep statutory public ownership
protections where they actually apply.

A formal vote adds work and may expose confidential bids. Use
published delegation for low-risk surplus items, aggregate related
transfers, and allow time-limited protected material with independent
access. Opposition and affected people need actual time and usable
evidence before an irreversible change. Appraisal, negotiation,
ownership, regulation, settlement and audit have different controllers.
Common advisers, lenders or political sponsors can still weaken
independence. Protect urgent judicial relief and publish actual
control and retained risks. A reviewing body cannot force its own
valuation or use a hearing to appropriate money.

## Evidence, migration and next action

[Primary records](sources.md) show real differences between
consideration, valuation, retained risk and future income. The
[worked case](../settlement/public-asset-disposal-and-control/01-three-options-for-one-depot.md)
shows that a higher closing cash amount can coexist with lower
two-year fiscal value, and that the retention preference can change.
No actual asset sale, source, lawful adoption, participation or
superior service has been established.

Adopt prospectively with independent appraisal and a voted legal
scope. Preserve valid title, leases, wages, debt, environmental and
service duties, public trust restrictions and all paid succession
requirements. Treat an internal move as a transfer of actual
control and accounts without new consolidated money. Pending
transactions keep their governing law unless lawfully changed.

Next trace one real public asset from original title and control
through the retain, concession and sale accounts. Test the dated
cash, retained liability, actual user and worker receiving and
regulator independence at close and one later window. Reopen on
mispriced continuing risk, failed handover, common buyer control
or a better qualified public alternative.
