# Penalties and delivery: hidden coercion and displaced obligations

*Part of [special harms](README.md); authored boundary and quantity examples.*

| Case | Required candidate disposition |
|---|---|
| H14 A convicted person receives 1,300 monthly and needs all of it | C−E is zero, so a fine unit is zero. No minimum fee, partner imputation or automatic work/prison conversion. P16 still compares a lawful no-sanction/other proportionate response; poverty does not supply grounds for custody. |
| H15 Another person has C=2,500, E=1,300 and a reasoned 180-unit fine | Unit=20; total=3,600. Monthly collection is at most 300, so unchanged means pay it in twelve months. If all later surplus disappears, pause/reduce; original 24-month expiry includes pauses. No increased units or delayed sealing because payment failed. |
| H16 Nonessential reachable liquid assets exist but a partner's home is disputed | Q02 can assess proved net liquid assets, with ownership/liens, exemptions and usable value. It cannot assume a disputed house or inaccessible partner fortune is cash. Defense can contest each component; protected housing/tools/care are not sold for a fine. |
| H17 A child community placement collides with school and care | The 60-hour/six-month, three/week and 12/month limits all apply, with safe receiving, access and voluntary agreement. A nominal 60 hours in six weeks fails despite fitting the total. Adjust/end, credit work and preserve child voice. |
| H18 A funded placement never opens and the software records breach | No deliberate able noncompliance is proved. The office retains service/remedy responsibility; shorten/end rather than automatic custody or extended supervision. Urgent challenge and counsel cannot wait for the ordinary seven-working-day adverse-variation hearing. |
| H19 Five related corporate labels each seek the maximum fine | Establish each defendant and Q07 attribution. For the same course, Q08 uses one highest band/gain cap, not five stacked percentages. An owner/parent is not guilty merely by relation; a proved sham/control/asset transfer remains examinable. |
| H20 An essential public provider's board knowingly authorizes harmful acts | No immunity, but identify personal/body fault separately. An internal fine is no national income; user care, wages, records and accepted successor remain due. Do not finance a penalty by deleting essential receiving. An urgent narrow stop may still be necessary under separate lawful grounds. |
| H21 An acquired business faces correction, liquidation and victim claims | Independent court, workers/users/creditors and innocent-owner notice, viable correction versus dissolution, and actual successor/asset mapping are required. No assumed public guarantee, unpaid private help or proceeds before recovery. B/U priorities and needs remain. |
| H22 Police seek a car owned by an uninvolved relative as offense proceeds | Q10 needs traceable benefit or proved offender ownership/intentional instrument use and proportionality, not association. Give owner/secured claimant notice/challenge and return the rightful owner's property before Treasury proceeds; no double recovery. |
| H23 A fine remains unpaid or a person is held after sentence expiry | Noncustodial record disclosure runs from final judgment; custodial clock uses the earlier of actual release and lawful expiry. Poverty or unlawful delayed release cannot extend routine exclusion. Correct downstream adverse labels while preserving safe public reasons. |
| H24 Police open a file just before the prosecution period ends | A file alone does not pause Q12. Proper charge/usable notice, actual tolling grounds and independent proof apply. No revival of an expired bar or backdated service; seriousness does not excuse unreliable old proof. |

A bounded workload illustration considers **20 adult 120-hour measures and
10 child 48-hour measures**: 2,880 placement hours, plus an authored 0.1
supervisor-hour per placement-hour and two intake/correction hours for each
person, totaling 348 staff-hours. This is a **proposed gross workload**, not
30 actual convictions or a safe supervision ratio. Two hundred allocated
staff-hours leave a 148-hour gap before travel, peaks, defense, court,
worksite safety, insurance and receiving. Do not treat unpaid work as wages
saved or an additional 2,880 hours of already-budgeted service delivery.

Q01 rejects automatic addition of fine, work and custody for the same course;
required repair/protection has its separate grounds. Q03 expiry does not
erase civil victim claims. All 37-function country bids remain unqualified
for this incremental work: investigators, child advocates, independent
experts, courts, means assessors, safe placements, monitors, records staff
and accepted essential successors need unique cost/period attribution in C06.
