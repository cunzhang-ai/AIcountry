# The national bargain and its strongest practical competitors

3 November 2026 · Design 141 · [CS13–CS18](../../country/C01/03-country-case-and-competing-designs.md)

Retain the democratic/service-first/plural country as the working design, with
real retain/repair, direct-public, U+C2 and mutual/common competitors. Revise
the institutional response to failure: justify consequential new compulsory
steps through existing lawful public decisions, compare simpler/repair routes,
preserve necessary independent judgments and pay actual usable alternatives.
No additional simplification regulator, automatic model veto or new tax.

This completes a bounded institutional-level comparison, not full rival legal
codes, calibrated countries or proof of global superiority. No lower burden
or cost is assumed merely from a card's name. All actual matched source,
independent affected review, authority, qualified receiving and lives remain
unknown. Earlier source studies and partial financial books retain their own
historical scope; no favorable result transfers to this whole package.

[CS14](../../country/C01/03/06-current-country-and-strong-alternatives.md) now
clarifies the common country boundary: fundamental democratic/bodily/essential
protections and actual accrued/due claims persist; every unadopted numerical
increment is not a universal floor. Rivals may choose different prospective
TW/NC/AL/EB/CL terms with explicit human/resource/transition consequences and
actual adequacy assessment. [CL](../../country/C05/17-care-leave-and-return.md)
closes a positive time/security gap, not proof the preferred whole offer wins.
