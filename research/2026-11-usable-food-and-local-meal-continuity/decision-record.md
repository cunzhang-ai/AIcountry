# Design293. Usable food and local meal continuity

10 November 2026 · Research selection · No actual enactment

## Baseline and decision

[Design292](baseline.json) retains plural food production, an income
and food-access floor, a rotating buffer, independent food-safety
powers and a dated public budget. The new decision is which route
can provide suitable meals through the same market, water and power
failure, including the next delivery. Apply the
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md). Codex assisted
research and drafting. Actual affected input and independent
professional qualification remain open.

## Alternatives and selection

- **Keep ordinary retail and income support, with an ingredient
  reserve.** Prefer it where households can buy and prepare safe
  suitable food and independent access exists. This keeps ordinary
  choices and uses less dedicated public capacity. It fails in
  the constructed loss because the money and ingredients cannot
  be converted into a meal within the relevant windows.
- **Combine qualified local preparation with suitable direct meals.**
  Select this conditional option for the declared loss. It retains
  existing kitchens and ordinary school or care obligations. Both
  rounds require accepted receiving and the full dated cash. At
  seven total units, the narrower first-delivery option leaves
  three residents without their next meal unless another qualified
  route works. Keep that loss visible and seek timely repair.
- **Maintain distributed suitable ready-to-eat capacity for all.**
  In the comparison this is more expensive. It better protects
  against an added kitchen, water or power loss if routes are
  genuinely independent. It has its own rotation, dietary,
  delivery and stock-expiry costs and can still fail if these
  are assumed rather than funded.

[FM01–FM05](../country/C09/09/01-usable-food-and-local-meal-continuity.md)
selects the ordinary planning, activation and review mechanism.
The [scenario](../settlement/usable-food/README.md) shows why the
same stock figure and the first successful dispatch can coexist
with failed access. The [primary evidence](sources.md) qualifies
last-stage and local production mechanisms only. Prices, needs
and outage paths in the example are authored.

## Authority, objection and migration

Elected bodies approve eligible sources and scope. The existing
competent local officer orders lawful delivery. The safety officer
retains food-safety decisions. A paid independent review route can
address exclusion, unsafe delay and failed receiving. No new
rationing or private transfer power is selected. Existing payments,
meal entitlements, wages, reviews and due dates retain their law.
Reconcile actual overlapping support only through valid existing
same-component rules.

A prepared-meal route can narrow choice, impose stigma or crowd
out ordinary provision. Keep practical choices, privacy, access
without an app and independent ways to report unmet needs. Compare
return to ordinary stores and income support as soon as safe
receiving is verified. Keep a lawful accepting successor, stock
and safety evidence, contracts, actual capacity and original
claims through any transition. No new duty begins by indexing.

## Next bounded decision

Compare two actual delivery windows with one willing local authority,
an independent food-safety route, a qualified kitchen, retail or
ready-to-eat providers and relevant affected people. Verify water,
power, receipt, actual costs, delivery, dietary suitability and
replenishment under a plausible common loss. Reopen after repeated
delivery failure, loss of school or care service, excluded diets,
unsafe substitution, or a more capable distributed alternative.
No real deployment or outcome has been verified.
