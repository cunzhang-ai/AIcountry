# C09. Keep protected water deliverable through successive dry seasons

*Design406 · WSR01–WSR08 · Proposed ordinary water allocation and continuity rules.*

A basin may have water for discretionary uses this season and still be
unable to supply essential needs and protected flows in the next. A lawful
allocation must state what it draws from storage, what remains usable at
the next deadline and who bears the cost of keeping that option open.

**WSR01 — Set a public rule before dry conditions force the choice.** The
Assembly or competent elected authority under prior law adopts a basin plan
with a rolling horizon covering at least the next protected dry season and
the next verified refill or alternative supply, subject to a stated review
date. Specify which inflow, demand and failure scenarios govern commitments,
why their risks are acceptable, protected rights and ecological limits, usable
storage bounds, triggers, operator, independent reviewer and funded sources.
Hear upstream and downstream communities, workers, affected collective
rights-holders and opposition. A domestic plan cannot displace customary,
territorial or treaty rights or bind a foreign supplier by assertion.

**WSR02 — Carry physical stocks and legal commitments separately.** For
each useful interval and connected source, reconcile opening measured stock,
actual and projected inflows, storage and conveyance losses, minimum flows,
withdrawals, usable returns, commitments and closing usable stock. Identify
treatment, power, staff, travel and seasonal delivery bounds. Exclude
inaccessible storage and retain nonwaivable safety, quality and ecological
limits. Account for groundwater drawdown and later river capture in their
actual locations and periods. A release, transfer or reuse can enter only
one compatible source path. A forecast, permission or private balance
cannot serve as stored water. Disclose any right that cannot yet be delivered.

**WSR03 — Protect a dated minimum carryover before new optional releases.**
In the enacted adverse case, work back from each protected service and
flow deadline and the qualified successor window. Determine the minimum
usable carryover needed after verified independent supplies, then limit
currently discretionary releases to the remaining compatible amount.
Do not reduce current protected services or override an existing right to
create that reserve. Apply WS09 class rules within the residual envelope
and use existing lawful variation or separately paid agreements for other
commitments. Include a qualified minimum closing stock at the end of the
comparison or expose the later gap. No repeated resetting of the horizon
may treat the same opening stock as newly available.

**WSR04 — Choose how to share the burden, including the cost of waiting.**
Compare early discretionary restraint, funded demand reduction, qualified
independent supply and phased change to production on the same service and
ecological obligations. Identify whom each option harms and when, including
small users, people without a reliable connection, seasonal workers and
communities exposed to withdrawal or displaced production. Use separate
lawful income, worker, food and essential-service support with dated
funding and receiving. Credit water saved only where and when it is
actually available for the protected use. A higher bid, an unpaid promise
of conservation or an annual average cannot supply the missing stock.

**WSR05 — Reassess before the next avoidable loss.** The Steward uses the
enacted observation and forecast triggers to revise only the authorized
operating scope. When qualified observations or delivery evidence cross a
trigger, publish the original baseline, change, affected allocations and
next decision date. Check whether a higher inflow is usable at the intake
and whether dependent operations can actually receive it. A failed gauge,
shared power outage or missed review supplies no new release authority.
Use a separately qualified observation or continuation route. The Steward
cannot use discretionary release, optimistic inference or endless delay
to change the publicly adopted risk rule.

**WSR06 — Expose a simultaneous failure of protected duties.** If due
personal or service needs, protected ecological conditions and required
carryover cannot all be met, record each uncovered obligation by place and
time. Use authorized direct delivery, lawful demand measures and prior
source-specific urgent protection with actual cash, qualified operators and
usable receiving. Protect those with no access to the ordinary network.
An infeasible balance creates no priority, environmental waiver, private
requisition power or serial emergency term. Return persistent conflict
and financing to the competent elected authority and applicable rights
review. Relief protects as much as the law and physical supply allow while
keeping missed service and restoration claims open.

**WSR07 — Release the reserve only for its lawful purpose or on a verified
return to ordinary allocation.** Name the payer, operator, accepted receiver,
expiry and useful time before relying on reserve release. Restore ordinary
discretionary allocation only when qualified stock, flow, quality and delivery
show the remaining protected windows can be met under the adopted rule.
A passing rain event, temporary import or favorable central forecast alone
does not restore capacity. Reconcile original authorizations, arrears,
return flows and continuing duties through paid succession. Preserve court
review and safe disclosure of withheld or unequally applied releases.

**WSR08 — Test authority and actual receiving before commencement.** Existing
Steward, Director, Reviewer and courts retain their respective powers. Fund
measurement and independent verification, usable appeals, reserve operation,
continuity and paid migration. Test at least two successive dry seasons,
shared failures and effects on current households and dependent production.
AI may compare lawful schedules, flag common dependencies and express
uncertainty. It cannot set the acceptable risk, authorize a draw, alter a
priority or certify that water reached people. Reopen on repeated forecast
error, exclusion, unfunded burdens or an equally protective less costly path.
