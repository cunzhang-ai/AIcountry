# Social and fiscal settlement topics

The stable [settlement entry](../SOCIAL-FISCAL-SETTLEMENT.md) states the scope and
links to the specification in three parts:

1. [Guarantees, income and work](01-guarantees-and-income.md).
2. [Financing, shortages and reference scenarios](02-financing-and-scenarios.md).
3. [Common-account results and research selection](03-results-and-selection.md).

The [design-56 commitment inventory](commitments/README.md) reconciles all
currently named functions and selections, exposes missing allocations and
tests a combined shortage. It does not replace the preserved September
scenario's assumptions or certify the expanded country design.

The [design-57 continuity package](continuity/README.md) supplies candidate
ordinary-law clauses and bounded cost/time/path tests under that shortage.
It preserves unknown actual quantities and compares a national direct
provider/reserve rival without claiming operational or social superiority.

Illustrative scenario numbers are not current empirical data or enacted budgets.
Check later C04–C07, C09 and C13 choices through the [country design](../COUNTRY-DESIGN.md)
and [status register](../DESIGN-STATUS.md).

The [design-58 income package](income-floor/README.md) provides the substantive
needs/resource/tax rules and population/launch reference. Its large financing
gap changes the next priority to a whole national revenue/production package.

The [design-59 joined national package](national-economy/README.md) supplies
planned GDP/five-sector/stock/resource identities and six rival sensitivities.
Private and physical shortfalls hold all cases from an operating claim.
