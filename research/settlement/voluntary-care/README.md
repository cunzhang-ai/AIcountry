# Buy the ability to stop providing unpaid care

*Design289 · Constructed first-budget comparison.*

One local service window has four simultaneous assessed adult home
support needs. A uses an ordinary paid shift. B uses agreed respite,
booked as ordinary care. C and D choose help from their respective
relatives. Each critical task needs one qualified person at its site.
A and B's work costs three units each. A separate qualified reserve
costs one unit to hold and two to activate. There are willing, qualified
paid workers and enough travel time if correctly reserved. The
hypothetical common event makes C's and D's helpers unavailable.

| Choice | Ordinary outlay before either draw | Cost of one call / two calls | Result under both calls |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Existing paid roster with no accepted reserve | 6 | 6 / 6 | C and D remain uncovered unless another safe paid route actually accepts. |
| Ordinary roster plus one shared reserve | 7 | 9 / 9 | Only one extra resident receives care. The unmet task and original deadline remain. |
| Ordinary roster plus two independently available reserves | 8 | 10 / 12 | C and D both receive support without using A's shift or B's respite. |
| Four regular staffed slots | 12 | 12 / 12 | All receive care when needed. Ordinary planned costs are higher and recurring dependence on dispatch falls. |

For the reference payer, twelve units available by the simultaneous
dispatch and payment needs qualify the two-reserve route, subject to
actual acceptance. On a nine-unit envelope the one-reserve choice
provides a usable addition and leaves one support need exposed in
the joint event. Elected authorities must see the shortfall and the
cost of fixing it. A payment owed later cannot finance this call.
A second dispatch attempted after the last safe time adds no coverage
for the elapsed interval. Preserve the booked respite and disclose
any lawful emergency reassignment as a loss to the affected household.

The choices preserve voluntary help until it changes. Fully staffed
provision can preserve it too, by making qualified staff available
without compelling home entry or formal visits. It is stronger
where withdrawals become routine or travel is difficult. The
declared comparison omits prices, actual travel networks, costs
outside the single window and uncertainty about future demand.
Before scaling, budget full schedules, allocate simultaneous calls
across localities and price the next reserve after depletion.
Do not infer a national reserve ratio from this case.
