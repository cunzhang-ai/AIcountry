combining modernization and capacity additions at 13 schools. They are
different sets and services. Both were assessed against 57.7 in capital over 2026–2029.
The separate 22.3 service provision covers cleaning, education and minor
improvements over the period. The 2026 values for the selected proposal
are 7.7 capital and 7.3 service. The alternative shows a rounded 7.0

## Disposition under the proposed country rules

Retain the published budget and authorized modernization baseline in this
comparison. Keep capacity additions in the elected comparison. The records
do not establish that changing the citywide capital framework, reversing the
school choice or taking money from early care yields a better lawful bundle.
Apply the same useful-access check to both options. If a pupil at a school
awaiting either type of work lacks lawful usable toilets during the school
day, verify the deficiency and give it a funded interim remedy or record
the unserved obligation. The school and facility owners have different tasks
in delivering that remedy. The elected body considers any additional permanent
money or displaced project with its real source, while the applicable service
and review rights continue. The available records cannot price the interim
route or identify a school whose deficiency warrants a different order.

## Counterfactual stress on release and carry-forward

Illustrative units, separate from all Copenhagen money and projects. Suppose
42 of a 50-unit current envelope is already committed and due, and two
prepared stages can each proceed independently. A costs six and restores
the only accessible route to a due service. B costs seven for an improvement
with a usable existing route. If both mature, 42 + 6 + 7 = 55, exceeding the
envelope by five. Commit A under valid authority when its crew and receiving
are confirmed. B remains a conditional project. If the next period has 18
usable and 15 already due, the nominal reprofile of all seven for B still
leaves four unfunded. Its operator needs a lawful source, independently useful
reduction or elected deferral with the old service in place. The same principle
applies if a qualified crew or closure window is binding before money.
The comparison says nothing about actual Copenhagen release limits.
