identity/control and monthly fit matter alongside arithmetic. The old FD07

No pooled net fiscal total is offered: G/local/S, insurer/bank/client/pension/CCP,
private returns and owned FX are not freely fungible. Taxes on already funded
public wages reduce an explicit consolidated net cost, not independent output
or first-day cash. Internal group loans/reporting do not convey title or NF tax
loss relief. Retained housing equity, gross export turnover and unreceived
recovery remain distinct from actually available Treasury sources.

Current unknown-source account: all37 actual current costs remain unknown;
new official-risk losses and full public/private transition are unqualified.
A credible forecast can inform an elected budget, but a new guarantee needs a
priced scenario/source/incidence comparison before anyone claims readiness.
The account is prepared by existing responsible authorities/Treasury and
challenged through existing source/court/opposition routes, never a claimant's
mandatory technical dossier or universal public sensitive-person ledger.
