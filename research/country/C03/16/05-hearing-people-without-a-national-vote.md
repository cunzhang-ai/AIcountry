# AR01–AR05. Hear people before changing the national rules that govern them

*Design318 · Proposed ordinary legislative protection.*

**AR01 — Open a route when a live bill directly changes people's legal
position.** When an ordinary national proposal materially changes
residence, permission to work, family unity, citizenship or an
applicable common protection for people without a national vote,
the responsible committee provides affected people a protected route
to the existing scrutiny record. Publish the relevant effect and
invitation with the exact draft in useful time. An affected person
may ask the Clerk to correct an omission or late material change.
Use proportionate proof of affected connection. Citizenship,
immigration paperwork, membership in a recognized organization or
a member sponsor is no entry condition. Preserve other public
speech, petition, election and urgent individual routes. The
procedure concerns the live decision. It does not create an
unbounded new agenda or decide an individual immigration case.

**AR02 — Make the route safe and usable.** The Assembly service funds
independent accessible intake, interpretation, suitable timing and
necessary participation support. Accept direct, assisted and protected
testimony and actual limited mandates. People may submit their own
reasons without exposing identity or status publicly where lawful.
Keep sensitive evidence with the proper custodian. Do not repurpose
it for enforcement merely because someone spoke. Give members and
other participants an answerable account of material reasons with
protected evidence access under applicable law. No compulsory
attendance, organizational intermediary or AI system follows.
A person can decline participation without affecting a claim.

**AR03 — Preserve materially different reasons before the vote.**
Use the existing committee window, funded under the actual budget.
Give affected people a useful chance to correct the rendering of
their reasons and present alternatives. Group copies and related
claims only where separate effects and objections remain legible.
Give the elected decision-maker the qualified material accounts
and publish the committee's response, material disagreements,
unheard groups and limits before ordinary final disposition.
The response explains which choices were considered and why.
A materially changed text needs fresh affected scrutiny where
its effect changes. When prior law permits urgent action earlier,
record that scope and fund the earliest subsequent hearing and
correction. Urgency cannot be invented by shortening notice.

**AR04 — Correct exclusion while a choice remains.** The Clerk records
the first protected request and routes a challenge to the existing
independent process and competent court where authorized. Supply a
qualified alternate for an implicated officer or unavailable support.
Where an actual order requires a process repair, execute it within
useful time and applicable powers. Courts can protect access,
records and existing individual rights. They cannot choose the
bill or count consent. A documented failure preserves continuing
review and applicable remedies. It grants no automatic veto, vote
or new authority. Election eligibility, NI threshold counts,
individual clocks and lawful emergency powers remain separate.

**AR05 — Keep the arrangement accountable without ranking people.**
Publish safe evidence of actual participation, process failure,
qualified support, cost and elected dispositions. Keep open cases,
earned payments, original dates and lawful successors across
elections. AI may translate, assist drafting and flag distinct
arguments. Participants may correct its rendering and use a
qualified person. Neither message volume nor digital identity
establishes a mandate. Reopen on systematic exclusion, unsafe
data use, repeated missed hearings or a better route, including
a separately justified prospective franchise change under higher law.
