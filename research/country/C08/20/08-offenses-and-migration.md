# Closed wrongs, fair proof and continuity of actual people

*RP15–RP16; part of [C08/20](../20-reproductive-law-and-parentage.md).*

**RP15 — Actual unauthorized conduct, never outcome-based pregnancy guilt.**
Only prospective enacted precise elements permit punishment. Apply all P
fault, burden, causation, defenses, evidence/exclusion, counsel, trial/finality
and Q means/body/aggregate protections. Prosecution proves actual conduct,
knowledge of absent/withdrawn required consent or the specified sale purpose;
a missing form, clinic loss, disputed identity, donor label or AI score is
not that proof. Genuine material error and properly raised defenses are
assessed with P burdens. Seeking/receiving lawful care, pregnancy outcome,
disability or failure to conceive supplies no offense or search authority.

| Closed offense | Conduct, fault and bounded maximum |
|---|---|
| RPO01 Nonconsensual reproductive use | Intentionally perform abortion, sterilization or invasive contraception on another person, retrieve/use another person's actual gametes or reproductive biomaterial, create an embryo for reproduction, inseminate or transfer an embryo, knowing the actual required specific bodily/material consent is absent or withdrawn; maximum five years. Actual lawful clinical-emergency grounds and the expressly adopted narrow [UA09 therapeutic ground](../23/03-consequential-and-therapeutic-acts.md) remain, including unavoidable therapeutic removal/pathology rather than reproductive retrieval/use; no inferred authority from kinship, samples, credentials or research consent. |
| RPO02 Nonconsensual material research | Intentionally use actual gametes/embryos or material within RP03 for reproductive/embryo research knowing the required separate research consent is absent/withdrawn; maximum two years. A digital simulation or public sequence alone is not that material use, and no general ownership of others' genes is created. |
| RPO03 Sale/compelled surrender of a child | Knowingly offer, arrange, give or receive consideration whose actual condition is acquiring/surrendering a child or a purported ownership/parental title, intending that exchange; maximum ten years. Actual RP06 noncontingent costs/time compensation and lawful proportionate professional services are excluded; on a credible lawful-payment basis prosecution disproves it. No automatic guilt for a poor family, cross-border child or ordinary caregiving. |

A patient accused of an independently specified intentional unauthorized use
has ordinary personal proof/defense rights, not guilt from being pregnant or
receiving a clinic's assurance. Coercion, actual inability and exploitation
must be heard. These offenses never authorize forced abortion, continued
pregnancy, surrender, medical search or exclusion from care. Existing K/G
serious harm, coercion/fraud/slavery or atrocity liability needs its own full
elements; no analogy or duplicate same-course punishment by combining labels.
Clinical/licensing/research-boundary breaches are not automatically crimes;
actual RP/V duties allow necessary protection/correction and bounded proof.

No conviction for conduct under 16; at 16–17 use P child process/aggregate five-
year ceiling. Adult same-course maximum 25, no mandatory minimum/death or
indefinite preventive custody. Q02/Q08 fine bands, Q10 proceeds and B repair
retain ownership, needs and no duplicate recovery. RPO01/02/03 use Q12's
six/three/twelve-year periods respectively, with only its proved bounded
concealment/evasion toll and any separately applicable child-victim start.
No pregnancy-outcome inquisition or revival of expired source-law cases.

**RP16 — New rules do not approve themselves or erase families.** A real
public/legislature must lawfully adopt the package and parameters after
qualified/affected review of autonomy, developing fetal interests, origin,
plural parenthood, coercion, paid burdens, science and actual capacity.
No clinic/registry/AI creates binding new status or changes consent by default.
Map old pregnancies, children/status, material, anonymity promises, storage,
claims/estate windows, licenses, prices, orders and actual receivers to lawful
successors. Preserve existing stronger protections/accrued rights pending
authorized revision, lawful leniency and safe child support. New origin rules
are prospective; do not reveal legacy private records through an index update.

Actual care, independent advice, birth/origin registration, counsel/courts,
privacy, storage, funds and accepted successors must be funded before new
activity is called ready. Rights/urgent support continue on failure; no
private default or doctrinal disagreement justifies abandonment. Complete
remaining conflict-detention/technical sector law, then whole-country audit;
reopen on original evidence, excluded needs, coercion or a better model, not
an automatic demand for another small scenario or claimed social progress.
