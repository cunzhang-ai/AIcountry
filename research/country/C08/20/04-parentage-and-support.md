# An intended commitment, a birth and a contested status are different acts

*RP07–RP08; part of [C08/20](../20-reproductive-law-and-parentage.md).*

**RP07 — A lawful basis for each parent and each obligor.** B28's person
giving birth is the initial legal parent, plus any undisputed lawful co-parent
acknowledgement/order. A valid RP03 nonparent donor is not a parent by genetics;
an ordinary known genetic parent outside that exception retains B28's actual
proof/support route. No automatic marriage, two-parent ceiling, DNA-score
judgment or donor-contract transfer of a child.

Each adult intended parent gives an independently advised specific parenting/
support commitment before authorized insemination/transfer. They may withdraw
before that actual insemination/transfer; creating/storing an untransferred
embryo alone does not lock the future parenting commitment. After the
authorized insemination/transfer they cannot escape
child-support responsibility because of relationship breakdown, child's
characteristics or a missing genetic link. A court determines actual assent,
lawful scope and contributions; unauthorized use cannot conscript a material
source into parenthood. This is an express prospective support ground, not
an ordinary consumer debt or a right to another's body/child.

In third-party pregnancy, the gestating person's transfer consent is only
after birth and independent advice, with B32's30-day revocation before a
final order. During that period a safe temporary carer and accepting service
are named with review; there is no automatic separation at birth. An intended
parent's pre-use commitment may ground an order despite later avoidance,
without compelling their personal care or overriding the gestating person's
post-birth refusal. On refusal/revocation, hear all lawful claims under B28/B29:
no forced surrender; no promise that intended adults acquire custody. Existing
safe relationships and the child's independent interests matter. A court may
specify multiple parental/support roles on actual grounds, never by averaging
adult preferences or treating payment as title.

After finality, a coercion/fraud challenge uses ordinary protected civil
review and stable-care safeguards; no automatic erasure, new parent, deadline
reset or physical handover by software. Adoption remains B32 and cannot be
used to relabel a prenatal sale as unpressured post-birth consent.

**RP08 — The child receives before adults finish litigation.** Register
existence, provisional identity and actual available origin facts at birth;
mark disputes safely without making services conditional on an uncontested
parent. Clinical care, shelter, food, birth documents, nationality/residence
review, education and supported safe care proceed through C02/C07/B receivers.
No fee, adult misconduct, donor anonymity or payment default renders a child
unregistered, abandoned, stateless by administrative fiat or outside equality.
Actual citizenship rules still need their lawful case assessment.

B30 allocates support among actual obligors/means and received resources once;
RP07 adds the pre-use intended commitment ground without assuming its money
is available. Public I/S receiving acts while claims proceed. No compulsory
contact, forced reunification, liquidation of child's essentials or debt
imprisonment. Clinical/child/civil services keep first receipt until an accepted
safe handoff. A private contract, genetic test or failed clinic cannot appoint
its own final judge or certify that ordered care was actually received.

[MB02–MB06](../../C02/06/01-birth-and-descent.md) now supplies the nationality
reference: actual legal parentage differs from genetics/support, later proof
of a parent at birth differs from later adoption, and already acquired citizenship
survives family/origin correction. A child receives identity/care during proof;
RP's method/consent holds and B child authority remain separate.

[VR02–VR03](../29/01-event-and-proof.md) supplies the actual birth proof and
provisional receipt route. The child receives established facts without
waiting for a parentage or nationality determination. RP and MB merits
remain separate.
