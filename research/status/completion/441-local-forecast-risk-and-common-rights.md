# Local forecast risk and the common floor

*Design533 · Scoped finding*

The [canonical rule](../../country/C04/08/11-local-forecast-risk-and-common-rights.md)
keeps the standard basis available and makes any local election, settlement
and wider sharing an express fiscal decision. The [comparison](../../settlement/local-forecast-risk-and-common-rights/01-who-bears-the-later-settlement.md)
uses Copenhagen's council record to distinguish a gross tax effect, estimated
net local result and later exposure for nonparticipating councils. The
constructed case tests both paying sides and shared reserves. Actual
adoption, settlement and resident outcomes remain unverified.
