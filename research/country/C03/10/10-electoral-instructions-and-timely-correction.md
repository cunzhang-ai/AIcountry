# Preserve voting access through false or impersonated instructions

*Design299 · ER21–ER25 · Proposed election administration and civil relief.*

**ER21 — Keep authoritative instructions usable through independent
routes.** The competent career electoral officer keeps the current
lawful times, places, eligibility routes and voting methods in
authenticated, versioned records. Provide accessible paper, direct
local help, telephone and digital routes as appropriate. Preserve
independent custody and a tested alternative for a compromised
credential, official account or vendor. A person can verify a
message without identifying their ballot choice, opening an account
or trusting an AI detector. The officer publishes a correction
only within their existing lawful operational scope. The publication
does not decide disputed political claims or alter a lawful poll.

**ER22 — Verify the actual access risk and original evidence.**
Give voters, candidates, journalists and workers a direct accessible
report route and original receipt. Preserve the relevant message,
time, apparent source, actual operative instruction and evidence of
reach and urgency with protected access. Distinguish material false
directions about how, when or whether one can vote from policy
disagreement, satire, journalism and lawful advocacy to abstain.
A message falsely attributed to a candidate matters to this rule
where its operative instruction creates a concrete risk to voting
access. An apparently authentic instruction issued by an official
can also be wrong. Voice, watermark, number of shares or a model
classification alone proves neither authorization nor unlawful
conduct. Preserve independent review and safe source correction
where the responsible office is implicated.

**ER23 — Correct access first and narrowly bound any compulsory
relay.** On independently verified operative facts, the competent
electoral officer can issue a factual correction and use funded
channels within prior law without deciding fault. Where that route
cannot reach affected voters in useful time, an expressly adopted
civil power may allow an independent court to order a specified
relay to the affected scope. Require clear and convincing evidence
of a material operational falsehood or deceptive attribution with
concrete imminent access harm, and a practicable, necessary and
proportionate correction. Identify the exact factual notice,
channel, sender, population, deadline, lawful source and expiry.
Give the affected parties notice and a useful opportunity to
answer under B17 and any earlier applicable safeguards. A relay
does not create an offence, a general duty to monitor speech,
a removal order or a power to compel political endorsement.
Any separate speech restriction retains its own enacted grounds
and review.

**ER24 — Fund the route and check who can still use it.** Price the
actual earliest voting windows, staffed languages and access,
distinct sender and records, independent court and civil
execution, and a usable secondary channel. Reach affected people
through already authorized carriers, local offices or other
independent routes without giving parties new voter data.
Where the actual group cannot be reached safely, publish the
gap and use any lawful alternate access. Verify timely correction
separately from publication, a delivery report, actual understanding
and the opportunity to vote. A later correction may help later
voters. It does not restore a lost earlier opportunity. Preserve
the original eligibility and voting clocks, secret ballots and
existing case-specific remedies.

**ER25 — Keep election validity and continuing protection separate.**
Report material failures, independent evidence and actual relief
with privacy for voters and reporters. Preserve the original
record if an authority's own instruction was wrong. Apply ER16
and EL in their exact scope. A correction, even after verified
wrongdoing, does not itself void ballots, declare a winner, extend
a term or change voting dates. Adopt prospectively under RR with
funded accepted record and execution successors. AI may help
translate or compare source records under human responsibility.
It cannot decide legality, order a relay or certify its own reach.
Reopen if the correction targets lawful opposition, misses people
without access, shares the compromised controller or arrives late.
