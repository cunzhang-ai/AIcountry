# WF85–WF89. Form the workforce that will actually deliver the first budget

*Part of [national source and capacity](../58-country-source-and-capacity-frontier.md).
Design245 research selection.*

**WF85 — Account for recruitment, training and retention as different flows.**
For a material staffing promise, specify the qualified task, service location,
useful window, workload and continuing need. Track offers, accepted entry,
supervised placement, assessed qualification, willing paid deployment and
retained service by cohort and occupation. Count actual usable shifts at each
required date. Keep the previous employer, service or other use of time in the
account where material, with safeguards for private records. A national total
cannot settle a local shift or an occupation-specific deficit. Compare
recorded exits, persistent vacancies, omitted needs and changed service
quality as well as completed training.

**WF86 — Protect current services while producing new capability.** In the
first repair and preparation motion, make retained staff, paid return,
transfer and training separate elected choices. Before binding a new
discretionary cohort, assign paid learner time, qualified supervisors,
assessment, accessible support, actual protected origin and receiving
coverage, and a lawful paid closeout. Count a supervisor away from ordinary
work and any paid replacement once. A trainee contributes to an operating
shift only for work safely delivered within competence and under supervision,
net of the supervision used. Test a common loss in the same service window. Compare staggered intake
or protected mentor overlap with a single larger start. Use lawful and
accessible selection for fewer places, with no assumption that a deferred
applicant has been served. An authorized preparation envelope can remain an option while the release
conditions fail. It cannot be counted as funded staffed expansion. Preserve
already bound wages, instruction, transitions and teach-out under their law.

**WF87 — Share a workforce without exporting the harm or restricting mobility.**
National elected budgets may fund voluntary training and paid backfill
agreements across local, public, cooperative and other authorized providers.
Name the receiving employer and service, original duty owner and legal funding
for each overlap period. A participating locality can keep its lawful delivery
choice and challenge the allocation. A funded public staffing plan cannot
depend on leaving another essential service below its safe duty. Show which
origin work persists, can be covered lawfully or has an expressly elected and
lawful change. Publish remaining deficits and support lawful recovery.
Workers remain free to change jobs or leave. Providers gain no recruitment
veto, non-compete privilege or forced service term. Any lawful recruitment
from abroad must also examine origin effects under C10, without assigning
new restrictions to workers or assuming foreign capacity.

**WF88 — Buy the measure that addresses the observed bottleneck.** Compare
strong retain and repair, supported return, paid training, lawful redeployment
and redesigned delivery. Use the same protected needs, worker terms, dates
and scarce roles. Where mentors and incumbent cover are the binding constraint,
test retention and repair before binding a new intake. Where verified
structural exits or unavailable qualifications dominate, prepare and pay for
an actual replacement pathway ahead of those exits. For a standing
service replacing exceptional cover, test paid overlap, independently
qualified origin cover and the first and next ordinary rosters.
Separate additions from transfers, attrition and losses of usable
receiver access. Report where a moved worker fills a destination
shift while an origin need persists. Hold only a dependent optional
service promise under its own conditions. Workers retain their
right to leave and each service owner retains its duties. No permanent priority
follows from the order chosen for the first authored case. Compare who must
wait and who bears added travel, unpaid time or risk. AI may check rosters,
compare alternative schedules and support voluntary learning. Count released
time only after verified safe net use, with correction, qualified human and
non-AI routes available. Use lawful DB, BCV and JC for source, scope and release.

**WF89 — Renew from measured receiving and independent challenge.** Review each
accepted cohort and paid retention window against earlier service, relevant
demographic or demand changes, dropout and actual shifts retained. Keep
intake, qualification, receipt, ongoing access and improved lives distinct.
Report subgroup or place gaps safely and preserve materially different
reasons for departure. Correct unsafe burdens without lowering licensing
standards or punishing learners for taking paid alternatives. Keep lawful
costing, oversight and user and worker challenge outside the provider's sole
control. If source or proof fails, report the gap, retain standing duties and
seek an actual funded correction. Reopen with observed late exits, verified
mentor release, a lawful net new hire, a better supported non-training
alternative or a changed service calendar.
