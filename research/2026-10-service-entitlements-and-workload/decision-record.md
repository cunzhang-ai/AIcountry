15 October 2026 · Country-design research selection, not enacted law

## Choice, alternatives and normative reasons

Select **S01–S16**: an explicit necessary-care package; free foundational,
early and catch-up learning with several legitimate methods; functional
support and optional childcare; and workload/transition rules. Keep national
finance and the existing three distinct receiving/review chains. A pure cash
route may preserve choice but cannot be assumed to buy unavailable safe
care; uniform central public-only provision can produce capacity but also
concentrate gatekeeping. A different funded mix, stronger cash option or
more limited prospective package remains a real alternative with explicit
rights continuity and distribution, not an unnoticed provider list update.

The seven-day first clinical assessment and fourteen-day care assessment
make ordinary access reviewable while preserving earlier useful-time duties.
Education ages 6–18, early access from 3, offered 20/30 hours over 39 weeks,
and 40-hour/48-week optional childcare are declared reference commitments,
not measured optima. Hours are offers rather than compulsory seat/screen
time; equivalent learning and the child's independent rights remain.
The childcare baseline is not a cap or a presumption of unpaid family care
outside it. Age boundaries do not amend civil/criminal/political majority.
First qualifying pathways have fair published entry and an accessible
alternative, not a guarantee of every preferred course or a job.

Objections retained: a broad health floor can exceed real domestic capacity;
new service commitments may impose unacceptable taxes/opportunity cost;
school-age duties and assessment can become intrusive or ideological;
public childcare can crowd out wanted informal life; hours can be mistaken
for outcomes; legal clocks may shift queues rather than deliver care.
These concerns require genuine public comparison and original evidence.
Neither a macro identity nor the research author settles their value conflict.

## What the workload comparison establishes

The old framework already allowed shared components without merged rights.
Some wording nevertheless treated every hour as one task. Clarify that a
valid joint activity may produce multiple outcomes, while its resources
count once. In the fictional preschool case, teacher and carer share actual
learning/supervision; buying both complete teams would double-book 312,000
hours. Actual dual quality is unknown. Declining early learning does not
remove care, and sharing time does not authorize shared personal records.

Authored 100,000-person bands, take-up and assessed needs generate known
partial care work 3,844,000 hours against 2,700,000 in the old 2,250-FTE
backbone. Extra 10% absence increases the gap. A gross education headcount
can pass while learning-support time fails. Primary base, mental/reproductive
increments and clerical tasks keep distinct skills; labels share base work,
but specialized increments are not free. Other service work stays unknown.

A shared clerical pool has exactly 36,000 annual hours for records/election
work yet lacks 5,000 hours in the two due months. More annual reporting
cannot transport unused earlier time into that deadline. A set of rounded
additional roles would repair these particular absence/peak constraints at
known salary components 39.70 million, before unknown training, sites,
source cash, private displacement and other costs. Those repairs are neither
selected country headcounts nor an affordable launch. The old pass against
an authored staffing minimum cannot approve this changed demand schedule.

All ratios, caseloads, time and marginal pay are authored assumptions,
not clinical/pedagogical standards. The example tests neither legal clocks,
real people nor every S duty. Actual whole-country prices, sources, local
shifts, privacy and executed remedies remain unknown. No AI gain, lower
take-up or unpaid family substitute is used as a resource.

## Authority, evidence and continuity

Assembly law/appropriation controls material entitlement and burden changes;
sector/local operators, qualified professionals, evidence custody, review,
court execution and opposition keep separate powers. Follow appointments,
funding, source/vendor control and available independent execution together.
Neither a benefit list nor a shared workload model gains authority over
every person or adopts its own new procedure. Preserve unfamiliar needs
and alternative media/methods instead of forcing them into one schema.

S has a proposed exact new-service start, with source classification, funded
overlap, staff/places, stronger existing rights, pending claims and lawful
successors. Failure is non-delivery, not an automatic cut, technical veto,
indefinite ministerial delay or executive term extension. E/I/Y/T/U and
actual software/source law continue. No personal testimony was collected.

The [WHO package overview](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240020689)
and [UNESCO rights explanation](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/what-you-need-know-about-right-education)
were retrieved for transparent coverage/access/quality context only. They
neither calibrate the numbers nor endorse this selection. No full text,
treaty-compliance, clinical or pedagogy audit is claimed. Pinned old models
retain only their fictional population/role scopes, not transferred review.

[Validation](validation.json) checks workload identities, genuine shared
time, independent take-up, rounding, distinct skill/period shortfalls,
candidate repairs, snapshots and dependency identity. This is same-author
consistency evidence only. S is a research selection; actual authority,
qualified readiness and observed better-life effects remain unestablished.
