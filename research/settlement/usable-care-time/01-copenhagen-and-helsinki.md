# Existing care paths in Copenhagen and Helsinki

*Design199 · Scope of the original comparator evidence.*

## Copenhagen

The 21 October committee record retained an open decision about redirecting
2.7 million DKK set aside to reverse earlier reductions in extended or weekend
opening. The administration noted low observed inquiries for the combined
care route and identified its minimum-hours rule as a possible cause.
The committee sought more evidence before another recommendation. This
research supplies no later disposition of that allocation.
[Committee record](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/B%C3%B8rne-%20og%20Ungdomsudvalget/m%C3%B8de-21102026/referat/punkt-5)
· [Design198 reconciliation](../released-capacity/01-copenhagen-service-and-budget.md)

The 2025 Danish consolidation, section 85a(1)–(5), gives a specific
combination route for documented work-related need, including an eligible
single parent, with a minimum of ten average weekly flexible hours
assessed over at least four weeks. The city explains that families
arrange the private carer for this combination route. The statutory
minimum governs that route. The separate round-the-clock route may still
be relevant. The city lists Haraldsgården and Månehuset and requests
evidence of the relevant hours for its extended service. Published
sites and application rules show an option, with actual vacancy,
accepted pickup, feasible travel and any case-specific eligibility
unverified. [Section 85a](https://www.retsinformation.dk/api/pdf/251135)
· [Combination route](https://www.kk.dk/borger/dagtilbud-og-skole/dagtilbud-og-pasning-0-6-aar/overblik-over-dagtilbud-og-pasningsmuligheder/kombinationsplads-privat-pasning-i-kombination-med-dagtilbud)
· [Listed round-the-clock providers](https://www.kk.dk/borger/dagtilbud-og-skole/dagtilbud-og-pasning-0-6-aar/skriv-op-til-vuggestue-og-dagpleje/se-vuggestuer-og-dagplejere)

For the city's published 2026 extended-hours surcharge at a
round-the-clock institution, 425 DKK a month plus 112 per relevant
attendance, up to a 1,060 monthly cap, gives **873 DKK** for four
qualifying attendances in one billing month. The city publishes a
different weekend charge of 187 DKK a day for a child under three years
and one month, with no monthly cap for that line. Four such days
illustrate **748 DKK** for the separate weekend line. Do not stack
these illustrations or assume how another fee or assistance applies.
They exclude ordinary fees and say nothing about an actual family's
net invoice, take-up, provider cost or national source. [City tariffs](https://www.kk.dk/borger/dagtilbud-og-skole/priser-og-tilskud/priser-for-dagtilbud)

A 6 November administrative reply reported 41 listed pedagogical vacancies
in early care in its 5 November snapshot, within 52 listed pedagogical
vacancies on its combined sector map. It separately reported that 47.7%
of pedagogue job advertisements for the city's nurseries and kindergartens
did not result in a hire in 2025. These scopes cannot be multiplied or
interpreted as a late-shift vacancy rate. The reply says the administration
lacked data to identify particularly difficult neighborhoods or facilities.
[Recruitment reply](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/2026-11/06.11.%20Svar%20til%20Yildiz%20Holm%20Akdogan%20%28A%29%20om%20rekruttering%20og%20ledige%20stillinger%20i%20kommunens%20daginstitutioner.pdf)

## Helsinki

Finnish Act 540/2018 section 13 specifies shift care as necessary
for work or study of a parent or guardian. Helsinki's published
implementation distinguishes a regular extended placement and a
shorter or intermittent need met through the ordinary setting and a
second provider when needed. It offers a public receiving mechanism
besides family purchase. The ordinary advance schedule is due by
16:00 on the preceding Tuesday. Staff assess whether a sudden change
can be met, can look for an alternate centre and request written
proof. The guidance does not promise that every sudden change can
be accommodated. It uses about fifteen days in three months to
describe regular use, with differing wording at the boundary.
This comparison relies on the distinct occasional route, not an
interpretation of the exact fifteenth day. [Section 13](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2018/540)
· [Municipal extended-hours guidance](https://www.hel.fi/en/childhood-and-education/early-childhood-education/daycare-centres)

The new country adopts the distinction between intermittent need and
stable regular placement. It keeps its own broader need criteria and
worker rules. Neither local case supplies observed causal effects on
access, a staffed spare shift or comparable national costs.
