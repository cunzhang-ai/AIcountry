# AT09–AT12. Share gains and test the successor

[Technology transition](../18-technology-transition-and-shared-gains.md)

**AT09 — Attribute each gain before making promises.** Compare the
same task and quality with and without the change. Deduct real
compute, energy, verification, error, learning, maintenance and
supply costs. Identify which gains appear as safer work, released
time, lower prices, wages, profit, tax or an actual owned return.
These are linked allocations and consequences. They cannot all
be counted as new claims on the same money. Include local,
ecological and foreign effects and any future cost. AI can help
reconcile and test alternatives. Responsible people attest actual
receiving, ownership, tax basis and lawful scope.

**AT10 — Give workers bargaining space and keep the wider choice
public.** Apply WL/TW to safer design, paid released time, retained
pay and voluntary redeployment before a consequential reduction.
A financeable agreement needs actual accepted terms and workloads.
Budget the covered work, transfer and training as distinct tasks.
Workers and firms choose terms within law. Individuals retain
portable EB and basic protection on their own grounds. A firm's
refusal to bargain is challengeable under applicable law. Neither
an agreement nor any private profit allocates the national gain.
The Assembly compares durable broad-base reform, lawful ownership
returns and other sources with net collection, loss bearers and
recurring service costs. Taxing a reported AI task is no default
source. A new permanent cash promise must meet FB02.

**AT11 — Keep essential control and actual delivery independent.**
Apply PC, competition, procurement, DP and GA in their own
scopes when the same controllers shape work, infrastructure, public
contracts or access to evidence. Test a qualified independent
provider and the service's own paid fallback. Publish source
access and concentration without exposing protected originals.
A savings entry cannot supply an unqualified shift. Check the
actual next recipient and independently challenge a material
gap before reliance. A provider exit, legitimate worker refusal
or loss of a specific skill requires paid correction, with
existing rights and lawful authorities intact.

**AT12 — Review the successor and carry continuing claims.**
Check actual source, job and service effects every quarter while
the material response is open. Group related evidence under the
original date and preserve newly affected claims. A revised estimate
or change of office does not restart the response. Record the elected
disposition, continuing work and any unresolved gap before closing
public follow-up. Reopen on unsupported collection,
qualified capacity failure, loss of contestability, unequal
exposure or a better complete alternative. Name the actual legal
start, responsible bodies, recurring and finite sources, duties,
worker and creditor claims, records and paid successors under
FT/OP/T. Reconcile existing WL, I05, EB, Y, MS, FB and JC before
lawful adoption. Neither the thresholds nor a modelled surplus
alter current powers or enact a fiscal measure.

[DP33–DP37](../../C11/09/08-verified-gains-and-public-service-receipt.md)
applies these distinctions to public-service AI. Track the destination
of verified released hours. A funded ordinary route, actual labor terms
and the elected appropriation retain their separate force.

[TN21–TN25](../../C06/59/06-income-shift-and-public-finance.md)
tests the ordinary tax source when wages, contributions and realized
owner income move differently. Keep original earned rights, actual
refunds and lawful general backing in the same comparison.

[AT18–AT22](05-technology-gains-and-paid-time.md) gives workers a
paid bargaining opportunity and a usable response before material
gain-based changes. Keep temporary learning, actual paid release,
regular pay and independent service coverage visible.
