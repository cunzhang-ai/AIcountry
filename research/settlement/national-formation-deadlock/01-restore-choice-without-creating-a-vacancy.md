# Restore public choice without creating a vacant legislature

*Design526 · Constructed constitutional and operating comparison.*

Assume a competent 100-seat Assembly returned by the first lawful repeat.
Groups of 40, 35 and 25 all support their own nominees. On each tested nominee,
the other two groups vote against. Even the existing minority route fails.
There is no operative stay on seats or votes. Groups of 40 and 35 can still
form the requisite majority for a standing-supply motion on its own merits.
Neither group thereby commits to the other's nominee. In every option, keep
the limits on caretaker powers, the ordinary election date, earned benefits,
continuing care and independent adjudication and remedy.

## Compare governance and the cost of resolving the impasse

The authored envelope is 16 due-date cash units. There are 11 units already
assigned to continuing care, payments and necessary review. These protect the
original people on both dates. Distinct local case and election crews are
available if actually booked on their lawful sources. A further five units
can be appropriated under existing procedures. For this comparison they may
finance an additional election or a separately qualified service improvement.
Any unused amount can return for a later vote only when its lawful owner can
release it. No cash figure stands in for a qualified staff assignment.

| Prospective rule | Use of the five units | If representation changes | If the split persists |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keep the lawful Assembly with elected supply and renewed formation attempts | No extra poll. Up to five can finance qualified care access beyond the standing duties. | A new parliamentary agreement can form a government on the ordinary route. Electors choose again at the fixed ordinary election. | Continuing supply is possible on recorded votes. The public has to wait for the ordinary contest to replace representatives. |

Assume the correction window and staffing also fit. For the paired vote let
verified opening be day 0, final qualification and call day 65 at the latest,
first voting day 141 and closing day 155 at that latest call. If ordinary first
voting is day 250, the paired disposition must be complete and the successor
convened by day 190. Assume both results were certified and usable notices
given on day 167. The ordinary 30-day post-notice filing period reaches day
197. A timely petition on day 175 without an operative stay leaves both results
operative for the pre-enacted convening on day 185. It remains reviewable after
handover. If an operative order instead blocks transfer through day 195, the
paired disposition misses day 190. A standing Assembly can vote supply while
it remains legally competent. The same timing failure occurs if original
records arrive only on day 195 and the necessary certificate is still missing.
The original regular contest continues and judicial findings and remedies retain
their ordinary scope. No late ruling becomes an extra legislative term.

With a corrected opening electorate of 100,000, petition qualification takes
5,000 valid signers. A result of 26,000 yes and 25,000 no passes the replacement
question. A result of 24,000 yes and 20,000 no does not. Counts are illustrative.
Cast ballots and petition support are distinct, and a person can endorse a
new Assembly without endorsing any prime minister. If members lawfully invest a
prime minister before the call, the petition ends. If they invest one after
the call, the public can weigh that fact and the lawful poll continues. A
brief investiture cannot restore a used recovery ballot.

## Test whether government and residents can use the same calendar

A necessary court intake shift and an election certification shift both fall
before day 180. In this exercise the 11 standing units already pay for the
court intake and independently executed relief, two concurrent care shifts and
an existing benefits run. The five additional units qualify only when an
independent election reviewer and alternate receiver actually accept the
separately booked work. If the election manager instead assigns the one court
interpreter to election work at the same time, the ledger still balances but
the proposed activation plan fails. Preserve the court and service assignments
and use separately paid qualified election cover if available. A deficiency
known before adoption or legal commencement keeps the proposed option outside
that mandate. A loss after a lawful call follows operative contest law and
independent review. Officeholders may neither suppress a result nor divert
protected care or remedy capacity on their own. Record affected residents'
actual receipt, delay or unresolved duty. Do not allocate the five units twice.

Now hold the political split fixed and remove the extra staffed cover or bring
the ordinary election too close. Continued fixed-term Assembly, ordinary supply
and the protected regular contest remain viable. Conversely, an automatic poll
can perform better where ballot demand is demonstrably high, faster public choice
is decisive and its independent resources exist. The paired path is the working
selection because it lets electors withhold or give a new parliamentary mandate.
It costs more to access and can reproduce factional deadlock. A directly elected
or fixed collegial executive is a separate structural rival. It could stabilize
executive appointment while leaving supply conflicts and changing removal and
accountability. It would require a comparison of the whole division of powers.

## Constitutional comparators

Sweden's Instrument of Government uses four rejected nominations and a subsequent
election, with an extraordinary poll within three months if an ordinary one is
not due in that period. Spain's Constitution requires dissolution and a new
election after two months from the first investiture vote without confidence.
These establish actual automatic-return mechanisms. They do not show that a new
poll yields agreement, or that the authored thresholds and costs work here.
