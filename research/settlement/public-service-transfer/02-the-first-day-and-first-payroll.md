# Follow a transferred service through its first day and first payroll

Illustrative units and cases, not Danish budget amounts or observed staff.
Assume a valid statutory transfer date and named authorities. A locality
previously spent 100 on the relevant services, including 70 on the function
now transferred and 30 on continuing local services.

| After legal transfer | Local authority | Successor |
|---|---:|---:|
| Own recurring finance after lawful reassignment | 30 | 70 |
| Payment under a lawful operating agreement | Receives 55 | Pays 55 |
| Actual ordinary operation | Spends 85 including retained 30 | Spends 15 directly |

The local operator can keep part of the service under the accepted
contract. Local 85 and successor 15 total 100 in actual operation.
In a budget presentation that includes the successor's transfer, gross
85 plus 70 less the internal 55 also gives 100. The 55 is one payment
and separate authority, not 55 extra services. The outgoing authority
still needs 30 for its retained services and the contract-funded part
of actual local operation.

Assume the necessary short overlap costs eight more, with separately
authorized timely sources of five for the successor and three locally.
Actual first-period cost is 108. A headline reassignment of 70 cannot
pay this overlap on its own. If the local three is missing, the one-sided
five leaves a gap. If the contract money arrives after local payroll,
the full-year account can still miss pay. Correct the lawful due payment
or use an expressly authorized dated bridge under MF/LD. Keep arrears
and the same underlying obligation reconciled once.

## At 02:00 an accepting service still matters

At midnight the successor's legal duty begins. One person needs an
overnight acute nursing visit by 02:00. A different person has a
continuing local home-care visit in the same window. The named acute
worker is unavailable. The scheduler lists the home-care worker as
the acute backup, with no compatible rescheduling or other cover.

- Switching only the budget or legal owner leaves the acute visit unmet.
- Reassigning the listed worker could meet the acute visit while missing
  the other person's due care. Count both outcomes.
- An independently staffed, reachable alternate already funded and
  authorized in the overlap can take the acute visit if it accepts in
  time and actually reaches the person. The local care proceeds separately.
- If the alternate also fails, the successor uses its existing lawful
  safety and receiving route, records the missed duty and supplies direct
  review. The outgoing owner continues the retained local visit without
  taking back the whole transferred function.

Select the funded independent alternate in this authored case. The legal
transfer takes effect in each scenario. A payment, referral or late repair
does not erase an earlier failure. The same test applies to a school place,
a benefit determination or a pending remedy, with its own service law,
consent, staffing and useful time. No actual cost or workforce is inferred.
