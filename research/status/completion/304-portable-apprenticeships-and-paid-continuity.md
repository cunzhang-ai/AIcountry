# Design396. Preserve apprenticeship progress and pay through disruption

[AL21–AL27](../../country/C07/22/05-portable-apprenticeships-and-paid-continuity.md)
protects first receipt, verified learning, worker rights and a funded
receiving option. Preserve earlier wage claims and the learner's
choice through a move or an employer's exit.

The [worked case](../../settlement/portable-apprenticeships-and-paid-continuity/01-two-apprentices-and-one-mentor.md)
separates credits, compatible mentors, funds and the next deadline.
Actual staffing, authority, costs and outcomes remain open.
