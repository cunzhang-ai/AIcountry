# PB01–PB04: personal needs cannot be a leftover in a national spreadsheet

*Part of [C05/14](../14-ordinary-life-and-household-production.md); design 124.*

**PB01 — Give the reference ordinary life a visible component scope.** The
19-row authored book names quantity, purchaser quote, unit, person/age scope and
purpose for food, clothing, personal care, connection, cultural/social activity,
extra uncovered travel, holidays, services and private choice. Shared home bills,
consumer-durable replacement, essential care/learning/assistance, work/access
and extraordinary needs have separate owners/time; none disappears because it
is outside this list. This prices a named subset, not nutrition, a compulsory
lifestyle or complete adequacy. New/unfamiliar needs remain admissible.

**PB02 — Keep private cash and voluntary use.** Retain I13–24's ordinary cash
minimum inside N, actual usable-resource rules, extra needs and lawful component
receipt. No item purchase/AI behavior is an eligibility condition, ordinary
private cash needs no itemized permission, and a statistical purpose category
confers no authority over private life. A chosen service can replace a matching
expense only under actual legal scope, recipient/quality/time and receipt rules.
S/MO/HN spending, a booked slot or another person's use is not automatic Q.

**PB03 — Use component-specific child needs in the new reference.** The old
.6 child factor was provisional, not a ceiling. Here food, clothing, connection,
personal and social components have distinct authored age coefficients. The
named annual quotes are 3,564.75/5,367/7,414/8,553CU for 0–2/3–5/6–12/13–17,
compared with adult 8,715. These are not endorsed diets, developmental norms or
new rates. The teen quote exceeds old.6×13,248 by 604.20 per person in this
case, while other quotes are lower. Do not net that difference against parents'
or other children's money; actual children and functional need require assessment.

**PB04 — Preserve unpriced ordinary life without inventing its suppliers.**
The old 13,248 adult reference exceeds these named components by 4,533; that
balance is unmapped, not evidence of excess benefit or goods already produced.
The comparison keeps the old nominal envelope wherever larger and reports new
excess components separately:1,222.4412 m total research envelope, not a G payment
or adopted B. No new benefit cut/rate, adequacy certificate, CPI or tax yield
follows. Actual larger/earlier rights and I's independent correction continue;
complete ordinary-life qualification requires real context, prices and judgment.
