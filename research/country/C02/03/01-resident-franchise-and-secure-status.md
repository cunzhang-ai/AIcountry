# RFV01–RFV05. National political standing follows actual habitual residence

*Design319 · Proposed constitutional and electoral choice.*

**RFV01 — Select one continuous year for the national resident vote.**
From age 18, a noncitizen with an actual habitual home in the country
and one continuous year of habitual residence has the same national
vote and candidacy as other national electors, subject to the existing
lawful individual rules. Replace the earlier four-year electoral
threshold prospectively. Six cumulative national habitual months
and an actual local electoral home remain the local rule. Secure
residence and voluntary naturalization retain the separate MB four-year
basis. The citizen franchise, including its overseas scope, continues.
Work, income, ownership, sponsorship, a particular nationality and
having a secure residence card are no additional electoral condition.

**RFV02 — Prove the actual home and protect genuine continuity.**
Count actual habitual residence, including childhood, asylum,
disputed administrative status and other periods when the underlying
home is established. Apply a calendar year from the proven beginning,
with ordinary legal calendar rules. Temporary absence with a continuing
habitual home and protected involuntary absence do not break it.
Before the year is complete, a genuine end of the home starts a new
continuous period on a later return. Once qualified, preserve proof
of the qualifying period. A person who permanently leaves loses the
current resident basis for participation while abroad and can recover
it on genuine habitual return without another full year. This creates
no right to enter. Preserve earlier legally vested standing. Neither
a shared address nor an account verifies residence by itself.

**RFV03 — Keep qualification independent of admission and enforcement.**
The independent electoral service verifies political eligibility from
proportionate multiple evidence, safe declarations and usable ordinary
review. A pending residence card or another agency's processing date
does not determine the person's actual period. No proof rule may force
public disclosure of a refuge address or an unsafe status fact. Use
EL's applicable date, funded correction and conditional-ballot
procedure in their exact scopes. A credible dispute needs timely
resolution and fair proof. The old or new threshold supplies no vote
when actual eligibility is unproved. Existing neutral enforcement,
protected absence and timely judicial remedies retain their scope. AI can help compare dates and
offer translation. It cannot approve residency, decide the ballot
or infer a preference.

**RFV04 — Choose the law before applying it to any electorate.**
Use the competent constitutional adoption or founding procedure.
The existing authorized electorate decides an amendment under its
own law. Under RR, preserve the protected immediate succession,
including lawful repeats, and give the new rule an exact lawful
future application. A policy expansion supplies no early restoration
exception. Use the proper operative electorate and sources for each
ordinary called contest, actual voting-day referendum and NI
opening under its own law. Preserve lawful prior support acts,
individual remedies and original records. No pending process can
recompute a past count. Fund notices, registration, case assistance,
protected campaigning and independent correction on the same calendar.

**RFV05 — Keep political choice, services and public scrutiny distinct.**
The shorter interval reduces the period of national governance
without an equal ballot for people with an established shared
life. It still excludes some recent residents. Keep AR's affected
input, lawful association and actual service and protection rights
on their independent terms. Compare immediate actual-residence
standing, the prior four-year choice and status-dependent systems
with the same proof and capacity. Report missed access and
proven errors without publishing political preferences or residence
histories. Reopen on evidence of real exclusion, unreliable proof,
unsafe use of records, a better supported threshold or a different
legitimate franchise settlement.
