1 November 2026 · Design92 · Companion to the [commitment inventory](README.md)

[PS01–PS20](../../country/C06/37-physical-supply-and-paid-trade.md) now adds
resource-fit production, food/water/reuse/energy/trade quantities, distinct
operator/review work and explicit initial stocks/foreign purchases. [Paired
subsets](../physical-supply/README.md) preserve failed irrigation, seasonal,
outage, common-controller and export-source claims. No new actual field is filled.

Food receipt includes public meals once; new packing/distribution does not
remove clinical/catering/inspection duties. Water source/reuse operators are
additional to existing network/sewer roles. Metering, regulator evidence,
plant quality and independent review are not automatically the same work.
H temporary dwellings, work/pay transition, I/S/Y, elections and remedies remain.

Substitute only actually matched old/new purchases and assets; do not add
whole old food/water/electricity lines to the new prototype, treat existing
source standby as stock, or spend one reserve for two obligations. Cash, CFC,
principal, imports, capital formation and title purchases remain separate.
Any new price selection needs joined I03/I05/IC/Y/tax/source review rather
than transfer of old FD affordability, benefits, pensions or final reviews.

Original37 function/61 prior-selection identities, historical bids and all
actual-null fields remain unchanged. Their current identity pins do not
establish source validity for altered conclusions. Actual source law, climate,
diet/safety, skills/shift calendars, territory, foreign harm, initial capital,
paid stocks, public participation and received remedies remain unverified.

requirements, not free accepted workers; new10GWh scope needs reconciliation
with old60GWh bus-depot use and cannot silently validate a complete power account.
