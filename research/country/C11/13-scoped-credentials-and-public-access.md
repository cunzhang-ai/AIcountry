# C11. Scoped credentials and public access

*Design238 · CRD01–CRD12 · [C11 knowledge and AI](../C11-knowledge-culture-and-ai.md) · Proposed research law.*

A person can prove a necessary fact or authenticate a specific act
without joining a universal account. The service remains answerable
for acceptance and correction. The proof system must keep legitimate
access working when a provider, device or signing authority fails.

| Part | Governing rule |
|---|---|
| [CRD01–CRD04. Proof and divided trust](13/01-proof-and-divided-trust.md) | Separate authoritative facts, access and legal powers. Protect voluntary channels and limit linking. |
| [CRD05–CRD08. Failure, review and recovery](13/02-failure-and-safe-recovery.md) | Isolate a suspect scope, preserve first receipt and keep independently established acts. |
| [CRD09–CRD12. Actual acceptance and public control](13/03-acceptance-and-public-control.md) | Pay for simultaneous alternatives, contest operator power and carry legal claims through change. |
