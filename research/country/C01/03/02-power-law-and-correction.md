# Country case: democratic power, law and correction

*Canonical CS03–CS04; part of [C01/03](../03-country-case-and-competing-designs.md).*

**CS03 — Public control is a requirement and a fallible mechanism.** Equal
standing, usable opposition and peaceful replacement are desired rights in
themselves. The additional causal hypothesis is that contestable information,
credible alternatives and a power to replace rulers can correct damaging
policy. Democratization/GDP evidence supplies limited support for one long-run
economic relationship, not this exact parliamentary/CJ design, distribution,
freedom or proof that every democracy always grows faster.

Trace appointment/removal, funding, original information, independent review,
execution and actual receipt under a hostile incumbent. Nominally separate
appointers can share a patron; a court can be captured or exhaust its reserves;
a qualified challenger can fail to receive campaign access before voting.
A lawful informed electorate may retain an incumbent: defeat is not the success
criterion. A technical or AI government may perform a task quickly without
acquiring legitimate sovereignty over people. Good outcomes do not replace
actual popular control.

**CS04 — Verification must connect to correction.** The causal chain is
competent independent checking → usable findings → safe political/legal action
→ actual repair. Procurement studies distinguish measured delivery checks from
additional meeting attendance and show that responses/substitution can matter.
Disclosure alone is not a guarantee of punishment, lower national corruption
or a valid criminal conviction. Random selection can reduce selective targeting;
risk/complaint work can address problems a draw misses. Both need lawful scope,
actual competence, protected sources and fair challenge.

[C04's funded audit plan](../../C04/03/02-delivery-procurement-and-integrity.md)
now includes risk-directed and genuinely random work, original transactions and
actual delivery checks where relevant. It creates no new bureau, universal 100%
audit rate or power to harass opponents. Inspect related contracts, hiring,
exclusions and supplier substitution rather than report success from one narrow
loss measure. Publication must reach affected people in useful time, with safe
response and actual corrective execution under existing law.

C08's definite offenses, fault, defense, finite custody, independent courts,
consent and civil remedies constrain domination and wrongful harm. They are
normative/procedural choices with plausible mechanisms, not causal evidence that
the drafted crime/agency code reduces total harm. Victim protection, erroneous
punishment, cost, actual safe release and received repair must all be examined.
More prosecution or recorded complaints alone proves neither safety nor failure.

Retain the current democratic/legal reference provisionally; no read study
selects CJ's exact seats/thresholds over a strong ordinary-court rival. If added
selection/review bodies consume urgent remedy capacity without better rights
protection, compare simplification. If original findings do not reach people,
authorities prevent challenge, corruption shifts negate the claimed correction or cause unacceptable harm,
or orders do not execute, narrow/reject the affected advantage claim even with
formally correct appointments. Other substitution remains an unresolved offset,
not automatic proof that every part of the mechanism failed.
