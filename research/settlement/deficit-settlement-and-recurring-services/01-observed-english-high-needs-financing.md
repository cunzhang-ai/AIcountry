# England. The historic deficit and the cost of continuing support

In 2024 England's National Audit Office reported that 101 of the
152 local authorities submitting returns had overspent their high-needs
budget in 2022–23. It distinguished an accounting treatment for
cumulative Dedicated Schools Grant deficits from the underlying
pressure of continuing provision. The DfE's October 2024 central
forecast of GBP 4.6 billion cumulative deficit at the end of
2025–26 was a forecast, with the earlier override end date then
assumed. It is not the outturn, the later grant or proof that pupils
received timely support.
[National Audit Office report, summary and paragraphs 2.30–2.35](https://www.nao.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/support-for-children-and-young-people-with-special-educational-needs.pdf)

Leicestershire's Schools Forum outturn report gives this distinct
local reconciliation in GBP million.

| Account | Reported amount |
|---|---:|
| 2025–26 high-needs gross expenditure | 155.74 |
| 2025–26 high-needs funding received in that account | 121.04 |
| High-needs in-year funding shortfall | 34.70 |
| Net in-year overspend across the grant blocks after offsets | 30.9 |
| Net grant deficit carried forward from 2024–25 | 48.3 |
| Cumulative net grant deficit at March 2026 | 79.2 |

The two displayed offsets are GBP 0.9m and GBP 2.9m on the other
blocks. The net historic stock and the high-needs in-year shortfall
are different figures with different scope. The published local
outturn does not establish individual service or independent
receipt verification.
[Leicestershire Schools Forum report, paragraphs 3–5](https://democracy.leics.gov.uk/documents/s196967/Schools%20Forum%20DSG%20Out%20turn%20report%202526.pdf)

On 29 October 2026 the Department for Education published
the High Needs Stability Grant allocations. The programme covers
90% of eligible historical deficit subject to an approved local
plan and its other conditions. The Leicestershire row, local
authority 855, lists GBP **71,319,310**, subject to final audited
outturn as stated in the workbook. This is the published allocation.
It is distinct from a payment or a mechanically calculated
percentage of a rounded local report. The determination restricts
use to the eligible historic deficit subject to an explicit excess
exception. Where the accounting override prevents immediate
application, the determination requires the applicable grant
to be held in reserve pending lawful application.
[DfE publication and allocations](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/high-needs-stability-grant-2026-to-2027)
· [Grant determination, Annex B](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6acf4ea6a57ddfe32196ac67/High_needs_stability_grant_determination_letter.pdf)

These records establish a reported annual flow, an accumulated
balance and a separate national settlement mechanism. They do
not establish an exact remaining local liability after receipt,
actual receipt or an adequate recurring budget and staffing plan.
The proposed country should require those determinations separately.
