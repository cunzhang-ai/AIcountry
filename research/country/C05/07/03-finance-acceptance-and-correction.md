# N09–N12: prior sources, accepted delivery and correction

*Part of [C05 capability candidate](../07-production-learning-and-capacity.md); design 66.*

**N09 — Fund the path before relying on its destination.** Through the C06
joint account, attribute baseline, incremental, overlapping and unknown
items by component/person/period. Reconcile acquisition, gross training pay,
teachers, displaced work, sites, materials, maintenance/replacement, imports,
records, appeals, overlap and return bridges. Contributions/taxes deducted
from pay have counterpart claims; do not count gross and net as two costs.
Actual source law, cash due, currency, lenders/partners and public loss limits
must be explicit. Future GDP, assumed efficiency, a pension receipt margin
or another person's private wealth is not an opening appropriation.

Public grants, debt, equity, guarantees and purchases have different claims,
risks and legal owners. No forced pension investment, automatic Treasury
credit, unpriced guarantee or use of the bank-resolution fund finances the
portfolio. The ordinary budget may deliberately borrow for a worthwhile
capability; zero net debt is not the objective. Disclose service, holder,
rollover and external obligations and their continuing sources.

**N10 — Separate mandate, legal permission and acceptance.** Elected
appropriation authorizes bounded spending; sector law controls permits,
professional safety, privacy and people's rights; receiving operators attest
usable capability under independent inspection/review. None substitutes for
the others. Stage funding against observable work and protection, retaining
funded correction, supplier exit and source records. An inspector does not
choose national taxes; a minister cannot approve unsafe service by announcing
that a milestone passed. A technical evidence gap is not an indefinite veto
on an already enacted benefit or the Assembly's lawful choice under uncertainty.

**N11 — Keep failure and continuing duties visible.** At an overrun, delay,
common-controller failure or rejected technology, the accountable agency
records delivered/missing capacity and affected claims, protects people under
E/T/I/Y and prepares repair, replacement, scale change or closure for the
competent elected authority. Contract remedies and court execution remain
available. Do not erase wages, pensions, essential access or compensation by
renaming the project; technical rollback cannot reverse every loss.
Compare alternatives to the same completion/service horizon, carrying later
operating, maintenance and bridge obligations. A cheap unfinished snapshot
cannot win by omitting its remaining work.

**N12 — Learn without entrenching the delivery method.** Preserve safe
original observations, costs, dissent, dependency scope, ownership and the
condition for reopening. Use actual received capability, quality, time and
harm alongside financial accounts. A safe no-change/reject result is valid.
People may propose new media, organization or social models without fitting
one technical schema; disclose unsupported meanings and mediator power.
The community can challenge the architecture through the existing democratic
and research routes. Updating a project register, passing an artifact check
or copying an agent adopts no new procedure or sovereign authority.
