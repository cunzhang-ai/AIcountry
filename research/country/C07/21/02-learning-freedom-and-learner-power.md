# ED06–ED10. Learning develops agency, not institutional loyalty

*Part of [ED public learning](../21-public-learning-and-school-power.md).* 

**ED06 — Divide authority rather than choose a truth owner.** Assembly law sets
national entitlement/minimum content/source; local elected bodies set lawful
place/language/cultural plans and budget within it. Material binding curriculum
changes use actual DR/LR/primary-law competence. Teachers/subject communities
choose and revise lawful methods/materials with professional responsibility,
plural evidence and accessible correction. Government/parent/teacher majority
cannot ban lawful criticism of the country, impose a partisan oath or override
an individual right. Courts protect law and fair process; the inspector cannot
write the nation's preferred history or make one theory immune to criticism.

**ED07 — Protect both staff judgment and pupils.** Public heads/directors are
responsible for employment supervision through actual C04/WL fair process.
Professional misconduct/performance needs specified duties, evidence, reply,
proportionate measures and independent appeal, with relevant peer expertise.
Career protection is no immunity for abuse or veto over lawful curriculum.
Separate temporary paid reassignment for actual safety from a guilt finding;
no political purge, parent popularity poll, automated grading target or
professional association's final private blacklist. Criminal allegations use
existing PO/OJ law, not a school tribunal or newly invented teaching crime.

**ED08 — Learners have a direct voice.** Schools provide safe, accessible
individual channels and regular opportunities for pupils, workers and families
to organize their own views on school life, rules, teaching experience and
improvement. Their separately chosen representatives or another workable
method cannot be handpicked by the head or counted by replicated AI accounts.
The head/director records what was heard, material disagreement and reasons
for consequential choices within actual authority; protected first receipt and
useful-time correction remain. A pupil may speak privately, use chosen support,
criticize a parent or decline a meeting without losing a place. No compulsory
school council, attendance quota, parent cash/time contribution or public
personal dossier is required; pay staff time, access and independent child aid.
Majority preference cannot authorize abuse, remove another child's rights or
substitute for the child's actual assent where law requires it.

**ED09 — Preserve plural equivalent routes.** Home, community, mixed and future
learning methods may meet S05 through proportionate actual learning/safeguarding
assessment. The education inspector checks lawful equivalence with relevant
teachers/assistance, reasons and direct review, not ideological sameness,
continuous household surveillance or a single exam/seat/device requirement.
A poor family or unfamiliar medium is not proof of neglect. Identify actual
unexamined meaning or evidence; offer supported interpretation/another lawful
route without deleting the concept. Child voice/protection and safe access
remain; no ED home-entry, custody, bodily test or attendance-enforcement power.

**ED10 — Tools cannot take over the educational relationship.** HC's retained
understanding/transfer and accommodation checks apply to public AI support.
An assistant may explain, translate, tutor or document within its mandate;
teachers retain professional assessment and learners usable non-AI/human help.
Preserve proportionate correction/appeal of credentials and material assessment,
not a parent's right to dictate a mark. Procurement/model updates cannot amend
curriculum, grade a child into civic inferiority or silently share behavioral/
family profiles with employers, police or benefits. PD/source authority governs
any separate disclosure. A credential is not a job, political mandate or proof
that a generated answer became the learner's retained understanding.
