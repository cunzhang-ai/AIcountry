# Choose a first budget that keeps independent duties working together

*T43–T48. Candidate first-programme integration rules.*

**T43 — Place the continuing country on the account first.** For each
budget window put the actual prior-law duties, owners, useful deadlines,
staff, supplies, independent review and paid execution on one calendar.
Mark the legal authority, cash and actual receiving separately. Separate
standing duties, proposed continuity appropriations, existing binding
commitments and optional new work. Include the next payable period and
disclose material national functions outside the quantified comparison.
The incremental schedule cannot be called a financed country while
those duties and sources remain unknown.

**T44 — Use a common account without pooling rights or money.** Identify
the amounts and allowed uses of each source, and test what every payer
can actually release. Reserve independent review and ordinary access
beside the services it protects. Count each qualified hour, accepted
vehicle, safe lot and available amount only once in overlapping
windows. A stock of food is separate from its funded transport and
receiving. General cash cannot replace a missing qualified officer.
Restricted cash cannot fill a general gap unless an independent lawful
source decision makes it available. Reconcile the sending and
receiving ends before counting a transfer or displaced work.

**T45 — Give elected members exact operable alternatives.** Compare
continuation on existing authority, a separately authorized limited
continuation, and a broader lawful programme where feasible. Use the
same evidence available at the decision time, duties and resources.
Record which acts need a separate vote, qualified release, paid
reserve activation or later decision. Reconcile any exact separate
continuity vote with the remaining whole budget. Present the
uncovered people, costs and deadlines of each alternative. An
appropriation, an independently qualified method or a political
agreement is not proof of staffed and financed delivery.

**T46 — Hold only the unqualified step and carry original duties.** Keep
existing lawful operations and interim routes where they can deliver.
If a proposed optional start uses cash, authority or independent
capacity needed for the protected package, defer or narrow the start
through its lawful decision. A stalled vote, staffing loss or missing
resource changes what can be delivered, without extinguishing due
claims. Before the last useful date, put a separately feasible
correction to the competent authority and keep independent scrutiny
and remedy reachable. Use actual sector priority law in shortage.
A comparison cannot assign a new priority between people's rights.

**T47 — Show exhaustion, restoration and the next call.** At each new
window publish the settled sources, paid work, accepted goods, due
claims and independent verification that carry forward. Show what
expires or was already used, which sources are new and who can
lawfully act. Reconcile unresolved earlier duties before asserting a
new headroom amount. A later vote or receipt may repair a loss.
Preserve the earlier failure and original remedy. No new programme
can rely on the released money or work until it has actually become
available and has been authorized.

**T48 — Select the narrow complete sequence supported by the case.**
In the constructed comparison, use the separately voted water
maintenance, qualified income-surge cover and staged food releases
together. Keep independent review on the protected baseline.
Defer the optional AI pilot because no usable balance remains in
the tested window. The decision is conditional on actual authority,
availability and receiving. Reopen if later cash, staff, food
safety or a better fully funded option changes the outcome. AI may
compare schedules and find duplicate commitments. Legislative,
financial, clinical and judicial decisions retain their lawful
owners. Give opposition and affected people useful access to
alternatives and independent challenge.
