# PT19–PT24: public custody, limited assurance and an honest transition

*Part of [C08/26](../26-property-transfers-and-secured-credit.md); proposed public duties.*

**PT19 — Who keeps records and who can replace them.** Assign land-title
and ordinary-security records to the existing C04 property-record function,
under a statutory Chief Property Registrar appointed by Cabinet through C04's
open qualified competition, published reasons, conflict checks and court access.
Apply the protected career tenure, misconduct/failure process, qualified deputy
180-day outer routine-management limit and14-day unexpected recruitment duty
expressly specified for the parallel OE26 registrar. This is an express PT
assignment, not authority acquired by analogy or a new elected-sovereign board.
Actual property/records/evidence/privacy competence includes equivalent practice;
no minister/party executive, conflicted conveyancer, lender or AI vendor can hold
the post. Qualified assigned officers sign their own formal acts; the Chief/
minister cannot direct named title outcomes or finally adjudicate their own
contested certification. Ordinary independent courts determine contested
substantive rights. Assembly controls law and funded service budget; audit,
PD supervision and judicial execution have distinct sources. Court tenure and
removal do not come from the Registrar. Elections do not purge career keepers.

Fund actual local/offline receiving, safe original custody, search, backups,
interpretation and independent adjudication; fees/fines/asset seizures are no
autonomous source. Ordinary assisted filing/search is free at the public point
of use, paid from G; separately requested conveyancing is not a mandatory guild
monopoly. No private index vendor, blockchain operator or model owns the legal
priority clock. Public can challenge service, organize alternatives and seek
ordinary elected replacement of the general method while rights persist.

**PT20 — Notice, decisions and correction.** Distinguish a security notice
from certification that the private debt/collateral statement is true. Record
original land lodgment, security receipt and actual searchable effective time
separately. PT08 security effectiveness begins only when the authorized minimum
notice is actually queryable through the public index; an offline receipt or
backlogged upload cannot secretly backdate perfection. Enter admissible notices
without delay in actual receipt order; allow assisted public query. A temporary
outage does not cancel already valid continuously registered interests, but
search results must state unavailability/last completeness rather than certify
an empty index. New notices waiting for real public availability bear a disclosed
priority risk; parties may postpone, use lawful tangible possession or seek
specific court preservation, not fabricate perfection or consent.

Land formal applications and challenged registry decisions use AP/B16 protected
first-receipt help/30-working-day disposition with one reasoned at-most30-working-
day extension, fair adverse reply and direct delay/urgent court review. A
substantive dispute requires ordinary-court determination, not a clerk's
unreviewable quiet-title judgment. Within five working days of identifying it,
give usable issues/source reasons and assist a protected referral; do not reset
original receipt or claim the court has already decided. Material affected
holders/occupiers receive safe direct notice and B reply before adverse change;
actual informed agreement can complete an uncontested lawful transfer without
an invented waiting period. A bare objection creates no new asset freeze;
parties can seek B17's specific independent preservation. Never register a
known void instrument as a shortcut to closing a backlog. Security notice errors
need prompt owner notice and accessible correction/challenge; a debtor's report
alone neither proves fraud nor cancels a valid creditor. Invalid notice creates
no substantive lien. A nonmisleading clerical defect still permitting reliable
standard party/asset search is not automatic forfeiture; a material misidentifying
notice is ineffective for the missed scope, with actual evidence and review.

**PT21 — Precisely scoped state exposure.** Retain B38 true-title restoration
and net direct reasonable reliance compensation for incorrect official title
certification, and OE27's separate entity assurance. This Act **expressly adds**
Treasury liability without personal officer-fault prerequisite for proved net
direct reasonable reliance loss caused by a materially wrong official security-
index search/effective-time certification, or an office-attributable failure
to publish an admissible duly received authorized notice in its required order.
Prove attributable act/omission, timely usable submission, actual expected lawful
priority absent that failure, reliance/causal loss, actual interests and credited
recovery. Mere private false content, ineligible notice, correctly disclosed
outage, accurate unperfected status or market loss is no automatic assurance.
Material adverse correction never secretly primes an intervening lawful holder;
the public operational-loss claim bears the remaining proved office error.
Use C06's visible Treasury public-claims account, B19 payment/execution and
B37 discoverability/periods. No fully funded reserve, all-fraud insurance,
fee yield, actual appropriation or recoverable seller value is presumed. Credit
received wrongdoer/insurer recovery once, with lawful subrogation after payment.
The state cannot buy relief from a claimant by requiring permanent public
financial testimony or erase due compensation when the office budget fails.

**PT22 — Useful publicity and private life.** Make transaction-relevant
parcel/right qualifications and a targeted security index genuinely searchable,
including assisted searches without an account or creditor's permission.
Use stable safe identifiers and verification assistance; public output contains
the minimum needed to distinguish the party/asset, collateral class, ceiling,
status and times. No universal public name-to-household/debt/relationship map,
bulk personal dossier, intimate address exposure or unlimited secondary profiling.
Provide protected verification to materially affected parties and courts under
PD; safely contact displaced/threatened holders. Binding claims remain usable
despite protective redaction. Retain source instruments/priority history securely
for the right's life and unexpired connected claim/execution periods, plus
specific lawful holds; cancel/expire obsolete notices in the live index without
deleting protected correction evidence. No automatic permanent public blacklist.

**PT23 — Prospective law and old obligations.** The coordinated adopting Act
sets a fixed component date at least180calendar days after enactment, later
actual RR-mandated dates where required; no readiness extension by the custodian.
Actual old title/security/lease/assignment/priority, pending sales/cases, restraint
and prescription dates retain their source law until an expressly lawful
migration supplies notice, heard interests, actual source and continuity. Do
not require old holders to win a new online filing race or convert an old
secured debt into unsecured by this research. Map legal rank separately from
new search display; preserve legacy qualifications and frozen original evidence.
New advances/collateral/renewals take only the effect actually authorized, not
an assumed reset of an old claim. Missing safe new operation means no new
claim of readiness: actual due old service/remedy law continues, with elected
lawful correction, paid receiving and public failure disclosed. Do not enact
contradictory commencement/repeal language that silently erases old rights.
Before adoption reconcile exact legacy sources, fees, state exposure and actual
independent court/search/records capacity. Paid workers/vendor contracts,
arrears, existing public claims and actual restricted-source ownership persist.

**PT24 — Choice, retained objection and reopening.** [Adversarial routes](../../../settlement/property-title/01-adversarial-routes-and-rivals.md)
compare repair/no change, title-insurance/strong purchaser finality, this selected
holder/notice/court route and stronger public/collective tenure/credit alternatives.
None can discard work, housing, collective/ecological or democratic requirements.
Absent true titles, source law, independent affected judgment, simultaneous
paid staff, court execution and cash is missing evidence/capacity, not proof
that a chosen rule works. Reopen on displacement, secret liens, unaffordable
credit, lender entrenchment, official/mediator capture, privacy harm, unpaid
restoration or a better substantive rival. Next inspect ordinary common-building
and neighbor/use disputes against actual existing H/B/technical choices; retain
adequate rules and repair only a real remaining country-design omission.
