# F9. A child's identity is disputed while a carer escapes violence

*Part of [Whole-country stress](../../FOUNDING-AND-STRESS.md); integrated design 55 research, not a real-world result.*

**Events:** A child and noncitizen carer move across localities after intimate
violence. A partner controls the only digital family account and disputes the
child's birth and parentage record. The first registrar will not correct it;
school, health, benefits and shelter each request a different document. A child
service considers removal because the carer lacks housing. The respondent
disputes the allegation and seeks contact and access to the child's record.

**Candidate response:** The first safe intake preserves the claim date and
arranges confidential shelter, health care, school access and provisional
subsistence without inferring nationality or parentage. A registrar preserves
original evidence and gives reviewable reasons; a competent court decides
contested status, protective contact and any proposed removal under their
separate laws. The child receives age-appropriate independent support and
voice. The child service offers housing and practical help before treating a
material shortage as parental failure; actual danger can justify the least
restrictive urgent protection with prompt independent hearing and a funded,
safe placement. The respondent receives a fair challenge through safe
disclosure, without controlling the applicant's location or payment channel.
The Treasury and receiving locality account for shelter, school, carer time,
transport, legal help and the continuance of any lawful maintenance duty.

**What remains unproved:** real registrars, court hours, interpreters, a safe
placement, confidential records, alternate payment, qualified care staff and
cross-locality funding may be absent or controlled by the same person or
vendor. Provisional access does not prove status; a protection order does not
prove guilt; a court ruling does not deliver a safe night or correct every
downstream record. Compare delayed aid, wrongful removal, missed serious harm
and unfair orders using original cases and affected-party accounts before
claiming this architecture improves family safety.
