# LR06–LR10: hearing people is separate from an actual council vote

*Part of [C03/14](../14-local-law-and-public-control.md); design112 candidate.*

**LR06 — The public body prepares the case.** A council member or authorized
issuer publishes draft/source, lawful existing baseline, precise proposed
condition, people including nonusers/neighboring communities, no-change/fewer-
rules/substantive rivals, original evidence/uncertainty, cumulative effects,
distribution/cost, actual proposed receiver/payer and transition. A resident's
initial problem report needs no draft legislation or technical dossier. Career
legal/source staff provide paid drafting/help; technical disagreement remains
answerable, not an official's policy veto or a fabricated independent review.

**LR07 —21days input,30 for material effects.** Ordinary drafts have at least
21 complete calendar days following draft-publication day0. Use at least30days
if protected access/equality, essential service, serious human/environmental risk,
compulsory charge, coercive/review burden, irreversible data/physical commitment
or a material cross-boundary duty may change. Higher sector periods prevail.
Missing credible effect evidence cannot prove the ordinary lower class; use30
or narrow/withdraw. No cost percentage alone makes a rights change harmless.
Reasoned disposition is due within14days after input closes, before complete L.
Missing that duty remains a reviewable delay, with original harm/remedy dates.
Late actual reasons may support a later prospective L when the complete input,
source and affected scope remain valid; no official can permanently veto a
proposal merely by withholding a timely report. Do not backdate compliance or
restart valid input automatically. Material new effects, evidence or objections
still require the affected renewed input; stronger source deadlines remain.

Provide paid oral/private/collective/offline and future-media input, translation,
care/time/travel and outreach to missing affected people. Record material examined/
unexamined reasons, dissent, framing objections and translation loss, not an AI
consensus or presumed public approval. Actual individual hardship/rights claims
travel immediately to their competent receiver/useful-time remedy, not this queue.
A single real proceeding may satisfy overlapping duties only when its complete
source, affected scope, text, timing, access and objection requirements all hold;
old consultation or an unchanged file alone does not transfer support.

**LR08 — L is complete actual public and council receipt.** After input/disposition,
L is the actual calendar day when the Council Clerk has the final packet AND it
is freely accessible publicly: exact text/version/source, changes, effects/cost,
reasons/input dispositions, remaining objections, actual proposed receiver/source,
future start/sunset and transition. Day0 is L; all final7complete days1…7 pass
before the first ordinary vote on L+8. No chair acknowledgment, backdated signature,
booking or missing dynamic annex replaces the event. Protected receipts/independent
copies preserve a timely blocked filing, not an invented complete packet.

**LR09 — Every new binding general norm needs full n.** Approve/reject exact
text only on L+8…L+30 inclusive, with at least floor(n/2)+1 actual yes and that
many lawful members present, n the full statutory council including vacancies.
This expressly adds the special general-rule threshold to EL11, not all ordinary
internal votes. No abstention, recusal, reduced turnout, casting vote, committee,
expert/AI certificate or silence supplies missing approval. A later operative
amendment requires new L; timely court/records correction does not authorize a
secret policy edit. Related items may share facts/hearing only while each exact
motion, effect and minority objection remains independently visible.

Council ordinance adoption is actual making if its source assigns lawmaking to
the council; no mayoral assent veto. For an instrument legally assigned to a named
delegated issuer, approval is not making: require their actual lawful signature
by approval+7calendar days and before the council's legal end, or it lapses.
The issuer has no political reconsideration veto; a genuine legality concern is
reasoned/published with direct urgent courts, not signature by silence. A clerk/
judge cannot fabricate missing making or convert approval into a different power.
No valid ordinary vote by end L+30 lapses the proposal; old rights/law keep sources.

**LR10 — Publish actual law and respect stronger clocks.** Register the actual
vote, source/making, enforceable text, start/sunset and implementing payer/records
receivers. Ordinary effect is no earlier than published future start AND day8
after actual complete operative public notice C (C is day0), after valid making.
A recorded proposal is not that notice. Longer sector notice wins; no unpublished
or expired norm supports adverse action. This additional notice costs time and
may delay a beneficial rule; it is not empirical optimality or guaranteed safety.

NF19 keeps proposal≥120days/input30days/disposition≤14days and final vote PLUS
publication≥60days before1January. The same genuinely complete NF process can
meet LR07; final LR vote also needs L+8…30 AND the earlier NF deadline. Council
rate adoption is making, no extra executive discretion. No late vote/publication
is backdated to create a rate. Where an Act has expressly adopted NF,
a failed vote preserves the last applicable lawful rate. The 1% opening
value belongs to the benchmark and applies only if expressly adopted.
Where NF has not been adopted, continue the actual lawful prior source
and rate. Failed consultation or a vote creates no rate.
The joined Act must expressly distinguish the14-day reasons duty from a permanent
bar: late reasons may precede actual L, without curing delay or extending NF60.
LR neither extends tax deadlines nor creates a fee/base. The adopting Act must
express this joined route; unchanged national budgets/sector primary procedures
retain their actual authority rather than being replaced by a council label.
