# Two places and one capital budget

A dense district and a remote district need the same type of qualified
primary care. They have 60 and 20 protected timely encounters in the
illustrative service window, with additional elective need above those
figures. The current funded routes supply 60 and 12. The 12 remote
encounters are staffed locally and survive the stated main road closure.
The eight uncovered remote encounters are existing duties throughout
the comparison. The same service and staff quality are assumed.

New gross capital can use at most 12 units, of which at most 10 can be
paid by national funds. The dense district has two lawful capital units
available and the remote district one. The recurrent ceiling is six.
Only one qualified commissioning crew is available per early or later
work window. The authored care and transport teams are separately
staffed as specified. Apply prior legal allocation rules to every unit.
No project can use another programme's money because the totals fit.

The hub serves eight additional remote encounters ordinarily. A main-road
closure removes five of those because the planned receiving route crosses
the closed segment. The selected remote route supplies five through the
repaired local site and three through outreach. Its protected independent
local team can take the three if the ordinary outreach is interrupted by
the stated closure. The team and overlap are included in the six recurring
units. It has its own tested access, staff and equipment under the stated
stress. A second failure at its local power source could reduce remote
receiving to 17. No readiness claim covers that wider loss.

Select the fourth option under the authored values and conditions. Use
the sole commissioning crew on the remote site in the early window and
the smaller dense-district improvement later. The early remote repair
must finish before the first affected deadline. The dense improvement
cannot also claim the early crew, and the existing 60 dense encounters
continue. An ordinary hub-plus-shuttle package remains a strong option
if an independently staffed route protects the missing five at an
acceptable price. Dense expansion alone offers more elective encounters
while leaving eight remote duties unfulfilled.

The remote repair and outreach cost eight capital units. A uniform 25%
local match would require two local units, though only one is lawfully
available. The selected co-financing uses that one and an authorized
national seven. Dense repair uses its own one plus national three.
Both money and actual delivery must be authorized. If the local one
arrives late, a lawful bridge needs its own dated source. Do not pretend
the aggregate balance made it available. If the dense district's
protected demand rises to 70, the selected plan leaves two dense duties
unmet. That loss requires a new lawful decision or correction even
though the remote district still receives all 20.

This is an authored portfolio exercise. Real decisions must include
current maintenance, residents' other services, employment, ecological
effects, retained rights, and both local and national opportunity costs.
