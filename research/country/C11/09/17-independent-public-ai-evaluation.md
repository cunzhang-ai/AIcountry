# Keep public AI evaluation independent of the supplier

*DP78–DP83. Candidate evaluation rules for material public reliance.*

**DP78 — Set the service test before examining offers.** The lawful sector
owner sets the task, protected outcomes, ordinary comparator, relevant
populations, error classes, deadline, uncertainty and decision rule under
its existing legal powers. Publish the method and safe examples in time
for challenge. Include assisted and non-digital routes, rare serious
errors and the human work required after output. An aggregate success
rate cannot waive a legal protection or erase a failed group. Amend a
material criterion before applying it to a new comparison, with reasons
and equal treatment. Keep earlier results and explain differences.

**DP79 — Pay for an evaluator outside the buying and delivery chain.**
For covered high-consequence reliance, the adopting law assigns
qualified domain, methods and accessibility assessment to an existing
independent review office. Give it separately voted staff and a lawful
alternate outside the buying office, service operator and evaluated
supplier. Publish conflict and substitution rules. The independent
office controls the final sample, run records and assessment. The
supplier may explain limitations and contest an error. It cannot choose
the final cases, evaluate its own result or pay the assessor. Audit
checks control and evidence. The competent public buyer decides the
award and the sector owner authorizes actual reliance under separate
law. A technical assessor cannot decide a person's entitlement.

**DP80 — Keep shared evidence reusable and fresh evidence independent.**
Use an open set for explanation, accessibility and routine regression.
Qualify consequential reliance on independently selected fresh cases
or another justified test with equivalent protection against known
exposure and omissions. Publish the sampling method, coverage, relevant
definitions and how protected evidence can be independently inspected.
Use synthetic or minimized evidence where it tests the same meaning,
and lawful protected originals where necessary. Pin the selection
date, training or prior exposure information that can be checked,
test configuration, case rules, scoring sources and authorized users.
Disclose unknown provenance. A set of paraphrases or graders derived
from the same source does not establish independent support. Do not
give suppliers private case records or a right to train on test inputs.
When updating or renewing material reliance using evidence from prior
operation, independently check how earlier routing and human follow-up
selected the cases that produced observed labels.
Fresh observations drawn only from the same earlier queue still
share that selection. Apply DP21 and PCO17–PCO21. Use lawful
purpose-limited independent observation where material, keep
unobserved cases unknown and distinguish changed outcomes from
pre-existing need. No training right follows from a complaint or
corrected case. Review material learned changes under DP38–DP42.
Provide protected independent replication, timely challenge and
publication of safe retired examples. Rotate or replace a materially
exposed sample and preserve safe history.

**DP81 — Grade the operated service and name the limit of reliance.**
Use the actual configuration and compare the supplier route, a strong
ordinary route and any narrowed hybrid. Have qualified people derive
expected outcomes from law and evidence. Preserve reasoned disagreement
and safe routes for cases without a single correct outcome. Measure
errors, uncertainty, group coverage, exceptions, paid human review,
actual useful-time capacity and recovery. Keep distinct findings for
advice, decisions and service receipt. A score from another version,
location or legal regime qualifies only its supported dependencies.
Where a protected scope fails or is unknown, do not authorize new
material reliance there. A narrower use requires an enforceable scope,
an independently checked routing rule and a qualified, paid ordinary
route for everything excluded. Neither a threshold nor an evaluation
case creates evidence against a resident.

**DP82 — Preserve the independent basis at renewal and exit.** Maintain
lawful access to methods, results, proof, skilled assessors and test
material needed for another supplier or ordinary route. Budget that
work before purchase. A material version, data, use, evaluator or
dependency change requires fresh review of affected claims, including
known events and complaints. A shared defect suspends only affected
reliance under DP09–DP12 and DP38–DP42. Continue valid payments, intake,
appeals and qualified ordinary decisions on their original dates. If
the actual alternate fails, report unpaid or undelivered duties and
seek lawful funding or correction. A new award does not discharge
the predecessor's duties.

**DP83 — Review the evaluator and repair what the test missed.** Give
elected oversight, affected people, workers and competing suppliers
a usable route to challenge material exclusions, comparators, rubric
changes or conflicts through existing forums. Publish safe results
and uncertainty separately from the award, permission and actual
service. Keep test access protected where disclosure would reveal
personal facts or defeat a live independent check. A reviewer outside
the challenged evaluation can inspect relevant originals and require
a lawful rerun or correction. Follow actual harm to an independently
verified remedy under LX and ordinary law. AI may help compare or
translate tests under a scoped mandate. It cannot certify its own
reliance, select a legally decisive benchmark or close a case. Reopen
on common errors, unexplained grading, reviewer capture or a simpler
route that better serves residents.
