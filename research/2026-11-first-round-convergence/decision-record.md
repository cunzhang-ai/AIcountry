*14 November 2026 · First-round country proposal*

## Decision and baseline

Freeze the [Design517 working baseline](baseline.json) for the edited files.
Use the proposed [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision record](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).
No change to that research method is proposed. The proposed settlement concerns
an existing polity choosing lawful reform. The country has no actual adopting
population. The exercise distinguishes participating in a public process, voting
in an election and authorizing a constitutional change.

Three parallel AI-assisted reviews covered mandate and ordinary law, economy and
services, and communication and long-term improvement. They examined the same
project corpus. Their findings are analytical input, with shared underlying
sources. They supply neither independent empirical corroboration nor actual
affected-party participation. Final integration reconciles their separate
findings with the exact existing candidate law.

## Reasoning and alternatives

- First supply can repair delivery now while a different provider may give more
  reliable ordinary receipt. That claim depends on independent paid availability,
  gross local cash, input from affected residents and continuity after the first
  call. A reservation made for court access remains a separate use.
- A direct public provider can reduce contractual dependency. It can also displace
  staff from an existing service, concentrate local power or impose startup costs.
  A mutual provider can support more user control with viable exit and an actual
  lawful backstop. Both remain eligible on the same neutral test.
- A larger fiscal package could address multiple exclusions earlier. Its required
  sources, labor and service cutoffs can conflict. Temporary cash, uncollected
  taxes and estimated AI savings do not resolve a permanent operating gap.
- Earlier resident-assisted rendering matters when the ordinary route cannot
  protect an urgent choice. Keep the paid human route first. In the reference the
  public-works checker is the initial optional evaluator. A separately supported
  choice can replace it if the two offers compete and the communication remedy
  is the earlier qualified need.
- A new territorial settlement can be legitimate. It needs its own affected
  public, authority and obligations. For retrofit, distinguish lawful effect and
  participation from substantive legitimacy when incumbents or exclusion distort
  the process. Apply existing competent review.

## Result, continuity and reopening

[Proposed T133–T137](../country/C14/06/30-first-round-public-decisions.md) are the
canonical opening selections. The [integrated settlement](../FIRST-ROUND-SETTLEMENT.md),
[status register](../DESIGN-STATUS.md) and [operating blueprint](../OPERATING-BLUEPRINT.md)
describe the same opening. Detailed law remains with the owning chapters.
A material change in the design prompts fresh review; general analytical
support carries only within its original scopes. Established claims and pending
reviews keep their governing laws, dates, payer and remedy. An unadopted
proposal grants no authority or spending. No new governing research procedure
or software permission follows.

A first public docket can be assembled once an actual competent public and
prior-law route exist. Specify exact perimeter, reviewable authority and
protected electoral cutover. Compare one priced continuity option and a qualified
rival on the same affected people and next due window, within the competent
body's adopted source and review limits. Until an actual authorization exists,
use the bounded research comparison and its explicit assumptions. Reopen on a
credible exclusion, materially changed electoral text, a dated resource
shortfall, evidence of loss outside the observed route or a qualified rival
with better protected receipt. The general objective of better lives remains
open to real evidence.
