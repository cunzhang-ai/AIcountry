# C08. Equality law and effective remedies

*Part of [C08 everyday law and justice](../C08-everyday-law-and-justice.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

The Assembly enacts a common civil equality floor linked to [C02's equal
standing](../C02/05-equal-standing-and-differentiated-needs.md). It defines
direct exclusion, apparently neutral rules with unjustified unequal effects,
harassment in covered relationships, denial of reasonable accommodation and
retaliation for a lawful complaint or witness. It also states material
exceptions, legitimate differential duties, evidence rules, limitation
periods and remedies. Criminal liability, if any, needs separate precise
offenses and ordinary proof; a civil finding cannot itself impose a criminal
sentence. A statistical disparity calls for inquiry, not automatic guilt.

Each sector keeps a named first receiver and a power to correct its own
decision: employers and labor inspectors for work, housing authorities and
courts for tenancy, clinical and school reviewers for care and education,
financial supervisors for payment access, electoral bodies for political
access, and police command with outside review for enforcement conduct.
Complaints can be made in person, with assistance or through a lawful digital
route. A first receiver preserves the claim date and evidence, prevents
retaliation and transfers only to an accepting body. A person can go directly
to court or independent review where allowed; no equality office can force
them to exhaust an unsafe internal channel. Urgent interim relief is available
when a lost job, home, treatment or vote cannot later be restored by damages.

For civil claims, a claimant supplies facts capable of supporting a specific
inference of unequal treatment or effect. A respondent with exclusive access
to selection rules, records or model configurations must disclose relevant
evidence under judicial protection and show the lawful basis and proportionate
necessity of a contested distinction. The court tests contrary evidence and
third-party rights; it cannot assume that every difference proves bias.
Public bodies owe recorded reasons and retained source evidence for material
decisions. An AI score, proxy variable or vendor trade-secret claim cannot
become an unchallengeable reason, but disclosure must protect other people's
privacy and legitimate security. Group-level evidence can support an inquiry
while individual remedies still require a legally sufficient case.

An independent equality inquiry service may investigate patterns across
sectors, publish protected findings, provide neutral public guidance, and
bring or assist a defined public-interest case. Case representation and
advice for opposing parties require separate counsel and protected records;
the inquiry service cannot represent both sides of the same dispute. It
needs defined standing for a systemic case. Affected people can contest its
scope, disclosure and proposed settlement; it cannot waive their individual
claims or publicize protected identities by treating inquiry as consent. It
cannot finally adjudicate its own investigation, impose a general quota,
order arrest, decide elections or replace a sector's lawful safety judgment.
The Assembly appoints its leadership by a recorded vote after open
nominations, published qualifications, interest disclosure and hearings
accessible to opposition and affected associations. Fixed staggered,
nonrenewable terms limit dependence on reappointment; prior law defines a
bounded vacancy arrangement without renewing an incumbent indefinitely.
Removal needs stated cause and independent court review. Its statutory
budget and source access follow an audited payment route that an implicated
minister cannot selectively stop; extraordinary spending still needs the
elected budget. Government,
large providers and advocacy organizations can each try to capture it;
independent courts, sector bodies, affected associations and direct claimant
standing remain alternate routes. Its failure or refusal does not extinguish
an individual's claim. A court can order correction, access, accommodation,
compensation, a bounded practice change or evidence preservation, with an
identified executor and verification of receipt. Rights do not end at a
published recommendation.

Complaint and outcome data are disaggregated only where safe and lawful.
Measure rejected applicants and nonusers as well as complainants; few
complaints may mean fear or inability to enter. Protect witnesses and small
groups against reidentification, but permit an independent challenge when
privacy is invoked to hide a pattern. The C06 account funds interpreters,
advocates, inspectors, protected evidence, courts, accommodations and actual
remedy execution. Existing sector laws, contracts, pending claims and
collective rights require lawful migration. No selected research text
changes liability or authorizes data collection now. Reopen if the inquiry
office becomes a political censor, sector remedies remain disconnected,
claimants cannot obtain evidence, or an adopted standard causes serious
unjustified burdens or exclusions.

[SR repeated public failure](30-repeated-public-failure-and-remedy.md)
specifies how a proved common breach reaches a bounded remedy, individual
correction and a lawful closure decision. It creates no general
eligibility or public-interest standing beyond actual law.

[CI political freedom and livelihoods](../C03/04/01-political-freedom-and-livelihood-protection.md)
uses the ordinary court for a linked coercive threat and preserves the
first protected receipt across sectors. Equality, work, housing and safety
claims keep their own grounds and remedies. The urgent route separates
livelihood protection from delivery of the civic opportunity.
