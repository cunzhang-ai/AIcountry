# One wrong fact, two public decisions

This constructed case tests [RUC01–RUC08](../../country/C04/06/07-public-record-correction-and-restored-rights.md).
A public earnings record incorrectly attributes income of 90 to April. A housing
payment and an essential travel award each use that figure. On day 0 the issuer
confirms April earnings were 30. The example assumes both programmes have
verified the other eligibility facts, and each has lawful authority to correct
its own decision.

| Decision | Original result | Required act |
|---|---|---|
| April housing payment, already denied | Threshold 50, due amount 5 | Housing officer corrects the April denial and confirms usable payment by day 2 |
| April travel award, pending removal | Threshold 40, existing scheduled trip | Travel officer preserves and confirms the approved trip by day 1 |
| May travel award | Uses May earnings of 80 | Decide independently under May's evidence and law |

An authenticated April correction reaches housing on day 0. An inaccessible
normal intake leaves travel without acknowledgment. The issuer escalates that
day to the named alternate, who accepts the restricted-scope correction by
the day 1 cutoff. The travel provider confirms the trip on day 1. Housing
releases the 5 units. A returned transfer still leaves 5 due. A verified
alternate payment receives the full 5 on day 2. Repeating the alert or
receiving an old April extract after correction does not reinstate the denial
or create an additional housing award.

Compare a correction that only changes June earnings. It provides no basis
to amend the April denial. Compare also the historical correction with neither
a usable alternate travel route nor usable payment. The exposure and delivery
failure remain open even though the central source is correct. Record the
missed window or uncovered amount and independent review separately.

These are assumed thresholds, deadlines and receiving paths. The reproduction
checks their implications; it supplies no measured error rate, capacity or
service improvement. [Reproduction](verify-record-correction.py).
