# Delegated law: challenge before harm and deliver the remedy

*DR17–DR20; part of [C03/11](../11-delegated-rules-and-effective-public-control.md).*

**DR17 — Direct, answerable legality review.** A materially affected person,
provider/worker, council, competent collective claimant or member whose statutory
scrutiny right is blocked can approach ordinary courts without a useless agency
appeal. Credible imminent application supports pre-enforcement review of source,
classification/cumulation, mandatory process, accessible versions, dates and
rights. Filing does not itself stay a rule: B17 requires particular lawful
interim grounds, fair response and third-party protection. B16 individual review
runs from usable notice; publication alone cannot consume a victim's unknown
injury claim. B15/B37 periods/tolling remain. Required evidence travels under
B14 safe disclosure; no person must produce a secret model's internal file ID.
A constitutional question retains CJ jurisdiction, not a new executive court.

**DR18 — Separate illegality from policy defeat.** A court may restrain an
unlawful effect, declare/annul an unauthorized rule, require lawful process or
reconsideration, restore an owed service/payment or order a proved refund/loss
under actual B/sector standards. It identifies version, scope, affected class,
stop time and lawful residual duties. It cannot write a new tax, benefit,
technical value or missing appropriation, or cast an Assembly vote. Affirmative
approval never cures an ultra vires punishment or unconstitutional burden.
Parliamentary annulment of a valid rule is ordinarily prospective; rights and
particular past illegality remain separately reviewable. No fine, detention
or adverse license act can rest on an unpublished/expired/invalid rule.

**DR19 — Positive payer and executor.** Each source Act assigns an accountable
career implementing officer and records-correction officer under C04; the rule
registrar records actual stops/versions/orders, and known material implementing
receivers obtain correction within24elapsed hours or earlier useful time.
Receiver acknowledgment must distinguish actual reconsideration/restoration
from a message received. The Treasury's protected career state-judgment payer
pays finally determined refunds/compensation within30working days of final
judgment or earlier applicable right; grounds, attribution, fault and causation
remain B/sector-specific, not insurance against every public policy loss.

The adopting Act expressly extends B19's bounded continuing appropriation and
B18 civil-enforcement service to executing specified already determined rule-
record/correction/notice and verified-payment acts here, with C04 preassigned
external conflict rotation, separate credentials and audited pay. No minister
may veto their own case. No substitute may select new eligibility, tax, policy,
foreign command, search or appropriation. Actual shortage remains an unpaid
judgment and elected C06 financing/receiving duty; a court order mints no cash,
FX or worker. Own-office wrongs have outside ordinary review, not self-clearance.
Willful obstruction needs independently proved duty/ability/fault and fair
process; an empty account is not automatic personal guilt.

**DR20 — Preserve people and history.** Invalidation is not automatic collapse
of all past judgments, contracts, wages, treatments, permits or third-party
transactions. Stop ongoing unlawful coercion promptly; competent forums give
particular relief with stronger rights, good-faith reliance, protected needs
and due process. A good-faith recipient does not automatically repay a state
benefit because the state used an invalid rule. No retroactive punishment for
prior conduct without valid enacted offense/fault. Original receipts, claims,
appeals, worker/creditor/source/foreign obligations and paid archival/custodial
succession retain dates. Keep safe reasons/corrections, not permanently public
sensitive testimony. Neither expiry nor a successful registry check is a
received remedy; all-controller failure must remain honestly visible.
