# LX17–LX23. Keep binding public interfaces open to independent implementation

*Design388 · Proposed ordinary law within [public rules and verifiable implementation](../12-public-rules-and-verifiable-implementation.md).*

**LX17 — Identify who makes an interface binding.** When an authority
requires a material shared specification for access to a public service,
evidence, payment, review or public procurement, identify its lawful
issuer, users, version and delegated scope. Apply the test to requirements
imposed in practice through purchasing, accreditation, a sole platform or
an incorporated external standard. Distinguish shared formats, operational
conventions and legally material choices. No standards body, contractor,
digital office or AI can create substantive eligibility, a new proof
burden or a public power. A material binding change uses the applicable
elected or delegated-law procedure and independent challenge.

**LX18 — Assess the actual dependence before choosing a common profile.**
Compare existing compatible routes, a shared minimal profile and a
more centralized solution on the same dates, rights and costs. Identify
downstream uses, languages and accessible media, shared funders and
controllers, licensing, certification, original records, lawful failure
and replacement. Hear affected residents, local service owners and
independent implementers, including people outside the default medium.
Publish the reasons, known limits and conflicts before final selection.
A coordination office may maintain a catalog and compare versions.
The lawful issuer keeps the choice and the duty to answer for it.

**LX19 — Buy an implementable public boundary.** Publish the exact
binding specification, normative meanings, permitted variations, versions,
tests and lawful-use conditions in accessible form. Use terms permitting
independent implementation and audit without discriminatory licensing
or an incumbent's case-by-case permission. Prefer a royalty-free
implementable core for new binding profiles. A narrow exception needs
lawful authority, reasons, actual costs, review, expiry and a funded
alternate. Protect personal records, genuine secrets and security
material through scoped access. Open specifications confer neither
unrestricted data access nor a right to privately owned code or model
weights. No certifier or technical seal becomes the only way to file
a claim or prove a lawful act.

**LX20 — Keep legal meaning and unfamiliar evidence through exchange.**
For material facts define source, authority, unit, operative period,
receipt and event dates, disputed and missing states, exact permitted
inferences, protected access and lawful corrections. Preserve extensions
and original evidence where necessary to avoid changing meaning.
Unsupported fields stay explicitly unresolved and go to a qualified
ordinary route. They do not become zero, consent, denial or a fresh
filing date. Local or future formats can supply usable equivalent
evidence without adopting the entire shared profile. No interface
creates a central identity, compulsory medium or universal life record.

**LX21 — Test independent operation, including a lost common controller.**
Use independently reasoned expected cases and verify original receipt,
legal status, prior payments, appeal and actual delivery. Before new
material reliance show two independently controllable routes or a
qualified staffed ordinary route for the protected scope. Include loss
of a common implementation, evaluator, runtime, host, permission or
certification where material. An alternate that passes the same tests
can still inherit the same mistake or authority dependence. Qualification
and disputes stay reviewable outside the vendor. Cost each route and
any work displaced at the next call. If a scope fails, hold the new
reliance and preserve existing duties through lawful alternatives.

**LX22 — Change versions through a paid, bounded transition.** Pin
effective versions and reject automatic legal uptake of upstream revisions.
Publish old and new compatibility, affected scope, open cases,
overlap, costs, exception dates and verified successors. Preserve usable
older evidence and a safe way to resolve any unsupported meaning.
Maintain access and original rights during cutover. Keep actual rollback
or an independently qualified alternative. A materially changed
interpretation, controller or dependency needs fresh qualification.
Emergency technical repair under existing authority cannot replace
legal adoption or excuse a missed remedy. An end date can end
an interface option, while lawful claims and usable evidence survive.

**LX23 — Protect challenge, succession and plural development.** Give
people direct human and accessible routes to report a material omission,
blocked entry or incorrect mapping. Use existing independent courts,
privacy and audit routes and actual sector execution. Publish safe
evidence of failed transfers, lost access, market dependence and missed
deadlines. Reuse evidence only within its qualified scope. People and
AI may propose mappings and compare counterexamples. They cannot confer
legal force, approve a dependent release or certify their own results.
Adopt prospectively with funds and a clear source of authority.
Reopen on captured certification, lost meaning, inaccessible licensing,
unfunded operation or a simpler equally protective arrangement.
