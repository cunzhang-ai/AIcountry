# EL13–EL16: a choice that can be expressed without a compulsory machine

*Part of [C03/13](../13-equal-franchise-and-usable-elections.md); design111 candidate.*

**EL13 — Full field, neutral access and actual people.** National and local ballot
materials show every qualified list's distinguishable name/identifier and whole
candidate pool/frozen order in an official accessible directory. Use a publicly
witnessed neutral order before V, identical stable IDs across formats; no purchased
position, incumbent-first list or personalized AI shortlist. Test order/rotation
rivals rather than claim a public draw removes position effects. The selected
paper route is a clearly paginated list-choice booklet and optional person-name/
ID field, with full chosen-list directory available in private at the booth and
by free advance request. A simple list vote never requires reading/ranking all
nominees or knowing an ID. Instructions repeat that only one list choice counts
across all pages. Layout/rows/font/audio navigation need actual user assessment,
not a fixed tiny font to fit an unexpectedly large field.

The actual nominee bound is min(2N×qualified lists, K), with K the independently
proved distinct P-eligible consenting candidate supply; one person cannot appear
on multiple lists. K is not E_call by assumption: birthdays, corrections/eligible
unregistered people and lawful diaspora can differ. Public guide/pool printing,
offline lookup/assistance and confidential booth time use the actual field/growth,
not200 lists, a fictional duplicate person or free online search. No full public
candidate dossier is sent to every voter merely to satisfy an artifact check.

**EL14 — Preserve the clear primary choice.** Count one clear list choice from
a mark/name/identifier in its primary field; clearly canceled marks do not count.
With no explicit list choice, an unambiguous valid nominee indication implies
that nominee's list as the primary choice; multiple indicated nominees all from
one list can imply that list but yield no personal preference. Multiple uncanceled
explicit list choices, or incompatible implicit lists, are an overvote without a
primary vote. Do not let a preference override an explicit different list.

A personal preference counts only when one unambiguous eligible nominee on the
chosen list is indicated, by unique ID or a name/source sufficiently clear under
pre-enacted intent rules. An absent, multiple, unknown, cross-list or withdrawn-
before-V preference does not invalidate a clear primary list. An empty ballot is
blank, not an incumbent vote. Original handwritten intent needs trained answerable
human adjudication/observation and courts; the illustrative parser only consumes
already-interpreted symbols and cannot prove handwriting/translation accuracy.
Show these consequences in practice materials before anyone casts a real vote.

**EL15 — Supported expression, no proxy mandate.** Provide independent secret
paper/accessible marking, large print, tactile/audio/communication aids, suitable
languages, paid chosen assistance, interpretation, travel/mobile options and
enough private time. No diagnosis, reading test or account-based ability score.
A person may choose their assistant; neither household head, caregiver, clinician,
party worker nor AI infers a political choice or substitutes their own. Support
may convey the person's actual meaning across media; verify it by an accessible
return channel when possible, not a compulsory speech/signature format. If no
specific preference can presently be expressed even with support, do not invent
a ballot; retain assistance rights and political standing, not presumed abstention.

Offer a paid independent alternative if the chosen person coerces or cannot help;
protect report/escape without confiscating essential assistive devices. Keep only
necessary assistance/custody records, not a political-belief/medical profile or
copy of marked choices. Private ballot receipts confirm lawful receipt/status,
not a purchasable proof of vote. Physical remote/coerced voting risks remain;
no method or model can certify free individual choice from a recorded click.

**EL16 — Channels and a discriminating test before readiness.** Select in-person
secret paper, properly staffed mobile/home/clinical/custody voting and absent paper
return/authorized safe foreign delivery, with protected original custody/return
and alternative accessible transport. Embassy/foreign-post channels require actual
lawful cooperation; domestic authority cannot promise foreign performance.
Remote internet return is not the reference. Future methods may displace it only
by express law after secret/individual/equal access, coercion, verification,
recount, failure, private life and mediator-power comparison with usable offline
alternatives. Assistance cannot become permanent delegation of the vote.

Use a prepared safe usability protocol at actual candidate-field sizes with
ordinary, low-literacy, disabled, older, minority-language, displaced and remote
voters; include nonusers and those unable to finish. Compare mixed/single/closed/
regional alternatives, list-only, implied/cross-list preference, correction and
receipt tasks. Record errors, help/time, exclusion and qualitative reasons, not
just aggregate speed or an AI score. No real participants/test results/budget
exist here. Arithmetic and a sample booklet are not actual comprehension.
Unusable ballots require lawful timely repair and paid capacity, never a quiet
entrant cap, unauthorized count change or election postponement by the office.
