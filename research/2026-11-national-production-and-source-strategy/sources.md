# Original accounting sources and interpretation limits

Accessed 3 November 2026. These sources explain accounting concepts; none
calibrates the circuit, guarantees a policy or endorses this country design.

[ONS, Blue Book 2020, Institutional sector accounts](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/grossdomesticproductgdp/compendium/unitedkingdomnationalaccountsthebluebook/2020/nationalaccountsataglance),
section 5, was opened/read. It explains the matched sector net-lending positions
in a fully balanced account. Only that conceptual scope is reused; its 2019
figures are not current UK evidence, AIcountry observations or transferable
tax/source parameters. No claim that measured national statistics always close
without discrepancies, boundary choices and revisions is made.

[ECB, Balance of payments and international investment position methods, 2025](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pubbydate/2025/html/ecb.bopiipbook202507.en.html),
general principles, was opened/read. It distinguishes current/capital transactions,
financial positions/transactions and valuation, including reserve assets. This
informs the title/flow distinction, not a full BPM-compliant statistical model
or authority for foreign intervention. The circuit omits foreign income/capital
transfers, investment, revaluation flows and discrepancies explicitly; actual
data would need them. Neither accounting closure nor a valuable asset is an
accepted, accessible due-date payment source.

The OBR flow-of-funds original page returned 403. Search excerpts were located
but are not treated as a read original or an independent evidence family. No
labor elasticity, trade forecast, inflation estimate, bond market capacity,
actual reserve or superiority claim is sourced from these pages.
