## Eight joined source/control/handover arguments

These use current selected roles, not newly appointed people or successful tests.
A failed fallback stays an actual delivery failure, rather than a fictional cure.

|---|---|
| I05/Y paying administration, G/Treasury and restricted S | Person receives actual net basic/earned/advance; keep existing proof and appeal. Protect work/old units when remittance fails. ## Country choice and finite closure

Retain the service-first candidate; reject a claim that unchanged sources alone
fund its target or that this partial comparison prices all37 functions/later
obligations. Strong retain/repair, express new-source service-first, U plus
services and different provider/ownership/time/security packages remain real
choices with losses as well as benefits. Capital, price/work response, I05
assessment breadth, private resources, ecology and foreign reliance may change
the preference. Published arithmetic cannot choose their values for a public.
