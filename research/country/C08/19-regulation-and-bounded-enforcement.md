# C08. Regulation with bounded power and effective correction

15 October 2026 · Design 74 · Proposed V01–V12, not enacted law

Select a common regulatory floor: prior defined duties, proportionate evidence
powers, fast but expiring protection, independent review, proved fault before
punishment and a funded successor when a necessary service stops. It applies
to public, private and cooperative providers and the state's own protectors.
A license is not political permission to live, organize or criticize.

The [M01–M16 financial statute](../C06/24-financial-law-and-loss-allocation.md)
supplies a specified monetary/bank application. Sector technical standards,
measured exposure limits and competence cannot be inferred from a generic
power to protect people. Omitted duties create no penalty by analogy.
B civil, P criminal, K/Q sanctions and U insolvency retain their own scopes.

[Cases and the financing conflict](../../settlement/regulatory-finance/README.md)
expose why a formally balanced national book can still be legally unusable.
New authority, staff and insurance liabilities have no verified actual funding.
This selection does not adopt new law, settle every technical sector standard
or demonstrate superior regulation. Reopen on chilling lawful activity,
selective enforcement, regulator capture, unavailable remedies or evidence
that a structural alternative protects people with less dependence.

[TS01–TS16/TD01–TD12](22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md) now
adds exact candidate technical entry/operation duties and the explicit VD10
predicate. V06 proof, maxima, correction windows and V08 same-course protections
remain; missing local annexes create no penalty or unproved authorization.

[NB01–NB36](../C06/44-nonbank-finance-and-client-protection.md) adds distinct nonbank technical/promise/property rules and the expressly closed prospective VD13 B-only predicate. No bank-rule analogy, new custody/monetary penalty band or automatic investor/insurer guarantee; missing source schedules create no offense.
