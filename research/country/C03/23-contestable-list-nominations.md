# C03. Contestable list nominations

*Design233 · NM01–NM12 · [C03 democratic government](../C03-democratic-government.md) · Proposed research law.*

An electoral list can narrow the public's choice before anyone casts a
public ballot. A group that puts candidates forward must offer a usable
route to propose, hear and select people under a known rule. Qualified
selectors decide the consenting pool and its fallback order. The state
protects the process, privacy and practical redress. The existing voter
preference, proportional seat count and independent candidacy remain.

| Part | Governing rule |
|---|---|
| [NM01–NM04. Who can choose and stand](23/01-association-and-selection.md) | Preserve voluntary association, fix selectors and give candidates a route past leadership gatekeeping. |
| [NM05–NM08. Timely receipt and enforcement](23/02-calendar-and-independent-relief.md) | Set common latest dates, separate evidence custody from political choice and deliver relief before the cutoff. |
| [NM09–NM12. Freeze, funds and commencement](23/03-freeze-funding-and-migration.md) | Recognize completed results, preserve valid separate choices and pay for independent operation and transition. |
