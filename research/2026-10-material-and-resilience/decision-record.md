## Selected arrangement and genuine competitors

| Question | Selected working design | Competing arrangement and unresolved reason |
|---|---|
| Production and work | Firms and cooperatives make ordinary investments and bear commercial risk; the Assembly separately funds a bounded public-capability portfolio and explicit public-enterprise service duties; independent worker organizations bargain and contest automation transitions. | A market-led residual state may discover and finance supply with less public administration but can leave essential capacity and worker adjustment absent. Directed public production and credit can coordinate long projects but concentrate jobs and finance in a political controller; an external-currency state cannot issue the missing settlement balances. Direct-public and cooperative predominance remain possible public choices. |
| Water, power and food | Democratic public stewardship and an enacted essential/ecological floor; public or licensed network operators with separate safety/reliability inspection and a funded successor; plural generation and food production with public food security, reserves and last-mile duties. | A unified public utility can clarify ownership but combine supply, employment and oversight under one incumbent. Regulated plural contracts can use existing capability yet leave natural bottlenecks and failed-provider gaps. Local commons can support resilience but cannot alone guarantee a national floor or shared catchment/grid safety. Ownership remains a lawful contextual choice. |
| Disaster and health hazards | Fund ordinary surveillance, maintenance and sector duties first; local civilian incident coordination and bounded national mutual aid; each restrictive measure needs its own enacted ground, support, review and end. Invoke D9 exceptional authority only for a named power unavailable in ordinary law. | Cabinet-wide emergency command may coordinate quickly but can replace elected distribution and opposition with executive discretion. Purely local response can miss spillovers and poorer places. Ordinary law alone is preferable where it truly suffices; ordinary labels must not conceal durable extraordinary restrictions. |

The combination requires an investment and physical-resource account shared with
C06. A public network, private factory, reserve warehouse, shelter and hospital
cannot each claim the same technician, backup generator, site, water flow, imported
part or external-currency payment. Public loans and guarantees are not bank
resolution funds. Tariff relief and income assistance need cash while renewal
requires capital and workers; apparently cheap service funded by unmaintained
assets is deferred deprivation. Technical evidence bounds feasible options but
does not allocate distributive power. Existing local and national guarantees
remain during shortage; elected institutions must expose and decide lawful
tradeoffs within rights and ecological duties.

An authored compound case checks the chain: a grid failure stops water treatment
and refrigerated food while a heat/flood event and infection increase clinical
demand and imported repair parts are delayed. Operators perform preauthorized
safety actions and report losses; the water, power, food and health authorities
arrange actual interim supply and mutual aid; a local civilian lead coordinates
rescue and shelter. Clinicians make individual clinical judgments. Police enforce
only lawful orders. Treasury reports usable funds and due claims; the Assembly
decides new finance or material priority. A cabinet cannot use the disaster to
create new tax, favored credit or permanent surveillance. A court can protect
rights and expose non-delivery but cannot create parts, water or workers. This
is a logic test, not a measured response or readiness result.

## Objections, migration and status

Material objections remain. The public-capability portfolio may become patronage
or crowd out useful investment; worker consultation may be merely formal or
unavailable to precarious workers. Public stewards and inspectors may share
appointing and funding controllers; a licensed monopoly can capture both tariff
and supply. A protected essential quantity may be underfunded or induce hidden
deferred maintenance. Ordinary health laws can normalize broad restrictions,
while a complex emergency ladder can delay urgent action. Each objection needs
separate evidence and affected-party judgment; more detailed prose cannot settle
the value tradeoffs. Alternative ownership and response models remain open.

A further same-controller AI integration check identified one repairable
constitutional loophole in the initial C10 wording: if extraordinary review
depended only on whether an ordinary statute already authorized a restriction,
an incumbent could pre-enact and repeatedly rename broad health orders to avoid
D9's cumulative limits. The revised working design classifies by *actual effect*.
Narrow, rights-consistent hazard measures remain possible under ordinary law and
independent review. Long-running broad but non-derogating restrictions need
continuing elected authorization; a measure or series that effectively suspends
protected democratic participation or seeks constitutional derogation triggers
the protected emergency limits if permissible at all. Exact thresholds and
legal language remain for legitimate adoption. This same-controller check is
not independent public assurance.

The supporting studies cite ILO, OECD, IMF, WHO, IEA, FAO, UNDRR and UN human-rights
materials as primary institutional comparison sources. They give relevant
questions and general constraints, not observed AIcountry effects, cost numbers
or endorsement of this combination. Some evidence families and sponsoring
institutions overlap. New events and material objections require fresh review
even if these documents are unchanged.

Design 9 affects the proposed authority package, social/fiscal settlement,
spatial and external-commitment choices, health and emergency candidates, old
labor/utility/food acts, competition/procurement, bank and public-enterprise law.
No predecessor review transfers automatically. Actual migration would identify
ownership and title, network condition, workers and collective agreements,
creditors and pension claims, tariff debts, licenses, stocked goods and expiry,
supplier and import contracts, public guarantees, ecological liabilities,
patients and pending remedies. Receiving operators, funded overlap, lawful
compensation, safe data transfer and independent review must precede a claim that
the new provider or crisis route is available. Publishing design 9 changes no
source-edition permissions, statute or existing private right.

**Evidence qualification:** document comparison, primary institutional guidance
and authored stress logic only. **Research selection:** the three conditional
arrangements enter the working design. **Legal authorization:** none.
**Operational readiness:** unknown for operators, repair crews, stock locations,
foreign settlement, clinical surge, shelters, inspectors and cash. **Observed
outcomes:** none. Same-controller AI review cannot stand in for workers,
residents, firms, utilities or democratic authority.

The next bounded real-world decision is whether an affected public in a lawful
jurisdiction wants to compare this package with more public, market-led or
community operation and a different emergency allocation. The first useful
deliverable would be an original asset/worker/cash/ecology map for one essential
area and a witnessed joint power-water-food-health interruption with affected
users and workers, including who actually receives remedies. If no jurisdiction
or mandate exists, keep the design qualified and accept concrete contradictions
or rivals; do not invent a national reserve target or declare the country ready.
Reopen the selections if real costs make the fallback impossible, if public or
private control blocks democratic opposition, if scarcity falls unequally on
weak groups, or if ordinary-law response causes avoidable harm or permanent
coercion compared with a lawful alternative.
