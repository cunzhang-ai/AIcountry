# C13. Serious illness, death and a survivor gap

*Part of [C13 lived experience](../C13-lived-experience.md); integrated design 55 authored stress, not an observed event.*

A person with advanced illness lives far from the regional hospital. They
want relief at home and have a recorded refusal of one invasive treatment.
Their adult child disputes it; another trusted supporter fears that the
hospital portal has not shown the latest change. The only local pharmacy
has no prescribed pain medicine, and the paid home-care worker is absent.
The person also supports a dependent child. After death during a network
outage, the cause is uncertain, a funeral provider demands payment before
releasing records, and the surviving household cannot access income.

C07's clinical network must send an accepting team, obtain medicine or an
equivalent safe route, and arrange actual paid support rather than count
an unread referral. The clinician hears the person's current supported
choice first, checks the advance refusal's scope and version, and offers
urgent independent review of a material dispute. A financial shortfall
cannot be relabeled clinical futility. If death occurs, a qualified
professional verifies it; an independent investigation preserves evidence
if there is a real neglect or violence concern. C08's registrar records
the fact and offers a correction route; safe body care and a culturally
usable option proceed without forcing the family to buy a package.

The C07 protection office gives the dependent child an accepting care,
housing and interim-income route while the civil process determines the
survivor and estate claims. C06 funds the same vehicle, pharmacist, home
worker, certifier, registrar, investigator, court and body-care site once;
it records each undelivered service as such. An AI copy of a stale document
cannot override the person or settle a disputed death.

This exercise fails if symptoms remain untreated, a family or cost office
silences the person, a missing prescription is counted as medicine received,
a death is declared from an app, an investigation becomes a pretext to
withhold a certificate indefinitely, or the child loses food during an
estate dispute. No actual patient, dependent, law, provider, records or
outcome has been observed.
