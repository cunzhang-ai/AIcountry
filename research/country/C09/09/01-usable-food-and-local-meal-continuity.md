# Usable food through interrupted markets and utilities

*Design293 · FM01–FM05 · Proposed food-access and procurement rules.*

**FM01 — Plan the food floor by how people can actually use it.**
The existing local food-access authority under C07, supported by
the C09 food authority, sets a funded receiving plan for the
declared shortage window. Compare actual market offers with
ordinary purchasing power, accepted ingredients, local preparation
and distributed ready-to-eat food. Identify the people missed by
each route, including those without safe water, cooking, transport
or suitable products. Use task-relevant clinical and nutrition
judgment and reasonable dietary and cultural choices. Preserve
direct offline access and appeals. A person need not accept a
single official diet, share an unrelated diagnosis or join a
registry to report urgent need.

**FM02 — Buy a complete meal route and a paid next delivery.**
Budget the actual lots and usable life, safe water and power,
staff, preparation and hygiene, cold storage, packaging, delivery
and accepted receiving. Compare ordinary suppliers, compensated
local kitchens and distributed shelf-stable alternatives through
the same utility and route failures. Count scarce water, workers,
vehicles and fuel once, after already due uses. A school or care
kitchen can accept extra work within lawful capacity while its
ordinary meal duty remains covered. Keep owner, lawful release,
expiry, first delivery and actual replenishment separate. An
unpaid household, volunteer or supplier promise supplies no
assured shift or transport.

**FM03 — Switch on actual access failure within existing authority.**
If safe, affordable market access remains usable, continue the
ordinary personal payment and retail route. When a timely offer
fails, the authorized local officer releases the paid suitable
alternative under its own terms and records the affected people,
deadline and decision. Competent food-safety officials retain
testing, holds and recall. Trace affected batches to receivers,
preserve earlier notice and activate an actually accepted
substitute. A food receipt, voucher or in-kind provision cannot
silently cancel an independent cash right. Existing matched
credit rules require their lawful basis and actual same-component
receipt. Available safe parts may be delivered while a remaining
need is still open.

**FM04 — Correct who actually misses out.** Distinguish stock,
prepared meals, dispatch, accepted arrival and useful receipt
by place and relevant needs. Use independent accessible contact
to identify missed routes, with minimum necessary information.
Protect the person who cannot use the nominal alternative or
declines it. The local authority provides an accessible reason
and funded correction with useful-time review through existing
channels. Apply existing lawful priority rules if scarcity
remains. Preserve the shortfall and original claim. No shortage
or model inference authorizes coercive rationing, unsafe food,
unlawful restriction of benefits or diversion of protected water.
AI may check route feasibility and suspected duplication under
human responsibility. It cannot decide individual entitlement
or food safety.

**FM05 — Fund recovery and the transition back to ordinary access.**
Check both initial provision and the next call before counting
the whole period as protected. Restore normal delivery after the
accepting shops, kitchens, care providers and households can
actually use it. Preserve standing duties, earlier missed dates,
valid procurement, wages and evidence through a successor or
expiry. Disclose capacity and losses to the elected source owners.
Price usable rotation, verification, independent review and any
replacement of expired stocks. Adopt prospectively under OP/T
and retain existing authorities. Reopen if the plan shifts work
to unpaid households, excludes a dietary group, exhausts a
protected source or fails a second useful delivery.
