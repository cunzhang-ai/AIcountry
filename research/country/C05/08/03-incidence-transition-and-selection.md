# Incidence, transition and the current selection

*Part of [C05/08](../08-income-ownership-and-country-choice.md); design 69.*

Compare normal conditional accounts and distribution **at the same baseline
production** separately. A different required-output equilibrium changes
profits and dividends as well as taxes; it can conceal who bears a reform.
All three cases retain the same current public bids, workers, insurance
history, replacement and external contracts. Full unpriced duties remain
unknown. Spending all modeled household cash at fixed prices is an authored
closure assumption, not a social objective or a forecast of savings.

| Same-production result | A: I05 | B: universal base/tax change | C: I05/mutual purchase |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| People gaining monthly cash | 0 | 58,000 | 30,000 |
| People losing monthly cash | 0 | 26,000 | 5,000 |
| People unchanged | 100,000 | 16,000 | 65,000 |
| One-year no-cash-loss sensitivity, million | 0 | 423.579 | 192.333 |

These are authored cohort counts, not voters, consultation or estimates of
social benefit. A retired person gains 266.67 monthly under B at common
output, while a middle-income support-paying parent loses 368.75 and that
parent's child gains 566.67. Gains do not automatically discharge another
person's due obligation. C's equal capital cash is partly offset by reduced
I05 top-ups; it changes future wealth/control as well as first-year cash.

The no-cash-loss amount is an **unfinanced sensitivity**, not a promised
bridge, a legal right to unchanged investment returns or compensation due
to every loser. Lawful, publicly justified redistribution may impose losses.
Distinguish accrued/legal claims from expected future returns, then state
which transitions are protected, who pays, on what dates and with which
accepted receiving/remedy path. Never quietly add a bridge while reporting
the unbridged normal account as financed.

**Design69 selection, now superseded on destination by [IC](../09-income-capital-and-democratic-investment.md):** retain I05 as the conditional working candidate;
hold B and C as substantive rivals and qualify A's previous normal closure.
B demonstrates a different privacy/universality bargain, but its tax and
incidence tradeoff has no public selection or behavior/collection support.
C exposes acquisition and control costs rather than providing free income.
A's lower gross transfer bill does not prove better access or lives.
Neither losers nor an unverified rival create an automatic status-quo veto.

Compatibility requires coordinated income/tax, pension/basic, support,
ownership, credit, insolvency, data and payment changes, with existing due
claims preserved until lawful revision. Current software and constitutional
boundaries remain. No dated prior approval transfers to these changed models.

Reopen on original workload/price/output and tax-response evidence, affected
distributional choice, credible purchase terms or a stronger rival. The
current next priority is the jointly sourced IC country allocation, preserving
ordinary-law choices and actual unknowns. Earlier schedules are now drafted.
No operating package, real authority, readiness or superiority is established.
