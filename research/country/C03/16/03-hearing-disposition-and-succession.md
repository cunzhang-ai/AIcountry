# NI09–NI12. A hearing must reach an actual public decision

*Part of [national initiatives](../16-public-national-initiatives.md).*

**NI09 — Protected consideration without an MP gate.** Clerk lists qualified
initiative within seven calendar days ofQ through a prepublished competent
committee/plenary assignment route. Speaker, committee chair and Cabinet cannot
withhold it for lack of a member sponsor. An accessible hearing occurs no later
thanQ+60calendar days, with normally14days usable notice, paid participation/
interpretation/support and safe input from organisers, materially affected people,
relevant opponents and qualified source witnesses. Free public legal/cost help
serves the question, including material uncertainty and rival proposals, without
requiring a fiscal certificate or promoter ideology. A hearing is not a vote.

Actual ordinary statutory sector/affected/rights consultation remains required;
FA's30-slot portfolio does not consume or expire this protected initiative.
Compatible hearings may share paid work only while distinct material claims,
arguments and public decisions stay visible. Independent PM/editorial work is
not compelled airtime. Practical language/non-digital access and public source
inspection cannot rely on a preferred AI summary or ranked popularity feed.

**NI10 — A recorded Assembly disposition byQ+180.** Require an actual protected
plenary vote on a reasoned disposition within180calendar days ofQ. It may enact
an exact ready measure only after its actual ordinary legal stages/vote/source
requirements; adopt an exact lawful nonlegislative policy/oversight resolution;
reject/retain the baseline with public reasons; or once adopt a
specific FUNDED drafting mandate under NI11. Record exact motion, member votes,
material minority reasons and which original requests are answered/refused.
A ministerial letter, committee shelving, generic further study or unrelated
measure is not a disposition. No turnout, quorum or favorable votes are invented.

The protected hearing and usable material affected input must precede every
NI10 disposition, including rejection/retention or its funded drafting mandate, with actual opportunity for
members to consider them. A hearing after the vote does not cure that breach.
Ordinary preparatory work may begin earlier without discharging the hearing.
A positive nonlegislative resolution states the actual competent actor, lawful
existing power/source, requested act, dates and follow-through; it supplies no
missing executive, local or spending authority. Record any required further
actor's decision as pending, not performed implementation. A concrete public
position is distinct from promised service delivery, and a referral alone is no
settled disposition. DB/E/NP continue actual implementation/remedy duties.

A proposal to change tax, spending or an entitlement discloses actual authority,
incidence, full joint source/qualified capacity, legacy and transition. NI gives
no appropriation or implementation permission. Assembly can reject a proposal
without pretending its cost is zero or constitutional objections resolved.
Neither threshold nor hearing supplies a referendum, direct popular enactment
or a vote about an individual person's case. Pending NI does not stay annual
budgets, elections or other existing useful-time duties; actual confidence continues.

**NI11 — One bounded drafting route, no permanent referral.** If exact legislation
is not ready, the Assembly may byQ+180 give one mandate naming the actual Clerk/
competent drafting service, paid resource/affected review, tasks and exact deadline
no later thanQ+360 for final ordinary legislative enactment or a recorded plenary
rejection/retention of the requested change. Cabinet may assist but cannot own
the only draft. No repeated mandate, renamed study or committee transfer resets
this outer duty. Actual mandatory fair consultation and legal stages remain;
if unfinished by the outer date, record failure and preserve public correction,
not automatic passage, retroactive legality or a fictional completed rejection.

Show material differences from the supported question and reasons. Organisers
and affected dissenters can challenge misdescription but cannot veto Parliament's
own lawful amendment. An amended bill is the Assembly's responsibility and never
inherits signatory approval, a plebiscite mandate or old final review. Optional
future work after a final rejection uses its own ordinary elected authority,
not an assertion that this initiative was enacted. Actual adopted implementation
and actual received benefits remain separately accountable through DB/E/NP.

**NI12 — Succession and omission do not erase the public question.** Qualified
NI consideration/disposition duties survive dissolution, election and Assembly
replacement. The original clock and supported question remain, while unfinished
bills keep their actual lapse/renewal law and require the successor Assembly's
fresh lawful decision. Old support establishes agenda access only, not a mandate
for an old bill or old members. No former Speaker/cabinet stays in office to
finish an initiative. A failed lawful sitting/notice/quorum is missed duty,
not deemed action, poll postponement or a new self-convened legislature.

Existing Clerk/qualified deputies carry their actual listing/record duties;
prepublished lawful parliamentary presiding/quorum rules apply. Ordinary courts
can enforce receipt, count, required access, lawful listing/hearing/process and
truthful records within competence; they cannot cast votes, deem an initiative
rejected or adopted, tax, or appoint a preferred government. If all channels fail,
report actual unmet duty and retain source/rights/peaceful replacement remedies.
An overdue proposal is not silently expired by a docket quota or calendar reset.

The separately authorized [public confirmation option](06-public-confirmation-of-service-withdrawal.md)
is a vote on a defined enacted withdrawal. NI09–NI12 remain agenda and disposition duties.
