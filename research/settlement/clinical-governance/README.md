# A public clinical service with outside safety and coverage review

Design164 · [HG01–HG20](../../country/C07/05-health-service-and-patient-control.md)

**Select integrated public clinical operation, internal purchasing/access and
review outside the provider/purchaser command.** The National Clinical Service
(NCS) is the state's public clinical employer/service, not a new commercial
insurer or political region. Qualified additional providers remain; no compulsory
internal market or extra governing board is selected. This makes existing
S/HN public responsibility concrete without claiming that those rights were absent.
