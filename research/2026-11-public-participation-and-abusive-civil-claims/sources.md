# Primary records for public participation and early civil review

- [Canada Supreme Court Reports, 2020 volume 2, Pointes Protection and Bent v. Platnick](https://decisions.scc-csc.ca/scc-csc/scr/en/7719/1/document.do)
  contains the reasons for 2020 SCC 22 at page 587 and 2020 SCC 23
  at page 645. The first preserves dismissal of the contract claim.
  The second preserves a defamation action's ability to proceed at
  that stage, with dissent. The Court's [Pointes case summary](https://www.scc-csc.ca/judgments-jugements/cb/2020/38376/)
  and [Bent case summary](https://www.scc-csc.ca/judgments-jugements/cb/2020/38374/)
  document the disposition and context. The underlying law is Ontario's,
  and the reported outcomes do not set AIcountry thresholds.
- [Ontario Protection of Public Participation Act, 2015](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/s15023)
  supplies the statutory background for these decisions. The worked
  comparison uses the Supreme Court's actual interpretation and
  separates disposition from a final finding of fault.
- [Directive (EU) 2024/1069](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32024L1069),
  particularly Articles 1–4 and 6–15, supplies a distinct cross-border
  comparator for civil safeguards, early dismissal, review and costs.
  The proposed domestic scope, clocks and funding here are independent
  choices. The directive supplies no estimate of the worked court load.

The case pair illustrates two procedural outcomes. Neither source
establishes actual staffing, frequency or observed gains under the
proposed route. The hearing and all workload units are constructed.
