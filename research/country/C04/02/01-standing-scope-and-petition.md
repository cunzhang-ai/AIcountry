# TS01–TS04. Standing, scope and a usable trigger

*Design 143 · [Territorial-status reference](../02-territorial-self-government.md) · Proposed charter/framework division, not current law.*

**TS01 — Distinct decisions and accessible initiation.** Ordinary service districts,
municipal competence and boundaries retain their actual statutory route, rights
review and funded successors. Creation or loss of constitutionally protected
self-government, or a change of national sovereignty, uses this charter route
and A9. It does not condition nonterritorial rights or existing collective remedies.
Any affected person, association, elected council or accountable rights-holder
representative may seek supported scoping. A complete legal dossier, platform
account or government recognition of the movement is unnecessary. Receivers
preserve the first request's date and meaning, offer help within five working
days, accept accessible human and future communication methods, and separate
expression, understanding, representation and legal consent. Agent copies do
not multiply a mandate. Confidential testimony is not automatically published.

**TS02 — Reviewable scope before support is judged.** The applicant proposes an aim
and map; the reference unit is one existing municipality or a contiguous union
of whole municipalities inside actual jurisdiction. Historic, enclave or collective-
rights departures need publicly enacted objective criteria, evidence and review;
no official may optimize a political map after seeing expected support. Preserve
countermaps and overlapping claims without declaring an exclusive identity or title.
Certification fixes geographic extent: later municipal renaming/merger does not
silently change it or transfer support; material extent change needs a fresh claim.
The proposed express EA scope jurisdiction certifies admissibility, not political
merit or sovereign entitlement, within **90 calendar days from first request**.
One reasoned extension of at most 90 calendar days must be notified before expiry,
with actual necessary work stated. Provide at least 30 calendar days for accessible
countermaps and objections within that period. This special scoping clock does not
restart urgent, electoral or court clocks. Material aim/map change needs a newly
supported claim; correction does not silently transfer reviews to changed meaning.

**TS03 — Area petition, distinct from national A9.** After scope certification S,
allow a 12-calendar-month collection period, protected as at least 12 months in
the charter. D must fall within that collection period; later correction may
prove support already valid at D, not add new post-D signers. Qualification requires **ceil(N_D / 20)** unique valid supporters,
where N_D is the positive affected-resident electorate as of **D, first presentation
seeking qualification**. A clerk's completeness or certification date cannot move D.
Correct genuine roll errors as of D, with independent challenge and safe audit.
N_D absent or genuinely zero establishes no mandate. Signers must qualify when
signing and on D, with no effective withdrawal before D; later changed views remain
visible but do not retrospectively erase a valid negotiation trigger. Agents need
real, bounded principal mandates; one principal counts once. Material uncertainty
is unresolved, not a fabricated zero or verified signature. This 5% is not A9's
national 5% petition: RR06 retains its national initiative-opening denominator,
signer dates and exact-text rule. No signature transfers automatically between them.

**TS04 — Resident standing and independent processing.** Area standing uses existing
local political eligibility: age18, six cumulative months of national habitual
residence and one genuine electoral home in the proposed area, including existing
forced-displacement protection. Moving municipality does not restart national
residence. Ownership, an employer, a platform account or group membership supplies
no extra vote. A citizen permanently abroad without a domestic electoral home has
national standing, not an invented area ballot. Petition qualification uses D;
final area-ballot eligibility uses the actual voting day under TS11, not ordinary
EL anticipatory P. Existing applicable stronger rights remain protected.

Qualification from D uses AP/B16's five-working-day help and 30-working-day decision,
one notified extension of at most 30 working days and full 20-working-day adverse
reply; a timely reply does not authorize automatic rejection at the outer limit.
Direct independent omission/error review remains. Express legislation gives the
existing EA this limited function with its seven-member, five-present/four-actual-
assent rule and ordinary judicial merits review; vacancy, recusal or delay does not
create new appointers, lower quorum or map approval by silence. Courts may correct
legal/record errors and compel actually owed processing, not decree independence.
