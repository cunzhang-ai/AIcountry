# Pay for electricity that reaches a service in time

*Design194 · Evidence and worked comparisons for [OC01–OC08](../../country/C09/20-essential-service-outage-continuity.md).*

## A named wastewater facility

McKinleyville Community Services District's March 2026 board packet
describes its wastewater microgrid. The staff memo identifies 500 kW of
solar, a 250 kW storage power rating, an existing 500 kW diesel
generator and a local control panel.
It reports solar operation in December 2022 and the battery intertie in
July 2024. Staff described continuing storage and control-system issues.
The 5 million USD grant-and-loan package was split equally and covered
several projects, including the 3.8 million USD microgrid. It is not a
5 million USD microgrid purchase or a grant alone. The EPA guide
describes 712 kWh of storage in its earlier case summary. That reported
energy rating is not the later tested usable energy, charged reserve
or independently demonstrated duration. The storage and generator are
not evidence that every upstream lift station, home pump or communication
path was powered in the same outage.
[District packet, item D.10](https://www.mckinleyvillecsd.ca.gov/files/a055e17e7/An%2BAgenda%2BMarch%2B4%2C%2B2026%2BPACKET.pdf)
· [EPA case summary](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2024-06/240524-powerresilience-23012t4.pdf)

For December 2024 through November 2025 the contractor attachment reports
674,039 kWh of solar output and 43,231 kWh delivered from storage, chiefly
at peak billing periods. It derives a **61,420 USD** savings figure from
253,144 USD modeled baseline utility cost, less 107,112 USD in utility
bills and a calculated 84,612 USD loan payment. At the same bills and
loan line, the comparison reaches zero at a modeled baseline of 191,724
USD. That sensitivity is about the published comparison, not a financing
plan, household bill effect or verified full lifecycle saving. The
comparison omits a complete same-scope outage and operating-cost account.

The attachment also states that the first annual loan payment was due in
December 2024, but includes a loan-payment line in its Year 2 ending in
November 2024. Keep that timing unreconciled, along with dollar-scale
differences between rounded displayed rows and totals. Do not reuse the
published three-year total as audited dated cash. The Board tabled the
March agenda item for April discussion. Neither inclusion in the packet
nor contractor reporting establishes independent validation of site
resilience. [Attachment and table scope](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-essential-service-outage-continuity/sources.md)
· [Board minutes](https://www.mckinleyvillecsd.ca.gov/files/788dd6cad/An%2BAgenda%2BApril%2B1%2C%2B2026%2BPACKET.pdf)

Storm Arwen offers a different observation. Ofgem reports nearly 4,000
customers without power for more than a week. Its research with customers
affected for at least 48 hours included households that lost water because
their pumps needed electricity. Re-energizing the network is an important
milestone. Actual water, warmth, contact and safe receiving need their
own follow-up. This British event and the California asset are distinct
evidence, not parts of one installed system.
[Ofgem final review, sections 2.4–2.5](https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2022-06/Final%20report%20on%20the%20review%20into%20the%20networks%27%20response%20to%20Storm%20Arwen.pdf)

## A four-hour joint acceptance test

This is an authored case. It does not reconstruct the district plant.
Assume no generation or recharge for four hours, a safely connected island,
150 kW usable output and 500 kWh available on the delivery side. Assume
startup, transfer, quality and staffed controls work for these tasks.
The water task begins in hour 1. All three can run together during its window.

| Task | Useful window | Power | Energy |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Wastewater treatment process | Hours 0–4 | 100 kW | 400 kWh |
| Drinking-water pump | Hours 1–3 | 20 kW | 40 kWh |
| Public contact and receipt of urgent claims | Hours 0–4 | 5 kW | 20 kWh |
| **Joint requirement** | Four hours | **125 kW maximum** | **460 kWh** |

The scalar power and energy checks both pass at the declared scope. The
protected energy leaves up to 40 kWh of discretionary margin in this
state. If routine peak shaving uses 100 kWh immediately before the event,
400 kWh remains and the combined service promise is short by 60 kWh.
The example diagnoses infeasibility. It does not decide which service
must lose power. Apply actual lawful safety, sector, PS or CP rules if
the shortage cannot be avoided. If the only controller fails, no
deliverable backup is qualified until independent operation is
established. A nameplate calculation cannot substitute for that test.

## What government buys

| Arrangement | Benefit | Scope that must still work |
|---|---|---|
| Repair the existing grid and retain mobile aid | Lower fixed duplication, useful where travel and switching beat the service deadline | Simultaneous calls, blocked route, fuel, hookup and actual qualified shift |
| Protect a fixed reserve and contract for independently accepted service, selected where necessary | Keeps short-deadline loads usable through loss of the ordinary feeder. Leaves verified excess capacity available for normal operations. | Usable charge and control, safe transfer, paid upkeep, common failure and later replenishment |
| Provide the same protection through another physical service route | Stored water, safe temporary provision, an alternate staffed site or offline receiving may cost less than duplicating electrical equipment | Quality, consent, travel and accessibility, actual capacity, clear legal receipt and handback |

Compare these alternatives by the same service, not by equipment purchased.
The selected rule buys local backup only where restoration or another
safe path cannot be relied on by the service deadline. One incident
can exhaust multiple receiving routes. The designated officers record
the first unmet duty and remedy where a contract, dispatch or device
cannot deliver. Actual physical design, costs, legal authority, public
input and measured outcomes remain open.
