# Trigger, neutral purpose and prior checks

*Part of [PX open contests](../22-open-contests-and-neutral-enforcement.md).*

**PX01 — Protect the political function on equal terms.** Implement
the proposed A10 protection against using public enforcement to
favor or suppress political participation. Apply ordinary legality
and neutrality throughout the year. The expedited route applies
when a specific public act or refusal poses a reasonably foreseeable,
substantial loss to an identifiable lawful election, campaign,
referendum or elected deliberative opportunity before ordinary
review can work. It protects affected people regardless of party,
office or whether a timely attempt to qualify has been finally
decided. It includes residents, lawful organizers, campaign workers
and necessary providers with a direct affected interest. The route
does not cover every incidental burden or abstract disagreement.
It gives no additional eligibility and no officeholder privilege.

**PX02 — Give the same law, reasons and necessary timing.** A public
actor must identify its competent authority, original facts, lawful
objective, exact scope and need for the timing and duration of a
material restriction. Address feasible less restrictive means and
their actual safety and evidence consequences. Check prior practice,
similarly situated cases, related actions, specific material
instructions and shifting explanations. Protect sensitive comparators
and witnesses. Lawful criticism or participation cannot by itself
supply grounds for a violation. An affected person can present
specific facts supporting a credible inference of improper use
without proving private motive first. The authority must then
produce the relevant original record and justify the act under
ordinary law and this rule. Timing alone proves no misconduct.
A lawful underlying aim does not justify unrelated political harm.

**PX03 — Require independent authorization before planned material
restrictions.** Before an intended nonurgent coercive measure
that the authority knows or reasonably should know will cause the loss in PX01,
seek a competent ordinary court's prior decision on the material
restriction. This applies to the effect, including linked arrests,
seizures, asset restraints and administrative acts within public
control. Related actions are evaluated together where their effects
combine. The court authorizes only a measure supported by its own
source law and a demonstrated need for the scope and timing that no
adequate less restrictive measure can meet. Set reasons and expiry.
Ordinary lawful inquiry, evidence preservation and nonrestrictive
steps continue under their own rules. Use narrow protected or
without-notice review only where law permits, with reasons,
shortest necessary scope and prompt effective challenge. A claimed
emergency does not waive this check. Immediate protection already
lawfully allowed under P, LF, HP or other applicable law retains
its own grounds and shorter clocks. If prior judicial authorization
is required and absent, the planned restrictive step must wait.
