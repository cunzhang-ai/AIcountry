# RC16–RC20. A rescue promise has a physical and democratic source

*Part of [civil rescue](../13-civil-rescue-and-incident-command.md).*

**RC16 — Account for the whole source once.** Budget actual qualified intake,
clinical receiving, rescue/relief/standby/training/rest, management, outside inquiry,
court/record execution, interpretation/privacy, human/AI verification and non-AI
fallback. Include stations/sites, safe water/power/fuel, protected clothing,
maintained equipment/vehicles, access, medicines where lawfully needed, usable
communications and spare stocks; trace owned FX/imports, donor release/return,
worker/pension/insurance/contract/property/creditor tails and paid overlap. Count
compatible shared support once; never count one clinician/driver/responder twice
in simultaneous plans or a free volunteer/family/foreign/model reserve. Existing R01 rescue/preparedness and
all37 function duties/source limits continue; add no invented rescue tax, debt,
ring-fenced pension sweep or adequate unit price. Budget authority is not actual
due cash/qualified supply, and shortage is not zero need or permission to coerce.

**RC17 — Failed delivery remains a public problem.** Intake/Director seeks
actual accepted lawful receiving, narrower safe rescue/support and qualified aid
within useful time, documenting failed arrival and unmet harm rather than closing
a ticket as success. C04 national limited fallback retains actual source and
review; missing local funds do not cancel the guarantee or compel neighboring
work. Existing food/shelter/medicine/dependant support, HP/CP displacement/return,
recovery claims and safe records persist after the scene ends. Separate unlawful
refusal, genuine capacity absence, lawful disagreement and negligent harm through
actual evidence/ordinary remedies; no automatic indemnity for every loss, immunity
for honest titles or readiness claim from a published plan. Protect available
help even when independent inquiry/source/communications also fail; none creates
new power or extends an expired allocation. Expose that remaining failure.

**RC19 — Keep genuinely different institutions available.** Retain/repair
existing fire/dispatch and local mutual aid could cure appointment ambiguity with
less disruption. Strong national unified rescue may pool rare capabilities and
reduce locality inequality, while concentrating source/command and weakening
local influence. A decentralized community/common service may preserve trust,
knowledge and autonomy, while needing reliable due standby and safe delegation.
Plural contracted providers may add capability, while fragmenting readiness,
records and failure debt. The selected local professional public backbone plus
accepted aid prefers visible universal duty and replaceable local control; it
is a hypothesis, not a proven optimum or a requirement that all services merge.
Substantive disagreement about locality, solidarity and central command cannot
be averaged into a response-time score. A real setting may justify displacement
of this model rather than another compensating institution.

**RC20 — Reopen on the missing evidence, not a document count.** The dated
sources and written adverse paths examine actor/power/source coherence, not
original local casualty/dispatch data, actual participation, independent field
receiving or national affordability. Seek affected residents/responders/clinicians/
volunteers/localities and independent evidence without public sensitive testimony
by default. Reopen employer/scale/dispatch/command selection on actual geography,
coverage inequality, avoidable delay, cumulative capture, unsafe method/AI,
volunteer displacement or a better complete paid rival. Next inspect the integrated
country's ordinary/adverse/paid operation for the largest substantive gap; do not
automatically add a rescue agency, specialist protocol or scenario series. Full
source, actual authority/readiness and better lives remain independently unknown.
