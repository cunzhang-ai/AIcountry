# C13. Time-sensitive care and a disputed child's status

*Part of [C13 lived experience](../C13-lived-experience.md); integrated design 55 authored stress, not an observed event.*

A person in a remote district needs time-sensitive pregnancy care. A local
clinician refuses a lawful service, the next facility is far away, and an
abusive partner can see the household's shared health account. The same
ambulance is needed for a complicated birth. A younger patient seeks
confidential advice but fears automatic disclosure to adults who may
harm them. No office may call a referral or app booking completed care.

C07's health purchaser must find an accepting, funded clinical route
with transport and privacy, while the clinician retains a fair personal
process and emergency care cannot be withheld. C08 protects the
patient's informed choice and an adolescent's supported voice and
safety under law. A safe complaint and urgent independent review must
arrive before delay makes the decision irreversible. C06 shows the
same ambulance, professional and facility once and exposes unmet claims
if physical capacity is insufficient; an AI forecast grants no coercion.

In a second branch, a child is born after an assisted arrangement whose
adults dispute parentage and a clinic's record is incomplete. The C08
registrar records the child's existence and issues usable provisional
proof. The relevant C07 authority provides essential access under its own
law. A competent civil process hears the child and affected adults,
preserves original consent and medical records, and assigns continuing
care duties under valid law. A contract, genetic test or registry field
does not privately decide the child's entire future. The C07 network
still owes newborn and postpartum care during the dispute.

This exercise fails if a lawful service is unreachable, privacy lets an
abuser control care, an emergency is used to override consent without
its specific legal ground, or an identity dispute makes a child invisible.
It cannot prove real capacity or settle contested values; actual people,
law, staff, distances, records and outcomes are absent.

[VR civil registration](../C08/29-civil-registration-and-identity.md)
distinguishes the event, usable proof, status and actual care, and requires
a correction to reach the agencies acting on the wrong record.
