# HT48–HT51. Require an affirmative decision to extend a major essential-service contract

*Design530 · Part of [lawful handover](../19-lawful-handover-and-actual-control.md) · Proposed prospective ordinary law.*

**HT48 — Set the decision window before the award.** Apply to a new or
materially reprocured public operating contract for an essential service where
continuation could materially bind a future elected budget or constrain
replacement of the provider. Aggregate linked instruments under existing
procurement and budget law. State the fixed term, last useful date to
procure a successor, legal notice and confirmation deadlines, gross
obligations, review and exit terms at award. Require an affirmative,
authorized decision for an optional material extension. A missed notice
cannot itself bind a new term under contracts made under this rule. An
already authorized fixed term can span elections when its service and
financing justify it. Apply stronger existing rules on concessions and
any separate constitutional protections within their scope.

**HT49 — Pay for a continuation path that leaves a real choice.** Before
award, compare a justified longer fixed term, a shorter term with a qualified
and separately priced continuity option, and ordinary independent or
successor provision without an extension. Where procurement or handover
could run past expiry, identify the earliest realistic replacement date,
including competition, funded review, paid overlap and accepted records.
Where needed, precompete a paid minimum continuation option with a fixed
scope, aggregate ceiling, one final sunset and separate authorization and
payment. Its term follows the evidenced time to reach a lawful successor.
Book the option cost, exercise cost and subsequent operating capacity on
their own dates. Verify staffing, lawful records, critical inputs and
independent service and review. An option held on paper supplies no cover.
If no such continuation can be funded, use the viable alternative or
present the longer commitment and the choices it forecloses for express
elected decision before it binds.

**HT50 — Separate the continuity act from the policy decision.** The
applicable Assembly or council sets the authorized scope and finance.
Before the contractual cutoff, the legally designated officer issues any
affirmative notice within exact prior powers and verifies usable receipt
at the counterparty. A caretaker may exercise only the narrowly authorized
and funded minimum where due service needs it and qualified alternatives
cannot receive in time. Authority to exercise that option creates no power
to award a further term. Use independently held proof and an authorized
alternate receiver for late or denied authentication. Silence, a draft,
AI advice or disputed message alone supplies no exercise. Preserve
independent useful-time challenge and all effective court orders.

**HT51 — Protect people through exercise, expiry or failure.** Track actual
resident service, worker pay and remedies through the last and first
operating calls and reconcile one payment per obligation. Publish which
options remain executable and why any were lost. If a bridge is missed or
exhausted, use already authorized, paid ordinary or emergency alternatives
within scope. Carry unpaid or unserved obligations and raise the necessary
elected correction. A successor's election does not change the contract's
legal scope. Keep stronger statutory rights, previously binding contracts,
continuing claims and lawful remedies on their own terms. Legacy contracts
with automatic extension are subject to their actual notice law until
lawful consent, amendment, expiry or reprocurement.
