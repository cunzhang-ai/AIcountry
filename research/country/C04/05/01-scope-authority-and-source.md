# SC01–SC04: public buying starts with authority and a real need

**SC01 — Cover public purchasing without licensing private life.** The proposed
Act covers national/local authorities and OE publicly controlled bodies buying
goods, works or services, including mixed control in substance. Commercial and
noncommercial public mandates retain separate accounts. Private contractors'
material subcontracting follows the disclosed public contract's safeguards;
this does not regulate every private customer, cooperative exchange or job.
Public grants, equity, loans, licenses, employment and land acquisition retain
their own IC/OE/WL/H law; relabeling a purchase cannot evade these rules. Foreign
providers need real forum, performance and enforcement terms under C08; domestic
law alone supplies no foreign title, seller or enforceable overseas remedy.

**SC02 — Retain elected purpose and separate execution.** Assembly primary law
sets national scope, thresholds, rights and exceptions. Elected local councils
choose local purposes within LR competence; national purchasing assistance gains
no local appointment/policy veto. Existing responsible sector executives propose
need/retain/repair/public/cooperative/private alternatives and gross sources.
C04 qualified career procurement staff administer an award, with attributed
evaluation by competent unconflicted staff, including one outside the buying
team for material awards. Independently assigned qualified delivery verifiers
check receipt; existing Treasury/local payers pay; AA audits; ordinary courts
review and existing officers execute. C04 recruitment, reasons, protected removal,
paid source and conflict rules apply. No bidder, ministerial favorite or common
AI vendor controls award, verification and the only challenge credential.

**SC03 — Authorize the whole commitment.** Before a new discretionary award,
identify output/quality, authorized buyer, lawful gross multiyear commitment,
annual cash, paid staff/maintenance, acceptance and closure source. Compare
repair/continued provision with a new purchase. Tax returned later, recoverable
VAT, withheld client money, hoped-for savings and privately owned FX are not
today's purchase source. Distinguish purchase from capital/title transfer,
depreciation, renewal, interest and principal. IC risk/guarantee limits and NF
tax clocks survive. A Treasury check of authority/source is not an industrial-
policy veto; absent source never erases existing due service or contract claims.

**SC04 — Admit the current input gaps.** In the firm/plural/A reference, 66
purchase rows total about **123.986m fictional CU**. Only **29.804m** links to
current partial ingredient/utility/housing interfaces; these are not certified
suppliers either. The other **94.182m** remains budget/task quotations without
current matched supplier recipes. Six clinical rows alone total **43.52m**.
Those amounts are neither full national costs nor proved lower bounds. The
13 PB nonfood rows include one unassigned choice envelope and a partial garment
offer; no complete national nonfood producer account follows. Preserve every
unknown and prior obligation, and reject a complete-source/ready-supply inference.
Do not invent a succession of producers just to make the national account close.

[SC30](08-source-disposition-and-paid-transition.md) now retires the six
clinical quotations as adequate-price/source inputs. Their original sums stay
historical; no zero cost, spending cap or reduced care follows. SC21–SC28
supplies the critical-input architecture without qualifying any actual recipe.

[BC commercial responsibility](../../C10/14-cross-border-buying-and-responsibility.md)
now adds a distinct general connected-buyer duty covering public/private sourcing
and SC material subcontracting. SC01 itself still supplies no licence over
ordinary private buying. Public buyer own terms/payment/response, real gross
source, verification, actual due rights and bounded exit retain SC owners;
a supplier code or cheapest compliant-looking bid cannot discharge BC duties.
