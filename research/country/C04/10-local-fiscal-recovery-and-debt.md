# Keep local government and essential services working through financial distress

Design325 · LD01–LD18 · Proposed country rules

**Select early local recovery, funded continuity for common duties and a
narrow debt adjustment for expressly eligible future financing.** Separate
late payments, a continuing gap in essential service funding, and debt
that cannot be sustained. Residents retain their rights, elected council,
opposition and independent remedies through each path.
