# NC01–NC05. A care place needs present, competent people

*Part of [NC](../20-early-care-relationships-and-staffing.md); prospective
organized-group standard, not a rule governing ordinary private family care.*

**NC01 — Preserve optional care independently of employment.** S10's offer
from infancy through12, reference40 hours/48 weeks, assessed additional cover,
no compulsory use or unpaid-family assumption remains. NC specifies publicly
funded organized group care before the sixth birthday, including an early-
learning episode counted toward that offer. It is no new attendance duty,
admission power, household licence or exclusion of unqualified parents.
Actual childminding/individual care retains its distinct appropriate safety
law and comparison; this group schedule is not imposed by analogy on all help.

**NC02 — Select a conservative group candidate, disclose its price.** At
each actual care period, maximum children per present qualified adult is3
before age2,4 from second to third birthday,6 from third to sixth. Normal
stable home groups contain no more than6/8/12 respectively. Assess actual
children, support/communication, activities, sleeping/eating/toileting, rooms
and safe evacuation; these may require smaller groups/more competent adults.
These numeric choices prioritize attention and stable relationships. They
are normative hypotheses, not clinical guarantees, causal superiority findings
or permission to trim individualized support to the ratio. Higher-ratio,
larger-group and differently organized qualified models remain genuine rivals.

**NC03 — No accounting pool across incompatible rooms.** Separate concurrent
groups each need their own actual staff. A mixed-age home group defaults to
its youngest child's stricter ratio/group limit; safe siblings/shared activity
can be planned without forced separation, but cannot evade present needs by
renaming rooms or averaging ages. More flexible mixed grouping needs express
prospective elected standard and qualified safeguarding assessment, not a
manager's source-saving waiver. A birthday need not force a disruptive move;
the existing stricter safe arrangement can continue. Group boundaries are
care arrangements, not ownership of children or restrictions on family life.

**NC04 — Count competence and availability, not labels.** Count an adult only
while actually available for that group's care, with recognized relevant
competence, supported induction and lawful safeguarding/first-aid cover for
the task. Qualifications are evidence, not proof of good relationships.
Trainees need paid supervision; they cannot replace an independently competent
adult before qualification. A manager doing accounts, absent supervisor,
worker on break or person promised simultaneously to another group is not
present cover. Necessary individual assistance is separately counted; it is
not diluted across a room to make a ratio pass. No live surveillance of every
interaction or permanent child/worker dossier is required by these counts.

**NC05 — Tools do not silently become staff.** AI translation, administration,
planning or suitable learning aids can support actual people under C11/DP/PD.
A screen, sensor, remote connection or model account counts as no present
adult. Claimed savings need actual net task/quality/fallback evidence. Future
care forms can challenge this architecture through legitimate prospective
comparison and child/worker protections; a vendor update or benchmark cannot
change the current human-presence requirement or legal responsibility.
