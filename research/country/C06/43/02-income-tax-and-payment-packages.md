# SD05–SD08: two explicit personal tax and payment packages

*Part of [C06/43](../43-country-source-and-delivery.md); design 106 proposed law.*

**SD05 — Specify the I05 path's distributional benchmark.** Retain for explicit
comparison one annual personal allowance **A=0.4M** on
TA-defined aggregate ordinary personal receipts Q after lawful worked-income Y.
Tax is **T(max(0,Q−A))**, where T is PA10's20/35/45/55% scale with widths
0.5M/0.5M/1M/unlimited. Apply the allowance once to a person, including a child,
not once per employer/account or spouse; unused allowance is nontransferable
and nonrefundable. Scale M follows PA10's independent evidence/nominal protection,
not a wage residual. The0.4 factor is a contestable distributional choice, not
an optimal rate or a national source estimate. It is not an extra I05 resource.

[TN05–TN06](../59/02-source-choice-and-real-alternatives.md) withdraws this
numerical schedule's default replacement preference; keep its explicit personal
incidence benchmark and conditional C2 comparison. Actual lawful retrofit
sources continue until express prospective replacement with the joined source/
service/legacy/paid-transition package. No source-law date or due right changes.

I05 remains exempt. Compute E/O and pension disregard from actual net receipts
using PA12 proportionate attribution over original taxable Q; subtract the
allowance before calculating total tax, not again from each income category.
The existing I05 formula,20%-basket earnings disregard/50% withdrawal, lawful
asset buffer and necessary housing/access/support recognition remain. Age-specific
Y basic is paid/matched once under its own law, not a second U. NF company,
VAT/site/transfer law and actual Y contribution/source-year caps stay distinct.

**SD06 — Protect U with one untapered allowance on other receipts.** Design 131
[PW01–PW04](../53/01-foundation-tax-and-work.md) selects **C2** as the preferred
conditional full-U counterpart. For other actual taxable Q after lawful Y,
excluding U, tax is **T(max(0,Q−0.4M))** with PA10's unchanged rates/widths/M.
The allowance is individual, nonrefundable and nontransferable, once across all
payers. U remains excluded, not an extra deduction or condition on work/assets.
Residual needs and Y basic match once. I05 A uses the same tax function, without U.

Prefer a transparent progressive scale over an additional allowance withdrawal.
This value choice gives more to higher taxable receipts and needs more source:
current four-case fixed-price arithmetic adds 17.1504–17.24544m fictional CU/year
of net G personal incidence against C1. I05/U totals are unchanged in those
cases; no growth or new source is assumed. Neither A nor C2 is a funded launch.
Active I05 and lawful Y can still leave only 20.25% of an additional gross wage
at higher bands; the cumulative I10 review and necessary-cost protection remain.

C1 remains a genuine rival and its dated results remain exact: A_U(Q)=0.4M
through M, then 0.4(2M−Q) to 2M, zero above; tax T(max(0,Q−A_U(Q))). Its63%
maximum personal marginal region and 16.65% gross-wage retention with Y/active
I05 are not C2 liabilities. Its earlier65,000-person gains versus C0 and8,000
individual losses versus A remain scoped comparisons, not proof of C2 outcomes.
C0, a revised ordinary scale and other income/service mixes remain alternatives.

**SD07 — Migrate without a tax cliff disguised as reform.** Only an express
coordinated Act, no earlier than PA19/TA15's 12-month notice start, can commence
SD05 or a changed full-U tax package. Existing actual allowances/net protections
remain until lawful source-year matching; no retroactive application or assumed
old rate. A and proposed C2 give one full calendar-year 0.4M allowance to a TA
taxpayer, including first/last partial residence/source years, without day
proration or a second allowance for foreign accounts. This values simplicity
and avoidance of entry/exit cliffs over proportionality; it is no source claim.

The designated full-U package begins a 1 January under public equal-treatment
rules and paid transition. Later qualifying entrants receive U from actual
eligibility, keep that year's A protection and use the enacted package in the
following full year. Proposed C2 has the same tax function as A: there is no
following-year allowance withdrawal and no extra C1-style tax-overlap saving
to transfer into this case. Every cohort still needs actual payment, old-net
protection, partial-year U and administration/source matching. Nobody waits for
a tax classification before receiving due U; politics, work and AI use are no
eligibility test. Involuntary nonreceipt invokes provisional protection and
actual correction, not another allowance. Already commenced U and exclusive
source-period arrears treatment survive; no old rights disappear by this draft.

**SD08 — Pay withholdings and refunds honestly.** TA/RG/TX determine filing,
actual payer credits, remit-by 15th, annual 30 April reconciliation, independent
review and actual ten-working-day refunds. A designated primary payer may apply
only its declared share of A; multiple payers reconcile one total, without a
shared family dossier. Excess deduction requires provisional needs protection
and actual refund, not an assumed future credit. Compare net person resources,
G outlay less actual tax, S contribution and provider cost separately. Selected
examples are fixed-price arithmetic, not full transition, take-up or country yield.
