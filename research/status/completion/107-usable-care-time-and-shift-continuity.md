# Scoped finding. Protect the actual care window

Design199 specifies an occasional or regular late window without a
minimum purchase, lost daytime place or surcharge solely for timing
within covered equivalent care. Lawful roster repair can win before
adding capacity. The responsible care bodies retain paid accepting
backup, family and child choice and independent correction.
[Canonical design](../../country/C07/24-usable-care-time-and-shift-continuity.md)

The published Copenhagen and Helsinki routes distinguish real
institutional alternatives. An authored interval and staff calculation
shows a gap despite less than 40 needed weekly hours and a ratio
failure after one absence. It neither measures suppressed demand
nor establishes a staffed, financed service or better lives.
