# I30–I35. Coordinate benefit reductions when earnings rise

*Part of the [individual income floor](../06-income-floor-and-safe-resources.md). Design351 research selection.*

**I30 — Test combined usable resources on the same facts.** Before
a prospective income-linked change in a designated benefit or public
fee, tax and programme owners compare the person's applicable taxes,
contributions, cash entitlements, charges and service access on both
sides of relevant thresholds. Hold need, covered service, other actual
resources and period constant to identify the effect of income alone.
Calculate what is actually usable on the relevant payment and service
dates separately from statutory amounts. Show changes in unavoidable
work or care costs, different needs, supply and private prices
separately. Respect individually controlled resources. Do not presume
that one household member can spend another's money.

**I31 — Prevent a policy-created fall on the scheduled margin.** For
income-linked withdrawals and fee increases placed on the Assembly's
coordination schedule, require that higher received earnings alone
leave at least the same after-tax usable resources after the covered
charges, at fixed comparable needs and actual access. Publish flat
margins and compare feasible schedules that leave a positive gain.
For new or amended schedules, the competent elected bodies name the
payers, contribution and tax terms, order of adjustments, term, source
and actual receiving. A local authority's lawful vote and applicable
national source stay distinct. Prefer repairing the combined schedule
to relying on repeated temporary relief. Existing legal entitlements
and powers retain their scope until competent prospective change.
A calculator cannot invalidate or rewrite an award.

**I32 — Provide a useful-time bridge for a verified cliff.** Where an
enacted schedule provides for a temporary correction, the named
public payer pays the verified policy-caused shortfall within its
enacted amount cap, by the first affected useful deadline. Compare
matched lower and higher earnings using the governing sources.
Use the lesser of the verified remaining loss and the stated cap,
for at most two scheduled payment periods for the same transition.
Remove the gap through a lawful coordinated schedule before bridge
expiry where possible. Give a reasoned decision on any remaining
shortfall, preserve separate urgent protection and provide direct
independent review. A cap is neither a funded award nor permission
to leave a standing duty unmet. Preserve the bridge's purpose by
excluding the same payment from the reductions it is intended
to correct within the enacted schedule.

**I33 — Keep the actual service and the person reachable.** A care,
housing or disability service loss requires a separate service and
rights decision. A cash calculation does not supply a qualified place,
affordable tenure, accessible travel or support at the needed time.
Name the service owner and accepted route before counting continuity.
Give the person a short inspectable statement of the changed income,
each decision and its source, relevant cash dates and the earliest
challenge. Accept assisted and non-digital proof. A genuinely unpaid
wage uses I27 and other applicable protection. A pay forecast cannot
start a withdrawal on the premise that the person has already received
the money.

**I34 — Pay and review the whole transition.** Cost the statutory
schedule, bridge payments, staffing, service continuity and any
legitimate later recovery separately. Use gross cash by date and
the actual owner. Avoid double-counting a reduction in a fee that
was already funded or a single public transfer through two ledgers.
Compare a phased withdrawal, separately retained service, a bridge
and a broader less assessment-dependent design on the same outcomes.
Give independent reviewers and elected bodies protected source access.
AI may compare lawful schedules and flag a cliff. Qualified officers
decide the case and elected bodies authorize rates, scope and funding.

**I35 — Revisit the result after expiry and migrate prospectively.**
Keep original awards, pending corrections, wage claims and lawful
sources and recipients. Verify actual receipt and usable services
during the bridge and in the first period without it. Publish safe
results on households left worse off, missed due dates, affected
groups and actual costs. Reopen on displaced costs, unclaimed support,
repeat cliff transitions or a simpler funded alternative. Adopt
prospectively with paid review and named successors.
