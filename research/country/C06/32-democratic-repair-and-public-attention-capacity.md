# C06. Capacity for democratic repair and finite public attention

*Integrated design 83 · Consumer of [RR](../C14/08-democratic-repair-without-self-authorization.md)
and [FA](../C12/03-finite-attention-and-lawful-closure.md); not a funded increment.*

Amendment, consultation, individual legal casework and actual enactment have
separate owners and clocks. One queue, AI vendor or unqualified “democracy”
overhead cannot do all four. Assembly law authorizes general process and money;
a lawful local body chooses within its own scope. RR creates no executive repair
power, and FA's completion report enacts no law. Existing permissions/holds stay.

| Existing function | Actual additional duty attributed once, not a new owner |
|---|---|
| D01 | Exact amendment/version/rank/certification and predecessor-law migration |
| D04/D05 | Roll/signature verification, actual access, ballot/campaign and opposition agenda; organized sponsorship/control and public-resource misuse checks |
| D03/D08 | Language/disability access, affected-scope outreach and actual local alternatives |
| D07 | Qualified merit staff/alternates, conflict review, bounded contracts and usable vendor exit |
| D11 | Independent legal aid, old-law amendment and ordinary process review, urgent claims, judicial shifts and received correction |
| K01/K02 | Protected originals, version/translation/issue records, bounded AI verification and independent evaluation, with no truth or adoption monopoly |
| R04 | Actual appropriation, commitment, verification, Treasury payment, custody and reconciliation |
| R09/K03 only when used | Communication/cyber continuity and offline alternative, with no policy or electoral authority |

Price participant/advocate time, paid care/access, sampling/outreach, translation,
interpretation, original-source inspection, coordinator/alternate, protected
custodian, judicial shift, opposition scrutiny and accepted correction separately.
A participant hour is not a clerk's hour; an AI answer isn't original-source
verification. Existing clinical, MB/UA, P09 liberty and ordinary court duty cannot
all consume the same person simultaneously. Source sharing/model repetition is
not independence, and institution labels alone do not establish actual control.

Publish the bounded cycle's task/output, hours, procurement/compute/storage ceiling,
qualification and paid transition before commitment. No cost-plus promise to
process all generated novelty. A limited formal record must disclose what was
not retained/examined; a cheap summary is not a verified replacement for protected
source access. If required independent review/access cannot be delivered, report
the gap and use a lawful qualified alternate, narrower choice or deferral; missed
capacity does not create consent, cancel pending rights or postpone an election.

All actual increments, qualified simultaneous staff, safe sample frame, participant
acceptance, prices, source cash and received remedies are unknown. Existing37
function bid amounts/components stay unchanged and do not qualify RR/FA. SF is
one old-scope protected-period standby; additional adopted dues need recomputation
and actual independent safeguarding, not reuse of the same stock as a new fund.
Client, pension/insurance property and protected service duties remain distinct.
E15 can execute an exact already due record/payment; it cannot certify an amendment,
close consultation, select judges or approve a new appropriation. Publication,
invoice and referral are not actual access, payment or accepted receiving.

[NP](33-protection-production-and-national-expansion.md) now includes these
actual later duties in the national basic/continuity and cohort schedule. No
new independent shift or payer source is supplied by an old payroll share.
The core is task-bounded, not every institutional invoice; existing rights,
professional safety and source-law payment priority remain distinct.
