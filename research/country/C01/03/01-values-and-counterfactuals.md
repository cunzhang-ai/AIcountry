# Country case: values, constraints and counterfactuals

*Canonical CS01–CS02; part of [C01/03](../03-country-case-and-competing-designs.md).*

**CS01 — First principles do not uniquely determine a constitution.** People
are vulnerable, interdependent, diverse and capable of cooperation/conflict;
knowledge, attention and material resources are limited. Equal political
standing, bodily agency, freedom from domination and responsibility to others
are explicit value choices. They are not statistical conclusions or deductions
that prescribe nine judges, a particular tax rate or a school timetable.
Physical/accounting consistency is necessary for promised delivery but cannot
select whose interests should prevail. Preserve value disagreement openly.

Better lives include safety/health, usable resources/time, relationships,
autonomy, political power and ecological/future options. These may conflict.
No average income, satisfaction or model score compensates for eliminating
opposition or arbitrary confinement. Do not infer that every existing state is
bad at every function, or that one novel country can be proved best for every
person/value/context. The ambition remains improvement beyond present failures;
a universal superiority claim remains unestablished, not silently redefined
as a passed file check or a favorable example.

**CS02 — Compare strong complete rivals at the right level.** A real adoption
needs its actual inherited jurisdiction/rights, population, endowments, prices,
workforce, external partners and transition. Until those are known, these are
institutional model comparisons, not measured rankings of named countries:

| Rival | Genuine alternative and possible advantage | Burden/failure the comparison must retain |
|---|---|---|
| Retain and repair | Preserve working services, ordinary courts and informal cooperation; remove specific barriers with less transition | Inherited exclusion and concentrated power may remain; source-law duties still apply. |
| Representative social insurance with plural supply | Broad cash/insurance and contracted services with fewer national coordination layers | Assessment, supplier refusal, fragmented accountability and thin markets can exclude people; competently staffed versions may outperform NP. |
| Comprehensive public provision | Public workforce/assets and earlier broad guarantees change bargaining, access and responsibility | Acquisition, recruitment, management, overlap, monopoly/patronage and opportunity costs; no free ownership or instant supply. |
| Decentralized mutual/community provision | Contextual knowledge, relationships and plural communication with less national mediation | Unequal resources/exit, local domination and shared national/external duties need lawful settlement. |
| Delegated technical administration | Competent bounded experts/tools can simplify specific tasks and reduce delay | Control of framing/source/appeal and unreviewable delegation can dominate. AI-exclusive sovereignty fails the democratic requirement even if a task performs well. |

AIcountry's NP/CJ/I/S reference can lose to a simpler or structurally different
model. Compare inherited and new duties, distribution, actual sources and the
same preparation/completion/operation/renewal period; do not fix old artificial
bids or omit a rival's strongest arrangement. Separate a local treatment effect,
a complete sector alternative and a national regime. Evidence at one level
cannot validate the others automatically. Missing evidence differs from a value
choice; a contested right is not settled by the largest causal coefficient.
