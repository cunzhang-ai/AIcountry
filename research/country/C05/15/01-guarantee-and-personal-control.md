# SG01–SG04: what every person can actually rely on

*Part of [C05/15](../15-service-first-personal-security.md), design 136 candidate.*

**SG01 — Guarantee protection to the person.** Retain I01–I24 ordinary and
additional-needs protection, paid universal essential S/MO capacity, secure
housing/repair under H and individual Y basic/earned/survivor rights. I05's
actual-resource test determines the cash top-up, not moral worth, employment
status or a duty to accept unsafe work. No presumed partner/parent resources,
pooled household head, compulsory care or AI account becomes the gateway.
Family support counts only under existing safe actual-receipt/matching rules.

**SG02 — State the cash rule rather than promise an undefined floor.** Retain
I05: `max(0, N − O − A − 0.5 × max(0, E − 0.2B))`, with its existing resource
definitions, asset buffer, pension disregard and protected correction. Ordinary
need remains at least `max(B − Q, 0.20B)` inside N, with distinct unmet housing,
recognized work/care/access and due-period replacement needs. The 20% is no
extra universal payment and no cap on individual cash need. B's adequacy,
age factors, costs, replacement schedules and actual Q require their existing
evidence and law; do not lower them to force an affordable-looking aggregate.

SD05 retains `T(max(0, Q_tax − 0.4M))` on actual taxable personal receipts after
lawful Y, with PA's20/35/45/55% bands and separate tax-resource attribution.
`Q_tax` is not I14's received in-kind `Q`. One person receives one allowance;
there is no additional spouse/employer/agent allowance, refundability or new
tax enactment here. Separate expense compensation stays exempt as specified.
Unpaid wages, promised services, blocked money and loans are not received cash.

[Design155 TN](../../C06/59-tax-sources-and-country-operation.md) keeps that
specified distributive benchmark but withdraws its default numerical replacement
preference. SG's service-first guarantee remains; actual lawful retrofit sources
continue until express prospective replacement, with complete incidence/source
and paid-transition choice. No source adequacy, tax change or due-right delay.

**SG03 — Protect personal control and real service choice together.** Public
availability requires paid receiving, accessible routes, qualified staff,
maintenance and useful-time remedies, not only a nominal entitlement. General
S/MO services do not automatically reduce the ordinary cash need. Match a
lawfully creditable, voluntarily received ordinary component once; IC02's
practical suitable cash alternative remains. No provider may compel a communal
lifestyle, arbitrary treatment, unpaid relative work or ideological conformity.
Privacy, treatment consent and a child's separate rights remain distinct.

Preserve AS's non-employer assistance route, supported optional direct employment
and paid fallback. Do not call control over a service the same thing as taking
on its employment, procurement, clinical or custodial liabilities. No service
substitution for cash is authorized merely because a public provider is cheaper.

**SG04 — Security must survive ordinary changes.** Existing I07–I09 advance,
shock/uncertain-receipt protection, agency assistance, good-faith correction,
direct review and safe offline routes continue. A job, provider, home, family
or AI/vendor change must not create a payment gap through an obsolete income
assumption or a new technical identity. Reuse valid evidence within its lawful
purpose and complete scope; changed needs/source facts require correction,
not a permanent eligibility score or universal linked dossier.

IC17–IC20/Y06 qualification remains separate from cash receipt/renewal. Proved
covered work survives employer default; no double credit, collection or period.
Restricted S receipt, new annual units, existing pensions and full liability
remain different entries. None of these positive guarantees is established
as actually delivered or fully financed by writing the formula.

[EB finite transition](../16-portable-earnings-and-work-transition.md) adds a
person-paid capped-history buffer, leaving SG's continuing I05/SD05/service/
earned architecture intact. A bridge claim/refusal/vendor or actual receipt
must not gate I05/clinical/housing rights; actual net receipt alone enters I05 E
once under existing source, with independent Y06 credit qualification.
