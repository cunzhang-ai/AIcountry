# MS13–MS16: make the tradeoff answerable and reversible

*Part of [national stabilization](../61-employment-prices-and-household-security.md).*

**MS13 — Inspect both losses, not just a target score.** Existing MC decisions,
M14 band-miss reports, EV independent evidence, ordinary hearings, AA and courts
must distinguish observed prices/work/received services, forecast counterfactuals,
uncertainty and distribution. Sustained below-band demand failure deserves
attention as well as above-band inflation. Parliamentary annual monetary/fiscal
scrutiny examines both objectives and the actual role of the fiscal/capacity
package, with opposition, workers/employers, carers, service users and excluded
people able to challenge. Do not turn a single unemployment or inflation figure
into presumed misconduct, mandate compliance or a causal proof of success.

**MS14 — An unresolved tradeoff returns to democratic choice.** At ordinary
annual scrutiny, MC explains the maintained or revised medium-term path, actual
misses and why alternatives were rejected. If it cannot give a credible joint
path, disclose that conflict and request Assembly consideration of the general
mandate and fiscal/capacity choices. No report, hearing delay or rejected advice
changes the mandate, votes, rates, source law or term. The Assembly may retain
or prospectively revise through competent law with usable opposition and rights
review; a minister cannot secretly substitute a named decision. Courts review
power/procedure/rights and execute actual due remedies, not guarantee jobs,
appoint an economic school or decide the preferred policy rate.

**MS15 — Pay existing roles and preserve continuity.** Budget actual distinct
monetary/fiscal/EV expertise, original data, safe access, non-AI receiving,
worker/affected participation, independent counsel/courts and execution. Reconcile
these hours and source/currency/dates with GA/DP/SC/services and the whole-country
account once; no forecast productivity or old37-row envelope funds them by label.
The objective amendment requires actual competent prospective law, published
commencement and role/record/contract/claim migration; it is no actual appointment
or rate change. Existing fixed seats/votes, risk limits, typed transactions,
contracts, debt, accrued/due rights and paid OP/T successors continue. Neither
research selection nor changed forecasts rewrite old promises or permissions.

**MS16 — Preserve the strongest objection and alternatives.** The old flexible
inflation mandate already rejects automatic austerity and allows supply-sensitive
paths; retaining it with better fiscal protection is a strong simpler rival.
A joint mandate can blur accountability, enlarge discretion, weaken inflation/
FX trust and hurt the very households it intends to protect. It supplies no
worker, import, useful task or lender, and cannot eliminate real losses.
Employment-only expansion, employer retention, a voluntary public-job/learning
guarantee, external currency and broader elected fiscal-money regimes require
their own complete source/power comparison; they are not silently combined here.

Select the balanced mandate because involuntary work exclusion should be an
express coequal governing concern, with price responsibility retained. This is
a value/institutional hypothesis, not a proved empirical winner or novel idea.
Reopen on persistent prices/work/access harms, forecast/evidence failure,
capture, fiscal/monetary conflict, missing independent receiving or a better
whole alternative. Next return to the largest unresolved joined country need/
work/service/source comparison; no automatic inflation-model or agency series.
