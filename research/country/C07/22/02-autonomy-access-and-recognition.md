# AL06–AL10. Public access, autonomous inquiry and honest recognition

*Part of [lifelong learning](../22-lifelong-learning-and-renewal.md).*

**AL06 — Public responsibility with plural learning institutions.** Local public
education is the service debtor, not merely a voucher issuer. Public colleges,
community/worker institutions, qualified additional providers, apprenticeships
and negotiated external provision can deliver accepted routes under actual
contracts. Identify each actual employer, source, accepting place and academic
competence. No funding contract nationalizes private assets, commands foreign
acceptance or lets a failed supplier erase the public duty. Small local courses
can use existing accountable employers; no compulsory new board for each class.

For publicly established autonomous post-foundational colleges/universities,
select OE-compatible non-profit public legal entities with their own employing
and asset/liability responsibility under express charters. This is a specified
form candidate, not an automatic transformation of old institutions. Foundational
schools remain under ED; professional licensing and KR research/knowledge rights
keep their separate law. National/local general funding remains elected.

**AL07 — No minister or professional caste owns the institution.** New public
advanced-institution governing boards normally have equal voting thirds elected
by actual staff, by current/recent learners and appointed by the accountable
owner-side minister/local executive through C04 open qualified selection. At
a 13-week average of 1,000 covered workers across the controlled institution,
this public-sector proposal reserves half the seats to the workforce;
the default becomes one-half staff, one-quarter learners and one-quarter public.
Count actual workers once across controlled campuses and preserve stronger
existing worker rights. This sector proposal stands on express public institutional
law and its own funding. This narrow prospective sector rule
replaces OE20's owner-side board selection and supplies learner-seat terms only
for these new statutory bodies. OE13 workforce elections/recall and four-year
worker terms remain, including actual part-time/disputed-retaliation eligibility.
The charter fixes actual seat numbers consistent with those shares,
normally six seats (2/2/2), or twelve (6/3/3) at the large-worker threshold,
actual constituencies, conflicts, accessible elections, vacancies and published
terms before reliance: staff/public terms four years, learner terms one year,
with lawful replacement and paid participation. A person in several constituencies
chooses one electoral roll for that cycle; accounts/agents do not multiply votes.
An incumbent head cannot appoint its own electoral majority or a final reviewer.

The board chooses/removes the head through open qualified appointment and fair
OE/C04/WL process, by a majority of its full statutory membership; vacancies are
not affirmative votes. It controls lawful assets, budget, employment and public
access obligations, with safe learner/staff agenda rights and inspectable reasons.
No constituency alone has a majority. These shares/terms are provisional policy choices,
not demonstrated independence. Actual charity/community/private governance stays
under its own lawful form and public-access contract, not this public franchise.

**AL08 — Protect academic judgment and lawful public accountability.** A chartered
elected academic senate of qualified teaching/research staff with learner
representation decides academic programmes, standards and methods within law;
individual assessments require competent conflict-free examiners and independent
academic appeal, not political or student popularity votes. The board cannot
order a false pass, censor lawful inquiry or purge teachers for dissent. Academic
freedom includes criticism of the country, its funders and AI systems. Ordinary
safety, equality, employment, public-source audit and executed court relief apply.

Elected bodies may change prospective public priorities through actual law and
budget, not secret loyalty conditions or retaliation against lawful criticism.
Programme closure/reorganization needs public reasons, affected voice and paid
continuing/teach-out routes; academic status gives no unlimited public purse or
unreviewable exclusion. No new national university guardian/head is created.

**AL09 — Recognise capability without buying a licence.** Competent educational
assessors use published proportionate standards for prior formal/informal,
foreign, modular and future-medium learning, including necessary accommodations
and protected evidence. Accept actual demonstrated equivalent learning; do not
force repeat tuition because the route lacks a familiar platform/brand. State
unknown meanings and missing safety evidence rather than silently dropping them.
Records are portable with lawful access/correction, not universally public.
Professional licensing remains with its actual independent competent source;
a course, AI certificate, institutional label or elapsed plan confers no licence.

**AL10 — Scarce places need fair selection and a meaningful alternative.** Publish
actual prerequisites, qualified places, selection reasons, schedule and outside
challenge. Do not use wealth, political agreement, mandatory AI, employer favor
or a predicted lifetime fiscal score. Demonstrated readiness/necessary safety
may matter. The public guarantor offers a funded genuinely suitable alternative
route/provider/intake under AL05; an unrelated short class is no substitute for
a qualifying aim. An alternative must serve the accepted substantive aim or a
revised aim the learner freely accepts, not impose a different career/field to
meet a deadline. If no suitable funded route exists in time, record breach and
actual interim learning/support with continuing remedy, not fulfilled access.
The person can contest suitability, choose to wait for a
preferred specialty or decline without losing basic protection. No preferred
specialty seat or university is guaranteed on demand; capacity cannot be invented by a lottery.

separate route from established learning or practice to an actual licensing
decision, with paid alternatives and continuing original application dates.
