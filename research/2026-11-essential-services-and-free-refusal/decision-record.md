# Design323 decision on free refusal in essential public services

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design322, SG, AH, AS, AP, LRT, JC and the current framework. The
country already rejects work, ideology or AI use as general gates to
basic protection. Select a directly reviewable cross-service rule against
using one due right to obtain another choice. Keep ordinary common intake
where its alternatives operate, with funded independent execution and
structurally separate control when common control defeats actual refusal.
The project assistant authors the choice. Competent elected and affected
public processes control adoption and resources.

## Alternatives and evidence

Retain an integrated provider with ordinary complaint handling, use a
shared front door and independent execution, or permanently separate
essential functions and intake. Integration can lower administration and
support a usable joined plan, while its operator may acquire leverage over
several protected interests. Complete separation can contain abuse and
make responsibilities visible, while increasing coordination burdens and
cost. Select scope-limited independence with a positive receiving test
and a requirement to separate controlling authority where that test fails.
The design accepts higher recurrent and transitional costs to make exit
effective. Legitimate safety conditions, fair work and review rights retain
their own protection. A notice or money commitment alone supplies no
accepted replacement.

The [comparators](sources.md) address particular dimensions of dependence,
consent and cutoff. Their rules do not transfer to all public services.
The constructed care and income case compares actual deadlines and
eligible receiving on a single stipulated budget.

## Authority, migration and reopening

Use ordinary primary law for covered essential acts, grounds, evidence,
continuity and direct relief. Existing review and execution offices retain
their jurisdiction. Fund independently controlled payment and actual
receiving, accessible notice and assistance, and existing binding duties.
Adopt applicable conditions prospectively. Review contracts, name
responsible authorities and accepting substitutes, and preserve affected
workers' pay and outstanding claims. The provider under challenge cannot
control sole evidence or interim release. Publish safe aggregate failures
and material common controls for elected scrutiny.

Exercise one combined income and home-care failure with consenting people,
affected workers, lawful test records, a mock cutoff and paid independent
receiving. Check lost time, retaliation, coerced assent, record access,
untimely relief, staff overlap and whether a resident can leave without
repeating a dossier. Compare integrated repair and independent intake
under matched obligations. Reopen on recurring service degradation,
common-controller failures, displaced care or a simpler reliable path.
Actual public authority, costs, participation and outcomes remain open.
