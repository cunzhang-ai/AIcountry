# Primary sources on public guarantees and portfolio exposure

1. Swedish National Debt Office, *How the Debt Office issues
   guarantees and loans*. The agency describes assessment and
   risk-related fees and states that a parliamentary reduction or
   waiver needs other funding. It separately describes payment
   when a guarantee is invoked and later recovery from the borrower.
   [Official method](https://www.riksgalden.se/en/our-operations/guarantee-and-lending/how-the-debt-office-issues-guarantees-and-loans/).
2. Swedish National Debt Office, *Central Government Guarantees
   and Lending – A Risk Analysis*, 2021. This primary portfolio
   assessment distinguishes the ordinary portfolio and special
   arrangements, and discusses aggregate risks and uncertainty.
   It shares institutional evidence with the method above.
   [Official risk analysis](https://www.riksgalden.se/globalassets/dokument_eng/garantier/risk-analysis/central-government-guarantees-and-lending---a-risk-analysis-2021-l.pdf).

These are comparators for actual pricing, aggregate exposure
and subsequent collection. They do not establish a settled cash
reserve, adequate fee or fiscal capacity in AIcountry. The case
uses separate authored amounts and legal assumptions.
