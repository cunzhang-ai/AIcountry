# EG13–EG18. Connect large loads without shifting costs to households

*Design374 · Part of [ordinary electricity law](../19-electricity-service-and-grid-access.md) · Proposed research law.*

**EG13 — Qualify each requested load and competing reservation.** Apply
this rule to any proposed new load or increase that materially changes
network works, firm headroom, protected service or public exposure,
regardless of technology or ownership. Publish ordinary materiality
criteria under adopted law. The operator records each phase, location,
ultimate controller, mutually exclusive sites, firm and flexible
quantities and actual need date. Keep parallel applications open
where law permits. Offer simultaneous binding reservations only when
both can actually proceed independently. Readiness may be proved by
proportionate alternative evidence. An application or a promise of
jobs is neither a resource commitment nor measured demand.

**EG14 — Reserve only a complete and independently qualified phase.**
The network operator and system operator verify physical capacity,
required works and actual accepted dates under EG02–EG04. Compare
staging, ordinary firm access, genuinely voluntary flexibility and
independently operable alternatives against the same existing rights.
Keep protected homes and essential services on the same dated
physical account. Check electricity, water, environmental limits and
other material capacity at their own useful windows. A behind-the-meter
facility has its own verified rights, cost and failures. It does not
waive the consequences of relying on grid backup. Ordinary water,
planning and ecological decisions remain separate. Flexibility needs
usable interruption and safe recovery load, including a coincident
failure or call. Test materially overlapping returns on the same
constrained connection. The competent operator accepts the ordinary,
interrupted and restart windows and any safe release cap or sequence.
Record available MW, outstanding MWh, required ramp and recovery time,
affected protected demand and the original completion deadline. Keep
the heat and other protected returns on the same calendar. Charging,
heating and computing using the same capacity or release signal may
not be qualified separately for the same recovery window. A financial contribution buys no priority over a
protected need or licence to exceed an ecological limit.

**EG15 — Assign the cost of delay, underuse and exit before committing work.**
Under EG05, the network owner and applicant show efficient attributable
works and cancellation or underuse costs by phase, dates, actual
alternative use and remaining risk. Compare bounded prepayment, staged
security and binding minimum capacity charges within ordinary authority.
Cover the committed attributable exposure before incurring it, with
enforceable sources actually available when needed. A parent promise
with the same failing source is not independent backing. Avoid shifting
a cancelled project's costs into ordinary household tariffs. A public
share requires an express elected decision on demonstrated shared value,
source and loss. When early claims or genuine resale proceeds arrive,
reconcile them once and return excess as required. A deposit cannot
replace a qualified site, service or delivered capacity.

**EG16 — Apply milestones to the party that controls them.** The
operator gives separate accepted dates for customer work, network
work and independent approvals. Give usable notice, reasons and
independent review before adverse release under EG03. Do not penalize
an applicant for an operator's own missed milestone or for exercising
a timely lawful challenge. Reassess the amount and duration that
remain justified during delay. Recover only authorized attributable
losses after verification. Release unused capacity or excessive
security once prior rights and pending review permit. Stop unsupported
later phases without closing a valid operating phase. A change of
owner or queue number does not erase liabilities, reset deadlines or
duplicate a reservation.

**EG17 — Keep allocation, spending and review independently answerable.**
Elected bodies set the classes, tariffs, subsidy and any public
exposure. Network and system officers retain their distinct decisions
and statutory safety duties. Affected residents and competing
applicants can obtain usable reasons and challenge common ownership,
unsupported priorities, exposure or missed protection under EG and
ordinary law. Qualified independent reviewers need actual separate
appointments, funding, original records and enforceable remedies.
Publish safe aggregates on requests, reserved, energized and used
capacity, cancellations, recovery costs and displaced work. AI may
reconcile declared inputs and replay alternatives. It cannot approve
a reservation, permit, tariff, public guarantee or allocation.

**EG18 — Verify both the later service window and the exit account.**
Before each new phase, check actual energization, usable load,
interruptions, recovery, protected water and service, actual paid
cost and the next scheduled need. Verify heat and other protected
receipt through the restart rather than counting the reserved load as
delivery. Reconcile actual metered use and settlement before treating
avoided purchases or flexibility payments as an available source. Report any unmet duty or cost
under its original date and owner. Preserve accepted connections,
legitimate reliance, existing claims, evidence and review through
a supplier, operator or government change. Reopen on speculative
repetition, common-source security failure, displaced essentials,
underused public assets or a cheaper qualified expansion.
