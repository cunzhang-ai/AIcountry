# Source scope: technical law is not a copied universal standard

15 October 2026 · Part of [technical safety](README.md)

The dated [source record](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-technical-safety-and-access/sources.json)
separates eight documentary entries into six institutional families. Three WHO
works are not three independent institutions; linked summaries/AI interpretations
are not observations of this country's outcomes or actual affected participation.

- [WHO Streets for Life (2021)](https://www.who.int/campaigns/un-global-road-safety-week/2021/streets-for-life):
  inspected campaign text supports considering 30 km/h where vulnerable people and
  traffic mix. It supplies no proof for our entire road law, 18-year driving age,
  25 km/h assistance exemption or 50/80/100 maxima; those are national hypotheses.
- [WHO medical-device framework publication](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/who-global-model-regulatory-framework-for-medical-devices-including-in-vitro-diagnostic-medical-devices--annex-3)
  and its [linked TRS 1045 Annex 3 PDF](https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/biologicals/ecbs/annex3-gmrf-who_trs_1045.pdf?download=true&sfvrsn=88867b3a_3):
  actual classification, reliance/recognition and post-market portions inspected.
  The page describes 126 pages/prepublication wording; the linked 2023 report PDF
  rendered 147 PDF pages. Do not pretend these are identical publication objects
  or that every appendix was reviewed. No complete clinical conformity is claimed.
- [WHO drinking-water guideline (2022)](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240045064):
  publication overview inspected. The [located table reproduction](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK579466/table/ch7.tab10/)
  supplied the E. coli/100 mL search excerpt, but opened as a challenge page; the
  WHO full-download request returned 403. Full method/table context was not
  inspected. Our candidate remains explicitly subject to qualified source review.
- [IAEA SSG-55, published online text](https://nucleus.iaea.org/sites/nss-oui/Published%20Collections/m_b1697aee-6e30-4a17-904e-f69faaaef9fd/m_b1697aee-6e30-4a17-904e-f69faaaef9fd__4_0.Html):
  inspected scope, planned nonmedical exposures, graded controls and distinct
  justification/optimization/limits. Not complete GSR Part 3, medical/nuclear
  licensing, numerical dose annexes or actual national legal applicability.
- [ICAO announcement, 27 March 2026](https://www.icao.int/news/icao-strengthens-global-framework-accident-investigations-addresses-conflicts-interest):

  It states Amendment 20 applicability on **23 November 2028**. Do not apply
  that future date as current law or claim to have read the complete Annex 13.
- [ILO C155 located English text](https://www.ilo.org/dyn/normlex/en/f?p=NORMLEXPUB%3A12100%3A0%3A%3ANO%3A12100%3AP12100_ILO_CODE%3AC155):

  qualification, not full treaty inspection or a verified national ratification.

No complete publisher-byte archive, all-sector source survey, independent qualified
engineering/clinical/legal review, actual local annex, funded capacity or observed
superiority exists. Actual access failures are preserved, not turned into citations
claiming a full review. New annexes need their own exact sources and fresh decisions.
