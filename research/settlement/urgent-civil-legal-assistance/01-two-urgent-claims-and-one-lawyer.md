# Two urgent claims and one available lawyer

This constructed case tests [UCL01–UCL08](../../country/C08/25/13-urgent-civil-legal-assistance.md).
One household faces a possession step at hour 8. Another faces loss of
protected household electricity at hour 7. Each contacts the service
before hour 2. Assume each has a plausibly arguable request that
requires independent advice and preparation beginning by hour 3.
Each preparation and urgent appearance consumes one complete staffed
assignment. The original rules leave courts two qualified hearing slots
and competent execution routes. No merits result is assumed.

The first provider has a material conflict in the housing case and is
already committed to another client during the same interval. One
qualified lawyer outside that office can accept either new case. Listing
that lawyer twice leaves one household without timely help regardless
of which they accept. The commissioning service has a separate lawful
alternate who can take the remaining case at a cost of three units if
cash, lawful authority and acceptance are confirmed by hour 3.

| Staffing and finance | Timely prepared applications |
|---|---:|
| Shared lawyer listed for both | At most one |
| Independent alternate accepts with three units settled by hour 3 | Two |
| Only two units settle by hour 3, one at hour 4 | At most one |
| Full timely cash with no accepting qualified alternate | At most one |

A case can reach the court on time and still receive no relief after a
lawful hearing. If the court orders preservation, check that the relevant
officer actually stops the challenged step by its original deadline.
A late order, unavailable executor or unsafe supply remains a separate
failure. Direct court access, notice and response continue regardless of
which branch applies. The comparison assigns no priority between claims.

These figures test conflict, paid capacity, original deadlines and actual
execution. They supply no finding on liability, safety, qualification
or real legal aid cost. [Reproduction](verify-urgent-assistance.py).
