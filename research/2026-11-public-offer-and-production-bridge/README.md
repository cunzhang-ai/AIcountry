# A public offer connected to the whole production system

Design177 ·4November2026 · No new fiscal or constitutional authority

[WF57–WF60](../country/C06/58/13-public-offer-production-and-source.md)
reconciles observed national uses and public production/fiscal accounts, then
compares retained uses with one explicitly declared service/capital increment.
Public payroll alone misses upstream production; multiplying gross output does
not create money. Supplier costs already inside a purchase count once.

The5bnDKK final-use vector represents hypothetical hospital/early-care/non-level-
education/additional-software activity at original2023 boundaries. It prices no
complete legal programme and chooses no appropriation. Simple requirements are
about6.689bn gross output,3.915bn GVA,6,233 full-time wage-earner equivalents and
0.819bn source-boundary imports. These views are not additive bills or sources.

Whole observed import layers reconcile allocated and unallocated supplies.
Selected import duties3.612439bn are domestic valuation entries rather than
foreign supplier payments; reconstructed GDP/imports match rounded national
account displays within published precision, without forcing exact equality.
The increment's actual duty/FX split, available source, qualified capacity,
price responses, private work displaced and paid transition remain unknown.
