# CI01–CI06. Protect political freedom where people depend on a gatekeeper

*Design368 · Part of [civic independence](../04-civic-independence.md) · Proposed civil law.*

**CI01 — Make coercive use of a livelihood relationship a civil wrong.**
A public, commercial or cooperative controller must not materially threaten,
condition or impair work, wages, a home, payment access or an essential
service in order to compel, deter or punish someone's lawful political
participation or nonparticipation. This includes use of a related party,
intermediary or an adverse act against a member of the person's household.
Participation includes voting, abstention, lawful organizing, candidacy,
support for a rival and refusal to disclose a ballot or political choice.
Neither market dominance nor loss of the opportunity is required to
challenge a credible coercive threat. Lawful advocacy, ordinary criticism
and voluntary association remain protected. Competing on policy or applying
an independently justified rule is not coercion. A political party may
select its own representatives by lawful criteria without conditioning
an unrelated livelihood. A time correlation or an algorithmic inference
about belief alone establishes neither the civil wrong nor individual fault.

**CI02 — Give a person a protected route outside the controller.**
The affected person or a representative acting on their authority may
file directly with the ordinary duty court through existing protected
assistance. Sector intake preserves the first receipt and transfers the
claim only to an accepting court route. No complaint to an implicated
provider, disclosure of a vote or membership list, or consent to broader
data sharing is required. The claimant gives specific facts linking
the material threat or deprivation to a protected act. The court can
require relevant records within the controller's possession and test
its independent lawful ground, with protected comparators, notice and
meaningful reply. The civil court decides the link on the applicable
civil proof standard. Sector owners preserve records and valid existing
duties. They retain their own decisions, including genuinely urgent
safety actions. A separate pending sector claim does not block a request
to protect the original civic opportunity.

**CI03 — Decide interim protection while it can still work.**
For a credible claim with an identifiable imminent and otherwise
irrecoverable civic loss, use the existing ordinary duty court and
PX04's proposed six-elapsed-hour limit from first protected receipt,
or earlier when execution needs more time. Preserve any earlier
applicable clock. The judge must record the specific link, likely loss,
necessary time, effect on others and the least restrictive workable
response. Temporarily preserve a due payment, ordinary access or current
lawful position where that is safe. Where continued provision is unsafe,
unlawful or ineffective, use an already authorized, staffed alternative
within its actual source. Restrict without prior notice only under
ordinary court safeguards, with a reasoned order, prompt adversarial
review and expiration by 48 elapsed hours unless independently renewed
after a meaningful opportunity to reply. A filing alone stays nothing.
Final relief may stop coercion, restore access, correct records or
award proved loss. A court cannot create an appropriation, guarantee
a job or freeze a genuinely unrelated lawful decision.

**CI04 — Protect the person and the civic opportunity as separate duties.**
Before relying on the interim order, identify a qualified unconflicted
judge, confidential contact, accepting livelihood provider, lawful payer
and actual executor. Trace appointments, removal, funding and records
through the same controller and its intermediaries. Where the allegation
involves a public actor or the sector's protector, use the existing
eligible conflict substitutes and direct court route. Lawful election
officers independently restore available ballot or campaign access
within existing law. Verify actual pay, safe use or continuing access
and that the civic opportunity was usable before its deadline.
Accept voluntary protected testimony of receipt. Record delivery
without tracking individual participation, affiliation or ballot
choice. If the fallback fails, preserve both original losses and
continuing claims. The applicable electoral
contest law alone governs any result challenge or repeat. A credible
claim, delay or lost opportunity supplies no automatic electoral result.

**CI05 — Reduce the underlying dependency where remedies arrive too late.**
At a material elected decision about a public contract, essential-route
change or public employer's concentration of gateways, compare ordinary
enforcement, independently usable provision and structural separation
of the relevant controls. Apply the same test to direct-public,
contracted and mutual provision. Verify that switching is practical
before a loss, including accessible transport, dates, staff, recipient
control and ordinary operating cost. Prefer an independently operable
route where recurring credible pressure or a common controller defeats
timely correction, if the source and tradeoffs are qualified. The Assembly
or competent local body chooses the method and pays for it under current
budget law. Competition remedies keep their own grounds and authority.
No authority may demand political participation data as a condition of
receiving a service, including a substitute.

**CI06 — Carry obligations through change and audit actual freedom.**
Protect existing wages, leases, benefits, pending cases and stronger
remedies through any change of provider or government. Fund duty coverage,
safe assistance and execution against concurrent demand before
commencement. Measure timely receipt and the accessibility of the
alternative to non-complainants with safe aggregate methods. Complaint
volume alone gives no assurance. AI may identify relevant records or
compare lawful service routes for an authorized case, with review and
minimal data. It must not infer private political beliefs, assess
credibility or choose relief. Reopen when a protector shares effective
control, deadlines repeatedly fail, disclosure exposes participants or
a simpler structural alternative demonstrably protects more freedom.
