## Objections, corrections and continuing claims

Targeting may protect more people at the same budget; a universal transfer
can displace urgently useful care/investment. Keep that substantive rival.
Universal payment does not remove residence, identity, taxes, housing/access
assessment or power in payment rails. No administrative saving is credited.
Taxes and behavior can reverse pre-finance gains; no promise to compensate
every lost expected capital return is made. Broader redistribution remains lawful
only through legitimate prospective law and applicable rights.

Public investment can crowd out scarce skills, add fees and favor insiders.
Existing elected bodies choose within scope; qualified staff/acceptance,
outside-team scrutiny, source evidence, direct courts and opposition retain
distinct roles. AI cannot award sovereign power. Read-only review corrected
an accidental centralization of local windows, separated reaching a cap from
actual impairment, and corrected the I05 rationale. A loss cannot replenish
gross commitment authority. No claims of actual staff independence follow.

Existing I05/Y/services, accrued rights, court orders, debt, employee/creditor
claims, private titles and due dates continue. Actual base receipt precedes
component subtraction; no arrears vanish. Windows end new awards at expiry,
not contracts/servicing/remedies; lawful successors and funded closure are
specified before commitments. No SF/pension/client/bank/FD source is counted
again. Current software, canonical law, constitutional holds and method remain.
