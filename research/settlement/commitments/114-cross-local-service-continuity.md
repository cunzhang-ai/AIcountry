# Commitment 114. Keep duties through a domestic move

[XR01–XR10](../../country/C04/09-cross-local-service-continuity.md)
separates lawful origin responsibility, host interim action, accepted
service and the final paying authority. Preserve the first request,
earlier deadlines, safe location, independent remedy and prior claims.
Protected school continuity and an enacted bounded national advance
address timing and payer disputes. Pay and settle each episode once.

No automatic change of residence or authority follows. Existing sector
and local powers, financing, original claims and paid successors
remain. Actual payer, worker and delivered protection are unverified.
