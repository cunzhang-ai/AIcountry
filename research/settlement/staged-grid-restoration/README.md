# Staged grid restoration and essential service receipt

The [country design](../../country/C09/20/01-staged-grid-restoration-and-essential-receipt.md)
selects a safe electrical sequence, protected service delivery through
transfer and a separately funded next call. This support is divided by
what each piece can establish.

1. [Observed Iberian restoration](01-observed-iberian-restoration.md) traces
   distinct restoration paths and limits the inference to recorded endpoints.
2. [Authored comparison](02-authored-restoration-comparison.md) distinguishes
   startup power, sustained supply, handoff endurance and receiving.
3. [Reproduction](verify-restoration.py) checks the documented intervals
   and the authored assumptions and arithmetic.
