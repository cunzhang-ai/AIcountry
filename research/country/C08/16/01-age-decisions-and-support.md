# Ages, real choices and bounded support

*B21–B23; part of [C08/16](../16-family-agency-property-and-estates.md).*

**B21 — Majority without a capacity forfeiture.** Civil majority is 18.
Adults retain legal capacity, including disabled and older people and those
in care. Diagnosis, perceived decision skill, unwise choices or an AI score
cannot appoint a substitute owner of their legal life. Make communication,
independent advice, chosen support and safe direct access available.
Existing B01/B03 and advance-care protections continue. Political, criminal,
sexual-protection, employment, clinical and benefit ages remain separate;
turning 18 does not erase a pending child-case protection.

**B22 — Young people's acts and bodily decisions.** A young person can
make routine beneficial purchases from their actual lawful resources or
an expressly delegated budget. From 16, they can directly manage earned
pay and personal benefit/payment accounts and assent to lawful work under
separate protective labor law. Under 16, provide supported ordinary access
and an independent route where a household gate is unsafe; no household
head owns the child's identity, resources or complaint.

No under-18 person is bound to a loan, guarantee or open-ended future debt
merely by assent, an adult co-signature or a platform credential. Major
disposal/charging of their property requires a child-court finding of actual
benefit, supported voice, independent advice and absence of a safer option;
publicly owed care is not financed by default from the child's assets.
Routine restitution is limited to an actual retained usable benefit with
B08's defenses and subsistence protection; no penalty interest, personal
labor or deprivation of essentials. At majority, full informed voluntary
affirmation can bind a lawful future obligation; silence alone cannot.
Fraud or harm retains its separate actual fault, age and proof rules.

For clinical decisions, support actual understanding of the choice, material
effects and alternatives; presume decision-specific understanding from 16,
without excluding younger people who can make the supported choice. No
blanket parental veto or exposure to an abusive household. A supported,
informed bodily choice is not overridden merely because of age or professional
disagreement; an urgent independent court can resolve disputed understanding,
coercion or legality. An emergency exception needs its separate general-law
grounds and cannot erase an ascertainable valid refusal. This strong autonomy
choice, including grave cases, remains a disputed normative candidate, not a
treaty prescription. K13's protection age is not a clinical-consent test.

**B23 — Support and necessary asset preservation.** A chosen supporter or
agent has only the actual B03 mandate, shared across copies, with conflict
disclosure, accounts, revocation and direct challenge. Marriage, kinship or
payment for care grants no additional authority. A presently communicated
will controls over an older supporter's preference; interpretation must
preserve unfamiliar communication rather than demand one approved medium.

If will cannot be communicated despite serious support efforts, a court may
authorize only specific asset-preserving acts grounded in the best available
interpretation of the person's own will/preferences. It cannot remove legal
capacity, choose a spouse, make a will, authorize treatment, alter a vote or
grant general substitute discretion. Protect existing necessary payments and
access; no blanket account freeze. A pre-court emergency hold is at most
48 continuous hours, limited to an identified imminent asset loss, with
immediate court access and no administrative self-renewal.

The first court order lasts at most 14 days, with supported participation
and independent advice as soon as possible; any continuation needs fresh
specific reasons and an order of at most 90 days, with earlier challenge.
Separate custodian, independent advocate and court roles; relatives or
vendors with interests cannot certify their own authority. Preserve source
evidence and accounts privately; end/revise promptly when direct choice is
available. No clock or communication failure supplies consent, and protective
custody of assets cannot become confinement of the person.

[UA01–UA20](../23-necessary-care-and-unknown-will.md) now supplies a separate
proposed narrow residual source for necessary personal/care acts when actual
choice/authority does not resolve the act. B23 itself still authorizes assets
only. Actual refusal/opposition, independent advocacy and agency remain protected;
new consequential bodily acts require UA's own adopted grounds and court process.

[YC09–YC12](../../C07/27/03-voluntary-transition-and-succession.md)
assigns paid voluntary transition and accessible return to support
after child care. B21 adult agency controls on the eighteenth birthday.
