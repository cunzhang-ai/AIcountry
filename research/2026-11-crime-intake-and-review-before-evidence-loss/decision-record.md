# Design250 decision record. Crime intake and review before evidence loss

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned Design249](baseline.json)

## Decision and diagnosis

Select [OJ49–OJ54](../country/C08/25/11-report-to-investigation-and-review.md).
Make first receipt, known evidence deadlines, actual safety support,
investigative decisions and independent review a single accountable path.
Retain separate police, prosecution, adjudication, defense and support
authority. Apply the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md)
and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

P06 already supplies protected receipt and closure review. A person can
still encounter an unassigned report, a silent hold or a missed expiring
lead before receiving a formal decision. A review after loss of the
evidence cannot deliver the original opportunity. Some cases have no
reasonable remaining lead and should close with reasons. Inadequate
staffing or other avoidable failure needs its own account, not a
merits label. People exposed to repeated harm or unable to reach a
digital channel need timely support and a safe complaint path. The
accused, witness and unrelated people retain their own rights.

## Compare coherent alternatives

| Choice | Strong reason | Material exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Preserve ordinary intake with review after formal closure | Lower standing review demand and deference to proportionate investigators. | Silent nonassignment can evade review and allow material evidence to disappear. |
| Give every report a full mandated investigation before closure | Offers an easily stated promise of equal treatment. | Cannot guarantee meaningful leads. It could displace urgent cases, encourage intrusive collection and create fake compliance in thinly staffed services. |
| Keep reasoned proportionate inquiry and a protected useful-time review path | Can address failures before loss while allowing defensible closure. | Needs paid separate staff, genuine independent access, case prioritization and effective lawful preservation. A false deadline or overcollection can still harm people. |
| Prioritize likely charges or high measured clearance | Can increase the number of visible outcomes with given staff. | Rewards easy evidence and can suppress hard reports, contrary evidence or necessary safety work. |

Select the third design. The 24-hour outer decision time for credible
expiring leads, seven-day status and 14-day ordinary review with one
bounded extension are proposed parameters. Earlier actual useful time
and other stronger law prevail. None gives a new evidence power, a
charge, a tolling of legal limits or automatic liability. Test their
real workload before adoption. The access and remedies require
separate usable investigators and reviewers.

In the [authored comparison](../settlement/crime-intake-review/02-three-reports-one-short-window.md)
select reasonable early action on two expiring leads, retain the next
window for stable evidence and independently fund a limited alternate.
The same priority cannot manufacture capacity when both routes fail.
Support proceeds on its own lawful grounds.

## Evidence, authority and migration

The [reported outcomes](../settlement/crime-intake-review/01-reported-outcomes-and-what-they-show.md)
illustrate case-mix differences. They establish neither a wrongful closure
rate nor a causal benefit of the proposed protection. Official inquiry
and review guidance supplies a procedural comparator. The scenario
models staff and deadline arithmetic. It supplies no observed safety,
deterrence, public approval or guilt.

Adoption requires express lawful duties and review authority, source
and privacy safeguards, protected funding and accepting police,
prosecution, support and court roles. Use the outside-command route
if the ordinary chain is implicated. Maintain original receipt,
liberty, evidence, limitation and appeal dates and existing claims
across staff, record and vendor transfer. Actual affected participation,
independent review, enactment, paid receiving and outcomes remain open.

## Next bounded country decision

Within an actual approved casework and oversight ceiling, compare an
ordinary intake with a timely independent alternative on one protected
sample of complaints. Follow one credible expiring lead, one genuinely
exhausted report and one affected person with urgent needs from receipt
through actual action. Inspect original deadline and handoff evidence
safely, alongside defense, privacy and workload effects. Record received
action or a named lawful owner and source for each missed duty. Reopen
for avoidable lost evidence, biased nonrecording, intrusive preservation,
overload, compromised reviewers or a simpler equally timely route.
If independent records are unavailable, disclose that limit and retain
individual access to existing protection.
