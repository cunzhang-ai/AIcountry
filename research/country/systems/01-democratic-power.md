# Democratic power and lawful force

*Integrated design 77 research map; the linked topic files contain the current detailed choices. This map does not enact law.*

| Public question | Selected operating answer | Detailed design |
|---|---|---|
| Who can rule, and who can remove them? | An elected Assembly controls law, tax and spending; a replaceable cabinet governs within law; opposition, courts and distributed election evidence protect a peaceful transfer. | [legislature](../C03/01-legislature-and-delegation.md) · [opposition and handover](../C03/03-opposition-courts-and-handover.md) |
| Who makes equal standing enforceable across public and private power? | A common civil equality floor, sector correction, independent cross-sector inquiry and direct court access connect accommodation and evidence to executed remedies. The inquiry service cannot finally adjudicate its own case or monopolize complaints. | [rights and differences](../C02/05-equal-standing-and-differentiated-needs.md) · [enforcement](../C08/11-equality-law-and-effective-remedies.md) · [capacity](../C06/17-equality-and-access-finance.md) |
| Can a challenger actually compete against an incumbent's money and state machinery? | Prospective law gives qualified newcomers usable baseline access, exposes material finance and paid reach, bars diversion of public assets, and routes urgent repair to independent courts without giving a finance office power to ban lawful opposition. | [campaign access](../C03/07-political-finance-and-campaign-access.md) · [finance](../C06/11-democratic-contest-finance.md) · [hostile-incumbent test](../C13/14-incumbent-campaign-capture.md) |
| What if the elected Assembly cannot agree on a government? | Majority investiture is tried first; a publicly supported minority candidate can then govern only if no full majority rejects them. A full majority can dismiss a cabinet, and repeated formation failure returns to voters under prior law. The outgoing cabinet has narrow caretaker powers while existing services and rights continue. | [government formation](../C03/06-government-formation-and-caretaker.md) · [continuity](../C06/04-shortfall-and-continuity.md) |
| Can elected decision-makers actually handle all the powers reserved to them? | Prior law routes local, interlocal, national, delegated and urgent choices by affected scope; an Assembly capacity account exposes combined queues and forces an elected response to harmful backlog without transferring power by delay. | [legislative capacity](../C03/05-assembly-capacity-and-scope.md) |
| Can this political form work at the country's actual scale? | The national Assembly/local-council arrangement is conditional: before a first election, a legitimate founding public compares it with an elected regional tier and issue-scoped joint services using real representation, travel, resource and decision-capacity evidence. A failed reference gives no extra power to cabinet or AI. | [national form](../C14/05-scale-and-national-form.md) · [scale exercise](../C13/06-scale-and-representation-stress.md) |
| How can distinct peoples and places share political power? | Personal cultural rights, existing collective claims, local democracy and a reviewable route to negotiate territorial status coexist; neither a national majority nor a local ballot alone erases others’ rights or changes sovereignty. | [rights](../C02/01-personal-and-collective-rights.md) · [territorial status](../C04/02-territorial-self-government.md) |
| Who may use force or decide a crisis? | Prospective law, separate civilian institutions and independent review govern ordinary justice and protection; elected and constitutional limits constrain exceptional power. | [coercive power](../C08/04-coercive-power-and-official-abuse.md) · [war powers](../C10/03-defense-intelligence-and-war.md) |

The [criminal core](../C08/12-criminal-core-and-procedure.md) separates
prospective harm/fault, independent defense and trial from immediate protection.
Custody cannot reset through transfer or care labels; quotas or pursuing-office
fine income cannot substitute for safety, lawful proof or executed remedies.

The [civil core](../C08/13-civil-obligations-and-executed-remedies.md)
keeps independent public challenge, source evidence and actual execution
reachable against private and state defendants; a judgment is not receipt.

[V01–V12](../C08/19-regulation-and-bounded-enforcement.md) bounds inspectors,
licenses, urgent stops and punitive money. Independent proof/courts, safe
receiving and elected public-risk decisions prevent regulatory labels from
creating a second sovereign or an automatic punishment route.

[CD conflict custody](../C08/21-conflict-detention-and-release.md) uses narrow
POW/actual neutrality grounds, independent civilian liberty review and actual
release. Command cannot own its final review/source chain. Existing humane
and remedy duties survive an election or vendor change; they neither renew a
war mandate nor create general civilian/NIAC internment. [C10 control](../C10/08-conflict-custody-and-civilian-control.md)
requires practical opposition and independently funded executors.

[TS technical safety](../C08/22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md)
keeps material public risk choices and money with elected law, bounded detail
with qualified issuers and contested guilt with courts. Independent source/
assignment/funding and opposition scrutiny must remain usable even for public
operators; neither insurer nor AI becomes a sovereign gatekeeper.

[CP national reconciliation](../C06/28-national-commitments-and-residual-scarcity.md)
now joins corrected productive support, enacted new-service/accrual dates and
narrow residual public-stock powers. Existing rights/elections/remedies retain
their own sources; expert/AI evidence cannot authorize an unproved resource,
indefinite readiness delay or self-renewed emergency mandate.


[PC continuous political finance](../C03/12-continuous-political-finance.md) adds
year-round/pre-call limits and organizing credits, actual-local/plural referendum
access, AI/service control evidence and exact restrained enforcement. Ordinary
issue/unpaid speech remains distinct. New public source and prospective law are
required; practical opposition, safe privacy and independent receipt are unproved.


[EL equal franchise and usable elections](../C03/13-equal-franchise-and-usable-elections.md)
now selects one national PR/list seat rule and local whole-area councils, with
real home/date/source correction, optional preferences, receipt and lawful
succession. Direct territorial-person mandates/ward guarantees are sacrificed;
party/rounding/field risks remain. Old mixed/price tests are not current proof.


[LR local general-law control](../C03/14-local-law-and-public-control.md) adds
actual full-n rule votes, affected input/agenda, prior-source72h urgency, precise
publication/expiry and local payer/outside execution. National rights and local
competence remain distinct; a new registry proves no actual independence or relief.

[AX public dissent](../C03/18-peaceful-assembly-and-public-dissent.md) joins
between-election physical/future assembly, actual forums and usable expression
with existing electoral opposition. Neither viewpoint hostility, absent notice,
accounts/agent copies nor a police/court order creates guilt or a mandate.
Bounded acts need actual law, paid independent receiving and specific remedies.
