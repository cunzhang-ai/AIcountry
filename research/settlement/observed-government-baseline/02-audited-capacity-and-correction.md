# Same-year outturn and two documented failures of capacity

## What the financial and operational audits found

The national audit office's August 2026 report 19/2025 assessed the
2025 central state accounts as free from material misstatements and
appropriations as generally respected. It made exceptions, including
roughly DKK 291 million in railway overspending without authorization.
This was an opinion about the state accounts, with a distinct perimeter
from OFF3's consolidated general government. Financial audit did not
supply a finding of reliable service. The State Accounts agency
reported that the accounts were presented to Parliament on 26 June
2026 and placed final parliamentary consideration after the final
public accounts committee report expected in February 2027.
[Auditor's report](https://www.uk.rigsrevisionen.dk/audits-reports-archive/2026/aug/report-on-the-audit-of-the-danish-public-accounts-for-2025) · [State Accounts agency](https://oes.dk/statsregnskab/statens-regnskaber/statsregnskabet/statsregnskabet-2025/)

Report 20/2025, section 4.1, found that 730 of roughly 1,900 prison
officers averaged 35 twenty-four-hour duties in 2025, across 41 of
59 prisons. The auditors found the examined arrangements breached
rest rules. The report recorded DKK 28.5 million of compensation
paid across 2022 and 2025 for breaches of the separate 48-hour rule.
Do not book the whole amount as a 2025 cost or an estimate of
rest-compliant replacement. The October 2026 ministerial response
reported regulator-approved agreements covering all 41 sites from
15 August, with conditions on rest and recovery. It distinguished
the compensation from the use of twenty-four-hour duties. This
documents a reported later change in legal arrangements. It does
not show which later shifts met the conditions in practice.

Section 5.2 found missing recovery plans for two critical court
systems. One civil case system took 12 days to restore after accidental
deletion in February 2025, against the administration's accepted
28-hour system recovery interval. That duration measures technical
recovery, not the exact delay or harm in each case. The October
response reported a new operating contract with annual tests and
planned tests before full implementation in March 2028. The response
also identified limits on ministerial direction of the court
administration. It does not establish an executed recovery test or
which urgent claims received timely alternatives.
[Auditor's operational report, sections 4.1 and 5.2](https://www.rigsrevisionen.dk/Media/639225602093624025/sr2025.pdf) · [Justice minister's response](https://cdn.rigsrevisionen.dk/rigsrevisionen/Media/639286205594339469/20-2025-jm.pdf)

## First-budget choice in those functions

- **Custody.** Cost lawful ordinary shifts, relief and training with the
  existing corrections authority and workforce, preserving independent
  safety review and continuing custody claims. Compare a lawful negotiated
  schedule with actual protected rest, and a more conventional staff
  pattern with paid cover. Count only accepted, compatible shifts when
  testing either. Prior compensation and a scheduling tool do not
  provide safe staffing. Where no safe plan meets existing duties, retain
  open claims and elected source correction. Any release, placement or
  reduction in custody uses its own judicial and statutory authority.
- **Courts.** The independent court authority tests timely case intake,
  safe records and hearing and execution capacity under a shared failure.
  Compare actual retained secure recovery, a separate receiving path and
  a funded supplier replacement with paid overlap. Count the time of
  affected people and existing staff. An administrative dashboard or AI
  case aid can help trace omissions, with DP safeguards, while judicial
  decisions and protected access remain outside the operator's control.

**Select repair of each verified weak route and independently checked
capacity before depending on it for new duties.** A credible later
correction can replace a prior finding for its actual scope, with
the old claims and record preserved. The first budget identifies these
repair costs separately from any wider proposed change. They have not
been priced here. No new executive power or cross-sector budget
diversion follows.
