# Workload starts with delivered services, not an assigned headcount

*Part of [service-floor comparison](README.md); fictional people/times, not safe staffing advice.*

The reference population is 100,000: ages 0–2/3–5/6–17 are 3,000/3,000/
14,000; adults 80,000. These new age bands, needs and take-up are authored,
not inferred from old cohort evidence. Early-learning users and preschool
care users are independently 2,400, with overlap 2,400 in this case. Care
does not require learning participation. Under-3 care users are 1,800.

| Known service component | Annual role-hours |
|---|---:|
| Early learning: 200 groups × 20 hours × 39 weeks | 156,000 teacher |
| Foundational learning: 560 groups × 30 hours × 39 weeks | 655,200 teacher |
| Preparation at half those contact hours | 405,600 teacher |
| Individual learning support: 1,400 × 2 hours × 39 weeks | 109,200 support |
| Under-3 care: 300 groups × 2 carers × 40 hours × 48 weeks | 1,152,000 care |
| Preschool care outside/alongside learning, with valid joint supervision | 612,000 care |
| Adult assessed care: 5,000 × mean 8 hours × 52 weeks | 2,080,000 care |

Group sizes 12 early/25 school/6 under-3, two preschool/under-3 adults and
all durations are fictional. National rounding is no local roster, safety
standard or adequate transport. At 1,200 declared allocatable hours/FTE,
care alone needs 3,844,000 hours against 2,700,000 in the old 2,250-FTE
public backbone: a normal gap 1,144,000. A further 10% unexpected absence
raises it to 1,414,000. Unknown accredited private capacity cannot fill it
by assumption. Other care/individual high needs remain unmeasured.

The new education split 1,978 teachers/100 support/200 other totals the old
2,278 reference FTE, without certifying real skills. After 10% absence its
aggregate hours exceed shown learning work, but support alone lacks 1,200
hours. Unmeasured adult/advanced/admin tasks mean no spare workforce is proved.
Preparation is inside the 1,200-hour workload; ordinary non-task allowances
are outside it. Do not subtract the same allowance twice.

During a genuine joint preschool episode, a qualified teacher plus a carer
provide learning and safe supervision together. Outside learning hours,
two qualified carers provide care. Actual worker hours total 768,000,
including 156,000 teacher contact and 612,000 care. Buying the full care
team and a separate full learning team for those same periods would count
1,080,000 hours, double booking 312,000. This advantage requires actual
dual quality/safety; overlapping labels alone cannot establish compatibility.
The 4,608,000 child-care hours include 1,872,000 child-learning hours; they
are distinct outcomes, not additive unique child-time or twice the wellbeing.

Likewise, 300,000 primary episodes include 30,000 mental and 20,000
reproductive labels, with overlap 5,000. Count base clinical work once,
then distinct specialized increments and documentation: general/notes
120,000 hours, mental 12,500, reproductive 6,666.67 and clerical 30,000.
Different professional roles are retained, not collapsed into one generic
clinician. Specialized increments/notes require actual evidence and skill;
shared episode identity does not make their work free. Other clinical work
is unknown, not spare capacity inside the national 3,445-FTE heading.
