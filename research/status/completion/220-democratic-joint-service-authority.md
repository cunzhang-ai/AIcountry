# Democratic joint-service authority

Design312 · Scoped result for [country completion](../../DESIGN-COMPLETENESS.md)

[JS01–JS05](../../country/C04/01/01-democratic-joint-service-authority.md)
gives councils a defined vote for material operating choices within a
joint service. Existing national powers, nonmember rights, continuing
duties and elected replacement remain independently enforceable.

The [bus comparison](../../settlement/joint-service-authority/01-bus-network-and-democratic-control.md)
reproduces the two thresholds, an outside levy beyond the compact and
two approvals competing for the same vehicle and budget. The design
can still deadlock. Actual legitimate authority, review capacity,
paid receiving and public outcomes remain open.
