# A mature operating year needs an earlier paid transition

The 2030 full-use reference depends on capacity available before its first use.
A separate authored 2029 schedule shows thirty/seventy-percent capital-import
milestones and initial stock/fill/charge by assumed acceptance. It is not evidence
that construction, safety, supplier delivery or training can finish on those dates.
If acceptance/source fails, withdraw the full-use claim and preserve existing
rights/interim protection; no readiness statement delays a due right or election.
Actual prestart interest, fees, overlap, working cash and source are unprovided.
The current interest calculation uses new-asset quote principal only, not a
complete opening debt or capitalized-cost reconciliation.

Food, water and power each have their own normal import, new asset import,
initial stock, ownership, payer and actual acceptance question. The firm portfolio's
normal named imports plus the two disjoint private goods total 127.289 million FU/year.
That is neither full national demand nor a common currency pool, available source
or lower bound on actual need. Housing, medicine, unpriced inputs/foreign debts,
seasonality, tax and many other calls remain; CUcash is not accepted foreign money.
Uniform monthly imports are a payment sensitivity, not a seasonal supply proof.

Keep retained 322 million CU in physical assets separate from new construction and old liabilities.
The four thousand retained public-home title quote is not another new 600 million CU purchase.
The proposed station pool is an actual new-capital call in its own hypothetical
scope—26.06 million CU in the firm/plural reference—not paid merely because future renewal
is budgeted. Real overlap, title and equipment compatibility must be qualified.
Do not re-add the retired generic network or assume an earlier proposed asset was built.

Housing programme 181.098 million CU includes paid instruction. The joint workers' salary
is allocated by actual reference rebuild/repair hours, not duplicated into two
separate full crews. The 72.441 million CU rebuild investment-call reference is distinct from
108.657 million CU repair, temporary accommodation and learning. This is not certified asset
capitalization; the other-cost/energy scope remains a hypothesis. Electricity
is inside the old other-cost envelope only in the explicit included case; if not,
its bill is added and the source comparison changes. No unmatched cost vanishes.

A structure-share hypothesis assigns public programme scope to 4,000 of 75,000 titles,
not the occupied-home fraction or free property. Public rebuild calls draw once
from the separately funded housing renewal reserve; repair/temporary/learning
uses have their separate operating source. Private owners' rents, repair charges,
insurance and renewal cannot be pooled without title, consent and matched claims.
No compulsory sale, informal labor or new acquisition authority is selected.

Four-percent new-asset interest affects prices. Twenty-year equal principal adds
19.825 million CU annual cash in the firm portfolio and is not another production cost.
Holding full renewal cash alongside repayment is a separate conservative cash
policy, not an automatic law: using it for debt service carries future replacement
obligations and needs actual authorization. Equity, grants or different loan terms
have different control/return claims, not free resources. None is actually supplied.

Appropriation, commitment, delivery and payment must be distinguished; loan finance
and principal are not ordinary revenue/expense. These classifications inform this
separation, without certifying the model as national accounts. [IMF GFSM2014, selected paragraphs 3.59,4.20,4.23–4.26,9.44](https://www.imf.org/external/pubs/ft/gfs/manual/2014/gfsfinal.pdf)
Neither an accounting manual nor a reserve label determines the country's preferred
allocation, authorizes money issue or proves material/foreign capacity.
