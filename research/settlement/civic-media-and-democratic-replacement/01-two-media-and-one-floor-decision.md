# Replace a civic medium without changing the choice

*Design458 · Constructed deliberation and floor example.*

A 12-seat resident panel compares two eligible alternatives for an
accessible water collection service. The panel can secure one protected
elected floor slot. It neither makes the service law nor appropriates
money. The existing RDA process requires three verified designations for
qualification, one quarter of the commissioned seats rounded up.
The highest verified support gets the slot. A verified tie goes to
the published auditable lot. Each panel seat has one designation.
An exact later elected vote, a lawful budget and delivery still follow.

The first mediator offers a spatial demonstration and assisted speech.
The second offers a written map and direct human interpretation.
One community authored A to include a staffed mobile return route for
people unable to cross a slope after dark. B proposes an accessible
fixed receiving site with its own route. A compressed rendering
removes A's return route, while the other presentation preserves it.
A privately held demonstration can establish this difference for a
competent reviewer. Neither provider acquires access to the person's
full movements, private reasoning or unrelated evidence.

## Original receipts, replacement and the cutoff

At the initial cutoff, independently verified support is A four,
B three, and three abstentions. Two other eligible participants
encountered the materially defective A rendering. One submitted
an affected designation and another used the direct clerk's
accessible intake after the presentation prevented a usable choice.
Neither establishes support on the exact choice yet. The old
provider's export omits the direct intake and a minority objection.
A replay from a copied agent repeats a verified A designation and
arrives after the relevant mandate was revoked. It carries no
additional designation and does not erase the earlier valid act.
The public receiver already has the date and scoped original receipt.
The reviewer can verify the omission without the former provider's
approval. Keep the minority objection separately available even
if the eventual lead cannot change.

| Choice at the deadline | Effect on the floor slot and other work |
|---|---|
| Treat migrated counts and absence as authoritative | Apparently A leads 4–3. The apparent result hides the two affected opportunities and the objection. It cannot qualify the slot. |
| Preserve verified acts and restore both affected opportunities | If both choose B with the corrected terms, B leads 5–4. If one chooses B and one abstains, the verified result is 4–4 and the published lot applies. If both abstain, A leads 4–3. |
| One participant confirms B and one remains unreachable | Observed designations are 4–4. The unresolved choice can change the disposition. This is not a verified tie. Hold the slot on the original process. Record whose choice remained unavailable. |
| Both choose A through the alternate route | A leads 6–3. The original seven verified designations and three abstentions remain. No panel designation becomes an elected vote. |

The next provider's convenience copy cannot supply the missing
meaning. The direct clerk and separately assigned reviewer arrange
a qualified explanation of the original conditions, with two paid
interpretation appointments reserved in the correction window. A
separate lawful route for the objection preserves safe custody.
If both media rely on the same absent interpreter or owner,
two interfaces still supply at most the capacity actually accepted.
No person must publish private source material to regain standing.
The fixture assumes all other eligible business can proceed on its
separate lawful schedule. If the original slot expires, preserve
ordinary sponsorship and review and record the lost opportunity.

## When both communities still disagree after verification

The same affected area has two elected local authorities. Each
already maintains its own lawful route for residents and accepts
the replacement of its own medium. Both can describe the same
bridge and the same hour. Their independently verified understandings
of a joint route still differ. One community considers a return
journey part of the commitment. The other offers an outbound trip
and reserves its return capacity for existing service. This is a
substantive difference after correction, rather than a bad translation.
The communities may retain distinct interpretations and local plans.

| Scope | Decision and continuing duties |
|---|---|
| Existing local trips | Continue on the competent councils' original authorities, accepted schedules and protected deadlines. Replacement of a mediator changes none of these commitments. |
| Separable common information | The competent bodies can publish an exact jointly authorized service notice with independent accessible renderings. It lists each existing route and its actual limits. It creates no new transport commitment. |
| Shared transport route | A new proposal would need one exact legally operative scope, distinct approved budgets and an accepted roster. An outbound-only motion must explain how the return need is met and preserve ordinary duties. One common vehicle and driver are already reserved for a protected trip in that window. Two local agent promises cannot allocate the same unit or displace that trip. |
| Difference remains unresolved | The competent joint or elected route may consider a newly funded, exact return-inclusive proposal. Until then keep the shared new trip unstarted, record the unresolved return need and preserve the direct challenge and existing service routes. The lawful floor and budget rules still apply. |

Before the replacement both local renderings describe the trips
accurately. The first agenda groups the return objection under local
access and the second shows only transport additions. The second
forum therefore never receives a promised material reply about the
shared route. Preserve the selected objection and its original reply
clock. The competent public offices give both forums scoped notice,
useful time to answer and the competing versions. Correct grouping
or visibility only where it impaired the shared opportunity.

A joint human reconciliation can state the exact overlap and the
remaining alternatives, with one accessible presentation from each
community. It does not speak for either local authority or resolve
the transport allocation. Publish the narrow shared meaning and
each material objection with safe attribution. If new terms change
who pays, rides or provides a return, they are a new decision under
the existing notice, approval and acceptance rules. A lawful scoped
notice can proceed while the shared trip remains unresolved.

## A fair comparison of institutional forms

Use independent public receipt and scoped correction with plural
media. This costs two interpretation appointments, accessible intake
and a reviewer who can see the original meaning. The ten unimpeded
participants need no renewed affirmation. A full rehearing would
take twelve participant appointments on these assumptions and can
be warranted if the impairment is widespread or the initial source
cannot be safely reconstructed. A staffed direct hearing could
avoid provider dependence and retain contextual understanding.
It is a live rival if reachable and affordable. A single certified
mediator could reduce transfer effort but would still need independently
reachable original evidence and challenge. Compare actual comprehension,
missed participants, payer and scheduling burdens and retained dissent.

If the alternative procedure itself changes who can qualify, it
requires competent prospective adoption. This exercise restores the
affected choice using the existing rules. It supplies no budget
or delegated power to the panel, the providers or their agents.
