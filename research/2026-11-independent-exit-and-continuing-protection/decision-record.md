# Design475 decision record

**Baseline and method.** Use Design474, the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md)
and [baseline hashes](baseline.json). This is a prospective research
choice under existing kinds of housing, civil, status and local law.

**Selection.** Prefer independent tenure where compatible with the job.
For genuine on-site need, provide confidential planning before departure
and test a finite independent option where ordinary routes cannot offer
a real choice. Keep current after-exit protection, review and safe
receiving. Treat coercion of an ordinary lawful choice or claim through
an affiliate's independently due service as a narrowly scoped civil
wrong under adopting law. Require proof of actual conditional pressure,
not mere common control. Use existing court procedures and actual
funded relief.

**Rivals and objections.** Reactive search can avoid idle commitments;
it may still leave departure impractical during a critical window.
An independent tenure and ordinary service route avoids the linkage
when it is actually viable. Reserving scarce public supply may reduce
other people's access. Require H07 priority, qualified capacity,
transparent opportunity cost and limited paid expiry. The precise
scope of exceptional options needs elected funding, public discussion
and qualified review.

**Continuity and correction.** Keep actual wages, residence rights,
notice, care and legal relief on their existing clocks. Preserve
confidential contact and accepting staff. An inquiry starts no status
or insurance clock and grants no mandate. No new disclosure authority
or remedy follows from a shared record. Reopen on privacy failure,
displaced claims, lost receiving or a less costly workable route.
[Sources](sources.md) distinguish primary context and authored tests.
Affected participation, real operations and better lives remain open.
