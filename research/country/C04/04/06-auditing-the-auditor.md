# AA26–AA30: the audit office cannot certify its own independence

*Part of [C04/04](../04-public-audit-and-accountable-oversight.md); design121 candidate.*

**AA26 — A separately selected external examiner.** Assembly recruitment/
procurement staff run C04 open transparent competence/conflict procurement for
an external examiner of the audit office, independently paid from the Assembly
source, not by AG discretion, private sponsor or recovered fines. A qualified
provider/named responsible professionals need actual public-audit/accounts/
privacy competence, no AG/audited-office management or materially dependent
consulting/client/control ties. Publish terms/criteria/eligible offer reasons,
source, conflicts and objections before award; open offers at least 30 calendar
days. AA06/B16 material adverse reply/disposition and fresh necessary source
vote apply; 21-day award notice cannot shorten them. No sole guild or AI certificate.
After at least 21 calendar days' proposed-award notice, appointment needs more
than half full statutory N and ceil(O/3) of the AA07 frozen non-government cohort;
O=0 supplies no new appointment. Record actual contract price/source and direct
B court procurement/criteria challenge. This distinct source is not proof that
Assembly patrons, providers or payment operators are independent in practice.

**AA27 — Finite external work and no adverse-finding dismissal.** Provider mandate
is three years, with no consecutive renewal by the same controlled provider
and at least three years' separation before another fresh lawful award. Publish
actual control/lead changes, not a renamed firm/partner reset. Preplanned annual financial audit
and at least one selection/quality/control review in the mandate cover the audit
office, safe source access and actual deliverables. A replacement fills only the
remaining mandate after the same general procurement/votes; open planned succession 180 calendar
days before expiry, unexpected replacement within 14 days. No automatic holdover
or privately appointed case friend. Termination needs proved material misconduct,
conflict/incapacity or persistent unjustified contract failure, notice/reply,
Assembly recorded reasons and direct independent review; disagreement with findings
is no cause. Protected procurement/worker claims survive. Expiry without a paid
qualified successor leaves missing external assurance, never AG self-certification.

**AA28 — Publish the office's own accounts, review and reply.** External examiner
reports directly to Assembly/public with AA18/AA21–AA22 safe reply/publication,
not the AG's approval. Actual unconflicted professionals, original source and
paid time are required, not another account under the same controller. Publish
the office budget/spending/coverage, external opinion/quality limits and AG's
AA24 response. No external examiner judges its own fees/conflict dispute or gains
compulsory criminal powers. Its own alleged wrongdoing uses unimplicated ordinary
courts/prosecutors and procurement/source law, with actual party defense.

**AA29 — Civil, status and criminal own-office cases.** AG appointment/removal/
personal pay and audit-office budget legality/retaliation use the expressly
assigned AA09 OJ16 three/five ordinary status route, not CJ12 by analogy. Other
record/report/contract/staff/affected claims use their actual ordinary B/OJ/WL
forums and periods. Exclude real selectors, actors and personal interests;
a court having been audited or universally named is not alone disqualified.
Actual direct all-judge conflicts retain OJ19/CJ12 limits, prior visiting-source
requirements or visible absence of a lawful final decider. Suspected audit crime
goes to unimplicated P/K investigators/prosecutors; use OJ29 preselected external
case officers only where the prosecution command is actually implicated, not
for every auditor complaint or a newly hired loyal prosecutor.

**AA30 — Preserve normal public criticism and protection.** Safe reports about AG,
staff, examiner or a minister can reach lawful courts, independent reporting,
Assembly and prosecution without the accused's permission. First receiver assists
an ordinary account and protects existing urgent sources/people; neither court,
audit nor support waits for a criminal conviction. Apply actual anti-retaliation,
source/whistleblower/privacy and defense law; no published allegation establishes
guilt or universally cancels all audits. Staff/complainants may organize and
challenge the design, assumptions and mediator. Missing every independent channel
is constitutional failure in practice, not rescue by a second institutional label.

[WB](../../C08/31-protected-disclosure-and-evidence.md) supplies the
common civil protection and outside route for reports involving the
auditor. AA and ordinary courts retain their separate mandates and
the duty to verify actual delivery.
