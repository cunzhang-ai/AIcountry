# C03. Resident deliberation and elected response

*Design278 · RD01–RD05 · Proposed ordinary national procedure.*

Residents need a way to turn lived experience into an alternative that
elected representatives must address. Use a temporary, paid panel for a
bounded choice with genuine room to change. Keep standing support in the
Assembly service. Voters retain equal electoral standing and representatives
remain answerable for the decision.

**RD01 — Open one decision with time and money to change it.** Any member may
move to commission a panel through existing Assembly agenda rights. A public
initiative may seek the same decision through [NI](16-public-national-initiatives.md)
and retains its protected hearing and disposition. A recorded Assembly vote
sets the affected scope, lawful decision-maker, live baseline, question,
participant and work caps, available cash, dates and protected floor slot.
The initial reference allows one new ordinary national panel per legislative
year. Publish declined requests and the reason for the selected priority.
The report is due within 90 days of opening and the floor disposition within
60 days of receipt or the report deadline, whichever comes first. Existing
earlier deadlines govern. Set the opening and reserve a usable floor slot
before drawing, leaving enough time to compare the options.
Existing petitions, statutory hearings, [FA](../C12/03-finite-attention-and-lawful-closure.md)
consultation, emergency, budget and constitutional routes retain their own
clocks, powers and paid capacity. An elected body may act earlier where duty
requires and must record the effect on the commissioned question.

**RD02 — Pay for a fair chance to take part.** The Assembly service publishes
the lawful resident frame, coverage gaps, invitation and replacement method,
support offer and material gaps that would disqualify a result before the draw.
Use voluntary, auditable random invitations and a predeclared stratified draw
among respondents. Use replacements from the same declared strata. Budget and
advance reasonable pay, leave support, travel, care, access, interpretation
and offline participation. Keep refusal private and safe. Report nonresponse,
attrition and missed coverage without exposing individuals. Provide a funded
route for affected people outside the draw, including children, cross-border
residents or people missing from registers where relevant. That route carries
their accounts and objections under their own names or protected attribution.
Keep gaps visible in both routes. A material gap requires timely supported
repair or an explicitly unqualified report. Panel membership gives no mandate
from other residents and creates no electoral eligibility.

**RD03 — Give participants control of their account.** The Assembly service
procures facilitation under published terms, discloses controller interests
and names a funded substitute before opening. The panel can challenge its
question's framing within the commissioned scope, request evidence and
counter-advice from separately controlled sources, and seek replacement of a
conflicted facilitator from the Assembly service, subject to RD05 review.
Material refusals receive public reasons with protected source review. Members
retain their independent [PE evidence route](21-parliamentary-evidence-and-independent-access.md).
Use a common accessible evidence record and allow speech, written accounts,
site visits or other methods suited to the question. No platform is required.
AI may assist at participants' option with interpretation, translation or
drafting. Participants can correct the rendering and use a human alternative.
Preserve provenance, disagreement and originals under appropriate custody.
An AI has no seat or vote and cannot score residents, select submissions or
attribute assent. Protect sensitive testimony and independent organization.

**RD04 — Require an elected choice on exact text.** By the report date publish
the baseline and one implementable recommendation with costs, distribution,
dependencies and unresolved harms. Record the actual panel votes, abstentions
and material minority alternative or reasons in the members' own terms.
A qualified recommendation receives a protected recorded floor disposition on
its exact version. A deficient or missing report receives a recorded procedural
disposition at the reserved slot, with the gap and any remedial action named.
The Assembly votes on taking forward the exact text or rejecting it before
voting on a separately identified revision. It may first refer one specific
question for repair with a dated return. A referral must return for final
disposition within the original deadline. Keep the original recommendation and objections available.
Record reasons and the vote of each representative. A disposition neither
appropriates money nor substitutes for enactment, permits or other authorities.
A valid appropriation still needs funded, accepting delivery. Publish the
legally competent body's decision, the actual appropriation and a dated,
independently checkable delivery and outcome record. Identify pre-existing
actions, changed actions and unverified claims separately.

**RD05 — Restore lost access while a choice remains.** The implementing law
assigns the Assembly service paid custody, administrative correction and a
published successor. Residents and members may ask the service for immediate correction and
seek prompt independent review in the competent ordinary court for breached
statutory selection, access, source or scheduling duties. The named service
officer and Assembly presiding officer execute any order within their duties.
A lawful remedy restores participation, a record or a floor opportunity.
It leaves the decision and ordinary legislative votes to their lawful bodies.
If support, review or floor access cannot be restored in time, publish the
failure, keep existing duties and do not infer agreement. Fund and finish
opened cases and earned participant payments on their original terms. Review
the first case against a staffed ordinary hearing with paid affected access.
Continue, narrow or discontinue future commissions by prospective public
decision. Check participant burden, new information, effects on the decision
and actually received change before expanding.
