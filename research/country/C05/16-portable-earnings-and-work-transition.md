# Portable earnings protection and voluntary work transition

Design168 · EB01–EB16 · [C05 economy/work](../C05-economy-and-work.md)

**Select a finite, person-paid earnings-transition bridge alongside the existing
I05/service floor.** It follows the person through genuine short-time, job loss
and new work; a firm neither owns it nor decides the claim. This adds adjustment
time above/alongside needs protection, not a guarantee of a prior lifestyle,
permanent job, public training place or the country's first shock response.

exception and progressive-source intention do not prove that distribution fair.
