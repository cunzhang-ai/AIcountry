# Public authority, decisions and budgets

*Part of [Authority and legal transition](../AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

## 1. Authority begins with the people, under continuing rights

A legitimately established constitution grants and limits each public office.
The Assembly is the ordinary national lawmaker; people and members may propose
laws and organize opposition. The [C03 rule](../country/C03/06-government-formation-and-caretaker.md)
forms and dismisses cabinets; delay renews no mandate. Elected local councils
make local policy within national rights and delegated tax/service powers.
Courts decide disputes and protect rights without general legislative power.
Public officers and AI tools have only lawful delegated tasks.

Publication in the project research library is not promulgation of a statute. A
ballot, authorized vote, effective date and authenticated public legal register
have separate functions. No developer key, owner account, model verification or
research majority is sovereign. A valid enacted text needs a lawful authorizing
decision by the competent body, an exact final text, a public record, rights review
where applicable and an operative date. An independent publication office authenticates
the record; it cannot withhold an enacted law for policy reasons. A refusal or altered
publication is challengeable, with the original vote and signed text preserved.

The public can replace government and propose institutional change without AI approval.
Protected constitutional functions—equal political standing, meaningful opposition,
effective rights remedies and peaceful transfer—cannot be abolished through an
ordinary law, budget rider, procurement contract or emergency order. Their legal
forms may change through a legitimate constitutional route that preserves the
functions. A court may enforce that constraint under reviewable reasons; no court,
AI assurance office or political party becomes an irremovable guardian.

The reference design uses these ranks so a binding promise cannot change through
an easier instrument than the one that created it:

| Rank | What it secures | Who may change it, and what lower ranks cannot do |
|---|---|---|
| Legitimately founded constitutional charter | Equal political standing and protected electorate, genuine opposition and replacement, universal and minority rights and remedies, democratic legislative control, elected local existence, a lawful route for territorial-status claims, basic essential-access and ecological duties, non-sovereign AI, emergency and amendment limits | Only the valid protected amendment route; no statute, budget, contract or model update can disable the function by cumulative effect |
| Assembly framework statutes | Election formula and administration, service entitlements and responsible providers, taxation and currency/bank regime, legal procedure and force powers, shared resources, public evidence and technology, local competences and funding | Assembly legislation under constitutional rights, published reasons, affected-scope review and explicit transition; material incumbent-benefiting election changes cannot govern the next contest |
| Annual appropriation and elected local enactment | Cash authority and lawful local choices within statutory duties and competence | Elected bodies within higher law; a missing or reduced annual line does not repeal a standing claim or move an unfunded duty to another government |
| Regulations, case decisions and contracts | Bounded general technical rules, execution and individually reviewable applications under a statute and appropriation | Statute fixes content, purpose, scope, issuer, scrutiny and any subdelegation; material rules need affirmative Assembly approval, routine rules have public notice and elected call-in, and urgency has a prior-law expiry; no silent eligibility, force, tax, resource or constitutional change through administration, AI or vendor terms |
| Research and public evidence | Proposals, observations and criticism with traceable versions and protected sources | Not law; publication, signatures or AI agreement do not create a mandate or transfer an existing right |

The charter would protect the *function* of an institution where replacement must
remain possible; it would not freeze the current reference ballot formula,
commission headcount, currency choice, benefit amount, provider mix,
statistics software or transport mode. The constitution's founding text must
itself define its electorate and outer residence condition; an ordinary
registration statute may administer and broaden access, not narrow the protected
class. Numerical and institutional wording remains a proposal until a real
founding public selects it. A constitutional social duty requires a reasoned,
funded and reviewable statutory delivery scheme, not a judicially invented tax
or an unlimited immediate promise from absent materials. Accrued statutory
claims survive budget lapse and institutional replacement under the applicable
law. An enacted change can revise future entitlements within protected rights,
with explicit transition rather than an invisible accounting redefinition.

## 2. An end-to-end public decision

| Stage | Competent actor | Binding output | Independent check or correction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agenda and analysis | Citizens, elected members, cabinet, local councils, experts and AI within mandates | Proposal and evidence, not law | Public alternatives, opposition agenda rights, source/interest disclosure and accessible participation |
| General law, tax and appropriation | Elected Assembly under constitutional voting rules | Exact enacted text and funding authority | Courts examine legality/rights; public and opposition can replace lawmakers |
| Executive and local policy | Cabinet and elected local councils within enacted law | Regulations, plans and lawful contracts | Assembly scrutiny, local elections, independent review and fiscal audit |
| Individual decisions | Competent agency officer or lawful adjudicator | Reasoned, reviewable case decision | Fair notice, access to material, timely independent remedy; automation is contestable |
| Commitment and payment | Authorized service officer, separate verifier, treasury and accountant | Recorded obligation, actual lawful disbursement and reconciliation | Audit of authority, cash, recipient receipt and delivered service |
| Legal remedy | Independent competent court or tribunal | Order with identified obligation and executor | Appeal and public compliance reporting with protected data |
| Rule correction | Assembly or constitutionally authorized people | Amended law, prospectively effective unless lawfully specified otherwise | No administrative or AI self-amendment; pending claims and dissent receive explicit treatment |

A claimant may seek urgent court protection directly where delay would defeat the
right. The court cannot conjure missing workers or lender funds. It can identify
breach, order action within lawful competence, preserve a claim, award lawful relief
and require the state to disclose and correct a resource failure. Treasury and the
responsible provider must report actual execution; a judgment unimplemented because
their controller blocks funds is a constitutional failure, not a completed remedy.

## 3. Taxation, budget and the route from rights to delivery

The Assembly alone enacts taxes, rates and exemptions within the constitution.
A local council may use a tax or rate only under clear national authorization and
local democratic procedure; a regulator cannot create a tax by changing a model
parameter. The revenue authority assesses and collects under published law, with
independent taxpayer challenge. The tax statute assigns national and local bases,
delegated local rate bands, shared receipts and an appealable equalization formula;
neither Treasury nor an AI service may alter them through a forecast. A taxpayer
has notice, accessible filing, reasoned assessment, proportionate inquiry,
privacy, timely refund and independent review. Currency and debt powers need distinct enacted
mandates, financing limits and public risk reporting. The working reference is
a conditional domestic currency, not a claim of adoption or a source of imports.
The Assembly sets monetary objectives and appointments by law; the monetary
authority operates within that mandate with public accounts, no general policy
veto and no automatic Treasury credit. A complete external-currency package
remains available for a legitimate founding choice.

Cabinet prepares a consolidated budget and a funded guarantee-and-delivery schedule.
The Assembly adopts the annual appropriation and any material tax or spending change.
It must see obligations, local funding gaps, cash timing, staffing, maintenance,
debt service, contingencies and alternatives. A treasury forecast is evidence,
not legal authority. Treasury executes the enacted schedule and releases funds
against lawful commitments; it cannot disqualify a person, delay an inconvenient
court order or reallocate another agency's ceiling by software rule.

Keep the treasury's four distinct controls: a valid appropriation, authorized
commitment, independent confirmation of delivery or eligibility, and payment plus
reconciliation. For urgent provisional assistance, the authorizing law may permit
a documented payment on stated provisional evidence before full later verification.
The payment is still within a delegated ceiling and audited after delivery; requiring
complete conventional documents before the need's due time would defeat that law.
One person, agent or contractor cannot create a supplier, approve, verify and pay
the same claim. The independent reviewer must be able to inspect protected evidence
without treasury or the challenged provider dictating the finding.

National government enacts essential guarantees and their funding responsibilities.
Local elected authorities choose lawful delivery methods and receive need-based
equalization; the national authority remains accountable for an unfilled legal floor.
An inadequate transfer is a challengeable public funding decision. It does not
authorize the locality to revoke a resident's right, or a court to compel an
uncontracted private supplier without law. Pending claims and accrued contract
payments remain liabilities when a ministry or provider changes.

## 4. Budget lapse: lawful power on day 91

Distinguish a standing authorization from the annual budget. The proposed general
law establishes a **continuing essential-obligations schedule** for specified
statutory benefits, staffed essential services, due judgments, existing lawful
contracts needed for continuity, and minimum election/court/audit operations. It
states eligibility, cost rules, responsible payer, due dates, permitted renewals,
public accounts and review. The Assembly may amend it through ordinary law subject
to constitutional rights. It cannot be silently cancelled by failing to vote on
an annual budget. It must be reviewed and forecast each year so continuation does
not become an invisible permanent budget.

A separately preauthorized bridge covers other **existing** indispensable operations
for up to the candidate's 90 days, using actual obligations due rather than a
monthly fraction of the prior budget. It cannot finance new programs, political
appointments or permanent contracts. By the first 30 days cabinet discloses the
gap and a successor proposal; by 60 days Assembly holds a recorded vote on a normal
budget or a distinct lawful continuation. The caretaker executive cannot renew the
bridge by relabeling it or declaring an emergency. Courts review rights-related
shortfalls and procedural abuse without acquiring a general tax power.

At day 91 the temporary bridge expires. **The previously enacted standing schedule
continues** for its defined obligations, while other activity requires a new lawful
appropriation. Accrued obligations do not vanish. [BLC](../country/C06/66-budget-lapse-and-paid-continuity.md) proposes an expressly preauthorised, separate settlement of eligible accrued bridge debts. This resolves the earlier missing
legal authority for core day-91 activity in the proposed design; it does **not**
prove enough cash, qualified staff, suppliers or fair service levels exist. If
financing fails, publish arrears and interruption before they occur, preserve claims
and the independent remedy, and require an Assembly decision on actual options.
No deadline converts silence into a new government or law. Standing entitlements
themselves are open to democratic amendment with applicable rights and transition
protections; they are not an untouchable fiscal veto over future voters. [DB ordinary budget method](../country/C06/60-democratic-budget-and-execution.md) adds a proposed current procedure, no present spending authority.
