# Design416. Protect both lawful policy change and career administration

[PSV01–PSV07](../../country/C04/03/05-career-service-and-democratic-direction.md)
sets who directs policy, controls merit selection, reviews functional
reorganization and restores actual service and execution. Ordinary
statutory, professional and local powers retain their legal scope.

[Worked change of government](../../settlement/career-service-and-democratic-direction/01-new-policy-and-continuing-service.md)
tests loss of access, delayed lawful rollout and shared staff.
Actual independent reviewers, funding and service performance remain open.
