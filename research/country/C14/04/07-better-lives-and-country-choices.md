# PCO22–PCO26. Use lived evidence to choose between funded country designs

*Design459 · Part of [public conditions](../04-public-conditions.md).*

**PCO22 — Compare the choice the public can still make.** For material
renewal or expansion, put the exact continuation, strongest feasible
repair and materially different delivery arrangement on a common
record under CS13–CS15. Use the same original eligible population,
rights, protected duties, dates and materially constrained resources.
Compare a credible alternative use of each common marginal budget
and scarce paid role. State genuinely different full costs and funding
when one option needs more. Include ownership, control, transition
and ordinary ongoing work. Explain and qualify any scope differences.
A cheaper start cannot conceal the people or obligations it leaves.

**PCO23 — Keep claims at the strength the evidence supports.** Give
each material outcome its own result, original denominator, uncertainty
and next useful date. Retain missed recipients, paid and unpaid time,
worker safety and independent challenge. Distinguish receipts from
longer-lived effects and association from causal attribution. Apply
PCO17–PCO21 when an alternative's outcomes are exposed to the
intervention. Use missing-outcome bounds where defensible. Overlapping
bounds may leave the comparative direction unresolved. Keep verified
harm and continuing legal protection visible even in that case.
No score can balance a verified legal breach against a benefit.

When a claimed improvement depends on services people must use together,
declare the original cohort requiring all relevant components and the
compatible period, places, coordination, accepted choice and duration
that make the combination usable. Publish component access and joint
usable access separately. Distinguish provider task completion from
actual receipt. Count a combined result only on evidence about the same
person and compatible route. Where privacy or incomplete records prevent
confirmation, report supported bounds or leave the joint result unknown.
Marginal counts for different populations, dates or need definitions
cannot establish overlap. Apply PCO09: use lawfully scoped local checks,
limited sampling or a person-held account where sufficient. Protect
original testimony, independent access and refusal. A statistical
comparison creates no combined entitlement, compulsory record or change
to separate rights, deadlines and remedies.

**PCO24 — Make options earn their next decision separately.** For each
proposed release show authority, eligible funds, simultaneous paid
capacity, independent challenge and actual delivery under the ordinary
and material adverse windows. Hold the dependent optional change
when its source or necessary protection fails. Preserve lawful
continuing claims and apply their remedies. An option that fits can
remain a genuine choice even when another is blocked. When qualified
options trade off different outcomes, publish who gains and who bears
the cost, with the uncertainty and forgone alternative. The competent
elected body chooses and gives reasons under PCO14 and applicable
budget law. A model, analyst or affected majority cannot decide an
individual's protected claim.

**PCO25 — Reopen affected comparisons when a result changes the choice.**
A material loss, corrected series or gain can change which exact
alternatives remain coherent. Pin the earlier evidence and decision.
Give each competent owner the affected scope, then reassess the
relevant complete rivals on the same revision. Keep qualified
unaffected results and outstanding claims. A new outcome definition
or horizon may be useful. Publish it beside the original comparison
and explain the bridge or why no bridge is valid. A revised headline
cannot erase the earlier measure, people or missed duty. A revised source or
delivery scope needs its own lawful disposition. Give opposition a
usable opportunity to present an exact funded alternative under
existing procedures. Apply the earliest applicable regression,
review or individual relief deadline. Preserve disputed meanings
and material dissent.

Before the scheduled disposition, the independent reviewer identifies
which exact operative clauses, affected groups and resources rely on a
disputed result or interpretation. Keep the original measure and any
qualified rival account available in an accessible and safe form.
Separate a disagreement about the strength of an option from proof of a
named legal release condition. The competent authority applies the
enacted condition and its review route, including PCO10 for the
operative series and lawful fallback. Where evidence of that condition
fails, hold the dependent unbound scope while considering separately
qualified repair or verification on its own vote. Where the disagreement
only concerns comparative merit, the elected body may decide among
lawful options and gives reasons on the disputed evidence. Preserve the
original review and relief clocks. Apply EV25–EV30 to affected uses,
including independent review of a shared AI or human rendering.
Check the meaning of materially relied-on events against original
provider and recipient evidence where a common tool may erase
differences such as booking, arrival and actual use.

**PCO26 — Keep narrower claims and real reversal available.** An
improvement in one scope can justify a bounded change. For wider
claims, require compatible evidence on continued protection, actual
lived outcomes, feasible resources and transition across the whole
affected area and horizon. Budget independent comparison, complaint
and a paid successor before renewal. Keep ordinary repair, narrower
scope or lawful reversal available with accrued rights and actual
receiving. AI may reconcile cohorts, compare assumptions and expose
sensitivity. Responsibility for evidence, spending, delivery and
public choice stays with the competent actors. Reopen when losses,
new evidence or a more effective whole rival change the selection.
