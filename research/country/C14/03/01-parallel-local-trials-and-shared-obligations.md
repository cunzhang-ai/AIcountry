# Parallel local trials with common obligations

*Design297 · LT01–LT05 · Proposed rules for the future country.*

**LT01 — Allow different local designs within the same rights floor.**
A competent elected local body can authorize a bounded service or
policy trial under its existing lawful powers and funded scope.
Name the baseline, intended change, people affected, ordinary
alternative, evidence, expiry, remedy and paying successor.
Where a different legal power is needed, obtain an express decision
from the authority that can grant it. An experiment does not
suspend national rights, elections, judicial protection or
protected ecological and service duties. Preserve genuine choices
for people who cannot leave or do not agree to participate.

**LT02 — Separate compatibility of ideas from compatibility of
commitments.** Keep each local proposal, affected scope, source
observations, disputed meaning and important objection legible.
Accept speech, demonstrations, written or simulated proposals and
human or AI assistance with original attribution and challenge.
A shared representation needs to carry the operative resource,
time, effect and authority. Record material meanings it cannot
carry. A technical merge, matching descriptions, new model or
agent replication cannot transfer permission, settle disagreement
or multiply rights to scarce resources. Keep distinct evidence of
communication, assent, authorization and actual execution.

**LT03 — Settle concurrent claims through the existing competent
route.** Assess shared water, staff, records, funds and effects on
nonparticipants against the same actual legal baseline. Count
original continuing duties first. Where lawful independent scopes
fit, allow them to proceed separately. Within existing powers,
affected councils may agree a dated nonoverlapping allocation
with actual permits and lawful owners. If a material residual
would impose a duty or burden beyond those powers, use the
[C04 interlocal route](../../C04/01-national-local-authority.md)
and the Assembly's existing final decision. Give downstream
communities and other affected people a usable hearing and
independent challenge. Compare lawful narrowing, sequencing,
separate funding, choosing one trial and keeping the ordinary
route. A first filing or an earlier proposal vote creates no
priority over someone else's obligation or a shared reserve.

**LT04 — Verify the actual scope again at release and material change.**
Keep each competent authorization, owned funds, physical inputs,
qualifications, separately due services and actual receiving
visible. A phased option needs accepted restoration or release
before its next use. Test the declared common loss. A forecast
benefit from a trial is not present capacity. An optional release
that no longer qualifies is held or narrowed only under an exact
lawful alternative. Preserve already bound contracts, claims,
worker pay and ongoing protection. Independent review can enforce
actual rights or stop unauthorized action under existing law.
It cannot award the missing interlocal budget or choose policy
through a technical ruling.

**LT05 — Transfer evidence with limits and close the experiment
without closing claims.** Record actual outcomes, failures,
comparability, coverage, dissent and remaining uncertainty.
Show changes in the comparison window and definitions. A local
gain supports a fresh decision elsewhere within its proven scope.
It is no authorization to scale, merge or continue automatically.
Assign funded restoration, records, contracts, pending cases,
individual remedy and independent verification on expiry or
replacement. People and workers keep their original rights and
review clocks. AI may reconcile schedules or flag conflicts,
without adjudicating scope, accepting external harm or deciding
whether a trial becomes law. Reopen on unresolved common harm,
loss of source, forced participation or a better matched rival.
