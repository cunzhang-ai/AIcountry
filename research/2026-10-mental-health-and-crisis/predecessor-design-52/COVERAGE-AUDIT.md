# Functional coverage audit of the country design

5 October 2026 · Integrated design 52 research audit; not a second country design

This is a check against the request to design the major functions needed to
run a country. **Specified** means the linked current chapter names a first-
order authority, delivery or resource path, challenge and continuity rule;
it does not mean a complete enacted code, funded capacity or observed success.
**Partial** means the linked chapters acknowledge a function but leave a
material institutional choice unassigned. The canonical choices remain in
[COUNTRY-DESIGN.md](COUNTRY-DESIGN.md) and its [C01–C14 chapters](country/README.md).
The [UN functions-of-government classification](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/cofog/revision)
supplies an [external expenditure-function cross-check](2026-10-end-of-life/function-crosswalk.md),
not a proof that every right or life transition works. A broad category can
hide a failed handoff.

This audit found a communications/freight omission addressed in the
[design-38 record](2026-10-communications-logistics/decision-record.md) and
three further responsibility gaps addressed in the [design-39
record](2026-10-service-boundaries/decision-record.md). The
[design-40 market-power decision](2026-10-market-power/decision-record.md)
adds practical economic exit and product-harm routes. The
[design-41 national-form decision](2026-10-national-form/decision-record.md)
qualifies the national/local architecture against real scale and alternatives.
The [design-42 formation decision](2026-10-government-formation/decision-record.md)
connects a fragmented Assembly to a limited caretaker and repeat-poll route.
The [design-43 life-course decision](2026-10-life-course-services/decision-record.md)
assigns separate health, school and care delivery and review duties.
The [design-44 sovereign-debt decision](2026-10-sovereign-debt/decision-record.md)
adds an elected route for debt distress, creditor claims and service continuity.
The [design-45 disaster-recovery decision](2026-10-disaster-recovery/decision-record.md)
joins immediate protection, local/national rebuilding authority and distinct
insurance, compensation and public-finance claims.
The [design-46 ecological-transition decision](2026-10-ecological-transition/decision-record.md)
joins an elected emissions path to distinct ecosystem floors, reliable
replacement capacity and the burdens carried by people and places.
The [design-47 public-health decision](2026-10-public-health/decision-record.md)
assigns ordinary prevention, warning, local access, data protection and
separate coercive authority before a crisis.
The [design-48 food-system decision](2026-10-food-systems/decision-record.md)
joins ordinary food supply, safe diets, household access, production and
ecological limits while keeping binding safety and scarcity powers separate.
The [design-49 political-competition decision](2026-10-political-competition/decision-record.md)
connects campaign money, usable challenger access, incumbent state-resource
misuse, paid reach and executable pre-vote remedies.
The [design-50 reproductive-life decision](2026-10-reproductive-life/decision-record.md)
connects bodily choice, accepting clinical care, protected records and
independent child status through contested adult claims.
The [design-51 animal-welfare decision](2026-10-animal-welfare/decision-record.md)
adds animal suffering as a distinct public concern with enforceable care,
inspection, food and ecological boundaries, and a costed transition.
The [design-52 end-of-life decision](2026-10-end-of-life/decision-record.md)
uses an external function check and a whole-person journey to connect
relief, patient choice, death records and survivors without merging powers.
Every row in this bounded function list has a first-order research route;
that does not prove the list exhaustive or that a person can use a service.
Context-specific
statutes for air, sea, rail and specialized product classes require valid
authority and actual geography. A complete clause inventory, affected-public
choice, funded delivery and comparison with existing countries remain
separate requirements. Reopen this audit whenever a missing state function,
excluded person or failed cross-system duty is identified.
