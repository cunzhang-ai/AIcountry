# Duties, causal harm and the party who must repair it

*B09–B12; part of [C08 civil core](../13-civil-obligations-and-executed-remedies.md).*

**B09 — Care, defective products and AI services.** A person/entity that
intentionally or negligently breaches an actual duty of reasonable care and
causes foreseeable material injury owes compensation for proved loss. Judge
reasonable care by actual task, control, knowledge and feasible precautions;
not all bad outcomes prove negligence. Causation needs a material contribution
within the duty's protected risk, with fair defenses and contrary evidence.
For a defective product, the responsible producer and domestic importer owe
civil repair for causally proved injury without requiring proof that they
knew the defect. Defect means less safety than reasonably owed for intended
and reasonably foreseeable use, assessed on evidence of design, manufacture,
instructions and feasible safety; misuse/causation defenses require facts.
An update cannot retrospectively erase the failed version or safety duty.

AI deployment follows actual control and duties: identify design, release,
instructions, update, use, supervision and relevant source uncertainty.
An unknowable model output does not itself relieve a responsible supplier of
owed care; an operator does not automatically bear all vendor fault. Clinical,
financial and essential-service obligations retain their stricter sector
standards. Ordinary service negligence and defect responsibility are distinct;
public procurement cannot waive the user's nonwaivable floor. Alleged severe
misconduct retains P01–P20's separate criminal elements and proof.

**B10 — Employer, contractor and state responsibility.** An employer/entity
is civilly responsible for work carried out through its personnel and closely
connected misuse of entrusted functions, subject to proved scope/causation;
the harmed person need not first convict or bankrupt the employee. Contracting
out does not remove a public/sector nondelegable protection duty. Independent
contractor and parent-company liability follows their own actual control,
undertakings and law, not every corporate relationship or label. The state
answers for unlawful official acts in entrusted functions through an accessible
civil claim and lawful Treasury route; it cannot direct a claimant only to
an insolvent official. Fair hearing protects accused workers. State recovery
from a culpable wrongdoer is separate and cannot delay the victim's established
payment. A lawful public policy's ordinary disadvantage is not automatically
a compensable wrong; acquisition, rights and statutory compensation have their
own grounds. The court cannot replace elected policy merely through damages.

**B11 — Multiple causes and contribution.** For the same indivisible injury,
each defendant proved responsible for a material causal contribution or
applicable nondelegable duty is liable for full compensatory repair, with
fair contribution between responsible parties. A plaintiff cannot recover
the same item twice, and no innocent actor is liable solely because others
are insolvent. Separate divisible harms are allocated on proved causation.
A responsible party seeking contribution identifies actual fault/control and
risk allocation; a victim need not solve every internal supplier relationship
before claiming against a legally responsible defendant. Independent corporate
personality is not erased merely to find money; fraudulent asset concealment
needs its distinct source-law and evidence route.

Insurance, earned benefits, basic support and compensation are traced by item
and period. An actual duplicate payment of the same compensable loss is
reconciled under law; mere access to general services or an unreceived offer
is not compensation. No automatic clawback of a person's floor, pension or
safe access arises from a pending case. Insurer/subrogation and public-support
recovery need defined lawful scope and notice, not hidden payment netting.

**B12 — Forms and limits of restoration.** Courts may declare rights, correct
records, order lawful repair/performance, stop a continuing wrong or award
proved pecuniary/nonpecuniary loss with reasons. Privacy, dignity and bodily
injury can require more than returning a price; quantify actual impact and
uncertainty proportionately without a fabricated universal wellbeing score.
Civil compensation is not a private tax or an improvised criminal fine.
A remedy identifies actor, recipient, act/amount, date, lawful asset/payment
source, executor and verification. Third-party and respondent protections
continue. Missing cash or physical supply is an unmet duty requiring lawful
response, not evidence of compliance or permission to invent a tax/worker.
Actual private/government solvency, insurance, damages valuation and received
restoration remain unverified in this draft.

[AS05/AS11/AS12](../../C07/18-person-directed-assistance.md) now distinguishes
actual employer, person-directed service, payroll custody, B10 nondelegable duty
and a prospective public co-obligation for defined ordinary employment dues.
No blanket recipient fault/immunity, general tort indemnity or old-debt guarantee
follows. B09–B11 causation/defenses and actual source-law claims remain separate.

[CB19–CB22](../../C09/14-shared-buildings-and-neighbor-rights.md) expressly adds
ordinary neighbor-use protection/abatement with lawful occupier/child standing,
source control, substantial specified interference and reciprocal context.
Compensation for creating/authorizing that unlawful interference does not need
an additional negligence finding; inherited/third-party hazards need actual
knowledge/control/feasible action as specified. This is new scoped statutory
liability, not all discomfort, land-price loss or public-policy disadvantage.
B09–B12 separate personal injury/defect/state/multiple-source proof and same-loss
credit remain, with no new punitive offense or automatic public indemnity.
