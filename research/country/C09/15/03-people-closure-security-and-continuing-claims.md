# NR09–NR12. The operator's exit must not export its mine's costs

*Part of [mineral rights](../15-finite-resources-and-mineral-rights.md).*

**NR09 — People, homes, water and work are not bid inputs to waive.** Preserve
actual required collective/customary consent, affected participation and direct
review, housing/land/relocation/compensation, water/ecological floors and C09
future-options duties. Community representation needs actual scope/authority;
a chief's signature, donation, job promise or AI consensus is no whole-community
consent. Publish actual local burdens, benefit/cost arrangements and public
service-funding incidence, not personal sensitive testimony. WL/TW workplace
voice, protected refusal, wages/rest/learning/exit and actual child/hazard work
law apply to every operator. No free migrant/relative/volunteer crew or compulsory
reskilling. Mine-dependent local housing, water, care, transport and livelihoods
need accepted paid continuity beyond closure, not an unfunded community promise.
Clinical rights, rescue, protest and force keep their own separate permissions.

**NR10 — Secure actual closure before exposure grows.** Apply TS11's exposure-
matched financial plan as an independent operating/phase condition regardless
of public/private/collective mineral title: before new exploration
surface disturbance and before each new/materially enlarged production phase,
secure the independently assessed cost of already incurred and proposed-phase
rehabilitation/closure/monitoring obligations, including realistic failure,
source/price/FX and duration risks. Update at least annually and on material
physical/cost/control/source change; additional security precedes additional
exposure, no self-certified future top-up. Independent relevant assessment,
actual enforceable cash/security/insurance or lawfully funded public self-
insurance and actual qualified executing capacity must be identified. Use only actually legally available funds/instruments or validly released
collateral; forming security does not reprioritize old secured creditors, evade
U clawback or transfer another owner's assets. No affiliate
promise, future profit/royalty, duplicated collateral, general Treasury credit
or bond amount as liability ceiling. Public operators require actual secured
resources too; state ownership alone is no exemption or proof funds exist.

Funds/claims held for that defined purpose need actual protected custody,
beneficiaries, permitted investments/withdrawal, insolvency/counterparty limits
and court access before reliance; the general company/Treasury cannot spend
them as profit/tax or pledge them again. A real surety/insurance exclusion or
residual unfinanceable harm remains, not zero. If a new phase cannot meet its
source/rights condition, do not authorize it. A funded bond cannot buy an
unlawful irreversible loss, and security is not actual land restoration.

**NR11 — Release only performed obligations; preserve real tails.** Operator
performs progressive restoration and safe shutdown, worker/community continuity
and the stated monitoring/source duties. Existing independent qualified safety/
environment roles verify actual work, source records and receiver acceptance;
the public owner/operator cannot grant its own final absolution. Partial security
release matches only demonstrably discharged exposure, with actual challenge,
remaining secured duties and lawful reversion. No automatic release merely
because a permit expires, a company sells equipment or a model reports recovery.
Exact title/site/record/source transfer and lawful closure certificate do not
erase independently surviving victims', worker/creditor or long-tail statutory
claims. Actual P/K fault and ordinary B/U/M priority/defenses remain; no new
retroactive creditor preference or blanket good-faith immunity is implied.

**NR12 — Abandonment is a public safety duty with an actual payer.** The assigned
public environmental/land authority supplies lawful urgent safety/containment
through existing TS/HP/RC grounds and actual qualified receiving, then pursues
operator/security/other actual liable parties through proper source/courts.
The Act names source, custody/executor and any bounded public shortfall mandate
before grant. A limited public advance creates only its actual recovery claim,
not a fictitious reimbursed source, general private-debt guarantee or new tax.
For old/orphan sites, disclose unknown operator/title/security and actual
appropriated shortfall/competent recovery; an unfunded reserve is no remediation.
No unsafe abandonment because a deadline passed or forced unpaid local cleanup.
If every payer/receiver fails, preserve available lawful protection and claims,
report actual unmet harm and seek competent funded elected correction. Do not
count one repair team/bond/vehicle across simultaneous mine, clinic and public
control tasks or extend force/source powers to make the plan appear complete.

[CLN01–CLN08](../21-industrial-cleanup-and-insolvency.md) adds the wider
industrial closure and insolvency interface. It keeps NR10 phase security,
with a prospective registered charge and residual funding only within
the new Act's defined scope. Earlier secured ranks remain protected.

[CLN09–CLN14](../21/03-legacy-contamination-and-long-term-protection.md) adds
a legacy portfolio and continuing-use review for sites without collectible
cover. Keep site assurance and public fallback under their separate sources.
