# WF09–WF12. Annual expense room is not a usable source

*Part of [WF](../58-country-source-and-capacity-frontier.md); RF/MC/NF/PA/Y,
bounded elected debt and all existing source-law boundaries remain unchanged.*

**WF09 — Expose the conditional frontier.** Let `B=97.513bnDKK` be reference
net lending, `R` an explicitly assumed net revenue change, `H` the conditional
extra compensation, `O` other qualified net incremental expense and `L` a
chosen annual-balance comparison floor. Conditional net lending is `B+R−H−O`;
the maximum `O` compatible with that criterion is `B+R−H−L`. Missing actual
`O` leaves the candidate result unknown. A zero floor is a sensitivity, not
an annual-balanced-budget constitution, debt ban or criterion for suspending
rights. Bounded elected borrowing needs its own actual terms/source and
intertemporal consequences; a negative balance alone does not prove failure.

[TN01–TN06](../59-tax-sources-and-country-operation.md) rejects source neutrality
within three declared covered-income proxy experiments. R=0 remains a sensitivity,
not the default for a PA/SD replacement package; its numerical preference is
downgraded to a distributive benchmark. Actual full-base/net-yield bridge unknown.

Matched37→32 with all reference compensation affected and no task gain leaves
26.800bnDKK before other increments, below1% of reference GDP. Add an authored
1%GDP other-cost stress27.813: conditional net lending becomes−1.013bnDKK.
A separately assumed2%GDP revenue fall gives−56.639. Conversely, allowing a
−1%GDP comparison floor admits the first case without improving its actual
net lending. These outcomes demonstrate dependence on costs, revenues and
chosen criterion. They neither measure actual reform costs nor establish a
required tax increase, safe debt capacity, austerity rule or funded country.

**WF10 — Trace external money and timing.** Accrued revenue/compensation and
annual net lending are not gross receipts/payments or opening usable cash.
Separate due-date receipts, withholding, benefit recovery, procurement,
principal, credit, restricted assets and accepted finance. An authored cash
path with20 due before40 later receipts ends+20 but has an earlier20 gap.
Internal G/S/local transfers and pension backing are uses/receipts inside a
consolidation, not new national revenue. No reference surplus, private saving,
uncollected claim or future output is an already owned source for due pay.

**WF11 — Keep material, capital and foreign counterparts.** Full feasibility
needs skill-specific labor, imports, food/energy/water/sites, maintenance,
capital renewal, ecological limits and displaced private/future uses with
actual prices and accepted allocation. Buying existing financial equity
changes title/financial claims; it creates no new output by itself. An
authored50 equity-acquisition payment therefore creates0 new production,
not a rule treating all land/building transactions alike. Public acquisition
still needs lawful actual funds and protected creditor/owner/worker successors.
Reference private/non-S.13 foreign claims are not Treasury FX. An additional
foreign need30 with unknown actually owned/accepted source stays unresolved.
Negotiated regional provision needs actual counterpart acceptance and exit.

**WF12 — Carry commitments through the transition.** Count current I05/SD05,
service receipt, earned Y, private plans/old relief, health/care/school, H/I03
repair, opposition/elections/courts, PM standing source/buffer, HP compliance/
work loss, PT record liability, CB repairs and DP independently usable support
with legacy contracts, debt and source-specific dates. Preserve restricted
funds and no duplicate reserve. No proportional use of one reference purpose
silently prices these new guarantees. Do not charge both full retained service
and its replacement, or erase old service because a replacement is proposed.
Actual entitlement/source incidence, incomplete matches and unresolved costs
remain explicit; no model buys permission to postpone a due right.
