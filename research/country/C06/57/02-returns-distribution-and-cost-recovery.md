# PF05–PF08: distinguish investment units from a periodic promise

*Part of [private retirement tax](../57-private-retirement-tax-and-personal-capital.md); no foreign method or rate is adopted.*

**PF05 — Identify the actual owner and taxable receipt.** Passive fiduciary
custody of identifiable members' owned units/claims is not a gift to an
unallocated private foundation or the manager's own profit. Actual NF02/NF28
proprietary trading/accumulation bodies and underlying company profits retain
their taxes; a pension name/licence creates no entity exemption. A disguised
private accumulation vehicle retains NF28's entry/returns/earnings-first rules.

Personal interest/distributions actually and unconditionally available are PA
receipts, including an available cash distribution voluntarily reinvested.
Unrealized unit appreciation or a restricted pool's undistributed return is
not personal cash merely because an agent reports it. Apply the actual owner/
vehicle/source rules equally to comparable investment custody; no new annual
plan-asset levy or blanket untaxed return is created. Previously recognized
personal income reinvested is basis, not taxable again on the same return.

**PF06 — DC unit disposals recover proportional indexed tax basis.** For
new PF DC interests of the same beneficial owner/source cohort, aggregate
actual pre-disposal unit value A and unrecovered tax basis B across providers.
Each original basis lot retains its acquisition date and TA06 general-CPI factor,
not below original nominal basis. For gross actual redemption W within A,
tax-free recovery is `min(W, B, W × B/A)`; positive remaining gain is W minus
that recovery, subject to ordinary actual disposal costs/TA06 loss rules.
Allocate recovery proportionally to indexed lots and reduce original remaining
cost accordingly; never reapply an old inflation rise twice.

Use the common actual cutoff for simultaneous redemptions. Account splitting,
transfer, nominee copies and provider choice create no new basis/allowance.
A missing value/history is contested, not zero; A=0 cannot fund positive W.
Old/foreign source cohorts require PF15's explicit bridge rather than automatic
commingling. A full genuine exit can leave an evidenced TA06 capital loss,
offset only against gains under its same-year/five-year rules, not wages,
public pension, an ordinary benefit or a refundable market-loss guarantee.

**PF07 — Genuine nonmarketable annuity/DB payments recover capital first.**
For a periodic promise without personally owned redeemable/tradable units or a
reserved unilateral cash-out right, let C be its proved unrecovered nominal
tax-recognized cost. Exclude `min(payment, C)` and reduce C; include the remainder
in PA pension income. Once C is exhausted, subsequent payments are fully taxable.
An employer's untaxed old promise supplies no invented C. Ordinary acquired
contract cost is the actual own premium after the applicable source treatment;
no further TA06 inflation index accrues during this income-promise phase.

This intentionally selects simpler capital-first timing over an actuarial
exclusion ratio; it can defer tax and change distribution and must be priced
as such. No new tax life-expectancy table is required, while NB13–NB20 funding/
pricing/solvency still need actual qualified actuarial work. A redeemable
investment wrapper remains PF06 despite an annuity label. New liquidity or a
genuine commutation uses the actual resulting asset/payment and remaining basis,
never a fresh cost or retrospective cancellation of earlier lawful acts.

**PF08 — Preserve source on transfer, fees and final loss.** Actual accepted
same-owner fiduciary transfer carries remaining tax basis, original DC dates,
separate resource-capital history and unrecognized return without new personal
cash. A payment actually placed at the person's disposal is not that transfer.
Converting owned units into a different annuity/DB promise is an ordinary
TA06 disposal/exchange and actual purchase, not a tax-free pension rollover.
Recognize the actual consideration and fund/disclose its lawful tax-payment
route; a redirection by providers does not waive the gain. Only actual own
purchase consideration establishes new contract cost; no double old basis.
Real fees enter genuine
cost/value once, not as both basis increase and another deduction.

On final economic termination after all member/survivor/guarantee rights settle,
unused proved cost is an ordinary capital loss, not a pension guarantee or new
negative PA payment. A provider rename, partial transfer or surviving promise
does not close the contract. Carry source-qualified basis through accepted
run-off/successors; no forced loss or taxation of unavailable proceeds.
