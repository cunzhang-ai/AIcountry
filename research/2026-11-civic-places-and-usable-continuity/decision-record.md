# Design317 decision on usable civic-place continuity

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design316, the Design33 civic-place floor, AX, MF, IV and the
working method. Select ordinary free access, fair bookable use and
a funded replacement obligation for material changes. The project
assistant authors the proposal. Legitimate elected bodies retain
authority to adopt and fund it.

## Evidence and competing choices

The Wirral inquiry gives a direct example of a closure proposal
reviewed against need rather than assets alone. DC library room
rules illustrate actual free and restricted use. Neither source
establishes a welfare effect for AIcountry. Compare existing site
repair, co-location, qualified independent providers, ordinary
commercial vouchers and the proposed floor. Use of rooms is one
condition of voluntary association. Time, safety, access and
private choice also matter. A fixed facility count can waste
money and a service guarantee needs actual early staff and sources.
Publishing use cannot become surveillance of associations.

## Selection, continuity and next action

Select CPV01–CPV05 for a free ordinary drop-in and fair bookable
network. In the fixture choose two accepted evening rooms for two
simultaneous uses. Keep planned withdrawal conditional on actual
continuity of the floor, and allow competent urgent closure while
recording and repairing lost access. Existing lawful claims, land,
contracts, pay, access and remedies retain their terms and actual
funded successors. The design neither adopts local law nor creates
an automatic funding transfer.

Price a real weekly schedule with one current use, one unserved
group and a time-sensitive lawful booking. Check venue, staffing,
travel and challenge before a closure. Reopen on repeated exclusion,
failed succession, unaffordable work, or a better less formal route.
Actual affected participation, independent review, adoption, paid
readiness and improved lived outcomes remain open.
