# C09. Communications access and network power

*Design224 · CN01–CN12 · Proposed ordinary service and continuity duties.*

**Guarantee a usable basic communications service, open access at persistent
bottlenecks, and a tested route to time-critical help.** Keep ordinary
service, emergency delivery and political control separate. Public,
cooperative, community and private networks can operate under the same
receiving duties. Where several viable networks already deliver the floor,
keep them. Where a persistent bottleneck prevents entry or continuity,
use regulated shared infrastructure with plural provision where feasible.
Where no operator accepts the funded duty, require a named residual service
route before commencing the guarantee. C09 retains its distinct freight
and physical correspondence rules.

1. [Basic service, actual access and public source](22/01-basic-service-and-source.md)
   defines the offer, practical eligibility, price and responsibility.
2. [Open carriage, bottlenecks and exit](22/02-bottlenecks-open-carriage-and-exit.md)
   separates network control, ordinary technical discretion and remedies.
3. [Independent fallback, review and change](22/03-independent-fallback-and-control.md)
   governs usable failover, democratic control and continuing rights.
