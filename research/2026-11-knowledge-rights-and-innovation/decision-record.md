## Values, diagnoses and competing whole choices

Knowledge can be shared without consuming the idea, while discovery, people,
labs, compute, power, manufacture and upkeep remain scarce and risky. Exclusive
rights may reward creation and also obstruct entry/cumulative learning/access;
public funding can support common needs and become patronage. These mechanisms
and incentive effects are hypotheses, not a proved national causal ranking.
Values include creator livelihoods, autonomy, privacy, plural criticism, practical
repair/care, future learning and enforceable public control. No aggregate patent
or publication score substitutes for these potentially conflicting values.

Retain 94avoids a new office burden but leaves the ordinary-rights chain undefined.
Strong exclusivity/market purchasing can reward risky private work, but can lock
out users and charge twice for publicly bought rights. Universal public-open work
removes some copying barriers but needs paid grants/wages, compensation, actual
supply and contestable agenda control; prizes shift risk to funded entrants.
Select **limited private/collective rights plus paid public access and plural
innovation**, with field-specific public-open/private alternatives able to win.
Neither blanket repeal nor a central AI, ministry or expert board owns knowledge.

## Selected rules and material tradeoffs

KR01–KR12specifies territorial exclusive rights; automatic human copyright and
creator/performer layers; examined 20-yearpatents; marks/design/origin, seed 20/25,
chip layouts/secrets, limited learning/repair/accessibility/research and authorized-
item international exhaustion. Human contribution is tested; autonomous generation
creates no automatic new exclusive right. Actual third-party/data/collective claims
survive. Commercial copying for training requires actual permission or a specific
valid defence, not internet availability; facts/style/independent thought stay free.
Long terms, own-seed half royalty, exceptions and no automatic machine monopoly are
explicit contestable choices, not demonstrated optimal incentives or treaty compliance.

KR13–KR16uses existing IC/C04 elected windows and qualified assignment. National
Assembly and local councils retain their scopes. Fully paid nonsensitive technical
foreground defaults to bought reusable rights with verified actual worldwide chain,
or an explicit narrower authorized exception/rejection before award. Rights cannot
be bought from a grantee who does not own them. Publication uses a fixed dated
milestone/earlier acceptance and one pre-specified<=12-month embargo, not endless
postponed acceptance. Creative commissions purchase agreed paid uses, not everything.
Negative findings, arts, maintenance and independent inquiry can receive paid support.

KR17–KR20maps critical dependencies and paid continuity only where operational
essential-service dependence exists, not every arts/basic-research award. An ordinary
court may grant a specific remunerated nonexclusive essential patent licence with
proof, alternatives, paid preparation/delivery, scope/expiry and review. Actual use
still needs all safety/rights/receiving conditions. Patent use is not secret/copyright/
foreign transfer, equipment or forced worker know-how. Semiconductor/export limits
remain explicit. No actual treaty/domestic legal availability is asserted.
Reject a blanket 12-month final licence cap: repeated trials may frustrate investment
and continuity. Definite expiry<=underlying right, annual/material review and
termination with legitimate licensed interests/safe handback replace it; B17 interim
clocks do not become a permanent licence. This is a real rejected draft choice.

KR21–KR28uses a C04 career registry/director chain, no EV/IP truth sovereign;
ordinary courts decide final validity/infringement, with protected answerable proof
and actual execution. Select narrow knowing commercial counterfeiting/piracy K25
with fine-only lower Q band, corporate attribution, needs/collection/finality limits
and proved proceeds, not IP-only imprisonment. Digital scale uses proved value,
not inflated download/account counts. This displaces the draft two-year-custody
rival; deterrence, poverty effects and actual treaty fit remain unresolved evidence.

KR29–KR32separates grant, product, royalty, prize, owner return and tax. Preserve
old contracts/rights, wages/royalties, public-domain status, Y units/net/index, H/TW,
privacy and current service claims through funded successors. Proposed new default
commencement is first 1 January>=18 monthsactualAct, with expressly enacted scope/cohorts
and notice/bridges. A readiness inventory cannot postpone existing or commenced rights.
Source-law reconciliation may change the candidate before adoption, not quietly erase dues.
