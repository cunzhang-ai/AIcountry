# Reference attribution without duplicate spending

*Design 57 authored allocations inside the design-56 year-2 S compound case.*

The old S service envelopes total 774 million fictional CU in year 2.
Income support is separately 120 million in the compound case: 100 retained
plus 20 authored additional need. The [inputs](../../2026-10-continuity-law/inputs.json)
assume core staffing shares inside existing required FTE. They allocate
each envelope at its constant average cost per required FTE, including
nonwage costs. This is an explicit **scenario placeholder**, not observed
occupational pay, a measured unit cost or proof that the new law was funded.

| Old sector | Whole envelope, million CU | Assumed core FTE / whole required | Assumed included core cost, million CU |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Health | 240 | 200 / 3,100 | about 15.484 |
| Education | 145 | 0 / 2,050 | 0 |
| Care | 110 | 100 / 2,000 | 5.5 |
| Housing/networks | 105 | 30 / 730 | about 4.315 |
| Justice/safety/defense | 92 | 150 / 1,000 | 13.8 |
| Democracy/admin/science/culture | 82 | 100 / 1,000 | 8.2 |

The residual costs stay in their original sector envelopes. The core is
**included**, not a second budget added to 774. Zero education core means
education is outside this bounded continuity allocation; its full 145
and ordinary duties remain counted. Justice/admin shares do not establish
the skills or independence needed for a particular court or contest.
Network shares do not prove transport, power or offline payment delivery.
The 120 transfer envelope does not price E06's basket, eligibility or take-up;
provisional and established support for the same need/days require reconciliation.

## Protecting one part leaves other claims unmet

Assume for arithmetic that the whole selected core is allocated first.
That is a test condition, not a new executive priority power. A real
allocation must identify the displaced duties and lawful elected grounds.

| Sector after the same loss | Core assumed protected | Remaining required FTE | Remaining available FTE | Remaining gap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Care | 100 | 1,900 | 1,745 | 155 |
| Justice/safety/defense | 150 | 850 | 768 | 82 |
| Democracy/admin/science/culture | 100 | 900 | 818 | 82 |

The 52-million-CU year-2 financing gap also remains. No new tax, borrowing,
recruitment or supplier agreement was inserted. A label such as protected
minimum cannot discharge the residual care visit, fair hearing or other
lawful duty. Skilled people, safe shifts and independent execution remain
unproved even inside the core. The [sample roster](02-due-times-rosters-and-paths.md)
shows why an annual assigned share cannot certify a safe day.

The [candidate E17–E20 rules](../../country/C06/19/05-resources-failure-and-commencement.md)
require due-time attribution, recorded displacement and an elected response.
Retain the authored reference separately from actual quantities, which
remain `null` in the inventory. Replace average attribution with qualified
task, place, shift and supplier costs before an operational claim; recount
the whole package if the new detail cannot fit the old envelope.
