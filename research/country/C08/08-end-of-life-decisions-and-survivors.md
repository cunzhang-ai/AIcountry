# C08. End-of-life choices, death records and survivors

*Part of [C08 everyday law and justice](../C08-everyday-law-and-justice.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

National law protects a person's current informed acceptance or refusal of
health care, with the same legal capacity and chosen support as in the
[civil-status floor](01-civil-law-and-personal-status.md). An advance-care
statement records values, likely choices, trusted contacts and any
decision-specific prospective refusal; law sets its formation, access,
interpretation, updating and revocation. It is made with accessible advice,
conflict checks and protection against pressure from heirs, payers or care
institutions. Clinicians seek the person's present will and preferences,
including nonstandard communication, before treating a document as final.
An advance refusal applies only to its enacted scope and circumstances;
an ambiguous or outdated statement triggers supported interpretation and
urgent independent review, not automatic treatment or automatic withdrawal.
A trusted contact may communicate evidence of the person's will but does
not acquire an unrestricted power over the body, property or political voice.

A clinician's specific treatment recommendation, a patient's refusal, a
scarcity priority, a disability support decision and a death determination
have different legal tests. A family disagreement cannot create a veto over
the competent patient's choice; suspected abuse of an advance document or
deliberate denial needs immediate protective review with both patient and
affected people heard where safe. A court can protect a lawful choice and
order a prompt reconsideration but cannot invent a national coverage rule.
The current reference retains no assisted-dying authorization or new exception to
otherwise applicable P/K law. This is a selected retain/no-change boundary, with
the cost to people seeking a voluntary assisted exit openly retained; future
lawful public reconsideration can propose a complete alternative. A registry,
clinical label or AI cannot create that authority. Lawful refusal, proportionate
symptom relief and lawful withdrawal remain distinct from assisted dying.

[UA10](23/04-life-sustaining-care-objection-and-liberty.md) now expressly governs
necessary life-support decisions where relevant will genuinely remains unknown:
prior heightened court review, actual bodily therapeutic function/benefit/burden,
independent relevant clinical/palliative evidence and no cognitive/social/fiscal
criterion. Known applicable refusal/choice still follows its own lawful scope.
UA16 separately addresses safe minimum dependent continuation after failed renewal,
without fictional consent or new escalation. The disputed nonconsensual authority
requires exact lawful adoption; actual source law continues meanwhile.

## Death, the body and the living

An appropriately qualified professional verifies death and records medical
cause under enacted rules; C08's civil-status service registers the fact
with a usable certificate, correction route and protection for private
information. Cause, manner and legal status are different findings. A
suspected unlawful death needs independent evidence preservation and
investigation; an uncertain identity or missing person needs a defined
provisional process before any legally final death finding. No official may
conceal a death or delay a lawful certificate to avoid a service, custody
or compensation claim. A person without documents still receives dignified
handling and an identification effort.

The person's lawful wishes about donation, body care and memorial practice
receive priority subject to safety and other people's rights. Where wishes
are unknown, affected relations can participate under a clear conflict rule
and independent review; marriage, religion or a registry field cannot
automatically erase a chosen relationship. Local authorities ensure an
accessible, dignified low-cost body-care and burial or cremation route for
people of different faiths and none, with safe transport, workable sites,
environmental and health rules and no compulsory ceremony. [C09 land
planning](../C09/01-land-water-and-shared-planning.md) owns siting and
shared resource effects; C07 public health owns specific disease precautions.
A funeral provider
cannot hold the only certificate or body record hostage for payment. Disaster
or mass-death procedures still preserve identification, family notice,
individual records, investigation and proportionate rites as far as possible.

Death starts separate claims rather than one automatic account transfer.
The C07 social-protection service gives dependants and qualifying survivors
timely notice, interim subsistence and review of pension and other statutory
benefits. A child or dependent adult gets an accepting care and housing
route while legal status is resolved. A lawful estate administrator preserves
assets and debts, identifies valid wills, heirs, creditors and pending
claims, then accounts under court review. Disputed parentage, partnership,
donation or debt cannot suspend immediate food or care. No AI model,
hospital, funeral company or family elder decides the whole inheritance.
Bereavement support is offered without compulsory disclosure of intimate
beliefs or conversion of grief into a psychiatric diagnosis.

Existing advance directions, care plans, powers, wills, donor records,
death certificates, pending investigations and accrued survivor claims
continue under valid law until a lawful successor receives them. No actual
death law, records, body-care sites, hearings or capacity exists for
AIcountry. Reopen if a family or hospital overrides the person's will, a
death cannot be independently verified, rites exclude a community, or
survivors lack an executable claim during a records dispute.

[B33–B36](16/05-wills-estates-and-missing-persons.md) now supplies proposed
will, dependant/default estate, administration and missing-person rules.
Bodily/rite wishes, child receiving and Y survivor insurance remain separate.
No old death record or agent inference can distribute contested property.

[VR05–VR08](29/02-death-and-correction.md) separates medical confirmation,
identity linkage, a civil record and an operative B36 court declaration.
A correction reaches the affected payment and service owners, while estate,
survivor and suspected unlawful death routes retain their own decisions.
