# YC01–YC04. Safe receiving and stable relationships

[Stable child care](../27-stable-child-care-and-adult-transition.md)

**YC01 — Keep a named duty through actual receipt.** The local child
protection service takes the first lawful contact and identifies a named
caseworker and paid duty substitute. It retains case and source
responsibility through actual authorized handover. Record the original
time and grounds for any restriction and which person actually received
the child. Give direct and safe access to family support and the separate
B31 child judge. B31's imminent danger test, 24-hour hearing and order
limits continue. A funding dispute, unavailable bed or move never extends
a restriction, creates consent or proves neglect.

**YC02 — Match the child to a real place.** The caseworker considers
available family support, willing and suitable kin or chosen family,
qualified foster homes and small staffed therapeutic arrangements in the
child's actual circumstances. Give the child usable communication and
private independent support to express their wishes. A joint sibling
placement and continuity of safe relationships are the ordinary
preference, subject to individualized safety, wishes and assessed needs.
Explain an unavoidable separation, specify actual contact and review its
reason at each required hearing and before avoidable delay. A free room
or family tie alone cannot qualify receiving. For any move confirm
accepting carers, safe travel, school access, treatment, usable contact
and original records. Place proximity is valuable where safe, including
across a boundary. A safe distance can also protect a child.

**YC03 — Plan for stability and a genuine route home.** The authority
records the child's current wishes, safety, actual schooling and care,
contacts, feasible support for return and the next meaningful decision.
Authorize or review a compulsory change only under the applicable
judicial source. Review the delivery plan with the child and their
representative within 30 calendar days of first placement, sooner at the
existing earlier legal or useful deadline, and before a planned move.
Keep further review tied to the actual case and its B31 order. Assess
progress on concrete safety and support rather than compliance with
a generic parenting course. No target for placements, reunification,
adoption or days in care decides an individual case.

**YC04 — Give the child an outside route.** Offer a paid independent
advocate and private contact outside the home and placing chain within
48 elapsed hours of actual placement, and earlier for the B31 hearing,
urgency or an existing duty. Maintain usable assisted and offline access.
The advocate does not replace the child's separate legal representative.
The child, safe carers and workers may directly reach independent
inspection and the competent court under their standing. Keep safe
contact information from an alleged abuser and provide an answerable
basis for contested decisions through the lawful evidence route.
Preserve the child's own account, including disagreement. A provider,
shared vendor or AI summary cannot close a complaint or attest to its
own independence.
