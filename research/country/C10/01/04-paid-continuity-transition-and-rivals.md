# RX13–RX16. Capacity, failed control and lawful transition

*Design144 · [Safety and emergency continuity](../01-public-safety-and-emergencies.md) · No real-world preparedness certificate.*

**RX13 — Pay for the complete independent chain.** Existing C04/C06 OP/T owners
must budget qualified rescue/health/local crews, physical reserves and logistics,
safe accommodation/income support, interpreters, accessible human/offline and
other-provider routes, original records, alternate Assembly sitting and actual
ordinary/CJ courts, defense, outside-command investigation and accepted remedy
execution. Preserve roles that require independence; do not promise one person
simultaneously to command, authenticate, review and execute the challenged act.
Treasury pays actual due annual/standing sources under separated controls; no
emergency label supplies missing appropriation, FX or new national priority.
Actual finite capacity, same-source dependencies and ecological losses count.
Old37 function bids are not prices or staffing ceilings for the added duties.

C09 animal evacuation/veterinary needs share vehicles/water with human rescue,
which has priority. C08 mass-fatality identification, independent inquiry, safe
family notice and proportionate rites continue; command cannot erase a missing
person. Durable rebuilding, relocation and permanent land use return to C09
ordinary elected/rights processes; temporary exclusion is no permanent land plan.
Clinical, privacy, membership, border, external force and conflict-custody duties
retain actual sources. A foreign partner/contractor cannot receive hidden sovereign
power, and a domestic vote cannot erase a treaty or third-party creditor right.

**RX14 — Failed capacity is a substantive cost.** Independent alternate ordinary
receivers, preauthorized duty panels, protected sitting/records and paid exit
preparation are necessary, not presumed present. Failure to appoint, communicate,
fund or convene supplies no new judge, lower vote, renamed custody, automatic
consent, renewal or term. Keep ordinary lawful safety and protection obligations
and disclose unmet duty with actual harm; if their execution also fails, record
failed constitutional control. Peaceful lawful public correction remains, not a
fiction that an AI or another protector now has authority.

The selected no-derogation design may prevent a response that would save lives
in an extreme catastrophe if constitutional procedures/resources physically fail.
It may require release under an actual custody source without a timely lawful
order; danger does not itself create a replacement confinement power. It also
requires costly independent continuity before crises. This residual risk is a
material objection, not evidence all restrictions are forbidden or a judgment
that physical inability is personal misconduct. The rival narrowly enumerated
constitutional derogation could buy flexibility but creates a highly capturable
suspension power. Actual comparative safety/freedom outcomes are not established.

**RX15 — Prospective compatibility.** The no-derogation/protected-functions charter
needs actual legitimate predecessor/founding adoption before it governs. Under a
compatible coordinated implementing Act, the ordinary declaration/source-record/
broad-restriction procedure has a **180calendar-day enactment lead time**. Where
A9/RR protects current offices and immediate succession for a changed emergency/
review rule, that component starts only at the **later of the180-day date and its
express lawfully fixed RR-compatible date**. No component silently overrides a
constitutional hold; the closed predecessor-law restoration exception requires
its own actual proof, forum/votes and reviewed date, never this design's approval.
Publish component dates, legal source inventory, pending orders, staff/pay/contract
successors, court access and retained records. No readiness certificate moves them.
This is a selected lead-time hypothesis, not permission to operate before competence
or proof all receivers are ready. Earlier due/urgent rights remain on actual law.

The adopting law must expressly resolve prior derogation/declaration mandates and
set prospective transition for ongoing orders: research does not repeal real law,
retroactively criminalize lawful acts, restart a custody/DR clock or erase claims.
New/mutated measures after commencement use the new law; valid older individual
orders retain only their actual source and sunset, with existing challenge rights.
A material existing general restriction's continuation after its own lawful expiry
requires the compatible new process. No old 30-day AI renewal, generic emergency
prerogative or software permission is silently migrated. RR protected succession,
actual tax/treaty/defense rules and source-law poll dates remain unchanged.

**RX16 — What reopens this choice.** Reopen on independently qualified evidence of
specific avoidable catastrophic harm, disguised ordinary derogation, repeated
exclusion, unmanageable source/review load or a rights-preserving rival with a
complete paid transition. Hear affected survivors, disabled people, minorities,
workers and responders without claiming consultation that did not occur. A new
media/agent arrangement needs actual meanings and mandates, not an account-based
new electorate. Monitoring failure is uncertainty, not continued justification.
Source qualification, research selection, public authority, operational readiness
and lived outcomes stay separate. After this interface repair, return to a finite
whole-country gap review rather than assume the latest emergency subquestion is
the next national priority.
