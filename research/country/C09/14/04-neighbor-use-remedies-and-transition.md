*Part of [C09/14](../14-shared-buildings-and-neighbor-rights.md); a selected civil duty.*

**CB19 — Actionable interference, not a right to a silent city.** A person
with actual control of a place/activity must not create, authorize or knowingly
continue a substantial unjustified interference with others' lawful ordinary

ordinary use and feasible less harmful means. Actual ordinary occupancy, sleep,
care, accessibility, family and chosen lawful work matter; equal protection is
not limited to unusually valuable land or an ideal healthy adult. Ordinary
children, reasonable repairs, normal city visibility or lawful beliefs/company
alone are no interference. Unfamiliar innovative use is neither automatically
unlawful nor immune; a judge must reason from the listed harms/rights, not

**CB20 — Standing, source responsibility and fault.** A lawful actual

no ownership, landlord permission, completed injury or majority complaint is

A proved source creator/controller owes proportionate abatement without making

poor neighbor or diagnosis is presumed a dangerous source.
