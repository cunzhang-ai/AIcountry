# Completion finding. The early-care promise now has two accountable duties

Design192 specifies ordinary local provision and national gap finance
with prepayment before obligations fall due, plus a limited national
service fallback when local receiving fails. Existing claim dates and
independent review follow the child. The local council retains its
ordinary authority. [Canonical choice](../../country/C07/23-funded-local-early-care.md)

The Copenhagen 2026 records reconcile a named phase-in grant to fee
relief, staffing and other local uses. This resolves a specific
appropriation comparison and tests two distinct failure modes.
Actual recurring source, tax incidence, paid shifts, uptake and effects
remain open. [Scope and tests](../../settlement/funded-local-care/README.md)
