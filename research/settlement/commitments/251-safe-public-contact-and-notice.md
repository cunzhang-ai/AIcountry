# Design338. Fund safe public correspondence and independent review

[SCN01–SCN05](../../country/C04/06/05-safe-public-contact-and-notice.md)
requires separate public provision for local contact, independently usable
fallback, access help and court directions. Preserve existing dates,
mandates, payments and remedies through failure or channel change.

The [decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-safe-public-contact-and-notice/decision-record.md)
retains case-by-case arrangements and a national mailbox as alternatives.
Actual costs, accessible receiving, safe disclosure and court work are open.
