# DF01–DF05: a civilian chain with an accountable professional head

*Part of [DF](../12-civilian-defense-and-service.md); no new force mandate.*

**DF01 — Name the chain and its purpose.** The elected Cabinet sets lawful
defense objectives within Assembly law, mission and source authorization.
Its civilian Defense Minister issues authenticated mission orders to a human
Chief of Defense; registered professional commanders execute their lawful
scope. A lawful acting minister uses the actual government-formation statute,
not a military appointment. No Chief, personal guard, contractor, model or
security council holds separate sovereign authority or decides who won an
election. Foreign forces retain actual agreement/jurisdiction, not an assumed
place in this chain. C10/03's actual attack/mission law and expiry still govern.

**DF02 — Appoint competence without creating a military protector.** Cabinet
appoints Chief from an open C04 qualified public shortlist, with accessible
entry, job-related operational/rights/management competence, conflicts and safe
public reasons. No required party allegiance, personal loyalty test, wealth,
AI ranking or merely inherited seniority. Existing merit/independent court
challenge applies. This ordinary executive command appointment deliberately
has no judicial-office term, protected opposition confirmation or governing
military seat: elected policy must remain replaceable. Assembly retains real
budget/mission/law/scrutiny powers, not appointment of tactical targets.

The national state is the public service employer through its Defense Ministry.
Chief appoints subordinate commanders/personnel only within the actual lawful
staffing/qualification/merit mandate; civilian ministry staff retain their C04
source. No employment power over courts, outside investigators or the Treasury.

**DF03 — End command without pretending it proves misconduct.** Cabinet may
end the Chief's operational assignment for lawful policy, operational or
accountability reasons through a reasoned recorded act. It cannot erase
career/pay/pension rights, punish lawful dissent/reporting or fabricate criminal
guilt. Protected status, reassignment/redundancy and discipline require the
applicable C04/WL process and independent ordinary court relief. A paid
temporary stand-down can protect operations during genuine review; not an
indefinite unpaid suspension. Removal of command neither grants the former
Chief a veto nor creates an imaginary replacement post or funded pension.

**DF04 — Register actual public units, not personal forces.** Primary law
defines force classes, employment/source owners and mission limits. Every unit
has a lawful responsible command and financial/record custody; no parallel
party/personal guard or unaccountable auxiliary through a company, charity,
reserve label or vendor. Civilian contractors can supply lawful support under
SC/TS/DP without receiving a private sovereign coercive franchise. A military
unit is not an outside civilian police investigator, election authority or
general care/clinical command. Existing LF/CD/IA boundaries remain separate.

**DF05 — Keep vacancy finite.** Law predesignates qualified deputies and
succession order; the actual deputy may perform Chief management for at most
180elapsed days from the original vacancy, without resetting by person/title.
Ordinary duly assigned field officers retain only their own existing lawful
mission/safety duties after that limit, not every reserved Chief power.
Earlier source/acting limits prevail. Cabinet must make a lawful appointment
or seek competent prospective correction; no unqualified ghost head, expired
consultant/credential holdover or military emergency government. Vacancy cannot
extend a mission, custody, parliamentary or election clock.
