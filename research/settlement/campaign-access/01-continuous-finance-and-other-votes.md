## Actual rules, source and time

[Continuous private/public resources](../../country/C03/12/02-continuous-money-and-public-access.md)
lock each donation's amount/B at actual receipt; later basket changes cannot
revalue an earlier act. A rolling source budget covers eligible natural people
and all affected political accounts. Local-only political eligibility cannot
be converted into a national donation by a party transfer. No donor preapproval
registry or political-membership dossier is required; attestations, assisted
records, specific audits and court challenge handle actual sources.

An annual person's public credit is B0/200 in each eligible national/local track,
not a private donation or a second vote. It is additional to contest credits,
with a separate source and actual beneficially restricted disposal. Annual new
instructions close30November and protected corrections15December; people first
eligible later use the next annual round, without losing franchise/contest credits.
Public money cannot pay private consumption, loyalty, fictitious workers or an
AI principal's invented constituency. Funded directory entries require a real
first reserved/paid instruction; pending filing/help/turnover costs stay visible.

[Local/referendum rules](../../country/C03/12/03-local-contests-and-referenda.md)
use the actual lawful body, question and electorate. A one-track council poll has
one credit, not an invented national second track. National source pays the
common minimum; a council's optional enhancement needs its own lawful equal
source. Existing local nomination/count/continuity law needs express reconciliation;
finance registration alone cannot create a complete local electoral system.

For the proposed C14 final-text referendum, close is certification+120days,
first voting14days earlier; the106days before first voting preserve at least90
for consideration. Qualified committees get the actual forum by V−21; a booked
slot is not delivery. A final annulled-question repeat uses the same120-day close
from judgment, with express C14 source-law change and paid capacity. Neither a
finance official nor a caretaker selects a more convenient poll date.

[Enforcement](../../country/C03/12/04-records-enforcement-and-delivery.md)
extends protected EA finance careers, distinct access/payers and B18/B19 execution
expressly to this new scope. Five-day ordinary unused-source correction does not
excuse knowing material use. New punitive PC scope needs an express prospective
VD12 class B amendment with actual control/fault/material value, court-only means-
based/limited body fines and no new detention or speech offence. Small mistakes
remain civil correction. Expanding a statutory offence cannot happen by analogy.
