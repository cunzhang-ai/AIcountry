# Proportionate sentence, actual release and an honest code boundary

*P16–P20; part of [C08 criminal core](../12-criminal-core-and-procedure.md).*

**P16 — No-sanction comparator and finite limits.** After lawful proof, give
individual reasons for the least restrictive adequate available response,
including no penal sanction where law allows, subsistence-protecting fines,
restitution, bounded community measures and custody. A missing affordable
alternative is a delivery failure, not aggravation; custody needs independent
legal justification and cannot fill a missing fine/community schedule. No mandatory prison
minimum applies to this core. Gravity, proved fault and harm matter; poverty,
silence, diagnosis, lawful dissent and unproved allegations do not aggravate.
Multiple labels for the same conduct cannot multiply punishment for the same
harm. The aggregate ordinary K01–K24 custodial term for a single factual course
is at most 25 years; child conduct remains capped at five. Custody already
served, including unlawful or relabelled deprivation for the same case, is
credited in full. These are provisional value choices, not calibrated safety
claims. Later distinct crimes require fresh independent prosecution; future
risk cannot extend a finished sentence. Death, torture, corporal punishment,
slavery, indefinite preventive imprisonment and irreducible life sentences
are unavailable under this candidate. [G11](../18/07-sentence-trial-and-remedy.md)
now defines proposed international-crime ceilings; actual treaty/source
compatibility is not established merely by retaining this ordinary maximum.

**P17 — Money and community restrictions.** A fine is proportionate to harm,
fault and actually usable resources, protects essential living costs and
dependants and has an affordable installment/correction route. Inability to
pay alone never converts it into imprisonment. Deliberate evasion requires
its own proved lawful ground; wealth does not buy immunity for serious harm.
Compensation, public victim support and criminal accountability are separate.
No fine or community service may be imposed without a prospectively enacted
unit/range/duration schedule; missing parameters grant no discretionary power.
[Q01–Q06](../15/05-individual-sanctions-and-breach.md) now supplies explicit
uncalibrated candidate units/durations. Community conditions are specific,
time-limited, practically available, reviewable and funded; unlawful or
impossible conditions cannot manufacture breach. A material escalation needs
a hearing and lawful grounds. An alternative must not widen supervision to
people otherwise entitled to no sanction; no involuntary therapy or private
household labor is assumed to finance it. Restoration remains optional with
safe refusal/withdrawal, independent advice and no victim-support penalty.

**P18 — Humane custody and separate control.** Civilian corrections executes
court orders separately from police/prosecution, under national rights and
lawful regional operation. Clinical services retain professional responsibility
and ordinary consent. Independent inspectors can enter, protect complaints,
preserve injury/death evidence and seek enforceable correction. Proportionate
facility safety measures have reasons, minimum scope and review; administrative
segregation cannot become a new sentence. Court, inspector, clinical evidence,
defense, Treasury and executor need distinct competent roles and real paths,
not merely different names. Inhumane conditions require urgent court/receiver
correction; lack of money never licenses torture or secret detention. A lawful
custody option needs actual safe capacity, not a prison bed projection.

**P19 — Release and restoration.** Periodic review examines lawful necessity,
conditions and possible less restrictive dispositions. At half the imposed
term, RL supplies automatic judicial review and presumptive release with a
proved-necessity exception. The Act defines the effect of a missing or expired
continuation order. Earlier review and independent appeal retain their own
sources. [RL01–RL15](../28-sentence-review-and-release.md) Treatment,
work-program participation, inability to obtain housing or an opaque risk
score cannot prolong the court's expiry. No punishment for optional treatment
refusal. Corrections and receiving local services fund and document identity,
housing, healthcare, income, lawful conditions and accepted handoff before
release. If a receiver fails, release still occurs and its unmet service duty
is escalated through continuity remedies. Restrict public criminal-record use
by purpose, lawful duration, correction and review; an old conviction is no
unlimited employment, housing or civic exclusion license. [Q11](../15/07-proceeds-records-and-limitation.md) now supplies candidate
sealing clocks; they are not proof of downstream reintegration.

**P20 — Costs, migration and incomplete statutes.** Before competent adoption,
map source offenses, pending cases, accrued claims, custody times and sentences;
apply lawful leniency and assign independent review/correction, never bulk
registry deletion. Preserve support and valid cases through funded successors.
Police/prosecution performance may not use arrest, charge or conviction
quotas or individual rewards for those counts. Fines/forfeiture do not fund
the pursuing office; lawful receipts use the ordinary elected Treasury route.
Safety, errors, rights and actual remedies remain distinct assessments.
Cost distinct investigators, prosecutors, independent counsel, judges, clerks,
forensics, safe witnesses, custody health/inspection, receiving services,
records and executed judgments in C06's joint account. T01–T12 do not postpone
an enacted liberty limit or extend a caretaker. No full-function cost, actual
qualified roster, source-independent evidence or delivered remedy is verified.

implementation and context-specific annexes remain incomplete. [TS/TD](../22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md)
now adds explicit technical predicates; no analogy authorizes missing crimes/
sanctions. Next audit the whole ordinary-law/resource/public-control settlement.
