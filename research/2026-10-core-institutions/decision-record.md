## Diagnosis, rival models and selections

Design 6 could name a health or care entitlement while relying on a contract
without any provider able to take over. It required non-AI access but did not
assign the owner of identity, exchange, decisions, technical standards and appeal
as a coherent public-power chain. Its criminal-law safeguards did not settle the
penal choice between prevention, restoration, supervision and confinement. These
are text and institutional gaps, not observations that an existing country failed.
More staff might solve some failures; different control of resources and authority
might solve others. Neither a new office nor a common data layer creates staff,
safety or democratic legitimacy.

| Decision | Selected working arrangement | Serious rival and unresolved tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Health, education and care | Public guarantee and sector-specific public delivery backbone, with regulated additional providers; separate democratic funding, purchaser/provider, professional, safety and rights decisions. | A predominantly direct national service could provide clearer command and less selection but concentrate employment and professional power. Vouchers alone may offer choice where providers exist, yet buy no worker or school place in a thin market. Mixed provision adds transaction cost and can drain the public backbone. |
| Digital and AI power | Publicly governed layered infrastructure: law and funding by Assembly; records and consequential decisions by sector authorities; limited technical operator; separate data, sector, electoral, audit and court review; equivalent assisted routes and practiced exit. | Sector autonomy with contracts can be simpler where common dependencies are small. One integrated state platform may reduce coordination cost but combine authentication, records, eligibility, speech reach and appeal in one capturable controller. Layering can itself create cost, interfaces and hidden shared-vendor dependence. |
| Crime and justice | Differentiate immediate safety, prospective criminalization and fair proof, proportionate disposition with a no-sanction comparison, voluntary restoration, humane custody when justified, and delivered reintegration. | A funded version of the prior draft may be enough if a real system already provides the same boundaries. Deterrence-first custody can protect against some grave threats but expands coercive error and capture; restoration-first can preserve relationships but expose dependent survivors or privatize official abuse. The selected model is complex and can widen supervision unless restrained. |

This selection retains **incompatible alternatives** as real alternatives, not
ingredients silently absorbed. Public services are not required to use one
ownership form everywhere; the public backbone is an explicit provisional choice.
No-sanction comparison does not bar proportionate custody for grave harm.
Restorative refusal is not an aggravating fact. A human signature on an AI output
is not meaningful review without competence, time and power to change it.

## Joined authority, capacity and objections

The Assembly establishes rights, crimes, taxation, spending and purposes by law;
local elected bodies arrange lawful local services; professional workers make
bounded case judgments; prosecutors and courts decide individual criminal cases;
sector authorities remain answerable for delivery. A technical operator cannot
define entitlement or the electorate. A contractor cannot end a public duty by
exiting. A regulator cannot certify its own provider's success. Opposition and
affected people need information and independent challenge, but neither an
advisory user board nor AI agent votes replace democratic authority. Reviewers'
appointment, removal, funds, data access and remedy execution must be traced
against common political or vendor control.

The three choices compete for **the same** trained people, premises, transport,
data security, payments and public cash. Public capacity in hospitals, schools
and care, alternate digital routes, legal aid, investigation, shelters, humane
custody and reintegration must enter C06's obligation and physical accounts.
Existing treatment, education, care, legal claims and custody duties continue
while institutions change. A court can require lawful action and expose a
shortfall, but cannot create a clinician or a school building. Where resources
are absent, elected institutions must choose lawful financing or revision while
unmet claims stay visible.

Material objections remain: a public provider can become a patronage network;
mixed provision may select easy cases; a purchaser and regulator may share a
controller; a single health pool may ration invisibly; local school or care
control may preserve unequal access. Digital layering may raise cost and cyber
exposure while concealing a common cloud or credential owner; mandatory human
review may become ceremonial. Criminal alternatives may expand surveillance;
under-protection may leave serious violence recurring; prosecutors can use
discretion to shield allies. These are not resolved by scores or written checks.
People directly affected would have to contest definitions, tradeoffs and remedies.

The supporting analyses cite WHO, UNESCO, ILO, UNDP, OHCHR, NIST, OECD and UN
criminal-justice instruments as primary institutional sources for general
standards, risks and design questions. Their evidence families may overlap.
They do not measure AIcountry, endorse this combination or establish independent
validation. New events, source corrections and objections must be checked before
reusing these conclusions.

A further same-controller AI integration check found two missing executors in
the initial design-7 wording. A national service guarantee could either permit
cabinet seizure of a local school or care network on a vague failure claim, or
leave no one authorized to take over. The revised C04 requires a prior-law
observable trigger, local notice/cure where safe, narrow temporary sector powers,
preauthorized resources, independent review and return to local control; it
distinguishes refusal from shortage and national underfunding. The penal model
promised humane custody and release without naming an executor. The revised C08/C10
select separate civilian corrections under court orders, independent inspection,
health-authority clinical duty and a funded local reentry handoff. These are
bounded research repairs, not independent validation or actual capacity.

## Migration, status and next bounded decision

Design 7 changes C04, C06–C08 and C10–C14. Affected consumers include the
proposed A1–A9 authority chain and older health, education, care, privacy,
AI-service, procurement, criminal-code, criminal-procedure, prison, victim-support,
family, budget and local-government manuscripts. No earlier review transfers to
new legal language. A real transition must inventory law, staff, contracts,
insurance liabilities, school places, ongoing treatment and care, pending reports,
cases, appeals and custody, protected data, credentials, keys and vendor
dependencies. It needs successor authorities, funded overlap, safe records,
fair process for workers and providers, and review of prior convictions affected
by a changed evidence rule. It cannot retroactively create crimes, lengthen
sentences, remove an existing service by renaming it, or make a digital credential
the price of a constitutional right.

**Evidence:** textual comparison and official conceptual sources, without original
AIcountry observations or independent review. **Research selection:** the three
arrangements enter design 7 as conditional reference choices. **Legal authorization:**
none; existing source-edition permissions and constitutional holds remain.
**Operational readiness:** unknown, including trained staff, actual replacement
providers, safe custody, secure interoperable systems, independent institutions,
subannual cash and physical sites. **Observed outcomes:** none. Link checks and
authored scenarios demonstrate neither social progress nor safe operation.

The next bounded *real-world* decision is whether an affected public in a lawful
jurisdiction wants to compare this package with genuinely different democratic
packages. If so, its authorized process should first produce one current-law and
dependency map for a representative area and test a joint service/provider,
digital-outage and safety case with actual staff, cash, rights reviewers and
affected people. Exact offense and sentence provisions, service standards and
technical duties would then need fresh legislative and constitutional review.
If no such jurisdiction or mandate exists, maintain this as a qualified reference
design, invite concrete contradictions or alternatives, and do not invent readiness.
Reopen the selection if a simpler lawful architecture delivers equal or better
usable access and public control, if public capacity is captured, if digital
alternatives are unusable, if penal alternatives widen coercion without safety,
or if affected people reject the value or authority choices.
