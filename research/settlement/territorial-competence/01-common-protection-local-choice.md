# Govern at the scale of the obligation

*Design508 · First-round institutional choice and constructed comparisons.*

A local council can remain elected while losing every useful choice through a compulsory procurement framework, shared platform and conditional funding. A fully autonomous council can also pass a material cost downstream or fail to serve a resident who cannot enter the local channel. Select a protected national floor with enforceable local competence. Separate an outcome standard, a common method, a temporary remedy and permanent reassignment. Keep the lawful elected and judicial routes usable when a minister or local majority contests that distinction.

| Founding setting | Selected path and strongest rival |
|---|---|
| Compact polity with reachable legislature | Start with elected local choice, nationally funded common protection and an Assembly decision on genuine residual effects. Compare a consolidated national provider where local fixed costs are high. Preserve locally controllable methods where they meet the same floor. |
| Polycentric polity with repeated decisions inside a coherent area | Compare repaired Assembly capacity, bounded compacts for funded operations and an elected regional body with exact delegated classes, independently paid receipt and a transferred budget. Select the regional route if national repair repeatedly fails, the region actually owns the decision and its start and continuing operation qualify. Keep material effects outside its scope on their lawful route. |
| Plural constituent or protected territorial setting | Separately test the negotiated constitutional allocation, including asymmetric or federal competence, national equality and continuity. Where applicable protected self-government requires a separate disposition, identify the rights holders and competent authorities before relying on ordinary regions or a nationwide vote. Maintain access to individual remedies and distinct collective rights through any disagreement. |

## One national objective, two local routes

A proposed national rule requires an essential transport booking to become a confirmed trip by its useful deadline. A dense municipality can meet that outcome through ordinary dispatch. A remote one can use a funded telephone and radio dispatcher, but the proposed compulsory app stops accepting requests during a local outage. In both routes keep an independent appeal and existing travel claims. Assume twenty eligible people in each area and matching qualified vehicles.

| Constructed option | Dense municipality | Remote municipality | Consequence |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Compulsory app | 20 timely trips | 12 timely trips | Eight people lose timely receipt despite the national system showing the remote office as active. |
| Common receipt requirement with an equivalent local route | 20 timely trips | 20 timely trips | Requires a separately funded remote shift and verification of actual trip receipt. |
| Local discretion through an unsigned referral | 20 timely trips | 12 timely trips | Reporting the remote dispatch problem transfers neither vehicles nor timely accountability. |

Select the funded equivalent local route when it qualifies. A standardized minimum dispatch message may be necessary when adjacent areas share a driver and otherwise both count the same trip slot. The common message still leaves the local receiver its method. If the remote shift is paid from a line already required for a second trip or judicial transport, the stated alternative fails. Keep the due trip and restore the independently funded route or record nonreceipt, useful-time remedy and the following call.

## A refusal from each direction

If national finance makes the remote shift conditional on use of the compulsory app, separate the amount actually owed from optional support. Apply the ordinary grant and court rules. If the local majority refuses any verified route for an unpopular group, protect those residents through the lawful remedy and separately funded limited substitute. Scope and pay both interventions. A case can require timely service and reject a broader compulsory platform simultaneously. Neither event transfers the council's future policy jurisdiction. A court order, a valid payment and an executed trip remain distinct.

A compact involving three councils also proposes an extra draw that materially affects a fourth municipality and a collective rights holder. Use the lawful material-effects route with direct outside participation and applicable separate consent. A limited region over the first three could decide its properly assigned in-area classes but cannot dispose of the outside effect. Reuse the earlier matched docket to test the alternative. If the national route lacks capacity for the material residual, keep existing operations and provide the applicable urgent relief. Return the unresolved allocation to the competent elected body. Neither the compact majority nor a predicted consensus authorizes it.

An inward-facing phase can proceed only if independently lawful, financially viable and physically operable without counting the disputed intake or exporting its burden. A 70–30 regional vote and an enacted national appropriation do not supply a separate consent over a protected site. Use distinct standing and competent review where the right or scope is disputed. Keep current safe dispatch funded, and give downstream users a direct remedy without changing their electoral standing.

## Count the people and resources on both maps

Use the existing [equalization benchmark](../../country/C06/42/06-home-deferral-revenue-share-and-equalization.md), with illustrative need and capacity in the same units. Two catchments each have standardized need of 50 and own capacity of 10 and 90. Applying the illustrative grant formula separately yields 42.5 and zero. Applying it to a merged unit yields 25. The difference of 17.5 is a change in the formula result. It becomes a defensible financing change only when the proposed authority demonstrates lawful pooling, actual cash at each cutoff and usable delivery and review throughout both old catchments. Compare the old and new structure before voting, preserve legally due grants on original terms and state any transition source and expiry. Even if all 25 grant units reach the poorer catchment, its own 10 leave 15 needed. If only five units from the wealthier catchment can lawfully arrive by cutoff, there is a ten-unit local gap. Distinguish a permanent restriction on eligible capacity from a transfer that is simply late. If funds remain restricted to the other catchment or the qualified receiver fails, preserve the existing route, correct the shortfall and hold the dependent prospective transfer.

The comparison uses hypothetical receipt counts. It tests the legal and operational distinction between protecting an outcome and requiring an identical method. Actual comparative outcomes, adoption, autonomy settlements and resource use require different evidence.
