# T37–T42. Choose the first programme before outcomes are known

*Design414 · Proposed addition to [staged capacity and protection](../06-staged-capacity-and-protection.md).*

**T37 — Compare plans that can be followed in real time.** For a material
common risk, the first budget compares complete sequences of decisions
with a dated record of what evidence is usable when. Include ordinary
duties, worker and claimant rights, elected and independent functions,
and actual authority. Scenarios with the same usable evidence at a
decision time must share the same commitments and protected allocations
made by then. They may diverge only after an available, lawful, material
distinction. A portfolio that starts a different long-lead project in
each branch before anyone could tell them apart is a retrospective
comparison. It cannot support actual readiness or release.

**T38 — Work back from the last useful start.** For each material response,
show when the warning can be detected and independently verified, who
can lawfully decide, and the real time for payment, procurement, travel,
handover and receiving. Include urgent review before the relevant loss.
Distinguish early signals from confirmed harm. Test missing, contradictory,
late and inaccessible signals, including a shared failure of the evidence
and delivery routes. After the useful start has passed, count only an
actually qualified faster route. Do not move an old obligation's deadline
or treat later evidence as if it were already available.

**T39 — Vote a priced preparation and response path.** Compare at least
one fixed preparation, waiting with ordinary repairs and a staged
alternative where feasible. Report the specific constraint if a route
cannot qualify. Give the legislature the actual first
commitment, cost of unused options, worker and donor cover, lawful
authority, later source and stop conditions on the same calendar.
A bounded option names its willing providers, accepted capacity,
conditions, allowed simultaneous calls and expiry. Paying to retain
alternatives does not authorize all calls. Reserve existing claims,
independent review and ongoing operations before new discretionary
exposure. State what public needs wait and who bears that loss.

**T40 — Apply only the authorized branches and expose failure.** Name the
officer and original evidence for each permitted activation. Define
what may proceed when the signal is missing or several calls arrive.
Preauthorization stays within its exact scope, independent judgment and
source. If multiple risks materialize and no funded lawful combination
works, carry all existing duties, use qualified ordinary and urgent
paths and report uncovered acts. A model cannot resolve a statutory
priority conflict. Material new scope, authority or spending returns to
competent elected choice without pretending a late vote meets an old
deadline.

**T41 — Preserve public control and check receipt.** Retain the adopted
sequence, observations available at each date, option costs, affected
people and actual service. Independent reviewers can reproduce the
chronology without the proposer or AI supplier. Give affected people
and opposition timely access and a real route to challenge unrealistic
signals, budgets, delay and uncovered groups. An earlier option payment
can expire while a resident's claim and remedy continue. Compare delivery,
missed windows, unused costs and the actual next-call balance before
reporting recovery. Keep judicial, clinical and elected decisions in
their existing scopes.

**T42 — Apply the comparison to the first programme.** The constructed
joint budget below selects a paid single-call reserve in its declared
single-failure scope. It leaves compound failure, lost evidence and
unfunded replenishment explicit. A different observation date, provider
agreement, risk or cost can favor fixed preparation, earlier preventive
capacity or a different voted package. AI may replay chronologies and
flag impossible assumptions. It supplies no usable warning, accepted
staff, appropriation or lawful priority by itself. Adopt only through
ordinary source law with funded successors. Reopen on persistently late
signals, unaccepted capacity, displaced standing service or a stronger
matched rival.
