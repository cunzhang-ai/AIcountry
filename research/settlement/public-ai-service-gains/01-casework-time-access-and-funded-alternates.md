# A faster desk and a slower route to help

An authored service window begins with the same 100 eligible attempts,
including 20 people using assisted or non-digital entry. Twelve have an
urgent deadline. The urgent and assisted categories can overlap; neither
is added to the 100. The same law and staff qualification requirements
apply. A completion means a lawful disposition and useful receipt, and
the ordinary deadline continues for unresolved attempts. Figures include
the same unresolved and early abandonment definitions in each comparison.
Any actual cost or program result would need original records.

| Route | Routine casework hours | Verification, rework and support hours | Independent review and remedy hours | Training and separately reserved fallback hours | Total paid hours | Resident active hours | Incremental non-labor cost units |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Retain current route | 90 | 8 | 10 | 8 | 116 | 50 | 0 |
| Competent simpler route with clearer rules and assisted intake | 82 | 7 | 9 | 8 | 106 | 44 | 1 |
| Broad AI use across intake and drafting | 62 | 19 | 14 | 15 | 110 | 60 | 6 |
| Bounded optional drafting and source navigation | 68 | 10 | 9 | 11 | 98 | 40 | 3 |

The paid hour columns do not overlap. Staff and resident hours are
different units of burden for decision purposes. The exercise allows up
to three incremental non-labor units in the window. It does not assume
any wage cut, turnover or new funding. Hours spent waiting for a decision
are not added to active resident hours.

| Route | On time out of 100 | Assisted or non-digital on time out of 20 | Urgent on time out of 12 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|

The broad option saves 20 routine desk hours relative to the competent
simpler route, but adds four paid hours overall and 16 active resident
hours. It misses an urgent deadline, makes assisted receipt worse and
exceeds the non-labor limit. The larger overall completion count cannot
repair those failures.

The bounded option uses eight fewer paid hours than the competent simpler
route and requires two additional non-labor units relative to it. It
retains the same independent-review hours and the stated trained fallback.
Select it in this case, conditional on the ordinary and alternate routes
actually working. In a follow-up schedule, six of the eight releasable
hours are reserved for identifiable assisted cases and two for correction
or training within the same authorized team. Those assignments assume
the right skills and dates. Count no salary reduction and make no claim
about further improvement until it is received. The cheaper simpler
route remains a live alternative.

For a shared-root failure, assume nine ordinary urgent slots survive.
A tested independent route has three additional distinct slots. Together
they meet 12 urgent duties in the same window. If the nominal alternate
shares the failed root, all three are unavailable and only nine are
served. Limit the affected reliance, retain original deadlines and use
existing urgent correction. The alternate's staffing was already paid
in the held fallback account and cannot also supply the six reassigned
hours. This exercise reports only urgent service in the outage, without
assigning an unobserved outcome to the rest of the queue.
