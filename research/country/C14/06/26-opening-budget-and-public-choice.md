# T117–T120. Authorize, operate and review the same opening programme

*Design456 · Part of [staged capacity and protection](../06-staged-capacity-and-protection.md).*

**T117 — Put complete alternatives before the Assembly on one schedule.**
The government tenders the continuing settlement, a separable stage
and a material structural alternative where feasible. For each exact
motion identify the legal authority, each payer's gross amounts and
settlement dates, recipient, concurrent staffing and supplies,
commitment point, ordinary duties and remedies, and next period.
For each proposed budget release from another purpose, identify its
actual service, protected source, due users and work, useful dates,
and any cost of keeping or restoring access. Apply IV18–IV22 and
IV28–IV32 to material deferrals. Even uncontracted work can carry a
standing service. Count an eligible reallocation only after the
competent vote and confirmation that any required substitute is
usable and funded in the same adverse path. Keep the challenged source
and the resources needed by the substitute on the gross calendar.
Qualify national and local decisions independently under GP, DB, BCV
and MF. Expose unpriced duties and any effect on scope. A protected
independent analyst must be able to inspect original evidence, check
resource conflicts and explain which proposal relies on each finding.
Affected people and opposition retain their existing routes to
challenge inputs and submit exact alternatives. Use the same available
evidence in every branch indistinguishable at the vote or release.

**T118 — Give an executable improvement its own lawful start.** Distinguish
settlement and institutional authorization from paid delivery and
independent access to correction. A separate binding vote and each
applicable local approval identify the chosen motion. An operator
confirms receiving before committing or releasing, on the latest
useful dates in the voted instrument. If a material challenge changes
the exact text, scope, source or delivery dates before binding, rerun
affected scrutiny and consent. If the deadline passes, continue the
lawful standing programme and any separately effective fallback.
Do not infer assent from an agenda preference, silence, model output
or a vote for another budget. Tell people what improvement and whose
opportunity are deferred.

**T119 — Keep public obligations through a failed promise.** After a
commitment binds, a missing source, changed estimate or lost delivery
path invokes continuity, funded alternatives and applicable remedies
for the original claimant and date. Keep the elected decision, actual
cash, standby resources and timely service records distinct. Preserve
independent review and legal execution through the same shock. Use
reconciled records to stop duplicate payment or double assignment
of workers. Report remaining nonreceipt separately from later repair.
Reopen the affected optional or continuing line with a proposed
replacement and explicit funding. Existing protection survives.

**T120 — Renew only on a new joined decision.** Independently compare
timely useful receipt, ability to act and displaced burdens for the
original affected groups, people missed or refused, and any source
service that lost capacity. Preserve a safe direct complaint route.
Publish the uncertainty of the comparison and actual deadline losses.
A positive aggregate can justify considering a new motion. Renewal
or scale also requires an explicit vote, continuing source, qualified
workers and a separately funded second call and independent challenge.
End optional authority at its voted term while carrying all accrued
duties. AI can reconcile and compare scoped evidence. Its forecast
supplies no appropriation, approval, finding of consent or actual receipt.
