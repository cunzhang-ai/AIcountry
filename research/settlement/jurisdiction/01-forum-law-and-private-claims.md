# Forum and applicable law: a connection is not a verdict

*Part of [jurisdiction journeys](README.md); authored cases X01–X12.*

| Case | Required proposed distinction and remedy path |
|---|---|
| X01 An AI replica runs on a domestic server while every material party/act is abroad | B41 requires an actual defendant/activity/performance/injury connection; server/account location alone does not create universal civil or criminal jurisdiction. Preserve meaning/source and examine real control. No automatic operator liability. |
| X02 A person files at the wrong domestic local court while two clerks dispute venue | B42 preserves good-faith domestic filing/triage and accepted transfer, with pending orders/records. It does not manufacture foreign limitation compliance or give a clerk power to reject an urgent claim without a competent receiving route. |
| X03 A remote worker's platform chooses a foreign code and exclusive costly tribunal | Examine actual work center, dependence and B04/B43 mandatory protections; terms cannot remove protected access by nominal assent. A genuinely advised commercial choice has its distinct route. Law, court and arbitral enforcement are separate questions. |
| X04 A seller targets local customers but claims only its foreign incorporation matters | B41/B43 examines purposeful transaction/local delivery, actual residence and protected consumer terms. Mere website availability is insufficient, but targeted delivery is relevant. No conviction, all-group liability or claim payment follows from forum alone. |
| X05 A locally controlled project causes serious harm abroad under a permissive foreign permit | Hear the materially controlled domestic decisions and applicable home duties under B44; original affected-community evidence and causal defenses remain. The forum cannot direct a foreign regulator or promise overseas enforcement; unavailable remedies stay visible. |
| X06 A foreign status record declares a disabled adult incapable | Preserve identity and lawful severable effects; do not import general guardianship over B21/B23 equal agency. Actual assistance, independent advice and bounded asset/clinical authority stay separate. Foreign nationality/form alone is no refusal ground. |
| X07 A child is moved to obtain a preferred custody court and a foreign order demands immediate return | Actual habitual residence, prior competent proceedings, wrongful movement, urgent presence protection, child voice and present grave risk need independent review. No automatic favorable forum from movement or dangerous irreversible return from a database order. Actual applicable treaty remains to be proved. |
| X08 A foreign commercial judgment purports to settle parentage and a local land title | B45/B46/B47 require specific status/situs authority and severability; the generic commercial route cannot certify those matters. Child receiving/title preservation continue. A registry import is not a universal legal judgment. |
| X09 A foreign award contains principal 100, lawful costs 10 and punitive 50; 40 has been paid | Under stipulated lawful compensatory recognition, amount 110 leaves 70. Exclude the punitive increment unless a separate actual lawful route exists. Planned collections 45/50 must be reconciled to 45/25 for that balance, not 95. No arithmetic proves recognition or actual receipt. |
| X10 The foreign court's result differs from domestic policy but gave genuine notice and defense | A disliked result alone does not trigger fundamental-rights refusal or a merits retrial. Examine specific material fraud, jurisdiction, protected rights and actual process; recognize the compatible severable effect with independent challenge. |
| X11 A creditor seeks an embassy account after winning against a state-owned supplier | Decide actual defendant, separate state/firm assets and jurisdictional/execution immunity source; company ownership or a judgment alone makes no embassy funds attachable. Contractual/diplomatic options and real unmet claims are reported, not assumed paid. |
| X12 Two foreign insolvency representatives each inventory the same asset | B50 requires genuine main interests, original authority, affected access and one asset/claim ledger. A second mirror does not create funds; U's protected property/claims and confirmation standards remain. Recognition does not automatically import a foreign discharge or a taxpayer guarantee. |

All examples stipulate only the stated facts. No court, claimant, injured
community or foreign authority participated. Fair notice, material proof,
applicable source law and actual jurisdiction cannot be supplied by this table.
