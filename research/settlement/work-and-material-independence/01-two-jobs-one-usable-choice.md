# Test whether workers can actually refuse

*Design511 · Constructed bargaining, mobility and local-service comparison.*

Separate an individual's confidential exit option from public responsibility for a labour market that repeatedly leaves entire groups with no alternative. Keep ordinary wages, due support, free refusal, independent bargaining and individual remedies in every branch. Both existing and prospective employers can answer a proposed finding. Publicly financed alternatives require their own authorization.

## Count feasible choices for the same workers

In the constructed settlement, two towns share a labour catchment. Twelve workers in the affected occupation can otherwise take a credible job offer with adequate pay, independently verified shifts and transferable qualifications. Two listed recruiters are controlled by the same firm as their current employer and cannot provide an independent alternative. One separate employer offers up to twelve actual places. Independent assessment confirms compatible care for workers A–H and transport for workers E–L at the necessary dates. All other enforceable dues, including basic income protection and the participating workers' housing conditions, are held equal. Counts are people, not service acts.

| Proposed response | Result on this evidence |
|---|---|
| Count external job offers and repair ordinary recruitment only | Up to twelve offers remain, but only E–H currently have both compatible supports. |
| Repair travel or care alone | Four extra transport places for A–D, or four extra care places for I–L, give eight workers a usable offer, assuming exact receiving. |
| Fund and accept both narrow repairs | The independent employer and both service owners can cover twelve. Check resources at the originating services and the next funded call before counting them. |
| Buy a separate employer or worker-led production option | Real rival if control, payroll, work, customers, money and the same personal conditions qualify. A renamed recruiter owned by the original controller yields zero independent additional offers. |

Residents can stay, negotiate, turn down a job or leave with ordinary rights. The competent authorities select paid repairs when the same people can actually use independently confirmed opportunities. If job demand is missing, adding transport and care does not create work. Compare a costed new employer, worker-led acquisition or broader income support that buys time and reduces forced acceptance. Pay attention to whether work can continue without a public guarantee to one incumbent and who carries production risks. Protect independent bargaining during every branch, including after a public or cooperative provider enters.

## Follow a decision to its adverse branch

One support alternative uses the same driver as an already due medical trip. The overlap defeats those simultaneous slots. Continue the old trip and direct relief, use an independently qualified replacement or return the exact missing procurement to lawful decision. If the genuinely independent employer is then bought and no other independent place can be accepted, the same service repair leaves zero independent jobs for this cohort. The case for competition review concerns the effect of that acquisition against a real alternative future under the applicable legal test. If ownership was always lawfully concentrated and no breach is proved, use the elected public-service and labour route. Do not merge case evidence into a power to restructure through a market study.

In a separate onboarding branch, the outside employer controls its own strategy but an incumbent-controlled verification intermediary can block six of the twelve offers before their start date. Both support repairs then leave six usable offers. Lawful portable proof accepted by an independent verifier restores twelve on these assumptions. Ordinary common vendors that cannot block a timely receiving path leave all twelve possible. In a distinct later stress, the outside employer and incumbent depend on one buyer. Once its orders stop, all twelve new jobs can disappear despite independent hiring. A verified replacement buyer and usable working capital could preserve eight in this branch. Do not book those eight without committed orders, lawful cash and confirmed work. Use continuing household protection and independent bargaining through the shortfall, and bring productive alternatives to elected funding decisions.

A shortage can make training and wages preferable to funding a nominal new employer. Use the same people and dates to check new qualified work, safe household conditions, real unpaid burdens, services lost at origin, ability to contest work conditions and the options left after the next roster. This example assumes jobs and certain receipt for the specified supports only. Actual effects need real workers' and residents' evidence and independent verification.
