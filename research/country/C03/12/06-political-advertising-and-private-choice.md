# PC25–PC31. Protect private political choice in paid distribution

*Design387 · Proposed ordinary law under [continuous political finance](../12-continuous-political-finance.md).*

**PC25 — Apply the targeting rule to actual paid electoral delivery.**
Use PC01–PC03's paid electoral definition and actual principal.
Cover material creative selection, audience exclusion, bid
optimization and distribution carried out for that paid activity,
whether chosen by sponsor, publisher, broker, intermediary or AI.
Prove the paid act and the actual link; a shared platform or similar
views do not establish a campaign. PC01's ordinary issue advocacy
and PC16's material issue disclosure keep their existing scope.
Editorial choice, satire, direct conversation, unpaid sharing and
an individual’s own choice of a news or campaign source retain
ordinary protection. General privacy law remains separately enforceable.

**PC26 — Allow context and voluntary subscription, bound personal targeting.**
Permit lawful paid placement based on the subject of a publication
or other nonpersonal context, and broad coverage of the actual contest
area without using individual profiles. For any other permitted use
of personal data, require directly supplied task-relevant data and
separate, explicit, freely revocable permission for the stated paid
electoral use and controller. Show a neutral route without giving
that permission. Do not condition ordinary access, work, services or
voting on agreement. Do not target or exclude using inferred beliefs,
sensitive identity, health, economic distress, private communications,
protected public records or proxies for these. Do not expand a
permissioned list to similar unconsenting people. Keep a genuinely
requested subscription distinct from permission to build a profile
or follow someone across services.

**PC27 — Make the message and actual placement inspectable.** The
publisher gives a usable sponsor and placement explanation at
first delivery. Under PC16, archive the actual material versions,
dates, sponsor, material inclusion and exclusion criteria, value
or range, and safe aggregate delivered reach. For generated or
adaptive variants retain original evidence of what was delivered.
Make a substantively different message inspectable when first
used. Record material later changes and keep the existing invoice
correction timetable. Routine translation or formatting preserves
its source and any substantive difference. Use a safe public record
or summary where an original would expose private data, with an
independent protected route to inspect necessary originals.
Public records must not disclose audience identities, lists,
individual profiles or small groups from which these can be inferred.

**PC28 — Test the route that actually determines reach.** The effective
sponsor and provider keep responsibility for their respective facts
and decisions. Give authorized electoral and privacy reviewers
independent access to necessary original delivery and version records,
including material platform optimization, exclusion and evidence of
lawful permission. A declared broad audience cannot excuse proved
profile-based delivery. Accept individual and collective complaints
through a paid safe route, without requiring disclosure of a vote,
complete device history or public identification. Source protection,
purpose limits, bounded retention and independent review govern.
No duty to collect or reconstruct a national political profile follows.

**PC29 — Correct the paid act without controlling the argument.** Use
existing direct PC18, PD and court routes on original deadlines.
Require concrete evidence of an unlawful distribution practice or
omitted material disclosure, safe reply and proportionate remedy.
Prefer timely correction of sponsor, delivery rule and archive, or a
lawful contextual substitute. Where an applicable independent court
order is necessary, address only the proved paid placement, use or
data practice and preserve unrelated speech and lawful dissemination.
No automatic suspension, new crime, official truth decision, wider
message removal or vote annulment follows. ER21–ER25 separately
governs false voting instructions. Keep limited review, expiry and
adversarial safeguards for any urgent order.

**PC30 — Pay for usable correction and safe alternative access.**
Price independent archives, verification, hearings, execution and
reachable public explanations under the existing accountable bodies.
Keep baseline contestant access and independent voter information
working under CF and EL. Verify actual corrected delivery and the
earliest opportunity still reachable. Report earlier lost
opportunities separately. A shared platform, payer, source or
credential cannot supply two independent routes. Preserve legitimate
cross-border speech and apply only actual lawful domestic or
cooperative jurisdiction over paid acts and their controllers.

**PC31 — Review effects, market exclusion and new media.** Test broad
contextual distribution, bounded voluntary personal delivery and a
disclosure-only rival on the same privacy, cost, reach and dissent
conditions. Use prospective PC and RR adoption, paid successors and
original contest dates. Preserve actual permissions and accrued
remedies within their lawful scope. AI may compare retained
versions or identify audit gaps. It cannot infer consent, decide
political truth, compel a correction or certify its own reach.
Reopen on evasion, inaccessible lawful campaigns, a chilled
publication, privacy harm or a better independently usable route.
