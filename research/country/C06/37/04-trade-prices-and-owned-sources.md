# PS13–PS16: paid trade, prices and owned sources

*Part of [C06/37](../37-physical-supply-and-paid-trade.md); design92 research.*

**PS13 — Imports have products, suppliers and a payment date.** Domestic
production and paid trade serve actual needs together. Record commodity/quality,
quantity, source/control, route, currency, landed quote, delivery, duties,
payment/credit dates and cancellation/default. Distinguish foreign production
and shipping from domestic unloading/distribution and taxes; avoid buying the
same transport or ingredient twice. Resilience requires practically distinct
routes/accepting alternatives, not two brokers drawing on the same source.
Trade remains plural under C10/customs, labor, safety and external-harm law;
the supply account creates no state import monopoly or new treaty power.

**PS14 — Prices must reach the household and public account.** Full producer
prices reconcile qualified pay, intermediates, land, capital services, actual
finance, taxes, losses and allowed returns. Named physical/payroll/border quotes
are not complete retail/utility tariffs. Reconcile new prices with I03 housing,
I05/IC ordinary provision, S received services, Y indexation, tax/contribution
incidence and current owners' contractual obligations. A low import bill is no
affordability finding; a high tariff is no net public revenue before assistance.

The new prototype substitutes only matching physical scopes; it does not
silently replace old fixed-price GDP, tax, pension or benefit histories. Do not
add its new water/food/electricity inputs to the old full purchase lines as a
second national cost. An actual new price/supply selection requires fresh joined
source/incidence review; accrued/overdue and foreign/source-law claims continue.

**PS15 — Owned reserves are not one national pot.** Separate public purchasing
authority, private importer funds, bank/client assets, monetary reserves,
pension property, SF standby, supplier credit and foreign-debt receipts. A
source record needs actual owner, currency, release authority, date and continuing
claim. Expected receipts, inventory valuation and recoveries are not cleared
money. Public equity/new borrowing needs its own lawful source and risk account;
there is no forced private FX sweep, debt purchase, profit payout or household saving.

Opening food/water stocks require additional one-time purchases/production and
safe custody. Normal rotation replaces ordinary purchases; do not buy the whole
reserve again annually. Add actual spoilage, emergency draws, replenishment,
warehouse/tank peaks and working capital. A protected stock cannot simultaneously
fund ordinary payroll or be counted as an untouched second contingency.

**PS16 — Export an accepted product, not a balancing residual.** Retire the
old500m export assumption as support for this supply choice. For an export
proposal show buyer/controller, product/quality, paid human/AI inputs, imported
tools/services, electricity, delivery and collection. Actual source claims use
settled receipts at their due dates; orders and author-created buyer scenarios
are conditional, not actual exports or state revenue. Actual exports are unknown,
not asserted to be zero; this model credits no verified receipt.

The optional200,000billable-hour digital-service order would gross20mCU but
needs285,714 human task hours at70% billability,258 annual professional posts,
imported services, power and pre-receipt finance. These are new obligations,
not an automatic AI dividend or idle workers. Gross private receipts do not
belong to Treasury; taxes/distributions need actual law and receipt. Even an
accepted order cannot be sized merely to close the country's import gap.
