# NS05–NS08: production, net financial saving and monetary finance

**NS05 — Reconcile the whole circuit, not just a payer's missing cash.** Keep
G/local/S, monetary, firm/bank, household and foreign ownership before explicit
consolidation. In a complete consistently valued account, public net borrowing
has private and foreign net-lending counterparts. This is a transaction identity,
not a chosen production level, lender offer, welfare result or instruction to save.
Actual statistical discrepancies, investment, foreign income/transfers and
valuation changes cannot be silently omitted in a real national application.

In the independent authored circuit, public pay 90 + purchases 40 + transfers 60
minus household tax 30/firm tax 10 gives **150u net borrowing**. Household net
lending 80 + firm/financial-sector40 + foreign30 matches 150. Selling 30f of
actually public-owned FX at 1u/f reduces new domestic claim issuance to 120u;
the asset sale is not recurring tax revenue. No number scales to the 100,000-person
country. Existing cash-book peaks, held renewal/other scope, principal, tax
receivables and partial source gaps are not a national deficit to plug into it.

**NS06 — Preserve private choices and distribution.** Household saving, company
retained profit, private investment, bank credit and received capital income
are different. A matching financial counterpart need not be a willing purchaser
of the offered bond on the required date. Bank lending changes gross paired
private claims, not aggregate private net wealth by itself. Do not spend all
benefits on paper to generate a tax base or count unpaid household care as cash.
The circuit assumes accepted quantities/receipts to examine compatibility; it
does not estimate desired saving, demand, wages, inflation or output response.

**NS07 — Retain RF without a household-budget analogy.** Recurring tax/insurance,
actual distributable returns and expressly bounded debt remain the ordinary
planning choice, not a constitutional annual balanced budget or a claim that
all public spending needs prior private saving. Deficits can accompany desired
private net saving, external positions, investment and shocks. A persistent
deficit requires its continuing instruments, distribution, real/price/foreign
effects and lawful authority explained; it is neither automatically immoral
nor automatically sustainable. Do not choose residual debt or new issue to make
an unprovided economy pass. RF/MC actual source and Treasury-credit boundaries
remain until legitimate coordinated revision.

**NS08 — Compare bonds and fiscal issue on the same scope.** In the circuit,
accepted bonds and separately authorized zero-interest fiscal money can both
leave 120u new domestic claims and the same150u public net borrowing/real flows.
They differ in instruments, holders, rates, liquidity and future losses. A
5% payment on those new claims costs 6u next period; it is not modeled as a free
zero-rate continuation. Both examples lose their one-period new-finance authority
in period 2; the separate repeated-FX stress is not a renewed lawful spending path.
A 100u bond offer fails the120u financing requirement even when an identity holds.
No model score, fiscal office, central AI or central bank chooses new taxes,
benefit cuts, public debt, issue authority or postponed elections by itself.
