# CRD01–CRD04. Proof and divided trust

[Scoped credentials](../13-scoped-credentials-and-public-access.md)

**CRD01 — Choose a scoped public proof service.** Prior law specifies
the purposes, authority and review for material public credential use.
The relevant source custodian attests an authoritative fact within its
own scope. A credential operator may convey proof or authenticate an
act. The relying service verifies and decides under its applicable law.
Separate the fact, its current proof, the actor's authority, the
person's consent and the delivered outcome. A credential does not
create status, mandate, eligibility, payment or ownership. A vendor
or optional AI tool cannot acquire these powers by adoption or scale.

**CRD02 — Publish the real trust and acceptance boundary.** Each
competent service publishes a purpose-specific acceptance rule
under prior law, naming source and issuer, permitted proof,
recipient, freshness, expiry, revocation handling, alternate
verification and review. Set scope and rights through law.
A separately qualified service officer administers acceptance.
DP may maintain interoperable descriptions and a public directory
of institutional policies and providers. The directory holds no
person index and grants no legal authority. Reliance requires
tested scoped custody and an independent source route. A common
standard does not require one signing root, vendor, recovery
operator or contract. A provider or key reused under another label
remains the same relevant dependency.

**CRD03 — Make portable proof optional and lawful refusal practical.**
Allow qualified local, assisted, paper or other usable verification
under the same legal standing and due times. Do not condition an
essential public service or political right on enrolment in a wallet,
a particular device, a routine biometric or an AI account. Preserve
case-specific proof and existing sector requirements. Apply the
least burdensome sufficient verification for the act. Help the person
retrieve or correct source evidence. Record first receipt even when
a necessary fact is still disputed. A slower or geographically
unreachable alternate channel is a capacity failure. The service
must fund a route that can actually reach people in time.

**CRD04 — Minimize identifying links and private exposure.** Request
only the fact and context needed under PD and applicable law.
Prefer scoped identifiers or unlinkable presentations where possible.
Test timing, metadata, issuer and wallet logs, common controllers
and repeated exchanges for correlation. A different alias alone
does not establish privacy. Do not build a general verification
history, person index or mandatory cross-service identifier.
Keep source, relying decision and any necessary audit record in
their lawful scopes. A person can contest an unnecessary request
or inferred link without losing unrelated services. Existing
sector-specific retention and compelled-access law retain their terms.
