# Design245 decision record. Staffing formation and the first service budget

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned Design244](baseline.json)

## Decision and evidence

Select [WF85–WF89](../country/C06/58/20-staffing-formation-and-first-service-capacity.md).
Carry the existing first motion for repair and paid preparation, with
separate authority for commitment and release. Where supervisor and
existing service coverage are tight, put a costed retention and return
alternative before the Assembly alongside the preparation option.
A national learning and staffing programme has to maintain both the
supplying and receiving services. The country cannot improve one region
by recording the same qualified worker in another region's unpaid margin.
People retain the ability to move, organize and choose their own careers.
Use the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

The [observed Danish comparison](../settlement/staffing-formation/01-danish-training-agreements-and-roles.md)
shows reported 2024 municipal apprenticeship agreements above the combined
target with an assistant-stream shortfall. Older dropout data and separate
published funding for supervisors concern different stages. The historical
case identifies a failure in treating all offers and training as usable
service. It does not show whether training, working conditions, service
reorganization or another route would solve the binding constraint in a
particular AIcountry setting.

## Compare real options

| Choice | Strong reason | Material exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Preserve current staffed duties and repair working conditions and access to voluntary return | Can protect near-term work using already qualified people and improve their lives. | Retention can expire and does not replace structural exits or create missing qualifications. Voluntary return needs actual accessible paid terms. |
| Purchase or transfer already qualified capacity | Can supply an urgent receiving service sooner when real extra qualified time exists. | Scarce staff may be lost elsewhere. A provider contract does not erase another duty or turn unqualified time into care. |
| Start a paid training cohort with funded supervision, backfill and teach-out | Can build otherwise unavailable qualifications and expand career choice. | Draws scarce mentors before it generates reliable shifts. Completion, voluntary deployment and retention differ, and binding learner claims continue if the service plan fails. |
| Redesign tasks and safely verify released time | Can remove burdens on workers and residents without moving a worker from a second place. | Tool use can increase checking, paperwork, appeal burdens or common failure. Only net verified staff time and safe received service count. |

In the [authored comparison](../settlement/staffing-formation/02-two-areas-and-a-training-cohort.md)
fund retention and voluntary return and keep a smaller staggered cohort
as a separately voted conditional release. The full intake loses protected
coverage if its early replacement fails. The smaller cohort with retention
fits both limits in the declared loss, at the cost of committing the full
illustrative margin and deferring some learners. Retention alone leaves
room for other needs. The selection relies on a value choice to form one
later qualification-based service shift where coverage and closeout are
actually verified. It does not give this allocation priority over a better
matched whole-budget alternative.

## Authority and transition

National and local elected source decisions use DB, BCV, MF and JC.
Participating employers, schools and service owners attest contractual,
qualified and actual receiving capacity in their scopes. Professional
qualification, care and direct claims retain their established authorities.
Treasury and independent oversight reconcile shared money and protected
duties without allocating careers. A real domestic or cross-border
recruitment path must name origin and downstream effects within applicable
rights and existing C10 protections. Preserve actual jobs, lawful pay,
education, remedies and open successor duties through failure and migration.
Do not seek repayment from learners to cover ordinary forecast error.
Actual affected-party participation, qualified independent review, law,
operating readiness and observed welfare remain open.

## Next bounded country decision

Compare one real shared care role across two participating service owners
within their approved staffing and review resources. Obtain matched paid
rosters, protected workload, origin and receiver constraints, qualified
supervision and contracted retention, backfill and teach-out costs.
Include actual user access and worker time on the same useful dates, with
private evidence held safely. Complete one comparable record of who can
receive care under each budget or record exact uncovered duties and who
can lawfully correct them. Reopen on verified structural exits, a stronger

assurance rather than inferring missing work or failed people.
