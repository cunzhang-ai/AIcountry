# C08. Protect people who report serious wrongdoing

*Design202 · WB01–WB12 · Proposed civil protection and evidence law.*

**People can report serious wrongdoing outside the institution involved
and seek protection before retaliation defeats the report.** The
competent sector examines the underlying facts. An independent court
protects the person, evidence and fair hearing. The relevant officials
still decide any service, budget or other public act under its own law.

| Canonical part | Selected rule |
|---|---|
| [WB01–WB04. Reach a protected receiver](31/01-protected-people-and-channels.md) | Set scope, external entry, safe custody and a response that preserves the original evidence. |
| [WB05–WB08. Public disclosure and retaliation](31/02-disclosure-and-retaliation-tests.md) | Protect proportionate disclosure, identify material retaliation, allow timely relief and apply a civil causation test. |
| [WB09–WB12. Executed remedies and independent correction](31/03-remedies-and-public-correction.md) | Preserve defense, actual pay and service, independent oversight and evidence correction without a central truth authority. |

This common civil floor joins the current sector protections and applies
only through valid adoption. [B17–B20](13/05-orders-execution-and-collective-claims.md)
governs urgent court relief, independent public execution and received
remedies. Existing stronger protection and separate offenses, privileges,
safety and privacy duties remain.
