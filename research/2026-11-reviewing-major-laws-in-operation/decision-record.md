# Design355 decision on reviewing major laws in operation

## Baseline and selection

Pin Design354, PCO01–PCO10, PE, GP, PWR, ordinary remedies and the
[working method](../FRAMEWORK.md). PCO04 starts a response to an independent
finding. PWR requires express continuation of defined material delegations.
Select a scheduled elected disposition for a material primary measure even
when no independent finding has yet been received. The adopting Act and
Assembly procedure control the actual choice. The project assistant proposes
the 24- and 36-month limits, 60 days for evidence and one-tenth endorsement
for the minority route. No actual schedule, reviewer or funds have been
authorized.

## Alternatives and objections

A ministry-only review consumes fewer independent resources but lets the
operator control coverage and whether an issue reaches a vote. Expiry of
whole primary Acts puts harder pressure on a legislature, but creates a
risk of services and accrued commitments falling into a gap. Keep express
expiry for powers where existing law specifies it. Select a separate
review calendar and elected options for primary measures, with evidence
and agenda access funded outside the operator's discretion.

Some consequences mature slowly, and small legislatures may be overloaded.
Set a later outcome test without moving the first decision on implementation,
unmet needs and early effects. Publish why scopes can share hearings or
evidence, and price protected staff and hearings across all starts before
deciding their timing. A missing review stays a duty and can reach an
ordinary procedural remedy. No reviewer can substitute a policy decision.
An evaluated majority could still vote to retain a bad policy. Preserve
effective opposition, disclosed reasons, direct rights remedies and voter
replacement. Protect private testimony and publish bounded comparison
rather than constructing a permanent life record.

## Scoped evidence, continuity and next action

The [primary sources](sources.md) support dated scrutiny and a distinction
between review and expiry. The [constructed comparison](../settlement/reviewing-major-laws/01-a-review-that-survives-implementation-delay.md)
tests a delayed launch, changed operator name, denominator change,
overlapping deadlines and a fixed budget. It establishes no real effect,
authorized amendment, funded review team or received remedy.

Carry original dates, evidence scope, rights, partial results and money
through the next government and any prospective amendment. Before
implementation, compare two real material measures and their next review
windows against actual committee, review, witness and fiscal capacity.
The responsible legislature would commission this within its authorized
scrutiny budget, with consent and safe access to affected evidence.
Stop at an exact agenda and capacity comparison. Reopen if priority
displaces protected hearings, the common reviewer is dependent on the
operator, or ordinary scrutiny provides equivalent follow-through at
lower cost.
