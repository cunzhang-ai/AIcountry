# PCO01–PCO05. Compare lived outcomes and respond to hidden regression

*Part of [public conditions](../04-public-conditions.md). Design252 research selection.*

**PCO01 — State the claim and the population before comparing.** For a
material national outcome claim, specify the measure, original data,
period, jurisdiction, eligible or affected population and what practical
change is meant. Keep three comparisons distinct: actual population
conditions, a same-scope comparison against a credible alternative and
the effect plausibly attributable to the intervention. Include relevant
cost, safety, worker and resident time, collective control and future
resource effects. Write down earlier exclusions and data vintages.
Precommit what would qualify as improvement, deterioration, a mixed
result or unknown. Do not infer that longer lives, higher cash income
or more completed cases settle another dimension of a good life.

**PCO02 — Show who is missing and what changed within comparable groups.**
Where coverage or composition can affect the claim, publish the actual
observed figures, a fixed-composition comparison where meaningful, and
separate counts or credible bounds for excluded or unobserved people.
Identify the reference weights and any material sensitivity to another
defensible reference. Name changed eligibility, channel, migration,
loss, nonresponse or classification that can explain movement. A fixed set of weights does
not follow individuals or identify a cause. When justified, use limited
lawful cohort evidence or safe sampling rather than tracking everyone's
life. Do not put unknown outcomes into the success or failure column by
default. Where the number missing is itself uncertain, state that limit
and use qualified sensitivity or leave the total unresolved. Preserve
the original published series alongside corrections and expose effects
on affected people before claiming that a headline moved.

**PCO03 — Keep gains, regressions and protection duties distinct.** Publish
the result by important dimension, place, material affected group and
future exposure, with safe privacy limits and relevant uncertainty.
A broad superiority claim needs actual support across its material
scope and maintained protected conditions. Narrow gains can be reported
within their supported bounds. A verified loss in another important
dimension remains a separate result. Where evidence cannot resolve
tradeoffs, name who gains, who bears the loss and which body decides.
An actual rights breach or missed duty keeps its individual remedy.
A credible severe or irreversible harm signal needs assessment and
lawful protection in useful time. No good national average justifies
loss of opposition, arbitrary coercion or ecological limits. A mixed
result can support a lawful elected choice with stated reasons and
continuing remedies.

**PCO04 — Make a serious qualified finding reach a decision.** An adopting
Act would require a named lead authority to present a costed response
and any already lawful urgent action within 30 calendar days of the
Clerk's protected receipt of a qualified independent finding of
material systemic regression or exclusion. Prior law identifies the
competent independent audit, evidence or review authorities and a
challenge route. The assessed body cannot decide whether its own
finding qualifies. Identify affected scope, source and uncertainty,
severity and the original harm or useful date. No causal finding is required to expose the risk or
consider protection. The Assembly holds a protected hearing and makes
a recorded disposition within 60 calendar days of that original receipt,
subject to actual sitting, notice and vote rules. Use prior lawful
alternative procedures where available. The elected body can choose
repair, retain with reasons, limited verification or a funded change.
Record unavailable evidence, an unheld vote or missed deadline openly
and preserve the direct review route. Prove attempted delivery through
ordinary protected evidence if the Clerk fails to register it. A filing
does not authorize
spending, stop a law, extend a term or delay earlier rights and remedies.
Repeated copies or changed office do not reset the same issue's clock.
A materially different finding keeps its own time and duty.

**PCO05 — Correct the evidence and follow the response to actual receipt.**
Use C11 source and revision duties and ordinary independent audit, review
and courts. Give affected people, workers, local bodies and opposition a
real challenge route for missing evidence, inappropriate interpretation
or failure to implement a lawful remedy. Keep measurement producers,
deployment owners and elected decisions distinct. Protect testimony and
allow independent inspection without publishing small groups or tracking
individual lives. AI may check common baselines, report possible shifts
and compare stated alternatives. It cannot choose weights for a binding
public decision, close a claim, decide whose account counts or certify its
own effect. Record actual work, paid correction and received results
alongside the elected response. Reopen on changed coverage, credible
contrary testimony, failed receipt or better comparative evidence.
