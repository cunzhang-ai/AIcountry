# AS09–AS12: the worker's rights must not be the cost of personal choice

*Part of [C07/18](../18-person-directed-assistance.md); design 116 candidate.*

**AS09 — Real employment conditions.** TW's actual hours, staged normal week,
paid breaks/leave/learning, minimum cash pay, bargaining and total-person rest
apply to assistance work, including dependent platform and nominal contractor
arrangements. Intimate domestic work has the same common protection floor;
no blanket private-household exclusion is created. A paid relative remains a
worker for actual employment, not a compulsory guardian or source of free care.
Voluntary informal help can cease without creating a debt or unpaid replacement
duty. Scheduling software and agents cannot waive either person's choice.

Before a start, disclose in accessible durable form the actual employer, tasks,
regular hours/pay, paid travel/handover, deductions/contributions, leave, changes,
complaints, notice and backup. Missing paperwork does not invalidate performed
work or due pay. An assessment is not an employment contract, and day-to-day
support instructions do not authorize unlawful clinical acts or unsafe work.

**AS10 — Safety without control over private life.** Employer and care authority
assess actual task/environment risk with recipient and worker, arranging training,
necessary equipment, qualified supervision and reliable relief. Clinical/technical
permission and competence retain C07/TS sources; a course, vendor certificate or
AI answer is no full clinical delegation. Independent inspection uses C04/TS/PD
lawful access and proportional evidence, not a new open home-search power.

Respect bodily privacy, supported decisions and the person's ordinary choices.
A worker can refuse specifically unsafe/unlawful work without retaliation;
that does not cancel care. Immediate handoff/rescue follows existing E/UA/LF
sources without invented force, restraints, entry or unlimited compulsory duty.
A risk label is no blanket service ban, forced institution or excuse to leave a
person alone. Workers and recipients have confidential independent report/help
routes even if a provider, relative, politician or mediator controls the household.

**AS11 — Fair pay, responsibility and insurance.** Pay actual ordinary wages
on TW's regular cycle, at least monthly, with lawful itemized deductions and
actual tax/contribution remittance. Keep paid time, net received cash and service
receipt distinct. Unpaid wages, accrued leave/pension and damages retain their
own source-law defendants; B09–B11 applies to actual negligence/control/causation,
not automatic recipient fault or immunity for deliberate wrongdoing.

Fund adequate employment advice, safety and required insurance in the authorized
package; actual coverage/exclusions, insurer capacity and uninsured exposure must
be disclosed. NB insurance rules remain, with no unlimited public tort insurance
or indemnity of deliberate harm. An insurer's denial, worker complaint or civil
claim does not itself authorize suspension of the recipient's care. State B10
nondelegable/official liability remains distinct from a provider's private debt.

**AS12 — Explicit public backing for covered ordinary dues.** This candidate
adds the national Treasury protection payer as statutory co-obligor for lawful
ordinary gross wages, accrued leave, employer contributions and lawful notice/
termination dues attributable to the recognized assistance package and accepted
terms. This applies when the employer/custodian fails under any approved route,
including supported direct employment. It is a new public gross cash exposure,
not a general firm/lender/investor guarantee, all alleged damages, discretionary
unapproved extras or a promise of permanent employment.
Lawful statutory pay increases and recognized necessary corrections are not
excluded merely because an officer failed to revise the allocation on time.
The new backing covers obligations accruing from commencement; earlier dues
need an express funded migration acceptance and retain their old defendants.
Lack of a valid recipient employer election cannot defeat dues for recognized
necessary work actually commissioned through the public care chain; resolve
the actual employer/funding dispute separately without deeming an election.

A worker may claim directly with assisted proportionate evidence; employer
noncooperation or missing agency records cannot require first bankrupting the
recipient/provider. Pay undisputed established dues by their ordinary due date;
a late claim receives an undisputed advance within one calendar day of receipt,
with earlier essential-hardship rules applying. A genuinely disputed balance has
reasons, provisional protection and direct B/TW review. Record actual receiving:
a payment instruction alone has not paid wages. Preserve source-law earlier clocks.

Pay the same wage/period once, crediting actual earlier receipts, not a public
allocation merely sent to the failed agent. Treasury obtains only the matched
lawful recovery/subrogation claim against the actual obligated party; expected
recovery is no current source. No ordinary cash, care budget, protected worker
fund or innocent recipient asset is automatically seized. Any recipient recovery
needs independently proved personal misuse/fault and B/U protections; mere lawful
direct-employer status does not finance the public backstop. Employer obligations
outside this scope remain their actual law. Fraud collusion needs evidence, not
an algorithmic presumption; actual necessary care and undisputed worker rights
continue during independent inquiry.
