# DF11–DF15: personnel rights, independent scrutiny and peaceful succession

*Part of [DF](../12-civilian-defense-and-service.md); professional service is not political ownership.*

**DF11 — Volunteer and reserve terms are actual commitments.** Retain18 as
minimum armed/reserve service, professional voluntary recruitment and voluntary
reserves. A supported freely accepted written agreement names duty, role/risk,
pay/training/rest/leave, injury/veteran/survivor protection and finite reserve
call terms under prior law. An agent copy, recruitment debt, emergency label
or previous voluntary exercise is no consent to indefinite mobilization.
Compulsory service is not selected here; any later proposal needs separate
competent prospective legislation, finite necessity/fair selection, rights
review and a nonpunitive conscientious alternative, not a command decree.

**DF12 — Close WL19's undefined command exception.** Ordinary volunteer/support
roles use WL19's at-most14calendar-day exit notice or stronger earlier rights.
Only an actual armed-command/mission duty separately and freely accepted under
express prior primary law may use a longer stated notice, never more than
90calendar days from protected receipt of resignation. No commander/minister
can reset it by deployment/title, invoke staff shortage as an indefinite hold,
or retrospectively extend already binding shorter terms. Serious coercion,
unsafe/impossible notice and urgent court relief retain their existing grounds;
paid actual safe handover is separate from compelled personal performance.
No punitive training debt, passport seizure or detention for leaving. These
14/90 limits are contestable rights/readiness hypotheses, not optimal estimates.

**DF13 — Refusal and conscience need a safe lawful route.** Preserve protected
refusal/reporting of manifestly unlawful orders, independent legal advice,
bodily/clinical consent and actual court remedies. A genuine conscientious
objection receives prompt independent consideration and safe nonarmed paid
assignment where actually available while resolved, without inventing a post
or extending the exit clock. A commander cannot make conscience a sovereign
policy veto; neither an allegation nor obedience automatically settles
individual criminal responsibility. Fair proof and due process remain.
Ordinary income/care/housing rights cannot be withheld to compel service.

**DF14 — Reuse actual civilian scrutiny, extend scope expressly.** The DF Act
names the existing Assembly security committee's defense mandate, reusing
IA09–IA12's actual places/allocation/Clerk support and individual protected
source/court routes. No new committee majority or military clearance veto over
lawful inspection; secrecy receives safe independent review, not public release
of dangerous details. AA, ordinary courts, PO and plural public information
retain distinct tasks; same patrons, pay, HR, custody or vendors can capture
them together. Their overlap must be paid once with actually separate judgment.

**DF15 — Armories and records follow lawful offices, never election winners
chosen by force.** Actual C03/CJ/EL government law determines succession;
service members neither adjudicate a disputed ballot nor appoint a caretaker.
OP/T/DP transfers valid mission, personnel/pay, contracts, keys/custody and
protected evidence to lawful accepted receivers. Changing Cabinet does not
erase valid case acts, earlier pensions or command responsibility; retaining
a password cannot extend an expired office. If armed actors refuse, record
failed civilian control and use actual lawful independent protection/execution,
not a fictional guarantor, new coup power or a claim that a court order alone
restored democracy. Keep urgent personal/service/custody rights and evidence.
