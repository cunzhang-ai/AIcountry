The elected residual body decides the unresolved effect with affected-party
input. Local actors retain equivalent lawful methods. A separate part can
proceed only within its own authority and without defeating the remaining
choice. Temporary operation follows its lawful end and handback.

The [constructed comparison](../settlement/cross-local-effects/02-a-shared-limit-with-local-methods.md)
extends the published catchment exercise. It varies availability and
compares independent local methods with a uniform approach on the same
limit. The EPA source describes how an existing programme separates an
allocation and jurisdictional plans. It does not verify the exercise's
assumed costs, ecological protection or legal rights.

An objection is that decentralized methods can hide future or downstream
losses and need additional verification. Require equivalent physical
protection, source rights, actual funding and useful independent evidence.
A common method can qualify through the prior separate necessity test.
Preserve rights, prior permits and their competent correction, ordinary
local choice, opposition and the original review clock. Actual affected
participation, authorization, paid capacity and observed results remain
to be established. Reopen when a common limit cannot be enforced, wider
intervention becomes durable in practice, an outside group loses
protection, or ordinary repair secures the same outcome with less burden.
