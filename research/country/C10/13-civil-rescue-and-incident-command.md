# Civil rescue, accessible dispatch and bounded incident command

Design218 core and Design290 protected transport · RC01–RC33 · [C10 safety](../C10-safety-and-international-relations.md)

**Select a local public professional fire/rescue backbone, supported by paid
mutual aid and national civilian coordination.** Existing HP/CP powers named
local incident leads and a Cabinet coordinator without specifying their full
employer/appointment/replacement chain. This supplies those actors and ordinary
rescue delivery; it does not select a general safety commander above other law.
Community knowledge, voluntary help and compatible joint-service arrangements
remain valuable. An extra ministry, rescue board or compulsory citizen service
is not selected. The national C04 essential-guarantee duty remains.
