# BC11–BC15: a usable claim, an accountable public response

*Part of [C10/14](../14-cross-border-buying-and-responsibility.md); design187 candidate.*

**BC11 — Reach an actual court without purchasing access.** Actually affected
foreign and domestic people have equal direct B41 standing against a connected
buyer for BC duties. B13–B20 assistance, proportionate representation/collective
claims, safe notice, interpretation and earlier useful-time relief apply.
An authorized worker/community representative may assist; a buyer's preferred
NGO is no substitute for a mandate. Use safe remote evidence/representation
where lawful; a border visa, travel here, digital account or prior company
mediation cannot be an extra condition of filing. B42 assisted competent
receiving preserves the domestic date; foreign dates/recognition remain distinct.

**BC12 — Hidden buyer facts cannot make access fictitious.** An initial claim
needs credible facts identifying a material BC risk, connected buyer and
alleged failure, not a completed chain map or all internal contracts. B14/B17
courts can preserve and order targeted relevant buyer-held evidence with
necessity, reply, privilege and PD safeguards. Protect identities through
counsel/closed handling where compatible with a fair defense. Unjustified
noncompliance or destruction permits a reasoned, contestable inference under
ordinary B evidence law; it is not automatic proof of every alleged injury.
No foreign search, mass device access, compelled unsafe witness disclosure or
AI risk score conviction. Actual retaliation engages its applicable law and
urgent protection, not a newly invented BC crime.

**BC13 — Prevention and damages answer different questions.** Ordinary courts
can declare duties and order proportionate specific domestic-controlled inquiry,
cessation or correction on a proved applicable BC breach. B17 proportionate
interim prevention/preservation may address a credible threatened breach and
protected serious harm before actual loss: establish the duty, specific grounds
and necessity, without treating mere linkage/risk as breach. BC12/B14 targeted
evidence relief retains its own credible-claim grounds. Full damages require
BC07's actual causal loss; a preventive order is no liability judgment for all
chain harm. Defendants receive notice/reply, reasoned full merits, appeal and
prompt review of urgent without-notice measures. B18/B19 lawful executors enforce
actual private/public debtor orders within their own assets/source rules.
No court invents a foreign sovereign command, an appropriation or unavailable
replacement capacity. Actual claimant receipt remains distinct from an award.

**BC14 — Existing officers, express limited added functions.** The BC Act
expressly extends WL31's Work Standards Director and qualified case officers
to BC02 work, coercion, trafficking, child exploitation and organizing/
complaint-related buyer duties, including connected sourcing abroad without
buyer employment. Assigned qualified child/protection expertise is additional;
this is a civil prevention function, not transfer of all victim services or
criminal prosecution. No WL34 advance or employer wage debt extends
solely because of this addition. For BC02 community/ecological harms, Cabinet's
responsible environment executive must designate qualified C04 career case
officers within the existing environmental administration; those officers have
a statutory right/duty to bring supported BC13 civil prevention/correction
applications in ordinary courts and receive reports, not a minister's case veto.

The Act gives both officer routes express standing and a duty to bring
supported BC13 civil prevention/correction applications, within their assigned
harm predicates, against the covered buyer's own duties. Joined harms use
assisted accepted allocation without losing interim protection or dates.
Both routes use qualified source/evidence capacity and B42 assisted transfer;
an ordinary report need not arrive as a legal dossier. C04 recruitment/conflicts/
protected discipline, assigned substitutes and direct judicial challenge apply.
No defendant handles its own report: claims against a public buyer go to
unconflicted officers and directly to independent courts; head/minister conflicts
have direct court receiving. V03/V04 powers retain their actual lawful scope;
this Act adds no foreign entry, standalone public seizure or final officer fine.
Courts may compel a required public response, not dictate a named case's outcome.
Paid staff/counsel and direct affected claims remain necessary against capture.

**BC15 — No inferred customs ban or criminal analogy.** Customs retains C10/05
release and actually enacted product/trade restrictions; BC's civil duty is no
automatic shipment confiscation, forced-labor import ban, nationality blacklist
or collective boycott. A particular BC13 order may bind a connected buyer's
specific controlled transaction under its grounds/review; it does not authorize
a customs officer to create a national embargo. Assembly would need a distinct
rights/treaty/source-tested Act for broader exclusion. Sector safety, PD, GA,
employment, VD and P offences retain their actual predicates; no new crime,
fine base, strict liability or corporate criminal presumption is adopted here.
