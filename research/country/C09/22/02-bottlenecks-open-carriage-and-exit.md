# Bottlenecks, open carriage and customer exit

*Part of [CN communications access](../22-communications-access-and-network-power.md).*

**CN05 — Open an essential bottleneck on enforceable terms.** A publicly
funded shared transport bottleneck carries prospective wholesale access
conditions. For a privately funded persistent bottleneck, the C09 regulator
must show market power, impracticable timely alternatives and expected public
benefit, jointly checking the existing competition record where relevant.
Use the least intrusive remedy likely to work. Start with an independently
reviewable reference offer that covers actual interconnection points, ordering,
minimum committed capacity, repair, service quality, applicable security,
queue order and attributable costs including reasonable renewal. Apply the
same terms in substance to affiliates and independent users. Audit delays,
capacity denials and the wholesale to retail margin. The regulator can order
specific interconnection, repricing or remedy within statute after a fair
hearing, with timely interim relief and independent appeal. Account for
property, existing contracts and applicable compensation. Reassess market
scope on expiry and when real substitutes become available. Structural
separation or public acquisition needs separate authority and evidence that
this remedy is insufficient. Safe maintenance windows and real capacity
limits use published, reviewable rules.

**CN06 — Keep spectrum and shared sites contestable.** Assign spectrum use
under prior law with defined frequencies or resources, geography, duration,
coordination, interference limits and renewal or expiry. Consider lawful
shared or local access when a nationwide exclusive assignment is unnecessary.
Publish reasons, common control and material conditions, and allow
independent challenge. Radio access, pole or duct space and access to a
building each require the applicable title and safety permission. The
regulator can enforce actual harmful-interference limits within its remit.
Emergency coordination follows published prior-law limits. A spectrum,
infrastructure or interconnection decision supplies no authority over
political content, elections, user identity or lawful private conversation.

**CN07 — Require open carriage in ordinary access.** Within a published
service class carry lawful traffic without preferential treatment based on
viewpoint, affiliation, commercial payment or the carrier's own content.
A user's lawful choice of application, service or accessible equipment
cannot justify degrading the basic offer. Allow proportionate, necessary
technical management for real congestion, network integrity or security,
and time-limited emergency priority under separately enacted criteria.
State the reason, scope and end condition and protect the evidence for
independent review. Different technical needs can justify narrow
application-neutral service classes when their operation leaves the basic
offer intact. An order restricting specific communications must rest on
separate authority with its own rights, necessity, lawful authorizer and
remedy. No CN power to intercept, decrypt or shut down a network follows.
On a credible discrimination complaint, an independent reviewer may order
prompt scoped restoration under statute, require protected examination and
direct correction before a time-critical loss where feasible. Preserve
notice and remedy without collecting payloads merely to prove equal treatment.

**CN08 — Make exit work in practice.** At the user's authorized request,
the receiving carrier coordinates a timely switch and eligible number or
identifier portability under published interoperability and safety rules.
Preserve continuity through an agreed cutover, or maintain the old service
where a failed cutover leaves it lawfully usable. Give accessible notice
through a channel the person can safely use, support fast reversal of
unauthorized transfers and preserve complaints and owed credits. Handle
coercion, compromised accounts and lack of ordinary identity documents
with bounded alternative proof under privacy law. Preserve separately
applicable obligations of terminating providers. Two retail offers may
allow meaningful switching on one network. Continuity credit for an
independent path requires separate CN09 evidence.

[MP21–MP26](../../C05/11/10-usable-switching-and-continuity.md)
provides a common rule for designated service transfers. CN's separate
network duties and stronger protections continue.
