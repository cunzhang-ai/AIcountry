# Preserve current tenancies and open homes for entrants

Design332 · Constructed comparison for [H21–H25](../../country/C09/12/05-housing-preservation-and-net-supply.md)

A local housing budget has 60 illustrative units of capital and 12 of
annual recurring authority. The authored elected objectives are to protect
at least five at-risk tenancies and open three suitable affordable entrant
offers by month 6. Existing urgent duties and two valid rights of return
keep their own authority. The two returning households have existing
funded interim receiving outside this investment envelope. The repair
route restores their homes. The construction route lawfully replaces
those same two dwellings, with any additional interim cost included.
The acquisition-only route leaves their eventual return unresolved.
Assume the qualified routes have independent
inspection, lawful finance, accessible prices and the paid staff listed
in the relevant option. Capital and recurring units are different scopes.

| Package | Capital / recurring | Position at month 6 and later |
|---|---:|---|
| Buy 10 occupied homes | 40 / 10 | Eight evidenced at-risk tenancies can be retained, with two other continuing tenancies. No additional physical home, restored dwelling or entrant offer. |
| Repair five currently unusable dwellings | 30 / 5 | Restore five to service. Two are reserved for valid returns, leaving three entrant offers. No physical addition and no prevention of the separate threatened losses. |
| Buy six occupied homes and repair five unusable dwellings | 54 / 11 | The acquisition costs 24 / 6 and retains five evidenced at-risk tenancies. The repair restores five homes, with two returns and three entrant offers. No physical addition. |
| New-build and replacement package | 60 / 12 | None of the new homes is ready at month 6. At month 18, ten completions replace two removed homes. With paid temporary receiving and two reserved replacements, this could add eight net physical dwellings and eight entrant offers. It does not protect the separate at-risk tenancies. |

Choose the mixed package on the stated objectives and capacity. Confirm
actual retention, repair acceptance and offers separately. The sixth
purchased household can gain security without being counted as a loss
prevented. Keep the later new-build option visible as a genuinely different
answer to persistent scarcity. The unspent capital 6 and recurring 1
do not qualify the later construction commitment.

If only four repairs qualify by month 6 and both valid returns can use
them, only two entrant offers qualify. The resulting shortfall is one.
Keep the fifth repair and protected earlier claims visible. If the available
four cannot accommodate a particular return, that household's entitlement
and safe receiving remain live. A spare capital balance or a revised count
cannot perform an unfinished repair. Compare independently accepted
extra provision or a revised elected schedule with actual near-term access.

The [decision record](../../2026-11-housing-preservation-and-net-supply/decision-record.md)
retains the alternatives and limits. The assumptions and outcomes are
constructed, without implying actual project cost, stock or effectiveness.
