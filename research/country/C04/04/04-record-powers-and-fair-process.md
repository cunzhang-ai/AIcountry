# AA16–AA20: necessary audit access with real limits and court protection

*Part of [C04/04](../04-public-audit-and-accountable-oversight.md); design121 candidate.*

**AA16 — Precise ordinary record orders.** Statutory auditors can require existing
nonprivileged public-financial/necessary in-scope counterparty records actually
held or lawfully required under AA02. State legal basis, question, items/period,
use/retention/privacy, competent receiver and challenge; no general fishing in
private life or demand for an invented forecast. Give normally at least 20 working
days and usable assistance/reasonable format. B16 source/reply and actual ability
apply; contested compulsory access/privilege goes to ordinary courts, not AG's
final judgment. Urgent legal preservation/earlier lawful safety duties remain,
not an auditor-created search or personal criminal-answer compulsion.

**AA17 — Bounded preservation, not acquisition.** On particular evidenced risk
of imminent destruction of specified in-scope original records, an authorized
auditor may serve a preservation notice for at most 30 calendar days. Preserve
custody/minimum necessary integrity, not seizure, ownership, new access, freezing
money or perpetual public testimony. No unilateral same-course renewal/reset;
ordinary court may order a necessary fixed continuation up to 90 calendar days
per fresh review under B safeguards. Existing longer lawful retention/actual
P/claim source rules are not shortened. A holder may seek immediate useful-time
review; an office/vendor's common credentials are no independent backup.

**AA18 — Fair findings and actual contrary material.** Before final material
adverse findings, supply actual source/substance and proposed characterization
to affected auditees/persons with at least 20 working days for a usable reply,
funded proportionate assistance where needed and protected contact. One reasoned
extension at most 20 working days for actual access/complexity may be given,
not serial ministerial delay; actual court access/fairness rights survive.
Consider material reply/original counterevidence, correction and dissent, not
only the office summary. New material adverse matters need fresh appropriate
reply, splitting completed findings if necessary rather than suppressing all
work. Nonattendance or silence is not assent, guilt or a bar to publication.
Ordinary reply does not bar immediate lawful preservation, competent safety/
loss referral or a narrowly supported provisional warning under actual powers;
state uncertainty, no premature guilt or new coercive authority.

**AA19 — No secret adverse proof or forced confession.** Privileged/self-
incriminating or sensitive material has actual B/P/PD protections and court
access, not AG's unilateral exemption. Official accounting records do not become
unrelated private testimony by label, nor does an audit compel a person's criminal
confession. An unchallengeable secret cannot support an adverse final finding;
use safe disclosure, qualification or exclusion/limits with reasons. Preserve
contrary/tainted material lawfully for actual abuse review, never launder it
through agreeing AI outputs or another vendor. No punishment arises merely
from late filing, missing annex, genuine dispute or good-faith inability.

**AA20 — Direct independent remedy, no audit trial.** Affected people may approach
ordinary courts under B/OJ for power/scope/retaliation, confidentiality, record
orders, harmful false findings or urgency without serial compulsory audit appeals.
Auditor's own final opinion is not a binding civil/criminal merits decision;
actual legal liability/proof and appropriate source remedies remain with courts/
competent authorities. Court can protect, correct unlawful effect and require
lawful reconsideration, not write the auditor's preferred policy or invent
appropriation. No new VD15/offense/fine base follows; independently enacted K/P
or actual order-enforcement elements need their own proof, defense and authority.

[PI13](../../C03/17/03-public-access-audit-and-effective-remedies.md) expressly
proposes a coordinated statutory extension for compliance with lawfully required
public-office interest/contact/draft/disposition records, including Assembly/
local records, beyond the prior public-resource nexus alone. AA04 and AA16–AA20
limits/privilege/reply/courts remain; no general advocate/client/private search,
criminal, policy, vote/seat, payment or promulgation veto follows. Actual prior
mandates stay until valid prospective adoption; no source/review transfer.
