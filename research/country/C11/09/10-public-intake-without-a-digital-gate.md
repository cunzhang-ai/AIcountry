# DP43–DP47. Keep an accessible public intake when AI helps with contact

*Design285 · Proposed extension to [public digital operation](../09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md).*

The first programme should fund a direct public intake with simple
navigation, authorized receipt and independent review. AI may assist
with general information or a chosen transfer, if that use is supported
separately. A useful public service includes the person who cannot use
the common tool or whose request crosses an organizational boundary.

**DP43 — Separate general advice, a request for action and formal receipt.**
The public service owner publishes what each channel can do and lets
people choose a staffed route at the outset. Use existing accessible
phone, in-person, written and other lawful routes for actual claims.
A public channel that receives a request within its lawful scope applies
[AP](../../C04/06-reachable-administration-and-finite-decisions.md)
and the sector's receipt rules. A person seeking general information
can remain anonymous where lawful. If they ask to file, challenge or
obtain urgent help, offer direct authorized receiving and communicate
the effect of each step. A conversation, answer or suggested form
alone supplies no award, refusal or completed submission. Review an
apparent filing lost or misdirected by the authority under existing
timeliness and correction law. Preserve proved original receipt and
a useful route to prove a disputed attempt.

**DP44 — Keep the handoff open until someone can act.** The original
public owner remains responsible for a covered handoff until a named,
competent receiver actually accepts it. A pending transfer keeps its
original clock and a usable status. The receiving officer confirms
the accepted scope, next step and accessible response to the person.
Move only the minimum permitted and necessary information. Preserve a
direct way for the person to clarify what was requested. When no one
accepts in useful time, activate the funded alternate or escalate the
unserved case under ordinary law. The accountable officer verifies
actual receipt and any due urgent protection. A switchboard transfer,
queue insertion or automated closure is a separate event.

**DP45 — Keep accountable proof without retaining everyone's conversation.**
Hold the minimum lawful receipt, material original facts and reasons with
the actual service custodian. When an AI output materially affected a
person's route or treatment, preserve the minimum material evidence for
independent correction, with safe lawful access and expiry. Keep optional
service-quality sampling and general advice records separate. A resident
can obtain usable proof through an equivalent offline route. Refusal of
optional monitoring or a missing recording does not establish that the
person failed to act. No standing authorization for a shared personal
log, new identity requirement or universal recording follows.

**DP46 — Separate the trial budget from the budget for standing access.**
Elect or lawfully authorize a finite optional trial with a named task,
paid receiving, independently staffed fallback, exact expiry and paid
exit. Compare a competent ordinary route with clearer language and
trained navigators before using real claims. Test language and accessibility,
misleading advice, repeated contact, wrong transfer and a shared platform
or credential loss. Count useful timely results by service and affected
group from first contact, including missed and unresolved cases.
The public service owner can stop or narrow the affected optional use
under DP09–DP12. The staffing floor and useful-time routes persist
through loss. Forecast avoided contacts or labour savings finance
neither the trial nor a cut in staffed hours.

**DP47 — Keep first-budget disposition and later scaling separate.** Hold
the integrated first programme's accelerated intake addition when the
review or funding condition fails. Where an exact ordinary improvement
has independent value, budget and authority, decide it separately.
Publish actual receiving, time, staffing, unserved demand, safety and
cost, with protected independent inspection and actual remedies.
Only a fresh authorized decision under DP33–DP37 may use qualified
gains or adjust the standing route. If the trial ends, preserve live
cases, original receipt, open correction, staff coverage and lawful
custody. A receipt model or vendor cannot choose policy, priority,
eligibility or the outcome of a dispute.
