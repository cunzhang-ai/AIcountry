# Design258 decision record. Essential imports and usable settlement

10 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned Design257](baseline.json)

## The national choice

Select [NS21–NS25](../country/C06/54/06-essential-imports-and-usable-settlement.md)
for material dependencies in first and recurring budgets. Keep useful
trade, diversify the actual receiving routes, and qualify paid domestic
alternatives and regional help for their exact scopes. Select a hybrid
only where it delivers the due service under the relevant stress and
treat the second window separately. A domestic appropriation, a
foreign reserve entry and a shipment are different resources.

The Assembly controls public source and priority law. Treasury and
the monetary authority each act within the existing account and
ceiling. Sector buyers, licensed sellers and foreign counterparties
must be able to act on their own lawful terms. Border, safety and
clinical authorities retain actual judgment and responsibility.
Verify local receipt independently. The payer's failure or interest
does not control access to the original records or outside remedy.
Use the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

## Evidence and alternatives

The reported Sri Lanka end-2022 reserve stock had a large conditional
component. Actual emergency trade credit had specified uses. World
Bank reporting identifies fuel-related procurement and different
endpoints for medicines and LPG. These facts support distinct
checks on currency and final receiving. They do not isolate causal
effects or establish that a single foreign facility, reserve rule or
form of rationing worked for every household.
[Historical records and original evidence](sources.md)

A market-led diversified trade portfolio preserves choice and can
outperform an expensive domestic reserve. It can lose several
apparently independent suppliers if they share a route or eligible
currency. A regional common facility buys scale and solidarity, but
a headline credit ceiling can exceed the partner's physically safe
offer. A standing domestic route can reduce dependence and may cost
more or shift ecological, labor and maintenance burdens. Retain it
where actual quality, lifecycle and independent alternatives compare
well. Immediate domestic money alone cannot supply foreign title or
replace port and local logistics.

The authored case selects air delivery of medicines, a smaller
regional fuel draw and paid domestic repair for the first interruption.
It preserves the partner's service floor and a binding public debt
payment. It fails under an additional regional corridor loss and
shows a separate funding gap at the second call. A simpler qualified
route can displace it. The case supports the timed portfolio choice,
not a quantitative rule for all imports or reserve adequacy.
[Matched comparison](../settlement/essential-imports-and-usable-settlement/02-currency-routes-and-second-call.md)

Prefer transparent actual-cost purchasing and narrow public support.
Scarce public currency can create favoritism, parallel access and
price subsidies. Use elected purpose and cap, trace accepted goods and
use, protect equal challenge and disclose aggregate support. Preserve
lawful trade, private title, external and worker claims. Compare
voluntary sale and actual paid demand reduction before seeking a
different measure under its own law. Neither the country nor its
supplier can transfer risks to a neighboring public by assertion.

## Compatibility, limits and next choice

Keep existing MC/Treasury source and currency limits, sovereign debt
law, JC and FB dated budgets, CP allocation, SC buying, MQ clinical
receiving, C10 clearance and treaty authority, RC donor protection,
and existing independent challenge. Require a lawful successor for
each open delivery, current payment, dispute, asset or record.
No new reserve pool, private surrender, ongoing import subsidy,
emergency borrowing or transfer of government authority is adopted.

The published reserve figures reconcile within their reported scope.
Delivery reports have distinct endpoints and the other comparison
is authored. Actual affected participation, independent qualified
review, authorization, operating readiness, and better lives remain
unverified.

For the next first-budget comparison, name one actual imported input
for the same due care, water and independent public service calendar.
Carry exact seller, source and currency, customs dates, first and
second delivery, applicable debt and credits, paid domestic shifts
and local receiving. Test loss of the primary route and one shared
settlement or receiving dependency against actual protected work.
If material records are missing, present the remaining exposures
and lawful alternatives to the elected decision owner. Reopen on
changed settlement terms, unavailable partner stock, unsafe domestic
substitution, shifted harm or stronger qualified evidence.
