# Own the assets, fund the maximum and preserve drawn debt

Design 99 · Proposed research, not enacted law or funded bank protection.

**BF05 — Separate the assets before a payout.** M05 proved segregated client property is outside the
bank estate and returned to its actual owner, not booked as an insured deposit or M09 loss buffer. A
host-bank deposit held for a client follows the **actual legal debtor/beneficial look-through** and
joins the client's direct deposits to the same licensed legal bank for one 12B cap if M07 applies; a
platform and a bank cannot each pay the same covered balance. Insured bank deposits remain bank
debts paid by insurer or an accepting receiver; uninsured balances stay at M10's lower rank. SF05's
G/S safeguarded public-payment assets belong to their actual public/insurance owner outside the
failed lender; neither Treasury standby nor insurer reserve automatically becomes bridge capital.

**BF06 — Full maximum liability, not a hoped-for net cost.** On every funded growth decision,
calculate `C` as the sum across all separately licensed member banks of each actual beneficiary's
current **still-unpaid maximum covered legal claim** (including attributed omnibus shares,
principal/interest and independently valued eligible foreign-denominated deposits), counted once.
Crystallized failure claims use their BF04 T0 B/FX; still-live unfailed exposure is refreshed under
the lawful current B/rate. When a lawful national I02 adult B update
increases the M07 cap, refresh live exposure as of its lawful effect.
A local supplement or a voluntary food receipt does not itself change B.
Record the headroom and cash consequences separately from benefits.
Do not revalue crystallized claims or hold ordinary due income.
Let `F` be the deposit insurer's legally owned, unencumbered, presently
accessible liquid fund assets, including only a facility draw that has **actually settled into its
ownership** and excluding future premiums, forecast recoveries, uncalled promises, G/S SF standby
and client property. Let `L` be the enacted **total outstanding-advance ceiling** of M08's Treasury
facility, and `D` all its currently unrepaid prior draws including due principal; on any day before
newly protected growth require **usable undrawn legal headroom `max(0,L−D) ≥ max(0,C−F)`**, plus a
separate proved cash/settlement source able to meet actual due draws. D must not exceed L; a clamped
zero never legalizes an overdrawn facility. L and D measure outstanding advance principal; accrued
interest/charges retain their separately recorded due liability and lawful source, and
capitalization needs its own ceiling authority. Neither F nor a drawn D is credited twice, and later
recoveries reduce D only after actual repayment. The Assembly must increase the hard ceiling/source
if this inequality fails; a standing formula cannot spend beyond it. Refresh on coverage, group,
foreign-rate and fund changes, with safe published aggregate and confidential detail. Currency/rate
stress, simultaneous failures and replacement costs need a separate actual readiness test; this
legal arithmetic alone proves none. On one calendar show the original
eligible advance and balance dates, usable receipt, the day any draw
actually settles and principal outstanding after repayments. Check a
second claim under the same controller failure. An insured entitlement
or late settlement neither counts as timely access nor settles a
separate wage, meal or treatment claim. Exposure over headroom is a disclosed funding breach and
requires lawful elected source action; it does not cancel existing insured claims or retroactively
deem deposits unprotected.

**BF07 — Fund, backstop and payers.** Retain M08's annual **0.4% of average actually covered
balances** member levy and separate **2% target in five years**; the fund is the insurer's property,
not bank equity or Treasury general cash. The Assembly legislates backstop maximum, maturity,
interest/price, creditor rank, repayment, ultimate loss and actual budget/borrowing source; Treasury
executes an already authorized eligible draw to the insurer without a case-by-case political veto.
The insurer claims officer issues the coverage decision, a separate protected fund/payment executor
verifies available fund and Treasury settlement and instructs the named receiving payer, who
confirms actual credit. If the lawful line exists but cash cannot settle on time, publish the M07
arrear, seek urgent court/elected source correction and protect E/I/basic payment by their
independent due routes. No central-bank overdraft, reserve label or fee claim manufactures missing
cash. Recoveries and future levies repay D **only when actually received and applied to that debt**;
the principal/interest obligation survives even if C later shrinks, and any unrecovered public loss
is explicit, not forecast reimbursement.

**BF08 — SF and compensation are separate books.** SF05's 179.695833 authored standby is owned as
G's 159.695833 plus **social-insurance S's 20**, outside the failed bank only if actual custody
works. Neither G nor S standby enters the **bank-deposit insurer's** `F`: the two insurers are
distinct legal owners with different due beneficiaries. An actual later lawful transfer to the
deposit insurer would require its own source/title, social-claim continuity and reduction in S's
available standby; it is not a free M08 fund asset. G/S standby pays only its own already lawfully
due public/social items through appointed payers, not an unvoted bank backstop or bridge. M11's
separately appropriated liquidation-comparator compensation account is **additional** to `C−F` and
any M09 bridge capital, not a netting credit, automatic insurance fund use or a guaranteed paid
claim. Elected budget/NP/CP account for cash, contingent maximum and priority impacts of all four
stocks/claims, plus source-custody and court costs. Old G425/S100/E200/SF examples are authored
stock identities, not available country funds.
