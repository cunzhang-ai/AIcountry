# Design448. Published rates, duties and observed scope

- [North Denmark Flex25 award](https://www.ntrejse.dk/nyheder/2025/feb/resultat-af-flex25)
  and [availability terms](https://cdn.ntrejse.dk/nordjyllandstrafikselskab/Media/638688977392990109/Udbudsbetingelser-Flex25---r%C3%A5dighedsvogne-a.pdf).
  The 25R-ANB award row gives the price and evaluated annual amount.
  Sections 2.7, 3.5, 3.10 and 3.11 describe evaluation, dispatch,
  actual payment and indexation. The historical tender does not
  offer capacity to this proposed country.
- [ATAX–3F agreement](https://atax.dk/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Overenskomst-2025-2028.pdf),
  §§2, 4 and 6. Normal hours, payroll dates and rates for the
  identified work scope. The [Danish Road Traffic Authority](https://www.fstyr.dk/erhverv/taxi/lovgivning-og-tilsyn/loen-og-arbejdsvilkaar)
  lists the agreement. It does not establish the terms of any
  particular awardee's payroll or the cost of a replacement shift.
- [Capital Region notice 618634-2025](https://udbud.dk/detaljevisning?noticeId=362312c6-6e82-48bc-b291-66e6f13a6a8d&noticePublicationNumber=00618634-2025&noticeVersion=01),
  [transition briefing, slide 5](https://edagsorden.regionh.dk/cms/HtmlPublication-10270/enclosures/4.pdf)
  and [February 2026 award announcement](https://www.regionh.dk/presse-og-nyt/pressemeddelelser-og-nyheder/Sider/Kontrakter-om-siddende-patienttransport-p%C3%A5-plads.aspx).
  The tender estimate is for a procurement, the briefing reports
  setup under separate interim arrangements, and the announcement
  names three suppliers for five geographic areas. None gives
  delivered appointments or an independent standby price.
- [Copenhagen Council trial report, June 2025](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Borgerrepr%C3%A6sentationen/m%C3%B8de-26062025/referat/punkt-6).
  Seventeen workplaces entered, fourteen changed scheduling and
  about 300 workers opted in. The evaluation uses surveys,
  interviews and focus groups. It supports testing voluntary
  scheduling changes and supplies neither a retained-pay reduction
  estimate nor independent causal evidence of resident receipt.
