# Recurring taxes: noncash work cannot hide its tax or pension contribution

*NF29–NF32; part of [C06/42](../42-recurring-tax-and-noncash-work.md).*

**NF29 — Actual labor and delivery, not every benefit.** Y's10% personal-labor
base includes actual cash and delivered private noncash work remuneration,
including marketable and nontransferable household use valued by TA04's actual
period/comparables. Exclude necessary work tools, actual clinical/disability/
travel costs, ordinary public services, U/I05, TW public replacement, pensions,
true reimbursements and capital/gift receipts under their own source law.
Evidence separates work from capital, no automatic percentage of every business
receipt or AI-final classification. Required/minimum contractual cash wages,
safe alternative and supported assent remain; a perk cannot unilaterally replace
them. A promise alone creates no new V/g/c credit. Independently proved covered
work, wage/accrual and old units retain their source rights while promised
benefit value/delivery remains unresolved; no fictional S cash follows.

**NF30 — Employer funds both increments as remuneration.** Let O be other
annual PA10 income after its lawful Y, V net delivered private benefit after
employee payment, q=.10, T the one actual PA10 liability. Retain TA04's unique
**g=T(O+V+g)−T(O)**. Add **c=q(V+g)/(1−q)=(V+g)/9**. The extra gross labor
package is **P=V+g+c**, Y increment c=.10P; after deducting c, taxable increment
is V+g, hence g still satisfies the original equation. The employer funds g to
G and c to S; the worker keeps the benefit/baseline cash and owes no repayment
or deduction from U/I05. This is an explicitly funded employee contribution,
not a hidden extra employer levy or a no-tax advance.

At flat 20%, V100 gives g25, c13.888889, gross 138.888889. Equal gross cash less
Y/tax gives 100 under those same facts. This is tax/contribution arithmetic parity,
not equal freedom, I05 resources, prices or pension value. Use the Act's currency
minor unit and common half-up annual tax/Y rounding; choose the smallest funded
P whose rounded post-Y incremental tax leaves at least V, deliver any unavoidable
rounding surplus to the worker. No employee top-up corrects a rounding shortage.
These algebraic identities are unrounded; final currency allocation follows
the least-P rule, and statements preserve exact bases and actual rounded cash.

**NF31 — Several employers, one reconciliation.** Allocate g in proportion to
actual employer V; unrounded c_i=(V_i+g_i)/9. Allocate the actual rounded total
c proportionately to V_i+g_i, preserving total P/g/c and any paid rounding
surplus as covered worker cash. Source/period traced. Round actual payer totals
with largest fractional remainders/public lot for exact ties, never multiply the
person's annual liability. Revenue reveals only each employer's necessary
adjustment, not unrelated job/partner income. At actual delivery the Act creates
employer g/c remittance debt and matched worker tax/Y discharge; default preserves
proved covered work and no double collection, but G/S record money only on
settled receipt. Public default/credit exposure is explicit, not source funding.

**NF32 — Delivery, payment and correction clocks.** Employer computes provisional
g/c from lawful known receipts at delivery and remits/reports separately by 15th
next month. Annual reconciliation uses actual O/V once, by 30 April. A later lawful
employer top-up is due 30working days after usable final notice, with TX reply/
review/stays and original source limitations, no new tax on undelivered value.
Refund actually paid excess only after correcting worker/source ledgers; cancel
unremitted overstatement rather than pay it out. Named G/S payers and earned-
service officers trace actual sources, accepted receipt and necessary record/
recipient correction; neither an invoice nor a promised refund pays tomorrow's
pension. Wage/insolvency/occupancy claims and direct courts survive payer default.
