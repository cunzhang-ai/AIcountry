# Keep national grants compatible with elected local control

*MF31–MF36. Candidate implementing rules for national-to-local finance.*

**MF31 — Identify what is owed and what is offered.** Each national transfer
schedule identifies the legal source, owner, amount, decision date and
receiving duty. Keep statutory equalization, essential-duty funding and
settlement of existing claims distinct from optional project support.
Pay the undisputed lawful amount by its original date. A council's
decision on an optional offer cannot become a condition of receiving
that amount. Changing the title, channel or responsible ministry does
not change a continuing duty. Ordinary elected budget and formula
revisions follow their own prospective law and continuing-claims rules.

**MF32 — Tie a condition to the funded purpose.** State each term in the
authorized public offer, with its relevant evidence, purpose, duration,
cost, correction and exit. Use proportionate terms for lawful use of
funds, safety, accessible outcomes, interoperability where needed or
recovery of established misuse. Allow an equivalent lawful local method
where it meets the same funded purpose. Preserve local decisions over
unrelated services, personnel, political activity, procurement, taxes
and elections. A generally binding national standard requires its own
legislative authority and funded commencement. A grant agreement cannot
supply that authority or waive a protected right. An equivalent
local operating change remains within delegated authority when it
preserves the enacted cohort, duties, source and required legal
instrument. Apply applicable local material change procedure and
verify timely accessible receiving and direct challenge. Return only
a changed legal effect or condition to the competent authorizer.
A national administrator cannot impose an extra approval step for
a method the law already leaves to the council.

**MF33 — Test practical choice against the correct baseline.** Before
opening a material grant programme or imposing a new condition,
publish what happens if an eligible council declines. Use the lawful
continuing baseline and the dates of cash, staffing, public duties and
affected residents. Examine cumulative effects of related offers,
reclassifications, recovery, shared provider contracts and changes to
ordinary funding. Consult affected councils, opposition and residents
on the alternatives, including poorer authorities that lack a reserve.
A council may lose the additional project by declining it. A loss of
existing rights, a legally due transfer or practical control of an
unrelated local power requires correction. If a supposed optional
programme is the only qualified source for a common duty, bring the
duty and source through MF and the elected budget before treating
refusal as a viable option. A signed agreement alone does not establish
that choice was available.

**MF34 — Make the two decisions separately and publicly.** The competent
national elected body authorizes a programme and its source. The local
council accepts or declines a material optional offer by recorded vote
on the exact funded terms, including costs after the grant ends. Publish
a lawful alternative and unresolved gaps. Show maintenance, local
contributions, other projects displaced and the route for return or
exit. Keep binding contracts and earned claims on their own authority.
Material new conditions apply prospectively after the applicable
decision and notice. Neither one council's refusal nor a court
challenge authorizes a different council to spend the funds.

**MF35 — Correct a breach without interrupting protected services.**
A distinct Treasury payer executes due transfers under their existing
authority, with a preassigned unconflicted alternate where the offer
administrator could obstruct release. Use notice, a fair chance to
answer, and scoped recovery or withholding under applicable law for
misuse or breach. Separate the affected project amount and preserve
undisputed transfers and public duties. If the ordinary receiving
route itself cannot safely deliver, use only a funded existing
continuity or service backstop and account for it once. The council,
its opposition and directly affected people have an accessible
independent route under ordinary jurisdiction. The court can enforce a
due payment or set aside an unlawful term within its powers. Separate
the project from a defective term only where the lawful offer and
budget can operate independently. A court cannot vote new finance
or extend the national service backstop. Verify actual cash, lawful
receiving and the next due service. Preserve any shortfall and remedy.

**MF36 — Audit the choice and keep the country revisable.** Publish safe
distributions of awards, terms, refusals, corrections and actual services
by relevant fiscal and need groups. External audit checks the original
source and controls. Give legislators, local representatives and affected
people a usable route to expose favoritism or concealed service loss.
Carry continuing commitments, accurate records and paid successors
through a change of government or grant administrator. AI can compare
scopes and find bundled conditions. It cannot determine legality,
attach conditions, certify consent or authorize a transfer. Reopen on
repeated indirect control, genuine gaps in common service, unnecessary
restrictions or a stronger lawful source design.
