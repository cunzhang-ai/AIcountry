# NM09–NM12. Freeze, funds and migration

[Contestable nominations](../23-contestable-list-nominations.md)

**NM09 — Keep selection and public qualification distinct.** Before V−40,
validly revise the collective result only by the prior lawful selector
procedure, including any predeclared rule for an actual candidate refusal.
Actual personal consent and withdrawal retain EN04's scope. A timely list
filing and timely assent may be proved or corrected through ER05's V−30
historical-proof window. This express proof provision also covers the
collective outcome and fallback order actually established by V−40.
A late attestation can prove that outcome. It cannot conduct a late
selection, import a new name or transfer an endorsement to another list.
Original proof never restores an opportunity that was already lost.

**NM10 — Limit the remedy to the proved injury.** Preserve a separately
valid nominee or rank if the court can establish its independent lawful
selection, consent and severability with notice to affected people.
Keep the same minimum capacity and support gate. Do not infer a valid
list or automatic exclusion from a partial defect. Where a whole lawful
pool cannot be established, apply the existing qualification and judicial
disposition within their actual scope. ER07's narrowly authorized
provisional count remains available only under its own law and proof.
EL19, ER16 and ER17 control succession, postpoll claims and any repeat
vote. No assumed votes, administrative postponement, automatic annulment
or new mandate follow. A party cannot recall a public representative
or change the frozen successor pool by expulsion.

**NM11 — Fund small-list access and independent execution.** Price the
public process before adoption. The ordinary budget funds a common
basic receipt, secret-ballot support, custody and assistance route
available from the start of a compliant selection, without requiring
prior seats, final qualification or a mandatory fee. Price actual
selecting populations, paper, interpretation, custody, review, adverse
load and backup together with later EN/CF/ER and PC duties. Cabinet
proposes funds, elected bodies appropriate them, career officers
administer objective access and independent courts decide disputes.
Separate member persuasion, actual political spending and neutral
delivery in the existing PC records. No new contest credit or hidden
charge against later equal baseline support. Publish timely access,
unresolved harm and resource failures without exposing selectors.

**NM12 — Enact prospectively and test cumulative power.** Coordinate the
express EN02/04, ER03/05, EL06/09 and PC ordinary-local joins under
competent law and applicable RR succession protection. Identify funded
appointments, custody transfer, exact start, affected contests and
the last safe dates. Preserve valid old filings, rights, records,
grants, contracts, pending remedies and paid FT/OP/T successors. When
one chain controls membership records, communications, tally, filing and
judicial execution, test whether affected people can reach an alternative
through each failed stage. Reopen on observed faction exclusion,
party-state capture, excessive entry burden, unusable secrecy or a
better whole alternative. Research adoption changes no live contest.
