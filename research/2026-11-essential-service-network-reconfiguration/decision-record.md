# Design403 decision. Preserve usable access when essential services move

## Baseline and scope

Use [Design402](predecessor-design-402/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md), the
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).
This AI-assisted selection applies to planned changes that may materially affect
access to an existing publicly assured essential service. Residents already
using a route, people previously excluded, workers and neighboring sites are
affected. MF14–MF24 already distinguishes fixed costs and neighborhood access.
FT addresses transfers of legal functions, and PL protects service at material
asset disposal. The gap is a decision and staged handoff for reconfiguring
the service network itself, even when authority and title stay the same.

## Rival arrangements and the selected rule

One rival keeps the established network and repairs it. This preserves
familiar access and existing relationships, with possible persistent quality,
workforce or geographic problems. Another concentrates specialized work and
reduces duplicate fixed costs. Its benefits depend on actual journeys, spare
qualified capacity and who bears new time and care burdens. A hybrid keeps
local receiving for people who cannot use a larger site while sharing qualified
capacity where appropriate. It adds scheduling and transition costs and may
be infeasible in a small workforce.

Select a general rule for comparing and conditioning material changes. It may
authorize any of these arrangements when the applicable service floor,
real resources and remedy conditions are met. It does not make the hybrid
the national default. Keeping a site open cannot excuse inadequate services
elsewhere. Requiring a reversible stage can be expensive, and a failing
site may be impossible to reopen. Permit a separately qualified alternative
and price the opportunity cost of preserving options. Check serial small
cuts and hidden transfers of household costs against the same access baseline.
No independent reviewer or affected-party participation was secured.

## Decision, financing and continuity

The competent elected body authorizes the material change and its source.
Existing sector authorities retain their own appointments and judgments.
Residents, workers and opposition can present alternatives and challenge a
material omission. Independent review with protected evidence access and a
paid executor must be usable before irreversible action. Assess control of
funder, operator, evidence and relief separately. The national funder applies
common law and finance without acquiring the local decision by default.
Use existing interlocal law for material unresolved cross-boundary effects.

The service mandate, finance, asset and procurement decisions retain their own
authority and dates. Test whether real receiving survives both the transfer
and loss of a shared worker or route. Existing awards, wages, accommodations,
contracts, prior rights and unresolved remedies follow their lawful owners.
Uncertain financing or a failed standby keeps the optional irreversible step
on hold and exposes any unresolved service obligation. Operational responsibility
stays with authorized humans. AI may compare schedules and identify gaps.

## Evidence and continuation

[Primary comparators](sources.md) establish workable subjects for scrutiny.
The [matched case](../settlement/essential-service-network-reconfiguration/01-a-hub-and-an-accessible-local-route.md)
tests dated costs, qualified visits, transport, demand and independent standby.
This is design evidence under assumptions. Operational inputs, real sector
thresholds, paid independent review, legal authority and observed outcomes
remain unverified. [SNR01–SNR08](../country/C04/08/07-essential-service-network-reconfiguration.md)
records the selected common rule.

An authorized service owner and affected localities could next reconstruct one
real proposed relocation with observed round trips, qualified slots, households
missed, gross overlap and the next operating period. Include reasons and
operating effects of a viable retain-and-repair rival. Funding and safe
participant access are unresolved. Reopen if the rule preserves unworkable
sites, shifts unpriced costs to people, systematically misses earlier
exclusion or fails to deliver a qualified independent alternative.
