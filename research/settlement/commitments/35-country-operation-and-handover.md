# Current duties need paid operation and an exact successor

Design 122 · [OP01–OP24](../../country/C06/45-country-operation-and-handover.md)

All 37 original functions and 61 selections retain exact objects/bids/actual nulls;
all 61 inherited reference objects remain unchanged. The new separate operating
register links actual delivery categories, control, public/private/restricted
source and paid transition questions. It is not an exhaustive clause-level law
census or qualified national bill. All 34 current dependency notes retain scope.

Default compatible new support to existing C04/local services, with actual staff,
access, conflict, source and safe alternate arrangements. Independent powers,
selectors, budgets, protected records/publication and direct courts remain under
their own law. Shared payroll/hosting gives no policy, information or payment veto.
No generic admin bid supplies free OJ/AA/AS/WL/OE or current country capacity.

The same paid hour, asset, grant, receipt and economic loss is attributed once;
keep gross owners/claims and distinct loss heads. Unknown cost prevents a complete
total and is not zero; overlap also prevents automatically adding each old bid.
Capital/acquisition/CFC/principal/interest, G/local/S/client/private and owned FX
remain separate. Existing source/commencement and useful-time rules persist.

Adopting law must assign every transferred pending duty, case, record, contract,
worker/creditor/pension/foreign/source claim to an exact dated legal successor.
Pay authorized overlap and safe receiving; after transfer the successor remains
answerable even if the retained operator supplies a paid fallback. No new shared
guarantee, postponement power, automatic reversion, tax, new penalty or universal
person dossier. Neither a bank loan nor a judgment creates real capacity.

The authored 24-month example has 26 net source but 24 peak cash need before the
last receipt; an accepted 24 bridge costs.96 at 2% simple annual interest and leaves
25.04 after repayment. It does not establish a national source, future solvency,
actual legal lending/FX or receipt. Exact current sources/capacity/costs remain
unknown, including all new implementation/overlap/record/cover/advice costs.
