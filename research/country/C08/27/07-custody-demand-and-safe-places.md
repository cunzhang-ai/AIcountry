# PO25–PO28. Plan custody around lawful demand and safe shifts

*Design214 · Part of [C08 civilian enforcement](../27-civilian-enforcement-and-public-control.md).*

**PO25 — Put the demand created by law in the public budget.** For a
material change in remand, sentencing, review or recall, the proposing
government brings the existing lawful path and its proposed changes to
the Assembly with a joint FB and DB account. The corrections authority
supplies safe aggregate occupancy and flow evidence. Courts supply
independent aggregate evidence through their own authority. Separate
pending cases, lawful admissions, transfers, due release, changes in
time served and workload outside custody. Compare at least an ordinary
and a weaker path, including court delay, staffing loss and unusable sites.
Identify the people who bear the costs and include victims' safety,
defense, courts, clinical care, worker preparation, reintegration and
continuing local duties. No official can derive an individual's order
from a planning total. Elected changes apply prospectively under
applicable criminal and constitutional law.

**PO26 — Count usable places at the time and site people need them.**
For each site, the corrections director and warden identify places that
are physically safe, lawful for the case and supported by qualified
rosters, independent clinical receiving, counsel access, interpreters,
transport and a lawful contingency. Capacity is the set of placements
that fit these conditions together, including protected rest, training,
accessible conditions and overlapping duties. Count actual compatible
receiving rather than assuming all places are interchangeable. A vacancy
in another class or region, an unstaffed building and a newly enrolled
trainee supply no immediate staffed placement. Keep trainees, transport,
clinical and civilian custody roles distinct. An authorized schedule
exception needs its own source and actual compliance. A budget or AI
roster cannot supply it. Inspect cumulative use and the simultaneous
availability of independent remedies.

**PO27 — Reconcile the actual occupancy and resource paths.** Keep a
timed opening count, admissions and transfers, lawful exits and the
next peak by legal class and site. Match each transfer once on both
ends. A temporary empty place between departures and arrivals is no
standing reserve. When calculating demand, keep court orders and dates
distinct from forecasts. Compare same-period booked staffing with actual
presence, lawful rest, absences and pending qualifications. The duty
holder separately presents appropriations, gross receipts and expenses,
settlement before use, restricted capital, ongoing roster and upkeep
and lawful received alternatives. Net underspending does not release
capacity without the FB09–FB12 proof of actual coverage and usable
source. No compensation payment can supply the shift it concerns.

**PO28 — Protect court decisions from capacity management.** The
Assembly chooses general law, sources and prospective start dates. The
Civilian Corrections Service manages places, staff and lawful transfers.
Independent judges decide remand, sentences, release and recall.
Local and clinical services keep consent and receiving duties. No
appropriation, occupancy cap, forecast or staff shortage extends a
liberty order or selects an individual's punishment. Community measures
must be independently justified and actually usable under P, Q and RL.
Keep a no-supervision comparator where applicable, so the availability
of a cheaper device or service cannot widen control. A transfer must
preserve original dates, rights, health and realistic access to defense,
review and permitted contacts under its source law.

[OJ41–OJ48](../25-ordinary-justice-and-public-prosecution.md) adds shared
court and defense capacity to the same planning comparison. Custody and
release dates keep their original legal grounds.

[CA01–CA07](../12/06-court-appearance-and-pretrial-liberty.md) adds
funded attendance and verified warrant correction. Count actual hearings
and changed lawful admissions separately. Assume no cash saving from a
change in projected admissions until independently reconciled.
