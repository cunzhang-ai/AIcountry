# Design410 decision. Consolidate the cash account and preserve independent execution

## Baseline and affected scope

This AI-assisted selection uses [Design409](predecessor-design-409/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md),
the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).
DB and FB already distinguish authority, cash, restrictions and ordinary
execution. TU provides a backup for established household cash, and JC
tests shared failure in the first budget. The missing operating rule is
how multiple public functions retain executable payments across a common
release failure, including an unresolved earlier instruction and an empty
second-window reserve. Recipients, workers, suppliers, courts, local
authorities, taxpayers and operators are affected.

## Rivals and selection

Repair one central payment route. It may reduce idle balances and routine
reconciliation but leaves common execution and source-control exposure.
Keep independent full balances and payment authority for every entity.
That can reduce some dependencies but can strand liquidity, hide exposure
and fragment oversight. Select a consolidated public account with bounded,
independently executable balances for scheduled classes. Keep simpler
ordinary and lawful local options where they protect the same obligations.
A backup needs its own actual funds, authority, evidence and receipt.

The main objections are duplicate spending, costly idle balances, shared
bank or authorizer dependence, and failure of central oversight. Use
nonoverlapping release authority, independent finality evidence, bounded
funds, an actual second call and reconciliation. Compare total costs and
misses, including loss of the backup itself. Uncertain settlement may
require separately authorized urgent aid. It does not justify blind replay.
No independent reviewer or actual affected party was secured.

## Authority, compatibility and execution

The elected legislature decides scope and source. Treasury retains the
consolidated account and ordinary lawful controls. Named alternate officials
may execute exactly the duties authorized by law. Ordinary sector owners
and qualified payers retain merits and payment duties. Independent courts
and auditors retain their existing review and execution powers. Test
appointment, source access, evidence, control of payment and the remedies
against hostile withholding as well as operational failure. Neither shared
software nor the main payer may act as the sole gate to independent relief.

Carry actual claims, restrictions, court orders, escrow, earned pay, settled
transactions and unresolved finality through lawful funded succession.
Keep ordinary banking and custody rules. Source laws govern separately
funded advances and any later same-obligation credit. Replenishing a route
does not erase a late payment or confer a second release. AI may identify
possible conflicts, with qualified officials retaining authority.

## Evidence and continuation

[Primary comparators](sources.md) distinguish consolidated accounting,
payment processing and finality. The [constructed comparison](../settlement/public-cash-and-independent-execution/01-one-balance-two-payment-windows.md)
tests trapped cash, exclusive assignment, uncertain settlement, an
independent advance and the second call. [TPE01–TPE08](../country/C06/60/11-public-cash-and-independent-execution.md)
records the research selection. Actual adoption, operating costs, legal
authority, funded readiness and observed receipt remain unverified.

A competent public finance office and independent payment reviewer could
next reconstruct one real multi-programme outage with lawful original
records. Identify first-window balances, legal authority, actual settlement,
independent receipt, review and successor funding. Compare repair and
scoped prefunding with the same due claims. Staff, funds and safe access
are unresolved. Reopen on repeat double payment, excessive idle liquidity,
political withholding, stranded duties or a simpler equivalent route.
