## Diagnosis and alternatives

The mixed rule's independent personal seats reduce proportional-list N−I; list-
backed territorial winners need list-vote coverage. Genuine geographic mandates
and locally organized independents benefit. But a political backing/control
classification can alter seat entitlement, and a national elector without a
domestic home had no closed territorial assignment. A second national track
and all-inline preference pools also add interface/source burdens. These are
institutional/semantic gaps, not observed voter mistakes or evidence of fraud.

Alternatives: retain mixed with equal map/foreign track and strong classification
review; single national flexible-list PR; closed national lists; regional/STV/
multi-member representation with a complete map/absent route. Compare nomination,
voices lost/gained, practical person control, public support/cost/records and
handover, not only seat arithmetic or a wish for simpler ballots. The real local
mandate and independent-entry losses cannot be averaged away.

Values: equal political standing, individual supported choice, opposition and
peaceful control. Definitions: list vote, optional preference, person capacity,
home, actual-date versus known-P eligibility and source. Hypotheses: count, gate,
local size, calendar, medium/layout, assistance and behavioral response. Evidence:
root logical counterexamples and limited official guidance; no real electorate,
market/source quote or actual affected-user observation is supplied.
