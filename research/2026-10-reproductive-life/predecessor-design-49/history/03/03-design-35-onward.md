# Design history: design 35 onward

*Part of [Design history](../../DESIGN-HISTORY.md); historical research selections, not enacted law.*

36. **Integrated design 35: cumulative legislative capacity and decision
    scope made explicit, regional alternative remains conditional.** The
    [capacity decision](../../2026-10-legislative-capacity/decision-record.md)
    retains Assembly finality for unresolved material common choices but
    adds a prior-law scope route and one public account of combined bills,
    rules, treaties, crises, territorial requests and hearings. Harmful
    backlog requires a reasoned elected response, not automatic executive
    authority or a new regional map. C03, C04, C06, C09, C13 and C14 align;
    the design-34 predecessor is preserved. No real Assembly docket,
    affected electorate, staff, lawful competence or observed outcome
    validates the route.

37. **Integrated design 36: future options before material hard-to-reverse
    choices.** The [future-options decision](../../2026-10-future-options/decision-record.md)
    adds a prior-law trigger, long-horizon and cumulative-effect account,
    less damaging alternatives, elected reasons, enforceable floors and a
    credible restoration path at the competent scope. It rejects a general
    unelected guardian veto. C01, C03, C06, C09, C13 and C14 align; the
    design-35 predecessor is preserved. No real territory, project, affected
    public or observed result validates the choice.

38. **Integrated design 37: automation gains, income security and public
    power joined.** The [automation-settlement decision](../../2026-10-automation-settlement/decision-record.md)
    retains plural production and general taxation while adding a joint
    account of earnings, contributions, tax receipts, prices, service capacity
    and common AI control. A prospective trigger leads to an elected package
    comparison, not an automatic robot tax, income cut or public monopoly.
    C05–C07, C11, C13 and C14 align; the design-36 predecessor is preserved.
    No real economy or observed result validates the response.

39. **Integrated design 38: communications and essential logistics assigned.**
    The [decision](../../2026-10-communications-logistics/decision-record.md)
    assigns separate connectivity and freight stewardship, regulates plural
    operators, funds usable alternate paths and distinguishes legal notices
    and physical goods received from digital records of dispatch. Network
    control does not confer censorship or scarcity power. C03, C06, C07,
    C09–C11, C13 and C14 align; the design-37 predecessor is preserved.
    No real network, corridor or delivered outcome validates the choice.

40. **Integrated design 39: sanitation, cyber recovery and border clearance
    receive distinct responsibility chains.** The [decision](../../2026-10-service-boundaries/decision-record.md)
    assigns full sanitation/waste service stewardship, bounded cross-sector
    cyber assistance and coordinated customs release with separate tax,
    safety and protection powers. C06–C11, C13 and C14 align; the design-38
    predecessor is preserved. No real facility, system, border, affected
    public, lawful authority or observed outcome validates these choices.

41. **Integrated design 40: real market exit and product repair assigned.**
    The [market-power decision](../../2026-10-market-power/decision-record.md)
    adds a statutory, reviewable route for cross-sector economic dependence
    and a separate common consumer/product-safety floor. Remedies require
    actual switching, product removal or repair, while public firms and
    regulators face capture checks. C03–C06, C08–C11, C13 and C14 align;
    the design-39 predecessor is preserved. No real market, product,
    affected public or observed outcome validates the selection.

42. **Integrated design 41: the national form becomes conditional on real
    scale and public choice.** The [national-form
    decision](../../2026-10-national-form/decision-record.md) retains the
    Assembly/local arrangement only as a reference to compare with a directly
    elected region and issue-scoped joint services before a first contest.
    The founding public must choose the full electoral and territorial
    package from actual representation, workload, rights and resource
    evidence. Failure cannot transfer power to cabinet or AI. C02–C04, C06,
    C12–C14 align; the design-40 predecessor is preserved. No actual
    electorate, geography, lawful authorization or delivered result
    validates a form.

43. **Integrated design 42: government formation and caretaker power receive
    a complete failure route.** The [government-formation
    decision](../../2026-10-government-formation/decision-record.md) first
    seeks full-majority investiture, then tests a supported minority candidate
    against a majority veto. A full majority may replace or dismiss a cabinet;
    repeated failure calls a prior-law poll while a narrow caretaker executes
    only continuing lawful duties. C03, C06, C13 and C14 align; the design-41
    predecessor is preserved. No actual coalition, lawful first rule, staffed
    transfer or observed result validates the selection.

44. **Integrated design 43: health, education and care gain distinct delivery
    and remedy chains.** The [life-course services
    decision](../../2026-10-life-course-services/decision-record.md) keeps
    national effective access and public residual capacity but assigns
    separate clinical, school and care decisions, independent review, paid
    fallback and a voluntary cross-service interface. C04, C06–C08, C13
    and C14 align; the design-42 predecessor is preserved. No actual public,
    professionals, assets, financing, lawful authorization or observed result
    validates these arrangements.

45. **Integrated design 44: sovereign debt distress gains a public burden-sharing
    route.** The [sovereign-debt decision](../../2026-10-sovereign-debt/decision-record.md)
    distinguishes timing, solvency, foreign-payment and physical shortages;
    requires an elected negotiation mandate and exact binding vote; and carries
    bank, pension, creditor and service effects together. C06, C10, C13 and C14
    align; the design-43 predecessor is preserved. The integrated overview's
    operating questions were relocated into four system maps for navigation,
    without changing those prior substantive answers. No original contracts,
    real public, lawful authority, creditor consent or observed result validates
    the route.

46. **Integrated design 45: disaster recovery gains a durable democratic and
    financial route.** The [recovery decision](../../2026-10-disaster-recovery/decision-record.md)
    separates immediate protection, insurer claims, legal compensation and
    public rebuilding. Local elected planning compares safe repair, adaptation
    and supported movement; the Assembly authorizes national finance and
    cross-boundary burdens. C03–C04, C06–C07, C09–C10, C13 and C14 align;
    the design-44 predecessor is preserved. No real hazard, affected public,
    insurer, fiscal capacity, lawful authority or observed result validates
    the route.

47. **Integrated design 46: climate and nature transition gain a democratic
    whole-system route.** The [ecological-transition decision](../../2026-10-ecological-transition/decision-record.md)
    places a measured multiyear emissions path and distinct habitat/water
    floors under elected law, joins sector reliability and transition finance,
    and preserves worker, local and external burdens. C05–C06, C09–C10, C13
    and C14 align; the design-45 predecessor is preserved. No actual
    territory, baseline, treaty status, replacement capacity, public choice
    or observed outcome validates this research selection.

48. **Integrated design 47: ordinary population health gains a noncoercive
    operating chain.** The [public-health decision](../../2026-10-public-health/decision-record.md)
    assigns elected local prevention, national technical warning, separate
    clinical care and C10 compulsory authority, protected data and explicit
    finance. C04, C06–C07, C10–C11, C13 and C14 align; the design-46
    predecessor is preserved. No actual affected public, lawful data power,
    teams, laboratories, supplies, trust or observed health result validates
    this research selection.

49. **Integrated design 48: ordinary food security gains a whole-system
    choice.** The [food-system decision](../../2026-10-food-systems/decision-record.md)
    joins safe and adequate household food to production, labor, market
    power, land/water, imports, reserves and separate safety enforcement.
    C05–C10, C13 and C14 align; the design-47 predecessor is preserved.
    No real population, jurisdiction, diet, stock, law, public choice,
    budget, staff or observed outcome validates this research selection.

50. **Integrated design 49: political competition gains a financed,
    enforceable pre-vote route.** The [political-competition decision](../../2026-10-political-competition/decision-record.md)
    gives lawful new contestants baseline access, makes major sponsorship
    and paid reach inspectable, limits incumbent use of state resources
    and places urgent repair with independent courts. C03–C06, C11,
    C13 and C14 align; the design-48 predecessor is preserved. No real
    electorate, political-finance law, independent enforcement, usable
    campaign resources or observed replacement validates this selection.
