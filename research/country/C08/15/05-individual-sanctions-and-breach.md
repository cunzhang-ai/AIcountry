# Usable fines and community measures with hard endpoints

*Q01–Q06; part of [C08/15](../15-special-crimes-and-bounded-sanctions.md).*

**Q01 — Proportionate choice.** P16's no-sanction comparator, gravity/fault,
aggregate custody limits and full time credit govern K01–K24. For a natural
person's same factual course, choose one principal penal response: fine,
community measure or custody. Do not add full alternatives together.
Civil restitution and independently justified protection retain distinct
grounds; disguising additional punishment as protection is reviewable.
Wealth, refusal of optional restoration or a missing provider cannot justify
custody. No sanction may exceed the applicable statutory gravity ceiling.

**Q02 — Fine units and means.** A court sets zero to 60 units for offenses
with a custodial maximum up to two years; zero to 180 for maxima above two
and up to five; zero to 360 above five. The same-course total cannot exceed
360 units, or 90 for conduct at 16–17. Severity sets units; resources set
their value. One unit is **max(0, C − E)/60**, where C is monthly actually
usable resources and E verified essential living/access costs and legally
due dependant support. Match actual shared expenses once. Use a reasoned
90-day receipt history and material forward changes; income labels alone
do not establish access. Measure C before penal fines; the fine itself is
not an additional need creating a public top-up. No positive minimum unit
or administrative surcharge.

Proved legally reachable, nonessential liquid assets may enter C at one
twelfth of their net realizable value; protected essentials, disputed third-
party property, inaccessible partner wealth and illiquid housing/pension
claims are not automatically spendable. Disclose valuation, ownership, liens,
other due obligations and contrary evidence. No forced sale of protected
housing, essential work tools or health/care resources finances a penal fine.
The court can reduce units/value to avoid disproportionate impact, with
reasons and appeal; it cannot increase gravity because a person is rich.

**Q03 — Collection and expiry.** Monthly collection across all penal fines
is at most 25% of the person's then-current safe C−E surplus, never benefits
needed to meet E. Civil recovery must also leave that needs floor; do not
allocate the same surplus twice. Provide accessible reassessment on any
material change; verified loss permits prompt pause/reduction without a fee.
The fine ends at payment or 24 calendar months after the usable final order,
including pauses; cancel unpaid penal residue then. Child fines end within
12 months. A genuine inability to pay never creates custody or extra units.
Fraud/fabricated evidence needs a separate proved offense; it does not
extend this order silently. Victim restitution is a separate B/U claim and
does not expire merely because the penal fine does.

**Q04 — Chosen community work.** With informed agreement and independent
advice, a court may choose a suitable community measure: adults at most
180 hours within 12 months, six hours/week and 30/month; for conduct at
16–17, at most 60 hours within six months, three/week and 12/month.
Education, paid work, care, disability/access and safe travel bound the actual
schedule. The same-course aggregate is not multiplied by charges. Refusal
of a proposed placement is not guilt, breach or a basis for greater punishment;
select another independently justified lawful response using P16.

An accepted funded public/nonprofit placement supplies safe supervision,
transport/access costs, equipment, insurance and grievance access; no private
profit, forced household care, replacement of ordinary paid posts or victim
contact is assumed. If no suitable placement exists, reduce/end the measure
or choose an independently lawful response, not automatic custody. Hours
cannot be self-certified by an AI or converted into assumed fiscal savings.

**Q05 — Conditions and supervision.** Any condition must be necessary,
specific and practically available, with an identified receiver. Total adult
community supervision lasts at most 12 months, child supervision six,
sharing Q04's clock. No punishment by compulsory therapy, general work test,
home detention, continuous location tracking or blanket internet ban is
authorized by this schedule. A separate protective order needs its own
grounds, duration and review; it cannot evade the penal cap.

**Q06 — Failure and correction.** A caseworker records actual reasons for
noncompletion and first offers safe adjustment. Escalation needs usable
notice, counsel and an independent hearing within seven working days of a
proposed adverse variation; urgent challenge remains immediate. Prosecution
proves deliberate material noncompliance and actual ability beyond reasonable
doubt before a punitive variation. Inability, unsafe work, provider failure,
care or system outage alone is not breach. Credit all completed work; variation
cannot increase original total units/hours, extend original clocks or worsen
the principal response merely for noncompletion. No automatic fine-to-work/
prison conversion. Fresh harmful conduct uses a fresh case. Failure of the receiving
office retains its funded service/remedy duty, not a new offender debt.

RL uses Q05's restrictions and the same-course supervision ceiling for
conditional release. The release order and recall end within the single
RL07 window. Q06 governs punitive variations of community measures. Judicial
recall of the custodial principal response requires RL's separately proved
current serious risk and its own hearing. [RL06–RL10](../28/02-conditions-and-recall.md)
