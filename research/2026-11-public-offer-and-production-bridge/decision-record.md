## Competing diagnoses and choices

A public-payroll-only account misses supplier work/imports. Adding all upstream
turnover to that budget instead counts the same purchase repeatedly. An old
fiscal revenue headline can also include own-account production and imputed
receipts; subtracting one noncash side invents a deficit. These are different
accounting problems from actual insufficient workers, poor services, unfair
burdens or weak democracy. More data cannot settle those value/causal questions.

could displace it, but no actual context or data presently qualifies that model.
