## Priorities distinguish design from missing reality

The largest concrete **shared written defect located in this scope** is the
B16 start controlled by completeness, despite receipt and anti-reset duties.
It can permit indefinite ordinary waiting and lets an incumbent gatekeep lawful
entry without an explicit refusal. This is an inference from our own clauses,
not a measured rate of abuse or proof all sectors lack deadlines. Income,
status, data, electoral and urgent routes already contain important exceptions.
Repair this contradiction now; do not add a super-agency or another fiscal table.

Complete candidate cost/source/incidence and simultaneous independent staff are
important but empirically unqualified. Missing local facts do not prove missing
written institutions or justify inventing resources. Detailed private funded-plan
tax remains a positive design gap; it does not outrank this common standing rule
in this review. Means-test intrusion, public/private ownership and constitutional
form remain real disputes; coverage tables cannot settle them.

## Real alternatives and reasons

- Retain completeness-based timing: allows necessary inquiry and flexible cases,
  but a relevant unavailable proof can indefinitely postpone an enforceable date.
- Receipt-based duty with help and review, selected: exposes delay without proving
  eligibility; risks rushed denials, more reasons/help and displaced staff work.
- Tacit approval or deemed denial: gives predictable nominal status but unsafe
  approval can harm others, and deemed denial shifts delay costs to applicants.
- Reduce substantive assessments or choose rights-preserving U: may remove some
  proof burdens; it changes the national distribution/source bargain and does not
  remove identity, safety, consent or all additional-needs questions. Remains live.

No comparator establishes the chosen numbers as optimal or a superior country.
Next compare the **updated whole bargain** with strong retain/repair and simpler
rights-preserving alternatives, including cumulative administration, public control,
nonclaimants, ordinary freedom and paid transition. End in retain/revise/reject,
not an automatic AP subquestion or added institutional layer. Urgent harm/new
original evidence or a larger contradiction can reprioritize.
