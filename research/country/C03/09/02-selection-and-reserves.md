# EA05–EA08: complete the three sources without shared-control mythology

**EA05 — Two legal-source seats.** Under an express enabling law, the separate
[EA21–EA25 electoral selection formation](06-independent-electoral-selection.md)
chooses L1/L2 commissioners and their reserves under EA03. Its nine places
and exact appointment vote retain the mixed categories and six-of-nine
requirement with one affirmative vote per category and six lawful members
present. Use open candidate entry and only lawful preselected alternates
for conflicts. Members cannot choose themselves. An election-appointment
challenge goes directly to ordinary courts under EA16. The existing CJ06
shared-source arrangement remains the historical comparison and the
applicable process owner only where lawful transition preserves a case.

**EA06 — Two opposition-source seats.** Use CJ05's actual closed governing-cohort definition and
fixed-roll protections, expressly applied to this different appointment. The governing cohort
comprises cabinet participation and publicly recorded coalition/confidence-and-supply
agreements; one occasional policy vote is insufficient. At process opening publish named
occupied non-government seat positions and denominator O. Initial vacancies do not enter O;
later vacancies do not lower it. A lawful successor may vote only while outside government; a
member entering government cannot supply opposition approval. Individual switches do not
retrospectively count votes. Unsupported/unresolved classification allegations do not subtract a
position. A genuinely new government/agreement requires published reassessment and a fresh vote
with no transferred approvals. An unconflicted ordinary court reviews election-selection
classification before effect, not a minister authenticating ideologies.

Any member of that protected non-government cohort may nominate an EA03-eligible person.
Appointment requires **ceil(3O/5)** affirmative recorded votes of the fixed O, without any
additional cabinet/full-Assembly approval; absence/abstention does not shrink it. O=0 means this
source cannot appoint. Votes/reasons are public. This differs expressly from CJ05's judicial
appointment threshold; its ceil(3N/5) plus ceil(O/3) formula is not copied. A small or commonly
controlled opposition can still choose a partisan nominee; no rule certifies independence by
deleting lawful opponents' votes. No opposition source is created from fake officeholders at
founding.

**EA07 — Three public-source seats.** Use a separately drawn36-adult-elector panel for each
scheduled P-seat primary/reserve process, following CJ07's inclusive lawful roll, published
residence/age/access-language strata and pre-drawn replacement order, voluntary paid service,
care/travel/disability/interpretation support and protected source/candidate-hearing access. No
compulsory cultural identity/political-opinion test, vendor redraw or replicated-agent vote. At
least27lawful members must be present; each appointment needs24affirmative votes of the fixed36,
secret authenticated ballots and aggregate reasons. A direct personal/candidacy conflict uses
only the next eligible pre-drawn same-stratum replacement, never a preferred facilitator's
recruit. Primary and reserve require separate approval. Open direct candidates under EA03;
material objections, sponsors, contact and adviser/mediator selection are disclosed safely. The
panel expires180days from its first sitting; a fresh draw has no transferred approvals. These
election-specific uses of CJ07 receive fresh review under EA16; no CJ12 jurisdiction or
constitutional-judge approval is imported.

**EA08 — Timetable and one finite reserve per seat.** Open scheduled primary/reserve
selection180days before expiry; publish unresolved pool/qualification/availability by90days and
failure/hearing by30days. Unexpected vacancies open the same ordinary source process
within14days, with no shortened qualification notice. Before a decisive source vote, publish all
consenting nominees, common-criteria applications/reasoned exclusions and safely summarized
material conflicts for at least 14 calendar days, with usable objections and a reasoned source
response before voting. This common notice applies to all three sources and the founding route;
it is distinct from publishing a proposed appointment and qualification reasons at least 21
calendar days before taking office; every primary/reserve independently meets their source's
threshold. If multiple candidates pass for one place, a further single-choice ballot requires
that same fixed threshold; no plurality fallback. A person accepts one slot only. Good-faith
refusal, source disagreement or absence creates no misconduct, threshold reduction or term
extension.

Each seat has one named consenting same-source/criteria/vote reserve with its exact expiry.
Check eligibility, conflicts, paid standby/ordinary-work release and actual availability every
six months and before activation. Established temporary inability/recusal activates only that
reserve while needed; permanent vacancy activates them for the remaining term. If neither can
serve, the seat is unavailable. Replenishment uses the original source/process/remaining term,
not an ad hoc substitute selected for a pending dispute. New ordinary appointments may act only
under prospective conflict/assignment rules with adversarial review; they cannot retroactively
supply a missing vote. Source selection failure never migrates to cabinet, another source or the
commission itself.
