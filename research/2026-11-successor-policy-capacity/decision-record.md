The strongest rival applies existing sector scrutiny and ordinary repair,
with lower administrative burden. If a competent ordinary route meets the
same deadline with a real alternate, credit it in full. An extra vote can
delay useful construction or prevent a supplier from offering better terms.
Allow an explicit narrower or longer commitment with its service reason,
first re-entry date and enforceable protections. Opposition can contest and
offer a funded alternative under existing law. It acquires no policy veto.
Preserve rights, prior enforceable contracts, ordinary public work and urgent
orders on their terms. No fixed margin, reserve fund or new office is selected.

Actual enactment needs competence, thresholds within existing law,
record ownership, funded review and any selected readiness work on their
real sources. Test the political choice and service receipt together in a
real adopter's affected scope. Reopen if protecting the option causes a
material missed duty, qualified ordinary repair does the same with less
burden, procedural stacking needlessly delays a benefit, or a nominal
alternative cannot receive a lawful change. The frozen first round retains
its own status. This decision changes the working proposal.
