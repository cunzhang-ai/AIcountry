# Who pays for the shorter week

*Design473 · Denmark 2025 observations and a separate prospective
compensation sensitivity. Amounts are DKK billion.*

The [matched work account](01-observed-2025-and-boundaries.md)
reproduces 1,191.239 million government employee worked hours and
442.275 billion in employee compensation. The compensation matches
OFF3 exactly at the published 2025 vintages. OFF3 assigns 315.593
billion, or 71.36%, to the regional and municipal subsectors.
The government hours memo supplies no worked-hours split across
those employers. Employee compensation includes actual and imputed
contributions. It is an accounting measure rather than a wage quote,
transfer entitlement or appropriable cash.

## Hold the service task fixed and vary its funding

Assume all observed compensation belongs to groups working an
equivalent 37-hour reference week and undertaking identical tasks
at the same quality. Shorter hours proportionally reduce covered
task hours. Recruit proportionally at the same annual compensation,
with unchanged hourly task output, no changes in other expense or
revenue and no adjustment to existing transfers. An additional
one thirty-sixth of the compensation reproduces the same task hours
at 36. Five thirty-seconds does so at 32. This scales the whole
compensation bill deliberately as a sensitivity. Actual eligibility,
part-time terms, recruiting wages, demand, schedules and effective
hours vary. No estimate of actual coverage or achievable hiring
follows.

Using the same model, the second step adds 56.820 billion above
a fully funded 36-hour path, including 40.545 billion in regions
and municipalities. The 32-hour column already includes both
steps. Each displayed amount is independently rounded. A small
positive baseline margin may coexist with many different revenue
timings, deficits, restrictions and service needs at particular
payers. A reported annual balance also supplies no independent
test of local solvency, any balance target or an available transfer.

## Decide whose source moves and who can actually deliver

| Proposed decision | Disposition under this sensitivity |
|---|---|
| Keep current standards while funding qualified repair and paid preparation | Select for the observed-reference first motion. Preserve current duties and earned rights. State which workers' additional usable time is deferred and which services still need repair. |
| Enact a funded 36-hour start with a separate 32-hour decision | Bring to an exact vote with an annual source by payer, authority and dated local receipt, accepted shifts and independent review. A hypothetical central transfer matching local increments is 8.766 at 36; this returns each local subsector to its original balance and leaves consolidated lending unchanged relative to the modeled 36-hour path. |
| Enact both stages under one separately supported law | Show the full later increment and recurring source. A temporary transfer covering the first year or an offer of ordinary municipal borrowing does not fund the 32-hour recurring need. Compare a durable transfer with truly available own sources and independently verified task savings. |

A hypothetical central transfer matching the local 32-hour
increment of 49.311 leaves a central statistical balance of
21.014, before addressing the social funds' own coverage. It
does not change consolidated lending or move a single qualified
shift. There is no separate assumption of new interest, unchanged
private-sector employment, effects on prices or taxes, or access
to any particular clinic on time in a real system. Those effects
are unestimated. Give any actual transfer its own voted formula,
cash date, appropriation, recurring treatment and ordinary local
choice. Expiring aid and unsettled receipts retain their limits.

Compare funding by actual payer with paid local repair and
verified demand or process improvements, including safe, scoped AI
assistance where measured. Count savings within the employer,
qualified task and service window where they are demonstrably
released. Keep private provision and voluntary transfers explicit,
including their origins, wages and effects on prices. Neither
common legal coverage nor a national statistical surplus changes
the existing law on local revenues or supplies an accepted worker.
Preserve any enacted date and seek lawful correction if a source
or a roster fails. Review safe resident receipt alongside usable
time, pay and burdens for workers and households.

## Does a favorable year change the choice?

Use a separate [fixed-calendar sensitivity](../fiscal-durability/README.md)
for 2015–2025. Follow WF06's assumed effective task hours of 1,308.6,
1,269.0 and 1,110.6 across the three weeks, which include fixed paid
non-task time and therefore reduce task capacity by more than the
proportional calculation above. On the same original OFF3 balances:

| Fixed-calendar comparison | Years with negative statistical balance, out of eleven | 2025 statistical balance |
|---|---:|---:|
| Historical baseline | 1 | 90.924 |
| 36-hour sensitivity | 3 | 77.123 |
| 32-hour sensitivity | 5 | 12.074 |

The 36-hour sensitivity crosses below zero in 2016 and 2020 as
well as 2015. The 32-hour sensitivity also crosses below zero in
2017 and 2018. This distinguishes the stages under the same historic
inputs and the same fixed-calendar mechanism. Do not add the two
mechanisms or treat frequency as a forecast, financing target or
annual balance rule. Use each plausible range to question actual
recurring sources and test temporary risk response separately.

A signed workforce plan also needs intact receiving. The
[assistant and helper placement figures](../staffing-formation/01-danish-training-agreements-and-roles.md)
show how 9,958 agreements exceeded a 9,000 combined reference
while assistant agreements fell 510 below their own target in
2024. This is a different period and scope. Do not multiply either
year's pay bill by these agreements or count commitments to train
as qualified shifts. Verify actual qualification, paid mentor and
origin cover, and receiving through the next service window.
