# ECF01–ECF05. Keep elections ready and payable through the full cycle

*Part of [financing a fair political contest](../11-democratic-contest-finance.md).
Design255 research selection.*

**ECF01 — Vote the readiness and event budgets before the call.** The
Assembly sets the ordinary multi-year financial framework and enacts
standing limits and sources for essential contest duties. Each annual
budget shows three distinct requirements.

1. Pay recurring readiness in years with and without a poll. Name
   qualified staff, reserve appointments and drills, roll correction,
   open contestant intake, secure records, storage, accessible
   premises and independently usable communication and voting routes.
2. Finance the scheduled event and a bounded acceleration for a
   lawful early call. Stage cash, equipment, printing, dispatch,
   qualified shifts, campaign access and remedies before each relevant
   deadline. Test the earliest legally possible timetable in scope.
3. Pay timely independent review, actual lawful remedy, count,
   custody, certification and handover. Price a bounded repeat poll
   or recount where the underlying law provides for it. Reserve
   independent court and counsel capacity without booking it twice.

Define each tranche's lawful purpose, authority, payable dates, cap,
cash, actor, alternate and termination. Subtract the readiness work
included in a later event account once. Report shared facilities and
provider costs once without hiding actual competing capacity. Budget
local operations and necessary outside challenge alongside ordinary
benefits, justice, care and other essential services under JC.

**ECF02 — Activate money through the lawful act that fixes the contest.**
Routine readiness follows its own schedule. The competent electoral
authority records the valid published call or other existing lawful
trigger. The pre-enacted Treasury instruction releases the required
dated tranche within its cap for that scope. Court review and payment
remain independently reachable. Neither the executive nor a contestant
can require a further discretionary sign-off. The electoral authority
cannot enlarge the cap, call an election on its own, assign cases to
a favored judge or spend on a different contest. Give local operators
usable cash and staffing before due receiving, with an independent
lawful payer for obstruction or route failure under BLC. A dispute
over a discretionary addition cannot block an undisputed duty.
A bank balance, authorization and successful execution are separate.

**ECF03 — Hold an elective cutover when it could consume the contest.**
Define the protected window by the earlier of a valid call and the
first legally protected act for that contest, and keep material
protection through the affected count, review and handover. Set
earlier staging and training deadlines when the work requires them.
Publish the affected functions and when each hold ends. A still-open
case extends protection only where the change would impair that case.
During the window, withhold elective migrations that could materially
impair voting, opposition, independent review or another co-scheduled
protected service. A sector authority may release a genuinely isolated
change or a necessary and bounded safety repair under existing law
with reasons, tested alternate receipt, lawful rollback and a paid
operator. A new shared platform cannot certify itself. A late budget,
vendor failure or unresolved cutover changes no legal voting, review
or term deadline. Permit timely independent challenge to a staged
release, denial or prolonged hold. Preserve secrecy, valid filings,
ballots, source evidence and actual public access.

**ECF04 — Reconcile, replenish and expose the gap.** Give election
operators, opposition and other affected parties lawful access to
protected original budget, supplier and readiness evidence. The
independent reviewer and court may compel an already authorized
payment or specific execution under their own powers. If cash, scope
or operating capacity is genuinely absent, identify each threatened
act and seek a lawful urgent source choice. A finding creates no
appropriation, debt, new electoral power or authority to defer voting.
Preserve existing claims and remedies. After execution, show actual
local receipts, unpaid claims, accessible capacity and costs.
Replenish or release unused money only through the pre-enacted
rule or a new vote. A repeat event does not reset the cap, authority
or procurement term by itself.

**ECF05 — Keep electoral safeguards usable across governments.** Audit
appointments, staffing, actual balances, the ability to reach protected
records and the accepted alternate at both ends. Test an early call
in a non-poll year and a hostile release refusal or loss in the
same window as ordinary care, household payments and justice. A
disclosed lawful shared solution can replace separate components
where the same duties and independence survive. Keep the whole
funding and control chain reviewable under EA, ER, HT and DB.
AI may reconcile the work and flag dependencies and missing
evidence. It cannot trigger a call, certify readiness or release
funds. Leave protected work, resources, authority and open challenges
with lawful successors. Reopen after material late receipt, exclusion,
repeated budget transfer, common dependency loss or a better qualified
way to buy the same readiness.
