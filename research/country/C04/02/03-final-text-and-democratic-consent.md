# TS09–TS12. Exact settlement and separate democratic consent

*Design 143 · [Territorial-status reference](../02-territorial-self-government.md) · Subject to actual predecessor law and legitimate adoption.*

**TS09 — The text people authorize.** Before any final vote the exact settlement
states map, legal rank, powers, elections, courts, force, revenue/equalization,
currency, essential services and physical/ecological dependencies; land/title,
public/private debt, pensions, contracts, workers, nationality, residence/franchise,
foreign obligations, transition resources, successors, conditions and dates.
It states what each vote authorizes and what it cannot bind. Unknown assets,
foreign consent or paid capacity cannot be described as secured. A procedural
source check authenticates lawfully required material and gives reasons/review;
it does not empower EA, Treasury, a court or AI to veto policy on its merits.
The public consideration period begins only on lawful final-text certification C,
not scope certification S or petition qualification K. An unsettled negotiating
report is not a certified final settlement or a repeal of national A9 initiation.
A valid national A9 exact-text initiative does not require an area5% petition,
completed talks or Cabinet signature. Under the already adopted TS implementing
law, lawful national certification C also calls the separately scoped area question
on that exact text/map and PC14 calendar. TS scope S is an area-petition stage,
not a new executive veto over A9. Exact scope/standing disputes follow competent
source-law judicial review; officials cannot substitute their preferred proposal.

**TS10 — Two publics and protected rights.** Protected autonomy/sovereignty change
requires **both** the prior valid national A9 route and a separately counted
**majority of valid yes/no area votes**. Under the current A9 candidate, the
Assembly route needs two-thirds of full membership and a national valid-vote
majority; the national direct-petition route needs its own 5% qualification and
three-fifths of valid national yes/no votes. An area petition cannot substitute
for either national route. No turnout quorum; a tie or zero valid yes/no votes
fails approval. Do not average the two publics or use aggregate welfare as consent.
Any actual applicable collective consent, individual-right protection or foreign
legal requirement remains separately necessary within its lawful scope; ordinary
property owners gain no invented universal veto.

This chooses shared national authorization over unilateral area majority because
national rights, territory and common obligations are also affected. It retains
a serious objection: a national majority may repeatedly block an otherwise
justified area wish. Bounded negotiation, reasons and review do not eliminate that
value conflict. This procedure promises a usable process, not a favored outcome,
and cannot extinguish an applicable international self-determination route or
invent authority for a whole constitutional-order replacement.

**TS11 — Votes, dates and paid public choice.** For an adopted TS area question,
express framework law extends PC14's final-text calendar: lawful call at C,
P=C+120 calendar days and first voting day V=C+106. Required national and area
questions use separate electorates, results and scoped campaign credits, with
shared costs paid once. Each eligible person may vote in each distinct required
question, never twice in the same question. Existing national RR06 uses actual
predecessor-law eligibility on ballot day. Area eligibility likewise uses actual
ballot-day standing and electoral home under the already valid TS charter; an
accepted first lawful ballot fixes one area connection. Proposed new-status
citizenship or franchise cannot ratify itself. Ordinary EL's future-P eligibility
is not imported into these constitutional/status ballots.

PC's 1% committee-access endorsements are separate from TS 5% initiation and
national A9 petitions; voter-directed credits do not require committee membership.
New area campaign acts use actual-date eligibility, published sources, plural
committees, fair usable reach and ER remedies. Existing called polls retain their
actual law. The at-least-90-day consideration and ordinary 180-day A9 outer bound,
narrow lawful court postponement and PC14 unchanged-question annulment/repeat
exception retain their precise scopes. A lawful unchanged full rerun uses judgment
+120/+106 under that express prospective exception, not a fresh executive call.

**TS12 — No transferred mandates or succession loophole.** Material changes of map,
text or operative conditions require fresh applicable qualification and consent
from each required public. A result on another question is not reusable approval.
An unchanged partial result survives only if actual law and an independent court
permit severability, unchanged scope/conditions and the original approved dates;
otherwise obtain fresh necessary consents. Preserve actual new objections/evidence.

Calling a measure territorial reform does not evade A9/RR protected current terms
and immediate succession. Territorial boundaries, strategic relocation or selective
citizenship changes cannot remove opponents or become RR's neutral restoration.
A genuine whole-order founding needs its own actual legitimate authority; this
candidate, an EA certificate or an AI tally supplies none.
