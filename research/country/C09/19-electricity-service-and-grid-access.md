# C09. Electricity service, grid access and accountable reliability

*Design193 · EG01–EG18 · Proposed ordinary electricity law.*

**Keep the public grid independent of electricity sellers. Secure ordinary
service and a funded default supplier, and allocate scarce connection
capacity under elected rules.** Existing [PS power engineering and
control](../C06/37/03-power-time-and-public-control.md) and [backup-power
allocation](../C06/37/06-critical-public-power-and-actual-priority.md)
address engineering and scheduled temporary public backup. EG supplies
ordinary operators, charges, access, service failure and continued delivery.

| Canonical part | Selected duties |
|---|---|
| [EG01–EG04: control and ordinary grid access](19/01-operator-and-connection.md) | Separate public system operation, networks and sellers. Actual connection paths, milestone-based scarce capacity and voluntary limited service. |
| [EG05–EG08: paid service and failure of a supplier](19/02-tariffs-and-supply-continuity.md) | Elected attributable charges, essential access, independent relief and paid supplier-of-last-resort. |
| [EG09–EG12: reliability, outage decisions and succession](19/03-actual-reliability-and-succession.md) | Validate common dependencies, lawful rotation and real restoration, operator review and paid transition. |
| [EG13–EG18. Large connections and paid capacity](19/04-large-grid-connections-and-paid-capacity.md) | Qualify stages, attributable costs and funded exit through shared power, water and recovery constraints. |

Both working households and large load owners have access to fair published
terms. A household's protected essentials cannot serve as cheap interruption
capacity. Large industry and computation may use voluntary flexible service
on actual feasible terms. The country selects no technology quota, national
retail tariff, forced transfer of assets or unsupported reliability guarantee.

dispatch, service and emergency authorities distinct.

[CBG climate budgets](27-climate-budgets-and-essential-continuity.md)
adds a separate gross-emissions account and elected correction.
A measured ceiling does not itself instruct a dispatcher or meet
the essential service receiving obligation.

[MS17–MS21](../C06/61/05-essential-energy-shock-and-affordability.md) joins
the applicable tariff and actual protected supply to a voted household
bridge. A relief credit cannot substitute for physical power, safe
alternatives or timely correction.
