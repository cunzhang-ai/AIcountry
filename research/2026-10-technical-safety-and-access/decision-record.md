# Defined technical duties without an unaccountable technology sovereign

15 October 2026 · Decision 78 · Conditional research selection, not adoption

## Baseline and decision scope

Design 77 has common V regulatory safeguards, nine duty predicates, sector
safety/access promises, C04 bounded rulemaking, B/P remedies and civilian power.
It insufficiently specifies which technical acts need permission, operational
evidence and renewal, and how actual safety investigation/repair continues.
Preserve the [439-artifact baseline](predecessor-design-77/SNAPSHOT.md).
The proposed framework and decision template guide research only; no procedure
approves itself or overrides actual constitutional holds and current law.

Select a common technical statute and explicit domain predicates within C08,
with C09 operation, C07 clinical product/care and C06 resource consumers. This
is not authority to regulate, license, build, experiment or deploy. A legitimate
competent public must decide actual adoption under existing rights/law. No
territory, treaty-party status, actual operator inventory, affected participation,
sponsor mandate or independent qualified reviewer is supplied. Same AI controller.

## Diagnosis, alternatives and research selection

A form-only regulatory solution may miss unsafe design, underinvestment, bad
work incentives, ownership and unavailable alternatives. TS02/C09 therefore
place safer systems, maintenance and access before a blame/permit response.
[Alternatives](../settlement/technical-safety/02-alternatives-and-burdens.md)
compare unchanged general promises, private professional/insurance governance,
public direct provision and the selected plural operators with exact public
duties, graded entry and distinct assessment. Public provision remains a real
rival, potentially preferable by sector, not a footnote absorbed into licensing.

Select [TS01–TS16/TD01–TD12](../country/C08/22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md)
conditionally. Ordinary low-risk life/support/repair/speech/AI use is not licensed.
Listed consequential activities have defined entry, actual controllers, operating
conditions and safe cessation. Bind exact public standard versions, units/methods/
uncertainty, lawful source and prospective dates; no private paywall, dynamic
foreign incorporation or black-box risk score supplies coercive law.

TD defines transport, buildings, dangerous plant, public water/food, medicines/
devices, reserved acts, environmental uses and radiation. Nuclear-fission/fuel-
cycle operation, deliberate open self-propagating engineered-organism release
and launch/reentry need particular prior Acts and competent regimes. Ordinary
unknown innovation is not automatically banned. Actual jurisdiction/foreign
consent and distinct bodily/privacy/force law remain necessary.

Road defaults propose age 18, an assisted-cycle 25 km/h license exemption and
30/50/80/100 km/h categories, with actual separation and safe local conditions.
Only mixed-street 30 is informed by the inspected WHO campaign; other values
are uncalibrated choices. The water 100 mL candidate has explicitly partial
source retrieval, not a full verified method or safe-water certificate.

Automation approval identifies actual domain, controller, feasible fallback and
human capacity where needed; no passenger/patient is made a nominal rescue
operator by contract. Validated routine corrections within a prior change envelope
need not fully relicense; changed purpose, population, barrier, control or domain
requires scoped prior reassessment. New incidents/objections reopen support even
when dependency hashes are unchanged. Agent copies multiply no mandate/evidence.

TS06 proposes competent conflict-screened assessment assignment with bounded public
fees instead of an applicant purchasing its preferred final judgment. Real alternate
expertise/source access/funding are unproved; scarcity or cost may defeat this choice.
Relevant external evidence needs actual configuration, purpose, population, local
conditions and adverse-event review, not a logo or automatic duplicate testing.

Serious scheduled incident knowledge reaches the competent receiver within 24
actual hours and earlier where useful warning is needed; uncertainty, privilege,
original logs and protected third-party reports remain distinct. TS08 safety
investigation continues independently alongside justice, with court-resolved access,
family communication and protected factual reasons. No new verdict power, blanket
witness immunity, public clinical archive or promise of completed correction follows.

VD10 explicitly adds precise technical conduct to V's closed prospective schedule.
V06 proof, A's notice/20-working-day correction, B's material knowing/reckless fault,
existing monetary ceilings, child rules, same-course credit, periods and defenses
remain. No accident, undefined threshold, missed form or model score is guilt.
TS02 explanation alone creates no punitive predicate. Stop/repair uses V/B's
bounded grounds/hearings; no new general custody, tax or emergency power arises.

## Evidence, dissent and cumulative power

[Sources](sources.json) distinguish eight entries/six institutional families:
WHO road/devices/water, IAEA, ICAO, ILO, UNOOSA and UNECE. Actual device PDF
classification/reliance/post-market portions were inspected; page metadata 126
pages differs from the linked TRS 1045 rendering of 147 PDF pages. WHO water
table/full download and several treaty URLs failed retrieval; indexed excerpts
are recorded as partial, not full inspections. No publisher-byte archive or
complete technical/clinical/treaty conformity is claimed.

ICAO's 27 March 2026 announcement specifies Amendment 20 applicability on
23 November 2028. It informs our independence diagnosis without pretending that
future amendment is current national law. No complete Annex 13 was inspected.
[Twelve authored cases](../settlement/technical-safety/01-cases.md) are reasoning
prompts, not independent observations, legal verdicts or actual participation.

More forms or technical oversight could increase exclusion, delay and capture.
The selected model may be worse than capable public provision or well-governed
shared services. Preserve these objections and seek original evidence that can
displace the model. Material risk/access/fiscal choices remain elected law;
qualified bounded detail does not become a power to replace it. C04/V appointment,
removal, conflict deputies, opposition scrutiny, ordinary funds and court execution
apply; operator/promotion, assessment, enforcement and safety learning must be
materially distinct. Shared sponsors/vendors/datasets are disclosed, not counted
as independence. Public ownership also needs outside scrutiny and lawful handover.

## Compatibility, resource and continuity result

[Instrument register](../settlement/technical-safety/04-instrument-register.md)
names actual missing local annexes and their owners. A national generic design
can specify duties without inventing geology, aircraft, doses or clinical evidence.
Annex-dependent new permissions require actual instruments; no invented limit
supports punishment or automatically cancels all existing lawful essential activity.
TS15 maps existing licenses, source versions, pending cases, assets, workers,
injuries, insurance/closure and accepted successors before lawful commencement.
Stronger actual duties/current law survive until authorized compatible revision.

C06/27 counts assessment/metrology, maintenance, investigation, courts, safe
substitutes and long-term loss by actual skill/shift/place/controller. Private
insurance is a counterparty claim/cost, not new repair capacity or automatic public
guarantee. No old bid/SF cash, future fine, shared collateral twice or AI saving
funds the increments. All actual quantity, costs, qualified sources/capacity,
consent, adoption, received remedy and outcomes remain unknown. Failed stop/
receiving chains remain unresolved obligations, not certificates of readiness.

Research result: conditional TS/TD/VD10 selection. Evidence: qualified partial
source inspection plus authored reasoning. Legal authority: none verified.
Readiness and observed superiority: unknown. Checks address organization/identity
only, never engineering safety, licensing competence, guilt or better lives.

Next audit the full country, its real democratic controls, ordinary freedom,
legal compatibility, household/production/services, single resource/cash account,
transition and improvement method. Address the most consequential incompatibility
rather than automatically write another sector study. Reopen on excluded people,
undefined coercion, captured expertise, failed repair, impossible capacity or a
