# Test communications access and network power

*Design224 · Support for [CN01–CN12](../../country/C09/22-communications-access-and-network-power.md).*

The selected country compares three separate results. Can a resident
receive the ordinary service at its full price? Can they change carrier
or challenge discriminatory treatment in time? Can a named critical
receiver actually be reached during loss of the ordinary network?

- [Observed bottlenecks, affordability and outage](01-observed-communications.md)
  qualifies what distinct official records establish and where their
  scope ends.
- [One locality and one failed network](02-locality-and-shared-failure.md)
  tests three actual policy choices against one authored baseline and
  identifies the uncovered budget and incident windows.
- [Decision and successor duties](../../2026-11-communications-access-and-network-power/decision-record.md)
  records the selected rule, rival, migration and evidence that can change it.
