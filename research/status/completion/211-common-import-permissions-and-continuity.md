# 211. Common import permissions and continuity

[Design303](../../2026-11-common-import-permissions-and-continuity/decision-record.md)
selects procurement and continuity on verified dependencies rather
than supplier count. [NS26–NS30](../../country/C06/54/07-common-import-permissions-and-continuity.md)
keeps existing contract claims, lawful foreign authority, owned
dated currency and licensed local receiving separate.

The [two-window comparison](../../settlement/common-import-permissions/README.md)
tests shared permission loss and a dated gross-cash constraint. A
late refund does not finance an earlier alternate. Actual qualified
suppliers, protection, public authority and patient receipt remain open.
