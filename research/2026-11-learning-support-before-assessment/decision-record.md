# Design336 decision on learning support before assessment

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design335, ED01–ED33, XR04, FT, existing learner protections and
the working framework. Current design protects support during
interruptions and execution after an award. Add a specific pre-award
decision clock, fund the immediate educational response separately
from assessment, and carry comparable support into the first affected
teaching opportunity after transfer. The project assistant authors
the choice. Actual lawmakers and competent public bodies retain
their duties and authority.

## Alternatives and evidence

Compare a formal assessment gate, school-led early adjustments without
a fixed decision deadline, and a dual route with early support and
independent assessment. A formal gate can standardize evidence while
leaving avoidable learning loss. Ordinary early adjustment is more
flexible, but a school can leave a harder case in a queue. Select a
bounded interim decision and an enforceable assessment deadline with
paid independent review. The proposed dates have costs, and unsuitable
standardization or rushed assessment is a real risk. Preserve consent,
professional scope and more protective dates.

The [primary comparators](sources.md) supply equal education, school
support and comparable services at transfer. They do not establish
the exact two-day or 30-day choices, qualified staff or observed benefit.

## Authority, migration and reopening

The education owner coordinates a named school and outside assessment.
Existing professional, child, clinical and court roles remain distinct.
Elected budgets fund actual support, independent assessment and
ordinary receiving. Preserve original due dates, awards, stronger
duties, safe records and actual paid handover. Adopt prospectively
with qualified staff and independent review.

Exercise two first-week schedules with consenting learners, carers,
teachers and qualified independent support. Compare time to actual
use, missed teaching, effects of early action, shared staff and
access under a lost outside slot. Reopen on hurried or delayed
assessment, unequal provisional support, repeated lost transfers,
unlawful disclosure or a simpler effective route. Actual adoption,
delivery costs, learner participation and outcomes remain open.
