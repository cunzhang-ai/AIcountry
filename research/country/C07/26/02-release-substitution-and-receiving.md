# Activate a lawful response and protect each patient

*Part of [MQ critical medicines](../26-critical-medicines-and-safe-continuity.md).*

**MQ04 — Separate actual usable stock from stock on paper.** The
qualified stockholder reconciles traceable lots, release status,
expiry at use, presentation, legal control and reserve commitments.
The receiving pharmacist or facility checks lawful release, condition,
necessary instructions and actual ability to use the batch. Keep
quarantined or recalled lots separate. TD09 independently decides
quality, recall and any temporary import under specifically enacted
authority. A temporary route names the exact product, source,
presentation, conditions, traceability, label or instructions,
expiry and review. Foreign approval, emergency procurement and a
clinical request confer no domestic release by themselves. No
exception supplies a new compounding, licensing or compulsory
treatment power.

**MQ05 — Dispatch before the clinical window closes.** The NCS
duty officer starts the funded continuity route when available
evidence cannot support ordinary delivery by its last safe start.
Use only authorized stocks and funded receiving, with OC/RC and
ordinary procurement rules where applicable. Match stock to each
place's own safe deadline, storage, staff, transport and known
conditions for use. Check that a transfer leaves the supplying
place and the next response window protected. The shortage notice
states responsible officers, scope, lawful release, actual arrival
and timing limits, current gaps and review. It cannot change an
entitlement, divert committed private property or declare a new
emergency power. Disagreement or unavailable approval triggers
existing correction and assistance, without silent renewal.

**MQ06 — Give the patient a qualified choice that can be delivered.**
Where enacted law permits, a dated product-specific shortage
protocol may allow a qualified pharmacist to dispense a precisely
specified alternative after checking the patient's needs, contraindications
and actual consent. Publish eligible presentations, exclusions,
required competence, checks, records, expiry and a prescriber route.
A change that needs an individual treatment decision goes to a
qualified prescriber or other lawfully authorized clinician. A
substitute needs its own lawful supply, delivery capacity and
verification of the consequences for its existing patients. No
unattended AI substitution, generalized interchange or penalty for
refusal. The original team remains responsible until an accepting
provider and the patient have a safe plan.

**MQ07 — Preserve actual receiving and individual correction.**
Apply enacted clinical priorities to real harm, urgency and medically
usable alternatives. Do not rank people by income, political standing,
predicted social worth or volume at their facility. Use the existing rule for
comparable claims and document uncertainty and any unresolved need.
Offer an accessible explanation, independent useful-time clinical
review or court protection where applicable, and a safe transfer
or alternate receiving route where available and accepted. Pay covered
transport, interim care and clinical work through the authorized
source. A stock reservation, pharmacy dispatch, substitute offer
and actual treatment are separate records. The service remains
responsible for uncovered cases and the original treatment window.
