# Two dry seasons and one reservoir

This constructed comparison tests [WSR01–WSR08](../../country/C09/16/07-multi-season-water-reserves.md).
One reservoir starts with 100 usable water units above nonwithdrawable storage.
Assume protected flows of 12, physical losses of 2 and protected household and
service demand of 38 in each season. All rights in the worked discretionary pool
are assumed to be subject to the lawful reduction. First-season inflow is 12.
The adopted dry comparison uses second-season inflow of 5. The first known
protected window after season two needs an additional 4 units of closing stock.
Assume the stated withdrawals, flow releases and deliveries are compatible in
quality, time and location. No alternative draws from this same reservoir.

After first-season protected flows, losses and deliveries, 60 units remain.
For the adopted dry case, carryover of 51 is needed because the second
season requires 38 plus 12 plus 2 plus 4, less inflow of 5.

| Choice | First-season discretionary delivery | Second-season protected delivery | Closing usable stock |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Allocate 25 now using only current-season needs | 25 | 26 of 38 | 0 of 4 |
| Retain 51 now for the adopted dry case | 9 | 38 of 38 | 4 of 4 |
| Allocate 25 now with a qualified independent second-season source of 16 | 25 | 38 of 38 | 4 of 4 |

The final option delivers 16 independently to protected users, so only
22 units of protected use draw on the reservoir in season two. The option
needs actual committed capacity, payment and receiving before reliance.
No price or real supplier is assumed. Retaining the 51 reduces first-season
discretionary deliveries by 16 compared with allocating 25. That cost to
present users belongs in the elected comparison.

If second-season inflow instead falls to zero and carryover is 51, delivering
the maximum current protected water after flows and losses gives 37 of 38
and leaves zero for the closing requirement. A qualified direct source of
5 would bring current receipt to 38 and leave 4 stored. A late, unfunded,
incompatible or dependent source cannot do so. This branch reports both
shortfalls. It does not select a new priority between protected needs.

The figures demonstrate only the stated physical accounting and dated
availability. They provide no local forecast, legal permission, ecological
threshold or evidence of safe delivery. [Reproduction](verify-water-reserves.py).
