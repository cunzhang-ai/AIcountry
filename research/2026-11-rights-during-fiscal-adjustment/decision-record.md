# Design352 decision on protecting rights during fiscal adjustment

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design351, FB01–FB35, enacted minimum duties as a future-law
assumption, equal standing, DB, JC and the working framework. Existing
design preserves authorized duties when a release is blocked. Add a
distributional and rights test to proposals to reduce actual access
through changes in budgets, charges or service inputs.

Select an express elected decision for material reductions, a protected
minimum, an outer time limit for the proposed temporary route and
independent verification of mitigation. The project assistant authored
this selection. Elected bodies choose funding and lawful prospective
rules. Sector owners execute. Courts enforce existing applicable
law and independent remedies within their authority.

## Alternatives and objections

A uniform freeze is quick and can concentrate loss on people with
little margin or no private alternative. A broad rule against every
reduction can lock in waste and prevent improvements elsewhere.
Select a minimum with matched, independently challengeable reasons
for reductions above it. Keep transparent revenue, deferral and
resource repair in comparison and show the households and future
projects that bear their costs.

The [primary sources](sources.md) describe justification of
retrogression and distributional appraisal. The candidate's exact
scope, temporary limit and protected decisions need political
and independent review. Additional reporting and restricted
timing can also impose costs. Pay for useful-time scrutiny and
compare lighter routes on the same access and rights.

## Transition and reopening

Keep actual accrued claims, source restrictions, courts and remedies.
Adopt prospectively with funded reviewers and actual sector capacity.
No qualified independent or affected review, enactment or observed
delivery has occurred.

Exercise a revenue shortfall with people affected by care and income
loss, service owners and independent review. Compare continued
access and burdens at the original deadline and after the temporary
measure ends. Reopen on hidden cost shifts, concentrated losses,
unfunded remedies or a better feasible lawful response.
