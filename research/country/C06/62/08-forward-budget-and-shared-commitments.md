# FB42–FB47. Decide a common forward budget before binding new commitments

*Design362 · Part of [lasting services and fiscal shocks](../62-lasting-services-and-fiscal-shocks.md).*

**FB42 — Vote a common limit and an unallocated margin.** Before choosing
material additions at the ordinary budget, the Assembly records a vote on
the common four-year expenditure path, baseline and protected unallocated
margin under FB01. State coverage, cash and accrual treatment, debt and
asset effects, prices, restricted funds and excluded scopes. Use comparable
accounts for spending, foregone net revenue, guarantees and other material
ways of financing the same policy; do not add unlike amounts to one sum.
Apply a current-year new-commitment limit through the budget law. The
forward path is the declared comparison and parliamentary baseline for
later-year effects; future spending requires its own valid authority.
The elected budget can revise the path openly. If the first vote fails,
keep the last adopted path visible and use the ordinary DB budget and
amendment procedure to present a replacement. No failed vote authorizes
new spending. No fixed percentage or annual balance target is selected.

**FB43 — Charge all proposed commitments to the same remaining room.** Put
each separable option and linked package on the account for every affected
year. Subtract existing and selected costs, agreed source restrictions and
the margin once. A proposal fits only within both the applicable limit
and qualified source path. Preserve actual gross cash and recurring
capacity as separate constraints. Include linked local delivery, operating costs,
exposed guarantees, reviews and commitments extending beyond year four.
A different sponsor, classification, new contract or year-end move cannot
use the same room twice or move a continuing cost outside scrutiny. The
margin is neither an appropriation nor a reserve of workers. Show exact
contingent claims, approved risk ceilings and scenario costs separately
from ordinary expected spending. Credit a saving only at the scope and
dates qualified under FB09–FB12.

**FB44 — Compare packages and let the elected body change the plan.** Present
a continuing-law path, a smaller or staged package and a materially different
funded alternative on the same baseline. Show when people gain access,
what a deferral costs, who pays, and which adverse path would expose a due
duty. Price preparation separately and hold unadopted stages where needed.
The Assembly can choose a changed limit, additional net source, lawful
reprioritization or a time-limited response by recorded vote on the exact
text and effect. Ordinary member alternatives retain paid analysis under DB.
A forecast, model or officer cannot block an elected choice within law.
A change of limit alone supplies no appropriation, money, staffing or
approval to breach a protection.

**FB45 — Reconcile the old and new envelopes without erasing the past.**
At each budget, Treasury identifies changes to continuing-law cost,
qualification or source, economic assumptions, proposed policy, actual
receipt and remaining margin, with the earlier voted path still visible.
Distinguish shifts of scope or dates from genuine improvements. The
independent fiscal office and ordinary audit may reproduce the calculation
and challenge omitted cost, downside or double use. The successor
government can propose a new package through ordinary elected procedure;
preserve existing duties, binding contracts, original dates and verified
source custody through handover.

**FB46 — Treat a lost margin and an unpaid duty as different events.** On a
material deterioration, identify the breached planning margin, any actual
authority or cash gap and the earliest threatened service, payment or
review. Use existing authorized funding and ordinary correction first.
Report choices before the useful time through DB, JC and FB05–FB06,
including a lawfully financed finite supplement where justified. A
planning breach creates no authority to postpone standing claims or cut
essential services. An unadopted addition can wait under its lawful
conditions. A binding commitment retains its actual claim and remedy.
A temporary margin change names its purpose, source, exposure, expiry and
route back to ordinary funding. A recurring loss needs a recurring choice.

**FB47 — Verify the people, money and delivery beneath the plan.** Before a
lawful release, check original due dates, owned cash, qualified staff,
paid local receiving, maintenance, elected and independent protection on
the same schedule. AI can reconcile proposals and replay alternatives from
inspectable inputs. It cannot make a forecast legally spendable, move
protected finance, certify a worker or decide an allocation. Publish actual
service and receipt beside the reconciled path. Reopen on a repeated margin
call, omitted tail, shifted burden or a cheaper qualified whole package.
