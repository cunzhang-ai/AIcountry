# MF19–MF24. Follow equalized resources to people within a locality

*Design360 · Part of [funded local mandates](../08-funded-local-mandates-and-equalization.md).*

**MF19 — Compare access at the scale of the duty.** For each material
common-floor service in the local budget, show who is eligible, how they
can reach the service, who delivers it and when it is due. Break out
material differences by service catchment, access needs and relevant
groups using lawful, proportionate evidence. Include people unable to
apply or use the ordinary route and short-lived population changes
where they affect the duty. Use actual receipt, useful travel or waiting
and qualified capacity. A municipal average, facility count or voted
amount cannot certify a missed local route. Protect privacy in small
groups and allow qualified independent access to necessary originals.

**MF20 — Give the council a real budget choice within the common floor.**
At annual budget and before a material change, the council compares the
qualified cost of reaching the affected people with its actual, dated
sources and staffed routes. Show the funded floor, alternatives for any
unmet need and proposed optional enhancements in the same account.
Local elected bodies can choose different delivery methods and spend
different amounts where that meets the same applicable duty. Do not
use a uniform amount per resident as proof of equal access. Credit
shared trips, venues, staff, receipts and other public sources once
under MF03. Different health, education and local service authorities
retain their own decisions, payers and rights.

**MF21 — Separate internal allocation from missing source or capacity.**
When a material gap is credible, the responsible local officer states
which part comes from lawful resource assignment, dated cash, qualified
staffing, geography, omitted need or delivery failure. Use independent
service and finance evidence and disclose unresolved causes. Compare
reprioritizing unbound local resources under lawful authority, buying a
paid shared route, and a verified national source correction where
necessary. An assessed transfer needs its own lawful source; it is no
automatic reimbursement for local choices. An available balance cannot
supply staff or buy a service outside its legal purpose. A binding
local contract keeps its own performance, exit and remedy rules.

**MF22 — Give residents a direct correction route.** A resident,
worker or relevant body may report a lost or inaccessible common-floor
service through ordinary assisted intake. Preserve first receipt and
earlier urgent sector duties. A substantiated material finding by the
competent sector authority or an authorized independent reviewer starts
the duty to give a reasoned account and a funded corrective proposal
within ten working days. Where an elected decision is needed, the
council records its disposition within 30 calendar days of the finding.
The assessed council cannot veto an independent finding. Publish missed
deadlines and use the earliest lawful correction.
Give affected people and local opposition safe access to evidence and
alternatives. Permit direct independent review of scope, rights and
execution; a resident need not establish the entire pattern to enforce
an individual right.

**MF23 — Keep the remedy directed at the service.** Protect due work and
actual lawful sources while the cause is checked. Use MF16 or LD14–LD18
only within their adopted coverage, authority, cash and expiry. Where
a local choice caused the shortfall, correct that choice and use any
lawful recovery separately. The national funder cannot withhold an
unrelated grant, pick the council's politics or take over general local
government. If no lawful funded delivery fits the original window,
record the unmet duty, who bears it and the independent remedy. Verify
receipt and the next service window before reporting recovery. An
interim ride, session or visit is counted only once and where it actually
met the relevant requirement.

**MF24 — Reconcile evidence and revise prospectively.** Publish safe
comparisons of need, lawful appropriations, staffed delivery, actual
receipt and pending correction. Cover omitted neighborhoods, dissent
and recurring costs under PCO and annual MF assessment. A structural
need change returns to the next qualified source decision, including
fixed costs and successor obligations. Reopen on repeated exclusion,
an unverified change of map, worsening privacy or an equally protective
simpler method. AI may compare schedules and point out omitted areas.
An AI score cannot decide eligibility, funding weights, allocation or
receipt. Preserve actual claims, lawful contracts and funded review
through local boundary, operator or source change.
