# PA05–PA08: products, prices, assets and the five linked tables

*Part of [C06/38](../38-work-pay-prices-and-country-income.md); design93 research.*

**PA05 — Give ordinary private goods real contents.** Alongside PS food/water/
power and H housing, specify clothing, hygiene, communications, household tools,
repair, mobility and ordinary private services by pieces, mass, trips, hours,
stock/life and actual usable access. Keep clinical/disability/additional needs,
personal discretion and voluntary extra demand visible. An aggregate unit is
usable only with stated contents and valid scope; an undefined other-goods
residual cannot receive zero cost or nominal-spending-proportional production.

The authored pack includes imported garments/hygiene/devices/appliances, local
retail/repair/network/services and bus mobility. It is neither compulsory shopping
nor an adequate basket. Household collection consumes time/journeys; a shop count
is not accessible delivery. Repairs do not automatically extend replacement life.
Household/device ownership does not become a smartphone condition for rights.

**PA06 — Price the producer, then the household.** Domestic prices expose
paid roles, intermediates, upkeep, land, capital consumption, actual finance,
tax and returns. Imported price boundaries distinguish overseas delivery, border,
domestic handling and taxes. Use the maximum of compatible task/coverage duties,
not their sum; count distinct workers/tasks separately. Internal electricity
charges need a complete producer tariff, not only an imported-energy quote.
Unknown upstream costs remain unknown even when a named subtotal is computable.

A cheaper material or partial cost quote cannot reset I02 ordinary protection,
I03 housing, Y indexation or an old entitlement. A new price/basket selection
needs same-needs quality, actual availability, household incidence, personal cash
and fresh source review; never lower the basket simply to balance the budget.

**PA07 — Classify opening wealth and new purchases.** For each asset state
owner/title/security, location, vintage, remaining service, gross/net basis,
accepted capacity and included predecessor component OR paid new formation/
acquisition, with paired finance. Do not count both. Land is separately paid and
nondepreciating; CFC, renewal cash, principal and actual replacement differ.
Public purchase of an existing asset changes ownership, not production by itself.

The household92.5mCU device/appliance reference stock is consumer durables,
not operator capital or proof of inherited assets. The108.33m operator replacement
quote and3.140m working inventory are different stocks. Existing usable stock
need not be bought again; absent stock needs actual money and delivery. Neither
new quotes nor repaired lives revalue unrelated old assets/debt automatically.

**PA08 — Keep the five-table account open and bounded.** Final goods identify
quality, recipient and timing; producers identify inputs/work/capacity; jobs
identify paid people/hours/continuing claims; assets identify ownership and paid
transactions; cash identifies due payer/receiver, purpose and source. Link unique
activities and replacement scopes before summing. Research gaps admit new goods
and social forms; the five tables are not a closed national permission taxonomy.

Replace only matched old inputs: the new bus recipe replaces its identified old
service/fleet/fuel/work scope, not all L10 housing/mobility duties. Its10GWh
private-site recipe provisionally replaces PS's10GWh placeholder, not an extra
10GWh demand. Reconcile bus-depot energy with the retained60GWh service-building
line before accepting a net change; equal numerical totals do not prove scope.
Remaining roads, routes, land, suppliers, insurance, private choices and job/
source matches block a full national completion claim. They do not grant a
technical office power to cancel dues, prohibit private enterprise or enact taxes.
