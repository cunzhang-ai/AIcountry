# Private retirement saving with an explicit tax and resource chain

3 November 2026 · Design 142 · [PF01–PF16](../../country/C06/57-private-retirement-tax-and-personal-capital.md)

Select ordinary post-source private saving, actual cash-choice remuneration for
new work-sponsored funding, proportional indexed DC disposal and nominal
capital-first recovery for a genuine nonmarketable periodic promise. Actual
income retains one PA tax function; no new private contribution relief, annual
asset levy, blanket return exemption, early-withdrawal penalty or tax-free
cross-form rollover. Public Y and I05 remain distinct sources/rights.

| Choice | Gain or purpose to investigate | Cost/disagreement retained |
|---|---|---|
| Selected post-source saving | Personal ownership and source clarity; no new deduction subsidy or actuarial tax table for cost allocation | More current remuneration/source burden, possible lower occupational saving, capital-first timing/distribution and record costs |
| EET contribution relief and later income taxation | Deferral may encourage saving/liquidity | Deduction incidence/caps/avoidance, deferred source and old after-tax basis still need coordinated law |
| TEE-style exempt private return/payout | Simple late receipts and known post-tax contribution | Foregone future tax/distribution; no inference that licensed returns are already exempt |
| Annual pension-yield tax | Earlier asset-return tax receipts | Valuation/locked liquidity, losses, prior/foreign tax and payout basis; no foreign rate or source imported |
| Pro-rata annuity exclusion | Spreads capital recovery across expected payments | Tax factors/estimation/correction, longevity/distribution and records; not automatically worse or defeated by this selection |

Capital-first does not mean every pension is exempt. With no proved cost, a
source-qualified all-pre-tax claim can be fully taxable; missing evidence is
not automatically zero cost. Redeemable/tradable units retain TA06's real-gain
index and proportionate recovery, not the annuity rule by a product label.
Prudential DB/annuity pricing still needs qualified actuarial evidence.
