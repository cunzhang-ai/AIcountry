# WL47–WL53. Give workers effective control over consequential automated management

*Design384 · Proposed ordinary labor law under [work contracts and collective power](../12-work-contracts-and-collective-power.md).*

**WL47 — Regulate the effect on work.** Apply this route before a
material new or changed use of an automated system that directs or
monitors people and materially shapes work allocation, hiring, pay,
workload, hours, discipline or access to work. Include nominal advice,
ranking, cumulative loss of offers and instructions through a
contractor or platform. Determine duty holders by actual control
under WL. Give the covered process and privacy protections to people
subject to materially dependent platform work or supplied labor
without deciding all other aspects of status. Do not use internal
designations to evade rights. Routine clerical help or voluntary
tools that do not materially control work keep their ordinary
legal duties. Apply the same tests to rule-based and AI tools.

**WL48 — Give affected workers a paid decision process.** Before live
reliance, the employer identifies the affected work, actual
controller, purpose, exact versions and proposed uses. Give
independent worker representatives and affected workers timely
accessible notice, paid preparation, necessary original evidence,
a confidential route and qualified advice. Compare the existing
competent route, less intrusive improvement, worker proposals and
the proposed system on actual work, earnings, time, safety and
quality. Include overlooked shifts, recruits and agency and
platform workers through lawful representation or safe direct
input as applicable. Where no representative has authority, provide
a protected worker-chosen route under WL without employer selection.
Use proportionate procedures for small workplaces. No worker must
disclose unrelated personal data to participate.

**WL49 — Require agreement or an independent narrow decision.** For
covered consequential use, the employer needs a written lawful
agreement for the exact scope with competent independent worker
representation, or prior approval by the competent independent
labor forum under an expressly adopted route. The latter requires
a real paid opportunity to negotiate and a reasoned finding of a
material legitimate need, usable benefit or protection, and absence
of an equally effective less burdensome feasible route. The forum
tests actual coverage, necessity, privacy, paid staffing, human
authority, next-cycle continuity and enforceable correction.
It may allow only a defined scope, purpose, term and safeguards.
An agreement or decision cannot waive rights or supply individual
assent or contractual change required by WL07. A deadline, deadlock
or absent representative grants no deployment permission. Use
existing lawful work, a nonbinding safe test or separate strictly
necessary safety action on its own authority.

**WL50 — Put boundaries on evidence and adverse use.** Retain WL10
and PD limits, including purpose restriction and the prohibitions on
whole-life tracking, mandatory intimate recording and political-belief
scoring. Do not use lawful collective activity or off-duty private
communications as a management input. Collect only necessary
task-related data, with defined access, expiry and correction.
Before a material adverse act, a competent human with authority
examines actual relevant facts and the person's account through a
route independent of the disputed inference. Require usable reasons,
protected evidence and timely reply, even if the result is described
as the withdrawal of assignments or access to an account. Where the
basis cannot be independently verified, hold the unsupported act.
Urgent lawful safety measures retain their own grounds and review.
A second interface showing the same score supplies no independent proof.

**WL51 — Give correction a route to the next payroll or shift.**
Workers and representatives can directly reach existing inspector,
privacy and ordinary labor review within their scopes. Preserve
undisputed wages and existing guaranteed hours. An applicable
independent order can stop a use, restore lawful access or shifts,
correct the source and give payable relief under ordinary law.
Verify delivery at payroll, the roster or the competent work receiver
before closing the case. Keep the actual deadline and accrued loss.
On a shared error preserve protected evidence, stop affected reliance,
identify other affected decisions and correct them without reusing the
faulty inference. Disclose unresolved receiving without retaliation.
Employer and provider responsibilities follow control and existing law.

**WL52 — Test actual gains and share them through lawful decisions.**
Use a reversible, paid test before wider reliance. Reconcile real
worker and service-user time, qualified review, errors, unpaid work,
provider expense, actual wages and independent delivery against an
ordinary alternative. Do not count work transferred to a worker,
resident or family as productivity. Negotiate allocation of any
verified gains under AT, including shorter paid work, wages, training
or improved service. Public spending and taxation retain elected
authority. The deployment agreement alone creates no new entitlement
to a gain or permission to reduce guaranteed pay. Preserve funding and
ordinary staffing for rollback and its next service window.

**WL53 — Reopen on materially changed reliance.** Specify worker and
employer roles, independent review, rights through expiry, safe
evidence and actual fallback before adoption. A materially changed
use, data source, controller or affected scope requires fresh
authorization and qualified comparison. Use prospective migration
with existing terms, accrued rights and review intact. AI may check
rosters, compare plans and find conflicts. It cannot give worker
consent, grant deployment permission, make an adverse finding or
verify delivery on its own. Reopen on coercion, hidden unpaid work,
exclusion, unreliable reasons or a less burdensome effective method.
