# LF01–LF04: an actual task and a second, stricter force test

*Design 97 research selection; no enacted power or proven readiness.*

**LF01 — Scope and lawful actor.** Apply this ceiling to any domestic public enforcement or
protective use of physical force against a person by police, border/custody staff, B18 statutory
civil executors and exceptionally lawful civilian-task defense staff. A contractor or assistant
is bound by this ceiling when actually participating but acquires no sovereign arrest,
detention, seizure, protest-control or potentially lethal authority through procurement; the
named public officer retains accountable command within actual control. Clinical intervention
and UA/C08/09 consent, emergency care and restraint have their own grounds; this chapter creates
none. P04's private defense of self/others is distinct and cannot become a private enforcement
franchise. In armed hostilities C10/G's applicable law remains separate; a military uniform does
not relax this ceiling in a civilian law-enforcement role.

**LF02 — Two legal tests, not force by order.** First identify an enacted duty, valid individual
arrest or court-authorized civil/protective act, competent actor and exact person/place/time.
Prior enacted law must separately authorize physical enforcement for that actor, task and mode;
a duty, warrant or order alone supplies none. LF restricts that existing authority and grants no
means. Second show, at the moment force is used, concrete necessity to protect a person from
identified physical harm or execute that already lawful act when safe workable nonforce means
are insufficient. Give an intelligible request/warning and chance for safe compliance when
feasible; de-escalation, distance, time, communication, interpreters, accessible support,
shielding, voluntary care and unarmed responders may be safer than contact. A mere score,
diagnosis, debt, administrative convenience, political view, refusal of an optional service or
commander's preference is no force ground. The officer may decline or stop an unsafe execution
and refer the underlying owed claim to its lawful receiver; stopping does not erase the claim or
authorize a new coercive order.

**LF03 — Least harmful effective act, continually reassessed.** Limit contact, restraint, device
use and duration to the least injurious feasible means for the actual lawful objective,
considering the person's and bystanders' risk. The force ends as soon as the immediate need
ends; no general tactical order renews a person's restraint. Record the original moment a person
is not free to leave under P09 or the applicable civil/clinical law, even if called a rescue,
escort or transport. Do not use force as punishment, retaliation, extraction of information,
humiliation or a shortcut around missing staff. Methods designed to obstruct breathing, torture
and cruel or degrading treatment are prohibited. Expected injury, person-specific vulnerability
and safe alternatives matter; "less lethal" is not harmless. An emergency, property loss,
administrative deadline or a purportedly lawful arrest does not lower LF04's life threshold.

**LF04 — Potentially lethal and intentionally lethal.** Potentially lethal force includes
firearm discharge toward a person and any method that creates a substantial foreseeable risk of
death or serious injury in the actual circumstances. It is permitted only when strictly
necessary to protect a person from an imminent threat of death or serious injury and less
harmful effective means are not available. Force applied with the purpose of killing, or knowing
death is practically certain under P02, is permitted only when strictly unavoidable to protect
life from an imminent threat; the serious-injury limb alone does not authorize intentional
killing. Direct it only against the actual threatening actor, with warning if safely feasible
and precautions for others. Escape, past offense gravity, resistance, property, a crowd label or
failure to obey an order alone cannot satisfy either threshold. A commander, minister, model or
equipment preset cannot supply the missing immediate facts or issue an automatic
person-targeting decision. This is a normative domestic choice, not a claim that every external
treaty states these exact words.
