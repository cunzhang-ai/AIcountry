# C09. Ecological loss and verified replacement

*Design320 · Ecological protection and development permission · Research selection.*

**ER01 — Specify the protected function before applications.** The competent
elected legislature establishes reviewable ecological floors, their spatial
scope and relevant seasons or events. Separate, for example, flood attenuation
at exposed homes from native habitat connectivity. The steward keeps the
lawful baseline, cumulative committed losses, protected margins and
uncertainty with original observations. Deliberate or unauthorized damage
does not reset the baseline. Classify unique or functionally irreplaceable
features by the applicable place and recovery window. Give reasons, an
accessible challenge route and safe access to source evidence, including for
affected upstream and downstream communities. Protect sensitive locations
and testimony without concealing the basis of the finding from independent review.

**ER02 — Keep the floor through every stage.** Apply avoidance, footprint and
timing changes, and on-site repair before considering substitution for residual
loss. Where protection depends on a substitute, release the damaging stage
only after independent verification that the new capacity is additional,
legally secured, functioning in the affected system and available for the
applicable exposure and duration. Prove each function with the applicable
measurement and uncertainty rule. A forecast, a restoration contract and an
unrelated habitat improvement cannot be entered as received capacity. Deduct
prior allocations and keep common dependencies visible. Irreplaceable losses
carry zero replacement credit. A floor still governs where no credible
substitute exists. Separately disclose lawful residual loss above floors.

**ER03 — Decide the service and site together.** The competent elected authority
sets the plan and pays for lawful service alternatives. Apply binding
constraints in ordinary permissions through the designated officer with
reviewable reasons. Compare smaller or rerouted construction, another site,
delayed construction with funded interim provision, and retaining the asset
with a different service route. Report quantities, recipients, timing,
uncertainty and opportunity costs separately. Keep accrued shelter,
access or safety obligations with the responsible authority and its budget.
When lawful options fail to meet all needs, disclose who waits, preserve
independently reviewable claims and fund the available interim protection.
Use C09's prospective law and rights-review routes for changes to floors.
The prior-law emergency power remains limited to immediate protection.

**ER04 — Secure performance and enforce the limit.** Publish the phased
permission, baseline and release test before any irreversible work.
Secure duration, land access, maintenance, measurement and a lawful accepting
successor. Price a collectible assurance and funded contingency separately
from physical replacement. Protect access to timely review and interim relief.
A breached margin, failed replacement or material revision triggers the
published notice, protection and hold before further damage; the responsible
authority executes and verifies the remedy and returns any new choice to
the competent decision-maker. Where lost capacity cannot be recovered in
time, record the breach and deliver legally available direct protection and
redress. Relief and continuing restoration duties survive operator failure.

**ER05 — Keep evidence correctable across revisions.** Register functions and
allocations with source, dates, status and affected scope; retain historical
values and independent objections. Share the basis across linked permits
under applicable data protections. Recheck downstream consumers and
affected people before relying on revised methods or evidence. Keep permits,
accrued claims and legal effect under their governing instruments. Introduce
this method prospectively with funded assessor, challenge and successor
capacity. AI can assist comparison or flag overlap; authorized accountable
people establish findings and decide. Revisit the choice if independently
measured protection is lost, an alternative delivers equivalent protection
with less exclusion, or the release rule blocks essential supply without a
practicable lawful route.

The [matched case](../../../settlement/ecological-loss/01-wetland-and-housing.md)
illustrates operation. Actual floors, field verification, lawful approvals,
costs and delivered protection require jurisdiction-specific evidence.
