## Diagnoses and coherent alternative models

A right can fail because the duty is vague, a private signature hides coercion,
an agent/registry invents authority, a powerful party controls evidence, a
plaintiff cannot afford the route or a judgment lacks a responsible payer.
More navigation fixes none of those substantive gaps by itself. More statutes
also do not create counsel, cash, assets, safe housing or trusted source records.

Compare retaining/funding modular manuscripts, contractual/private-forum
resolution, one uniform centralized code/regulator and the common enforceable
floor with local supplementation. Retain/repair avoids disruption where
existing rules already work. Private forums may be quick and appropriate for
informed equal parties but can bind dependent people to a patron's rules.
Uniform centralization may ease portability but concentrate framing, appointments
and enforcement. Select the common floor with voluntary advised settlement,
independent court review and explicit delivery responsibility. The competing
models/no change remain live; no measured welfare ranking is available.

## Substantive choices and their costs

B01–B04 retain equal capacity and distinguish authentication, operative meaning,
assent, actual/apparent authority and receipt. Private apparent authority needs
the principal's own representation and reasonable actual reliance; public
powers cannot arise through it. Replicas share one scope/resource ceiling.
Routine valid delegation needs no per-transaction human click. Revocation/
expiry and offline reservation need reachable notice and source evidence;
last-arriving synchronization cannot assign the loss. Preserve honest commerce
without making vulnerable principals insure all vendor/agent errors. Ordinary
contract terms cannot waive nonwaivable rights or force dependent users into
exclusive pre-dispute private justice.

B05–B08 retain good-faith performance, proportionate repair, reasonable mitigation,
property distinctions and actual restitution. No compulsory personal labor or
fictitious receipt of unavailable resources closes a gap. Registry correction
hears holders/third parties; a good-faith buyer's loss finance remains a distinct
unverified obligation. State acquisition cannot be a database correction.

B09–B12 add civil product-defect responsibility without proving producer/importer
knowledge, employer/state responsibility for entrusted acts/nondelegable duties
and full repair for proved indivisible causal harm with contribution. These
are normative risk allocations, not automatic guilt for job titles, every bad
outcome or corporate affiliation. Courts assess cause/defenses and prevent
duplicate compensatory recovery. Clinical/service care, lawful public-policy
tradeoffs, state compensation and criminal proof remain different questions.
Liability alone does not establish insurer, defendant/state assets or paid loss.

B13–B16 provide first-receipt jurisdiction, proportionate aid, accessible civil
proof and respondent-held records, preserving privilege/privacy and no automatic
criminal inference. Retain candidate discovery-based three-year civil limitation
with actual-access tolling and the source's ordinary administrative 5/20/30+30/
60-working-day schedules. Those clocks are uncalibrated; urgent liberty/clinical/
due-payment duties prevail. Disability does not remove capacity or itself
resolve tolling. Special limitation/conflict schedules and actual forum capacity
remain unfinished. No agency can manufacture incomplete receipt indefinitely.

B17–B20 explicitly propose broader civil 24-hour urgent consideration and
72-hour ex parte hearing opportunity, at most one further 72-hour necessity
extension, alongside private/state execution and controlled collective claims.
E13–E15's earlier review is **not transferred** to broader scope. Prior adopted
source law must provide a bounded continuing payment/record substitute; it
cannot choose policy or create tax/staff. A debtor's inability is not detention;
seizure/recovery commissions are barred. Opt-in is the representative baseline;
a narrow statutory opt-out route requires actual usable notice/exit for uniform
money claims and cannot silently waive individualized rights. Trace distributions,
not just settlements. Insolvency/priority/exemptions and actual loss finance
remain necessary, not supplied by the title of an enforcement office.

## Evidence, current comparators and objections

The September manuscript and current topics are one authored project family,
not independent observations. Twelve [journeys](../settlement/civil-core/README.md)
pose incomplete facts and the necessary independent route; they do not compute
verdicts or show actual capacity. [Checks](validation.json) establish exact
predecessor preservation, current dependency identity and organized clauses/
cases only. A new manifest or named role cannot establish lawful adoption,
independent professional review or delivered protection.

The official [UNCITRAL automated-contracting overview](https://uncitral.un.org/en/mlac)
was checked through indexed material on 14 October; it identifies electronic
contract recognition, output attribution and unexpected outcomes. Direct page
retrieval failed and the guide PDF returned 403; full article inspection or
adoption of that model law is not claimed. Our private/public authority and
consumer rules are explicit design choices. The [OECD 2025 toolkit](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/toolkit-for-access-to-justice-and-people-centred-justice-systems_aecf7f78-en/full-report/the-oecd-recommendation-and-implementation-toolkit-on-access-to-justice-and-people-centred-justice-systems_c7aba4f8.html)
was retrieved; it explains the 2023 Recommendation and a broader continuum of
services, institutions, empowerment and evaluation. It supports the methodological
comparison, not our liability, deadlines, costs or superiority. No original local
case, rights-holder participation or practical execution evidence is available.

Material objections: strict defect/institutional responsibility can price out
small suppliers or hide individual accountability; cause/control defenses and
contribution remain, while costs/behavior are unknown. Apparent authority can
harm disconnected principals; actual representation/notice is required and no
credential-only binding is allowed. Strong procedural rights can add delay;
proportional routes and actual safety deadlines prevail, without claiming staff
exists. A uniform floor can suppress local cooperation; express local competence,
voluntary settlement and different media remain available. Collective settlements
can enrich sponsors or erase dissent; notice, narrow scope, judicial review and
actual distribution are required. These are authored objections, not public assent.
