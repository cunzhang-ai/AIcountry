# Design260 decision record

**Decision and baseline.** The Design259 selection uses nine CJ06 people
for constitutional and electoral legal-source appointments and uses those
same nine alongside three ordinary-only judges for ordinary judicial
selection. The present proposal replaces that shared ordinary formation
through competent prospective law. The [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md)
and [handoff method](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md) guide this research.

**What can fail.** A single political dispute, conflict cluster, interruption
of legitimate selection or compromised controller can remove several
appointment routes at once. A separate cause is insufficient court
workers, safe records or resources for ongoing cases. More appointers
cannot cure a missing independent operating path. A valid disagreement
on a candidate is part of the appointment system. Suppression of an
eligible nomination or refusal to perform a required procedure belongs
in a separate review channel. [Historical evidence](sources.md)

**Alternatives and choice.** Retaining the shared formation uses fewer
qualified selectors and can share ordinary administration at lower
cost. It leaves the demonstrated overlap in appointing capacity. Select
separate ordinary selection, with distinct people and paid receiving
and without changing the 12-seat/8-vote rule or category approvals.
A full split also separates the constitutional and electoral legal sources.
It has additional principal and alternate obligations, and is a live
rival if the residual CJ06 coupling remains material and local capacity
supports it. An executive or simple-majority takeover after a deadline
would allow a bloc to profit from waiting. Reject that takeover.
[Matched authored comparison](../settlement/appointment-independence/02-authored-appointment-comparison.md)

**Tradeoffs and objections.** The selected split needs nine more principal
slots and can require nine more alternates than the shared design.
Separate appointments could still depend on the same patron, professional
association, registration platform or payer. Inspect dependence and
the actual ability to convene, make payments and review in time. Lay
roles require paid release and accessibility. The cost includes displaced
court time, independent representation and executing remedies. Existing
visiting judges may cover lawful hearing work if independently qualified,
paid and available and if their departure leaves a usable route at the
origin. Prospective votes and case remedies keep their own legal owners.
No affected-party participation or independent local qualification has
occurred in this revision.

**Authority and migration.** Preserve CJ and EA denominators, exact
terms, source-specific jurisdictions, prior court appointments, record
custody, paid claims and all original hearing and remedy dates. An
express enactment establishes a fixed prospective boundary after credible
qualification. Source owners preserve pending selection and challenges
under actual law and document exact custody transfers. Require fresh
reviews, votes and notices where the lawful process changes. Normal
judicial status and election review continue. AI may reconcile dates and
flag dependencies. It cannot redraw candidates or transfer a vote.

**Next decision.** Compare the shared and two separate variants over
one appointment calendar with a constitutional appointment, an EA
appointment and a set of real ordinary vacancies. A joint registrar and
independent parliamentary cost route should supply applicable rolls,
independent custody, exact notice windows, payment and protected
casework displaced or covered. Continue the same budget and useful-time
court checks even when a formation cannot decide. Agree actual funding
and affected-party participation before a live trial. Reopen on repeated
CJ/EA coupling, common route failure, unaffordable lost hearing time or
stronger comparison evidence.

**Status.** The historical evidence and authored bounds are scoped.
The split is the current research selection. Enactment, funded
qualification, independent affected-party review and observed improvements
remain open.
