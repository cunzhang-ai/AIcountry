# Accountable automation and genuinely separate assessment

*TS05–TS06; part of [technical safety](../22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md).*

**TS05 — Approve an actual operating domain and responsible controller.** For a
scheduled consequential automated function, identify the deployer, manufacturer,
maintainer and actual operational controller, with respective duties under B/V.
Disclose tasks, places, people, environmental limits, expected intervention,
communications, safe fallback and dependencies. An AI cannot hold a sovereign
mandate or absorb an owner's legal responsibility. A passenger, patient or
worker is not the fallback operator merely because a disclaimer names them.

Human supervision is required only where actually effective and lawful; do not
pretend one remote worker can rescue simultaneous failures across an arbitrary
fleet. Prove observation, understanding, intervention time, workload, access and
safe failure in the proposed use. An independently evidenced autonomous minimum-
risk action may be safer than an impossible demand for instant human takeover.
This does not relax C10's particular-human-target decision or other rights laws.

New software, agents, communication media and distributed teams may satisfy a
functional duty through proven equivalent means. A high benchmark, model lineage,
several copied agents or a safe simulation is not actual domain qualification.
Retain deployable boundaries, source evidence and a usable stop/restriction route;
no self-authorized domain expansion from unsupervised learning.

**TS06 — A paid certificate is not independent judgment.** The competent public
sector decision-maker can rely on qualified public, regional or accredited
assessment within published scope, with original evidence and reasons. For
required independent conformity review, assign from a competent conflict-screened
pool by disclosed neutral rules; fees follow the lawful public schedule rather
than a developer selecting and rewarding its own final reviewer. Actual capacity
and cost of this choice must be counted, not assumed.

Disclose common controllers, finance, test datasets, laboratories, vendors and
subcontractors. Independent means material freedom to inspect and disagree,
including contrary evidence, not a different model/account/corporate name.
A qualified replacement outside the conflict uses predesignated rules and
actual access; the minister cannot choose a friendly reviewer for a named case.

Recognize genuinely relevant external evidence or joint assessments where law
permits, checking the actual product/configuration, purpose, population, methods,
local power/supply, source access and subsequent adverse events. Recognition is
not automatic foreign-law import or a presumption that an export-only certificate
proved domestic safety. Final statutory decision and accessible remedy remain
with a competent accountable body; no duplicate test merely to protect a local
industry, and no new human/animal experiment without its separate lawful basis.

[DP](../../C11/09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md) now supplies the
public shared-support/sector operating handoff. TS05 qualified minimum-risk
autonomy remains possible; no universal human instant takeover or manual copy.
Actual sector authority, independent required assessment and physical receiving
still apply. Director's warning or supplier fix is no operating qualification,
new permit, national shutdown order or legal adverse decision.
