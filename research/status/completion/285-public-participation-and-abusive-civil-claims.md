# Design377. Preserve civic participation through early civil review

[APS01–APS07](../../country/C08/13/08-public-participation-and-abusive-civil-claims.md)
adds an independently executed early route for civil claims arising
from public participation. It narrows unsupported demands while
preserving supported privacy, reputation and other civil claims.

[Two observed decisions and a worked comparison](../../settlement/public-participation-and-abusive-civil-claims/01-two-claims-before-a-public-hearing.md)
test differentiated disposition, funded defense and arrival before
the opportunity. Actual staffing, independent review, authority,
participation and effects remain open.
