# One contest, a freeze and a credible threat

*Authored counterfactual. All events, evidence, staffing and times
are assumptions. The outcome in a real case would depend on facts,
law, counsel and actual independent decisions.*

## One calendar and independent routes

Hour 0 is the beginning of V minus 22 days, where V is the
first lawful casting of a ballot under ER01. The list has
qualified under the existing procedure. The planned vote
and earlier nomination rules are fixed. The venue for an
already authorized campaign event needs lawful payment and
access by hour 12. The event begins at hour 14. The relevant
ballot print lock is hour 24. First voting remains at hour
528. The proposed urgent review clock is independent of
those milestones.

## A genuine investigation and an overbroad planned restriction

Officials have admissible grounds to investigate a specific
possible donation offence. The planned seizure of the whole
campaign record system and a proposed freeze of all campaign
payments would together shut down the event. Assume narrow
protected copies can preserve the needed original evidence
and any legally questionable payment can be isolated without
disabling the lawful venue payment. At hour 1 the campaign's
representative files a credible urgent claim. The interim
decision is due by hour 7, with any earlier useful-time need
prevailing. The blanket planned measure requires prior review.
The ordinary inquiry continues.

Assume the ordinary court rules at hour 5, bars the blanket
restriction and preserves lawful evidence and payment duties.
An independently credentialed executor and payer make the
venue payment and authorized access usable by hour 10. The
event can proceed. If the same order is instead executed at
hour 15, the protected event is lost, even though the judge
ruled in time and the ballot file is still printable at
hour 24. Record the uncorrected harm and any actually
available substitute opportunity. The later decision on
a challenge to the result still needs ER16 proof.

If an independent bench is unavailable, the proposed blanket
measure cannot begin by default. Existing lawful evidence
preservation remains available. Report the missing reviewer,
preserve the filing and use actual lawful substitutes. No
vote date or eligibility changes by a missed remedy clock.

## A different factual branch with imminent harm

Assume instead that independently available evidence shows
a concrete, serious and imminent threat to a witness, and
that a summons or narrower separation would not work.
An ordinary P09 arrest at hour 4 can proceed if all of its
own legal requirements hold. A judge hearing the matter at
hour 8 can authorize only individually necessary protection
within ordinary law, after defense and separate consideration
of narrower means. The 24-hour outer P09 deadline would be
hour 28, after the print lock. That outer limit is not a
reason to wait, to hold after grounds end, or to defer the
separate useful-time election remedy. In the assumed branch
there is no independent finding of electoral ineligibility.
The ballot officer preserves the valid candidacy and ordinary
support while the court tests any safe lawful opportunity
that can coexist with the witness protection.

If the governing side suppresses a comparable case against
its own ally, the victim and witnesses retain ordinary help
and a direct unconflicted review. The reviewer can preserve
evidence and require lawful independent consideration. The
court cannot order a charge or give an incumbent a selective
investigative holiday. A candidate's or officeholder's status
also supplies no shield against a substantiated case.
