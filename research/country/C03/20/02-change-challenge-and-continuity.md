# GP05–GP08. Change, challenge and continuing authority

**GP05 — Decide the compatible set through actual votes.** Put the first
programme before the existing Assembly scrutiny route and reconcile its
financial measures to the first ordinary budget or earlier competent vote.
Invite concrete member alternatives, ordinary public scrutiny and material
minority objections under DB and C03. An investment, tax, new entitlement or
coercive rule still requires its own applicable lawful authorization. A
minority Cabinet may gain different support on different measures. A failed
measure or budget has the existing consequences. It does not count as a
confidence vote. Agreements cannot bind a future legislature, replace an
individual member's vote or shift court, local or professional competence.

**GP06 — Explain the change before it changes people's lives.** When seeking
a material change, publish a brief reconciliation with the last programme
and the controlling law. Distinguish a new policy bargain from a changed
forecast, court decision, resource loss or newly discovered defect. State
affected people, duties and costs, who bears delay, proposed interim
receiving, source and real alternatives. Supply it at the relevant existing
vote or before material lawful executive action where useful time permits.
For urgently permitted acts give the account as soon as the existing
notice rules permit. Append reasons and dissent. If a promise was abandoned
or a proposed stage never authorized, mark that explicitly. A programme
change leaves binding contracts, benefits and other existing obligations
on their own lawful tracks.

**GP07 — Make procedural remedies work against the government.** Keep paid
independent analyst access, equal public release, member alternative and
ordinary outside complaint routes. The existing Assembly, oversight and
courts can address missing records, unequal access and unlawful expenditure
under their competences. No official can decide whether a political promise
is wise or enforce it as a statutory benefit without its separate legal
ground. Do not treat a missing report as new supply, an election annulment,
a default appointment or a power to withhold a due payment. Preserve
original versions and sources through hostile handover and protected
confidential retention. Public reasons and private campaign work have
separate custodial and access rules. AI can reconcile versions or estimate
consequences, with disclosed sources and accountable human checks. It
cannot confer authority, rank contestants for access or publish a private
draft without permission.

**GP08 — Adopt prospectively and compare actual use.** Use existing analyst,
committee, audit and review roles and funded administration. An adopting
law sets the 30-day reporting requirement, request limits and independent
backup, explicitly preserving ordinary first budget, emergency, caretaker
and formation rules. Preserve live requests safely, exact budgets, old
rights, continuing employment, claims, records and lawful succession.
Measure useful estimates available to smaller contestants, leaked material,
unresolved and duplicated dependencies, actual member alternatives, the
public's ability to trace changes and actual cost and delivery. Compare with
a simpler public baseline and ordinary parliamentary scrutiny. Reopen on
systematic gatekeeping, leaks, repeated missed legislative duties or a
less burdensome arrangement that lets people exercise equivalent control.

[BCV competing budgets](../../C06/60/05-competing-budgets-and-floor-choice.md)
gives qualified alternatives a protected path to an exact final vote.
A published governing agreement conveys no priority or inferred vote.
Separate formation, source and appropriation decisions retain their scope.

[BCV06–BCV10](../../C06/60/06-minority-access-and-floor-recovery.md)
lets elected members separately give priority to an exact budget that
missed the ordinary gate. A governing agreement or reserved priority
conveys no budget assent, source or change of office.
