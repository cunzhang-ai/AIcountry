wages, support and tax/contribution assumptions. Amounts, child factors,
asset buffers and rates are not empirical calibration or real fiscal authority.
The three arrangements all have a financing gap in this partial launch ledger.
Do not select a cheaper basket merely because its arithmetic gap is smaller.

Individual access is selected conditionally for agency and safe control;
the illustrated amounts are held from an operational claim. Income is not
a replacement for paid care, housing supply or political/rights remedies.
Actual cost and received protection in the [national inventory](../commitments/README.md)
remain unknown. This package specifies a previously missing material
settlement; it does not complete ordinary law or establish better lives.
