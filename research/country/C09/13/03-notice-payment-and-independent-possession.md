# LA11–LA15. Notice, funded payment and independent possession

*Design145 · [Public acquisition](../13-public-acquisition-and-secure-relocation.md) · Candidate prospective procedure; earlier/stronger rights survive.*

**LA11 — A notice must leave a real choice.** Before the elected taking decision,
serve accessible individual notice on known affected holders/users and public safe
notice with the exact proposed purpose/map/right, alternatives, source/relocation
plan, valuation method and independent-help/challenge routes. Allow at least
**60 complete calendar days** before that decision, including at least30calendar
days for supported objections while location, scale and terms can change.
The first usable-notice date is day0; days1–60 are complete, so decision is no
earlier than the start of day61 in the prepublished governing civil calendar.
Actual stronger notice/service rules and real meaningful participation still apply. Record
material reasons/dissent and safe testimony rather than publish a personal ledger.
A materially enlarged right/map or different purpose needs fresh affected notice;
correction cannot secretly transfer old objections/reviews as assent to new terms.
No complete dossier, agent account, title credential or group representative is
required to report a problem. Direct urgent rights relief remains available.

**LA12 — No indefinite reservation by notice.** The first related public acquisition
notice expires after12calendar months unless the lawful decision and necessary
funded possession process complete within it. Before expiry, the same competent
elected body may grant **one extension of at most12calendar months**, with refreshed
scope/need/source, affected hearing, reasons and actual vote. A renamed stage or
contractor cannot reset that notice history. Expiry gives no title or consent;
accrued loss, support and ordinary property/permit rights continue. A genuinely
new proposal needs a new lawful process and must disclose cumulative prior blight.

Notice alone freezes no lawful sale, ordinary use, repairs, tenancy or appeal.
Where a project-specific reservation actually eliminates reasonable lawful use
for12calendar months, the affected holder can require an ordinary court to order
its release or a lawfully funded acquisition/disposition within90calendar days,
subject to earlier useful relief. It cannot order a new tax or fabricate purchase
cash; if lawful acquisition/source fails, that project-specific restriction ends.
General justified environmental/planning law and other actual independent grounds
are not erased or automatically compensated by this remedy. No promise protects
speculative price or a proposed unlawful use.

**LA13 — Make money and replacement genuinely available.** Before possession,
fund and pay actual undisputed compensation and necessary relocation/support,
name lawful Treasury/local/project payer, actual source, accepted providers and
usable dates. Appropriation, loan approval, a model reserve or inaccessible escrow
is not the person's receipt. The project budget covers full actual replacement/
transition and plausible disputed exposure; the general housing floor is not a
free construction subsidy or a substitute for the project's own liability.

For absent owners, competing title or a genuine amount dispute, an ordinary court
may authorize a separately safeguarded **actually funded** deposit only after real
notice/help/settlement attempts and independent valuation/adequacy examination.
Release undisputed sums without waiver, preserve real actual source for shortfall,
separate each contested interest and pay court-held actual returns to its lawful
beneficiary. No requirement to accept a risky project bond or state-IOU instead.
Deposit is neither tax revenue, usable personal income nor a determination of
title/consent. A blocked deposit secures principal but does not automatically
stop LA10 delay protection; credit actual trust returns once toward that duty,
with the public payer owing any lawful remainder. It never replaces required collective consent, safe receiving or
protection against irreversible nonmonetary harm.

**LA14 — Independent authorization before nonconsensual possession.** The acquiring
body must obtain an ordinary court order, after meaningful affected hearing, that
actual elected/statutory competence, lawful purpose/necessity, exact parcel/right,
notice, protected rights, compensation/deposit and safe replacement conditions
are established. No new land court or ministry appellate board. Courts review
legality/rights and determine valuation disputes, not a preferred road or public
welfare score. Compensation disagreement alone is no unconditional owner veto
where lawful source, adequate deposit, actual protection and the court's findings
are present. Affected people can seek B17 useful-time interim relief; physical
removal cannot precede that real opportunity, be fixed by an unreviewed timer or
count a family's agent silence as consent. Appeal uses existing courts and actual
stay/urgency rules; no automatic suspension of every unrelated public service.
Existing B20 lawful joined/representative proceedings may share records/work,
with actual mandates, affected hearing and rights-specific findings preserved.
No per-person technical dossier or fictional duplicated court capacity is required.

**LA15 — Possession does not grant force or extinguish other claims.** The precise
order names actual receiving officer/executor, date, rights acquired, continuing
rights, payer, records and safe housing/access. Only authorized civil execution
under B18/LF can act, with independent evidence and actual clinical/support needs
protected; a project contract gives no police/weapon/detention power. Prevent
homelessness and avoidable interruption of life-sustaining services. A child,
nonconsenting adult, owner or worker retains their own rights; household-leader
consent is not theirs. Wrongful taking/violence has actual separate restoration/
compensation/accountability grounds, not automatic guilt or immunity.
