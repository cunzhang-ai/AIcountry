# EV17–EV20. Trace the use and review the decision

**EV17 — Assign the duty to the body that used the evidence.** A public body
using a published or shared administrative observation, model result or material
AI transformation as an input to a consequential decision must be able to
identify its controlling legal rule, evidence and transformation versions, time
period and official responsible for follow-through. Scope includes a plausible
change in rights, payments, essential provision, formal restrictions or the
affordability of a material public commitment. Assess related errors together
when their cumulative effect can change a decision or burden an affected group.
A batch or programme record can
serve multiple decisions where it preserves the ability to find affected cases.
Preserve meaningful differences in units, boundaries, populations and dates
across uses, including when a provider changes identifiers or ownership.
The same duty follows a delegated calculation. Record a recoverable
description and lawful access to relevant supporting inputs. Routine background
reading and private use create no general logging or identity-linking duty.

**EV18 — Give a usable warning before the next consequential use.** The source
or transformation custodian issues a warning upon credible evidence of a
material defect and updates it when facts are confirmed. The affected scope,
vintage, uncertainty, next update and known dependent offices travel with the
warning. Distinguish an error under the original method, routine new information,
a method or definition change and genuinely changed conditions. Preserve the
original release and a separately labelled correction or estimate. Use secure
channels for restricted material and notify the public and opposition on equal
terms about public impacts. A custodian can mark its own output unreliable and
apply its existing publication powers. It cannot rewrite another body's legal
decision. Source failure leaves the receiving office's discovery duty intact.

**EV19 — Acknowledge, bound and decide.** Each decision owner acts on the
earliest credible discovery in its office or authenticated material warning it
receives. Promptly protect imminent payments, essential service, evidence and
access to review using existing legal powers. Acknowledge and name the owner
within seven calendar days. Within 30 calendar days issue a reasoned disposition
or an independently reviewable bounded plan naming the unresolved dependency,
interim protection and next decision date. An earlier statutory deadline or
last safe time controls. Classify which uses are confirmed, reasonably likely
or unresolved. Compare effects under the legally controlling version and the
corrected observation, accounting for groups that may have been omitted.
A person can identify a likely affected decision without producing the source
file. Invite evidence of harm and reliance through ordinary accessible channels.
Where people can be identified safely, send direct notice in time to act. For
others use public notices, relevant intermediaries and accessible claim routes.
A missed acknowledgement can go directly to existing outside review.

**EV20 — Use the lawful route for each decision.** Recalculate where the law
requires corrected inputs, retain the governing vintage where law fixes it, and
use existing reconsideration powers for materially dependent discretionary
decisions. State reasons when effect is immaterial or existing protection is
adequate. For an authorized fallback specify scope and end. Where a new rule
or appropriation is needed, send the elected body the confirmed effects,
alternatives, proposed payer and uncovered obligations. For legislative facts
and public deliberation, publish a traceable correction for equal scrutiny.
Election challenges and judgment reopening follow their existing legal grounds,
jurisdiction and time limits. A warning or new model output supplies no
independent power to alter votes, final orders, contractual terms or offices.
