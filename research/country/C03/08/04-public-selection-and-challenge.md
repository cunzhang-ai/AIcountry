# Constitutional review: public selection and appointment challenge

*Canonical CJ07–CJ08; part of [C03/08](../08-constitutional-review-and-public-control.md).*

**CJ07 — Paid public source.** Three seats derive from separately drawn panels
of thirty-six adult electors, one panel per cohort's primary/reserve selection.
A usable vote needs at least twenty-seven present and twenty-four affirmative
votes of the fixed thirty-six. Absences do not shrink the denominator. Judges
are human persons; replicated agents and extra accounts have no additional votes.

An auditable random draw uses the lawful electoral roll, with proportionate
strata for residence, age and the country's lawful access/language arrangements.
Publish strata and replacement order before invitations; no compulsory cultural
identity or political-opinion test. Participation is voluntary, paid and supported
with care, transport, disability accommodation, interpretation and safe remote
or in-person access. Decliners are replaced by the next pre-drawn person in the
same stratum. Recheck resulting exclusion and nonresponse; calling a panel random
does not establish that people under coercion or without lawful status are heard.
The technical operator cannot privately change the roll or redraw disliked voters.

Panel members receive plural case material, source access, candidate hearings,
independent advice and time to question proponents. Disclose organized contacts,
sponsors and mediator selection. Neither an AI summary nor a facilitator chooses
the candidate pool. Appointment ballots are secret, with an authenticated count
and aggregate reasons; publish material objections without identifying private
votes. A member cannot assess or vote on their own candidacy or a direct personal
conflict; use the next eligible pre-drawn replacement, retaining the fixed
thirty-six denominator. Failed replacement is a selection failure. Primary and
reserve need separate approval; consent to one is not the other.

**CJ08 — Timetable, votes and challenge.** Open scheduled selection 180 days
before expiry; publish unresolved qualification/availability issues by day 90
and outstanding failure by day 30. Publish a proposed appointment and qualification
reasons at least twenty-one days before taking office. Unscheduled vacancies use
the same ordinary process and minimum notice; a reserve serves only under CJ11.
Sources may debate and repeat approval rounds on each candidate; every successful
candidate must independently reach that source's fixed threshold. If more than
one qualifies for one seat, a further single-choice round between them requires
the same threshold. Failure means an unfilled seat, not a plurality winner.
A public panel expires after 180 days from its first sitting; a fresh audited
panel may start under the unchanged procedure, with no transferred approvals.

Ordinary challenges run thirty days from usable publication, preserving later
concealed-fraud/material-conflict claims. CJ12's external chamber reviews criteria,
exclusion, denominators, conflicts and procedure, not preferred political views.
A challenge is not an automatic stay; only a reasoned judicial order addressing
material risk suspends entry. Preserve defense and third-party rights. Invalidity
does not automatically erase every prior judgment: the external chamber states
prospective correction and any necessary reopening for material bias/unlawfulness,
respecting criminal protections. Good-faith rejection, absent candidates or lack
of agreement is not misconduct. No timeout appoints anyone or extends a term.
