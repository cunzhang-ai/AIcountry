# Actual control and a closed set of grounds

*CD01–CD02; part of [C08/21](../21-conflict-detention-and-release.md).*

**CD01 — Protections attach to the person, not the site label.** This proposed
national statute binds this country's officials, forces and attributable
contractors wherever they actually control restraint, including transit,
ships, hospitals, joint sites and offshore custody. Identify actual effective
control and lawful ability to act; a foreign partner or delegated agent does
not remove our own acts/duties. This is the candidate's protective scope,
not an assertion that every treaty/jurisdiction question is globally settled.

Record first actual restraint when the person cannot leave, custodian, safe
location, actual grounds, injuries/needs, source/evidence and each handoff.
Use the earliest supported time where records conflict; lost/uncertain records
are not a new starting event. Preserve an independent civilian-register copy
outside implicated command, with confidential counsel/court access and safe
chosen-person/protective notification within 24 hours. Protect risky location/
family information with a specific independently reviewed safety measure,
not secret detention, a public doxxing register or an indefinite notice veto.

Humane treatment applies before qualification, regardless of nationality,
political affiliation, reciprocal conduct or legality of the initiating war.
Application of protection does not legalize unlawful external force; a cabinet,
Assembly vote or detention order supplies no new war/intervention power.
Military necessity is not a general suspension of C02/P bodily/liberty rights.

**CD02 — Identify the actual conflict and each liberty ground.** The statute
allows only CD03/04's limited POW status custody in an actual international
armed conflict (IAC), CD05's actual neutrality-obligation military custody,
or separately valid ordinary criminal/strict immediate-defense custody.
Publish a reasoned conflict/legal-basis account with protected necessary
annexes and direct independent challenge. Riots, peaceful opposition, a
terror label, border crossing or a worldwide campaign slogan is not IAC.
Do not merge different theaters/actors to keep one person's war indefinitely open.

No general involuntary civilian-security internment, preventive detention
for non-international armed conflict (NIAC), or automatic occupied-population/
former-service detention follows from this Act. IHL humane-treatment rules
and some permissive treaty grounds are not themselves this candidate's
unlimited national power. A future broader power requires a fresh explicit
rights-reviewed Act and legitimate choice; it cannot approve its own adoption.
Civilians with a real POW-protection entitlement do not lose it, nor does
that entitlement alone establish a necessary liberty restriction.

If our actual existing international duties conflict with a chosen narrower
method, identify the exact instrument/person/act and reconcile through lawful
legislation/consultation before an operation is called ready. Existing law
and protections are not erased by this research. No person falls outside
humane care because actual qualification or the proper legal basis is unresolved.
