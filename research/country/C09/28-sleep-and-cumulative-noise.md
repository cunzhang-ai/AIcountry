# C09. Sleep and cumulative environmental noise

*Design343 · NSE01–NSE06 · Proposed ambient-noise duties.*

**NSE01 — Protect sleep and ordinary use at the place of exposure.**
The Assembly establishes enforceable environmental noise standards by
place, use and time under ordinary law. It funds the common floor.
Qualified evidence informs separate measures for cumulative night
exposure, repeated loud events and day or evening exposure. Publish
what each metric measures, its interval, uncertainty and enforceable
limit. Use source-specific health evidence for its actual scope.
No single average settles every effect. Protect homes including rental
homes, care sites and existing quiet public places. Consider residents
whose sleep occurs at other hours when designing local measures.
Ordinary household activity, lawful assembly and expressive activity
retain their own rights and reciprocal duties. This creates no general
night curfew or power to police speech.

**NSE02 — Observe multiple sources and include people missed by the map.**
The local environmental service measures or estimates exposure where
people actually live and rest, with independent calibration and
recorded uncertainty. Distinguish road, rail, freight, construction and
industrial contributions and keep comparable measurements over time.
Combine like-period acoustic energy using the correct scale. Track
event peaks and timing separately. Publish source coverage, unmeasured
places and the range supported by the evidence. Accept accessible
reports from tenants, workers and caregivers. A report can start an
inquiry without a meter, landlord's permission or medical diagnosis.
Measure in a proportionate way without recording intelligible private
speech or routine individual movement. AI may compare routes and flag
gaps. Qualified staff verify material readings and decide within law.

**NSE03 — Check both places before approving a material change.**
The competent permitting or transport authority evaluates all material
contributions and repeated events from the proposal and existing
operations. Include neighboring receptors and affected routes.
Withhold ordinary permission for a new exceedance unless enforceable
source controls prevent it from first operation, with funded follow-up.
Where a change materially affects an already exceeded metric, require
its conditions to reduce that burden on a dated path without creating
or worsening another exceedance. Apply existing
environmental, planning, housing, emergency and appeal rules.
Pay for protected access to the evidence and a useful opportunity
to challenge. A voluntary retrofit or an offset in another area does
not enlarge the permitted external exposure at the affected homes.

**NSE04 — Assign a lead and fund the correction.** When a material
exceedance is verified, the local environmental service names a lead
within five working days. For a cross-boundary source or receptor, the
competent regional or national authority names the coordinating lead
within that period while local receiving duties continue. Publish a
costed correction plan within 20 working days from verification,
with source owners, exact measures, public funding where needed,
verification dates and the people still exposed. Earlier urgent
protections remain. Compare source reduction, timing and operating
changes, genuinely quieter routes and optional building measures.
Operators owe the measures attributable to them under actual law and
review. Elected bodies fund shared infrastructure and residual duties.
Existing individual nuisance, health and judicial remedies continue.

**NSE05 — Preserve essential service and safe living conditions.**
An unavoidable short exception for rescue, urgent repair or another
defined essential service uses its own lawful ground, exact affected
scope and expiry, a least-harm option and timely independent challenge.
Track its actual events. Routine activity cannot continue under serial
exception. Pay for a quieter alternative where feasible. Housing
mitigation requires a usable result in normal living conditions with
safe ventilation, temperature and accessibility. A home cannot be
counted protected by assuming residents will keep windows closed
where no safe alternative exists. Renters can reach help directly.
Preserve tenure, safe occupancy and existing housing duties through
works. Keep temporary protection, verified indoor improvement and
remaining outdoor exposure distinct.

**NSE06 — Verify the next ordinary period before declaring correction.**
Check the original and receiving areas across comparable periods and
representative events, with protected resident evidence. Publish safe
findings on exposure, peak events, remaining uncertainty, paid
mitigation and lost access or service. Existing independent review
can order evidence, timely lawful correction or relief within its
powers. Adopt prospectively with a costed monitoring service, named
payers and lawful migration for existing permits and claims. Reopen
on shifted harm, misleading averages, weak verification, repeated
exceptions or a simpler approach that secures equivalent protection.
