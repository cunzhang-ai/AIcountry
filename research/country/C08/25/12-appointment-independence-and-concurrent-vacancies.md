# OJ55–OJ59. Keep judicial renewal independent across institutions

*Design260 · Proposed ordinary judicial selection and continuity duties.*

**OJ55 — Separate ordinary judicial selection from constitutional and
electoral selection.** Implement the distinct twelve-person ordinary
formation in [OJ06–OJ10](02-selectors-appointments-and-founding.md).
At the same time no principal or alternate may serve in both that
formation and the CJ06 formation. Keep the judicial and practitioner
rolls open under the same substantive qualifications. Distinct votes and
lay draws fill separate terms. Give the ordinary formation a funded
right to accept candidacies and objections, access authenticated source
records and convene when a shared administrative service fails. Routine
services may be shared if separately authorized custody, payment and
receipt can operate through the declared common loss. Price principal
and alternate coverage and the release of judges from their other
duties. Neither formation can direct judgments, enlarge its own
jurisdiction, discipline judges or appropriate its own funds.

**OJ56 — Track the appointment chain through concurrent loss.** Before
each appointment cycle, the responsible registrars separately assess
ordinary, constitutional and electoral appointments that are actually
due. Use the statutory denominator, each category's lawful availability
after alternates and actual notice, evidence, review and entry dates.
Identify the common controller for each required roll, nominations
channel, alternate roster, clerk, payer and independent court or remedy
route. Assess direct conflicts and common patronage without assuming
that every member with the same profession or policy view is controlled.
Publish the affected function, whether the block concerns process,
resources, candidate eligibility or a valid refusal to consent, the
qualified duties that remain, and the next lawful decision date. Keep
individual safety and private evidence protected. Check scheduled and
unexpected loss against existing notice windows, including a boycott
that lasts through two recruitment rounds.

**OJ57 — Keep courts usable without awarding a contested appointment.**
If a lawful appointing formation cannot decide, preserve the original
seat, vote threshold and term limits. Replace unavailable selectors
only through their prior lawful alternate or renewal route. The Court
Service Registrar opens the [OJ45–OJ48](10-shortfall-relief-and-court-repair.md)
capacity response where an actual case or protected deadline is exposed.
Use qualified existing judges and eligible already appointed visiting
judges only in their lawful scope, with paid counsel, clerks and
execution. Check the court that loses time when another receives it.
Keep each ordinary or constitutional case, ballot task and remedy with
its existing lawful owner and original deadline. If an original remedy
has no qualified timely path, record the affected duty and seek funded
correction through its applicable law. A partial operating response
does not appoint a judge, certify an election or produce a constitutional
judgment that the competent body cannot make.

**OJ58 — Compel procedural duties and protect genuine disagreement.**
An affected applicant may use the OJ16 appointment challenge. A person
facing actual late or lost relief may use the ordinary case-delay and
public-remedy route. Competent courts may order performance of a clear
procedural duty, protect a record or review an unlawful exclusion,
subject to scope, proof and actual execution. They may require a lawful
meeting or decision within the applicable rules. A refusal to endorse
a candidate on lawful grounds is a valid exercise of the appointing
vote. The Assembly reviews funding and the combined failure pattern in
public under existing parliamentary scrutiny and appropriations. Neither
a parliamentary majority, an executive, a reviewing judge nor a
technical system acquires an appointment vote through that review.

**OJ59 — Replace the arrangement at a lawful published boundary.**
Before the prospective change, publish and fund the fixed commencement
date, separate selection calendars, responsibilities for current
candidacies, open challenges, earned pay, files and their authorized
receiving. Existing judges keep lawful tenure and litigants keep their
claims and remedy dates. Preserve existing procedure and decision owner
for pending selections where lawfully staffed. If a lawful process
cannot finish, let the competent reviewer address the failure under its
own law. Starting a fresh competition requires notice, fresh consideration
and independent review of transferred evidence. A prior endorsement is
not a vote in the new formation. No selector sets their own continuation
or picks which pending vacancy moves. Reopen on repeated coupled
failure, an unstaffable open roll, unacceptable loss of hearing capacity
or a simpler rival that protects the same timely access and safeguards.
