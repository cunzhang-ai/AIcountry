# KR13–KR16: fund useful discovery and buy defined public access

*Design 95 research candidate; not enacted law, a funded offer or demonstrated
superiority.*

**KR13 — Fund public purpose before a possible gain.** Under
[IC08’s](../../C05/09/03-public-investment-charter.md) maximum five-year authority for
*new commitments*, the Assembly sets the national framework and authorizes national windows;
elected councils authorize local windows within C03 competence, without national
funding conditions taking unrelated local powers. The competent elected body specifies
purpose, cash by due date, total commitments, maximum contingent exposure, outputs,
access rights, confidentiality grounds, paid evaluation and closure. Existing sector or
local agencies administer within competence; no new sovereign research ministry or
automatic evergreen fund is formed. Grants may buy valuable negative findings,
maintenance, creative work, training and open standards as well as commercial
discoveries. A prize needs a pre-funded, fixed maximum and verified achievement; it
cannot finance entrants' initial risk or be paid from hypothetical future tax savings.
Honest failure within authorized work is not fraud.

**KR14 — Separate purpose, award, verification and use.** The competent elected body
sets the window; the existing sector agency publishes a call and receives proposals
without mandatory AI ranking. Qualified substantive reviewers give attributable reasons,
including at least one outside the buying team, as IC09 requires; shared sponsors, data
or control are disclosed, not relabelled independent by model count. An accountable
director makes a reasoned award with conflict/recusal rules. Losing applicants and
affected workers/users can challenge unlawful exclusion, capture or altered terms
through C04/C08 routes. Treasury checks lawful source and payee; a different qualified
verifier accepts contractual work. A regulator separately decides any clinical, product,
environmental or research-participant permission. Neither grant award nor a positive
paper is market approval. Missing qualified assignment prevents a new discretionary
award; it does not suspend existing care, lawful dues or public criticism. Current
IC09/C04 recruitment, conflict/rotation and reasoned director-removal rules apply;
recipient or minister cannot choose a case evaluator to get one outcome. Public purpose
can include basic research, arts, replication, local practical knowledge and maintenance
without a commercial-return requirement.

**KR15 — Buy defined foreground access, with a real publication default.** Before
commitment, the contract inventories background rights, third-party data, foreground
outputs, creator/worker pay and who may use each result. For *fully publicly paid
non-sensitive research and technical results*, publish usable methods, negative findings
and rights terms by the earlier of actual acceptance or the dated contractual
report/release milestone; a single pre-specified embargo of **at most twelve months,
measured from that milestone, not a repeatedly postponed acceptance** may protect
filing, validation or a reasoned delivery sequence. A longer restriction or permanent
redaction requires a distinct lawful privacy, consent, safety, security or
existing-rights ground, public metadata/reasons and independent challenge; sensitive
source records stay under justified custody. Commissioned creative works instead receive
an agreed, paid public exhibition/access/archival scope and no blanket transfer of every
future use. This twelve-month maximum is a provisional policy number, not an empirically
proven optimum. For partial funding, buy a priced, scoped public-purpose license rather
than seize unfunded background rights. Any exclusive public-funded license needs ex-ante
reasons, limited use/time, access/step-in duties and a published comparison with a
nonexclusive offer. No post-award secret expansion of ownership. If work is incomplete,
release the existing authorized material, status and limitations as contracted without
pretending the result is complete; separately protected evidence stays safe. The default
foreground licence for fully paid nonsensitive technical results is nonexclusive,
worldwide, royalty-free and irrevocable for compliant uses: read, reproduce, modify,
maintain and redistribute, including commercially, with attribution, identified changes
and no false endorsement. Source and actual licensed technical documentation accompany
software where needed for those uses. Foreground patent permissions are bought on the
same expressly stated use/territory basis from the actual holder; no right in
third-party background or personal data is inferred.
Before award, verify the necessary employee/co-owner/foreign chain of title or
permission for the claimed worldwide scope. If unavailable, disclose exactly the
narrower acquired territory/acts and decide under the published window whether a
priced prospective exception is permissible or reject the new award. Domestic or
contingent permission must never be labelled worldwide access. Old licensed versions keep their
permissions through later model, vendor, law or grant-manager changes. A
restricted/exclusive exception needs prospective explicit authority, scope, price and
comparison, not a secret award amendment.

**KR16 — Pay for work and accept an actual deliverable.** Milestones pay authorized work
and protect workers on time, including honestly reported failure; the separate verifier
checks original methods, work, quality and agreed release, with privacy and trade-secret
protections. No patent filing, paper count or AI citation score substitutes for accepted
knowledge or justified negative results. A second payment for manufactured product,
professional use, translation or continuing maintenance is legitimate only for a
distinct priced task or background right. Stop new awards at the window cap; preserve
accrued wages, contracts, source custody, appeals and a funded successor. Renewal
compares repair, private/cooperative research, public-open work and no change, with
actual cost and usefulness rather than award volume.
