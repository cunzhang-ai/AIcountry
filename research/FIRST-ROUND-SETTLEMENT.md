# AIcountry first-round settlement

*Design520 · First-round stable research edition for public review*

[Edition guide, archive and checksums](2026-11-first-round-stable/README.md).

Choose a **constitutional parliamentary democracy with a service-first plural economy**. Its purpose is practical independence. People should be able to live securely, leave a harmful dependence, obtain a timely remedy and replace those who govern them. The first-round design chooses the distribution of power, the material bargain and the first governing programme. The legislature, local councils and courts exercise distinct authority. AI can assist their work under public law.

## The first governing programme

| In order | What to choose | What the decision must protect |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Establish lawful scope | Use the existing-polity reform as the first-round reference. The competent prior public process determines territory, electorate, rights and liabilities, then adopts independently operable charter choices and prior-law first-contest instruments through their proper routes. | Timely review of credible exclusion, fair alternatives, lawful prior authority, independent court, funding, actual execution and review of the result. Apply each new electoral rule only at its lawfully effective contest, including any protected delay. A new territorial compact requires its own legitimate process. |
| 2. Start government and protect supply | Hold the first contest under prospectively fixed rules. Verify accepted control at the receiving end. A competent Assembly can vote capped or ordinary supply separately from investiture. | Civil service, due claims and lawful prior obligations continue on their own clocks. A called contest follows its existing contest and review rules. |
| 3. Vote continuity and repair | Publish a schedule of due duties by place and owner. Vote named repair for blocked receipt and independently usable opposition, remedy and service routes. Include the cash, paid cover and qualified receiver for the next call. | Unfunded due duties remain visible and actionable. Keep the scope of optional improvements separate and compare a genuinely different delivery choice on the same duties and resources. |
| 4. Decide persistent gaps before finite cover ends | If ordinary repair still cannot deliver, return exact provider and recurring source alternatives to the competent elected bodies. Retain lawful sources until any replacement takes effect. Consider a separate, bounded public-works draft-checking evaluation after protecting ordinary democratic and service access. | Check household and worker burdens, original deadlines, local receiving, a weaker fiscal year and paid exit. A public AI rendering pilot is a separate motion and comes later in the reference schedule, unless an earlier documented communication barrier merits a different lawful choice. |
| 5. Check delivery and vote again | Follow actual receipt, remedies and displaced burdens through ordinary and adverse windows. Renew, repair or replace the scoped choice on current evidence. Fund the existing proposed schedule for major-law review and keep quicker remedies. | Preserve continuing rights, pending reviews and the ability to replace a provider, mediator or government. Compare against strong repair and an executable structural rival. |

## What remains for a real adopter

The [fourteen canonical chapters](country/README.md) give the selected institutions and ordinary rules. [Design status](DESIGN-STATUS.md) records live objections and rival arrangements. Adopting bodies still need exact authority, electorate, territory, seat count, calendar, officeholders, lawful sources, minimum service schedules and receiving capacity. Those choices require affected-party participation and publicly reviewable evidence. Timely receipt, a second funded call and real alternatives are needed to test whether lives improve. The [completion audit](DESIGN-COMPLETENESS.md) tracks these separately.

The [country resource account](settlement/first-country-resource-account/01-the-first-budget-on-one-perimeter.md) gives one observed reference for evaluating sources, and the [clinical pathways comparison](settlement/clinical-pathway-bottlenecks/01-when-compliance-and-care-differ.md) illustrates why service timing and receipt need separate evidence. The [first local adoption trial](settlement/first-adoption-trial/01-test-payment-access-before-expansion.md) supplies a bounded payment comparison if a competent payer is available.
