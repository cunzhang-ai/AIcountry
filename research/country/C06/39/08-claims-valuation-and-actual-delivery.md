# Claims, independent valuation and actual delivery

Design 99 · Proposed research, not enacted law or funded bank protection.

**BR08 — One usable claim and the original payout clocks.** At the original BF03 M07 event
(including a court-established earlier T0), the insurer opens assisted in-person/phone/remote claims
and obtains protected beneficiary/account records under prior law, without requiring a smartphone or
complete technical dossier. It identifies each actual beneficiary, licensed bank legal debtor (with
connected control separately disclosed), principal/lawful interest, proved joint/trust share,
domestic-unit failure-day FX conversion, insured/uninsured portions and disputed facts. Pay the
undisputed covered essential advance up to one B within **24 actual hours** of that original BF03
event, and the remaining undisputed covered amount as soon as possible and no later than **seven
working days** from it, crediting the advance once. Identity/share or fraud disputes do not stop an
undisputed portion, confiscate it, or create an automatic tax/undue-loan set-off. Give a person a
usable reason and direct B17 review of a delayed/disputed portion. Ordinary U liquidation of a
licensed bank does not erase this insurance clock or turn it into an M09 bridge vote.

**BR09 — One property and priority ledger.** A qualified receiver maintains a court-answerable
source ledger of original title, client/trust segregation, deposit account/beneficiary shares,
collateral and linked netting, due wages/dependants, each M10 class, disputed and estimated values,
every M07 advance/payout, insurer subrogation after actual payment, bridge assumption, distribution,
M11 claim and received amount/date/currency. Client property is returned outside the estate; a
shortfall or contested title is stated rather than fictionalized as bank capital. Valid security is
realized only to its fair net collateral value, and deficiency enters its lawful unsecured class.
Within the estate apply M10's prospective bank hierarchy, U04's protected wage/dependant tier and
pro rata equality, with reasoned M10 same-class departure and M11 compensation where actually
permitted. U continues to govern access, fairness and ordinary liquidation except the bank-specific
priority that the enacted M10 expressly displaces. A purported fraud suspicion gets protected
investigation and court adjudication, not a private or administrative confiscation. Publish safe
class totals, not a permanent public map of every household's finances.

**BR10 — Independent M11 comparison, not a bailout fiction.** Commission an independent dated
valuation of each disputed bank asset/liability and of what the claimant would have received in
ordinary lawful bank liquidation **at the original M09 entry date**, under M10, with source
information reasonably then available, actual lawful secured/client rights, sale and administration
costs, and uncertainty. It excludes extraordinary public aid and hindsight perfection. Compare the
claimant's matched actual received resolution value and timing; compute a positive shortfall once,
with any M07 insurance payout/subrogation shown symmetrically so depositor and insurer do not
collect the same covered portion twice. A later source correction receives a fresh independent
reasoned M11 valuation of the original entry-date counterfactual and actual receipts, not a newly
chosen failure date or moving hierarchy. Give affected claimants a protected answerable method,
input ranges, challenge, expert assistance and ordinary appeal; the resolution authority cannot
finalize its own counterfactual by secret AI forecast. A proved M11 deficit is owed by the
separately appropriated public resolution-claims account, in addition to M08 insured-payout
liability, with actual double-payment credits for the same loss. Unlawful interference can also
receive feasible B17/M11 correction on its own ground; this formula is no immunity for illegality.

**BR11 — Notice, claims and execution without fictitious cash.** Issue a safe but usable notice of
each entry, class/transfer decision, claim amount, route, due date, original clock and review to
known claimants and public service points. B13 first receipt survives office referral,
language/disability/network barriers and receiver replacement. A claimant may seek B14 source
disclosure, correction and B17 urgent relief; B15's ordinary knowledge-based limit governs personal
loss unless an enacted M11-specific clock is prospectively selected, with blocked-access fairness
preserved. An omitted insurer finding, unpaid M07 amount, client-property return, wage, estate
distribution or awarded M11 sum becomes an attributable due claim against its actual obligor and
source. An M11 award names a payment date no later than 30 working days after final enforceable
judgment; insufficient appropriated cash leaves an arrear and immediate B19/elected-source action,
not a fictional payment or judicial tax. Execute a private estate/receiver duty under B18 and a
public Treasury/claims duty under B19, with LF's separate force ceiling; a court cannot invent fund
assets, FX, insured coverage above 12B or an unsecured-claim priority. If actual M08 cash/backstop,
M11 appropriation, accepted operator or court executor is missing, preserve due dates and amounts,
expose arrears and require lawful Assembly/Treasury receiving action. A written transfer instruction
or credited ledger is not received cash. The 30-working-day award limit is an outer candidate; a
shorter specified due date and useful-time relief for independently protected needs remain earlier.
M07 insured payment is not postponed to a final M11 judgment.

**BR12 — Transition and legal boundaries.** The adopting Act inventories existing bank deposits,
insurance/guarantees, title, currencies, collateral, netting, wages, contracts, tax/payment
finality, pensions, pending U/bank cases and foreign obligations, then names accepted lawful
insurer, resolution, bridge/receiver, court, Treasury and payment successors with paid source
access. New M10 hierarchy and M09 stay/bridge powers are prospective; no old claim is silently
demoted, no old rescue promised anew and no penalty is added. MP15 merger review still governs a
permanent buyer's control change on its own exact threshold/clock; an M09 temporary bridge protects
clients but does not supply a competition clearance or appoint its reviewer. PD limits data to
reimbursement, valuation and challenge purpose, while allowing necessary protected source evidence;
KR, worker and client rights survive transfer unless actual law/contract says otherwise. Foreign
recognition is source-specific and cannot be declared by a domestic order. Preserve stronger current
lawful protection where the new system cannot yet operate; absent funded actors is an unmet duty,
not a reason to restart 24-hour, seven-day, 72-hour, two-working-day or two-year clocks.
