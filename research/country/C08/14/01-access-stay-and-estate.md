# Accessible entry, fair notice and the assets that can be used

*U01–U04; part of [C08 private insolvency](../14-private-insolvency-and-debt-recovery.md).*

**U01 — Eligibility and competent entry.** A person/ordinary enterprise unable
to pay due obligations without losing protected needs can request court
assessment directly through assisted/offline as well as digital intake.
Neither minimum debt nor an upfront fee excludes a no-asset applicant.
Accept an account; help assemble debts, assets, actual income, claims and
safe contact. A creditor can petition on evidence of an actual unpaid valid
claim, with debtor notice and a chance to contest abuse/mistake. The court
reasons on inability, dispute, source rights and a suitable procedure; no AI
credit score establishes insolvency, moral fault or a loss allocation. Direct
urgent protection retains B17 and shorter applicable limits.

**U02 — Stay and safe continuation.** Only a lawful opening/interim order,
not a raw application or account label, stays specified collection,
foreclosure/asset execution and estate dissipation. Notice states estate,
covered acts, duration, exceptions and challenge. Urgent protection can be
ex parte under B17's actual hearing safeguards. Maintain current necessary
housing/utilities/clinical services under their own due laws; old debt alone
cannot become an automatic essential-service cutoff. A provider is not
compelled to deliver unfunded discretionary new goods. Secured/other parties
can seek reasoned relief for real prejudice, with subsistence and third-party
protection. Police/criminal process and independently warranted imminent-
danger protection are not stopped by a financial stay. Ongoing maintenance
and post-opening necessary wages keep separate duties; no blanket immunity.

**U03 — Estate, evidence and exemptions.** Identify assets actually owned
and reachable, third-party/trust/client property, valid security and contested
title. A spouse/housemate's wealth is not the debtor's property; an agent's
credential does not prove receipt. Exclude ordinary personal goods, necessary
disability/care equipment and proportionate tools actually needed for work.
Keep liquid buffer of three months of verified personal needs, including
actual safe housing/essential expenses, under the I package's access rules.
High-value surplus is not universally exempt. Review any realization of a
home through actual title/security, notice, fair sale and the separate safe
housing receiver; no software foreclosure. Existing security and newly
proposed exemptions need explicit source-law transition, not silent retroactive
extinction. Fraudulent concealment needs actual control/intent evidence.

The trustee can ask a court to avoid a materially preferential or undervalue
transfer in the preceding 12 months when the debtor was insolvent or the
transaction caused insolvency and the recipient knew or reasonably should
have known it. Protect ordinary good-faith fair-value trade and dependants'
actual necessary support. Recover only the improper benefit after notice/
defenses, not every family payment or historical gift. Deliberate fraudulent
transfers retain their separately enacted longer/other-law route; the new
lookback cannot create one. Publish unknowns; collect only necessary protected
records rather than requiring permanent public financial testimony.

**U04 — Priority and unreceived losses.** Return property that is not the
estate. Valid security is paid from its collateral's lawful net realization,
subject to adopted exemptions/necessary realization costs and court protection;
any deficiency enters the ordinary unsecured pool. Do not silently give a
new lender first security over an existing claimant. In the unencumbered
pool, pay reasonable court-approved administration/necessary preservation
costs, then accrued essential child/dependant maintenance and recent three-
month wage claims (per worker capped at three months' verified needs), then
ordinary unsecured claims pro rata, then expressly subordinated controlling-
insider instruments and owner residual. Ordinary tax arrears have no automatic
priority over ordinary private claims. Family relation alone creates no
insider class; prove control and instrument. Law fixes equal treatment inside
each class and reviews disputed amounts. Fees cannot consume every small
estate: no-asset processing needs an enacted public source, not fabricated
recovery. Wage excess remains an ordinary claim. All amounts not recovered
are named losses/unmet claims; insurance or basic support is credited only
if actually received for the same item under its distinct law.

[WL34–WL36](../../C05/12-work-contracts-and-collective-power.md) now proposes a
limited general-revenue wage advance. Its matched Treasury recovery enters the
prospective ordinary unsecured pool after remaining worker-priority claims;
it does not acquire a new security or cut U04's unpaid worker claims. Actual old
security/source rights and employer debt survive; a public advance is no new
estate asset or assumption that recovery is cash.

**Design148 ordinary security specification.** [PT07–PT18](../26-property-transfers-and-secured-credit.md)
now supplies prospective attachment, total secured ceiling, public notice/actual
possession and continuous priority. Only attached perfected rights have U04
secured estate rank; unperfected security enters the unsecured pool on opening.
Functional new price-retention/qualifying financed acquisitions are security,
not hidden non-estate full ownership. Genuine third-party custody/operating
leases remain excluded. No after-opening filing cures secrecy, no purchase-
money leap, and no silent reprioritization of legacy rights. U avoidance,
protected needs/costs/stay/discharge and exact M/NB exceptions remain unchanged.

**Design221. Scheduled site charge.** [CLN05](../../C09/21/02-insolvency-residual-source-and-closure.md)
adds an express prospective exception for specified site proceeds only.
It respects earlier perfected rights and U04's protected unencumbered
claims, and ranks ahead of later consensual security on those interests
within its registered ceiling. The exception requires coordinated enactment.
