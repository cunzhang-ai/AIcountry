# Keep people and places viable through concentrated job loss

*Design329 · AT23–AT27 · Proposed place-based work and service transition.*

**AT23 — Map the actual dependence before the closure or loss.** On credible
evidence of a material contraction that meets AT02 or threatens a due duty
under AT03, the responsible work, local finance and service authorities
prepare a joint dated account within AT06's original timetable. Include
direct, dependent and supplier workers, households, employer-linked homes,
utilities and transport, and communities sharing the tax or service shock.
Use actual labor-market and service boundaries. A boundary for grants
does not determine who can file a wage, housing or benefit claim.
Distinguish current receipts, credible lost capacity, unpaid claims,
restricted closure money, work available elsewhere and uncertainty.
A promised employer or modelled job count establishes no recovery.

**AT24 — Protect two usable choices for people.** Keep original wages,
earned rights, benefits, essential service and housing duties on their
own clocks. Make work assistance and optional paid learning available
without requiring relocation, participation or agreement with an employer.
Residents who want to move can seek scheduled portability, accessible
travel and paid receiving under the applicable law. Price actual affordable
housing, care, school or necessary work access at the receiving end.
Residents who stay retain the common service floor and independent
claims. A legitimate voluntary move, a collective land or community
decision and an allocation of public services are separate acts.
A missing placement or unaffordable host offer is an exposed need.
No family, council or AI chooses for an adult or supplies consent.

**AT25 — Replace the lost common source explicitly.** The local elected
body selects a lawful service route with residents and opposition.
The national legislature prices the common duty through MF and LD,
including actual net own-source change, continuing fixed costs,
and a separate host allocation where required. Use a bounded, authorized
continuity source only until a specified lawful settlement or review.
Put a persistent gap into the next elected recurring package.
Keep household payments, ordinary local transfers, host capacity,
supplier and worker claims, and operator-funded site restoration separate.
Reconcile one transfer or actual released capacity once. No anticipated
sale, investor promise or cleanup security pays for wages or basic services.

**AT26 — Compare recovery that can outlast the departing employer.** Put
to the competent elected bodies the protected services and portable
support, a credible local diversification option, and any requested
employer rescue on the same dated sources. Compare actual suitable work,
who can receive it, owner concentration, tax receipts, operating costs,
external and ecological effects and the harm if a new provider fails.
Prioritize continuity and credible small-scale or staged commitments
where a larger package lacks accepted operators, purchasers, jobs or
recurring money. A targeted rescue requires additionality, limits,
review and expiry under TN. Preserve a lawful exit and viable alternatives
before signing an exclusive dependency. Stated job creation cannot
discharge the common service floor or existing cleanup and creditor duties.

**AT27 — Check whether residents received the choice.** Give workers,
residents, affected localities and their opposition a usable route to
challenge coverage, receipts and service conditions. Publish actual
paid claims, remaining service, accepted optional moves, refused or
unavailable options, and jobs at usable pay and hours, with protected
evidence. Include people who remain, return or leave and those missed
by ordinary employer records. Assign the original dates, legal owners,
follow-up and paid successor under AT12, FT and T. AI may compare
matched schedules and alert to concentration or missing groups. It
cannot select a site, determine eligibility or certify a recovery.
Adopt prospectively and reopen on involuntary displacement, expiring
sources, continuing job or care gaps or a stronger local alternative.

checks household, home and municipal exposure in one calendar. Actual
