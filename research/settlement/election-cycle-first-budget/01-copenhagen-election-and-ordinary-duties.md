The election was 26 elapsed days after the call. The 30 April council
decision was 37 elapsed days after the poll. This dates one approval,
not the first availability of money or the time anyone was paid.
The health and care figure is a prior-year scope check. The exact
city-month court order and execution, ordinary care receipts, the
social-assistance payout and a common failure affecting them all
are unobserved in these records. Keep the court and care rows open.

## One transfer, no duplicate source

The city's Table 1 has **DKK 34.951m** in net estimated 2026 costs.
The vote uses the rounded DKK 35.0m description. Table 2 shows
DKK 34.951m out of the general operating pool and the same amount
to Culture and Leisure. Consolidation leaves zero change from this
transfer itself. It finances a purpose inside the existing city scope.
It is neither a second source of revenue nor an audited outturn.

The report describes recurring system, storage and readiness costs and
says Budget 2025 funded the described recurring provision in municipal
and regional election years without providing it in other years.
The 2026 estimate shows system cost of DKK 1.800m and physical and
digital storage of DKK 0.565m. A separate DKK 2.000m line combines
readiness, planning and other expenses. Do not classify the whole
estimate, the three lines or the mixed line as an exact continuing
baseline without disaggregated records. The same note records five
new polling places in 2025 and a national electoral system deployed in
all municipalities at that election. No inference follows that these
changes caused the later complaints or that the older recurring gap
persisted through the 2027 budget.

The city's separate 2025 local-election record contains complaints
about changed polling areas and physical access. They identify
particular practical obstacles and do not estimate population-wide
nonaccess. Keep provision for site access and assistance in the
annual readiness baseline and test whether a person can actually
use them. A total vote count cannot resolve the complaint.

## Disposition for the first budget

Prefer a capped source that can lawfully be used before the original
deadline. Preserve Copenhagen's demonstrated ability to organize
a completed contest and treat lawful existing prefinancing as a live
rival if records establish it. Budget the continuing readiness floor
even in a year without voting. Price and test lawful activation,
independent hearing and execution, local accepting sites and
simultaneous receipt of protected ordinary work. A general reserve
could meet the same rule if its money and authority are genuinely
protected against other calls. Use the 2026 observations on their
own basis. The earlier 2025 Danish national account remains a
country-wide source comparator with a different period and perimeter.
