# Three small acquisitions and one local market

This constructed case tests [SCM01–SCM08](../../country/C05/11/11-successive-acquisitions-and-market-choice.md).
Assume the relevant domestic area has four independently controlled home-care
agencies, including the acquiring group. Their brands may remain after sale.
Assume customers and workers actually use these agencies as alternatives.
The group has a combined domestic turnover with each target above 10,000T.
The acquired activities overlap in the same area and their independently
verified historical turnover is shown in each transaction's frozen T units.
No separate MP14 gateway designation applies. All first binding events occur
after commencement.

| Transaction | Overlapping target turnover | Series total | Independent controllers if closed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| First agency | 180T | 180T | 3 |
| Second agency | 170T | 350T | 2 |
| Third agency | 165T | 515T | 1 |

The old individual turnover gate misses all three. The series gate requires
filing for the third, before control transfers. Earlier lawful closings keep
their status. A court must still determine whether the third deal would
substantially and durably reduce practical constraints. If verified independent
entry can replace the target in useful time, the controller count by itself
is inadequate proof. If instead care recipients have no accessible substitute
and workers would lose the only credible alternative employer, obtain evidence
of duration, exit conditions, wages, care and any independently funded operator.
A transaction decision must include live visits, staff rights and usable receiving
rather than assuming that blocking a sale keeps a financially weak firm running.

If the first agency has actually returned to independent ownership before
the third deal, the series total drops to 335T. The same is true if the
first deal falls outside the 36-month lookback or is outside the covered
activity. A still-operative earlier binding deal within the window counts
even before its control transfer. In the simultaneous-commitment branch,
the same three activities can trigger filing at the common binding event.
Where credible specific evidence points to harm below the threshold, MP16
remains available on its own evidence and time limits.

The figures are constructed. They show which transaction needs review under
the two filing rules. They supply no market definition, causal effect or actual
price, wage or care forecast. [Reproduction](verify-successive-acquisitions.py).
