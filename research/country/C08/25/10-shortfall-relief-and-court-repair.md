# OJ45–OJ48. Reduce real delay without trading away justice

*Design215 · Part of [C08 ordinary justice](../25-ordinary-justice-and-public-prosecution.md).*

**OJ45 — Respond when the next lawful step is at risk.** A registry or
competent service that learns of a material risk promptly informs the
assigned court, the responsible independent administrator and any
other office needed for relief, through lawful safe channels. If the
court is unavailable or implicated, use the already competent alternate
route. Preserve the original clock and communicate usable options to
affected parties. Use actual authorized staff, assigned case relief
and alternate receiving. Preserve reviewable reasons for delay and
safe proof of what the parties received. Execute existing legal effects
of missed or expired orders, including release where due. This creates
no new default victory, detention renewal, compulsory settlement or
dismissal by statistics.

**OJ46 — Repair the resource that actually blocks progress.** The
existing independent court authority leads the court-side plan.
Choose among qualified staffing and counsel, better disclosure,
remote or local sessions with meaningful participation, independent
record recovery, or simpler procedure adopted under its own law.
Count displacement, travel, paid overlap, qualifications and funded
execution. A transfer needs a competent accepting court, usable
participation and preserved assignment and prior dates. Remote
service needs a viable alternative where privacy, access or evidence
requires it. Optional mediation leaves direct court access and real
consent. A person's inability to proceed digitally, settle or waive
a right cannot become a delay penalty.

**OJ47 — Fund repair without controlling the result.** The Assembly
reviews material shortages and finances the needed court, legal-aid
and executor capacities through their existing distinct sources.
A finite effort to clear an older stock has its own end date, paid
overlap, and an ordinary staffing and review plan after it ends.
When a repair falls short, publish the accountable general choice,
the protected services at risk and alternatives. The independent
court retains individual decisions, lawful assignment, counsel and
review. An analyst or minister cannot enforce a target by delaying
money in a named case. Preserve SR and B for actual remedies and
track compensation and other relief separately from backlog measures.

**OJ48 — Keep case aids contestable and assess real outcomes.** AI
may assist bounded access, transcription, translation or aggregate
capacity analysis under OJ34 and DP. Protect confidential defense,
protected witnesses and original evidence. Apply DP17–DP24 where a
tool changes access, proof burden or schedule. Preserve safe ordinary
alternatives and independent review. Measure actual access, defense,
waits, losses and delivered relief across old and new cohorts. A
faster statistic or new vendor service supplies no mandate or proof
of better justice. Preserve prior cases, staff, evidence and remedies
through lawful T adoption. Reopen on hidden older cases, lost access,
unfunded counterpart work or a better ordinary route.
