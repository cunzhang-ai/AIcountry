# Paid sentence review, release and reintegration

Design 190 adds a bounded judicial review and release duty to existing
P19 and PO execution. It requires real counsel, interpreters, independent
court time, corrections staff, safe transport, housing and health
receivers, income access and victim protection. The relevant general
revenue and local appropriations must cover work at the same time as
existing obligations. A modeled decline in prison occupancy supplies no
available source or automatically transferable workforce.

Pending decisions, original dates and credits, protection and remedy claims,
staff, records and service obligations need accepted paid OP/T successors.
Lawful leniency and earlier release rights govern old judgments. The
whole-country source account must carry the added court and community
requirements and any genuinely released capacity separately. [Country account](../sentence-release/README.md)
