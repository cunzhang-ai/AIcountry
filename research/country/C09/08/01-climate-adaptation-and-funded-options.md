# ADP01–ADP08. Keep future adaptation options open before safety is lost

*Design399 · Proposed adaptation, service and funding duties within [climate and ecological transition](../08-climate-and-ecological-transition.md).*

**ADP01 — Set adaptation responsibilities in the places at risk.**
The Assembly enacts common safety and access duties and the national
source for material shared risks. Each competent local or joint
authority names accountable owners and adopts a place-specific plan.
Use the actual catchment, coast, heat exposure or water supply system,
including service dependencies and effects outside the jurisdiction.
Record current harm, future ranges, groups exposed, applicable safety
and ecological duties, and existing service and repair claims.
Measure lived protection as well as asset exposure. Adaptation,
emissions reductions and ecological restoration retain separate
units, authority and tests.

**ADP02 — Compare materially different paths on the same conditions.**
Use common exposed people, sites, rights, dates, plausible hazard
ranges and maintenance assumptions. Compare continuing repair,
in-place adaptation, reducing exposure through changed land use,
and voluntary relocation or service resiting where credible.
Price construction, ongoing operation, ecosystem effects, energy,
access, safe shelter, worker needs, host capacity, residual loss,
decommissioning and what the option rules out later. Track effects
on renters, people with low incomes and households with access
needs. A reduction in asset loss cannot silently justify worse
basic protection or shifted risk upstream or downstream.

**ADP03 — Set warnings, the safety limit and the latest useful
decision separately.** Name qualified observation owners,
independent verification, indicators, uncertainty, and the point
at which a service or lawful safety condition would fail. For each
plausible path, work back from the earliest defensible exposure date
through participation, land access, permits, procurement, work,
commissioning and verified receiving. State the last useful date
for the exact choice and set a prior review trigger. Compare
changed observations, deterioration and coupled shocks. Missing
data requires review of uncertainty and a precautionary funded
interim option where lawful. It cannot be counted as evidence
of safety. A forecast or warning does not issue an order or
approve a project.

**ADP04 — Pay for real future choices without committing them by default.**
A competent elected body can buy proportionate, reversible design,
survey, testing, options over land by agreement and other lawful
preparation that preserves a specific viable choice. Compare cost,
expiry, compensation, ongoing access and the result of not renewing
the option. Preserve private and collective claims, planning review
and lawful use. Use LA and FR for any separate acquisition or
relocation authority. A risk label or an unfunded reservation does
not acquire land, clear an occupant's rights or qualify a future
project. Compare early construction when waiting would predictably
close the safer option.

**ADP05 — Give material changes a timely elected disposition.**
When a verified trigger, shortened lead time, failed preparation
or qualified evidence changes the viable choices, the responsible
owner transmits the original evidence and exact options to the
competent body. Publish reasons, independently test the dates, and
schedule a recorded choice by the last useful date, sooner where
existing law requires. Include local, host and downstream affected
people and preserve usable opposition review. Resolve cross-local
authority under C04. A trigger supplies no budget or authority
to displace people. A missed vote leaves the known risk, prior
duties and next lawful route visible.

**ADP06 — Finance the current duty, the switch and the years after it.**
Within FB and IV show separately paid maintenance and readiness,
finite preparation, staged capital, funded interim protection,
operation, replenishment and long-tail closure or relocation.
Publish the four-year fiscal path and material later commitments,
with actual workers, land, materials, payment dates and owners.
An option is usable only within its legal duration and accepted
sources. Count other sectors' claims on staff, access and reserves
once. Compare phased spending and the cost of a lost route.
National and local contributions retain their separate votes,
settlement and restrictions. An annual forecast or insurance
payment cannot fund the same work twice.

**ADP07 — Protect people and services through a missed switch.**
Where delay or a stronger hazard makes the proposed route too
late, publish the service and people exposed, useful date and
responsible owners. Use existing lawful funded protection and
seek an exact viable substitute, including reachable alternate
service, safe temporary accommodation and accepted host capacity.
Preserve original entitlements, property and tenancy claims,
independent review, individual choice and ordinary emergency
limits. Relief, forced acquisition and permanent resettlement
retain their separate grounds. Keep current service and lawful
maintenance through handover, verify actual protection and
identify any exposed period. Finite protection does not certify
a permanent solution.

**ADP08 — Keep the plan revisable and accountable.** Publish an
annual reconciliation with the budget and review sooner on a
material trigger. Keep original observations, versions and safe
geographic evidence. The competent elected owners make and can
replace the plan through ordinary law. Independent reviewers
test hazard evidence, feasibility, unequal effects and missed
delivery within their jurisdiction. AI can compare scenarios,
dates and coupled dependencies. It cannot set the safety floor,
take land, certify receiving or replace affected participation.
Adopt prospectively with paid custodians and valid prior rights
and duties intact. Reopen on changed evidence, missing funding,
repeated harm, a lost option or a better qualified route.
