# Two approvals and a separate constitutional preference

Design334 · Constructed comparison for [CRV06–CRV10](../../country/C14/01/05-constitutional-counterproposals.md)

A valid public initiative and a qualifying Assembly alternative propose
incompatible ways of appointing the same public audit leadership.
Assume both texts preserve applicable rights, checks and protected
succession in their own prospective scope. The initiative is certified
on day 0, closes on day 120 and first votes on day 106. Under the
proposed pairing rule, the final material and findings must be usable
by day 16 to give 90 full days before voting. No date is changed by
the Assembly proposing an alternative on day 17.

| Authored question | Valid choices | Effect |
|---|---:|---|
| Citizen initiative approval | 630 yes, 370 no | Passes the three-fifths threshold. |
| Assembly alternative approval | 670 yes, 330 no | Passes the majority threshold after its own Assembly authorization. |
| Conditional preference | 540 initiative, 460 alternative | The initiative prevails after both approvals, even though fewer approved it in its separate yes/no question. |

If the initiative receives only 590 yes out of 1,000, it fails.
The Assembly alternative can take effect on its own lawful schedule
if it has passed. A preference for the initiative cannot qualify it.
If both pass and the verified preference count is tied, the initiative
prevails. If a material preference record is lost and cannot be
recovered, a final judgment must identify whether the approval
results are valid and severable. With both intact, the prior-law
repeat asks the preference alone. The case supplies no assumptions
about a winner before that lawful result. Protected successor
contests and other constitutional conditions retain their own dates.

Compare raw affirmative totals, a standing preference for the public
initiative, and separate approval with a conditional choice. Raw
totals answer which text got more yes votes in its own question,
which can differ in turnout and threshold. Automatic initiative
priority preserves the petition route but gives electors no way to
select the other text when both pass. The proposed third question
adds explanation, ballot and review work. It lets the public choose
between two approved texts while keeping the exact original vote
unconditional on an Assembly alternative.
