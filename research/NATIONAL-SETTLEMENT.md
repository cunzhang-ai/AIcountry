# AIcountry national settlement

*Design548 · Edition 1 · English research design*

**The country gives every person the practical means to live, dissent,
leave a harmful dependence, obtain relief and help replace the government.**
Its institutions must work for people with little money, time, mobility,
technical fluency or political influence. The charter and independent remedies
protect people when majorities, administrators or suppliers fail. Ecological
limits and the resources needed by future generations constrain what any present
government can promise.

These starting points combine practical constraints and deliberate public
values. Proportional voting, the mix of services and income, and the scope of
local discretion are institutional choices open to challenge.

Use evidence from existing countries to identify where arrangements fail,
what a repair costs and which people actually benefit. Judge the selected
design and its strongest rivals on the same rights, inherited obligations,
resources and dates. The [inherited-power comparison](settlement/first-round-mandate/08-what-the-first-mandate-inherits.md) tests both an

same durable protection.

## Who decides

People and voluntary groups choose how to live, organize, work and cooperate
under general law protecting others' rights. Public permission requires a
specific lawful purpose and authority. Elected local bodies choose delivery
and local improvements. The national government secures equal standing,
independent justice, a funded common floor and defined responsibilities that
cannot be carried at a narrower level. A sponsor seeking a new material power
must show why lawful voluntary action, ordinary repair or local cooperation
cannot supply the protection, and how the power can be reviewed or ended.
The Assembly funds common outcomes and recognizes equivalent lawful local
methods. A council can decline an optional national platform or grant and
retain due funding and unrelated powers. When material effects cross a
boundary, the competent joint or elected body decides the unresolved
allocation or safeguard. Equivalent lawful local methods remain available,
and temporary control returns through an accepted handoff.

governance while honoring existing worker rights and agreements.

## The opening decision

Start through the competent prior public process in an existing polity.
Put three decisions before their respective competent bodies.
Carry out compatible repairs on actual existing powers. Submit changes
to a constitutional electorate, election adjudicator or executive
appointment through the competent prior constitutional route before
using them. A rejected reform leaves separately lawful repairs in
operation. [Danish constitutional comparison](settlement/first-round-mandate/12-the-existing-constitution-governs-the-change.md)

| Instrument | Scope |
|---|---|
| Democratic authority | Protect rights, competitive elections, opposition, independent review and peaceful replacement. Decide inherited high-risk powers expressly. |
| Continuity supply | Carry lawful obligations and finance ordinary justice, standing services, upkeep and binding external duties. Verify actual funds, work and receiving. |
| Demonstrated repairs | Fund only named failures of the essential floor or democratic operation on the same first and next dates. Start with ordinary law and local choice. Use a narrowly enacted repair where those routes cannot deliver. |

A prescribed enterprise board formula, new broad entitlements, new tax, currency or
work-time instruments, and new public AI deployments go to separate later
decisions. Existing law and bounded lawful assistance continue on their terms.
A local temporary housing commitment or a paid connection between services
can qualify as an exact repair where ordinary routes demonstrably fail.
The optional public-works AI evaluation is outside opening supply.
Use the [national choices and evidence](status/current/06-national-choices-and-evidence.md) to see which claims already have
observations and which are constructed. The [first evidence and budget choice](settlement/first-round-mandate/11-first-evidence-and-budget-choice.md)
selects ordinary repair for the documented payment problem. A new route
earns a separate decision only if it improves on the strongest lawful
ordinary alternative on the same people, deadlines and full cost.
[first-budget comparison](settlement/first-round-mandate/10-a-limited-first-budget.md)

For the first mandate, preserve the separation between the vote that confers
office, the vote that authorizes spending and the evidence that a public action
reached its recipient. A government can lose a policy vote and still hold
office. A service can fail despite lawful appropriation. Each failure goes to
the authority that can remedy it. The first term must leave a real lawful policy
choice to its successor, after pricing binding inherited obligations and
independent service continuity. If several discretionary decisions together
would eliminate the last credible way to change a major policy in useful time,
the elected body must decide that loss explicitly before it binds. It can
choose a long commitment for a concrete public benefit and state when a
real alternative can return. Price actual control of sites, staff, evidence
and handoff alongside cash. [successor policy choice](country/C03/19/12-successor-policy-capacity.md) · [joined care comparison](settlement/first-round-mandate/09-a-successor-cannot-spend-a-roster.md)

## Evidence and review

Measure timely usable services, practical opposition, executed remedies and
qualified staffing against the strongest feasible ordinary route. Compare
the same people, duties, original deadlines and full costs. Use the original
rights and remedies for any missed duty. Preserve records and lawful authority
so elected successors can change policy while essential services continue.
[First-mandate exercise](settlement/first-round-mandate/05-verify-the-first-mandate-as-one-country.md)

For public works, use competent staff and fixed checks on the original
record. Keep targets, forecasts, start dates, actual delivery and spending
authority distinct. Any separate AI evaluation must demonstrate an advantage
over that baseline on fresh cases. [ordinary works calibration](settlement/first-public-ai-programme/02-an-ordinary-check-on-public-works.md)
