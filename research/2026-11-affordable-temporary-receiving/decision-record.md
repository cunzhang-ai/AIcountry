# Design540 decision record

*14 November 2026 · Make temporary housing affordable at the point of use*

Pin [Design539](baseline.json), [HL13–HL17](../country/C09/25/04-affordable-receiving-and-refund-exposure.md),
[AP19–AP23](../country/C04/06/09-usable-public-service-guarantees.md),
[HDC01–HDC06](../country/C07/05/06-hospital-to-community-and-safe-receiving.md)
and the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md). Select
[HL23–HL27](../country/C09/25/06-affordable-temporary-receiving.md) for
publicly arranged emergency and transitional placements. Use ordinary
affordable supply and timely lawful cash first. If a necessary temporary
placement otherwise depends on unverified payment, contract the covered
cost to the housing payer before the resident incurs it. Existing rules
on ownership disputes, due benefits, care and refusal govern separately.

The [constructed comparison](../settlement/affordable-temporary-receiving/01-a-room-without-avoidable-debt.md)
tests one unresolved award, an expiry, and competing calls. The original
Ombudsman decision identifies an observed affordability failure. Model
charges, resources and alternative rooms are assumed. Assistant review
examined the resident's ability to change one component without losing
the other bookings. Actual affected-party review and lawful authorization
are still required.

The strongest objection is that directly securing temporary charges can
raise prices, crowd out another placement and make a finite route
routine. Require negotiated eligible rates, a gross dated ceiling and
the same ordinary lawful allocation order. Compare available lower-cost
places, timely income correction and direct public procurement. Use
minimum evidence. Make the payer's contract enforceable and available
to the resident. Preserve actual accepted care and travel. No existing
private debt is rewritten by this proposal. No new standing supply,
eligibility rule or AI decision authority follows from the selection.

A real adopting body must specify triggers, rates, funds, provider
terms, review and the next-period receiver. Reopen where budget or
provider expiry shifts costs back, admissible alternatives are withheld
or crowding displaces comparable need. Check timely affordable receipt
and actual private arrears jointly. Earlier written rights and the
frozen edition retain their scope.
