# Two patients and a funded exit from conditional coverage

*Design376 · Observed programme activity and a separate constructed comparison.*

## What the reported activity measures

NHS England reports 1,180 interim-funding notifications and 2,083
managed-access notifications from April to September 2024. The two
categories total 3,263 notifications. Reported drug expenditure in
that period was GBP 5.35 million and GBP 81.45 million, respectively,
or GBP 86.80 million combined. A notification is a recorded intended
start and may differ from actual treatment. The spend is drug cost
and does not provide total clinical, review or transition costs.

In the cumulative group reported through the same release, 34 of
39 reappraised managed-access treatments were recommended for routine
commissioning in the referred populations, or 87.2%. The reappraised
group is selected and already assessed. That percentage cannot be
applied to new treatments or taken as a causal success, population
benefit or actual receipt rate. The notification and reappraisal
numbers have different time scopes and units.

## A separate two-window comparison

The illustrative offer concerns one legally marketable treatment,
with uncertainty about benefit in its proposed group. The standing
essential care, clinical staffing and ordinary relief for all
patients remain separately protected. No cost or clinical property
in this example comes from the observed programme.

There are nine incremental cash units and two qualified start
slots in the early window. Evidence and independent assessment
cost two early units. Each start needs three early units for
treatment and monitoring. A final review costs one later unit.
Each enrolled person requires two later units and an accepting
qualified slot for the clinically appropriate continuation or
transition. Later cash and actual slots must be independently
verified before recruitment.

| Offer and failure | Result |
|---|---|
| Retain ordinary care and prepare evidence | Protect standing duties. No new conditional start or presumed benefit. |
| Open two conditional places with five later units and two accepting slots | Eight early units and five later units fit. Two qualified people may start if consent, evidence and clinical conditions also hold. |
| Open three places | Eleven early units and seven later units are required even if three start slots become available. The proposed cash cannot support it. |
| Reduce later cash to four, or lose one accepting slot | The two-person offer fails. One place can qualify on its own authorized terms before admission. An expected supplier rebate cannot fill the gap before it arrives. |
| Lose the later source after admission | Preserve existing patient-specific duties, publish the funding gap safely, use the lawful urgent care and budget routes, and hold new admissions. A safe exit still needs an accepting provider. |

The choice is the smaller evidence-limited offer only when the early
and later paths are both funded and qualified. At review, a negative
or inconclusive result can end new starts. Any continued care for
existing patients uses the original clinical law and actual safety.
The example establishes no treatment recommendation or source.
