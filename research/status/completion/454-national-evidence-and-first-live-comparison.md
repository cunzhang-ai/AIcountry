# Design546 completion finding

The [national evidence comparison](../current/06-national-choices-and-evidence.md)
connects fourteen chapter choices to their current supporting evidence
and conditions for reversal. Four questions determine the first public
decisions. The boundaries of legal authority, resource readiness and
observed receipt remain separate.

The [first comparison](../../settlement/first-round-mandate/11-first-evidence-and-budget-choice.md)
uses Copenhagen's strong existing payment path as the baseline and selects
ordinary repair on the available evidence. A bounded alternate still needs
the actual payer, participating residents, dated costs, independent evidence
and legal release. National and cross-jurisdictional effects remain open.
