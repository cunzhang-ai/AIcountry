# Protect the first payment when the revenue calendar changes

*Design532 · Observed Danish forecasts and releases with a separate constructed budget.*

Keep the [2025 national resource reference](../first-country-resource-account/01-the-first-budget-on-one-perimeter.md)
as published. Compare a different question in 2026. How much does the calendar
of central-government receipts and financing change within one year? What
would a first budget wrongly authorize if a later estimate were treated as
money that an independent institution or local authority could already use?

## Three different kinds of public evidence

Danmarks Nationalbank publishes a monthly distribution around the Finance Act
and economic revisions. It draws on fiscal estimates, past payment patterns
and known changes to payment dates. Its workbooks use DKK millions. The
following extraction converts to DKK billions and rounds to three decimals.
No 2026 monthly values enter the preserved 2025 annual account.
[Method and workbook index](https://www.nationalbanken.dk/da/vores-arbejde/stabile-priser-pengepolitik-og-dansk-oekonomi/prognose-for-statens-betalinger)

| Workbook line | Finance Act Jan / Feb / year | August review Jan / Feb / year |
|---|---|---|
| Pension yield tax excluding DSP, row 34 | 7.200 / 28.800 / 36.000 | 22.498 / 24.167 / 47.000 |
| Net financing requirement, row 7 | 21.464 / −24.124 / 14.510 | −2.187 / −19.720 / 8.910 |
| Gross financing requirement, row 17 | 25.203 / −18.711 / 88.330 | 8.068 / −8.480 / 103.680 |

The August version contains earlier months of an updated distribution.
It cannot stand in for information available at the Finance Act stage.
The net full-year financing requirement falls by about 5.600 in the
updated version while the gross requirement rises by about 15.350.
These are financing-flow lines within the central-government schedule.
Their difference is neither free cash nor an estimate of service capacity.
Neither tax line is an unrestricted Treasury balance. The forecast labels
and exclusions apply on their own scope.

· [August workbook](https://www.nationalbanken.dk/media/uqrneqd2/oer2.xlsx)

The separate February settlement-date release recorded a central-government
net financing requirement of −19.7 billion for February and −21.9 billion
for January–February. It reported January and February closing central
government balances of DKK 164.114129 and 180.885559 billion. Those are
settlement aggregates and month-end stocks. They do not establish gross tax
receipts by institution, daily minima, spending authority or a municipality's
receipt. Keep the dated releases and the original decision evidence.
[February settlement release, Table 2 and balance sheet](https://www.nationalbanken.dk/en/news-and-knowledge/press/archive/2026/foreign-exchange-and-liquidity-and-monthly-balance-sheet-february-2026-03-03-2026)

Statistics Denmark's September release raised estimated first-quarter
general-government net lending by DKK 13.9 billion. A DKK 10.9 billion
pension yield tax revision explained part of the change. Its 2026 figures
for several taxes still used ministry estimates. A revision of a
consolidated fiscal statistic cannot confirm that a January payment was
authorized, settled or paid to a resident. The Danish tax agency separately
describes the standard January and May payment deadlines for different
institutions. The law also provides for eligible on-account payments.
Neither a legal deadline nor a statistical revision identifies individual
gross receipts, refunds or final cash availability.

## Decide how to fund the original date

Use constructed units independent of Denmark. A competent Assembly must
fund 4 units of local care, 2 of independent court and election work at the
first cutoff and 4 of continuing protection at the second cutoff. Inherited
unrestricted ordinary sources yield 3 early and 4 later. The additional
recurring source yields 3 by year end, with the original estimate putting it
before the first cutoff. A late path moves all three to the second cutoff.
The independent institutions have usable payment channels and actual crews
if their own funding reaches them on time. The separate local payer must
actually receive its lawful transfer. In every option annual cash excluding
any reserve is 10. Hold operating need constant.

| Exact first-budget choice | Early cash under a three-unit delay | Second cutoff and another year |
|---|---|---|
| Rely on the original forecast | 3 against 6 due | Later 7 covers the remaining 4 and could settle eligible arrears. Harm and original missed dates remain. |
| Prefund the dated gap from an existing lawful public reserve | 3 ordinary + 3 reserved against 6 due | Later 7 covers 4 due and can restore 3 after actual eligible receipt and reconciliation. If the source disappears, there is no restoration and no repeatable solution. |
| Substitute a qualified recurring package with earlier settlement | 3 ordinary + 3 separately costed, legally usable and settled against 6 due | Later 4 covers 4. Secure a new lawful decision, incidence comparison, reliable collection and receiving before relying on this package. |

Select the funded reserve for the stated pure delay only if the amount,
purpose, payment authority and actual receiving are confirmed before the
first cutoff. The reserve already belongs to the authorized public payer.
It has an opportunity cost and cannot pay two simultaneous calls. Apply
the recurring source comparison if the same three units disappear or the
reserve cannot be replenished in time. The narrow use of later actual data
is to update future decisions and settle valid claims. It cannot qualify
past receipt. Preserve ordinary service and remedy scope while dependent
optional expansion waits. AI may reproduce the worksheet and flag revised
dates. The payers and independent reviewers verify actual payment.
