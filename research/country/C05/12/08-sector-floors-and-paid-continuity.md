# WL41–WL46. Sector pay floors with funded care continuity

*Design353 · A candidate route under [WL25](04-independent-collective-power.md).*

**WL41 — Give a sector a route to propose a common floor.** Select
an initial candidate for paid in-home personal assistance under the
ordinary labor, care and budget laws. Compare evidence of poor pay,
unpaid working time, turnover and service loss with alternative
causes such as scheduling, travel, supervision and thin public rates.
A worker or employer association, ordinary public initiative or
Assembly member may propose a statute. Voluntary multi-employer
agreements keep their own lawful scope and ratification. The Assembly
alone can make a proposed floor binding on outsiders by enacting its
exact scope, amounts, working-time definitions and dates. A hearing,
private agreement, executive announcement or failure to object
creates no obligation for outsiders.

**WL42 — Hear the people who would bear it.** The Work Relations
Registrar verifies actual coverage and bargaining mandates, publishes
safe aggregates of worker and employer support against separately
estimated coverage denominators, discloses common-control groupings
and identifies missing populations. The elected committee hears
nonmembers, workers in small providers, direct-hire assistants and
employers, users of assistance and their chosen representatives,
and public commissioners. Provide paid access, an independent route
outside the workplace and usable time to contest scope, competing
terms and burdens. Neither a large chain nor a recognized union
chooses the representatives of outsiders. A public commissioner
discloses its purchasing interest and cannot certify representation.
Published reasons address coverage gaps, objections and viable
alternatives before the Assembly votes. AI may reconcile definitions
or model schedules. Mandates, testimony and decisions belong to
actual people and their authorized institutions.

**WL43 — Enact a wage floor by work done.** The candidate scope
covers employee personal assistance in private homes, including
direct hire, staffing intermediaries, subcontractors and public
employment. Ordinary worker-status and employer tests apply.
Publish covered tasks, mixed-duty attribution, paid handovers,
required work-related travel between assignments and mandatory
training. General pay, time and stronger terms survive. For each
covered hour apply the highest applicable cash minimum, preserve
any separately due premium and record the underlying hours once.
A dispatch label or intermediary cannot erase work. Exclude
price fixing, market division, exclusive representation of outsiders
and compelled membership from the public measure. The employer
remains liable from the effective date. A direct-hire recipient
gets payroll assistance and a separately funded lawful allocation
where eligible; a care recipient's dependence cannot justify a wage waiver.

**WL44 — Legislate a paid transition alongside the new duty.** Before
voting on commencement, publish an independent same-date account
of payroll, public purchase and personal-budget rates, costs for
private payers, qualified staffing, and review capacity. Identify
small or remote providers and what actually replaces their hours if
they leave. Put the new public rate, payable amounts, accepting
operators, early cash and independent relief on the same calendar
as the first lawful payroll. Assign separate authority for each
source and contract. Compare retain and enforce, voluntary
bargaining, the common floor, and direct public receiving for an
unserved route. Select a later date by law if the start cannot be
funded. Once effective, a failed reimbursement or unstaffed
replacement cannot suspend the wage, the existing care award or a
remedy deadline. Carry forward existing contracts, collective terms,
care duties and pending cases under their own law.

**WL45 — Follow the wage and the visit.** The labor inspector verifies
paid time and actual payroll, including outside the dominant
provider, with confidential access and the existing independent
court route for coverage, missing pay or retaliation. The care duty
owner separately checks each owed visit,
skill and usable time, and arranges a paid backup or qualified
public operator when delivery is threatened. Use the existing
advance, protected pay and direct care remedy only within their
actual authority and funded scope. A subsidy, reported roster or
late replacement counts only for what was actually received, on
the actual date. Any funding for an employer remains conditional
on lawful delivery and verification. Public recovery from an
employer stays separate from worker pay and resident protection.

**WL46 — Review before deciding what follows.** For an initial two-year
schedule, fund a public comparison after the first full payroll
quarter and a review at least six months before expiry. Compare
real pay including travel, missed visits, continuity of worker,
turnover, private prices, availability in small markets and paid
transition costs. Disaggregate affected groups and distinguish
changes plausibly caused by the floor from other changes. The
Assembly chooses renewal, revision or lapse. The exact schedule
states its successor minimum and transition. Lapse ends the
temporary statutory increment prospectively under that schedule.
It cannot cancel accrued wages, erase surviving individual or
collective terms, withdraw awarded care or defer a remedy. Earlier
review is warranted if wages rise but essential visits fall or if
the comparison shows scheduling repair would achieve more.
