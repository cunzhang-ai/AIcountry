# Design297. Parallel local trials and shared obligations

10 November 2026 · Research selection · No actual enactment

## Baseline and decision

[Design296](baseline.json) retains local discretion, interlocal
and national residual authority, water limits and funded learning.
The practical question is how independent communities can pursue
different designs without making incompatible promises against the
same source. Apply the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md)
and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md). Codex assisted
research and drafting. No affected council or independent reviewer
has qualified the proposed trial rule.

## Alternatives and selection

- **Run one common programme or use only the ordinary route.** The
  ordinary route preserves scarce capacity and may already meet
  the need. One national programme can improve comparability and
  settle common resources under prior law. It can also exclude
  meaningful local choices or reduce genuine disagreement to a
  uniform intervention.
- **Allow separately authorized compatible local trials.** Select
  this architecture with a shared resource account and review.
  Narrow A and original B can both fit the declared bound and
  budget. Neither inherits permission from the other. The smaller
  A answers a narrower question, and its loss of wider coverage
  remains visible.
- **Sequence full trials or choose a single lawful option.** A
  paid sequence can preserve original scope where the later
  supply and restoration qualify. It may take longer and its
  results may be affected by changed conditions. A single
  trial gives up the other learning opportunity. Keep the
  funded ordinary route available.

[LT01–LT05](../country/C14/03/01-parallel-local-trials-and-shared-obligations.md)
sets the compatibility and authority rule. The [worked comparison](../settlement/parallel-local-trials/README.md)
tests a contested common draw, a smaller scope and an actual
second window. The [sources](sources.md) support distinctions
about rights and shared measurement. All case figures and
permissions are authored.

## Objection, authority and migration

A shared coordination channel could become a veto or favor large
municipalities. Keep directly accessible proposals and challenge,
the existing materiality test, ordinary permits and the Assembly's
reserved decision only where competent local agreement cannot
resolve material common burdens. Protect people outside the two
trial areas and original claims. The trial or evidence record
cannot approve its own change. Keep technical evidence, public
authorization, actual consent and receiving distinct.

On expiry or lawful replacement, preserve rights, wages, records,
accepted cleanup and open remedies. Where an optional scope
ceases to fit, apply only the existing lawful disposition.
Record prior commitments separately from the power to continue.
Share evidence within its scope without adopting another
jurisdiction's result automatically.

## Next bounded decision

check common legal scope, independent source measures, funded
ordinary delivery, affected nonparticipants and real second-window
restoration. Compare compatible narrow, sequenced and ordinary
routes before a live trial. Reopen on changed water, incomparable
effects, systematic exclusion, failed restoration or a qualified
simpler design. Actual authority, readiness and outcomes are open.
