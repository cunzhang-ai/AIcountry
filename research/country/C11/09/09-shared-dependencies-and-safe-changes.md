# DP38–DP42. Keep public control through shared software and AI changes

*Design276 · Proposed release and continued-service rules.*

**DP38 — Identify the actual service version and who can change it.**
For material reliance, record the application and effective
configuration, model or ordinary engine, instructions, evidence
retrieval, permissions, outputs and any shared updater, identity,
host or key that can change behavior or prevent recovery. Include
who can initiate changes and whether the operator can verify,
defer, isolate or restore them. Retain the relevant prior record
and measured limits. A signed release identifies a source. It
does not prove legal behavior or independent recovery. Where
the owner cannot qualify an opaque change path, use it only
within a supported scope that cannot obstruct a protected duty.
Protect supplier rights and data. Compare usable alternatives
without demanding ownership of every upstream component.

**DP39 — Stage changes by actual shared exposure.** The responsible
sector owner authorizes each material adoption after representative
shadow or isolated tests and independent evidence where required.
Set an authorized exposure limit, pass and stop conditions, original
service deadlines and a tested route outside the change path. Count
one updater or recovery controller across primary and backup
systems, even when products have different names. Avoid activating
a common material change in both routes during the same protected
window. Account for other agencies using the same worker, facility
or payment route. Share only the needed dependency and readiness
signals. A coordinator can expose the conflict. Actual sector
officers and elected sources govern commitments. Urgent security
repair uses the earliest safe qualified scope and a real alternate.
An obsolete or compromised build is not a fallback merely because
it can be retained.

**DP40 — Contain affected behavior beyond the first application.**
When a relevant defect is credible, use DP09–DP11 and LX to identify
which dependent uses may share the affected version, configuration,
interval or input transformation. Notify responsible downstream
owners using independently reachable channels. Suspend material
reliance on affected queued instructions, cached assessments and
reused model outputs before any further consequential act until
their lawful basis is verified. Uncertain provenance goes to scoped
ordinary review. Preserve admissible source evidence, first receipt,
existing claims and actually executed decisions. A local shutdown,
model update or technical rollback cannot decide their validity.
A warning about shared risk grants no general cross-sector stop
or access to individual records.

**DP41 — Restore useful service and reconcile the work left behind.**
Before reopening the affected scope, verify real recovery at the
receiving office and compare the correct current and earlier legal
rules. Keep independent access during reconciliation. Account for
pending intake, acted and unacted recommendations, paid claims,
returned payments, appeals and real outages. Exercise an isolated
restart and the actual alternate, including loss of the vendor,
update channel or ordinary login. The responsible owner verifies
unresolved harm and actual receipt with the applicable reviewers.
Restoring a process, version or dashboard leaves the resulting
backlog and missed dates open. Use
[LX](../12-public-rules-and-verifiable-implementation.md) for
lawful reconsideration and distinct remedies.

**DP42 — Make the cost and power to switch visible to elected bodies.**
Put change and recovery authority, approved exposure, paid fallback
and measurable actual service on the same first-budget calendar.
Report material unmatched work and assumptions to the Assembly,
opposition and independent auditors. Preserve earlier permissions,
source restrictions and protections through a government or supplier
change. AI may discover affected dependencies or compare traces.
The public duty owner authorizes release, the receiving office
verifies delivery and independent review remains reachable. Reopen
on repeated common failures, loss of meaningful inspection, slow
security response or a simpler qualified ordinary service.
