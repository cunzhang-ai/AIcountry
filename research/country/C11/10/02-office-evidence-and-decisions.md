# GA07–GA12: a competent decider that can itself be challenged

*Part of [general AI capability](../10-general-ai-capability-and-public-control.md).*

**GA07 — A bounded civilian office.** Establish a General AI Safety Office within
national technical-safety administration, separate from public DP operation,
procurement, research promotion, intelligence and model providers. Assembly law
sets GA scope and duties; the responsible national minister answers for general
policy/budget, with no instruction to decide a named case. The Office makes GA
classification and exposure decisions only, not sector licences, speech policy,
elections, sovereign law or a national master shutdown. This is an explicit new
subject/function, not a discovery of hidden TD12 or DP powers.

**GA08 — Appointment and succession.** C04 open career recruitment publishes
job-related safety/evaluation/legal competence, accessible competition, conflicts
and a qualified shortlist. Cabinet appoints one Director for five nonrenewable
years with public reasons and opposition scrutiny; no provider or model vote.
Removal needs proved serious misconduct, persistent material statutory failure
or inability after accommodation, notice/response and direct ordinary-court
review with interim protection. Disfavored findings or lawful dissent are no
cause; a stayed removal admits no second substantive chief.

Protected career case officers exercise their own statutory classification and
permit powers under published allocation/recusal rules. The Director designates
a qualified deputy in advance; acting routine management ends after180 elapsed
days, without new policy or senior appointments. Cabinet begins recruitment
within14days; repeated relabeling cannot reset it. Officers continue their own
powers if the chief/deputy is absent; unavailable reserved acts remain disclosed,
not transferred to a minister, DP, vendor or autonomous agent. This office-only
appointment rule expressly replaces generic V10 staggered-panel implications.

**GA09 — Assessment is distinct from permission.** The Office assigns competent
external technical assessors through conflict-screened rotation, separately
checking common employer, funding, methods, data and controller. The developer
cannot buy its preferred verdict. Public evaluation capacity and cross-border
cooperation may supply expertise, never presumed foreign access/independence.
No provider's model output or common-source restatement counts as an independent
assessment. The legal decision officer is distinct from the assessor/provider;
actual absence of qualified scrutiny is disclosed and reaches GA12 court review.

**GA10 — Proportionate, inspectable safety evidence.** Before covered exposure,
provide configuration/version and actual proposed acts, severe capability tests,
control/containment limits, foreseeable misuse and common failures, affected
nonusers, safer alternatives, access/security, incident/withdrawal arrangements
and responsible paid people. Distinguish observations, assumptions, uncertainty
and value judgments; record failed tests and material objections. Reuse evidence
only within its complete valid scope after checking new events. Capability-
changing tools, permissions, learning, replication or distribution need fresh
review of the change and dependencies, not automatic review of all old work.

Assessors need lawful secure access sufficient to test claims and inspect
material original results; a glossy summary is insufficient. Independent testing
must itself protect nonparticipants. No forced public release of dangerous
methods, personal data, private testimony or weights. Preserve PD/KR custody,
defense access, correction and bounded retention; explain lawful withholding.

**GA11 — An actual risk judgment, not proof of perfection.** Authorize the stated
act/configuration only where demonstrated, proportionate barriers reasonably
control identified GA02 pathways, considering residual severity/uncertainty,
misuse feasibility, benefits, distribution and safer usable alternatives.
Refusal/conditions require specific evidence that the proposed exposure leaves
an inadequately controlled severe pathway; missing certainty alone is insufficient.
An average benchmark, compute number, insurance policy or self-issued certificate
is also insufficient. Publish reasons, limitations, dissent and safe evidence;
protect contested secrets with meaningful court/independent counsel access.
No Office can declare one political worldview safe or infer danger from criticism.

**GA12 — Finite, challengeable decisions.** Decide a scope request within30 and a
permission request within90 elapsed days of protected first receipt. Requests for
necessary evidence are reasoned and proportionate; incompleteness does not reset
or pause either clock. The Office decides on available evidence, with the stated
burdens, and identifies what would change an adverse outcome. No approval by
silence and no unreviewable permanent provisional hold. Applicant and materially
affected nonusers can reach ordinary courts directly, including delay, scope,
evidence, merits, conditions and useful-time interim relief without exhausting
internal review. The court can substitute permission/refusal/conditions on the
record or order a finite competent determination; it cannot fabricate expertise.
Where neither competent judgment nor safe narrower exposure is available, the
specific covered exposure remains unperformed and the capacity conflict public;
contained lawful work and actual unrelated services continue. Courts, counsel
and technical assistance require GA21 money, not just a right to file.
