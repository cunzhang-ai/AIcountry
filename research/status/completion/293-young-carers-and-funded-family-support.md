# Design385. Give young carers a right to stop and receive support

[YCR01–YCR07](../../country/C07/07/01-young-carers-and-funded-family-support.md)
gives children confidential access, a separately funded adult
care route and educational recovery. It protects voluntary
relationships and applies child-protection grounds separately.

[The worked household and primary records](../../settlement/young-carers-and-funded-family-support/01-before-school-and-after-bedtime.md)
test first and recurring visits, individual choice and a distinct
school remedy. Actual household participation, legal authority,
qualified staffing and improved lives remain open.
