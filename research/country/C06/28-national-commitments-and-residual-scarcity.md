# C06. National commitments, dated expansion and residual scarcity

3November2026 · Design162 · CP01–CP16 research, not enacted allocation power

The country cannot infer usable production from available cash, implement all
new guarantees on an unsupported common date or leave surprise scarcity to an
undefined future rule. Select **dated elected expansion with protected existing
rights and a bounded temporary public-stock/logistics rule**. Reject an unconditional
simultaneous launch and hidden growth, unpaid work or executive rationing as
balancing items. This is not selection of an affordable operating country.
