# Design350 decision on usable provider switching

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design349, B04, existing competition and consumer duties, CN08,
privacy and the working framework. Existing law in the design covers
contractual exit, sector portability and competition remedies. Add a
prospective common transfer duty for specifically designated household
services, including responsibility for actual cutover, authorized data
and distinct billing correction.

Select a named receiving coordinator with the incumbent's continued
duties until safe receipt or a separately chosen end. Apply tighter
sector rules where they govern. Elected bodies designate scope and
authority, existing sector bodies implement it, and courts and independent
review retain their mandates. The project assistant authored the choice.

## Alternatives and objections

Portability alone can reduce paperwork with limited provider burden.
It can leave an unresolved failed handoff. A universal switching service
can simplify access and concentrate control and failure. Select sector
coordination with independent fallback where needed. Keep simpler
individual cancellation and ordinary bilateral agreements where they
provide equivalent access and continuity.

The [official comparators](sources.md) supply sector-specific examples of
coordinated switching, consent, compensation and implementation failure.
The one-working-day acknowledgment is a candidate. Smaller providers can
face disproportionate costs. Verify usable access and interoperability
and compare a lighter route on the same service and rights.

## Transition and reopening

Fund competent help, review, fallback and transfer of current cases.
Keep valid claims, stronger sector protections and existing periods
under their own law. Actual adoption, affected participation, qualified
independent review and operational results remain open.

Test a failed switch and a separately unverified mandate with households,
small and large operators, and an independent reviewer. Measure actual
service, data scope, overlap cost and access to correction. Reopen on
unauthorized transfers, repeat interruption, excessive burden or
a better feasible route.
