## Situation and competing explanations

The previous Finnish preliminary citation was too thin for present reuse. A
later original author abstract materially distinguishes income from the
conditionality mechanism; see [sources](sources.md) for exact scope/access
limits and the shared original experiment. Do not average sources or count
publisher/Kela/VATT copies as replications. No participant records or testimony
were collected. Full methods and original foreign law were not independently
inspected. This is qualified analysis, not a complete systematic evidence review.

IC already protects extra needs and earned rights, but ordinary cash migration
left an avoidable ambiguity: could someone lose independently justified Y06
credit merely by ceasing an unneeded means-tested application? Distinguish the
value of unconditional personal security, the empirical effect of extra income,
actual eligibility and actual finance. A simpler application is no service,
funded lifetime pension or proof of democratic consent.

## Alternatives, disagreement and selected disposition

1. **Retain all wording:** avoids churn, but leaves outdated support and a
   cash/credit gateway ambiguity. Reject this narrow no-change option.
2. **Treat U as automatic credit for everyone:** simple, but adds new lifetime
   promises without eligibility/source choices, and makes work/default matching
   unsafe. Reject; a different pension architecture needs an explicit proposal.
3. **Require ongoing cash claims to obtain credit:** administratively familiar,
   but forces unnecessary applications and can disrupt actual eligible periods.
   Reject this gateway where qualification is independently established.
4. **Separate ordinary cash, qualified credits, services and fund receipt:**
   select IC17–IC20; preserve actual qualification, old rights and direct appeal.

Reliable adequate targeted protection with the same services/earned rights
remains a genuine structural rival to conditional U+C2. Retain U's explicitly
defeasible independence preference and A/I05's access-first operating reference;
neither is an empirical winner. An objection that U's extra universal expense
crowds out actual service/credit delivery remains unresolved. Conversely,
accurate targeting can still impose intrusive/time costs. Affected people have
not chosen this tradeoff. No aggregate welfare score resolves it.
