# Delegated law: one member can require a real elected decision

*DR09–DR12; part of [C03/11](../11-delegated-rules-and-effective-public-control.md).*

**DR09 — Thirty complete calendar days, not sitting days.** Publication date L
is day0. A member may call an exact routine instrument through the end of
**L+30**, identifying the provision and a reason concerning policy, scope,
evidence, burden or procedure. Any one actual elected member qualifies,
including governing members; no chair, party leader, sponsor count, ideological
or account test. An authorized agent can deliver that member's exact instruction,
not supply an additional mandate. The Clerk records it on receipt; a disputed
reason cannot be screened out for political merit. Direct court/assisted access
preserves a timely blocked attempt. An uncalled routine rule cannot have ordinary
effect before **L+31**, and still requires actual lawful making/signature by
its named issuer and authenticated prospective commencement. Ordinary actual
making is permitted only on L+31..45; a material/called rule must first have its
actual approval. The signed final packet is not this making act. Silence is not an
Assembly vote; it only leaves the Act's narrow routine delegation available.

**DR10 — Material or called means affirmative.** Every material instrument,
and every timely called routine instrument, requires **floor(N/2)+1 actual yes
votes and at least that many members present**, where N is full statutory
Assembly membership including vacancies. This is a special delegated-instrument
rule, not a new voting rule for all primary legislation. Quorum, abstentions,
recusal and vacancies cannot lower it. No chair casting vote, proxy inference,
AI majority or an expert certificate supplies missing votes. The exact final
text is voted approve/reject; amendments return to DR12 or primary legislation.
Approval may occur only **L+31 through L+45 inclusive**. Ordinary effect is no
earlier than L+31, the published start, and the calendar day after actual
approval/making. End-of-day L+45 without valid approval lapses a material/called proposal;
no new legal effect arises from backlog, missing quorum or refusal to vote.
Every ordinary proposal also lapses if its named issuer has not lawfully made
the exact rule by end L+45; approval creates no unlimited later issuance window.

**DR11 — Protected agenda has a failure path.** The Clerk lists a timely call
within7calendar days of receipt, independently of committee permission. Material
packets receive the same protected listing within7days of L. Publish the exact
motion/scrutiny packet at least7calendar days before an affirmative vote.
Standing law reserves a scrutiny sitting at noon on L+45 at the Assembly's
lawful place/authorized alternate, with earlier votes possible on L+31..45
after notice. All due items retain separate motions; a giant bundle cannot
conceal unrelated burdens. The Speaker presides, then the lawful Deputy, then
a published rotation drawn by the Clerk in an open auditable lot among all
consenting nonminister elected members at the Assembly's first sitting, with
no party/Chair shortlist. For an already sitting Assembly at commencement,
the Clerk draws before commencement (or within14days if legally impossible
before); no draw/available lawful presider means no fallback sitting. Publish
order/conflicts, use the next available unconflicted member, never reset the
order for a preferred motion. This role supplies no extra substantive vote.
Clerk records and convenes mechanically, never votes or legislates.
The issuer cannot cancel listing, notice or reserved time. No available lawful
presider, venue, members, source access or time means no affirmative decision,
not a fictional sitting or emergency. Courts can enforce these duties and
protect existing rights but cannot cast the substantive vote.

**DR12 — One conversion, no endless personal renewal.** A call converts a
proposal once; many calls may share its hearing while preserving distinct
reasons. Withdrawal by the first caller does not undo another timely call.
Once validly approved, the unchanged substance cannot be suspended anew by a
single member; ordinary repeal, court review and new evidence remain available.
Any operative post-L change withdraws and republishes a complete packet with
fresh L, except an expressly identified clerical correction that cannot change
meaning/effect; even that correction preserves prior versions/reasons and is
reviewable. New material effects also reopen the affected mandatory consultation.
Renaming a called/lapsed proposal does not restore routine status: substantially
the same proposal needs affirmative approval after a fresh lawful process.
New information materially changing tier, affected scope or the proposal's
support requires an updated packet/fresh L and any newly necessary consultation,
even without textual amendment. Check material new events before effect; no
review of stale premises silently transfers. A real policy/effect change,
not its author's label, determines what is new.
