# C04. Effective take-up and early benefit delivery

*Design254 · TU01–TU17 · Proposed public administration and benefit duties.*

**The public office must make a due benefit reachable without making
private life legible to the whole state.** Use the least burdensome
lawful route for each entitlement. Release established dues with
verified evidence. Offer safe assisted confirmation where material facts
or choices remain. Preserve direct access, useful-time help and actual
delivery for people who are absent from official records.

1. [Who starts the work and on what authority](12/01-entitlement-start-and-safe-choice.md)
   distinguishes due payments, assisted claims and ordinary outreach.
2. [Evidence, correction and useful receipt](12/02-proof-correction-and-delivery.md)
   assigns protected proof, human decisions, payment and remedy.
3. [Finance, outside control and change](12/03-source-review-and-continuity.md)
   tests the whole population reached, protected staffing and future changes.
4. [Independent payment and receipt](12/04-independent-payment-and-receipt.md)
   keeps established cash accessible without an account or proxy and assigns a funded backup.
