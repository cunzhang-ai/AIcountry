# A move with a real deadline for each person

*Design201 · Authored paired scenario. Costs are illustrative planning units.*

## Same people, same obligations

An adult and child arrive in a neighboring area on a school day
after an unsafe move. A known assistance plan requires two
competent workers to be present from 18:00 to 20:00. A separately
authorized clinician must confirm how necessary medication will be
available before 20:30. The child needs an accessible school route
for the next scheduled school day at 09:00. The ordinary home
cannot safely receive them that night. These times and resources
are authored assumptions. The adults and child have distinct
rights and choices. A shared request can describe the problem
without giving every agency their clinical notes or safe address.

| Duty | Immediate lawful owner and evidence of receiving | If the first option fails |
|---|---|---|
| Safe lodging | The competent host housing or survivor service arranges an accessible accepting place and protected contact. Preserve any original housing claim | Record unavailable lodging and seek the lawful funded sector fallback. Sending the address to an unsafe person fails the response |
| Assistance | Origin's existing duty and contracts continue in their scope. Host care receiver verifies that two named, qualified workers can attend and the person consents | Activate the authorized funded alternate before the need, or record the uncovered visit and pursue immediate protection and review |
| Clinical continuity | Receiving clinician verifies consent, dose, safe supply and handoff with necessary records and pharmacy or clinical staff | Use the earlier clinical escalation rule and an actual alternative. A successful care visit does not prove medication receipt |
| Education | Keep the original school if a safe return trip and support work and the family chooses it. Otherwise a competent host accepts a provisional place by the first scheduled school day | Provide a lawful supported interim teaching path where feasible, keep the original date and record any missed learning |

With a planned move, the strongest retained arrangement can use
the origin team and agreed host relationships without extra national
payment. Test that path first if actual geography, safety, consent
and staff commitments fit. In the sudden case, the origin team
cannot safely reach the new place by 18:00 and no council has
accepted final liability. The host can start an eligible service
before the payer dispute is resolved under the proposed law.
No official need decide that the family moved permanently to
authorize this interim response.

## Follow the service cost through a dispute

An authorized provider offers the full assistance episode at 240,
with confirmed eligible financing needed for acceptance. Assume the
origin's final ordinary share would be 180 if its existing liability
is confirmed, and a separately enacted national schedule covers the
additional 60. These are hypothetical figures, with no fee imposed
on the family. Other services and overlap have separate scopes.

| State of the payer decision | Authorized provisional cash | What remains after actual reconciliation |
|---|---|---|
| Known and available source | Origin pays 180 and national payer pays 60 | Provider receives 240 once |
| Origin share disputed and advance expressly covers 240 | National payer pays 240 to the accepted receiver before the applicable acceptance deadline | If origin liability for 180 is established and recovery actually arrives, the national net cost for this episode is 60. The 180 transfer settles liability and is not a second service |
| Only 150 of usable authorized advance and no other cash or accepting arrangement | Full 240 is unavailable by the necessary time | Gap is 90. Seek lawful capacity or source correction. A court or model cannot assume payment or order beyond its actual authority |
| National paid 240 but origin owes no part | No assumed recovery | Name the actual liable source under the prior decision and the law. If no other recovery is available, the national cost stays 240 |

If payment authorization is available and the provider lacks staff,
the service still fails. Two residents each need two qualified
workers in the same two-hour window. Four workers and eight worker-
hours are required in this scenario. One two-person standby team
offers four worker-hours and can meet at most one of the two episodes.
No second registration or invoice creates capacity. Check any lawful
staggering against safe time and each person's actual need. Use
applicable prior safety and scarcity rules and retain the unserved
claim if no funded safe alternative exists.

A shared identity or payment outage requires a paid independent
route. If a prior case number is inaccessible, protect the earliest
request with usable evidence and verify at the relevant authority.
Protect the child's school support and the adult's consent and safe
contact separately. If relief is later granted, record actual
delivery and any late or continuing loss under SR. One recovered
payment does not prove that care or learning was received.
