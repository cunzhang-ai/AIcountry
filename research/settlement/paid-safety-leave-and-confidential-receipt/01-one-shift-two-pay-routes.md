# One protected shift, two pay routes and a separate service

*Design386 · Constructed comparison with [primary records](../../2026-11-paid-safety-leave-and-confidential-receipt/sources.md).*

A worker needs four scheduled hours on day 2 to arrange safe
receiving with an advocate. The pay rate is 2 units per hour.
Actual ordinary pay is due by day 4. One replacement shift
costs 3, with its own authorized funds and qualified worker.
The safety service separately confirms actual receiving on
day 2. The worker has enough remaining leave. The ordinary
employer route is safe in one case and unsafe in another.

| Path | Result |
|---|---|
| Ordinary leave and private payroll | Protect the four hours, pay 8 by day 4 and fund the separate replacement. Keep the leave reason out of ordinary records. |
| Employer payroll and contact unsafe | A protected outside receiver confirms absence before the shift. Eight settled public units pay by day 4 through an independent safe route. Keep the employer's lawful liability and independent cover separate. |
| Public payment sent to an unsafe account or arriving on day 5 | There is no certified safe on-time receipt. The original pay duty and direct correction continue. |
| Only seven public units or an unfunded substitute | Seven does not discharge eight. An otherwise lawful leave still protects the worker's time. An uncovered duty or shift remains visible. |
| Paid leave but no service available | Leave and lawful pay continue. The housing, care or safety route is still an open duty. |

The first advance uses eight units of actual public funds before
a disputed employer recovery on day 9. If another eight is due
before that recovery, it needs a separately available source.
Eight paid to the worker through one route is eight received,
even if two institutions have related account entries. No
reimbursement or added liability is assumed in the worker's pay.
This is a timing and privacy comparison, without a national
cost estimate or claim that the person has become safe.
