# Optional monitoring and standing income support

This constructed comparison exercises
[DP58–DP63](../../country/C11/09/13-meaningful-choice-and-valid-assent.md).
Twenty people have established income support due on the same date.
The service separately offers optional personal monitoring to tailor
reminders. The device, staff and ordinary payment routes are assumed
lawful where used within scope. None of the 20 needs to consent to
monitoring to receive the payment.

The former process counted each completed contact as permission.
Evidence shows 12 distinct informed authorizations. Five people gave
no response to the optional offer and three explicitly declined. The
common count of 20 therefore includes eight unsupported permissions.
The correction stops optional use for those eight, checks any previous
disclosure and preserves all 20 awards and their original due dates.

| Response | Valid monitoring permissions | Standing payments by original due date |
|---|---:|---:|
| Keep a completion-based permission count | 12 of 20 recorded | Independently due in all 20 cases |
| Separate the choice and use the funded ordinary route | 12, with distinct proof | 20 received if the staffed and electronic routes both work |
| Same choice with one two-person staffed appointment lost | 12, with distinct proof | 18 received and two still owed |

In a separate choice of payment channel, 12 residents select an
independently verified direct route and eight select staffed receiving.
The channel never depends on monitoring permission. Staffed receiving needs four
units to fund four appointments with two-person capacity per
appointment in the declared case. Two more units pay independent
review and contact correction. A loss of one appointment leaves six
staffed places and exposes two due cases. Funding for principal is
separate. Redirected users retain their original dates and receive
useful-time help to reach the authorized alternative. An unstaffed
alternative leaves a visible breach.

An authorized automatic payment stays due independently of an optional
choice. A representative with authority to ask about a payment has
no authority to permit monitoring. The [comparison check](verify-public-assent.py)
checks the distinct acts and capacity. Actual permissions, independence,
costs, service experience and privacy remain open.
