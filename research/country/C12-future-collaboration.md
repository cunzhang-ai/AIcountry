These parts form one canonical C12 chapter. The [integrated design](../COUNTRY-DESIGN.md)
and [status register](../DESIGN-STATUS.md) hold cross-chapter commitments.

[NI public national initiatives](C03/16-public-national-initiatives.md) now
links human/AI-assisted public proposals to protected ordinary parliamentary
consideration, actual source/capacity and fresh lawful decisions, not account votes.

[Parallel local trials](C14/03/01-parallel-local-trials-and-shared-obligations.md)
shows how communities keep distinct proposals, objections and
media while matching any concurrent commitments under actual law.
Evidence can converge while the competent decisions remain separate.

[Scoped evidence correction](C11/11/03-scoped-evidence-correction-across-decisions.md) follows
a material correction through shared source families and local interpretations.
Each competent owner decides within its actual authority.

[CM01–CM05. Civic media and democratic replacement](C12/05-civic-media-and-democratic-replacement.md)
adds the current rules for replacing a medium while public choices,
dissent and local obligations remain live.
