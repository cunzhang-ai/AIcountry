# Publisher evidence, new cautions and original-observation limits

Inspected 3 November 2026. These are original publisher pages/summaries, not raw
observation reanalysis or three independent tests of a national constitution.

[OECD, Modernising Access to Social Protection](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/modernising-access-to-social-protection_af31746d-en.html),
28 May 2024, abstract/executive summary: access can fail despite formal coverage;
linked data/simplified enrolment can help but require privacy/inclusion safeguards.
This stocktaking does not identify our I05/U winner, rates or national savings.
No quoted take-up estimate is transferred; underlying cited trials/raw data
were not reanalyzed. Navigation dates in2026 are not the report's publication date.

[Eurofound, Digitalisation of social protection](https://www.eurofound.europa.eu/en/publications/all/digitalisation-social-protection),
28 October 2025, introduction/method and key findings: atypical users can be left
out; evidence on how expected released human/financial resources were actually
used is thin. The report uses literature, correspondents and desk research.
This supports qualifying a redeployment assumption, not claiming no digital
savings exist or that human-only systems are better. The full PDF/raw country
inputs were not reanalyzed; publisher page scope is explicit.

[World Bank, World Development Report 2017: Governance and the Law](https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/wdr2017),
publisher About/Main Messages: institutional functions and unequal power shape
implementation, entry and exclusion. Used as a diagnostic framing, not causal
validation of AIcountry, a particular board size or a simple coordination model.
The full book's underlying observations were not newly inspected.

OECD/Eurofound may share national administrative accounts/literature; different
organizations are not assumed independent original evidence. The World Bank
summary synthesizes other work. The current reasoning is same-controller AI,
with no actual affected participation or qualified independent final review.

The ten design 85 study reports remain historical support with their own source,
setting/revision/counterevidence limits. No fresh blanket validity claim or
positive effect estimate from them is reused to approve the changed package.
Current SG/FP/AP, ordinary/adverse/paid paths and existing laws were checked for
compatibility; the comparison creates no territory, financing or source-law power.
No actual whole-model costs, staff redeployment, causal wellbeing or democratic
operation were observed. New events, source corrections and material objections
must be checked again at a consequential final decision.
