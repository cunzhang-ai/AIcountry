The [inputs](inputs.json), [model](model.py) and [results](results.json) test only
fixture arithmetic, schema/scope and bounded time conditions. They are not

## A. Care without an app is a real receiving task

Authored online intake takes 0.25 staff-hour. This person's assisted intake 1.5 plus
interpretation 0.5 requires 2 hours before clinical work; only 1 is available.
Gap 1remains. A paper/offline receipt preserves claim/date and urgency but supplies
no clinician or completed care. No compulsory app, AI training or unpaid relative
fills it. The comparison proves neither that apps are bad nor that assisted
entry is free; language/skills and safe shifts need their paid C06 source.

## B. Source correction is not a corrected housing decision

A hypothetical wrongly linked mental-health proxy produced an adverse housing
score. Source repair 1 h, feature/model review 2 h, housing redecision 1 h and independent
challenge 1 h total 5 h. Available task-specific hours are 1/1/2/1: also 5 h, yet feature
review is 1hshort. The extra housing hour is not automatically a qualified model
reviewer. Only the source node is declared completed; three stages remain.
A completed-stage flag inconsistent with this same-window capacity is rejected,
not counted as a cure. Actual corrected model, redecision and remedy remain null.
A real finding of faster work would need a reinterpreted case/evidence, not
pretending a source edit or row deletion proves every downstream loss repaired.

## C. A minimal threshold proof is not an earned pension

An invented “over 65” threshold-only pilot costs issuer 8 CU, verifier 5 and first-
year audit/training 2: named first-year subset 15. Required paper/assisted fallback
cost P is unknown, so full example cost remains null, not 15. The age is a
hypothetical question, not a change to Y eligibility. Person-specific earned
units, payee, underlying evidence and actual receipt remain separate unknowns.
Changing the claim to citizenship, pension entitlement or clinical need cannot
inherit this pilot's cost/meaning. Fewer fields or a signature prove no anonymity,
current consent, true fact or institutional authorization. Issuer/phone failure
must leave a paid assisted route rather than an automatic claim denial.

## D. One vendor, several distinct protection duties

One forensic core 40 h plus health response 30, benefit response 30 and coordination 15

## E. A personal agent does not get a second purpose by copying itself

The declared booking-example mandate covers booking and listed symptoms to the
chosen clinician. Scope-only classification puts the requested booking inside,
and archive training/insurer disclosure outside. Actual mandate validity and
data basis are still null. A known training action cannot be inserted into the
same booking version to authorize a new purpose; new semantics need a new case.
A declared already-valid booking is not erased by deleting the chat; that does
not prove received care or model unlearning. Direct/assisted alternative 0.75 h
has a separate paid requirement, not an invented AI productivity saving.

## F. Routing does not restart a controller request or urgent protection

UTC fixture: first usable request 1 November 10:00, referral 20 November. The ordinary
30-calendar-day due is 1 December 10:00, not 20 December. A5 December answer misses it.
One genuinely complex, timely reasoned-notice extension could move the assumed
outer due to 31 December; the model checks only declared flags/time, not notice
content, receipt or legal validity. Earlier-than-trigger events do not count as
this fixture's response. Additional extensions, new 31-day rules or a changed
legal clock need fresh interpretation rather than automatic review reuse.
Urgent protection is due 1 November 12:00 but given 15:00: it fails separately,
even if a later ordinary answer could fall within an extended outer period.
B16 working-day/holiday/jurisdiction calendars are not computed by this UTC case.

## G.72 hours is not permission to leave a person unprotected

Reasonable awareness 1 November 08:00, regulator and affected notice 4 November 08:00
meet the fixture's 72-hour outer bound. Useful protection due 1 November 10:00 was
given 13:00, so timely protection fails. Without-undue-delay, safe content, actual
notice receipt and clean recovery are not qualified. Earlier generic warnings
are not silently counted as notice of this event. Actual material-risk grounds,
phased notices, identities and protected recipients need their own lawful facts.

These examples select no superior country or universal privacy technology.
Independent source observations, actual affected choice, paid skills, lawful
authority and received remedies remain necessary for stronger claims.
