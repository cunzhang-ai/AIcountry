# PS21–PS26. Choose the last public backup source before the crisis

Design166 · [Physical supply](../37-physical-supply-and-paid-trade.md)
· Proposed ordinary resource law, not emergency authority or a safe supply plan

**PS21 — A narrow standing source rule, not a larger CP pool.** Select an ordinary
Critical Public Backup Power Act for temporary generators, mobile batteries,
associated fuel and qualified connection/operation expressly listed in advance
under actual public ownership/control. The Act identifies source/territory,
public employer/qualified duty operator, original release/title conditions,
covered critical tasks, payment, scrutiny and prospective lawful commencement.
It must expressly vary any affected public-use earmark through competent law;
a source registry or minister's label cannot do that itself. Actual private,
foreign, leased/restricted or separately owned assets keep their release/contract
terms and accepted consent/lawful compensation rules. No general grid-wide demand
cut, private taking, compelled work, tax/debt, pension sweep or military mission
follows. Protected resources outside this exact schedule retain CP's exclusions.
This is separate source-specific ordinary law anticipated by A8/E11/PS12, not
renewal of CP's exceptional24/72-hour allocation or an RX declaration.

**PS22 — Establish genuine joint infeasibility.** Before diverting a covered
source, identify concurrent actual tasks, minimum useful loads/energy, location,
connection/transport/startup/switching, fuel/charge, competent operation and latest
safe interruption times. Use credible original current observations and actual
sector competence, not a site label or model risk rank. First remove legally
deferrable public comfort/administrative loads, use compatible partial/sequential
supply and seek actual accepted alternatives under their lawful source terms.
Do not call an uncharged battery, unsafe connector, unaccepted crew or promised
foreign generator an available substitute. No duty requires futile delay before
imminent harm; explain why a feasible narrower route cannot work in useful time.
Technical safety isolation/cascade prevention retains actual TS/TD/PS grounds;
this statute does not compel unsafe switching or override ecological/source law.

**PS23 — Apply the rule through existing qualified public operation.** The
existing public power/source employer designates qualified duty officers through
C04 merit/protected careers and advance source/territory/relief assignments.
The assigned public electricity duty operator decides/executes the particular
safe source/load/run under the Act. Treating clinical duty officers supply scoped
clinical facts; actual election/court/other task custodians supply their original
access/record/deadline facts. Seek both accounts where useful time permits;
no unavailable second signature vetoes an otherwise established lawful urgent
act. Disclose missing/conflicted support and obtain actual unimplicated help
when reachable. The advance conflict/relief scheme uses an actually qualified
preassigned unimplicated alternate where available; an accused actor cannot
select its own-case reviewer or control sole evidence keys. Without an actual
alternate, disclose failed independence and preserve only own lawful urgent
protection, not a fictional replacement. RC coordinates requests/access, not
clinical or grid sovereignty.
Neither an incumbent, provider, custodian nor AI gains a general veto, patient
choice, reserved clinical act or public mandate from holding the source.

**PS24 — A stated value priority when compatibility really fails.** For this
covered source, select feasible prevention of credible imminent death or
irreversible grave bodily harm first, including concrete bodily danger from
lost care/legal protection or an actual physical cascade. The electricity must
actually be capable of preventing that harm in time. Next prevent imminent
irreversible loss of sole original ballot/evidence records or an actual expiring
political/remedy opportunity; then protect recoverable essential interruption.
Use the minimum sufficient actual task bundle, preserving every compatible load.
A foreseeable but speculative chain about national security, future prosperity
or policy success is no first-tier bodily claim. This is a provisional elected-
value choice for this source, not science proving that survival always defeats
freedom, nor permission to suspend other rights for an aggregate gain.

Within comparable clinical needs, competent clinicians apply S04's published
urgency/expected benefit/patient will; the electrical officer does not allocate
named treatments. Across genuinely comparable urgency, protective effect and
displacement use a protected recorded neutral draw, or safe rotation only where
no indivisible task is harmed. Do not rank by institutional prestige, current
possession, party, wealth, account/model count, age/disability social worth or
first arrival. Actual relevant clinical facts remain distinct from those proxies.
Unlike value conflicts cannot be averaged into one AI or monetary welfare score.

**PS25 — The hard choice really changes who receives power.** If the sole usable
scheduled generator can avert an imminent patient's death or preserve a sole
expiring election opportunity, with neither compatible sequence nor actual safe
substitute, the bodily-protection task receives it under PS24. The lost political
opportunity remains a grave democratic delivery failure; a lawful source order
or later remedy does not show both duties were fulfilled. Conversely, if an
actual clinical task safely tolerates10minutes and5minutes can preserve the sole
ballot originals with actual safe switching, preserve them then supply care.
Those are authored timing conditions, not universal medical/engineering margins.
A hospital name is no perpetual priority for its office lights or parking system.
If powering neither task can avert its harm, do not claim a rescue benefit that
does not exist; preserve actual achievable protection and disclose the failure.

**PS26 — Source allocation is no authority over people or a mandate.** Patient
will, ordinary necessary-care permission, force/search, custody/clinical ceilings,
private testimony, ecological duties and actual protected constitutional content
remain. A source choice cannot authorize torture, arbitrary killing, source tampering or allocation for the purpose of suppressing
evidence/opposition, extra detention, a guessed vote, poll postponement, changed
franchise/count/appointment/term or a new executive policy power. Preserve actual
originals wherever actually feasible and record any foreseen unavoidable loss;
knowing an incidental loss is not the same as an allocation made to suppress it.
The order neither proves nor immunizes K12/K23 fault; P04 necessity, prior
sabotage, proportionality and fair proof retain actual independent adjudication.
No source priority itself authorizes withdrawal of an existing indispensable
treatment, deliberate killing or a reserved clinical act; actual clinical law
and P04 limits still apply. Switching away from an already relied-on necessary
life-sustaining function requires its separate clinical/C08/UA10 basis and
process; calling it generator movement creates no withdrawal/futility/consent
exception or unlimited right to one particular device. Pursue usable alternate
political/remedy access;
never invent it. PS may authorize temporary powered-task unavailability without an
already working substitute under the coordinated RX04 qualification. It cannot
certify the constitutional legality of every consequence or declare practical
opposition/remedy delivered. A8's protected content and actual competent review
can reject a particular act or this proposed exception; relabeling it rescue
cannot cure that result. Actual current source law/holds remain until adoption.
