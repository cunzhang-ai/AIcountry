# Correction, cross-boundary responsibility and acute protection

*Part of [AQ clean air](../24-clean-air-and-cumulative-exposure.md).*

**AQ04 — Assign a funded correction when an area exceeds a limit.**
The existing environmental authority is the duty owner, including
when no individual source is found in violation. After verification
of a binding exceedance, publish the affected places, expected
harm, available immediate protection and interim responsibilities
within 15 calendar days. Within 90 calendar days, the competent
authorities must adopt a funded correction plan within their powers
or identify exact missing authority, protection still due and the
decision the elected body must take. Earlier useful protection or
a shorter applicable law prevails. Review actual progress at least
quarterly until verified correction. Test the quickest feasible
lawful reduction against real capacity and protect those with high
exposure and fewer alternatives. A missed date is a reviewable
failure. A submitted plan does not cure an exceedance.

**AQ05 — Match each control to its source, place and transition.**
The plan identifies accountable measures for public and private
sources, lead times, costs, actual operators, expected change at
affected sites and independently measured receipt. Compare repair
and cleaner provision with traffic, land-use, fuel and operating
changes. Check whether a measure shifts pollution, jobs, prices
or necessary journeys into another place or onto poorer residents.
Within an enacted class the environmental decision-maker may
impose prospective revised operating conditions supported by
evidence of a controllable contribution, workable alternatives
and proportionate burden. Give notice, a hearing and direct
independent review. Use the separate rights and correction rules
for contracts and compensation. Protect essential heating, paid work transitions
and reachable care while changing sources. Public money pays
only duties and justified assistance authorized under its own law.
Polluter payments, prior contractual obligations and liability
retain their own grounds. Do not promise a transport, heating
or power substitute without the actual capacity and source.

**AQ06 — Keep responsibility across a boundary.** Use the existing
national environmental authority to coordinate shared air regions
defined by actual transport and sources. Local bodies retain their
lawful choices of means. The national authority owns the shared
evidence and default preparation and correction duty where no
competent joint arrangement accepts it. For a material conflict
beyond its enacted powers, bring the exact allocation and funding
question to the Assembly with affected local and upstream/downstream
participation. Fund national requirements under the applicable
funded local mandate law. Do not let one locality export its burden by moving a
route or monitor. Cross-border notice, source control and shared
work use actual treaty, consent and payment routes. No domestic
plan orders a foreign operator or assumes an outside contribution.

**AQ07 — Act on harmful episodes with usable alternatives.** The
local public-health and civil-protection officers use a preauthorized
funded response for a serious episode, including smoke, dust or a
release, based on observed conditions or a qualified imminent risk.
Give accessible warnings, verify receiving sites, filtered space,
transport, safe work changes and people who cannot travel or hear
the alert. Join the heat, power, care and communications duties.
Where weather or an external source limits local control, preserve
the actual readings and protection needs even if enacted law
treats the episode differently in a specified compliance account.
Notice sent and actual protection remain separate. Any compulsory
restriction uses its existing narrow law, necessary scope, expiry
and independent review. A warning alone cannot supply that power.
