# ED11–ED15. A real place, bounded discipline and usable correction

*Part of [ED public learning](../21-public-learning-and-school-power.md).* 

**ED11 — Public money cannot buy selective exclusion.** Local education
administration gives each S05 claimant an actual named reachable public place
or freely chosen equivalent lawful route, with necessary travel/support under
existing rights. Publicly funded additional schools meet the same access,
core/no-extra-fee, disability/language, worker and accountability conditions.
No donation, parent interview, party/faith pledge, AI behavior score or hidden
ability gate selects foundational pupils. Published lawful relevant need,
continuity and travel/geographic priorities may allocate oversubscribed sites;
remaining equal claims use an inspectable fair draw, with independent challenge.
This is a default rights/entry candidate, not a best statistical matching rule
or guarantee of every preferred site. S07 scarce advanced-path selection and
private unfunded institution law remain separate.

**ED12 — A site is not the entitlement.** No head/provider can expel a child
from S05 or make the household find and finance a substitute. Necessary
accommodation, learning/support assessment, safe safeguarding and staff/other
pupils' protection coexist. A reasoned safe transfer may change the named
place only through lawful local allocation, child voice, actual accepted
qualified receiving/support and proportionate travel, with independent appeal.
No dumping route, punitive segregation or compulsory home isolation counts as
an equivalent place. Actual unavailability stays an unmet public duty, not
misconduct by the child or permission for unpaid parental teaching/care.

**ED13 — Initial safety restriction is narrow.** For specific evidenced grave
immediate risk at a named school site, the head may temporarily restrict that
site's attendance for at most2calendar days from first exclusion, with immediate
safe supervision/receiving arrangements, accessible reasons, child voice where
safe and prompt notice to local education administration and the outside
inspector. This is a civil site-protection power, not detention, punishment or
a new broad health/diagnosis exception. Hearing cannot be skipped longer than
actual urgent safety requires. Corporal/humiliating punishment, restraint as
teaching discipline, loss of food/essential support and diagnostic/AI guilt
are forbidden; actual LF/HP/clinical/rescue powers keep separate grounds.

**ED14 — Continuing restriction needs outside judgment and real learning.**
Beyond ED13's limit, only a conflict-free education inspector may issue a
reasoned site-specific protection order, at most10calendar days per order,
after effective supported reply as soon as safe. Reassess actual necessity,
accommodation and less restrictive safe arrangements; new labels, changed head
or term boundaries do not restart the original initial period. Any continuation
needs fresh evidence/reasons and independent useful-time court access. Safe
qualified learning/support from the first missed ordinary session remains due;
a device, referral or unpaid parent is no automatic equivalent. Expiry ends
this ED restriction; separately satisfied actual safety law retains its own
scope/clocks, never an automatic new order. A lasting transfer is ED12's
accepted placement decision with review, not endlessly relabelled suspension.
No order proves an alternative worker or a safe restored relationship exists.

**ED15 — Remedy need not pass through the alleged abuser.** A learner, chosen
supporter or safe representative can reach inspector/court directly about
exclusion, access, indoctrination, unsafe practice, assessment or retaliation.
No parent, teacher, head, purchaser or AI gatekeeper can require prior complaint
to itself. Protect confidential source/child aid and meaningful defense under
actual PD/OJ/civil law; do not disclose intimate testimony publicly or promise
anonymous evidence proves guilt. ED's coordinated law expressly assigns
correction/access/receiving orders within its scope; B19/ordinary courts and
lawful executors supply independent enforcement, no new search/fine/force.
AP ordinary outer clocks cannot override E/S's earlier safe time or legal
hearing rights. Direct interim relief may be necessary before a lost term;
an issued order is not an attended lesson or received remedy.
