# C06. Source finance and independent public-payment continuity

15 October 2026 · Design 391 · Proposed SF01–SF17, not adopted or funded law

Select a **conditional hybrid**: a separately safeguarded public-payment
alternative, genuinely paid bank equity and plural credit with actually
disbursed working capital. This revises the principles-only plural-finance
reference; public continuity must not depend on a failed bank, its insurer,
its sole vendor or a favorable AI account score. It is not universal narrow
banking or a guarantee of every lender's survival.

bank residual risk; the public bank share falls from 25% to 12.5%; public
and private dividends are recalculated. Domestic known net claims remain
2,485 and domestic real assets 2,515, with net foreign claim −30. Neither
asset conversion nor a lower required GDP is a new resource or welfare gain.

complete insurance/compensation funding or superior outcome is established.

actual rights/capacity cannot be reduced to make the reference pass.
