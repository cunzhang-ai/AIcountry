# JC01–JC04. Shared windows and due duties

[Joint continuity](../65-joint-continuity-and-first-budget.md)

**JC01 — Reconcile material concurrent commitments in one budget.**
For the first budget, each annual renewal of material shared commitments
and a material change before new reliance, Treasury reconciles the
affected sector plans under the existing elected budget procedure. Use
the same scenario, territory, interval and financing scope. A dependency
is material if its loss or concurrent use can defeat a protected task,
an existing due obligation, a practical remedy or a democratic act.
Reuse verified operating plans and review only the affected scope. A
department cannot omit a duty by outsourcing it or reclassifying it as
contingency. A routine change with unchanged dependencies and exposure
does not need a new consolidated proceeding.

**JC02 — Name the whole path and its claim on capacity.** The legally
responsible service owner identifies the beneficiary or protected class,
original due time, useful interval, lawful decision maker, qualified
shift, controlled supply, transfer route, accepting operator and needed
execution. Use protected records for identifiable cases. Distinguish
listed, reserved, dispatched and received capacity. Include existing
donor, maintenance, relief and replenishment duties when a path moves
capacity. Independently test local duties when the supplying authority
claims spare stock. An aggregate budget, headcount, kWh figure, nominal
substitute or AI estimate cannot establish any particular timely route.

**JC03 — Test simultaneous performance and correlated loss separately.**
Publish the test assumptions and demand basis and show a constructive
schedule under the material shared failure and overlap conditions.
Identify maximum concurrent demand, operating duration and staffing at
the original due times, with travel, setup, paid relief, connection and
readiness for the next due obligation where material. Test any shared
site, supplier, route, access credential, payment operator or decision
maker that could defeat several paths. Two contracts or devices can
depend on one controller. Stagger work only where the service owner
establishes that the revised window still meets the original duty.
Disclose important excluded or unknown failures and who may challenge
the chosen scope before reliance.

**JC04 — Identify each exposed duty and its useful alternative.** When a
schedule cannot serve all original obligations, identify each incompatible
window, affected receiving path and options for timely mitigation.
Report unavailable capacity separately from unlawful withholding or
refusal. Separate existing due rights from possible new promises. Keep
sector priority rules, independent remedies, and actual rules for
election evidence and missed deadlines. A budget schedule cannot assign
an emergency priority, merge public authorities or count a late act as
timely. If an incident requires choice before the budget can change,
the competent existing authority acts under the applicable law and
records the uncovered duty and mitigation.

For genuinely incompatible enforceable ordinary orders, apply
[SR23–SR27](../../C08/30/06-conflicting-public-orders.md).
Price timely independent review and compatible ordinary capacity.
Reconciliation does not grant a stay or vary a judicial order.
