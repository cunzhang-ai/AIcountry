# C11. Retained human judgment and learning alongside AI

*Integrated design 85 · Proposed HC01–HC10; not an adopted deployment permission.*

**HC01 — Assess the capability the public actually relies on.** When AI supports
learning, legally required human judgment or a critical human fallback, the
responsible public/sector professional owner evaluates retained understanding,
error detection, transfer and usable independent support alongside assisted
output. The question is whether the person/team can perform its real duty when
the tool is wrong, unavailable or outside scope, not whether every worker can
rebuild the model or manually repeat every obsolete technique. The source,
quality, affected population and task define the assessment; no universal
psychometric score or test frequency is created.

Do not infer learning from completed assignments, independent review from a
human signature or public capacity from a benchmark. Distinguish immediate
performance, short-term retention, longer-term transfer and team continuity.
Original studies support investigating these differences; no general professional
deskilling effect or optimal national pedagogy is established. A lawfully
qualified alternative technology can support independence where it avoids the
same relevant failure/control, rather than preserving one historical method.

**HC02 — Safe and accessible assessment.** Use proportionate protected practice,
simulation, qualified observation or independently supported work at comparable
safety/quality, preserving disability/language accommodations and necessary
assistive tools. “Unaided” never authorizes removing access technology or unsafe
live withdrawal. Patient care, essential payments, liberty and worker livelihood
cannot be jeopardized to generate a test. Ordinary qualification/supervision
uses its actual professional and employment law; research interventions require
separate lawful ethics, participation and consent arrangements where applicable.

Teachers/clinical/professional authorities set task-relevant learning/competence
standards within law, with student/worker/client voice and independent challenge.
A ministry/vendor cannot impose political loyalty or a single AI worldview by
calling it AI literacy. Protect privacy, fair assessment and alternative paths;
no automatic adverse eligibility/employment decision from a model score. This
public deployment condition creates no general ban on private lawful AI use.

**HC03 — Budget competence and a real fallback.** C11/N/NP owners fund teacher/
reviewer/instructor time, protected practice, independent source access, error
correction, accepted alternative tools/people and turnover/renewal before relying
on released capacity. Count these costs and displaced tasks once. A second
agent/model controlled by the same supplier is not an independent reviewer.
Required human review needs actual time, authority, competence and power to
refuse the recommendation; the vendor cannot verify its own sole safety route.
If learning/review/fallback deteriorates materially, revise/limit wider reliance
and restore safe capacity under existing authority. Do not erase wages, learning
access, pending claims or safe service during tool replacement. Existing legal
safety/clinical obligations can require action sooner than a research test.

**HC04 — Reassess changed tools and contexts without self-approval.** Pin tool,
task, support method, relevant source/evaluation scope and actual competent
owner. New model/media, changed curriculum/work, observed errors, dependence or
access loss trigger fresh scoped review before reuse of an affected gain claim.
Report assisted output, retained/transfer/fallback ability, burden, excluded
people and uncertainty separately. A safe retain/no-AI/simpler-tool result is
valid. Neither a favorable evaluation nor this research adopts new authority.
Existing C11/C12/TS professional, privacy, consent and democratic boundaries stay.

The [comparative source scope](../../settlement/country-case/03-ai-and-learning-evidence.md)
keeps task-specific output and school-learning reports distinct. Actual retained
competence, qualified independent evaluation, cost, source and safe receiving
remain unverified for this country. No old workforce/AI percentage finances it.

[TW13](../C05/10/03-leave-learning-and-income.md) now specifies64 paid learning-
activity hours with separate breaks, qualified instruction and actual cover.
Only matched valid learning can share another duty; independent review/teaching
and unspecified preparation remain work, not free AI gains or deleted tasks.

[DP05–DP08](09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md) now explicitly tests
reliance against common update/controller/host/key/record/payment failures and
simultaneous qualified capacity. A residual path may use independently qualified
technology; preserve actual legal human judgment/challenge and access aids,
not every historical manual technique. Unknown shared roots/staff are not
independent availability or forecast-funded retained competence.

[AL continuing learning](../C07/22-lifelong-learning-and-renewal.md) keeps
future media and optional AI compatible with actual retained/transfer competence
and accommodations. A new course, completed assisted task or portable record
confers no mastery, professional licence, agent mandate or job.

[HC05–HC10](05/01-ai-and-succession-of-public-competence.md) joins paid
practice, mentor succession and qualified duty cover over the actual
replacement interval. The tool, workforce and lasting budget decisions
require their respective evidence and authority.
