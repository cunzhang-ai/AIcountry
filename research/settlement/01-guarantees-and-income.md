# Guarantees, income and work

## 1. What the country guarantees and who delivers it

The Assembly enacts a national guarantee schedule: covered people and needs, eligibility,
quality, accessibility, response times, responsible authority, funding and enforceable
remedies. Essential protection applies to persons in need; ordinary resident entitlements
and cross-border reimbursement require explicit coverage rules. Citizenship alone cannot
silently become a condition for all services. Do not equate universality with identical
spending: high needs and remote delivery require different resources.

| Guarantee | Accountable delivery | Funding and correction |
|---|---|---|
| Essential healthcare and urgent stabilization | National health authority; regional/local clinical providers retain professional responsibility | Pooled public funding, funded high-cost needs and independent coverage/waiting-time remedy |
| Basic education and supported access to learning | Local education authority under national access and curriculum rights | Need-adjusted operating grants, separate buildings budget, continued placement after a successful challenge |
| Care and supported autonomy | Local care authority, with national fallback responsibility for uncovered need | Needs-based funding for paid care, respite and accessibility; no presumed compulsory family labor |
| Secure access to housing and essential networks | Local housing/network authorities; national supply and equalization duties | Housing supply, maintenance and affordability assistance reconciled; remedy does not erase lawful private claims |
| Income security across childhood, disability, job loss and old age | National social-protection authority with local accessible intake | Portable benefits and assistance; provisional emergency payment, safe review and subsistence protection |
| Justice, safety and democratic participation | Separate courts, defense, oversight and lawful civilian agencies | Protected, explicit operating funds; remedy execution cannot depend solely on the office challenged |

The responsible authority remains answerable when a contractor fails. A local fiscal
shortfall triggers a published national response, not automatic abolition of the
entitlement or an unfunded instruction to another locality. Tax collection, entitlement,
delivery and review have distinct accountable offices; a single national app is optional.

## 2. The income and work settlement

Use a legislated needs basket to set the income floor, including household composition,
housing costs and disability-related costs without assuming resources are shared safely.
Ordinary support may reduce progressively as usable income rises. Evaluate the combined
effect of tax and benefit withdrawal: changing one schedule cannot conceal a new cliff
in another. No particular minimum amount or withdrawal rate is calibrated here.

Do not require employment, compulsory unpaid care, an AI interview or a criminal complaint
as a condition of emergency necessities. Contributory insurance and pension claims remain
distinct from basic assistance. Simplified provisional assessment and later safe checking
reduce the need to finance an emergency personally, with independent correction of denial.
They do not eliminate error, administrative discretion or the need for public oversight.

The later [retirement-security selection](../country/C07/04-retirement-security.md)
specifies an individual basic old-age payment and a capped earnings-related
public-insurance layer with publicly financed care and interruption credits.
Its financing, age and benefit amounts are **not** costed by the invented
three-year illustration below; they must enter the actual whole-country account
before any adoption or commencement claim.

An unconditional cash component remains a legitimate alternative. Compare its **gross
appropriation, net tax/benefit incidence, service effects and coverage** separately.
The comparison here adds a modest uniform payment without cutting existing protection;
it is not an evaluation of every universal-income model. Funding it by tax reform or
replacing overlapping benefits would require a different integrated package. No disability
or care entitlement disappears merely because a general payment begins.

The [design-58 income-floor specification](../country/C05/06-income-floor-and-safe-resources.md)
now provides candidate person-level assessment, formula, due calendar and
transition. Its [reference comparison](income-floor/README.md) exposes
unfunded starts and does not calibrate adequate needs or all tax bases.
