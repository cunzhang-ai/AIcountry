# Decision record: equal standing and enforceable equality

5 October 2026 · Integrated design 55 research selection; not enacted law

## Baseline and question

The [design-54 predecessor](predecessor-design-54/README.md) preserves the
prior current design. C02 declared protection against discrimination, and
sector chapters rejected specific exclusions, but the country lacked one
rule for indirect effects, accommodation, evidence held by a provider,
cross-sector patterns, interim relief and execution. The question is how a
person actually challenges exclusion while preserving legitimate safety,
clinical need, association, political-membership and resource distinctions.
No affected AIcountry population, jurisdiction or source-law inventory exists.

Affected people include workers, applicants, tenants, patients, learners,
disabled people, minorities, migrants, political dissenters, employers,
providers, staff and people whose data would be collected. Values include
equal status, freedom of association and expression, privacy, clinical
judgment, property, public capacity and fair respondent process. A disparity
may reflect discrimination, different needs, geography, genuine job
requirements, selection into a service or bad data. Original application
records, rejected applicants, nonusers, provider rules, accommodation
requests and affected accounts are needed to distinguish these diagnoses.

## Whole alternatives

| Package | Plausible gain | Serious failure or objection |
|---|---|---|
| Retain constitutional declaration and separate sector rules | Low central bureaucracy; specialist context | People must rediscover a different test and evidence gate at every institution; linked controllers escape view |
| One central equality body investigates, decides and orders every remedy | Uniform standard and visible responsibility | A single captured protector can censor claims, overrule clinical or political competence and delay every case |
| Courts alone apply a common civil rule | Independent adjudication and case-specific process | Evidence, money, language and time barriers leave structural exclusion invisible and urgent opportunities gone |
| **Selected: common rights floor, sector remedies, independent inquiry and direct courts** | People can obtain sector-specific repair, cross-sector investigation and a separate final adjudicator | Requires more capacity and can create referral loops or conflicting standards unless first receipt and review are real |

The selected [C02 scope](../country/C02/05-equal-standing-and-differentiated-needs.md)
recognizes compounded harm, individualized accommodation, justified
differences and privacy. [C08](../country/C08/11-equality-law-and-effective-remedies.md)
enacts a common civil test, sector first receivers, an independent pattern
inquiry with no final adjudication power, direct courts and executed repair.
[C06](../country/C06/17-equality-and-access-finance.md) counts the physical
and legal capacity; [C13](../country/C13/20-cross-sector-exclusion-and-remedy.md)
tests one vendor across work, housing and care. This does not choose a
numerical quota or make every unequal outcome unlawful.

## Primary comparators and their limits

The [OHCHR practical guide to protecting minority rights](https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/documents/publications/2022-11-28/OHCHR_ERT_Protecting_Minority%20Rights_Practical_Guide_web.pdf)
explains how comprehensive anti-discrimination law can translate principles
into actionable rights. The UN committee's [General Comment 20 on
non-discrimination in economic, social and cultural rights](https://docstore.ohchr.org/SelfServices/FilesHandler.ashx?enc=5zH7M%2BHbYJMU37WbxGthVPx7WUpiBJyiLIDSIlxLcGfeK%2FZizLEFlv6GcpeKDmaM7bBv1692o99zZu%2BO78uSVw%3D%3D)
discusses accessible institutions, structural harms and effective remedies.
The [ILO employment-discrimination convention](https://normlex.ilo.org/dyn/nrmlx_en/f?p=NORMLEXPUB%3A12100%3A0%3A%3ANO%3A%3AP12100_INSTRUMENT_ID%3A312256)
is a comparator for employment opportunity and genuine job requirements.
The [UN disability-rights convention](https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/Ch_IV_15.pdf)
is a comparator for equal protection and reasonable accommodation. These
sources are neither AIcountry law nor proof that this institutional split,
evidence rule, protected-ground list or cost boundary will work in a real
jurisdiction. The selected independent inquiry is a project hypothesis.

## Power, objections and continuity

The Assembly sets rights, ordinary exceptions, process, public data limits
and funding. Sector authorities and providers explain and correct their own
decisions; an independent inquiry service exposes patterns and can support
defined public-interest litigation; courts decide contested cases and issue
orders. Claimants, respondents, associations, opposition and independent
inspectors retain alternative paths. Trace how inquiry leaders are nominated,
appointed, funded, removed and supplied with records; test whether a cabinet,
major provider or advocacy network can capture the service and the court
execution chain together. An AI vendor cannot define protected groups, set
the legal test, conceal a proxy or determine liability.

Serious objections persist. A broad protected-ground list can make ordinary
choices unpredictable; an overly narrow one misses compounded harm. A civil
evidence inference may help a claimant whose evidence is held by a firm but
must preserve respondent disclosure and defense. Disclosure may expose
medical, identity or other applicants' data. Accommodation cannot create
workers, housing or funds instantly; an undue-burden exception can also
become a routine excuse. Public-interest litigation can displace what the
affected person wants. Identity counting can reveal or freeze categories.
The law must distinguish unequal treatment from justified different care,
citizenship rules, political rights and distinct Indigenous claims. These
objections cannot be resolved by an average equality score.

Any real adoption inventories constitutional clauses, labor and housing
law, disability duties, school and health rules, election rules, migration
categories, protected records, contracts, pending cases and accrued rights.
The new civil standard and inquiry power require prospective lawful adoption,
staff, protected evidence, counsel and alternate review. Existing claims
and lawful distinctions continue until competent amendment; no research text
creates a new data demand or liability today.

## Result and bounded next test

Integration review also found an obsolete completion claim in the founding
package. Its earlier stress cases and September fiscal exercise do not cover
the current set of obligations. The working status and overview now require
a current commitment and capacity reconciliation before another isolated
service expansion. This is a correction of claimed design completeness,
not a change to the preserved numerical results or a demand for real
statehood before desk-design work can continue.

Evidence qualification: international primary guidance plus internal
comparison, without local observations or affected-party participation.
Research selection: common civil equality floor with sector correction,
independent cross-sector inquiry and direct courts. Legal authority: absent.
Readiness: absent. Observed outcomes: absent. The bounded test in a real
jurisdiction follows one applicant across work, housing and care, including
nonusers, a genuine safety exception, accommodation request, contested
evidence, urgent relief, order execution and a captured inquiry office.
Compare the selected split with court-only and centralized adjudication using
original evidence, lawful obligations, cost and affected choice. Reopen if
the office captures the field, courts cannot execute timely relief, private
data exposure outweighs benefit, or a rival delivers stronger equal standing.
