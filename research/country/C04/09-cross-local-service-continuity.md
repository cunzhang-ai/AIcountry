# C04. Common services continue across local boundaries

*Design311–520 · XR01–XR20 · Proposed domestic continuity rules.*

**A move changes the place where a person needs a service. Lawful
responsibility follows through an accepted handover or an interim duty.**
Receiving a family, changing the payer and deciding permanent residence
are separate acts. Local democratic authority and national residual
responsibility continue together.

| Canonical part | Selected rule |
|---|---|
| [XR01–XR05. From first contact to actual receiving](09/01-protected-intake-and-actual-handover.md) | Protect plans and dates, assign immediate host receiving and keep care, school, housing and clinical authority distinct. |
| [XR06–XR10. Pay, resolve and protect](09/02-payment-dispute-and-continuity.md) | Assign interim finance and final liability separately, fund shared capacity, provide direct correction and protect safe contact and records. |
| [XR11–XR15. Recurring places and portable support](09/03-recurring-places-and-portable-support.md) | Keep a responsible owner for each award, agree repeat windows and pay separately for delivered service and valid overlap. |
| [XR16–XR20. Fund portable essential support](09/04-funding-portable-essential-support.md) | Separate paid host capacity, ordinary origin funding, advances and the verified national residual. |

The rule applies to common domestic services across areas. Existing
residence, election, tax, protection and territorial rules keep their
own effects. [MF](08-funded-local-mandates-and-equalization.md) governs
normal and transition sources, [AS15](../C07/18/04-change-and-continuity.md)
preserves existing assistance obligations, and sector authorities
keep earlier deadlines and separate standards.

[YC07](../C07/27/02-places-funds-and-control.md) applies this route to
a child placement and voluntary adult support. A host's prior protected
receiving remains in the same capacity account.

[HL06](../C09/25/02-secure-home-and-accountable-handover.md) uses
host receiving and interim finance for unsafe housing and departure.
A transfer request alone neither houses a person nor assigns final liability.

[AL21–AL27](../C07/22/05-portable-apprenticeships-and-paid-continuity.md)
applies protected cross-local receiving to an apprenticeship.
Training, wage claims and actual host acceptance keep their own scope.
