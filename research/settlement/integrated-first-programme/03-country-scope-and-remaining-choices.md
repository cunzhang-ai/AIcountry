A feasible incremental result leaves every unpriced national line open.
Before presenting the reference as a real budget, the proposing authorities
need a common legal, source and capacity baseline and credible ranges for
every material omitted exposure. An existing failure needs repair or
publicly exposed continuing risk in both the retain and the reform path.
An actual withdrawal, transfer or new national duty returns to the
competent bodies for its own ordinary vote and supported start.
