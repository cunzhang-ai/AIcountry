## What the first motion has to cover

Use a constructed four-week window with eight paid vehicle hours
of reserve per week and four full-time drivers in a separate
ordinary roster. The 32 reserved vehicle hours have a DKK 12,736
component at the historical award rate. They are a sensitivity,
not a purchasable fraction of the awarded package. In this
illustration an independently dispatched vehicle and consenting
worker would still need to be secured. Apply no forecast AI saving.

| Motion | Service and roster effect | Price and exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Repair and redistribute agreed hours | Keep ordinary hours. Move willing shifts and verify actual appointment coverage and rest. | Fund repair and planning. No uniform wage or reserve saving is evidenced. |
| Repair with paid reserve and prepare reduced hours | Buy only verified reserve capacity. Keep ordinary roster while staff, sources and independent review are secured. | DKK 12,736 vehicle component in the sensitivity, plus distinct dispatch, qualification and review costs if required. |
| Joint reserve and 36-hour start | Release one paid hour per driver per week. Where all hours were needed, separately replace 16 ordinary hours over four weeks. | Same vehicle component and a distinct DKK 2,724.32 ordinary base wage component at the October 2025 comparator. Actual cover includes the worker, compatible shift, premiums, rest, contributions and accepted origin cover. |

The 16 hours concern the ordinary roster. If the reserve actually
covers those same gaps, retire the duplicate backfill line and test
whether a simultaneous urgent call can still be met. In a 32-hour
stress sensitivity the ordinary replacement requirement rises to
80 hours on the same assumptions. Do not count the regular driver,
their vehicle or their paid hours again as standby. In either
reduction the incumbent's weekly pay is preserved. Higher
intensity, skipped breaks or unpaid family substitution are
failures to test, with no wage or service savings credited.

Suppose all four weeks fall in one accounting month. Flex25
settlement pays a provisional amount with the statement by the
tenth banking day of the following month, subject to the actual
terms. The ATAX–3F comparator permits payroll within four banking
days of a 14-day period ending, or by the last banking day of
the month for monthly payroll. If these terms apply to the same
provider, both ordinary payroll dates can fall before public
settlement. Cash available later cannot cover that earlier
need. Verify the provider's liquidity and responsibility without
making a patient or worker advance the public share. No actual
cash balance or invoice date was obtained.

Two simultaneous journey calls in a shared-dispatch outage
require two independently assignable vehicle-and-driver pairs,
compatible clinics and useful travel windows. Two supplier
names on the same unavailable dispatch route deliver zero
through that route. One verified alternate can cover at most
one of these simultaneous calls. Preserve both underlying
service claims and record any missed receiving. Advance repair
and price a standby route and roster change separately. The
available public evidence does not qualify a joint start.
