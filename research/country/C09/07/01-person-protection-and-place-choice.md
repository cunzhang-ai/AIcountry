# FR01–FR04. Protect people before settling the future of property

*Design216 · Part of [C09 disaster recovery](../07-disaster-recovery-and-return.md).*

**FR01 — Give displaced people a direct recovery route.** Within the
existing C07, E and XR duties, the first competent public receiver
accepts an occupant's safe contact and usable evidence of displacement
or credible loss of habitability. A tenant, subtenant or other actual
occupant can use that route without the owner's action, an insurer's
decision or a reliable internet connection. Keep original dates and
provide help with alternative proof. Assign a named housing and service
receiver, give usable contact and tell people who is responsible for
care, school and income continuity. A missing title or disputed claim
must not delay due urgent protection. The route does not decide title,
fault, permanent tenure or compensation.

**FR02 — Separate immediate danger, future risk and legal consequences.**
A competent officer uses the existing temporary safety law for immediate
danger, with its own evidence, review and expiry. Separately record the
future hazard range, exposed people and critical services, feasible
mitigation, residual risk, uncertainty and relevant sites. Give actual
occupants and affected neighboring people safe, directly usable notice
of material findings that affect their safety or use, with relevant
evidence and independent correction. Protect addresses, testimony and
competing rights. A model, insurance withdrawal, voluntary buyout offer
or expired temporary exclusion does not itself change a land or occupancy
right. Material long-term restrictions need their own lawful decision,
affected hearing and independent review. A project cannot evade LA by
calling a compelled taking a risk classification.

**FR03 — Choose a funded place plan with affected and receiving people.**
The competent elected body compares safe return, household and shared
mitigation, changed uses and supported voluntary movement at a common
scope. Test actual useful completion dates, the hazard across the
catchment, added risk downstream, maintenance, costs at the receiving
place and continued access to livelihoods, care, education and cultural
ties. Keep collective rights and applicable consent separate. Prefer
feasible safe repair where it protects people at acceptable full costs
and preserves their choice. Where no timely acceptable mitigation can
support safe occupancy, make voluntary movement practicable. Compelled
possession or movement needs the applicable separate law, including LA
and its independent safeguards where applicable. Neither lack of funds
nor a new valuation supplies that authority.

**FR04 — Verify actual housing and daily life before counting a move.**
A funded recovery plan identifies independently usable support for
occupants as well as any acquisition payments. Before a project
relies on return or movement, confirm that a competent receiving
service accepts and the resident can actually use the safe dwelling,
tenure, transport, school, income and care arrangements. Preserve the
person's informed choice and the right to contest an unsuitable option.
Pay project-caused additional needs and host capacity from the lawful
source rather than displacing another household's due service.
If a dwelling is unavailable, unaffordable or inaccessible, the
receiving duty and remedy remain open. Preserve the applicable statutory and accrued rights. The housing
receiver must explain the options actually available to the household.
