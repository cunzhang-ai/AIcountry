# Design225 decision record. Safe home temperatures and continuity

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned design 224](baseline.json)

## Why change the country design

C09 gave general duties for safe homes, essential electricity and
outages. It left the enforceable indoor outcome and the first receiver
unclear when a building defect, inability to pay and loss of supply
coincided. [TH01–TH12](../country/C09/23-safe-home-temperatures-and-continuity.md)
joins those duties, names the housing case owner and protects immediate
help while the parties resolve final liability. This selects a national
requirement with measurable local schedules and public review.
The [research method](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md)
govern the research record. Actual authority remains with competent
elected and statutory bodies through their lawful processes.

The values are equal access to immediate protection, privacy and
practical tenure. The physical constraints are indoor exposure over
time, a usable supply, a reachable receiver and paid staff. The
cost-effectiveness and displacement effects of particular repairs,
energy sources or shared utilities remain contextual. A common
measured outcome does not eliminate disagreement about whose costs
should be funded or what scarce work starts first.

## Choices under the same duty

| Choice | Strong case | Failure or objection |
|---|---|---|
| Retain existing repair and enforce it better, plus bill support | Fastest where the building and supply already work and affordability or an ordinary defect is the binding barrier. Preserves contracts and qualified crews. | Leaves inherently unsafe rooms and common-failure load untreated. A complaint closure or energy credit can precede safe receiving. |
| More mechanical equipment and firm grid | Direct protection where envelope changes are insufficient or unsafe. Can support additional uses. | Coincident load, electricity bills, fuel and shared outage exposure remain. More ordinary connection capacity alone leaves the failed feeder unavailable. |
| Reduce exposure, preserve affordable active service and buy verified fallback | Improves housing and can reduce peak dependence. Keeps the local repair and owner-control structure. | Needs up-front work, seasonal tests, ventilation and actual rent protections. It may be slower than active service alone. A vulnerable household needs interim help while work is pending. |
| Public or cooperative shared heat utility | A genuine structural option where density, existing assets and energy sources support it. Can replace duplicated equipment and strengthen renewal. | Captive consumers, fixed network cost, concentrated outages and protected exit need independent tests and paid succession. Prefer no exclusive district grant without evidence against the other options. |

Select the mixed duty, with repair as the first choice where it
works and active protection immediately where needed. A locality
may choose a shared system through its own evidenced process.
The [neighbourhood comparison](../settlement/safe-home-temperatures/02-peak-load-and-actual-receiving.md)
shows that better ordinary coverage and better failure coverage
can support different choices. No national equipment mandate,
retrofit yield or agreed final financing is assumed.

## Control and continuity

The Assembly chooses the entitlement and appropriations. Existing
health and building authorities own evidence and qualified standards
advice. Elected councils fund and direct lawful local implementation.
Inspectors, hearing staff and courts need sources and evidence
outside the control of the landlord, utility or implicated office.
Review existing appointment, removal, cash, access and execution
chains before commencement. Residents can organize, report without
continuous monitoring, submit evidence and challenge a failed or
captured authority through the existing external routes. A contractor,
provider or model cannot change the floor or close a case on its own.

Keep existing repair, rent and occupancy rights, property and creditor
rights, grid and heat obligations and independent sector powers. Use
FT/OP/T for lawful paid succession and honor open reports, interim
placements and repayments. Each new building duty needs a prospective
standard and transition with an actual crew and source. Where the
new duty cannot start, keep the existing legal protections and record
the uncovered work and elected choice. No emergency fiscal or
coercive power follows.

## Next bounded comparison

For one locality, examine one exposed building group, the actual
local feeder and an independently powered alternative receiving site.
Use summer and winter observations, voluntary and accessible occupant
input, total bills, commissioned performance, rent and displacement
effects, repair labor and simultaneous load. Compare ordinary repair,
additional active supply and a matched mixed option. Stop the test
before exposing people to danger. Reopen on persistent unsafe
exposure, inequitable retrofit burdens, a change in climate or
availability, common failure or a better feasible option. Actual
affected-party participation, independent local review, enactment,
paid execution and better lives remain unestablished.
