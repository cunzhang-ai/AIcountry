# HT18–HT23. Give voters and successors the same fiscal starting point

*Part of [lawful handover and actual public control](../19-lawful-handover-and-actual-control.md). Design347 research selection.*

**HT18 — Publish a continuing-law baseline before the first ballot.** The
Assembly's independent budget analysts publish a common fiscal baseline
no later than 30 calendar days before the earliest lawful voting day,
using the operative electoral calendar. Keep a standing version through
the term for a lawful short-notice replacement or unusually compressed
contest. Where a lawful call leaves less time, publish the latest
verified version and its gaps promptly and update as evidence permits.
Use the same date, legal perimeter and definitions for eligible
contestants and the public. Preserve confidential optional requests
for campaign costing under GP. A missing report or estimate cannot
change voting, investiture or office.

**HT19 — Show obligations, usable resources and operational limits
separately.** Treasury and each lawful custodian provide dated originals
for current law, executed commitments, material off-budget exposures,
restricted funds, near-term receipts, disputed calls and indispensable
service and review capacity. Distinguish authorized spending, money
settled to the lawful account, qualified staffing and expected receipts.
Count related public entities and linked commitments under their
applicable scope while retaining legal liability and fund ownership.
Show the first 30 days of continuing duties and material longer tails.
Publish the amount or range where supported and identify material
unknowns and independent disagreements. Costing against this baseline
does not create a funded promise or an appropriation.

**HT20 — Publish material changes while the baseline is being used.**
Responsible custodians transmit a material correction or new event
within two working days of learning it, earlier where a known decision
requires. Analysts publish an accessible dated change or a provisional
notice of its scope and uncertainty within two working days of receipt,
or before a known voting or fiscal decision when feasible. Preserve the
original as seen, identify changed assumptions and affected decisions,
and distinguish a correction, a new binding act and a contested claim.
Make the same safe facts available through common public channels,
including non-digital access. No selective release by the government
or access to a rival's private costing. Protect personal and lawfully
sensitive records with reasons for any redaction. Independent
parliamentary evidence and urgent review remain available.

**HT21 — Give the successor an opening cash and delivery account.** By
seven calendar days after lawful appointment, Treasury and sector
owners publish a public opening account and give authenticated details
to the competent successor and independent reviewers. Identify what
changed from the last public baseline, the first 30 days of due work,
actual sources, staffed receiving, soon-expiring options and unresolved
calls. Handle duties falling before the report on their original clocks
through existing career, sector and payment authority. Identify what
the incoming government may change, what needs a new vote and what
already binds. Reconcile the account with the first budget and GP
programme while preserving their independent timelines. A briefing,
signature or receipt of records cannot determine who takes office.

**HT22 — Give elected bodies a choice when headroom disappears.** If
verified change or unresolved material exposure can affect a proposed
release, present lawful continuation and exact independently viable
alternatives under DB, OL and JC. Preserve restricted accounts, due
services, wages, elections and independent review. Record the original
payer, due date, claimant and actual funded response. Hold only the
affected optional release where its conditions fail. Do not treat a
disputed call as payable or an anticipated receipt as available.
A shortfall calls for lawful correction and scrutiny. It neither
cancels a valid obligation nor enlarges caretaker authority.

**HT23 — Keep independent evidence and the paid handover usable.** Fund
analysts, source custodians, accessible publication, independent evidence
access and ordinary execution separately. Provide an unimplicated
alternate for withheld records. Preserve versions and disputed inputs
through election, replacement and transition. AI can reconcile source
versions, compare dates and identify conflicts. Officers and independent
reviewers verify evidence within their mandates. Elected bodies keep
fiscal decisions. Adopt prospectively and reopen on withheld updates,
selective access, repeated surprise liabilities, excess burden or a
simpler route that secures equivalent public control.
