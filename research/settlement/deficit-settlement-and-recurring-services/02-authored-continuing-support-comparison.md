# Clear an old balance and keep new learning support deliverable

The units and cash dates in this comparison are authored and have
no conversion to the English grant. Assume the original qualified
education and independent review duties and other protected services
already have their separate sources. The model isolates an additional
high-needs scope with a historical deficit of ten units, whose legal
status and eligible original costs are established in the fixture.

Nine restricted units are expected from a historical settlement
and one separate local unit is reserved for the residual. Only
actual authorized receipt and application close the historical
balance. Neither source can fund the following continuing needs.

| Ordinary annual account | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Continuing qualified support needed | 8 | 9 | 9 |
| Existing recurring source | 6 | 6 | 6 |
| Additional recurring requirement | 2 | 3 | 3 |

Each additional annual unit funds one independently qualified
support slot in this fixture. Assume two such slots are available
in year 1 and three in later years, with actual acceptance
needed before reliance. Assume qualified existing support stays
funded. The independent review work remains separate. A finite
transition line contains three uncommitted units. An elected
ordinary alternative would redirect three annual units from a
genuinely unbound optional programme and forgo that programme.
The authorities still have to enact the exact scope and changes.

| Route | Effect on continuing support |
|---|---|
| Settle the historic balance only | No change to the annual gaps of two, three and three. |
| Use the three-unit finite line for annual gaps | Two in year 1 and one in year 2 can be financed. Two remain missing in year 2 and three in year 3. |
| Vote the three-unit recurring reallocation and a one-unit early bridge | Covers all annual requirements on the stated assumptions, with one early bridge needed for year 1. |
| Rely on a weaker continuing yield of two | Covers annual year 1 scope, subject to timing. One remains unfunded in each later year. |

Two additional early payments fall due in year 1 before all the
new recurring source is usable. Only one of its three units
arrives before those payments. The lawful one-unit bridge from
the finite line covers the earlier gap. Repay that unit from the
later two actual recurring units under the bridge terms. This
restores the finite source, with one later net unit available
under its own lawful purpose. It does not make the annual
recurring source four. A contingent or restricted historical
grant cannot supply the bridge.

The comparison selects the recurring option with the qualified
early bridge under the declared conditions. If only two later
qualified slots materialize, the third later duty remains exposed
despite sufficient money. Existing lawful learning and remedy
duties remain due. A lower-cost qualified model with the same
actual access could displace the selected option. The fixture
establishes neither actual receipts nor an enacted budget.
