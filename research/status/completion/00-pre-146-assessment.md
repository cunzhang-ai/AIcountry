## What changed after the whole-country audit

[CP01–CP12](../../country/C06/28-national-commitments-and-residual-scarcity.md) corrects
the public-output/basic-price-GVA split, retires old productivity/resource
interpretations and preserves unaffected frozen GDP/cash/bids. The [new bridge](../../2026-10-whole-country-reconciliation/README.md)
computes a declared historical subset. About +50.32% required GVA/FTE for the
old book and +50.19% for the SF hybrid are nominal fixed-scope requirements,
not achieved production or evidence that the new country is affordable.

CP replaces blanket new S/Y starts with an exact Assembly-enacted service/
coverage/new-accrual schedule, paid overlap, interim protection and equality
review. Existing/accrued and already overdue claims retain their source dates;
no provider, Treasury or AI readiness certificate indefinitely postpones them.

A residual rule now has a selected narrow public/voluntary pool, relevant urgency,
comparable-claim treatment, actual receiving, direct review and 24/absolute 72-hour
outer powers. It does not silently redistribute protected already due resources,
create stock, seize private labor/property or renew itself when the Assembly
cannot sit. This can preserve an unequal baseline; wider accountable diversion
is a real rejected-default rival, not a value conflict solved by arithmetic.

## Keep design completion separate from later real-world claims

A desk proposal can specify coherent rules without founding an actual country.
A context parameter may remain conditional only with a selection rule, bounds,
competent authority, dependencies and transition; 'decide later' alone is missing
design. Actual local engineering/clinical/treaty annexes require real context;
they cannot truthfully be invented to make a checklist green.

Legitimate founding/affected participation, source-law reconciliation, enactment,
appropriations, qualified staff, actual safe receiving/executed remedies and
observed wellbeing remain separate unverified later facts. Nor can absent public
authority excuse undefined powers or an inconsistent research account. Same-
controller multi-agent analysis is not qualified independent or lived review.
Preserve prior editions and safe correction history, including errors. The
[country design](../../COUNTRY-DESIGN.md) and [status](../../DESIGN-STATUS.md) remain the
current proposal/decision entry points; this audit adopts no governing procedure.

[Design 100 personal tax and accountable revenue](11-tax-and-democratic-design-gaps.md) adds positive liability, direct full merits, actual refund payer and finite collection while retaining missing nonpersonal taxes, practical campaign rules and integrated sources. Structural choices, evidence, legal adoption, qualified capacity and received outcomes remain separate.
