# Design299. Electoral instructions and timely correction

10 November 2026 · Research selection · No actual enactment

## Baseline and question

[Design298](baseline.json) retains election administration, existing
narrow impersonation relief, plural media and separate independent
review. A correction may be valid and still miss people who heard
the false instruction. Apply the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md)
and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md). Codex assisted the
comparison. No affected voter, operator or independent reviewer
qualified the proposal.

## Alternatives and selection

- **Keep ordinary election information and voluntary correction.**
  This minimizes compulsory public involvement and is sufficient
  where the actual affected people can use it. The authored
  online-only route misses the offline groups after compromise.
- **Maintain independent authoritative sources and a funded targeted
  correction path.** Select this architecture. Use existing lawful
  communication first. Add only a specific civil relay power under
  competent enactment and independent proof where timely correction
  otherwise fails. The reference reaches both groups. A weaker
  budget can serve only the local channel on its actual terms.
  Neither result restores the two earlier missed opportunities.
- **Use platform restrictions or expanded central speech control.**
  A proven specific breach may have an existing lawful remedy.
  Removing a message does not inform a person who already received
  it. Reject a new general political-truth or takedown power here.
  Compare a wholly voluntary independent route if it can reach
  the same people with less interference.

[ER21–ER25](../country/C03/10/10-electoral-instructions-and-timely-correction.md)
sets the operational and prospective civil boundaries. The
[comparison](../settlement/electoral-instruction-correction/README.md)
uses authored reach, deadlines and costs. The [evidence](sources.md)
supports the distinction between operational information and an
apparent source. It gives no turnout or correction estimate.

## Authority, objection and migration

A correction power could be used against opposition or to track
voters. Confine it to independently verified operative facts and
concrete access harm. Use protected source and distribution records,
direct challenge and independent paid execution. Safeguard satire,
journalism and lawful abstention advocacy. Keep a route independent
of the original official channel. The ordinary administrator can
inform the public without deciding fault. A competent court can
order only its expressly adopted corrective scope.

Apply any change prospectively under RR and actual election law.
Preserve past ballots, claims, court orders, lawful staffing, original
deadlines and pending result challenges. Separate actual receiving
from communication delivery and actual free choice from either.
A correction or failure cannot determine validity by itself.

## Next bounded decision

Exercise one independently authorized local information route in
a safe scenario with a compromised official channel. Verify actual
available sender, offline and telephone access, useful receipt,
challenge and a second protective call. Reopen after missed people,
a selective official response, common control, unnecessary personal
data or a stronger ordinary alternative. No real deployment,
mandate or benefit is established.
