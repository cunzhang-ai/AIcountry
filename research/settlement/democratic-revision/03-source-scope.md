# Democratic revision: documentary source scope

*Read15October2026; three primary texts in two institutional families.*

The [Venice 2024 stability declaration](https://docs-venice.coe.int/api/Document?pdffile=CDL-AD%282024%29027-e)
was inspected as the official three-page PDF. B1–2 does not treat stability as
supreme or a reason to preserve contrary electoral standards. B6 restricts
post-call change to necessary binding-judgment implementation and requires
usable implementation time. This supports investigating delay versus repair;
RR's closed exclusions, predecessor electorate and no-self-authorization are
our normative choices, not a claim Venice requires this exact constitution.
The document references the 2022 referendum code; that whole code was not read
for this selection and is not claimed as inspected evidence.

[OECD 2022 participation principles](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-guidelines-for-citizen-participation-processes_f765caf6-en/full-report/component-7.html)
were read for purpose, timely response, accessible participation, source/records,
privacy, resources and evaluation. These are design guidance, not observations
that FA's finite selection works under unlimited novel generation.

[OECD 2021 deliberative principles, Annex B](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/evaluation-guidelines-for-representative-deliberative-processes_10ccbfcb-en/full-report/component-9.html)
were inspected for participant-selected advocates, inclusion, time, coordination
and evaluation. They favor coordination separate from the commissioning body;
FA retains that operational separation but reserves lawful closure/policy to
competent public authorities, with ordinary courts for legality. Its agenda
draw is explicitly not a statistically representative citizens' assembly.
The text's contextual four-day recommendation is not imported as a universal
statutory optimum. The two OECD texts are not independent empirical samples.

No primary election/process observations, original sample frame, affected-party
participation, qualified external review, full legal conformity assessment or
outcome comparison was obtained. Rendered primary pages/PDF text were read;
publisher bytes were not archived. [Dated sources](../../2026-10-democratic-repair-and-finite-attention/sources.json)
records exact URLs and inspected scope; local hashes identify our artifacts,
not immutable copies of changing remote pages. Same-controller agent advice
adds reasoning, not a new source family or a democratic mandate.
