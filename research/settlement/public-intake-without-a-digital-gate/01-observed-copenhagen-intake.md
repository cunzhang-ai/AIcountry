# Copenhagen. A contact forecast, an operating boundary and a review question

## Planned costs and forecast savings

The January 2026 committee record describes about 700,000 staff-handled
telephone contacts in 2024. It forecasts about 300,000 telephone contacts
automated annually, equivalent to 12.5 staff years in the business case,
and plans to adjust staffed hours on the main number from January 2027.
These counts describe contacts and a future workload assumption. The
forecast numerator and the earlier workload do not give an observed
resolution rate or the number of people served. The committee took note
of the briefing. That is distinct from the earlier investment decision.
[January committee record](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Kultur-%2C%20Fritids-%20og%20Borgerserviceudvalget/m%C3%B8de-27012026/referat/punkt-11)

| Planning line | Million DKK | Scope |
|---|---:|---|
| Development | 9.550 | January briefing |
| Common municipal platform | 3.075 | January briefing, separate from development |
| Implementation envelope | 13.125 | Annex Table 2, sum of the displayed years, includes 0.500 startup operation |
| Lower annual staff expenditure | 6.400 | Annex projection at full phase-in in 2028 |
| Annual variable cost, upkeep and allocated platform operation | 3.500 | Annex departmental projection at full phase-in |
| Annual net reduction | 2.900 | Annex projection in 2026 prices and pay levels |

The annex's implementation envelope equals development plus the shared
platform and 0.500 for startup operation. Keep the price basis and
accounting perimeter in the annex. Its 3.500 annual operating cost
includes only the department's allocated platform share. Without the
projected staff expenditure reduction the same modeled departmental
lines would show a 3.500 operating cost increase, before any additional
safeguards. This is a conditional reading of the table, with no claim
about realized spending or actual staffing. [Budget annex, Table 2](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/agenda/3a272486-0e32-431f-855c-6cf8666f6e8a/77ccaad0-e2b6-4faf-a611-be4ce3f8db97-bilag-1.pdf)

## Published scope and independent scrutiny

The municipal operating notice describes general information and
routing to services such as help at home. It directs individual
casework to staff, says general conversations do not require
personal details, and describes transcript masking and a 90-day
quality-improvement retention period. It describes personal
assistance during staffed telephone hours. This is a description
of the declared route. It does not verify successful transfer,
retention practice or protected first receipt for anyone.
[Municipal operating notice](https://www.kk.dk/om-kommunen/databeskyttelse/databeskyttelse-i-forvaltningerne/databeskyttelse-i-kultur-fritids-og-borgerserviceforvaltningen/talebot-hos-koebenhavns-borgerservice)

In August 2026 the Parliamentary Ombudsman announced inquiries into
AI uses in several municipalities, including direct public contact
tools in the wider inquiry. The Copenhagen letter inspected here
asks about a different casework tool and the municipality's wider
AI practice. It does not establish a finding about this voice route.
[Ombudsman announcement and letters](https://www.ombudsmanden.dk/find-viden/nyheder/2026/ombudsmanden-spoerger-til-kommuners-brug-af-ai-til-sagsbehandling)

We infer that approval to buy technology and a forecast about call
handling should be separate from a decision to reduce staffed access.
The inspected records do not supply matched evidence of residents'
timely filing, urgent outcomes, repeat contact or usable receipt
through an outage.
