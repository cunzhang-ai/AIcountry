# Reach actual activities and prevent harm at its source

*TS01–TS02; part of [technical safety](../22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md).*

**TS01 — The law regulates a specified activity, not technological identity.**
Apply TD01–TD12 only within actual national territorial/personal/control powers,
with an identifiable operator, product, site or reserved act. Public and private
operators meet the same applicable floor. A domestic purchase or global platform
account creates no worldwide inspection power. Actual treaties, immunities,
foreign access and cross-border duties remain separate questions under B/P/C10.

Ordinary association, speech, chosen support, noninvasive information, household
food preparation, low-risk repair, walking and ordinary cycling require no
technical license under this Act. General civil duties and actual serious-harm
law still apply. AI use alone is neither a licensed profession nor a dangerous
activity. Unfamiliar future methods receive reasoned functional classification,
with challenge, not exclusion because they lack a familiar name.

The schedule identifies the responsible sector role, entry condition and
conduct. C04/C09/C07/V allocate public functions; it creates neither twelve new
ministries nor one final super-regulator. Land use, working conditions, privacy,
clinical consent, taxes, criminal culpability and extraordinary force retain
their own laws. A safety certificate cannot authorize an otherwise unlawful act.

**TS02 — Reduce the danger before allocating blame.** Within actual control,
operators must identify foreseeable serious hazards, maintenance/degradation,
common failures, affected non-users and feasible safer alternatives. Prefer
eliminating the hazard, safer substitution and engineering protections before
relying only on warnings, training or individual vigilance. Record a proportionate
reasoned choice; this is no promise that every accident can be prevented.

Demonstrate critical barriers in the actual place/use, including power, network,
supply, maintenance and emergency failure. A normal average score or one perfect
demonstration does not establish safe operation. Preserve worker/user reports
and contrary observations. People reasonably withdrawing from an imminent serious
work danger receive protection against retaliation and usable review; do not
compel return while the danger persists or require a finished expert dossier.

An accident is not itself guilt. Actual breach/control/fault/causation, feasible
ability and P/V/B defenses require their own proof. A final worker's error cannot
erase upstream design, unsafe staffing, incentives or the public operator's
maintenance decisions. Nor does a systemic explanation automatically prove any
named manager criminally liable. Material residual risk and distribution remain
public choices where law reserves them to elected authority.

[GA01–GA24](../../C11/10-general-ai-capability-and-public-control.md) separately
proposes upstream general-capability severe-risk testing/release law. It is an
express new Act/remit, not an expansion of TD01–TD12 by analogy. AI use alone
still needs no TS licence; a GA permission supplies no TD sector permission.
