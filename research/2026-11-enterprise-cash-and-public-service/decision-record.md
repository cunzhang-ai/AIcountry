# Design439. Separate enterprise survival from service continuity

Design438 is the pinned predecessor. OE41–OE46 assign and fund public
mandates. T79–T84 carry the operator, sponsor and creditor cash into
the first programme with distinct corporate and service alternatives.
Use Denmark's DSB as a bounded historical comparison. This revision
used AI assistance and parallel evidence review.

Retain ordinary performance under a funded contract where possible.
Consider exact early contract settlement and separately qualified
credit where it preserves due service. An alternate operator can
limit rescue exposure but needs a lawful, staffed handover. A wider
recapitalization can preserve capability with fiscal risk and a need
for explicit elected choice. Select early settlement in the worked
case only where the existing authority, funding and actual receiving
qualify. It does not discharge the next cash requirement. Keep
enterprise creditors' claims and any guarantee terms separately
visible, including where a guarantee and a service shock coincide.

The [sources](sources.md) establish reported revenue, financing and
mandate terms within their own scopes. The constructed scenario tests
two deadlines. No actual payment audit, affected participation,
independent qualified review, appropriation or enactment occurred.

Keep existing service and worker rights, contracts, evidence,
authority and paid independent remedies through succession.
For a real opening schedule, match one service payment with the
operator's dated financing and protection through the next call.
Reopen on a change in facility availability, guarantee exposure,
actual receiving or relative cost. Full programme readiness remains open.
