# Repayment from safe resources and a real end to ordinary debt

*U05–U08; part of [C08 private insolvency](../14-private-insolvency-and-debt-recovery.md).*

**U05 — Actual means and reviewable contribution.** Use net safely available
resources after lawful tax/contributions and actual necessary personal/child/
disability/work costs, reconciling the same support/bill once. A creditor
cannot deem a spouse's income, an inaccessible account or an unreceived award
available. Do not require borrowing, compulsory treatment, unsafe work or
unpaid family care to meet a plan. The candidate plan contributes half the
positive excess above verified needs; earnings within need produce zero
ordinary contribution. Keep U03's protected buffer and a bounded way to rebuild
it; do not repeatedly exempt cash then count it as paid. Change actual expense,
earnings and care circumstances promptly with notice/independent challenge.
A court can approve a lower feasible amount with reasons; an income prediction
cannot become an impossible fixed payment. Rates/needs are hypotheses, not a
validated credit-market or clinical adequacy claim.

**U06 — Ordinary discharge and finite process.** After nonexempt estate
realization and a compliant plan, included ordinary pre-opening unsecured
debt is discharged by the adopted rule at 36 months from lawful opening,
unless a timely court order under U07 imposes a bounded extension. Later
certification records that effect; trustee paperwork cannot postpone it. A person with no realizable nonexempt assets and
no reasonably evidenced positive excess receives prompt discharge after
notified claim review; the court must decide that request within 90 calendar
days of opening. A blocked decision retains direct alternate review and the
recorded unmet duty, not approval by silence or a reset clock. No three-year
wait is needed solely to verify poverty.
Creditors have a funded proportionate hearing on actual concealment/dispute;
missing records outside the person's control cannot defeat cooperation.
Voluntary fair settlement can finish earlier. The court issues the exact
included/released claim record; providers correct collection/credit records.
A fixed plan that becomes genuinely unaffordable is varied, not automatically
revoked. Transfers, a new trustee or failed software cannot restart the clock.

**U07 — Exceptions, misconduct and affected people.** Discharge does not
cancel future child/dependant duties, valid remaining collateral rights,
third-party property, unrelated post-opening obligations or a separately
lawful criminal disposition. Accrued child/dependant maintenance and proved
intentional violence/fraud compensation are protected from ordinary discharge;
amount, actual fault and exception status require fair independent adjudication,
not a creditor's label or an unproved accusation. These exceptional claims
can survive beyond the ordinary deadline but collection of pre-opening
exceptions uses U05's same half-excess ceiling after current family duties,
with actual ability, subsistence and independent payment review;
no inability-based detention or forced work. Public care/safety and an owed
support recipient's floor remain distinct, not presumed paid by a poor debtor.
Their eventual unrecovered loss is explicit; an unfinanced victim fund cannot
be called delivery. The value conflict is retained, not averaged away.

Material intentional asset/claim concealment or fraudulent abuse can justify
one reasoned extension of ordinary discharge up to 36 further months, or
correction of the fraudulently obtained portion, after notice, meaningful
proof and appeal. Ordinary failed business, mistaken estimates or needed
support transfers are not fraud. The state/creditor proves the alleged abuse
under its applicable civil standard; criminal punishment retains P's separate
proof. Correct the affected claim/benefit, not all unrelated discharge by an
opaque blacklist. Even misconduct gives no open-ended process, punishment
quota, loss of vote or denial of essential services.

**U08 — Guarantors, private records and fresh participation.** Personal
and small-firm guarantees need clear informed scope; involuntary household
or unlimited hidden guarantees are not supplied by kinship or an AI summary.
Debtor discharge does not automatically discharge a valid consenting guarantor,
but that person's own eligible case can be coordinated without pooling
unrelated property or restarting limits. Notify both and prevent double
collection. Record the actual loss bearer, lender provision, contribution and
continuing rights. Public insolvency notice includes only data needed for
claims/proceedings; safe private records and correction survive. Credit systems
label discharge accurately and expire an ordinary completed-case adverse flag
after three years, with earlier correction of errors; this does not require
new lending or erase necessary lawful confidential source history. No civic,
housing/service or employment exclusion follows insolvency status alone.
