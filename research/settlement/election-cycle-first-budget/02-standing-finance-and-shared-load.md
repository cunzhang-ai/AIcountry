# Compare early election funding while ordinary obligations are due

This authored example takes the observed order of events as a
question to test. All units, dates expressed as offsets, staffing
and liabilities below are hypotheses for the candidate. They do
not convert DKK or substitute for Copenhagen's protected records.

## Due cash and independent receipt

An early election can be called in a year without a scheduled poll.
The pre-existing legal timetable and authority are identical across
options. The existing election administration requires two units
for readiness before the call, three more by the first protected
access date, five more by the poll and two more before the useful
deadline for a count and remedy. Thus cumulative electoral needs
are 2, 5, 10 and 12. The elected laws in this example allow
exactly those duties. Civil, service and payment obligations stay
on their own sources. By the poll four local care units and two
ordinary justice units are due. Five household payment units are
due at the final stage. All three ordinary sources are separately
authorized and fully used. Ordinary and electoral remedies have
distinct receiving staff and lawful funding.

| Financing arrangement | Authorized cash available cumulatively at the four due dates | Effect under an executive's discretionary refusal |
|---|---|---|
| Maintain two units, then rely on a discretionary general-reserve transfer | 2, 2, 2, 2. An after-window transfer could bring the total to 12. | Early access, the poll and timely review lack 3, 8 and 10 units. Later money leaves missed opportunities and claims visible. |
| Enact a standing ordinary floor and dated event and review tranches | 2, 5, 10, 12, under the prior-law trigger and cap. | All four cash dates fit if an independent authorized payer, accepting operators and held balances actually work. |
| Protect the same dated capacity inside a common contingency account | 2, 5, 10, 12 if the ceiling, priorities and accessible cash are ring-fenced. | Can meet the same duty. Four units informally counted for care as well would leave four units short across the two scopes. |

The first choice is a strong rival where lawful earlier access is
demonstrated and another approval is only reconciliation. The second
is selected as the transparent standing rule. The third can implement
the same rule without a new permanent institution if its protection
and alternate are enforceable. A separately endowed electoral body
would reduce ordinary Treasury control, but would add custody,
allocation and budget-oversight risks. The design keeps the Assembly's
authority and tests the funded alternate and oversight chain. A failed
alternate leaves an exposed duty and available lawful review, with
no new power to call, postpone or annul the election.

## Same-window operating choice

Assume four qualified technical shifts are actually free during the
overlap. Election access and review support require two. The
household payment route requires two. A proposed optional shared
migration needs two more. No finance decision supplies the missing
two shifts, and a cutover that shares the only signing authority
can harm both routes. Keep the current protected routes and delay
the optional cutover in this case. When separate qualified teams and
independently accepted receiving are verified, a scoped change may
be possible. Urgent repairs under existing powers still receive
their own necessity and alternate-route test.

If a second event is lawfully ordered after a first contest fully
used its cap, require an unspent, separate lawful repeat provision or
a new vote. Do not re-open the spent tranche. Preserve outstanding
claims and original remedies while securing the source. The
[budget lapse rule](../../country/C06/66-budget-lapse-and-paid-continuity.md)
continues to govern bridges and late obligations.
