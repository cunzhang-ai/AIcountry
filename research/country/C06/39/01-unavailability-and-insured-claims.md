# Deposit unavailability and the original insured claim

Design99 · Proposed research, not enacted law or funded bank protection.

**BF01 — Two decisions, not one rescue switch.** M07 insured-deposit unavailability and M09
critical-function resolution are distinct. The insurer's preassigned qualified statutory claims
officer finds M07 unavailability and instructs insured payment within enacted scope, without the
resolution panel, minister, bank owner or insurer chief approving a case. M09's separately
constituted authority may transfer/write down/bridge only on its additional nonviability **and**
public critical-function necessity findings. A decision not to use M09 leaves insurer payout and
ordinary U bank liquidation available. Neither an M07 finding nor the 12B limit guarantees uninsured
credit or bank survival.

**BF02 — Objective M07 event and first clock.** Each licensed legal member bank has its own
objective T_bank. A deposit is unavailable when that bank fails to meet a **lawfully due** covered
withdrawal because it cannot pay from its own lawful resources and no evidenced prompt private or
solvent M15 cure can meet that due obligation. The first such qualifying event, or an earlier
effective inability-based liquidation/closure order stopping repayment, crystallizes all otherwise
eligible deposit balances owed by that bank for insurance, including its undemanded term deposits;
the insurance amount includes lawful accrued interest through that date, not unearned future
interest, and insurer subrogation retains the actual underlying estate rights. An application,
interim stay, mere risk forecast, disputed claimant instruction, one service outage with accessible
funds and an accepted alternate route, or a capital ratio breach alone is not unavailability. An
earlier objectively unpaid due withdrawal remains the earlier T0. The bank must report such a
funding default to supervisor and insurer within two actual hours of known occurrence, with
protected original balances, maturity, cash, settlement and cure sources; the court/receiver must
promptly transmit an effective closure order and source record to the insurer. The officer must make
a **preliminary statutory finding authorizing immediate undisputed payout, or reasoned rejection**
within six actual hours of the first *documented credible* bank/supervisor/depositor/court report or
own original observation, after the bank's immediate feasible reply; an uncompleted source
verification cannot become an indefinite gate. A depositor may submit an ordinary accessible report;
no complete technical dossier is prerequisite to first receipt. Conflict uses a predesignated
qualified case substitute, not bank or chief selection. These six/two-hour clocks require an
actually paid 24-hour qualified roster, counsel, independent record access and payer, none yet
established; failure gives a reasoned omission and direct court remedy, not automatic guilt or
insurer power beyond law. This is insurance-only crystallization: it does not by implication
accelerate the estate contract's original maturity, promise unearned future interest or change other
creditors' rank.

**BF03 — No delay by recognition.** M07 time zero is that licensed bank's earliest objective BF02
qualifying event, not the date an officer or court finally notices it. The insurer records that

transfer or identity request does not restart that event. The court may order the existing
insurer/payer to decide and pay immediately, with a reasoned arrear/remedy for elapsed duties; it
cannot make a past payment actually received. The bank and affected depositors have urgent
answer/review, without an automatic payout stay; source-error correction does not claw back an
innocent payment merely by relabelling it. Actual due claimant ownership and source facts still need
proof.
