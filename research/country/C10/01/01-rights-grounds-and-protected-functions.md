# RX01–RX04. Rights do not disappear when conditions worsen

*Design144 · [Safety and emergency continuity](../01-public-safety-and-emergencies.md) · Candidate constitutional/statutory allocation, not actual law.*

**RX01 — Select no domestic derogation.** No declaration, renewal, statute,
contract, foreign request or AI output may make a constitutional right cease
to apply during an emergency. A restriction within that right's lawful terms
is different from derogation. Constitutional emergency review is a constraint,
not an enabling route for an otherwise prohibited act. The country does not
claim a generic constitutional necessity power. Binding foreign/humanitarian
obligations retain actual scope; a treaty's possible permission is not domestic
competence. Internal amendment cannot abolish A9's protected democratic functions.
This does not freeze ordinary policy quantities or prevent A13's express lawful
prospective revision within constitutional rights. A11's essential-access duty and
C06's lawful scarcity allocation do not promise absent workers, stock or FX; actual
unmet claims remain duties for source-backed public correction, not rights erased.

**RX02 — State the protected content.** Torture/cruel or degrading treatment,
slavery/servitude and prohibited forced exploitation, secret detention/enforced
disappearance, capital punishment, nonconsensual medical/scientific experimentation,
arbitrary killing and punishment without applicable prior law remain forbidden.
C08's actual legality/leniency and independently applicable international-crime
rules remain; no retroactive offense or harsher penalty is created here. Everyone
retains legal personality and inner freedom of thought, conscience and belief.
The state cannot buy compliance with threats to nationality, basic personhood,
family safety or essential services. Genocide, persecution, collective punishment
and arbitrary discrimination gain no exception. This is protected content, not
an exhaustive code or a license to weaken another existing protection.

A nonderogable right need not be absolute in every manifestation: a specifically
justified unsafe-building exclusion may affect worship, while inner belief cannot
be compelled. Life protection does not authorize a new lethal threshold; LF and
actual armed-conflict law keep distinct grounds. Medical care without ascertainable
will remains the disputed narrow UA source, not research experimentation or a
present-refusal override. Naming a right in this paragraph does not silently
settle its contested applications or every treaty's independent requirements.

**RX03 — Justify an ordinary restriction right by right.** The responsible public
body identifies accessible prior law, competent actor, permissible purpose under
the specific right, original facts/uncertainty, causal connection, actual affected
scope, feasible less intrusive measures, duration, unequal burdens and remedy.
It bears the justification burden before independent review; an affected person
need not disprove a secret risk score. Public safety, public health or “balance”
is not a general-purpose legal ground for every interference. Do not offset one
person's absolute protection against an aggregate benefit, or disguise an arbitrary
classification as need. Narrow ordinary rules remain subject to rights and actual
sector thresholds even if the same facts could support a lawful emergency label.

Legitimate conflicting rights require reasons within their actual terms; no universal
AI welfare score settles them. Distinguish values, definitions, empirical uncertainty
and legal conclusions. Preserve meaningful objections and new contrary observations,
including safe testimony not fit for permanent public release. Unsupported future
media or shared-situation meanings require disclosed interpretation, not silent
conversion into consent, criminal intent or a threat category.

**RX04 — Practical public control remains.** An emergency cannot change franchise,
seat conversion/boundaries, current terms, succession, appointment sources/votes,
constitutional review, amendment rules or ordinary tax by executive instrument.
A9/RR still governs ordinary institutional changes, including current/immediate
succession and the closed predecessor-law restoration exception. No emergency
labels a desirable change neutral restoration. Opposition, independent journalism,
association, union activity, lawful status advocacy, access to counsel and actual
rights remedies must remain usable. Hazard controls may change a place or method
only on their own lawful grounds with practical alternative access; they cannot
eliminate the underlying political function or select favored speakers.

**Design166 physical-source qualification.** Separately enacted
[PS21–PS32](../../C06/37/06-critical-public-power-and-actual-priority.md) proposes
one express qualification to the alternative-access precondition above: the
scheduled critical public backup-power allocation may leave a powered political/
remedy task temporarily unavailable without an already working substitute.
This changes that prior blanket condition for this source allocation alone;
it does not authorize hazard closures to eliminate political functions. Actual
unavailable/lost access remains democratic/constitutional delivery failure, not
fulfilled relief. No order certifies all resulting rights interferences lawful;
A8's protected content, no derogation and competent independent review still
constrain/reject an act. No change of vote/term/source law or executive extension
follows. PS28's proposed no-fault contest ground has its separate RR transition;
current constitutional/software/permission holds and actual prior law persist.

A physically impossible poll is not an executive extension. Existing electoral
source law/competent courts supply only actually authorized narrow remedies;
RX supplies no extra postponement, caretaker term or date-choosing jurisdiction.
Catastrophic constitutional discontinuity remains a genuine failure, requiring
legitimate public/legal recovery rather than an AI or project owner's government.
