# FB09–FB12. Budget balances, service recovery and capacity genuinely released

*Design198 · Proposed addition to [lasting services and fiscal shocks](../62-lasting-services-and-fiscal-shocks.md).*

**FB09 — Explain a material variance before assigning it to a new use.**
For each material favourable budget variance proposed for disposition, the
responsible agency and Treasury reconcile the enacted and revised scope,
actual expense and cash, lawful unperformed contracts and due claims,
restrictions, and the service actually available to eligible people. Show
the gross lines before netting. One programme can overspend in operations
while projects and earmarked funds go unused. Distinguish lawful finished
work, demand or price change, a lawful cancellation, late start, vacancy,
unpaid obligation, failed access and unresolved cause. One cause can affect
multiple lines. Reconcile overlaps once, keep needs and ownership explicit
and distinguish spending authority from usable cash.

Treat a reduced need or a genuine change in method separately from an
unfilled duty. Where the lower cost reflects missed receiving or deferred
work, assign the existing owner and a dated lawful payment, repair or
challenge path. Preserve prior due dates, lawful contract terms and
individual execution. Protect genuinely uncommitted funds from a
spend-by-year-end incentive. Prior law controls carry-forward, restricted
money, lapses and cancellations. The label alone neither earmarks the
balance nor frees it. Use the existing DB report or budget submission
before a vote on disposition. An earlier urgent remedy follows its own time.

**FB10 — Count a recurring release at its demonstrated net scope.**
An efficiency claim used to pay for a lasting offer must identify the
specific avoidable payment or other recurring spending displaced, the date
and the lawful budget change that realizes it. Compare against a realistic
same-scope retain-and-repair path, including changes in case mix and demand.
Show delivery through a completed relevant operating cycle, including
observed peak conditions or an explicit narrower supported claim and
separately funded peak provision. Compare usable access, due-time receipt,
quality, correction, safety and continuity for the people most likely to
lose access. Record known exclusions and foreseeable load changes. Before treating
low take-up as low need, compare feasible hours, price, distance, eligibility
and assisted access. Invite safely reachable people who could not apply or
use the offer and disclose whose experience is missing. Use safe sampling
rather than a universal register. Faster decisions and a short trial are
limited evidence.
Projections of future efficiencies may be shown as scenarios. Unsubstantiated
amounts cannot be presented as qualified recurring sources.

Deduct new recurring public spending for operation, review, correction,
accessible alternatives, reserves and other transferred costs at the
consolidated scope. Profile finite deployment, contract exit, retraining,
overlap and continuing claims separately. Show costs and time displaced
to households, workers, other providers and the environment as separate
incidence. A compulsory shift to unpaid household care or an unlawful
reduction in access cannot qualify as an efficiency. A real prospective
policy reduction remains an express elected value choice under FB02 and
the applicable rights. Price paid hours and usable released capacity
separately. Retaining a worker for another task can release capacity with
no reduction in payroll, and the same release cannot fund two offers.

**FB11 — Match the decision to the money, the service and its receiver.**
Cabinet or the responsible local executive brings the disposition to its
competent elected body under DB. Trace the owner, cash date and receiving
service. Where the cause is lack of workers, useful sites or a payment
operator, compare repairing that constraint with funding another qualified
route or leaving the resource available for lawful later choice. Preserve
the status of the worker, supplier and each resident's due right. Finite
authorized uses can rely on actually usable finite sources after prior
duties, restrictions and liquidity are addressed. A material permanent
expansion uses qualified recurring sources under FB02. The Assembly
can choose a smaller scope, another supported source or a finite trial
with exact terms. Neither an outstanding verification nor this rule
automatically stays a right, takes local resources or gives a reviewer a
veto over legislation.

For a material uncertain release, any trial and eventual reallocation
identify who can restore the existing lawful service and how staff, access,
cash and records would reach them. Do not book paid fallback and the same
capacity as released. Give members and affected local bodies usable
assumptions and alternatives through ordinary budget rights. Keep affected
residents' reports and corrections reachable through AP and actual
independent challenge, with protected records. Use existing AA audit,
competent courts and SR enforcement within their respective powers.

**FB12 — Correct adverse results without erasing obligations.**
A material shortfall in the receiving or source evidence triggers DB
and FB06 promptly, before the first affected due time where possible.
Earlier sector protection and court orders remain in force. Separate late payment, delivery failure, a
false saving and deterioration from unrelated conditions. Keep undisputed
amounts, enforceable orders, supplier and worker obligations, maintenance
and earned rights on their own schedules. Identify the replacement source,
service restoration or open failure. The executive cannot silently remove
an enacted service or allocate the same shortfall to households twice.
Record actual receipt under SR where a public order applies.

AI may reconcile accounts, flag probable misclassification and compare
scenarios, with source provenance and a paid human alternative. It cannot
certify a permanent source, erase an unresolved line, dispose of money or
approve the accuracy of its own deployment. Independent evidence and
ordinary political decision and appeal remain distinct. Keep funded
record, payment and service successors through OP/T and HT. Reopen when
there is material changed demand, a missed service deadline, a shared
failure, transferred workload or a comparable better path.
