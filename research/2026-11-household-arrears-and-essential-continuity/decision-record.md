# Design330 decision on household arrears and essential continuity

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design329, HL, ordinary tenancy, EG, WS, income protection, U,
B17 and the working framework. The country already protects essential
supply and safe housing and has independent court relief. Add one
dated coordination route for current bills and a narrow pre-action
pause where covered rent arrears alone threaten a safe primary home.
The project assistant authors the choice. Actual lawmakers, review
and case officers retain their distinct powers.

## Alternatives and evidence

Compare ordinary sector-by-sector handling, a general creditor
moratorium, and current-service coordination with a narrow pause.
Separate handling may be adequate where ordinary routes meet their
dates. A general stay provides broader time but neither finances
current charges nor treats different rights and providers distinctly.
Select funded continuation and a short, reviewable pre-action pause.
The selected seven-day and 30-day boundaries are design choices.
Landlords can face delayed receipt and contested scope. Retain fair
notice and timely independent challenge, including separate
lawfully funded support where justified.

The [primary comparators](sources.md) describe existing moratoria,
pre-action duties and energy-payment support. They do not establish
the candidate's deadlines or the benefits claimed for the worked case.

## Authority, migration and reopening

The prospective Act defines covered rent claims, eligible homes,
evidence, duration and independent review. The local steward coordinates
and may pause specified new proceedings within law. Existing orders
stay within judicial authority. Income and utility officers retain
their own decisions. Legislatures fund the actual first and later
protection. Existing contract rights, source rules, orders and stronger
sector protections survive the change.

Test an actual joint intake with consenting tenants, creditor and
provider input, independent review and qualified safe receiving.
Follow the first missed date and next bill period. Compare actual
household cost, late payments, access, creditor exposure and outcomes
with ordinary repair. Reopen on repeated pauses, worsening arrears,
coercive consent, delayed service or a simpler protective route.
Actual legal authority, appropriation, staff and outcomes remain open.
