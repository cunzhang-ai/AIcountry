# MF25–MF30. Fund local services when several fiscal shocks arrive together

*Design372 · Part of [funded local mandates](../08-funded-local-mandates-and-equalization.md) · Proposed fiscal law.*

**MF25 — Preauthorize a service reserve for joint shocks.** The Assembly
may finance a reserve under MF16 for common duties exposed to a verified
involuntary revenue loss, material new need or failure of already due
public settlement. Vote its source, eligible acts, maximum gross exposure,
payment dates, finite advance period and a separately priced second call.
Test geographically shared revenue and workforce losses alongside an
isolated failure. Keep ordinary equalization, person-side protection and
independent election and court funding on their own authority and account.
A reserve ceiling, projected tax receipt or an expectation of reimbursement
is not settled cash. Conditional borrowing needs its own valid voted terms
and actual counterparty settlement.

**MF26 — Separate the shortfall and identify the service it threatens.**
Apply the adopted representative tax-effort baseline. Separate loss from
local tax choices, discretionary expense, an ordinary timing delay and a
durable reduction of the base. First execute undisputed amounts already
owed, subject to the existing source and remedies. For each proposed
reserve advance, the local authority and competent sector owner show the
uncovered duty, earliest cash cutoff, last useful service date, smallest
safe and deliverable package, paid operator and actual acceptance. Deduct
only lawful sources available in time. Include an accepted cheaper
alternative where it preserves useful receipt. A locality's voluntary
revenue decision does not enlarge its reserve claim. Residents still
retain due protection and any independently lawful LD backstop.

**MF27 — Compare simultaneous calls before making a discretionary release.**
Treasury's career officer compares documented calls, including competing
claims expected before the relevant payment cutoff. Use an enacted
priority schedule based on protection of rights, seriousness of
irreversible harm and the latest useful time, with qualified sector
assessment and recorded reasons. Protect every already due payment and
reservation within its own prior authority. Give remaining money to
complete verified packages in the enacted order. Make partial releases
only where the sector owner verifies safe useful delivery or a
lawfully accepted bridge. A proportional split that delivers neither
service does not qualify. Where otherwise equivalent claims cannot
both fit, use the prior law's independently witnessed neutral tie
procedure. Neither application speed nor political affiliation breaks
a tie. Record unfunded calls at their original dates.

**MF28 — Pay without controlling the local government.** The competent
career officer issues a reasoned, scoped allocation and the statutory
payer executes it by the original useful date. Publish safe evidence,
cash used, rejected requests and outstanding protection needs. Affected
councils, opposition members and residents retain direct independent
review under applicable law with access to relevant protected evidence.
Use an eligible substitute when the authorizer, payer or reviewer is
implicated. Verify appointments, funding, usable records and execution
under MF and B19. The national source may require lawful reporting and
actual common-duty receiving. It cannot demand a coalition agreement,
a particular lawful local delivery model or a favored contractor.
LD14–LD18 remains a distinct service action with its own grounds.

**MF29 — Decide the next call before treating the first as solved.** Track
settled local cash, accepted service, the next call, and whether national
receipts or backup suppliers fell at the same time. Where the reserve
cannot cover the qualified set, publish the exact shortfall and bring
the earliest lawful supplement, funded shared delivery or smaller safe
protection to the competent elected process. Use only previously lawful
commitments until an alternative takes effect. Do not renew a temporary
advance or reset its clock to cover a permanent need. Treat a persistent
gap under MF17 and FB with a recurring source or a lawful ordinary
service redesign. Record actual lost service even if later finance arrives.

**MF30 — Settle once, carry obligations and check distribution.** Reconcile
advances against the applicable final source only once. Identify separately
excess, unpaid duty, delayed recovery and continuing source. Carry lawful
claims, contracts and safe records through a change of payer or council.
Report whether regions repeatedly lose actual service under the rule.
AI may reconcile amounts, assumptions and deadlines. It cannot decide
severity, choose fiscal priorities, compel allocation or attest actual
receipt. Reopen on politically selective timing, repeated correlated
exhaustion, supplier dependence or a simpler equivalent funded route.
