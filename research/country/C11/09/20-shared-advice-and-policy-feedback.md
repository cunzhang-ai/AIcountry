# DP95–DP99. Keep shared advice from manufacturing its own evidence

*Design513 · Part of [public digital operation](../09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md).*

**DP95 — Review a shared inference when it can change several public decisions.** Before material reliance, the responsible owners identify affected decisions where a common forecast, objective, data transformation or measure of success could steer two or more authorities toward reinforcing action. Include ordinary models and reused forecasts. Trace source observations, inferred quantities, policy assumptions and case or service effects. Include advice that changes resource allocations, queues or access before a formal decision. Different suppliers and qualified officers can still depend on the same assumption. A common authentic source alone does not establish a common faulty inference. Leave routine clerical and genuinely independent work on its own route.

**DP96 — Distinguish need from what the policy allows people to do.** Where a decision would itself change the observation later used to evaluate it, compare the exposure with decisions held constant, informed alternatives and direct independent evidence for the affected population. Count open requests, unavailable or inaccessible routes, displaced demand, actual receipt and unknowns separately. Use safe shadow comparisons, separately controlled observation or other qualified designs. Do not deliberately withhold a due service to collect evidence. Require an accountable reviewer to test a plausible contrary explanation before relying on the disputed inference to cut protected capacity or to claim a causal gain. A manual signature, a higher prediction accuracy on admitted cases or consensus among models does not supply the missing observation.

**DP97 — Give every decision an accountable owner and a joint alternative.** The existing support office convenes affected owners and reconciles exact dependencies, timelines, independent observation, public finance and remedies. Compare retaining competent services, using AI for a separable support task and applying an independently verified change to the joint service. Where a shared model may still save time, retain that supported use under its exact purpose and assign any verified gain to usable work. For material shared reductions or changes in authority, present the cross-sector alternative to the competent elected bodies using ordinary votes; each sector retains its powers and responsibility. Fund an independent challenge, and give affected people and opposition timely access and a response. Preserve separately staffed authorship and submission of a qualified alternative that the common rubric cannot represent. Carry the exact text and material objections to consideration under the applicable elected procedure through a route outside the sponsoring tool. This scope adds no cross-sector veto or new decision authority to a coordinator, model, auditor or court.

**DP98 — Stop the unqualified use and follow affected acts.** If the model cannot distinguish actual receipt from feedback caused by the policy, hold only legally conditional downstream reliance on that claim. Use existing independent review, escalation and remedies for binding acts and actual failures. Send the scoped correction to the responsible decision and service owners under DP40 and EV. Preserve earlier rights, original dates, lawful alternatives, directly supported useful parts of the service and purpose-restricted source custody. Check whether redirection worsens another duty and whether the next service window can accept it. Voluntary evidence from people who could not enter the service does not decide their eligibility or authorize new cross-service linkage.

**DP99 — Verify independence through ordinary change.** Recheck shared exposure when a valid observation has been replaced by a post-decision metric, rights or thresholds change, a use crosses sectors or a common software, legal or data dependency changes. Independent comparison is done by someone able to inspect the relevant originals and responsibly test the alternative without relying on the decision's disputed derivation. It need not duplicate each model or every dataset. Verify that a public employee can document a reasoned disagreement without a supplier or supervisor penalizing mere divergence through ratings, hours or assignment. Apply existing employment and independent review rules to actual conduct and restore any practical means of disagreement. Report actual service, unknowns, costs, contested scope and independent review outcomes before expansion. Reopen when a simpler ordinary method matches outcomes, actual gains fail to reach people, or control rests with the same gatekeeper across independent bodies.
