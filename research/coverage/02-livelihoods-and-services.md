**Design168 earnings transition.** [EB01–EB16](../country/C05/16-portable-earnings-and-work-transition.md)
adds a capped-history person-paid buffer within the L04/L06 work/income interfaces,
not missing first subsistence protection. Actual source/agency/history/older-
insurance/tax/I05/Y matching is unpriced; the original37 financial objects remain,
and this pointer proves no full national function census or employment outcome.

[WF49–WF52 joint offer](../country/C06/58/11-paid-time-joint-capacity-and-net-source.md)
retains paid time and services, separates actual unmet need/qualified task gain,
private/public/foreign recruitment, G net source and restricted S. Original row/
function identities and obligations remain; labels/bids do not price all new
analysis/cover or establish a closed census, actual source or safe receiving.

[AL adult capability](../country/C07/22-lifelong-learning-and-renewal.md) adds
person-initiated renewal, a distinct continuing offer, academic/worker/learner
governance and paid access/continuity. Original37 single-owned function rows
remain; old education/N bids price no full AL scope and the census is open.
New actual capacity/source/independent choice and outcomes remain unknown.

[YC stable child care](../country/C07/27-stable-child-care-and-adult-transition.md)
qualifies placements, funds independent receiving and follows a young
adult to actually accepted housing and support. Staffing, cost and
outcomes remain open within the existing care function.

[HL housing loss](../country/C09/25-housing-loss-and-secure-receiving.md)
assigns prevention, qualified emergency receiving and ordinary tenure
with paid continuity under the existing housing function.

[AT technology transition](../country/C05/18-technology-transition-and-shared-gains.md)
joins earnings, fiscal sources and actual services in an observed-loss
response. Actual incidence, affected input and funded receiving remain open.
