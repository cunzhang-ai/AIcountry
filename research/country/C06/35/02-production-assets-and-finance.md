# FD04–FD06: production, assets and counterpart finance

*Part of [C06 joined bridge](../35-finance-production-and-delivery-bridge.md); design87.*

**FD04 — Compare the same production measure.** Six accounts are households H,
general public G, commissioned service suppliers E, other consolidated domestic
production F, restricted insurance S and rest-of-world R. This is a planned
country ledger, not six actual institutions or an exhaustive industry model.

Public output equals public wages + consumed inputs + capital consumption;
public **value added** excludes purchased inputs. E invoice minus its inputs
is E value added. F output is solved with household desired spending, domestic
inputs, actual capital purchases and external trade; imported intermediates
are deducted for F value added. Add household product tax for market-price GDP.
Expenditure GDP uses final public/household consumption, actual capital and
exports minus imports. Public rent appears once as H spending, subtracting it
from public final consumption. S's3m administration payment is an internal
public-sector counterpart, not extra consolidated production or revenue.

The selective-relief plan requires F value added 2,731.911m against an
authored nominal benchmark of 2,149.909m, a 582.002m difference at the same
price boundary. [IC13–IC14](../../C05/09/04-production-before-returns.md) now
retires that comparison as a physical-supply test or ranking: onshoring can
raise domestic GVA while reducing imported requirements. The old numerical
scale and multipliers are hypothetical value comparisons, not independent
capacity, achieved productivity or AI gains. Actual quantities, skills,
food/water/energy, delivery and firm-level constraints require their own account.

**FD05 — Distinguish assets, upkeep, reserves and purchases.** CFC estimates
capital used. The main plan actually purchases full annual replacement plus
the selected retrofit flow; a half-renewal sensitivity retains the difference
as restricted G cash or E cash inside its paid invoice. That difference is
not extra public revenue or replacement delivered. Specialist upkeep excludes
routine payroll/materials already costed. Public4m subscription for E's retrofit
creates a G financial equity claim; E's actual4m capital enters GDP once.

F's opening private structures10,650m and other productive assets2,000m are
explicit stock hypotheses. Private site land2,840m and public housing land160m
are separately valued, nondepreciating stocks; they neither generate construction
nor spendable receipts. Actual title, territorial claims and productive engineering
are unknown. Initial service assets are not bought a second time in operating
uses. A target balance sheet is not evidence that founding sources exist.
Keep the unknown pension asset H/liability S and accrual L(t) paired, never zero.

**FD06 — A funding residual is not an overdraft or consent.** The model's
opening settlement balances are explicit claims on an external counterparty,
with its matching liability. This accounting representation does not select
foreign currency, a central bank, a deposit license or actual source access.
Gross public coupons, due principal, partial rollover offers, new lender offers,
minimum public cash and restricted renewal are separate. New year-end borrowing
creates next-period coupons, not a zero-cost recurring finance source.

Only disclosed fractions of current voluntary H saving and unrestricted firm
retention are offered as new bonds; principal already repaid is not counted
again as another source. E renewal money and bank coupon retention are excluded;
new F claims belong to nonbank firms, with actual firm/source controls unverified.
S makes no automatic new loan. Legacy bank175exposure/equity200 remains only an
opening prudential hypothesis; a changed consolidated F account is no bank approval.

All four plans leave unmatched requests and negative G payment residuals.
Those numbers are **unexecuted budget diagnostics**, not closing cash assets,
an actual debt issue or permission not to pay an existing right. Valid funded
closing assets remain unknown. Retain the lawful shortage/review/correction
route and elected source choice; neither Treasury nor an AI manufactures a loan.

[PA01–PA08](../38-work-pay-prices-and-country-income.md) now replaces residual
private payroll as national supply support with matched work/product/asset/claim
accounts. This retains FD's frozen planned-cash identities and scoped delivery
choices; they do not establish actual jobs, supplier prices, assets or collected
revenue. Hypothetical wages/profits cannot validate their own physical source.
