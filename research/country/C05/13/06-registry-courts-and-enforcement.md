# OE26–OE30: incorporation is not permission to evade public correction

*Part of [C05/13](../13-enterprise-and-association-constitutions.md); design118 candidate.*

**OE26 — Actual Registrar and case powers.** Cabinet appoints an Enterprise
Registrar through C04 open qualified public competition/shortlist, reasons,
conflicts, usable objections and direct B16/B17 review. Require actual corporate/
association/accounts/evidence/privacy competence including equivalent practical
experience, not a sole guild, party or AI gate. No simultaneous minister/legislator,
party executive or materially conflicted paid incorporation/enterprise controller.
Protected career tenure ends only through lawful resignation/retirement/reorganization
or proved misconduct, persistent material failure/inability after accommodation,
reply/reasons and independent court protection. Election defeat, lawful dissent
or an inconvenient registration/correction is no Cabinet-pleasure purge ground.

Registrar appoints/disciplines qualified registration, control/records, correction
and enforcement staff under C04. Statutory officers sign their own assigned acts;
minister cannot direct a named filing and no officer finally reviews their own
contested act. Predesignated unconflicted qualified substitutes use prior allocation;
Registrar's own conflict goes to ordinary courts. A qualified C04-appointed deputy
has at most180 elapsed days' routine funded management, no reset, new policy or
discretionary senior appointments. Existing statutory officers retain own duties
when that expires; absent management is disclosed, not invented. Cabinet opens
unexpected recruitment within14 days; prepare known departures beforehand. First
establishment permits funded qualified case officers via existing C04 recruitment
without bypassing head competition. Work Relations Registrar retains OE workforce
ballots in its separately enacted scope, not Enterprise Registrar's discretion.

**OE27 — Correct the record and specify official reliance.** Assisted status/
authority/title-error reports receive preserved date, safe reasons and direct B
relief without first suing a vendor. Proposed contested corrections need original
evidence, notice/reply and competent adjudication; a database edit is not title
acquisition or guilt. A public filing receipt is no state assurance of a private
capital value, statement's truth, safety, permission or solvency.

This Act expressly adds Treasury compensation for proved net direct reasonable
reliance loss caused by the office's materially wrong operative certification of
entity existence/identity or recorded decision authority, without needing personal
officer fault. Prove actual certificate, attributable office error, reliance,
causation/loss and no duplicate recovery; mere private fraud uploaded without
such official assurance is not covered. B defenses and limited source review
apply, not all lost future profits or investor losses. This is a new scoped public
exposure, not an analogy silently expanding B38's title-certification assurance.
Claim, actual paid restoration and later responsible-party/insurer recovery are
distinct; expected recoveries/fees/fines are not current source.

**OE28 — Direct courts and actual execution.** B/WL/sector routes give independent
full merits, aid and useful-time protection, B17 outer consideration at most24h
and earlier actual safety/claim rules. No compulsory registry grievance, private
arbitration or member technical dossier before a serious claim. Registry, entity,
worker/class and affected claimant get fair evidence/representation and actual
source-law appeal/periods. Ordinary court-appointed experts/receivers must be
qualified, conflict-free, paid and replaceable, not chosen solely by the defendant.
Orders name actual liable party, act/amount, source/time, recipient and verification.
B18/U private execution and only expressly adopted B19 public payment scope apply;
no forced personal labor, new tax/land appropriation or court-created business.
If no receiver/cash exists, retain the unmet remedy and competent elected source
obligation; an entered judgment/correct register is not actual restoration.

**OE29 — A closed new VD14 only.** Civil correction, restitution, work and safety
remain separate. After competent prospective adoption, VD14 permits V06 classB
only for deliberate material falsification of required entity identity,
decision authority, ultimate control or actually paid-capital certification to
mislead the register/relying parties; or a director's knowing/reckless authorization
of an OE10 distribution despite proved failure of its actual mandatory asset/
liquidity or overdue-dues protection. Prove actual control/ability, precise duty,
materiality, source and V/P notice/defenses. Genuine valuation/judgment dispute,
ordinary loss, missing technical annex, good-faith error, association belief or
late filing alone is no such offense. Registrar investigates/applies V correction;
independent ordinary civilian courts alone punish. Unchanged V06/Q maxima, V07
attribution/P proof and V08 one course apply: no classA, custody, new gain base
or fine-funded office. Existing fraud/other duties use their own actual law.

**OE30 — Foreign source and privacy boundaries.** Recognize a genuinely formed
foreign body and applicable B forum/law/cooperation, with actual domestic activity/
public-policy/work/sector duties. No domestic incorporation rule invents foreign
consent, global collection or evidence access. Actual beneficial-control inquiry
is purpose-limited; protect safe source, sensitive members and defense access
under PD. Registry/register vendor/AI outputs are not independent observations.
A foreign label or law choice cannot strip actually applicable domestic worker/
creditor/public protections, nor can domestic law silently transfer foreign title.

[PT19–PT23](../../C08/26-property-transfers-and-secured-credit.md) expressly
assigns the separate C04 property-record function/head with OE26's stated
career appointment/tenure/deputy protections; Enterprise Registrar gains no
land/security case power. PT official notice assurance is a new distinct source,
not expansion of OE27 by analogy; no private uploaded statement is certified
true simply by receipt. Actual shared staff/source costs cannot be counted twice.
