# The depot and a binding early deadline

*Design480 · Constructed extension of the public asset comparison.*

The [earlier depot case](../public-asset-disposal-and-control/01-three-options-for-one-depot.md)
keeps transport and environmental duties common. This extension
adds an already authorized finite transition need of four units
payable at closing and no available opening cash. Assume prior
law allows the stated financing and capital uses only after an
exact elected decision. A separate qualified one-unit recurring
source can serve the authorized costs each year. It has the same
cost and incidence in each comparison. All borrowing terms are
authored; both years' service and open claims still need proof.

| Route | Opening funding | Each of years 1 and 2 | Asset and service at the end |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retain and repair | Borrow 9 to fund 5 of environmental work and renewal, and 4 of common transition | Repay 5, including 0.5 interest, from 4 of received net depot income and 1 of other recurring funds. If the net depot income falls to 3, each year lacks 1. | Public depot and continuing ability to choose delivery. Estimated residual value is 12 in the earlier central case, with no cash assumed. |
| Finite concession | The qualified concession yields 2 after environmental and transition costs. Borrow 2 for the rest of the common transition. | Repay 1.5, including 0.5 interest, against 1 of received net return and 1 of other recurring funds. Room is 0.5 each year under these terms. | Public title with restricted operational control for the term. The operator must meet the funded handback with verified title, assets and service. The earlier residual estimate is 8. |
| Sale | Six eligible units of net cash at closing pay the finite 4 if the elected law allows that purpose. The remaining 2 retain their capital restrictions. | Pay 2 per year for purchased transport service in the earlier modeled two years. One continuing unit is missing each year from the ordinary funding unless another lawful source is available. | Transfer of title and future income. The contingent receipt of 3 may fail and cannot establish a recurring source. A subsequent service period needs its own price and funding. |

The fees are part of actual gross debt service. Do not use the
estimated depot value for repayment, presume a future refinance
or count the contingent sale payment in the first period. Check
actual payment and source protections through both years. A
one-unit outside reserve would cover only the first call under the
weaker retention case if that shortfall persists.

Select retention in the central case if actual receipts and
rights qualify and the elected body prioritizes keeping control.
If verified weaker proceeds leave retention unfunded and the
concession is qualified, bring that bounded rival to a separate
decision, with paid continuity, independent enforcement and
genuine handback. No fiscal comparison supplies those conditions.
The option holder, workers, residents and competing providers
retain usable challenge. If no candidate can meet the existing
duty, protect lawful ordinary activity and bring the residual
gap to the competent budget decision.
