# AA06–AA10: the audited Cabinet does not hire and fire the external audit chief

*Part of [C04/04](../04-public-audit-and-accountable-oversight.md); design121 candidate.*

**AA06 — Open qualified appointment.** Protected Assembly recruitment staff
receive direct consenting applicants for AG, open at least 30 calendar days,
with public statutory criteria, qualified names/exclusion reasons and conflicts.
Require 12 years of relevant audit/accounts/public-finance/legal or demonstrated
equivalent work, actual audit/evidence/privacy competence and accountable
leadership. No single guild/degree, wealth, ideology, party loyalty, recipient or
AI ranking owns entry. Qualifications/exclusions have B16 evidence/reply/direct
review; ordinary 20-working-day adverse reply cannot be cut by appointment notice.
A new adverse material fact needs fresh necessary reply/reasons/source vote.

**AA07 — Explicit elected source, actual dual votes.** Any Assembly member may
nominate an eligible applicant. After at least 21 calendar days' usable public
candidate notice/hearing, a recorded single-name vote needs ceil(3N/5) of full
statutory N and ceil(O/3) from the protected non-government cohort. This Act
expressly applies CJ05's actual governing-cohort definition/frozen positions/
denominators and new-government fresh-vote rules, not CJ12's constitutional-only
forum. O=0 cannot appoint. AA09 ordinary courts review criteria, source and
classification, not select their preferred AG. No vacancy/abstention/timeout
lowers N/O or appoints a plurality winner. Shared government/opposition patrons
can still capture this process; labels do not prove independent authorization.

**AA08 — Eight personal years, no holdover.** AG serves eight years from lawful
assumption, nonrenewable with no return as acting or renamed audit chief.
Replacement receives its own eight years; it does not extend the old personal
term. Open recruitment 180 days before known expiry, within 14 days of an
unexpected vacancy. A predesignated C04-qualified unconflicted career deputy
may carry funded routine management at most 180 elapsed days from vacancy,
no serial same-vacancy reset, new audit policy or discretionary senior appointment.
Statutory case/report officers continue their own existing duties after that
limit; a missing chief cannot suppress completed lawful reporting or urgent
record protection. No acting title creates a new term or government mandate.

**AA09 — Closed removal and independent own-status review.** New AG status uses
expressly assigned OJ16 ordinary three-person first formation/two votes and a
different five-person appeal/three votes, full actual presence, with disclosed
conflicts and safe source/defense. Removal grounds: serious corruption, deliberate
office abuse, intentional material qualification fraud, continuing incompatible
office, permanent inability after accommodation, or persistent material unjustified
statutory failure after actual notice, reasonable paid support and opportunity.
Prove actual duty/control/ability; missing resources, lawful reports/dissent,
good-faith error or election defeat are not grounds. Punitive misconduct needs
proof beyond reasonable doubt; actual functional inability clear and convincing
evidence. Express OJ18 paid 14+one14/outer28-elapsed suspension and seven-calendar-
day removal appeal/stay/blocked-access protections apply here by this Act.
No policy-confidence dismissal or allegation alone removes the chief.

**AA10 — Incompatibility without a loyalty test.** AG cannot be a minister,
legislator, operational party/campaign executive, paid lobbyist, audited-entity
manager or materially conflicted provider while serving. Four years after those
leadership/paid-control roles is the proposed entry cooling period; ordinary
views/advocacy or a diagnosis are not exclusions. Disclose actual source ties,
recuse or divest/cease incompatible interests with fair source review. No blanket
immunity for own fraud/abuse, and no punishment for normal lawful independent
audit. These term/experience/cooling values are authored hypotheses, not optima.
Actual old appointments/staff/rights keep AA36 lawful transition.
