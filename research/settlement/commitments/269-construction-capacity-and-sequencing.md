# Design356. Fund the build, independent acceptance and continuing service

[IV33–IV38](../../country/C06/63/08-construction-capacity-and-sequencing.md)
joins actual crews, existing private calls, paid inspection, protected renewal
and dated public funds. Neither a rival bid nor a shared subcontractor gives
an extra qualified slot. Budget separately for staging costs and origin cover
when adding actual capacity.

The [comparison](../construction-capacity-and-sequencing/01-two-windows-and-one-inspection-market.md)
retains binding and due work through an outside commitment and defers one
unbound stage. Actual sources, supplier consent and service receipt remain open.
