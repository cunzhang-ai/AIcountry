# LD01–LD04. Diagnose, protect and fund

**LD01 — Report the first threatened payment and service.** When credible
evidence shows that a locality may miss a lawful obligation, the career
finance officer informs the council, relevant national grant and sector
owners, and independent review. An affected resident, supplier, worker
or member can raise the evidence through an existing outside route if the
office fails. Within seven calendar days, and earlier where useful time
requires, issue a preliminary account with known gaps, named owners and
the next decision date. Profile owned unrestricted cash, restricted
balances, due transfers, genuine arrears, payment dates, claims and
essential service resources. Separate temporary settlement problems,
structural shortfalls before debt, burdens of debt, unlawful withholding
and physical shortage. More than one can occur. A forecast, warning or
missed report does not itself declare default, excuse payment or change
an elected mandate.

**LD02 — Keep care, pay and remedy on their lawful paths.** Apply existing
payment, procurement and service duties before the first missed useful time.
Continue lawful undisputed transfers. The designated national or sector
owner uses the applicable paid fallback where a common duty cannot be met
locally. Keep actual supply, legal authority and received service separate.
An approved interim payment goes to the lawful receiver under the relevant
source rules and is reconciled against the same obligation once. New
discretionary commitments follow existing authority and spending limits.
The shortfall supplies no power to stop an election, deny an appeal or
redirect another entity's restricted cash. If authorized money, actual
staff or a lawful receiver is missing, record each unmet duty and its
consequences. Existing independent review remains reachable.

**LD03 — Match the instrument to the verified problem.** Correct an
allocation error or late transfer under MF. A voluntarily accepted,
lawfully funded advance for a temporary cash gap requires independently
evidenced repayment from dated usable receipts, and safe recovery after
continuing due services. Compare finite grant, sector delivery or budget
reprioritization for a temporary real shock. A persistent deficit in a
common duty returns the matching national source and duty to an elected
choice under MF and FB. A locally chosen optional burden returns to its
competent council. Publish actual debt service, interest, term, asset sale,
guarantee and resulting service effects for any proposed financing.
Capital receipts or permission to capitalize operating costs cannot
establish a recurring net source. Do not repeatedly roll a short bridge
into permanent finance. A debtor, creditor or national grant officer
cannot approve new national money without its lawful source.

**LD04 — Preserve local choice and confine corrective conditions.** Local
elected authorities submit recovery options under their own powers. A
competent national elected authority sets funding and conditions for its
own new finite support within prior law. Conditions name the funded
purpose, accountable officer, end and delivery test. They may require
reliable records, independent checks and remedy of a proven misuse.
They cannot divert funding needed for protected common duties because
another locality overspent, dictate unrelated policies, extend mandates
or take control of local appointments or elections. Assess shared shocks
across all claimants before counting the same national reserve more than
once. Existing narrowly authorized service substitution names the funded
task and successor. It does not confer general government. Publish actual
local and national choices, continuing gaps and separate review routes.

[LD14–LD18](04-local-service-backstop.md) applies where the prior
ordinary transfer or voluntary service path cannot reach the useful
deadline. Keep original lawful transfers and local powers, with the
specific national act limited by actual funding, review and expiry.

[MF31–MF36](../08/08-local-grant-conditions-and-self-government.md)
tests grant conditions against existing lawful duties before applying
ordinary fiscal recovery or a separate paid backstop.
