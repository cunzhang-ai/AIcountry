# Readiness and limits

*Part of [Founding and whole-country stress](../FOUNDING-AND-STRESS.md); integrated design 59 research, not enacted law.*

## 5. Design completeness and operational readiness

The **working reference design** includes a political order, rights, an
economic/social settlement, authority and payment chains, a founding route
and adverse cases. That is substantial first-order coverage, not verified
completion. The [functional audit](../COVERAGE-AUDIT.md) tests named duties;
it is not a complete legal code or a joint feasibility assessment of the
current edition. The [earlier fiscal exercise](../settlement/03-results-and-selection.md)
uses invented residents and a limited service workload; later institutional
and service commitments cannot inherit its results merely because their
chapter headers share an edition number.

The [design-56 inventory](../settlement/commitments/README.md) now maps all
currently named functions and selections to proposed responsible actors,
shared resources and remedy executors; its current quantities remain unknown.
Remaining design work includes exact ordinary-law and due-time delivery
rules, identified legacy conflicts, and whole-package comparison under
combined resource, institutional and political failures. Every assumption
and unpriced obligation must stay visible. A reference scenario can test
internal consistency without a real jurisdiction, but cannot prove observed
social superiority. Its choices remain contestable, including against
substantively different country models.

No claim of actual statehood, founding consent, legal commencement, reliable delivery,
independent courts, funded benefits or observed wellbeing can be made. An operational
decision would require real people and territory, a lawful adoption route, complete
current-law reconciliation, opening assets and obligations, tested staff and payment
capacity, and actual public participation. The reference case's 100,000 invented
residents are an accounting denominator, not a ratifying population.

The [combined annual test](../settlement/commitments/06-combined-results.md)
fails its limited scope and leaves full feasibility unknown. The
[design-57 candidate Act and tests](../settlement/continuity/README.md)
now provide that bounded specification, including safe-time precedence,
bounded ex parte hearing and disclosed residual shortfalls. Actual resources
remain unknown. The [design-58 income rules/reference](../settlement/income-floor/README.md)
now specify that content and expose a large unfunded launch. Next reconcile
a complete national revenue/production/spending package;
attribute costs before treating a service envelope as sufficient. A real jurisdiction
and affected public are necessary for legitimate adoption and empirical
validation; their absence is not an excuse to leave tractable design work
unfinished. Keep design coverage, legal authority, operational readiness and
observed outcomes as separate findings. Reopen when a missing function,
credible rival, legal contradiction or harmful interaction changes a choice.

The [design-59 joined account](../settlement/national-economy/README.md) now
compares six planned economic cases. All remain held: GDP/sector identities
or annual public cash success do not establish private finance, qualified
physical capacity, currency access or an attainable pre-growth transition.
