# CS16–CS18: justify compulsory burdens and keep the country revisable

*Design 141 · Part of [the country case](../03-country-case-and-competing-designs.md); future-country candidate, not an adopted research procedure.*

**CS16 — Repair and simplify before adding a compulsory institutional step.**
For a consequential generally binding change adding a required application,
examination, certificate, data disclosure, approval or handoff, the responsible
elected body or lawfully delegated issuer must identify the protection sought,
actual legal power, affected people and usable existing alternatives. Compare
removing an unnecessary requirement, repairing a current role, shared support
with separate judgments and an additional mandatory step. State why the
selected burden is warranted, including uncertainty, exclusion, repeated proof,
delay, worker/resident time and control of information/payment/review.

Prefer a usable existing or simpler rights-preserving route where it can do
the job. This is no presumption that a provider may judge its own failure:
independent courts, lawful warrants, fair reply, consent and necessary separate
judgments retain their actual source. A claimed saving cannot remove them.
An elected body can choose an additional institution with public reasons,
proper law, resources and paid continuity; no mathematical optimum is required.

Select an ordinary-public-law requirement subordinate to constitutional rights.
Use the existing Act/delegated-rule/local-law decision and explanation route,
not a new simplification office, certificate or preliminary approval. Individual
fact requests, ordinary decisions within existing competence and informal
cooperation do not each require a new national study. A clerk, fiscal model or
AI cannot veto legislation, waive law or let a changed process approve itself.

**CS17 — Put released capacity and usable choice in the actual account.**
New digital/AI routes may help some people and exclude others. Lawful reused
facts, direct human help, collective assistance and future communication remain
possible without a universal record or compulsory mediator. A faster electronic
handoff cannot prove understanding, consent, a qualified successor or receiving.
Removing an interface and removing an independent judgment are different acts.

Do not fund continued human/other-provider access only from forecast digital
savings, or count an assumed freed worker twice. OP/T must name actual paid
cover, retained capability, records, source and safe succession. A workforce/
service change needs its competent lawful decision, practical alternative and
continuing-rights protection; no vendor upgrade retires a human route by itself.
The [current publisher evidence](../../../2026-11-whole-country-bargain-and-institutional-burden/sources.md)
qualifies access and redeployment assumptions; it supplies no national saving,
causal winner or new data permission. Existing claims and earlier safe times
continue even when the new route fails or its expected gain never appears.

**CS18 — Retain the bargain, revise the institutional response to failure.**
Retain CS13's democratic/service-first/plural/public-responsibility country as
the working design. Preserve strong retain/repair, direct-public, U+C2 and
mutual/common alternatives with their conflicting burdens and choices. Reject
automatic institutional multiplication, sole-platform access, tacit transfers
of authority, apparent savings as actual staffing and old partial financial
figures as a national winner. CS16–CS17 is the consequential refinement; it
does not choose a new tax, benefit, provider monopoly or constitutional form.

The preference remains a reasoned value/design hypothesis, not demonstrated
superiority or proof it costs less. The bounded whole-bargain comparison is
complete at this institutional level; original empirical/matched-cost work,
source law, independent affected participation, adoption, paid readiness and
observed better lives remain separate unfinished facts. Earlier studies retain
only their inspected historical scope, not renewed approval of the package.

The next identified positive-rule gap is voluntary funded-pension tax treatment:
resolve contribution, return, withdrawal and basis together with PA/TA/NF/NB/Y
and I05, comparing ordinary treatment and genuine special-regime alternatives.
Do not infer a licence-created relief, foreign asset levy or new public guarantee.
This follows the country gap review after the whole comparison, not the latest
source's research tail. Complete that finite legal/design choice while actual
finance/qualified review stay unverified. Urgent harm, capture/exclusion,
compulsory burden, source failures or a stronger whole rival can reprioritize
or reopen the current bargain. A justified no-change choice is legitimate.

**Design142 follow-through:** [PF01–PF16](../../C06/57-private-retirement-tax-and-personal-capital.md)
now supplies the identified private-plan written tax choice. It preserves CS16's
proportionate existing-role approach, qualified actual costs and competing values.
Its PF16 legal-spine review supersedes the private-tax next task here; no new
common procedure, actual law or automatic source/final review is adopted.
