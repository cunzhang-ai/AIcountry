**QE11 — Retain NP conditionally; reject an affordable-country claim.** The
joined benchmark changes the budget/recruitment/planning baseline. Preserve
I05/Y and optional service content, including actual receipt, instead of making
the basket, offer, wage or law cheaper just to pass. Known unpriced families
include rare/neonatal specialist capacity, war/atrocity/custody/reconstruction,
actual supplier/utility/food/asset labor, full pension tails and foreign partners.
They are missing costs/supply, not zeros. No existing oldbid/SF assumption fills
them. Meaningful democratic replacement and due remedies stay design constraints;
good annual arithmetic cannot replace them or authorize sovereign AI decisions.

**QE12 — Next select a fundable production/finance/calendar package.** Complete
an explicit progressive mix of company/capital/land/consumption and wage sources,
I05/Y incidence and actual source counterparts, an independently scoped output/
import/asset bridge, and useful-time court/receiving/staff calendars for the same
population/needs. Compare a lawful retained baseline and real alternatives;
reject the package if they cannot coexist. The completion condition is a coherent
conditional country allocation, not another oversight body or more concepts.
Actual original context evidence, affected participation and qualified independent
review are required for later qualification, not manufactured by AI agents.
Reopen now for a due harm, omitted duty, stronger objection or superior alternative.
