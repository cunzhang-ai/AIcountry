# WF95–WF99. Fund care placements and cover the mentor's work

*Design283 · Proposed extension to [workforce formation](20-staffing-formation-and-first-service-capacity.md).*

Select a first care cohort by its protected placement and service
schedule. Vote on training and current service repair as separate uses.
The site doing the teaching must be able to pay for the work and
keep the care it already owes usable.

**WF95 — Put placements and protected care in the same calendar.** The
school, learner employer and receiving site jointly identify the
qualification, placement dates, learners and competent supervisors.
The service owner states the relevant care floor by task, site and
shift and names any duties the mentor or learner would otherwise do.
Use approved workforce conditions and paid cover with actual authority,
skills and acceptance. Compare permanent retention and return as well
as a new cohort. Check the site that would supply a substitute for a
loss of its own due service. A general staff number or regional surplus
cannot cover a shift that needs a specific capability.

**WF96 — Fund teaching and backfill where the expense falls.** The elected
budget names the gross cost and payer for learner pay, release of the
mentor, cover for displaced ordinary work, accommodation and language
support where needed, assessment and protected completion. Use an
authorized advance or usable own source where a site cannot pay ahead
of reimbursement. Identify shared or existing funds once and keep
uncommitted reserves separate. A placement may start only within the
exact budgeted scope and after the school, host and origin service
accept their duties. Keep the learner's and provider's enforceable
claims on their applicable due dates.

**WF97 — Bind only places that can be taught without relying on dropout.**
Use maximum concurrent demand from already binding places, with a paid
alternate for the declared common loss. A forecast of people leaving
may inform recruiting and outreach. It cannot finance a promised place
or count as released mentor time. Keep expressions of interest and
conditional offers clear about their status. Stage an exact smaller
intake with paid later placements where it preserves both learning and
care. Only a separately authorized narrower choice can start on changed
terms. Offer transparent allocation, support and review to applicants
who cannot start. When cover fails after commitment, use the approved
backup or restore ordinary service and arrange safe, paid continuation
or closeout under actual law. No untrained replacement or compelled
unpaid work supplies the missing shift.

**WF98 — Measure net delivery and qualified receiving separately.** During
training count independently usable care only where supervision, lawful
scope, user rights and actual service are verified. Retain the mentor's
time and any existing learner assignment once. After independent
assessment, count only voluntarily accepted, paid, usable shifts within
the assessed competence. Separate employer hire, hours, retained service
and origin loss. A qualified person can choose another employer,
reduce hours or leave. No institution gains a retention veto. Compare
safe service and worker burden with the no-cohort alternative rather
than treating headcount or a wage reimbursement as a capacity result.
For a continuing service, require a separately accepted next roster
after retirement, departure, travel and protected rest. Price the
replacement and ordinary reserve at their own dates. Preserve actual
user choices and independently verify first and next receiving.

**WF99 — Keep review and recovery useful at the next placement window.**
Publish the scope and dates for intake, expected assessment, completed
assessment, deployment and retention, with small groups protected.
Keep incomplete cohorts and unmatched denominators visible. On a method
change, retain earlier definitions and compare the restated same-scope
baseline. Course records and unique people need separate counts. Review
user receipt and worker and learner complaints under existing independent
routes. AI may compare schedules and find overlap with traceable data,
with human correction and a direct alternative. Keep learning assessment
and service assignment with their lawful authorities. Before renewal,
verify actual costs, covered ordinary service and retained capacity.
If the case for more places fails, maintain prior duties and reconsider
the next uncommitted cohort rather than withdrawing existing rights.
