# RF13–RF16: make the present–future bargain explicit

*Part of [C06/36](../36-recurring-finance-and-pension-commitments.md); design 89 candidate.*

**RF13 — Choose the package, not a positive fiscal cell.** Compare current
I05/mixed services/Y, IC universal ordinary provision with extra needs, the
local-access/shared-specialist organization and genuine funded/ownership rivals.
Show ordinary needs, actual received services, work/time, personal incidence,
current and new pension rights, annual/gross borrowing, due-day sources, private
working capital, import settlement and paid migration. Do not hold obsolete
profits/taxes when income or ownership changes. Qualified negative findings
can defeat a complete launch while leaving its structural destination open.

Select recurring tax/pooled-insurance funding, bounded elected borrowing,
prospective one-W accrual ceiling and access-preserving shared capability.
Retain IC's destination as conditional; reject its currently unsourced full
launch, mandatory individual prefunding and imaginary immediate savings from
the new cap or shared service. No numeric tax package has been selected.

**RF14 — Preserve the incompatible values and losers.** The new pension cap
limits public replacement above median earnings. Keeping two-W with higher
solidarity burdens, a larger funded public reserve, or a well-qualified
alternative is a genuine rival. Universal provision can crowd out targeted
care; local duplication can preserve access at higher cost; sharing can weaken
local bargaining and practical exit. Pension expectations, creditor returns,
present services and future taxes involve choices that cannot be averaged away.
No consent, public mandate or reviewer independence follows from AI agreement.

Those with weak records, irregular work, disabilities, older/younger claims,
unpaid care, remote access and no public voice need practical protected routes
to challenge the package. Actual participation has not occurred. Opposition
can offer different sources/delivery and replace the executive through existing
elections; keeping services operating never justifies postponing that choice.

**RF15 — Migrate with dates, sources and successors.** Assembly law specifies
separate tax, contribution, service, new-accrual and payment dates, original
claim matches, paid overlap and lawful responsible authorities. Councils retain
their own powers. No blanket ministerial readiness condition substitutes for
the enacted dates. Unreceived replacement cannot be subtracted from I05/Y;
arrears and existing court orders remain. A missing source defeats an optional
new commitment claim, not the individual's existing right or legal remedy.

Old annual pension units, source indexation, survivor contingencies, restricted
cash, titles, contracts, workers/creditors and pending challenges have named
lawful successors. A new tax-benefit system cannot retroactively reprice them.
No SF standby, pension cash, bank redemption, client asset or FD receiving debt
is simultaneously source for ordinary benefits, investment and closure.
Preserve safe evidence/correction history without making private testimony public.

Treasury executes lawful payments; source custodians preserve original records;
independent courts review legality and actual execution. Cabinet/Assembly use
E19/NP when the adopted package fails, including a publicly recorded refusal
or disagreement. No fiscal office, fund manager, technical expert or AI chooses
an emergency tax, debt workout or general priority. Existing CP/NP powers and
their finite scope govern actual scarcity; this research adopts no new procedure.

**RF16 — Next resolve the bounded country allocation.** The next output is a
quantity/task/price-consistent country operating and transition alternative,
with original active pension histories and actual-or-explicitly-authored cohort
flows, matched source/claim dates and credible recurring net incidence. Hold
ordinary needs/rights visible; examine changed work/output, housing/production
and service organization rather than ratcheting nominal GDP or taxes to pass.
The prior55,000-worker fixed-price fixture is not a national production ceiling.

Missing actual territory, history or engineering can remain bounded contextual
parameters with an accountable selection/transition rule; they cannot be set
to zero, invented as observed evidence or used to postpone every desk choice.
Research lead prepares the alternative; qualified fiscal/actuarial/engineering
and affected-party judgment and actual intervention budget are absent.
Reopen on source, record, price, behavior, access, capture, longevity, new
objections or an alternative with demonstrably better receipt/control/cost.
If monitoring is unavailable, readiness and superior outcomes stay unproved.
