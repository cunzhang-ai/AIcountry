# Actual international cooperation, capacity and continuing obligations

*G13–G14; part of [C08/18](../18-international-crimes-and-accountability.md).*

**G13 — Distinct courts and actual duties.** A request from an international
court needs its actual constituting instrument, jurisdiction/admissibility
and applicable cooperation basis, case/person/order, domestic implementing
power and rights/safety review. Do not invent party status, a referral,
reservation, immunity waiver or foreign receiver. Domestic judges perform
their actual identity/process/rights role, not an unauthorized appeal on
the international court's merits; challenges/consultation go to the competent
court/authority under the real instrument. Preserve source text and conflicts.
For the inspected Article 59 model, the proposed domestic implementing route
checks identity, proper arrest and respected rights; it does not retry the
international warrant's merits. Interim-release requests notify the competent
international chamber and give its recommendations full consideration before
a reasoned domestic decision. Actual current instrument/scope must be checked.

P23's ordinary extradition route, including its 30-day file condition, is
not automatically the regime for international-court surrender. Domestic
constitutional protections and funded independent liberty review remain;
actual custody/release and treaty consultation require an explicitly reconciled
implementing rule before adopting the arrangement. No indefinite executive
hold, secret rendition or automatic transfer by an alert follows an unresolved
conflict. Record the missing lawful path rather than claim full treaty conformity.
Actual existing obligations are not unilaterally canceled by a desk proposal.

Evidence/witness/asset assistance retains B51, lawful purpose/scope, original
source, challenge, protection against torture/coercion, proportional records
and safe onward-use limits. A foreign classification or a domestic mission
order creates no evidence waiver. An irreversible risk challenge must be
capable of meaningful independent action before harm; a paper assurance
cannot cure persisting prohibited risk. Account actual handover/receipt,
not merely dispatch, agreement, data convergence or a returned receipt token.

**G14 — Capacity, lawful commencement and revision.** C10/07 owns preventive
records, stop/report duties and safe civilian continuity; C06 must attribute
independent investigators/prosecutors, defense, judges, interpreters, source/
forensic custody, protected witnesses, humane custody, receiving and claims
once by skill/date/location. The existing country-book bid is not qualified
for this increment. No unmeasured AI saving, confiscated asset, fine, unpaid
relative or promised international assistance is a current source of funds.

Before authorized commencement, map each current offense, act date, actual
treaty/amendment, actor/age, immunity, pending case, custody/penalty, judgment,
limitation and victim/service obligation to lawful successors. Preserve
the accused's legality/leniency and victims' ongoing support. No retroactive
new national offense, automatic amnesty, revived bar, lost record or bulk
release/collection follows this research. Where past international criminality
is asserted, prove the actual then-applicable basis and jurisdiction; the
new schedule is not its own retrospective authority.

Reopen on an ambiguous act/context, disguised group/status liability,
failed source challenge, command causation error, childhood harm, sham/captured
review, disproportionate punishment, incompatible treaty duty or unmet safe
receiving. Preserve material dissent and unfamiliar future meanings; new
media/agents do not multiply authority or silently alter definitions. New
legal text, a valid hash and more prosecutions do not prove better lives.
V/M, RP and [CD](../21-conflict-detention-and-release.md) now provide scoped
financial, reproductive and conflict-custody candidates. [TS/TD](../22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md)
now adds technical duties; next audit ordinary-law and whole-country coherence.
