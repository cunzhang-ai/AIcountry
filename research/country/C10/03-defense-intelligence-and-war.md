# C10. Defense, intelligence and war

*Part of [C10 safety and international responsibility](../C10-safety-and-international-relations.md); integrated design 102 research, not enacted law.*

The [defense and war-power decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-defense-and-war/decision-record.md)
selects a **provisional** division between standing defense and an expanded
campaign. Prior law may authorize immediately necessary defense against an actual
armed attack and protection of forces; the cabinet records the legal ground,
orders, area, expected civilian risk and resources and notifies a protected
Assembly channel within a proposed 24 hours. An urgent cross-border action to
stop an ongoing attack requires its own legal assessment. Repeated actions pursuing
one foreign campaign cannot continue beyond a proposed seven days without an
affirmative Assembly mandate. That public mandate states purpose, theater,
force classes, duration, fiscal ceiling, civilian duties, reporting and an exit
rule, with a protected classified annex. It expires within a proposed 90 days
unless affirmatively renewed. Silence or failure to convene is not consent:
standing territorial defense and safe withdrawal remain available under prior
law, but the executive cannot enlarge the campaign or renew its own mandate.
These clocks are research hypotheses, separate from [RX](01-public-safety-and-emergencies.md) declaration ceilings;
no domestic constitutional derogation is selected. Neither an emergency declaration nor a
domestic vote makes an unlawful external use of force lawful.

The elected cabinet sets lawful defense objectives; a minister issues a recorded
mission order; professional commanders direct operations within it. They serve
constitutional offices through an election, not a party, and cannot certify
results or veto a successor. Service members need legal advice, protected
reporting and fair discipline, including a route to refuse manifestly unlawful
orders under enacted law. Civilian courts retain criminal and rights jurisdiction.
[IA/IL/IE](10-civilian-assessment-and-intelligence-limits.md) now selects civilian
strategic assessment/warning, no separate domestic covert service and no new
noncriminal intrusion. Actual independently lawful criminal/defense sources
retain their own limits; the future adopting Act must end incompatible old
standalone covert mandates prospectively, not preserve them by legacy labels. [IA09–IA12](10/03-parliamentary-members-staff-and-access.md) completes the existing
Assembly committee: fixed authorized places/votes, actual cohort allocation,
Clerk counsel/technical staff, separate source and each member’s protected
inspection/court route without a clearance or committee-majority veto. It scrutinizes but does not
authorize war or direct operations. Outside-command investigation of serious
official abuse survives secrecy, mobilization and a cabinet change.

Applicable humanitarian law governs targeting, detention and civilian protection.
New weapons and material software changes receive documented legal review before
use and when context changes. AI may assist logistics and analysis, but a model
profile is not a lawful target decision. This reference prohibits autonomous
selection and engagement of persons without a human decision on the particular
target. A narrow defensive interception of defined weapon objects requires a
separate bounded legal and reliability review, a named human controller, feasible
stop controls and preserved logs. An unreviewable system is not fielded.

The reference uses a professional volunteer force and voluntary reserves. Any
compulsory service needs separate prospective legislation, finite necessity,
fair selection and nonpunitive alternative service for conscientious objectors.
The C06 account must include training, maintenance, medical and veterans' duties,
independent investigation, fuel, imports, foreign currency and civilian shelters;
one worker or reserve cannot cover both military and civilian plans unnoticed.
Major security assistance and continuing defense commitments still require
Assembly authorization and fiscal and rights analysis. Security procurement,
records, armories and command credentials transfer to lawful successors with
independent custody and access arrangements that a defeated cabinet or vendor
cannot block. Existing treaty, worker, creditor and harmed-civilian claims survive
mobilization and demobilization.

Territory, neighboring states, defense needs, treaty access and founding sovereignty
remain unspecified. This design neither claims land nor promises security guarantees
that no other party has made.

A domestic territorial-status vote does not by itself change an international
border, treaty party, military command or a person's external protection.
Any negotiated status change must identify applicable international obligations,
neighboring and externally affected interests, citizenship and movement paths,
defense assets, debts and civilian safeguards before a lawful commencement.
Security services may protect people under existing law while negotiation occurs;
they may not treat peaceful autonomy advocacy as an enemy operation or use a
manufactured emergency to preempt the constitutional route.

The executive cannot turn an emergency declaration into a new tax, a postponed
election or a renewed mandate. Assembly renewal, independent necessity review,
opposition oversight and a real expiry are part of the same proposed rule. Treaty
review and signature follow an elected mandate; AI can analyze but cannot ratify.

[C08 P21–P24](../C08/17/07-criminal-reach-and-transfer.md) now specifies
bounded criminal reach/cooperation, with actual immunity and international
competence separate. Rome Article 80 is not an automatic mandate for national
thirty-year/life penalties. [G01–G14](../C08/18-international-crimes-and-accountability.md)
now defines a national candidate; [C10/07](07-atrocity-prevention-and-legal-control.md)
adds preventive control/records and national 18 service. [CD01–CD16](../C08/21-conflict-detention-and-release.md)
now defines narrow status custody and actual release, with [C10 control](08-conflict-custody-and-civilian-control.md).
Actual compatibility, safe independent capacity and source finance remain unverified.

Ordinary domestic defense-assisted law enforcement also retains [LF](../C08/24-lawful-force-and-civilian-response.md). The positive [IA/IL/IE assessment statute](10-civilian-assessment-and-intelligence-limits.md) now selects **no new intrusive noncriminal collection power as the substantive endpoint**, accepting a hidden-threat warning loss; an external-collection or exact covert-warrant rival needs a new complete rights/need/source choice, not a presumed missing appendix. P07 criminal warrants, PD25 notice delay, emergency labels and foreign assistance do not supply that missing power; actual valid source law is not silently repealed.

[DF01–DF20 civilian defense](12-civilian-defense-and-service.md) now concretizes
public employer/Chief appointment and command/status separation, neutral force/
individual rights, no military commercial estate or command criminal forum,
actual outside inquiry and finite voluntary duties. Its prospective12-month
organization/service start does not reset this24-hour/seven-day/90-day mission
law, RX/LF/CD, original commitments or earlier/stronger personnel rights.
No new attack, mobilization, coercion or AI target authority is created.
