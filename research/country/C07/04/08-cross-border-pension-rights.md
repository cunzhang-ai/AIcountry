# Y30–Y35. Carry earned pension rights across borders

*Part of [retirement rights](../04-retirement-security.md). Design345 research selection.*

**Y30 — Keep domestic earned rights payable on their own record.** A
move abroad does not cancel an earned Y pension or its applicable
indexation. The domestic pension authority determines and pays the
established earned amount under ordinary Y law, including a holder's
lawful start choice. Provide a lawful payment route for entitled
recipients abroad where available, with an accessible domestic route
when the person chooses it. The same due date applies. Domestic rights
start from the first covered period. A treaty, a foreign record or an
additional period of residence is unnecessary for domestic accrual.
Y basic protection retains the ordinary-residence test, including
protection for people whose actual center of life has not changed.
Keep needs support, vested legacy rights, survivor claims and foreign
entitlements under their own law.

**Y31 — Coordinate eligibility without transferring accrual.** An
applicable enacted agreement may combine recognized periods to meet a
partner's eligibility test. Identify which schemes, periods, people
and benefits it covers. Convert and allocate periods under published
lawful rules. Count the same time once for the combined qualification
test. Distinguish genuinely concurrent lawful coverage from duplicate
reporting of the same work. Each authority decides and pays its own
amount under its law and the operative agreement. Foreign earnings
and credits do not create domestic Y units, transfer contributions or
extinguish separately vested entitlements. Where an agreement provides
both independent and coordinated calculations, apply its comparison
rule and explain the result. Preserve disputed periods without treating
them as settled.

**Y32 — Make a cross-border claim reachable from either side.** The
pension authority receives protected filings and provides a named
liaison, interpretation and non-digital contact. Confirm the filing
date and next steps within five working days. Send only necessary
evidence to an authorized counterpart. A filing has effect in the
partner system only under an operative agreement or that system's
law. Explain where separate filing is needed and assist in time.
Keep the original domestic date and any recognized partner date.
Pay the independently established domestic amount while the foreign
or disputed part is investigated. If an outside reply is still
missing at 30 working days, give a reasoned status, assistance and
a dated next action with direct review. This status neither extends
ordinary decision clocks nor confirms foreign entitlement. Accept
proportionate alternative proof and safe identity or life verification.
Avoid unnecessary biometric enrollment or unsafe contact with a
foreign authority. Existing review and earlier relief continue.

**Y33 — Verify receipt across currency and payment boundaries.** Name the
payer, amount in its source currency, due date, accepting institution
and account or lawful offline recipient. Disclose conversion basis
and charges before a route is chosen. Count actual settlement and
usable receipt separately from approval and dispatch. If a route
fails, protect the original due date, keep unpaid amounts visible
and arrange an independently lawful, authorized alternate. Verify
nonsettlement or transfer of settlement responsibility before replacing
a possibly settled payment. Pay or execute lawful domestic remedies
through the responsible authority. Neither the pension service nor
an AI can compel a foreign payer, bypass lawful restrictions or
guarantee a foreign exchange rate.

**Y34 — Make any new treaty and its budget an elected choice.** Apply TR
to approval, commencement, changes and exit. Publish the participating
schemes, scope, applicable contribution law for posted or concurrent
work, agreement on overlaps, evidence limits and each country's actual
funding duties. Avoid dual contributions only within the applicable
coordination rules. A coordination certificate records governing law
within its scope. It is not a tax exemption or a benefit award.
Separate the actuarial effect, administrative cost, cash dates, currency
needs and earned and basic sources. When no agreement applies, assist
the person with lawful separate claims while paying independently
established domestic dues. No cross-border receipt or Treasury backstop
is assumed.

**Y35 — Protect accrued claims and correct actual failures.** Name the
payer and record custodian for pending periods and valid old rights
through agreement changes, retirement moves or a shared outage. Keep
legal scope, confidentiality, time limits and human appeals usable
through transition. AI may compare certified periods and trace receipts.
A qualified officer resolves conflicts and owns a domestic decision.
Independent review can remedy domestic error within existing authority.
Publish safe aggregate timing, rejected routes and unresolved periods.
Adopt prospectively with paid liaison, lawful funding and actual
accepting routes. Reopen on duplicate accrual, missing pensions,
unusable proof, unpriced liabilities or a simpler effective route.

**Keep the domestic payer active during an external transition.** Pay the independently established amount by its original date and preserve evidence and dates for a separate partner filing. An unresolved agreement or non-use of a foreign channel cannot cancel a standing domestic right. Fund authorized access assistance while formal effect and foreign receipt remain separately verified.
