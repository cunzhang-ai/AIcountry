# External responsibility: from principle to an accountable decision chain

4 October 2026 · Integrated design 29 research selection; no enacted rule

## Identity, baseline and decision

The baseline is the exact [design-28 predecessor](predecessor-design-28/README.md).
The earlier C10 selected accountable interdependence but did not fully specify
who could start material provisional treaty obligations, who could withdraw,
what prevented an irreversible border return during review, or how critical
imports and serious harms abroad reached an accountable domestic decision.
The decision here is whether to keep that thin principle or specify a bounded
operating chain. The author selected the latter for the working proposal.
No population, legislature, treaty, court or foreign partner approved it.

## Competing explanations and choices

One diagnosis is that foreign affairs need broad cabinet discretion because
negotiation is fast and confidential. Another is that the main danger is
dependency on foreign supply and a self-sufficiency strategy would provide
better security. A third is that lack of prior public authorization and remedy
lets the cabinet, lenders, carriers and contractors shift costs to people who
cannot influence the decision. These are rival causal claims; no actual
jurisdictional evidence establishes which danger dominates here.

| Option | Mechanism and attraction | Material failure |
|---|---|---|
| Retain design 28's general principle | Small, flexible statement of democratic oversight | Provisional application, exit, border acts and import breakdown remain unassigned |
| Cabinet-led closed diplomacy | Fast negotiation and coherent external representation | Side obligations can bypass budget, rights review and opposition; secrecy can become permanent |
| Broad self-sufficiency and restrictive entry | Limits some foreign chokepoints and immediate reception demand | Consumes scarce land, workers and energy; can evade individual protection and external duties |
| Every external act by Assembly or plebiscite | Direct elected visibility | Routine cooperation stalls; a domestic vote cannot compel partners or erase accrued duties |
| **Selected tiered accountable interdependence** | Cabinet negotiates within law; elected authority controls material lifecycle; protection, supply and foreign harm have named routes | Needs capable legislative review, lawful secrecy, court and reception capacity; partners and supply remain outside national control |

The selection expresses values as well as empirical hypotheses. Democratic
control, individual protection and not exporting serious harm count even when a
closed or restrictive route might be faster. Claims that this will improve
security, prosperity or refugee outcomes are untested.

## Scope, dependencies and continuing duties

The Assembly's prior authority covers material ratification, provisional
application and withdrawal, public guarantees or foreign borrowing, security
commitments and arrangements changing rights, critical systems, tax power or
durable public spending. Cabinet can negotiate and undertake bounded technical
cooperation under prior law. The exact text, translations, implementing law,
rights and resource effects, continuing duties and lawful exit conditions enter
the public register and C06 account. A domestic vote does not by itself release
an external debt, treaty duty or accrued protection claim.

A public protection office receives a claim at a point under national authority;
an independent challenge must be able to act before a prohibited return.
Admission, long-term residence and political membership remain distinct.
Reception requires a real local authority and nationally supported places,
workers, language access, care and schools. Critical imports have stewards,
routes, payment currency, alternatives, reserve/release rules and failure time.
Serious foreseeable harm abroad from this country's public or domestically
regulated activity enters the authorizing account; domestic complaint and review
depend on actual jurisdiction and standing, with diplomatic or contractual
routes and explicit residual harm otherwise.

This changes C02, C06, C07, C10, C14 and candidate A8. C09 already records
offshored ecological harm. The integrated overview and status register now
carry the selection. Existing lawful rights, contracts, treaty duties, pending
claims and ordinary services continue during any later real transition.
Relocating chapter text into topic files is independently documented in the
[organization record](../2026-10-current-design-organization/README.md).

## Sources, objections and limits of review

The [Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties](https://legal.un.org/ilc/texts/instruments/english/conventions/1_1_1969.pdf)
is a primary comparator for good-faith performance and treaty termination;
applicable treaty law in a real jurisdiction must be established rather than
assumed from this research. [UNHCR's account of the 1951 Refugee Convention](https://www.unhcr.org/about-unhcr/overview/1951-refugee-convention)
and [non-refoulement guidance](https://emergency.unhcr.org/protection/legal-framework/access-territory-and-non-refoulement)
support review before a protection claimant is returned to risk; actual scope
and procedure depend on applicable law. The [UN Guiding Principles on Business
and Human Rights](https://knowledgegateway.ohchr.org/bhr) inform the harm and
remedy hypothesis. The [International Law Commission's transboundary-harm
articles](https://legal.un.org/ilc/texts/9_7.shtml) are drafts, not a binding
convention. Original treaties, border cases, suppliers, budgets and affected
people's accounts were not available or reviewed.

Objections remain live. Assembly scrutiny may be too slow for a narrow window;
the enacted statute needs a defined routine class and lawful urgent route,
without treating delay as cabinet consent. Disclosure can damage negotiation or
personal safety; specific temporary withholding requires review and cannot hide
tax, force or return authority. A court might be captured or unfunded; appointment,
records, budget and enforcement independence must be tested in the complete
democratic chain. Reception and supplier resilience compete with current care;
the same C06 account must expose that choice. Foreign people may lack standing
or safe testimony; research text cannot manufacture their participation or
jurisdiction. A critical partner can refuse an agreement despite a domestic
mandate. A protection claim is not proof of eligibility, and a complaint is not
proof of liability; competent evidence and fair defense remain necessary.

Only a qualified research selection is made. There was no affected-party
participation, independent source-case review, legal authorization, staffed
operation, budget appropriation or observed outcome. No AI reviewer count would
substitute for these gaps. The prior design snapshot remains available; fresh
review is required before any actual decision.

## Next bounded question

For one real potential adopting jurisdiction, can an affected public compare
this package with cabinet discretion, stricter self-sufficiency and other
democratic alternatives using its actual treaty inventory, border cases,
supplier routes, foreign claims, costs, staff and lawful adoption path? A
comparison is complete only when continuing obligations and a feasible remedy
receiver are traced through the transition. Material new treaty duties,
documented return harm, external supply breakdown or affected-party objection
reopens this selection. If original evidence cannot be obtained, retain the
research label and do not report readiness or superiority.
