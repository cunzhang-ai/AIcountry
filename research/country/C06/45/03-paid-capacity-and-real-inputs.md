# OP09–OP12: a paid position must fit the actual duty

*Part of [C06/45](../45-country-operation-and-handover.md); design 122 candidate.*

**OP09 — Derive tasks and availability before claiming staffing.** For each
current function connect need/standby, qualified task, place, due window and
consent to provider, employed or contracted time, salary/cover, supplies,
independent review and execution. Use TW's actual leave/break/learning/rest and
stronger sector rules, not QE's old 1,080 as universal law. Required travel,
records, handover, learning, board/council and AI supervision are human work.
Deduct a matched hour once; actual joint output is not double-counted service.
Paid leave is paid time but not available service time. Private/family care is
not a compulsory balancing workforce. Annual, peak and geographically useful
capacity are separate constraints; do not cancel a due service to fit a chart.

**OP10 — Reserve simultaneous qualified and independent capacity.** A safe
care roster, ordinary judge, external-status judge, election official, inspector
and training supervisor need their own authorized availability. Even workers
with two relevant skills cannot occupy two concurrent assignments. Counterparty
review needs actual conflict separation, not another account/AI copy. Fund paid
relief, specialist cover and useful-time courts in the same scenario. The fixture
requires 12 compatible worker-hours in one eight-hour window:12 annual hours from
one person do not fit. A separately qualified second worker can fix that declared
collision, but not prove a real roster, competence, consent or independence.

**OP11 — Carry the material and capital chain.** Each critical good/service
needs actual inputs, inventories, maintenance, receiving sites, water/power,
transport/communications, owned imports and lawful environmental capacity.
Retained assets keep title, useful-life and repair obligations. Acquiring a
private provider is a paid transfer of claims; building a new provider is new
capital/work. Do not treat acquisition, capital formation, annual CFC, renewal
cash, principal and interest as the same expense. Retain actual unpriced rows;
new stock cannot be supplied by an assumed profit or an unreceived tax credit.
A costed supplier offer still needs actual acceptance and safe operation.

**OP12 — Assess AI and shared systems through net consequences.** Compare
human time/output before and after on matched tasks, including paid oversight,
error correction, energy/hardware, procurement, security, accessibility and
fallback. Record people displaced, new paid work and claims; no automatic tax
on imaginary productivity or mandatory adoption. Future media may carry shared
situations, interpretation and delegation, but do not confer consent, merge
mandates or erase unfamiliar meanings. Keep lawful non-digital routes and
replaceable mediators. Quicker processing is useful only if people receive
better control/service without exporting hidden work or arbitrary power.

The 37 old bids do not price these additions; unknown cost is not spare capacity.
TS's first-use lead time cannot postpone existing urgent/due obligations.
