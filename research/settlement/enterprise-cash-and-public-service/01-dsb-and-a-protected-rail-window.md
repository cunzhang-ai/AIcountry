The revenue components shown leave 1,452 of other revenue.
The three cash flows reconcile to a decrease of 1. Net debt

The observed report also gives no

## Constructed cash shock

The operator has 4 usable units plus 2 settled fare units
before 9 of protected obligations fall due. An earned 3-unit
contract payment is late. An uncommitted line of 5 is unavailable.
A committed facility of 2 is drawable and can settle before
the deadline. Drawing it leaves a gap of 1. If the sponsor can
lawfully settle 1 of the same contract liability in time, the
operator can meet the protected payment. Credit that payment
once. The remaining contract settlement of 2 cannot both repay
the 2-unit draw and cover 2 of next-period protected costs.
With no other sources the operator needs a lawful maturity
extension or funded new cover. Assume no guarantee is called.
A guarantee call would be a different obligation for its issuer.
An unfunded optional expansion stays pending, and service
receiving still needs separate verification.
