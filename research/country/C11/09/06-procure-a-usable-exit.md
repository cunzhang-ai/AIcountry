# DP25–DP28. Buy a service the public can continue independently

*Design220 · Part of [public digital operation](../09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md).*

**DP25 — Define the service that must survive the supplier.** For material
reliance on a contracted digital or AI service, the sector duty owner
specifies the required service during ordinary change and sudden loss.
Include effective intake and contact, original dates, continuing lawful
payments, protected human decisions, independent challenge and actual
receipt as applicable. Set the latest safe transfer and useful decision
times by existing duties. Compare competent ordinary provision,
replaceable optional assistance, another qualified operator and in-house
operation at the same service scope and real cost. A simpler lawful
route may continue the essential work. Full duplication of the tool is
required only where actual service safety warrants it.

**DP26 — Separate the public record from the optional tool.** The
competent public body retains lawful custody or independently usable
access to authoritative records, versioned rules, exact decisions and
reasons, essential configuration and relevant audit and settlement
proof. Record factual evidence, interpretation and AI output distinctly.
A format must convey status, authority, context, dates, disputed facts
and necessary access restrictions. Preserve protected originals where
needed to interpret unsupported or unfamiliar meaning. No missing
field can silently change a claim into a denial, waiver or fresh
application. Use purpose-limited export and retention, access logging
and independent review under PD. Lawful person data is not a vendor
asset to be bought back. This does not give the public body unrestricted
ownership of personal information or a right to the provider's model.

**DP27 — Price and contract the exit before award.** Under SC, specify
the usable service schedule, accepted formats and version boundaries,
timely export and support, needed operation and audit permissions,
scope of subcontractors, secure access and reissue of credentials,
overlap, actual successor, cost and source. Include orderly termination
and an abrupt loss of supplier cooperation or upstream capability.
Secure narrow, paid licences or independently held materials only
where genuinely necessary and lawful, with explicit release, access,
security, expiry and third-party rights. An escrowed program or model
can remain unusable if runtime, skills or permissions are missing.
Verify the operator's lawful ability to use what the agreement delivers.
Material renewal and lock-in changes use SC13 and DP08 under their
actual tests, including disclosed switching and continuing costs.
Earned contract and worker claims keep their own source.

**DP28 — Require a successful handover exercise before relying on it.**
The sector owner and a qualified verifier outside the supplier conflict
witness the alternate performing representative live-duty scenarios
under safe test conditions. Include loss of the original supplier,
any critical common host, credential, permission or upstream service,
and paid peak demand. Keep actual first receipt, pending rights and
authoritative payment history. A signed declaration or readable
sample file does not show that a resident can complete the service
or reach independent review. Record tested scope, gaps, protected
evidence, funding and who accepts each task. Retest material changes
and current readiness at least annually for critical dependencies,
using safe witnessed or equivalent operating evidence. A failed exit
test blocks new material reliance at that scope until corrected.
Existing service and lawful remedies continue through their own
funded owners and alternatives.
