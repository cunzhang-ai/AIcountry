# Agreement, property and liability: find the actual legal act

*Authored cases for [civil core](README.md); not judicial findings.*

| Case | Proof, protection and responsible route |
|---|---|
| V1 Three replicas each buy 100 under one 100 mandate | B03 gives one aggregate authority, not 300. Identify receipt, reservation, principal representation, merchant reliance, delivered goods and vendor failure. Actual authority/apparent reliance, restitution and separate negligent representations need distinct findings. No automatic principal debt or merchant loss is presumed; shared signatures do not resolve the conflict. |
| V2 A user revokes an agent while its vendor is offline | Verify accessible notice, receipt, expiry/limits and the relying party's actual knowledge. Preserve valid earlier commitments; reconcile events with independent challenge. An unread vendor update cannot erase a contract or impose a new one; future-media consent is not inferred from synchronization order. |
| V3 A worker's agent accepts a clause waiving wage claims | B04 bars the nonwaivable waiver regardless of signature or model confidence. Preserve actually owed wages and work evidence; a voluntary lawful settlement after a dispute needs genuine advice/refusal and review. Neither employer-controlled arbitration nor the worker's poverty removes court access. |
| V4 A registry error transfers a home to a good-faith buyer | B07 requires original/third-party notice, title evidence and interim protection. Correction does not prove who ultimately bears the loss. Actual state/wrongdoer responsibility, protected possession and compensation source need hearing and execution; no perfect-title fund is assumed. Essential housing help follows need separately. |
| V5 A connected product injures a user after a vendor update | Preserve operative versions, source tests and instructions. B09 requires proved defect and causal injury without proving vendor knowledge; actual use/misuse defenses and supplier/importer liability remain. A recall or refunded price may leave bodily/consequential loss unresolved. Neither all operators' guilt nor actual compensation is established. |
| V6 An outsourced public carer causes injury | Identify entrusted work, actual acts/control, causal duty and employer/nondelegable public responsibilities under B09–B11. The state cannot send the claimant only to an insolvent worker. Criminal gross negligence is separate; reasonable clinical error or scarce resources do not automatically prove a crime. Health/safe care continue while disputed compensation is heard. |

Case facts remain incomplete. These questions do not allocate a verdict by
account name, diagnose capacity or presume valid authority from a credential.
A signer, child, tenant, worker, supplier or public official did not actually
participate. The same AI author and shared predecessor material are not
independent corroboration.
