# Design314 decision on unrecorded public-service reliance

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design313, DP01, DP05–DP08, DP38–DP42, DP48–DP52 and the working
method. Select a way to discover material reliance without national
surveillance or a central license to operate every public service.
The project assistant authors the proposal. Existing elected and
sector institutions retain authority and legal responsibility.

## Evidence and alternatives

An IRS inventory with formal controls still missed some contracted
uses. The audit established information and scope defects and noted
the loss of quality-review staff. It did not measure the full
affected public. Existing inventories are a useful starting point.
A vendor list can help contracts without discovering actual influence.
Comprehensive surveillance creates serious privacy and working-life
burdens. A scoped, risk-directed check of real service paths provides
a route to oversight with smaller evidence requirements. The example
tests why discovered dependence changes actual capacity obligations.

## Selection and continuity

Select DP53–DP57. Use existing service and audit owners. Preserve
lawful records, dates, employment terms and independent review.
Fund checking and the ordinary alternate. A useful ordinary tool
can remain, while unqualified material reliance requires scoped
correction. A false or disputed report requires fair examination.
A change to a listing never resolves a person's claim or determines
which party was at fault. No new technical or research governance
power follows. Apply prospectively through actual competent law
and qualified funded succession.

## Reopening and next action

Compare one ordinary service contract and one actual workflow with a
qualified independent reviewer and a paid alternate, including a
joint demand window. Reopen on recurring unrecorded influence,
retaliation, excessive disclosure, shared dependency or a better
verified approach. The record establishes an observed oversight
failure and an authored service effect. Affected participation,
independent review of the proposed rules, adoption, actual readiness
and lived improvements remain open.
