# A remedy must survive evidence control and the last delivery step

*Authored cases for [civil core](README.md); not observed performance.*

| Case | Required path and failure that remains visible |
|---|---|
| V7 A landlord/platform controls proof of an unlawful eviction | First receipt preserves date; counsel/urgent independent protection precede irreparable home loss. B14 permits proportionate ordered records with privacy/privilege safeguards and a reasoned civil inference for unjustified withholding. No automatic criminal guilt or property seizure follows. Actual safe receiving and lawful possession need distinct execution. |
| V8 An agency repeatedly declares an income application incomplete | B16 requires usable relevant requirements/help and reasons; a later form cannot reset an already due payment or safe-time duty. Direct court review remains accessible. Silence does not approve everything, but a continuing unmet statutory claim is not cured by another checklist. B19 must trace any due order to the lawful Treasury/receiver. |
| V9 A poor claimant wins but the defendant is insolvent | B18 needs actual lawful assets, exemptions and third-party priority. A judgment cannot invent money or jail the debtor for inability. Insurance, contribution, receiver and insolvency/discharge routes need their own law; the claim is unpaid, not delivered. Preserve subsistence and report unresolved loss finance. |
| V10 Treasury refuses a final compensation order | Identify officer, due source, actual cash and receipt. B19's adopted bounded substitute can execute only previously lawful records/payment, not tax or general policy. Distinguish refusal from shortage; independent fault review and elected finance/receiving alternatives retain the claim. A renamed office with the same blocked key is no independent executor. |
| V11 Thousands of users face the same small hidden charge | B20 tests common facts, representative control, meaningful notice/exit, fees and actual distribution. A lawful uniform-money opt-out route is conditional; unread platform silence does not waive individualized rights. A settlement press release or unclaimed compensation remaining with a sponsor is no completed remedy. |
| V12 A child or threatened person could not file before three years | B15 assesses actual minority, concealment, threats and blocked access with reasons and defendant/third-party fairness. No diagnosis automatically removes capacity or fixes tolling. A stale record does not automatically defeat credible proof; no new research rule silently revives or cancels an already expired lawful claim. |

Actual response/hearing times, representation, notice, records, debtor/state
solvency and receiving capacity are unknown. No claimant's safety or result
has been demonstrated. The common code retains partial-failure reporting,
source-law migration and reopening; artifact checks are not independent
legal review or evidence of better life.
