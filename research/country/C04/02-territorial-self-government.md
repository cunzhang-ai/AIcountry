# C04. Territorial and nonterritorial self-government

*Design 143 · TS01–TS16 candidate · [C04 public administration](../C04-public-administration.md) · Research, not enacted law.*

The country protects cultural and collective rights and a usable route to seek
territorial change. Design143 replaces the open-ended design55 initiation promise
with a selected procedure. It does not select an actual territory or manufacture
founding authority. The exact design142 predecessor is preserved in the
[dated baseline](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-territorial-status-and-constitutional-coherence/baseline.json).

| Canonical part | Decision |
|---|---|
| [TS01–TS04: standing, map and petition](02/01-standing-scope-and-petition.md) | Existing local standing, reviewable scope, 5% affected-resident support; protected receipt and distinct national A9 mandate |
| [TS05–TS08: negotiation and disagreement](02/02-negotiation-and-public-options.md) | Qualified petition obliges bounded paid negotiation; no preliminary permission ballot or compulsory mediator |
| [TS09–TS12: exact text and consent](02/03-final-text-and-democratic-consent.md) | Existing national A9 plus separate area majority; actual ballot-day franchise, campaign resources and protected succession |
| [TS13–TS16: continuity and commencement](02/04-continuity-remedies-and-transition.md) | No automatic sovereignty, lost nationality, debt cancellation or service cutoff; paid successors and objective legal conditions |

The core standing, trigger, final authorization and rights safeguards belong in
the legitimately adopted charter. Ordinary framework law supplies administration,
resources and clocks without narrowing those protections. Numerical thresholds
and periods are explicit research choices, not empirical optima or adopted rules.
The current alternative is ordinary rights repair/retained boundaries. A unilateral
area decision and a national discretionary veto over even opening talks are real
rivals rejected here, with the national-veto objection retained in TS10.

Nonterritorial language, culture, religion, association, internal dissent and
nonmembership remain protected without using this route. Applicable Indigenous
and other collective land, governance and treaty rights retain their independent
sources and remedies. No AI, group agent or office assigns a person's identity.
A local majority cannot extinguish those rights or the civil equality of minorities.

[Legal-spine review and limitations](../../settlement/territorial-status/README.md)
identify the scoped coherence repair and the next whole-country question. No
actual affected-party participation, authority, funding, operation or better-life
outcome is established by the selected procedure.

**Clause-family scope.** Here TS denotes **C04 territorial status**. The distinct
C08/22 technical-safety TS clauses retain their own source/meaning. Use the chapter
and linked canonical path when handing off a clause; no unqualified TS label can
transfer authority, support or review between them. Historical artifacts stay exact.
