# Necessary care: source-law transition and a contestable choice

*Canonical UA19–UA20; part of [C08/23](../23-necessary-care-and-unknown-will.md).*

**UA19 — A proposed exception must be adopted openly.** Preserve B21/B23 legal
agency/asset boundaries, S consent/receiving duties, E's lack of consent/force
powers, C08/08–09 refusal/liberty protections and RP specific bodily authority.
UA is the explicit new residual legal source, not a reinterpretation claiming
those earlier texts already authorized it. RP01/RPO01 need the exact narrow
UA09 therapeutic exception; no other reproductive permission changes silently.
C08/08 retains no assisted-dying authority as an explicit no-change selection;
a future lawful public process may propose another complete model.

The CRPD Committee's GC1 prefers own will/preferences and rejects substitute
medical consent. Its2018 correction makes substitute-regime characteristics
alternatives: retained nominal capacity, single-act scope and judicial review
cannot alone establish compatibility. A truly unknown-will necessity act may
remain contrary to that interpretation. Disclose this substantive objection,
not a drafting defect cured by renaming guardianship. National law, actual
treaty duties, qualified legal/clinical review and disability/affected participation
must be reconciled before any legitimate adoption; this project has none.

A competent existing procedure and affected public choose exact prospective law,
scope, resources and commencement. Prior actual law/software and constitutional
holds continue until authorized revision. Map people, actual choices/advance
records, existing agents/orders/treatment, claims, source privacy, paid staff and
receivers to lawful successors; no blanket incapacity conversion, new guardian,
retroactive authorization, forced disclosure or deadline reset. Reassess old
orders individually, preserving stronger rights and safe lawful care. The new
method does not approve itself or erase accrued remedies for prior abuse.

**UA20 — Retain alternatives and know what could displace this one.** Strict
nonintervention without actual choice protects against paternalism but can permit
avoidable harm or engineered emergency. Interpretation-only centers the person
but can become fiction when relevant evidence is absent. Broad best-interests
substitution offers more reach at the cost of power to define a worthwhile life.
The selected narrow necessity route permits some acts without known consent and
can therefore contradict a person's unknown wishes. These value conflicts remain.

Hypotheses are that explicit scope, opposition stops, independent representation,
finite court orders and actual receiving constrain abuse better than hidden
substitution or abandonment. No numerical score, artifact check or AI agreement
proves that hypothesis. Reopen on misclassified refusal, inaccessible support,
normalizing/covert treatment, ineffective independent review, prolonged control,
unjustified reproductive/life-support acts, unmet basics or a better complete rival.

RR/FA now selects revision/attention; next the joined full-duty
material/transition comparison, then objective-wide review. Do not automatically
extend a local care study or treat unknown actual adoption as unfinished wording.
