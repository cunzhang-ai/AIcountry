# DP64–DP69. Give public decision-makers an independent first view

*Design358 · Part of [public digital operation](../09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md).*

**DP64 — Apply the safeguard where advice can change a person's position.**
For a class expressly covered by the adopting law, use this rule before
material adverse decisions involving essential service, contested public
debt or another specified high-consequence public act. Cover nominally
advisory systems where their recommendations, evidence selection or queues
materially shape the result. Identify the decision, official, operative law,
facts needed and useful time. Routine clerical help and independently
verified favorable payments retain their lawful routes. This rule creates
no new authority to automate decisions or enlarge a court's or agency's
powers. Stronger sector safeguards continue.

**DP65 — Establish the case before seeing the recommendation.** The
responsible officer first examines the operative rule, relevant original
records and affected person's evidence through a usable route outside the
material derivation being checked. Record the initial facts, missing proof,
plausible alternatives and any provisional disposition before showing an
automated assessment, suggested reason or previous inferred status.
Do not postpone relevant original evidence, including evidence favorable
to the person or a credible urgent warning. Translations and summaries
remain attributable and their originals remain inspectable. The record
protects a reasoned change of mind. It is neither a forced verdict nor
an employee productivity score. If the officer already saw the conclusion,
identify that exposure and use the separately staffed route when required.

**DP66 — Verify real differences before an adverse act.** After the first
view, show relevant advice, version, limitations and traceable grounds.
Treat recommendations as leads. Verify any material change of view
against inspectable source facts and applicable law and record the
reasons for accepting or rejecting it. If disagreement reveals a
material unresolved fact or common transformation, or an independent
first view is unavailable in a covered case, use a qualified alternate
outside the relevant dependency before relying on the advice adversely.
Different brands, signatures or jobs with the same derived file do
not provide independent support. An already competent officer may
make an ordinary lawful decision on sufficient source evidence.
An unresolved adverse recommendation alone supplies no proof.

**DP67 — Keep timely decisions, protection and outside review usable.**
The sector owner pays for source access, initial review, qualified
alternate decisions and original deadlines on the same schedule.
Apply ordinary notice, response and challenge rules. Give affected
people the material grounds, source access permitted by law and a
direct route outside the operator. Do not withhold an already due
award or move the first-receipt clock because a check is incomplete.
Use separately authorized urgent action or interim protection on
its own evidence and deadlines. If no qualified route can act in
time, report each unresolved duty, seek paid correction and keep
ordinary review open. Hold the unsupported adverse act. Neither
delay nor reviewer absence creates consent, proof or a new award.

**DP68 — Test judgment and capacity against a competent ordinary route.**
Before material reliance and after changes, compare recommendations
shown at the start, recommendations shown after the first view, and
competent ordinary work under the same law and deadlines. Use lawful
shadow cases or protected testing without exposure to unsupported
adverse acts. Compare missed relevant facts, erroneous actions,
useful-time decisions, group coverage, unpaid resident effort, review
work and remedies. Use qualified assessors outside the common derived
source and count actual staff conflicts. If no route with advice
demonstrates usable improvement and meets the safeguards, retain the
ordinary route and fix its constraints. Record breaches if ordinary
capacity itself fails. A lower disagreement rate or faster signature
does not qualify a route.

**DP69 — Follow the same faulty inference into other uses.** On a credible
common fault, the sector owner and DP coordinator trace the affected
scope under DP38–DP42. Use EV and LX for decisions already made,
overlooked cases and delivered correction. Share only the necessary
lawful dependency information across sectors. Protect original evidence
and scoped case records, record unresolved exposure and retain valid
rights through transfer. The AI supplier, approval officer and
independent review route remain answerable within their own powers.
Reopen on an omitted original, changed advice path, missed deadline,
dependence of the purported reviewer or a simpler equally effective route.
