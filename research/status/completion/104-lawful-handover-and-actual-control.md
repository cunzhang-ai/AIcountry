# Completion finding. Legal succession and practical control have separate checks

Design196 assigns who verifies the effective act, withdraws expired
control, grants lawful scoped access and continues already owed duties.
It gives separately accountable recovery and direct relief a defined
path. [Canonical rules](../../country/C03/19-lawful-handover-and-actual-control.md)

A documented foreign transition budget and distinct technical standards
inform scoped preparation. A desk case tests conflicting sources and
loss of actual access. No actual handover, qualified independence,
lawful adoption, readiness or public outcome is verified.
