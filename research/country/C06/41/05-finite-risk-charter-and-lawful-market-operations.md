# Money: finite public risks without an annual payment cliff

*MC17–MC20; part of [C06/41](../41-money-and-financial-public-control.md).*

**MC17 — A continuing primary-law charter, not annual blank permission.**
Before new operations, the Assembly enacts finite typed domestic-unit ceilings
for aggregate gross ELA drawn plus enforceable undrawn commitments, each bank/
common-control group across all monetary credit, including ordinary repos and
exceptional facilities without double counting; intraday credit; open-market
holdings/commitments by
eligible class and issuer, and sovereign exposure. It specifies eligible
counterparties/collateral, independent valuation/minimum haircuts, maturities,
settlement terms, loss/provision rules, operating budget and any separately
enacted fiscal indemnity. Separately type each FX intervention ceiling and
unencumbered floor in its actual deliverable owned settlement currency (or
specified deliverable basket/date); report domestic valuation separately.
Domestic appreciation is not new foreign cash or a larger foreign floor.
The charter caps each deliverable foreign-currency holding/commitment and
specifies a calendar-year gross FX-sale/intervention-outflow maximum: actual
repurchases/inflows replenish owned stock but never refund used gross headroom.
No netting across currencies or new forward/swap/foreign-borrowing class is
selected by default; those need explicit primary-law exposure/settlement terms.
No blank, unknown or “as necessary” field permits that class of new exposure.
These are context calibration fields: actual stock/outflow/stress scenarios,
contagion/credit/employment effects and outside rights/source review must support
amounts chosen by the Assembly in the joint C06 account. No universal nominal
cap or actual capacity is invented here; without a valid amount no new risk.

The charter continues until lawful amendment, with mandatory annual audit,
stress publication and MC29 hearings. Reject automatic annual reauthorization/
90-day cliff: a stalled budget must not by itself end lawful monetary functions.
This favors continuity and risks stale expert-shaped ceilings; annual review
cannot silently expand/index them. A higher ceiling, new class, guarantee or
fiscal facility needs the competent prior Act. The public can contest/replace
the architecture prospectively; the committee cannot rewrite its own charter.

**MC18 — Keep gross exposure, obligations and ownership.** Count drawn and
actually binding undrawn claims once across related subsidiaries and promised
facilities; collateral limits credit loss, not gross public exposure. Count
an exposure from legal commitment through actual repayment/termination, not
until an expected recovery. Known common control cannot be divided by account,
agent or name. No netting against insurer F, G/S/SF standby, client/pension
assets, future tax or speculative recovery. Perform existing legally due
liabilities/commitments under their actual source and disclose overrun; ceilings
bound new commitments, not cancel prior claims or contractual interest.
No authority to create new risk from a policy deadlock or missing risk field.

**MC19 — Typed operations, not a fiscal back door.** The ordinary charter
permits authority-issued institutional settlement balances, their own policy
rates; standardized short secured operations with eligible licensed solvent
banks; genuinely secondary domestic-unit central-government securities at
independently supported market prices within enacted holdings/maturity risk;
and managed-float exchange of actually owned reserves with willing counterparties.
No ordinary corporate/sector credit purchase or development subsidy is selected.
Those require a different explicit primary-law/public-loss choice. New public
issues or a prearranged dealer/affiliate take-out require M15's separate direct-
credit statute, not a secondary-market label. Automatic Treasury overdraft
remains zero; tax, borrowing and lawful spending remain elected fiscal powers.

**MC20 — Actual transactions and foreign limits.** Career operators execute
within actual valid terms/mandates, independently verified title, conservative
valuation, collateral control and uncommitted source. Record trade/controller,
price, settlement, original facts/uncertainty, holdings, reserve-liability
counterpart and expected/stress risk. The monetary committee cannot force a
market seller/foreign correspondent to accept a transaction or promise par
conversion. Treasury FX, monetary FX and private exporter currency are distinct;
encumbrances and the lawful owned-FX floor are not available intervention cash.
Reserve creation is a liability/asset operation, not Treasury tax revenue,
real output, free imports or recipient receipt. Existing denominations and
foreign-law contracts change only through valid consent/law with remedies.

Design108 retains a distinct sovereign fiscal-issue rival for comparison, not
an MC19 operation or present source. A nonrepayable fiscal transfer requires an
express competent primary-law change to the current Treasury-credit boundary
and an honest liability/loss/public-control account. Reserve creation is not
distributable profit or owned FX; no model number chooses a ceiling or amends
actual due claims. Current charters, courts, source records and authority remain.
