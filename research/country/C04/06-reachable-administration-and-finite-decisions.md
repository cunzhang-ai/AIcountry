# C04. A state that must hear, decide and answer for delay

3 November 2026 · Design 140 core and Design446 access path · AP01–AP28 research candidate, not enacted law

**Retain separate competent decisions, but end the ordinary clock that starts
only when an office calls a file complete.** A person needs a reachable duty
to act, not merely the right to appeal an adverse decision that never arrives.
This changes the common B16 default, not substantive eligibility, political
authority, professional judgment or a shorter/special source-law procedure.

[B16](../C08/13/04-access-evidence-and-public-decisions.md) retains the ordinary
five-working-day help, 30-working-day decision, one at-most-30-working-day
extension, ordinary 20-working-day adverse reply and notice-based judicial
filing rules. The decision clock now uses protected first receipt. Neither
transfer, missing evidence, an AI queue nor a new case label restarts it.

These numbers remain contestable hypotheses. A deadline cannot supply missing
facts, staff or lawful resources. A reasonless refusal at day 30/60 is not
completion. Delay and evidentiary uncertainty must become challengeable, rather
than an indefinite unreported queue or automatic entitlement/licence. Fair
hearing and urgent protection survive a missed deadline.

The [country review](../../settlement/administrative-standing/README.md) compares
retain/repair, receipt-based duties, tacit approval and substantive simplification.
There is no new super-agency, compulsory platform or automatic new court power.
Legitimate lawmakers retain programme rules; existing ordinary courts determine
lawfulness and owed relief within their jurisdiction. All current actual law,
software, permissions, constitutional holds and common research method remain.

Actual independent affected review, staff/records/payment capacity, full country
source and better lives remain unverified. Old claims and stronger source laws
retain paid OP/T successors. Reopen on rushed denials, excluded applicants,
unusable proof, third-party harm, delay displacement or a stronger simpler model.
