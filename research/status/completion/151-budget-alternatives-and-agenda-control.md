# Design243. Budget alternatives and agenda control

Give eligible exact whole budgets a protected path to a final floor
vote. Use a common indicative vote to order alternatives. Retain the
full-Assembly threshold and separate authority for binding adoption.

- [Canonical clauses](../../country/C06/60/05-competing-budgets-and-floor-choice.md)
- [Worked comparison and reproduction](../../settlement/budget-alternatives/README.md)
- [Decision and transition](../../2026-11-budget-alternatives-and-agenda-control/decision-record.md)

inclusion, funded scrutiny and comparative outcomes remain open.
