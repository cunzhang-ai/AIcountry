# TN13–TN16: the other taxes cannot stay invisible

*Part of [TN](../59-tax-sources-and-country-operation.md), design156 research.*

**TN13 — Match the whole recurring-source family.** Extending TN beyond PA is
necessary: NF's company25%, VAT10%/zeros, site1–2% and transfer10/20% also
change the source regime. A reference welfare budget cannot retain all its
receipts while silently replacing those rates/bases. Fresh SKAT2023 records
259.320105bnDKK in TWO VAT leaves:254.162052bn ordinary VAT and5.158053bn
One Stop VAT to Denmark. The broader turnover group270.457955bn includes
11.137850bn of other sources; it is not all VAT. Select leaves once; do not
add them again to their parent or total. Statistical tax revenue, assessed
company liability, due-date cash and candidate legal bases remain distinct.

**TN14 — Show the price convention.** In a declared unchanged eligible-base
experiment, reducing25% to10% gives new receipts `V*(.10/.25)` when tax-exclusive
base is fixed; keeping tax-inclusive spending fixed instead gives
`V*(.10/1.10)/(.25/1.25)`. With no additional removed base, these are103.728042bn
and117.872775bn, reductions155.592063bn/141.447330bn. These are two authored
demand conventions, neither behavioral predictions nor bounds on actual NF
yield. New zero-list, registration, border/refund timing and compliance need
their own matched base. A20% removed-base sensitivity is authored, not an
observed food/care share. Changing exempt clinical/education supply to zero
can create input refunds; public grants without purchased supply remain outside
VAT, not automatically taxable/refundable service turnover. Never count both
lower consumer prices and unchanged tax-exclusive producer revenue without
stating the actual pricing/demand counterpart.

**TN15 — Company profit is not a homogeneous22% tax base.** Fresh SELSK3 gives
a corporation count of94,109,486.852bnDKK taxable income and103.280bn assessed tax.
Mechanical25% of that aggregate base is121.713bn,18.433bn higher; this deliberately
omits actual relief/base/behavior changes and is NOT candidate yield or its
upper bound. The financial-industry row is nested, not additive; it is also not
the exact statutory cohort. Official guidance distinguishes ordinary22%,
financial25.2% in2023 and some cooperative/other special treatment. Joint-tax
reporting assigns subsidiaries to the parent; NF's separate unit/no group-loss
rules therefore need a fresh bridge. Assessed103.280bn differs0.817516bn from
SKAT's corresponding102.462484bn; preserve the gap, no extra source or invented
zero-credit22% reconstruction. Different vintages/credits/periods remain material.

**TN16 — Trace who receives a tax change.** VAT remitter, consumer, producer,
worker, owner and public recipient are not one person. Lower taxes may reduce
prices, increase margins/quantities or change wages; none is guaranteed.
Test increases and decreases separately at relevant service and cash
cutoffs, including regulated bill adjustment, charges bundled into rent
and effects on workers and other customers. Map direct tax and pass-through
once, with residual or uncertain burdens visible. Reuse comparable
observations only within their evidenced period and market. Combine
actual retail basket and I05 need/receipt correction once; no automatic basic
cut because a rate fell. Company tax and later personal dividends are separate
legal events with cumulative incidence, not two free sources from the same
profit. Land deferral/80% local shares, equalization, received-transfer thresholds,
private pensions and restricted Y keep their own bases/title/claims. No site
value, private wealth, carbon receipt, net AI gain or G-accessible S is inferred
to fill the unpriced gap. Those mechanisms remain real public alternatives.
