# Failed assumptions retain rights and require a lawful choice

*SF09–SF10; part of [C06/25](../25-source-finance-and-payment-continuity.md).*

**SF09 — A failed source is not a new discretionary rescue power.** The
hybrid reference fails its liquidity subset when no usable sovereign market
exists, deposit withdrawal is 60%, or the fixed-coupon perpetual price halves.
Capital protection does not make every due payment liquid. Preserve actual
M assessment, lawful solvent-bank facilities and distinct resolution/loss
rules; no assumed new monetary loan, public guarantee, account freeze or
priority shift repairs a failure automatically. A future facility needs
actual eligibility, collateral, source, price, repayment and authority.

The protected standby has an actual asset outside the failed lender, but
still needs independent operating access, execution and safe recipients.
Bank failure, custodian failure, common cloud failure and sovereign currency/
resource failure differ. Existing claim, legal authority, available cash,
executed payment and lived protection remain separate. A safe reserve unit
cannot deliver foreign medicine, staff a clinic or extend its own mandate.
Do not use an institution's score or insurer's label as proof of any of them.

The numerical subset assumes qualified opening values, a liquid market,
full conditional normal cash flows and existing aggregate operating costs.
New safe-operator, monetary, legal, valuation, insurance and compensation
requirements remain unpriced/unqualified. A50 extra expense or missing source
is not funded by the same old bid, hidden asset recovery or model saving.
Even apparent total cash can include earmarked standby: show unrestricted
cash separately. The public cannot be promised two simultaneous payments
from one reserve, or permanently funded continuity from one month's stock.

**SF10 — Adoption and migration require actual authority and real offers.**
A real affected public must choose the general arrangement and instruments
under lawful constitutional/Assembly powers; actual owners choose their
particular voluntary asset and equity trades. Operational monetary independence,
judicial review and prior existing contracts remain. A new source method
cannot approve its own rule changes or silently transfer old reviews.

Before commencement, validate lawful titles, maturities/priority, new note
terms/price, investor/borrower assent, full source settlement, beneficial
ownership, equity control, safe-cash custody, mandate limits, bank/monetary
losses, fees, tax, FX and all actual staff/cash. Map old accounts, reserves,
claims, pending remedies and due services to accepted successors. No compulsory
household portfolio conversion, pension raid, retroactive offense or assumed
same-meaning model update. Consent to a payment tool is not consent to lending,
a change of constitution or an AI's future expanded mandate.

The [reference and decision](../../../settlement/source-finance/README.md)
finish a bounded conditional source/claims comparison, not the country.
Next complete reproductive/conflict-detention and identified technical sector
statutes, then audit the whole ordinary-law, resources and effective-public-
control design. Reopen finance on actual market/source/balance-sheet evidence,
changed payment needs, cost, harmful access effects or a stronger rival;
do not automatically add another invented macro scenario in lieu of missing evidence.
