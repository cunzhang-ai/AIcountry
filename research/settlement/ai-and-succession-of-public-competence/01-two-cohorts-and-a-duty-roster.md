# Two cohorts and a public duty roster

*Constructed Design369 comparison · Three annual windows · Illustrative units only.*

A public service has six fully competent paid workers in window one.
Without replacement, known departures leave four in window two and three
in window three. Its protected casework requires four simultaneous work
blocks without the disputed assistance. Independent review requires a
separate fifth block in every route. One distinct qualified mentor block
can supervise two paid entrants during a window. Neither entrant supplies
any current duty or review block. Successful entrants become competent
caseworkers in the following window and accept paid work. This model
assumes retained reviewer and mentor qualifications only where expressly
stated. Noncompleters and departures earn no later capacity credit.

A paid block costs one model unit per worker or entrant. Assistance costs
one further unit per window. Assume for comparison that qualified help
could reduce ordinary casework to three blocks. This hypothetical gain
changes neither the independent review block nor casework capacity
required in the loss-of-assistance path. The model spends on current
duties before opening a new cohort. It assigns the same roster only
across mutually exclusive service paths, never across simultaneous duties.

| Route | Workers by window | Paid cost by window | Protected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary service with paid renewal | 6, 6, 7 | 8, 8, 9 | Four casework blocks, separate review and mentor all fit in each window. |
| Fast assisted route that closes entry work | 6, 4, 3 | 7, 5, 4 | Assisted casework and review fit initially and in window two. The independent route fails in window two. Even assisted casework with review fails in window three. |
| Bounded assistance with paid renewal | 6, 6, 7 | 9, 9, 10 | Both service paths and the paid cohort fit. Actual net benefit from assistance still requires comparison. |

The ordinary route is the supported default in this comparison until
the additional cost of bounded assistance buys a verified improvement.
Where one of the first two entrants does not qualify, the bounded route
has five staff in window two. It can cover four independent casework
blocks and separate review, leaving no distinct mentor. If it opens no
second cohort and buys no qualified overlap, only four remain in window
three. A separately available and paid qualified mentor in window two
could preserve the second cohort. One extra budget unit alone cannot.
If all applicants qualify but the mentor leaves, a general caseworker
likewise does not supply the missing mentor qualification. If review
depends on the disputed system, none of the staffing totals establishes
independent review.
