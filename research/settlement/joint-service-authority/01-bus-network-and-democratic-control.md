# A shared bus service with local control and wider accountability

*Design312 · Constructed authority, capacity and vote comparison.*

Four elected councils establish one bus compact by the same exact
recorded approval. A, B, C and D have 58, 20, 12 and 10 units of
population respectively. These are authored assumptions. An outside
area E sends some users into the service. Its health routes and the
water catchment cross different boundaries. Applicable outside
rights remain. A bus compact acquires no authority over either network.

The councils fund 12 units of continuing service and have 6 additional
usable units and one qualified vehicle and driver for the same window.
The shared choice is a new evening extension serving the largest town
or an interchange connection for small towns and the center. Each
option costs 6 and uses that vehicle. An approved vote cannot deliver
both. Protect the prior 12 and inspect the ordinary floor before any
optional start. Assume the two stated options meet their legal scope.

| Exact recorded vote | Council and population counts | Result under the compact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A alone votes for its extension | 1 of 4 and 58 of 100 | The extension is not authorized. |
| B, C and D vote for a connection | 3 of 4 and 42 of 100 | The connection is not authorized. |
| A, C and D vote for the funded interchange connection | 3 of 4 and 80 of 100 | The choice passes. Qualified service still needs actual acceptance. |
| A and C vote yes, D is absent and B votes no | 2 of 4 and 70 of 100 | The absent seat stays in the denominator. |
| A, C and D vote for the interchange and to levy a charge on E | 3 of 4 and 80 of 100 | The combined motion exceeds the compact. Put a lawful separate version to an exact vote. |

Population voting alone permits A to decide. Equal votes among
councils permit B, C and D to decide over a majority of the member
population. The selected rule requires broader agreement in this
example. A can still block all optional decisions and two smaller
councils can do the same. Continued failure warrants funded and
public consideration of a different decision rule, the ordinary
Assembly residual, or a directly elected alternative on its own
lawful scale. A larger tier can simplify decisions when demands
persist. It requires funded opposition, a usable direct ballot and
legitimate new authority and can fit one network poorly.

Area E can challenge an outside burden or loss of its established
rights. Its residents can raise a material journey effect through
the public route even when the exact motion lies within scope.
An existing multi-council service contract can be cheaper where no
material allocation discretion needs to be delegated. Loss of the
new vehicle holds the optional start while actual due services
continue on their sources. A later compact exit cannot cancel a
driver's lawful pay, an accrued resident remedy or an existing debt.
Verify the successor and applicable rights separately.

Bavarian law supplies a real example of municipal representation
and optional vote weighting by association statute. It does not
supply this dual threshold or evidence that the selected design
delivers better bus service. [KommZG, articles 31–33](https://www.gesetze-bayern.de/Content/Document/BayKommZG/true)
