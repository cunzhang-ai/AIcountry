# Democracy needs usable public dissent between elections

Design 189 · [AX01–AX20](../../country/C03/18-peaceful-assembly-and-public-dissent.md)

Existing C02 rights, LF07 assembly protections, RX03 restriction justification,
B17 useful-time courts and PD27/PM21 safeguards remain real design commitments.
What was missing was the affirmative ordinary assembly/public-space act and
explicit bounded task source. No claim that the preceding design endorsed
indiscriminate force, lacked all remedies or required protest permission.

| Practical choice | Selected rule | Material tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| May people assemble? | Right without prior permit or compulsory notice; spontaneous/anonymous/leaderless forms protected | Less compulsory advance information; actual service/protection planning still needed |
| Is there a usable place? | Practical accessible civic forums and existing public-access rights; private title/facility/safety remain lawful | Real land/source and competing users; no automatic private mall occupation |
| Who facilitates? | Existing qualified C04 public-space career officers, accepted local/national coordination and PO actual command | Actual paid competence, public-buyer conflicts and political budget capture |
| Who restricts? | Prior ordinary court for material planned assembly-specific conditions; precise serious harm/least restrictive scope | Judges/counsel/rotas cost actual capacity and can be captured; no routine court event licence |
| What if harm cannot wait? | Narrow predesignated civilian command, immediate useful-time court and six-hour absolute outer unilateral clock | Neither waiting entitlement nor crowd custody; no serial/renamed restart or replacement of refused/expired judicial order on same facts |
| What physical act? | Explicit scoped minimum sworn-police escort/removal plus separate LF necessity/life ceiling | No new weapons/arrest/search/containment/crime; unsafe execution must stop |
| What about publication? | Court/precise substantive wrong, grave-harm test for advance/provisional suppression including takedown; final remedies actual proof/defenses | Privacy/body/child safety and protected criticism remain contested; no truth licence or whole-network switch |
| Who pays and receives relief? | Actual public sources and useful-time independent claims/execution; no basic policing/medical/cleaning fees | Simultaneous lawful care/court/police/local duties and actual source remain unqualified |
