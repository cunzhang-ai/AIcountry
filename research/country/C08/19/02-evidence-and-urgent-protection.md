# Necessary evidence and protection that cannot renew itself

*V03–V04; part of [C08/19](../19-regulation-and-bounded-enforcement.md).*

**V03 — Evidence powers follow the actual activity.** A competent inspector
can require necessary relevant activity records, identified samples and
safe access to a licensed workplace within prior statutory scope, with
purpose, period, confidentiality, privilege and usable challenge specified.
No general access to homes, private messages, unrelated people or foreign
servers follows. Nonconsensual entry/search, forced seizure or personal
surveillance needs a specific independent warrant and applicable P safeguards;
V creates no warrant exception. A criminal investigation cannot disguise
itself as a routine inspection to defeat silence, counsel or exclusion.

Separate original observations, transformations, uncertainty and shared
sources. Preserve exculpatory material and a protected independent copy
where the subject controls a key platform. A model flag triggers human
inquiry, not a finding of breach. Record access/retention is purpose-limited;
protected testimony is not automatically public or available for training.
Self-incrimination/privilege cannot be punished as failure to cooperate.
A disputed excessive request has an urgent independent challenge, without
losing evidence by default or granting a blanket obstruction immunity.

**V04 — Narrow urgent protection.** On documented credible facts showing
imminent serious harm, the competent sector official may order only the
necessary temporary stop, isolation, warning or preservation already within
the enacted power. Identify act, affected people, evidence, safe receiving,
executor, useful time and appeal. Mere noncompliance, unpopular activity or
a forecast score is insufficient. No criminal guilt, general asset freeze,
compulsory clinical treatment, confinement or new search power follows.

Give notice immediately unless a court permits a narrowly necessary delay;
offer actual response as soon as feasible. A measure without prior hearing
reaches an independent meaningful hearing within 72 actual hours under B17,
with its one strictly necessary extension of at most 72 hours. No relabelled
serial order restarts that event. At the hearing the regulator must establish
the lawful protection grounds and necessity; a later bounded order specifies
expiry and review at least every 30 days while urgent grounds continue.
No indefinite emergency license suspension. Existing shorter sector/liberty
clocks prevail; this route cannot displace general health or detention law.

An unsafe product/activity may have to stop before a replacement is ready;
that necessity does not discharge the named public receiving duty. Disclose
unserved people and obtain lawful delivery/funding action, rather than calling
interruption either immunity for the firm or completed protection for users.

**Design147 HP join.** [HP](../../C10/11-ordinary-health-and-hazard-protection.md)
provides the express special ordinary health/place permissions; V04 creates none.
HP individual first restraint needs meaningful hearing/order by24continuous hours;
specific unreviewed place closure ends at72hours without V04/B17's extra72-hour
extension. HP bodily consent, civil entry, targeted enforcement and DR/LR general
rules remain separate. Technical/regulatory duties do not resurrect an expired
health order or imply criminal guilt, treatment or a permanent land acquisition.
