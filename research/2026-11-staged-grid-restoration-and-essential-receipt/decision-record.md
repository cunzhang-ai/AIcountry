# Design259 decision record

**Scope and baseline.** Extend the Design258 country design with the
national and local sequence for widespread electricity restoration under
OC and EG. OC01–OC08 already requires protected service deadlines, usable
reserve, safe alternates and a qualified handback. It does not resolve
how to compare national energization with the duration of multiple local
service bridges and a second trip before replenishment. The applicable
[research framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [handoff method](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md)
guide this proposal.

**Evidence and rival explanations.** The [operator event record](sources.md)
distinguishes restart attempts, local electrical areas, synchronous connection
and later network endpoints. The [authored comparison](../settlement/staged-grid-restoration/02-authored-restoration-comparison.md)
asks which routes can meet the same protected duties. Some interruptions
reflect limited aggregate supply. Others follow a startup constraint,
concurrent dependence, inaccessible receiving or unclear control. Extra
generation can remedy the first case while leaving the others exposed.
The case assumes independent treated water through H1, and a trained local
crew, specific start demand and an external tie. It supplies no affected-party
participation or actual cost estimates.

**Selection.** Retain bulk-first restoration where the qualified path
meets all applicable service deadlines and compare it with autonomous
local service. Prefer the combined path in the authored first window
because it covers the stated services through the modeled H3 handoff
and then to H5. The local-only store is exhausted before H5.
This result is conditional. A second call before recharge defeats the
base fixture even after timely transfer. A late tie requires an earlier
alternate. In a live comparison, include the option of delivering a
sector service directly if its actual route is safer, more reliable or less
costly. Fund the first protected interval and the next plausible call
before making a resilience claim.

**Authority and objections.** A single technically qualified operator can
control switching within scope. Public classes and priority criteria require
prior law and are subject to independent review and effective correction.
A published rule can still favor politically central places if it measures
load alone. Record affected homes, usable access and material exceptions.
Technical risk can justify a hold while activating the proper alternate.
It cannot silently extinguish a due claim. Local stock and crews may share
the same access, controls or working time as the primary route. Check
actual independence and price the reserve alongside ordinary work.
Public evidence must protect medical and grid-security details.
Original dates, donor limits and safe technical authority continue.

**Migration.** Update C09's outage and electricity entries and the C06
common-failure comparison together. Carry existing duty, authority,
connection, donor, payment, worker, and remedy records into each stage.
Introduce no new procurement, emergency declaration or independent
appropriation. Source evidence applies only to the documented event and
method scope. A changed path, duty, source, qualified receiver or safety
bound requires fresh local review. An AI reconciliation may carry context
and flag a discrepancy. It cannot transfer approval to a changed route.

**Evidence and authority status.** Original operator-derived evidence and
authored arithmetic were inspected within the documented scope. OC09–OC13
is a research selection. Legitimate enactment, independent technical and
affected-party review, paid operational qualification and improved resident
outcomes are open.

**Next bounded decision.** Ask the participating electricity, water, care
and communications operators to reconcile one lawful territory-level
service window with the first-budget dates. Compare the paid bulk route,
a matched local bridge and direct sector substitutions under a late tie and
a renewed trip. Deliver a dated ledger of source, paid staff, safety,
resident receipt, independent challenge and replenishment. Keep ordinary
services and democratic acts in scope wherever the same source supports
them. Actual time, financial cap, site permission, counterparties and
owners must be agreed before any live exercise. Reopen on a repeated start
failure, common loss, adverse receipt finding, review-capacity loss or a
lower-cost route that meets the same duties.
