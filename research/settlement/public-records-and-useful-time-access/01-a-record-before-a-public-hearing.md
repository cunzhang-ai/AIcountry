## One public hearing

A resident requests an existing summary and the material underlying
record on working day 0. A statutory consultation hearing opens on
working day 7. The public custodian holds the summary. A contractor
holds the original underlying record on the authority's behalf.
Parts of it are private and must be withheld. Its remainder can
be disclosed without identifying protected people. The case assumes
lawful authority, a promptly established scope and existing redaction
standards. Use only available funds and qualified capacity.

| Route or failure | What reaches the resident before day 7 |
|---|---|
| Ordinary complete response on day 8 | Both lawful portions can arrive within the outer ordinary window, after this hearing opportunity. |
| Early rolling disclosure with six units | Two units deliver the usable existing summary. Four separately assigned units buy source retrieval, qualified redaction and release of the usable underlying record on day 6. |
| Five units, or an independent original unavailable | The qualified summary can still arrive on day 6. The underlying record is not credited. The authority preserves the record and gives the actual reason and direct review. |
| Redaction cannot be verified | Release the qualified summary. Protect the sensitive underlying content and seek expedited independent review. Do not replace the source with a generated answer. |

A standalone public information commissioner is a structural alternative.
Its own appointments, money, review limits and delivery capacity would
need to be priced. The scoped selection uses agency records officers
and a direct independently executable court route, alongside regular
proactive release. In the constructed case, the needed transfer and
redaction capacity can make the difference before the hearing. A
refusal or missed hearing may justify relief on the existing law.
It does not automatically decide or stop the underlying public proposal.
