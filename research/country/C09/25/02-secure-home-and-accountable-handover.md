# HL05–HL08. Secure tenure and accountable handover

[Housing loss and secure receiving](../25-housing-loss-and-secure-receiving.md)

**HL05 — Offer ordinary tenure as soon as it is usable.** Give the
eligible person a reasoned durable plan with usable options within
14 calendar days of first receipt, and sooner when needed to avoid
loss. Start ordinary H or private tenancy directly when a qualified
home, accepted terms, actual access and ongoing source are ready.
Use the existing allocation order and individualized suitability.
Provide chosen lawful support under S, AS or health law independently
from ordinary tenure. No proof of sobriety, treatment compliance,
institutional stay or predictive readiness is required merely to
qualify for housing. Ordinary lawful conduct, consent and immediate
safety protections apply. A voucher, consent form or offer alone
cannot prove occupation or affordability. Keep urgent receiving
while securing a suitable alternative for someone who needs it.
Reassess unresolved cases with the person at least every 14 calendar
days without restarting the original claim.

**HL06 — Make departure and relocation the duty of actual receivers.**
A hospital, child-care authority, prison or disaster service planning
a foreseeable departure offers a confidential route to the steward
early enough to secure accepted housing. With lawful authorization
and actual necessity, send minimum useful information. Offer direct
access if the person declines the transfer of records. The sending
service owns its lawful departure and continuing duties. The housing
steward owns actual receiving under its scope. Missing housing never
extends detention or permits involuntary treatment. Preserve original
release, tenancy, YC and care claims. Across a boundary use XR
actual host receiving and interim financing within their own scope.
The person's choice, accepted place, payment and final liability
are separately recorded. No compulsory move to settle jurisdiction.

**HL07 — Trace the remedy to a person getting inside.** Affected people
can reach the existing unconflicted ordinary court directly, with
actual assistance and protected originals. Apply E13 and B17 urgent
relief within their scopes and sooner than an avoidable loss of home.
The court may determine due duties and order qualified interim or
substitute performance under applicable law. The steward and lawful
operator verify arrival, accessible keys, safe contact and actual
availability through the ordered period. The court does not assign
an occupied home, appropriate money or appoint a carer by assumption.
Record unresolved stock or staff failure and seek timely lawful
elected correction. Provide safe reasons and a way to challenge a
refusal, unsafe placement, coercion or an adverse use of data.

**HL08 — Safeguard location and personal choice.** Keep private
location, sensitive housing history and temporary contact details
under PD with independent original access for actual review.
The steward can use assistive tools to check known vacancies, match
access features and identify payment gaps. Only an accountable
human receiver verifies actual availability and an affected person's
agreement. An AI score cannot create eligibility, denial, relocation,
consent or a police alert. Do not require continuous tracking or
automatic disclosure of a protected location to landlords, family,
immigration or police. Separately authorized and proportionate
access under applicable law retains its own proof and oversight.
