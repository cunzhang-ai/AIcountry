# Design404. Preserve independent choices through successive acquisitions

[SCM01–SCM08](../../country/C05/11/11-successive-acquisitions-and-market-choice.md)
adds prospective filing for a bounded sequence of overlapping acquisitions.
It retains separate merits proof, independent court control and original
deadlines and protections for prior transactions.

[Worked case](../../settlement/successive-acquisitions-and-market-choice/01-three-small-acquisitions-and-one-local-market.md)
tests overlapping control, simultaneous commitments, divestment, elapsed time
and double counting. Actual competition effects and paid readiness remain open.
