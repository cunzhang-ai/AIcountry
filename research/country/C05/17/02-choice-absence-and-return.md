# CL07–CL12. Care support supplies no authority over another person

**CL07 — Actual willing care, independent rights.** The recipient's actual
will and lawful child/best-interests arrangements govern the care relationship.
Use supported communication and actual B/UA authority where will is unknown;
leave grants no guardianship, treatment consent, custody, home-entry power or
right to force intimate contact. The carer may decline/stop without losing
ordinary I05, care, residence or political rights. Recipient refusal is not
worker fault. A lawful paid professional assignment retains AS/WL wages and
safeguards; it is not relabeled family leave to reduce its pay/source duties.

**CL08 — Medical recovery is separate from relationship time.** A gestating
person keeps actual TW11 medical absence, clinical support, accommodation and
RP bodily choice whether or not a live child or continuing placement exists.
A second actual carer can use arrival time while that person recovers.
Medical inability is not itself child-arrival caregiving; it does not silently
consume that arrival pool. For the same person/hour identify the actual ground
and due replacement once; retain TW's actual medical clock and proof rather than
resetting it by renaming an ongoing incapacity. Compassionate CL06 needs no
clinical diagnosis. No blanket compulsory post-birth work ban is created here;
actual unsafe-work/clinical protections remain. No claim of treaty compliance
or replacement of stronger lawful maternity/recovery rights follows.

**CL09 — Notice without an employer veto.** Give28calendar days' notice of
foreseeable leave and a usable intended pattern where reasonably possible;
for birth timing, sudden illness, unsafe separation or urgent placement notify
as soon as safely practicable. Protected oral/assisted/paper routes have equal
effect. Late notice for good reason does not extinguish time/pay. Employers
receive necessary dates/pattern and eligibility confirmation, not reproductive,
diagnosis, custody-testimony or private relationship dossiers.

The right arises from actual qualifying facts/source, not employer approval or
a completed benefits award. Employer can propose a safer workable alternative
with reasons; no unilateral deferral for staffing/budget shortage. A genuinely
agreed change cannot waive the floor; contested timing goes directly to existing
independent labor/courts with useful-time interim protection. No prolonged
internal appeal must precede urgent help. Protected first receipt starts the
actual applicable clock; CL creates no longer substitute for earlier duties.

**CL10 — Real absence and proportional use.** A person may choose continuous
or part-time leave, without required on-call work, monitoring an AI, availability
or compulsory learning. Changed patterns use actual notice/agreement or urgent
facts, not a new bank. Normal rest, annual/public leave, sickness and CL are
separate; do not charge the same otherwise scheduled hours to two leave banks
or count ordinary nonworking hours as lost work. A person may suspend CL for
another valid absence with source/period matching; no automatic consumption
because a calendar includes a holiday. Real concurrent different carers can
both need time, unlike one person's replicated claims. Paid CL does not mandate
continuous child contact or ban useful respite, professional care or education.

**CL11 — A usable return and continuing standing.** Return to the actual same
job on no less favorable lawful terms; if genuinely unavailable, an objectively
equivalent role with pay, hours, seniority and earned general progression
preserved. Time in protected leave counts for employment continuity/seniority;
statutory annual leave accrues for the otherwise scheduled protected CL hours
as if ordinarily worked. Actual stronger contractual/other benefits continue.
This new earned-rest liability keeps its employer/source; it is not included
in the direct CL replacement or a reason to charge CL and annual leave together.
No demotion, hidden performance penalty or nonrenewal because of care/claiming.
This creates no permanent job, expired fixed term or immunity from independently
proved genuine WL redundancy/closure with its notice, severance and claims.
Employer bears the ordinary source-law burden of showing claimed unrelated
termination grounds; no AI risk score or absence quota supplies it. Retain
actual reasonable accommodation, voluntary reduced-hours agreements and a fair
return plan. Lost CL eligibility never automatically dismisses the worker.

**CL12 — Cover workers and public mandates retain separate law.** Employers/
service owners pay qualified replacement, recruitment, training, handover, safe
breaks/rest and actual service continuity. Cover staff keep truthful contracts,
WL notice/pay/pension/termination and legitimate renewal rights; an early return
cannot silently cancel their dues. No family or unsafe existing crew is free
cover, nor can shortage force CL refusal. Independent/self-employed care time
needs actual business/income continuity, not a fictitious employer payroll.

Elected offices, judges, ministers and other reserved constitutional functions
retain their actual office/source rules. CL can support a person's qualifying
care/pay within that source but creates no proxy vote, substitute MP, vacancy,
reduced N/n, term extension, appointment shortcut or invented delegation of a
reserved act. Use actual lawful office succession/continuity; absence is no
permission for an AI, inspector or service manager to govern in their place.
