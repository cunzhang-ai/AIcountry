# Before school and after bedtime

*Design385 · Constructed household with [separate primary evidence](../../2026-11-young-carers-and-funded-family-support/sources.md).*

A teenager is expected to provide essential morning and evening
help for an adult relative. The teenager privately asks to stop.
The adult wants to remain at home and accepts a qualified
replacement. The local care authority receives the request on
day 1. Before school on day 2, an early visit must be safely
received. A separate later visit must be received before bedtime.
The school's education duty includes an accepted recovery session
for missed work. The child also wishes to continue occasional
voluntary company that carries no essential task.

The early visit costs 4 units and the later visit costs 3.
Learning recovery costs 2. These costs are separate on the
cash schedule. Assume they can be combined only where the
appropriate budgets or a lawful joint schedule each authorizes
their amounts, and staff are distinct and available at the
specified times. Continuing rent, income, ordinary lessons
and other care are separately funded. Recovery is independently
due and offered on day 3 in this example.

| Path | Actual result in the constructed first window |
|---|---|
| School adjustment only | The school can recognize the barrier and offer recovery, but the essential morning and evening tasks remain uncovered. |
| Seven units and both accepted visits | The adult receives both care acts and the teenager can stop those tasks. The two-unit learning recovery still needs its own source. |
| Nine units and both visits and learning accepted on time | Both care and learning routes are funded, and the teenager may stop essential tasks without shifting them to a sibling. |
| Nine units but the first visit is late | The later visit and learning recovery can still occur. The missed early care and any lost school access remain visible. |
| Adult declines the first provider | A coercive handover is unavailable. Seek a lawful suitable alternative, retain the child's direct help and report the unmet adult task. |

If both visits recur the next day, another seven units and
qualified workers are needed on their own dates. The first
window does not purchase future work. An accepted school
session is not proof of sleep, free time or long-term outcomes.
No result depends on the child proving parental wrongdoing.
