# C06. Financing an ecological transition without hidden costs

*Part of [C06 public finance](../C06-public-finance.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

The Assembly's ecological package needs one cash, debt and physical account.
Treasury and original sector custodians separate public grid and building
capital, private compliance costs, household price changes, industrial and
farm adjustment, worker income and care, local revenue loss, ecological
restoration, imported components, maintenance and decommissioning. An old
plant's remaining book value is not automatically a public debt; a new line's
capital appropriation is not delivered power. Carbon-charge receipts decline
when the covered taxable base shrinks under the actual rate and
exemptions. Under FB56, compare faster and slower abatement within
the same legal limits, a plausible zero-receipt period and the
protections that continue after the charge ends. Keep ordinary repair,
qualifying relief and restoration liabilities on their own terms.
Trace household and worker incidence and supply in both paths.
Use separately authorized recurring sources for standing duties.
Fees, refundable restoration security, penalties and ordinary tax receipts
have different legal uses and due dates.

The Cabinet presents coherent funded alternatives rather than a single
aggregate abatement cost: pollution pricing with timely household relief;
sector standards and regulated investment; public capability or ownership
where justified; and lower-demand changes with usable substitutes. Compare
who actually pays, when alternatives arrive, reliability and land/water
needs, taxpayer risk, firm exit and external harms. The Assembly enacts tax,
spending, guarantees, compensation where required and any public ownership
mandate. Local equalization and transition transfers use published need and
capacity rules, not a minister's reward for political allies. Existing
contracts, accrued wages, pensions, permits and property claims require
lawful prospective treatment and fair review.

No model may count the same worker, grid connection, import currency or
climate credit across several promises. C11 publishes source and method
history; independent audit and opposition can inspect the public-risk and
incidence account, with private details protected where justified. Funding
worker and household support before a price change is different from
announcing a rebate after the bill. The [C09 ecological route](../C09/08-climate-and-ecological-transition.md)
sets the authority and separate environmental floors. Any borrowing stays
within the [C06 debt rule](06-sovereign-debt-and-distress.md); any existing
right or service shortfall stays visible under [C06 continuity](04-shortfall-and-continuity.md).
A promise of future innovation, unverified offset or foreign supplier is
not present capacity. The amount and instrument mix remain unknown until a
real jurisdiction provides records and an authorized public chooses the path.

[AQ clean air](../C09/24-clean-air-and-cumulative-exposure.md) prices
qualified measurement, local corrective means and verified acute receiving
alongside the distinct climate and worker accounts. Keep authorized public
assistance, private duty and recurring service costs separate.

[CBG climate budgets](../C09/27-climate-budgets-and-essential-continuity.md)
joins actual gross headroom to dated firm service and household and
worker protection. Price a delayed line and its paid backup without
using later receipts or forecast removals as current finance.
