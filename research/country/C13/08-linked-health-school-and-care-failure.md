# C13. A linked health, school and care failure

*Part of [C13 lived experience](../C13-lived-experience.md); integrated design 55 authored stress, not an observed event.*

A remote child needs a continuing medicine, accessible school transport and
daily personal support. Their parent also cares for an older relative and
works a fixed shift. The national health purchaser, elected local school
authority and local care authority each issue a valid offer, but the clinic
appointment, bus route and care visit overlap. None can call this effective
access solely because its own booking exists. The family may request a
voluntary joint plan; each authority must still issue its own reasoned
decision and provide a direct appeal. The child's voice and the older
relative's choice are heard separately; the parent is not presumed to be
available as unpaid backup.

Then the care contractor closes, the bus operator stops service and the
clinical network's record system fails. The care authority must obtain an
actual accepting worker or safe place; the school authority must arrange a
reachable place and suitable travel; the clinical network must preserve
treatment and a safe manual record route. A shared coordinator can track
handoffs but cannot decide medical necessity, education exclusion or legal
capacity. The independent safety, [ED education-inspector](../C07/21-public-learning-and-school-power.md)
and care review routes remain open, with direct child aid and ordinary courts. If only one accessible vehicle or interpreter exists, C06 exposes
the collision, and prior lawful scarcity rules govern immediate safety;
the Assembly decides any new material common priority. A model cannot
quietly assign the family to the last available option.

The test records the legal claim, actual cash, qualified worker, medicine,
place, travel and delivered remedy for each person. It distinguishes a
temporary clinically safe substitution from missed treatment, a paper
school place from attended learning, and a paid care shift from a relative's
unrecorded labor. It also asks whether an appeal arrives before loss becomes
irreversible and whether the three purportedly independent reviewers share
one vendor or funding controller. Without real geography, workers, budgets
and affected participants, this authored sequence proves no fallback works.

[The ED two-week exercise](../../settlement/learning-continuity/02-two-learners-and-one-support-slot.md)
separates actual teaching from paid support in the same time window. It also
tests lost instruction after a provider-imposed reduction.
