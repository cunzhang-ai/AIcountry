# AP01–AP04: public action cannot depend on an immaculate file

*Part of [reachable administration](../06-reachable-administration-and-finite-decisions.md); design 140 proposed law.*

**AP01 — Identify the duty before demanding a dossier.** Apply the common
process to an individual administrative determination or complaint that law
requires a competent public office to decide. It is not a new licence for
ordinary lawful life or a right to have every political proposal enacted.
Legislation/delegation/FA consultation, litigation, criminal proof and sector
professional decisions retain their own procedures. Actual source-law special
clocks and stronger protections prevail; an office cannot invent an exception.
State-initiated adverse proceedings keep their own initiation/notice/hearing/
expiry duties without waiting for a person to apply.

**AP02 — Protect a usable first request.** B13/E02 receipt needs enough to
identify a requested public act or problem and a safe way to continue with the
person or authorized representative, not proved eligibility, a complete form,
universal identity credential or legal classification. Help clarify an unclear
request within B16's help period; ordinary advice does not become an application
without the person's understanding. Urgent credible need still gets its earlier
route. A receiver who lacks competence keeps routing until a competent receiver
accepts; the first protected date survives transfer and a failed appointment.

Give a usable reference and preserve the source account, interpreted request,
date and uncertainty through lawful minimal custody. No receipt token is the
exclusive proof of attempted filing: a court can examine testimony and other
records if the office suppresses or loses it. A person can use another lawful
channel without creating a second entitlement or resetting the original claim.

**AP03 — Assign responsibility for each material fact.** Early help identifies
the legal criterion, why a missing fact matters, lawful evidence options, who
can reasonably obtain it and what the office will do. Seek already-held public
records through an actual lawful purpose/access route; no universal cross-agency
merge, coerced consent or new surveillance follows. If access is unlawful or
unsafe, use admissible alternatives or disclose unresolved proof; do not pretend
the record exists. Protect privilege, third-party privacy and source security.

Do not require an inaccessible employer, controlling relative, foreign office,
sole proprietary credential or deceased witness to cooperate before considering
available testimony/alternative proof. This does not establish an unproved fact
or override a substantive lawful evidence standard. Agency allegations retain
their own burden; missing documents or nonresponse alone are not fraud or guilt.

**AP04 — Make proof requests finite and contestable.** Give necessary items
together where reasonably possible, with proportionate help, safe ways to reply
and a reasonable opportunity. Later genuinely material evidence can be sought
with reasons, but never starts a new ordinary clock. Preserve a requester's
objection to relevance, feasibility, cost, meaning or disclosure; a score or
agent summary cannot settle it. A common support worker may help several
services, but their decisions, consent and source permissions stay distinct.

[AP13–AP18](06-essential-service-duty-while-responsibility-is-disputed.md)
adds a funded interim route for designated essential interfaces when
responsibility is disputed. The first date and proof protections continue.
