# Money: actual votes, career operations and a narrow statutory exception

*MC05–MC08; part of [C06/41](../41-money-and-financial-public-control.md).*

**MC05 — Three actual votes, not a Governor's discretion.** A monetary policy
disposition needs three agreeing yes votes and at least three lawful participants
of fixed five. Vacancies, recusal, abstention and on-call availability do not
lower this. Authenticated joined remote participation is permitted under prior
accessible procedures; three incompatible preferences are not a decision.
Elect the chair/Governor by those same votes for two years, nonconsecutive and
never beyond their seat expiry. No casting or extra Governor vote. Without a
chair, the next actually available unconflicted member in the published fixed
seat order administers only. Direct disputed recusal is decided by three
unchallenged lawful members if available; otherwise an ordinary court decides.

Publish eight ordinary meetings in the annual calendar. Any two members may
require an additional meeting within24elapsed hours or earlier useful time;
the records officer issues notice independently of chair permission. Urgent
policy retains the same votes, source and publication, no necessity majority.
No quorum/agreement means no new policy. The last independently valid rate/
facility continues only within its own law/expiry; career duties remain their
own. No court, minister, AI or retired Governor supplies missing policy votes.
This may leave prices/employment/payment stability insufficiently protected.

**MC06 — Named employer and separated case decisions.** Three committee votes
appoint/discipline a protected career Monetary Operations Director through C04
open qualified competition, reasons/reply and direct courts. Director employs/
assigns/disciplines qualified deputies, source/credit/collateral, market,
settlement and records/correction staff under prior merit/conflict rules.
Committee/chair cannot demand a personal signature to suppress an officer's
own enacted case/settlement/referral duty or order a preferred source finding.
A predesignated qualified Deputy has at most180calendar days routine funded
management from the original Director vacancy; no senior appointments, new
policy or acting-title reset. Recruitment opens within14days. Own statutory
functions continue after180days, not missing managerial powers. An express
founding Act may appoint qualified nonmanager staff through Cabinet/C04 only;
it cannot invent a committee, first rate, risk charter or permanent Director.

**MC07 — Deliberately amend the general DR boundary by primary law.** The
competent Act expressly permits MC05 operational decisions on the authority's
own administered policy/eligible-bank reserve and lending rates, and execution
terms of enumerated open-market/settlement/secured-liquidity/owned-FX transactions
inside MC17–MC20's charter. They do not wait for DR30/45 call-in or an Assembly
vote per rate/trade. Routine execution of an already valid decision is assigned
to career operators, not five people signing every trade. Exact decision,
vote/time, source and risk are recorded/published; an AI cannot set the policy.
No arbitrary numeric interest-rate cap is claimed optimal. Negative rates need
explicit primary permission for the authority's own institutional balances;
no compulsory levy on household cash/accounts follows. Courts review legality/
rights, not choose a better rate. Rate decisions can still harm debtors/savers.

**MC08 — No exception for shadow regulation.** MC07 does not cover general bank
capital/liquidity/borrower/conduct rules, licensing or facility eligibility,
new counterparty/collateral classes, risk ceilings, deposit protection, loss
priority, development subsidy, fiscal indemnity, debt/overdraft/guarantee,
exchange controls, compelled conversion or the statutory mandate. Use DR or
primary law as appropriate. Monetary transaction pricing is not permission to
rewrite private contracts, impose a general public charge or disguise a new
market-access criterion. Existing access/posted entitlements remain reviewable.
The source Act/DR fixes criteria and classes before career case decisions;
a policy-rate minute cannot amend them. M13's system rules remain separate.
