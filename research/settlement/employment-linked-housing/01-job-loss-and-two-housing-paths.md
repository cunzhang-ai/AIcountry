# Job loss and two housing paths

This constructed comparison exercises
[RT26–RT31](../../country/C09/18/06-employment-linked-housing.md).
Three workers leave on day 0 and get usable housing notices on day 2.
One household rents an ordinary home from a company linked to the former
employer. Two others occupy genuine on-site dwellings, with independently
established operational need to use the homes for their successors. One
of the on-site households needs an accessible dwelling and a usable
journey to care. The outgoing workers' established wages remain due.
Any work or residence permission is decided separately under MB.

| Response | Ordinary home | On-site homes |
|---|---|---|
| Treat every home as part of employment | Would put the household out when the job ends. | Would allow immediate loss of accommodation. |
| Keep ordinary tenure and let notice alone end service occupancy | Ordinary tenancy continues. | At the end of notice, one household may have no suitable place. |
| Keep ordinary tenure and verify paid receiving | Ordinary tenancy continues under its own law. | The first possible execution is day 32. Authorize only after a court decision and actual suitable receiving. |

The first on-site household has a safe place available by day 32 for 120
units of lawful transition cost. A second advertised place costs 100 but
cannot accommodate the second household's access needs. The only verified
accessible place and journey cost 180 and are available on day 36. These
are separate compatible offers with named providers. A common employer
security has 220 available. Spending 120 for the first household leaves
100. The two suitable transitions need 300, leaving 80 to fund.

The court can allow the first possession on day 32 if the receiving
requirements are met. For the second, the court checks the need for a
funded hold and the local housing authority's accepting route. The
illustration stipulates that 180 includes necessary paid continuity
through day 36. The competent public source can provide 80 on day 31.
With that supplement the second placement can be accepted for day 36.
Without it the receiving gap stays open and there is no automatic
possession. The local authority records the actual interim and requests
elected correction under existing law. Do not count a different
household's home, an unsuitable room or speculative recovery as cover.

In the ordinary home the owner can pursue any genuinely available H
ground with independent review. Leaving a job supplies none. In all
three cases a worker can collect belongings, pursue unpaid wages and
use an independent contact route. The [arithmetic check](verify-employment-linked-housing.py)
checks the two dates, access and common security. Actual affordability,
safe receiving, labour effects and adoption remain open.
