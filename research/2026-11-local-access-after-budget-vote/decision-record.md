# Design492 decision record

**Scope.** Compare a local intake change on the [Design491 baseline](baseline.json)
using the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md). Grant terms,
budgets, access needs and all candidate results are constructed.

**Selection and objections.** Keep the local choice of an accessible
equivalent where the exact grant delegates it, within its cohort,
sources and duties. Recheck readiness separately. A mandatory
unsupported medium can deny practical access despite unchanged
wording and cost. A genuinely funded optional mediated route remains
a rival to the human-only route. An actual scope or instrument change
requires the applicable fresh decisions. Dependency does not justify
national control of methods already left to the council.

**Continuity.** Preserve signed and legally effective commitments,
ordinary accommodations, first receipt and direct review. Hold only
the dependent optional scope under its own law. Keep contested
original meaning and correction with each authorized receiver.
Published access measures do not include private source material.
A new channel does not extend the budget or establish consent.

**Evidence and reopening.** The [comparison](../settlement/local-access-after-budget-vote/01-local-change-and-original-rights.md)
and [sources](sources.md) test conditional authority, staffing and
access. Reopen on actual participation, unsafe use, a changed legal
scope or failed ordinary relief. The next design comparison should
test whether neighboring councils can maintain interoperable proof
and simultaneous challenge without making a national provider the
only usable route. Actual adoption, readiness and outcomes are open.
