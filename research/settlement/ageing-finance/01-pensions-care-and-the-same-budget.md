# Pensions and care draw on the same country

*Design313 · Published Danish projection and separate authored source test.*

## One original projection

The European Commission and Economic Policy Committee's 2024 Ageing
Report provides Danish baseline shares of GDP. The base year is 2022.
Later dates are projections under the report's policy and modelling
assumptions. They are neither the current observed budget nor
AIcountry's future prices.

| Public expenditure, % of GDP | 2022 | 2030 | 2040 | 2050 | 2060 | 2070 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Gross public pensions | 8.3 | 9.3 | 8.8 | 7.8 | 6.9 | 6.8 |
| Health care, excluding health long-term care | 7.4 | 7.2 | 7.4 | 7.6 | 7.7 | 7.8 |
| Long-term care | 3.0 | 3.9 | 4.7 | 5.2 | 5.8 | 6.2 |
| Education | 5.8 | 5.4 | 5.4 | 5.1 | 4.9 | 4.9 |
| Published total | 24.4 | 25.8 | 26.3 | 25.7 | 25.2 | 25.8 |
| Published health and care risk scenario, additional percentage points | — | 0.2 | 0.5 | 0.9 | 1.1 | 1.3 |

Use the published total. Displayed component sums differ by a tenth
in some years due to rounding. The baseline total is highest among
these selected years in 2040. Adding the published risk difference
gives 26.8 in 2040 and 27.1 in 2070 at displayed precision. This
scenario changes health and long-term care assumptions together.
Keep a different longevity or employment sensitivity separate
unless its joint assumptions have been specified and recomputed.

At displayed precision, pension spending in 2070 is 1.5 percentage
points below 2022 and care spending is 3.2 above. The separate
change column in the source reports minus 1.4 and plus 3.3 from
unrounded figures. These are shares of GDP. They do not show
nominal reductions, freed cash, who pays or which shifts are
staffed. Private occupational pension expenditure, separately
projected at 4.0 and 6.3 in 2022 and 2070, stays outside public
pension finance. [2024 Ageing Report, Denmark, printed pp. 256–257](https://economy-finance.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2024-04/ip279_en.pdf)

## A separate budget exercise

Use authored recurring units after all other continuing purposes.
An older period raises protected public pensions from 10 to 11
while qualifying payroll contributions fall from 8 to 7. General
backing therefore rises from 2 to 4. Required public care rises
from 3 to 5. Health, education and all other obligations and
their sources are held fixed in this exercise. The single pool
of recurring general headroom above the original pension and
care costs is 3. No private asset or pension buffer is available
to the care payer.

| Proposed disposition | Money and delivered capacity |
| --- | --- |
| Let each sector plan against the same 3 units | Each 2-unit increment fits alone. The joint 4-unit increase leaves 1 unfunded. |
| Use one unit of debt for the standing gap | It pays at most the first gap if lawful and settled before the duty. The recurring gap remains before any financing cost. |
| Vote one additional recurring unit and use an identified paid local bridge | The 4-unit increase is covered on the annual account. If new cash arrives after the first care due date, separately fund the first payment. |
| Cover both cash windows but use a common qualified worker twice | Money fits. One of the two added care shifts remains uncovered. |
| Vote the recurring source, pay the bridge and accept two distinct shifts | Both duties can be paid and received under these assumptions. Reconcile repayment of the bridge to the received source once. |

In the example, two new care shifts are required. A qualified local
retention agreement covers one. A second must be separately accepted
without withdrawing an already due service. Illustrate a 1-unit
earlier bridge repayable from the delayed revenue, under prior lawful
authority and from a separate usable balance. It creates no new net
recurring source. When the bridge or second shift is unavailable,
report the specific due gap and use competent correction. No overdue
care claim becomes an optional future start. A genuine private-funded
pension alternative still needs the same old accrued payments,
loss and funding transition.

Select the paid, recurring joint correction over using the
pension reserve, family time or emergency debt as a permanent
source. The numbers establish which plans fail in this example.
No actual tax base, revenue, appropriation, pension payment or
care capacity is inferred.
