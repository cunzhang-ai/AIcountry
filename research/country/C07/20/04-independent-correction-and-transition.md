# NC16–NC20. Correct unsafe care without abandoning children

*Part of [NC](../20-early-care-relationships-and-staffing.md); existing public
care, inspection, finance, employment and ordinary court roles remain distinct.*

**NC16 — Use an accountable existing chain.** Assembly law sets material
quality/coverage/source; Treasury funds actual need-adjusted capacity. Elected
local care authorities and C04 career care receivers arrange and accept
delivery, named provider managers employ/control staff, and the existing
independent care/education inspection function examines access and safety.
Publish the actual public entity/appointment/removal/source/control chain.
Inspectors need relevant competence, protected C04 tenure, separate own-case
review and conflict-free assignments; the provider or contract buyer cannot
choose the final reviewer of its own failure. AA/OJ/ordinary courts retain
source scrutiny, independent merits and executed remedies. Actual independent
people/funding are unproved, not supplied by naming these roles.
[ED04–ED05](../21/01-authority-employers-and-review.md) now names this inspection
function's state employer/career head and own-case court route. NC standards,
care scope/180-day quality start remain; ED school site powers do not apply
by analogy to under6 care or grant a universal clinical licence.

**NC17 — Inspect actual conditions proportionately.** Check present staffing,
group/shift/qualification and continuity, child/family experience, actual
excluded/unserved need and safe records. Staff and families have confidential
direct complaints and chosen independent aid without employer/provider veto.
No universal camera, automated child score, public intimate testimony, private-
home search, new data linkage or penal sanction follows from NC. Existing
PD/child-protection/force and court-access law determines distinct powers.
An inspector's model or numerical pass proves no actual child's wellbeing.

**NC18 — Keep safe receiving while correcting failure.** Use existing
inspection/care/court powers for reasoned correction, unsafe intake control,
qualified substitution and funded actual receiving; necessary earlier urgent
protection and useful-time challenge remain. Administrative/contract source
law and B19 executor scope require express coordinated enactment for new NC
orders, not research as present authority. A ratio breach is no automatic
crime or parental fault. Closure without accepted alternative leaves the
public care duty and provider/worker/child claims outstanding. No inspector,
AI or Treasury may cure a source shortage by silently raising ratios,
compelling family labor or pausing the statutory care clock.

**NC19 — Prospective coordinated quality transition.** Proposed new NC group
requirements commence on the exact date stated by a coordinated Act, no
earlier than180 calendar days after it. Fund trained staff, safe room/group
changes, supported child relationships, contract/payroll/record successors
and paid overlap. Existing stronger protection and original due S/TW/rights
dates continue; this notice creates no extension of an earlier enacted duty.
Publish unmet preparation and use existing lawful protective correction if
the date is missed, not automatic source certification or a reset. Do not
force an overnight move or dismiss workers to balance a new group schedule.

**NC20 — Keep disagreement and revision substantive.** Ratios/group sizes
are hypotheses prioritizing attention, relationships and staff conditions,
not empirical proof of best care. Retain flexible mixed groups, competent
childminding, higher-ratio teacher-led arrangements and different public/
plural/common provision as real rivals with costs/control and child interests.
Actual children/families/workers, including nonusers/high-need/sparse places,
have not participated in this project selection. Reopen on original quality/
access/relationship/source evidence, failed staffing or a better alternative.
Next return to the whole country after this bounded material/positive-rule
repair; do not automatically launch another childcare-detail study. Actual
full source/better lives and ultimate goal completion remain unproved.
