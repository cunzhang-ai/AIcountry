# What can lawfully start when only part of a programme is approved

The same hypothetical retrofit has existing cash, housing and care
duties, pending cases, a first election, independent hearing and
continued ordinary service. Treat these as a single protected baseline
with separate actual law. They continue in each option. No foreign
case or budget amount supplies an input. An independent help desk
would improve access for the first budget interval under ordinary law.
Renewing that temporary desk later requires its own source. A new housing-support
scheme pairs an expanded award and a new verification power with
a trained independent remedy and dual-running capacity. The powers
and duties of this proposed scheme are coupled by its adopting law.
Award, review and effective acceptance cannot be silently dropped.

| Dated additional use | Early cash | Cumulative cash | Recurring cash | Early qualified slots |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Protected baseline including electoral handover and accrued case review | 7 | 12 | 6 | 3 |
| Standalone accessible intake under existing law | 1 | 2 | 0 | 1 |
| New award and verification provision | 2 | 3 | 1 | 1 |
| Independent new remedy and ordinary paid overlap | 1 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| Dated limits | 10 | 18 | 8 | 5 |

The cash windows are nested. Eligible purposes and qualified roles
remain separate. Assume the exact slots match in the feasible rows.
A unit of cash cannot replace the reviewer's slot. The old and new
systems have some common administration but the overlap cost is
already included in the last row of work. No early retirement saving
or grant is assumed. Existing case owners and rights retain their
pre-start authority and clocks.

| Exact decision path | Feasible and lawful outcome in this fixture |
|---|---|
| Protected baseline plus independent intake only | 8 early, 14 cumulative, 6 recurring, 4 slots. Can be elected and operated under ordinary law. Expanded award remains proposed. |
| Protected baseline plus whole new scheme | 10 early, 17 cumulative, 8 recurring, 5 slots. Can start if each vote, source and receiver is effective by the date. |
| Both additions at once | 11 early, 19 cumulative, 8 recurring, 6 slots. The cash and slot limits fail. |
| Scheme law receives initial assent, independent appropriation is removed before final adoption | The cheaper part fits cash but cannot start as an authorized group. Return the exact change. Standalone intake remains a separate vote. |
| Exact scheme is already enacted and due, then the independent receiver fails | Keep the duty and original due date, do not use any unsupported verification power, provide independently lawful interim aid and expose missing remedy. Seek elected correction and useful-time relief. |

In this comparison choose the baseline and standalone intake while
the remedy appropriation or receiver is unqualified. This preserves
a useful improvement but leaves the additional award unmade.
If the coupled proposal is later qualified, the elected choice may
prefer it, cost a later standalone start or retain the simpler model.
Another real rival is a publicly delivered expanded award that uses
ordinary verification and existing independent review, if its actual
coverage and workload fit. Assess the cost of waiting to people
without current aid. A funded old route can still be a poor or unequal
route. Actual cost, qualification, access and outcomes remain open.
