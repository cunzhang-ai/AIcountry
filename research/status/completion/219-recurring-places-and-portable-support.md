# Recurring places and portable support

Design311 · Scoped result for [country completion](../../DESIGN-COMPLETENESS.md)

[XR11–XR15](../../country/C04/09/03-recurring-places-and-portable-support.md)
keeps a responsible authority for each active service, a repeating
schedule and usable changes, with minimum location disclosure.

The [household comparison](../../settlement/cross-local-continuity/03-recurring-places-and-portable-support.md)
separates paid cancellation, actual support and a journey.
It tests gross costs, an inadequate reserve and a worker assigned
to another protected duty. The assumptions are authored. Qualified
receiving, lawful finance and actual comparative outcomes remain open.
