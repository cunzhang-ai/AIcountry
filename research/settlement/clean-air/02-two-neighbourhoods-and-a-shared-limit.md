# Two neighbourhoods and a shared limit

*Authored counterfactual. All concentrations, contributions, costs and
effects below are synthetic units. The additive table holds one
pollutant, one long-term measurement period and one set of people fixed.
It is a reasoning check, not a dispersion or clinical model.*

## Keep each receiving place visible

Assume two neighbourhoods with equal exposed populations. A fictional
long-term ambient cap is 10. Background contributes four at each
location. All current facilities hold valid permits and comply
with their individual limits. Source contributions are stipulated
for this example rather than attributed from the ambient reading.

| Place | Background | Industry | Freight | Heating | Combined baseline |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| North | 4 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 8 |
| River | 4 | 3 | 4 | 2 | 13 |

North meets the cap. River exceeds it by three. The authority
owes a local response even if no emitter is found individually
in breach. A mean of 10.5 does not identify the receiving place,
and an operator's permit compliance does not decide a damages case.

## Compare controls on the same cash and service scope

The available incremental budget is four one-time and two annual
units. Previous lawful payments and protected services continue.
The table assumes the stated works, heating and freight services
are available. Verify access, worker impacts and contracts before
any real commitment.

| Option | One-time / annual units | North | River | Implication at the stated central estimate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Uniform reduction of 25% in the controllable contributions at each place | 3 / 2 | 7 | 10.75 | The regional mean falls to 8.875, while River remains above 10. |
| Reroute freight without other control | 2 / 1 | 10.5 | 10 | The regional mean falls to 10.25. North becomes a new exceedance. |
| Clean freight and heating measures without the harmful diversion | 4 / 2 | 6.5 | 9.5 | Each place meets the cap at its central estimate. The bounds and observed conditions still need review. |

The uniform option scales industry, freight and heating by 0.75 and
holds background constant. The reroute raises North's freight term
to 3.5 and reduces River's to one. The third option keeps industry
fixed, lowers North and River freight to 0.5 and 1.5 respectively,
and lowers heating to one at each place. Its annual envelope
includes one unit of household transition support. The households
must actually receive safe and affordable heat before old service is
lost. A promised vehicle or connection does not satisfy that duty.

Select the third path for funded action and measurement in this
case. It has the strongest stated distribution under the budget.
If River's total can be 0.8 higher than the central projection,
its upper scenario is 10.3. That evidence does not qualify a claim
of resolved exposure. Continue the existing protection, verify the
actual observations and reserve a competent decision about further
control if needed. The reroute fails the locality protection test
even though its regional average improves.

## Keep a separate short-term response

Now assume an acute smoke episode. Its alert and response use a
different averaging period and actual exposure evidence. The annual
table cannot determine a safe short-term condition. Public health
may need usable clean indoor space, transport, communication and
care for people who cannot safely travel, while housing officers
protect against heat and the power operator protects the necessary
supply. Count staff, routes and power already promised to other
duties. Any authorized distinction in the compliance account leaves
the measured event and unmet protection visible.
