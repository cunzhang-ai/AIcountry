# C12. Finite public attention and lawful closure

*Integrated design 83 · Proposed FA01–FA12; not an adopted project procedure.*

Unlimited genuinely different arguments can exceed public attention even when
copies are perfectly grouped. Promise a finite, challengeable examination and
honest uncertainty, not that every possible worthwhile argument has been read.
This reference covers major ordinary national policy consultations: a proposed
law or delegated material policy change with national affected scope. Mandatory
sector hearings, petitions, constitutional votes and individual legal cases keep
their own procedures. Local forums may choose lawful alternatives within their
scope; none automatically merges into a global platform or edition.

1. [Scope, access and bounded intake](03/01-scope-access-and-receipt.md): FA01–FA03.
2. [A substantive attention portfolio](03/02-selection-and-deliberation.md): FA04–FA06.
3. [Control of mediation and lawful closure](03/03-mediation-and-closure.md): FA07–FA09.
4. [Correction, migration and rivals](03/04-correction-and-migration.md): FA10–FA12.

The 30-slot/90-day reference is an authored institutional hypothesis, not an
empirically validated optimum or a requirement that every informal conversation
use this form. General legislation can replace it prospectively after public
comparison, rights/equality review and actual funding. A commissioner cannot
rewrite the rules mid-case to avoid an unwelcome argument. Substantive legality
and elected decision remain distinct from completion of consultation.

[Capacity](../C06/32-democratic-repair-and-public-attention-capacity.md) and

expose source, mediator and staffing failures. This selection resolves a proposed
finite-closure choice; it proves neither public consent nor practical inclusion.
