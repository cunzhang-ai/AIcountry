# A medical supply agreement with two different clocks

This is an authored case. The existing agreement recognizes particular
medical import certificates and has funded commitments already made.
It rolls into another year on day 120 unless a valid non-renewal
notice is received by day 60. Pending duties and disputes survive
to their applicable extent. An earlier authorization establishes a
specific protective objection route for proposed joint-body changes.
There is no assumption about these terms in any actual treaty.

| Day | Event | Decision and consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Complete separate non-renewal proposal and continuity account received | Ordinary scrutiny can conclude from day 30 if real hearing and vote occur. Retain and repair remains a live option. |
| 25 | A joint body circulates a proposed annex with a material fee. The agreement's objection deadline is day 45. | A fee exceeds routine scope. The Cabinet's named officer uses the previously authorized objection and verifies receipt on day 40. Ordinary material approval could not be given before day 55 even if the text were complete on day 25. |
| 40 | A materially revised successor agreement is received complete | Earliest ordinary vote is day 70. Day 47 is the earliest expedited vote if there is separately approved urgency, safe access, an actual hearing and a substantive vote. |
| 50 and 55 | Separate exit vote, then valid notice received | With actual votes and lawful protection in place, day 55 meets the day 60 deadline. The treaty ends on day 120 under the assumed instrument. A new agreement has its own authority and effective conditions. |
| 60 | No notice received under the other path | The old agreement's renewal terms control. A later domestic vote does not reset day 60 or erase the inherited external term. Seek lawful revised terms if desired. |

For this exercise use 120 statutory seats, including two vacancies.
Ordinary material approval needs 61 actual yes votes and members
present. Shortening the period needs 80 actual yes votes and members
present. An urgency motion with 79 yes votes on day 47 fails even
with the vacancies. The ordinary path for the new text stays at day 70
at the earliest. The separately disclosed old-agreement exit decision
can still use its own date and lawful vote.

Assume the proposed successor would allow provisional implementation of
a new fee. Signature would trigger it only if the actual instrument
made that the agreed act. Before that material provisional step, the
Assembly would have to approve its exact scope and funded end route.
A temporary label or a new vendor contract cannot take that decision.

For a lawful exit, the ordinary clinical duty still needs usable
supplies on day 120. Assume a lawful alternative import route,
qualified receiving and the following separately confirmed uses.

| Due | Required work | Spendable units |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Day 90 | Protected records and claims handover | 1 |
| Day 100 | Qualified inspection and acceptance | 2 |
| Day 110 | Dated foreign-currency payment and first substitute stock | 3 |
| Total | Initial bridge only | 6 |

The exercise assumes continuing later supply is separately priced
and owned where the full bridge is considered. An annual promise
of six units with only five usable by the respective deadlines
leaves an actual gap. Suppose the missing unit is day 110 currency.
The order or new text cannot supply it. Record the uncovered route,
use existing lawful alternatives and compare retention, a negotiated
extension or a genuinely funded source while there is still time.
If exit is already effective, the clinical and claim owners still
owe their lawful duties. Residents need actual medicines and usable
remedy dates. A verified external notice and a safe domestic service
handover are two different outcomes.
