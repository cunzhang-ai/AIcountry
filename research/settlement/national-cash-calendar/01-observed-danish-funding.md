# Compare fiscal flows, borrowing and account balances in 2025

The [existing Denmark 2025 baseline](../observed-government-baseline/01-accounts-transfers-and-work.md)
retains DKK 90.924 billion of consolidated general-government net
lending and the state accounts' DKK 13.0 billion annual net cash
outflow. Those are already separate perimeters. The Nationalbank's
annual report adds actual central-government borrowing and balance
sheet context. In Appendix A1, domestic and foreign debt together
were DKK 586.705 billion at year end, while its reported measure
of central-government debt was DKK 235.437 billion after specified
adjustments and assets. Gross borrowing exposure and reported
debt after those adjustments both matter. [Annual debt report,
chapter 2 and Appendix A1](https://www.nationalbanken.dk/media/q5cf5y2l/central-government-borrowing-and-debt-2025.pdf)

## Keep the reported funding variants

The report narrative gives DKK 135 billion of total funding
requirement. Appendix A2 reports DKK 152.0 billion as its funding
requirement. The displayed domestic subtotal of 121.6 plus 10.5
foreign long-term repayments and 2.5 commercial paper amortization
gives 134.6, consistent with rounding to 135. Adding the displayed
net cash need and detailed domestic and foreign rows gives 152.1,
close to the appendix total at displayed precision. The appendix
notes perimeter and accounting differences. This reconstruction
shows how the published figures can arise arithmetically. It does
not identify the full definitional or transaction bridge between
them. Keep the discrepancy open. Neither figure is a discretionary
spending ceiling. [Annual debt report, page 6 and Appendix A2](https://www.nationalbanken.dk/media/q5cf5y2l/central-government-borrowing-and-debt-2025.pdf)

The balance fell by DKK 50.238494 billion from end-2024 to end-2025.
The December release's corrected table reports 28.4 for December
net financing need and 12.9 for the year. Its prose retains a
different figure. Use the corrected table for that release and
keep the annual report's 13.0 separately. The corrected annual
domestic gross financing need of 133.6 less domestic gross
borrowing of 84.2 gives 49.4 in the liquidity table. This is
the government's effect on banks' net position in that table.
It is a different measure from the 50.238494 drop in the state
account, and a complete bridge is outside the inspected scope.
The account includes deposits of government funds. These are
selected month ends, not a daily minimum, an appropriation or
proof a particular payer held unrestricted cash.
