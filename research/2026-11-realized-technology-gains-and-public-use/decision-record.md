# Design418. Make public uses of technology gains visible and contestable

## Decision and baseline

Design417 has enforceable public-investment terms, worker bargaining,
separate public receipts and ordinary fiscal controls. AT15 sends
settled returns through the budget. It leaves open how a voter can
distinguish a genuine gain from relabelled spending and compare a
one-off household payment with actual service access. This decision
defines a disposition rule for returns already owned by public bodies.
It was prepared with AI assistance. Actual authority remains with the
competent elected bodies under their own budgets.

## Alternatives and working selection

Ordinary budget treatment without a dedicated comparison is simple
and avoids earmarking by revenue label. It can hide the group that
receives the gain or a transfer from ordinary provision to a volatile
source. A standing equal dividend gives households choice and avoids
merit tests when lawful. It can be partially offset by other rules,
exclude some people and make volatile earnings politically hard to
save. A long-horizon common portfolio can share returns across cohorts.
It requires actual assets, qualified and replaceable governance, risk
controls, liquidity and an enforceable draw mandate. A special purpose
can also entrench obsolete spending.

Choose an open recorded appropriation on a reconciled baseline, with
a finite-use rule for unusual receipts and a separate law for a
permanent payout or investment pool. This leaves all feasible uses
contestable and protects the ordinary floor and earnings rights.
Require a plain account of what old resources are displaced or freed
and who receives the net effect. The worked case selects the access
repair over an equal payout because an established access defect
has a fully funded remedy. That result depends on the stated purpose,
service fit and continuing capacity. Direct cash remains a real
alternative when control over spending or acute material needs matter
more. This rule changes neither ordinary revenue sources nor ownership
of private returns.

## Sources, objections and migration

[Public comparators](sources.md) show distinct constitutional and
statutory approaches to return, principal and legislative appropriation.
They concern petroleum-derived public wealth. They supply no assumed
AI proceeds, national fund design, effect estimate or return target.
No residents, workers or independent reviewer participated. A financial
comparison without actual nonusers, accessibility and workers can miss
who bears the change. Protect participation and independent audit within
ordinary privacy and remit, and disclose missing evidence.

Apply prospectively to the identified public technology returns. Keep
original title, worker and creditor priority, valid contracts, restricted
principal, ordinary tax and benefit law, and existing C06 authority.
Treasury and local lawful payers keep distinct account responsibilities.
The public asset owner verifies settlement. Elected opposition can
inspect the baseline and test displaced funding through ordinary
parliamentary powers. The existing external auditor tests source and
substitution. Courts and ordinary reviewers retain their independent
claims jurisdiction and remedies. Budget owners name and fund successors
before changing a programme or payment route. No research record creates
a fund or authority to pay a dividend.

## Scoped result and next test

Documentary comparators and a constructed budget qualify a candidate
allocation rule. Independent review, real balances, adoption, delivery
and improvement in actual lives remain open.

For the first lawful candidate receipt, test two successive budgets
from settled account through voted use and verified household or
service receipt. The existing fiscal office and independent auditor
would trace principal, reserves, late claims, benefit offsets,
appropriation substitution and the next period's staffed obligation.
Completion requires an account of residual liabilities, people
left out and actual net benefit or failure. No actual receipt or test
budget has been established. Reopen if repeated fiscal substitution,
failed uptake, unstable returns or higher closure costs change the
choice. A sustainable recurring source may justify a different rule.
