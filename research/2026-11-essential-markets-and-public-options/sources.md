# Design498 evidence and limits

- The [UK Competition and Markets Authority's 2022 final report](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/childrens-social-care-market-study-final-report/final-report)
  documents difficulty finding suitable placements in England, Scotland and Wales.
  Its assessment addresses risk of unexpected exit by some heavily indebted
  larger providers and related concerns about staffing, commissioning and
  placement supply. The findings concern those children's care markets.
  They do not identify an optimal ownership form for adult care or
  measure the results of an AIcountry remedy.
- The [FTC's 2025 final Welsh Carson consent order announcement](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/05/ftc-approves-final-order-welsh-carson)
  describes future notification and limits on involvement under that order.
  It records allegations and a settlement, rather than an adjudicated
  finding on the legal merits. The example supplies no candidate
  filing threshold or real provider capacity.
- The [successive acquisitions case](../settlement/successive-acquisitions-and-market-choice/01-three-small-acquisitions-and-one-local-market.md),
  [provider exit case](../settlement/repeated-provider-exit/01-two-exits-and-an-ordinary-service-choice.md)
  and [equalization comparison](../settlement/equalization-and-local-service/01-balanced-total-and-local-deadlines.md)
  are constructed decisions. Keep their separate dates, controllers
  and resource perimeters. The joined motion's units are authored.
