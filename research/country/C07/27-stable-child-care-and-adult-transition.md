# C07. Stable child care and the transition to adulthood

*Design234 · YC01–YC12 · [C07 social floor](../C07-social-floor.md) · Proposed research law.*

Give each child in public care a safe, actually staffed place, a
practical voice and stable relationships. Make the placing authority
responsible through moves and offer voluntary support into adulthood.
[B31–B32](../C08/16/04-child-protection-and-stable-care.md) continues
to govern removal, court review, stable family care and adoption.

| Part | Governing rule |
|---|---|
| [YC01–YC04. Safe and stable care](27/01-receiving-and-relationships.md) | Separate the court's authority from the service's delivery and keep actual care, contact and independent challenge available. |
| [YC05–YC08. Capacity and public responsibility](27/02-places-funds-and-control.md) | Qualify whole placement paths, fund willing carers and paid backup, and resolve local payment separately from receiving. |
| [YC09–YC12. Adulthood and succession](27/03-voluntary-transition-and-succession.md) | Preserve adult agency, offer an accepted transition and keep actual claims and funded successors through implementation. |
