# Decision record: serious illness, death and continuing duties

5 October 2026 · Integrated design 52 research selection; not enacted law

## Baseline, bounded decision and participation

The [design-51 predecessor](predecessor-design-51/README.md) preserves the
prior country. C07 lists palliative care but does not name a delivery or
decision chain for relief near death. C08 mentions death registration and
estates without linking them to advance choices, body care or a dependant's
immediate support. C06 contains no distinct capacity account for those
handovers. The question is how the country protects a person's present
will and relief while keeping clinical judgment, legal death, investigation,
ritual, inheritance and survivors under their own accountable decisions.

This is qualified project analysis, not participation by patients,
children, disabled people, carers, clinicians, bereaved people, faith and
nonreligious groups or people who work in body care. No AIcountry public
has authorized a law. The values are bodily agency, relief, preservation
of life, truth about death, culture, equality, privacy, care for dependants
and limits on state and family power. The moral dispute over assisted dying
is not a missing medical parameter and cannot be settled by a model score.

## External function check and rival diagnoses

The [UN Classification of the Functions of Government](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/cofog/revision)
has ten broad expenditure divisions: general public services, defense,
public order, economic affairs, environmental protection, housing and
community amenities, health, recreation/culture/religion, education and
social protection. Existing C01–C14 chapters address these broad areas,
but a broad expenditure mapping is not proof that a person can complete a
life event or enforce a right. The UN's [survivors class](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Structure/Detail/en/4/10_3_0)
specifically names pensions, death grants and funeral-related support.
The [ten-division crosswalk](function-crosswalk.md), together with a journey from illness through death to a
dependant's next payment, exposed an omitted cross-system handoff. COFOG
does not dictate AIcountry entitlements, moral choices or agency design.

Unrelieved suffering can arise from a legal limit on medicines, no staffed
home service, a finance incentive, a provider's reluctance, inaccessible
communication or a family controlling the record. A missing survivor
payment can instead arise from death verification, legal status, registry,
banking or care gaps. Original patient and family accounts, clinical and
pharmacy records, rejected referrals, civil case timelines, vital-record
corrections and benefit receipt would discriminate these mechanisms.
No local observations exist.

| Whole package | Possible benefit | Serious objection or failure |
|---|---|---|
| Retain a generic health entitlement and ordinary family/estate law | Leaves diverse clinical and cultural choices open | No one must deliver relief, check a disputed advance refusal or sustain a dependant during a record gap |
| Hospital-centered life-prolonging default | May avoid premature death from prognostic error and keep specialist treatment nearby | Can impose unwanted intervention, isolate people, neglect home relief and mistake resource intensity for respect |
| Family, charity and private hospice lead | Can adapt to relationships and local belief with plural providers | Income, residence, family control and uncompensated labor decide access; deaths and claims may remain invisible |
| **Selected: public relief and supported choice with separate death and survivor chains** | Links accountable clinical access, advance choice, verification, dignified body care and interim protection while keeping distinct powers reviewable | Requires difficult medicine regulation, after-hours staff, independent review, records and fair resource allocation |
| Broad assisted-dying entitlement or general duty to preserve life regardless of refusal | Each gives priority to a different moral view about control and protection | Neither follows automatically from palliative-care evidence; both can create grave coercion, abandonment or unwanted treatment without a legitimate public choice |

The selected [C07 path](../country/C07/10-serious-illness-and-end-of-life-care.md)
assigns relief and clinical responsibility; [C08](../country/C08/08-end-of-life-decisions-and-survivors.md)
governs advance choice, death records, rites, estates and dependants;
[C06](../country/C06/14-end-of-life-and-survivor-finance.md) counts capacity;
[C13](../country/C13/17-serious-illness-death-and-survivor-gap.md) tests a
person's full journey. It recognizes treatment refusal and proportionate
symptom relief but creates no new assisted-dying power. Any such scheme
requires a separate prospective democratic decision and lawful transition.
The opposite positions retain their objections; they are not averaged away.

## Primary sources and limits

[WHO's palliative-care guide for primary care](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/integrating-palliative-care-and-symptom-relief-into-primary-health-care)
supports service integration and continuity, while WHO's [palliative-care
account](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/palliative-care)
identifies patient and family needs beyond symptoms. Its [morphine access
report](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240075269) addresses
safe availability of an essential symptom medicine. The [Convention on the
Rights of Persons with Disabilities, Article 12](https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/Ch_IV_15.pdf)
is a primary rights comparator for legal capacity, support and safeguards;
actual treaty applicability requires a real state and inventory. WHO's
[cause-of-death guidance](https://www.who.int/standards/classifications/classification-of-diseases/cause-of-death)
separates medical certification from the civil registration and statistical
chain. Its [transplantation guidance](https://www.who.int/health-topics/transplantation/)
is a comparator for distinct donation rules. These sources neither enact
AIcountry law nor prove this package's operation or superiority. They do
not resolve assisted dying, contested treatment, cultural rites or how to
rank two people when medicine or staff is scarce.

## Authority, objections and transition

The Assembly defines the health package, controlled-medicine law,
advance-choice effects, clinical and death-verification standards, donor
consent, registration, local body-care floor, survivor benefits and funding.
The C07 purchaser and clinical networks deliver treatment and relief;
independent professional judgment applies case-specific care. C08 courts
correct legal errors and the civil-status service records death, while
independent investigation handles suspected unlawful death. C06 reports
scarcity to elected authority. C11 preserves source and version histories.
No hospital, relative, insurer, funeral firm, political minister or AI
inherits all of these powers from a single record. Test appointment,
funding, information and execution under a hostile minister, absent
specialist and corrupted portal before calling the remedy real.

The reference faces serious objections. A broad right to refuse care may
be misused by a coercive family or by a system that offers too little
support; a state that overrides refusal for protection can compel grave
bodily intrusion. Controlled-medicine safeguards can prevent diversion
but also block relief. A physician's prognosis can be wrong; a second
opinion cannot conjure an unavailable specialist. A public body-care floor
may suppress minority rites if local capacity and plural rules are weak.
The no-new-assisted-dying selection leaves some people's claims of
unbearable suffering unresolved; a future permission would face risks
of pressure, unequal care and irreversible error. These are live moral
and operational disputes, not defects erased by an aggregate outcome.

An adopter must inventory current consent, disability, emergency,
controlled-medicine, death-investigation, registry, donation, body-care,
inheritance, survivor-benefit, housing, child-care and privacy law. Existing
advance directions, valid treatment wishes, prescriptions, medical records,
living patients, death certificates, wills, donor records, investigations,
estate claims and due benefits continue under valid law until an authorized,
funded successor takes them. No new clinical withdrawal, death finding,
investigative power or burial restriction commences by publishing research.
Claims about readiness require actual trained staff, medicine, pharmacy
stock, mobile care, certifiers, private records, courts, safe sites and
interim payments, including a network-outage path.

## Result and bounded next test

Evidence qualification: international guidance and a project-authored
crosswalk, not original AIcountry observations or independent review.
Research selection: a separate, integrated end-of-life and survivor route.
Legal authorization: absent. Operational readiness: absent. Observed
relief, respected choices, accurate deaths and survivor outcomes: absent.

The next bounded test in a real jurisdiction is one witnessed journey from
serious illness to the dependant's first payment, including an outdated
advance record, rural pharmacy failure, disputed death and minority rite.
Collect original accounts and current law, identify the exact responsible
actor and capacity at each handoff, and compare the selected public route
with hospital-led and family/market alternatives. The responsible public
authority must decide any binding change. Reopen if a person's will is
lost, relief is unreachable, deaths are misrecorded, a survivor's claim
cannot be paid, or a democratically chosen rival better meets the values.
