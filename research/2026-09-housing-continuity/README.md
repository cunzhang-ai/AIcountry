# Safety without losing a home or livelihood

Drafted 24 September; checked 25 September 2026 · Proposed research continuation · Not enacted

This study compares seven days of temporary accommodation, voluntary remaining at
home with lawful protection, and a move into longer-term housing. It does not presume
that a person exposed to violence should leave their home or that remaining is safe.

The main correction is to evaluate **usable choices**, not a referral or a low price:
actual control of money, lawful occupancy, enforceable protection, children's continuity,
accessible accommodation, and a funded next step all matter separately.

- [Candidate provisions](continuity-candidate.md) connect protection, housing, benefits,
  employment and family obligations without conflating their legal decisions.
- [Matched comparison and cash ledger](seven-day-comparison.md) show why a sufficient
  weekly envelope can still leave help unavailable on the first two days.
- [Decision record and sources](decision-record.md) preserve objections, affected
  clauses, source limits and the next consequential question.
- [Inputs](ledger-inputs.json), [calculation](cash_check.py), [results](ledger-results.json),
  [source manifest](source-manifest.json) and [validation](validation.json).

The earlier [overnight-access candidate](../2026-09-safe-access/README.md) is preserved.
No actual placement, independent assessment, lawful order or public funding is asserted.
