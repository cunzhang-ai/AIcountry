The missing chain matters because a safe dwelling and a reachable daily journey
depend on land, water, grid, builders, maintenance, vehicles, workers and cash,
as well as price, location, tenure and nondiscrimination. Whether physical
shortage or unequal access dominates in an actual place is unknown. The value
choice is to secure a usable national floor without making every household's
location or transport mode a central state decision. No observed household,
firm, locality or asset establishes that this choice is superior.

## Alternatives, selection and common constraints

| Model | Benefit | Failure or unresolved value choice |
|---|---|---|
| Retain design 10's plans, markets and targeted assistance | Avoids adding another authority and may be enough where existing providers already deliver. | No named commissioner, maintenance payer or receiving operator secures a safe home and journey through shortage or closure. |
| Predominantly direct national housing and passenger service | Could visibly pool public capacity across places. | Concentrates tenancy, employment, travel and capital power nationally; transition, acquisition and maintenance costs are large and cannot be wished away. |
| Regulated plural builders/operators without public residual capacity | Preserves diverse investment and local adaptation. | Contracts and cash support may fail in thin markets; insolvency does not produce another home, vehicle or driver. |
| **Selected conditional local responsibility, plural supply and public residual capacity** | Elected local authorities commission housing and accessible mobility under a national floor, with diverse providers and a funded lawful fallback. | Local exclusion, patronage ownership, insufficient transfers and a fallback sharing the failed provider's controller could defeat it. |

The Assembly would define the floor, funding, competence and rights route. Local
elected bodies would name separate housing-capacity and mobility authorities,
organize land and network choices, and fund or commission real capacity. C04's
interlocal preparation and Assembly-reserved vote would settle material unresolved
cross-boundary allocation. A public operator is neither automatically safer nor
automatically independent: safety inspection, tenancy/rider remedies, appointments,
funding, source records and execution must work against public as well as private
controllers. The mobility floor concerns reachable essential destinations across
walking, wheeling and passenger modes; it is not a claim to unlimited preferred
travel. Housing protects safe interim accommodation and a lawful path to secure
access without taking property or rewriting tenancy by administrative shortcut.

C06 must account separately for capital, operating support, repair, asset renewal,
debt, public guarantees, rents, fares, affordability support and national/local
equalization. C09 must check serviced land, safe water, grid, hazard and ecology
before approving the whole package. A builder, driver, parcel, power connection
or external-currency balance cannot serve two simultaneous promises. Low rent
or fare cannot be funded by deferred decay. If capacity cannot meet lawful
claims, authorities must disclose the shortfall, provide lawful interim help
with available resources and return a general allocation or funding choice to
elected institutions; a court cannot manufacture assets.

The authored stress case loses safe homes to flooding while a major passenger
operator fails and a replacement site's grid connection is delayed. Housing,
mobility, utility, care and Treasury actors have separate duties; the Assembly
decides a material cross-local distribution after affected people can challenge
alternatives. The hypothesis fails if the receiving fleet or stock is merely
paper, if it shares a failed controller, if an interim dwelling severs needed
journeys, or if no funded executor can comply with a rights order. This is a
logic test, not an exercise with observed residents or equipment.

## Support, objections, migration and status

Primary comparative material from [UN-Habitat](https://unhabitat.org/metropolitan-planning-and-finance-for-adequate-housing-experts-group-meeting-report)
links housing to metropolitan land, transport, services and finance. The
[OECD housing-governance review](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/bricks-taxes-and-spending_7a22f9a6-en/full-report/component-3.html)
describes national and subnational roles. The
[International Transport Forum's funding review](https://itf-oecd.org/future-public-transport-funding)
distinguishes fares, public budgets and beneficiaries of improved access, and
its [accessibility review](https://itf-oecd.org/delivering-accessibility)
emphasizes operational maintenance and usable access. These institutional
analyses frame questions; they do not observe AIcountry, certify this mixture or
provide a local cost estimate. Original observations, shared-source tracing,
new events and opposing accounts require fresh review before legal adoption.

Serious objections remain: a local majority can exclude newcomers or displace
lower-income residents; a national override can disenfranchise a locality;
tenure protection can interact with future supply; private operators can select
profitable routes and residents; public operators can reward allies; rapid
building can impose water, climate, heritage and ecological losses. Owners,
tenants, builders, transport workers, riders, disabled people, carers, adjacent
communities and future residents have noninterchangeable stakes. They have not
participated. The design records conflicts rather than treating a combined
accessibility score as consent.

Affected consumers include C04/C06–C07/C09/C13–C14, the social/fiscal and
territorial choices, housing-continuity and resource-access studies, existing
property and tenancy rights, transport concessions, safety law and the C10
disaster route. No prior review transfers automatically. A lawful transition
would inventory titles, tenancies, deposits, debt, buildings, repair liabilities,
routes, vehicles, depots, workers, contracts, grid and water connections,
ecological claims and pending cases. It would name receiving authorities,
compensation and review, fund operating overlap and protect sensitive records
before ending an old provider. Existing lawful safety, service, property and
ecological duties continue during an interlocal deadlock; delay grants neither
a permit nor permission to abandon residents.

**Evidence qualification:** primary institutional comparisons and same-controller
desk logic only. **Research selection:** conditional elected-local responsibility
with plural provision and demonstrable public residual capacity enters design 11.
**Legal authorization:** none. **Operational readiness:** unknown for land,
homes, routes, staff, cash, safety review and receiving capacity. **Observed
outcomes:** none. No publication or research-index update adopts a rule.

The next bounded test in a lawful interested jurisdiction is an original joined
stock/route/rights/cash map for one connected housing-and-travel area and a
witnessed building-loss plus operator-exit exercise, compared with the direct-public
and regulated-plural alternatives. The adopting public authority, not this project,
must set a feasible budget, obtain affected-party participation and decide.
Completion would require actual stock and travel observations, legal obligations,
subannual finance, workers, assets, ecological constraints, an independent remedy
and delivery to the claimant. With no such jurisdiction or budget now, retain
the choice as conditional. Reopen it if capture, displacement, unusable access
or inability to staff the fallback is shown; elapsed time cannot supply consent.
