# C08. Conflict detention, independent review and actual release

15 October 2026 · Design 77 · Proposed CD01–CD16, not enacted custody authority

Select a narrow conflict-custody statute: actual international-conflict POW
and identified neutrality grounds, independent civilian liberty review and
humane treatment everywhere this country controls restraint. No general
involuntary civilian security internment or special non-international-conflict
internment power is created. Ordinary criminal, imminent-defense and clinical/
child protections keep their specific grounds. No status label establishes guilt.

narrower powers. These choices are research values, not complete treaty
implementation, actual detention orders or a claim of superior war outcomes.

Proposed first review 24 hours, full status decision seven days, adult renewal
30 days/child seven, and limited disciplinary restriction seven days need
qualified legal and operational review. POW criminal pretrial custody uses
the shortest P limit, 90 actual days and three calendar months; do not confuse
it with independently justified status custody or treat either as a blank check.
Actual jurisdiction, treaties/custom, trained independent staff, safe places,
foreign consent and all costs remain unverified. [TS/TD](22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md)
now supplies technical candidates; next audit the whole ordinary-law/resource/
public-control settlement rather than automatically extend conflict research.
