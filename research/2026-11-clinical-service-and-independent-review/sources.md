## Our selection and counterargument

HG01–HG20 is our reasoned provisional institutional choice: state NCS integrated
public employer/service, qualified C04 heads, internal purchasing/access,
protected clinical judgment and outside safety/coverage review with actual
ordinary court correction. Integrated care is **not** treated as proof that
legal/employer integration is necessary. A separate purchaser/autonomous provider,
regional/user/worker common, insurance-led plural or strong existing repair can
be better in context. Political/labor/professional concentration, weaker choice,
scarce competence and extra review/time costs remain material objections.

Needs-based public budgets without routine internal sale contracts, targeted
protected case/quality inspection, actual outside Reviewer/court roles and
180-day acting/12-month method numbers are authored hypotheses. No source proves
them optimal or supplies actual authority/people/budget. Country rights/source
law remains actual until competent prospective revision. This is not public
health surveillance/HP coercion, social-care/ED takeover, TD market/licence or
TS09 serious-inquiry/punitive integration. S/HN actual covered care/consent/
receiving/foreign/journey dates and all stronger source protections persist.

Sixteen cases are written institutional arguments, not patients, dispatch/
diagnostic software or safety trials. AI uses remain actual HC/DP/TD-scoped
support; no measured public saving/health gain is assumed. All actual public/
private/independent-care staff, qualified review/interpretation, materials/
capital/maintenance/FX/journeys/legacy/transition cost/source and readiness stay
unknown. A public label/two posts/common pay do not establish independent
judgment or execution. [Decision](decision-record.md) records exact baseline,
continuing duties, dissent and bounded country next action.
