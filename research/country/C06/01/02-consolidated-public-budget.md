# C06. Consolidated public budget

*Part of [C06 revenue and budget](../01-revenue-and-budget.md); design121
organization preserves current scoped provisions, not new tax/source authority.*

The elected Assembly authorizes the consolidated budget. Treasury executes it; separate
audit and legislative scrutiny examine actual payment and delivery. Central, local and
controlled public entities share one account of taxes, transfers, expenditure, liabilities,
guarantees and public assets. Internal transfers must not be counted as new national funds.
The [joint schedule](../18-joint-commitment-and-delivery-schedule.md) identifies
baseline inclusion, incremental costs and overlapping resources before
claiming a full package funded. Unpriced duties remain explicit unknowns.
During [government formation](../../C03/06-government-formation-and-caretaker.md),
the Assembly can still enact an appropriation. The caretaker may disclose
technical estimates and execute only prior-law obligations; a stalled
investiture neither renews the annual bridge nor cancels accrued claims.

The account keeps source transactions with the responsible revenue, Treasury,
bank, service and local custodians. A separate C11 public evidence service makes
official cash, workforce and physical series comparable, publishes methods,
uncertainty and missing coverage, and preserves earlier versions. It cannot
turn an estimate into spendable cash or change a legal entitlement. Audit,
opposition and affected people need lawful access to challenge both source
suppression and misleading aggregation; protected evidence access and the
service's routine publication require real staff and bounded standing funds.

The term-end [public-conditions account](../../C14/04-public-conditions.md)
also requires appropriated field observation, independent comparison,
privacy protection, public hearings and a remedy path for suppressed evidence.
Publish those costs beside services and independent review; an attractive
dashboard is not a free saving or a substitute for delivering a right.

Cash, qualified labor, land, energy, materials and ecological capacity are separate
constraints. More money does not instantly produce a nurse, home or power connection.
Market-power and product-safety remedies also need funded investigators,
laboratories, claimant help, alternate suppliers and transition workers; a
prospective fine is not available budget cash before lawful collection.
The [C14 national-form comparison](../../C14/05-scale-and-national-form.md)
costs each whole national/local or regional package with its tax base,
equalization, staff, elections, courts and handovers. One worker or receipt
cannot finance several proposed levels of government at once.
The [C07 health, school and care chains](../../C07/03-health-education-and-care.md)
also need separate high-need and fixed-place costs, qualified replacements,
transport and independent review. A contractor's low unit price cannot be
booked as a saving if the public receiver lacks workers or a safe place.
Each commitment must identify an entitlement, delivery authority, workforce, capital,
maintenance, due dates and remedy funding. Borrowing and automatic stabilizers are
available only under adopted fiscal rules and actual financing conditions.

The [design-58 joint income/tax reference](../../../settlement/income-floor/README.md)
substitutes represented wage taxes for the old personal-tax line and keeps
restricted contributions separate. It finds an unfunded launch under its
declared prices/cohorts; no additional tax, AI gain or lower basket is silently adopted.

The [joined national account](../20-national-economic-package.md) separates
production taxes, capital consumption, owned returns and private finance;
a fiscal surplus alone does not establish supplied or received protection.

The [operating book](../22-country-operating-resource-book.md) retains historical bids; the [FD bridge](../35-finance-production-and-delivery-bridge.md) joins current tax/price/asset/source hypotheses and rejects four unfunded launch claims.
[RF](../36-recurring-finance-and-pension-commitments.md) separates recurring/capital/financial sources; [TW](../../C05/10-work-rest-and-paid-capability.md) adds explicit paid-cover/learning/sickness requirements. [H housing](../../C09/12-secure-housing-and-repair.md) adds acquired stock, operating/renewal cash, individual support and temporary-home duties with actual sources unknown. No full-duty cost, numeric source package or received finance is established.

[AA23/AA31–AA36](../../C04/04-public-audit-and-accountable-oversight.md) now proposes
six-/nine-calendar-month public-account/opinion clocks after actual lawful year-end,
with a new calendar-year national reporting reference and protected old periods.
Separate audit budgets/external examination, actual public-risk/worker/court/record
cost and source are unpriced. Opinion/disclaimer/publication is not recovery,
Treasury cash, a new tax year or fiscal authority over elected law.

[DB01–DB20 ordinary budget government](../60-democratic-budget-and-execution.md)
now supplies member scrutiny/costing/amendment, explicit programme/commitment/
payment permissions and lawful execution/year-end rules. Original authority,
standing/bridge/AA/source and actual rights remain; no new money is supplied.
