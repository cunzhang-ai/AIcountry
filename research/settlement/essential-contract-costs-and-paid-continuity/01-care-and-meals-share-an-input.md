# Care and meals share one budget exposure

*Design381 · Constructed comparison with separate [primary procurement records](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-essential-contract-costs-and-paid-continuity/sources.md).*

Care and meal contracts each have 100 units due in the first
window. These base dues and existing wages have their own sources.
A lawful shared source has 15 additional units settled by day 3.
An optional service expansion would need five of those units.
Accepted care and meals are due by day 4. Both contracts have
separate accepting providers and exposed energy costs.

| Contract | Fixed component | Eligible labor or food | Eligible energy | Increase due |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Care | 60 | Labor 30 × 10% | 10 × 20% | 5 |
| Meals | 60 | Food 25 × 20% | 15 × 20% | 8 |

The components, signs, dates and weights are part of the original
tender. Assume no duplicate cost support, favorable hedge or lag
affecting this first window. Providers can execute if their
lawful due amounts reach them on time and separately accepted
labor, food, energy and receiving stay available. Paying only
one provider gives no assurance about the other.

| Path | Result under the declared assumptions |
|---|---|
| Qualified component clauses | Both adjustments require 13, leaving 2. Hold the optional five-unit start. Pay and verify both service streams independently. |
| Separate forecasts | Either adjustment alone fits with the five-unit project. Testing contracts separately gives the wrong joint result. |
| Uniform 10% increase across all costs | Requires 20 under those different contract terms. The gross source falls short by 5 and the uplift includes fixed costs. |
| Firm prices | No index payment is due. Compare the full bid risk premium and capacity after the shock. In the constructed weak-cash path the meal provider cannot absorb an eight-unit gap before day 4. Check a separately funded alternate. |
| All 15 available only on day 5 | The 13-unit obligation can still be owed, but the late cash cannot certify both day 4 deliveries. Verify a timely lawful alternative or report the remaining failure. |

With a 20% energy fall and no other movements, the same formula
lowers care by 2 and meals by 3. A fall counts as headroom only
when the lawful payment change and continued operation are both
qualified. A new window with the same shocks costs thirteen units again.
In this example the two remaining units cannot cover it.
No private arrears or unpaid work is assumed to fund the difference.
