# Original observations for Design204

Inspected 9 November 2026. The case numbers and selected rules are authored.
The comparator sources describe different institutions and questions.

## Norway's State Project Model

The Ministry of Finance's 2025 circular R-108 applies to state investments
above defined thresholds. It separates external review of the concept
appraisal before government concept selection from external review of the
management case and cost estimates before parliamentary investment and budget
consideration. Section 3 distinguishes related packages and individually
presented components. Section 5 calls for distinct concepts including a
do-nothing alternative and examination of monetized and nonmonetized effects.
These are institutional procedures. The circular does not establish actual
reviewer independence, lower cost or better outcomes. The Norwegian thresholds,
risk percentiles, ministerial functions and legal system are not imported.
The publisher's PDF was inspected directly. A local capture was unavailable.
[Official English circular, sections 2–5](https://www.regjeringen.no/globalassets/departementene/fin/2025/statens-prosjektmodell/circular-108-25-the-state-project-model.pdf)

## England's school estate

The National Audit Office's June 2023 report studied the condition of England's
school buildings, the Department for Education's information and funding, and
support to responsible bodies. The audit reports that 38% of the buildings
were believed to have passed initial estimated design life, which can be
extended with adequate maintenance. It reports average annual departmental
capital expenditure of £2.3 billion for rebuilding, maintenance and repair
over 2016–17 to 2022–23. It distinguishes that spending from the department's
2020 estimate of £5.3 billion a year after planned expansion to maintain schools
and mitigate the most serious risks. Estimated life does not diagnose a
particular building or measure a repair backlog by itself. Neither figure prices
AIcountry or establishes that universal rebuilding would improve schools.
[Original report, pp. 4–7 and 38–40](original-sources/nao-condition-of-school-buildings-2023.pdf)
· [Publisher copy](https://www.nao.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/condition-of-school-buildings.pdf)

The first source supplies a concrete governance comparator. The second
documents why the condition of existing assets and the balance of repair
and replacement belong in public investment decisions. Neither observes the
[worked crew and cash collision](../settlement/public-investment/01-school-grid-and-court.md).
