# Eight residents, two meal windows and a shared outage

*Design293 · Constructed local service and first-budget comparison.*

Eight residents each need one suitable meal in each of two
successive windows. Five can use meals from an authorized local
kitchen. Three need an individually suitable ready-to-eat route
because the assumed kitchen and collection arrangements cannot
meet their access or dietary needs. The area has lost ordinary
shops, piped water and household cooking. The five kitchen meals
use separately funded safe water and power. That capacity has
accepted staff, inputs and delivery for both windows and has
already protected ordinary school and care commitments.

| Additional choice | Cumulative cash by window one / two | Qualified meal places in the declared loss |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Extra cash through ordinary shops | 4 / 4 | 0 / 0 while suitable accessible offers are absent |
| Unprepared ingredients and delivery | 5 / 5 | 0 / 0 while safe household preparation is unavailable |
| Qualified kitchen and first ready-to-eat delivery | 7 / 7 | 8 / 5 |
| Qualified kitchen and both ready-to-eat deliveries | 7 / 10 | 8 / 8 |
| Distributed suitable ready-to-eat meals for everyone | 7 / 12 | 8 / 8 with independently accepted supply and transport |

The kitchen costs four units in advance for its two windows.
The first three suitable ready-to-eat places add three units
early. A separate three-unit purchase must be accepted and
funded by the second delivery deadline. With seven early and
ten cumulative, the mixed route qualifies in the declared
loss. With seven in total, the same first delivery works and
three residents miss the second. A later reimbursement or a
shared inaccessible vehicle does not close the gap. A qualified
larger distributed plan costs twelve in this case and can
survive loss of the kitchen, assuming its own routes remain open.

If all eight can again reach and use suitable market offers,
additional lawful money can be useful without extra public
meal delivery. Actual food access must be checked before
closing an interim route. Existing cash benefits are separate
from the choices in the table. Reaching a loading dock,
sending an invoice or receiving ingredients does not count as
a meal. The units, service windows and cohorts are invented to
expose dependencies. The exercise does not set a diet, price,
benefit amount or operational standard.
