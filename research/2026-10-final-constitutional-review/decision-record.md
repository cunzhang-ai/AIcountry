## Diagnosis, values and rival explanations

A constitution saying 'independent courts' is insufficient if one patron owns
qualification, appointments, budget, original records and enforcement. Democratic
standing, usable opposition, fair hearing and peaceful replacement are values;
appointment and appellate structures are fallible mechanisms, not proof of them.
Specialization may protect legal review; ownership, class interests, profession,
material shortage and informal patronage may matter more than formal coordination.
More panels can increase expense without changing actual control.

Compare unresolved prior reference, a dedicated court, a supreme ordinary-court
bench and legislative finality with political scrutiny in the [rival table](../settlement/constitutional-review/03-alternatives-and-reopening.md).
A supreme bench can save duplication and combine appeals, but exposes both final
routes to its appointment/docket leadership. Legislative finality increases
elected final policy control but weakens judicial remedies against a majority
that disables future competition. These are substantive alternatives, not labels
absorbed into CJ. No external comparative causal test establishes the winner.

subject to shorter hearing clocks. National interim suspension requires five,

days from first related restraint. Expiry can restore harmful operation; that is

These and all appointment/experience/time parameters are normative hypotheses.

Direct personal conflict is not cured by 'necessity'. If all lawful deciders
