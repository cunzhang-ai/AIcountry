# Design history: design 77 onward

*Part of [design history](../../DESIGN-HISTORY.md); research, not enacted law.*

78. **Integrated design 77** selects [CD01–CD16](../../country/C08/21-conflict-detention-and-release.md):
    closed actual POW/neutrality grounds, no general civilian/NIAC internment,
    independent civilian 24-hour review, full status hearing within seven days,
    bounded renewals, humane paid care and limited noncustodial discipline.
    Ordinary civilian courts hear own-force/captive criminal cases; separate
    actual grounds/clocks, shorter POW criminal ceilings and case credit apply.
    Release follows the actual end of grounds/active hostilities, with safe
    receiving, independent transfer challenge and continuing missing/injury claims.
    [C10 control](../../country/C10/08-conflict-custody-and-civilian-control.md) and
    [C06 capacity](../../country/C06/26-conflict-custody-and-release-capacity.md)
    join command, funds, courts, care and succession without renewing war powers.
    [Alternatives/cases](../../settlement/conflict-custody/README.md) preserve a
    genuine broader-internment rival and unpriced costs of narrower power.
    The [423-artifact predecessor](../../2026-10-conflict-detention-and-release/predecessor-design-76/SNAPSHOT.md)
    and [decision](../../2026-10-conflict-detention-and-release/decision-record.md)
    distinguish inspected treaty/interpretive texts from own normative choices.
    No actual independent review, party status, public mandate, trained capacity,
    safe receiving, cost/source finance or observed superiority is established.
    Next close technical sector instruments, then audit the full country;
    reopen on incompatible actual duties, failed protection or a better model.

    exposes paid assessment, metrology, maintenance and received substitutes.
    [Cases/rivals/instrument register](../../settlement/technical-safety/README.md)

    The [439-artifact predecessor](../../2026-10-technical-safety-and-access/predecessor-design-77/SNAPSHOT.md)

    quantities/costs, receiving and observed superiority remain unknown.
    Next audit the complete national settlement and address its most consequential
    incompatibility; do not automatically create another technical micro-study.

    Correct market GVA by separating public output/inputs and product taxes;
    original baseline is 2311, corrected book requirement about +50.32% per
    market FTE and hybrid +50.19%, not the retired 52.33%. Frozen cash/GDP/bids
    remain unchanged. A no-growth counterfactual exposes a matched H saving/G
    deficit, not compulsory saving, inflation or an actual funding agreement.

    preserve existing/overdue rights and contribution/accrual consistency.
    Select only an actually available public/voluntary residual pool with
    declared priority/comparable-claim rules, first 24/absolute 72-hour expiry,
    direct review and actual receipt. Wider diversion remains a distinct rival.
    [460 exact predecessors](../../2026-10-whole-country-reconciliation/predecessor-design-78/SNAPSHOT.md)

    multi-agent analysis supplies no qualified independent review or public
    participation. No full-duty finance, actual production/staffing, adopted law,
    received remedy or observed superiority. Next complete the final court
    reference, membership/necessary agency, attention and joined material choice.

81. **Integrated design 80** selects [CJ01–CJ18](../../country/C03/08-constitutional-review-and-public-control.md):
    separate nine-seat final review, twelve-year nonrenewable cohorts, three
    appointment sources with fixed thresholds, finite same-seat reserves and
    five actual final votes. Ordinary urgent relief retains its own clocks.
    Preassigned external ordinary judges hear closed own-court appointment,
    personal-status, discipline and funding matters. All-decider direct conflicts,
    deadlock and simultaneous capture can still leave no final remedy.
    Bounded national interim expiry and fair finite suspension prevent provisional
    power from silently becoming permanent. [C06/29](../../country/C06/29-constitutional-review-capacity.md)
    joins lawful source/payment/execution; SF is one old-scope standby, not free
    new-duty coverage. [Rivals and authored journeys](../../settlement/constitutional-review/README.md)
    retain the supreme-bench and legislative-finality choices and material costs.
    The [474-artifact predecessor](../../2026-10-final-constitutional-review/predecessor-design-79/SNAPSHOT.md)
    and [decision](../../2026-10-final-constitutional-review/decision-record.md) preserve
    prior law, source limits, review corrections and pending claims. Shared-AI
    analysis is not independent qualified or affected-party review. No adoption,
    adequate simultaneous staffing/source, delivered remedy or superiority.
    Next membership/secure residence, necessary agency, revision/attention and
    joined material alternatives; do not automatically add another court study.

82. **Integrated design81** selects [MB01–MB20](../../country/C02/06-citizenship-and-secure-belonging.md):
    territorial birth, bounded overseas legal-parent descent, free four-year
    naturalization and vested secure home independent of sponsor/status papers.
    The adult resident vote remains separate; local eligibility has a six-month
    national habitual-residence reference with actual municipal residence.
    No involuntary citizenship deprivation, even for genuine fraudulent grants;
    forged credentials and stolen identity are distinct, fairly adjudicated cases.
    Corrupt irreversible electorate changes remain a serious retained objection.
    Stateless recognition, shared proof, independent sponsor exit, suspensive
    removal review and tightly bounded execution custody join [C10 receiving](../../country/C10/09-membership-admission-and-reception.md)
    and [C06 capacity](../../country/C06/30-membership-and-reception-capacity.md).
    The [489-artifact predecessor](../../2026-10-citizenship-and-secure-belonging/predecessor-design-80/SNAPSHOT.md)
    and [decision](../../2026-10-citizenship-and-secure-belonging/decision-record.md)
    preserve exact old law/research, source limits, alternatives and continuing
    rights. No actual treaty position, foreign recognition, public/qualified
    independent review, adoption, safe receiving or superiority. Next necessary
    non-emergency agency, constitutional restoration/finite consultation and
    joined material alternatives; do not automatically add another status study.

84. **Integrated design83** selects [RR/FA](../../settlement/democratic-revision/README.md):
    closed prior-law rights restoration, unchanged public amendment and no

    the proposed electorate cannot ratify itself. Finite substantive attention
    combines consequence-led, opposition/affected and human-drawn agendas;
    unexamined reasons remain visible without a perpetual new-argument veto.
    Individual legal claims and required hearings keep their separate clocks.
    [C06/32](../../country/C06/32-democratic-repair-and-public-attention-capacity.md)
    adds qualified capacity/source/receipt without pricing new dues as old bids.

    and the [decision](../../2026-10-democratic-repair-and-finite-attention/decision-record.md)
    preserve earlier contradictions and the proposed scoped-method clarification.
    Three primary documents/two families and same-controller AI reasoning do not
    establish public consent, qualified independent review, funded operation,
    numeric optimality or better lives. Next joined material/transition and
    objective-wide comparison; reopen on actual harm, exclusion or better models.
