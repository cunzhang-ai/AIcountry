# Shared dependence without private sovereignty

Design149 · [Canonical CB01–CB24](../../country/C09/14-shared-buildings-and-neighbor-rights.md)
· Proposed ordinary law, not an adopted resident or owner procedure

H already protected maintenance/tenure and public-option resident voice. PT06
already protected private units/shares. Their missing link was who decides
common-building spending, everyday use, deadlocked repair and entry, and who
actually pays/receives. CB supplies those positive choices; it does not create
a public municipality inside every building or require unshared neighbors to
join a club. Owner finance, resident use, material exposure and legal authority
are distinct. Qualified necessary maintenance cannot be voted away.

[Adversarial routes and real rivals](01-adversarial-routes-and-rivals.md)
retain concentrated ownership, low turnout, poverty, connected contractors,
minority intrusion and court/source failure. [Decision and sources](../../2026-11-shared-buildings-and-neighbor-rights/decision-record.md)
pin the predecessor and original scope. Authored vote/share/bill examples test
only numerical consistency; no actual resident consent, structural safety or
independent adjudication is computed.

CB17 uses the existing I03 necessary primary-home cost rule, not a new all-owner
capital guarantee, public loan/lien or free reserve. Owner-wealth incidence and
tenant alternatives need a genuine public source/comparison. Next return to a
finite whole-country review of major choices/cumulative power/source/AI/life
paths; do not keep following building subquestions while the country is missing
larger choices. Actual adoption, operational ability and outcomes stay distinct
from completing a hypothetical design. No current law or software changes.
