# Cash, currency, timing and genuinely different choices

*Part of [protected-stage reference](README.md); authored failures, not predictions.*

| Declared case | Result through year 8 | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| Construction/training cash 120 only | First cash shortfall in year 3; unpaid planned requirements 176.4 while retaining buffer 10 | Missing salaries/maintenance cannot disappear or be charged to unpaid carers |
| Full envelope cash 296.4, usable exports 12 | Paid planned costs 286.4, cash 10 and FX 90; conditional acceptance at end of year 6 | No actual source, private replacement, national benefits, qualified operator or legitimate decision verified |
| Same domestic cash, export forecast never received | Cash fits, FX first fails in year 2; unmet foreign payments total 81, reserves held at 75 | Cash cannot commission unavailable imported inputs; the arrears persist |
| Two-year delay, capital cost +20% | Year-8 cash 16.4, but the year-6 physical deadline is missed and service starts only in year 9 | A favorable closing cash balance hides later obligations, not timely protection |

The delayed case costs 280 within eight years because no operating maintenance
has yet arrived. Through **two actual operating years** it requires 345.6,
compared with the reference's 286.4. Preserving buffer 10 leaves additional
funding need **59.2** beyond the same 296.4 opening allocation. Do not call
the fixed-window difference an improvement. Each case also needs continuing
annual salary/maintenance of 32.8 after construction; no indefinite funding
source or future replacement is established. Paying only part of a year's
bill leaves allocation unknown; the model credits no asset after unpaid cash
or FX appears. It is not an actual cancellation or arrears-assignment plan.

The four numerical cases are sensitivities within one project form. They do
not substitute for comparing different institutions:

| Public choice | Mechanism and substantive tradeoff |
|---|---|
| Retain and repair | Preserve lawful receivers and accrued claims; direct maintenance, hardship and remedy repair toward harms. Avoid acquisition/overlap while leaving existing patronage or unreliable supply to address. No change is valid if alternatives worsen lives. |
| Protected plural stages | Publicly chosen staged standards/investment, voluntary paid workforce change and licensed plural/local supply under rights. May reduce lock-in and preserve exit; fragmented contracts, rents and unequal local capacity need remedies. |
| Acquisition and direct provision | Purchase/transfer assets under source law, use public delivery and bargaining, fund integration and democratic control. Can reduce private gatekeeping but adds purchase/carry, management load and public-patronage risk; a dividend is not free finance. |
| Simultaneous new-system launch | Start all duties and supply changes together. Stronger common coherence does not resolve the revealed cash and physical gaps; reject an unconditional operating claim. |

Select protected staged **rules**, retain the existing income research choice,
and leave ownership/model choices open to the legitimate public. Do not
select illustrative prices, a six-year statutory wait, compulsory export
conversion, new tax rates or automatic benefit netting. The previous national
income-floor gap remains unresolved even in the paid-envelope case. Actual
harm, costs, freedom, working time, participation and enforceable control
must distinguish these models. One AI's authored objections are not assent.
