# SR13–SR17. Pay for urgent execution when the ordinary route fails

*Design268 · Proposed public-remedy and first-budget duties.*

**SR13 — Keep the first request, order and execution dates separately.**
A person reporting threatened urgent loss gets a usable first receipt
through the existing statutory intake. A court, competent provisional
decision-maker and payer retain their separate legal clocks and duties.
Record what remains unassessed, a qualifying order or provisional act,
the assigned executor, original deadline, lawful source and actual
payment or service received. A referral or an incomplete file cannot
close the protected first receipt. Existing rules govern what is
needed for an order and how urgent protection proceeds while proof is
outstanding. Track missing intake, pending determination, delayed
payment and undelivered service separately. Give affected people a
safe way to report changed urgency without starting again.

**SR14 — Preauthorize a narrow, paid execution route.** The competent
elected budget specifies the covered orders and lawful interim acts,
the maximum and dates, eligible payers, independent access and a
qualified execution roster. Use a protected part of the continuing
[B19 appropriation](../13/05-orders-execution-and-collective-claims.md)
where expressly adopted and applicable. Price the independent record
path, staff, receiving provider and the next plausible call.
Compare ordinary sufficient funding, a shared standby and a
separately held increment. Select a protected increment where
common expenditure, payer control or replenishment delay would
otherwise defeat a material remedy. An order within lawful scope
does not authorize a new source, expand eligibility or change the
elected cap. A shortage of staff or accepted providers stays visible
even when money is held.

**SR15 — Execute the due scope and settle the payer dispute separately.**
The named competent payer or prior-law substitute verifies the order
or other eligible act, required limits, available authorized balance
and receiving route. Pay the lawful undisputed amount or procure the
authorized direct protection before the useful time. Preserve separate
commitment, verification and payment authority. An eligible person
can use an authorized direct route where prepayment would make the
remedy inaccessible. Do not require them to finance an interagency
dispute. Track any temporary public advance and the actual final payer
under existing settlement law. A disputed reimbursement is a claim.
It restores usable capacity only after actual receipt and lawful
availability for reuse. Reconcile paid amounts once and do not turn
a good-faith recipient into an interagency debtor.

**SR16 — Keep the second call independent and reviewable.** After a
material use, record remaining money, staffing and scope by the next
useful date. If an implicated agency controls the ordinary route,
use the previously funded, competent substitute and its own record
access within the enacted limits. Keep timely court access even
when the reserve is empty. If the cap, safe executor or authorized
source is exhausted, name the precise exposed duty and activate any
lawful protection still available. Use the competent elected route
for an additional source and publish the verified shortfall safely.
Do not charge unrelated benefit, care or electoral balances or
extend an order deadline to hide a gap. A court can require an
account and enforce an available lawful route under existing
jurisdiction.

**SR17 — Verify the remedy and repair the actual bottleneck.** Report
original intake, independent determination, lawful payment, acceptance
and useful-time service as separate results. Include cases absent
from the main system and older unresolved cases in qualified scope.
Keep clinical, family and protected case evidence in safe custody.
Where recurrence persists, identify whether the deficit is in
ordinary need, decision work, paid execution, provider receiving,
a common controller or lawful replenishment. Match the correction
under [JC](../../C06/65/05-matched-protected-window-and-second-call.md)
and LD, with continuing obligations and the lawful successor.
AI may help trace missing or duplicated records. It cannot classify
a person as legally ineligible, issue a remedy, release a payment
or certify delivery. Reopen on repeat delay, missing evidence,
cash exhaustion or a less burdensome qualified ordinary route.
