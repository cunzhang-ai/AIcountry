# Scarcity without an unelected allocator

4 October 2026 · Integrated design 34 research selection; not enacted law

Prepared by an AI assistant without affected-party participation, independent
review, a jurisdiction or a real inventory. The [exact design-33 predecessor](predecessor-design-33/README.md)
is preserved. This record selects a working-country rule, not an emergency
power, ration order or claim that the mechanism can operate today.

## Triggering problem and competing explanations

C06 exposes common shortages, C09 calls for preadopted sector priorities, and
C10 gives a local incident lead and Assembly control of material
redistribution. They do not specify what happens when water, grid, clinical,
food and shelter systems simultaneously claim the same generator, fuel,
workers or access road before the Assembly can resolve a new tradeoff. One
diagnosis is insufficient stock: only investment and mutual aid can fix it.
Another is missing lawful priority: real stock may exist, yet incompatible
orders or unequal access let an operator choose. A third is a failed
communication or sitting route: the rightful decision-maker cannot act in
the first hours. The selected arrangement addresses the second and third
while preserving the first as a physical limit.

The [WHO resource-allocation ethics guidance](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/coronavirus-disease-covid-19-ethics-resource-allocation-and-priority-setting)
supports transparent, inclusive and revisable allocation, warns that simple
arrival order can favor better-positioned people, and distinguishes the
principles appropriate to different medical resources and scarcity stages.
It is medical guidance, not a national cross-sector law. [UNDRR's risk-governance
account](https://www.undrr.org/implementing-sendai-framework/risk-governance)
supports explicit roles and cross-sector coordination, not this proposal's
specific authority chain. There is no observed AIcountry shortage or local
evidence establishing the selected package's performance.

## Whole-package comparison

| Package | Possible advantage | Decisive risk or conflict |
|---|---|---|
| Price and contract order, with ordinary operator discretion | Fast and uses existing supply channels | Wealth, monopoly, contract position and digital reach may determine access to life-sustaining goods |
| Equal shares or first arrival for all | Simple, apparently neutral | Ignores different need, dependency and access; queue position reflects unequal information and mobility |
| Expert or AI optimization with one national objective | Fast cross-sector modeling | Conceals value choices, uncertain data and political authority inside a technical operator |
| Cabinet emergency discretion until the Assembly can act | Flexible in a surprise | Can convert delay and repeated declarations into durable executive allocation power |
| **Selected preenacted staged authority plus elected unresolved tradeoff** | Allows bounded first-hour action while keeping new material choices public and rights-reviewable | Statutory plan may be incomplete, sitting route may fail, and even fair procedure cannot create supplies |

The [new C06 topic](../country/C06/05-cross-system-scarcity.md) places
resource-specific criteria, observable triggers and temporary authority in
prior Assembly law. Local and sector actors execute those powers. The cabinet
reconciles cross-system use of **preassigned** nonoverlapping reserves, then
returns any unprovided material tradeoff to the Assembly. Courts decide
legality and protection, qualified professionals decide individual clinical
cases under their standards, and operators retain narrow safety competence.
Neither one universal ranking nor a new emergency sovereign is selected.
The statute must include a limited residual default for an unforeseen clash,
so the first officer is not left to invent a priority by calling inaction
neutral. It defines protected stocks, a necessity finding, a tie rule and a
review path before the emergency.

## Coherence, affected scope and objections

This choice depends on physical stocks, source records, secure communication,
qualified staff, alternate Assembly sitting, funded delivery, judicial access,
affected localities and actual treaty and private-law duties. Prior allocation
from a common reserve must show what is taken from the supplying locality or
future repair. Interim acts cannot use another person's current legal claim
as a free reserve. Existing contracts, land titles, clinical duties and
cross-border obligations need exact lawful migration, not a generic scarcity
clause. C06's commitment account must distinguish lawful entitlement,
allocated stock, physical receipt and still-unmet claim.

The coordinator is an existing answerable cabinet officer, not a new
sovereign body. Sector custodians keep original stock and delivery records;
C11's evidence path protects their versions; opposition receives timely
lawful access; a court or other preassigned independent reviewer can order
correction, and named service officers must execute it with real stock.
Appointment, removal, pay, communications, fuel custody and enforcement may
still converge under one controller. A formal appeal then offers no effective
protection; the actual chain must be tested against a noncooperating cabinet,
operator and reviewer before claiming democratic readiness. If review or
notice fails, no new authority follows from silence; preserve the claim and
evidence for the first reachable lawful forum, while existing narrow safety
powers follow their enacted expiry and fallback.

An objection is that the Assembly cannot prewrite every disaster and a rigid
plan may kill people. The selected law therefore permits bounded, reversible
life-preserving interim action with recorded reasons and rapid elected
review; exact duration and force depend on real institutional capacity, not
an invented national clock. The contrary objection is that a flexible
“life-preserving” label can cover favoritism or indefinite emergency rule.
The statute must enumerate the acts, restrict cumulative effect and preserve
opposition, source evidence, review and lawful succession. If the protected
sitting route is unavailable, officers keep only those narrow previously
authorized acts and report failed notice; a missing vote does not approve a
new allocation. A court can stop unlawful distribution but cannot issue a
replacement national budget.

Another objection is that a national rule can erase local knowledge and
Indigenous, downstream or foreign claims. The statute must define affected
scope, preserve their lawful rights and give local bodies and people a route
to contest factual premises before and during execution when possible.
Actual participation has not happened here. A clinical need or ecological
limit may justify different treatment; race, status, political allegiance,
wealth or possession of a digital credential does not. No authored case
proves that these distinctions will be applied fairly under stress.

## Status and next bounded test

Evidence qualification: WHO and UNDRR guide fair process and coordination;
they do not validate this country's institutional performance. Research
selection: preenacted staged authority with elected material choices. Legal
authorization, operating readiness and observed outcomes: none. No estimate
of reserves, distribution losses, reviewer hours or affected-party preferences
has been independently verified.

Test a compound outage in a real affected jurisdiction: one generator, a
clinic, water pumping, cooling shelter and food delivery, with an unavailable
ordinary Assembly chamber. Compare the selected law with market, equal-share
and broad executive packages using original stock and delivery records,
rights, actual response time, unmet needs, diversion losses and opposition
access. The test ends when every allocation and denial is traceable to its
authorized source and the displaced duty is visible, even if no package
performs well. Reopen the selection if interim discretion repeatedly expands,
an elected route cannot meet real timing, excluded people lack remedies or a
different model better protects life and democratic control.
The responsible public research role, jurisdiction, participation plan,
competent authorization and resource ceiling remain unassigned; this text
does not authorize a real emergency trial.
