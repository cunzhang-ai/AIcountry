# Design311. Recurring places and portable support

[XR11–XR15](../../country/C04/09/03-recurring-places-and-portable-support.md)
preserves the active award and original obligations while public
authorities accept separate service windows. Lawful paid overlap,
different tasks and actual delivery retain separate records.
Use prior sector powers, independent challenge and a paid lawful
successor when a location or operator changes. Privacy and consent
stay scoped to the task. Publication creates no new public power.

The [decision record](../../2026-11-portable-services-across-recurring-places/decision-record.md)
retains the simpler per-visit and direct national rivals and sets
out the paid test still needed.
