# C11. Keep one public risk assessment from becoming a judgment about the person

*Design408 · ASI01–ASI08 · Proposed ordinary public-operation duties.*

A public body may need to verify an income fact or act on a concrete warning.
A label about a person's general trustworthiness can instead spread from one
service to another and change their access everywhere. Give each use of a
fact its own lawful purpose and preserve an independent route to each right.

**ASI01 — Prohibit portable adverse classifications.** A public body or
its delegate must not make, request, buy, share or use a general assessment of
a person's trustworthiness, compliance or social worth for material adverse
treatment across unrelated public functions. Apply the rule to scores,
categories, lists, proxy signals and human-maintained equivalents. Material
effects include delay, extra proof, loss of ordinary access and escalation to
intrusive review. An accurate original observation, a vendor change, consent
to an unrelated service or a human signature does not authorize this use.
Do not infer such a classification from a person's refusal of optional data use.
Legitimate adverse findings in a specific case retain their separate law.

**ASI02 — Transfer only an authorized fact or specific warning.** Allow a
necessary, purpose-bound fact where the recipient has an actual legal basis
and the fact is relevant to the receiving rule. Identify the origin, period,
purpose, reliability, correction route and limits. Where source law authorizes
a specific warning for serious harm or a lawful investigation, share only the
information, recipient and time necessary for that task. A warning remains a
lead; separate lawful grounds, independent evidence and fair process govern
any adverse action. Neither route carries a general profile or opens other
databases. Preserve assisted alternative proof and source-law privacy.

**ASI03 — Demonstrate that receiving decisions can stand on their own.**
Before material use and on any expansion or changed dependency, the duty
owner identifies what the signal changes at intake, in the queue, in evidence,
at decision and in execution. Test versions, cached results and manual
workarounds. Compare with the same lawful case without the out-of-purpose
signal, including how the refusal of optional processing changes the route.
Where an opaque derived result cannot be separated from a prohibited use,
hold that reliance before it can burden the person. Keep an independently
usable ordinary route on original deadlines. Protected evidence may be
examined without a compulsory common identity or a national case profile.

**ASI04 — Give a reachable first report and timely protection.** An affected
person can report repeated barriers or a suspected shared inference at an
ordinary accessible office without identifying the vendor or proving the
whole pattern. Preserve protected first receipt and original rights. The
receiver keeps an accountable contact until competent sector offices accept
the relevant parts. Each takes case-specific protective action under its own
law before the useful deadline. Do not require the person to reproduce the
same evidence at every office. Share only necessary lawful dependency and
case facts; respect safe contact, representation and source protection.

**ASI05 — Contain the use even if the underlying observation is true.**
On credible material evidence, the responsible sector owners promptly
isolate the affected use and dependent outputs, preserve lawful originals,
notify known relevant recipients and check whether the signal changed an act.
Use EV, DP and RUC for scoped tracing and correction as applicable. Keep
legitimate use at the originating office on its own authority. The shared
digital support function can relay a scoped notice and expose conflicts.
It has no power to stop unrelated decisions, direct a court or browse
individual records across sectors. Accuracy review alone cannot cure a
purpose violation.

**ASI06 — Restore each affected route and verify receipt.** Authorized
officers use inspectable lawful grounds to reconsider materially affected
open or completed acts under their respective law. Search for people blocked
before a decision was recorded. Correct queues, proof demands and decisions,
and trace due payments or services to actual receiving. Give usable reasons,
continuing urgent protection and independent challenge. If a service deadline
has passed, preserve the missed opportunity and applicable remedy. A report
does not establish entitlement or revoke a valid independent finding.

**ASI07 — Keep challenge outside the common chain.** An independent data or
sector reviewer and the competent court retain their separate source powers.
They can examine protected original grounds, trace actual use and order
applicable relief without a vendor's permission. Elected bodies set scope and
fund timely staff, review, records and delivery. Give opposition and affected
people safe access to material aggregate evidence, missed protections and
systemic objections. Test who can appoint, fund, obstruct or replace the
relevant offices. AI may assist comparison and case finding. It cannot
establish relevance, permission, priority or delivered relief.

**ASI08 — Price simultaneous relief before reliance.** Test actual qualified
ordinary capacity and independent review across the services exposed to one
signal. Count shared staff, credentials, source custody and payers once.
Preserve favorable and unaffected lawful operation. If the fallback cannot
meet all due work, record each gap, assign its remedy and seek elected source
correction. Adopt prospectively with prior claims, cases, lawful data uses,
protected custody and deadlines carried to named successors. Reopen on
functionally equivalent relabeling, coerced disclosure, missed services or a
simpler equally protective evidence route.
