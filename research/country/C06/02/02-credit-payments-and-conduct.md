# C06. Money, credit and payments: Credit, payments and financial conduct

*Part of [C06. Money, credit and payments](../02-money-credit-and-payments.md); integrated design 104 research, not enacted law.*

## Credit, payments and financial conduct

The selected [credit-and-finance architecture](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-credit-and-finance/decision-record.md)
links normal lending to that failure regime. Payments, savings, investment and
credit carry different claims and risks; a resident must know whether a balance
is an insured deposit, safeguarded client asset, investment at risk or debt.
Basic lawful payment access needs a usable public or regulated provider route
separate from acceptance of a loan, political profiling or an AI account.
Credit is not a general entitlement: lenders may decline a loan for evidenced
legitimate reasons including repayment risk, while discriminatory exclusion,
deceptive terms and a provider's control of the only payment channel remain
reviewable. Public development loans, guarantees and preferential rates
require the C05/C06 legislative mandate,
subsidy and expected-loss account; the deposit insurer and central-bank
liquidity window cannot be used to conceal them.

Banks bear prior-law capital, liquidity, governance, concentration and
connected-party limits proportionate to their activities. Nonbank lenders,
funds, insurers and platform intermediaries are supervised for the risks they
actually create, especially leverage, short funding of long assets, promises
of redemption, bank exposures and common controllers. Moving a loan to an
affiliate or labeling it a token does not remove a material risk from the
perimeter. Supervisors can obtain lawful source evidence, including exposures
that are not visible on a bank's balance sheet, with privacy and independent
challenge. They publish aggregate credit, arrears, leverage, concentration,
housing exposure and contingent public support; uncertainty and unobserved
nonbank activity remain explicit. One asset-price model or AI score is neither
a solvency finding nor a command to approve or deny an individual's credit.

The Assembly establishes a financial-stability mandate and enumerates the
permitted system-wide tools, bounds, decision rule, review and expiry. A
statutory financial-stability council with one voting member from each of
the monetary authority, prudential supervisor and financial markets and conduct supervisor
may, by recorded majority, activate a specified, time-bounded capital,
liquidity, exposure or borrower-risk tool
for a stated systemic risk; the competent supervisor applies it to regulated
entities. Prior law supplies conflict and vacancy substitutes; when the
council cannot lawfully decide, sector supervisors retain only their own
existing powers. Treasury supplies fiscal facts but has no council vote or
veto over publication or prudential action and cannot authorize a bailout
through the council. The council cannot set taxes, direct a named loan,
assign homes, change deposit coverage or suspend political payments.
[MC09–MC16](../41-money-and-financial-public-control.md) now supplies fixed
chief/reserve appointments and actual council2yes/2present of3; the monetary
source uses the next eligible current member, other sources their preapproved
same-seat reserves. A career Deputy never inherits a council policy vote;
no minister's case-specific replacement or shrinking denominator. A bounded statutory
budget and direct lawful source access cannot depend on one regulated firm;
industry fees and exceptions are audited. The Assembly may revise the
general tool mandate prospectively, not pick a named winner or borrower.
Council decisions show evidence, expected distributional and credit-supply
effects, dissent and renewal conditions, with Assembly
scrutiny, affected-borrower and small-firm hearing where time permits, and
judicial legality review. An urgent rule receives prompt later hearing;
representative challengers can contest its lawful scope without selecting
individual borrowers. A rule's emergency label does not make it permanent.
Sector supervisors retain ordinary licensing and enforcement; the council
does not become their universal appeal judge.

A financial markets and conduct supervisor distinct from the prudential bank
supervisor enforces clear total price,
fees, currency and variable-rate exposure, proportionate assessment of a
borrower's ability to repay, fair credit-data access/correction and
non-harassing collection across banks, brokers and digital lenders. Its
complaint determinations remain subject to an independent ombud or court
route. AI-based underwriting and intermediaries remain
answerable to the licensed provider; a score cannot replace a reasoned adverse
decision and independent challenge. Foreclosure and seizure follow notice,
court-controlled due process, subsistence protection and safe housing duties
where law provides them. Accessible restructuring and personal or small-firm
insolvency can discharge qualifying unpayable debt under prospective law while
respecting secured claims, workers, other creditors and fraud findings. Neither
a blanket debtor rescue nor automatic repossession solves a systemic shock.
Conduct review, prudential supervision, resolution and courts keep distinct
mandates, evidence and appeals; the same financial sponsor must not control
all four. [M01–M16](../24-financial-law-and-loss-allocation.md) now proposes explicit
capital/liquidity/exposure, limited tool, protection and loss rules. They need
actual qualified review/adoption; no generic borrower-risk power or new public
guarantee follows. U retains ordinary debt relief and M the bank exceptions.

Bank insolvency, payment-system outage, inflation or exchange-rate shock, and
sovereign cash shortfall trigger different duties. Bank resolution uses the
preauthorized fund and loss hierarchy; any new taxpayer bailout or change to
statutory guarantees requires an affirmative
Assembly decision. A payment outage activates lawful alternate and assisted
channels while preserving transaction evidence and due claims. If Treasury lacks
cash, it follows enacted due-date and essential-obligation rules with only lawful
funds, publishes arrears and who bears interruption, and presents lawful tax,
borrowing, reprioritization or specifically authorized monetary-financing options
to the Assembly. A receiver cannot spend the social budget, and Treasury cannot
use a bank rule or AI priority score to cancel an entitlement. If incompatible
nondeferrable claims exceed lawful finance or actual deliverable capacity, the
conflict remains public and legally challengeable; a court cannot create nurses,
imports or foreign exchange by ordering domestic issuance.

The [civil core B01–B20](../../C08/13-civil-obligations-and-executed-remedies.md)
adds agreement/agent attribution, provider/control liability, usable evidence
and lawful debt/judgment execution. A receiver or collector cannot use
nonpayment to jail a debtor or erase subsistence; insolvency priorities,
discharge and exemption now have [candidate U01–U12](../../C08/14-private-insolvency-and-debt-recovery.md).
This is no new lending, actual discharge, general bailout or inherited
source-law amendment; credit/worker/victim effects require review.

[SF03–SF06](../25-source-finance-and-payment-continuity.md) distinguishes
disbursed working credit, paid common equity, actual reserves and a separately
owned public standby. A credited deposit is not external settlement cash;
future renewal, safe-account migration and insurance capacity are not assumed.

[NB01–NB36](../44-nonbank-finance-and-client-protection.md) now owns positive nonbank custody, fund/insurance/private-pension, lender and securities/CCP duties. NB21 supplies the distinct20% nonbank credit capital/30-day liquidity candidate; M bank requirements and MC source/authority stay separate. No new investor/insurer/public pension guarantee or completed technical calibration follows.

[SD29–SD32](../43-country-source-and-delivery.md) now traces bank deposit creation against producer debt, NBF funded equity/term cash and direct equity, including gross inventoryVAT and connected-group capital. A passing ratio is no paid source/settlement or viable borrower; no old private mortgage automatically becomes bank assets.

[Design148 PT](../../C08/26-property-transfers-and-secured-credit.md) supplies
the ordinary property/security law previously left to source-specific rules:
real attachment, disclosed ceiling, actual continuous notice/possession rank,
retail goods exception and court realization. Actual bank M/NB client/market
source, U rank/stay and old mortgages remain; loan capital and an AI valuation
are neither ownership nor a repossession writ.

[TU13–TU17](../../C04/12/04-independent-payment-and-receipt.md)
keeps an established public cash award available to a person without
a safe account. The original payer and funded backup retain responsibility.
This public payment duty neither guarantees a deposit nor grants credit
or access to unrelated balances.
