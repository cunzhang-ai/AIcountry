# C05. Personal security, plural ownership and useful investment

3 November 2026 · Design 136, retaining IC investment/credit rules; SG supersedes design88 destination · IC01–IC20 research selection, not enacted law

**Design136 [SG01–SG12](15-service-first-personal-security.md) now selects the
service-first individual I05/SD05 guarantee as preferred continuing architecture.
Universal ordinary cash remains a substantive rival, without presumed destination
status. Retain separately protected needs/services/earned rights and IC's plural,
paid ownership and bounded public investment through accountable authorities.** Reject a single
national investment owner and a blanket leveraged mutual purchase as the
default source of people's incomes. Neither redistribution nor public ownership
alone creates the productive capacity to sustain these choices.

Design88 previously changed design69's I05 destination preference; design136
now supersedes that change for explicit material-priority reasons.
I05 remains the actual working payment specification and transition protection
until an authorized, funded replacement reaches each affected person. There is
no selected affordable full-country operating bundle, new tax enactment or
permission to postpone already due claims. The retained U foundation expresses independence and equal standing; it is
not an empirical finding that
universality always improves welfare or pays for itself.

The [joined reference](../../settlement/income-investment/README.md) compares
unchanged I05, a 20% ordinary base and a full ordinary base, holding original
claims and services fixed. It first holds capital receipts/taxes fixed, then
recalculates output, profits, receipts, transfers and voluntary finance. None of
the twelve authored closures is funded. The extra ordinary payment primarily
replaces an assessed entitlement for low-resource people; it does not count as
an extra cash gain for everyone. Taxes needed to fund it may change incidence.

A separate 50 million CU productive-capital example replaces a named import
input rather than multiplying GDP by an invented productivity coefficient.
It illustrates requirements and failure, not a national energy plan, actual
investment offer or funded multiyear trajectory. Maintenance, supplier diversity,
repair and reliable private/cooperative provision can beat new public construction.
