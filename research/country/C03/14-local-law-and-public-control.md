# Local rules, elected responsibility and an executed remedy

2 November2026 · Integrated design112 · LR01–LR24 research selection, not enacted law

**Every new externally binding local general rule needs an actual majority of
full statutory council membership.** No routine negative-approval route lets a
mayor, technical office, vendor or AI impose a new condition by council silence.
Genuine fact updates inside a previously enacted rule, lawful individual decisions,
professional judgment and voluntary cooperation keep their own routes. The
national DR30/45 procedure remains national, not a municipal supervisory veto.

This is a specific local reference under C04 and EL09–12, not new local general
competence or a power to override national rights/sector law. The rule vote is
a special full-n requirement; ordinary internal council business still uses
EL11's own rule. An affirmative vote cannot cure absent authority, a secret
condition or missing consultation. If a lawful new rule fails, old independently
valid law and due rights continue; policy delay and harmful lawful legacy remain.
Late input-disposition reports retain delay liability but need not permanently
bar a later complete prospective process; source deadlines and valid input remain.
