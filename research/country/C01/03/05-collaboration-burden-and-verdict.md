# Country case: collaboration, total burden and current verdict

*Canonical CS11–CS12 historical support; [CS13–CS18](07-compulsory-burdens-and-country-disposition.md) now owns the current national choice and next action. Earlier evidence/next-task wording below retains design85 scope.*

**CS11 — Better communication is not the same as more authority.** Future media,
shared situations and human/AI collaboration may improve understanding and make
new arrangements possible. Interpretation, delegation, consent and execution
remain separate. Model agreement, data convergence and copied agents establish
neither independent evidence nor a political mandate. Diverse people may share
one source and still hold different values. Preserve unfamiliar meanings and
unsupported translations rather than silently force all cooperation into text.

FA's finite portfolio/closure and RR's narrow restoration are authored choices,
not validated by a randomized national test. A local direct-ballot study supports
examining knowledge/perceived legitimacy separately from project selection;
it cannot endorse FA's slots, referendum thresholds or global consensus.
Legitimate direct local allocation and situated mutual provision remain real
rivals to representative meetings for appropriate scopes, with rights/resources/
agenda formation still constrained. No medium is a universal sovereign procedure.
Retain current limits and reopening, not mandatory conversion to a new platform.

**CS12 — The whole stack can fail while every part looks reasonable.** Added
assessment, advocacy, source checking, procurement, independent review and survey
work consume professional/client time and can repeat intimate testimony. Their
marginal protection must be compared with displaced care, delay, exclusion and a
simpler route. Reuse valid purpose-limited underlying facts/support where lawful,
but final judgment, actual conflicts, new material events and changed proposals
need fresh review. A shared form cannot merge distinct consent or authorities.

Use existing sector/Assembly/NP capacity accounts, not a new burden regulator.
Compare actual complete journeys including nonusers, failed referrals, private
participation costs and simultaneous service/election/court duties. Preserve
voluntary coordinator/assistance and multiple safe entrances. Simplify where
rights are protected at least as well; no aggregate cost score waives a person's
legal protection. Successful artifacts and procedural completion are neither
qualified staffing nor actual repair, and unknown hidden costs remain unknown.

**Current verdict.** Retain the democratic/plural/essential-access/NP country as
a provisional design reference with the audit, outreach and retained-capability
corrections. No whole-country winner, globally dominating design or validated
numeric constitution is established. Read studies illuminate some mechanisms;
justice, status, ecology, national production, future media and their combined
transition remain incompletely evidenced. Preserve this asymmetry rather than
spread one study's favorable result across the country.

This supplies an objective-wide written comparative argument; it does not close
the full-duty numerical material package or all broader evidence gaps. Next
complete a context-explicit quantity/source/transition comparison using the
current requirement recipes, or obtain discriminating evidence for a consequential
choice that could displace the reference. Actual harmful events/contradictions
take priority. Do not automatically create another local study because one
paper's final paragraph suggests it. Reopen on affected objections, burden,
failed control/receiving, new source evidence or a stronger complete rival.
