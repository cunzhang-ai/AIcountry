# Denmark. One aggregate surplus can hide a specific training gap

A September 2025 record by the capital-region council of Local Government
Denmark reports agreements made by municipalities in 2024. The Ministry's
June 2021 five-year agreement identifies the two annual placement targets.
Subtract the stated target from the reported contract count for each
stream. This compares the measures as published by the institutions,
without certifying realized coverage of all regional or private providers.

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Social and health assistant | 5,490 | 6,000 | −510 |
| Social and health helper | 4,468 | 3,000 | +1,468 |
| Combined | 9,958 | 9,000 | +958 |

The positive combined count therefore coexisted with an assistant-stream
gap. No substitution rate between the roles follows. The Danish ministry
also reported 55% assistant and 43% helper dropout for entrants in **2018**,
a different cohort and measure. The figures establish that cohort outcomes
are separately material. Applying those rates to the 2024 agreements
would mix denominators, periods and population. The available source here
does not link any particular 2024 agreement to subsequent shifts.
[2024 political agreement, printed p. 1](https://www.uvm.dk/-/media/filer/uvm/aktuelt/pdf24/jan/240126-endelig-version-aftaletekst-om-sosu-uddannelserne--tilgaengeliggjort-version-.pdf)

Denmark's 2024 political agreement separately funds vocational school and
municipal or regional supervisor measures. The ministry identifies an
annual DKK 34.8 million allocation for professionalizing supervisors
in 2025–2028, including 11.7 million for state education costs and a
23.1 million municipal and regional application pool. Those allocations
are neither observed spending nor verified cover for each supervised shift.
The separate later tripartite agreement sets 2027–2031 placement targets
of 6,500 assistants and 4,500 helpers per year. Treat these as future
commitments in that agreement, independent of 2024 reported agreements.

Keep this stream evidence separate from the project's 2025 general-government
and non-government national accounts. Source coverage, prices, training
periods, employment and service acceptance need a new join. The comparison
supplies no quantified AIcountry recruitment yield or available national
staffing margin.
