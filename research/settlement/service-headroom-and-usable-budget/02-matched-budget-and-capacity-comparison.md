# Compare three constraints before releasing the same protected work

The unit amounts, available staff and dates here are authored. A
local authority needs four units of protected work by day 30. Each
requires one lawful spending unit, one available cash unit and
one qualified, separately paid and accepted unit of capacity.
The other protected duties and independent control are fully
reserved in the case and cannot supply any margin. There is a
three-unit optional improvement, with no binding claim yet.

| Verified position by day 30 | Units | Scope |
|---|---:|---|
| Service ceiling room under the applicable law | 5 | Excludes another locality's existing allocation |
| Cash authorized and available for this protected use | 2 | Another 2 from the same authorized grant is due on day 45 |
| Qualified staffed work with actual acceptance | 3 | A fourth unit can move from funded optional work with no other due function exposed |
| Restricted capital cash | 5 | Outside the lawful purpose and ceiling of this case |

Compare the same protected work.

| Response | What can reach the service by day 30 |
|---|---|
| Allocate existing ceiling room only | Up to two units. The cash is still short by two. |
| Lawfully advance the already scheduled two units of grant | Up to three. One qualified unit is still missing. |
| Advance the two units and reassign the qualified optional unit within actual law | All four can qualify if the responsible service accepts. One unit of ceiling room remains. |
| Claim the separate capital balance as service money | No additional eligible cash or staffed service results. |

The matched option uses the existing grant earlier with no increase
in cumulative authorized funding. The day 45 balance cannot also pay
a new service. Show the reduction in optional work and obtain actual
acceptance of the replacement. The original wider optional start does
not qualify on the remaining ceiling, cash or staff.

Now assume a two-unit budget revision in the same service class
raises counted net service spending because one authorized receipt
falls. An equal reduction in a separate state payment makes the
change cash-neutral in the case. Applicable law treats the latter
adjustment outside the service ceiling perimeter. The verified
room is now three even though the advance still leaves four cash
and all four qualified units could work. A lawful additional unit
of ceiling space, or a separately authorized and funded route that
actually uses another valid scope, is needed for the fourth unit.
A relabelled municipal invoice would not supply either.
If neither route qualifies, report the unmet service and act on
the applicable existing interim duty without extending its deadline.
These are not measured municipal amounts, actual staffing or
a test of Danish accounting rules.
