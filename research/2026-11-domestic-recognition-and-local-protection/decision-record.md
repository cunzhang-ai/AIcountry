# Design401 decision. Recognize a product claim and decide site risk separately

## Baseline and scope

This AI-assisted research selection revises [Design400](predecessor-design-400/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md)
under the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).
The baseline decentralizes ordinary local decisions, provides an elected route
for material interlocal effects and carries professional competence across
localities. It leaves product evidence and receiving conditions insufficiently
distinguished. This choice concerns covered domestic product claims. It affects
local residents and workers, suppliers, laboratories, operators, receiving councils
and authorities responsible for continuing essential services.

## Alternatives and selection

Separate full tests retain local assessment but repeat evidence even when the
same claim, test and risk have already been resolved. Uniform national control
makes entry predictable at the cost of weakening the receiving area's ability to
respond to site-specific conditions. Automatic origin recognition makes entry
easier but lets the originating authority's tolerance of risks or a private
certifier's findings determine receiving outcomes too broadly. Select a positive
common scope with portable product evidence and separate lawful local decisions.
Retain the option of stricter proportionate local conditions and nationwide
correction for credible common defects.

The strongest objections concern harmful standards-shopping, local protectionism
disguised as site inspection, correlated failures of recognized laboratories, and
unequal capacity to enforce a nominally common rule. Positive scope, common
qualification, contestable local reasons, prompt batch warnings and separately
funded review address those mechanisms in the proposed text. A failure of those
controls is reason to restrict or defer a class. No independent dissent record or
actual affected-party participation was obtained.

## Authority and continuity

The elected Assembly sets scope, harm thresholds, appropriations and sector
delegations. Elected councils retain ordinary authority over lawful local
conditions. The public can contest either route through elected representation
and ordinary rights review. Public officers responsible under prior law retain
adjudication and enforcement. Laboratories and software have evidentiary roles.
Independent review must be able to reverse an unlawful restriction and have the
order executed. Implementation must verify appointment, funding, evidence access
and replacement across those chains. No appointment, review budget or backup
supplier has been secured.

Consumers of the change include territorial authority, product safety, licensing,
public purchasing and essential service delivery. Keep current legal permissions,
service duties, protected data, complaints and remedies through any transition.
Adopt covered claims and effective dates prospectively through the authorized
legislative process. A late complaint or new incident may invalidate previously
portable evidence even if the product documentation is unchanged.
