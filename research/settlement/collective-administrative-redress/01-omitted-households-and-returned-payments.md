# Omitted households, returned payments and a late remedy

This constructed comparison exercises
[LX11–LX16](../../country/C11/12/04-proactive-redress-and-missing-cases.md).
A public programme establishes that a version of its routine rules
suppressed legally due supplemental payments. Day 0 is the common
finding. Its operating list contains 100 people. Independent intake
and prior award records provide 25 people with five reliable overlaps.
The known set is 120 people. It is a lower bound. People whose intake
left no retained record can come forward through a funded offline route.
Set the owner by working day 2 and publish the search and execution plan
by working day 10. Apply any earlier duties immediately.

At the first funded review checkpoint, 15 people have established,
unpaid and immediately payable claims of 50 each. Eight appear in the
operating list and seven are found only in the independent records.
The office also identifies two deceased recipients with established
amounts of 50 each whose lawful estate representatives are still to
be verified, and four cases with material facts unresolved. Five
people have separate potential recovery cases, including one of the
people with an established supplemental payment. This creates no credit
against that payment.

| Response | Verified payable principal sent | Received at first checkpoint | Known immediate shortfall |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Use only the operational list | 400 | 400 | 350 outside the batch |
| Check the independent records and send 15 payments | 750 | 600 | 150 returned |
| Use the paid alternative for three returned items | 750 principal in total | 700 after two replacements arrive | 50 remains open |

The scheduled payment source has 850, including 100 reserved for the
two unresolved estate recipients. A separate paid execution and contact
source covers the check and rerouting. Those sources cannot be used as
proof of receipt. Three initial payment instructions return, all for
people outside the operating list. The lawful payer verifies nonsettlement
before replacements. Two people receive replacements and the third is
still unreachable. Track instructions, actual settlement and continuing
work separately. The estate amounts are excluded from the immediate
delivery denominator until authority to receive is proved, while
remaining visible as known reserved obligations.

The amount payable to the person with a separate alleged debt remains
50. Do not net it against an unrelated claim. For the four unresolved
cases preserve dates and independent review and pay any separately
proved part. A correct new programme release leaves the failed
receipt, open estate claims and possible omitted people in the
account. The [arithmetic check](verify-collective-redress.py) reproduces
scope, returned payments and outstanding principal. Real proof,
appropriations, privacy and receipt remain to be verified.
