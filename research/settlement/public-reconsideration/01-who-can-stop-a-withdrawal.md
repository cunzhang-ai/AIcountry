# Who can stop the loss of essential access?

*Design519 · Institutional comparison and constructed decision*

The [selected national opening](../../FIRST-ROUND-SETTLEMENT.md) uses existing
lawful democratic procedures. The prospective [public confirmation
option](../../country/C03/16/06-public-confirmation-of-service-withdrawal.md)
addresses a different question. After a capable parliamentary process,
can voters reject a chosen future withdrawal of the last usable independent
essential-service route? Keep the ordinary initiative for policy origination,
rights remedies for legal breaches, and separate constitutional adoption
for a binding confirmation power.

## A choice in which the national designs differ

Assume an Assembly considers retiring independent assisted receipt for an
existing benefit. The ordinary route and proposed substitute share a single
custodian. One group can use neither if that controller fails. Operational
evidence establishes the dependence. In this constructed polity the statute
could nevertheless do this prospectively without breaching a higher law.
If actual higher law requires a usable alternate, its remedy applies
immediately regardless of any consultation or vote.

- **Strong repair under existing law.** Fund assisted petition intake, an
  independent review, an exact public alternative and a recorded elected
  vote. If the Assembly retains a genuinely independent alternate, this
  achieves protected access. Credit it fully. A court does not exchange a
  lawful policy choice for its own.
- **Selected prospective confirmation.** If the Assembly insists on
  withdrawal and valid requests qualify, an electorate can reject the
  unchanged operative clause before it binds. If the Assembly instead
  preserves an independently usable route, use ordinary law and allocate
  no causal gain to an unused ballot. A confirmed withdrawal may still
  leave serious unmet needs. Report them separately and apply existing
  rights; public assent does not make a service usable.
- **Broader Swiss-style challenge.** An independently authorized referendum
  could submit a wider class of laws to direct voter choice. Price
  election staff, enforcement, campaigns, withheld reforms and calendar
  collisions on the same dates as the narrower option. A rights-protected,
  independently staffed broader scheme remains a constitutional rival.

For the constructed comparison there are 1,250,001 eligible people at
opening. One percent rounded up is 12,501. With 12,501 valid requests,
the poll qualifies. With 12,500 it does not. A report of more accounts,
a petition on a different text or endorsement by a replicated agent does
not supply the missing person. With 200,000 votes each way the tie leaves
the prior rule. With 200,001 for confirmation and 200,000 against, the
withdrawal proceeds only when its other lawful conditions are satisfied.
A failure to reach a final lawful result by day 180 keeps the prior
protection under the proposed lapse rule.

Assume maintaining the existing alternate costs three units in each of
two windows, protected electoral operations cost two additional units in
the second and the payer can use three then five. The option fits only
if the second window includes genuinely independent staff for both
functions. If the two units arrive late or the sole interpreter is
already committed to court, keep the original service and independent
court obligations on their clocks. The closure cannot start by default.
For a valid early revised Act that retains the independently usable
alternate, ordinary repair uses three per window and does not incur the
optional poll cost. The figures are illustrative and exclude no relevant
work within this fixture; real costing must include source checks,
appeal, any permissible urgent relief and paid succession. Carry due
claims and contracts across any outcome.

## What would count as an improvement

Compare the strongest lawful repaired incumbent with the new authority
on the same service, original cohort, rights, useful deadlines and
resources. Credit the binding option only for protection the stronger
ordinary route could not produce on the same record. Include people
outside the ordinary channel, timely actual service, affected people's
ability to object, alternative campaign reach, full public and household
cost and follow-up. If the outcomes and effective public control are
equivalent through the next funded call, keep the simpler incumbent for
that scope. Publish any unresolved choice between direct final control
and retaining elected final responsibility as a value judgment.

Before seeking actual adoption, specify the authoritative source, exact
coverage, court forum and staged public choice with real affected
participants. Then independently cost the original and funded fallback
under coincident voting, litigation and service demand. A real political
judgment and subsequent outcomes remain separate from the constructed
comparison.
