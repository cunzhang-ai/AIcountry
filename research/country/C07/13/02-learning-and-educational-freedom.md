# S05–S08: a public learning offer with several legitimate paths

*Part of [C07 service candidate](../13-public-service-entitlements.md); design 67.*

**S05 — Free foundational learning, not loyalty training.** From the sixth
to eighteenth birthday, each learner has a free foundational place with
qualified teaching, materials, meals needed for usable attendance, travel,
language/disability support and a safe receiving school. The learning duty
belongs to responsible adults/public authorities with the child's voice
and agency protected; lack of a service or poverty is not a child's offense.
The core covers literacy, numeracy, science, history, civic/legal understanding,
arts, health, ecological knowledge and critical digital/AI understanding.
No party, faith, employer or platform chooses the child's political loyalty.

The default public offer is 39 weeks and 30 supported weekly hours including
age-appropriate breaks, with a locally published safe calendar. Those hours
are offered capacity, not a mandatory screen/seat quota for every learner.
Protect rest, play and informal life. A different safe schedule or home,
community, mixed or future-medium route can meet equivalent learning and
safeguarding through proportionate independent assessment and appeal. Never
infer equivalent access merely from a device or remote-course registration.

**S06 — Early learning and genuine catch-up.** From age 3 to 6, offer optional
free early learning, with the reference 20 weekly hours over 39 weeks,
age-appropriate play, language, relationships and development support.
Parents and children may choose other adequate arrangements; public care
and disability support remain separate rights. Adults who missed foundational
education retain a free accessible completion route, without an age-based
expiry. Proportionate prior-learning recognition avoids forcing repeated
instruction and must remain challengeable rather than automated exclusion.

**S07 — A first qualifying pathway and honest limits.** A person completing
foundational learning can seek a publicly financed first vocational,
apprenticeship or advanced-learning pathway with suitable entry/preparatory
support. Guarantee an accessible meaningful route, not every preferred
institution, field or course on demand. Publish prerequisites, places,
selection reasons, costs, recognition and appeal; a scarce specialty needs
fair selection and a funded alternative rather than a fictive place. [AL01–AL20](../22-lifelong-learning-and-renewal.md)
adds person-initiated meaningful qualifying renewal alongside N's paid contracts;
its64-hour continuing offer does not cap a first/approved renewal pathway. A credential is not a job guarantee or final professional license.

**S08 — Local and professional freedom with independent correction.** Elected
local bodies protect language/cultural choice within national equal rights;
teachers and subject communities revise instruction with professional freedom.
Learners/families can challenge inaccuracy, exclusion, discipline or political
indoctrination. A binding curriculum change follows authorized public law,
not a minister/vendor's software update. Multiple learning/wellbeing outcomes,
including excluded and departing learners, guide correction; no single test
or AI profile determines worth, funding, civic status or career allocation.
Optional tutoring and future media retain protected records, explainable
loss/limits, human assistance and a usable non-AI alternative.

[HC](../../C11/05-retained-human-capability.md) applies to publicly relied-on AI
learning support. Offered hours, credentials and completed AI-assisted work
do not establish independent understanding/transfer; preserve safe alternatives,
access accommodations and actual teacher/reviewer capability.

[ED school power](../21-public-learning-and-school-power.md) supplies actual
employer/leadership/inspection, learner/teacher voice, equal funded admission
and bounded civil site restrictions with continuous learning/ordinary review.
S05–S08 ages,39/30 and20-hour offered capacity/first-path rights persist; no
new attendance crime, teacher-contact quota or compulsory learning medium.

[XR04](../../C04/09/01-protected-intake-and-actual-handover.md) protects a
displaced child's existing usable school or a funded provisional receiving
path. Keep S05 support and independent correction while records or the
payer are disputed.

[ED21–ED28](../21/05-missing-learning-and-effective-receiving.md) follows
actual loss and usable teaching even when a learner remains on the roll.
It preserves earlier S05 due times and ED safety and remedy rules.
