# A common election fiscal baseline and first-week operating account

Design347 · Scoped finding for [country completion](../../DESIGN-COMPLETENESS.md)

[HT18–HT23](../../country/C03/19/05-election-fiscal-baseline-and-first-week.md)
selects a common continuing-law baseline, independently published
changes and a first-week account tied to actual appointment.

The [first-week comparison](../../settlement/election-fiscal-baseline/01-first-week-cash-and-late-liability.md)
checks a late disclosure, restricted balances, a disputed call and
continuing duties. Actual access, budget control and delivery are open.
