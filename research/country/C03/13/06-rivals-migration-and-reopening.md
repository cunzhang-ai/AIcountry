*Part of [C03/13](../13-equal-franchise-and-usable-elections.md); design111 candidate.*

**EL21 — Reject the current mixed count as the preferred working reference.**
Its independent territorial seats can reward geographic personal mandates and
let a local independent win without a national organization. They also make a
sensitive backing/control classification change N−I and the national seat mix;
list-backed first-place candidates may not sit. Diaspora/missing-home assignment
and a second national track add unresolved equality/access questions. The new
single national PR count makes all nominees face one seat rule; finance control
still matters, but not as a personal-seat entitlement exception.

This is a value/architecture selection informed by exact counterexamples, not
an empirical finding that proportional lists always govern better. National
party visibility, concentrated local minorities, independent organizing, small
preference-active groups and famous people can still dominate or lose out.
Local whole-area PR also sacrifices ward guarantees. Protected Indigenous/
territorial rights and geographic service/access do not vanish with the seat map.
Retain mixed, regional/multi-member transferable-vote and closed/flexible-list
rivals with their own count, map, support, burden and takeover risks. More simple
ballot fields or successful count code alone does not settle public control.
