# Receive early signals and investigate without confusing learning with guilt

*TS07–TS08; part of [technical safety](../22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md).*

**TS07 — Urgent signals arrive before complete certainty.** A scheduled operator
must give immediate protective warning/rescue information where serious harm is
ongoing or imminent. Notify the competent sector receiver as soon as practicable,
no later than 24 actual hours after responsible operational knowledge of a death,
serious injury, serious contamination or critical barrier failure with credible
potential for those outcomes in its scheduled activity. This proposed outer bound
is no permission to withhold earlier useful warning or wait for proven causation.

Provide known facts, actual time, uncertainty and safe follow-up; update material
corrections. Preserve relevant original logs, versions, maintenance and contrary
accounts under lawful proportionate retention, privacy and independent access.
Do not require a raw public clinical file or a speculative final diagnosis.
Accessible third-party/anonymous/worker reports need no certification/account.
Safeguard good-faith reporting and source challenge; false findings and retaliation
receive distinct review. Deliberate fabrication or destruction needs actual proof.

P silence, privilege and non-self-incrimination remain; no reporting clause
compels an incriminating personal narrative or treats requested counsel as breach.
Lawfully required contemporaneous operational records are not automatically erased
or wholly immunized by a later safety report. Disputed access uses V03/P warrants
and courts, not a technical body's self-declared criminal-search exception.

**TS08 — Independent safety learning can continue alongside justice.** For a
scheduled accident causing death/serious injury, major contamination or credible
catastrophic barrier failure, assign a competent independent investigation
function outside the implicated operator, licensing case and prosecution command.
It may be pooled regionally by lawful agreement; there is no presumption that a
new domestic office is staffed or a foreign body has accepted the task.

Investigate causal barriers, maintenance, human/system interaction, incentives
and actual emergency response, publishing protected factual reasons, uncertainty,
recommendations and the responsible recipients' reasoned responses. Families
receive safe timely factual communication. A pending criminal/security allegation
cannot silently terminate this distinct investigation or let the suspect operator
own the only source copy. A court resolves genuine evidence/privacy conflicts.

This inquiry cannot convict, waive victims' claims, dictate elected risk policy
or promise every witness blanket immunity. Independently obtained criminal/civil
evidence keeps its legal route; protected safety accounts receive judicially
controlled lawful access and no automatic public/model-training release. Record
which original observations support each conclusion and whose account is missing.
A recommendation is neither a proved offense nor completed corrective delivery.
