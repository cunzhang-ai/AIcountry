# Design312. Democratic joint-service authority

[JS01–JS05](../../country/C04/01/01-democratic-joint-service-authority.md)
requires the same exact lawful compact, recorded council approvals,
bounded commitments and verified service capacity. The two majorities
apply within that scope. Independent courts and elected bodies keep
their distinct powers. Taxes, nonmember burdens and territorial
authority require their own law.

Carry worker pay, contracts, resident duties, pending remedies,
funded assets and record custody through expiry or withdrawal.
The [decision record](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-democratic-joint-service-authority/decision-record.md)
identifies the better direct, compact and contractual alternatives
to test with a real calendar and paid review.
