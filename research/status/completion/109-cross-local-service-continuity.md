# Scoped finding. Services must be accepted when a household moves

Design201 adds an operative domestic cross-local route, maintaining
actual existing duties and requiring competent host interim action.
It specifies provisional school receiving, a bounded separately
enacted payer advance, due dates, safe contact and independent
resolution. [Canonical choice](../../country/C04/09-cross-local-service-continuity.md)

The England and U.S. sources distinguish duties and a case of
interrupted care. Authored tests expose conditional recovery,
missing up-front cash, and one shared backup unable to serve two
families. Source scope, adopted authority, safe staffing and actual
outcomes remain open. [Case and evidence](../../settlement/cross-local-continuity/README.md)
