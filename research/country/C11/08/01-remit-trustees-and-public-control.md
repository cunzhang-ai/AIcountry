# PM01–PM06. Public purpose without ownership of political truth

*Design146 · [Public media](../08-public-media-and-editorial-independence.md) · Proposed statute, not an actual appointment.*

**PM01 — A concrete service.** Establish a non-distributing statutory public body,
without private voting shares or a broadcast/news monopoly. Offer a source-checked
accessible national/local public-interest bulletin each day and a weekly public-
affairs/investigative programme, including honest ongoing work where new findings
are not established. No scandal/output quota may manufacture claims. Provide
corrections/archive access, actual needed language/disability formats and a usable
non-internet route through audio/community access/print where necessary. Cost the
actual geography, need and safe distribution before claiming those offers delivered.
Everyone may receive the service without political membership, a fee or AI account.
No official national worldview, government approval or loyalty to AIcountry is required.

**PM02 — Purpose and governance are not daily editing.** Assembly statute fixes remit,
source, ownership, rights and correction; six trustees govern budgets, appointments,
workplace safeguards and statutory delivery. No minister, trustee, donor, advertiser,
valuer of audience metrics or AI may order a particular editorial conclusion,
suppress a lawful investigation or select interviewees for partisan advantage.
The body controls only actual owned/contracted assets/rights under ordinary law;
public ownership gives no privilege to seize third-party content, source or audience.
Publish statutory service plans and material control/funding conflicts, preserve
safe source privacy and distinguish governance records from confidential journalism.

**PM03 — Six seats with parity, not a ministerial owner bloc.** Three seats are elected
by the actual workforce under OE13/WL's eligible-worker roll, secret proportional
list/person-preference/candidate-capacity method and genuine one-worker/one-ballot.
Citizenship, union membership or a platform is no worker gate. Willing qualified
independent nominees may stand; no manager selects an artificial workforce. The
other three public-purpose seats are one appointed by **ceil(3N/5) actual assents
of the positive full statutory Assembly N**, and two by separate paid public panels.
The same individual holds only one seat; no source may fill another's vacancy.
Always-three worker seats meet OE parity and exceed its smaller-enterprise minimum.
This sector statute expressly replaces OE20 ministerial owner-side selection here.

All nominees must be adults with actual supported ability for the job and disclosed
interests, without an ideology/legacy-media-credential test. Current ministers,
legislators, party executives or contestant campaign managers, and persons within
two years of leaving those posts, cannot be trustee/chief/complaints editor.
Ordinary party membership, union activity, criticism and lawful beliefs remain free.
An executive/beneficial controller of a competing media firm, paid distribution
supplier or material contractor cannot hold those offices while that conflict persists;
ordinary small diversified holdings use disclosed proportionate conflict rules.
Public-media employees retain their represented workplace interest openly.

**PM04 — A real temporary public selection, not copied authority.** Each public-panel
seat uses a fresh36-person panel of voluntary actual adult national electors, with
**27 present and24 actual assents** for its primary and separately for its reserve.
Express law authorizes Clerk/C04 recruitment staff to draw from the actual lawful
roll using CJ07's prepublished auditable access/language/residence/age-strata method,
with paid time, care/travel/access and plural independent advice. This reuses the
method, not the judges, an existing jury, its mandate or EA's ungranted authority.
No compulsory cultural label or political-opinion profile; disclose exclusions,
nonresponse, organized contacts and shared controllers. The custodian verifies
objective criteria, cannot redraw unwanted electors or bar a lawful viewpoint.
A panel expires180days after first sitting; a fresh panel transfers no approvals.
Open nominations and hearings,21-day proposed-appointment publication and direct
ordinary-court challenge preserve safe records; no plurality/default appointment.

**PM05 — Terms, votes and succession are actual.** Worker seats retain OE13's four-year
terms and20%-petition/majority-valid-vote recall, protected against owner retaliation.
The three public-purpose seats have six-year nonrenewable terms. Before inaugural
nomination, publicly draw their initial2/4/6-year slots; afterward each is six years.
Public sources separately approve a same-seat reserve for only the unexpired term;
a reserve cannot extend expiry or hold two seats. Worker vacancy uses OE13's actual
list/assisted-election route. No reserve appears from an allegation alone.

A board disposition requires **five lawful members participating in that item and four actually
assenting to that exact disposition of the fixed six**. Vacancies/recusals/copies
do not lower either count; recused persons do not count toward that item's quorum.
Four votes necessarily cross worker/public classes,
without any named-source veto or chair casting vote. Choose an administrative chair
by that vote, at most two years and never beyond their seat; failed choice leaves
only published seat-rotation meeting administration, not a new policy mandate.

**PM06 — Removal and failed selection.** Public-purpose trustees leave early only on
an independent ordinary court's proved serious misconduct, persistent material
statutory breach, corrupt conflict or inability after reasonable accommodation,
with notice, defense and appeal. Lawful disagreement/unwelcome coverage is no cause.
Any actually affected person, competent integrity investigator or one-fifth of
full Assembly may use ordinary standing/source law to seek relief; no full technical
dossier or board permission bars a report. A court's final removal is not subject
to a new political veto, and the court appoints no replacement. Worker-source recall
is separate. Start ordinary scheduled selection180days before expiry; actual sources
must continue an unresolved process, without lowering votes, renewing incumbents,
Cabinet appointees or an AI trustee. Failure remains visible with actual consequences.

OE14's missing-enterprise-organ actor cannot fill a PM seat, choose an editor or
assume public editorial policy. Any separately authorized temporary custody act
stays ministerial and bounded; no ordinary company remedy creates a media appointer.
