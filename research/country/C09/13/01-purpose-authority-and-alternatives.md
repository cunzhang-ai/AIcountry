# LA01–LA05. A real public purpose and a lawful decision

*Design145 · [Public acquisition](../13-public-acquisition-and-secure-relocation.md) · Proposed ordinary-law content, not enacted authority.*

**LA01 — Define the power and limits.** Under a valid enabling Act, acquire only
specified existing land/property rights demonstrably necessary for an identified
public project: essential networks, safe public housing/services, public mobility,
ecological restoration/protection or an expressly legislated comparable public
infrastructure function. Publish the actual service/land/ecological objective,
beneficiaries, distribution, map, duration and implementing public owner. A new
category requires primary law; private profit, tax yield, party support or an AI
score alone is not public purpose. This route does not nationalize an enterprise,
confiscate criminal proceeds, issue a patent licence, impose tax or decide
sovereignty. Each has a different actual source. It cannot appropriate disputed
collective title by declaring land vacant or changing a territorial boundary.

**LA02 — An elected authority must choose.** The competent elected local council
approves an ordinary local project and exact acquisition mandate by floor(n/2)+1
actual assents and that many present of its positive full statutory membership,
under LR, prior national law and lawful appropriation. A reserved national or
material unresolved cross-local project requires an actual Assembly primary Act
identifying project, outer map/right, public owner, source ceiling and constraints
under C04's allocation; the national majority cannot quietly seize local powers.
Existing general statutes do not let a minister choose a new acquisition purpose.
Cabinet/local career officers implement the approved bounded mandate. A developer,
contractor, AI, joint-service forum or court receives no independent taking vote.
The vote neither resolves private title nor waives collective consent/rights.

**LA03 — Compare before making displacement inevitable.** Compare retain/repair,
no project, different location/scale, negotiated purchase, lease/easement and
compulsory acquisition with the same actual service objective and rights. Count
existing relations, historical displacement, downstream/ecological duties, informal
use, business/worker effects, host-community services, maintenance and restoration,
not just cheapest registered land. A voluntary-looking sale under a credible threat
of taking retains LA safeguards; needing public assistance is no consent. State why
less intrusive feasible means fail; do not assert every cheaper option is adequate.
Genuine value conflict/nonreplaceable rights remain, not averaged away by a score.

**LA04 — Acquire no more than is needed.** Select the least intrusive adequate
right and duration: a paid easement or temporary lease where it suffices, not
automatic whole freehold. Record severance/access effects on the remainder. A
holder can require acquisition of an unusable/unsafe/inaccessible remainder where
fair repair is not feasible, with independent proof and no duplicated value.
Existing actual mineral, water, lease, security and ecological interests retain
separate sources; title does not imply every extraction/development permission.
Ordinary lawful safety/environmental regulation does not guarantee constant market
value. A substantive appropriation/exclusive public control cannot evade LA by a
regulation, contract or software label; disproportionate regulatory burdens remain
independently reviewable under B/RX without an invented universal loss indemnity.

**LA05 — No shortcut from emergency or privatization.** This selected Act creates
no generic urgent requisition, police-entry or demolition power. Rescue/temporary
safety acts retain actual separate source/compensation duties; RX declaration,
SC procurement and C06 scarcity create no missing title. Preassign qualified public
stocks/mutual aid and buy reserve access before foreseeable emergencies. A later
proposed urgent seizure must justify its own exact rights/source/capacity choice;
it is not an assumed necessary appendix. This accepts a possible emergency-access
cost rather than disguising a seizure as rescue.

Public acquisition cannot merely assemble cheap land for a favored speculative
buyer. A contracted operator has only the approved service mandate, open SC award,
actual public-use/continuity conditions and enforceable recovery on misuse. A
material changed project/private onward purpose needs a fresh elected public
choice and rights/source review, not the original headline's transferred approval.

[NR01–NR04](../15/01-title-public-choice-and-separate-powers.md) preserves this
separate mineral/land/collective source test. It adds public/common custody only
for legitimate public/actually ungranted rights, not discovery-as-acquisition;
new site/closure duties apply regardless of title without transferring it.

[LU ordinary planning](../17-development-permission-and-serviced-land.md)
confers no title, eviction, acquisition or possession power. Actual applicable
LA mandate, replacement, payment/security and independent safe possession
remain necessary where a serviced-land proposal requires public acquisition.
