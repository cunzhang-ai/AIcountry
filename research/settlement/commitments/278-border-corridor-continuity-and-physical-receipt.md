# Design365. Fund inspection, custody and physical receiving through a corridor loss

[BCR01–BCR06](../../country/C10/05/01-border-corridor-continuity-and-physical-receipt.md)
assigns physical handling, inspection, lawful storage and next-call capacity
separately. A shared inspector or facility contributes only its verified
usable slots. Faster handling does not clear a lawful hold.

The [comparison](../border-corridor-continuity/01-two-terminals-and-one-inspection-team.md)
joins first and later demand with a separately funded supplement and a lawful
reservation. Actual sources, acceptance and outcomes remain open.
