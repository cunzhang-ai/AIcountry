# MO01–MO04: scope, ordinary life and the person's choice

*Part of proposed C07/17; design 94 research candidate, not enacted law.*

**MO01 — A person-level route.** I01 ordinary residents, including children,
have the new ordinary mobility claim individually. No citizenship/franchise,
income/assets, job, household-head, sponsor, party or digital-account gate.
People outside that scope retain actual urgent, sector, return and protection
rights under their source law; mobility does not decide admission or status.
Use supported/offline proof and provisional urgent treatment during dispute.
Necessary assistance, a child's safe lawful receiver and an access companion
are provision duties, not a reason to consume another person's allocation.
The service supplies qualified assistance when no willing suitable companion
exists; it does not order family members to drive or escort.

**MO02 — Protected necessary journeys.** Provide the actually necessary route
to received health/learning/care, lawful work or self-directed livelihood,
providing chosen necessary care, court/legal remedy, voting and other compulsory
or protected public participation. Sector owners retain their own eligibility,
consent, clinical/child safety and earlier useful-time duties. The mobility
authority cannot deny transport because the destination concerns opposition,
an unpaid carer, irregular work or a lawful unfamiliar form of learning.
Necessity requires an actual task/place/time and proportionate usable options,
not a social-worth score. This is not unlimited travel to any preferred job,
provider, event or address. A reasoned dispute has direct correction and interim
protection; it cannot cancel income, care or housing or compel relocation.
Ordinary voluntary party meetings and private social occasions need no purpose
assessment when using MO03; campaign procurement keeps its separate law.

**MO03 — Two ordinary-life return journeys.** Each person may use two flexible
return journeys in each seven-day service week to a reasonable local hub or
equivalent chosen destination within the defined service area. A usable hub
combines ordinary shops/services, accessible public space and useful connections;
an isolated token stop is insufficient. No proof of the outing's purpose,
companions, belief or party is required. Offer at least two outward choices
on each day, suitable return choices allowing a two-to-six-hour outing, and
at least two evening and two weekend outward/return opportunities weekly.
A person may choose a different adequate stay length where actual service permits.
Use one published national service-week calendar across operators and localities,
displayed in usable local time; moving, extra accounts or providers cannot reset it.
A confirmed return crossing week end belongs once to its outward week; safe return
remains due and does not consume the next week's minimum. Partial calendar years
use the actual service weeks, not a presumed52-week legal year.
The first partial eligible service week carries the full two-return minimum,
not prorated away; lawful later cessation preserves a confirmed safe return
and unresolved breach/remedy. This is a normative scope choice with real cost.
These are available opportunities, not compulsory uptake, tradable credits or
an annually bankable stock. Genuine voluntary nonuse does not create cash debt.
Provider-caused loss remains a breach with correction and ordinary legal remedy.
Work, school or care cannot be assigned to consume this ordinary-life minimum.
One chosen actual trip may satisfy several legitimate purposes; count its
physical work/cost once and consume the flexible allocation only by the person's
informed choice. Necessary sector support remains independently claimable.

**MO04 — Physical receipt and a genuine alternative.** A booking, voucher,
remote link or transport payment is not arrival at a receiving place. A remote,
mobile-service or closer alternative qualifies only if the relevant duty can
lawfully be met that way, the person actually agrees and required quality,
privacy, access and receiving exist. Preserve physical alternatives and lawful
professional/court authority. Silence, poverty or missing transport is no assent.
Emergency clinical/safety/child transport keeps its qualified sector route;
ordinary dispatch, cash or an unqualified vehicle cannot substitute for it.
