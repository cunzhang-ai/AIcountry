# C04. Accountable administration and services: Civil service and delegated administration

*Part of [C04. Accountable administration and services](../03-administration-and-services.md); integrated design 103 research, not enacted law.*

## Accountable administration

Adopt national guarantees and local funding responsibilities together. The national
authority remains responsible for arranging a lawful response when a locality cannot
meet an essential guarantee. A grant shortfall cannot silently remove the person's
claim or force another locality to absorb it without resources. The local authority
retains its duties and an accessible route to contest the funding decision.

An essential-service fallback requires prior law, not a cabinet declaration that
local government has failed. The statute sets observable non-delivery and danger
triggers, notice and a chance to cure except where urgent protection cannot wait,
and prompt independent review available to residents and the locality. A named
sector authority may temporarily use only the staff, records, facilities and
preauthorized funds needed to keep the guarantee running. Its powers have a
published scope and time limit, respect worker rights, privacy and property due
process, and require public accounting and a route back to local control. The
authority reports whether the cause was unlawful refusal, missing staff or
inadequate national funding;
intervention cannot manufacture capacity or displace a council's unrelated powers.
Professional clinical and teaching judgments remain governed by their own law.

A professional civil service works for lawful institutions rather than a party or
vendor. Cabinet directs lawful policy and may appoint a limited, disclosed set of
political advisers; ordinary and senior operational posts use open, job-related
competition, accessible entry, reasoned selection and appeal. Cabinet may choose
a senior manager from a qualified public shortlist for a defined mandate, with
published reasons and conflicts. Acting appointments expire under law and cannot
be serially renewed to bypass the competition. Career protection does not prevent
proportionate discipline, reassignment or dismissal for proved misconduct or
performance failure with due process. Professional staff can give candid advice,
then must execute a lawful elected decision; they cannot acquire a policy veto by
controlling staffing, files or implementation dates. A new cabinet cannot purge
career staff for their vote, lawful dissent or service under its predecessor.

Under the enacted delegation, a named public issuer publishes an exact proposed
general rule, source clause, affected scope, reasons, evidence, cost and
alternatives through accessible channels. It applies the [FA finite-attention rule](../../C12/03-finite-attention-and-lawful-closure.md)
for major national consultation, distinguishes examined from unexamined material
objections, preserves separately required legal consideration, and discloses
common sponsorship and records reasons for the final text. An authenticated
register supplies the operative version, start and end dates, higher-law
authority and court or Assembly correction. An internal manual, vendor term,
external standard or AI threshold with general binding effect must follow
that rule's legal route; relabeling it as implementation does not enlarge
executive power. Cabinet can direct lawful administration, while professional
and local actors retain their statutory judgments. A regulation cannot
silently shift an unfunded national duty to a locality or make a private
operator the only route to an entitlement.

[TS03/TS12](../../C08/22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md) now
requires freely accessible exact technical versions, declared units/methods/
uncertainty, bounded expert detail and elected control of material risk/access
changes. Assignment, sources and funds must permit actual dissent; shared
controllers or different model labels cannot establish independent review.

[DR01–DR24](../../C03/11-delegated-rules-and-effective-public-control.md) supplies
the current positive national process: every ordinary draft gets accessible
consultation, followed by a separate final30/45-day scrutiny/making window;
material/called rules need actual full-N approval. The Assembly Clerk's paid
registrar, named issuer/records officer, protected Treasury judgment payer and
expressly scoped B19/B18 executor have separate duties. A nonmaterial operative
legacy amendment uses the routine route; no guidance/software/foreign version
can evade it. Existing professional/local statutory judgments remain distinct.


[LR local general law](../../C03/14-local-law-and-public-control.md) now separates
local general-rule approval from lawful fixed-criteria input/individual acts.
Its protected Clerk, local implementing/records officers, municipal judgment
payer and expressly scoped external civil execution need actual source/paid
independence; no national policy clearance, local self-appropriation or AI veto.

[OJ](../../C08/25-ordinary-justice-and-public-prosecution.md) now expressly assigns
ordinary judicial selection/status, Court Service Registrar and prosecution/case-
officer roles. C04 merit safeguards apply where named, not a Cabinet power to
appoint judges or direct charges. Shared support has separate cases/records/pay;
OJ new180-day law does not restart existing DR/LR/work/service source duties.

[PO154 civilian enforcement](../../C08/27-civilian-enforcement-and-public-control.md)
now assigns police/external-investigation leadership and corrections career
source. PO's expressly named head nomination/confirmation/status terms are not
bypassed by C04's generic Cabinet shortlist power. Actual ordinary staff merit,
professional duties/source, protected dissent and court challenge remain.

[DF civilian defense](../../C10/12-civilian-defense-and-service.md) uses C04
open qualified careers/public shortlist for Chief and state personnel, with
Cabinet-replaceable command assignment and separately protected employment.
This is no PO/CJ protected-term guardian or Cabinet power over a court/
investigator; actual DF source/appointment/paid transition needs its adopting Act.

[RC public rescue roles](../../C10/13/01-public-employer-and-bounded-leadership.md)
uses EL11 local executive/C04 qualified Director and Cabinet career coordinator,
protected staff/assignment review and actual national limited guarantee fallback.
Council/Assembly source scrutiny, lawful joint service, no caretaker patronage
or partisan purge, actual paid standby and original claim successors persist.

[PSV01–PSV07](05-career-service-and-democratic-direction.md) supplies senior
merit selection, functional-change review and paid lawful execution.
