# C11. Public rules and verifiable implementation

*LX01–LX23 · Proposed ordinary implementation of A1 and A3.*

**A person can trace a consequential public decision to the rule and
evidence that actually governed it.** A legal version, the software or
manual procedure implementing it, an individual's decision and a received
remedy are distinct. Keep authoritative law publicly usable. Require
verified implementations for material reliance and repair the people
affected by a common error, including those who never complained.

1. [Authority, interpretation and independent checks](12/01-authority-interpretation-and-tests.md)
   defines what an implementation may do and where it must refer a decision.
2. [Release, individual trace and correction](12/02-release-trace-and-correction.md)
   preserves the legal time, case records and actual delivery through errors.
3. [Public control, continuity and revision](12/03-public-control-and-succession.md)
   assigns independent scrutiny, paid fallback and migration.
4. [Proactive redress and missing cases](12/04-proactive-redress-and-missing-cases.md)
   assigns a dated search, verified release and continuing responsibility for failed receipts.
5. [Replaceable public standards](12/05-public-standards-and-replaceable-implementation.md)
   requires independent implementation, preserved meaning and a paid version transition.
