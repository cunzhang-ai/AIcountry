# Design444. Protect the budget of independent review

Design443 is the pinned predecessor. DP36 already qualifies released
hours and DP79 already requires independently funded evaluation.
T95–T99 address the remaining budget choice when an adverse result
would both erase projected savings and increase demand for review.
The revision used AI assistance and parallel examination.

Compare strong ordinary service, bounded AI with separately funded
evaluation and failure response, and broad reliance coupled to a
review reduction. The latter can claim early gains while starving
its own correction route. A separately funded alternate costs
real money and can still share the same personnel. Select bounded
reliance in the worked case only with an accepting independently
funded alternate. Otherwise retain ordinary service. Preserve
ordinary approvals, pay, rights and earlier legal dates. A
material proposed cut needs an explicit elected disposition under
existing law. Technical results do not decide the budget.

[Sources](sources.md) document bounded external evaluations.
The worked comparison is constructed, with no actual appropriation,
independent qualified review, adoption or affected participation.
Keep protected records, independent access and actual remedies
through handover. Test one real joint service, original appeal
calendar and paid alternate. Measure observed staff redeployment,
case receipt, reviewer control and the full costs in normal and
adverse periods. Reopen on unreliable funding, shared control,
missed deadlines or a simpler lawful route.
