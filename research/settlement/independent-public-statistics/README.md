# A service budget when the coverage series breaks

*Design300 · Constructed comparison. Counts, costs and dates are assumptions.*

A local accessible booking service serves 1,000 people eligible for
the same public offer in each period. The earlier comparable
estimate put unmet need at 90 of 1,000. A digital administrative
series in the current period observes 800 and records 48 of those
with unmet need. The known eligible population of 1,000 is separately
qualified for this example. The missing 200 include people who use
assisted channels or do not appear in the online workflow. The
department wishes to retire a discretionary assisted route and
redirect its coming-period budget. There is a lawful route to retain
it for the next period. Existing service claims continue in every
option.

Before seeing the data the elected body set an evidence condition
for the narrower closure motion. The upper bound of the qualified
all-eligible unmet-need rate must be no more than 8% before the
budget vote on day 4. Meeting the condition would only allow that
motion to proceed. Accessibility duties and other legal constraints
still apply. The statistical release is due on day 0.

| Choice | Statistical cost units | Evidence usable by day 4 | Budget result |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Adopt the dashboard at face value | 1 | 48 of 800, or 6%, only among the observed | Would wrongly qualify closure by replacing the denominator |
| Publish the observed count and coverage notice | 1 | Between 48 and 248 of 1,000, or 4.8% to 24.8% | The closure condition is unresolved. Use the lawful retention motion |
| Fund the release and an independent offline coverage check | 5 | The assumed defensible range of 34 to 44 unmet cases among the 200 yields 8.2% to 9.2% | The closure motion fails its evidence condition. Compare retaining or repairing through their own votes |

The offline check reaches the review office on day 3. It does not
receive personal service decisions or merge records into a common
identity file. Its range stands for a qualified range in this
constructed case. A real survey would have to earn it through
independent frame checks, error assessment and privacy review.
These are deterministic bounds in the scenario, not computed
confidence intervals. The 8.2% to 9.2% range still includes the
earlier 9%, so a rise or fall in unmet need remains unresolved.

With four statistical cost units, buy the one-unit release, publish
the full coverage gap and preserve the funded retention choice.
If the independent check arrives on day 5 or shares the compromised
collection channel, the day 4 decision can use only the limited
day 0 evidence. A later qualified result may inform the next
authorized decision. If a fresh, timely qualified check instead
bounds total unmet need at or below 8%, the closure motion can be
considered under the same procedure. No estimate supplies an
appropriation or relieves a due service by itself. Fund the actual
continuation separately and record what optional uses wait.
