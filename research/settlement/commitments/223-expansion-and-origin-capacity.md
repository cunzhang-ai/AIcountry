# Design310. Expansion and origin capacity

The [national expansion rules](../../country/C14/03/02-expansion-with-origin-and-receiving-capacity.md)
put trial and later cohort obligations beside actual origin work and
funded interim access. Lawful existing dates, contracts and remedies
continue. Free movement and separate elected authority apply to any
backfill or staffing agreement. The later operator needs verified
acceptance, actual source and paid transition. A success measure
cannot create money, transfer authority or set a legal start.

The [decision record](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-service-expansion-and-origin-capacity/decision-record.md)
preserves the rivals, limits of the observed teaching study, the authored
care allocation and unresolved operational and adoption conditions.
