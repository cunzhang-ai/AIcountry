## Rival diagnoses and a single failure path

Three diagnoses demand different repairs. **Bad appointments** suggests changing who nominates and removes watchdogs. **Material choke points** suggests secure funding, independent records and alternative communications. **Defective coercive succession** suggests that valid orders may never be executed because security and treasury personnel recognize the incumbent rather than the lawful office. More seats or a higher nomination threshold addresses only the first, and can make vacancies easier to engineer. Data and interviews from actual appointees, election offices, local administrations, courts, media operators and security staff would be needed to distinguish which dependency dominates.

Authored stress sequence: the governing coalition helps select nominal opposition representatives and professional nominees, defers a court vacancy, cuts legal-aid and election operations during a fiscal dispute, routes voter and party records through a common vendor, dominates public communication, and directs security staff to deny investigators access. At the fixed election, local counts and challenges are delayed. The coalition asserts that no successor is certified and continues to exercise ordinary policy powers. Under the existing design, the scheduled mandate ends, caretaker powers narrow, the preadopted essential-obligations schedule continues, ordinary courts may protect individual claims, and an unresolved count does **not** install either side. Yet the remedy still needs accessible evidence, a competent and funded forum, payment and communication channels, and personnel able to execute lawful custody and transfer orders. If all are under one controller, a formal judgment cannot restore power by itself.

This traces five control points, not five new offices: **appointment** of reviewers and electoral staff; **funding** of their staff, counsel, public media and physical polling; **information** in rolls, counts, case files, public notices and digital credentials; **enforcement** by treasury, civil servants and civilian security; and **continuity** when a term or bridge appropriation ends. A coalition may capture these cumulatively through individually legal choices. Conversely, disagreement over appointments or budgets alone is not proof of unlawful obstruction, and a regulator must not classify peaceful critics as fake opposition merely because they vote together.

The structural alternative is not a new unelected power to choose policy, identify the winner or extend the previous government. It is a continuity arrangement for **evidence, access and remedy**. The elected Assembly still sets laws and money, courts still decide cases within competence, local election officers and courts have no authority to invent a national result, and civilian security may enforce only valid legal orders. No model permits a security commander, vendor, AI system or party to settle a disputed election by force or an administrative timeout.

## Two proposed changes for C03 and C14

**1. Select a preauthorized, distributed electoral-rights operating circuit.** In C03, make independence mean that an affected voter or candidate can preserve a challenge and obtain evidence, a competent hearing and an executable order when the national electoral leadership **and** the principal digital operator do not cooperate. The actual jurisdiction should assign before the contest: local custody and public reconciliation of original poll evidence; rights-preserving access for parties, observers and courts; ordinary-court power to grant specified urgent relief when a specialist bench is vacant; protected legal assistance; direct scheduled payment for core electoral, court and audit duties; and a legal custodian of original records and credentials separate from the litigating executive. Access to sensitive data remains purpose-limited and challengeable. Appointment-source diversity counts only after common funders, nomination pools, employment ties and vendors are inspected. A reserve is usable only if eligible people, quorum, pay and access are established; a vacancy never lowers the selection rule or grants an executive an acting appointee. This integrates the continuity correction's unresolved ordinary-court fallback without silently treating it as already enacted.

**2. Make a contested-transfer exercise a C14 commencement gate.** Before calling the democracy operational, the adopting public's lawful institutions must demonstrate that a noncooperating outgoing coalition can be prevented from silently extending ordinary authority, while a disputed result is resolved by evidence rather than presumption. Exercise withheld publication, records, credentials, treasury release and security cooperation together. Record which official starts each clock, which lawful powers expire, which services continue under preadopted authority, who may file and inspect, which court can act if appointments fail, how a lawful order reaches its executor, and what happens if that executor refuses or lacks capacity. An unverified handover is a visible readiness failure. Neither a failure to certify nor the mere end of a caretaker period creates a winner, a new sovereign guardian, or authority to cancel existing rights. The exercise may reveal that an alternative constitutional model or prior institutional rebuilding is needed.

The first change reaches election, court, audit, budget, records, media and security law. It must be reconciled with C02's expanded electorate, C06's standing essential-obligations schedule, C10's civilian command and C11–C12's replaceable digital systems. The second reaches founding/transition phases and the national opening account. Existing claims, staff protections, lawful contracts, local election duties and pending cases continue until authorized successors accept them; prepositioned custody and reserve staffing cost money. These changes do not authorize a parallel police force, a permanent judicial budget set by judges, or government control of independent media.

## Support, objections and decision status

The [OSCE Copenhagen election commitments](https://odihr.osce.org/sites/default/files/f/documents/3/3/16815.html) connect genuine elections with honest counts, fair campaigning, media access and installation of the duly elected winner. The [Venice Commission electoral code](https://venice.coe.int/images/SITE%20IMAGES/Publications/Code_conduite_PREMS%20026115%20GBR.pdf) addresses impartial election administration, fundamental rights and stable rules. International IDEA's [electoral-management independence primer](https://www.idea.int/publications/catalogue/independence-electoral-management) treats financial and functional autonomy, not legal labels alone, as relevant. The [Venice Commission rule-of-law checklist](https://www.venice.coe.int/images/SITE%20IMAGES/Publications/Rule_of_Law_Check_List.pdf) includes access to justice and effective judicial decisions. These primary institutional standards support the questions asked; they do not validate this proposed distribution of functions, prove a given staffing model, or determine AIcountry's lawful constitutional form.

Serious objections remain. Separate local custody can fragment standards, create inconsistent records, expose voter privacy or empower local incumbents. Direct funding can become a hidden self-protecting appropriation; elected lawmakers must set bounded schedules and review actual cash and performance. Protected counsel and public access can be abused through frivolous litigation or disclosure of sensitive evidence; standing, proportionality, sealed review and adverse-party process are necessary. Ordinary-court emergency competence can turn many local judges into rival constitutional courts unless scope, appeal and conflict rules are fixed beforehand. Security staff may refuse every lawful transfer order; no written succession rule can guarantee obedience against concerted coercive capture. These are reasons to condition selection and test capacity, not to declare the problem solved.
