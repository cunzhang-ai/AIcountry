# Design372 decision on local fiscal shocks and service reserves

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design371, the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md), MF14–MF18,
LD14–LD18, B19, MS09–MS12 and JC. MF16 already allows a bounded
advance and calls for prior priorities. The missing choice is the
ordinary release when several verified authorities need one reserve,
the money is indivisible in effect and national receipts may fall too.

[MF25–MF30](../country/C04/08/06-local-fiscal-shocks-and-service-reserves.md)
selects an enacted reserve schedule, independent qualification, complete
service packages in prior lawful order and a second call tested on dated
cash. The Assembly and competent local bodies retain source and elected
scope. Career officers apply the law, sector owners qualify delivery
and courts retain independently executed review.

## Alternatives and objections

Wait and settle through the ordinary formula where the actual deadline
allows. A bounded early reserve can preserve useful service but needs
liquidity, live qualified providers and independent timely assessment.
An equal split may be appropriate for divisible protection and may
strand both tasks when each requires a minimum. A stable assigned tax
source or durable joint provision is a structural rival with recurrent
cost, shared dependence and control consequences. Compare it on the
same residents, original duties and dates.

A reserve can invite patronage, speculative forecasts or local
undercollection. Use representative effort, independently verified
purpose, published reasons and paid opposition and court access.
Protect local elected provider choice and separate lawful charges.
An unserved lower-priority locality retains its duty, explanation
and remedies. Repeated geographic deprivation needs prospective
review of the source and enacted priorities. No arithmetic priority
turns a fiscal official into a clinical authority or permits a
blanket hold on already due payments.

## Evidence, migration and next action

[Official fiscal records](sources.md) distinguish real advances,
successive forecasts and a dated listed payment. The
[constructed comparison](../settlement/local-fiscal-shocks-and-service-reserves/01-two-localities-and-one-national-reserve.md)
tests simultaneous claims, a late supplement and a separate national
revenue decline. It qualifies a research choice, with no affected-party
participation, adoption, actual staffing, source or service improvement.

Keep prior grant installments, paid duties, claims, elections and
valid commitments. Transfer safe records and reservation custody to
a paid successor. Reconcile each advance once. Use a bounded exercise
with two local service owners and the Treasury duty desk to verify one
shared money window, one missed supplier and one later call. End with
original dates, actual acceptance and a priced exposed gap, without
changing live priority. Reopen when the reserve repeatedly fails,
funding is selectively withheld or a qualified recurring source or
joint route better protects the same duties.
