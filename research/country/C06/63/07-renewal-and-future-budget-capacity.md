# Protect renewal capacity across budget years

*Design324 · IV28–IV32 · Proposed investment and budget rules.*

**IV28 — Carry the due work across the election and budget boundary.**
For material assets and services, Cabinet and affected local owners submit
a joint renewal schedule with each annual investment budget. Start with
the previous schedule and explain every material change in condition,
method, dates, service and liability. Show the current budget and each
later period reached by a material new commitment or identified common
constraint. List future cash separately from usable appropriations.
For every protected work window name the necessary people, parts,
shutdown access, safety evidence, affected residents and accepting fallback.
Retain unresolved work even if the owner, accounting class or estimate
changes. Separate qualified lower-risk deferral from an unsupported or
missed safe window. Asset cost, fiscal liability and service loss keep
their own measures.

**IV29 — Reserve necessary work and fallback before an optional start.**
Before accepting an optional commitment, compare the dated cash and
qualified capacity left after binding claims, safe maintenance and renewal,
and any temporary service needed to meet due obligations. Reserve a
protected window with its actual lawful owner and release terms. Count
only qualified capacity that can be kept available in the window.
Commit within a lawful ceiling only if the project and continuing work
fit through all periods they jointly affect, with later funding risk,
operating obligations and an affordable exit stated. An expected surplus
cannot be spent twice across years, and a forecast future appropriation
is not a lawful source. Separate independently useful preparation
from commitments that create unavoidable continuation costs. If a
new phase would displace protected work, hold that optional phase
until a lawful package and receiving route qualify. Keep already
binding commitments and their exit costs visible.

**IV30 — Make a proposed displacement a separate recorded choice.**
If a government proposes to release a protected reservation, retime
material renewal or reduce service during work to enable a new start,
present a separate recorded disposition before binding that start. Show
the original and proposed schedules, whose safety or access changes,
other uses lost and the exact funding and acceptance of the substitute.
Compare repair now, a staged service-preserving path and structural
replacement or retirement where applicable. The competent elected body
may authorize a changed schedule within law and demonstrated safety.
Technical officers keep their own safety competence. The vote grants no
permission to cross a floor, manufacture cash or cancel an accrued claim.
If no available package protects all existing duties, publish the uncovered
acts and seek a funded correction while executing prior-law protection.

**IV31 — Act on a failed dependency before the last useful date.**
The lawful service owner names the last safe start, independent warning
source, fallback payer, accepting operator and return-to-budget trigger.
If an expected receipt, qualified crew or safe continuity route fails,
execute the authorized contingency before the service deadline where
possible. Stop relying on an unsafe operation under prior law. Protect
access for people who cannot use the ordinary substitute. Recalculate
remaining work and the next call before releasing any freed capacity.
A bridge already consumed is not a fresh reserve. Report actual receiving,
delayed work and earlier loss separately. Where relief remains unavailable,
keep the original claim and exact unmet service visible.

**IV32 — Give residents and opposition a way to test the schedule.**
Publish enough original evidence and comparisons for affected residents,
opposition and auditors to contest selective inspections, optimistic
expiry dates or shifting burdens to a weaker locality. Protect confidential
and sensitive source details. Existing review and courts address lawful
duties and relief. Qualified accountable people inspect safety, while
elected authorities control priorities within law. AI may trace schedule
conflicts and forecast scenarios, but cannot certify safe deferral, deem
an obligation satisfied or allocate funds. Adopt prospectively with
funded staff, cross-local source agreements and continuing old claims.
Reopen on earlier deterioration, recurring displacement, failure of
substitute services or an equally protective simpler process.
