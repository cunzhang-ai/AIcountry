# TN05–TN08: choose taxes and the public offer together

*Part of [TN](../59-tax-sources-and-country-operation.md); a research choice.*

**TN05 — Revise the numerical preference, retain the positive architecture.**
PA10's20/35/45/55% bands and SD05's0.4M allowance remain the specified
distributional benchmark for A/I05, and the same schedule remains C2 within
the conditional U comparison. They are no longer the preferred default
replacement revenue package for the future country. This is a narrower
selection than rejecting individual progressivity or pretending the proxy
proves these rates impossible. Existing authored cases keep their exact
schedule; never relabel old C1 yield, a new M or a stronger tax as its receipts.
Retain I05's personal-resource protection, services, old Y rights and the
substantive C2-versus-taper preference within U; U remains a real rival.

**TN06 — Prefer source-preserving retrofit pending an express replacement.**
In the selected retrofit research path, retain the actual lawful recurring
source system until a competent prospective Act expressly replaces identified
sources/periods. No foreign rate is thereby adopted and no actual jurisdiction
exists. Prepare the replacement tax, personal net incidence, restricted
insurance, service/workforce/legacy and paid succession as one public choice.
The Act names rates, bases, M evidence/fallback and exact dates under existing
PA19/TA15/SD07 notice/protections; no minister, accountant or AI may set a
residual rate or grant a readiness veto. A new settlement instead needs its
positive founding source law and paid opening obligations, not an imaginary
incumbent tax system. This research preference cannot postpone commenced rights.

**TN07 — Keep incompatible source choices genuinely available.**

| Alternative | Mechanism and attraction | Burden or reason it may lose |
|---|---|---|
| Retain/repair current lawful sources and useful provision | Avoid unsupported receipt loss and unnecessary transition; improve access/control within explicit paid choices | Can retain unfair incidence, intrusive bases and inadequate services; present taxes are not automatically justified |
| PA/SD benchmark with a complete revised mixed-source package | Explicit personal protections plus named lawful receipts or bounded elected finance for the full offer | Extra company/product/site/wealth burdens have incidence, behavior, administration and ownership effects; statutory rates are no yield |
| A different individual scale/base or allowance | Can distribute burdens differently while preserving equal personal standing and I05 | Requires fresh net-person and source evidence; no automatic higher residual rate, household pooling or canceled need |
| Rights-preserving U or public/cooperative ownership change | Can reduce assessment dependence or change bargaining and returns | Needs its own tax, asset acquisition, paid skill/capital/transition and governance; ownership is no free source |
| Simpler retained services or different normal hours | Can reduce added compulsory work or paid staffing requirements | May sacrifice desired access/time; explicit lawful democratic choice, not hidden provider cuts or forced relatives |

**TN08 — Do not treat a tax table as consent or money.** Elected source law,
plural opposition/information, independent RG/TX merits/refund review and actual
remedy execution remain required. Affected workers, care recipients, children,
owners, taxpayers and future/foreign counterparts have different exposures;
an aggregate balance cannot settle their value conflicts. Existing private
assets, restricted S, future tax claims, forecast growth and AI savings are not
general usable cash. Bounded elected borrowing remains a distinct real option
with actual counterparties and risks, not an illicit deficit or a zero-cost
solution. No new regulator, vote threshold or adopted community procedure.
