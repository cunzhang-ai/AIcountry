# Design291. Joint AI purchasing and public fallback

10 November 2026 · Research selection · No actual enactment

## Baseline and decision

[Design290](baseline.json) retains SC's nonexclusive pooled buying and
DP's paid exit, independent alternate and shared-release checks.
Individual buyers can nevertheless each qualify an optional service
while relying on the same limited recovery staff. Another failure
is supplier terms that make a discounted award depend on more
government work moving into the same dependency group. Apply the
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and the
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md). Codex assisted research
and drafting. No affected person or independent professional verified
the selection.

## Alternatives and selection

- **Retain service-specific purchases and negotiate volume savings.**
  Less joint administration can be justified where failures are local
  or contracts purchase genuine distinct cover. The reference wider
  offer appears cheapest and passes local tests, but misses two due
  tasks on the actual joint roster.
- **Permit a bounded nonexclusive joint purchase with real reserves.**
  Select this architecture as a conditional option. The reference
  smaller scope and ordinary redesign both protect all six tasks
  at eight units. Prefer ordinary redesign while an AI advantage on
  actual receiving remains unverified. Keep the scope for a separately
  authorized useful task if it qualifies. Show the price and forgone
  advantages of a wider offer.
- **Maintain separately operated services and suppliers.** This can
  improve exit and political and operational independence. It consumes
  more of the illustrative budget and still requires different real
  operators, credentials and keys. Separately run, competent ordinary
  services remain a full rival rather than an obligatory fallback layer.

[DP48–DP52](../country/C11/09/11-joint-ai-purchasing-and-public-fallback.md)
selects the commitment, scope and correction rules. The

## Objections, authority and migration

A joint office can become the dominant buyer, over-disclose technical
weakness or delay ordinary procurement. Limit collection to material
dependency, offers, capacities and rights with independent protected
inspection where necessary. Keep sector source decisions, local
acceptance, timely purchase challenge and independent review. No
evidence register can redirect a duly committed shift or override
standing law. Existing recipient records stay with lawful owners.
Keep valid contracts, worker obligations, prior decisions, claims
and exact dates when a supplier exits or the authority narrows scope.
Elected bodies approve any new material source. A public officer
makes lawful awards and the receiving owner verifies service. AI can
assist scoped comparison but holds no contracting or case authority.

and an independent review route on one peak service interval.
Witness loss of the common control and recovery under a separate
staffing shortfall without suspending live rights. Compare a paid
