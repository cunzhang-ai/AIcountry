# One country and two operating arrangements

The [joined book](../48-joined-country-operating-book.md) retains PB's 100,000
people, 50,000 occupied homes and 75,000 retained structures. It starts from
the named component model in design107, not the 1,053.05m historical function
bids. Every component has one pricing owner and every original function remains.
Original bids and actual-null fields are preserved without becoming ceilings.

The plural case retains the declared 60% direct/40% supplier operating split
for eligible components and 6% supplier margin. These are modeling hypotheses,
not law or empirical performance. The direct rival changes eligible domestic
operations to public provision and service procurement from 300 contracts to
40; independent review, private households and necessary external specialist
care remain. Neither case nationalizes all private production.

The direct rival's named allocation is 15.412427m lower but its public all-new
asset-title quote is 318.170m higher. This is no actual acquisition price or
necessary additional investment: legacy title, vintages, purchase compensation,
management, finance, transition and actual supplier costs remain unresolved.
The same labor calendar does not establish equal quality, practical choice,
worker power, flexibility or public accountability. No winner follows from
an assumed margin; retain conditional OP and keep the rival live.

The no-change alternative retains design124's separate scoped references and
avoids false precision, but leaves known incompatible source totals unresolved.
A residual state/full-U arrangement changes benefits and service duties; paid
greenfield changes physical context. They remain whole rivals, not numerical
columns obtained by deleting inconvenient costs from these two cases.

This is not the complete national budget, a lower bound, GDP, tax yield, a
deficit or proof that either arrangement is affordable. Some older scopes are
retired while replacement physical and current legal scope is still unpriced.
