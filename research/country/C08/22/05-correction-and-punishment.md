# Enforce actual duties without licensing arbitrary coercion

*TS09–TS10; part of [technical safety](../22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md).*

**TS09 — Use V's narrow protection and correction powers.** Competent sector
inspection, original source preservation and specific warrants follow V03/P.
For actual imminent serious harm, V04 permits only necessary temporary stop,
isolation, warning or preservation within enacted power, with B17's meaningful
independent hearing within 72 actual hours and at most one justified 72-hour
extension. No serial label resets, general freeze, confinement or new search.
Subsequent necessity/expiry and V's shorter applicable review remain intact.

Ordinary correction requires notice, usable reply, actual duty/breach, reasons
and challenge under B/V. Do not wait for an accident where a specific existing
preventive duty is materially breached. Distinguish a protective operating
condition from punitive exclusion; actual restored safety permits restoration.
A safer rival may demonstrate equivalence under TS03 instead of buying one
incumbent's technology. Proof of a certificate does not rebut contrary harm.

An unsafe operation may stop before replacements arrive. Name the public
receiving/continuity owner, actual resources and useful due time; failure to
supply them remains an unmet obligation. No occupant/patient is punished for
an operator's permit failure. Property acquisition, eviction, forced treatment,
seizure and criminal custody need their own substantive law and independent
process. A safety inspector gains no general allocation, tax or emergency power.

**TS10 — TD does not create an offense by analogy.** Only the actual conduct
explicitly scheduled by TD and expressly added as VD10 (or the actual existing
VD03/04/05/07/09 duty) can engage V06's A/B
monetary process. For A, only a precise legally required operational record or
report, ordinary negligence, usable notice and at least 20 working days to
correct. For B, only knowing/reckless material substantive breach or deliberate
material false/obstructed lawful records. Prove control, ability and relevant
fault beyond reasonable doubt in an independent civilian court with full P/Q/V
protection. TS02's general explanatory hierarchy alone creates no extra penalty.

V's existing maxima, limitation, same-course credit, child rules, defenses and
independent appeal remain; no new imprisonment, point tariff, mandatory minimum,
fee surcharge or inspector-funded fine quota. Timely correction does not erase
actual bodily/other civil claims. K crimes require their own harm, fault,
causation and prospective elements; an accident, late report, unvalidated limit,
AI score or missing mandatory annex is not automatic crime or guilt.

TD's new reach is prospective only. No dynamic foreign reference, normative
uncertainty or delegation gap may be supplied against a defendant by software.
Actual approved operating conditions and annexes must be proved applicable at
the act; lawful silence and protected disclosure cannot become obstruction.
