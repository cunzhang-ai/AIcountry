# When public services teach the model what to predict

*Design513 · Constructed national and local assessment.*

A municipal transport office and a regional care authority use forecasts from separate vendors. Both systems treat trips completed as local demand. One recommends withdrawing a driver after an outage. The other reallocates visits to the places appearing to have transport. The resulting fewer completed trips and visits are taken as confirmation. Both vendors and both offices can be independent in ownership while the same premise shapes the decisions.

## Compare the same people, service and dates

In two catchments independently establish twenty people in each who need both an accessible journey and a confirmed appointment in the stated period. In A, eighteen actually receive both. In B, the transport outage limits completed journeys to eight; all eight receive appointments. The twelve other people still need service. Protect both catchments' existing duties and keep the actual people outside the ordinary system visible. The exercise uses authored complete ground truth for comparison. Unknown eligibility and receipt in a real assessment need their own evidence.

| Option | What happens in the next comparable period under stated assumptions |
|---|---|
| Reallocate based on observed completion alone | The remaining accepted transport and care capacity in B falls from eight to six. Six actual receipts do not show that underlying need fell from twenty. |
| Retain the ordinary available route without replacing outage capacity | Keep eight in B. Twelve still lack the joined service. |
| Compare a funded repair at the actual bottleneck | Restore four accessible trips. If twelve independent appointment places are reserved and agree to receive, B reaches twelve. With only eight places it still reaches eight. |
| Move another locality's protected stock to make the repair appear free | Four trips potentially reach B, but an independently due trip in A cannot be reassigned without lawful protection and actual replacement. Record loss and reprice the motion. |

Prefer the qualified joint repair where it reaches more residents without displacing due service. A true demand decline is another possible diagnosis. If independent evidence on the same rights and dates finds eight rather than twenty need both components, ordinary repair can be excessive. Do not infer demand from old cases alone either. Keep individual case authority and the same privacy controls. Measure useful receipt, uncovered needs, parts supplied separately, caregiver time, origin displacement and actual total cost through the next cycle.

## Make AI support the useful decision

Use AI to flag conflicting transport and care schedules and model the two diagnoses. Have human professionals verify one travel, appointment and receipt chain and an independent statistically qualified reviewer test the relevant population evidence. Compare deterministic scheduling or ordinary planning and charge independent observation and review to the same funding. A second model trained on the same completed-trip records adds no new observation about the omitted twelve. Another trusted source can supply contrary evidence even when it uses the same tool to format its report.

If a shared assumption is found before an optional reduction, hold the dependent reduction on its applicable conditions and put a scoped funded alternative to the competent authorities. Where a reallocation already binds or services are missed, keep original claims and urgent review on their own routes. Use ordinary political decision for the policy or budget and source-specific relief for individual duties. Do not use a defect in a shared demand estimate to cancel an unrelated lawful trip or award.

If careful independent evidence cannot be gathered by the first due service, protect lawful available care and access without treating unknown demand as zero or incurring a new unauthorized commitment. Preserve the underlying source, exact decision and correction across a change of vendor, elected body or public operator.

## Verify that a different judgment can reach the decision

In a separate exercise the ordinary roster documents four reasoned objections to an AI recommendation. Independent review confirms three and rejects one on its merits. An agreement quota suppresses the three confirmed objections before that same review can hear them. Three errors then reach residents in this fixture even though agreement appears higher. Test staff time, an independently reachable reviewer, actual payroll and assignment correction and the next supported disagreement. Ordinary performance and misconduct review remain available on their actual grounds.

The opposition proposes a funded joint repair based on continuity of care. The common submission tool accepts only proposals ranked by completed trips. Preserve its exact independently prepared text and evidence through a lawful alternate submission, accurate sharing and the competent body's consideration. A public data format may help compare the amounts but cannot decide whether a qualified alternative can reach the agenda.

As a distinct procurement test, two local products can share one external permission for runtime, support or payment. If it is unavailable by the next service window, neither counts for that scoped task even if local hardware and nominal contracts remain. Compare independently exercisable capacity, a separately accepted external route and competent ordinary work under the existing purchasing and portfolio rules. Preserve useful local or international components that pass the actual common-failure test.
