# A whole public offer, with paid democratic control

Design159 · WF41–WF44 · Research selection; no fiscal or commencement amendment

**WF41 — Retain the country, narrow the financing claim.** Retain the preferred
SG individual personal protection, essential services, earned public Y rights,
plural production and democratic institutions. Retain the actual lawful
source-preserving retrofit as the initial research/transition path, with express
competent prospective choice for replacement. Reject treating that path as a
financed endpoint for every added promise. Also reject calling a selectively
priced pension/hour comparison a complete national public offer. This is a
material qualification of coherence, not adoption of foreign law, an annual
balance rule or withdrawal of due rights. All TW36/32, Y/RF, force, election,
court, source and stronger old-law dates and obligations continue unchanged.

**WF42 — Compare genuine packages, not independently attractive parts.**

| Whole alternative | What its public choice must expose |
|---|---|
| Strong retain/repair | Keep actual lawful source and old programmes; repair access, control and paid capacity. This can outperform wholesale replacement. Identify unmet rights rather than calling the baseline free or just. |
| Service-first target with express new sources | Show the same people's gross/net work, care, pension, I05, service and tax changes, old/new claims and full paid transition. New sources need their own political and economic justification; a residual balancing rate is not a proposal. |
| Rights-preserving U plus services/earned rights | Include broad cash, actual tax interactions, extra needs, qualified care and legacy costs; compare privacy, autonomy, unpaid work and receiving. Neither expense alone nor assessment breadth settles its value. |
| Different production/ownership/service/time/security | Compare direct public, cooperative/common and plural provision, actual bargaining/capital/loss, negotiated external reliance and simpler/smaller defense where context permits. Less coercion or a different hour path requires its own express prospective choice; no presumed saving or unilateral clock change. |

Do not average incompatible ends or make the latest version the default winner.
Preference for service-first remains provisional; source/privacy/admin breadth,
transition burdens or worse lived outcomes can defeat it. Future communication
may compare situations, service journeys and negotiated commitments as well as
text. A model, mediator, agent copy or shared database supplies neither assent
nor an extra mandate. Participation, interpretation and legitimate votes remain
different; actual affected-party comparison has not occurred.

**WF43 — Annual arithmetic is only one constraint.** Cabinet/competent sector
owners prepare the offer; elected law/source authority remains with the
Assembly and applicable local/constitutional processes. Treasury's account is
not a veto over court relief, elections or existing due provision. Trace each
source/title, actual paid employer, qualified receiving role, independently
funded challenge, enforceable order and accepted successor. Due cash and owned
foreign settlement need separate dates and counterparties; an accrual surplus,
negative floor or court order does not deliver them. Courts/civilian inquiries
cannot depend on the alleged abuser's voluntary task allocation. No new central
budget guardian, shared master credential, military business estate or free
family/AI/foreign reserve appears here. The [eight joined paths](../../../settlement/country-frontier/05-joined-public-offer-and-source.md)
state concrete current owners and what still fails if funds, keys or staff are
withheld. They are written arguments, not successful operations.

**WF44 — Finish a bounded decision rather than reopen every detail.** This
comparison closes with a failed source-neutral central experiment, some
conditional partial passes and an unresolved actual whole-country offer.
Next construct one matched old/new net-source and legacy-payment bridge for
the same bounded pension/work population, together with the largest identifiable
additional paid service/independent-control cost and a retain/repair rival.
Deliver identified compatible support, missing amounts and a reasoned retain,
revise or reject outcome; stop rather than collect indefinite extra aggregates
if source matching cannot be qualified. Existing public aggregate data may
bound the result; sensitive testimony/microdata are not required merely to fill
a register. Actual participants/resources/budget are uncommitted. Urgent harm
or a larger country contradiction can reprioritize this work.

The coherent offer is not a compulsory omnibus bill: separate lawful competences,
reserved constitutional votes, rights, funded successors and exact commencement
rules survive. New values, original source corrections, qualified costs or actual
participation reopen the choice. No study approves itself, certifies all prior
clauses, adopts the framework or establishes operational readiness/superiority.
