# C06. Pay for technical safety, open entry and actual repair

15 October 2026 · Design 78 · Proposed obligations, no verified appropriation

[C08 TS/TD](../C08/22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md) now adds
precise candidate assessment, operation, reporting and remedy duties. They do not
create inspectors, instruments, safe replacements or liability reserves. The
existing 37-function book remains a bid map, not qualified full workload/cost.

| Due function | Actual operator/payer chain to qualify | Shared constraint or unpriced increment |
|---|---|---|
| Binding public standards and classification | Named lawful sector issuer; elected ordinary appropriation; public access and independent courts | Qualified engineering/clinical/legal time, accessible full text, actual data and affected hearing |
| Entry, equivalent competence and assessment | Sector decision-maker, conflict-screened accredited/public/regional assessors; bounded statutory fees and actual public assistance where chosen | No applicant-selected final assessor, duplicate test monopoly or assumed external laboratory slot |
| Routine safe operation | Responsible private/public operator, owner and contracted maintainers under actual duty | Paid maintenance, spares, qualified staffing, energy/water, site and safe fallback count once |
| Early warning and independent investigation | Sector receiver plus materially independent investigation function; separate public source custody | Actual 24-hour receiver, specialized investigation, protected witnesses/families and court-controlled evidence |
| Stop, correction and received substitute | Proved responsible operator and named public continuity owner; lawful payer and actual accepted receiver | Recall stock, safe shelter/water/care, repair workers, transport, notice and disability/language access |
| Injury, ecological restoration and closure | B/U/TS11 operator/insurer/secured/public self-insurance under actual lawful terms | Exclusions, tail claims, counterparty exposures, funded public shortfall and safe decommissioning remain explicit |

Separate included core, actual incremental requirement and displacement of ordinary
health, elections, courts, safety, education and production. Account by specialty,
shift, location, controller and real time; the same engineer or instrument cannot
be simultaneously assigned in every sector. A shared laboratory is useful only
if real competing samples, custody, validation and due dates can be met.

Fees have bounded authorized costs and incidence; fines do not finance inspectors
or supply a revenue target. A private insurance premium is an actual household/
enterprise cost and a counterparty liability, not a second public resource or
proof that tail loss is covered. Public guarantees/self-insurance require elected
scope and source finance; one bond, collateral pool or reserve counts once.

All current sector quantities, incremental costs, qualified rosters, actual market/
public insurance, safe receiving and delivery remain unknown. SF source/standby,
old country-book bids and projected AI savings do not qualify new duties. Actual
risk-annex values and exposure evidence must precede any claim that a mandatory
reserve or licensing minimum is funded and usable.

Transition pays for old safe service and new competence/source custody together,
with accepted successor duties. Missing an assessor cannot silently close every
essential service or license an actual hazard; preserve current law, report the
unmet duty and fund a lawful response. A complete accounting table does not solve
the material conflict or demonstrate a superior country.

[MQ critical medicines](../C07/26-critical-medicines-and-safe-continuity.md)
counts timely batch verification, quality review, paid clinical substitution
and actual receiving separately. Do not fund a standby shift or reserve lot
twice under procurement and clinical service.
