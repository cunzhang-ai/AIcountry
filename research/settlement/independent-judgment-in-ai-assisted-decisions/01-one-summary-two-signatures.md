# One summary, two signatures and an omitted original

Design358 · Constructed comparison under
[DP64–DP69](../../country/C11/09/14-independent-judgment-before-adverse-action.md).

A housing-support authority has 30 due files in a single service
window. These are undecided cases with no previous adverse action. Twenty are routine favorable decisions supported by a
separate, unaffected verified rule and source path. Ten require
disputed facts to be checked. An extractor omitted a dated original
in four contested files. The first adviser and a second nominal
checker receive the same derived record. In this declared case the
four omissions would lead to four erroneous adverse decisions.
The omissions do not affect the 20 routine files.

One qualified work unit is a notional slice of actual decision
time. Routine verified processing takes one unit per file. Each
contested first view from the originals takes two units. The
illustrative advice-assisted workflow has one more unit of qualified
reconciliation in each of four cases after a documented material
disagreement. Compare an ordinary officer route that keeps the same
verified one-unit process for routine cases and decides disputed facts
directly from the originals in two units per file. Assume both routes
find the stipulated originals. The four extra units in the assisted
route are for reconciling conflicting grounds before reliance, with
no new source evidence contributed by the recommendation.

| Route | Qualified units needed | Result under the declared assumptions |
|---|---:|---|
| Use the shared summary and obtain two signatures | 30 in the fast screening lane, with the second signature included | Four erroneous adverse decisions if signed and released; no independent check |
| First view from originals and then check material disagreement | 20 + 20 + 4 = 44 | All 30 due files can be decided correctly if all 44 units are available |
| Competent ordinary route with the same verified routine process | 20 + 20 = 40 | All 30 can be decided correctly without using recommendations |

If only 40 of the 44 qualified units are available in time, the
illustrated first-view route completes 26 final decisions and keeps
four cases open. The unsupported adverse acts do not issue. Separate
existing protection and review remain due under their law. Earlier
lawfully established payments continue. If an ordinary authorized
decision-maker can decide one of those files directly from sufficient
original evidence in the available window, record that delivery
instead. This stress assumes no such extra available route. Later
review can resolve the files without erasing the missed dates.

Select the competent ordinary route in this case. It uses four fewer
units than the first-view assisted route and reaches the same result
with sufficient staff. The fast lane uses less time by failing the
protection test. Reconsider recommendations only if they show a
material service benefit on a matched comparison that includes
reconciliation, independent review and people outside the assisted
path. The constructed workload does not establish a staffing saving.
