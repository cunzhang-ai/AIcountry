# Design319 decision on national resident political standing

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design318, C02 political membership, MB, EL01–EL04, A10,
NI05 and RR01–RR07 with the current working method. Replace
the four-year national electoral threshold with one continuous
year of proved habitual residence in the prospective design.
The project assistant authors the choice. Existing competent
constitutional authority and the actual affected public control adoption.

## Evidence and alternatives

Compare the earlier four-year period, immediate actual habitual
residence, one continuous year and status- or nationality-based
models. The original New Zealand and UK comparators demonstrate
different institutional boundaries. No empirical comparison
establishes an optimum. A full legislative term is a substantial
cost to people already subject to law. A year balances earlier
power against the need to establish a durable present home without
using formal status as the gate. Equal candidacy follows the
same political standing. The normative choice remains revisable.
Faster enrolment increases the verification and assistance
workload. A hostile official could use proof or removal to obstruct
standing. Protect independent assessment, privacy and timely remedy.

## Selection, migration and reopening

Select RFV01–RFV05. Preserve six cumulative months for local
eligibility, the four-year secure residence and naturalization
basis, citizen standing and prior valid rights. Keep separate
electoral, status, case and service decisions. The elected transition
names exact applicable dates, source rules and paid successors.
Use the predecessor electorate for adoption and protect its immediate
succession under RR, including repeats. Prepare new capacity and
help without changing eligibility during the protected contest.
Carry NI opening and referendum scope on their governing dates.

Price an independently reviewed registration and correction workload
for a real legally defined electorate, including people without
ordinary documentation. Reopen on verified exclusion, unreliable
proof, changed mobility, avoidable delay or a stronger lawful
alternative. Actual assent, independent review, adoption, staffing
and observed democratic outcomes remain open.
