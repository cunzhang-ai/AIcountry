# Design201 decision. Keep services reachable across a domestic move

*9 November 2026 · Research selection · Exact design200 predecessor.*

## Situation and choice

MF07 already separates an accepted handover from interauthority
payment. AP protects first receipt. AS15 retains origin obligations
and host urgent protection for personal assistance. The positive
gap is a common operating rule across distinct services when a
planned or unplanned move changes the usable place and the liable
body is unknown or disputed. Select XR01–XR10. Preserve actual
origin commitments and assign competent host interim receiving.
Keep each sector's lawful decisions, dates and paid responsibilities.
Where established arrangements deliver the same protection, use them.
[Canonical choice](../country/C04/09-cross-local-service-continuity.md)
· [Exact predecessor](baseline.json)

For the proposed country, select a bounded enacted national advance
for immediate eligible receiving when the regular source cannot pay
in useful time. Final liability remains separately determined and
reconciled with actual payments. Protect the child's ordinary school
when safe, chosen and usable. Select a real provisional receiving
path from the first scheduled school day after protected receipt
where ordinary learning cannot continue. This implements S05,
with actual funded teachers and access. Use a 30-day reassessment
of unresolved handover from protected receipt or interim action,
whichever was earlier. The interval is a proposed outer reporting
choice and preserves earlier rights and available urgent review.

## Evidence, alternatives and objections

England's adult-care rules and U.S. school stability give actual
alternatives to a single universal receiving office. The Devon
Ombudsman decision shows that a legal duty and a referral can
coexist with delayed support and family spending. Scope the
observations to the actual case and distinguish a later payment
and its backdated date. Choose a public continuity route with
advance finance, instead of assuming a child's school transport
cost share works for other sectors. [Original sources](sources.md)

Direct handover under local compacts preserves working
relationships and can be simpler. A host-only responsibility is
clearer in one area but can punish receiving places with unfinanced
cost. A single national service could remove funding disputes and
concentrate failures or political control. Portable cash can
preserve worker choice where somebody can accept. Neither geography
nor software alone settles these differences. A national advance
can be abused or wrongly assessed. Require enactment, limits,
distinct review and reconciliation, with dates, informed choice,
protected record access and real receiving. No use of a protected
address as the price of care. [Alternatives](../settlement/cross-local-continuity/README.md)

## Continuing law and next test

Preserve MF sources, AS15 and FC sector terms, AP/E earlier
deadlines, S/ED/HN and housing duties, protected PD records,
B19/SR effective relief, TS and national/local powers, and
paid OP/T and HT. The proposed addition requires lawful funded
adoption, record, payment and accepted worker succession. It
does not update an actual residence, payer, election or benefit.

No actual affected-party participation, qualified independent
review, enactment, ready capacity or lived outcome was obtained.
Reopen on evidence of cost shifting, unsafe disclosure, missed
transfers or a simpler equal path. Test an actual accepted household
handover alongside the other locality's obligations, including
the first service, original and final payer, a shared standby
conflict and delivery of any independent remedy.
