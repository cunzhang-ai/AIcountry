# SCN01–SCN05. Reach people safely without requiring a home address

*Part of [reachable administration](../06-reachable-administration-and-finite-decisions.md). Design338 research selection.*

**SCN01 — Give everyone a free, usable route for public correspondence.**
A person may choose a staffed local counter, confidential forwarding,
a suitable voluntary representative or an accessible private channel.
The local administration names a responsible officer. Any designated local
counter accepts the request and keeps its original receipt date through
transfer. Offer same-day temporary contact when an essential service or
hearing is at immediate risk and a routine choice within five working days.
A person need not produce a lease, fixed address, device, sponsor or proof
of abuse to use a counter or obtain safe contact. Use proportionate,
assisted authentication before releasing protected contents. Where contact
is needed only to make a filing or provide urgent help, preserve that act
while proof is resolved. Offer a practical in-person or other non-digital
choice, with paid help, interpretation and accommodation as needed.

**SCN02 — Separate contact, legal residence and authority to act.** Use a
contact address or routing reference for the specified service, proceeding
or set of senders the person chooses. Accept it for that purpose across
covered agencies and courts. If actual residence matters under separate
law, establish the necessary fact independently with proportionate proof and
protected review. Collection, receipt or forwarding grants only the
specified mandate. A representative needs separate express authority to
accept formal service on the person's behalf, waive, consent or act on the
merits. The person may change or revoke a channel immediately on safe
verification. Preserve lawful prior receipt and stop further use of an unsafe
route as soon as the office has credible warning. Carry pending items to a
safe receiver without resetting filing dates. A payer, landlord or other
party receives proof relevant to the case without the person's location.
Limit retention and access to the routing record, including location
inferences in receipts and operational logs. Protected judicial examination
requires a specific legal basis, necessity and the least revealing workable
disclosure. Using the service cannot add a flag to unrelated public records.

**SCN03 — Start adverse notice clocks on a usable opportunity to answer.**
The sender retains responsibility for lawful notice. Record the packet,
required content and accessible format, authorized receiver and date when
it reached the person or express service agent in usable form. Distinguish
dispatch, arrival at a relay and usable delivery. Require reliable evidence
of the latter, which can be shown without a signed acknowledgment. Send
neutral alerts only when safe. Treat returned mail, an unavailable counter
or an inaccessible digital packet as failed delivery. Re-route through
a separately controlled option and preserve the sender's own decision
and service deadlines. A public body cannot impose an ordinary adverse
default, interrupt a standing essential payment or run out a reply period
solely because it dispatched notice or a relay scanned it. Independent
review can stay or reverse a defective step, restore a full usable reply
period under the applicable law and order execution and repair within its
jurisdiction. Preserve proof and remedies for harm already suffered.

**SCN04 — Let a judge manage contested civil service without exposing a person.**
The filing party may promptly seek an individual service direction under
ordinary civil procedure. Require evidence of reasonable safe attempts,
a method likely to provide meaningful access, the exact packet and a usable
response interval. The court can order protected delivery through an
independent receiver and review necessity for any disclosure. Only a court
can allow the proceeding to advance without ordinary proof of receipt, on
specific evidence of effective opportunity or deliberate refusal of a
lawfully tendered, usable notice. A missed pickup, displacement or refusal
to share a home address cannot establish deliberate refusal. Where a real
opportunity remains unproved, use lawful case management and bounded
interim preservation under the existing legal grounds. Keep the filing
party's original date and access to relief. Any independently lawful urgent
order retains its own grounds, review and expiry. Apply existing stronger
special procedures. The relay and any AI have no power to deem service,
enter a default or enlarge jurisdiction.

**SCN05 — Fund a route that can survive failure and verify actual access.**
The Assembly funds the common floor through enacted national and local
sources, including access in sparse areas. Local bodies pay protected
handling, alternate pickup or safe forwarding and qualified access help.
The court service funds independent service directions, challenges and
review. Test backups for shared staff, premises, power and records before
counting them. AI may track sealed delivery status or assist with accessible
formats under a limited mandate. Verify the original text, the actual
receiver and any corrected date through a human route. Publish safe
aggregate delivery and failure measures. Migrate prospectively with lawful
pending claims, original dates, confidential records and paid handover.
Review on repeated failures, disclosure, strategic delays, excessive costs
or a simpler route that preserves both parties' practical rights.
