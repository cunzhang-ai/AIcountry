# Parliament can scrutinize an executive that withholds evidence

Design219 · PE01–PE08 · Proposed ordinary law under A2

Give each Assembly member a usable route to request material records of
public action, seek independent review and present missing or contrary
evidence before the affected vote. Keep protected facts, legislative choice,
court review and execution with their own actors.

| Canonical part | Working choice |
|---|---|
| [PE01–PE04. Request, preservation and protected access](21/01-request-preservation-and-protected-access.md) | Member standing, actual originals, itemized refusal, protected independent inspection |
| [PE05–PE08. Timely remedy, useful scrutiny and safeguards](21/02-usable-remedy-and-legislative-control.md) | Executed orders, pre-vote opportunity, bounded records access and paid continuity |

Apply this route to material national scrutiny of public action. Keep
individual claims, audit and specialist oversight under their own authority.
The [comparison and hostile-executive case](../../settlement/parliamentary-evidence/README.md)
distinguish a request, an order, access and what members can actually use.
