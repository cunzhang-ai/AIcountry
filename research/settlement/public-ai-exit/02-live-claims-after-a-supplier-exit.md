# A benefits service after the optional supplier becomes unavailable

This is an authored exercise. The public service uses a contractor for
optional language and document assistance. Its law, case decision,
receipt and payment functions have public owners. The incident starts
at hour 0 when the supplier and its identity endpoint become unavailable.
The affected service has an independently held case journal and lawful
access to settlement and review records. In the alternative without those
capabilities, a second hosted interface uses the same identity endpoint.

| Test case | Required result and reason |
|---|---|
| A. Payment sent earlier, local acknowledgement absent | Check authoritative settlement. It shows receipt by the correct payee. Preserve that evidence. A replayed message generates zero new payments. |
| B. Due benefit has never been paid | Verify the accepted determination and bank state. The last useful instruction is at hour 6 for settlement by hour 10. One qualified officer checks and issues it from hour 4 through hour 6. Verify the resulting settlement and receipt. |
| C. New assisted intake at hour 2 | An independent receiving officer records first receipt at hour 2 through the staffed ordinary route. It remains the operative date after import. Assistance can be requested without an AI account. |
| D. Pending human review at hour 8 | Carry the protected evidence, original deadline and unresolved status. A separate qualified officer acts from hour 6 through hour 8. Reissue working reviewer access through an independent route. |
| E. Legacy expression that the receiving format cannot represent | Keep the protected original and flag its significance. A case officer with independent interpretation works from hour 6 through hour 8. Any reasoned decision is due under its own rule at hour 12. No forced reapplication or loss of relevant proof. |

The public team safely restores the scoped service by hour 4. Two
qualified case officers have six compatible hours between them after
their continuing duties. Case B needs two hours before hour 6, D two
hours before hour 8, and E two hours with separately available interpretation.
C uses a distinct staffed intake shift. Bank reconciliation for A
and settlement for B have their own available officer and payment
rail. Six case-officer hours fit only with the stated assignment.
If usable access returns after hour 4, this path misses B's cutoff.
Two hours of qualified work must fit before hour 6.

If the second qualified case officer is absent, the remaining four
hours can cover B and D in this example. The next suitable E shift
is assumed to start at hour 13, after its deadline. The service
records E as unmet, seeks independently available protection and
retains the original clock. It does not substitute an unqualified
worker or a reconstructed model guess. If settlement for A is
unverified, treat the state as unresolved and use the applicable
payment and urgent protection route. Keep it separate from a
verified payment and from a known unpaid case.

| Architecture | Result under the same interruption |
| --- | --- |
| One integrated supplier with file export only | Some records may be readable, but identity, actual decision status or lawful settlement may remain unavailable. |
| Two hosts with the same failed identity endpoint | The second interface does not receive these tasks on time. |
| Independent public core with replaceable optional assistance | A–E can reach the stated protected paths under the timing and capacity assumptions. |
| Competent ordinary service with no supplier dependency | Can meet the same legal service if it has the same records, suitable workers and accessible alternatives. It remains a real choice unless optional assistance shows a verified net gain. |

A viable contract prices the independently usable records and retained
expertise as part of the service. The example does not price any national
option or assume vendor licences, unpaid help or deployment savings.
It shows why passing an export test without a deadline, protected
exception route and staffing test can leave rights unusable.
