# LR01–LR05: who can bind a locality, and what has really changed?

*Part of [C03/14](../14-local-law-and-public-control.md); design112 candidate.*

**LR01 — Effects, not the document label.** A local general rule prospectively
controls a class of people/places/activities through legal or practical access,
charges, services, permission, inspections, liability or challenge conditions.
Ordinances, delegated rules, mandatory forms/manuals, procurement/service terms,
tariffs, model thresholds and incorporated standards qualify when they do this.
A mayoral circular, contractor click-through or learned-model release cannot
supply powers which a published local rule would lack. Genuine advice/voluntary
cooperation and lawful fact-specific professional decisions are not automatically
new rules. A recurring adverse pattern warrants source inquiry, not automatic
personal guilt or a politician overriding a clinician's actual clinical judgment.

**LR02 — Prior powers and rights remain prior.** The national constitution/
Local Government Act/sector statute and lawful municipal charter assign actual
territory, purpose, persons, council/issuer, instruments, limits, funding, courts
and duration. Council law stays inside that authority and national rights. No
local vote invents crimes/maximal penalties, search/force/surveillance, a tax base,
debt/guarantee, core-service exclusion, electorate or protected status change.
NF19 permits a bounded elected local rate, not a new tax by this procedure.
C04/C14 governs material interlocal/unshared burdens, autonomy and distinct rights;
a minor external effect alone does not transfer every local decision nationally.
National technical offices cannot veto lawful local policy merely by calling it
nonpreferred. Legality, common rights and actual prior intervention law remain.

**LR03 — Named public responsibility, no private lawmaker.** A council may make
an ordinance in its own statutory competence. A delegated local issuer needs a
named prior statutory/ordinance clause specifying purpose, scope, permitted
criteria/effects, subdelegation, outer duration, source and scrutiny. Contractors/
AI prepare evidence or execute bounded lawful tasks; their contract/label/model
majority is no independent coercive or legislative mandate. A public-service
provider cannot add an eligibility test through its own software. Who chooses,
replaces and funds an input, mediator or technical method must be answerable.

**LR04 — Executing a fixed rule is different from changing its policy.** An
already enacted function can lawfully update a measured fact or index only with
its pinned meaning/units, eligible source, validation/uncertainty and missing-
data fallback, bounded effects, timing, record, responsible officer and challenge.
For example, a fixed legally specified tariff index may change its output as the
proved index changes; no new policy coefficient or access condition is implied.
A model's advice is not itself an authoritative observed fact. Unknown or disputed
inputs receive actual source-law provisional protection/review, not an invented
zero, adverse score or automatic withdrawal. Individual lawful applying acts and
emergency/rescue powers retain their own source and safeguards.

Changing a threshold, objective, weights, model behavior, input-source policy,
missing-value treatment or enforced interpretation is a new rule when it changes
the general condition, even if today’s output happens to stay equal. A silent
vendor/foreign update cannot inherit approval.
Preserve unfamiliar future semantics and known translation loss; if enforceable
meaning cannot be freely known/answered, narrow or leave the new norm unmade.
No paid license, dynamic link or opaque model is the sole public legal condition.

**LR05 — Whole change, exact version and original source.** Compare related live/
announced changes since the last lawful council baseline; versions, partners,
geographic batches or renewals cannot divide one cumulative burden. New approval
may set an explicit baseline but cannot conceal still-live effects and losers.
Pin source-law clause, operative version, meaning, affected consumers, continuing
duties and correction. Any operative post-L change needs fresh complete L and
new vote; material changed effects/new objections require renewed affected input.
A proved nonoperative clerical correction is logged with prior version/reasons,
not a backdated new condition. Unchanged words do not preserve stale support if
its actual dependencies/events changed. Courts decide legality, not preferred policy.
