# C07. Ordinary mobility and journeys people can actually use

*Design206 · MO01–MO29 · Proposed country rules.*

Select an accessible scheduled-network backbone with qualified, paid
demand-responsive receiving where the network cannot deliver the protected
journey. Existing local mobility authorities deliver; national equalization
funds the enacted floor. No new sovereign transport coordinator is created.

The proposed new ordinary floor is **two flexible return journeys per person
per week**, free at use, to a reasonable usable local hub or an equivalent
chosen destination. It needs no income, asset, employment, party or app test.
Assessed necessary work/care journeys and existing school, clinical, safety,
legal and electoral duties have their own scope and useful times. Ordinary
life is not merely whatever capacity remains after commuting.

The numbers are contestable normative defaults. They are not derived from a
treaty, the old bus bid, observed demand or an optimum. The authored cases
in the linked reference expose packing, empty running and coincident accessible
vehicle failures. Annual passenger-kilometres never qualify a particular trip.

Current source-law sector journeys and stronger rights continue. Proposed
additions require express dates, paid preparation and sources; this research
neither commences them nor authorizes a provider to postpone an enacted date.
If core supply fails, retain the unmet claim and existing useful-time relief,
CP/NP and elected correction. No coerced driver, unpaid relative, forced move,
unsafe vehicle or assumed remote consent makes the offer delivered.

The [authored reference](../../settlement/access-evidence/README.md) exposes
annual quantities and coincident departure failure.

This closes an ordinary mobility scope choice. Actual geography, demand,
vehicle/driver qualification, access, costs, law and affected-party judgment
remain unknown. A better complete network, contracted or plural alternative
may displace the candidate.
