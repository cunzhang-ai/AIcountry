# PI11–PI15. Contrary voices and correction need actual owners

**PI11 — Disclosure does not equalize influence.** Decision owners publish
usable source-based packets and seek materially affected perspectives, including
people facing poverty, disability, work/care time or unsafe public identification.
Existing AH/NI/FA/member-opposition help and DR/LR/DB consultation provide actual
accessible assistance, translation and paid source where due. No new commission,
compulsory community representative or proof of a full technical dossier before
reporting a problem. Payment for proper participation is not corruption, nor
are copied AI responses distinct affected people. Retain original objections
and answered/unanswered material, not a fabricated consensus or equal-contact
quota. Missing contrary participation remains a limitation, not consent.

**PI12 — Record custody and correction have named existing roles.** Actual
Assembly/local/department records officers receive, preserve, publish and correct
office records under PI/PD. Clerk cannot finally decide disputed private benefit,
mandate, guilt or constitutional validity. An affected person/officeholder can
seek safe factual correction and append material disagreement with reasons;
retain lawful correction history and source custody. Existing AP/PD/ordinary
court routes supply actual receiving and useful-time interim help. Allegations
need no organized campaign or threshold petition; serial internal approval by
the implicated official is not required. No record complaint cancels a seat.

**PI13 — Expressly add the narrow AA subject.** The coordinated PI/AA Act
extends AA01–AA02/AA16 compliance audit to these lawfully required public-office
interest/contact/draft/disposition records at actual national/local scope,
including Assembly offices, beyond a merely assumed public-money nexus.
It does NOT grant general access to every advocate's clients, private wealth,
family records, legal advice or political beliefs. Necessary counterparty
information requires its own precise source/consent/court basis, not office
nondisclosure becoming a private search warrant. AA04/AA16–AA20 preservation,
privilege/self-incrimination, actual reply, safe findings, court limits and
external own-office examination remain. The same office's copied records do
not establish independent audit or an auditor's merits/force/prosecution power.

**PI14 — Effective relief follows the actual hierarchy.** Ordinary courts can
enforce actual record/correction/retaliation/confidentiality and administrative
conflict duties, preserve relevant evidence and grant lawful case-specific
interim relief under B/OJ. For a proposed record-production/correction order,
name the actual lawful custodian, content, safe recipient, due time and paid
executor; E15 substitution applies only to an expressly assigned existing duty.
Established public dues/compensation need actual B19 source/scope, no new unlimited
Treasury guarantee. No court/AA/Clerk may use PI as a general legislative policy,
budget, tax or promulgation veto. CJ constitutional jurisdiction and material
references remain distinct; missing disclosures do not automatically annul a
statute, award or vote. Late disclosure cannot be reported as restored opportunity.

The coordinated A2/PI source also creates a NARROW PUBLIC-INTEREST LEGALITY
CLAIM: a resident within the authority's territory, or another person with a
concrete legal/material connection to the identified benefit, and an actually
authorized representative may challenge an identified particular-benefit public
act on credible facts of a material officeholder/controlled/connected interest.
No distinct denied service, competing bid, bribery proof, taxpayer payment,
party permission or one-fifth Assembly support is required. General disagreement
with policy or wealth alone is insufficient. Ordinary-language facts/available
originals suffice to seek intake and necessary protected source assistance;
a complete private financial dossier is not an entry condition.

This is an actual statutory PI case in ordinary courts, with fair reply and
case-specific relief under the coordinated A2/PI/B/OJ source, not automatic
standing to review every policy or direct nationwide invalidation. A necessary
material statutory-constitution question follows CJ01; CJ02's one-fifth abstract
route is unchanged. Judicial findings must identify actual jurisdiction and
constitutional source. No automatic stay on filing. Proportionate interim
preservation can concern the specifically challenged benefit within actual law,
protecting due essentials, workers/innocent third parties and existing claims;
no general budget freeze, seat/vote change or invented alternate spending.
Necessary CJ questions/own-court exceptions retain actual prior competence.
Actual public/sector legal-aid and cost rules support reasonable good-faith
claims without a reward bounty; abusive litigation needs proved abuse and
its lawful remedy, not punishment for raising an unsuccessful objection.

**PI15 — Distinguish proof, consequence and correction.** Competent employers
apply actual prospective discipline; Assembly/local lawful discipline retains
its source and mandate safeguards. Courts decide actual contract invalidity,
return of prohibited benefit or proved B-law loss with defenses and proportional
remedy. No automatic personal debt, family penalty or 'corrupt' finding from
an incomplete register, honest uncertainty or allegation. Intentional concealment
can be investigated under actual law, not a newly invented crime/fine band.
K09 still needs its defined knowing improper benefit/unlawful-power bargain;
MP competition law supplies no general political-viewpoint or influence offence.
No false immunity for proved misconduct, and no source/office shortage erases
a due remedy; actual failed receiving/cash/execution must be reported as failure.
