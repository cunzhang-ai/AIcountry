## Decision

Select [IV18–IV22](../country/C06/63/05-maintenance-and-service-continuity.md).
Authorize and monitor maintenance and real service continuity alongside
new capital. Record material deferral and the corresponding protected
people, work window, budget, lawful risk limits and successor. Keep
estimated overdue work distinct from a payment liability. Use the
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

Ordinary asset and investment rules address condition and safe commissioning.
The remaining incentive is to treat an unmade repair as savings, renew
visible assets while losing service through existing ones or transfer a
defect to a successor's books. A new estimate can also change because of
price, survey or scope rather than a change in physical condition.
The current budget must present each of those explanations and keep the
actual urgent deadlines in view. Elected bodies choose resources and
safe lawful options. Professional officers retain their ordinary safety
powers. An inspection can establish a constraint without itself
appropriating money or selecting a public project.

## Compare real alternatives

| Choice | Strong reason | Material exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Retain ordinary capital and maintenance budgets with a flexible annual split | Allows a quick response to genuine demand or operating change. | The next government, public workers and residents can bear deferred costs that the current budget does not reveal. |
| Set a universal capital share or reserve percentage for maintenance | Makes upkeep visible and can reduce opportunistic deferral. | Fixed percentages ignore age, actual condition, available crews and whether retirement provides a better lawful service. |
| Require costed upkeep and continuity and a recorded choice for material deferral | Makes real risk and service visible while preserving lawful repair, replacement, reuse and exit. | Needs reliable evidence and qualified oversight. Complex reporting can waste scarce capacity or be used to delay routine work. |
| Replace or exit a failing asset with a funded accepting service | Can remove a long-term concentration of risk and lower avoidable future costs. | Early capital, travel, overlap, site restoration and actual receiving can defeat an attractive headline case. |

Choose the third design, retaining the fourth where safer or cheaper at
matched scope. In the [authored choice](../settlement/maintenance-continuity/02-two-budgets-and-a-service-deadline.md)
repair plus a paid alternate fits dated cash and meets both deadlines.
The expanded facility remains unstarted. Phased exit can also meet the
duties, with higher stated money and travel. The maintenance schedule
does not impose a universal reserve rate or lock in every incumbent asset.
