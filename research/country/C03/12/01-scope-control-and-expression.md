# PC01–PC04: regulate paid electoral power without licensing dissent

*Part of [C03/12](../12-continuous-political-finance.md); design110 candidate.*

**PC01 — Continuous political finance, narrow speech boundary.** Political party/
candidate/civic electoral accounts keep source/asset/control records throughout
the year, not only from call. Paid electoral persuasion means purchased production
or distribution which, on the whole original message and its context, directly
asks for the election/defeat of an identifiable party/candidate or a specified
answer to an identified public vote. Exact magic words are unnecessary; criticism
of policy, a minister, injustice or a possible future law alone is insufficient.
A regulator must prove the paid act, electoral appeal and relevant principal;
likelihood of political effect, audience overlap or an AI prediction is no substitute.
The rule applies before call when that appeal exists. A call does not retroactively
convert earlier ordinary speech into campaign expenditure.

Independent ordinary issue advocacy remains outside electoral donation/spend caps,
including paid advocacy by associations or businesses. Purchased public-policy
advertising whose delivered cost/B(t) fractions sum to at least .05 in a
rolling365 days
has PC16 sponsor/value disclosure, not an electoral permit or truth test. Editorial
journalism, genuine subscriptions/sales, satire, private correspondence and unpaid
personal expression remain distinct. Covert paid placement cannot claim an
editorial exception merely by format. Wealthy issue sponsorship can still frame
the agenda; that retained risk does not justify a regulator treating every
opponent as an electoral advertiser. Wider issue caps remain a real rejected rival.

**PC02 — One effective principal needs proof.** Aggregate actual common control
of budgets, candidate selection, electoral strategy or requested/jointly planned/
materially directed paid distribution, across legal entities, vendors, accounts,
places and agents. A controlled association or shell cannot be independent of
its controller for limits. Specific jointly planned expenditure is attributed to
the directing contestant; genuinely independent costs remain the independent
principal's. Each cash/resource entry is counted once in accounts, while it must
satisfy every applicable ceiling. Similar views, open-source models, a shared
lawyer/vendor, public information or uncoordinated endorsements alone prove no
common control. A genuine later merger subjects future acts to the combined
unexpired histories, without making earlier independent lawful acts illegal.
Give original facts, reasons, reply and direct useful-time courts;
an allegation alone cannot freeze due support or create a combined principal.

**PC03 — AI copies do not multiply political rights or unpaid value.** Humans
retain franchise and contribution entitlements under C02. A bounded agent may
transmit a specific revocable funding instruction, not infer one from conversation,
employment or a ballot. Paid staff, commissioned synthetic media, boosting,
outsourced data/compute and externally donated production/distribution capacity
enter actual attributable service cost or evidenced ordinary replacement value.
Use independent comparables; where absent disclose a supported cost/range and
contested allocation, not zero or a guessed price per generated message.

An ordinary person's unpaid advocacy and incidental use of their personal device/
ordinary available tools is not a forced labor or imputed-wealth donation. A
business's dedicated subsidized compute, purchased reach or staff time is not
that exception; trace the actual provider/controller and benefit. No inspection
of all personal prompts, model weights or private political beliefs is authorized.
A million accounts/agents create neither a million donors nor mandates. Human,
AI and mixed media keep the same substantive paid-source/control rules.

**PC04 — Public office cannot buy a permanent campaign.** Year-round official
benefit lists, schools/clinics, staff, public advertising/procurement, state-owned
enterprises and government AI channels cannot be used for party electoral appeals,
loyalty payments or selective denial to critics. Necessary warnings, service
notices and lawful public explanation continue, separately priced and recorded,
with public purpose, audience criteria, controller and no party fundraising.
Prominence or political criticism alone is no misuse finding. Political office,
public media editorial independence and private party work have different budgets.
Career workers can refuse unlawful party tasks with protected report/review.
No finance office, platform or model adjudicates political truth or blocks lawful
opposition pending its own investigation. C04/PD/MP/K remedies retain source law.

**Design146 PM join.** [Public media](../../C11/08-public-media-and-editorial-independence.md)
keeps ordinary journalism, PC/ER guides/forums and official alerts separately labeled/
funded, with actual shared work once. Its no-advertising institution creates no new
contestant source, eligibility, airtime quota or press licence. PM16 also applies
SC objective public-advertising buying/disclosure outside that Service; criticism
is neither an award criterion nor automatically paid express-electoral advocacy.

[NI13 ordinary public initiatives](../16/04-finance-source-transition-and-rivals.md)
retains this issue/electoral boundary and sponsor/value/control rules; collecting
proposal support is not itself an identified ballot or new election account.
Actual electoral appeals remain covered. No new donation/spend cap, party credit
or referendum subsidy is inferred; concentrated paid issue reach remains a risk.

[PI public-decision duties](../17-public-decision-influence-and-interests.md)
now adds public-office-side material influence/interest/draft records and
proposed self-benefit protection. It does not extend PC's electoral definition,
issue caps or sponsor threshold, require ordinary advocates to register, or
make a shared political view proof of control. Real PC appeals retain their source.
