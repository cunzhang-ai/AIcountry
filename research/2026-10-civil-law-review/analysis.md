# Ordinary law as an operating system for a democratic country

2 October 2026 · Supporting analysis for C03–C05/C07–C08/C14 · Research proposal, not enacted law

## Decision this analysis serves

Should AIcountry's reference country retain separate statements of civil and
administrative rights, adopt one uniform national code and court system, or select a
common legal floor with express local discretion and an end-to-end route from problem
to executed remedy? The recommendation here is the third option. It would amend the
working country design, not current law, the project's publication permissions or any
person's present legal position. The [country design](../COUNTRY-DESIGN.md), [status](../DESIGN-STATUS.md), [framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md)
govern the research context. The [authority candidate](../AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION.md)
allocates national legislation, local competence and judicial review; this analysis
tests whether that allocation can operate in ordinary disputes.

The pinned [16 September source manuscript](../2026-09-readiness/source-documents.json)
contains a civil code, administrative-procedure act and family-care act. They already
propose legal capacity, contracts, property, civil wrongs, consumer remedies, notice,
reasons, review, supported decision-making, and protection against arbitrary family
separation. The [everyday-justice candidate](../2026-09-everyday-justice/README.md)
develops criminal process, immediate protection and recovery. This proposal preserves
those useful protections. It does **not** claim that the old manuscripts are empty or
that the justice candidate completed an ordinary civil code.

The structural gap is the distance between a written right and the ability to use it.
The current working design gives C08 two opening sentences on civil and administrative
law and says judgments must be executed. It does not allocate a complete path for a
worker owed wages, a child disputing a guardian's misuse of property, a renter facing
an unlawful eviction, a small business challenging a licensing decision, or a person
with a court order against an agency. Jurisdiction, affordable advice, evidence access,
temporary protection, appeal, judgment execution, and conflicts between national and
local law are not yet one accountable system. This is a design diagnosis from the
project texts, **not** an observation that a real AIcountry court failed.

## Values, support and rival explanations

The normative commitments are equal legal personality, fair hearing, private and
public accountability, genuine local self-government, and remedies that people can
actually obtain. Speed matters but cannot simply displace a defendant's chance to
answer or an affected third party's property rights. Legal informality can help people
resolve problems; it can also hide coercion, especially when employment, housing or
family support depends on the stronger party.

The [ICCPR Article 14](https://treaties.un.org/doc/Publication/CTC/Ch_IV_4.pdf)
states an equal-courts and fair-hearing principle for the determination of rights and
obligations in a suit at law. [CRPD Articles 12](https://www.un.org/development/desa/disabilities/convention-on-the-rights-of-persons-with-disabilities/article-12-equal-recognition-before-the-law.html)
and [13](https://www.un.org/development/desa/disabilities/convention-on-the-rights-of-persons-with-disabilities/article-13-access-to-justice.html)
state equal legal capacity, support in its exercise and procedural accommodation.
The [OECD's 2023 access-to-justice recommendation](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/toolkit-for-access-to-justice-and-people-centred-justice-systems_aecf7f78-en/full-report/the-oecd-recommendation-and-implementation-toolkit-on-access-to-justice-and-people-centred-justice-systems_c7aba4f8.html)
describes a continuum of legal and nonjudicial services. These are external comparison
standards, not endorsement of this institutional design or proof that a particular
court architecture works. A real jurisdiction would have to determine its own treaty
obligations, constitutional structure and existing legal capacities.

There are several possible causes of an unusable right: the legal rule is missing;
the rule exists but the person cannot understand or afford the route; the adjudicator
lacks independence; the remedy arrives too late; or the losing party controls the
means of enforcement. More statutes fix only the first. Conversely, a navigation desk
cannot repair a rule that delegates all discretion to the executive. Both possibilities
must be tested before assigning a case to another agency.

## Three coherent institutional choices

| Choice | Mechanism and advantage | Main failure and cost |
|---|---|---|
| Retain the modular manuscripts; fund and publicize them | Least immediate disruption; preserves locally developed rules and courts while improving awareness and staffing | Different modules can leave a person without a competent forum or executable remedy; each service can say another office owns the failure. Funding without competence or independent enforcement cannot close that gap. |
| Enact one uniform national civil and administrative code, centralized courts and enforcement | Easier hierarchy and portability, fewer local conflicts and common rights; potentially simpler for cross-boundary firms and people | Central lawmakers and appointments become a single point of capture; nationwide detail may erase lawful local adaptation, overburden central capacity and prevent a usable transition. |
| Enact a national common-rights and procedure floor with expressly delegated local supplementation, nationally protected judicial independence and accountable local access | Portable rights and process coexist with elected local choices; a single claim route identifies who must finish a remedy | Requires an explicit conflict rule, records interoperability, trained local and appellate staff, equalization, and independent enforcement. Without them, the apparent balance becomes jurisdictional ping-pong. |

The third is selected as a **working hypothesis** because it is compatible with C04's
local powers, C03's national democratic lawmaking and C08's common rights. This choice
is not an empirical ranking. A real jurisdiction with very weak local capacity might
reasonably select temporary uniform administration with independent local access; a
federal founding settlement might protect more regional lawmaking than this reference.
Those alternatives should not be silently absorbed into one nominally universal code.

## Proposed national legal operating architecture

**L1 — Hierarchy and competence.** The constitutional rights floor binds national and
local authorities and private actors as enacted law specifies. The elected Assembly
enacts a common civil core: personhood and capacity; contract and nonwaivable terms;
property, title and registration correction; family and child protection; baseline
employment and consumer rights; civil wrongs; insolvency and creditor priority;
limitation, evidence, procedure and remedies. A local council may legislate only under
an express constitutional or national grant: land use, local services, and additional
tenancy, workplace or consumer protection where authorized. The national floor and
valid local law must name which rule governs cross-boundary conduct. Courts resolve
competence disputes with reasons; an agency cannot choose a convenient forum to
reduce rights. This does not require identical housing prices, family services or
property records everywhere.

**L2 — A usable entry route.** A person or small organization may bring a dispute in
person, by accessible remote channel, or through authorized assistance. The receiving
service identifies the potentially responsible body and preserves an initial date;
referral is complete only on acceptance. A person's failure to name the correct law,
locality, defendant or AI category does not destroy an otherwise timely claim. Legal
information, advice, interpretation, disability accommodation, and independent
representation are funded according to stakes, complexity and power imbalance, not
reserved for accused persons. The ability to decline automated assistance must remain
practical. A defined jurisdiction and conflict-of-interest rule prevent the agency
being challenged from selecting its own judge or mediator.

**L3 — Proportionate resolution with a court route.** Advice, negotiated correction,
ombuds review, mediation and specialist tribunals can resolve suitable matters, with
clearly identified legal effects. Settlement is voluntary when a person depends on the
opponent for housing, wages, immigration status or safety; no compulsory private
arbitration or confidentiality term may waive a protected public right or prevent
independent review of a public act. Courts remain available for contested legal rights,
coercion, representative claims and urgent protection. Simple claims can use simplified
process, but parties retain notice, meaningful evidence access, a chance to respond,
impartial decision, reasons and appeal or review proportionate to the stakes.

**L4 — Time-sensitive and systemic relief.** A court can grant narrowly tailored
interim relief where waiting would make the final remedy useless, with prompt notice
and challenge unless notice itself would defeat protection. Examples include stopping
an eviction pending a hearing, preserving wages or contested assets, or restoring an
unlawfully withheld essential service. The court must address harm to defendants and
third parties, duration, security where justified, and reversibility. An ordinary
administrative completeness deadline cannot restart an urgent duty; a nonresponse
permits an order to decide and appropriate interim protection, not automatic approval
of a dangerous activity. Repeated similar harms may support a representative or
public-interest claim with defined standing, notice, conflict rules and affected-party
participation. It cannot settle absent people's rights merely because an organization
or AI speaks for them.

**L5 — Enforceable decisions, including against government.** Every final order names
the obligated party, act or payment, due date, lawful funding or asset source, executing
office, and a way to verify receipt. An enforcement service separate from the losing
agency may execute judgments against private assets with notice and protected
subsistence, third-party interests and lawful appeal. For a state or local duty,
the responsible treasury and service authority report compliance; refusal, genuine
cash shortage and physical incapacity are distinguished. Courts may compel lawful
performance, preserve claims, award remedies authorized by law and require public
reporting, but do not invent a tax, seize essential public facilities or create
workers. The Assembly and responsible elected local body must face the documented
shortfall and fund or lawfully revise their general rules. The person retains an
independent claim; an adverse budget cannot quietly extinguish it. A shared controller
of court appointments, treasury credentials and enforcement remains an unresolved
constitutional failure, even if this text names separate offices.

**L6 — Continuity and correction.** Record each claim's first contact, relevant law
and edition, interim measure, decision, appeal, outstanding obligation, executor and
actual receipt. Limit sensitive publication and access; a public statistic is not the
case file. New law identifies how it treats accrued contracts, property, employment,
family orders, judgments, pending proceedings and limitation periods. A court may
interpret law and remedy a case; repeated defects return to the elected lawmaker for
general correction. No court, regulator, AI or publication clerk changes a general
rule merely by updating a template or dataset. Claims and lawful protective orders
need named successors during jurisdictional reorganization, provider exit and
government turnover.

## Interfaces, resources and a counterexample

This architecture affects C03's independent judiciary and opposition, C04's local
competence/equalization, C05's work and market power, C07's services, C08's civil and
administrative law, and C14's transition. It needs court and legal-aid staff, case
intake and interpretation, registry integrity, independent enforcement personnel,
public payment authority, physical access points, and records that can travel between
localities without exposing protected data. Do not book these resources again as
criminal-defense capacity. The existing [justice capacity exercise](../2026-09-everyday-justice/capacity-and-evaluation.md)
is synthetic and limited to three criminal/protection pathways; it provides **no**
civil caseload, court-hour or legal-aid budget for this proposal. Numerical deadlines,
fees, thresholds, court districts and protected amounts cannot responsibly be set
without an actual population, geography, case mix and fiscal account.

Consider a worker who moves to a second locality after an employer withholds wages
and threatens eviction from employer-linked housing. An administrative labor office
accepts a report, but the employer calls the matter contractual; the landlord calls
it a tenancy dispute; each local office says the other has jurisdiction. Under L1–L4,
one receiving office keeps the filing date, the court or authorized tribunal assigns
the legal issues without voiding the claim, funded advice prevents an uninformed
waiver, and temporary relief can preserve housing while both sides are heard. Under
L5, an award is not success until wages and housing protection are actually delivered.
If the local payment/enforcement staff are absent, the failure is recorded and
escalated; the text alone has not solved it. This is an authored test, not field
evidence or evidence that either party is right.

## Opposition, authority and next bounded decision

The strongest objection is that a national common core may displace local law and
informal help, while legal aid and independent enforcement may be costly enough to
compete with housing, care and health. Another is that a government can use a
representative claim or consumer authority to harass political opponents. The draft
answers by limiting national competence to common rights/process, requiring express
local delegation, preserving voluntary informal resolution, separating case and
general-rule decisions, and providing judicial challenge to collective action. Those
answers are design safeguards, not proof of adequate staffing or absence of abuse.
The opposite objection remains: local variation can leave a migrant or small firm
unable to know their rights. The conflict and intake rules address that risk only if
they are intelligible and funded.

No affected-party participation, legal authorization, operational test or observed
improvement occurred. The research conclusion is to add L1–L6's operating chain to
the main design and list full code reconciliation as open. The immediate bounded next
question is whether the existing source manuscripts can be mapped to this hierarchy
without contradictory jurisdiction, deadlines, limitation periods, enforcement powers
or unfunded legal-aid obligations. The deliverable would be a clause-level conflict
and resource map for a real proposed adoption setting, with pending claims protected.
Revisit the selection if affected people prefer a different local/national division,
if a lawful founding settlement allocates competence differently, or if actual access
and delivery evidence favors a simpler architecture. This analysis provides no
permission to adopt its rules.
