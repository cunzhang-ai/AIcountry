# Later-duty dependencies in the national commitment account

*Design 82; preserved prior dependency prose plus the UA increment. Research, not adoption.*

The [criminal-core draft](../criminal-core/README.md) adds responsibility,
harm, defense, bounded liberty, sanctions and correction. Its reasoned cases
require distinct investigators, prosecution, counsel, judges and executors;
no actual justice quantity/cost or delivered remedy is inferred from them.

The [civil core](../civil-core/README.md) adds agent agreement, property/
liability, evidence and private/state/collective execution. Its twelve
authored journeys create no actual claims, loss fund or measured capacity.

The [completion assessment](../../DESIGN-COMPLETENESS.md) does not close
the 37-function denominator. [Private insolvency](../private-insolvency/README.md)
adds candidate loss/discharge duties, not public cash, actual lending or a
funded victim/worker remedy.

The [demand/stock/timing extension](../steady-economy/README.md) closes some
conditional normal accounts and prices an additional skill-reserve target.
It preserves unknown actual duty costs and accrued claims; required output,
prior sources and qualified receipt cannot be inferred from zero balances.

[Special harms/sanctions](../special-crimes/README.md) now adds proposed
K13–K24/Q01–Q12 and a gross community-placement workload. Its 348 authored
staff-hours exceed a 200-hour allowance before omitted duties. This is not
a measured requirement or an additional funded country-book allocation;
all actual quantities, costs and delivered remedies remain unknown.

[Family/civil B21–B40](../family-civil/README.md) adds scoped personal/child/
estate duties and prospective official-registry compensation. [C06/23](../../country/C06/23-family-claims-and-registry-risk.md)
names receiving/public-risk owners; private support, shared estate assets and
Y survivor pots count once. Actual increment, contingent exposure, due cash
and safe capacity remain unknown; no existing bid becomes fully qualified.

[Connected legal cooperation](../jurisdiction/README.md) adds forum/law,
recognition, evidence and protected transfer, with authored duplicate-credit/
clock/staff examples. No foreign partner or extra independent court/translation
capacity is supplied by a country-book share; actual quantities/costs remain unknown.

[G01–G14/C10/07](../international-crimes/README.md) now adds defined atrocity
acts/control and prevention, with explicit national variations and source
limits. Specialist investigation, defense, courts, source/clinical custody,
safe witnesses, children and received remedies remain unpriced/unqualified;
no conviction, confiscation or AI saving supplies actual operating finance.

[V/M](../regulatory-finance/README.md) adds common enforcement and specified
financial duties, insurance/compensation public risk and a failed sovereign
concentration source. Actual incremental staff, quantity, costs and finance
remain unknown. No old source, bid or regulator name qualifies this package.

[SF source/continuity](../source-finance/README.md) now separates actual
claim conversion, bank dilution, working credit and an independent standby.
All actual source/cost/capacity values remain unknown; a financial closure
never qualifies old workload bids or newly required operators/courts.

[RP reproductive statute](../reproductive-law/README.md) now adds precise
consent/origin, gestational support/status, storage/death and future-method
duties. Separate clinical, child/court, private compensation, public receiving
and protected custodians; actual incremental quantity/cost/source remains
unknown. SF cash or a historical bid cannot qualify these new obligations.

[CD conflict custody](../conflict-custody/README.md) now adds paid 24-hour
review/counsel, humane care, independent source/inspection and safe actual
release/transfer, with continuing injury/missing claims. [C06/26](../../country/C06/26-conflict-custody-and-release-capacity.md)
keeps each increment, shared shift, qualified receiver and actual cash unknown;
old bids/SF accounts, forced labor or future reimbursement cannot qualify it.

[TS/TD technical safety](../technical-safety/README.md) now adds paid expert
assessment, laboratories, independent investigation, safe substitutes and
long-term repair/closure under [C06/27](../../country/C06/27-technical-safety-and-repair-capacity.md).
Actual quantities/costs, qualified capacity and funding remain unknown; all
old bid components/amounts stay unchanged and unqualified for these increments.

[CP reconciliation](../country-reconciliation/README.md) now corrects GVA support,
requires expressly enacted new-service/accrual dates and selects a narrow residual
pool/priority with bounded authority. Actual new-duty quantities/costs, source
cash, qualified people and received remedies remain unknown. The old bids retain
amounts/components only, not automatic approval of these further obligations.
[CJ constitutional review](../constitutional-review/README.md) now adds paid
selection, finite constitutional reserves, external ordinary formations, counsel,
independent source/payment and received execution. [C06/29](../../country/C06/29-constitutional-review-capacity.md)
keeps each increment and simultaneous shift unpriced/unqualified; the old SF
protected-period scope must be recomputed before claiming new-dues coverage.
[MB belonging](../belonging/README.md) adds status proof, citizenship/residence,
sponsor exit and protected admission/removal. [C06/30](../../country/C06/30-membership-and-reception-capacity.md)
attributes specialized casework, independent judges, safe places and receiving;
all increments/source/qualified capacity remain unknown and old bids unchanged.

[UA necessary care](../necessary-care/README.md) adds real communication support,
independent evidence/advocacy, useful-time ordinary review, accepted clinical/care
shifts and safe dependent continuity. [C06/31](../../country/C06/31-necessary-care-and-independent-review-capacity.md)
keeps each increment/source/qualified capacity unknown; old bids/SF do not cover
new authority or duties automatically. This relocation adopts no procedure.

Design 83 adds [RR/FA capacity](../../country/C06/32-democratic-repair-and-public-attention-capacity.md):
old-law review/electoral execution, bounded paid inquiry, opposition/affected
agenda, safe sources and actual correction. All new increments/qualification
remain unknown; no old bid or the single SF standby is requalified. Next the
joined material/transition alternative and objective-wide comparison.

Design 84 [NP](../../country/C06/33-protection-production-and-national-expansion.md)
now selects protection-first mixed provision, actual paid receiving/review,
progressive burdens and linked I/S/Y/safeguard dates. The full37-function
requirement recipes preserve actual unknowns, historical bids and claimed
obligations; they do not qualify a national full-cost/supply package.

Design85 [CS/HC](../../settlement/country-case/README.md) adds real audit/delivery
checks, safe nonuser outreach/help and retained professional/learner capability
assessment to affected D07/D11/K01/K02 and I/S/Y functions. These increments are
unpriced/unqualified, not free existing hours or national AI productivity gains.

Design86 [QE](../../country/C06/34-reference-costs-and-delivery-conflicts.md)
adds named quantity/price/time/asset estimates across all37 owners with I05/Y,
paid transition and common-scope ownership alternatives. Each is a subset cost,
not a full requirement, accepted worker, due source or delivered remedy. Historical
bids/actual-null fields and old SF scope remain unchanged; use the new reference
for scenarios only, retaining missing duties/FX/actuarial/calendar qualification.

Design87 [FD](../../country/C06/35-finance-production-and-delivery-bridge.md)
now adds joined price/tax/I05/Y/capital/source accounts and proposed public
receiving/calendar changes. Four offered-source bundles fail. Frozen86named
costs/bids are prior subsets, not current full requirements or a source; new
administration, title, shifts, pension tails and received remedies remain unqualified.
No old SF scope or actual-null inventory field is reclassified as funded.

Design88 [IC](../../country/C05/09-income-capital-and-democratic-investment.md)
now changes the preferred ordinary-income destination while retaining I05/Y
continuity and selects plural paid ownership and bounded investment windows.
New base, payment overlap, award/verification work, capital and closure are
explicit requirements with actual sources/capacity unknown; old SF/FD money
and prior bids are not a second source. No new award or adoption is authorized.

Design89 [RF](../../country/C06/36-recurring-finance-and-pension-commitments.md)
now separates recurring sources/debt and adds old-unit-preserving prospective
pension cap, due-day insurance liquidity and access-preserving shared services.
Pension cash seed, paid notices/records/overlap/travel and continuing cohort
claims remain sourced requirements, not free duties. No old SF/bank/client
asset or inter-public coupon is reclassified as a new national source.

Design90 [TW](../../country/C05/10-work-rest-and-paid-capability.md) adds proposed
paid work/rest/learning/medical requirements and unchanged-care staffing.
Breaks, source payments and overlapping valid learning count once; worker
counts are not FTE/qualified shifts. New cost/source unknowns remain explicit.

Design91 [H](../../country/C09/12-secure-housing-and-repair.md) adds secure covered
tenure, paid cost-rental title, allocation/review, renewal and temporary dwellings.
Actual sources/skills/life/receipt remain unknown; old bids/FD care places are
not a second housing allocation. See the [paired subset](../housing/README.md) and [PS dependencies](08-physical-supply-dependencies.md).
