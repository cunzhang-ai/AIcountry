# Finite protection, real choice and a quota that does not punish tiny claims

Design168 · [Canonical EB](../../country/C05/16-portable-earnings-and-work-transition.md)

I07/I02/SG already protects current income/price/needs shocks; WL preserves owed
pay/notice/severance, TW medical replacement and Y independently qualified credits.
The new choice is bounded earned-income adjustment time, not missing subsistence.
The selected personal payment differs from a firm-retention subsidy, and its
actual comparative benefit/cost is not established by studies of those schemes.

| Cross-system chain | Reconciliation |
|---|---|
| Real loss → actual protected receipt/help | National labor/source or actual funded coordination, no employer-signature, complete dossier, political or AI-score gate |
| Annual capped C → current E | C historical normal labor/cap/hour units; E actual period-earned work pay, including unpaid source. Current I05 still uses actual usable cash; wage default remains full WL debt/advance route |
| Private/TW/old replacement → gross M | Actual qualified net receipt+lawful withholding/tax-credit discharge, not merely net cash or an unpaid gross award |
| Spell subset → amount | Fixed week/zone, C/E/M matched to exact eligible interval; no second fraction by lost workdays/payment batches |

Fresh critique rejected the original rule using one whole future quota day for
any positive payment. Authored W52,000/one genuine200working day produces unrounded80total
bridge in the182-day spell. Under that draft it consumed all182quota days; even
a full later working year could leave no useful new bridge. The selected amount
quota consumes0.7full-W equivalents instead. Given a new qualified spell after
that working year, the same bounded exercise has unrounded payment20,720 rather than0 out of a
20,800unrounded full bridge, with the prior80 counted. These are hypothetical units and
independently supplied legal/event facts, not a national wage/cost/effect estimate.

The182-day same-event end remains. A persistent25% loss cannot renew itself
forever because it spends fractional equivalents; actual13-week recovery/new
covered loss is separately required. Tiny/partial coverage and monetary quota
never extend that original calendar end. Amount-equivalent correction may free
future undecided quota, not a new expired spell. Any actual legal reliance/error
remedy exceeding ordinary cap belongs to its own proved/funded source; passing
normal-case arithmetic is no claim every error/remedy payout meets an absolute cap.
