# Keep public finance working when earnings shift from wages to capital

*Design326 · TN21–TN25 · Proposed fiscal and social-insurance rules.*

**TN21 — Separate the change in production from the change in public sources.**
When observed collections and qualified forward accounts indicate a material
shift in income sources that can impair due services or earned protection,
the finance ministry and independent fiscal office reconcile labor income,
business surplus, owner distributions and recorded tax bases on the same
periods. Test real changes, relabeling of work income, cross-border attribution,
costs, debt and payment delays separately. Retain plausible alternative
causes. Compare actual net assessments, refunds, collectible cash and dates.
Show how changes affect households, localities and qualified service work.
Use AT's first useful deadlines and ordinary quarterly follow-up. A national
profit total or an AI exposure forecast supplies no usable money.

**TN22 — Keep general and earned-benefit accounts distinct.** Budget the
general service and income floor beside the restricted contribution account
for existing earned claims. Trace lost contributions and continuing credits
through the applicable pension law. If the contribution route is short,
use only explicitly authorized general backing with dated payer, usable
source and amount. Consolidate the transfer once and retain any general
service gap created by it. Compare any lawful funded reserve draw, ordinary
general backing and a prospective durable source revision. Keep valid
awards and accrued credits in force. A revenue shortfall does not itself
change the rate, age, formula or a person's qualification. Borrowing or a
finite reserve can cover a bounded cash period, with its cost and end shown
separately from permanent backing.

**TN23 — Test ordinary realized capital income before charging work or AI use.**
For a persistent shift, prefer a source package that enforces existing
attribution and closes unjustified asymmetries between economically
equivalent remuneration, distributions and realized gains, before raising
the burden on low earnings or essential consumption. Compare a prospective
capital-income base reform with retaining ordinary taxes, an explicit
progressive alternative and duly owned public returns. Require independent
taxpayer remedy, paid administration, actual local and foreign attribution,
and uncertainty for timing, losses and behavioral response. Align company
and personal layers with clearly specified relief. Distributions enter the
personal calculation at their own post-company-tax scope. No tax on a
model, device, predicted job loss or reported productivity is selected.
Existing taxes and constitutional powers continue until lawfully changed.
The numerical TN benchmarks remain benchmarks.

**TN24 — Give elected members the amount and the choice that remains.** Put
to the Assembly separately the finite protection of the next obligations,
the amount and source of any transfer to the earned-benefit account, and
the new recurring net package. Show adverse collection, affected payers
and beneficiaries, and the dated balance of protected services and
independent review. Evaluate actual pass-through to wages, prices, owners
and local sources with bounded alternatives. Avoid using assumed savings,
foreign receipts, private asset gains or refundable assessments to close
the gap. No forecast or advisory body sets a residual rate or cancels
a due act. If no funded package covers all obligations, state the amount,
deadline, bearer and competent correction while carrying existing claims.

**TN25 — Keep the evidence and the right to revise.** Publish safe source and
incidence accounts, forecasts and errors through existing independent
fiscal review. Give opposition and affected workers, taxpayers and
recipients a usable hearing. Keep actual entitlement, refund and service
receipt separately verifiable. AI may compare scope, lag and tax-layer
interactions under lawful custody. It cannot attribute taxable income,
authorize transfer or declare obligations funded. Adopt prospectively with
notice, dated sources, old tax and pension rights, qualified administration
and lawful successors. Reopen on weaker collection, unexpected incidence,
displaced essential work or a better durable package.

tests the sources and remaining gap. Actual rates, tax incidence,
