# C13. An unsafe product behind a dominant gateway

*Part of [C13 lived experience](../C13-lived-experience.md); integrated design 55 research exercise, not an observed event.*

One platform controls a town's main online marketplace, payment access,
warehouse and several employers. A connected water-system part sold through
it begins failing. The platform also holds a major public procurement
contract and owns the complaint interface. A low price and several seller
names do not prove that residents, workers or the utility can switch.

The C09 water operator isolates unsafe equipment under its technical law and
provides safe interim service. The C08 product inspector preserves batch and
software-version evidence, orders a lawful narrow recall if the hazard is
proved, and gives notice and a remedy to people who bought or rely on the
part. C04 keeps public delivery and procurement records outside the platform's
sole custody. C05 examines common control, retaliation and practical exit,
with defendant process and court review. C06 funds replacement stock,
inspection and alternate payment/warehouse routes; fines cannot be booked as
cash before collection. C03 protects residents and workers who organize or
criticize the firm, without making criticism itself a competition finding.

The stress test fails if the regulator's only source, payment or replacement
supplier is the target, if a recalled part remains in service, if a worker
must lose housing to testify, or if a court's order cannot be executed. A
real test needs original transaction, ownership, hazard, capacity and
political-finance evidence plus affected-party accounts; this scenario is
not evidence that such a platform or product exists in AIcountry.

[Design-74 V](../C08/19-regulation-and-bounded-enforcement.md) now specifies
inspection/protection, finite monetary proof and actual receiving. [M](../C06/24-financial-law-and-loss-allocation.md)
keeps client property, bank loss and public risk separate; a shared platform
or a new public guarantee cannot silently supply the missing replacement.
