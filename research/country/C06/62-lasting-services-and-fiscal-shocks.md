# C06. Lasting services and fiscal shocks

*FB01–FB65 · Proposed fiscal rules for the country design.*

**Keep permanent public obligations on recurring net sources. Authorize finite
shock finance separately through elected budgets.** Parliament can choose the
mix of taxes, genuine reallocations, provision and bounded borrowing within
its existing powers. Payment of existing rights follows their governing law.
This design adds an explicit source test and response path to [FP](56-national-fiscal-settlement-and-source-boundaries.md),
[TN](59-tax-sources-and-country-operation.md), [DB](60-democratic-budget-and-execution.md)
and [MS](61-employment-prices-and-household-security.md).

**FB01 — Show four budget years and the commitments they leave behind.**
At each ordinary budget, publish the budget year and the following three
years separately for the enacted baseline and material proposed changes.
Assess related measures together where their cumulative cost, effect on
rights or exposure is material. Give members ordinary DB access to challenge
an omission or the baseline. Show a central path and at least one weaker path in which plausible receipt,
price, financing and staffing changes can coincide. Explain the joint
assumptions, people and services exposed, and unresolved items. Identify the
date and duration of each source. Show all material post-horizon debt service,
earned claims, maintenance, workforce, capital renewal and other binding tails
at their actual scope. Use an explicit later-year schedule or a range where
amount or timing is genuinely uncertain. Update after a material source or
service change. The four years are a proposed planning span. DB controls the
actual period and scope of appropriations and commitments.

**FB02 — Vote on a permanent offer and its recurring source together.**
For a material new or enlarged continuing national commitment, the proposing
government must show durable recurring net receipts, sustainable lawful
reallocations or both. Identify the source law, collection date, net yield
after material offsets and administration, incidence and sensitivity. For a
reallocation, show the actual spending displaced, who loses, any continuing
claims and the release of usable capacity. Disclose finite launch costs
separately. An asset purchase carries the staffing, operation and upkeep
it requires. Present programme and source in a linked legislative and budget
decision with separately identifiable choices and ordinary member amendment
rights. Separate Acts may use coordinated commencement under their own terms.
A proposed permanent expansion with an unresolved source returns to the
Assembly for a funded scope, a finite authorized offer or express reconsideration.
A model estimate or missing review cannot suspend an enacted or due duty.

**FB03 — Keep authority, accrual, cash and material capacity separate.**
For each material programme path, reconcile actual source owner, net recurring
yield, legal commitment, dated gross payment, accessible lawful liquidity,
currency and owner-specific settlement. Join the service to its required
workers, inputs and successor capacity. Record public transfers once at the
joined scope and preserve distinct G/S/Y custody, fund and legal balances.
A surplus on an accrual account may coexist with a missed payment, inaccessible
foreign settlement or an unstaffed service. Keep incompatible, missing or
restricted items visible. Use actual local mandates and funded local
receiving when national services depend on them.

**FB04 — Assign variable receipts by their source and use.**
For a genuinely finite receipt or a windfall above the supported recurring
path, identify actual lawful availability after due obligations and existing
restrictions. Leave an uncommitted excess available for elected finite
disposition, such as reducing liabilities, meeting identified transition
and capital costs with recurring upkeep covered, or building accessible
public liquidity. Forecast a volatile recurring tax under both paths and
state which part supports the continuing offer. A tax remains a possible
recurring source when its base is volatile. A one-year receipt or favorable
forecast alone cannot support additional permanent expenditure. DB governs
authority and carry-forward. This rule creates no standing fund, automatic
earmark or claim over pension assets.

**FB05 — Finance a temporary shock through a finite elected instrument.**
A material departure triggers prompt DB disclosure of the first payment or
service at risk, available lawful liquidity and feasible alternatives. Use
authorized liquidity and any valid standing response first where they fit
the obligation. Where further finance is needed, bring the applicable DB
supplement or other lawful measure to the Assembly in time to address the
first risk. A borrowing or other finite authority states the purpose,
maximum exposure, currency, duration, repayment or restoration path, and
ending condition. State what remains under ordinary law when it ends.
An extension needs a new accountable decision and evidence about persistence.
SF05 remains a distinct payment-interruption alternative. Count its liquidity
only for an eligible use, and never again as general shock capacity.
There is no automatic borrowing authority, central bank appropriation or
numerical annual-balance trigger.

**FB06 — Resolve a persistent gap in public while honoring existing claims.**
If changed evidence shows a lasting mismatch, present lawful continuing
receipt, reprioritization and programme alternatives in the current
supplementary or next ordinary budget as urgency requires. Report real
losses that financing cannot avoid, including supply and staffing limits.
Keep I05 and other valid standing payments, accrued rights, earlier dates,
actual TW obligations and DB execution remedies in force. Use existing
emergency and continuation rules at their applicable scope. Where no timely
funded path can be established, disclose the breach or expected failure,
affected groups, accountable office and available court or other remedy.
A shortfall gives no official permission to ration outside the law.

**FB07 — Keep the assumptions contestable and the decision replaceable.**
Treasury and Revenue publish source definitions, alternative paths and safe
reproducible derivations through DB and EV. Give ordinary members and opposition
the same usable costing access at the time of decision. AA audits and the
courts enforce lawful duties within their jurisdictions. Record unresolved
source and incidence disputes, forecast corrections and responsibility for
delivery. No auditor, forecaster or AI certifies a new appropriation or
blocks an Assembly vote. Record funded owners of the old and new account
through a change of government. The public can replace the officials who
choose burdens through its existing democratic institutions.

**FB08 — Apply this choice prospectively through lawful transition.**
Retain actual lawful sources until express prospective replacement. Preserve
both TW stages in the design, all actual obligations and notice periods,
and paid OP/T succession. Apply FB as a research selection for the linked
country proposal. Actual source laws, authority, transition capacity and
results each require their own evidence. Review the path when actual
programme costs, incidence, rights, cash or resource constraints change.
The next comparison must join a defined service change and an identified
lawful source with net incidence, due-date payments and qualified delivery
in the same setting.
