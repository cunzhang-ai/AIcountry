# Recognizing a foreign decision without outsourcing rights

*B47–B48; part of [C08/17](../17-jurisdiction-and-legal-cooperation.md).*

**B47 — Ordinary civil recognition.** Provide a domestic statutory route,
not conditioned on diplomatic reciprocity, for an authentic operative foreign
civil/commercial judgment enforceable at origin. The applicant proves the
judgment, relevant effect, court connection, usable notice and claimed amount/
act. Qualifying connections include defendant residence, actual relevant
establishment, voluntary protected consent or a purposeful substantial link
to contract performance/material harmful conduct. Do not enforce on claimant
nationality or an incidental server alone. Foreign land-title adjudication
needs the situs court; no indirect rewrite of our or a third state's register.

Respondent and affected third parties receive usable notice, relevant original
proof and independent challenge. Refuse or sever where recognition rests on
material fraud, no genuine opportunity to defend, an incompatible operative
same-case judgment, impermissible forum compulsion or a manifest fundamental-
rights violation. The domestic court checks those grounds without conducting
a fresh trial merely because it dislikes the foreign outcome. An ordinary
appeal pending at origin permits a reasoned reviewable stay, not a silent
permanent refusal. Recognition can cover only the compatible severable part.

This general route covers compensatory civil obligations, with lawful costs
and interest considered on their actual basis. Do not import a punitive
increment, foreign criminal fine, tax/customs charge or public coercive order
as ordinary damages. Those and family, insolvency, sovereign-debt, regulatory,
arbitration and immunity questions need their specific applicable law/treaty
and B45/B50/P safeguards. A foreign label is not conclusive classification.
Domestic law may recognize matters a particular treaty excludes; it must
state that separate basis, not claim the treaty did the work.

**B48 — Preserving a real claim.** A foreign request or pending case may
support an application for domestic interim relief, but is not itself an
arrest, seizure or surveillance order. Require B17's specific plausible claim,
irreparable-risk/proportionality findings, local authority and safe respondent/
third-party protection. Preserve only identified relevant assets/evidence;
subsistence and innocent joint property retain their protections. No general
freeze, travel ban or data mirror arises from a foreign alert or AI match.

The assigned judge considers urgent relief before useful protection is lost,
at latest B17's 24-hour consideration ceiling. Ex parte relief gets a
meaningful hearing opportunity within 72 hours, with at most one further
72-hour necessity extension; no country/office transfer resets it. No
response is approval. After expiry, the underlying claim persists, but the
unrenewed restriction does not. Renewed bilateral relief needs an actual
hearing, specific continuing reasons, expiry and earlier challenge, not an
indefinite preservation label. Earlier child/liberty/clinical limits prevail.

Execution remains a separately reviewed domestic act under B18/U, with
actual priority, paid/credited amounts, actual currency conversion, assets
and receiving checked. A recognized order creates neither money nor foreign
staff/consent. Safe housing, child support and essential care do not wait
for foreign recovery; their own accountable receivers remain responsible.
