# IC15–IC16: a real choice with continuing obligations

*Part of [C05/09](../09-income-capital-and-democratic-investment.md); design88 candidate, qualified by design135 IC17–IC20.*

**IC15 — State the disagreement and pay for change.** Compared with retaining
I05 as the final architecture, a universal ordinary base prioritizes secure
personal receipt and less earnings assessment of ordinary survival. Its
opportunity cost is substantial: paying people already above the ordinary
floor can compete with housing, care and investment. A well-run targeted
guarantee may provide greater adequate protection at the same budget. Keep
that rival live; no numerical welfare score resolves this value choice.

The retained U argument is that ordinary security should not depend on recurrent
proof of earnings/resources. [Design136 SG](../15-service-first-personal-security.md)
now chooses service-first individual protection instead of a presumed U destination;
this independence objection remains material, not resolved by the new preference.
I05 already rejects employment and presumed partner-wealth conditions; its
possible misapplication and assessment burden need evidence, not a caricature
of its written protections. This is a defeasible public-value research choice. It is
not a declaration that those dependence harms disappear with a bank transfer:
housing, taxes, employers, payment rails, identity and family power still matter.
Actual affected people have not chosen between these complete packages.

Do not use this preference as a promise of an unfunded launch. Assembly must
choose a jointly sourced tax/payment/service and transition package, published
person-level effects, actual supply, paid administrators, safe receipt and
exact dates under NP/CP. Current I05, Y, services, accrued rights, court orders
and contracts continue until lawfully changed; no ministerial readiness
certificate can postpone their due dates. All new overlap cash and staff
need sources, without counting the existing SF reserve, FD receiving debt,
pension contributions, client money or bank capital again.

Moving ownership requires actual valuation, consideration, title and seller
receipt; moving services requires accepted staff/assets, records, private
evidence custody and funded interruption remedies. Preserve applicable
worker/creditor rights and pending challenges. Public authority to tax or
regulate is distinct from participation in a cooperative. Agent copies and
model labels supply neither independent review nor a public mandate.

**IC16 — Next finish a coherent country allocation, not another free dividend.**
The income reference's same-tax comparisons deliberately expose costs; they
do not select rates. For the selective-tax full base, endogenous production
and profits raise the gap from 367.730 to 511.256 million CU. Perfect-receipt
gains before funding taxes do not establish gains after taxes, inflation,
changed work or investment. No full-country finance claim is accepted.

The next bounded output is a complete alternative for the same ordinary
needs, services and continuing pension claims, with dated gross sources,
tax incidence, transition balances, productive requirements and losers made
explicit. Compare retaining I05, a fully sourced universal destination and
a materially lower-cost service/production organization; do not tune an
unmeasured GDP coefficient until the budget turns green. A justified retain,
narrower coverage proposal or rejection remains possible, subject to rights.

The research lead prepares that comparison; actual fiscal, legal, engineering
and affected-party qualification is missing and is not delegated to an AI
majority. No public intervention, procurement or testimony collection is
authorized here. Reopen on materially different tax/incidence, source,
prices, capacity, payment exclusion, ownership terms, capture, objections or
new original evidence. If monitoring is unavailable, superiority and safe
launch remain unproved rather than presumed.

Evidence qualification, research preference, lawful adoption, funded capacity,
actual delivery and lived outcomes remain separate. The [decision record](../../../2026-11-income-and-investment-settlement/decision-record.md)
states the inspected dependency scope and received objections; its existence
does not establish consent or transfer earlier reviews to these choices.
