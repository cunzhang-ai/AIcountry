# Two ways a replaced public rule can still act

*Design481 · Constructed tests of renewal and independent evaluation.*

## Expired legal authority with accurate old data

An optional recertification power expires. Among twenty continuing
payments, two are blocked at the payer by an accurate historic
marker saying recertification was incomplete. The same marker
also blocks a linked trip for one of those two people. The original
recertification form is already disabled. A separate person has an
operative reduction that lawfully became binding before expiry and
retains ordinary appeal. No pending dispute authorizes backdating.

Check whether each use of the marker has living legal authority.
Where no such authority remains, the separate payer and transport
receiver stop applying the marker as a condition, preserve the
original evidence and use independently verified current entitlement.
The two payments and one trip must actually reach their own
receivers by their own deadlines. This concerns two people and
three service effects. An operative prior reduction remains on
its source-law terms. A scheduled action that never became binding
may not take effect on the expired authority alone.

A common tool that merely hides the source field leaves the
downstream blocks intact. An independently funded ordinary route
can restore timely receipt if it accepts the corrected scope
without restarting the application. Otherwise record the uncovered
payment or trip, and use existing independently executable relief.
Check the next batch before crediting expiry as effective.

## A new model trained on decisions its predecessor selected

Two fixed groups have ten cases each. Routing chooses eight cases
in A and two in B for review. The assessed cases reveal four
pre-existing eligible cases in A and zero in B. If those counts
serve as all labels for the next queue, the evidence falsely
suggests that B can be assigned lower priority. Separately selected
and lawfully observed checks of the remaining cases identify one
more in A and five in B under the same definition. Both groups
then have five eligible cases. These are constructed complete
counts, with no assumption that a real audit can reach all cases.

In a real evaluation, compare a lawful independent sample or an
otherwise valid independent design, preserving unobserved cases
as unknown. Distinguish pre-existing need from outcomes changed
by human follow-up, assistance or delayed access. No protected
service is withheld to generate a sample. If independent evidence
cannot qualify the revised queue, retain competent ordinary
intake and only independently supported narrow assistance.
Individual appeals and corrections retain their own purpose.
They do not automatically authorize model training or reuse.
A materially changed queue takes its own competent review.
AI can help compare records but cannot approve its successor.
