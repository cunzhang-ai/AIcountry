# OB01–OB04: an observed baseline is more than a GDP figure

**OB01 — Pin the economy, year and vintage.** Use Denmark's national
2023 accounts as one external comparator, not a selected territory or public.
DKK denotes Danish kroner; NPISH means nonprofit institutions serving households.
NAN1/NASL1/NASL2/NAN3 share30 June 2026 releases. OFF3 fiscal data keep 3 June 2026
and NASFK financial data 30 September 2026 table vintages. The archived June
financial query failed; no exact archived alignment is claimed. Financial and
nonfinancial net-lending totals match at reported precision, which is not proof
that every cell/vintage is identical. Later revisions can reopen the comparison.

**OB02 — Keep production, final use and capital distinct.** In current-price
billion DKK, the selected published reference reports GDP 2,781.3; imports 1,681.6;
household consumption 1,210.2; NPISH consumption 39.6; government consumption 636.8;
gross capital formation 666.6; exports 1,909.6. The separately rounded components
leave a 0.1bn supply/use residual, retained rather than forced into a new industry.
Intermediate consumption is not added again to GDP. Replacement, new capacity,
inventories and retained assets still need their own physical/claim/time accounts.
These are recorded transactions, not guaranteed future output or inflation-free
space for new demand. Gross exports are not domestic value added or Treasury FX.
[Original NAN1 table](https://www.statbank.dk/NAN1); frozen query/data in the comparison account.

**OB03 — Do not rename statistical sectors as ownership.** S.13 is general
government; S.1W is the rest of the domestic economy, including corporations,
households and NPISH. It is not synonymous with privately owned trading firms.
Market public corporations/public control and the general-government statistical
boundary differ. Financial S.11/S.12/S.14/S.15 can be separated in the companion;
ultimate ownership, mandate and worker/user power still need OE/MP/C04 evidence.
A lower S.13 share after a corporate reclassification is no improvement by itself.

**OB04 — Retire invalid shortcuts, retain bounded evidence.** GDP per head is
not a median work wage, a local tax base, a personal means assessment, owned
assets or safe service capacity. Compensation includes its source-defined
components; operating surplus/mixed income is not all distributed taxable profit.
In NASL1 S.13, gross operating surplus 65.866bn equals CFC 65.866bn and net surplus
is zero; that gross figure is not free public earnings. Fiscal net lending 97.513bn
is a different concept. No DKK-to-CU/FU conversion or scaling to 100,000 people
is selected. Retain actual unknowns, status codes and source definitions; do not
give unfamiliar concepts zero value merely because the current model lacks them.
[Original NASL1 table](https://www.statbank.dk/NASL1) retains production/income scope.
