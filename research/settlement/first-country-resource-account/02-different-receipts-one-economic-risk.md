## What can overlap in an actual country

groups in 2025 paid €19.526 billion in net corporation tax, 59% of the grouped
net receipts on the reported basis. The same groups recorded €5.106 billion in
PAYE employment income across 43,066 employments. Employment income is a
payroll base rather than income-tax collected, and employments are records
rather than unique individuals. Figure 6 also identifies social insurance

established service dates, people, paid workforce, case review and ordinary

The normal-looking two-window margin is eleven against ten due. The combined
event reduces receipts through more than one account, giving six across both
cutoffs. A finite reserve of two can protect the first deadline. The second
still lacks two, and the spent reserve is unavailable in the next budget.
If the second call instead follows a temporary delay and documented later
settlement, a qualified reserve can remain the cheaper answer. Under the
persistent branch, the separately settled recurring two in each window gives
six and four on time while preserving the separate reserve. An otherwise
eligible transfer arriving after its last useful date does not qualify. An
alternative requiring a one-time draw of four could serve both windows once,
subject to law and actual settlement. It still needs a credible next-year
source. Compare four years using the same disclosed annual shocks rather than
treating this pair of windows as a national forecast.

## Test people, goods and the next funded call

On the shared shock, assume the ordinary first local delivery operator loses
one of two qualified dispatch slots needed to carry essential goods.
The funded court and election staff are distinct and remain usable in this
branch. A local alternate vehicle is acceptable only with a separately
qualified, paid driver, legal load, safe travel and actual receiving at that
date. If its complete authorized incremental cost is one more unit, the
six-unit first-window cash requirement rises to seven unless another fully
qualified route already has a paid slot. An aggregate surplus in an
earned-benefit account cannot be reassigned without its own lawful authority.
If the second-window alternate shares the same exhausted driver or supplier,
restoring a payment fails to establish recurring material capacity. Put an
exact funded local replacement or another separate lawful source to decision.
Hold dependent optional expansion when receiving cannot qualify. For existing
duties use the competent source-law response and keep original claims,
actual missed dates and review visible.

In a separate policy-induced branch, each candidate recurring source supplies
two additional units at each cutoff before behavioral effects. A charge on
the same fuel and delivery market immediately adds one unit to the eligible
first-window local service cost and a rebid loses a qualified dispatch slot.
Its resulting six available units therefore face seven required and one
missing slot. A source outside that scope can preserve the original six-unit
requirement and both slots in this branch if its own net receipts and household
burdens qualify. When the external dispatch shock also happens, that second
source again needs the priced alternate. The same delivery cannot be counted
twice as both the affected slot and its replacement. Publish the price and
capacity assumptions. They are an invented feedback test, not a predicted
effect of a particular tax.

A territorial public backbone is the wider rival under CS14. Price its
independently staffed care and network routes, statutory intake, vehicles
and accepting critical suppliers on the same dates. Include qualified
recruitment, title or use rights, maintenance, paid old-route overlap and
who covers the work left at the origin. Preserve independent judicial and
electoral operation. If the public provider can avoid the shared contractor
and protect both windows within usable gross funding, put that exact
alternative to the elected bodies. Public ownership alone offers no
independent driver, input or payer. If competent ordinary repair meets the
same obligations at equivalent protection and lower cost, retain it.

Report local receipt, paid and unpaid work, public control, and who bears
tax and service losses, including people who never reached ordinary intake.
Verify when independent review and political participation are actually
reachable alongside the affected household's services. A resident might

and emergency clocks remain distinct. No Irish or Danish national
