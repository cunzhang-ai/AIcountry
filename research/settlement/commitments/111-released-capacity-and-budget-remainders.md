# Commitment 111. Assign a budget balance by its actual cause

[FB09–FB12](../../country/C06/62/01-underspending-and-released-capacity.md)
pairs actual delivery and remaining claims with budget, expense, payment
and source. Distinguish finite balances, real recurring net releases,
usable redeployed capacity, late services and displaced burdens. Use
existing elected decisions for lawful disposition and correct
unsubstantiated releases before they cause new delivery failures.

No source is inferred from fewer applications, a staffing ratio,
unspent permission or AI-estimated hours. Actual restricted funds,
due claims, local accountability, independent review, paid standby
and successor duties continue. [Matched evidence](../released-capacity/README.md)
