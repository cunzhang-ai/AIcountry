# BCV06–BCV10. Minority budget access and a recoverable floor

*Part of [democratic budgeting](../60-democratic-budget-and-execution.md).
Design244 research selection.*

**BCV06 — Let the Assembly recover one excluded exact text.** Keep the
ordinary BCV03 one-designation rule, ten-percent gate and existing DB03
notice and submission rights. A member can file one request per annual
budget voting sequence to put a timely exact alternative on a protected
priority ballot. The request uses the existing DB03 fourteen-day submission
deadline. It does not commit the member's ordinary designation or budget
vote. At the ordinary five-day lock the Clerk publishes which timely exact
requests remain below the ordinary gate. An identical text already qualified
through the ordinary route does not enter recovery under another label.
A timely omission can be reviewed
and corrected. An official, model or party leader cannot screen the request
for political merit or require the requester to belong to a recognized group.
This recovery is for competing whole budgets, not every amendment, claim
or expenditure line. Ordinary rights to originate those remain.

**BCV07 — Separate agenda authority from budget approval.** In a lawful,
preannounced sitting by four calendar days before the scheduled final vote,
hold one recorded priority ballot over the remaining requested texts.
Each actual member can select at most one exact text or abstain. Count
positions together against the same list before publication. A proposal
receiving at least floor(N/2)+1 distinct selections, with that many actual
members present, gains the first final vote ahead of the ordinary sequence.
N is the full statutory Assembly. At most one text can qualify because
each member has only one selection and fewer than half of all seats
remain available after a majority. The ordinary and recovered final
docket together contains at most eleven distinct exact texts. A tie or divided support below the
threshold fills no slot. The ballot asks for a final vote on that text
before any incompatible ordinary text. It approves neither the text nor
its spending. Publish choices, absence, abstentions and source versions.
Approval still requires a separate DB04 vote on the exact unchanged
text. A failed final vote moves to BCV04's ordinary sequence, whose
docket remains available even if the recovered text received fewer final
yes votes than it received priority selections.

**BCV08 — Count people once and keep real alternatives distinct.** The
Clerk shares a ballot entry for proved identical operative texts and
binding conditions, preserving all sponsors, reasons and receipt dates.
A renamed version, new account or agent copy creates no additional
selection. An actual change to eligible people, source, delivery duties,
implementation control, authority, timing or another operative term is
not merged just because cost and title match. Publish the comparison and
provide prompt challenge and correction before counting. Material disputed
meaning receives separate presentation unless identity is established.
The common table may share evidence for related texts without merging
votes or deleting a distinct effect. If competing substantive versions
divide priority support, disclose the split. Their votes cannot be pooled
or transferred to a compromise that members did not select.

**BCV09 — Make the extra choice usable without letting it consume the
ordinary docket.** Fund proportionate first-pass comparison of timely
requests from receipt and reserve actual staff, interpretation, non-digital
access and hearing time for one additional whole package. Before the
priority ballot, publish each exact text and a common first-pass account
of its material differences, dependencies, unknowns and affected people,
including safely reportable grounds raised by small groups. Provide a
direct timely way to correct a material omission. A full costing is not
a condition for consideration. After selection, secure actual DB03–DB05
and PE scrutiny before approval. Confirm source and cash independently
of the political vote. Preserve ordinary DB02–DB03 assistance and hearing
rights even if no text receives priority. A right, due payment or direct
remedy never needs a budget nomination.

An unavailable lawful presider, loss of quorum, uncorrected access failure
or exhausted scrutiny capacity must appear as an unheld ballot or a
documented procedural breach as applicable. Use the existing lawful
alternate, PE and ordinary judicial routes for timely correction. A missing
clerk or costing cannot be treated as a merits rejection. No application,
readiness claim or missed checkpoint creates an appropriation, an automatic
stay, a new urgency exception or an extension of fiscal or political
office. A lawful resumed ballot or duly required rerun corrects the same
procedure. A postponed final sitting does not on its own provide another
priority attempt. A material replacement requires fresh lawful scrutiny
and its own actual votes. It cannot inherit the reserved first vote.
Existing BLC duties and avenues for a later appropriation continue.

**BCV10 — Keep the gate reviewable under actual exclusion.** After each
annual budget, the Clerk reports which timely alternatives had no useful
reply, lost priority, were never put to a final vote or were displaced by
an enacted incompatible vote. Publish protected reasons and the capacity
actually used. Preserve accessible responses to minority proposals without
releasing protected personal evidence. Reconsider the gate, the added slot
and time window prospectively if small groups repeatedly lose a usable
path, agenda or review is manipulated, or the ordinary docket is displaced.
The majority recovery protects demonstrable cross-member support. It
leaves unresolved how often a proposal without a current majority can
persuade enough members while the decision is open.
