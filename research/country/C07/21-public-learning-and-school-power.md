# C07. Public learning and accountable school power

ED01–ED43 working country selection, not enacted law

**Keep a free public foundational backbone with plural lawful learning routes.
Give elected authorities real source/planning responsibility, qualified staff
professional responsibility, learners independent voice and an outside-school
inspection/remedy route. No government, parent majority, profession or AI owns
a child's education.** S05–S08 already supplies the substantive entitlement;
this makes its employer/leadership/correction chain concrete.
