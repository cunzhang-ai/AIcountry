# Public conditions: lives, power and future capacity

*Part of [C14 public conditions](../04-public-conditions.md); design85 organization of the current canonical account.*
## A public account of lives, power and future capacity

Before each ordinary Assembly election after a governing term, a statutory
public-conditions account
places the governing term beside its inherited starting conditions, earlier
terms and carefully chosen contemporary alternatives. It reports material
security, health, care, learning, housing, safe mobility, usable time, social
connection, ecological condition, safety, practical political opposition and
executed rights remedies as distinct dimensions. It also reports debt,
maintenance, workforce and resource stocks needed to sustain those lives.
The [campaign-contest account](../../C03/07-political-finance-and-campaign-access.md)
separates ballot access, support received before voting, sponsor and media
concentration, state-resource misuse, urgent orders and executed repair.
A clean finance filing cannot establish that an opponent could reach voters.
The [ecological transition](../../C09/08-climate-and-ecological-transition.md)
reports direct emissions, imported harm, habitat and water condition, energy
reliability, household burden, worker outcomes and delivered restoration as
separate results; one falling carbon series does not settle the others.
The [population-health account](../../C07/08-population-health-and-prevention.md)
separates prevention reach, avoidable harm, unequal exposure, missed warnings,
data-correction and coercion complaints, and service actually received.
An alert count, vaccine shipment or falling reported cases cannot stand in
for trusted access or prove that unrecorded people are well.
The [mental-health and crisis account](../../C07/11-mental-health-substance-use-and-crisis-care.md)
separates requests, accepting care, crisis and overdose response, unmet
need, forced acts, privacy harm, housing and work continuity and delivered
remedies. A fall in recorded crises may signal fear of the service.
The [reproductive-health account](../../C07/09-reproductive-health-and-early-life.md)
separates timely wanted care, denied or delayed service, coercion and
mistreatment, pregnancy and newborn harm, privacy breaches, child-status
correction and actual remedy, with intimate data protected.
The [serious-illness account](../../C07/10-serious-illness-and-end-of-life-care.md)
separates relief received, unwanted treatment, missed support, death-record
accuracy, rites and the dependant's first usable payment, protecting
intimate choices and excluding no one because they died before a survey.
The [food-system account](../../C09/09-food-systems-and-nutrition.md)
separates availability, affordability, dietary adequacy, foodborne harm,
stability, producer and worker livelihoods, ecological condition and food
actually received. A lower price or fuller warehouse is not a complete diet.
The [animal-welfare account](../../C09/10-animal-welfare-and-one-health.md)
separately reports species-sensitive welfare observations, coverage and
executed corrections, disease, research use, import exposure and the human
food and livelihood consequences. A safety certificate or population count
cannot stand in for individual animal condition.
After a disaster it separates rescue, temporary protection and durable
recovery: safe occupancy, resumed services and work, continuing displacement,
contested land or insurance claims and changed future exposure by place.
The [C09 network and freight account](../../C09/05-communications-and-logistics.md)
adds usable connection, legal notice, route interruption and actual essential
delivery, including people who had no online or nearby alternative.
It separately reports safe sanitation and waste outcomes, material cyber
outages and repaired claims, and lawful border release delays affecting
essential goods. A restored dashboard, collected bin or customs release order
is not proof of safe treatment, remedy or receipt by the intended person.
The [national-form choice](../05-scale-and-national-form.md) also requires
place-sensitive evidence: representation and opposition access, travel and
communication burdens, Assembly and local workload, executed remedies and
service reach. A national average cannot show that a remote electorate has
practical control; a regional institution's existence cannot prove it either.
Its [automation account](../../C05/04-automation-income-and-power.md) distinguishes
task exposure from observed earnings, prices, public revenue, service access
and control of work and civic infrastructure.
The [C05 market account](../../C05/05-market-power-and-real-exit.md) reports
usable worker, buyer and supplier alternatives, common control and executed
remedies alongside prices; C08 reports actual product removal and repaired
harm, not only recall announcements or fines.
For material hard-to-reverse choices, the [C09 future-options record](../../C09/04-future-options-and-irreversibility.md)
shows what was authorized, what stock or freedom was lost, whether promised
repair was funded and delivered, and whose objections were unanswered.
People affected by this country's trade, security or environmental choices
abroad, people who left or never reached a public service, and future claims
cannot disappear merely because they are outside a resident survey.
The founding election uses an honestly incomplete opening baseline rather
than a fictional record of national performance.
