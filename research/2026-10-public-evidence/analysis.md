# Public evidence without an official monopoly on truth

3 October 2026 · Bounded institutional analysis for C03/C06/C09/C11/C14 · Same-controller AI research recommendation, not enacted law

## The unresolved national choice

The [integrated country design](../COUNTRY-DESIGN.md) requires original election
evidence and usable opposition information (C03), a common cash and physical
account (C06), local ecological measurement (C09), independent science and
media (C11), and qualified observations before policy renewal (C14). These
functions need more than truthful intentions. Who may inspect the original
observations, define a comparable measure, preserve its history, correct a
mistake, publish an unfavorable result, and keep those acts possible after an
election or vendor failure? The current design protects sector records and
plural expression but does not assign this **public evidence system** as an
answerable national function. Its absence could let a service authority
declare its own failure solved by changing a category, while the public,
opposition and court cannot reconstruct the original claim.

Four acts must remain distinct: an operational record of an event; a public
statistical estimate constructed from records or observation; an evaluation
interpreting causes and tradeoffs; and a legal decision that creates a right
or obligation. A statistics office may question an official number but cannot
set housing eligibility or tax liability. A court may require preservation
and correction under law but cannot choose the nation's preferred estimate
or research theory. A publisher's disagreement does not erase a person's
underlying service claim. The [UN Fundamental Principles of Official
Statistics](https://unstats.un.org/fpos/) support professional independence,
transparent methods, confidentiality and impartial access as general
comparators; they do not prescribe AIcountry's institutional form or establish
its capacity.

## Coherent alternatives

| Model | Mechanism and appeal | Failure and power risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Retain distributed sector reporting** | Treasury, election offices, utilities, health services, courts and local authorities keep and publish their own evidence; audit, courts, press and researchers challenge them. This avoids a new office and preserves domain expertise. | A challenged office controls source facts, definitions, release timing and access. Cross-sector comparisons can be incompatible, and a vendor or cabinet can suppress several records at once. Press and courts may lack protected access to reconstruct the series. |
| **Single insulated national evidence authority** | One independent institution owns common collection, national statistics, archives and evaluation, with protected funding and appointment. It can impose standards and produce coherent accounts. | Its own methods, access decisions or leadership can become one point of capture. A centralized dossier may threaten privacy, local meanings and professional judgment; a hostile incumbent could seize one store and thereby silence many sectors. Legal insulation does not create independent servers, field workers or remedy executors. |
| **Selected conditional reference: federated custodians plus separate public evidence service** | Sector and local bodies keep their original operational records; a separately governed service produces official cross-sector statistics, preserves verifiable public records and coordinates contestable evaluation. Plural academic, professional, community and media inquiry remains independent. | Coordination and privacy controls are costly. Sector custodians can still withhold or alter source material; the public service can still be captured through appointments, budget, cloud infrastructure or professional gatekeeping. The required separation must be tested across the full chain. |

The third model is a conditional research choice, not a claim that more offices
automatically create independence. It would lose to the first if existing
sector custody and outside scrutiny already provide comparable, accessible
evidence without common capture; it would lose to the second if dispersion
causes uncorrectable gaps and a central institution can be governed without
creating a single coercive information gateway. None of those conditions has
been observed for AIcountry.

## Authority, custody and publication chain

**Elected rule and bounded mandate.** The Assembly would enact which public
records must be made and retained, official-statistics purposes and minimum
publication duties, privacy and security limits, audit access, lawful remedies
and funding. It would decide contested policy and definitions of legal rights.
Neither a minister, chief statistician, archivist nor AI could turn a changed
metric into a changed entitlement. The Assembly could amend the mandate
prospectively under constitutional rights, but a governing majority could not
privately withdraw the opposition's access to existing evidence or delete
unfavorable vintages. Courts would review legality and protect rights, not
certify a politically favored narrative.

**Original sector custody.** Each competent election, revenue, service, court,
utility or ecological authority would keep the event record needed for its
own lawful task, with a named custodian, lawful retention period, tamper-evident
change history, access log and successor. A person could challenge a material
error in their own record through an accessible route with interim protection
where delay risks liberty, subsistence, care or a vote. Correction would append
the reason and provenance rather than secretly overwrite the prior state.
Sensitive testimony and personal records would not become permanently public
because the institution needs an audit trail. Sector decision-makers would
remain answerable for their acts; depositing records elsewhere would not
transfer their service duties.

**Public evidence service.** A statutory service separate from Cabinet policy
units and sector delivery would receive only lawfully scoped data and protected
access to original evidence, including field checks when authorized. Its
professional statistics function would publish methods, definitions, source
families, uncertainty, missing populations, revisions and comparable time
series on a published schedule. An archival function would preserve authenticated
enacted texts, public budgets, decisions, methods and earlier statistical
vintages, with lawful restricted access for private or security-sensitive
material. Evaluation would be commissioned and reviewed through plural teams,
including outside researchers and affected people's challenges, rather than
declared true by the statistics producer. These functions could share secure
infrastructure but must have separate review of one another's errors and
conflicts. The technical C11 operator would maintain systems under contract;
it would not choose what a statistic means or whether a criticism is released.

**Appointments, money and control.** The Assembly would set published merit
criteria, an open nomination and hearing process, staggered terms and
for-cause removal for evidence-service leadership, with independent court
review of disputed removals. Staff would have professional and whistleblower
protections. A preauthorized bounded operating schedule would fund records
preservation, collection, fieldwork, privacy, accessibility and routine
publication through ordinary budget delay; elected institutions could change
future appropriations openly subject to existing duties. If an appointment
stalls, prior law would assign narrow acting custody and scheduled publication
to qualified existing staff, without granting the caretaker new rule-making
power or an indefinite mandate. A formal opposition role in scrutiny and
review of denied access is essential, but a veto by any party or professional
guild should not let nonappointment silence the office. Real independence
requires checking who actually controls the candidates, pay, data sources,
servers, protective officers, courts and execution of orders.

**Method changes and disputed meaning.** The statistics function would control
technical methods within law, with public reasons, affected-user consultation,
professional challenge and archived old series. A material classification,
coverage or algorithm change would identify who becomes newly invisible and
publish a bridge or explain why comparison is impossible. A minister could
propose a question and a resident could contest a category, but neither could
order a favorable estimate. Changes to eligibility or political membership
would require the relevant democratic and protected legal process, not a
statistical revision. AI transformations would identify their input evidence
families, model role and known loss of meaning; outputs from several agents
using the same source would not count as independent observations. Local and
unfamiliar concepts could be preserved alongside national comparisons rather
than forcibly averaged into one proxy.

**Access, privacy and remedies.** Publish aggregate results and methods in
usable formats and languages with equivalent non-digital access. Protected
source material would be disclosed only under a specific legal purpose,
minimum-necessary access and inspectable custody; neither universal openness
nor blanket secrecy is acceptable. People would have a route to correct
personal facts, institutions and researchers a route to challenge methodological
error, and opposition and affected communities a route to challenge unjustified
withholding or misleading suppression. An independent reviewer could order
preservation, inspection, correction or release within law; a court could
enforce it with named custodians and funded access. Where disclosure would
endanger a person, the remedy could use protected inspection, safe summary or
restricted access rather than publishing identifying testimony. Statistical
criticism must remain protected; a reviewer cannot compel agreement with the
service's causal interpretation.

## A shared-controller stress test

Suppose a governing coalition faces a water and housing failure before an
election. Sector offices change the definition of a serviced dwelling and
delay water-quality results; a common vendor holds the original records and
backups; the cabinet delays a new chief statistician's appointment and cuts
the service's operating cash. Several AI reports repeat the same altered
source and appear to corroborate one another. Under the selected model,
sector custodians must preserve original readings and eligibility decisions;
the evidence service must retain prior vintages, disclose its changed method
and source dependency, and use its standing funds and acting succession rule
to publish on schedule. Opposition, residents and independent researchers
can request source-grounded review through privacy-protecting routes; the
court can order lawful preservation and access. A separate operator or
protected physical copy must make the order executable if the vendor refuses.

The safeguard **fails** if the same coalition controls all source staff,
archival keys, acting appointment, budget release, court access and enforcement,
or if an independent reviewer cannot inspect the raw material safely. A formal
appointment rule or replicated AI audit would not cure that failure. The
test must distinguish a genuinely disputed measurement from unlawful
suppression and absent field capacity. It must not let a publication deadline
declare a contested estimate true. Records and continuing service claims
survive a cabinet or provider change, while lawful privacy restrictions
survive public pressure to expose individuals.

## Integration, limits and next question

For **C03**, independent election evidence, opposition information and
government replacement need source custody and public-archive succession
beyond the election office itself. For **C06**, Treasury and service operators
keep transaction responsibility while a separate service makes fiscal,
workforce and physical accounts comparable and exposes missing observations;
audit can challenge both. For **C09**, local ecological and network readings
retain place-specific meaning while national statistics disclose shared
resource assumptions and uncertainty. For **C11**, public knowledge gains a
governed production, archive and correction chain alongside protected science,
media, culture and replaceable technical infrastructure. For **C14**, trials
and evaluations use original evidence, visible revisions, plural interpretation
and elected policy choice; an improved score never adopts a law by itself.
The C04 local autonomy, C08 legal remedies and C12 alternative communication
routes remain applicable.

The selected service would require staff, secure records, fieldwork, translation,
privacy practice, backup media, independent reviewers and enforcement funds in
C06's same public account. Its collection powers could burden privacy, local
autonomy and small organizations; its categories could erase experiences it
cannot measure. Its leaders could become an expert elite or a single public
gatekeeper. The lawful answer is bounded purpose, contestable meaning, plural
outside observation and feasible exit, not a claim of neutral omniscience.
Older sector, data, audit and AI-sovereign clauses would need fresh lawful
reconciliation, with source records and pending correction claims transferred
to named successors; this research text does not change them.

**Evidence qualification:** the UN principles are a general official-statistics
comparator, not evidence that this design works in a real polity. The rest is
internal institutional reasoning, not observed national data. **Research
selection:** the federated-custody and separate-service candidate above.
**Legal authorization:** none. **Operational readiness:** no jurisdiction,
staff, independent controller map, funded archive, original national records
or tested alternate operator. **Observed outcomes:** none. No affected public,
independent reviewer or statistical profession participated in this decision.

The next bounded question is whether an adopting jurisdiction can preserve,
inspect and correct one contested cross-sector evidence chain during a
noncooperating cabinet and vendor transition. A responsible elected body would
commission a map of source custody, legal access, appointment and funding
controllers, archival successors, privacy risks, actual staff and due cash,
and would let affected people and opposing analysts challenge the frame.
Completion requires a witnessed preservation-to-remedy exercise and comparison
with retained sector reporting and a single central authority. No territory
or study budget is available now. Reopen this selection if that exercise shows
source access cannot survive shared control, if categories systematically
erase affected people, or if the separated service is less contestable than
the coherent alternatives.
