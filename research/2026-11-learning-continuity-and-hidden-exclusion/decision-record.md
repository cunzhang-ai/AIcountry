# Design217. Keep foundational learning usable through interruptions

## Baseline and issue

Design216 is the pinned baseline. Use the current framework and decision
template. S05 and ED already define plural learning, local public responsibility
and a narrow safety restriction. XR04 protects cross-local transfer. The gap is
a provider-induced or practical loss of the learning offer outside formal
suspension. A child can stay on a school roll, have some materials sent home or
move to an alternative that nobody can actually deliver. In those situations
the child may lose teaching while each institution reports a different success.

## Alternatives and selection

Select ED21–ED28. Use existing owners and preserve the child's practical
relationship with learning. Act on serious interruptions, repeated loss or an
unverified material reduction. Assign a local owner, preserve first dates and
make temporary teaching possible before final placement and assessment. An
outside inspector reviews an institution-initiated material reduction within ten
teaching days at most. Earlier access, safety and court duties prevail. The
deadline is a provisional operating choice, not an estimate of optimal timing.

Strong ordinary repair is the first rival and part of the selected response.
Better classroom support, timely adjustments and a qualified substitute
can prevent the need for a parallel pathway. Retain the live alternative of
using ordinary supervision without a separate timed inspection step where
equivalent protection can be demonstrated. An ordinary attendance campaign can
support routine reengagement but can also reward an inaccurate denominator
or pressure a child to use an inaccessible place. Centralizing all provision in
one specialist service could pool scarce skill, with travel, loss of relationships,
segregation and common failure risks. A payment or voucher can expand choice
where an actual provider exists. The funding alone cannot produce a trained
worker at a required time.

England's administrative series identify different populations and rates.
The inspectorates observed both gaps and usable local responses. One
Ombudsman decision distinguishes actual fault, an agreed remedy and receipt
of instruction. These sources support the decision's distinctions, without
proving the design superior or estimating its workload. In the authored case
the shortage is a concurrent support shift rather than teaching headcount.

## Authority, privacy and transition

Keep ED02–ED05 administration and independent review, ED09 plural routes,
ED12–ED15 site and correction rules, S05 and S06 learning and catch-up, and
XR, MF, DP, PD, SR and OP/T. A school may not decide the merits of its own
contested reduction. Elected local bodies fund actual work under existing law.
Assembly authority is needed for a new source or substantive duty. The inspector
can check and order within its lawful scope. The court remains available and
separately checks inspection. No compulsory new family dossier, diagnosis,
home inspection, public child trace, attendance penalty or AI decision power is
selected. Leave voluntary participation, safeguarding and clinical law distinct.

For any authorized implementation keep original dates, independent challenges,
privacy limits and paid staff. Map actual outstanding provision to competent
accepted officers and providers, with service overlap. Families and staff
must not have to recreate claims lost by the institution. Dated editions retain
their scope. Actual affected participation, independent qualified review, legal
adoption, funded operation and better learning outcomes are open.

## Reopen with a paid school-calendar comparison

Test one authorized local term with paid accessible input from learners,
families, staff and independent reviewers, and an anonymized record of
shortened offers and equivalent alternatives. Compare school-based repair
with a shared alternate slot and show concurrent qualified staff, source,
actual teaching, useful-time correction and effects on other learners. Use
ordinary routes where they already work. Reopen for delayed remedy, coercive
placement, hidden denominator change, unsafe disclosure or evidence that the
inspection burden displaces more useful work than it protects. Without safe
custody, do not collect identifiable testimony.
