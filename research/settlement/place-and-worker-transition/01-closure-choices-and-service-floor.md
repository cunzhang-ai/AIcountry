# One town after the loss of an anchor employer

Design329 · Constructed comparison for [AT23–AT27](../../country/C05/18/06-place-and-worker-transition.md)

A closure affects 100 workers including supplier staff. This case stipulates
a 10-unit verified first-period household income duty through existing
programmes. It is separate from the employer's accrued wage, safety and
remediation duties. The town's fixed and marginal common-service cost is
20 before and after the first shock. Its qualified ordinary local source
drops from 14 to 8 and the existing national grant remains 6, leaving
6 uncovered. The shared additional first-period public envelope is 20.
All amounts are illustrative.

Twelve households request a supported move. The host can accept eight
complete routes with suitable housing and services at a joint cost of 4,
including its additional work. The remaining four lack a qualified
receiver. Assume no established individual right to the specific optional
move. Preserve any ordinary urgent housing or care claim separately.
Local housing, school and utility access for those who stay use the
20-unit common-service schedule.

| Package | First-period disposition |
|---|---|
| Employer rescue at 14, with household relief at 10 | Requires 24 before repairing the common-service gap or buying host access. Claimed jobs and future receipts are unverified. |
| Household relief and mobility alone | Costs 14 and leaves the town's service obligation unfunded by 6 unless it makes a separate budget decision. |
| Protect both resident choices and the common floor | Spend 10 on the verified household duty, 6 on common services and 4 on eight accepted moves. The 20-unit envelope fits. Four optional moves still lack capacity. |

Select the third package on the authored conditions. It funds no
employer rescue or ongoing expansion. Protecting the floor and
supporting voluntary moves can be done together. If the host loses
four of its eight accepted slots, fund the four remaining qualified
routes at the stipulated 0.5 each and disclose eight unresolved
requests. The two unused units do not supply homes or permission
to move anyone. Review the receiving and separately fund lawful
urgent protection if needed.

If the local source stays at 8 and the cost at 20, the ordinary grant of
6 still leaves a recurring gap of 6 after the finite support expires.
An authorized 6-unit recurring correction would close that gap. A
separate 4-unit preparation option would qualify small local employers
and a training provider for a later shared-work offer. The next budget
would need 10 for the common correction and preparation together before
any accepted job or build commitment. A diversification proposal claiming
5 of future annual tax receipts still
supplies zero for current planning if the receipts are unqualified. If
all 5 later become lawful and durable, there is still a residual 1
under the stipulated common cost. An operator's cleanup bond is
restricted and remains outside both budget calculations.
