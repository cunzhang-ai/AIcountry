# CB07–CB12: finite mandates rather than a permanent developer or manager

*Part of [C09/14](../14-shared-buildings-and-neighbor-rights.md); normative voting choices.*

**CB07 — Ordinary owner decisions.** One principal unit has one owner vote,
cast through its actual owner or jointly authorized representative; co-owner
disagreement is no extra vote and leaves that unit in the full denominator.
Ordinary budget, necessary-plan contractor selection and manager appointment/
replacement require actual yes from more than half of the full registered
owner-vote entitlements (initially one per principal unit)
**and** more than half of fixed ordinary common-cost shares. Units with arrears,
unsold units and absentees remain counted; abstention is not assent. No second
meeting shrinks the denominator, chair casting vote or developer-vendor default.
Subdivision cannot increase a preexisting unit's aggregate voting entitlement
or shift another's cost without lawful heard reallocation; replacement subunits
share its old vote/cost entitlement pending that actual change. Notify people
how fractional interests are represented; no silent rounding into extra votes.

**CB08 — Optional improvement, borrowing and reserved rights.** A new
discretionary capital improvement or association loan needs at least three-
quarters actual owner votes and three-quarters common-cost shares, with full
costs, alternatives, actual finance and repayment schedule. The same protected
full roll applies. No mandatory personal owner guarantee, private-unit lien or
pledge of restricted repair reserves; a lender takes only actually authorized
association assets/claims under PT/U. Changing private title, ownership shares,
or an acquired individual use/easement needs each materially burdened holder's
actual lawful consent or an independent applicable court/LA source, not this
supermajority. Ordinary safety/access renewal is CB12, not optional capital
merely because it costs more. New luxury cannot masquerade as required repair.

**CB09 — Resident ballots and fair process.** For funded ordinary-use rules,
at least one-third of all eligible adult residents must participate, rounding
the required participant number upward, and actual yes must exceed half of
participants, including abstainers in that participant denominator. No resident
count/AI response is consent by itself; an unknown or genuinely empty roll
cannot supply a vote. Keep the last lawful rule on failure, while actual safety/
access duties continue. This lighter turnout rule is deliberately different
from property-cost decisions; small active groups and disengagement remain risks.
Give both owner/resident ballots14calendar days' usable notice, exact text,
cost/scope, alternatives, supported/offline/secret voting and protected objections.
Any affected person can put a relevant proposal before the next ordinary ballot;
a manager cannot filter criticism. Publish safe tally/reasons/dissent, protect
individual votes and direct B challenge. A discretionary adverse new use rule
starts no earlier than14calendar days after usable outcome notice; no vote
retroactively approves a breach. Necessary protection need not await a ballot.

**CB10 — A named and replaceable manager.** Owners appoint an actually
competent person/provider under CB07 for a stated task/budget/maximum12-month
renewable mandate; small buildings can use a qualified owner without inventing
a permanent paid committee. Qualification follows actual tasks and lawful
technical roles, not a compulsory private guild. Publish actual control,
conflicts, fees, safe records, complaints, limits and substitute contact.
The same CB07 vote can replace management; lawful earned worker/contract dues
and paid records/cash handover remain. No automatic long exclusive developer
contract, renewal lock, termination bounty or manager control of the electorate.
Managers implement lawful resident-use decisions and actual owner commitments;
they cannot finally determine disputed title/debt, punish or authorize eviction.
Expired delegation ends new discretionary commitments, not existing safety/
contract/service duties; owners must arrange a real funded successor.

**CB11 — Procurement without self-dealing.** Publish proportionate independent
comparison, actual quotes/connected controllers, qualification and full lifecycle
cost; no fixed expensive tender ritual for a replacement light. A material
related-party award additionally needs yes from more than half of all unconflicted
owner-vote entitlements and more than half of their cost shares, plus CB07/CB08's
ordinary full-denominator threshold. Conflicted assents cannot satisfy either
threshold; owning an ordinary shared benefit is not alone a special conflict.
If no unconflicted constituency exists or conflicts prevent lawful approval,
seek independent ordinary-court approval only
of a proved necessary/fair transaction; nobody approves their own exception.
A court's narrow necessary-repair power under CB18 is separate from voting.
Immediate qualified making-safe cannot wait for tenders: preserve original
need, scope, price/source and later challenge, not a permanent urgency exception.

**CB12 — Required maintenance does not depend on turnout.** The association
owes safe structure, fire/access and essential shared systems, ordinary repair
and a qualified rolling ten-year maintenance/renewal plan updated annually and
on material change. Distinguish actual condition, uncertainty, service life,
work, funding and receiving from a predicted calendar. A small safe building's
plan may be a short condition/work/source schedule; no annual full engineering
report merely because a year passed. Critical judgments need
actual independent competent evidence; a salesperson's AI forecast is not a
building inspection. Owner approval chooses lawful means, not whether the duty
exists. Manager's express statutory task permits necessary routine work within
funded accepted plans and minimum immediate making-safe on qualified evidence,
with actual contact/source records. No unilateral optional redevelopment,
unbounded new loan or private entry follows. Apply H04's earlier immediate/
24-hour danger response and ordinary2-working-day acknowledgment/7-calendar-day
plan/30-calendar-day repair with independently challengeable safe extension.
An owner/resident can report directly to association, landlord, technical role
or court; a landlord's forwarding delay cannot reset first protected receipt.
Disputed tender, fault recovery, empty votes or unsafe nonpayment does not cancel
the duty. Missing safe cash/crew remains actual unmet repair, not an order that
magically completes it; CB17/CB18/H receiving and elected scarcity law apply.
