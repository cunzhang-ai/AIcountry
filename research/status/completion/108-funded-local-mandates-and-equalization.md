# Scoped finding. Join national funding to actual local duties

Design200 fills a material source and authority gap between general
equalization, sector finance and local receipt. MF01–MF08 requires
a financed new assignment, no double attribution, timely undisputed
payments, lawful independent correction and local method choice.
[Canonical clauses](../../country/C04/08-funded-local-mandates-and-equalization.md)

The Danish records identify allocation, budget conditions and
separate municipal and national amounts. Authored cases test unequal
local needs with equal population, a year-end balanced path with
a due-date shortfall, a grant conversion and a shared staff failure.
No actual national source, qualified cost, lawful implementation
or received service follows. [Matched case](../../settlement/funded-local-mandates/02-one-source-several-duties.md)
