# Design211. Adaptive home support

A person can preserve a defined service floor while changing the ordinary
method with a qualified team. A material reduction returns to an independent
public determination. Urgent additions, funded relief, clinical handoff and
actual pay keep their own authority. Budget changes preserve recurring costs
and genuine shortage. A strong existing Danish rival already has integrated
adaptive care. Evidence about its structure and reported continuity supplies
no general comparative winner. Actual access, accepted workers, costs and
better lives remain unresolved.
