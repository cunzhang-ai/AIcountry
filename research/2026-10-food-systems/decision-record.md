# Decision record: food as an ordinary system of living

5 October 2026 · Integrated design 48 research selection; not enacted law

## Baseline and question

The [design-47 predecessor](predecessor-design-47/README.md) preserves the
working country before this choice. C09 names a food-security authority for
availability, safe procurement, reserve rotation and last-mile delivery;
C07 provides income and emergency in-kind access; C08 protects buyers and
sector food safety. They do not yet join ordinary dietary adequacy, retail
access, production and labor, land and water, import exposure, food safety,
market power and the household remedy in one elected resource decision.
The question is who can protect sufficient, safe and usable food every day
without turning a food office into an unreviewable planner or a state diet
authority.

This authored choice uses the proposed [framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md). It does not represent affected
participation, independent review, consent or a change to current law.
The values at stake are freedom from hunger, health, producer autonomy,
consumer choice, fair work, ecological continuity, privacy, culture,
international responsibility and democratic control. People affected
include eaters who cannot use ordinary shops, producers, farm and food
workers, traders, retailers, school and care users, downstream communities
and people abroad who bear imported harms. None has been heard in an
AIcountry jurisdiction.

## Rival explanations and whole packages

Hunger or poor diets may result from insufficient physical supply, low
income, unsafe water, high prices, market gatekeeping, distant retail,
unpaid care time, unsuitable food, unsafe products or land and ecological
damage. These are empirically different and can coexist. Original household
journeys, producer contracts, price and margin data, stock quality,
environmental observations, clinical evidence and nonuser accounts would
distinguish them. A retailer's sales data or an AI reconstruction alone
cannot show what a person ate or why.

| Package | Mechanism and possible benefit | Serious objection or failure |
|---|---|---|
| Keep separate C09 stock, C07 cash and C08 safety duties | Uses existing owners and little new coordination | No one must reconcile ordinary access, nutrition, ecology, imports and producers when all change together |
| Central food-planning ministry with production targets and household allocation | May direct stock and crisis response quickly | Concentrates land, purchasing, data and coercive power; can erase diets, local knowledge and dissent |
| Mostly market-led food provision with cash support and general competition law | Protects plural supply and household choice | Thin markets, common buyers, unsafe goods or inaccessible shops can leave cash unable to buy food |
| **Selected: joined statutory food-system duty with distinct sector decisions** | Assembly sees one supply/access/safety/ecology account; C09 coordinates evidence and plans, C07 delivers personal access, C08 enforces safety, C05/C10 retain market and trade roles | Handoffs can be slow, data can be incomplete, and a coordinator can still become captured by suppliers or cabinet |
| Predominantly local food sovereignty | May reflect place, culture and ecological knowledge | Unequal land, urban dependency and cross-place shocks may overwhelm local capacity; national redistribution still needs legitimate law |

The selected [C09 route](../country/C09/09-food-systems-and-nutrition.md)
does not choose a single ownership form, national diet or numeric stock.
The [C06 account](../country/C06/10-food-system-finance.md) names cost and
resource collisions; the [C13 exercise](../country/C13/13-drought-price-and-food-safety.md)
tests crop, price, water and contamination together. Real affected-public
choice could reject this arrangement for a better local, central or market
package.

## Evidence and limits

[FAO's sustainable food and agriculture account](https://www.fao.org/sustainability)
distinguishes availability, access, utilization and stability; it also
connects economic, social and ecological sustainability. The
[FAO right-to-food guidelines](https://www.fao.org/right-to-food/resources/voluntary-guidelines/)
address participation, accountability, resources, markets, safety,
nutrition and monitoring. The [CFS food systems and nutrition
guidelines](https://www.fao.org/cfs/policy-products/voluntary-guidelines-and-policy-recommendations/voluntary-guidelines-on-food-systems-and-nutrition)
link nutrition to food-system policy. [WHO's healthy-diet
guidance](https://www.who.int/en/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet)
notes that diet must be safe and varies with person and context; its
[food-safety strategy](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240057685)
addresses foodborne harm. These official sources identify relevant
dimensions, not evidence that AIcountry's institutional split is superior.
The FAO and CFS guidelines do not themselves enact AIcountry law or settle
its actual treaty duties. No current event, real local observation or
affected testimony was verified for an AIcountry territory because none
is defined.

## Authority, compatibility and continuity

The Assembly chooses the food-access floor, food authority remit, reserve
and trade powers, standards, budgets and any new scarcity rule. Cabinet
proposes and executes within law. C09's technical authority records and
warns but cannot tax, ration, requisition, rewrite a land title or waive
an ecological floor. C07 owns household and sector meal claims, C08 the
specific safety order and buyer remedy, C05 labor and competition, C10
trade and foreign commitments, C11 original evidence and correction.
Local councils and affected communities influence siting and delivery
within their lawful scope. Courts review rights, competence and procedure;
they do not choose the national crop mix. The appointment, funding, data,
review and enforcement chain needs testing under a cabinet or dominant
buyer that dislikes an adverse warning. More agencies alone do not make
the chain independent.

An adopter must inspect actual food, agriculture, fisheries, land, water,
labor, safety, trade, procurement, social-assistance, privacy, budget and
local-government law. Existing tenure, permits, wages, contracts, benefit
claims, safety cases and source evidence survive until competent law
assigns successors. New reserve or procurement commitments need actual
cash, safe storage, inspectors, roads and alternate suppliers. The same
stock, cold room or worker cannot be pledged to clinical, school and
emergency routes simultaneously. International cooperation remains bound
by actual treaties and partner consent; import diversification cannot
export unaccounted harm.

Objections remain live: the food authority might hide poor diets behind
availability, food support might stigmatize, producer subsidies might
entrench incumbents, and broad nutrition policy might police private life.
Conversely, weak coordination could delay response or leave a central
buyer unchecked. Missing evidence about causes is separate from the
normative decision about how much public responsibility and choice to
guarantee. A good aggregate nutrition result cannot justify arbitrary
coercion or the loss of an individual remedy.

**Evidence:** official conceptual and normative guidance plus authored
design; no AIcountry original observation or affected participation.
**Research selection:** joined food-system duty with separate access,
safety, production, ecological and trade powers. **Legal authorization:**
none. **Operational readiness:** no jurisdiction, stock, staff, law,
budget, supply contracts or tested delivery. **Observed outcomes:** none.

The next bounded test would compare this split with a central-planning
and a market-led package in one real possible jurisdiction, following an
actual household, farm and unsafe lot through a simultaneous supply and
price shock. An authorized research team would need lawful source access,
participant protection and an approved time and cost limit; none exists
here. Reopen the selection if food remains inaccessible despite aggregate
supply, safety and access decisions conflict without remedy, ecological
damage is hidden, producers or suppliers capture the authority, or a rival
performs better under real democratic control.
