# C06. One country operating reference, with separate powers and sources

2 November 2026 · Design 125 · Research selection, not an adopted budget

The country now has a joined **named-resource reference** for all 37 audited
functions, later commitment modules, essential services and ordinary private
life. It replaces superseded components once; it does not price every current
obligation. The reference year is an authored mature 2030 cross-section, not
an unaged 2026 cohort, a launch date or a claim that transition is complete.

Existing OP public-backed plural provision remains conditional. Direct public
domestic provision is a genuine rival. The selected contribution is a
common-scope operating reference; no new office, governing procedure, source
power, entitlement cap or election clock is created. Full actual national cost,
source yield, qualified capacity, legitimate affected choice and observed better
lives remain unknown.
