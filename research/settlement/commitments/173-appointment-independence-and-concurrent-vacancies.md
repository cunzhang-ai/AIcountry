# Commitment extension 173. Appointment independence and concurrent vacancies

[OJ55–OJ59](../../country/C08/25/12-appointment-independence-and-concurrent-vacancies.md) carries ordinary judicial selection,
protected claims, pay, cases and evidence into a separately staffed
process. Preserve statutory appointing votes, hearing clocks and judicial
independence even when an appointment remains unresolved.
