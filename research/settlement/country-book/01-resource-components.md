# Unique resource components and proposed payroll

*Part of [country operating book](README.md); authored bids, not a calibrated budget.*

Eight payroll components replace older public-pay aggregates. Higher/middle
reference annual pay is 60,000/30,000; no wage rule, actual skill or job
assignment is enacted. Source workers must be willing and qualified, with
existing claims and prior work accounted for.

Public ordinary operating cash is 1,053.05. Existing 16 network maintenance
and 0.5 training-site maintenance are included, not added again. Public
gross replacement 29.75 and private replacement 96 equal the declared wear
on public assets 595 and market assets 1,920. Public replacement already
contains old-project 5.4/training-facility 0.35. Private costs, tariffs and
public mission costs still need actual inclusion/overlap and title review.

Shares allocate these exact same components across all 37 functions. They
are analytical bid conventions, not proof of public priority, staff transfer,
independence, actual duty quantity or complete functional cost. Unpriced
requirements and crisis/legacy obligations are not made zero.
