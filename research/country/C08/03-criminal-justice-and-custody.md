# C08. Criminal justice and custody

*Part of [C08 everyday law and justice](../C08-everyday-law-and-justice.md); integrated design 70 research, not enacted law.*

## Criminal justice and custody

The selected [penal model](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-core-institutions/crime-justice.md) gives prevention
and immediate safety their own lawful grounds; neither depends on conviction.
The Assembly defines prospective offenses, culpability, defenses and maximum
sanctions for serious harm or risk where civil, regulatory and service responses
are insufficient. Police investigate both inculpatory and contrary evidence under
defined powers; independent prosecutors give reviewable reasons for charge,
noncharge or lawful diversion; independent courts determine guilt; defense has
practical access to counsel and evidence. Torture and coerced evidence are excluded
under the stronger everyday-justice candidate. Custody clocks cannot reset through
transfers or a new administrative label. A report, risk prediction or failure to
prove an allegation is not itself proof of a crime by anyone.
Under the [reproductive-autonomy route](06-reproductive-autonomy-and-parentage.md),
a pregnancy outcome is not by itself an offense or grounds to compel
testimony; enacted serious coercion or violence cases still require
their own elements, evidence, defenses and fair trial.
Under the [crisis-liberty rule](09-mental-health-crisis-liberty-and-consent.md),
distress, self-harm, diagnosis and help-seeking are not offenses. A separate
harmful act still needs its own prospective elements and proof; clinical
records or prediction cannot turn a person into a suspect by default.

After guilt is proved, the court compares a legally available no-sanction outcome,
restitution, a subsistence-protecting fine, bounded community measures and custody.
It chooses an adequate, proportionate response with reasons and independent appeal.
An alternative must not place someone under supervision who otherwise would have
received no sanction; inability to pay cannot alone turn a fine into imprisonment.
For grave culpable harm, including serious violence, lawful custody remains possible
when a less restrictive adequate response will not protect people or satisfy
proportionate accountability. It needs humane conditions, independent health care,
defense and family access where safe, external inspection and actual release planning.
Sentence length cannot depend on an opaque future-risk score or on treatment,
homelessness or administrative delay. Basic services and lawful supervision after
release need named providers; a completed prison program is not reintegration.

An enacted civilian corrections authority, separate from police and prosecution,
executes custody and lawful community supervision under court orders. Regional
operation answers to national rights and budgets; courts control sentence and
release legality, while an independent inspector can enter facilities, protect
complaints and require a court-enforceable correction. The health authority remains
responsible for clinical care in custody through protected professional judgment.
Before release, corrections and the receiving local service authorities agree a
funded, reviewable handoff for documents, housing, treatment and support; a local
shortfall cannot extend a lawful sentence. Neither a prison commander nor a
software record may postpone a release order. Whether a more local corrections
model could meet these duties with less concentration remains open.

Restorative processes are optional under enacted safeguards and cannot replace
investigation of serious or official abuse. Participants receive independent advice,
safe refusal and withdrawal; coercive dependence, threats and unequal local power
must be assessed. Refusal cannot increase punishment or remove support. Victim
protection, a protective restriction and child or property arrangements have their
own grounds, expiry and review, separate from guilt. A false or mistaken allegation
does not justify automatic punishment of the reporter; an accused person can contest
unlawful detention, overbroad restrictions and data disclosure promptly.
The [interpersonal-safety route](10-interpersonal-violence-protection-and-fair-process.md)
applies this separation to intimate, family and sexual harm: a survivor can
receive help without a charge, a time-limited protection order needs its own
evidence and challenge, and serious violence retains ordinary criminal proof.

The [candidate criminal core P01–P20/K01–K12](12-criminal-core-and-procedure.md)
now specifies fault, participation, defenses, harm elements, counsel, exclusion,
24-hour first hearing, aggregate remand and finite sentence boundaries.
Minimum responsibility 16 replaces the older 14 in this research draft;
child conduct before 18 retains its separate five-year ceiling. Numerical
values remain hypotheses, not adopted law or a complete special code.

[K13–K24/Q01–Q12](15-special-crimes-and-bounded-sanctions.md) now supplies
proposed special harms, needs-protecting fines, chosen finite community
measures, attributable body sanctions, confiscation, records and prosecution
periods. These are new research choices, not fresh approval of all old rules
or an enacted complete code. Actual safety, funding and independent actors
remain unverified; no penalty receipt finances assumed national capacity.

[OJ21–OJ30](25-ordinary-justice-and-public-prosecution.md) now selects public
prosecution leadership/careers and actual case accountability: open Assembly
dual-vote seven-year DPP, protected staff powers, written instructions/own signed
overrides, protected dissent, direct review and three preselected external case
prosecutors for implicated command. P/K proof/defense/force/periods remain;
courts cannot order guilt or an unsupported charge. This closes the119 positive
choice, not actual staff/source/capture. Prosecutors, defense, investigators and
judges remain separate; pending cases and actual source-law mandates continue.

[PO](27-civilian-enforcement-and-public-control.md) now supplies concrete police,
external-inquiry and civilian-corrections leadership/employers, with separate
case/court/source authority and actual original-clock/receiving duties. PO13's
corrections director is a protected ongoing C04 career; Police Chief/DCI use
separately stated confirmation/term/status law. No P/K/force/penalty change.

burden for continued custody and a fixed endpoint for conditions and recall.
