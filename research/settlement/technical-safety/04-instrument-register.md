# Statute-to-instrument register: specified decisions, missing local evidence

*Part of [technical safety](README.md); a register enacts no rule or permit.*

| Duty owner | Candidate substantive choice now specified | Required actual instrument/source before annex-dependent permission or penalty |
|---|---|---|

| TD12 radiation/exceptional activities | Graded source control, no automatic patient/general dose equivalence; prior particular Act for nuclear/open self-propagating release/launch-reentry | Radiation/source/medical-exposure annexes and particular Acts with competent evidence, international duties, real long-term capacity/liability |

For every instrument record exact issuer/source clause, free full operative text,
protected original evidence, applicable scope/units/method/uncertainty, approval
route, effective/expiry dates, review and transition owner. Material risk/access/
fiscal changes return to elected law under C03. An update does not approve itself.

These are explicit unresolved instruments, not an assertion of zero cost or an
invitation for an inspector to invent conditions. Ordinary duties already stated
can stand where lawfully adopted without a missing numeric annex; actual current
law remains until authorized revision. Annex-dependent new activity cannot receive
an unproved new authorization. Existing essential operations need TS15's lawful
mapping, actual hazard correction and paid continuity, not blanket cancellation.

A coherent generic country proposal can specify who decides what and the duties
that constrain them; it cannot truthfully invent local geology, operating aircraft,
clinical evidence or a safely staffed radiation regime. The whole-country audit
must distinguish this context boundary from a missing national design choice and
from a real unresolved contradiction. No blanket completion claim follows here.
