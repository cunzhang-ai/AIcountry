# JC23–JC27. Keep shared emergency capacity usable across regions

*Design271 · Proposed first-budget and emergency allocation rules.*

**JC23 — Fund local response and a distinct national call capacity.**
The Assembly defines eligible services, common minimums, trigger,
permitted assignments, source, expiry and review. Elected local bodies
and qualified responders maintain paid local first response and may
agree lawful mutual aid. The national budget identifies each callable
team or asset, its custodian, paid availability, reach, rest and return
time, maintenance, transport, finance and dependencies. Measure what
remains for the sending area after release, including foreseeable
simultaneous need. Preserve its applicable floor. A lower-capacity
locality can receive ordinary support under [MF](../../C04/08-funded-local-mandates-and-equalization.md)
to establish that floor. Do not count the same resource in two protected
plans unless both can be met on their dates. Keep statutory powers and
sovereign or territorial agreements within their own scope.

**JC24 — Apply a preauthorized rule when lawful requests conflict.**
Use the designated public dispatch officer within a published statutory
mandate when concurrent eligible requests exceed available timely help
or ordinary coordination would miss a useful deadline. Separate lawful
and physically feasible assignments from competing harms. Compare
compatible whole assignments and credible substitutes for each
locality. Within each service's law give priority to imminent,
avoidable death or serious irreversible harm and account for severity,
time, uncertainty and consequences at both ends. Keep the lawful
safety and staffing floor of each assignment. If cases remain materially
equivalent, apply the prepublished neutral tie rule with a record of
eligible cases, and use an independently auditable draw if authorized
and feasible in time. Queue order and regional contribution cannot
stand in for harm or access. Do not batch past a safe dispatch time.
Use the relevant sector law when priorities conflict across services.
The Assembly decides any residual common authority or change of priority
under [C04](../../C04/01-national-local-authority.md). Dispatch does
not acquire a general power to ration rights or command local services.

**JC25 — Give a resource one issuer at a time and verify receipt.**
Each calling right names one custodian, specific resources, an interval
and a successor. Dispatch records the assignment, current custodian,
accepted local incident lead and latest safe arrival. A transfer of
calling authority requires accepted handoff. If coordination fails,
use preassigned nonoverlapping local or regional authority with
locally verifiable limits, safe paper or radio records and the applicable
ordinary rescue powers. An unreachable custodian does not make its
reserve available to a second dispatcher. Reconcile commitments and
report conflicts before rejoining the shared pool. A status update,
routing message or departure does not establish safe arrival, usable
operation or release after rest. The receiving commander confirms
those facts in their own scope and can refuse unsafe deployment.

**JC26 — Review before irreversible loss where time allows.** A local
incident lead or directly affected person can report omitted facts,
incorrect status or discriminatory treatment through an accessible
independent route, with direct help for places that cannot file
quickly. Publish criteria before use, give reasons at dispatch or as
soon as safety permits, and protect identities and operational
details. Keep a qualified independent duty reviewer with separate
funding, records and a live contact route. The reviewer can require
timely reconsideration and refer lawful interim relief to the
competent court, within applicable authority. A challenge need not
hold safe rescue in place. A refusal, denial or late arrival stays
visible with the exposed task, original deadline, lawful substitute
and remedy route. Preserve the duty and residual harm after closure
of the dispatch decision.

**JC27 — Test the reserve after the first dispatch.** Verify next-call
readiness, transport, rest, payroll, damage, replacement, cash and
accepted release. Fund a geographically and operationally independent
second route where the elected comparison needs one. Report each
uncovered task if cash, communications or real staffing are insufficient.
Where recurring use shows the ordinary base is too small, put the
cost of ordinary capacity, prevention and alternative service
organization to an elected source decision under [FB](../62-lasting-services-and-fiscal-shocks.md).
AI may compare compatible schedules and expose double booking, biased
inputs and missing reports. A responsible officer authorizes a real
dispatch. Responders verify safe operation, and the independent
reviewer determines the review outcome through existing authority.
