## Diagnoses, alternatives and reasons

One failure is low resources; another is treating other people's property
as the claimant's accessible money; a third is conflicting tax, benefit,
housing and care timing. A public cash transfer may repair money access
without producing a carer or affordable home. Agency, solidarity, safe
child access and work choice are values, not a national welfare score.

A pooled assessment can reduce public outlay and recognize real shared
consumption, but automatic partner-income deeming can sustain private
dependence. Safe pooling with protected independent access remains open.
B assesses persons and actual component/receipts, with voluntary cooperation,
public claimant help and source-limited evidence; it can cost more and
burden administration. Select B conditionally for person-level standing,
not because the model proves it cheapest or causally best. C universal
personal payments plus targeted costs can reduce intrusive targeting and
stigma; differently financed versions remain legitimate. The illustrated
C uses the same assumed taxes and costs more, which is not a general rejection.

The 50% taper, 20% earnings disregard, pension disregard, three-month
asset buffer and twelve-month usable-asset draw are explicit hypotheses.
Actual needs values use goods/access observations and prospective elected
method, not a GDP percentage or a cheaper basket chosen to pass a ledger.
I02 preserves personal cash agency against complete in-kind substitution;
I03 credits one real shared bill and requires an actual safe alternative
before a reference cap removes unavoidable housing support. I06 distinguishes
paid support from owed support, preserves child access and separate maintenance
law, and prevents a second cost deduction or new national resource.

## Objections, control and migration

Individual eligibility may subsidize nominally affluent households; disclose
that distribution and assess actual received resources, without forcing
marriage/abuse proof before protection. Expense recognition can subsidize
landlords or poor work arrangements; use real housing alternatives, supply,
labor duties and evidenced necessary cost rather than automatic luxury
reimbursement. More matching records can burden people or expose survivors;
government assembles proportionate evidence and private correction, ordinary
declarations and no compulsory family dossier remain valid. Disregards can
be arbitrary or expensive; keep their values contestable and jointly costed.
Asset plans cannot treat blocked balances as cash. These are authored
objections; they are not public assent or independent reviewer endorsement.

Existing public-administration appointments, separate revenue/payment,
evidence custody, audit/opposition and independent courts retain control.
No new super-authority sets policy or approves its own mandate. Due claims,
accrued earnings pensions, disability cost support, child protection and
privacy survive transition, with source/person/period/component reconciliation.
Actual inventory costs/quantities remain unknown; reference data is separate.
