# Compatible water, explicit bills and a rejectable public default

*Part of [water settlement](README.md); all numbers below are authored units.*

Two users may each hold a100-unit gross grant while their authorized depletion
is100 and20 because returns differ. A compatible60-unit depletion pool gives
50 and10 depletion under the chosen proportional rule, with50gross each at
those qualified return assumptions. Giving30net each instead would require150
gross for the second user and break its100gross cap. Source/time/rate/quality/
lag constraints must hold too; these are not actual return coefficients.

Taking100/returning60 consumes40. Taking70/returning20 consumes50: a smaller
diversion need not save water for others. Reusing12 of an original20return leaves
8downstream, not the old20 plus another12. A groundwater pumping permission
cannot erase delayed river capture at expiry. A zero-net use still has a gross
rate/conveyance/quality/time limit; annual rain cannot cover a dry-month outage.

A135cost,10already-provided resources and15compatible public payment leaves110
for ALL customer tariffs. Farm30/industry20 leaves household60, not another
110-minus50 pool billed in addition to those same commercial receipts. Optional
farm expansion40 raises its attributable share rather than silently the home's;
a public subsidy needs its own identified paid source. Unreceived assistance is
no operator cash, and the same bill cannot be funded twice. These are conditional
identities, not a selected tariff or qualified full-country source.

Fixed readiness charges can be regressive, small systems costly and historical
rights exclusionary. Compare explicit equalization/public investment and actual
needs with a uniform tariff, real market allocation, elected catchment authority,
community management and retaining current licensed/plural operation. The direct
public new/residual default may lose that comparison. More public titles or an
outside career Reviewer can concentrate common patron/source power. No full
cost/independence/hydrology/affected choice or observed winner is established.

Existing power, TD07 health safety, CP pools/clocks, I05/EB/Y/work/source and
actual prior rights continue. Exact paid OP/T successors carry permit/title/
collective/customer/worker/pension/contract/concession/creditor/security/record/
foreign claims. First-January≥12months is a prospective method hypothesis, not
current adoption, delivery or a clock reset. The next whole-country gap can be
elsewhere; water-engineering/tariff questions do not automatically set the queue.
