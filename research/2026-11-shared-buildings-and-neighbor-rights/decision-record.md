## Situation and requested decision

Continue the full future democratic-country design. Inspect148's bounded
common-building/neighbor question rather than assuming every topic is missing.
H04 already gave repair/receiving clocks, H08 resident voice, PT06 private-unit/
share protection, B tort/execution and TD05 actual building duties. Retain them.
Missing were ordinary common-property decision/cost/manager/deadlock/access
allocations and a direct substantial neighbor-interference rule. These design
gaps differ from unknown actual titles, condition, competent people, source/
authority and outcomes. Filling them does not complete the whole country.

The causal problem is shared physical dependence plus unequal control of title,
money, information and exit. A roof cannot be repaired one title at a time;
democratic labels or faster AI scheduling cannot supply money/control. Values:
safe private life, protected property, practical resident voice, reciprocal use,
nonpunitive need support and reachable independent redress. Definitions separate
owner, resident, affected person, source duty, lawful act, receipt and completed
work. Empirical claims about better outcomes remain unproved. Thresholds,
shares, horizons and mandates are explicit normative hypotheses.

## Alternatives and selection

Retain familiar source law/informal cooperation where actual protection works;
do not add paperwork just to make a new edition. Owner-only governance aligns
financing but leaves renter life dependent on landlords. Stronger resident dual
consent can constrain ownership but adds deadlock/source/property disputes.
Cooperative/public/common-service alternatives need legitimate acquisition,
real capital/worker/source and safeguards against their own monopoly/exclusion.

Select CB01–CB24: narrow common-property association for newly separately owned
shared buildings; single-owner landlord remains responsible. Required safe
maintenance never needs majority permission. Owner budget/manager decisions
need more than half full registered owner entitlements and cost shares;
optional capital/loan at least three-quarters both. Split units preserve old
aggregate entitlement until lawful reallocation. No majority sells private
units, shifts title/shares, creates a private levy lien or forces personal loans.

Actual adult resident use decisions within the actual funded lawful envelope
need one-third participation and majority of participants including abstainers;
one person, no landlord/rent/AI gate. Retain H08's stronger funded management
choice in its own scope. Fourteen-day notice/outcome safeguards, accessible
secret/offline/future means, direct claims and actual child/other affected voice.
No automatic sovereign power from a committee, data convergence or copies.

Manager has a replaceable at-most12-month bounded task/budget, actual records/
source/control disclosure and paid continuity. Related-party conflict assents
cannot approve their own award; where lawful threshold is impossible only an
actual independent necessary/fair court source can substitute, not shrink the
roll. A short proportionate plan suffices where real risk permits; no compulsory
paid committee, guild or annual full engineering dossier for every small home.
Ten-year rolling renewal horizon/annual review is not a safety/life certificate.

CB13 actual source cost shares, floor-area default for new absent alternative,
separate service/meter use and exact minor-unit rounding prevent duplicated
bills. Reserves/source/cash/actual work differ. CB15 levies are several owner
liabilities, ordinary current days or special levy-date, with actual prior notice/
cure and court/U recovery. No debt-based service/vote cutoff, private fine,
automatic security or new Treasury guarantee. PT/H/NF old creditors/tenants stay.

CB17 uses existing I03/I05 recognition of actual necessary primary-home running
and qualified protected safety/renewal share. No new all-owner subsidy/loan/lien
or assumption of association debt. Direct qualifying paid receipt can avoid
fronting cash; match expense/reserve/insurer/developer receipts once. Actual
public source/incidence, repair and owner-wealth benefit remain contested; do
not hide a private subsidy behind a fictional depreciation or a free reserve.

CB18 courts can order due repair/cost, bounded paid task receiver and specific
private-unit access with consent or necessary narrow order. Ordinary notice
alone creates no entry/search; immediate actual rescue keeps HP/technical/LF.
Entry, contractor acceptance, funding and completion remain separate. CB19–CB21
sets specified substantial interference/context/control and direct lawful
occupier/child claims; source abatement/attributed compensation without an
extra negligence prerequisite as specified, not all discomfort or normal child/
city life. Public benefit/permit alone erases no established remedy; actual
rights acquisition and forced sacrifice need their own law/LA, not a nuisance
score. No new technical numerical limit or punitive nuisance offense is chosen.
