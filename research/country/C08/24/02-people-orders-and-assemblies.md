# LF05–LF08: people, lawful orders and democratic public space

*Design 97 research selection; no enacted power or proven readiness.*

**LF05 — Children, disability, crisis and care.** A child, disabled person, person in mental
distress, intoxicated person or person communicating in an unfamiliar way retains the same
protection and a usable supported voice. Neither status nor an AI risk prediction is
physical-danger evidence. Use accessible communication, actual de-escalation and a qualified
child/clinical or chosen support route where it can protect in useful time. A physical
intervention needs its own observable act and LF02–LF04 tests; a missing bed, school
disciplinary demand or family preference supplies none. Restraint, sedation and compelled
treatment retain the narrower C08/09/UA/body-law consent and review grounds; LF does not extend
them. A present valid refusal is not erased by another officer's request for speed. A clinician
receives a person for care under clinical law, not as a police custody substitute.

**LF06 — Arrest, custody and civil execution.** P09's arrest predicate and original custody
clock, P08 counsel and P10 remand limits apply regardless of force label. Non-dangerous active
physical resistance to an otherwise lawful arrest may justify only LF02–LF03 minimum contact
under separately enacted physical- enforcement authority; flight or refusal alone does not
justify a firearm or high-risk restraint. A separate actual imminent grave threat is assessed
under LF04, not inferred from the resistance. In custody, use force only for a specific present
safety or indispensable lawful control need, then restore ordinary humane conditions and care;
no routine painful compliance or disciplinary weapon use. B18's executor may carry out a
specific court act within property, third-party and subsistence limits; a civil order creates
neither arrest nor weapons power and an unpaid debt never supports bodily punishment. A
home-loss or seizure operation names a lawful safe receiver, private-property safeguards and
review; if safe execution fails, preserve the judgment and report non-delivery rather than
invent a debtors' police.

**LF07 — Assemblies and political equality.** Peaceful assembly, reporting, union activity,
opposition and lawful civil disobedience remain protected against violence. A disputed or
procedurally unlawful but nonviolent event does not by itself justify a weapon or indiscriminate
force. An assembly dispersal needs a distinct enacted ground, identifiable serious present harm,
named competent decision maker, bounded place/time, usable warning, safe exits and timely
independent review; target an individual's violence where feasible instead of attributing it to
the crowd. Planned policing may protect participants and bystanders but cannot create a secret
party- control list or make journalists state-accredited. No chemical/kinetic device, vehicle or
automated system may be used indiscriminately against an assembly. LF04 alone governs any
potentially lethal act, even during violent disorder. Protect independent recording, safe source
custody, defense access and vulnerable identities under PD; neither privacy nor classified
tactics hide the applicable public legal threshold.

**LF08 — Emergencies, defense and foreign reach.** C10's hazard-specific enacted isolation,
evacuation, closure and constitutional emergency checks remain necessary; declaration or
civil-protection command alone creates no new force authority or extended restraint clock. A
utility, school, insurer or public-health officer cannot acquire police force through a service
duty. Defense staff doing exceptional domestic law enforcement act under a named civilian lead
and LF, not battlefield authority; hostilities and conflict custody retain their own C10/G/CD
tests. A domestic official acting abroad needs actual jurisdiction, host consent or applicable
treaty/law and C08/17 review; LF is a restriction on that official, not permission to cross a
border, seize a foreign device or transfer a person. Foreign assistance, private guards and
shared AI alerts cannot move the force decision into an unreviewable outside operator.

*Part of [LF01–LF16](../24-lawful-force-and-civilian-response.md); no new underlying coercive power.*

**Design147 explicit HP task.** [HP15/HP17](../../C10/11/03-places-rules-entry-and-force.md)
now supplies only the statute's specified civil entry/rescue/minimum-contact
enforcement for sworn civilian police on actual HP direction/order. LF's second
necessity/life tests still constrain it; a clinician/private guard/model gains no
power. This is neither automatic physical force by any health order nor medical
sampling/treatment, chemical restraint, psychiatric intervention or a new weapon.

[AX07–AX10](../../C03/18/02-restrictions-and-enforcement.md) now expressly
proposes the separate ordinary assembly restriction/task source LF07 required:
prior ordinary court for planned material assembly-specific restrictions,
strict six-hour outer unilateral civilian command and scoped minimum sworn-
police physical escort/removal. LF01–LF04's second necessity/life tests still
apply; no AX arrest/custody/search/weapon/collective-containment power or crime.
Actual existing law must be validly revised before any new AX power operates.
