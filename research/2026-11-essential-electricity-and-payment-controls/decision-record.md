# Design407 decision. Preserve essential electricity through payment-control failure

## Baseline and purpose

This AI-assisted selection uses [Design406](predecessor-design-406/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md),
the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).
EG06 already protects minimum electricity from inability-to-pay cutoff,
including prepaid and intermediary arrangements. EG07, HL and income rules
govern ordinary correction and dues. The missing implementation choice is
who keeps actual power and settles its cost when the payment control blocks
service with no formal cutoff decision. Tenants, non-account occupants, care
recipients, suppliers, landlords and workers are affected.

## Alternatives and selected arrangement

Keep current billing modes and improve hardship outreach and emergency credit.
This preserves familiar budgeting choices but depends on timely top-up, creditor
practice and connectivity. Use ordinary billing only for all domestic service.
This removes balance-based cutoff but can remove preferred cash planning and
increase unresolved debt. Select voluntary reversible prepayment only where
an ordinary route remains usable and the protected service can continue.
Separate current assistance from old debts and make actual power receiving,
cash and source claims independently visible. Price an ordinary tariff or
simpler billing alternative where protected use cannot be reliably separated.

The strongest objections are accumulating unpaid claims, failure of physical
supply and inappropriate disclosure of vulnerability. Define voted support
and actual operator costs separately, keep existing outage procedures, and
share only scoped protection facts. The guarantee neither makes an unfunded
promise usable nor permits harmful cost recovery. No independent reviewer
or actual affected participant was secured for the selection.

## Control, enforcement and transition

Elected lawmakers specify the floor and source. Network and site operators
retain safe operational control, suppliers handle billing and notification,
benefit officers authorize support, and the independent reviewer and courts
decide lawful correction. An occupant has safe access independent of a
landlord or subscriber. Review can inspect original authorizations and verify
actual delivery. Test actual independence across staffing, records, funding
and replacement before relying on the arrangement.

Carry existing claims, credit balances, proof, customer choice, protected
occupant rights, contracts and review clocks under their source law.
Give prepaid users a qualified successor route before migration. Keep
prior debts and current obligations separate, preserve independent recovery
and expose costs that exceed actual appropriations. Financial protection
does not create physical electricity or authority to isolate an unsafe line.
AI may assist reconciliation and issue detection without control of service.

## Evidence and bounded continuation

[Primary regulation and observed implementation](sources.md) identify practical
risks and comparison rules. The [constructed case](../settlement/essential-electricity-and-payment-controls/01-two-billing-windows-and-a-live-line.md)
checks current payment, old debt, late cash and actual supply. [EPC01–EPC08](../country/C09/19/05-essential-electricity-and-payment-controls.md)
records the research selection. Enactment, actual staffing, ordinary charges,
physical independence and observed outcomes remain unverified.

A competent energy reviewer and two service operators could next trace a
lawfully observable two-period case involving an occupant outside the account.
Check actual cutoff cause, safe service, money settled by the deadline,
worker time, remedies and any new debt. Funding and safe access are unresolved.
Reopen on unsafe outage, repeated reliance on temporary credit, unpayable
liabilities, hidden gatekeeper control or a simpler protective ordinary tariff.
