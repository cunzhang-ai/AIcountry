# AL16–AL20. Pay a continuing capability and preserve future choices

*Part of [lifelong learning](../22-lifelong-learning-and-renewal.md).*

**AL16 — Count the full public offer and displaced work once.** Assembly source,
need-adjusted education grants and competent elected local/public-institution
budgets fund the actual AL scope through DB/NP/RF/NF/SC, with no new residual
learning tax, student debt assumption or guaranteed AI dividend. Distinguish
AL02's offered64-hour capacity, chosen take-up, first/renewal route duration,
teacher contact, preparation/assessment, placements, stable actual qualified
staff, salary/replacement, support/care/journeys, rooms/equipment, capital/
maintenance, peak geography, independent review, failed completion and paid
continuity. Actual compatible shared learning/employer costs count once.

Full availability needs credible staffed expansion/receiving and a lawful funded
schedule, not a simultaneous one-to-one tuition assumption or perpetual100%
use assumption. No instructor is free because another service already pays
their wages. Show actual work displaced and paid qualified cover; preserve
learners, teachers and care/service users together. Public tuition refunds,
internal grants and payroll netting are no new national source. Original37
functions and old education/N bids do not price full AL; census stays open.
Actual full public/private/foreign/legacy/FX/transition cost/source is unknown.

**AL17 — Assess learning and agency without sorting lives.** Separate useful
access, chosen participation, retained/transfer capability, qualification,
actual occupation/job entry, income/time/care, civic/personal agency and harms.
No completion rate, credential count, satisfaction, AI task score or observed
wage association proves a causal whole-country gain. Include excluded, declining,
interrupted and departing learners with safe proportionate evidence and unknowns.
No compulsory private-life tracking or expected fiscal worth determines funding.

Original UNESCO recommendation is normative context, not domestic power.
Selected OECD2025 survey analysis concerns mainly job-related learning at25–65,
not every older/civic learner or proof of causality. The2026 policy brief is not
an independent new experiment; do not double-count the underlying study family.
Numbers/eligibility/governance here are contestable hypotheses, not estimated optima.

**AL18 — Exact new law, dates and paid old successors.** Proposed additions and
new public-institution governance begin first1January at least12calendar months
after coordinated AL/S07/OE/education/source law, naming actual new applicants,
accepted plans, institution/board/electoral cohorts, programme dates, qualified
reviewers, sources and paid OP/T overlap. Existing first/catch-up plans, N/TW,
stronger education/academic/worker terms, public/private charters, property,
pension/contract/security/creditor/record/foreign obligations and actual hold/
constitutional/software/contribution permissions retain lawful successors.

No automatic national takeover, mass reappointment, invalidation of prior
qualifications or reset of TW36/32, Y, source, election, AP/court and existing
service clocks. A missed enacted start remains breach/actual interim receiving,
not a minister's readiness veto, vanished right or unqualified substitute.
Historic education.education-act v8 requires adult schedules; retain its full
object and actual context, not its older child-age wording as a new current rule.

**AL19 — Keep a smaller offer and different ownership choices real.** A plus
S06/S07 and explicit N project/need funding can focus scarce instructors and
limit new cost, but leaves independent later renewal dependent on portfolio/
employer selection. Portable money accounts can widen provider choice but risk
price capture, complex purchasing and unequal usable access; a cash balance is
no suitable place. Broad unrestricted publicly funded repeated degrees offers
more freedom but greater source/qualification crowding. Direct national adult
colleges and voluntary common institutions have different power/cost tradeoffs.
AL chooses a person-initiated service with reasonable meaningful renewal, not
unlimited preferred-course purchase or abolition of those alternatives.

**AL20 — Reject failure and reopen the choice.** Captured public/provider/academic
chains, nominal unsuitable alternatives, inaccessible support, unnecessary proof,
unsafe credentials, political retaliation, unpaid placements, exclusion of old/
care/disabled learners, unfinanced promises or a better complete paid rival can
narrow, revise or displace AL. Applicable protections survive method replacement.
No new common research procedure, authority or final review adopts itself.
Next the largest integrated ordinary/adverse/paid-country gap, not an automatic
university-board, course-credit or adult-hour series. Full feasibility, legitimate
affected choice, safe independent operation and better lives remain unproved.
