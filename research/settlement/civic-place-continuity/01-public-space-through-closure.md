# A replacement for a shared place must work when people can use it

*Design317 · Observed inquiry and a constructed evening comparison.*

## Public source evidence

Section 7 of the UK's Public Libraries and Museums Act 1964 provides
a public library duty with staffing and maintenance. The 2009 Wirral
public inquiry examined a decision to close 11 libraries. It found
the council had not adequately assessed local needs and advised
against endorsing the plans. The report distinguishes demand, actual
use and the circumstances of people who could not readily use the
remaining services. It does not establish that any branch must remain
open forever or quantify wellbeing effects.
[Act as enacted, section 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1964/75/section/7/made)
· [Wirral inquiry, paragraphs 14–20 and 6.19–6.22](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/77447/wirral_local_inquiry.pdf)

DC Public Library offers free bookable meeting rooms for defined

## Two simultaneous uses during repairs

A neighborhood library hall must close temporarily for a verified
urgent repair. Existing local law in the example protects free
evening drop-in use during the affected weeks. A lawful civic group
also has an existing protected booking before a council decision
and needs a room at the same time. Neither use can be moved to
the afternoon. The ordinary branch is unavailable. Five units are
usable after other continuing obligations.

The school offers one independently accessible and staffed room
for three units. It can host either use, but the quiet drop-in and
the meeting cannot safely use it together. A community provider
offers another accessible staffed room for two units on the same
conditions. Their staff, sites and sources are distinct. The
commercial venue accepts vouchers in the daytime only. These costs,
availability, legal duties and useful times are authored.

| Choice | Timely free drop-in | Timely booked meeting | Cash for accepted evening use |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Use the daytime vouchers only | Unavailable | Unavailable | 0 |
| Use the school room for the protected booking | Unavailable | Available | 3 |
| Fund both qualified evening rooms | Available | Available | 5 |
| Fund both rooms on paper but the shared access route closes | Recheck actual receiving. Under a complete route loss both fail. | Recheck actual receiving | 5 assumed committed |

Select the two accepted rooms in this example. At four available
units, the second room cannot be bought alongside the first. Report
the unserved use and seek lawful correction. Applying actual prior
law may select a different disposition where deadlines or urgency
differ. If the civic group is unlawfully denied, an appropriate
independent order can preserve access before the meeting while the
authority retains its duty to fund and staff it. A rescheduled
meeting after the public decision does not repair lost opportunity.
A public list of venues or a second booking cannot create capacity.

Compare ordinary repair, differently timed uses where genuinely usable,
community leases and a recurring public facility. A funded community
room can be the less costly durable option with protected access,
paid succession and accountability. A voucher can help only where
a qualified venue is actually available on the necessary terms.
