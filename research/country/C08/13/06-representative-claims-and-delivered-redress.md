# RCR01–RCR06. Decide a common civil issue and deliver the individual remedy

*Design361 · Part of [civil obligations](../13-civil-obligations-and-executed-remedies.md).*

**RCR01 — Define the shared issue and who can represent it.** Under B20,
a person with an affected claim or a legally qualified organization can
ask a competent ordinary court to certify an opt-in proceeding. Show a
substantial common issue, a workable group definition, adequate and
conflict-free representation, and a benefit over separate cases.
Certification decides the scope and handling of issues. It decides neither
liability nor the amount owed. Different damages or defenses can remain for
individual proof. Give respondents and affected people notice, meaningful
reply and review. Disclose who controls the action, funds it and can affect
settlement. Replace a conflicted representative or divide materially
different interests into separately represented groups before deciding them.

**RCR02 — Join the common proof while retaining usable local access.**
The competent court can coordinate related domestic cases under enacted
assignment and review rules. Choose the lead by legal competence,
practical access, independent custody, available hearing time and actual
enforcement. First filing, sponsor preference and the respondent's home
forum do not settle assignment. Local receiving courts preserve first
receipt, counsel, accommodations, necessary originals and urgent relief
within their powers. A transfer requires an accepting court, funded
handling and a record of issues and dates. A refusal or unavailable lead
leaves the existing lawful case open. Decide only the certified common
issues together. Keep distinct claims and lawful defenses with a named
receiver. This process creates no foreign jurisdiction or new liability.

**RCR03 — Require actual participation and preserve time.** Give affected
people free, usable notice, accessible enrollment and an explanation of
binding effect, alternatives, fee exposure, deadlines and withdrawal.
Protect private proof of assent. The adopting law preserves a claim
lodged in time by actual enrollment within the certified or provisionally
specified scope. If certification is refused, scope is narrowed or a member
lawfully withdraws before the common determination, preserve the longer
of the time remaining at timely enrollment or 60 calendar days. Count
that period from usable notice to proceed separately. Specify custody and transfer of their
original filing. No already expired claim revives. Urgent protection and
existing longer safeguards keep their own dates. An outsider's silence
or inclusion in a contact list neither enrolls them nor alters their
ordinary clock. Any B20 monetary opt-out route requires separate express
law and actual notice safeguards.

**RCR04 — Examine the settlement, including those it would leave out.**
Require an independent court to approve a representative settlement
after usable notice, time to object and access to a confidential route
for sensitive dissent. Examine who gives up what, actual fees and sponsor
interests, comparable recovery, distribution costs, payment security and
what happens on failure. A sponsor cannot buy a wider release or control
a claimant's distinct interests. Use separately represented groups or
reject the settlement if interests cannot be handled fairly. The final
order specifies who is bound and the exact issues released. Nonparticipants,
nonwaivable rights and unapproved distinct losses remain outside the
release. No administrative or AI recommendation determines fairness.

**RCR05 — Approve a distribution that can actually be executed.** The
order names the lawful payer, independent executor, usable source,
recipient or eligible proof, due date and review. Credit only actual
receipt against the same loss under B49. Record promised, secured,
sent, settled, returned and disputed amounts separately. Give recipients
a safe alternate receiving route and a court route for rejection, partial
payment and late execution. Keep residual amounts and separate harms
open as required. Unclaimed money follows a disclosed lawful custodial
and disposition rule, with no private appropriation by the representative.
Use B18, B19 or insolvency only within actual authority and priorities.
A group order creates neither new assets nor a state guarantee.

**RCR06 — Fund common and individual work on the same schedule.** Price
accessible notice, independent representation, reply, court and execution
capacity beside urgent and nonparticipant cases. Verify an actual receiving
court and payer before treating a transfer or distribution as usable.
Publish safe aggregate results and unresolved work. Protect original
evidence, individual dates and actual authority through a change of
representative, venue or operator. AI may organize common material and
flag overlaps for review. It cannot infer assent, settle an issue, merge
claimants or certify payment. Reopen on unfair scope, inaccessible notice,
returned distributions, failed custody or a simpler route with timely relief.
