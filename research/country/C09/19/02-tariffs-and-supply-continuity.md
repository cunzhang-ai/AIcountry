# EG05–EG08. Paid supply and essential access

*Part of [ordinary electricity law](../19-electricity-service-and-grid-access.md).*

**EG05 — Separate network cost, energy and public guarantees.** The Steward
submits an annual and lifecycle allowed-revenue account for system and
distribution functions. Independently review attributable staff, losses,
safe operations, maintenance, renewals, capital and reliability. Price
network capacity and delivered energy separately. Distinguish committed
capacity from actual consumption and use proportionate measurement where
metering is inaccessible. The Assembly sets the permitted calculation,
shared cost distribution and national assistance. Competent elected local
bodies vote authorized local charges under LR. Operators calculate bills
and the independent reviewer checks conformity. Merchant energy and
hedging retain their lawful contracts, with transparent offers and exit.

A dedicated connection applicant normally funds its attributable works,
including only its justified share of joint capacity. Identify the cost
of common reinforcements and expressly allocate public benefits and losses.
Credit grants, provided resources and other receipts once. Keep depreciation,
cash financing, debt principal, late payment and restricted money separate.
Households do not silently fund optional expansion. Essential assistance,
public standby and the default-service exposure each have voted sources.

**EG06 — Inability to pay cannot remove essential electricity.** Keep safe
minimum use for the resident's actual heating or cooling, medical and
communication needs and related essential tasks under C07. Determine the
amount by the actual household, equipment, climate and alternative access.
Provide usable billing, prompt help and provisional protection where delay
risks deprivation. Make lawful assistance reach the bill and operator
without counting it twice in the I account. A prepaid meter, remote
control, landlord instruction or intermediary contract is subject to the
same protection. No cutoff or limiter for inability to pay may deprive an
occupant of the protected essential supply. An engineering isolation
for actual safety remains possible, with actual safe alternatives.

**EG07 — Correct the bill or outage through a real remedy.** Give notice,
reasons, readings and a non-digital correction route. An affected resident
or worker can reach the independent reviewer and existing court directly
through AP/OJ, with a useful-time interim decision where material harm is
at risk. Preserve the first protected date. While a material bill or
disconnection dispute is heard, protect the disputed service insofar as
safe and preserve undisputed payment duties. Collect actual lawful debts
through ordinary civil remedies. Do not transfer another occupier's debt,
infer unauthorized use from a forecast, or create new seizure authority.
Provide actual restoration, account correction and lawful redress as
applicable. A favorable decision without a receiving executor remains open.

**EG08 — Prepare a supplier before a seller fails.** The Steward arranges
a predesignated funded default supplier through an open ordinary process,
with a public or publicly controlled offer if no independent provider can
accept. It serves households without a usable supplier and takes over
covered household service on lawful exit or failed settlement without a
supply interruption attributable to the transfer. The grid still needs
actual physical power. Keep the existing meter, essential protection,
billing evidence and independent exit choice. Notify the resident promptly
through usable channels. Apply an elected or lawfully delegated cost-based
default tariff under EG05 and keep all essential protections. The fallback
transfer cannot tie the customer into a fixed-term deal or exit fee.
No old debt is assigned to the new supplier or occupant by default. Refund
and credit claims remain against the legally responsible party or their
applicable safeguarded source. Keep actual consent and later supplier choice.

Price expected supply, hedge and common-supplier failures, opening cash,
collateral, meter/record continuity and funded takeover in advance.
Use lawfully available bond or other prior security once at its scope.
Name the voted source for the remaining exposure. A public guarantee is
not a loan of restricted money or unlimited seller rescue. If there is
no accepting funded successor, the Steward keeps the claim open and
brings the failure to DB/FB and existing remedies. It cannot report
continuity from a licence or automatic customer reassignment.

[TH07 affordability and safe use](../23/02-retrofit-energy-and-source.md)
checks actual indoor protection, paid household support and thermal load.
The electricity operator retains EG duties while housing officers keep the
resident's claim and arrange any separate interim receiving.

[HL18–HL22](../25/05-household-arrears-and-essential-continuity.md)
joins dated utility bills, rent and benefits during a cash shock.
Preserve EG06–EG07 and distinguish ongoing minimum supply from
lawful recovery of older claims.

[EG13–EG18](04-large-grid-connections-and-paid-capacity.md) applies
EG05 to material new load, with attributable cancellation and underuse
exposure backed before optional works. A proposed future use alone
creates no household tariff source.

[EPC01–EPC08](05-essential-electricity-and-payment-controls.md) specifies the receiving and payment
conditions for EG06. Ordinary charges, due claims and EG07 remedies continue.
