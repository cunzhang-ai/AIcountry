# AEC01–AEC05. Keep essential assistive equipment usable for its service life

*Part of [national clinical governance](../05-health-service-and-patient-control.md). Design339 research selection.*

**AEC01 — Cover a working function and name its owner.** The regional National
Clinical Service (NCS) network names a team responsible for publicly covered
essential mobility, positioning and communication equipment. Cover assessment
and choice, fitting, training, maintenance, repairs, interim provision and
needed replacement within the enacted package. Preserve the person's choice
of lawful equipment and qualified service where available. Record the
functions needed in the person's ordinary settings, the useful response
window, authorized contact and how to request help in an accessible way.
A diagnosis, delivery receipt or closed repair ticket does not establish
continued access. A person visiting another region can seek qualified
local receiving while the networks settle which budget pays.

**AEC02 — Act before the loss becomes an exclusion.** Accept a report from
the person or a chosen supporter without requiring the supplier's release
or a fresh eligibility assessment for the established need. Provide a
qualified response within 24 elapsed hours after protected first receipt,
and earlier when a known safety, communication or other essential-use
window requires it. For a planned shutdown, recall or supplier exit, contact
affected users through safe authorized routes and arrange cover before the
affected function is lost. Offer repair in place, a fitted loan or another
safe and voluntarily accepted arrangement by the useful window. Make a
reasoned repair or replacement decision within five working days of first
receipt, or sooner where required by existing law or to prevent serious
harm. If only partial continuity is possible, identify the missing function,
provide the best usable interim support, give an actual next date and enable
urgent outside review. Refusing an unsafe or inaccessible substitute
preserves entitlement, the original date and other independently due care.

**AEC03 — Buy support through supplier and platform exit.** The public
purchaser compares total paid service lives, independent lawful repair,
fitting capacity, parts, trained technicians, accessibility and local
operation during failure. Define a support term, required functions and
costed exit obligations at purchase. Before releasing a material purchase,
demonstrate that a qualified successor can service the covered configuration
using lawfully available parts, tools, current instructions and scoped
service access, or demonstrate a funded, timely alternative replacement
route. For a connected device, demonstrate continued core function or a
safe fitted alternate through loss of the vendor's account or network.
Any clinically necessary dependence requires recorded justification and a
funded contingency. Keep usable configuration and user-controlled content
exportable where technically and lawfully possible. Preserve communication
privacy and user control over transfers. Proprietary contracts do not
authorize unsafe repair, hidden remote withdrawal of core function,
compulsory telemetry or unrelated disclosure.

**AEC04 — Pay for continuity before collecting from the responsible firm.**
The NCS operating source pays the qualified receiver, transport, fitting,
standby, interim device and replacement when necessary. A coverage team
may choose a safe timely repair or suitable replacement with the person.
A nominal minimum age, ownership dispute, insolvency claim or uncollected
supplier payment does not itself justify delaying an owed function.
Pursue bonds, valid warranty, contractual or insolvency recovery separately
under existing law. Keep a dated record of accrued claims and actual
receipts. Test the whole alternate path, including authorized service
access, staff, stock and transport, under the same common failure. A device
on a shelf cannot also cover another person in the same period.

**AEC05 — Verify function in use and give the person a remedy.** Verify that
the person received equipment or equivalent agreed support, can use its
required functions and knows whom to reach if it fails. Record any remaining
limits, duration and missed ordinary activities separately. A safe interim
device can preserve one essential function and leave another unmet. AI may
track stock or flag common dependencies. A qualified human checks fit and
safety, the person chooses whether to accept, and the independent clinical
reviewer can order timely correction within existing powers. Seek ordinary
judicial relief and execution when needed. Elected budgets fund recurring
capacity and disclose safe aggregate outcomes and unmet duties. Adopt with
prospective procurement terms and funded legacy support. Reopen on prolonged
loans, repeated functional losses, vendor lock-in, excessive cost or a
simpler service that provides equally usable continuity.
