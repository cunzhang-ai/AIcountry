# Paired first motions on the same observed economy

[Matched national work](README.md)

Both first motions retain existing people, lawful dues, contracts,
rights, workers and service duties. Neither inherits free fiscal
space or presumes compliance with every current duty. Use the
[observed 2025 reference](01-observed-2025-and-boundaries.md)
for the shared setting. This is a research comparison of governing
choices, without an actual appropriation or new national law.

## What to put before the Assembly

| Motion | First budget decision | What still needs a vote and a paid operating path |
|---|---|---|
| A. Strong retain and repair | Continue working sources, rectify verified duty failures and fund accepted repairs. Keep functioning care teams, labor arrangements, operator alternatives and independent scrutiny. | Cost and address inherited exclusion, unsafe work and delay. Retention is no promise of unchanged prices, full receipt or zero repair cost. |
| B. Same repair and staged service-first reform | Carry the same repairs. Vote separately on finite paid capability and accessible service preparation, with named costs and due-date cash. | A separately funded 36-hour stage can be proposed once actual payroll, rest, independent cover and private counterpart are shown. Return a complete 32-hour option for separate elected choice. Existing rights continue. |
| C. Same repair and both stages authorized together | Authorize both stages and associated continuing reforms only with an actual matched source, qualified future receiving and paid transition. | Neither a national output forecast nor a first-stage approval can pay for the successor. Show the whole provision and what happens on a weaker path. |

Under the evidence here choose continuation and named repair
plus qualified finite preparation under B's order. The research
choice retains the longer staged working design and leaves a
funded faster alternative open. There is no affirmative basis
here to authorize either nationwide hours start or additional
permanent service on assumed gains. Specific actual appropriations
and roles require their own lawful evidence and public decision.

## Test a national claim using the same task volume

These figures are authored arithmetic on the observed employee
volume. They assume every employee hour contracts in the ratio
shown, unchanged quality and task demand, no extra hiring or
transfer, no new service and retained nonemployee hours. The
37-hour reference is an assumption, rather than an observation
about every worker's schedule. One reference-hour equivalent
means the original assumed task output of one hour. A negative
shortfall is a surplus in that same normalized scope.

| Sensitivity | Government shortfall, million | Non-government shortfall, million | National signed sum |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 37 to 36, no gain | 32.196 | 80.199 | 112.394 |
| 37 to 32, no gain | 160.978 | 400.993 | 561.971 |
| 37 to 32, 15.625% same-task gain in each scope | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 |
| 37 to 32, about 21.898% gain outside government only | 160.978 | −160.978 | 0.000 |

The sums use unrounded values. The last two rows produce exactly
the same national signed result. They have different receiving
requirements. A voluntary transfer or purchased task must be
compatible with the actual place, skill, employer terms and
continuing source before reducing the exposed amount. National
output, accounting compensation and a provider's released time
give no proof of those conditions. Even the matched-gain row
settles no case-level qualification or outage. The 36-hour
same-task gain required under these assumptions is 2.778% in
each scope. Neither gain is an estimate or a minimum for
a differently scoped actual reform.

## Carry the rest of the country through either choice

| Continuing scope | Evidence required for actual authorization |
|---|---|
| C01–C04. Rights, democracy and administration | Independent decision and enforcement chains, actual old claims, paid lawful deadlines and power handover |
| C05–C07. Work, income, finance and services | Actual payroll and private counterparts, retained and incremental services, restricted funds, cash at due date and ordinary receipt |
| C08. Everyday law and justice | Court, defense, custody and remedy capacity under the same staffing stress |
| C09–C10. Places, ecology and external duties | Physical and climate budgets, maintenance, actual fuel, transport and foreign settlement |
| C11–C14. AI, revision, lived effects and transition | Separately qualified aid and exit, affected input and consent, replacement and original claims, independent performance evidence |

Each proposal names included and incremental work and leaves
missing observations visible. Do not add unrelated planning
vintages or subtract a scoped case from the national total.
New authority, staffing, due money and real service are separate
conditions. Better lives require actual evidence across groups,
including people harmed by a delay or by an accelerated start.
