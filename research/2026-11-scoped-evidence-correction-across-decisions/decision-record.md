# Design433. Reassess affected uses without erasing valid work or rights

## Decision and baseline

Design432 is the pinned predecessor. The working framework already
requires scoped reassessment. EV17–EV24 assign notice, decision and
remedy under actual law. A correction can travel through a model,
translation, local report or shared resource plan. When these uses
are treated as identical, owners may either miss important decisions
or reopen unrelated ones. Select EV25–EV30 and clarify the proposed
research method with the same distinction between evidentiary
dependence and authority. This revision used AI assistance.

## Alternatives and burden

Publication with voluntary downstream review costs less and may
suffice for genuinely immaterial uses. It leaves high-impact
undocumented use and missed recipients with no named decision
owner. A comprehensive registry and universal restart can expose
some omitted links, at large cost in privacy, time and local
judgment. Select a scoped duty on existing owners for material
uses, including a targeted search for reasonably suspected gaps.
Prefer the simpler route where it can reach the same affected
people and deliver correction in time.

A producer can suppress a warning, a decision owner can disclaim
dependence and a purported reviewer can share their controller.
Use ordinary direct challenge, lawful access to original evidence,
an unimplicated review and receiving route and evidence of actual
delivery. Preserve disagreement about interpretation without
granting an evidentiary custodian or routing system authority over
another body's decision. Make private and informal uses exempt
from general logging. Repeated generated reports gain no priority
through volume. Newly evidenced losses remain reviewable.

## Evidence, authority and migration

The [technical reference and constructed comparison](sources.md)
give a way to distinguish derivation from a new observation.
Neither estimates real review workload or effects. No affected
participation, qualified independent review, legislative adoption
or public appropriation was obtained. Existing authorities retain
their powers and clocks. A competent legislature would have to
fund and adopt the proposed institutional duties. Research owners
can use the method proportionately without changing contribution
permissions, current constitutional holds or approval procedures.
No proposed procedure approves this revision or itself.

Apply prospectively with a lawful receiver for open reviews,
original warnings, accrued pay and protected records. Keep
existing review and relief routes. Earlier versions remain safely
recoverable under lawful custody. Substitution of an interface
must preserve material semantics or expose what it cannot carry.
Delete or restrict sensitive material under its applicable rules,
with a safe correction event and reassessment of surviving support.

## Next bounded comparison

Use one material funding or access correction with separate owners,
an unknown use and a pending due payment. Compare publication alone,
scoped tracing and wider review on actual response time, useful
receiving, omitted people, staff hours and privacy. Include at least
one non-digital channel and directly affected people. Reopen on a
missed material use, unnecessary interruption, unreviewable secrecy
or evidence that a simpler service delivers correction as well.
