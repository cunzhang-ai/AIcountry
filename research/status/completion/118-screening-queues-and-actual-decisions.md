# Design210. Screened public access and timely decisions

The current design covers responsibility for consequential routing, timely
access, paid alternate queues, common-source failures and correction of actual
treatment. A final human decision alone leaves the routing effect unresolved.
A competent ordinary route is the comparison and the alternative. Research
selection is conditional on lawful purpose, qualified benefit and paid
protection. Actual comparative access, independence and delivery remain open.
