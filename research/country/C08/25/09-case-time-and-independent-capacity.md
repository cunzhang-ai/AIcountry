# OJ41–OJ44. Make time to justice an operating duty

*Design215 · Part of [C08 ordinary justice](../25-ordinary-justice-and-public-prosecution.md).*

**OJ41 — Reconcile what enters, what remains and what was received.**
The existing independent court administration keeps comparable safe
aggregate accounts by jurisdiction and material case type. Distinguish
first protected receipt, filings, transfers in and out, reclassification,
reopened cases, pending cases by age, and actual dispositions. Show
withdrawals and settlements separately from adjudication. Report delay
from receipt to protection, hearing, decision and, with the responsible
executor, receipt of relief. Mark unresolved records and cases unable
to enter where safely observable. Track the age and distribution of
open and closed matters separately. Match a transfer on both sides
and keep the original receipt. Count a continuing claim once even if
a reporting category or system changes. Publish safe disaggregated
counts and any material changes to the definitions.

**OJ42 — Preserve each case's time and useful hearing.** Apply existing
E, P, B, AP, CJ and sector deadlines and protections. The independent
registry supplies an accessible first receipt, named judicial owner
and usable next step under the published case-assignment rules. The
assigned court reviews meaningful time risks, evidence and actual
participation. It can order a fair preparation timetable within its
jurisdiction. Offer interpreter, counsel and accommodation routes as
needed. Notice of a missed date explains the event, continuing rights
and prompt outside review. A person may ask for useful-time interim
protection or challenge unreasonable delay through existing law.
Preserve legal effect, limitation, procedural defense and original
liberty clocks. A plan, transfer or digital outage cannot restart them.

**OJ43 — Protect urgent duties and the ordinary queue.** Independent
courts assign case-specific urgency under law. The administration
uses prior public criteria for scheduling and protects compatible
reserve capacity for statutory deadlines and imminent serious harm.
Allocate usable sessions for aging ordinary cases and report material
diversion, reasons, replacement dates and who bears the delay.
Never impose a fixed throughput quota that overrides urgency or
necessary defense time. Longer waiting can prompt review without
controlling the result or turning every case into a first-come rule.
Keep free filing, direct challenge and a reachable alternate where
they already apply. No admission target, prosecution category or
machine ranking decides which person's right can be missed.

**OJ44 — Join the whole case chain without joining its powers.** For
material proposed changes in policing, prosecution, legal entitlement
or appeals, the proposing government presents shared case and resource
assumptions under GP, DB and FB. Independent court and legal-aid
authorities provide their own workload, qualified capacity and
constraints. Count time for evidence disclosure, defense, counsel,
interpretation, hearing, appeals and execution by place and due
window. Include transfers, outages, repeat work, independent reviewers
and access costs. Keep appropriations and control separate. Test a
weaker simultaneous demand and staffing case. Parliament may choose
a lawful different source or legal policy. An upstream budget cannot
order a hearing, reserve a judge or extend existing deadlines.
