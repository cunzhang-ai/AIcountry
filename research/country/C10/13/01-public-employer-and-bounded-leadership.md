# RC01–RC05. Public responsibility before an incident

*Part of [civil rescue](../13-civil-rescue-and-incident-command.md); proposed design165.*

**RC01 — Name the ordinary public debtor and employer.** The elected local
public authority is responsible for ordinary civilian fire response, physical
rescue, search and hazard preparedness in its lawful service territory. Its
administrative Civil Rescue Service employs qualified responders and operates
public stations/equipment; it is not a commercial insurer or a new sovereign
region. No residence, citizenship, subscriber status, insurance or payment gate
may precede urgent protection. National administration retains C04's essential-
guarantee/funding duty where a locality cannot deliver, through its actually
enacted limited fallback rather than an invented takeover. Public responsibility
cannot be contracted away. Qualified additional/cooperative/voluntary provision
may help, without automatic private-debt guarantee or force authority.

**RC02 — An accountable qualified human Director.** The EL11 council-elected
executive lead appoints the local Civil Rescue Director through C04's open,
job-related qualified shortlist, published reasons/conflicts and direct ordinary
review. Council controls lawful plans/budget/scrutiny and can replace its executive;
Director holds a protected career, not an independent policy franchise. Require
actual rescue/incident-management/rights competence, not party loyalty or an AI
rank. Director appoints qualified station/shift leads and designated deputies
through merit careers, with actual task qualifications and paid cover. Neither
headship nor a title authorizes every technical, clinical or enforcement act.
A limited caretaker cannot use this provision to make new senior patronage.

**RC03 — Give the existing local incident lead a source.** Under the coordinated
RC/HP/CP Act, Director is the default local civilian incident lead for physical hazards and the local CP
residual-resource schedule, including a lawful non-fire conflict; clinical/public-
health decisions remain with their actual sector officers. An advance duty scheme
names qualified deputy/on-scene lead assignments, territorial limits, conflicts
and source-law acts. A qualified designated field lead acts within that assignment
when the Director is absent or personal signature cannot arrive in useful time.
Already authorized responders perform their own immediate HP rescue duties
without waiting for a new command certificate. Relief/expansion requires a named
accepted lawful handover of scope, original dates, resources and unmet claims,
not automatic power for everyone using the radio. No change resets a clock.
For cross-boundary scenes use the prior lawful interlocal assignment; absent
agreement, each competent officer keeps only its own lawful territorial/task
actions and seeks actual accepted aid/review. Neither the largest crew nor the
coordinator acquires all jurisdictions by arrival. Do not delay own lawful
immediate protection while inventing a shared sovereign.

**RC04 — Concretize the existing national coordinator, not a national commander.**
The state employs a career National Civil Rescue Coordinator within existing
national administration; Cabinet appoints through the C04 qualified public
shortlist with relevant competence, reasons/conflicts and ordinary review. The
responsible Cabinet minister answers to the Assembly for preparedness, accepted
mutual aid and lawful national support. This names CP07's existing coordinator:
exceptional consolidation needs that affirmative Cabinet decision within its
original time/pool. Ordinary information, reserve planning and requests stay
within source law and owner/release terms. Coordinator cannot direct a patient's
treatment, police case, school, court, election, defense mission or cyber restore,
compel aid or declare an unsourced emergency power. Cabinet retains its own
lawful democratic responsibility; it cannot hide behind a technical officer.

**RC05 — Replacement, shared provision and continuing duties.** Executive/Cabinet
may lawfully change a head's prospective operational mandate/assignment with
reasons, C04 reply/status protection and independent review; no partisan purge,
unproved complaint as guilt or career veto over lawful elected policy. Responder
status/discipline uses C04/WL and actual professional competence; protect candid
hazard reports, lawful dissent, worker rest/exit and safe temporary cover. Acting
Director/coordinator/deputy head assignments expire at most180elapsed days from
original vacancy, no serial resetting; earlier stronger law prevails. A field
duty assignment is not permission to evade this head-vacancy limit. Existing
qualified officers retain only their own continuing powers if no head is lawfully
available. Councils may choose actually funded joint service under existing C03
law: name the public employer, delegates, budget/control, service territories,
qualified lead and lawful exit/claim successors before transfer. A common badge
cannot erase any council's electorate, national guarantee or public responsibility.
