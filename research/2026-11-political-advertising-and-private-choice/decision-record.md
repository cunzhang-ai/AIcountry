# Design387 decision. Govern actual paid targeting and protect lawful argument

## Baseline and selection

Design386 distinguishes paid electoral appeals, paid issue disclosure,
editorial and ordinary speech under PC01–PC03 and PC16. ER21–ER25
addresses false instructions affecting voting access. The unresolved
case is a paid appeal with a compliant sponsor label but materially
different content or delivery chosen by an unseen personal profile.

Select [PC25–PC31](../country/C03/12/06-political-advertising-and-private-choice.md).
Keep contextual and freely chosen delivery. Require directly supplied
facts and separate permission for permitted personal-data delivery.
Exclude inferred beliefs, sensitive and exploitative bases and their
proxies. Require inspectable material versions and independent source
access, with bounded review and actual correction. Do not shift
authority over truth or lawful private speech to government or platforms.

## Alternatives and objections

Retain sponsorship disclosure alone. It costs less to enforce but
allows the delivered system to contradict a sponsor's broad label.
A full ban on paid electoral distribution removes covered targeting
but also removes useful lawful outreach, especially for new entrants.
Select scoped restrictions on methods of paid delivery alongside
protected contextual speech and plural ordinary access.

Reporting each material variant could disadvantage small entrants
or expose private groups. Require proportionate formats and protected
originals without a named audience archive. Review common provider
exits and any chilling effect. A personal-data rule can be abused
against opponents by overclassifying issue speech. Preserve PC01's
actual paid electoral scope, evidence, outside review and narrow
remedy. Keep ordinary opinions and voluntary subscriptions usable.

## Evidence, compatibility and next action

[Primary sources](sources.md) distinguish an actual EU legal model
and related implementation guidance. The [constructed contest](../settlement/political-advertising-and-private-choice/01-three-messages-and-two-voting-windows.md)
checks three variants, the original record and a paid remedy across
two deadlines. No affected participation or independent local legal
or privacy review has qualified this proposal.

Use prospective PC, PD and RR authority. Name paid record custodians,
finance and privacy reviewers, independent courts and actual executors.
Keep protected data minimization and original dates, valid paid
obligations and old contest authority. Reassess lawful processing
when a use or purpose changes. This edition changes no actual
rules or system permissions.

Next test a material paid appeal in two independently controlled
channels, including a new entrant and nonusers. Verify delivered
variants, sources, receipt and the earliest usable correction
without exposing vote choices. Include a genuine editorial and
a protected private complaint. Reopen on selective enforcement,
missing receipts, privacy loss, reduced access or an equally
effective simpler rule.
