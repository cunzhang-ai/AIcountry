# Candidate Act E13–E16: remedy and political continuity

*Design 57 proposed clauses; [Act entrance](../19-continuity-law-and-delivery-package.md).*

**E13 — Urgent independent consideration.** A person or lawfully authorized
representative may approach a competent court directly where delay threatens
essential access, bodily agency, a timely defense or effective political
participation. A preassigned qualified duty judge shall consider lawful
interim protection within 24 elapsed hours of the court request's receipt,
earlier if a later
decision would be useless. Filing, interpretation, accommodation, evidence
preservation and proportionate legal help shall be accessible without the
challenged office's permission. No filing or appeal fee shall be charged
for protection under this Act. Assignment, recusal and alternate venue
follow published prior law; a missed clock does not appoint a new judge.
Urgency does not establish final facts, guilt or a new benefit category.

**E14 — Fair interim orders and actual execution.** An interim order shall
state authority, provisional findings, parties, precise act, latest useful
time, lawful payer/resource, executor, review and notice. Ex parte relief
requires stated necessity and the least prejudicial lawful scope; the
affected opposing party shall receive protected notice and an opportunity
to be heard as soon as possible and no later than 72 elapsed hours from
the order. For an unavoidable barrier, one further period of at most
72 hours requires a fresh judicial finding of necessity, least prejudicial
scope, notice attempts and the exact new hearing time. Serial orders or
renamed parties shall not restart this limit. Where lawful notice and
meaningful opportunity are provided, nonattendance does not veto a hearing.
If the limit is missed, the ex parte order cannot continue by silence;
underlying statutory payments, service duties and lawful protections retain
their separate force. Any shorter protective liberty/coercion limit prevails;
this Act shall not extend force or detention by silence. An appeal is not
an automatic stay absent applicable law or a reasoned court order.
Verify actual receipt or execution. Record refusal, cash shortage and
physical incapacity distinctly; a judgment cannot manufacture a worker.

**E15 — Preauthorized substitute, no policy succession.** Where an obligated
official unlawfully withholds a due record or verified payment, a substitute
designated by enacted law may execute only the exact duty after a competent
court's finding or another source statute's specific trigger. The substitute
shall have separate credentials, source access, lawful funds and conflicts
review; an incumbent's replacement nominee or second app label is insufficient.
Law shall designate qualified career roles selected through open merit and
a published assignment/recusal rotation, with standing pay and separate audit.
The challenged office shall not select or remove its named-case substitute,
withhold its source access or control its payment credential. Discipline
requires stated cause, fair procedure and independent legality review;
the substitute cannot choose its own continuing mandate or remuneration.
It shall not determine new eligibility, tax, general priority, judge selection,
private property taking or a disputed election winner. Protect notice,
record integrity and independent audit. If the substitute also cannot act,
report failed remedy and continuing claim rather than asserted compliance.

**E16 — Political access before its opportunity ends.** The ballot office
shall give a reasoned qualification decision under prospective election
law within five calendar days of E02 filing receipt, sooner if a later
decision would make participation ineffective. A further-document request
shall not restart the clock; missing state-held records require lawful
alternative proof, not incumbent permission. Silence does not qualify or
disqualify a contestant, and direct independent review remains available.
Treasury and public-access providers shall deliver its defined equal baseline support
within 48 elapsed hours after qualification, earlier where the lawful
campaign opportunity would otherwise expire. The formula, qualifying rule,
nomination calendar and appropriation shall allow usable pre-vote delivery
and independent correction; late certification caused by official refusal
shall not erase the contestant's timely first filing. The office shall not
substitute ideology, prior seats or incumbent permission for statutory proof.
Courts may order lawful access and preservation under E13–E15. The Act shall
not itself postpone a poll, select a winner, change ballot rules or renew a
caretaker. Such matters retain their exact prior-law forum and conditions.
Assembly sitting, opposition communication, vote evidence and lawful handover
shall retain their enumerated standing support through shortage and budget lapse.

Grant levels and ballot deadlines remain the election statute's distinct
public choices. A payment made after voting shall not count as timely access.
The proposed judicial clocks are hypotheses; no real duty court is certified.
