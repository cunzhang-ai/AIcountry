# Two provider exits and the choice of a standing service

*Design489 · Constructed test of paid continuity and provider choice.*

Continue the [provider closure comparison](../remedy-through-closure/01-one-closure-four-surviving-duties.md).
Two contracted home-support teams exit before the protected service
returns to ordinary operation. The accountable public owner remains
responsible for covered visits, records and existing remedies. Each
call below is for two time-sensitive visits after ordinary work.
These are authored units, dates and qualification assumptions.

| Service window | What the owner must protect |
|---|---|
| First call | Two visits left uncovered by provider A. An existing public backstop has two paid slots available. |
| Second call | A's first recipients have accepted separately funded ordinary receiving. Provider B then exits. Two different visits are due. The first backstop team can do one by the deadline after protected rest and existing assignments. |
| Later ordinary windows | Survey hidden demand and continuing caseload. Test whether two qualified specialist slots can recur, and whether both residents can receive support on an independently challengeable route. |

B used the same staffing intermediary as A, which failed before
both exits. Independent record access is also required. Two provider
names on one unusable scheduler or custodian add no second route. For this case the regulator verifies that both losses
arise from the same unresolved failure. The second exit does not
restart an applicable exceptional clock. If evidence instead shows
a separate cause, apply the governing distinct activation test.
The provider case does not itself trigger national intervention
in local government. Any LD14–LD18 intervention must separately
qualify on its own statutory facts.

## Compare funded ways to serve the next call

Budget three incremental units a cycle and two separate startup
units for the pilot comparison. Existing duties, payroll and
independent complaint capacity have their own protected resources.
A slot includes a qualified worker, paid travel, time, applicable
resident consent and actual receiving. All prices are constructed.

| Arrangement | Payable and qualified second call | What can happen afterward |
|---|---|---|
| Use the original finite public backstop | If its original authority remains in force, it has one usable slot for one unit. A second named roster relying on that same unavailable worker adds zero. | Report one missed visit. A second call or contractor exit cannot renew the backstop. |
| Ordinary public standby plus independent mutual aid | Use a separately authorized one-unit ordinary standby slot in place of the temporary slot. Reserve one voluntary cooperative slot for one further unit. The cooperative must independently staff it, accept the task and preserve its own residents' floor. This scope requires one startup unit for qualification and handoff. | Both visits qualify for two recurring units if the cooperation still holds. If the sending locality has a competing protected call, its slot is unavailable. Verify the return, rest, renewed staffing and the next call before relying again. |
| Ordinary public specialist service | An ordinary separately voted service can provide two slots for three a cycle, with two startup units and a crew independent of the failed intermediary. The team must actually be ready at the second deadline for credit in that window. | Both visits can qualify on the stated inputs. The two slots replace earlier cover for those visits. The ordinary mandate is limited to this protected class. Preserve other eligible public, cooperative and private routes where available, with independent review and portability. |

Prefer the qualified combined route at two a cycle while it
protects both people without moving harm to the sending locality.
If repair of a shared scheduler or custodian could restore genuine
independence, price that under the same readiness test. If a
simultaneous sending call is recurrent and the combined route
cannot meet the protected floor, bring the ordinary specialist
service to a separate elected choice. Use it for the proven scope
when two startup units, three recurring units, payroll, suitable
receiving and independent review all qualify. At one startup unit
it cannot start on the stated terms. It may be prepared while the
lawful backstop operates, with separately funded overlap. A legally
expired temporary authority supplies no bridge. If neither route
can deliver, preserve original due claims, lawful urgent alternatives,
review and the disclosed service gap.

## Keep a standing provider replaceable

A standing public service can be the only practical route in a
thin market. Preserve the resident's choice of worker or schedule
where feasible, a safe process to refuse a proposed handoff, and
direct review with a payer and records outside the operator's
control. Disclose when no qualified alternative exists. The temporary dispatcher
cannot choose its own successor or become the sole source of evidence
for a complaint. Give affected residents and opposition a timely chance
to propose a qualified alternative. A compact requires its own lawful
council decisions, protected exit and receiving at both ends. Maintain
interoperable records on a minimum lawful scope, paid handover and
a realistic replacement path without forcing residents to switch
or giving a provider control over the complaint against it.
No extra charge or transfer of unrelated data follows from using
a backup. Existing independent service and worker claims continue.

Reassess after the next ordinary service window, with an accepting
receiver and protected cases before ending any temporary work.
Compare the same duties, actual departure causes, resident choices
and costs on both ends. A persistent need for public capacity can
justify retaining it by ordinary law. It does not by itself justify
excluding another qualified provider or prolonging exceptional powers.
If the ordinary vote is missed, preserve ordinary duties and lawful
independent relief while exceptional authority ends on its own clock.
A separately proved new failure enters a separate decision under
existing law and cannot absorb old uncovered cases.
AI can expose shared staff or custody and compare time windows.
People with legal authority decide the service, payer, release and
remedy. Public performance measures keep withheld consent, unavailable
alternatives and actual missed visits visible.
