# Follow actual claims, ownership and different institutional choices

*SF07–SF08; part of [C06/25](../25-source-finance-and-payment-continuity.md).*

**SF07 — A matched book separates six powers and eight claim holders.**
Keep Treasury, monetary authority, bank/credit vehicle, safe custodian,
producers, households, social insurance and foreign parties distinct.
Trace every asset to its counterpart liability and controller. A scope
consolidation removes internal claims, never the actual fiscal or monetary
mandate. Public bank ownership diluted by outside capital cannot retain its
old dividend rate by assertion; protected customer reserves are not bank assets.

The conditional hybrid preserves real assets 595 public/1920 producer and
known domestic resident net claims 2485; foreign net claim 30 accounts for
the difference. Unknown pension L(t) remains a paired H asset/S liability,
never zero. Gross sovereign debt becomes 1005 instead of 1080; it is still debt.
Bank assets 424.0925 loans +95.304167 reserves +175 sovereign paper balance
494.396667 deposits +200 common equity. Separate custody assets/liabilities
are 179.695833; monetary sovereign 200 +own FX75 balance total reserves 275.
Treasury FX100 remains separate and is not spent twice or pledged by default.

Map legacy 80 to its actual holder. The closing snapshot is a reference
date, not permission to postpone an actual maturity. The same unchanged
bill/untouched-standby boundary also tests 80 before first receipts.
The hybrid's remaining H bonds cover that redemption: G bank cash/bonds
fall 80; H bank cash rises 80 and its bonds fall 80. Principal is not income,
GDP or new public revenue. In the full-safe rival, part of that legacy debt
has moved to the monetary authority; its principal payment extinguishes
corresponding monetary liabilities instead. A single Treasury deduction
without the recipient and changed claims is not a reconciled maturity plan.

**SF08 — Select the hybrid conditionally; preserve a real rival.** The old
E100/sovereign 250 source remains rejected under M03. Reducing borrowing
alone cannot supply the entire safeguarded public period from old reserves.
The selected reference adds real common capital/source conversion and funded
working stock, with a distinctly owned standby; it does not merely waive a ratio.

A full-reserve payment/entirely equity-funded credit alternative is also
specified. Keeping new borrowing 175 would require 499.0925 of the named
household bonds, above the preserved 425 portfolio; no extra investor is invented.
Reducing new borrowing to 95 allows full reserve 594.0925 with equity conversion
419.0925, but exchanges almost the whole household sovereign portfolio for
bank/credit residual risk and leaves less Treasury cash. Its missing-remittance
cash boundary fails. This can still be preferred on legitimate risk/access
values with a differently funded whole package; it is not a decorative option.

Neither more marketable paper nor more public reserve claims creates actual
output, clinicians, imports, public consent or independence. Lower required
GDP reflects changed demand/claims, not productive progress. Keep ownership,
private safe-account demand, credit access/cost, fiscal loss, currency exposure
and public-control consequences visible when retaining or displacing a model.
