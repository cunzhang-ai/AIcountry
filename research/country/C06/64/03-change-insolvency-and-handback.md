# Changes, insolvency and handback

*Part of [PL public concessions](../64-public-concessions-and-future-budget-control.md).*

**PL09 — Treat material renegotiation as a new public choice.** Recompute
exposure and alternatives when a price, term, refinancing,
guarantee, controller or retained risk changes. Apply SC13
competition and DB10 authority to related amendments as a
group. Renew scrutiny where a revision would materially
change the elected comparison or the parties eligible to bid.
Share gains from material public-backed refinancing where
the elected terms provide for them. A disclosed formula can
execute within its existing authority. A side letter or a
threat to interrupt service supplies no authority for a new
payment or extension. Publish the scope, conditions and
expiry of any lawful temporary continuity purchase. It ends
when the authorized bridge ends.

**PL10 — Rescue the service through its own lawful route.** Before
the award, name the qualified fallback operator, exact
authority, physical access, independent credentials, staff,
working capital and source. Test relevant common failure.
If a provider defaults, apply the negotiated and lawful
remedies, time-bound lender cure or approved substitution.
A lender's substitute meets the same ownership, control,
service and safety tests. An operator change does not bypass
competition, courts or the affected elected body. When a
lender cure cannot deliver in useful time, the public service
owner activates its accepting alternative. Preserve valid
property, creditor, workers' pay, pension and remedy rights.
Support for preserving service is separately valued, scoped
and voted when needed. It does not automatically make private
equity or debt whole. Genuine remaining gaps stay visible.

**PL11 — Value termination before paying and verify handback.** Before
award, set a separate trigger, method, cap and source for
the legal compensation under each end path, including
verified asset condition, unperformed work, rectification
and collectible security. Use independent valuation, a
protected challenge route and a timely lawful provisional
or disputed-sum procedure where the final figure is
uncertain. Avoid paying the same asset or loss twice. Do
not combine all future operating payments with termination
payments when the contract makes them exclusive. Count
concurrent takeover, rectification and replacement costs
with any amount still due. Test the receiving operator,
asset records, rights, data and credentials before release
of handback security. Start while repair and transition can
still be completed within the lawful term. Rebid, public
operation or another lawful successor is an actual choice,
without automatic renewal.

**PL12 — Keep the decision revisable and the rights continuous.**
Report full-year outturn, forward commitments, risk changes,
unmet services, public and user costs and actual delivery
through expiry. The legally responsible contract owner
monitors delivery. The existing independent audit, legislative
and court functions keep their own appointment, source,
protected evidence and remedy execution. Users, providers
and opposition can challenge exclusion, hidden guarantees
and failure to provide an ordered remedy. Verify common
controllers and funded receiving during a hostile handover.
Prospective enactment does not rewrite existing obligations.
Map leases, claims, employment, records, asset title and
continuing finance under FT/OP/T. Reopen on persistent
lock-in, uncollectible protection, material exposures, weaker
service or a better feasible operating and financing path.
