## Alternatives, objections and review

The [constructed comparison](../settlement/standing-public-ai-capacity/01-one-shared-service-three-independent-duties.md)
tests an ordinary repair alternative, owner-operated minima with optional shared
support and an integrated operator with true independent cover. It prices the
additional cost of a successor at the next concurrent deadlines and compares
directly staffed language access with optional shared tools. The public sources
there support limited claims about separable data exchange and implementation
needs. They cannot validate those costs or the national choice.

Pooled staff can make rare expertise available to small authorities. Separating
control can increase cost and still leave a rural service short when another
office hires the same worker. Keep qualified common training and staff sharing
where the dispatch rights, privacy and coincident work are independently usable.
An integrated operator with genuinely separate paid receiving can displace the
selection if it performs better on the same protection and recurring envelope.
Keeping independent systems also creates maintenance and accessibility costs.
Fund and observe those costs rather than assuming all necessary work can be done
on paper. Elected bodies retain budget choices subject to governing law and
timely independent challenge.

Assistant review examined fiscal allocation, institutional authority and rival
arrangements. It identified two corrections adopted here. Elected councils
retain their own budgets. The integrated operator's first-call cover fits the
same current envelope as the alternatives. This review did not supply affected
participation, institutionally independent review or legal approval.

## Migration and the next comparison

Before retiring any legacy route, the competent bodies name the duty owners,
independent receivers and executing officers, applicable law and appropriation,
qualified local help and paid overlap. Keep original records within lawful
custody, outstanding claims on their original dates, and existing review,
employment, payment and contract obligations. Existing enactment, permissions
and the frozen [Design520 edition](../2026-11-first-round-stable/README.md)
retain their status. A payee or applicant does not have to resubmit because a
shared service was replaced.

The bounded next decision is to choose between the three arrangements on a
single costed court, election and essential-payment schedule at an actual
competent adopter. Use the applicable elected budget ceiling. Require dated
obligations, live staffing and successor availability, independent original
custodians, lawful gross payment capacity, actual receiving and published safe
reasons. Invite affected residents, opposition, adjudicators and delivery staff
through independent routes. Do not count simulated loss as actual execution.
Reopen on missed deadlines, common custody, unavailable appropriations,
selective denial, a staffing conflict or an integrated arrangement with better
protected receipt. Until such evidence is available, authority, paid national
operation, resident outcomes and comparative superiority remain unverified.
