# 213. The first programme under concurrent losses

[Design305](../../2026-11-first-programme-concurrent-losses/decision-record.md)
places additional due water and care on one first-budget schedule.
The earlier energy and training option fits each shock in isolation
and falls short under both. The [transition rules](../../country/C14/06/07-first-programme-under-concurrent-losses.md)
require a newly qualified exact choice and protection of already
binding work. The [constructed test](../../settlement/integrated-first-programme/06-concurrent-losses-and-binding-duties.md)
retains ordinary repair as a comparator and exposes a weaker
baseline with unmet care. Actual readiness and outcomes are open.
