# Design263 decision record

**Baseline and requested change.** Design262 selects service-first
individual income protection, ordinary advances, actual resource
evidence and an earlier hardship route. It does not specify the
ordinary assessment treatment for a verified monthly wage shifted
across the boundary by a non-banking day. Select a narrow rule for
that shift and coordinate the same dated budget with actual wage
loss. Use the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision handoff](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

**Evidence and competing explanations.** The court and agency
records distinguish a timing artifact from real variation in pay.
A third problem is an incorrect or inaccessible wage record.
A calendar correction addresses the first. The actual resources,
wage claim and receipt rules address the others. The historical
records are evidence about a different benefit system. No local
worker, claimant, payroll controller or independent authority has
participated in this proposed rule. [Source scope](sources.md)

**Selection and rivals.** Select one verified regular monthly wage
per scheduled assessment period for ordinary early-day shifts
with actual usable receipt. Keep actual resource hardship protection
and the applicable rules for true late pay, changed work and nonmonthly
earnings. The raw rule has simpler sources but can lose annual
support and concentrate hardship. Rolling averages could reduce
regular variation while carrying a lost wage into current assessment.
Keep them only as a qualified prospective rival. An individual
unconditional foundation reduces assessment dependence but requires
its own income, tax, service and genuine loss comparison. In the
authored regular-wage case, the narrow correction costs 10 additional
units across the two periods and shifts 50 to the first period.
Actual missed wages still require a timely 40-unit correction
under the stated needs. [Authored comparison](../settlement/income-timing-and-usable-resources/02-cash-and-assessment-comparison.md)

**Authority and safeguards.** Use existing elected authority over
the assessment law and its appropriations, a responsible benefit
decision officer, protected source evidence, independent review
and actual Treasury and service execution. A benefit officer can
verify the ordinary earnings item without editing employment or
contribution rights. Neither an employer nor an AI match determines
entitlement. A service claimant can correct records offline with
proportionate alternative proof. Deliberate calendar manipulation,
wrong source reporting and legitimate actual income volatility
remain separate. Risk that late and irregular work is disadvantaged
requires a matched qualification of those groups.

**Migration and financing.** Maintain current and earlier valid
awards, first claim dates, due wages, separate contribution credits,
access-cost rights and original remedies. Use explicit prospective
authority, a verified two-period link and no duplicate attribution
at the boundary. Maintain actual income and provisional cash
protection under their own scope. A later reduction is a forecast
until it is lawful and payable. Appropriate and release the early
cash and staff before relying on the reform. Preserve the independent
source and safe receipt paths.

**Next bounded decision.** Compare one verified stable-pay cohort
with month-end shifts, four-weekly workers and genuinely late pay
under one year of actual income and awarded receipts. Include
low-access, disability, agency error and self-employment cases.
Treasury and benefits officials should publish anonymized dated
cash, incurred recovery, unresolved claims and local review staffing.
Specify the funding ceiling, review capacity and legal authority
before an operating test. Reopen on recurrent hardship, unsafe
data requirements, delayed corrections or a simpler funded design
meeting the same duties. Enactment, funded local execution and
better lives remain open.
