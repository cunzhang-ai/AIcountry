# Safe access during an overnight surge

24 September 2026 · Proposed research continuation · No real service deployment

**Research choice:** preserve a directly reachable, independently governed support
route alongside police access. Allow lawful emergency response and support to proceed
in parallel. Neither a police report nor a provider's approval of someone's beliefs
may become a condition of essential help. This develops the existing J1/J2 commitment;
it does not claim to invent it or establish independent providers in practice.

The [candidate access rules](access-candidate.md) specify responsibility, safe contact,
data access, refusal remedies and institutional accountability. The [overnight dossier](overnight-dossier.md)
compares two sequences using the same four households, rooms, vehicles and budget.
The [decision record](decision-record.md) contains source scope, dissent and migration.

The [model](night_model.py) executed five conditions for each sequence. In the invented
baseline, parallel support gives earlier modelled arrivals. Neither route places the
household needing accessible transport when that vehicle is unavailable, nor the
two-person household when its suitable room is unavailable. A reserve budget alone
does not create a replacement vehicle or room. These are scheduling results, not
observations of safety, institutional independence or crime reduction.

Inputs and results: [declared assumptions](night-inputs.json), [calculated traces](night-results.json),
[source manifest](source-manifest.json), [validation](validation.json).
Earlier [everyday-justice provisions](../2026-09-everyday-justice/candidate-provisions.md)
remain preserved. All coercive acts still require separately established lawful authority.
