# Keep the country accountable between a failed budget and lawful succession

*Design522 · Constructed opening comparison*

The previous [opening case](../opening-resilience-and-public-choice/01-govern-and-deliver-without-the-common-channel.md)
puts a limited supply motion before a still-competent Assembly. This case
distinguishes a rejected motion or missing quorum in a competent Assembly
from a judgment that removes the Assembly's legal capacity to enact supply.
Only the latter branch can use the separate preauthorized supplement proposed
here. The proposed [BLC11–BLC15](../../country/C06/66/02-protected-supply-through-an-impasse.md)
place exact independent authority and cash before that moment.

## Compare real alternatives to a legal gap

Article 111 of Germany's Basic Law permits necessary federal expenditure in
specified categories until the successor budget takes effect, including
existing legal obligations. It provides capped borrowing authority if specified
sources do not cover that expenditure. Canada's federal Financial Administration
Act section 30 uses a different mechanism for urgent payments without another
appropriation during a defined dissolution-related window, with executive
reports, a signed special warrant, publication and reconciliation in the next
appropriation. The later window has an express prorogation exclusion. These
give strong legal alternatives with different breadth, discretion and timing.
Neither text alone establishes independently delivered care or ballot access.

## Put all necessary acts on the same clock

Assume a final court judgment on lapse day 45 annuls the whole legislative
election, before the day-60 supply vote. The lawful judgment-based repeat
opens on day 121 and closes on day 135. An old Assembly does not revive. In a separate branch, a
still-competent Assembly rejects supply or misses quorum. Its existing
process and remedies apply and the supplement stays held.
Lapse bridge operation ends before day 91. Any accrued-only closeout is a
separate source solely for day-90-or-earlier earned work. No new policy may
rely on it. This comparison gives the same people, source restrictions and
existing duties to every option. The early receipt of an eligible ballot does
not itself settle the later count or end the formation impasse.

If only two additional units settle in each window, water can work and
the later window remains one unit short. One way this happens is when a
shared revenue source supplies the first transfer and falls short on the
second. A separately authorized and independently funded reserve can
complete the later unit if it has settled and its route remains usable.
The earlier two units already spent on water cannot be reused. If the later
two units are reserved for the due care, the succession receiver remains
uncovered. Protect any prior reservations and apply each existing
purpose and remedy. The comparison itself sets no priority between care and
democratic control. If the cash totals fit but the alternate payer and
ordinary payer depend on the same unavailable controller, mark all affected
lines by actual deadline. Another signature does not make the receiving
channel independent. If a separate water delivery is funded but unsafe or
unaccepted, the resident can still receive a care visit and miss the necessary
safe water. Publish both component receipts and the failed combination.

## Follow the person and the institutions beyond expiry

Keep protected evidence of the original cash and care claims, usable water,
opposition access, any executed judicial order and each allowed step of an
eligible ballot. The resident's evidence may be lawfully held by different
receivers. Use a supported personal account or qualified scoped linkage with
applicable privacy safeguards to verify sequence. If the sequence cannot be
verified, record the unknown. A late delivery, settled invoice or completed
appointment does not restore a prior lost opportunity. The resident can
challenge a specific missed act without diagnosing the fiscal failure.

At expiry preserve older duties, earned worker and supplier claims, pending
orders, the identity of the lawful successor and the actual authority that
still allows payment. If no competent later budget has taken effect, record
each succeeding gap and use only independently authorized alternatives.
For an additional authored outcome check, eight people each need an essential
service, independent correction and usable civic access in the same window.
Two arrangements each reach six for each component. Under the first, missed
people are F and G for service, G and H for review, and F and H for civic access.
Five people receive all three. Under the second, misses are D and E, E and F,
and G and H respectively. Only three receive all three. Report unknown journeys
as unknown, and publish supported bounds where the comparison can justify them.
Civic access means an effective opportunity. It includes free abstention and
does not involve collecting voting choices. Use a voluntary resident-side or
lawfully scoped independent sample under PCO09 and PCO23. Include people
outside ordinary intake where need and opportunity can lawfully be identified.
Separate the funded human comparator from any optional AI route, and verify
receipt again after a common failure or at the next due call.

An exact adopter must supply the legal texts, funded source, next date, paid
rosters and eligible population. The conditional preference for a narrow
prior supplement is a design selection under these assumptions, with actual
performance and comparative lives still open.
