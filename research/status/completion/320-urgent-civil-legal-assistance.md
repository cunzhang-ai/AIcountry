# Design412. Provide conflict-free civil assistance before essential loss

[UCL01–UCL08](../../country/C08/25/13-urgent-civil-legal-assistance.md)
assigns early advice, scoped representation, independent review and paid
alternates, with a real handoff to the competent court and executor.

[Worked case](../../settlement/urgent-civil-legal-assistance/01-two-urgent-claims-and-one-lawyer.md)
tests simultaneous urgent claims, a conflicted provider, dated cash and
actual execution. Real workload, cost and outcomes remain open.
