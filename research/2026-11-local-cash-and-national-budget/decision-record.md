# Design436. Qualify a local payment with both sides of the transfer

Design435 is the predecessor. T55–T60 set the opening perimeter
and MF09–MF13 already protect the resident through reimbursement
delays. T61–T66 bring that local duty into the opening programme's
consolidated and payer-specific accounts. The revision used AI
assistance and a parallel evidence review.

Use Copenhagen in the same 2026 historical comparison as the
central government. The local [source record](sources.md)
supplies dated reports with distinct meanings, including a later
adjustment path and a displayed reconciliation residual of
negative DKK 1.0 million. There is no full transaction ledger,
verified unrestricted balance at a duty date or verified
affected-party participation. No independent qualified review,
lawful adoption or appropriation was obtained.

Compare arrears with a funded local bridge, a national advance
and a lawful alternate payer. A funded local bridge can be
simpler and preserve ordinary control. It can shift delay and
denial exposure onto a weak locality. An advance can secure
timely payment and reduce that exposure, with cash and loss
risk for the funder. A direct alternate can protect an urgent
duty where local receiving fails, while adding administration
and risking displacement of local responsibility. Select a
bounded advance for the case where the local route is competent,
liquidity is short and a lawful central source can reach it in
time. Keep actual receipt and final reconciliation separately
reviewable. No new power to cut services or impose political
conditions is selected.

Retain original legal dates and ongoing claims. Keep the
elected budget, lawful grant conditions, local delivery, privacy
and independent remedy in force through adoption or succession.
Use one real opening transfer to reconcile both postings,
purpose and settlement, and follow it to actual local receipt
and a protected payment. Test simultaneous claims and the
next funded period. Reopen on missing legal availability, a
persistent bridge or a more reliable ordinary payer route.
The complete opening budget and country-wide outcomes remain open.
