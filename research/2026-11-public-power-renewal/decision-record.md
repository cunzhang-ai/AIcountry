# Design340 decision on legislative control of continuing executive powers

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design339, A2, DR01–DR24, PE, the existing committee and Clerk roles
and the working framework. The country already controls enactment,
urgent expiry and a member-led annulment motion. That still leaves an
ordinary continuing power in place when an executive withholds evidence
and a majority avoids an effective renewal vote.

Select a prospective fixed term for a defined class of burdensome
national delegations, backed by independent evidence, a protected Assembly
decision and a funded expiry plan. In the reference case a material
recertification power ends unless renewed by express primary law.
Entitlement and ordinary proof remain under their own law.

## Alternatives and objections

Retain case-by-case call-in and annulment. This preserves flexibility,
with dependence on someone overcoming inertia. Require scheduled review
without expiry. This protects stable administration, with less practical
leverage when the government can ignore findings. Apply a wide automatic
sunset. This can reduce accumulation but can swamp the Assembly and put
service duties at risk. Select targeted expiry and require useful
pre-expiry review. Keep narrower review-only and selective repeal as
live alternatives if actual workload or disruption proves excessive.

The four-year maximum, review offsets and early-review threshold are
institutional choices. The [official comparisons](sources.md) inform
scrutiny, confirmation and expiry. They do not establish these parameters.
A majority can still renew poor policy through competent law. Concentrated
evidence control, simultaneous expiries and short election transitions are
serious objections. The design uses existing independent evidence and
procedural review and separates expiry of the power from public obligations.

## Transition and reopening

The Assembly controls enactment and financing. The Clerk and subject
committee hold the agenda and record duties. The operator owes an effective
handover and the courts handle legality and individual relief. This is an
assistant-authored selection. No outside qualified or affected-party review
or enactment has occurred. New powers use the rule prospectively. Review
existing powers in order of actual burden without changing their dates
through an inventory. Preserve valid accrued claims and ordinary duties.

Exercise two concurrent reviews across a change of government. Include
an uncooperative ministry, affected users, independent auditors and a
legislature facing real agenda limits. Measure actual evidence access,
staff time, service receipts and continued uses of expired authority.
Reopen on routine deadline evasion, unserved rights, capture of review,
ineffective hearings or a simpler alternative with equivalent public control.
