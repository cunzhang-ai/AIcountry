# Constitutional review: discipline and material independence

*Canonical CJ13–CJ14; part of [C03/08](../08-constitutional-review-and-public-control.md).*

**CJ13 — Accountability without removal for a ruling.** Anyone may report serious
misconduct through protected human/offline/remote intake; a complaint need not be
a complete legal dossier. A separately assigned civilian investigator and prosecutor
outside the implicated court gather lawful evidence. An accusation, AI score,
unpopular interpretation, lawful dissent, good-faith nominee rejection or peaceful
opposition is not proof or an automatic suspension.

Removal grounds are serious corruption, deliberate abuse of judicial office,
intentional material qualification fraud, a proved continuing incompatible office
or permanent inability to perform essential duties after reasonable accommodation.
A diagnosis, disability, age stereotype or lawful judicial interpretation alone
is not a ground. Punitive misconduct needs proof beyond reasonable doubt;
functional incapacity needs clear and convincing evidence of the actual duties
and failed reasonable accommodation, not an assumption of dangerousness.
Criminal conviction follows the separate P/K process and finite lawful sanctions.

Three unconflicted ordinary appellate judges, assigned from the CJ12 roster,
hear discipline with disclosure, funded defense, witnesses, reasoned findings
and public reasons with necessary privacy. Two affirmative votes may find a
ground and order a proportionate remedy; removal is stayed for a seven-day appeal
window and, if appealed, until the different CJ12 chamber's final disposition.
That chamber reviews facts and law, requires its four votes and cannot rely on
unproved accusation. Courts may correct or censure lesser established misconduct;
no secret council can dismiss a judge or punish an adverse lawful judgment.

The three-person panel may unanimously suspend only on evidenced immediate risk
to the proceedings, people or judicial acts, for at most fourteen elapsed days,
with immediate external appeal and an adversarial hearing within seven days.
One reasoned fourteen-day renewal is the maximum before final disposition;
related accusations or new labels cannot restart twenty-eight days. The reserve
serves only under CJ11. During an unresolved removal appeal the judge remains in office. Suspension
ends absent an operative final removal/incapacity order; after its hard ceiling
lawful duties resume, subject to independently justified actual-case recusal.
Repeated nominal recusals cannot become an unreviewed office-wide suspension.
An unresolved appeal is neither final removal nor exoneration. Investigation
may continue without disabling the bench indefinitely. These periods are design hypotheses.

**CJ14 — Funding without self-appropriation.** The Assembly enacts a bounded
standing schedule for judges, reserves, filing, clerks, counsel, interpretation,
records, source backup and lawful execution. Core lawfully due amounts continue
under that schedule beyond the separate ninety-day bridge; this is neither
unlimited previous spending nor a power to enlarge remuneration or a program.
Career commitment, independent verification, Treasury payment, safeguarded custody
and exact preauthorized substitution are distinct roles in [C06/29](../../C06/29-constitutional-review-capacity.md).

Refusal to pay an existing lawful due goes to an unconflicted ordinary financial
court; a disputed constitutional judge's personal-remuneration entitlement stays
within CJ12. General constitutional-court budget legality/retaliation goes finally
to CJ12, not back to the beneficiary court. CJ12's direct-personal-interest rule
also applies to financial courts and correction formations; assigning another
beneficiary cannot make an all-judge pay case impartial. Independently due payments
and urgent ordinary claims continue within their lawful competence. Review may compel available authorized
payment, disallow unlawful discrimination or require lawful reconsideration.
It cannot appropriate new money or substitute judicial policy for Assembly
allocation. Genuine shortage remains an unpaid obligation with a funding decision
and protection/remedy duties, not compliance or automatic seizure of a pension.
