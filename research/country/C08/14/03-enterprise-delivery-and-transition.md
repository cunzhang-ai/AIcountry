# Enterprise rescue, lawful closure and independent delivery

*U09–U12; part of [C08 private insolvency](../14-private-insolvency-and-debt-recovery.md).*

**U09 — Viable restructuring with real sources.** A viable ordinary firm
can seek a disclosed plan instead of immediate liquidation. Show actual
cash, stocks, contracts, workers, imports, collateral and committed new
finance, with unfavorable cases and comparison to orderly liquidation.
Expected future GDP/revenue is not a received loan. Distinguish independent
claim classes and allowed amounts; a confirmed plan needs two-thirds of
voting claim value in every impaired consenting class. Related votes and
agent/account splits cannot fabricate another mandate; record controllers.
Court confirmation requires notice, fair valuation, feasibility and no
nonconsenting creditor worse off than its lawful liquidation comparator.
Any dissenting-class binding needs that protection, at least one genuinely
impaired consenting class, respect for senior claims and no owner retention
at a nonconsenting class's expense. No new security priority overrides existing
collateral without consent or demonstrated lawful adequate protection.

A simplified route is available when actual assets/claims and service
dependencies are sufficiently bounded for fair notice/valuation; the court
gives reasons and converts to ordinary procedure if hidden complexity defeats
those protections. It cannot waive rights by calling a firm small.
Give eligible firms proportionate accounts/help and up to 90 days to present a
plan, with one court-reviewed extension up to 90 days for specific viable
work and actually funded continuation. Shorter safety/cash exhaustion controls
actual operation; neither duration licenses unpaid current wages or unsafe
production. Workers receive voice on affected work, protected claims and
actual continuity; creditor votes do not waive labor, clinical, environmental
or democratic rights. A plan cannot force new credit, labor or public subsidy.
Critical licensed services need their separate safe receiving/fallback even
where the company cannot continue. Public acquisition/cooperative alternatives
need lawful voluntary or elected scope and actual purchase/operation finance.

**U10 — Loss and closure.** If no feasible lawful plan exists, liquidate
through fair source-backed valuation, notice, challenges and U04's priorities.
Preserve contracts/records, actual secured/client rights and workers' wages;
assign safe service receivers rather than treating company closure as the
end of every person's claim. Enterprise discharge/dissolution concerns that
entity's included liabilities; it does not automatically free personal
wrongdoers/guarantors or liability for proved fraudulent asset transfers.
No shell rename or fresh AI account erases old duties. A failed business
alone is no crime, general director ban or permanent civic exclusion.
Creditor losses, layoffs, receiving cost and outside harms remain visible.

**U11 — Neutral case actors and paid correction.** Ordinary courts decide
opening, stays, valuation disputes, confirmation, exceptions and discharge.
A qualified insolvency officer/trustee is assigned through auditable rotation,
conflict rules and an alternate if implicated; no principal creditor or
minister chooses the officer for a named case. Publish bounded neutral
fees and reasons; no seizure/recovery commission or creditor-conditioned
funding buys decision authority. A no-asset access route requires prior
Assembly funding and independent counsel/help. Treasury and court/payment
sources must survive a defendant/vendor refusal under B19, without creating
unlimited judicial appropriation. Parties can challenge officer misconduct,
valuation and missing transfer directly. Verify releases, claim distributions,
credit correction and safe receiving; case closure is no actual payment.

**U12 — System and migration boundaries.** Bank resolution, safeguarded
client balances, pensions and sovereign distress retain distinct C06 law;
private discharge cannot spend restricted insurance or authorize a taxpayer
bailout. Assess creditor bank/pension exposures, pricing, entry, risk shifting
and household incidence in the joined national account before scaling these
rules. A legal write-off is a creditor loss, not new national output or cash.
Inherited liens, contracts, guarantees, family/support claims, court dates
and pending judgments need explicit lawful prospective/transition treatment,
affected notice and competent successors. No software update adopts a haircut.

The three-year plan/flag, half-excess share, buffers, lookbacks and voting/plan
periods are candidate values requiring actual credit/worker/victim comparison.
Cross-border recognition and sector-specific exceptions require their own
valid law/treaty; there is no universal reach. Reopen on exploitation of
exceptions, harmed dependants/victims, absent receivers, captured officers,
credit exclusion or a better lawful model. No actual lending, funding,
participation, execution or improved outcomes is demonstrated here.

[B50](../17/05-parallel-cases-and-insolvency.md) now supplies a proposed
recognition/coordination route without automatically importing discharge,
spending restricted assets or demoting creditors by nationality. Actual
foreign authority, sources, private claims and safe execution remain unverified.

[OE21–OE25](../../C05/13-enterprise-and-association-constitutions.md) now specifies
ordinary entity change/distress/closure and actual successors, retaining U worker/
secured/creditor needs and sector M/NB failure rules. A merger/split/strike-off
cannot silently erase claims or transfer client/public assets; no new ordinary
Treasury guarantee, insolvency estate asset or work compulsion follows.
