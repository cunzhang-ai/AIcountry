# Design227 decision record. Effective take-up and early benefit delivery

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned design 226](baseline.json)

## Gap and decision

C07 already requires safe outreach, AP protects the first request,
and AH funds help on the person's side. Those duties can coexist
with repeated applications for settled entitlements and delayed
payments to people outside clean records. Select
[TU01–TU12](../country/C04/12-effective-take-up-and-early-benefit-delivery.md)
to give each covered programme a duty to release established payments
and an assisted choice when facts or consent are missing. Keep the
actual sector, custodian and payer responsible. The proposed
[working method](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md)
govern this record. Legislation and authorized budget decisions
remain separate.

The values are equal usable receipt, control of disclosure and
practical exit. The legal rule defines eligibility, amounts and
effective dates. Data quality, reduced work, take-up and errors are
empirical. Whether an entitlement should be universal or targeted
remains an elected choice, with complete cost and burden compared.
A selective early route cannot substitute for that choice.

## Alternatives and objections

| Alternative | Strong case | Material objection |
|---|---|---|
| Repair direct intake and person-side help | Preserves human judgment and open access without new data exchange. | Still leaves repeated requests for already settled facts and makes receipt depend on knowledge or time. |
| Pay established dues and offer assisted confirmation | Removes repeat entry for clearly defined cases and directs paid help to uncertain or unsafe cases. | Needs safe records, protected exceptions and an actual payer. Better documented people might gain while higher needs remain hidden. |
| Enroll through a central multi-programme match | May reduce separate forms and reveal overlapping eligibility. | A common error, coerced disclosure or shared vendor can block many rights. Sensitive facts and actual agency still need separate determinations. |
| Remove eligibility tests or provide the service directly | Reduces proof burdens and can remove costly targeting. | Changes entitlements, incidence, real capacity and source. Requires a complete enacted choice rather than a processing change. |

Select the second route with a simpler ordinary route and lawful
simplification tested first. Start only on narrow existing awards or
expressly enacted triggers with a separately usable intake route.
No presumed outcome follows from the proposed two-working-day
triage clock. Time and safe contact need actual funded receiving.
The [joined case](../settlement/effective-take-up/02-early-delivery-and-hidden-need.md)
shows that released administration time, usable money and unfulfilled
urgent protection can all diverge.

## Control and transition

An existing career sector officer authorizes each due release or
inspectable batch. Source custodians retain limited facts and
their actual responsibilities. A separately accountable Treasury
payer verifies and pays within its authority. The Assembly chooses
the programme, source, disclosure and remedy law. Independent
courts, audit and privacy authorities require their own appointments,
protected finance, lawful evidence access and actual execution.
Inspect dependencies shared by the operator, reviewer and fallback,
including contractors and source records. No minister, vendor or
AI may use a change to a common identity root as final authority
over benefit decisions.

Apply AP, AH, PD, DP, EV and the original income or pension law.
Protect continuing awards, first receipt, due dates, previously
chosen options, safe communications and complaints. An official
or records change neither resets entitlement nor expands lawful
data use. Use FT/OP/T to map funded successive work. Correct the
source and actual receiving separately. A harmed person can
seek ordinary independent correction without first appealing to
the failing software or using an identity wallet.

## Next bounded test

Compare a competent staffed route with early release for one
already settled benefit and one lawful human-confirmation route.
Inspect a safe matched population, missing groups, actual payment,
staff time and ordinary access during failure. Seek affected people
and independent review without assembling a universal case file.
Reopen on repeated false matches, hidden displacement, inability
to correct and pay, privacy harm or a simpler same-scope route.
Actual participation, independent operational review, lawful
enactment, paid execution and better lives remain unestablished.
