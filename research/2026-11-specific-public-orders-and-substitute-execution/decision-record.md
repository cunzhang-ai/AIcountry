# Design295. Specific public orders and substitute execution

10 November 2026 · Research selection · No actual enactment

## Baseline and question

[Design294](baseline.json) prices protected repair before optional
programmes. B19 and SR13–SR17 already provide a narrowly funded
independent record and payment route. That route can still lack
authority to complete a specific act needed for an established order.
Use the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and the
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md). Codex assisted the
analysis. Actual affected parties and an independent professional
have yet to review the proposed change.

## Alternatives and selection

- **Use ordinary enforcement and the funded record and payment
  route.** Prefer it where it can reach actual performance on time.
  It pays the first claimant in the example and cannot supply
  the second claimant's legally required confirmation.
- **Authorize a qualified substitute for the fixed act.** Select this
  narrow option for the declared failure, conditioned on a valid
  court direction, existing scope, funds, consent and accepted
  receiving. It adds a separate qualified slot and credential.
  It cannot decide a contested claim, choose care for a person,
  commandeer a resource or exercise general agency power.
- **Fund a durable ordinary independent deputy or reorganize the
  service.** This costs more in the constructed case and may be
  preferable if repeatedly needed. Preserve it as a real rival.
  A broadly empowered court-appointed manager could resolve more
  failures while concentrating control over unrelated cases and
  budgets. Require separate competent law and compare less
  intrusive ordinary repair before any structural change.

[SR18–SR22](../country/C08/30/05-specific-public-orders-and-substitute-execution.md)
selects the activation, receipt and handback mechanism. The
[worked comparison](../settlement/specific-public-orders/README.md)
uses authored hours and costs. The [primary evidence](sources.md)
motivates the specific-act boundary, with no transfer of foreign
procedural or constitutional authority.

## Authority, objection and migration

A court could become a parallel executive or prefer well-resourced
litigants. Limit execution to specific operative obligations under
express law and use qualified prior appointments and safe direct
access for affected people. Keep separate spending, service,
adjudication and independent challenge. An unavailable official
does not imply personal fault. Preserve ordinary authorized
actions, evidence, wages and original rights. A revised order
cannot restore elapsed time. Expire the actual credential,
verify receipt and hand duties back on accepted lawful terms.
An exhausted budget or absent provider stays a real unresolved
gap. The first budget must show actual execution and the next call.

## Next bounded decision

Use one safe simulation with a consenting payer, sector duty
owner, reviewer, receiving operator and people with relevant
experience. Test a specific established act, missing ordinary
credential, parallel payment and provider refusal. Compare the
cost and useful-time receipt with a staffed ordinary deputy.
Reopen after excessive access, new common control, repeated
reliance or lack of a funded second call. Actual authority,
adoption, operational delivery and improved outcomes remain open.
