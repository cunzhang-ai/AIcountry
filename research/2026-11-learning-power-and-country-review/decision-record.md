## Diagnosis and substantive rivals

Learning builds practical future freedom and civic/scientific understanding;
it can also reproduce dependency if a parent, politician, profession or vendor
controls teaching, access, employment and complaints together. This is a causal
institutional hypothesis, not actual school-effect evidence. Scarce competent
staff/materials and unequal private resources may explain failure better than
more governance; lived relationships and informal cooperation have value.

Retain the local public backbone with plural equivalent routes; use existing
C04 merit/public guarantee/NC inspection/ordinary court structures, not a new
permanent school council or national sovereign education board. Strong local
repair may be simpler/better; voucher/parent choice may improve exit yet worsen
selection/resources; community/worker schools may strengthen responsibility
yet close out dissent; national schools reduce some local dependence but risk
central capture; stronger learner self-government gives real agency yet cannot
erase adult safeguarding/paid duties. They remain different real alternatives,
not automatically absorbed into this choice. Actual scale/source/public values
could reject ED. No test-score, efficiency metric or country ranking settles it.
