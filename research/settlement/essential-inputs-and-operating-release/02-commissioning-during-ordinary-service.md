# Commissioning new equipment through a live public network

*Design470 · A constructed branch of the national operating comparison.*

Use the [Design467 schedule](01-paid-at-port-and-needed-at-the-site.md):
18 domestic cash units, four dispatch slots, three qualified review slots
and two independent communication routes. Ordinary repair R uses
15 cash, three dispatch and two review, with both communication
routes protected. The local stage L adds three cash, one dispatch
and one review. The two stages reach the same local service recipients
as in the earlier case. The units, outage and working schedules here are
constructed. Copenhagen's January and May transitions supply a reason
to test overlapping delivery, with no observed shared outage or approved
site implied.

## A successful installation that would interrupt the continuing service

The compatible local module arrives at hour 42. The in-place
installation and test at hours 42–52 need an isolation of the branch
that feeds a care facility and an assisted home route. Their original
safe water operation needs continuous pressure through hour 48. In
this branch, there is no safe restart before that delivery, although
the new module could be signed off at hour 54 and would be usable at
hour 60. The paid installer and independent safety verifier are
available. The source of failure is the shared live system.

| Choice on the same protected work | Cash, dispatch and review | Decision in this branch |
|---|---|---|
| R, plus any previously authorized and separable first-stage work on its own funding | 15, 3, 2 in this second window | Preserve existing water and care, original remedies and the independent routes. Report the deferred local offer. |
| L, in-place connection during hours 42–52 | 18, 4, 3 | Reject this window. On-time commissioning for the new users would interrupt standing service before hour 48. |
| L, temporary parallel route before isolation | 19, 4, 3, plus a separately booked utility operator included in the price | Requires one additional usable domestic unit, lawful route and independent acceptance before isolation. Restore and verify the original supply through the separately funded next call. |
| L, a later isolated connection | Authorize separately if it fits the verified later schedule | Preserve present service and disclose the missed hour-60 offer. An already binding claim retains that deadline and its remedy. |

The temporary route needs its own water source, independently
accepted load and quality test and safe operation before hour 42.
Neither another valve connected to the same unavailable supply nor
a paid installer supplies those facts. The one-unit premium includes
operation during the first isolation. The stated later service costs
need a distinct operator and paid restoration and verification near the
hour-84 call. If that separate allowance has not been verified,
the tested path covers only the first commissioning window.
No dispatch or review slot is moved from ordinary service.

Select R in the adverse window under the 18-unit cap and keep
authorized preparation that still has independent value. If a
lawful appropriation raises the usable cap to 19 and all relevant
actors accept, put the protected parallel route to its own
decision. Compare it with a narrower isolated site where the
original public choice and actual different reach are explicit.
No numerical assumption supplies a physical backup or its price.
Where the continuing network actually fails, apply existing water
and care continuity law and record losses before considering an
optional start.
