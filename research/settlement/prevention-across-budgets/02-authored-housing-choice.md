# Authored first-budget choice. Keep the home and keep the service

All amounts, households, windows and capacity are constructed. All paths
fund the same protected baseline, including existing housing, urgent
shelter, clinical access, courts and elections. Consider residents already
waiting under the same lawful housing priority. They can accept a suitable
ordinary tenancy and may separately choose support. The options offer no
new means of checking eligibility or withdrawing a right.

| Exact optional motion | By first commitments | Total in year one | Recurring annual source | Accepted homes and backed support places |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A. Repair ordinary intake and access without opening new tenancies | 1 | 3 | 2 | 0 |
| B. Same intake repair and three additional secure tenancies with support available | 2 | 6 | 4 | 3 |
| C. Same intake repair and six additional secure tenancies with support available | 4 | 12 | 8 | 6 |

The intake repair pays for a reachable housing adviser and follow-up for
residents whose current access is blocked by repeat visits or missing
assistance. It opens no extra homes. Measure successful access separately
from a later tenancy. First commitments fall on day 30. Year one totals include day 30.
All figures are incremental gross uses and include the fallback and
independent review required for their scale. Housing and support operators
must be ready at the same time. The recurring source also backs any
lawful later obligation from a tenancy entered in the phase. No participant
is removed at the end of the comparison. The alternatives are nested and
mutually exclusive. Any new cohort uses only actual uncommitted resources.

For C, lawful national money contributes 3, 8 and 5 units at the three
boundaries. The local housing appropriation contributes 1, 4 and 3.
Under the illustrative political choice, elect both by foregoing a new
national administrative upgrade and a local nonessential building refit
with the same schedules and no binding claims. Keep those lost benefits
visible. Ordinary intake and B could instead run on a qualified national
source alone if the local steward accepts delivery under existing authority.
For a lost contribution, national headroom of 3, 8 and 5 can carry B, with
three or more backed places. Two backed places leave A feasible and both
housing cohorts held. Any need left unmet is visible in all paths. Where the
baseline falls below a duty, restore or remedy it under its prior authority.

A forecast attributes five annual cost units to lower crisis-service use.
Even if actual service use falls by that much, a retained hospital team
can have zero cash release. One hypothetical payment of two units to
a shelter operator can cease later, but replacement readiness costs one
unit. After independent review and an elected reallocation, at most one
unit is a lawful later source in this case. It cannot pay day 30 or be
allocated simultaneously to two services. Hospital staff available for
other cases remain on the hospital roster. These scenarios do not use any
of the Canadian trial's estimates.

Select C in the constructed case with both qualified appropriations and six
backed places. Select B if the local contribution fails and all the narrower
conditions hold. Select A if there are fewer than three backed places, and
retain the ordinary baseline if even A lacks qualified resources. A lost
payer after a lawful commitment triggers the existing continuity and review
routes. Existing occupants keep their rights and any actual funding failure
is reported, even if a new intake cohort must close.
