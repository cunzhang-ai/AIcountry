# Design-44 organization note

5 October 2026 · Relocation of the integrated overview's question table

The design-43 [predecessor](predecessor-design-43/README.md) preserves the
single integrated question table before this move. Its twenty-two questions
and operating answers now appear in four current system maps: [democratic
power](../country/systems/01-democratic-power.md), [livelihoods and
services](../country/systems/02-livelihoods-and-services.md), [shared resources
and external dependence](../country/systems/03-resources-and-external-dependence.md),
and [knowledge and change](../country/systems/04-knowledge-ai-and-change.md).
Their former C-number shorthand was replaced with direct links to canonical
numbered topics. The new sovereign-debt question is an additional design-44
selection, explained in the separate [decision record](decision-record.md).

The integrated [country entry](../COUNTRY-DESIGN.md) now states the common
settlement and points to the four systems. [Design status](../DESIGN-STATUS.md)
continues to hold unresolved conflicts and priorities. Its forty-three
individual selections moved without changing their wording from the long
single table into four [system registers](../status/01-current-selections.md).
The fifty-seven predecessor legacy conflicts plus the new debt conflict moved
to four [conflict registers](../status/02-legacy-conflicts.md), retaining their
claims and rebasing only local links.
Detailed topic files
under `country/C01`–`country/C14` remain the canonical current design. Dated
studies and edition snapshots retain their paths and contents as history;
none is promoted to current law by this navigation change.
