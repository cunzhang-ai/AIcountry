# DB11–DB15. Execution cannot reverse the public's law

*Part of [democratic budgets](../60-democratic-budget-and-execution.md).*

**DB11 — Lawful checks, not political withholding.** Treasury profiles payments
against actual due dates, lawful source and independent verification, retaining
urgently permitted provisional evidence rather than a complete-file gate. It
may refuse an unlawful payment instruction with prompt exact reasons, safe
escalation and actual AP/V/B19 review, not pay unlawful orders to satisfy a
political target. A signature/paper approval or a central-bank credit proposal
is not actual money or owned FX. B19 scoped execution and independent payers
retain actual continuing authority; no new Treasury/court tax or borrowing power.

Budget ceilings are maximum permissions, not orders to waste the full amount.
For a discretionary project without an implementation duty, changed facts may
justify reasoned nonuse/cancellation within actual contract/worker/source law.
Identify the foregone output, alternatives, effects and unspent permission;
material programme-wide abandonment returns to Assembly disposition through
protected agenda, with a proposal/report within30calendar days of that decision.
No report or silence authorizes redirecting its resources. Actual urgent safety
may require an earlier lawful halt; the unmet duty and remedy remain visible.

Where standing law or an explicit schedule imposes delivery/implementation,
Cabinet/Treasury cannot remove it by discretionary nonuse, political conditions
or a release hold. Withheld opposition/court/local dues retain actual law.
Distinguish genuine evidence/source failure from disguised cancellation, seek
the competent lawful change and preserve earlier E/claim dates/remedies. The
30-day political response is no stay of those duties, no generic permission to
defer them and no court power to select a preferred project. Annual review can
change lawful future policy; appropriation alone supplies no unlimited new right.

**DB12 — A cash shortage remains a public source failure.** Publish expected
shortfalls before useful time where possible, actual arrears/unmet receiving,
source ownership/currency and lawful alternatives. Existing NP/E19/SF/RF/MC
rules govern actual priorities, protection, permitted source conversion and
elected correction. DB neither invents a uniform pro-rata cut nor grants a
creditor-first, largest-agency or ministerial political priority. A shortage
forecast itself supplies no private seizure, monetary finance, new tax or
withholding authority. Claims, original due dates and remedies persist.

An authority can seek actual permitted financing, a source/appropriation change
or qualified safe interim receiving within law. Missing source/executor remains
failure, not delivery or an automatic veto by a financial expert. Real urgent
physical safety/clinical restrictions retain their own competence and review;
a budget label cannot overrule them or erase resulting unmet service duties.

**DB13 — Year-end expires unused permission, not the person or debt.** At the
actual fiscal year-end, unused annual permission for NEW discretionary commitments
lapses unless a valid Act already authorizes a dated multi-year period. Unused
annual payment authority also lapses unless its exact source law carries it;
identify the successor permission needed to pay lawful outstanding obligations.
Actual contracts, wages, earned rights, court orders, accrued claims and their
records survive. No automatic December spending race, forgiveness of arrears
or re-entry of an old commitment as a new purchase. Account actual deliveries/
expense when due, payment at actual date and source separately.

**DB14 — Carry-forward must be specific and counted once.** An Act may expressly
carry only a named unspent authority/amount/purpose/source into a defined later
period, including accepted capital milestones. Identify existing commitments,
actual remaining headroom, release conditions and expiry; no generic executive
percentage or indefinite rollover. Carry-forward is permission, not new revenue,
received cash, a second commitment or a second acquisition of the same asset.
Reconcile any new annual provision to the carried permission, identifying total
authority actually available; a new line cannot secretly double the same ceiling.

Unspent cash belongs to its actual legal owner/source, subject to custody and
existing liabilities; it is not the agency's private bonus or a general Treasury
fund merely because a permission expired. Restricted pension/security/deposit/
foreign money keeps its law. An appropriation lapse does not reverse irreversible
harm or authorize retroactive refusal of a mature claim.

**DB15 — Keep the existing lapse and caretaker settlement.** Actual standing
essential-obligation law continues under its defined purposes/rules/payers,
including applicable continuing judgments/contract needs and democratic/remedy
operations. The distinct preauthorized bridge covers only its existing lawful
indispensable operations for up to90days, not monthly pro-rata old expenditure.
Actual day30 disclosure/day60 recorded decision and day91 boundary remain; no
renaming, caretaker, election, emergency or missing new budget renews it.

A missed DB01/04 timetable neither approves the Cabinet text nor makes the
Assembly unable to pass a lawful late budget/limited continuation. Accrued duties
survive; other new activity needs actual authority. C03 members can propose a
budget during government formation, and actual lawful confidence/replacement
and peaceful handover are separate. No new spending, vote, term or automatic
first-year source is created by the clock. Current constitutional holds remain.
