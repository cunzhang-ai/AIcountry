# C05. Count shared workers once and protect their rest

*Design409 · SSR01–SSR08 · Proposed implementation of existing work and service duties.*

A safe shift requires a qualified person who can lawfully accept it, useful
time in the right place and a provider able to receive the work. A vacancy,
a list of willing workers or a provisional availability reply supplies none
of those commitments on its own.

**SSR01 — Keep existing rest and employment rules across employers.** For
covered paid work and material standby, apply TW02–TW05 to each person across
their covered jobs, with stronger professional protections where applicable.
Identify actual work, travel between assignments, constrained waiting, breaks,
prior commitments and the interval of safe availability. Ordinary private
activity stays outside work records. An essential duty, employment form or
shortage cannot waive the floor. A worker remains free to hold multiple jobs
and to refuse optional work under the applicable law.

**SSR02 — Verify only what the assignment needs.** An employer verifies the
qualification, role, interval and compatibility of the proposed work. Prefer a
worker-accessible statement of compatible availability or other adequate
evidence. Where sector law requires independent verification, provide a paid,
confidential route through a qualified verifier or protected inspection.
Share the outcome, applicable interval and limitations, without routinely
sharing another employer's name, unrelated schedule, earnings, private life
or protected status. Keep auditable originals with their lawful custodians.
Provide an assisted or offline route and a way to correct a mistake.
No employer, broker or platform gains a general right to monitor second jobs.

**SSR03 — Separate availability, a paid hold and a binding assignment.**
An inquiry gives no reservation. Any limited hold requires actual agreement,
published bounds, an end and lawful payment for constraints. It confers no
general exclusivity. Before crediting a covered assignment, require the
worker's informed acceptance or an actually authorized representative acting
within a bounded mandate, a named lawful employer, the acceptance of the actual
receiving service where distinct, an exact role, location and interval, lawful
pay, rest and travel compatibility, and a usable confirmation. Confirmations may use different media. Distinct intermediaries
and replicated agents cannot allocate one person more than once. Uncertainty
or silence supplies no assignment. Preserve existing lawful employment duties
and claims on their own terms.

**SSR04 — Resolve conflicts before counting coverage.** A receiving employer
rechecks compatibility at commitment and before duty starts if relevant
conditions changed. Simultaneous offers stay provisional until the worker or
lawful representative confirms compatible commitments and releases any
conflicting hold. Where offline coordination is necessary, a delegated
commitment must use a prior nonoverlapping interval and actual limited
authority. Disagreement, delayed confirmation or a stale record must be
resolved through a direct human route before counting the disputed capacity.
Do not cancel a binding shift or wage claim on the authority of a schedule
conflict. Apply the relevant contract, safety and remedy law.

**SSR05 — Pay and verify handover and the next service interval.** Each
provider accounts for origin cover, actual qualified arrival, paid handover,
travel, breaks, standby and rest before the next call. Use the same dated
staff and cash account across ordinary, surge and mutual-aid duties. Where a
handover runs late, secure actual relief, recheck downstream assignments and
record the work and pay owed. An agency cannot shift the shortfall to the
worker or the patient's family. Use [JC](../../C06/65/06-domestic-mutual-aid-and-concurrent-need.md)
for lawful public reserve and its genuine next call. An unavailable shift
stays unavailable even when finance was approved.

**SSR06 — Route a shortage without making a person a reserve.** The responsible
service keeps its duty when a worker cannot safely accept. Arrange separately
paid and qualified relief, redesign, or lawful priority and continuity under
the existing sector rules. Do not conceal a missed service or presume a
transfer to another provider. No new compulsory work or standing emergency
exception follows. Immediate necessity retains its narrow source, actual pay,
independent scrutiny and protected recovery before redeployment.
A justified safety report or refusal cannot cost an unrelated job or service.

**SSR07 — Keep independent correction and worker choice usable.** Workers,
representatives and affected users can report unsafe scheduling, exclusion,
unpaid waiting or failed handover through existing accessible labor and sector
routes. Preserve original dates and protected evidence. Labor and safety
inspectors and courts act under their own powers and can check the real work
and remedies. Fund review and a lawful alternate outside the implicated
provider or broker. Publish safe aggregate missed shifts, lost rest, paid
cover and actual service. AI may compare schedules and identify conflicts.
It cannot infer acceptance, certify safety, discipline workers or set public
priorities.

**SSR08 — Compare staffing choices and migrate prospectively.** Compare a
provider's own paid reserve, a bounded cross-provider pool and changes to
tasks or ordinary staffing on the same time, safety and service assumptions.
Include existing shifts, next-period work, unpaid burdens and the cost of
administering private availability evidence. The elected authority funds
the chosen public arrangement. Named employers retain actual worker and
service duties. Migrate lawful schedules, pay, disputes and custody with
funded successors. Reopen on persistent double booking, unsafe fatigue,
exclusion of multi-job workers or a simpler equally protective route.
