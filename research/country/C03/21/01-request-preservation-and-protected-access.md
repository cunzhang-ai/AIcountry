# PE01–PE04. Give members a direct request and a safe route to original evidence

*Part of [parliamentary scrutiny](../21-parliamentary-evidence-and-independent-access.md).*

**PE01 — Give each member standing to request material existing records.**
A member may request an existing record or identifiable class reasonably
connected to an Assembly decision or authorized national oversight of
public action. State purpose, materiality and the relevant useful date.
No chair, caucus, Cabinet or committee majority must sponsor it. Include
operative instructions, original versions, recorded contrary evidence,
method, validation and use of public money or power. A new study follows
ordinary costing and inquiry routes. Assembly staff help narrow requests,
record first receipt and use accessible ordinary and protected intake.
Residents, workers and sources can use existing public, audit, witness
and WB routes independently. This member right does not condition them.

**PE02 — Preserve the original and answer by the useful date.** A named
public records officer promptly identifies and secures scoped originals,
versions, custody and relevant lawful logs after receiving a proper
request. Maintain a proportionate hold during review, with purpose and
end conditions. An ordinary response within five calendar days from first
receipt gives the existing usable record or access, or specifies held
items, disputed material, search scope, missing items and feasible
completion dates. Respond sooner where useful scrutiny would otherwise
be lost. Material already available is supplied as soon as safely possible.
A specific substantial search may need more time, with reasons and direct
review. The response clock does not reset on referral, relabelling or a
new custodian. Publicly funded providers' relevant official work records
fall within this route where an enacted duty or valid contract gives
the public entity lawful control. A private party's unrelated records
stay under ordinary law.

**PE03 — Require precise reasons for limiting access.** The lawful
custodian gives the requester a sufficiently informative inventory or
safe gist, with the particular ground, extent, available remainder,
possible access modes and review date. Protect identifiable safety risks,
personal data, active case integrity and actual legal privilege on their
own grounds. Use redaction, a secure reading place, nonidentifying
technical results or role-limited access as appropriate. A Cabinet,
vendor or classification label alone cannot settle the claim. Protect
lawful confidential deliberation where its grounds apply. Distinguish it
from the operative decision, its authority and separately existing
implementation evidence. An official should explain an operational
instruction without exposing protected legal advice or forcing a public
waiver. Access, public release and later use have separate decisions.

**PE04 — Let an ordinary court review refusal and delay directly.**
The requesting member, or a person whose protected evidence or rights
face concrete harm from proposed disclosure, may use ordinary independent
review without exhausting a committee or agency route. Give safe notice
and fair reply. The custodian justifies the actual restriction or documents
the search. The adopting law authorizes confidential judicial inspection
of identified originals where necessary to verify the claim, with legal
privilege and other protections decided by the court under governing law.
Sealed inspection solely to resolve a claim preserves applicable privilege.
Use a safe gist or an independent, qualified representative where needed
for effective challenge. The requested disclosure remains within actual
legal bounds. No alleged secrecy or unseen assertion substitutes for
independently tested evidence where fair process requires it.
