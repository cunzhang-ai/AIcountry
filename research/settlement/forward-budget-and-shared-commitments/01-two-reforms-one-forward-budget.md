# Two reforms and one forward budget

Design362 · Constructed comparison under
[FB42–FB47](../../country/C06/62/08-forward-budget-and-shared-commitments.md).

The four years use comparable public expenditure units. Assume the
central path has lawful financing capacity up to the voted expenditure
path, without pretending this finances a particular day or funds a
provider. The weaker path reduces financing capacity and raises
continuing-law costs. The voted expenditure path stays on record.

| Account | Y1 | Y2 | Y3 | Y4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Voted expenditure path and central financing capacity | 112 | 116 | 120 | 124 |
| Central continuing-law cost | 100 | 103 | 106 | 109 |
| Unallocated planning margin | 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 |
| Central room for additions | 9 | 10 | 10 | 11 |
| Weaker financing capacity | 110 | 113 | 116 | 120 |
| Weaker continuing-law cost | 100 | 104 | 107 | 111 |
| Weaker room for additions with margin retained | 7 | 6 | 5 | 5 |

Both sponsors can show a central-path pass separately. Neither can
claim the other proposal's space again. Compare the planned expansions
with two more specific packages.

| New work, separately priced | Y1 | Y2 | Y3 | Y4 | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Full care expansion | 6 | 7 | 8 | 8 | Fits central room alone |
| Full accessible housing-support expansion | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | Fits central room alone |
| Both full expansions | 10 | 11 | 12 | 12 | Exceeds central room in every year |
| Smaller care programme plus full housing support | 9 | 9 | 10 | 10 | Fits central room, exceeds weaker room |
| Staged care plus independently useful housing preparation | 6 | 4 | 4 | 4 | Fits both planning paths |

In the last row, 4 each year buys a limited care cohort, and 2 in
year one pays for accessible housing preparation without starting the
ongoing housing-support programme. This case selects that sequence
while the weaker forecast remains plausible and no additional durable
source qualifies. It delays access for households outside the care
cohort and for those waiting for new housing support. Existing due
services remain in the baseline. Exact cohort sizes, useful dates
and post-year-four costs are not inferred from these units. Before
a real release, the continuing 4-unit obligation would need a
qualified recurring source beyond the example.

If the smaller combined package has already bound, it still requires
9, 9, 10 and 10. After the weaker financing path and continuing-law
costs, even consuming the full planning margin would leave one unit
unfinanced in each of years three and four. Keeping the margin leaves
a larger policy choice. Preserve bound claims and use a lawful
funding or delivery correction. Taking away a planned margin does
not create money, and a late revision cannot restore the original
service date.

Even the staged option needs an actual payer and provider. If the
first payroll needs 3 before only 2 usable units arrive, one unit is
uncovered that day despite the annual pass. Do not release an unbound
start until lawful timely finance and qualified receipt exist.
The case supplies no actual appropriation, price or staffed service.
