# C06. Financing a fair political contest

*Part of [C06 public finance](../C06-public-finance.md); integrated design 101 research, not enacted law.*

Prospective Assembly law creates a bounded election-contest appropriation
covering voter information, baseline campaign access, lawful public support,
audit, accessible filing, independent legal help and rapid judicial relief.
The account separates money given to a contestant from public venues,
mailing, media time, translation and accessible travel that have real
staff and asset costs. Parliament sets the general formula before the
contest; Treasury releases due support by law and cannot delay an
opponent's payment for a political reason. A bounded standing reserve
keeps core election and remedy operations functioning during a budget
lapse, subject to public accounting and later elected revision.

Eligibility and distribution must be feasible for genuine new parties,
civic lists and independents; prior seat share alone cannot assign all
public resources to incumbents. [EN/CF/ER](../C03/10-democratic-entry-and-campaign-resources.md) now selects
two active entry permissions/1% call-roll support, equal paid noncash access,
B*/200 voter-directed credits per lawful track, fixed call-roll caps and private
limits, with staged receipt before earliest voting V. Actual public adoption,
media/court/source capacity and independent review remain unqualified. A contestant's public funding cannot
be double-counted as public media access or venue service, and donated
labor cannot be budgeted as free state staff. Reporting names actual
disbursement date, benefit received, denied access, in-kind value and
remedy execution separately.

Public-office communication, advertising, procurement, grants and
subsidies remain in their service budgets with source records. The
election finance account identifies party-directed diversion and any
lawful restitution claim without suspending essential public services.
It also counts finance-audit staff, privacy protection, courts,
translation and alternative payment or publication routes. The same
reviewer, public hall, mailing route or digital provider cannot be
assigned to mutually exclusive campaign and service duties at once.
The [C03 contest rule](../C03/07-political-finance-and-campaign-access.md)
sets substantive limits; no fiscal officer may change candidate rank,
lawful speech or an election result through a payment ledger.

[ECF01–ECF05](11/01-readiness-trigger-and-paid-election.md) finances
recurring readiness in years with and without a poll and gives lawful
early calls and independent remedies dated sources under existing
Assembly and Treasury authority. It protects actual receiving
during a competing cutover or a budget lapse.
