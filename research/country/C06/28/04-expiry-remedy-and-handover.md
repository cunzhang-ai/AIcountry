# End exceptional allocation power without abandoning the person

*CP07–CP08; part of [national commitments](../28-national-commitments-and-residual-scarcity.md).*

**CP07 — First 24 hours, absolute 72 hours, no tacit renewal.** The local lead's
residual decision lasts only while its actual necessity persists, at most 24
elapsed hours from the first actual related allocation. Cabinet's existing named
civilian coordinator may consolidate within the same enacted pool only through an
affirmative recorded Cabinet decision before expiry, ending absolutely 72 elapsed
hours from that earliest act. Define source, recipients, purpose, quantities,
receiving, costs and end. No continuing emergency label, altered officer, place,
resource code or informally begun order restarts a materially continuous conflict.

Immediately notify affected lawful local/sector owners, opposition, independent
review and the Assembly through funded alternate routes, with protected original
records and aggregate public reasons. Unavailable communication, reviewer or
Assembly sitting grants neither consent nor extension. A further material burden,
extension, different pool or priority requires the competent elected law, not an
executive certificate. A truly distinct later event needs its own independent
facts; it cannot extend the expired related allocation by renaming it.

Expiry ends further exceptional allocations. It does not demand unsafe interruption
of a lawful rescue, confiscation of received aid, repayment of consumed supplies
or cancellation of another independent continuing duty. Minimum supplies already
assigned for the residual interval can be used as lawfully authorized; do not
commit months of future resources on the last hour to evade expiry. Return safely
usable unconsumed stocks under recorded arrangements. Existing clinical/ordinary
powers remain specific, never a disguised power for another exceptional allocation.

**CP08 — Direct remedy and receipt, with real continuing obligations.** Affected
people and helpers can reach independent review without an account, full dossier
or delay through internal referrals. Courts review actual pool authority, false
earmarks, source evidence, relevant priority, equality, expiry and executed relief;
they cannot create stock or choose a new general national allocation mandate.
Protect urgent individual relief and original records when the lead/vendor is
implicated. No institutional title or copied AI report proves independence.

Each order identifies actual receiver, useful due time, lawful payer, displacement,
return/ongoing owner and received outcome. An accepted message or stock dispatch
is not actual safe delivery. If receiving fails, preserve the unmet duty and source
rights, provide available lawful protection and seek funded correction; the expired
residual power does not revive. Wages, losses, pending claims and independent remedies
survive elections, vendor failure, recovery and territorial/operator transition.
Actual personal criminal guilt, if alleged, still needs its own P/K proof; an
impossible resource conflict or late report alone does not establish a crime.
