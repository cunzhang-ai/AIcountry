# Equal political standing, usable ballots and complete local elections

2 November2026 · Integrated design111 · EL01–EL24 research selection, not enacted law

**Replace the working national mixed personal/list count with one nationwide
proportional list choice and an optional personal preference.** Independents may
stand as one-person civic lists under the same count. This removes the count's
special independent-seat exception and the need to assign overseas voters an
artificial national territory. It sacrifices a direct territorial-person mandate
and can make geographically concentrated independent entry harder. Local councils,
paid constituent access and protected territorial rights remain distinct.
