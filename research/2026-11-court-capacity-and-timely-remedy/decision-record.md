## Selection and material objections

Select OJ41–OJ48. Preserve the original claim and clock, protect
lawful urgent duties and make material diversion from ordinary cases
visible. Price competent hearing, defense, registry, safe fallback
and execution together when expanding upstream work. Preserve each
owner and independent decision. Immediate triage helps those at
risk and can repeatedly displace ordinary people. Protect usable
ordinary sessions, report actual displacement and give aging cases
a real review path. A rigid reserved percentage can leave scarce
time idle or block urgent access, so the administration applies
prior criteria and existing protection with reasoned adjustments.

Retain the strong existing backlog repair and lawful simpler
procedure as real alternatives. Where an accessible routine route
can supply the same relief, prefer it to creating another stage.
Faster hearings can sacrifice meaningful defense or accessibility.
Increasing judicial staff may help but can miss a distinct counsel,
records or executor bottleneck. Neither ranking by one measure nor
a policy of automatic timeouts is selected. Institutional analysis
or fiscal pressure supplies no case authority.
