# BLC11–BLC15. Protect essential acts when neither a budget nor a new government is ready

*Design522 · Proposed standing supply and safeguards*

**BLC11 — Choose and fund the protected post-bridge window before lapse.**
Use BLC01 standing authority to schedule a bounded, purpose-specific supplement
for an identified gap after the ordinary bridge ends. Place it before the
Assembly with ordinary supply, approved balances and strong repair of existing
authority. Use a separate prospective appropriation only where standing routes
cannot carry the gap and a bounded successor-supply calendar can be priced. Set fixed final dates, named tasks and payers, actual sources and
ceilings before relying on the supplement. Include the post-poll interval where
necessary, using the actual election and formation rules. Cover specific already
authorized public duties whose loss would impair independent democratic
replacement, timely judicial protection or immediate essential receipt. A
supplement does not extend the 90-day operating bridge or repurpose accrued-only
closeout. The option uses ordinary purpose-specific financing.
Budget necessary procurement, payroll and reserve funding before the latest
usable date under their own authority. Test whether apparently separate
sources depend on the same taxpayers, settlement provider or operating shock.
Recheck net settled cash at each cutoff. Count previously spent reserve cash
as available again only after actual lawful replenishment.

**BLC12 — Split the execution of the schedule across existing lawful owners.**
The lawful budget authorizes each purpose and sets the latest useful time. The
existing responsible office confirms its eligible act under source law. The
named statutory payer settles it. An independently funded custodian and review
route checks activation against dated legal evidence, records, actual recipient
and duplicate instructions. Release the additional line only if an operative
judgment or electoral rule has left no legally competent Assembly for supply
when the decision was needed, and no equivalent operative source can fund the
eligible purpose by its useful time. A rejected motion, boycott, failed quorum
in an otherwise competent Assembly or emergency declaration does not qualify.
The law names the independent custodian that verifies those facts and the
review of a refusal or mistaken certificate. Existing election and judicial bodies retain their
own operational and review authority. Where the ordinary payer is unavailable,
only the prior-law alternate within the same purpose and ceiling acts. An
authorized independent verifier identifies timing, authority or scope defects
without taking over the budget or the underlying service. Neither the
caretaker nor the body whose refusal is challenged controls all the gates.

**BLC13 — Preserve political choice during the window.** A competent Assembly
uses the existing day-30 disclosure, day-60 vote and any earlier mandatory
response under E19. Keep a usable independent opposition proposal, vote and
separate confirmation of lawful presence. A budget, limited supply and
confidence retain distinct effects. Where no competent body or lawful quorum
is available, publish each failed legislative act and carry only authority
already in force. Apply the independent election calendar and its own lawful
review. A missed or disputed election, missing investiture, deadline or review
order cannot enlarge a budget line or extend a term.

**BLC14 — Protect the same people across the gap and its end.** Test each dated
payment, actual essential service, executed order, opposition opportunity and
eligible ballot independently. Where protection depends on several acts, check
whether the same person could use them in sequence. Include people outside the
ordinary route. Keep privacy, sector urgency, local competence and existing
lawful reservations. Identify at least one independently paid later call and
price settlement, restocking and recovery separately. Release only an accepted
and authorized package. Keep source-specific balances and any prior uncertain
payment visible. AI may compare the schedules and show uncovered duties.
Actual offices decide, receive and verify. A genuine shortage remains a breach
or an exposed duty within its own law, with direct review and any actual relief.

**BLC15 — End the authority on its own terms.** Use fixed expiry or a verified
earlier handback expressly provided for in the enactment. Scope and receipt of
the new appropriation are separate findings. Verify a lawful successor, actual
settlement, later-day staffing and pending cases before assuming handback.
No new episode, delayed poll or relabelled invoice renews the supplement.
When its finite authority or funds end, preserve accrued claims and dates,
publish the remaining exposure and use the next competent elected decision
with the existing lawful remedies. Reopen the design on a missed cutoff,
partisan activation, repeated use as ordinary supply, common controller or a
qualified cheaper and equally protective predecessor route.
