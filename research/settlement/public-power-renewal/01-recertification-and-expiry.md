# Recertification, withheld evidence and the expiry date

This constructed comparison exercises
[PWR01–PWR06](../../country/C03/01/01-review-and-renewal-of-material-powers.md).
An Act establishes a continuing assistance entitlement and separately gives
the agency four years to require annual recertification from the entire
cohort. The power is material. The authority for targeted verification of
a relevant change in circumstances rests in another provision. A sample of
100 existing awards includes three timely requests for help with proof and
two pending challenges to earlier reductions. Twenty payments are due
after expiry. This case stipulates that all 20 remain due under ordinary
benefit law.

The expiry date is day 0. The Clerk opens the review on day minus 180.
The department fails to submit its required account on day minus 120.
The committee takes independently sourced evidence on day minus 75 and
retains the missing departmental evidence on the docket. A scheduled
election leaves no sitting for the protected decision on day minus 45.
The new Assembly takes its seat on day 5. No competent renewal Act is in
effect on day 0. The support budget covers the ordinary payments.

| Response | Authority on day 0 | Existing payments, help and review |
|---|---|---|
| Scheduled review while the power continues | The separate recertification power survives under this rival's law. | Payment can remain exposed to the added procedure. |
| Expire the entire programme | Both the entitlement and the power would end under this rival. | All 20 payments lose their legal source in this case. |
| Expire only the recertification delegation | End the requirement in every channel on day 0. | All 20 payments remain due. Assign the three help requests and two challenges to named receivers. |
| Valid separate renewal Act in effect by day 0 | Only the powers in the exact enacted renewal may continue. | All otherwise due payments, live help and reviews retain their protection. |

In the selected case the benefit agency sets aside 12 hours of paid staff
time for forms, online and in-person changes, direct notices and accessible
handover. An independent parliamentary evidence budget buys six hours of
checking and support. Neither budget is a source for the 20 payments.
A further automated rule that applies the same expired requirement cannot
continue merely by changing labels. A later Act effective on day 5
permits new exercises only from day 5 within its terms, subject to any
further applicable rulemaking. A pending challenge to a prior reduction
is heard on its own merits. Any continued adverse step based on the
expired power can be challenged and corrected.

If another review needs six of the same parliamentary hours before day 0,
the 18 funded staff hours are already assigned. The Clerk discloses the
six-hour shortfall and seeks lawful paid cover and a recorded priority.
No earlier agenda date, underlying payment or legal sunset moves solely
because the combined workload cannot fit. See the
[comparison check](verify-public-power-renewal.py) for the dates, funding
and authority branches. Actual comparative workload, outcomes and rights
protection need independent evidence.
