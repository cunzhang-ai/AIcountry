# Design401. Make qualified product evidence portable

[DMR01–DMR08](../../country/C04/01/03-domestic-recognition-and-local-protection.md)
gives scoped product evidence common effect and keeps place-specific decisions
with accountable receiving authorities. Dated review and funded continuity
remain required when a restriction threatens an essential service.

The [worked case](../../settlement/domestic-recognition-and-local-protection/01-one-product-test-two-sites.md)
tests duplicate testing, a mismatched use, a failed site inspection, a batch hold
and backup payment and receipt. Actual verification capacity, costs and outcomes remain open.
