# Real repair and public control of experts

*TS11–TS12; part of [technical safety](../22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md).*

**TS11 — Repair must reach affected people and places.** B/V require a named
operator/public receiving owner, actual payer/assets, useful due time and verified
receipt. Replacement, refund, bodily compensation, safe accommodation, continuity
and ecological restoration are separate claims; a certificate, fine or closed
recall ticket pays none. Preserve B collective claims, independent evidence and
usable direct court access without waiting for a licensing inquiry to conclude.

TD's consequential operators need a prospective proportionate liability/closure
funding plan: actual insurance, secured resources or a lawfully funded public
self-insurance arrangement matching the relevant harm and maintenance/decommissioning
period. Publish exclusions, insolvency limits, counterpart claims and the lawful
payer for a shortfall. The Assembly must set any mandatory minimum or public
backstop using exposure evidence and real finance; there is no invented universal
insurance number, automatic unlimited Treasury guarantee or exemption from civil
liability because a hazard was insured. Until an annex-dependent requirement is
specified and usable, it supplies no invented permit/penalty condition.

Do not count one bond, collateral asset, repair worker or transport slot toward
several simultaneous duties. Small operators need usable pooled/public alternatives
where selected and funded, not an incumbent insurance cartel. U/M/B determine
insolvency, client/worker/victim claims and actual loss order. A compensation plan
is not compensation received; long-lived harm survives sale or license expiry.

**TS12 — Expertise operates within replaceable democratic authority.** Assembly
sets law, substantial risk choices, tax and appropriation; cabinet executes law.
C04/V10 qualified public appointment, disclosed interests, opposition scrutiny,
protected removal and predesignated conflict deputies govern sector decision and
assessment roles. Careers, law and source access survive a hostile cabinet change;
a minister cannot appoint a friendly replacement for one disputed case.

Separate operation/promotion, conformity assessment, license enforcement, independent
safety investigation and final punitive judgment in functions and material control.
Shared administration can save work only where source access, dissent, assignments,
funding and practical appeal remain independent. A state-owned operator receives
no weaker scrutiny or automatic certificate. A private board has no public veto.

Bounded industry fees go through lawful schedules and ordinary elected accounts;
fines cannot finance pursuit. Publish aggregate waits, access costs, unsafe failures,
false findings, corrected harm and remaining claims with safe source access for
opposition/courts. Protect private testimony, not an operator's monopoly on reasons.
Users/workers and excluded entrants can propose alternatives and challenge captures;
AI can assist interpretation/testing but cannot decide the final public mandate.

[NR10–NR12](../../C09/15/03-people-closure-security-and-continuing-claims.md)
now gives mining a positive phase-before-exposure closure/source requirement
regardless of title. Existing and proposed-phase exposure need actual lawful
security, never an already pledged asset or retroactive creditor preference.
Partial release needs independent performance/receiving; actual long-tail claims,
U/M/B rights and a named bounded public shortfall source persist. No universal
bond price, public guarantee or restored-land finding is supplied by this pointer.

[CLN](../../C09/21-industrial-cleanup-and-insolvency.md) assigns actual early
protection, a bounded prospective site charge, continuing liability and
accepted closure. TS/TD remain the competent technical route.
