## Diagnoses, values and genuine alternatives

Cash promises cannot create safe products/receiving. A shortage can arise from
resource mismatch, source concentration, unpaid skills, distribution, prices,
foreign payment or capture; more coordination/AI alone does not discriminate.
Physical conservation and actual rights constrain each choice. Adequate private
life, meaningful public control and not exporting harm are values; quantities,
prices, yields and outages here are authored hypotheses, not original observations.

- Retain regulated plural/import-heavy provision: dispersed initiative and less
  domestic land/work/capital; foreign route, price and payment dependence remains.
- Selective domestic mixed production plus paid imports: preserves local skills,
  outside options and some resilience; can use scarce water and public capital.
- More domestic irrigated/firm supply: can save land/work or reduce one import
  outage, but increase irrigation, fuel imports, pollution and common-source risk.
- Central self-sufficiency/production assignment: could concentrate purchasing
  and planning but threatens exit/private choice and cannot bypass resource limits.
  Reject its default mandate; do not average it into the selected plural model.

The selected architecture can lose locally: an import or retained private
operator may beat a public/domestic build under equal rights, climate/ecology,
continuity and paid transition. Public/common new core assets give a lasting
public network option; existing title/concessions are not converted for free.
