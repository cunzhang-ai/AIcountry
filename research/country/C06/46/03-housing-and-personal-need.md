# LC09–LC12: a home bill is not a personal ordinary basket

*Part of [C06/46](../46-life-needs-and-paid-capacity.md); design 123 reference.*

**LC09 — Keep structure, occupants, title and useful service separate.** The
retained 75,000-structure H reference is not 75,000 observed occupied households
or the same denominator as income-resource groups. Actual geography, condition,
occupancy and voluntary sharing decide dwelling needs. Do not force cohabitation,
count a vacant/inaccessible place as received housing or impute a cohabitant's
wallet. Public stock acquisition changes ownership and claims, not the number
of homes. New formation, safe repair and supported movement have real resource
and relationship burdens under H; no aggregate house count overrides a person.

**LC10 — Retain repair's costs and failing life/temporary-stock branches.**
The revised integer reference has 469 rebuilds/1,406 repairs,2,745 role-separated
posts and 40,322 new-input tonnes. Quarter starts with prior-year carryover need
322/322/323/323 temporary homes, not the old 293 average bound; the named quote is
180.318 m CU, including 323 annual temporary leases. Same-day returns precede starts;
late completion, accessible-location fit, safe repair life and actual receiving
remain unqualified. The retained separate 20-year repair-life stress is
285.49425 m/4,644 posts, not this integer schedule. These are partly private housing-
production allocations, not another G service invoice or actual vintage program.
Do not add them to old owner/landlord repair/reserve costs or care adaptations
without actual item/period overlap and payer reconciliation.

**LC11 — One shared bill, actual personal incidence and receipt.** Preserve
I13–24: ordinary personal B, housing, work/care/access and essential replacement
are separate component needs. In a weekly fictional example B100/Q100 received
and lawful ordinary credit leaves 20 ordinary cash need. One home bill 200 shared
by A120/B80 is allocated once. A's promised but unreceived 50 support leaves 120
housing need and 140 total N; actual same-bill receipt 50 leaves 70 housing and 90 N.
This is N before the existing I05 resource/disregard/taper rules, not an automatic
140/90 grant. Share allocation is a declared lawful expense split, not authority
to allocate liabilities or resources between actual cohabitants. S/MO/HN public
spending is not automatically ordinary Q; neither are booked future payments.

**LC12 — Match the personal and provider sides without a second source.** A
public rent/utility/provider payment needs an actual due bill, correct person/
period, legal source and received avoidance before need is reduced. Credit the
same payment once and retain the landlord/provider's actual collection/costs,
private title, deposits and creditor/worker claims. Cost rent can exceed personal
affordability; H/I assistance requires actual source. Public funding then tax
return is a gross-cash sequence, not independent productive source. Keep ordinary
cash choice, actual useful time, private life and replacement needs visible;
a household external benchmark cannot be pasted into personal B plus its already
included rent/care components as another bill.
