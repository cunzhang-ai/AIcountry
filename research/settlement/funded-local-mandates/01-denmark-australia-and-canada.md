# Existing fiscal arrangements separate different obligations

*Design200 · Scoped evidence for the [national and local decision](README.md).*

## Denmark. A budget correction and a separately distributed grant

The ministry's 30 June 2025 notice for 2026 reports about 82.3 billion
DKK in municipal block grants and about 26.5 billion redistributed
among municipalities through general equalization. The latter is
redistribution, not a second net national receipt. The ministry
describes separate equalization for the tax base and spending need,
with intermunicipal contributions balancing transfers under the general
system. Actual receipts and individual service outcomes are outside
the notification. [2026 notice](https://www.skim.dk/nyheder/2025/juni/tilskudsudmeldingen-for-2026-er-nu-sendt-til-kommuner-og-regioner)
· [Equalization description](https://www.skim.dk/indenrigs-ressortomlagt/kommunal-og-regionaloekonomi/tilskud-og-udligning/tilskud-og-udligning-for-kommunerne)

The published 2026 block-grant distribution shows the following figures
in **thousand DKK**. Use the ordinary and conditional components of this
table only, before other grants, contributions or service expenditures.

| Locality | Ordinary grant | Conditional service line | Other conditional line | Published block-grant total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Whole country | 78,347,304 | 3,000,000 | 999,996 | 82,347,300 |
| Copenhagen | 8,754,128 | 352,748 | 117,592 | 9,224,468 |
| Lolland | 505,416 | 19,356 | 6,456 | 531,228 |

The conditional portions total 470,340 for Copenhagen and 25,812
for Lolland at the table's precision. These are components of a
reported grant distribution. They are neither a deduction actually
imposed nor a measure of local service needs. National totals are
shown once. The schedule has other tables with different boundaries.
[Official block-grant table 3](https://admin.ism.dk/Media/639039931769753840/Kommunerne_2026.pdf)

The national expanded total-balance procedure adjusts grants for
changed duties. A line for a proposed high-level cybersecurity law in
the 2026 law and circular programme shows 154.7 million DKK for 2026
and the two shown later estimate years. It is a separate budget-
adjustment line within that schedule. It does not identify a local
staff roster, show national net financing for that law, or add 154.7
million again to the published distribution.
[Government rulemaking guidance](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/retsinfo/2016/9150)
· [Official budget act document and annex](https://admin.ism.dk/Media/639039931769753840/Kommunerne_2026.pdf)

The ministry's budget-control account describes a 40% population-based
and 60% overspender-based split for a reduction where municipalities
collectively exceed spending limits in their accounts. This is a
published rule, with no estimate of reductions imposed for 2026 here.
It illustrates why an allocated amount and its conditions must be
read together. The proposed country retains its own locally
accountable common-floor boundary. [Ministry budget control](https://www.skim.dk/indenrigs-ressortomlagt/kommunal-og-regionaloekonomi/budgetsamarbejde/budgetlov-og-sanktionsregler)

## Australia and Canada. Revenue capacity and service needs are choices

Australia's 2025 Commonwealth Grants Commission review retains a
comparison of relative revenue-raising capacity and relative costs
of comparable services. It notes the limitations of an average policy
dominated by one state and the need for explainable tradeoffs between
policy neutrality, practicality and contemporaneity. This is a method
for allocating the GST pool within Australian law. It does not identify
the cost of a new locally assigned national floor.
[Commission methodology review](https://www.cgc.gov.au/sites/default/files/2025-03/2025R%20-%20Review%20outcomes%20-%20HFE_FINAL.pdf)

Canada's federal equalization description uses revenues that provinces
could raise at national average rates. It excludes provincial actual
spending decisions and fiscal results from that allocation and applies
distinct natural-resource and smoothing rules. A representative revenue
method limits direct grant rewards for undercollection. By itself it
does not measure where a new service is harder to provide.
[Finance Canada equalization description](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/programs/federal-transfers/equalization.html)

These records support the distinction among costs, representative
capacity, published transfers, conditions and cash receipt. They do
not measure the causal effect of the proposed architecture, prove
an alternative's inferiority, or calibrate the country's benchmarks.
