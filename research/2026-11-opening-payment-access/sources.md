# Design501 sources and scope

- The [Financial Stability Board's 2024 Key Attributes](https://www.fsb.org/2024/04/key-attributes-of-effective-resolution-regimes-for-financial-institutions-revised-version-2024/)
  and [operational continuity guidance](https://www.fsb.org/2024/03/fsb-guidance-on-arrangements-to-support-operational-continuity-in-resolution-revised-version-2024/)
  identify critical functions, shared operational dependencies and
  orderly resolution. They do not enact domestic powers or establish a
  facility, eligible borrower, receiving bank or funded payout.
- The [Bank of England's 2024 major-bank resolvability assessment](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/financial-stability/resolution/resolvability-assessment-framework/resovability-assessment-of-major-uk-banks-2024)
  identifies dated funding and liquidity needs in resolution. Its
  assessment cannot qualify bank or public operating capacity for the
  candidate.
- The [pinned opening currency analysis](../2026-11-monetary-commencement/decision-record.md),
  [one-month comparison](../settlement/bank-and-currency-stress/01-one-month-under-three-regimes.md)
  and [bank-failure exposure probes](../settlement/bank-failure/README.md)
  supply earlier distinct cases. Their balances, legal limits, receivers
  and dates are not carried into this new authored branch.
- The [retail continuity source record](../2026-11-opening-resilience-and-public-choice/sources.md)
  distinguishes card and merchant contingencies from actual public
  payout and accepted essential services. No observation implies that
  the Design501 couriers, banks or counterparties have accepted work.
