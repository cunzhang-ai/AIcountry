# Country design: chapter map

Integrated design 79 · Proposed research, not enacted law

The [integrated country design](../COUNTRY-DESIGN.md) explains how the
institutions fit together. The [authority table](AUTHORITY.md) states who makes each kind of decision. The [four system maps](systems/README.md) organize
cross-chapter operating questions by public purpose. Each C-number below is a stable chapter entrance;
its numbered topic files contain the canonical detail. Use the [status
register](../DESIGN-STATUS.md) for current choices and unresolved conflicts,
and the [four-part functional coverage audit](../COVERAGE-AUDIT.md) to find first-order
design gaps. Historical editions are not current chapters.

For example, routine prevention belongs in
[C07/08](C07/08-population-health-and-prevention.md), its financing in
[C06/09](C06/09-prevention-and-health-security-finance.md), coercive emergency
limits in [C10/01](C10/01-public-safety-and-emergencies.md), and the linked
failure exercise in [C13/12](C13/12-health-signal-and-community-trust.md).
The [system map](systems/02-livelihoods-and-services.md) connects those
separate owners without repeating their full rules.

The [cross-chapter operating map](INTEGRATION.md) follows shared authority,
resources and remedies. A new consequential proposal belongs in the affected
numbered topic, with its chapter entrance updated only if the navigation
changes. Update the integrated design and status together for a changed
working selection; preserve a predecessor and decision record. Relocation or
indexing alone grants no legal authority or new research procedure.

The [document-organization history](../history/05-document-organization.md)
explains earlier splits and links their preserved predecessors. Actual
ratification, funding, staffed delivery and observed results remain separate.
