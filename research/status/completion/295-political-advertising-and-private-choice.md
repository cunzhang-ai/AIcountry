# Design387. Protect private choice and inspect actual paid electoral distribution

[PC25–PC31](../../country/C03/12/06-political-advertising-and-private-choice.md)
keeps contextual paid speech, binds personal targeting and makes
materially different delivered versions inspectable. It preserves
private voter choice, editorial independence and narrow remedies.

[The worked contest and primary records](../../settlement/political-advertising-and-private-choice/01-three-messages-and-two-voting-windows.md)
test actual delivery, original evidence, independent correction and
distinct voting windows. Actual adoption, safe access and effects remain open.
