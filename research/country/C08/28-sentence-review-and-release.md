# C08. Sentence review, release and reintegration

9 November 2026 · Design 190 · Proposed RL01–RL15

A finite sentence sets the outer limit of custody. This proposal makes
continued confinement after the halfway point depend on a fresh judicial
finding of necessity. It gives people a release date they can enforce and
requires public services to prepare for their return. Existing P16–P20 and
PO13–PO16 establish the sentence, custody and receiving framework.

| Canonical part | Decisions |
|---|---|
| [Halfway review and the decision to release](28/01-review-and-release.md) | RL01–RL05 define credited time, automatic review, state proof and the effect of a missed deadline |
| [Conditions, expiry and judicial recall](28/02-conditions-and-recall.md) | RL06–RL10 define narrow conditions, a fixed recall endpoint, adjudication and urgent protection |
| [Receiving, safeguards and adoption](28/03-reintegration-and-continuity.md) | RL11–RL15 define paid support, victim protection, accountable actors, transition and alternatives |

The selected halfway presumption and finite recall window are contestable
design choices. Their safety, unequal effects and delivery costs require
qualified review and affected participation. The country account includes
worked cases and comparisons with automatic and discretionary release.
