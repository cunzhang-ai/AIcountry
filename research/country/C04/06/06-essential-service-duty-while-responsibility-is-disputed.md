# AP13–AP18. Keep essential services operating while public bodies dispute responsibility

*Design382 · Proposed ordinary law within [reachable administration](../06-reachable-administration-and-finite-decisions.md).*

**AP13 — Keep a responsibility dispute separate from eligibility and capacity.**
At protected first receipt, preserve the person's account, consent, existing
awards and earliest applicable deadline. For a material threat to an essential
service involving more than one potentially responsible public body, distinguish
a dispute about the payer or delivery owner, a genuine unresolved eligibility
or professional question, unavailable supply and unlawful refusal. Continue
established duties and undisputed components on their own authority. Give an
accessible contact and immediate action on any independent urgent grounds.
Neither a disagreement between agencies nor an unanswered referral makes
the resident ineligible or completes the public duty.

**AP14 — Assign an interim owner before the dispute.** For each covered
interface the implementing law must name the incumbent's continuing duties,
the local career office responsible for securing the interim route, the
qualified sector authorities and the funded national fallback. Use the
incumbent for its lawful continuing scope. For an unowned covered interface,
use the office named for the place of actual receiving, subject to a stronger
source rule. If it cannot act in useful time, the named existing national
sector authority takes the authorized interim action and records what failed.
A cross-sector schedule names which authority initiates the fallback for the
specified interface and which others must answer within their own duties.
The first receiver stays reachable until a competent actor accepts.
Use the same rule when both bodies deny ownership. Interim assignment does
not settle ordinary residence, final liability, clinical competence or consent.
No generic lead acquires a power to order treatment, grant a benefit outside
its law or substitute for the resident's chosen decision-maker.

**AP15 — Pay for safe action before settling the bill.** Under an enacted
national sector or joint continuity schedule, the named officer authorizes the
narrowest safe available qualified protection and the separate lawful
payer releases funds before a payer or assignment dispute is resolved. The schedule identifies covered interfaces,
evidence, eligible costs, provider, payer, period, gross cap, recurring
exposure and outside challenge. Use settled cash, actual acceptance and
qualified staff before counting coverage. Preserve choice, established pay
and existing charges under source law. A public accounting dispute cannot
make a resident advance the agencies' share or provide unpaid help.
Coordinate XR advances and E or sector relief by actual service and period
without paying the same cost twice. Where eligibility itself is genuinely
unresolved, use only separately lawful provisional or urgent grounds.
Neither an appropriation nor this assignment creates a merits presumption.

**AP16 — Resolve the durable duty and reimbursement in parallel.** Send
each disputed authority a reasoned, time-stamped statement of the acts,
records, payer questions and protected deadlines. Obtain a named decision
and remedy route from each competent body. If they cannot agree before the
useful deadline, preserve service under existing lawful interim authority
and make the scope or omission directly challengeable. The applicable
court or independent source forum decides disputed authority, illegality or
cost liability within its jurisdiction. Apply the ordinary or shorter
source decision and reply clocks. The resident can challenge their
entitlement or unsafe interim route without exhausting an interagency
dispute. Reconcile only authorized, delivered costs once under DB and
applicable MF or sector law. An expected reimbursement is no usable cash.

**AP17 — Verify delivery and the next duty.** Confirm qualified personnel,
safe receiving, consent where required, payment and first actual receipt.
Record a separate receiving date for each dependent task. A temporary
arrangement ends under its terms, with a lawful and accepted successor
or an explicit unresolved shortfall. Prior duties, accrued claims and
independent remedies survive. Check overlapping cases, common payers,
provider or staff loss and the next due call without using the same
capacity twice. Review before any finite protection ends and earlier
when a missed act would harm the person. Where no funded and lawful
route can deliver, report and seek immediate competent relief and an
elected source decision. A priority rule must come from its own law.

**AP18 — Keep independent scrutiny and minimal records.** Provide direct
accessible challenge outside the disputed bodies and track original
contact, actual delivered help, unresolved cases, recurring disputes and
paid capacity. Give each competent receiver only necessary lawful facts.
AI may identify unanswered handoffs, conflicting dates and duplicate
costs. It cannot assign legal liability, determine clinical suitability,
infer consent or close the case from a referral. Adopt prospectively
with funded duty owners, existing claims and review dates carried
forward. Reopen if interim responsibility becomes permanent by delay,
providers are repeatedly unfunded, handoffs exclude people or a simpler
lawfully integrated service offers equal protection and accountability.
