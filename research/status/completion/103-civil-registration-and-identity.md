# Completion finding. Civil proof and its correction now reach a named receiver

Design195 specifies who registers a life event, issues free usable proof
and corrects wrong or missing records. A limited proof is different
from a status decision. An affected agency must verify that its own
service or payment has been restored. [Canonical rules](../../country/C08/29-civil-registration-and-identity.md)

UNICEF estimates distinguish missing registration from missing proof.
A scoped public audit shows why correcting a death entry can leave
payments unrepaired. The chosen architecture and authored cases establish
no actual staffing, receipt or public approval.
