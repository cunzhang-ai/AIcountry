## Values, diagnoses and real alternatives

Equal concern for people abroad, meaningful organizing/claim access and domestic
public control are values. Jurisdiction, actual foreign authority and available
work/source are constraints. Buyer terms influencing production harm is the
mechanism hypothesis; no local causal effect or net benefit is observed.
The problem may instead chiefly be supplier coercion, worker power, concentrated
ownership or weak local institutions. A buyer checklist alone cannot settle it.

| Whole choice | Mechanism / possible benefit | Burdens, opposition and failure |
|---|---|---|
| Retain/repair territorial B/employer/product law, SC terms and negotiated trade/worker compacts | Fewer general duties; collective agreements can create actual worker power and remedy | Independent private buying may lack a specific preventive obligation; negotiated foreign acceptance is not assured |
| Selected BC own-conduct and reasonable linked-risk duty | Changes actual price/time/order/payment behavior, gives affected people a direct claim and public civil response | Evidence/court workload, weak buyer influence, price increases, supplier exclusion and capture may outweigh protection; causal distinction can be litigated |
| Strict whole-chain guarantee and broad import exclusion | Simpler claimant recovery and stronger purchasing leverage | Blanket exit may strand wages, conceal sourcing and exclude poorer producers; national blacklists can become protectionism; goods/FX and public insurance sources still needed |
| Worker-led binding agreements, direct public/cooperative sourcing or ownership change | Redistribution of bargaining/control, enforceable collective obligations, fewer exploitative intermediaries | Needs genuine mandates, viable alternative producers, source and practical independent worker freedom; public buying itself can abuse power |

Choose BC provisionally as a bounded additional civil floor, with actual
reasonable action and causal defenses. Do not subsume rivals as decorative
modules. A worker-led/ownership alternative can replace it. BC's specified grave
floor is narrower than UN guidance's broad human-rights responsibility: this is
not full implementation of that guidance or proof of sufficient protection.
Actual broader applicable rights remain; omitted cumulative/non-grave harms
are a material objection requiring review, not proof they do not matter.

grounds. No general court licence over every risky purchase. Existing WL and
designated qualified C04 environmental officers gain express civil application
standing/functions; nonemployee coercion/trafficking/child exploitation has an
assigned route. No overseas raids, officer-final fines, new VD crime, blanket
import ban, household licence or universal supplier identity graph.

case officers retain statutory action and protected source, with direct ordinary
courts/conflict routing and no minister's named-case veto. Public budgets,
managerial pressure and selective evidence remain capture routes. Opposition,
worker organization, actual evidence access, AA public-buying audit and direct
claims matter together; none is demonstrated independent merely by being named.
AI may assist safe translation/source comparison, never confer jurisdiction,
prove consent or decide which foreign people can be sacrificed for local savings.
