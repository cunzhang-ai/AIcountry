# An income shift can weaken two budgets at once

Design326 · Authored comparison for [TN21–TN25](../../country/C06/59/06-income-shift-and-public-finance.md)

Hold service and earned-benefit law fixed. The original payable public
package has general duties of 100 and earned payments of 20. After the
income shift, authorized income support increases general duties to 106.
Amounts below are illustrative units for matched periods. The change
could reflect technology, demand or remuneration structure. No causal
diagnosis, tax rate or national forecast is inferred.

| Net collected source or payable use | Original | After shift |
|---|---:|---:|
| General tax receipts linked to labor income | 50 | 40 |
| General company profit tax receipts | 20 | 24 |
| Other general receipts | 30 | 30 |
| Total general receipts before backing | 100 | 94 |
| Restricted contributions | 20 | 16 |
| General duties before backing | 100 | 106 |
| Earned payments | 20 | 20 |

There are two deficits: 12 in the general account and 4 in the
restricted account. An authorized general transfer of 4 pays the
earned-benefit shortfall, leaving the general account short 16.
On consolidation, total external receipts are 110 and total final
duties are 126. The internal transfer adds no national revenue.

| Alternative | Effect on the same budget |
|---|---|
| Retain sources with a separately funded 16-unit one-period general reserve | Covers both original deadlines if the reserve is usable. Leaves a recurring gap of 16 absent another change. |
| Reform ordinary tax bases for realized owner income with a recurring net 12 and retain existing ordinary sources | General receipts become 106. The 4 backing transfer leaves a continuing general deficit of 4. |
| The same net 12 plus a separately qualified recurring source of 4 | Covers the stipulated recurring uses. The additional source and who bears it require an actual elected choice. |

The reform row assumes 15 of incremental gross personal collections,
less 3 of refunds. The illustrative personal base concerns post-company-tax
distributions and realized personal gains under their own rules. Corporate
24 remains its own receipt. Test actual behavioral and timing effects
before relying on either layer. If the gross 15 arrives after the
first due period while 3 of refunds are already payable, the first
period's combined financing gap is 19. A 16-unit reserve then leaves
3 uncovered even when the later annual plan balances. If the original
company gain of 4 also fails to collect, the structural gap after the
net 12 increases from 4 to 8.

The baseline selects the finite reserve if legally available for the
first gap, evaluates the ordinary realized-income reform, and leaves
the residual recurring choice explicitly open. A legal transfer is a
payment between accounts, while a reserve draw is use of a past source.
Neither should be counted as new recurring tax. Verify actual money
and receipt, care workforce and resident eligibility separately.

bound the comparison. The amounts show accounting and timing effects,
not empirical tax yield or a proof of distributive superiority.
