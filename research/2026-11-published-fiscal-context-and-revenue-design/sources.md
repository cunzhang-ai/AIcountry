# Original sources and bounded interpretation

Retrieved3 November2026. Frozen metadata, exact requests and JSONSTAT originals
for SKAT/OFF3/OFF29/OFF10 are in `original-sources/`.

- [StatBank metadata endpoint](https://api.statbank.dk/v1/tableinfo/SKAT?lang=en&format=JSON), same endpoint for other tables.
- [Numeric data endpoint](https://api.statbank.dk/v1/data): exact POST bodies saved as `*-query.json`; all non-time categories retained, OFF3 sector TOTAL, years2021–25.
- Metadata vintage3 June2026,08:00; SKAT unitDKK1,000, othersmDKK. Conversion only to billionDKK, retaining original labels, status, rounding and negative net entries.

[Taxes and duties](https://www.dst.dk/en/Statistik/emner/oekonomi/offentlig-oekonomi/skatter-og-afgifter)
provides tax-type context and update date. Administrative assessments/accounts
are revised; these are not candidate tax bases or yields.

[Government finance statistical presentation](https://www.dst.dk/en/Statistik/dokumentation/documentationofstatistics/government-finance-statistics/statistical-presentation)
identifies general-government and national-accounts scopes. Imputed contributions
and production entries are not equivalent to owned cash. Tax types, purposes
and economic transactions answer different questions.

[Accuracy and reliability](https://www.dst.dk/en/Statistik/dokumentation/documentationofstatistics/government-finance-statistics/accuracy-and-reliability)
identifies estimation, classification and revision limits. Absence of sampling
error is not proof of accuracy or a matching source-law bridge.

[Matching3 June2026 release](https://www.dst.dk/da/Statistik/udgivelser/NytHtml?cid=52178)
marks2024/25 provisional and supplies fiscal/purpose/transfer context.
[Later26 October2026 EDP notice](https://www.dst.dk/nyt/54370)
reports rounded2025 balance90.2b. Retain it separately from June OFF3's90.924b;
no EDP1 raw series was retrieved or silently substituted.

These share an official administrative-account evidence family, not four
independent confirmations of a social model. Research/arithmetic are same-
controller AI analysis, with no actual affected participation or public mandate.
SKAT minus selected OFF3 taxes/social contributions approximately matches the
customs cell, but inspected documents do not verify the exact legal bridge.
Keep inference/residual separate from proved institutional ownership.

No household microdata, reform elasticities, detailed private-plan source law,
matched candidate cost/source, authority or actual delivered outcomes obtained.
