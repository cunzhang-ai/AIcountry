The bounded question is the working national design's **default monetary
architecture**, not a claim that one system fits every territory. Design 17
kept external currency because an earlier scenario prudently assumed no
issuing power. That was a useful stress premise but not a demonstrated reason
to make it the default future-country design. It joined domestically available
labor and services to a potentially scarce external means of payment while
leaving monetary authority, inflation and exchange risk outside the main
institutional choice. The revision must improve design completeness without
inventing real acceptance of a new currency.

**Values:** equal political control over distributive choices, credible payment
and social claims, protection against inflation and favoritism, and honest
burden-sharing with outsiders. **Constraints:** existing legal denominations,
creditor rights, available workers and goods, import and foreign-debt payments,
constitutional democracy and finite operational capacity. **Empirical claims
still untested here:** that people would use the new unit, that monetary and
fiscal institutions can resist capture, that inflation stays manageable, and
that either package gives better service access. **Design hypothesis:** a
domestic settlement unit with a bounded, publicly accountable issuer can make
domestic payment continuity more governable without giving officials free real
resources or unlimited authority. Confidence and actual production could
defeat that mechanism.

## Coherent alternatives on the same unknown starting conditions

No actual population, balance sheet, territory, exchange reserves or inherited
law exists for this comparison. Each option therefore carries the same unknown
opening deposits, debts, import needs, social duties and productive capacity.

| Package | Power and expected mechanism | Decisive loss or failure |
|---|---|---|
| Retain design 17's conditional external currency as the sole reference | Prevents assumed domestic issuance; demands usable external cash, correspondent access, supervised banks, pre-funded resolution and disciplined fiscal choices. | Domestic payroll and benefits can fail when external liquidity disappears even if local labor and supplies exist. A state cannot supply its own external lender-of-last-resort currency. Its finite reserves and first-hour chain remain useful as a stress branch. |
| **Selected conditional domestic unit with a public monetary authority** | Elected law defines mandate, appointments, limits and payment finality; the authority operates domestic settlement and bounded liquidity for eligible solvent banks; separate resolution absorbs bank failure. Treasury finance needs its own authorization. | Issuance can feed inflation, depreciation, capital flight or political credit; it cannot create imported medicines, energy, qualified staff or foreign exchange. Weak institutions or deep currency substitution may make the unit unusable. |
| Currency board or hard peg with a domestic legal unit | Clear conversion rule may help confidence if reserves and lawful governance are strong; private domestic payments use a national unit. | Credibility demands sufficient accessible reserves and strict redemption. A promise beyond actual cover can force crisis rationing. Peg defense can subordinate domestic employment and service access to reserve preservation. |
| Immediate public monopoly over all deposits and credit | Could simplify payment integration and direct funds to public goals. | Concentrates allocation, information and opposition funding under a state controller; a bank failure becomes a fiscal failure and private alternatives disappear. No evidence warrants this concentration. |

Select the domestic package as a **research reference**, because a future
constitutional democracy should explicitly design the monetary power it might
use and test its domestic and external limits together. It is not preferred
unconditionally over external currency. The older external package is retained
as a complete choice if an adopting public finds a new unit implausible or
unlawful, and as a combined bank/correspondent-loss stress test. A currency
board needs its own reserve and legal proof; it is not silently blended with a
managed float. More monetary discretion is not itself social progress.

## Authority, crisis and democratic-control chain

The proposed constitutional rank protects the people's control of law, tax,
budget, appointments, opposition and remedies; the currency's name, policy
instruments, ordinary targets and bank rules are mutable statutes. The Assembly
enacts a central-bank law with price stability and employment support, publishes
the objective hierarchy and changes it prospectively. Public qualifications,
staggered terms, open hearings and recorded cross-party scrutiny precede
Assembly confirmation; dismissal is for stated cause with independent review.
The authority has instrument autonomy inside the law, audited accounts and
reasoned public decisions. C11 preserves source statistics separately from
the issuer's policy interpretation. Opposition, affected people, auditors and
courts can challenge unlawful conduct, hidden transactions and appointments.
Courts cannot select the policy rate merely because they prefer a forecast.

Domestic settlement is legally final under enacted rules. A taxpayer who
lawfully pays through an authorized intermediary is not taxed twice because
the intermediary later fails. Accessible cash, assisted and alternate payment
channels must be tested; one vendor cannot decide which residents can pay tax
or receive benefits. Ordinary banks remain plural and supervised. A bank
meeting statutory solvency criteria may receive time-limited, priced,
collateralized emergency liquidity within an exposure ceiling. Supervisory
evidence and the liquidity decision remain distinct. If solvency is not yet
established, this facility is withheld pending prompt assessment; only a bank
found nonviable enters resolution. Their controllers,
data and disagreements are disclosed to competent review. A nonviable bank
enters separate resolution under the enacted loss order, deposit coverage and
fund. Owners and creditors face prior losses; covered depositors receive only
authorized protection. Any fiscal indemnity, extraordinary recapitalization
or guarantee beyond the enacted ceiling needs Assembly approval. A resolution
official or AI model cannot convert a bank loan into a hidden public grant.

Treasury cannot run an automatic central-bank overdraft. A temporary direct
credit facility, if allowed at all, needs a prior law setting purpose, ceiling,
term, price, loss bearer and disclosure. Neither a cabinet request nor a
central-bank emergency label changes tax, appropriation, social entitlement or
debt limits. If Treasury lacks lawful finance, it reports due claims and
arrears under A6 while cabinet submits actual options to the Assembly. If
the Assembly delays, existing legal duties and available lawful payments
continue; an expired bridge does not authorize money creation or cancel
claims. If the issuer cannot operate or is captured, lawful alternate
payment custody and court/audit access protect records and claims, but no
fallback is presumed capable of fixing inflation or foreign shortage.

The reference uses a managed float, with **no guaranteed foreign conversion
rate**. FX intervention uses available uncommitted reserves under published
purposes and limits. Foreign-currency contracts and import bills keep their
denomination until lawfully changed. The shared C06 account distinguishes
domestic liquidity, inflation exposure, reserve drains, due foreign currency,
actual imported goods, labor and ecological capacity. It reports who bears
depreciation, price rises, interest changes and payment delay, including
residents with fixed incomes and firms dependent on imports. Exchange or
capital restrictions need time-bounded legislation, rights review and
distributional disclosure. No issuing institution receives a hidden power
to ration speech, opposition finance or exit.

The [IMF central-bank transparency principles](https://www.imf.org/en/data/cbt/principles)
support clear mandates, autonomy, accounts, government relationships and
emergency-liquidity disclosure; they do not choose this project's objectives.
The [BIS study of emergency liquidity solvency](https://www.bis.org/publications/fsi-brief-29-solvency-requirement-emergency-liquidity-support)
explains why viability and resolution judgments must be coordinated without
equating liquidity with solvency. The [FSB 2024 resolution attributes](https://www.fsb.org/2024/04/key-attributes-of-effective-resolution-regimes-for-financial-institutions-revised-version-2024/)
inform continuity and creditor-loss design without promising a funded receiver.
The [IMF lender-of-last-resort study](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/001/2016/010/article-A001-en.xml)
explains the finite foreign-currency liquidity constraint, and its
[exchange-intervention analysis](https://www.imf.org/en/blogs/articles/2024/10/10/when-foreign-exchange-intervention-can-best-help-countries-navigate-shocks)
describes tradeoffs under FX shocks. These are separate evidence families;
none contains original observations about a real AIcountry.

## Compatibility, obligations, transition and objections

The change affects C06's fiscal/payment design, C07 service delivery, C09
imports and physical systems, C11 evidence, C12 automated finance, C13
external relations, C14 founding, proposed A5/A6 and F2. The design-17
bank-loss, tax-finality, Treasury shortfall and democratic-bailout rules are
retained across both monetary packages. The old external-currency selection
is displaced as the default only; its operational requirements and creditor
warnings remain available. No existing review is treated as approving the
changed proposal. The earlier three-year scenario still assumes its own
external financing and cannot validate design 18 or be relabeled as its cost.

A real transition must inventory the lawful opening unit, deposits, loans,
wages, benefits, taxes, judgments, guarantees, public debt, foreign debt and
supplier contracts by denomination, maturity, owner and governing law.
Neither the research choice nor a new flag converts or cancels them. A lawful
conversion, if proposed, needs creditor and resident participation, individual
notice, non-discrimination, compensation/remedy rules, court review and
cross-border agreements where needed. The opening C06 account must cost
central-bank capital and staff, settlement infrastructure, cash access,
supervision, resolution, deposit protection, Treasury custody, independent
audit, import reserves and interim dual-system operation. The same reserves
cannot underwrite a peg, bank guarantees and medicine imports at once.

Material objections remain: central-bank appointments may be captured by
the parliamentary majority; operational autonomy can conceal distributive
rate and credit choices; a legislative override can be abused; a statutory
credit ceiling may be too rigid in a crisis; currency confidence may fail;
and nominal local payments may buy less food or shelter after depreciation.
The external package might better protect purchasing power in a particular
context, especially if it is already deeply used and reliably accessible.
The selected package might make people worse off. An affected public must
compare those possibilities, including dissent that cannot be averaged away.

**Evidence qualification:** conceptual comparison of cited primary sources
and local predecessor texts, with no original jurisdictional data or affected-
party testimony. **Research selection:** conditional domestic-currency
reference and complete external alternative. **Legal authorization:** none.
**Operational readiness:** none shown for currency acceptance, settlement,
bank resolution, foreign exchange or staff. **Observed outcome:** none.

The next bounded question is whether a proposed adopting jurisdiction can
pay one month of legally owed domestic claims and essential imports after a
bank run and exchange shock, under *each* complete package, without inventing
credit, reserves or staff. A public finance team, bank supervisor, deposit
representatives, service providers, importers, opposition and independent
reviewers would need an audited opening balance sheet, contracts, settlement
routes and lawful mandate. The deliverable is a comparable due-date account
of payments completed, arrears, inflation/FX incidence, affected rights and
remedies, plus recorded dissent; no such field budget or authority exists in
this project. Reopen design 18 if actual confidence, foreign exposures,
institutional capture, material objection or an operating failure favors the
external package, a currency board or another coherent system. Until then,
neither choice may be reported as adopted or proven superior.
