# TS05–TS08. Negotiation without manufactured consent

*Design 143 · [Territorial-status reference](../02-territorial-self-government.md) · Proposed duties, not an actual negotiation mandate.*

**TS05 — A qualified demand starts work.** Qualification K obliges a funded public
Assembly hearing and formal negotiation beginning no later than K+60 calendar days.
No separate first ballot asks permission to talk: the petition authorizes only
this process. Publish accessible options and objections by K+180 calendar days;
by K+365 publish either an exact proposed settlement or a reasoned disagreement
and performance report. There is **one extension of at most 180 calendar days**,
requiring an actual majority of the full Assembly before K+365, public necessary-
work/source reasons and an affected-area hearing. Cabinet, mediator or software
cannot extend it. Renaming a file, changing facilitators or merging discussions
cannot restart the clock. At the outer date record what occurred; no agreement,
consent, boundary or sovereignty arises from timeout. Courts enforce owed acts,
not compelled political agreement. No valid national A9 initiative is held hostage
to completion of these talks; existing national initiation rights remain usable.

**TS06 — Who speaks and who can bind.** Cabinet negotiators act under a public,
bounded Assembly mandate. Initiators, elected local councils and actual collective
rights-holders select their own accountable representatives; opposition and people
who reject the dominant movement receive usable access and independent advice.
Workers, service users, enclaves, neighboring authorities and the national public
carrying common obligations must be heard without pretending their distinct rights
are one electorate. Representation is not itself power to bind a person, collective,
Assembly or final voter beyond an actual mandate. Permit competing lawful proposals;
sharing compatible support work creates no first-arrival monopoly or blanket ban
on later peaceful claims. Existing enforcement and urgent remedies do not wait.

**TS07 — Replaceable facilitation and plural communication.** Facilitation is optional,
jointly selected and replaceable. Failure to agree on a facilitator cannot prevent
direct lawful meetings, documented exchanges or publication of disagreement.
Participants can contest framing, translation, agenda, maps and summaries and retain
material meanings that do not fit a standard form. AI may translate, compare options
and expose assumptions under bounded instructions; it cannot classify a people,
select its representatives, certify consent or impose one shared interpretation.
Simulations, shared situations and evolving commitments may support understanding;
legal assent still requires an authorized act with inspectable scope. Keep public
reasons and dissent with safe correction history, without exposing sensitive sources.

**TS08 — Resources and real alternatives.** Through existing OP/T and enacted budgets,
name actual payer, qualified staff/time, independent advice, accessible offline and
other-provider routes, records, translation, travel, security, court and campaign
capacity, accepted receiving operators and successor obligations. Separate actors
whose independence is necessary; no single vendor or incumbent controls intake,
map, money, information and final review. Existing 37 function bids are not prices
for this workload; missing resources remain unknown/unmet. Unlawful refusal is
recorded and challengeable, while a missing resource is not conjured by an order.
If review or execution also fails, preserve claims, expose the failed-control chain
and seek lawful elected correction; no invented substitute sovereign appears.

Options include retaining boundaries with enforced rights, nonterritorial guarantees,
shared services, elected territorial autonomy and the requested status. Each states
losses, dependencies and feasible continuing duties. More redesign is not a measure
of progress. Genuine disagreement can be a lawful result; refusals to receive,
hear, disclose or execute existing protections cannot be renamed disagreement.
