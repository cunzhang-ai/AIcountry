# Design271 decision record

**Baseline and choice.** Design270 shows why qualified regional
backup can matter when a local award would miss a deadline. JC
already preserves protected duties when a shared route fails. The
new decision concerns two localities claiming the same callable
public resource in an urgent interval. Use the
[working method](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision handoff](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).
The proposal applies to expressly adopted domestic emergency scopes.

**Diagnosis and support.** A request can remain unfilled because
qualified teams do not exist, the ones that do exist cannot reach
the site in time, a command chain cannot release them, or choices
conflict. Delayed reporting or an inaccessible dispatch route can
hide need. US wildfire records show a delegated allocation function
and substantial unfilled national crew orders. These records do not
attribute a missed deployment to any specific diagnosis in our
design. [Observed scope](sources.md)

**Alternatives and choice.** Compare ordinary bilateral aid, a paid
local floor with bounded national matching and an independent second
route, and stronger permanently distributed capacity. For the
authored calls, choose the bounded national match and the second
shift. Early joint matching uses the same first two units. The
second shift needs a third unit from the identified unbound activity.
A local-only team or earlier prepositioning can beat shared dispatch
if distance makes aid consistently late. Prevention can change the
number and timing of the calls. Their actual costs and benefit
remain open. [Authored budget](../settlement/domestic-mutual-aid/02-authored-dispatch-and-second-call.md)

**Democratic control and objections.** The Assembly sets exact scope,
sources, prior priority, floor and expiration. Responsible public
employers roster qualified dispatcher and successor under existing
appointment and removal protections. Local incident commanders
supply field evidence and decide safe receiving. Independent review
requires distinct paid contact, records and court relief. A center
could favor politically connected regions, and local authorities
could hoard by overstating protected need. Retain both local and
independently checkable evidence, accessible reports and reasons.
Require prompt outside review where useful and publication of
aggregate cross-region gaps and challenges. A budget vote is no
permission to alter a right, ignore a safety floor or transfer
nonmember obligations. Preserve material objections and distinct
sector decisions.

**Migration and next decision.** Pending calls and lawful mutual
aid agreements retain their actual assignment, payer and clock
through change. Trial one time-bounded pair of concurrent incidents
and a further call in a no-service-interruption tabletop before a
live test. Resource the exercise through approved existing staff
and specify a budget cap and dates before any real work. Count
actual travel, qualified rest, offline custody, receiving and
independent reconsideration. A missed floor, inaccessible report,
late arrival, lost communication or repeated resort to reserve
reopens the choice. Actual lawful adoption, funding, affected
participation, readiness and improved lives remain open.
