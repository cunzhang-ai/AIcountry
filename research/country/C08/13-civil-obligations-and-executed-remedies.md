# C08. Civil obligations must end in an obtainable remedy

15 October 2026 · Integrated design 72 · Candidate common code, not enacted law

Select B01–B20 as the common civil/administrative floor: equal persons,
real agreement, accountable agents, property and wrongs, accessible proof,
independent adjudication and actual execution. Local supplements stay within
express competence; sector guarantees retain stronger protections. Ordinary
people need no legal dossier before asking for help. A judgment is not a
received remedy, and AI assistance cannot settle authority or consent.

This retains the earlier common-floor/local-supplement choice and useful
September contract/property clauses while removing diagnostic capacity
language, clarifying agent reliance and fixing the gap between liability
and a responsible payer/executor. It adds selected civil defect responsibility,
state/employer responsibility and indivisible-harm contribution boundaries.
These are substantive normative proposals, not observed best-practice effects.

[Case journeys](../../settlement/civil-core/README.md) cover wages, unsafe
products, property-record errors, duplicated agent orders, blocked evidence,
public nonpayment and small repeated harms. They are authored questions, not
actual litigation, funding or affected-party participation. A blockchain,
smart contract or agreeing models cannot replace a fair remedy.

Keep retain/repair, contractual/private-forum and uniform centralized-code
alternatives live. Select a common enforceable floor with express local
supplementation and voluntary settlement; reject compulsory private justice
for dependent users and a super-regulator judging its own cases. More formal
paperwork does not itself improve lives. Courts do not acquire legislative
tax, scarcity, clinical or political power through this code.
