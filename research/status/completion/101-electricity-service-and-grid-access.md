# Completion finding. Ordinary electricity service now has a defined chain

Design193 specifies public system operation, accountable distribution, fair
scarce connections, voluntary flexible tranches, protected household service
and an accepting default supplier. It separates a safety trip, elected
allocation and service restoration. [Canonical rules](../../country/C09/19-electricity-service-and-grid-access.md)

Official outage, legal and regulatory sources inform three different
mechanisms. A bounded node example distinguishes firm and limited
connections and exposes the recovery and shared feeder constraints.
Actual source, engineering, paid reviewers, legal adoption and better
lives remain unresolved. [Comparison and limits](../../settlement/electricity-service/README.md)
