its rules, not whether this research may enact them.

The design already assigns institutions, services and remedies, but its
later choices were not consistently mapped to constitutional commitments,
framework statutes, annual budgets and local acts. Without that map, an
ordinary majority could change a protected franchise through a registration
rule, or a court could mistake a broad constitutional social duty for authority
to create an unappropriated service. A past budget scenario also cannot fund
later statutory promises merely because all appear in one design. These are
internal legal-logic concerns, not observed failures in a jurisdiction.

## Coherent alternatives and selected division

| Model | Gain | Failure or unresolved value choice |
|---|---|---|
| Retain design 12's mixed statements and resolve rank case by case | Preserves room for a future founding public and avoids invented legal detail now. | Incumbents and judges could choose rank after a dispute; transition and reliance would be unpredictable. |
| Constitutionalize the full institutional and service design, including numeric rules | Makes some guarantees hard to withdraw and gives courts strong textual instructions. | Freezes untested ballot, monetary, provider and benefit hypotheses; can put ordinary budget and technical allocation under judicial control. |
| Leave only procedural democracy in the constitution; all social and ecological protection in ordinary law | Keeps elected revision simple and fiscally responsive. | A temporary majority could hollow out basic protection and future ecological capacity while formal ballots continue. |
| **Selected: protected functions plus amendable delivery statutes** | Constitution protects equal political standing, practical opposition, replacement, universal rights, basic social/ecological duties, local democratic existence and review. Elected statutes define providers, fiscal rules, quantities and remedies; budgets pay within law. | Requires reasoned courts, a capable legislature and an explicit response to real scarcity. Functional review could itself become an unaccountable veto if not bounded. |

Under the selected reference, the founding charter must itself fix the ordinary
electorate's boundary and a maximum residence condition, subject to a legitimate
founding settlement. Statute may broaden access but cannot narrow the protected
class through identity, registration or administrative interpretation. A later
constitutional change to the boundary uses the valid protected route and cannot
give present officeholders control of their next contest. The charter also
protects genuine periodic elections, an opposition able to become government,
judicial remedies that can be executed, and transfer after defeat. Assembly
law controls taxation, ordinary policy and the detailed ballot. A ballot formula,
seat count or commission size is not declared timeless; material changes need
prior publication, rights review and prospective effect that does not reward
the current governing coalition.

The first Assembly exposes a bootstrap dependency: it cannot enact the ballot
that elected it. The legitimate founding or predecessor process must approve
the exact initial election, dispute and handover rules before polling. A later
Assembly may enact future ordinary election law within the protected charter.
The [founding sequence](../FOUNDING-AND-STRESS.md) now makes the statutory
bridge explicit, while preserving the
[pre-hierarchy founding version](FOUNDING-AND-STRESS-pre-hierarchy.md)
(SHA-256 `003d3e3c7933356d4661bca1529d78dc0ec4e22e8a07c9ca5268948bed252819`).

Universal bodily, civil, fair-process and nondiscrimination rights are protected
at constitutional rank. The charter would impose a justiciable duty to establish,
fund, deliver and review a basic essential-access floor and to protect the
ecological conditions of future life. It does not claim that a constitution
creates a home, nurse, water flow or external currency. Framework statutes
specify coverage, quality, response time, finance, responsible provider,
interim help and enforcement. A court can remedy arbitrary exclusion,
unlawful withdrawal, hidden shortfall or refusal to use available lawful
capacity; it cannot set tax rates, name an uncontracted supplier or replace
an elected allocation with its preferred national plan. Where a guaranteed
minimum cannot presently be supplied, claims and accrued liabilities remain
visible, urgent feasible protection continues, and elected institutions must
give a funded public response. A shortage is neither automatic compliance
nor a power to conscript unpaid family care.

The constitutional floor also protects elected local government and a lawful
division of powers, while statute defines particular service, tax and shared-area
competences with equalization and review. The national Assembly cannot simply
transfer an unfunded duty to localities or change boundaries without a lawful
affected-scope process. The charter protects professional independence and
contestable public evidence as functions; the specific statistics service,
technical operator, bank regime, public-enterprise portfolio, provider mix,
transport mode and currency choice remain enacted, amendable institutions.
The emergency prohibition on self-extension and suspension of democratic
competition is constitutional; hazard-specific measures and operational
resources are enacted separately. AI has no independent sovereign or coercive
authority; its changing technical arrangements are statutory or contractual.

Annual appropriations and local enactments implement these laws. They cannot
silently repeal a standing entitlement, change eligibility by an accounting
code or acquire a constitutional effect through a contract. Changes to a
statutory guarantee require an express prospective statute, reasons, the
updated whole-country cash/workforce/material account, affected-party access,
transition for accrued claims and independent review. This is not an absolute
bar on reducing a benefit: a lawful elected change may respond to new evidence
or real capacity, within constitutional rights. Material election and oversight
changes are assessed cumulatively across statutes, budgets, appointments,
information channels and enforcement. A court states a specific constitutional
defect and the effective remedy with reasons and appeal; it does not inherit a
permanent policy veto. The predecessor amendment process governs its own
replacement, and no new research wording approves itself.

## Evidence, objections, continuity and result

The [International IDEA amendment primer](https://www.idea.int/publications/catalogue/constitutional-amendment-procedures?lang=en)
frames the tension between adaptation and short-term partisan amendment.
The [Venice Commission Rule of Law Checklist](https://www.venice.coe.int/webforms/documents/?pdf=CDL-ad%282016%29007-e)
frames legality, legal certainty, abuse prevention and access to justice.
The [UN economic and social rights covenant](https://2covenants.ohchr.org/About-ICESCR.html)
connects progressive realization to available resources and nondiscrimination.
The [South African constitutional text](https://www.justice.gov.za/legislation/constitution/)
is one primary example of differentiated amendment procedures and resource-aware
social rights. None proves this hierarchy best or transfers a court's doctrine
to an unformed country. The selected wording is a normative research proposal;
no original population, asset, case or constitutional procedure has been observed.

Material objections remain. A protected social duty may invite judges to
supervise budgets without democratic accountability; a weakly specified duty
may leave excluded people with only reports. A protected franchise boundary
may entrench an unfair founding compromise; ordinary-law flexibility may
allow slow cumulative capture. Courts and archives can share the incumbent's
funding and personnel, while a formal referendum can occur under economic or
media domination. A challenger must be able to contest the whole control chain.
These value conflicts cannot be settled by claiming that one layer is
scientifically required. Rival constitutional ranks remain open for a real
founding public to compare.

Affected consumers are C02–C14, the proposed A1–A9 authority nucleus, the
earlier D1–D12 democratic candidate, budget/treasury, service, land, emergency,
criminal, digital and research-statistics proposals. The new hierarchy does
not automatically adopt any of them. An actual transition needs a clause-by-clause
inventory of the then-current constitution, laws, contracts, claims and public
assets; a competent old-rule authorization path; exact replacement wording;
published commencement stages; funded overlap; and successors for accrued
rights, pending cases, elections and records. Existing legal duties continue
until lawfully altered. A court order and administrative transfer must have
actual people, cash and access to execute it. If no lawful founding jurisdiction
exists, the hierarchy remains a design candidate only.

**Evidence qualification:** primary comparative constitutional materials and
internal dependency analysis, without local observations. **Research selection:**
the layered legal hierarchy enters the working design and the authority candidate.
**Legal authorization:** none. **Operational readiness:** unknown for a founding
public, elected bodies, courts, money, workers, publication and enforcement.
**Observed outcomes:** none. Same-controller drafting is not independent review.

The next bounded action for a lawful interested jurisdiction is to map one
constitutional social or electoral protection through its current statute,
annual budget, local delivery, individual case and effective remedy under a
noncooperating government. Compare the layered package with fuller
constitutionalization and procedural-only protection, using original local
records and affected-party reasons. A competent public authority must set the
budget and procedure; this project cannot. Reopen the research selection if
the protected floor cannot be enforced without judicial allocation of the
whole budget, if ordinary statutes can still disable opposition, or if
constitutional amendment is practically inaccessible to an equal public.
