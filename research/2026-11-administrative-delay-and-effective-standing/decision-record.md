## Values, facts and competing choices

Value: a person should not need an office's voluntary final refusal to challenge
its failure to act. Legal/physical constraint: a deadline supplies neither facts,
eligibility, safe capacity nor new authority. Empirical outcome: unobserved.
Design hypothesis: a receipt-based duty with necessary proof/help and direct
review improves accountability enough to justify its staff/hearing burden.

Retain completeness timing has flexibility but a controlled start. Tacit grant
can injure third parties; deemed denial exports delay costs. Substantive simpler
programmes and a rights-preserving U bargain can remove some proof burdens but
change resources/distribution and do not replace all safety/status decisions.
Prefer receipt-based ordinary processing without a new super-agency. Retain
stronger and different sector procedures; no latest-study result settles U.

## Selected rules and objections

AP uses B16's existing 5/30/one-at-most-30 working-day defaults and 20-working-day
ordinary adverse reply, changing the start to protected first receipt. Necessary
proof may continue but cannot reset it. Closure as incomplete is not a decision;
a genuine adverse finding needs fair help, actual proof standard and reasons.
No automatic approval, denial, citizenship, unsafe licence or new cash entitlement.
Omission/obstruction is directly reviewable under existing ordinary jurisdiction.
Late disclosure cannot truncate hearing or erase overdue time. Courts keep their
own powers; review/actual owed relief is not automatic merits success.

Preserve E06/E07 elapsed-hour/calendar-day protection, PD09 calendar-day access,
MB and electoral/liberty/clinical special clocks, source dates and old dues.
Numbers remain normative hypotheses. Rushed denials, pressure to waive reply,
more help/reason/court workload and displaced ordinary services are material
objections. Unknown facts or external impossibility can outlast the default;
retain fair process, honest overdue causes and independent case-specific review,
not invented compliance or perpetual officer extensions. No timetable is proved
achievable by declaration. Actual independent professional/affected review missing.

## Public control, source and continuing duties

Existing career/sector receivers and decision officers, lawful managers, record
custodians, qualified professionals, ordinary courts, Treasury and B19 executors
retain their distinct appointment/funding/information/execution chains. Managers
can arrange qualified cover inside competence; they cannot reset time or judge
their own case finally. AA/EV keep limited audit/evidence roles. Elected bodies
choose law, source and burdens; experts, models and copied agents gain no votes,
sovereign mandate, compulsory records access or policy veto. Complete capture
or absent staff can still defeat relief; no guaranteed guardian is invented.

OP/T must pay assistance, proof, reply/decision, urgent receiving, court/aid,
records, payment/execution, correction and transition without duplicate standby
or imaginary AI savings. All 37 complete functions, 61 original selections and 79
inherited references remain exact. Old rights, pending first dates, safe records,
wages/pensions/contracts/creditors and foreign obligations retain paid successors.
AP12 preserves earlier existing due dates/overdue failures; pending cases get
only prospective new ceilings, no forgiveness, mass revival or retroactive penalty.
Final adoption needs fresh review; unchanged inputs do not transfer old approval.
