# Decision record for Design468

**Baseline and procedure.** Design467 and the [research method](../FRAMEWORK.md)
supply the baseline. [Baseline hashes](baseline.json) retain the earlier
scope. Use existing parliamentary, judicial and budget power.

**Selection.** Apply cumulative review to the structure of a common
operating budget. In the constructed comparison select shared routine
support with separately paid protected release, execution and later
review. Retain fully separate operations as a live option.
[Selected rules](../country/C14/08/05-democratic-safeguards-in-a-shared-budget.md).

**Reasons and objection.** Joint purchasing can reduce cost without
delegating sovereign functions. Savings may exist only because control
over information, payment and execution moved to one organization.
Separate reserves cost real staff and money and can be overspecified.
Use the smallest protected scope that works under the same failure.
A narrower less costly option qualifies where the later call and
independent actors are actually available.

**Compatibility and consumers.** Preserve prior jurisdiction, review
assignment, appropriations, individual rights and ordinary care.
A threatened function uses its existing correction route and earliest
useful time. Neither prior approval nor a ruling supplies missing cash
or a substitute judge. Correct SC27's cross-reference to SC14 for
undisputed supplier payment, preserving scope and original clocks.

**Limits and reopening.** The comparative costs, dates and operating
conditions are authored. Actual affected participation, staffing,
elected choice, legal adoption and executed relief remain open.
Reopen on selective failure, incorrect source scope, a conflicted
reviewer, a lost later call or a more effective funded arrangement.
