# A common budget and different effects on ordinary lives

*Design459 · Constructed comparison of delivery models and a second-period decision.*

## What outside evidence can establish

In the Oregon Medicaid lottery study, coverage was estimated to lower
positive depression screening by 9.15 percentage points (95% interval
−16.70 to −1.60) and catastrophic out-of-pocket spending by 4.48 points
(−8.26 to −0.69). The measured change in glycated hemoglobin was
0.01 points (−0.09 to 0.11). The study also estimated higher medical
spending. The lottery changed the opportunity to apply, with the
estimated coverage effects relying on the study's instrumental-variable
assumptions. Its participants and roughly two-year observation cannot
establish effects on a nationwide provider system. Distinct dimensions
and horizons remain visible, including inconclusive effects. No estimate
from Oregon prices or predicts the care offers below.
[Baicker et al., Tables 2, 4 and 5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3701298/).

Crépon and colleagues varied the share of eligible young jobseekers
offered placement assistance across 235 French labor markets and
randomized offers within those markets. Their eight-month direct
gain in stable employment was partly offset by estimated displacement
among nonparticipants. The estimated net employment effect was
uncertain and the direct gains did not persist. That experiment
motivates testing affected people and duration together.
[Crépon et al., abstract and Section 7](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w18597/w18597.pdf).

## Same population, constraints and duties

The constructed legislature compares home-support delivery within the
same constitutional bargain. There are twenty eligible households,
including the people who did not use the main application channel.
The deadline and outcome definitions are pinned. A separate protected
service in the sending locality keeps its original due time. The common
incremental limit is six cash units and four paid peak blocks per
period. Protected existing duties and independent court and appeal
capacity are funded separately in the fixture. Prices below include
limited ordinary review, paid backfill and replenishment where stated.
Any new enacted duty would keep its original authority and remedy.

The outcome rows below are alternative constructed evidence records.
A real decision would need a credible comparison design and uncertainty
on each effect. Timely receipt bounds are counts of confirmed timely
receipts up to the total possible if all unknowns were timely. They
show missing-outcome uncertainty only. Household time entries are
constructed hours for one common measured household group in the
same week. They describe two distinct time categories and cannot
be subtracted to assert a net benefit.

## Disposition at two successive votes

**First vote.** R, P and D fit the stated ordinary window and their
declared protected conditions. M is held in the relevant scope because
its required overnight receiver is absent. Its lower cash use does not
qualify that service. P's higher confirmed count cannot establish a
better timely outcome than R or D. P offers greater resident-directed
time in the measured group. D imposes less unpaid help. They are distinct
political choices. In the fixture the competent legislature votes P
for a single period for resident direction, funds the agreed unpaid
help safeguards and leaves the others available for reconsideration.
The relevant first-period assignments are separately accepted.
The decision requires a fresh vote for the next period.

**Second vote.** Independent review verifies that the second-period
P backfill is now assigned to a protected origin service. Correct
that resource assumption in every option and supporting claim that
used it. P would need another paid block and one additional cash unit
in this fixture. It cannot continue its optional scope on the same
envelope. P's first-period time result remains in the evidence. A proposal to
report only households that submitted a completed form would change
the denominator. Report that series separately if useful and retain
all twenty eligible households and original deadlines for this choice. The budget
cannot move the origin shift without lawful funded cover. No aggregate
gain, AI schedule or favorable first vote covers the displaced duty.

The opposition submits exact D under the existing PCO14 route. Its
vehicle and crew are distinct from the disputed backfill and its
cost includes renewed delivery and review on the same calendar.
Compare D with R, including R's lower resident-directed time and
greater unpaid burden in the measured group and D's uncertain wider
effect. Select D for the second period in the fixture, because the
separately accepted mobile crew reduces unpaid burden without
consuming the exposed origin shift. Put its exact financing and
delivery through the ordinary national and local votes. If that
route or its approval fails, continue R where it can deliver and
carry binding duties. Where neither alternative covers those duties,
record the uncovered act and bring the funded remedy to the
competent authority.
A narrower P with a separately qualified source remains eligible
for a future exact vote. The whole country comparison retains the
other [constitutional rivals](../../country/C01/03/06-current-country-and-strong-alternatives.md).

Any P commitments already binding need a funded handoff that
preserves choice, appointments and original due remedies. If the
required carryover cannot fit inside the stated D allocation, the
new D release waits or needs a separately voted source. An affected
household can directly contest omitted service or unsafe handoff.
No observation here establishes a winner among entire countries.
