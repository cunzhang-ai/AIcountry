# H16–H20: retained stock, repair and a paid transition

*Part of [C09/12](../12-secure-housing-and-repair.md); design91 research selection.*

**H16 — Keep stock, occupancy and supply distinct.** Retain the authored
75,000 structures/titles as the old reference endowment, not an observed
adequate/available stock or a national dwelling target. Its75,000 resource
groups do not prove shared homes. Inspect actual vacancies, safe private space,
access, geography, household choices and journeys before a new spatial plan.
Buying500 occupied homes changes ownership, not national supply. Consented
compact/clustered homes and shared facilities may be compared with dispersed
private space; count actual rooms, journeys, energy and operating work, not a
blanket density/AI saving. Protect informal cooperation and the right to decline.

**H17 — Repair first where it preserves an adequate home.** The competent
owner compares retention, staged repair, rebuilding and an available alternative
at comparable safety, access, remaining life, private space and journeys.
Independent technical acceptance must cover structure, fire, moisture, climate,
access and essential systems; owner's self-certification cannot release a
saving. A failed repair hypothesis can be rejected. Skilled work, tools, waste,
temporary homes and transition pay enter the same C06 account under TW time.
Do not erase existing worker pay/obligations when less future work is needed.

The [quantity comparison](../../../settlement/housing/README.md) uses authored
50t/2,000task-hour rebuilds and12t/1,500-hour repairs. A25%/75% mix is a test,
not a statutory quota or measured engineering recipe. It earns no automatic
extension of old asset life, lower depreciation, debt haircut or national fiscal
saving. Recovered usable material, imported/new inputs and disposal are distinct;
the same recovered material cannot also be new quarry output. Construction and
routine maintenance/repair scopes must not overlap unnoticed.

**H18 — Temporary dwelling, return and overlap.** Before a planned works/repair
move or authority-directed relocation,
provide an accepting suitable temporary home with storage, accessibility,
school/care/work journeys, safe contact and funded moving/overlap costs.
For immediate danger receive people as soon as safety requires; evacuation
alone decides neither title nor permanent relocation. Preserve tenancy/return
after repair and prevent double housing charge; independently verify safe return.
Permanent loss uses its separate necessity/property/compensation process and
C09 disaster recovery. A care-reception bed/place is not a temporary dwelling.
FD's already committed receiving capacity remains committed to that purpose.
Ordinary arrears/owner-occupation possession follows H02/H14 rather than a
blanket temporary-move condition; the public receiving duty and bounded
funding/stock-failure route continue, with both unmet claims recorded.
Temporary dwelling leases need actual stock, dates, occupants and owners;
an annual throughput lower bound is not a safe construction/moving calendar.

**H19 — Prospective migration, continuing duties.** A duly enacted Act names
actual commencement dates, with new covered tenancy/public-stock terms beginning
on the first1 January at least12 months after enactment as the default notice
rule. Existing lawful tenancies, secured claims, deposits, accrued payments and
pending orders retain source-law dates/priority until an expressly authorized
rights-reviewed migration specifies terms, losses, compensation and funding.
No office can certify away old rights or keep extending a due obligation by
calling capacity unready. New safe-access/remedy provisions apply only within
their enacted scope; H02 is not retroactive priority legislation.

Local/elected schedules identify owner, operator, acquisition source, debt,
repair receiver, independent court/inspector time, I03 payment, jobs, vacancies,
temporary dwellings and overlap before commitments. IC authority expiry stops
new awards; it does not terminate leases, debt service, tenant committees,
renewal or repair. Failure follows H10/H15 and prior scarcity law, not executive
expropriation, compulsory cohabitation, private FX sweeps or an unfunded promise.

**H20 — A choice that can lose.** Retain stronger tenancy law even where
conversion is rejected. Prefer new/repair supply over paid acquisition when
actual shortage relief dominates tenure conversion at comparable rights/cost.
Reopen the public option on exclusion, retaliation, unsafe/unaffordable homes,
debt/renewal failure or a better feasible plural alternative. Inspect original
tenant/applicant/worker observations safely, including nonusers and people who
cannot exit; technical analysis is not their participation. This desk selection
does not demonstrate enacted rights, actual stock, a national source or welfare.

**Design145 LA join.** [Public acquisition](../13-public-acquisition-and-secure-relocation.md)
now supplies a separately authorized route to acquire a necessary site/right;
H's cost-rental/repair guarantee itself still grants no taking power. Price actual
rights, net replacement, creditor/tax and tenant/worker/host burdens; no free public
land, ownership by budget label or removal into an unreceived housing promise.

[H21–H25](05-housing-preservation-and-net-supply.md) requires
an elected portfolio with separate retention and entrant objectives.
Report physical stock, restored service, reserved returns and offers
with their own dates, actual acceptance and paid owners.

[H26–H31](06-housing-redevelopment-and-secure-return.md) applies
the receiving, affordable return and funded overlap tests to planned
replacement of occupied affordable homes.
