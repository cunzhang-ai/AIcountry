# One aggregate score and two legal routes

Constructed selection exercise for assistance with ordinary public benefit
casework. No output is used against a real claimant. Compare a current
supplier, a possible replacement and qualified ordinary handling. One hundred
fresh, independently graded exercises cover 80 standard cases and 20
protected cases involving older dates, accessible evidence or urgent
correction. The protected group is deliberately oversampled for inspection.
Do not treat these weights as national prevalence.

The stipulated screening rule requires at least 90 correct outcomes overall
and at most two material errors among the 20 protected exercises. It is a
test rule, not a legal error allowance. Any unverified consequential output
remains barred from reliance on its own. A severe failure would require
separate action on its actual scope.

| Route | Standard correct out of 80 | Protected correct out of 20 | Overall correct out of 100 | Protected errors |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Current supplier across the whole task | 80 | 16 | 96 | 4 |
| Replacement across the whole task | 80 | 14 | 94 | 6 |
| Qualified ordinary handling across the whole task | 72 | 18 | 90 | 2 |
| Independently verified assistance for standard work, ordinary handling for protected work | 80 | 18 | 98 | 2 |

The last row stipulates a separate run with a qualified routing rule, paid
verification and an independently reachable ordinary route. It cannot
be inferred by adding the best columns from two suppliers. Select this
narrow use for a paid reversible trial. An illustrative operating window
needs three qualified verification hours and five other qualified hours for
protected work, all independently funded and usable by the deadline.
The trial leaves the already authorized full ordinary fallback available.
It does not establish grounds to cut the reserve or wages.

At seven usable hours in the trial route, the same apparent accuracy gives
no authority to claim timely capacity. If another duty consumes an hour or
ordinary handling cannot retrieve original evidence, withhold the proposed
reliance and use the separately funded fallback. Record any failures on
their original dates if it also fails. A fresh award does not change the
status of old claims. Audit appeal and payment separately from selection.
