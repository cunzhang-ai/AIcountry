# Design499 sources and scope

- The [OECD's public finance resilience analysis](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2023/06/public-finance-resilience-in-the-transition-towards-carbon-neutrality_55f6fb35/7f3275e0-en.pdf)
  models fiscal effects of policy instruments and changes to underlying
  tax bases. Its scenarios do not predict a specific date or level of
  AIcountry collections, appropriations or protection.
- The [European Environment Agency's ETS spending data](https://www.eea.europa.eu/en/datahub/datahubitem-view/d702153d-e2d0-4a0b-a2e1-06ed3a43d07c)
  records reported national use of auction revenues for 2020–2024.
  The [official reporting overview](https://climate-energy.eea.europa.eu/topics/climate-finance/use-of-ets-auctioning-revenues/intro)
  explains the scope of the EU system. These observations demonstrate
  actual revenue uses and restrictions in a distinct legal context.
  They supply no candidate revenue floor or local settled cash.
- The [four-year recurring-source comparison](../settlement/recurring-revenue-and-budget-choices/01-four-years-and-a-real-revenue-choice.md),
  [owner-worker incidence extension](../settlement/recurring-finance-and-incidence/01-success-and-the-next-budget.md)
  and [climate continuity exercise](../settlement/climate-budget-continuity/02-two-budgets-and-a-grid-delay.md)
  supply prior separately constructed paths. Design499's prices,
  receipts, years and operating limits are authored rather than pooled
  or projected from those records.
