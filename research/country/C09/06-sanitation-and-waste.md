# C09. Sanitation and waste as delivered public duties

*Part of [C09 land and ecology](../C09-land-and-ecology.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

Safe drinking water does not by itself remove excreta, wastewater or discarded
material. Prior Assembly law sets a health-protecting sanitation floor and
limits on discharge, dumping and worker exposure. Local elected authorities
must name a sanitation service steward for every inhabited area. The steward
is answerable for the complete route: usable toilets or other suitable
facilities, containment, safe emptying or sewers, transport, treatment and
lawful end use or disposal. Sewer ownership is not a substitute for serving
people who are unconnected, mobile or without a formal address. The same law
names a distinct solid-waste steward for prevention, collection, sorting,
transfer, treatment and safe disposal; it may share staff or contracts with
sanitation only where risks and accounts remain separately inspectable.

National health and ecological authorities set evidence-based safety floors
and monitor cross-boundary harm. Local stewards can use public, cooperative or
licensed private operators under enforceable service contracts. Separate
inspectors verify actual facilities, effluent, sludge, waste destinations,
worker protection and neighborhood exposure, including public operators. The
steward must publish service gaps and reasoned corrective plans; residents and
workers can report harm without losing service or employment. Orders to repair,
stop an unsafe release or compensate under law need independent review and an
identified enforcement office. A test result alone does not authorize a
coercive household search or displacement.

The Assembly funds the guaranteed floor through the common C06 budget and
adopts fair tariff, subsidy and equalization rules. Operators account for
energy, water, chemicals, vehicles, land, treatment capacity, skilled staff,
maintenance, safe sites and downstream communities. No claimed recycling
benefit counts until material is actually recovered or safely treated; waste
export cannot erase domestic responsibility for foreseeable harm abroad.
Health-care and other hazardous waste requires source separation, licensed
handling and a traceable end point under distinct health/safety law. The
C07 health authority retains clinical duties; the waste steward cannot decide
which patients receive treatment.

Before failure, each contract or public charter names outage notification,
spare capacity, manual procedures, mutual aid, records custody, a temporary
receiver, wage and safety continuity, and a lawful payment source. A flood,
strike, contamination or treatment-plant failure requires safe interim toilets,
water protection, collection or containment and accessible public notice.
Where several places need one pump, truck, generator or treatment site, C06's
prior-law scarcity stages govern the shared stock; a steward cannot allocate
another sector's supplies by calling them sanitary. Local bodies retain
ordinary route and facility choices, but upstream savings cannot impose an
unreviewed downstream disease or pollution burden.

Actual sites, geology, settlement patterns, existing licenses, budgets and
operators remain unknown. The [decision record](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-service-boundaries/decision-record.md)
compares this split stewardship with status quo and a single national utility.
Reopen it if local stewardship cannot protect exposed people, equalization is
unfunded, or shared-control capture defeats inspection and remedy.

[WS water settlement](16-water-allocation-and-service.md) separates abstraction/delivered supply from this wastewater/waste duty. Actual return place/time/quality, treatment/receiving and attributable charges must reconcile once; a supply licence, reuse or a household water bill does not certify safe sanitation or duplicate its source.

[OC outage continuity](20-essential-service-outage-continuity.md) requires
a tested and funded route to safe treatment or alternate containment.
Verify received sanitation and water quality separately from power restored.

[LCY product lifecycle](26-product-repair-and-material-responsibility.md)
assigns covered product financing and verified material disposition
while the waste steward retains actual safe receiving. Keep municipal
continuity, verified producer funding and legacy public obligations distinct.

[MW01–MW08](06/01-municipal-waste-and-safe-receiving.md) specifies
accessible municipal collection, material accounting, safe host
conditions, alternate receiving and paid site closure. Wastewater
and clinical streams retain their separate authorities and duties.
