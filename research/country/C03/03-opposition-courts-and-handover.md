# C03. Democratic government and the ability to remove it: Opposition, courts and peaceful handover

*Part of [C03. Democratic government and the ability to remove it](../C03-democratic-government.md); integrated design 80 research, not enacted law.*

Opposition receives practical agenda access, public information, questioning rights,
fair campaigning conditions and leadership of public-accounts scrutiny. Residents can
originate proposals; [compensated resident panels](24-resident-deliberation-and-elected-response.md) can investigate and bring a bounded recommendation to an elected vote. Neither panels nor referendums bypass individual rights or become an incumbent's
device for eliminating future competition. Public and independent media remain plural.
The constitutional future is also contestable: the [protected change route](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-constitutional-change/decision-record.md)
lets both Assembly members and electors initiate exact amendments, requires a
genuine public final choice, and delays changes that would benefit current rulers.
It does not give Cabinet a plebiscite power or let a court reject lawful policy
because it dislikes the proposal.

The [EA01–EA25 electoral charter](09-electoral-administration-and-public-control.md) now completes the seven-seat electoral commission: three sources, fixed five-present/four-vote rule, finite reserves, career duties, ordinary courts and an expressly authorized founding route. It selects no actual officeholder or poll outcome. Judicial review and audit also require specified appointment sources,
protected removal rules, independent information access and funding that the challenged
office cannot withhold. Appointment plurality alone is not independence. The detailed
[democratic candidate](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-09-democratic-control/democratic-constitution.md) supplies
starting rules; its [continuity correction](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-09-democratic-continuity/continuity-candidate.md)
limits claims about deadlock, reserves and handover. Actual N and the lawful municipal map still require the founding-context
selection. EL05 now selects one national constituency; the earlier national
territorial-seat D ceiling belongs to the retained mixed-count rival, not this
current reference. Earlier-called contests keep actual source law; neither
research selection proves optimal representation or changes those mandates.

The [CJ01–CJ18 reference](08-constitutional-review-and-public-control.md) now
selects a separate nine-seat constitutional court: twelve-year nonrenewable seat
terms, three four-year cohorts and three appointment sources with fixed votes.
It completes the historical D5 hypothesis without enacting it. Ordinary courts
retain civil, administrative, criminal and urgent individual-rights jurisdiction.
Five actual votes are needed for a final national disposition; no quorum or
agreement means no final disposition, not presumed constitutional approval.
One named same-source reserve per seat is finite; vacancy cannot lower a
threshold, extend a term or create a cabinet acting judge. Preassigned external
ordinary judges finally hear the closed own-court appointment, personal-status,
discipline and funding cases. Even that route can fail from genuine direct
conflicts or capture. Narrow ordinary relief cannot invent a national judgment.
The [material chain](../C06/29-constitutional-review-capacity.md) joins standing
funding, source records, separate payment and actual civilian execution without
judicial self-appropriation or a second copy of the existing SF standby.

The reference democracy also needs a preauthorized, distributed route from
electoral evidence to an executable remedy. Local election offices retain
authenticated original poll evidence and a lawful reconciliation route; candidates,
voters, observers and courts receive timely, rights-protecting access. Ordinary
courts have specified urgent individual-rights competence if a specialist bench
is vacant, with appeal and conflict rules set before the dispute. Core election,
court, legal-aid and audit operations have bounded standing funding and record
custody independent of a challenged cabinet or its principal digital provider.
After a lawful result or order, named civil and security officers perform transfer
duties; neither local officers, courts, vendors nor a missed deadline invent a
winner. This [democratic-continuity choice](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-operating-settlement/democratic-institutions.md)
distributes evidence, access and execution, not sovereign power to a new guardian.
Its independence depends on actual staff, usable cash, distinct controllers and
obedience to lawful orders; labels and duplicate AI reviews do not establish it.

Election records and public accounts have named lawful source custodians and
protected archival successors under C11. Candidates, opposition and residents
can contest suppression or material error through timely, privacy-protecting
access to original evidence. A changed statistical definition cannot alter
eligibility, erase a valid ballot or certify a preferred winner. An official
archive preserves the enacted rule and previous record version, while election
administrators and courts retain their own decision and remedy duties.
The [communications continuity rule](../C09/05-communications-and-logistics.md)
requires a physically usable path for local count evidence and lawful public
notice when backhaul fails; a network provider cannot certify or suppress an
election result through control of transmission.

Before a general election, opposition and local representatives must be able
to inspect and challenge the [public-conditions account](../C14/04-public-conditions.md)
through lawful, privacy-protecting access to its sources and comparison rules.
They can put rival explanations and funded responses before voters without
needing cabinet or AI approval. A missing report is a remediable failure of
publication, not a reason to postpone an election or extend an incumbent.
The [campaign-finance route](07-political-finance-and-campaign-access.md)
adds timely support and court-executed repair when an incumbent diverts
public assets or dominant paid reach; a regulator's allegation cannot
become a unilateral opponent ban or an automatic change to the count.

A legally effective change of government must transfer command, records and treasury
access within its scope. [HT practical handover](19-lawful-handover-and-actual-control.md)
provides that route. A losing executive has no veto. Peaceful opposition to the design itself is
protected; democratic safeguards must not become an excuse to suppress critics.
This includes lawful advocacy for autonomy or a different territorial status.
National and regional leaders must not choose a ballot map, suppress dissent
inside a proposed region, or withdraw ordinary services to change the result.
Independent count evidence and remedies apply to an authorized status vote as
well as to an Assembly election, while its legal effect must be fixed beforehand.
Command transfer includes armories, security authorizations, communications
credentials and custody of investigative records under lawful succession rules;
neither a commander nor a vendor certifies its preferred election result.

After a fragmented result, [government formation](06-government-formation-and-caretaker.md)
is an Assembly choice under the pre-enacted rule; the outgoing cabinet is
already caretaker from the fixed election-period start. A Speaker cannot
suppress a lawful candidate,
withhold a first vote to postpone the clock or choose a government alone.
An Assembly majority can dismiss a minority cabinet even without agreeing
on a successor; the caretaker's narrow powers and standing obligations
continue while a new lawful choice is made.

[Design120 OJ](../C08/25-ordinary-justice-and-public-prosecution.md) now supplies
the ordinary judicial/prosecution personnel/control candidate identified by119.
CJ constitutional source/votes remain exact; ordinary12-seat selection, actual
status/prosecution/source/own-case routes need express lawful adoption. Names
and formal votes prove no actual independence. [AA121](../C04/04-public-audit-and-accountable-oversight.md) now supplies A1's
positive public-audit choice; its Assembly/common-patron/source risks remain.
All political dates/actual cases/laws remain protected, not delayed by audit readiness.

[PO civilian enforcement](../C08/27-civilian-enforcement-and-public-control.md) now
concretizes police/external-inquiry/corrections command and source succession.
Public general policy remains elected, case/force/proof independent, and an
expired chief cannot retain office through keys or a consultant/deputy title.
Actual paid independent execution remains unverified; no military/AI guardian.

[NI ordinary national agenda](16-public-national-initiatives.md) supplies
the direct public implementation promised above, with verified scoped support,
paid hearing/drafting and a finite recorded Assembly disposition. Existing
member/opposition/panel, LR/RR/TS and direct individual remedies retain scope.

[PE parliamentary access](21-parliamentary-evidence-and-independent-access.md)
implements protected opposition scrutiny for ordinary national public action.
The requesting member can contest refusal or delay and use timely protected
agenda time. Actual source review and executive compliance remain separate.

[PX open contests](22-open-contests-and-neutral-enforcement.md) tests the
timing and combined effect of planned public restraints before a lawful
opportunity disappears. It preserves neutral investigation and witness
protection under their own law and tests actual execution separately.
