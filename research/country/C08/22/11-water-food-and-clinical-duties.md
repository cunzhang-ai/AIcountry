# TD07–TD10: water, food, medicines and actual professional competence

*Application of TS01–TS16; [technical safety](../22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md).*

| ID / competent role | Defined entry and substantive operating duty | Limits and separate rights |
|---|---|---|
| TD07 / public-health water-safety reviewer, separate from supply operator | A public drinking-water supply needs an authorized accountable operator, source-to-user hazard controls, representative independent sampling, maintenance, prompt contamination warning and actual safe alternate water. Candidate microbial floor: E. coli not detectable in a properly collected 100 mL sample by an adopted validated method; a detection triggers investigation/protective response without waiting for average annual failure. | Sampling frequency, validated test/detection rules and source-specific chemical/radiological limits need lawful annexes. A negative sample is not whole-system safety. Private household collection is not automatically public utility operation. |
| TD08 / food-safety authority | Commercial food production/supply requires traceable accountable registration, hygienic handling, separation of known serious contamination, accurate material safety information and prompt lawful recall. Slaughter, commercial infant-formula manufacture and industrial processing of food requiring controlled pathogen destruction need prior site approval and validated critical controls. | Ordinary home meals/gifts need no commercial permit. Listed allergens, microbial/time/temperature limits and validation methods need exact instruments; a nutritional preference or unfamiliar cuisine is no hazard finding. Animal welfare retains separate law. |
| TD09 / medicines/device authority, separate from public purchasing | Manufacture/import for commercial supply and marketing authorization of products represented to prevent/treat disease by medicinal action require quality/identity, evidence of the actual claimed benefit and harms, controlled production and continuing adverse-event/recall duties. Implantable/life-supporting devices and devices whose output directly directs a material clinical intervention need prior risk-proportionate conformity authorization. Lower-risk devices use accountable registration and specified conformity duties. | Intended function/claim, not marketing vocabulary, determines scope. Published exact classes, evidence and quality annexes must precede approval. Generic/relevant established evidence can suffice with justified equivalence; no mandatory duplicate human/animal trial or automatic foreign approval. |
| TD10 / clinical/professional competence authority with direct court appeal | Reserved acts are surgery, invasive anesthesia/airway management, prescription/dispensing of a medicine specifically restricted by law, clinical ionizing-radiation operation, and signing legally required structural or safety-critical operating certification. They require demonstrated scoped competence, valid authorization, actual consent where applicable and safe facilities/support. | General health discussion, peer support, translation, ordinary personal assistance, noninvasive learning and self-care are not a professional monopoly. Task-sharing with proved competence is lawful; a credential title alone proves neither current ability nor independent review. |

TD07's 100 mL choice follows a located WHO guideline table; the table was visible
in search excerpts but full retrieval failed. It is a candidate pending qualified
method/source review, not a claim that the entire WHO guideline was inspected or
that one test proves national water safety. Acute warning and safe alternate
supply must consider the actual hazard; boiling is not assumed to remove every
chemical or radiation problem.

TD09 includes diagnostic software where its intended function meets the actual
risk definition; not all AI, wellness tools or conversations are medical devices.
Claims/configuration, population, care pathway, calibration and actual user
understanding matter. A safety label cannot create clinical benefit, regulatory
approval cannot guarantee access, and insurance coverage is a separate C07 choice.
The patient retains clinical choice, privacy and B/RP research/material consent.

A clinician's justified individualized use of an otherwise lawfully supplied
medicine/device outside a marketed indication does not automatically require a
new marketing authorization or become an offense; actual evidence, informed
choice, professional ability and ordinary safety/records apply. It is no excuse
for deceptive commercial promotion or compulsory experimental treatment. A
compassionate access route requires a competent lawfully specified decision,
uncertainty, consent and actual supply, not a new approval created by this sentence.

Ordinary first aid and justified immediately necessary rescue retain their
unlicensed/necessity routes under actual P/B law; a guild rule cannot require
withholding lawful emergency help.

Only a precise applicable enacted TD duty/annex engages VD/V; missing chemical
limits, licensing categories or research permission are not supplied by analogy.
No provider's permit expiry alone licenses abandonment of a patient. C07/C06
must arrange safe continuity while actual law decides the disputed practice.

[MQ04–MQ06](../../C07/26/02-release-substitution-and-receiving.md)
specifies a prospective shortage response within actual product release and
professional authority. A shortage does not waive TD09/TD10, give an import
permission or decide consent and clinical use.

[WS26–WS32](../../C09/16/06-small-water-supplies-and-safe-receipt.md)
assigns routine oversight and funded alternate drinking water for
covered off-network supplies under the applicable health standards.
