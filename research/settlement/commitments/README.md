[Structured inventory](inventory.json) retains each selection-to-function mapping, resource class and source hash. A mapping expresses a responsibility or dependency; it is not an additional expenditure. The whole-country account applies to all functions. Many duties share a service component, but their legal scope, due times and consent cannot be silently merged.

For every row, current cost, qualified quantity, actual delivery and executed remedy are **unknown**, stored as `null`. A model envelope is possible older
support, not a sufficient allocation of the current duty. In particular,
the old ledger includes justice and democratic administration in aggregate;
it would be wrong both to omit them and to count their entire cost again.
Included, incremental and overlapping allocations require actual attribution.
The present inventory supplies none of those actual quantities. The
[continuity reference](../continuity/01-reference-cost-and-capacity.md)
discloses authored included core shares separately; those shares neither
add a second budget nor populate actual national cost or delivery.

The 16 resource classes identify shared dependence, not fungible workers:
`clinical`, `education`, `care`, `justice`, `admin`, `technical`, `elected`,
`sites`, `transport`, `power`, `water`, `materials`, `channels`, `time`,
`cash` and `fx`. Actual reconciliation needs specialty, item, location,
controller, due period, available amount, maintenance, standby and displacement.
Separate payment, notice and record paths even though all appear under channels.
One label appearing in two rows is a question to inspect, not proof of overuse.

The [dated decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-commitment-reconciliation/decision-record.md)
preserves the design-55 predecessor, assumptions, frozen inventory, exact
model/result dependencies and checks. Current source changes reopen affected
mappings; successful link checks do not transfer prior support. A missing
function can be reported normally; authors must update this denominator and
coverage rather than reject an unfamiliar claim because it has no row.

The [continuity](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-continuity-law/decision-record.md),
[income](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-income-floor/decision-record.md) and
[joined economy](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-national-economic-package/decision-record.md)
records have different pinned support scopes. Earlier review does not
transfer to changed rules. The national reference now exposes private
finance and physical/FX failure as well as fiscal gaps. The
[protected-stage reference](../transition/README.md) adds paid overlap,
source availability, safe acceptance, missed dates and post-horizon
liabilities without establishing full-country affordability or adoption.
