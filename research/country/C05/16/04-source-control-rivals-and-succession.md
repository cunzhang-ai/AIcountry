# EB13–EB16. A new buffer needs a real country source and a real alternative

*Part of [earnings transition](../16-portable-earnings-and-work-transition.md).*

**EB13 — General public funding, not an imagined insurance reserve.** Select
explicit general-revenue/appropriation funding through existing social protection/
Treasury, no new employer levy, Y10% sweep, mineral-rent assumption, autonomous
fund, MC overdraft or implicit debt. Actual RF elected temporary stabilization
borrowing can be chosen only within its existing authority/source limits. Falling
work/tax receipts and rising claims may coincide; compute ordinary and recession
cohorts, early cash, contributions/credits, tax/I05 offsets, public/private demand,
source/FX and legacy effects, not gross benefit as net cost or a constant-income
yield after people lose earnings. Actual recoveries are no prior source.

Pay administration/assistance/history/privacy/qualification, actual independent
review/executor/record/cash/non-AI routes, voluntary work/learning receiving,
qualified cover and old-tail/transition once. Existing L04/L06/L07 and all37
functions/later NR/other duties continue; no old bid includes the new benefit by
label. EB above the needs floor is not automatically NP useful-time basic
priority ahead of care/medicine/opposition. Apply only actual source-compatible
NP/E priorities and RF/FP/TN law; source failure preserves claims/protection and
requires E19 elected correction, not a secret first-come cap or eligibility cut.
New law must choose actual sources/cohorts/date before committing it; research
is no staffed funded entitlement or Treasury readiness veto over existing dues.

**EB14 — Real control, assistance and future work forms.** C04 protected career
claim/payer roles issue reasons within statute, with AP/V unimplicated ordinary
court/executor and AA scrutiny, not employer/benefits vendor final judgment.
Inspect appointment/removal/pay/source keys across actual controllers; distinct
labels/models are no independent reviewer. The employer cannot withhold a due
public claim by refusing application/signature; use proportionate qualified
original worker/payment/other proof with actual help and lawful requests, not a
complete-life graph. Protected status, politics, union, disability or a referee's
approval is no risk proxy. No new search/force/punitive/immigration power follows.

Use appropriate future/shared work evidence, direct speech, supported expression,
agent relays or nontext forms while separating meaning, work, pay, delegation and
consent. Disclose unsupported hour/pay/property semantics instead of silently
erasing them; actual compatible proof/partial decisions and I05 help remain.
Plural mediation/offline/human review and paid correction/exit must work, not just
be offered by the same failed vendor. Replication neither proves work nor earns
another cap/mandate. Safe testimony and necessary records are not permanently
public or model-training data merely because a claim used them.

**EB15 — Keep a simpler country and other responses real.** Retain I05/WL/N/Y
without new earnings smoothing as a genuine simpler, less insider-biased source
choice; it accepts sharper transition toward basic needs. Employer retention
can preserve useful relationships but makes firms gatekeepers and may subsidize
unviable/already-retained jobs. Direct voluntary public learning/repair/work
may create capability but needs actual instructors/tasks/materials/receivers;
no automatic job promise. A proportional-work-duration bridge is a real rival
to the selected calendar-reference amount/calendar spell/proportional quota;
it can better target
brief-work claims but adds an assigned-history ledger and different exclusions.
These cannot be combined free of their incompatible power/source effects.

The selected portable capped buffer favors prior earners over newcomers/unpaid
carers and may pay people with assets; I05 taper can reduce poorer recipients'
marginal gain more. Earlier-care reference, cap and progressive-source intention
are not proof of fairness or complete net-work incentives. Reject/displace the
choice if those losses, weaker mobility, capture, voluntary-activation cost or
full paid source outweigh protection. Evidence from employer schemes does not
validate this individual buffer or80%/52/182/728/13 as an optimum. More programmes
and successful arithmetic are no better lives.
