# Provide learning support before the final assessment and through transfers

*Design336 · ED34–ED38 · Proposed ordinary learning and access rules.*

**ED34 — Start with the barrier the learner encounters.** The school and
ED22 public owner receive a credible report or observation of materially
inaccessible learning from a learner, family, teacher or other safe
source. Record original receipt, first known loss, what is usable and
what requires support. Provide safe ordinary adjustments on usable
educational evidence, with the learner's supported voice. No prior
diagnosis, failed course, formal plan or parent account is required
for that response. Offer safe confidential help and a way to disagree.
Preserve applicable clinical scope, individual consent and independent
safeguarding. An attendance record or model prediction establishes
neither need nor ability by itself.

**ED35 — Give the interim decision and final assessment separate dates.**
The responsible local owner names the competent school or service
receiver and makes an accessible initial support disposition within
two teaching days of credible receipt, sooner at the first useful
opportunity. Specify the teaching, access adjustment or paid interim
support, receiver, usable date and actual gaps. In parallel, where a
material needs assessment is required, issue an independently
reviewable decision and, where due, an executable plan within 30
calendar days of the same receipt. One reasoned extension of up to
10 calendar days is allowed for necessary evidence with timely notice
and independent challenge. Preserve any stronger or earlier rule.
Staff or evidence failure, refusal of an optional tool and transfer
do not restart the clock. Keep safe due support active while lawful
assessment, consent or review remains unresolved. A missed deadline
creates an exposed duty and remedy, with no automatic diagnosis,
placement, funding or loss of rights.

**ED36 — Carry usable support into the next school.** Under XR04 and FT,
the receiving school offers comparable safe support from the first
affected teaching opportunity using the existing plan or sufficient
lawful evidence. Confirm teacher, aids, appropriate place, travel,
scope and the first actual service. The responsible public owners
reconcile who pays and accepts under prior law and carry unresolved
work and original dates. A source or district dispute cannot suspend
due support. Use lawful protected copies and the learner's account
to set suitable interim action while original documents are sought.
Do not impose a diagnostic retest or accept a reduced timetable
because a payment or transfer record is missing. A genuine changed
need may be assessed and decided on its own facts.

**ED37 — Budget initial adjustments, specialist work and transfer cover.**
At adoption and each budget, the competent bodies price routine
classroom support, independent assessment, actual qualified staffing,
transport, specialist aids and accepted alternates. Show how one
shared slot is used across present plans and new urgent barriers.
Within existing duties use prior-law priorities based on harm, useful
time and relevant need, with reasons and direct challenge. A final
plan or an older claim does not by itself decide every conflict.
No displaced learner, unpaid carer or unqualified AI supplies a
missing slot. Name uncovered work, use the available qualified
interim route and seek the competent funded correction. Keep
educational and clinical finance distinct.

**ED38 — Verify both learning and remedy.** Give the learner access to a
named public owner and the outside inspector or court without awaiting
a final diagnosis. Verify the first and later use of each material
support, not just placement or paperwork. Review whether measures
enable participation without forced disclosure or segregation.
Retain old loss and separately commissioned recovery under ED29–ED33.
AI may reconcile dates and flag double bookings. It cannot diagnose,
issue a plan or certify a child's receipt. Introduce the rules
prospectively with paid staff, original claims, continuing protections
and an offline route. Reopen on repeated delays, unequal support,
failed transfers or a simpler equivalent path.
