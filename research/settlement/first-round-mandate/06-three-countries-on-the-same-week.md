# Three country arrangements in the same week

*Design535 · Constructed comparison of authority, actual receipt and policy choice*

An incumbent loses office under a lawful, effective result. At the handover,
the common digital operator stops serving public offices. A national transfer
misses its first local cutoff. A resident staying in a second locality is
already owed income and portable home support. A second resident is owed an
ordinary visit by the same host. The opposition needs its own original evidence
and an executable remedy. The successor wants to switch the host to a qualified
local provider after the existing contract expires. None of these choices may
delay a legal deadline, assume consent or cancel a valid inherited claim.

The exercise compares three architectures with identical underlying rights,
people, places, due dates and inherited obligations. The stronger repaired
predecessor can enact any specific qualified repair available under its own
law. Its opponents and independent institutions receive the same protections.
The figures and agreements below are stipulated, including the lawful
appropriations. The example does not estimate the national cost of any rival.

## Let the alternatives reverse the choice

Under those assumptions all three preserve the protected acts. The second
is the preferred **scope-specific choice** if the local electorate's
provider choice is valuable and plural ownership is preferred within the
stated costs. The direct-public option can make an elected change too. It
spends one extra first-window unit and preserves choice among separately
qualified public teams. It can win on different cost, reliability or
resident-choice evidence. The institutional choice cannot be inferred
solely from which spreadsheet balances.

- If strong repair can price the same qualified switch at one unit in the
  second window, it can also make the change for 13. Its first-window cost is
  lower. Retain strong repair for this comparison absent a demonstrated added
  benefit from changing the institutions. If the inherited polity can
  separately authorize and fund the same residual-payer reform, give it
  credit for the same cost and receipt. That improvement alone cannot
  justify wider constitutional replacement.
- If the plural route needs one extra unit in the second window for a valid
  license or a separately staffed accepted handoff, its protection still fits
  at 12. The proposed switch then costs 14 in total and must wait. The
  direct-public route can still change public teams at 13. Where that change
  meets the same intended resident choice and public purpose, prefer it for
  the tested scope if the first-window cost of 14 is funded.
- If usable first-window funding is 12, only strong repair fits as specified.
  This branch removes two eligible care-side units and preserves the seven
  restricted units for income and civic work.
  The plural and direct-public continuity schedules are short by one and two
  units. A common emergency appropriation cannot change those numbers unless
  each lawful source and receiver actually qualifies. Before new commitments
  bind, a competent body can retain the repaired route if still available on
  its own exact terms. If a duty is live and the route fails, preserve the
  obligation, fund available relief and report the person and date missed.
- If the alternate care receiver can verify the visiting resident's award
  only through the withdrawn operator, its funded visit does not qualify in
  any architecture using that receiver. Where the independently recorded
  income and the host's ordinary visit still arrive, record those two
  successes alongside the missed visiting care and the failed combination
  of timely income and care for the visiting resident. An AI-generated
  reconstruction cannot authorize payment or care. The resident can use
  the applicable direct claim and review route.
- If the new provider has all funds and staff but lacks the resident's
  required assent, keep the valid existing award and reachable ordinary
  route. A copied preference or previous consent cannot authorize the
  switch. Preserve the original deadline and report any actual gap.

This is an ordinary lawful transfer of office. Delayed finance and failed
