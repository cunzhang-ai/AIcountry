# Decision record for Design461

**Baseline and procedure.** Use Design460 and the [research method](../FRAMEWORK.md).
[Baseline hashes](baseline.json) preserve the predecessor. Use existing
intake, scheduling, professional, payment and review authority.

**Selection.** Where the issue is circular confirmation, allow
ordinary competent actors to use exact conditional reservations
within their lawful scope. Where an actual safety or service act is
missing, require a qualified first act or hold the dependent move.
Name a sponsor to secure dependencies for a planned change. Seek an
exact elected source and responsibility when no lawful owner covers
an uncovered step. [Selected rules](../country/C04/06/10-cross-sector-obligation-ownership.md).

**Reasons and objection.** Independent tasks can all be offered and
still leave a person without a usable route. Coordination uses staff
and ties up real slots, so a qualified ordinary first step or
accessible route may be preferable. A jointly funded interface
has an incremental cost. A single operator might lower coordination
costs but needs independent review and its own accountable source.
Neither coordination nor integration creates missing qualifications.

**Compatibility and consumers.** Preserve AP, PSW, MF, sector
law and original court and budget jurisdiction. Each sector
retains consent, receipt, time, standing duties and any binding
act. Keep protected facts with competent receivers. A new cross-sector
schedule needs authority and evidence of accepted paid capacity.

**Limits and reopening.** The comparison uses authored dates,
prices and staffing. Actual authority, affected participation,
independent review and useful-time receipt remain open. Reopen
on recurring circular failures, incomplete prior routes, missed
service or a less burdensome funded alternative.
