# Constitutional review: documentary sources and review limits

*Design 80; limited source inspection, not comparative causal proof or legal advice.*

The [official German court Act](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/englisch_bverfgg/englisch_bverfgg.html)
was inspected in English, with translation metadata including the amendment of
22 December 2025. Sections 2–8, 15 and 18–19 illustrate concrete choices about
composition, qualification, appointment, quorum, voting and recusal. Germany
uses two eight-member senates; its Act includes holdover and a delayed transfer
of election power between electoral organs. CJ deliberately selects neither
holdover nor source transfer. Textual comparison cannot show which is safer.

[Current German Basic Law Article 93](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gg/art_93.html)
was separately inspected in German. It now contains composition/term rules,
including twelve years, no reelection and holdover. This guards against using
an older article number as current law. The full English Basic Law retrieval
failed; no complete Basic Law or conformity review is claimed. These two German
texts are one institutional family, not independent empirical observations.

[South African Chapter 8](https://www.justice.gov.za/legislation/constitution/chp08.html)
was returned in an initial open; the inspected appointment/removal material
includes executive involvement and a two-thirds Assembly removal resolution.
The page marks its text 5 July 2021; repeat retrievals timed out. This is partial,
dated documentary comparison, not current consolidated certification.
The court's [role page](https://www.concourt.org.za/index.php/about-us/role)
was also inspected. It describes an eight-judge quorum and acting judges, but
its constitutional-only jurisdiction account and old court names signal stale
material. Exclude it as support for current South African jurisdiction. No
freshness claim or wholesale transplant follows an official domain alone.

The exact [source register](../../2026-10-final-constitutional-review/sources.json)
records access/failures and exclusions. No publisher-byte archive, full national
case-law review, empirical appointment/capture study or cost/workload sample was
made. These documents show institutional possibilities and differences, not
observed democratic superiority or a mandate to enact this package.

Root and task-agent analysis share one AI controller and sources. Fresh internal
reviews found renewal/exclusivity, staggered election, opposition succession,
selector recusal, interim-expiry, removal-appeal, exceptional-jurisdiction and
funding-attribution defects; the current draft corrects them. They are not
independent qualified judicial review or actual affected-party participation.
No residents, litigants, judges, defenders, minorities or public selectors were
consulted, appointed or represented by these agents.

Nine seats, twelve years, mixed nine-member selectors, public thirty-six,
fixed thresholds, time limits and finite reserves are normative hypotheses.
They require legitimate choice and contextual workload/qualification review.
Neither source agreement nor artifact validation establishes legal authority,
practical independence, usable cash, received remedies or better human lives.
