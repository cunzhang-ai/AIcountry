## Important revision: insurance belongs to an actual bank debtor

The initial draft's connected-group12B cap could mix a first failed bank's T0
with later U/term/FX claims or accelerate healthy sisters. [The unit choice](coverage-unit-choice.md)
compares that with rolling-group and lifetime caps. Select **12B per actual
beneficiary per licensed legal bank** prospectively; each bank's objective first
qualifying event crystallizes its eligible accounts/term principal and lawful
accrued interest for insurance only. Estate maturity/rank and future unearned
interest do not change. Healthy separate banks remain privately payable; later
bank failures have their own T_bank/B/FX/cap/advance. Ownership/receiver labels
cannot reset one old bank debt or pay it twice.

This increases possible C: two connected licensed banks each owing4,000 to one
person can require8,000 rather than former group6,000 with B500. No automatic
L increase, free source or claim of unchanged protection. M08 must price/fund
all per-bank exposure, consolidated control risk and prospective license/growth.
A licensed member's funding-bar breach does not strip an otherwise eligible
claim; expose the shortfall and require lawful source/containment, not retroactive
uninsurance. Unlicensed outsider/nondeposit product cannot create a state promise
by advertisement alone; mislabeling an actual insured bank debt defeats none.

Post-commencement legal consolidations have six-calendar-month nonadditive
notice/exit protection: max(normal merged-bank cover, capped remaining predecessor
legacy), not sum. Current failed-bank T_bank B/FX values live components; original
crystallized claims stay original. Traced predecessor external withdrawal reduces
its ledger; pooled withdrawal consumes nonlegacy first then reduces legacy pro
rata to remaining pool, no recharge. Internal transfers create no bucket. Prior
consolidations/paid or crystallized claims gain no new grace. These detailed
hypotheses preserve actual higher prior guarantees and need paid tracing/exit.
