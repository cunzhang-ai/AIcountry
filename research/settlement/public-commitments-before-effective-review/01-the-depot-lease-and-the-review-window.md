# The depot lease and the review window

This constructed case tests [PCR01–PCR08](../../country/C03/01/02-public-commitments-before-effective-review.md).
A council considers a 20-year depot lease. A separate five-unit allocation
pays already due wages and operation. An optional two-unit allocation permits
one unit for independent site assessment and one for a recoverable reservation
under an enforceable return arrangement. No existing duty depends on the lease.

A public announcement on working day 0 omits the actual binding terms.
Usable final notice comes on day 3. The ten complete following working days
are 4 through 13. The first eligible release day is 14 if all other conditions
hold. Assume any equivalent sector interval ends by day 13. A stronger
sector interval through day 15 would move first release to day 16.

| Proposed act | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Independent assessment on day 4 within its lawful source | May proceed |
| Fully recoverable reservation on day 4 with no exclusive control or material exit liability | May proceed under the stated terms |
| Four-unit nonrefundable deposit and early exclusive possession on day 6 | Block the release despite calling the signature preliminary |
| Covered release on day 14, with an effective court order through day 17 | Block the release while the order binds |
| Ordinary wages and operation due on day 6 | Pay five units from their original allocation |

An affected user applies on day 10. The application alone does not extend
the waiting interval. In a branch with narrow interim relief served on day
12, the officer or a lawful alternate must receive it in time to block the
day 14 transfer. If both paths fail and the transfer occurs, report the
actual premature transfer and seek restoration. The order alone is no proof
of execution. Earliest potential release in that branch is day 18, conditional
on actual authority and any further merits order. A genuine material
revision with usable notice on day 8 gives the changed act a fresh window
through day 18. It does not disturb unrelated acts or the five due units.

The case compares timing, exposure and actual receiving roles. It does not
establish a real contract's validity, costs or staff capacity.
