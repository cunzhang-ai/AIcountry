# Elected public risk, foreign constraints and lawful transition

*M15–M16; part of [C06/24](../24-financial-law-and-loss-allocation.md).*

**M15 — No technical bailout or silent redenomination.** Treasury's default
automatic overdraft is zero. Any direct monetary credit, public development
subsidy, new indemnity, guarantee, bridge capital or enlarged insurance line
needs prior Assembly statute/appropriation with purpose, maximum exposure,
actual source, maturity, price, repayment, incidence and ultimate loss bearer.
A statutory M08 draw needs no new case vote within its actual authorized
funded ceiling; moving beyond it does. Neither tax arrears, bank assets nor
monetary FX reserves are uncommitted Treasury cash. The public-claims account,
deposit backstop, bridge and existing services remain distinct obligations.

Ordinary bank liquidity is time-limited, secured and only for independently
assessed solvent viable banks; capital/liquidity ratios alone do not prove
solvency. A doubtful bank gets prompt assessment and separate M09 protection,
not rolling liquidity that conceals owner loss. Publish collateral valuation,
concentration, pricing, exposure and any narrowly delayed counterparties.
Fiscal indemnity/monetary recapitalization beyond enacted risk needs elected
approval; central-bank loss cannot be presumed nonexistent because its unit
can issue currency. Missing currency, confidence, resources and acceptance
remain real constraints, with due claims and no automatic entitlement cuts.

Retain the managed float, no guaranteed conversion and separately owned FX.
A domestic bank order does not alter foreign governing law, collateral,
foreign branches or another jurisdiction's guarantee. Seek actual recognition/
cooperation under B41–B52 and specific financial instruments; disclose refusal.
Capital-flow/conversion restrictions need a separate finite rights-reviewed
Act, not M13, an AI priority or a payment provider's hidden queue. Keep lawful
existing denominations unless consent or valid law with remedies changes them.

**M16 — The new law must not approve its own launch.** Before commencement,
affected public/practitioner review and competent adoption must settle actual
jurisdiction, license perimeter, valid E/X/L/O, independent accounting,
B coverage, protected beneficiaries, insurer fund/financing, court/receiver
capacity, cyber/vendor failure and all actual public contingent exposures.
Numbers here make the candidate contestable; they are not validated capacity.
[NB01–NB36](../44-nonbank-finance-and-client-protection.md) now selects distinct
nonbank title/promise/failure, insurance/private-pension and market/CCP rules;
line-specific actuarial calibration/operating tables remain unfinished. No
inherited bank approval or automatic public guarantee fills them.

Map existing deposits, coverage/guarantees, contracts, securities, security,
netting, tax finality, pending claims, provider licenses and accrued rights
individually to lawful successors. Prospective ratios need paid transition
and a safe compliant route; no retrospective penalty for an old lawful holding.
The new bank hierarchy cannot silently subordinate old claims. Existing
protections continue until lawfully changed, without automatic extension of
new emergency powers or cancellation of unreceived remedies.

Do not reapprove the old country book: its bank sovereign position 250 with
E100 exceeds M03 by 150 in its own fictional units. Reopen the source package,
compare new funded equity/diversified holders with public safe payments and
narrow banking, and retain or displace the plural-finance candidate explicitly.
A new allocation without actual source/capacity evidence does not close this
choice. Use current [design status](../../../DESIGN-STATUS.md) for next priorities;
this old study's queue is not an independent current country agenda.
