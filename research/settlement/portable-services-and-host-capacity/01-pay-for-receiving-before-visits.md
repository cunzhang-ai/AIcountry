# Fund the receiving shift before counting portable visits

*Design476 · Constructed operating comparison with separately scoped
historical source context.*

The smallest unit that can be hired and kept ready is often a shift,
a vehicle or an accessible room. The smallest unit billed after use
may be one visit. The [existing recurring service case](../cross-local-continuity/03-recurring-places-and-portable-support.md)
checks an accepted booking and a late change. Here the uncertainty is
earlier: can anyone keep the accessible destination staffed when
people already have portable rights but no individually confirmed move?

The [Copenhagen reimbursement account](../intergovernmental-reimbursement/01-observed-funder-locality-and-receipt.md)
distinguishes a municipality's payment duty and later intergovernmental
recovery. The former office reports 5,142,875 DKK in items needing
action, of which 4,666,195 DKK were older than a year. These are
different duties and amounts from this exercise. They support separate
treatment of accumulated receivables and actual cash. They supply no
rate, staffing observation or evidence of late individual receipt.

## Two weeks, one available team and three real funding arrangements

In this example a receiving area can contract for one rostered,
accessible service block per week. It pays 6 units before each
window for keeping the qualified worker, vehicle access and a
suitable site independently available. Actual covered visits cost
2 more each. The block can deliver at most three visits. Residents
retain the same underlying rights and direct independent review.
Prior local services and their already committed resources are
outside, and protected from, the illustrative extra envelope.
All prices and capacity are authored. Assume no other sector
duty is lost to staffing the block.

One covered visitor uses week 1. Three need week 2. The two weeks
therefore cost 8 and 12, or 20 gross. Payment by episode alone at
a blended rate of 5 also looks like 20 over both weeks. It yields
5 and 15 only after actual visits, while the first fixed bill of 6
is payable ahead of them. The host has no unrestricted bridge in
this fixture. A later high-volume week cannot make the first offer
accepted or repay the price of a missed visit. The host is not
required to take a provider's unsecured credit.

| Funding arrangement | What can actually qualify |
|---|---|
| Pay only for completed visits | Equal nominal two-week spending would leave the first fixed payment uncovered. Keep this option where there is independently financed ordinary capacity, or fully qualified working capital and accepted delivery. |
| Fixed and usage payments under one audited local or joint compact | Reserve 6 each week before the roster deadline, then pay 2 per actual visit. Record any unused staffed availability and pay only legitimate covered costs. The example fits the same 20-unit cap across both weeks, with spending of 8 and 12. |
| Voluntary shared regional delivery, or a separately financed national sector route | Qualifies only when the proposed service owner has powers, funds and independent accepting providers. For example, a funded fixed 5 plus 2 per visit could use 18 over the same weeks. A bid alone shows no compatible journeys, worker or availability. |

Select the fixed and usage payments on the stated evidence. If
one more person already entitled to care needs that second
window, the three-visit maximum exposes a specific missed need.
A separate competent operator offering one accepted visit for 5
helps only if its cash, service and access can be obtained in
time. Use applicable sector urgency and protections, including
the initial local users. Absence of data on origin is not a
priority rule. A qualified wider or regional path can displace
the chosen route. Consider retaining ordinary local access
when demand is low, and compare opportunities forgone from idle
capacity, cancellation, restricted use and another protected
group that cannot use the proposed site.

## Reconcile the payer's invoices and preserve direct review

If 4 units of week-1 fixed cost came from an expressly
authorized sector advance for that same invoice, the later full
6-unit final allowance for that invoice leaves only 2 to pay
the provider once the advance has actually reached it. Any public
repayment or replenishment is separately recorded under existing
authority. A lawful unused availability payment is labelled
as such. A fixed payment already covered by general
equalization cannot be charged again under the compact.
Pay and independently verify the usage lines in their own scope.
In a separate liquidity check, two 2-unit draws exhaust a 4-unit
revolving advance. An unpaid approval to reimburse either draw
leaves nothing for a third call. A funded ordinary compact or other
lawfully settled payment can cover that call on its actual date.
Do not require a new permanent address, confirmation from the
origin or a household advance for a covered episode.

When a host reports a funded block but cannot accept a resident,
keep the accountable host and origin duties under their ordinary
scope. Give the person an accessible independent route with the
original deadline. The funder may dispute or correct the capacity
invoice on its own route. An audit decision alone cannot supply
a worker, stop a lawful service or change who governs the locality.

## When a bridge becomes a permanent staffing problem

A separate LD exercise starts on day 1. It assumes properly
reviewed activation and enough lawful cash, review and accepted
providers for the due visits on days 12 and 31. The same
unresolved local payment failure also exposes a new resident's
visit on day 61. This is a distinct case from the 20-unit compact.
The first period covers days 1–30. Day 31 is within scope only
with the single lawful extension covering days 31–60. A new
arrival supplies no new period for the same failure. Protect
their day-61 claim and any ordinary available urgent delivery.
If evidence establishes that the first route was restored and
a distinct later failure occurred, test its actual cause, powers,
available workers and funds separately. Keep both dated records.

Before expiry compare funded local repair, a voluntary joint
compact and independently reviewable ordinary sector provision
authorized by the elected bodies. Use the actual appointed
payer and accepted next service, and budget a lawful handback.
A failed vote or missed handback remains a visible service gap
with direct remedies. Local elections, opposition and unrelated
powers continue on their own authority.

If a national payer caused the failure by withholding an undisputed
transfer, address that act in parallel. Pay the transfer through the
unconflicted route and restore lawful local receiving. Independently
fund and execute any earlier qualifying visit if the transfer cannot
reach it in time. An emergency visit gives no right to deduct an
unrelated due transfer or claim credit for delivering it. Review the
root and recipient separately.
