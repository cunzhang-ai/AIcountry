# LF13–LF16: paid civilian protection and a replaceable model

*Design 97 research selection; no enacted power or proven readiness.*

**LF13 — Relief without presumptive guilt or a command veto.** People may seek immediate care,
safe separation, record preservation/correction, independent investigation, B13/B17 urgent
restraint of an unlawful method and delivered B18/B19 compensation/restoration under the actual
civil test. Neither a camera gap nor a machine flag automatically proves the officer's crime,
consent, innocence or the victim's false report. Applicable enacted K offenses, including
K01/K02/K05/K11/K12 where their elements are proved, retain P fault, defenses, disclosure and
independent prosecutor/court process; this chapter adds no criminal offense, V fine, personal
strict liability or court power to command a missing worker. A lawful temporary removal from
weapons/custody access for evidenced safety uses C08/04's fair review and paid replacement, not
a guilt declaration. Victims and affected dependants keep confidential participation and direct
remedy routes even if the prosecutor does not charge. Public officials are not immunized by a
superior's unlawful instruction or a contractor label.

**LF14 — Workers, alternatives and actual receiving.** Assembly/C06 funds trained unarmed and
specialist civilian response, interpreters, child and disability support, safe transport,
de-escalation time, protective equipment, medical receiving, officer first aid, independent
source custody, forensics, prosecutors, defense, courts and delivered remedies in the same
whole-country resource book. A crisis clinician or community mediator cannot be conscripted as
an unpaid police substitute, nor can an unarmed worker be forced to take unbounded danger.
Record time, travel, simultaneous shifts and shared vendors once. A lack of safe response
capacity is an exposed unmet public duty and funding choice. Understaffing alone supplies no
force ground; actual immediate danger still receives the LF02–LF04 assessment. Staff may
report/refuse manifestly unlawful force through protected ordinary work and outside-command
channels, subject to fair examination of actual duties and feasible alternatives. Community
safety without coercion receives real participation and outcome comparison, not a decorative
referral in a police plan.

**LF15 — Commencement without a weapons amnesty.** Before adopting the new ceiling, inventory
current force powers, warrants/orders, equipment/contract rights, qualification, injuries and
pending investigations, source records, union/worker claims, people owed care or civil
execution, and receiving services. Select commencement of the new ceiling and reporting/remedy
duties 180 calendar days after the Act, with paid preparation and device-specific qualification
before use; readiness cannot move that legal date. Existing stronger rights apply meanwhile. The
new Act does not retroactively create a defense, offense, seizure, arrest or immunity, revive a
barred claim, pause a court order or erase a victim's prior protection. Existing valid source
law and stronger rights continue until authorized revision; unsafe untrained new deployment does
not start merely because a date has passed. A missed readiness gate is reported with lawful old
protections and direct remedies, never a minister's silence-based force mandate. Crosslink P/B,
C08/04/09/10/21, C10/01/03, PD, C04 workers and C06 staffing/finance; no duplication of the
external investigator or a new national police commander is presumed.

**LF16 — Compare and revise the protection model.** The reference selects the positive ceiling
plus plural civilian care and safety services. Retaining generic necessity and after-the-fact
punishment saves institutional cost but leaves a commander and public unable to know legal
operational limits before irreversible harm. Moving much crisis, youth and neighborhood work to
unarmed independent civil/peer teams may reduce coercion and build trust, but needs paid
staffing, safe dispatch, actual acceptance and a distinct response to imminent serious violence.
A fully armed central safety command may shorten dispatch but concentrates physical, data and
political power and is not selected by a model speed estimate. Compare source-backed injury,
false force, unserved danger, response time, legitimacy across groups, worker safety, complaints
reaching independent review and received remedy; one number cannot settle whose liberty bears
the cost. Reopen if force is renamed as care/civil execution, officers or civilians are left
unsafe, an outside investigator lacks sources, opposition is chilled, or a complete lower-force
alternative works better. These are research hypotheses, not actual public authorization or
proof of superior lives.
