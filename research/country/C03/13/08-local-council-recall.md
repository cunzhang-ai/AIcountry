# Let residents recall a local council without breaking proportional representation

*Design335 · LRC01–LRC05 · Proposed ordinary local constitutional rules.*

**LRC01 — Give the local electorate one midterm opportunity.** For the
ordinary four-year EL council, select a single neutral petition window
opening 18 calendar months after the original term begins and lasting
90 calendar days. A current eligible local elector can submit support
without a fee, party sponsor, council vote or misconduct finding.
No organizer owns the question or blocks other lawful supporters.
Keep the question available throughout the window. The independently
verified eligible opening electorate supplies the denominator, corrected
for source error on that date. Count each actually eligible signer at
the time of assent once, with safe revocation until close, assisted
offline access, protected originals and independent challenge. At least
15 percent of the opening electorate, rounded up, triggers the poll.
The administrative result is due within 30 calendar days of close,
with direct useful-time review of errors or withholding. Resignation,
new list labels and repeat polls do not restart the original term window.

**LRC02 — Vote on recall and the conditional successor separately.**
A positive verified petition calls the poll automatically under prior
law. Set final close at certification plus 90 calendar days and
first voting 14 days earlier. Fit ordinary early-call entry and
public support before that first vote. The recall close must precede first voting in the next ordinary election.
Late withholding remains reviewable, with no extension of the ordinary
election timetable. The ballot has a yes/no
question on ending the entire council's current mandate and a separate
full EL proportional council choice. Electors may answer either or
both independently. Apply PC's referendum safeguards to the recall
question and ordinary local list and campaign safeguards to the
replacement ballot. Preserve public opposition, donor limits and
equal candidate terms. Do not charge duplicate shared costs or let
party, government or vendor credentials control either track.

**LRC03 — Require an affirmative public mandate to end the term early.**
Recall succeeds only if valid yes votes exceed valid no votes and
reach at least 25 percent of the corrected opening electorate,
rounded up. Blank ballots do not count as either choice. If recall
fails, the ordinary council continues and the conditional ballot
confers no mandates. If recall succeeds, end the old mandates on
usable lawful recall certification and apply the valid EL result
to the full statutory council size for the remaining original term.
Current members can stand on equal terms subject to ordinary law.
A council-wide poll can displace capable opposition members as well
as a governing majority. Preserve proportional entry and seats at
replacement. Do not use this route to remove an individual list seat,
alter a map, remove national legislators or override protected status.

**LRC04 — Preserve democratic and service continuity if a count fails.**
Qualification, recall and replacement remain separately reviewable.
Use ER16 and EL20 only within their expressly adopted scopes.
A final finding may retain one question and order only the defective
one repeated when lawfully severable. For this paired poll, apply
EL20's final-judgment plus 90 days close and 14-day voting interval
to a required repeat of either unchanged question. A repeat must
close before first voting in the fixed ordinary election. If it
cannot, report the unresolved midterm choice and preserve the
ordinary election and applicable review, with no renewed term. If recall is valid but a replacement
cannot be ascertained, prior-law limited caretaker and career duties
continue until a lawful successor, without legislative powers for
recalled members. If recall itself remains unresolved, no result is
inferred and the incumbent's ordinary lawful status follows the
applicable orders and original expiry. Protect the ordinary election,
lawful payroll, services, records, creditors and pending remedies.
No council or executive can use a declared vacancy to choose its own
substitute legislature. An actually completed valid recall vote is
the only one of its kind in the term. Judicial correction and repeats
are part of that vote.

**LRC05 — Pay for an independent process and preserve other remedies.**
The adopting law and budget name funded petition access, qualification,
campaign help, recall and replacement ballots, review, any repeat and
actual handover. Keep ordinary elections, council confidence and
independent wrongdoing or rights proceedings available. Recall is a
political choice, not a conviction or disqualification. AI may detect
duplicates for human verification or check count arithmetic. It cannot
decide eligibility, infer assent or certify a successor. Adopt only
prospectively under applicable protected-succession rules. The
thresholds and dates are design choices. Reopen on strategic petition
obstruction, exclusion, minority displacement, unsafe interruption
or a better accountability design.

The [matched recall case](../../../settlement/local-council-recall/01-midterm-petition-and-conditional-election.md)
tests one window, both thresholds and continuity if replacement fails.
Actual authority, funded access and outcomes remain open.
