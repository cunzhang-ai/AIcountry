# Design237 decision record. Product repair and material responsibility

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned Design236](baseline.json)

## Decision

Select [LCY01–LCY12](../country/C09/26-product-repair-and-material-responsibility.md).
The predecessor covers product defects, safe treatment, industrial
closure and procurement continuity. It leaves ordinary repair
after purchase, controlled software tools and product-cohort
return funding without a joined duty. Add a prospective
responsible first placer, an independent support path, secured
take-back and actual material receiving. Use the
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

The seven-year initial support term is an authored candidate for
specified classes. Prospective class law must qualify it for
actual safety, household burden and capacity. Functional
continuation matters where devices support real services. Keep
defect and warranty liability, IP, qualification and safety
distinct. No evidence that collected material was recovered
follows from paying for collection.

## Real choices

| Alternative | Strong reason to retain it | Material exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Keep consumer remedies and public collection, use grants for repair | Simple for small suppliers and can reach legacy goods. Preserves existing safe operators. | A buyer may still lack parts, and public finance can absorb recurring costs without influence over new design or exit. |
| Secure product-cohort responsibility and protect independent repair | Gives buyers a real choice and funds return before a seller leaves. Can preserve function and reduce new input. | Fees can raise initial prices. Pooling can entrench sellers or repair chains. Safe operation, workforce and source need real review. |
| Price waste and rely on replacement with improved treatment | Can be appropriate where safe repair is uneconomic, unsafe or energy intensive. | A disposal charge alone does not create parts, repair access or usable replacements and can displace downstream harm. |
| Public or mutual device ownership and long-term service | Can control design, repair and end use directly. Useful for some essential fleets. | Title, capital, support, plural exit and user choice require paid capacity. Mandatory leasing can make people dependent on a new owner. |

Select the second with a separate public route for legacy goods
and urgent gaps. Strong public or mutual provision may be best for
some covered uses. Keep independent repair open even when a pool
finances disposal. A recipient can decline a proposed repair.
The [100-device case](../settlement/product-lifecycle/02-one-hundred-devices-and-two-ledgers.md)
tests the comparative cost, failure of shared tools, qualified
capacity, due assurance and unverified exports in separate ledgers.
It does not establish general cost, carbon or material savings.

## Authority and transition

The Assembly chooses classes and source obligations. The existing
waste steward owns qualified receiving. Independent consumer and
technical authorities and ordinary courts retain their respective
remedy and review. Apply B/TS/TD, SC, BC, lawful import and waste
classification, and existing insolvency priorities. Preserve
retail, warranty, worker, creditor and environmental claims and
paid FT/OP/T successors. Record separate control of the assurance,
tools and treatment records. Keep future adopted obligations
prospective and existing permissions and law within their terms.
Models can aid batch matching and cost comparison without
certifying safety, appointing a receiver or deciding liability.

## Next bounded evidence and reopening

Compare one actual covered class with a qualified independent
repair route and a credible replacement pathway. Follow paid
work, software continuity, sourcing, ordinary users, controlled
funds and actual return through a supplier-exit stress. Obtain
protected worker and user input, independently verified downstream
records and measured physical results. Reopen on an unsafe
shortcut, burdens shifted abroad, blocked repair, unaffordable
access, insufficient security or a better complete alternative.
Actual adoption, funds, affected participation and better outcomes
remain open.
