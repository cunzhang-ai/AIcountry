# Property, actual support and a finite estate

*Part of [family/civil journeys](README.md); authored quantities and cases.*

| Case | Proposed result and unresolved actual delivery |
|---|---|
| J13 A shared acquisition worth 180 has valid debt 30 and three equal participants | Net property is 150, each share 50. If one dies with separate assets 70, their gross estate is 120, not 220 or the whole 180. Identify actual liens, authority and contributions; this arithmetic proves neither the asset value nor the legal shares. |
| J14 The 120 estate pays debts 40, reasonable costs 5 and a lawful dependant provision 15 | Residue 60, assuming actual valid sources/priority and applicable tax zero only in this illustration. With two qualifying partners and two descendant branches, partner pool30 gives15 each; descendant pool30 gives15 per branch. Two grandchildren in a deceased child's branch get7.5 each. Partners do not each inherit half the entire residue. |
| J15 An insolvent estate has assets100, valid secured debt60, costs5, priority maintenance25 and ordinary claims50 | Under the stated U priority assumptions, remaining10 gives ordinary creditors20% recovery; heirs receive zero and do not personally inherit the missing40. Collateral, actual U wage/tax/other priorities and costs need proof; this is not a general estate-solvency certificate. |
| J16 A child needs1,200 and has200 actual non-means-tested resources; obligors have safe surplus1,200 and500 | Remaining need1,000 allocates705.882/294.118 before rounding. If first obligor already safely delivered300 toward that amount, remaining cash is405.882/294.118; count the300 once. These are different authored prices from old macro cohorts, not a new national support schedule. |
| J17 The same child has only100 combined available obligor surplus | Cap total prospective private payment at100; show900 uncovered, with immediate I/S receiving and no fictional receipt. Unpaid existing arrears remain separate. A public means-tested bridge does not make the private duty disappear or authorize clawback that removes the child's essentials. |
| J18 A new tie, an estate and multiple survivors each claim the same public pension | Y09 keeps one loss-proportioned pot; estate assets and ongoing I/S rights remain distinct. A new compact/agent account is not an extra pot, and private future inheritance is not cash received today. |
| J19 An AI reconstructs a will and a benefiting caregiver authenticates it alone | B33 needs actual voluntary operative intent and independent reliable formation evidence; the inference is not a will. Protect actual originals and contrary proof. A competent court, not family rank or a vendor's current summary, resolves the dispute. |
| J20 A missing person is declared dead, then returns after good-faith distributions | B36 requires specific evidence/search and judicial process, not elapsed time alone. Restore live identity now; adjudicate remaining assets, innocent reliance, protected support and child safety separately. A civil death record cannot close an official-disappearance inquiry. |
| J21 A fraudulent title edit dispossesses a holder while an innocent buyer relies on official certification | Restore lawful ownership through hearing; buyer's seller/wrongdoer and B38 official-certification loss claims are separate. Treasury is the named public-risk payer only for proved official certification loss, not every private fraud. No presumed funded assurance reserve or evicting from an unresolved match. |
| J22 An estate or employer relies on a deadline despite blocking meaningful notice/access | Apply B37's specific period, actual discovery and reasoned B15 tolling. No claim is lost through an impossible responsible-office handoff. Long periods do not reverse proof or mandate permanent public testimony; criminal Q12 remains a different clock. |
| J23 A relative keeps a competent adult's vote, keys and medical records during asset custody | B23/B39 gives no guardianship over the person. Direct agency, safe clinical/privacy access and urgent independent correction remain; status or a family signature is not consent. Original custody scope and actual executor must be checked. |
| J24 A court orders care/compensation but the shared budget has no available receiver/cash | Preserve the claim, actual safe interim receiving and independent remedy; elected C06 funding action is required. A judgment, ledger or later insurer recovery is not delivery. Do not claim readiness by moving the loss to an unconsulted household or future taxpayer. |
