# C08. Coercive power and official abuse

*Part of [C08 everyday law and justice](../C08-everyday-law-and-justice.md); integrated design 61 research, not enacted law.*

The [LF01–LF16 force ceiling](24-lawful-force-and-civilian-response.md) now supplies the second necessity/means test for an independently lawful task, including B18 civil execution. It uses this existing outside-command chain; it adds no search, arrest, clinical or military power.

## Coercive power and official abuse

The same enacted rights floor follows coercive power across police, intelligence,
border control, detention, emergency units and contractors. Search, surveillance,
detention, force, movement restrictions, coercive denial of essential resources and
compelled disclosure each require a specific lawful purpose, authorizer, record,
limit, independent review and remedy. Changing a uniform, software system or
contract cannot create a new power. Contractors cannot receive sovereign
detention, protest-control or lethal-force authority by procurement. Serious
abuse by protectors requires investigators and charging decisions
outside their command, lawful access to evidence, protected witnesses and an executor
able to carry a court's order into effect. Fair process protects accused officials.
The [crisis-liberty boundary](09-mental-health-crisis-liberty-and-consent.md)
also applies to a clinician, mobile responder or police officer acting on
mental distress: a treatment label cannot reset a custody clock or conceal
force, injury or a death from outside review.

The [public-power abuse decision](../../2026-10-public-power-abuse/decision-record.md)
makes that requirement an operating chain. A person can report to an accessible
service, a court, an ombud or a prosecutor without first obtaining the implicated
commander's permission. A death or serious injury in custody or after official
force, or a missing detainee, triggers prompt preservation and notice to an
investigator outside the implicated command under prior law. A report of
torture, retaliation or evidence obstruction can be lodged through several
independent routes; the implicated unit cannot decide whether it is credible
enough to reach them. An initial evidence hold is distinct from a coercive
search, which requires its own legal grounds and court control. The first
custodian must protect life and preserve the scene, original custody time,
logs, body or facility recordings, weapons, communications and medical
evidence; it cannot retain sole control of the inquiry. The external
investigator has enacted direct-access and urgent court-order routes if the
unit or vendor refuses handover, with a recorded failure if evidence is lost.
Independent medical and forensic access, safe witness contact and defense
evidence access are funded. Privacy protection does not allow the accused
office or its vendor to erase contradictory material.

A case-allocation law gives the external investigator and a prosecutor outside
the implicated command timely authority over evidence and charging. Conflict
checks and a predesignated alternate jurisdiction apply if either is implicated;
the assignment and claimed conflict can be challenged before a court. The
same outside-command rules apply when an investigator or prosecutor is accused;
a minister cannot choose a friendlier investigator after an incident. A
reviewable noncharge decision gives reasons without promising a conviction.
Courts control warrants, liberty, admissibility, guilt and remedies; prosecutors
remain separate from judges. Prior law assigns judges and provides recusal,
reassignment and appeal for a judicial conflict; an allegation of judicial
corruption or evidence suppression uses an external criminal route and a
separate fair disciplinary process. Neither a prosecutor nor cabinet can
replace a judge in a named case or overturn a judgment through discipline.
An officer may be temporarily removed from weapons, custody or evidence
access on stated safety and integrity grounds,
with prompt review and replacement staffing, without treating an allegation
as guilt. The Assembly sets general law and funding and can examine systemic
failure, but cannot direct a named prosecution or acquittal.

The enacted criminal code must distinguish torture, intentional or reckless
unlawful official force or detention, deliberate evidence destruction and
retaliation for protected reporting, and corrupt diversion of public power
or assets from mistake and ordinary professional disagreement. It must state
conduct, fault, causation where relevant, lawful defenses, participation,
command responsibility and maximum sanctions before use. Rank alone is not
criminal guilt; a superior's liability needs a defined act, order, assistance
or culpable failure under an actual duty. Existing general offenses apply to
officials and contractors too. No emergency, superior order, outsourced
service or AI classification silently creates an offense or a defense to
torture. The older core code's numeric maxima remain provisional and are not
adopted by this design revision.

Immediate care, protection, record correction, lawful compensation and a
delivered civil remedy do not wait for criminal conviction; nor does a civil
finding erase the accused person's criminal presumption of innocence. A final
order names the liable state or person, payer, executor, due date and evidence
of actual delivery. Lawful recovery from a wrongdoer is separate from making
the injured person chase an insolvent officer. Audit and protected public
reporting disclose deaths, serious-force events, complaints, independent
investigation, charging, case delay and remedies by relevant group with
small-group reidentification safeguards, without turning sensitive testimony
into a permanent public file. Shared political,
financial or technical control over investigator, prosecutor, forensic source,
court and payment is a critical failure even when the offices have different
names.

Justice funding includes prevention, defense, courts, accessible reporting, safe
accommodation, victim and witness support, forensics, custody, community supervision,
reintegration and enforcement of judgments. A court order or case closure is not
evidence that a person received the remedy. Specialized offenses, sentence ranges,
civil gaps and service standards need reconciliation before any complete-code claim.

[Candidate K11–K12 and P06–P15](12-criminal-core-and-procedure.md) now
specify torture/obstruction elements, actual-duty participation and outside-
command evidence, disclosure, suppression and review. Office rank, failed
service or a diagnosis is not guilt. No quota or self-financing forfeiture
may create a pursuing office's incentive to punish under candidate P20.

[IA/IL/IE](../C10/10-civilian-assessment-and-intelligence-limits.md) now selects civilian strategic warning without a distinct domestic secret service/new noncriminal intrusion. It expressly scopes ordinary civil-record/payment execution and state judgment source for its own actual wrongs; P07, K24, LF, outside-command prosecution and particular offense proof are unchanged, never supplied by an intelligence alert.

[OJ26–OJ30](25-ordinary-justice-and-public-prosecution.md) now supplies written
prosecution control, protected dissent and three preselected external case
prosecutors with independent status/source for implicated-command cases. Activation
follows prior neutral rotation and unimplicated court grounds, not a minister's
post-incident nominee. Existing automatic preservation, P07 warrants, outside-
command investigators, defense/periods and LF ceilings continue; no extra police,
secret civil-supervision or prosecutorial lawmaking power is created.

[PO01–PO24](27-civilian-enforcement-and-public-control.md) now gives this existing
external function its Civilian Investigation Service/employer/Director, separate
police leadership, own-director allocation and actual source/control boundary.
These roles supply no extra investigative/force powers, review monopoly or
presumed independence; original LF/OJ/P/B protections and claims persist.
