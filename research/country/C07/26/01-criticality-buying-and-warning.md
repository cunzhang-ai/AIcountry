# Define the risk, buy capacity and require early warning

*Part of [MQ critical medicines](../26-critical-medicines-and-safe-continuity.md).*

**MQ01 — Protect an actual clinical pathway.** The Assembly defines
the public continuity duty, funding and delegation. The National
Clinical Service (NCS) proposes a reviewed schedule with the separate
TD09 medicines authority and evidence from clinicians, pharmacists,
patients, localities and suppliers. Specify the medicine presentation
and use, harm from interruption, safe treatment window, feasible
alternatives and the receiving routes that depend on it. Include
indications without usable alternatives, low-volume needs and
patients relying on community or remote delivery. Test local failure
even if national supply is adequate. Criticality guides preparation
and response. It neither confers product authorization nor replaces
the ordinary coverage law. Publish reasons, scope and a challenge
route, and refresh the schedule after a material change.

**MQ02 — Buy clinical coverage through independent supply paths.** NCS
purchasing uses the existing SC process to compare retained contracts,
split or nonexclusive awards, rotating stocks, paid surge capacity and
qualified public or cooperative production. Map manufacturer, ingredient,
qualified line, testing and packaging, cold chain, transport, source
currency and relevant intellectual property or know-how. Two brands
or lots with the same relevant failed dependency cannot count as
independent. Reserve enough licensed time, stock and transport for
the named concurrent duties. Pay for verified availability and rotation
separately from accepted units where this prevents an incentive to
increase unnecessary use. Compare price, exposure, actual safe days
and continuity cost. Regional sharing and new manufacture require
their own rights, owned inputs, operators, time and source. Where
independence is unavailable, disclose the shared risk and maintain
an actual last-resort route or report its absence.

**MQ03 — Report while prevention is still possible.** For designated
products require manufacturers, importers and wholesalers to report
credible material loss, discontinuation, quality interruption and
lead-time or demand changes they can substantiate. NCS providers and
dispensing pharmacies report material access and receiving failures.
Report foreseeable events as soon as known. An imminent threat to a
protected window reaches the existing NCS duty officer and TD09
authority within four elapsed hours of identification, and sooner
where useful protection requires it. Other credible material changes
reach them by the next working day. Exact dates are proposed duties
subject to an enacted schedule and funded reception. Give patients
and staff independent, accessible reporting. Reconcile conflicting
source data, protect narrow commercial and patient details and publish
an actionable scope. A good-faith report is not proof of fault,
permission to conceal a defect or an automatic adverse sanction.
