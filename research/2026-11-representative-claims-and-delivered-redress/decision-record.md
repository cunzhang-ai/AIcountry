# Design361 decision on representative claims and delivered redress

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design360, B17–B20, B49, civil court capacity, insolvency and the
[working method](../FRAMEWORK.md). The common code permits representative
claims and protects individual rights. Select an executable opt-in
procedure covering common issues, domestic coordination, protected
filing, settlement review and actual distribution. The project
assistant proposes the 60-day protected period for timely enrolled
claims after a narrowing, denial or lawful withdrawal. The adopting
Assembly sets law and sources. Ordinary courts control certification,
merits and execution within jurisdiction. No case, liability, date
extension or payment is authorized by this research.

## Alternatives and objections

Separate cases preserve individual control and local access, but may
repeat the same proof or make low-value claims too costly. An opt-out
monetary class could reach more people, including people hard to
notify, but risks binding people who never had a usable chance to act.
Keep B20's separately enacted, narrow option open. Choose assisted
opt-in and defined common issues for this design. Maintain independent
urgent relief, ordinary defenses and each separate claim. Costs of
counsel, response and individual proof can erase any efficiency gain.
Compare with direct lawful sector redress and better ordinary intake.

A lead court can centralize control, a sponsor can capture settlement,
and a large group can conceal distinct losses. Require accepting venues,
paid local receiving, conflict review and separate representation when
needed. Protect assent and originals. Keep actual distribution, residual
claims, appeal and independent remedies on their own schedules. No fee
or asserted prior credit becomes a second claim to a member's money.

## Scoped result, transition and next action

The [primary comparators](sources.md) describe common-issue and
settlement safeguards. The [constructed case](../settlement/representative-claims-and-delivered-redress/01-one-common-issue-separate-receipts.md)
checks scoped representation, a narrowing order and actual recovery.
It establishes no actual lawful case, participation, judge time, finance
or recovery. Existing cases, settlements and limitation defenses retain
their lawful scope. Adopt prospectively with a funded receiver, dated
sources and continuing access.

Within an authorized court research budget, compare one type of repeated
low-value harm across two venues. Use protected, lawfully available case
materials and willing affected participants. Measure common and individual
work, notice and actual receipt on the same dates, with ordinary urgent
cases preserved. Stop at a costed comparison and review of one proposed
group boundary. Reopen if notice is unusable, a sponsor can control scope,
local relief is delayed or a simpler remedy reaches more people fairly.
