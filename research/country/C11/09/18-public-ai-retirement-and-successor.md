# DP84–DP88. Keep public services working when an upstream AI product retires

13 November 2026 · Integrated design 443 · Candidate public reliance rules

**DP84 — Contract for advance notice and inspectable version identity.**
For material public reliance, the purchaser specifies how it receives
authenticated notice of retirement, forced updates, loss of support,
permission or upstream service, with effective times, affected interfaces
and usable export and transition assistance. Set the notice period from
the last safe migration date and evidenced workload. The contracted
supplier must pass on upstream notices within its control and identify
unverified dates or dependencies. Procurement independently checks the
effective version or equivalent evidence, including updates behind an
unchanged name. An emergency withdrawal can be necessary under existing
law. Require prompt notice and use the prepared alternate. A notice term
cannot force continued unsafe or unlawful operation.

**DP85 — Keep three dates and the last useful service time separate.**
Record product support or access end, the contract's legal end and the
last date lawful safe use is supported. Keep original service and
appeal dates. At the earliest material risk, identify the actual uses,
pending work, qualified staff and separately controlled alternative.
If useful time is short, use the existing
[essential-service exit](../../C04/05/09-essential-service-exit-and-recovery.md)
and DP38–DP42 routes. A live contract or callable endpoint cannot
extend safe use. Ordinary procurement claims remain enforceable.

**DP86 — Buy a smaller safe bridge only where it can actually work.**
Compare immediate qualified ordinary operation, finite supported
continuation in a verified safe scope, and a staged successor.
Keep the bridge purpose, expiry, funded workload and exit explicit.
The sector owner verifies each use and the protected simultaneous
capacity across agencies. Independent scrutiny and ordinary intake
must remain available. Keep old and new uses outside the same
failed controller where independence is needed. If no supported
version can lawfully serve a material task, use the qualified
ordinary route and report any uncovered duty for lawful correction.
An automatic supplier migration supplies no authority for reliance.

**DP87 — Verify the successor against old obligations and current law.**
Carry original receipts, contested meanings, rights, open cases and
executed payments with usable evidence under DP29–DP32. Verify
legitimate access, scope and fresh task performance under DP78–DP83.
Where a claim remains uncertain, route it to competent review with
its original deadline. Avoid duplicate payment or replay of an
already executed decision. The new route must have actual accepting
staff and an independently verified next service window. Fund and
maintain the old fallback until lawful handoff or safe termination.

**DP88 — Keep sector authority and public budget choice.** The
purchaser enforces notice, access and exit terms. Each lawful sector
owner limits or authorizes reliance and verifies actual receiving.
The shared coordinator exposes correlated dates and commitments
without taking over agencies or courts. Elected bodies choose the
funded protection where existing sources fail. Publish safe
independent evidence and actual missed service. AI may reconcile
dependency and version records. It cannot approve its successor
or suspend rights. Reopen on disputed support, unsafe continuation,
missed handoff or a stronger qualified ordinary route.
