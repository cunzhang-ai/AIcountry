# PC16–PC20: answerable accounts and no finance-police veto

*Part of [C03/12](../12-continuous-political-finance.md); design110 candidate.*

**PC16 — Timely, bounded public reasons.** Covered accounts retain dated actual
source, amount/index basis, controller, purpose, expenditure/delivery, asset/
liability and shared-cost records. Paid electoral messages identify controller,
source class, distribution dates and material audience-selection criteria at
first distribution; enter public value/range/archive within48elapsed hours,
correct the invoice amount within seven days of usable actual invoice. No
private recipient list, political profile or inferred audience belief is public.
PC01's material ordinary paid issue advertising has the same sponsor/range
notice and archive duty, without electoral caps or a political-truth test.

Annual political accounts publish receipts, spending, assets/debts and protected
source aggregates within30calendar days after each quarter and90days after year
end; during a called contest, additional covered receipts/outlays above B*/20
are reported within48h. Keep original event dates; reporting never postpones
the act's source/value test. Donor units above .05 per principal in a rolling
365days trigger public donor-name/aggregate disclosure, with contemporaneous
court shielding of endangered identity while amounts/effective controller and
protected audit remain answerable. No public membership or voter-credit graph.
Ordinary records have six-year purpose-limited retention, then deletion; a specific
lawful live hold preserves only necessary material/period. Public donor identities
follow the same retention, not an immutable political history. Safe aggregate
corrections/reasons can remain after identity removal. No new mass account search,
interception, home entry or unrelated source access without its own lawful power.

**PC17 — Existing offices, express new scope and real pay.** Extend EA13's
protected career finance head/investigators to PC records, source/control and
paid-ad inquiries with its prior published case/risk/conflict rules and ordinary
courts; keep EA's seven seats, selection and4votes/5present unchanged. EA Access
Officer determines objective receiving/support, career Treasury/local source
payers pay and CF03 fiscal agents hold actual restricted property. EA13 inquiry
cannot suspend these acts. Related office cases use unconflicted ordinary courts,
independent existing audit/integrity/prosecution and protected original custody;
implicated leaders do not allocate/judge their own case.

Expressly extend ER12–14/B18/B19 career civil executors, separate credentials,
preassigned rotation and bounded standing payment authority to PC annual/local/
referendum duties. A court must first establish the exact verified withheld act;
no executor grants eligibility, levies tax, borrows, chooses policy or commands
foreign payment. National appropriations pay common support/career/audit/court
minimums and actual local receiving costs; Treasury does not require ministerial
approval of an opponent's platform. Local enhancements have their own lawful
payer. Shared cloud, employer or sole data supplier remains a capture risk even
when offices have different names. Price alternate records, providers and paid
court/credential capacity with other elections, remedies and clinical duties.

**PC18 — Correction and restraint need actual control.** Anyone may report a
credible problem in ordinary language through assisted/offline routes; no complete
technical dossier or public donor identity is required. First usable receipt is
preserved; urgent E13/B17 consideration within24h/earlier useful time, no serial
completeness reset. Give source-linked reasons/reply, a five-day ordinary source
correction opportunity where unused funds can safely be segregated, and direct
EA16 ordinary court/appeal without mandatory investigator exhaustion. Punitive
fault, actual entitlement and whether timely repair remains possible are separate.

Only a competent court may order return of identified unlawful funding, source/
record correction, lawful paid equivalent access or a proportionate stop to
proved specific unlawful paid distribution. Urgent ex parte action has the prior
72h hearing/answer safeguards; preserve unrelated lawful speech, actual worker/
vendor claims and ballot secrecy. No general platform shutdown, party prohibition,
ballot removal, due-credit freeze or vote annulment follows from a finance report.
PD/K/MP crimes require their own exact facts/process. A receipt, order or payment
after useful political time is mitigation, not restoration of already lost choice.
If a substitute also refuses/lacks source, expose nonperformance and keep the
original claim; do not invent a superior sovereign or extend officeholders.

**PC19 — Express narrow VD12 extension, not punishment by analogy.** The adopting
Act prospectively extends ER18–19 VD12 class B only to an actual PC principal,
donor, source/public-asset controller with control/ability who knowingly/recklessly
(a) accepts/provides/routes/uses prohibited source or retains unused excess after
the five-day correction chance, with proved unlawful value at least B(t)/20;
(b) incurs/directs/materially coordinates excess covered expenditure of at least
max(B(t)/20,5% of the applicable fixed ceiling); or(c) materially falsifies a due
source/controller/value record to hide such a qualifying(a)/(b) act. Lock the
law/index basis and actor's ability at the act. For a rolling cap, prove only
the current act's actual value exceeding its remaining normalized capacity,
using that act's locked ceiling/basket; do not fine earlier lawful costs again.
Aggregate traceable split payments/controlled agents in a proved same course
under ER18, once and with each act's locked basis, not each shell in isolation.
Disputed good-faith valuation,
prompt pre-use return, ordinary mistake and lawful issue/volunteer/editorial work
are not this offence. No punishment for a later reclassification alone.

Court-only Q02/individual180-unit and ER19's expressly scoped body alternative
(max prior Q08 bases or twice proved unlawful value) apply, same-course value
once, zero minimum/means/proportionality/worker-creditor protections and ordinary
P/V appeal. ER18's six-year B charge period and only proved bounded concealment
extension remain. Other PC report breaches use specific civil correction; no
new implicit V crime, detention or ideology offence. Funds/fines never finance
this office. Until enacted, current VD12 scope/old contracts and acts retain law.

**PC20 — Finance remedies and vote authority remain different.** National ER
validity/mandate rules retain their national scope. Local material contest cases
use ER16–17's proof/petition/urgent-merits standards but only the actual affected
local mandates; lawful local continuity/caretaker law and fixed body size govern,
not national cabinet succession by analogy. A lawfully needed local repeat closes
at final judgment+90days, V=judgment+76, preserving the60-day call/qualification
package. PC14 governs referendum repeat. No court awards a favored winner.
A jurisdiction lacking lawful local nomination/continuity rules is not ready by
this finance extension. Its source reconciliation is an explicit remaining
legal prerequisite, not a power to postpone an already lawful due election.

**Design166 local physical-failure scope.** PC20's ER16 extension expressly
selects the new strict material physical/no-fault voting-or-ascertainment and
after-fixed-P before/without-certification ground for the actual local contest,
subject to separately authorized prospective scope/RR holds. Test actual local
seat/choice effect and severability, not national EL07 seats/cabinet status.
Existing local+90/+76, fixed n and direct first-receipt/merits/mandate protections
remain; no new source/dates/authority by an ER cross-reference alone.

[PC25–PC31](06-political-advertising-and-private-choice.md) applies PC16
to material versions and actual paid delivery. Preserve existing deadlines,
protected evidence and the limited PC18 remedy.
