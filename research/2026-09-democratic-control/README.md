# Democratic control: a substantive constitutional candidate

Start with the [candidate democratic constitution](democratic-constitution.md)
and its [capture exercises](capture-exercises.md).

The user identified that a democratic label does not establish democratic control.
This iteration makes democracy an explicit project-design commitment and proposes
concrete institutions, authority limits, opposition rights and enforcement paths.
It rejects AI-exclusive lawmaking as the desired political endpoint while preserving
the current software and canonical text pending their applicable revision routes.

The numerical arrangements are candidate design choices, not proven optima. The
electoral system, parliamentary executive, appointment arrangements and renewal
rules must be compared with other democratic designs. Democratic participation
and peaceful replacement themselves are not traded away for a favorable AI score.

All operational exercises remain unperformed. No country, electorate, appointments,
funding, ratification or independent endorsement is invented. See the
[iteration record](iteration-record.md) and [validation](validation.json) for scope.
