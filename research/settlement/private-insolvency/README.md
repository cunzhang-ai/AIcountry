# Private insolvency: debt relief must expose its losses

14 October 2026 · Design 63 · Authored boundary cases, not observed proceedings

[U01–U12](../../country/C08/14-private-insolvency-and-debt-recovery.md) specifies
entry, stay, protected resources, priority, personal discharge and firm
restructuring. The [whole-country audit](../../DESIGN-COMPLETENESS.md) explains
why this reaches a cross-system gap while leaving national finance open.

| Case | Required independent route and unresolved consequence |
|---|---|
| W1 No-asset debtor owed ordinary unsecured credit | Accept without a debt/fee threshold, review actual assets/excess and notify creditors. If genuinely no means, ordinary discharge is prompt; no compulsory three-year poverty watch. Verify actual corrected records. The creditor loss is not paid or state-guaranteed. |
| W2 Debtor has a wealthy but controlling spouse | U03/U05 count owned/safely received assets/support, not presumed household access. Protect confidentiality and dependants; asset concealment needs actual proof. A joint address cannot create a guarantee or pool property. |
| W3 Unpaid wages and a bank's valid security compete | Identify collateral/third-party property, actual security, realization cost and the unencumbered wage priority; notify workers and lenders. No universal full payment is promised. Separate income/receiving support must be funded; write-off is no delivered wage. |
| W4 Harmed person holds intentional-violence compensation | The exception needs proved fault/amount and independent hearing; neither accusation nor acquittal alone decides it. Actual ability/subsistence review limits collection. Unrecovered victim loss remains and care/safety do not wait; an unfunded public compensation account is no remedy. |
| W5 Small firm proposes rescue with forecast exports | Confirm only a feasible lawful plan with real counterpart commitments, source valuation, class protection, worker/current-duty and currency paths. A forecast alone cannot fund continuation or justify delayed unpaid wages. Liquidation and safe service transfer remain alternatives. |
| W6 Officer claims a recovery fee and resets the plan after an outage | U11 bars recovery-commission incentives; independent rotation/conflict and direct review apply. Preserve original opening/date/claims and actual distribution. A renamed account/trustee cannot restart ordinary discharge or create authority. |
| W7 Debtor hides an asset while family support was genuinely necessary | Separate actual intentional concealment from lawful needs transfers; hearing/appeal can correct the affected benefit and bounded extension. No model score, kinship inference, imprisonment for inability or erasure of all unrelated discharge. |
| W8 Owner and spouse guaranteed a failed business | Prove each informed valid guarantee and actual rights; coordinate eligible individual cases without pooling property or double recovery. Enterprise closure does not automatically release or condemn either guarantor. New credit remains a lender decision under fair rules. |

Cases are reasoned hypotheses with incomplete facts. No borrower, creditor,
worker, victim, dependent, practitioner or legitimate authority participated.
