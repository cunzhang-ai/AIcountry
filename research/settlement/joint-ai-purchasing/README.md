# Six due services and one shared AI dependency

*Design291 · Authored comparison for a single first-budget window.*

The same useful-time window includes three housing casework decisions
H1–H3, two payment verifications B1–B2 and one independent appeal R1.
Each requires a distinct, qualified, timely slot. Original eligibility
and decisions remain with public staff. AI can organize supporting
work but cannot decide the appeal, housing claim or payment.
The assumed primary failure disables a common model or updater.
In the broader shared offer, an ordinary alternate handles only
H1, H2, B1 and R1. Individually, every sector has seen a successful
standalone test with extra shifts available. The shared test has four
slots on the actual deadline. Different court, housing and payment
skills remain non-interchangeable.

| Choice | Due cash units | Under loss of the common model or updater |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Wider shared purchase | 6 | Four due tasks have qualified cover. H3 and B2 are exposed. |
| Narrow shared purchase for H1, H2 and B1 | 8 | Buy three specific alternate slots for the scoped work. Keep H3, B2 and R1 on staffed ordinary routes. All six can be covered. |
| Separately operated qualified services | 9 | All six covered, with separately tested sources and staff. Requires a larger envelope. |
| Staffed ordinary redesign | 8 | All six covered in the assumed loss. Uses ordinary training and access work instead of purchasing AI assistance. |

The wider purchase costs four units for the technology and two for
its ordinary fallback. The scoped purchase costs three for technology,
two for dedicated qualified fallback and three for ordinary work
outside scope. The authored comparison already includes due dates and
whole costs for this limited window. No saving is transferred to a
national budget. On an eight-unit envelope the viable alternatives
are the scoped purchase and ordinary redesign. Choose ordinary
redesign while comparative usefulness is unverified. The scoped
option can replace it only with its own exact authorization and a
verified useful improvement on the same protection and costs.
At seven units the wider purchase looks affordable but leaves two
protected duties exposed. A smaller service proposal or funded repair
must address the gap without closing the underlying claims.

A second provider controlled by the same updater fails in the same
test. If a replacement housing worker is promised to both a housing
decision and the appeal, even different times on marketing materials
cannot cover a coincident window. Actual agreed time and qualification
govern. Losing an ordinary staffing controller after the first
switch may expose additional tasks. Preserve pending decisions,
already authorized payments and original review dates. These
figures are assumptions about a declared roster, prices and failure,
with no measured actual service or productivity effect.
