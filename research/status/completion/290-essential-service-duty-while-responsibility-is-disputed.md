# Design382. Keep essential service delivery open during public responsibility disputes

[AP13–AP18](../../country/C04/06/06-essential-service-duty-while-responsibility-is-disputed.md)
assigns a predeclared interim owner and payer for covered essential
interfaces, with separate professional judgments and final liability.
It preserves actual deadlines, current rights and direct challenge.

[Primary evidence and the two-service case](../../settlement/essential-service-duty-while-responsibility-is-disputed/01-two-duties-and-a-disputed-payer.md)
test timely cash, distinct tasks and the next due call. Actual adoption,
affected participation, independent review and funded delivery remain open.
