# Design204 decision. Make maintenance and competing investments one elected choice

9 November 2026 · Research selection from Design203

## Gap and choice

DB authorizes aggregate commitments, appropriations, cash and year-end
execution. FB requires multiyear source and ongoing obligations. SC governs
buying and contract changes. CP handles dated expansion and bounded shortage
response. These rules constrain individual investments. They leave ordinary
cross-programme timing, comparison with maintain-and-repair, material project
changes and actual service acceptance spread across sector provisions.

Select IV01–IV08. At commitment and annual budget decisions, join material
projects on shared constraints, operating obligations and funded maintenance.
Keep lawful project stages that can be reconsidered, followed by independent
technical checks and acceptance into service. Reserve elected policy and
expenditure decisions, existing owner and safety powers and legal challenge.

## Evidence and alternatives

R-108 supplies a real distinction between concept selection and investment
authorization. The NAO school estate audit describes estimated age, condition
information and maintenance/funding tradeoffs. Neither verifies the proposed
scope or an empirical superiority claim. The authored portfolio demonstrates
a different and specific issue. Separate feasible files can collectively
exceed both money and usable work. Under its assumptions, grid renewal, safe
school repair and timely accessible hearings fit where a new school and both
other commitments do not. The result depends on the funded repair and the
accepted continuity route. Longer school service life requires its own
comparison and real renewal evidence.

Keep sector-by-sector approval as a simpler rival for routine and independently
deliverable work. A central infrastructure authority could keep scarce expertise
and choose investments coherently, but its own control of reviews and finance
would make an abusive priority harder to overturn. A concession can provide
genuine long-term service if its staff, capital, transfer of risk and lawful
exit are verified. A lower first-year charge alone supplies no added crew
and may leave future payment and guarantee exposure. Do not force one
ownership model or a single capital ranking.

## Public power, objections and continuity

Existing elected bodies can replace their investment choices, introduce
alternatives and amend commitments within source law. The responsible service
owner reports need and operating results. Independent reviewers receive the
original condition, dependency and cost records. Give public and opposition
usable reasons and distinguish sensitive information from convenience
secrecy. A reviewer can report a legal or technical problem. Existing
authorities enforce restrictions. Neither a project assessor nor a fiscal
model selects elected priorities.

A sponsor can divide one scheme, overstate shared capacity, delay repairs to
improve a short-term budget or classify irreversible procurement as study.
Use functional dependency and cumulative exposure, original baselines,
independent commissioning and review of the procurer's own performance.
The strongest objection is additional lead time for a necessary service.
Retain proportionate rules for routine maintenance, existing urgent safety
powers and protected continuation. Locally lawful work needs no new national
permission. If assessors, contractors and auditors share control or staff,
record failed independence and find a legally competent alternative. Clauses
do not supply the money or independent workers that process consumes.

Preserve DB, FB, CP, SC, MF, LA, LU, EV and sector rights and expiry rules.
Maintain accrued claims, contracts, joint and foreign commitments, worker
pay, accepted successors and the original record when a project stops or
changes. An enabling law needs prospective thresholds, available staff,
independent channels and lawful handling of live work. The change grants no
authorization or endorsement to a current public programme.
