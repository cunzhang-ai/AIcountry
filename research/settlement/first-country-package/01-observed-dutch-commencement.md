# A national law starts while prior cases and alternate routes continue

On 14 March 2023 the Dutch Senate agreed to the 1 January 2024
commencement date of the Environment and Planning Act. The Senate
record described five earlier postponements tied to problems with the
digital system. The 20 March royal decree fixed the legal date
under section 23.10. The related 5 April decree coordinated numerous
other acts and instruments, with precise exceptions and ordering.
Its explanation treats the general cutover as one indivisible moment
for identifying prior law in transitional provisions. The precise
ordering for other effects still matters. [Senate record](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/nieuws/20230314/omgevingswet_gaat_in_op_1_januari)
· [Commencement decree](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2023-89.html)
· [Related instruments](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2023-113.html)

Article 4.3 of the implementing Act preserves prior law for qualifying
applications made before commencement, subject to the statutory
exception, until finality or the decision taking effect as applicable.
The competent authority's guidance distinguishes this path from other
transitional rules and new applications. The legal cutover therefore
did not put all pending case work on one new procedure.
[Implementing Act](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2020-172.pdf)
· [Competent authority guidance](https://iplo.nl/regelgeving/regels-voor-activiteiten/overgangsrecht/overgangsrecht-vergunning/)

In December 2024 a later decree extended specified alternative
publication standards to 1 January 2026, with scoped rules for drafts
and subsequent amendments. This is a historical decision about a
technical route and its limits after legal commencement. It is not a
finding about current law or the practical usability of any municipality.
[Alternative publication decree](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2024-422.html)

The observed instruments show distinct decisions about when new law
starts, which prior cases continue, and how a limited substitute can
remain authorized. They do not establish actual access, readiness, cost
or environmental outcome. [Evidence scope](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-first-budget-package-and-lawful-start/sources.md)
