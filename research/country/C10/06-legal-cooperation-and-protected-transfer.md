# C10. Legal cooperation and protected transfer

15 October 2026 · Design 73 · Proposed public responsibility, not treaty membership

[B41–B52/P21–P24](../C08/17-jurisdiction-and-legal-cooperation.md) assigns
forum/law/recognition, evidence, concurrent proceedings and protected transfer.
Cabinet negotiates within existing law; the Assembly authorizes material
treaties/implementing law and future-budget exposure. Independent courts
decide individual legality, rights and execution. No ambassador, police alert,
platform or AI sends a person or property abroad by itself.

The justice cooperation service receives/transmits authenticated requests
and preserves records/dates; foreign affairs identifies actual treaty scope,
partners, reservations, conflicts, permissible exits and diplomatic options.
Courts, defense and service authorities retain their separate competence and
direct routes. An outsourced/third-country transfer cannot bypass the same
non-refoulement, humane treatment, child and meaningful-review protections.
Prohibited transfer is not cured by a different carrier or country label.

An actual cooperation request records source authority, persons/acts/assets,
purpose and legal basis, scope, original evidence, known dependencies,
conflicting laws, required notice, safety, actual receiving and challenge.
Relevant sensitive material uses safe limited channels and independent
access, not publication into a permanent global intelligence or training file.
Interoperability cannot collapse request, interpretation, consent, mandate,
judgment and execution into one machine-accepted event. Replication never
multiplies legal power or an asset/share/claim.

No country is assumed to recognize our plural compacts, orders, discharge,
paper assurances or a project calling itself a state. Funded alternate lawful
routes and truthful remaining gaps are needed. A foreign-law/privacy conflict,
immunity or refusal can leave an unresolved remedy; report it rather than
claiming worldwide enforcement. People keep urgent domestic safety, identity,
income, clinical and child receiving while those issues are adjudicated.

C06 counts translation, independent advice/judges, source/cyber custody,
actual reception/transport/clinical handover and executed claims alongside
all existing duties. No partner promise, future recovery, penalty yield or
generic country-book share proves qualified capacity or actual financing.
Record due/transmitted/accepted/decided/executed/received states separately.

[G01–G14](../C08/18-international-crimes-and-accountability.md) now supplies a
national international-crime candidate; RP and [CD](../C08/21-conflict-detention-and-release.md)
now specify reproductive and narrow conflict-custody candidates. Technical sector
law, actual court/treaty implementation and capacity remain open. Source law/treaties
continue until authorized compatible migration. Newer text, diplomatic
convenience or a technical standard does not enact a rule or settle a named
case; lawful democratic replacement and outside-command abuse review remain.
