# Design509 decision record

**Basis and choice.** Under the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md), build
on [Design508](baseline.json). Select an ordinary elected response to a
persistent capacity failure with distinct bridge, durable and relief decisions,
through a common dossier for related duties. Keep affected people, sectors
and payers on their original clocks. Require an independently sourced view of
receipt and missed people before claiming improved access or using the claim
to redirect ordinary service. The [canonical design](../country/C06/62/11-durable-capacity-and-public-choice.md)
leaves legal priorities with competent law and individual judgment within
each source. It retains narrow first-hours authority and its expiry. For a durable physical conflict, prefer a prospectively enacted finite ordinary allocation schedule over repeated emergency powers or silent priority by sector. Test the schedule against legally authorized shares, timely receipt and a comparable-claim rule that accounts for repeated exclusion.

**Alternatives and objections.** A rolling bridge can prevent earlier harm
and strand the next cohort. A training-first programme can solve the endpoint
and fail every intermediate window. Reorganized provision can improve real
capacity or export cost to households. Prioritize a staffed transition with
independent short-term protection where it fits. If it fails, compare the
severity and reversibility of unmet duties under previously enacted priorities
and put the resource choice to the Assembly. The government can exercise
only its existing lawful authority while waiting. A material recurrence test
could add administrative cost. Preserve initial notice and remedies, accept
ordinary reports and reuse the existing review and elected budget processes
rather than require each affected person to complete a national dossier.

**Handoff.** Carry accrued rights, existing mandates, provider/worker terms,
protected minimums, incident clocks, current sources and purpose limits.
A new statute would need a competent authority and means to staff scrutiny,
case review, services and any separate substitute. The parallel internal
review supplies neither independent affected participation nor qualification
of a real operating route. [Sources](sources.md) preserve observation,
projection and hypothetical evidence as distinct inputs.
