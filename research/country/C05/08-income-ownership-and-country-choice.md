# C05. Income, ownership and the choice of country model

1 November 2026 · Design 88 · Earlier comparison retained, destination revised; not law

The country retains **universal essential services, ongoing Y retirement
claims, plural production and I01–I12 individual income protection** through
transition. [SG01–SG12](15-service-first-personal-security.md) now selects the
service-first individual I05/SD05 guarantee as preferred continuing architecture;
rights-preserving universal ordinary cash remains a substantive rival. The
earlier IC destination preference is superseded; paid plural ownership remains.
This page preserves the older comparison, whose numeric B differs from IC. It has not selected an affordable operating
package. Means assessment is a substantial power over people, not a neutral
administrative detail; concentrated private or collective ownership also
creates political and economic dependence.

The [same-service comparison](../../settlement/country-models/README.md)
tests three distinct mechanisms: A retains I05; B pays a universal personal
base with changed taxes and assessed additional needs; C retains I05 while a
resident mutual buys existing private equity with an explicit seller loan.
They hold the current public-service bids and national workforce constant.
They are not exhaustive social models or equal-cost welfare experiments.

All three close a conditional annual cash account by deriving required
output. None supplies actual production, complete duty costs or a funded
transition. B reduces required output partly by reducing some people's
cash; C relies on an unverified acquisition price and debt service. A also
retains severe production, assessment and failure risks. No model wins
because a calculated resource constraint becomes green.

Design69 retained A provisionally rather than selecting B or C on those calculations.
Design88 supersedes that destination preference for stated independence/equality
reasons, not because the old B tax fixture or C acquisition became qualified.
This does not give current wealth holders a veto over redistribution or
make I05 permanently preferable. Universal receipt and distributed ownership
remain substantive alternatives, including combinations not modeled here.
Their practical independence, incidence, source finance and public value
choice require fresh comparison before a consequential selection.

The whole-country audit now replaces the completed grouped-law next task.
I05 remains a conditional mechanism, not a ready numeric bundle. Reopen this
economic choice on credible workload/output evidence, distributional/public
choice, or materially different ownership/finance terms. Do not continue
inventing macro parameters in place of those missing inputs.
