# WL36–WL40: preserve rights while making the labor settlement contestable

*Part of [C05/12](../12-work-contracts-and-collective-power.md); design 117 candidate.*

**WL36 — Prospective coordinated law and paid transition.** Competent Work/TW/
AS/I/TA/Y/B/U/V/Treasury law commences new WL rights, offices and wage advances
180 calendar days after enactment with exact worker/contract/representative/case/
source/record successors, funded recruitment/ballots/aid, payroll and paid overlap.
This does not restart TW's separate36/32-hour stages or postpone earlier rights,
AS obligations, useful-time protection or elections. Existing stronger terms,
wages/leave/pension/notice, representation/agreements, private/secured/foreign and
privacy claims retain actual dates and source-law transition. No automatic old-debt
public guarantee, backdated offense or bulk revival of barred claims. Specify how
prospective noncompete/variation rules displace future restrictions after notice,
without canceling accrued lawful pay/compensation or calling old lawful conduct
criminal. Old/current actual software/constitution/contribution holds remain.
An unfinished office/table cannot create no-worker status, zero needs, assumed
agreement or a technical readiness veto over commenced rights. No rule reviewing
this proposal can approve its own adoption.

**WL37 — Different social models remain possible.** Select individual work floors,
independent collective power, bounded binding workplace consent, fair business
adjustment and explicitly funded limited wage protection. Retain stronger sector
bargaining, worker board co-determination/ownership, voluntary enterprise/safe
cooperation and cash-first/full-U designs as structural rivals. An at-will contract
plus basic income may ease adjustment but leaves employer coercion; reject it as
sufficient substitute for collective/anti-retaliation rights. Priority-only wage
recovery costs less public cash but can abandon workers in assetless failure;
retain as a real funding rival, not equivalent protection. Symmetric unpaid lockout
and broader workplace voting have distinct power/exit tradeoffs, not a metric
solution. More protection can raise cost, exclusion, informality or closure;
less protection can shift arbitrary risk to workers and weaken political freedom.

**WL38 — Future collaboration keeps actual scope.** Work can occur in shared
situations, asynchronous teams, transient projects and unfamiliar forms. Preserve
original meaning, interpretation loss, actual commitments and chosen medium rather
than forcing every relation into one account/text contract. Communication is not
agreement; delegated management is not consent; collective representation is not
ownership or sovereign authority. Agent copies multiply neither votes nor actual
human hours. No convergence score sets a mandate or suppresses minority objection.
Make mediators/sponsors replaceable and keep offline/direct help practical without
forcing every informal exchange into formal employment. Novel claims can require
new law, not silent deletion, automatic equivalence or an AI ruling.

**WL39 — Observe actual lives and shared dependence.** EV/source custodians keep
original work/pay/price and safe correction history without permanent public worker
testimony. Examine actual bargaining/exit/voice, pay receipt/rest, arbitrary
retaliation, exclusion, closed businesses and displaced service/household work,
including nonclaimants and absent/irregular workers. Qualified analysis is not
affected participation, independence or legal authority. New facts/objections need
fresh review even with identical inputs; numerical bounds/green checks do not
validate the social model or certify readiness/superiority.

Price private wages/notice/severance/representation/replacement and public inspection,
Registrar/ballot, advice/courts, gross wage advances/credit losses and transition
once across all37 functions and later commitments. Old staffing/108 source quotes
are no ceiling or free new capacity. No free AI, presumed volunteer, fine, recovered
asset, restricted S/Y/client/insurer/pension/CCP or owned privateFX pays these duties.
Actual original rates/people, safe rosters, source/legacy/actuarial/foreign acceptance
and observed outcomes remain unqualified; scarcity is not permission to erase claims.

**WL40 — Reopen and choose the next country gap.** Reopen on arbitrary dismissal,
forced status/consent, captured representation, inappropriate action restrictions,
unsafe receiving, unpaid dues, harmful entry/closure, new work forms or a stronger
whole rival. Missing monitoring preserves uncertainty and duties, not consent.
Next specify ordinary company/cooperative/association law: legal forms, governance/
worker participation, actual capital/title, limited liability/control, director
responsibility, member/minority rights and lawful succession. Existing B/MP/U/IC
already supplies substantial rights/competition/insolvency; they are not a complete
positive enterprise constitution. Verify that gap against current sources before
choosing details. Finish an integrated alternative/control/source/transition design,
not another isolated labor ratio. Full local/country source, tax/actuarial/FX/legacy,
legitimate choice and actual lives remain open; urgent harm/larger contradiction
may reprioritize the next bounded desk research.
