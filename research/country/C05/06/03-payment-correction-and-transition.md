# Income clauses I07–I09: payment, correction and transition

*Design 58 proposed clauses; [income package](../06-income-floor-and-safe-resources.md).*

**I07 — Advance calendar and changing income.** Pay ordinary protection in
advance on the first calendar day of each month through a safe individual
cash/non-digital or chosen equivalent route. An initial eligible period
is prorated from the protected first claim date and paid upon determination;
continuity's seven-day hardship route covers earlier useful need. Assess
resources actually expected to be usable in that month with source evidence;
a historical average shall not treat a lost job's old wage as present cash.
Birth, disability cost, household separation and receipt failures have a
direct adjustment route; an adverse shock triggers provisional reconsideration
within continuity's earlier safe-time/24-hour rule. Legal entitlement,
payment instruction, safe actual receipt and delivered service are separate.

**I08 — Accessible correction, no automated final denial.** A reasoned
decision shall show eligible scope, basket/components, receipts and period,
tax/contribution treatment, matching cost credits, calculation and independent
remedy. An AI score, unexplained bank inference or family's refusal of a
common account shall not finally deny protection. Use assisted channels,
interpreters and necessary legal help; the person can enter urgent court
directly. Later income reconciliation distinguishes agency error, changed
circumstances and proved misrepresentation. Apply continuity's subsistence-
protected recovery and defense rules; a good-faith later correction is
not automatically a criminal case or a deduction from another person's claim.

I08 also joins the [effective-access duty](../../C07/01-effective-access.md):
fund safe practical help for potential nonclaimants/nonusers without presuming
eligibility, refusal or fraud from nonresponse. Source-law claim dates, privacy,
actual resources and independent remedies remain; no automatic backdated award.

**I09 — Continuing benefits and explicit migration.** Map every inherited
payment, pension, credit, disability allowance, housing claim and pending
case to source law, person, period, safe successor and receipt. A predecessor
basic payment may be included in a new basic guarantee only on a proven
same-person/same-period/component account; earned pension rights and
distinct cost compensation retain their full legal source. Do not count
one old envelope both as replaced and as free savings, or remove a claim
because its amount is not yet classified. Unresolved obligations remain
visible and provisioned separately, with actual lawful interim protection.
The old 45/15/40-million reference components are assumptions, not such a map.

Before an operative date, enact income and tax/insurance parameters, source
classifications, advance-payment authority, safe record sharing and review.
Fund overlap and actual receiving staff during transfer; test a missed wage,
unavailable partner account, child support dispute, price surge and payment
outage with an executed remedy. Legal clocks cannot certify capacity.
Prospective revision preserves accrued claims and effective minimum access;
a missed funding/start milestone is an unmet duty, not indefinite ministerial
permission to withhold the floor. Current software and source law remain unchanged.

[NP14–NP18](../../C06/33/05-dates-preparation-and-migration.md) now joins I09's
income/tax/insurance start and advance-payment staff with new S/Y coverage and
indispensable review/receiving dates in one expressly enacted national schedule.
Preparation-decision dates are separate; an I05 payment is not a delivered
care shift or a new power to suspend existing due protection.

[I19](06-received-services-time-and-replacement.md) adds a due-period
essential repair/replacement need after actual matching-purpose funds, not a
forced loan or unconditional capital grant. I05 resources still apply; early
useful-time receiving, independent correction and stronger old rights survive.
[I24](07-source-control-context-and-transition.md) supplies prospective succession.

[WL34–WL35](../12-work-contracts-and-collective-power.md) now adds a limited
wage-substitution advance, keeping the original wage/leave/notice TA/Y source
classification and actual I07 receipt month. Actually received net labor advance
enters I05 E with the existing disregard/taper; the I05 award never becomes its
own resource. It is no unpaid historic wage or second worker receipt on employer
repayment to Treasury. Actual employee withholding is inside gross; distinct AS/state/full
employer claims and protected correction remain.

[IC18–IC19](../09/06-income-security-and-credit-continuity.md) separates current
I05/U cash status from independently qualified Y06 interruption credit. No
unneeded cash renewal is a credit gateway; disputed credits do not block
ordinary protection. Proved unpaid covered wages remain covered for retirement
without being treated as received I05 cash or credited a second time.

[Design140 AP](../../C04/06-reachable-administration-and-finite-decisions.md)
closes the common ordinary completeness-clock gap. E06/E07's earlier hardship/
14-calendar-day income determination, actual due payments and I07–I09 survive;
B16's working-day default supplies no extra waiting time or new eligibility.
Necessary proof/help and direct omission review preserve actual source rules.

[EB reference/receipt distinction](../16/02-reference-amount-time-and-payment.md)
uses historical earned pay only for its separate temporary insured-base measure.
It never treats a lost/unreceived wage as current I05 usable cash. I07's first-
month actual-resource/earlier shock route and I08 safe correction remain; actual
net EB joins existing aggregate E under the separately coordinated law.

[I25–I29](08-income-timing-and-usable-resources.md) distinguishes a verified
early regular monthly wage from actual missed, late or blocked pay.
Keep one assignment per wage, I07 advances and E useful-time correction.
A receipt may have a different ordinary assessment date under express law.
