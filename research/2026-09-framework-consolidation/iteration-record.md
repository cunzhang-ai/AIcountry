# Working framework consolidation

20 September 2026 · Revision consolidation-1 · Prepared proposed research method

## Problem and decision

The user requested improvement of the project framework after opening competing
ways of thinking. The existing entry point remained a navigation page for a growing
chain of proposals. It named 3.1 as a working hypothesis and linked rival models,
but a contributor still had to reconstruct the common method across several
documents and distinguish it from one preferred social architecture.

Selected change: make [FRAMEWORK.md](../FRAMEWORK.md) a self-contained working method
and provide [one decision record](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md). Separate the method,
competing models of society and contextual decisions. Connect research actions to
what is actually unresolved. The coordination-centered lineage remains a candidate;
the method does not select it as the best national design.

Alternatives considered: add another architecture version; choose a favored branch
without new evidence; leave the distributed documents unchanged; or consolidate
the method with explicit decision routing. The last removes a concrete continuation
problem while preserving the substantive disagreement from the previous iteration.

## Challenge and reconciliation

Drafting and challenge were by one assistant; there was no independent review.

- Consolidation could make one worldview universal. Response: separate the explicitly
  normative research method from candidate social models, which can be displaced;
  acknowledge that the method itself is contestable.
- A decision route could recreate a rigid linear pipeline. Response: the route is
  chosen by the unresolved question; inquiry may reframe the situation, a source
  event may reopen qualification and retaining the baseline is a valid result.
- A short guide could erase material protections. Response: use the preservation
  map, retain detailed sources, state review qualification and all distinct status
  dimensions, and preserve predecessors unchanged.
- Requiring rival explanations could manufacture empty alternatives. Response:
  compare credible mechanisms and explain infeasibility; the lenses are optional
  prompts, not a fixed taxonomy or quota.
- A single record could exclude ordinary contributors. Response: free-form reports
  remain valid; the steward develops the record before consequential qualification.

Same-author decision walkthroughs: a new source correction leads to scoped
reassessment, not automatic repeal; unknown authority permits preparation but not
binding action; a promising simulation leaves observed outcomes unestablished;
irreducible values call for an applicable decision process rather than fabricated
weights; a preferable baseline permits a reasoned no-change result. These are
conceptual checks, not executable tests or observed social outcomes.
