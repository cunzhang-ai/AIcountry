# RF17–RF21. Fund retirement income and usable care across the same years

*Design313 · Proposed national finance and delivery rules.*

**RF17 — Compare the same generations and service needs.** In the
ordinary budget publish the existing legal path and the cost of
maintaining actual access as people age. Use the same population,
prices, work and source assumptions for ordinary and adverse paths.
Alongside the first four budgets, show intervening peaks and later
periods through the longer RF09 pension account. Keep closed accrued
claims and later obligations explicit. Separate public pension outlay,
tax actually received, restricted contributions, private pension
income, health care and long-term care. Identify which statistical
scopes overlap before consolidating. A baseline that uses another
country's age or benefit law cannot price this country's promises.

**RF18 — Give each public source one lawful use.** Reconcile the
insurance account's gross pension payments, eligible contributions,
working cash and required general-revenue backing. Count that
backing once in the national and local account after removing
internal transfers. Show separately the actual cash available to
each payer before each due date. Do the same for national funding
and local care delivery. A declining expenditure share, future
private asset, intergovernmental reimbursement or possible AI
saving supplies no current fiscal room. Keep the pension working
reserve available for its lawful use. Price care standby and
replenishment separately.

**RF19 — Put qualified care on the same schedule as the money.**
Distinguish living longer from spending longer in need of help.
Compare ordinary and adverse healthy-life, paid-work, care-needs,
wages, receipts and qualified staffing assumptions. Price care at
home and in institutions where both are safe and chosen. Count
travel, fixed remote provision, retention, paid mentors and the
loss of qualified time elsewhere. Carry voluntary help only within
the scope actually offered and fund the lawful response when it
ends. A falling school-age cohort, higher pension age, improved
forecast or additional grant does not by itself qualify a care shift.

**RF20 — Make one elected source choice for both duties.** The Cabinet
and ordinary members present exact alternatives under DB and BCV.
Keep accrued pension and current care duties on their governing law.
When a persistent gap appears, choose recurring lawful funding,
a real and explained release of unbound spending, or an expressly
enacted prospective change with its actual effects on generations
and service access. Prefer funding ordinary protection and paid
care preparation together to covering a permanent gap with recurring
emergency debt or shifting work to families. A finite paid bridge
requires its own amount, useful date and source. Show the people
who bear the costs and publish the actual remaining gap. An
independent fiscal office verifies and can publish dissent. Neither
its forecast nor a demographic rule changes an entitlement, tax or
public authority.

**RF21 — Review actual payments and care together.** On a material
deviation in need, money or staffing, show which pensions and
services were due and actually received, which qualified capacity
is available, and what the lawful alternate can do in time.
Use the existing elected budget and independent remedy routes.
Compare affected places and people, including people who never
reached an assessment. Preserve earlier dates, earned units,
care commitments and funded successors through any change.
AI can check overlapping assumptions, flows and public explanations.
Authorized officers verify receipts and professional acceptance.
Reopen on material unmet need, retirement or workforce changes,
a corrected source, or a better funded ordinary alternative.
