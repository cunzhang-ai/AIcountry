# Democratic budget government for the whole public offer

Design170 · [DB01–DB20](../../country/C06/60-democratic-budget-and-execution.md)

**Choose a member-amendable programme budget with explicit authority and actual
execution accountability.** Existing roles perform the work; no fiscal guardian,
new tax rate, balanced-budget rule or imaginary source is introduced.

| Actual country choice | Consequence and remaining burden |
|---|---|
| Earlier preparation |150/120-day baseline/proposal strengthens pinned120/60; full sources/needs and paid scrutiny, no copied foreign law |
| Practical alternatives | Any member, protected source/costing help/minority capacity and timely pre-vote opportunity; no analyst or Cabinet admission veto |
| Specific budget vote | Actual full statutory majority for national appropriation/material supplements, separate from DR/local/other primary/confidence rules |
| Distinct authority | Programme/new commitment/current payment/standing dues/actual cash, full fixed-project exposure not first instalment; employment not lifetime procurement |
| Limited execution | Input discretion within actual duties, no hidden interprogramme transfer, prior supplements and no political release hold on due rights |
| Honest year-end | Unused permissions versus surviving debts; express dated carry-forward once, no new cash or repeated old purchase |
| Source and control | Actual monthly/quarterly/13-week accounts, outside AA/courts, local source autonomy and paid exact succession |
