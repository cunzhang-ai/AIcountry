# CBG09–CBG12. Review and continuing duties

[Climate budgets](../27-climate-budgets-and-essential-continuity.md)

**CBG09 — Assign implementation and make burdens visible.** The
Assembly chooses legal ceilings, burdens and appropriations.
Cabinet and sector owners bring executable packages. Local elected
bodies retain lawful siting and service decisions. Show who can
actually switch equipment, move safely, access transport or keep
work and care. Fund necessary qualified installation, household
support, worker transition and local revenue correction before
relying on them. Follow existing C06 source law and affected rights.
A falling emissions base or a finite auction receipt does not
support a new permanent entitlement by assumption. Preserve existing
accrued pay, pensions, service and lawful compensation claims.

**CBG10 — Let evidence and remedies survive the authority reviewed.**
Independent qualified review under existing evidence, audit and
environmental powers tests original inventories, price and service
assumptions, inspection and actual correction. Give members,
affected residents, workers, lawful collective rights holders and
qualified public-interest challengers useful access and a protected
review route. Use existing courts to enforce duties, scope and
lawful grounds and to stop unlawful action. Courts do not set the
tax or choose the power mix. Neither an expert commission nor a
model can claim consent on behalf of future people. AI can compare
assumptions and schedules with original source access and visible
uncertainty. Responsible humans and institutions keep lawful decisions.

**CBG11 — Carry existing law and actual obligations through change.**
Use prospective authority and actual statutory and treaty safeguards
for any revised path. Map permits, funded receiving, operating
contracts, maintenance, cleanup, payments, records and pending
claims to accepted FT/OP/T successors. Keep published vintages and
physical cumulative emissions. An amendment cannot create a past
emission reduction or erase a recorded breach under the law then
applicable. Honour ongoing and stronger protections. New tax,
property and operation powers require their own law.

**CBG12 — Reopen on real delivery and adverse evidence.** Test budget
headroom, independent firm service, affordability and ecological
effects separately before reliance and during material change.
Reopen on grid delay, source revision, failed receipt, weak funding,
shifted foreign harm, breach of a separate floor or a better complete
alternative. The period, timing and non-banking rules are proposed
institutional parameters. Actual emissions, paid staff and accepted
service must support any readiness or improvement claim.
