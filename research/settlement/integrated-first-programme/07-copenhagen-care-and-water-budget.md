*Design306 · Observed benchmark for the first-programme decision.*

## 2025 results and the 2026 decisions that followed

The Health and Care Committee approved its 2025 accounts on 10 March
2026. The corrected service budget was DKK 5,739.793m and service
expenditure was DKK 5,725.004m, a DKK 14.789m underspend. Within the
same frame, home care overspent by DKK 18.5m and nursing by DKK 18.2m.
Those two variances total DKK 36.7m. The rest of the service frame
offsets them by about DKK 51.5m using the rounded sublines. The
department cites private provider prices and higher needs for
continuous attendance in temporary and care stays in the home-care
line. It cites assigned hours, sickness absence and temporary staff
in nursing. The home-care and nursing results are already within
the frame. They supply neither the cost of one extra shift nor an
estimate of unmet need. [Committee accounts, table 1 and older people section](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Sundheds-%20og%20Omsorgsudvalget/m%C3%B8de-10032026/referat/punkt-3)

A separate 10 March decision forwarded requests for DKK 8.788m
net service carry-forward. Its scope includes discretionary
requests. The 30 April council decision approved technical
transfers and named new initiatives. The Health and Care Committee's
own row in table 2 shows −DKK 0.1m in net technical service
carry-forward at the published precision. That row covers a different
scope from the March request and the city table shows some transfers
between committees separately. The same April decision reports DKK
8.7m in remaining citywide contingency service spending allowance and
financing. This is a published pool position. A specific allocation,
cash date and accepted operator would establish its contribution to
a new start. [March request, table 1](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Sundheds-%20og%20Omsorgsudvalget/m%C3%B8de-10032026/referat/punkt-4)
· [April decision, tables 2–4 and decision](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Borgerrepr%C3%A6sentationen/m%C3%B8de-30042026/referat/punkt-3)

The April agreement gives two relevant purposes their own lines.
It sets DKK 5.8m of 2026 service spending for a dementia package
(SU06) and DKK 3.0m for afforestation through the Climate Forest
Fund (ØK33), including land in HOFOR's drinking-water catchment.
These give the comparator actual authorized activities. Preserve
their purpose, payer and year. A reserve shift or emergency water
purchase would need its own source and scope. The 21 May council
approval sets the city's continuity arrangements. The submission
has no financial consequences in its own scope and describes an
emergency-power feasibility study already under way. A separate
approval and accepted supplier or staff would qualify operational
capacity. [April agreement, SU06 and ØK33](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/2026-04/rev2%20Aftaletekst%20OFS%2025-26%2009.04.26_1.pdf)

## The water company's own 2025 accounts

HOFOR Vand København A/S reports 2025 revenue of DKK 865.620m.
Note 1 identifies DKK 328.556m of supply-area sales, DKK 120.498m
of sales to utilities outside Copenhagen and DKK 416.566m of
regulatory adjustments, about 48.1% of reported revenue. The
company reports DKK 204.201m annual profit, DKK 0.050m in bank
deposits and DKK 108.177m in receivables from related entities at
year end. Note 9 allocates the annual result to retained earnings.
Customer under-recovery includes DKK 545.082m noncurrent
and DKK 64.158m current receivables. Customer over-recovery includes
a separately reported DKK 21.509m liability. Under the stated
accounting policy, the company reports its cash flows in the
group's consolidated accounts. These numbers carry the company's
boundary. The balance-sheet deposits give one dated amount and
leave facility access, payable commitments, public authority and
reserve procurement to their own evidence. [Company annual report, printed pp. 10 and 16–21](https://www.hofor.dk/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Aarsrapport-HOFOR-Vand-Koebenhavn-AS.pdf)

## First-programme disposition on the inspected records

| Choice | What the observed records qualify | Open condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retain ordinary delivery and prepare repair through the existing authorities | Existing service frame, named dementia and catchment measures, and council continuity plan | Establish actual committed water procurement, protected care shifts and resident receipt on two dates |
| Fund an independent joint reserve for an exact service window | A specific political budget route and a documented coordination structure | Obtain a priced qualified reserve, distinct receiving capacity, explicit contribution from each payer and dated funds |
| Release an incremental heating, training or tenancy motion | Preparation within existing purposes where separately authorized | No exact additional first-programme release can be qualified from these records |

Select the ordinary repair and preparation path for this evidence
context. An already due repair or housing obligation keeps its
lawful source and deadline. The constructed loss exercise continues
to illustrate a feasible heating-first branch under its stated
inputs. The observed figures and constructed units have no common
price conversion. A matched operating comparison still needs one
actual receiving location and two dates, staffing by task, usable
payer cash, a priced independent reserve, and observed outcomes.
