*Part of [C03/12](../12-continuous-political-finance.md); design110 candidate.*

**PC10 — Local contests have actual bodies, not invented national tracks.** For
each lawfully defined elected council/body, use its actual C02 electorate and
pre-existing ballot qualification/count/ward powers. Apply CF01–06 paid100-word
accessible guide, seven-minute presentation/Q&A, paper request, private credits
and PC13’s whole-credit principal cap to each actual distinct list/candidate track; never invent
a second credit for a one-track poll. Use the local call roll, B*, q and actual
first voting V/final close P. A list uses B*(6+E_call/200), an individual candidate
B*(1+E_call,d/200), each territorial share allocated once. Independent paid appeals
use max(B*, E_call B*/1000), including tiny places where CF's zero-like proportional
cap could prevent one practical message. Continuous PC05/06 limits also apply.

The adopting law must expressly supply lawful local nomination, fixed poll and
at least V−60 call, V−40 final support/nomination cutoff, V−35 qualification,
V−33 guide and V−21 actual forum; regular calls use V−90. Existing lawful local
qualification is not replaced by a national1% gate merely for finance convenience.
An incompatible actual nomination law needs express prospective reconciliation,
not fictitious qualified recipients or finance-office poll postponement. National
EA career administration administers the common support floor with local original
records, separately funded Treasury/local payers and ER execution. National
financing covers this minimum; local elected enhancements need lawful equal
terms/source and do not buy additional private/donor caps. No unfunded local mandate.

**PC11 — A referendum is not two appointed party franchises.** These rules cover
an actually lawful exact question/option set and electorate, including C14

paid appeals or receipt of a voter credit through a PC08 fiscal account.

contests under the coordinated PC Act; other CF entitlement/source limits remain.

A qualified/registered committee's question expenditure ceiling is B*(2+E_call/200).
A nonregistered independent purchaser may use its own lawful opt-in covered fund

An office cannot merge lawful petitions or defer a vote to save campaign money.

have several legally distinct annual/contest/question credits but no duplicate

segregated public sources and individual purpose rights. Pay/price each actually

petition rights and election dates do not vanish if the model's cash reserve is
exceeded. Expose an unpaid duty and obtain elected lawful source action; no
hidden recipient ceiling, prorated statutory credit or administrative veto.
