# C06. Candidate Continuity and Effective Access Act

*Integrated design 57, selected 14 October 2026 from the 13 October draft; proposed statute, not enacted law.*

This bounded ordinary-law package implements the [joint commitment
schedule](18-joint-commitment-and-delivery-schedule.md) for due income,
urgent clinical/paid care, independent review and election continuity.
Its E01–E20 clauses identify powers, clocks and failure consequences.
Other service, criminal, liberty, child, privacy and election laws retain
their separate scope. A general continuity act cannot enlarge them.

| Canonical part | Clauses and purpose |
|---|---|
| [Scope, receipt and standing authority](19/01-scope-receipt-and-authority.md) | E01–E04: legal boundaries, first date, custody and statutory payment authority |
| [Due income and provisional protection](19/02-income-and-provisional-protection.md) | E05–E08: due payments, seven-day hardship bridge, determination and correction |
| [Urgent health and paid care](19/03-urgent-health-and-paid-care.md) | E09–E12: safety deadline, accepting receiver, lawful allocation and continuing care |
| [Independent remedy and political continuity](19/04-remedy-and-political-continuity.md) | E13–E16: urgent court, actual execution, distinct substitute and pre-vote access |
| [Resources, failures and commencement](19/05-resources-failure-and-commencement.md) | E17–E20: attributed budget, breach, elected correction and continuing obligations |

Candidate administrative outer limits are 24 elapsed hours for hardship
decision, 14 calendar days for initial income determination, two elapsed
hours for urgent-care routing, six for a paid-care interim arrangement,
24 for urgent judicial consideration and 48 for baseline contest support
after qualification, with five calendar days for a qualification decision.
These are hypotheses for testing, not permission to
wait where safety or effective participation requires an earlier act.
A missed clock neither grants coercive power nor extinguishes a claim.

The [reference allocation and delivery tests](../../settlement/continuity/README.md)
attribute a bounded core inside the old six envelopes, disclose displacement
and test a 24-hour roster with multiple demands and common-controller loss.
They do not calibrate national need, guarantee full coverage or erase the
design-56 compound cash/staff failures. Actual function-level costs in the
national inventory remain unknown; authored reference allocations are
reported separately. A resource optimizer cannot select a national priority.

This selection keeps ordinary sector institutions and direct courts. It
creates neither a new central claim gate nor an automatic executive or AI
power to change benefits, deadlines, elections or force. Office labels
describe proposed statutory roles; an actual commencement act must designate
lawful entities, people, funds and independent routes. Review these clocks
and the resource package together, with affected people, before operation.
