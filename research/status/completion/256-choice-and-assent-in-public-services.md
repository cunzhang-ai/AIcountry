# Meaningful choice and valid assent in public services

Design348 · Scoped finding for [country completion](../../DESIGN-COMPLETENESS.md)

[DP58–DP63](../../country/C11/09/13-meaningful-choice-and-valid-assent.md)
protects usable alternatives, act-specific assent and correction across
staffed, digital and future agent-mediated public services.

The [worked comparison](../../settlement/choice-and-assent-in-public-services/01-optional-monitoring-and-standing-support.md)

capacity. Actual participation, delivery and effects remain open.
