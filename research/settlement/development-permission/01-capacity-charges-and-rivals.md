# Distinguish a permission, a contribution and a delivered home

Design172 · [Country choice](README.md)

If water can serve ten extra homes but sewer capacity can serve two, five
planning permissions are not five safely occupiable homes. An independently
served two-home phase may proceed only through actual source/TD05 acceptance;
remaining works and rights stay due. A contribution or bond creates no water.

For an authored shared-work example,100 cost units less20 actual compatible
public grant leaves80. An evidenced25% eligible attributable project share
means20 project units, with60 requiring named other sources. The20 payment is
not enough to commission the100-unit asset. Shares are not invented national
rates, and a grant is credited only to the actual cost it lawfully supports.

For one actual eligible100-unit cost, nonoverlapping compatible public30,
provided resources10, paid connection15 and accepted in-kind20 leave25. A
future tax/promise/security or different operating-period receipt is no such
credit. A full bond without work does not reduce secured exposure; valid
replacement coverage must independently preserve actual remaining obligations.

At day0 an identifiable parcel/use/scale application receives protection. A
new ordinary plan at day25 cannot change that pending proposal's selected
substantive planning regime at its AP decision. Necessary proof and independent
source/safety duties still apply. Blank filings freeze nothing; lawful refusal
ends that application, material revision uses new receipt, and unlawful delay
retains remedy. Existing valid unsurrendered grants survive later plans within
their own terms, with exceptional statutory changes requiring lawful transition.

Zero general parking minimum is not zero accessible spaces. Independently
proved needs/standards govern actual access, drop-off and emergency/servicing,
not a percentage of an optional ordinary-parking count. Voluntary lawful parking
and existing private rights remain; public journeys need actual funded capacity.

Discretionary elected project permission may handle unusual values but risks
insider bargaining. Rule-bound permission can entrench exclusion in its rules.
National entitlements can constrain local exclusion but miss geography and
self-government. Public serviced-land/master development can coordinate works
and capture value but requires actual title/capital and limits on central power.
A founded elected region may match shared pressures but adds paid transition.
These are alternatives for affected choice, not failed designs by definition.
