# SD17–SD20: democratic commencement, failure and effective remedies

*Part of [C06/43](../43-country-source-and-delivery.md); design 106 candidate.*

**SD17 — Elected law owns the schedule.** Cabinet prepares one public package
through existing accountable administration; the Assembly controls taxes,
expenditure, material borrowing and exact new entitlement/cohort dates. Local
councils retain their competences and source law. Publish the same base facts,
unknowns, claims, incidence, ecological/FX exposures and full rival for opposition
and affected people before the material choice. DR's applicable legislative/
delegated-law routes remain; SD creates no alternative approval board or AI
sovereign. A model pass, private offer, silence or successful file check cannot
authorize public spending, institute taxes or bind an unwilling counterparty.

**SD18 — No readiness veto over due rights or elections.** Source/delivery
failure before a proposed expansion defeats a research launch claim. After
lawful commencement it is a delivery failure: keep the liability, provide actual
interim protection, accessible complaint and useful-time independent execution
under I/CP/E/B19 and the relevant service law. Only competent lawful revision
can change prospective burdens; officials cannot shift the start, impose means
conditions on commenced U, seize restricted assets or cancel voting to balance
accounts. A sunset of a power does not sunset a personal entitlement.

**SD19 — If the core itself fails, expose the political choice.** Compare
actual source/collection repair, paid replacement procurement, lawful producer/
asset changes, accepted voluntary finance and competent restructuring, with
losers and old priorities visible. A capacity note is not a new rationing power.
Existing narrow shortage/safety law governs only its actual scope and clock.
If no authorized safe substitute can receive a person, record the unmet useful-
time claim and continue urgent relief; a referral or unserved order is not a
remedy. Payment failure cannot become deemed taxpayer/creditor consent or labor
compulsion. Actual court/payment/receiver capacity must also be funded.

**SD20 — Prevent the protectors from becoming the government.** Keep elected
appointment/removal, career administration, independent source custody, court
review and actual execution traceable across the whole chain. Disclose common
controllers even where institutional names differ. Protect challengers' usable
information, campaign access and remedies while testing peak budgets; no funding
certificate may silence opposition. Assess recurrent assessment/valuation/data
and procurement power as well as expenditure totals. Same-controller agents
supply challenges, not affected-party consent, qualified independence or votes.
New communication/coordination methods may change how the package is understood;
understanding, delegation and consent remain separate, contestable acts.
