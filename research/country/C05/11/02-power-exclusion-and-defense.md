# Actual power, exclusion and a workable defense

Design 98 · Proposed research, not enacted law or verified operation.

**MP07 — Durable power.** A body is dominant only when original evidence shows it can materially
worsen price, wages, quality, availability or access in a defined relevant activity for at least
**12 consecutive months** without timely effective loss to usable alternatives, or controls a
nonduplicable essential input with the same practical result. Consider capacity/entry, buyer/worker
counterpower, legally payable imports, actual switching and public options. A big, successful or
temporarily sole provider is lawful absent MP08/09 conduct; public ownership gives no immunity. A
12-month ability/effect test can be established prospectively; no claimant must wait twelve elapsed
months for protection. Shorter independently established sector/civil rights remain.

**MP08 — Indispensable-input exclusion.** A dominant controller breaches if, after a specific
feasible request, it refuses or materially discriminates in access to an input the requester needs
to compete, duplication or effective substitution within 12 months is infeasible at lawful realistic
cost, supply is within the controller's available safe capacity, and the refusal is likely to remove
a viable independent constraint for at least 12 months. A due safety, privacy, security, credit or
resource limit is a defense only to its proved extent and with less restrictive workable options
considered. No perpetual free access, compelled confidential source-code transfer, uncompensated
labor or automatic below-cost tariff follows. Competent court sets a finite safe access price/term
after independent cost evidence if disputed.

**MP09 — Contract and predatory exclusion.** A dominant controller breaches if (a) its exclusivity,
tying, retaliation against lawful multi-homing, or conditional loyalty discount materially deprives
a reasonably efficient actual/potential rival of the demand/input needed to reach minimum viable
operations within 12 months, with no timely usable alternative; or (b) it knowingly prices below
attributable avoidable cost for a sustained campaign and can plausibly recoup through later control,
access or wage suppression. Prove causal foreclosure, costs, duration and feasible rival, not mere
lost sales or low consumer prices. Ordinary volume discounts, protection of paid investment and
genuine short-run promotions remain lawful when they do not meet these elements. No
political-opinion classification by the authority; C03/civil law separately protects equal political
access. Reasonably efficient means feasible under fair access and realistic entrant scale, not
forced replication of the incumbent's unlawful advantage; the law does not guarantee every rival's
success.

**MP10 — Necessary conduct and proof.** For MP08/09 the defendant may show documented safety,
privacy, genuine capacity shortage, lawful IP scope, verified nonpayment risk or efficiencies that
cannot be achieved by a materially less restrictive means; court weighs actual parties and
downstream workers/users. Inconvenience, incumbent margin, a merely predicted AI benefit or donor
favor is not enough. Authority proves market, dominance, specific conduct and likely material
exclusion under ordinary B14 civil merits; no fault is needed for proportionate cessation, while any
punitive MP/VD11 claim requires its separately specified fault and proof.

*Part of [the competition statute](../11-competition-and-economic-power.md); normative thresholds and institutions, no authority from publication.*
