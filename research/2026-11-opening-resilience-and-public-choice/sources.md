# Design495 observed reference and constructed comparison

- The [Danish Constitutional Act, sections 14–15, 33, 45–46 and 88](https://www.thedanishparliament.dk/-/media/sites/ft/pdf/publikationer/the-constitutional-act-of-denmark.pdf)
  provides the older appointment, confidence, composition, supply and lawful amendment paths. The [Interior Ministry's election results](https://www.valg.im.dk/valg/folketingsvalg/resultater-fra-seneste-folketingsvalg/)
  and the [3 June 2026 appointment instrument](https://stm.dk/media/nishv4wc/kgl-resolution-af-3-juni-2026.pdf)
  confirm the historical clock. They establish no contemporaneous shared outage, constitutional breach or counterfactual election result.
- The [Copenhagen May committee minutes](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Sundheds-%20og%20Omsorgsudvalget/m%C3%B8de-20052026/referat/punkt-13)
  give the municipal integration target. The [opening accounts](../settlement/first-year-obligations-and-receiving/01-common-opening-and-overlapping-care.md)
  carry national, city, regional and utility scopes separately. The [funding review](../2026-11-opening-funding-and-delivery/sources.md)
  identifies a possible funding line but supplies no provider or dated cash for the case.
- [NemKonto](https://www.nemkonto.dk/en) describes the default account for
  public payments. Its [payment-specific account](https://www.nemkonto.dk/en/citizen/nemkonto-for-citizens/payment-specific-account/)
  and [waiting account](https://www.nemkonto.dk/en/citizen/ventekonto/)
  documents show receipt alternatives and limits. The listed current payer scope of
  the waiting account is narrower than the scope of NemKonto itself. The study
  assumes neither that Copenhagen care pay uses that account nor that a manual
  payout is available at the fixture cutoff.
- [Danmarks Nationalbank's June contingency report](https://www.nationalbanken.dk/en/news-and-knowledge/press/archive/2026/danes-are-paying-more-digitally-making-offline-payments-an-even-more-important-contingency-26-06-2026)
  describes retail disruption and offline card, cash and instant-payment options.
  Retail resilience cannot establish timely payer settlement or resident care.
- The separately constructed [handover test](../settlement/government-handover-and-essential-service/01-one-handover-three-protected-deadlines.md)
  and [monetary stress case](../settlement/bank-and-currency-stress/01-one-month-under-three-regimes.md)
  motivate the modeled dependencies. Their budgets and incidents are not
  observations or pooled funds. The six cutoffs, route costs and receipt times
  in Design495 are authored assumptions.
