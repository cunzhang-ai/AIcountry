## Real alternatives and selected hypothesis

Treaty-first recognition gives negotiated reciprocal standards and potentially
less heterogeneous proof/cost, but may exclude people without an actual
usable agreement. Broader actor/defendant-based reach may improve external
victim access yet imposes competing commands and sovereignty/control risks.
Select connected domestic statutory cooperation with specific treaty/public
routes, not compulsory reciprocity for a person's basic ordinary civil claim.
Its diverse foreign-law and access costs may be worse than treaty-first;
keep that rival if actual networks/capacity support it.

B41–B52 defines actual forum links/accepted domestic transfer, scoped contract/
harm law and mandatory weaker-party protections, status/child/property
continuity, compensatory recognition, bounded interim relief, parallel cases,
one insolvency account, safe service/evidence and accountable cooperation.
P21–P24 defines territorial/limited personal criminal reach, ordinary foreign
finality and credit, protected extradition/actual handover and separate
international accountability. C10/06 separates justice transmission, foreign
affairs, Assembly commitments, courts and actual service receivers.

No server/account, foreign alert, data agreement, vendor/AI output or official
request creates coercive power. No new worldwide police authority, automatic
foreign law/fine/status, treaty membership or custody-clock reset. Actual
international duties/immunities are not discharged. P21's personal child-
protection exception to dual criminality is a proposed value choice, not
universal jurisdiction; P22 ordinary finality leaves a declared potential
impunity conflict for a precise further international-crime statute.
