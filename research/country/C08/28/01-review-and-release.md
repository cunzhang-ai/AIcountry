# RL01–RL05. Continued confinement requires a current reason

*Design 190 candidate. Part of [C08/28](../28-sentence-review-and-release.md).*

**RL01 — Apply the rule to the sentence actually being executed.** Apply RL
to operative domestic custodial sentences under the ordinary P/K code and
the national G code through G11. Use the law applicable to conduct before
18 and any stronger liberty rule. Pretrial remand, civil or clinical
protection, conflict custody and enforcement of foreign or international
judgments retain their own sources and clocks. Jurisdiction and an actual
transfer instrument determine the latter cases.

**RL02 — Establish the dates at sentencing and review automatically.**
The sentencing court records the imposed term, lawful expiry and point at
which credited same-case custody reaches half that term. Include earlier
custody and unlawful or relabelled deprivation as P16/G11 require. Use one lawful executable
term for overlapping same-course counts within the existing aggregate ceiling.
Credit the same elapsed custody once in that course. Independently applicable
distinct cases retain their actual authority and clocks.
Correction of a wrong calculation benefits the person at the actual date.
An appeal, facility transfer or replacement record supplies no fresh clock.

The court registry schedules the first review with enough time for real
preparation and a decision before the halfway point. Corrections opens
release planning at sentence receipt and supplies the original clock and
material evidence. The person receives usable notice and funded independent
counsel. If the halfway point is already reached at sentencing because of
credit, decide release at that hearing before authorizing further custody.
Earlier legality, leniency, humanitarian and ordinary appeal routes continue.

**RL03 — Presume release and place proof on the state.** At the halfway point
the ordinary civilian court orders release unless the state establishes
by clear and convincing evidence a specific, substantial and reasonably
near-term risk of death, serious bodily harm, sexual violation, coercive
confinement or severe exploitation of another person, and demonstrates why
feasible lesser measures cannot adequately protect them. Severe exploitation
here means forced labor, trafficking or coercive deprivation of essential
resources. Clear and convincing
means a firm conclusion on tested facts and their connection to the risk.
It supplies neither a numerical AI threshold nor a substitute for criminal
proof in a new case. The original judgment limits custody in every event.

Gravity and past conduct may inform an individualized assessment of a
current risk. Offense category, institutional convenience, poverty, lack of
housing or work, silence, refusal of optional treatment and a model score
alone are insufficient. The state must explain specific facts, uncertainty,
contrary material and practicable alternatives. The state must show that custody would remain necessary if it performed
its applicable receiving duties. Identify protection that can actually be
arranged, direct the responsible receiver to meet due obligations and record
any shortfall. A failed referral cannot supply the finding of necessity.

**RL04 — Decide at a fair hearing and give a finite order.** Use independently
assigned qualified judges, safe disclosure and meaningful response under P/OJ,
with defense assistance, interpretation and the opportunity to challenge
expert or official evidence. Victims may provide relevant evidence and seek
safety measures through a supported route. Neither attendance nor a victim's
agreement is a condition of decision. The court addresses protected facts
without relying on an unanswerable secret allegation.

An order continuing custody specifies actual findings, why lesser measures
fail and the next hearing and expiry. It may last at most six calendar months,
or three for conduct before 18, and ends earlier at the original expiry.
Schedule reconsideration automatically before that order ends and provide
earlier review on material change or unlawful conditions. Further confinement
requires a fresh finding and order under the same test.

**RL05 — Execute the release date.** Once the halfway point arrives, custody
under this sentence requires a valid, usable continuation order. Its absence
or expiry requires release by the named corrections executor. A timely valid
conditional release order governs that release. In the absence of such an
order, the release is unconditional and discharges the original custody
authority at the due time. A pending
application, missed hearing or unavailable receiver cannot hold the person.
The ordinary criminal appeal formation hears challenges to RL continuation,
conditions and recall, with urgent relief through existing liberty review.
An appeal or stay carries no custody authority beyond the RL deadline. Separate
actual lawful holds must identify their own grounds and clocks. Officers
execute a due release and report unmet protection or support immediately.
