# Refinance without disguising the intraday debt peak

*Design472 · Constructed debt mandate and cash comparison.*

The [Danish first-quarter reference](../opening-debt-and-cash-window/01-denmark-opening-quarter-and-release.md)
reports gross redemption and gross borrowing separately. Its aggregate
figures cannot establish an intraday debt peak, lawful borrowing limit
or unrestricted cash. This case uses authored units and times.

Opening debt is 100 and an ordinary gross outstanding limit is 100.
Ten is due at 09:00. The Treasury has two in usable cash above prior
standing uses and its minimum. A settled issue of ten is offered for
08:00. The two contracts otherwise meet the stated financing mandate.
An issue at 08:00 makes gross debt 110 until redemption actually
settles, even if end-of-day debt would again be 100.

| Exact choice | Gross outstanding peak | Usable outcome |
|---|---:|---|
| Ordinary limit and pay first | 100 | Eight short by 09:00 beyond protected uses. Retain original creditor and service claims. |
| Ordinary limit and borrow first | 110 | This breaches the ordinary ceiling. An expected repayment supplies no discharge. |
| Prior elected overlap for this issue, followed by actual redemption | 110 | Lawful in the stated window if the exact voted prerequisites and acceptable settlement hold. Debt reaches 100 at redemption. |
| Legally simultaneous matched exchange | 100 | Only if valid actual discharge and issuance take effect together on accepted terms. Verify creditor consent and receiving. No assumed match or legal netting. |

Select the preauthorized overlap where timely simultaneous exchange or
unrestricted prior funding is not verified. The contemplated vote
identifies the instrument, gross peak of 110, currency, end of the
finite issuance window and exclusive use of proceeds for these
maturities. Price the issue and its later servicing alongside the
earlier standing duties. The transaction does not establish a new
discretionary spending balance.

If the old redemption is rejected after the issue binds, gross debt
stays 110 and its existing claims and instruments survive. Keep the
proceeds restricted. Stop further use of this authority. Notify the
independent reviewer and elected bodies and follow the specified
settlement reconciliation and finance decision. In the fixture the
issuance window closes at the end of the day and a budget disposition
is due on the next lawful business day under the exact vote. Any
failure is published on its own timeline. No expiry erases an
outstanding debt or authorizes new borrowing, repeated overlap or
diversion of proceeds. The protected domestic and foreign-payment
calendars retain their original dates.
