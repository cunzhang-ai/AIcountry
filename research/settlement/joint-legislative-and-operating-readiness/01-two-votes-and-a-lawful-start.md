## Where both texts wait

On day 0 the Assembly adopts a schedule that requires the council's
H appropriation to be effective. The council votes on day 8 but
its appropriation requires the matching national schedule to be
effective. A minister reports two favorable votes. Neither act
provides the effective counterparty appropriation required by the
other. No lawful clerk, minister, court or AI can deem one to be
first merely because both bodies favor H. Existing service and
energy funds continue on their own authority.

Compare the arrangements before offering either vote. If the
council can lawfully pass a useful unconditional local source
schedule first, with known exposure if the national vote fails,
that is simpler. Use it where practical. Where both bodies
need protection against an unmatched commitment, use a common
activation event if expressly authorized under their respective
prior laws. Preserve each body's independent right to reject.

## A narrow common activation event

In a revised matched-text comparison, each vote adopts its own
exact conditional authorization on day 0 or day 8. The two
authorizations are formally valid before any transfer or lease.
Their operative appropriation provisions take effect only at the
verified common event defined by both laws: timely proof that
both exact instruments are valid, remain in force and match on
purpose, scope, sources, actual amounts and eligible dates. The
preannounced event is the same for both, and its latest valid
time is the end of day 10. Neither text requires the other
party's actual performance for this particular condition.
All remaining legal and operating conditions retain their own
effect. If actual provision or payment is itself a prerequisite,
require its actual completion or another lawfully voted sequence.

## When the missing prerequisite is actual funding

In a separate constructed energy case, a new charge has been
drafted to begin only after its direct household protection is
actually in place. That protection needs 3 lawful units and
accepted receiving by day 20. The charge could yield 5 by
day 30. Neither adopting both texts nor matching them earlier
supplies the 3. A separately authorized and settled launch
source could finance the protection first. Use actual receipts
for replenishment only where law permits. If the protected
household route still fails, the dependent new charge remains
uncommenced under the exact condition. Preserve any valid
preparation costs and existing household rights. Where prior
law already imposed a charge, correction follows its own
legal route. These are different terms and figures from H.
