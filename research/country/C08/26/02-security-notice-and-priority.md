# PT07–PT12: an enforceable debt is not automatically a first claim on everything

*Part of [C08/26](../26-property-transfers-and-secured-credit.md); prospective ordinary security.*

**PT07 — Function, attachment and a finite ceiling.** Treat an agreed
transaction securing payment/performance as security whatever its label,
including a price-retention sale, financed acquisition with obligatory/nominal-
price ownership transfer, or security assignment. For these new financed goods
acquisitions, PT03 buyer title is expressly subject to seller/financier security,
not secret full ownership against the buyer's other claimants. A genuine lease
with substantive lessor residual ownership/custody is not converted by duration.
Require a deliberate authenticated accessible security agreement, actual grantor
disposition power and an actual obligation supported by delivered value or an
enforceable value commitment. Identify collateral, debt/advance class, actual
interest/cost terms and a disclosed maximum total secured monetary amount,
including interest/costs. Only actual due valid obligations up to that ceiling
are secured; surplus valid debt is unsecured. No notional borrowing, valuation
or filing alone attaches security. Attachment to future identified business
assets/classes occurs only when actual rights are acquired. Blanket future
household goods, future labor earnings, benefits, accrued public pension,
essential disability equipment and U-protected necessities cannot be pledged
away under PT. A home is not universally exempt; H/U and safe realization apply.
No collateral includes another person's share, safeguarded NB/M property or
restricted public assets without their actual independently lawful authority.

**PT08 — Third-party effectiveness.** A valid ordinary movable security is
effective against third parties through a grantor-authorized notice actually
accessible in the public security index, or continuous actual consensual
possession of identified tangible goods by the creditor/independent custodian.
Leaving goods with the grantor or a controlled nominee is not possession.
The notice states safe stable party identifiers, collateral/class, maximum total
secured amount, start/expiry and contact for protected verification, not private
medical, family or complete loan records. No automatic consumer-goods perfection,
secret title retention, or extra bank-control priority is selected. Land
security instead needs PT05 recorded authority; a movable filing cannot pledge
land. A removable item remains movable unless actual annexation makes its
removal materially damage the structure/essential service: then land-right
consent and disclosed land security are needed; no post-annexation secret fixture
priority. Preserve genuine prior rights through PT23, not a new label.

**PT09 — Time, scope, renewal and release.** A signed grantor authorization
can permit advance notice for a specified future security/class/ceiling, but
creates no security until PT07 attachment. No commercially unrelated new debt
inherits a reserved old position. Choose a stated notice term of at most five
calendar years; a shorter agreement/source ends it earlier. Renewal during the
last six calendar months, with continuing actual authority and unchanged scope/
ceiling, preserves the continuous position. Additional collateral/ceiling takes
only its new effective time; cannot prime intervening claims. A lapse, cancellation
or unbridged loss of possession ends that position; later filing has a new time,
not a restored old rank. Changing method without a gap can preserve the same
position. Filing after a court insolvency opening does not cure earlier secrecy.
After actual discharge/end of commitment, release the notice and return pledged
goods within five working days, earlier safety/ownership duties prevailing.
Give a release on the undisputed separable portion; no fee or unrelated debt
holds it hostage. A false filing or disputed discharge has direct PT20/B relief.

**PT10 — Competing security and creditors.** Subject to actual special law,
rank attached perfected movable security by the earliest continuously effective notice
or qualifying possession for that same scope. An authorized advance notice can
therefore outrank a later filing although attachment occurs later. A perfected
interest outranks unperfected security, regardless of ordinary knowledge; proved
fraud/collusion remains separately challengeable. Between unperfected interests
outside insolvency use earlier actual attachment; a truly simultaneous rank
shares available proceeds pro rata by actual secured amounts. An unperfected
interest is only an unsecured estate claim at U opening. A judgment alone is
not security: a valid court levy/charge with actual protected public notice
competes from its effective time, without jumping prior perfected security.
For duly registered land security, PT05's earliest valid authorized lodgment
sets its position on completed registration; a movable notice supplies no rank.
U costs/exemptions, special M/NB rules, tax source limits and lawful existing
priorities retain their exact scope. An officer cannot write a new preference.

**PT11 — No hidden acquisition leap.** New equipment/stock finance and title-
retaining sellers use the same actual public-notice priority; no purchase-money
superpriority or grace period against intervening buyers/creditors. A later
financier needs an actual senior release/subordination or available unsecured
risk capacity. A senior cannot collect above its actual debt/ceiling or obstruct
release after discharge. The selected rule is simpler but can entrench a blanket
business lender and make equipment finance harder. Express pre-acquisition
purchase-money priority with advance senior notice is a substantive rival;
do not describe its rejection as a proven gain for borrowers or new enterprise.

**PT12 — Identifiable proceeds and one recovery.** Security covers actual
identifiable lawful disposal proceeds, including an identifiable payment claim,
within the same outstanding obligation/total ceiling. A registered notice's
express proceeds field preserves continuous original priority for those actual
proceeds; without that field, proceeds attach between parties but require their
own timely public notice for third-party effectiveness. Possession of an original
item alone does not perfect nonpossessed proceeds. No automatic tracing into all
future wealth. In a commingled money account, the maximum traceable amount is
the lower of original identifiable proceeds and the lowest intervening actual
balance, excluding proved others' owned funds; unrelated later deposits do not
replenish it. Actual tracing disputes need a court, not a bank/model sweep.
PT security in the grantor's actual account claim gives no ownership of bank
reserves or another depositor's/client's assets. Actual M/NB settlement/custody
and protected receipt control apply; no invented negotiable-money exception.
If materials become inseparable in an authorized mass/product, security reaches
only their proved attributable input-value share capped by the secured debt;
separate contributors share that reachable value proportionately, preserving
others' property and stronger existing rights. Neither processing nor a token
multiplies collateral. Realization of original item and proceeds credits the
same debt once; total collection cannot exceed actual valid unpaid obligation.
