# Recurring taxes: service rights and insurance cash are different ledgers

*NF33–NF36; part of [C06/42](../42-recurring-tax-and-noncash-work.md).*

**NF33 — Genuine independent work has its own net source.** Self-employed in-
kind fees enter actual net personal-labor earnings after genuine TA05 costs/
capital separation, alongside cash fees. A genuine independent client is not
an employer by payment medium: no automatic g+c payroll or access to the
supplier's other income. Parties may choose a disclosed sufficient cash component.
Annual new net-labor Y self-assessment/payment is due30April next year with
the personal return, separately routed to S and never client withholding.
The worker bears actual contribution/tax and liquidity risk with TA/
TX protected needs/installments/direct court, not seizure of U/I05 or a
fictional receipt. Record S debt/payment and proved source work separately;
no contribution credit is cash funding. A disguised employment relationship
uses actual B/work-law evidence and NF30, not contractual relabeling. Mutual
informal aid/genuine gifts are not trade because an agent helped deliver them.

**NF34 — Real compensated service period, no duplicate year.** Gross covered
work includes actual cash labor plus P once. Attribute remuneration to actual
compensated work/paid-leave periods, not an extra service year at late payment.
Arrears/bonuses keep evidenced period allocation/source W and correct existing
units; no synthetic second year. Benefit accrual uses its actual source-year
ceiling: RF06's1W only from its later lawful commencement, earlier source
ceilings (including the prior2W candidate) are not accelerated by NF collection, with uncapped10% contribution,1.25% accrual/best48 and
original revaluation/survivor rights. Amount above the applicable source-year ceiling is solidarity, not units
or personal savings. Preserve all prior earned units and stronger source law.

**NF35 — Care credits and real resources.** Y06 extra credit is the positive
difference between .5W×actual eligible period and already covered gross work
for that same period, within the annual cap. Paid leave/P already counted earns
no duplicate credit; no credit from low pay alone. G transfers10% of actual
additional credit base to S, not a permanent unexplained lump sum. Distinct
necessary carers may qualify for their own actual time without forced family
work/marriage. Explain actual annual base/credit/units and payment/default.

Deemed g/c statutory discharge is not worker cash for I05. A safely delivered
necessary in-kind component reduces only its matching need once; a private
luxury cannot pay rent/food or be reimbursed as a need. Saleable actual proceeds
have their own usable-resource treatment, no double cash+component subtraction.
Unsafe/ceased benefit restores the actual unmet component/receiving claim. Thus
equal tax/Y package cost may yield different residual awards; do not claim total
cash/in-kind parity or presume assent from a household head.

**NF36 — S owns received contribution, not the whole tax package.** Revenue's
protected contribution case officers determine/collect actual Y under this
express scope; social-protection career officers certify service/credit and
keep protected source statements. Separate S payer/custody handles actual
receipts/refunds, G Treasury handles tax and enacted care-credit transfers.
Explicitly extend RG/TX notice, independent full merits/court, source powers,
neutral interest/refund and bounded collection to the new noncash Y duties,
with S-specific payer/credit routing; no self-certifying director or CJ12 shortcut.
Old lawful Y liabilities/periods keep their original law and stronger remedies.
Y10 advance pension and RF08 four-month post-due usable reserve remain; new
receivables, gross employer cost or an annual surplus cannot fund a pre-due bill.
Price new accrued liabilities/default/public credit and75-year/tail uncertainty,
not just a first-year S revenue gain or unsupported permanent worker cohort.
