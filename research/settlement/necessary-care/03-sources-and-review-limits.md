# Necessary care: original sources and their limits

*Design82; limited legal-text analysis, not qualified clinical/legal endorsement.*

[CRPD General Comment1](https://documents.un.org/doc/undoc/gen/g14/031/20/pdf/g1403120.pdf)
(19May2014), selected paragraphs13–22,27–29 and40–42, distinguishes legal capacity
from assessed decision skill, favors actual will/preferences and rejects substitute
medical consent. The [2018 corrigendum](https://docstore.ohchr.org/SelfServices/FilesHandler.ashx?enc=XBJrja1NI23jjzoD%2BXg0DS7wIHEnIp93rTAvLUIgIGhNhE2likgU4AkIThrKpkkNWeW7IQ3hrAlle3kxQv83LLDoo%2BSXjSfNEtrtgzN4f%2FU%3D)
changes paragraph27's regime characteristics to alternatives. Thus nominally
retained capacity, single-act scope and judicial supervision cannot establish
Committee compatibility. UA deliberately preserves the substantive objection;
it does not claim that 'necessity' is source-approved supported consent.

[CRPD Article12](https://www.un.org/development/desa/disabilities/convention-on-the-rights-of-persons-with-disabilities/article-12-equal-recognition-before-the-law.html)
was located in official search excerpts by a task agent. Root direct UN/OHCHR
HTML opens failed403; no complete treaty inspection/party or reservation review
is claimed. The Committee texts were actually readable. These are one UN-system
family and interpretive evidence, not observed social outcomes or a public mandate.

[England and Wales' Mental Capacity Act section4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/9/section/4)
provides a genuine best-interests comparator, addressing participation, recovery,
wishes/values and life-sustaining-treatment motivation. [Section6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/9/section/6)
defines restraint to include movement restriction without resistance, with specified
limitations. Direct rendered opens failed; official section XML bodies were fetched
and read on15October2026 through the publisher's data.xml endpoints. This selected
inspection does not certify the entire amended Act, case law or clinical practice.
Its capacity-based structure is not silently imported into UA.

The [source register](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-necessary-care-and-unknown-will/sources.json)
records access, failed rendered/XML tool routes and actual alternative retrieval.
Five documentary entries/two institutional families are not independent empirical
studies. No publisher-byte archive, full medical evidence review, actual clinical

Illustrative procedures in the authored cases have no actual patient indication.
