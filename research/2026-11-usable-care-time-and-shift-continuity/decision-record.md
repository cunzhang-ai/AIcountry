# Design199 decision. Cover the actual care interval without discarding the child's ordinary place

*9 November 2026 · Research selection · Exact design198 predecessor.*

## Situation and choice

S10 already provides optional care without a work test. NC08 already
requires a compatible journey and receiving calendar. FC provides local
delivery and national gap and fallback finance. TW/WL governs notice,
worker choice and urgent employment changes. The remaining positive
gap was a route for a small late need while the ordinary place, work
and transport each appear available. Select CW01–CW08 for organized
early care. Preserve the ordinary place, assess the necessary interval
and use the ordinary assessed family fee basis within the adopted
floor. Existing fee law applies pending competent funded transition.
[Canonical choice](../country/C07/24-usable-care-time-and-shift-continuity.md)
· [Exact predecessor](baseline.json)

An employer or competent public scheduler should repair an avoidable
conflict within its own authority and duties. A worker cannot be
forced to change shifts to remedy a shortage. If compatible scheduling
does not work, use paid targeted provision with stable relationships,
including an accepting intermittent public route. Keep optional chosen
leave, home care and direct budgets on their own terms. A public
authority or employer cannot treat its deadline as a mandate over a
child, another worker or the family's private calendar.

## Evidence, alternatives and objections

The Copenhagen combination rule's ten-hour average minimum and
family-arranged care do not cover every small need. The separate
round-the-clock option and lawful local retain/repair remain real.
Actual admissions and invoices are unknown. Helsinki distinguishes
intermittent care from a long-term placement and publishes a route
for short-notice requests with capacity constraints. Adopt the
functional distinction while retaining AIcountry's broader qualifying
need and labour protections. The authored paired case compares
retiming the originating shift, extension, pooled late care and cash.
[Comparator evidence](../settlement/usable-care-time/01-copenhagen-and-helsinki.md)
· [Matched case](../settlement/usable-care-time/02-one-household-and-one-shift.md)

A blanket restoration could benefit geographically dispersed and
regular need but assumes scarce posts and safe uptake. An occasional
shared service can save staffing where safe demand clusters and
introduce burdensome travel where it does not. Verify the child's
own experience, worker rest and effects on ordinary provision.
Moving a provider or paying for equivalent late care requires actual
workers, public money and a feasible return. Fee parity is a selected
distributional choice with explicit net cost, no price estimate.
Local trials retain earlier dues, real alternatives, a safe rollback
and ordinary authority. Defeat this selection where simpler repair
offers comparable access, continuity and voice without shifted harm.

## Continuing authority and what reopens the decision

Preserve S10 additional scope, NC02–NC08, FC advance and 30-day
exceptional limits, CL and TW/WL protections, MO actual access,
AP/E deadlines, SR delivered relief, FB/DB source control and
paid OP/T and HT handover. No common calendar delegates authority
or transfers consent. Preserve the ordinary place and record actual
unmet needs until a paid lawful successor receives them. Preserve
original evidence and source limits; no inherited final review.

This research obtained no actual affected-party participation,
qualified independent assessment, adoption, paid implementation
or lived outcome. The next bounded test follows a provider, one
otherwise excluded family and a competing user of the backup
through an observed window, actual bills, staffing and independent
challenge. Reopen on revealed exclusion, displaced ordinary work,
worse child continuity, lack of a real payer or equal less disruptive
provision. The 40-hour reference and all illustrative rota inputs
remain hypotheses with distinct scopes.
