# HG27–HG30. Fund earlier care where the actual pathway fails

*Design517 · Proposed application of [health and patient control](../05-health-service-and-patient-control.md).*

**HG27 — Keep service and legal clocks separately reportable.** For a priority pathway report the first request, applicable referral and decision dates, workup start and completion, accepted treatment start, clinical or patient time outside the ordinary schedule, and each legally relevant offer or escalation. Preserve the original clock alongside any legitimately adjusted clock. Publish the measured denominator, case definition, cutoff, pathway attrition and unobserved scope. A legally complete referral duty may coexist with late or unverified care. Use lawful independent review on both findings, without treating every later start as a breach.

**HG28 — Keep responsibility through transfer.** The host authority tracks the case until a named qualified provider accepts responsibility under the applicable law. Record whether an offered appointment was realistically usable: accessibility, transport, interpretation, extra costs, relevant follow-up and informed choice. A genuine choice to defer and medically appropriate waiting retain their own clinical meaning. Where a person declines because the offered route is unusable, keep the barrier visible and verify an independently reachable alternative. Keep the existing duties and original remedy active unless changed by the actual applicable law. An independent reviewer can check timely effort, lawful duty, safety and received care separately. An audited handoff preserves custody and notice without requiring unrelated disclosure.

**HG29 — Put a scoped service motion to the first budget.** A regional or equivalent service owner traces a defined pathway and location across the original dates before buying a related expansion. Compare receiving-hospital coordination, access support and accepted standby against confirmed local diagnostic or treatment capacity. Where quantified reasons or cohorts cannot support a diagnosis, fund a time-limited ordinary audit and useful assistance for already due work. Preserve the known waiters, unsuccessful referrals and people not yet inside the formal intake frame in separate counts or defensible bounds. Add qualified physical capacity where repeated timely receiving or source verification actually shows it is the constraint. Compare the strongest feasible ordinary repair using the same public resources. Show any lost appointment, origin or new-provider duty and the next service window.

**HG30 — Publish improvement without making the measure a new eligibility rule.** A public report separates statutory administration, actual timing, delays by reason, still-open or unobserved cases and treatment receipt and later safety. Use independent interpretation and protect small or confidential groups. Published aggregate rates do not establish the circumstances of a particular person. A model can reconcile event definitions and find inconsistencies. Clinicians retain safe priorities and patient consent; elected bodies decide funded service methods; disputed rights use their existing review path. Reopen on stopped transfers, missing people, recurrence after the first appointment or credible evidence favoring a different repair.
