# LR11–LR15: agenda power, finite urgency and a real default

*Part of [C03/14](../14-local-law-and-public-control.md); design112 candidate.*

**LR11 — A chair cannot own the only route to a hearing.** Any actual elected
councillor may introduce a scoped proposal or request a rule/legacy response
without mayor/committee permission. A supported petition by min(20,ceil(E_term/100))
distinct affected natural people, at least1 for a positive reference, also earns
protected consideration; E_term is the council's published lawful call electorate,
not a later partisan estimate. Affected nonvoters/outsiders can join; their input
creates no municipal vote/sovereignty. Authentic supported personal assent counts
once; copies/agents or a representative do not multiply a person's mandate.
Children/other persons retain their own lawful supported/representative rights;
no fabricated consent is required to bring their existing legal claims.

Give free ordinary-language intake/assistance, protected identity/evidence and
one necessary defect/help notice within24h. List a qualified member/petition item
within7calendar days, and provide a reasoned draft/action/no-change/source response
for consideration at a protected sitting no later than receipt+30days, normally
with7days notice. This is a right to actual consideration/reasons, not guaranteed
adoption or bypass of LR07/08. Ordinary single-person reports still get competent
source/rights response; they need no petition to seek individual protection.
Missing official electorate records receive court/source help, not a fictitious
zero or an excuse to erase a timely credible request.

**LR12 — Ready rules have protected vote time.** Clerk lists each complete L
packet within7days and reserves a scrutiny sitting at noon L+30 at the lawful
place/authorized accessible alternate; earlier L+8…30 votes need7days actual
notice. Chair, lawful deputy, then prepublished open-lot rotation of consenting
unconflicted nonexecutive councillors preside. Draw at first sitting or before
LR commencement for an existing council, with disclosed conflicts and no chair
shortlist. Registrar/clerk mechanically convenes/records but has no legislative
vote. No lawful presider/quorum/source/time means no decision, not a fake sitting.
Ordinary shared resident/taxpayer interests are not automatic mass recusal;
specific personal conflicts retain C04/ordinary review, never lower full n.

Actual lawful remote/alternate sitting needs existing legal authority, member
identity and preserved expressed vote, public access/record and answerable source,
not an agent's inferred attendance. A petition response sitting cannot turn an
unconsulted text into law. Protected motions retain distinct reasons/decisions;
no giant unrelated bundle conceals a new charge or access cut. Related actual
requests may share a hearing while each pending duty/loss stays visible. Backlog
triggers capacity/source/elected choices, not a new claim-expiry quota.

**LR13 — Urgency is prior authority, not a timetable escape.** A temporary
new general norm may bypass LR ordinary stages only under an already enacted
specific higher/source clause naming issuer, observable grave danger, scope,
permitted urgent means and outer expiry. Prove why existing law/individual lawful
protection cannot suffice, minimal effects and actual receiver/funding. Immediate
usable publication and Clerk/council notice≤24elapsed hours/earlier useful time,
with direct B17 review, remain. Expiry is the EARLIER of source limit and72elapsed
hours from the first actual related effect; partners, renamed versions or software
cannot reset it. No new generic mayor emergency, crime/tax/force/data power or
appropriation. Sector/C10/CP lawful powers retain their own distinct source and
shorter constraints, not an assumed extension from this LR exception.

A council vote alone does not waive missing consultation/final notice. Continued
general effect needs completed ordinary LR or an actually competent higher primary
Act expressly authorizing its own process/means. If unavailable, the temporary
norm ends; existing lawful rescue/service acts continue at their real scope,
not as the expired norm relabeled. A dangerous protection gap may remain and
requires honest national/local elected source response, not self-renewal.

**LR14 — Failure and repeal have defined legal effects.** An unapproved, unmade,
withdrawn or lapsed new proposal never binds. Its proposed repeal never erases
a valid old rule. A lawfully made replacement's later expiry/annulment does NOT
automatically revive its predecessor; source statute/court states the residual
law and continuing duties. Courts can urgently stop an unlawful burden without
waiting for policy consultation. To repeal a lawful old policy, use the ordinary
competent LR/primary route; members/petitions can force response/ready-vote steps,
not a positive vote. The majority may retain a harmful lawful baseline; that
status-quo loss is real, not cured by a calendar or consultation report.

**LR15 — Council succession is not proposal succession.** The council's actual
legal end lapses unfinished new proposals, including approved but unmade delegated
texts. An election call alone does not end it. Made valid laws continue until
own expiry/replacement; no outgoing chair gains new ordinary policy power under
EL caretaker limits; pending approval does not authorize making in breach of
those limits. A new council needs fresh final L/approval and renewed input
where new effects/dependencies require; compatible underlying input can be reused
only at its complete valid scope, with new events/objections checked. Old pending
claims/orders, wages/contracts, grants and useful-time duties retain source dates.
