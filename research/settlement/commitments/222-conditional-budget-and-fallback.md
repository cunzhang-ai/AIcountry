# Design309. Conditional budget and fallback

The [budget rules](../../country/C06/60/09-conditional-budgets-and-protected-fallback.md)
carry separate competent decisions into the exact final schedule.
A temporary reservation is limited to legally available uncommitted
national resources. A failed commencement condition restores a
protected vote and releases that reservation under the enacted terms.
An independently operative instrument, accrued claim, elected term
or lawful contract retains its own effect. No transfer of local power
or official ability to choose a different budget follows.

The [decision and migration record](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-conditional-budget-and-fallback/decision-record.md)
identifies legal review, actual time, evidence, authority and unresolved
operational capacity. Pending conditional decisions need clear terms
under the actual transition law before the rule is applied.
