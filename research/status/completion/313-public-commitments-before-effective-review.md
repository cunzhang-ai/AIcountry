# Design405. Keep public choices open until a remedy can be heard

[PCR01–PCR08](../../country/C03/01/02-public-commitments-before-effective-review.md)
requires usable notice and a finite concurrent interval before scheduled acts
that would materially foreclose a public alternative. It preserves due
services and requires actual execution of narrow independent relief.

[Worked case](../../settlement/public-commitments-before-effective-review/01-the-depot-lease-and-the-review-window.md)
tests incomplete notice, a material revision, early exposure, failed handoff
and protected payroll. Real review and execution capacity remain unverified.
