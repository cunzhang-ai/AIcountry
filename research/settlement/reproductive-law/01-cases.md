# Ten authored boundaries and the person who still needs receiving

*Support for [RP](README.md); no actual patients, judgments or consultations.*

| Case | Proposed treatment and remaining duty |
|---|---|
| RL01 A young person needs confidential care and fears home disclosure | B22/RP01 supports actual understanding, with presumption from 16 and younger choice possible. No blanket family veto. RP02/S03 requires timely accepting care and private access; donor/third-party-assistance 18 differs. Actual clinician/transport/court capacity is not established. |
| RL02 A former partner withdraws consent to an untransferred embryo | RP03/RP09 stops new transfer despite sunk cost or an intended parent's wish. Storage/preservation receives separate authority and funding. A court cannot manufacture consent; after actual transfer no contributor dictates the pregnant person's decision. |
| RL03 Friends record a nonparent donation outside a clinic | A genuine pre-use scoped record can establish the RP03 exception; no clinic/account monopoly. A backdated label cannot erase an existing child's duty. Court proves actual consent/role and B30/I/S receives support meanwhile; genes or signatures alone decide neither status nor guilt. |
| RL04 A gestating person refuses post-birth transfer after expenses were paid | Prenatal surrender is unenforceable; post-birth consent is revocable 30 days before finality. No forced handover, repayment tied to surrender or loss of care. Independent court hears temporary care, relationships, support and actual post-birth will. Intended adults never bought custody. |
| RL05 Intended adults withdraw when a child has an unexpected condition | RP07's actual pre-use commitment can ground support and a lawful status order; no characteristic/relationship escape. No forced personal caregiving either. Child receivers supply actual care while private responsibility/means is adjudicated. Promised private payment is not received public relief. |
| RL06 A donor-conceived adult requests origin after the clinic closes | RP04 preserves a minimal sealed link with accepted custody; adult access needs no donor/parent veto. Identity is not compelled contact, present address or automatic parenthood. Safety may change secure delivery, with challenge. Unknown records are unknown, not guessed from mandatory DNA tracking. |
| RL07 Stored material remains when fees stop or an owner dies | RP09 requires usable notice, protected hold, accepted transfer/review and actual funding. Debt/kinship grants no use mandate. RP10 needs specific prior posthumous consent; estate eligibility and inherited claims are separately assessed, never a reason to deny an already born child's care. |
| RL08 A foreign gestation arrangement has an unlawful payment term | RP11/B45 reviews actual authority, notice/consent and severable child status/support. Refusing child sale does not erase identity or authorize forced return/detention. No presumed global treaty supplies recognition; keep receiving and direct court relief. |
| RL09 A lab changes an embryo-like construct's label to evade the clock | RP13/RP14 requires independent functional classification before new work; no software/clinic self-exemption. The 14-day boundary is not safety proof or clinical permission. Protect unrelated lawful research, actual material consent and review. |
| RL10 A child exists after an unauthorized unfamiliar reproductive technique | RP14 provides provisional identity, safe care and independent representation. It does not retrospectively permit the experiment, appoint an AI as parent or declare the child rights-free. Operator V/RPO/K/G liability retains its own elements, defenses and adjudication. |

The cases preserve substantive disagreement; they do not measure harm rates,
quality, acceptance, independent review or actual receiving. A clinician,
origin custodian, gestating-person adviser and child advocate cannot all be
one interested provider simply carrying different labels.
