# HG06–HG10. Coverage, money and treatment are different decisions

*Part of [public clinical service](../05-health-service-and-patient-control.md).* 

**HG06 — Elected coverage, publicly reasoned technical detail.** S01–S04
necessary-care entitlement remains the basis, including no job/contribution/
citizenship/paperwork or point-of-service debt gate. Assembly law decides
material exclusions, experimental/high-cost general coverage and rationing
changes under rights, clinical/affected evidence and challenge. Exact technical
annexes/formularies within valid delegation use TD/DR and published evidence,
uncertainty/version/control; a vendor, purchaser, clinician guild or ministerial
budget instruction cannot secretly narrow S. NCS may propose revision, not
approve its own new powers. A product approval is no covered/reached treatment;
a new research/compassionate intervention needs its own actual source/consent.
Use of a lawfully supplied product outside its marketed indication does not
alone establish research status or exclusion from S coverage.

**HG07 — The individual clinical plan belongs to competent care and choice.**
The actual treating team assesses indication/urgency, explains options/uncertainty
and supports the person's informed will under B/RP/UA/mental-health law. No
household head, fiscal-worth/AI score, coverage officer or politician consents
for the patient. Material second opinion and supported interpretation remain
available; qualification is scoped to actual practice. A protected objection or
refusal does not cancel ordinary care, relief or political standing. S03's
seven-calendar-day usable routine assessment and earlier E safe-time duties
still govern the complete path; referral/review does not restart them.

**HG08 — Make an adverse public decision reachable.** Purchasing/access
administration names the actual officer, lawful boundary, facts/clinical advice,
missing evidence and whether the problem is coverage, payment, delay or absent
capacity. It preserves AP protected first receipt/help and earlier clinical
protection, not a complete-file or private-insurance gate. Urgent stabilization,
due continuity/relief and ordinary assessment cannot wait for a purchasing panel.
Give usable reasons and routes directly to outside HG11 review/ordinary courts;
no mandatory internal exhaustion or compulsory combined tax/health account.
An appeal does not turn a disputed treatment into automatically safe/approved
care, but cannot be used to conceal unlawful delay or remove existing support.

**HG09 — Fund needs and real available capacity, not an internal billing game.**
Assembly health appropriations/Treasury lawful source fund NCS's needs-adjusted
network budgets, fixed competent capacity, actual case mix and qualified cover;
monitor safe quality/receipt and actual private/public input payments. A pure
visit target cannot reward selection of easy cases or hide unmet need. Public
networks cooperate under their duties with explicit real resource allocation,
not optional internal contracts whose payment dispute blocks a patient. NCS
purchasing still applies actual SC price/lifecycle/source to external provision.
No automatic saving, new levy/debt/guarantee, restricted pension/private sweep
or invented public surplus. Shortage remains unmet entitlement with lawful
interim/cross-network receiving and elected source response; NCS cannot enact
a new general triage rule, borrow secretly or claim that its budget met every duty.

**HG10 — Clinical freedom is accountable, not a right to abandon.** Managers
cannot order an unsafe/nonconsensual act or politically selective care; clinicians
need relevant facts, scope/competence, disclosure/conflict control and fair
challenge. Publicly owed care cannot be sold privately to that patient through
a self-interested referral. Material clinical refusal needs a reason, safe
continuity and lawful actual alternative where available; no one is ordered to
perform an unsafe act to satisfy a finance officer. NCS arranges qualified cover
within actual work/exit/rest law; a shortage is no permanent forced employment.
Existing civil/professional/criminal responsibility requires its own source,
fault/proof and independent process, not adverse outcome or an AI flag alone.

adds separately funded appraisal, starts, review and exit to the
conditional offer. The NCS purchaser retains its lawful scope,
and the clinical and elected decisions remain distinct.
