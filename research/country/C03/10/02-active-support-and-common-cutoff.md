# EN05–EN12: active support, a fixed gate and timely source correction

*Part of [C03/10](../10-democratic-entry-and-campaign-resources.md); design111 EL revision.*

**EN05 — Two actual active entry permissions.** Each EL01 eligible person may
support at most two distinct national lists at once for this called poll, with
free revocation/reassignment until V−40. This is entry, not their vote, public
credit, party membership or agreement with every nominee/plank. Copies/agents/
accounts do not multiply it; one person cannot use both slots for the same list.
Source meaning can be recorded by paper, supported speech or future media with
original assent, scope, time, safe custody and challenge, not a compulsory wallet.
No public supporter directory, paid/loyalty/coerced signature or self-delegating AI.

**EN06 — The national and local list threshold is explicit.** Under EL06 freeze
positive E_call and fixed N (or local n) at lawful call. Require
s=min(ceil(E_call/100),ceil(E_call/(4N))) active eligible support per list at V−40.
This replaces the predecessor fixed national1% and separate territorial gates
only in new coordinated EL scope. It never raises that old national threshold,
but former district independents may face greater organizing cost. Missing roll/
N is unsupported context, not a fictional zero or administrative cap on rivals.

**EN07 — Bound the field honestly.** With actual eligible cutoff endorsers R,
qualified lists≤floor(2R/s). R need not equal E_call: corrected/known-P eligibility,
new enrolment and lawful diaspora remain. Apply favorable proved official roll
correction commonly with reasons/help; no selective denominator after seeing
rivals. After-cutoff proof can establish an actual lawful pre-cutoff act, not
invent a new supporter. Actual distinct nominees K additionally bound total
names≤min(2N×lists,K); do not assume K=E_call or count one person twice.
A larger lawful field creates a source/work duty, not a hidden200-list ceiling.

**EN08 — Preserve each act and safe proof.** EA career officers receive each
assent/revocation once and reconcile actual protected originals across channels.
Publish totals/defects, not private political links. A valid later change replaces
the earlier active instruction; an office's old token or replicated submission
cannot hoard more slots. Alternative proof, state-held facts and assisted records
must work before the decision becomes useless. Unknown semantics require help/
clarification and source disclosure, not silently dropping an unfamiliar medium.

**EN09 — Whole calendar before first voting.** Ordinary call/filing is at least
V−90, early/supplement/repeat V−60; V is first lawful casting and P=V+14 for the
reference. Manifest/assent/endorsement facts freeze V−40, final qualification
within five calendar days thereafter (ordinarily V−35), proof cure to V−30,
normal ballot/guide production V−21. Preliminary help within five days of first
receipt and guide within48h after qualification remain. No late nomination or
predicted eligibility by silence. C14 lawful founding/map/roll/source and C03/
EL repeat authority precede call, not vice versa.

**EN10 — No political shortcut through administration.** Designated EA career
nomination/records officers apply objective rules in their own enacted scope,
with preassigned qualified conflict substitutes, actual source and EA16 direct
courts. Cabinet/commissioner/finance head cannot select a named opponent's fate.
A commission meeting/quorum is not a clerk's new policy power or a hold on the
clerk's own statutory act. Local officers have their actual C04 employer and
common source/court floor, not a national shortcut to appoint a council.

**EN11 — Cure and receive a useful remedy.** Give one precise necessary defect/
help notice within24h, accept lawful alternative proof and decision within the
applicable five-day/earlier useful time. No serial reset. Timely cutoff facts
can be corrected under ER/EL04 through direct E13/B17 urgent ordinary courts,
source-linked reply/counsel and real corrected ballot/guide/payment/receiving.
V−21 print is no immunity for a proved wrong. Neither a finance allegation nor
a missed deadline creates qualification, a ban, a winner or extra polling day.
Late relief is only mitigation of already lost opportunity; preserve harm/claims.

**EN12 — Retain the whole-choice cost.** Two active slots, seat-related/1%-bounded
gate,2N pools and segmented preference are contestable hypotheses. Incumbent
hoarding, retaliation, organizing/care cost, large fields and concentrated-person
loss remain. EL's single PR/closed/mixed/regional rivals require actual full-field
paid support and safe affected-user comparison. Exact probes are not actual
franchise/control evidence, adoption, funded source or proof of superior democracy.
