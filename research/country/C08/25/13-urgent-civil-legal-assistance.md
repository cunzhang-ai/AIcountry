# C08. Make independent civil legal help available before essential loss

*Design412 · UCL01–UCL08 · Proposed ordinary civil and administrative legal assistance.*

A person can have a court right and lose their home, subsistence or essential
service before obtaining the help needed to exercise it. Fund early advice,
conflict-free urgent representation where necessary, and a usable handoff
to the court and the actual remedy.

**UCL01 — Give free first assistance on a credible urgent civil account.**
For threatened loss of a primary home, essential supply, statutory subsistence
or another expressly scheduled serious civil or administrative interest,
offer free initial advice and help to preserve access before the useful
deadline. Accept direct or assisted contact through ordinary local, court,
phone and offline routes under B13 and AP. A credible account is enough
to start. Do not require a complete financial file, platform identity,
written notice where unavailable, or approval from the party complained
against. Preserve criminal defense and stronger specialized rights under
their own law. Ordinary litigation and full representation retain
their separately enacted scope.

**UCL02 — Authorize timely representation when a fair chance requires it.**
A qualified, conflict-free assistance officer may approve the least scope
needed to preserve a meaningful chance to seek urgent lawful relief.
Identify the threatened harm, last useful start, practical ability to
proceed, any timely safe alternative and the reason for the service.
Use an initial grant for a plausibly arguable need where ordinary means
or fuller merits checks would consume the useful window. No upfront charge
or security for the urgent grant. Give a prompt reasoned and independently
reviewable decision if refused or narrowed. Approval, referral or absence
of counsel does not decide the underlying rights, stay an order or create
a court remedy. Continuing aid uses its enacted need, complexity and means
rules, with safe notice and transition before any funded handoff ends.

**UCL03 — Keep provision independent of the respondent and the court.**
Establish a bounded civil legal assistance service with a separate enacted
budget and no authority over courts, prosecution or regulators. Select a
qualified career director through C04 open competition, a public shortlist,
protected opposition scrutiny and a recorded majority of the full Assembly.
Use a single six-year term. A recorded majority of the full Assembly may
remove for proved serious misconduct, persistent material statutory failure
or inability after accommodation, with fair reply and direct court review.
Name a qualified acting successor before vacancy, with up to 180 calendar
days of ordinary management and no power to renew that period. Career
officers retain their lawful case duties. The director administers commissioning,
capacity and payment. No politician, donor, court administrator or director
may direct the substance of advice, an individual claim or a settlement.
The professional owes duties to the client and under ordinary law.

**UCL04 — Fund conflict-free receiving, including both sides where due.**
Contract qualified local, nonprofit, cooperative or other providers under
ordinary open rules and retain a distinct paid alternate. Check conflicts
and shared staffing before disclosure of sensitive facts. A provider
with an adverse client or a material control conflict cannot become the
person's lawyer by referral or organizational renaming. Protect the
individual's informed choice of counsel within qualified available capacity.
Where parties have materially conflicting interests, provide separate
professionals and protected records where both qualify. If no suitable
provider accepts, the commissioning service retains the routing duty
and reports the gap without exposing privileged material.

**UCL05 — Count all the time and authority needed for useful relief.**
Match counsel, interpretation, evidence access, client consultation,
court or agency capacity, lawful notice and a qualified executor on one
calendar. Protect existing cases and staff rest. Count a shared lawyer,
interpreter or funding release once. Pay necessary preparation and
conflict cover before the last useful start. Report acceptance, hearing,
order, execution and actual receipt separately. Ordinary direct court
access continues when legal help fails. Use B17 or the competent sector
route for actual urgent protection within its existing powers. A late
appointment leaves an earlier missed opportunity open.

**UCL06 — Preserve privilege, voice and separate decisions.** Keep advice
and client records under professional privilege and applicable law.
Share the minimum necessary scheduling, expenditure and receipt evidence
with oversight. Give the client interpretation, disability support,
safe contact and a usable route to replace assistance or contest its
scope. Do not condition aid on settlement, waiver, data sale or acceptance
of an AI tool. AI may help with client-approved drafting or lawful search
under professional supervision. It cannot decide eligibility, direct a
case, waive a claim or substitute for a confidential qualified adviser.

**UCL07 — Keep challenge and source control outside the commissioning chain.**
Give direct review of delay, refusal or provider conflict to an uninvolved
competent reviewer and the ordinary court within their respective powers.
A court can order an existing lawful assistance duty or grant available
relief. It cannot invent a lawyer or new appropriation. Treasury executes
authorized amounts without case direction. Use JC and DB to report shared
shortages and finance ordinary and surge service. Protect the court's
merits, respondent's reply and each affected person's independent access.
Publish safe aggregate misses and restored protection, without exposing
case strategy or small-group identities.

**UCL08 — Qualify real provision before commencing a class.** Enacted
schedules name eligible matters, sources, ordinary and urgent routes,
geographic access, alternates and actual oversight. Compare paid advice
alone, scoped representation and simpler or earlier resolution on the
same protections and deadlines. Keep independent professional choice
where simpler procedures fail. Carry clients, privileges, fees, prior
aid, filings and open remedies through any change of provider.
Reopen on recurrent conflicts, early missed deadlines, unpayable duties,
poor client control or a simpler equally protective route.
