## Diagnosis, original context and different models

Contracts, professional authority and centralized public employment can each
create power/dependence. One internal invoice may block care without changing
need; a merged service may remove that interface yet concentrate appointment,
clinical labor/source and information power. Separate purchaser/provider can
improve challenge or multiply denial gates. Neither better coordination nor a
new board automatically delivers care, retained trust or meaningful patient
agency. Scarce actual clinicians/inputs/source may be more important than form.

Direct selected WHO2016 original framework sections and resolution were read;
the framework explicitly does not require structural merger. WHO2019 governance
landing overview only, full403 uninspected. Exact raw PDFs, reading/capture/
shared-family limits in [sources](sources.md). No underlying clinical trials,
local original patient/staff records, matched ownership comparison or actual
independent reviewer obtained. Normative policy context is not a prescription
for our public employer/review architecture, numbers or superior outcomes.

Compare integrated public operation/internal purchasing/outside review;
separate pooled purchaser/autonomous providers; regional/local or worker/user
common networks; insurance-led plural; strong retain/repair. Same S rights,
source/control/paid workers, actual quality/receiving/foreign and old claims in
each. Retain the overall public-backbone/plural country, make its public core
concrete. No compulsory purchase monopoly or countrywide private takeover.
