# Candidate provisions A1–A3: Public law, legislation and publication

*Part of [Authority and legal transition](../../AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION.md); integrated design 179 research, not enacted law.*

**A1 — Public source and legal hierarchy.** Public authority arises from people
under a legitimately adopted constitution. Constitution, statutes, lawful regulations
and individual decisions have that order of precedence. Each binding instrument
has an exact public text, competent adopting body, effective date and challenge
route. An unpublished instruction, model output or software change is not law.

**A2 — Legislative and local competence.** Elected Assembly members and the people
through enacted proposal routes may originate general laws. The Assembly alone
enacts national statutes, taxes and appropriations. Local elected councils legislate
only within express constitutional or statutory competence. Neither an AI system,
minister, publication officer nor court may replace the required legislative vote.
Courts retain lawful constitutional and rights review; local variation cannot
remove the national rights floor.
Prior law assigns local, interlocal and national decisions by affected scope;
an overloaded docket cannot transfer an Assembly vote to the executive or AI.
Protected opposition access must allow a public response to missed legal duties
without majority committee permission; courts enforce access, not policy choice.

**Proposed design179 constitutional addition to A2 — Particular self-benefit.**
Public acts, including primary legislation, may not confer a particular financial
or material advantage on an officeholder or an actually controlled/materially
connected financial interest without an actual public purpose, objective justified
terms and unconflicted lawful implementation. No privilege is lawful because of
public office or intervention. Formally general criteria tailored to a known
connected beneficiary remain particular in substance; a nominee/agent/trust label
cannot cure proved continuing material particular benefit/control. Genuinely general benefits, lawful general office
remuneration, compensation/rights and necessary public contracts are not barred
by identity, but must satisfy their actual equal/public terms. Declaration, a
named appropriation or an express ordinary-law exception cannot waive this
constitutional constraint. Particular-benefit proposals identify the actual
interest/beneficiary, purpose, alternatives, terms and source for a recorded
competent decision. CJ retains statutory constitutional validity; ordinary courts
retain actual case protection/material reference. Clerk/auditor gains no policy,
promulgation, vote, seat, government-formation or fixed-denominator veto.

The coordinated law must provide the narrow PI public-interest legality claim
for residents or persons with a concrete legal/material connection to an identified
particular benefit, on credible facts of a material officeholder/controlled/
connected interest. No separate denied service, competing bid, proved bribery or
one-fifth legislative support is required. Ordinary case protection and a necessary
CJ constitutional reference retain actual jurisdiction, fair reply and no automatic
stay; generalized policy disagreement supplies no universal merits action.

[PI01–PI20](../../country/C03/17-public-decision-influence-and-interests.md)
provides the proposed ordinary records/conflict/source package. This new candidate
constitutional paragraph requires legitimate founding or valid prior A9/RR adoption
and applicable holds; it is not adopted law, existing-right restoration or a change
to actual constitutional/software/contribution permissions. A1/A2's prior vote/
competence and all A3/A9/A10/RR protections otherwise remain.

**A3 — Publication and AI assistance.** The authenticated public register publishes
the exact enacted text and history after the lawful decision. A technical assurance
failure may be reported and corrected; it does not create an unaccountable veto over
enactment. AI tools can draft, compare, translate and advise within revocable mandates.
People can challenge their framing and use effective non-AI routes. Research versions
and four-stage model reviews are labelled as research, not promulgated law.

operative A1/A3 source. The lawful promulgation and rights clocks retain
their scope.
