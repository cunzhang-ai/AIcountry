The source-clause inventory has not identified a complete lawful territorial-status
route, recognition of distinct collective claims, or every rule governing an
affected boundary. Design143 candidate A15/TS01–TS16 supplies a positive initiation/final-consent
procedure, not actual power to amend land titles, local charters or treaty duties. A real adopter must
inspect all then-current constitutional, property, local-government and external
commitments, including claims absent from the pinned manuscript, with their
holders and lawful procedures before using this route.

The old four-stage AI pipeline can remain an optional research-quality practice. Its
hashes establish source integrity for research artifacts, not democratic consent,
judicial validity or operational performance. Changing this manuscript's legal
architecture needs a legitimate fundamental route; changing a README cannot do it.
