# Design200 decision. Put a funded national payer behind a common local duty

*9 November 2026 · Research selection · Exact design199 predecessor.*

## Gap and selection

C04 already sets common guarantees and local methods. NF23–NF24
already models representative capacity, grant and monthly payer.
FC already supplies the early-care fallback. The positive gap is
joining these and other sector duties in one authority, attribution
and due-date account. A locality can be credited twice with the same
unrestricted resource while the national ledger reports enough money.
Aggregate compensation can also miss a locality with a different
burden. Select MF01–MF08, with the scope of a new compulsory task and
its source voted together, sources attributed once, continued local
choice and independent execution of due obligations. This gives
residents a route through competing government accounts.

· [Exact predecessor](baseline.json)

The Assembly owns the common floor, new funding and prospective
scope. Elected councils control lawful local service methods and
optional spending. Career Treasury calculation and payment roles
stay distinct. The local provider and sector authority remain
answerable for timely protection under their actual law. A court
can review law and enforce an available duty without approving a
tax, new spending ceiling or coercive takeover. No review or model
is a veto over adopting an authorized law. A failed allocation
cannot cancel an existing right or award new political power.

## Evidence, competing choices and objections

Denmark's 2026 schedule distinguishes net intermunicipal transfers,
national block grants, conditional parts and a budget adjustment for
a new duty. Its published collective sanction exposes a real design
choice. Do not withdraw protected common-floor resources because
other localities overspend. Australia illustrates assessing need and
capacity together, with limitations of average policy. Canada
illustrates representative revenue capacity. These have different
legal and institutional scopes. None is evidence of actual
Copenhagen or Lolland payroll or service receipt.

· [Original evidence](../settlement/funded-local-mandates/01-denmark-australia-and-canada.md)

General block finance and local repair remain strong where they
actually fund and deliver the same obligations. Per-sector reimbursement
can be useful for a finite additional task and can reward higher
spending or impose delay. Direct national delivery or a shared local
pool can work where operational scale matters, subject to actual
paid transfer, political control, site, journey and fallback. Prefer
a local method with a national enforceable funded floor in the
working country. More measurement can harden a wrong benchmark or
reward gaming. Use contestable needs, source-law facts, material
scope, safe disclosure and an explicit weaker-case path. A justified
simpler alternative can displace the selection. The difference
between a real gap and an optional local policy remains contestable.

## Authority, continuing duties and reopening

Preserve NF benchmark scope and TN's actual-source preference, FC
sector advances and exceptional limits, LR local rule and vote,
TS territorial status, DB/FB standing and recurring source, AP
receipt, B19/SR effective relief, and HT and OP/T real pay, control,
records and successors. No new rate, tax power, fund, local balance
guarantee or right to charge an unwilling neighbor arises. Pay the
lawful undisputed amount by the source-law date. Track a late
transfer, a contested formula and an unserved resident separately.

This is a design selection using scoped publisher evidence and
authored cases. No affected party took part in the proposed country's
choice. Actual authority, independent qualification, source,
paid capacity and lived outcomes remain open. Compare one same-period
common duty in two places through a valid payable source, paid staff,
political withholding or shared failure and actual receipt. Reopen
on mismatched costs, undisclosed redistribution, arbitrary condition,
blocked execution, lost local voice or a better simpler path.
