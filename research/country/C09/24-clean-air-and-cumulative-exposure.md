# C09. Clean air and cumulative exposure

*Design231 · AQ01–AQ10 · Proposed ambient-air duties.*

**Protect the air people actually breathe in each affected place.**
Measure ambient exposure separately from emissions at individual sources.
Assign a funded response when an area exceeds its legal limits even
if each operator meets its current permit. Prevent a solution in one
neighbourhood from creating an unlawful burden elsewhere.
