# Public attention: mediation and closure

*Canonical FA07–FA09; part of [C12/03](../03-finite-attention-and-lawful-closure.md).*

**FA07 — Replaceable mediation with a complete control chain.** Existing merit
administration commissions the team through open job-related selection under
C04, publishing competence, sources, pay, relevant controllers and conflicts.
The assignment lasts only for the specified cycle plus funded record handover.
Policy sponsors, their advisers/contractors and materially interested suppliers
cannot lead grouping or adjudicate a challenge to it. The elected authority
sets lawful scope and appropriation beforehand but cannot secretly change
rankings or edit the participants' reasons. Operational process decisions belong
to the team within that prior law, subject to correction and courts, not policy
choice. A team cannot appoint its own complaint reviewer.

A participant may ask for another facilitator/interpretation without surrendering
the issue, and can seek reassignment by a different career supervisor outside
the challenged assignment, with recorded reasons within five calendar days or
earlier useful time. This supervisor receives no policy veto and is not final:
direct ordinary-court review remains available. Removal requires an evidenced
conflict, misconduct or inability to deliver the defined task, with fair process;
policy disagreement is not cause. Replace from the previously qualified conflict-
screened roster under recorded assignment rules. If no independent alternate
exists, acknowledge the failure and seek lawful correction/deferral, not a second
vendor model described as independent review. Courts can require lawful staffing
and correction within existing authority; they do not choose the preferred policy.

Public source custodians keep protected originals, authenticated summaries and
correction history independently of a vendor's working account. Participants,
opposition and courts can inspect the material needed to challenge the process,
subject to safe evidence controls. Appropriated bounded contracts include human
verification, storage, exit and accepted record handover; privately multiplying
agents does not enlarge payment. Treasury cannot condition an already due lawful
payment on a favored policy conclusion. Replacement preserves receipts, mandates,
claims, accrued pay and challenge dates, with a named receiving custodian.

**FA08 — Closing inquiry is not enacting law.** The legally designated public
issuer signs a reasoned closure report answering the selected substantive
portfolio, stating changed/retained choices, dissent, unexamined arguments,
intake losses, source gaps and the risks of proceeding. It explains why residual
uncertainty is acceptable, or narrows/defers/withdraws. It may close without
consensus or complete coverage; it cannot announce that all material objections
were resolved. The competent elected body decides reserved policy, and the
legally delegated issuer only its actual powers. Neither team nor model can
certify legality, override a court or convert consultation into public consent.

**FA09 — Failed completion has a finite consequence.** At day 90, or day 120 after
the single lawful extension, report failure if the promised process is unfinished.
No default adoption, automatic enactment or silent endless consultation follows.
The issuing elected body must expressly retain the baseline, withdraw, narrow
and reconsult, or authorize a new funded cycle under valid law. A lawful decision
without a mandatory adequate process is challengeable; a nonmandatory consultation
cannot acquire a veto over a power that already permits decision without it.
Ordinary existing duties, rights, orders and election dates continue. Statutory
mandatory consultation cannot be bypassed merely by calling failure urgent.

[RDA01–RDA08](../../C03/24/01-deliberation-and-protected-alternatives.md) separates a
qualified competing resident text from closure of deliberation. FA keeps its own scope.
