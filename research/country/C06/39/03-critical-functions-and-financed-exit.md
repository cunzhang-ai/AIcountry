# Critical-function entry, loss and a financed exit

Design 99 · Proposed research, not enacted law or funded bank protection.

**BF09 — M09 critical entry and the default.** M09 entry requires its existing sourced 30-day
nonviability/no-credible-timely-private-cure test **and** proof that ordinary U liquidation plus
BF04 payout and separately funded public/safe payment routes cannot preserve named lawful critical
functions in their useful time with less interference. The prudential supervisor submits original
cash, collateral, asset/liability, vendor and foreign-law accounts; insurer, bank, workers, clients
and affected payment owners may give protected evidence. The resolution panel states entry or
reasoned refusal, actual alternatives, affected claims and what will happen by the next due time. If
it lacks its fixed votes or cannot establish the extra public test, no exceptional
transfer/write-down/bridge occurs by default; U receiver, M07 payer, M06 correction and SF
public-payment owner still have their own authority/duties. Refusal is reviewable; it is not a
finding that services were received or the bank healthy.

**BF10 — Least interfering funded plan.** Within M09's exact authority, choose only a named, funded,
qualified receiver/bridge/transfer or reverse-ranking M10 loss operation that can actually maintain
the critical route. Preserve M05 property and M10 collateral, recent wage tier, covered depositor
payment, distinct uninsured rank, prior taxes/ordinary claims and two-working-day stay limits. Owner
equity and subordinate claims absorb before senior eligible classes; no officer can spend covered
deposits, wage rights, client property or another bank's capital by declaring criticality. A
bridge's existing two-year maximum and six-month review survive; beyond that needs Assembly law and
explicit loss. If the operation cannot meet funded/accepted execution, use lawful winding down of
each actual original or successor estate with mapped claims, plus due insurer/public routes, and
disclose unserved people; no automatic reversion of a valid prior transfer or perpetual rescue
follows. No M08 insurer-fund contribution to bridge beyond actual insured claim satisfaction is
selected without a separate finite enacted cost/priority rule; no implicit depositor bailout of
shareholders.

**BF11 — Court, loss and aftercare.** Give immediate source/reasons and M09's meaningful independent
hearing within 72 actual hours, with earlier useful-time relief when a payment, liberty or property
transfer would otherwise be irreparable. Ordinary courts can review competence, fact, hierarchy and
claims; later M11 compensation compares each claimant's actual resolution treatment with ordinary
lawful liquidation at entry date, no hypothetical public rescue, paid from its own appropriated
account. Compensation is not an assumed reversible transfer or a substitute for M07's due payment.
One person can receive distinct insured payout and independently owed M11 loss compensation only on
separately proved grounds; credit the same value once. Accepted M12 receiver/payer records actual
deposit access, due benefits/tax/wage/election payments, safe ID correction, client assets and
foreign refusal; a completed resolution order is not received service.

**BF12 — Source/transition and bounded default.** M15 keeps zero automatic Treasury overdraft,
solvency-only ordinary liquidity and an express Act for any new public bridge/guarantee; no bail-in
tax, private-loan cancellation or foreign-law redenomination. Existing valid deposits, coverage,
maturity, security, netting, U ranking, workers, court claims and contractual currency continue
until a compatible authorized amendment, with source-law loss and paid transition. A bank
license/growth exceeding funded M08 headroom is blocked prospectively under M01/M08 with direct
review, while earlier insured rights persist. Specific payer/source failures create arrears,
court/elected repair and an actual cost account, not default cancellation or emergency rationing.
Reopen if insured coverage crowds out the social floor, M09 is captured, 24-hour advance cannot be
delivered, narrow reserved payments outperform plural banking, or original evidence changes the
dependency map. [Commencement](09-commencement-and-compatibility.md) fixes the new 180-calendar-day
floor; a source/readiness inventory cannot move it. Existing higher coverage, lawful source rights
and originally due clocks survive until a specific compatible amendment. Post-commencement
original-event duties cannot retroactively create a new punitive default for a pre-commencement act.
A licensed M07-member bank's breach of the protected-growth funding bar does not remove otherwise
eligible depositor coverage accepted before a lawful prospective membership/insurance termination:
apply the existing cap and record the increased unfunded liability. Ordinary depositors need not
audit Treasury headroom to retain that right. Any prospective termination needs competent source
law, usable individual/public notice and safe lawful alternatives; prior covered claims survive. An
unlicensed outsider or falsely advertised new uninsured investment does not create state insurance
merely by taking money; actual misleading-conduct and compensation remedies retain their own law. No
regulatory breach automatically raises L, draws missing cash or grants a new V/K punitive predicate.
