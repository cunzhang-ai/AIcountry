# Finite filing, court holds and commercial reliance

Design 98 · Proposed research, not enacted law or verified operation.

**MP15 — Filing and clocks.** Parties to a reportable concentration file before a voluntary control
transfer and observe standstill from the first binding control agreement/public bid until clearance
or lawful expiry. If control instead arises without a voluntary implementing act and could not
reasonably have been foreseen, file within five working days of reasonable awareness and refrain
from new integration or nonessential exercise of newly acquired control while review is live.
Preserve necessary ordinary service/property protection within prior law. The unforeseeable event
itself is not retrospective gun-jumping; VD11 still requires its own implemented act, actual ability
and knowing/reckless fault. First usable filing states controllers, domestic nexus/attribution,
overlapping/vertical/labor relations, affected commitments and alternative service; protected data
remain under PD. Within 5 working days the authority gives one exact missing-item list or the
objectively sufficient filing is treated complete from original receipt; a challenged list receives
prompt ordinary-court review, with no serial reset. From complete filing, Phase 1 is **30 working
days**; a reasoned, served opening alone starts Phase 2 of **90 further working days**. A qualified
career decision officer may clear or accept **voluntary** narrow conditions; a contested adverse
binding condition or merger prohibition requires the authority to apply to an independent ordinary
court **before the live deadline**. Filing alone extends no standstill. One independent court may
hold completion for at most **30 further working days total for that transaction**, across all
phases and any call-in, on evidenced serious irreversible competition harm and a merits timetable;
no actor/renaming/late information request renews it. If the live standstill expires without lawful
court prohibition/hold, parties may close **without deemed merits clearance or immunity**; the MP17
post-close merits window applies. Court/authority must state which claims, workers and services
survive a pause. Return of already owned client property and correction of due payments under
M06/M07 are not a new ownership merger and cannot be held for this review. A lawfully constituted
M09 authority may use only its own enacted, time-limited client/deposit/payment bridge, with its
useful-time court and loss/FX limits; that bridge is no automatic approval of a lasting buyer or
waiver of MP review of permanent control. The unresolved M09 appointment rule cannot be supplied by
the markets chief. A timely omission list may identify only objectively essential missing filing
facts and cannot postpone a clock once they were actually received; disputed completeness has direct
useful-time court review. After Phase 2 opens within Phase 1, its 90 days run from that actual
served opening, not from a later restated theory. B16 ordinarily gives 20 working days for adverse
reply; court/authority must plan within the deadline rather than shorten defense for their own
delay. Administrative clearance/voluntary conditions are assigned to the preassigned career decision
officer, separate from investigators. Publish a safely redacted
transaction/controller/scope/deadline notice within five working days of usable filing and give
usable notice to known specifically affected groups/representatives, without demanding a full market
dossier or public private data. At decision issuance publish the operative clearance/conditions and
an answerable redacted evidential gist, and notify identified affected participants/known
specifically affected parties. B16 appeal runs from usable publication or individual notice as
applicable, with blocked-access/unfairness protection. A timely ordinary appeal is not the forbidden
second official merits review; silence is no rights waiver and an appeal is no automatic stay. An
accepted condition cannot waive third-party rights; a material property transfer still needs MR03
court authority.

**MP16 — Below-gate call-in.** No universal small-acquisition notice or automatic standstill. Within
**60 calendar days** of the first publicly verifiable signed/closed transaction event (and never
later than **12 months after actual completion**), the authority may serve a reasoned low-gate
inquiry only on specific original evidence of MP11 likely harm and domestic nexus. The
target/acquirer may voluntarily seek review. An interim stop for an unclosed low-gate deal needs an
independent court's served order under this **express merger-only MP16 ground** of serious
irreparable competition harm, with B17 notice/reply and fixed expiry. The order consumes the **one
MP15 maximum 30-working-day court hold** for this transaction, not a second period; no authority
forecast, borrowed V04 general stop or retrospective gun-jump offence creates a hold. A low-gate
transaction completed without such an order is not unlawfully consummated merely because later
challenged. Concealment does not create a self-issued agency hold; MP17 controls any later unwind
action. The 60-day inquiry window never tolls or extends MP17: a call-in served shortly before its
true post-completion outer date leaves only that remaining time to file a merger-structural court
claim. A later information request or voluntary review cannot reserve a longer period. The court
must receive any such timely claim before the applicable outer date; a pending inquiry is not a
filed claim. A voluntary sufficiently complete low-gate filing starts the same MP15
30/90-working-day review arithmetic for that inquiry, without itself creating a statutory
standstill. For a call-in, time begins at the served reasoned notice identifying its essential
facts; later source requests cannot keep it pending indefinitely.

**MP17 — Post-close merits and reliance.** A claimant with ordinary standing seeking to unwind or
impose a merger-specific structural condition on any deal closed without formal clearance, whether
below the gate or after standstill expiry must file in ordinary court within **12 calendar months
after effective control passed**. Proved deliberate material concealment of the control transaction
instead permits filing within **12 calendar months from proved discovery, capped at 36 calendar
months after completion**; no unlimited discovery toll. A valid affirmative clearance binds only the
materially and honestly disclosed transaction; deliberate material concealment permits the same
discovery/36-month outer window, while ordinary timely appeal of clearance remains. Ongoing
ownership structure alone is not a fresh exclusion or a clock reset; a genuinely new control change
is a new transaction, while individual B15 proved-loss claims and **new or continuing conduct with
independently proved MP04/08/09 elements** retain their own periods. A pending case alone creates no
hold. Before a reasoned merits disposition, any merger-only temporary stop on completion or
integration consumes the same single MP15 30-working-day court allowance; an expired allowance
cannot be renewed as a fresh B17/V04 forecast. Ordinary provisional protection of separately proved
cartel/exclusion acts retains its own grounds. Merits orders require an answerable case, proof and
MR03 review/transition, not the passage of a timer.

*Part of [the competition statute](../11-competition-and-economic-power.md); normative thresholds and institutions, no authority from publication.*
