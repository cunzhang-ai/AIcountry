# RT16–RT20: protect entrants, pay the system and leave real alternatives

*Part of [private rent/entry](../18-private-rents-tenure-and-housing-entry.md).*

**RT16 — Stayers are not the whole housing public.** Genuine new private rents
remain RT02 agreements. Newcomers, young people, movers and people leaving an
unsafe home may face higher initial rents or less stock; current tenants gain
more protection and can become less mobile. Do not declare this equal access.
Keep H06–10 need-based cost-rental entry, no incumbent/local-residence cartel,
H20 repair/new-supply preference, LU conforming serviced development and actual
mobility/essential access. No acquisition of occupied units is counted as new
supply, and no rent cap proves more construction, maintenance or available land.

Compare actual net rental stock, conversion/withdrawal, safe quality, new-entry
prices/queues, displacement, mobility, landlord financing/returns and who bears
costs; separate general conditions from this policy's causal effects. Protected
reported testimony, statistical samples and original records suffice where
valid; no universal landlord/tenant identity graph. A contrary entry/supply
finding can defeat the selected private-rent bargain, not just prompt another
report. Tenancy control is not a dwelling-linked cap across all turnover.

**RT17 — Existing competent roles, real paid independence.** Use ordinary H/B
civil courts and lawful execution, C04 advice/receiving, I03 administration,
EV index publication and elected housing/source bodies. No new board, private-
landlord licence, universal pricing permit or mandatory national rent registry.
Pay judicial/technical/counsel/translation/record work and actual independent
conflict receiving; the public landlord cannot adjudicate its own cost-rent
case by calling it RT. No assumed rent saving, new NF site-tax yield, fine or
AI labor finances these duties. Full housing/source/work/FX and paid overlap
must join WF/DB/OP-T once, not an old37-row envelope or spare private labor.

**RT18 — Evidence and continuity without permanent surveillance.** Give tenants
and lawful successor parties accessible actual contract/payment/index/allowance
records; use ordinary B/H proof if a document is lost, not presumed rent0 or an
invented highest market price. Purpose-limited safe custody, retention/claim
periods and source correction retain PD/KR rights. Keep actual household choice,
personal resource control and non-AI help. Future nontext/agent communication
can prove actual understood terms/mandates under B/C12; a copy or an AI rent
estimate supplies neither agreement, authority nor original data.

**RT19 — Enact the transition explicitly and prospectively.** A competent Act
must choose dates and particular old/new contract/secured-claim migration with
rights review, losses, compensation where law requires and actual paid sources.
Use H19's first1January at least12months after enactment as the candidate notice
reference for new RT duties, coordinated with that law; it does not restart
already enacted H/repair/assistance/court/election duties. Identify each ongoing
tenancy's lawful starting base and first annual review/index window; no
retrospective rent cut or clawback of actually lawful old-period rent. RT07's
downward ceiling applies prospectively after that valid transition.

A preexisting future market-review clause is not retrospectively fraud; valid
prospective RT can constrain it only through the explicit lawful migration,
not a clerk's reinterpretation. Stronger actual protected terms/accrued dues,
deposits, old security priority, pending orders/claims, staff/pay/pensions,
contracts/records and accepted paid OP/T successors survive. No readiness
certificate delays an actual date, and research starts no tenant liability.

**RT20 — Select the bargain but preserve disagreement.** Keep free genuine
initial rent and CPI-bounded continuation as the research choice. Market review
plus actual assistance/new cost-rental supply is a strong rival with price
flexibility but possible displacement and public subsidy of scarcity. Dwelling-
linked/negotiated rent regulation can protect entrants but increases boundary,
valuation, investment and transition conflict. Public rent insurance can shift
risk to explicit pooled sources but requires real finance and can subsidize
landlord rents. Permanent public/community cost rental remains H's separate
selected option; no competing regime is silently combined into a free package.

The choice values practical tenure/predictability over unrestricted continuation
repricing. CPI, lag, review/exception terms and efficacy are hypotheses, not
proof of adequate rents, new supply or superiority. No actual affected tenant/
landlord/newcomer/worker choice or independent qualified legal review occurred.
Reopen on original exclusion/supply/quality/price/capture/source harm or a better
whole alternative. Return to the largest whole-country needs/source/control
question; no automatic rent-index or city-study sequence.

Keep genuine initial agreements and assess actual net supply separately.
