# BCV16–BCV20. Preserve a real budget choice after a condition fails

*Design309 · Proposed addition to [democratic budgeting](../60-democratic-budget-and-execution.md).*

**BCV16 — Separate an effective budget from the part that awaits another
decision.** Before the exact vote, identify any optional schedule that
cannot take effect until another law, separately competent elected body
or expressly enacted commencement condition is satisfied. State the exact
provision or decision, legal effect, responsible body, proof and latest
useful date. Record any parts that take effect independently, with their
separate approval where needed. Give each legislature or elected council
its own vote under its own law. The condition applies only to the dependent
scope. Existing dues and independently authorized work continue on their
own authority. A ministerial forecast, support statement or unverified
report cannot stand in for the named act. Routine release checks under
DB24 retain their own scope.

Avoid requiring each body's instrument to have already taken effect
before the other's provisions can commence. Prefer a simpler lawful
sequence with an independently useful first instrument where feasible.
Alternatively, where both source laws authorize it, the competent
bodies may each validly make and publish their own exact conditional
instrument with a common event for the affected provisions to take
effect. Name that event, the latest time, both exact scopes and
restrictions, sources, remaining lawful conditions and the prior
custodians who preserve independently usable proof. Verify compatible
terms, continued validity and any operative restriction. A lawful
material replacement requires fresh assent in the affected scope.
Expiry or late proof gives no retrospective effect. A common event
that verifies authorizations does not prove actual payment, delivery,
consent or a different required prior act. Keep these conditions and
their review on their own authority.

**BCV17 — Reserve the choice for a defined period.** The first affirmative
vote can enact an expressly conditional schedule. For that schedule the
Clerk records provisional displacement of incompatible alternatives.
Reserve only the legally identified uncommitted national resources the
schedule could use, with dates, a cap and the cost of waiting. A reservation
gives no authority to commit money or treat another body's resources as
available. Price retained staff and receiving capacity and respect valid
existing uses. Set the last day early enough for a real fallback sitting,
source verification and delivery where feasible. Publish the date and
tentative sitting before the first vote. If a useful fallback is already
impossible, disclose that before voting. A duly operative conditional
schedule displaces incompatible alternatives for its scope.

**BCV18 — Reopen the recorded choice upon failure.** An exact rejection,
other established failure under the enacted condition or expiry without
qualification ends the uncommenced schedule and its reservation under its
own terms. A separate explicit future law is needed to revive it. Restore
the next eligible, unchanged alternative to a protected final vote under
BCV11–BCV15. Reconcile it to any effective independent parts, actual funds
and accepted capacity. If this requires a materially changed text or
comparison, apply ordinary scrutiny and fresh ordering to the remaining
choice. Only an actual binding vote authorizes the fallback and only the
affected scope returns. Where no qualified alternative can reach a lawful
vote in time, record non-decision and use existing BLC and duty law.
A lost sitting never supplies a yes vote, extends the conditional period,
or shifts the original deadline.

**BCV19 — Preserve obligations that have already taken effect.** Publish
when and how the condition was proved or failed and which instruments
took effect. A failure after lawful commencement, including a delayed
transfer after a lease binds, follows the ordinary funded correction,
continuity and remedy rules. Keep prior liabilities, work and residents'
claims against their original authority. For a narrower substitute,
reconcile all used cash, binding claims and already booked capacity before
any new decision. Separately enacted tax, debt or other changes retain
their own effect. In drafting, make a companion burden's commencement
depend on the exact protection it needs where lawful. Where the protection itself must be available before the burden
starts, identify its separate earlier lawful source and actual
receiving. Receipts from the uncommenced burden cannot fund that
prerequisite. Account for actual later receipts and binding
preparation separately. A later rejection of a budget cannot repeal
an independently enacted charge.

**BCV20 — Give independent bodies usable time and an enforceable record.**
The Clerk publishes exact scoped versions, evidence, pending conditions
and challenges. Require a timely response and verified use of the existing
alternate presider, record access and useful-time correction. Allow
the other body to reject or change its proposal and publish effects
without forcing its agenda. A changed local law supplies no assent to
a different national text. A compliant delegated change of method
does not reopen matching votes. Recheck affected readiness and
hold any dependent optional release that lacks a qualified receiver.
If the change alters an exact required instrument or the funded legal
scope, verify fresh affected assent and the ordinary fallback.
Neither failure nor a local correction displaces independent effective
duties or the original opportunity to challenge. AI can flag dependencies, dated reservations
and incompatible starts. It has no vote, release authority or power to
decide a disputed event. Reopen on routine forfeiture of viable fallback,
unfunded standby, manipulated delay or a stronger jointly approved
arrangement.
