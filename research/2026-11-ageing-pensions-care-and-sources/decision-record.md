# Design313 decision on joint retirement and care funding

## Baseline and question

Pin Design312, RF05–RF09, FB13–FB16, WF85–WF89 and the proposed
research method. Compare maintaining public retirement payments and
usable care under the same population, work and general funding
path. The project assistant authors the selection. Actual elected
bodies and lawful service authorities retain their decision powers.

## Evidence and alternatives

The 2024 Danish projection illustrates a decline in the gross
public pension share alongside a larger increase in long-term care.
Its intermediate totals and its health and care risk scenario
produce materially different timing and pressure. No share is
an available public source. The authored exercise shows one fiscal
collision and one workforce constraint, without transferring any
projection to the new country.

Compare separate budgets with a matched plan, recurring debt with
a financed near-term bridge, and an openly voted lasting funding
or prospective design change. A more funded pension scheme is
a real rival only if it covers continuing old claims and actual
care alongside new assets. A national tax rise can place unfair
burdens on particular workers or households. A funded reallocation
can remove other valuable work. Retain both as alternatives for
actual distributional choice. Keep current care and pension rights
through shortage and independently check delivery.

## Selection and next action

Select RF17–RF21. Keep one consolidated account with dated legal
sources, qualified staffing and separately funded pension working
cash. In the example choose one incremental recurring unit, a paid
bridge and separate acceptance of both shifts. This is a budget
comparison, not enactment of a tax or change to existing insurance.
Prior rights, cases, earned units and effective source law transfer
only through the competent prospective procedure and funded successors.

Price a real local care roster and a national contribution payment
window on the same dates. Reopen on corrected projections, new
unmet need, cost shifted to households, a material source failure
or a superior whole alternative. Actual affected participation,
independent review, authorization, paid readiness and observed
outcomes remain open.
