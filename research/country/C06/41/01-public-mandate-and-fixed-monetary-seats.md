# Money: an elected mandate and a finite monetary decision body

*MC01–MC04; part of [C06/41](../41-money-and-financial-public-control.md).
Design104 research, not enacted law.*

**MC01 — Keep the public choice above the instrument.** Retain the conditional
domestic currency/managed float and M14's revisable2% annual consumer-price
objective and1–3% reporting band. [MS01–MS04](../61/01-joint-mandate-and-voluntary-work.md)
now expressly revises the objective hierarchy: medium-term price stability and
the highest sustainable voluntary employment are joint responsibilities, neither
automatically superior. A stated temporary price deviation must confront both
persistent work loss and inflation/foreign risks, with a credible return path.
This is a revisable value balance, not equal numerical weights, an optimal
formula, unemployment quota or guaranteed outcome. The Assembly enacts the general mandate, instruments and
outer public risks; it can change them prospectively through valid law, rights
and proper consultation. Neither a monetary committee nor Cabinet can veto an
enacted budget, cancel due services or create a secret preferential loan.
External currency/narrow payments remain a genuine complete-package rival.

**MC02 — Five fixed human seats.** Five paid members have nonrenewable five-
year seat terms, with one expiry annually. Before inaugural nominations the
Clerk publicly draws labels onto initial1/2/3/4/5-year slots from the Act's
fixed commencement date. Later expiry remains tied to that seat, not arrival.
Delay consumes the term. Each seat has one consenting separately approved
reserve with exactly its expiry; established recusal/inability activates only
that reserve, and permanent vacancy activates it for the remainder. Neither
person gains another term by moving labels; reserve service belongs to the
same single seat term. No dual primary/reserve seat or double vote. Verify
actual qualification/conflicts/paid availability every six months and activation;
replenishment uses the original public process, never a case-specific appointee.
Unfilled/unavailable/conflicted primary and reserve means an empty seat.

**MC03 — Parliament actually appoints.** The Assembly Clerk runs accessible
C04 recruitment and reasoned qualification/exclusion. Any consenting person
may apply; any member can propose a qualified consenting candidate without
Cabinet or agency shortlist veto. Require five relevant years or demonstrated
equivalent competence in monetary/economic, payments, financial risk, labor/
price evidence, public law or accountable administration, with source/numerical,
mandate and rights judgment. No PhD, wealth, guild, political agreement, one
economic school or AI ranking is a condition. Publish interests/objections;
ordinary courts review unlawful exclusion, not appoint the preferred economist.

Open scheduled recruitment180 calendar days before expiry with at least30
application days; unexpected vacancy opens within14days. Publish nominees and
material exclusions at least14days before the decisive vote, and a successful
appointment/reasons at least21days before entry. Each primary/reserve needs a
separate recorded **ceil(3N/5)** yes vote of full statutory Assembly N. Vacancies
and absences do not lower it. Multiple nominees passing separate ballots need
a further single-choice ballot at the same threshold, no plurality fallback.
No approval by silence, Cabinet necessity or absent opposition. Actual founding
appointments require the competent C14 authorized selector, not project accounts.

**MC04 — For-cause removal with real outside review.** No member/reserve may
also be minister, legislator, judge, senior party/campaign executive, financial
lobbyist, regulated-firm director/controller, supervisory/resolution/insurer case
officer or hold another financial policy seat. Require two years since those
political/director/lobbyist roles; ordinary association/advocacy is protected.
Disclose actual sponsor/holding/family conflicts proportionately; divestment,
recusal and privacy have direct review. Cooling rules can restrict expertise.

An unconflicted ordinary court may remove on an ordinarily entitled person's,
one-fifth-of-full-N members' or competent integrity investigator's petition for
proved serious misconduct, persistent material statutory breach or inability
after accommodation. Require answerable grounds, defense and ordinary appeal;
bad forecasts, policy dissent, bank embarrassment or government change alone
are no cause. Allegation alone cannot suspend/activate a reserve. Necessary
interim suspension/recusal needs reasoned independent authority and B17 fair
hearing; permanent removal follows ordinary appeal time or first ordinary
appeal, subject to lawful stays. Fixed expiry is never extended. No CJ12 own-
court jurisdiction is imported. Actual direct-conflict exhaustion remains failure.
