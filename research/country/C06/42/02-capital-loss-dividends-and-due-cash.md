# Recurring taxes: capital cost, dividends and an honest collection calendar

*NF05–NF08; part of [C06/42](../42-recurring-tax-and-noncash-work.md).*

**NF05 — Finite cost and loss rules.** Deduct equipment/software in five equal
annual portions from first actual use (20% of eligible basis per year), buildings
in25 years (4% per year), no land depreciation. The25% company-profit rate is
a separate rule; these recovery periods are not25% equipment/5% building rates.
Inventory uses documented cost once on sale. Disposal includes consideration
less remaining basis, without repeating a capital deduction. For ordinary noncore financing, let I be genuine business borrowing expense,
R included taxable NONCORE interest income and E positive operating earnings before
those financing flows/tax/depreciation/amortization. Deduct gross I only up to
R+.30E, never more than actual I; R stays included once, not deducted again.
Unused genuine interest carries five
subsequent tax years, oldest first, inside each year's cap. No principal/sham
loan/owner-consumption deduction. A proved net operating loss carries five
subsequent years against that same body's positive profit, not wages, affiliate
income, a refund or a reset via a shell. A lawful merger successor needs proved
continuing-business/source continuity; it cannot purchase unrelated loss relief.
For actually licensed bank/credit intermediation, evidenced ordinary third-
party customer/funding interest used in that activity is an operating cost
before this EBITDA measure; the cap applies to other net corporate borrowing
expense, not the institution's gross depositor cost. A label/affiliate invoice
is no exemption; actual license/activity, purpose and independent terms must
be proved. Core receipts/funding enter that net operating margin once and cannot also
enter noncore R/I or enlarge the separate cap. Allocate mixed activity by
traceable actual flows/costs under NF06, no duplicate use of core income.
Connected financing retains NF06 scrutiny. Thus interest receipts100
less genuine core funding90 leaves10 before other costs, not taxable70 created
by capping90 at30. This distinction avoids taxing gross financial turnover.
These restrictive five/25/30%/five-year choices can penalize long investment and
real loss cycles; no optimality or Basel/OECD compliance is claimed.

**NF06 — Related prices need answerable evidence.** Revenue identifies parties,
actual control, transaction and reliable independent comparable; genuine function-
comparable cost/margin evidence may help when product comparables fail. Missing
reliable data is disclosed, not a final AI number or automatic group-turnover
uplift. TX06's reasoned best available estimate/rebuttal, necessary original
records and ordinary full merits apply; no fictional zero or certain market
margin. Correct both domestic parties consistently; foreign corresponding
adjustment needs actual cooperation. No public family/political graph, unlimited
home/device access or retroactive group liability is conferred.

**NF07 — Deliberate classical dividend taxation.** Domestic company-to-company
dividends are excluded at the resident company receiver with traceable actual
payment/title; no second company layer. Natural shareholders include only actual
distributions in PA10, with TA09 provisional withholding credited once. Retained
profit is not received shareholder cash. No personal company-tax imputation,
gross-up or corporate credit is selected. Profit100 taxed25 leaves75: a wholly
20% personal marginal case adds15, total40; at55% it adds41.25, total66.25.
These high combined burdens, retention/debt/avoidance incentives and worker/
consumer incidence are real objections. Actual-paid imputation/flow-through are
substantive rivals with different credit tracing/default/refund costs.

Resident companies credit foreign income tax actually paid on the same included
net income, capped at its domestic25% tax, no cash refund or credit for another
entity/period/merely asserted liability. Foreign dividends remain included absent
actual treaty relief. Preserve old lawful taxed pools/bases/credits by period;
this design does not retroactively cancel an adopted foreign/domestic credit.

**NF08 — Annual liability has an exact due event.** File/pay the undisputed
calendar-year company balance by30April next year. No new quarterly company
advance is presumed; Treasury must finance this seasonal lag lawfully. TA09
personal dividend withholding uses its separate15th-next-month duty/credit.
Explicitly apply RG/TX case, reply/review/refund/neutral interest and original
four-year assessment/six-year collection (only proved bounded concealment/
evasion extensions) to this base and due event. No proposal/inquiry/judgment
resets it. Late actual foreign tax can support same-income correction under
TX/B15, not an imagined source or unlimited reopen. A loss is no cash refund.
