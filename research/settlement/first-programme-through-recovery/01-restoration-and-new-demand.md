# Restoration, missed work and new demand

This constructed comparison exercises
[T31–T36](../../country/C14/06/10-first-programme-through-recovery.md).
After a shared interruption, a region reopens ordinary care and water
work. At the first checkpoint it knows four missed care tasks and two
missed water tasks. An independent check identifies two additional
earlier care tasks. The opening recovery scope is six care and two water
tasks, with their original dates. The numbers represent tasks with
verified compatible skills within each service in this case.

| Seven-day window | Newly due care and water | Ordinary paid capacity | Additional qualified capacity under the recovery option | Unresolved at window end under the recovery option |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First | 8 and 4 | 8 and 4 | 3 care and 1 water | 3 care and 1 water |
| Second | 9 and 5 | 8 and 4 | 4 care and 2 water | 0 care and 0 water |

Two independently funded review appointments are due in each window
and remain booked under all options. No service task replaces them.
The two extra tasks found by the first check fit within those
appointments. Sector owners verify the actual care and water work
under their own urgency rules. In the first window, ordinary
capacity can meet all new work while leaving all eight older tasks
open. If the owners instead complete the six old care and two old
water tasks first with ordinary capacity alone, six new care and two
new water tasks remain unserved. A queue number alone conceals this
difference. With ordinary capacity alone in both windows the account
ends with seven care and three water tasks unresolved.

The full recovery option pays four incremental units in the first
window and six in the second, with an accepted qualified shift per
unit for the service specified in the table. These are paid shifts
available in addition to the continuing base. Review and ordinary
sources are separate. A discretionary start needs two additional cash
units and one of the same qualified second-window water shifts.
Even if the extra cash arrives, taking that shift would leave one
water task unmet. Hold the discretionary start under the declared
budget and protected dates. If an independently paid water shift
fails, the same one-task gap remains and needs a lawful remedy.

Completing all identified tasks in the two windows does not settle
any separately proved harm from the interruption. If the third
window receives nine care and five water tasks again, the recurring
base is short by one shift of each skill after the temporary source
expires. Before closure, show a separately authorized recurring repair
or a named continuing gap. The [arithmetic check](verify-recovery-windows.py)
reproduces flows, constrained capacity and the next call. Actual costs,
qualification, arrival rates and results remain open.
