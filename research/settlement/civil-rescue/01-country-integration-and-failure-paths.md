# Civil rescue in the integrated country

Design165 · [Canonical RC](../../country/C10/13-civil-rescue-and-incident-command.md)

An ordinary fire report can arrive by speech, relay or another usable method
without a subscriber/account. Actual public intake seeks qualified rescue;
HG/NCS receives clinical need, a safe housing/care route receives displacement,
and PO acts only on its own actual grounds. RC lead may use only applicable
HP protection/entry/extraction; a shared room does not merge those permissions.
First receipt, actual safe time, care will and useful-time court access survive.
A called team that never arrives leaves an unmet guarantee, not a successful case.

The fourteen entrance headers/navigation were checked for topic ownership;
selected canonical cross-sector dependencies were read. This is not a new full
fourteen-chapter/all-clause, original-casualty or national-affordability audit.
All twelve goal requirements in the [completion criteria](../../status/completion/01-objective-and-proof.md)
remain relevant: lives, democracy, major functions, useful AI, first principles,
future contributions, future communication, ordinary law, viable economy,
ecological/future limits, safe transition and English usable organization.
RC improves a proposed ownership/dispatch chain; none of those whole-goal
requirements is established by its existence or by passing artifact checks.

[Sixteen written adverse arguments](../../2026-11-civil-rescue-and-incident-command/adverse-register.json)
cover failed arrival, local poverty, repeated crews, compromised Director,
coordinator expansion, omitted Cabinet vote, clock resets, a protected stock
collision, forced clinical treatment, private volunteer force, evidence capture,
ambiguous AI mediation, offline exclusion, unsafe donor release, a stranded
child and failed old-employer transfer. They inspect stated control/source/
delivery/claim paths, not actual responses or a modelled probability of safety.

Retain/repair, national unified rescue, community/common and contracted plural
backbones differ in ownership, solidarity, authority, trust and readiness. Local
professional public responsibility plus aid is selected provisionally, not a
response-time optimum. Centralized rare-capability pooling and stronger community
control could displace it with a better complete paid case. No new board is an
automatic answer to a failed existing chain. Next country action remains a
whole-design largest-gap review, not a queue of additional rescue studies.
