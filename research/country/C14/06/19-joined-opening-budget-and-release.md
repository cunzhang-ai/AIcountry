# T85–T90. Test all opening payers on one calendar

13 November 2026 · Integrated design 440 · Candidate first-budget rules

**T85 — Assemble the common opening budget.** For every material
function, carry the legal payer, available and restricted assets,
continuing duties, dates and responsible receiving office into one
versioned baseline. Cover the first 90 days and the second-year
operating and debt tail. Retain later binding exposures. Keep the
[whole-country scope account](../../../settlement/integrated-first-programme/03-country-scope-and-remaining-choices.md)
as the coverage register. Each missing material line keeps the
affected whole-budget conclusion open. Separate historical
comparators from the proposed country's own authority and money.

**T86 — Reconcile only counterpart entries within the stated perimeter.**
Use one traceable obligation identifier and distinct due, settlement
and receipt dates. Retain both entity entries for each transfer.
Eliminate matched reciprocal flows once when both entities are in
the consolidated perimeter. Keep external payments, unmatched
entries, restricted balances and contingent exposure visible.
Netting for presentation gives no entity power to spend another
entity's cash. Keep all qualified staff and physical receiving on
the same dates through the common calculation.

**T87 — Test concurrent calls on common funding and common controls.**
Use one ordinary case and one credible combined interruption with
dates. Include refinancing, local transfers, insurance backing,
enterprise compensation and independent democratic and judicial
duties. Record distinct failure paths for payment rails, source
custody, required approvals and qualified staff. Show which duties
lack timely money or execution before assuming a fallback. The
same account, facility, alternate operator or staff time cannot
be reserved for several simultaneous obligations. Mark legally
incompatible uses and separate any actual guarantee call.

**T88 — Give elected bodies funded choices on the same baseline.**
Compare retain and repair, a phased start and a faster start on
the same scope and stress. Identify each source, amount, last
useful date, owner and recurring cost. An option can proceed only
within competent law and its qualified funding and receiving.
Preserve existing duties if an optional step fails. If the
common baseline itself cannot meet all due obligations, report
each exposed duty, apply existing lawful priorities and submit a
funded correction through the competent elected and review routes.
No common spreadsheet assigns new priority between lawful claims.

**T89 — Price the repayment and next receiving before drawing.**
A reserve or standby facility must have actual scope, price,
settlement, expiry and a lawful recipient. Carry its repayment
and remaining exposures into the next payment window and year two.
Do not count an advance as a later receipt again. A late payment
can restore funds but does not cure an earlier missed deadline.
Keep a separate, accessible remedy and verify actual receipt.

**T90 — Keep the comparison open to challenge.** Treasury and
each accountable owner attest to their own records. Independent
analysts, auditors and remedies retain separate access and
resources. Show the public and opposition the matched choices,
protected baselines and material gaps on useful dates. AI may
reconcile and compare the cases. It cannot allocate authority,
choose a losing claimant or confirm service. Preserve prior
claims and lawful custody through handover. Reopen on a changed
baseline, uncovered due date, correlated failure or stronger
funded alternative.
