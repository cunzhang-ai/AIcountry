# Responsibility and evidence: questions a verdict must answer

*Authored boundary cases for the [criminal-core draft](README.md); not observations.*

| Situation | Required analysis and separate continuing protection |
|---|---|
| C1 A withdrawn allegation after a dependent person receives shelter | Withdrawal/unproved testimony alone proves neither fabrication nor innocence. K12 needs intentional obstruction or unlawful retaliation; no automatic reporter charge. Police preserve both evidence directions with safe contact; shelter/clinical support remains need-based and the accused retains protection-order challenge. |
| C2 An AI-assisted care decision contributes to a death amid a staffing shortage | K02 requires a specific duty, actual ability, proved causal contribution and express reckless/gross-negligent fault. Identify deployment and supervision decisions and original logs; neither death nor job title proves the operator/developer/director's guilt. Civil service repair and independent clinical/official review need not wait for conviction. |
| C3 A command destroys footage; three later models agree on guilt | Preserve originals and outside-command evidence. P12 asks whether each source actually derives from tainted material; agreement is not independent support. Exclude unusably secret/coercion-derived material and independently investigate K12 without making that inquiry proof of the original accused's guilt. |
| C4 A person takes essential food during immediate otherwise unavoidable danger | K07 still needs dishonest taking/permanent deprivation; P04 requires a real necessity assessment and state proof against a properly raised defense. Poverty alone neither establishes nor automatically excuses theft. Fund safe food access independently; punish only if the complete case is proved and the available response justified. |
| C5 A peaceful critic is accused of causing public instability | No core offense of disloyalty, instability or insulting officials exists. Require an actual published offense, particularized act and fault; refusal to praise a cabinet is not K04/K10. Direct court liberty review, political access and complaint evidence must survive incumbent control. |
| C6 A company uses staff and AI to conceal a material risk | Identify authorized acts, specific legal disclosure/safety duties, actual knowledge/ability and causal/reliance elements. Do not combine unrelated employees' information into fictional corporate intent. Natural persons have personal defenses; a company criminal charge needs an enacted corporate offense/sanction schedule; [Q07–Q09](../../country/C08/15/06-organizations-and-continuity.md) now supplies a proposed extension needing fresh review. Civil/regulatory repair and protection retain their distinct grounds. |

These analyses preserve potentially incompatible interpretations until reliable
sources and fair process resolve them. A case being difficult is not authority
to broaden an offense or replace proof with a score. Noncharge/closure needs
safe reasons and uninvolved review; court legality review cannot compel guilt.
Civil causation, safe-time support and temporary protection have their own
burdens. Actual respondents, witnesses and affected workers did not participate.
