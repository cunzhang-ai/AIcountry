# ED21–ED24. Find lost learning and keep a public owner

*Design217 · Part of [ED public learning](../21-public-learning-and-school-power.md).*

**ED21 — Act on a material interruption of usable learning.** A learner,
supporter, family or teacher may report loss directly to the school or local
education administration, including without school records or a parent acting
for the learner. A provider who knows of a serious interruption, a repeated
loss of expected learning or a materially shortened offer without a verified
equivalent must notify the local administration promptly and explain the
available help and challenge route. Ordinary short absences on a viable plan
use ordinary support. Distinguish illness and needed rest, actual inaccessible
provision, an imposed reduction or unsafe placement, and a freely chosen
adequate alternative. An absence percentage, referral, school roll or missing
contact is a signal to understand the situation. It supplies no finding of
fault, neglect or consent. Let the learner speak privately and with accessible
help where wanted. Where silence may reflect danger, use safe contact and
applicable safeguarding law.

**ED22 — One named local owner maintains the offer and dates.** The ED02
education authority names a responsible officer using existing intake and
review routes. Preserve the first known missed protected opportunity and
the first report or notification as distinct dates. A retained school remains
responsible for instruction and adjustments within its role. The local owner
checks access with the learner and promptly arranges suitable interim teaching
when due, with the necessary teacher, aid, language, travel, meals and safe
setting. Use relevant evidence from learners, teachers and competent health
workers. Immediate educational adjustments need no single clinic's certificate.
Clinical treatment and individual health judgments retain their own rules.
Transfers, diagnosis, placement disputes and a new term cannot restart the
record of loss. Use XR04 for cross-local responsibility and accepted transfer.
An unknown destination remains an unresolved case. Use only lawful minimum
inquiries and continue to offer a practical route to return.

**ED23 — A shorter timetable needs a reason and a real next step.** For an
institution-initiated material reduction of the usable offer, record what
the learner can use, what is lost, the teaching and supports that remain
due, and the review date.
Give reasons and a copy through a safe accessible route. Use the existing
outside inspector for a review no later than ten teaching days after the
reduction first takes effect, sooner when useful access is at risk. Timely
notice and a protected inspector calendar are part of making that deadline
real. Preserve ED12–ED15 and any earlier right or safety clock. The same loss
cannot be restarted by changing the timetable label or the provider. Until
review, the authority continues to owe due suitable learning. The inspector
sets a bounded date to reassess any continued reduction, with fresh evidence,
the learner's supported voice and an accessible interim offer. An independent
decision may support a suitable health-adjusted or chosen equivalent path. A shortened
timetable used to keep a child away for safety must satisfy ED13–ED14.
A missed review triggers direct correction and preserves all claims. It
supplies no permission for continued exclusion or forced unsafe attendance.

**ED24 — Count receipt, recovery and remaining loss separately.** Verify the
actual teacher, support, travel where needed and accessible place, and follow
whether the learner could use it. Track the elapsed gap, offered suitable
instruction, actual access and any materially lost teaching/support. Keep
lawful rest and adequate chosen alternatives distinct from failed provision.
Keep the learner's place and route back while interim help operates, unless
a lawful, accepted transfer changes it. Catch-up after failure uses actual
qualified time and the learner's needs. A payment, enrollment, assigned
worksheet or accepted referral alone cannot close an unmet learning duty.
Close active follow-up on verified restoration, a suitably supported chosen
equivalent or a lawful transfer accepted in practice, as applicable. Preserve
the right to return and record any unresolved remedy separately. A learner
can decline voluntary support through a safe accessible route. Record the
choice separately from service receipt and apply any continuing duty under
its own law. Direct ED15 and court routes remain available through actual relief.

[ED34–ED38](08-learning-support-before-assessment.md) sets a
separate interim disposition and assessment decision before final
award, preserving first receipt and qualified transfer support.
