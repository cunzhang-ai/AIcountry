# Primary records for the local fiscal shock comparison

- [Canada, Federal-Provincial Fiscal Arrangements Act, section 6](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/F-8/section-6.html)
  defines the fiscal stabilization payment. The [regulations, sections 32–38](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-303/FullText.html)
  describe applications, estimated advances and reconciliation. These are
  provincial arrangements with different authority, eligibility and
  limits from the proposed local service reserve.
- [Finance Canada, 2015–16 Supplementary Information Tables](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/corporate/transparency/plans-performance/departmental-results-report/2016/supplementary-information-tables.html)
  reports the two rounded amounts and months under Fiscal Stabilization.
  These are reported intergovernmental cash advances.
- [Alberta Budget 2022 fiscal plan](https://open.alberta.ca/dataset/6d0f1358-beb5-4bb7-8da1-a350a138039c/resource/36771cab-bee0-44b5-99ad-a03d88da653c/download/budget-2022-fiscal-plan-2022-25.pdf)
  and [Alberta Budget 2023 fiscal plan](https://open.alberta.ca/dataset/fe2a75b5-571b-4b44-8b04-66a6d81aaba4/resource/7f532f18-4416-4b40-b387-e7d03b43d6dd/download/budget-2023-fiscal-plan-2023-26.pdf)
  report the successive forecasts for the same revenue decline in
  their federal-transfer discussion.
- [Finance Canada's listed payments starting in 2023–24](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/programs/federal-transfers/fiscal-stabilization-payments-made-provinces.html)
  reports the Alberta 2020–21 claim row, payment date and amount.
  The [Alberta annual reports index](https://www.alberta.ca/government-and-ministry-annual-reports)
  specifies fiscal years ending March 31. The cited payment list does
  not provide a complete earlier transfer or local service history.

The [observation file](observations.json) transcribes these scoped values.
The worked timing and amount differences use that file. The allocation
case uses independent illustrative figures. None of these sources
establishes a proven municipal service threshold, actual fiscal room,
the cause of the forecast difference, or a causal service effect.
