# Design211. Make home support adaptable without weakening the person's right

## Baseline and decision

Design210 is the pinned baseline. S09 provides an individual need-based
entitlement. AS gives voluntary control and a non-employer route. E and NP
assign useful-time relief and paid receiving, while HN retains clinical
responsibility. S09 plans and reviews did not resolve who can vary a live plan,
when a set of small reductions becomes a determination, or what a provider
can draw on while a permanent change is decided. Select HS01–HS08 to do so.
Use the existing research framework and decision template. Adopt no procedure
or law by editing this record.

## Evidence and what it can distinguish

Danish enacted law and Copenhagen's programme already support flexible
integrated elder care. A municipal funding correction identifies partial
source replacement with no evidence of more hours thereby. Separate earlier
recipient evidence supports the importance of known workers, but cannot
establish the effect of teams or the later citywide reform. The proposed
country needs a comparison against that strong retain-and-repair arrangement,
including rapid ordinary reassessment and local public responsibility.

## Selection, rivals and objections

Allow person-led and professional adjustment within a protected,
individual floor. An independent public decision is required to cut that
floor, including through repeated omissions. Keep specified plans and AS
routes usable. The principal diagnosis is delay and displacement between
the actual change in a person's life and the legal and paid decision. A
different diagnosis is scarcity or worker churn. Expanding discretion in
a fully booked team leaves genuine needs unmet. The combined support,
clinical and source example separates those mechanisms.

A more detailed time-and-task system protects against underdelivery and
can be preferable where the person needs a predictable routine or distrusts
discretion. Flexible planning risks a contract becoming an invisible cap,
with the hardest cases passed between providers. The public assessor,
paid outlier route, aggregate reporting and independent correction address
that risk, but each consumes work. Direct public provision can lower
contract friction and still weaken practical exit. Person-directed budgets
increase control for some and can transfer responsibility without real
suppliers. Preserve each as a real choice or comparator. No review team,
publication metric or cheaper route gets to override individual rights.

## Democratic and financial control

The elected Assembly sets the entitlement and funding obligations. Elected
local authorities choose lawful provision and pay for actual teams. A
qualified care officer owns public determination, separately from the
provider's commercial or managerial interest. Recipients can change
assignments, reach outside AP and B review and receive the resulting relief.
Workers keep ordinary pay, limits, bargaining and protected reporting.
No clinical, private-decision or emergency power follows. Adoption and
migration need actual S16/T dates, existing plan and record successors,
a retained alternate, paid overlap and a recurring source. Earmarked
transition funds remain finite. No assumed wider use of the Danish data,
no participation by the affected public and no independent qualification,
legitimate enactment, paid operating capacity or observed result in this
proposed country.

## Bounded next comparison

Compare one defined care network using matched existing demand and
qualification, with a specified-plan route capable of rapid amendment.
The local authority would produce a recurring and transition ledger and
simultaneous shift test with clinical receiving. Independent assessors,
workers and people with varied support and communication needs would
check actual time, control, missed visits and external correction. A
costed study requires authorized paid time and safe recruitment. Until
those inputs exist, retain the same-capacity unresolved result. Reopen
on rejected high-need cases, covert reductions, unavailable alternatives,
unpaid household replacement, a better specified route or an unfunded
end to transition.
