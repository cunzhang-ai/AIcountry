# Design412 decision. Make urgent civil legal assistance a usable public duty

## Baseline and affected scope

This AI-assisted selection uses [Design411](predecessor-design-411/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md),
the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).
B13 funds advice and representation by need. OJ33 includes paid legal
help in the court capacity account. Existing law separates counsel and
prosecution and supplies direct urgent routes. The remaining design choice
is who accepts the person and pays for conflict-free advice or scoped
representation in a civil case before loss, with independent review if
that route fails. Claimants, respondents, service users, providers, legal
workers and taxpayers are affected.

## Alternatives and selection

Rely on private counsel, general information and simplified intake.
This is easier to administer but can leave a person unable to frame
an urgent claim against a more powerful party. Provide universal full
representation in every civil dispute. This provides wider professional
help but can divert scarce counsel from serious and imminent losses.
Select free first advice for covered urgent civil interests and scoped
representation where necessary to preserve practical access. Fund an
independent reserve and retain simpler or earlier lawful resolution
where sufficient. Use the same deadlines and ordinary rights across
comparisons. The proposed leadership term and acting limit are explicit
institutional choices.

The main objections are limited professional supply, repeat dependence
on one provider, privileged evidence leaks, and discretion over merits
or means being used as a delay gate. Retain early access, minimal evidence,
separate merits decision and independent conflict cover. Continuing aid
needs its own enacted criteria and source. No independent qualified
review or affected-party participation was obtained for this selection.

## Public control, continuity and remedy

The Assembly enacts and funds the service and appoints its career
director through open scrutiny. A qualified officer authorizes aid
on defined grounds. Conflict-free counsel answers to the client
and professional law. The independent forum controls merits, case
procedure and relief. A named executor implements any order. Court
review and direct intake remain usable without the respondent or
aid provider's permission. Test appointment, removal, evidence and
payment control before relying on the service, including complaints
against its own office. Track capacity and outcomes without publishing
privileged material.

Preserve criminal defense, specialty aid, earlier claims and review
deadlines, provider duties, protected records and client choice.
An aid referral cannot stay a court act or decide a claim. Migrate live
cases through accepted paid successors and verify actual receipt.
AI may support lawful research or drafting but cannot make any
eligibility, litigation or merits decision.

## Evidence and continuation

[Primary comparators](sources.md) show different roles for early advice,
duty counsel and effective access. The [constructed case](../settlement/urgent-civil-legal-assistance/01-two-urgent-claims-and-one-lawyer.md)
checks concurrent urgent needs, conflict, advance cash and execution.
[UCL01–UCL08](../country/C08/25/13-urgent-civil-legal-assistance.md)
records the research selection. Lawful enactment, professional availability,
costs, safe client access and observed benefits remain unverified.

A competent court service and independent civil aid provider could next
reconstruct one lawfully inspectable urgent docket with two co-due cases.
Compare referral, consultation, disqualification, available qualified hours,
last useful starts, completed hearings and actual receipt. Budget and
safe access are unresolved. Reopen on recurring conflict, unreachable aid,
late remedies or a simpler route providing equivalent access and protection.
