# Country alternatives and the remaining source question

Design151 · [WF13–WF16](../../country/C06/58/04-country-choice-transition-and-reopening.md)

Retain the working service-first country with TW36/32, paid public competence,
plural production, independent political/legal remedies and protected old
claims. Do not select a new financing regime from a foreign ratio. A published
welfare state already spends on many relevant purposes; assuming all duties
are new duplicates baseline costs. Assuming matching labels already buy the
candidate's stronger rights hides increments. Both mistakes can reverse a
whole-country comparison.

| Genuine alternative | Mechanism and potential gain | Unresolved loss/source/control |
|---|---|---|
| Strong retain/repair | Keep useful institutions/services and reduce added compulsory work | May preserve inadequate rights/access and longer working time; current real comparator not a rights-free fiction |
| TW32 with paid capacity and simpler tasks | Preserve more personal time while retaining services, pay and review | Net new qualified workers/materials/capital and source; no free overtime or family substitution |
| More/centralized AI | Potential scale and cheaper useful tasks | Source, supervision/error/fallback, common control and actual net gain; no automatic administrative/clinical saving |
| Smaller/distributed tools and plural providers | Relevant independence and practical choice | More interfaces, skill/energy/site duplication, actual accepted fallback and public buyer competence |
| Direct public or mutual/common provision | Different bargaining, ownership and investment priorities | Actual purchase/renewal/old claims, competent management, capture and individual exit |
| U+C2 plus protected services/earned rights | Greater income independence with conditional work incidence | Different net tax/credit/retirement/source/distribution; no presumed destination or empirically proved winner |
| Negotiated regional provision | Potential shared specialists/supply/reserves | Foreign consent, restricted ownership/currency, due-time capacity, exit and claims |

Longer hours are a real value/resource rival, not an administrative permission
to undo TW. Public/private/common labels do not establish productivity,
democracy or quality. People bearing taxes, unpaid care, lost work, longer
journeys, transition asset costs or foregone time need separate incidence.
No benefit average exchanges away effective opposition, liberty or due relief.

Coverage includes current personal/service/earned guarantees, actual home and
maintenance claims, democratic/justice/control work, digital support, PM source
and buffer, HP work loss, PT notice liability, CB repair and legacy contracts.
The register's37/90 scope is preserved exactly; no new programme is costed
from an old bid, headcount or COFOG total. No implied universal private-loss
guarantee, forced repossession, data sale, source seizure or new tax relief.

The frontier answers a bounded question: how much other net expense fits an
explicit reference annual criterion under stated compensation/time/revenue
assumptions? It cannot answer the next decisive question: what are the matched
actual or defensibly bounded income/tax/earned-retirement and service/skill
increments, and who bears their net cost, work and source risk? Complete that
finite comparison next, including retained/replaced costs and real rivals.
Actual adoption is separate from design completion; absent ratification is
not itself a missing positive rule. No qualified full-cost, actual source,
independent affected judgment, safe operation or better-lives finding exists.
