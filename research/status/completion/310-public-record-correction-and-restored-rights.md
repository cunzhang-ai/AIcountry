# Design402. Make public record correction restore rights in practice

[RUC01–RUC08](../../country/C04/06/07-public-record-correction-and-restored-rights.md)
links a corrected assertion to affected decisions, accountable receivers and
delivered remedies. Each competent authority applies the governing law for the
relevant period. Preserve privacy, original dates and independent challenge.

[Worked case](../../settlement/public-record-correction-and-restored-rights/01-one-fact-two-public-decisions.md)
checks a missed intake, an April correction, unrelated periods, a stale extract
and a returned payment. Operational coverage and observed outcomes remain open.
