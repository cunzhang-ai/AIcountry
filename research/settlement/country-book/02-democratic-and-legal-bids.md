*Part of [country operating book](README.md); fictional million units, not actual requirements.*

Each amount is a proposed ordinary operating allocation from unique common
components. It is not a complete cost estimate, an appropriation or evidence
of sufficient staff, lawful power, independence or received remedy. Actual
requirements remain unknown for every row.

The [resource components](01-resource-components.md) allocate each shared
payroll/purchase item once; the [full results](../../2026-10-country-operating-book/results.json)
retain exact shares and amounts. Existing sector law and independent
correction remain separate. A classifier cannot reassign a person’s claim.

Standing operating allowances do not price founding, major reconstruction,
guarantees, all pending liabilities, future renewal or every service need.
Unfamiliar duties can reopen the map; 37 rows are no closed country census.
