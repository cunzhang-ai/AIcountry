# One tax fact and two services

This constructed case tests [ASI01–ASI08](../../country/C11/09/16-public-scoring-and-purpose-limits.md).

Assume housing may lawfully verify an independently established earnings
fact for the relevant period. The ride uses its own access evidence.
Neither office has a lawful reason to use the tax label or the late filing

| Remove the label but use one shared reviewer with time for just one case | At most one of the two duties can be completed before the deadline | Report the other duty as uncovered |

remaining legal facts and the actual payer and ride are available. The single
reviewer branch checks both feasible assignments without selecting legal
priority. In practice a priority needs its own sector law. If the housing
office cannot obtain the permitted earnings fact, it uses another lawful

An approved payment that is returned or a ride that fails to arrive is still
an unfulfilled duty.

The case tests scope, capacity and actual receipt. It supplies no measured
prevalence, tax-policy effect or clinical or housing outcome.
