# Two assistants, one stale index and an early voting deadline

Use a constructed group of 100 voters with the same early deadline.
A newly qualified candidate appears on the official field. Two
commercial assistants still use one stale index. A different brand
and a switch that turns profiling off leave that source unchanged.

In the exercise, 60 voters can retrieve the complete information
through the public paths before their deadline. A feasible public
repair adds 10 distinct voters. A scoped direct discovery route
also reaches 35 voters, of whom 10 are already covered by the
repaired public routes. The combined result is 95 of 100.
The remaining five need direct assisted help or another lawful
route. The arithmetic assumes the distinct paid staff and source
access for both improvements. An outage at the primary official
source needs the authenticated alternate. If that source is itself
wrong, the electoral authority and existing review must resolve it.

| Same group and deadline | Timely verified retrieval |
| --- | ---: |
| Existing public paths with shared commercial index failure | 60 |
| Public repair alone | 70 |
| Public repair and scoped direct route | 95 |

Select the scoped duty in this case only after the independent
authority verifies necessity, alternatives, lawful scope and
funding. If public repair can reach the same voters in useful
time with comparable access and less burden, use it alone.
Wider ranking control would change editorial and political power
without necessarily improving access. A correction after the
early deadline leaves the original loss on record, even if
later voters benefit. No conclusion about vote choice, actual
election effects or participation follows from this example.
