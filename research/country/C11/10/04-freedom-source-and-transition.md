# GA19–GA24: preserve inquiry, democratic control and paid continuity

*Part of [general AI capability](../10-general-ai-capability-and-public-control.md).*

**GA19 — Freedom and practical access.** Protect lawful research, scientific
criticism, security reporting, association and plural human/AI collaboration.
No ideology test, nationality exclusion, compulsory official model, model voting
weight or inference that all open-source contributors control a release.
Use accessible free scope advice, proportionate submissions and publicly funded
independent evaluation/counsel where need would otherwise exclude meaningful
research or challenge. Support is subject to enacted appropriation, not a false
promise of free expert labor. A community project has the same substantive severe-
risk duty and a usable route to compliance, not a paper exemption or corporate-
sized fixed burden. PD/KR/WL/MP and protected individual choice remain.

**GA20 — Democratic government chooses the boundary.** Assembly ordinary law,
subject to constitutional rights, sets harm grounds, proxy, powers, source and
material changes. The Office publishes nonbinding technical methods and case
reasons after accessible scientific/worker/community/affected-public scrutiny;
it cannot add a new danger category, lower a legal trigger or expand prohibited
acts through a manual, emergency or automatic foreign standard. NI initiatives,
opposition scrutiny, AA audit and ordinary courts remain usable. Publish actual
case delays, exclusions, conditions, reversals, incidents, distributions and
common dependence, safely redacted; expose missing competence and funding.
Annual Assembly scrutiny can retain, narrow, replace or repeal prospective law.
Public-value disputes are not settled by evaluator consensus or model confidence.

**GA21 — Pay the whole chain.** Elected national appropriation must cover distinct
Office/career staff, secure containment/evaluation, independent assessors, counsel,
judges/executors, accessible reports/help, incident receiving, source protection
and paid legacy handover. Distinguish operator research/control costs from public
review and third-party remedy. Bounded enacted cost-recovery fees, if later
chosen, enter public accounts and cannot let providers select assessors or fund
pursuit through fines. No fee schedule/rate or funded headcount is selected here.
Reconcile scarce technical, power, hardware/import, justice and source duties
with DP/SC/clinical/defense and whole-country claims once, not duplicate budget
labels. No assumed AI productivity, future fine, confiscation or foreign promise
finances this office. Full source/capacity remains unqualified.

**GA22 — A lawful, prospective start.** An actual competent Act must establish
powers, first appointments, paid assessment/court access, safe custody and
published receipt routes before GA starts. A legislature must fix and publish
the future date and lawful paid migration, not delegate an indefinite private
research ban to a readiness declaration. Missing prerequisites prevent reliance
on this new permit mechanism before lawful commencement; they do not suspend
existing rights/law or create retroactive GA punishment. After lawful start,
capacity failure preserves scoped duties and GA08/GA12 relief/continuity; it
neither switches GA off nor resets a readiness date or restricts unrelated inquiry.
Existing actors receive assisted inventory and
case-specific lawful notice/transition; no automatic certification or global
weight recall. Actual existing serious hazards remain reachable under their
present valid powers. New covered exposures after lawful commencement follow
GA regardless of when a model was trained; no fiction that old training grants
an unlimited right to new release. No actual start is claimed in this project.

**GA23 — Obligations outlive institutional change.** At renewal/repeal/failure,
identify lawful accepted OP/T successors for staff/pay/pensions, contracts,
secure evidence, pending scope/permit/court cases, downstream warnings, due claims
and funded remedies. Permission cannot erase an earlier claim; new material
capability/control facts reopen only affected support and exposure. Technical
rollback does not reverse copied weights, personal injury, privacy loss or a
public mandate. DP/public/sector operation, C12 consent and current actual
constitutional/software/contribution permissions/holds remain separate.

**GA24 — Keep alternatives real and the hypothesis defeasible.** Sector/ex-post
law avoids a concentrated upstream gate but can act too late for irreversible
harm; insurance/shared voluntary evaluation can improve practice yet exclude
uncompensable outsiders. Broad frontier licensing or a moratorium may prevent
more exposure but concentrates power, deters research and needs stronger public
justification. Narrow GA is the selected research bargain, not proved optimal.
Compute is an imperfect proxy; dangerous capability tests, legal thresholds,
assessor independence and prevention efficacy are unresolved. No party has
participated here as an affected public. Reopen on original contrary evidence,
false classifications, capture/exclusion, inadequate protection or a superior
whole alternative; if monitoring fails, expose uncertainty and use existing
law rather than inventing a new mandate. The next bounded country comparison
must carry actual research/entry, nonuser protection, paid technical/legal work,
whole-source opportunity costs and democratic replacement together.
