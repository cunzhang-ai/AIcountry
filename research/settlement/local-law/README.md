# A local government cannot make new law by silence or a software release

2 November2026 · Design112 country reference, not adopted local procedure

**Select actual full-council approval for every new externally binding local
general rule.** Keep lawful fact updates inside pre-enacted criteria, professional
individual decisions and voluntary cooperation running under their own law.
[LR01–LR24](../../country/C03/14-local-law-and-public-control.md) now specifies
this boundary and its public input, authority, vote, expiry and remedy chain.
National DR remains its own rule; no national agency acquires a policy veto over
a lawful council choice. [Decision and exact probes](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-local-law-and-public-control/README.md).

| Local act | Source and control |
|---|---|
| New ordinance changing a general local condition | Actual statutory local competence, affected input and floor(n/2)+1 actual yes/present, full n including vacancies; council adoption is making |
| New delegated general criterion/form/tariff/software condition | Named source/issuer, same council approval, then actual lawful issuer making within7days; approval is not a signature |
| New observed input under an unchanged enacted formula | Exact prior meaning/units/source/fallback, qualified actual validation and answerable record; no new coefficient, weight or eligibility condition |
| Lawful individual/professional act, genuine advice or voluntary cooperation | Its actual sector/ordinary law, not an automatic new-rule vote or politician's clinical override |
| Grave urgent new general norm | Only a specific already enacted power; earlier source expiry or72elapsed hours from first effect, no version/partner reset |
| Existing unlawful burden or owed service | Direct independent court/sector relief and actual source execution; no wait for a policy petition |

This replaces a tempting local routine/material shortcut with a stronger default.
It reduces classification power to bypass a vote but costs member time and may
preserve harmful lawful old rules. Broad discretion, national-style call-in,
direct statutory floors/fewer rules and lawful direct participation remain whole
rivals; neither more process nor fewer rules automatically means better lives.

## Ordinary sequence and a vote that actually occurred

Publish a scoped draft and source, affected people including nonusers/neighbors,
no-change/real alternatives, evidence/uncertainty, cumulative effects, cost,
receiver/payer and transition. Input lasts21complete calendar days, at least30
for rights, essential service, serious harm, irreversible or compulsory-burden
changes; longer law wins. Reasoned disposition follows within14days. Unknown
material effects cannot establish the shorter class. Existing hardship/rights
claims go immediately to their own receiver, not into that consultation queue.
Late actual reasons preserve delay/remedy liability but can precede a later
prospective L if the source, input and affected scope remain valid. Withholding
a timely report cannot confer a permanent official veto. No backdated compliance,
automatic restart of valid input or extension of stronger NF/source deadlines;
new material effects, evidence or objections still require affected renewed input.

L is the actual complete final public packet AND Council Clerk receipt, not an
issuer's signature or chair's acknowledgment. Seven complete final days pass;
vote only L+8…L+30. In a9-seat council five actual yes and five present are needed:
three yes among five present does not enact a general rule. Approval by silence,
a committee, model, absent vote or reduced filled-seat denominator is unavailable.
An operative change or stale material support needs renewed packet/input as applicable.

Council adoption makes its own ordinance without mayoral assent. A separate
statutory delegated issuer must actually make/sign within7days of approval and
before the council's legal end; no political refusal power or fake signature.
Ordinary effect is no earlier than the future published start and day8 after
complete operative public notice C. No valid vote by L+30/unmade by deadline
means no new rule; a court cannot cast the missing policy vote. Actual binding
terms and versions must be freely knowable across paid paper/assisted/future media,
not hidden in a private model, licensed dynamic link or changing vendor manual.

NF19's local site-rate deadline remains. For the illustrative1January2028 year:
proposal3September2027,30-day input to3October, disposition/final L17October,
first LR vote25October and final vote PLUS publication by2November. Waiting until
the generic L+30=16November would miss NF's stricter deadline. Late publication
keeps the last lawful rate, initially1%; it cannot be backdated or become zero.
One genuinely complete process can meet both input duties; no old partial report
substitutes for the actual current scope, original sources or fresh final decision.

## Agenda power, urgency and a real correction

Any actual councillor can introduce a scoped proposal without chair/committee
permission. A petition of min(20,ceil(E_term/100)) distinct affected people also
gets listing within7days and protected consideration/reasoned response by30days.
Nonvoters/neighbors may be affected; petition/assistance is not a new sovereign
vote. Ordinary single-person complaints and individual legal claims need no
petition or technical dossier. A response item cannot bypass consultation to
adopt an unready rule; a ready packet has reserved actual vote time at L+30.
Chair, deputy and published nonexecutive rotation do not create members/quorum
when none can lawfully act. Clerk records/convenes, never votes. Backlog remains
visible rather than classified into consent or used to extend an incumbent.

Urgent general effect has no new default authority: show specific source, grave
observable danger, minimum effects, actual source/receiver and why existing lawful
acts cannot suffice. Publish immediately, notify within24h/earlier useful time,
and end at the earlier source limit/72hours from original effect. A related new
model or council vote cannot renew it or erase ordinary input. If no proper
ordinary or expressly authorized higher-law route exists, it ends; a dangerous
gap may remain. Existing lawful rescue/service duties continue within their own
source, not a renamed emergency. Neither a new crime/tax nor missing money appears.

Local Clerk/registrar has C04 merit appointment/tenure and separate readable pay,
protected original copies and direct outside courts. Implementing/source-correction
officers must verify actual restored access, not receipt of a message. Municipal
judgment payer has actual local source and30working-day/earlier-right duty;
there is no automatic national indemnity or fine-funded office. Express B18/B19
external preassigned civil credentials execute only determined record/correction/
notice/verified-payment acts. No substitute legislates, taxes or creates cash.
If this fails too, the original claim/arrear and actual failure remain visible.

## Work and continuity are part of the institutional choice

The authored120-rule/9-member case needs420hours per member/year for prep/meetings,
plus1,080staff hours in distinct roles. A500-rule/25-member case needs1,750hours
per member, above its authored800-hour rule allotment before budgets/elections/
casework. These are sensitivity inputs, not paid rosters or legal work quotas.
Repeated measured facts inside a valid formula need not become new rules; changed
policy weights do. Sharing staff/hearings saves only proven actual cost, with
opposition/affected reasons and independent duties retained. Existing duties do
not expire when an invented capacity target is exceeded.

[The official Charter text](https://rm.coe.int/european-charter-of-local-self-government-/1680b0fbec)
informs questions of local competence, lawful supervision and adequate resources
(Articles4,8,9), not these particular clocks or a treaty-compliance claim.
[OECD's regulatory checklist](https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/public/doc/128/body-text.en.html)
asks about legal basis, alternatives, burden and public views; it does not establish
that rulemaking is the best answer or this country has original outcome evidence.

Competent coordinated law must adopt LR prospectively at180days, with source,
staff, records/courts and pending-case succession; RR and actual constitution/
software/permissions remain. Actual old rules/claims/grants/contracts/wages,
privacy/foreign obligations and useful-time protection retain their source.
Expiry does not revive a repealed predecessor or cancel due rights. A harmful
valid legacy can persist after a failed repeal; meaningful public control and
received correction are unproven. Next design the nonbank financial perimeter;
