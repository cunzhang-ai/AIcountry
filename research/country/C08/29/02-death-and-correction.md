# VR05–VR08. Keep a registry mistake from withdrawing rights

*Part of [civil registration](../29-civil-registration-and-identity.md).*

**VR05 — Death, cause and a person's identity need distinct proof.**
Accept the competent medical verification and a supported identity link,
or an operative court declaration under B36. Record which ground applies.
Record an established unidentified death in its own restricted event record
without assigning another person's identity. A missing-person report,
unverified hospital message or probabilistic match does not retire a living
person's credential or authorize distribution of their estate. Preserve
a suspected unlawful death's evidence and independent referral. The
qualified professional and investigator retain their own findings on
death, cause and suspected conduct. Issue limited proof of established
death without exposing confidential medical cause or waiting for an
unrelated estate dispute. Respect lawful evidence holds and body care.
Only notify affected recipients on the necessary lawful grounds under
VR10. No funeral provider can control the only usable proof.

**VR06 — Keep the person reachable while evidence is checked.** Start
from AP's protected receipt. The office provides ordinary proof assistance,
independent evidence access and a reasoned decision within applicable
clocks. No complete-file rule, fee, agent, digital account or particular
biometric is a condition of receiving a report. Use relevant compatible
records, direct attestations and testimony. Seek a safe reply where
an adverse decision affects another person. Where documents or a record
link are disputed, preserve urgent health, safety, education and
subsistence under C02/C07. A real collision may require manual verification
of particular authority or payment to avoid wrong-person disclosure or
duplicate execution. It does not justify a blanket freeze of either
person's distinct rights. Existing nationality, custody, estate and voting
qualifications have their own merits and urgent review.

**VR07 — A collision is a disputed link, not an identity transfer.**
Keep plausible distinct identities separate until reliable evidence shows
a duplicate or mistaken link. A second authorized, unconflicted human
officer reviews a proposed merger or an administrative death-to-person
link that is materially disputed. Contested legal status goes to the
competent court. Preserve originals and reasons and give affected people
a safe opportunity to challenge. A similarity score, shared address,
common name, kinship or common controller is insufficient by itself.
Do not merge commitments, debts, votes, care histories or agency grants
because an internal record link is corrected. Respect actual court
orders, including the B36 route to correct a declared death.

**VR08 — End adverse reliance and follow the repair to receipt.** On
credible evidence of material error, mark the exact affected link or
proof disputed and alert known authorized users to restrict reliance
within useful time. Respect operative orders and seek urgent judicial
change where their terms require it. Provide urgent provisional proof
and an actual protected route to service while the matter is resolved. Correct and
reissue at source. Under PD10, require identified material recipients
to acknowledge and decide necessary correction, including derivative
flags and wrongly blocked cases. Each recipient repairs within its
own law and records actual restored access, unpaid amounts and any
remaining impediment. The ordinary court can order interim proof or
lawful restoration and name the executor and deadline. Use existing
compensation, due-payment and B19/E15 mechanisms within their actual
scopes. An unreceived order or third-party refusal stays open. The new
duty creates no blanket loss insurance or automatic claim eligibility.
