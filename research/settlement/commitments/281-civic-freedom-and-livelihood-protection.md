# Design368 commitment. Protect livelihoods and political opportunity together

[CI01–CI06](../../country/C03/04/01-political-freedom-and-livelihood-protection.md)
joins direct court access, safe assistance, source records, an
independently usable livelihood route and a separately received civic
opportunity. Name the actual court, ordinary payer, executor and
receiving provider. Count dates from the first protected receipt.
Record existing wages, tenancy, valid sector orders and actual civic
losses separately. A remedy, a replacement venue and an electoral
contest each have their own authority and conditions.

The [matched comparison](../civic-freedom-and-livelihood-protection/01-a-worker-a-home-and-a-civic-deadline.md)
uses an illustrative finite reserve. Real lawful appropriation, capacity
and receiving remain unknown. Preserve original duties and pending
claims when a contract, provider or government changes.
