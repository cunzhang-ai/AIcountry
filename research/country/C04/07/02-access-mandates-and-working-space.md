# AH05–AH08. Useful help, a bounded mandate and a private working space

*Part of [person-directed assistance](../07-person-directed-public-assistance.md).*

**AH05 — Useful-time access, not an acknowledgment loop.** Give substantive
initial help and a named qualified next step within five working days of first
protected help request, earlier where a deadline/safe need requires. For complex
work, agree an actual source/competent receiver/task/date plan; an appointment
or generic referral alone is no completed advice. Proportionate relevant proof
can follow. Do not wait for AP's ordinary30/60-day decision ceiling to protect
an urgent claim; existing sector/B/E urgent times remain earlier.

Identify known imminent deadlines and the person's choice of actual protective
filing/qualified receiving without false safety or eligibility findings. Declining
an assistant/AI does not waive a claim or defer its source clock. Missing helper,
records or specialist remains an unmet duty and immediate receiving/correction
problem; no forced family worker, unsafe advice or automatic extension of a
court/ballot/clinical period follows. Existing direct channels stay reachable.

**AH06 — Advice and authorized filing are different acts.** A private working
conversation is not automatically an application, withdrawal or agency evidence.
Explain which channel/act can have legal effect. Under an expressly coordinated
AH/AP Act, contracted/public AH receivers are authorized receiving channels for
covered ordinary AP administrative requests when the person actually asks or
validly mandates filing. Their actual receipt then preserves AP first date,
usable reference/source and accepted handoff, even if the competent agency
receives later. State the identified requested act and unresolved proof, not
fictional eligibility or a form-completeness date. No receipt token is sole proof.

Special court/election/NI/clinical and other source-law filings keep their actual
required receiver/form/consent; AH cannot invent jurisdiction or backdate them.
The helper arranges a real timely competent transmission/receipt, or discloses
failure and seeks actual lawful relief. Public service advertising or an AI's
assertion alone creates no tax, benefit, appropriation, vote or apparent public
power. Do not disguise a public authorised receiver as a private tool to evade
receipt, or reclassify personal deliberation as filing without understanding.

Expressly protect good-faith dates for the PUBLIC AUTHORITY'S own attributable
holding-out of a covered AH/AP receiver. If that designation expired/was
withdrawn without usable notice, a person's reasonable good-faith actual timely
filing or evidenced attempt through the officially represented channel preserves
that AP receipt date against the responsible public authority. Prove the public
representation, request/attempt, reliance and notice; a provider's own false
advertisement alone is insufficient. The authority arranges actual accepted
receiving/source correction. This limited statutory date rule supplies no
eligibility, benefit, tax, force, court/electoral jurisdiction or continuing
authority for an expired provider, and compels no unpaid private work.

**AH07 — Mandates are minimal, revocable and person-specific.** Authority may
cover particular documents, explanations, communications or routine lawful acts
with actual scope, time/resource bounds and permitted subdelegation. Copies
share the mandate/ceiling; no inferred assent from using a tool. A helper is not
a guardian, clinician, court advocate, employer or public decision-maker by
being helpful. Protected waivers, medical/political consent and reserved acts
need their actual competent source/current assent. Routine delegated lawful
filing needs no ceremonial extra click if the actual mandate already covers it.

Revocation, disconnected expiry, notices, third-party reliance and valid already
made obligations follow B02–03/C12. Replacement cannot silently abandon urgent
work or erase an earlier date. Chosen shared plans do not merge participants'
private interests or let one family member consent for another. Paid interpreting
preserves unfamiliar meaning/uncertainty and the person's own stated priorities.

**AH08 — Confidential work is not a national life dossier.** Separate the client's
working notes/drafts and chosen AI support from the minimal actual filing,
provider/payment record and agency decision source. Transfer only the selected
authorised content on an actual lawful PD basis; do not grant agencies blanket
access because they fund advice. Give actual access/correction/exit and lawful
bounded retention/live holds, protected third-party information and safe contact.
No compulsory cross-agency record merge, commercial profiling, training use,
political ranking or sale of private assistance data funds this service.

Necessary lawful independent court/safety/disclosure duties retain their exact
grounds and safeguards; do not promise absolute secrecy or that every AI chat
has lawyer-client privilege. Reserved legal representation needs its actual
qualified professional and privilege law. An outside complaint needs safe source
access and a record of material advice/authority/corrections without making
private testimony permanently public or requiring disclosure of all life history.
