# TW01–TW05: work must not consume the rest of a person's life

*Part of [C05/10](../10-work-rest-and-paid-capability.md); design90 candidate.*

**TW01 — Normal hours and covered people.** Select a staged normal paid week of
36 hours, then 32 hours, for adult employees, public employees and substantively dependent platform/
contract workers, with proportionate part-time rights. Genuine independent
enterprise remains distinct; a label, account split or AI intermediary cannot
hide effective employer control. Under 18 work, pregnancy/accommodation and
safety-critical professions retain stricter protections. No paid-work obligation
is imposed as a condition of ordinary income, care, residence or political rights.

**TW02 — Count actual human work and constraint.** Include required preparation,
records, handover, travel between assignments, training and employer-controlled
waiting. Standby that materially prevents private use of the period counts as
work; unrestricted availability needs its own agreed limits/payment and actual
call time counts. Ordinary commuting is separate, but a relocation cannot hide
new travel or resident costs in a claimed saving. Assess actual constraints,
not whether the worker uses a phone, a workplace or an agent.

AI runtime alone is not human working time; required human monitoring, review
and response are. Delegating to software cannot waive rest, consent to overtime
or relabel required work as voluntary community contribution. Different media,
async messages and copied agents do not reset one person's clock. Record necessary
human work and actual instruction/assent; receipt or a model-generated reply is
not proof of the worker's consent.

**TW03 — Overtime and breaks.** Pay at least 1.5 times the ordinary cash hourly
rate for work above the agreed ordinary schedule or the enacted normal-stage limit (36 then 32), whichever limit
protects the worker earlier, without duplicating the same premium. Premium liability
is per effective employer; aggregate its common-control contracts. Independent
second jobs do not automatically charge a premium to a different employer. Overtime
requires genuine worker agreement; refusal cannot remove basic rights, a home
or a job by retaliation. Cap total work at 40 hours averaged over four weeks,
48 in any rolling seven days and 10 in any 24 hours. Apply these safety maxima and rest per person across all covered jobs, not
just common-control firms. Employers coordinate minimum safe availability
without claiming authority over private life. Common-control employers
cannot divide the same job to avoid either wages or limits. No individual opt-out or
collective waiver can remove the floor; genuinely shorter agreed hours remain.

Statutory paid breaks count inside normal, overtime and maximum paid-duty
hours; subtract them only from task availability. Provide at least 30 uninterrupted paid minutes before work exceeds six continuous
hours. A constrained on-call meal is not a break. Continuous services need actual
qualified relief at the break time, not just annual spare hours. Work-pattern
comparison subtracts paid breaks from available task time once; it does not add
the same break again as an extra service demand.

**TW04 — Distinct daily and weekly rest.** Require 11 uninterrupted hours
between duty periods. Work separated by less than 11 hours belongs to one duty
period, whose first-start-to-last-finish span may not exceed 10 hours; paid
breaks remain inside it. Daily rest uses elapsed time, not a midnight reset.

Require a 35-hour weekly rest block preceded by the distinct 11-hour daily rest:
46 continuous hours. Use a published common reference week (default Monday 00:00
national civil time), not a movable employer/account week. A counted 46-hour
block starts in each reference week and may cross its end; the same hours cannot
satisfy two weekly blocks. Longer leave contains distinct qualifying blocks.
No more than six days with any covered work may occur between weekly blocks.
The 48-hour maximum remains rolling 168 hours, separate from the anchored weekly
rest rule. Actual inter-shift intervals and statutory rest must be checked;
an annual sum or changing timezone does not reset a person.

More protective professional rules prevail. Night work, fatigue and individual
accommodation need qualified sector limits within these outer protections;
night premiums do not buy an unsafe schedule.

Reject a standing essential-service exemption. A roster or budget shortage grants
no new coercion or power to cancel rest. Necessary immediate rescue uses only
applicable prior-law powers/necessity, with exact hours and harm recorded,
independent scrutiny, pay and additional compensatory rest before safe redeployment.
It is a failure to repair, not a permanent staffing method. An employer cannot
discipline a justified unsafe-work refusal or make one worker abandon a patient
to conceal its failure to provide a safe handover.

**TW05 — Practical disconnection and protected evidence.** No off-duty response,
location tracking or productivity score is required merely because an employer
can reach a worker. Use advance published schedules; a material employer change
with less than seven days' notice needs genuine agreement or separately lawful
urgent grounds, expenses and accessible correction. Preserve reasonable refusals,
care responsibilities, disability and religious/cultural arrangements.

Cumulative covered jobs need safe total-hour coordination, not a public private-
life dossier. Keep worker-accessible records and minimal availability certificates;
disclose another employer's identity or health/political activity only with a
specific lawful necessity. Labor inspection and independent courts can obtain
protected evidence proportionately. A mediator, vendor or AI is not final judge.
