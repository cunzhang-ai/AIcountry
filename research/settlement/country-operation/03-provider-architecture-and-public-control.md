Eliminating a private margin may change fiscal distribution; retaining public
surplus may fund maintenance. Neither is itself a physical resource gain or
proof of comparative cost. Keep public fiscal cash, national real inputs and
private household/enterprise incidence distinct. Count actual common support
once, independent judgment separately. More formal checks can consume scarce
professional/learner time; CS16's simpler existing-role route remains relevant.

## Evidence and a finite result

[Original audit reports and later publisher context](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-provider-architecture-and-public-control/sources.md)
provide bounded mechanism evidence. Selected methods/corrections and later
changes were inspected; underlying private/government records and interview
transcripts were not independently obtained. Different reports sharing cases/
records are not independent replications. There is no matched A/B/C population,
service-quality, whole-cost, independence or distributional comparison and no
actual affected-party choice. Historical forecasts are not current source prices.

Reasoned selection: retain B as the provisional country, keep A/C and other
whole social rivals; reject an inferred universal form winner, calibrated
cost ranking or wholesale transfer from these cases. Shared support remains
scoped, no new agency/monopoly/mandatory gate/rate/date or previous-review
transfer. Public responsibility is an assigned duty, not a demonstration that
its cash/people/physical capacity exists. All actual full costs/sources,
qualified independent receiving, authority/readiness and better lives stay unknown.
