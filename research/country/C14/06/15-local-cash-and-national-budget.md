# T61–T66. Match the national transfer to the local payment

13 November 2026 · Integrated design 436 · Candidate first-budget rules

**T61 — Keep both sides of each public transfer.** For a material
transfer in the opening programme, identify the legal obligation,
sending payer, recipient entity, purpose, currency, amount, effective
date, and latest useful settlement time. Match the same underlying
transfer across the two accounts, with their different posting dates
and restrictions. Remove the internal flow once for the consolidated
view while retaining both obligations and the cash needed at each
end. Municipal redistribution is separate from additional national
funding. Confirm the end recipient or accepted local service under
[MF09–MF13](../../C04/08/03-reimbursement-and-resident-receipt.md).

**T62 — Keep local liquidity measures distinct.** Report aggregate
and average holdings, booked commitments, unallocated budget room,
and actually usable cash by owner, purpose and due date. Preserve
collateral, capital and third-party restrictions, legally available
credit and unpaid liabilities. Show first-90-day obligations alongside
the second-year balance and any material later adjustment, even if it
falls beyond year two. An average balance or a later net award cannot
pay a bill before settlement. Treat unobserved legal availability,
cash dates and commitments as unknown.

**T63 — Choose a cash mechanism for each material duty.** Compare
ordinary reimbursement with a demonstrably funded local bridge,
a scheduled national advance and a lawful direct or other alternate
payer. Use the same underlying duty and cutoffs. For a common
national obligation whose receiving locality lacks safe cover,
prefer a bounded, appropriated advance up to the lesser of verified
uncovered due costs and lawful available authority. Show who pays
if a claim is reduced or delayed. Give local elected bodies
control of lawful delivery and discretionary finance. A schedule
does not turn an earmarked grant into general funds.

**T64 — Trace revisions and concurrent claims.** When a grant or
cost changes, reconcile the national authority, local exposure and
original purpose. Verify which receipts were available at which
dates and identify the exact unpaid duty. Reserve each sum once.
If more than one locality needs the same authorized source, apply
existing lawful priorities and disclose uncovered amounts before
the deadline. No blanket clawback, delay of valid resident payment,
or grant condition directed at local political choices follows.
Use the ordinary lawful appropriation and review routes for any
remaining correction.

**T65 — Qualify starts and close advances through actual receiving.**
Keep already due services, pay, hearings and remedies on their
original clocks. Hold an unsupported optional release or submit
an exact narrower alternative to the competent body. Keep the
receiver, review and fallback staffed through the next due date.
Credit an advance to the same eventual liability once where law
allows. Replenish a reserve only from actual lawful settlement.
A bank credit does not prove that a worker or resident received
the service or payment.

**T66 — Preserve an auditable and replaceable chain.** Use existing
Treasury, local audit, Assembly scrutiny and ordinary independent
review. Provide elected bodies and affected people usable access
to safe reconciliations and preserve protected source evidence.
AI may match entries and identify contested dates. Competent
officers verify authority, actual receipt and remaining exposure.
Carry open cases, original deadlines and legal restrictions
through a successor. Reopen on repeated timing gaps, unsupported
local reserves or a cheaper qualifying payer arrangement.
