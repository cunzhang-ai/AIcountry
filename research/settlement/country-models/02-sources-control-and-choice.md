# Evidence, institutional power and the scope of the choice

*Part of [country model choices](README.md); consulted 15 October 2026.*

The World Bank's 2020 [UBI guide overview](https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/socialprotection/publication/exploring-universal-basic-income-a-guide-to-navigating-concepts-evidence-and-practices)
distinguishes universality from targeted assistance and discusses exclusion,
incidence, financing and implementation. It cautions that pilots cannot
settle systemwide financing, inflation, pension and political questions.
This supports asking those questions; it does not validate our rates,
fictional cohorts, behavior, fund price or a future AIcountry result.
Its historical country descriptions are not used as current 2026 facts.

All quantitative inputs trace to pinned **authored** income, service,
productive-capability and country-book files, not original household,
production or market observations. Shared upstream sources across these
files remain one dependency family, not independent corroboration.
The same AI author/controller prepared alternatives and checks; actual
affected participation, independent qualified review and authority are absent.

| Power chain | What must remain contestable |
|---|---|
| Needs-based A | Elected basket/taxes and funded administration; safe claimant evidence; a payer cannot also control all independent correction/execution |
| Universal B | Elected coverage/taxes and replacement of matching components; reliable personal payment; independent tax and additional-needs challenge; no compulsory family pooling |
| Mutual C | Voluntary acquisition; member replacement and information; worker/user interests; manager/custodian/creditor control; usable alternatives and independent court execution |

Appointing different nominal offices does not establish independence if
funding, information, infrastructure or enforcement share one controller.
Universal payment cannot license unrestricted identity surveillance; shared
ownership cannot convert financial membership into sovereign votes.
Neither resident units nor AI account counts establish political authority.

**Retain/hold:** keep I05 conditional and hold B/C. B's privacy and reliable
base may warrant its taxes and redistribution; that is an unresolved value
and empirical choice, not disproved by some losers. C may broaden wealth
and bargaining power; first-year cash and book values do not establish
acquisition feasibility, plural control or future investment. A's assessed
floor may itself need replacement if exclusion or coercion persists.

The fresh calculator preserves the pinned book's component and counterpart
rules but replaces its single affine-root assumption with an exact
piecewise solver; changed benefit thresholds require that change. The
baseline reproduces the old result exactly. This is a new comparison,
not an edit or renewed approval of the frozen design-68 artifacts.
