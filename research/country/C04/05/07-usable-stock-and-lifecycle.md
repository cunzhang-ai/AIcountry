# SC25–SC28: usable stock, maintained capability and actual receipt

*Part of [SC](../05-public-buying-and-continuous-supply.md); design 137 candidate.*

**SC25 — A reserve is qualified usable capacity.** For material critical inputs,
sector experts specify risk/lead-time/use/alternatives, appropriate buffer,
location, rotation, replenishment and its paid source. No universal number of
days fits every medicine, reagent, fuel, spare or purchased professional service.
Separate physical presence, legal title, lawful released quality, condition,
expiry, reserved competing use and reachable transport. Unreceived orders,
expired/recalled/quarantined goods and unqualified staff are not usable standby.
Retain the unmet need and useful-time response rather than declaring it served.

For medical products, C07/TD09 retains actual quality/authorization/recall
competence; use product-appropriate storage, tracing, expiry rotation and
independent release. Unsafe stock remains identified and controlled pending
lawful investigation/return/disposal; removal from usable stock is not physical
destruction or cancellation of a supplier claim. Product-specific temperatures,
shelf life and substitutions need qualified evidence, not this generic rule.
Purchasing never supplies dispensing authority, treatment consent or a new
clinical scarcity rule. Coordinate lawful stock cooperation without assuming
that other countries must bear the resulting shortage.

**SC26 — Pay for the whole device or service path.** Compare acquisition with
installation, consumables/reagents, trained operators, validation/calibration,
maintenance/spares, power/water, information security, lawful software/support,
renewal and safe disposal. An owned scanner with no accepted reagent/service
path is not a functioning diagnostic service. Separate current purchase,
stock, asset/title, annual consumption/renewal, finance and decommissioning.

Use lawful specifications for interoperable alternatives, repair access,
documentation and bounded continuity rights where justified and actually
obtainable. Do not require unsafe substitution, copy private know-how, grant
clinical approval by contract, assume free vendor support or surrender public
decision/record access to a single platform. Public provision faces the same
competence, maintenance and financing requirements as an external supplier.

**SC27 — Finance availability before booking savings.** Price inventory/standby,
storage, qualified assurance, replacement, waste and paid backup separately
from expected routine use; avoid duplicate purchase/expense and buffer claims.
Name lawful gross advances, VAT/refund timing, owner, currency and due date.
Restricted patient/client money, taxes withheld, private FX, a future refund or
unaccepted facility/claim cannot fund today's order. Reserve design does not
grant new borrowing, monetary issue, seizure or private-asset sweep powers.
Pay accepted undisputed dues under SC14; insolvency does not cancel worker,
supplier or patient rights. Actual full costs and sources remain unknown here.

**SC28 — Keep receiving, assurance and correction distinct.** Sector operators
record actual batch/service receipt and dependencies; independently assigned
competent verification checks its own defined scope. TD authorities decide
quality/conformity, clinicians individual care, buyers contracts and Treasury
payments; AA examines spending and courts provide existing relief/execution.
No actor's certificate proves all others' facts. Supply signals can use future
media and bounded agents, but observation, interpretation, reservation, ordering,
release, delegation and consent remain separate acts with protected originals.

On a common-node failure, recall, supplier exit, cyber outage or broken border
route, maintain interim care/service, reassess accepted substitutes and activate
only actually authorized/funded continuity. Do not reset useful-time clocks,
ignore a court hold, ration by an AI price score or presume that a listed
alternative has staff/stock. Keep unfulfilled claims and displaced duties visible.

[MQ critical medicines](../../C07/26-critical-medicines-and-safe-continuity.md)
specifies the receiving deadline, alternate product and subsequent patient
demand for medicines. SC retains procurement and usable-stock accounting.
