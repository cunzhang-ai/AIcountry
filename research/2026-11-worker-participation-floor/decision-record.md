## Mechanism and objections

Bargaining with employers can produce gains and usable time without another
statutory charge. It can leave workers outside the lead group's surplus even
where the lead directs their work. The selected candidate makes the group
obligor answer for the calculated floor. Keep strong upstream bargaining and
a publicly funded alternative on the same record. The lawfully equivalent
agreement can meet the duty. Participation with a different employer for its
own surplus remains a separate claim, and a deployment agreement supplies
no substitute. Departure preserves the original work period and its claim.

Use the [comparative and accounting record](../settlement/worker-participation-floor/01-a-share-that-survives-employer-change.md).
France provides actual legal fallback, the option of immediate payment and a
qualified insolvency comparator. The [archived working paper](../2026-11-worker-gains-through-transition/original-sources/france-profit-sharing-working-paper.pdf)
reports observed differences in compensation under a historical reform.
Individual profit-sharing was imputed from firm data. No result estimates
this rule's incidence or effects on supplied workers. The worked example's
financial and policy parameters are hypotheses.

A group obligation adds compliance and cash exposure, and can lead to
division, outsourcing or wage substitution. A gradual size phase-in and
actual control review address some channels. Small and genuine independent
suppliers remain distinguishable. An enforceable award can be uncollectible.
The selected trial leaves a participation balance ordinary unsecured under
the applicable insolvency law and gives no standing guarantee. A separately
financed employer-funded guarantee or protected custody may improve actual
recovery at a measurable cost. Keep ordinary wages, contributions, benefits
and creditors under their own rules and count public support for outsiders
under its own budget.

## Migration and next decision

Use prospective periods only. Preserve existing bargaining, ordinary labor
obligations, worker claims, successor evidence and independent appeal. An
exact first-sector instrument must price representation, verification,
grievances, independent receiving and insolvency exposure before starting.
Compare the same workers, production, adjusted household resources and
service receipt through two paid windows following closure of the first
covered period. The competent legislature must adopt a real budget and
limits. Reopen when measured money is offset by pay or benefit losses, an
employer transfer erases access, accrued claims fail under insolvency, or
ordinary bargaining secures the same protection with lower burdens. No
new tax, funding mandate or nationwide rate follows from this record.
