# Legacy conflicts: knowledge, ai and change

*Unresolved older-law and transition issues in integrated design 55; research, not enacted migration.*

| Earlier conflict or gap | Current working resolution | Continuing law, evidence and transition dependency |
|---|---|---|
| Older evidence rule is weaker than the everyday-justice candidate | Apply the stronger proposed exclusion of torture/coerced evidence and the specified review of derivative evidence | Criminal procedure, investigation training and remedies through legitimate adoption |
| AI lawmaking/AI review references in energy, health, research, criminal procedure, digital services and administration | Assembly has binding general-rule competence; independent professionals and AI provide evidence within mandates | The [full source-clause map](../../AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION.md) identifies material replacements; unreviewed references and status prose still require current-edition reconciliation |
| Digital privacy, AI-service and procurement manuscripts do not settle common-controller power across identity, data exchange, vendor operation and appeals | Layered public infrastructure with sector decision owners, limited technical operator, distinct oversight and equivalent non-digital routes selected | [Digital-power review](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-core-institutions/digital-ai-power.md) is research; old AI-sovereign references, data/procurement powers, shared credentials and vendor contracts require legitimate reconciliation and tested succession |
| Sector reporting, public accounts, resource measurements and policy evaluation lack a common provenance, revision and archive route independent of the assessed office | Federated original custodians plus separately governed official statistics/archive and plural evaluation; methods and suppression reviewable without a central personal dossier | [Public-evidence comparison](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-public-evidence/analysis.md) is research; statistics, records, privacy, audit and source-edition law plus actual controllers, staff, backups and enforcement need reconciliation |
| Current evidence and learning duties do not require a whole-country comparison or elected response before voters judge a governing term | A multi-dimensional public conditions account, prepublished comparisons, protected missing-person and external-harm evidence, opposition hearing and reasoned elected response are selected without one binding wellbeing score | [Public-conditions decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-public-conditions/decision-record.md) is research; original baseline, credible comparators, safe testimony, staff, law, independent controllers and public acceptance are missing |
| AI provisions mainly constrained capture and harmful automation but did not choose useful tasks, their owners or the conditions for keeping them | Plural task-mandated portfolio for access, option comparison, professional support, maintenance/hazards and inquiry, measured against non-AI service with funded exit | [AI public-capability decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-ai-public-capability/decision-record.md) is research; present law, source data, staff, compute/energy/water, real users, errors, alternative providers and outcomes remain unverified |
| Agent replication and high-speed mediation can turn message volume into formal public attention; revocation during disconnection leaves prior obligations and scarce shared resources contested | Distinct claims and affected stakes govern public response, with sponsor and framing challenge; agent acts have bounded mandates, offline ceilings and prior-law reliance and reconciliation | [Agentic-democracy decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-agentic-democracy/decision-record.md) is research; real civic dockets, legal notice, staff, privacy, remedy execution and affected people's choices remain untested |
