# C06. Paying for food-system security

*Part of [C06 public finance](../C06-public-finance.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

The Assembly separately authorizes food-access support, food-security
analysis, inspection, laboratories, reserve purchase and rotation, transport
and cold-chain capacity, and any public procurement or producer transition.
It records cash by due date, imported inputs and foreign currency, land and
water limits, workers, storage losses, safe disposal and review capacity.
Income support, a stocked warehouse and a functioning delivery route are
different expenditures and different claims of performance. An assumed
hospital saving or higher farm output cannot fund today's appropriation.

The budget compares direct household income or food support, competition
and market-entry measures, supplier diversification, public reserves and
more direct public production as coherent alternatives. It identifies who
receives a subsidy, who pays higher food or tax costs, and who bears the
risk of spoilage, failed harvest or an unneeded stock. Producer payments
cannot be booked as household nutrition without evidence of received food.
Procurement requires purpose, fair process, beneficial-controller disclosure,
quality testing, replacement supplier and independent challenge; an
emergency contract remains a public liability and cannot become party
patronage.

National equalization funds local access and delivery based on measured need
and ability to serve, without rewarding an authority for hiding hunger or
punishing a place for reporting it. A shock account keeps available safe
stock, household purchasing power, reserve release, price support,
replacement purchase and foreign-payment exposure distinct. The same
truck, fuel, storage, water or worker is counted once. When enacted claims
and actual supplies diverge, Treasury reports the gap; the elected Assembly
chooses any new burden within rights and ecological floors. Neither the
food-security office nor a model acquires a tax, rationing or borrowing
power from this account.

The [C09 food-system route](../C09/09-food-systems-and-nutrition.md) assigns
substantive duties, [C07](../C07/02-housing-mobility-and-utilities.md)
protects person-level access, and [C08](../C08/05-consumer-and-product-safety.md)
retains safety and buyer remedies. No real production, prices, contracts,
stock, dietary need, revenue or staffed inspection has been verified.

[FM06–FM11](../C09/09/02-food-reserve-release-and-replenishment.md)
specifies the separate source for release, rotation and replenishment.
An accepted purchase remains distinct from a received delivery.
