# Design310 decision on trial evidence and national expansion

## Baseline and request

Pin Design309 and the proposed working method. Apply CP03–CP04,
NP14–NP18, LT01–LT05 and WF85–WF89 on their original scope. Select
a prospective comparison and interim provision rule when a successful
trial competes for resources with later cohorts and its sources.
The project assistant authors the proposal. Elected bodies decide
actual cohorts under lawful authority.

## Evidence, hypotheses and rivals

The Kenya teaching experiment demonstrates why even a nominally similar
intervention needs an operator-specific review. It provides no care
effect size. The authored care example isolates an additional
mechanism, one pilot and its planned expansion drawing on the same
outside workforce. Local effects can also reflect need, infrastructure,
legitimate refusal or organization. A decline after scale does not
establish staffing as its cause.

Compare ordinary repair with no extra delivery, immediate replication,
a paid interim and later adapted path, and an earlier common offer
using genuinely additional resources. Immediate replication moves an
unacceptable gap to the origin. A direct common offer may avoid unfair
delay where independent paid capacity exists. A common obligation with
variable local means may be preferable to replicating the first provider.

## Selection and continuing rights

Choose LT06–LT10. Compare the shared resources and actual outcomes
before choosing dates, give waiting groups an accountable interim
option, and keep the ordinary national and local decision routes.
Prepare the later adapted offer in the case. No area gains control
over where workers may work. Review detects risks and cannot postpone
an existing entitlement. A later vote preserves earlier lawful
contracts, worker claims, records, protected source access and funded
handover. Independent remedy retains its original jurisdiction.
Funding interim delivery can divert resources from a full earlier
offer. Disclose that loss and revisit if a better same-scope alternative
can be staffed and paid.

## Next bounded action

Price a receiving and an origin roster on the same real duty dates,
including the people without recorded claims and the first feasible
resupply. Test whether the ordinary interim route can stay usable
during the later provider change. Reopen on evidence of withheld
need, lost capacity, changed outcome, material objection or a better
paid choice. Affected assent, independent review, authority, paid
readiness and better lives remain unverified.
