# Country case: production, durable human capability and shared limits

*Canonical CS08–CS10; part of [C01/03](../03-country-case-and-competing-designs.md).*

**CS08 — Feasibility is necessary, not an outcome victory.** One national
source/claims account can expose double spending and unpriced promises. It
cannot create a worker, public consent or a good life. NP's paid accepting
capacity may improve continuity but costs idle/overlap time and can entrench
patronage. Plural ownership may support exit or concentrate gatekeeping; direct
public supply may reduce contracting friction or amplify state dependence.
No read source settles the national ownership/tax package. Actual voluntary
qualified work, displaced output, household incidence, savings/investment,
renewal and import/foreign settlement are required for a calibrated comparison.

The old fixed-price GVA requirement, cash identities and resource proxies are
authored constraints/examples. They are not observations of achieved production,
engineering limits or future AI gains. Full37-function quantity/cost/source
estimates and structural rivals remain unfinished. Do not label an unknown
package impossible or affordable merely from its missing estimates.

**CS09 — Assisted output and enduring human capability differ.** A primary
single-firm AI study supports task-specific heterogeneous output gains; it does
not identify national wage/labor demand, clinical staffing or sovereign capacity.
A separate school experiment finds that easier AI-assisted practice can coexist
with poorer subsequent unaided performance. Guarded assistance can mitigate that
short-run loss without proving superior long-term learning. Neither finding
establishes a general gain or general harm across all AI/media and professions.

Retain C11/N07's bounded use and net-gain tests. Add a proportionate requirement:
when learning or independent human review/fallback matters, assess unaided or
otherwise independently supported retained ability, safe transfer and actual
fallback competence alongside assisted output. Preserve the person's necessary
assistive technology/accommodations; “unaided” never means removing access tools
or forcing dangerous live withdrawal. Safe simulations, protected practice,
qualified observation and independent errors can reveal dependence without
putting patients, rights or livelihood at risk. Human interventions need their
separate lawful consent/safety/ethics arrangements.

Fund reviewer/teacher/instructor competence, practice, non-AI capability and
source access before claiming real released capacity. No ceremonial human click,
higher homework score or trained-vendor label demonstrates independent oversight.
If learning/review deteriorates, revise/limit the tool and restore capacity before
wider reliance; preserve wages, learning access, current rights and safe service.
This can favor better augmentation, simplification or no AI, not a general ban.

**CS10 — Ecology and outsiders cannot be a balancing residual.** Material
throughput, reliable networks, source water, land, waste, emissions, animal
suffering and hard-to-reverse options constrain this country's choices. An
aggregate nominal output fit does not establish them; moving damage or unpaid
work abroad does not improve the complete account. Lawful defense, migration,
trade and treaties need actual partners, constrained force and safe receiving.
National averages cannot omit displaced people, downstream communities or
future claims. No newly read whole-country/ecological field comparison validates
these choices; current C09/C10 sources retain their own inspection limits.
Values, particular measurements and untested institutional hypotheses remain
separate. Reopen on physical/external harm or a less harmful complete supply path.
