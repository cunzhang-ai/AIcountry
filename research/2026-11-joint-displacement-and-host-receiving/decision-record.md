# Design400 decision. Keep multiple displacement routes jointly deliverable

## Baseline and selection

[Design399](predecessor-design-399/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md) gives host and
origin authorities separate duties, an advance and a durable
relocation option. When several incidents draw on the same receiving
places, a budget can still count an available home, a worker and
transport as if they can serve the same household at the same time.
It can count a transfer from existing tenants as additional housing.

Select [FR26–FR33](../country/C09/07/06-joint-displacement-and-host-receiving.md)
under the [working research method](../FRAMEWORK.md). Join the services
needed for a real placement on the same dates and protect both existing
and newly exposed people. Give lawful sector owners independent
responsibility within the joined offer. Qualify existing and standby
routes and price the second interval before relying on it.

## Alternatives, burden and authority

Use ordinary origin-by-origin referral and cash support. It preserves
local choice and can work when markets and services have spare capacity.
It can fail when separate claims count the same scarce unit or care.
A centralized national allocation system could compare all supply at
once, but can create a new source of coercion, unsafe disclosure or
priority decisions beyond its authority. Select local and sector
authority with shared dated evidence and a funded national alternate.
Compare additional direct public supply and genuinely available
temporary stock with independent worker and resident evidence.

An emergency lease can reduce ordinary availability or increase rent.
Check genuine additional capacity, enforce existing tenancy rights
and show displacement rather than hiding it. Reject an unlawful
eviction as a source of vacancies. A combined account can also slow
an urgent decision or expose protected locations. Permit safe partial
and independent action, maintain earlier clocks and protect only
necessary evidence. Elected bodies authorize sources and powers,
competent officers remain responsible for priorities and decisions,
and independent review can test omission, coercion or false capacity.
A joint calendar supplies no new discretion to change legal rights.

## Evidence, transition and next action

[Primary records](sources.md) document tight housing markets, different
shelter and accommodation roles, and host risks requiring scrutiny.
The [constructed case](../settlement/joint-displacement-and-host-receiving/01-two-origins-two-hosts-and-one-care-route.md)
tests overlapping assignments and later cash. It establishes neither
real-world cost, affected consent, lawful adoption nor durable outcomes.

Adopt prospectively under ordinary recovery, finance, XR and sector
laws. Name the host lead, independent national alternate and each
actual payer, receiver and review route. Preserve contracts, affected
workers, existing resident placements, prior dates and review, while
requiring separate authorization for compulsory action.

Next trace two real origins and one receiving area across two actual
windows. Compare accepted housing and care, host prior commitments,
turnover, travel and independently available standby. Reopen on new
unlawful displacement, ignored needs, unsourced funding, a closed
route or a better qualified arrangement.
