# Design294. Protected corrections and first-budget choices

10 November 2026 · Research selection · No actual enactment

## Baseline and question

[Design293](baseline.json) retains the matched first programme
and separate protected floor. Design286 priced K for loss of a
shared controller in the optional energy route. A change to
ordinary care, food or review can expose a different shortage.
Test those corrections and the optional programme together
without transferring prices across earlier examples. Apply the
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md). Codex assisted the
analysis. Actual affected participation, review and authorization
remain open.

## Alternatives and selection

- **Keep ordinary duties and the costed correction.** This leaves
  optional gains unrealized and retains more capacity for existing
  services and uncertainty. Select it in the weaker constructed
  case without a qualified additional review slot.
- **Retain narrower separately authorized starts.** In the revised
  reference E plus paid preparation fits, while the three-part
  option does not. Compare housing or preparation alone and
  disclose the forfeited energy benefit. In the weaker case E
  alone has the stated cash but lacks independent review. A
  separately paid, available and authorized review slot changes
  the feasible choice. The choice to use it for E is an explicit
  priority judgment conditional on the expected benefit.
- **Try the larger start with added cash and staffing.** This
  can still be an elected choice where all actual sources and
  qualified slots exist. Its gains must be compared with the
  work displaced and the cost of carrying the correction. Do
  not finance it by borrowing a protected court or electoral slot.

[JC28–JC32](../country/C06/65/07-protected-corrections-and-first-budget.md)
selects the common-budget rule. The

uses unchanged earlier option prices and explicitly authored new
correction and staffing terms. The [evidence](sources.md) supports
the distinctions, with no claim about actual budget sufficiency.

## Accountability and continuity

Reconcile the real payer and authorized staffing before release.
Preserve separate elected votes, prior authority and judicial,
electoral, clinical and payment owners. In the binding housing
branch keep the original lease and care support, identify the
cash and staffing shortfalls and retain independent relief.
A duty to pay remains visible even if the date has passed. Keep
the continuing baseline, repair and optional costs separately.
Disclose who waits under each choice. Maintain protected source
access and no AI or Treasury power to adjudicate an individual case.
