# TA01–TA04: person, place, period and received remuneration

*Part of [C06/40](../40-tax-law-and-accountable-revenue.md); design 100 research candidate, not enacted law.*

**TA01 — One person and one period.** The legal tax unit is each natural person, including a child with their own genuinely owned taxable income under supported agency, for one calendar year 1 January–31 December. A sole trader's business is part of that person's unit, not a second allowance or corporation. PA10's M for that year is the prepublished lawful scale: initial M uses the latest independently measured, lawfully published Y05 annual median W fixed by the adopting Act before PA19 commencement; if no trustworthy W exists, that Act itself must name and fund a protected provisional nominal M with disclosed divergence, rather than an officer inventing zero or moving the statutory start. Each following 1 January uses the maximum of **previous lawful M**, **previous M × the new verified general-CPI ratio if actually available**, and **new verified W if actually available**; omit a missing component with a published reason, never replace it by zero, repeat an old inflation ratio as new, or freeze the whole scale when another verified component is higher. A later favorable threshold correction yields a refund, but a late adverse M policy/definition revision does not retrospectively raise tax; ordinary newly proved taxpayer facts retain their own TX assessment window. Do not infer W from model payroll. Partial-year residents and domestic-source nonresidents combine their own income for that calendar year under **one** PA10 schedule, with no extra bands for accounts, jobs, residency switches or spouse. The PA19 start and prior net protections remain.

**TA02 — Tax residence is an evidenced personal connection, not citizenship.** An individual is resident from the day they establish an actually available principal home **and** ordinary life centered here, assessed by address/use, work, care and chosen social ties without treating marriage, children or a credential as conclusive; alternatively from the 183rd day of actual physical presence in any rolling 365 days when no evidenced principal home elsewhere explains a temporary visit. Residence ends on an evidenced durable transfer of ordinary life abroad; absent that evidence, it ends after 183 consecutive days away without an available principal home here. Document exact start/end days with notice, safe challenge and treaty override. Do not deem citizenship, refugee status, voting, an account, platform location or a short visit to be worldwide-tax residence. A resident is taxable on PA11 receipts actually obtained during resident days wherever sourced; a nonresident is taxable only on the defined domestic-source receipts. Pre-arrival foreign income and post-departure foreign income are not pulled in merely because the same person had a local day in the tax year. Domestic-source receipts before/after residence still enter that person's single annual schedule. A person can contest dual residence; an actually applicable treaty prevails and no state can unilaterally claim treaty credit/foreign enforcement.

**TA03 — Define domestic source by activity or debtor, not a server.** Domestic work remuneration is attributable to actual work days physically performed here, including remote work for a foreign payer, with documented mixed-day allocation. Domestic sole-trader business income is attributable to actual local operations/fixed place or a person habitually concluding local contracts, with arm's-length costs; mere domestic customers or hosting by itself does not create a personal business establishment. Domestic land/building rent and gain, gains on assets of that local business, dividends/interest paid by a domestic legal debtor, and statutory TW/earned-pension payments from a domestic public payer are domestic-source candidates. Resident receipts from all those sources and lawful foreign receipts enter PA11; nonresident foreign securities gains, a foreign pension, or mere foreign account ownership do not become domestic personal income without a separate enacted nexus. Royalties from rights actually used under a domestic licence are sourced to that use, with traced allocation, not the artist's location or a global platform address. Treaty-specific allocations and independently proven foreign-law limits take priority. Corporate/digital-services, product, land-value, inheritance/gifts and pollution bases are not generated by this personal-income source rule.

**TA04 — Actual receipt and one ordinary foundation exclusion.** PA11 taxable income is cash/accessible noncash consideration **actually and unconditionally received** in the year, including the portion of paid wages used to satisfy proven authorized tax withholding, **after deducting only the lawful employee Y contribution under PA11**, not merely invoiced wages, blocked bank credits, an unrealized gain, an estimated market value of care or a due but unreceived U/I05 amount. Marketable noncash remuneration counts at evidenced realizable value. A nontransferable household good/service actually delivered for discretionary personal use as remuneration also counts: private dwelling, car use or fee-school tuition cannot escape tax merely because it cannot be resold. Value only the delivered period at the lesser of equivalent reasonably available local arm's-length price and an independent supplier's actual charge, less employee payment; for employer-owned property use comparable rental/use value, not its entire purchase price. Required tools, protective equipment, genuine travel/site lodging away from ordinary home, disability access, necessary clinical care, ordinary public education/health and exact cost reimbursement remain excluded. An employer cannot declare private consumption necessary merely by requiring this pay form. Original comparable/supplier sources must be answerable; no credible comparable means no adverse invented AI valuation pending ordinary evidence/review.

Report minimum benefit-period/value/employee-payment facts without a private clinical/care dossier.
