# Protect usable time to vote

*Design511 · Proposed first-contest duty within [equal franchise and usable elections](../13-equal-franchise-and-usable-elections.md).*

**Guarantee access that fits paid work and care.** Before call, the adopting election instrument specifies neutral access through ordinary, early, assisted and authorized mobile voting, and a funded residual route where work or necessary care leaves no safe usable voting window. Measure from the permitted release through the actual receiving cutoff, including reasonable travel, foreseeable waits, assistance and return coverage. Possible opening hours alone do not establish access. The electoral authority publishes covered procedures before the election; existing stronger leave and access continue. The rule provides opportunity and preserves freedom to abstain.

**Assign each duty before relying on it.** An employer who can schedule an eligible employee away from a workable voting opportunity supplies the shortest sufficient paid release when no usable agreed route exists. Pay the ordinary lost scheduled earnings, with a neutral proof and correction rule. Where platforms or clients control actual accepted work blocks, applying the duty must address retaliation and contract reallocation within the applicable employment or sector law. For people without an employer that can release and pay them, publish a separate election-funded residual route for essential replacement care, access and eligible lost earnings on pre-enacted neutral terms. Pay qualified cover without diverting from a due care or safety duty. A preauthorized alternate route is required where immediate release from a safety-critical assignment is infeasible; if no route can receive the person, record the uncovered opportunity and seek independent useful-time correction. No one must disclose a vote, party preference or evidence of casting a ballot to receive support. Verify only the eligibility and practical timing needed for the duty.

**Give urgency its own receiver.** A safe oral, offline or assisted request reaches an authorized officer before the real cutoff. Notify the employer or service owner only of the bounded facts necessary to execute the decision. A scoped labour or electoral remedy can enforce a duty before close within applicable authority. Pay correction, actual missed opportunity and any election contest have distinct remedies. Document any uncovered groups and joint staffing loss without public identification. The proposed duty supplies no automatic poll extension, new way to count a late ballot or power to change an election already called. AI may model demand and conflicts. It cannot determine whether an elector has effectively voted or grant exception by prediction.
