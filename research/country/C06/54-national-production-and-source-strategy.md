# A national production and source strategy

*Part of [C06](../C06-public-finance.md); design 133, 3 November 2026.
NS01–NS35 research selection, not an enacted industrial or monetary policy.*

**Choose a service-first, diversified productive retrofit as the primary
country strategy.** Protect people's basic capabilities and practical political
control; improve housing, care, essential supply and useful infrastructure;
support plural production and voluntary trade without making cheap labor,
resource rents, an export miracle or assumed AI gains the foundation of rights.
This is a conditional strategy choice, not a sourced launch or a demonstrated
improvement over existing countries. Regional common provision remains a real rival.

The [paired sector-flow account](../../settlement/national-strategy/README.md)
uses independent authored units, not a new national GDP or producer forecast.
It exposes how a superficially improved domestic cash figure can accompany
worse external resilience or worse household resources. The original 37
function interfaces and all later duties remain; none is funded by an identity.

[WF](58-country-source-and-capacity-frontier.md) follows the observed baseline
with a finite all37/90-scope, paid-time/expense frontier. Service-first retrofit
and real whole rivals remain; no new production, taxable base, foreign workers
or validated AI gain is inferred. Actual net source/service increments remain open.

[WF73–WF76](58/17-whole-country-demand-and-work.md) makes the real-resource
strategy explicit: needs-protecting joint production/time rebalancing within
plural retrofit. Public priorities and private agency remain distinct; no
reference industry/final-use label authorizes cuts, career assignment or foreign
claims. Retain essential/democratic/earned duties and TW, with actual paid
training, retention, voluntary staffing and source; aggregate money/hours do
not establish capacity. Strong retain/repair and different whole rivals remain.

[NS31–NS35](54/08-reciprocal-supply-and-common-shortages.md) prices reciprocal aid under simultaneous needs, independently funded alternatives and actual household protection.
