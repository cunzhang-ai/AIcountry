# Two councils and one conditional national offer

This is a constructed comparison in cash units. Two elected councils
each need ten units to maintain a protected service this period. North
has two units of own-source funding and eight due from statutory
equalization. South has four and six respectively. The own-source
amounts are settled and committed to the service. Qualified staff and
service routes already exist at each council. The grants must settle by
the next payroll. No safe bridging source exists on these facts.

Separately, an elected national programme has eight additional units
available for optional public-building access upgrades, four for each
council. Assume each can qualify under published objective conditions
and any applicable later upkeep is separately funded. The grant
administrator proposes requiring the councils to transfer control of
unrelated hiring to the ministry. North rejects the condition. South's
council would accept the grant only on lawful project terms.

| Rule | North | South |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bundle due equalization and the upgrade with the unrelated condition | If the administrator holds the eight due units, only two are available for the protected service. The loss is eight units. The project cannot fix payroll. | South also declines the unlawful condition. If the six due units are held, the protected service is six units short. |
| Pay equalization and offer an unconditional extra allocation | Keep the ten for the protected duty. A valid new allocation could finance local priorities. | Keep ten and allocate the additional four if separately authorized. There is no assurance the allocation funds this specific upgrade. |
| Pay equalization separately and offer a grant with a relevant access test | Keep the ten, even if North declines the lawful project offer. A refusal may leave the building upgrade undone. | Keep ten for the protected duty and use four for the accepted upgrade. The council chooses a lawful method that meets the test. |

The third rule fits the stipulated objective. It preserves a meaningful
choice and ties an additional programme to a particular improvement.
An unconditional grant is a real rival where the elected purpose is
general fiscal capacity and local discretion. A national building
standard applying to both councils would require its own legal and
funded route. Even under the selected grant terms, an unaccepted North
award is neither spent nor automatically available to South.

If the ordinary transfer is unlawfully blocked, the independent court
can direct the authorized payer to carry out the existing duty. Check
that the alternate can release the eight units before payroll and that
the local account can receive them. If neither payer can settle by the
deadline, pursue any already authorized funded protection and show the
unpaid eight and any missed service. A later settlement does not turn
a missed payroll into timely delivery. Ordinary service funding,
project commitments and remedy remain separately accountable.
