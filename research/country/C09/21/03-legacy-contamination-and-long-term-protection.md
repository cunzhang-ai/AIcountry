# CLN09–CLN14. Protect people and fund the long tail at legacy sites

*Design363 · Part of [industrial closure](../21-industrial-cleanup-and-insolvency.md).*

**CLN09 — Accept evidence before a payer is identified.** The environmental
authority provides assisted reporting for residents, workers, local bodies
and affected right holders. Keep the first receipt, observed exposure,
uncertain pathways, known past use and source custody distinct. A credible
signal reaches a qualified case officer who can arrange independent
assessment under applicable law. Use prior-law urgent water, housing,
health and safety protection while evidence and liability are checked.
A poor map, missing sample or unresolved operator does not establish
safety. Protect private health and location data while publishing enough
site and use information to prevent harmful reliance. Listed sites and
affected people have direct routes to correct a classification or delay.

**CLN10 — Allocate the legacy budget by exposure and actual protection.**
The environmental authority publishes separate schedules for urgent
protection, investigation, continuing controls and elective redevelopment.
Compare severity, duration, preventable harm, exposed people and ecological
dependence under stated uncertainty. Include people outside the site and
those without ordinary services or safe reporting routes. Population counts,
land value and political visibility cannot decide priority on their own.
Price workable alternatives and interim receiving. Give affected places,
people and opposition usable evidence and challenge before the elected
portfolio choice. A ranking, score or map neither authorizes the work nor
proves liability. Keep earlier statutory duties and urgent rules in force.

**CLN11 — Give legacy work a separate paid source and owner.** The Assembly
votes a distinct legacy-site envelope and dated purpose under CLN06, FB and
DB. Identify the responsible environmental custodian, lawful payer, each
local or sector service owner and qualified accepting operators. Cost
protective supply and access, assessment, remediation, verification,
maintenance and post-work monitoring separately. Join travel, sites,
workers, facilities and budgets once across simultaneous cases. Do not
rely on speculative land sale, an uncollected recovery or a restricted
current-operator reserve. Seek recovery from legally liable parties under
the ordinary process, retaining defenses and existing priorities. Credit
only actual same-purpose receipts once. A protective public payment does
not decide ultimate liability or guarantee private losses.

**CLN12 — Keep inspection and maintenance after the visible work ends.**
Before relying on a remedy, the inspector independent of the operator
and development promoter states the supported use, continuing restrictions,
observable protection criteria, review dates, accountable custodian and
paid route if conditions change. Provide accessible notice to lawful
occupants, users and future parties who depend on the use conditions.
Keep funded monitoring until independent evidence supports a lawful
change. Use a site-specific schedule, with annual public reconciliation
of due work and sources and earlier review on a missed inspection, new
exposure or proposed change of use. A transfer or accounting close
does not certify safety or end the duty. Do not claim unrestricted
use where the evidence supports only a defined use.

**CLN13 — Reopen on new evidence and preserve the earlier duty.** When a
source, operator or monitoring result fails, verify the changed hazard
and identify the first uncovered action. Use the applicable urgent
protections and independently usable review. Recheck paid receiving and
the next period. Hold an unbound elective stage if its protection or
finance fails. An already due duty keeps its original date and remedy.
Where no qualified route fits, disclose who remains exposed and seek a
lawful funded decision. Do not transfer the duty or its cost to another
locality without authority and actual acceptance. Keep accrued liability,
property, compensation and collective claims in their own proceedings.

**CLN14 — Verify what people received and keep the duty transferable.**
Report separately what was assessed, funded, physically completed,
independently verified and actually received, with privacy protection.
Preserve records, original dates, restrictions, continuing funds and
lawful recovery claims when an operator or government changes.
AI can compare monitoring histories and flag a missing area or due
inspection. It cannot establish liability, rank people out of protection
or certify conditions. Reopen on persistent unequal protection, failed
monitoring, new adverse evidence or a safer funded alternative.
