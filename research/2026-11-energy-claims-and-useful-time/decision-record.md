# Design284. Energy claims and useful-time protection

10 November 2026 · Research selection · No actual enactment

## Baseline and decision

[Design283](baseline.json) keeps the targeted bridge and funded supply
in the constructed first programme. MS17–MS21 requires verification
of actual receiving. This selection specifies a local payer route and
the next proof needed before relying on a real launch. Apply the
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).
Codex assisted the research and drafting. No affected participant,
external reviewer, delivery authority or elected body took part in
this design decision.

## Diagnoses and alternatives

A household may be short of cash, have an unresolved building fault
or lack usable access to the account. Local cash may be absent even
where a later grant is plausible. The [Copenhagen records](sources.md)
separate resident assistance and later reimbursement and disclose a
limited estimate of administration costs. They do not estimate
the effects of a new advance, changes to eligibility or receipt of
safe heat.

- **Ordinary local funding with reimbursement where justified.**
  This can work if the payer already owns enough eligible cash at the
  deadline and the standing authorities and providers deliver. Later
  reimbursement may be useful where the claim is economically and
  legally justified. Treat it separately from initial funding.
- **Select a bounded advance or sufficient standing local funding.**
  Finance early awards and usable receiving before binding. In the
  [authored case](../settlement/energy-claims-and-useful-time/02-authored-cash-and-heat.md),
  the standing source only covers the awards, so choose the separately
  authorized advance for the alternate and its replenishment. Where a
  standing source offers the same protection with less burden, prefer
  that route. Preserve an exact narrower choice or ordinary repair
  under its actual authority.
- **Centralize direct payment and physical delivery.** A national
  payer or utility may reach some recipients more quickly through a
  well-established route. It could reduce multiple local transfers,
  but a unified authority would need legal powers, paid site receiving
  and separately usable review. A billing credit can leave indirect
  customers and failed building systems exposed. Consider it where
  access is demonstrably wider or the ordinary local route is captured.

## Authority, failure and migration

Elected bodies choose the source and cap, the local officers hold the
first report and verify household receipt, the utility and property
controller own separate checks and lawful repair, and independent review
keeps the path usable during disputes. The payer cannot infer tenant
receipt from its own credit. Preserve lawful original evidence without
requiring a common household database. Use funded, accepted backup
and report failure if a shared carrier or records route is lost.

Keep valid awards, prior deadlines, eligibility and billing and repair
remedies across programme expiry and reconsideration. Separate reopened
files, revised decisions and actual restored protection. An intergovernmental
grant refusal or optional decision not to pursue it does not determine
a resident's original eligibility. The funding and technical records
have no paired observations of timely physical receiving. Real source,
readiness, independent review and improved lives remain open.

## Next bounded action

For one competent local district obtain the dated payer record, a
resident's accepted ordinary and alternate routes and the utility and
building checks for the same protected interval. Include an indirect
account and the next reserve call. Compare the actual gross advance
with an eligible standing source. Verify independent correction and
useful-time receipt before relying on the real first motion. Reopen on
late cash, failed pass-through, unavailable safe receiving, repeated
grant administration or a better complete alternative.
