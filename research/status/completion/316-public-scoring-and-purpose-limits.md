# Design408. Protect separate public rights from a shared adverse label

[ASI01–ASI08](../../country/C11/09/16-public-scoring-and-purpose-limits.md)
blocks out-of-purpose adverse classifications and preserves scoped fact
reuse. It assigns timely cross-office reporting, independent review
and the execution of each affected remedy.

[Worked case](../../settlement/public-scoring-and-purpose-limits/01-one-tax-fact-and-two-services.md)
tests an accurate source fact, an unauthorized label, one shared reviewer
and actual receiving. Operational protection and outcomes remain open.
