# Design229 decision record. Open contests and neutral enforcement

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned design 228](baseline.json)

## Decision and limits

A10 and ER already protect open qualification and timely election
remedies. OJ and P already require independent prosecution,
ordinary liberty standards and outside-command review. The
remaining gap is foreseeable practical exclusion through the
timing and combination of enforcement measures even where the
ballot and nominal legal case remain intact. Add a prospective
A10 anti-retaliation and enforcement-neutrality floor and
[PX01–PX10](../country/C03/22-open-contests-and-neutral-enforcement.md)
as its ordinary-law implementation. Follow the
[working method](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).
The proposed changes do not adopt a constitutional rule, authorize
a case or amend the rights of any actual contestant.

Equal political standing, genuine competition, victim safety and
equal accountability are design requirements. A timely remedy
can matter as much as a later finding. The six-hour first interim
decision is a normative candidate with a shorter useful-time
standard, not evidence of actual courts' capacity. The test adds
no substantive offence, candidate ban, emergency power or office.

## Whole alternatives at the same legal scope

| Alternative | Strong case | Material objection |
|---|---|---|
| Retain ordinary P, OJ and electoral relief with more staffing | Preserves equal process and avoids a second urgent gate. Adequate where actors can see cumulative harm and execute on time. | A later ordinary remedy can miss a specific opportunity even while formal eligibility remains. Separate lawful acts can combine to deny effective competition. |
| Require recorded neutral grounds, prior review of planned harmful restrictions and urgent executable relief | Targets timing and real effects on anyone with a concrete political interest. Lawful ordinary investigation and urgent protective action remain available. | Adds demand on duty judges, counsel and custody. Captured reviewers or selectively staffed alternatives can defeat it. |
| Parliamentary permission or temporary personal inviolability | Can shield legislators from a hostile executive where ordinary courts are weak. | A majority can shield allies or lift protection against rivals. Nonmembers and newly organizing residents remain exposed. Evidence and witness protection may degrade while cases wait. |
| Executive intervention in prosecution subject to written publication | A traceable elected law officer can account publicly for intervention under its own law. | A published or delayed direction still enables case control. It does not solve selective timing or assure independent relief. |

Select the second option under the existing OJ/PO and election
structure. Do not borrow a parliamentary immunity or ministerial
case intervention. The [timed case](../settlement/neutral-enforcement/02-two-paths-through-one-campaign-clock.md)
shows lawful protection of a campaign opportunity, failure of
late execution and a distinct case of urgent action on real
witness danger. A general freeze or arrest is never justified
by the usefulness of an election result.

## Control, scarcity and lawful transition

Existing appointment and conflict rules select the available
ordinary judge and any outside-command case officer. The
Assembly provides standing sources. Fund defense, interpretation,
record access, duty hearing, lawful alternates and actual
execution without diverting the existing general liberty floor.
Trace custody, payroll, selection, credentials, record originals
and order delivery under a hostile incumbent and hostile reviewer.
Opposition, voters and independent representatives can use
lawful challenge and oversight. A court, audit or executive
office cannot allocate the mandate or suspend a poll by delay.

Prospective commencement requires competent A10, A9/RR and
ordinary transition as applicable. Preserve old contest law,
original case dates, valid orders, witness and defense
protection, valid ballots and funded OP/T/HT successors.
The six-hour rule does not restart P09, change an already
called calendar or put an unavailable outside prosecutor in
office. Record the unmet protection if independent staff or
execution are missing.

## Bounded next test

Compare one time-sensitive official action and one credible
ordinary criminal threat under funded ordinary review and PX.
Use actual duty rosters, access rules, original time stamps,
paid counsel and a verified alternate executor. Seek qualified
legal review and affected-party participation. Reopen if
good-faith enforcement is routinely obstructed, officials evade
scope, real harms are consistently discovered too late, or
parallel ordinary urgent work is displaced. Actual enactment,
independent review, staffing, execution and improved democratic
outcomes remain unestablished.
