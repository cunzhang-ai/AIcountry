# Design523 decision record

*14 November 2026 · National and local recurring source choice*

## Baseline and decision

Use [Design522](baseline.json), the existing [research framework](../FRAMEWORK.md),
the already selected first mandate and FB55–FB65. Select
[FB66–FB70](../country/C06/62/12-common-exposure-and-durable-supply.md).
They make materially shared economic and operational exposures explicit before
a new enduring commitment can rely on the first or forward budget. Standing
national and local duties keep their actual laws, sources, earlier dates and
remedies. Retain the [frozen Design520](../2026-11-first-round-stable/README.md)
as the first-round baseline.

Three parallel analytical reviews examined recurring finance, cross-payer scope
and whole-country structural alternatives. They share the project corpus.
No direct participation or independent empirical confirmation follows from the
number of analyses. [Primary observations and institutional terms](sources.md)
support the existence of overlapping tax bases and a strong stabilization rival.
They do not establish the magnitude or probability of a shared shock in the
proposal. The worked case supplies an internally explicit reconciliation only.

## Objections and exact consequences

A downside test can wrongly delay valuable work if it assumes every bad outcome
at once. Use a coherent publicly stated shared path, verify scope independently
and show a conflicting qualified alternative and a more adverse branch where
that changes the decision. Prospective authority is distinct from finance and
actual receiving. Give a qualified reserve credit where the gap is principally
timing and durable net sources and replenishment can be shown. A durable draw,
ordinary taxes, lawful recurring reprioritization and public or interlocal
delivery remain real alternatives with distribution and opportunity costs.
Unknown risks should remain explicit rather than converted into veto power for
forecasters. Already-due obligations retain their own rights and remedies.

The [matched fiscal and supply comparison](../settlement/first-country-resource-account/02-different-receipts-one-economic-risk.md)
supports ordinary repair with immediate protected relief, an elected recurring
alternative where the loss persists, and independently priced material delivery
before release. The first reserve use does not fund the next call. Even a
qualified fiscal correction cannot supply a lost worker or accepted vehicle.
Compare equivalent real gains from competent predecessor law before crediting a
new institution. Require scoped data access and direct objection for parties
outside the standard route.

## Migration and next decision

No tax, reserve authority, staffing or contribution procedure is changed by
selecting this research proposal. A real first budget needs dated payer,
service and overlapping source evidence and an exact independently scrutinized
local and national choice. Use the common two-window account and four-year
tail to compare one funded recurring correction with qualified ordinary repair
or a real stabilization alternative. Actual adopting bodies set sources, caps
and rights-valid terms. Keep this work separate from an assumed countrywide
surplus. Reopen on material shared receipts, worker or supply dependence,
unfunded operations, unacceptable household incidence or an equally protective
option with lower costs. Adoption, operation and comparative better lives
remain separate findings.
