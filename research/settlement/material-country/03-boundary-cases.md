# Material-country boundary cases

*Authored reasoning and finite cash/capacity examples; no actual country or verdicts.*

| Trigger | Selected response and retained limitation |
|---|---|
| An official says all 37 functions have positive bids, so launch is funded | Bids are not requirements or qualified source. The full-duty matrix preserves unknown values; no feasibility follows. |
| More nominal output is solved from available household cash | NP04 rejects that as achieved production; test actual service/market supply, voluntary spending/investment, imports and distribution. |
| Public care payroll is relabeled emergency reserve | Identify released tasks/qualified shift and real replacement, or count no new reserve. Existing3448/3560 fixture roles are not free increments. |
| A person receives a care top-up but every qualified provider declines | Payment is received; care is still not delivered. Actual paid residual/preaccepted capacity and independent remedy remain due under their own source law. |
| A contractor's failure is discovered at night | A paid accepted alternate may activate only its actual contracted shift/stock/access. Penalty, directory or future procurement isn't receiving. |
| Public available cash 100, core essentials45 and democratic functions15, comparable unsecured arrears 80, new optional20 | In the authored legally fungible candidate class, core 60, arrears 40 pro rata, optional0; arrears 40 remains unpaid. This does not declare real secured funds fungible or reprice physical care. |
| Some arrears are owed to a person now without food | Apply neutral I/E need/useful-time criteria and independent review. An entire wealthy creditor's claim cannot be relabeled core. No pension/client asset diversion. |
| Available cash is below even the minimum core | No feasible operation certified; use actual prior-law relief and urgent elected correction. The calculation supplies no detention extension, tax or cancelled election. |
| A large court compensation judgment seeks the whole national cash pool | Preserve judgment/claim and actual source priority; no court self-appropriation or Treasury disregard. NP priority applies only after its own lawful compatibility/adoption, with independent review. |
| Treasury pays a friendly ministry first and calls opposition scrutiny optional | Enumerated functional schedule and source evidence control; exact classification/payment is challengeable. Institutional labels are no uncapped protected bill. |
| A new licensing/coercive power is ready but defense/review is absent | The new power waits for its required safeguards. Existing rights/claims do not wait for a future national package. |
| Government publishes studies but no actual expansion decision | First120-day proposal/180-day disposition and annual public choice are independently remediable process duties, not automatic adoption. A reasoned retain vote remains politically contestable. |
| An enacted service date arrives during engineering delay | Duty is overdue; safety remains real, notice/interim receiving/funded correction remain due. No official readiness certificate erases either law or hazard. |
| Private firms retain profit and households save | Legitimate with real counterparts; not forced payout/consumption or automatically available public finance. Revise supply/use/finance forecasts honestly. |
| Taxing high incomes raises less than expected or private FX sellers decline | Actual source/price/behavior failure; revise lawful burdens/scope, financed bridge or timing with claim continuity. Nominal revenue isn't physical service or state ownership of exports. |
| A delayed clinical course extends beyond the comparison window | Carry completion, bridge, renewal and later operation into both rivals' accounts. A cheaper unfinished snapshot cannot win. |
| A new cabinet rejects the old preparation programme | It can revise prospective policy within law, carrying contracts, pay, source records and due claims to actual successors. No completion-protected government. |

The numerical model compares legally different cash-priority assumptions and
actually accepted versus promised standby in a tiny authored due window. It
preserves restricted funds, unpaid claims and limits. It deliberately does not
invent all current function quantities/costs; national feasibility stays unknown.
