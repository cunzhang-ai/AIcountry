# F6. A protected protester is gravely injured by an officer

*Part of [Whole-country stress](../../FOUNDING-AND-STRESS.md); integrated design 55 research, not a real-world result.*

**Events:** After a lawful opposition gathering, a person is seriously injured
and detained. The implicated unit holds the video and custody log, disputes
the outside investigator's access and calls the force necessary; the assigned
prosecutor has a material conflict.

**Candidate response:** Emergency medical care and an independent custody hearing
proceed immediately. The first custodian has a legal duty to preserve original
evidence, log the actual detention time and notify the preassigned outside
investigator. The investigator seeks an urgent preservation or production
order if access is refused, recording any loss that the order cannot reverse.
A lawful alternate prosecutor takes the case under the prior conflict rule;
forensic and defense access are protected. A temporary duty or weapons
restriction receives review and replacement staffing without deciding guilt.
The injured person can seek protection and civil relief separately from any
criminal charge. **What remains unproved:** the alternate investigator,
prosecutor, medical examiner, evidence route, court and Treasury must have
distinct effective controllers, staff and funds. If every route depends on the
same command or vendor, the case is a constitutional failure, not proof that
the officer is guilty or that a formal transfer occurred.
