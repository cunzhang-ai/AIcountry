# Prepared protocol: can people exercise the actual proposed franchise?

2 November2026 · Not conducted, funded, authorized or a certification procedure

## Question and rivals

Assess whether people can discover an option, express their own choice privately,
receive the intended ballot/credit, correct a source error and understand how a
person receives a seat. Compare EL single flexible-list with mixed two-vote,
closed-list and regional/multi-member rivals using equivalent actual fields,
languages, time/access support and resources. Do not choose only a small easy
sample ballot or rank systems solely by speed. This is not a web/front-end task.

A qualified accessible-election team with actual affected participants and an
independent reviewer would own the assessment. Root research cannot supply those
roles or governing authority. Before recruitment obtain a legitimate safe study
mandate, actual funding, fair paid time, language/care/travel/accommodation and
protected withdrawal/complaint/custody arrangements. No volunteers, diagnoses,
party affiliations, real voting choices or disability records are needed to make
a green artifact. Do not collect sensitive testimony where safe custody is absent.

## Participants and meaning

Begin with small iterative walkthroughs chosen by access needs, followed by an
explicitly funded broader comparison; a planning target of60 adults is a hypothesis,
not a powered sample, representativeness claim or minimum vote-competence score.
Include older/low-literacy voters, people using visual/tactile/audio/communication
assistance, minority-language/remote residents, displaced/multiple-home people,
newly eligible residents and nonusers who cannot finish. Self-described access
needs suffice; medical labels or a political-belief test do not qualify a person.
Use supported accessible consent, chosen helper and independent alternative;
compensation survives refusal/withdrawal and is never tied to a correct answer.

Use fictitious lists/names and assigned nonpolitical target tasks, not persuasion
for real parties. Participants may decline any task. Observe their chosen method,
interpretation, correction, help and effort; never let an assistant select a vote.
Ask a return explanation in their chosen medium; a polished English answer is
not the criterion. Separate inability to use the artifact from unwillingness or
an unfamiliar valid meaning. A participant's failure never affects citizenship,
franchise, benefit, service or employment.

## Same-scope tasks and full-field exposures

1. Verify which national/local rights apply to a citizen never domiciled inside,
   a local-only resident, a protected displaced voter and someone vesting by P.
   Correct a missing record without a landlord/platform or invented district.
2. Find an assigned list at early/middle/late positions in the actual field.
   Include the N40/320-list envelope, grown-roll field and N400/3,200-list stress,
   not only five famous options. Formats may vary only with equivalent choices.
3. Cast a list-only vote, matched person preference, candidate-only implied list,
   cross-list/multiple preference and a corrected primary mark. Distinguish losing
   a preference from losing a clear primary. The model's interpreted tokens are
   not an observation of paper/audio/tactile use or handwriting accuracy.
4. Privately inspect the chosen list's full actual-sized pool/fallback order;
   compare full-inline, segmented directory and closed-list alternatives. Check
   optionality, nominee identification, neutral ranking and whether a tool hides
   unfamiliar lawful options. No forced giant-font-shrunk all-name sheet.
5. Request a chosen assistant, private fallback, paper/mobile/absent route and a
   receipt that discloses status without proving the vote. Simulate a late/duplicate
   outer envelope and a false prior-vote flag; never expose an inner marked ballot
   to settle identity. Do not simulate coercion by actually threatening a person.
6. Use accessible first-receipt/court-help examples before V and at P, distinguish
   actual correction from an order/invoice or after-vote mitigation. Explain what
   a count, capacity exhaustion, fixed full N/n and supplemental poll do; no guessed
   mandate on timeout. Measure provider/counsel/record interactions as real work.

## Observations, protection and stopping

Record unambiguous intended versus actually recorded choices for assigned tasks,
unknown meaning, invalid/overvote events, navigation/help, time/fatigue, inaccessible
routes, confidentiality/agency loss and participants' reasons. Report cohort/task
variation, missing participants, uncertainty and harms alongside aggregate numbers.
Do not average a group losing privacy or primary votes into an overall score.
Neither60 people nor zero observed errors proves an equal free election.

Any unresolved design-caused loss of a clear primary, inaccessible sole channel,
helper override, sensitive identity exposure or unavailable source/remedy blocks
an actual readiness claim and requires redesign/retest of the affected dependency.
This does not give a researcher or officer power to postpone an existing lawful
election, change its count, remove a candidate or invent funding. Competent elected
and court source-law decisions/remedies remain, with actual lost opportunity
recorded. A politically attractive result cannot override direct participant harm.

Use coded minimal records with participant-held correction/withdrawal routes,
separate compensation details and no reusable voter identities/political profiles.
Set a lawful short raw-data schedule before consent; a candidate14-day postanalysis
raw deletion plan needs actual safe review/holds and participant rights. Preserve
safe aggregate reasoning/correction history without permanently public testimony.
Publish what was inspected, not an assertion that unknown users or future media
were represented. Current actual participant count, budget, findings, independent
review, lawful authority and operating readiness are all absent.
