# OJ49–OJ54. Reach a review while a crime report can still be acted on

*Part of [ordinary justice and prosecution](../25-ordinary-justice-and-public-prosecution.md).
Design250 research selection.*

**OJ49 — Record the report and assign a lawful next act.** Use P06's
protected first receipt and assistance, including phone, in-person and
safe representative routes. Record the allegation, existing case
references, safe contact, receiving officer, immediate harm and
specific time-sensitive leads. A report remains distinct from a
recorded offense and proof of an offense. A routing label, duplicate
marker, missing suspect or incomplete file cannot erase the report
or its clock. Give an initial reasoned status by seven calendar days
from protected receipt, sooner for urgent protection or evidence.
If usable notice is unsafe or impossible, record the reason, safe
attempts and the available review route. Notice does not promise a
completed investigation. Maintain the original date through referrals
and verify actual acceptance.

**OJ50 — Act on reasonable time-sensitive leads within actual powers.**
A qualified investigator assesses a specific, credible expiring lead
as soon as practicable. Record the source, known last useful time,
lawful scope and named accepting actor. Take or seek the minimum lawful
preservation step before known loss where practicable, with a recorded
decision by 24 elapsed hours after protected receipt of the credible
lead or earlier useful time. Document action, justified refusal, actual
impossibility or shortage. Record the difference between a request, its
acceptance and the usable material actually preserved. A specific
voluntary request can preserve material without authorizing inspection.
Compulsory access or preservation requires its own prior law and any
P07 warrant. No retention of unrelated records, generalized platform
search or mass monitoring follows. Preserve contrary evidence and
source provenance. Similar reports can prompt scoped inquiry without
creating corroboration from copies or inferring guilt from a pattern.

**OJ51 — Give non-action and closure different reasons.** Record whether
the matter remains unassigned, awaits a known lawful step, has been
accepted by another authority, lacks a reasonable remaining line of
inquiry, has been closed for another lawful reason, or reached a
charging decision. Identify material lines pursued and their outcomes,
remaining reversible actions, unmet steps, resource reasons and any
lost evidence. No suspect, elapsed time, automated risk or a target
for cleared cases cannot by itself justify closure. A capacity failure
remains a capacity failure even if recovery becomes impossible.
Notify safely and give an intelligible route to review without disclosing
protected witnesses, suspects or sensitive evidence unnecessarily.
Support and urgent civil protection retain their own criteria.

**OJ52 — Give direct, funded independent review before useful time is lost.**
The affected person or safe representative can ask an uninvolved
prosecutor to review a refusal, closure or material inaction,
without first completing police complaints or proving an offense.
Accept a plain request without a case code where the original report
can lawfully be found. Preserve original receipt. Triage a live risk
to evidence or safety against its actual useful deadline. Seek an
operative response before known loss where possible. If it cannot be
done, record the reason and any still usable protective step. For
ordinary review give a reasoned disposition within 14 calendar days of protected
receipt, with one reasoned extension of at most 14 days for a complex
case, communicated before expiry. Earlier legal and useful-time duties
prevail. The reviewer can uphold lawful closure or require a specified
lawful reconsideration or investigative step within adopted powers.
Name the competent executor, source, evidence scope and useful time.
Use OJ28–OJ29 when the review chain is implicated, and preserve direct
court legality and urgent protection. Review supplies no warrant,
required charge, renewed limitation period, presumption of guilt or
punishment. An unstaffed review is a failure, not a decision.

**OJ53 — Pay for ordinary follow-through and verify correction.** Use
existing police, support, prosecution and court roles and distinct
source lines. Reserve trained independent reviewer time and a paid
investigative alternate against declared simultaneous loss. Apply
lawful risk, time and evidence priorities and disclose missed
obligations. A support worker, investigator and independent reviewer
are separate resources. A reviewer can order or seek only the remedy
the law permits. Record whether evidence was actually kept, a review
received or a safety measure delivered. If useful time has passed,
record the lost opportunity and pursue any remaining lawful action,
support or remedy. An accepted complaint or later allocation cannot
rewrite the earlier failure.

**OJ54 — Detect suppression without rewarding coercion.** Audit samples
from protected first reports, unresolved and closed cases and review
requests. Separately account for missing registration, delay, no lead,
loss of a lead, capacity gaps, review corrections and actual receiving.
Include safe accounts of people who could not reach the service without
building a universal surveillance register. Charge and closure rates
are descriptive, not quotas. Publish safe aggregate patterns and
independent correction under existing PO, AA and SR. AI can organize
original material, flag a possible deadline or suggest a comparison.
A competent person checks provenance, acts and records reasons.
AI cannot decide whether to record a report, close a case, find guilt
or extend investigative powers. Preserve P07–P15, open claims,
staff, sources and fair trial through migration.
