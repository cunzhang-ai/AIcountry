## Who has power and who must answer

Existing MC conduct careers license custody/payment, managers/brokers/advice,
nonbank lending and venues. Prudential careers additionally license insurer,
funded pension and CCP promise/funding risk; the monetary authority has its real
settlement role only. Fixed chief/reserve appointments, protected career cases,
separate elected operating pay/source custody and direct ordinary courts continue.
One source/consultant serving several roles is no independent review. National
DR governs authorized changed general schedules; a new AI criterion is not a
mere fact update. Ordinary people need no technical dossier to report a problem.

On nonbank failure, an ordinary court appoints an unconflicted qualified paid
administrator, names the actual property/pools/claims and reviews return/runoff/
U restructuring or closure. No new sovereign rescue panel or financial chief
chooses its own final judge. Private judgments use B/U estate/civil execution;
State officials' own wrongs use MC28's protected Treasury payer and express
bounded external execution. Missing funds/foreign recognition/skill is recorded
as failure and a continuing claim, not a fabricated receiving account.

## Whole alternatives and what remains unproved

A public safe-payment/public-pension system with fewer private promises can
reduce fragility but concentrates provision. Disclosure/private contracting
reduces entry costs but shifts proof/tail/custody burdens to users. Cooperative
finance can improve voice while concentrating local losses. A levy-funded
investor/insurer guarantee needs explicit coverage, premiums, headroom, loss
bearers and source, and remains a rival rather than an implied promise here.
Current NB can be rejected on credit/insurance exclusion, failed remedy or a
better whole alternative; more licensed processes do not establish better lives.
