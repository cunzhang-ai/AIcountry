## Evidence and genuine alternatives

The [Copenhagen records](sources.md) describe the budget case, declared
information service and oversight questions. A call that avoids a
staffed queue may represent a correct answer, a repeated attempt or a
lost right. Real barriers include inaccessible official language,
a missing receiver, insufficient staffing and shared platform failure.
The records do not show which diagnosis dominates for the proposed use.

- **Select funded ordinary repair first.** Fund clearer information,
  assisted filing, an accepted receipt and useful-time independent
  correction. Authorize an exact smaller motion only where its source
  and qualified receiving are supported. It may leave repeated simple
  questions or after-hours information gaps.
- **Trial an optional assistant alongside the ordinary route.** Give it
  a bounded information or chosen navigation task, separate funds and
  independently usable exit. It may improve access for some people,
  including those who find written instructions hard to use. A more
  accessible voice tool may still fail across languages, accents,
  disabilities or providers. Treat missed and unresolved cases as
  evidence and compare the whole cost against ordinary repair.
- **Use a common automated first contact and reduce staffed intake.**
  This can reduce handoffs and increase availability if the service
  genuinely works. It concentrates gatekeeping and shared dependencies.
  Any future broad route would need a practical direct choice and
  evidence to support a separate staffing decision. Reject the authored
  version that misses more urgent cases despite a higher overall count.

## Authority and continuing obligations

The sector owner owns lawful receipt and accepted handoff. A contractor
supplies only the named task. Keep material records under the responsible
service authority and quality samples separate. The resident can reach an
independent reviewer without the implicated platform or a recording.
Publish the real comparative scope, omissions and due remedies. If the
tool fails, the original dates, claims, wage obligations, applicable
priorities and lawful records continue. Stop only affected optional use
and arrange paid transfer, repair or lawful closeout.

The [constructed comparison](../settlement/public-intake-without-a-digital-gate/02-authored-service-receipt.md)
tests limited cash, independent review, common failure and a specific
urgent handoff. It does not qualify the actual budget or forecast gain.
The first national comparison retains its earlier numerical assumptions.
No experiment itself authorizes processing new data, staff reductions,
adjudication or country-wide use.

## Next bounded action

For one existing service, measure staffed navigation and assisted filing
from first contact through accepted handoff and delivered protection.
Include people who use no account or AI and those needing interpretation
or urgent correction. Agree a capped comparison and usable independent
route before any later trial. Reopen on lost receipt, changed operating
scope, inaccessible fallback or a better qualified alternative.
