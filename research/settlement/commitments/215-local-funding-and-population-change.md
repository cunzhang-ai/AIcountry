# 215. Local funding and population change

| Owner | Distinct obligation | Receipt or failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assembly and local councils | Vote common scope, source, revisions and any conditional continuity authority | Show fixed and variable cost, standard effort, existing receipts and later exposure. No hidden redistribution. |
| Statistical producer and calculation officer | Publish qualified inputs and a contestable assessment | Distinguish changed method, need and base. Preserve source and old lawful vintages. |
| Treasury payer and local service operator | Pay the undisputed amount and capped advance under prior law | Verify owned cash and resident receiving on original dates. Preserve failure and unpaid claims. |
| Independent review and successor | Check evidence, authority, actual receipt and reconciliation | Count the same amount once. Keep residents' claims separate from any later public recovery. |
