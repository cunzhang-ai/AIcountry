# One activity account and matched financial counterparties

*Design 59 baseline, million fictional CU per year. All quantities and sector assignments are authored.*

| GDP by expenditure | Amount | GDP by income | Amount |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Household final consumption | 1,800 | Employee compensation | 2,280 |
| Government nonmarket consumption | 798 | Product taxes | 265 |
| Public capital formation | 60 | Other production taxes | 155 |
| Enterprise capital formation | 342 | Gross operating surplus | 300 |
| Exports less imports | 0 | — | — |
| Total | 3,000 | Total | 3,000 |

Public service **cash** is 774: wages 400 plus purchases 374. Noncash public
depreciation 24 makes nonmarket consumption 798; it is not another current
cash bill. Private wages 1,880 plus public wages 400 are the same 2,280
cohort wages. Public/private depreciation totals 120; private enterprise
gross surplus 276 less depreciation 96 gives taxable profit **180**, not
the residual 720 obtained by subtracting wages alone from GDP.
The old transfer study did not claim this production closure.

Other production taxes comprise land 100, environmental 15 and business
40 in this fixture. Product tax 265 is charged on the chosen tax-inclusive
consumption. Actual compensatory relief 40 is a separately paid transfer
and credit against needs, not a second subtraction from both revenue and
expense. Corporate tax at the baseline 30% is 54. Half of after-tax profit
is distributed: 63, with assumed valid opening public share 25% yielding
15.75 and household share 47.25. Household capital receipts and domestic
public interest are separately charged; received insurance 45 keeps its
own entitlement and reserved contribution account.

|---|---:|---|
| Households H | +819.85 | Planned accrual balance, including obligations that may remain unpaid; not actual savings available to lend |
| Enterprises F | −183 | Investment needs finance beyond current retained earnings and depreciation |
| General government G | −831.85 | Before borrowing, cash draw and unpaid liabilities |
| Restricted insurance S | +188 | Current retained funds with future liabilities still unpriced; not general floor cash |
| Rest of world R | +6.5 | Matching foreign government interest after equal export/import values |
| Sum | 0 | Every transaction has two counterparties |

At most 60 new borrowing and 50 cash draw are assumed available, leaving
721.85 unfunded. This differs from design-58's 764.7 partial gap because
the new fixture changes tax bases, ownership receipts, earned-pension
classification and paid relief together. The difference is not observed
progress, a correction of immutable figures or proof of a feasible reform.
Actual extra function costs and legal/control/readiness remain unknown.

Consolidating a specified government/enterprise envelope cancels matching
taxes, dividends, purchases and other internal flows. It does not create
a second public revenue stream. Actual institutional control determines
the appropriate envelope; the model's 25% or 60% alone does not classify
real firms. Neither a balanced GDP identity nor planned sector balance
establishes that people received protection or that lenders agreed to finance it.
