# I25–I29. Keep income protection stable when the pay calendar shifts

*Design263 · Proposed personal income and first-budget duties.*

**I25 — Record three distinct facts about each material wage.** For
[I05 and I07](../06-income-floor-and-safe-resources.md) keep the
contractual pay date and wage scope, actual safe usable receipt,
and benefit period to which the received net amount is attributed.
The benefit authority obtains the minimum usable evidence of the
amount, cadence, change and any duplicate. Payroll transmission,
bank availability and the worker's legal claim are distinct.
Provide safe alternative proof and correction. No universal account
history or employer confirmation as the sole proof is required.
A receipt has one ordinary income assignment, with corrections
linked to the original item.

**I26 — Match a verified early monthly payment to its ordinary period.**
When a scheduled regular monthly wage is paid early solely because
of an established non-working day, banking closure or the resulting
normal payroll convention, keep it in the assessment period of its
ordinary scheduled pay date. The authority verifies actual usable
receipt, the pre-existing cadence, the amount and the absence of a
second allocation. Correct the two affected awards together, retaining
one personal disregard per assessment period. The person may dispute
a classification without asking the employer to change the payment.
A bonus, overtime, genuine arrears, contractual pay change or a
separate earnings source uses its own actual-payment treatment.
Split mixed packets where evidence permits. Unresolved classification
gets a timely human decision and protected interim handling.
Regular weekly, fortnightly and four-weekly payments remain real
separate earnings. Any prospective cadence-based rule for them must
prove actual usability, be enacted explicitly and preserve I07 and E06.

**I27 — Respond directly when expected money does not arrive.** A
contracted pay date, report or past average supplies no usable cash.
An advance determination may use the reasonable I07 forecast, with
an explanation and a direct adjustment when usable pay changes.
An expected payment that becomes unpaid, late or blocked triggers
the original [E06–E08](../../C06/19/02-income-and-provisional-protection.md)
useful-time review of actual resources. Make a same-period corrected
award or deliver a safe provisional bridge by the applicable deadline.
A genuinely late payment enters the appropriate actual receipt period
when safely usable under its source law. Do not shift late cash
backward to manufacture access at an earlier date or mechanically
charge back timely support. Later recovery requires its own lawful
ground, notice and protected-floor review. Lawful wage replacement
and employer recovery keep their distinct routes. In the hardship
assessment, establish actually usable resources for the stated need
and days, net of prior matching use. Ordinary attribution alone
does not show a person can pay a bill.

**I28 — Budget the actual extra payment on its actual date.** The
first budget and later changes compare a raw receipt rule, narrow
attribution and a genuine less assessment-dependent alternative
over a full seasonal cycle and a verified missed-pay interval.
Report individual award totals, dated payments, upfront cash,
subsequent changes, nonreceipt, assessed need, staff and privacy
cost, and tax and earned-credit effects separately. Do not assume
that timing corrections balance over two months or that later
reductions fund today's support. Budget the early liquidity,
non-offsetting costs and actual correction/receipt staff under
[JC](../../C06/65-joint-continuity-and-first-budget.md). Do not
change the general taper or asset rule by changing the calendar.

**I29 — Keep support through dispute, correction and succession.**
Give the person an inspectable short account of the wage's expected
date, actual receipt, ordinary assessment assignment, paid award and
earliest independent challenge. Use I08, E and TU for useful-time
relief and actual receiving. Preserve original awards, wage and
earned-credit claims, safe evidence and independent execution
through a source or provider change. A changed formula needs
prospective law, a paid overlap and original date protection.
AI may match potential calendar shifts and flag missing or duplicate
evidence. A responsible officer owns a disputed attribution,
and people can correct or access support offline. Reopen after a
missed payroll, repeated irregular gaps, administrative exclusion,
unsafe recovery or a simpler funded rule that protects the same
people and needs.
