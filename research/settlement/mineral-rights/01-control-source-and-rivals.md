# Mineral control, finite receipts and burdens that survive closure

Design167 · [Canonical NR](../../country/C09/15-finite-resources-and-mineral-rights.md)

The country previously regulated land/water, public acquisition, pollution,
capital/work and tax but did not select the mineral-right/award/depletion chain.
A clean-energy demand or unknown geological stock is no mine, and a resource
boom is not the chosen service-first economic foundation. NR fills that positive
choice while leaving the size/quality/source of any actual sector unknown.

| Actual proposed path | What cannot be inferred |
|---|---|
| Original title/collective evidence → competent protected source finding | A new discovery or incomplete registry proves no absence of existing owner/collective right |
| Elected depletion programme/rights/ecological scope → finite public right | A grant price does not authorize surface entry, displacement, water take or a prohibited habitat loss |
| Qualified C04 public-owner award → independent title-neutral TD11 site/phase permission | Public/private/cooperative label or a successful bid proves no safety/closure capacity |
| Already incurred+new phase exposure → actual legally available TS11 security | New expansion cannot leave the old tail unfunded, seize pledged lender assets or count one guarantee twice |
| Actual closure → qualified independent acceptance/receiver and proportional release | Licence expiry/shell transfer does not discharge damage, worker or long-tail claims |
| Actual owner cash/tax/security → itemized separate public accounts | A geological valuation, prospective export, tax invoice or restoration bond is no received unrestricted source |
| Net finite-owner cash → existing actual NP/source core → remaining replacement capital | Future inheritance cannot veto actual prior useful-time duties; not every ordinary invoice is a mineral cost/core claim |
| Explicit full-N budget/Act further current use → foregone replacement disclosed | An earmark creates no wealth, generic tax, automatic dividend, permanent mineral welfare or autonomous fund |

An old unsecured closure exposure10 plus genuinely new phase2 requires actual
lawful security12 before that new approval under NR10/15. These are authored
units, not a mine quotation or retroactive priority. A prior vested grant/
renewal must receive its proper lawful revision, not relabelling. Old liability
is neither cancelled nor magically funded if enlargement is refused.

A state operator is held to actual source/security and outside safety/review;
common selectors/Treasury/keys can still capture the chain. Existing courts,
AA and PO/OJ actual roles hear lawful exclusion/retaliation/fraud/obstruction,
with fair fault proof. No new mining police/Director/committee or guilt by
complaint. A public price/share is not compensation for lost bodily/collective
rights, and a local leader cannot contractually consent for everyone.

| Real whole arrangement | What changes / real burden |
|---|---|
| Inherited private mineral title + regulation/tax | More continuity/decentralized initiative, fewer acquisitions; concentrated windfalls and weaker public depletion control remain |
| Selected common public title + finite plural grants | Elected finite-stock choice/replaceable operation/visible consideration; public patronage, valuation/award and source enforcement still cost |
| Direct public production | More operational control/possible retained return; actual capital/qualified work/loss and owner-regulator conflict, no free public assets |
| No extraction + repair/reuse/trade | Avoids some local irreversible loss/tails; actual input replacement/imports, foreign harms, ownedFX and forgone useful output remain |
| Ordinary voted current use of owner receipts | Less earmark rigidity/more present room; stock depletion/volatility and future claims remain, no permanent income from a finite sale |

Initial37 coverage/financial rows remain intact and unpriced for this new
mineral scope. Actual geology/title/access/cost, technical/clinical/source
receivers, work/lab/custody/price/FX, restoration and public-tail/old claims must
be reconciled once across all country duties. A mine's potential rent cannot
fill an unknown national deficit. Existing SG I05/services/earned preference
continues; the NS15 stale U-destination wording is corrected, not reselected.

The review also checked ordinary shock protection: I07 current usable income,
I02 basket-price update, Y06 unemployment credit and RF/MC already exist. A new
unemployment earnings bridge is a separate real policy choice, not smuggled
into mineral law or assumed absent basic protection. Next whole-country largest-
gap review, not another automatic mine/royalty/instructor case series. No actual
participation, constitutional adoption, complete source or outcome proof follows.
