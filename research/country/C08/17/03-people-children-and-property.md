# Personal status, children and property across boundaries

*B45–B46; part of [C08/17](../17-jurisdiction-and-legal-cooperation.md).*

**B45 — Continuity without importing coercion.** Use actual habitual residence
at the relevant act/status decision as the ordinary personal-law connection,
with formation law and acquired lawful status considered separately. Adults
do not lose legal capacity at a border. Recognizing a birth, relationship or
child does not automatically enforce every foreign guardianship, property,
tax or medical consequence. No family form or foreign nationality alone
justifies refusal of a protected identity, maintenance or service claim.

A foreign adult guardianship cannot override B21/B23 equal capacity; give
supported direct access and independently assess its claimed act. A child
marriage record cannot compel marriage, sex or domestic adult status, while
the child's identity, safe exit, support and other valid claims remain.
Different same-sex/plural/customary status alone is no fundamental-rights
exception. Apply actual rights/procedure and protect severable lawful effects;
neither wholesale erasure nor unexamined database import is the default.

Child care/protection ordinarily belongs with the child's actual habitual
residence and a court able to hear their circumstances. Actual presence
permits minimum urgent lawful protection. Wrongful removal cannot alone
manufacture a preferred permanent forum; seek the prior competent court's
safe lawful cooperation while independently examining present grave risk,
child voice and effective remedies. Do not order irreversible return or
family separation before a credible protection risk can be heard. This
does not presume which foreign order or treaty governs a real case.

Parentage, adoption, support and estate recognition need their own B28–B40
grounds, original authority and fair review, not a generic commercial judgment
stamp. [RP11](../20/06-cross-border-and-delivery.md) now proposes donor/gestational
child-status continuity with severable recognition and actual authority checks;
foreign/adoption law and actual source/treaty implementation remain separate.
Keep safe provisional care, identity, food and payment during any conflict.
Record which effects are recognized/refused and who owes the unmet duty.

**B46 — Situs, estates and unfamiliar assets.** Rights in land/immovable
property and their registers ordinarily follow the lawful situs. A domestic
court cannot rewrite a foreign land register by decree. It may determine
personal obligations within actual jurisdiction, with foreign effect and
innocent title interests separately examined. B38 official-certification
risk remains prospective and local to the responsible public authority;
recognizing a foreign document does not guarantee its title/value.

For the movable estate, default to the deceased's last actual habitual
residence law; an authentic will may choose the law of an actual nationality
held at the choice or death. Situs/creditor/dependant mandatory rules,
public transfer tax and limits on disposing of someone else's share remain.
Keep the estate, Y survivor pot and actual received I/S resources distinct.
Concurrent administrators reconcile assets, debts and receipts once; no
paper residence or extra agent creates another distributable estate.

For contractual digital assets, securities or intellectual-property rights,
identify the underlying right, actual issuer/custodian, governing instrument
and applicable statutory regime. A token address or last synchronized copy
does not settle ownership, location or authority. Preserve unfamiliar
semantics, practical control and protected claims for independent classification;
do not silently discard them or treat every token as land/money. Interim
preservation has separate grounds and cannot decide an unresolved property class.

[KR01–KR32](../../C11/07-knowledge-rights-and-innovation.md) now supplies the
proposed domestic intangible-right categories; this conflicts rule does not
create foreign title or make a domestic patent/exception effective abroad.

For exclusive IP, validity/scope/term/exceptions and infringement follow the law
of the territory for which protection is claimed, segmenting multi-territory
acts; general direct-injury location does not select worldwide IP law. Forum,
proved foreign rights and executable reach remain B41; contracts/royalties
remain B43 and connected secrecy/unfair competition B43/B44.

[PT01–PT24](../26-property-transfers-and-secured-credit.md) supplies domestic
ordinary title/transfer/security rules only where this B forum/law rule actually
selects them. It is no global perfection passport: a foreign situs, changed
asset location, mandatory registry or claimant needs actual applicable source
and lawful migration. A domestic notice/time cannot silently prime a foreign
holder or create bearer title to unfamiliar tokens.
