# Design416. Give elected government effective control and preserve impartial execution

## Decision and baseline

Design415 requires open competition, qualified shortlists, expiring acting
appointments, candid advice and execution of lawful decisions. It protects
career workers against a partisan purge. The operative gap is a change in
control of the work without a formal dismissal, and the corresponding
risk that a manager can defeat a lawful successor by withholding delivery.
This revision proposes an ordinary national career-service law within
existing constitutional boundaries. The legislature decides adoption and
funding. The record was prepared with AI assistance.

## Alternatives and reasons

Agency-led self-enforcement is low cost where appointments, staff
assignments and access are already contestable in time. It gives the
same leadership control of several ways to bypass the safeguards.
A central merit commission with authority over all selection and
reassignment can enforce a uniform standard. It also concentrates
appointment and delay powers across services and can displace lawful
local, professional and elected choice. The working selection retains
agency hiring and lawful policy direction. It uses a common independent
roster for senior operational selection and joined scrutiny of material
changes to control of statutory duties. It provides distinct paths for
employee, applicant, resident and elected-government claims.

The objections are real. A cross-agency roster can become a closed
profession, an Assembly minority can withhold replacement, and broadly
triggered review could paralyze necessary staffing changes. The safeguard
applies to senior operational appointments and material transfers of
control over statutory duties. Fund qualified lawful temporary
coverage, use transparent rotation and review conflicts. Ordinary
management continues under existing authority. Review requires an
actual claim, carries no automatic stay and targets the affected act.
The candidate voting thresholds, terms and notice period need independent
capacity tests. A simpler decentralized mechanism may prevail if it
can reliably protect equal access and execute lawful directions.

## Evidence, migration and public control

[Primary comparators](sources.md) establish legal examples of merit,
neutrality, candid advice and independent appointment investigation.
They do not establish the efficacy of this selection or the case
staffing ratios. The constructed example shows why a new mandate,
a valid claim and actual capacity must stay on the same calendar.
No affected staff, applicants, residents or independently mandated
reviewer participated in this revision.

Keep existing employment, accrued claims and valid appointments.
Apply the proposed selection and material-change rules prospectively
at lawful commencement. Existing sector appointments and individual
decision powers remain with their own holders. Before applying the
roster, the Assembly must establish an appointment and removal route,
vote separate funds, and provide independent custody, review and
execution. Ministers remain replaceable and answerable for directions.
Protected staff must act on lawful instructions. An assessor or manager
cannot extend their mandate through a vacancy, queue or unavailable file.

## Bounded verification

Documentary evidence and the constructed comparison support a research
choice only. Enactment, adequate staffing, genuine review independence
and observed resident outcomes remain open.

For one real department, a lawful employer and independent reviewers
would test a senior appointment with a conflict, a reassignment affecting
due claims, and a lawful incoming direction that management contests.
Complete when they can produce dated notices, qualified panel and
substitute assignments, independent access to records, a paid review
and two observed service deadlines, including disposition of any failure.
No departmental sponsor or exercise budget has been established.
Reopen on a shared appointment bottleneck, a review queue that misses
useful dates, failed enforcement or credible evidence of a less
burdensome way to preserve both lawful direction and impartial service.
