*Part of [C05/14](../14-ordinary-life-and-household-production.md); design 124.*

more separate-home reference, voluntary shared/community homes, paid new housing,
repair/retained stock and private/cooperative/public provider choices as genuine
alternatives. Fewer homes can change service demand and source, but crowding,

statistical estimates are not project participants or consent. No new review
procedure, sovereign AI, household identity database or complete-field requirement
for ordinary problem reports is adopted. Protect safe source/private correction.

records/services/workforce/foreign/title and contracts. Any competent substantive
reform must state its own authorized dates, affected consumers, paid transition
and remedies; a hash/index/simulation or technical rollback cannot enact it.
