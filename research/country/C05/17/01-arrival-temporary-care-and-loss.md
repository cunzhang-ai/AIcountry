# CL01–CL06. Personal time is not owned by an employer or household head

**CL01 — Separate purposes and actual coverage.** Covered TW/WL workers have
a personal right to qualifying absence; genuinely independent workers have
portable public income for actual covered labor loss, no fictitious employer
or reinstatement. Use the competent national protection/work source, preserving
MB and actual foreign coordination rather than claiming all worldwide earnings.
No marriage, gender, citizenship-of-the-child, co-residence, minimum employer
service or ideological test. Actual lawful care and work/loss still need proof.
People outside paid work retain I05, services, Y06 qualification and personal
agency, not invented lost wages. Pregnancy/birth/loss recovery retains TW11 and
clinical/RP grounds independently of live birth, parentage or surrender of a child.

**CL02 — A concrete child-arrival offer.** Select52 person-normal-week
equivalents of job-protected arrival leave for each child's birth or actual
lawful adoption/long-term foster or kin-care arrival before18. Actual supported
caregiving and the child's lawful safe arrangement govern; pending papers need
no invented custody transfer. Genetics, a paid intention, donation or naming a
parent does not alone create a claim. Ordinary moves, registration/account
changes or a new nominal carer do not renew the pool. A genuinely new lawful
long-term placement is a distinct arrival episode, with actual reasons and
continued child safeguards; no compulsory adoption or purchased parental right.

**CL03 — Equal initial shares, meaningful personal reservation.** The actual
holders are persons with continuing parental or lawfully assigned long-term
day-to-day care responsibility under actual RP/B child arrangements, including
safe provisional placement; not every assisting relative, friend or respite
helper. Leave administration records that source with minimal protected proof;
it cannot create parentage, custody or a household licence. Actual holders
without current paid work retain the same reservation for possible later work
within the window, but cannot claim cash or consume work-loss weeks merely by
caring while not employed. This can leave some reserved weeks unused; it is an
explicit individual-time tradeoff, not an adequate-total-childcare guarantee.

At actual arrival divide52 equally among n such holders; reserve `min(16,52/n)`
weeks of each initial share personally. Only the balance may be voluntarily
transferred to another actual holder. Two holders get26 each/16 reserved each/
20 transferable total; three get17⅓ each/16 reserved each/four transferable.
An actual sole holder gets all52. Employer, first filer, income, gender,
account or replicated agent cannot choose n or own another's time. A person
may decline care/leave without losing ordinary rights; voluntary withdrawal,
nonapplication or higher salary does not release their personal reservation.

Temporary incapacity/absence permits only lawful transfer/use of transferable
uncommitted time; the unavailable person's personal reservation remains for
recovery. Only death, actual loss of the lawful ongoing care role, or substantiated
absence/incapacity/safety exclusion expected throughout the remaining window
allows release of that person's unspent reservation to the actual remaining available
holder(s), equally unless they genuinely agree another allocation. No first-filer
priority. Preserve other valid used/committed time and use protected provisional
proof/reasons with direct independent challenge; no abuser signature/contact.
An employer's refusal or ordinary convenience is not such unavailability.
If circumstances later change, exceptionally released time already lawfully used
is not restored as a new pool or clawed back from an innocent carer; state the
remaining balance and individual loss openly, with actual existing remedy rights.
New real holders share only remaining transferable/uncommitted time prospectively;
no retrospective dilution of protected reservations or renewal of52. Unused
personal reservations ordinarily expire; restoration under CL04 concerns only
actual still-owned lost opportunity, not weeks already lawfully consumed elsewhere.

**CL04 — Count people, lost schedules and the actual window.** Use arrival
leave within24calendar months of the actual arrival. A person's full combined
usual covered schedule lost in a calendar week uses one person-normal-week;
partial leave uses lost normal hours divided by that person's combined usual
normal hours, at most one week. Concurrent carers each use their own actual
fraction, not one shared child-week. One person's hour can be allocated to only
one child/episode; twins have distinct pools but no duplicated pay or time.
Different part-time weeks are not equal actual care/task hours. Record actual
hours/dates for source and qualified cover. No cash-out of unused leave.

Actual medical impossibility or unlawful employer/administrative obstruction
preserves the specifically lost opportunity through reasoned extension/restoration
and independent remedy, not automatic forfeiture or a new repeatable bank.
Ordinary voluntary nonuse does not extend the window. Services remain available;
leave is no surveillance requirement or continuous24hour personal-care pledge.

**CL05 — Temporary chosen-person care.** Select up to six person-normal-week
equivalents per person across all jobs/recipients in any rolling52weeks for an
actual temporary serious health/functional/care disruption or necessary support
near life's end. Include a sick child, chosen friend/relative and actual urgent
provider failure where support is willingly undertaken; no marital/tax-dependant
or common-home test. Normal companionship alone is not an earnings-loss claim.
For one continuing episode, use ends26calendar weeks after first CL use; pauses,
renamed diagnoses or a new account do not restart it. A genuinely distinct new
event needs actual changed facts; the rolling six-week limit still applies.
Use the first three scheduled days on protected self-certification; necessary
longer proof confirms functional need/role without giving the employer a diagnosis.
Caps are contestable choices; chronic/enduring needs keep S/AS/clinical/I05 and
accommodation rights, not a duty to exhaust unpaid relatives after week six.

**CL06 — Loss needs a humane handoff.** Actual child death or breakdown/end
of a genuine lawful arrival placement gives each directly affected actual care
holder up to two normal weeks of protected compassionate absence and CL income
for actual work loss. Pregnancy loss expressly covers the gestating person and
actual intended lawful arrival-care participant(s), even before any live arrival
or holder registration; genetics, paid intention or an unconsented claim alone
is insufficient. No clinical diagnosis is needed for ordinary grief.

Ordinarily start within14calendar days and finish within42calendar days of the
event. Actual medical inability or unlawful obstruction suspends the affected
start/use window for the proved period, with reasoned safe review; no token-hour
start or double charging medical leave is necessary. Other voluntary nonuse does
not create an indefinite bank. This separate bounded event allowance resets
neither52 arrival nor six temporary weeks; one event/person counts once.
Actual TW/clinical/urgent protection can continue independently. Stop future
arrival claims whose actual care basis ended, preserve due old cash/rights and
give safe return/support; no instant work-order, automatic custody judgment or
contact with an unsafe person. Later correction retains fair reply, subsistence-
safe recovery and actual unreceived-cash distinctions.
