# T55–T60. Make opening obligations payable before releasing a reform

13 November 2026 · Integrated design 435 · Candidate first-budget rules

**T55 — Fix a legal opening and reporting perimeter.** Name the
jurisdiction, opening date, legal continuity and every material
public payer. Map opening debt, guarantees, contract and remedy
claims, restricted balances and separate local, social-insurance
and enterprise funds. Trace any disputed successor liability under
[OL01–OL05](../02/05-opening-liabilities-and-protected-budget.md).
A comparator may establish observed exposures within its own
perimeter. It supplies no legal successor or national funding.

**T56 — Keep the forecast, gross schedule and cash bridge distinct.**
Keep the forecast known when the programme is considered, each
material revision and the later outturn. At each due date record
currency, owner, restrictions, principal, interest, collateral
and qualifying settlement. Retain distinct gross obligations and
net financing figures. Do not spend an expected auction, a gross
account balance belonging partly to other purposes, or the central
bank's reserves. Preserve source revisions, rounding and
unexplained residuals with their effects on qualification.

**T57 — Carry the first 90 days and the second-year tail.**
Under [WF90–WF94](../../C06/58/21-national-cash-and-first-budget.md),
match dated payment windows to protected operations, elections,
court orders, ordinary staffing and independent review. Include
fixed or contingent later liabilities and operating needs through
the second year. Separate available cash, lawful authority and
actual receiving. Where evidence stops at a month end, keep the
intramonth minimum and permitted-use breakdown unknown. Assign
material gaps to the responsible custodian and reviewer.

**T58 — Choose between three actual start paths.** Use the same
continuing-law baseline for retain and repair, phased reform and
accelerated reform. Compare a normal financing calendar, delayed
or smaller refinancing and interruption of a payment route.
Identify the exact activity, necessary overlap, first binding
commitment and subsequent independently staffed receiving.
Price any authorized prefinancing or reserve including the cost
of keeping it available. Where early work is independently useful
and funded, allow it without committing a later release. An
optional release requires a qualified same-date source, cover
and elected authority. Keep already due duties and remedies
on their legal dates and make any shortfall visible.

**T59 — Give the elected budget a useful-time correction.** Treasury
and debt managers reconcile opening positions and actual
settlement. Sector and local owners verify lawful availability
and receiving. Independent budget analysis and affected people
can challenge material omissions and hidden transfers before
an optional irreversible start. The legislature chooses extra
authority, a dated bridge or an exact narrower option. Apply
ordinary urgent remedy to endangered due duties. No model,
cash scheduler or forecast grants an appropriation, changes
an effective date or chooses whose lawful claim goes unpaid.

**T60 — Preserve continuity and report the limit of the finding.**
Carry claims, asset and debt terms, workers, due dates, open
appeals and independent routes through political or technical
handover. Report historical observation, design selection, legal
authorization, funded readiness and received results separately.
Until material jurisdictions, restricted cash and obligations
are reconciled, keep the whole budget unqualified. Reopen on
a material revision, mismatched settlement, uncovered duty,
changed scope or a stronger same-baseline alternative.
