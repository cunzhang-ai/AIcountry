# Pay for the lawful chain and preserve duties through change

*CD15–CD16; part of [C08/21](../21-conflict-detention-and-release.md).*

**CD15 — Capacity is a prior public obligation, not a detainee resource.**
[C06/26](../../C06/26-conflict-custody-and-release-capacity.md) attributes
qualified duty judges, independent counsel/interpreters, clinicians, humane
places, records, neutral visits, transport, safe release and actual remedies
by place/shift. [C10/08](../../C10/08-conflict-custody-and-civilian-control.md)
assigns command/legislative handover. The same judge, medic, reservist, custody
system or safe bed cannot cover simultaneous civilian/military duties twice.
No forced prisoner work, future indemnity, foreign cost recovery, confiscation
or predicted AI saving is current finance or accepted independent capacity.

Before any planned operation depending on custody, show actual lawful grounds,
protected 24-hour access, status/review coverage, humane conditions, original
records, alternate systems, accepted receiving and finance under an elected
mandate. Standing immediately necessary defense does not disappear because
capacity fails, but failure creates no permission to harm a surrendered person,
ignore a judicial limit or invent preventive power. Disclose the unmet duty,
seek immediate lawful protection/review and correct actual capacity. Do not
call a mission/country ready because a template or clock check passes.

Actual treaty/custom scope, foreign force/territory consent, military roles,
neutrality, transport and safe sites are unknown. New 24/seven/30-day choices,
clear-and-convincing standard and discipline limit are normative hypotheses.
The clock structure supplies no evidence of a staffed judge, timely hearing,
accepted visitor or received release, and this is no full law-of-war code.

**CD16 — Change a method without losing people or authority.** Before lawful
commencement map each actual detainee, first restraint, age, reason, applicable
conflict/treaty, pending review/case, remaining term, injury, family/source
record, money and receiver to competent successors. Review old constraints
individually; no automatic collective release/transfer, lost claim, revived
prosecution, new retrospective penalty or lawful leniency denied by software.
A new domestic restriction is not proof that old international obligations
ceased. Actual conflicts need qualified lawful reconciliation/adoption.

Mission/emergency mandate expiry, election defeat, contractor failure or
territorial change never erases ongoing protection/remedy; nor may a commander
use a captive population to renew a foreign campaign. Transfer continuing
lawful custody/functions through a separately authorized actual successor
with rights, resources and independent evidence. Failed handover retains
unfulfilled duties, not a self-extending incumbent mandate.

Future communication/media, remote adjudication and delegated tools need
actual meaning, source uncertainty, private advice and effective challenge.
Agent replication or several matching risk outputs multiply neither the
mandate nor independent review. Inform people of changed methods and preserve
applicable protections; unfamiliar status/needs cannot silently be omitted.

This finishes a bounded national conflict-custody candidate. [TS/TD](../22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md)
now supplies scoped technical law; next audit the full country: law,
shared material/cash/people, ordinary life, democracy and competing models.
Reopen on actual source-law conflict, hidden custody, late remedy, capture,
unsafe receiving, impossible operational burden or a better rights/security
model. More clauses or detention statistics are not proof of better lives.
