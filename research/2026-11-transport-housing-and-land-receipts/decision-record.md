# Design316 decision on transport, housing and land receipts

## Baseline and requested choice

Pin Design315, IV13–IV22, the national investment and budget rules,
H and LA, LU06–LU10 and the working method. Choose how a material
project must carry resident access and finance when land value,
rent or tenure changes. The project assistant authors the proposal.
Elected authorities retain project, source and land decisions.

## Evidence, rivals and objections

London and Copenhagen show distinct statutory or ownership sources
and liabilities. The observations do not establish actual household
effects. The constructed comparison isolates two possible failures,
planned routes becoming unaffordable and receipts arriving too late.
Other housing market and service changes can produce the same
effects and require independent evidence. Compare ordinary repair,
a smaller coordinated offer, a larger protected extension, and
retained public land or differently financed development. A levy
can shift costs to tenants, workers and other places. Retention of
public land may sacrifice cash, transfer risk or preserve affordability.
Additional evidence and remedy costs can slow an otherwise useful
project. Avoid a broad new process for routine works.

## Selection, migration and reopening

Select IV23–IV27 for material choices. Measure original residents'
usable housing and access, finance protections before exposure
where lawful, and reconcile actual money, asset disposal, borrowing
and ongoing duties separately. In the authored case the funded
repair, feeder and housing route fits. Retain the stronger
extension as a real financed alternative. No new tax, taking,
entitlement to a particular address, or transfer of powers follows.
Keep applicable prior laws, tenure, compensation, accrued contracts,
records and funded independent receiving through adoption.

Price a site and useful journeys with a real local housing payer,
a transport operator, current occupiers and an independent budget.
Test lower or later receipts before commitment and observed housing
and travel later. Reopen on exclusion, unpriced loss, failed
receipt, new funding or a better whole offer. Actual participation,
authority, paid readiness and improved lives remain open.
