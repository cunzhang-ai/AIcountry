# C09. Prevent housing loss and deliver a safe home

*Design330 · HL01–HL27 · [C09 land and ecology](../C09-land-and-ecology.md) · Proposed research law.*

Keep a safe home where a person wants to stay and prevention can
work. When a person lacks safe accommodation, the local public
housing steward owes actual emergency receiving. Offer an ordinary
secure home with independent voluntary support where appropriate.
Permanent tenancy, immediate safety, income and treatment remain
distinct duties.
