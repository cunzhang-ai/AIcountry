# HG16–HG20. Keep delivery paid, public control real and change prospective

*Part of [public clinical service](../05-health-service-and-patient-control.md).* 

**HG16 — NCS owns the complete public receiving failure.** Network heads and
Purchasing/Access Director arrange actual safe interim/continuing treatment,
clinically suitable local/visiting/regional/external alternatives and HN paid
journey/advance/dependant support under actual source/consent/partner law.
Contractor failure/foreign refusal/technical shutdown never makes a referral,
queue entry, held payment or nominal alternate completed care. Separate clinical
error, coverage disagreement, source default and absent supply; preserve the
original due claim and useful-time direct review. Maintain trusted continuing
clinical relationships where safe/usable, not involuntary churn to meet a metric;
this gives no power to compel a particular clinician's indefinite employment. NCS cannot force a foreign
clinician, take private assets or promise that an unstaffed public backup exists.

**HG17 — One current source and qualified time account.** Fund actual public
and purchased clinical staff, preparation/learning/rest/leave, specialist/on-call
cover, reviewers/counsel/independent experts, management/records/privacy,
interpreters/non-AI help, laboratories/medicines/rights/maintenance/sites/power/
water/waste, transport/dependant cover, owned FX, capital/legacy and paid overlap.
An office name is not automatically a full-time person; share compatible support
with actual competence/time while separating final adverse-case judgments.
Do not count a reviewer as simultaneously treating patients, a driver as a care
reserve, an external contract plus its inclusive payroll, or clinical savings
as already-received source. All actual included/incremental quantities/prices,
net receipts/cash/foreign acceptance/simultaneous independent receiving remain
unknown. Six retired clinical quotes and HN's authored route are not current
adequate source prices. TW's earlier hours/rest/learning and stronger terms persist.

**HG18 — Democratic budgets do not authorize medical domination.** Elected
law/source and parliamentary/opposition/public/AA scrutiny remain; NCS and
review chiefs publish safe accounts of access, case mix, unmet needs, quality/
time/agency, source/complaint/correction and remaining claims through EV-compatible
original evidence/uncertainty, not a single score. Patients/workers/excluded
people have accessible protected input, own chosen representation/other workable
methods and reasoned response on consequential plans; no compulsory unpaid board,
public dossier or majority vote on an individual's treatment. Material binding
change uses actual law, valid evidence/dependencies and fresh review; a new AI
version or clinical management guideline cannot approve itself or become a new
mandatory sovereign rule. Direct human routes and continuing obligations survive.

**HG19 — Prospective organization, no clinical-time reset.** A coordinated
competent Act starts the new NCS employer/head/purchasing/review/order method on
first1January≥12calendar months after enactment, naming exact scope/date and
pending cases/records/worker/contracts/pensions/property/creditors/cash/foreign
and paid OP/T successors. Until lawful transfer, actual prior employers/sources
remain answerable; after it, named successors inherit specified old duties.
No automatic private nationalization, public corporate-debt guarantee or mass
reappointment/calendar reset. Existing S03 seven-day/earlier E safe-time, HN180,
ED/NC/TW/Y/RF/force/CP/election/court/source/stronger old dates/claims continue.
A missed enacted method/delivery date is non-delivery, no ministerial readiness
extension or cancellation; actual authority/acceptance is not made by this text.

**HG20 — Keep the organizing choice rejectable.** Select integrated ordinary
public operation with internal purchasing/access and outside clinical-rights
review, retaining actual qualified additional provision. Compare separated
purchaser/autonomous provider, local/worker/user-governed common networks,
insurance-led plural arrangements and strong retained repair with the same
care/consent/source/control/old claims. Coordination does not require merger;
one state employer may concentrate political/labor power and reduce exit.
Separate agencies may improve challenge yet add interfaces/budget disputes.
Our form/180-day acting/12-month method choices are hypotheses, not WHO mandates,
empirical optimums or proof of independent control. Full national affordability/
safe receiving/authority/outcomes remain unverified. Reopen on actual patient/
worker/affected experience, qualified source/clinical/control evidence, harmful
centralization/exclusion or a better whole alternative. Next integrated country
ordinary/adverse/paid operation and major-gap review, not an automatic specialty,
licensing-board or health-cost study tail; more prose is no better-life result.
