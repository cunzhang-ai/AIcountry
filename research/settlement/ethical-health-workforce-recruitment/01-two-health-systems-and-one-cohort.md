# Two health systems and one cohort

Constructed comparison in qualified shifts per window and incremental
cash units. The destination can staff eight of ten protected care
shifts. The origin can staff all nine of its protected shifts, with
no spare qualified cover. Two people already working there are
considering the destination's published jobs. Each remains free to
apply, decline, leave or move under applicable law. The exercise
compares effects of active programme choices. It does not assign
either person to an employer.

The budget has ten incremental cash units over two windows. Existing
destination payroll covers the ten ordinary shifts, including the two
vacancies. Six incremental units buy two qualified temporary return
shifts in window one, including the cost of readiness and paid cover.
The returning workers accept no later commitment. The other four
units can support a lawful origin agreement. On these stipulated
terms the origin can pay and retain two genuinely additional,
qualified shifts by window two, with continuing operation funded
there on its ordinary source. Required destination recruitment and
recognition work has an existing independent source for this
comparison. No unused money elsewhere is presumed.

| Choice | Window one | Window two |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active immediate recruitment without a source agreement, with both people accepting | Destination 10. Origin 7. Two origin shifts uncovered. | The origin gap persists if no independently funded replacement arrives. |
| Fund temporary domestic return only | Destination 10. Origin 9. | Destination 8 once the funded temporary work ends. Origin 9. Additional domestic retention remains a real option if it can be funded and accepted. |
| Fund domestic return and a staged joint programme | Destination 10. Origin 9. Cost 6 plus the separately appropriated 4 for origin preparation. | If two new origin shifts are verified in time and both workers freely accept and qualify at the destination, destination 10 and origin 9. |

Select the staged comparison on these conditions. If only one new
origin shift is in place, a release requiring two verified shifts
does not qualify. Narrow the next discretionary stage under actual
law or retain the gap and seek domestic or other qualified cover.
If both source shifts exist but only one person accepts a job,
destination receiving is nine, while origin receiving is ten if the
other person continues there. Use actual decisions. No hiring
commitment or person can be counted twice.

The four-unit appropriation may already be committed when a recruitment
stage fails. Its recovery and continuing origin support follow their
own terms. It is not a second source for local cover. If the first
window's return shifts cannot be staffed, the finance alone leaves
two destination shifts uncovered. Report the original gaps and actual
service and review routes. A bilateral signature establishes neither
safe staffing nor worker consent.
