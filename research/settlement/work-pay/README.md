## A real tax choice, not a relabeling

Protect ordinary U with one exclusive source-period exclusion; it cannot shield
unrelated wages during nonpayment. The candidate taxes other ordinary receipts
at20/35/45/55% over widths0.5M/0.5M/M/unbounded, starting at the first unit.
M initially equals independently measured W; its disclosed protection formula
can later differ from W. Rates are normative hypotheses, not a sourced launch.

Taxable gross U with an exactly matching exclusive offset produces the same net
result. An **additional12,000 personal-income allowance** is a genuinely different,
more generous rival, applied to other work, pension and capital income.
Retained I05 is another rival; older people retain Y basic
in that comparison, not zero ordinary old-age protection. At earned pension15,000,
removing that general allowance lowers the example's disposable resources by
2,400CU; this is a real loser, subject to prior net/source rights and migration.

With B13,248/housing6,000 and received wage12,000, the selected example pays
1,200 Y contribution,2,160 tax,U13,248 and residual3,004.80. The extra personal-income
allowance rival has no tax and residual1,924.80: tax recovery raises residual
needs by1,080, so2,160 gross tax is not2,160 net general savings. Employer,
price, pension and source effects are still additional. TW replacement12,000
bears tax but no worked-wage Y levy; net-business labor is not turnover.

Proportionate tax attribution across other work/pension/capital is selected;
work-first attribution can change residual awards despite identical total tax.
Food/cash options leave tax on identical private receipts unchanged. Unpaid U
is never received resources; provisional protection/arrears reconciliation and
