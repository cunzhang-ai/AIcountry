# Available sources, paid overlap and safe acceptance

*Candidate T05–T08; part of [C14 protected stages](../06-staged-capacity-and-protection.md).*

**T05 — Finance before commitment.** Treasury presents one dated national
schedule distinguishing lawful appropriation, actual available domestic cash,
restricted funds, loans with maturity/carry, voluntarily available foreign
currency, invoices and contingent liabilities. Each programme identifies its
included, incremental and overlapping share of baseline costs. Construction,
training wages, duplicate-service overlap, transition compensation, operations,
maintenance, replacement, inspection and independent remedies are priced or
left explicitly unknown. Current services and general claims remain in that
same account. Do not spend future growth, an unreceived grant, private saving,
restricted insurance or one reserve twice. A project can be funded while the
national income floor remains unfunded. Providers receive no new appropriation
or deficit power from an apparently balanced spreadsheet.

**T06 — Paid people with real displacement.** A worker is counted once by
specialty, place, qualification, shift and due task. Pay trainees and protect
voluntary entry, work rights and lawful exits; supervision, apprenticeships,
qualification and independent appointments take time and capacity. A paid
vacancy or online course is not a competent care shift or independent judge.
Reallocation reduces the source workforce and may reduce output or another
service; technology gains require evidence, not a residual productivity label.
Existing receivers remain staffed during handover. No compulsory unpaid public
work or private household care is assumed to close a staffing gap.

**T07 — Contracts and supplies.** The competent operator procures within
Assembly-authorized scope through the ordinary open, challengeable C04 route.
Disclose beneficial control, material dependencies, contract stages, cancellation
and defect liabilities, land/permit effects and source-specific foreign payments.
Imports are part of the invoice and are also traced in FX units; tracing them
is not a second domestic expense. A private export earning requires willing
conversion and a usable settlement path; it is not state property. Capacity
must remain usable under the declared adverse event. Water-saving does not
create catchment water; reused return flows, independent supplies, ecosystem
floors and downstream rights are reconciled once. Public ownership, regulated
plural operators and local cooperation remain substantive alternatives.

**T08 — Paid and tested acceptance.** Existing sector operators own delivery;
competent engineers/clinical authorities verify task-specific safety, distinct
inspection challenges their evidence, and Treasury reports paid obligations.
A documented compound receiving test includes actual users, independent
review and executed remedy before an operating claim. Partial acceptance
names its usable scope; a purchased asset is not automatically a safe service.
Source records and role-separated signatures need original evidence and
common-controller scrutiny, not another AI approval label. The Assembly can
choose a different lawful supply model; neither an engineer nor Treasury can
certify a political mandate. No actual receiving or independent capacity has
been demonstrated here.

[CP01–CP04](../../C06/28-national-commitments-and-residual-scarcity.md) now
corrects productive-account support and requires an express dated expansion
schedule. The old GVA residual/ratios are retired as current support; fixed
hashes or financial closure cannot qualify real supply or paid overlap.

[NP](../../C06/33-protection-production-and-national-expansion.md) now selects
protection-first actual receiving/independent capacity and progressive source
choices. Derive demand and usable supply under the same adverse scenario;
prior workforce, old preparation envelope and projected AI gain are not current
capacity/cash. Paid bridge, displaced tasks and later renewal stay in scope.

work, paid backfill and distinct independent acceptance.
