# Protect local services during fiscal failure without transferring government

*Design325 · LD14–LD18 · Proposed bounded statutory delivery authority.*

**LD14 — Identify the exact threatened common duty.** The national sector
authority may activate a scheduled service backstop when documented evidence
shows an imminent material failure of a statutory common service, the local
authority cannot or will not deliver the act by its original useful time,
and ordinary payment, correction or voluntary joint delivery cannot reach
that time. Use the least intrusive qualified route. Financial distress,
political disagreement, a weak forecast and a rejected recovery proposal
are insufficient grounds. Identify actual local receiving, the uncovered act,
its deadline, and residents affected. Give the local council, opposition and
affected residents prompt accessible notice and a direct review route.
When time permits, offer the council the evidenced option to deliver the
act itself within the original deadline.

**LD15 — Release national support for a named act only.** The adopting law
preassigns a national sector career officer, a conflict-checked substitute,
covered services, service thresholds, eligible providers, protected cash
and qualified capacity. The officer gives a reasoned written activation for
the task, residents, receiver, terms, date, ceiling, records and end.
The national payer finances its authorized share from the actual source.
Reconcile lawful local payments and common reserves once. Keep the
ordinary local route for all tasks it can still deliver. A council's
refusal to approve a separate national policy does not block the backstop.
The officer cannot exercise local taxing, borrowing, legislative, appointment
or election powers, assume unrelated contracts or redirect local assets.
Creditor adjustment, worker pay and unresolved reimbursements retain their
own rules. Neither a budget nor an order supplies an unqualified provider.

**LD16 — Review in time and end exceptional authority.** Where the first
useful deadline allows, notify and hear the council and affected people
before activation. Otherwise give immediate reasons, protect the ability
to contest and submit the urgent activation to independent ordinary court
review without waiting for a claimant. Give any affected person, worker,
council member or relevant provider direct urgent access. An urgent
activation expires within 72 elapsed hours unless the court has confirmed
its lawfulness and necessity after an opportunity to answer. The court
may narrow or stop it and enforce existing rights. Review receiving and
scope at least every seven calendar days. Every activation ends at
completion or 30 calendar days, whichever comes first, with one reasoned
court-confirmed extension of at most 30 calendar days for the same failure
within the enacted cash and authority. Use separate prior-law urgent care
and other ordinary duties if review, a source or receiving fails. No timer
creates funds, and a renamed task does not restart the exceptional period.
Independent review checks actual restoration and the continuing
cause of lost delivery. New recipients, contracts or batches
arising from the same unresolved failure keep the original
authorization clock. A separately established new failure retains
its actual scope and activation requirements. Mere proximity or
a common budget classification proves no common cause. Preserve
every individual's original deadline and ordinary protection.
Where a national payer contributed to the failure, seek independent
execution of any separately due ordinary transfer under MF35.
Protect urgent service on its own authority while attribution is
examined. Activation neither justifies the withholding nor changes
the council's entitlement to payment.

**LD17 — Fund the return and keep the original local decision-makers.**
Before release, record the conditions for local or lawful joint receiving:
named accepting authority, dated cash, paid workers, case and order access,
and the first confirmed delivery. The council remains elected and free to
choose a lawful recovery package and ordinary provider. Reconcile current
and next-cycle service before handback. The competent authorities must
bring any persistent gap to ordinary elected finance and service law
before expiry, with a dated proposal on a lawful sitting or
decision calendar. Compare a funded local repair, a voluntary
joint route and an ordinary sector arrangement where the
competent elected bodies expressly authorize it. Each shows
its costs, staffed receipt, independent review and exit.
Apply SNR02 and SNR07 to distinguish a repairable operator loss
from a persistent capacity gap. Own the next deadline without
silently renewing exceptional authority. Provider exit alone
does not trigger a national intervention in local government.
A missed disposition supplies no extension. An unready handback is an exposed duty with independent
remedy, not an extension of exceptional powers. Preserve live claims and
local elections, opposition access and funding, records and lawful payroll
through both activation and handback.

**LD18 — Report the remaining service and source gaps.** Publish safe
aggregate evidence of activation grounds, costs, paid shifts or other
received service, unserved people, shared constraints, expiry and handback.
Independent review can inspect protected originals. AI may reconcile
schedules and flag overlapping capacities. It cannot activate the backstop,
choose a provider or certify receipt. Adopt prospectively with costed
ordinary support, independent review and expiry staffing. Reopen on
partisan selection, serial activation, service displacement, failed return
or a simpler local route that meets the duty in time.
