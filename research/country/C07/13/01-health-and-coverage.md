# S01–S04: clinically needed care and an explicit public boundary

*Part of [C07 service candidate](../13-public-service-entitlements.md); design 67.*

**S01 — Essential care without a job or insurance gate.** People under the
country's jurisdiction have direct access to the essential public package,
without an employment, contribution, citizenship or completed-document test.
It includes assessment, prevention, diagnosis, necessary acute/chronic care,
mental-health/substance-use care, reproductive and maternal/newborn care,
rehabilitation, essential medicines, disability support and palliative relief,
with clinical continuity and safe transport where needed. Apply C08 consent,
liberty and reproductive rights; neither a diagnosis nor poverty authorizes
coercion. Public finance covers necessary use without a point-of-service
charge or debt condition. Planned cross-border purchase/export still needs
actual law/partners; no assumed foreign agreement funds treatment.

**S02 — A reasoned boundary, not hidden rationing.** The default covers care
within these categories with a justified clinical indication and reasonable
expected benefit, with uncertainty and patient preference disclosed.
An elected-law schedule defines material exclusions, experimental access,
new high-cost coverage and their evidence/opportunity-cost reasons. A
purchaser's secret list, vendor update or cost target cannot narrow it.
Clinical and affected expertise inform proposals; the Assembly decides
material general coverage under rights and independent challenge. Urgent
stabilization, due continuity and relief cannot be withheld while officials
debate a new general rule. No guarantee of every requested enhancement or
unproven intervention is inferred from the necessary-care standard.

Publish covered categories, clinical indication/formulary standards, reasons,
uncertainty, correction and safe aggregate distribution. Essential oral,
vision and hearing treatment belongs when necessary for health/function;
an aesthetic label cannot by itself decide the case. Apply equal standing
and individualized reasons rather than social worth, income, identity or
an AI prediction of future productivity. Independent clinical/court review
can correct discrimination/error; it does not enact a national tax budget.

**S03 — Useful time governs the complete path.** Provide a usable qualified
routine first assessment within seven calendar days of the protected request,
not merely an offer of a later appointment. A person may choose a later time
or decline without losing the standing claim; silence is not consent.
Urgent assessment/protection uses E's earlier safe-time rules. Assessment
records the necessary next step, receiving operator and safe deadline;
tests, referral and treatment do not restart the clock or become completed
care merely because an appointment exists. A clerical intake or unattended
AI assessment cannot substitute for required professional judgment.

**S04 — Continuity and fair scarcity.** Clinicians apply clinically relevant
urgency, expected benefit and patient will within prior law; equally placed
claims use a published fair tie process, not wealth or political influence.
Material new rationing criteria require elected law, not an emergency budget
instruction. A shortage remains an unmet claim with reasons, safe interim
care and direct review. The public network must fund an accepting substitute
on closure, drug failure or outage; a payment, referral or unavailable
medicine is not a received remedy. Existing distinct C07/C08 routes continue.

[UA](../../C08/23-necessary-care-and-unknown-will.md) now supplies the separately
proposed unknown-will legal source where no actual choice/authority settles an
act. S coverage itself gives no consent. Actual objection/refusal, clinical
safety, accepted receiving and resource duties remain independently necessary.

[HG01–HG20](../05-health-service-and-patient-control.md) now names the state NCS
public employer/Chief/network/internal purchasing and outside clinical review.
S01–S04 category/consent/boundary/seven-day/earlier-safe-time and actual receiving
duties above persist; no new contribution/insurance gate or deemed consent.

[CTC01–CTC07](../14/01-conditional-treatment-coverage-and-continuity.md)
qualifies an expressly authorized conditional offer under S02.
Existing assessment, treatment, ordinary continuity and fair
review remain on their own terms.
