# EL01–EL04: one person, the actual franchise and a usable correction

*Part of [C03/13](../13-equal-franchise-and-usable-elections.md); design111 candidate.*

**EL01 — Specify the date without narrowing the person.** Preserve C02/MB:
citizens including citizens abroad, and noncitizens with one continuous habitual
year or previously qualified actual return under [RFV](../../C02/03/01-resident-franchise-and-secure-status.md),
have the national vote and equal candidacy from18.
Local political eligibility needs six cumulative national habitual months and
one actual local electoral home; a municipal move does not restart that period.
Refugee/disputed-status/childhood/custody time follows MB's actual residence proof,
not employer, landlord, nationality-document or AI consent. A noncitizen who has
permanently left and lacks the protected resident/return connection has no invented
nonresident franchise. Ordinary conviction, custody, disability, diagnosis or
supported decision-making is no automatic political disqualification or competence
test. Existing exact lawful individual incompatibility remains separately reviewable.

For a called contest assess age/residence-duration at final close P for voter,
candidate, entry endorsement and called public credit alike. A known birthday/
vesting date by P can support early action; the coordinated Act expressly permits
that anticipatory electoral act, not general civil majority or a predicted future
citizenship/residence grant. Facts must be independently provable; no fabricated
future move. Actual death/status change after a lawfully accepted vote/endorsement/
credit does not retrospectively erase it; proved initial fraud/ineligibility has
its own review. Candidate refusal/death follows EL19, not the voter rule.

Annual PC07 credits and PC05 private donation eligibility remain actual-date tests,
not anticipatory P rights; no local-only donor money becomes national. This is an
explicit new called-date choice, not already controlling law. Earlier-called
contests keep their actual rules and useful-time protection under EL23.

**EL02 — Home is a real connection, not an account address.** One person may
choose one local electoral home among genuine habitual connections for a given
municipal contest. Property ownership, family headship, a workplace, temporary
hospital/prison location or platform account does not determine it. Use actual
life/source evidence, safe declarations and alternative proof; no public refuge
address. Homeless/mobile residents may identify the community where they actually
live through a safe contact route. Each adult chooses independently, not per household.

Internal forced displacement may preserve the previous lawful home or a chosen
new actual connection, once only; no compelled removal manufactures a new electorate.
This is an express protective local-franchise residence clarification. A voluntary
permanent new-home choice ends the former connection for that contest. A citizen
permanently abroad with no retained actual domestic home keeps the full national
ballot, but gets no fictitious local vote/annual local credit. Someone never
resident domestically needs no ancestor's address or artificial national territory.
An actually locally eligible nonnational resident keeps the local ballot alone.

Keep home updates possible until voting; ordinary pre-registration does not lock
one into a home. Fix the local connection on the first lawfully accepted ballot;
a later move creates no second vote. A candidate files actual local connection
by V−40 and must retain lawful local eligibility at P; safety absence is not an
automatic move. A speculative future move cannot qualify a new slate after cutoff.

**EL03 — Continuous source records, no possession of political identity.** EA02
career officers keep protected original eligibility, home, correction and unique
person evidence with independent lawful copies and a paper/assisted channel.
No single ID, biometric, commercial account, device or network is mandatory.
Give a free usable own-record/entitlement statement and reasons for missing or
conflicting items. Public data show aggregate rolls/corrections and exact count
rules; broad voter names, participation, addresses and preferences are not public.
A claimant/observer/court gets necessary protected answerable evidence, not a
universal voter graph. Source errors do not silently erase a lawful national or
local track or force disclosure to a hostile landlord or state.

**EL04 — Correction has a deadline, not a veto.** Acknowledge the first credible
request, provide one exact necessary defect/help notice within24elapsed hours
and a reasoned ordinary decision within five calendar days/earlier useful time.
No serial completeness reset or repeated demand for state-held proof. Direct
EA16/E13/B17 ordinary courts, assisted counsel/translation,24h urgent consideration
and actual independent source correction/execution remain available. A finance
allegation, missing commission quorum or contested provider cannot hold a due
registration/verified payment. Silence is not eligibility or a vote.
A timely eligibility dispute may use EL17's sealed conditional ballot; missing
proof stays unresolved, not an AI guess or blanket rejection of other lawful votes.
If remedy fails, expose the actual lost opportunity, source/capacity and claim;
no term extension, constitutional shortcut or declaration of equality by deadline.

**Design143 referendum scope.** This part's called-P anticipation is the ordinary
Assembly/local election reference. A9/RR06 constitutional and TS final status
ballots use actual voting-day eligibility under their already valid source law.
National initiative-opening and TS area first-presentation denominators retain
separate scopes; ordinary EL does not supply consent to a new franchise.
