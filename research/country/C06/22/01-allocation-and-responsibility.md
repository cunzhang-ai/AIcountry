# Allocate once, expose the missing requirement

*Part of [C06 country book](../22-country-operating-resource-book.md); design 68.*

Each component identifies purpose, period, economic type, people/assets,
currency/import share, responsible operator and allocation across functions.
Shares sum to one; they explain where a common resource is attributed,
not new spending or a transfer of sector authority. Genuine joint service
may meet several rights; incompatible tasks, shifts and independent reserves
cannot reuse the same capacity. A different classification alone changes
neither a person's claim nor the country's resources.

The current 37-function boundary is a research map, not a closed list of
human needs or a universal definition of a state. An unfamiliar duty or
social form must be preserved, assigned and examined, not discarded because
the book has no row. Actual cost, required quantity, funded bid, qualified
capacity, delivery and executed remedy have separate fields. Unknown does
not become zero through a table, model or unspent appropriation.

The candidate book deliberately reconstructs payroll instead of treating
the old 400/438.25 aggregates as complete. It assigns 4,000 higher-pay and
8,363 middle-pay full-year positions plus 32 two-month contracts, drawn from
the same 55,000-worker reference. Occupational qualifications, willingness,
prior work displaced and individual income continuity still need proof.
The reference includes a public-office remuneration allowance; it selects
no Assembly size, founding electorate or actual appointment.

Purchase bids contain medicine, care, learning, networks, courts, democratic
operation, evidence/AI and external responsibilities. They include the old
maintenance 16 and training-site maintenance 0.5 once. Public replacement
29.75 includes old-project 5.4 and training-site 0.35; adding those again
would double count. Private commercial costs, tariff incidence, local
equalization, public-enterprise duties and actual asset/control boundaries
need their own reconciliation. A G purchase is not automatically a second
household bill or proof of a particular operator's cost.

Every function receives a *proposed ordinary bid*. Its workload, legal
completeness, actual source and adequacy remain unverified. Founding,
disaster reconstruction, compensation, guarantees, inherited litigation,
future care/learning, renewal and other contingencies have unknown remaining
requirements; a preparedness allowance does not price their eventual loss.

Sector/local authorities prepare quantities and options; Treasury compiles
and reconciles; separately governed evidence custody preserves originals;
Assembly law/appropriation chooses the material burden. Courts, qualified
professionals, opposition and affected challenge retain their separate
powers. Follow appointments, funding, source/vendor control and executed
orders together. The budget compiler cannot become a service, political or
source-data super-office. An ordinary claimant need not supply this book
before receiving assistance, provisional protection or independent review.

Design 79 [CP](../28-national-commitments-and-residual-scarcity.md) preserves
these bids while withdrawing the erroneous market-GVA reporting. Actual RP/CD/TS
and residual-law increments remain unknown, not absorbed into an old share.
