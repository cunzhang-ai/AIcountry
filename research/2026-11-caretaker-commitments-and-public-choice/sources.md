  a [28 March 2025 notice](https://www.pmc.gov.au/news/federal-election-2025-caretaker-conventions-now-place)

  Its [background](https://www.pmc.gov.au/resources/guidance-caretaker-conventions/2-background-and-overview-caretaker-conventions),
  [major policy guidance](https://www.pmc.gov.au/resources/guidance-caretaker-conventions/3-major-policy-decisions)
  and [major contracts guidance](https://www.pmc.gov.au/resources/guidance-caretaker-conventions/5-major-contracts-or-undertakings)
  distinguish ordinary administration, the decision date and routes
  for a major commitment that cannot be deferred.
