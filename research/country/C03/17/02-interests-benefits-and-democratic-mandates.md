# PI06–PI10. Particular self-benefit is not cured by a declaration

**PI06 — Declare material interests before acting.** An officeholder declares
known material financial/control/outside-work interests and concrete employment
negotiations relevant to the public function before first participation; give
the competent records officer an initial declaration on entry, material updates
within14days and annual confirmation. Relevant connected-person interests mean
an actual economic/control connection, not every relative, belief or friendship.
Use necessary public descriptions/value bands and protected detail for review;
no home addresses, child files or compulsory private-life/asset graph. A broad
index fund/general resident interest differs from a particular controlled asset.
Actual relevance and omission need reasons, reply and review, not an AI risk label.

**PI07 — Public office is not a private paid service.** Decline/return personal
gifts, preferential loans, hospitality or other benefits offered because of or
in exchange for public authority/access. Source-record actual offers/disposition
with safe detail; minor refreshments on equal terms at an open working event,
ordinary private social gifts without that nexus, lawful public pay and necessary
official costs under actual public source are not private patronage by default.
No disguised family/controlled-entity channel cures an actual office benefit.

Do not agree to supply private clients with paid parliamentary/public-authority
advocacy, inside access or decision services, directly or through another person.
That prohibited undertaking is unenforceable after lawful commencement; ordinary
B restitution/actual liability retains proof, defenses and existing valid rights.
Genuine teaching/professional work, journalism, public policy discussion or union
membership is not banned merely because remunerated. An outside salary alone
proves no purchased official act; actual undertaking/control/benefit is required.
Public salary and authorized opposition/member support fund the public role.

**PI08 — Administrative conflicts need a real unconflicted decision.** A
particular own/connected contract, licence, grant or private appointment requires
reasoned case-specific disclosure and proportionate reassignment, recusal or
necessary divestment through the actual competent source. Preserve a qualified
lawful receiving officer, source and useful time; an absent successor is failure,
not authority for an interested proxy/AI to decide. Broad class-wide tax, wage,
tenancy, care or service interests do not automatically disqualify every worker,
parent, taxpayer or property holder. Procurement/court/other stronger actual
conflict safeguards continue. Declaration does not by itself resolve a conflict.

**PI09 — Preserve democratic mandates and identify particular benefits.**
Assembly/council legislative votes, government formation/confidence/replacement
and constitutionally assigned appointments/status decisions retain A2/A10/RR
and their actual procedures. No allegation, Clerk, auditor or committee can
suspend a member's vote, shrink fixed N/n, create a vacancy/substitute or turn
an integrity opinion into a no-confidence veto. Administrative private posts
are distinct from constitutional government formation. Members disclose actual
interests; the Assembly's lawful discipline/public censure and courts' actual
jurisdiction remain, with no new seat/vote disqualification selected here.

An exact proposal conferring a particular material benefit on an officeholder/
controlled or materially connected interest identifies beneficiary/control,
actual interest, purpose, alternatives, amount/source and claimed legal departure
before a separately identifiable recorded disposition within the proper vote.
An appropriation/recipient name is no implied entitlement, conflict waiver,
SC bypass or instruction to pay. DB implementation belongs to an actually
competent unconflicted officer; genuine due rights and source checks remain.
The required legislative vote is not replaced by a 'disinterested' smaller
majority, auditor certificate or referendum invented by this procedure.

**PI10 — Select a narrow constitutional constraint, not an ordinary-law fiction.**
The coordinated proposed A2 addition binds public acts including primary law:
particular financial/material advantage to an officeholder or controlled/
materially connected interest must have an actual public purpose, objective
justified terms and unconflicted implementation; no privilege because of office
or intervention. Genuinely general benefits, lawful general office remuneration,
compensation/rights and necessary public contracts are not banned by identity,
but must satisfy their actual lawful equal/public terms. Disclosure, a named
budget line or an express ordinary override cannot waive this constitutional
constraint. Formally general criteria tailored to a known connected beneficiary remain
particular in substance; a nominee/agent/trust label cannot cure proved continuing
material particular benefit/control. A merely declared public purpose without
answerable grounds is insufficient; courts review legality/particular privilege, not a preferred policy.

If an Act expressly grants an unconditional particular benefit, ordinary PI
alone cannot defeat that higher primary law. Existing CJ nationwide statutory
constitutional review, ordinary courts' material reference/actual case protection
and source remedies apply only under the legitimately adopted constitutional
rule and actual powers. No automatic invalidity, payment veto, guessed judgment
or new court. This is a proposed constitutional selection needing actual A9/RR
adoption/holds; it is not present law or closed restoration of a prior right.
