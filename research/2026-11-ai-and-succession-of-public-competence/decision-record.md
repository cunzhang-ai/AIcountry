# Design369 decision on AI and succession of public competence

## Baseline and selected change

Pin Design368, the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md), HC01–HC04, N05–N08,
DP33–DP37, DP64–DP69, and WF85–WF89. These distinguish output, competence,
fallback and staffing formation. The unresolved decision is whether a public
operator may remove the practical work that forms future decision-makers on
the strength of current gains before the new pathway demonstrates delivery.

[HC05–HC10](../country/C11/05/01-ai-and-succession-of-public-competence.md)
requires a paid successor path when removing material competence-forming
work. Separate bounded assistance from irreversible workforce or budget
reduction. Keep existing elected budget, career, employment and professional
qualification authorities. The procurement team has no licensing role.

## Alternatives and objections

A maintained ordinary route can provide valuable supervised work, but
some inherited tasks teach little and create delay. Staged assistance can
improve learning or service while preserving paid practice. A shared
training route can replace obsolete exercises and increase access. It can
also hide dependence on one instructor, source or employer, or transfer
the cost to the originating public service. Compare actual participation,
learning transfer, review, retention, labor and total costs on matched dates.
Prefer the competent ordinary route where the evidence supports only that
route. A better qualified assisted or shared route may displace it.

The strongest objection is that waiting for a real replacement cycle can
lock in an outdated model of work. Equivalent demonstrated capacity and
bounded trials remain available. Assess the required duty and the learning
path, with current workers, learners and service users, rather than retaining
posts mechanically. An equivalent qualification assessment can use accessible
simulation. It cannot jeopardize live rights or substitute activity measures
for independent competence. Rotation can shift time away from due work,
and a staffing order can be used to exclude entrants. Keep paid cover,
transparent role-based assessment and direct ordinary challenge.

## Evidence, migration and next action

[Primary studies and institutional comparator](sources.md) support treating
present output, learning and workforce replacement as distinct. The
[constructed comparison](../settlement/ai-and-succession-of-public-competence/01-two-cohorts-and-a-duty-roster.md)
tests a retirement sequence, combined use of workers and lost qualification
or supervision. It does not show actual rates, affordable deployment,
approval, affected participation or observed public outcomes.

Preserve wages, training rights, existing valid qualifications, authority
to decide, pending cases, protected records and original deadlines.
Map qualified successors and any paid overlap before changing a live route.
Do not import prior evaluation results beyond their tested population,
task, staffing, rules, access technology and source controls.

A bounded next assessment would have one authorized service compare a
competent ordinary workflow and protected staged assistance in a nonbinding
training cohort, with paid practice and an independent transfer assessment.
Use existing professional and worker review, no live unsupported decisions
and no reduction based on the trial itself. End with a usable-time coverage
account through the next expected departure and the actual training costs.
Reopen if mentor or reviewer dependence, replacement time, accessibility
or improved alternative instruction changes the choice.
