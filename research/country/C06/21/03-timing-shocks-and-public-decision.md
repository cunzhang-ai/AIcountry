# Annual balance is no evidence of timely protection

*Part of [C06 operating reference](../21-demand-consistent-operating-reference.md); design 64.*

Track domestic cash and foreign settlement separately through due dates.
Private payroll 155.27 monthly also needs a prior conditional working line
160 before sales; aggregate market balance zero is no free payday cash.
The authored calendar pays public obligations on day 1 and receives regular
cash/export receipts on day 21 in 30-day reference months. A prior working
cash balance and contracted FX facility, including fee/interest, bridge
those dates. An unused line is contingent access, not an existing asset;
draw creates a liability, repayment removes it. Validate provider, law,
conversion, collateral and adverse-event access before relying on it.

The 45% normal fixture keeps monthly Treasury cash above its declared 100
buffer and repays FX working draws. A 10% export decline uses 25 reserve
room and leaves about 22.54 facility debt at the first year's end. Repeating
the loss exhausts actual intramonth headroom in month 14, with unmet foreign
requirements accumulating. Annual headroom and a first-year pass cannot
support perpetual crisis or erase owed imports/interest. The new liability
also adds carry; it cannot be reset with the calendar. A rollover contract
not received requires owned sources/explicit lawful alternatives, even where
one repayment temporarily fits opening cash.

Commissioned firm energy, independent water/savings and trained staff are
credited only in their declared delivered scope. In the 45% reference,
conditional ordinary and stated energy/water loss tests fit, while the same
cash model fails before commissioning. The 10% labor-loss exercise still
lacks 265 clinical, 160 education, 55 network and 2 technical FTE.
Added care, justice and administration are not substitutes for those skills.
No roster/role/controller is actually qualified here. Geographic, pollution,
food/material and safe-time
delivery tests remain uncompleted; a national aggregate cannot accept a patient.

A further conditional reserve target moves 538 workers into distinct roles
for annual pay 21.45, leaving 43,952 market workers. It closes the stated
10% losses only within its preserved proxy. Its 34.97% private-productivity
comparison uses the retired GVA measure and is not current support; CP does not
recompute this older case. Initial training/sites remain unpriced. This is a
costed payroll target, not a trained roster or an adopted staffing ratio.

A bank book balances without proving liquidity or solvency. The reference's
60% deposit withdrawal needs 210 against liquid reserves 175, a gap 35
before any valid additional facility; with payroll working deposits, the
peak gap is 128.16. The reserve-target sensitivity compares conditional
liquidity 140 with eligible face 275/haircut 40%; its residual gap is zero
only as a capacity comparison. Draw costs/repayment, valid collateral and
solvency remain untested. Neither line is an actual commitment. Lending needs
collateral/solvency and separate resolution/deposit-protection tests; ownership
by the state does not authorize taking insurance or essential-service cash.
Preserve receipts, client property, court remedies and actual failed delivery.

Cabinet presents whole income/pension/production/ownership alternatives,
rights continuity, original evidence, who gains/loses and costed adverse
paths. Assembly decides actual taxes, spending, debt and statutory changes;
opposition, local bodies and affected people retain alternative routes.
Technical accounts and models cannot select a political mandate, prolong
emergency power or adopt their own parameters. E/T/U duties and current
software/source law remain unchanged. Retain/no change remains legitimate
when a proposed transition would worsen life or effective public control.
