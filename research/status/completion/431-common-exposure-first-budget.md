*Design523 · Scoped national choice*

make overlapping revenue and real delivery dependencies explicit across national,
local and earned-benefit payers. A new permanent commitment requires both dated
qualified finance and actual receiving on the same disclosed adverse path.
Standing duties retain their rights and remedies.

The [joined budget case](../../settlement/first-country-resource-account/02-different-receipts-one-economic-risk.md)
separates the ordinary receipts, common loss, reserve, recurring correction,
and an independently payable dispatch failure. The original Irish data help
identify overlap between nominal tax bases. The delivery and fiscal quantities
are authored and no actual adoption, source, readiness or better life follows.
