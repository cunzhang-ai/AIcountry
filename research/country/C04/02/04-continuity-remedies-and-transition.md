# TS13–TS16. Continuing law, remedies and paid transition

*Design 143 · [Territorial-status reference](../02-territorial-self-government.md) · No actual map, authority or commencement established.*

**TS13 — Distinguish consent from operative legal effects.** A lawfully approved
settlement specifies exact operative dates and objective preconditions, their
competent verifiers, sources, challenge and predefined effects of material delay
or failure. No undefined Cabinet readiness certificate or unilateral new date.
Domestic political authorization does not bind foreign states, transfer their
assets or provide FX, recognition, treaty consent or resources. Conversely, no
blanket foreign-state veto is created over internal democratic self-government.
Each legal condition must be justified within its actual scope. A deadline cannot
create missing jurisdiction, foreign agreement, money or consent. Material new
terms return to TS12 rather than an executive rewrite of what people approved.

**TS14 — No clean slate or punishment for advocacy.** Pending status leaves current
law, elected councils, courts, services, grants, claims, wages, pensions, legal
title and applicable collective rights in force. Peaceful advocacy and opposition
are protected. Neither side may use emergency powers, service/funding cutoff,
strategic relocation or selective nationality to manufacture consent. A person's
nationality is not automatically lost; a new nationality label does not alone
change actual tax residence or extinguish existing protection. Provide actual
legal status/choice and independent remedies under applicable law.

Asset/debt/pension allocation binds only within competent authority and actual
contracts; reallocating debtor responsibility between public authorities does
not unilaterally novate third-party creditor rights. Old judgments, liabilities,
workers and ecological duties pass to named, funded, accepting successors under
OP/T with usable records and independent enforcement. A changed boundary cannot
create an unfunded service island. A future start does not erase an earlier due
claim; currency, import, clinical and urgent-remedy continuity need actual means.

**TS15 — Remedy without invented sovereignty.** Ordinary independent courts, with
express statutory jurisdiction, can review scope admissibility, lawful standing,
records, refusal, omission, discrimination, use of public resources and observance
of valid approval conditions. They may compel owed process/available protection,
require reasons and received execution, and give timely lawful interim relief.
They cannot choose a political map, impose agreement, legislate a new tax or
decree sovereignty because officials were late. EA's actual fixed votes, judicial
appointment/source/removal protections, independent complaints and OP/T enforcement
remain necessary; the same controller across actors is not independent review.
Full capture or absent paid receivers remains a failed-control condition, not
proof that another office, emergency or AI should take the power. Actual lawful
opposition and peaceful replacement remain the democratic correction route.

**TS16 — Compatibility, commencement and reopening.** The protected charter rules
must be validly adopted through actual predecessor/founding authority before
first use; they cannot approve themselves. The ordinary procedural reference
starts **180 calendar days after a coordinated valid implementing Act**, with
actual date, jurisdiction, resources, records, appeal scope and receiving operators
published. This is an explicit lead-time hypothesis, not an adoption or operational
readiness finding. Earlier existing rights, pending claims, urgent protections
and called polls retain their actual source rules; incompatible changes require
express lawful transition, not migration by a research index.

The five-percent trigger, two-public authorization, local standing and periods
are contestable selections. Reopen on qualified evidence of systematic exclusion,
manufactured maps, negotiation obstruction, minority harm, unusable capacity or
an actually justified rival; do not silently vary thresholds in a live case.
No actual territory, ratifying public, independent affected review, full cost,
foreign legal qualification, paid operation or observed better lives is established.
The next bounded country action is the A8 ordinary-rights limitation/emergency-
derogation interface, rather than an automatic series of territorial refinements.
