C09 shared planning and the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md). The

but leaves direct receipt, scope triage, funded participation and a

## Alternatives and objections

Separate local review is faster and more accountable for local effects.
It can miss cumulative pressure and people outside the deciding electorate.
Voluntary compacts keep local control where every required power and consent
can be obtained. They cannot bind outsiders or dispose of their rights.
A directly elected region could hear a durable common docket with better
attention and knowledge. It would need a legitimately chosen boundary,
funded transferred powers, practical opposition and independent remedies.
Retain national residual resolution with local default for the declared case.

An applicant could split phases to avoid review, while an opponent could
file a weak claim to delay a lawful use. Use the same materiality standard,
assisted receipt, conflict-free preliminary scope review, and narrow judicial
protection under prior law. A filing is neither an automatic stay nor approval.
A central majority can still favor stronger places. Preserve outside voice,
public reasons, protected consents and direct remedies, and make the cost of
national delay visible. Publish safe evidence without exposing private or
collective testimony unnecessarily. Consultation cannot waive a right.

## Qualified result, continuity and next action

The [sources](sources.md) support cross-boundary participation and effect
mapping. The [constructed case](../settlement/cross-local-effects/01-three-councils-and-one-catchment.md)
checks divided applications, transfer of receipt, funding and changed capacity.
It does not establish actual physical limits, consent, participation, forum
capacity, adoption or better lives. Preserve existing lawful titles, services,
valid permits, time limits and accrued obligations. A residual vote does not
waive the applicable sector approval or release conditions.

With consenting authorities and affected parties, compare one real shared
resource decision and a minor local case against the same hearing, legal,
review and service calendars. Stay within authorized research resources and
stop at a comparable assignment of powers and a priced first docket. Reopen
if sector protection is displaced, a group is excluded, or the Assembly
cannot sustain the docket despite a funded schedule.
