# C06. Scarcity across essential systems

*Part of [C06 public finance](../C06-public-finance.md); integrated design 79 research, not enacted law.*

An account can reveal that water, power, food, clinical care, shelter and
transport all need the same fuel, workers or usable sites. It cannot decide
who goes without them. The Assembly must enact the purposes, authority and
review path for temporary allocation **before** a foreseeable shortage. No
operator, minister, vendor or AI receives general power to change legal
entitlements merely because its forecast says demand exceeds supply.

## Enacted priorities with context-specific judgment

The statute names covered resources and territories, observable triggers,
source records, lawful stocks and contracts, responsible local and national
actors, temporary measures, expiry and renewal path. It states how to protect
life-sustaining access, urgent health needs, ecological conditions of future
supply, dependent services and people least able to avoid harm. It tests
expected benefit and avoidable irreversible loss without reducing persons to
one national welfare score. Arbitrary distinctions by wealth, political view,
identity, digital access or order of arrival are forbidden. Relevant clinical
judgment remains with qualified clinicians under separate public standards;
technical safety decisions remain with licensed operators. Exact priority
between unlike needs remains a public value choice requiring elected law,
not a fixed universal algorithm or a declaration that all claims can be met.
For a surprise conflict the statute also supplies a **residual provisional
rule**: the protected stocks and existing duties that cannot be diverted,
the limited immediate harm a named officer may prevent, evidence needed for
that finding, the rule for materially equal claims and prompt independent
correction. An undefined phrase such as “save lives” is not itself an
allocation mandate.

The plan identifies how a locality can request assistance, which existing
claims and ecological duties continue, who hears downstream and cross-border
effects, and what physical capacity a transfer takes from the supplying area.
Public reasons explain the allocation class and likely unmet needs without
publishing a patient's or vulnerable person's private record. People can
challenge unlawful exclusion, factual error, inaccessible distribution and
misapplication before an independent reviewer with power to order available
lawful protection. A remedy cannot create missing stock; the unfulfilled duty
and its actual consequences remain visible.

## The first hours and return to elected control

During a sudden compound shortage, the named local civilian lead and sector
operators may execute only their **preadopted** safety and continuity powers.
A named cabinet coordinator can reconcile records and deploy only preassigned,
nonoverlapping reserves and mutual aid on the statute's terms. The coordinator
logs source observations, alternatives, who bears each diversion, its end time
and the officers who can reverse it. It immediately notifies affected local
bodies, opposition, independent reviewers and the Assembly through its
protected alternate-sitting route. Multiple emergency labels cannot restart
an expired authorization or convert a temporary allocation into permanent
taxation, taking of property, treaty withdrawal or change of franchise.
If communication or the sitting route fails, officers preserve the attempted
notice and use the lawful alternate path when reachable; failure supplies
neither consent nor an extension of power.

If a material cross-sector tradeoff is not authorized in advance, responsible
officers may provide only the statute's bounded residual interim acts
and must seek an Assembly choice as soon as its lawful sitting route permits.
An elected decision states the affected scope, distribution, payer, remaining
claims, review and return path. Neither an Assembly delay nor a court vacancy
creates a new cabinet mandate; existing narrow safety duties and lawful claims
continue. Independent courts review legality and individual protection but
do not choose the country's permanent priority or manufacture supplies.
Clinical case decisions use their own lawful triage and appeal path, not a
minister's named-patient order.

After the shortage, original stock, order, delivery and denial records support
individual correction, compensation where law requires it, public audit and
prospective statutory revision. Capacity is rebuilt in the same C06 account;
calling a person served in a database is not delivery. The [scarcity decision
record](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-cross-system-scarcity/decision-record.md) states rivals,
dependencies and what would reopen this working selection.

[CP05–CP08](28/03-residual-scope-and-priority.md) retains actually available
uncommitted public stocks, lawful preparedness releases and voluntary aid.
[CP13–CP16](28/07-committed-logistics-and-continuing-claims.md) now adds only
scheduled public logistics temporarily committed to less urgent services, on
strict grave-harm/source/safe-receiving/downstream conditions. Protected critical
care/dependence/ecological/democratic/remedy functions remain outside absent an
actually accepted lawful safe substitute; no broad private requisition follows.
Actual imminent bodily-harm/dependent-service priority precedes a narrow
comparable-claim draw/rotation. Local first authority ends within 24 elapsed
hours; affirmative Cabinet consolidation ends absolutely within 72 of the first
related act. No failed sitting, new label, forced private resource or ongoing
claim renews power. Direct review and real receipt apply. This limited
pool can still preserve inherited inequalities/be insufficient; uncommitted-only,
broader requisition and actual paid redundancy remain distinct real alternatives. Current law is unchanged.

**Design144 rights join.** [RX](../C10/01-public-safety-and-emergencies.md) excludes
domestic constitutional derogation and new priority by declaration. Existing
bounded residual acts/reserves, qualified sector decisions and actual elected
allocation remain their own sources. The14/30/90 declaration clock neither
restarts a scarcity/DR order nor pretends absent stock/source has been received.
Accrued claims and paid continuation survive expiry; all rights are not converted
into impossible guarantees of every physical quantity at once.

[RC21–RC28](../C10/13/05-shared-reserve-and-conditional-release.md) qualifies a
shared reserve before reliance. Keep useful donor cover, actual dispatch and
receiving distinct from reported stock. CP retains its own allocations,
protected scope, remedies and original clocks.

[Persistent physical scarcity](62/11-durable-capacity-and-public-choice.md) requires its own ordinary elected schedule, effective scope and end time. The first-hour residual powers keep their own source and expire on their original clocks.
