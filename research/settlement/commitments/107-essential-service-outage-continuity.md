# Commitment 107. Preserve useful service through an electricity outage

[OC01–OC08](../../country/C09/20-essential-service-outage-continuity.md)
joins a named sector duty, last safe start time, independently accepted
alternate supply and actual receipt. Reserve enough usable capacity for
those duties before normal sales. Price the remaining lifecycle,
qualification, lawful acceptance and human receiving.

Local and national guarantors, EG, WS, RC, PS, CP, RX and court and poll
authorities retain separate powers. Existing claims, source and paid OP/T
successors continue. The district case provides scoped funding and
operating evidence. Candidate source, safe operation and actual outcomes
remain open. [Comparison](../outage-continuity/README.md)
