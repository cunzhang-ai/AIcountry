# Joint AI purchasing with separately usable public services

*Design291 · DP48–DP52 · Proposed procurement and reliance rules.*

**DP48 — Plan reliance across the public duties that can fail together.**
Before a material AI contract, renewal or expansion, each sector owner
supplies its existing protected duty, original deadline, casework
authority, needed staffing and independently usable alternate. The
existing public digital support office assembles the affected purchases,
dependency groups, paid claims on recovery and relevant procurement
windows. Trace effective control of the model or ordinary engine,
licensing, update, hosting, credentials, source records, staff, payments
and permissions. Use scenarios that can affect several offices together.
Another brand, local demonstration or annual headcount cannot establish
concurrent coverage. The comparison needs service capacities and scope,
with protected inspection for necessary sensitive evidence. It does not
need a central resident record.

**DP49 — Reserve the real recovery work before committing new reliance.**
Identify the provider, lawful receiver, qualified role, time window,
usable scope, named payer and expiry for each indispensable reservation.
Ask the provider and affected duty owners to reconcile prior and pending
claims. Credit the same resource only once in any overlapping window.
A conditional offer may hold a finite slot on authorized terms and
its price must be visible. It supplies no permission to bind a service
or use another body's reservation. Credit multiple possible uses only
where qualified sequential delivery or lawful mutually exclusive
commitments are established. Competing offers that do not fit return
for an exact choice or a narrower use. Existing due duties retain their
law and priority. First signature alone cannot create a public priority
or take an already committed slot.

**DP50 — Compare the whole discount against rights and usable capacity.**
Under SC, compare competent ordinary operation, a scoped nonexclusive
joint purchase and separately operated alternatives on the same protected
needs. Price minimum spending, overlap, training, lawful exit,
subcontractors, independent staff, adjudication and the ordinary
non-AI route. Disclose discounted or otherwise attractive terms that
would require wider dependency, exclusivity or a capacity claim
another office needs. Prefer shared buying where it buys a useful
public result with funded independent delivery, and limit it where
only a smaller task fits. Verify any benefit under DP33–DP37 before
crediting it. The competent elected authorities control material
spending and the service owners accept actual scope. The shared
office supplies evidence and proposals without a new budget veto,
case assignment, procurement monopoly or power over local acceptance.

**DP51 — Give the affected owners usable notice and a remedy.** Publish
the material purchase scope, commitment, cost and declared dependency
loss with safe access to reasons, limits and affected alternatives.
Use SC for award and challenge, AA for independent audit, and sector
courts or reviewers for actual lost access. No bidder or affected
provider controls the only recovery evidence or appoints the reviewer.
Maintain paid review independent of the failed route. Where a reserve
cannot serve an already due task, expose the original claim and lawful
response. Limit affected optional reliance and continue eligible
payments, first intake, pending cases and remedies on original dates.
AI may compare offers and flag conflicts. It cannot award a contract,
infer a missing reservation or decide whose protection is lost.

**DP52 — Reconcile cumulative changes through real handback.** Recheck
on new reliance, expiry, upstream control change, reduced cover or a
credible incident. Test the joint workload and subsequent recovery
interval safely before claiming that an alternate can carry them.
After loss, record actual receiving and reconcile pending or acted
advice under DP40, DP41 and LX. Keep spent standby, renewed capacity,
claims and independently verified receipt separate. In migration
preserve original rights, lawful contracts, prior exposure and funded
handover. Move to another supplier or a competent ordinary route
where it meets the actual service better. Reopen if shared purchasing
restricts practical exit, creates an unaccountable gateway, displaces
ordinary work or lacks a demonstrated public benefit.
