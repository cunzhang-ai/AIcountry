# FB13–FB16. Show what the existing country will need to keep working

*Design212 · Part of [C06 fiscal durability](../62-lasting-services-and-fiscal-shocks.md).*

**FB13 — Publish two baselines for the same four years.** At the ordinary
budget, the Treasury and actual sector duty holders present both the
existing legal and voted path and the estimated work and source needed to
maintain its lawful service and access under changing conditions. The
first retains the law, appropriations and their expiry, claims, liabilities
and actual starting performance. The second includes changing needs,
verified unmet duties, continuing costs, maintenance and secure alternatives.
Separate preserving a lawful existing duty from renewing a finite programme
or making a new offer. An expiring appropriation and a continuing right can
coexist. An estimate of desired quality does not create a right or a
permanent appropriation. Actual need can require a material change even
where real aggregate expenditure is constant.

**FB14 — Reconcile claims on each source and on scarce work.** Use a
common context, valuation and time horizon. For each material difference,
show original obligation, affected group, duty holder, net recurring
amount, finite transition, owner and due-date settlement, resource and
receiving commitments, and relevant future tails. Join national, local,
independent and contracted operations without adding transfers to
consolidated income. Retain dated funding by each legal payer. Test
qualified capacity and useful time after existing work, leave, training,
replacements and approved standby. Keep separately protected reviewer
capacity and scarce material, local and foreign dependencies in scope.
A released school expense in one locality does not qualify a nurse or
finance a different payer's due contract. Verify any actual conversion,
training, borrowing, resource release or transfer under ordinary law.

**FB15 — Measure service need without enshrining inherited exclusion.**
Use relevant population, actual access and unmet need, job and site
availability, unit cost and maintenance evidence. Compare groups and
territories where averages conceal serious effects. Keep observed activity,
modeled need, actual lawful entitlement and chosen quality distinguishable.
Do not extrapolate low use in an excluded population as low need.
Count affected household time and unpaid work separately from cash.
Carry lawful claims and identify excluded users in both retain-and-repair
and reform. Use proportionate data, safe aggregates and qualified review
with independently accessible evidence. Reuse common evidence only for
unchanged scope. AI can reconcile compatible claims or flag missing
dependencies under DP. Ministers, providers and courts retain their own
judgment. No central personal registry is required.

**FB16 — Expose what each proposed budget makes possible.** Under DB and
GP, the Cabinet presents at least one viable form of retaining and repairing
essential functions, and any preferred extension in the same context.
Where no viable path is established, publish the exposed duties and
missing resources. Set out the proposed change from each baseline, named
distributional losses and the effects of waiting. Test a weaker credible
combination of receipts, staffing or input risks with dated consequences.
Opposition and ordinary members can introduce competing funding,
delivery or staging plans using the same evidence and available support.
Explain how a proposed saving will actually protect, change or remove a
service. Keep material objections and separate legally authorized,
qualified or unresolved elements. Existing DB and AA rights remain.
