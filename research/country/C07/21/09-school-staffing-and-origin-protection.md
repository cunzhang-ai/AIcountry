# ED39–ED43. Keep qualified teaching available across schools

*Part of [public learning](../21-public-learning-and-school-power.md). Design337 research selection.*

**ED39 — Count lost teaching by school, cohort and subject or required support.**
Before each term the local education director checks the lawful offer against
named, qualified, paid staffing, including accommodations, paid preparation
and travel. Open a staffing correction when the same cohort loses a required
teaching or support opportunity to staffing on ten teaching days in a rolling
thirty teaching days, or when the director can verify in advance that this
threshold will be reached in the upcoming term. Keep the original offer and
date of each shortfall, including sessions removed or combined because of the
shortage. Record lawful changes to the offer with reasons and preserve earlier
losses. Accept accessible reports from pupils, staff or families and verify them
against more than the payroll. Individual continuity, assessment and review
duties start on their existing, often earlier, clocks.

**ED40 — Name the owner and fund a timely response.** The local director issues
a written disposition within ten teaching days of the trigger and before the
first affected session when a forecast permits that earlier response. State
the missing provision, the paid repair and start, and how pupils can obtain
interim teaching and challenge it now. When timely funded coverage is
unavailable, disclose who will still miss service, make the best available
lawful interim offer, and send a dated funding request to the competent elected
authority. For shared shortages, one accepting lead coordinates across councils.
Each authority remains answerable for its pupils and commitments. Under the
enacted grant, the national education authority funds verified gaps in local
capacity by published need, with fixed costs and travel for sparse areas.
Local appropriations fund ordinary delivery. An unfunded disposition exposes
a breach or unmet need and starts review. Earlier individual remedies remain
available without waiting for a council or budget decision.

**ED41 — Protect origin teaching and worker choice.** Compare retention and
working conditions, willing return, lawful new recruitment and supported local
training with a voluntary shared appointment. Before counting any transfer or
shared appointment as correction, confirm the worker's consent and paid terms,
qualified cover for displaced duties at the origin, and a usable timetable
in both places. Pay preparation, travel, mentoring and overlap. Test shared
dependencies in the same teaching window and identify who can act if cover
fails. An origin employer gains no control over a worker's lawful departure.
The affected education owners owe correction if free movement leaves a gap.
Comparable remote or community teaching needs the required human instruction,
local support and accessibility under ED. Independent AI tutoring can
supplement it. School leadership retains responsibility for professional
judgment and service, and AI may identify staffing conflicts with a traceable
schedule and a human correction route.

**ED42 — Separate the decision, delivery and remedy.** The local director owns
the correction. The education inspector can receive a protected report directly,
inspect both schools and identify hours actually received, continuity of the
teaching relationship, lost instruction and any new origin shortfall.
Use independent education review and existing court and execution routes to
correct unlawful denial or delay. Within twenty teaching days of receiving
a funding request under ED40, the competent elected body holds a public
vote on the next response and states whose offer still falls short. It may
approve a funded repair or record its refusal and reasons. A missing vote
is an independently reviewable delay. Publish aggregated provision by
subject, area and access need where disclosure is safe. Authorized reviewers
can inspect protected detail
where public cells would identify a pupil or worker. Keep deployment, actual
instruction and later recovery separate. Correction at one school leaves any
origin failure open.

**ED43 — Give temporary funding a review date and protect live provision.**
At the end of the first complete teaching term after implementation, compare
actual receipt, unresolved learning loss, ongoing staffing, full costs and
both schools' outcomes. Renew exceptional funding only by a recorded lawful
decision with an identified ordinary funding route or dated successor. Keep
valid pay, appointment, pupil and remedy obligations through any grant expiry.
Begin prospectively with costed supervision, independent review, protected
record access and paid migration. Review the trigger and the funding choice
if timetable changes hide loss, the same neighborhoods keep lending staff,
professional continuity worsens, or a simpler arrangement protects both
schools at lower cost and burden.
