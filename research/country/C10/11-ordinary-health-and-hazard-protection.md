# C10. Ordinary health and hazard protection

*Design147 · HP01–HP24 · [C10 safety](../C10-safety-and-international-relations.md) · Candidate Act, not actual restriction authority.*

Select **supported voluntary protection first, with narrowly grounded ordinary
court-backed separation and physical hazard powers where genuinely necessary**.
An emergency declaration, disease label, foreign recommendation or AI score
supplies none. RX's no-domestic-derogation ceiling stays intact. This supplies the
positive ordinary permissions/endpoints identified in the145 whole-country review.

Fourteen days of exposure-only confinement is a normative legal ceiling, not a
claim all pathogens become safe then. Established ongoing grave transmissibility
can support continuing short judicial orders with escalated independent review,
without an arbitrary biological90-day assumption. Potential prolonged confinement
is an explicit retained domination cost; no periodic form proves necessity or
consent. Refusal of a bodily procedure alone proves neither risk nor an offense.

No new medical-treatment coercion, diagnosis-specific commitment, status offense,
health-questioning power, general surveillance, property requisition or unreviewable
health sovereign. The source-limited physical enforcement below is express law,
not permission borrowed from LF's ceiling or a regulatory licence. Existing
stronger/earlier protections, ordinary clinical care, actual source claims and
current software/constitutional/contribution boundaries remain until lawful change.

risk, failed courts, unsupported compliance and foreseeable lost safety. Actual
public, disease/event, legal sources, qualified simultaneous staff, places, paid
protection, independent affected review and better lives remain unverified.
