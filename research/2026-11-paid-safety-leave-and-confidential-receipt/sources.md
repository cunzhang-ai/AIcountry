# Primary records on paid safety leave and confidential delivery

1. Australian Fair Work Ombudsman, *Family and domestic violence
   leave fact sheet*. The official account describes ten paid
   days annually for full-time, part-time and casual employees,
   available from the start of work. It distinguishes hours that
   would have been worked and casual rostered hours, describes
   notice and evidence, and requires pay slips not to identify
   this type of leave while relevant records are maintained.
   [Official fact sheet](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/tools-and-resources/fact-sheets/minimum-workplace-entitlements/family-and-domestic-violence-leave).
2. Australian Industrial Transformation Institute, *Independent
   review of the operation of the paid family and domestic violence
   leave entitlement in the Fair Work Act 2009*, 2024, section on
   confidentiality, printed pages 61–63, and stakeholder evidence
   on administration. It records difficulties with routine records
   and payroll, small workplaces, and situations in which an
   employer and respondent may have overlapping control or ties.
   It also reports objections that a centralized public process
   could create a new administrative barrier. These are findings
   and submissions from an implementation review. Reported
   individual cases do not establish prevalence or a causal effect.
   [Original report](https://www.dewr.gov.au/download/16523/final-report-independent-review-fair-work-amendment-paid-family-and-domestic-violence-leave-act-2022/40703/final-report-independent-review-fair-work-amendment-paid-family-and-domestic-violence-leave-act-2022/pdf).

The proposed domestic hours, payroll boundary, advance and work
protections are separately authored. The comparator supplies no
local cost estimate, verified privacy or actual implementation.
