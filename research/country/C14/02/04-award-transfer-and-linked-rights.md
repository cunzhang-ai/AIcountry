# LRT01–LRT05. Transfer benefits without breaking linked rights

*Part of [transition and service commencement](../02-transition-and-commencement.md).
Design246 research selection.*

**LRT01 — Transfer the operative award and pending work separately.** For
each material statutory transfer, publish the cohort, governing law and
operative dates. In protected custody carry the last valid decision, period,
rate, original first receipt, due dates, pending change reports, open
appeals, arrears, relevant consents and the actual roles that can resolve
each item. The elected transfer instrument identifies which law applies
to each unsettled period and case. An existing recipient continues on
the last applicable operative decision until its ordinary lawful expiry,
variation or replacement. The recipient need not reapply solely because
a body changes. A transferred award does not dispose of a pending increase,
reduction, evidence dispute or appeal. Retain the original dates and decide
each under its proper law. Pay any resulting arrears from the correct
source. Keep the independent remedy and earlier urgent protection reachable.

**LRT02 — Carry the evidence used by linked services.** Before transferring
an award, identify services that rely on it, including lawful local
concessions, transport, housing support or reduced charges. Classify each
as a statutory entitlement, contracted provision or discretionary offer
and retain its own criteria. For each existing qualifying relationship,
name a receiving body, the minimum evidence it needs, the validity period,
the continued funding and the challenge route. Where the new award uses
another name, identifier or proof system, an express transitional law
or lawful receiving agreement recognizes the old proof for its original
scope or supplies equivalent usable evidence. Authorities with separate
powers keep their own lawful decisions. A bridge cannot silently extend
eligibility, override another jurisdiction or make a private offer a
statutory right. Keep genuine exceptions and unmapped dependencies visible.
A person or service can report a missing dependency and get a timely
answer or urgent sector protection without reopening the main award.

**LRT03 — Make the transfer usable before the old route closes.** Give
accessible notice of the responsible bodies, dates, evidence and routes
for changes and review. Test that an eligible person can actually use
each material linked service in its useful window, even without a new
account, network or disclosure of unrelated conditions. Provide qualified
assisted and offline routes. Record evidence used and limit purpose,
access and retention. A provider may need an eligibility confirmation
and date, without diagnosis or payment history. A missing field or
technical error cannot be treated as an adverse decision. Retain the
lawful prior evidence within its scope and resolve a contradiction under
ordinary notice, urgent safety and review rules. For a proposed change
schedule, use a retained or staged route where linked receiving is
unready. For an already operative transfer, the successor remains
answerable and must use prior-law fallback or disclose the breach.
Readiness reports cannot suspend law, extend office or reverse a transfer.

**LRT04 — Keep one accountable payer for each due obligation.** Identify
the owner, lawful source, executing route and handoff period for every
due instalment. Reconcile period, liability, payment instruction,
settlement and actual receipt. Use an obligation reference across
authorized payers without requiring a new universal identifier.
The fallback needs its own competent authority, accessible restricted
cash, safe source records and a tested receiving route. Release a
replacement ordinary payment only when nonpayment or safe transfer of
settlement responsibility is established. Do not infer nonpayment from
missing status, or receipt from a submitted instruction.

Where settlement status cannot be established in useful time and
subsistence is at risk, use any expressly authorized continuity advance
or other urgent protection within its own source and limits. Disclose
the potential overlap and fund that exposure before relying on it.
Reconcile when receipts are known. Credit the same obligation once.
Record actual gross payments, any authorized recovery and remaining
public cost separately. No automatic debit, reduction of protected income
or compulsory repayment follows from an institutional error. Any recovery
uses actual authority, notice, independent challenge and hardship protection.
If there is no lawful funded fallback, record the missed deadline and
seek competent source and remedy action.

**LRT05 — Close after confirmed receiving and name the long tail.** Review
at 30 calendar days from the applicable legal change and at 30-day
intervals while material transfer failures remain unresolved. The review
date does not shorten entitlement, extend an advance or restart a claim.
Verify payment, useful linked access, received remedy and the owner of
any unresolved dispute or late settlement. Reconcile paid interim staff
and reserve overlap under T, FT and BLC. Publish safe aggregate failures
and actual repairs. Preserve purpose-limited evidence and direct challenge
through changes of agency, vendor or government. Keep explicit owners
and lawful routes for open cases after administrative close. AI may
trace dependencies, flag conflicting statuses or translate. It cannot
make an award, authorize data sharing, certify receipt or decide recovery.
