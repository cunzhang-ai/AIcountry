# Continuing executive power under affirmative democratic control

Design340 · Scoped finding for [country completion](../../DESIGN-COMPLETENESS.md)

[PWR01–PWR06](../../country/C03/01/01-review-and-renewal-of-material-powers.md)
selects a fixed expiry for new material delegations, independent scrutiny
and express primary-law renewal. It preserves the underlying service and
requires a funded plan when a power ends.

The [worked comparison](../../settlement/public-power-renewal/01-recertification-and-expiry.md)
checks withheld evidence, an election gap, late legislation and standing
payments. Actual legislative workload, compliance and outcomes are open.
