# TW25–TW28. Keep the shorter week and service access feasible together

*Design222 · Part of [work, rest and paid capability](../10-work-rest-and-paid-capability.md).*

**TW25 — Match paid capacity to the service windows.** For an essential
or materially dependent service, the competent owner matches accepted
opening, travel, handover, qualified work, paid breaks, leave, training,
replacement and independent remedies in the same roster windows.
Check the availability of the particular people and scope after
other obligations, not annual headcount alone. Rehearse an
unavailable worker or shared fallback where material. Compare paid
relief, a different distribution, process repair, reduced avoidable
demand and new hiring. Give affected residents and workers access to
alternatives and costs. A provider's duty to meet service hours and a
worker's rights retain their own clocks. No presumed family substitution
or projected AI gain can meet the schedule.

**TW26 — Fund the separate stages and useful alternatives.** Before
enacting the timetable, Cabinet's public programme and budget show
both TW18 stages with regular hourly and salaried pay, actually
financed paid overlap, private as well as public worker and price
effects, and sector receiving under central and credible weaker
paths. Match each proposed stage to the ordinary and adverse
recurring funding of the authorities that pay for standing work,
including services purchased from other employers. Compare the
second increment against the funded first-stage level and show
the remaining burden after any temporary support expires. Use actual FB, DB and WF source boundaries. Compare strong
retain and repair, a funded 36-hour first stage with a later separate
32-hour choice, and both stages enacted together with accepted capacity.
Keep the working preference for both stages and TW18's candidate
dates. The choice of adoption, prospective scope and resources
belongs to the competent elected body. An enacted stage binds
without a fresh technical permission. A forecast deficit or missing
review does not amend or suspend it.

**TW27 — Give the failure a named, lawful remedy.** A worker may seek
ordinary independent correction of wrongful pay, hours, intensity,
retaliation or a pattern departure. A service user keeps the actual
sector remedy, without first resolving the workforce dispute.
Inspect actual payroll, schedule, assignments and received service
within existing lawful access. Require timely lawful correction,
including pay and meaningful release where due. Restore paid qualified
cover through the responsible employer, service owner and source.
Do not use repeated exceptions, compulsory overtime or unpaid care to
conceal a failure. A material source or staffing gap goes to elected
correction through FB/DB. Maintain protected duties and disclose actual
unmet work. Courts enforce applicable duties within jurisdiction,
with no court-set general budget or labour policy.

**TW28 — Review distribution and keep stronger terms through transfer.**
Publish usable aggregate evidence of released time, total constrained
hours, earnings and prices, work intensity, vacancies, outcomes and
lost service by affected group, including irregular and dependent
workers and people with care duties. Distinguish proposed work
reorganization from verified net gain and correct mistaken use of
evidence under EV. Protect privacy, dissent and a working ordinary
route for appeals. Paid OP/T succession keeps regular pay, service
rights and original clocks through an employer, software or operator
change. Reopen on persistently missed safe coverage, increased unpaid
work, reduced access, inadequate pay, capture or a verified better
whole alternative. A later change uses the competent prospective
law and retained claims, not a new work-hours authority for an AI,
buyer or inspection office.
