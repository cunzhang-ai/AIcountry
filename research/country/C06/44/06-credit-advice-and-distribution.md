# NB21–NB24: credit and advice require responsibility, not profiling permission

*Part of [C06/44](../44-nonbank-finance-and-client-protection.md); design113 candidate.*

**NB21 — Lending without deposit privilege.** A licensed nonbank lender uses
paid equity, actual disclosed term securities/wholesale borrowing and authorized
committed finance, not deposit-like balances/client property. Select eligible
own equity at least20% gross funded credit plus full undrawn/guaranteed/bounded
additional derivative exposure, counted once. No bank8% certification by analogy;
circular funds and unbounded unknown loss do not qualify. Same-currency accessible
liquidity covers100% contractual30-day payments/draws; no projected collections
or automatic monetary loan. Paid committed lines need enforceability, currency,
availability and attribution to the provider's exposure too, not two usable pots.
Daily warning/monthly reporting and a reasoned paid correction/no-new-risk plan
on breach; no ratio-triggered automatic guilt, repossession or guaranteed rescue.
The20% choice may ration useful credit; less restrictive wholesale leverage and
public/relationship lending remain rivals, with their own genuine losses.

**NB22 — Repayment assessment serves the borrower too.** Before retail credit,
show total amount/cost/fees, effective annual rate with cashflow assumptions,
FX/variable-rate/payment scenarios, security, arrears and exit. Reasonably assess
verified safely available income, existing essential costs/debts and stressed
repayment, not assumed family support, collateral appreciation or refinancing.
No credit entitlement or compelled approval; refusal gives usable actual reasons
and correction/independent challenge. No political participation, AI-account use,
protected identity or intimate data as eligibility proxy. Lawful narrow fraud/
financial-crime requirements retain their own evidence/process, not a general
secret score or permanent exclusion. Collection and foreclosure use B/U and safe
housing duties; fee capitalization cannot evade a separately enacted price rule.
No universal numeric usury cap is invented by a supervisor; total-price fairness,
coercion and abusive-term remedies remain B/M and elected further price choices.

**NB23 — Distribution creates duties beyond a warning.** Advisers must act in
the actual client's interests within their agreed scope, disclose and manage
commissions/affiliates and compare reasonable lower-cost suitable alternatives;
no remuneration tied solely to a harmful sale. A retail broker executing a
specific unsolicited order explains actual product/risk/cost; complex leveraged/
illiquid products require demonstrated understanding and ability to absorb the
stated loss, without replacing personal agency with a wealth-only certificate.
Inadequate understanding forbids that broker's recommendation/distribution until
addressed, not ownership of lawful assets or an official's power over private life.
Advice, general education, advertising, execution and client authority remain
distinct; a persuasive AI recommendation does not exempt its actual provider.

**NB24 — Correct the wrong sale, not every losing market.** Specific deception,
unauthorized trade, unsuitable advised sale or fiduciary breach has reasoned
B civil restitution/loss/cause and ordinary court relief; a lawful disclosed
investment decline alone is no automatic provider debt. Accessible free-to-file
complaint intake gives reasons within15working days, one justified extension
to35total; faster M06 provisional next-working-day retail payment restoration
and other urgent rights prevail. Known undisputed sums continue. No mandatory
company-controlled arbitration or complaint filing fee blocks direct court/aid.
Public standard-form and aggregate harm inquiry uses existing conduct/V powers,
not a new authority to merge everyone's finances or silently punish by account ban.

[PT01–PT24](../../C08/26-property-transfers-and-secured-credit.md) now supplies
ordinary real collateral/attachment, bounded public notice and rank, actual
default/twenty-working-day cure and court-controlled realization. NB capital/
liquidity/repayment tests do not prove title or permit private repossession.
Ordinary-course goods buyers can take free of seller-created security; stolen
ownership/client custody does not become lawful inventory. No hidden purchase-
money preference or new M/NB special priority/guarantee follows.
