## Substantive choices and their costs

| Whole arrangement | Gains, losses and source obligations |
|---|---|
| Retain generic sector procurement/support | Avoids new job/contract/report formalization and can work under actual adequate source law. Candidate leaves named common-operator/incident powers unresolved and common roots can remain invisible; real source law may justify retaining it locally. |
| Selected sector authority + bounded optional common capability | Enables useful shareable AI/access/support and explicit response without a political master platform. Requires paid specialist capacity, independent paths and often more complicated interfaces; source/turnover/security cost may outweigh a task's gain. |
| One integrated national public AI/digital operator | May simplify procurement, quality, resource and data exchange. Concentrates model framing, keys, records and practical access; public ownership alone cannot make its appeal independent. Needs real split powers/exit rather than an emergency veto. |
| Independent sector-only/smaller AI portfolio | Can reduce correlated dependence and preserve familiar competence/control. Can cost more, duplicate work or leave service access/learning gains unrealized; actual lower cost or safety cannot be assumed. |
| Negotiated regional/cooperative/shared infrastructure | May pool genuinely scarce expertise while limiting one national vendor. Requires actual counterpart consent/title, paid capacity, compatible protection and exit; foreign/regional resources are not automatically available. |

These alternatives disagree about ownership, coordination, privacy, efficiency
and independent capability. They cannot be resolved by averaging model outputs
or assigning one scalar national progress score. A safe no-AI/simpler outcome
is valid, but refusing useful assistance also has an opportunity cost. Actual
affected workers/nonusers/disabled people/challengers, source-qualified reviewers
and foreign counterparties have not participated in this desk selection.

Preserve all national functions, rights and ordinary/changing/hostile-government
paths. The next priority is their complete source/capacity feasibility frontier,
not proof that one digital incident form exists. Actual better lives, independent
execution and public authority remain unverified; continued research is no enactment.
