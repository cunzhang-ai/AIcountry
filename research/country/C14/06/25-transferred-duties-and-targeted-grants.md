# T113–T116. Pay for transferred duties and find people missing from the handover

13 November 2026 · Integrated design 454 · Candidate implementation schedule

**T113 — Give each funding stream a distinct obligation and owner.**
For a material transfer, record the underlying legal duty, the
authority that pays for it, each operating agreement, and the separate
appropriation, settlement and renewal. Keep transition compensation,
general equalization, restricted grants and provider invoices in
their actual scopes. A higher spending ceiling or a grant for
another service supplies no cash for the transferred duty.
Show gross obligations, claimed changes, lawful offsets and the
first and next actual payment dates. Reconcile after receipt.

**T114 — Reconcile the people due service before relying on a cutover.**
Apply FT05–FT08 and the bounded search methods of LX12. Compare
the origin roster, independently held lawful award, referral or
delivery records, and the successor's accepted work. Check a
safe sample outside the main export and give residents an accessible
way to report omission without repeating a valid claim. Identify
retained duties, uncertain cases and duplicate entries by distinct
obligation and period. Keep unmatched records protected. A match
or referral does not establish eligibility or delivery by itself.

**T115 — Protect the missing case at its original time.** A verified
due case belongs to the legally responsible owner on its original
clock. Activate prior-law provisional support where appropriate
while merits are unresolved. The named successor obtains an
authorized, separately paid fallback where an operator cannot
receive. Test staff and other resources across simultaneous
duties. Hold only an affected optional expansion if the route
fails. The legal transfer, ordinary duties and independent urgent
review retain their own effect. Record actual nonreceipt and
continuing liability when no lawful route can act.

**T116 — Give elected bodies a priced choice and keep the next call.**
Compare accepted ordinary repair, paid finite overlap and changed
operating scope or funding. Separate required services from optional
improvements, show what each reallocation displaces and keep affected
people and opposition able to challenge the facts. Fund any remedy
and later receiving within the same calendar. AI can help reconcile
dated exceptions and shared resources. It cannot decide eligibility,
merge disputed identities, accept a care assignment or certify
receipt. Carry records and accrued obligations through renewal and
reopen on a missing case, a source change or a stronger funded route.
