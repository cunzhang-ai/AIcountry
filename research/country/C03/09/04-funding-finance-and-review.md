# EA13–EA16: separate inquiry, usable money and independent correction

**EA13 — Finance investigation is a protected career function.** Within the existing electoral
administration, appoint a named head of campaign-finance investigation through a separate C04
open merit/qualified public shortlist and reasoned EA10 vote, with protected career
tenure/discipline, remuneration and direct court challenge. No new sovereign finance bureau or
party nominee board. Staff, records and case-assignment rules are distinct from
ballot/roll/count operations; shared clerks/sites save cost only without controlling sole
evidence/intake. The head recruits, assigns and disciplines finance staff under C04 open career
procedures, job-related grounds, usable notice/answer and independent ordinary review; neither
secretary nor cabinet may reassign or dismiss them for a named case. The commission handles the
head under EA02; personal conflicts use predeclared qualified substitutes and direct courts. The
head directs case teams under published material-risk/complaint and bounded source-audit
selection/conflict rules. Commissioner, minister, contestant or donor cannot pick a named case's
investigator/outcome. Predesignated qualified career substitutes handle conflicts/vacancies only
within prior assigned competence; no ad hoc patron or AI assignment. For allegations involving
the commission/secretary/finance head itself, preserve direct ordinary-court and existing
independent audit/integrity/prosecution routes with protected original access; implicated
leaders cannot finally judge or block those complaints.

The finance function receives timely reports and may require only records necessary to an
expressly enacted campaign source/value/coordination/state-resource duty, with purpose/period,
safe small-supporter custody and B review. Private-home entry, seizure, communications
interception or new unrelated account access requires its separate specific judicial authority.
It gives reasoned findings/referrals and requests lawful correction; it does not unilaterally
ban a contestant, withhold a due equal baseline, decide political truth, fine or invalidate
ballots/results. Serious penalties, proved disqualification and count/contest validity require
the competent independent court under exact enacted elements, fault/proof, causal relevance,
defense and appeal. No generic V authority invents missing campaign offence/penalty limits;
existing K offenses retain their own elements under P process. Technical reporting error is not
automatically a lost seat. Misused funds/paid distribution/records receive useful-time specific
relief without silencing unrelated lawful speech.

**EA14 — Campaign opportunity and funding survive conflict.** E16's five-calendar-day
qualification and48elapsed-hour baseline support follow first usable receipt/qualification,
shortened to actual opportunity; no serial document request resets them and official missing
records admit lawful alternative proof. The separate ballot administrator applies the
prospective objective rule; finance allegation alone grants no hold. Assembly enacts a bounded
access formula, due-date appropriation and independent challenge before contestants incur costs;
Treasury and access providers execute it without ideological approval. [EN/CF/ER](../10-democratic-entry-and-campaign-resources.md) now selects the national Assembly eligibility/support formula and explicitly amends E16 for that future scope: preliminary5days, final qualification5days after V−40 freeze, guide48hours, actual forum by V−21, with V earliest lawful voting. Current-called/other contests retain their own source law; no research number adopts itself. Actual prices, staff, media/court and funding remain unqualified. Parallel lawful referendum/status-vote campaigns need their actual committee/access
rules, not forced party labels. Missing money or delay is remediable harm and an unpaid claim,
not equal competition or presumed qualification.

**EA15 — Actual delivery source and hostile handover.** Assembly appropriates bounded recurrent
and poll-peak staff, local evidence, transport, accessible/offline venues, legal aid, technical
advice, courts, public support, standby/reserves and accepted civil execution under C06/NP. Core
scheduled election duties have the enacted standing-source/continuity route and direct Treasury
payment, never commission self-appropriation or a new tax/debt power. Price EA23
lay, practitioner and judicial time, plus CJ06, OJ06 and actual appeal peaks alongside other protected
work; one person/cloud/server/cash balance cannot cover simultaneous duties twice. No fines,
vendor gifts or predicted AI savings fund the office. Publish safe budget incidence, waits,
missing records/capacity and received remedies. Career staff have protected reporting and
law-bound command; actual source custodians and authorized civil/security executors, not a
parallel force, carry a lawful court/result/transfer order. If they refuse or lack cash/physical
capacity, preserve claims and expose the failure through direct courts/elected source action; no
label guarantees recovery from universal capture.

**EA16 — Direct ordinary review, CJ only in its own scope.** Applicants, voters, candidates,
affected organizations with proper authorization and other persons with ordinary standing can
challenge selection/qualification, O classification, assignment, records,
roll/candidate/access/certification or finance action through competent unconflicted ordinary
courts without serial internal exhaustion. Preserve first filing, urgent useful-time relief,
B17/E shorter safeguards, disclosed prior allocation and distinct ordinary appeal; no
commission, selector or reviewed vendor chooses its judge. A challenge is not an automatic stay.
Protected evidence must give a fair answerable case. Commissioner appointment/removal/recusal
and the commission's own funding/data cases are not self-adjudicated; current CJ12 does not
extend to them. CJ01 retains only the constitutional component/nationwide constitutional
disposition under its fixed votes; no ordinary appointment challenge creates a second
constitutional court. Review of **election-selection acts** follows this ordinary route,
including applicable legacy CJ06 acts. CJ12 retains its constitutional-judge selection
jurisdiction. Correct process/qualification or require lawful reconsideration, never appoint the
court's favorite commissioner. Select B16's 60-working-day appointment/exclusion challenge
period from usable publication or individual notice as applicable, extended for blocked
access/unfairness. B17/E useful-time relief remains earlier; this filing period is not a stay or
an incumbent term extension. Concealed fraud/material conflict remains reviewable after those
ordinary windows; valid prior acts are not automatically erased by a later appointment
defect—competent court states necessary bias/material-error reopening, third-party protection
and prospective correction under source law.

*Part of [EA01–EA25](../09-electoral-administration-and-public-control.md); proposed law, not enacted authority.*


[PC16–PC20](../12-continuous-political-finance.md) expressly extends these protected
career inquiry, separate access/payer and ordinary-review/paid-execution functions
to annual/local/referendum finance, without changing EA appointments/votes.
It supplies no new office-level ban, own-appropriation or political-truth power.
Actual PC source law, original evidence and receiving/court capacity are required.

[ECF01–ECF05](../../C06/11/01-readiness-trigger-and-paid-election.md)
distinguishes recurring readiness, the early-call release and paid
review and handover. EA selection, powers and actual legal deadlines
continue. A cutover or source dispute cannot reset a contest.
