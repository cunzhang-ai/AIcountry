# CW01–CW04. Give time, care and continuity practical meaning

*Part of [usable early care](../24-usable-care-time-and-shift-continuity.md).*

**CW01 — Assess an interval people can actually use.** For an S10
early-care offer, the local receiver identifies the child's needed dates,
earliest safe handoff, feasible pickup and the latest safe limit,
accessible journeys in both directions, and ordinary and exceptional needs. It checks
the child's sleep, relationships, communication and additional support
with the family, child as appropriate, and competent providers. Work,
study, necessary recovery, health, civic and other lawful ordinary
needs may affect timing. Entitlement retains S10's criteria, with
no employment, sponsor or family-exhaustion test. A request for one
necessary interval is eligible for assessment regardless of its share
of the week. Preserve existing assessed additional support and family
choice.

Use a family account, simple timetable or another proportionate route
to establish relevant time. A material conflict can justify targeted
verification and independent correction. Help a family without a
standard roster or digital record. Do not require wage slips, the name
of another employer or the reason for a protected activity unless
specifically necessary and lawful. Keep a usable date, time zone and
elapsed-time interpretation where needed. An ordinary request need
only describe the practical problem.

**CW02 — Offer intermittent care without a minimum weekly purchase.**
For assessed need within the adopted S10 scope, the local authority
offers the compatible existing place and hours first where safe. It
then considers paid extension by the child's known group, a qualified
shared late session or a stable accepting backup with safe travel.
Occasional episodes may use the latter without relinquishing a
daytime place. For regular use, select a stable provider and team,
with the child's and family's supported agreement. Choose by actual
journeys, relationships, rest, accessibility, safety and staffing.
Apply the NC ratios and qualifying presence at each interval for
organized groups. Individual or home-based care follows its own law
and appropriate safeguards. A provider may use economical minimum
shifts when safely funded, while an eligible family has no minimum
purchase, employer role or compulsory attendance. Do not duplicate
overlapping early learning or care hours in the service account.

**CW03 — Keep the child safely received during a change.** A confirmation
states accepting staff and place, coverage and handover times, pickup
and return arrangements, charged amount, safe contact and the activated
backup path. Use accessible communication and a protected offline
alternative. A late pickup, missed transfer or unavailable worker
triggers immediate safe supervision and the existing receiving,
safeguarding and assistance routes. The provider stays responsible
under its lawful duties until an authorized person or competent
receiver actually accepts. Pay that coverage. A changed schedule
creates no new detention, placement or parental-consent power.
Support familiar adults, meals, rest and an appropriate settling-in
period. Returning to usual hours keeps the ordinary place or an
accepted lawful successor. Do not repeatedly displace a child to
consume whichever opening is cheapest.

**CW04 — Finance the time difference at the public level.** Select the
same ordinary assessed family fee basis for equivalent covered care
within the adopted floor. No special family surcharge solely for the
eligible time of day or a provider's short-notice failure. Current
lawful charges and stronger protections persist until competent
prospective change with a matched cost and payer. Additional hours
and optional extras keep their separate lawful assessment and
price rules. Under FC, price qualified workers, actual contact,
noncontact and standby time, accessible travel, safeguarding,
support and the lost receipt from any displaced lawful fee.
Verify who benefits and who bears any tax or reallocation. Count
each payer and useful capacity once. Separate a benefit on the bill
from a child actually receiving care.
