# Design236 decision record. Technology transition and shared gains

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned Design235](baseline.json)

## Decision

Select [AT01–AT12](../country/C05/18-technology-transition-and-shared-gains.md).
C05/04 already chooses plural production, worker bargaining and a
joint account. EB pays a finite person-side bridge. MS and FB
protect lawful automatic stabilizers and elected fiscal action.
The added choice fixes national, local and urgent escalation
conditions, a timely elected disposition and a successor test
for collected source and qualified receiving. Use the
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

Act on observed harm or loss of control without requiring an
official AI attribution first. The 5% and 10% thresholds, two
quarters and 10/30/45-day terms are proposed parameters.
Voluntary paid-time improvements should not activate a loss test
through hours alone. Small, hidden or missing populations can
raise evidence independently. A timely correction can be due
even before the trigger is confirmed.

## Compare substantive responses

| Arrangement | Strong reason | Material exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Keep sector duties and ordinary budget response | Uses existing authority and avoids mandatory extra proceedings when harms are contained. | Separate payroll, earned-fund, qualified-service and revenue accounts can miss a joint failure or a concentrated local one. |
| Early person-side protection, limited bridge and durable elected source | Keeps receipt and bargaining working while alternative burdens can still change. Protects authority against a state or vendor model. | A broad base can miss mobile rents. Finite finance can buy time without securing a successor. Qualified work can remain scarce. |
| Target automation directly and share a universal payment | Makes the intended gain distribution visible and can reduce need-test burdens with a sufficient actual source. | Defining and collecting the base can fail or shift costs. Cash does not create qualified services. A fully sourced universal package remains a rival. |
| Public or mutual ownership of strategic capacity | Can change bargaining and access before collection. It may generate genuinely owned net returns. | Needs title, investment, maintenance, skilled operators, independent governance and a viable exit. Future dividends and assumed acquisitions cannot bridge current claims. |

Select the second response. Within its ordinary authority, use
negotiated time and capability changes, proven common infrastructure
or durable source reform where the evidence supports them. Keep the
ownership and unconditional options as whole rivals. No general
technology levy, new subsidy or tax rate is selected. The
[matched fiscal and staffing case](../settlement/technology-transition/02-two-quarters-and-protected-shifts.md)
selects a recurring package under stated assumptions, with a finite
bridge for due payments. It exposes a remaining gap under weaker
collection or loss of the qualified alternate.

## Authority, participation and compatibility

Treasury, Revenue and sector owners reconcile protected original
evidence. Independent evidence and audit functions and ordinary
courts keep their applicable access and correction. The Clerk
protects alternative costing and a timely Assembly disposition
within existing law. A budget vote is required to allocate
public burdens. Workers and employers control actual bargains
within law. People outside paid employment and affected regions
retain independent rights, participation and challenges. Models
may reconcile assumptions but cannot decide eligibility, cause,
pay, tax, consent or a public allocation.

Keep DB/MS/FB/RF, WL/TW, EB/I05/Y, existing source law and
independent sector mandates. Scope the new procedure and source
at lawful commencement. Preserve contracts, wage, worker, pension,
creditor and benefit claims, evidence and paid FT/OP/T successors.
No existing law or implementation changes by research selection.
