# Joint tax/income schedules and the cost of owned returns

*Part of [C06 national package](../20-national-economic-package.md); design 59 research.*

Keep [I01–I12](../../C05/06-income-floor-and-safe-resources.md) person-level
needs, safe resources and accrued claims as the current conditional candidate.
Compare its taper/tax combination with genuinely different pooled, universal,
and integrated credit systems under the same production, prices, ownership,
source duties and actual payment routes. Show gross positive taxes and
payments and the net result; merely netting old flows does not fill a gap.

The [credit alternative](../../../settlement/national-economy/02-packages-and-limits.md)
is a research rival: a 65%-of-gross-work charge less certified needs credit,
after a 10% contribution, leaves a 25% earned increment where its conditions
hold. That changes distribution relative to the 50%-of-net-income taper;
it is not an administrative refactoring or adopted tax parameter.
Evaluate leisure, work quality, actual care costs, privacy, income volatility,
nonclaiming people and distribution alongside marginal rates. A lower gap
does not prove better lives or sufficient supply.

Tax law, ordinary benefit eligibility, private records and payment decisions
retain separate owners and independent challenge. A future integrated
calculation could exchange a limited need-credit certificate rather than
clinical/family records, but needs lawful custody, correction and actual
non-digital access. It must not force a combined account or let a tax dispute
withhold an essential benefit. No automatic legal set-off of earned pension,
due support, another person's claim or unrelated tax arrears is created here.

Public/cooperative/private capital returns require a source-account trail:
gross output and costs; production taxes; depreciation and restoration;
net taxable income; actual corporation/personal taxes; retained maintenance
and investment; lawful declared distribution; owned share; currency and
cash received. A Treasury forecast is not a dividend declaration or authority
to strip an enterprise's replacement capacity. Actual ownership and control
need original titles, contracts and mandate, not a model's asset label.

For new public ownership, compare lawful purchase, compensated transfer,
voluntary participation and no change, including price, financing, foregone
taxes, risk and transition. Opening ownership is an explicit assumption,
not free evidence that the public already possesses the asset. A portfolio
may improve public capability or worker/consumer control without yielding
enough cash to fund all guarantees; disclose that distinction.

Use the C05 enterprise appointment, mandate, audit, worker voice and
independent market/court chains. Test whether one minister, party or vendor
controls jobs, housing, information, public dividends and complaints together.
Public ownership cannot substitute patron dependence for private dependence.
The Assembly controls material investment/tax mandates and loss limits;
neither an enterprise board nor AI chooses sovereign distribution.
