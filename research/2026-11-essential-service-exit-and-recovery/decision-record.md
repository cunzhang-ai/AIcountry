10 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned Design255](baseline.json)

## The institutional decision

Select [SC33–SC37](../country/C04/05/09-essential-service-exit-and-recovery.md).
Essential contracts buy a replaceable service. Keep core public
decisions, usable records and original claims accessible outside the
vendor's permission. Trigger handover preparation by the last safe
decision date. Transfer every safely accepted part, and limit any
lawful bridge to what is still necessary. Finance the whole transition
gross, including independently funded verification. Trace disputed
gains and actual recovery on a separate account with a separate close.

The existing duty owner specifies required receiving. The existing
sector executive chooses within SC12 and its enacted source.
The Assembly or competent elected body controls new authority and
money. Independent reviewers need direct lawful evidence, their own
paid scope and a challenge route outside the operator. Courts
retain existing orders, rights and review. The agency, not the
supplier, remains answerable to people through each handover.

## Observations, objections and alternatives

The South African judgments qualify several failures that can coexist.
Original service dependence exposed grant recipients after the contract
was found invalid. In 2018 the successor difficulty and late preparation
required an additional cash-only decision. In 2026 the Court reconciled
audited amounts and overlapping claims, and left collection to the
creditor process. A referee appointment was recalled in the intervening
proceedings after payment responsibility and administration disputes.
Those are different problems with different times and actors.
[Original judgments and scoped calculations](sources.md)

An immediate qualified transfer is simplest where it preserves all
duties. A gross short continuation may protect people when no other
route is yet ready but invites repeated dependence, broad control and
unverified pricing. Choose the qualified transfer plus an exact
residual bridge where it has a lawful, paid exit. A standing direct
public route is a serious rival where it can provide equal quality
and independence at acceptable operating cost. It still needs
separate independent review and actual accepting capacity.
[Matched first-budget choices](../settlement/essential-service-exit/02-residual-bridge-and-paid-successor.md)

The narrower bridge can burden staff, cost more in a short window and
be brittle if proof is shared. Early preparation and partial transfer
reduce exposure, but cost money even in normal times. Protect lawful
worker, provider and third-party claims. Avoid charging suppliers
arbitrarily for a public authority's own breach. Require prospective
terms, independently checked actual costs, contestable fee and any
security only on explicit lawful grounds. A competent court separately
determines disputed liability. No approved operation imports the South
African court's constitutional remedial power. No financial award
changes creditor ranking or manufactures budget cash.
