# Design511 decision record

**Basis and selection.** The [Design510 baseline](baseline.json) and
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) carry private, common and public production,
minimum rights, practical freedom of refusal and worker voice. Strengthen
buyer-side evaluation by showing independently usable work and essential
prerequisites for the same people. Where an ordinary market leaves lawful but
persistent dependence, select a funded elected choice of bargaining, sector
repair or a distinct independent provider as needed. Apply existing competition
merits and process to proved conduct or transactions. The [canonical choice](../country/C05/11/12-work-and-material-independence.md)
creates no offence, expanded investigation power or automatic structural order.

**Rivals and objections.** Compare enforcing wages, ordinary labour and benefits,
portable supports and stronger collective voice with separately costed direct
public or worker-led capacity. An existing provider may have genuine scale
benefits, and a purported independent entrant may share key control or lack
real work. Public and cooperative substitutes have the same evidence and
receiving burden. Confidentialities and family choices make a universal
linked file unacceptable. Use affected-party access, safe voluntary evidence
and appropriately bounded aggregates. None of these options makes the worker
responsible for proving a market diagnosis to receive earned or urgent rights.

**Evidence and migration.** The [sources](sources.md) and authored exercise
separate legal principles, externally observed mechanisms and invented
quantities. Continue all accrued claims, ongoing inquiries, original merger
clocks, election, complaint, wage, housing and residence rights. Existing
confidential individual exit and common-control relief retain their own law.
Three parallel internal review scopes do not establish public assent.

**Next decision.** Under a real adopting or local lawful authority, test one
actual occupational catchment. Have qualified staff verify ordinary rights,
existing hiring and each practically usable support, test alternatives with
workers and service owners, and price one exact elected and one structural
alternative. Reopen on evidence of false independent control, a better safe
offer, widespread suppression of worker voice or burdens moved to other
protected people. No delivery or superiority follows from the comparison.

**Political participation.** Extend the practical-time test to the first
contest. Select a prospectively funded residual rule for actual shift and
care constraints after ordinary voter access is assessed. Employer pay
applies to covered release. Separate election supply pays qualified
replacement care and scoped eligible time loss where no employer supplies
it. Protect abstention and ballot secrecy. The joint resource test covers
essential patients and the original polling cutoff; refusal or shortfall
invokes existing remedies. [Operative choice](../country/C03/13/09-usable-time-to-vote.md).
