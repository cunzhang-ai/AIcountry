# CRD05–CRD08. Failure and safe recovery

[Scoped credentials](../13-scoped-credentials-and-public-access.md)

**CRD05 — Bound offline reliance and reserved acts.** An offline
presentation may support prior specified low-consequence or
reversible tasks within the proof's actual scope and expiry.
The initial proposal caps offline use based on cached revocation
status at the end of 24 elapsed hours from the last qualified status check,
with shorter task, source and expiry limits controlling. The cap
applies only to classes separately authorized on actual risk.
Actual knowledge of a material compromise overrides a cached
status. No cached assertion alone changes political eligibility,
authorizes coercion, redirects public money or transfers legal
title. Apply actual sector authority, current independent proof
where needed, and B03/C12 notice and nonoverlap rules. Intake and
existing lawfully provisional protection can use other valid evidence.

**CRD06 — Isolate the affected use and issue usable warnings.**
On credible compromise of an issuer, verifier, key or source,
the responsible sector officer records the grounds, affected
purposes and time interval. Suspend fresh reliance within the
smallest material scope under applicable DP and sector authority.
Give scoped notice and review under earlier actual DP/PD/sector
clocks. Test the backup for the same source, controller and
operating failure. Preserve original evidence and safe proof of
unaffected acts. Neither an alert nor a technical directory erases
legal status, suspends an election or authorizes broader private
data access. Keep lawful alternate service and independent review open.

**CRD07 — Recover access without requiring the missing channel.**
The qualified source custodian and relying service offer a safe
direct recovery route, including assisted original evidence and
documented independent verification. A person need not produce
the lost device or obtain an abusive controller's consent.
Issue a scoped replacement when warranted, cancel affected
access and preserve prior lawful claims and dates. Give affected
people safe reasons, protective correction and usable direct review.
An independently verified new credential supplies only its own
scope. When originals conflict, preserve both and route the
material dispute to the competent decision maker. A model can
transcribe and compare. It cannot resolve legal status or assent.

**CRD08 — Reconcile the act before restoring reliance.** The
existing sector owner authorizes restoration with separately
qualified proof of lawful custody, accepted operations and the
relevant cause corrected or contained. Examine source, issue
time, actual authority, use and final receipt. Do not void a
proved prior lawful act or replay a settled payment on key
revocation. Block only the unverified consequential action in
its actual scope. Preserve a legitimate due payment or filing
and use a separately lawful alternate if available. A lost
credential, unavailable reviewer or signature alone resolves
neither an evidentiary dispute nor a recipient's lawful rights.
Use existing court, electoral and payment correction rules.
