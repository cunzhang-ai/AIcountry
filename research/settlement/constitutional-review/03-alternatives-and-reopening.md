# Constitutional review: incompatible choices and reopening

*Design 80; a bounded constitutional design choice, not proof of a best state.*

The problem is concentrated power without a usable remedy, including capture
of its supposed protectors. Judicial expertise alone, faster deliberation or
more AI agreement cannot solve it. Rights and democratic replacement are value
commitments; the causal protection of any institutional form is uncertain.

| Whole choice | Mechanism and gains | Control, transition and losses |
|---|---|---|
| Keep the previous research reference unresolved | Retain ordinary courts, plural appointment principles and D5 as a candidate while examining context | Avoids pretending nine seats fit every nation, but leaves no selected final voting/own-court architecture; cannot call that a finished country |
| Supreme ordinary-court constitutional bench | One preexisting final court supplies constitutional expertise, appeals and urgent routes; less duplication and potentially easier staffing | Its leadership, appointment and docket chain can concentrate both ordinary and constitutional final power. Requires exact assignment, recusal, finite term/ordinary-career reconciliation, plural sources and own-case external review, not merely changing the sign |
| Separate CJ court, selected conditionally | Specialist final constitutional jurisdiction and three source routes make capture of ordinary supreme leadership alone insufficient; ordinary urgent remedies survive some national failure | Needs paid primary/reserve and external ordinary capacity, public deliberation, counsel, source access and lawful execution. Fixed thresholds permit harmful nondecision; no source label establishes independence |
| Legislative finality with political rights scrutiny | Elected representatives retain final constitutional policy control, exposed to elections, opposition and public reasons | Fewer final judicial vetoes but weaker enforceable protection when a majority controls elections, speech or remedies. Requires a credible complete alternative protection chain; good aggregate outcomes do not substitute for replacement |

The selected mechanism preserves a final legal remedy without making the
ordinary supreme leadership its only owner. This is a reasoned research choice,
not an observed ranking. A small polity lacking enough unconflicted legal staff
may rationally select the supreme-bench rival; merely naming external judges
cannot fix scarcity. A legitimate public can reject CJ without accepting an
AI sovereign, and choose a complete lawful democratic alternative.

Retained objections include minority obstruction, professional clustering,
nominal opposition collusion, public-panel framing/nonresponse, too many paid
selectors, transition delays, and the conservatism of unresolved splits.
Personal independence can coexist with common class interests. Fixed national
interim expiry prevents indefinite provisional government but can restore a
harmful law before a final ruling. No metric averages these conflicts away.

Before any actual operation, obtain affected participation, independent qualified
review, exact lawful adoption, complete ordinary/special appellate assignment,
workload and paid coverage, actual source availability, protected records and
received enforcement. Existing claims and custody clocks cannot be sacrificed
to staff the new institution. The author cannot certify these conditions.

Reopen on systematic source blockade, common patron control, harmful unresolved
cases, inaccessible processes, frequent reserve exhaustion, judicial retaliation,
missing independent pay/records, failed civilian execution or a better supported
complete rival. Evidence should distinguish actual benefit, distribution, wrongful
coercion, usable opposition and peaceful replacement, not only average welfare.
