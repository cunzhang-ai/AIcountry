# One opening budget under three monetary arrangements

*Design482 · Constructed comparison, with the same claims in each branch.*

An existing jurisdiction proposes to change government institutions.
The inherited currency is L. The import agreement and foreign-law debt
are denominated in F. N would be a new domestic unit. These are
invented units, owners and deadlines. The Denmark–Copenhagen opening
accounts inform the choice to use a real predecessor baseline when
one is known. Denmark uses its own krone under a fixed exchange rate
policy against the euro. The reference supplies neither this example's
exchange rates nor a source of authority, infrastructure or reserves.

| Due | Owner and obligation | Usable source at the relevant cutoff |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Treasury owes 8 L in accrued wages. | 8 L held for payroll. Affected workers must be able to use their pay. |
| Day 2 | Health importer owes 4 F for an essential shipment. | 4 F settled in its eligible account. The supplier and clinical receiver still have separate acceptance duties. |
| Day 3 | Debt office owes 3 F on a foreign-law instrument. | 2 F held for debt. A contracted 1 F purchase arrives on day 4 in the stressed branch. |
| Day 5 | Treasury proposes a new optional supplier contract with a maximum cost of 3 N. | No N commitment is binding. Staged issuance requires a separate lawful monetary instrument, independently recorded operational release and an effective appropriation. |

The importer cannot lend its four units to Treasury and pay the
supplier on time. The debt office is short 1 F in each arrangement
unless a separate authorized source arrives by day 3 or the
counterparty gives a valid extension. The gap does not establish
that the creditor accepts partial payment. A favorable translated budget
total and domestic issuance do not cure the shortfall. Hold new
obligations that rely on the short line. Keep the debt, its original
date and any remedies or renegotiation within the competent route.
The budget can still appropriate separately executable lines.

## Choice before committing the new work

| Arrangement | Consequence in the example |
|---|---|
| Continue the lawful predecessor | Pay the 8 L and preserve established access if predecessor law, ownership, settlement, oversight and resolution all continue. Repair the 1 F gap independently or retain it as uncovered. This is the opening reference with these assumptions. |
| Adopt an external currency arrangement | Suppose an accepted F settlement provider exists, but wages still need L and the provider gives neither conversion liquidity nor bank insurance. An F account does not discharge wages. A priced lawful correspondent or replacement L route could close the access gap. External-currency use still needs a local loss allocation and remedy. |
| Start a bounded own-currency class | Keep the existing claims in L and F and carry their lawful payment paths. Permit only the separable new N contract after its own authorization and settlement rehearsal, with workable supplier terms, prices and funded overlap. Where the N route has no independently funded resolution or remedy, keep the contract unbound. N issuance supplies no F. |

An existing arrangement can also have harmful fees, poor access or
an unavailable settlement link. The test does not entrench it.
A sufficiently priced external alternative can reduce exchange
exposure for F-denominated purchases while increasing dependence on
a correspondent and external decisions. An own-currency regime can
add domestic policy discretion while introducing a second payment
route, new prices and new loss exposures. A broader cutover needs
actual evidence on those effects, affected people and external ties.
There is no selected real currency or conversion rate. Cost both
routes until an elected review of prices, recipient access and loss
exposure. An operator applies exact commencement conditions and
publishes affected lines, reasons and the date for challenge. It cannot
extend or switch a regime by withholding a certificate.

## One outage, two distinct questions

During payroll the provider accepts a file but cannot establish
whether a 2 L wage settled. A bank closure does not answer that
question. Carry the original wage claim and use independent finality
evidence. If the first instruction is proved void, a lawful alternate
can pay the wage on its existing terms. If settlement is final but
the worker cannot access their deposit, test the applicable scheme's
coverage and original failure date. Keep
the bank debt and any funded insurance payment as distinct legal
claims, with their respective denominations. If instead Treasury's
prefunding was trapped before wage settlement, Treasury still owes
the wage. Recovering Treasury's bank balance is a separate matter.
Where settlement status remains unknown, a distinct, previously funded
1 L emergency advance reaches the worker under an existing protection.
It gives the worker usable relief where there is an accepting merchant.
The wage remains under independent reconciliation without replaying
the instruction. Record wage receipt as unknown pending evidence, keep
the full unsettled exposure in view and report any verified late pay. Any recovery follows lawful review. An asset recovery
forecast supplies no emergency cash.

On day 2 the worker without a bank app needs a meal. In the
adverse branch the separate 1 L advance restores no timely merchant
access. An independently funded meal provider with one available
place accepts the person directly. This delivers the meal without
discharging the wage or the bank debt. If the provider has no paid
slot, record missed essential receipt even if a wage credit or an
insurance entitlement is visible. Allow direct assisted challenge.

If the predecessor route is itself unavailable, disclose each uncovered
line, enforce lawful available protection and bring the residual to
its competent elected or judicial decision. If the N contract already
became legally binding, a failed new route leaves the claim due.
Do not automatically move it to L, extend its date or revive an
expired system. Test the next payroll batch and actual access to
essentials, including without a bank app. AI can help surface currency,
calendar and finality discrepancies. Legal effect, a loss allocation
and each resident's claim remain decisions of the competent people.
