# Two routes and three works with one protected deadline

A neighborhood has two viable street routes, East and West.
East requires a two-day water renewal and a two-day fiber job.
They can work in a shared window with separate work areas and
technical approvals. West requires two days of planned bridge
maintenance, originally on days 2–3. Its lawful maintenance

pickup and temporary access arrangement for this trip. East
becomes the usable route again on days 3–4 if qualified checks
and reopening finish by the end of day 2.

Separate East jobs cost 7 and 6 units. The shared East window costs
9 and retains the required distinct utility and reinstatement
checks. West costs 3. A settled works and access source has 14
units. The usable alternate for the day 2 trip costs 2.
The example has two distinct qualified verification slots at
East before reopening. Reopening depends on both. A shared work
window leaves the existing independent network routes intact.

| Choice | Result |
|---|---|
| Keep separate East closures and each operator's original dates | East is closed on days 1–4 and West on days 2–3. Two days lose both routes. The construction alone costs 16. |
| Share the East window and keep West's original slot | The works cost 12, but both routes close on day 2. A saving cannot substitute for access. |
| Share East on days 1–2 and phase West to days 3–4 | Cost 14 with paid access. One route stays usable, the trip has a confirmed provider and West meets the stated deadline. |
| Same sequence with 13 settled units, a declined trip or one inspection slot | Do not count the combined route as ready. Qualify an affordable safe alternative or change the optional start before the window. |
| West must close immediately for a verified hazard | Use the separate emergency authority. If East cannot offer safe access, seek a real alternative and record unmet duties. The planned schedule alone no longer qualifies. |

If a proposal puts both critical services into one location and
a single interruption can remove both, its shared construction
cost does not qualify the resulting operation. Test a genuinely
independent backup or retain the separate network routes.
