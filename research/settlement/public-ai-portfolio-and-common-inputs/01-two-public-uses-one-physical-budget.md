# Two public AI uses and one physical budget

*Design465 · Constructed portfolio choice for two public offices.*

## Evidence and scope

A published field study of 5,172 customer support agents reports an
average 15% increase in resolved issues per hour after AI assistance,
with different effects across workers. Its commercial service
measures do not establish public merits, appeal, accessibility
or spendable savings. The IEA estimates all data centre electricity
use at around 415 TWh worldwide in 2024 and projects about 945 TWh
in 2030 in its base case. These figures are neither AI-only energy
nor evidence of capacity at an actual connection.
[Field study](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjae044)
· [IEA account](https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai/energy-demand-from-ai).

## Shared capacity and exact choices

A public support office compares improving assisted benefits intake
(A) and providing optional municipal language access assistance (B).
The human receiver decides what was filed. Neither proposal changes
eligibility, privacy rights, spending law or statutory deadlines.

There are 8 powered shared compute blocks in the ordinary window
and 6 under physical stress. Existing protected systems use 4 in
each. Capacity and use are measured for the same place, interval
and quality. The residual is 4 ordinary and 2 adverse. A and B each
need 2 in their full optional operating window. The tools can pause
on previously agreed terms. Their existing duties and any received
filings continue through protected human routes. There is no
assumed priority over another essential load.

The constructed baseline includes 8 qualified benefits merits
blocks and separately funds 4 distinct appeal and review blocks.
Existing human channels remain open. Other services' original
work and review keep their own budgets. For the improvement,
the Assembly can authorize 4 discretionary units for the period.
Amounts include the declared operator, checking and closeout work.
No forecast payroll saving enters this envelope.

| Exact motion | Ordinary result | Adverse and later obligations |
|---|---|---|
| A alone, cost 1 | Faster intake brings 11 requests requiring timely determinations into the window against 8 merits blocks. Three are exposed. | Tool suspension does not withdraw a received claim. Original deadlines, direct intake and funded appeal remain. |
| Split A and B tool purchases, cost 2 | Both fit ordinary compute. Keep the unspent two visible. It cannot buy the three merits blocks needed for the declared A load. | Both cannot run together in the stressed window. Hold a combined reliance promise on these terms. |

| R. Strong ordinary service and repair | Retain ordinary intake and due work. Bring any existing gap forward without treating new demand as a model problem. | Preserve protections, prior sources and correction on their own original clocks. |

P and Q can use the same nonpersonal definitions, accessible
language glossary, public test cases and portable connectors.
The respective sector custodians keep live records and decisions.
The public support office maintains only independently qualified
common assets on its voted scope, with documented revisions and
direct challenge. In this test choose a nonexclusive common layer.
A single compulsory runtime would concentrate failure and decision
influence. Fully separate local maintenance avoids that runtime
but may duplicate costs and leave smaller languages with weaker
coverage. Verify actual local access and both direct options before
wider reliance.

The 4-unit envelope cannot fund P and Q together. The models and
funders are hypothetical. The example assumes P's human merits
workers, independent reviewers and records do not depend on the
failed compute service. Q's human outreach is not proof that the
same people would file under both options. Pursue eligible people
outside either route and preserve the original comparison scope.

## Select a bounded use and keep the next call funded

Select P in the constructed vote given the earlier demonstrated
benefits deadlines, actual staff and usable fallback. Q is a real
alternative with stronger local language provision in the fixture.
The rule can favor Q when verified need and receiving differ.
Record the deferred B scope and keep the existing direct language
route. Do not claim general national superiority from that choice.

In a later adverse window the same 11 merits blocks are due.
An additional eligible person reached after an outreach round
adds a twelfth due case. The additional challenge raises independent
review demand to 5. P's 11 merits and 4 review blocks each run short
by one. Preserve the new claim and the original cohort on their
actual clocks. Recheck the later staffing before a new outreach
round. A separate qualified merits block is needed for the twelfth
case. P's original 4 review blocks cannot also cover the fifth. A separate
authorized 1-unit source and independently accepted reviewer are
needed if the review call is to be covered. The review unit cannot
also finance the merits block. If a source or person is missing,
preserve original appeals and intake, carry compatible work and
hold further optional widening. Report and seek correction for
the actual exposed case. No paid merits or model capacity can be
silently counted as an independent appeal. If a receiver actually
loses a filing during the return to ordinary operation, trace its
original date and remedy under the applicable rules.
