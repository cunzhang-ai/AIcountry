# Preserve voluntary care and fund the right to stop

*Design289 · HS14–HS18 · Proposed application of the home-support floor.*

**HS14 — Keep the assessed need visible when help is voluntary.**
The local care authority records the recipient's required support,
safe times and choices independently of who currently helps.
An offer of informal help can change the agreed delivery plan
only for tasks both the recipient and the helper freely accept.
Either can decline or withdraw separately. Confirm a helper's
actual ability, scope and availability without demanding a
particular relationship, household role or disclosure unrelated
to care. Silence, co-residence and past help do not establish
assent. Keep ordinary informal relationships outside formal
registration when the authority is making no delivery decision
that relies on them.

**HS15 — Give both people a usable way to change the plan.**
Provide accessible, confidential direct contact to the existing
local care authority, including outside routine office hours
where urgent needs require it. Accept notice of scheduled breaks,
sudden inability or withdrawn consent through either person or
an authorized advocate. Agree planned respite with the people
involved, using the assessed support and applicable law. When
agreed, reserve it in the ordinary service roster. The authority
activates its existing paid receiving route for the affected task
at its original useful time. Use the earlier safety and emergency
rules in AS16 when they apply. No renewed eligibility test,
proof of family breakdown or minimum notice delays needed
protection. A helper may seek support independently of the
recipient's clinical or personal decisions.

**HS16 — Purchase cover against simultaneous withdrawal.** Keep
accepted ordinary shifts and booked respite distinct from reserve
availability and activated work. Where a critical task relies
on voluntary help, name a qualified fallback, lawful payer and
last dispatch time. Buy independent reserve capacity for plausible
overlap, including two people relying on one helper, a common
infection or shared transport failure. Count skills, travel and
rest and the source site's continuing duties before dispatch.
Repeated calls require staffed replenishment. Give preference
to ordinary staffed delivery where interruptions recur or a
shared pool cannot reach people in time. Price workforce, pay,
standby and review under [WF100–WF104](../../C06/58/23-voluntary-care-and-national-capacity.md).

**HS17 — Record an uncovered need and preserve both people's rights.**
If paid receiving is short, use the existing lawful priority and
urgent review routes. Deliver independently safe support as it
becomes available. Do not cancel agreed respite as a silent
budget adjustment, assign a shift to a relative or presume
consent to an institutional place. Record any reassignment,
who lost access, the actual harm or risk, the next lawful
receiver and funded correction. A worker, recipient or helper
can challenge a false attendance, involuntary assignment or
missed support without a provider's permission. AI may flag a
duplicate booking or suggest routes, with no power to infer
consent, determine need, prioritize a resident or certify delivery.

**HS18 — Migrate plans without demanding renewed proof.** Identify
open decisions and rosters where essential support depends on
unconfirmed help. Preserve their need assessment, service dates,
existing wages, eligible support and pending remedies. Reconfirm
only what is required for a changed delivery arrangement.
Where lawful support has been withdrawn on an unsupported
assumption, review the original decision and restore available
timely care under existing powers. Store the minimum necessary
contact and schedule details with protected access. At programme
level disclose anonymized reliance, missed and late visits, respite
actually received, and gaps after both the first and second call.
Keep high-risk unmet groups visible. Reopen where purchase rules
cause coerced care, more missed visits or an avoidable shift from
chosen family life to formal services.
