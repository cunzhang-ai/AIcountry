# Necessary care: the person remains a legal agent

*Canonical UA01–UA03; part of [C08/23](../23-necessary-care-and-unknown-will.md).*

**UA01 — A decision, not a class of people.** Adults retain legal capacity and
all civil/political standing. Diagnosis, disability, age, poverty, homelessness,
legal status, an unwise choice or cognitive/AI score is no trigger. The question
is whether this person's actual choice about this specific act can be established
now after proper support, not whether an official approves their reasoning.
No finding presumes inability in another decision, future contact, vote or life.
B21/B22 child/young-person protections remain; UA's unknown-adult route does not
replace a young person's ascertainable supported bodily choice or child law.

**UA02 — Real support before residual power.** Provide accessible information,
independent advice, language/sign/assistive communication, suitable time/place,
familiar safe contacts and relief of avoidable barriers within lawful clinical
authority. Attend to behavior, emotional responses, routines and nonstandard
expression. A person who does not use speech may communicate clearly. Repeating
one inaccessible interview or an AI's low-confidence label is not support.

Document what was tried, what helped/failed, actual expertise, any likely recovery
of communication and the harm of waiting. Support remains ongoing during care;
it need not exhaust every hypothetical future technology or force invasive
investigation before a useful-time decision. Missing ordinary support is a
service failure to correct, not automatic evidence that will is unknowable.

**UA03 — Follow actual personal evidence first.** Distinguish current supported
choice; an authentic applicable advance direction/specific actual authorization;
a defensible interpretation from the person's own values/history; and genuinely
unknown relevant will. Check advance scope, activation conditions, authenticity,
revocation and uncertainty. A current ascertainable choice is not displaced by
an older helper's preference; a valid applicable refusal cannot be overruled
because it seems harmful. Disputed applicability has urgent independent review,
not automatic forced treatment or withdrawal.

Familiar people may contribute evidence without owning the answer. A marriage,
inheritance interest, clinical role or general care plan supplies no body-wide
mandate. If evidence supports several incompatible interpretations, preserve
them and reasons; no averaging, model consensus or universal supposed wish to
live supplies the person's consent. Legal sufficiency of any prior authority
uses its actual source law, not a new retrospective power under UA.
