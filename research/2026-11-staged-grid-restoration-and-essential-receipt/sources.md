# Source scope for Design259

The ENTSO-E Expert Panel's final report of 20 March 2026 reconstructs
the 28 April 2025 blackout using evidence from system operators.
Chapter 5 is the primary source used here. Its operator records are one
event evidence family. The summary page and prior factual report are
publication context rather than independent corroboration. The original
PDF is preserved byte for byte and pinned in the source manifest.

- Sections 5.3.1 and 5.3.2, pp. 364–369 and 372–373, establish
  separate Portuguese startup attempts and local restoration areas and
  the 18:36 CEST first synchronous connection through Spain.
- Section 5.3.1, p. 371, identifies the Portuguese transmission endpoint
  and the last delivery point.
- Sections 5.5.1–2, pp. 381 and 386, distinguish the two systems'
  transmission and distribution endpoints and describe the roles of
  cross-border support. The 0 MW figure in §5.5.2 describes capacity
  settings in that stage and is separate from the synchronization result.
- Public distribution restoration endpoints carry no independently
  demonstrated clinical, water, communications or electoral outcome here.
  Inference is limited to the need to retain distinct receiving dates
  and independently verify each service.
