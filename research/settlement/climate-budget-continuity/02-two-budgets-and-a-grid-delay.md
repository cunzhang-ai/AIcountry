# Two budgets and one delayed grid connection

[Climate budgets](README.md) · Authored comparison

The gross domestic CO2 caps are 100 and 80 for two adjoining
periods, with a cumulative cap of 180 through the second.
All emissions are in one consistent invented physical unit.
Near the end of the first period, independently measured
emissions total 94. The replacement line is delayed. At a
specified peak the protected service needs 12 MW of firm
delivered supply. The grid can deliver 9 MW without the old
unit or qualified substitutes in the declared conditions.
Firm MW at this peak is distinct from annual energy, household
bills and overall grid readiness.

| Package for the remaining first-period window | Added cost | Period gross | Firm MW at the protected peak |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Retire now with current accepted supply | 0 | 94 + 4 = 98 | 9 |
| Keep the old unit for the window, if separately lawful | 4 | 94 + 12 = 106 | 12 |
| Repair existing supply and procure independent reserve | 8 | 94 + 6 = 100 | 9 + 1 + 2 = 12 |
| Buy two qualified independent substitutes | 9 | 94 + 5 = 99 | 9 + 3 = 12 |

The declared amounts cover the same short window with actual
accepted capacity by the last safe start. Continuing costs,
protected household and worker claims and the later line are
assumed funded in the common baseline. Both qualified options
include an accepted end and transition to the line. If the delay
outlasts the window, a fresh actual path is necessary. The old
plant package is shown for comparison. This example supplies no
permit or dispatch authority. Under the assumed site conditions
the repair and both substitute routes meet other applicable
floors and accepted service in the passing cases.

With 8 available units, select repair plus reserve, the only
listed feasible package under both first-period tests. At 9
the lower-emission substitute package is also feasible and can
be chosen if its extra cost is justified. At 7 no listed package
meets both requirements. If the repaired 1 MW is unavailable
at the peak, the 8-unit package delivers 11 MW and fails service.
This calls for a funded alternative or a recorded gap under
existing law. Cash and a passing emissions number do not supply
the missing megawatt.

## Preserve the cumulative account

If actual separately lawful urgent operation takes first-period
gross emissions to 106, record the 6-unit overrun. The second
period's nominal cap is still 80. Its initial usable ordinary
headroom is min(80, 180 − 106) = 74. A second period using 75
would pass the nominal period cap but end at 181 and miss the
cumulative cap by 1. A period using 74 returns to the cumulative
ceiling of 180. The first-period overrun remains in the record.
A verified first-period gross result of 98 instead leaves 82
under the cumulative calculation, but the second period remains
limited to 80. Under the selected gross cap, even a separately
verified removal would not erase the first-period overrun.

If original independent evidence revises the pre-decision 94
to 96, keep the old vintage and show the revised account. At
the same costs and dispatch, the two firm replacement routes
now project gross 102 and 101. Neither is a complete compliant
plan on those facts. That factual correction differs from
amending the ceiling. It reopens the choice and timely service
protection under applicable law.
