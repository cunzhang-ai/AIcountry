# Keep the service funded when the tax base shrinks

*Design480 · Constructed fiscal comparison.*

Retain the same ecological limits, standing right to safe heat,
ordinary remedies and effective access in both paths. Assume a
standing three-unit heat and maintenance duty in every period and
finite additional retrofit work of three, one and zero units in
the first three periods. Existing repair duties and paid workers
keep their lawful terms. The case stipulates settled levy receipts
after authorized refunds and collection costs in every period.
Treat the figures as assumptions about the covered base and
collection schedule, rather than as national emissions or a forecast.

| Fiscal path | First three periods of net levy receipts | Remaining cash after costs | First period with zero receipts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slower abatement within the same caps | 6, 6, 4 | 0, 2, 1 | Still owes 3 from a durable source. A lawful carry can cover one such bill only. |
| Faster abatement within the same caps | 6, 4, 2 | 0, 0, −1 | Still owes 3. A separate one-unit reserve could bridge the earlier gap once. |
| Finance the standing duty from a qualified recurring source | 6, 4, 2 on the faster path | Standing cost paid separately. Finite levy-funded use is restricted to 3, 1, 0. | Standing cost stays covered if the recurring payer and service qualify. |

Compare the recurring source's incidence, restricted levy uses and
forgone projects with lawful retention of existing sources. Do not
credit the unused levy as recurring revenue or assume further
appropriation. Assign lawful environmental liabilities, reserves and
actual services separately. If an individual relief obligation can
lawfully end because their need ends, verify that change and leave
continuing maintenance, binding contracts, open claims and
remedies on their own clocks. Preserve low-income and indirect
account access under the applicable rules.

Select the independent recurring source for the standing service
on these assumptions. For the finite work, verify cash before
binding, price completion and safe closeout, and finance any
necessary fallback in advance. Apply the earlier timing rule
where a new charge is conditional on actual protection.
Do not prefer slower abatement to protect receipts. Report a
faster-than-expected fall in the covered base at the next useful
elected correction, with no automatic change in tax, ecology or
entitlement. If sustainable financing is unavailable, the elected
body must address the original service gap and keep lawful
alternatives available. Existing protections retain their scope.

## Compare the same worker-owners across the two recurring sources

In a separate branch of the [four-year budget](../recurring-revenue-and-budget-choices/01-four-years-and-a-real-revenue-choice.md),
the mixed source M and the progressive personal source P both
settle seven general-account units in a weaker year. Seven is
required for care, earned backing, independent public control and
bridge interest. Assume that under M, a lawful shift from wages
to owner distributions leaves earned contributions one unit lower
and triggers one extra unit of authorized general backing. M's
seven receipts have not already been reduced for this backing.
M is one short. If P's separately verified path leaves earnings
and contribution cash unchanged, P qualifies on this criterion.
A funded revision of M can still compete. Do not assume P is
immune to similar shifts or infer a fiscal event from a contract
label. If M's reported net source already accounts for the same
loss, make no second deduction. Keep earned claims, classification,
refund rights and the two actual payers distinct.
