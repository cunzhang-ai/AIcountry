# C07. Necessary care needs an accepting clinical network

2 November 2026 · Design 123 · HN01–HN12 candidate, not enacted law

**Select local first-contact, continuing and urgent care backed by paid visiting,
regional and external specialist routes where they are safe and actually usable.**
A small national population cannot be treated as proof of local specialist capacity.
Existing S01–S04 coverage, consent, useful-time duties and public residual liability
continue. A foreign contract, AI consultation or funding approval is not treatment.

| Canonical part | Country choice |
|---|---|
| [HN01–HN04: clinical responsibility](19/01-local-care-and-accepted-referral.md) | Local accepting networks, named clinical responsibility, real specialist scope and failure |
| [HN05–HN08: usable access and finance](19/02-patient-costs-and-public-purchasing.md) | Paid travel/support, advance access, public finance and replaceable contracted capacity |
| [HN09–HN12: control and transition](19/03-correction-transition-and-rivals.md) | Independent correction, private records, exact source succession and genuine local/visiting/network rivals |

This uses the existing national health purchaser and clinical/local delivery
chains, not a new referral sovereign, insurance gate or international authority.
Elected law chooses material general coverage and fiscal burdens. Qualified
clinicians determine actual indication/urgency with patient will; courts and
independent clinical review retain exact powers. No model certifies a safe service.

[The joined needs/resource reference](../C06/46-life-needs-and-paid-capacity.md)
prices an authored 220-case additional external-treatment route at 6.738 m CU,
including reservation, specified access/transport and named staff. Actual partners,
foreign law/currency, clinical safety, dependent-care/earnings replacement and full
case mix remain unqualified. This is neither an accepted quote nor adequate annual
reserve. Retained local care is not automatically cancelled when a route is added.

[HG clinical governance](05-health-service-and-patient-control.md) now identifies
the state NCS public employer/head/networks, purchasing/access function and
outside Chief Reviewer/ordinary courts used here. HN actual route/journey/
advance/consent/foreign source and180-day new-duty start remain, no reset by
HG's later organization/method or new patient/insurer gate.
