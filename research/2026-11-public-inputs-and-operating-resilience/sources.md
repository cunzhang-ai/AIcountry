# Original sources, inspected scope and nontransferred authority

3 November 2026 · Research notes; not clinical instructions or an enacted standard

## WHO storage/distribution guidance

[WHO TRS 1025 Annex 7, Good storage and distribution practices for medical
products (2020)](https://www.who.int/docs/default-source/medicines/norms-and-standards/guidelines/distribution/trs1025-annex7-%281%29.pdf).
Original PDF sections 1–2,12.26–12.37,13,15–16,18.37–18.41 and19 were inspected.

The guidance distinguishes usable from damaged/expired stock, calls for expiry
rotation and recorded stock reconciliation, product-appropriate storage,
qualified equipment/personnel and defined outsourced responsibilities. It
excludes patient dispensing and does not cover pharmaceutical starting-material
distribution or all manufacturing rules. No universal buffer duration, dose,
national demand, adequate procurement price or medical substitution is supplied.

Our use is bounded: these distinctions inform SC25–28 and abstract failure
cases. Qualified product-specific annexes, actual clinical/regulatory judgment
and national law remain necessary. The WHO catalog also identifies a later 2022
remaining-shelf-life publication; its full text was not inspected, so the 2020
document is not called the latest comprehensive rule. No independent original
stock observations, inspections, patient records or actual qualification occurred.

## Contemporary pooled-procurement/stock proposal, not imported law

[Council of the EU, 12 May 2026 provisional-agreement release, updated 30 June 2026](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/12/critical-medicines-act-council-and-parliament-reach-provisional-deal/).
Read the original public-procurement, collaboration, contingency-stock and
next-steps sections. This is a distinct source family from WHO technical guidance.

The reported provisional agreement combines resilience-related procurement,
collaborative purchasing and transparent proportionate stock safeguards, including
voluntary reallocation. The release still describes endorsement/formal adoption
as subsequent steps; no current final legal force is inferred from that record.
It is a policy comparison, not measured effectiveness or a source for AIcountry.

The older Council background page and this later release describe different
negotiation thresholds. No threshold is imported, combined or silently treated
as one final rule. Critical Medicines Alliance's original page returned 403;
we do not claim to have read its full report. No foreign pool membership,
manufacturer, stock, export permission, funding or enforceable remedy transfers.
