# Retain a competitive electoral form; improve its operation

Equal standing, practical opposition, enforceable remedies, public control and
peaceful replacement are requirements. Incidence, comprehension, task times and
available staff are empirical questions; the model does not observe them.
The diagnosis is a possible concentration of source, dispute and attention work,
not an established excess of political choice or proof that citizens cannot cope.

| Whole choice | Mechanism and tradeoff | Research disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Retain current rules and independently expand every task | Separate final decisions, all information and ordinary direct courts; fewer sharing/privacy dependencies but more repeated source work | Credible fallback; retain its resource sensitivity, no presumption that reuse is necessary |
| Retain national PR/person preference with scoped support, voluntary common issues and plural information | Reduce compatible duplication while retaining individual facts, choice and fresh decisions; adds source-custody, mediator and coordination risks | Conditional operating reference, not an actual administrative mandate |
| Closed national lists | Same list proportionality, shorter person-choice task and party responsibility; party leadership controls the elected order | Do not select without an affected public accepting lost personal choice; real rival, not inherently illegitimate |
| Four equal ten-seat regions | Smaller local field, territorial representatives and local administration; smaller district magnitude and boundary power | Do not select for an attention claim alone; territorial/accountability benefits remain contestable |

A finite example gives each region A10,000/B9,000/C1,000 votes. Four ten-seat
counts return A20/B20/C0; one forty-seat national count returns A20/B18/C2.
A dispersed five-percent current loses all regional representation in this example.
It proves a possible tradeoff, not the actual geography or universal superiority
of national PR. In a two-seat A60/B40 example, the optional preference elects A2
where closed party order elects A1. List shares alone cannot settle member control.

Keep EL entry thresholds, two active endorsements per person per body, nominee
limits, source/intent law, exact count and current deadlines. No signature/deposit
increase, ideological test, official AI shortlist, new election postponement or
automatic case-dismissal fee is selected. Regional, party-responsibility and
no-sharing choices can displace this reference on better evidence or public choice.
The cost of a full field cannot resolve these value disagreements by arithmetic.
