# Voluntary pregnancy assistance and payment without ownership

*RP05–RP06; part of [C08/20](../20-reproductive-law-and-parentage.md).*

**RP05 — A two-stage adult arrangement, never prenatal surrender.** Permit
prospective third-party pregnancy assistance by adults 18+ through a licensed
clinical/legal process with private independent advice for the gestating
person and separate advice for intended parents. Identify actual material
contributors, bodily risks/uncertainty, source/consent, post-birth status,
care/support roles, money, records and accepted receivers before clinical use.
The same autonomy/post-birth protections apply whether or not the gestating
person contributes an egg. No child guarantee, approved-family ideology,
partner permission or existing-child requirement arises from this clause.

All clinical choices remain the pregnant person's under RP01/RP02. A clause
requiring abortion/continued pregnancy, fetal testing, a delivery technique,
lifestyle surveillance, compelled confinement or mandatory child surrender
is unenforceable; no specific performance, debt penalty or care/immigration
threat enforces it. Intended parents must understand this before choosing
and committing resources. An independent adviser checks actual understanding,
coercion and practical safe exit, not whether a poorer person deserves choice.
Poverty/disability/family form alone disqualifies nobody; due I/S support
must be available irrespective of participation, continuation or withdrawal.

After birth, RP07/B32 require actual independent post-birth transfer consent,
30-day revocation and a reasoned child-court order. A signed prenatal plan,
a delivery event or a clinic report cannot replace that stage. A supported
agreed temporary care plan can operate while status is heard; no adult owns
a child or can force another into unpaid personal caregiving. Direct urgent
child protection has B31 grounds, counsel, receiving and review, not a shortcut
to obtaining the child for an intending family.

**RP06 — Pay real burdens, not for obtaining a child.** Allow documented
reasonable clinical, travel, access, legal and necessary care costs, actual
lost earnings and a publicly legislated time/burden allowance available on
equal terms for comparable contribution. No uncosted claim that pregnancy,
care or donation is free labor. Reconcile actual income/support once under
I/B; a future promise is not a received resource. Any private arrangement
states affordable sources, due intervals, independent custody and remedies.

For the candidate, the gestation allowance is one I02 ordinary adult monthly
basket per 30 days of actual pregnancy assistance, prorated, plus clinically
needed recovery time up to 90 days on the same basis. Necessary aftercare,
proved lost earnings and injury claims retain their distinct grounds beyond
that allowance. A donor's time allowance uses actual reasonable time at the
published national median hourly earnings, not genotype or number of children.
For the same donated hours, pay the greater applicable time/lost-wage amount,
not both. The pregnancy burden allowance is distinct from proved wage loss;
no actual expense or lost-wage item is paid twice. These are provisional value formulas,
not calibrated fair prices. Material changes require Assembly law with actual
burden/equity evidence; no preferred-child price or secret tariff. After-tax
received contribution/time allowances and replaced earnings use I05's E
retention branch for the needs test, not full O withdrawal. Exact cost refunds
match the same recognized cost once and are not extra usable income. This
classification neither makes pregnancy an employment condition nor creates
a tax exemption, compulsory work or public coverage of the private promise. Income
review uses minimal protected proof of actual amount/cost and receipt, not
a general demand for clinic, gamete, origin or genetic files. Necessary fraud
inquiry still needs specific lawful scope, source safeguards and review. It is owed for actual contribution/time
regardless of pregnancy success, disability/sex, miscarriage, abortion,
post-birth consent or a dispute. No recovery/bonus tied to surrender or
withholding future healthcare. Legitimate advice/clinical administration may
charge transparent proportionate service costs; no brokerage fee per child
acquired. A licensor's authority does not authorize sale or compulsory labor.

Before a new clinical arrangement, independently held funding/security must
cover its agreed contribution/care/aftercare obligations and a funded failure
receiver, not a guessed child price or insurer payout. If absent, do not
start a newly proposed commercial service on a promise. Existing pregnancies,
people born and due care/support still receive lawful public protection;
private default creates actual claims, not automatic limitless state indemnity.
