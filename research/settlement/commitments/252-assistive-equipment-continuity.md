# Design339. Fund assistive equipment through its service life

[AEC01–AEC05](../../country/C07/05/05-assistive-equipment-continuity.md)
pays qualified fitting, repair, alternate equipment, independent service
and necessary replacement through a named public payer. It keeps valid
warranty and recovery claims separate from usable funds.

The [decision](../../2026-11-assistive-equipment-continuity/decision-record.md)
keeps warranty-led and public-workshop provision in comparison. Actual
appropriations, local capacity, user choice and restored function remain open.
