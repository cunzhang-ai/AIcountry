# RF05–RF09: pensions are current payments and continuing rights

*Part of [C06/36](../36-recurring-finance-and-pension-commitments.md); design 89 candidate.*

[Design157 Y17–Y24](../../C07/04/06-pooled-source-and-life-course-comparison.md)
retains these rates/old units/source dates and clarifies the complete G credit
plus remaining backing in36 authored stationary scenarios. Common-factor
method notice applies only new share elections; no RF24-month ceiling/date
reset, actuarial-price claim or restricted S-to-G source follows.

**RF05 — Select pooled, tax-backed insurance.** Retain Y's public defined
earned benefit, uncapped 10% labor contribution, general-revenue care credits
and explicit public backing. The IC ordinary foundation and Y basic match once;
earned/survivor, access and clinical claims remain additional. Four-month usable
cash protection is liquidity, not a fully prefunded individual investment promise.
Reserve investments require actual lawful sources, custody and risk authority.

Reject compulsory funded-account conversion as the default fiscal repair.
Diverting contributions creates a present hole while old pensions stay due.
Any genuinely funded rival needs both the closed legacy transition and paid
new assets, custody, loss, access and fees. A G bond held by S is simultaneously
G debt; its coupon cannot finance both accounts from outside the country twice.
Voluntary additional saving remains possible under ordinary financial law.

**RF06 — Select a lower prospective ceiling with notice.** New service after
the Act's express commencement earns 1.25% annually on a benefit base capped at
`1 × W` rather than`2 × W`; W remains the independently estimated same-year
full-time-equivalent median annual earnings. Contributions above the ceiling
remain explicit solidarity, not promised units. Care/illness/unemployment credits
retain Y06's 0.5W target and actual eligible fraction, without duplicate wage credit.
Other earned/service, survivor and ordinary-basic rules remain unchanged.

The candidate notice rule is the first 1 January at least 24 months after enactment;
the Act states the actual date and split service periods. This is a proposed
source-law change, not a silently adopted second-year start. No ministerial
capacity certificate can delay an enacted date or erase old units. Y16's ten-year
protection against age increases remains; this change does not raise age.

**RF07 — Preserve earlier units and their original law.** Record each prior
service/credit period, eligible base, annual earned unit, revaluation basis,
claimant, obligor and survivor contingency. Revalue the old unit under its source
indexation; never apply the new ceiling to its past base. Within Y’s same 48-year
scheme, best 48 selects the highest revalued old-two-W/new-one-W units.
Separate inherited, foreign or other-source vested claims keep their own source
accrual/duration rules and are not capped at48 by this migration. A new lower unit cannot replace a
higher old one or reduce already accrued entitlement. Keep the full correction
history safely, including periods outside the selected48. Missing evidence
leaves a disputed/unknown claim, not an invented zero or an AI-final classification.

The cap reduces future benefits of people above W relative to the old candidate,
while preserving median/care-credit examples. This is a distributional choice,
not neutral efficiency or immediate cash saving. Younger high earners lose some
expected future replacement at unchanged contribution rates. They retain equal
political standing, objection and lawful revision routes. The two-W rival remains
substantive if its full public burden is openly chosen and actually sourced.

**RF08 — Cash must exist before the first due payment.** Pay Y10 in advance,
with four months of scheduled earned/survivor/admin cash still usable after
ordinary due payments. Compute each event's opening usable balance, actually
received pre-due sources, due cash, post-due reserve and next receipt date.
An appropriation, long bond, annual surplus or expected month's contribution is
not day-one cash. A contribution office cannot delay a pension to meet a buffer.

In the selective-tax fixture, annual earned/survivor/admin is249.783 million CU,
the next monthly due20.815 and four-month post-due reserve83.261. If opening25
is actually usable and nothing else arrives before the due payment, the initial
cash seed is79.076. It includes due-period working cash, not just58.261 needed
to raise an undated25 balance to83.261. It is neither another annual benefit nor
GDP. If receipts remain after each due day, the pre-due working cash cannot
simply be reclaimed at year-end while claiming the same post-due protection.

Any funding before day1 needs its own actual contract/date, first-year coupon,
fee and counterparty. The annual frozen model's end-year loan offers do not
demonstrate that source. The79.076 comparison is a conditional minimum before
those extra financing costs; it defeats a complete-source claim, not a right.

**RF09 — Continue generations and explain transfers.** The75-year account
starts with actual active workers' prior units, current retirees, pending/foreign
claims, credits and existing survivor pots. Age transitions, deaths, births,
entry/exit and work histories change contributions and benefits; do not freeze
15,000 retirees forever or initialize every active person with no past rights.
Derive each G care-credit transfer from 10% of actual additional credited base;
the old 6 million line implies60 million base, not a perpetual free subsidy.

One death creates at most Y09's one capped actual-lost-support survivor pot.
Existing500 pots are a stock with eligible recipients and expiry/death rules,
not500 new permanent pots every year. Beyond year75 retain the accrued closed
claims and long-lived survivor tail, with assumptions/unknowns explicit. Show
cash gaps, discount sensitivity and who pays; a present-value summary, funded
account conversion or automatic life-expectancy rule cannot cut statutory rights.

The [authored cohort comparison](../../../settlement/recurring-finance/README.md)
now follows75 annual periods plus a bounded accrued-Y tail. Under its stated
history/mortality/birth assumptions, required G backing before reserve in year 25
is308.640 million CU with two-W versus273.683 with one-W after notice.
Those are unpaid source requirements, not projected actual taxes or full public
costs. First-year half-cycle expected payments are a different timing fixture
from FD's full annual known claims; their difference is not a reform saving.
Actual foreign/inherited claims, future age-specific care and complete national
sources remain unqualified, outside the bounded fictional-Y tail.

company/VAT/land/transfer claims are no pre-due S cash or duplicated G source.
