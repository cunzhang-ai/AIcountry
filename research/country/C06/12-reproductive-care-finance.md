# C06. Paying for reproductive and early-life care

*Part of [C06 public finance](../C06-public-finance.md); integrated design 76 research, not enacted law.*

The Assembly appropriates a public reproductive-health package within
the ordinary clinical budget and identifies coverage, provider payments,
transport, emergency capacity, newborn and postpartum care, safe records,
independent complaints and continuity after provider failure. It tests
actual staff, facilities, medicine, blood supply, transport and work
time by place. A contraception visit, completed birth service, safe
abortion, infertility intervention and newborn follow-up have different
resource paths; a pooled budget line does not make each one available.

Infertility and new technologies need an explicit coverage and
opportunity-cost choice with fair reasons and appeal. Public finance
cannot purchase consent from a donor or gestational person or turn a
child into a transferable asset. Household income, paid leave, housing
and childcare support are separately costed through C05/C07; they may
help people choose family life but cannot be credited as a guaranteed
rise in births or as an automatic pension solution. No tax, benefit,
immigration or employment rule may penalize a person for a lawful
reproductive choice through an undisclosed demographic target.

The [C08 legal route](../C08/06-reproductive-autonomy-and-parentage.md)
sets consent and status limits; the [C07 service route](../C07/09-reproductive-health-and-early-life.md)
defines clinical delivery. C06 reports expenditure, service received,
unmet lawful demand, staff shortfalls and remedies separately. No real
population, fiscal account, staff roster or clinical outcome is known.

[RP01–RP16](../C08/20-reproductive-law-and-parentage.md) adds the specific
legal candidate, with different owners/resources:

| Continuing/new duty | Actual payer and missing increment |
|---|---|
| Confidential ordinary/urgent care | Existing clinical purchaser/network provides accepting care, transport and follow-up; no pregnancy or identity dispute suspends a due service |
| Pregnancy/donor burden payments | Actual private obligors/security pay agreed statutory contributions, noncontingent on surrender/outcome; public payment needs separate enacted coverage. Formula amounts are hypotheses, not existing cash |
| Material/origin custody | Licensed custodian and independent identity service need specimens, source links, protected records, backup and accepted closure transfer; no mandatory genomic database or unpaid family's archive |
| Intended support and post-birth orders | Child/civil services supply child representation, separate adult advice, actual review and safe receiving; private obligations count only when received |
| Estate/foreign/novel-method disputes | Judiciary, registrars and clinical specialists need specific competence and actual source/foreign review; a treaty/project label supplies neither money nor authority |

Never count the same medical cost, wage loss, allowance, support or child
service twice. No blanket public guarantee covers a failed private broker,
every stored embryo, every future technique or an unavailable donor. Existing
I/S/B urgent receiving and lawful claims persist on private default. The new
increment's quantity, price, contingency, funding and practical independence
remain unknown; old country bids/SF standby do not qualify this package.

RP06's E-branch treatment preserves I05's retention for actually received
contribution/time compensation; exact cost refunds are matched once. Count
the public income-floor incidence and actual taxes/source payments explicitly;
no new macro saving or full qualified national cost follows from this label.
