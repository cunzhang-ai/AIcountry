# Design397. Make public asset disposal an accountable whole choice

[PL13–PL20](../../country/C06/64/04-public-asset-disposal-and-control.md)
requires an independent comparison, an elected mandate, safe actual
handover and a separate capital receipt. Preserve the public's
ordinary governing power and existing enforceable claims.

The [depot comparison](../../settlement/public-asset-disposal-and-control/01-three-options-for-one-depot.md)
tests dated cash, retained income, handback and residual assets.
Actual title, costs, authority, readiness and outcomes remain open.
