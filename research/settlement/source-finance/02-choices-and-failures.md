The hybrid's normal bank liquidity counts actually marketable sovereign
paper at the authored 80% value; absent an actual usable market it has a
102.4545 shortfall. At 60% bank-deposit withdrawal the conditional liquid
shortfall is 61.333833. At doubled required yield, an otherwise unchanged
fixed-coupon perpetual price halves; capital remains positive but liquidity
is 32.4545 short. No automatic facility, rollover or bailout fixes these cases.
These are conditional sensitivity results, not observed market outcomes.

Treasury's total cash can look sufficient while standby is earmarked. The
hybrid separately keeps ordinary active cash nonnegative after legacy 80,
a whole year's 10 remittance withheld and one more unchanged reference bill:
15.608333 remains, with standby untouched. That is a cash boundary, not
proof of next-year taxes, bank income, labor, output or pension adequacy.
Two gross public periods without receipts breach the declared 100 cash floor;
a one-period backup cannot promise perpetual crisis financing.

Changed ownership changes public dividends and modeled available consumption.
Required GDP 4263.485653 is a demand/account condition, not achieved output or
an improvement over old 4266.887252. Whole-country physical, external, future
rights and independent delivery tests still apply; arithmetic does not settle
risk preference or popular control. Neither AI agreement nor portfolio size
is actual consent to this financial architecture.
