# Decision record for Design460

**Baseline and procedure.** Design459 and the [research method](../FRAMEWORK.md)
define the baseline. [Baseline hashes](baseline.json) preserve the
predecessor. Existing fiscal, election, service and review rules keep
their own authority.

**Selection.** Put durable receipts, reprioritization, bridge and
repayment in one elected comparison. In the constructed budget,
select the mixed source with an independently financed timing reserve.
Compare the broader ordinary source and a smaller source with a
genuine spending release. Retain ordinary repair wherever qualified.
[Selected rules](../country/C06/62/10-recurring-revenue-and-budget-choices.md).

**Reasons and objection.** The owner-income-only proposal has favorable
central receipts but fails the declared lower path. A mixed source is
also exposed to common deterioration and can be less attractive than
the progressive ordinary source or credible reprioritization. Only a
specific verified incidence and capacity comparison can settle those
choices for a real adopting public. The bridge consumes interest and
displaces a capital project. The timing reserve must be separately
available when needed.

**Compatibility and consumers.** Retain FB, TN, DB, MF and the lawful
earned-payment scopes, each authority and original claims. Keep
repayment and restricted funds on their own dates. Applicable existing
review rules and genuine institutional alternatives remain. A forecast
revision carries no new appropriation.

**Limits and reopening.** Danish releases support only the cited
accounting observation. The receipts, costs and votes in the example
are authored. Actual incidence, collecting and paying authority,
independent review, capacity and outcomes are open. Reopen on changed
collection, a proved offset, missing principal finance, a second call
or a better funded alternative. The next real decision requires a
complete dated source and delivery account for the affected payers.
