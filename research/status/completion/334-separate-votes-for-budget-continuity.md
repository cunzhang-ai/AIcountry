# Design426. Give a timely continuity appropriation its own vote

[BCV21–BCV26](../../country/C06/60/12-separate-votes-for-budget-continuity.md)
protects a separate decision on existing services, an exact funding
and expiry scope, and independent disposition of the whole budget.

[Worked continuity vote](../../settlement/separate-votes-for-budget-continuity/01-one-service-and-a-rejected-budget.md)
tests a rejected budget, a separately supported duty and the remaining
fiscal choice. Actual voting, funding and receiving remain open.
