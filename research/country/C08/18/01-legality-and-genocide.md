# Defined law and specific genocide intent

*G01–G02; part of [C08/18](../18-international-crimes-and-accountability.md).*

**G01 — Legal scope.** G02–G05 and G06's express incitement/attempt rules
define the candidate's four international-crime families. Prosecution proves
every material/context/fault element and disproves a properly raised defense
beyond reasonable doubt; a label, casualty estimate or government designation
is not proof. P01–P20 and B/P source, custody, defense and review protections
apply unless an expressly identified G rule is more protective or narrowly
different. No inference from silence, rank, ethnicity, association or diagnosis.

This text and the linked closed act schedules are the proposed operative
national definitions; preserved external legislation is evidence/comparison,
not another lawmaking authority. No updated treaty, judgment, AI policy,
foreign statute or model changes an offense automatically. Unknown future
conduct/meaning receives explicit interpretation, not analogy that invents
a crime. Strict interpretation benefits the accused where real doubt remains.
Civil protection and other existing offenses retain their independently proved
grounds if a G context is not established.

The domestic natural-person schedule applies to conduct at 18 or older.
Ordinary conduct at 16–17 retains P05 child proceedings and its five-year
aggregate maximum; under 16 conduct cannot be convicted. This is a national
choice, not an inference that ICC jurisdictional age mechanically binds
every domestic court. Children recruited or coerced remain rights holders,
with funded safe receiving and no punishment disguised as compulsory care.
Child means under 18 here unless a row states a narrower threshold. Prove
criminally material victim/actor age beyond reasonable doubt; a protective
service's cautious age assumption is not automatic conviction evidence.

No new international-crime conviction of a legal body is created here.
Actual culpable natural persons can be prosecuted; an entity's independently
proved K offense/Q07–Q09, civil responsibility, asset recovery or lawful
regulatory correction remains. Neither corporate registration nor AI use
shields a person, and no employee is automatically guilty for the entity.

**G02 — Genocide.** With the specific intent to destroy, in whole or a
substantial part, a national, ethnic, racial or religious group as such:
intentionally kill members; cause serious bodily/mental harm; impose
conditions of life calculated to cause physical destruction; impose measures
intended to prevent births; or forcibly transfer children to another group.
Prove the protected group, targeted substantial part where relevant, act,
causal result/circumstance and destructive intent. Serious harm follows K01/
P02's actual danger/lasting impairment, not disagreement or presumed insult.
Biological or physical group destruction is distinct from lawful cultural
change, migration or merely disapproved politics; forced acts keep their
own independently proved protection/penal route even without genocide intent.

Context, orders, repeated acts and original records may support an inference
of intent, open to contrary proof. Numbers, an AI's predicted demographic
effect or a political resolution alone do not establish that mental state.
Political/social groups outside this specific genocide definition do not
lose protection: G03 persecution, ordinary harms and civil equality retain
their scope. The candidate does not settle possible future nonhuman personhood
or erase unfamiliar affected groups by an administrative classification.
