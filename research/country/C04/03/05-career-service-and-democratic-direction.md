# Career administration under democratic direction

These candidate clauses implement the [ordinary merit and direction rules](01-civil-service-and-rulemaking.md)
for national career administration. Bodies with distinct appointment,
removal or decisional protections follow their own law. Elected local
bodies can adopt compatible safeguards through their own lawful authority.

**PSV01 — Assign policy, advice and execution to distinct roles.** The
responsible minister sets lawful policy within enacted powers and
appropriations, gives a dated direction and answers to the Assembly for
its effects. Publish the decision, authority, cost and reasons subject to
specific lawful protections. Career managers owe candid advice, an
implementation plan and execution by the lawful dates. Record material
risks and alternatives. A professional objection goes to a named official
or competent review route. It does not suspend a lawful policy by itself.
Where authority is genuinely disputed, apply the ordinary urgent
determination route to the disputed act and continue severable standing
duties. Statutory individual decisions remain with the designated officers.

**PSV02 — Keep merit enforceable at the point of appointment.** Each
agency runs accessible competitions against published job functions and
evidenced qualifications. A qualified majority of the selection panel
for senior operational posts comes from outside the appointing and
reporting chain, drawn by a published rotation from a standing
cross-agency assessor roster. The Assembly appoints roster members
after an open, published merit assessment and recorded hearing. The
reference rule requires three fifths of all seats and one third of the
non-government members as recorded at the opening of the process,
rounded up, with a nonempty second group.
Use staggered four-year terms. Removal before expiry requires an
ordinary court order for proved serious misconduct or incapacity under
the adopting law. The Clerk assigns and replaces conflicted assessors
through the published rotation. Use unexpired qualified roster members
and lawful temporary cover during a vacancy. If no independent panel
can be formed, hold the affected selection and publish the gap. Current
management and lawful services continue on their existing authority.
The service employer may select from the panel's qualified shortlist,
including where the Cabinet already has that selection power, and
gives recorded reasons.
Use independent review for a biased specification, assessment or
appointment. Give employees ordinary protection and due process.
Disclosed political advisers may leave with a government and have no
standing authority over career staffing or statutory individual cases.

**PSV03 — Follow actual functions through a reorganization.** Treat
related changes to classification, reporting lines, acting appointments,
case assignments, payroll, work access and operational contracts as one
change where they affect the same function. For a material change in
who controls a statutory duty, the sponsor gives affected workers and
service owners notice and publishes a safe implementation statement
before effect, ordinarily allowing ten working days to inspect and
seek review. Identify the lawful owner, reasons, staff, access, due
services, elected objectives, funding and handover dates. Continue
routine management under ordinary authority. Genuine abolition,
redeployment, discipline and new delivery methods remain available
under applicable law with their own worker and service protections.
Where immediate existing-law action is necessary for safety, state its
legal basis, narrow scope and expiry, and provide prompt review.
No reclassification, contractor or sequence of acting appointments can
supply a power reserved to qualified career or protected statutory posts.

**PSV04 — Keep a qualified, paid route for vacancies and disputed control.**
The agency names lawful temporary successors for time-sensitive roles and
tests separate access to records, payroll and delegated execution. Acting
authority follows the statutory trigger, scope and expiry for the
substantive vacancy across changes of title or holder. At expiry use the
lawful alternate and continue standing career duties. A vacancy or
challenge does not confer a power to appoint or fund additional cover.
Where qualified cover runs out, record the affected duty, payer, original
date and recovery decision. Give genuine change a staffed path on its own
authority and timetable. Preserve ordinary pay, claims and accepted work.

**PSV05 — Give each side an enforceable route before the opportunity goes.**
Affected workers and applicants can reach the ordinary employment or
administrative review forum under the adopting Act. The minister can
seek prompt administrative review or lawful management correction for a
specific obstruction of a lawful instruction. Residents can challenge
missed duties directly under AP and related sector law. Assembly
members use parliamentary evidence powers for systemic obstruction.
A competent court can preserve the minimum access or function needed
in time, direct a lawful rerun or execution, or order other available
relief. A filing creates no automatic stay. Protect prior notice,
confidential evidence, timely reply and independent review of any
urgent order. Review an assessor's conduct through ordinary law with
an unconflicted replacement. A reviewer cannot appoint a preferred
minister, adopt a policy, or create a budget.

**PSV06 — Separate the budget, records and actual correction.** Vote
a named source for roster work and the existing forums, with release
through lawful Treasury and judicial or employment administration
routes outside the challenged reporting line. Publish scope, workload
and any proposed cuts in time for Assembly scrutiny. Protect original
directions, assessment records and service queues under purpose and
retention limits, with a preassigned alternate custodian for disputed
access. External audit examines the money, process and common
controllers. It does not select candidates. The public employer names
the unconflicted executor for each lawful remedy and verifies restored
access, pay or service through the next useful deadline. Where funding,
access or delivery fails, show the remaining breach and the elected
or judicial correction still needed. Separate a missing service from a
disputed appointment. Use [cumulative democratic review](../../C14/08/04-cumulative-impairment-of-democratic-control.md)
where measures threaten an actual protected democratic function.

**PSV07 — Protect equal access and review the system.** Publish aggregate
selection, temporary coverage, delay and service effects with safe
disclosure. No party loyalty test, applicant reputation score or compulsory
AI account may enter a career competition. AI may help compare published
criteria and identify gaps in staffing or records. A named qualified
person checks evidence, issues reasons and remains answerable for the
decision. Roster design, terms and funding remain open to lawful elected
change and external challenge. Reopen the safeguard if the roster
becomes a closed profession, review repeatedly arrives late or a
simpler route preserves equal access and delivery under a hostile
minister or management team.
