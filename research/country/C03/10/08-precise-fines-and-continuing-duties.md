# ER18–ER20: narrow VD12 and paid prospective transition

*Part of [C03/10](../10-democratic-entry-and-campaign-resources.md); design101 research, not enacted law.*

**ER18 — Prospective narrow monetary duty, not V by analogy.** Select new **VD12 class B only** for a contestant principal, actual donor, public-asset controller or genuinely independent paid third-party controller with actual statutory control/ability who knowingly or recklessly: (i) after call fixes usable limits, accepts, provides, routes, uses or knowingly retains beyond CF07’s five-day correction opportunity a CF07-prohibited entity/foreign/public source or an above-cap natural-person benefit whose attributable same-controller value in this contest is at least **B*/20**; (ii) incurs, directs or materially coordinates actual CF08 paid campaign expenditure above the applicable national/district/independent-third-party cap by at least the greater of **B*/20** and **5% of that cap**; or (iii) deliberately materially falsifies/omits a legally due sponsor, beneficial-controller, source or paid-value record so as to conceal a qualifying (i)/(ii) amount. Aggregate split payments/controlled agents once on actual evidence, not similar viewpoints. Smaller/ordinary mistakes remain subject to precise civil return/correction, not class B. An independent court decides each exact act, fault, value/attribution and fine under V06/V07/P defense and appeal; no custody, automatic ballot ban, political-speech offense, volunteer/press ban or K23 expansion. Reasonably disputed valuation, unknowing receipt promptly returned before use, ordinary journalism/personal unpaid advocacy, lawful public grant and noncontrolling similarity of messages are excluded. Class A for correctable records after V06's 20-working-day notice is a rival, but adds little pre-vote deterrence and could punish harmless errors; retain civil correction instead. The charge period is V08's six years for B (concealment only its proved ≤three extra), same-course Q/V anti-stacking and zero-minimum means safeguards.

**ER19 — Fine ceiling choice and no double value.** Existing V06 class B individual ceiling is 180 Q02 means-based units; body Q08 middle ceiling is the larger of 5% verified defendant revenue or twice *net illicit monetary gain*. That may be weak against a party/corporate spender whose electoral advantage is not monetary gain. Enact an **express VD12-only alternative body upper ceiling** of the larger of the existing two bases **or twice the proved unlawful campaign value**: the prohibited received contribution *or* the actual above-cap outlay/directly controlled benefit, counted once for the same traceable flow/course. A knowing prohibited contribution used during the contest is not made lawful by returning it after the vote; an actual timely pre-use return and later restitution matter to liability/proportional amount under their exact rules. The court may fine zero and must consider proportionality, actual assets, worker/creditor/public-service effects, restitution and V08 same-course/K finality; no annual forecast revenue, political-benefit valuation, automatic group turnover or office funding from fines. The added value ceiling is a real increase to Q08/V06 law **for VD12 only**; existing Q08 does not already cover political reach. Rival: retain Q08 unchanged to avoid severe deterrence/chilling, while admitting intentional wealthy overspend may outstrip the fine. No penalty finances pre-vote access or proves that a bought advantage was reversed.

**ER20 — Migration and observation.** Adopt only prospectively before nomination with actual EA02/EA13/EA16/E15/Treasury/provider scopes, appropriated baseline and credit maximum, trained staff, original rolls/assignment/payment records and independently usable courts. Price the V−33 guide and V−21 actual forum against the **actual corrected two-active/EL06 entry upper bound and distinct nominee supply**, not a guessed 200 or a hidden cap; preauthorize one lawful 90-day repeat poll plus its court, public-access and subsequent C03/06 formation period in C06/04's standing source, with published cash and workforce tests. That source is a bounded appropriation, not proof of cash or a new caretaker spending power; another failure needs competent public finance action and cannot silently postpone the poll. Preserve prior-called contests, valid grants, donor contracts and lawful speeches, pending claims and exact old penalty rules; no new VD12 retroactivity. Publish qualification denials/reversals, practical pre-vote guide/venue/media reach, grants actually received, concentration of privately paid reach, protected voter-credit assignment errors, court orders executed by original due dates/before V, and separately during V…P as mitigation, postpoll claims and overdue cash. Reopen the threshold/calendar, support mode or court/penalty choice on measured exclusion, coerced private assignments, coordinated vendor capture, unaffordable public credits, chronic reruns or a stronger complete rival. No actual electorate, media prices, qualified roster, source cash, court delivery or superior democratic outcome is proved.
