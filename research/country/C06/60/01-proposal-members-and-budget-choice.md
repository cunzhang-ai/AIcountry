# DB01–DB05. The government proposes; elected members can change the offer

*Part of [democratic budgets](../60-democratic-budget-and-execution.md).*

**DB01 — A whole offer with an honest predecessor.** Retain Cabinet preparation,
Assembly national appropriation and C03 member/public proposal rights. For the
first actual calendar financial year under the new method, require the baseline/
whole-package alternatives at least150calendar days before year start, and a
complete Cabinet budget proposal at least120days before. This prospectively
strengthens the pinned manuscript's120-day baseline/60-day proposal minimums;
it does not erase them from history or change any actual tax/source year.
Use AA's separately authorized calendar reporting reference, RF ten-year cash/
current/capital/debt path and Y's own longer claims, not a new five-year limit.

Show retained law and due obligations, proposed changes, full public offer,
local obligations, tax expenditures, public entities/guarantees, physical/skill/
foreign/source exposure and paid transition. Distinguish estimates, authority,
actual cash and received outcomes; no frozen benchmark or forecast AI gain
funds a promise. NP15 annual alternatives/disposition duties remain, including
its rule that a breach alone does not invalidate an otherwise lawful budget.

**DB02 — Member access and costing without an expert veto.** The Assembly's
existing Clerk/administration assigns qualified career budget analysts through
its own lawful merit staffing, with an explicit source/assistance budget and
protected equal member access, not Cabinet appointment/control of their analysis.
Existing finance/sector committees coordinate scrutiny; publish material
minority reasons and source disagreements. Any actual member may seek a scoped
costing/source reconciliation and propose an exact budget/amendment; no minister,
party, committee chair or analyst certificate is an admission condition.
Give a scoped reasoned response within seven calendar days: existing usable
evidence, safe lawful access, actual gaps and proportionate analysis/help/date,
earlier when otherwise useless. Reserve actual capacity for minority/individual
alternatives; a Cabinet-first queue cannot exhaust it. A response is not a
guarantee of complete modelling or permission to reject an uncosted proposal.

Treasury/sector authorities provide original lawfully accessible data, versions,
methods, existing commitments, assumptions and reasons for withholding. Protected
personal/security records use qualified safeguarded access, not indiscriminate
publication or secrecy from all outside scrutiny. Publish the request queue,
limits and reasons; shared sources/controllers are disclosed. Missing evidence
is unknown, not zero. Costing may be unresolved; members can still vote under
actual law with uncertainties and burdens visible. Court/AA/source access is
real and paid; an analyst/model cannot choose spending, tax or a debt mandate.

**DB03 — Alternatives reach an actual sitting.** Clerk registers exact member
alternatives/amendments and lists a timely request within seven calendar days
independently of Cabinet/committee permission, using C03 protected agenda and
lawful Speaker/Deputy/available prior-law sitting arrangements. Listing is not
adoption. Existing constitutional sitting/election/formation rules prevail;
a missing presider, quorum, evidence or deliberation is no fictional vote.
For an ordinary annual final-budget vote, Clerk gives at least21calendar days
public schedule notice. A material member alternative received at least14days
before that vote gets the DB02 response and an actual protected opportunity to
present/contest its text and material objections before the final vote. It may
share related scrutiny, not disappear after Cabinet business or receive merely
a post-vote listing. Moving the vote earlier cannot defeat a timely request.
A material Cabinet change after that deadline needs a fresh real opportunity
and source response for affected alternatives before approval, not transferred
assent. Actual prior-law urgency retains its own limits, no new DB urgency power.
Preserve the reasons and response for unexamined alternatives under FA; grouping
common reasons cannot erase a distinct material burden or conflate exact texts.
Agent copies/one member's many submissions create no extra seats or unlimited
simultaneous hearing time. Record actual capacity failure and supply paid repair.

No minister-only financial initiative is selected. A proposal changing totals,
tax, debt or standing law states the exact competent legal changes and full
consequences; a reduced programme cannot be presumed a lawful reduction of a
benefit claim. Assembly can revise its prior proposed fiscal totals through an
express recorded decision under RF/FP, not a permanent first-vote lock or a
mechanical balanced-amendment rule. Coherent lower-cost/retain choices remain.

**DB04 — An exact budget needs elected approval.** Choose, for national annual
appropriation and material supplementary allocation, floor(N/2)+1 actual yes
votes and at least that many actual members present, N the full statutory
Assembly including vacancies. No casting vote, AI majority, abstention, proxy
inference or financial certificate fills missing votes. This is a specific
proposed budget-law rule, not DR's30/45 calendar or a general amendment of all
primary-law votes; actual higher-law compatibility/holds require coordinated
adoption. Publish the exact voted schedules, changes, sources and effective dates.

Ordinary target is a final recorded disposition at least14calendar days before
year start. A late or rejected budget starts no new authority; actual lawful
approval can still occur later under DB15. A Cabinet defeat on an appropriation
is not automatic dismissal, investiture or term extension; actual C03 confidence/
caretaker/replacement rules apply separately. Assembly may adopt a member's
lawful alternative while a caretaker continues within its actual powers.

**DB05 — Understandable scrutiny, not a financial plebiscite by account.** Before
ordinary votes provide usable complete proposals, source changes and enough
actual scrutiny time under existing legislative law, including excluded/local/
worker/service-user views and genuinely different whole offers. Exact technical
annexes and safe private-source review accompany plain accounts of who gains,
pays, loses time and bears failure. Late material amendments disclose changed
incidence/dependencies and need fresh actual scrutiny; no old review transfers.
For a material joint source and protection proposal, put the same original
household and payer calendar behind each exact option. Give opposition and
affected nonrecipients usable access to the source burden and opportunity
cost. Explain disputed assumptions and whether a material change reverses
protection. A qualification failure supplies no expert veto over an
otherwise lawful vote. Scope operational release under its own law.
DB creates no automatic ten-day/DR standstill or extra emergency exception.

Future speech/shared situations/simulations/agents can explain choices while
interpretation, delegation, consent and elected votes remain distinct. No
mandatory dashboard/life graph, AI optimum or consumption-weighted franchise.
Hearing/publication alone is not affected consent. Paid plural explanation,
non-digital access and useful outside challenge must survive a hostile Cabinet.
The timetable is a hypothesis with real workload cost, not proof of democracy.

evidence for releases that depend on disputed facts.

gives the Assembly a separate renewal choice for new targeted support.
Compare alternatives and retain lawful old claims before treating an
