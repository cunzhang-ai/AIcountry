# Original observation scope and unsupported matches

4November2026 · No personal child records, interviews or private testimony

Raw responses, complete metadata and exact requests are retained. Population,
childcare payroll/enrolment and municipal accounts have related administrative
origins but distinct definitions. Nine tables are not nine independent trials.
The comparison obtains no independent causal or affected-party validation.

All values concern2023, not an outcome of the future country. Source vintages
are kept separately. Four age-stock means are not exact annual child-years;
no headcount/FTE ratio calibrates actual participation or age-specific uptake.
BOERN2 component sums preserve+1 rounding residual; private sums preserve−1.

[Childcare presentation](https://www.dst.dk/en/Statistik/dokumentation/documentationofstatistics/childcare-before-school-start/statistical-presentation)
describes annual gross FTE including noncontact work and managers, care-category
rather than exact NC birthday bins, and agency reporting only from2024.
A qualification label does not certify NC04 competence, availability or
relationships. Its original HTML was read, not treated as an actual room audit.

[Childcare processing](https://www.dst.dk/en/Statistik/dokumentation/documentationofstatistics/childcare-before-school-start/statistical-processing)
identifies BOERN8's separate student/manager/early-transfer/social-staffing
adjustments. BOERN1 does not apply the same adjustments. BOERN10's5977 early-
kindergarten units are not an exact split at NC's third birthday. Do not divide
children by BOERN8 to invent baseline staff or treat excluded support as surplus.

[The original2023 release](https://www.dst.dk/da/Statistik/udgivelser/NytHtml?cid=49704),
published30October2024, explains the newly adjusted2023 reporting scope.
Its2.9/5.9 are reference annual ratios, no evidence of uninterrupted qualified
presence or our own law. Later2025 headline ratios do not replace2023 observations.

The private-table metadata expressly omits care periods outside the residence
municipality and warns of different validation. No comparable public/private
staffing ratio or complete private coverage can be obtained by pooling these
counts. Raw original labels and missing cells are retained; credential or
provider categories are no actual guarantee of receiving.

REGK31's LOBM price definition is1000DKK despite the generic metadata unit '-'.
Divide by1000 for millionDKK.52514 is the consolidated institution function;
other functions, family/private arrangements, capital and source/transfer
counterparts remain outside. Gross29.861942bn +signedincome−9.064656 equals
net20.797286. Wages24.289653 are part of gross and not separately added.

REGKC group092 explicitly mixes parent payments with possible sibling/free-
place subsidies. Its five ownership entries are disjoint;0 means not specified,
not an aggregate total. Sum sales−7.593474bn is not all household payments,
a fee-abolition price or additional national revenue. No mismatched average
cost/pay per observed FTE is used to price model task equivalents.

The previously selected NC/S10/TW rights are normative;40/48, ratios/groups
and actual presence are retained. Full normal-offer group choice, stationary
population,10% preparation,44 scheduled weeks/110break/64learning/10%unexpected
loss are explicit planning assumptions, not measured2023 calendars/attendance
or a new legal leave/preparation rule. Extra needs,6–12, later cohorts, migrant/
unregistered protection, non-group care, individual receiving, current wages/
suppliers/qualified cover and paid transition remain unpriced.
