# Danish custody in 2025. One operating account and a separate programme

## An operating year with nominal underspending and actual pressure

Danmarks Fængsler's 2025 annual report, tables 1, 2 and 11, records
the following for operating account §11.31.01. Amounts are million
DKK in the report's current prices. Receipts are displayed as
positive here. The report's March 2026 sign-off states its
appropriation figures include the then-proposed 2025 supplementary
appropriations. The Folketing later adopted the 2025 supplementary
appropriations law on 25 June 2026. That formalization is distinct
from the report's sign-off and from receipt on any particular day.

| Operating account | Budget as reported | Outturn | Outturn less budget |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Expense | 5,026.3 | 5,073.0 | +46.7 |
| Revenue | 183.8 | 238.5 | +54.7 |
| Net expense | 4,842.5 | 4,834.5 | −8.0 |

The net variance comes from revenue exceeding the report's budget
by more than gross expense did. The report attributes much of
the increase in external operating income from the prior year to
a changed classification of payments from the immigration agency.
That year-on-year account is separate from the same-year variance.
It cannot be used to assign all of the 54.7 variance to that change.
Fines and the separate grant accounts do not enter the operating
outturn above. The reported carry-forward is 519.9 at year end.
It does not identify an uncommitted recurring source.

The same operator reported average use of nominal capacity at
102.7%, above its policy target of 95–96%, and continued shared
occupancy of cells. It reported a net gain of ten prison-officer
and 31 transport-officer annual equivalents during 2025. The
reported overall staffing measure of 4,891 has a wider scope,
including staff beyond prison-officer roles and a distinct count
for Greenland. Its 2026 budget figure of 5,211 is a plan. Neither
figure is an actual compatible night roster. The independently
audited 2025 rest problems and the October 2026 response qualify
different parts of the same operation.

## Keep the political agreement, legal acts and actual operating results distinct

The June 2025 political agreement combines tougher penalties for
certain serious offenses, alternatives and reintegration measures,
changes intended to reduce some pretrial custody, construction
and workforce initiatives. Its programme table counts continuation
of prior programmes and avoided reprioritization reductions as
well as new measures. A separate table presents the corrections
appropriation profile. These are different perimeters and must not
be added. Figures below are **million DKK on the agreement's
2025 price-and-wage basis**. Keep them apart from current-price
outturn and agency forecasts. The figures are a planned source
and cost path, with important obligations after the agreement ends.
[Agreement, tables 1–3](https://www.justitsministeriet.dk/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Aftale-om-strafreform-og-kriminalforsorgens-oekonomi-2026-2030-1.pdf)

| Published planning figures | 2025 | 2026 | 2030 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Programme cost | 187.1 | 1,703.9 | 3,146.5 |
| General reserve share | 187.1 | 1,675.8 | 3,006.0 |
| Financing expected within corrections | 0.0 | 28.1 | 140.5 |
| Separate corrections appropriation profile | 5,190.7 | 5,502.4 | 6,329.9 |

For 2030 the reserve and expected internal share sum to the
published programme cost. Without the expected internal 140.5,
that part of the plan needs a different source or a lawful change.
The agreement provides for a budget analysis to identify later
internal financing. It does not establish that the release was
achieved. The agreement also identifies separate costs elsewhere
in the criminal process, from 38.5 in 2026 to 49.6 a year in
2027–2030. Existing courts and receiving services therefore remain
part of the resource question even when the corrections account
balances. Do not infer a free transfer between these accounts.
[Agreement, tables 2–3 and budget analysis](https://www.justitsministeriet.dk/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Aftale-om-strafreform-og-kriminalforsorgens-oekonomi-2026-2030-1.pdf)

The Folketing's finance committee record identifies the approved
December 2025 budget item 128. The original February 2026 Act 263
implements parts of the agreement, with different original effective
dates under §14 and subsequent amendment noted by the publisher.
Approval, enacted law and actual deployment remain separate.

This existing arrangement makes a strong comparison because it
