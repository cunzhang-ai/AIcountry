# Secure the first night without passing an unsettled bill to the resident

*Design540 · Constructed opening-budget exercise and observed failure mechanism.*

An adult is ready to leave hospital and cannot safely return home. They
accept an accessible room near their usual care team. The resident can pay
two housing units each period after other essential expenses. The room
charges seven units on arrival and seven at the next due date. An anticipated
allowance could cover five per period. Its eligibility is unresolved and
the resident has no cash to advance those five. A separate due transport
and care package costs three per period, fully paid from its own budget
with named workers. The window boundary is the last day to confirm the next
safe place, which precedes the second charge. All figures and deadlines
here are assumptions.

| Offer | Gross housing cost over the two periods | First-night and next-period effect |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary charge-bearing referral with prospective allowance | 14 provider charges. Resident incurs 14 liability and has four available. | The room and care can operate, but the person cannot safely rely on payment approval. If the award is denied, the ten-unit uncovered balance becomes private arrears. Reject this as an affordable public placement. |
| Corrected ordinary income or a cheaper suitable room | The ordinary payer releases the verified five per period by each bill date, or an actually suitable room is confirmed at two per period. | Both can work if verified and accepted in time. The second period needs its own confirmation. Neither path exists in the base branch. An arrangement available on the second date cannot cure the first charge retroactively. |
| Publicly purchased or guaranteed affordable temporary room | 14 to the provider, with four affordable resident units and ten public housing units. | Commit five housing units per period and have the provider accept the split before occupancy. The resident keeps separate income and care rights. This works even if ordinary support is denied, provided the two dated public installments and separate care are secured. |

In the base branch only five public housing units are available at the
first deadline. Fund one period on those exact terms and disclose its expiry.
The second period needs another timely five, or an accepted suitable lower
charge or ordinary payment path. Compare at the booking deadline, rather
than after a second liability could bind. If no source qualifies, retain
the first period's protection, escalate the next housing gap and use any
independently lawful safe alternative. No charge for the public share
from the protected first period moves to the resident. Do not claim secure
placement for two periods when only one is funded.

If the first cash pickup overlaps the accessible ride, ask the ordinary
cash payer for an accepted alternative pickup or staffed delivery in time.
Keep the affordable room and compatible care bookings within their lawful
hold while testing that change. A cheaper room that requires dropping the
existing care visit is a different offer. Count the independently due cash,
safe arrival and first visit separately.

In a competing-call branch, two residents have the same five-unit support
gap and the same first deadline. A capped housing schedule has seven
eligible units and only one staffed accessible room at that time. It
can confirm one five-unit commitment if an actual provider accepts.
The two residual units cannot fund a second five-unit shortfall. Add
neither the first resident's funds nor the restricted care line to the
available housing amount. Use existing housing priority and urgent
review, record the second resident's unmet duty and preserve separately
available assistance. Even ten housing units would leave a room or
staff constraint until an independent alternative accepts.

The Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman in England found that a
council offered interim accommodation to a person leaving hospital without
adequately establishing affordability amid uncertainty about income support.
It also found that insufficient practical help and failure to revisit the
continuing gap contributed to unresolved arrears. These are findings about
one investigated case and its recorded decisions. They identify a mechanism
and provide no estimate of national frequency or cost. [Ombudsman finding, paragraphs 54–64](https://www.lgo.org.uk/decisions/housing/homelessness/25-008-433)

Prefer the verified ordinary offer whenever it secures the same safe place,
care and affordable second period with less public burden. Otherwise the
selected schedule buys a specific, charge-protected temporary placement and
assigns the risk of delayed funding to its public payer. A real adopter
must verify local providers, gross cash, costs, legal enforceability and
actual timely receipt with affected residents. [Canonical rule](../../country/C09/25/06-affordable-temporary-receiving.md)
