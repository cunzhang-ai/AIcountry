# Two schools and a shared teacher

This constructed comparison exercises [ED39–ED43](../../country/C07/21/09-school-staffing-and-origin-protection.md).
The remote school and the neighboring school each owe 20 scheduled hours of
qualified instruction to a cohort. The remote school can deliver 12 and the
neighbor 20. The current term's opening timetable already shows ten teaching
days with losses, including the first day, across the first 30 teaching days.
The comparison below covers a 20-hour slice of that timetable. The verified
forecast triggers a disposition before the first affected session. The first
affected pupil can invoke earlier individual protections.

| Option | Remote hours | Neighbor hours | Unmet hours | Additional paid cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Current capacity | 12 | 20 | 8 | 0 |
| Bonus draws a teacher away for eight hours | 20 | 12 | 8 | 600 |
| Local paid return alone | 16 | 20 | 4 | 300 |
| Paid return, shared appointment and covered origin | 20 | 20 | 0 | 1,300 |
| Same appointment, origin cover and purported alternate share a failed provider | 16 | 20 | 4 | Contract obligations still apply |

All money units are illustrative. Each extra paid hour costs 50. For the
bonus option, the added pay covers eight hours of instruction and four
hours of paid preparation and travel. The origin school's paid base has
no spare qualified teacher to recover those hours. Local return covers four
remote hours plus two preparation hours. The shared teacher contributes
four remote hours with two preparation and two travel hours. An otherwise
uncommitted qualified neighbor replacement teaches four hours with two hours
of preparation. The three added assignments cost 1,000. An independent
alternate receives a 300 paid commitment covering four remote hours and
two preparation hours if needed. The competent local budget has 300 and
the eligible national grant pays the remaining 1,000. Losing that grant
leaves the local return as a funded repair with a four-hour gap.

If the origin cover fails before the timetable starts, cancel the shared
assignment under its agreed terms and keep the original teacher at the
origin. The independently paid alternate can then teach the four remote
hours. If both supposed covers share the failed provider, protect the
origin as before and report the four missing remote hours. Confirm the
remote replacement before counting the repaired timetable as delivered.
Keep any separately accrued payments and original missed hours visible.

The figures assume subject and language competence, compatible calendars,
willing workers and usable pupil access. The [arithmetic check](verify-school-staffing.py)
covers the quantified choices and threshold. Real travel, reasonable
adjustments, staff terms, instruction received, lawful funding and learning
remain to be verified. An origin pupil's opportunity and a teacher's
continuing relationship deserve separate reporting even when totals match.
