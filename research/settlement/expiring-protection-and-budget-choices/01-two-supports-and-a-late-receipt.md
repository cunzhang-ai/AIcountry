# Two expiring protections and a later tax receipt

*Design393 · Constructed first-budget comparison in abstract units.*

A temporary heating credit and a temporary meal route end on the same
date as a temporary tax reduction. All paths retain existing standing
benefits, enforceable housing and service duties, any already accrued
payments and appeals. The current lawful floors are financed outside
the incremental account below. The comparison concerns six households
with continuing unmet needs after those baseline services. Three have
both heating and meal needs, two have only heating needs, and one has
only meal needs. That is five households with heating needs and four
with meal needs, nine needs among six households. Amounts below are
cash costs across the group, not amounts paid to any one household.

Ten units are available for new lawful provision in window 1.
Window 2 brings two units of recurring general capacity and three
units from tax collection after the temporary reduction expires.
All cases assume actual lawful settlement of that tax by window 2.
A missed receipt reduces the window 2 source to two. Unspent window 1
cash can be used later only under express carry authority and actual
settlement. The constructed options below grant that authority where
needed. Compare the options against the same deadlines and scope.

| Alternative | Window 1 uses and effect | Window 2 uses and effect |
|---|---|---|
| Scheduled expiry with existing services only | No additional expenditure. All nine needs remain uncovered in the stated baseline. | Same unmet needs unless an independent improvement occurs. Accrued claims continue. |
| Renew both temporary supports at current reach | Heating credit costs 4 and the meal route 5. A staffed route and accepted credit cover all nine needs. One unit can carry. | Repeating both costs 9. Even with carry and the tax receipt, only 6 is available. There is a gap of 3. No later borrowing or cut to prior claims is assumed. |
| Fund a bridge and repair with an exact smaller recurring route | Heating and accessible meal bridge plus the repair work cost 8. Both routes cover the same nine needs in this window. Two units can carry. | The in-place repairs must have been independently accepted. Upkeep and a shared staffed meal route cost 4 in total. All nine needs are covered if both alternatives actually deliver. Five units of recurring capacity cover this scope without using carry. |

The third path is selected in this fixture. It assumes the in-place
heating changes are safe and accepted by the five households before
the original window 2 deadline, that the alternative meal provider
can serve all four households and that the tax yield recurs in the
amount and period stated. If those conditions fail, use an available preauthorized alternate
or return to the Assembly for a funded choice. If the heating work misses its deadline,
the proposed substitute route costs four additional units. The
resulting total of eight exceeds even the seven units including
the permitted carry. The gap of one stays visible. If the tax
does not settle, the two carried units can cover one otherwise
qualified window of the four-unit package. The recurring source
then falls short by two, so this one-time bridge cannot qualify a
continuing promise. Affected residents keep standing protections
and see the earlier unmet need in the public account.
