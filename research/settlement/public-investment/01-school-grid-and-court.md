# A school, grid and court in one investment calendar

This authored example is in illustrative currency units and qualified
commissioning crew-weeks. The crews are substitutable within the relevant
work and scheduled access window. The pool is 26 paid crew-weeks and year-one
capital cash and authority are at most 80 units for the three named uses.
Figures are cash needs, with separate commitment ceilings required for any
binding later obligation. There is no additional current money or crew.

| Work | Year-one cash | Crew-weeks in the shared window | Separate later exposure |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Already committed urgent grid renewal | 30 | 11 | Existing authorized claims continue |
| Court safety and accessibility retrofit | 24 | 7 | Interim hearing access continues through commissioning |
| Replace the school | 46 | 14 | 20 cash in year two and 8 each year of operation from opening |
| Repair and adapt the school | 18 | 7 | 7 cash in year two and 3 each year of operation from opening |

The school options are mutually exclusive. On the stated assumptions,
independent engineers can certify the repaired school for eight further
years with its full works, maintenance and inspections. Replacement aims
for 30 years. The repair plan authorizes 25 in total for its two-year work,
profiles 18 and 7 as separate payments, and requires the stated operating
source. For the case, those 7 and the 3 annual operating units are separately
available and the school can remain safe and usable through phased work.
Capacity for the later renewal remains unknown. Temporary school access is
included in its construction figures. Neither service life is an observed
result. The court has a current access duty. Its temporary site is funded only
through the work window, with no accepting, equally accessible extension.

**Choose repair this year with the grid and court work.** The combination
takes 72 units and 25 crew-weeks. Replacing the school with both takes
100 units and 32 weeks. Even if the Assembly approved 20 more units of cash,
six crew-weeks would still be missing. Dropping the court makes replacement
fit at 76 and 25, but sacrifices its due accessible hearing route without a
safe equivalent. Remaining room in the repair case is eight units and one
crew-week of separate, bounded headroom. Neither is a free long-term source.
Repair exposes future renewal and operations. It does not establish lower
whole-life cost at a common service horizon. Reopen if school repair cannot
deliver safe access, the prior obligation changes or a funded alternate
source of crews and receiving capacity becomes available.

A financing offer with only 22 in first-year cash can look affordable and
still need 14 of the same crew-weeks. Require its full dated payments, usable
risk transfer, guarantees and exit. Without an independently accepted crew
or a changed programme, it does not solve the collision.

## Changed facts after preparation

Suppose eight units of lawful preparation have already been spent on
replacement before this comparison. Treat them as historical expenditure
outside the decision-date available 80 stated above. Disclose them. Compare
remaining costs to repair, replace or stop, including actual binding
termination and restoration costs if present. In the stated case there are
none. If a new survey instead shows that the repair cannot keep pupils safe,
qualified authorities apply existing safety law and arrange lawful interim
education. Return the new project and service choices promptly to the
responsible elected bodies. Neither an unused appropriation nor this plan
can make an unsafe school available or erase the education duty.
