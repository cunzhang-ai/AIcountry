# Children: protection without criminalizing consensual peer life

*K13–K14; part of [C08/15](../15-special-crimes-and-bounded-sanctions.md).*

**K13 — Sexual acts involving a protected young person.** Intentionally
perform penetration or sexual contact with a person under 16, knowing that
age or consciously disregarding a substantial risk that they are under 16.
The prosecution proves age, conduct and fault. No marriage, guardian's
permission, gift, AI credential or asserted maturity supplies an exception.
Penetration has a 15-year ceiling; other sexual contact five years. K03
separately covers lack of free consent at any age; overlapping labels cannot
multiply punishment for the same conduct/harm.

The age-only prohibition excludes freely chosen, nonexploitative contact
where the younger person is at least 14 and the older is no more than
24 calendar months older. It excludes neither coercion nor an act obtained
by exercising care, supervisory or legal authority, exploiting dependency,
or conditioning money, accommodation, protection or a job on the act.
When a credible peer-exception basis is raised, prosecution disproves it;
the accused has no burden to prove innocence. This protects adolescents'
agency while retaining a definite boundary, rather than discretionary
assessments of whether a child looks mature.

For any person under 18, knowingly exercise actual care, supervisory or
legal power over them to obtain a sexual act: the same 15/five-year ceilings
apply regardless of the ordinary consent age. Prove use of that power;
a nominal title, historical relationship or disapproved peer relationship
alone is insufficient. A genuinely mistaken belief can negate required
age knowledge/recklessness; it does not excuse known nonconsent or coercion.
No compulsory intimate examination or universal identity registry follows.

Under P05, an alleged actor below 16 cannot be convicted, and a 16–17-year-old
retains child process and the aggregate five-year ceiling. Neither the
protected child's own involvement nor seeking clinical/sexual-health help
is an offense. Actual immediate danger uses separate lawful protection,
with child voice, independent support and no disguised penal placement.
The 14/16/18 boundaries and 24 months are normative candidates, not scientific
constants, new medical-consent ages or authority for adult coercion.

**K14 — Sexual exploitation in recorded material.** Knowingly recruit,
coerce or arrange an actual person under 18 for a recorded sexually explicit
act, or intentionally make that exploitation record, has a ten-year ceiling.
Knowingly distribute such material has a five-year ceiling. Knowingly retain
it for sexual use or circulation has a two-year ceiling. Prove the actual
child, explicit content and knowledge/recklessness as to protected age;
ordinary nudity, medical care, education or a childlike synthetic character
does not establish these elements. Explicit content means sexual acts or
genital display made for a sexual purpose, not a regulator's taste judgment.

A child's own voluntary self-created material, and a private consensual
exchange between peers meeting K13's age/absence-of-exploitation conditions,
do not criminalize those young participants. This does not license adult
solicitation, commercial exploitation, unauthorized onward sharing or
coercion. K15 independently addresses identifiable intimate-image abuse,
including fabricated images; it cannot be mislabelled actual-child K14 proof.

Inadvertent receipt, proportionate safe preservation/reporting, and necessary
professional or judicial handling without exploitation are excluded. Require
purpose-limited restricted custody, minimal necessary copies, victim privacy,
defense access under safe court arrangements and verified removal/restoration.
Do not replicate abuse material in open research or generate examples of it.
An attempt needs P03's substantial step and specific intent, not a profile,
fantasy, conversation label or predicted propensity alone. Support and
removal do not depend on identifying a convictable offender.
