# LC05–LC08: people need chosen support, not an institutional cost target

*Part of [C06/46](../46-life-needs-and-paid-capacity.md); design 123 reference.*

**LC05 — Retain the child's offer and the adult's agency.** Keep S/AS actual
need, chosen assistance/non-employer access, worker protections and public fallback.
The reference retains childcare offers, individual/night/adult support, travel,
assessment and separate review; ordinary education is a distinct program. A
child's 40-hour place cannot shrink because employees work 32 hours. Relatives,
patients and chosen private relationships are not compulsory unpaid cover.
An adult need is not capped by the input row or a convenient staff ratio.

**LC06 — Compare shared rosters and dedicated teams without prescribing a home.**
In the authored 100 continuous posts, contact 876,000 hours plus 12%documentation
requires 981,120 task-hours. A pool dividing by 1,130.4 gives 868 whole-post annual
bounds;100 separately funded nonsharing integer teams give 900,32 more/0.960 m
ordinary annual pay. This difference is a declared staffing-organization choice,
not a universal minimum for 100 homes. Paid part-time/local relief pooling can
change it if actual routes, break coverage, qualifications, rest and consent fit.
Neither annual bound proves a roster; travel/site differences remain unpriced.
Do not force co-residence or institutional care to claim a cheaper denominator.

**LC07 — Keep the full named care account and unknown additions.** The 13-row
care reference with those dedicated teams has 7,363 whole-post bounds/221.760 m
pay,3.100 m expendable inputs,.940 m upkeep and 6.358333 m renewal funding:
232.158333 m direct-public allocation; the declared mixed case is 237.728693 m CU.
These are fictional planning scopes, not actual demand/price or all current
AS/WL/TW/OE liability. The 67 m all-new care-asset quote, especially adaptations,
needs actual title/overlap with housing repair and accepted paid source. No
provider choice makes public fallback, employer dues or insurance risk free.

**LC08 — Match learning and independent work once.** Clinical practice 8,000
hours may be counted within already paid/excluded learning only if the same
people, actual activity, qualification and period match; independent 1,000 hours
and supervision remain. The conditional health bound falls by eight posts/
.480 m nominal pay in that case. It is not an actual saving: retained workers
still receive pay, and usable reassignment/new hiring avoided needs proof.
No generic course or AI completion score silently removes required work.
Clinical/education/worker preparation, paid governance and independent review
retain their own tasks and source rather than all disappearing into learning.
