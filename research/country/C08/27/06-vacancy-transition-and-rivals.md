# PO21–PO24. Finite leadership gaps and a revisable civilian model

*Part of [PO](../27-civilian-enforcement-and-public-control.md); no appointment
by silence, case-completion holdover, expanded powers or immunity on failure.*

**PO21 — Routine administration and own case powers survive differently.**
Each service predesignates a qualified unconflicted career deputy through C04.
During a head vacancy/inability the deputy may manage existing appropriations,
routine staffing/contracts/records for at most180 elapsed days from the original
gap, without serial renamed deputies resetting it. No permanent head appointment,
new general programme, reserved policy or discretionary senior appointments.
Police/investigation officers and custody/release/records/pay staff retain their
own already enacted case/duty powers after180; they do not inherit all chief
powers. Actual LF incident commanders still use prior role/task authority.
Absence of all actual qualified staff is non-delivery, not a reason to invent
an acting chief or extend custody, force, a called election or a remedy date.
Founding law may recruit qualified ordinary staff/deputy by the same open merit
route before a permanent chief; no deputy is deemed the permanent head.

**PO22 — Exact prospective, paid succession.** New institutional/source/status
arrangements commence on a coordinated Act's express date at least180calendar
days after enactment. Inventory actual public entities/powers, people/pay/union/
contract/pension rights, sites/devices/source licences/records, victims and
pending criminal/civil/status/custody cases, old liability and independently
accepted receiving. Preserve original law/clock/ground/claim and stronger
protections until their lawful source revision. Appointments confer no new
offense, retrospective warrant, penalty, surveillance or immunity. PO adds no office to RR’s protected constitutional roster by itself. No readiness
certificate postpones an earlier due service, court order, election or release.
Missing new preparation requires lawful protective correction and disclosed
failure, not a secret extension or fictitious independent body.

**PO23 — Keep substantively different safety models.** Strong retain/repair
of generic police with C04 careers could cost less; autonomous local police
can improve proximity but fragment cross-place protection or entrench local
power. Directly elected chiefs can strengthen one public link and politicize
cases; Cabinet-only appointment can be responsive and concentrate coercive
control. The selected public nomination/confirmation and separate inquiry
adds hearing, deadlock, skill/source and common-patron risks. Independent
unarmed/peer care can reduce coercion but needs real receiving/safety resources;
a central armed safety/clinical command remains rejected. Legitimate prospective legislative reorganization remains possible under
existing constitutional/RR and paid successor/worker/claim protections; an
executive rename cannot remove a protected head or alter case powers. Actual local context
and qualified evidence can displace this form; newer institutions are no better
life proof. No public, worker/accused/survivor/opposition/foreign review is invented.

**PO24 — Test the whole country next.** After this14-entrance/selected-control
review and positive repair, prepare an integrated paid operation/succession
comparison across actual current democratic, ordinary-law, material/service,
source and new police/inquiry/custody duties. Trace ordinary and hostile/
shortfall handover, accepted qualified independent execution and continuing
claims; keep conservative assumptions/unknowns and real simpler/structural
rivals. This is country-design research with actual budget/reviewers unknown,
not another automatic police-detail study or a demanded preexisting ratification.
Reopen on original capture/retaliation/unserved danger, source/rights conflicts,
bad expiry/received-remedy paths or a better whole alternative. Full source,
actual public control/readiness and superior outcomes remain unproved.
