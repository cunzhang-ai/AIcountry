# C07. Reproductive health and the start of life

*Part of [C07 social floor](../C07-social-floor.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

The essential health entitlement includes age-appropriate sexual and
reproductive information, contraception and its removal, fertility
assessment, pregnancy care, safe birth, postpartum care, pregnancy-loss
and abortion care, and the newborn's own clinical needs. The Assembly
sets the lawful scope, public funding and revision procedure under
[C08's bodily-autonomy and parentage floor](../C08/06-reproductive-autonomy-and-parentage.md).
Clinical professionals use current evidence, informed consent, safety
review and patient-specific judgment. No minister, insurer, partner or
model can quietly remove a lawful service or make an unsafe individual
choice. An entitlement does not promise every costly technology or a
particular pregnancy outcome; coverage differences require stated
reasons, fair criteria and an appeal.

The national health purchaser and its clinical networks must show
reachable first contact, trained staff, medicine and equipment, referral,
transport, after-hours care, private records and an accepting provider
when one facility closes or an individual clinician does not provide a
lawful service. Rural distance, disability, age, language, work time,
violence risk or missing residence documents must not turn a written
right into a journey no person can make. Telehealth can expand access
where clinically appropriate, with a non-digital path and real follow-up;
an app booking is not received care. A medical emergency follows a
specific clinical and legal route, not a general public-health police
power. The same midwife, ambulance or operating room cannot be booked
simultaneously for routine birth, urgent care and another service.

Pregnant people receive respectful care and genuine choices within
clinical safety; informed refusal, pain relief, privacy, support person
and a route to report mistreatment do not vanish at the hospital door.
Newborns have their own health, registration and protection interests,
which the clinical team and C08 status service must carry without
making the birthing person a legal nonperson. Postpartum physical and
mental-health support, infant feeding help and a safe care handoff need
actual staff and follow-up. Death, injury, coercion, denied care and
missing follow-up are independently reviewed with safe testimony,
professional fair process and a usable remedy; a facility cannot audit
its own serious failure as a complete answer.

Infertility prevention, diagnosis and treatment are considered in the
same public coverage and opportunity-cost process as other health care.
The Assembly compares benefit, harm, expected uptake, cost, waiting time,
equity and the burden on people who cannot or do not wish to have
children. A funded fertility service does not confer a right to another
person's body, gametes or child; C08 governs consent, records and legal
parentage. New techniques require lawful safety and ethical review,
published evidence and prospective rights rules before broad use.
Demographic targets cannot dictate clinical advice or patient consent.

C06 counts clinics, qualified teams, transport, medicines, laboratories,
privacy review, aftercare, independent complaints, child support and
replacement capacity once across health, public health and care.
C14 reports access, quality, coercion and delayed-care complaints,
pregnancy and newborn harm, patient experience and actual resolution
by place and group without collecting intimate testimony unsafely.
No actual population, clinical network, budget or observed outcome
validates this research route.

[RP01–RP16](../C08/20-reproductive-law-and-parentage.md) now supplies proposed
consent, donor/origin, protected pregnancy assistance, storage/death and future-
method law. Clinical receiving, confidential advice and newborn care remain
due while status/support is disputed. New legal availability does not qualify
a technique or finance every requested cycle; S02 and actual safety still apply.
