# C07. Medicines, devices and safe clinical methods within a complete care path

15 October 2026 · Design 78 · Proposed research, not clinical advice/approval

[TD09–TD10](../C08/22/11-water-food-and-clinical-duties.md) owns marketing,
production/conformity and reserved-act duties; C07 owns actual access to clinically
needed care. Product quality, evidence of benefit/harms, patient choice, safe use,
public purchasing/coverage and received clinical service are distinct decisions.
The same public health provider cannot certify its own serious failure as an
independent reviewer. Separate actual mandates and source/appointment controls.

Choose relevant proportionate evidence, quality assurance and continuing adverse-
event/recall duties rather than a single technological label. Source configuration,
intended population/use, actual care pathway, supply/power and user understanding
matter. Do not require repeating adequate established evidence merely to create
a local industry, or accept an export-only/different-version approval as relevant.
Actual recognition needs lawful scope and qualified source review under TS06.

Clinical teams can make justified individualized use of a lawfully supplied product
within actual professional competence and informed choice, including a reasoned
unmarketed indication. This is distinct from marketing an unproved benefit,
research, compassionate supply, forced care or a new experimental intervention.
Each needs its own actual authority, consent and protections; RP future-method
holds and B clinical choice cannot be displaced by an innovation label.

Public procurement/coverage considers benefit, uncertainty, actual alternatives,
opportunity cost and whole-path supply, with independent challenge for a harmful
exclusion. It neither grants market permission nor waives a product's harm.
Patients need usable warnings, continuing medicines/support and real received
replacement when a product, algorithm or vendor fails. Closing a recall ticket
cannot discharge clinical responsibility or prove bodily compensation.

[KR17–KR20](../C11/07/05-essential-knowledge-and-paid-receiving.md) now adds
actual rights/know-how/inputs and paid receiving. Court-issued patent use does
not supply trade secrets, workers, foreign authority or clinical approval.

C06/27 counts qualified assessors, clinicians, laboratory/metrology, translation,
privacy/source custody, safe substitution and actual remedy with all ordinary
care. Commercial fees, a patent, a certification logo and predicted AI savings
supply no actual resource. Necessary capacity, prices, current source law,
qualified clinical review and observed health outcomes remain unverified.

[SC21–SC32 critical inputs](../C04/05/06-need-and-upstream-capability.md) now
adds shared-source, qualified usable stock, lifecycle/repair and paid fallback
to buying. Six legacy clinical budget quotes are not adequate source prices.
TD09/TD10 approval/recall/competence and actual C07 care/dispensing/consent remain
separate; no buffer model supplies a product annex, clinical substitution or
foreign stock access. Existing useful-time care and independent remedies continue.

[HG public clinical governance](05-health-service-and-patient-control.md) names
NCS public delivery/purchasing and the outside clinical-rights reviewer. It
does not absorb TD09/10 licensing or TS09 serious-safety inquiry, approve
products/experiments or erase source/consent/competence and actual receiving.

[MQ critical medicines](26-critical-medicines-and-safe-continuity.md)
specifies the product-dependent shortage clock, lawful dispatch, patient
substitution and verified receipt. The existing product, device and
clinical-method safeguards continue within their own scope.

[AEC01–AEC05](05/05-assistive-equipment-continuity.md) gives assistive
equipment a useful-time clinical receiving and replacement route.
Keep device conformity and consent distinct from repair and paid receipt.

[CTC01–CTC07](14/01-conditional-treatment-coverage-and-continuity.md)
adds a finite public coverage route with independent appraisal, actual
starts and a funded safe transition. Product permission, clinical
choice and ordinary entitlements retain their own authority.
