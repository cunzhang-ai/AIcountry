# Democratic repair: review, electorate and time

*Canonical RR05–RR07; part of [C14/08](../08-democratic-repair-without-self-authorization.md).*

**RR05 — Existing authority decides the exception.** The sponsor asks the final
constitutional forum competent under the law already in force for a reasoned,
public early-effect determination. Use its existing jurisdiction, appointments,
recusal, evidence, voting and correction rules, with accessible legal assistance
and a real opportunity for opposition and affected people to challenge the case.
Publication/redaction protects safe testimony and leaves the reasons inspectable.
The proponent bears clear and convincing proof of the closed factual predicates;
value/legal interpretation still needs reasons, not a fabricated probability.
No approval follows from a deadline, split, absent quorum or universal direct
conflict. Ordinary law continues, including timely case-specific rights remedies.

After CJ's lawful commencement, its actual fixed final thresholds apply, with
CJ12's closed external forum for directly personal court-status questions. Neither
CJ12 nor this exception supplies jurisdiction before its lawful commencement.
A court checks legality and the exception, not whether it prefers the policy.
The cabinet, coordinator, national technical service and project's AI cannot
certify restoration or select a substitute final reviewer when lawful review fails.
Publish the early-effect determination before the final 90-day consideration
period. The supported text must state the requested date and any expressly
approved delayed fallback; a court cannot invent a fallback or severability.
A pre-ballot determination is conditional on unchanged operative text and facts;
a material change requires fresh review, never silent transfer of approval.
A factual change alone may be revalidated on fresh public reasons without
restarting the text clock if the exact text, date, scope and conditions stay
unchanged and meaningful timely challenge remains possible. Changed operative
terms, dates or conditions require valid renewed authorization/consideration.
No early effect while revalidation is pending; if lawful timing fails, use only
an expressly approved delayed fallback, otherwise no early commencement.

**RR06 — The proposed electorate cannot ratify itself.** Initiation, petition
thresholds and the referendum use the electorate and denominator actually valid
under predecessor law, including effective judgments restoring an existing
entitlement. The amendment's new electorate cannot count itself into the vote
that creates it. For the research reference, the initiative-opening date fixes the lawful elector
denominator for the 1%/5% thresholds, rounded upward to a whole signature;
actual erroneous inclusion/exclusion is correctable without changing that date.
Signers must be eligible under existing law when signing; referendum voters
under existing law on ballot day, including ordinary new eligibility and lawful
correction. A new proposed electorate cannot supply its own missing signatures.
Independent review of official error can order a usable corrective collection
period only within its lawful scheduling powers, never deem signatures supplied.
Certification records the roll/version, count, dates and disputes safely; a research
population estimate, model consensus or account count is no denominator.

Existing law may itself be illegitimate or deny equal standing. This reference
admits that a formally valid old-law vote does not establish a just constituent
settlement. A genuine founding/replacement process needs its own legitimate
affected public and authority; it is not an exception a minister can proclaim.
Preserve the objection and existing judicial relief rather than invent approval.

**RR07 — No shortcut once a contest is underway.** The full amendment route
retains at least 90 calendar days of accessible consideration of its exact final
text and a ballot within 180 days of lawful certification, subject only to its
narrow already lawful judicial postponement. RR supplies no new extension or
shortening power. Material text changes need the valid predecessor treatment of
signatures, approvals and renewed consideration; cosmetic corrections cannot
be used to restart a clock indefinitely or hide a substantive change.

Before an election is called, the court must find an actual lawful schedule for
notice, registration, challenge, accessible voting and implementation early enough
for voters, contestants and officials to understand and use the repair. There is
no generic “AI makes it ready” exemption. If that schedule cannot fit, early
application to this contest is refused; existing urgent remedies continue.
An amendment using RR must be lawfully adopted and operative before the call
to govern that contest. A conditional RR permission is not itself a binding
existing-rights merits judgment and cannot permit post-call ratification/effect.
After the election is called, only strictly necessary execution of an already
binding judgment restoring an existing right may affect that contest under its
existing enforcement powers. A newly adopted amendment does not acquire an
early-effect exception merely by being called restoration. No automatic cancelled
poll, executive postponement or wider change follows from the judgment.
