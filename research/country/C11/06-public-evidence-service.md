# Public evidence without a sovereign truth office

Design 94 · EV01–EV16 · Research selection, not enacted law

Select federated original custodians and one statutory professional evidence
service led by a publicly appointed chief, with no governing expert board.
Public appointment, protected publication and plural inquiry replace the earlier
unspecified leadership arrangement. They do not establish actual independence.

Six years, three fifths,180days and the process clocks are contestable normative
hypotheses. Adoption, source-law compatibility, actual staff, qualified independent
review, affected participation, funding and outcomes remain unestablished.
Current law/software, contribution permissions and constitutional holds remain.

[OB observed comparison](../C06/55-observed-economy-and-country-comparison.md)
now illustrates source-vintage, sector, cash/in-kind and stock/flow distinctions.
Published aggregate estimates and their shared source family are not an actual
individual dossier, independent outcome judgment or governing authority.

**Design146 role boundary.** [PM](08-public-media-and-editorial-independence.md)
owns public journalism and internal editorial correction, separately from EV.
Publication/identity of original statistics is not authority over news framing,
editor appointments, access ranking, media funding or a journalist's source.

[EV17–EV24](11-evidence-corrections-and-public-reliance.md) assigns material downstream review and follow-through to existing decision owners. The EV01–EV16 powers and legal vintage rules continue.
