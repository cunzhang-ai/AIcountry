## Baseline and choice

Pin Design332, A9, RR, existing referendum access, review and the
working framework. The prior design requires an exact text, independent

partial amendments. Choose separate authorizations and votes for
materially independent choices, with limited disclosed dependencies

A legitimate predecessor procedure must adopt it.

## Alternatives and evidence

Compare an omnibus vote with a broad subject, strict severance without
dependencies, and separate votes with exact operational links. Omnibus
text can preserve bargains but can let a popular reform carry an
unrelated expansion of power. Strict severance can leave a lawful
institution without its necessary implementation or safeguards.
Select a separability test based on operative effects, not the title,
with equal application to both proposal routes. The test can be
contested, add time and confer practical blocking power on an
unavailable reviewer. Provide funded early challenge, preserve
unchanged independently authorized questions, and protect exact
petitions from being rewritten or recertified.

The [primary comparators](sources.md) show separate votes and different
review boundaries. They supply no outcome estimate for this proposal.
The worked case isolates each threshold and contingent effect.
