# EV21–EV24. Protect claims and deliver correction

**EV21 — Carry due claims and address harmful reliance separately.** Keep
accrued pay, executed contracts, vested claims and statutory recovery or
refund protections on their lawful tracks. Before reducing a planning
allocation, assess commitments induced by a sufficiently specific official
communication, including its conditions and what the recipient could reasonably
have known. A competent elected body may authorize a finite bridge for verified,
unavoidable net loss or essential service continuity. Deduct lawful cancellations,
recoveries and overlapping payments. Record who actually bore the loss. A
forecast alone supplies no entitlement to its face amount. Assess both recipients
that relied on the overstatement and people omitted or underfunded by it. Carry
restricted funds and local lawful responsibilities separately. Unfunded new relief
remains an unmet financing decision, and existing due obligations remain due.

**EV22 — Follow the remedy to the person or service.** The public programme owner
keeps the case open for each unresolved disposition and checks what the recipient
actually received by the relevant deadline. Confirm a posted payment, usable
service, restored access or another ordered remedy with evidence suitable to its
purpose. An authorized payer and an accepting provider remain distinct. A missed
due time stays visible even if the claim is later repaired. Existing administrative
review and courts can address delay, wrong legal effect or failure to execute.
Audit can examine common implementation and exposure with controlled access.
Keep confidential records compartmentalized. Public reports state coverage,
unresolved uses and delivery shortfalls without exposing personal details or
protected sources. Repeated breaches use the existing SR remedy.

**EV23 — Preserve independent judgment and workable access.** A person can
report a possible error or challenge its effects without an AI, a specialist
account or an exact version number. Machine notices, signed messages, voice
and in-person evidence can all lead to the same official duty. Confirm machine
alerts by accountable staff and protect claim and appeal access while uncertain
cases are checked. Shared source families or models cannot independently
corroborate one another. An office whose own decision is disputed cannot control
the sole intake, review record or remedy executor. Limit handling to material
uses and the minimum necessary data. Never infer waiver, consent or a final
disposition from a platform acknowledgement or a person going offline.

**EV24 — Adopt prospectively and test actual correction.** The adopting law
must name covered decision classes, lawful access to earlier records, delivery
owners, paid alternates and independent routes. Pilot the duty on a high-impact
funding formula and a recurring individual payment. Keep applicable source-law
appeals and accrued obligations through migration, including those opened before
the change. Measure time from credible discovery, missed due dates, the fraction
of affected people reached, unresolved uses, privacy harms and burdens displaced
onto existing services. Compare with improving current release and appeal
channels. Reopen if tracing becomes surveillance, actual correction comes no
sooner, or a simpler paid route reaches the same people in time. The seven and
30 calendar days are proposed defaults and carry no authority before adoption.
