# OB09–OB12: an annual surplus is neither spare cash nor transferable sovereignty

**OB09 — Compare like fiscal scope.** Government consumption 636.8bn DKK is about
22.9% of rounded GDP. OFF3 total government expenditure 1,321.267bn is about 47.5%:
it has a broader scope, including transfers/capital. Total revenue 1,418.780bn is
not tax revenue alone, and accrual/statistical receipt is not all due-day cash.
The 97.513bn fiscal net-lending result does not establish January liquidity, free
new commitments or a price for all future needs. The current-country public
component book contains different payroll/purchase/asset/reservation/held-scope
semantics; it cannot be compared as if it were an ESA consumption aggregate.
[Original OFF3 table](https://www.statbank.dk/OFF3) supplies the separate fiscal companion.

**OB10 — Retain both sides of the national financial position.** In the core
nonfinancial accounts, general-government net lending 97.513bn coexists with
163.449bn in the rest of the domestic economy; total 260.963bn is matched by
foreign −260.963bn. The 1m constituent rounding difference remains. Financial
transactions match the reported national/government/foreign totals in this
companion, without forcing every cell to close. A public surplus can coexist
with positive domestic nongovernment net lending because the external sector
borrows in aggregate. This is not a strategy every country can copy at once,
an automatic national FX pool or a requirement to engineer an export surplus.
[Original NASL2 table](https://www.statbank.dk/NASL2) supplies the nonfinancial sector positions.

**OB11 — Do not import the monetary environment as a free input.** Danmarks
Nationalbank's primary explanation describes a krone/euro fixed-rate policy
within ERM II and its own monetary division of responsibility. AIcountry's
managed-float MC candidate is different. Danish interest, exchange stability,
European institutional relationships and foreign acceptance are not inherited
by the new country. Own-unit, peg/shared and foreign-currency options retain
their different authority, source, loss, treaty/exit and legacy consequences.
No assertion that one wins follows from this aggregate accounting benchmark.
[Danmarks Nationalbank's explanation](https://www.nationalbanken.dk/en/frequently-asked-questions/questions-regarding-fixed-exchange-rate-policy)
is the monetary-context source; it is not this project's selected currency authority.

**OB12 — Retain the source architecture, narrow claims.** RF ordinary finance,
MC typed limits and the separately authorized finite fiscal-issue rival remain.
Neither national financial wealth nor a general-government surplus is an actual
offer to finance AIcountry; a domestic monetary entry does not supply a foreign
seller or clinical staff. Annual accounts also do not establish a current-law
taxpayer's liability. All original source/ownership/currency/due-date boundaries,
restricted S/client/renewal/political assets and actual paid OP/T claims continue.
