# Evidence for opening liabilities and protected budget choices

| Primary source | Observation | Design use and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [New Zealand Treasury, 2023 Pre-election Economic and Fiscal Update, page 68 onward](https://www.treasury.govt.nz/sites/default/files/2023-09/prefu23.pdf) | The update separates fiscal forecast entries from material risks whose timing or amount cannot be reliably assigned. It discloses that not every significant risk may be identified. | Preserve unquantified exposures and snapshot dates. A published risk statement does not supply a call date, funding or authority for the proposed first budget. |
| [UK National Audit Office, Whole of Government Accounts 2023–24](https://www.nao.org.uk/reports/whole-of-government-accounts-2023-24/) | The audit certificate records draft accounts data for 280 consolidated entities and no submission for 201 entities that Treasury identified for consolidation. The auditor disclaimed an opinion because material gaps prevented sufficient audit evidence. | Distinguish estimates, unavailable returns and verified balances. The counts do not measure hidden debt in those entities or show that any individual service was unfunded. |
| [IMF Executive Board, Mozambique misreporting decision, November 2016](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2016/11/21/pr16521-imf-executive-board-considers-mozambique-misreporting-under-the-policy-support-instrument) | The Board addressed previously undisclosed external borrowing of about USD 1.37 billion contracted during 2012–2015. It found inaccurate reporting of central government and government-guaranteed debt and documented the effect on the programme assessment. | Examine obligations beyond the headline budget and actual currency and authority. This historical decision does not settle private-law validity, liability allocation or effects under this country's proposed rules. |

The [cash comparison](../settlement/opening-liabilities/README.md) is
authored. It uses a single declared payer, two reports of one possible
call and explicit cash dates. No observed quantities were used as
parameters for the comparison. The sources qualify three separate
mechanisms, rather than measuring the proposed design's outcomes.
