# C07. Personal assistance without compulsory employer risk

2 November 2026 · Design 116 · AS01–AS24 research candidate, not enacted law

**Select person-directed assistance with an actual non-employer route, optional
supported direct employment, paid worker protection and continuing public
receiving.** A person should control intimate support without having to run a
business, accept the provider's preferred lifestyle or surrender care when an
assistant, payroll agent or employer fails. Workers retain their own rights.

This makes [S09–S12](13/03-care-childhood-and-agency.md) operationally specific.
It adds no fixed hours cap, wealth test or ordinary-income withdrawal. Existing
clinical, housing, legal-capacity and child protections remain separate.

The public/local care authority must offer a person-directed public or genuinely
accepting contracted/cooperative employer route, with paid help to choose or
switch. It may not make direct employment the price of individualized care.
The recipient chooses the degree of delegation; declining detailed management
is no loss of voice, control or support. An intermediary that only calculates
payroll does not silently become the employer or absorb the person's liabilities.

AS12 explicitly proposes public backing of covered ordinary assistance-employment
dues when the approved employer/payroll route fails. It is not general business
loss insurance or a guarantee of every damages award. This is a new gross
payment/source exposure, not a free restatement of the earlier service budget.
Higher old rights and source-law employer/creditor claims survive.

The [current comparison](../../settlement/assistance/README.md) separates source
choice, actual authority and delivered support, and tests care hours, paired
work, paid time, restricted money, failure claims and a bounded continuous rota.
Neither its invented prices nor an annual-hours pass qualifies a national roster
or full source. A written right to direct care has not proved actual freedom.
