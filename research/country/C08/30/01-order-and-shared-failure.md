# SR01–SR04. Follow the order to an actual recipient

*Part of [public remedies](../30-repeated-public-failure-and-remedy.md).*

**SR01 — Assign the act and its execution separately.** For a binding public
order, the court records the responsible public body, exact lawful duty,
covered recipients, amount or act, original due time, lawful source,
executor and useful evidence of receipt. The existing Court Service
Registrar receives implementation information without adjudicating merits.
The obligated agency identifies a responsible career officer, with actual
Treasury or service counterparts and accepting alternates where law allows.
Send usable notice to the affected person or authorized representative.
Recording an implementation task adds no waiting period to a binding
order. An appeal affects execution only under applicable law or an
operative stay. If service or authority is lost, use direct urgent relief.

**SR02 — Record performance in parts with actual dates.** Track issuance,
lawful authorization, due amount or act, disbursement or dispatch, actual
receipt, lateness and what remains. A blocked bank credit, unaccepted
care referral, unlocked account without restored benefit or published
new rule without corrected decisions leaves its own obligation open.
Verify using proportionate records, actual recipient evidence or a
safe independent check. Do not require an impossible signature or
disclosure. Preserve unchallenged portions and original claim dates.
No double payment, substitute performance or waiver follows from an
acknowledgment. When delivery is impossible, keep the breach or unmet
duty visible and report the real available protection.

**SR03 — Identify the binding constraint before choosing the response.**
Distinguish disputed scope or stay, refusal by an able official, blocked
records or payment channels, unavailable lawful cash, and unavailable
qualified people or physical service. More than one may apply. A court
may compel the exact established duty and use the preauthorized B19/E15
record or payment substitute within its actual scope. It may require
evidence, reasons and a lawful protective alternative where available.
A shortfall does not prove personal fault. A finding of obstruction needs
notice, evidence of duty and actual ability, fair hearing and an enacted
consequence. A recovery path supplies neither tax authority nor an
unavailable worker.

**SR04 — Open a shared repair only for a proved legal problem.** A person
with an affected claim, a duly authorized representative under B20 or a
public-interest body within existing standing can seek a bounded repair
in the competent court. Require a finding of an existing public duty
and a material unlawful practice or continuing failure that causes
repeated breaches or creates a concrete imminent risk of repeated
breaches. Explain why isolated correction is insufficient and why
the proposed remedy is within jurisdiction, necessary and proportionate.
Hear the respondent and materially affected parties with safe notice
and protection. Interim relief follows B17 and the earlier source-law
limits. A complaint count, model pattern or policy disagreement
can prompt inquiry but cannot prove unlawfulness.
