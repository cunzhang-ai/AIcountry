## Copenhagen's 2026 phase-in

Denmark's education ministry reported a first national installment of about
140 million DKK to municipalities in January and a similar planned monthly
schedule during 2026. That is a public-to-public financing flow. In Copenhagen,
the City Council on 26 March approved a 2026 fee reduction from May and the
associated appropriation. The 22 April committee record reports the local
grant received and the partition below. All amounts are million DKK.
[National payment report](https://uvm.dk/aktuelt/nyheder/2026/januar/260121-penge-til-flere-ansatte-og-lavere-foraeldrebetaling-i-dagtilbud-er-nu-sendt-afsted-til-kommunerne/)
· [Council decision](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Borgerrepr%C3%A6sentationen/m%C3%B8de-26032026/referat/punkt-25)
· [Local allocation record](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/B%C3%B8rne-%20og%20Ungdomsudvalget/m%C3%B8de-22042026/referat/punkt-2)

| Local 2026 scope | Million DKK | What the record establishes |
|---|---:|---|
| State grant to Copenhagen | 200.7 | Local administration reports receipt for the phase-in |
| Fee-related council appropriation | 100.6 | Estimated reduction of 91.5 in municipal fee receipts plus 9.1 in additional private-provider grants |
| More pedagogical staff | 81.0 | Allocation to this purpose |
| Other early-care measures | 19.1 | Separate committee allocation, including recruitment and improvements to premises |

The appropriation lines reconcile at the published precision:
`91.5 + 9.1 = 100.6` and `100.6 + 81.0 + 19.1 = 200.7`. The city estimated
the fee receipt change at about 11.4 million per month. Keep the official
eight-month estimate of 91.5 instead of replacing it with eight times the
rounded monthly value. The approved monthly tariffs excluding lunch were reduced by
606 DKK for childminding, 617 for nursery and 345 for kindergarten under
the decision. A tariff is different from a net household payment after
support or the gain of a child who could not use a place. The 81.0 does
not certify paid or present staff. The case supplies an adopted
short-period financing allocation and a municipal report of receipt.
Actual invoices, qualified rosters and the underlying national net source
and tax incidence were not verified. [Council fee and budget terms](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Borgerrepr%C3%A6sentationen/m%C3%B8de-26032026/referat/punkt-25)

## Retain, revise or extend

The published 2026 national finance-act booklet provides a distinct
education reserve line, with 57.8 million DKK in 2026 and 1,781.3 million
annually from 2027 in its narrative. The ministry's December recalculation
put the package's 2027 onward national estimate at 1,898.4 million and
revised the estimated additional pedagogical staff from about 1,360 to
1,530. The 117.1 million difference is about 6.6% of the earlier amount.
The estimates and reserve are different fiscal objects and are not added.
The 2026 city receipt is neither a recurring local entitlement nor a share
that can be projected unchanged into later years. L35's fee amendments
were referred to committee after first reading on 20 October 2026.
[Reserve account and source scope](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-funded-local-early-care/sources.md)
· [Recalculation](https://uvm.dk/aktuelt/nyheder/2025/december/251218-lovforslag-om-aendring-af-dagtilbudsloven-er-sendt-i-offentlig-hoering/)
· [Bill timetable](https://www.ft.dk/samling/20261/lovforslag/l35/tidsplan.htm)

| Arrangement | Implication for a continuing care floor |
|---|---|
| Retain or repair the existing local and national settlement | Preserves real administration and local discretion. Budget changes and support can improve access, with actual later funding and delivery separately assessed. |
| Needs-based national gap funding with local delivery and fallback, selected | Aligns the common duty with recurring finance and an enforceable service path. More complex to estimate, and needs funded independent challenges and standby capacity. |
| Direct national care delivery | Unifies payer and employer, but moves actual staff, assets and control nationally and needs a full paid handover. Local knowledge and local accountability can weaken. |
| Expand portable fee relief without securing extra places | Helps eligible users facing fees, but gains depend on their actual bill. Families facing missing places or incompatible hours need a receiving service. |

## Adverse paths for the same offer

- **Fee relief continues and cash arrives late.** In a desk case using the
  city's rounded monthly line, a month with about 11.4 million DKK less
  municipal fee receipts needs that much incremental financing for that
  component if there is no usable cash covering it. The actual local
  receipt calendar and private-provider payment dates are unknown.
  The sensitivity does not imply that a transfer was late. FC03 requires
  the authorized money before the first payment at risk, with a separately
  profiled provider grant and payroll. A late transfer is first a liquidity
  problem. A lasting higher cost belongs in FB's recurring source decision.
- **The money arrives and qualified cover does not.** Use NC12's existing
  two-group case with four competent adults present for 40 hours weekly.
  On the stated absence day the five remaining people can offer at most
  32 normal hours versus 35.2 needed including preparation. This is an
  authored NC case, not a Copenhagen roster. FC06 can cover with actual
  competent workers or usable alternative care. A grant, advertisement,
  funded trainee or AI schedule cannot close the day by itself.
- **Both fail at once.** Keep the family claim date and the worker and
  provider debts. Use an independently available fallback where safe.
  Report uncovered time and arrears separately if that fallback also fails.
  The law does not permit unsafely enlarging groups or imposing unpaid
  household replacement. Both the care complaint and fiscal shortfall must
  reach their respective decision-makers.

The 2026 local allocation closes for the published purposes. A full
four-year source, actual paid roster, incidence and equivalent child access
remain open. The selected rule makes the unpaid or undelivered part
visible and assigns it an accountable owner and remedy.
