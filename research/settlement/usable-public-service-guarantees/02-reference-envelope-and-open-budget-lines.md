# A single observed envelope for the first national operating comparison

Denmark 2025 is the common historical reference. The
[original-source government account](../../2026-11-observed-government-baseline/decision-record.md)
reconciles DKK 1,472,839 million of general-government expenditure.
Its purpose groups exhaust that total. The
[matched work account](../matched-national-work/01-observed-2025-and-boundaries.md)
reconciles DKK 442,275 million of government employee compensation and
reports 1,191.239 million government employee hours. Compensation is
already represented in the fiscal account. Hours and quarterly FTE
have different statistical definitions and supply no second workforce.
The figures describe Denmark, with no AIcountry money or legal effect.

The purpose lines are a **single expense partition**. Functions named
in the right column identify dependencies and unresolved tasks. Their
placement is a research crosswalk, not a second accounting allocation.
An access support can sit within health, education, social protection
or another source law. Charge the resulting supplier claim to its
actual payer once and eliminate only matched internal transfers.
Independent review, shared digital infrastructure and overlapping
services need a role and dated cost wherever they actually sit.

| Proposed first motion | What the common account can support now |
|---|---|
| Retain and repair | Use the complete historical purpose perimeter for comparison. Bring forward discrete repairs with existing authority, funds and staffed delivery evidenced. Record inherited unmet need. |
| Phase in new guarantees | Prepare named classes and cost both new and continuing duties on the same dates. Bind each increment only when its own source and receiving path qualify. |
| Start several guarantees together | Keep available for elected choice after overlapping providers, appropriations, remedies and next-window demand have been reconciled. The present evidence leaves its affordability open. |

Household care and expenses, private production, public-enterprise
balance sheets, external commitments, imported physical inputs and
other out-of-perimeter resources stay visible beside this account.
Do not treat a statistical surplus or domestic asset as an owned and
authorized source. Each candidate must use the same continuing
rights, prices, scope and ordinary and adverse dates. The new access
test supplies a constructed mechanism and a cost structure only.
Neither it nor the historical envelope prices the nationwide change.
