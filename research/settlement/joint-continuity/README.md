# Joint continuity and the first budget

Design232 · English research · [Canonical budget rules](../../country/C06/65-joint-continuity-and-first-budget.md)

This module compares several plans for the same adverse interval. A
plan must meet simultaneous tasks, survive the declared common failure
and pay for its recurring operation.

- [Four duties and two failures](02-four-duties-and-two-failures.md)

The [first-budget and later-cohort comparison](../whole-budget-continuity/README.md)
tests annual financial and care commitments. The
[shared-reserve case](../shared-reserve/README.md) tests mutual aid
and simultaneous receiving. This module carries an explicitly timed
legal and electoral workload through both a physical and a budget
constraint, with independent decision makers and original records.
