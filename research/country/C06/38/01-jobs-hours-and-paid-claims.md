# PA01–PA04: work, hours, pay and continuing claims

*Part of [C06/38](../38-work-pay-prices-and-country-income.md); design93 research.*

**PA01 — Income and production must support one another.** The country account
joins demanded products/services, producers/imports, jobs/persons, assets and
cash/claims. Statistical forecasts may use original observations, samples and
validated aggregate models; no complete government directory of private life or
prior permission for every firm/job is required. But a chosen wage total minus
public payroll is not evidence of private employment, production or collected tax.
Retain FD's residual-F algebra as a frozen comparator, not this national supply plan.
GDP is derived from compatible production/income/use accounts, not chosen to fund
the proposal. A balanced identity can describe an unexecuted or impossible plan.

**PA02 — Distinguish people, paid equivalents and usable time.** Record actual
employer/role, normal hours, paid leave/breaks/learning, qualifications, location,
shifts, wage, status, acceptance and continuing obligations. Annual role bounds,
seasonal people, constitutional capacity, contracted hours and actual delivered
work are different quantities. One person/hour cannot serve incompatible roles.
Do not infer assent, full-time availability or private money pooling from a job,
relationship, household group or replicated account. Preserve chosen informal
cooperation without assigning it an unpaid public balancing duty.

**PA03 — Resolve the low-wage reference honestly.** In the55,000-worker fixture,
15,000 people earn12,000CU/year with unspecified hours. At the authored median
and TW32-hour minimum, full-time ordinary cash pay must reach18,000:90m extra
gross employer pay. Alternatively, consented part-time contracts can retain12,000,
with lower paid-hour supply. Neither choice occurs automatically; existing hours/
salary, notice, bargaining, minimum-pay dates and remedies retain their source law.

At two-thirds hours, five shorter days yield806.4 annual task hours, while three
longer days yield747 because paid-break incidence differs. Half-time yields604.8.
Do not multiply full-time task hours mechanically or call55,000 people55,000 FTE.
Use source-defined median hourly earnings for TW and independently defined W for
Y/tax; annual-income rank, GDP/worker or a balancing residual is neither statistic.

**PA04 — Follow the employer, worker and insurance effects.** A funded wage
rise can change prices, profit, hiring, I05/IC, tax, contributions and future Y
units. Do not silently take it from an unexplained private payroll or guarantee
unchanged owner returns. A12,000→18,000 worked-pay change adds600 employee Y
contribution, not600 unrestricted general tax. Low pay alone earns no Y06 credit:
qualified care/illness/disability/involuntary-unemployment periods and their
actually covered earnings must match. No eligible period means no G credit;
full eligible-year assumptions can instead change300 to0. Partial periods require
same-period earnings, within the annual ceiling. Prior units remain untouched.

Where an old contract really continues, preserve actual due pay, notice,
severance, training and earned/lease/creditor rights. Superseded fictional wage
assumptions are not actual contracts; unknown actual claims are not zero. Losing
or declining a job does not itself remove personal needs protection or political voice.
