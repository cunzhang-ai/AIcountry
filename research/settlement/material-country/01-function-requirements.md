# Function requirements: a current scope, not a finished cost estimate

*Design 84 · The [machine-readable matrix](../../2026-10-material-country-and-public-backbone/requirements.json)
uses all 37 existing function owners and their canonical sources.*

For each owner, map demand/events, task, useful time, duration/grouping/travel,
qualified role/place/shift, ordinary/leave/teaching/standby time, site/material/
maintenance/renewal, independent review, actual payer/source/currency and accepted
receiver. Cost quantity×actual rate by period and owner, plus genuinely distinct
capital/overlap/contingency. Reconcile shared joint work once; incompatible roles
need separate time. Unknown quantities/rates/qualified capacity produce unknown
requirements, never a national zero total. Actual claimant help needs no dossier.

The matrix retains every existing owner, actual null and old bid. It adds an
explicit requirement recipe and package/dependency scope, not invented demand,
qualification or a claim that37 rows exhaust national functions. Current later
RP/CD/TS/CP/CJ/MB/UA/RR/FA duties are included in the scope; their unknown
incremental requirements do not disappear into a historical gross overhead.

For conditional context selection, sector/local authorities derive quantities
from actual affected/service/case scope and safe useful times, give uncertainty
and rivals, and propose finite sources/commitments. Assembly law sets coverage,
source, maximum exposure and dates. Qualified safety/clinical judgments and
independent legal challenge constrain operation. A high invoice cannot allocate
its own funding; a low budget cannot redefine an overdue claim as no need.

Supply cannot be extrapolated from nominal GVA. Tax/bank finance and separately
owned FX have counterpart claims; stock, renewal, material/ecological limits and
household/voluntary-work displacement need their own account. No matrix/check
qualifies every function until its actual requirements and simultaneous sources
are estimated and safely tested. This missing calibration remains material.
