# Design393 decision. Treat expiring protections as a joint budget choice

## Baseline and selection

[Design392](predecessor-design-392/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md) requires independent
fiscal evidence, a four-year budget path, lawful release and distinct
continuing duties. It leaves a specific application open. Several
temporary measures can end together while budget comparisons assume
continuation, a full release of resources or a new tax receipt at the
wrong date. Household need and lawful commitments can survive the date.

Select [FB48–FB54](../country/C06/62/09-expiring-protection-and-budget-choices.md)
under the [working research method](../FRAMEWORK.md). Require the enacted
expiry path, a separately identified continuing-policy path and feasible
replacement choices on common dates. Track overlapping effects across
people and providers, actual resources, valid accrued claims and funded
receiving. Docket known decisions in useful time while preserving
ordinary votes, prior authority and independent review.

## Alternatives and objections

Retain ordinary budget review with literal statutory expiry. This keeps
legal authority clear, but separate votes or accounting lines can omit
a concentrated loss or late source. An automatic extension would smooth
some cliffs, but would create commitments beyond the voted duration and
could keep ineffective measures in place. Prefer an express decision
with expiry and lawful fallback visible. Where the need is durable,
ordinary recurring provision may be better than repeated renewal.
Compare direct repair, shared delivery or household transfers on the
same group, dates and continuing obligations. The constructed case
selects a finite bridge plus repair and recurring provision under its
assumptions. Other contexts may support renewal, ordinary expiry or
a different intervention.

Recording a material expiry and giving advance notice has recurring
cost. Join related material items under DB and FB instead of requiring
a new dossier for each payment. An analyst can report a failure or
qualification gap while members retain the right to vote. Do not infer
individual circumstances from a combined administrative record.
Parties may challenge evidence with protected information. A common
fiscal gap does not establish whose lawful service should wait.
Use only the competent prior-law or newly voted decision to resolve it.

## Evidence, transition and next action

[Primary records](sources.md) distinguish legal expiries, forecasting
conventions and observed aggregate spending. The [constructed case](../settlement/expiring-protection-and-budget-choices/01-two-supports-and-a-late-receipt.md)
tests the release and renewal arithmetic, overlapping needs, delay
and an actual recurring source. It does not establish local prices,
participation, authority, readiness or observed improvements.

Adopt prospectively with paid records, Clerk scheduling, independent
fiscal analysis, affected access, appropriation and accountable receiving
owners. Preserve earlier lawful ends and notices, already accrued
rights, claims, existing floors and separate source authority. A failed
replacement does not extend an expired programme, and a lawful expiry
does not discharge an independently owed duty.

Next reconcile one jurisdiction's two same-season expiring supports
with dated appropriations, actual cash, overlapping affected demand
and available alternate service. Compare scheduled expiry, renewal and
a funded replacement. Reopen if a receipt moves, an exposed group is
omitted, existing duties continue at a different cost or a better
qualified alternative emerges.
