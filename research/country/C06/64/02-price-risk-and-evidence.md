# Pay for service and price the risk

*Part of [PL public concessions](../64-public-concessions-and-future-budget-control.md).*

**PL05 — Put risk on the party that can change or carry it.** For
each material risk, record who controls it, who initially pays,
who ultimately bears the loss, and the mechanism that enforces
the allocation under failure. Check independent equity,
collectible security and contractual recourse against the
actual loss, timing and common ownership. A weak vehicle with
a guarantee from the same failing group does not transfer its
risk. Limit a project's exposure to risks outside its control
when this produces lower public cost or better reliable
service, and state the retained costs. Test the whole relevant
public portfolio for shared lenders, suppliers, indexes,
foreign currencies, ecological and demand shocks.

**PL06 — Make public guarantees exceptional and finite.** Ordinary
private demand, refinancing and currency losses stay with
the parties who accept them, except for a separately voted
specific guarantee. A public commitment to buy agreed units
of actually accepted service is an ordinary specified payment
duty. It cannot conceal a guaranteed profit or a payment for
unsafe service. Each exceptional guarantee states public
purpose and alternative, beneficiary and ultimate controller,
trigger and verification, currency and cap by period and total,
expiry, funded cash path, any fee or upside share and recovery
rights. Do not guarantee every bidder's financing or rely on
unpaid future households. Report direct obligations,
contingent commitments, and service rescue that the public
might later choose in separate columns. Assess simultaneous
calls against actual lawful cash and foreign settlement.
Within the applicable DB/RF authorizations, assign each shared
reserve once by simultaneous claim and due date. A new award
that would exceed the available joint cover needs a new elected
source or revised terms before commitment. An expected loss
estimate is not a cap or available funding.

**PL07 — Pay on independently verified service and usable access.**
Distinguish construction acceptance, maintained availability,
actual receipt and valid accrued payments. Match each
performance payment and deduction to safe, usable delivery and
review. Allow the supplier to contest a deduction promptly.
Continue undisputed earned payments under SC. Protect users
with a reachable correction route, the regulator's actual
tariff and access powers and a workable fallback. Price all
bills, fees and indirect charges under the same service
comparison and show who bears them. Public procurement,
clinical and regulatory judgment remain separate. A cheaper
public payment achieved by shifting an unaffordable bill or
unpriced maintenance to a household is a different choice.

**PL08 — Keep the contract and operating evidence independently
reachable.** Publish the signed commitments, guarantees,
beneficial control, key risk and payment schedules, material
amendments, current exposure and expiry. Protect personal and
legitimately sensitive technical details by narrow reasoned
redaction with an independent route to challenge. Grant
Treasury, sector regulators, audit, protected parliamentary
scrutiny and courts actual access within their distinct powers.
An operator must provide verifiable condition, maintenance,
performance and trigger evidence. Keep usable originals and
a lawful alternate custodian so neither the buyer nor the
provider controls the only record. AI may flag comparisons,
hidden overlaps and missing evidence. Competent officers
verify original terms and own decisions, and elected bodies
retain the fiscal choices.

[DB26–DB32](../60/10-public-guarantees-and-common-fiscal-risk.md) puts
concession guarantees on the common fiscal-risk account. Keep PL06
exceptional authority, original terms and independently verified claims.
