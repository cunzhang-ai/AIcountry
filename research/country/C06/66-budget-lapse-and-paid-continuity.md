# C06. Budget lapse and paid continuity

*Part of [public finance](../C06-public-finance.md). Design242 research selection.*

**BLC01 — Fix the scope before lapse.** The Assembly establishes, through
ordinary law and subject to rights, the continuing essential-obligations
schedule separately from the expiring annual budget. For each line, identify
the existing legal duty, eligible recipient or service, amount or formula,
due or useful-time date, authorised funding source and ceiling, ordinary
and alternate payer, lawful accepting party and protected challenge route.
Treasury and the duty owner verify cash and qualified delivery by the
relevant date, including simultaneous calls against the same money or
provider. Report uncovered duties as exposure. The executive cannot add
a purpose to the standing schedule by renaming a bridge expense. An omitted
line or cash shortage does not cancel a statutory claim. Review coverage in
public before each budget year ends. Give affected people and opposition
members usable access to challenge omissions and propose changes.

**BLC02 — Preserve democratic choice through the lapse.** Day 1 begins on
the first day of the new fiscal year without a valid successor budget. Keep
the existing bridge for specified operations through day 90 and the
standing schedule for its own purposes. By day 30 publish a dated account of
continuing duties, bridge commitments, anticipated arrears and competing
successor options. By day 60 record a vote under the Assembly's existing
procedures on a lawful budget or a separate limited continuation. Members
can introduce funded alternatives even under a caretaker. An obstructed
vote or late disclosure warrants direct review under existing procedures.
It grants no appropriation, extends no term and does not stop the clock.
A lawful later vote can change future authority on its own terms.

**BLC03 — At expiry separate operating authority and settlement.** At the
start of day 91 the bridge ceases to authorise operations. Before lapse, an
Act may separately reserve a named source and nominal ceiling to settle
eligible bridge debts on days 91–120. The research candidate selects this
30-day closeout. It covers only fixed commitments lawfully incurred during
the bridge under its actual scope and settlement terms. Work must have
been earned or goods delivered by the end of day 90, with verification
under the original contract. Record the actual incurred, earned or delivered,
due and settlement dates. A late invoice or
appeal may establish a pre-existing debt. Neither creates new authority or
resets a deadline. The closeout carries no grant, extension, renewal, new
work or transfer to a different purpose. The Act names the disposition of
the remaining balance at expiry. Treasury records the actual reserved and
unspent balances and reconciles every payment with the successor budget
to avoid duplicate authority or payment.
At the end of day 120 the separate permission expires. Unpaid lawful
claims retain their dates and remedies. Identify the other authority or
subsequent appropriation required to pay them. Rights and obligations under
a different lawful source keep their own terms.

**BLC04 — Route refusal and shortage differently.** Treasury and the duty
owner classify each unpaid or undelivered line by payment authority, usable
cash in that purpose, operational acceptance and actual receipt. Disclose
more than one failure if applicable. For unlawful refusal or payer outage,
use a verified lawful alternate within the same purpose, ceiling and source.
Prior law names who may activate the substitute and the conditions for
doing so, with prompt independent challenge. The official whose refusal
is challenged cannot control activation or the substitute's access.
The B19 court-ordered route applies only to its adopted scope. Outside that
scope, the alternate needs its own prior legal authority. Independently test
access to source records, credentials, payment rail and final acceptance.
A second signer on the same disabled system is still exposed. Reconcile
the alternate's payments to the original debt before retrying. Where all
routes fail, report the missed due date, preserve direct challenge and seek
lawful restoration. Where cash or authority is missing, report the exact
gap and take a lawful source or appropriation proposal to the Assembly.
A reviewer may compel existing lawful execution. New taxes, borrowing and
changes of protected purpose require their own law.

**BLC05 — Trace arrears to people and continuing services.** Report the
earliest threatened deadline before interruption, the due and actual amount,
which people or services bear delay and what interim protection is actually
usable. Preserve private case evidence in protected custody. A recipient can
raise a missed payment or service through accessible direct channels without
reconstructing the budget. For unsafe service risk, use the competent
prior-law protection and verify actual receiving. A late appropriation or
payment can repair some losses while the earlier breach, lost voting or
appeal window and associated remedy remain visible. Handover preserves the
claims, records, authorised access and tested payers. It leaves the fiscal
clock, deadlines and actual allocation of power in force.
