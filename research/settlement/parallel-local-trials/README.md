# Two local experiments and one catchment

*Design297 · Constructed public authority and resource comparison.*

Two elected councils propose lawful but different water-use
trials. A tests a small reuse retrofit and B tests a different
recirculation scheme. Their operations would use three and four
units of the same residual water in one window. Six units are
available after protected drinking water, prior valid uses and
ecological duties. A downstream locality does not sponsor either
trial. It retains access to reasons, a useful hearing and existing
water, environmental and remedy rights. The units are authored.

| Exact option | Shared water in the first / second window | Public cost | Disposition |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| A and B at their proposed size, together | 7 / 0 | 5 | No release against six residual units. Each could fit alone. |
| Narrow A and original B, together | 6 / 0 | 4 | Can fit with a separate A decision, actual shared authority and both valid permits. |
| Original A then original B | 3 / 4 | 5 | Can fit with a qualified second window and funded restoration between them. |
| A only | 3 / 0 | 2 | B's opportunity is deferred. Needs the exact authorized choice and accepted supply. |
| B only | 4 / 0 | 3 | A's opportunity is deferred. Needs the exact authorized choice and accepted supply. |
| Ordinary service only | 0 / 0 | 0 incremental | No trial effect. Existing services and environmental duties remain due. |

A narrower A can answer a smaller question. It cannot inherit
claims about the original number of properties or wider use.
The example's four-unit budget supports the narrower parallel
choice at the stated six-unit water bound. If the pre-release
bound falls to five, hold the parallel start. Either trial alone
can fit if its exact alternative has its own public authority,
permitted residual and funds. Otherwise keep the funded
ordinary route and return the changed proposal for decision.
A late second window or a changed season can affect what the
sequential experiment learns even if its water is payable.

A reports avoided use of potable water. B reports gross
withdrawals and measured return. These answer different questions
until shared boundaries, quality, timing and return flows are
reconciled. Preserve both observations and their limits. Predicted
future savings cannot finance either trial's current draw.
A shared data display or agent-generated merger does not supply
a missing water permission, local vote, budget or third-party
protection.

If a trial has already bound a lawful contract when a new limit
prevents pumping, stop or narrow within actual law and preserve
wages, accrued claims, restoration, existing service and review.
Nobody gains permission to continue drawing water merely to avoid
a payment. The first approved case, its later cash and any
continuing duty remain separate. An unassessed residual is unknown.
