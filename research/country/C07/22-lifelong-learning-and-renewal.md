# C07. Learning remains a public capability throughout life

Design173 ·4November2026 · AL01–AL27 proposed country design, not an enacted offer

**Choose person-directed continuing learning and meaningful qualifying renewal,
with academic autonomy and actual paid support.** People can initiate renewal
without a sponsoring employer or government production project. They can also
learn for civic, cultural, personal and community purposes. Participation is
voluntary; basic income, health, residence and political standing do not depend
on accepting training or using AI.

S06 already guarantees foundational catch-up, S07 a first qualifying pathway,
N06 paid project-training contracts and TW13 paid employment learning time.
AL preserves these and fills the independent continuing/renewal route. A modest
annual offer cannot replace a real first or renewal qualifying pathway.

The [joined choice and examples](../../settlement/lifelong-learning/README.md)
compares this public-service route with a smaller continuing offer, funded
project retraining and portable money accounts. Actual instructor/placement/
care/source capacity, independent affected/legal/academic review and outcomes
remain unknown. Public learning buys no professional licence, job or political
loyalty; a new credential or completed AI task is not demonstrated capability.
