# GA13–GA18: govern actual access and deliver actual correction

*Part of [general AI capability](../10-general-ai-capability-and-public-control.md).*

**GA13 — Permission identifies an act and its practical limits.** Name controller,
configuration, users/access class, tools/resources, capability boundaries,
conditions and evidence/milestones for widening. Prefer a demonstrated narrower
access or controlled staged exposure where it reasonably addresses the hazard,
not an automatic nationwide ban. Effective least privilege, independent physical/
network barriers and accountable operators matter more than an instruction to
obey. Do not assume stopping one process stops copies or prior commitments.
Permit scope is public subject to safe redaction; no secretly different standard
for government, major firms, universities or community projects.

**GA14 — Irreversibility changes the choice.** Releasing readily executable weights
can defeat later access controls. Assess incremental practical capability against
what is actually available, realistic adaptation/copying, defensive/research/
competition benefits and feasible limited alternatives. Open weights are neither
a blanket exemption nor a prohibited identity. Where GA02 applies and barriers
cannot accompany distribution, refuse that distribution with specific reasons;
consider a narrower workable route under GA11, not mandatory exclusive state
ownership. A refusal is reviewable, not censorship of ordinary theory or dissent.
No permit promises global recall of published weights. No generic mandatory user
identity or permanent prompt-surveillance system is selected.

**GA15 — Serious signals reach a responsible person.** Controllers must protect
people immediately within actual lawful control, preserve relevant safe evidence
and notify the Office without undue delay, at most24 actual hours after reasonable
recognition of a credible severe GA02 incident or failed critical barrier with
external exposure. Initial notice can be incomplete; useful updates follow as
facts become known. Distinguish observed harm, attempted action, near miss,
simulation and uncertain attribution. No need for a finished technical dossier.
Existing shorter urgent rescue/sector reporting and actual rights continue.
Protected worker/researcher/nonuser reports need safe ordinary channels and
anti-retaliation remedies, not corporate permission or public dangerous detail.

**GA16 — Stop only the power actually held.** An operator can suspend its own
unsafe access. The Office can issue reasoned V protective directions for the
specified controlled exposure or apply to ordinary courts for restraint, evidence
preservation and proportionate correction under this Act. Urgent coercion follows
B17/V04: independent meaningful hearing within72 actual hours, with at most one
strictly necessary72-hour extension. No restart, renamed classification or series
of orders creates indefinite unreviewed restraint. Nonurgent refusal/revocation
uses notice, adverse evidence, response, reasons and direct GA12 full merits.
No new arrest, covert search, device-wide backdoor, intelligence exemption or
cross-sector kill switch; police/sector bodies retain their own actual powers.

**GA17 — Withdrawal must preserve people and claims.** Name executable access
revocation, credentials, downstream notifications, lawful secure custody and
accepted service successors. Warn affected people safely and coordinate actual
sector owners, including essential clients, paid staff and persons relying on
an assistive tool. No essential-service abandonment, secret deletion of claims
or compulsory transfer to one vendor. Restore only the specific lawful service
once demonstrated barriers/receiver exist; the Office does not inherit another
sector's judgment. Copied weights may be unrecoverable: report that limit and
mitigate actual reachable harms without claiming a fictional rollback.

**GA18 — Enforce duties without inventing crimes.** This proposed Act authorizes
civil compliance/protective orders, full ordinary-court review and actual lawful
V09 execution/cost recovery with proved source and reasonable costs. It creates
no new VD offence, administrative punitive fine, imprisonment or strict criminal
liability. Existing offences require all their own prior elements, fault and
proof; incomplete notice, model size and an accident do not supply them. Civil
loss, injunction, privacy, product and compensation claims retain their distinct
elements; a permission is no immunity. Insolvency does not produce an unlimited
public guarantee. Practical remedies require identified assets/payers, accepting
executors and GA21 resources; a published warning is not delivered repair.

[WB protected disclosure](../../C08/31-protected-disclosure-and-evidence.md)
supplies outside access and civil remedies for protected reporting
while the existing GA and sensitive-information controls retain
their exact source and safety scope.
