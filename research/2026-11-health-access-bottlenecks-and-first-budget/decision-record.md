# Design429. Fund a verified bottleneck in the first health budget

## Decision and baseline

Design428 separates observed fiscal baselines from available money.
Design164 assigns a public clinical backbone, needs-based finance and
independent review. The remaining first-budget choice is how to
distinguish a treatment delay from cost or distance and use an
increment on the part of the care path that can actually change
receipt. This revision was prepared with AI assistance. Clinical
decisions and financing retain their competent owners.

## Alternatives and working choice

General fee relief is a viable option where payment is the verified
barrier. Additional assessment alone is a viable option where
downstream care has unused qualified capacity. Joined assessment and
treatment capacity can repair a waiting bottleneck, at an actual
price in workers, sites and downstream services. Transport, mobile
care and language support may better address a different constraint.
Select locally verified end-to-end capacity repair for a demonstrated
waiting bottleneck. Compare the actual rivals on the same budget and
days and preserve ordinary cost, travel and assistance rights.

The observed Danish and Finnish comparison motivates checking
the affected income groups and reported reasons. It cannot establish
the cause of observed differences or a funding level for a new
country. The illustrative allocation shows why more assessments
alone can leave treatment unchanged. Buying coupled capacity can
increase complexity and displacement. Require a named accepting
provider, paid cover and independent evidence of completed care.

## Scoped result and next test

The original survey and fiscal comparison qualify a reasoned priority
to test. Actual local cause, costs, authorized funds, service readiness
and resident outcomes remain open.

For one actual treatment path, use independently inspectable
attempted contacts, accepted assessments, referrals, treatment,
unreached groups and relevant cost and travel accounts. Compare
the same-law and same-budget alternatives through first receiving
and the next affected roster. Complete when the responsible network
and independent reviewer reconcile lost requests and actual care
and can show who still waits and why. No exercise budget or service
owner has been committed. Reopen on an excluded group, changed
bottleneck, evidence correction or a stronger ordinary alternative.
