# OJ16–OJ20: who judges the judges without becoming their employer

*Part of [C08/25](../25-ordinary-justice-and-public-prosecution.md); design120 candidate.*

**OJ16 — Ordinary status jurisdiction.** Prior law assigns ordinary appointment,
career/transfer, judicial discipline and personal-status disputes to a distinct
three-person ordinary appellate status formation; two affirmative votes of the
full three, all three lawfully present, are needed. Appeal in facts/law goes to
five different qualified appellate judges; all five present, three affirmative
votes of fixed five. The latter is final ordinary disposition, subject to CJ's
actual constitutional competence, not a new third general appeal. Use an annual
predeclared conflict-tested draw/rotation and lawful eligible reserves, excluding
selectors, investigators, underlying-case judges, actual sponsors/close personal
interests and first-stage participants. No lawfully appointed available formation
means no final status decision, not a ministerial appointment/removal. CJ12's
closed constitutional-own-court seven-person jurisdiction is expressly unchanged.

**OJ17 — Closed grounds, real proof and fair discipline.** Removal requires proved
serious corruption, deliberate judicial-office abuse, intentional material
qualification fraud, a continuing incompatible office or permanent inability to
perform essential duties after reasonable accommodation. Lawful judgments,
criticism, opposition, diagnosis or disagreement with management are not grounds.
Punitive misconduct requires proof beyond reasonable doubt; functional incapacity
needs clear and convincing actual-duty/accommodation evidence. Lesser established
misconduct can receive reasoned proportionate correction/censure with fair source,
reply and appeal; a label cannot hide a punitive measure or bypass its proof.
Suspected crime uses the separate P/K outside-command investigation/prosecution,
funded defense and fair trial. A misconduct complainant need not prove a crime
before lawful interim protection, records or an ordinary remedy.

**OJ18 — Finite suspension and removal appeal.** The three-person formation may
unanimously order paid suspension only on evidenced immediate risk to people,
proceedings or judicial acts, for at most 14 elapsed days; meaningful adversarial
hearing within seven days and immediate five-person appeal. One fresh reasoned
14-day renewal is the maximum; same facts/labels cannot restart the outer 28 elapsed days from first suspension,
including gaps. Removal
is stayed for a seven-calendar-day appeal opportunity and, on timely appeal,
until final five-person disposition. Inaccessible notice/counsel or serious
blocked access permits a reasoned appeal extension, not reward for concealment
or an extension of the suspension ceiling. After that ceiling duties resume
absent final lawful removal, with independently justified particular-case recusal;
serial recusals cannot become an unreviewed office-wide suspension. Actual
consensual medical leave/accommodation remains available without treating an
unable person as fit or compelling work. Investigation
continues but delay is neither exoneration nor removal. No timeout lowers votes,
extends custody, assigns a replacement judge or cancels protected claim dates.

**OJ19 — No judge finally reviews their own personal case.** A status judge's own
case goes to a different preassigned formation under OJ16; criminal allegations
use OJ29 outside-command prosecution and unimplicated investigators. For Court
of Appeal appointment/administrative acts, exclude actual deciding selectors/
actors, not the whole judiciary solely for diffuse institutional interest.
Public reasons disclose ties without making allegations disqualify everyone.
A direct personal pay/tenure interest cannot be cured by calling another beneficiary
external. Prior-law visiting ordinary judges may be used only if already properly
appointed, actually unconflicted and paid/available; a remuneration contract
subject to the contested rule does not make them neutral. If every lawful route
has genuine direct conflict, disclose the absence of a final decider; preserve
undisputed dues/urgent claims without inventing a sovereign arbiter or necessity
exception contradicting CJ12. Mere universal naming is not such a conflict.

**OJ20 — Protect parties, not only officeholders.** Status proceedings have safe
public reasons, protected originals, funded independent representation where
needed and affected-party notice/reply. Preserve lawful case allocation, original
custody/claim clocks, actual prior orders and criminal finality when a judge is
replaced. A successor can rehear material evidence under fair law, not rubberstamp
secret notes or discard all prior acts. Complaint, discipline, civil liability
and guilt are distinct; no personal immunity from proved own wrongdoing and no
automatic damages for an honestly unconflicted legal error. Ordinary appeal and
actual B/source responsibility remain, with no new blanket Treasury guarantee.

[AA09/AA29](../../C04/04-public-audit-and-accountable-oversight.md) expressly
proposes OJ16 three/five status jurisdiction for AG appointment/removal/pay and
audit-office budget legality/retaliation. Actual adoption supplies that extension;
OJ/CJ words alone did not. Ordinary report/record/contract/staff claims keep
actual B/OJ/WL scope, not automatic special-status formations or CJ12 by analogy.

[PO04](../27/01-services-leadership-and-appointment.md) expressly proposes this
ordinary OJ16/OJ18 status process for the named Police Chief and Civilian
Investigation Director, with PO's actual-duty grounds and conflict exclusions.
It is no general expansion to all civil servants, CJ12 own-court jurisdiction
or actual adopted procedure. Corrections' ongoing career uses C04 ordinary review.
