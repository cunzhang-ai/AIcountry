# KR29–KR32: one account, continuing claims and a replaceable selection

*Design 95 research candidate; not enacted law, a funded offer or demonstrated
superiority.*

**KR29 — Do not promise the same return twice.** C06 records grant cash, prize
liability, procurement for delivered goods, licensing fees, royalty shares, equity
ownership, taxes and any avoided prior purchase by owner, period and scope. A funded
invention's agreed public-use license may eliminate *that* later license fee; it does
not erase manufacturing, quality, distribution, repair, background IP or independent
private investment. A creator's retained royalties are not public cash; tax on realized
income is a separate claim. Expected patents, GDP uplift, future royalties and
hypothetical price savings do not fund current I05/IC duties or replenish the IC window.
Count lower price, higher wages, retained profit, public receipts and dividends as
alternative allocations of the same realized value, not additive windfalls.

**KR30 — Entry and old claims.** Before any effective date, publish a
jurisdiction-specific inventory of domestic/foreign rights, pending applications,
accrued royalties, licenses, public contracts, litigation, treaty duties and critical
medicine/seed/software dependencies. New terms and grants operate prospectively; valid
prior rights, accrued causes and lawful public-domain status are not silently canceled
or revived. Named successors maintain registries, source copies, royalty payment and
pending review. Where a lawful transition or public-use license changes an existing
claim, identify payer, compensation basis, price, due date and appeal before use; fund
needed substitutions and legal aid in C06's single operating book. These unknowns
prevent claiming an authorized ready launch. An adopting Act chooses explicit scope and
dates with the required notice and paid bridges; an official’s missing inventory or
readiness certificate cannot postpone an existing or already commenced right. New
ordinary rights/exceptions/office default start is the first 1 January at least 18
months after actual enactment, subject to expressly enacted source/cohort dates and
stronger old rights. New crimes or longer burdens are prospective only; actual leniency
and expired bars retain the P/Q migration route. New publication/licence terms apply to
notified future calls, not retroactive release. Existing
grant/royalty/wage/privacy/service and appeal obligations, public-domain status and
permitted past uses survive with named funded successors; NP preparation/disposition,
protected U/Y/H/TW and current constitutional/software/contribution boundaries remain.

**KR31 — Compare the actual architecture.** Retain/repair private exclusivity plus
ordinary procurement is less administratively demanding and can reward risky investment,
but may preserve essential bottlenecks and repeated public payment. The selected
limited-rights/licensing plus plural grants/open procurement preserves
private/cooperative invention while paying for access and qualifying execution. A
patent-free prize/public-research architecture avoids some exclusion and litigation but
shifts agenda, valuation and failure to public funders and needs actual recurring
revenue; it can replace the selection in a defined field. Do not absorb it as compulsory
universal central planning. Registry, patent counsel, grant committees and public buyers
can still form one patronage network; shortened rights/open terms can also reduce
investment or drive secrecy. No current evidence establishes the selected model as
superior.

**KR32 — Review distribution and reopen.** Report who applied, was paid, received
credit, could license or repair, made a usable product, remained excluded, and bore
work, ecological, supply or foreign harms. Compare private exclusivity, full-cost
public-open work, a capped prize and the mixed contract on the *same* purpose and time
horizon. Preserve dissent from creators, patients, workers, small entrants and foreign
suppliers; a funder's or model's second opinion is not independent participation.
Evaluate funding, legal adoption, accepted capacity and observed lives separately.
Reopen on material title, price, safety, actual supply, creator-income or public-access
evidence. A no-change finding is valid. A source-backed annual account covers grant
choices, creator receipts, examination delay, upheld/reversed rights, royalties, real
accessible copies/repair, excluded users, concentration and public-use delivery, with
safe source families under EV. No new central personal dossier or truth score. Next
compare the joined country’s unresolved substantive gaps and strongest complete rival
before following another local IP question; actual source-law and professional
calibration remain bounded later work, not invisible completed evidence.
