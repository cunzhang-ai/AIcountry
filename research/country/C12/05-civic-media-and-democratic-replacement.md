# CM01–CM05. Keep democratic choices usable when civic media change

*Design458 · Part of [future collaboration](../C12-future-collaboration.md).*

**CM01 — Keep the civic act with its lawful public receiver.** When a
public decision depends on a mediator, the commissioning body names
a competent receiver for each operative act. The receiver controls
the evidence of receipt, authority, timing and the exact choice,
independently of the medium's supplier and the political sponsor.
Budget direct, assisted and offline access on the original calendar.
Let local forums and participants choose media and interpretive
methods under their own authority. A common receiver for a shared
decision verifies only its lawful scope. It acquires no authority
over private dialogue or another community's decision.

**CM02 — Preserve the distinctions that made a choice possible.**
Preserve the submitted expression or a lawful way for its custodian
to present it, its relevant context and protected limits, the
interpretations actually offered, explicit author corrections and
material objections. Keep an attributable record of consequential
filtering, grouping or ordering decisions, promised replies and the
lawful reasons for selection.
A new medium may express meaning differently. It may carry verified
equivalent meaning, mark an unsupported aspect or ask for qualified
interpretation. It cannot silently assign a single interpretation,
change a proposal's operative scope or give a grouping legal effect.
A participant who cannot share sensitive evidence generally can
present it to the authorized reviewer under existing protected
procedures. Use the minimum evidence and retention required for
the act, with a safe route to correct or lawfully withdraw it.

**CM03 — Replace the medium without restarting people's rights.**
Separate the public issuer's decision to change the commissioned
route from participants' decisions to use another or withdraw
an agent mandate. Keep original lawful receipts, unresolved
objections, protected deadlines and standing duties through the
change. Check authority and revocation at each affected act under
C12/02. A transferred identifier, old summary or later agent replay
cannot create support or erase an earlier valid choice. Require
fresh confirmation only when meaning, authority or effective
opportunity was materially impaired. An independently verified
unchanged choice retains its effect. Preserve the authors' disputed
meaning and original legal effect for any already adopted act
under ML and ordinary review.

**CM04 — Restore the affected decision through existing rules.**
The responsible public office compares the consequential public
choices before and after migration, including exclusions from
discovery and cases outside the provider's export. Give affected
forums reciprocal notice of material objections and a usable reply
when separate agendas omitted a shared consequence. A separately
accountable reviewer checks material differences and can require
a scoped correction within existing powers and deadlines. Show
which result could change on the unresolved claims. Do not infer
missing support or create a general stay. Where an unresolved
defect could change a protected disposition, withhold that
disposition under its governing rules. Preserve independent,
lawfully severable business. If independently checked interpretations
remain substantively different, let each local forum keep its lawful
practice and disagreement. For a shared binding act the competent
authority considers an exact common scope, separately votes new terms
or defers the inseparable part. A shared record or agreement on one
fact allocates no common worker, budget or scarce resource. Use existing hearing, sponsorship and
appeal routes if the original window closes. Preserve democratic
closure, individual challenges and the fact of any lost opportunity.

**CM05 — Prove that replacement works under disagreement.** Before
depending on a new commissioned route, test material dissent,
non-equivalent media, unavailable common upstream custody, scoped
revocation, overlooked eligible participants and actual use before
the deadline. Fund a separately controlled alternate able to receive
and correct, including trained interpreters where needed. Compare
a central mediation service, replaceable media with a minimal public
record, and lawful direct hearings or local practices. Retain ordinary
direct practice where it supplies the same protection with less
burden. AI may offer renderings and flag discrepancies. Competent
humans determine materiality, principals decide consent and elected
bodies decide public obligations. Reopen on hidden agenda control,
unusable evidence, recurring exclusion or a better equivalent route.
