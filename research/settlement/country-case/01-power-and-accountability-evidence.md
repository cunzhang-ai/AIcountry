# Original-study reports: power and accountability

*Design 85; author reports read through primary publications/author-hosted copies.*

| Study / inspected comparison | Result and transfer boundary |
|---|---|
| [Acemoglu et al., JPE 2019](https://economics.mit.edu/sites/default/files/publications/Democracy%20Does%20Cause%20Growth.pdf): country panel, dynamic controls/reweighting/regional instruments; pp48–57/79–82 | Authors estimate a positive long-run democratization/GDP relationship. Nonrandom identification and political treatment bundles limit interpretation. Not annual growth, startup finance, distribution, exact CJ/Assembly form or public-control measurement. |
| [Ferraz–Finan, QJE 2008](https://berkeley-econ.github.io/ffinan-site/papers/published/Finan_Audit.pdf): municipal lottery audit/public release before versus after election; pp710/717–721/744–745 | Average reelection effect is statistically insignificant; responses differ with disclosed corruption. Local-radio heterogeneity is observational. Audits and disclosure are combined; no automatic incumbent defeat or independent media-treatment estimate. |
| [Olken, JPE 2007](https://economics.mit.edu/sites/default/files/publications/Monitoring%20Corruption%20Evidence%20from%20a%20Field.pdf): randomized government audit and separate participation interventions in village road projects; pp202–209/243–244 | Audit treatment reduces engineering-estimated missing expenditure; extra participation does not significantly reduce the overall average. Missing expenditure is not identical to theft; family hiring substitution appears. No universal audit rate, national saving or criminal guilt follows. |
| [Olken, APSR 2010](https://economics.mit.edu/research/publications/direct-democracy-and-local-public-goods-evidence-field-experiment-indonesia):49 villages, direct ballots versus representative meetings with agenda generation unchanged; pp243–246/264–266 | Reported satisfaction/knowledge/legitimacy improve; general project-selection effects are smaller. Small sample, unchanged agenda and increased lobbying limit transfer. Not national constitutional consent, completed-project quality or FA's numeric design. |

The two Olken studies share a programme setting/research lineage; site overlap
was not established. Published and working versions of a study are one family,
not replications. Related Brazilian lottery papers can share the same programme.
Root/agents inspected reports, not underlying country codes, raw transactions,
engineering observations or individual ballots; external data validity was not
independently audited. These studies do not validate one combined constitution.

The canonical disposition is retain practical democracy and separate competent
audit, public understanding, contest and actual correction. Add bounded random
plus risk-directed audit with anti-targeting safeguards and actual delivery
checks; no extra oversight institution or fixed100% rule is inferred. A fair
electorate may retain an incumbent. Evaluate correction and freedom, not a
required political result or attendance count.
