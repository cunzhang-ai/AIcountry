joins a national allocation to each local payer, funded deadline and
protected duty. The [comparison](../../settlement/equalization-and-local-service/01-balanced-total-and-local-deadlines.md)
reproduces the nationwide and scoped local arithmetic. A constructed
case checks competing local calls and remaining recurring costs after
a temporary source expires. Existing MF and LD remedies retain their
own authority.

This closes the proposed disposition rule for this interface.

lives are separate open conditions. The integrated opening programme
still needs a common dated funded schedule and legitimate decisions.
