# GF01–GF05. Return a persistent formation deadlock to the electorate

*Design526 · Prospective constitutional option and implementing law.*

**GF01 — Open one public route after the first repeat fails.** Apply only to a
lawfully constituted Assembly elected at the first formation repeat, after that
Assembly has reached the existing four-rejection or day-30 boundary without
effective lawful investiture. The ordinary government-formation rules remain
available. The Clerk publishes the original dates and outcomes. Where an act
was withheld, use the substitute sitting and direct judicial review first. The
independent electoral authority opens the route on the original chronology once
lawful failure is confirmed. If the Clerk withholds the record, use the existing
alternate custodian and urgent judicial process. An operative order affecting
legal competence or formation controls qualification. A disputed or annulled
Assembly follows its own electoral remedy. Treat absence of a viable nomination, rejection and unlawful
obstruction separately. No cabinet, party or Speaker may call this poll on its
own. Use at most one recovery ballot per original fixed national term. A later
short-lived cabinet, resignation or change of Assembly does not reset it. Use
one petition window for the same failed repeat. A separately authorized
correction or recount concerns the same ballot and runs within GF04's boundary.

**GF02 — Let electors request a binding choice.** From verified public opening,
allow 30 calendar days to collect valid support from five percent of the
corrected opening national electorate, rounded up. Count qualifying electors
once, with revocation before close, protected originals, direct assisted and
offline access and timely review. Support requests the vote. It does not endorse
either outcome. The independent electoral authority publishes its count by day
45. Permit qualification challenges for ten calendar days after usable notice
and direct urgent review of unlawful delay. A positive final determination by
day 65 triggers one poll under prior law. A lawful investiture before the call
ends this request. Once called, the electorate decides whether the Assembly
should be replaced. A later investiture may inform voters and cannot cancel the
ballot. Missed determination is an independently reviewable failure. It does
not constitute an adverse count or confer power to hold a late optional poll.

**GF03 — Keep the Assembly until a lawful successor can receive its powers.**
Use one voting period for two independent ballots. One asks whether to keep the
Assembly or replace it at the lawful handover. The other selects a conditional
successor by existing full proportional national election rules. People may
cast either ballot or both. Replacement requires more valid yes than valid no
votes, with yes votes from at least 25 percent of the corrected opening
electorate, rounded up. Both results must be lawfully certified, with usable
notice, independently operative under applicable election law and free of an
operative order blocking transfer. A successor must be able to convene on the
pre-enacted calendar before any current mandate ends. Apply ER17's ordinary
filing and exceptional discovery clocks to each result under the implementing
law. A pending petition supplies no automatic stay. Preserve timely petitions,
interim orders and later invalidation remedies through handover. Neither a
pending count nor a valid yes vote standing alone creates a
vacancy. A valid paired result seats the successor for the unexpired original
term. A government appointed since the call enters existing limited caretaker
status at successor convening and faces ordinary formation again. A further
failed formation in that Assembly does not produce another recovery election
under GF or restart the spent first-repeat trigger for that impasse.

**GF04 — Protect the regular election and ordinary duties.** Use the existing
14-day voting interval and close 90 days after call. Before opening, use the
latest qualifying call date and allow at least 30 days after close for
certification, independent review and handover. The calendar must allow lawful convening at
least 60 days before first voting in the ordinary election. The last permissible
day for that paired disposition is the same boundary. If timely disposition
fails, preserve the lawful Assembly's original term and ordinary election.
Count correction can establish what happened without reviving an expired
replacement authority. Before transfer, a corrective GF poll may operate only
within this boundary and with its own lawful preparation period. After a
completed transfer, apply ER17's rules on prospective invalidation of a seated
Assembly, including lawful correction or repeat and no revival of its predecessor.
The regular election retains its independent calendar. The existing constitution, ordinary contest and judicial
remedies govern the latest lawful office dates. Protect opposition, polling
and accessible campaign access, separate original custody, review and execution
through the applicable elected funding and independent payment routes. Prior
law establishes the protected readiness costs and capped activation source for
the petition, both ballots, correction and handover before this power can take
effect. Budget continuing service, court and ordinary election work on the same
staffing and cash calendar. Each due claim keeps its original date. A competent
Assembly can still vote standing supply. Missing government formation alone
does not trigger the post-bridge supplement for loss of a competent Assembly.

**GF05 — Require its own adoption and evidence.** Any competent adoption uses
the applicable protected amendment process and prospective succession rules,
including independent qualification and actual handover. No officeholder gains
an extra term and no model, budget or petition decides investiture. Use separate
ordinary authority for any reform to executive selection. Compare continuity
with the current Assembly, a voter-triggered paired decision and an automatic
second repeat on the same public costs and deadlines. Test strategic refusal,
short-lived appointments, fragmented results, petition exclusion and concurrent
service and remedy demands. If after adoption a promised independent path fails,
apply existing timely remedies and report the specific nonreceipt. Do not
rewrite either ballot or take staff and money already committed to urgent duties.
Dates and thresholds are hypotheses for public decision and actual costing.
