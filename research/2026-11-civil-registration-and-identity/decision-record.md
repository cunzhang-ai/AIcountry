## Alternatives, risk and authority

Retaining or repairing existing local practice can preserve trust and
avoid burdensome migration. A common national number can improve
routine matching and speed verification, while propagating a wrong
status or a power abuse across many services. User-held proof can
reduce disclosure with real issuers and recovery, but does not reach
people alone when witnesses, devices or records fail. Select common
public accountability and limited exchange with actual offline
assistance. Show manual verification, repeat work, protective delay
and downstream execution costs before any comparative claim.

False positive and false negative death links both matter. Independent
second-officer review of materially disputed links and duplicate merges
can reduce irreversible error, while causing delay or embedding the
same office's bias. Preserve AP urgency, keep the affected proof and
care usable, retain the direct court route and verify recipient action.
The Assembly sets powers and funds, Cabinet and the career service use
actual C04 appointments, the local intake handles evidence and outside
review examines wrong or stalled acts. An authority cannot both
arbitrarily remove proof and prevent a usable challenge. Protected
purpose and necessity limit queries, including official requests. No
AI score, source label or simple matching rate decides the law.
