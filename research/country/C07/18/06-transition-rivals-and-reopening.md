# AS21–AS24: assistance remains revisable without leaving people unprotected

*Part of [C07/18](../18-person-directed-assistance.md); design 116 candidate.*

**AS21 — Enact a coordinated source and migration.** Proposed assistance,
TW/I/S/B/Treasury/custody amendments start 180 calendar days after competent
enactment, with paid preparation and exact person/employer/worker/contract/account/
record/source successors. S/E and already commenced rights keep their actual
earlier dates; no readiness certificate delays them, changes political power
or postpones an election. At commencement the public non-employer route and
scoped pay backing are obligations, not a voucher offered instead of actual care.
Unfinished procedures retain prior lawful protection and independent correction.
The AS date does not restart TW's separately enacted hours/pay stages or delay
any earlier employment, care or payment obligation.

Do not unilaterally convert an existing recipient into an employer, nullify a
lawful direct-employer election, seize funds, erase wages/leave/notice/pension or
force a new provider. Preserve stronger net/source/contract, support, tenancy,
creditor/estate, tax/contribution, privacy and foreign claims with paid overlap.
Actual transfer of employment or private contract needs source-law authority and
required understanding/assent; an indexed file or new service name is not that act.
Public AS12 subrogation is prospective to its payment/dues scope, not automatic
nationalization of old debt. Common method, actual software/constitution and
contribution permissions remain unchanged; no new procedure adopts itself.
The competent coordinated Act expressly places AS12 established public dues and
AS19 state orders within its adopted B19 payment appropriation/executor scope;
it supplies no new tax, compulsory worker, general damages guarantee or AI power.

**AS22 — Compare models that could displace this one.** Retain direct employment
with funded advice/backing, person-directed public/contracted employer provision,
user/worker cooperative routes and voluntary actual pooling. Management can be
chosen or delegated; no preferred social form is compulsory. The selected public
non-employer option reduces unchosen employer exposure but can increase public
pay/source burden, paternalism, scarce-worker concentration and provider capture.
Direct employment may give more control while exposing the person to difficult
conflict/liability; voluntary pooling can help or suppress privacy/spontaneity.
Compare whole source/control/quality/time and safe transition, not only hourly cost.

Reject cash-only abandonment, mandatory congregate care, unpaid family reserves,
workplace rights waivers and unconditional AI substitution. Voluntary residential
support retains the same ordinary civil/consent protections; no forced sharing or
diagnosis-based placement follows from a cost advantage. Disagreement over shared
living, risk and public burdens cannot be averaged into an unchosen routine.

**AS23 — Learn without claiming the test is a good life.** EV preserves qualified
original evidence, source families, missing views and safe correction. Inspect
actual choice/exit, relationships/privacy, supported agency, worker pay/rest,
missed care, harmful transitions and cumulative controller power, not only spent
budgets or completed tasks. Paid independent/affected inquiry and legitimate public
authority remain missing in this project; root-authored scenarios and current
original sources are neither actual participation nor independent final review.

Price all included/incremental/overlap/replaced/unknown help, skilled shifts,
retainers, payroll/advocacy/courts, worker-dues backing, insurance/claim losses and
foreign/equipment/transition sources once against the full country commitments.
Earlier bids and useful-time promises are no funded current capacity. No fines,
free AI, assumed contribution recovery, client/S/pension/insurer/CCP/FX sweep or
second allocation of the same worker pays this. No actuarial/source or cost saving
is established by a fictional rate, annual staff count or simple roster test.

**AS24 — Reopen, and return to the whole country.** Reopen on coerced employer
status, unusable route/exit, inadequate allocation, unpaid dues, unlawful home
control, forced work, unsafe/nonaccepted receiving, abusive mediator or a stronger
whole rival. Missing monitoring means uncertainty and continuing claims, not
consent. New evidence may justify retain/no change, rejection or a different model.

The next identified national gap is ordinary employment law: actual worker/
employer classification, formation and variations, fixed terms, dismissal/
redundancy, collective representation/action and usable remedies. Existing TW
already supplies hours/pay/leave and MP protects bargaining from cartel law;
they do not yet define that complete positive labor settlement. Finish a bounded
coherent contract/exit/collective-power candidate with meaningful employer/worker
alternatives and funding, rather than extend every care scenario indefinitely.
Complete local provider/working-cash and full national tax/actuarial/FX/legacy
sources remain missing; do not invent them. Actual urgent harm or a larger
country contradiction may take priority over this desk-research selection.

Design117 [WL01–WL40](../../C05/12-work-contracts-and-collective-power.md) now
specifies ordinary employment law. AS's28-day notice/full defined backing and
contact-versus-employment distinction remain; WL notice/advance caps do not reduce
them. Match any actual same-item wage advance once, preserving distinct employer
contributions and remaining actual AS dues. New work-law dates do not reset AS.
