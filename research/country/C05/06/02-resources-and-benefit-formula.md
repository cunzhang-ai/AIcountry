# Income clauses I04–I06: resources and benefit formula

*Design 76 proposed clauses; RP06 classification added, old formula retained; [income package](../06-income-floor-and-safe-resources.md).*

**I04 — Count actual usable resources.** Assess the person's after-tax and
lawful contribution earnings, received nonwork income and accessible
nonessential liquid assets. A due but unpaid benefit, blocked account,
controlling partner's wealth, primary home, necessary work tool or a future
loan shall not be cash. Law shall protect a three-month essential-resource
buffer and specify a fair twelve-month draw plan for usable assets above it;
record consumed stock once and reconsider a genuine loss of access.
Unrealized property value cannot compel a harmful immediate sale.
The agency assembles relevant state-held evidence and assists an ordinary
declaration; no universal family dossier or open-ended transaction surveillance
is an entry condition. Misrepresentation requires evidence and fair correction;
emergency protection depends on actual need, not a presumed collectible debt.

**I05 — Top-up with work retention.** Define `N` as the personal basket plus
unmet housing and recognized necessary work/dependant-support expenses,
less only matching actually received in-kind components. Preserve the
person-controlled cash component. Define `E` as after-tax/contribution work
income; do not deduct a recognized expense again from `E`. Define `O` as
received nonwork resources, and `A` as the lawful usable-asset draw.
The ordinary payment shall be `max(0, N - O - A - r × max(0, E - d))`.
The candidate `r` is 0.5 and `d` is 20% of the age-appropriate personal
basket, once per person and period. Public assistance and cost compensation
shall not themselves be taxed into a lower floor or fed back as resources
to erase their own award. Contributory pension remains paid in full;
disregard 20% of its received amount up to 20% of the personal basket in
this separate needs test. The basic old-age payment is a received nonwork
resource, never a reason to cancel an accrued insurance claim.

Compensation for recognized disability/access cost is a distinct due
payment or actual service, independent of earned-income withdrawal. Match
it against that cost once; an unprovided service is neither cash nor a
discharged claim. Uncovered necessary cost enters `N` with its own remedy.
Necessary work travel or paid care enters `N` only where not reimbursed or
actually delivered elsewhere. Recognize statutory employment/parental duties
without making unpaid relatives the funding source or paying a private
luxury choice as a necessary expense.

**I06 — Support received, support owed and internal transfers.** Credit
only safely received support, not a household head's promised access.
Recognized necessary support paid for another eligible person's needs
may enter the payer's `N` only with a matching recipient credit and no
second expense deduction. The same cash, period, bill or expense is reconciled
once; excess voluntary gifts or resource diversion shall not automatically
create a new ordinary entitlement. A mere shared address or relationship
shall not deem a transfer made. Safe component-level in-kind credit cannot
replace all personal cash autonomy. A child's missing legally owed support
triggers immediate public protection and separate lawful maintenance
enforcement against a responsible adult; it is not the child's debt or
proof of parental guilt. Voluntary pooling remains possible without waiving
individual eligibility, confidentiality, consent or direct payment.

Prospective anti-avoidance rules require stated grounds, necessary evidence
and independent review; they shall not deny actual urgent necessities or
invent coercive family support. [RP06](../../C08/20/03-gestation-and-noncontingent-payment.md) now classifies
received after-tax reproductive contribution/time allowances and replaced
earnings in E for this needs test; exact recognized cost refunds match once.
No presumed payment, employment obligation or tax exemption follows. The new
hypothesis needs fresh cost/incidence review, not reuse of the old macro pass.
Unsupported resource semantics stay disclosed,
with interim protection and human correction rather than a silent zero.

[TW11](../10/03-leave-learning-and-income.md) now proposes treating net actually
received statutory work-replacement cash as I05 E for the same disregard/taper,
without calling it worked labor or Y contribution earnings. Salary/replacement
matching, tax/credit dates and actual receipt need the prospective coordinated
law. Current software and accrued/source-law rights remain unchanged.

[PA13–PA16](../../C06/38/04-residual-needs-receipt-and-correction.md) now states
the IC-destination residual with received ordinary U deducted once, proportionate
tax attribution on other actual income and protected source-period correction.
U never also enters O; missing U/refund is no resource. Residual/access exemption,
current-law rights and unimplemented transition protection remain distinct.

Design115 [I14](05-component-standards-and-cash-control.md) and
[I17–I19](06-received-services-time-and-replacement.md) now make
ordinary need at least `max(B − Q, 0.20B)`, plus separately unmet housing,
work/care/access and uncovered due-period essential replacement. Q requires
applicable source law and matching actual voluntary receipt; public MO/S rights
are no ordinary credit by default. The minimum belongs inside N, not an extra
payment; I05's withdrawal, separate earnings disregard and protected resources
remain. Actual U matches once in the conditional residual, never again in O.

[Design142 PF10–PF12](../../C06/57/03-public-rights-resources-and-survivors.md)
keeps E before voluntary private saving and unavailable capital out of current
cash. Tax-indexed basis is not resource capital: trace already counted A,
actual nominal capital and new income once. For new genuine private retirement
income the existing aggregate pension disregard applies at Y02/Y04 or lawful
disability/survivor qualification, not merely by a fund label or early cash-out.
This prospective source-category choice preserves stronger old rules, the same
rate/cap and separate ordinary need; no new eligibility or receipt is presumed.

**Design145 land-capital join.** [LA16](../../C09/13/04-continuity-misuse-and-paid-transition.md)
keeps already-A capital conversion, actual newly received income, inaccessible
court-held claims and necessary costs distinct. A forced transaction creates no
new I05 disregard, family resource pool or O/A duplication. Cash/replacement title,
ordinary source gains and actual access require the existing rules; a headline
award or blocked compensation is not received basic protection.
