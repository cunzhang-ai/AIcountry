# NS17–NS20: selection, transition and the next discriminating comparison

**NS17 — Record a strategy choice, not a new funding certificate.** Select
service-first diversified retrofit, retain regional common provision as a
structural rival, and reject rents, export maximization, all-sector autarky or
assumed AI productivity as the default basis of rights. Retain actual RF/MC
and the finite separately authorized fiscal-issue comparison. The circuit
changes interpretation of source/asset/saving claims, not country spending,
taxes, source ownership or monetary authority. More financial assets, lower
domestic financing need and an accounting balance are not sufficient progress.

**NS18 — Preserve all due rights and paid transition.** Existing/commenced
services and I05/U/Y/H/TW obligations, jobs/pay, pensions/earned units, deposits,
creditors, contracts/title/licenses, cases, records and foreign duties survive
through OP/T successors. No prospective strategy, missing forecast or technical
certificate delays their lawful dates or an election. Any changed scale, currency,
tax, investment or monetary architecture needs competent express law and its
own incidence/source/claims migration. The independent circuit units have no
exchange or rescaling to country CU/FU. Actual constitution/software/contribution
permissions, holds and common proposed research method remain unchanged.

**NS19 — Keep the hardest objection.** The preferred strategy remains broad;
tax collection/distribution, household and firm behavior, costly essential inputs,
skills, public/worker/user capture, ecological harm and external consent may
defeat its scale. Resource/common-pool or more public provision may work better
in a verified context. These are not missing forms for an already proven winner.
Accounting identities constrain coherent claims; they supply no original demand,
productivity, independent affected judgment, safe supply or legitimate mandate.
Research selection is positive; evidence qualification is bounded; actual legal
authorization, funded operation/received remedies and better lives are unverified.

**NS20 — Next test against a whole observed economic baseline.** Prepare one
published whole-economy comparator, retaining its actual public/private/foreign
boundaries, prices, year, service/household/production and legacy scope. Identify
which proposed-country changes are specified, costed, unpriced or incompatible;
compare retain/no-change and at least one genuine income/provider/source rival.
Do not rescale GDP per head into a local tax base or transplant entitlement,
currency, free assets or public consent. Use original aggregate evidence rather
than sensitive private dossiers. Completion is a finite account of what the
baseline supports, contradicts or leaves unknown and a reasoned strategy/scale
disposition, not an inverted export/GDP requirement or a new fictional sector.

Existing researchers can prepare it, with no actual staffing/budget/affected
authority implied. Reprioritize on urgent harm/larger contradiction. Reopen the
strategy on original productive/source/distribution/external/control evidence,
new objection or a better whole alternative. Missing monitoring preserves
uncertainty and duties, not consent or demonstrated superiority.
