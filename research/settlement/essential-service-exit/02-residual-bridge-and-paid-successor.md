# Replace the essential operator on a schedule people can use

## The same duties, dates and choices

This authored case has 100 lawful payments of one unit each due
on day 10 and the next ordinary run on day 35. Assume an ongoing
payment duty, no authority to keep using the original award, and
a court hold that permits only a separate lawful residual purchase
under SC12. All operating costs use a separate source from the 100
units of benefit principal. Unpaid principal remains held for its
own duty. An old refund claim of eight is recorded as unresolved
collection and has produced no cash. The next payment run needs
its own principal and ordinary operating source.

The qualified alternate can safely pay 70 recipients by day 10.
It can qualify the remaining 30 by day 24 if it obtains the necessary
records, funding and access on day 4. The verified incumbent can still
serve the residual 30 by the deadline under distinct lawful authority
with no other change in the service. The arrangement reserves exclusive
release by recipient and the original claimant challenge. Independent
verification needs two units, separately paid, by day 10.

| Choice on the same due scope | First-run effect | Dated operating cost by day 10 |
|---|---|---:|
| Transfer immediately to the existing alternate | 70 paid on time, 30 exposed until qualified receipt | 5 successor + 2 verification = 7 |
| Retain the entire operation through a proposed new bridge | Could preserve all in physical terms, but takes the available 70 out of the proven alternate and exceeds the declared court scope | 10 incumbent + 5 successor preparation + 2 verification = 17 |
| Transfer 70 now and authorize a narrow bridge for 30 | All 100 are payable if the existing authority, cash, acceptance and records work by day 10 | 5 successor + 6 residual incumbent + 2 verification = 13 |
| Use a standing direct public operator built before failure | Can avoid reliance on the incumbent if staff, records, source and independent review already work | Requires a comparable ordinary lifecycle and reserve account. No immediate full capacity is assumed. |

Select the narrow bridge in this case if the required operating
source is available before day 10, including the five already
needed for the successor on day 4. The bridge expires by day 30, and the successor
has accepted the remaining scope by day 24 in the qualified path.
Verify actual receipt in the next run before closing operational
oversight. A failure in the last 30 does not stop safe work for
the first 70. Actual authorization, custody and funds are separate
from the number of cases an operator says it can process.

## Compare delayed finance and lost control

The ordinary operating source is 13 by day 10. In the weaker case
11 arrive by day 10 and two more by day 15. The total still reaches
13, but the split route has a two-unit early gap. A verified
lawful acceleration can close it without increasing the total.
Neither the old eight-unit refund claim nor the 100 of recipient
principal is an available source. Paying the two-unit verifier from
the already fully assigned fund would displace delivery. If the
acceleration fails, use the verified payable parts, record exact
exposure and seek an authorized source. The case supplies no rule
for ranking recipients of equal standing.

If the original supplier controls the only usable proof, all
100 are exposed to a hostile withholding of that proof, including
the supposed alternate's 70. If protected originals cover the
70 but the evidence for 30 is unsafe, only the independently
verified scope can release. The alternate must actually receive
the missing evidence and be paid before the original useful date.
No legal or technical authorization resets that date.

## Account for recovery separately

Suppose a later qualified judgment fixes eight units of net gain
and the estate actually pays two into the legally available
recoveries account after day 35. The original operating gap is
still two at day 10. An outstanding claim of six remains. If a
separate earlier order already covered part of the same gain,
deduct the overlap when assessing the additional amount, using
the original periods and legal scopes. Preserve any distinct claim
and any protected principal. Do not offset different creditor ranks
or count the same collection a second time. Financial closure has
its own competent authority and can follow a successful handover.
