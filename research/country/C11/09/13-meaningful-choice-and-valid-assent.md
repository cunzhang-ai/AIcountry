# DP58–DP63. Protect meaningful choice and valid assent in public services

*Part of [public digital operation](../09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md). Design348 research selection.*

**DP58 — Govern the effect on choice in every medium.** The responsible
service owner records the lawful purpose, material alternatives, default,
likely burdens and people affected whenever a public system materially
shapes a choice through ordering, recommendations, timing or prompts.
Apply the same duty to human scripts, forms, conversation, AI agents and
future interaction methods. An apparent explanation that materially steers
people remains reviewable. Publish the rule and reasons in usable form.
Apply ordinary DR or primary-law requirements where a change alters a
binding criterion, burden or consequence. A supplier update cannot
authorize a policy change.

**DP59 — Give decline and change a usable path.** Present lawful options and
their consequences before a material choice, with paid accessible human
help and a usable non-AI route. Keep the ordinary path visible at first
contact. Refusing an optional feature, research use, profiling or adviser
does not delay, reduce or condition a due service. Measure effort,
wait, price and actual receiving on both paths. Tailoring for language,
accessibility or goals a person states remains available. Public operators
and contractors cannot use undisclosed sensitive inference to pressure
people into optional disclosure, withdrawal, a more burdensome route
or a choice serving a political, supplier or cost-cutting target.
Retain truthful, clearly attributed public advice within its mandate.

**DP60 — Require actual assent for the act that needs it.** A lawful
withdrawal, waiver, optional material data permission or comparable act
requires a distinct affirmative expression after usable explanation and
a chance to correct it. Keep the minimum proof of the exact act, terms,
date, lawful actor and relevant authority. An authorized representative
or agent acts only within an express mandate where delegation is lawful.
Silence, a completed visit, continued service use, an inferred preference
or an agent's own statement cannot establish the person's assent.
Honor equally usable refusal or revocation through the original route
or an independent route. Preserve lawful accrued acts, applicable notice
duties and statutory due benefits paid without an extra application
under TU. An automatic favorable statutory payment is a legal duty and
creates no unrelated permission.

**DP61 — Review the choice before materially relying on it.** Before release,
compare a clear ordinary path and the proposed method on the same
rights, staffing and deadlines. Test comprehension, ability to change
course, repeat contact, mistakes, receipt, and people who leave before
filing. Test representative access needs with affected people and
independent review under lawful authority. Give evaluators the operative
scripts, versions and safe samples, including downstream agent actions.
No experiment may conceal material consequences, waive rights or
withdraw standing help. A take-up or cost target is evidence only within
its measured scope. An assent rate or shorter queue cannot substitute
for lawful decisions or timely receiving.

**DP62 — Correct an invalid choice in useful time.** A public owner who finds
credible evidence of material misrecording stops reliance on the affected
choice and keeps a funded ordinary route. A competent officer preserves
the original record and date, checks what was actually authorized and
restores the prior lawful position or reopens the choice where needed.
End unauthorized optional data use, review prior disclosures and use
lawful retention, restriction and remedy rules. Notify affected people
through a usable safe channel. A direct independent challenge requires
no vendor permission. Apply LX proactive redress for a common error.
Pay for any restored service and review through the original deadlines
and keep late or unserved cases open.

**DP63 — Keep sources and accountability through changes.** The sector owner
pays for effective alternatives, evidence, independent review and remedy
under the elected budget. Carry any lawful scoped mandates, pending
choices, open claims and paid receivers to successor channels. AI may
explain options and check mismatched records. It cannot supply consent,
waive rights or certify its own process. Publish safe results by affected
group and route. Reopen on coerced choices, asymmetric delay, selective
routing, new platform behavior or a simpler equally usable method.
