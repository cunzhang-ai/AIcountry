# Design374 commitment. Fund large connection phases and charge attributable exit costs

[EG13–EG18](../../country/C09/19/04-large-grid-connections-and-paid-capacity.md)
requires named capacity, dates, qualified work and enforceable backing
for each committed phase. Preserve protected services, lawful environmental
limits and separate court and budget authority. Count actual reusable
assets and received funds once. Carry accepted claims and deadlines when
a project changes owner or withdraws.

The [worked comparison](../large-grid-connections-and-paid-capacity/01-two-load-windows-and-an-exit.md)
supplies no actual connection, public guarantee or licensed works.
