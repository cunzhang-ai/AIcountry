# WL54–WL60. Give people time and safe pay to respond to violence

*Design386 · Proposed ordinary labor law within [work contracts and collective power](../12-work-contracts-and-collective-power.md).*

**WL54 — Protect time needed to act.** A covered worker can take
necessary leave to address interpersonal violence, stalking or
coercive control affecting them or a dependent in their care when
the action cannot reasonably and safely occur outside work.
Examples include obtaining care, safe housing or advice, attending
a proceeding, securing a dependent's arrangements or restoring
independent access to money. Coverage follows actual work and
control, including short-service and irregular workers. No police
report, prosecution, departure from a relationship or joint
application is required. Leave is a work protection. It makes no
finding of another person's guilt or authority over the dependent.

**WL55 — Make leave usable before the deadline.** Select up to two
of the person's normal workweek equivalents in a rolling 52 weeks,
available from the start of covered work, usable in partial shifts
and separate from annual, medical and other leave. Count each
qualified lost hour once across jobs. Calculate normal work under
TW from actual paid history, confirmed shifts or agreed short-service
work. Consider evidence of hours removed to defeat the right. No
actual lost work means no employment pay claim for that interval.
For an urgent first absence, accept protected self-attestation
provisionally and allow safe notice as soon as practicable, including
after the absence begins. Later review may require proportionate
lawful evidence through an independent protected receiver. No
unrelated dossier or disclosure to an implicated controller is
required. Keep ordinary safety, subsistence and advocacy routes
available where this leave ends or a person has no covered job.

**WL56 — Pay normal wages through an actually safe route.** The employer
owes usual regular pay for covered lost work on its ordinary pay
date. Preserve actual lawful stronger terms, with the same-hour
amount matched once. Use a private, worker-chosen reachable route
and a truthful ordinary pay statement without a specific safety
leave category. Separate protected proof and balance from routine
rostering, notifications and payroll access. Give a manager only the
time, authorized absence and changes needed for safe cover. Keep
original payroll and accrual evidence available under scoped lawful
audit. Ask about safe contact and receipt rather than assuming a
familiar account or shared device works. Missing privacy or a sent
instruction is not evidence that pay reached the worker.

**WL57 — Provide an independent route when the employer cannot act safely.**
The existing labor protection authority receives a direct claim
where disclosure to the employer or use of its payer is unsafe,
contested or unavailable. It verifies the limited employment and
leave facts and issues the minimum lawful work instruction needed
to protect absence. It can authorize a public advance of due leave
pay under an enacted ring-fenced general-revenue schedule. A separate
public payer executes actual safe receipt by the ordinary pay
deadline where the route qualifies. Use existing earlier hardship
protection where waiting for pay would defeat immediate safety.
Record, reconcile and recover an employer's displaced obligation
only on lawful evidence and with fair review. An expected recovery
is no first-payment source. Preserve undisputed pay and good-faith
subsistence. Genuine independent workers retain ordinary support
and any expressly enacted separate replacement, without fictional
employment, accrued hours or duplicate pay.

**WL58 — Preserve employment and challenge retaliation directly.**
Do not deny access, reduce shifts, discipline, dismiss or expose
someone because they request or take protected leave. Apply
ordinary lawful reasons, evidentiary protections and fair reply.
The worker may seek a safe schedule, place, contact or temporary
duty change under applicable work and safety law. The competent
inspector or court can order existing due pay, a narrowly tailored
interim protection, lawful restoration or another available remedy.
Verify the roster, safe notice and payment separately. An allegation
does not automatically displace a colleague or determine a criminal
case. No unresolved employer grievance blocks independent review.

**WL59 — Cover the work and the person on the same budget.** Keep
leave pay, actual substitute cover, confidential receiving,
verification and the next shift or payroll separate. Employers
plan ordinary lawful cover without making absence depend on a
colleague's unpaid work or excessive hours. A public advance needs
actual gross cash, private receiving, independent payment and an
alternate route. If elected bodies fund relief for smaller
employers, define it separately by actual eligible cost and source.
No staffing shortage or late reimbursement suspends due leave.
Keep shelter, clinical, dependent and income duties on their
original terms, with the unresolved gaps visible.

**WL60 — Keep control with the worker and review delivery.** Migrate
prospectively with named labor, payroll, service and review actors,
retaining accrued and stronger rights and safe original dates.
Audit safe receipt, lost hours, confidentiality, retaliation and
missed coverage without requiring public testimony. An AI may
reconcile lawful hours or flag late payment for human review. It
cannot determine abuse, infer eligibility from personal accounts,
contact a respondent or approve leave or repayment. Reopen on
unsafe disclosure, lost pay, inaccessible administration, displaced
essential work or a less intrusive route that delivers as well.
