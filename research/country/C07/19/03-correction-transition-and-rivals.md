# HN09–HN12: preserve consent, correction and legitimate change

*Part of [C07/19](../19-clinical-networks-and-specialist-access.md); design 123 candidate.*

**HN09 — Independent correction follows the actual complaint.** Preserve S,
sector clinical/coverage review, B/E and ordinary OJ courts for the particular
right/source; an independent reviewer is not the treating provider's purchasing
manager. Do not require a universal serial referral appeal before urgent relief.
Clinical error, coverage disagreement, unpaid source and absent supply are
different failures. Name the actual executor and useful-time interim protection;
a favorable review or compensation award is not a delivered treatment.

**HN10 — New communication assists rather than authorizes treatment.** Remote
consultation, shared situations, AI translation/triage and future media may help
understand choices, coordinate and preserve scoped clinical records. They do not
supply professional competence, hands-on care, free human oversight, patient
consent or foreign legal permission. Preserve unfamiliar meanings and known
translation loss, lawful non-digital contact, protected purpose-limited custody
and patient correction. Teleconsultation is not automatically an adequate
substitute for examination, procedure, trusted relationship or safe travel.

**HN11 — Commence additions with exact paid successors.** The coordinated
proposed HN health/finance/transport/AS/I/PD enactment starts new ordinary HN
duties 180 calendar days after competent adoption, naming actual dates, payer,
contract/claim/record/workforce successors and paid overlap under OP17–20.
Existing S/E clinical/coverage and stronger rights remain due earlier under their
own law; no ministry readiness certificate postpones them or an enacted date.
Preserve old cases, treatment continuity, workers/creditors, actual source and
foreign permissions, tax/insurance periods and patient confidentiality. No
contract/index self-enacts these obligations or creates new foreign authority.
HN creates no punitive class/new VD predicate or automatic criminal inference;
independently applicable P/K/V offenses retain their actual scope and proof.

**HN12 — Retain real clinical-system rivals and failure conditions.** A larger
local public specialty service, paid visiting teams/remote support, a regional
shared public service or plural external procurement can replace the working
network choice when actual need/quality/source/control comparison supports it.
Local redundancy may justify higher cost; travel can harm relationships and
access. An external route may fail on safety, foreign acceptance, capture or FX.
WHO's small-state discussion and NI's operational referral page motivate examining
these mechanisms, not their success here. This candidate expressly differs from
NI's published no-travel-advance rule by selecting direct payment/advances; it
does not import NI eligibility/panels or clinical exceptionality into S. No new
international-law, independent-review or superior-lives result is asserted.
