# Twelve authored boundary cases

*Part of [technical safety](README.md); not actual reports or simulated verdicts.*

| Case | Situation and proposed reasoning | Material uncertainty/actual obligation |
|---|---|---|
| TC01 | A fast road has a painted bicycle symbol but vulnerable users still mix. TD01's higher category requires actual separation; a sign or model reclassification does not suffice. Road owner design/maintenance duties accompany the 30 km/h mixed-street candidate. | Actual road geometry, visibility, access, vehicle/user evidence and useful enforcement remain unverified; the proposed number is no proof of safe streets. |
| TC02 | A robotaxi expands into flooding and calls its passenger the backup driver. TS05/TD01 require approved actual domain, responsible operating entity and feasible safe fallback; disclaimers cannot create competence or consent. | Actual sensors, stopping/escape, communications, workload and response need qualified domain evidence; no human or AI presence alone proves safety. |
| TC03 | A developer pays its preferred final assessor and the regulator uses the same test vendor. TS06 requires conflict-screened assignment, disclosed source/controller overlap and actual ability to disagree. | A differently named company or model is not independence; real alternate expertise/funding and source access must exist. |
| TC04 | A building owner missed a new form and residents face eviction, while a real blocked exit is also found. Missing paperwork alone is not imminent harm; actual hazard may require narrow protection, separate eviction authority and funded safe receiving. | Do not certify the hazard safe or call a closure ticket completed shelter. Occupants keep tenure, property, access and remedy claims. |
| TC05 | Two nominal companies use one dam/chemical barrier and claim separate safety budgets. TD06/TD11 examine actual common control, cumulative failure and shared physical capacity, with source-based individual attribution. | Common control and actual limits need proof; aggregation is not guilt by association or double counting of restoration resources. |
| TC06 | A clinical device has an export-only certificate for a different configuration. TS06/TD09 check same product/purpose/population/local supply and source; recognition cannot simply import a logo. | Actual clinical/quality evidence, lawful recognition, care access and adverse events remain separate missing facts; unnecessary duplicate trials also need justification. |
| TC07 | One representative drinking-water sample detects E. coli, while the annual average looks good. TD07 requires prompt investigation/protective response and real safe alternate supply, not a statistical deletion. | Sampling/method uncertainty needs actual review; a 100 mL candidate does not cover chemical/radiological safety and boiling is no universal cure. |
| TC08 | A worker reports a credible near-catastrophic barrier failure without a full dossier. Receive/protect, warn as useful and preserve original uncertain facts. TS07's 24-hour ceiling is not permission to wait. | Protected silence/privilege and actual required operational logs remain distinct; no automatic guilt, blanket evidence immunity or public witness file. |
| TC09 | A security/criminal allegation threatens to stop an accident investigation. TS08 preserves distinct competent safety learning alongside independent justice and court-resolved evidence rights. | ICAO's inspected 2026 announcement gives Amendment 20 applicability as 23 November 2028; that is not already operative law for our hypothetical country. |
| TC10 | A vendor delivers an urgent narrow security patch and a larger autonomous operating domain together. TS14 permits validated correction within a prior safe-change envelope; the domain expansion requires scoped prior reassessment. | Actual dependencies/new incidents may invalidate old support even with matching hashes; dangerous rollback or withheld essential access needs real continuity. |
| TC11 | A domestic firm claims an app license authorizes foreign vessel boarding and spacecraft launch. TD04/TD12 distinguish actual jurisdictions/foreign consent and the prior particular space Act; a software or safety certificate grants neither. | Actual treaty status, authority, accepted foreign cooperation, liability and long-term safe cessation remain unknown. |
| TC12 | No qualified assessor is available and the government wants to fund inspection through fines. TS12/TS13 reject pursuing-office fine income, silent approval and a rolling veto. TS15 preserves actual current law while safe assessment/receiving is funded. | An ordinary fee schedule, shared regional service or public provider needs actual capacity/authority; neither more forms nor a missing reviewer solves a real hazard. |

Preserve safe source reasoning and dissent. These cases identify what a court,
operator, affected people or technical reviewer would need to establish; they
supply no real facts, independent approval, actual license or successful remedy.
