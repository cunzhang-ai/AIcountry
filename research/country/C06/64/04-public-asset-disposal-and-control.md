# PL13–PL20. Decide what the public gives up when an asset changes hands

*Design397 · Proposed ordinary fiscal and service duties within [concessions and future budget control](../64-public-concessions-and-future-budget-control.md).*

**PL13 — Identify the public interest, control and continuing duties.**
A proposed material disposal must identify actual title, beneficial
control, legal authority, users, employees, leases, encumbrances,
maintenance, environmental obligations and future uses. Cover sales
of assets or equity, long leases, sale and leaseback, control rights
and related staged disposals with materially equivalent effects.
Assess linked transactions and real controllers together. An internal
transfer changes custody or mandate where lawful. It creates no new
consolidated proceeds. Statutory public ownership, public trust, protected
ecological or cultural obligations and other legal restrictions survive
until validly changed through their own process. Ordinary delegated
disposal of low-risk surplus property may proceed under published
limits and audit.

**PL14 — Compare retention and alternative use before locking the decision.**
Independently assess retain and repair, public repurposing, a bounded
operating lease or concession, and disposal where each is credible.
Use the same service, quality, time, legal scope and opening condition.
Price renewal, risk, retained liabilities, regulator and alternate
capacity, forgone net receipts and residual asset value. Show dated
usable cash, fair market ranges, consolidated finance, public option
losses and distribution separately. Compare waiting as an actual option
with its costs, opportunity and downside. Publish assumptions and
sensitivity. A market bid can help value a defined bundle. It cannot
by itself value a continuing right or show that cash serves the
public better than the asset.

**PL15 — Require accountable elected authorization for material transfers.**
Under proposed national and local law the competent elected body votes
the exact control change or a bounded sale mandate. A mandate identifies
the bundle, acceptable terms, protected conditions, price floor or
lawful exception, permitted period, review and retained obligations.
Return a material variation or expired mandate for a fresh decision.
Where basic law requires continued public ownership or another decision,
obtain the required authority separately. Elected authorization
does not dispense with planning, competition, sector approval or
review. Publish usable evidence in time for opposition and affected
users, workers and communities to challenge it. Temporary withholding
of genuinely bid-sensitive or protected material needs independent
access, reasons and a dated release review. Ordinary regulation and
future budget choices retain their lawful scope.

**PL16 — Check actual control and competition before accepting a bid.**
Separate the elected sponsor, transaction team, independent valuer,
sector regulator, settlement officer and audit. Use open competition
for a material sale unless a defined exception is independently
reviewable, reasoned and expressly authorized. Publish qualifications,
actual conflicts and the award rationale. Check beneficial ownership,
common lenders and relevant market power with competent regulators.
Price support, exclusivity, options, financing and restrictive covenants
with the bid. No private undertaking may transfer public lawmaking,
licensing or adjudication. A below-market grant of value for a lawful
public purpose must show its size, eligible recipients, alternative and
enforceable result, with separate elected authorization. Preserve
access to urgent independent relief before an irreversible close.

**PL17 — Fund service and rights before closing.** Identify the successor's
actual operating authority, accepted service, safe staffing, wages,
records, access, maintenance and receiving. Preserve valid tenancy,
labor, creditor, data, environmental and user rights under applicable
law. Assign legal obligors, independently enforceable commitments,
actual funds and any authorized interim operator through the transition.
Qualify continued essential service and a funded fallback before title
or operational control passes, according to the enacted mandate.
A sale price cannot replace an accessible service or release a protected
claim. If continuity or required authority fails, keep the lawful
existing route or use the voted alternate. No executive waiver or
presumed reacquisition supplies the missing capacity.

**PL18 — Keep usable proceeds and continuing risks on their own accounts.**
When sale is proposed to meet an early deadline, show which law permits
that use of actually settled net proceeds. Compare a qualified finite
retention bridge and an authorized concession or other practicable
route against the same early claim. Keep later principal, interest,
permitted service costs, foregone annual returns and retained or
lost control visible on the same calendar. Test a second settlement
and actual acceptance before crediting proceeds or handback.
Show the gross agreed consideration, paid closing cash, conditional or
deferred receipts, retained claims, transaction costs, debt settlement
and actual funded protection separately. Match indemnities and other
contingent exposures under DB. Count transferred liabilities only
when the relevant legal obligation actually moves. An indemnity from
an underfunded buyer provides no qualified capacity. Reconcile lost
net income and liabilities over later budgets. Net settled proceeds
enter the public capital account for voted reinvestment or debt
reduction. A separately voted finite transition can use an explicit
amount where lawful. Keep recurring services on actual recurring
sources. No same cash, asset, restricted fund or avoided cost can
support two promises.

**PL19 — Verify settlement, service and later commitments independently.**
At close, record actual settlement, transfers, original obligations
and unused conditions. Compare intended proceeds and outcomes with
dated cash, delivery, service quality, regulatory independence, worker
and user effects, and continuing public costs. Release safe contract
terms and the valuation record as limits expire. Audit and courts
retain independent evidence and remedy. Apply agreed recovery, repair
or lawful replacement where terms fail. Later price movement alone
does not prove corruption, nor does successful settlement prove that
users received the service.

**PL20 — Carry obligations through a later resale or policy change.**
Use lawfully binding successor conditions for ongoing service and
risk where the public relies on them. A subsequent transfer, provider
loss or insolvency invokes the applicable authority, oversight and
fallback. Preserve ordinary public options within law, with genuine
cost and feasibility of changing course visible. Apply prospectively
with paid independent appraisal, scrutiny, source custody and ongoing
enforcement. AI may reconcile terms or replay price and service
scenarios. It cannot set the reserve price, decide the transfer,
certify the buyer or release a service obligation. Reopen on lost
capacity, collusion, changed valuation, retained risk or a stronger
lawful alternative.
