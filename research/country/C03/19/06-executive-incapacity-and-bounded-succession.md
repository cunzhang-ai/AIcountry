# HT24–HT31. Keep government functioning through a temporary absence or a real vacancy

*Design398 · Proposed constitutional and ordinary-law duties within [lawful handover](../19-lawful-handover-and-actual-control.md).*

**HT24 — Authorize substitutes before they are needed.** The prime
minister nominates up to two consenting sitting Cabinet ministers
in order for temporary cover. The Assembly confirms each by a
recorded majority of its full statutory membership after public
notice and conflict scrutiny. Approval states applicable scope and
term. Keep a separate published schedule of qualified portfolio
substitutes under their governing law. A nominee, party deputy,
Speaker, career official or model holds no acting power by title
alone. The Clerk and Cabinet Secretary keep authenticated records
through separate custody and test lawful activation. Vacancies
or failed approvals expose gaps without invalidating the Cabinet
or relaxing the approval rule. Preserve Assembly power to replace
the government by its ordinary procedure.

**HT25 — Distinguish planned cover, sudden inability and vacancy.**
The serving prime minister can designate the first available approved
substitute for a planned absence by authenticated written direction.
The record gives the scope, start and end. For sudden apparent
unavailability, two current ministers with no claim to the affected
acting role or other material conflict may jointly record verifiable
facts showing that a necessary reserved act cannot await ordinary
contact. The Cabinet Secretary authenticates the signatures, independently
checks the source and direct contact attempts, and records the exact
activation and scope. Seek the officeholder's supported response where
possible. Try safe accommodations, including remote or assisted
communication. Disability, treatment, an allegation, a missing message
or political disagreement alone establishes no inability. Use the
roster in order. No notice or attestation declares the office vacant.

**HT26 — Limit the temporary powers and keep the original clock.**
Cover reaches only specified lawful, necessary acts that cannot
reasonably wait, including convening Cabinet and carrying out previously
authorized work needing a ministerial decision. Keep Cabinet quorum
and each minister's separate powers. A planned designation lasts
at most seven elapsed days. Sudden provisional cover lasts at most
24 elapsed hours from first activation. It confers no power to call
or cancel an election, alter succession, remove a rival, make material
new appointments, transfer public assets, expand emergency power
or bind new material fiscal or treaty commitments. Standing career,
sector and legislative duties continue on their own authority. An
expired delegation, repeat attestation or substitute lower on the
list cannot restart the clock for the same absence.

**HT27 — Hear a disputed scope before the next irreversible act.**
Give the officeholder, elected representatives and affected authorities
immediate access to protected evidence and an independent route to
challenge. Notify the officeholder safely, allow supported reply and
protect medical or other personal detail. A credible conflict over
activation or ability to return holds the disputed reserved act
pending a competent determination under HT04 and CJ. Keep lawful
uncontested operations and ordinary case-specific relief. The court
may determine competence or protect rights within existing CJ and
ordinary jurisdiction. It cannot select a government. Publish safe
reasons and an urgent hearing route, aiming at disposition before
the provisional period or an earlier useful deadline. If continued
absence is established and no operative order bars it, the Assembly
may authorize a single further period, at most seven elapsed days,
by recorded full-membership majority on the same constrained scope.
It must act before the current cover expires for continuity. An
unfinished review or missed vote supplies no further authority.

**HT28 — Start formation only when a vacancy actually exists.**
Death, effective resignation, lawful dismissal or another exact
constitutional vacancy event starts the existing formation process
on its own date. The Clerk records the underlying operative evidence
without deciding contested merits. Apply the same independently
reachable process if evidence conflicts. The limited caretaker
Cabinet continues within its own law. Its first available eligible
roster member may perform only expressly assigned caretaker
coordination through the applicable formation period. That role
uses the existing formation deadlines and confers no additional
ordinary government or election powers. A temporary inability or
the end of an acting period does not by itself start formation.
The Assembly may exercise its ordinary confidence and replacement
powers without requiring a finding about the officeholder's health.

**HT29 — Restore ordinary authority promptly when cover ends.**
An authenticated end to planned cover, established recovery, expiry,
a lawful replacement or an operative competent determination ends
the acting scope at its own legal time. Where recovery is contested,
use HT04 and the same independent route. Acting ministers cannot
condition a return on their approval. Custodians withdraw acting
access at its scope and verify the lawful holder's access through
HT05–HT10. Preserve valid acts and independently review disputed
ones. Neither return nor expiry erases a missed payment or relief
deadline. Inform affected institutions and publish a safe account.

**HT30 — Keep standing duties funded if every substitute is unavailable.**
Name protected career payers, service owners and independent reviewers
before reliance. Prospectively assign the routine execution of precisely
listed prior duties to the competent career or sector officers where
the applicable law permits. Specify the actual act, source of duty and
funds, ceiling, receiver, independent check and execution route. A
standing assignment can continue through exhausted formation; it
expires on its own terms. A former approval step may be removed only
by the competent adopting law and only for these bounded acts. Keep
actual policy, appointments and other reserved decisions in their
ordinary forum. Rehearse simultaneous officeholder, substitute and
record-custody failure. Budget actual Assembly and court receiving,
accessibility and work needed through the next deadline. A lawful
standing payment or service continues if its independent source
and receiver remain. Hold a genuinely unavailable reserved act
and report its effect promptly to the Assembly and affected people.
Seek lawful correction or replacement. No automatic promotion,
appropriation or blanket authority for the Cabinet Secretary,
judiciary, security agencies or AI follows.

**HT31 — Keep the duty reviewable, prospective and replaceable.**
Publish the active scope, original deadlines, legally received and
missed actions and any conflict or blocked remedy. Elected bodies
fund and scrutinize the chain, and independent bodies retain their
own authority. AI may compare times or flag inconsistent copies.
It cannot infer incapacity, authenticate office by itself or grant
powers. Enact through the applicable constitutional and ordinary
routes with paid transition. Preserve existing duties, appointments,
elections and lawful claims. Reopen on inaccessible proof, an
expired actor's accepted direction, failed independent review or
a stronger arrangement that preserves ordinary replacement.
