# Design428. Compare actual fiscal baselines before authorizing expansion

## Decision and baseline

Design427 selects a feasible incremental first-budget sequence under
constructed assumptions. It still needs an observed comparison that
can inform wider costing without lending its own balances to the
proposal. The project already carries a historical Denmark 2023
production account and a separate 2025 reference. This revision
adds a matched Denmark and Finland 2024 fiscal comparison. It was
prepared with AI assistance. Neither country is selected as a
jurisdiction or source of funds.

## Alternatives and selection

A single historical comparator is transparent but can hide which
choices rely on its particular financing or function mix. Combining
the most attractive totals from different sources gives an apparent
answer without a coherent economy or payer. Use both complete
country references on their own terms and separate fiscal totals
from functional allocation. In Denmark a mixed balance would show
3.3 instead of the reported 4.1. The analogous Finnish splice
would show −4.3 instead of reported −4.4. Keep the signed residuals,
the small displayed rounding effects and unreported API cells.
The source comparison does not determine the reason for the 2024
cross-table gaps.

The working disposition is retain continuing obligations and permit
only independently financed repairs or paid preparation until
the domestic operating account is qualified. A broader funded
reform remains a live elected choice. Requiring perfectly complete
foreign comparisons would delay justified domestic action. Hold
only the claim that depends on the unresolved foreign figure.
Show the costs of waiting and ordinary existing harms in the
same decision.

## Evidence, authority and migration

[Original snapshots and reproduction](sources.md) allow independent
inspection. The source figures establish fiscal and functional
accounting comparisons. They do not establish coverage, safety,
qualified staffing, household incidence, service delivery or superior
democratic control. No affected residents, fiscal authority or
independent reviewer participated.

Preserve the original Danish historical editions. Use the 2024
figures for the declared scope only. The Assembly decides tax,
appropriation and transition under applicable law. Treasury
reconciles owned cash and prior obligations. The independent fiscal
office and auditors keep original access and their own reports.
Service owners verify receiving. Opposition and affected people can
challenge the scope and the disposition. This changes no current
law, protected claim, role or source. Carry dates, obligations,
lawful custody and paid remedies into any actual budget decision.

## Scoped result and bounded next test

The published figures and exact extraction establish a matched
comparative starting point. Actual domestic finance, legal authority,
operation and better lives remain open.

For one actual prospective budget authority, reconcile the largest
protected service and benefit lines first to local law, measured
demand and the applicable cash dates. Then compare retain and repair
with one independently funded improvement under the same sources.
Complete when an independent reviewer can trace the exact voter
choice to qualified payers, costs and first receiving, with the
remaining national functions and residuals named. No jurisdiction,
data-access agreement or exercise budget has been established.
Reopen on a revised fiscal notification, resolved cross-table gap,
changed domestic scope or a stronger funded alternative.
