**QE02 — Count capacity and time separately.** At1,200normal deployable
hours, leave and ordinary non-task time are already excluded. A further10%
availability loss leaves1,080. Named contact, notes, travel, preparation and
HC practice are explicit; compatible task/shift demand uses their maximum,
while simultaneous locations, independent reviewers and specialties stay
separate. Round ordinary paid teams up; fractional retainer/elected roles and
temporary-role equivalents never establish actual people or qualification.
Participation time/pay and displaced public/private work are additional named
accounts. The adverse reference needs12,722.133direct paid
role-equivalents, plus2.429public-release
replacement and12.144private-work
displacement; compatible actual skill supply remains unknown. Commodity and
specialist-maintenance workers are inside the remaining market sector, not free.

**QE03 — Offer, attendance and receipt differ.** The full optional40h/48week
childcare offer, school wrap/holidays, assessed extra weeks and individual
supports remain costed even when uptake is uncertain. School meals include
those holidays:4.57million ingredients-priced meals,13.71million CU; only the
actual-receipt branch credits matching food once in I05. Declining a place
neither creates spare accepted capacity nor proves refusal of safe help.
Adult support includes varied intensity, continuous/awake nights, second workers,
travel and documentation; it is not an eight-hour mean or unpaid family supply.
