# C09. Keep essential electricity available when payment controls fail

*Design407 · EPC01–EPC08 · Proposed implementation of the household electricity guarantee.*

A household can lose essential electricity without a formal disconnection.
A meter can exhaust its credit, an intermediary can withhold access, or a
payment system can deduct old debt from money needed for tonight. Apply the
existing essential supply duty to these actual effects.

**EPC01 — Protect the occupied home and the useful period.** Apply EG06
when a payment, credit, account, tenancy or control arrangement could interrupt
the household's essential use. Include prepaid and shared meters, submetering,
supplier switching, automated limits and landlord instructions. Use the actual
occupants, equipment and useful times for safe temperature, medical, care,
communication and other protected uses under sector law. Give occupants a
direct route whether or not they hold the account. Determine need without a
universal registry or appliance-level monitoring. An accessible report or
other credible evidence can establish grounds for provisional protection while
a competent officer verifies the details.

**EPC02 — Make domestic prepayment voluntary and reversible.** Offer a usable
ordinary billing route without a deposit that defeats essential access. A new
prepaid choice requires informed, revocable agreement by the lawful customer
and protection for other occupants. Debt, tenancy, supplier replacement and
a tariff change do not supply agreement. A person may return to ordinary
billing without a continuity gap or penalty. Keep supported and offline
options where needed. An existing prepaid arrangement must pass the same
continuity test; if it cannot, fund a transition to a qualified ordinary route.
This does not assign the existing debt to another resident or extinguish it.

**EPC03 — Remove financial interruption from the protected service.** The
supplier must notify the responsible network or site operator in useful time
of an authorized protection; the operator must preserve or restore actual
supply before the relevant harm. Test exhaustion of credit, delayed records,
loss of a payment or communications channel and failed supplier transfer.
Protected service cannot depend on remote confirmation arriving on time.
Where a safe, qualified separation of protected and optional use is
unavailable, keep the ordinary connection energized insofar as technically
safe, carry ordinary lawful charges and review the actual exposure.
A payment hold does not energize a failed line. Physical outage or a necessary
safety isolation uses OC, TH and applicable emergency duties, with actual
independent receiving and lawful review.

**EPC04 — Separate today's supply from old claims.** Show current protected
use, other current charges, paid assistance, prior arrears and disputed amounts
separately. Allocate money authorized for current essential supply to that
obligation. Do not deduct another person's arrears, a disputed amount or an
old debt from the essential credit or the payment identified for that service.
Any lawful separate recovery preserves the protected floor and follows EG07,
HL and ordinary review. Give occupants safe receipts, a correction route and
usable notice of genuine liability. A landlord, intermediary or supplier
cannot condition this protection on accepting a new loan or waiving a claim.

**EPC05 — Keep nonshiftable essential use affordable and price the guarantee.**
Where tariffs vary by time or an occupant cannot defer essential use, assess
the lawful cost in the actual necessary interval. Do not count a cheaper
unusable time as an affordable offer or credit a vulnerable household as
voluntary flexibility without valid agreement and actual safe substitutes.
Use EG05 and applicable income and energy law for the tariff and timely,
dated support. The elected budget names the payer and source for authorized
support, interim service and standby. Verify what settled and what was
delivered. Report unpaid lawful claims or unreconciled provider exposure
separately. A funding gap does not authorize loss of protected supply or
an invented charge on the occupant.

**EPC06 — Intervene before the last safe time.** Provide safe accessible
contact and qualified assistance before an expected cutoff where feasible,
including an occupant-initiated urgent route when a balance or limit changes
without warning. Assign a human case owner, competent operator and independent
review path. Preserve actual supply during a material dispute under EG07.
If the issue is a real physical loss, arrange the qualified accepting
alternative, with care, travel and power matched through the next useful
period. Confirm service or state the actual gap and competent remedy.
Do not close a case from a sent credit or a meter status alone.

**EPC07 — Preserve privacy and inspect actual failures.** Share only the
necessary service scope, place, period, action and lawful receiver. Keep
medical, financial and safety details with the responsible office. Measure
protected use, interruptions, corrections, debt and costs using lawful
proportionate evidence and include people who never obtained an account.
Outside review can access protected originals and order timely correction
within existing powers. AI may identify unexplained losses, deadline conflicts
and duplicates. It cannot infer consent, diagnose an occupant, authorize an
interruption or certify actual receipt.

**EPC08 — Migrate with a funded next interval.** The adopting law names the
supplier, network, intermediary, default-service, payment and review roles.
Test non-account occupants, payment and supplier failure, physical outage,
stale limits and a second billing period. Qualify protected supply before
commencing an optional billing or control arrangement. Keep current rights,
debt defenses, complaints, credits and orders under their own law with paid
successors. Reopen on repeated loss, unpayable provider exposure, unsafe
disclosure, growing household debt or a simpler protective ordinary tariff.
