# Independent execution and funded transition

*Part of [PX open contests](../22-open-contests-and-neutral-enforcement.md).*

**PX08 — Make the ruling work outside the implicated chain.** Before
reliance, trace each relevant enforcement, review, payer and
execution role through appointment, conflict assignment, protected
removal, standing source, evidence and actual credentials. The
court names the lawful custodian, payer or service provider who
must carry out the order and the latest useful time. Apply existing
E15/B19 and criminal or sector execution rules to the exact duty,
with independently accessible records and a funded lawful substitute.
Verify actual release, record access, payment or restored opportunity.
A signed order, booked hearing or authorized transfer gives no
credit for an opportunity that has already passed. If a substitute
or record is unavailable, disclose the failure without granting a
new office or power to the incumbent.

**PX09 — Inspect cumulative use without building a political register.**
Each authority retains protected originals of the measures, reasons,
times, authorizations and effects it owns. Where a court finds a
concrete common incident or legal basis, review only the related
records needed to test cumulative interference. Protect secret
votes, lawful association, donors, witnesses and defence evidence.
Fund safe direct reports, a time-limited protected view where
necessary and public aggregate reporting of timeliness, unmet
orders, repeat interference and corrections. An audit or AI may
flag unexplained timing or shared control for human checking.
It cannot surveil political affiliation, establish fault, select a
target, order a coercive act or adjudicate electoral qualification.

**PX10 — Enact prospectively and preserve every continuing claim.**
Use the legitimate A10 and ordinary enactment route, including
any applicable A9/RR holds. Price real judges, counsel, substitutes,
secure records, execution and communication before reliance.
Reserve capacity for concurrent urgent liberty and non-electoral
protection rather than displacing it. Keep pending cases, evidence,
witness safeguards, independent mandates, accrued rights and all
existing shorter clocks under lawful succession. Review whether
opportunities were actually restored, credible cases continued,
and proceedings or reviewers were selectively obstructed. Reopen
on retaliation, routine late relief, tactical delay by officeholders,
common capture, unaffordable standby or a better same-rights route.
