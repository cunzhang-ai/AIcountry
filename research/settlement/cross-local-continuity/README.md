# Moving without dropping a public duty

*Design201 · Comparison for [XR01–XR10](../../country/C04/09-cross-local-service-continuity.md).*

A local boundary can separate the place of service, responsible public
body and paying account. The person can need help tonight while those
institutions disagree. Compare the same planned or forced move with
the same disability support, learning, medical and housing needs.

[Primary law and documented failure](01-care-and-school-comparators.md)
· [Worked move, payment dispute and capacity test](02-household-across-a-boundary.md)

| Arrangement | Strongest reason for it | Failure to test |
|---|---|---|
| Strong retain/repair of origin service and existing compacts | Existing workers and relationships can remain through a funded direct handover, often with little new administration | An origin team may be too far away or unable to enter safely. A different council's duty can remain disputed. A compact needs actual participation, money and review |
| Host interim action, continuing origin obligations and bounded national advance, selected | Places authority for immediate help near the person, with separate funding and final allocation. Can function for sudden moves and unknown residence | Host may face clustered demand, duplicate invoices or unfair burden. Ring-fenced money and common eligibility cannot create two workers or enforce consent |
| Immediate assignment and finance solely to the host | One accountable council, simple legal trigger and potentially faster local response | Can shift unfunded cost to places that receive displaced people and incentivize refusing contact, travel or documentation |
| National direct administration of portable services | A single payer and nationally usable staff pool can reduce local allocation disputes | Central capacity and records can fail across areas. Transfers of sites, employment and voice need lawful paid change. Actual local receiving remains necessary |
| Portable cash or direct purchase | A person may keep a chosen worker or arrange a safe alternative when providers can accept | Money alone leaves a person negotiating supply and may not protect timely school admission, safe housing or clinical care |

Select the second path where existing arrangements do not already
deliver it. The source law determines permanent responsibility.
Keep the old rights and the first date, and put a finite national
advance behind immediate eligible receiving while a payer is
unreachable or disputed. Require legally bounded appropriations and
funded local choice. Preserve a simpler voluntary arrangement if it
works for both receiving and supplying communities under fair law.

A central payer can generate delay or demand excessive proof. Give
people a direct remedy, paid alternate and protected record route.
A provisional payment can lead to overuse or shifting costs to other
places. Apply existing standard-cost rules, episode reconciliation
and independent recovery after decision. Keep the new advance within
its law, without treating a funding ceiling as authority to cancel a
service. The hardest case is a move where all alternatives share the
same unavailable people, payment system or credential. A lawful
assignment then remains distinguishable from delivery.

Actual jurisdictions, safe routes, qualified shifts, lawful
authority, review and outcomes are open. The cases below qualify
specific failure distinctions, not all-country readiness.

The [recurring places comparison](03-recurring-places-and-portable-support.md)
adds repeat service across areas and a late location change. Compare a
standing plan with ad hoc booking and a single national provider.
