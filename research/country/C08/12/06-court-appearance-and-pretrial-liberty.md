# CA01–CA07. Make court appearance possible and protect pretrial liberty

*Design378 · Part of the [criminal core](../12-criminal-core-and-procedure.md) · Proposed ordinary law.*

**CA01 — Require only the appearance the court needs.** The court
identifies which parts of a criminal proceeding require personal
appearance, for what reason and with what means of participation.
Use representation by counsel for suitable procedural matters if
lawfully permitted and consistent with the person's rights. Offer
an accessible physical route and, where lawful and fair, an agreed
alternative that preserves confidential counsel, interpretation,
evidence and the right to be heard. Preserve in-person safeguards for
liberty hearings under P09. A scheduling preference, tool or provider
cannot create a condition of freedom.

**CA02 — Give usable notice and an early way to fix barriers.** Give
safe, accessible notice of the case, obligation, date, location,
changes, available support, challenge and possible consequences.
Record lawful service and evidence of practical access separately.
Keep protected contact under SC, with an offline human route and
counsel access. Optional reminders may reinforce notice. Delivery
logs, a portal entry, silence or refusal of a reminder cannot alone
show knowing evasion. The court may find deliberate avoidance of
service from specific independent evidence of actual knowledge and
a feasible opportunity to respond. Protect witness and survivor
locations and keep unrelated personal records out of the inquiry.

**CA03 — Fund the least restrictive way to reach court.** At first
contact and after a material change, the registry and defense identify
the next feasible arrangement. Through an elected OJ and DB
appropriation, provide timely interpretation, disability access and,
where needed, directly paid travel or dependent-care support.
Preserve liberty deadlines and the opposing party's fair opportunity.
Where the arrangement fails, the judge considers an available
substitute, representation, or an earliest fair reset under P.
No debt, app use, monitoring, unavailable accommodation or unpaid
helper may be the price of attendance support or ordinary release.
Verify actual availability and acceptance in time for the hearing.

**CA04 — Review a missed appearance before coercion.** The registry
checks for service error, access barriers, lawful excuse and current
case status, through safe contact and defense. The court normally
uses prompt corrected notice and a usable return date or another
lawful alternative. A warrant based on absence requires independent,
recorded findings of a material required appearance, timely usable
notice or proved deliberate evasion of service, concrete evidence
of knowing avoidance despite a feasible opportunity and necessity
after considering less restrictive means. Repeated absences alone
cannot prove any of these elements. Hear defense before issuance
where feasible. If prior hearing would frustrate necessary action,
give reasons and immediate access to counsel and challenge. A
warrant brings the person to a judge under P09. Any remand needs a
fresh P10 decision on its own grounds and original clocks.

**CA05 — Preserve protection on separate grounds.** A missed court
date, delay in support, or expiry of custody does not remove a
separately valid witness or survivor protection order. Use the
existing timely protective route for concrete threats under its own
authority and review. Do not make a survivor contact, transport,
supervise or house the accused. A documented threat or imminent
interference may justify a separate P or other lawful intervention
even while a service error is corrected. Neither a service shortfall
nor the gravity label of the charge supplies those grounds.

**CA06 — Withdraw a bad warrant where it can still be executed.**
Provide direct urgent review through counsel, court or accessible
outside intake. A judge can stay or withdraw the challenged warrant,
correct the case and give the next lawful direction. Registry and
each authorized executor confirm propagation of the operative order
to the queues and copies they use. Treat an unconfirmed copy as an
open execution incident. Use the verified authoritative status or
contact the court before acting on that copy. On correction, stop
execution, end confinement under the invalid order immediately
unless a separate lawful basis exists, and preserve review and
remedies for any loss. Prevent a corrected missed hearing from
returning as a false adverse entry. Keep protected audit evidence
and access for challenge under existing authority.

**CA07 — Keep decisions independent and measure all burdens.** Court
funding, scheduling and execution need paid capacity and independent
review under OJ, PO and DB. Compare a plain notice improvement, the
funded least restrictive route and a supervision-based rival against
unconditional release wherever lawful. Report actual appearance,
erroneous or stale warrants, detention time, safety, elapsed case
time, user burdens and excluded populations separately. AI can
reconcile public schedules and aid staff with accessible reminders.
It may not decide evasion, set release conditions, control warrants
or infer risk from nonresponse. Review active absence warrants under
lawful court authority on migration. Preserve original dates, orders
on separate grounds, protections and accrued remedies.
