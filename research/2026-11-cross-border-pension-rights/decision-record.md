# Design345 decision on cross-border earned pension rights

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design344, Y01–Y29, the first-period earned formula, RF funding, TR
treaty authority and the working framework. The baseline preserves
earned rights on a move and leaves foreign coordination to applicable
law. Specify domestic earned payment abroad, funded claim assistance,
separate partner liability and a real receipt route.

Select independent domestic payment and negotiated coordination for
qualification, with scoped transfer of evidence. Preserve the existing
residence-based basic layer. Elected bodies enact the domestic scope,
budget and any treaty. A qualified officer decides each domestic claim.
Foreign authorities retain their own legal competence.

## Alternatives and objections

Separate unassisted claims are simple for administrations but can strand
short careers and overload people with different proof rules. Exporting
the basic pension as well would extend security to some former residents,
with a different public-finance and residence bargain. A unified
international pension would require agreed finance and jurisdiction
well beyond the proposed country. Select domestic portability and
targeted reciprocity. Keep broader coordination and an exported basic
layer as distinct alternatives subject to a full democratic decision.

The [primary comparators](sources.md) support aggregation, proportional
responsibility and assisted claims in specified legal settings. They do
not establish this country's eligibility, sources or foreign acceptance.
The five- and 30-working-day milestones are proposed. Multiple currency
routes, evidence requests and checks against duplication add costs.
Require safe proof and test the risks of repeated withholding.

## Transition and reopening

Preserve stronger accrued, legacy and survivor claims, lawful treaty
terms and valid pending dates. Fund domestic liaison, ordinary payment
and human review through moves and treaty change. This is an
assistant-authored selection. No qualified independent or affected review,
foreign consent, enactment or actual delivery has occurred.

Exercise one delayed partner case and one failed payment route with
consenting residents abroad and at home, a lawful liaison and independent
reviewers. Compare time to usable receipt, duplicate periods, FX costs,
administrative work, foreign source gaps and actual needs protection.
Reopen on excluded people, unsafe foreign contacts, persistent payment
failures, unpriced obligations or a stronger viable arrangement.
