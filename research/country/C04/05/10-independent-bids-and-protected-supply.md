# SC38–SC43. Verify independent competition and independent delivery

*Design366 · Part of [public buying](../05-public-buying-and-continuous-supply.md).*

**SC38 — State the independence needed before bidding.** For a material
procurement where independent rivalry or fallback matters, the buyer
publishes how it will assess effective control, independent preparation
and actually separate delivery capacity. Require proportionate
declarations of decisive controllers, relationships material to the
tender and named dependencies needed for execution. Provide a route
for small bidders, lawful joint offers and equivalent proof.
Keep bid-sensitive and private information in protected custody.
Common administrative services may be legitimate. Compare lots and
terms for access without fragmenting the actual commitment.

**SC39 — Separate the questions before reaching a conclusion.** Check
three findings independently: common economic control under MP02,
whether offers were prepared or coordinated in a way material to
this tender, and whether delivery and fallback depend on the same
people, assets or decisions. Use original evidence and fair reply.
Count one economic unit once when assessing independent market rivalry.
A relationship does not by itself exclude a tender. Two distinct
economic units may still offer the same unavailable subcontractor.
Count that capacity only at its actually accepted scope and time.
Flagged similarities, automated scores or common service providers
give leads, without establishing misconduct.

**SC40 — Keep award correction and enforcement separate.** The buyer
requests targeted explanations, preserves dated evidence and applies
the published criteria. A material concern gets a reasoned disposition
before award or an express lawful recompetition, with corrected notice
and the applicable SC09 standstill. Do not invent new criteria after
opening bids. Suspected unlawful coordination goes to the competent
competition authority under MP. Only the authorized process can find
liability or order a sanction. If independent rivalry is weak but
an otherwise qualified offer may lawfully proceed, disclose market
scope, price comparison, real alternatives and any unqualified
reserve. Publish safe reasons and give affected bidders and eligible
entrants a timely court challenge. Protect response rights and
investigative evidence without concealing material grounds.

**SC41 — Buy a reserve for an actual failure, on its own source.** A
material continuity requirement names the protected tasks, useful
dates and common loss to withstand. Validate the accepting staff,
assets, lawful access, mobilization and next call. A fallback shares
its main provider's failure unless evidence qualifies it for that
specific loss. Compare ordinary repair, the proposed main award and
independent reserve, and an independent public or cooperative
alternative where feasible. Show gross cost, lawful sources,
avoidable concentration and the consequences of no award. Do not
credit a cheaper dependent offer as satisfying a published independent
requirement. A budget limit does not supply authority to omit it.

**SC42 — Preserve service and the original award challenge.** When a
disputed award or failed tender threatens an essential duty, use
existing SC12 and any more specific continuity route only within
their original authority, funding, court order and expiry. Show the
residual service, dated money and acceptance. A temporary purchase
cannot validate a disputed award or become its covert extension.
Publish unresolved shortages, retain earned pay and valid claims,
and return new authority or changed scope to the competent elected
body. An alternative that uses the same failed controller or scarce
team remains exposed. Residents and workers retain their own remedies.

**SC43 — Check changes after award and carry the record.** Require
disclosure and fresh scoped qualification for a material change in
control, subcontracting, staffing or reserve. Apply the actual
variation, competition and lawful exit rules. Verify the first
receipt and the next protected call. Publish safe oversight data
with due evidence access for audit, competition review and courts.
AI may find possible connections or duplicate capacity and compare
options. It cannot infer control, exclude bidders, determine
infringement or certify an independent route. Preserve current
contracts, proof, dates, payments and remedies on succession.
Reopen on concealed control, weak participation, a failed reserve
or a simpler qualified route.
