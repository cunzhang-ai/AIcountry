*Support for [RP](README.md); no adopted law or observed superiority.*

| Whole option | Mechanism, value conflict and resource consequences | Working disposition |
|---|---|---|

Child/public protection cannot be removed to make it simple |

Extra process can burden access; paid independence, participation and resources remain unproved |

The one-I02-basket/30-day gestation allowance and median-hourly donor-time
formula recognize contribution without pricing a child's transfer. Recovery
allowance ends at 90 days; necessary health, proved injury/lost earnings and
ordinary support retain their distinct claims. Reconcile overlapping items
once. No government promise makes another person's consent purchasable.
RP06 applies I05's E retention to actually received after-tax contribution/
time and lost-earnings compensation; exact cost refunds match actual costs,
not extra resources. This prevents an unannounced full withdrawal while
leaving public cost and tax incidence explicit and still uncalibrated.
Essential care differs from coverage of every fertility cycle, elective
storage, private allowance or unproven method.

Material opposition remains: fetal interests and autonomy; origin access
and donor privacy; compensation and exploitation; intentional support and
adult exit; plural status and continuity; future research and developmental
protection; estate closure and later-born descendants. A source, court label,
majority survey or score cannot average these into consensus. A future public
may reject the candidate within lawful rights/procedures.

## Actual delivery chain and missing quantities

Clinical services need qualified consent assessment, safe treatment, urgent
transport, newborn/postpartum care and an accepting alternative. Origin/storage
needs actual material records, protected specimens, backup power, custody,
access/correction and a funded closure receiver. Courts need independent
child representation, separately advised adults, urgent hearings, disclosure
and actual executable orders. C06 counts existing E/I/S/B duties once, with
private payments/escrow, coverage, missing obligors and actual losses distinct.
M05 custody does not insure a pregnancy or guarantee a child.

No actual donor inventory, pregnancy demand, clinic/transport roster, escrow,
allowance cost, storage turnover, court load, independent custodian or qualified
budget is known. Old 37-function bids and SF cash stock do not establish the
increment. A birth count is neither an automatic pension solution nor evidence
of better lives. Fines, demographic targets, unpaid relatives and predicted
AI savings do not finance missing people or accepted remedies.
