# Applicable law: autonomy with actual protections and external duties

*B43–B44; part of [C08/17](../17-jurisdiction-and-legal-cooperation.md).*

**B43 — Contract choice is scoped.** Parties making a genuinely voluntary
professional commercial contract may choose an identifiable body of national
law, with accessible operative terms and B02/B03 assent/authority. Nonstate
trade/model rules may supply contract terms, not sovereign powers or a way
to displace mandatory law. A choice of law is not a choice of court, consent
to enforcement or acceptance of a future private model update.

Absent valid choice, use the habitual business law of the characteristic
performer (seller/service provider), subject to a demonstrably closer material
connection and applicable mandatory rules. For employment use the ordinary
place of work, including genuinely mobile/remote work's actual center rather
than a software address. For a consumer transaction directed to the person's
habitual residence, retain that residence's mandatory consumer protections.
General web availability alone does not show targeted business; actual
solicitation, delivery and transaction context do. Property/tenancy follows
B46. Publish reasons for a closer-connection departure, not a hidden ranking.

Dependent workers/consumers and protected child/personal acts cannot lose
the otherwise applicable mandatory floor through advance foreign-law/forum
terms or compelled private arbitration. A meaningful later advised settlement
has B04's route. Which mandatory laws actually apply needs a real connection
and reasoned conflict analysis; do not select every favorable rule in the
world or invent a uniform outcome. Public taxes/eligibility/coercion and
nonwaivable rights cannot be reassigned by an agent's contract.

**B44 — Wrongful harm and controlled activity abroad.** Ordinarily use the
law of the place of direct injury that was reasonably foreseeable from the
activity, not an unrelated downstream economic effect. Where all materially
affected parties share an actual habitual residence, its law may govern
their private relationship unless another mandatory/public/third-party
interest prevents that displacement. A substantially closer connection needs
stated evidence and reasons; nationality or registry location alone is not it.

For activity materially directed, controlled or publicly authorized here,
apply this country's mandatory duties to those domestic decisions within
their enacted scope even when injury is abroad. A foreign permit does not
erase an applicable home duty; a home judgment does not command a foreign
regulator. Hear original external worker/community evidence safely, actual
decision structure, contrary sources and B09–B12/P causal defenses.
An adverse outcome or a common investor/AI vendor alone is not liability.

Use a narrowly reasoned fundamental-rights exception for an otherwise
applicable foreign rule requiring slavery, coercive status loss or comparable
incompatible deprivation. Preserve severable legitimate rights and a usable
substitute applicable rule; do not simply dismiss the injured person's case.
Different lawful foreign policy or an unfavorable outcome is insufficient.
Recognition, remedies, actual foreign authority and treaty duties remain
separate. No political official chooses a named case's convenient law.
