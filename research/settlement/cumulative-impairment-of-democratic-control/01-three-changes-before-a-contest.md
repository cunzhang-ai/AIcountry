# Three ordinary measures and one expiring opportunity

*Design379 · Constructed mechanism test, separate from the [observed institutional record](../../2026-11-cumulative-impairment-of-democratic-control/sources.md).*

An eligible contestant contests an exclusion on day 0. Day 8 is the
last lawful day when a correction can reach ballot production.
Existing qualified work takes one day each for protected original
record access, independent hearing and decision, and service and
correction. Work is sequential for this particular dispute. Three
measures add two days each. A record custody transfer adds retrieval
time, an appropriations schedule delays a paid independent sitting,
and a new execution schedule delays implementation. No substantive
election deadline or officeholder's tenure is amended.

| Arrangement | Received result under the declared assumptions |
|---|---|
| Baseline | Day 3. |
| Any single measure or pair | Day 5 or day 7. Assessing isolated effects or even pairs misses the loss. |
| All three | Day 9. A ruling on day 6 reaches production too late. |
| Verified independent alternative for one link | Day 7 if the corresponding two-day delay is avoided without shifting another obligation. The authority and funds have to exist before reliance. |
| Proposed alternate shares the blocked source or payer | Day 9. A distinct name does not change access. |
| Narrow protection under existing law | Keep the original execution route for this affected class if lawful, funded and received. An unrelated budget change and the other two measures can remain if separable and otherwise lawful. |
| Reviewer is unavailable or correction cannot be executed | Report the exact failure and preserve evidence, rights and original dates. No automatic approval, substitute judge, term extension or deemed correction. |

Compare a mandatory preclearance veto and isolated legality review
against the selected joined disclosure and challenge. The former
might catch more interacting measures but lets a new gatekeeper
delay ordinary spending and lawful reform. The latter avoids that
gatekeeper but has no required way to detect this shared loss.
The selected path uses the competent court's existing assignment
and voting rules. Separately model a two-day execution delay issued
by the reviewing institution itself. Its effect on the deadline is
identical and its rules need a lawful unconflicted review path.

The case checks one eligible claim and original clock. It makes no
claim about intent, actual national funding or real case outcomes.
