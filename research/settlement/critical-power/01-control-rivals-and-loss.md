# Governing a physical impossibility without inventing a successful country

Design166 · [Canonical source choice](../../country/C06/37/06-critical-public-power-and-actual-priority.md)

The former exclusion was not neutral: the resource's existing location could
determine which person's urgent duty failed. The new priority changes that
choice for an exact scheduled resource. It does not create the second source
or eliminate competing political values. Duty, operational allocation, actual
rights interference, fault, result validity and delivered recovery stay distinct.

Compatible sequence must be tried where actually feasible: authored5-minute
ballot preservation followed by care within its actual10-minute safe tolerance
can preserve both. Where both have an immediate indivisible need and no compatible
source, bodily protection is chosen and the political loss remains. Actual
clinical facts, startup/travel and unsafe switching can invalidate that authored
sequence; it is not medical advice or a field-tested engineering schedule.

A knowingly unavoidable incidental record loss is not automatically the purpose
of suppressing evidence. Neither distinction proves innocence or excuses actual
K12/K23 conduct/prior sabotage. Likewise, free casting followed by loss of the
only originals is not falsely called an unfree casting: ER16 explicitly tests
reliable lawful ascertainment too. Missing certification cannot make that new
court path unreachable, but it supplies no automatic failure judgment or judge.

| Real rival | Tradeoff retained |
|---|---|
| Protected exclusion + paid redundancy | Avoids this discretionary political-access loss but may preserve first possession and preventable bodily harm; actual spare source/maintenance/source still costs |
| Broader critical allocation | Can reach more bottlenecks, but adds concentrated authority/rights/property/clinical and transition burdens; not adopted by a source schedule |
| Distributed common/local reserves | May remove the bottleneck, with actual title/skills/charge/acceptance, local inequality and solidarity costs; no free community readiness |
| Absolute exceptional cap | Limits prolonged captured allocation, but can end authority while the same actual bodily threat and failed legislature remain; selected standing-law review is not proof this risk is solved |

An official can still gain political advantage from an induced outage. Rules
forbid new authority/immunity and require real evidence/independent inquiry;
they cannot certify that sabotage/capture is prevented. Repeated collisions
defeat a readiness/superiority claim and need paid ordinary elected repair.
