# TW21–TW24. Make a shorter week real in the roster and paycheck

*Design222 · Part of [work, rest and paid capability](../10-work-rest-and-paid-capability.md).*

**TW21 — Give affected workers an actual choice of patterns.** Before each
enacted reduction, the employer makes the old and proposed paid schedules,
regular pay and availability terms usable for workers and their chosen
representatives. Propose qualified shorter patterns and hear reasons,
including predictable care, access, travel, rest and ordinary private
time. Use lawful individual agreement or representative collective terms
within WL. A shorter day, rotating whole release and a compressed week
can have different consequences. Protect better individual terms,
reasonable accommodation and genuinely chosen existing short schedules.
A worker may decline to disclose the private purpose of time away.
Grant a requested lawful pattern unless actual service, safety,
coworkers' rights or a proved disproportionate operational burden
prevents it. Apply fair published criteria to conflicting requests.
Give an accessible reason and independent challenge. Representation by a person or agent
requires an actual mandate. Silence and a posted roster supply no consent.

**TW22 — Give the reduction an enforceable default when agreement fails.**
In a regular roster, keep existing ordinary workdays and start times
and remove the required ordinary hours from the end of each duty day,
in proportion to its prior paid ordinary hours. Publish actual release
times and reconcile rounding within the weekly maximum. Preserve paid
breaks, stronger rights and safe maximums. For a variable roster use its
representative lawful pattern under WL06, with reviewable reasons. Leave
a genuinely lower agreed schedule intact. Where default placement would
cause a specific unsafe or legally incompatible service gap, use a
funded lawful alternative with genuine agreement where possible. An
unagreed temporary placement is an express statutory exception to
WL07 for the position of reduced ordinary hours only. Use the minimum
necessary change with written grounds, existing notice and rest, an
accepted service path and direct review. It lasts at most one published
roster cycle or four weeks, whichever is shorter. A materially continuous departure needs independent lawful review
under existing labour adjudication before any continuation. A new
roster label or office does not restart the period. Neither a departure
nor missing review can
raise the normal limit or create standing overtime or a waiver. If
the proposed pattern cannot safely operate, use existing continuity
and elected funding routes and disclose unmet provision.

**TW23 — Protect ordinary weekly cash pay across pay methods.** Apply
TW08 to existing regular ordinary cash earnings whether paid hourly,
by salary or by another regular method. For the same existing job
and participation, the shorter statutory limit alone cannot reduce
guaranteed ordinary weekly pay. Use valid agreed ordinary earnings
and the representative WL06 pattern where a guarantee arises there.
Show hours, preserved cash, base rate, lawful premiums and deductions
separately. For genuinely irregular voluntary work preserve actual
agreed protections, with no invented full-time guarantee. Distinguish
lost optional overtime from protected ordinary pay and show its income
effect. No sham change of contract, title, schedule guarantee or pay
component can evade the rule. Existing stronger terms, wage floors,
actual voluntary contract changes and independent pay remedies remain.

**TW24 — Measure released time as well as ordinary hours.** Compare
old and new paid duty, uncompensated work, constrained availability,
schedule changes, working intensity and workers' usable time with
actual output and service. A required task still counts as work if
moved to a device, home, training label or standby message. A deleted
shift with the same quota and unpaid spillover is a breach to correct.
Use safe worker reports, bounded records and actual evidence. No
required home activity tracker or disclosure of care or civic life.
AI can compare candidate schedules and identify incompatible assignments.
It cannot consent for the worker, reject a claim or validate its own
estimate of released time.
