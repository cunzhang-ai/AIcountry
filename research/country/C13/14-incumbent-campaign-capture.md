# C13. A challenger against the state campaign machine

*Part of [C13 lived experience](../C13-lived-experience.md); integrated design 129 authored stress, not an observed election.*

A governing coalition faces a credible new civic list. The list qualifies
under published rules, but Treasury delays its public campaign payment.
A major contractor buys favorable ads through several controlled firms;
a public broadcaster favors ministers, while an official benefit-message
campaign uses resident data in the weeks before polling. A dominant
platform silently narrows the challenger's paid reach. Some small donors
fear retaliation if their names become public. An incumbent tries to influence the mixed-source EA leadership, protected
career finance investigator, Treasury and providers together, and demands
suspension for an incomplete report. Cabinet appointment of the election
regulator is not the current lawful candidate arrangement; capture or unlawful
pressure must not be disguised as its normal appointment rule.

The assigned ER career officer verifies ballot status under prior rules;
Treasury owes the published support on time. The EA protected career finance function, separate from ballot decisions,
traces controllers and in-kind value using lawful records, protects small
supporters and states a reasoned allegation. C11's public-media and source
custodians preserve schedules, ad versions, targeting and official
benefit-message evidence; C04 staff can report misuse safely. The investigator cannot by itself disqualify a candidate, hold a due baseline
or certify an election result. EA mixed appointments, career protection and
OJ ordinary review remain the current proposed chain; their labels do not prove independence.

Before voters decide, an independent court can restore due payment and
access, preserve evidence and stop proven misuse on a narrow ground while
allowing essential service notices and lawful political criticism.
Opposition and affected residents can inspect the material public record
without receiving private benefit or donor data. The court's order needs
Treasury, venue, broadcaster or platform compliance through named lawful
executors and an alternate channel if one provider refuses. After polling,
any penalty or contest remedy requires proof, proportionality and respect
for valid votes; a mere filing error cannot erase the challenger.

The route fails if a late payment cannot restore lost opportunity, one
cabinet controls regulator, broadcaster, data and court execution, or
disclosure exposes vulnerable supporters. It also fails if an overbroad
anti-disinformation rule lets the incumbent silence the opposition.
No real campaign, platform, electorate, controller map, law, staff or
outcome is represented by this authored exercise.
