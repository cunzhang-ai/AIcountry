The CJ06 core nine keep their exact constitutional powers. A separate
twelve-seat ordinary formation has six judges, three practitioners and
three lay electors. Eight affirmative votes and at least one from each
category are required. No lower threshold from vacancy, no Cabinet confirmation veto, and
no automatic promotion from one court/role to another. Selection is not control
of judgments, discipline or an unlimited budget. A joint inaugural exception
needs explicit legitimate constitutional adoption; no source pool is invented.

DPP's open Assembly appointment uses3/5 full N plus1/3 frozen non-government O;
no O means no new appointment. Ordinary prosecutors retain own lawful case
powers during bounded head vacancy. Three separately preselected external case
prosecutors have actual independent terms/source for implicated-command cases,
with their own judicial accountability. Names/source quotas do not prove freedom
from shared patrons. Existing CJ12 seven-person constitutional-own-court route,
P/K/V proof/sanctions, B execution and stronger source rights/clocks stay exact.
