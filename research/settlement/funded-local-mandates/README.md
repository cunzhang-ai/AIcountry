# National guarantees, local discretion and money that actually arrives

*Design200 · Decision comparison for [MF01–MF08](../../country/C04/08-funded-local-mandates-and-equalization.md).*

One locality cannot turn a common entitlement into a discretionary
project. National finance cannot treat every place as if the same service
costs the same or as if an announced grant arrived before payroll.
Choose the accountable owner, lawful resources and receiving path for
the same duties. A funded local minimum need not centralize service
methods or reimburse every local spending choice.

| Arrangement | Reason to retain it as a real option | Hard case |
|---|---|---|
| Strong retain/repair with existing general grants and local discretion | Lower transaction cost, known administration and locally legitimate priorities. Can work where the law, settlement and actual receipt already align | Aggregate compensation or voluntary agreement can leave a place with a disproportionate new duty. A coalition of councils cannot decide away an excluded resident's rights |
| Formula-based common support, with a scoped sector correction and local delivery, selected | Recognizes geographically different needs and protects local methods while making the national payer accountable | Benchmarks can miss uncommon needs, reward gaming or impose a hidden national model. Protected method review and actual open gaps remain necessary |
| Reimburse actual expenditure per sector | Directly identifies a particular eligible cost, useful for defined transition or specialized extra need | Fragmented budgets, bureaucratic matching and risk of rewarding high spending. A reimbursement after invoice can leave the provider unpaid when needed |
| Direct national delivery of the common core | Can unify responsibility and reduce counterparty disputes when direct scale or worker pools matter | Must receive existing staff, assets, data and local duties lawfully and pay for transition. Central power can exclude local voice or create one fragile payment path |
| Shared local pool, with national residual | May match the actual service geography, keep workable local relationships and share a rare specialist | A pool cannot tax without authority or make one backup available to two people at once. Contribution disputes and supplying-area losses remain |

Choose the common support and bounded sector correction in the proposed
country. Let a simpler retained arrangement win if it provides the same
timely rights, fair distribution and contestable local power at lower
burden. Allow a sector correction for actual source-law scope and a dated
gap, with an express funding decision. Preserve locally financed choices
that comply with common rights and their own lawful resource constraints.

Reject deduction from a protected common service source for another
locality's fiscal overrun. A jurisdiction can still enforce proper
spending authority and correct misuse. Separate ordinary finance,
discipline, and immediate harm rather than making the resident bear
the repayment risk. This choice entails possible national interim cost
and moral hazard. Keep a scoped independent review and recovery route
instead of open-ended automatic finance.

A verified calculation establishes allocation arithmetic, not an adopted
programme or actual receipt. This comparison identifies a structural
source conflict and a test for fixing it. It supplies no funded national
settlement, price schedule or qualified cost estimate for all services.
