# National package: claims, payment priorities and physical shortage

*Canonical NP10–NP13; part of [C06/33](../33-protection-production-and-national-expansion.md).*

**NP10 — A surviving debt is not an unlimited first call on resources.** Preserve
valid wages, pensions, contractual debt, compensation, judgments and their source
rights. Their existence alone does not set an immediate national cash priority
above every present necessity or democratic function. Neither a judgment amount
nor a ministry's appropriation can manufacture absent cash/qualified time.
Conversely, a new budget ceiling cannot erase an accrued obligation or declare
an unpaid service delivered. Existing creditor priorities, binding orders and
restricted property remain effective until their own competent lawful change;
NP is no unilateral Treasury default/restructuring power.

**NP11 — Enact a bounded public-payment schedule before shortage.** The proposed
national enabling law establishes its standing priority prospectively, with
explicit source-law compatibility, funds, claims/classes, review, duration and
arrears treatment. Only actually available legally fungible unrestricted public
cash enters; separately owned insurance/client assets, purpose-bound grants and
SF standby retain their own lawful scope. A transfer needs its proper authority.
Public insolvency/creditor restructuring still uses C06/06/M and actual contracts.

Reserve the adopted basic-service and democratic-continuity commitments needed
in useful time, bounded by real tasks and enacted amounts; suspend/cancel only
legally deferrable optional new commitments before drawing on them. After that
core, pay comparable remaining ordinary unsecured due public cash claims in the
same enacted class pro rata on the due amount. No first-come invoice, litigant's
wealth, party relationship or Treasury preference changes that share. Different
lawful secured/priority/source classes are handled separately, with accessible
review of classification and actual hardship under I/E. Publish partial payment,
unpaid principal/indexation/interest as actually owed, displaced duties and source
receipt. No sum becomes paid twice because a court or alternate also executes it.

This reference intentionally prefers current basic capabilities to full immediate
payment of some other public arrears where legally authorized. It imposes real
creditor/worker harm and possible financing costs. Compensation or pay owed to a
person in present need may enter the core only through the neutral adopted need/
useful-time rule, not because a powerful creditor renames its entire debt essential.
Class totals include hidden/late claims and corrections; estimates are not a
priority title. Material error needs corrected lawful allocation and remedy,
not automatic clawback of a vulnerable recipient's consumed necessities.

**NP12 — Core insufficiency is a failure, not payer discretion.** Within the
new lawful fungible class, no residual-class payment occurs while a legally
payable useful-time core requirement remains unfunded. If core cash itself is
insufficient, apply only pre-enacted source/need/continuity rules, record the
unpaid duties and immediately trigger useful-time relief and E19 correction.
Treasury may not invent a new ranking or blanket pro rata of unlike capabilities.
Existing separate lawful priorities/orders retain NP10–NP11 treatment.

Cash priority is not clinical or physical triage. Clinical care,
liberty review, essential networks and actual force keep their distinct lawful
criteria. Do not distribute unlike treatments or judge shifts pro rata by price.
The schedule exposes competing time/skills/material constraints and safe partial
or alternative delivery. If even the indispensable functions cannot coexist,
there is no claim of feasible operation: use actual prior-law relief/shortage
powers and seek urgent elected correction while recording unmet rights and harm.
No hidden queue, coerced family work, unsafe discharge, postponed election or
extra detention is authorized to make a model pass. NP does not enlarge CP's
24/72-hour residual pool or give an emergency tax/confiscation power.

**NP13 — Keep power replaceable during failure.** C04 operators own delivery;
Treasury compiles/performs exact lawful payments, not eligibility or new policy.
Cabinet presents E19's response within seven calendar days of material shortfall;
the Assembly records its actual decision, refusal or unresolved disagreement.
Fund opposition/source scrutiny, ordinary courts and exact substitute execution
within their authorized scope; E15 selects no national priority or new judge.
Courts can correct illegal exclusion, retaliation, classification and withheld
authorized duties, not legislate a preferred tax or national allocation. Follow
appointment/removal, pay, original information, independent review and actual
receipt together. If every alternate fails, report constitutional/service failure;
a deadline never creates approval or prolongs the cabinet until its project works.

[PS21–PS32](../37/06-critical-public-power-and-actual-priority.md) now makes one
resource-specific prior choice for scheduled public backup power, with explicit
possible loss of political access. This is not NP cash ranking, a CP expansion,
new bodily authority or proof indispensable functions can jointly operate.

[NR14 finite-owner receipts](../../C09/15/04-public-receipts-finite-wealth-and-transition.md)
expressly preserves actually adopted source-compatible NP11–NP12 payable core/
prior source claims before discretionary capital commitment. Resource-attributable
costs are not every ministry invoice; unavailable/bond/client/restricted assets
are not new fungible money. Any remaining default capital account may be zero.

[EB13](../../C05/16/04-source-control-rivals-and-succession.md) does not label
all historic-earnings replacement useful-time basic priority. Existing actually
adopted NP/source core/custody/creditor law controls; added general-revenue bridge
risk, recession claims and due cash cannot take medicine/care/opposition funds
by name or cancel accrued rights through a secret first-come cap.
