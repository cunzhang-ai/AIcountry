# When a public body loses the evidence for a person's rights

*Design322 · RLW01–RLW05 · Proposed ordinary civil administrative process.*

**RLW01 — The public duty and the date survive record loss.** When a material
record is inaccessible, corrupted or lost during ordinary operation or a
handover, the responsible duty owner identifies the decision, date, missing
fact, custody obligation and feasible sources. Preserve originals and contrary
evidence, record the first affected cases and appoint a paid case receiver.
Keep valid awards, judgments, existing payment duties and pending requests
under their original law. A verified personal copy or other lawful evidence
may establish an operative award. Missing status alone supplies no ground to
cancel it or repeat a possibly settled payment. Use LRT and TU to resolve
the actual payer and settlement status separately.

**RLW02 — Apply a narrow, rebuttable finding about the lost procedure.** In
covered administrative cases, where the public body or its contractor was
required to make or retain the material proof and the person offers a specific,
credible account of a filing, response or other procedural act, accept that
act and its adequately supported date unless the remaining reliable evidence
makes it more likely than not that the act or date is materially different.
Assess the account in context with available lawful originals, including
assisted testimony. A public receipt token or independent document is not
mandatory. No missing public record, unexplained AI label or derivative copy
of the failed source rebuts the account by itself. Give both sides an
opportunity to answer material contrary evidence. The competent officer
finds the fact with reasons and direct independent review. The rule protects
access and dates. It confers no substantive eligibility, amount, consent,
property title, conviction, election result or permit. Stronger protections
and legally separate evidentiary standards govern their own matters.

**RLW03 — Pay urgent protection before completing the case when necessary.**
For scheduled essential-support programmes, a separate statutory interim
payment is due when the loss blocks a plausible claim, the person has a
verified imminent essential shortfall, and timely ordinary payment or
another accepted route will not cover it. An assigned career officer decides
before the last useful time, with direct independent review and an authorized
substitute. Pay the verified uncovered need within the published cap and
period, selected here as at most two scheduled instalment periods and never
more than 30 calendar days for the same incident and case. The programme
schedule specifies the amount cap and funded coverage before commencement.
Notify and reassess before expiry and arrange any continuing emergency or
ordinary support under its own law. The interim period does not change the
original case clock or cancel an outstanding duty. Established awards use
their normal source and do not consume this cap. Preserve any earlier or
stronger urgent service obligation.

**RLW04 — Carry the failure cost and settle by person and period.** The public
programme appropriates interim aid and review separately from accrued awards.
Secure timely alternative payment and execution under existing law. A vendor
refund or damages claim enters usable cash only on actual collection. Under
the programme statute, credit an interim amount only against a later payable
award for the same person, purpose and period, with a statement of what
actually settled. Otherwise the properly granted interim amount remains
public expenditure. Any fraud recovery needs separate proof, authority,
notice, challenge and hardship protection. Never net it against unrelated
benefits. Where an authorized route fails, report the exact unmet amount,
deadline and remedy owner. Disclose shared staffing and capped exposure
alongside continuing due claims in the elected budget.

**RLW05 — Keep the remedy independent through transition and future systems.**
Use the same rule for paper, voice, assisted, digital and AI-mediated
procedures. The duty owner carries lawful scope, case dates, dissent, source
limits and open orders to the receiving office. A claimant can act without a
supplier, AI or successor account. Give independent reviewers protected access
and an executable remedy. AI can locate possible evidence or compare periods.
It cannot supply original proof, decide whether someone is credible or
authorize a payment. Investigate common loss or withholding and publish safe
aggregate outcomes. Keep each unresolved case assigned through closure of the
technical incident. Adopt prospectively with paid assessors, independent review
and statutory interim funding. Preserve accrued claims and live proceedings
under their governing instruments. Reopen on unequal proof burdens, repeated
expiry, privacy harm, unreliable rebuttal or a simpler route delivering
equivalent access and relief.

The [worked transfer](../../../settlement/public-record-loss/01-lost-files-and-essential-support.md)
compares protection, adjudication and gross costs. The substantive law of
each benefit and actual evidence, appropriations and outcomes remain separate.
