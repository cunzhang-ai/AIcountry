## Competing arrangements and selection

Rely on case-by-case review of outcomes with ordinary human approval.
This preserves local flexibility but permits the same improper input to
shape several independently signed acts. Forbid all reuse between services.
That reduces propagation but can force people to provide the same evidence
again and interfere with specific lawful warnings. Select a functional
prohibition of portable adverse profiles with tightly scoped transfer of
lawful facts or specific warnings. Keep independent challenge and actual
delivery in each sector. Protected manual proof and lawful specific
cross-office cooperation remain usable.

The strongest objections concern hidden relabeling, barriers to useful
investigations and simultaneous reassessment when many services use a
common provider. Trace actual effects before use, preserve specific
statutory action and count shared fallback capacity once. A broad dataset
or a capable classifier cannot resolve these institutional tradeoffs.
No independent qualified review or actual affected-party participation
was obtained.

## Authority, continuity and remedy

The elected lawmaker sets the protected scope and resources. Sector owners
decide what is relevant and lawful, and operators execute within their
assigned powers. Independent data and sector reviewers and courts use their
own jurisdictions and original evidence. They must be able to act without
the implicated supplier or shared digital director. Residents and opposition
can challenge the scope with protection for private evidence. Review must
test actual appointment, funding, source control and enforceable receipt
before reliance. Coordination alone supplies no new actor or permission.

Preserve earlier lawful evidence, due benefits, orders, existing review
routes and deadlines. If the purpose was unlawful, isolate that use and
correct actual affected acts under existing legal processes. An accurate
source does not need falsification to remove the downstream label. No
universal file, general suppression power or portable finding is created.
Carry old records and pending cases to lawful funded successors under DP,
PD, EV and RUC as appropriate.
