# A finite chief and protected statutory careers

Design 98 · Proposed research, not enacted law or verified operation.

**MA01 — Public choice, finite office.** Assembly law defines the actual prohibited conduct,
merger/access predicates, thresholds, information/correction powers, limitation, sanctions,
administrative/judicial routes and funding; no general 'harmful company' test or duty invented by
guidance. One chief serves six nonrenewable years from lawful assumption, no holdover, return as
acting chief or power to choose a permanent successor. Ordinary party membership, criticism and
unpopular lawful business models are not disqualifications. Require demonstrated
competition/rights/fair-process competence and accountable administration, with seven years relevant
law, economics, forensic finance, public/worker/consumer enforcement or equivalent demonstrated
work; no compulsory single degree/guild, industry list or AI qualification veto. Disclose actual
interests, sponsor/employment ties and ability to serve. Current minister/legislator, senior party
executive/campaign manager, paid political lobbyist or controlling/managing officer of a materially
supervised enterprise cannot serve; require four years since those roles. Actual relevant conflicts
require recusal/divestment and ordinary judicial review, not permanent exclusion by allegation.
These qualifications/cooling periods are research choices.

**MA02 — An open pool and a fixed public vote.** Existing Assembly recruitment staff advertise for
at least 30 calendar days, receive all consenting applications, verify published job-related
criteria with disclosed qualified advice and publish eligible nominees, reasoned exclusions and safe
conflict summaries at least 14 calendar days before a decisive vote. No universal private shortlist
owner. Any Assembly member may nominate an eligible person; accessible public hearing, objections
and reasoned response precede a recorded single-name vote. Appointment needs both ceil(3N/5)
affirmative votes of full statutory Assembly membership including vacancies and ceil(O/3)
affirmative votes of the fixed protected non-government cohort. No absence/abstention lowers either
denominator; O=0 means no appointment. Publish proposed appointment/qualification reasons at least
21 calendar days before entry. Adopt B16's 60-working-day challenge period from usable
publication/individual notice with blocked-access/unfairness extension and
concealed-fraud/material-conflict protection; useful-time B17 relief is earlier, no automatic stay
or old-term extension.

O follows the actual CJ05 definition: cabinet participation and publicly recorded
coalition/confidence-and-supply agreements establish the governing cohort, not one policy vote.
Publish named occupied non-government seat positions at opening. Initial vacancies do not enter O;
later vacancy does not reduce it. A lawful successor may approve only while outside government;
entering government removes capacity to supply protected approval, never retrospectively counts an
old vote. Unsupported/unresolved classification allegations do not subtract seats. Genuinely new
government/agreement requires published reassessment and a fresh vote with no transferred approvals.
Ordinary unconflicted courts review classification/selection; no minister certifies authentic
opposition. This is a new markets rule, not transferred CJ05 approval or CJ12 jurisdiction.

Open scheduled recruitment 180 days before expiry, unexpected vacancy within 14 days; publish
unresolved qualification/availability by 90 days before expiry and failure/hearing by 30. First
ballot within 30 days of the eligible list; repeat at least every 30 days while the competent
Assembly can sit, reopen applications after two failed ballots. No plurality/simple-majority,
executive, self-appointment or silence fallback. A court corrects unlawful process, not a
representative's good-faith refusal or the choice of nominee. Parliamentary inability to agree
remains a visible failure, not a secret deemed appointment.

**MA03 — External removal, not retaliation.** On petition by a person with ordinary standing, one
fifth of full Assembly membership or an existing competent integrity investigator, ordinary courts
may remove for proved serious misconduct, persistent material statutory breach or inability after
reasonable accommodation. Notice, defense, source access, unconflicted assignment, reasoned B17
judicial suspension and ordinary appeal apply. Neither cabinet nor chief's reviewer can remove for
inconvenient lawful findings, business unpopularity, honest reversals or a changed government.
Removal takes effect after appeal time unused or one ordinary appeal decided, subject to competent
further review/stays. No second substantive chief during unresolved removal. Chief
appointment/removal/own-office funding/data cases use ordinary courts; CJ retains only its actual
constitutional scope, and CJ12's closed own-court chamber is not a markets tribunal.

**MA04 — Career appointment and separation of employment power.** The chief appoints, through C04
open job-related competition, an operational secretary and a separate senior hearing/decision
officer; the chief is their reasoned C04 disciplinary authority, with proved grounds, usable
notice/reply, protected career tenure and direct ordinary-court review. Cabinet cannot
appoint/remove/reassign them for a case. The secretary recruits/assigns/disciplines administrative
and investigative staff through C04; the senior decision officer recruits/assigns/disciplines
hearing/decision staff through the same rules. Separate fixed prior qualification, rotation,
conflict and exceptional-reassignment rules govern case teams and hearings; an investigator may not
decide the same case's contested adverse corrective order. Chief sets published general lawful
priorities/caseload expectations and may require reasoned progress reporting, but cannot dictate
evidence/final findings/court outcome, privately select a favorable hearing officer or bypass fair
assignment by discipline/transfer. Necessary general reassignment needs attributable reasons, no
case-directed pretext, preserved dates/defense and direct review. Protected discipline does not
create immunity for proved misconduct or unperformed lawful duties. Direct conflicts use
predesignated qualified substitutes and ordinary courts, not the implicated manager choosing their
own reviewer.

**MA05 — Vacancy cannot become a case veto.** Chief appoints a qualified career deputy by C04 open
competition run by existing Assembly recruitment staff before vacancy. At vacancy/suspension the
deputy has at most 180 elapsed days of routine funded management only; from day one no new material
general policy, wider power or discretionary senior appointments. Afterwards no acting-chief powers,
relabelling cannot restart the period. If no qualified unconflicted deputy exists, statutory
officers immediately continue only their own enacted duties. Crucially, the adopted Act assigns
investigations, necessary record notices, court applications, ordinary V05 determinations and the
chosen statutory merger/access decisions directly to named qualified career roles, not a chief's
mandatory personal signature. Those powers continue with prior conflict/assignment rules even beyond
180 days; chief-reserved managerial acts stay unavailable and disclosed. No new major case needs a
vacant chief's permission, but no employee gains a missing legal power. For first establishment
only, an express prior Act assigns existing Assembly recruitment staff to run the funded C04 open
competitions for non-managerial statutory case roles, with published job criteria, qualified advice,
conflicts, reasons and direct ordinary review; the Assembly career Clerk makes the reasoned career
appointments under those criteria. This narrow founding designation cannot appoint the chief,
operational secretary or senior decision-office manager, and cannot choose a preferred officer for a
pending case. The enacted case powers attach to qualified ordinary case officers and prior
assignment rules, not a missing manager signature. After establishment MA04 governs ordinary
employment. A Clerk/recruitment label proves no independence: actual lawful appointers, qualified
staff, funds and courts remain unverified; no personnel means actual unmet work and direct court
access, not cancelled claims or invented availability.

*Part of [the competition statute](../11-competition-and-economic-power.md); normative thresholds and institutions, no authority from publication.*
