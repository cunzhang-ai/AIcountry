# Necessary care: honest uncertainty and essential continuity

*Canonical UA04–UA06; part of [C08/23](../23-necessary-care-and-unknown-will.md).*

**UA04 — Name the absence of knowledge.** When relevant will remains genuinely
unascertainable, identify the decision, time, actual uncertainty, serious support
attempts, evidence/objections and latest useful action time. Never-communicated
preferences are possible; do not invent a biography or treat diagnostic membership
as a person's view. This finding neither consents nor removes legal agency.
The new statute must expressly authorize any residual act. B23 asset custody,
E funding, S service eligibility, a signature field or an AI prediction does not.

**UA05 — A narrow standing basic-care duty.** Where necessary, no applicable
refusal/actual opposition exists and delay would threaten basic bodily safety,
permit essential noncoercive ordinary sustenance, safe oral food/drink as clinically
appropriate, hygiene, warmth, comfort, pain assessment, safe positioning and
communication assistance. Continue a previously lawful essential-care plan only
within its actual authority; this is not carte blanche for old prescriptions.
No fresh court order is required for each drink or position change.

Record necessity, responses and adverse signs; seek familiar least intrusive
methods and recheck each encounter. Assign an independent advocate within
seventy-two elapsed hours of identifying continuing unknown-will dependence,
earlier where protection requires; missed assignment is a remediable breach,
not a reason to stop basic care or expand its scope. The person/any concerned
party can seek direct independent review without provider consent.

This is an express statutory permission for limited care without ascertainable
consent, not silence-as-consent. It excludes tube feeding, new drugs/invasive
procedures, restraints, covert medication, behavioral sedation, locked placement
and property disposal. A routine-care label cannot bring them back. Clinically
unsafe feeding or observed resistance requires pause and appropriate assessment;
the duty is not to force a fixed checklist or ignore swallowing/pain risks.

**UA06 — Preserve a home without inventing a private contract.** A substantial
new nonclosed care arrangement, essential move or personal-service act uses UA07
and prior court review where no actual authority exists. Compare real familiar,
home/community and nonclosed residential options; scarcity alone is no preference
or ground to confine. Preserve tenancy/property and actual relationships; no forced
permanent home sale, private loan or unlimited future debt. The public authority
contracts/pays in its own lawful service role rather than forging patient assent.

The order identifies receiving staff/place, transport, continuity, review and
assisted ability to leave. An unlocked door is insufficient if people prevent
actual assisted departure. Family may refuse unpaid work; withdrawal triggers
public paid receiving, not compelled labor. B23's separate asset grounds and
actual ordinary payment duties remain, without authority over treatment or life.
