# T13–T17. Start the first country programme in coherent parts

*Design273 · Proposed implementation of [protected stages](../06-staged-capacity-and-protection.md).*

**T13 — Put the whole first decision on one schedule.** The first
government programme identifies three classes for every material
component. Carry currently owed or enacted due duties, record funded
preparation that can lawfully proceed on its own, and list prospective
additions or transfers that still need a public decision. State the
exact legal texts, start dates, affected people and areas, ordinary
and adverse resource paths, accepted operators, independent remedies
and due duties outside the change. Carry the first lawful electoral
and governing transition on its own prior source. A later policy
programme cannot retroactively supply founding authorization.
Use [GP](../../C03/20-government-programme-and-public-choices.md),
[DB](../../C06/60-democratic-budget-and-execution.md) and
[JC](../../C06/65-joint-continuity-and-first-budget.md) for their
separate votes and budgets.

**T14 — Name which changes need one another and why.** At proposal,
mark components as jointly effective, independently effective, or
expressly contingent within prior law. A new burden, enforcement
power or withdrawal of a service route must have its indispensable
protection, ordinary or interim delivery, and usable independent
remedy in the same scope from its first effect. An opposition right
or court must be usable before the power it checks can rely on it.
Preparatory work or a beneficial addition can start separately if
its authority, work, price and continuing duties stand on their own.
Only genuine legal, operational or incidence dependencies justify
linking votes. The Assembly can reject the bundle and decide a
separate viable text. Avoid requiring one omnibus vote on unrelated
matters. Keep competent local decisions distinct.

**T15 — Decide the exact revised package after partial assent.**
For a material amendment, lost appropriation, absent local acceptance
or adverse review, compare the actual remaining text against its
dependency scope. Before the elected start is fixed, put any new
material combination or slower start back to the competent body
with cost, rejected parts, alternative and exposed residents. Use
already elected contingent routes only within their precise scope
and conditions. Where law expressly makes commencement conditional,
name the actual decision, final date for disposition, published
evidence, challenge and default. An unresolved option returns for
a recorded decision, including reasoned rejection. Neither a report,
minister, funder nor software release can suspend an existing claim,
an election or a date already fixed by law. A missed due date is
a breach requiring protection and correction under T03–T04.

**T16 — Fund any period when both systems must operate.** Specify
which rule and office applies to work before, during and after the
boundary, including cases filed before it, payment and appeal already
due, evidence access and accepted transfer. Retain prior clocks and
applicable substantive protections. Keep an old route usable until
the applicable lawful successor is funded and accepts its work, within
the governing law. If the predecessor office must end by law, name
a separately lawful interim operator. Include real parallel costs,
training, record custody, workers, financing and deadline-safe
public access. A legacy software shutdown, source-law repeal or
uncertain saving cannot substitute for that work. A partly shared
workforce counts only within its simultaneous capacity. Qualified
review protects against both an indefinite dual system and early
retirement. Pay already valid claims after retirement through their
own lawful route.

**T17 — Publish the resulting first decision and keep failed duties open.**
Use a single public decision page stating what continues, what has
been authorized for preparation, which complete new groups take effect,
what remains held or rejected, and which already due duties are failing.
Record who voted or accepted, applicable law, dated sources, responsible
receivers, review and the next elected decision. Under existing
Assembly agenda and NP rules use the next protected disposition
date. Continue ordinary governing work if some components fail.
Verify post-start receipt and preservation of political control
separately. AI can compare texts, dates and resources and show a missing
dependency. It cannot combine separate votes, accept a public function,
start a law or certify readiness. Reopen if a prerequisite fails,
the retained route loses practical access, or a better whole option
has qualified support.
