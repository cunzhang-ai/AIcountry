# HL09–HL12. Housing capacity, finance and correction

[Housing loss and secure receiving](../25-housing-loss-and-secure-receiving.md)

**HL09 — Buy suitable capacity and a genuinely independent backup.**
The elected housing authority funds a continuing public residual
and can lawfully lease, reserve or buy qualified service from plural
operators. Count accepted use by accessibility, staffing, security,
location, transport and time. Do not double-book a place shared with
care, refuge or disaster obligations. Test common building, power,
vendor, payment and host losses under JC. Compare prevention and
direct durable receiving with repeated interim purchasing, including
real supply and worker burdens. A licensed room still needs suitable
acceptance. Preserve a credible ordinary supply account under H/LU
rather than assuming an unlimited private vacancy or new public unit.

**HL10 — Separate the money that prevents, receives and continues.**
Assembly and competent local elected bodies fund the statutory
floor under MF and DB. Treasury and the steward show prevention
payments, emergency reserve and actual use, lawful compensation and
repairs, and continuing rent, support and upkeep. Match I03, housing
programmes and E06 only for the actual same cost and period under
their credit rules. Keep other accrued and earned claims. Check
cash due before key access and the recurring net source for the
existing or new lease. Show whose existing use would be displaced.
Potential savings elsewhere, future recoveries and undelivered
vacancies do not fund an earlier duty. An underfunded plan leaves
named exposure and a choice of lawful correction.

**HL11 — Measure whether housing actually lasts.** The steward
reports prevention that held, unsafe nights, accepted ordinary
occupancy, repeated loss, ongoing cost and actual remedies. Publish
safe aggregates by need and place with missingness and safe outside
sampling of people who never reached intake. Separate voluntary
moves from loss and a corrected original entry from a new case.
Independent audit and review test actual scope and source. An
operator must not be rewarded for closing files, excluding hard
cases or displacing a person into another district. No league-table
score decides eligibility.

**HL12 — Transfer claims and provision without losing their dates.**
Prospective enactment names the paying bodies, duties, exact start,
funded rosters, accepted places, ordinary units, qualified suppliers
and actual review capacity. Apply FT/OP/T to existing tenancies,
owner and worker claims, original dates, records, payments and
continuing cases. Keep stronger applicable protections and the
sector authority of each body. Reopen on recurrent unsafe nights,
involuntary moves, unpayable rent, illegal data use, displacement or
a better complete alternative. Actual source, staffing and safe
receiving determine operational readiness.
