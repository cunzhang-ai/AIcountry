# FB55–FB59. Choose a durable revenue response before the bridge runs out

*Design460 · Part of [lasting services and fiscal shocks](../62-lasting-services-and-fiscal-shocks.md).*

**FB55 — Compare exact uses of the same fiscal room.** When a
material recurring revenue loss threatens protected functions or
a material new source is proposed for protection, use the four-year account to compare retaining sources with lawful repair,
a diversified source change and a credible alternative burden.
Separate gross assessments, refunds, collection costs and settled
receipts by period and payer. Show recurring expenses, earned backing,
independent institutions and the first due payment separately. Show
who bears each instrument under plausible incidence and behavioral
responses. Where source changes affect the same worker-owners, test
shifts between wages, distributions and retained profit together
under TN21–TN25. Reconcile any changed earned contributions and
general backing once, separately from the general tax yield. A rate or revenue assumption needs its own legal and
evidentiary basis. No automatic tax, transfer or service cut follows
from a forecast.

Test the source and the offer together at household and service cutoffs.
Reprice attainable essentials after plausible changes in retail prices,
unavoidable bills, wages and fees. Reapply actually lawful benefit rules
once, with paid timing, gross cost and affected groups preserved. Show
new shortfalls among nonrecipients and existing losses that deepen. A
source that defeats the protected outcome fails that release as proposed.
Compare a different source, a fully funded correction and continued
standing law. Preserve independently due claims. Use direct review and a
separately funded urgent route for actual nonreceipt under their own law.
Where the source legally depends on prior usable protection, apply
[BCV19](../60/09-conditional-budgets-and-protected-fallback.md) and
fund that protection before the first affected bill.

**FB56 — Separate uncertain revenue from owned timing protection.**
Test the receipts and service costs that can deteriorate together.
Do not count a favorable annual forecast, uncollected tax or restricted
asset as cash available on a due date. Identify which revenue can
support the ordinary programme under the stated adverse path.
An elected finite bridge names its source, fee, settlement, maturity,
repayment and later usable capacity. Keep principal, debt service and
ordinary operating costs on their own schedules. A repeated draw
requires its own qualified source. Keep the structural residual visible.

Test declining receipts caused by the intended success of the policy
as well as default, delayed payment and recession. Keep the lawful
taxable base, rate, exemptions, refunds and settlement calendar
separate from total output or environmental measures. Compare a faster
or more complete success path and the first relevant zero-receipt
period where plausible. Verify which costs actually end and which
services, debt, maintenance and claims remain. Provide for standing
duties through a qualified durable source. Use genuinely finite
receipts for authorized scoped work with lawful closeout. A slower
improvement may collect more revenue and retains its own service
and ecological costs. Do not budget a need for the harm to continue.

**FB57 — Bring the actual burden and opportunity cost to a vote.**
Present distinct texts for finite protection, durable net sources
and any reprioritization or phased offer. Show the affected groups
and the services, assets or future options forgone. Protect
independent scrutiny, earned claims and the essential floor under
FB36–FB41. A permanent saving counts only if an actual lawful
expense ends, no protected duty or binding claim is displaced and
the relevant receiving capacity remains usable. Use the normal
elected budget and source decisions, including local approval and
ordinary taxpayer remedies where applicable. No verified recurring
margin authorizes new spending by itself. Where a finite funding
need could be met by sale or concession, compare net proceeds at
closing against retention with a qualified bridge on the same due
dates. Record loss of income or control, repayment and handback
alongside enduring service and independent scrutiny. Keep residual
asset estimates and restricted capital cash out of the recurring
funding account.

**FB58 — Close the repayment and second-call schedules together.**
Before relying on a funded response, reconcile each lawful payer's
ordinary settlement, reserve use, debt maturity, independent
execution and resident receiving. Reserve a separate path for a
foreseeable second call when the first uses the available room.
Hold the dependent optional addition if that scope cannot be funded
or operated. Keep existing service duties, enforceable claims
and their original dates. If neither the original nor the alternate
delivers, disclose the exact cash or capacity gap and give the
competent elected body the funded correction choice.

**FB59 — Verify the recurring choice and its transition independently.**
Compare observed net receipts, incidence, capacity and service
against the exact voted scope and dates. Give opposition, workers,
recipients and taxpayers timely access to safe findings and direct
challenge. AI may reconcile accounts and compare scenarios. It
cannot decide incidence, establish receipt, set a tax or appropriate
cash. Reopen on weaker collections, shifted burdens, missed service,
repeated bridge use or a better rights-preserving source. Keep
the predecessor and binding claims through any later change.
