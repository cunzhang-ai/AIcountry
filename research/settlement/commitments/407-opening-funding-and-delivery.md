# Design494 continuing commitments

The [first-round settlement](../../FIRST-ROUND-SETTLEMENT.md) keeps
effective duties, original dates, live files, independent remedies and
external obligations through establishment. A new budget, authority
or medium cannot silently take those obligations over. The
[first funding choice](../opening-funding-and-delivery/01-which-source-can-pay-for-the-first-visit.md)
tests one prospective addition, charging all binding standby and
activation costs before the relevant cutoffs.
