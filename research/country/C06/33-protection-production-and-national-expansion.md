# C06. Protection, production and national expansion

*15 October 2026 · Integrated design 84 · Proposed NP01–NP20; not enacted law.*

Select **protection-first mixed provision with paid receiving capacity and
progressive financing of dated expansion**. Preserve universal essential access,
plural production, person-controlled income, earned retirement rights and local
choice. Do not launch the whole proposed catalogue on an assumed productivity
surge. Do not leave expansion forever behind an unspecified readiness study.

1. [What the country funds first](33/01-selected-package-and-protected-functions.md): NP01–NP03.
2. [Real production, households and financing](33/02-production-households-and-sources.md): NP04–NP07.
3. [Paid receiving and independent capacity](33/03-receiving-and-reserves.md): NP08–NP09.
4. [Payment priority, physical shortage and public control](33/04-shortage-and-control.md): NP10–NP13.
5. [A dated duty to choose and prepare](33/05-dates-preparation-and-migration.md): NP14–NP18.
6. [Rivals, costs and conditions for replacement](33/06-rivals-and-reopening.md): NP19–NP20.

This changes allocation and sequence: indispensable receiving, maintenance and
independent remedy capacity precede optional new breadth; already included roles
are reattributed, not repurchased. Where dependable supply is absent, finance a
public/local residual or an accepted prepaid substitute before relying on it.
The surviving debt and its immediate payment priority are different questions.
Tax incidence and displaced uses are chosen openly, not hidden in a required GDP.

The [old 23-component book](22-country-operating-resource-book.md) remains a
historical bid comparison, not this package's staffing ceiling or full estimate.
[SF](25-source-finance-and-payment-continuity.md) remains a conditional financing
architecture with one old-scope standby, not money for these new duties.
[CP](28-national-commitments-and-residual-scarcity.md) keeps its narrow residual
powers; NP does not turn them into general diversion or forced private service.

[Country requirements and alternatives](../../settlement/material-country/README.md)
trace all 37 currently audited functions, current later duties, household effects
and comparable preparation/operation horizons. Unknown quantities/costs are
explicit variables with selection rules, not zeros or proof of affordability.
The conditional institutional choice is selected; quantified full-duty feasibility,
actual authority, safe capacity and superior outcomes are not established.

[Design86 QE](34-reference-costs-and-delivery-conflicts.md) now supplies a
joined named-cost benchmark rather than numerical bid shares: optional capacity
is costed separately from receipt, I05/Y and asset/training sources stay joined,
and failed useful-time court/place/operator cases remain explicit. It is not
a qualified full-duty estimate or affordable-country finding; no oldbid or SF
scope is requalified. NP remains conditional pending source/production/calendars.

[FD](35-finance-production-and-delivery-bridge.md) now joins current sources,
price-indexed I05/Y, planned GDP/capital and voluntary finance. Four tested
source bundles fail; negative payment residuals are not overdrafts or claims
on restricted property. Court/receiving redesign is conditional and paid, not
actual qualification. NP remains a protected design aim, not a ready allocation.

[PS01–PS20](37-physical-supply-and-paid-trade.md) now specifies the physical
food/water/power/trade operating alternative. Whole-country prices, jobs/wages,
initial assets and owned recurring/foreign sources remain to be joined; failed
physical/seasonal/outage cases and continuing I/S/Y/H duties cannot be averaged away.
