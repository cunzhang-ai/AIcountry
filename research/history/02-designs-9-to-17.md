# Design history: designs 9–17

*Part of [Design history](../DESIGN-HISTORY.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

10. **Integrated design 9: material provision and ordinary resilience connected,
    readiness open.** [Production/work](../2026-10-material-and-resilience/production-work.md)
    specifies who risks commercial investment, who authorizes public capability
    spending and how workers can bargain and move through change.
    [Essential systems](../2026-10-material-and-resilience/essential-systems.md)
    identify answerable water, power and food authorities, lawful operators,
    safety reviewers, affordability and maintenance funding, scarcity duties
    and replacement paths. [Disaster/public health](../2026-10-material-and-resilience/disaster-health.md)
    separates funded ordinary preparation and sector action from exceptional
    D9 power, with supported and reviewable restrictions. An integration check
    added an effect-based boundary so serial ordinary-law labels cannot evade
    elected or constitutional limits. The same workers,
    physical reserves, imported inputs, ecological capacity and external cash
    constrain all three. They are institutional choices, not operating assets.
    The [design 9 decision](../2026-10-material-and-resilience/decision-record.md)
    records their comparison, conflicts and migration.
11. **Integrated design 10: candidate choice and lived time specified, readiness
    open.** The [candidate-gate comparison](../2026-10-democracy-and-time/candidate-gate.md)
    gives voters an optional say over compensatory-list names while preserving
    territorial independents, nonparty lists, party association, rapid nomination
    remedies and a protected cutoff before the election. The
    [time-and-care comparison](../2026-10-democracy-and-time/time-and-care.md)
    chooses a national hours/rest/scheduling floor, bargaining above it and
    portable care-leave income connected to real replacement care and shift staff.
    Both choices need the same electoral/court and labor/service budget; neither
    a party rule nor an AI model can create the missing capacity. The
    [design 10 decision](../2026-10-democracy-and-time/decision-record.md)
    records rival designs, objections, migration and the next lawful tests.
12. **Integrated design 11: housing and everyday mobility made operational,
    readiness open.** The [housing-and-mobility comparison](../2026-10-housing-and-mobility/analysis.md)
    identifies an elected local delivery responsibility, national access and
    funding floor, plural builders/operators, independent checks, maintained
    public residual capacity and lawful receiving paths when homes or routes
    fail. It compares retaining the present plan, predominantly direct national
    provision and regulated plural provision without a backbone. A compound
    flood, transit failure and grid delay tests the connection to C04/C06/C09,
    but no housing stock, routes, staff, currency, jurisdiction or affected
    people's choice has been demonstrated. The
    [design 11 decision](../2026-10-housing-and-mobility/decision-record.md)
    records the selection, objections, migration and next test.
13. **Integrated design 12: public evidence made contestable, readiness open.**
    The [public-evidence comparison](../2026-10-public-evidence/analysis.md)
    assigns original records to accountable sector custodians and comparable
    official statistics and archives to a separately governed service. It
    retains plural external inquiry, method/version disclosure, safe personal
    correction and review of suppression without a compulsory national personal
    dossier. It compares unchanged sector reporting and a single centralized
    evidence authority, then tests a ministry and common vendor suppressing
    housing and water facts before an election. Appointments, budgets, staff,
    source access, privacy, backup custody and executed remedies remain
    unverified. The [design 12 decision](../2026-10-public-evidence/decision-record.md)
    records the selection and migration.
14. **Integrated design 13: legal rank and lawful change specified, adoption
    open.** The [legal-hierarchy decision](../2026-10-legal-hierarchy/decision-record.md)
    compares leaving rank implicit, constitutionalizing every detail, and
    protecting only election procedure. The selected layered reference puts
    equal standing, democratic replacement, rights, essential/ecological duties
    and local elected existence in the proposed charter; mutable institutions
    and service standards in Assembly statutes; cash and local choices below
    them. The [founding sequence](../FOUNDING-AND-STRESS.md) now requires the
    initial election rule to be valid *before* the first Assembly exists.
    [A10–A14](../AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION.md) state candidate franchise,
    access, local, change and evidence clauses. This neither adopts the charter
    nor proves that a court, budget or provider can make the duties effective.
15. **Integrated design 14: AI assigned useful public work, deployment open.**
    The [AI public-capability decision](../2026-10-ai-public-capability/decision-record.md)
    compares retaining largely manual/sector tools, a central national AI
    administrator and plural task-mandated assistance. It selects a reference
    portfolio for optional resident agents, public option comparison,
    professional support, utility/hazard forecasting, legal/evidence work and
    research. Every use has a lawful task owner, impact boundary, comparable
    non-AI route, full resource account and exit. No current state deployment,
    public participation, independent field evaluation or observed life
    improvement follows from the text; AI copies grant no mandate.
16. **Integrated design 15: election count selected, public adoption open.**
    The [count-rule decision](../2026-10-election-rule/decision-record.md)
    compares overhang-and-leveling MMP, fixed-size list-vote cover, open-list
    proportional representation and candidate-ranked districts. The fixed-size
    reference includes every eligible positive-vote list in allocation without
    an added percentage threshold, gives independent plurality winners personal
    seats, and seats list-backed territorial plurality winners only within list
    entitlement.
    It resolves a real seat-count contradiction, but N, D, district map,
    timing, ballot comprehension, nomination/finance rules, source evidence and
    executable appeals still require legitimate first-election law and capacity.
17. **Integrated design 16: one commitment account selected, feasibility open.**
    The [whole-country resource decision](../2026-10-whole-country-resources/decision-record.md)
    compares sector-only budgets, a central technical veto, one elected package
    with federated source accounts, and an automatic priority ladder. It selects
    the elected package with distinct legal, cash, physical-delivery and remedy
    states across C03–C14, so a new promise cannot borrow the same staff, site,
    reserve or external currency twice without exposing the collision. It also
    limits indefinite executive delay of future-start services. No new national
    affordability estimate is invented: the earlier three-year scenario does
    not cost the current design, and real opening obligations, tax incidence,
    physical capacity, public choice and lawful enactment remain unavailable.
18. **Integrated design 17: constitutional change opened to the electorate,
    adoption open.** The [constitutional-change decision](../2026-10-constitutional-change/decision-record.md)
    compares D10's Assembly-only supermajority route, executive plebiscites,
    standing constituent conventions and dual initiation. The reference keeps
    two-thirds Assembly support for its route, adds a direct elector-petition
    route needing three-fifths public approval, and requires public reasons,
    challenge and delayed effect for incumbent-benefiting changes. Courts
    protect democratic functions without a general policy veto. The previous
    procedure governs its replacement. Petition percentages, deadlines,
    public independence, valid founding and operating capacity remain untested.
