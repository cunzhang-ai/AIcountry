# C06. Required demand, maintained assets and a financeable reference

15 October 2026 · Design 64 · Conditional research comparator, not a funded country

A proposed country budget must reconcile **what people can actually spend,
what producers can supply, all counterpart claims and when payment is due**.
Select demand-consistent, steady-asset and opening-stock/timing checks as
part of the existing joined-account requirement. No new tax/pension law or
mandatory dividend policy is selected through a successful calculation.

| Part | Country-design consequence |
|---|---|
| [Income, demand and economic choice](21/01-income-demand-and-choice.md) | Required output follows declared cash income and uses; a GDP residual is not voluntary consumption |
| [Owned assets, future claims and funding](21/02-assets-claims-and-funding.md) | Opening wealth has paired claims, gross replacement is paid, pension obligations stay visible and prior finance cannot come from later output |
| [Timing, shocks and public decision](21/03-timing-shocks-and-public-decision.md) | Due-date cash/FX, rollover, skill-specific shortage, bank liquidity and received remedies remain independent conditions |

The [reference comparison](../../settlement/steady-economy/README.md) uses
unchanged 100,000-person needs/wages and a fully declared post-commission
book. Three earned-credit/closed-legacy/full-payout comparators have zero
planned current balance in household, market, government, insurance and
foreign sectors, replacement equal to wear and no net borrowing in normal
conditions. This is a **conditional accounting result**, not proof that
those institutions, claims or goods exist or that a deficit is always bad.

The 45% work-retention rival requires output about 3,739.79 million under
fixed authored prices, public costs and consumption behavior. Private real
value added per remaining market worker must be about 33.06% above the old
reference. It cannot be called funded at output 3,000 or obtained by adding
a GDP label. Some groups lose cash relative to I05 and the pension rival
changes future accrual; aggregate balance cannot decide those value conflicts.
