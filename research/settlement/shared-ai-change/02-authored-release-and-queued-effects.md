# Preserve simultaneous service when a common update fails

A clinic has three urgent assisted scheduling tasks due before
the next staffed window. A housing office has four protected
intake decisions due in the same interval. Two normal digital
tools and a nominal fallback use one signed update channel and
a common login. An ordinary independent crew can take four
of the seven tasks in this interval. The sector rules in this
fixture put the three urgent clinical tasks first when the
crew is shared. Real cases would follow their actual law and
professional judgments. Assume that a qualified independent
clinical route can carry all three clinic tasks at a cost
of three additional units for the interval, including staffing
and isolated recovery access. Housing then has the four
independent crew slots. The numbers are authored.

| Exact choice under the common loss | Clinic done by deadline | Housing done by deadline | Remaining exposure |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Update all shared paths and use the four-slot crew | 3 | 1 | Three housing decisions |
| Stage the application only, leaving the updater and login shared | 3 | 1 | The same three decisions |
| Pay the isolated clinic route and keep its recovery outside the shared path | 3 | 4 | None in this fixture |
| Same paid route, but recovery credentials also depend on the failed login | 3 | 1 | Three housing decisions |

Choose the isolated route for this assumed common loss. Full
ordinary operation without the shared dependency is a structural
rival if it can staff both scopes. Alternative technologies,
a different recovery controller or an ordinary route may work.
An older version is useful only within its continuing safety
and legal scope. Include time needed to fix vulnerabilities and
the real cost of two operating routes.

After the update, a cached recommendation to close a housing
case cites the affected release. The resident's earlier filing
and deadline remain. Preserve the recommendation as evidence and
send the decision to a qualified ordinary officer. A separate
payment already made under valid authority is not replayed after
restore. If disputed, it follows its own review. A future
recommendation whose material dependency cannot be identified
does not support an adverse act until qualified.

The comparison isolates the effects of shared change and recovery
control. It does not establish nationwide resilience, safe care,
actual price or better lived outcomes.
