# C03. Keep review effective before material public commitments

*Design405 · PCR01–PCR08 · Proposed ordinary safeguards for scheduled public acts.*

A formal vote or contract may come after the practical choice has already been
lost. Identify the first act that would make a lawful alternative materially
harder to choose. Give affected people and opposition a usable opportunity for
review before that act, and preserve necessary services throughout.

**PCR01 — Apply the rule to scheduled material acts.** Primary law specifies
covered classes, objective scope, deciding bodies and materiality. Start
with material concessions and public disposals, capital stages requiring
fresh elected authorization and scheduled irreversible service or ecological
changes. Cover related steps across agencies, budgets and legal instruments
according to their combined practical effect. Include binding payments,
guarantees, exclusive control, avoidable irreversible work and transfers of
essential operating capacity where those acts would foreclose an alternative.
Ordinary administration, primary legislation, votes, court orders and
performance of established legal duties keep their own processes.
A large cost or controversial policy alone does not establish coverage.

**PCR02 — Identify the first consequential act and publish the actual choice.**
The sponsor gives the lawful authority, operative decision or mandate, options,
payer, receiving parties, protected duties, open challenges and earliest dates
for covered acts. Show which commitments are already binding and which remain
conditional. Explain significant private conditions and practical effects in a
usable public account. Give lawful access to necessary originals for independent
review. Protect sensitive material without concealing reasons material to a
challenge. Use accessible public notice and safe direct notice for known
persons where the source law requires it. The office that benefits from delay
cannot make its own incomplete notice conclusive.

**PCR03 — Give review time without stacking clocks.** Before a scheduled
material act, allow ten complete working days after usable final notice.
Preserve a stronger or longer applicable sector requirement. An equivalent
lawful sector notice and standstill can satisfy both duties on their actual
scope and run concurrently. A material change to authority, exposure or
affected scope needs fresh notice and the interval for the changed act;
minor corrections do not reset it. Use existing direct review for a deficient
notice, artificial disaggregation or an obstructed route. No discretionary
sponsor waiver. Expiry leaves substantive authorization and any binding
review order to be checked. It never deems the decision lawful.

**PCR04 — Allow bounded preparation that preserves the choice.** Under
existing lawful authority, allow paid assessment, preservation and other
separable steps with real value even if the project stops. A conditional
agreement must leave rejection or redesign practically available. Disclose
all preparation costs, recoverable advances, cancellation terms, collateral
and protected dependencies. Do not hand over exclusive control, incur a
material termination or expectation liability, or start damaging work before
the relevant conditions. A refundable label needs enforceable and usable
return. An option or preliminary agreement cannot guarantee approval or make
public funds available before lawful appropriation.

**PCR05 — Route urgent objections to an independent decision and executor.**
Use existing legal grounds, forums and interim standards. Give affected
people and authorized challengers a direct route with practical help, and
preserve opposition's lawful access and ability to present alternatives.
Identify the threatened act, earliest useful time, probable harm, proposed
narrow relief and costs to others. Give fair reply and time limits under the
governing law. Filing alone creates no additional stay. An interim order
identifies the exact restriction, expiry, lawful executor and receiving
officer. The court can preserve lawful choice and enforce limits. It does
not choose the policy, vote money or approve the contract.

**PCR06 — Keep performance and the release decision separate.** Record
authorized notice, expiry, prerequisites and actual receipt of any operative
order at the officer controlling signature, payment, registration or site
access. Before the specific release, that officer verifies conditions against
the authorized source. If the ordinary route fails, use the preassigned
lawful alternate. A log, sent instruction or stay in a court file alone
does not prove the restricted act stopped. Pay due wages, protect assets
and continue unaffected lawful services. Immediate necessity uses only
earlier independently authorized safety or service powers for the narrow
work and duration required. It cannot exercise the disputed long-term option.

**PCR07 — Preserve remedies and third-party rights after premature action.**
If control, payment or work moves too soon, identify the act, harm, recipient
and continuing duties. Seek the competent remedy under existing source law.
Stop separable further unlawful action. Restore feasible options and protect
workers, good-faith third parties, accrued claims and essential receipt.
Invalidity, restitution, compensation, creditor consequences and reversal
require their own lawful findings. Disclose remaining irreversibility even
after an order. Neither procedural fault nor a missed deadline creates a new
crime, automatic forfeiture or guarantee of an expectation payment.

**PCR08 — Fund usable review and migration.** Assign notice, custody, review,
continuity, release and execution to existing lawful roles before commencement.
Check coordinated acts, late notice, conditional liabilities, absent staff,
failed alternate execution and urgent ordinary services on the same calendar.
Publish safe performance and missed protections. AI may reconcile dated
records or flag linked commitments. It cannot determine standing, issue a
hold, certify notice or release public control. Adopt prospectively with
current duties, binding contracts, orders and accrued claims protected.
Reopen if review routinely arrives too late, lawful service is displaced,
or equivalent sector procedures give effective protection with less burden.
