# Automation gains, income security and public power

5 October 2026 · Integrated design 37 research selection; not enacted law

Prepared by an AI assistant without workers, nonworkers, firms, unions, service
users, tax administrators, elected lawmakers or a real economy. The exact
[design-36 predecessor](predecessor-design-36/README.md) is preserved. This
record changes a working country-design hypothesis, not tax or labor law.

## Defect and competing diagnoses

C05 already selects plural production, bargaining, essential services and an
income floor. C06 taxes several bases and C07 separates basic retirement
support from earnings insurance. Yet a large shift in paid work can hit
household income, care-leave and pension contributions, tax yield, local
budgets and bargaining power together. A concentrated model/compute owner
could also control jobs, information and public service contracts. Treating
each as a separate shock would falsely book the same productivity gain or
contribution receipt several times. This is an internal design vulnerability,
not evidence that AI will cause mass unemployment.

The [ILO's 2025 task-exposure assessment](https://www.ilo.org/publications/generative-ai-and-jobs-2025-update)
finds exposure and expects many occupations to change, while distinguishing
exposure from job loss. An [IMF staff fiscal analysis](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/sdn/2024/english/sdnea2024002.pdf)
models several uncertain labor and tax paths, examines capital-income taxation
and cautions against a generic special AI tax. These sources have different
methods and populations; neither establishes AIcountry's future employment,
tax yield or best institutional choice. Actual adoption, prices, wages,
profits, taxable presence, care work and physical inputs must be observed.

## Whole packages, not one tax knob

| Package | Possible gain | Failure or value conflict |
|---|---|---|
| Keep the existing distributed C05–C07 clauses without a joint automation account | Low new process cost; flexible sector decisions | Earnings, contributions, tax, services and political concentration can fail together while each office assumes another pays |
| Rely mainly on private adoption, training and later labor-market recovery | Potential innovation and low public intervention | Training cannot create a vacant job, earnings or a noncaptured tax base; present losses can destroy bargaining and civic independence |
| Levy every “AI replacement” and pay a universal dividend | Visible gain-sharing outside employment | The tax base and displaced task are hard to define; imports and avoidance can outrun collection; promised cash may exceed receipts and cannot create care workers |
| Publicly own all strategic AI production | Direct potential public return and access control | Requires capital, skill and operating independence; one governing-party platform can combine jobs, data, speech and service appeals |
| **Selected plural economy with a joint distribution/capacity account and elected adaptation** | Keeps existing rights and plural innovation while exposing nonwage financing and ownership power before an elected package choice | Government may respond too late; broad taxes and public options may fail to capture mobile rents; no real evidence yet favors the package |

The selected [C05 rule](../country/C05/04-automation-income-and-power.md)
does not make a forecast self-executing. Basic services and the income floor
remain independent of payroll status; accrued insurance claims survive. A
material observed shift brings labor, fiscal, service and ownership evidence
into one account, with competing remedies considered together. The Assembly
sets the reporting trigger before a particular disputed transition, and
opposition can challenge its suppression without acquiring policy power.
The Assembly can lawfully choose a different tax, cash or ownership package
after public comparison. A dedicated robot tax, national AI monopoly and universal
dividend are alternatives, not adopted assumptions.

## Authority, distribution, compatibility and migration

The Assembly sets tax bases, benefit rules, public investment and competition
powers; local authorities deliver their assigned services. Employers and
workers bargain within labor law; neither a private provider nor a union
settles the national allocation. C11's source custodians and public evidence
function keep observed data separate from model outputs; C06 reconciles
revenue, claims and delivery; independent tax, labor and competition review
can challenge classifications and abuses. Trace their appointment, funding,
provider dependency, source access and order execution. A shared vendor or
wealthy owner could capture several nominally separate safeguards. An AI
forecast has no vote, tax power or authority to declare a job obsolete.

People already outside stable paid work, carers, older residents, disabled
people, young entrants and workers in a single-employer place need separate
accounts. Lower consumer prices can help them but do not prove their income
or services remain usable. Firm owners and small businesses face real
investment and compliance costs; a rigid technology levy could reduce
valuable capability. Workers deserve voice and transition income without a
permanent job guarantee that a government cannot deliver. Current and future
pensioners have different claims; future contribution reform cannot erase
accrued benefits. A special AI dividend without real proceeds cannot replace
existing care, disability or housing duties.

This selection is compatible with C03's elected control, C05's plural economy
and bargaining, C06's existing tax/shortfall account, C07's income and pension
rights, C11's bounded AI portfolio and C14's public-conditions account. Its
consumers include revenue and social-protection authorities, local service
budgets, employers, public AI purchasers and courts. Publication changes no
current tax, benefit, contract or model mandate. If legitimately adopted, a
statute must map existing contribution liabilities, funded credits, public
contracts, private property and pending appeals to lawful successors; any
new base, public stake or time rule applies with enacted transition, rights
review, treaty compatibility and practical collection capacity. The research
method cannot approve its own economic package.

The strongest objection is that the selected design offers disclosure and
response but no guaranteed share of AI rents. A universal social dividend or
broad public ownership could distribute power more directly. They require
credible funding, administration, compensation and control against a public
monopoly; retain them as real rivals for a jurisdiction with evidence. A
second objection is that a general income floor can leave people trapped in
complex need tests. C05 already keeps an unconditional component open; compare
its actual net incidence, take-up, administrative burden and service tradeoffs
rather than declaring it unaffordable or superior in the abstract.

## Status and bounded next test

Evidence qualification: official ILO exposure estimates and IMF staff fiscal
scenarios show uncertainty and possible coupled risks; the joint-failure gap
is an internal design inference. Research selection: retain plural production
and general taxation while adding a cross-system distribution and ownership
response. Legal authorization, operating readiness and observed outcomes:
none. No original AIcountry tax, employment, firm-control or service data
exists; no affected party or independent reviewer participated.

For an actual jurisdiction, build three source-backed trajectories for one
budget term: labor complement with higher wages, uneven displacement with
regional concentration, and strong productivity with a falling labor share.
Reconcile household income, pension/care-leave contributions, tax collection,
profit location, prices, care and essential-worker supply, public AI costs,
market control and legal claims. Compare the selected package with a funded
unconditional payment and a bounded public-ownership package, including
transition capacity and affected people's accounts. The lawful study owner,
budget ceiling and jurisdiction remain unassigned. Reopen the selection if
observed revenue, bargaining, service access or political competition falls
below protected duties, or if a coherent rival delivers more usable freedom
without creating a new controller of public life.
