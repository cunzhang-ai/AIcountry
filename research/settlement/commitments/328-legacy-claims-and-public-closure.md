# Design415 commitment. Carry public obligations through closure

[LPC01–LPC08](../../country/C14/02/06-legacy-claims-and-public-closure.md)
assigns accrued, disputed and later identified claims to a legally
competent office with cash, records, casework and independent review.
Ending intake, dissolving the old office and releasing cash each require
their own authority and evidence. A short reserve does not reduce the
lawful amount owed.
