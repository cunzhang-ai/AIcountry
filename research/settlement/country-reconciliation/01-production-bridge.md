# Production bridge: correct the measure, preserve the actual limits

*Part of [country reconciliation](README.md); fictional million units, not national data.*

The old operating-book and descendant comparisons defined market GVA as GDP
minus government output at cost. That output includes purchases, and GDP also
includes product taxes. Under the fixture convention, government own GVA is its
payroll plus CFC; market basic-price GVA is GDP minus product taxes minus that
own GVA. Public purchases are not a second subtraction from national value added.

## One counterfactual that exposes the incompatible assumptions

Keep the original corrected market GVA per worker, current public bids/wages,
tax/benefit/ownership identities, replacement and foreign contract assumptions.
The selected hybrid then has a goods-consistent hypothetical household expenditure
1751.647429 and GDP 2972.441874. The income/profit rules supply household available
cash 2794.972184. If spending follows the lower goods vector, planned household
cash saving is 1043.324754 and Treasury cash deficit is −1043.324754, with separately
posted counterpart flows. If all available household cash is spent as originally
assumed, the discrepancy remains. This is not an inflation prediction or a
recommendation to cap consumption or force saving. No matching household lending
agreement, debt-service path, appropriation or actual output has been supplied.

Voluntary saving, retained investment, changed production or publicly chosen
redistribution are real alternatives, but each changes the source/distribution
schedule. Household financial capacity is not automatically available to the state.
The calculation rejects only treating all original assumptions as simultaneously
delivered without their productive or financing counterpart.

The separately declared GVA-index rebase gives hybrid energy 1285.404732 GWh and
water 6.802698 million m³. The water-loss vector still fails 6.6. These are new
proportional hypotheses, not engineering requirements, actual ecological supply
or a validation of old 1297.60/6.884 physical claims. Prices/mix, locations and
unknown new duties can invalidate any aggregate GVA-to-resource relation.

The bridge preserves the old simplified financial-service and nonmarket valuation
scope. It is neither complete SNA2008 conformity nor migration to SNA2025's revised
capital/data/depletion concepts. Qualified accounting/production review, workload,
full duty costs, actual independent capacity and observed welfare remain absent.
