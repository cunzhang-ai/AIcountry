# C09. Elected plans, reliable development permission and serviced land

*Design172 ·4November2026 · LU01–LU20 proposed research, not a building permit.*

**Choose enforceable plan-conforming permission, bounded case decisions and
attributable infrastructure obligations.** Elected councils make lawful local
plans; qualified existing officers apply them without private-neighbor veto or
individual political bargaining. Material changes use actual elected law.
Planning permission, ownership/access, safe construction and safe occupation
remain distinct. No national density number, land seizure or new planning board.

Current C09/H/LA/CB/TD05/LR/AP and the pinned housing manuscript already provide
planning, clear rules, infrastructure funding and appeals. LU supplies the
ordinary permission/condition/source/phase settlement, not the first housing law.

For genuinely new ordinary residential planning decisions under the coordinated
Act, remove mandatory minimum general private car-parking quotas. Necessary
accessible parking/drop-off, emergency/service access and actual transport needs
retain their own funded law; neither zero ordinary stalls nor a public map
supplies accessible journeys. Owners may provide lawful voluntary parking.

[Country selection and examples](../../settlement/development-permission/README.md)
retain discretionary, national entitlement, public master-development and
regional alternatives. Actual sites, rights, safe services, costs, sources,
affected authority and housing outcomes remain unknown. More permissions or
higher site value do not establish more safe, affordable occupied homes.

[RT private continuation rents](18-private-rents-tenure-and-housing-entry.md)
adds no permission, land/title or new unit. LU conforming decisions, serviced
capacity, H actual supply/entry and protected neighbor/worker/environment duties
remain; regulated rent alone cannot prove construction or fund a network.

[ER ecological replacement](04/01-ecological-loss-and-replacement.md)

The plan and budget keep alternatives and existing housing claims live

[IV33–IV38](../C06/63/08-construction-capacity-and-sequencing.md) takes credible
