# C03. Legislative capacity and the scope of a decision

*Part of [C03 democratic government](../C03-democratic-government.md); integrated design 56 research, not enacted law.*

The Assembly cannot give every project a direct vote and still scrutinize law,
budgets, executive rules, external commitments, public-power abuse and its own
replacement. A nominally democratic approval that members cannot understand
or opponents cannot contest is not effective public control. The answer is
not an unlimited transfer to cabinet, experts, vendors or AI. The country
uses prior law to assign a decision to the smallest **competent and accountable
public scope** consistent with rights and the people who bear its effects.

## Decide at a lawful scope

The constitutional and statutory settlement reserves national rights,
franchise, general law, national tax and spending, foreign commitments and
unresolved material common obligations to the Assembly. Elected local councils
decide within their defined territory, budget and national floor. Councils may
make voluntary, reviewable interlocal compacts within powers each already
holds; a compact cannot tax or bind a nonmember or erase downstream rights.
For a material shared choice beyond those powers, the C04 interlocal process
prepares alternatives and the Assembly retains the final vote under the
current reference design. A durable directly elected region can replace that
route only after a valid public founding and prospective transfer of specified
powers, finance, records, pending claims and review. No agency's map or
indicator creates the region by itself.
The [C14 national-form choice](../C14/05-scale-and-national-form.md) makes
this Assembly residual conditional on a pre-founding test of actual
representation, recurring docket capacity and delivered remedies. A failed
test requires a legitimate choice of a funded repair or another complete
authority package, verified before operation.

Assembly law must define the materiality and affected-scope tests for each
route before a particular dispute. The test considers rights, duration,
irreversibility, money and scarce physical capacity, persons outside a map,
cumulative effects and whether a local decision would impose a duty on others.
A person or affected council can challenge a self-serving classification;
the reviewer decides competence and legality, not the preferred policy.
The [C09 future-options duty](../C09/04-future-options-and-irreversibility.md)
uses the same prior-law scope test for material hard-to-reverse choices; it
does not send every long-lived local permit to the national Assembly.
Technical and routine delegated instruments remain inside C03's enacted
purpose and effect-sensitive scrutiny. Emergencies use C10's prior-law
limits, including C06's bounded first-hour scarcity acts. Delay at the
Assembly never supplies an executive or technical veto or approval.

## Count the work required for democratic judgment

The Assembly's own public administration, with protected opposition access,
publishes a forward **decision-capacity account** alongside C06's common
budget: expected statutes and amendments, budgets, material regulations,
treaties, territorial requests, interlocal residuals, crisis renewals,
investigations and overdue duties. It records committee and plenary time,
qualified staff, public and affected-party hearing time, translation, source
access, independent review, implementation and pending-case follow-through.
The same committee, expert or resident cannot be counted as available for
simultaneous hearings. The account shows actual queues, missed statutory
steps, distribution of delay and which group controls agenda and
classification. It is evidence for elected judgment, not a clerk's power
to reject a claim or a quota that expires rights.

The [C06 joint schedule](../C06/18-joint-commitment-and-delivery-schedule.md)
tests this scrutiny, opposition time and alternate sitting beside service
and remedy capacity in the same event. Annual gross administration FTE
cannot certify elected deliberation or a source-independent emergency hearing.

When the account or affected people document a missed legal duty or material
delay, opposition and affected local bodies can use a prior-law agenda route
without majority committee permission. Parliamentary staff publish the queue
and put a response item in protected opposition time at the next lawful
sitting. Courts can enforce publication and listing, not direct the vote.
Related claims may share a hearing only if distinct harms remain visible.

The Assembly may fund staff and sitting time, repair consultation or committee
processes, prospectively change materiality thresholds within protected
rights, lawfully revise a service promise, or begin the separate founding
process for an elected regional competence. It compares the full package,
including new offices and transferred revenue, rather than assuming another
tier is progress. The majority must state what remains unresolved and why;
courts can enforce a lawful hearing, source access or existing duty, not enact
the missing allocation. Incumbents cannot revise electoral or opposition
rules for their own current contest through a workload claim.

Opposition agenda space, ordinary local discretion and direct individual
remedies continue through backlog. A committee shortcut cannot bundle
unrelated burdens beyond meaningful scrutiny; a missed time target neither
approves a plan nor ends a claim. The [capacity decision record](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-legislative-capacity/decision-record.md)
explains why the Assembly-reserved territorial choice is retained only
conditionally, and what evidence would displace it.

[DR09–DR12](11/03-members-votes-and-protected-agenda.md) now gives national
rulemaking its exact one-member conversion, fixed full-N votes and protected
L+45 sitting. Simultaneous motions need actual separate scrutiny/time; missed
capacity lapses a new proposal rather than passing it. This specified process
does not turn the capacity account into a policy veto or a power to compel votes.


[LR local-law capacity](14-local-law-and-public-control.md) now supplies a distinct
full-n local norm vote, protected member/affected petition agenda and paid source
account. Parameter execution can reduce repeated voting only inside an enacted
rule. A missed capacity target creates neither approval nor an authority transfer;
national residual/rights and actual local discretion retain their lawful scope.

[DB02–DB05](../C06/60/01-proposal-members-and-budget-choice.md) now specifies
equal member source/costing help and actual protected pre-vote opportunity,
with paid minority capacity and finite triage. Clerk/analyst cannot decide
policy or demand a fiscal certificate; missing scrutiny is not a fictional vote.

[NI09–NI14](16-public-national-initiatives.md) now adds a qualified public
ordinary national agenda without an MP sponsor. Count actual intake/support/
verification, source help, protected hearing/plenary/drafting and paid succession
beside these existing duties. Neither FA triage nor a docket quota erases it;
no signatures create an Assembly vote, law or unpriced independent staff.

[PE01–PE08](21-parliamentary-evidence-and-independent-access.md) adds paid
source preservation, secure interpretation, court execution and a bounded
pre-vote agenda item for material withholding. Joint hearing and protected
triage preserve genuinely distinct objections and actual ordinary case cover.

[JS01–JS05](../C04/01/01-democratic-joint-service-authority.md)
defines a bounded delegated service decision with council and population
majorities. Count its hearings, review and deadlock on the same calendar
as the national residual. The compact cannot acquire reserved powers.

[LCQ01–LCQ06](05/01-legislative-continuity-and-quorum.md) supplies the funded
alternate sitting and mandate, quorum and assent checks. Preserve the
docket and continuing duties when a binding vote cannot occur.
