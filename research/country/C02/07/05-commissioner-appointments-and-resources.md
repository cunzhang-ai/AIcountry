# PD17–PD20: one bounded supervisor under public control

*Design 96 ordinary-data research candidate; not enacted law, funded readiness or
demonstrated superiority.*

**PD17 — Select one limited chief.** Assembly law establishes a six-year nonrenewable
Commissioner term beginning on lawful assumption, no holdover or return as acting chief.
Existing Assembly recruitment staff run an accessible competition advertised at
least30days, verify published job-related competence in rights law, information systems,
evidence and accountable administration, and publish eligible names/reasoned exclusions
with protected evidence and direct ordinary-court challenge. Equivalent demonstrated
work may qualify; no sole guild/degree, industry, vendor, party or AI qualification
veto. Any Assembly member nominates an eligible candidate; notice, conflicts and an
accessible hearing precede a recorded single-name vote requiring three fifths of full
statutory Assembly membership. The chief cannot select their permanent successor.

**PD18 — Failure and removal stay external.** Open scheduled recruitment180days before
term expiry, unexpected vacancy within14days; first ballot within30days of the eligible
list, repeat at least every30days while the competent Assembly can sit, and reopen
applications after two failed ballots. No simple-majority, executive, self-appointment
or deemed-approval fallback; unlawful delay/exclusion is judicially correctable without
the court choosing a preferred chief. Ordinary courts may remove for proved serious
misconduct, persistent material statutory breach or inability after reasonable
accommodation, upon petition by one fifth of full Assembly membership, the existing
competent integrity investigator or a person with ordinary standing. Unwelcome findings,
lawful dissent or a changed cabinet are no ground. Give notice, defense, recusal, appeal
and B17 judicial suspension only; no executive suspension on allegation. Removal takes
effect after appeal time expires unused or one ordinary appeal is decided, subject to
competent further review/stays; no second chief during the unresolved removal.

**PD19 — Career functions survive a vacancy without inventing a chief.** The chief
appoints a qualified career deputy through C04 open competition run by Assembly
recruitment staff before vacancy. During vacancy/suspension the deputy has at
most180elapsed days of routine funded management; from day one no enlarged powers, new
material general policy or discretionary senior appointments. Afterwards no acting-chief
powers; no relabelled absence resets the clock. Already appointed statutory decision
officers and custodians continue only their own enacted, assigned case/preservation
duties, including narrow V04 protection; ordinary corrective orders must be within their
expressly enacted competence. If no unconflicted deputy exists from the outset, those
officers perform only their own duties immediately. Chief-reserved acts remain
unavailable and consequences are reported; direct courts and Assembly appointment duties
continue. No new officer, replacement case selector or competence arises from silence.
C04 career discipline/appeal and prior published assignment/rotation/conflict rules
apply; a minister or chief cannot choose a substitute to secure a named outcome.

**PD20 — Fund work, not successful penalties.** Assembly appropriates bounded staff,
technical inspection, protected custody, accessible intake/advice, independent court
challenge and execution; indispensable statutory duties use NP's bounded standing-source
process, not an unlimited constitutional budget. Treasury pays actual lawful dues
without selecting favored cases. Publish budget incidence, waits and missing capacity;
cuts require lawful prospective treatment, not deletion of claims. Lawful bounded
administrative fees, if enacted, enter elected accounts; neither fines, vendor money,
award quotas nor the number of investigations finances or bonuses the office. No tax,
new borrowing or service deprivation follows from a funding shortage. This single-chief
reference expressly displaces V10's generic staggered-panel/confirmation description
**for this information-rights office only**, preserving its external review, conflict,
source, funding and fault constraints and other sector panels unchanged.
