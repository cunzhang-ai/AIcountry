# C07. Nationally funded early care with local delivery

*Design192 · FC01–FC08 · Proposed implementation of the S/NC care floor.*

**Local care authorities owe the actual offer. The national government funds
the gap between the common standard and the assessed ordinary local source,
and owes the service fallback if local delivery fails.** This choice uses
existing national and local roles. It gives families a route to care when
a ministry, council or provider fails to act. The [national-local
settlement](../C04/01-national-local-authority.md) governs other sectors on
their own terms. The [early-care standard](20-early-care-relationships-and-staffing.md)
continues to govern this service.

**FC01 — Fix the financed floor and the ordinary local share in law.** For
publicly funded organized early care under NC, the Assembly adopts a
locality-by-locality schedule within its ordinary fiscal powers. The schedule
shows the standardized efficient cost of the common floor, the assessed local
contribution and the net national transfer. The cost includes eligible
unserved need, additional child support, usable hours and travel, qualified
staff, noncontact work, premises and recurring safe backup. Budget finite
transition separately. Determine the local contribution from existing
equalization, standardized revenue capacity and actual lawful responsibilities.
Deduct that contribution and other usable recurring receipts for the same
duty from approved gross cost. Record any lawful household payments net
of assistance separately. Count general grants already in the local
contribution once, and only count a restricted receipt where its terms fit.
The national transfer covers the positive remainder from recurring national
budget sources. The Assembly must identify net funding and incidence under
FB before selecting new permanent scope. Local enhancements remain locally
financed under LR. No foreign fee rate or new levy is selected.

**FC02 — Price reachable care and correct mistaken assumptions.** Do not
derive need solely from attendance, vacancies or waiting lists. Include
people discouraged by cost, distance, hours, disability or inaccessible
application, using proportionate protected evidence. Apply benchmark rates
for comparable competent work and ordinary efficient provision. Include
demonstrable unavoidable rural, accessibility and safeguarding costs.
Providers and localities can show exceptional unavoidable cost and affected
people can contest missed need under existing review. Each shared case,
worker, facility or transfer is allocated once at the appropriate scope.
No formula rewards discretionary local underspending, assumes unpaid care
or refunds every provider cost.

**FC03 — Advance the money before the first obligation it must meet.**
Treasury pays regular monthly advances to the named lawful local receiver
under the elected commitment and payment schedule. The first advance,
and any amount needed earlier for lawful payroll, provider payment or paid
transition, must arrive by the applicable due date. A Council publishes
the recipient, known pending costs and payment path for its own budget.
Use recorded authority and dated cash to distinguish an appropriation,
a transfer in flight and an accessible receipt. For a disputed allocation,
pay the authorized undisputed amount on time. Make in-period need corrections
through the statutory schedule and DB supplement when required. Reconcile
each quarter without clawing back a family's care or bypassing worker and
contract claims. A permanent cost increase needs an FB recurring-source
decision.

**FC04 — Show who bears a fee change and who provides extra care.** Keep
current lawful fees and stronger fee protections until competent prospective
change. A fee reduction identifies the actual bills reduced, support already
received, additional public net cost, and the lawful loss of provider
receipts to replace. Additional care separately requires accepted shifts,
space and support. Show which families gain, including families already
paying little or unable to take up care, and how national receipts or
reprioritization affect them. A transfer between public budgets is counted
once and cannot be offered as the country's net new revenue. Lawful prior
rights, S access and the ban on work or unpaid-family tests apply.

**FC05 — Give a family one entry and two accountable public duties.** The
local care receiver accepts the first lawful request, keeps the original
date and arranges an accessible, safe, accepted place with reasonable continuity.
Where a timely safe offer is at risk, the receiver must open the fallback
while it seeks local correction. A family or authorized advocate can also
reach the national care authority and independent OJ or court review
directly through existing routes. National and local authorities share the
minimum lawful record. A referral, reimbursement voucher without an
accepting provider, or pending dispute over which budget pays does not
discharge the offer. Use S's urgency, NC safeguards and existing review
clocks. Explain any unmet hours, next decision, responsible public entity
and remedy in usable form.

**FC06 — The national fallback brings resources and has a defined end.**
The national care authority must arrange qualified temporary provision
under existing C04 fallback powers when timely local receiving has failed
or is credibly at risk, whether the cause is national nonpayment, a
provider exit, local refusal or unavailable local staff. It uses prearranged
paid public or plural capacity, including travel or accessible local
delivery where necessary. Treasury funds that capacity and its activation
from authorized amounts separate from the ordinary advance schedule.
An accepting provider, safe handover and lawful worker coverage precede
any transfer of care. The intervention is limited to the failed service
and ends when a lawful funded local successor accepts the duties. Give
notice and a useful chance to cure unless urgent protection cannot wait.
Publish reasons, powers and an expiry date. A temporary order lasts at
most 30 calendar days. Continued exceptional authority requires fresh
independent court approval before expiry for at most 30 days at a time,
with actual source, necessity and a handback plan. Expiry ends the
exceptional authority while ordinary service and claim duties continue.
Local elections, unrelated budgets and local powers continue. Resolve
allocation, recovery and actual fault separately without charging families
for the failure.

**FC07 — Make residual failure visible and reviewable.** Where the fallback
also lacks staff or source, the national care authority keeps the service
claim open, identifies what can safely be delivered, and reports unmet
care and the responsible national and local offices. The responsible
minister brings an urgent funding or capacity choice to the Assembly under
DB and FB. Apply existing lawful scarcity rules and independent remedies.
No care duty ends because an allocation is exhausted. AA audits funds,
inspection checks actual care, and OJ/courts review their respective claims.
Ministers and providers cannot withhold authorized care money for political
loyalty. AI can help compare schedules or identify payment mismatches with
protected data and human correction. It cannot decide access, alter the
source formula, discharge claims or count as staff.

**FC08 — Carry the actual programme and worker obligations into any start.**
At prospective adoption name the lawful payer, local receiving body,
independent reviewers, funding law, accepted fallback, records and paid
OP/T successors. Preserve the NC19 notice and earlier due claims, S and
TW schedules, CL time, child relationships, worker pay and retirement
rights. The Assembly can revise scope or timing prospectively before a
new duty is due, through the competent procedure. Budget or appointment
changes cannot reset existing dates. Reopen on material mismatch between
actual costs and grants, exclusion, structural capacity loss or evidence
that a different model protects children and local control better.
