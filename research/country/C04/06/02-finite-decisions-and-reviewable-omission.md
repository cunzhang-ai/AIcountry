# AP05–AP08: the state cannot control when its own deadline begins

*Part of [reachable administration](../06-reachable-administration-and-finite-decisions.md); ordinary default, not a replacement for special law.*

**AP05 — Start with protected first receipt.** B16's ordinary decision duty
runs from AP02 first receipt, not a declaration of completeness. Within the
first 30 working days issue a lawful reasoned disposition or, before expiry,
one notified reasoned extension of no more than 30 working days. Identify
actual complexity/necessary inquiry, the work and final due date. Referral,
staff/vendor change, repeated information request, acknowledgment, queued AI
assessment and agency silence are neither decision nor suspension. A notice
sent after expiry does not erase the missed duty or create a fresh extension.
Use the applicable prepublished statutory working calendar, not an officer's
invented holidays; useful-time elapsed-hour/calendar-day duties remain distinct.
The ordinary due duty includes usable lawful notice, not merely an internally
dated decision. Substitute service needs actual statutory grounds and review;
nonresponse, an app delivery flag or a failed inaccessible channel proves none.

**AP06 — Decide on reasons, not a processing target.** A disposition states
authority, relevant findings and uncertainty, applicable proof standard, lawful
criterion, result, covered period, necessary continuing action and usable
review. Mere closure as incomplete or an unanswered proof request does not
dispose of the claim. Consider available evidence and admissible alternatives
with actual help; do not deny merely to meet the clock. Where a material
criterion genuinely remains unproved, an adverse finding must justify that
conclusion under substantive law after fair opportunity, rather than turn
missing paperwork into conclusive ineligibility. Correct error and receive
later material evidence through existing reconsideration/appeal routes without
erasing the original claim, covered period or lawful arrears.

Silence is not a grant of citizenship, money, a hazardous licence or third-party
property. Nor does an expired clock finally deny them. Existing authorizations,
earned status and due obligations keep their actual law; failed notice does not
undo an already effective source-law act. No new provisional
payment or safety permission arises solely from this ordinary procedure.

**AP07 — Make omission independently challengeable.** B13/B16 ordinary courts
can hear refusal to receive, unlawful proof demands, failure to give required
help/reasons and unlawful delay without an artificial adverse merits notice or
serial internal exhaustion. Earlier urgency remains directly reachable; do not
wait 30/60 days when useful protection would be lost. The notice-based B16
filing period cannot silently expire during an ongoing unnotified omission.
An agency cannot end review by relabelling the same omission as a new file.

The court may require lawful processing/disclosure, interim protection or an
actually established owed act within existing powers. It does not certify
unknown safety/eligibility, invent a new tax or choose general policy. Existing
B17–B19 notice, third-party safeguards, proportionate appeal and named lawful
payer/executor apply; an order or payment instruction is not received relief.

**AP08 — Do not buy speed by losing a hearing or essentials.** Preserve B16's
ordinary 20-working-day adverse reply and applicable narrower urgent source
exceptions. Plan disclosure/reply inside the decision period. Late new adverse
material cannot shorten fair reply or automatically restart the clock. If the
original due date is missed, record it and preserve review/protection while
completing fair process; an existing competent court can set a specific lawful
timetable, not an officer's perpetual extension. Record requester-chosen extra
reply time and genuine external impossibility separately from official refusal;
elapsed delay is not automatic personal misconduct or compensable fault.

E06/E07's 24-hour hardship/14-calendar-day initial-income routes, MB special
status dates, PD09's calendar-day controller request, liberty/clinical/electoral
limits and actual payment dates remain their own clocks. No additional wait,
reapplication, expiry of urgent protection or acceptance by silence follows.

An E07 initial determination is not automatically a final adverse disposition
when fair reply remains due. Required urgent/provisional findings, existing
protection and E13 review retain their exact law; an officer cannot count an
unresolved interim record as final closure or invent eligibility to meet time.
