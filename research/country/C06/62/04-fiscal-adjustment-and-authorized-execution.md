# FB21–FB25. Keep enacted duties payable during fiscal adjustment

*Part of [lasting services and fiscal shocks](../62-lasting-services-and-fiscal-shocks.md).
Design257 research selection.*

**FB21 — Classify the change before moving a duty.** On a material
reduction, common freeze or threatened deadline, Treasury and the
responsible duty owners identify the applicable source law and
operative obligations. Distinguish a lawful prospective programme
change, routine timing or case verification, a cash or physical
shortage, and a restriction on available authority or funds.
Several can coexist. Show separately who can commit, verify, release
and receive the relevant amount or service, and the earliest deadline
or expiry at risk. Publish the unchanged counterfactual schedule and
the proposed change. Evidence of slower spending triggers examination.
It does not by itself prove withholding, capacity, fault or entitlement.
A finance forecast, system rule or missed clock does not amend an Act.

**FB22 — Protect the work required to exercise the right.** Include in
each matched budget the qualified staffing, payable shared inputs,
local accepting sites, independent review and alternate authority
needed for every protected duty. Record how a hiring, travel,
procurement, systems or payment restriction can reach an exempt line
through a dependency. Include costs and time shifted to households,
workers, future entrants and local governments. Existing statutory
dues and minimum effective democratic and legal functions stay under
their governing law. Identify narrower controls or affordable ordinary
routes before cancelling useful capacity. A category exemption or
aggregate cash balance does not demonstrate payment or protection.
A genuine lack of resources stays visible with the particular claims
and lawful urgency rules.

**FB23 — Put any material national adjustment to a real fiscal choice.**
Compare the existing lawful path, targeted reprioritization, actual
source changes and a viable limited response using the same dates and
protected duties. Show whether a receipt can lawfully arrive earlier,
whether a finite advance can be repaid, and which later service or
taxpayer bears the effect. Distinguish delaying a genuinely unbound
option from changing an enacted obligation. Prefer pausing an unbound
start and using a verified lawful timing repair over a uniform release
percentage where the two protect more of the same due scope. A
permanent shortfall needs a separate recurring decision. An automatic
aggregate guard may trigger disclosure and a vote, or apply to an
expressly limited class of safely deferrable commitments under prior
law. It cannot reduce protected duties by default. Present exact
competing amendments under DB and BCV. Standing sources remain bounded
and ordinary due claims, interim support and dissent continue.

**FB24 — Make restrictive execution reviewable before harm or expiry.**
An official applying a material common restriction records its legal
basis, scope, amount, dates, owner, exceptions and release test.
Give a protected usable notice to affected duty owners and access to
members, independent reviewers and those affected. Do so before an
intended restriction. A necessary urgent safety or payment-security
step under existing authority may act first, with usable notice and
independent referral by the next working day or earlier where the
original duty or an applicable shorter clock requires. Record a
responsible human on first protected receipt of a missed or threatened
duty. Keep authorized, verified unaffected work and the undisputed
portion available. A minister, fiscal unit or vendor cannot repeat
the same hold under a new name or change the due date by hiding a
release rule. Any legally authorized restraint stays specific, reviewable
and time-bounded. Reasonable verification and an approved seasonal
schedule remain available within their actual law.

**FB25 — Restore actual execution and report real gaps.** If authority,
owned cash and qualified receiving are available but blocked, an
already authorized alternate payer or executor acts within that same
scope. Independent courts may order timely records and execution
within their jurisdiction. This includes direct access outside an
implicated controller and before the useful date or expenditure
expiry. If authority, cash or physical capacity is missing, name
the exact gap, use existing lawful interim protection and bring
lawful fiscal alternatives to the elected body. Preserve binding
claims and original clocks. After expiry, any settlement uses
standing or expressly voted authority under DB and BLC. Publish
restored amounts and actual service, delayed or displaced delivery,
and remaining financial and physical shortfalls. AI may reconcile
the dated plan and flag common restrictions or suspect coding.
A named official checks the facts, and an independent reviewer can
challenge them. AI neither classifies an individual entitlement nor
releases, withholds or appropriates funds. Reopen on hidden nonreceipt,
lost independence, changed cash, new obligations or a better rival.
