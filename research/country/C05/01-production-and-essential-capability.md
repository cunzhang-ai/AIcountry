# C05. Production, ownership, work and the gains from automation: Production, ownership and essential capability

*Part of [C05. Production, ownership, work and the gains from automation](../C05-economy-and-work.md); integrated design 66 research, not enacted law.*

The selected economic model is a plural mixed economy: competitive private enterprise,
cooperatives, public provision and commons where their purposes fit. The state protects
entry, contract, consumer rights and collective bargaining while constraining monopoly,
fraud and the conversion of concentrated wealth into political control. Bankruptcy
provides a lawful route out of unpayable debt; poverty is not a crime.

The selected [production arrangement](../../2026-10-material-and-resilience/production-work.md)
gives firms and cooperatives ordinary investment decisions and commercial risk within
labor, consumer, competition and ecological law. The Assembly separately funds a
bounded public-capability portfolio for essential networks, service capacity,
training, maintenance and supplier diversity. Cabinet proposes costed alternatives;
responsible agencies and public enterprises execute explicit mandates. Each public
enterprise reports its commercial account separately from funded noncommercial
duties, with professional management, worker voice, public audit and independent
challenge. Its board receives an open, competency-based appointment route,
published ownership mandate and reviewable removal; the appointing minister
cannot use informal instructions to turn its assets into party finance.
Neither an optimistic return forecast nor a lender's preference is an
appropriation. Concessional credit, equity or guarantees need a prior legislative
purpose, cap, loss account and review under C06; the bank-resolution fund cannot
finance industrial policy. More direct public production and more market-led
provision remain genuine alternatives, conditional on actual capability and power.

Ordinary credit is a private or cooperative risk decision under general law,
not a substitute for a social entitlement or a ministerial allocation list.
The [credit-and-finance decision](../../2026-10-credit-and-finance/decision-record.md)
selects regulated plural intermediation for the working design, with public
investment confined to separately appropriated mandates. It compares that
choice with lighter regulation, directed public credit and narrow public
payments. No amount of lending alone establishes productive investment or
better lives; asset-price inflation and household debt can disguise a loss
of real access.

Plural ownership matters only if people can actually refuse domination. Competition
and sector authorities should examine common beneficial control across essential
employment, land, credit, payments, public contracts and communications, including
contractual dependencies that a simple market-share measure misses. Lawful responses
can include open entry, collective bargaining, tenant security, nondiscriminatory
access, interoperable infrastructure, procurement separation, public options and,
where justified, structural remedies. Each response needs published reasons,
independent challenge, compensation where law requires it, a transition plan and
evidence that an alternative can operate. The state cannot call an incumbent public
monopoly a solution if the governing party then controls its jobs, housing or data.
The [equal-standing rule](../C02/05-equal-standing-and-differentiated-needs.md)
and [C08 remedy](../C08/11-equality-law-and-effective-remedies.md) distinguish
unjustified exclusion from a genuine role requirement or other lawful
economic distinction; a shared employer and housing gate needs cross-sector
evidence without one regulator deciding every claim.

Workers and residents need enforceable protection for organizing, whistleblowing
and lawful political activity, including routes to interim relief when dismissal,
eviction or exclusion would make later vindication useless. Employers and owners
retain fair process and defenses for genuinely unrelated decisions. Public services,
portable social protection and secure tenure reduce the cost of losing a job or
political patron; they are part of democratic independence as well as welfare.

Essential networks require public ownership or enforceable public-interest regulation,
transparent access and continuity obligations. This design selects the guarantee and
accountability, not one ownership form for every industry. Public enterprises need
accounts, professional management and democratic oversight; public ownership alone
does not prevent patronage or inefficiency.

An essential-capability strategy links critical services to trained workers, maintained
facilities, supplier diversity and actual domestic or importable inputs. It compares
production and replacement capacity with fiscal transfers: paying more cannot supply
an unavailable medicine, care worker or power component. The strategy protects
workers and ecosystems during change; it does not presume national self-sufficiency
or place all production under one public planner.
The [C09 food-system route](../C09/09-food-systems-and-nutrition.md)
applies this plural production and real-exit settlement to farms, fisheries,
processors, traders and retailers while keeping tenure, labor and ecological
claims visible.
The [market-power rule](05-market-power-and-real-exit.md) assigns cross-sector
investigation, defendant process and workable remedies. A plural ownership
label does not prove that workers or customers can actually switch.
The [automation settlement](04-automation-income-and-power.md) tests whether
changes in paid work, public revenue and concentrated AI ownership can be
handled as one economic shift without presuming either joblessness or abundance.

The [joined economic package](../C06/20-national-economic-package.md) now
traces wage/output, tax, equity and depreciation counterparts before using
owned returns to fund protection. Paid acquisition and foregone private
funds must be shown; public ownership and larger GDP do not by themselves
supply cash, actual capability or effective independent control.

[N01–N12](07-production-learning-and-capacity.md) makes the productive
path explicit: elected bounded public investment alongside ordinary plural
enterprise, paid learners/instructors, real net technological gains and
accepted receiving capacity. A one-time post or degree is not a maintained
capability. Its joined reference adds preparation/return obligations and
attributes replacement; it does not establish higher national productivity.

[PS01–PS20](../C06/37-physical-supply-and-paid-trade.md) now selects resource-fit
plural domestic production and diversified paid imports, with matched physical
inputs, paid seasonal work and common/public new essential-network assets.
It supplies no GDP residual, unallocated skilled workers or automatic AI exports.

[OE01–OE40](13-enterprise-and-association-constitutions.md) now supplies positive
enterprise/association forms and compatible public-owner governance: actual
capital/liability, worker board power, member freedom, direct remedies and exit.
Neither public/private/cooperative title nor AI assistance proves effective
control, sufficient production or consent. IC paid acquisition/source remains.
