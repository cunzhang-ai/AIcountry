# Design423. Fund a safe exit before the ordinary pension begins

## Decision and baseline

Design422 selects a common pension age and cohort factors, preserves
accrued earnings and acknowledges that some people cannot safely
continue their actual work to pension age. Y04 and Y19 call for a
separate funded route. They leave its qualification, payable amount
and handover open. This revision defines a reference bridge. It was
drafted with AI assistance. Only competent law, funds and an
authorized receiver can make it payable.

## Alternatives and choice

Prevention and actual paid accommodation can preserve both income and
agency. They should be available regardless of age. They do not supply
a safe option for every task or worker. An earlier standard pension
age for a class of occupations is simpler to administer if risk is
closely tied to clear exposure. Job labels can exclude people doing
equivalent work. The existing voluntary early-share route can reduce
the annual amount of the elected share under the common factor. A stronger general income floor
could protect people without proving work exposure. It requires a
broader recurring source and does not itself enforce workplace safety.

Select an independently assessed bridge tied to actual unsafe work or
functional restriction and covered earnings loss, alongside the
ordinary rights. It adds a route without requiring a reduced pension,
unsafe trial or compulsory transfer. The candidate 60 percent and
one-basket cap are comparison parameters. A disability, job title
or years of work alone are insufficient proof. The main objections
are a heavier burden of proof for people with missing records, a new
incentive for employers to shift cost to the state and lower effective
gains once I05 adjusts. Offer independent proof and ordinary workplace
enforcement. Publish both the gross budget and actual net receipt.
Compare the bridge against a stronger basic and safer work on the
same dates before adoption.

## Evidence, control and migration

[Primary comparators](sources.md) show a senior benefit assessed against
recent work and a separate exposure-points approach. They neither
validate the proposed threshold nor estimate the number of eligible
people or work that can safely be retained. The constructed comparison
uses the existing I05 taper under an explicit replacement-earnings
classification. No affected worker, service or independent reviewer
participated in this revision.

Adopt prospectively with explicit age, exposure, decision and finance
rules. The Assembly votes general revenue and any lawful tax and
benefit interaction. Employment and safety authorities enforce
workplace duties. The benefits authority decides and pays the bridge
with independent occupational evidence and ordinary outside appeal.
The pension payer remains responsible for the first pension. Price
staff and the paid review and correction path. Preserve old shares,
all accrued credits and established income, care and employment
claims. Use existing rights to protect a late handoff. No AI,
actuary or employer controls individual eligibility or the elected
budget.

## Scoped result and next test

Documentary evidence and exact case arithmetic qualify a research
choice. Enactment, claimant access, fiscal viability and better lives
remain open.

For one occupation and a comparison group performing similar tasks,
the responsible sector, income and pension offices would compare
paid prevention, ordinary protection and the bridge through actual
safe work and the first pension due date. Complete when an
independent reviewer can trace usable earnings, assessment access,
gross funding, effect on other claims and both payments, including
workers who could not prove their history. No exercise budget has
been committed. Reopen on systematic exclusion, employer cost
shifting, insufficient coverage or a stronger ordinary alternative.
