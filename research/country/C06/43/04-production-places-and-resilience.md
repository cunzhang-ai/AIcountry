proposal funds elections/opposition, careers, Treasury/revenue, courts/defense,

miss harvest peaks. Taxes do not repair these physical failures. Preserve PS's

paid seasonal cover, geography/scale and actual dietary adequacy.

research exposure specification is not an enacted engineering safety standard.
