# VD01–VD14: the closed regulatory duty schedule

*Application of V11; part of [C08/19](../19-regulation-and-bounded-enforcement.md).*

These are proposed precise duties, effective only through lawful prospective
adoption. A/B means V06's fault and monetary ceilings, never automatic custody.
Apply to a provider/controller's relevant actual activity, not everyone with
an account. All referenced standards must actually be lawfully in force.

| ID / competent owner | Scheduled act/omission | Available V class |
|---|---|---|
| VD01 Financial / prudential or conduct supervisor | Taking repayable public deposits, holding/transmitting client funds, or commercially offering credit/investment custody contrary to M01's applicable license; breaching M02/M03 required capital, liquid assets or exposure limits; deliberately misclassifying these claims | B; A only for a precise periodic prudential record/return required by law |
| VD02 Financial conduct / conduct supervisor | M04/M05 material total-price/risk deception; unauthorized use/commingling of client property; denying an established basic-payment right on political/AI-account grounds; deliberate failure of M06's transaction correction duty | B; A only for required transaction/claim records |
| VD03 Work / labor inspector | Failure to pay actually due agreed/statutory wages, comply with enacted hours/rest/collective-right duties, or prevent a specific serious workplace hazard within an expressly enacted safety duty and actual control | B; A only for required wage/hour/safety records |
| VD04 Products and food / competent safety inspector | Supplying a product/food known or recklessly disregarded to present a specific serious health/safety risk; material deception about a safety characteristic; failure of an actually served lawful warning/recall or traceable-batch duty | B; A only for enacted batch/recall records; not every nutritional preference |
| VD05 Land, water and environment / environmental or utility authority | Exceeding an actually enacted permit/emission/extraction limit or operating without a license expressly required for the activity; material breach of a specific funded/secured restoration duty | B; A only for legally required measurement/permit records |

| VD07 Essential networks / utility, communications or transport authority | Unauthorized disconnection of a person's established essential-access right; material breach of an enacted network safety/continuity duty within actual control; material deception about availability | B; A only for required outage, safety and due-delivery records; no general duty to prevent every disruption |
| VD08 Data and delegated AI / competent information-rights supervisor | Material processing/disclosure outside an enacted lawful basis or scoped delegation, or deliberate failure of an established access/correction right; a model's changed output alone is insufficient | B; A only for enacted access/security decision records; speech content is not licensed here |
| VD09 Professional care / competent clinical or professional authority | Performing a specifically licensed high-risk professional act without its required authorization, or materially violating an enacted consent/competence/safety condition within actual control | B; A only for legally required clinical/authorization records; informal chosen support is not licensed by this row |

ER19 expressly adds a VD12-only body maximum alternative of twice proved unlawful campaign value, one traceable course once, not an interpretation already contained in Q08. |

[MR05–MR06](../../C05/11/07-remedies-penalties-and-redress.md) gives the exact
closed acts, V/P/Q fault/attribution/maxima/periods and no custody. MP20 fixes
prospective commencement. A breached below-gate court order uses only its own
independently enacted enforcement route, never VD11 by analogy.

Public agencies meet the same applicable duties. Necessary urgent rescue,
protected disclosure, lawful silence, technical impossibility and other P
justifications/ability rules require actual adjudication. A missing permit
threshold, unresolved science or an inspector's preferred practice creates
no offense. K crimes, taxation, all election matters outside the exact prospective VD12,
public-health force and emergency restrictions retain their distinct law;
V does not supply missing substantive elements for them.

[PD01–PD32](../../C02/07-private-life-and-personal-data.md) supplies ordinary
data bases/rights and supervisor control. It does not automatically add every
new duty to VD08: civil correction and precise enacted A/B predicates remain
distinct, with actual fault, ability and independent court punishment.

No punishment for unperformed technical calibration or a general late claim/report. |

[WL32](../../C05/12-work-contracts-and-collective-power.md) now applies VD03 to
actually enacted precise labor duties only. Civil contract/representation
correction is distinct; technical form errors, good-faith disputed status or
union dissent alone are no offense. No new VD14/class/gain base or automatic
penalty follows from WL; ordinary V/P proof/ability/notice and independent courts remain.

| VD14 Enterprise / Enterprise Registrar investigates; independent ordinary civilian court punishes | Only [OE29](../../C05/13/06-registry-courts-and-enforcement.md): deliberate material falsification of required entity identity, decision authority, ultimate control or actually paid-capital certification to mislead the register/relying parties; or director knowing/reckless authorization of an OE10 distribution despite proved mandatory asset/liquidity or overdue-dues protection failure, with actual duty/control/ability, materiality and full V/P proof | B only under unchanged V06/Q maxima, V07/P defenses and V08 one-course protection; no classA, custody, new gain base or pre-adoption act. Ordinary late filing, genuine valuation/judgment dispute, loss, missing annex, belief or good-faith error alone creates no offense. |

Design118 expressly adds this closed OE-only predicate through competent
prospective primary law. Design117 WL's no-new-VD14 choice remains labor-scoped;
neither edition's navigation itself creates a legal duty or punishment.

[AA audit](../../C04/04-public-audit-and-accountable-oversight.md) adds no VD15,
class/gain base or offense for late records, a missing annex or audit disagreement.
Any actually independent K/P or order-enforcement act needs all its own enacted
elements, source/fault/ability/defenses and courts. Findings are not punishment.
