# C11. Knowledge, culture and AI as public capability: Public knowledge, records and culture

*Part of [C11. Knowledge, culture and AI as public capability](../C11-knowledge-culture-and-ai.md); integrated design 94 research, not enacted law.*

[KR01–KR32](07-knowledge-rights-and-innovation.md) now supplies ordinary IP,
public research/creator contracts and essential-use/paid-receiving rules.
EV authenticates sources; it neither grants IP nor awards research money.

Protect independent science, arts, journalism, open criticism and culturally diverse
public spaces. Public research funding uses transparent purposes and contestable
selection without requiring ideological agreement. Schools and media must enable people
to question the state, including the country's founding design.

Public knowledge must be available across the languages people actually use
for political choice and legal protection, with qualified interpretation and
original-language records where their meaning is disputed. Cultural and
educational support may sustain dispersed communities as well as places with
autonomy; funding cannot be traded for official agreement with a territorial
settlement. AI translation can widen access but does not settle an ambiguous
ballot, treaty, land account or person's identity. Affected speakers and
independent reviewers need a correction route before a mistranslation becomes
binding or an excluded community loses its chance to object.

The selected [public-evidence arrangement](../../2026-10-public-evidence/analysis.md)
separates four acts: recording an event, constructing an official statistic,
interpreting causes, and making a binding legal decision. Election, revenue,
service, court, utility and ecological authorities keep the original records
needed for their own duties, with named custodians, lawful retention, visible
correction histories and successors. A separate statutory public evidence
service makes cross-sector statistics comparable, preserves authenticated
public law, budget, method and statistical vintages, and coordinates plural
evaluation. It receives only purpose-limited lawful access, not a central
personal dossier. Sector authorities remain responsible for the underlying
service and decisions; the evidence service cannot change eligibility, law
or budget allocations. Independent universities, professional bodies,
communities and media may investigate and disagree without its license.
The [C07 population-health route](../C07/08-population-health-and-prevention.md)
uses original clinical, local and environmental observations under specified
purpose and privacy law; C11 preserves methods and corrections without
becoming a compulsory health registry or a restriction authority.
For [mental-health and substance-use care](../C07/11-mental-health-substance-use-and-crisis-care.md),
the clinical custodian protects original records and correction history;
public analysis can examine exclusion and coercion without making a
searchable list of distressed people for employers, police or campaigns.

The Assembly enacts record and publication duties, access and privacy limits,
remedies and bounded operating funds. Evidence-service leaders need public
merit criteria and open hearings. [EV01–EV16](06-public-evidence-service.md) now
selects one six-year nonrenewable chief, three-fifths Assembly appointment,
external for-cause removal and bounded career continuity, replacing this
reference's earlier unspecified staggered leadership. These rules
do not themselves create independence. Review the shared controllers of
nomination, source staff, pay, servers, archive keys, courts and execution.
An opposition and affected-party route to inspect unjustified withholding,
with protected access for sensitive material, must be usable before a later
report loses its value. A separate operator or lawful protected copy must
make preservation and access orders executable through vendor failure.

Official methods and release dates are published in advance; material changes
state their reasons, uncertain coverage, missing groups and relationship to
earlier series. Corrections append provenance rather than silently replacing
an unfavorable result. People can challenge errors in their own consequential
records with interim protection; researchers and communities can contest
methods or suppression. Safe testimony and personal records are not made
permanently public merely to preserve an audit trail. National comparability
must leave room for local meanings and observations that a common category
does not represent. An AI summary records shared source families and its
interpretive role; many models repeating one altered source do not corroborate
it. A statistician, archivist or evaluator cannot become the final judge of
political truth or legally bind the public through a changed metric.

The public record of law includes each exact enabling statute, authorized
general rule, prior version, effective and expiry date, legislative scrutiny
and court correction. Its custodian authenticates publication and preserves
the source; it cannot decide the rule's legality or policy merit. Where a
model or external standard supplies operative general criteria, the legally
effective version and human-readable consequences are registered and
challengeable. Updating software, a statistical definition or an outside
standard alone does not amend a public duty.

Service-usability evidence follows a complete lawful journey, including failed
entry, repeated testimony, time and travel, handoff delay, abandoned claims,
delivered protection and remedies. People who never reached an office or declined
an agent cannot vanish from the assessment. Source custodians and independent
inquiry may compare these patterns with staffing and shared-controller records
under privacy protection; a single score or an agency's self-reported closure
does not establish that a right was usable.

The [public-conditions account](../C14/04-public-conditions.md) uses those
records to compare actual lives across terms and justified external contexts.
The evidence service publishes definitions, missing groups, uncertainty,
source families and old-to-new method bridges; plural evaluators separately
dispute causes and alternative policies. Its mandate includes the cost of
field observation and safe accounts from people who never entered a service.
Neither a national average nor a favorable international rank certifies that
the country improved every life or fulfilled a legal duty.

A plural information environment needs independent private, public and community
media, with visible control and material funding relationships for influential
outlets. Public media funding and appointments must resist incumbent retaliation;
their editorial choices must remain independent. Media concentration review and
fair access to dominant distribution channels protect opportunities to reach others,
not a state power to certify truth or demand ideological balance from each speaker.
The [C03 political-campaign route](../C03/07-political-finance-and-campaign-access.md)
requires timely paid-sponsor and distribution records while protecting
public-media editorial independence, small supporters and unpaid speech.
Narrow rules against threats, fraud or unlawful coercion require reasons and timely
independent review. People retain private conversation and anonymous political
expression where consistent with others' rights; transparency duties focus on
institutions and significant organized influence rather than universal identity logs.

**Design146 positive media choice.** [PM01–PM24](08-public-media-and-editorial-independence.md)
now specifies the public-media remit, six-seat worker/public governance, appointment/
external removal, independent editors, actual standing source, plural distribution,
correction and paid change. The earlier independence promise above remains its
purpose, not a separate unspecified institution. EV authenticates statistics/records;
it cannot choose the newsroom, factual interpretations or private-media permission.

[OA01–OA07](../C04/03/04-public-records-and-useful-time-access.md)
provides general request standing, source retrieval, protected
partial release and independent court execution. Evidence authenticity,
privacy, special oversight and public interpretation remain separate.
