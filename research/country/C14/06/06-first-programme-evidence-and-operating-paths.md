# First-programme evidence and operating paths

*Companion to the [matched first programme](05-integrated-first-government-programme.md).*

**Observed-context disposition.** The [Copenhagen check](../../C09/25/04-affordable-receiving-and-refund-exposure.md)
does not qualify three actual leases. Continue due protection and choose
bounded, independently justified preparation until net suitable homes,
affordability through known funding changes and gross local cash are
verified. Bring any viable smaller offer for its exact lawful decision.
The earlier numerical comparison retains its assumed inputs.

The [care placement check](../../C06/58/22-care-training-and-mentor-cover.md) similarly
conditions actual care preparation on dated host commitments and funded
qualified cover at both sites. Compare ordinary retention and an exact
staged intake. A training vote creates no current staffed capacity.

The [Copenhagen energy check](../../C06/61/06-energy-claims-and-useful-time.md)
conditions the actual energy bridge on usable local cash, a separate
bill and receiving path and accepted second-call supply. Prefer the
ordinary standing route if it qualifies. Where it does not, price a
capped advance instead of relying on retrospective recovery. The
constructed joint programme retains its original budget assumptions.

[DP43–DP47](../../C11/09/10-public-intake-without-a-digital-gate.md) retains
the hold on the accelerated intake trial. A separately authorized and
funded ordinary navigation improvement may start if it qualifies.
Evaluate optional automation only alongside a staffed independent route.

**Use a common release register.** The [joint comparison](../../../settlement/integrated-first-programme/04-first-release-register.md)
identifies exact authority, dated gross sources, accepted scope,
independent cover, and the effect of missing evidence for each option.
Price any paid reservation and binding closeout once. Use the existing
elected release conditions under [JC](../../C06/65/03-release-and-bound-obligations.md).
The responsible officer distinguishes still-optional choices from already
binding rights, and records actual acceptance and useful receiving.
The current comparator qualifies bounded preparation and exact
conditional offers. Real release evidence for the four starts remains open.

**Test the household's actual path across first programmes.** Apply
[TH13–TH17](../../C09/23/04-household-protection-and-usable-receiving.md)
where temporary protection requires care and transport at the same
address and within the same period. Price the concurrent receiving,
independent alternative and funded continuation on their own sources.
Care training creates no immediate qualified shift. A secured tenancy
or accepted supply does not itself establish this household's safe
receiving. Use the [constructed household comparison](../../../settlement/household-protection/README.md)
as a separate service-path test. It supplies no additional release
or spending authority.

**Price the existing care floor through loss of unpaid support.**
Apply [WF100–WF104](../../C06/58/23-voluntary-care-and-national-capacity.md)
to the continuing baseline. Compare actual local roster, planned
respite and usable paid standby on the same need. Carry concurrent
calls, worker cover and the funded independent remedy on their real
dates. A new training place can help future recruitment and supplies
no present coverage. An assumed saving from unpaid support does not
fund any optional first-programme start.

**Price critical transport through competing calls.** Apply
[RC29–RC33](../../C10/13/07-accessible-evacuation-and-protected-transport.md)
to simultaneous rescue and existing protected access. Use actual vehicles,
qualified crews, usable routes, source terms and destination acceptance.
The same driver, lift or road cannot qualify two independent routes.
Budget paid turnaround and preserve the original access and review claim
if the reserve does not cover the second call.

**Test material digital purchases on the combined work calendar.**
Under [DP48–DP52](../../C11/09/11-joint-ai-purchasing-and-public-fallback.md),
compare the whole offer, including non-AI service, appeal and recovery.
Keep different work and protected reservations explicit. The
[worked comparison](../../../settlement/joint-ai-purchasing/README.md)
chooses ordinary repair until a narrower assisted option proves a useful
gain on the same costs and qualified cover. It supplies no funding or
authority to start the held public-intake trial.

**Reconcile opening liabilities before binding the optional programme.**
Apply [OL01–OL05](../02/05-opening-liabilities-and-protected-budget.md)
to the same known dues and declared earlier calls. Show actual payer,
currency, funded contingency and missing source. Price a separately
usable narrower choice where the larger start fails. Continue valid
prior duties and preserve any late-discovered claim and review.

**Fund edible receiving through a repeat interruption.**
Apply [FM01–FM05](../../C09/09/01-usable-food-and-local-meal-continuity.md)
to continuing food protection. Compare market access, prepared meals
and independently accepted delivery through the next window. The same
water, kitchen shift or vehicle cannot cover two incompatible duties.
Qualify the early cash and the cumulative funded replenishment separately.
