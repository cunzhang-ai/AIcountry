# FR09–FR13. Move safely without breaking the relationships people choose

*Design261 · Proposed recovery, local decision and funding duties.*

**FR09 — Offer collective recovery as a real option.** When a safe
return may be impossible and residents ask to preserve common places
or practical networks, the recovery authority funds proportionate
development of a group option alongside safe adaptation and direct
individual assistance. Tenants, actual occupants, residents who are
temporarily absent and people without a property claim can initiate
and shape the housing and service proposal. Keep existing customary,
collective, religious and property authority distinct. A trusted
community institution may receive an expressly scoped negotiating
mandate from those it represents. A neighborhood tally, an owner's
claim and a public hearing do not establish that mandate or determine
a collective legal interest. Preserve the option of keeping a
shared facility while residents choose different safe homes.

**FR10 — Use distinct decisions for a common place and for each move.**
Record consent and authority for each common land or facility decision
under its applicable law. The originating elected body chooses its
public recovery contribution. The receiving elected body or competent
service authority approves only the siting, staffing, infrastructure
and finance within its own jurisdiction. A host decision cannot
withdraw direct essential protection or ordinary access owed under
[XR](../../C04/09-cross-local-service-continuity.md). A decision by a
collective rights-holder applies within its legal scope. Each affected
person retains independent advice, an accessible offer, confidential
choice about housing and services, and their own tenant, title,
benefit or legal claim. Membership, assistance and a safe individual
route cannot depend on supporting the common plan. Apply existing
protection where abuse, dependency or disagreement within a household
affects real choice. Compulsion, land acquisition and applicable
collective consent remain under their separate law.

**FR11 — Price and accept the host capacity before relying on it.**
The recovery budget records each additional dwelling with secure
tenure, actual utility and transport capacity, lawful school and care
receiving and communal facility maintenance. Keep separate the
ordinary host baseline, existing unmet needs, project-caused capital
and recurring costs and contributions from each authorized payer.
A receiving authority certifies the actual increment and its
available date and shows which other people would otherwise lose
access. Count the increment only once and maintain ordinary
allocation rules. Where a host cannot accept the scope, seek a
smaller phase, another feasible site or continued protected
alternatives. The national guarantor and original duty holders keep
their lawful funding and continuity responsibilities during a dispute.
Neither a pending transfer nor an owner's payment finances an
unrelated host obligation.

**FR12 — Stage commitments as choices and facts change.** The elected
recovery plan publishes the minimum financially and physically workable
phase, protected start dates, acceptance tests, reversible preparation
budget and who bears the risk if participation, site readiness or
funding changes. Use actual commitments from competent parties for a
release, with personally identifiable choices kept private. An
expression of interest supports preparation. Release optional
irreversible project work only after verifying the lawful phase, usable
finance, independent host acceptance and a continuing safe alternative.
If departures or a late site make the minimum phase unviable, stop
new optional commitments, keep valid prior claims and review the
smallest feasible alternative. Provide an independently usable
individual route for people who withdraw or decline and preserve
existing legal rights and notices. Do not reclaim essential aid or
pass failed project costs to residents because they exercised a choice.
Honor valid, informed and independently reviewed contractual
commitments on their own terms.

**FR13 — Verify people, common places and the old site separately.**
Track safe occupancy, tenure, usable work and service journeys,
access to the common place and ongoing operation, including access
for residents who choose to live elsewhere. Preserve older cultural
or ecological access wherever lawful and safe. Assign a funded steward
for both sites, with maintenance, safety and record handover at
ordinary succession. Keep the original receiving and remedy dates,
support continuity and a confidential route to correct coerced consent,
unequal offers or unusable host services. The elected budget reviews
actual acceptance and residual need. AI may reconcile dates, cost
shares and capacity or flag duplication using scoped records.
People and institutions within their respective mandates determine
consent, siting, eligibility and completion.
