# Convert technology gains into paid usable time and safe service

*Design328 · AT18–AT22 · Proposed work and service transition rules.*

**AT18 — Compare delivered work before assigning the claimed gain.** Before a
material reliance change affecting workload, pay, scheduled hours or an
essential service, the employer gives affected workers and their chosen
representatives a usable baseline and proposal. Record actual tasks, hours,
quality, waiting, failures, review, training, tool costs and downstream work.
Include regular dependent contractors and work moved to residents or unpaid
carers. Separate temporary transition and steady-state work. A task-hour may
be released only in its actual period and skill. Cash, productive capacity,
paid free time and received service have different accounts. No mandatory
private-life tracking or vendor estimate can establish the gain.

**AT19 — Give workers a paid bargaining opportunity before converting the gain.**
The employer must give affected workers meaningful paid time, original
relevant evidence, protected assistance and a reasoned response to a
worker-proposed allocation if offered, before implementing a material change in
quotas, ordinary hours, pay or staffing based on the gain. Compare shorter
paid duty at retained regular pay, safe redeployment or useful training,
and alternative pay, output or staffing choices. Include workers whose
terms or bargaining unit differ and hear service users where access may
change. Existing lawful agreements, consent, redundancy, bargaining and
nonwaivable floors govern the decision. An unresolved dispute gives no
unilateral permission to impose a change otherwise barred by law. The
ordinary lawful route and due work continue while it is heard. A paid
reversible test and urgent safety action retain their own narrow authority.

**AT20 — Prove qualified cover in the same useful window.** Before committing
a paid release or reassigning work in an essential service, name the
exact persons or qualified roles, hours, skills, locations, consent,
lawful pay, date and actual accepting service. Keep independent fallback
and the next demand window. An administrative hour does not replace a
night-care shift merely because both are paid. Verify actual wages,
received services and unconstrained time. A released hour cannot also
fund training, retained output or a reserve. If the workload or source
does not qualify, preserve due worker and resident rights and bring the
uncovered work to the competent service owner and budget authority.

**AT21 — Test a durable allocation and restore service after a shortfall.**
A material new ongoing arrangement compares a paid and reversible first
step, at least two full representative roster cycles, and a credible
failure or loss of the tool. Select dates and seasonal tests proportionate
to actual risk. The service owner verifies useful access and qualifications,
and workers can challenge hidden labor, lost pay, pressure or changed
terms through independent labor review. A lawful agreement specifies who
bears transition and fallback costs, earned pay, original claims and
the conditions for lawful retiming. No later loss claws back time or
pay already earned. No trial or technical review can postpone a statutory
stage already in force. Preserve sector and worker remedies independently.

**AT22 — Keep the allocation a democratic and accountable decision.**
Use independent sector and labor sources for material quality, cover,
earnings and released time. Elect budget, public service and source
changes under existing powers. Employer and workers decide terms through
their lawful routes. An AI can reconcile dates and estimate scenarios
from lawful evidence, without approving its own use, representing a
worker without mandate or certifying service. Preserve affected workers'
terms, evidence, private time and paid successors on change of tool or
operator. Reopen on uneven gains, intensified or unpaid work, qualified
cover loss or a better supported allocation.
