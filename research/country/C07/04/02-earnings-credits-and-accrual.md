# Y05–Y08: ongoing earned rights and financed interruption credits

*Part of [C07 retirement candidate](../04-retirement-security.md); design135 clarification, retaining design105 source-law change; not enacted.*

**Y05 — Contributions and benefit bases differ.** The reference worker levy
is 10% of actual personal labor earnings, including self-employment/platform
work, without an earnings ceiling. An employer remits withheld money and
is accountable for default; proof of covered work preserves a worker's
accrual and avoids collecting the same withheld sum twice. Self-employed
cash reconciliation and good-faith error use I08's protected correction.
No new employer levy is hidden outside the wage account. Contribution
incidence, price/wage responses and informality require separate evidence.
Capital returns retain their separate tax treatment. Work, capital and
expense disputes need reasoned evidence and court challenge, not a final
AI classification or surveillance of every private transaction.

For new service from the express commencement, cap labor earnings at the
independently estimated full-time-equivalent median annual labor earnings `W`
for that service year, replacing the former two-W candidate prospectively.
[RF06–RF07](../../C06/36/02-pension-units-cash-and-generations.md) selects the
first1January at least24months after enactment as the candidate notice rule;
the Act states its actual date and split periods. Preserve every earlier
earned unit and its original indexation/survivor contingency, never recap
past bases. Best48 replacement cannot reduce accrued value. The published statistical method and protected originals are
contestable. Contributions above that base are an explicit solidarity
transfer, not additional pension units or personal savings. This redistributive
choice may be challenged; model balance does not decide its fairness.

**Y06 — Care and interruptions count without double accrual.** Qualified
child/dependant care, illness, disability and involuntary unemployment before
65 receive a credit target of `0.5 × W × eligible fraction of the year`.
The credited addition is the positive difference between that target and
already covered earnings for the same period, within Y05's annual ceiling.
Paid leave already recorded as earnings is not credited twice. Allocate
each person's overlapping periods once, with accessible evidence and appeal;
distinct necessary carers may qualify for their own actual time. Unemployment
credits respect safe suitable work, accommodation, care limits and protected
refusals; basic/needs protection has no ideological work test. There is no marriage condition
or lifetime care-credit cliff. Eligibility recognizes actual needed support,
not an order that a relative perform unpaid care. General revenue transfers
10% of the additional credited base to insurance; this payment is not proof
that the eventual liability is fully financed. People keep independent
care, income and disability claims while credit evidence is disputed.

[IC18–IC19](../../C05/09/06-income-security-and-credit-continuity.md) makes
credit qualification independent of receiving/renewing I05 or U cash. Separate
assisted/offline qualification remains available; U alone grants no credit.
Use proved same-period covered earnings even when the wage/remittance is unpaid;
protect those outstanding claims rather than double-credit the missing cash.
Required G-to-S funding, actual S receipt and future liability remain distinct.

**Y07 — First-year accrual, bounded career base.** Each complete service
year adds an annual lifetime pension of 1.25% of its credited base, revalued
under Y03. Prorate short periods. Use the best 48 revalued annual bases;
further higher bases replace lower ones, never reduce accrued entitlement.
There is no minimum qualifying-years cliff and no personal investment-return
promise. The ceiling bounds accrual within Y’s same48-year scheme, not separate
inherited, foreign or other-source vested claims. Those retain their own source
rules and are never retroactively put through this ceiling or best48 limit.
The ledger must distinguish earned annual pension, current due payment,
survivor contingency and actuarial liability; none is the person's cash asset.

**Y08 — Portability and replacement.** The authority issues understandable
annual service/credit statements with source, corrections and continuing
obligations. Multiple employers, agents or accounts cannot duplicate one
person's period. An employer, provider or territorial change maps records,
assets, claims, pending reviews and payers to lawful successors. Foreign
claims require actual coordination; no unratified agreement creates cash.
Protected records and ordinary assistance survive vendor or AI replacement.

[TW medical/learning law](../../C05/10/03-leave-learning-and-income.md) preserves
actual covered salary and Y06 additional illness/care credit without duplication.
New G-funded replacement is not itself worked wages for the10% levy. Statutory
normal hours and median-hourly minimum pay do not revalue old annual units or
reset the source-year W; prospective tax/credit matching needs explicit law.

[PA work/pay](../../C06/38-work-pay-prices-and-country-income.md) now traces
wage/contribution/new-unit changes and qualifies credit by actual eligible
periods, same-period earnings and annual ceiling. Low pay alone is no Y06 credit.
Tax scale M may diverge from independently observed W; it never revalues Y bases,
prior units or ordinary-basic matching. New tax/cost treatment needs express law.

[NF29–NF36](../../C06/42-recurring-tax-and-noncash-work.md) now gives delivered
private noncash work the same10% labor base, employer-funded g/c, individual
service-period allocation and distinct actual S receipt. Genuine independent
net-labor in-kind fees use actual costs/capital evidence, not client payroll.
No promise alone creates V/g/c; proved work/source rights survive nondelivery.
The1W ceiling applies only from RF06's later lawful date; earlier actual source
ceilings/units remain. Above that applicable ceiling is solidarity, no new unit.
These express future-law selections do not change current payroll by analogy.

**Design147 new absence category.** [HP21](../../C10/11/04-paid-compliance-claims-and-transition.md)
adds verified protective absence as a prospective explicit Y06 target of actual
ordinary Y05-covered earnings for missed hours, capped by one-W-equivalent normal
hours/year. Preserve any stronger applicable existing target; add only the greater
target's positive shortfall after actual covered earnings/other credit for those
hours. No double care/illness/HP/work unit or recap of old rights. G owes10% backing
of the additional credited base to S; default preserves claim/accrual, creates no
new national source. HP replacement cash is not itself labor/Y levy or work.

[EB11](../../C05/16/03-overlaps-tax-credits-and-correction.md) makes bridge
cash neither worked earnings/10%Y levy nor automatic Y06 credit. Actual
qualified unemployment/care/illness, protected refusals, unpaid covered wages,
period matching and G-to-S source remain independently assessed. EB's voluntary
transition/no-extra-search choice changes none of those qualifications.

[CL17](../../C05/17/03-portable-pay-and-overlaps.md) selects direct G care-leave
cash as replacement, not worked wages/10%Y levy or automatic earned units.
Actual continued salary retains its original payroll law. Existing Y06 actual
qualified needed care, same-period covered earnings/credit and separately paid
G-to-S backing remain; no full-wage credit, duplicated unit or old-unit change.

[Y25–Y29](07-earnings-records-and-portable-correction.md) carries a disputed
earnings record through each competent work, insurance and income
decision. Preserve original periods, the difference between covered
and received pay, and independently verified receipt.
