# Use matched fiscal evidence before expanding the national offer

*OB17–OB21. Candidate rules for the evidence used in the first budget.*

**OB17 — Match the measure before carrying it into a decision.** For each
external fiscal reference record year, data release, denominator,
general-government perimeter, accounting concept and whether the measure
is a flow, stock, expenditure purpose or available cash. Compare complete
observed references on their own terms. Use a common classification to
check which public functions need local costing. Preserve material
differences in who pays, who receives and how services are provided.
A peer country's government revenue or expenditure share gives no
domestic tax rate, spending ceiling or operating source.

**OB18 — Keep conflicting totals and missing cells visible.** Reconcile
top-level functions to the published functional total separately from
expenditure, revenue and net lending in the fiscal notification.
Record the signed residual and what can plausibly be explained by
publication rounding, scope or a documented revision. Keep unknown
cells and unsupported explanations visible. Use independently
inspectable original sources. A conflicting aggregate cannot be
allocated to ministries, set to zero or counted as money. Suspend
reliance on the affected claim while preserving unaffected work and
the original service and fiscal duties.

**OB19 — Compare real alternative first-budget motions.** Present
retain and repair against any narrower paid preparation and broader
prospective change on the same domestic legal, source and receiving
baseline. Use external references for coverage and sensitivity, with
actual differences explained. For each new commitment name the
applicable source, cash date, retained ordinary capacity, staffed
independent review and remaining gap. A historical surplus may
coexist with protected balances or later obligations. In the absence
of qualified domestic finance, retain lawful continuing obligations
and authorize only independently funded repair or preparation.
The next elected vote decides any broader start and its genuine
funding choice. This rule creates no new standing appropriation.

**OB20 — Reprice the service and fiscal interfaces separately.** Test
earned rights, care, payments, local services and other material
functions against the applicable gross and consolidated accounts.
Follow functional expenditure to its economic type and liable payer
before comparing actual costs. A social protection heading includes
different claims and modes of provision. Price statutory benefits,
staff, net sources, transfers, capital and renewal on their own
scope and dates. Apply WF90–WF94 for independent funding and actual
settlement. Do not credit a tax, a family contribution, a borrowed
amount or an AI saving that the operating choice has not secured.

**OB21 — Give revisions a bounded and contestable effect.** Publish
original versions, residuals, exact consequences for the budget
and independent dissent. Give elected opposition and affected
people timely access to the method and alternatives. A new data
release changes support only where its scope, amounts or meaning
affect the actual choice. Competent officers and elected bodies
retain their respective decision roles. AI may reconcile source
tables and flag missing or overlapping measures. It cannot impute
a legal entitlement, recognize cash or authorize an expenditure.
Reopen on a resolved reconciliation, a material new observation,
lost access or a stronger funded alternative.
