# A balanced annual book still needs sources and safe delivery

*Part of [C06 country book](../22-country-operating-resource-book.md); design 68.*

The following is the preserved design-68 opening comparison, not the current
SF source choice. Its separate financing hypothesis is a
commercial bank purchases sovereign debt 250 and creates a corresponding
Treasury deposit. A prior public-to-insurance transfer 75 yields Treasury
cash 500 and insurance cash 100; total deposits 600 balance the bank book.
This requires elected borrowing authority, a willing prudent bank and valid
capital/valuation. It is not automatic Treasury or monetary credit, free
wealth or a verified contract. Initial public assets, titles and old source
transactions also remain unverified.

Keep Treasury FX 100, monetary FX 75 and private export receipts distinct.
Normal public imports/coupons use the Treasury's own reserve, replenished
by a contracted purchase from private exporters. Private import/working
settlement uses a separate private line, with fee/carry; no public reserve
is pledged to make private headroom appear larger. Conversion and gross
foreign costs are components of the same expense, not two budget bills.
Actual supplier, lender, exporter and currency access must be demonstrated.

In the reference, ordinary day-1 costs are conservatively reserved before
day-21 receipts. Insurance begins the first month at its normal four-month
floor after payment. A joint old/new debt maturity drops Treasury intramonth
cash below its floor even where the annual closing principal payment fits.
Under a 10% gross export loss, the private line first fails in month 9,
assuming its contracted public FX sale still occurs. This is not authority
to prioritize the state or proof that the sale would actually be honored.
Unpaid requirements cannot be booked as received imports, treatment or wages.

Larger public deposits/working credit worsen bank concentration and liquidity
exposure. The conditional collateral/facility comparison is not solvency,
eligible pledge, authorized draw or funded repayment. Preserve separate
resolution, deposit/client-asset protection and lawful access to essential
payments; state equity does not make these risks disappear.

Ordinary energy/water fit only the authored aggregate vector; the stated
water-loss condition fails. Locations, food/materials, emissions, transport,
workload/shift sufficiency and independence remain unknown. The proposed
S repair roles are not produced or funded by the old N pipeline; new
qualification, displacement and transition costs remain open. Its prior
535.725 envelope and unused 10 buffer cannot be added again to public cash.

Report allocation, estimated requirement, legal authority, due cash, qualified
capacity, receiving and outcome independently. An evidence/readiness hold
does not suspend existing rights or create a technical policy veto. Ordinary
law, E/I/Y/S/N/T/U protections, independent remedies and elections continue;
the Assembly must expose a new burden/funding/coverage choice where needed.
Further evidence can reject this candidate rather than turn every objection
into another compulsory procedure. Actual law/software remains unchanged.

[Design-74 M03](../24/02-liquidity-exposures-and-conduct.md) adds a further
legal-candidate failure: sovereign 250/E100 exceeds its 100%-of-E ceiling by
150. The old transaction remains preserved, but cannot be reused as compliant
source finance. No concentration exemption is granted to fit the budget.

Current [SF](../25-source-finance-and-payment-continuity.md) instead uses G425,
S100, E200/sovereign175 and separate standby, conditionally. [CP](../28-national-commitments-and-residual-scarcity.md)
retires the old GVA/productivity/resource interpretation; the new bridge is a
limited measurement correction, not actual production or engineering evidence.
Cohort starts and residual powers now have separate elected/protection limits.
