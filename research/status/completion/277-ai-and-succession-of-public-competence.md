# Design369. Renew the people who make and review public decisions

[HC05–HC10](../../country/C11/05/01-ai-and-succession-of-public-competence.md)
adds a staffing and budget decision before public operators remove work
that develops needed judgment. It prices paid learning, mentor renewal,
transfer assessment, ongoing service and independent review across the
replacement interval. It applies to assisted, outsourced and directly
operated public work.

The [worked comparison](../../settlement/ai-and-succession-of-public-competence/01-two-cohorts-and-a-duty-roster.md)
checks separate duty, review and mentor assignments. It keeps the competent
ordinary route when assistance has no demonstrated net gain and exposes
lost independent service before the fast assisted route fails in ordinary
operation. Actual qualification, lawful source, participation, adoption
and improved public outcomes remain open.
