# PO09–PO12. Outside command means actual people, sources and powers

*Part of [PO](../27-civilian-enforcement-and-public-control.md); concretize the
existing C08/04/LF12/OJ external function, not a new adjudicator or prosecutor.*

**PO09 — Give external inquiry a separate public employer.** The Civilian
Investigation Director appoints qualified investigators, forensic/source and
records staff through C04 open protected careers, with actual paid standby/
training and safe field access. The Police Chief cannot appoint, discipline,
direct, close or control sole pay/evidence credentials for this service.
Its investigative mandate covers serious official force/custody abuse,
torture, corruption, retaliation and obstruction through existing P/K grounds,
including police/corrections and other implicated public/contract command.
It gains no general secret-service, political-surveillance or guilt function.

**PO10 — Direct receiving and existing automatic triggers.** LF12's serious
death/injury/missing restrained person/potentially lethal-act triggers and
C08/04's direct reporting/preservation operate without the implicated Chief's
credibility permission. Staff with prior statutory allocation preserve/access
evidence, seek actual P07 warrants and investigate under an unimplicated
prosecutor; no mandatory personal Director signature can stop these duties.
An ordinary unimplicated prosecutor remains competent; OJ29 external case
prosecutors activate only on its actual implicated-command grounds. Accused
officials retain silence, defense, fair disclosure and proof. An inquiry is
no guilt finding, automatic dismissal or new power to enter a home/clinic.

**PO11 — A director's own case leaves their command.** Prior case-allocation
law must name accepted qualified outside-command investigator arrangements,
including actual employing public entity, competence, scope, conflicts, paid
release/standby, record custody and statutory powers. Where reasonable source
facts implicate the Director/assigned chain, an unimplicated ordinary court
activates the next eligible preassigned receiver under a prior neutral rotation,
with safe reasons and challenge. Automatic receiving/preservation/urgent rights
do not wait for this allocation ruling; an allegation alone proves no misconduct.
A different region under the same implicated chief is insufficient.

An actual unimplicated police or other lawfully appointed public investigator
may serve only with independent assignment/career/pay/access protection outside
the implicated chain and accepted task/competence/capacity. The original employer
cannot retaliate, reassign away the case or withhold sole credentials. No new
private-contractor/foreign/military power follows. If both commands/all actual
alternates are implicated or unavailable, record failed independent receiving,
protect urgent evidence/liberty/claims through competent existing courts, and
use only actual lawful existing routes/prospective approved appointments.
No fourth officer, jurisdiction or consent is invented and nobody gains immunity.

**PO12 — Investigate the protectors without erasing due process.** DCI staff
have their own statutory assignment and evidence duties; Director's recusal
cannot leave the accused controlling pay, scene, witnesses, source/vendor logs
or final closure. Head/status review uses PO04; staff status uses C04 independent
ordinary court review, and crime follows P/OJ with unimplicated investigators.
Court/career source orders need an actually competent authorized executor and
safe receiving; a written order is not independent delivery. Shared selectors,
Treasury, HR, keys, forensics or patrons can defeat nominal separation; assess
actual controllers and due windows. Separate titles or models prove nothing.

[DF10/DF14](../../C10/12-civilian-defense-and-service.md) names actual civilian
military-abuse assignment/source access in the prospective DF Act. Existing
PO/P/OJ case powers, unimplicated receiving and own-director conflict rules
apply; no new search/force, military investigator or assumed staff independence.

[RC15/RC18](../../C10/13-civil-rescue-and-incident-command.md) expressly
assigns existing serious official-abuse receiving/source duties for public
rescue/coordination in its proposed coordinated Act. PO/OJ/P powers, independent
own-director allocation and fair proof remain; no new search, guilt or staff.
