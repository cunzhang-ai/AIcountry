# Design415. Preserve claims when a public institution closes

[LPC01–LPC08](../../country/C14/02/06-legacy-claims-and-public-closure.md)
specifies lawful closure stages, a funded successor, independent remedies
and separately authorized release of restricted reserves.

[Worked pension closure](../../settlement/legacy-claims-and-public-closure/01-one-closed-scheme-and-a-late-claim.md)
selects a limited funded handover and tests both missing money and failed
delivery. Actual claimant reach, successor capacity and funding remain open.
