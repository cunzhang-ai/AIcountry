# Property journeys that can defeat an apparently clean record

Design148 · Authored adversarial questions, not observed people or verdicts

| Situation | Selected PT route and retained cost |
|---|---|
| Forged home transfer with an innocent buyer | B38/PT02 restores true holder after fair proof; actual buyer seller claims and incorrect-official-certification compensation are separate. No eviction from a database match; no promise a fraudster/state has paid. Purchaser finality is a real rejected rival. |
| Two signed home sales with different filing order | Verify actual disposition power and validity. Earliest valid PT05 lodgment reserves position on lawful registration, not backdated ownership/tax days. A bare contract gives B relief, no second title; dishonest collusive priority is challengeable. |
| Displaced customary holder absent from new index | First registration does not erase actual collective/customary/occupancy rights. Protected notice, plural source proof and court hearing; actual recognition/foreign law may be contested. No empty-map acquisition. |
| Ordinary shop goods financed by the shop's bank | PT13 buyer for value/lawful delivery free of seller-created security absent actual known violation; no duty to read hidden loan terms. Creditor bears its debtor/proceeds risk, not a surprise household repossession. Stolen goods remain another holder's property. |
| A repairer sells a borrowed item as stock | Mere commercial possession creates no owner authority. PT02 retains original title and buyer restitution; no universal merchant-entrustment exception. A reasonable buyer may still suffer uncollectible loss. |
| Old blanket lender versus new equipment financier | PT10/PT11 continuous public notice wins within actual scope/debt/ceiling. No hidden purchase-money leap. Actual release/subordination needed; incumbent power and harder useful finance remain costs. |
| A future-asset notice was filed before a rival, but goods are not yet owned | No PT07 attachment before actual rights/value source. At later acquisition an authorized continuous advance notice can determine rank. A notice alone never creates ownership or a loan. Prior binding assignment of an actual/future claim limits later disposition power under PT04; no clean search guarantees its absence. |
| A late renewal or ceiling increase tries to steal an intervening rank | Lapsed scope takes a new time; timely unchanged renewal retains it. Additional ceiling/collateral takes only new time. Old rank does not expand by editing a record. |
| Offline security receipt during failed publication | Preserve receipt and actual outage evidence; no secret backdating before public queryability. Delay/possession/court preservation are distinct options. PT21 wrong office publication-order loss needs actual causation, not unconditional perfection. |
| False politically motivated lien notice | Notice is not a title/debt assurance or writ; direct prompt PT20/B correction/court and attributed private/state remedies. Fairly hear true creditor; a bare allegation alone does not wipe a valid claim. |
| Sale proceeds leave and unrelated money arrives | PT12 identifiable trace limited by lowest intervening owned balance; no replenishing claim on later unrelated deposits or someone else's client assets. No double recovery on original asset plus proceeds. |
| A smart loan remotely disables a necessary vehicle/home | PT15 forbids private disabling/lockout and requires actual court/receiver powers; valid debt remains. Minimum needs/equipment/housing safeguards do not declare a creditor paid. |
| Bankrupt employer used secret title retention on equipment | Prospective functional security must attach/perfect for secured U rank. Unperfected price retention is unsecured at opening; genuine lease/custody stays excluded. Old valid source security cannot be retroactively erased. |
| Old home lender faces a new public-tenancy promise | H15 exact funded/non-disturbance migration remains; PT does not make older mortgage ranks vanish. Prospective later finance takes already valid tenure; actual accepted safe receiving is separate from a paper successor. |
| Shared building majority proposes selling a dissenter's home | PT06 limits each holder/manager to actual share/mandate; safety/repair is not unit-sale authority. H/technical/B remedies supply some duties; inspect remaining common-building voting/cost/enforcement choices next. |

## Substantive alternatives and the rejected shortcut

| Arrangement | Mechanism and real tradeoff |
|---|---|
| Retain B floor and source-by-source existing law | Avoids transition/registry expansion and preserves familiar local/customary cooperation. Without a selected ordinary rule, parties still cannot infer rank/transfer outcomes from this national candidate; actual source law could justify retaining it in a real context. |
| Selected true-holder + public notice + judicial realization | Protects involuntary holders, separates debt/authority/rank and makes ordinary goods trade workable. Adds public operational liability/access cost; uncompensated buyer loss, lender entrenchment, limited privacy and slow costly execution remain. |
| Strong purchaser finality, mandatory title insurance and private realization | Moves loss toward the original holder/insurer and lowers some buyer/creditor uncertainty. Requires genuine premiums, solvent independent cover, excluded-risk/claims/eviction treatment and public limits; cannot relabel uncompensated displacement as efficiency. |
| Express pre-notified purchase-money priority | Can loosen the old blanket lender's grip and support new equipment/supplier finance. Must bound collateral, actual acquisition value, pre-notice, timing and source losses; adds exceptions and can reduce senior recovery/raise old lending prices. Not silently selected by calling new credit productive. |
| Stronger public/collective tenure and public/relationship credit | Reduces some traded-title/private-lender dependence and can stabilize homes/cooperation. Needs legitimate ownership/acquisition, actual capital/loss allocation and protection against political landlord/credit capture; does not make land/labor or old debts free. |

Actual affected holders, tenants, customary communities, low-income buyers,
small businesses, lenders, registrars, privacy claimants and court workers have
not participated. Their priorities can reject this choice; this analysis cannot
invent their consent. The alternatives disagree about ownership security,
purchaser finality, creditor power and informality; arithmetic cannot settle it.

## Costs, receipt and failed authority

Price actual service/court/valuation/legal-aid/execution work, time, safe files,
simultaneous independent source capacity and the new Treasury contingent risk.
Subtract only actually overlapping old allocations; no new-budget-as-total or
assumed spare old court staff. Public access free to users still has a payer.
Case issuance, compensation entitlement, appropriation, paid transfer and actual
receipt remain separate. Missing cash/independence cannot certify restoration;
elected funding duty and enforceable arrears persist without judicial new taxes.

More registries/speed/formalization may weaken useful trust and informal trade.
No ordinary goods register/account or mandatory lawyer is selected. Future media
must preserve source meaning, act, time, shared scope and challenge. The indexing
operator/AI cannot supply missing consent, settle foreign rights or replace law.
