# Observed interdependence and the limits of transfer

[Joint continuity](README.md)

FERC, NERC and regional entities documented that electricity losses at
natural gas facilities contributed to falling gas production during the
February 2021 cold-weather emergency in the south-central United States.
Their staff report estimates that power losses at midstream, wellhead
or gathering facilities caused 21.5% of the reported production decline.
The report allows both load shedding and weather-related outages as
possible sources of those power losses. It recommends stronger gas and
electric system coordination. The 21.5% describes gas production decline
in that investigation. It supplies no frequency for the failure of a
particular court, election or water service.
[Staff report and official findings](https://www.ferc.gov/news-events/news/final-report-february-2021-freeze-underscores-winterization-recommendations)

This supports testing a shared dependency in the same scenario. The
choice to protect each due function and to have elected bodies decide
the funded package is a design judgment. The fixture's loads, failure
modes, costs and receiving paths are declared inputs. Use actual facility,
operator, original-evidence and expenditure records to assess a real
package. Invite affected people, front-line staff and lawful opposition
to contest the test boundary through their own routes.
