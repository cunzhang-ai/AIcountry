# LU11–LU15. A bounded decision, a finite permission and protected reliance

*Part of [development permission](../17-development-permission-and-serviced-land.md).*

**LU11 — Use AP receipt, proof and actual reasons.** Existing planning officer
accepts assisted safe site/use proposals under AP protected first receipt,
five-working-day help/30-working-day ordinary decision/one bounded extension
and actual reply/omission review. Do not reset for a complete file, referral,
new platform or consultant. Clarify necessary evidence and distinct permissions;
a proponent need not supply an entire new national study. Exact operative facts,
reasonable assistance and uncertainty still matter. A ticked checklist is not
proof of compliance and a deadline is no approval by silence.

Public plans and safe decisions/conditions are accessible, with required source-
specific notice to actually affected owners/users/communities. LU adds no blanket
neighbor-signature or repeated plebiscite condition to every conforming repair.
Specific heritage/ecological/collective/tenant/third-party hearing duties remain.
Protected personal, refuge, commercial and security evidence uses actual lawful
access/correction, not a public household graph. A case officer cannot conceal
material objections, competent adverse facts or paid-provider conflicts.

**LU12 — Change the rule through its actual elected route.** Nonconforming
projects need redesign or a competent prospective plan/rule change under LR/
reserved national law. No uncodified individual bargain/ministerial variance.
An expressly pre-enacted bounded tolerance may be applied only within its exact
criteria/materiality/rights, not used as general rezoning. Record actual effects
and cumulative linked applications; ownership/account splits do not evade the
same site's real proposal. No AI or private reviewer expands the delegation.

A parcel-specific plan amendment needs public scope/affected reasons, actual
input/vote/source and lawful equality/rights review, not a covert project favor.
Required compensation/title/collective status remains separate. Courts can
correct unlawful rules or applying decisions; they do not choose ideal density
or allocate another government's unappropriated infrastructure budget.

For ordinary FUTURE plan amendments, select the substantive planning regime at
AP protected first receipt of an identifiable parcel, intended use and material
scale. Necessary evidence can follow with AP assistance; a blank/speculative
notice freezes no regime. The officer still decides within AP on proved facts:
after reasonable help/reply, material unproved compliance receives a reasoned
refusal, not indefinite 'incomplete' suspension. A fresh application after a
lawful refusal uses its new receipt regime; wrongful refusal/delay retains
actual restoration/remedy and cannot let the authority profit from its breach.

An applicant's material change in site/use/scale is a new revised proposal under
its receipt regime, without extinguishing a separate valid unsurrendered grant.
Requested clarification/nonmaterial correction does not reset the old regime or
clock. An applicant may elect the whole applicable newer regime, not mix its
benefits with old exemptions. A later plan alone does not revoke a valid issued
permission during its term. No receipt/grant freezes independently applicable
safety/ecology/source duties or transfers another's rights; their own lawful
cutovers, necessity/reliance protection and remedies apply. Exceptional statutory
changes need express competent rights-compatible transition, not a case shortcut.

**LU13 — Finite new permissions, not permanent resource options.** New ordinary
planning permissions under the Act allow five calendar years from valid issue
to genuine lawful commencement of their specified physical work. Mere fencing,
clearing, paper transfer or a token start unrelated to the approved phase is
not commencement. Specify meaningful phase works and reasonable completion/
abandonment conditions under the actual plan before grant; no perpetual dormant
option by renaming a shell. Five years is a provisional policy choice, not an
optimal investment/build time. Missing construction does not itself force sale.

A timely assisted extension request is a reasoned AP decision under actual prior
criteria/remaining source/third-party effects, not automatic renewal or arbitrary
new bargaining. Actual unlawful public delay/stay/blocked access needs source-
compatible court/finality/reliance remedy; no authority may profit from its own
breach by simply expiring the claim. Genuine source changes/necessary safety
are not silently waived. No new clock resets existing permits or AP/rights dues.

**LU14 — Actual work and occupation follow their own stage.** Planning grant/
financial security does not license construction before TD05/actual land/source
conditions, or occupation before required qualified safe water/sanitation/access/
fire/structural receiving. Phase-specific safe occupation can be accepted only
for actually independent safe served units, with remaining works/protection
and actual accepting operators. An annual headline of five permitted homes is
not five delivered homes or capacity for all phases. Unmet H/urgent shelter/
tenancy rights persist if a new site is delayed; no unsafe 'temporary' shortcut.

**LU15 — Preserve actual old permissions, neighbors and occupants.** Existing
valid source-law permissions/conditions, tenure, covenants/easements, collective/
customary rights, title/security, private agreements and accrued claims retain
their actual effect until lawful change. New plan benefits may change actual
future permitted use and NF valuation under its own source/notice; no invented
upzoning value, automatic indemnity for every market movement or private title
from a paid tax bill. A new decision is no eviction/redevelopment-possession
order; H/LA/CB repair/temporary home/return and actual court grounds apply.

A permitted change of site owner/operator identifies actual successor and
continuing conditions/claims under title/source law; it neither enlarges the
project nor erases old service/worker/creditor/restoration liability. Actual
court stays/third-party protection/finality remain, not assumed universal
suspension from any complaint or automatic start after an appeal deadline.

[H26–H31](../12/06-housing-redevelopment-and-secure-return.md) joins
phase-specific occupation with resident return and independent review.
A planning permission does not supply a possession order.
