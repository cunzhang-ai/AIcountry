# Public control, continuity and revision

*Part of [LX public rules](../12-public-rules-and-verifiable-implementation.md).*

**LX08 — Make implementation challengeable outside the provider.**
Publish the applicable rule, actual role of the technology,
controlling interpretation, version scope, verified examples and
material changes. Keep effective protected access for the affected
person, ordinary reviewer, audit, court and lawful parliamentary
scrutiny under their own powers. Anyone can report a concrete
counterexample through the existing inquiry or complaint routes
without preparing a technical dossier. Protect narrow legitimate secrecy
without hiding the material basis from competent independent review.
Retain lawful alternative custody for rules, applied evidence and
release records. The author, procurement office or provider cannot
control the only way to reproduce the reasoning or reach a remedy.
A general template, source-code licence or AI explanation alone
does not establish how a case was actually decided.

**LX09 — Pay for parallel judgment and actual correction.** Before
material reliance, assign legal interpretation, qualified ordinary
receiving, review, alternative verification, safe notice and actual
execution to staffed roles with lawful sources. Test shared controller,
runtime, legal interpretation, credentials and evidence failures.
Count work and available cash across simultaneous duties once.
A second model or service built from the same faulty interpretation
earns no independent verification credit. AI may compare drafts,
translate with disclosed limitations, propose counterexamples and
help find dependencies. Accountable people validate the result,
exercise statutory discretion and authorize actual changes within
their own authority. The digital support office cannot promulgate
law, set interpretation for a court or take over the sector.

**LX10 — Revise the method without losing claims or meaning.** Before
cutover, map the old rules and original dates, lawful interpretations,
payments, rights, pending decisions, challenges and evidence to actual
paid successors. Preserve unsupported terms and permit qualified
interpretation without creating a common mandatory medium, schema
or language of participation. Recheck affected consumers and useful
time under DP and FT/OP/T. Keep older evidence readable and secure.
Reopen on recurring legal mismatch, systematic missing records,
ineffective remedies, a more accessible method or capture of the
independent route. Use separately authorized changes to the law,
service or software. Publication of a method cannot approve itself.
