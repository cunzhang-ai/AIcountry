The credit rival uses one candidate gross-work charge/needs-credit schedule,
with separate contributions, pensions, capital charges and safe receipts.
An integrated calculation does not authorize set-off or remove independent
eligibility, privacy and review. Its 25% or 45% earned increment is a value/
behavioral hypothesis. The 75% corporate sensitivity is not selected
because it produces a green annual cell; investment, entry, bargaining,
prices, nonclaiming people and public control require actual comparison.
Public equity expansion also needs lawful price, authority and financing;
the 672 is a prior proposed asset/debt bridge, not new GDP or verified borrowing.

The larger cases assume GDP 4,500 and wages 1.5 times the same cohort wage,
with the needs basket price unchanged. That is an affordability assumption,
not observed real productivity, an industrial plan or an attainable path.
Household consumption is an expenditure assumption implied by the declared
GDP/service/investment package; where it exceeds disposable cash it needs
real finance. Planned counterpart lending, uncommitted insurance reserves
or GDP residual saving cannot silently supply it.

## The same larger case lacks resources

Ordinary national energy availability is authored firm supply 1,000 GWh
less standby 50. Baseline public/private need is 320 + 600 = 920; the larger
case requires 320 + 900 = 1,220, leaving **270 GWh** missing. Raw annual
water 9 million cubic metres less ecological 2 and standby 0.5 leaves 6.5.
Baseline needs 2 + 4 = 6; the larger case needs 8, leaving **1.5 million
cubic metres** missing. These quantities are not measured territory, safe
quality, climate/emissions or actual allocation authority.

The base qualified-public availability contains 10,090 FTE against 9,880
required sector FTE; their distinct roles and paid standby cannot be used
twice. The inherited 10% care loss still leaves 155 care FTE missing, and
two gross institutional envelopes each lose 82. Higher wage/GDP assumptions
do not train or appoint their replacements. They also do not prove separate
reviewers, safe shifts, accessible receiving sites or manual service operation.

Foreign reserve headroom is 25 in authored units. Equal baseline imports/
exports plus foreign interest 6.5 fit that limit. A 10% export shortfall
creates a 41.5 foreign-currency gap, or 53.5 in the larger case. Domestic
cash and a float cannot force a supplier or creditor to accept payment.
Critical medicine, fuel, food and parts still require actual routes and
continuity with external claims and harm visible.

## Research disposition

Retain plural production, person-level protection and explicit public
control. Select the joined account/transition condition, and **hold every
case from an operating claim**. Keep the credit, ownership and larger-output
models as substantive rivals requiring fresh lawful and affected review.
Next prepare attainable staged resource/finance alternatives before relying
on future output; do not hide the pre-growth cost, shifted private debt,
unpriced claims or a missed protection duty. A retain/no-change outcome is valid.
