# National package: what is protected and what changes

*Canonical NP01–NP03; part of [C06/33](../33-protection-production-and-national-expansion.md).*

**NP01 — Select a real allocation, not a target GDP.** Retain I personal-resource
protection, S essential health/learning/chosen care, Y earned/basic retirement,
plural producers and C04 local delivery. Allocate dependable receiving and the
ordinary independent remedy chain before genuinely optional additional coverage.
Finance paid public residual capability where an actual alternative cannot meet
useful-time access; do not promise an unspecified future spot purchase. Lawful
progressive taxation/reallocation, actual borrowing and productive preparation
may change who uses goods, who works and who bears risk. They are public choices,
not technical residuals. No-growth/repair or faster comprehensive provision can
displace this package if its complete distribution and capacity case is stronger.

**NP02 — One basic-service and democratic-continuity schedule.** An adopting
Act names actual persons/tasks, useful times, coverage, receiver, quantity rule,
competence/place/shift, material/source/FX needs, substitutes and remedy for:

- Safe subsistence, individual payment/access, adequate food, drinking water,
  sanitation, safe shelter and dependent essentials under I/S/C07–C09 law.
- Time-critical clinical, reproductive, mental-health and serious-illness care;
  chosen paid support and continuity of children/dependent people. “Essential”
  does not mean emergency rescue only, nor erase learning/prevention needs.
- Actual registration, accessible contest/campaign opportunity, voting/count,
  opposition scrutiny, lawful sitting, authenticated records and replacement.
- Independently staffed ordinary urgent courts, legal help, protected evidence,
  counsel/advocacy, execution and review of coercive acts, including CJ/MB/UA.
- The maintenance, paid workforce renewal, safe source custody, essential supply
  and communications needed to sustain those functions rather than merely open.

This is a functional core, not a fixed GDP share, a closed list of human needs
or everything a protected institution invoices. Link quantities to I/S/Y, actual
case/shift/contest duties and professional safety, publishing methods and gaps.
Do not narrow an existing right to fit the core label. Service eligibility is
independent of the applicable political-franchise waiting period. Emergency care/protection
and stronger source-law rights retain their actual scope for nonresidents too.

**NP03 — Core protection does not make a ruling class.** The schedule enumerates
lawful pay, tasks, standing commitments, contingency limits and actual sources.
Core offices cannot appropriate their own budgets, increase pay through judicial
necessity or put every institutional project ahead of people's necessities.
General prospective revision remains possible with opposition, independent legal
review and protection against retaliation or disabling democratic functions.
The ordinary legal-aid/court/election chain cannot be consumed by the apex court's
preferred expansion. Source custody, profession-specific judgment, payer and
reviewer remain distinct; one “national capacity office” is not established.
A core label is not competence, consent, a new force power or a claimed mandate.
