# Design434. Budget the work of changing the public service

## Decision and baseline

Design433 is the pinned predecessor. T43–T48 coordinate services and
independent duties on one dated budget. T05–T08 already require
qualified cover and paid overlap. The additional choice is to make
preparation, execution, exceptions and handback visible together
across multiple reforms before optional starts are scheduled.
This revision used AI assistance and parallel review of design gaps.

## Alternatives and reasons

Baseline repair can avoid conversion risks and protect current
service. It can also leave a persistent harm unresolved longer.
Simultaneous starts can improve access earlier where both implementation
and independent review have genuine spare cover. Sequential starts
limit common bottlenecks and preserve a route for people unable to use
a new service. They may defer a worthwhile change and prolong overlap.
Select a separately costed sequence subject to verified handback,
with simultaneous starts available when separately covered and lawful.
Use the same deadlines, duties and sources for all alternatives.

A budget office could turn its coordination role into a gate on elected
changes. Keep release authority under existing law, disclose estimates
and omissions, and preserve independent correction and enacted rights.
A transition team could shift burdens onto residents or current workers.
Count actual lost receiving and work transferred to other bodies. Take
staffing ranges and recruitment overlap seriously while preserving
worker choice and keeping hiring decisions with competent employers.

## Evidence, authority and migration

[Sources](sources.md) report actual oversight concerns about
implementation capacity and portfolio management. The [worked case](../settlement/first-programme-transition-capacity/01-two-reforms-one-qualified-team.md)
tests role-specific constraints. It does not price actual operations.
No external qualified independent review, affected-party participation,
appropriation or enacted programme is recorded.

Use existing elected budget and lawful sector powers. Preserve
original dates, current staff rights, open cases, old records, pending
review and independent execution through changes in provider or medium.
Give workers, affected residents and opposition access and direct review.
A project estimate or AI recommendation cannot change an effective date.

## Next bounded decision

Compare two material real changes sharing one delivery team and an
independent acceptance route. Reconcile current work, restricted cash,
overlapping recruitment, manual exceptions, second-window capacity
and the old route's actual acceptance. Require worker and resident
evidence and keep unmet service visible. Reopen if observed staffing
or delivery changes the binding constraint or a simpler repair works
better. Actual country-wide staffing, costs and outcomes remain open.

## Whole-country budget gap

The [programme scope account](../settlement/integrated-first-programme/03-country-scope-and-remaining-choices.md)
identifies material national lines still open. The constructed E+P+H
comparison assumes the protected baseline can operate. Before making a
national feasibility claim, use one specified jurisdiction and opening
date to reconcile inherited legal duties, opening liabilities, debt,
local and social-insurance flows, restricted and foreign cash, physical
capacity and independent democratic and judicial receiving. Compare
baseline repair, staged expansion and a faster programme on that same
account through 90 days and year two. Keep jurisdiction, lawful
authorization and any unverified obligation or source explicit.
