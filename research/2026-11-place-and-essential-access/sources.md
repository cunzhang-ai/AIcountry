# Design503 sources and boundaries

- The OECD's [Access and Cost of Education and Health Services](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/access-and-cost-of-education-and-health-services_4ab69cf3-en.html)
  models physical access and costs for specified education and health
  services in Europe. Its [rural healthcare comparison](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/delivering-quality-education-and-health-care-to-all_83025c02-en/full-report/delivering-quality-health-services-in-rural-communities_3780883e.html)
  describes the access and quality tradeoffs facing different territories.
  The published estimates do not assume the adopting state's geography,
  service rights, transit, budget or rosters.
- The Scottish Government's [National Islands Plan assessment](https://www.gov.scot/publications/island-communities-impact-assessment-national-islands-plan-2026/pages/5/)
  treats transport and digital connections as distinct conditions for
  access to services and employment. Its evidence does not calibrate an
  electoral rule or the joint Design503 outage.
- The [essential network comparison](../settlement/essential-service-network-reconfiguration/01-a-hub-and-an-accessible-local-route.md),
  [host capacity case](../settlement/portable-services-and-host-capacity/01-pay-for-receiving-before-visits.md)
  and [regional docket comparison](../settlement/replaceable-public-institutions/01-one-docket-three-forms.md)
  supply distinct constructed alternatives. The visits, costs and
  outage in this revision are authored, with their own units.
