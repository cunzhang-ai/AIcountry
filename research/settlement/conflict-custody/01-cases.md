# Ten authored cases: power, protection and actual exit

*Part of [conflict custody](README.md); reasoning prompts, not court verdicts.*

| Case | Situation and proposed boundary | Unproved facts and real delivery |
|---|---|---|
| CW01 | An attributable contractor restrains someone offshore, then transfers them to a hospital. CD01/CD04 use first actual restraint and civilian review within 24 continuous hours; neither contract nor geography resets it. | Actual control, earliest records, counsel, independent judge and safe clinical access require original evidence. |
| CW02 | An incumbent calls a protest a permanent terror war. Labels do not establish an armed conflict, POW qualification or CD05 neutrality duty. P needs its own prospective offense/individual grounds. | Independent source access and practical opposition must survive the incumbent's control of command and funds. |
| CW03 | An authorized civilian accompanying forces has no weapon; another person's qualification is genuinely doubtful. CD03 uses the complete applicable status test, not a fighter stereotype; protective treatment continues while a qualifying provisional order ends no later than the seven-day full hearing. | Actual authorization/status evidence and possible stronger protection require counsel and a competent court, not a model match. |
| CW04 | Foreign troops enter a genuinely neutral state; an escaped POW and a refugee arrive separately. CD05 requires actual neutrality law and distinguishes those categories, rather than interning every arrival. | Treaty parties/custom, actual individual role, permissible alternatives and non-refoulement remain separate facts. |
| CW05 | A recruited 15-year-old is captured. No conviction below 16; status benefits do not prove guilt. Any actual status restriction needs a child judge, specific necessity and at most seven-day fresh orders with safe alternatives. | Safe chosen care, interpreters and paid child specialists are not provided by the age rule or an empty welfare placement. |
| CW06 | A POW is held for return-to-hostilities prevention and also investigated for an alleged atrocity. Independent status and criminal grounds are recorded separately. Criminal-use remand has the shortest P aggregate, 90 days or three calendar months; no relabelled reset. | Real purpose, first case-attributable deprivation, defenses, actual days/months and sentence credit need source-based adjudication. |
| CW07 | Active hostilities in the relevant conflict cease but no peace treaty is signed. CD11 requires release without delay; an unrelated war or demand for reciprocity cannot extend this ground. A real remaining criminal order needs its own lawful limits. | Actual cessation, safe personal choices, usable documents, accepted transport and received freedom must be demonstrated. |
| CW08 | A foreign partner promises humane treatment but credible evidence indicates torture or disappearance. CD12 requires independent risk review and effective challenge before transfer; a promise alone cannot pass it. | Actual lawful receiving power, partner willingness/ability, monitoring and correction remain unproved; the blocked route does not create indefinite prison authority. |
| CW09 | A court order expires while the only bed, judge or transport is unavailable. A missing resource creates no extra detention power and never licenses killing, unsafe abandonment or refusal to protect a surrendering person. | The executor must secure lawful safe release/care and account for the violation and unmet duty; a paper order is not delivered relief or readiness. |
| CW10 | An election defeats the cabinet and a vendor threatens to erase custody records. CD13/CD16 require independent retained sources and funded lawful handover; existing duties survive, but custody does not renew the outgoing cabinet's campaign mandate. | Actual successor authority, usable records, payroll, independent appointments and unfulfilled claims must transfer, without publishing protected people. |

For each case ask who controls framing, sources, appointments, funds and
execution together; several AI accounts or office names do not supply independent
review. Human/AI testimony remains challengeable; actual participation and
consent cannot be inferred from these examples. Preserve disagreement and
unknown delivery rather than label a row a successful test.
