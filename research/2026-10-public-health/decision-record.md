# Decision record: ordinary public health before emergency power

5 October 2026 · Integrated design 47 research selection; not enacted law

This is an authored research selection under the proposed
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md). It does not establish an
independent review, affected-party consent or an operating authority.

## Baseline and decision

The [design-46 predecessor](predecessor-design-46/README.md) preserves the
working country before this selection. C07 promises prevention in the
clinical health package; C10 controls communicable-disease restrictions;
C09 assigns sanitation and other sector safety duties. These do not yet
name a day-to-day population-health service, source data and privacy limits,
local outreach, national laboratories, financing or a route from an uncertain
signal to voluntary help before coercion. The older `health.health-act`
manuscript in the [local source bundle](../2026-09-readiness/source-documents.json)
concentrates its public-health article on compulsory isolation and testing.
A future state that can restrict movement but cannot provide reachable
prevention or trustworthy early warning has the wrong operating balance.

The bounded decision is which authority maintains ordinary prevention,
monitoring and cross-sector health protection without merging individual
clinical care, population evidence, environmental enforcement and emergency
police power. Affected people include patients and nonusers, undocumented or
politically exposed residents, children, workers, local communities,
clinicians, laboratories, sector stewards and foreign publics. None has
participated in AIcountry's choice. Values in conflict include preventable
harm, autonomy, privacy, trust, equal access, truthful warning and collective
protection. No case count or model accuracy score alone chooses their balance.

## Rival diagnoses and packages

A missing public-health route may reflect thin prevention capacity, poverty
and unsafe work, fragmented data, distrust of authorities, environmental
exposure, inadequate clinical primary care, or too much reliance on
restriction powers. Original service journeys, source records, workers'
accounts, infection and noncommunicable harm, exposure data and trust
failures are needed to distinguish them. Absence of recorded illness among
people who cannot safely seek care is not evidence of good health.

| Package | Possible mechanism and benefit | Material objection or failure |
|---|---|---|
| Retain clinical and emergency arrangements | No new institution; care and urgent powers already have owners | Prevention, population warning, local reach and data boundaries remain unassigned between clinics and emergency command |
| Central national health-command agency with one person-level registry | May speed analysis and uniform orders | Concentrates sensitive data, messaging and coercive power; local conditions and political abuse may be hidden |
| Voluntary provider- or insurer-led prevention | May use established clinical relationships and plural innovation | Unprofitable outreach and nonusers can vanish; private data or coverage rules can become health policy |
| **Selected: nationally supported technical public health with elected local delivery, separated source custody and C10-only coercive powers** | Joins ordinary prevention, local reach and cross-place capacity while preserving distinct legal decisions | Multi-actor handoff can be slow, local capacity unequal, technical findings can be suppressed or overstated, and privacy safeguards need staff |
| Pure local public health without a national technical service | Can reflect local knowledge and democratic proximity | Shared laboratories, cross-border signals and unequal local finance can leave gaps |

The selected [C07 route](../country/C07/08-population-health-and-prevention.md)
assigns national reference laboratories, common methods, technical support
and protected public findings; elected local authorities maintain reachable
teams; clinical networks retain patient treatment; sector stewards retain
water, food, workplace and environmental enforcement; C11 preserves original
evidence. The [C06 account](../country/C06/09-prevention-and-health-security-finance.md)
funds ordinary and surge capacity rather than counting it twice. A health
finding can prompt help but not itself confer a compulsory power. The
[C13 exercise](../country/C13/12-health-signal-and-community-trust.md) tests
a false or uncertain signal amid distrust and shared staff shortages.

## Source support and limits

The [WHO essential public health functions](https://www.who.int/teams/primary-health-care/health-systems-resilience/essential-public-health-functions)
and [implementation handbook](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240088306)
distinguish surveillance, prevention, health protection, emergency management,
stewardship and multisectoral finance. [WHO's surveillance ethics
guidelines](https://tdr.who.int/publications/i/item/who-guidelines-on-ethical-issues-in-public-health-surveillance)
identify privacy, autonomy, equity and public benefit as design issues.
The [Immunization Agenda 2030](https://www.who.int/teams/immunization-vaccines-and-biologicals/strategies/ia2030)
emphasizes access and equity across routine programmes. The [WHO International
Health Regulations overview](https://www.who.int/health-topics/international-health-regulations)
describes capacities and cross-border duties for actual States Parties.
These are official comparators, not AIcountry's applicable treaty inventory,
local disease evidence or validation of this institutional package. The
older local health manuscript is a draft legal source, not enacted law or
independent empirical confirmation.

## Authority, compatibility and transition

The Assembly sets public-health functions, programme coverage, budget,
information powers, any compulsory ground and review. Local councils own
ordinary accessible delivery under national standards and equalization.
Public-health officers make and publish technical findings within law;
ministers cannot suppress source-backed warnings, while officers cannot
silently make policy, impose an unsupported closure or use public health to
rule an election. Clinical professionals decide individual care; sector
inspectors enforce their own safety law; C10 restricts exceptional action.
Original data remain with lawful custodians where feasible, with narrowly
scoped sharing, retention, protected challenge and no informal secondary
use for immigration, policing or commercial scoring. The complete chain
needs distinct appointment, funding, source access, remedy and execution
under an incumbent who dislikes the finding.

A future adopter must reconcile the older health act's isolation clause,
clinical, privacy, employment, environmental, sanitation, local-government,
budget, border and any applicable international-health law. Existing patient
claims, provider contracts, source records, workplace rights and pending
health complaints keep their lawful status until valid replacement. A
register migration cannot create consent or erase a correction request.
Before commencement, real laboratories, trained teams, safe record
systems, accessible clinics, vaccines or other supplies, cold chain,
translation, worker leave and independent reviewers must be funded and
rehearsed. A dashboard cannot staff a clinic or make a stigmatized person
trust a dangerous intake path.

Objections remain live. Separate custodians and local teams may delay a
warning; a narrowly unified system might perform better in an actual
jurisdiction. Independent technical publication can be exploited by
unelected experts or funders; a political ministry can also suppress
inconvenient evidence. Routine surveillance may cause disproportionate
privacy and stigma harms, even with legal limits. The decision to require
a particular vaccine or test is not made by this research selection; it
would need its own evidence, law, proportionality and public contest. No
aggregate health benefit cancels arbitrary coercion or political exclusion.

**Evidence:** official function and ethics guidance plus authored design,
without AIcountry original observation or affected participation.
**Research selection:** a bounded national/local ordinary public-health
chain with separated care, evidence, enforcement and coercion.
**Legal authorization:** none. **Operational readiness:** no jurisdiction,
public, statutory remit, laboratory, staff, stock or verified safe data
route. **Observed outcomes:** none; no claim of superiority follows.

The next bounded test is one real possible jurisdiction's primary-care,
public-health, environmental and emergency interfaces using original
workforce, incident, trust, privacy and access evidence. Compare this split
with a central service and a provider-led alternative, including a false
alert and a noncooperating minister. Reopen the selection if warning or
remedies are too slow, prevention excludes nonusers, data become coercive
or a rival offers better health and practical democratic control.
An authorized research team would first need a lawful source and participant
protection plan, then a time- and cost-bounded comparison approved by its
actual sponsor; no such team, budget or study is authorized here. If safe
source access or participation is unavailable, retain the uncertainty rather
than inventing an incidence rate or trust result.
