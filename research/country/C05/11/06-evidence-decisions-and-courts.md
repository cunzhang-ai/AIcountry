# Source evidence, bounded decisions and independent courts

Design 98 · Proposed research, not enacted law or verified operation.

**MA06 — Receive, assign and explain.** B13 receives human/assisted/offline reports from consumers,
workers, entrants, councils and bodies without a fee/account or technical dossier. Preserve first
receipt, urgency and accepted referral. The office's published evidence-based risk/complaint and
bounded random-source checking plan covers private, cooperative and public enterprises alike;
investigators receive cases by prior competent rotation/conflict rules with reasoned departures, not
donor/party requests. The chief may set lawful prospective priorities and expose backlog, not order
a favored case result. Initial assistance/administrative decisions obey B16 in their actual scope;
substantive competition/merger cases obey their specifically selected complete clocks and reasons.
No repeated irrelevant document demand resets receipt or makes a notification eternally incomplete.
A refusal/closure gives safe reasons and direct legality/delay review; a court does not force guilt
or require every unsupported allegation to become a full case.

**MA07 — Necessary records, protected answers.** Under expressly enacted V03 scope, inspectors may
require necessary relevant conduct, control, price, contract and processing/technical records, named
samples and voluntary safe business access with a stated case/purpose/period/custody and useful
independent challenge. Nonconsensual business entry also needs its specific independent judicial
authority; this Act licenses no general business activity. No private-home entry, seizure,
interception, unrelated messages or foreign-server compulsion follows; specific independent judicial
authorization and P/PD safeguards govern intrusive acts. Protect legal privilege/self-incrimination,
exculpatory sources and confidential workers, not a monopoly on answerable reasons. Courts can order
proportionate protected disclosure, qualified counsel/technical access, redaction/safe testing and
an intelligible gist; an unanswerable secret model cannot prove an adverse finding. A shared
platform preserves separately controlled necessary source access/copies; do not duplicate all
customer/worker life into a state dossier. Legitimate trade secrets/IP remain KR, privacy remains PD
and lawful public-source/whistleblower duties remain separate.

**MA08 — Bounded ordinary decisions, not a private prosecutor judging guilt.** A statutory senior
decision officer or preassigned qualified hearing officer outside the investigative team may decide
specifically enumerated nonpunitive V05 corrections after B16 notice, disclosure, actual
reply/hearing and reasons. Use B14 ordinary civil proof where applicable, actual statutory breach,
necessity and proportionate restoration: stop identified unlawful exclusion/coordination, correct
misleading required information or restore an established lawful access/contract duty only to the
extent the substantive Act actually grants it. A broad class-wide access tariff, permanent industry
redesign, compelled sale, contested property transfer or dissolution is not ordinary correction.
Conditions last only while needed and receive V05 six-month/material-change review. Do not call
punishment a correction or punish economic success, size, lawful vertical integration or unpopular
speech by itself. Sector safety, banking resolution, public-service continuity, clinical judgments
and elected burdens retain their own legal owners.

MP15–MP17 expressly assigns career clearance/voluntary conditions and court-only contested merger
prohibition/conditions; its exact clocks/lapse/concealment rules continue without a chief signature.
This charter adds no alternative size threshold, clock, deemed approval/prohibition or unlimited
information pause. If a material structural remedy is necessary, the office applies to the competent
court on the substantive law's ground before any applicable deadline; a chief's absence creates no
additional waiting power. Statutory silence consequences must be selected openly in that code, not
inferred from the existence of a chief. Direct urgent ordinary relief remains available when a
closing/exclusion would defeat a later remedy.

**MA09 — Urgency and punishment stay separate.** On documented credible imminent serious harm, the
competent career officer may use only V04's enacted necessary temporary stop/isolation/preservation,
exact scope and safe receiving. Notice and meaningful independent hearing within 72 actual hours, at
most one necessary 72-hour extension; no renamed case resets it. Urgent court consideration is
within B17's 24-hour outer limit and earlier useful time. Longer bounded orders/review follow V04,
not a self-renewed economic emergency. A necessary lawful stop may precede replacement; retain
remaining lawful service and unmet receiving duties. There is no immunity from a necessary lawful
stop simply because it is hard to replace a firm. V04 cannot extend an expired MP15 merger-only
forecast hold; only an independently proved underlying wrong has its own urgent route.

Only the substantive code's expressly adopted closed predicates, penalties, fault, limitation and
V/P/Q safeguards permit punishment. The office investigates/proposes; independent civilian courts
determine contested guilt/fault/amount with defense, proof/exclusion, appeal, actual ability and no
duplicate same-course punishment. An AI score, size, office title, group membership, failed forecast
or mere civil liability is not criminal/punitive guilt. V's existing VD schedule does not silently
supply competition offences. No authority-owned administrative fine, punitive licence charge,
custody or investigator quota; settlements need real informed choice and applicable judicial
confirmation, cannot waive third-party claims or buy immunity for distinct grave harm.

**MA10 — Courts decide material remedies with an executable transition.** Court-ordered
divestiture/property transfer/dissolution or a contested material structural/access remedy needs
exact substantive grounds, evidence, affected-owner/worker/user/creditor defense, less intrusive
alternatives and a funded workable receiver. The court reviews factual/legal necessity and
proportionate remedy; it does not own an economy-wide planning veto, choose a politically favored
buyer or seize resources outside law. Trace who actually owns/control/funds the successor,
independent data/keys, qualified accepted staff, safety approvals, due wages/pensions/contracts,
protected client property, third-party rights, compensation if owed and actual receiving service.
Same vendor/funder/staff choke points cannot count as independent alternatives by company-name
changes. B/U/M priority and C06/NP resource law remain; compulsory separation creates no cash or
labor. A court may reject or stage an undeliverable remedy while retaining proved claims/lawful
protection; regulator/public option funding cannot be presumed unlimited.

**MA11 — Direct ordinary review and the office's own case.** Applicants, respondents and affected
people with ordinary standing may directly challenge appointment/exclusion, source demands,
delay/closure, assignments, corrective orders, merger acts or nonexecution in competent unconflicted
ordinary courts without futile internal exhaustion. B16's ordinary 60-working-day review clock,
usable notice/blocked-access protection, B17 urgency and distinct ordinary appeal apply unless the
substantive Act expressly selects a particular compatible clock. A challenge is not automatic stay;
reasoned court orders and shorter due rights prevail. No chief, investigated enterprise or shared
adviser chooses the reviewing judge. The office cannot finally decide its own data, funding,
procurement or personal leadership wrongdoing; direct courts and existing independent
audit/integrity/prosecution routes have protected sources and original-record access without chief
consent. Chief's general institutional interest does not erase all office competence, but actual
personal/prior-case conflicts require predeclared substitutions or external court determination.
CJ01's constitutional questions use its existing route and do not suspend ordinary protection
automatically; no new court, CJ12 expansion or universal impartiality fallback is invented. No
competent unconflicted actual forum means disclosed unmet remedy, not office self-approval.
