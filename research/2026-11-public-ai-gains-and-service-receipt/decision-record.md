9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned Design246](baseline.json)

## Decision

Select [DP33–DP37](../country/C11/09/08-verified-gains-and-public-service-receipt.md)
to connect evaluation of a public AI task to its funding and actual use.
Compare a competent simpler route, measure from first attempt to receipt
or remedy and give each verified gain a named destination. Protect
existing obligations, human routes and lawful labor terms. Follow the
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

Earlier DP assesses useful reliance, queue fairness and safe exit. The
remaining question is whether a nominal improvement becomes shorter
waiting, better access, different workloads or genuine fiscal savings.
A deployment can make the operator's preferred measure improve while
public staff at the next step and people without a digital route bear
more of the work. Public institutions cannot pledge those hours to
several incompatible improvements. Allocate usable time within the
funded service mandate and require the proper budget or labor decision
for a material new disposition.

## Compare the real alternatives

| Choice | Strong reason | Material exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Retain a competent ordinary route and repair its forms and assistance | Lower non-labor costs and fewer common vendor failures. | Some avoidable drafting or navigation work may remain. Retention alone does not make an inherited queue fair. |
| Use broadly integrated AI and book routine task savings as capacity | Can speed common work and increase the headline number of completed cases. | Can shift work to verification, appeal and applicants. A claimed improvement may eliminate the staff needed for safe exit. |
| Allow scoped optional support and allocate measured gains to actual receiving | May increase accessible and timely service while preserving independent judgment. | Requires paid checking, genuine alternatives, separately funded evaluation and lawful operating expenditure. Some scopes will lose to simpler service. |
| Use public pooled or other suppliers for the same scoped support | Can retain more control over change, access and costs. | Common financing, model roots and skills can defeat claimed independence. A different supplier does not itself improve service. |

Select the third option in the [authored case](../settlement/public-ai-service-gains/01-casework-time-access-and-funded-alternates.md).
The broad option finishes more on time in aggregate but adds paid and
resident work and misses a protected urgent deadline. The bounded route
qualifies in the stated window while adding two non-labor units over the
simpler route. Its eight possible paid hours are assigned to assisted
work, correction and training. They are not a recurring fiscal saving.
The reserve remains funded. The selected two-cycle minimum concerns
material reserve reductions and recurring savings. Evidence may require
more time, and any lost protected route warrants immediate action.

## Evidence, authority and migration

[Official UK studies and guidance](sources.md) support taking reported
task improvements seriously while measuring separately whether people
receive lawful services. Self-reported time savings, nonrandom uptake
and missing baseline observations limit what those studies can establish.
The fixture supplies a reproducible comparison of four service designs.
It establishes no actual hours, public savings, residents' experience
or legal approval.

Sector owners operate under DP and original service law. Independent
review uses protected original data and its own funding. Users and staff
may challenge a service or measurement without a new AI account.
Treasury and the elected Assembly keep source and budget decisions.
Workers retain pay, rest, association and bargaining under applicable
law. Maintain ongoing cases, appeal dates, corrections, contracts and
paid fallbacks through transition or vendor change. Actual affected
participation, independent review, authority, readiness and outcomes
remain open.
