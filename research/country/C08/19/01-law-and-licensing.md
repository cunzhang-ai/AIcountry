# Prior law and fair entry into regulated activities

*V01–V02; part of [C08/19](../19-regulation-and-bounded-enforcement.md).*

**V01 — No agency-made general coercive mandate.** Assembly legislation
identifies the regulated activity/person, specific duty, competent issuer,
evidence/protection powers, permitted instruments, maximum sanction, review
and continuing-service owner. C03's material-rule affirmative approval applies.
A technical standard is public, versioned, accessible without a private paywall
and prospective; an agency/model/vendor cannot silently update legal duties.
A guidance note, license form, emergency label or foreign standard is not law.
Lawfully delegated reversible detail cannot change protected eligibility,
political standing, general force, taxes, crimes or penal maxima.

The rulemaker publishes expected access, entry, worker, household, fiscal and
transition effects, including cumulative burdens across agencies. Competing
methods can demonstrate equivalent safety where the statute permits them;
a dominant incumbent's proprietary system is not the only admissible method.
No compelled public testimony merely to complain about a rule. Existing
rights, privacy and protected evidence continue during review or revision.

**V02 — A license has a defined activity and reachable challenge.** License
only an activity expressly listed in an enacted sector statute because of
its particular risks, not ordinary association, informal care, speech or
all use of AI. Publish qualifications, safe operating conditions, bounded
cost-based fees and decision criteria. Recognize demonstrated equivalent
competence where lawful, with supported assessment and reasoned limits;
wealth, political loyalty, nationality labels and an AI score cannot replace
those criteria. Employment/immigration rights retain their separate laws.

Use B16's usable help, 30-working-day ordinary decision, one reasoned
extension of at most 30 and meaningful adverse reply. Special urgent rights
and due times prevail. The office identifies missing necessary items once
where reasonably possible; it cannot keep resetting receipt. Silence grants
neither a dangerous-activity license nor an official veto beyond review.
B13/B16 direct independent court access includes unreasonable delay and
an interim lawful access/protection order; no compulsory serial internal
appeals. A court can require a lawful decision, not certify unknown competence.

Conditions/denial show actual risk and less restrictive alternatives. A
withdrawn safety license can be restored when actual conditions are met;
permanent exclusion by name or a disguised punishment needs its own strict
statutory grounds and V06 process. Existing valid authorizations and accrued
rights require V12 mapping, not wholesale invalidation by a new research text.

[AP01–AP12](../../C04/06-reachable-administration-and-finite-decisions.md)
fixes B16's default to protected first receipt. Necessary new proof does not
reset it; a missed deadline can be challenged but does not certify safety or
create a licence. Full fair reply and third-party/continuing-service rights remain.

[PQ01–PQ06](../32-fair-licensing-and-portable-competence.md) now specifies
the independent case for reserving an act and the funded route to recognize
equivalent or partial scope. V02's lawful evidence and review rules continue.
