# SD09–SD12: gross commitments, dated cash and actual owners

*Part of [C06/43](../43-country-source-and-delivery.md); design 106 candidate.*

**SD09 — Finance gross delivery before recycling tax.** The proposed Treasury
schedule identifies recurring national G, local own revenue and equalization,
restricted S/Y, insurer/client/bank/standby property and private producer funds
separately. Gross wages, contracts and cash support must be paid before taxes
on them can return. Show internal taxes/transfers only in an explicit consolidation;
they reduce net public cost but do not create independent national production.
Company profit must follow real costs, separate legal units and NF treatment;
no GDP, wage, profit, VAT or loan residual may be solved merely to cover promises.
RF's recurring-source and bounded-debt rules remain, without a new annual
balanced-budget constitution or fiscal guardian. Credible forecasts can support
an elected budget; readiness/receipt claims need their stronger evidence.

**SD10 — Order the first payments before their later sources.** One schedule
shows first-day I05/U/Y/local advances, gross-price VAT protection, supplier and
public payroll, urgent housing/care/courts/elections, refunds, inventories,
training, debt maturities and imported inputs by due date and payer. NF's
12-month company/VAT/transfer/Y start,18-month site/grant and RF24-month new-unit
clock cannot finance earlier dues. Annual company/transfer settlement is normally
next 30 April, quarterly VAT/site cash later than January, while refunds and local
monthly advances may be due sooner. Opening owned cash or authorized accepted
bridging finance must meet that interval; a later appropriation is not earlier
cash. An unpaid claim remains a liability and useful-time failure, not new debt
acceptance, cancellation or a permanent emergency source.

**SD11 — Keep public and private initial stocks paired.** Attribute each
retained, new, replaced or lawfully acquired asset once, with title, financier,
maintenance, remaining life, replacement and old creditor/worker claims. Separate
capital formation, CFC, renewal cash, loan principal, interest and financial equity.
Include producer inventory, households' durables and unpaid shopping/cooking/care
time without commandeering private choices or treating relatives as free staff.
Public ownership requires paid acquisition/formation and accountable management;
zero assumed supplier margin is not productivity. Old QE service assets and new
PS/PA water/retail/bus assets are not automatically additive or freely retained.

**SD12 — Foreign payment has its own owner and currency.** Record each critical
foreign due claim's purchaser, accepted vendor, currency, route, gross/offset cash,
usable opening foreign property and actual source. Private export turnover is not
Treasury money; restricted pensions/client/SF, expected grants, bank capital and
an unreceived order cannot fund imports by merging accounts. The dated PS+PA
quote subtotals compare named exposure only; they are not a national FX bill,
exchange guarantee or financed external deficit. Trade, suppliers and new
technology remain legitimate alternatives with their external welfare/ecology,
consent, quality and lawful-title burdens visible.

The [design 107 comparison](../../../settlement/country-delivery/04-matched-country-comparison.md)
reattributes 80m of included L07/R10/D08 common service to four local payers with
NF grants, and offsets only matched cost-rental owner costs against received
rent. It uses monthly seasonal/civic/independent-work profiles and separates S
annual claims/reserve from G. Quarterly D prepayment is timed once, not added
again as annual expense. Tax attribution and its entry cash stress are authored
approximations, not qualified actual withholding/collection. Missing VAT/transfer
sources, legacy claims and private inputs have opposing effects; the resulting
assessed-source gap/profit frontier is not a complete national deficit or lower bound.

For the authored supported-home branch, require an express lawful procurement
term: actual same-cost rent reduces the provider invoice, or after G prepayment
the provider refunds the proved duplicate funded amount. Only actual settlement
returns G cash. A public owner's same-cost rent credit is separately evidenced.
Absent this term, title, collection or refund, do not credit private rent to G;
recalculate the source gap and preserve tenant/creditor rights. The model's
monthly receipt assumption is not an executed contract or a seizure power.

[Design108](../../../settlement/country-delivery/05-production-title-and-receipts.md)
now links named producer receipts to wages before PA/Y/I05 calculation. A job
count, unaccepted offer, booked loss or unused employee fraction is no income
receipt. Retain actual employer/creditor claims and reprice the household's needs;
never count unpaid earnings as cash or erase an earned pension unit. A loss needs
an identified finite absorber, repricing or lawful exit, not automatic G subsidy.
Public purchase revenue first needs public payment and is not independent export.

Keep fiscal-money issue as a genuinely different research rival. It could change
a domestic nominal financing constraint under express new primary authority;
it does not itself create qualified work, output, owned FX or trustworthy prices.
Current MC19/M15 Treasury-credit boundaries and RF borrowing rules remain. Neither
an ordinary budget/model nor new reserve liabilities enact or fund that rival.
Tax/debt and monetary alternatives need the same real/public-control comparison;
a financial gap alone proves neither real impossibility nor safe money finance.


**Design109 financing choice.** Retain RF ordinary tax/insurance/bounded debt,
not routine fiscal grants. The [paired financing comparison](../../../settlement/country-delivery/06-financing-and-public-control.md)
rejects a missing-source-to-real-impossibility inference as well as free-issue
claims. A bond exchange changes liquidity, not private net financial wealth;
paid reserves/CB bills can retain interest costs. Trace primary and interest
balances, private and foreign owners, actual payments and price/FX/need effects
before claiming improvement. Existing source/appropriation law still controls.

The fiscal-money rival is now a distinct12-month maximum new-principal primary
mandate: enacted numerical gross ceiling, eligible appropriated G uses, timing,
paired accounts and explicit loss/rate exposure. Treasury requests verified G
payments; MC operators execute only lawful authority, without choosing fiscal
beneficiaries. Ordinary monetary rate powers remain separate. Renewal needs
fresh primary law; expiry does not reverse deposits or cancel valid dues.
No model, appropriation alone, CB profit label, netting or emergency self-renewal
adopts this alternative. Required source/input/VAT/credit and independent affected
assessment remain incomplete; neither money nor bond fixtures prove a launch.

[SD25–SD32](../43-country-source-and-delivery.md) now supplies the joined114 comparison: within-chain VAT credits/border cash and export refunds, gross inventoryVAT funding, bank/NBF/equity paired claims and separate G/S/local/FX gaps. No S sweep, unpriced current duty or company assessment is a current collection. Exact scoped comparisons remain distinct from a complete national deficit/GDP.
