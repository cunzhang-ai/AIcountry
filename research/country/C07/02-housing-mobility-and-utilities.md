# C07. A social floor that can actually be used: Housing, mobility and utilities

*Part of [C07. A social floor that can actually be used](../C07-social-floor.md); integrated design 94 research, not enacted law.*

| System | Selected design | Connection that must hold |
|---|---|---|
| Health | S01 essential-care scope for people under national jurisdiction, no payment/identity gate, accountable clinical decisions | Staffing, medicine supply and waiting-time remedies are funded with the package |
| Education | Free accessible basic education; plural inquiry, teacher responsibility and pathways back into learning | Missing documents, disability or criticism cannot remove access; AI tutors retain human alternatives |
| Housing | [H01–H20](../C09/12-secure-housing-and-repair.md) secure covered tenure, permanent public/community cost rental alongside plural provision, usable space, repair/return and I03 affordability support | Cash assistance cannot substitute for building capacity; protection does not erase property rights or rent obligations |
| Care | Supported autonomy, child protection and dependable disability/older-person support | Care recipients and carers retain separate rights; unpaid labor cannot silently fill staffing gaps |
| Income | Portable social protection, assistance for uncovered essential needs and provisional emergency help | Assess usable resources, safe receipt, appeals and subsistence before recovery |
| Food, water and mobility | Safe essential supplies and accessible connections to daily life | Maintenance, reserves, local access and environmental costs enter the same budget |

H01–H20 supplies the proposed ordinary housing regime; [MO01–MO16](17-ordinary-mobility-and-effective-journeys.md)
now specifies necessary journeys, two free flexible returns weekly, bounded
travel/booking times, actual receiving and independent remedies. The numerical
standards are research candidates; local engineering and lawful funded adoption
remain unqualified. A safe interim dwelling and a reachable essential journey may
be urgent claims under that law; neither creates an unlimited right to a
preferred address or door-to-door service. Local housing and mobility
authorities must show actual safe stock, travel time, usable hours, barriers,
cost and continuity before counting an offer as delivered. Independent building
and transport safety reviewers inspect public and private operators alike.
The [interpersonal-safety service](12-interpersonal-safety-and-survivor-support.md)
needs a private, accepting safe place and travel route when the current home
is dangerous; an unused unit or an inaccessible bed is not received safety.
A landlord's insolvency or unsafe building needs tenancy, deposit, property,
creditor and safe-accommodation process; a transit operator's exit needs lawful
step-in, route and ticket records, accessible vehicles, depots, trained workers
and payment authority. Paper receivers cannot carry residents or house them.
The [housing-and-mobility comparison](../../2026-10-housing-and-mobility/analysis.md)
retains direct-public and regulated-plural alternatives and the risk that a
local public provider becomes a patronage landlord or excludes newcomers.

Water and electricity law protects an essential, safe level of access against
termination solely for inability to pay, with a fair billing dispute route and
an explicit funded tariff or assistance schedule. This does not cancel lawful
debts or promise unlimited use. Food access combines income protection with
physical supply and emergency in-kind delivery when money cannot buy food at
an accessible place. The public steward remains answerable for a safe alternative
after a licensed operator's failure; a completed transfer notice is not delivered
water, power or food. The [essential-systems comparison](../../2026-10-material-and-resilience/essential-systems.md)
preserves different operational forms for networks and food production under
one national guarantee and ecological account.
[TH safe home temperatures](../C09/23-safe-home-temperatures-and-continuity.md)
adds the indoor safety floor and a named housing case owner. It separates
repair, energy affordability and interim help while preserving actual tenure.
The [ordinary food-system route](../C09/09-food-systems-and-nutrition.md)
also tests nutrition, food safety, travel and culturally usable access, with
person-level help and appeal here rather than a compulsory diet or app.
Basic [communications and logistics duties](../C09/05-communications-and-logistics.md)
support reachable emergency contact, legal notice and physical delivery of
food, medicine and repair parts. A resident need not own a smartphone to
claim care, vote or receive an effective remedy. The
[basic communications offer](../C09/22/01-basic-service-and-source.md)
tests the whole household price, assisted installation and actual
receiving without a permanent address or compulsory digital identity.
The [sanitation duty](../C09/06-sanitation-and-waste.md) follows a safe
facility and treatment route through to disposal; a water connection or rent
subsidy alone does not prove a dwelling has safe sanitation.

The housing and emergency-access studies now serve this common social-floor design.
Their case rules are supporting specifications, not separate national architectures.
After a disaster, the [C09 recovery route](../C09/07-disaster-recovery-and-return.md)
keeps temporary safe housing and each person's services distinct from insurance,
property compensation and any elected permanent rebuilding choice. A lost title
or policy cannot become a gate to urgent shelter.
Education, healthcare and care are equally central; the volume of recent housing
analysis must not determine the country's actual priorities.

The later [S01](13/01-health-and-coverage.md) controls essential-care scope;
this map no longer narrows it to residents. Actual reception, cross-border
legal competence, staffing and financing still require source-based provision.

[EB transition buffer](../C05/16-portable-earnings-and-work-transition.md)
adds finite person-paid smoothing, not a substitute for received I05/housing/
care/mobility or a newly compulsory claim. Its historical/earned measure is not
current usable I05 resources; actual net payment and distinct source duties
remain, including when a workplace wage is owed but unpaid.

[WS water service](../C09/16/03-service-costs-and-customer-rights.md) supplies connection/default receiving, attributable cost/elected tariff and actual assistance/debt distinctions. This carries forward the essential-access protection above, not a first water right; safe alternate, additional needs and ordinary source/remedy duties persist.

[RT01–RT20](../C09/18-private-rents-tenure-and-housing-entry.md) supplies the
private continuation-rent boundary beside H's cost-rental option. A valid cap/
refund is no actual affordable receiving; I03 necessary-share/source matching,
newcomer access, safe stock and all earlier housing/remedy duties remain.

[EG electricity service](../C09/19-electricity-service-and-grid-access.md)
specifies the person-side bill and connection route, funded minimum use,
independent review and actual receiving after a supplier exits. A physical
outage and a billing cutoff have distinct operators and remedies.

[HL housing loss](../C09/25-housing-loss-and-secure-receiving.md) gives
the local steward a reachable prevention, safe accommodation and
ordinary tenancy duty. Separate suitable housing, the actual journey,
chosen care and paid ongoing affordability.
