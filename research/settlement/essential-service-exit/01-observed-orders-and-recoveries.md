# An order can protect payment while collection stays unresolved

## Service continuity and control

The South African Constitutional Court's 2017 order temporarily
suspended the effect of invalidity for 12 months from 1 April 2017.
It required the agency and Cash Paymaster Services to maintain grant
payments under specified conditions, with limited data purposes,
financial accounting and independent monitoring. The order's legal
authority was South Africa's own. It illustrates the risk of a service
that depends on a contractor after the tender has been found invalid.
It supplies no general power to renew an invalid contract in AIcountry.
[Constitutional Court, 2017 operative order](../../2026-11-essential-service-exit-and-recovery/original-sources/black-sash-2017.pdf)

The Court's March 2018 order extended suspension a further six months
for the cash-payment service. The August reasons record late
preparation, insufficient candour and a contingency plan the initial
application had omitted. The limited extension sought to avoid
serious harm to recipients. These are findings about authority,
scope and preparation. The order does not verify timely receipt by
each resident. [Constitutional Court, 2018 reasons and reproduced order](../../2026-11-essential-service-exit-and-recovery/original-sources/sassa-2018.pdf)

## Published rand amounts with different legal meaning

The 2026 Court used an audited profit baseline with specific
adjustments. Paragraphs 50 and 111–117 give the relevant amounts.
The earlier judgment is included only to the end of September
2018 in this calculation. The Court considered existing overlapping
claims and selected a practicable disposition given insolvency and
the cost and uncertainty of further inquiry.

| Item | South African rand |
|---|---:|
| Certified profit across three suspension periods | 252,590,152 |
| Retrenchment provision adjustment | +107,033,718 |
| Accepted adjustments to empowerment costs | +325,443,704 |
| Prior judgment including interest to September 2018 | −528,994,505 |
| Existing claim for work not performed | −74,786,892 |
| **Ordered adjusted profit refund** | **81,286,177** |

The existing proved claim on the earlier judgment was 632,894,722
rand, which included later interest. Substituting that proved amount
for the period-limited deduction would mix accounting periods. The
refund order allowed the agency to prove a concurrent claim in the
liquidation. It does not establish cash collected. The judgment also
reports that a referee appointment was recalled after disagreement
about who would pay and other administration issues. It did not
resolve all disputed group transfers. Separate valid service,
financial records, assessed remedies, collection and actual
independent oversight. [Constitutional Court, 2026 order and reasons](../../2026-11-essential-service-exit-and-recovery/original-sources/black-sash-2026.pdf)

## What the first budget can use

The 2017, 2018 and 2026 judgments are related evidence from one
procurement history. They qualify the distinction between keeping
services lawful and usable, securing an exit and eventually
recovering money. They do not price a new service or establish a
complete counterfactual. The country design retains timely,
independent courts and a separately paid receiving route. Before
a budget relies on a bridge, require the actual appropriation,
qualified staffing, safe data access and accepted receiving.
Budget the successor and ordinary rights gross of uncertain
recovery. Keep the affected person's direct remedy available.
