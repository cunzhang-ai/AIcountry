# C07. Education that builds independent learning and civic power

*Part of [C07 social floor](../C07-social-floor.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

The national Assembly establishes a free, usable foundational education
entitlement with an age-appropriate duty to ensure children can learn. A
national floor protects literacy, numeracy, science, history, civic and legal
understanding, arts, digital/AI criticism, health and ecological knowledge;
[S05](13/02-learning-and-educational-freedom.md) selects the candidate foundational ages6–18; exact curriculum and material revisions require legitimate law and public
review. It protects inquiry and factual correction, not loyalty to this
country's institutions. Learners may criticize the constitution, parties,
religions and AI systems without losing a place. Children have their own
voice and safeguarding rights; parental and cultural claims matter but do
not erase the child's education or permit harmful exclusion.

Elected local education authorities guarantee public school places, reachable
travel, safe buildings, qualified teachers, accessible materials and an
accepting school after a provider fails. National need-adjusted finance covers
disability support, language access, poverty, sparse routes and fixed local
capacity. Accredited additional schools may receive public funds only under
equal-access, curriculum-floor, staff and public-account rules; admission,
fees or opaque screening cannot move high-need learners to the residual
public school. Lawful home or community learning alternatives must show
equivalent learning and child protection through a proportionate, appealable
assessment, without a state monopoly over every teaching method.

Local elected bodies set place plans and room for regional language, history
and culture within the national rights floor. Teachers and subject experts
develop and revise instruction with protected professional and academic
freedom; students and families can challenge inaccuracies, discrimination,
unsafe practice or political indoctrination. Neither a minister nor a
platform vendor may rewrite a binding curriculum through an update. An
independent inspector checks access, safety and outcomes without choosing a
single lawful worldview; courts protect rights and procedure, not a preferred
history lesson. Collective claims about language or education require the
separate C02/C04 affected-scope process.

A learner has a named school, timely support assessment, accommodations,
safe attendance, fair discipline and a direct route to correct records or
contest exclusion. A serious safety restriction needs reasons, interim
learning, a proportionate hearing and review. A closed school, disaster or
teacher shortage triggers a funded receiving place and transport; a screen
or remote lesson is not equivalent for a child who cannot use it. Credentials
and graduation decisions require published standards, correction and appeal.
The [C07 youth mental-health route](11-mental-health-substance-use-and-crisis-care.md)
offers confidential, age-appropriate help and safe referral; a school's
diagnostic suspicion or AI flag is not an offense, compulsory treatment or
automatic exclusion. Child protection and a concrete safety incident keep
their own lawful grounds and review.
Assessment reports multiple learning and wellbeing dimensions, including
people who dropped out, without making a single test score the school's
funding or a student's civic worth. AI tutoring is optional support with
privacy, teacher oversight and a non-AI path.

Adults need accessible routes to basic learning, retraining and civic
education alongside work and care. Publicly funded vocational and advanced
places use published entry, cost and appeal rules; the state cannot promise
every requested course or direct a person's career. C06 counts teachers,
rooms, transport, interpreters, learning materials, placement partners and
maintenance against health, care and other commitments. The [decision
record](../../2026-10-life-course-services/decision-record.md) keeps local
school power and the national floor as a testable choice, not a proven
education outcome.

[S05–S08](13/02-learning-and-educational-freedom.md) supplies candidate age, free-place and early/adult pathway rules. Its hours are offered capacity, not compulsory seat/screen time or a restriction on equivalent future learning methods.

[HC](../C11/05-retained-human-capability.md) now separates assisted assignment
performance from retained understanding/transfer. Teachers use safe, accessible
assessment with necessary assistive tools and student voice; public tutors
need evidence of learning as well as easier completion. Tool design/practice
may be revised without banning private lawful use or withdrawing education.

[NC under6 organized care](20-early-care-relationships-and-staffing.md) now
specifies actual staffing/groups/relationships for joint early-learning/care
before the S05 boundary. Shared episodes count once; school39-week/30-hour
capacity does not cover all S10/holiday need. This imports no attendance quota,
new majority age, sovereign AI or new authority over private family belief.

[ED01–ED20](21-public-learning-and-school-power.md) now concretizes the public
employer/director/head and national career inspector, professional/learner
voice, equal funded entry,2-day initial/10-day outside site protection and
actual receiving/paid succession. These are civil site limits, not compulsory
attendance, custody, a new crime or a change to S05 ages/offer or B21 majority.

[AL01–AL20 lifelong learning](22-lifelong-learning-and-renewal.md) now adds
person-directed continuing/qualifying renewal beyond employer or public-project
sponsorship, autonomous public advanced institutions and paid support/continuity.
S06/S07 first/catch-up rights and ED school power remain;64 adult offered hours
are neither a full pathway ceiling nor an extra paid-leave or wage entitlement.
