## Coherent alternatives and retained objections

Seven staggered commissioners distribute judgment but add paid selection,
professional clustering, opposition veto, mediator power and quorum failure.
One nonrenewable chief is simpler but concentrates the blocking point; cabinet
administration is easier to replace but exposes contestants to incumbent
control before useful relief. No option guarantees survival of joint capture.
The all-public inaugural route concentrates fourteen choices in one panel;
a real founding instrument and protected noncommission-owned roll are required.

A force ceiling and paid unarmed/community response are selected over generic
necessity plus after-the-fact punishment. An independent predominantly unarmed
civil response remains a serious whole alternative, requiring funded acceptance
and a safe grave-threat response. A stricter life boundary may allow a fleeing
suspect to escape; past offense alone is not the present threat. Staff safety,
unserved danger, chilled opposition and victim remedy remain distinct outcomes.

## Shared delivery and continuing duties

The [authored simultaneous case](../../settlement/public-power/README.md)
plans 164 distinct role-hours with 115 available and a 49-hour planned-work gap. Equal aggregate
hours in the wrong skills do not cure election, court, care or evidence gaps.
The actual 36-person panel route is included, with only 26 attending and therefore no quorum despite 24 votes. Full-panel absence is ten people; the quorum alone lacks one. Its minimum panel-plus-support subset is 137 hours against 115, with 22 missing role-hours. This is a partial invented workload, not actual pay or the whole-country cost. Qualified people, prices, actual owned cash/FX, public authority,
professional/affected-party review, actual receipt and outcomes remain unknown.
Human/AI helpers share a controller; their count is not independent evidence.

Preserve original filing/restraint dates, actual old appointments and claims,
workers/contracts, privacy/source protection, U/Y/H/TW/NP/CP and creditor/foreign
obligations. No fines, predicted AI gain, free public service or duplicated CJ
staff fund the package. New LF duties commence 180 calendar days after an Act;
existing stronger law applies meanwhile and readiness does not move that date.
Election dates/transition need an explicit lawful instrument, not selector consent.

Next choose a bounded operative competition package and its entire power/remedy
chain against court-only and essential-gateway alternatives; preserve the other
national gaps above. Reopen priority for real immediate harm/election failure,
a stronger whole alternative, new evidence or missing useful-time execution.
Structural selection and artifact checks are not adoption, readiness or better lives.
