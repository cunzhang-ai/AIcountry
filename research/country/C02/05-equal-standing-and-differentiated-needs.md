# C02. Equal standing and differentiated needs

*Part of [C02 membership and rights](../C02-membership-and-rights.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

Every person under the country's authority has equal protection and practical
access to justice. Public offices and entities providing employment, housing,
education, health care, finance, transport or other generally offered goods
must not deny or degrade access because of race, descent, sex, pregnancy,
sexual orientation, gender identity, disability, health status, language,
religion or belief, political opinion, age, family
status, social origin, economic position or an analogous ground defined and
reviewed by law. A person's combination of characteristics may create a
harm that no single-category inquiry detects. Political opposition,
residence status, care dependence and use of an AI-free channel cannot be
proxies for exclusion. The Assembly enacts the exact civil scope, protected
grounds and limits through public contest; this research text does not
retroactively create an offense.

Equal standing does not require identical treatment. A child needs distinct
protection and an adult with a disability may need a usable accommodation.
Clinical urgency, safety, need-based support, progressive taxation and
lawfully defined political membership can justify different treatment within their own rights and
competence. A claimed exception needs a real purpose, evidence, a proportionate
fit and a reviewable less-exclusionary alternative; an inherited stereotype,
administrative convenience or opaque model score is not enough. Reasonable
accommodation is an individualized, timely duty with an enacted undue-burden
boundary and an interim access route. A provider cannot call an inaccessible
offer equality because everyone received the same form.
The respondent must substantiate a claimed undue burden against applicable
resources, alternatives and existing accessibility duties; its chosen budget
line or a vendor's default setting alone cannot establish the exception.

Legislation may authorize targeted, proportionate measures to repair
documented exclusion, with affected participation, published purpose, costs,
review and an end or revision condition. Such measures do not give an office
permanent power to assign people to compulsory identity groups or set quotas
without law. Voluntary self-identification can improve evidence, but a person
may seek ordinary care, housing, work or legal help without surrendering
sensitive ancestry, belief, gender or health data to one universal registry.
Statistical comparisons need consent or a specific lawful ground, minimal
collection, safe aggregation and independent correction.

Equality duties coexist with privacy, expression, association, religious
freedom, property, due process and legitimate plural institutions. A court
must resolve specific conflicts under constitutional and enacted law; neither
an equality office nor a platform classifier may silently rewrite them.
Indigenous and other historically grounded collective land, governance and
treaty claims retain their distinct authority route under C02/01. [C08's
enforcement rule](../C08/11-equality-law-and-effective-remedies.md) connects
individual repair and systemic investigation without giving one protector a
monopoly over complaints. Reopen if the legal categories exclude actual harm,
evidence collection exposes people, accommodations are merely paper, or
targeted measures themselves become an unreviewable hierarchy.

[FB36–FB41](../C06/62/07-rights-and-distribution-during-adjustment.md)
applies equal standing to combined fiscal burdens and independently
verified mitigation. Preserve existing minima, cases and remedies.
