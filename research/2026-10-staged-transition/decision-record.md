## Diagnoses and competing institutional choices

An unexplained transition can fail through missing material finance,
displaced workers, source-law incompatibility, controlled review or a promise
that stays perpetually conditional. Faster coordination alone cannot solve
these different failures. Compare retain/repair, protected plural stages,
state acquisition/direct provision and simultaneous launch. Retain/repair
preserves receivers and avoids purchase/overlap but may preserve patronage;
plural supply can support exit but add rents and uneven capacity; direct
provision may reduce private gatekeeping but adds purchase, integration and
public-patronage risk. A legitimate public must assess whole alternatives.

Select the stage **rules**, not a single owner, institution form, tax rate,
permanent implementation council or optimistic output forecast. Retain/no
change remains a real option. Popular replacement continues while projects
are unfinished; neither courts nor technical assessors select the political
mandate. A missed enacted due date is a breach requiring remedy and elected
correction, not an operator's power to redefine commencement indefinitely.

When the export forecast is never received, domestic cash still fits but
foreign payment first fails in year 2, with unmet planned payments 81.

additional national costs; some refer to the same unpaid planned invoices.

With two-year delay and capital cost +20%, year-8 cash still appears favorable

Material objections remain: staging can disguise austerity or delay rights;
T03–T04 distinguish research proposals from enacted due claims and preserve
remedy. Apparent reversibility can ignore cancellation costs; T07/T11 carry
contracts and lawful successor duties. Central accounts can control framing;

replaceable mediators. A technical gate can entrench a protector; it supplies
evidence, not political tenure, and remains challengeable. A paid worker
count can hide unpaid supervision or lost private output; both stay unknown.
These are reasoned authored objections, not public assent or resolved values.
