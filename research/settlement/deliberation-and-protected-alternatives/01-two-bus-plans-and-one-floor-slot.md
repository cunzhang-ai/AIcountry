# Two bus plans and one protected sitting

This constructed case tests [RDA01–RDA08](../../country/C03/24/01-deliberation-and-protected-alternatives.md).
A 24-seat commissioned panel compares two proposed bus improvements.
The main recommendation adds a direct route at a seasonal cost of nine
units. A competing text combines a main route and a staffed feeder at
a cost of twelve. Both specify the existing protected access and funding
needed for actual delivery. The feeder aims to serve people who cannot
reach the main route. These are stipulated proposals and costs.

Six residents support the exact feeder text for consideration. One agent
submits four duplicates for one of the six. There are still six people.
A separate text with five supporters stays in the record and misses the
one-quarter threshold. The panel report arrives on day 80. The existing
RD date for disposition is day 140, earlier than day 150 calculated from
the report deadline. No added disposition resets that date.

The sitting has three units of standing support and needs two units of
independent work per text. With seven units and a qualified alternate,
both texts receive their separate votes. A sponsor's merged text is
available as a later revision. A vote on it cannot replace either
disposition. If the ordinary presider omits the feeder, the authorized
alternate can restore the vote at a lawful resumed sitting by day 139. An attempted correction on day
141 misses the original opportunity.

With only six units of eligible support, the two full paths cannot be
credited. The case records the lost alternative disposition, preserves
the main recommendation's paid route, and leaves independent correction
available. Smaller groups' reasons and ordinary rights remain visible.
A successful take-forward vote produces no spending or buses. The
competent bodies still need enacted authority, funded staff and service
receiving before a claimed improvement.

These figures test the scope, deadlines and shared resources. They do not
measure travel, support or actual effects in any locality.
