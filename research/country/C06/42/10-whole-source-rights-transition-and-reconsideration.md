# Recurring taxes: one source account, enforceable rights and explicit limits

*NF37–NF40; part of [C06/42](../42-recurring-tax-and-noncash-work.md).*

[Design156 TN17–TN20](../59/05-country-package-and-source-continuity.md) retains
mixed-source architecture and these rights/notice/date boundaries, downgrading
the NF numerical default replacement preference to explicit benchmarks. Actual
lawful retrofit sources persist pending express prospective joined replacement;
no new approval body/readiness veto, automatic tax or transfer of final review.

**NF37 — Select no generic environmental windfall.** This Act adds no general
carbon/emissions/resource levy. Existing exact pollution/water/waste duties,
measured treatment fees, public resource/land leases and emissions law remain.
A future price/charge needs actual units, upstream actor/nexus, rate/path or
cap, border treatment, overlap, independent measurement and paid household/
worker/grid/transport transition under the ecological package. No agency fills
it with a price chosen to close the welfare deficit. Declining emissions,
volatile royalties or sale of public assets are not permanently growing ordinary
revenue. This no-new-tax choice can underprice harm/forgo source and remains
contestable against a joined carbon/rent package; it is no pollution immunity.

**NF38 — Extend existing rights, not an omnipotent tax office.** The competent
Act expressly applies RG/TX to these company/VAT/land/transfer/new-noncash-Y
bases with each named unit, original annual/quarter due date and G/local/S
payer. Four-year assessment/final-notice bar, only court-proved bounded concealment,
full merits/direct ordinary courts, disputed collection stay,10working-day
undisputed refunds and six/evasion-only-eight-year collection remain. Deferral
maturity and transfer installments have only NF21/27's express original events;
no hidden perpetual lien or owner-family debt. K22/V offenses require existing
actual elements/fault/notice and court procedure; no new crime by tax label.

Existing Revenue officers, separate Tax Merits Review Head, qualified land/
contribution roles, Treasury refund/distribution/grant payers and S staff are
C04 appointments with protected own-case competence, independent source access
and ordinary own-office review. Extend scoped B19/B18 determined record/notice/
refund execution expressly in the adopting NF Act, with external conflict
rotation/credentials/pay. No court sets rates/extraordinary public risk or invents
staff/cash/FX; actual unpaid judgments/receivers remain visible. No universal
wallet, life log, political targeting, quota, bounty or provider-controlled appeal.

**NF39 — Fixed prospective dates and actual overlap.** Company/VAT/transfer/
new noncash-Y base starts first1January at least12months after enactment;
site levy/local share/equalization starts first1January at least18months.
Opening valuations/capital bases/loss-credit continuity, M/zero-list, lawful old
terms and actual source notices are set before those dates, no readiness
extension. RG/TX assistance at180days applies only already-lawful taxes. New
ratios cannot recap old service; RF's1W new-service date stays first1January at
least24months, not this earlier collection start. Until then actual older Y
benefit ceilings/units retain their law, even on newly covered remuneration.

Tax only post-start performance; genuine prepayment for future taxable supply
enters VAT once at post-start performance, no completed-old-supply retrocharge.
Preserve prior terms/net promises, depreciation/basis, mortgages, leases, old
period liabilities/credits/refunds/limits, grants, contracts, client/worker/
foreign claims and court dates through named paid successors. Changes in actual
prices/tax may require correction/essential protection, not automatic compensation
for every investor price loss. Current software/constitutional holds do not change.

**NF40 — Compare a whole country, not an attractive gross yield.** Consumption-
heavy simpler taxes, rent/wealth-heavy sources, PA-only/public returns, company
imputation/flow-through, building levy/ground rents and no new transfer tax are
real alternatives. Selected mixed sources spread collection but add complexity,
combined company/dividend burden, small-business threshold/retention incentives,
land capital loss, home-deferral/renter inequality, unequal local discretion and
unproved foreign/source access. More taxes/formalization need not improve lives.

G net cash separates actual company/VAT/transfer/national land receipts, refunds,
protected gross-basket advances, local80% transfers, equalization, paid source/
review/court/receiving and old due obligations. S contribution/care-credit,
insurer F/SF/client/pension property and owned FX remain distinct. Corporate
accrual, assessed land, deferred transfers, commitments and forecast productivity
are not received source. No double VAT/public levy/internal G/S transfer or
new state enterprise tax creates national output. Publish distribution, actual
prices/wages/supply and lost/unexamined coverage before claiming affordability.

Next compile one coherent current country source/delivery package against all
continuing claims, production/owned-FX/qualified-time limits and strong whole
alternatives. Exact local delegation, political finance/map and nonbank technical
laws remain. Reopen on unmet essential protection, overburdened investment/work,
unpaid refunds/default, valuation/source capture, local-service failure or a
better simpler source package. Actual affected/qualified review, legitimate
adoption, operational capacity, received remedies and superior lives remain
separate unproved facts; numerical/file checks cannot substitute for them.
