# PD09–PD12: usable access, correction, objection and limited retention

*Design 96 ordinary-data research candidate; not enacted law, funded readiness or
demonstrated superiority.*

**PD09 — Notice and inspectability.** Give a short usable notice at direct collection
naming controller, purpose/base, categories, sources, significant recipients, retention
date, transfers, rights and human/assisted contact; an indirect collection gives notice
before first consequential use/disclosure or within 30 days, subject to PD08’s narrow protected journalism/archive/research rule; other
necessary notice delays require a specific law and independent review with bounds.
A controller retains a purpose-and-transfer register, not a central personal dossier. On
request, disclose whether data are used and supply intelligible data, origin,
recipients, duration, material inferences and reasons for a consequential decision,
protecting another person's rights through redaction or safe review. Confirm receipt
within five working days; answer within 30 calendar days of first usable request, once
extendable by a further 30 calendar days for genuine complexity with notice/reasons
before the first period expires, while B17 urgent protection uses the actual useful
time. No fee for ordinary requests; repeated abusive requests need a reasoned reviewable
response. Verify identity proportionately without demanding a universal credential. This
is the stricter controller data-request clock, expressly distinct from B16’s
30-working-day administrative-decision schedule; ordinary supervisor proceedings retain
B16, with earlier urgent sector/B17 protection in both. Do not reset first receipt by
routing, an irrelevant document demand or an identity app. Ask only reasonably necessary
proportionate identity clarification; give accessible help and provisional urgent
protection during genuine uncertainty.

**PD10 — Correct, restrict, erase.** Correct false or materially misleading records and
notify known materially affected downstream users without undue delay. When an original observation must remain
as evidence, append the correction, disputed status, author and date, and stop
unqualified adverse reliance; do not silently overwrite it. Erase data when no live
base/purpose remains, on valid consent withdrawal where no other base exists, after a
successful objection, or after the required retention period. During a genuine
litigation, clinical, safety, election or public-record hold, retain only necessary
protected material, restrict other uses and name owner, review date and eventual
deletion test. An agency cannot invent an indefinite hold to defeat erasure; deletion
cannot destroy due wages, medical continuity, protected testimony or a pending defense.
A controller informs material recipients of correction/erasure or explains impossibility
with a challenge path. The source controller notifies known material recipients without
undue delay and obtains acknowledgments/action or reasoned failure, not a silent
feasibility excuse. Each recipient checks derived features/models, open cases and
completed consequential decisions within its own legal scope. Correcting a source is not
reopening every judgment automatically; responsible owners apply their correction/review
law with useful-time interim protection. Deleting training rows does not prove model
unlearning. If the affected version may still materially expose the person or drive a
consequential wrong and removal cannot be verified, restrict that affected use, provide
an accountable alternative, replace/retrain where justified, test residual exposure and
disclose uncertainty. Do not suspend unrelated lawful care or preserve every raw record
forever.

**PD11 — Move and object.** On a person's request, provide their supplied/observed data
held for consent or requested-service performance in an interoperable usable form within
PD09's clock, and transfer directly where safe and feasible. This does not reveal other
people's private data, proprietary model internals or an official eligibility decision
as a transferable status. A person may object to PD04(f) processing on their
circumstances; the controller must stop unless it proves an overriding necessary ground,
and direct marketing stops on objection without a balancing override. Objection to a
public task receives a reasoned necessity/rights answer and court route; it does not
cancel a valid law or another person's claim. Portability must not be a monopoly gate or
a reason to abandon accessible nontechnical channels.

**PD12 — Purpose-bound retention and legal holds.** Set a purpose-specific
deletion/review date at collection. Routine precise-location traces default to no more
than 30 days and routine service/security telemetry to 90 days unless a documented
necessary longer purpose or enacted sector schedule applies; incident preservation
identifies the particular event, custodian, access and review date. Clinical,
child-status, election, court and public-law records use their stronger specific duties,
not these telemetry defaults. These are maximum defaults for otherwise necessary routine
traces, not permission to collect or keep them for the whole period. A longer
controller-claimed need identifies exact records, purpose, necessity, access, end/review
trigger and challenge; another label or unlimited hypothetical future dispute is
insufficient. Preserve only the necessary safe evidence of a real duty or reasonably
supported legal exposure; maintain a finite review/deletion plan. Backups carrying
erased data are inaccessible for ordinary use, roll out under a documented cycle no
longer than 90 days absent a specific lawful hold, and reapply correction/erasure before
restoration. A backup is not a new use base.

failed notice and executed correction for covered administrative acts. PD retains
