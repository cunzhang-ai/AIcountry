# Design419. Fund national duties without acquiring unrelated local powers

## Decision and baseline

Design418 already protects due transfers, standardizes need and revenue
capacity, bounds fiscal recovery and preserves elected councils during
an essential-service backstop. The remaining choice is how to review
conditions across ordinary grant programmes, before accepting an offer
or withholding due finance. A ministry could make refusal costly by
bundling an optional project with money for a continuing legal duty.
This record was prepared with AI assistance. Lawful adoption and
appropriation remain decisions for the competent elected authorities.

## Alternatives and selection

A broad unearmarked transfer gives councils genuine scope to choose
priorities. It is a serious alternative where the public purpose is
general fiscal capacity and balanced access. It gives less assurance
that additional funding achieves a particular national purpose.
A national service or direct standard can protect residents when a
shared need requires uniform legal duties or scarce interregional
capacity. It needs a lawful scope, source and provider and can reduce
local responsiveness. Treating national grants as unrestricted
bargaining tools can coordinate activity cheaply in some settings.
It allows unequal capacity and existing dependence to distort assent.

Choose separately paid statutory finance, public and purpose-related
conditions on new optional offers, and an actual refusal test across
linked changes. A lawful technical or spending term can protect a
funded project. Local councils retain competing lawful delivery
methods and may refuse the project. Their refusal can leave the
improvement undone. The national legislature can change its lawful
programme or enact a generally binding and funded standard through
the ordinary process. These implementing clauses neither entrench an
existing formula nor give a council a veto over national law.

The material objection is that conditions and due payments can be
formally separated while the poorer locality still cannot afford its
existing duties. Use the dated continuing baseline, show qualified
capacity and correct the shortfall through MF, fiscal recovery or
the bounded service backstop. Require an explicit accounting when
a separate prospective budget choice moves the baseline. Do not
assume that all lower funding is unlawful coercion.

## Evidence, authority and migration

[Primary comparators](sources.md) establish examples of separating
equalization from special-purpose grants and protecting local discretion.
They provide no operational proof for the proposed refusal or review
test. The case uses two stipulated revenue capacities, one common
service and a separately authorized optional programme. No council,
resident or independent reviewer participated in this revision.

Adopt prospectively for affected offers and revised conditions, with
the correct authority for each transfer and actual, separately staffed
release and correction. Retain existing claims, lawful contracts,
own-source revenue and valid maintenance commitments. The national
legislature authorizes finance and conditions. Councils accept or
decline on the published terms. Treasury executes due cash. An
independent court reviews lawfulness and can enforce an existing
duty within jurisdiction. External audit examines the original
ledger and actual delivery. A grant body cannot appoint its own
reviewer or withhold funds from the independent route. Where that
route fails, disclose the missed remedy and carry the obligation.

## Scoped result and bounded next action

The documentary comparison and two-council exercise support a
research selection only. Actual finance, independent review,
authority, local decision and observed delivery remain open.

Take one existing formula payment and one proposed project in a
poor and a better-resourced locality. Trace refusal, applicable
law, dated cash and the first due service through ordinary payment
and an independent correction route. Include proposed local costs,
the consequences of failing either route and actual resident
receipt. Complete with separately confirmed fiscal and service
outcomes for both localities. There is no committed exercise budget.
Reopen on repeated indirect conditions, timing failure, wider
unserved duties or a less burdensome source and decision model.
