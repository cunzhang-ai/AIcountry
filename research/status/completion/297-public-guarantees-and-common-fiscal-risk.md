# Design389. Give public guarantees a common account and dated authority

[DB26–DB32](../../country/C06/60/10-public-guarantees-and-common-fiscal-risk.md)
makes actual exposures visible across issuers. It separates
expected loss, legal cap, cash, counter-guarantee timing and an
unpromised rescue. Preserve the affected public service separately.

[Primary records and the worked calls](../../settlement/public-guarantees-and-common-fiscal-risk/01-two-guarantees-and-one-due-date.md)
test shared risk and a late recovery. Actual cash capacity,
legitimate approval and better service remain unverified.
