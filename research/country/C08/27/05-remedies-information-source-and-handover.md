# PO17–PO20. Public control requires information, resources and actual execution

*Part of [PO](../27-civilian-enforcement-and-public-control.md); no source
monopoly, police self-financing or technical veto over a lawful public change.*

**PO17 — Several reachable routes, useful-time relief.** A person may contact
police, investigation service, prosecutor, court, ombud or existing protected
support without first completing the challenged service's hierarchy. Ordinary
problem reports need no full technical dossier. P06 receiving, LF aid/records,
independent closure review, B/P liberty/urgent protection and B18/B19 actual
compensation/execution keep their grounds. No conviction prerequisite for
civil protection or access; no promise courts must order a charge/conviction.
Preserve original receipt/dates and safe participation for accused and affected
people. A withdrawn/unproved complaint is not a fabricated-report offense.

**PO18 — Inspect power and unserved harm, not just outputs.** Publish safe
district/general priority/resource reasons and aggregate response/unserved
danger, custody/force, evidence failures, complaints/outside inquiry, delay,
lawful release and received remedies in existing public-accounting cycles.
AA, independent source/PD authorities, opposition scrutiny, plural media and
ordinary courts get actual lawful access with narrow justified privacy/security
limits. Chief cannot monopolize the original records or certify its own
compliance. No arrest/conviction/forfeiture quota or predicted-crime leaderboard
can stand for safety, trust, discrimination or personal/political liberty.

**PO19 — Pay separate legal roles and sources.** Assembly/C06 appropriates
actual police, external inquiry and corrections duties plus existing qualified
care/court/defense/forensic/record/complaint/technical and transition capacity.
Use actual source ownership/currency and nonoverlapping skilled peaks, retained
staff/old claims and independent fallback. Fines, forfeiture, private sponsorship,
data sales, unbuilt AI or internal transfers are no assumed source. No pursuing
office retains a punishment bounty. Separate cost/source custody prevents a
Chief from withholding case money, but cannot itself prove Treasury/patron
independence. Missing paid capacity stays unmet/unknown, not acquittal, guilt,
an enlarged force power, a fictional officer or erased remedy.

**PO20 — Lawful succession cannot depend on a chief's password.** Election
changes general policy through existing law; fixed-term heads and career staff
retain only their lawful mandates, not power to veto the new government.
Expiry/removal ends reserved head acts; source records, equipment/keys/payroll,
valid case acts and accrued private/public claims pass to actual lawful
receivers under OP/T/DP. Protected records/status publishers and authorized
outside-conflict executors must have usable independent access for their actual
scope; no national master credential or military emergency substitute.
No expired chief may retain reserved acts through a deputy, consultant,
contractor or software account; a lawful successor
cannot rewrite old evidence. Failed actual handover is recorded and remedied,
never presumed successful from title changes or a court order alone.

[OJ49–OJ54](../25/11-report-to-investigation-and-review.md) joins report
receipt, expiring leads and review of non-action to actual protected
casework. Preserve PO's independent source and misconduct boundaries.
