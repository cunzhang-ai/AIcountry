# The first budget when water, care and existing housing commitments overlap

*Design305 · Constructed extension of the integrated first programme.*

Use the same dated envelopes, options and units as the
[protected correction case](05-protected-corrections-and-options.md).
Carry its ordinary correction R and the independent energy reserve K
within any energy option. Compare two further facts that could coexist
during a prolonged disruption. The case assumes a qualified path to
each additional continuing duty. It prices each separately from R and
K. No observed audit or other case supplies these quantities.

| Additional due work | National day 30 / day 90 / recurring | Local own day 30 / day 90 / recurring | Day 15 owned foreign cash | Additional peak slots |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Replacement supply for the ordinary public water service after a supplier delay | 2 / 2 / 0 | 0 / 0 / 0 | 1 | Already staffed independently of the optional logistics roster |
| One additional qualified home-care slot after local receiving fails | 1 / 2 / 0 | 1 / 1 / 0 | 0 | One care slot, distinct from the two in R |

The water authority and Treasury verify accepted goods, actual
settlement, licensed receiving and replenishment. The local care
operator confirms a qualified staffed slot and actual service before
the duty expires. Both receive under existing assumed appropriation
and lawful authority. These duties have separate sources and use
the applicable available cash of the same budgets. Election, court
and ordinary independent review capacity remains separately protected.

| Exact optional choice in the reference path | Water loss only | Care loss only | Both losses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary protected repair, no optional start | Fits | Fits | Fits |
| Energy bridge and paid care preparation with K | Fits | Fits | Short one early and one cumulative national unit |
| Energy bridge with K | Fits | Fits | Fits with no spare early national cash or peak administrative review |
| Paid care preparation | Fits | Fits | Fits, leaving the near-term energy increment unserved |
| Secure tenancies with voluntary support | Fits with actual advance | Short one care slot | Short one care slot |

After both losses the reference optional envelope is national
3 / 6 / 6, local own 2 / 5 / 5, foreign 1 and care / review /
utility logistics 1 / 2 / 2. The proposed exact choice is the finite
energy bridge with K, given the declared immediate harm if the
incremental energy support is absent. It consumes three early
national units and both peak review slots. This branch presumes
that the valid bridge-only option was separately approved, including
the reserve, and each actual receiver can accept it. The missed
training start and unoffered new tenancies are real consequences.
Without the exact vote or qualified route, return for decision on
a separate care-preparation option or ordinary repair.

Under the weaker envelope both additional costs can be assigned
cash on the stated dates, but the new care slot is missing. The
result leaves an uncovered continuing care duty and qualifies no
new start. The case still carries the water and other compatible
duties. Reopen actual staffing and protected finance before
calling the repair baseline fully delivered.

## If the three tenancies have already bound

Keep the signed leases and their due support in the baseline. A
new, separately qualified receiving provider can cover the single
missing care slot if accepted by the people involved and the
competent local operator. In the reference, one additional early
national unit, included in day 90, and one local unit by day 90
fund it. It adds one net care slot with qualified paid replacement,
without any double use of R or diverted court and election capacity.
With actual national advance and accepted support, the leases and
both continuing corrections fit. There is no remaining early
national cash for either optional energy or training start.

If the housing advance is unavailable on the first date, the
binding four-unit gross local housing payment has two units of
own cash and remains two short. Replacement staffing does not
settle that payment. If the alternate care slot is missing, the
related support remains uncovered even with timely cash. A later
advance or acceptance cannot restore timely service retrospectively.
