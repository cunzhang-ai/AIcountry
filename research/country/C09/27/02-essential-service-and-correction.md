# CBG05–CBG08. Essential service and correction

[Climate budgets](../27-climate-budgets-and-essential-continuity.md)

**CBG05 — Commit to received service and measured reduction together.**
Cabinet presents a funded sequence covering grid, storage,
generation, buildings, transport, industry, agriculture and
affected services. Compare retain and repair, lower demand
with usable alternatives, regulated investment and other feasible
packages at the same essential needs and environmental scope.
For retirement or conversion, identify the last safe start,
actual firm receiving path, crew, fuel or power, connection,
spare capacity, end date and paid backup. Test common failure
under JC and the relevant sector law. Count an emission cut
only within its actual delivery and inventory scope. Declared
installed capacity, a tax proposal or a forecast cannot supply
a missing firm megawatt.

**CBG06 — Trigger correction before a gap becomes unavoidable.**
A measured breach, credible forecast exceedance or material
loss of a necessary firm service path triggers the responsible
environmental and sector officers to notify Cabinet, the
Assembly and the independent evidence route. Preserve the
first qualifying date. Cabinet publishes costed alternatives
within 30 calendar days, sooner at the last safe start or
earlier applicable time. Show remaining headroom, uncertainty,
unmet needs and who bears each loss. Act on ordinary sector
duties and seek needed lawful finance in parallel. An unavailable
alternative remains visible. Its absence is neither a reason
to close the case nor a grant of exceptional power.

**CBG07 — Protect urgent needs under actual law.** An operator can
perform a necessary immediate act only within applicable power,
safety or emergency authority. Any climate-law accommodation
for that act needs an express prior statutory ground, defined
scope, original necessity, earliest end, review and recorded
emissions. This proposal itself grants no operation, extension,
rationing or immunity. All resulting emissions count. If they
cross a ceiling, record the overrun separately from a finding
about legality or fault. Protect timely safe receiving and
applicable AQ, water, habitat, worker and household duties.
Avoid an irreversible normal retirement based on a future
substitute that has not been accepted.

**CBG08 — Make correction an actual elected choice.** The Clerk uses
existing DB, PE and protected agenda rules to support real
alternatives and a recorded Assembly disposition within 60
calendar days of the trigger, sooner where useful time or law
requires. Lawful vote and sitting requirements continue.
Choose a funded correction or consider an exact prospective
primary-law revision with its physical, rights, cost and treaty
effects. Compare the same observed emissions against old and
new paths. A missed vote supplies neither approval nor authority.
Treasury, the grid operator and an AI cannot revise a ceiling,
appropriate funds or set national emergency priorities.

**Compare safe lower demand with firm backup.** Before irreversible retirement, verify the required service for the same households and sites through the next adverse period. Count the backup’s gross emissions and other physical limits. Count demand reductions only when usable warmth, care and access remain protected. Handle a lawful urgent response and the cumulative correction on their original legal clocks.
