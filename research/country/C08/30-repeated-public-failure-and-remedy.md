# C08. Make public remedies reach people and prevent repeat harm

*Design268 core, Design301 conflict procedure and Design445 residual loss · SR01–SR32 · Proposed ordinary public-law remedies.*

**Enforce the person's order and, when the same unlawful cause will keep
harming people, require a bounded public repair.** The responsible agency
proposes and executes lawful means. An independent court can require and
test an effective remedy within its jurisdiction. The elected Assembly
keeps legislative and fiscal choice. Individual relief proceeds during
any wider repair and does not depend on its completion.

This specifies implementation within [B19–B20](13/05-orders-execution-and-collective-claims.md)
and [ordinary justice](25-ordinary-justice-and-public-prosecution.md).
It requires competent prospective adoption. It gives no court power
to enact a tax, choose a supplier or assume executive management.
