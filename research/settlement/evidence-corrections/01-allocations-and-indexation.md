# Worked decisions after a public evidence correction

All amounts and actors below are illustrative. These are counterfactual decisions
under the proposed rules. They do not estimate English schools' losses.

## A fixed school envelope and a broken forecast

A national school programme has 100 units of appropriation and cash for ordinary
allocations. Its published indicative schedule was 68 for A and 42 for B. A
boundary code omitted pupils from an affordability calculation. An independently
checked calculation with all pupils yields an authorized final split of 60 and
40. No indicative grant was paid. The notice was specific enough for A to rely
on it in making six units of otherwise eligible commitments. Two can be
cancelled lawfully and one unit of the remaining cost is independently covered
by another existing source outside the schedule. A's residual verified cost is
three. B shows no unavoidable new cost in this example. Both schools and their
families retain ordinary routes to show omissions, actual disruption and costs.

| Decision under the stated facts | School allocation | Other funding required | Uncovered evidenced reliance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Final split with ordinary appeal only | 60 + 40 | 0 | 3 at A |
| Honor the whole indicative schedule | 68 + 42 | 10 | 0 if that funding can lawfully cover the loss |
| Final split plus a targeted continuity bridge | 60 + 40 | 3 | 0 after A actually receives 3 |

The gross new authorization for a selected bridge is three. It needs a named
lawful purpose, cash and competent payer before the next useful time. Otherwise
the 100 allocation remains and the three stays exposed. A's wages or valid
contracts come due under their own law even before a bridge decision. The bridge
cannot reuse B's ordinary 40, promise the same unit twice or infer the existing
source is available until confirmed. If A qualifies for later reimbursement for
the same cost, reconcile it under the bridge terms and law. A payroll advance
requires separate authorization if needed and is reconciled against any later
payment for the same liability. If the cohort boundary omitted students in
other programmes, the responsible bodies must separately check those uses.

## Two indexation statutes with the same corrected observation

A first published growth rate is 2% and the corrected estimate is 3%. For a
100-unit base, a statute fixing the first published rate gives a due amount of
102, even while evidence on the historical error remains visible. A separate
statute using the authenticated correction for the relevant period gives 103.
If its payer sent 102, the one-unit balance remains due until received. For a
downward correction, apply the actual recovery, notice and good-faith protection
rules before attempting any collection. The source's warning cannot decide
either legal result. A later method improvement may also be statistically useful
while the legal replacement series still awaits prospective authorization.

## The office knew before the formal notice

On day 0 the school programme verifies a credible mismatch through its own
staff. Payroll is due on day 4 and the final transfer on day 10. The source
office sends a warning on day 9 and the vendor resends it on day 12. The
programme's acknowledgement is due by day 7 and its ordinary disposition
or reviewable plan by day 30 from day 0. The payroll protection assessment
needs to happen before day 4, and any safe lawful transfer decision before
day 10. A fresh message cannot restart the clock. The programme makes the
already authorized, independently verified part of the transfer and seeks any
additional lawful protection in time. A missed payroll remains a missed due
time even if the error is acknowledged later. A family may go to independent
review when the office is silent. If the independent route is unavailable,
record the unresolved remedy failure and use any existing lawful alternate.
An unanswered notice supplies no cash and does not count as receiving.
