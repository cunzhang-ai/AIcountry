# Receiving reports, investigating facts and limiting custody

*P06–P10; part of [C08 criminal core](../12-criminal-core-and-procedure.md).*

**P06 — Receive and preserve without a credibility gate.** Human, phone,
accessible remote and safe third-party intake receive an account without an
AI score, identity account or complete dossier. Assign a responsible receiver,
safe contact and protected receipt. Immediate clinical/shelter/support duties
follow need; investigative and temporary protection decisions use their own
legal grounds. Preserve relevant source evidence proportionately, with safe
access/retention. Referral ends the first receiver's triage responsibility only
when a competent successor accepts it. Closing/noncharge decisions give safe,
reviewable reasons; uninvolved prosecution reviews closure and a court can
review legality. Neither can order conviction. Unproved, withdrawn or mistaken
reports do not establish knowing fabrication or a reporter's offense.

**P07 — Investigate under specific powers.** Police collect inculpatory and
contrary evidence. Search of homes, devices, communications and similarly
private records needs a particularized independent judicial warrant stating
scope, purpose, objects, duration and lawful data use. No fishing warrant
follows an alleged public-order offense or score. The only warrant exception
here is an immediate life threat or imminent destruction of specified material
evidence when obtaining the warrant is impracticable: record necessity,
minimum scope and actual time, and obtain judicial review as soon as possible,
no later than 24 hours. It is not a retrospective warrant or standing exception.
Retention, continuing surveillance and further access require their own prior
lawful authority. Clinics and support providers do not become default criminal
record suppliers. Official-abuse evidence uses the separate outside-command
route; changing actor or vendor cannot create a power.

**P08 — Counsel and questioning.** Explain grounds, silence, counsel and
interpretation in usable form at actual first restraint and before questioning.
Provide independent defense at no cost whenever liberty, criminal charge or
coercive interrogation is at stake; people may choose other qualified counsel.
Allow confidential access without prosecutor control, with chosen-person
notice and independent medical care. Counsel delay permits no merits
questioning; strictly necessary nonincriminating questions for immediate
rescue must be recorded, never used to evade defense. An accused can obtain
independent technical assistance under P11. Neither silence, requesting advice
nor refusing optional mediation creates an adverse inference of guilt.

**P09 — Arrest and first liberty hearing.** Arrest requires reasonable
particularized grounds for a published offense and a stated necessity that
cannot be met by summons or a less restrictive route. Original custody time
starts when the person is actually not free to leave, whatever uniform,
facility, contractor, hospital or software label is used. Explain grounds and
record the custodian, location and notice; no secret detention is lawful.
A competent independent judge hears the person with effective defense as soon
as possible and within **24 continuous hours**. In-person presentation is the
default with confidential counsel and inquiry into injuries. Any lawfully
allowed remote option needs equivalent safeguards and a usable request for
in-person review; transmission failure creates no extension. No police/prosecutor extension
is authorized by this candidate. Release earlier when the ground ends; failure
to obtain a lawful timely order requires release, not custody by silence.
The 24-hour ceiling is no entitlement to hold someone that long. A claim for
immediate liberty review is directly accessible at any time. Child cases
receive priority and the same hard outer limit; shorter applicable law prevails.

**P10 — Remand is an exceptional fresh decision.** Prosecution shows concrete
necessity to prevent flight, witness/evidence interference or serious imminent
unlawful harm, with sufficient offense grounds and consideration of alternatives.
A charge, seriousness label, homelessness, poverty or prediction alone is not
enough. No money-bail requirement makes freedom depend on wealth. Narrow,
funded, reviewable conditions cannot require an unavailable address, unpaid
carer or compulsory therapy. The judge gives reasons and sets expiry: first
order at most seven days, each fresh renewal at most 14 days. Defense can
challenge earlier. Aggregate pretrial deprivation for the same factual episode
is at most **90 days**, or **180** for an alleged offense with a statutory maximum
above ten years after individualized grave-case reasons. Count original
restraint, all transfers and relabelled compulsory holds; do not reset through
new counts about the same episode. Actual future independent crimes require
fresh evidence and cannot fictionalize that episode's continuation.

Expiry without a new lawful timely order requires actual release. Resource
shortage, adjournment or unfitness does not extend the aggregate cap. The case
may continue with a lawful fair trial and less restrictive protection; custody
cannot become anticipatory punishment. Separate imminent-danger rescue or
protection cannot be serially used to defeat the same liberty ceiling. All
new numbers are candidate values requiring measured docket, geography and
capacity review. They do not revise actual existing law or demonstrate safety.

[CD09–CD10](../21/05-criminal-process-and-clocks.md) distinguish independently
justified conflict-status custody from actual criminal-use restraint. Ordinary
P limits remain; POW criminal remand uses the shortest applicable P aggregate,
90 actual days and three calendar months. Each actual ground, original clock,
credit and expiry needs independent proof; no military label resets the case.

[IL01–IL16 assessment](../../C10/10-civilian-assessment-and-intelligence-limits.md) now distinguishes civilian warnings from this criminal process. No NAU task/PD25 notice order, foreign warning or ministerial emergency can invoke P07 without its actual source grounds/actor; a prospective legacy transfer does not manufacture an offense, warrant or new custody clock.

**Design144 declaration boundary.** [RX](../../C10/01-public-safety-and-emergencies.md)
selects no domestic constitutional derogation. Its14/30/90 declared-authority
limits are not P09 custody periods or hearing extensions. A general declaration,
CJ case, new officer or missing source does not supply arrest grounds, timely
remand, secret detention or continued custody by silence; actual earlier limits win.

**Design147 health source.** HP's distinct ordinary civil health restraint has
its own original24-hour hearing/release rule and proof; suspected contagion is
not a published offense or P09 arrest predicate. No new HP status/noncompliance
crime or punitive contempt detention. An actual independent crime still needs
P/K grounds, evidence/defense and original clocks; neither label resets actual
related restraint or supplies bodily test/treatment authority.

[OJ49–OJ54](../25/11-report-to-investigation-and-review.md) carries P06
through actual evidence deadlines, inaction and paid independent review.
P07 warrants, defense rights and original liberty and limitation clocks
keep their existing legal scope.

[CA01–CA07](06-court-appearance-and-pretrial-liberty.md) requires actual
access and independent findings for an absence warrant. Actual arrest
starts P09, and continued custody needs separate P10 grounds and clocks.
