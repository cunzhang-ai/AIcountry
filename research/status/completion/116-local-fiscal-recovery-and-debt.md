# Scoped finding. Local services can survive a fiscal failure without losing elected control

Design208 separates late transfers, continuing service gaps and
unsustainable eligible debt. It assigns early notice, legally funded
continuity and a narrow court-supervised future debt adjustment with
local and creditor votes and useful remedies.
[Canonical clauses](../../country/C04/10-local-fiscal-recovery-and-debt.md)

Same-year national and local fiscal observations and distinct legal
comparators inform the choice. The two-locality case tests different
responses and the prospective class vote. Actual costs, payment,
adoption and outcomes remain unverified. [Comparison](../../settlement/local-fiscal-recovery/README.md)
