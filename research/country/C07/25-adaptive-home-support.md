# C07. Adaptive home support with a protected service floor

*Design266 core and Design289 voluntary care · HS01–HS18 · Proposed national and local arrangements.*

The existing care authority owes adequate support for daily life under S09.
Select a care plan that guarantees the person's necessary support and allows
a known team and the person to adapt its delivery as life changes. The person
can choose a more specified plan or person-directed assistance. The public
authority retains the duty to pay, decide disputed needs and arrange delivery
when a provider fails.

| Canonical part | Choice |
|---|---|
| [HS01–HS04. Individual plan and authority to adapt](25/01-floor-choice-and-live-changes.md) | Guaranteed useful support, recipient and worker control, rapid provisional increases, independent review of cuts |
| [HS05–HS08. Funding, delivery and public correction](25/02-paid-capacity-and-accountability.md) | Real recurring capacity, risk allocation, continuity and review across providers and over time |
| [HS09–HS13. Home nursing and protected demand](25/03-home-nursing-and-protected-demand.md) | Clinical scope, funded capacity and protected service through staged transfer and operating change |
| [HS14–HS18. Voluntary care and paid continuity](25/04-voluntary-care-and-paid-continuity.md) | Separate choice, booked respite, concurrent withdrawal and paid replacement |

[The comparative case](../../settlement/adaptive-home-support/README.md)
tests this choice against a strong Danish retain-and-repair comparator,
more specified task authorizations and individual budgets. No age threshold,
new clinical power or universal bundled contract is selected. The proposed
right turns on assessed need and actual receipt. Costs, safe deployment and
comparative improvement remain to be established.
