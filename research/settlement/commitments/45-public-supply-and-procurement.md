Design 132 · [SC01–SC20](../../country/C04/05-public-buying-and-continuous-supply.md)

All 37 original complete function/bid/actual-null objects, 61 selections and 71
inherited references remain exact. Procurement is not a new sovereign function
with a free bid; it adds demands across existing buyers, source and remedy owners.

The 66 current purchase rows are mapped without replacing or adding them; none
becomes a qualified current contract, source or producer payroll by the map.
The 13 nonfood reference rows likewise create no actual demand, output or tax.
Publication/evaluation/court/continuity/record and transition costs remain
unpriced. A material 5M threshold is a proposed procedural trigger, not a price,
funding cap, excuse to split need or limit on any due rights.

Existing contracts, paid wages/pensions, deposited/customer property, creditors,
licenses/title, pending challenges, tax years, private/foreign counterparties and
safe correction history survive through paid OP/T successors. No retrospective
canceling, blacklist, contract seizure, unpaid workforce or sweep of private FX.
New procurement commitments require actual source/competent authority; old due
services and useful-time remedies do not wait for a new tender or blank annex.

Common method, actual constitution/software/contribution permissions and holds
remain unchanged. Proposed 12-month commencement needs express primary law and
funded receiving, without enlarging current executive power. A continuity
purchase does not bypass a court hold, extend an incumbent or replenish its own
limit by renewal. Legal authority, qualified independent capacity, received
remedies and better lives remain unknown; final reviews never transfer merely
because a name, model or input hash is unchanged.
