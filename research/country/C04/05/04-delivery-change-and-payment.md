# SC13–SC16: a contract remains answerable after signature

**SC13 — Do not replace competition through amendments.** Publish options and
objective price/FX adjustment rules before award. An unforeseen new function,
material scope/term expansion, changed controller or cumulative discretionary
price increase above **10% of the original gross ceiling** requires fresh
competition/authority unless an actually applicable SC12 exception permits a
bounded temporary purchase. An originally disclosed mechanical index adjustment
is not that discretionary increase, but still needs actual lawful source and
accounting. Smaller changes also re-compete if they would have changed eligible
bidders or the result. Count related changes cumulatively, not each separately.
No contractor buys a general future monopoly or new sovereign power by variation.

**SC14 — Verify delivery, preserve useful exit and pay lawful dues.** Name the
qualified verifier, evidence, defect/safety tests, stock/maintenance, milestones,
custody/data/keys, paid handover and receiving operator. Physical acceptance,
invoice approval and actual service receipt are different events. An AI flag,
invoice, title certificate or expiry of time is not clinical/safety acceptance.
The buyer acknowledges a valid invoice within five working days and identifies
specific disputed portions within ten; qualified acceptance plus no evidenced
payment dispute permits processing, not invented physical receipt. Pay the
undisputed accepted portion within **20 working days** of valid invoice and
qualified acceptance, whichever is later. Prior faster due terms survive.
Use existing C08 contract/default-interest and actual source/remedy rules; no
new unpriced interest rate, unilateral retrospective price cut or tax sweep.

**SC15 — Keep failure losses and service duties distinct.** Supplier working
capital, unpaid pay, deposits, intellectual-property use, asset title, tax,
creditors and remedies retain their owners under WL/OE/NF/insolvency. Unreceived
recoveries are not successor funding. Service authority must trace a paid,
qualified receiving route; step-in uses actual lawful contractual access or
separate legal power, not free seizure of a private business. Lack of safe
replacement remains an unmet public duty. Courts, payers and existing executors
preserve useful-time correction; they cannot create an appropriation or stock.

**SC16 — Exclude proven serious wrong, not opponents.** Eligibility decisions
use the disclosed task-relevant competence/source criteria, reasons and urgent
review. Wider punitive exclusion from new public contracts requires an ordinary
independent civil-court order for proved intentional corruption, fraud, unlawful
collusion or grave deliberate contract abuse, with notice/defense, proportionality
and a maximum **two years** under this candidate. Actual changed controllers,
remediation and affected scope matter; no automatic family/group guilt or
perpetual blacklist. Existing criminal/competition law keeps its own elements,
proof and penalties. Honest failure, one bid, insolvency, an AI risk score,
criticism or lawful refusal of AI is not guilt. A separate evidenced safety
failure can defeat a specific offer without waiting for punishment, with review.

**Design146 official-media buying.** [PM16](../../C11/08/03-funding-reach-and-future-media.md)
requires objective actual audience/access/cost grounds, published awards and
quarterly issuer/recipient/control/gross-spend records within30calendar days
after quarter end for national/local official
advertising/notices outside the public Service. SC owns actual source/award/remedy;
no editorial favor, forced positive story, new press licence or universal ad ban.

[DP25–DP32](../../C11/09/06-procure-a-usable-exit.md) qualifies digital or AI
exit milestones before reliance. Preserve current SC competition, due
accepted payments, permitted rights and actual successor capacity.

[PL09–PL12 concessions](../../C06/64/03-change-insolvency-and-handback.md)
adds the elected reauthorization, independently valued termination and
verified handback for material long commitments. SC competition and
earned-payment clocks continue, including through qualified substitutes.

[SC44–SC49](11-essential-contract-costs-and-paid-continuity.md) applies
SC13 to disclosed, justified component adjustments and pooled exposure.
The lawful threshold, challenge and original SC14 payment clock continue.
