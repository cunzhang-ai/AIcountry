# Design371. Match first-budget coverage for housing and warmth

[WF105–WF110](../../country/C06/58/24-housing-warmth-and-budget-coverage.md)
keeps direct actual-need entry, common populations, feasible overlap
ranges and separately funded housing and warmth receipts.

The [official-data comparison](../../settlement/housing-warmth-and-budget-coverage/01-evidence-and-first-budget-choice.md)
reproduces Denmark and EU-27 observations, tests a poverty-only gate
and shows a constructed capacity limit that straddles the coverage range.
It supports a scoped budget rule. Actual qualified delivery, participation,
lawful adoption, costs and outcomes remain open.
