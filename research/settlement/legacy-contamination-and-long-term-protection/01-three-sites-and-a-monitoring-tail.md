# Three sites and a monitoring tail

Design363 · Constructed comparison under
[CLN09–CLN14](../../country/C09/21/03-legacy-contamination-and-long-term-protection.md).

No collectible operator or assurance is available in this example.
Site A needs urgent safe-water delivery costing 6 in period one, then
2 each in periods two and three for continuing protection and independent
verification. Site B already has a lawful restriction and needs 2 in
period one, then 1 each for maintenance and monitoring. At site C
the currently affected scope is uncertain. An investigation costs 2
in period one. Elective redevelopment at C would use another 6 in
that period. Do not assume any land proceeds. All amounts are
constructed budget units and give no physical treatment instructions.

| Choice | P1 use | P2 use | P3 use |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Available legacy finance | 10 | 7 | 4 |
| Keep A and B safe and investigate C in P1 | 10 | 3 | 3 |
| Add the independently assessed C preventive stage in P2 and its monitoring in P3 | 10 | 7 | 4 |
| Choose visible redevelopment first, retain B and investigate C | 10 | Unpriced | Unpriced |

In the last row the P1 budget is spent while the six-unit A duty
remains uncovered. No obligation disappears. The matched selection
funds A, B and investigation first. If the investigation supports
the C preventive option, it can be chosen only on the actual
four-unit P2 source, one-unit P3 follow-up and qualified receiving
assumed in the third row.

If P2 financing drops from 7 to 5, A and B still require 3 and
the C stage has only 2 left against its cost of 4. If it remains
an unbound preventive option, hold it while verifying the next
useful date. If new evidence instead makes the same work due,
the two-unit shortfall becomes a disclosed unmet duty. Seek a
lawful protection and funding route without cancelling it.
A promised two-unit recovery that settles in P3 cannot pay the
P2 work on time. It also cannot be used twice.

The model assumes that qualified operators and inspectors can
accept each funded task by its date. If they cannot, the cash
comparison fails to establish service. It prices no later real
monitoring tail. All continuing protection and restricted use
beyond P3 need their own future schedule.
