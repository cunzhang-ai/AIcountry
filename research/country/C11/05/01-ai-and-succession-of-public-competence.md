# HC05–HC10. Keep a public service able to train its own successors

*Design369 · Part of [retained human capability](../05-retained-human-capability.md) · Proposed research law.*

**HC05 — Identify the work that produces future judgment.** Before a public
operator materially removes, automates or outsources tasks that develop a
competence needed for a public duty, document how current workers learned it
and how future workers can acquire it. Apply the same rule to consolidation
or downsizing without AI. Include competence to decide, investigate
exceptions, disagree with an automated recommendation, hear challenges,
operate an independent route and teach the next cohort. Measure actual
learning and receiving under HC01–HC04. Work that merely occupies junior
workers can go. Preserve a demonstrable path to competence when the work
that once produced it changes. The condition applies to contractors where
the public offer depends on them, through their lawful terms.

**HC06 — Compare the whole replacement interval.** The sector employer
proposes a continuing route, a bounded assisted route that preserves paid
practice, and where feasible a different way to develop or obtain the skill.
Show competent workers and instructors already available, date and scope
of expected departures, supervised capacity, likely noncompletion, time to
qualification, accepted independent review and actual paid assignments.
Use the longer time needed to replace each bottleneck, including a mentor
or examiner if the transition depends on one. Show one realistic combined
loss of ordinary and backup availability. A certificate, course seat,
vacancy or vendor help desk counts only for its actual scope. Disclose
ranges rather than assuming that every person trains, qualifies and stays.

**HC07 — Fund practice without taking it from due service.** The elected
budget owner funds necessary teaching, learner pay, protected practice,
assessment, relief, accessible learning and any origin or receiving cover.
The sector employer assigns duties and bargains under ordinary labor law.
The professional or statutory qualification body verifies competence
independently of the vendor and of the procurement's savings target.
Use real supervised work where safe or a qualified accessible simulation.
Keep necessary assistive tools and test against unfamiliar cases without
the material derivation at issue. Learners never supply unqualified
signatures or fill independent-review posts. A mentor, alternate officer
and duty reviewer may share one paid person only when the uses are
compatible and actual time is counted once. Cross-service training
compacts must identify the payer, provider, access, origin cover and
receiving obligations. They cannot require workers to stay.

**HC08 — Make the staffing decision separately from the tool decision.**
A sector can authorize a bounded assistive use on its own supported merits
and existing terms. Before an irreversible reduction of staffing or learning
capacity on the strength of it, obtain independent evidence through one
actual replacement cycle, or equivalent demonstrated capacity at the same
task, scale, deadlines and independence. Bring pay, workforce structure and
recurring budget changes to their respective lawful decision-makers.
Compare observed gains with all costs including successor formation and
shared-route demand. Do not book durable savings or replace an operative
fallback based on faster assisted work alone. Keep a paid bridge when the
competence horizon extends beyond the budget or contract. The elected
budget may choose another qualified design. It cannot deem a competence,
independent decision or continuing duty delivered.

**HC09 — Intervene before an uncovered date.** At each material release,
compare actual departures and qualification results with the next protected
duty, independent review and training windows. Recheck when enrollment,
mentors, licensing, source access or learning quality deteriorates, even
while assisted service indicators improve. If the next route cannot qualify,
hold only the unsupported expansion, release of reserve or spending
reduction under applicable law. Keep due services and enforceable claims
on their original terms. Activate separately funded substitute coverage,
retain or buy qualified overlap, or return an exposed duty to the competent
elected process under DB, JC and the sector's remedies. A shortage does
not create a licence, waive a review or authorize required unpaid work.

**HC10 — Keep succession portable and contestable.** Preserve original
case records under their lawful owners, usable nonpersonal learning
materials and independent access, and accepted evidence of competence
when a supplier or government changes. Affected workers can challenge
inaccessible training, unsafe practice or an improper assessment through
existing routes. Publish cohort and service results in safe aggregate
form and describe exclusions, failure cases, dependence and actual
spending. AI may help design exercises and estimate demand and
coverage. It cannot license, assess a worker adversely by itself or
certify independence from its own supplier. Reopen on uncovered cohorts,
shared assessors, lost placements, deteriorating judgment or a
demonstrably better way to sustain the duty.
