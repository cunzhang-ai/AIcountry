*Part of [C08/25](../25-ordinary-justice-and-public-prosecution.md); design120 candidate.*

**OJ36 — Competent prospective coordinated law.** OJ/CJ/Assembly/Prosecution/
Court Service/C04/B/P/Treasury law starts new ordinary OJ duties 180 calendar
days after competent enactment, with actual authorized selection before any new
post acts. The joint founding exception requires the proper constitutional/
predecessor procedure and legitimate founding public; neither selectors nor
first judges approve themselves or operate CJ12 before its adoption. Preserve
existing valid appointments/tenure/retirement, staff/accrual/pension/contract,
cases/records/claim dates, defense/appeal rights, judgments and particular
foreign/privacy/source rights. Exact enacted successors and paid overlap are
needed; lawful authorized restructuring remains possible with review/protection,
not every incumbent's veto or a clerical purge. New discipline applies only in
its lawful prospective/source scope; no retroactive new crime or revived bar.
Old detention/election/useful-time duties never wait for an OJ readiness certificate.

**OJ38 — Select a hypothesis, retain genuine rivals.** Select protected professional
ordinary courts, half-judge mixed appointment with public/practitioner participation,
Assembly-qualified DPP and preselected outside-command case officers. Direct
executive appointment with effective courts may be simpler but risks career/case
pressure. Peer-majority self-government may protect legal independence while
entrenching professional exclusion. Elected judges/prosecutors or lay juries can
bring public voice but need actual money/independence, fair proof and safeguards
against popularity/wealth. Shared/visiting or external-jurisdiction service can
reduce fixed burden only with lawful competence, actual consent and source.
Full alternatives may displace the selection, not become labels in a new council.
12/8, seven/six years,75 and qualification periods are authored choices, not proven
optima. More bodies, vetoes or faster procedure are not evidence of better lives.

**OJ39 — Protectors remain accountable.** Judges, DPP, selector members/case officers
cannot retain incompatible operational political/paid litigant interests; ordinary
judges also use OJ22's four-year leadership cooling rule. Existing actual protected
rights receive OJ36 transition. A prior profession/client, diffuse interest or
lawful opinion is disclosed/evaluated, not automatic personal guilt. Inspect
appointment patrons, career incentives, budget, source custody, instructions,
actual case allocation and execution together. No list of accounts, qualified
source label or shared model verifies independence. Disclose exclusions, costs,
failed protection and safe participant objections, including people unable to
participate/exit. Do not make punishment counts or nominal rights the outcome.
