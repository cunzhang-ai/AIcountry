# PO29–PO32. Act before a shortfall reaches the next lawful deadline

*Design214 · Part of [C08 civilian enforcement](../27-civilian-enforcement-and-public-control.md).*

**PO29 — Escalate a credible projected loss in useful time.** The
warden promptly alerts the corrections director when actual staffing,
conditions or receiving can no longer support the next required
placements. For threatened lawful access, release, humane conditions
or staff safety, use the existing direct court, clinical, labor and
inspector routes. Notify through a route outside the affected operator
where necessary, and early enough to permit useful relief. Keep safe
interim protection and the original clocks. Alert defense counsel and
other required persons through lawful safe channels where a case is
affected. A lack of evidence about particular placements is disclosed
as unresolved. No national average overrides a local condition.

**PO30 — Buy and test compatible relief before counting it.** Use
actual authorized civilian relief, safe legally permitted transfers
accepted by the receiving authority, and any individual alternative
available through the competent court under existing law. Record
acceptance, transport, staff, health and communication, accountable
payer, useful time and remaining duties. Test simultaneous demand and
the resources a transfer removes from another place. A duty holder
may move a person only on the applicable case and placement grounds.
No use of police cells, clinical wards, compulsory overtime or another
jurisdiction as a default overflow follows. Apply CD and the separate
foreign, defense and consent rules where applicable. A person may
challenge an unsafe placement or unlawful detention and receive
actual correction under existing authority.

**PO31 — Keep unmet claims visible and return the general tradeoff to
elected control.** If suitable relief cannot meet a due obligation,
record the gap and seek independent immediate protection. Execute due
release and report missing help, following RL and local receiving
law. Maintain lawful victim protection, notice and support. No staffing
dispute, shortage or missed hearing supplies new detention authority.
The Assembly receives the general consequences, funded alternatives,
and losses under existing GP, FB and DB routes. It can fund earlier
qualification, repair, different lawful provision or a prospective change
in general law. Neither the legislature nor a corrections manager can
order an individual verdict or rewrite a case's dates. Independent
inspection tests humane conditions and work with safe access to
prisoners, workers, original records and courts.

**PO32 — Compare real safety and freedom across remedies.** Retain
existing candidate sentencing and review grounds. Select verified
rest-compliant staffing, dated operational repair and usable receiving
before adding new reliance on the affected pathway. Retain expansions
where lawful demand and accepted resources justify them. Compare safe
retained sites, lawful negotiated rosters with verified rest, more
stable staffing, and qualified community provision without widening
control. Track actual missed care or hearings, paid overtime and rest,
worker and prisoner safety, victim protection and local receipt.
AI can support reconciliations and scenario comparison under DP, with
independent source access and an alternate. Keep case decisions and
independent review outside the planning tool. New funding and scope
need lawful T commencement and paid successors. Reopen after changed
demand, later compliance evidence, lost relief or a better safe rival.
