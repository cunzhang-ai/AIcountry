# Production, distribution and matched counterpart accounts

*Part of [C06 national package](../20-national-economic-package.md); design 59 research.*

Cabinet and sector/local authorities must expose a proposed economic
package's outputs, prices, household needs, public services, maintenance,
investment, wage/income distribution, imports and exports before treating
a tax base or social promise as supported. The evidence office records
original method and uncertainty; it does not choose economic goals.
GDP is an activity account, not wellbeing, political authority or available
cash. Distinct rights, distribution, harms abroad and future limits remain
in C14's public-conditions and C06 commitment accounts.

Use consistent income and expenditure classifications. Market-price GDP
cannot be divided only into wages and “profit”: relevant production/product
taxes and capital consumption matter. Gross operating surplus is not all
taxable or distributable net income. Separate public wage and purchased-input
cash from noncash public depreciation in nonmarket output; account for
replacement investment once, with its real funding and assets.
One wage receipt cannot be added again as a second public wage bill or
unpaid family service. This account does not value every relationship or
force private and informal cooperation into a government transaction form.

Every financial flow needs a payer, recipient, source, period/currency,
lawful mandate, due date and payment state. Private support moves existing
resources between persons; taxes/transfers redistribute, not create GDP.
Keep households, producers, government, insurance and foreign parties
distinct. The [opening-book comparator](../21/02-assets-claims-and-funding.md) adds
producer/bank/monetary subledgers; actual bank and pension balance sheets
remain unverified. Five aggregate sectors do not complete them.
Government and controlled enterprises require both separate mandate accounts
and scoped consolidation: remove matching internal transfers, rather than
calling the same tax/dividend two external resources. Sector/control
classification depends on actual authority, not one share percentage.

An identity can close **planned** accounts while wages, benefits, interest
and suppliers remain unpaid. Do not label planned saving as cash available
to lend the government. State funded debt, lawful but unpaid obligations,
actual lender commitments, cash draw and outstanding remedy separately.
Never invent a loan to make a calculation close or assign a shortage to
particular claimants through an economist's balancing entry. Individual
priority and new burdens need competent law and independent remedies.

Reconcile the public account to household, firm and external stocks and
flows, including lifecycle maintenance and record/worker continuity. Unknown
output, supply, tax base, controller or liability stays unknown. A new
assumption is identified as a hypothesis; earlier aggregate studies do not
automatically approve a changed economic package.
