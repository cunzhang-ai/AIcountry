# W01–W14: protected people, care and essential relief

*Closed act schedule under [G04](../03-war-crimes.md); not an operational authorization.*

All rows require G04 conflict/nexus/protection and the stated actual fault.
G08/ordinary P defenses and prosecution burdens remain. The context is not
inferred merely from the accused's uniform or a civilian's nationality.

| ID | Prohibited act and boundary |
|---|---|
| W01 | Intentionally kill a protected person not lawfully targetable, including a civilian not directly participating or a person in custody/hors de combat; prove status/knowledge, actual causation and absence of justification |
| W02 | Torture, cruel/inhumane treatment or an intentional grave outrage upon personal dignity of a protected person; severe suffering/humiliation needs actual proof, not mere offense or lawful safety care |
| W03 | Intentionally cause serious protected-person injury, unlawful mutilation or medical/scientific intervention not justified by treatment and consent/protection law, causing death or seriously endangering health; identify the actual intervention and duty |
| W04 | Unlawfully confine, deport or transfer a protected person/population under applicable IHL; a validly protective movement differs from forcible displacement, and changing custodian never resets actual deprivation |
| W05 | Seize/hold a protected person and threaten death, injury or continuing detention to compel a state/group/person's conduct as a condition of release/safety; lawful detention alone is not hostage-taking |
| W06 | Intentionally impose/execute a penal sentence on a protected person without a regularly constituted independent court and indispensable fair-trial guarantees; prove the deprivation and actual denial, not a mere unpopular judgment |
| W07 | Rape, sexual slavery, forced prostitution, forced sterilization, comparably grave sexual violence, or forced-pregnancy confinement/nonconsensual abortion meeting G03's explicit material definition against a protected person; no marriage/order excuses coercion or removes lawful bodily choice |
| W08 | Conscript/enlist a child under 15 into armed forces/groups or use them actively in hostilities, knowing or under the actual circumstances having reason to know the age; prove age and the material recruitment/use. This minimum differs from G08's separate national 18 recruitment rule |
| W09 | Order coercive displacement of civilians for conflict reasons without their safety or imperative military necessity under IHL; required care, accommodation and safe continuity remain duties, not an excuse to expel at will |
| W10 | Intentionally use starvation of civilians as warfare by depriving indispensable survival objects or unlawfully impeding necessary relief; distinguish a genuine uncontrollable shortage from intentional deprivation/control |
| W11 | Intentionally use a protected person's presence to shield military objectives/operations from attack; a shared civilian/military locality alone is insufficient and never removes the attacking side's independent civilian duties |
| W12 | Intentionally direct an attack against civilians as such or individual civilians not directly participating in hostilities; a risk classifier or asserted association is not an actual targetable role |
| W13 | Intentionally attack protected civilian objects, including medical, educational, cultural, religious/charitable sites or undefended places, knowing they are not military objectives; assess actual status/precautions rather than treating a building label as conclusive either way |
| W14 | Intentionally attack humanitarian/peacekeeping personnel, units, vehicles or material actually entitled to civilian protection, or protected medical units/transports/personnel using lawful distinctive emblems; prove their actual status and actor's material awareness |

No victim is required to obtain a military certificate before clinical,
food, shelter or independent complaint receiving. Preserve original factual
records and safe witness/clinical access without making a permanent public
identity map. The specific criminal finding remains an independent court's
task with meaningful defense and no command/vendor secrecy exemption.
