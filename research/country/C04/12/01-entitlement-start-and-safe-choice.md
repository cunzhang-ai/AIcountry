# Who starts the work and on what authority

*Part of [TU effective take-up](../12-effective-take-up-and-early-benefit-delivery.md).*

**TU01 — Simplify the rule and assign the public debtor.** For each
covered income or in-kind benefit, the Assembly identifies the sector
authority, the receipt test, the effective-date rule, and the funded
entry, delivery and correction duties. Use existing local first
receivers and person-directed help under AP/AH. Before adding a
matching process, ask whether the programme can remove a repeated
claim, annual re-proof or immaterial eligibility test. Compare a
simple entitlement, a direct service and a means-tested alternative
where relevant, including who pays and who may be excluded. A
public schedule names the legal grounds and full scope of each
approved early-delivery route. Existing service protections and
sectoral authority continue.

**TU02 — Release established payments without another application.**
Where an existing lawful award or an expressly enacted event rule
already establishes the holder, amount, date, conditions and safe
receiving route, the responsible officer validates and releases the
due payment or instruction. The person can inspect the reason,
correct evidence and select an available lawful delivery method.
An already chosen pension start, for example, can require payment
without another claimant declaration. A claim that depends on
residence, income, safety, a valid recipient or unresolved custody
is routed for actual assessment. Record why the rule and evidence
establish the exact entitlement. This duty supplies no new
substantive benefit, imputed consent or departure from source law.

**TU03 — Offer assisted confirmation when facts or choices remain.**
Where the responsible office lawfully learns of a plausible need
but cannot establish the full claim, it offers a usable way to
confirm or decline the request and supply only the missing
material facts. An opt-in step at an existing contact can submit
a request and attest one fact for the chosen programme where
source law permits. The person controls that choice and has an
equivalent staffed route without the data exchange. Record and
triage a qualified, safely actionable notice within two working
days of receipt by the duty office under this proposed schedule,
or earlier where the useful window requires. Preserve the legal
event date or first protected claim date under the programme's
law. A general invitation and an unconfirmed lead neither
determine eligibility nor create a claim date on their own.

**TU04 — Keep an open path for people outside the records.** Fund
direct human, paper and accessible entry without a prior match,
device, account or identity token. A person can seek urgent aid,
dispute a mismatch or obtain advice without asking the outreach
office to approve it. Use chosen safe contact channels and
neutral communication when disclosure could cause harm. Where
no safe contact is established, keep help reachable through
authorized local services and community routes without disclosing
a possible benefit, parentage, location or health condition to an
unapproved third party. Silence may reflect refusal, exclusion,
error or danger. It carries no adverse evidentiary weight on its
own and cannot erase existing protection or payment.
