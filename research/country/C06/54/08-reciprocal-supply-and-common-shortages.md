# NS31–NS35. Make reciprocal supply usable when both partners need it

*Design514 · Proposed ordinary public finance and external supply arrangements.*

**NS31 — Keep ordinary exchange open and protect a defined essential service.** For a material recurring external dependence, the competent elected owners compare ordinary diversified trade, separately funded stocks, reciprocal support and scoped local capacity or demand reduction. Keep the original essential use and actual receiving date fixed. Value protections in the supply area as well as at home. Select reciprocal investment where it qualifies across simultaneous need and beats the complete alternatives. Maintain independently usable domestic or separately qualified external cover for what the partner cannot firmly supply. Local origin or a strategic label supplies no spending priority or immunity from review.

**NS32 — Make only the joint amount that can be performed count.** Before voting a reciprocal supply instrument, list rights already held, physical stocks, legal custody, minimum donor duties, release priorities, shared suppliers and export, transit, settlement and quality prerequisites. For the same adverse calendar, distinguish enforceable reserved capacity from conditional aid, a discretionary option or expected market purchases. Reconcile any competing reservations and third-party claims using scope-limited qualified verification. For a reciprocal exchange, identify the first independently lawful and executable release, or genuinely accepted synchronized custody and settlement with timed unwind. Verify each affected payer and accepting receiver. Where a call must be made before the ordinary route is known, price the option, paid overlap and any unused stock, with an exact activation deadline and lawful destination. Waiting for certainty must not silently consume the last usable alternative. Bound contingent support, show how it changes under a shared shortage and demonstrate a funded following call. A unit cannot protect both populations in the same window unless a lawful sequential route can actually meet both dates. Publish legally relevant terms, limits and contested claims while protecting sensitive inputs.

**NS33 — Buy an independent choice rather than accepting a disguised condition.** Separate ordinary price, reciprocal obligations, safety, proportionate assurance and dispute terms from wider proposed concessions. Ministers, suppliers and payment officers act only within their actual mandates. Record any supply term that would shift independent scrutiny, resident rights or control of a public decision and put the exact lawful terms and alternatives to the competent elected forum. Conditions beyond the government's power remain unavailable. Compare ordinary negotiated cooperation with an independently funded shorter bridge and a durable substitute. No urgent supply need changes treaty ratification, lawful disclosure or independent review. Preserve valid claims and consent on their own authority.

**NS34 — Keep households protected during adjustment.** Price each lawful option to actual service, not only to the border. Compare temporary stocked supply, safe qualified substitution, cost support and reduced genuinely discretionary demand. Verify accepted quantities, accessibility, local provider capacity and any required individual choice. Measure the combined burden of prices, fees, travel and unpaid work on households with least room to adjust and the population whose prior provision pays for the substitute. Apply existing fiscal and sector remedies to actual misses. A subsidy cannot deliver a missing input and a physical reserve does not establish household affordability. If some protection remains unfunded, expose the exact gap and return it to the competent decision.

**NS35 — Preserve access through expiry, replenishment and changed governments.** Reconcile rights, payments, dispatch, accepted local service, replenishment and outstanding foreign claims separately at each useful date. Test simultaneous refusal or reduced donor capacity, a delay at the paying or receiving endpoint, and a subsequent call with less stock. Bring a material renewed commitment back to the actual authorizer before expiry. Existing independently usable operations continue on their own law. Where qualified reciprocal supply fails, use only preauthorized stocks, independently accepted ordinary trade and lawful relief. When the country supplies aid, distinguish valid firm obligations from conditional aid. Apply the same release and remedy tests at home and protect both populations under the actual terms. Domestic shortage cannot itself turn an existing enforceable promise into a discretionary one. Keep the original missed service visible. AI may compare forecasts and dependency schedules; it cannot attest another government’s consent, set priority, certify safety or commit the country.
