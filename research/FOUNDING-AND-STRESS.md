# Founding, continuity and whole-country failure

5 October 2026 · Supporting establishment package; original exercise scope through design 32

This document follows the [authority package](AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION.md) and
[social/fiscal settlement](SOCIAL-FISCAL-SETTLEMENT.md). It gives the country design
an entry path and tests how its institutions would respond when several systems
fail together. There is no territory, founding population or legal authorization
attached to this research project. “Ready” below means a condition a real adopting
community must demonstrate, not a condition this document has already passed.
The [legal-hierarchy decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-legal-hierarchy/decision-record.md)
clarifies that a charter vote, later Assembly statutes, annual appropriations,
local enactments and operational commencement are different acts. The
[pre-hierarchy version](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-legal-hierarchy/FOUNDING-AND-STRESS-pre-hierarchy.md)
is preserved for comparison.

Each exercise is an authored stress hypothesis, not an observed result.
Later additions to the current country chapters do not inherit validation
from these earlier cases. The [readiness and limits](founding/05-readiness-and-limits.md)
separate remaining integrated design work from actual adoption and operation.

[Design522 compound exercise](settlement/first-round-mandate/03-protected-supply-through-impasse.md) runs election annulment, fiscal lapse and essential receipt on one calendar, preserving the original cases above.

[Design524 emergency accountability](settlement/emergency-expiry/03-review-and-delivered-repair.md) tests whether restrictions actually end and their remedies reach residents.
