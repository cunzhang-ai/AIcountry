*Design306 · Working decision for the Copenhagen evidence context.*

**T23 — Use the budget of the body that can act.** In a matched
operating context identify each responsible public body and operator,
the period and the decision in force at each date. Reconcile estimates,
voted appropriations, accounting results, owned liquid funds and
contractual receipts on their own scopes. A city, its water company
and a national fund each need lawful purpose and dated authority for
a contribution. Record both ends of an authorized transfer and count
it once. A public owner may propose an explicit contribution, with
the receiving mandate, effect on existing users and financing stated
before commitment. Preserve actual tariff, creditor and contractual
duties and the public bodies' respective decisions.

**T24 — Inspect the service beneath the annual variance.** In
Copenhagen's 2025 record, a health and care underspend coexists with
overspending in home care and nursing. HOFOR Vand København A/S
reports an accounting profit, customer receivables and much smaller
bank deposits. The 2026 decisions give selected purposes their own
budget lines and approve a continuity plan. Apply those records to
their specific scope and time. Keep continuing commitments and the
observed ordinary delivery arrangement in the comparator. A staffing
quote includes the professional task, acceptance, time and cost of
coverage at the origin and destination. A utility quote includes
qualified stock, settlement, delivery and restoration. Only usable
funds, actual authority and accepted capacity establish a start.

**T25 — Choose preparation where the release evidence stops.** Use
the observed Copenhagen arrangement as the retain-and-repair comparator.
Carry its existing purpose-specific approvals and prepare joint water
and care protection under the respective operators. The inspected
sources establish no release-ready incremental heating, training or
tenancy motion for the joint window. The conditional preference in
the separate constructed case remains a comparison under its own
assumptions. Seek an exact funded offer for a repair within standing
authority or an explicit joint reserve, with actual local and
national decisions as applicable. Preserve resident and worker claims,
independent review, elections and the next protected call in both.
The choice of direct provision, a purchased reserve or a shared service
depends on accepted capacity, cost, burden and control.

**T26 — Publish the missing proof as part of the decision.** For each
proposed start, give elected members, independent reviewers and affected
people the applicable public evidence and usable reasons. Record
which authority, payer, due-date funds, receiving commitments and
measured effects are known or unresolved. Keep protected detail with
its lawful custodian and provide a way to challenge the record.
AI can reconcile arithmetic, follow revisions and flag a double
count. Officers verify source scope and acceptance. Competent elected
bodies choose any new burden or appropriation. Reopen the recorded
decision with an exact proposal, a dated cash path and verified cover
for the first and second call. Use the actual lawful relief path
where a continuing duty is already at risk.
