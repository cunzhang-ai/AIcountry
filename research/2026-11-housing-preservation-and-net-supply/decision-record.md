# Design332 decision on housing preservation and net supply

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design331, H, RT, HL, LU, LA, IV and the working framework.
The country already values repair and protects secure tenure. It
recognizes that acquisition does not necessarily add dwellings.
Add an elected portfolio with separate preservation and entrant
schedules, and join the physical stock and restored service accounts.
The project assistant authors the choice. Competent elected authorities
and affected participants retain their actual decisions.

## Alternatives and evidence

Compare preserving at-risk tenancies by purchase or durable agreement,
repair or conversion, and serviced new construction. Acquisition can
protect people quickly while leaving the entrant queue unchanged.
Repair can supply early safe housing while leaving a later shortage.
Construction can increase physical supply but arrives later and may
require temporary homes and prior return. Select matched schedules and
a reasoned elected portfolio, with explicit change if one objective's
staff or money is moved to another. The proposed accounting adds work
and can expose rather than resolve incompatible objectives.

The [primary sources](sources.md) illustrate stock-flow measurement
and separate affordable-stock preservation programmes. The numerical
comparison stipulates outcomes and does not establish additionality,
optimal spending or an actual pipeline.

## Authority, migration and reopening

Elected owners decide purpose, funds and commitments. Existing sector
roles verify land, safety, affordability and actual receiving. Protect
tenancies, return rights, deposits, binding contracts and lawful creditor
priority. Preserve existing claims and funded operators through change.
No new acquisition power or waived site protection follows from
a housing budget. Independent review stays reachable.

Test one real area's portfolio against original dwelling condition,
occupied tenure, actual entrants, lawful finance and construction
dependencies. Compare early receiving and a later stock window.
Obtain safe affected participation, verify actual costs and retain
the rival that could work better. Reopen on lost affordability,
hidden displacement, repeated delay, failing supply or a simpler
effective portfolio. Actual adoption, units, funding and outcomes
remain open.
