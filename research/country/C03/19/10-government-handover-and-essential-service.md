# HT44–HT47. Keep democratic handover and essential services usable together

*Design457 · Part of [lawful handover](../19-lawful-handover-and-actual-control.md).*

**HT44 — Test the same failure across protected functions.** When a
material service or political cutover depends on a common provider,
record controller, payer or staffed role, the competent owners test its
loss on one timeline. Include outgoing directions, lawful successor
acts, ordinary services, opposition access and court execution.
Identify the legal boundary and the first useful deadline for each.
An alternate owned by a different institution counts only within the
scope it can independently verify, authorize, pay, staff and deliver.
Keep obligations absent from the main roster in scope and preserve
their original dates through ordinary intake. Publish safe aggregate
results with independent access to scoped evidence.

**HT45 — Buy a usable minimum across the whole window.** The competent
elected payers separately authorize the cost and scope of recovery for
their own functions. Compare one scheduled common alternate, distinct
paid alternates and a less dependent service design. A shared route
may serve several owners where all their original deadlines, different
legal authorities and protected data can coexist. Count each accepted
person, equipment slot and accessible funding source once. Where a
real timing conflict would otherwise defeat a protected duty, procure
an independent increment or change the dependent design before relying
on it. Keep public authority, essential work and review in the same
test. A reserve for a later call needs its own dated funds and staff.

**HT46 — Transfer office on law and carry the affected duties.** The
statutory boundary, prior orders and resident deadlines retain their
own legal effect. Ordinary offices execute compatible lawful work
and use funded alternates under HT07–HT09 and applicable sector law.
Each competent office or court handles its own disputed act. Limit a
hold to the optional action or reliance that needs the unavailable
authority or resource. If routes for protected duties still conflict,
use existing sector and order-execution law and report what remains
unserved. No provider, mediator, court, recovery officer or AI obtains
general spending, appointment, rationing or executive power by filling
a gap. Preserve independent challenge, confidentiality and any claim
for late or missed performance.

**HT47 — Require proof at both ends before restoring reliance.** Obtain
independent confirmation that the expired direction is rejected, the
successor can execute a lawful act, and affected people can reach due
service and challenge on time. A funded plan, valid signature or late
completion proves only its own event. The incoming government may
propose a durable change with separately funded scrutiny. Escalate an
ongoing failure to the existing competent payer, service owner and
review forum on the original clock. When the next call or a funding
source fails, disclose the remaining exposure and revisit the operating
model. AI may help reconcile the record. Restored access supplies no
evidence that its decisions are correct or that residents received care.
