# OB05–OB08: cash, services and financial claims cannot substitute by label

**OB05 — Compare actual capability as well as cash.** NAN3 households-plus-NPISH
consumption expenditure is 1,249.8bn DKK, while actual individual consumption is
1,693.7bn. The 443.9bn addition also separates adjusted from ordinary gross
disposable income. It represents additional individual consumption in kind,
not extra cash, all collective services or evidence that each person received
adequate care. Do not compare U cash with a welfare system's cash figure while
omitting its service offer. Nor can the public service cost be cashed out as a
recipient's equivalent housing/care resource without actual valid choice/receipt.
[Original NAN3 table](https://www.statbank.dk/NAN3) defines these household/NPISH aggregates.

**OB06 — Preserve pension/receipt distinctions.** Gross disposable income is
1,368.4bn, the pension-entitlement adjustment 99.0bn and gross saving 217.6bn:
the source identity includes that adjustment before subtracting expenditure.
It is not an independent extra cash benefit. Actual wages, accessible pension
payments, contribution/withholding, credited claims, imputed income, accrued
rights and liquid property remain distinct for PA/I05/Y. National accounts are
not an individual's receipt dossier or an authority to tax unreceived money.
Existing earned units and future cohort liabilities need the Y/RF actuarial
account; no balance-sheet total establishes that tail funded or absent.

**OB07 — Separate financial transactions from asset revaluation.** The financial
companion for S.14+S.15 reports opening net financial assets 7,315.322bn and
closing 7,837.933bn. Net transactions 102.883bn, revaluation 429.411bn and other
volume changes −9.684bn explain the difference within 1m published rounding.
Net worth, saving, newly received income and usable money are not the same.
These are owned household/NPISH positions, not Treasury assets or unencumbered
capital for the new country. Aggregate wealth reveals neither individual access,
liquidity, distribution nor consent to sell. Preserve separate creditor/title/
pension/customer duties and any actual greater protection through paid succession.
Conversely, negative corporate financial net worth alone does not establish
insolvency: nonfinancial assets and the statistical treatment of issued equity
need separate assessment under actual OE/creditor law.
[Original NASFK table](https://www.statbank.dk/NASFK); the frozen 2023 selection keeps its own vintage.

**OB08 — Use the finding in the country design.** Keep actual-receipt I05/PA/Y,
independent additional needs and universal essential services. No automatic
macro-statistical cash/means credit, wealth sweep, imputed dividend or public-
service charge is added. Retain A/I05 as the next access-first reference and
conditional U+C2 with C1/C0 rivals. This benchmark does not set B, child factors,
M, tax rates or new pensions, prove nominal 13,248 adequate, or establish a source
for any changed parameter. Value disagreement and missing evidence stay separate.
