# Design192 decision. Fund early care across levels of government

*9 November 2026 · Research selection · Exact design191 predecessor preserved.*

## Country question and scope

Design191 made a substantive fiscal rule selection and is prior progress.
Apply FB to a continuing national duty delivered through local authorities.
S10 and NC already specify the offer and duties. C04 assigns equalization
and fallback at the general level. The gap was an executable allocation
and payment path from the common floor to safe receiving. Select that
path for organized early care before age six, without altering other
services by analogy. [Exact predecessor](baseline.json)

Child safety, relationships, family agency, decent work and equal access
guide the choice. Municipal receipts, charges, accepted qualified staffing
and experienced care are different evidence. A current named phase-in
budget is useful because an actual local elected decision can be traced
from its public payer. The grant's finite scope cannot finance the full
future-country standard. [Evidence and limits](sources.md)

## Choice, authority and objections

The Assembly selects the floor, standardized costs, ordinary local share
and gap schedule through DB and FB. Treasury advances authorized cash
monthly before due obligations. The local elected body controls ordinary
organization, appoints and funds receiving through existing C04 roles,
and remains answerable for safe actual care. The national care authority
provides a limited fallback, using separately paid ready provision, with
review and return to lawful local control. Monthly advance and quarterly
reconciliation balance cash timing with adjustment. The proposed 30-day
intervention limit and court-controlled renewal balance local autonomy
with continuity where transfer takes longer. These periods are design
choices. The need for actual independent hearing capacity and a lawful
ordinary delivery path at expiry remains. Council, ministry and provider
cannot close the person's claim by disputing payment. Ordinary public
accounting, privacy and independent inspection and court review apply.
[Selected rules](../country/C07/23-funded-local-early-care.md)

Retaining or repairing a broad local grant keeps flexibility and existing
competence. An earmarked reimbursement can improve visibility, but delays
financing and may reward expensive providers or wealthy places that can
front cash. Direct national operation reduces the split between payer
and provider, while centralizing staff and control. Portable fee relief
improves actual affordability for some users without delivering a
missing service. These are live alternatives. The selected advance and
service fallback is conditional on real competence and budget, with
need adjustments that can be challenged.

The strongest objection is that a national schedule can covertly control
local councils, undervalue rural or disability costs, or reward reported
need while hiding quality. Put the calculation and its exceptions under
statute, allow local and resident challenges without data surrender,
separate evidence, audit, safety and merits review, and prevent
discretionary withholding of undisputed funds. The fallback has an
actual service scope and accepted handback. Partisan capture, forecasting
failure, recruitment bottlenecks and persistent underfunding remain
possible. Useful remedies require independent paid capacity. No adoption
or public judgment is inferred from the Danish example.

## Continuing duties and disposition

FP/TN/FB source and claims rules, DB votes and cash execution, LR scope,
NC19 notice, S protections, TW36/32, CL and paid OP/T succession remain.
Before authorized transition, map public/private grant and payroll
contracts, premises, existing subsidy and family claims, care plans,
accessible transport and real fallback across old and new owners. Use
minimal records and preserve the original first receipt. No researched
calculation grants authority or changes an old date.

Evidence: an adopted city fee decision, reported grant receipt and
published local allocations, plus separate national sources and desk
failure cases. Research: select the care financing and fallback mechanism.
Legal authorization: the foreign 2026 city decision and finance booklet
were verified at their stated scopes. No AIcountry adoption. Operational readiness: actual
future sources, court and care staff, timely payment and transition remain
unverified. Outcomes: unknown. This edition has no actual affected-party
participation or qualified independent review.

Reopen if funded costs, access, local discretion, tax incidence or
staffing differ materially, or an alternative does better across child,
family and worker outcomes. Next compare the selected care package with
retain/repair in a common locality and year, holding basic rights and old
dues fixed. Join dated net sources and the demand/production account to
actual staff and affordability before choosing a full recurring package.
This 2026 allocation may support only the unchanged scoped historical
comparison. No support or approval carries over to a revised programme.
