The proposed 24/72-hour limits are normative hypotheses, not evidence of staffed
local leads, Cabinet response, independent courts or actual transportation. This
rule deliberately chooses a smaller pool than a broad emergency allocation power.
It can be insufficient in an extreme collision; naming that limit is preferable
to pretending a vague life-saving instruction supplies unlimited authority.
Current legal authorities remain unchanged until competent lawful adoption.
