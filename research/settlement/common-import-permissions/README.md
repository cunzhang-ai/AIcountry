# Two sellers share a permit and a prepaid order cannot be replaced at once

*Design303 · Constructed comparison. All amounts, dates and receipts are assumptions.*

Two protected windows require four qualified treatment units by day 10
and four more by day 25. A rotating local stock can meet the first
window and costs two domestic units to release and deliver. The
clinical authority and local receivers accept it in this example.
The primary contract was lawfully prepaid at six foreign units
on day 1. A suspension on day 3 blocks its indispensable upstream
permission. A second seller has a separate plant and transport but
needs the same permission for the batch. Neither delivers under
the assumed failure. A promised three-unit refund only arrives
and becomes usable on day 30.

There were 12 owned usable foreign units for this work before the
prepayment, after other due claims and applicable reserve floors.
Only six remain on day 3. The maximum separately authorized gross
foreign expenditure for these orders is 13. The domestic envelope
is three units in total. The two limits are distinct and there is
no conversion in the exercise. The spot and reserved alternate
are mutually exclusive uses of the same independent producer's
four qualified units. Each must settle by day 8 and can reach the
local receiver by day 23 under its own permissions.

| Response after the common permit loss | Gross foreign expenditure if performed | Domestic cost | Day 10 / day 25 qualified receiving |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Local stock and second seller under the same suspension | 6 already paid | 2 | 4 / 0 |
| Local stock and independent spot purchase at seven foreign units | 13 | 2 | 4 / 0 because only six foreign units remain by day 8 |
| Local stock and a previously paid, independent reservation exercisable at six foreign units | 12 | 3, including the one-unit reservation | 4 / 4 |
| Local stock and a separately qualified domestic surge line with usable inputs | 8, including two for materials | 5 | 4 / 0 under the three-unit domestic limit |

Select the rotating stock and paid independent reservation on
the stated readiness and cost assumptions. The spot option would
fit if one foreign unit were actually returned and lawfully usable
by day 8, within the gross ceiling. A later return does not reopen
that date. A differently agreed future contract with a three-unit
first milestone could leave seven available for the spot option
after the same denial, assuming no other payment is owed before
day 8. That is a comparison for a new contract. It cannot undo
the existing six-unit payment.

If the reserved alternate shares the suspended permission, arrives
on day 26, fails clinical acceptance or comes with a condition the
buyer cannot lawfully accept, it does not cover the second window.
A separate payer and a different supplier name do not solve that
branch. Existing liabilities, remedies and each missed patient
window stay on their own clocks. Day 30's return can support a
later authorized purchase, never backdate a receipt.
