# Design394 decision. Give ordinary municipal waste a funded route to final receiving

## Baseline and selection

[Design393](predecessor-design-393/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md) assigns separate
sanitation and solid-waste stewards, ecological and health limits,
outage duties and covered producer responsibility. The positive
municipal design still leaves the minimum household offer, actual
destination evidence, working reserve and closure assurance generic.

Select [MW01–MW08](../country/C09/06/01-municipal-waste-and-safe-receiving.md)
under the [working research method](../FRAMEWORK.md). Give local
councils the delivery choice and a funded collection floor. Check
the real route to recovery or disposal and purchase an alternate
that can accept the covered stream in useful time. Protect host
communities, workers and lawful post-closure duties separately.

## Alternatives and objections

Retain locally determined collection and report aggregate recycling.
This is flexible and avoids national specification, but can obscure
who lacks a route and which tonnage only reached sorting. A single
national utility could buy scale and common standards, with longer
transport, concentration and less local influence over service.
Select local accountability, common minima, independently inspectable
destinations and voluntary lawful joint receiving subject to host
protection. Compare direct public, cooperative and contracted
operation under the same rights and funded failure requirements.

Full variable charging may encourage prevention but can shift costs
to people with unavoidable waste or drive material outside the
observed system. Select a common financed floor and allow a bounded
extra-service price only with real alternatives and protections.
The proposed collection intervals are hypotheses. A rural or
communal route may be preferable if it meets actual health and
access needs. Separate food collection can strain sites and workers.
Verify staffing and processing before adopting a new duty.

Local voters choose the service budget and accountable leadership.
Planning and environmental permits have separate review, and host
residents retain participation and independent challenge. Joint
facilities must show that actual boards, operators, sources and
inspectors are independent where required. A common contractor can
control nominally separate sites. Keep worker and resident reports
reachable without the implicated operator's permission.

## Evidence, migration and next action

[Primary records](sources.md) document actual collection rules,
published material accounting with a validation limitation and
financial assurance mechanisms. The [constructed comparison](../settlement/municipal-waste-and-safe-receiving/01-two-streams-and-one-closed-plant.md)
checks custody versus recovery, a second treatment call and separately
restricted cash. It does not establish local prices, participation,
authority, safety or delivered environmental outcomes.

Adopt prospectively with a named Solid-Waste Steward, independent
health and environmental staff, qualified operators, a national
residual source and paid succession. Preserve existing permits and
duties under their lawful transition, including distinct wastewater,
producer, clinical and legacy obligations. No assurance balance
becomes ordinary cash by moving the operator or title.

Next trace a real building, the relevant local routes and two receiving
facilities across two collection rounds. Include people unable to use
standard bins, affected host residents and the workers who deliver
the service. Check actual stocks, paid spare capacity and closure
security. Reopen on repeated missed rounds, unsafe hosting, unresolved
material differences or better comparable delivery.
