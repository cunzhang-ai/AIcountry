# One locality and one failed network

*Authored counterfactual. All units, service counts and timing are declared
assumptions. None are forecasts, an appropriation or a real incident test.*

## Ordinary service before the failure

A locality has 100 occupied premises. Of 80 that can be served, 64 have
usable ordinary access. Twelve willing households cannot afford the full
bill. Four different willing households need covered installation
assistance. The other 20 premises lack a physical connection and are
assumed willing and able to pay the resulting basic price if connected.
All four groups are disjoint. The remaining access routes for people
without premises still require a separate test.

The current lawful budget has a usable window of 14 one-time units and
4 recurring units per year beyond existing operation. The expansion to
reach 20 premises costs 12 one-time units. Installation support costs 2
one-time units plus 1 annual unit for continuing support. The price
discount costs 2 annual units. The candidate independent emergency route
requires 3 one-time and 2 annual units. Existing operation and renewal
outside these increments are assumed paid identically. The assumptions
do not authorize redirecting protected existing allocations.

| Choice | One-time / annual units | Ordinary receiving and exposed duties |
|---|---:|---|
| Independent emergency route first | 3 / 2 | 64 retain usable ordinary access. The alternative can serve its measured emergency task. The 36 ordinary gaps still need action. |
| Repair with price and installation support first | 2 / 3 | 80 usable once completed, 20 still physically unserved. Only 1 annual unit is left for the 2-unit fallback. |
| Build the reach and deliver price and installation support | 14 / 3 | 100 serviceable and potentially 100 using it after actual receipt. No one-time balance and only 1 annual unit remain for the fallback. |
| Same basic offer and independent emergency route | 17 / 5 | Needs 3 more one-time and 1 more recurring unit. Its resilience claim applies only to the tasks and capacity below. |

On these assumptions the full bundle cannot start together. Buying reach
alone would increase technical serviceability to 100 while 16 remain
excluded and only 84 can receive ordinary service. Applying CN04, the
guarantor must show actual receipt and bring the uncovered timing and
finance choice to the elected body. Existing claims and a funded route
for each currently owed duty continue. This case has no basis for
ranking the otherwise uncovered household expansion against an urgent
alternative without actual duties and timing.

## Shared core failure under a simultaneous demand

Two retail brands use the same core and its remote control. The disabled
core takes both out for the stated window. The receiving public-safety
dispatcher must receive up to 120 emergency contacts, including
accessible return contact where needed, by minute 30. In this
counterfactual, the technically independent route supports 90 distinct
contacts by that time if it starts by minute 10. A separately staffed
physical route can safely receive 20 distinct contacts in time if
opened by minute 5, including accessible journeys verified for those 20.
Its continuing cost is already in the stated common baseline. The two
groups are disjoint. Thus 10 are still uncovered even if the independently
funded network route starts in time. Starting either route after its
last safe start has no guaranteed credit for the late path. The funded
site alone supports at most 20. The dispatcher must keep all uncovered
cases visible and use the applicable lawful incident and relief routes.

At minute 4 the operator reports a plausible restoration at minute 15.
The duty officer starts the physical route then, and if the
independent route is funded and accepted, also starts it by minute 10.
An optimistic estimate gives no independent receiving evidence before
those starts. Actual dispatch, court receipt and election continuity
remain separate statutory tasks and cannot claim the same reserve.
Independent review verifies the contacts, actual failure modes, scope
and who could not reach the receiver before the deadline.

In ordinary operation, if a carrier also throttles an opposition
publisher while calling it congestion, the regulator preserves the
ordinary service class and actual management record. Affected users
can seek scoped restoration and court review. Real congestion evidence
must identify necessity, scope and end. The controller of the shared
core has no authority to decide whose speech matters. The emergency
exercise supplies no general censorship, data collection or network
command power.
