# TR05–TR08. Preserve authority through signature, application and amendment

*Design223 · Part of [treaty commitments](../15-democratic-treaty-commitments.md).*

**TR05 — Name the act and its effects before anyone signs.** Record
whether signature authenticates the text, triggers a pre-entry duty,
expresses consent or starts provisional application. Apply the actual
instrument and international law. Cabinet may authenticate or sign
subject to ratification only under an express prior mandate within its
actual scope, with any pre-entry duty disclosed. Notify relevant restrictions on the
representative to the other negotiating parties before the act where
applicable. Signature that itself
binds a material commitment or starts material application needs TR04
first. The authorized officer makes only the exact permitted signature,
exchange, deposit or notice with valid credentials. Record receipts and
effective conditions, including external partner acceptance as applicable.
A domestic approval does not by itself supply that acceptance, an
appropriation or the necessary domestic implementing law.

**TR06 — Vote on material provisional application before it begins.**
Specify the exact provisions, partners, start conditions, rights, costs
and end route, including what still binds after expiry. Give material
provisional use the same prior elected approval as other material
commitments, separate from final entry. Require external terms that
allow the authorized end and define valid notice. An internal ceiling or
deadline cannot itself end an obligation to a partner. The named officer
sends and verifies the actual notice under the instrument. If the partner
disputes it, use qualified legal review and the applicable dispute route.
Continue ordinary lawful protection and payment. No emergency, pilot,
supplier or exchange of letters supplies substitute authority.

**TR07 — Keep later changes within the same democratic boundary.**
A joint body may make narrow technical changes only where the treaty
and prior Assembly law explicitly define the scope, ceilings, notice,
affected territory, reporting and right to stop. Assess cumulative
effects. A new obligation, material fee, right, guarantee, legal power
or material loss of protection goes to TR03–TR04, including a material
change by interpretation, reservation or dynamic reference. Before
approving any change mechanism, identify a workable prior scrutiny and
objection path with actual external effect. A named Cabinet officer may
issue a protective objection under prior authority to avoid premature
acceptance. Seek narrower terms before relying on an unmanageable
mechanism. A missed domestic vote does not create consent at home.
If external law attaches consequences to silence, address those facts
and any breach rather than declaring them absent.

**TR08 — Keep an authenticated record of status.** The responsible
foreign affairs records officer and Assembly Clerk preserve the
mandate, exact texts, approvals, lawful reservations and objections,
external receipt, actual entry dates, applicable territories, amendments,
periodic obligations and end terms. Distinguish ratification, provisional
scope, domestic implementation and first receipt of service. Keep
authentic versions, translations and dissent identifiable. PE supplies
safe access to original evidence and independent review. Apply exact
publication and any applicable international registration rules.
AI may compare versions, flag semantic differences and assist notice.
It cannot authenticate consent, bind the country or resolve disputed
legal meaning on its own.
