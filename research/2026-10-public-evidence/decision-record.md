Design 11 requires opposition to inspect election evidence, the Assembly to
see fiscal and physical accounts, communities to challenge ecological plans,
and residents to learn from outcomes. Yet no institution had a complete duty
to preserve original observations, publish comparable statistics with visible
methods and earlier versions, or carry records through a hostile cabinet and
vendor handover. This is an omission in the written design, not proof of a
real-world failure. Truthful source records, comparable aggregates, causal
interpretation and legal decisions remain distinct. Neither official statistics
nor a model's summary settles contested public values or changes a claim.

## Rival models and selected chain

| Model | Advantage | Serious failure mode |
|---|---|---|
| Retain sector reporting, ordinary audit and outside scrutiny | Preserves domain expertise and avoids another public office. | Challenged service authorities can control definitions, release timing, source access and vendor records; incompatible series can prevent public reconstruction. |
| One insulated national evidence authority owning collection, statistics, archives and evaluation | Provides a single comparable account and visible professional responsibility. | Concentrates methods, personal data, archive keys and publication in a capturable public gateway; insulation on paper cannot guarantee independent cash or servers. |
| **Selected conditional federation of custodians and separate public evidence service** | Sector bodies remain answerable for event records, while a separately governed statistics/archive service publishes comparable, versioned public accounts; plural research and affected-party challenge remain possible. | Coordination and privacy are costly; the same patron could still control source staff, leaders, funding, infrastructure and court execution. |

The Assembly would enact purposes, record and publication duties, confidentiality,
access, funding and remedies. Source authorities would retain their operational
decisions and original records, with named custodians, lawful retention and
traceable correction. The evidence service would publish statistics and methods,
preserve authenticated public law and previous data vintages, and arrange
contestable evaluation; it could not redefine housing eligibility, tax liability
or political membership. Leaders would be chosen through public merit criteria
and hearings, staggered terms and reviewable for-cause removal. A bounded
standing schedule and qualified acting staff would protect routine preservation
and release through appointment or budget delay, without renewing a cabinet or
granting experts policy authority. Opposition, residents and independent
researchers need usable and appropriately protected routes to challenge
withholding, error or an erasing category. Courts enforce lawful access and
correction through named custodians; no court is made the nation's official
scientific interpreter.

Methods, missing populations, uncertainty, planned release dates, material
revisions and earlier series would be public. Personal correction appends
provenance rather than silently overwriting records. Sensitive testimony may
require restricted inspection or safe summaries, not perpetual public exposure.
Statistics and archive functions need mutual scrutiny; outside observation may
disagree without a license. A technical operator maintains systems but cannot
decide what a measurement means or suppress a lawful criticism. Human, AI and
joint contributors must trace shared source families; many outputs from one
altered input are not independent corroboration.

An authored stress case changes the definition of a serviced home and delays
water-quality readings before an election while a common vendor holds all
backups and the cabinet stalls the evidence-service appointment. The selected
chain requires source readings and earlier versions to survive, qualified
acting staff to publish within bounded funds, protected access for challengers,
and an executable court order through an alternate operator or protected copy.
It fails if the same controller can silence source staff, release budgets,
archive keys, courts and the remedy executor. A deadline cannot declare a
disputed number true, and a published statistic cannot extinguish the underlying
housing or water claim. This is logic testing, not observed resilience.

## Evidence, continuity and next test

The [UN Fundamental Principles of Official Statistics](https://unstats.un.org/fpos/)
and their [implementation discussion](https://unstats.un.org/capacity-development/handbook/html/Handbook/C3/UN_Fundamental_Principles_of_Official_Statistics.htm)
support professional independence, transparent methods, confidentiality,
correction and equal access as general comparators. The
[International Council on Archives](https://www.ica.org/resource/principles-of-access-to-archives/)
provides a public-record access comparator with lawful exceptions. These
sources do not endorse the selected institutional mixture or supply observations
of AIcountry. Source families overlap where AI analyses repeat them. Material
new objections and actual local evidence require fresh review before adoption.

The integration check also found a support-scope error outside the evidence
institution itself. The earlier three-year social/fiscal exercise was described
as fitting reference limits without always stating that it predates several
later operating commitments, including the evidence service. C13 and the status
now qualify that result to its original scenario. It is not reused as a cost
or staffing certificate for design 12; a real adopting authority must reconcile
all current commitments in one cash, workforce and physical account.

Material objections remain: an evidence office can become an expert elite;
standard categories can erase local meanings; disclosure can endanger a person;
secrecy can conceal official failure; permanent records can become surveillance;
federated offices can produce incomparable figures or coordinate suppression.
No single accuracy score resolves those value conflicts. Affected people must
be able to challenge framing, withholding and harmful release without having
to surrender the underlying service claim. Publication cannot be inferred
from more elaborate prose or repeated AI agreement.

This choice affects election and opposition evidence, fiscal audit, ecology,
service delivery, trials, digital infrastructure and successor duties. Prior
sector and data-law reviews do not transfer automatically. Actual migration
would inventory source and derived records, public-law editions, archival
keys, restricted testimony, lawful retention, vendors, backups, current method
series and pending correction or disclosure claims. It would name receiving
custodians, funded overlap, secure access and remedy executors before changing
the system. Existing record, privacy and service obligations continue; neither
an outgoing government nor an archive transfer can erase them.

**Evidence qualification:** same-controller analysis and primary institutional
comparators only. **Research selection:** conditional federated custody plus
separately governed statistics and archive service. **Legal authorization:**
none. **Operational readiness:** unknown for source access, personnel, cash,
privacy protection, backup independence and enforcing officers. **Observed
outcomes:** none. Updating the research index adopts no new public procedure.

The next bounded action for an interested lawful jurisdiction is a witnessed
preservation-to-remedy exercise over one disputed cross-sector evidence chain
during a noncooperating cabinet and vendor transition. Original source custody,
methods, old series, access law, appointment and funding controllers, secure
copies and actual remedy delivery must be mapped and compared with retained
sector reporting and a single authority. The competent public body would
set a real budget and receive affected-party and independent professional
challenge; this project has neither. Reopen the selection if source access
cannot survive shared control, if official categories systematically erase
people, or if the separated service is less contestable than a rival. No
monitoring gap or elapsed deadline supplies consent.
