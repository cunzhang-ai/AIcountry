# NS09–NS12: foreign access is an owned relationship over time

**NS09 — Retain useful trade without an export-surplus command.** Diversify
actual essential suppliers, routes, currencies and customers where worthwhile;
compare domestic repair/production, demand reduction, stocks and common provision
on matched quality/time/ecology. No economy can establish this country's offers
by an export target. Not every country can be a net exporter against all others.
An external deficit can support productive investment; a surplus can coexist
with poor access or coercion. Neither balance alone chooses the good society.

**NS10 — Check foreign title, seller acceptance and recurrence.** Current MC
charter typed-currency stocks, floors and gross sales/commitment limits remain.
Actual Treasury, monetary, private-exporter and customer FX are distinct; there
is no new common pool, surrender duty, par conversion or foreign lender promise.
Private receipts are offered voluntarily under actual terms after their own
due claims; retained assets cannot also back public imports. Borrowing or a
negotiated common facility needs real authority, counterparties and future
service/loss. A financial claim's domestic valuation is not a deliverable currency.

**NS11 — Keep reserve runoff visible even when domestic finance looks better.**
In the independent circuit, imports 50f/export receipts 20f require public-owned
sales 30f after private own use. Starting 100f with a 10f floor cannot repeat that
sale in period 4. The same foreign failure arises under bond/issue labels if
physical flows are held unchanged; it is not a predicted run or national reserve
adequacy rule. An export-receipt shock to 10f increases sales to 40f and brings the
failure to period 3, although required new domestic claims fall 120u→110u because
more public assets are sold. A lower domestic cash-finance figure can hide a
worse strategy. Do not treat that result as progress or choose exports residually.

At a 1.5u/f direct quote, public new domestic claims fall to 105u while household/
firm net lending falls 80→70u/40→35u. Foreign stocks still run down 30f. This is
fixed-flow repricing, not a full price/exchange response or an enacted index freeze.
Public FX valuation gains are not recurring revenue or extra deliverable stock.

**NS12 — More private FX is not automatically public capacity.** The separate
40f export-offer hypothesis is not a forecast. After its own 30f imports, a firm
can retain 10f or voluntarily sell it to households. Those choices leave the same
aggregate net-lending identity but different usable titles: with no offer, public
sales 20f hit the floor constraint in period 5; with the 10f offer, public sales 10f
last through the six-period test, not forever. The public receives fewer domestic
units from FX sales and needs more domestic claims, 130u→140u. Better foreign
resilience is not free domestic financing. Actual source, consent, dates and
residual worker/customer claims remain, as do the circuit's physical assumptions.
