# SD01–SD04: rights, universal availability and national priorities

*Part of [C06/43](../43-country-source-and-delivery.md); design 106 candidate.*

**SD01 — Start from the community and its claims.** CX's existing-community
retrofit remains the primary comparison. Retained homes, firms, relationships,
trained people and institutions bring their actual owners, conditions, repair,
contracts and old claims. No greenfield path gets free land or relief from the
same continuing burdens. The reference 100,000 population is authored, not a
founding electorate, a design ceiling or a licence to exclude unfamiliar needs.

**SD02 — Separate four kinds of commitment.** First, already-lawful/due U,
I05, Y, H, care, worker, creditor, refund, court, foreign and other claims keep
their own law and useful time. Second, universal essential-service availability,
accessible democratic institutions and independent remedies require paid standby,
even when nobody uses a particular place that day. Third, voluntary take-up
creates actual work/receiving needs; no compulsory care or family labor balances
an account. Fourth, a proposed new guarantee or expansion needs its own source,
capacity and exact enacted commencement. 'Optional use' never makes the
underlying due availability optional expenditure. Earned pensions are not an
expansion simply because cash or an old record is missing.

**SD03 — Choose access first in the next operating package.** Maintain I05's
individual actual-resource test, advance payment, work disregard/taper, extra
needs and accessible challenge. First repair useful-time income, service,
housing and remedy gaps and maintain productive/essential systems, before
expanding a new universal cash cohort. This is an explicit priority under
uncertainty, not a measured saving or a licence to defer any existing right.
The floor itself may be unsourced: record and remedy that failure, rather than
calling people above an invented affordable ceiling ineligible.

**SD04 — Keep ordinary independence as a real alternative.** [Design136 SG](../../C05/15-service-first-personal-security.md)
selects the service-first individual guarantee as preferred continuing architecture.
IC's U foundation remains a substantive rival, no presumed destination: recurrent
proof can deter claims and amplify employer/family/official power.
An access-first period can become a permanent exclusionary settlement; publish
nonclaim, delays, assessment burdens, autonomy and unequal actual access alongside
budgets. The Assembly and affected public may prefer full U, a different service
organization or no change. No model, adviser or fiscal office selects that
political destination. Paid institutional standby supplements personal control;
it does not replace a person's due cash or prove that targeting is superior.

Design108 rejects the inherited wage distribution as an independently supported
research economy. It remains a frozen comparator, not a forecast of employers,
paid work, income tax or S cash. There is no national employment quota, ministry
assignment of private careers or permission test for every private purchase.
Budget forecasts may use credible aggregate evidence; this microcase exposes
critical dependencies rather than substitutes for that evidence. None of this
cancels actual work already performed, wage claims, pension units or due protection.
