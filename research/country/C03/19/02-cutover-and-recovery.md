# HT05–HT08. Change who can act while continuing the state's duties

*Part of [lawful handover](../19-lawful-handover-and-actual-control.md).*

**HT05 — End the outgoing officeholder's expired access.** At the actual
legal boundary, sector career custodians withdraw the outgoing
role's expired powers from accounts, sessions, devices, physical keys,
service identities, signing paths and delegated human or AI agents.
Review pending and already signed actions under their actual source law.
Do not execute an unsigned or stale new direction under expired authority.
Preserve independently lawful acts, due claims, evidence and ongoing
service orders. Change institutional credentials where the outgoing
holder could still use them. Preserve secure access to necessary
historical records and verification material. Where safe disconnection
takes time, prevent unauthorized consequential action by isolating the
affected route within existing authority and report the remaining
exposure. Loss of political office does not remove independent career,
personal, defense, opposition or legal record-access rights.

**HT06 — Give the successor independently issued role access.** The lawful
receiver gets new scoped authority for the office and actual accepted access
to necessary records, systems, premises and controls. Do not reuse the
predecessor's personal login or expose unrelated records. Keep final
decisions and execution approvals with the holders already required by
sector law. Log each granted ability and its lawful source. The new
executive receives no administrator role over election, court, audit,
local or independent editorial systems by reason of succession.
Operational and foreign clearance requirements retain their lawful
purpose and timely review. An outgoing official cannot use them to
withhold records or manufacture a second approval.

**HT07 — Recover a particular capability without creating a master key.**
Require covered providers and public offices to maintain preauthorized
institution-specific recovery, independently usable protected records
and lawful alternate execution. If a credential or vendor is blocked,
an authorized unimplicated sector officer and a separately accountable
qualified custodian verify the same scoped recovery task and implement
it under prior law. They cannot make the disputed legal decision or
grant themselves a new substantive power. Record the source, scope,
receiver, actual actions and expiry. A temporary recovery credential
ends on task completion or 24 elapsed hours from issue, whichever
comes first. Continued exceptional access requires a fresh reasoned ordinary
court order within the same statutory scope and actual ability to
execute, for no more than 24 hours at a time. Routine scoped successor
credentials follow their own law. Missing independent help leaves the
exceptional path unavailable. Independently authorized ordinary acts
and earlier emergency duties can continue. No shared national override.

**HT08 — Keep independently authorized services working.** Career and
sector officers continue duly authorized payroll, benefits, safe care,
public communications, protective casework, courts and election duties
without a renewed political signature where existing law permits.
Budget the actual alternate payment and receiving path in advance.
Use existing Treasury/DB/SF and OP/T scopes. Preserve original
deadlines, claimant records and reconciled prior transactions, so a
second path does not pay twice. Identify which actions genuinely
require the new holder, and refer their failure promptly. No spare
credential creates cash, appropriates funds, expands caretaker policy,
authorizes force or erases an old liability.

[DPA01–DPA08](07-deferred-public-acts-and-lawful-handover.md) identifies the
lawful stage and receiving authority for each material pending act.
