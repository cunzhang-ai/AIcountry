# External function cross-check for the end-of-life decision

5 October 2026 · Research navigation, not a second country design

The [UN Classification of the Functions of Government](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/cofog/revision)
groups **government expenditure purposes** into ten broad divisions.
This check maps each division to a current AIcountry owner; it does not
verify all detailed classes, actual spending, legal authority, service
delivery or a person's lived journey.

| COFOG division | Current first-order design owner | Limit of this match |
|---|---|---|
| 01 General public services | [C03](../country/C03-democratic-government.md), [C04](../country/C04-public-administration.md), [C06](../country/C06-public-finance.md), [C11](../country/C11-knowledge-culture-and-ai.md) | Lawful staff, records, tax and remedies unverified |
| 02 Defence | [C10](../country/C10/03-defense-intelligence-and-war.md) | No actual threats, command or force |
| 03 Public order and safety | [C08](../country/C08-everyday-law-and-justice.md), [C10](../country/C10/01-public-safety-and-emergencies.md) | Crime, coercion and emergency routes need actual courts and limits |
| 04 Economic affairs | [C05](../country/C05-economy-and-work.md), [C06](../country/C06-public-finance.md), [C09](../country/C09-land-and-ecology.md), [C10](../country/C10/05-customs-and-border-clearance.md) | Industry, transport, energy, trade and finance cannot be certified from headings |
| 05 Environmental protection | [C09](../country/C09-land-and-ecology.md) | Habitats, water, waste and animal welfare have different tests |
| 06 Housing and community amenities | [C07](../country/C07/02-housing-mobility-and-utilities.md), [C09](../country/C09/01-land-water-and-shared-planning.md) | Land, safe stock, water and reachable routes unmeasured |
| 07 Health | [C07](../country/C07-social-floor.md), [C10](../country/C10/01-public-safety-and-emergencies.md) | The generic palliative mention lacked a complete service path before design 52 |
| 08 Recreation, culture and religion | [C11](../country/C11/04-shared-places-and-association.md), [C02](../country/C02/01-personal-and-collective-rights.md) | Voluntary association and diverse rites need actual access |
| 09 Education | [C07](../country/C07/06-education-and-learning-power.md) | Staff, places and learner remedies unverified |
| 10 Social protection | [C05](../country/C05/03-income-and-distribution.md), [C07](../country/C07/04-retirement-security.md), [C07 care](../country/C07/07-care-and-support-through-life.md) | The [UN survivor class](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Structure/Detail/en/4/10_3_0) exposed the missing first payment after death |

Design 52 adds [relief](../country/C07/10-serious-illness-and-end-of-life-care.md),
[death and survivor decisions](../country/C08/08-end-of-life-decisions-and-survivors.md)
and [finance](../country/C06/14-end-of-life-and-survivor-finance.md).
This broad crosswalk is a bounded check. A future detailed class-by-class
comparison may expose additional gaps, but it cannot replace original
service observations, legal reconciliation, public choice or funding.
