# Two localities and two different gaps

This is an authored decision with illustrative units. It uses no Danish,
English or United States source or public authority. Assume actual staff and
places for the stated minimum services, subject to the shocks below.

## A late payment and separately unsustainable eligible debt

Locality A owes ten for lawful common services on day 3. It has seven in
usable cash and an undisputed four-unit transfer due on day 2 that the
national payer has delayed. Correcting the transfer before day 3 gives
eleven. If payment cannot arrive in time, an authorized three-unit bridge
covers the immediate shortfall only if an independent check establishes
receipt and safe repayment by the bridge maturity. If all four arrive,
repayment of three leaves one before other dues. A delay without a
reliable revised date fails this liquidity alternative. Reconcile the
four to the same national obligation once. An advance is repayable and
cannot be counted as recurring grant income.

The annual view counts any recurring portion of that transfer once within
17 of lawful local resources. It shows 15 of continuing lawful duties
and four of annual eligible unsecured financing payments. Without changes, the recurring gap is two. Verified
lawful voluntary terms that reduce the current four to two would balance
this narrow account. If all affected creditors will consent, use the
simpler ordinary agreement. Otherwise consider the proposed eligible
composition. Keep the whole dated cash and service tail, any price,
principal, interest and restrictions in the actual plan. Merely deferring
two into the next year fails if its later profile cannot be financed.

In the one affected class, independent eligible principal totals 100.
Seventy support the plan, 20 oppose and ten abstain. The two-thirds
threshold is met, while a separate court and elected test remains.
If 15 of the apparent supporting principal instead belongs to an
affiliate excluded from the count, the valid total is 85 and only 55
support. The threshold fails. Excluding an affiliate's vote cannot
cancel any valid claim that it holds.

## A continuing common service gap

Locality B has 17 recurring lawful units, 18 of continuing duties and
one of eligible debt payments. The combined gap is two. Even removing
the whole financing payment leaves one of continuing services unfunded.
A temporary three-unit advance could move the due date of the problem
but would create a debt without a recurring source for repayment.

**Select different responses.** A uses prompt transfer correction and
an eligible workout only if lawful ordinary finance or voluntary terms
cannot close the debt problem. B needs an elected recurring source or a
lawful change of prospective obligations with interim protection.
If the missing unit is an existing national mandate, its competent
national owner must address the lawful funded delivery path. A local
election, court remedy, accrued pay or due care retains its governing
law. If both places lose the same driver pool or payment rail, cash
and adjusted debt alone serve neither. The national budget must show
the actual eligible source, paid receiver and any unmet duty across both
localities, without offering the same reserve twice.
