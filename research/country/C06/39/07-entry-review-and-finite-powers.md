# Immediate entry review and powers that expire

Design 99 · Proposed research, not enacted law or funded bank protection.

**BR01 — Two decisions and two original events.** M09 entry requires its three cumulative grounds
and RA03's fixed two actual votes. M07 insured-deposit unavailability is separately decided by the
insurer's statutory claims officer or a competent ordinary court on omission, without M09's
critical-function test. [BF01–BF04](01-unavailability-and-insured-claims.md) is the one precise
owner of the two-hour report, six-hour reasoned decision, earliest objective-event T0 with independent court correction,
24-actual-hour advance and seven-working-day covered balance. U liquidation, a new official,
receiver/vendor or confirmation does not restart that event. Court correction of late recognition
exposes arrears, not an invented on-time receipt or a judicial M09 vote.

**BR02 — Source record at entry.** Before an M09 decision, the supervisor supplies dated source
balance sheets, due cash obligations, realizable collateral/asset ranges, secured and
client-property claims, connected bank group and benefit ownership, current M15 solvent-bank
liquidity eligibility, private cure offers, payment-network dependencies and an ordinary U
counterfactual. Identify original observations, transformations, uncertain claims and named
independent sources; no model ratio or political instruction substitutes for a finding. Where useful
time prevents a complete valuation, record what could be checked, the upper/lower uncertainty and
why a narrow provisional act is necessary. No initial valuation counts hypothetical Treasury rescue,
uncommitted insurer assets, forced healthy-bank credit, future fines or foreign FX as available
cash. A qualified independent valuation role is preassigned or promptly court-approved with actual
fees and source access; the resolving office cannot select a favorable AI output as its own
independent check.

**BR03 — Automatic 72-hour court review.** Immediately send the entry order, alternatives, protected
evidence, transfer/bridge/stay plan, M10 creditor-class effects, funding and receiving map to an
independent ordinary duty court. The court provides a meaningful hearing within 72 actual hours
measured from the **first actual M09 entry** affecting that bank and failure event, regardless of
new controller, vendor, bridge, case number or revised reasons. Notify the bank, insurer, affected
owners, creditors, depositors, client-property holders, workers and known service dependants by safe
useful channels; give B13 assistance and protected B14 adversarial gist/qualified counsel, with
necessary privacy and trade-secret limits. No claimant must file merely to activate this hearing.
Ordinary participants may contest competence, predicate, alternatives, linked collateral, class
treatment, receiving and remedy; no bond or compulsory internal appeal excludes essential
protection. A shorter useful-time B17 hearing is available where a transfer would irreversibly
consume rights.

**BR04 — Court powers and missed hearing.** The court tests actual M09 grounds and least interfering
lawful response, including M07/U continuity, rather than replacing prudential expertise with a
political rescue choice. It may stop a new unlawful act, narrow an overbroad transfer/stay, require
a fresh attributed decision, preserve original records/client property and order feasible correction
or M11 compensation; third-party lawful completed payments/transfers are not automatically reversed.
If 72 hours passes without a meaningful hearing, no further irreversible M09 write-down, conversion
or transfer may be implemented, including a new step under a previously issued order, until a
competent court provides meaningful review and a reasoned continuation/correction order within
existing M09 scope; an already licensed, funded receiver may maintain narrowly necessary existing
safe access, client custody, due wages and M07 payments under their own continuing law. Any
immediately necessary protective act needs its own independent useful-time B17 order, not an
official's declaration that the missed clock restarted. Missing a hearing does not make the failed
bank solvent, cancel a depositor's due claim or turn an unlawful past act into lawful precedent. A
court's own inability to hear is disclosed as unmet judicial capacity and remains subject to direct
urgent challenge. A hearing means an actual answerable case, accessible defense and reasoned interim
disposition, not a registered file or unanswered remote call. A B17 protective order can preserve
rights under its own law; it cannot supply missing RA03 votes or initiate an unchosen judicial M09
write-down/bridge.

**BR05 — The two-working-day stay has one life.** A particular termination stay runs at most two
working days from the **first actual M09 entry**, identifying affected contracts and the moment it
ends. A second transfer, bridge vendor, substituted authority or new label concerning the same
failure cannot restart it. It reaches only termination triggered solely by resolution, while
preserving performance and termination for independent actual nonperformance, settlement finality,
collateral/netting packages and withdrawal of segregated client property. Direct B17 review may end
or narrow an unlawful stay; a court cannot extend this M09 stay by general B17 case scheduling. At
expiry counterparties regain their actual source-law rights; previously valid transfers and payments
remain subject to their own merits/third-party protections, not automatically void.

**BR06 — Bridge, transfer and lapse.** The M09 order lists every transferred asset, liability,
linked security/netting set, data obligation, license, paid employee role, receiving account and
foreign-law limitation. The accepted willing licensed bridge or buyer assumes only the enumerated
obligations and funded operating plan; client property remains outside the estate, and no unaccepted
worker, insurer or Treasury guarantee is invented. The bridge's maximum two years runs from its
first actual establishment for this bank/failure event, with reasoned review every six months of
solvency, safe delivery, due claims, alternative operators and exit. Assignment, new vendor, shell,
merged bridge or control change does not reset either clock. After two years, continuation needs a
fresh Assembly statute stating term, source and losses; absent that, the authority must execute a
preplanned lawful sale, handback or U wind-down with M12 accepted receivers and preserve accrued
claims. The ordinary six-month review is no extension power. If no safe receiver/cash exists, the
shortage and due obligations remain visible and require competent elected funding or court
protection; no authority can compel free capital or claim a paper bridge delivered deposits. At the
two-year boundary, a residual legal entity may keep records/property and wind down under actual
U/custody law; that does not authorize new bridge banking, new deposits/credit or continuation of
expired M09 powers. Distinguish legal-person survival for old claims from renewed operational
authority.

**BR07 — No foreign or force shortcut.** A domestic entry, stay, transfer or bridge order does not
alter a foreign branch, law, guarantee, collateral, currency or recognition. Use B41–B52 and actual
instruments for cross-border cooperation; a missing foreign permission is an execution limit, not
domestic legal authority to seize. B18/B19 name the actual enforcement route, and LF requires a
separate prior actor/task/mode power before any public force. No M09 order itself authorizes a
private collector's lockout, surveillance or coercion of staff. Protected dispute records follow PD
and B14; confidentiality cannot hide amount, currency, legal owner, ranking, due date or the
answerable gist of public loss reasons from claimants and courts.
