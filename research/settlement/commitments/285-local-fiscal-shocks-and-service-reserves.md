# Design372 commitment. Pay for complete local protection on the due dates

[MF25–MF30](../../country/C04/08/06-local-fiscal-shocks-and-service-reserves.md)
requires a voted source, cutoff, independent priority, paid qualified
provider and confirmation of actual service. Reconcile competing calls,
available cash, separate continuing duties and the next reserve draw.
Preserve local elected control, original claim dates and independent
remedy. A missed or duplicate transfer remains visible on its own date.

The [worked reserve](../local-fiscal-shocks-and-service-reserves/01-two-localities-and-one-national-reserve.md)
uses illustrative funding. The observed transfers establish neither
adopting authority nor local capacity.
