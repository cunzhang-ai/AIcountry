# OP01–OP04: begin with the same people and their actual claims

*Part of [C06/45](../45-country-operation-and-handover.md); design 122 candidate.*

**OP01 — One opening community, not a blank national balance sheet.** Retain
CX's retrofit reference and SD's authored 100,000 people. Do not infer their age,
health, household, migration, employment, title or currency distribution from
that number. Different alternatives inherit the same actual homes, networks,
firms, ecology, debt, pensions, contracts and due rights. Greenfield is separately
paid, not a free source of land, workforce or relief from old claims. The
reference is a research context, not a bounded list of people permitted to belong.

**OP02 — Maintain four distinct responsibilities.** Existing legally due claims,
universal service/democratic/remedy availability, actual voluntary use and new
prospective guarantees have different triggers under SD02. Publish essential
capabilities and their delivery owners, including people who do not claim or
cannot participate. Do not require a complete personal dossier to receive help.
Private wages, family obligations and supplier losses remain actual counterpart
claims, not automatically government expenditure or inaccessible family wealth.
Preserve I13–24's personal control and actual authorized component receipt.

**OP03 — Use the full function map without freezing society into it.** The
37-function operating register names delivery, support, independent protection,
source/capital/transition questions; the 34 predecessor commitment modules and original 61
selection mappings remain connected. Their presence is scope visibility, not
cost completeness or substantive revalidation. An unfamiliar activity or need
can add or change a row. Do not count a generic old justice/care/administration
bid as the full price of OJ/AA/AS/WL/OE or erase an already funded matching part.

**OP04 — Preserve household, national and external perspectives.** Compare
received needs, privacy/agency, paid and chosen unpaid time, ecological pressure,
work and bargaining, political opposition and enforceable correction alongside
cash. A cheap service may shift cost onto relatives, travel, workers or foreign
producers. A higher public budget may replace private bills without increasing
real use, or finance a real improvement. No welfare scalar, tax ratio, output
residual, survey mean or greater formalization establishes that lives are better.
Actual participation, authority, paid readiness and outcomes remain distinct.
