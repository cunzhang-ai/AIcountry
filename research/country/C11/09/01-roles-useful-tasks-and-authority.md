# DP01–DP04: support is an actual public job with no sovereign franchise

*Part of [C11/09](../09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md); prospective assignment.*

**DP01 — Coverage and reserved acts.** Apply to public digital/AI support and
critical service operators already holding an actual statutory essential-service,
financial, electoral, judicial, utility or public-delivery duty in scope of their
sector law. Private coverage is limited to actual M deposit/payment operators,
NB safeguarded-custody/clearing functions, licensed essential utilities/networks
and contracted statutory public essential-delivery functions, not every firm
with ordinary financial duties. DP creates no missing sector licence.
A private hobby, ordinary private expression or research tool is not
licensed by this Act. Agency budgets/procurement permit only actual delegated
service tasks within law, not new substantive eligibility, surveillance or force.
PD, B, AP, TS, M/NB, EA/OJ/CJ, professional and constitutional rules retain their
exact ownership. The task's whole actual effect controls; splitting one adverse
decision into harmless-looking recommendations cannot evade genuine judgment.

**DP02 — A career custodian, not an AI minister.** Assign a Public Digital
Operations Director within the existing C04 shared-support arrangement; this
post also performs C10's already proposed bounded technical cyber coordination.
Cabinet appoints through open qualified C04 competition with reasons, actual
systems/continuity/public-service/privacy competence, equivalent practice,
conflicts/objections and direct courts. Apply protected career tenure/removal,
qualified deputy's180-elapsed-day routine limit and14-day unexpected recruitment
duty expressly described in OE26. No simultaneous minister/party executive or
materially conflicted vendor/controller. Qualified assigned officers perform
their own technical/custody acts; head vacancy cannot erase their duties.

Director runs funded shared support, public service agreements, technical warning/
mutual-aid records and source-backed continuity planning. Director cannot direct
a judge, election, editor, tax/benefit case, clinical choice or protected-source
publication, obtain all institutions' keys, approve their own contested incident,
or compel their use of one host. Institutions retain actual funded independent
support where a shared controller would defeat their duties/remedy. Parliament
controls law/source; AA, PD, OJ/CJ and sector scrutiny keep their own powers.
Serious own-service misconduct goes to actual outside-command inquiry, not the
Director's final investigation. No new ministry, cyber court or AI licensing board.

**DP03 — Build useful capabilities that can be declined.** Within an actual
appropriated work plan the shared function may provide replaceable hosting,
accessible translation/navigation, source-preserving model evaluation, lawful
records exchange and common procurement/recovery expertise. Sector/public users
may retain qualified local, cooperative, other-provider or simpler tools; no
compulsory pooling of case records. A general assistant helps understand enacted
rules/options, prepare applications or compare policies, not certify their truth,
grant consent or issue a binding decision. Keep genuine human direct access;
no unlimited personal compute entitlement or permanent relationship dossier.

| Useful task | Accountable owner/action and actual limit |
|---|---|
| Claim help/translation | Person chooses assistance; AP receiver records legal receipt and clarification. Summary cannot withdraw a claim or waive reply. |
| Routine administration | Authorized rule/records officer can delegate narrow favorable/reversible calculations or uncontested payments within exact law, ceiling and quick correction. No fresh click for every payment; no final adverse essential effect by unattended AI. |
| Clinical/learning/inspection options | Actual qualified professional controls relevant judgment/support, HC learning and TS operating domain; patient/student/worker is no fictional fallback. |
| Infrastructure maintenance/safety | Actual operator may use forecasts and independently qualified bounded minimum-risk automation. New site allocation, coercion, personal target or resource burden needs its own competent source. |
| Democratic/evidence scenarios | Assembly/EV/source owners control their respective law/facts/publication. Expose alternatives and missing inputs; no personalized government political ranking or model veto over legislation. |

**DP04 — Admit a task through its actual owner.** The public/sector authority
names a qualified deployment officer with real scope, source access, refusal
power and paid time under C04/professional law. Officer publishes safe purpose,
legal delegation, actions/data, whole impact, actual ceiling/expiry, source/
benefit baseline, incident/exit and independent challenge; sensitive inspection
uses protected access. A material consequential use needs a distinct qualified
review outside the deployment/vendor conflict as TS/PD/sector law requires.
Director supplies support, not a universal approval stamp. No separate Act for
each clerical tool where an actual statute already delegates it; no procurement
contract supplies missing public power. A research/pilot label creates none.
Beneficial lawful automation remains possible; protected adverse/political/
clinical/force acts retain their own actual human authority and consent rules.

[AH core assistance](../../C04/07-person-directed-public-assistance.md)
now gives the person-side service debtor/provider/source outside this shared
technical function. DP can supply qualified optional tools; Director/model
gets no client files, blanket agent mandate, final claim or appeal power.
