# Design216. Sustain housing and lawful recovery after displacement

## Baseline and choice

Design215 is the pinned baseline. Follow the current framework and
decision template. C09 already chooses a comparison of safe return,
adaptation and supported movement. C06 supplies disaster finance. LA,
XR and FT govern related property, service and transfer duties. The
unresolved issue is whether a place plan can work for occupants when
property, money and public receivers are changing at different times.

Select FR01–FR08 as the working country design. Give actual occupants
direct access to help, evidence and review. Keep immediate danger,
future hazard, land rights and elected spending decisions separate.
Price early protection, shared mitigation, voluntary movement and
long-run land and service duties before relying on the plan. Show any
risk and local cost that moves with the money. Verify actual receiving
and carry outstanding cases into ordinary administration.

## Alternatives, evidence and objections

Retain safe repair as a real alternative, including shared catchment
works where physical risk and full costs justify them. Property-led
resolution can settle valid interests efficiently and still leave people
without a usable place. General relocation may avoid residual risk at
a site and impose lasting losses on a community that could have stayed
safely. A mixed place plan can preserve safe return and protect the people
who cannot return. Its higher coordination load requires real receivers,
timely money, review and operational evidence.

The Auckland accounts support this scope. The same Crown cap could
coexist with changed local costs, and closing an office required
transferring unfinished work. Existing navigation and risk communication
also show practical starting points. This evidence cannot establish each
occupant's experience or a causal advantage for the proposed design.
A blanket promise to replace all prior asset values would have unknown
future costs and different distributional effects. Offsets need a
loss-specific decision so that valid distinct claims are preserved.

## Compatibility and further decision

Preserve C07 and E immediate duties, C06 and FB spending authority, MF
local and residual funding, XR service continuity, LA protections for
lawful acquisition, and FT and T transfer rules. Keep first receipt,
valuation challenges, appeal clocks, safe data and unpaid obligations.
No new compulsory power, insurance guarantee, emergency extension or
budget follows. A funding decision does not change legal interests or
remove individual remedies. AI evidence is subject to DP and review.
A new implementation would require authorized mapping of existing owners,
cash and case records before a transfer, with paid overlap and acceptance.

Test one authorized local hazard scenario with a receiving community,
tenants, owners and people needing accessible housing, using actual
places and anonymized dates. Compare the strongest existing local route
with the selected package under late cash and incomplete hazard data.
Reopen for missed households, delayed actual occupancy, material risk
transfer, lost case ownership or better verified adaptation. Affected
participation, independent review, enactment, funding and lived outcomes
for the proposed country remain open.
