# Release food reserves against actual shortages and the next need

*FM06–FM11. Candidate rules for the national rotating food reserve.*

**FM06 — Give the reserve an enacted purpose and dated inventory.** The
Assembly sets protected purposes, usable stock ranges, funding, eligible
receivers and release and replenishment authority. Distinguish protected
food access, authorized commercial rotation and any separately approved
price intervention. Record food by ownership, quality, usable life,
dietary suitability, necessary preparation, location and delivery time.
Separate immediately usable stock from contracts, quarantined lots,
ordinary commercial inventory and already committed supplies. Test common
dependencies across warehouses, transport, fuel, water, money and staff.
Use safe public aggregates and qualified independent stock verification.

**FM07 — Choose a tool for the actual problem.** The food authority
compares accessible and affordable market supply, a price shock with
real food available, and a physical shortage or unusable route. The
income authority applies existing person-side support on its own law.
Prioritize qualified delivery of suitable food where money alone
cannot secure it in time. A wider market release needs its own legal
purpose and evidence on who can buy, price and producer effects, and
remaining protected supply. A change in a price index or headline stock
level cannot authorize release or certify food received. The local
food-access owner remains responsible for actual receiving under FM01–FM05.

**FM08 — Release the smallest useful complete amount for a named duty.**
The authorized officer identifies the eligible need, lot, food safety
release, transporter, receiving provider, paid resources and last
useful time. Use the next two or more credible delivery windows, the
same physical balances and the same affected groups before deciding.
Reserve identified later needs once. A lawful urgent departure from
the planned balance needs an explicit reason, competent authority,
review and a priced later protection path or an exposed gap. Keep
independent recall and substitution usable. Follow existing priority
law if supply cannot meet every claim. A reserve decision supplies no
new rationing, requisition, unsafe-use or entitlement power.

**FM09 — Rotate and replenish with independent dates and sources.** Plan
safe rotation before loss of usable life, using fair lawful purchase
and onward delivery terms. Do not release protected lots for rotation
on an assumed replacement before the required date. If a needed
rotation or inspection fails, update the usable balance, protect
current recipients and report any later gap. Separately record
procurement authority, gross cost, accepted suppliers, quality,
actual arrival and next callable delivery. A future purchase, expected
reimbursement, import allocation or signed option does not replenish
current stock. Include foreign supply and domestic producer effects,
and keep restricted or already committed assets with their owners.

**FM10 — Verify actual receipt and keep correction reachable.** Track
released, in transit, accepted and actually usable food separately.
Give a person without safe water, cooking, transport or digital access
a direct lawful route to suitable food and outside review. Distinguish
problems of affordability, dispatch, substitution, safety and nonreceipt.
An unmet duty retains its original date and applicable remedy. Keep
ordinary income and nutrition rights, and credit only an authorized
matching component that was actually received. Independent food safety
officials may stop unsafe lots under existing law. Report the effect
on other promised deliveries before redirecting shared vehicles or staff.

**FM11 — Return to ordinary supply and keep the reserve accountable.**
The elected authority reviews use, exposure, replenishment and loss
on stated dates. Where prices remain high, compare ordinary retail,
a funded cash response and recurring qualified provision before
making exceptional reserve use routine. Cost the origin, second
replenishment, alternate and funded handover separately. Protect
appropriate alternatives for food needs the ordinary route cannot
meet. Stop exceptional distribution under its own terms
when ordinary access works in the affected places, with verified
receiving and a qualified next route. Preserve outstanding food,
contract, pay and correction duties when operator or policy changes.
Audit original stock, source and delivery with protected access and
safe public reasons. AI may reconcile balances and compare dated
scenarios. It cannot certify safety, change priority or authorize a
release. Reopen on repeated spoilage, exclusion, market distortion,
incomplete replenishment or a better qualified ordinary supply route.
