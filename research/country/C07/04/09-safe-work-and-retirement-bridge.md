# Make safe work and retirement a real choice before pension age

*Y36–Y41. Candidate bridge implementing Y04 and Y19.*

**Y36 — Protect the work decision before the pension decision.** Offer a
person approaching standard pension age an independent route when a
persistent functional limit or evidenced occupational exposure makes
their existing work materially unsafe or infeasible. The reference
bridge covers ages 60 through the day before standard age 65. The
adopting Act must specify commencement and cohorts. Eligibility requires
a documented loss of covered work earnings from a reduction or exit
needed on those grounds. A person may accept safe reduced work or leave
without electing an early annuity. Maintain ordinary safety, disability,
income, housing and care protection at every age. An employer retains
duties of prevention, accommodation, wages and compensation. A bridge
payment cannot authorize hazardous work or discharge an employer's debt.

**Y37 — Assess actual work and feasible protection.** The authority
obtains proportionate evidence of tasks, exposure, functional limits
and workplace controls, using an independent occupational assessment
and the worker's account. Use published task-linked standards and
case-specific reasons. Ask only for necessary evidence. Keep a direct
route where the employer, a prior business closure or a disputed
record obstructs proof. Offer suitable accommodation, reduced hours
or voluntary transfer where a qualified, accessible and paid option
exists. A hypothetical job or unpaid family assistance supplies no
earnings. A worker need not attempt unsafe work, take an involuntary
placement or prove inability to do every job. Job titles, predicted
lifespan and model scores cannot decide eligibility. An exposure
schedule needs its own qualified evidence and public adoption. An
unsafe task remains subject to ordinary correction regardless of
whether it appears in the schedule.

**Y38 — Define the bridge and match only received resources.** Pay the
individual from a separately voted general-revenue source. For the
reference comparison use the positive gap between the lesser of the
ordinary adult basket and 60 percent of verified reference net covered
earnings, and actual net work earnings plus compatible wage replacement
safely received for the same loss and period. Use the existing EB
reference-earnings evidence and correction method to reconstruct
comparable net earnings on the bridge period. Keep EB’s own gross
assessment, calendar and term. Match only compatible net amounts
actually received for the same loss and days, under express law.
Set any withholding and corresponding gross appropriation explicitly. Keep unpaid wage claims and cost compensation
distinct. Under the proposed I05 treatment, classify net bridge cash
actually received as replacement earnings with the existing single
disregard and taper. Price this interaction at its actual date and
keep the hardship and higher-needs routes. The reference percentage
and cap need enacted law and qualified evidence. Neither an expected
offset nor an unreceived payment supplies current cash.

**Y39 — Carry a person into ordinary retirement without a gap.** Preserve
earlier earnings units, applicable credits and elected shares. Bridge
cash alone grants no new accrual and buys no claim on restricted pension
assets. The benefits authority and pension payer confirm the bridge's
last covered day, the applicable pension eligibility and first safely
received payment. Preserve original dates and separate review. Where
a pension payment is late, use applicable paid hardship and correction
routes. No automatic bridge extension or deemed pension receipt is
created. Ordinary Y04 elections retain their own informed, voluntary
decision and common factors.

**Y40 — Fund independent assessment, decision and appeal.** Assign a
qualified benefits officer to receive and decide the claim under
applicable source clocks, with an independent occupational opinion
and a staffed substitute outside the employer. Use E06 for earlier
urgent needs. Give the worker usable reasons, protected evidence and
independent challenge of denial, delay or unsafe conditions. Treasury
uses the actual general source and verifies safe personal receipt.
The elected budget prices full payments, assessment, accommodation,
independent review and the overlap with long-term care and pensions.
Keep later tax or needs effects separate from cash available at release.
If staffing or source fails, report the uncovered duty and use the
authorized remedy without changing the person's statutory dates.

**Y41 — Review who can choose and who bears the cost.** Publish safe
findings on applications, exclusions, evidence problems, safe work,
income receipt, transition and impacts across occupations and groups.
Compare ordinary prevention and accommodation, the funded bridge,
stronger general income protection and any proposed age change on the
same population and dates. Protect lawful mobility and private health
information. AI may help explain options, compare safe schedules or
check arithmetic. It cannot rate a person's fiscal worth, decide
fitness or approve a pension. Preserve records, worker rights and
paid successors. Reopen on exclusion, employer cost shifting, repeated
late payment or a stronger funded ordinary alternative.
