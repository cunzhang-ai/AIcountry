# Refusal, exit and continued access to essential services

*Design323 · FRE01–FRE05 · Proposed common protection for essential public provision.*

**FRE01 — Protect each due right from leverage over another choice.** A public
body, delegated provider or joint service may offer one door and coordinated
help. It shall preserve separate choices and decisions for a person's due
income, housing, health, care, education and utilities. It may not reduce,
delay or degrade an independently due benefit or service to obtain participation
in an optional programme, political or institutional loyalty, withdrawal of a
complaint, a particular provider or living arrangement, unrelated consent, or
use of an AI system. Apply to practical consequences as well as written terms,
including priority, price, effort, delay and destination. The responsible
public duty owner answers for its contractors and common infrastructure.

**FRE02 — Give each necessary condition its own reason, source and boundary.**
A provider that requires an act for the requested service identifies the
prior-law basis, concrete function or safety need, evidence and practicable
less restrictive ways to meet it. It provides an accessible notice and help
to challenge the condition. Consider alternative proof and delivery before
withholding the specific act. Refusing an optional package or withdrawing
its mandate cannot stand in for proof of a real change in need, eligibility
or actual resources. A person may decline a particular act without a record
claiming it was delivered. Necessary clinical consent, actual statutory
criteria, lawful safety restrictions and others' rights retain their own
procedures. This rule grants no power to compel care, override fair work or
allocate another person's protected capacity.

**FRE03 — Finance a usable route outside the controlling provider.** Before
assigning a sole or integrated provider, the programme owner tests ordinary
direct access and a paid alternate for each protected task whose interruption
could cause material loss. The alternate needs timely intake, qualified
receiving, lawful minimum evidence, independent payment and the ability to
act without the challenged provider's assent. Reserve actual capacity on
the joint calendar. A shared reception desk is compatible with this rule
where its failure cannot stop both paths. Use independently controlled
provision for the affected function where common control defeats practical
refusal or timely relief. Retain worker pay, consent and safe assignment
obligations. Compare recurrent costs, waiting, travel, privacy and bargaining
effects alongside nominal savings.

**FRE04 — Protect people before the sanction becomes a fait accompli.** A
credible specific threat, cutoff or materially worse route gives the person
direct access to existing independent review and urgent court relief,
with paid assistance and execution. The affected duty owner shows the
lawful basis and service-specific reason for the disputed condition with
original evidence. Reviewers may require available ordinary receipt,
scope-limited interim continuation or an independently accepted alternate
within their powers, with fair hearing and applicable urgent safeguards.
Keep current awards and earlier essential deadlines in force under their
law. Distinguish a coerced response, a genuine refusal, an actual shortage
and an unsupported allegation. Use separate notice and proof before changing
an underlying entitlement. Where qualified receiving is unavailable,
record the missed act, pursue existing safe interim protection and bring
the uncovered obligation to the competent budget authority.

**FRE05 — Keep refusals private and report actual delivery.** Record the
decision and necessary basis for review without turning refusal, complaints
or non-use into a cross-service risk flag. Keep safe direct contact and
purpose-limited evidence. A person's chosen agent acts only within a
revocable mandate. AI can identify scheduling and common-control risks
from authorized data. It cannot infer consent, decide whether a condition
is lawful or approve its own alternate. Review whole-provider patterns
through independent audit and existing remedy powers. Preserve prior
contracts and accrued claims under lawful prospective migration, with
new service and transfer arrangements funded before use. Reopen if
retaliation shifts to delay, the alternate cannot act, independent
capacity displaces comparable due care, or a simpler structure delivers
equivalent freedom and receiving.

compares coordination with actual freedom to leave. Operational capacity
and legal adoption require separate verification.
