# One balance and two payment windows

This constructed case tests [TPE01–TPE08](../../country/C06/60/11-public-cash-and-independent-execution.md).
The opening public balance is 20 units. A common release failure makes
12 inaccessible for the first useful payment window. Assume the remaining
8 are physically accessible, held under enacted purposes and can reach
recipients through a qualified independent route. In a comparison with all
20 units on the failed route, no new payment can execute in the first window.
Adding a second interface to that same route leaves the result unchanged.

| First-window obligation or authority | Amount | Verified disposition |
|---|---:|---|
| Established benefits, assigned exclusively to the alternate before the failure | 4 | Paid and received |
| Authorized court service payroll, assigned exclusively to the alternate | 2 | Paid and received |
| Separate resident's subsistence award submitted on the failed route | 3 | Settlement unknown at the deadline, no principal replay |
| Separately authorized urgent advance for that resident | Up to 2 | Two units received under its own terms and source |

Six units of the independent balance cover the first two obligations.
Two other units were lawfully reserved for the advance. The 12 inaccessible
units cannot be used to fund the same dates, and the advance does not close
the original award by itself.

If independent evidence in the next window shows the original instruction
did not settle, and the applicable law allows the received advance to
count toward the same obligation, one further unit settles the original
award. Assume a separately restored, legally available primary source
for that residual. If the full original award did settle, replay pays
zero principal and the advance follows its own law. If evidence remains
uncertain, keep the outstanding principal unresolved and the independent
remedy open. Record a later finding about actual receipt at its true date.

Separately, the alternate must meet a second-window call for four units.
The first-window eight are already used. Only three fresh lawful units
are accessible by the next deadline. The fourth arrives later. In this
branch the standing schedule identifies separate payable amounts of three
and one. One unit misses the deadline even though aggregate balances can
later reconcile. This comparison supplies no new priority rule or new
spending authority.

The amounts and dependencies are assumptions. The reproduction checks the
dated account and safeguards, not the real finality or cost of any payment
system. [Reproduction](verify-public-cash.py).
