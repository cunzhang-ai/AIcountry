# C06. One country operating resource book

15 October 2026 · Design 79 · Proposed allocation, not an affordable launch

Join the country choices in **one operating book**, with a single source for
each person, asset and cost; separate entitlement, proposed allocation,
estimated requirement, actual cash, safe capacity and received remedy.
Give every currently audited function a proposed bid without pretending
that a positive budget establishes a sufficient service or a complete law.

| Canonical part | Consequence for the country |
|---|---|
| [Allocation, responsibility and remaining needs](22/01-allocation-and-responsibility.md) | All 37 functions share one component ledger; a share is not a second bill or proven capacity |
| [Income, insurance and counterpart finance](22/02-income-insurance-and-counterparts.md) | I05, continued pension accrual, explicit backing and every owner remain in the same account |
| [Sources, dates and actual readiness](22/03-sources-dates-and-readiness.md) | Opening credit, independent FX, maturity, physical capacity, transition and authority remain explicit |

The [proposed operating book](../../settlement/country-book/README.md)
contains 8 payroll and 15 purchase components, allocated once across the
37 current functions. In the authored 100,000-person context, payroll is
491.05 million and purchases 562; public replacement 29.75 is separate.
The book includes the S workload repair roles and a mature pension-history
fixture; it replaces old aggregates instead of adding them again.

Under fixed hypothetical prices, full voluntary profit payout, existing I05
and specified tax schedules, normal planned five-sector cash closes with
ongoing pension accrual and explicit insurance backing. Required output is
4,266.89 million. Its reported 52.33% market-GVA/FTE comparison was based on
a mislabeled measure and is retired as current support. The [design-79 bridge](28/01-production-and-evidence.md)
gives about 50.32% for this old book and 50.19% for the selected SF hybrid.
These are declared-price required demand/productivity, not achieved production,
AI gain, actual source finance, welfare or a reason to ratify the tax rates.

The candidate fails the stated water-loss test; private foreign settlement
fails during the export-loss exercise; coincident principal maturities
violate the Treasury cash floor. Opening credit, new skilled staff, all
actual requirements and lifetime claims remain unverified. **No function is
fully workload-qualified and no complete operating country is established.**

Select the joined book and these qualifications as a research instrument
within the existing whole-country commitment choice. Bid amounts, tax/asset
assumptions and a particular financing contract are not adopted national
law. [C05/08](../C05/08-income-ownership-and-country-choice.md) now compares
targeted, universal-base and mutual-ownership architectures under the same
bids. Retain I05 conditionally; no operating package is qualified. Reopen on
discriminating source/incidence evidence, not a cheaper invented parameter.
Updating the book grants no governing power.
