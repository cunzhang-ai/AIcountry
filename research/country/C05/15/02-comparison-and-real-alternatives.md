# SG05–SG08: choose a country bargain and keep its strongest rivals

*Part of [C05/15](../15-service-first-personal-security.md), design 136 candidate.*

**SG05 — Revise the destination, retain the disagreement.** Design135 combined
service-first A/I05 operation with a preferred eventual U+C2 destination. Select
service-first individual guaranteed security as both the present research
reference and the preferred continuing architecture. Full U is now a live
alternative, not a promised later stage whose desirability is already settled.
This supersedes only that preference in IC01/IC15, SD04 and PW's country-level
framing. IC's plural paid capital/investment rules and C2's specified tax
counterpart *within a future U comparison* remain; A uses the same tax function.

The reason is priority, not a foreign model's verdict: resource claims should
answer inadequate protection and actual service needs before a presumed new
universal cohort. Do not make a temporary access-first stage excuse permanent
exclusion. The modelled ability to target money does not establish safe access,
low assessment cost, adequate nonwork life or political independence.

**SG06 — Compare complete U rather than a deliberately weakened substitute.**
The live U+C2 rival keeps the same essential services, actual housing/access/
care costs, source-law pensions/credits, old net protections, private choice and
independent remedies. Match ordinary U/Y basic once; extra needs remain assessed.
Its case must identify additional tax/source burdens, actual receipt, supply,
administration and paid overlap. No compulsory benefit cancellation, invented
growth or health/admin saving makes its budget neutral. A higher-source package
can still be worth choosing; the public may reject this working preference.

The [published-context and country comparison](../../../settlement/service-first-security/README.md)
keeps source law, household means, insurance, childcare charges, work hours and
retirement scope visible. Published cash incentive ratios are not absolute
living standards, observed work choices or candidate-country tax rates. A
source arrangement that cancels earned claims or presumes family access cannot
qualify the rights-preserving rival by retaining its favorable headline alone.

**SG07 — Service-first is not a single-provider or ownership command.** Retain
OP's public delivery backbone and voluntary plural routes, SC's contestable
buying/paid continuity and NS's service-first diversified retrofit. Direct
public provision, capable cooperative/private providers and negotiated common
provision are genuine alternatives for a defined duty. Compare quality, control,
worker conditions, entry/exit, concentration, capital/maintenance, price and
failure alongside cash; public margins are not inherently waste and removed
supplier profit is not an actual available source or a free public asset.

Keep real staff and suppliers, funded readiness, court/enforcement independence,
source ownership and external obligations. Replacing a provider requires actual
title/contract/record/payroll transfer, receiving capacity and paid overlap.
Cheap direct-public arithmetic does not settle this institutional choice.

**SG08 — Preserve dissent that could displace this settlement.** Repeated means
assessment can expose private lives, discourage claims, amplify family/employer/
official power and exclude people unable to prove need. Adequate U can protect
independence even for people with no immediate material shortfall. A could
also cost more than U under different sources, administration or behavior.
These are material objections, not implementation noise. Conversely, universal
cash can leave inaccessible services, unequal ownership, rent extraction and
expensive residual needs unchanged. No numerical average resolves the public
value choice. Neither same-controller AI analysis nor a source's household
model establishes affected participation or independent judgment.
