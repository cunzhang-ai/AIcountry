# Bounded reach, actual immunity and genuine adjudication

*G09–G10; part of [C08/18](../18-international-crimes-and-accountability.md).*

**G09 — National connections and a presence route.** Domestic G jurisdiction
exists for substantial material conduct/result here or a citizen/actual
habitual resident's conduct at the time, with no nationality/profile-based
guilt. No foreign legality exception excuses the defined core crimes.
Aggression requires our own leadership/state act or substantial territorial
conduct within lawful competence; this candidate does not assert universal
jurisdiction over every foreign state's political leadership.

For genocide, crimes against humanity and war crimes committed wholly
abroad by foreigners, select a presence-based route: lawful presence of the
accused and a demonstrated applicable international/customary or treaty
basis recognizing the particular core crime and domestic competence, not
merely a broader domestic extension. A preliminary independent court hears
that basis, source law/time, immunity and meaningful defense. Separate
actual recognized minimum from new national protected grounds/scope.
No lower proof burden, blanket refugee screening or citizenship selection.

Credible safely obtained information can be preserved/investigated under
actual lawful powers even if no accused is present; it creates no worldwide
search, abduction, rendition, foreign asset control or in-absence trial.
A domestic G trial requires lawful presence and effective participation.
If a genuine competent international/foreign route is usable, consider it
with G13 protections and independent reasons; an unaccepted proposed forum
cannot make the national case disappear. No victim's nationality is a veto.

**G10 — Office, immunity and prior proceedings differ.** No domestic official
position excuses a proved crime or creates a home-office permission to
block evidence. Actual international personal/functional/diplomatic immunity
and foreign-state privileges require a specific reasoned determination;
neither a general no-immunity slogan nor rank alone settles lawful competence.
Identify court, person, act, source, period, possible waiver and actual
cooperation obligations. ICC rules do not automatically waive every person's
immunity before a national court. A minister cannot select an expedient result.

Genuine competent final adjudication retains P15/P22 protection. As a narrow
international-crime exception to the **foreign** ordinary-finality bar, a
different independent judicial formation may permit a G proceeding only
on prosecution proof beyond reasonable doubt that the prior foreign case
was designed to shield the accused, or lacked independence/impartiality in
such a way that it was not genuinely intended to bring the person to justice.
Hear actual source/process evidence with defense; a different sentence,
acquittal, nationality or political disagreement is insufficient.

This exception does not reopen domestic acquittals under a generic allegation,
declare the underlying G elements proved or authorize repeat litigation until
conviction. Prior actual deprivation/payments receive full credit and G11
total-burden protection. A separately valid international court's competence/
admissibility requires its own actual source law. Safe victims' support and
civil remedies remain available without a fresh criminal conviction.
