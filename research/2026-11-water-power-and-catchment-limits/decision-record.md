# Design262 decision record

**Baseline and requested change.** Design261 protects connected
water and ecological limits under WS and recognizes correlated energy
loss under EG and PS. OC separates restored electricity from service
receipt. Select a common water and electricity operating comparison
before counting firm generation in a hot and dry period. Specify
who controls each decision and what remains when a safe route fails.
Use the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision handoff](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

**Evidence and rival diagnoses.** The French records distinguish
multiple technical constraints, a request, lawful authority, actual
operation and limited environmental evidence. A shortfall may reflect
too little qualified power, reliance on one river, a legal limit, or
poor delivery after power returns. They require different changes.
No affected person, environmental representative, operator or lawful
authority has participated in AIcountry's own decision. The proposed
country's permits and limits need original site evidence.
[Source and scope](sources.md)

**Alternatives and selection.** Keep ordinary site-specific limits
with generation reduced where needed. Compare paid flexibility,
independent supply and direct continuity on the same protected
loads. Retain a least-scope temporary operating adjustment only
where already available under valid law, within independent
safety and environmental bounds and through a funded handoff.
Reject a general power emergency override. In the authored case
ordinary operation, one firm independent MW and one lawful flexible
MW cover all protected demand. If the firm route fails, the modeled
temporary adjustment helps but leaves a two MWh protected gap.
A lower-harm qualified direct service may cover it.
[Matched comparison](../settlement/water-power-and-catchment-limits/02-matched-heat-and-supply-comparison.md)

**Control and objections.** The grid operator verifies reliability,
the competent environmental authority verifies the receiving envelope
and the plant safety authority keeps its own role. All depend on
paid independent evidence and actual enforcement. Sharing a meter,
data intermediary, appointment patron or water-dependent backup can
defeat practical independence. A system operator can overstate need
or omit a usable demand alternative. An environmental actor can
hide a material delay or uncontrolled uncertainty. Preserve original
evidence and reasons for challenge by affected downstream users,
workers, service users and elected opposition. Protect sensitive
infrastructure and household data. A court enforces lawful duties
within its scope. AI helps reconcile the simultaneous constraints.

**Migration and financing.** Do not change actual water, plant or
electricity permissions by changing capacity accounts. Preserve
prior claim dates, essential priorities, catchment protection,
emissions budgets, worker and host obligations and paid reserves.
New contracts and optional starts use actually qualified capability.
A later decision does not transfer prior reviews or erase injury.
Reconcile actual firm capacity and spending, deferral compensation
and donor burden under PS, EG, OC, WS, CBG, RC and JC.

**Next bounded decision.** Have the relevant operators and regulators
prepare one four-hour summer window with local water, health, power,
independent review and source records. Verify an ordinary route and
a credible substitute under common heat or loss of a tie, including
actual downstream receiving, financial cap, safe run-down and reserve
recovery. Reopen on changed flow or quality, late receipt, missing
monitoring, pressure for serial adjustments or a lower-harm firm
route. Report source qualification, actual legal authority, readiness
and outcomes separately.
