# OP17–OP20: change institutions without abandoning their duties

*Part of [C06/45](../45-country-operation-and-handover.md); design 122 candidate.*

**OP17 — Choose obligation-specific succession, not a national reset day.**
Reject an administrative all-at-once migration as the default. The competent
adopting source law names the old obligor, new legal successor, scope and exact
handover date for each service, employment, contract, record, report, case,
appeal, debt and foreign duty. Effective dates remain those actually enacted;
there is no new universal 180-day clock. A successor inherits the specified
pending obligation, not just favorable assets or future applications. Existing
constitutional/appointment/statutory change routes still govern, without a
universal incumbent veto or ministerial power to invent offices.

**OP18 — Pay for overlap and preserve actual receiving.** Retain the original
lawful operator during authorized overlap, pay both distinct work streams and
transfer staff/assets/records only under actual employment, privacy, title,
procurement and court protections. Match eligible paid reassignment, worker
assent where required, pension/leave, training and replacement to each task.
Use bounded lawful dual running or shadow tests without double payment for one
received outcome; do not make people resubmit a claim merely because systems
changed. Operational acceptance is evidence of receiving, not authority to alter
law, waive consent, reduce standards or postpone a date. A public claimant need
not obtain an interagency acceptance certificate to enforce their right.

**OP19 — Allocate failure liability explicitly in the adopting law.** Until
legal transfer the old obligor remains answerable; after it the named successor
is answerable for the transferred duty, including inherited arrears/claims.
A retained old operator may carry out a paid fallback under a lawful agreement
but does not erase the successor's liability. If both fail, keep E/I/S/B and
actual sector useful-time provisional protection, independent order and paid
execution routes; do not send a person back and forth or create joint liability
for every unrelated provider. No missing contract/record/cash or interoffice
dispute extinguishes a due claim. Nor does an unfunded judgment create supplies.
Material failure requires existing responsible elected/sector action, not new
executive emergency powers, automatic tax or delayed replacement elections.

**OP20 — No automatic legal rollback or budget saving.** Technical rollback
preserves actual records and claims; it cannot reverse a performed treatment,
private disclosure, earned wage, lawful vote, judgment, transfer or elapsed
protection window. A return of responsibility requires the competent source-law
route, exact successor and paid continuity. Release old staff, premises or funds
only when obligations actually ended or lawfully moved, including tails; payroll
retention may create spare qualified time without cash saving. Preserve safe
correction history and scoped nonpublic evidence; no permanent public testimony
archive or compulsory central life log. Actual authority and paid readiness
remain unverified for this candidate.

claims and reserve release where the predecessor scheme or body closes.
