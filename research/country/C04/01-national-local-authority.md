# C04. National and local authority

*Part of [C04 public administration](../C04-public-administration.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

## National and local authority

The reference allocation is decentralized government within common constitutional
rights. National institutions handle the rights floor, macroeconomic framework,
interregional redistribution, major networks and cross-border obligations. Elected
local governments shape land use and deliver or commission community services.
Regional arrangements follow geography and scale; no arbitrary map is assumed.
The [C07 clinical network](../C07/05-health-service-and-patient-control.md)
is a functional service area, not an elected political region with general
law, tax or territorial-status power.
The [C14 founding-form comparison](../C14/05-scale-and-national-form.md)
tests this national/local reference against an elected regional tier and
issue-scoped joint services before any first election or transfer of power.

Local discretion cannot remove national rights. National government cannot require
local delivery while hiding its cost. Equalization uses published standardized
service need and own-source revenue capacity, not a locality's discretionary
underspending or tax effort; the formula, protected data and changes receive
appeal and audit. Cross-boundary effects need joint arrangements
with clear responsibility; creating a committee does not dissolve anyone's obligation.
The [public-health route](../C07/08-population-health-and-prevention.md)
assigns ordinary local outreach and national laboratory support under these
funding and accountability rules; a local team may not be left with an
unfunded mandate or a national data office with local coercive power.
After a disaster destroys a local tax base, the [C06 recovery-finance route](../C06/07-disaster-risk-finance.md)
provides a law-bound continuity and national-transfer decision while the
[C09 recovery route](../C09/07-disaster-recovery-and-return.md) preserves a
local elected plan. Aid cannot become a minister's power to select loyal councils.

Local councils choose land use and service organization where effects remain local.
Shared catchments, housing and commuting areas, ecological systems and major networks
need legally scoped joint plans prepared with affected local governments and people.
The Assembly sets common rights, resource duties and conflict rules. For a material
unresolved allocation among localities, a statutory interlocal forum prepares
alternatives with affected people and councils but cannot tax or bind nonmembers.
The elected Assembly is the default final authority for the residual common
choice, voting its precise allocation, conditions, payer and implementing duties
with public reasons and independent rights review. Ordinary permits and valid
local plans proceed within prior law; a minor cross-boundary effect does not
require a national vote. An executive, technical forum or resource owner gains
no project approval or veto through delay. A directly elected regional authority
remains an alternative only after a lawful founding decision defines a durable
area, its affected electorate, powers, financing and review. The selected
[territorial comparison](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-operating-settlement/territorial-authority.md)
does not presume a permanent regional map or local unanimity as the country's
allocation rule.

The [C03 decision-scope rule](../C03/05-assembly-capacity-and-scope.md)
requires the statutory materiality test to be fixed before a dispute and the
Assembly to count its residual interlocal docket beside its other duties.
Repeated harmful backlog triggers a reasoned elected review of staffing,
scope and a potentially founded regional alternative, not automatic cabinet
approval or a veto held by the first local permit.


[PC10/15/17 local political finance](../C03/12-continuous-political-finance.md)
places the common minimum's source nationally, with local original records and
actually funded receiving/payers; optional council enhancements need their own
lawful equal source. It neither invents a ballot track/local nomination power
nor permits an unfunded mandate, political Treasury veto or poll postponement.


[EL09–EL12](../C03/13-equal-franchise-and-usable-elections.md) now supplies a
whole-municipality proportional council, reference size, four-year terms,
replaceable chair and bounded local continuity. It preserves protected territorial
status/rights and requires actual map/source and prospective RR-compatible law.
[LR01–LR24](../C03/14-local-law-and-public-control.md) now supplies exact local
ordinance/delegated scrutiny. A finance account/AI map cannot grant a council,
change a constituency or postpone an actual election.

**Design145 public acquisition.** [LA02](../C09/13/01-purpose-authority-and-alternatives.md)
selects a prior-law full-local-membership majority for a local acquisition mandate;
a reserved national/material unresolved cross-local taking requires an actual
Assembly project Act with map/right, public owner, source ceiling and conditions.
Ordinary career officers implement, independent valuers/courts review and actual
payers/receivers deliver. Neither planner nor forum gains a taking power by delay.

[FC funded local early care](../C07/23-funded-local-early-care.md) implements
the national common floor with needs-based gap advances, local service
responsibility and a limited reviewed national fallback. Its sector rules
preserve ordinary LR scope and do not extend to other sectors by analogy.

[MF funded local duties](08-funded-local-mandates-and-equalization.md)
joins the common floor, general and sector transfers, first payment
dates and distinct local delivery. Existing sector fallback and lawful
local choices continue. No new regional power or tax is created.

[XR cross-local continuity](09-cross-local-service-continuity.md)
joins an accountable host receiver, continuing origin obligations and
a separately funded provisional path without deciding local status
or abandoning a resident during a payer dispute.

[JC domestic mutual aid](../C06/65/06-domestic-mutual-aid-and-concurrent-need.md)
gives qualified dispatch a bounded role over expressly callable emergency
capacity when regions compete. Preserve local command, the sending minimum
and the Assembly's authority for changes of law or scope.

[LT01–LT05](../C14/03/01-parallel-local-trials-and-shared-obligations.md)
applies the existing materiality and residual common-choice route
to concurrent local trials. Lawful independent options retain
their own scope, and continuing claims retain their owners.

[JS01–JS05](01/01-democratic-joint-service-authority.md) specifies how
councils may decide within a common funded service compact. Separate
powers, independent review, Assembly residual authority and rights
outside the compact retain their own law.

[CLX01–CLX06](01/02-cross-local-effects-and-residual-decisions.md) supplies
the later scoped intake, funded affected-area hearing and dated residual vote
for this route. Existing local authority and lawful compact scope continue.

[DMR01–DMR08](01/03-domestic-recognition-and-local-protection.md) gives qualified product evidence common
effect, preserves place-specific protection and uses CLX for material residual effects.

[MF31–MF36](08/08-local-grant-conditions-and-self-government.md)
separates legally due transfers and purpose-related grant conditions,
with practical refusal and effective review.

[Protected local competence](01/04-protected-local-competence.md) applies the A12 choice to compulsory methods, cumulative indirect control, independent relief and durable transfer.

[CLX07–CLX11](01/05-limited-cross-local-disposition.md) limit decisions to
the unresolved effect, protect equivalent local methods and require lawful
handback of any temporary direction.
