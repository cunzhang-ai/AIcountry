# Voting through involuntary domestic displacement

*Design321 · VP01–VP05 · Ordinary national and local elections · Research selection.*

**VP01 — Keep political standing portable while people move to safety.**
Apply EL01–EL04's existing qualification and choice of one actual local
connection. Temporary involuntary absence preserves the lawful prior
connection unless the elector chooses a qualifying new connection before
their first accepted ballot. Host registration for shelter, a household
member's choice or a relocation order supplies no such choice. The
national ballot remains nationwide. Offer a safe proof and contact route
for people outside public accommodation, without ordinary documents or
unable to return. Keep the existing candidate and protected succession
rules on their own dates.

**VP02 — Make a paid host receiver a lawful place to vote.** Before the
contest, primary law authorizes cross-area secret paper receipt at
designated ordinary and alternate electoral sites, including lawful mobile
teams. The electoral authority publishes neutral activation criteria and
rosters qualified career officers and independent substitutes. An assigned
officer activates a prepared receiver on recorded evidence that actual
access has become unsafe or unavailable, with prompt notice and direct
review. No new permission from the origin council, Cabinet, shelter or
campaign is required. Make the route usable for equivalent disruption
regardless of the type of evacuation or its official label. Give the
correct national or local ballot and full neutral materials, accessible
offline assistance, a private place and a reachable alternative to any
institution where coercion or surveillance is reported. Capacity includes
the host's own voters.

**VP03 — Protect the actual receipt and count.** A lawful host receiver's
recorded acceptance by P is timely electoral receipt. Later custody
transfer under EL17–EL18 carries that original time. Give protected
receipt evidence without revealing a choice or refuge location. When
eligibility, local connection or a possible duplicate is unresolved,
accept a timely sealed conditional ballot with separate outer proof.
Check EL17's existing duplicate and replacement rules against independent
sources before opening any inner ballot. Preserve valid national and
local components within their respective contests. A later finding can
establish entitlement at timely receipt. A post-close cast, missing
ballot or lost opportunity cannot be supplied by inference.

**VP04 — Finance rescue, receiving and election access in the same window.**
The election authority budgets and contracts the minimum useful host
and mobile routes before the contest, with separate electoral control
and personnel. The competent budget funds independent assessment,
printing, custody, safe transport, notice and review. Test these
alongside actual evacuation, housing and onward care under JC and RC.
Keep protected commitments, qualifications and release authorities
visible. Use an uncommitted common reserve only within RC31. A shelter
provider receives people and care, while an electoral officer receives
ballots. A failed route leaves the underlying rescue, shelter and
electoral duties with their existing owners. AI may flag a blocked road
or combined queue from appropriately scoped data. It cannot select which
neighbourhood votes, determine standing or certify that someone had
a genuine opportunity.

**VP05 — Keep fixed clocks and independent relief through failure.** Give
affected electors and contestants useful-time access to the existing
court, with funded staff, source evidence, protective orders and a named
executor. Correct a wrong activation, refusal or diversion before P
where feasible. Report people whose access remains unproved, distinct
from received, conditional and counted ballots. Preserve source and
privacy and apply ER16–ER17's applicable materiality and scope tests
if repair fails. There is no administrative extension, inferred vote,
automatic annulment or renewed term. Introduce this route prospectively
under actual lawful transition rules, preserving earlier contests and
pending remedies. Reopen on unequal access, unsafe confidentiality,
shared control that defeats independence, or a better evidenced rival.
