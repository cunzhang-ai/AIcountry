# Belonging: removal and liberty have separate legal grounds

*Canonical MB13–MB14; part of [C02/06](../06-citizenship-and-secure-belonging.md).*

**MB13 — No banishment of an established member.** A citizen or vested secure
resident cannot be expelled/exiled or denied return; criminal responsibility
uses ordinary law. Citizenship is not a reward for innocence and residence
is not an employer's revocable asset. Identity disputes are MB07/MB16 cases,
not summary denationalization or an exception to an existing secure entitlement.

Before stable residence vests, an individual removal may follow only an actually
expired/refused lawful permission after applicable renewal/alternative routes,
proved material applicant fraud affecting that permission, or a court's finding
of a concrete serious public-safety threat with necessity/proportionality and
no adequate less intrusive response. A conviction, poverty, disability, ideology,
status-reporting complaint or group identity alone is insufficient. Four-year
residence cannot be interrupted merely to prevent its vesting or change a contest;
prove actual independent grounds, not an AI intention score or blanket national ban.

Give usable reasons/evidence, necessary counsel and thirty days from usable notice
to challenge, with tolling for actual inaccessible notice/minority/coercion.
Execution is barred throughout that filing window and a usable thirty-day
ordinary appeal window, plus timely filed merits review/appeal, until effective
determination. Silence is no waiver; genuinely informed voluntary departure
is separate. No mandatory serial administrative exhaustion.
New material non-return evidence receives urgent suspensive assessment before
transfer, not a general endless automatic stay for unsupported repeated filings.

A court verifies an actually accepting lawful destination, safe transport,
non-return protection, child's interests, family/dependency and pending claims.
No collective expulsion, offshore relabeling or contractor/consular final veto.
This banishment rule does not immunize anyone from otherwise lawful P/G
extradition/international-court transfer with preserved return rights.
C08/P21–P24 extradition and criminal grounds remain distinct; MB cannot bypass
their proof, shorter clock or receiving protections. Inability to execute leaves
protected stay where MB10 applies, not a missing-person record or detention ground.

**MB14 — No warehousing for immigration administration.** Missing documents,
uncertain nationality, an unmet quota or an unexecuted removal alone authorizes
no detention. Ordinary criminal or imminent-harm rescue requires its own actual
ground, independent judge and P/crisis clocks; an immigration allegation is no
criminal charge. Nonclosed accommodation, support and proportionate court-reviewed
contact arrangements are the default; no compulsory inaccessible tracking or fees.

For an adult's final lawful removal only, prior independent judicial authorization
may permit strictly necessary physical execution restraint where actual imminent
transport, accepting receiver and failed less intrusive alternatives are proved.
The court rechecks execute-time receiving/medical facts after review opportunities
are exhausted or validly waived and new material non-return risks are resolved.
The order names officers, exact route, medical/access needs, start and release.
The maximum is six continuous hours from first restraint, with at most twelve
aggregate hours in any rolling thirty days. Failed transport means immediate
safe release; a fresh attempt requires a new judicial necessity finding and
materially changed executable circumstances, never an administrative reset.
Count actual coercive control through officers, contractors, carriers, vehicles,
airports and offshore handoffs until genuine liberty or actually accepted lawful
transfer ends national control. Paper dispatch or boarding does not end the clock.
A planned journey unable to fit the ceiling is unavailable unless genuinely
noncustodial/voluntary; plan safe abort/release points before departure. Expiry
never permits unsafe abandonment or an automatic extension midflight. An unforeseen
overrun remains a breach requiring least restrictive safe action, immediate
judicial correction and actual safe release, not retrospective authorization.
Independent counsel/review stays usable during execution. These are uncalibrated
outer ceilings, never permission to wait or a substitute for clinical necessity.

MB14 does not extend or replace P23/P24/G, CD or child-care powers. Any actually
continuing different ground needs its own order and original relevant clock;
no immigration relabeling revives an expired ground. B52 only transmits/receives.

No child is held in migration execution custody. Safe travel follows a child-
specific judicial/receiving plan with supported voice and no locked staging;
absence of a safe lawful noncustodial route prevents execution. Do not separate
family as punishment or use a care placement to disguise detention. MB creates
no military role, indefinite civil confinement or new offense for needing papers.
