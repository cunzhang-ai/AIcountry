## Alternatives and research selection

Compare retaining the vague two-layer promise, a concrete continuing pooled
scheme, a stronger universal basic with no new earnings layer, and personal
funded accounts. Select **Y01–Y16 continuing pooled accrual plus individual
basic protection** as the present research candidate. Reasons: short careers
accrue without a vesting cliff, care/interruption receives an explicit credit,
old-age income need not require investment expertise, and public adjustment
can remain democratically contestable. It is not proof of affordability or
superiority. A less wage-centered basic or other architecture can displace it
through an explicit comparison and rights-preserving transition.

The chosen 1.25% accrual illustrates 50% of constant covered pay after 40
years; 48 calendar bases bound the career formula. The two-median base cap
and uncapped 10% contribution intentionally redistribute. The half-median
credit recognizes interrupted paid work without treating a family member
as compulsory labor. The 50% survivor pot bounds this insurance promise,
while other personal needs remain separately protected. Age 65, 5% price
trigger, four-month reserve, 75-year projection/tail and ten-year age notice
are explicit normative reference choices, not calibrated optimums. Ten percent
also retains the old modeled worker levy rather than hiding a new employer
charge; its economic incidence and adequate future rate are unresolved.

## What changes and what continues

The basic layer now has a basket-linked amount, ordinary-residence/no-wait
rule and equal standard age. Y specifies early/partial quotes, credited
earnings, best-base revaluation, survivor scope/duration, monthly receipt,
records/control, restricted cash, public backing and prospective change.
An annuity quote values both start schedules from the same earlier age,
including survival to deferred payment; missing real survival/tail evidence
cannot produce a valid quote or delay the ordinary pension.

disregard, E/T/U protection and actual software/source law remain unchanged.
New-service rules have a proposed exact statutory start; prior service,
indexation, disability, care, survivor and pending claims need lawful

No hypothetical total may erase unclassified predecessor obligations.

Current C07/04 is replaced by a short entrance and four children; three

## Checks, evidence and material objections

Thirteen authored careers, survivor boundaries, toy annuity checks and four
alternative aggregate histories test meaning. Forty years at 30,000 produces
15,000 annual earned pension. For 15,000 identical recipients, payments 225
leave known receipts 228/interest 5 less payment equal to 8, instead of the
old 188, **before unknown costs**. Cash 25 can pay the first 18.75 installment
but needs another 68.75 to pay and retain a normal floor 75. These are
different history fixtures, not forecasts, real cohorts or old-claim changes.
No unallocated margin is booked as an asset, available loan or full surplus.

Retained objections: earnings hierarchy and compulsory contributions may
be inferior to a stronger basic; best-calendar-year caps can disadvantage
long interrupted careers; credit eligibility can become intrusive; price
ratchets and survivor guarantees create real costs; health inequality makes
uniform early/deferred factors contentious. A pooled scheme remains exposed
to fewer workers, weak production, fiscal capture and missed delivery.
These objections are not resolved by averaging or successful code checks.
Future participation, actual evidence and political choice may reject Y.
