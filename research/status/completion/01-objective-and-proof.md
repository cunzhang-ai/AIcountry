These requirements preserve the whole intended country, not only what was
easy to write or verify. Ethical conflicts cannot be settled by averaging.
An aggregate improvement cannot compensate for destroyed opposition,
arbitrary detention or missing safe necessities. Treat uncertain evidence
as uncertain; do not infer absence of a design choice solely from absent
legal adoption or real-world operation.

Actual “better than all present states” would need multidimensional,
context-sensitive evidence and legitimate value choices, not a universal
ranking. A proposal may improve a known mechanism without proving global
dominance. Keep that limitation visible while completing the design itself.
