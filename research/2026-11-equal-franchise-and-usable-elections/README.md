classify controllers, infer a voter preference or decide real eligibility.

and labelled. The selected rule itself retains a splitting/rounding counterexample;
no test is allowed to hide it or convert it into a superiority claim.

New national choice: one proportional-list track, optional person, no territorial
independent-seat exception or artificial diaspora territory. Independents can
run one-person civic lists. Entry uses two active supporters and
min(ceil(E_call/100),ceil(E_call/(4N))). Old actual called laws and claims continue;
this is a prospective coordinated research change, not an enactment.

all ballot lists/names remain in the directory. No unproved allegation freezes
due access, groups' separate votes are not combined and courts cannot choose
political content. An actual unsupported affiliate claim has its existing facts/
remedies, not an implied new election crime or automatic ballot ban.
