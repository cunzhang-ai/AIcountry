# Copenhagen. Balances, payroll and usable opening hours

*Design198 · Primary evidence for the [country comparison](README.md).*

## Follow the 2025 balance through its owners

All amounts in this section are million DKK. The city reported 34,049 of
net service expenditure and a favourable variance of 249 against the
revised service budget, with rounded figures. Social services and
employment/integration each overspent against their revised service
budgets. The city's year-end liquidity was 16,817, most of it already
assigned to capital projects, future payments and other uses. These
figures have different account boundaries. [City council accounts](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Borgerrepr%C3%A6sentationen/m%C3%B8de-30042026/referat/punkt-5)

The children and youth committee's March record gives finer detail.

| 2025 scope | Favourable variance | What it includes |
|---|---:|---|
| Ordinary operations, all committee services | −72.8 | Operating overspend under that year's revised budgets |
| Unused lines, all committee services | 184.3 | 136.1 decentralized balances, 11.1 external or contract-linked, 37.1 investment or innovation |
| Net committee service variance | 111.5 | 184.3 − 72.8, a budget variance |
| Unused lines requested for carry-forward | 163.1 | 184.3 − 21.2 less requested by dental services. This is a request across the gross unused lines |
| Ordinary early care only | 15.0 | 55.4 unused lines and 40.4 operating overspend |

The committee attributed the rise in local balances partly to late-year
extra allocations, delayed expansion of dental capacity and difficulty
deploying additional school payroll allocations. The ordinary early-care
overspend was explained mainly by 2025 budget adjustments. Its amount
cannot be projected as a durable funding gap. Conversely the positive
net variance does not establish a durable release. The committee's
request and net variance have different denominators. The 21.2 reduction
in the dental carry-forward request does not itself establish a
recurring saving or a free cash balance. [Committee accounts and explanation](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/B%C3%B8rne-%20og%20Ungdomsudvalget/m%C3%B8de-11032026/referat/punkt-2)

The finance committee's controls record reports an increase in supplier
invoices missing their payment deadlines in 2025, with the problem greatest in the summer holiday
period, despite a council decision for immediate payment. This is a
citywide control finding. It neither quantifies care invoices nor
reconciles them to the 111.5 variance. It changes the comparison by
making staffed holiday payment cover and actual supplier receipt
separate tests. [Finance committee controls record](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/%C3%98konomiudvalget/m%C3%B8de-24022026/referat/punkt-3)

The city's 2026 carryover agreement distinguishes carry-forward and new
budget choices. Its BU30 earmarks 81.0 for more pedagogical staff in
2026 from national finance-act money. It schedules reporting every two
months, starting with the June forecast, and allows the committee to
reprioritize if take-up of the appropriation is insufficient. That is
the same staffing allocation already traced in [FC](../funded-local-care/README.md).
Count it once, separately from the 2025 variance. These agreed
monitoring terms do not report actual paid staffing or received care.
[Carryover agreement, financing and BU30](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/agenda/845428be-e405-4ed2-acac-4e02d5c739f3/7ac52881-8e3f-4f4e-a434-642eebfa13c5-bilag-2.pdf)

## The October comparison now includes administrative follow-up

The October forecast anticipated 90.0 of favourable variance for 2026
committee services excluding costs for displaced Ukrainians. This is
a forecast rather than an audited outturn. It consists of 52.7 decentralized
balances, 21.4 unused external and investment lines and 15.9 from ordinary
operations. Ordinary early care was forecast at 59.5 below budget, primarily
associated with fewer children than expected. Ordinary special education
was forecast at 40.8 above budget. Officials proposed returning 50.0 of
2026 service-budget headroom in a later budget decision. There is no
inferred recurring release or approval of that later transfer here.
[21 October budget forecast and decision](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/B%C3%B8rne-%20og%20Ungdomsudvalget/m%C3%B8de-21102026/referat/punkt-7)

The same meeting's staffing follow-up reports municipal clusters' plans
for 47.6 of the 81.0 staffing allocation. They planned 70% for hiring,
27% for retention and 3% for reducing debt, at the published precision.
The administrative rolling FTE and child-count series both declined
against the index reference, with the child count declining more.
This allows a higher ratio alongside fewer staff. Its aggregate cannot
show qualified presence at a particular opening hour. The note infers
the expected use of self-governing providers' funds from balances rather
than payroll access. This follow-up does not cover private providers'
use of the funds. The committee recorded that it would see another
follow-up in December. A planned use and a signed workforce payment
still have distinct evidence scopes. [Staffing follow-up, sections 2–5](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/agenda/fc9a3428-765a-4185-84ec-3058a64639b8/1f929ce5-cbfd-4637-8eee-18ebe4d68ee5-bilag-5_0.pdf)

A separate decision concerns 2.7 reserved to reverse an earlier reduction
in extended and weekend opening. Officials reported low use or inquiries
and proposed distributing that amount across ordinary early care instead.
The same report identifies competing explanations. Some extended-hours
places have ordinary allocation rules, and the combined flexible route
asks parents to find a private carer and requires an average minimum of
ten flexible hours per week. The report expressly raises that threshold
as a possible reason for fewer than five annual inquiries about the
combined route. It documents consultation with selected institutions
and a parent organization. That consultation matters but cannot by itself
measure all unmet need. On 21 October the committee asked for more
evidence about demand and deferred a new recommendation. This is an
observed retain/repair decision point, with neither a reinstatement nor
a transfer established by these minutes.
[Extended-hours record and decision](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/B%C3%B8rne-%20og%20Ungdomsudvalget/m%C3%B8de-21102026/referat/punkt-5)

The 2.7 earmark is separate from the 81.0 staffing allocation, the
50.0 proposed service-budget release and the 2025 balances. No amount
from those four scopes is added as a source for the same offer.
