# PA09–PA12: one protected foundation and one personal tax schedule

*Part of [C06/38](../38-work-pay-prices-and-country-income.md); design 93 rates retained; design 100 [TA/RG/TX](../40-tax-law-and-accountable-revenue.md) completes personal liability and administration.*

**PA09 — Protect the ordinary foundation and specify its tax counterpart.**
Retain IC's age-appropriate personal ordinary payment, independent of earnings,
assets, partner, employment, politics or AI use. Protect its matching cash amount
from ordinary personal tax. Design 131 [SD06/PW](../53/01-foundation-tax-and-work.md) now prefers C2:
one untapered 0.4M allowance on other Q at all receipt levels. C1's withdrawn
allowance and C0's first-unit treatment remain rivals, with historical cases scoped.
Residual I05 and true cost compensation remain exempt. This is an explicit
distributional choice, not an administrative saving or newly created resource.

Taxable gross U with an exclusive equal source-period offset is mathematically
equivalent. Prefer the protected exclusion in this working design; unused U
exclusion cannot offset unrelated wages, generate a refundable tax credit or
multiply across payers. Attach it once to the lawful ordinary payment, including
arrears; unpaid U is not income/resources. Actual matching in-kind credit reduces
the cash component under IC, not a second tax allowance. Meal/cash alternatives
therefore do not silently change the tax on otherwise identical private income.

**PA10 — Specify a contestable progressive benchmark.** Other ordinary annual
taxable income has 20% on the first 0.5M,35% on the next 0.5M,45% on the next M,
and 55% above 2M. Initial M is the independently measured Y05 full-time-equivalent
median annual earnings W, not the model's wage residual or a number chosen to
close a deficit. The rates/widths are a normative working hypothesis, not proof
of optimality, constitutional entrenchment or actual national affordability.
[TN05–TN06](../59/02-source-choice-and-real-alternatives.md) withdraws its
default replacement preference in design155. Keep the specified distributional
benchmark; retain actual lawful retrofit sources until express prospective
replacement through a joined source/service/legacy/paid-transition choice.

For the proposed annual 1 January update, tax scale M is max(previous nominal M,
previous M×verified general CPI, verified W). It can differ from current W when
the protection binds; never relabel it the observed median. Law fixes vintages,
source/correction and provisional fallback. TA01 omits only the missing new component, keeps the prior lawful M and uses any
other verified higher component; never repeat an old CPI ratio as new inflation
or freeze the entire scale. Favorable correction refunds; late adverse definition
changes do not raise past tax.
Material definition/rate changes require public elected law and migration.

**PA11 — Tax actual defined personal receipts, not labels or turnover.** Include
worked cash earnings after lawful employee Y contributions, net personal business
income after genuine expenses, TW replacement cash, earned/survivor pension,
distributed capital income, interest and defined realized gains. The ordinary
foundation attracts no10% worked-wage Y levy; neither does TW public replacement.
Retained company profit is not received shareholder cash. Corporate, transfer,
land and product taxes keep their own rules and incidence, without invented yield.
Child support/true cost refunds remain outside this ordinary tax base; their
I05 resource/cost matching is separate. Preserve treaty, foreign/source law,
prior tax periods and protected net promises until express lawful revision.

**PA12 — One individual and one attribution rule.** Adults and children each
have their own tax/payment unit across actual payers; no marriage pooling,
household head or allowance per account. Compute total progressive tax once.
For I05 classify that tax proportionately over received taxable nonfoundation
work/replacement, pension and other ordinary receipts. Retain the bounded
disregard on net pension. Publish work-first and other material attribution
rivals: equal total tax can produce different residual awards, so ordering is
not neutral bookkeeping. Tax collection does not confer final benefit authority
or create one universal family/relationship dossier.

**Current scope:** [SD05–SD08](../43/02-income-tax-and-payment-packages.md)
uses the same one-time fixed 0.4M allowance for A/I05 and preferred conditional
full U/C2, with U excluded in C2. The rates, attribution and actual source-year
rights remain. C1's 63% personal marginal region remains a historical/rival
case, not C2's schedule. Neither C0 nor C1 yield transfers to C2. C2 can still
retain only 20.25% of extra gross work with Y/active I05 at the highest band;
I10's cumulative review remains. No new tax is enacted by research.

[FP19](../56/06-owned-principal-and-country-disposition.md) clarifies voluntary
funded-plan withdrawals: verified already-taxed own capital and previously
recognized income are not fresh PA11 income; actual untaxed income retains its
source character. Recognize basis once, preserve prior relief/source law and
protected correction. No new plan deduction, deferral, levy or blanket exemption.
Public Y solidarity units are not private principal. I05 usable assets remain
separate; detailed private-plan tax treatment remains unqualified.

[Design142 PF](../57-private-retirement-tax-and-personal-capital.md) chooses
ordinary post-source private contributions with no new deduction/credit. Work
funding uses actual ordinary gross pay/Y/PA and a net-cash choice, not a deemed
receipt of an illiquid promise. Owned-unit disposal retains TA06 indexed basis;
genuine nonmarketable annuity/DB payments recover nominal cost first, then PA
income. This is an explicit prospective timing choice, not a renewed old-law
interpretation or source/optimality finding. Public Y basis is unchanged.
