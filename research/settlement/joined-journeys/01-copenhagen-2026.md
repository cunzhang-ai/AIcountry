# Copenhagen 2026. Road investment, transport rights and learning time

## Follow three distinct accounts

The 17 September City Council decision authorized a DKK 21.7 million
capital appropriation for 12 bus measures and a separate DKK 1.275 million
regional revenue appropriation. Table 2 presents the regional amount and
the net DKK 20.425 million change in the city pool. The future gross
capital profile is DKK 7.5 million in 2027, 9.2 million in 2028 and
5.0 million in 2029 at the stated 2027 price and wage basis. The proposed
stops and safer crossing at Arenaskole account for DKK 8.5 million of
the gross programme, including DKK 1.5, 2.0 and 5.0 million in those
three years. The regional share concerns another measure, number 7.
The budget includes works and preparatory studies. No verified operating
capacity, service deadline or current project completion follows.
[Council decision and Table 2](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Borgerrepr%C3%A6sentationen/m%C3%B8de-17092026/referat/punkt-10)

A separate school-service record approved changing the swimming-lesson
bus threshold from 2.5 km to 1.8 km for the 2026/27 school year. The
previous change from 1.5 km to 2.5 km had left schools arranging trips
below the threshold, sometimes consuming enough time to reduce teaching.
The administration expected a rise from 21 to 31 schools receiving bus
transport and about DKK 0.5 million less in planned savings, covered
within the teaching budget. Both counts are estimates and may vary.
This is an adopted transport rule intended to protect teaching time.
It does not confirm minutes in the pool or the coverage of disabled
pupils. No causal link to the Arenaskole capital project is claimed.
[Children and Youth Committee decision, item 2](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/B%C3%B8rne-%20og%20Ungdomsudvalget/m%C3%B8de-26112025/referat/punkt-2)

The [same-year early-care financing](../funded-local-care/README.md)
reported DKK 200.7 million for fees, staffing and other care measures.
It is a distinct restricted allocation. It cannot be used as school
bus cash, public network reserve or proof of an accepted childcare
arrival. These public budgets share a locality and year, without
evidence of common purchasable crews, usable cash or free margin.

## Where the responsibility stops

DOT describes one ticket across buses, trains, metro and light rail
within its valid time and zones. The Metro's own travel guarantee
describes conditional reimbursement up to DKK 300 for qualifying
alternative transport after delays of more than 30 minutes. It describes
initial payment by the rider, scope by affected line, a specific
elevator-outage route and exclusions including sufficiently notified
planned changes. It is a real remedy under its stated terms, with a
different scope from a guaranteed public-service arrival.
[DOT ticket and route scope](https://dinoffentligetransport.dk/en/plan-your-journey)
· [Metro guarantee](https://m.dk/en/travel-with-the-metro/contact-and-customer-service/travel-guarantee/)

EU Regulation 2021/782, for railway journeys in its applicable scope,
distinguishes through-tickets and protected missed connections.
Article 18 provides choices including continuation or rerouting for a
qualifying expected final delay, with accessible alternatives and a
conditional reimbursed self-routing provision. Coverage and exclusions
remain in the regulation. It does not imply that a local multimodal ticket
or a visit to a public service gets those exact rail-law terms.
[Official regulation, Articles 2, 3, 12 and 18](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A32021R0782)

## Decision in the same observed setting

Retain the existing funded network, improvement programme and school
transport adjustment as the strong comparator. They show that ordinary
network improvement and repair can solve particular needs. The records
do not support taking another programme's capital or grant for a
transport guarantee. Use the joined journey duty only for covered trips
under existing or expressly adopted authority and paid sources.
Measure usable school time, care arrival and safe return as separate
from ridership, published eligibility or a refund. No observed minutes,
completed construction, funded contingency quote or causal gain is
inferred for the proposed country.
