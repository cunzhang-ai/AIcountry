# Public decisions must answer for particular private interests

Design179 · Proposed PI01–PI20 and narrow A2 addition; no actual enactment

The country already has PC/NI political finance, procurement/C04 integrity,
K09 corruption, competition law, records and courts. Those are not a complete
common regime for ordinary non-electoral influence or legislators' particular
financial interests. A criminal corrupt bargain need not exist for lawful hidden
access or a particular self-benefit to undermine practical democracy.

[Canonical PI](../../country/C03/17-public-decision-influence-and-interests.md)
selects public-side material records, actual known principals/drafts, protected
private casework, timely interest disclosure, no private paid office service,
unconflicted administration, paid countervoice and ordinary correction. Actual
national/local records owners retain distinct custody. AA's subject expands only
expressly; no new sovereign guardian, contact licence or political-truth offence.

[Proposed A2](../../authority/provisions/01-public-law-A01-A03.md) constrains
particular office self-benefit even through primary legislation. PI supplies a
narrow ordinary public-interest claim on credible particular-benefit facts,
without requiring personal denial, a competing bid or one-fifth opposition.
Necessary constitutional references retain CJ competence. Neither an allegation
nor an audit cancels a vote/seat, changes N/n or blocks government replacement.

| Alternative | Mechanism / strongest unresolved cost |
|---|---|
| Retain/repair existing rules | Less additional work and exposure; existing powers can still miss lawful hidden influence and an express self-benefit Act |
| Selected PI plus A2 | Public owners answer for relevant outside influence and particular privilege; courts/records can be captured and work/source/privacy costs remain |
| Ordinary PI without A2 | Can improve records/conflict administration; cannot bind an express higher primary Act and is a materially different public choice |
| Broader issue-spending caps | May reduce purchased reach; defining political issues and enforcing caps risks speech, selective classification and evasive intermediaries |
| Ownership/redistribution/common provision | May reduce concentrated private dependence; title, investment, informal/public domination and paid transition remain substantive whole-country choices |

Publicity is not equal influence; greater formalization is not itself progress.
The selected package complements paid participation, plural media/production and
practical opposition; it does not prove these sufficient or displace structural
rivals. Hypotheses include14day ordinary records,12month matter-specific exit
and the prospective schedule, not empirical optima. Source costs remain unknown.
