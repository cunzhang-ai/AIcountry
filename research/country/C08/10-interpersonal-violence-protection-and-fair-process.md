# C08. Interpersonal violence, protection and fair process

*Part of [C08 everyday law and justice](../C08-everyday-law-and-justice.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

The country must protect people from intimate-partner, family and sexual
violence, stalking, threats and coercive control without treating a home as a
place beyond law. The risk and access burden is unequal across gender, age,
disability, migration status and economic dependence; services remain open to
all affected people. A report can lead to safety help before a crime is proved.
Ordinary disagreement, poverty, disability or an accusation alone is not an
offense. The Assembly specifies harmful conduct, culpability, evidence and
sanctions prospectively; neither an AI risk score nor a service intake creates
criminal guilt or a presumption that a parent is unfit.

An affected person may seek confidential advice and immediate clinical,
housing and payment help directly through [C07's receiving service](../C07/12-interpersonal-safety-and-survivor-support.md),
without first reporting to police, leaving a relationship or pursuing a
charge. Any mandatory disclosure for a child, an imminent concrete threat or
another narrow legal duty must have a specified ground, safe explanation,
minimum recipient and review. An adult's account is not automatically shared
with police, a partner, an employer or an immigration caseworker. A trusted
advocate may help the person communicate but cannot consent on their behalf.

Civil protection and criminal punishment have different grounds. On specific,
recorded evidence of immediate serious danger, a competent officer may use a
short emergency safety power defined by prior law; an independent court must
review it promptly. The court can issue a proportionate, time-limited
protective order, including contact, distance or temporary home-occupation
terms where lawful and necessary. It tests concrete risk, available less
restrictive measures, the protected person's actual safety, affected children,
housing and third-party rights. An order names the actor, duration, service,
enforcement and challenge route. The respondent receives reasons and a prompt
meaningful hearing, with safe handling of addresses and witnesses; renewal
requires fresh evidence and review. An emergency label cannot reset the clock
or turn an interim order into indefinite punishment. Lack of safe alternative
housing for either party is a public capacity failure, not a reason to leave a
person exposed or to impose an unreviewable exclusion.

Police must respond to a specific threat, breach or suspected offense under
bounded powers, preserve contrary as well as supporting evidence and protect
the person while maintaining the accused's counsel, disclosure, liberty and
fair-trial rights. Independent prosecutors give reasons for charge, noncharge
or diversion subject to applicable review; a failed prosecution does not erase
an independently supported protective claim. A protective order does not by
itself decide guilt, custody, divorce, property or residence. Mediation,
restorative work or a perpetrator program cannot be a required gate to safety
or a substitute for investigating serious violence; any participation needs
free, informed and revocable consent with independent advice.

Children exposed to harm have their own voice, safe care and court route under
[C08's child-protection rule](01-civil-law-and-personal-status.md). Contact or
shared parenting is not presumed safe merely because an adult order expires;
nor does an allegation automatically terminate parental rights. Competent
family and child courts assess specific risk, evidence, support and safe
contact without making a child repeatedly recount events to unrelated desks.
An immigration or work-permit dependency cannot make a person choose between
immediate safety and expulsion; [C10](../C10/02-external-relations-and-migration.md)
must offer a confidential status and protection route within valid law.

Health treatment, forensic evidence collection and a police statement are
separate choices. Time-sensitive care and evidence-preservation options need
clear consent, lawful custody, privacy and access to later correction. Public
agencies record orders and breaches only for defined purposes; a common AI
danger list cannot silently direct arrest, housing denial or child removal.
Independent inspection tests whether police, courts, health and shelters
actually execute orders, and whether protectors commit coercion or leak
locations. A person may challenge nonresponse, unsafe disclosure and wrongful
restriction; neither complaint nor an acquittal automatically proves a
reporter lied.

Existing orders, criminal cases, family arrangements, confidentiality duties
and accrued claims remain under valid law until prospectively reconciled.
Before commencement, fund an after-hours receiver, safe housing, clinical
care, advocacy, counsel, interpreters, police response, judicial review and
independent investigation. Report legal adoption, delivered safety,
wrongful restrictions and repeat harm separately. Reopen if protective powers
become a weapon for controlling a partner, if nonreporters cannot get help,
or if execution fails during nights and cross-boundary moves.
