# HL01–HL04. Prevention and the first safe night

[Housing loss and secure receiving](../25-housing-loss-and-secure-receiving.md)

**HL01 — Start at the first credible report.** The existing local public
housing steward provides a named duty receiver, reachable in person,
by telephone, through outreach and through a qualified substitute.
Protect the first attempt and the original useful deadline under E02.
Start prevention on credible foreseeable loss within 60 calendar days,
and earlier where ordinary housing or other duties require it. A
written eviction notice is unnecessary. Treat an unsafe, inaccessible
or coercive place as unavailable on the facts. Address, digital ID,
household controller, local connection and formal immigration proof
cannot gate urgent help. An actual person present in the jurisdiction
without safe accommodation can claim the urgent duty. Ordinary
prevention and durable eligibility retain their I01/H and applicable
sector grounds. A safe interim route continues while scope is disputed.
The receiving act changes no residence, admission or franchise.

**HL02 — Preserve a safe home when it works for the person.** With
their agreement, test an enforceable repair, corrected arrears or
benefit payment, funded short bridge or voluntary lawful agreement.
Give a reasoned prevention decision and an accountable action by the
last time it can work, at most five calendar days after receipt for
the initial decision. Apply any earlier E/H/RT and judicial clocks.
Verify whether the payment, repair or signed agreement actually secures
safe occupation for the stated period. A caseworker cannot promise a
tenant the owner's consent or make an owner house someone unpaid.
Possession, dues, title and urgent protection keep their separate
law and remedy. In an unsafe relationship, offer a confidential
exit without making contact, mediation or joint application a condition.

**HL03 — Deliver actual emergency accommodation.** When a person
has no safe accommodation, the duty steward assesses immediate
risk and issues a reasoned urgent disposition within four elapsed
hours of first receipt, earlier at the actual safety deadline.
Arrange a safe accepting place and usable transport by the first
otherwise unsafe night or earlier time of exposure. If already
unsafe, arrange receiving at the earliest safe time and record
each unmet interval. A decision or booking alone does not perform
the duty. The place must fit actual accessibility, power and care
needs, provide secure private space and hygiene, protect safe
relationships and preserve independent communication. Supply paid
support through its lawful receiver when needed. A person can decline
a specific place or request another; reassess actual risk and reasons.
A refusal of a place unsafe in their circumstances never forfeits
help. Review a declined suitable option on actual facts, without
punishment, forced cohabitation, treatment or detention.

**HL04 — Use a funded fallback and keep the shortfall visible.**
The steward arranges a qualified reserved or alternate place under
HL09. Test real receiving before moving someone. Protect urgent
health, safety, food, medication and safe contact while resolving
a shortage. A referral to an unsafe home, waiting room, police cell
or another district without acceptance does not discharge the duty.
Preserve original first receipt, record the missed safe time and
notify the independent remedy route in useful time. A capacity
shortage leaves a failed duty. It supplies no special power to
remove someone from public space or keep them in custody. Ordinary
lawful safety and use-of-space decisions remain separately reviewable.

[HL18–HL22](05-household-arrears-and-essential-continuity.md) joins
the due housing, income and utility dates and supplies a narrow
prospective pause. An existing court order retains judicial authority.
