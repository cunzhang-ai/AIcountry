# CF05–CF08: actual sources, private money and paid persuasion

*Part of [C03/10](../10-democratic-entry-and-campaign-resources.md); design101 research, not enacted law.*

**CF05 — Timing and real source.** Define this campaign-finance period as lawful poll call through close of polling `P`; a payment made before call for paid express electoral distribution **during** the period counts when distributed. [PC01–PC24](../12-continuous-political-finance.md) now proposes separate continuous tests for actually delivered pre-call electoral appeals; called-period timing still controls this CF test. Old acts retain their actual source law. A timely V−14 assignment rejected for the cap/source has CF04’s exact V−10/late-notice24h correction route with absolute V−3 outer; ordinary fresh assignments still closeV−14. Finance law/formula, accessible assignment mechanism, provider contracts and appropriation must precede nomination opening. The selected entry design uses an ordinary poll call by `V-90` (early poll at least `V-60`), common nomination/active-endorsement freeze `V-40`, correction/cure by `V-30` and print freeze `V-21`. Because a voter can revoke/reassign two active endorsements until `V-40`, pre-freeze help and preliminary eligibility checks are due within five days of first filing, but **final** qualification is determined within five calendar days after that common freeze; equal baseline activation then follows within E16's 48 elapsed hours, ordinarily by `V-33`. The coordinated Act must expressly revise E16's earlier first-receipt reading for this new national contest, prospectively and without a pre-freeze cash or media privilege for early filers. Earlier urgent decision/access remains due if the ordinary schedule would destroy effective participation; official silence never qualifies. Voter credit assignment starts on qualification and remains open until `V-14`; each validated assignment is paid to its restricted campaign account within 48 elapsed hours, earlier when otherwise useless. A final-moment assignment leaves at most twelve days to use cash; the `V-33` paid baseline gives earlier reach. Official delay cannot forfeit a timely first filing or due grant; E13–16 courts can order useful-time access and actual source execution, not deem a candidate qualified or postpone a poll by this finance rule. No winner-payback or result-conditioned debt. Unspent public cash returns after an independently reviewable closing account; lawful vendor wages and due claims survive.

**CF07 — No race to private patronage.** The source cap applies to receipts/benefits delivered during CF05's period and to earlier post-enactment contributions earmarked for this contest or actually used for its paid campaign, regardless of invoice/deposit date; general pooled member dues used in a campaign need pro-rata source attribution, while prior-law assets need the stated transition. One natural person actually politically eligible at receipt under C02/PC05 (not merely EL called-P anticipation) may give no more than `B*/2` to one effective principal and `B*` across all principals and earmarked independent express-electoral spending in the same Assembly contest, including candidate personal funds, earmarked gifts, controlled intermediaries, below-market discounts, beneficially controlled donors and coordinated in-kind support at evidenced ordinary replacement value. A public body, corporation, foreign government or other **external** legal entity may not donate to a contestant. A party/civic list may spend its own lawfully sourced member dues and accumulated assets under the same total spending cap, tracing underlying donor/controller and old-law title; internal party-to-its-list/candidate transfers are one principal's expenditure, not a second donation or new public credit. Grandfathering an accrued lawful old asset is a prospective transition decision, not automatic confiscation or a license for newly prohibited entity money. A third-party association's paid **express support/defeat** of a named contestant must use a separate opt-in account funded pro rata by permissible natural members; that account is one effective principal for each human's `B*/2` per-principal cap and shares the same `B*` individual overall cap; ordinary dues, corporate funds and public/restricted grants cannot be diverted there. Its lawful ordinary issue advocacy remains distinct, and political viewpoint similarity is not proof of electoral coordination. No anonymous campaign cash; a safe confidential audit record is required, while public identification starts only above `B*/20` aggregate per donor/principal and a competent court can shield an endangered supporter without hiding the amount/controller from independent audit. No fee or deposit for ballot or cash assignment. An ordinary unpaid volunteer's own speech/work is not valued as a forced donation. Private loans, guarantees, debt forgiveness and vendor deferral beyond 30 days are forbidden campaign funding; normal priced invoices with ≤30-day payment terms are recorded and any preferential term is an in-kind donation. This is restrictive of party finance autonomy and needs public contest, but the public credits and access floor reduce the liquidity objection.

**CF08 — Expenditure and third parties.** Every new national EL list-control principal, including a one-person civic list, has a national paid campaign expenditure ceiling of `B*(6+E_call/200)`; there is no national territorial-candidate subaccount/ceiling in new EL scope. Other actual local/ref bodies use PC/EL's own source. These ceilings freeze at call; later roll growth cannot retroactively make an earlier paid act unlawful or cause an incumbent-friendly mid-contest increase. An actual national expenditure faces the one national contest and additional PC rolling tests; an expenditure is valued once, with a disclosed objective allocation of mixed-purpose cost, and cannot be relabeled as a vendor/volunteer to escape the cap. Public in-kind access is disclosed at real public cost but is outside contestant cash/spend caps because every qualified entrant receives the same legally bounded service; private purchased substitutes count. A materially **independent** third-party controller may spend up to `max(B*,E_call B*/1000)` in the new national EL contest on paid express electoral persuasion during the defined campaign period; common-control entities aggregate. Genuine journalism, satire, ordinary policy debate and an individual's unpaid advocacy are not counted. Proved requested/jointly planned/materially directed spending is attributed to the contestant and its donor/spending caps, not also retained as independent. Paid distribution identifies actual controller, price, audience selection and time; material third-party sponsor/source accounts are timely public above `B*/20`, with protected small-member and endangered-person details. The Act must specify correction, return of unlawful source, actual-loss restitution and specific useful-time paid-distribution restraint; any punitive fine must be **court-only** under a separately enacted exact VD12 class B knowing/reckless material source/limit-evasion rule and its selected Q02/individual and Q08/body or express VD12-only body ceiling, not an implied finance-office or generic V power. ER19 expressly SELECTS the VD12-only alternative upper ceiling of twice proved unlawful campaign value alongside existing revenue/gain bases; it does not apply to unrelated V cases. The unchanged-Q08 rival remains dissent, not an undecided current maximum. Clerical error first gets correction; no money-report allegation by itself disqualifies, criminalizes, or cancels a ballot. Actual ballot/result relief remains prior law's independent causal test.

**CF07 source/punitive clarification.** Pre-call holdings earmarked for this contest are source-attributed/segregated under civil law; B*/the call-fixed numerical cap cannot retroactively make their earlier receipt a VD12 crime. At usable call/cap notice give five calendar days to disclose, segregate and return/restructure an unused prohibited/excess holding with practical source help. VD12 reaches only proved knowing/reckless post-call acceptance, retention after that correction opportunity, routing or use within actual control/ability under now-published limits. An actually delivered pre-call message or binding earlier act retains its then-effective law; prepaid benefits actually delivered in-period count civilly under CF05, with real ability/justification defenses for punishment. A provider’s old lawful wage/contract is not erased. PC expressly adds prospective continuous source/persuasion tests; this does not punish an earlier lawful act.

**CF08 source clarification for independent purchasers.** All actual paid express appeals for/against an identified Assembly contestant in this period—whether bought directly by an individual/company/foreign controller or through an association—must use the same CF07 eligible-human, consent, source/controller and donation limits. An individual’s own paid purchase is their contribution to that independent campaign principal and their across-principal total. A legal entity may not evade the rule by paying the media invoice directly: an independent electoral campaign uses an opt-in, separately accounted eligible-human fund with attributed member amounts; general corporate assets/dues/public grants and foreign-state funding are not alternative sources. Actual independent controller expenditure still obeys its CF08 limit. Ordinary journalism, satire, unpaid personal speech and genuine issue advocacy remain outside this narrow express-electoral category; paid sponsorship cannot be disguised as editorial independence, but political disagreement or policy criticism alone proves neither paid appeal nor coordination. PC01/05/16 now supplies the separate continuous source/paid-issue disclosure boundary, retaining ordinary issue advocacy outside electoral caps.
