# SC21–SC24: buy a functioning input path, retain public competence

3 November 2026 · Design 137 · [SC public buying](../05-public-buying-and-continuous-supply.md), not enacted law

**SC21 — The buyer supplies the missing specification.** For a consequential
critical input, connect the protected need/useful time to item or service,
quality/configuration, quantities and uncertainty, accepting operator, upstream
inputs, actual source/currency/date, qualified labor, lifecycle and failure
receiving. The responsible sector and its qualified buying staff prepare this;
patients, ordinary claimants and problem reporters need no technical dossier.
Aggregate uncertain quantities honestly; do not collect private testimony merely
to fill a purchase table. No national catalog or permission for every livelihood.

The 66 reference purchases are now grouped for inquiry with original identity,
owner and quotation preserved. A group is not a supplier recipe or a pool of
fungible workers. A clinical reservation, laboratory consumable, software
license and fuel invoice have different delivery/rights/resource dependencies.
Do not duplicate them as both a purchased input and a whole provider's payroll
or infer a physical quantity from a budget fraction.

**SC22 — Trace common dependencies before promising redundancy.** Identify
material manufacturer/site, ingredients/components, carrier/port, utilities,
specialist maintenance, rights/know-how and shared control where relevant.
Two commercial names or different AI/account identities can depend on the same
failed node. Check actual capacity and competing commitments, not only distinct
ownership; even independent factories may lack enough useful-time backup.
Protect lawful trade secrets and source safety through scoped independent
inspection; no public patient graph or arbitrary blacklist follows.

**SC23 — Pool demand without making one gateway sovereign.** Select retained
public critical-input competence with contestable plural supply, justified
nonexclusive pooled specifications/contracts where actually usable, and paid
local/sector continuity. Existing C04 career staff support sector/local buyers;
SC authority, courts, AA and separate technical/clinical verification continue.
No new purchasing ministry, compulsory marketplace or AI award authority.
Buyers remain accountable for actual receiving and lawfully due payments.

Compare suitable lots, capacity/stock reservation and alternate delivery routes
with single-winner/just-in-time contracts; publish material quality, price and
resilience reasons before award. Match any exclusivity/minimum volume and
redundant standby to actual costs. Do not force several suppliers where none
qualifies or accept sham bids to reach a count. Existing challenge, source,
variation, payment and limited-continuity rules are not bypassed by a supply plan.

**SC24 — External cooperation and domestic capability both need counterparts.**
Keep negotiated regional joint buying/shared reserves as a substantive option,
requiring accepted scope, contribution, title, release/return/loss, dispute,
privacy, lawful export/import and funded withdrawal/old-duty terms. Signals of
stock or common understanding are not consent, appropriation or a shipment.
Neither a foreign example nor a treaty aspiration creates membership or access.

Prefer repair, qualified local storage/maintenance/testing and diversified
accepted supply where they meet the actual need. Domestic public/cooperative/
private manufacture remains an explicit investment rival, not a promise of
self-sufficiency. Facilities, staff, licenses, inputs, safe scale, ecological
burdens, owned FX and transition all need sources. Do not assume free imported
expertise or make poorer source countries' care the residual funding resource.
