# Package comparison and future collaboration

## 3. Compare coherent packages, including the transition

A package links a mechanism to an institutional arrangement, people who act,
resources they use, distribution of outcomes, powers exercised and ways to correct
failure. Compare the baseline, a limited change and a structurally different option
where meaningful; explain if an alternative is infeasible rather than invent it.
No-change includes its likely costs and harms.

Use a shared comparison context where possible: population, period, starting
conditions, units, existing obligations and material boundary. If alternatives
cannot share a definition without losing essential meaning, report that disagreement
and limit the comparison. Do not force them onto one national happiness score.

For each package, answer the following at a depth proportionate to its consequences:

- What mechanism is expected to work, what could defeat it, and whose behavior changes?
- Who benefits, loses, is missing or cannot readily leave? What does participation
  actually establish, and what representation or authority is still absent?
- Which institution controls assets, information, interpretation and enforcement?
  Who chooses and replaces its coordination method? Are alternatives usable in practice?
- What resources, skills, cash and physical capacity are required by period, including
  training, overlap, maintenance and existing commitments? Reconcile shared accounts.
- Which effects cross the chosen boundary, including ecological and future effects?
  What irreversible exposure, failure recovery and funded remedies are involved?
- What happens while a decision is delayed? Trace the default distribution of power
  and harm, observable triggers, continuing duties and fallback dependencies. State
  the assumptions under which a remedy can actually complete, not only begin.

A preferable steady state can have an infeasible transition. A technically feasible
package can still be unjustified. A named appeal body or backup provider is not
an operational safeguard until the relevant capacity and authority are established.

Different institutional choices may remain incompatible. A combination needs its
own integration assessment; it cannot inherit the strongest claims of each part
while omitting their common costs or conflicts.

## 4. Choose the next action according to what is unresolved

| Current obstacle | Useful next research action | Unwarranted shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| Competing diagnoses | Find discriminating evidence or expose a value disagreement | Refine the favored institution before questioning its mechanism |
| Material feasibility unknown | Build a bounded resource or transition assessment | Call a persuasive narrative implementation-ready |
| Meaning disputed | Compare interpretations and preserve decisive differences | Treat successful data exchange as shared understanding |
| Evidence supports several acceptable options | Explain the tradeoff under the applicable decision process | Invent universal weights or claim the model discovered consent |
| Authority or participation absent | Prepare alternatives and name the unresolved governance question | Create authority through a research approval |
| Promising but untested mechanism | Design the least exposing informative assessment, with real prerequisites | Treat a simulation or authored example as a successful intervention |
| Existing arrangement remains preferable | Record a reasoned retain/no-change result and reopening condition | Manufacture a redesign to show activity |

Choose work by the consequence of the uncertainty, people affected, dependencies,
available capability and cost. Ask whether the result could alter a consequential
choice. Numerical precision is optional; reasons are not. A task must name a bounded
output, completion condition and resource limit or honestly state that the limit
is unmeasured. Additional prose alone is not an evaluation result.

Separate the following in every decision: evidence qualification, research selection,
legal authorization, operational readiness and observed outcomes. An unknown value
in one is not filled by a positive value in another. State what was actually checked,
what was not, which dissent remains and what decision is being requested now.

## 5. Support future collaboration through replaceable representations

Expression, interpretation, negotiation, delegation, authorization and execution
are distinct acts. Speech, drawings, demonstrations, simulations, asynchronous
agents and future channels may support different acts. A method can be useful for
exploration while insufficient for a consequential commitment.

Preserve enough meaning for the cooperative purpose: source/context, attribution,
transformation and known loss, decisive terms, mandate, affected scope and challenge
route. Keep unfamiliar concepts visible. Unsupported meaning cannot silently become
an accepted familiar category. Do not demand public archives of all private experience.

Separate people and institutions with standing from their accounts and agents.
A mandate states scope, duration, resources, reserved decisions, permitted
subdelegation and revocation. Agent replication does not multiply a mandate or
political standing. Prediction of a person's preference is not their authorization.
Controllers, sponsors and shared evidence matter more than the count of model names.

Assess mediation as power: filtering, framing, ranking and personalization can
change the choice being made. Preserve ways to contest the interpretation and replace
the mediator. Actual understanding and meaningful participation must be investigated;
a checkbox or a high-bandwidth interface cannot establish them.

Inquiry can be decentralized while exclusive commitments require settlement or
nonoverlapping prior allocations. Converging records does not reconcile incompatible
resource promises. Offline action needs explicit bounds; immediate global revocation
cannot be assumed across a disconnected network. Preserve human response opportunities
and applicable deadlines without converting silence or speed into new authority.

Collaboration methods are hypotheses to compare on understanding, agency, inclusion,
coordination, correction and effort. Do not optimize agreement or message throughput
as a substitute for better lives. Informal cooperation and private reflection need
not become formally governed workflows.
