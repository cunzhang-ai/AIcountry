| Arrangement | Public value and failure mode |
|---|---|
| Improve ordinary sector budgets and procurement | Fast and understandable for small repairs. Separate feasible files can consume the same crews or later budget. |
| Elected portfolio with bounded project stages | Makes that collision visible before major commitment. Independent review and project preparation need staff and can delay a useful project. |
| Central infrastructure agency with control of major approvals and a common delivery pool | Can professionalize scheduling and protect scarce expertise. It can also favor its own designs, impose a national veto over valid local priorities and become a single point of failure. |
| Rely on a private or public concessionaire to assemble and finance the service | Can bring accepted capital and integrate maintenance. Guarantees, recurring charges, lock-in and real staff requirements survive the financing change. |

Choose the elected portfolio for material commitments. Retain simpler existing
routes for routine lawful maintenance and quick safety action. Compare real
repair, replacement, staged work and contracted delivery without converting
everyone's interests into one monetary score. Separate a financial loss from
an interrupted service, ecological harm or foreclosure of meaningful alternatives.

The worked portfolio changes a decision because a cash ceiling and a work
window bind jointly. It gives an explicit first-year choice under stated
conditions, leaving future operation and whole-life effects open. It neither
prices a country's stock nor authorizes investment or asset transfer.
