# Two foundations for individual income security

*Part of [C05/08](../08-income-ownership-and-country-choice.md); proposed models.*

**A — actual-needs guarantee.** I01–I12 assesses each person's usable
resources, housing, disability and support duties, with disregards, advance
payment, safe access, correction and continuing accrued claims. A protected
income need not depend on an employer, partner or mandatory work placement.
The gain is targeted adequacy at a declared basket; the risk is exclusion,
surveillance, delay, discretionary disbelief and a combined tax/withdrawal
burden. A model that assumes every eligible person receives payment does
not measure those harms or prove its proposed safeguards work.

**B — universal personal foundation.** Every covered adult receives the
same adult base and every covered child the child base before assessing
earnings or a partner. It retains services and separately due earned pension,
survivor and access claims. Additional housing, functional and extraordinary
needs retain an assessed receiving route. Universal base receipt can reduce
the assessment controlling ordinary survival; it does not remove residence,
identity, safe payment or additional-needs disputes.

The authored B comparison pays 1,300 monthly per adult and 900 per child,
including a 300 personal allowance, with a work supplement capped at 300.
These are hypotheses in the old price/cohort fixture, not adequate actual
housing prices or calibrated statutory rates. Earnings contribute 10% plus
a 55% gross-wage tax; capital income pays 80% personal tax after 50%
corporate tax. The resulting combined distributed-profit rate is 90%.
No unchanged labor supply, investment, collection or prices are established.

B replaces only the matching same-person, same-period basic and tax-relief
components in this comparison; it does not add the old basic/VAT totals
again. Actual matching needs a lawful claim-level transition. Accrued earned,
survivor and access claims remain additional. Higher support payments owed
by a parent are neither canceled nor automatically reimbursed by a second
public allowance. Residual hardship protection remains in the fixture.

A child may gain while the paying adult loses. The state cannot simply
pool the child's gain into the adult's available resources or assume a
partner shares capital. Each person's control and independently due needs
matter; household totals alone cannot establish consent or adequate exit.

These models embody a value disagreement about universality, privacy,
reciprocity and distribution as well as uncertain behavioral effects.
Choose prospective rates and coverage through elected law, with opposition,
published person-level incidence, funded receiving and direct independent
remedy. Tax administration remains accountable to law; a payment operator
or AI cannot set eligibility values or change rights by model optimization.
No administrative saving is credited merely for using the word universal.

[Design88 IC](../09-income-capital-and-democratic-investment.md) selects a different
universal ordinary-provision destination with continuing I05 extra-needs/transition
protection. Its meal-net cash definition, prices and financing comparisons do
not reuse the old B amounts or tax rates above as a current funded package.
