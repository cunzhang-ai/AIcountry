# Primary records on road works coordination and its tradeoffs

1. United Kingdom, *New Roads and Street Works Act 1991*, original
   enacted text, sections 59–60. The statute establishes related
   coordination and cooperation duties for street authorities and
   undertakers and addresses safety, access including disability,
   and apparatus integrity. The proposal uses this as a precedent
   for a duty across separate owners, without importing the UK's
   allocation of legal powers.
   [Enacted text](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/22/pdfs/ukpga_19910022_en.pdf).
2. Ecorys for the UK Department for Transport, *Street Works Lane
   Rental Evaluation*, 2016, executive summary and sections 5.2–5.3.
   The original evaluation of Kent and London reports changed
   scheduling behavior and less disruption in monitored areas.
   It lacked a detailed counterfactual, so it could not identify
   how much was caused by the charges. Promoters disagreed about
   the additional effect beyond permits. The report discusses
   additional out-of-hours costs and possible deferred work.
   [Original evaluation](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/509203/ecorys-lane-rental-report.pdf).

The legal text and evaluation support considering coordination,
permit and price instruments separately. They do not estimate
the costs or effects of the proposed protected access, engineering
separation or local budgets. The constructed case uses authored facts.
