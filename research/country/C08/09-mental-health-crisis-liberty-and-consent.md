# C08. Mental-health crisis, liberty and consent

*Part of [C08 everyday law and justice](../C08-everyday-law-and-justice.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

Diagnosis, unusual behavior, substance use, disability and predicted risk
do not themselves justify detention, forced treatment, loss of legal
capacity or a criminal charge. Seeking help, self-harm and an attempt to
end one's own life are not offenses in this reference design. The person
retains supported decision-making, privacy, counsel, voting rights and
direct access to a court. A trusted supporter or advance crisis plan can
communicate the person's will within a limited, revocable mandate; neither
family, clinician nor AI inherits general control of the person's body,
housing or money.

The selected reference removes a diagnosis-specific civil commitment and
forced-treatment power. C07 must first supply voluntary, accessible crisis
care with a real accepting team. An immediate intervention to prevent
imminent serious physical harm uses **general, conduct-specific law**
applicable to anyone: an observable danger, necessity, minimum force and
duration, recorded reasons, medical care, prompt independent review and a
safe exit. Rescue does not turn into indefinite custody when a clinician
changes the label. Emergency treatment when someone cannot communicate
uses the same narrow necessity safeguards as other medical emergencies,
not a separate presumption that a psychiatric diagnosis cancels consent.
Seclusion, restraint or sedation cannot be routine treatment, discipline,
staff convenience or a response to missing beds; any immediate protective
act needs its own lawful ground and external scrutiny. A general safety
order does not authorize medication over objection by implication.

Police act on a separate, specific threat, suspected offense or lawful
protective order, with de-escalation and proportional force. Civilian crisis
staff do not gain search or arrest powers from their clinical task. If a
person is accused of harming others, ordinary investigation, defense,
proof, diversion and court rules apply. A prior diagnosis or AI forecast
is not guilt. People in custody retain clinical care independent of
police and prison command; a sentence or bail condition cannot be extended
because a provider has no appointment. If abuse occurs, C08's external
official-abuse route preserves evidence and independent investigation.

Children and adolescents retain supported voice, privacy, family support
where safe and the C08 child-protection floor. A parent's wish, school
discipline or public panic does not authorize detention or medication;
a specific immediate danger and a child's own legal safeguards apply.
A person refused admission, discharged unsafely or subjected to force can
contact an advocate, independent reviewer or court without the provider's
permission. Review examines actual records, less intrusive available
options, duration, clinical harm and the enforceability of a correction.
The Assembly funds legal aid, interpreters, independent facility access,
external inspection and a safe receiving service; a complaint box inside
the implicated institution is not an effective remedy.

Existing involuntary orders, treatment plans, detention time, pending
challenges and lawful clinical duties require case-by-case inventory and
authorized transition. Valid prior law continues until competent amendment;
the research selection alone neither releases a person into homelessness
nor authorizes a new detention. Before changing a real system, the state
must staff voluntary crisis services, supported housing, medical follow-up,
independent review and emergency response. Report legal authorization,
actual capacity and observed liberty and health effects separately.
Reopen if the general safety rule becomes disguised diagnosis detention,
voluntary services fail to protect people in crisis, or a competing
rights-preserving public arrangement performs better under actual evidence.

[UA](23-necessary-care-and-unknown-will.md) adds a separate proposed general
unknown-will clinical route, including mental-health proposals only on identical
actual necessity/support/review grounds. It creates no diagnosis-specific power,
actual-objection override, covert dosing, behavioral normalization or closed
placement. Nonconsensual residual permission remains disputed under the CRPD
Committee's interpretation; labels or formal retained capacity do not resolve it.

**Design144 RX join.** [Emergency continuity](../C10/01-public-safety-and-emergencies.md)
creates no diagnosis-based commitment, override of actual refusal, generalized
noncriminal confinement or substitute custody clock. UA16's minimum dependent
continuity remains its separate disputed source, without unsafe abandonment or
conversion into consent. Existing direct useful-time rights review survives.
