## Diagnosis, values and coherent alternatives

Treat body, material-use permission, identity/origin, legal parenting, support
and safe receiving as different acts/claims. A contract or clinic can otherwise
use payment/dependence to acquire bodily authority; donor genetics can be
mistaken for parental power; a disputed adult arrangement can leave a child
without a receiving service. Formal consent can fail without actual advice,
resources/exit and a usable remedy. These are diagnoses, not measured outcomes.

Compare principles with case-by-case invention; general prohibition of
third-party pregnancy/restricted assisted parenthood; market/intention-contract
control; and differentiated bodily/material consent with separate support,
post-birth status and public receiving. Select the differentiated candidate.
Preserve prohibition and stronger contractual intention as genuine dissent,
not merely inputs automatically absorbed into the selected model. No aggregate
metric settles fetal interests, autonomy, identity or exploitation.

## Consequential national choices

RP01/RP02 retains on-request abortion under safe professional judgment without
penal gestational cutoff or third-party authorization; newborn care is
independent, unsafe techniques need accepting alternatives. B22 supports
actual understanding from 16 presumptively and younger when supported, not a
universal parental veto. Individual prospective conscientious nonparticipation
cannot veto legally owed service or emergency care. This is a normative
choice, not an external guideline's declaration that ethical dispute is over.

RP03/RP04 requires specific source/collection/storage/reproduction/research
consent and future cell-derived use; withdrawal stops future insemination/
transfer, not the pregnant person's choice afterward. An actual pre-use
nonparent donor record can work outside clinics; signatures/genes alone do
not settle a dispute. Origin identity is accessible at 18, with earlier need
review and confidential notice/entry, not compelled contact or a public genome
register. New donations have no permanent anonymity promise; legacy claims
require actual lawful reconciliation. Ten recipient units is uncalibrated.

RP05/RP06 permits adult 18+ third-party pregnancy with independent advice,
practical exit and pre-funded contribution/care obligations. No prenatal
surrender or compulsory medical term. Pay actual costs/loss, a gestation
burden allowance of one I02 basket per 30 days plus recovery up to 90 days,
and donor time at the national median hourly rate; reconcile overlap once.
No outcome/surrender bonus, child price or brokerage per acquisition. These
formulas are contestable, not observed fair compensation or public money.
Received after-tax contribution/time and replaced earnings use I05 E retention;
exact cost refunds match costs once. The new incidence/cost is not priced by
old income/macroeconomic tests, and no tax exemption/employment mandate follows.

RP07/RP08 binds actual intended support after authorized insemination/transfer,
not mere embryo storage. A child's traits or an adult breakup do not erase
that responsibility. The gestating person's status transfer needs actual
post-birth advice/consent,30-day revocation and child-court order; payment is
not custody and no personal caregiving is compelled. Parentage, interim care,
support and bodily will remain separate; child services receive meanwhile.

RP09/RP10 specifies consent/storage/closure, ten-year adult terms, childhood
preservation through 20 and a12-month protected notice hold with court fallback.
Posthumous use requires specific prior consent/parent intention, not estate
ownership; after-death default estate eligibility has a36-month birth and
six-month notice window. Existing claims, ordinary already-conceived children,
actual estate limits, Y's single pot and I/S receiving remain separate.

RP11/RP12 preserves severable foreign child status with actual authority/safety
review and no blanket parentage-convention assumption. RP13/RP14 sets a
specific 14-day embryo-research boundary, prospective clinical holds for
heritable editing/cloning/derived gametes and entirely external gestation,
with functional classification and no rights-free child if an unfamiliar
case already exists. It supplies no technical protocol or promise of safety.

RP15 creates only three closed wrongs with 5/2/10-year maxima: nonconsensual
reproductive intervention/use, knowing nonconsensual research use, and sale/
compelled surrender for consideration. Full P/Q proof/defense, child process,
means/aggregate limits and 6/3/12-year ordinary periods apply; no crime from
a pregnancy outcome or clinical form alone. Actual K/G wrongs need their own
elements, with no duplicate punishment. Future law cannot approve itself.
