# A mistranslated condition can change the vote that reaches the floor

A resident panel has 20 commissioned seats. Five valid designations
are needed for its one protected competing text. Two original texts
qualify on substance. A proposes running a bus trial only after an
independent review and a funded accessible replacement route. B
proposes a different timetable. A public interpretation says the
replacement route is optional. This changes a condition of the
original proposal. The panel's main recommendation is unaffected.

At the initial cutoff A has four valid designations and two made
after the faulty interpretation. B has five valid designations.
Under an independent correction both affected residents receive the
original eligible choices in a usable form and explicitly choose A.
A then has six valid designations and takes the slot. B's reasons and
original five remain in the record. The elected Assembly retains its
own decision on both the main and competing recommendations.

| Disposition | Result in the example |
|---|---|
| Carry the two designations without asking | A appears to have six without valid support for its actual terms. |
| Discard the two and close immediately | B takes the slot while the process error still denies two residents a usable choice. |
| Correct and verify the two choices in time | A leads six to five on explicit and valid support. |
| Correct and receive one confirmation, one abstention | A and B tie at five. Apply the existing auditable lot. |
| Fail to reach the two before the deadline | Four and five are known. Record a procedural failure because the unavailable choices could alter the protected slot. |

If an affected participant switches to B instead, remove any
prior designation from A before counting B. If the author replaces
the substantive condition after cutoff, that is a new text for the
ordinary procedure and receives no inherited support. A small
misspelling with no change of operative meaning needs no new
designation. Protect other evidence, independent legal claims
and ordinary elected business throughout.

The commission has 3 illustrative units set aside for independent
review, accessible correction and floor handling. Each uses one.
At 2 units, the full scoped repair cannot be assumed. A full new
panel round would need more time and staff. If the alternate
interpreter shares the failed provider's control, the apparent
second route does not establish usable access. The stated numbers
are assumptions. No actual public vote, staff, funding or effect
is established.
