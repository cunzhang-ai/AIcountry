The directed source leaves match between payer and receiver. For example
the central-to-regional amounts are 142.210 current and 2.124 capital.

These are broad transfer aggregates, not block grant or service allocations.

gives 1,472.840 expense and 1,563.762 revenue. Preserve the +0.001 and

national accounts. The accounts provide no amount owned by a specific
payer before a given service deadline.

## The state budget, outturn and net cash tell different stories

The original 2025 Finance Act presentation, the appropriations after the
supplementary act and the state outturn are shown below in billion DKK.
The Finance Ministry dates passage to 12 December 2024. The initial
expenditure is the sum of the three expense columns in the Act overview.
The state accounts summary supplies the latter two rows at its published
precision. These rows keep the state budget perimeter. They do not
reconstruct the laws and cash calendars for all subsectors.

| State-budget basis | Expense | Revenue | Operating, capital and lending balance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Original 2025 budget | 937.9613 | 980.1920 | +42.2307 |
| After supplementary appropriations | 1,022.7 | 979.5 | −43.2 |
| State accounts outturn | 1,014.2 | 1,010.8 | −3.4 |

The state accounts agency then reports −13.0 net cash for the year
before government debt issuance or redemption. The bridge deducts
6.7 of on-lending and related items and 2.9 of balance movements
from the −3.4 balance. This account-based liquidity measure does
not show available cash on a given day. State-account expense
includes financial transactions such as equity purchases in its
own perimeter. The +89.748 OFF3 central net lending and +90.924
consolidated government net lending are statistical measures with
different boundaries. No subtraction between those two systems
is treated as a financing source. This explains why the voted
path, audited outturn and payable dates must stay identifiable.

## Purpose and employment are two different measures

OFF29 annual expenditure in billion DKK. OBESK1 public full-time employee
equivalents are simple means of four published quarterly averages.
These describe employee jobs in equivalent units. The allocation by
purpose is estimated from wage and administrative inputs, including
national-account wage information. It is not independent corroboration
of expenditure. The datasets use a common purpose classification, with
different measures and releases. [Statistics Denmark employment methodology](https://www.dst.dk/en/Statistik/dokumentation/documentationofstatistics/public-sector-employment--quarterly-/statistical-processing)

The fiscal purpose rows sum exactly to the official expense. Purpose
employment leaves differ from the official quarter totals by −1, −1,
+2 and +1 units, with zero unclassified. Preserve the published totals.
OBESK2 separately averages to 197,876.50 central, 2,010 social security,
133,584.50 regional and 439,442.50 municipal, with a one-unit residual
in the sum in Q4. These are separate purpose and employer partitions,
without a cell-level employer-by-purpose roster. Health uses about 17.5%
of expenditure and represents about 29.5% of these employee equivalents.
Social protection is about 40.9% of expenditure and 27.9% of the
equivalents. The expenditure includes purchases, transfers, capital
and other costs; a share difference cannot show worker efficiency or
transferable labour. Non-government operators and unpaid support
require separate data.

are household 1,297.4, nonprofit institutions serving households 46.6,
