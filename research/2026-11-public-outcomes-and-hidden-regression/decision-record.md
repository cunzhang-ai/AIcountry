# Design252 decision record. Public outcomes and hidden regression

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned Design251](baseline.json)

## Decision and diagnosis

Select [PCO01–PCO05](../country/C14/04/03-comparable-outcomes-and-hidden-regression.md).
Show observed outcomes, composition and uncertainty separately. Give
an independently qualified finding of material systemic regression or
exclusion an elected response with named responsibilities and deadlines.
Preserve individual rights and the original independent paths.
Apply the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

The existing public conditions account rejects a single welfare score
and includes distribution. It does not fully specify how to handle a
favorable headline produced by a change in who remains visible, or how
a serious finding reaches a decision between election reports.
An elected government can legitimately choose among funded lawful
responses to mixed outcomes. It should not be able to erase a worsening
dimension by changing the denominator, offering one average or
delaying all consideration until an election.

## Compare real alternatives

| Choice | Strong reason | Material exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Keep regular independent multidimensional reporting and ordinary political response | Leaves wide elected discretion and low standing agenda cost. | Serious but less visible losses can remain unaddressed between reports. |
| Use a weighted national welfare index and automatic resource response | A simple public signal can support communication and precommitment. | Weights can conceal incompatible values and reward selective inclusion. Automation can turn a statistical claim into spending or power. |
| Use separate observed and fixed-scope comparisons and require a timed elected disposition | Exposes concentrated loss, correction and uncertainty while keeping lawful choice with accountable people. | Requires qualified independent evidence, protected capacity and restraint against treating every fluctuation as systemic harm. |
| Put most weight on directly reported resident experience and participatory decisions | Captures lived losses that administrative categories can miss. | Participation can be selective or constrained by dependence. Direct accounts need protection and should be used beside original data and actual receipt. |

Select the third with the fourth as an essential evidence and public
participation route. The proposed 30- and 60-calendar-day periods are
accountability choices. Preserve ordinary elected procedures, earlier
urgent action and qualified direct challenge. Failure to act is
recorded, without giving a statistical office power to spend or
letting a favorable number certify a government.

## Evidence and transition

The [Wales comparison](../settlement/public-outcomes-and-regression/01-wales-health-and-distribution.md)
shows different health endpoints in the same periods and places.
The [authored access case](../settlement/public-outcomes-and-regression/02-coverage-weights-and-a-public-response.md)
shows composition and noncoverage effects and the need for a timely
public response even while a causal explanation is open. Neither is
evidence that the candidate is superior to Wales or any other society.

Adoption requires protected production and evidence access, prior
competence and materiality rules, paid outside scrutiny, accessible
participation, lawful sitting, budget and remedy paths. Publish changed
series with predecessors and protect small groups and safe testimony.
Maintain claims, publication history and follow-through during a change
of government or supplier. Actual affected participation, independent
review, enactment, funded operation and observed outcomes remain open.
