# One corrected observation, four different decisions

In a constructed local trial, a public register originally reports
120 distinct residents requiring a weekly vehicle. Its correction
finds 30 duplicate entries and a verified count of 90 in the same
period and scope. An English summary and a translated model output
both use the same register. An independently collected accessibility
audit remains separate evidence. The example's decision criteria
and costs are stipulated. A real council would need actual law,
participation, evidence, money and qualified review.

| Use | Dependence and authority | Disposition after correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Optional extra vehicle release | An elected appropriation requires at least 100 distinct eligible residents under the corrected register measure. The owner still must verify receiving and a lawful budget. | 120 met the numerical condition and 90 does not. Hold only the optional release under the existing condition and present narrower lawful options. |
| Accessibility repair | An independent site audit establishes an applicable accessibility defect and an existing authorized repair. The register supplied context only. | Record why the evidence supporting the repair survives. Keep the existing review and repair timetable. |
| This week's earned operator pay | The payment is due under an executed contract for completed work. The original forecast helped select the programme, but verified work controls this payment. | Pay on the original date and keep lawful claims. Compare the future discretionary commitment separately. |
| Neighboring trial | An oral briefing used a number matching the old register. There is incomplete evidence that it was a binding input, and the trial has a pending optional start. | Notify the local owner, seek the original briefing and its meaning, and require a qualified basis before any new reliance. Apply an interim hold only where lawful conditions require it. |

The source correction is one observation-family change. Matching
translations and models do not replace independent observations.
A recorded site audit stays qualified for the scope it actually
inspected. An unresolved neighboring use remains open even when
the other three have dispositions. If the correction reveals people
omitted rather than duplicate records, recheck lawful coverage and
actual access before comparing the options. Send protected details
only to competent receivers. The trial owner must verify any change
in receiving for affected residents.

A broad restart interrupts a due service and duplicates review.
Publication alone leaves the optional release relying on a failed
condition. The scoped route protects due work, keeps qualified
independent support and sends the uncertain use to a real owner.
The case leaves actual costs, speed and field results unverified.
