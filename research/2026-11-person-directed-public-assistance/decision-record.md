## Values, alternatives and research selection

People should be able to understand rules/options and pursue their own lawful
aims, without surrendering private life or political judgment. Agency efficiency,
benefit denial, credential counts and chatbot satisfaction are different from
usable person-side help. Real expertise, safe source/receipt, personal time,
care/access, independence and paid continuity constrain the design. Future media,
AI or human help preserve understanding, mandate, filing, decision and consent
as distinct acts; no central life model becomes the country's decision-maker.
