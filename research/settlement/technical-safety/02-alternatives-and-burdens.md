# Substantively different safety arrangements

*Part of [technical safety](README.md); research choice, not a universal optimum.*

| Model | Mechanism and reason to consider it | Present disposition and cost/objection |
|---|---|---|
| Existing general promises plus after-harm civil claims | Fewer entry gates, room for informal competence and direct liability. | Reject as complete reference: people exposed to irreversible third-party harm may not have practical information, exit or collectable compensation before catastrophe. Actual unnecessary licensing should still be removed. |
| Private professional/certifier/insurer governance | Specialized knowledge, contractual standards and loss incentives can adapt rapidly. | Retain as a real rival and source of evidence, not final public power. Selection/payment and unavailable coverage can exclude entrants, conceal sources and shift tail loss to nonparties. Actual comparative performance could justify more reliance. |
| Public ownership and direct technical provision | A public backbone can combine maintenance, universal access, knowledge and long-term liability without a private certification market. | Retain, and potentially select by field where it provides safer access. Public operation alone does not prevent political cover-up or self-inspection; it needs distinct source/judgment, real staff and democratic replacement. It is not absorbed into a permanent licensing preference. |
| Selected plural operators with exact public duties, graded entry and separate assessment | Preserve ordinary freedom and diverse delivery while limiting defined high-consequence activities, retaining independent source access and actual repair. | Select conditionally in TS/TD. It adds assessors, courts, labs, fees, waiting and compliance; it may be worse than a competent public provider in some fields. No measured superiority is supplied. |

The diagnosis could be wrong: underinvestment, ownership, unsafe employment and
car-dependent planning may matter more than a missing form. TS02/C09 therefore
require safer design, maintenance and usable alternatives. Do not respond to every
failure by adding a new gate, a central AI or another regulator. Real user/worker/
excluded-entrant experience and original comparative outcomes could displace the
selected institutional arrangement rather than merely amend its checklist.

Assembly controls material risk choices and money; bounded experts set justified
technical detail through lawful procedure. Neither public popularity nor technical
consensus alone determines a named person's guilt or permits a rights violation.
A successful average outcome cannot substitute for actual popular control, practical
opposition and a delivered independent remedy.

A paid conflict-screened assessment pool is one proposal, not demonstrated capacity.
Actual independence requires competent alternatives, protected source access and
permission to dissent, alongside the cost of assignments, disputes and replacement.
Industry fees can burden small entrants; public assistance/pooling needs real
appropriation. A shared cross-border service needs actual authority and acceptance.

No old SF cash, general overhead allowance, assumed AI productivity or future fine
revenue pays these increments. Insurance is a claim on another party with exclusions,
not newly created physical repair capacity. Existing harms, workers, households,
unsafe assets and long-term closure duties need a paid transition. Missing capacity
remains a substantive objection and possible reason to choose another model.
