# Decision record: interpersonal safety and fair process

5 October 2026 · Integrated design 54 research selection; not enacted law

## Baseline, scope and competing explanations

The [design-53 predecessor](predecessor-design-53/README.md) preserves the
prior current research. C08/01 gave domestic or intimate harm one paragraph:
confidential help, housing, care, payment and a temporary order with a
respondent challenge. It did not assign an after-hours receiving service,
clinical and forensic separation, order execution, continuing claims,
cross-boundary handoff or funding. C08's general criminal and court routes
could not alone show that a person is safe on a night when the threatening
party controls their home, money and phone.

Affected people include survivors and nonreporters, accused people, children,
care recipients, police and clinical workers, advocates, landlords, employers
and neighbors. Violence is shaped by unequal physical, economic, social and
official power; not every conflict or accusation is violence. No affected
AIcountry public has participated. The competing explanations for a failed
response include inadequate service reach, unsafe disclosure, police
nonresponse, unequal credibility, a weak order, a court delay, financial
dependence or abuse of protective powers. Original confidential accounts,
denial and response records, orders served and breached, clinical data,
legal outcomes and review of wrongful restrictions would distinguish them.

## Coherent alternatives and selected reference

| Package | Plausible gain | Serious failure or objection |
|---|---|---|
| Retain general criminal and civil routes with voluntary charity support | Few new institutions and little extra state power | A nonreporter has no reliable first receiver, safe place or payment path; a court order may have no executor |
| Police and prosecution as the mandatory entry gate | Concentrates reporting and evidence powers and may act quickly | Deters help where police, migration or custody consequences are feared; clinical care and housing wait for a charge |
| Confidential social and clinical service without urgent protective authority | Protects autonomy and access to care | A concrete violent threat or order breach may have no enforceable response |
| **Selected: independent services plus bounded protection and ordinary criminal process** | Gives help without a charge, time-limited orders with actual execution, and distinct fair criminal proof | Needs costly after-hours capacity and may still be captured or used to control an accused partner |

The selected [C07 service](../country/C07/12-interpersonal-safety-and-survivor-support.md)
keeps a direct confidential receiving route across care, housing, payments
and children. [C08](../country/C08/10-interpersonal-violence-protection-and-fair-process.md)
separates immediate safety, a time-limited civil order and criminal guilt,
with respondent challenge and independent review. [C06](../country/C06/16-interpersonal-safety-finance.md)
counts the staff, places and execution; [C13](../country/C13/19-nighttime-interpersonal-safety-failure.md)
tests a night-time failure. This is a working hypothesis, not a finding that
more orders or more prosecutions necessarily improve lives.

## Primary support and limits

The [WHO clinical handbook](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-RHR-14.26)
identifies immediate emotional and physical health, ongoing safety and
continuing support needs after intimate-partner or sexual violence. The
[WHO clinical and policy guidance](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241548595)
is a comparator for health responses, not AIcountry clinical law. The
[WHO child and adolescent guidance](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241550147)
emphasizes safety, choices and a child-centered response. The joint UN
[essential-services package](https://www.unwomen.org/sites/default/files/Headquarters/Attachments/Sections/Library/Publications/2015/Essential-Services-Package-en.pdf)
addresses health, policing/justice, social services and coordination for
women and girls subjected to violence. These sources make the missing
service interfaces visible; they do not prove that this country's exact
protection-order threshold, gender-inclusive eligibility, costs, law or
outcomes are right. The burden falls disproportionately on women and girls;
gender-inclusive access must not erase that evidence or targeted provision.

## Authority, objections and migration

The Assembly enacts offenses, protective-order grounds and expiry,
confidentiality exceptions, service entitlements, budgets and independent
remedies. Clinical professionals deliver care; local housing and benefit
authorities supply actual receiving places and payments; police act within
specific powers; prosecutors decide charges independently; courts review
orders, custody and family claims. Neither a case coordinator nor an AI tool
can turn one account into guilt, an arrest, a custody ruling or a deportation.
An independent reviewer needs access to original orders, denials, service
receipts, location breaches and evidence of official harm without making
every survivor's intimate testimony permanently public.

Hard objections remain. An emergency order before the respondent can be
heard can prevent harm but can also be wrong or weaponized; delayed hearings
and nominal appeals make it more dangerous. A mandatory police report can
destroy access, while a confidentiality promise without clear child and
imminent-harm exceptions is misleading. The person may choose to return to a
relationship; services must keep a safe route open without disguising serious
threats. A risk instrument may conceal discrimination and gain de facto
authority; nonuse alone does not remove human bias. Specialized offices may
improve skill but become a closed case monopoly. A gender-neutral description
can hide unequal exposure. These disagreements cannot be averaged away.

Any real adoption must inventory existing violence, sexual-offense, family,
child, tenancy, employment, health, evidence, data, migration and police law;
pending orders, custody claims, criminal cases and accrued rights survive
until prospectively handled by competent authority. Existing shelters,
clinicians, workers and claims need a funded transition. No research text
alters an order, creates a crime or authorizes coercion now.

## Result and next bounded test

Evidence qualification: official comparative guidance and internal design
review, without original local observations or affected-party participation.
Research selection: a confidential help route, actual material protection,
bounded interim order and separate fair criminal process. Legal adoption:
absent. Operational readiness: absent. Observed safety, liberty and family
outcomes: absent. The next test in a real jurisdiction is a witnessed
after-hours journey under phone and money control, followed through clinical
care, safe placement, order service, respondent hearing, child decision,
cross-boundary continuity and one wrongful or breached order. Compare this
package with service-only and police-gate alternatives using original
accounts, rights, costs and actual execution. Reopen if help remains unsafe
or unreachable, protective powers facilitate abuse, or a rival yields better
rights-respecting safety under legitimate public choice.
