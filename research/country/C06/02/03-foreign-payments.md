# C06. Money, credit and payments: Foreign payments and exchange

*Part of [C06. Money, credit and payments](../02-money-credit-and-payments.md); integrated design 74 research, not enacted law.*

## Foreign payments and exchange

The default exchange-rate reference is a managed float without a guaranteed
conversion rate. Intervention uses only lawfully held, uncommitted reserves under
published objectives and audited limits. The external account separately traces
foreign-currency debts, import bills, correspondent access and reserve drains by
due date; domestic issuance cannot settle a foreign-currency obligation unless
the creditor lawfully agrees. Capital-flow limits or exchange
allocation need explicit rights-reviewed legislation, finite duration and public
incidence reporting, not a hidden payment-operator decision. A faster domestic
payment does not establish imported medicine or fuel delivery. Prior deposits,
wages, contracts and debt retain their lawful denomination until changed by
consent or valid, reviewable law with specified remedies; no research text
silently redenominates them.
The [public-debt rule](../06-sovereign-debt-and-distress.md) governs any
proposed workout; a domestic decision cannot itself alter a foreign-law claim.

Each budget and material midyear or framework-law change uses the selected
[whole-country commitment account](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-whole-country-resources/decision-record.md).

The [national economic comparison](../20-national-economic-package.md)
adds private/public imports, export and foreign-interest counterparties.
A planned balanced trade flow is not actual settlement, and reserves
cannot be counted both as domestic fiscal cash and foreign continuity.

[M15–M16](../24/08-public-risk-and-migration.md) now specifies public-credit
and foreign-law boundaries plus lawful migration. Domestic deposit insurance
pays only its enacted domestic-unit claim, not a guaranteed foreign conversion.

[NS21–NS25](../54/06-essential-imports-and-usable-settlement.md)
joins accepted foreign settlement and actual essential receiving.
Reserve, currency, ownership, transaction and debt limits continue
under their own authorities.

## Decide scarce public exchange through prior law

Test each regime with the same protected claims through a bank failure,
exchange shock and second settlement call. Record owner and permitted use,
currency, source and ceiling, actual independent settlement by cutoff,
accepted physical delivery and loss incidence. The comparison may separate
a better route or inventory from a change of monetary regime. Test their
dependence on the same controllers, original records, collateral or staff.
Retain lawful earmarks, floors and creditor rights. Name each funding gap,
actual unpaid claim and missed service separately. A creditor's acceptance
of partial payment or changed terms needs its own lawful evidence.

Apply NS24 and CP05–CP08 only within enacted uses, shares, limits and
expiry. Where rival eligible uses compete, a prospective elected rule
must name evidence and priority, a paid alternate, direct independent
review and the authority to execute. If new source or priority authority
is needed, require an actual decision at the competent sitting, ordinary
or alternate. A blocked sitting, extra nominal balances, bank intervention
or technical ranking supplies no new allocation authority. Preserve
effective duties, original dates and lawful remedies. Reopen at the
next useful cutoff on lost substitute stock or usable receiving.
