# Design440. Fiscal perimeter and prior opening observations

The [IMF Government Finance Statistics Manual, chapter 3](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/display/book/9781498343763/ch003.xml)
describes consolidation of reciprocal flows and stock positions
within a stated boundary. It is an accounting reference. Each
legal payer's funding and settlement need remains a separate test.
Checked 13 November 2026.

| Opening dependency | Existing observed comparison | Material limit |
| --- | --- | --- |

funding arithmetic and does not price the proposed country.
