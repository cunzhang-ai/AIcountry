# Design395 decision. Keep residents' meaning intact through assisted public service

## Baseline and selected mechanism

[Design394](predecessor-design-394/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md) gives protected
first receipt, optional digital help, independent judgment, real
assent and separate authoritative legal versions. It still leaves a
gap between a person's mediated expression and the public act taken
on it. A tool can make service easier to find and can also quietly
turn a request for accommodation into a purported refusal.

Select [DP70–DP77](../country/C11/09/15-assisted-communication-and-public-receipt.md)
under the [working research method](../FRAMEWORK.md). Take first receipt
in a usable medium, qualify the meaning before consequential reliance,
keep urgent protective action available and trace later correction to
the final service. Fund qualified support separately from routine
navigation. Keep expression, interpretation, assent and authority
distinct in each media and vendor transition.

## Alternatives and objections

Retain general access duties and let each service choose support.
This permits local expertise but can leave informal translation,
missed deadlines and handoffs without an accountable operator.
An AI-first public gateway could give much broader low-cost initial
coverage, but its translation may create factual or legal meaning
the person never supplied. A universal live-interpreter requirement
would protect some interactions while making every low-risk contact
depend on scarce personnel. Select optional bounded support,
professionally checked standard material and proportionate qualified
meaning for consequential use. Compare qualified bilingual direct
work, separate interpretation and competent simpler non-AI material
at the same useful deadline.

Review may itself add delay and surveillance. Keep immediate lawful
protection, preserve the original receipt, and allow a safe
attributable record instead of routine recordings where lawful.
A small or remote language community may need paid shared capacity,
but two dependent vendors do not provide two available interpreters.
Workers, residents and independent reviewers can challenge unsafe
scope and overclaimed gains. Elected authorities retain the duty,
funding and means of replacing public leadership. No platform,
interpreter or affected party's companion can acquire public
decision power through the communication route.

## Evidence, transition and next action

[Primary evidence](sources.md) includes two written-translation
comparisons and official commissioning guidance. Their findings
cannot be transferred to signed, supported, oral or untested tasks.
The [worked case](../settlement/assisted-communication-and-public-receipt/01-two-requests-and-one-interpreting-slot.md)
tests overlapping demand and downstream correction. Actual local
cost, participation, readiness and results remain unverified.

Adopt prospectively with scoped model permissions, qualified staff,
accessible receiving, paid independent alternatives and lawful
records custody. Carry earlier claims, receipt dates, urgent powers,
appeals and separate authority for legal versions. A model replacement
does not renew a mandate or transfer validation for changed scope.

Next compare an actual housing intake and clinic support window.
Inspect professionally reviewed routine material, optional navigation
and direct qualified support, including people who never completed
intake. Trace a material correction to the final receiver and test
an outage with affected users and workers. Reopen on an unsupported
medium, repeat delay, shared supplier loss, unsafe disclosure or a
better qualified route.
