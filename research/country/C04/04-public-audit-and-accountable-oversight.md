# C04. Public audit and accountable oversight

2 November2026 · Design121 · AA01–AA40 research candidate, not enacted law

**Select a separately appointed Auditor General, protected statutory audit/report
work, timely fair public findings, direct courts and external audit of the audit
office itself.** This reconstitutes the existing external public-audit function;
it does not create a sovereign guardian, auditor's government or a duplicate
criminal/financial/electoral regulator. Public control needs actual lawful people,
sources, remedy and received correction, not an independent label.
