# RT01–RT05: initial agreement and a protected continuing price

*Part of [private rent/entry](../18-private-rents-tenure-and-housing-entry.md).*

**RT01 — Apply to actual ordinary residential use.** Cover ordinary private
residential tenancies within H's actual prospective scope, including disguised
short contracts or employment/platform labels that function as a home. Genuine
visitor accommodation is not a home tenancy merely by this Act; actual urgent
shelter/consumer rights persist. H public/community cost-rental cohorts keep
H11–H13, not RT's market initial-price rule. Public ownership or a nonprofit
label alone cannot waive a privately priced tenant's applicable protection.
Property/title, safety, H possession and B actual agreement remain distinct.

**RT02 — A genuine initial price remains agreed.** At genuinely new ordinary
letting after actual lawful full vacancy, disclose the dwelling, included space/
services, base rent, other permitted charges, deposit and future review clause
before assent/possession. The actual parties may freely agree that initial base,
subject to B's coercion/deception/unfair-term and H protections. No initial
comparables ceiling, target landlord return or actual affordability is inferred.
A signed market rent is not an I03 award or an adequate public alternative.
B already forbids unilateral changes without a lawful agreed/statutory source;
RT additionally limits what an original review clause may lawfully demand.

**RT03 — Define the price being controlled.** Base rent includes mandatory
payments to obtain/keep the residential space, including compulsory linked
parking/service packages or related-party fees. Prohibit new key money and
mandatory occupancy premiums outside lawful rent/deposit. Actual separately
metered/attributable third-party utilities at documented net cost may be billed
separately without markup/double recovery; show the basis before agreement.
Do not unbundle a previously included cost without an agreed/lawful matched
base reduction and actual source evidence. Owner taxes, acquisition debt and
appreciation are not separate tenant levies by renaming; actual NF/CB rules
remain. Optional genuinely separable services need real choice and prices.

**RT04 — Continuation is not a new first letting.** Sale, refinancing, change
of manager/platform/company, renewal/replacement paperwork and H05 lawful
succession do not reset the rent. A protected continuing co-tenant/occupant does
not lose the base because another leaves or a new co-tenant lawfully joins;
actual authority/consent/occupancy limits remain. A private room's separately
leased unit and a whole-home joint tenancy have their actual distinct scope.
Sham subdivision, temporary removal for repair and forced surrender are no
reset. Genuine voluntary lawful surrender/full vacancy followed by a different
new tenancy permits RT02; actual H/B claims and anti-retaliation survive.
This does not create a universal right to assign/sublet or freeze every unit
across all future tenants. Unit-linked regulation is a different choice.

**RT05 — Ceiling, not an entitlement to reprice.** Once per tenancy year, the
continuing ordinary monthly base cannot exceed the previous year's actually
lawful base multiplied by the prescribed annual CPI factor under RT06–08.
Do not capitalize RT10/11 temporary allowances, deposits, arrears or utilities
into that base. Base means the actual net ordinary recurring agreed rent after
permanent reductions, not cash received that month. Recurring/withdrawable
loyalty or introductory discounts count in that net base; an agreed expiry/step
still cannot demand more than the RT continuation ceiling. A genuinely separate,
fixed upfront nonrepayable credit or one-off waived billing period must disclose
the real regular base and exact credit before assent; its end is not an index
rise, but a recurring discount cannot be relabelled one-off to evade RT. The
landlord must prove the actual distinction under B/RT, not a headline price.

A genuine H04/B defect/compensation abatement or escrow does not permanently
lower the underlying undamaged base; apply its actual lawful reduction while
grounds persist, restore only after proved conditions and never recover abated
periods as debt. Arrears do not rebase rent to0. A valid
prior review clause and proper notice are needed for
an upward change; otherwise the lower agreed rent continues. Fixed/lower caps
or stronger lawful terms remain. Above-cap market-review/escalator clauses are
prospectively ineffective within RT19's valid migration; assent cannot waive
this statutory ceiling. No increase is banked because an earlier one was not
made, and no missed period becomes retroactive rent debt.
