# Safe revocation, final settlement and the money still owed

Consider an authored cash award of 120 due by the end of day 0.
The person already controls 45. An instruction of 50 to a previously
authorized agent entered settlement before the person withdrew the
mandate. The payer receives the revocation through a safe direct
route while there is still time to act. The final state of that
instruction is initially uncertain. The remaining 25 has never been
released. The person cannot safely use an account or send a proxy.

The selected public payer stops any still-controlled future release
to the old destination, checks actual finality and confirms 25
exclusively for the staffed direct point. Assume the point can
legally verify the person and is staffed and funded with 75 in the
ordinary case. For the separate example there is an express emergency
source with 20 available and a named official who can authorize it
after an urgent needs check. The bridge is not the same principal,
and the fixture specifies no automatic deduction from future awards.

| Day 0 alternative or event | Usable principal by the deadline | Principal whose useful receipt is missing | Distinct consequence |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Wait for a bank account or let the agent receive the money | 45 | 75 | Leaves both the unpaid part and disputed channel unresolved. |
| Copy the unpaid 75 to a second point while the 50 can still settle | No qualifying result | Unresolved | The two points could release 170 of principal in total, 50 over the award. |
| Pay the undisputed 25 while the earlier instruction is unresolved | 70 | 50 | A separate authorized 20 can bring usable money to 90 without changing principal or concealing lateness. |
| Confirm the 50 was cancelled or returned, then release 75 at the direct point | 120 | 0 | Requires actual cash, authority and accepted proof before the deadline. |
| Confirm the 50 settled to the old agent, with access disputed | 70 | 50 | Payment to the old agent and lawful discharge remain separate. Urgent protection and independent liability correction continue. |
| Confirm cancellation but find only 25 at the direct point | 70 | 50 | Qualification and reassignment do not produce cash. A verified funded backup must receive the remaining 50 in time or record the shortfall. |

A protected bridge does not cure a late underlying award. Record a
distinct legal outcome for an advance, recovery or later liability
payment. Two disconnected points can hold separate tranches only
after an authorized allocator gives each exclusive control. For
example, the direct point can hold 25 while a national alternate
holds 50 after cancellation. If the national alternate lacks safe
proof, cash or evidence of exclusive release, it cannot cover that
amount merely because an office signed a copy. A late return makes
the principal payable on its original claim, with separate late-loss
review. Neither an account repair nor a later withdrawal resets day 0.

These values test the separation of authority, finance and receiving.
They are independent of the observed Copenhagen amounts and the UK
procurement figure. They provide no estimate of the probability of
misdirection or the whole-country cost of a qualified fallback.
