# WL13–WL20: a job can end without arbitrary power or fictional continuity

*Part of [C05/12](../12-work-contracts-and-collective-power.md); design 117 candidate.*

**WL13 — Valid reason from entry.** Employer termination requires proved actual
capacity/conduct grounds or genuine operational need and a proportionate response.
Equality, temporary protected illness, reasonable accommodation, union/representative
activity, safety refusal or complaint cannot be disguised as those grounds.
Employer proves reason/process; worker needs a plausible protected claim, not
proof from unavailable employer records. No automatic permanent job, profit
entitlement, guilt or right to dismiss by paying a fixed fine.

**WL14 — Fair procedure and current safety.** Give actual allegations/relevant
protected evidence, accessible assistance, normally seven calendar days for reply,
a hearing opportunity and reasoned decision. Capability cases need reasonable
support/accommodation and warnings where correction is feasible. A specific
urgent safety threat may require immediate paid non-contact/reassignment and
prompt fair process, not presumed misconduct. An exceptional shortened process
needs proved necessity, no avoidable loss of reply and direct urgent court review.
Employer-controlled grievance is optional, not the final judge or claim prerequisite.

**WL15 — Employer notice.** Subject to stronger rights, give ordinary pay and
contractual benefits during notice or lawful equivalent pay instead:14 calendar
days below one completed service year;28 from one to below four;56 from four to
below ten;84 from ten. Actual ordinary weekly pay includes normal recurring cash
and uses a representative13-week ordinary average where variable, not an artificially
empty last week. Value actual contractual noncash/insurance benefits separately
without duplication. Notice/pay does not cure an unlawful reason. Summary exit
without notice requires proved serious misconduct/fair process; accrued dues survive.
AS's28-day minimum and stronger source/contract protections prevail.

**WL16 — Redundancy has a price.** A genuine role reduction/end needs disclosed
real operational grounds, fair nonretaliatory selection, alternatives and reasonable
redeployment/accommodation inquiry, not proof that all alternatives are affordable.
At one completed service year, redundancy severance is two ordinary-pay weeks per
continuous service year, proportionately including the remaining fraction, capped
at26 weeks under this new minimum; stronger terms and actual separate damages
survive. Before one year, notice/accrued rights remain without this new severance.
Use actual calendar anniversaries for completed years and the actual current
anniversary-year length for fractions, not a universal365-day shortcut. Severance
is additional to nonduplicated notice, earned wages/leave and pension claims.
For a29 February start, the civil-calendar anniversary is28 February in a
nonleap year unless a stronger source term applies. The date model uses an
express exclusive end instant; actual last-covered-day facts need qualification.

**WL17 — Collective reduction and automation.** If an undertaking proposes at
least10 dismissals in30 days or at least the greater of10 and10% of its workforce
in90 days, inform actual worker representatives and Work Relations
Registrar with reasons, numbers/selection, alternatives, costs and safe successors.
Consult for at least30 calendar days before first dismissal,45 if at least100
proposed in90 days. Aggregate actually controlled operations/evasive splits,
not unrelated firms. Small firms retain individual fair process. Seek alternatives
and bargain genuinely, not necessarily an agreement or a regulator's business
permission. A court may shorten only on proved unavoidable safety/insolvency
necessity with actual paid/claim/receiving protection; a forecast alone cannot.
TW/N capability, old contracts, creditor/U and actual service rights remain.
Use the actual affected workforce before the proposed reduction, rounding people
up for the percentage trigger, not a shrinking post-dismissal denominator.
Consultation must allow genuine alternatives before final selection; individual
notice/regular wages are reconciled by actual period, not paid twice for the
same working notice interval. No notice or consultation time is free labor.

**WL18 — Transfer and real closure.** Transfer of the same continuing operation
preserves actual service, wages, collective and accrued claims with a named lawful
successor; no shell/account restart. Private transfer needs competent law/required
assent, evidence, source and third-party rights. Insolvency U can produce real
losses and lawful closure, not unpaid new work, free public acquisition or an
automatic state guarantee of every job. Consult workers, price actual learning/
redeployment and accepting care/network successors; no fictitious next job.

**WL19 — Worker exit.** A worker may resign with at most14 calendar days' notice
under this new common maximum, or earlier better terms/agreement. Actual coercion,
serious employer breach or unsafe/impossible notice can justify immediate exit.
No specific performance of personal work, passport restriction or punitive training/
notice debt. Proved lawful actual-loss claims retain B/U notice, defenses and
protected needs; they cannot turn inability to pay into detention or forced work.
Preserve accrued wages/leave, records and safe existing handover duties separately.
Actual separately accepted armed/police command duties retain their own lawful
exit/review rules; WL creates no new forced-service or general command exception.

**WL20 — Restore rather than price a license to dismiss.** Courts can stop
retaliation, restore records/pay and order requested practicable reinstatement/
reengagement or actual compensation after hearing. Do not require unwanted intimate
AS contact or forced labor. No new general numerical compensation ceiling licenses
union/equality violations; actual causation, mitigation, fair defenses and no duplicate
recovery apply. Backpay, future loss, notice/severance and damages are traced by
item/period. Actual jobs/assets/cash may be absent; record the unmet remedy and
use lawful U, wage-protection and source routes, not an invented permanent post.

[DF12](../../C10/12/03-voluntary-service-rights-and-handover.md) now specifies
the proposed military endpoint: ordinary14-day maximum, only separately
accepted lawful armed-command/mission duties up to90days from protected
resignation receipt, stronger earlier terms intact and no unilateral extension.
DF's prospective method date does not extend current notice. It creates no
new police exception, punitive training debt, forced work or detention.

[EB09–EB12](../16/03-overlaps-tax-credits-and-correction.md) preserves full WL
wage/notice/severance rights and source periods. Actual forward notice differs
from severance/old unused-leave cash-out; no fictional wage weeks, erasure of
debt or employer gate. Post-coverage new EB has its own finite buffer/source,
not expanded wage-default guarantee or automatic WL35 subrogation.
