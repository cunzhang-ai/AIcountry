# Design398 decision. Cover necessary executive work without manufacturing a vacancy

## Baseline and selected mechanism

[Design397](predecessor-design-397/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md) distinguishes an
election, investiture, caretaker authority and practical handover.
Government formation mentions incapacity but the trigger, temporary
scope, authority to return and missed deadline under simultaneous
loss are under-specified.

Select [HT24–HT31](../country/C03/19/06-executive-incapacity-and-bounded-succession.md)
under the [working research method](../FRAMEWORK.md). Confirm a short
substitution order before need arises. Separate voluntary planned
cover, verified sudden unavailability and an actual vacancy.
Preserve independent standing duties, ordinary votes of confidence
and original deadlines. End the cover on lawful return or expiry.

## Alternatives and objections

Rely on ordinary Cabinet convention and flexible delegation. This
can be quick in a cooperative government. It is harder to verify
when both the acting claim and the incumbent's ability to respond
are contested. Give all executive powers automatically to a deputy
as another rival. This reduces gaps but creates a large incentive
to declare an incumbent unable and a weak boundary on irreversible
acts. A predesignated acting scope with separated evidence and
independent review supports urgent continuing work with fewer
opportunities to capture the office. A separately elected executive
deputy remains a structural rival. It can add a competing mandate
and changes the parliamentary form.

A majority-confirmed roster may fail in a divided Assembly.
An inaccessible officeholder may also be wrongly treated as unable
to act. Fund independent custody, supported response and ordinary
court and parliamentary paths. Where a vote or determination
fails, report the unavailable function and continue only lawful
standing work. The acting claimant cannot attest their own trigger.
A friendly court or Cabinet can still cooperate in abuse. Preserve
direct challenge, review the actual controllers, require original
evidence and keep ordinary replacement politically available. No
health disclosure, informal party choice or technical recovery
changes the tenure of a lawful officeholder.

## Evidence, transition and next action

[Primary comparators](sources.md) show several ways parliamentary
systems separate a substitute and the holder of office. The
[constructed case](../settlement/executive-incapacity-and-bounded-succession/01-two-signatures-and-a-due-payment.md)
tests a due payment, a reserved deadline, a false trigger, a genuine
conflict and actual receiving. Neither it nor the comparative law
establishes operational independence, costs, legitimacy or outcomes
for this country.

Adopt through the applicable constitutional process and coordinated
implementing law before reliance. Pay for Cabinet and Assembly
records, independent review and service delivery, preserving existing
office terms, mandates, oversight, actual duties and ordinary
judicial votes and limits. No research record confirms an actor.

Next rehearse loss of an officeholder, first alternate and main
record custodian across one actual service deadline. Include
accessible return, opposed evidence, standing finance and independent
relief. Compare protected prior delegation and a different succession
form on the same case. Reopen on unavailable review, accepted expired
instructions, abuse of attestation or better qualified continuity.
