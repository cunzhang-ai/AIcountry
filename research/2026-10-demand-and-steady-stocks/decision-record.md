## Objections retained and limits of the result

- Budget identity is not the goal. Funded pension accrual, retained productive
  profit and well-used debt may improve life; their counterpart and timing
  must be explicit. One-year zero cash does not settle long-run security.
- Closed-legacy contributions pay scheduled 45 with receipts 40/interest 5,
  but accrued `L(t)` is unknown and paired, never erased. No new earned
  accrual is a material rival, not an administrative saving or lawful default.
- Full payout is a voluntary ownership hypothesis after replacement, not a
  national compulsory distribution rule. Opening title/public shares, prior
  capitalization and working cash need lawful actual sources and pricing.
- Some groups lose cash; future pension wealth differs. Low/zero-earner
  preservation against I05 at identical inputs is no Pareto improvement.
  Saving, prices, work/time, informal life and distribution remain open.
- Bank/monetary consolidation removes matching claims for this account;
  it merges neither legal powers nor usable Treasury/monetary FX. A balanced
  bank can be illiquid/insolvent; collateral and facilities are conditional.
- Normal monthly cash passes the declared floor for the credit cases while
  current I05 and half-retained profit fail it despite annual financing.
  A 10% export loss first fails foreign payment in month 14 of repetition;
  the first year's reserve draw cannot be reused. Staff and supplier
  receipt remain separate from financial entries.
- GDP is required final-use value under fixed fictional prices and a legacy
  cost convention, not welfare or a certified 2025 SNA implementation.
  More public cost does not establish service quality or supply productivity.
