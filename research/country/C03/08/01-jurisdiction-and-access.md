# Constitutional review: jurisdiction and access

*Canonical CJ01–CJ02; part of [C03/08](../08-constitutional-review-and-public-control.md).*

**CJ01 — Limited final jurisdiction.** The separate constitutional court decides
nationwide constitutional validity of statutes and general rules, subject to the
closed CJ12 own-court exception, and constitutional
competence disputes between public authorities, and constitutional complaints
from actual rights cases. It decides the constitutional component of national
electoral disputes and C14 amendment procedure/democratic protections under the
previously applicable law. Ordinary electoral facts and counts remain with the
competent election and ordinary judicial routes; no missing evidence permits a
court to invent a winner, appoint a preferred cabinet or renew a mandate.

Ordinary courts retain civil, criminal, administrative and ordinary electoral
cases, statutory interpretation and case-specific rights relief. They can reject
unlawful administrative acts and interpret statutes compatibly with rights.
They refer a necessary, material constitutional-validity question for final
nationwide disposition; referral does not automatically stay the underlying case
or erase their power to preserve an actual claimant's rights. A prior-law ordinary
appellate rotation settles routing disputes without restarting filing dates.
An ordinary case order does not silently become nationwide statutory invalidation.

The court reviews constitutional legality, not whether it prefers a public policy.
It cannot set a tax, appropriate a program, legislate a benefit category, expand
its jurisdiction or treat a treaty as binding without the applicable legal route.
The legislature can change lawful policy prospectively, respecting rights,
completed judgments and C14; judges do not acquire a general merits veto.

**CJ02 — Standing and usable access.** An affected person or representative with
actual authorization may bring a constitutional complaint after an ordinarily
effective remedy, except when that remedy is unavailable, futile or too late to
prevent irreversible harm. An affected organization may represent a defined
shared rights claim with notice, protected dissent and no compulsory waiver.
Public authorities may litigate an actual competence dispute. At least one fifth
of the Assembly's full statutory membership may request abstract review of an
exact published statute or general rule; this does not replace individual access.
A claimant need not persuade Cabinet, an AI or a platform moderator to file.

The first lawful receiving court preserves the date and arranges accepted referral.
Human, paper, oral-assisted and accessible remote routes have equal legal effect
once the claimant's act and meaning are authenticated; an account is not the
source of standing. Public reasons identify jurisdiction and necessary questions.
Counsel, interpretation and protected evidence access follow C08 according to
stakes, complexity and power imbalance. No fee or elaborate dossier bars urgent
liberty or essential protection. The duty panel issues initial routing/hearing directions within thirty elapsed
days, earlier where useful protection requires it. It targets final determination
within 180 days; any extension needs public case-specific reasons, a next date
within ninety days and preserved interim protection within CJ10. Report missed
targets and backlogs to public scrutiny; they never deem a law valid, a petition
approved or a shorter protection clock extended.

[PI14 and the proposed A2 addition](../17/03-public-access-audit-and-effective-remedies.md)
now select a narrow ordinary public-interest legality claim on credible particular
self-benefit facts, not a requirement to prove a separate denied service or gather
one-fifth Assembly support. Actual ordinary-case protection and a necessary
material CJ reference stay within this jurisdiction; the existing one-fifth
abstract route, own-court exception and no automatic stay remain. This proposed
coordinated source requires valid A9/RR adoption/holds, not self-approved standing.
