# C13. A transition deadline with an unfinished grid

*Part of [C13 lived experience](../C13-lived-experience.md); integrated design 55 authored stress, not an observed event.*

An enacted emissions path expects an old high-emission plant to retire.
The replacement generator exists, but a transmission line is delayed; a
hospital and low-income households depend on the current grid. Workers and
a nearby locality depend on plant wages and taxes. The proposed replacement
uses imported minerals whose extraction may harm a foreign community.
An AI forecast reports that the national target remains reachable.

The grid steward reports actual firm supply, reserve and delivery limits,
not only installed generation. C11 preserves original outage, emissions,
land and import observations alongside the forecast. The Cabinet gives the
Assembly complete choices: accelerate a lawful line and interim capacity,
stage lower-demand or backup measures, revise the plant schedule, or propose
an exact prospective change to the statutory path. Each choice displays
emissions, clinical safety, bills and rebates, worker income, local tax,
water, land, foreign harm and cost. Neither the dispatcher nor Cabinet can
silently close the plant, invent an exception, or count an unverified offset.
Ecological and individual rights floors continue to bind every option.

C05 workers bargain over transition conditions and receive funded income,
learning and job-search time; the city needs a law-bound fiscal bridge.
C07 protects essential energy access and clinical continuity. C10 records
foreign sourcing and remedy jurisdiction. Local elected siting follows the
applicable land process; cross-boundary losses return to the Assembly.
Opposition and affected people can challenge optimistic engineering or
misleading incidence; a court reviews an unlawful permit or missed legal
duty but cannot make the grid or set a tax. A missed emissions path is
reported as missed until a lawful correction actually reduces it.

The test fails if a green capital budget is counted as delivered power,
workers are promised nonexistent jobs, household relief arrives after
shutoff, imported damage disappears, or a technical operator gains a
hidden rationing mandate. It tests institutional coherence only; no real
power system or transition result is demonstrated.

[EG's connection and restoration path](../C09/19-electricity-service-and-grid-access.md)
distinguishes the political allocation, actual safe capacity and people's
remedies. Test a delayed reinforcement or feeder failure with household,
water, clinical and public-control needs together. A flexible contract
or transfer to a default supplier cannot repair a failed feeder.

[The two-budget grid case](../../settlement/climate-budget-continuity/02-two-budgets-and-a-grid-delay.md)
tests the actual firm peak, expenditure and headroom together.
A lawful immediate act can preserve service while leaving a
measured overrun and less cumulative space in the next period.
