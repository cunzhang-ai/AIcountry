# National expansion and origin capacity

Design310 · Scoped result for [country completion](../../DESIGN-COMPLETENESS.md)

[LT06–LT10](../../country/C14/03/02-expansion-with-origin-and-receiving-capacity.md)
compares a later cohort, a successful earlier pilot and the services
that supplied its capacity. It protects prior commitments, gives
waiting groups a funded interim choice and retains genuine rivals.

The [care comparison](../../settlement/integrated-first-programme/11-expansion-and-origin-capacity.md)
shows that replication can displace origin duties, and that an
independently staffed route can reduce the waiting area's unmet need.
If the interim route fails after binding, record both the missed
duty and need outside the interim scope. The example is authored.
Actual comparative outcomes, authorization and paid capacity remain open.
