# WL26–WL30: collective action without a minister's strike veto

*Part of [C05/12](../12-work-contracts-and-collective-power.md); design 117 candidate.*

**WL26 — Protected action.** Peaceful collective withdrawal/slowdown over work,
collective economic/social labor policy or a materially connected labor dispute
can be protected, including nonunion organization. Do not label ordinary strikes
a security threat. Ordinary action needs a secret majority-valid-vote mandate
within six months for the still-live dispute and seven calendar days' specific
notice, unless agreed shorter. No turnout threshold or state license. Immediate
good-faith safety refusal/protection from imminent grave workplace danger does
not await this ballot or notice. Ordinary political speech/assembly remains
protected under its own law; this labor immunity does not automatically cover
a purely partisan job stoppage with no labor/social-economic grievance.
Active armed-forces/police coercive duties retain their separately enacted
command/continuity restrictions, independent voice and direct review; this is
no blanket public-worker ban, new conscription or executive strike veto.

Use accessible verifiable ballots with an actual roll/returning officer, protected
plural expression and original reasons, not a union official's claim that an
AI conversation is a vote. Registrar can assist neutrally; no employer custody of
individual choices. Mandate, scope, expiry, safe handover and practical correction
are distinct. Missing authority/meaning needs disclosure, not invented assent.

**WL27 — Essential continuity is task-specific.** Before predictable disputes,
public/service owners fund actual qualified standby and negotiate the narrowly
necessary life/health/safety tasks with workers and affected recipients. Mere
inconvenience, lost revenue, opposition or an entire sector label is no essential
minimum. Councils, hospitals, employers and ministers cannot unilaterally choose
all normal output as a minimum or conscript an individual.

An independent ordinary court may establish a disputed temporary minimum-service
arrangement on proved imminent grave harm, actual tasks/available alternatives,
least restriction, pay/rest/consent and both recipient/worker rights. It concerns
actual willing or validly precommitted lawful provision, not forced personal
labor, an indefinite strike ban or assumed staffing. Urgent B17 hearing/expiry
protections apply; a merits minimum order lasts at most seven calendar days and
renewal needs fresh material evidence and examination of funded alternatives,
never an automated same-reasons reset. Without actual lawful workers/means the
service remains unmet; the order cannot create them. E/UA/LF and actual prior
lawful rescue/handover duties retain their own grounds, not enlarged here.
This design leaves real scarcity/conflicting rights and public source burdens;
no right is declared fulfilled merely because the court balanced it.

**WL28 — No job destruction as strikebreaking.** Do not dismiss, permanently
replace or hire substitute labor solely to defeat protected action. Existing
non-strikers may freely do their lawful jobs; extra work still needs TW consent/
limits. Prior or newly accepted narrowly necessary WL27 safety receiving may
operate within its adjudicated scope, not maintain all output under a new label.
Public/AS receiving duties continue without compulsory relatives or strike service.

Wages for actually unworked strike time need not be paid unless a better law/
contract provides; only proportionate unworked pay, not a fine or deduction of
unrelated accrued dues. Workers ready to perform contracted guaranteed work keep
pay when an employer locks them out for bargaining pressure. Actual unsafe or
unviable operations may close through lawful safety/contract/redundancy routes;
this is no obligation to produce a loss-making good or guaranteed firm survival.
This asymmetric income-risk choice can increase closure/price pressures; bilateral
unpaid lockout is a genuine rejected-default rival, not solved by equal labels.

**WL29 — Protect action, prove separate wrongs.** No criminal punishment for
peaceful protected action, ordinary organizing or technical ballot mistakes alone.
Violence, intimidation, sabotage and intentional harmful acts use actual P/K/B
fault/causation, defenses and independent courts. No collective guilt or union
liability for every member act; its own authorized/knowing wrongful conduct and
causal loss must be proved. An innocent worker is no guarantor of union damages.
Material mandate/notice defects permit proportionate prospective correction and
urgent fair review, not automatic retrospective dismissal/damages for everyone.
A court states exactly which conduct/remedy is lawful; neither executive union
dissolution nor a broad employer injunction is a substitute for proof.

**WL30 — Finite, neutral dispute help.** Publicly funded voluntary mediation
and jointly accepted arbitration can help without delaying direct courts or
manufacturing consent. Recognition, employer liability, protected action, existing
contract rights and legislative policy retain different decision-makers. A
mediator discloses sponsor/framing/conflicts and can be replaced; no settlement
binds an unrepresented person or waives bodily, equality, accrued-pay or individual
rights without its required authority. Agreements need actual source/pay, execution
and safe succession. Unresolved disputes/absent capacity stay visible rather
than being reported as consensus, a completed digital process or superior lives.
