# C08. Access to courts and effective orders

*Part of [C08 everyday law and justice](../C08-everyday-law-and-justice.md); integrated design 62 research, not enacted law.*

## Access to courts and effective orders

A person or small organization can enter through accessible human or remote intake;
the first receiving service preserves the claim date and completes referral only
when another competent body accepts it. Advice, interpretation, accommodation and
civil representation are funded in proportion to stakes, complexity and power
imbalance, not reserved for criminal defendants. Voluntary settlement and
specialist review remain available, but dependence on an employer, landlord or
authority cannot force waiver of protected rights or a private forum. Courts
provide fair hearing, proportionate appeal and temporary relief when delay would
make a later remedy useless, with notice and protection for defendants and third
parties. Repeated harms may support representative claims only under defined
standing and affected-party safeguards.
The [equality route](11-equality-law-and-effective-remedies.md) assigns a
common civil test, protected access to respondent-held evidence and urgent
restoration when an opportunity will vanish before final judgment. Its
independent inquiry service cannot be the final judge of its own case.

Review follows the stakes and reversibility of the act. Prior independent
authorization remains necessary where enacted law requires it for detention,
intrusive surveillance, serious coercion or irreversible harm; silence never
authorizes those acts. A correctable routine service error may receive prompt
internal correction, but a person retains a direct independent route and urgent
interim protection under the applicable law. Several mandatory serial appeals
with no power to change the result are not an effective remedy. Each route must
have actual clerks, counsel, interpreters and time in the common resource account;
an ex-post audit is insufficient when detection or repair cannot work before harm.

The [C06 joint schedule](../C06/18-joint-commitment-and-delivery-schedule.md)
attributes civil, criminal, equality, liberty, campaign and administrative
work to qualified independent roles rather than one repeatable justice
reserve. Concurrent cases, recusal and failed payment or record custody
must be tested with ordinary service shortages; a funded gross envelope
does not establish a timely impartial hearing or executed order.

A person, affected organization or lawfully empowered public challenger can
contest a generally binding administrative rule's enabling power, scrutiny
class, procedure, published text or rights effect through an accessible court
route, including interim suspension when later judgment would be useless.
The court can set aside unlawful effect, protect existing claims and require
a lawful reconsideration; it cannot draft a new tax, benefit category or
safety standard. An individual may also challenge the rule as applied to a
particular case. A cabinet cannot make court review unavailable by calling
the operative rule advice, a form or a model configuration.

A final order names the obligated party, act, date, lawful funding or asset source
and executor; actual receipt is verified. Enforcement against private property
protects subsistence and third-party rights. For an order against government,
treasury and the service authority must distinguish refusal from missing cash or
physical capacity and report incomplete delivery. An independent enforcement route
cannot create a tax or worker, while the person's claim does not disappear in a bad
budget. Pending cases, accrued rights, limitation periods and judgments receive
named successors when laws, local boundaries or providers change.

Court independence includes usable filing, counsel, interpreters, clerks,
case evidence and payment, not only protected judges. The Assembly enacts
ordinary jurisdiction, qualifications and a bounded continuing schedule for
core court, legal-aid, archive and enforcement operations. Treasury pays due
amounts on an audited route that the litigating minister cannot selectively
block; the judiciary cannot appropriate its own unlimited budget. A case is
allocated by a published, auditable rotation or draw after lawful specialization.
Exceptions and recusal have recorded reasons and review. Judgments give reasons
and permit proportionate appeal. Serious judicial misconduct has
a separate evidence and disciplinary process with fair hearing, while suspected
crime uses an investigator and prosecutor outside the implicated court. Neither
cabinet nor a court president may replace a judge in a named case for the
substance of a lawful ruling.

For a public defendant, a judgment identifies the officer and treasury channel
that must act, the due date, the evidence of receipt and the review route.
A lawful appeal is not an automatic stay unless the governing rule or court
provides one. Refusal can trigger only enacted, individually reviewable
consequences; a preauthorized substitute may release records or a due payment
but cannot choose policy, tax or staff in place of elected bodies. Genuine
cash or physical shortage remains a visible unmet judgment and elected
funding question, not compliance by assertion. If the executive, treasury,
records custodian and enforcement staff all refuse, an order alone has not
secured constitutional control. AI may assist translation, search or scheduling
with contestable inputs and human/offline alternatives; it cannot secretly
assign a judge, determine guilt or issue a binding judgment.

[Candidate E13–E15](../C06/19/04-remedy-and-political-continuity.md)
specifies direct urgent consideration, free filing/appeal for this protection,
bounded ex parte hearing time and a preauthorized narrow payment/record
substitute. Shorter liberty limits prevail; serial orders cannot renew a
missed hearing indefinitely. Core duties survive an interim order's lapse.
No unassigned judge, new tax or general policy successor is created.

[B13–B20](13-civil-obligations-and-executed-remedies.md) specifies common
civil intake, evidence, limitation, public challenge, urgent relief, private/
state execution and collective distribution. Broader candidate civil clocks
and substitutes require fresh adoption/review; E13–E15 support is not
transferred. No court order creates a missing tax, worker or paid remedy.

[CJ01–CJ18](../C03/08-constitutional-review-and-public-control.md) now selects
the final constitutional reference and its closed external own-court exception.
Ordinary courts retain case-specific competence and shorter protection clocks;
referral is no automatic stay. [C06/29](../C06/29-constitutional-review-capacity.md)
attributes the added selection, reserve, external review and execution burden.
Neither a split national bench nor a new invoice creates a lawful remedy.
