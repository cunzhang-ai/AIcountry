# Design 95: knowledge rights selected; whole-country completion still unproved

1 November 2026 · Scoped substantive follow-up, not an all-clause certification

[KR01–KR32](../../country/C11/07-knowledge-rights-and-innovation.md) now specifies
ordinary expression/invention, related rights, marks/design/origin, seed and chip
rights, secrets, lawful use/repair/accessibility, AI/human and unfamiliar collective
claims. Paid public access, grant selection/release, essential-use remuneration,
qualified delivery, registry control, civil relief, fine-only commercial K25 and
prospective old-right migration form one canonical regime. C05/C07/C08 refer to it.
It replaces the identified undefined ordinary-IP seam, not existing actual law.

## What is selected and what remains disputed

Limited private/collective rights plus plural public-access grants/procurement
is selected over blanket exclusivity and universal compulsory open production.
The latter remain substantive rivals. Patent20/copyright life-plus50, seed
20/25/half royalty, free uses, international authorized-item exhaustion, twelve-
month release embargo and fine-only K25 are normative reference choices, not
proved optimum or blanket treaty compliance. Rights may discourage entry;
weaker exclusion may reduce investment or increase secrecy. Funders, registries,
intermediaries, courts and protected expert offices can themselves be captured.
No creator, patient, farmer, repairer or foreign supplier actually participated.
Same-controller AI drafting and review is not independent qualified/public review.

## Finite source and delivery conclusions

The [authored reference](../../settlement/knowledge-rights/README.md) tests
complete rights-scope matching, grant/product/royalty distinction, currency
availability, open-code/background/worker dependencies and creator/public receipt.
It does not qualify real prices, resources, lawyers, labs, factories or sources.
A delayed alternative can cost more in its first supply year despite a lower
unit quote; a patent licence does not remove that transition.
ExistingPS/PA/FD/QE hypothetical chains are not newly revalidated by this study.
Full four-frontier sources, workforce/operating and ongoing-pension qualification,
actual law, legitimate authority, safety and received remedies remain unproved.

## Next substantive country decision

A focused read of C02/01,02,05, C11/03 and VD08 finds general privacy, purpose,
minimum data and correction guarantees, plus a reference to an enacted lawful
basis/information-rights supervisor. These do not yet specify a general ordinary
personal-data regime: who can process what on which basis, controller duties,
usable rights/limits, cross-border duties and the supervisor's full control chain.
Sector clinical/child/safety/election/confidentiality rules already exist and
retain their own scope. IP permission, public availability and public funding
do not supply that missing lawful basis or consent.

Next deliver a compact ordinary privacy/personal-data candidate linked to
C02/C08/C11, comparing consent/property markets, purpose-limited public/private
processing and stronger data-minimizing alternatives. Specify actor/scope/bases,
meaningful rights, limits, accountable appointment/funding/information/execution,
failed provider/cross-border transition and actual paid obligations. Do not
create one national personal dossier or a copyright-style right over every fact.
Complete a bounded rule/authority/remedy comparison with objections and source
limits, then recheck the integrated country's highest remaining gap.

The full objective remains better lives, practical democracy, accountable AI,
material/ecological viability and all major national functions. Structural
specification, bounded numerical demonstration, lawful funded readiness and
observed outcomes are separate. No exhaustive prediction/permission for every
private job/good is required, and no checklist proves national superiority.
Reprioritize on real urgent harm, a contradiction or a stronger complete rival.
