# HS01–HS04. Who can change the support a person receives

**HS01 — Assess the person and agree a usable floor.** The local care
authority applies S09's need test and timing to adults receiving ongoing
publicly funded support in their own home. Age, employment and unpaid family
availability do not determine eligibility. Offer a named accountable team,
a named relief route and a reachable contact. The supported plan states the
essential outcomes, services and qualifications, minimum safe frequency and
useful windows, chosen ways of receiving support, and how to get more help.
State a specific task or duration when necessary for safety, assurance or the
person's control. Separate an optional offer, an agreed exchange for one visit
and the person's continuing entitlement. The person may ask for a specified
schedule and may choose or return to AS assistance without a new proof of
unchanged needs. A pension, restricted care budget or record of an offer is
separate from the service delivered.

**HS02 — Give day-to-day discretion to the person and qualified workers.**
Within the plan, the person and team may adjust timing and method, or exchange
compatible activities while preserving protected support. The person may
decline a visit, activity, particular worker contact or technology under
existing rights. Consent concerns the actual change. Silence, a relative's
signature without authority, unavailability and a worker's electronic
check-in cannot supply it. A refused visit does not cancel the next one or
end employment. Use paid supported communication where needed. Keep the
person's preferences usable across spoken, written and future media and
resolve uncertainty before treating an expression as consent. Clinical
treatment and delegation remain under health law and professional competence.
When will is unknown, use the narrow UA route where its own conditions hold.
No authority for restraint, surveillance or forced rehabilitation is created.

**HS03 — Act on new needs before the contract changes.** A worker, recipient,
chosen supporter or receiving service can report a credible material change
directly to the local authority. Where new help cannot safely wait, E09–E12's
earlier useful times and outer limits apply. A named qualified duty officer
can arrange paid provisional cover within the public care duty and approved
source. A provider may activate its already paid and authorized cover.
Both record the need, consent and who accepted the next duty. The statutory
authority starts reassessment from the first protected request or its own
documented discovery of an actionable change, using S09's existing assessment
window for ordinary cases. Referral, transfer or payroll delay preserves the
original time. A short provisional arrangement states its review and actual
next receiver. Expiry of a roster booking alone does not discharge an
outstanding necessary service.

**HS04 — Make reductions a reviewable public act.** An enduring material loss
of support, a worsened safe time window or a move to an unwanted setting
requires a reasoned public determination under applicable law and consent
where required. This covers cumulative small withdrawals with a material
combined effect. The person receives the proposed grounds, accessible
assistance and ordinary or urgent review under AP and B. The provider may
report facts and propose a change. It cannot make the reduction by changing a
roster, plan name or payment band. Maintain the last safe, lawful service
while the public authority decides. Where it cannot safely continue, the
authority arranges a lawful alternate and records what remains undelivered.
Protect the person's right to withdraw consent and any existing narrow
immediate safety action. The remedy must reach the provider and the payer and
be measured through actual delivery.
