# A connected forum and an accountable receiving court

*B41–B42; part of [C08/17](../17-jurisdiction-and-legal-cooperation.md).*

**B41 — Civil jurisdiction requires a connection.** Ordinary courts may hear
a civil claim against a defendant habitually resident here; a legal body
formed here or actually centrally administered here; a claim arising from
its actual local establishment; or substantial material conduct/performance
or direct foreseeable injury here. A valid voluntary forum agreement may
supply a connection within B04/B43 safeguards. Identify actual persons,
activity, assets and legal interests, not a predicted class of wrongdoers.
The precise applicable territorial/public competence still needs lawful founding.

The claimant's nationality, a passing data packet, an agent replica, global
website availability or the place where an AI happened to generate a summary
is insufficient alone. Hosting/training is not automatically the harmful
decision or proof of an operator's responsibility. Domestic direction/control
of activity abroad may establish a material link, but actual attribution,
duty, causation and defenses still require B/P proof. A forum finding is
neither liability nor power over every foreign affiliate or asset.

Foreign residents have equal standing, usable filing, evidence and assistance
under ordinary stakes/power rules; lack of diplomatic reciprocity is no bar
to a person's basic claim. A public act by this country's own authority
remains directly reviewable in its competent public-law court. Contracts
cannot export tax, force, public eligibility or rights review to a vendor.
Actual international privileges/immunities need their distinct judicial
determination; a domestic statute cannot pretend they do not exist.

**B42 — Local venue does not defeat national rights.** Within the country,
allocate the case to the competent subject/territorial court by published
law; choose usable defendant/performance/injury venues proportionately.
Local councils cannot create a rival crime, tax or national status through
venue rules. One accessible receiving court retains lawful triage until
an identified competent successor accepts records, pending dates and orders.
Interim protection does not lapse merely because clerks disagree on venue.

A good-faith request identifying a material claim/known parties through a
competent domestic intake preserves the domestic filing date while allocation
is resolved. It does not manufacture foreign limitation compliance or bind
a court outside the country. Give claimants actual help and reasons; a
jurisdiction objection is heard with respondent/third-party safeguards,
not an unexplained referral loop or inaccessible mandatory AI intake.
Deliberate process abuse needs its own proved lawful response, not closure
because a poor claimant chose the wrong counter.

No custody, seizure, foreign search or general evidence access follows from
receipt alone. Distinct urgent powers retain original grounds/expiry; courts
must preserve actual case history and give usable routes to challenge an
allocation, stay or refusal. A fee/recovery target cannot reward accepting
or rejecting foreign cases. Actual clerks, interpreters, counsel and independent
judges must be costed alongside the existing domestic caseload.
