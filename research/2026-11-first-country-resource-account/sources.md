# Design505 sources and scope

- The pinned [government account and original-table reconciliation](../2026-11-observed-government-baseline/decision-record.md)
  links official Statistics Denmark public accounts and aggregate
  supply and use. [OFF3](https://www.statbank.dk/OFF3) and
  [OFF29](https://www.statbank.dk/OFF29) describe statistical
  government accounts and purposes. The [pinned state-account review](../settlement/observed-government-baseline/01-accounts-transfers-and-work.md)
  keeps the distinct Danish state cash outturn separately sourced.
- [NABB69](https://www.statbank.dk/NABB69) and
  [NABP69](https://www.statbank.dk/NABP69) underlie the
  [matched employment and income account](../settlement/matched-national-work/01-observed-2025-and-boundaries.md).
  The observed totals do not establish qualified provider capacity.
  Work-hour contractions and productivity paths are constructed.
- The [Copenhagen dated source record](../2026-11-first-year-obligations-and-receiving/sources.md)
  and [municipal purpose review](../2026-11-opening-funding-and-delivery/sources.md)
  support distinct amounts and prospective constraints.
  Cash, acceptance and actual receipt remain open.
- The [joint stress comparison](../settlement/national-continuity-and-material-capacity/01-one-calendar-and-two-stress-windows.md)
  and [live commissioning](../settlement/essential-inputs-and-operating-release/02-commissioning-during-ordinary-service.md)
  use compatible modeled limits within their stated scope. No
  constructed cost or physical deadline is an observed Danish result.
  Real lived outcomes require independent eligible-cohort evidence.
