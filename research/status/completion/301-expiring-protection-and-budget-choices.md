# Design393. Make concurrent expiries visible before the decision date

[FB48–FB54](../../country/C06/62/09-expiring-protection-and-budget-choices.md)
requires a common comparison of scheduled expiry, continuing policy
and replacement. Carry original rights and actual receiving costs.
Require a recorded choice on exact terms before relying on new support.

The [two-window comparison](../../settlement/expiring-protection-and-budget-choices/01-two-supports-and-a-late-receipt.md)
tests shared need, initial cash, recurring resources and a failed
alternative. Actual source, affected participation, delivery and
outcomes remain open.
