# C07. Early care: relationships, present staff and paid continuity

3November2026 · Design153 · NC01–NC20 proposed country choice, not enacted law

**Retain optional universal childcare and select small, stable, competently
staffed early-care groups.** For organized publicly funded group care before
age6, select at most3 children per present qualified adult under2,4 at ages2–3,
and6 at ages3–6; normal home groups have at most6/8/12 respectively. Additional
needs can require more staff. These are contestable planning/legal candidates,
not scientifically proved optimal ratios or claims that care is already funded.

S09–S16, AS, I/H/MO/TW/WL and child/civil protections continue. This does not
license private family life, mandate attendance, cover every6–12 arrangement,
create a new regulator/crime, or price all care. Home childminding, individual
assistance and informal voluntary cooperation retain their separate safeguards
and real comparison; funding a group does not make all families employers.

[The living-bundle/capacity comparison](../../settlement/country-frontier/04-living-bundles-and-early-care.md)
preserves UK research prices, already subsidized parental fees and separate
authored wage/roster stresses. It selects no foreign entitlement, adequate B,
actual available worker, complete provider price or national financial reference.
Higher staffing/group costs, sparse-area access, constrained provider variety
and uncertain child outcomes remain material objections. Actual independent
affected review, source, lawful operation and better lives are unverified.
