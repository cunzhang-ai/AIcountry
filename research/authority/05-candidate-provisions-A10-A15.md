# Candidate legal provisions A10–A15

*Part of [Authority and legal transition](../AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION.md); A10 referendum scope and A15 reconciled in design 143, and A10 enforcement neutrality added in design 229; A11–A14 retained with their prior scope; research, not enacted law.*

## Candidate provisions A10–A15

**A10 — Franchise and practical competition.** The founding charter defines
protected national and local franchise and candidacy through a legitimate
public choice. Under the current C02/MB/EL reference, citizens including citizens
abroad, and noncitizens with one continuous habitual year or previously
qualified actual return under [RFV](../country/C02/03/01-resident-franchise-and-secure-status.md),
have national voting and equal candidacy from 18. Local eligibility
separately needs six cumulative national habitual months and an actual local
electoral home; moving municipality does not restart that period. Source rolls
and registration cannot narrow those entitlements. Ordinary Assembly/local called-contest eligibility
at P remains distinct from constitutional/status referendum eligibility on the
actual voting day under A9/RR06/TS11. Annual/private rights and corrections keep
their actual-date rules; a research population or agent account is no electorate.

Every nationally eligible person has one equally counted national vote; each
lawful local electorate retains its distinct equal local ballot. Both retain
practical standing and timely independent relief. Periodic elections on lawfully
fixed dates, lawful opposition, source-grounded counting and peaceful transfer
after defeat are protected functions; a missed resource plan extends no office.
The current **ordinary election-statute reference**
is [EL01–EL24](../country/C03/13-equal-franchise-and-usable-elections.md):
one fixed-size national proportional-list track with optional person preference,
nonparty civic and one-person lists, no artificial percentage threshold, capped
Sainte-Lague allocation and actual nominee capacity. There is no additional
territorial-person track or independent seat outside that allocation. Local
whole-area councils retain their separately fixed charter size and ballot.
This describes the selected statute candidate; it does not constitutionalize a
model's seat number, adopt the statute or prejudge a legitimate different form.

Before a contest, competent founding/predecessor law must fix its actual N,
local charter/map and jurisdiction, roll/entry/count/meaning/vacancy rules,
source/access/appeal calendars and lawful call. The Assembly elected by that
contest cannot retroactively authorize its own ballot. Existing-called polls
retain their actual source law until compatible lawful revision; this candidate
text changes neither the old manuscript nor live software permissions.

Election administration, qualification, funds, records, independent adjudication
and received execution follow [EA/EN/ER/PC and democratic operation](../country/C03/15-democratic-operation-and-public-understanding.md).
An allegation alone does not suspend an opponent. A general AI score cannot
select the field.

Public authorities shall not exercise or withhold enforcement powers to favor
or suppress lawful political participation. Law shall provide independent,
timely review and practical relief against measures threatening substantial
loss of a lawful electoral or elected deliberative opportunity, including
through cumulative action. It shall protect equal accountability, effective
defense, the rights of people harmed and necessary immediate protection.
Political office, candidacy or accusation alone confers no immunity, loss
of eligibility or extension of an elected term. The proposed
[ordinary implementation](../country/C03/22-open-contests-and-neutral-enforcement.md)
defines the review, evidence, execution and resource duties.

Ordinary electoral-form changes follow A9/RR protected
succession; RR's closed restoration exception cannot alter seat conversion,
district boundaries or the procedure approving itself. Failed nominations,
reruns or formation repeats do not consume protected immediate succession.

**A11 — Essential access and ecological continuity.** Every person has the
constitutional protection of a state duty to establish, fund and administer
an enforceable basic, safe essential-access floor without discrimination,
with feasible urgent interim assistance and transparent unmet claims. The
state must also respect enacted ecological duties needed for future life.
For a material hard-to-reverse choice, law must define an affected-scope
trigger, source evidence, feasible alternatives and restoration obligations
before authorization; an appointed advocate cannot replace the elected vote.
Assembly law names covered needs, quality and response
standards, responsible national and local authorities, funding, lawful providers,
interim protection and independent remedies. It must account for actual workers,
cash, land, water, energy, imports and maintenance. The court may order lawful
available protection, reasons, correction and public disclosure of unmet duties;
it cannot legislate a tax or create an unavailable worker or resource. A
shortage leaves claims and accrued obligations visible for an elected funded
decision; it does not certify delivery or compel unpaid family labor.
Missing or disputed identity evidence cannot bar urgent safety, basic education,
essential care or subsistence while a lawful status question is decided. This
provisional access does not itself decide nationality, parentage or the vote.

**A12 — Local democracy and shared duties.** Elected local government has
constitutionally protected existence and an enforceable sphere of effective decision subject
to national rights, shared ecological limits and funded guarantees. A national
restriction on local methods requires express law, proof of a material common
need that equivalent funded local methods cannot meet, and a scoped remedy.
A durable impairment of the protected sphere requires the applicable protected
constitutional decision. Grant, contract, technical and interim measures cannot
produce such an impairment in combination. Assembly statutes specify local
competences, revenue and equalization, shared-area planning, objective
service-failure intervention, affected-party review and return to local control.
No executive, planner or interlocal forum may obtain an unvoted power to tax
or bind others. A material unresolved cross-local allocation returns to the
Assembly unless a legitimately founded elected regional authority has the
defined competence. A transferred duty carries a lawful funding and claims
transition; a boundary or funding change cannot erase pending local obligations.

**A13 — Express change and continuing claims.** A framework statute may
change a future service or institutional method by a prospective Assembly
decision within constitutional rights. A material change states which people,
providers, localities, creditors, workers and ecological duties are affected,
the current and successor obligation, resources, reasons and effective date.
Annual appropriation, procurement, delegated regulation, data classification
and AI output cannot silently repeal statutory entitlements or protected
functions. Existing judgments, accrued benefits, wages, deposits, contracts,
lawful licenses and remedies remain with named successors unless altered by
competent law and fair process. The appointment, funding, information and
enforcement effect of several changes is assessed cumulatively; splitting
one disabling measure across statutes does not evade constitutional review.

**A14 — Evidence without an official truth monopoly.** Competent agencies
retain lawful source records and correction histories for their own acts. A
separately governed public evidence function publishes official statistics,
methods, uncertainty and earlier versions and preserves authenticated public
law and records with proportionate privacy and security limits. The Assembly
enacts its purposes, protected access, appointment, funding and remedies;
neither Cabinet nor an AI provider may suppress an unfavorable lawful release
or convert a changed measurement into new eligibility or a legal decision.
Affected people and opposition can challenge material error or withholding
through an independent route with an executable order. Scientific, community
and media interpretations may disagree; no evidence office adjudicates
political values or holds a sovereign veto.

**A15 — Cultural rights and territorial-status process.** The canonical
[charter provision A15](provisions/04-territorial-status-A15.md) now supplies
protected standing, a qualified negotiation trigger and separate final national/
area consent, linked to C04 TS01–TS16. It remains a candidate, not actual law.

These provisions allocate legal rank and an operating path but do not define
every competence, election formula, fiscal rate, local map, benefit amount,
offense or private right. Their subordinate schedules need exact text,
then-current source comparison, public participation and legitimate review.
A9 cannot bootstrap its own adoption from the proposed new rule; A10–A15 are
additional proposals, not a retroactive amendment to the preserved A1–A9.
