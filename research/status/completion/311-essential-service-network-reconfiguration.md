# Design403. Preserve essential access when service networks change

[SNR01–SNR08](../../country/C04/08/07-essential-service-network-reconfiguration.md)
requires a matched network comparison, dated source, staged receiving and
actual remedy before irreversible loss of local capacity.

[Worked case](../../settlement/essential-service-network-reconfiguration/01-a-hub-and-an-accessible-local-route.md)
tests a retained local route, hub consolidation and a hybrid, with increased
need and a lost slot. Actual staffing, access, financing and outcomes remain open.
