# Design219. Parliament must receive evidence before its choice closes

## Baseline and decision

Design218 is the pinned baseline. A2 protects practical opposition and
judicial enforcement of access. C03 defines agenda and ordinary scrutiny.
DB02–DB03 already supports any member's budget costing and alternatives.
AA and IA have their own evidence routes. The gap is an ordinary member's
path from disputed custody or refusal of material operational evidence
to independent review, actual receipt and a useful opportunity before
a vote. Keep individual remedies and political judgment separate.

Select PE01–PE08 as proposed ordinary national law. Give each member
scoped standing without majority permission, require preservation and
an itemized useful-time response, and authorize bounded confidential
court inspection and execution. An early record response and a timely
agenda item use proposed five-calendar-day boundaries for different acts.
Earlier applicable duties prevail. The Assembly retains lawful postponement,
amendment and voting authority. Unresolved facts stay visible when the
Assembly lawfully acts. A hearing cannot retrospectively supply time
that the decision actually required.

## Observations, alternatives and objections

Canada's protected review shows that legitimate source safety can coexist
with access. The arrangement took time and the review group reflected the
parties that agreed to it. Australia's Senate record shows a resolution and
subsequent official replies that left the contested evidence unresolved.
Neither record proves that the selected alternative would have changed
policy, vote outcomes or remedy for a particular person. The evidence is
scoped to parliamentary mechanisms, rather than all operation of either country.

Keep ordinary cooperative disclosure and audited public records as the
first route. A full-house order with negotiation can resolve difficult
secrecy questions and protect parliamentary independence. It depends on
political willingness and can leave a small minority without a useful
route. A majority-controlled committee or a specialist ombudsman can
screen requests and protect staff time. An extra gate may suppress a
credible challenge and an advisory order can remain unexecuted. Broad
parliamentary compulsion backed by punishment can threaten rights,
source safety and separation from investigation. Select direct scoped
civil access, controlled inspection and actual execution through existing
offices. Preserve usable access for any party and an ordinary court's
protection of sources and third parties.

Review can itself become a delay tactic, expose vulnerable people or
consume scarce judicial time. Use identifiable existing material, safe
access and a bounded pre-vote opportunity rather than an automatic stay.
If a judge, custodian or purported outside executor shares the challenged
controller, use ordinary independent allocation and genuinely separate
custody or disclose failed receiving. A ministerial summary, member vote
or AI interpretation cannot become the underlying evidence by repetition.

## Compatibility, migration and bounded next action

A2 and prior powers remain. Keep DB and DR dates, IA/IL and AA mandates,
WB/PD witness and personal safeguards, B17 rights and ordinary OJ
allocation, and SR's bounded response to proved repeated failure. The
new ordinary Act must expressly assign B19 execution for determined
record duties. It supplies no new search of private data, compelled
private testimony, parliamentary imprisonment or general confidence or
veto power. No legislation, appropriation or final review is adopted by
this record. Drafting and lawful implementation need competent review
of privilege, custody, access and independent judicial scope.

On an authorized start, preserve existing request and claim dates,
valid holds, protected archives, lawful staff and unresolved orders.
Map any contracted official records and independently usable keys
to paid and accepted successors. Revoke obsolete access at lawful handover.
Independent administration needs actual counsel, records time and
ordinary case cover.

Run one authorized protected budget or programme exercise with public
members and qualified counsel, actual source owners, safe witness access
and staff on both control paths. Compare ordinary disclosure, a negotiated
house order and the proposed independent route through hostile withholding
and a genuine safety objection. Test whether an actual member receives
enough evidence to contest the proposal before its vote, and verify
separate individual correction. Reopen for strategic delays, disclosure
harm, records capture or a simpler effective route. Actual affected
participation, independent qualified review, enactment, funding and
observed outcomes are still open.
