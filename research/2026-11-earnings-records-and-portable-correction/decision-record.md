# Design304. Earnings records and portable correction

11 November 2026 · Working selection · No actual enactment

## Baseline and gap

[Design303](baseline.json) preserves accrual on proved covered work,
corrects evidence use and separates wage advances from assistance.
It leaves a practical gap when a work intermediary fails and a
single missing period affects several authorities. A person can win
one correction and still lose cash or accrual because other offices
never receive it, use an incompatible meaning or count a recovery
as another receipt. Apply the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md)
and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md). Codex assisted.
No affected worker or qualified independent reviewer participated.

## Alternatives and selection

- **Use the employer report and separate ordinary appeals.** Cheap
  where records are correct and all routes remain reachable. A closed
  employer, conflicting labels or a missing report can strand claims.
- **Use protected shared proof with separate legal decisions.**
  Select scoped first receipt, assisted reconstruction and verified
  acceptance by each relevant existing decision owner. Keep original
  facts, legal effects, actual cash and remaining claims separate.
  Price the casework and independent paid access as well as the benefit.
- **Use one authoritative labor and benefits ledger or increase the
  uniform basic layer.** A unified authority can reduce some repeated
  proof but concentrates categorization and records and creates a
  common point of failure. A larger basic layer can reduce immediate
  harm and administrative burden but does not settle owed wages,
  accrued pension or each contribution. Keep it as a substantive
  whole-country rival on actual cost, agency and needs.

The shared route can misattribute facts, leak sensitive evidence or
turn an initial administrative assumption into a binding cross-office
classification. Keep a correctable scoped receipt, separate source
custody, actual legal jurisdiction, direct recourse and a person-held
copy. The existing EV response times and earlier source deadlines
apply. Keep paid-only proof distinct from covered but unpaid work.
Avoid requiring the individual to chase public evidence or repay a
withholding the employer failed to remit.

## Continuity, qualification and next test

[Proposed rules](../country/C07/04/07-earnings-records-and-portable-correction.md)
do not change rates, substantive coverage or current authority.
Preserve older periods, legal finality, notice, original rights,
ordinary recovery and protected records. Carry the actual payer and
pending work across employer or public vendor closure. [Primary
sources](sources.md) show existing proof routes and different
procedural scopes. The [worked case](../settlement/earnings-records-and-portable-correction/README.md)
exposes a source error even with the same pension base.

Test one consented missing record with separate work, insurance and
income officers and an independent paper route. Record correction
time, effort, disclosure, active liabilities and actual receipt on
original dates. Reopen after mistaken identity, undercounted work,
shared lockout, payment recovery counted twice or a lower-burden
ordinary method. Costs, authority and actual results remain open.
