# A benefit-debt programme before an ordinary budget vote

This is a constructed test. A scheduled vote at day 12 includes a
discretionary expansion of debt recovery, with ordinary notice already
given under DB. Existing law in the example requires individual evidence
before a debt is determined. Appropriation alone does not change that rule. Ordinary
benefits and valid existing claims continue. One member requests the
effective instruction, the validation report and a bounded batch of 40
decisions used for internal quality review. A supporter sends a
protected account of a disputed recovery through the separate WB route.
The member asks for the facts and an appropriate safe access mode.
No access to claimant identities is needed to explain the budget dispute.

| Time | Authority and action | What can be verified |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 0 | The Assembly Clerk logs the request. The responsible public custodian holds the specified versions and the contractor records under the assumed valid contract. | Original receipt, separately held copy and the custodian's authority |
| Day 5 | The department supplies a favourable summary, claims advice is privileged and says the underlying batch is held by a vendor. The member files for ordinary review and invokes the protected agenda item. | Summary received. Original instruction, scope and actual batch remain unresolved |
| Day 6 | The duty court considers urgent preservation under B17. The respondent has a safe opportunity to contest access and the requested scope. | Specific hold and protected reply. A hold does not deliver scrutiny |
| Days 7–8 | The court confirms the instruction and limited deidentified batch can be inspected under the assumed law. Advice remains protected. A preassigned civil executor can access and deliver the ordered copies with separate credentials after the agency misses the order's due time. | Independent reviewer and member's authorized aide verify readable records. If the executor has the same blocked key, receipt fails |
| Day 10 | A protected pre-vote item reaches the Assembly under its prior agenda rules. Members can question the official summary and distinguish protected information and remaining uncertainty. | Actual opportunity, differing reasons, and a revised or unchanged measure put to the Assembly by its own procedure |
| Day 12 | The Assembly considers the discretionary expansion. The sector and the courts separately address affected debt cases under their own law. | Actual elected disposition and a continuing record of individual claims |

In this exercise, the batch contains 12 decisions based only on averaged
data, although the summary described separate individual evidence across
the batch. The observed discrepancy is 12 of the 40 reviewed decisions.
The batch was selected for internal quality work. It does not establish a
population rate, personal guilt or the amount of a budget correction.
The competent authority reviews affected decisions and uses the applicable
I05, AP, B and EV protection and correction routes.

The exercise assumes a paid duty court, protected counsel and a scheduled
clerk/executor. This scope requires four hours of secure records work
and two hours of qualified interpretation. Both are available in the
right window after continuing case duties. If secure access instead takes six hours, there
are two hours of unmet records work. Added court or interpretation time
cannot supply that skill. If every secure path fails, the pre-vote item
must expose the missing evidence and lawful options. The Assembly can
amend or withhold the optional expansion or choose a lawful deferral.
An absent original supplies no approved expansion or general stay.
Binding payments and review of actual debts continue. If the pre-vote
hearing also fails, preserve the objection and pursue direct correction
under the actual procedural law. An order, debate or budget vote is
separate from actual relief for any household.
