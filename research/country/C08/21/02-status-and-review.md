# Protected status is not a guilt finding or a commander's final decision

*CD03–CD04; part of [C08/21](../21-conflict-detention-and-release.md).*

**CD03 — Prove actual POW qualification and a necessary custody purpose.**
The baseline is the published 1949 GCIII Article 4A categories, not a national
enemy list: actual party armed forces/associated forces, qualifying other
militias/resistance, regular forces of an unrecognized authority, authorized
civilian followers, qualifying merchant/civil-air crews, and the defined
spontaneous defense of nonoccupied territory. Apply each category's complete
actual test, not its shorthand; source authority, facts and any applicable
more-protective treaty/custom rule must be identified. Article 4B neutrality/
former-force cases and retained medical personnel are not silently discarded:
CD05 and this Act's express limits govern their distinct authority.

A current qualification is not criminal guilt, proof of targetability or
permission to hold any associated civilian. State the actual conflict-linked
purpose of preventing return to relevant hostilities/force and why a safer
less restrictive option cannot meet it. A journalist, medic, family, contractor,
algorithmic profile or political opponent cannot be detained to obtain leverage
or information. Benefit of POW protection in genuine status doubt remains
while a competent independent court determines status; it grants no authority
to make doubt last indefinitely. No unsupported 'unlawful combatant' category
removes minimum rights or substitutes for a lawful custody ground.

**CD04 — A civilian judge, defense and fresh necessity.** Immediately explain
actual reasons, available silence/advice and direct liberty challenge. Provide
independent funded counsel/interpreter and a meaningful civilian judicial
hearing as soon as possible, no later than 24 continuous hours from first
restraint. Remote review must genuinely allow private advice, evidence,
injury inquiry and participation; communication failure creates no extension.
The order is no substitute for actually hearing the person. No command,
prosecutor, contractor, treaty citation or AI signs its own liberty approval.

The state bears clear and convincing proof of the conflict, applicable legal
basis, actual qualification and necessary continuing restriction; this is a
national protective standard, not a criminal verdict. If concrete prima facie
status evidence needs resolution, the first judge may order a provisional
hold only to a full status hearing within seven calendar days of first
restraint. Presume protected treatment, disclose material reasons/source
uncertainty and hear the person's case; no decisive unanswerable secret case.
Failure to establish lawful grounds by expiry requires release from that basis.

Thereafter each adult status order lasts at most 30 days on fresh reasons;
child orders use CD06's shorter review. Earlier direct challenge/appeal is
always available on new facts, injury, identity or changed hostilities; no
mandatory sequence of useless military appeals. Keep all actual periods and
concurrent grounds visible. A later order cannot retroactively legalize an
unlawful gap. No missed clock authorizes killing, disappearance, unsafe
abandonment or an invented new hold: fund timely court/receiving, pursue safe
open alternatives and preserve the violation/remedy if that chain fails.
