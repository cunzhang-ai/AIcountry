# EG09–EG12. Reliability where the power is needed

*Part of [ordinary electricity law](../19-electricity-service-and-grid-access.md).*

**EG09 — Pay only for the service a reserve can actually supply.** Test the
relevant time, ramp, voltage, location, transfer limit, usable energy and
recharge before crediting reserve or firm connection capability. Test heat,
cold, flooding, fuel and network failure jointly where dependencies are
shared. Include actual upstream power for pumps, fuel processing and
communications and practical downstream uses by water, care and elections.
Qualification depends on source-specific engineering and demonstrated
operation under its scope. A multi-day outage may exhaust an otherwise
useful battery. A public standby payment buys named readiness and
performance, with correction and lawful consequences for false availability.
Do not count a single fuel path, worker, battery or import allocation twice.
Any retirement of an existing source carries actual replacement, known
remaining risk, permits, ecological obligations and funded repair.

**EG10 — Separate the protective trip from choices about who waits.** A
qualified duty officer can isolate a dangerous component or apply the
necessary protective trip under prior technical law. Prepare lawful
feeder-level curtailment and restoration schedules before an expected
shortage. The Assembly fixes ordinary statutory classes and the extent
of limited intervention. Operators implement physically feasible isolation
and the voluntary contracted reductions within their terms. When these
cannot maintain safe operation, use firm load shedding only under the
specific lawful safety and allocation grounds. Show which loads share a
feeder and cannot actually be separated. Within materially comparable
claims and feasible switches, rotate avoidable outage burdens and account
for accumulated exposure. A protected service's label does not insulate
every unrelated customer on its feeder at others' expense. Plan physically
separate supply or disclose the unavoidable tradeoff and seek elected
correction. No commercial or political preference governs restoration.

Prioritize technically safe restoration and protection under the applicable
law. Record actual interrupted and restored tasks, credible remaining time,
decision holder and accessible alternatives. Maintain useful independent
review, lawful Assembly/opposition access and actual poll and court paths.
PS21–PS32 remains a separate schedule for expressly covered temporary
public backup. CP and RX keep their own powers, clocks and limits. A
grid outage cannot create election authority or establish that a poll,
care task or remedy took place.

**EG11 — Keep independent evidence and a manual control path.** Separate
real-time safety, audit and merits review. Publish safe interruption,
reliability and cost records including cumulative losses by place and
affected classes. Secure protected topology, meter, household and emergency
information. Maintain funded independent communications, local controls,
trained people and a rehearsed route when a common vendor fails. A
validated tool may forecast, detect contradictions or recommend actions
under DP and TS. Automatic protective relays act under established safety
law and certified limits. An AI or vendor cannot set political priorities,
issue a debt cutoff, hide the original record or accept a reserve on
unproved forecasts. Restore human control and verify actual service.

**EG12 — Carry the people, assets and rights into lawful operation.** Before
any proposed prospective start, name actual title, licenses, workers and
skills, records, approved charges, suppliers, maintenance, access promises,
credits, creditors, safety instruments and paid OP/T successors. Preserve
actual due dates, land and collective rights, stronger protections, already
called polls and existing holds. A transfer of seller does not transfer
grid title or establish another jurisdiction's consent. Compare retain/repair,
integrated public service and regulated plural alternatives at the same
capacity, cost, control and transition scope. Reopen after material
forecast or service failure, a captured reviewer, new qualified capacity
or a better rights-compatible arrangement.

[OC09–OC13](../20/01-staged-grid-restoration-and-essential-receipt.md)
adds an approved restart path and a service-by-service handoff after
system loss. Record electrical safety, facility capability and downstream
receiving separately through the next plausible interruption.
