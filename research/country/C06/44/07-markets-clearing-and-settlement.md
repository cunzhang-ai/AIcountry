# NB25–NB28: make trading and settlement answerable without pretending all risk ends

*Part of [C06/44](../44-nonbank-finance-and-client-protection.md); design113 candidate.*

**NB25 — Public offers need an answerable issuer.** Before a public securities
sale give material issuer/controller accounts, uses, related dealings, cashflow/
loss/security/denomination/exit and dilution terms with source uncertainty and
continuing material corrections. Conduct registration checks disclosure/actual
lawful exemption, never endorses value. A private issue to genuinely informed
identified counterparties has proportionate records/anti-fraud duties, not public
advertising by an affiliate loophole. Equal timely material issuer disclosure;
limited confidential business negotiations require recorded legitimate grounds,
necessary delay and prompt release when misleading. Safe disclosure/source
challenge remains PD/B; no supervisor dictates lawful political opinions.

**NB26 — Specify market misconduct, keep punishment separate.** Prohibit actual
knowing/reckless materially false offer/trade information; knowingly trading on,
or tipping for trading, confidential material nonpublic issuer information obtained through a
proved duty breach, knowing that origin; and intentional sham orders/trades or
controlled wash/corner acts intended to create an artificial price/volume or
mislead actual participants. Mere profit, volatility, lawful short selling,
criticism, genuine liquidity provision, journalism or a public-data AI forecast
proves none. Conduct source investigation uses V/PD; correction/restitution need
actual facts. NB32's express closed duty alone permits V/P court punishment;
ordinary fraud/K crimes still require their own elements, no new financial jail.

**NB27 — Venue rules and actual settlement.** Conduct licenses venues/CSDs;
prudential licenses CCP risk with conduct customer rules and monetary authority
only actual settlement-account role. Publish objective access/fee/execution/
conflict/appeal and scoped circuit-breaker criteria; no operator's political
ban or undisclosed order preference. Segregate and daily reconcile actual issuer
and holder units; no securities overdraft or creation by database repair. Linked
asset/cash final transfer occurs only together (delivery/payment or payment/
payment), at a published legally defined finality point; prefinal failed trades
retain collateral/title/due claims. Correct errors under M06/B without unilateral
clawback of an innocent recipient or rewriting foreign finality. Bilateral
settlement remains a real alternative, with its own counterparty cost/risk.

**NB28 — A CCP must pre-fund its own risk rather than nationalize it.** Daily/
intraday current exposure/margin plus independently stressed loss and same-
currency liquidity for the two participant/common-control groups causing the
largest aggregate stress loss. This deliberately uses cover-two for all candidate
CCPs; the official PFMI has scope distinctions, not this universal rule. Credit
loss resources must be actually prefunded and legally available. Separate
liquidity can include genuinely enforceable due-time committed finance, with
currency, haircuts and correlation; anticipated replenishment/FX swap is not
prefunded cash. Default
waterfall: defaulting member's applicable margin and fund share, specified CCP
own paid capital, then mutualized actual fund under prepublished capped member
obligations. Other clients' segregated collateral does not pay someone else's
loss; actual own-customer positions/collateral may be ported only to a willing
qualified receiver with lawful rights. Keep CCP owner/business-runoff resources
separate from clearing collateral. Contractual loss allocation remains bounded/
disclosed; deficits go to NB29 ordinary court resolution, not bank bail-in,
open-ended member levies or a presumed central-bank/public guarantee. Modelled
cover-two cannot prove tail safety, executable default auctions or foreign assent.
