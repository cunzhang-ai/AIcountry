# Candidate Act E01–E04: scope, receipt and standing authority

*Design 57 proposed clauses; [Act entrance](../19-continuity-law-and-delivery-package.md).*

**E01 — Scope and non-expansion.** This Act applies to a due statutory
income claim, an urgent essential clinical or paid-care need, the independent
remedy for those duties, and lawful election/opposition continuity. It shall
not change political membership, create an offense, authorize detention or
forced treatment, alter a vote, waive a private party's defense, or expand
clinical coverage or benefit eligibility by administrative instruction.
The constitution and any more protective applicable law prevail. Research
publication and an automated eligibility output have no legislative force.

**E02 — Receipt and due-time origin.** An authorized local, sector,
professional, telephone, in-person or digital receiver shall acknowledge
the request in a form the person can use, preserve the earliest credible
attempt at that lawful route and identify an accountable officer. Receipt
shall be given during a reachable interaction or immediately upon recovery
of an unavailable route. An outage or incomplete form shall not reset the
date; the person may establish it by credible testimony and available
records. A material disputed date receives independent correction.
The first receiver retains routing responsibility until a competent
authority accepts the matter and gives the person usable contact and next
action. Receipt is not a final entitlement decision or proof of delivery.
The person may approach the sector or court directly without exhausting intake.

**E03 — Authority, support and records.** Every act shall identify its
lawful officer, source power, scope, affected person, due time and review
route. Officers shall assist with relevant evidence and accommodation;
no compulsory common account, AI agent, family dossier, police report or
relative's consent shall be a general entry condition. Specific clinical,
child-protection and evidence laws retain their separate requirements.
Use only necessary data through lawful custody and chosen sharing or an
applicable narrow legal ground. Preserve source, correction and execution
records safely, with purpose/retention limits and protected challenge.
A public breach account shall not disclose private testimony or safe locations.

**E04 — Standing duty and bounded payment power.** The Assembly shall
enact a continuing schedule covering formula-based income payments due,
lawful urgent service commitments, core receiving/clinical/care functions,
competent court/legal-aid/record/execution operations and specified
election/opposition access. Each line shall name its statutory source,
eligibility or task, due rule, responsible payer and cost/allocation rule.
Estimates shall be reconciled annually but an annual line shall not repeal
a standing claim. Spending authority extends only to duties calculated
under the enacted rule and enumerated operating commitments; it is not an
unlimited ministerial reserve. Treasury shall pay authorized due amounts
through an audited route and report cash shortage separately. Taxes,
borrowing, guarantees and a new general priority require competent elected
law; a standing duty neither creates currency/imports nor authorizes credit.
The separate 90-day bridge remains limited to its existing-operation scope.

The enacting schedules must supply existing program parameters and real
office designations. These proposed clauses supply no missing benefit amount,
electorate, health intervention, tax receipt, actual reserve or judge.
