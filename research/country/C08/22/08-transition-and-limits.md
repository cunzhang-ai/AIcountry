# Commencement, missing instruments and a bounded whole-country review

*TS15–TS16; part of [technical safety](../22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md).*

**TS15 — Map duties before a lawful change starts.** Identify each existing
operator, actual jurisdiction, license/standard version, workforce, asset,
source record, pending case, injury, contract, insurance/closure obligation and
receiving service. Name lawful successors, available funds, qualified staff and
real start dates. Preserve actual existing authorizations/rights unless and
until competently amended with due process; research text grants no license,
general amnesty, retrospective duty or collective closure/eviction power.

Apply new punitive predicates prospectively with lawful leniency and no revival
of expired bars. More protective current law and actual international duties
remain until authorized compatible revision. Existing evidence is not a prior
approval of a materially changed product/domain. Old injuries and contamination
claims survive ownership/vendor/territory change, decommissioning and political
replacement; migration and product identifiers must retain usable source links.

A missing engineering/chemical/site annex is a specific unresolved instrument,
not evidence of zero risk, a blank regulatory mandate or permission to punish.
No actual local annex is supplied by this country's hypothetical geography.
For TD12's specified exceptional new activities require a prior particular Act
and qualified regime rather than an official inventing approval conditions.
Ordinary unknown innovations remain under applicable ordinary duties until
competent law changes their classification; not every novelty is forbidden.

**TS16 — The selected model remains contestable.** Compare the baseline's vague
sector promises, private professional/certifier governance, all-public delivery
and the selected plural operators with public duties and separate assessments.
The selection favors entry, accountable expertise and source-based correction;
its staffing, delay, insurance, capture and compliance burden may outweigh benefits.
Public provision or shared external capacity could displace it in a specific field.
No single score or successful file check proves a superior institutional model.

The common statute and twelve domain predicates now have explicit candidate
owners and consequences. It is not a complete engineering code, actual treaty
implementation or proof that every sector can operate here. Actual standards,
exposure evidence, inspector competence, clinical safety and affected participation
remain qualified later work tied to an identified decision, not endless drafting.

Next audit the full national settlement: ordinary freedoms, actual democratic
control, legal compatibility, household/production/service incidence, one material
and cash account, transition and future updates. Select the most consequential
remaining incompatibility rather than automatically extend this schedule. Reopen
on an omitted person/activity, unanswerable standard, fabricated independence,
failed repair/receiving, impossible resource burden or a stronger different model.
Report evidence, research selection, legal authority, readiness and lived results
separately; the complete country-design objective remains active.
