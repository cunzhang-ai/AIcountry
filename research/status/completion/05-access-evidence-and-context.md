## Four independent conclusions

1. **Structural specification:** compatible powers/rights, ownership, ongoing
   claims, lawful budget/forecast/critical-exposure rules and correction for major
   functions. Missing actual territory does not excuse undefined powers.
2. **Bounded quantitative demonstration:** an internally matched example, with
   forecast coverage, omitted tails, distribution, capital/inventory and timing.
   It may test a hypothesis without being a complete or actual national account.
3. **Actual readiness:** lawful authority, sources, safe accepted staff/assets,
   real operating tests, functioning remedies and protected transition.
4. **Observed outcomes:** actual lives, democracy, external/future burdens and
   justified comparisons, including people excluded or harmed.

None implies the next. Full completion/superiority remains unproved. A country
need not list or approve every future private good, occupation or contract.
Representative evidence and bounded forecasts are admissible; important promised
capabilities still need operators, quality/quantity/time, critical inputs,
source/exposure/fallback and adverse scenarios. Undefined residual output is not
free productive capacity. Unfamiliar social forms remain admissible.

## Limits and next bounded action

The [reference](../../settlement/access-evidence/README.md) joins only named
PS/PA foreign quotes and conditional stock classes. It does not close the four
source frontiers. MO's104m/208m ordinary passenger-km cases do not qualify the
old150m bus reference or a single coincident accessible trip.
EV's scheduled data/publication and complaint costs are not free K01 overhead.

Next deliver a compact ordinary intellectual-property/knowledge regime:
compare limited exclusive rights, open/public funding and paid essential-use
access, with creators/workers/users, competition, consent/privacy, external
obligations, accountable decisions and paid transition. Decide what can be
copied/repaired/used, by whom, for how long, with what remedy and actual funding
limits. Keep innovation claims empirical and value choices explicit; a global
IP repeal or procurement licence alone is not a complete answer.
Reprioritize on an actual urgent harm, contradiction or better country alternative.
