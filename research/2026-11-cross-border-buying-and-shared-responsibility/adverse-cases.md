# Adverse cases for the written BC design

Design187 · Authored reasoning, no actual cases, testimony or legal judgments

| Case | Required distinction and written response | What remains unproved |
|---|---|---|
| Safe garment, independent factory uses coercion | Product safety/employment does not settle BC. Establish connected buyer, credible serious risk and actual reasonable response; link alone is no damages | Real buyer knowledge, influence and causation |
| Buyer cuts price and deadline below known safe feasible production | BC06 requires actual term/design/payment change or stopping own harmful act; a supplier code is no cure | Whether this term materially contributed and what safe alternative was feasible |
| Diligent small shop cannot know concealed upstream abuse | BC03 proportionality/foreseeability, no mandatory all-tier census; no strict warranty or automatic damages | Actual reasonable inquiry, new warnings and ability to act |
| Small order split among shell agents | Actual home buyer/controlled delegation stays covered; foreign affiliate needs its own attribution/nexus | Evidence/foreign service and assets, not a universal chain register |
| Home firm moves procurement team offshore | BC01 expressly covers its own controlled delegation wherever performed | Actual home defendant, decision responsibility and foreign execution |
| Host state prohibits independent unions | BC02 protects peaceful organizing despite foreign prohibition; independently proved violence retains actual law | Safe evidence and actual protection against retaliation |
| Commercial child exploitation with no employment contract | Under18 serious floor; Work Standards explicit nonemployee civil duties/qualified expertise; actual child services and crimes separate | Safe child evidence, real child-support/prosecution capacity |
| Claimant cannot travel or obtain buyer contracts | Direct assisted B13/B41 route and targeted B14 records; no visa/account/internal-exhaustion prerequisite | Lawful safe remote participation and usable counsel |
| Buyer destroys ordered records | Reasoned contestable adverse civil inference after unjustified noncompliance, no automatic guilt/all-loss assumption | Actual notice, privilege, other evidence and causal loss |
| Buyer contributed to an indivisible injury | B11 may require full repair, not a convenient buyer-share cap; later contribution between responsible defendants separate | Actual material contribution/indivisibility, recovery and no double payment |
| Serious risk but no actual breach or loss | BC12 evidence grounds separate; BC13 interim threatened-breach/serious-harm necessity, final correction actual breach, damages causal loss | Court capacity and factual thresholds; no automatic purchasing veto |
| Failed audit followed by instant factory exit | No certificate safe harbor/automatic ban; promptly stop own contribution, assess linked-harm response/finite exit, pay actual dues and feasible transition | Worker income, supplier willingness and safe alternative source |
| No leverage, ongoing grave harm and indispensable medicine | Demonstrable feasible response/urgent review cannot become endless engagement; actual essential backup/service claims and elected source persist | Real competing deaths/harms, lawful supply and viable replacement; no BC waiver or invented medicine |
| Grave watershed harm with no individual claimant able to litigate | Designated environmental officers have express court application duty/standing; qualified C04 status, public-buyer conflict route and direct review | Actual independent expertise, paid capacity and foreign restoration |
| Minister's favored public buyer causes harm | Unconflicted public officers/direct ordinary courts/AA public-contract audit; no minister named-case veto | Underfunding/cumulative capture and actual executed remedy |
| New office staffing fails after start | Claims/duties continue, direct court access and funded correction; no serial start reset | Actual substitute expertise/source and priority against domestic caseload |

These cases changed wording on delegation, organizing, nonemployee protection,
explicit public standing and interim relief. They do not prove court success,
burden proportionality, lower harm or overall country completeness. Collective
worker power and structural sourcing alternatives remain capable of displacing
the chosen duty; formal compliance alone is not the intended better life.
