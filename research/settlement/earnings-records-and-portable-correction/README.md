# One missing work record and several different public obligations

*Design304 · Constructed example. Amounts and payment dates are assumptions.*

A person worked through an intermediary that has closed. An ordinary
work decision establishes 120 in covered gross wages for one earlier
period. Sixty were paid in that period. The payer withheld six for
the worker contribution but did not remit it. The remaining 60 in
gross wages are overdue. The worker can substantiate the events from
different sources. No other deductions are assumed in this exercise.

The person also qualifies for an additional Y06 credit for that
period with a target base of 150. The applicable annual ceiling
does not bind. No earlier final care-credit award needs reopening.
Recognize 120 of covered earnings and 30 of additional credit.
The separate public backing due to insurance on the credit is three.
It enters usable restricted insurance cash only upon actual receipt.
A paid-wages-only record would show 60 of covered earnings and
90 of additional credit, booking nine rather than three in backing.
The combined base of 150 would hide the six-unit source error and
the missing wage record.

| Event | Earned or owed | Actual worker cash for this event | Actual insurance cash for this event |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Original paid portion, employer fails to remit | 60 gross paid, six withheld and still owed to insurance | 54 in the earlier period | 0 |
| Correct the covered earnings and additional credit | 120 covered earnings, 30 additional credit, three public backing due, 60 gross wages still overdue | 0 | 0 |
| Day 7, actual qualified WL34 advance of 40 gross on the outstanding wage | 20 of the original gross wage remains owed to the worker, subject to lawful matching | 36 | 4, remitted by the advance payer |
| Day 10, actual public transfer for the additional credit | Three clears the separate backing liability | 0 | 3 |
| Day 20, employer pays the remaining 20 gross and remits its two-unit deduction | No outstanding worker wage on the matched item | 18 | 2 |
| Day 30, employer remits the original six and pays Treasury's lawful matched advance recovery | Original remittance arrears and the recovery clear under their own sources | 0 | 6 from the original deduction |

The advance is assumed eligible, already funded and accepted on day 7.
The older amount was already overdue at the time of the protected
request. Its 40-unit approved cap is an input, not a new WL34 formula.
The ordinary insolvency and wage-recovery rules govern the actual
debtor and priority. Treasury's recovery on day 30 is a separate
public receipt and the 40 already paid cannot reappear as wage cash
or new insurance contributions. If day 20's payment is only promised,
the person still has an outstanding 20 gross and has received no
additional 18. If a pay slip or notice repeats the day 7 receipt,
recognize the same item only once. If there is no funded advance,
do not show the net payment or related remittance as received.

I05/I07 and any applicable earnings-replacement award use their
own periods and purposes. The example reports the cash facts and
no full award calculation. It does not grant any care benefit,
compulsory data link or recovery against the worker. It compares
employer-file-only administration, separate repeated applications,
and a scoped first receipt with shared proof and separate decisions.
