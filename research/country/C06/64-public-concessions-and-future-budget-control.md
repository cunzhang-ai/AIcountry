# C06. Public concessions and future budget control

*Design226 · PL01–PL20 · Proposed public-finance and service rules.*

**A contract can buy a service across several governments. It cannot
buy control of their budgets or their laws.** Compare long-term
concessions with public financing and ordinary procurement on the
same service. Put long commitments and guarantees to an explicit
vote within the competent legislature's authority. Make the party
paid to bear a risk able to absorb the loss. Keep essential delivery
and an affordable exit available if the arrangement fails.

1. [Coverage, choice and elected authority](64/01-coverage-choice-and-authority.md)
   compares delivery and financing and reserves material terms for elected decision.
2. [Pay for service and price the risk](64/02-price-risk-and-evidence.md)
   sets contract, cost, guarantee and public evidence requirements.
3. [Changes, insolvency and handback](64/03-change-insolvency-and-handback.md)
   keeps ordinary governing powers, public services and continuing claims usable.
4. [Material public asset disposal](64/04-public-asset-disposal-and-control.md)
   compares retention and transfer, keeps independent approvals, and funds service before control passes.
