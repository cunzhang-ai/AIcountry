# T18–T22. Choose the first programme when failures overlap

*Design305 · Proposed transition rules and working budget disposition.*

**T18 — Build one schedule for the actual affected duties.** Before
material optional commitments, the responsible finance officers and
service owners put plausible concurrent failures on the same calendar
as continuing duties and the proposed starts. Identify why the events
could overlap, including slow recovery between them. Name the payer,
territory, owner of foreign cash, qualified staff, original deadline,
accepted route and second call. Reconcile evidence and costs by
purpose, controlling source and time. Count the same failure, reserve,
payment and worker once. Separate genuinely independent failures
from overlapping descriptions of one loss. Show which combinations
lack evidence and who can contest the assumed coverage.

**T19 — Reprice continuing duties before selecting new work.** Show
the exact matched options under each failure separately and together
where material. Price ordinary repairs, owed resident and worker
protection, independent review, elections and operational recovery.
Reserve a named use of actual money and staff at each deadline.
Aggregate annual balance does not cover an early cash or skilled
staff gap. Where continuing work is uncovered, report its original
deadline and actual unmet scope. Seek lawful correction with existing
receivers and voted authority. Keep protected elections and remedies
on their own sources. A shortage grants no authority to divert their
staff or waive a protected claim.

**T20 — Use the exact lawful choice on the revised baseline.** Cabinet
presents the authorized narrow alternatives and the services and
opportunities lost under each. National and local elected bodies
decide within their own competence. Require current independent
source and receiving evidence before using an exact contingent
approval. A combined vote grants no authority for a narrower
combination by subtraction. In the constructed reference case,
prefer the separately authorized finite energy bridge where it
can reach households before harmful loss and replenishment is
covered. Retain paid care preparation as a separate vote and a
real alternative where its earlier start matters more. Preserve
the retain-and-repair alternative and show which opportunities
it forgoes. The scenario supplies no general rank between energy
and care, or between existing public and plural providers.

**T21 — Carry obligations already accepted before the new loss.**
An existing lease, due payment or other binding work stays in the
baseline at its lawful date and scope. Price the safe, accepted
substitute, gross cash through each payer and actual receiving
under the original authority. Show which actions are possible if
a transfer arrives late, the worker is unavailable or the receiver
declines. Do not treat an advance in transit as local cash.
A successor receives only the agreed lawful scope. Give affected
people original reasons, notice and timely access to review.
Unmet work survives as a claim. Renew optional commitments
only on their own valid authority and the remaining dated capacity.

**T22 — Retest only after a material change in the underlying facts.**
Reopen when a new due commitment binds, a source, worker or
operator is lost, an independent protection cannot reach its
deadline, or a better lawful route is evidenced. Keep independent
review and affected participation useful in the remaining time.
Verify actual receipt, paid overlap and the next call before
treating the revised programme as operating. AI may reconcile
dependencies, show disputed assumptions and check incompatible
schedules. Responsible officers establish the facts. Elected
bodies decide material choices and qualified operators accept
performance. Preserve this record and original claims on succession.
