# HG01–HG05. One accountable public clinical service, bounded management

Design164 · Part of [clinical service and patient control](../05-health-service-and-patient-control.md)

**HG01 — Select integrated public operation, not a compulsory internal market.**
Name the existing pooled public clinical backbone the National Clinical Service
(NCS), an administrative public service of the state, not a new commercial
corporation, insurer or sovereign tier. Integrate public employment, needs-based
planning and ordinary service budgets; retain separately assigned purchasing/
access, clinical judgment and outside safety/coverage review. Public networks
need no routine internal sale contract before due care. Actual cross-network
resources/acceptance still matter. Qualified public/cooperative/nonprofit/private
additional and accepted external specialist routes remain replaceable under SC/HN;
accreditation creates no right to a contract or unlimited publicly funded choice.
Population-health monitoring/warnings/HP, social care/AS/NC, school/ED and
political/local authority keep their separate powers and sources.

**HG02 — Name the public debtor, employer and executive.** The state remains
the S/HN public clinical-guarantee debtor and selected NCS employer through
national health administration. The elected Assembly sets coverage/source;
the responsible Cabinet health minister directs lawful policy and answers for
execution, not named-patient treatment or opposition retaliation. Cabinet appoints
a human Chief Executive of NCS from C04's open qualified public shortlist, with
relevant service-leadership competence, reasons/conflicts, protected career and
review. The Chief owns the complete lawful access/delivery chain, cannot send
an urgent claimant between internal budgets or make a contractor the only debtor.
This is not automatic state liability for all private provider bank/debt losses.

**HG03 — Clinical networks are operating units with actual heads.** Chief
Executive appoints network service heads after open C04 qualified competition;
network heads appoint actual clinically qualified leads/staff through lawful
merit/professional processes. Headship and authorization to perform a reserved
TD10 act are different; no manager or general clinical title licenses every
specialty. HN assigns a responsible treating clinician/team and accepted handover.
Clinical regions follow usable care routes, not a new elected region or authority
over education/local care/political membership. Existing municipal/other public
employers/owners change only under exact competent paid OP/T succession, not
because a network is relabelled NCS. Ordinary operational reconfiguration within
law needs protected notice/actual continuity, not a new national vote for every
rota; material general coverage/priority remains elected law.

**HG04 — Purchasing and access is an accountable function inside NCS.** Chief
Executive appoints a career Director of Purchasing and Access through open C04
qualified competition, accountable for actual covered-route decisions, SC
contracts/payments, HN travel/direct booking/advance and provider-failure cover.
Named lawful case officers issue individual administrative decisions with
source/reasons/clinical versus coverage/cash/capacity distinctions, assistance
and direct review. Shared support/compatible scoped work is possible, but no
purchasing manager finally reviews its own denial. It cannot set material
coverage secretly, approve market products, issue a clinical licence, override
patient choice or treat internal transfer prices as an eligibility rule.

**HG05 — Replace management lawfully; protect workers and judgments.** Cabinet
can lawfully change the Chief's prospective mandate/operational assignment with
reasons and C04 fair status/review, not partisan purge or a protected clinical
policy veto. Chief/network managers supervise staff under C04/WL/TD, with
specified duties, evidence, reply, relevant professional assessment and appeal.
No political oath, AI performance blacklist, guilt by complaint or professional
immunity. Pay safe temporary reassignment/cover where actual law permits. No
indefinite forced handover/work, strike ban or WL/TW exit/rest exception follows.
Chief/Director/network acting-head assignments expire at most180elapsed days
from original vacancy, no serial reset; earlier stronger limits prevail. Existing
qualified officers' own duties survive without a fictional replacement mandate.
