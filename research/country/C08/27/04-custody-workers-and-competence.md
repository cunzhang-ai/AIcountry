# PO13–PO16. Custody, careers and competent work stay separate

*Part of [PO](../27-civilian-enforcement-and-public-control.md); current P/CD/
HP/UA custody, health, force and actual release boundaries remain unchanged.*

**PO13 — Name the civilian corrections employer.** Existing C08/03's separate
Civilian Corrections Service has an accountable human Director appointed by Cabinet through C04
open job-related competition, qualified public shortlist, reasons/conflicts
and independent ordinary-court status review. This is protected ongoing career
employment, not an added fixed-term political/Assembly police appointment.
Require demonstrated lawful custody/release, humane conditions, workforce,
records and administration competence; rank/party/AI classification is no proxy.
Director appoints qualified regional wardens/officers through the same protected
merit route. Police Chief/DPP cannot appoint or direct its individual release.
Court-defined execution is not discretionary policing; common Cabinet/source
capture remains a real risk under this deliberately simpler appointment model.

**PO14 — Keep actual restraint and release accountable.** Police custody
before lawful transfer remains the named custodian's responsibility. Corrections
executes actual remand/sentence/community orders under their exact limits;
receiving officer accepts person/records, original clock, rights and care.
Warden/court-source records identify actual site/officer and direct counsel/
medical/complaint routes. Missing housing, paperwork, head, transport or clinic
does not extend a custody order or completed sentence. Independent clinical
services retain consent/professional authority; a corrections risk score or
care label supplies no compulsory treatment or new detention. Existing safe
release support remains an actual paid receiving duty, not a second prison term.

**PO15 — Professional careers do not excuse misconduct or a purge.** Chiefs,
directors and supervisors assign/promote/discipline staff under C04/OE/WL
actual competence/duty, accommodation, source, notice/reply and independent
court review. Lawful dissent, protected reporting, inconvenient evidence,
political views and service under a defeated government are protected.
Operational staff must execute lawful general law/duties; poor performance
after support can be corrected with fair proof. Evidenced immediate safety
may need bounded removal from weapons/custody/records under LF13's review,
qualified paid cover and no presumption of guilt. A union or chief cannot
bar outside inquiry; neither does a complaint automatically defeat worker rights.

**PO16 — Fund the actual skills and workload.** Reachable report taking,
protective-order execution, sexual/child/financial/cyber investigation, contrary
evidence, forensic/source custody, interpreters, victim/witness safety, defense,
court attendance, humane custody and reintegration need actual distinct roles,
shifts/travel/rest, preparation/training and independent receiving. A generic
officer/hour or data model is no qualified specialist. Existing laws determine
whether and how private financial/clinical records are accessible. Outsourcing
a technical task supplies no sovereign arrest/detention/force power by contract.
Shared support counts once while necessary independent work/peak capacity stays.

For RL cases, courts determine continued custody, release conditions and
recall. Qualified corrections staff track and execute the original deadlines,
prepare actual support and report new facts. Sworn civilian police execute
the narrowly specified judicial warrants subject to LF. [RL01–RL15](../28-sentence-review-and-release.md)
