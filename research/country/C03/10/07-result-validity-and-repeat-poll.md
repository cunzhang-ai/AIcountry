# ER16–ER17: contest proof, mandate effects and a lawful repeat

*Part of [C03/10](../10-democratic-entry-and-campaign-resources.md); design166 physical-ground revision of101; research, not enacted law.*

**ER16 — Postpoll validity standard.** A result is not invalidated by a technical reporting error, finance allegation, estimated impressions, a wrong prediction or a harmed candidate's assertion that they would have won. On a direct contest petition, an unconflicted ordinary court, with affected contestants/voters and protected original evidence, may order a fresh poll only on **clear and convincing evidence** of either (i) an unlawful access/finance/qualification failure materially depriving electors of a genuine free choice, or (ii) a material physical interruption/loss of necessary original voting/count source, whether or not fault is proved, materially preventing genuine free voting or making reliable lawful ascertainment of actually cast choices impossible; in either case the proof must also establish that the failure (a) could reasonably change the national EL07 capped list/person seat allocation, on concrete margins/reach/exclusion facts rather than a guessed favored winner, or (b) was so widespread that a free and answerable count/choice cannot be ascertained. The court first tests timely pre-vote repair, exact recount/source correction, compensation/restoration and whether unaffected votes/seats are lawfully severable. Intentional excess spending alone may justify injunction/penalty but does not automatically void valid ballots. A court does not award a seat to the petitioner, designate a winner, impose an office-level party ban or certify provisional tallies as a mandate. A necessary constitutional question follows CJ01's own fixed jurisdiction/votes; EA16 ordinary court retains the statutory contest facts.

Design166's added physical ground does not require a sabotage finding and does
not make any outage, missing duplicate, lawful dispatch or technical reporting
error automatically material. Apply protected original proof and the distinct free-choice or actual-source-
ascertainment limb above, plus seat effect or widespread unascertainability and
narrower remedies. Freely cast ballots later irrecoverably lost need not be
falsely described as an unfree casting to meet the physical-source limb.
Lack of fault is no proof a fair election occurred; physical failure is no proof
that a particular candidate won. This new ground applies only to the exact
prospective scope lawfully adopted under RR/ER; protected current mandates and
immediate-succession contests, including litigation/reruns, retain predecessor
grounds unless its competent restoration route independently authorizes change.

After the fixed final close P, a directly affected voter/contestant may seek an
ordinary court's physical-failure determination of the poll before or without
certification. Apply the same strict physical-ground proof/materiality and
narrower remedies, with ER17's first-receipt hearing/merits clocks. Do not require
false certification or a guessed count first. Missing certification alone proves
no failure ground, winner or automatic stay, and creates no invented filing-expiry

is an actual usable certification notice. A final physical-failure judgment of

national or severable scope; only actual existing mandates/government statuses

**ER17 — Finite petition, invalidation effect and lawful repeat poll.** The candidate Act fixes a **30-calendar-day** postcertification result-contest filing period from usable result/source notice. A specifically concealed material original record allows a new claim within 30 calendar days of proven discovery, subject to court-controlled B15 diligence/blocked-access protection and an absolute 180-calendar-day outer limit from certification; already pending timely claims continue. E13/B17 urgent preservation/correction remains available; a petition is not an automatic certification or government-formation stay. On first E02 receipt the court gives exact missing-item help, first useful hearing/status under E13 within 24 elapsed hours, and a reasoned merits disposition within **30 calendar days**, one documented necessary extension at most **30**, without resetting receipt through serial completeness requests. A missed decision leaves an unresolved lawful claim, not automatic invalidity or winner. These rapid clocks need paid judicial/counsel/source capacity and are normative, not guaranteed. Formation may occur before final contest judgment; the prospective invalidation/continuity rule below addresses that risk rather than using a rights-stripping filing cutoff.

If a final ordinary-court judgment under ER16 annuls the **full national** result, the affected new member mandates cease prospectively on that judgment; any cabinet formed on that invalidated investiture becomes the limited **C03/06 caretaker**, carrying due law/services but no new general policy. No predecessor Assembly revives, and judges, commission, caretaker or provisional totals gain no legislative seats or renewed term. If only legally severable votes/seats are annulled, preserve the rest at their actual adjudicated scope; full N/quorum and C03/06 confidence/formation thresholds remain fixed, and any prior investiture dependent on invalidated seats requires a separate reasoned lawful status determination, not an automatic new winner. Completed ordinary laws/payments are not blanket retro-invalidated; specific corruption, rights and reliance claims retain their courts and third-party protection. EA12's unresolved-result route and existing standing election/essential-duty source continue within actual prior law.

The enacted Act fixes the necessary repeat poll at **90 calendar days after final annulment judgment** under ER01’s **early/repeat** call/entry/support/print process: final close P is judgment+90days, use the **same pre-enacted Δ=14day interval**, V=judgment+76days, with the lawful automatic repeat-call trigger on final judgment. No caretaker, commission or judge chooses V after seeing the contestants. Thus60-day minimum preparation and every V-based opportunity offset remain intact. A prospective different interval must be fixed before the original contest and satisfy Δ≤30; a context needing longer is a reason to revise the whole prospective package before adoption, not a clerk’s unilateral postponement. Apply the least geographic/ballot scope compatible with EL07’s whole-constituency proportional allocation, lawful funding and protected original ballots. The court enforces that pre-enacted duty; it does not choose a politically convenient day. There is no separate national territorial seat to repeat. Any legally severable source correction/recount must preserve the whole fixed-N allocation and affected voters; a materially suppressed national list may require a full national poll. Preserve independently severable valid votes/seats and affected-voter hearing; do not treat an integrated count as separable by convenience. No missed deadline extends a caretaker, revives the former Assembly, creates an officeholder or postpones another lawful poll. If no lawful funded receiver exists, disclose constitutional failure and claims; a paper rerun date is not an actual poll.
