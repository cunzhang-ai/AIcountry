# Keep an essential service operating through the renewal date

*Design530 · Constructed procurement and successor-budget comparison.*

An essential home-support agreement serves the same original recipients in
each option. The award is made before the election. Caretaker day 0 begins
before the old form's notice cutoff at day 12. A successor takes office on
day 30. The ordinary contract ends on day 45. A genuinely independent local
service can receive residents on day 90 after lawful procurement, paid
training and accepted live records. Existing care, pay, court and election
duties continue. The ordinary operating cost through day 45 and the protected
judicial and electoral budgets are common to all offers and are funded
separately. Costs and times here are assumptions.

| Award structure | Cost after the common base | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Longer fixed term | 12 units in the first successor fiscal window and 10 in the next, with both binding at the original award | Protects this contracted service under the stated capacity assumptions. Reduces the supplier's exposure to early exit. Forecloses the separately qualified local delivery choice within the fixed term. |
| Affirmative finite option and planned replacement | One unit at the original award to reserve capacity. Four for the day 46–90 bridge and six for paid overlap and the local start in the first window. Ten for the second window. | Preserves the existing service to the accepted cutover. Lets the successor decide before binding the replacement. The continuation notice has a day 40 deadline in the awarded agreement. |
| Fixed expiry and a maintained independent ordinary route | Two units at the original award for readiness, then 11 and 10 | Qualifies only if ordinary capacity actually operates from day 46. Keeps the ability to replace the outgoing provider without its bridge or notice. |

The first and second windows each have 12 eligible units after common
obligations. The earlier authority has two units for readiness. At award,
the respective whole schedules cost 22, 21 and 23, including readiness.
The finite
option leaves two first-window units for another lawful use, relative to the
longer commitment, after spending one earlier on readiness. The reserve
operator retains paid capacity from day 46. The six replacement units include
its own distinct overlap roster and lawful custody of current care records.
At award, prefer the finite option with the viable replacement when the
elected bodies value earlier control and the reported costs and conditions
hold. By day 40 the authorized officer decides whether to exercise that
already awarded option. A longer fixed term can win where it secures a real investment or
continuity that a separately staffed and fully priced alternative cannot offer.

### Test failure on each side of the notice date

If the successor takes office on day 43, a designated officer can have issued
the essential-only notice by day 40 under an express prior-law caretaker
mandate. For this branch assume the independent backup can cover isolated
visits but cannot replace all bridge work by day 46. The officer must verify
that gap, the four-unit source, staffing and the counterparty's usable receipt.
The notice does not authorize any new ordinary term. If the contractor never
receives effective notice and the only receipt is an AI draft marked sent,
the option is unexercised. The isolated-visit backup cannot provide every
due visit from day 46. Use actual existing fallback within its scope.
Keep each uncovered visit on its original deadline and remedy route.

If a late verified debt of three uses the same 12 units, the bridge and local
start together require 13. The independently staffed expiry option needs 14
and the longer term requires 15 in the same window. None can be reported
fully funded. Preserve binding payments, separately price the bridge and
any already lawfully bound work, and return an exact adjusted proposal before
the option expires. A promised saving at the next budget cannot pay today's
overlap. If the bridge ends before an accepted successor exists, record the
unserved duty and use only lawful funded protection. Do not reset the sunset.

For a legacy agreement under which a day 12 omission lawfully binds a two-year
term, urgently review the actual contract, authority and evidence. Compare
serving effective nonrenewal while paying a qualified replacement, negotiating
a consented continuity change, or carrying the existing valid continuation.
The authorized custodian secures the original notice, evidence of service and
expiry under the actual contract. An inventory or pending review does not
change the contractual cutoff unless an operative legal act changes it.
If the term has already bound, the new rule supplies no retrospective release.
Review a disputed default or reliance claim through its own lawful process.

The US Federal Acquisition Regulation gives a bounded comparator for affirmative
notice and option exercise. Its standard service clause limits extensions
under that clause to six months in total. The option-exercise rule requires
available funds and a written determination. It also requires evaluation in
the initial competition and an amount specified or determinable from the
contract to satisfy the competition requirement. This establishes one legal form,
with no basis for importing its duration or inferring that a bridge will
deliver care. [FAR 52.217-8](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.217-8) · [FAR 17.207](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/17.207)
