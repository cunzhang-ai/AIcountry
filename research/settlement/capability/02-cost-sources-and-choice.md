Old capital/training and new facility/instruction uses are separate *declared
reference components*. Actual included/incremental/overlapping allocations
remain unknown. The old-project replacement 5.4 is already a component of
the design-64 public replacement 29.4: attribute it once, never add this
programme to that whole national budget again. No current actual duty is
priced or financed by this table. Imported inputs are components of bills,
not an extra national expense; real FX paths remain unverified.

Adding only eight years of eventual reserve salary 21.45 to the old prior
source gives 468. That is short of bills by 57.725, or 67.725 to retain the
buffer. The model exposes an unallocated financing gap; it neither selects
which person's wage is unpaid nor records negative balances as actual cash.
Full prior grant/appropriation is hypothetical, not a verified source. Debt
would add lender, service, currency and maturity obligations not priced here.

With a two-year clinical delay, compare ten years so every track receives
two post-completion operating years. Bills become 657.225 and prior source
667.225; normal prior funding is short by 131.5 including buffer. Other
operating staff, maintenance, replacements, training and bridges continue.
The target first fits in year 9 rather than 7; a cheaper year-8 unfinished
snapshot cannot establish the same capability. The model does not price an
instruction-loss/unplaced-learner repair or certify physical/foreign supply.

Known continuing programme components after training sum to 60.5 annually,
including attributed old-project replacement. This excludes baseline public
services/replacement, recurring turnover training, pensions, household
support, other duties, debt and shocks. Those are unknown rather than zero.
The old operating reference excludes the new seven-unit facility stock,
maintenance and wear; do not reuse its GDP/finance result for this changed
state without a fresh whole-account comparison.
