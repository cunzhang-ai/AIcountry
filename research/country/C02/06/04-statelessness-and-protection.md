# Belonging: statelessness and actually unsafe return

*Canonical MB09–MB10; part of [C02/06](../06-citizenship-and-secure-belonging.md).*

**MB09 — Shared inquiry, not proof of a universal negative.** A stateless person
is someone no state considers its national under the operation of its law.
The public status service and applicant share inquiry into states with actual
links—birth, legal parentage, residence or former nationality—not every country.
Read relevant law and actual operation, independently checked evidence and
credible testimony. Applicants supply truthful reasonably available accounts;
authorities gather their accessible sources. Determine statelessness to a
reasonable degree given proof difficulties, not a universal-negative certainty.
Deliberate material withholding may support a reasoned denial with review, not
a fictitious nationality. Give genuine evidential doubt appropriate weight; missing
papers alone prove neither statelessness nor foreign citizenship. Record which
links remain unsupported rather than fabricate an answer or erase a concept.

Nationality-undetermined is a protected processing description, not another
permanent caste. Determine promptly under MB15; allow direct merits review.
Separately assess refugee/non-return risk before contacting a possibly persecuting
state. No unsafe consular contact, surrender of private testimony or impossible
foreign renunciation certificate is mandatory. Recognition is this country's
lawful finding, not a power to order foreign officials to recognize or admit.

A recognized stateless adult under this country's jurisdiction immediately obtains
MB04 secure residence/work, including MB13 non-expulsion and return rights, plus
usable identity/travel documentation, a safe actual
receiver and ordinary-resident protection on arrival here. After two habitual
years, citizenship is available by supported voluntary application. Stateless
children habitually here have MB06's immediate acquisition rule. In-situ people
wrongly excluded from their own country's citizenship receive prior recognition/
restoration assessment, not automatic treatment as removable foreigners.

Later actual foreign nationality changes the statelessness finding and relevant
future application ground, not already vested secure residence/citizenship or
previous lawful claims. Treaty-benefit exclusions or criminal allegations do not
create a foreign nationality or erase a factual stateless finding; these broader
national protections are expressly selected. Provisional identity/care continue.

**MB10 — Protection is not determined by the nationality label.** A person with
a nominal nationality but no safe usable protection may be unable to return;
a stateless finding does not replace the independent non-return assessment.
Existing C10 bars prohibited return under actual applicable law and rights.
Pending claims have express provisional stay/work and humane reception under
this proposed statute; they are not powers derived from E01 or an income claim.

If no lawful actually accepting safe destination exists, no removal may occur.
Grant renewable protected stay/work while that fact persists, with at least
annual individual reassessment and ordinary appeal; official delay continues
protection rather than making a person undocumented. Review new adverse evidence
without restarting habitual residence. This does not presume statelessness,
citizenship or an immediate vote. MB04 governs four-year secure residence.
[RFV](../03/01-resident-franchise-and-secure-status.md) governs electoral residence separately.
The country funds its duties while transparently seeking safe lawful cooperation;
it cannot discharge them through a carrier contract or fictitious destination.
