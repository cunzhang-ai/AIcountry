# Design385 decision. Protect children's time through funded adult care

## Baseline and selection

Design384 protects any voluntary helper's right to stop under HS
and already provides ED adjustments and separate B31 child protection.
The gap is a direct child route when essential tasks are quietly
assigned to a child or school adjustment leaves the burden in place.
The service recipient's consent and the child's choice both matter.

Select [YCR01–YCR07](../country/C07/07/01-young-carers-and-funded-family-support.md).
Use confidential entry, named paid replacement and distinct learning
recovery, with the earliest source duty continuing. No claimed family
agreement can make a child the essential reserve. Safe chosen help
can remain. Give actual review and a paid handover where a child
turns 18, moves or changes school.

## Alternatives and objections

Retain generic family support with flexible schooling. This is
simpler and may help in voluntary arrangements, but can normalize
work that prevents ordinary learning, rest and relationships.
A family cash benefit may ease hardship but risks paying for
dependence without providing a suitable worker. Prefer separate
needs-based resources and actual replacement for essential tasks.
A broad ban on ordinary help would intrude on family relationships
and suppress the child's own choices. Select a scoped public duty
where material dependence or burden is at issue.

Targeted support can expose family privacy or stigmatize disability.
Use direct safe contact, separate consent and minimal records.
An adult can decline a specific provider, with a supported alternative
sought and any shortfall kept visible. Safeguarding remains
available under distinct grounds. Paid shifts and school recovery
cost real resources. When those are missing, record unmet needs
and lawful priority rather than putting the work on another child.

## Evidence, transition and next action

[Original sources](sources.md) distinguish reported unpaid help and
an enacted assessment right. The [constructed comparison](../settlement/young-carers-and-funded-family-support/01-before-school-and-after-bedtime.md)
tests separate funds and timetables. Neither families nor independent
local reviewers have qualified this proposal.

On prospective adoption, name the care owner, clinical and school
receivers, local and national payer, confidential assistance, and
independent review. Retain ED, HS, B31, AP and ongoing claims, consent
and clocks. Reconcile existing care and education budgets without
duplicate funding. No new power to remove a child or compel an adult
arises from the research.

Next test one local roster and two successive service windows using
safe accounts from children and recipients separately. Include a
declined provider, an inaccessible contact and an overlapping urgent
call. Check adult care receipt, school recovery and actual time.
Reopen on stigmatization, displaced work, a missed duty or an equally
protective simpler route. Actual authority, readiness and outcomes
remain unverified.
