# RX05–RX08. A declaration has bounded effects and a real end

*Design144 · [Safety and emergency continuity](../01-public-safety-and-emergencies.md) · Numerical hypotheses, not adopted clocks.*

**RX05 — A source-backed declaration, not a blank power.** A prior Assembly Act
must enumerate declaration competence, observable grave threat to life or essential
systems, why ordinary operation is inadequate, territory, exact temporary tasks,
resources and controls. Cabinet records an actual collective decision, publishes
and notifies Assembly/opposition and competent review receivers immediately and
within24 elapsed hours, earlier when useful protection requires it. An unavailable
platform requires an authenticated accessible alternate publication/notice route;
a secret/undelivered directive cannot impose an unknown adverse norm. Record actual
first effect, source/time and uncertainty; late registration does not move a clock.
Publication failure remains reviewable, not permission to suppress evidence.

Declaration can activate only explicitly preassigned civilian coordination,
qualified mutual aid/reserves and temporary administrative tasks under that Act.
It creates no new arrest, isolation, search, interception, medical treatment,
force, tax, taking, appropriation or treaty power. A specific sector/DR source
must independently authorize each measure and may end earlier. Named local civilian
leads retain rescue/shelter/transport/communications tasks; health professionals
retain clinical decisions, licensed operators technical safety. Intelligence
assessment has no domestic public-order command; military assistance needs its
separate civilian mandate. A new material cross-system priority returns to elected
law under C06 scarcity, not a Cabinet prediction or one pooled AI command.

**RX06 — Precisely bound declared temporary authority.** Initial declared authority
ends at the earlier actual stated expiry or **336 elapsed hours** from its first
related effect (14×24). Each continuation requires **ceil(3N/5) actual assents of
the positive full statutory Assembly N, including vacancies**, public necessary-work/source reasons and an exact
prospective expiry before the current authority lapses. Each lasts no more than
720 elapsed hours (30×24), and may not exceed **2160 aggregate operative hours**
(90×24) for the same emergency and materially continuing measures. Need ending
sooner ends the relevant authority. No vote, empty/vacant seats, notice, committee
silence or model agreement counts as approval. A late vote cannot legalize a gap.
After expiry without timely renewal, any continuing measure needs independently
valid ordinary law or new valid primary legislation. The same emergency gets no
second Cabinet initial period, and no Act resets an exhausted declaration budget.
First effect here is the actual declared-authority source, not an unrelated earlier
ordinary clinical/rescue act; its initial336-hour wall-clock does not pause in gaps.

**RX07 — Count the effects, not new names.** Maintain an inspectable union of actual
operative intervals for the same emergency/material continuation across ministers,
places, statutes, vendors and successor labels. Simultaneous hours count once;
a genuine gap gives no power and does not erase accrued hours. Record actual
source/action, affected scope, duty holder and expiry; disclose disputed linkage
for independent review. A distinct later threat does not refresh measures that
continue the earlier restricted activity. Classification follows facts/effects,
not an AI identity score; ambiguity cannot silently certify fresh authority.
Ordinary court/CJ jurisdiction stays distinct. Registration is evidence, not a
new approving office. Publish privacy-safe reasons and retain safe source history.

**RX08 — Ordinary legislation is no escape from rights or shorter clocks.** After
the declared90-day ceiling the same temporary declaration powers lapse. Continuing
rights-compatible measures need actual ordinary law through the valid legislative
route; no renewal resolution converts them into an Act.
The Assembly may enact new prospective rights-compatible primary law before or
during an emergency; it need not wait90days. Novel threats/technology are not
excluded from democratic legislation, but neither novelty nor urgency supplies
unwritten competence. RX does not freeze ordinary tax or future service policy
when the proper Assembly law changes it within A9/A13 and actual source rights. A primary statute still
cannot derogate from rights, extend office or give retrospective immunity.
Existing independently valid ordinary rescue/service duties remain on their own
source, not as a revived expired declaration.

DR13's urgent general rule retains the **earlier of its source expiry and start
of calendar day7 from first-effect day0**; a declaration lasting14elapsed days
cannot extend it. Normal consultation/publication/affirmation and actual making
remain necessary. An Assembly emergency-renewal vote is neither DR10 instrument
approval nor a primary Act. Individual P/UA/health/LF orders retain their separate
shorter grounds, consent, expiry and review; clocks are not exchangeable.

For newly adopted broad hazard measures that materially restrict movement,
assembly or ordinary public participation across a locality/general population,
source law must set an operative sunset **no later than90 calendar days from first
related general effect** and reassessment at least every7calendar days, earlier
on material change. Continuing that general restriction needs fresh primary
legislation, public right-specific reasons and preserved independent relief; no
serial executive renewal or blanket readiness extension. This is a prospective
ordinary-law selection, not an enabling power or a sunset on the underlying
service Act, individual clinical care or a specific technical safety duty.
The source Act fixes its governing calendar before effect; the first general-
effect date is day0, with this segment ending at the start of day90 at latest.
Only a valid fresh primary Act can authorize a new prospective segment of at
most90calendar days from its stated commencement, retaining the whole cumulative
history and existing rights; no executive reissue starts that segment. A genuine
gap confers no power. Missed weekly reassessment is independently challengeable
and supplies no evidence of present necessity or a restarted sunset.
Materially continuing restrictions count despite a changed title; actual effect
and existing source protections govern disputed classifications. Repeated lawful
legislation may still sustain a harmful restriction: expiry is no proof of justice.
