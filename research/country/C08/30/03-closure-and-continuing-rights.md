# SR09–SR12. Keep oversight useful and let it end

*Part of [public remedies](../30-repeated-public-failure-and-remedy.md).*

**SR09 — Revisit the first threatened delivery.** Require prompt notice
of a material delay, source loss, missing recipient or failed
handoff when it becomes known. The person can return through the
existing case's execution route or make an independent urgent claim.
No repetition of already established merits is required for the
same order. An order's special supervisory measures last at most
90 calendar days from the operative order. A further period of at most
90 days requires a
fresh reasoned judicial decision before expiry, after a meaningful
chance to be heard and proof that the measures are still necessary
and the least intrusive effective route. Disclose cumulative duration.
A lapse ends the special measures and preserves underlying orders,
due claims and ordinary enforcement. Shorter hearing, liberty and
protection deadlines still govern.

**SR10 — Close oversight on demonstrated receipt and a working next cycle.**
Require evidence that the identified unlawful cause has been corrected
and the required service or payment has actually reached its defined
scope, or an independently justified narrowing for explicitly resolved
parts. Verify the next ordinary delivery cycle's real source, staff,
records, accepting operators and complaint route where recurrence is
plausible. Give affected people a usable opportunity to contest reported
delivery. Preserve missed deadlines, late receipt and open individual
claims. No forecast, successful pilot, high percentage or payment total
by itself closes a whole repair. Publish safe reasons and record an
ordinary enforcement and reopening route. Unproved completion remains
unproved even if the exceptional order expires.

**SR11 — Keep an accountable account without a universal case dossier.**
Use protected originals and limited case links to reconcile due items,
partial receipt, late service, repeats and lawful exceptions. Publish
safe scope, denominators, unresolved cases, cash and physical gaps,
method and review. Suppress identifying details rather than suppressing
the finding. AI may aid translation, check dates, identify conflicting
ledgers or flag possible recurring causes in a bounded reviewed task.
Retain actual evidence and allow direct human challenge. A model
cannot close an order, decide personal fault, assign legal eligibility
or create a central claimant register.

**SR12 — Pay for execution and keep lawful successors.** DB/FB and
the standing law fund attributable clerk, counsel, verifier, Treasury,
sector, safe evidence and ordinary enforcement capacity. Include
shared staffing and costs once. Existing B19/E15 sources and ceilings
apply only to authorized scope. Keep OP/T successors for pending
judgments, protected evidence, staff, payments, contracts and provider
receiving. Actual authority, appropriate source and independent review
are adoption requirements, not facts supplied by this design. Reopen
on repeated unserved claims, inaccurate reporting, capture or a less
intrusive funded route that protects the same rights.

[SR28–SR32](07-remedy-after-lost-service-time.md) addresses proved remaining
loss when the service has been repaired but the original deadline has passed.
