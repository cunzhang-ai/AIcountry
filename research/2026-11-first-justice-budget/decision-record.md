# Design500 decision record

**Basis and question.** Continue the [Design499 baseline](baseline.json)
under the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md). Decide which first-budget
design can maintain survivor protection, effective defense and actual
public-order execution through a common access and staffing failure.

**Choice.** Finance ordinary capacities with scoped independent routes
where the accountable ordinary path fails. Reuse compatible sequential
work where confidentiality, funding and deadlines permit. Test a first
and second call without sharing one person's time across simultaneous
duties. An independent release route and survivor support survive loss
of criminal proceedings and each other's providers. The linked
[justice budget](../settlement/first-justice-budget/01-three-duties-one-first-budget.md)
records the rights, comparison and source boundary.

**Material objections and dependencies.** Small localities may lack
independent staff, suitable accommodation or original evidence.
Independent regional pooling can preserve capacity with funded and
accepted coverage, while separate local routes may be easier to enforce
through the next failure. Common remote services can reduce travel but
cannot be the only channel on which counsel, a protected location and
an executed order depend. Parallel review may share sources and confers
no affected participation or independent final authority.

**Compatibility and reopening.** Carry earlier receipt dates, urgent
routes, legal scope, paid judgments and safe independent sources.
A changed channel does not alter liberty grounds or authorize an
extension. A future adopter needs exact legal roles, qualified local
and regional staffing, payroll, independent authentication, location
protection and actual recipient acceptance. Reopen on a failed second
call, conflicting representation, unsafe disclosure or a verified
simpler route with the same protection. Actual authority, operational
readiness and outcomes remain open. [Sources](sources.md)
