# FT01–FT04. Allocate the duty and pay for the change

**FT01 — Specify the task and its authority before moving it.** A competent
elected authority specifies each material public function, affected territory
and users, exact legal commencement, continuing standard and accountable
owner. Separately name who sets standards, funds, decides individual
eligibility, employs, operates, holds assets and records, reviews and executes
remedies. Identify closely related retained functions and their dependency.
Require the applicable Assembly, local, territorial and contractual approvals
for each assignment. An operating agreement can delegate only functions
already lawfully delegable. It leaves the statutory owner responsible and
cannot create coercive, taxing, electoral or professional powers. A group of
public operators is not a new government.

**FT02 — Choose the operating route separately.** Compare retaining lawful
current delivery, a paid agreement with the outgoing body or another qualified
operator, and direct successor operation. Consider accountability, access,
relationship continuity, displaced capacity, expertise and alternatives
available to users and workers. A retained operator needs accepted scope,
authorizations, standards, payment, named live receivers and an enforceable
exit. Any legally authorized obligatory interim operation needs separate
defined scope, funds and exact end under the adopting law. It does not
confer general control or indefinite unpaid service. Use existing oversight,
procurement and safe choice rules. Keep actual constitutional and territorial
limits on who can change each assignment.

**FT03 — Reconcile both sides of the fiscal change.** At the adopting budget
and through actual handover, reconcile the old resource, the receiving budget,
recurring operating cost and separate dated transition expense. Identify
retained services, shared sites, stranded overhead, funded support, records,
asset condition, wages, pensions, pay dates and contracts. Allocate shared
cost and capacity using documented use and legal obligations rather than a
percentage of the headline grant. Include real paid overlap and backup.
Eliminate internal payments only at the joined reporting boundary. Show
each body's own authority, gross commitments, usable cash and who bears a
residual gap. Neither a national aggregate-neutral arrangement nor a reduction
in the outgoing budget proves that every receiving or retained service is
funded. A dated transfer cannot replace a worker, accepted facility or supply.

**FT04 — Move only lawful and usable assets, duties and work.** Assign title,
use, debt and maintenance for shared and dedicated premises, equipment and
inventories. Keep rights, contracts and accrued claims under their own law.
Identify affected jobs and workers' lawful choices, actual accepting employer,
pay, agreements, accrued rights, required qualifications and any needed paid
overlap or replacement. Give timely individual notice, assistance and review.
A worker, site or supplier counted for a retained service cannot also cover
an incompatible new commitment. Separate legal staff transfer, attendance,
operational credentials and actual capacity. Procure lawful bridge capacity
or report the gap. No forced or unpaid work is implied by service continuity.
