# Design404 decision. Examine loss of independent choices across successive acquisitions

## Baseline and public purpose

This AI-assisted research decision uses [Design403](predecessor-design-403/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md),
the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).
MP12 already combines steps of one binding arrangement and gives each
genuinely new deal its own gate. MP16 allows a specific below-gate inquiry.
Separate small control transactions can remove practical choices before a
worker, customer or enforcement office can detect the pattern. The decision
affects users, workers, small business owners seeking an exit, investors and
independent operators.

## Rival rules and retained disagreement

Retain existing deal-by-deal filing and improve targeted MP16 casework.
That avoids new routine filings but places early discovery on an office or
complainants who may lack the ownership evidence. A general mandatory notice
for every deal helps detection but delays small transfers and expands public
collection regardless of actual overlap. A market-wide predesignation could
reach small local groups, with boundary and discretionary control costs.
Select an added numerical cumulative gate for large acquiring groups and
related domestic activities, preserving individual MP16 on its own evidence
and clock. Retain repair, voluntary succession, independent purchase and
proportionate remedies in merits comparisons.

The proposed 36 months and inherited 10,000T/500T are research thresholds.
They can miss slow consolidation, low-turnover local buyers and acquisitions
of emerging entrants. Redefining a labor or service area may also change the
burden and selection. The law should rely on contestable actual overlap and
qualified sources, and adjust only prospectively after observing missed harms
and filing costs. No independent review or actual affected-party participation
was obtained.

## Decision control, legal compatibility and remedy

The Assembly sets and funds the rule. Existing MA career investigators and
deciders retain separate roles. The chief cannot issue a structural order.
Ordinary independent courts decide contested holds and prohibitions and
may order execution within their own law. Affected groups can supply
protected evidence, object to an omission and pursue lawful relief.
Review capacity and access to original ownership, activity and labor
evidence must be funded separately from expected penalties. Political
and provider succession retain the original case, deadlines and rights.

A new transaction can be reportable based on a defined prior series.
Earlier valid clearances, lawful closings and original challenge windows
remain effective. Related simultaneous deals share evidence within their
own clocks. No new criminal or regulatory penalty follows by implication.
For any remedy, preserve qualified services, wages, pensions, accrued
claims, client assets, creditors and real independent receiving. A substitute
operator or public provision needs its own authority and funding. AI may
reconcile source records and expose repeated counting but cannot adjudicate.

series in a geographically limited service or labor market. Compare original
