# AH13–AH16. A bounded public service must have an actual source

*Part of [person-directed assistance](../07-person-directed-public-assistance.md).*

**AH13 — Count the person-side task and every displaced hour.** National G/need-
adjusted grants, competent local budgets and actual public/provider contracts
fund the core, external conflict receiving, specialists within their own law
and paid source/review/continuity. No new residual tax, automatic recovery quota,
student debt, data sale or expected AI dividend pays it. B13/AP/DP help already
included is attributed once; AH's additional independent scope is identified,
not silently added to its own baseline or called a free existing staff member.

Cost actual episode duration/complexity, peak urgency, adviser source checking,
client/private-record work, translation/disability/care/travel, supervising and
qualified specialists, realistic handoffs/rework/non-completion, independent
custody/review/execution, compute/energy/hosting, capital/upkeep, vendor exit and
worker/contract/legacy/foreign transitions. Paid human alternatives need actual
qualified simultaneous capacity, not projected savings from the AI they replace.
Cases closed, chatbot responses and cheaper contact channels are not received
help. The functional census remains open; old D/L/digital bids price no full AH.

**AH14 — Pay the duty without funding the opponent's veto.** Fund standing
qualified availability and actual work, including an outside route whose source
cannot be blocked for the challenged named case. Source owners retain lawful
budget/audit and fair procurement/worker remedies, not power to read private
advice or direct its conclusion. National transfers/internal reimbursements are
not new national revenue. Competent coordinated law expressly includes established
AH public-dues/state orders in adopted B19 source/narrow execution scope; no
unlimited Treasury guarantee of all private advice losses or new bailiff tax.
Actual old claims, restricted source/creditor ranks and stronger law persist.

**AH15 — Prospective additions and safe learning.** Begin new AH delivery/receipt/
source duties first1January at least12calendar months after coordinated AH/AP/B/
DP/local/source law with actual coverage, authorised receiving acts, employers,
qualified provider/outside/source officers and paid OP/T overlap. Old AP/B13/C07/
DP/professional assistance and earlier safe dates do not wait. Existing pending
requests/cases, legal privilege/privacy, worker/pension/contract/creditor/custody/
record/foreign obligations and actual constitution/software/permissions/holds
need their lawful successor; no mass reappointment, global case migration or clock reset.

Compare actual usable help, understanding, accepted action, errors/dates,
retained agency, exclusions, source cost, worker/client time and conflict/exit.
GDS's selected pilot report is provider-reported usability/answer evaluation,
not causal realised benefit delivery or an independent national saving. Citizens
Advice's service description illustrates another delivery form, not proof of
independence/funding or transferable return estimates. All actual local benefit,
paid capacity and complete country source remain unknown; no test/self-adoption.

**AH16 — A smaller or different country can win.** Retain/repair existing agency
AP/help, C07 first receivers, B13 advice and DP's optional public tools without
an extra core service is a real cheaper/simpler rival; it can leave pre-dispute
or cross-agency help dependent on the agency's task/controller. A broader publicly
funded personal-agent utility may serve more private purposes but adds compute,
framing, source and surveillance exposure. Voluntary community/worker advice
or purchased independent representation offers different control/access costs.
AH chooses a limited person-side service, not every private product or new mediator.

Reopen actual exclusion, poor source/reliance, conflicts/capture, unsafe advice,
private exposure, nominal receiving, budget burden, incompatible simultaneous
staff or a better complete paid rival. Human/AI/future methods may change while
meaning, mandates, rights and paid continuity survive with fresh review. Next
largest integrated ordinary/adverse/paid-country gap, not an assistant quota,
provider-head, chatbot interface or new disclaimer series. Actual legitimate
affected choice, independent operation/source and better lives remain unproved.
