# Finance: a bounded three-source stability council

*MC13–MC16; part of [C06/41](../41-money-and-financial-public-control.md).*

**MC13 — Three sources, no Treasury vote.** Fixed council places belong to the
current monetary chair, Prudential Superintendent and Conduct Commissioner.
The monetary alternate is the next actually available unconflicted current
monetary member in published seat order, including when no chair exists; they
fill only that one source, not a new monetary committee majority. Other sources
use only their own preapproved same-seat reserve, never a career Deputy policy
vote. No person occupies two sources. Actual conflicts/expiry and substitutes
are recorded. A disposition needs two actual agreeing yes votes and at least
two lawful participants of fixed three, no casting or source-presence condition.
Remaining unconflicted two can determine a disputed recusal only if both agree;
otherwise an unconflicted ordinary court decides. A missing/conflicted exhausted
source is missing, not a Cabinet appointment or lower denominator.

Any one source may require a meeting within24elapsed hours/earlier useful time.
A preassigned career records officer in Monetary Operations supplies the
secretariat; all three offices retain independently usable protected originals.
Annual administrative presiding rotates monetary/prudential/conduct in that
published order, starting monetary at commencement, with next available source
for administration only. The chair cannot suppress meeting/source access or
cast an extra vote. Treasury may supply fiscal facts, not vote, veto reports,
license a bank or fund a rescue through this body. Shared staff/controllers
can still defeat independence and need actual funded alternatives.

**MC14 — Retain a closed tool menu.** M13 allows only a temporary addition up
to4percentage points to M02 equity (total at most12%); increases of M03 outflow
rates up to20points each (each at most100%); or lowering new non-sovereign
borrower-exposure ceilings by at most5points (never below5% of E). Combine
institutional/council equity requirements by the higher, not addition over12%.
No general debt/income cap, retroactive loan cancellation, named lending,
coverage, political-payment exclusion, tax or loss-priority choice follows.
Ordinary activation lasts at most six calendar months. One fresh reasoned
renewal of at most six months must lawfully complete before original expiry;
no gaps/renaming/overlapping replacements restart the cumulative twelve-month
outer from first ordinary effect. Thereafter continued extraordinary effect
requires an actual Assembly statute. Sector ordinary duties remain on lapse.

**MC15 — Council votes do not replace Parliament.** General M13 instruments
and renewals use DR according to actual cumulative effects. Material/adverse
rules need proper consultation, exact final L, full-N affirmative on days31–45,
and actual council making by45. Two council votes authorize the exact proposal
and its final lawful making; the earlier vote alone gives no binding effect.
Operative changes/material new support reopen the appropriate DR process.
Publish exact criteria, source/grounds, expected borrower/worker/small-firm
incidence, dissent, actual vote, first effect/expiry and independent challenge.
A rate/operational exception cannot launder a capital or access condition.

**MC16 — Resolve the fourteen/seven-day conflict explicitly.** Prospectively
replace M13's older14-actual-day emergency route with the earlier of its actual
source expiry and DR13's seven-calendar-day outer from first effect: date0
starts it, effect ends at start of date7; shorter hour limits still win.
Immediate usable publication and Assembly notice within24elapsed hours/earlier
useful time; direct B17 review, original start and no serial related reset.
An urgent council vote cannot waive mandatory consultation or the final window
for continued effect. A valid primary statute or genuinely separate existing
case protection may be available; otherwise the temporary general tool ends.
Fast systemic harm may therefore remain insufficiently controlled. The loss is
real; compare preconsulted contingencies or a different legitimate law, rather
than pretending a missed vote supplies protection or revives fourteen days.
