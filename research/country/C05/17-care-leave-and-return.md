# C05. Chosen care time, portable pay and a real return to work

Design180 ·4November2026 · CL01–CL24 proposed ordinary law, not enacted

**People must be able to welcome a child or support someone close without
being forced to choose immediate destitution or abandonment.** Public care,
clinical recovery, relationship time, leave income and employment rights perform
different jobs. A pension credit or nursery place supplies no job-protected leave.

This closes the deferred portable care-leave promise in [C05/02](02-work-time-and-care.md)
and [TW12](10/03-leave-learning-and-income.md). It retains TW36/32, S/NC/AS,
medical recovery and the public's ability to choose a materially different whole
bargain. [CS14](../C01/03/06-current-country-and-strong-alternatives.md) distinguishes
common fundamental protections and actual due rights from unadopted candidate
numbers; copying every number is not a condition for a genuine rival.

[Comparison, objections and source limits](../../settlement/care-leave/README.md)
retains more public care/less leave, longer leave/different replacement and
strong retain/repair alternatives.52/16/6/2,100% and the cap/window are normative
hypotheses, not empirical optima or a qualified national price. No actual public
assent, source, paid replacement or improved lives is claimed. Actual constitutional/
software/contribution permissions and protected office mandates remain unchanged.
