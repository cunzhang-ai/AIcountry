# AL01–AL05. Adults can initiate learning and change direction

*Part of [lifelong learning](../22-lifelong-learning-and-renewal.md).*

**AL01 — A person, not a workforce input.** Ordinarily resident adults18+
can use this route without an upper age, citizenship, current job, income,
asset, employer sponsorship, app or AI-use test. C02's actual scope, provisional
access and stronger rights remain; this creates no new border or residence
permission. Younger post-foundational learners retain S07 and supported agency.
Retired people, carers, disabled people and people returning after interrupted
learning have equal access. Civic/cultural/community and personal learning are
legitimate purposes, not only future taxable employment. No career, training,
political or ideological test is imposed on I05/Y/health or ordinary rights.

**AL02 — A continuing offer independent of a job.** Select64 supported learning-
activity hours per eligible adult in each successive12-calendar-month service
period beginning at protected first eligible request, free at use, with qualified instruction,
necessary materials and actual accessible support. This is offered capacity,
not compulsory attendance, a private-study meter, a cash balance or bankable
tradeable credits. Publish flexible useful schedules and plural subjects with
learner input; do not discharge the offer with an unwanted arbitrary course.
A voluntary competent group/shared activity may serve several learners;64
learner hours are not64 one-to-one teacher hours. Actual support needs govern.

No partial calendar year reduces the first64: a31December request starts its own
full12-month period. For a willing/available learner, the first usable AL02
episode starts within90calendar days and the full offer is usefully available
within that service period, unless the learner freely chooses later access.
AL05's12-month qualifying first-step limit cannot defer this continuing offer.
Provider/account changes do not reset a period or multiply64; genuine later
re-entry starts no earlier than the prior period ends. Voluntarily unused hours
are not banked, but missed public access retains its actual remedy/receiving
beyond period-end without consuming the next period's new offer.

This normative floor is distinct from TW13's proportionate paid employment time,
S06 catch-up, S07 first pathway, AL04 renewal, N contracts and existing library/
cultural access. It caps none of them. Informal inquiry/relationships and free
public knowledge are not rationed or logged against64. Provider-caused missed
access retains correction/receiving across year-end, not automatic forfeiture.
No personal lifetime learning dossier or future revenue forecast is required.

**AL03 — Make the existing first pathway meaningful.** S07 covers one accessible
first qualifying vocational, apprenticeship or advanced-learning route with
suitable preparation, not merely a short orientation or an arbitrary credential.
The accepted plan states the learner's chosen aim, prior learning, recognised
outcome, necessary duration/modules/placements, actual provider/assessment,
tuition/materials/access support and calendar. Necessary complete standard
pathway costs are public, including a longer full route when valid bridging
cannot reach the aim. It promises no preferred institution, job or licence.
Incomplete/inaccessible prior study is not automatically a discharged first path.

**AL04 — Later renewal is a person-initiated public service.** Revise S07's
dependence on funded N choices: an adult can seek a supported qualifying renewal route
where existing capability is insufficient or not practically usable for a
reasonable chosen new vocational, civic or community capability. Technological
change, disability/accommodation, care return, location and voluntary protected
career exit matter; a valid old certificate, current job or absence of failed
job applications cannot alone exclude. A different prestige label without a
material learning need does not require repurchase of equivalent qualification.
Use proportionate evidence/help and published reasons, not a compulsory career
profile, fiscal-return score or employer permission. There is no fixed waiting
period after a technology or life change. One active publicly supported plan
at a time, with recognition/bridging where genuinely sufficient and a necessary
full pathway where not; N's additional funded contracts remain available.

Full public renewal requires a substantial practical capability gap, a meaningful
achievable chosen aim and proportionate necessary support. Published assessment
compares genuine suitable alternatives, incremental qualified resources, previous
publicly supported learning and other learners' due needs. A succession of newly
chosen unrelated degrees is not automatically necessary renewal. An authority
may refuse disproportionate full-course repurchase with specific evidence/reasons
and a suitable lesser route where one exists; it cannot demand the person stay
in an old job or use a blanket 'budget exhausted' label as assessment. AL02 may
serve a personal learning purpose but cannot discharge an APPROVED necessary
qualifying renewal. Refusal/suitability is independently reviewable. These are
substantive contestable public priorities, not a promise of every repeated degree.

**AL05 — A finite decision and a real suitable route.** Existing local education
authority, through its Director, owes the public offer/plan; national education
administration owes funded equalization and accepted residual receiving within
C04. AP first receipt/help/reply/ordinary30-working-day decision and its one
bounded extension apply to public eligibility/funding decisions. A guidance
appointment, waiting-list number or provider referral is not an accepted route.
The plan states a suitable funded first learning step no later than12calendar
months after protected first receipt for a learner willing/available to begin
an appropriate step, or a later date the learner freely chooses. This includes
necessary publicly funded preparation/accommodation; readiness does not require
mastery of the final course's prerequisites. Later qualified stages have actual
accepted continuation/funding and dates; token orientation or missing public
preparation/support cannot discharge the plan or become learner-caused delay.
Actual mastery may take longer without a false pass. AL10 governs scarce specialties and suitable
alternatives. Missing capacity remains a breach/continuing remedy, not automatic
unsafe instruction, forced relocation, cancelled basic rights or approval by silence.
