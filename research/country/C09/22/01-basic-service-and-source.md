# Basic communications service and public source

*Part of [CN communications access](../22-communications-access-and-network-power.md).*

**CN01 — Define a basic offer people can use.** The Assembly enacts the
ordinary access floor, covered places, price protection, service guarantee and
finance. It names the existing C09 regulator as the technical standards and
enforcement owner and the existing civil service portfolio as public guarantor
and purchaser. The regulator publishes a schedule for service at occupied
premises and for reachable ordinary access for people without stable premises.
For each class test actual calls, messages and general internet use from the
receiving end during busy periods, including two-way accessibility and
representative work, education and public-service tasks. Set capacity, latency,
reliability, repair, installation and total price limits before commencement,
with reasons, protected tests and a public challenge period. Basic internet
access supports lawful general use. Each statute identifies who can revise
which technical parameter and when. A material reduction in entitlement or
budget needs elected authority and prospective transition. A new
communications medium may satisfy a task where it preserves practical
interoperability, privacy, freedom of choice and independent challenge.

**CN02 — Make the offer reachable at the point of use.** Apply the basic
offer to usual residence or practical local presence. Provide a way to prove
eligibility that does not require a permanent address, credit history,
smartphone or a single digital identity. Offer accessible enrollment and
repair requests, installation and reasonable equipment or assistance where
covered. Account for access controlled by landlords or building operators
under existing property, safety and access law, with bounded orders and
lawful remedies. Preserve voluntary nonuse. Separate technical serviceability,
a genuine priced offer, voluntary take-up, received usable service and unmet
requests. Measure materially different groups and places without exposing
people or treating a provider's coverage map as final evidence. Preserve
equivalent legal receipt through the agency that owns each election, care
or justice duty under its own law.

**CN03 — Match the all-in price to a recurring source.** Publish the basic
tariff including mandatory equipment, installation, taxes and charges,
showing one-time and recurring amounts. Compare it with residual household
means after the protected living basket and unavoidable local costs under
C06/C07. Fund any enacted targeted price and connection assistance from a
separately voted recurring or one-time source, as applicable, with timely
cash and accessible proof. Automatic qualification is permissible only under
a lawful minimal-data route with an independent correction and an equivalent
manual application. Measure each uncovered component before making a
readiness claim. Price renewal, repairs and continuing operation separately
from construction. Count tariffs, grants, shared reserves and claimed
efficiencies once. Any sector levy needs an explicit elected decision,
incidence and payment analysis, and protection against shifting costs onto
households the floor is meant to serve. A capital grant supplies no recurring
discount.

**CN04 — Assign and enforce a duty to connect.** The guarantor buys the
residual uncovered offer through geographically complete lots or other
lawful plural procurement and publishes the covered tasks, dates, price and
accepting carrier. Compare repair, existing networks and shared build before
an award. Verify reach, representative busy-period performance, genuine
installations and receiving before each relevant payment, with support for
harder cases and separate reporting for voluntary refusals. Preserve the
resident's claim even when a contractor misses a milestone. On a refusal,
publish or give the claimant a useful reason and preserve the original
request date. The regulator can issue timely correction within the enacted
duty, with direct independent tribunal or court review. Relief may require
an available temporary connection, installation by a fixed date, correction
or credit under law. If no funded carrier accepts, the guarantor must seek
a paid municipal, community, public or replacement operator or refer the
precise uncovered commitment to the Assembly. Existing rights and liability
remain live through that referral.
