# Design228 decision record. Critical medicines and safe continuity

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned design 227](baseline.json)

## Decision and its scope

SC already requires independent supply paths, usable reserves and
priced receiving. TD09 and TD10 own market entry, product quality
and reserved acts. The remaining gap is the product-specific
decision chain from an early shortage signal to timely dispatch,
individual substitution and actual clinical receipt. Select
[MQ01–MQ10](../country/C07/26-critical-medicines-and-safe-continuity.md)
for covered medicines, with NCS as delivery owner and independent
safety and rights oversight. The [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md)
and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md) organize this record.
Only valid legislation and finance can commence the duties.

Practical continuity, voluntary informed choices and independent
review are design requirements. The four-hour urgent reporting
period is a proposed outer bound with an earlier useful time where
necessary. Actual local lead times, clinical windows, alternative
use, protected reserves, useful capacity and costs need qualified
evidence. Receipt is individual. Coverage for one person cannot
come from unacknowledged harm to someone using the substitute.

## Viable alternatives

| Alternative | Strong case | Limitation or objection |
|---|---|---|
| Retain ordinary purchasing with better shortage notification | Keeps working contracts, limited fixed costs and local expertise. Prefer where the existing supply can deliver on time. | Reporting does not create stock, qualified substitutes, dispatch capacity or a safe treatment slot. |
| Public purchasing competence, plural verified paths and rotating reserves near receiving | Reduces common dependencies and can protect local clinical windows with targeted rather than blanket stocks. | Extra paid work, expiry and cost. The same inputs or transport may still fail together. |
| Public or cooperative production | Can retain critical capability and supply a market that persistently has no resilient bidder. | Requires lawful inputs, skills, reliable manufacturing scale and independently released quality. One domestic line may be a single failure point. |
| Regional shared reserves or joint purchasing | May widen qualified supply for small countries and spread limited standby costs. | Legal release, competing demand, independent routes, foreign payments and donor protections need separate evidence and agreement. |

Select the second architecture where it improves usable receiving.
Preserve sound existing provision and keep the structural alternatives
open for products and contexts where evidence warrants them. Use the
[two-region case](../settlement/critical-medicines/02-two-regions-and-two-deadlines.md)
to check stock location, actual substitutions and the next deadline.
The case selects no medicine, clinical protocol, purchasing volume
or national self-sufficiency target.

## Public authority and paid change

NCS purchasing owns contracts, clinical networks own response and
receiving, TD09 independently owns quality decisions, and licensed
clinicians and pharmacists own their reserved acts. The Assembly
votes the coverage and resource boundaries. Keep effective patient,
worker and supplier challenges, protected evidence and outside
clinical, audit and court review. Verify appointment, removal,
appropriation, source access and remedy execution through an adverse
handover. A purchaser cannot control the only original batch evidence
or dictate the clinician's decision.

Before any commencement, qualify one product and use, one protected
reserve or independent supply path, the lawful release chain and paid
alternate accepting staff. Preserve current supply, consent, ongoing
treatment, held products, contracts, payments and pending claims with
actual FT/OP/T successors. Protect testimony and patient information
while retaining auditable custody and public reasons.

## Next bounded test

For one locally critical medicine and a candidate alternative, inspect
real original supplier, release, reserve and receiving records. Compare
ordinary repair, regional rotation and a paid alternate with the same
patient protection and two successive windows. Seek voluntary patient
and worker input, direct receiving evidence and independent clinical
and fiscal review. Reopen on repeated local failure, unsafe substitution,
loss of the substitute's other service, unaffordable stocks or a
better feasible route. Actual participation, enactment, paid capacity
and improved treatment outcomes remain unestablished.
