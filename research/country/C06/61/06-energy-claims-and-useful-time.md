# MS22–MS26. Pay for household protection before the reimbursement

*Design284 · Proposed extension to [essential energy shock and affordability](05-essential-energy-shock-and-affordability.md).*

For the first energy motion, prefer a bounded, funded advance for timely
local protection where ordinary sources cannot carry the gross cost.
Use existing income, housing and supply authorities. Verify receipt in
the home or at an accessible alternative, and settle public accounts
on their separate dates.

**MS22 — Give each action a due time and an owner.** On a material energy
report, the existing [thermal duty officer](../../C09/23-safe-home-temperatures-and-continuity.md)
records the first report and the last useful time for safe receiving.
The relevant income officer identifies any separate payment or billing
deadline. The utility, property controller and lawful repairer identify
who can verify supply and the building installation. Where several
causes remain plausible, pursue the needed checks concurrently. Use
direct, assisted and offline intake under existing law. A resident may
report without a utility account. The original report is reusable
within lawful scope. Passing it to another owner neither resets its
clock nor completes the task. A missed acceptance triggers the named
fallback and visible escalation while the duty is still actionable.

**MS23 — Vote a gross local source with a short path to the household.**
Under [MF](../../C04/08/03-reimbursement-and-resident-receipt.md), compare
usable standing local funding, a lawful advance and a later reimbursed
route on the same deadlines. Price eligible household payment, technical
receiving, accessible alternatives and independent correction separately.
Elected bodies set the purpose, cap, source, release evidence, named
payer, period and replenishment of an advance. Record both payers' actual
transactions and the funded receiving capacity before relying on it.
Keep lawful resident awards and reserve cash in their distinct scopes.
A failed appropriation or transfer leaves an exposed obligation and a
need for lawful correction. It cannot cancel a prior claim.

**MS24 — Keep the public settlement proportionate and challengeable.**
Use already verified lawful records and publish the required evidence,
ordinary review, expected timing, attributable administration cost and
recoverable amount. Preserve original receipts and restricted access.
The responsible payer may decline to pursue a discretionary public
reimbursement when independently reviewable costs exceed expected
recovery, or seek a simpler lawful route. Publish reasons and effects
on local finance. This choice is subject to prior collection and public
finance duties. It has no effect on eligibility, due payment or access
to challenge. Separate denied, delayed and waived public recovery.
Credit authorized advances and final settlements once, with distinct
arrears and any owed repayment.

**MS25 — Close the heat and bill paths against actual receipt.** Apply
[TH](../../C09/23-safe-home-temperatures-and-continuity.md) to distinguish
network service, the building system and actual safe occupied space.
Use the qualified lawful repair, supported receiving or other accepted
alternative before the useful time. A supplier notice, completed payment
or restored feed alone cannot establish safe heat in a home. An indirect
billing credit requires evidence that the reduction reached the resident.
Pay an authorized direct route where the intermediary route cannot work.
Keep the independent correction path open for the bill and the safety
problem until each is resolved or publicly reported as unserved. Do not
require a tenant to front public protection, surrender a repair claim or
accept an unsafe or inaccessible placement.

**MS26 — Reconcile the first call and fund the next useful interval.**
The responsible authorities separately report usable household funds,
protected receiving, missed deadlines, remaining work, actual reserve
draws and later settlements. When lawful review or a corrected rule reopens
claims, trace preserved decisions through correction and actual relief.
Separate files examined, decisions revised and remedies delivered.
Check common carrier, property access,
staff and payment losses with [OC](../../C09/20-essential-service-outage-continuity.md)
and [JC](../65-joint-continuity-and-first-budget.md), including the next
call. Protect other due uses and independent review. An analyst or AI
can reconcile dates, record differences and identify a missing task.
Existing officers decide amounts, protected action and lawful correction.
Reopen the elected package when usable cash, qualified receiving or the
case for a simpler route changes. Carry valid prior payments, remedies
and repair duties through expiry or transfer.
