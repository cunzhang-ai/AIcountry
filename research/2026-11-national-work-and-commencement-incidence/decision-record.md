# Design473 decision record

**Baseline and procedure.** Use Design472, the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md)
and [baseline hashes](baseline.json). Join matched official scopes where
support exists. Preserve statutory choices and their own procedures.

**Selection.** Apply the local payer and service reconciliation to a
national standard that materially affects standing local work even when
legal responsibility does not move. Compare retain and repair, a
standalone 36-hour stage and a funded two-stage Act. Bring forward
ordinary protected repair and qualified preparation on the evidence
available. If the earlier stage qualifies alone, present it separately
and price the deferred improvement. If both qualify, preserve the
bundle as an actual alternative. Assess recurring source by payer and
the incremental cost above the funded stage, staff at the actual
window and net usable time and access.

**Objection and competing finance.** A sustained general transfer can
spread unequal local costs but adds exposure at the funding authority
and can increase dependence on it. Compare actual local sources with
a voted formula and settlement, and compare verified service or task
changes with unchanged quality and receiving. Protect local decisions
and independent challenge. Time-limited reserves can help a dated
shortfall. They do not provide proven long-run financing. No accounting
balance target or affordability verdict is inferred.

**Migration and reopening.** Retain existing worker and resident
claims and independent authority. Existing lawful dates do not wait
for a new readiness certificate. A later change requires the competent
legislature. Revisit on independently verified coverage, lawful
recurring sources and accepted rosters, a missed due protection or a
stronger service-and-time alternative. [Evidence](sources.md)
distinguishes observations and sensitivities. Actual adoption, affected
participation, nationwide feasibility and outcomes remain open.
