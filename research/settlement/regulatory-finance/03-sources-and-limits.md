All numerical regulatory choices need qualified accounting/financial, legal
and affected-party review, including credit exclusion, compliance cost,
procyclicality, public contingent risk and actual payment access. A high ratio,
fixed clock or independent-sounding institution cannot prove safety or a
better country. No empirical calibration, actual participation, state authority,
new staff, insurance finance or observed superiority has been obtained here.

An ordinary-regulation model with stronger public provision and a narrow-safe-
payment/credit split remain real rivals. Selection of one candidate cannot
silently absorb or extinguish disagreement. Reopen if practical access,
source finance or democratic control favors a substantively different model.
