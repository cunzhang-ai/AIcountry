# Design243 decision record. Budget alternatives and agenda control

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned Design242](baseline.json)

## Decision

Select [BCV01–BCV05](../country/C06/60/05-competing-budgets-and-floor-choice.md)
for competing whole budgets. Preserve every member's ordinary proposal,
costing and hearing rights. Give an exact alternative supported for
consideration by a bounded number of elected members a place in a common
indicative vote. Use those votes only to schedule final floor votes.
Keep the existing DB04 affirmative majority for the exact budget.
A defeat moves the agenda to the next protected alternative.
Apply [the working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision record](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

The baseline protects presentation but leaves the timing and availability
of an actual final vote unspecified. An incumbent can make even a majority
choose between its own text and lapse. An approval ranking can still be
tactical, especially where groups have several attractive backups. It
determines priority alone. The gate and vote order deserve scrutiny as
distributions of power. A witnessed tie draw shares an unavoidable order
among tied proposals without taking over the substantive choice. A given
tie order can still affect the final winner if several proposals can pass.

## Compare the real alternatives

| Choice | Strong reason | Material exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Keep protected hearings and a single scheduled final budget vote | Low floor workload and a clear line of government accountability. | The agenda can decide the result even when the Assembly would approve a different exact text. |
| Guarantee a bounded series of votes in public indicative order | Gives non-government alternatives a concrete route under the same threshold. A failed final vote need not trigger lapse. | Strategic indications, support gate exclusion and workload can still affect the result. Tie order matters where more than one package can pass. |
| Set the fiscal envelope first, then vote on allocations within it | Makes reconciliation tractable and exposes the aggregate budget choice. | Early grouping of ceilings can foreclose a coherent tax-and-service tradeoff. Formal permission to revise the envelope may still be hard to use. |
| Let each approved line enter independently | Makes the vote on each expenditure intelligible. | Compatible-looking votes can spend the same cash or book the same crew twice. A separate reconciliation could undo votes after the fact. |

Select the second design for mutually exclusive whole schedules. Keep
severable changes on their lawful routes and require real dependent source
legislation. The ten-percent designation gate, one designation per member
and five-day lock are explicit candidate choices. They bound the protected
docket while retaining ordinary rights. They could exclude groups with
distinct needs, favor organized blocs or leave unstaffed scrutiny even
with ten or fewer texts. The gate does not establish fairness. Preserve
priority for rights and actual access under existing law, including
outside parliamentary support thresholds. The selection does not prefer
any substantive package nationally.

## Evidence and migration

The [official comparators](sources.md) distinguish spending frameworks
from appropriations and show a rule on unrelated reconciliation provisions.
The [authored case](../settlement/budget-alternatives/01-same-money-and-different-majorities.md)
isolates the effect of a protected vote. It also checks abstention,
withdrawal of presence, overlapping finance, version changes and failed
first votes. It does not show how elected people would actually vote.

A competent enactment would require the Assembly's own authority and
rights review, trained protected clerks, supported plural evidence access,
lawful sitting and alternate presider, authentication, accessible voting
and review capacity. The same elected members and DB04 threshold remain.
An enacted appropriation still needs BLC, JC and original authority,
source and receiving conditions. A migration preserves existing budgets,
standing duties, pending alternatives, records and challenges. Adopt any
changed docket rules prospectively. The rules cannot approve themselves
or transfer approval from an earlier exact text. Actual independent
review, affected participation, enactment, operations and outcomes remain open.

## Next bounded decision

In the first public budget comparison, use three real, exact, materially
different schedules and one genuinely shared constraint. Let the protected
Assembly administration test access, headcounts, an attempted last-minute
substitution and an alternative presider within its voted staffing ceiling.
Use accessible public observation and safeguarded evidence. Compare time
and exclusions with a single vote and a two-stage fiscal-envelope rival.
Completion requires a named owner for each blocked path and original
records of which alternatives actually reached an actionable vote or
were displaced. Reopen for strategic exclusions, repeated material
ties, uncontrolled scope rulings, overloaded scrutiny or a simpler
sequence that demonstrably retains equivalent contestability. A missing
audit or sitting leaves a documented failure and the existing routes.
