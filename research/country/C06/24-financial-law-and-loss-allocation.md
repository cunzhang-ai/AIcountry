# C06. Financial law, protected payments and allocated losses

1 November 2026 · Design 104 · Proposed M01–M16, not enacted law

Select a specified national candidate for regulated plural finance: actual
claim classification, bounded prudential requirements, client-property
protection, reachable payments, limited insurance, transparent bank losses
and elected public risk. Safe public-payment access and a fully reserved
payments/narrow-credit architecture remain substantive alternatives.

Every new ratio, coverage multiple, premium and clock is a proposed hypothesis,
not an optimal number or certified Basel/FSB/IADI implementation. Actual bank
books, legal jurisdiction, markets, delivery and new costs remain unknown.
[V01–V12](../C08/19-regulation-and-bounded-enforcement.md) supplies inspection,
correction and bounded punitive proof without conferring sovereign budget power.

A consequential conflict is now explicit: the existing country book's bank
has equity 100 but holds sovereign paper 250. M03's domestic-sovereign ceiling
of 100% of eligible equity permits at most 100 before other exposure changes.
Retain the boundary and **reject that financing transaction as a compliant
M candidate**; do not grant an exemption to make the old reference pass.
At least 150 must find a separately funded lawful holder or the bank needs
real additional eligible equity. Neither route is an achieved source.
[Arithmetic and alternatives](../../settlement/regulatory-finance/README.md)
keep the old book intact as a now-incompatible comparison, not a ready launch.

[SF01–SF10](25-source-finance-and-payment-continuity.md) now supplies a
different conditional source: new sovereign 175/E200, paid equity/source
conversion, disbursed working stock and independently safeguarded standby.
The old E100/250 transaction remains rejected, not grandfathered. M03 now
explicitly covers previously unmapped stable-funding categories; actual
consent, pricing, complete prudential review and operating finance remain open.

[BF/RA/BR](39-bank-failure-and-protected-payment.md) now completes M07/M09 institutional and execution rules without revalidating ratios or the old book: independent insurer trigger/payout, fixed3panel/2votes, direct courts, actual sources including prior drawn debt, bounded review and accepted receivers.

[MC01–MC32](41-money-and-financial-public-control.md) completes monetary/
supervisory leadership, council votes, finite risk and bank-case/loss/redress
owners. It expressly reconciles M13 urgency to the source/seven-day cap and
separates own-rate transactions from DR banking rules, without changing M02/
M03 ratios, insurance/resolution authority or certifying a country source.
