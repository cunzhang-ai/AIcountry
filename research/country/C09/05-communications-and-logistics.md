# C09. Communications and logistics as shared infrastructure

*Part of [C09 land and ecology](../C09-land-and-ecology.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

Elections, courts, payments, clinics, food and repair crews depend on
communications and the movement of physical goods. A data platform does not
provide a radio path, road, port, warehouse or driver. National law therefore
assigns connectivity and essential-logistics duties separately from digital
content, service eligibility and ordinary private trade. Public, cooperative
and private operators may deliver them under enforceable public obligations;
no single provider or ministry should own every channel of contact and supply.

## Connection without compulsory digital life

[CN01–CN12](22-communications-access-and-network-power.md) supplies the
ordinary communications service, fair carriage and tested emergency
delivery rules. This topic retains the common network principles and the
separate duties for physical correspondence and logistics.

The Assembly defines a reachable, affordable basic communications floor and
funds the uneconomic parts it chooses to guarantee. An accountable sector
regulator assigns spectrum and network access under prior law, tests
interconnection, nondiscriminatory access, service quality, pricing and
operator continuity, and gives reasons and appeal for licenses or sanctions.
Prior law fixes plural appointment, protected removal, bounded funding,
source access and independent appeal, tested against shared controllers.
The technical regulator has no authority to rank
political speech, order a general shutdown or create surveillance powers.
Any lawful interception or emergency restriction uses its separate rights,
judicial and C10 routes. C11's public data-exchange operator cannot use a
technical standard to become the only gateway to a legal claim.

The floor is about usable connection: geography, disability and language
access, price, devices, skills, repair and service at the time needed. It is
not a duty to own a smartphone or an always-online identity. Courts,
election offices and essential services keep funded in-person, paper, local
radio or other suitable routes with equivalent legal receipt and protected
original records. A statute can define a basic physical correspondence
service for legal notice and remote communities, including operator,
coverage, quality, price and public subsidy; it need not grant a postal
monopoly or treat parcel commerce as a constitutional entitlement.

Before outage, operators map power, fuel, backhaul, towers, local switching,
shared vendors, spare parts, trained staff and cross-border links. Prior law
sets restoration, emergency interconnection or roaming, public warnings,
mutual aid, records custody and notice to critical users. Critical networks
need tested alternate paths and an accountable local method for reaching
people when both internet and mobile service fail. A missing electronic
acknowledgment cannot silently extinguish a vote, claim or court deadline.
Emergency priority is bounded by enacted criteria and review; a network
operator cannot decide which district, party or patient deserves access.

## Goods must reach people

An accountable transport and logistics steward maps essential corridors,
ports or border crossings where applicable, roads and rail, depots, cold
stores, vehicle capacity, drivers, fuel, customs and payment dependencies.
Safety, competition and environmental reviewers retain their own distinct
powers; local authorities decide ordinary local routes within law. The
steward compares repair, alternate modes, inventories and supplier routes
with actual delivery times for food, medicines, water parts and other
protected services. C10 covers lawful foreign access and agreements; a
national plan cannot command an overseas port or carrier.
The [C10 customs rule](../C10/05-customs-and-border-clearance.md) separately
governs domestic inspection, clearance and appeal at a lawful border.

Ordinary freight remains plural commerce under safety and fair-access law.
Critical public contracts name an operator, performance record, alternate
carrier or public receiver, worker and asset transfer, and payment source.
A dominant port, warehouse or platform cannot make a private refusal into
public rationing. If several essential services require the same truck,
radio channel, diesel stock or cold store, C06 counts it once and applies
its prior-law scarcity stages. A new material cross-sector priority returns
to the competent elected authority; neither a logistics model nor an
executive coordinator acquires general allocation power by delay.

Existing licenses, worker rights, property interests, supply contracts,
legal notices and pending claims continue through any operator change until
lawfully assigned successors can deliver them. C06 accounts for maintenance,
universal-service subsidy, spare capacity, inspections and funded fallback;
C11 retains source records and AI may assist forecasting and routing without
issuing a binding censorship, delivery-denial or emergency priority. Actual
sites, geography, operators, costs and lawful treaty obligations remain
unknown. The [decision record](../../2026-10-communications-logistics/decision-record.md)
compares ownership and coordination options. Reopen this selection if
regulated plural routes fail to provide usable access, if state control
captures civic communication, or if the fallback cannot move essential goods.

[TD01–TD04](../C08/22/09-transport-duties.md) now specifies mode entry,
operation, accountable automation and actual jurisdiction. [TS05/TS14](../C08/22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md)
permit proven future control methods while requiring domain/fallback evidence;
freight access, democratic communication and military authority stay distinct.

[SCN01–SCN05](../C04/06/05-safe-public-contact-and-notice.md) defines
a funded public correspondence route with independent fallback and
staffed access. The carrier's scan documents routing. The sector sender
or court establishes usable notice within its authority.

[BCR01–BCR06](../C10/05/01-border-corridor-continuity-and-physical-receipt.md)
tests shared inspectors and dated alternate corridors, including the
inland handoff and the next protected receiving window.

[SW01–SW06](11/01-shared-street-works-and-protected-access.md) tests
shared street windows and protects independent critical routes. Keep
physical service, safety and control distinct from work coordination.
