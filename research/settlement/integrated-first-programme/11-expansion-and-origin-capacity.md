# A successful care pilot and the next district's real capacity

*Design310 · Constructed national expansion choice.*

The integrated first programme funds qualified care preparation.
This exercise asks what to choose later if an actual trial has
delivered care. The numbers and observations below are authored.
They add no money or work to the existing first-programme comparison.

All blocks are compatible qualified, accepted care time in the same
service window, after ordinary travel and supervision. A, B and C
are distinct areas. Before the trial they can deliver 5, 5 and 7
blocks respectively against already protected care duties of 4, 5
and 6. The trial promises two additional blocks to A and has bound
both through the next comparison window. Its service is possible
with A's one spare block and one paid block loaned from C.
Net capacities become 6, 5 and 6. B has the greatest unmet need,
three additional desirable blocks beyond existing duties. Assess B
on the same need and service window in every choice.

| Choice after the trial | Care capacity A / B / C | Remaining new need in B | Protected or bound gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Retain and repair with no funded new delivery | 6 / 5 / 6 | 3 | None |
| Replicate immediately by moving three more blocks from C | 6 / 8 / 3 | 0 in the proposed receiving schedule | Three due blocks in C. No lawful release. |
| Paid ordinary interim route plus preparation | 6 / 6 / 6 | 2 | None, assuming actual mobile receipt |
| Later adapted offer, with distinct qualified local capacity | 6 / 8 / 6 | 0 | None, assuming the two local blocks and the mobile route are accepted |

Two incremental cash units are available in the first window.
The interim route costs one unit for an independently staffed and
available mobile block, with another unit for paid preparation.
A same-window direct expansion on genuinely additional staff
requires three blocks and five units. Neither is available that
early. The later proposal uses one retained mobile block and two
newly qualified local blocks within five separately appropriated
units for that window. Keep A's commitments and C's prior
duties on their original sources. Later figures are a condition
of a new choice, without assuming training becomes service.

Choose the interim route and preparation over replication in this
exercise. Compare it with a genuinely funded earlier common offer
if one becomes available. The interim operator's duties, reach
and actual receipt must be verified. If the mobile route fails
after the interim commitment binds and no alternate accepts, B
has a missed interim block and two still-uncovered future blocks.
Keep all three visible. The late stage then has only two verified
additional blocks and needs separate elected disposition for any
changed optional offer. Independent care and ordinary remedies
continue. Other areas or households cannot supply the missing
block by assumption.

The Kenya teaching study found different learning outcomes across
two operators assigned similar interventions within one experiment.
This supports checking whether the mechanism survives a change of
operator. It supplies no transferable care effect or staff estimate.
