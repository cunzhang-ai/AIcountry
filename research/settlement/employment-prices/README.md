# Stabilization should answer for both prices and involuntary work loss

Design184 · [Canonical MS01–MS16](../../country/C06/61-employment-prices-and-household-security.md)

The selected country now has a joint monetary objective: medium-term price
stability and the highest sustainable voluntary employment. This expressly
revises M14/MC01's secondary-employment hierarchy; it does not invent concern
for employment where none existed. The prior mandate was already flexible.

| National choice | What the country selects |
|---|---|
| Monetary responsibility | Both objectives,2% price reference/1–3% reporting band, reasoned medium-term path and unchanged lawful tools/votes |
| Demand weakness | Existing automatic person-side claims plus elected recovery/repair/capacity/source choices, not reflexive rights cuts |
| Supply or imported-price loss | Basic access, feasible real supply/substitution and explicit distribution of remaining losses; no promise everyone keeps all purchasing power |
| Persistent inflation or mixed shock | Evidence and possible tightening/easing/retention; neither label nor indicator decides automatically |
| Work and services | Real voluntary safe opportunities, lawful TW/care/learning/rest, no compulsory job or maximizing paid hours |
| Power and failure | Independent lawful rate decisions, elected fiscal choices, due claims and paid actual remedies; no additional council/instrument/source |

Automatic personal protection does not supply an automatic lender, foreign
settlement, new indexing formula, worker or material. Source failure remains
unmet duties and accountable elected correction. EB remains finite; its end
does not prove re-employment or end separate I05/S rights.

[Decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-employment-prices-and-democratic-stabilization/decision-record.md)
retains flexible-inflation, retention, voluntary public-job/learning and wider
fiscal-money alternatives. The main objection is real: joint objectives may
blur responsibility and weaken price trust without improving work. This is a
selected value/institutional bargain, not a funded stabilization programme,
distinct successful rate path, local policy adoption or proof of superiority.
