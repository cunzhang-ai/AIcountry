# Design363 decision on legacy contamination and long-term protection

## Baseline, proposed choice and authority

Pin Design362, CLN01–CLN08, NR12, WS, FB, DB and the
[working method](../FRAMEWORK.md). The existing design distinguishes
scheduled operator failure and a separate legacy budget. Select a
positive route for old sites without usable recovery, including
reporting, risk-based elected sequencing, restrictions and a funded
monitoring tail. The Assembly authorizes sources and scope. Qualified
environmental and service officers act under their existing authority,
with independent verification and courts for rights and liability.
The project assistant proposed the portfolio choice. There is no
actual assessment, source, participant or appointment.

## Alternatives and objections

A redevelopment-led portfolio may make sites useful faster or recover
land value. It can leave ongoing exposure unresolved and depend on
speculative receipts. A uniform investigation queue appears impartial
but can miss severe exposure or people with weak reporting routes.
Select prior-law urgent protection, need-sensitive investigation and
a separately priced remediation and monitoring schedule. Compare
lawful protection in place, a safer use and repair where supported.
Keep actual receipt and restricted use visible during uncertainty.

The approach can cause stigma, speculative loss or indefinite limits.
Require scoped public evidence, protected private details, timely
challenge and independent criteria for a change of use. An industry
levy could fund some work under separate law, but does not automatically
reach past liability. Keep recovery, protection, existing priorities
and gross appropriations separate. Concentration of review or testing
can leave the same sites unverified despite appropriations.

## Scoped result, transition and next action

The [primary comparators](sources.md) distinguish screening, funding
and continuing review. The [constructed case](../settlement/legacy-contamination-and-long-term-protection/01-three-sites-and-a-monitoring-tail.md)
checks a visible-project rival, a delayed recovery and two later
periods of protection. It establishes no real contamination, cost,
restoration, adoption or improved lives. Preserve original claims,
protected source custody, restrictions and qualified acceptance
through transfer and later use.

Within an authorized environmental research budget, compare one
known ongoing protection duty, one uncertain site and their next
monitoring deadline on the same staffing and cash schedule. Use
independently inspectable evidence and usable affected participation.
Stop at a costed lawful priority and remaining exposure. Reopen on
new hazard evidence, missing protection, a failed payer or a more
protective feasible route.
