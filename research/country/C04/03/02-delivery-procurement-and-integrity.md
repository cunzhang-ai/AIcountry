# C04. Accountable administration and services: Service delivery, procurement and integrity

*Part of [C04. Accountable administration and services](../03-administration-and-services.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

Each essential service has one publicly identifiable authority responsible for
getting a remedy delivered, even when several providers participate. That
authority owns the workforce, supplier, maintenance, replacement and complaint
plan and reports whether people actually receive the service. It cannot call
an underfunded vacancy a productivity gain or use a contractor to conceal
staffing obligations. Staff need lawful pay, training, workable caseloads,
safe reporting channels and time to correct mistakes. Professional clinical,
teaching, legal and inspection judgments retain their own duties. Informal
cooperation remains possible without turning every interaction into a state
workflow.

An accessible public office or legally designated provider or professional may
receive a person's problem without making that person first classify the law.
The first authorized receiver records a durable, claim-specific receipt,
protects urgent needs within its competence and routes each distinct duty to
the competent decision-maker.
The referral remains its responsibility until the receiving body accepts it;
law defines when the first date counts for a legal deadline. A disputed referral
goes to a named statutory competence or court route while urgent duties continue;
no office may close it by silently passing the file onward. People retain direct
routes to sector authorities, courts and independent reviewers. A shared clerk,
interpreter, accessible site or secure exchange may support several bodies, but
cannot become the sole credential or evidence store for both an official action
and its challenge. Policy, provider, safety, investigation and final review
remain separate where common control would defeat a remedy.

Public buying begins with an elected or lawfully delegated service purpose,
not a vendor's proposed system. The commissioning authority compares public,
cooperative, private and mixed delivery where appropriate, including workforce,
full-life cost, maintenance, market concentration, rights, failure reception and
exit. Competitive, proportionate tendering is the default. Bid criteria and
weights, material beneficial ownership, political/official conflicts, award
reasons, changes, payments and delivery results are recorded and disclosed with
justified protection for trade secrets, privacy and security. Requirements must
allow capable new entrants; splitting a contract or altering it after award
cannot evade competition. Sole-source or emergency purchase needs a previously
enacted ground, a narrow term and ceiling, recorded alternatives and prompt
independent review. Urgency does not authorize a new permanent surveillance,
force or eligibility power.
The [C05 market-power review](../../C05/05-market-power-and-real-exit.md)
checks when serial awards, common owners or shared infrastructure make a
nominally competitive procurement an essential-service bottleneck.

For a material purchase, the body defining need, evaluators, contract manager,
delivery verifier, Treasury payer and auditor have distinguishable duties and
source records. Shared services can supply a small locality with professional
procurement capacity, but cannot choose its policy or become the only supplier.
No bidder or sponsor verifies its own performance or controls the only complaint
channel. Workers, residents and losing bidders have appropriate routes to
challenge a breached standard, unlawful exclusion, corruption or non-delivery;
the remedy must name who can suspend, repair, recompete or provide the service.
AI may flag anomalies and compare options, but an automated score cannot award,
blacklist, certify delivery or dismiss a complaint without accountable human
reasons and review. The [state-capability decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-state-capability/decision-record.md)
compares this operating chain with patronage, central gatekeeping and outsourcing.

Contracts for essential delivery specify records custody, safe access, staff and
claim continuity, transferable data and keys, paid transition capacity and a
lawful receiving operator. The public authority retains the funded ability to
take over or recommission within a realistic period; a paper step-in clause is
not capacity. A contractor may perform authorized work but cannot gain sovereign
tax, arrest, detention, general eligibility, protest-control or lawmaking power
through procurement. Public enterprises and mixed operators face the same
service, conflict, audit and exit tests. The Assembly directs a public enterprise
through its lawful mandate, funded noncommercial duties and disclosed risk limits;
ministers cannot secretly demand preferential lending, employment or contracts.

Integrity protection covers ministers, career staff, local officials, public
enterprises, bidders and major subcontractors. Material interests and gifts
are disclosed to a statutory reviewer separate from the appointing or buying
office; recusal, reassignment, divestment or a bounded post-office restriction
follows the actual conflict, with privacy and proportionate appeal. A secure
independent reporting route
protects staff, suppliers and residents against retaliation and can preserve
evidence during a handover. Reports can reach audit, an ombud, a competent
prosecutor or a court within their different mandates; the implicated manager
does not control every intake. Audit, investigation, prosecution and adjudication
remain distinct, with appointment, funding and information safeguards against
both cabinet suppression and an abusive integrity office. A complaint is not
proof of corruption; inquiry protects defense, labor rights and confidential
sources. An allegation alone neither blacklists a rival nor halts an essential
service; narrow interim preservation or suspension needs reasons, time limits,
review and a receiving provider. If a procurement breach or public-enterprise
loss is proved, courts and competent authorities can order lawful correction,
recovery, compensation and discipline while the essential service continues
through a funded route.

Design 85's [comparative case](../../C01/03-country-case-and-competing-designs.md)
adds a bounded operating choice: the existing independently governed audit
function's enacted/funded plan combines risk/complaint-directed work with a
genuinely random selection component from a lawfully complete in-scope transaction
frame. Publish selection/probability, source/control, assignment, conflict and
challenge rules prospectively without exposing protected cases or enabling
interference. Government cannot quietly remove inconvenient contracts from the
frame or target only opponents; related/split transactions remain traceable.
Required urgent/safety work keeps its own duties rather than waiting for a draw.

Actual competent audit preserves original transactions and independently checks
physical/delivered work where appropriate. Inspect displacement/substitution
into hiring, exclusion or unaudited components, not only the selected loss proxy.
Attendance, extra panels and AI flags cannot discharge that work. Findings,
publication/actual reach, safe challenge, lawful correction and received remedy
are separate; audit findings are not guilt or a demand that voters dismiss an
incumbent. The legislature funds the scope/random component and actual shifts;
no universal 100% rate, new bureau, self-funded audit or fresh tax/force power.
Insufficient resources are disclosed under NP, not an excuse for selective
harassment or proof that an unperformed audit was complete.

C04 merit/qualified recruitment, protected reasoned removal, Assembly-bounded
pay/source and direct court routes continue for audit staff. [AA01–AA40](../04-public-audit-and-accountable-oversight.md)
now supplies the distinct external public-audit leadership/source/removal and
own-review choice identified by119: Assembly dual-vote8-year chief, statutory
case/report staff, bounded records/reply, publication without audited permission,
separate external examination and actual lawful court/prosecution routes.
This replaces the generic chief-source choice prospectively only through competent
AA36 law; it does not create a duplicate inspector, punish by report or make
actual independence/capacity true. Prior legitimate audits/appointments/cases,
source records, worker/provider/claim rights and lawful years/dates keep exact
successors/paid transition. Career/collegial/audit-court and simpler alternatives
remain real. No head controls every credential or finally reviews its own wrong.

[OP06 shared support](../../C06/45/02-delivery-support-and-independent-power.md)
now defaults compatible additional support to existing C04/local services with
actual paid scope, conflict separation and safe alternatives. This gives no
shared host/payroll/procurement provider access to protected records or veto over
independent appointment, source request, payment, case or publication powers.
Separate actual work/independent receivers remain paid; no old bid prices them.

[SC01–SC20](../05-public-buying-and-continuous-supply.md) now supplies the positive
prospective buying/challenge/continuity schedule behind these principles. It
retains these roles and actual law/rights; no old publication bid funds its new work.

[DP01–DP16](../../C11/09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md) concretizes
existing shared digital support and the already proposed technical cyber
coordinator; no new sovereign coordinator/ministry or mandatory common host.
Each independent institution retains actual case, source, publication and
budget powers/accepted support. Director's own misconduct goes outside the
implicated chain; G/shared/sector work needs actual included/incremental cost,
independent records/receivers and paid handover, not assumed AI gains.

[PI01–PI20](../../C03/17-public-decision-influence-and-interests.md) now supplies
common office-side influence/interests and explicit ordinary-legislator duties,
particular administrative conflicts, narrow public-interest standing and proposed
constitutional A2 self-benefit constraint. These existing integrity/procurement
protections remain, not an assertion that no integrity law existed. Actual paid
receiving/AA/court roles and A9/RR adoption/holds remain; no new guardian.
