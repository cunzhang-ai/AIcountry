# CTC01–CTC07. Fund evidence-limited treatment with a safe exit

*Design376 · Part of [medicines and safe clinical methods](../14-medicines-devices-and-safe-methods.md) · Proposed research law.*

**CTC01 — Decide distinct questions before making an offer.** Product
authorization, a public coverage rule, procurement and individual
clinical use retain their separate owners. An authorized indication
does not itself create public coverage or a finding of benefit for a
particular patient. The Assembly sets the material coverage boundary,
conditional-access authority and source under S and HG. Under that
law, the NCS purchasing and access owner may open a specified
conditional offer only after published appraisal by qualified
unconflicted reviewers outside the purchasing chain. Use existing
independent clinical review and career appointment safeguards. A
supplier, minister or model cannot settle a patient decision.

**CTC02 — Compare actual care and the uncertainty that matters.** Publish
the indication, relevant current care, potential benefits and harms,
uncertainty, affected groups, absence of alternatives and full pathway
cost. Include qualified staffing, testing, monitoring, care time,
patient travel, independent appeal and services that would be
displaced. Hear patients and clinicians in accessible forms and record
material dissent and conflicts. Compare supported ordinary coverage,
continuing competent care and a limited conditional route with
independently answerable evidence. Give equal standing without reducing
people to income, employment, disability or a single value score.
Rare or excluded groups need proportionate methods and actual
uncertainty, without automatic rejection or presumed benefit.

**CTC03 — Open only a defined and funded conditional offer.** Where
there is plausible meaningful benefit and uncertainty that the
proposed evidence can resolve, name the population, evidence question,
source, deadlines, enrolment scope, consent, independent safety
oversight, price and stop conditions. Protect necessary clinical
records and separate research permission from routine care. Declining
optional data or research cannot remove existing care, relief or an
independent review. Any evidence participation that is indispensable
for an expressly authorized study needs its own lawful, voluntary
consent. An ordinary contract cannot authorize an experiment. Lack
of feasible evidence calls for a reasoned ordinary or narrower
lawful decision rather than an indefinite conditional offer.

**CTC04 — Qualify later care before admitting the first cohort.** Name
the operator, paid clinical staff and usable funds for starts,
monitoring, an independently reviewed final decision and an
individually safe exit. Count gross cost and physical capacity through
the actual dates. Qualify supply, accepted alternative care and
patient-side support. Treat a future rebate as zero usable cash
until collected. Compare narrower authorized admission with retain
and repair where either current or later capacity fails. Allocate
qualified finite places by published clinically relevant priorities
and fair ties. Give reasons and ordinary care to people who wait.
A finite conditional offer cannot cap or cancel an existing entitlement.

**CTC05 — Decide at the review date and keep lawful care working.**
On the original dates, the competent coverage owner considers
ordinary adoption, evidence-supported narrower continuation, or
closure to new entrants. Material general changes use their elected
law route. No silent renewal or default full coverage follows from
a missed report, incomplete evidence or price dispute. Keep the
preauthorized and funded patient-specific transition on its own
terms. The treating team and patient decide safe continuation,
accepted alternatives or appropriate relief under current law and
safety evidence. Give a reachable independent challenge before the
loss of useful treatment time. An urgent safety stop keeps its
own authority. It triggers paid safe receiving and does not
guarantee continued use of the same treatment.

**CTC06 — Verify actual receipt and keep the payer answerable.** Follow
referral, acceptance, treatment actually delivered, missed care,
outcomes, burden and later continuity separately. Test original
source records and eligible people who never enter. Publish safe
aggregate results, limitations, cost ranges and reasons. Disclose
actual fiscal commitments with lawful protection for personal data
and limited confidential terms under OA. A patient can challenge
exclusion, unsafe transition or nonreceipt through HG and ordinary
courts. Fund timely review and enforceable execution outside the
purchaser's sole control. AI may reconcile evidence and scheduling.
It cannot determine suitability, ration care or authorize payment.

**CTC07 — Preserve the existing clinical floor through change.** Carry
valid decisions, original dates, consent, protected records,
contracts and outstanding remedies to a funded successor. Use
ordinary shortage and safety law if a supply or provider fails.
No successor acquires coverage power from a price agreement, a
change of software or transfer of records. Reopen on evidence
failure, missed dates, hidden displacement, harm to people waiting,
or a simpler supported ordinary route.
