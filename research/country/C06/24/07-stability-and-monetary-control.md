# Bounded system tools and a monetary mandate the public can change

*M13–M14; part of [C06/24](../24-financial-law-and-loss-allocation.md).*

**M13 — A closed temporary tool set.** Retain C06/02's three-member statutory
stability council, majority record, qualified conflict deputies, published
evidence/dissent, no Treasury vote and independent legality review. It can
for at most six months: add up to four percentage points to M02's equity
floor (total required ratio at most 12%); raise M03 outflow rates by at most
20 points each (at most 100%); or lower new non-sovereign borrower exposure
ceilings by at most five points (not below 5% of E). No other system tool is
authorized by this candidate. No forced retroactive cancellation of valid
loans; restrictions concern new/renewed exposure with a safe transition.
M02 individualized/council ratios combine by the higher requirement, not
addition above 12%. At expiry retain ordinary law; do not withdraw already
received equity or treat a released buffer as required shareholder payment.

One fresh reasoned council renewal of at most six months is permitted;
continued extraordinary effect after twelve months requires Assembly law.
A materially adverse new rule receives affirmative elected approval under
C03 before ordinary effect. [MC13–MC16](../41/04-three-source-stability-council-and-duration.md) now expressly
displaces the older14-actual-day route: source/DR13 earlier expiry, at most
seven calendar days from first effect (date0, end at start of date7), with
immediate publication, notice within24elapsed hours/earlier useful time and
court review. No serial related reset, skipped ordinary consultation/final
window or automatic renewal after a missed vote. Ordinary activation/renewal
uses completed DR before effect/expiry; a short urgent vote cannot bridge it. Lapse
removes only the extra tool, not ordinary supervision or current rights.
The council cannot set a named loan, a general borrower debt/income cap,
deposit coverage, tax, bail-in priority or political-payment restriction.
A new instrument needs new legislation, affected incidence and due-time review.

**M14 — Price stability with public control and employment responsibility.**
Retain the conditional domestic currency and operationally independent human
monetary authority under [MC01–MC32](../41-money-and-financial-public-control.md):
five fixed seats/three votes, exact Assembly selection/for-cause ordinary review,
careers, finite charter, public audit and lawful replacement. Its MC07 primary-
law operational exception covers own rates/typed transactions, not bank rules. The candidate's general objective is 2% annual consumer-price
inflation, with a 1–3% reporting band. [MS01–MS04](../61/01-joint-mandate-and-voluntary-work.md)
expressly replaces the secondary-employment hierarchy with joint medium-term
price stability and the highest sustainable voluntary employment; neither has
automatic priority. Temporary price deviation requires a reasoned joint path,
attention to persistence/foreign risks and expected return, not permanent price
abandonment. This is a revisable public value choice, not equal numeric weights,
an optimal formula, guaranteed work or a full-price wellbeing measure. C11's independent methods publish household/region effects
and uncertainty, including actual housing and essential-price pressure.

Use only enacted monetary interest rates, market operations and collateralized
liquidity within published risk bounds. No compulsory named private lending,
sector subsidy, Treasury overdraft or guarantee follows. A band miss requires
public explanation within 30 days and quarterly reporting of evidence,
expected correction horizon and employment/distribution effects until four
consecutive quarters within band; it does not mechanically require austerity,
unemployment, tax increases or denial of due services. Distinguish supply/import
shock, demand, price measurement and financial failure with original evidence.
The Assembly can revise the general mandate prospectively; cabinet cannot
secretly order a loan, replace contrary statistics or veto a rate decision.
Foreign/real resources and lawful fiscal choice remain separate constraints.
