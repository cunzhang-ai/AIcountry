# Candidate A8. Rights, emergencies and external commitments

*Design144 · [A7–A9 entrance](03-review-emergency-and-change-A07-A09.md) · Proposed constitutional boundary, not enacted law.*

**No domestic derogation.** Constitutional rights continue during emergencies.
No declaration, renewal, ordinary statute, contract or AI output may suspend their
application. Ordinary restrictions require lawful right-specific terms, competent
prior law, actual necessity/proportionality, feasible less intrusive means,
nondiscrimination, bounded scope/time and effective independent relief. An
emergency is not an alternative authorization for an otherwise prohibited act.

Torture/cruel or degrading treatment, slavery/servitude/prohibited exploitation,
secret detention/disappearance, capital punishment, nonconsensual experimentation,
arbitrary killing and punishment without applicable prior law remain forbidden.
Legal personality, inner thought/conscience, basic civil equality and source-law
legality/leniency remain. This is protected content, not a complete list or a
power to weaken other rights. Manifestation of a right may have its own ordinary
lawful restrictions; being nonderogable does not make every manifestation absolute.

Meaningful opposition, public information/association, electoral competition,
independent remedies and peaceful replacement cannot be extinguished. Declaration
cannot amend elections/franchise, boundaries, terms, succession, appointment sources/
votes, review, amendment or ordinary tax law. Ordinary institutional changes retain
A9/RR protected current/immediate succession and its closed prior-law restoration
exception. Physical impossibility gives no new executive postponement or mandate.

A prior Act specifies declaration authority, concrete grave threat, place, exact
bounded tasks, funding, actual publication/notice, review and expiry. Initial
executive declared authority lasts at most14elapsed days. Every renewal needs
three-fifths of positive full statutory Assembly membership including vacancies, public reasons and independent
review access, no more than30elapsed days each and90aggregate operative days for
the same emergency/material continuation. No relabeling, new territory/vendor,
late certificate or committee silence restarts it. An earlier source clock or
end of necessity controls. Ordinary legislation after expiry remains subject to
the full rights ceiling; it cannot derogate or confer retrospective immunity.

Declaration creates no new arrest, search, isolation, treatment, surveillance,
force, taking, tax, expenditure or foreign power. Ordinary DR urgent general rules,
custody, clinical, liberty, electoral and court limits retain their separate
shorter scopes and actual votes. Existing ordinary safety/service duties and
valid claims continue after exceptional authority ends. Missing court/Assembly/
source capacity is a real constitutional failure, not authority for Cabinet or AI.

Before foreseeable cross-system shortage, Assembly law sets resource-specific
triggers, affected scope, temporary allocation criteria, source records, bounded
residual provisional acts, competent officers, review and expiry. Operators act
for technical safety and coordinators deploy only preassigned nonoverlapping
reserves under that law. A new material priority requires an Assembly decision
through its protected sitting route; delay supplies no extra power or delivery.

Major treaties, defense commitments and guarantees require prior democratic
authorization; Cabinet negotiates within that mandate. Material provisional
application and withdrawal require the same prior elected authority, exact text,
rights/resource account and lawful continuity. Domestic approval cannot itself
terminate external obligations or individual protection claims. A contractor or
foreign partner receives no unreviewable return power or hidden fiscal/coercive
commitment. Actual international obligations retain their own scope.

Standing immediate defense uses prior law and recorded orders, with prompt Assembly
notice. Sustained force abroad needs affirmative bounded Assembly mandate/renewal.
Emergency power, command or classified committee silence supplies no vote.
Separate lawful classified scrutiny, rights/humanitarian review, protected unlawful-
order reporting, civilian criminal jurisdiction and command handover remain.
AI is advice, never ratification, sovereign war authorization or independent
lethal authority. C10's actual armed-force periods are not this declaration clock.
