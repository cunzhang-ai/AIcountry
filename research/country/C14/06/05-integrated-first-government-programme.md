# A first government programme with decisions that can stand on their own

*Design281 core and Design286 joint release · Working programme selection under [T13–T17](04-linked-choices-and-lawful-start.md).*

The selected service-first country starts by paying due protection and
repairing existing failures. Its first programme should also buy usable
improvements where it has the power, cash and capacity to deliver them.
Use the [integrated first-budget case](../../../settlement/integrated-first-programme/README.md)
as a scoped choice under declared assumptions. The figures do not give
a country a budget or turn pending rights into funded obligations.

**Start together where the dependency is real.** In the matched case,
select a finite targeted energy bridge with accepted supply, paid care
workforce preparation and a three-tenancy housing cohort with voluntary
support. Hold the separate public-intake modernization pilot. When early
funds or usable staff are weaker, compare protecting the housing start
with protecting preparation. The case selects the former if the homes,
local vote, advance and operating capacity qualify. If they fail before
commitment, the separately approved energy and preparation option can
stand by itself. The particular scale and priorities are choices in the
reference case. Under the [declared common loss](../../../settlement/integrated-first-programme/04-first-release-register.md),
price the independent reserve on the same budget. The weaker envelope
then supports E plus P on its exact approved terms. E plus H needs one
more early national cash unit and one independent review slot. Keep the
foregone housing opportunity and its cost visible.

**Carry the budget as a set of exact decisions.** Use
[GP](../../C03/20-government-programme-and-public-choices.md),
[DB](../../C06/60-democratic-budget-and-execution.md) and
[WF](../../C06/58/21-national-cash-and-first-budget.md) for the
programme, votes, owned cash and adverse paths. Compare exact motions
on the same inherited rights and independent capacity. National and local
bodies vote their own contributions. Reconcile an advance as a national
payment and local receipt without counting it as new resources for the
country. Keep foreign settlement, local acceptance and each indispensable
remedy on the appropriate schedule. The same ordinary approval of one
component cannot authorize a different remaining combination. Use the
valid exact contingent option or return the changed scope for decision.

**Name both what waits and the cost of waiting.** Use the
[chapter-by-chapter account](../../../settlement/integrated-first-programme/03-country-scope-and-remaining-choices.md)
to distinguish continuing duties, priced incremental starts, later
votes and unresolved national costs. Continued research selection of
a future policy gives it no first-budget source. A planned discretionary
purchase displaced by another use is a political loss to show openly.
An already due claim remains on its original clock. If supply or cash
fails after a valid commitment, show the original duty, shortfall and
lawful continuity or remedy. Do not use a pre-start condition to unwind
a valid lease, an earned payment or an enacted service duty.

**Keep the evidence and operational record useful.** Give independent
budget analysts the underlying payer, staffing and acceptance records,
with safe access to protected evidence. A support office or AI can
check the ledger, identify shared controllers and show conflicting
assumptions. Elected bodies still decide; the competent operator accepts
and delivers, and independent authorities verify actual remedies.
Reopen on a changed lawful baseline, absent receipt or capacity, or
new evidence that the foregone policy would better protect people.

The weaker case protects ordinary duties and holds new starts unless E

[Evidence and operating paths](06-first-programme-evidence-and-operating-paths.md)

**Price court-ordered performance as well as payment.** Apply
[SR18–SR22](../../C08/30/05-specific-public-orders-and-substitute-execution.md)
where an established public order needs a specific act beyond record
access or payment. Price parallel qualified execution, scoped authority,
actual receiving and independent review on the original deadlines.

**Carry the time residents can actually use.** Apply
[CS19–CS23](../../C01/03/08-usable-time-and-public-participation.md)
to material joint changes in care, access and participation. Cost paid
support, keep independent judgment and test compatible windows before
treating separate services or an invitation as usable.

**Qualify parallel local changes together where they share a resource.**
Apply [LT01–LT05](../03/01-parallel-local-trials-and-shared-obligations.md)
to shared staffing, supply and restoration. Keep independently feasible
trials separate, protect the ordinary floor and use the exact competent
decision where commitments conflict.

**Buy technology on a gross funded basis.** Under
[AT13–AT17](../../C05/18/04-public-investment-and-realized-technology-returns.md),
price the ordinary alternative, independently usable output and
earned rights. Disclose any conditional public return separately
and count only its actual lawful receipt on its own date.

**Keep voting instructions independently reachable.** Under
[ER21–ER25](../../C03/10/10-electoral-instructions-and-timely-correction.md),
price protected channels, accessible local help and civil execution
alongside the election. Record whom a late correction can still reach
and preserve a separate route to challenge a lost opportunity.

**Budget for independent measurement and a failed series.** Under
[PCO06–PCO10](../04/04-independent-public-statistics.md), price the
scheduled public release, safe independent check and actual service
contingency. Use the recorded evidence threshold and qualified scope
before releasing an optional change. Continuing claims stay due.

**Price execution when two orders need the same resource.** Under
[SR23–SR27](../../C08/30/06-conflicting-public-orders.md), fund independent
review and compatible ordinary alternatives on the same schedule.
Keep both original claims visible and check receiving separately.

**Fund the local service floor through population change.** Apply
[MF14–MF18](../../C04/08/04-funding-through-population-change.md) to price ordinary
needs and fixed costs before redistributing grants. Keep a dated lawful
advance or retiming distinct from the annual allocation. Protect the
next due call before moving money forward.

**Price a replacement before a late recovery.** Apply
[NS26–NS30](../../C06/54/07-common-import-permissions-and-continuity.md) to any material
common import permission. Test paid local protection and independent
receiving on both deadlines. Reserve actual foreign cash for the
replacement and keep any later refund on its own date.

**Protect earned rights after payroll failure.** Under
[Y25–Y29](../../C07/04/07-earnings-records-and-portable-correction.md), price the paid
independent casework, wage advance, additional credit backing and
next due payment. A revised record or expected recovery does not
supply actual funds.

**Price water and care failures together.** Under
[T18–T22](07-first-programme-under-concurrent-losses.md), the reference
can cover either shock and still start energy and training. With
both, choose the independently approved energy bridge alone if it
can arrive in useful time. If the housing leases already bind,
fund accepted support first. Those leases and the two corrections
leave no early national cash for either optional start.

**Use the observed Copenhagen starting point for real-world qualification.**
[T23–T26](08-first-programme-with-observed-budgets.md) carries approved care and
water-related purposes under their respective authorities. In this
context select ordinary repair and preparation. An exact reserve or
new start needs its own dated source, staffing and accepted receiving.
[T27–T30](09-reserve-funding-and-usable-restoration.md) traces approved reserve money
and prices local receiving through an independently accepted return.
