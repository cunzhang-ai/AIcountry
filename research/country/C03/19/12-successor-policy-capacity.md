# HT52–HT56. Preserve an executable choice through a change of government

*Design539 · Proposed ordinary law within [lawful handover](../19-lawful-handover-and-actual-control.md).*

**HT52 — Follow the means of changing policy across decisions.** Apply to
prospective discretionary government commitments or releases that, with
existing and sufficiently specified related acts, would materially foreclose
the last identifiable alternative within a major service or policy scope
after ordinary political replacement. Apply across instruments, bodies and
dates when they depend on the same finite cash, site, personnel, legitimate
source evidence, operating access or qualified alternative. Determine
materiality under the existing budget, procurement and commitment rules.
The sponsor shows the work affected and the earliest irreversible step.
The existing budget owner joins the record. A public owner discovering a
materially linked act sends the original scope and dates to that owner and
the competent forums before its own act binds.
Ordinary wages, enforceable rights, judicial acts, safety duties and performance
of binding contracts retain their routes.

**HT53 — Demonstrate a choice from appropriation to receipt.** Before
authorizing the first still optional act that would remove the last route,
the competent elected forum receives a joined comparison. Price the incumbent
policy and a materially different alternative with the same affected people,
binding obligations, fiscal exposure and service deadlines. Set the first
useful decision date, latest preparation date and actual accepting parties.
Test the first and next operating calls, the handoff and a plausible shared
loss. Book alternate uses of the same slot, asset or eligible cash once.
Bring the decision forward when the deadline to keep an option is earlier
than the date the optional act would bind.
Give opposition usable original evidence and time to present a qualified
alternative. A source account shows money. The comparison also shows what
can actually be bought, changed or delivered on time. Missing facts are
unverified. Expose when there was already no executable option at baseline
and assess whether the new act delays or blocks restoring one.

**HT54 — Require a separate elected decision to give up the last route.**
Where a funded, proportionate way exists to keep an executable alternative,
specify its owner, limits, sourcing, latest exercise date and costs. It may
retain access, buy finite standby, stage a disposal or use an independently
qualified ordinary arrangement. The next officeholder remains free to use
the route under its lawful authority. If the sponsor proposes to close the
last route or materially prevent its restoration, the competent elected body
decides that loss in a separate recorded vote before binding the act. Show
the concrete public need, cost of keeping the option, who bears the loss
and the earliest date meaningful choice could return. If separate bodies
must act, each votes on its own authority and identifies any required
linked commencement. Use existing applicable stronger rights limits.
A new vote supplies no money beyond its appropriation and no permission
to violate a protected right. General notice or a bundle vote cannot
substitute for the express choice. Where there was already no qualified
route and the act would leave restoration unaffected, use ordinary decision
and disclose the gap. No fixed fiscal margin or permanent standby duty follows.

**HT55 — Verify the retained route before counting it.** The public owner
verifies legally usable control, paid availability, independent execution and
accepted ordinary delivery. Recheck on a material dependency change and
at the handover. When a change depends on a supplier's operating method,
test a representative policy variation off the live system with a qualified
independent operator and downstream receiver. Carry open cases and their
original rights through the exercise. Pay for the test without taking slots
from due services or assuming the supplier will grant new permission.
If an option loses its site, crew or other necessary input,
hold the affected prospective release where its authority depends on that
option and return the changed choice to the competent body. Continue
separately authorized work and standing duties. Use existing urgency and
review routes for imminent harm. No one gains a new veto or power to bind
the successor from verifying a factual condition.

**HT56 — Carry the choice, its expiry and the continuing rights.** The
lawful successor receives operative terms, custody and named public
counterparties early enough to decide. Confirm notices or orders at the
actual controlling and receiving ends. Publish which proposed changes
remain executable, which are foreclosed, and the actual effect on service.
Existing audit and courts review legal compliance and exercise their existing
remedies. The elected body weighs policy. AI can reconcile cross-body
dependencies and surface missing originals. It cannot set materiality,
authorize spending, establish availability or make the choice. Apply the duty
prospectively. Retain existing enforceable liabilities and remedies and seek
lawful repair where past commitments already removed a choice.
