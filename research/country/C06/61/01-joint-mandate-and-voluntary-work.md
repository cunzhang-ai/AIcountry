# MS01–MS04: a joint objective without compulsory employment

*Part of [national stabilization](../61-employment-prices-and-household-security.md).*

**MS01 — Prices and voluntary employment are joint responsibilities.** Replace
M14/MC01's secondary employment/capability support with medium-term price
stability AND the highest sustainable voluntary employment under lawful work
conditions. Neither has automatic lexical priority. This is not equal numeric
weights for incomparable units or a guaranteed outcome. Consider persistent
involuntary unemployment, lost skills/relationships, essentials affordability,
price persistence/expectations, financial/foreign constraints and uncertainty
together. Output growth is not the sole welfare criterion; actual democratic,
bodily, service and remedy rights cannot be traded away in a monetary score.

**MS02 — Retain an anchor, allow a reasoned path.** Retain the revisable2% annual
consumer-price objective and1–3% reporting band. A miss is not itself an order
to tighten or ease. MC must select and explain a lawful medium-term path for
both objectives, comparing realistic alternatives and their likely timing and
losses. It may accept a stated temporary inflation deviation where faster
correction would cause disproportionate persistent work/service loss, and must
also confront evidence that delay would entrench inflation, harm vulnerable
people or worsen later adjustment. Employment cannot excuse a permanently
abandoned price objective; a price number cannot excuse unexamined joblessness.

State the expected return path, key uncertainties, affected groups and facts
that would change the decision. Missing evidence is not a forecast of success.
Retain M14's explanation within30days of a band miss and quarterly updates until
four consecutive quarters inside band. MC/Assembly's ordinary review remains;
no new formula, automatic rate move, fiscal order or punishment follows.

**MS03 — Work means a real voluntary opportunity.** Assess people seeking safe
lawful paid work, involuntary underemployment, discouraged search, duration,
access and actual vacancies/skills/place, not one headline unemployment rate.
Chosen care, learning, rest, reduced paid time or retirement are not failures to
be compelled into jobs. Do not reduce I05/S/Y rights, force relocation/unsafe
work, raise lawful TW hours or count a nominal slot as a useful received job to
improve an indicator. Productive gains may support time, services or income
through lawful public choice/bargaining; maximizing total paid hours is no goal.
No fixed natural-unemployment quota, universal zero target or right to a public
job is selected. A model's estimate of sustainable work remains contestable.

**MS04 — Preserve the actual monetary institution.** The conditional domestic-
currency/managed-float choice, MC's five fixed human seats, three actual yes
votes, statutory appointment/recusal/removal, careers, finite risk charter and
enumerated rate/market/collateral tools remain. No named-firm credit quota,
unsecured Treasury finance, employment cheque, fiscal veto, ownership power or
new emergency instrument follows from the mandate. Existing permitted liquidity
and bank resolution retain their separate conditions. External currency/narrow
payments remains a complete rival; it cannot be credited with independent
domestic monetary instruments it does not possess. AI assists evidence only.
