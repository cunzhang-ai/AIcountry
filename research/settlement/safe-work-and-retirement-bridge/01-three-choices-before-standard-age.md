# Three choices before standard pension age

Constructed monthly comparison in after-tax units. A 63-year-old
worker cannot safely continue the full duties of a long-held job.
An independent assessment supports a reduction. The worker accepts
available safe paid work at two units. Their verified reference
net earnings were ten. The ordinary adult basket and recognized
need in this example are five. No other counted income, assets,
cash-equivalent services or matching wage replacement are received.
Assume no additional withholding on the bridge in this exercise.
The existing I05 comparison uses a one-unit work disregard and
a 50 percent taper.

| Response | Cash and work effect |
| --- | --- |
| Safe reduced work and existing I05 only | Work pays 2. I05 pays 4.5. Total usable cash is 6.5. No new bridge cost. |
| Add the candidate bridge with safe reduced work | The earnings benchmark is the lesser of 5 and 6. The bridge pays 3 gross. I05 treats the received bridge as replacement earnings and pays 3. Total usable cash is 8. |
| Use the existing option of an early earned pension share | Requires a valid quote and independent voluntary election. Timing and lifetime effects depend on the common factor and chosen share. The case assigns no unsupported amount. |

Select the bridge in this example because the worker needs to leave
unsafe duties and the narrower income-loss protection increases their
usable cash while preserving the future pension election. Where paid
accommodation can maintain safe earnings, compare that route and honor
the employer's applicable duties. An early share remains a separate
choice. Unaffected workers and those who do not qualify retain ordinary
protection and employment remedies.

The gross bridge appropriation must provide three units on its due
date. The later 1.5-unit change in I05 reduces combined outlay in
this example from a gross increase of three to a net increase of
1.5. It supplies no money for the earlier payment. If the bridge is
late, I05 cannot treat its expected receipt as income. Retain original
claims and apply ordinary period matching and recovery safeguards.
If a separate one-unit matching wage replacement is already safely
received, reduce the bridge to two and count the three replacement
units once in the I05 comparison.

At standard age verify the last bridge payment, accrued rights and
first actual pension payment on their own dates. An instruction sent
to the pension payer cannot close the person's remaining hardship or
unpaid claim. No fiscal or actuarial result for an actual cohort is
inferred.
