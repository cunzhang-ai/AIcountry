# Candidate provisions A7–A9: Review, emergencies and constitutional change

*Part of [Authority and legal transition](../../AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION.md); A8 reconciled in design144; A9 in129; A7 retains its prior scope; research, not enacted law.*

**A7 — Independent review and replacement.** Appointments, removal, case access,
funding, order execution and transition records must be structured so the challenged
office lacks effective control of every remedy path. Opposition, lawful media and
civic association can organize alternatives. Valid electoral defeat transfers
office and resources. Missing appointments or an AI outage cannot create a new
acting judge, sovereign AI or renewed mandate by implication.
Assembly law must assign ordinary and constitutional jurisdiction, appeal and
case-allocation rules before disputes arise. A plural, publicly reviewable
qualification and appointment chain must prevent cabinet, governing majority,
court leaders or one professional guild from controlling every judicial seat.
Appointment delays neither reduce required votes nor extend a nonrenewable term.
If a final constitutional forum lacks quorum, ordinary courts retain only their
preauthorized, appealable urgent individual-rights competence; they cannot
declare themselves the final national constitutional court. Independent,
reasoned discipline addresses misconduct with fair process, not an adverse
lawful judgment. The Assembly enacts bounded continuing court and legal-aid
funding; treasury executes due payments without targeted ministerial veto.
Each public-body judgment names a responsible officer, lawful funding source,
due act and independent proof of delivery. Refusal has enacted, individually
reviewable consequences; a lawful substitute can perform only preauthorized
ministerial acts. Courts cannot levy taxes or manufacture missing capacity.
For death or serious injury in custody, grave official violence, a missing
detainee or credible torture/evidence-obstruction allegation, prior law assigns
evidence preservation and independent investigative and prosecutorial competence
outside the implicated command, with a predesignated conflict substitute.
Courts retain warrant, liberty, trial and remedy authority; Assembly funds and
oversees the general system without directing a named case. A complaint is not
proof, and protection or civil relief does not wait for conviction.
For civil status and family protection, Assembly law defines registration and
correction, lawful relationship and support mandates, child protection, supported
adult decisions, urgent protective orders and estate administration. Registrars
record evidence; they do not conclusively decide contested parentage, support
authority, custody or succession. Local services provide support and urgent
safety within law; independent courts review coercive continuation and disputed
status with children and affected adults able to be heard. A person's essential-access
claim and ability to challenge do not depend on a partner, parent or guardian
controlling the only account or identification channel. The reviewer and remedy
executor need records, funds and safe access outside the challenged household
and agency. A temporary order is neither criminal guilt nor permanent custody.

**A8 — Rights, emergencies and external commitments.** The canonical
[A8 charter provision](05-rights-and-emergencies-A08.md) selects no domestic
constitutional derogation, explicit14/30/90 declared-authority limits, continuing
ordinary rights/sector clocks and distinct external/defense authorization.

**A9 — Protected change and commencement.** A constitutional amendment follows a
legitimately adopted public route that preserves equal political standing,
practical opposition, periodic competition, enforceable remedies and peaceful
replacement. In the reference route, one third of the full Assembly may
introduce exact text; a 1% elector petition triggers supported drafting and
hearing, and a verified 5% elector petition sends exact text to a public vote
without an Assembly veto. The Assembly route requires two thirds of its full
membership and a majority of valid yes/no referendum votes. The direct
petition route requires three fifths of valid yes/no votes so a cabinet
cannot bypass the Assembly supermajority through a sponsored petition.
Both routes require published legal and resource effects, at least 90 days
of accessible public consideration, independent procedural/rights review,
a clear neutral ballot and fair opposition. No executive decree calls its
own constitutional vote.
The charter fixes the initiative thresholds, a collection period of at least
one year, objective verification and independent correction; implementing law
cannot narrow them or change rules during a live petition.
An Assembly opinion on a citizen initiative has a fixed deadline; silence
does not veto the ballot or enact it. The reference has no turnout quorum.
The reference opinion deadline is 60 days and the ballot follows no sooner
than 90 and no later than 180 days after lawful certification, subject only
to narrow independently reviewed postponement.
These thresholds and timetables are proposed hypotheses, not currently binding.
[CRV01–CRV05](../../country/C14/01/04-separate-constitutional-choices.md)
proposes distinct exact authorizations and votes for materially independent
partial amendments. A genuine dependency uses separately approved terms,
public reasons and the applicable predecessor-law dates. It cannot
transfer signatures, authorization or assent across questions.
[CRV06–CRV10](../../country/C14/01/05-constitutional-counterproposals.md)
proposes a paired vote for one independently authorized Assembly rival
to a public initiative. Preserve the original initiative timetable. Two
separate approvals are required before a conditional preference decides
which of the exact incompatible texts prevails.
Ordinary changes to elections, tenure, appointment, constitutional review,
emergency power or the amendment procedure do not govern current officeholders
or their protected immediate succession under [RR01–RR10](../../country/C14/08-democratic-repair-without-self-authorization.md).
For elected offices this includes nomination, count, litigation, recount,
lawful rerun and formation-failure repeats; no failed poll consumes protection.
All RR02–RR07 safeguards apply to the closed restoration exception, including
the competent **predecessor-law**
forum, its actual jurisdiction/votes and clear and convincing proof of a
necessary neutral repair of an already protected democratic function, with no
adequate narrower remedy, defined receiving and the unchanged public procedure.
It cannot extend terms, pack courts, change current appointment sources/votes,
alter seat conversion or boundaries, erase remedies or approve itself.
The reviewed early date and any delayed fallback must be expressly authorized;
changed text/facts require fresh scoped review. An absent forum, silence or
model agreement never certifies the exception. A newly desirable franchise or
policy is not automatically restoration of existing law. These provisions
reconcile the candidate core to RR; they do not adopt RR or bootstrap founding.
The predecessor procedure governs its own replacement until validly changed. The protected
democratic functions cannot be abolished by an internal amendment, although
their institutional form may be changed with equal standing and practical
public control retained. Founding needs an actual affected public, lawful
jurisdiction, clear membership, rights-preserving transition, opening accounts,
staffed institutions and ratification. No research edition, developer key or
project owner may declare commencement alone. A lawful founding or whole-order
replacement question is not solved by this future amendment mechanism.
