# KR21–KR24: an accountable registry without a knowledge sovereign

*Design 95 research candidate; not enacted law, a funded offer or demonstrated
superiority.*

**KR21 — An administrative service, not another sovereign regulator.** Assembly law
names the responsible national minister, statutory examination criteria, disclosure
rules and appropriations. Registry entries authenticate applications, grants,
assignments, licences and expiry; they never cure absent title or invent a right.
Copyright needs no compulsory registration for ordinary protection. The minister
publishes prospective administrative policy, never instructions to grant/refuse a named
case. Final contested validity/infringement and coercive remedies belong to ordinary
courts; existing V10 sector regulators retain their own appointment rules.

**KR22 — Director and staff.** Use C04's existing recruitment office: open accessible
competition, published job-related competence in rights administration, evidence and
accountable management, qualified public shortlist, disclosed conflicts and reasoned
cabinet appointment of one director for five nonrenewable years. No
recipient/industry/EV-chief nomination veto. Cabinet may remove before expiry only by a
reasoned decision proving serious misconduct, persistent material statutory/performance
failure or inability after accommodation, with usable notice/response and direct
ordinary-court review including interim protection; unwelcome case outcomes and lawful
dissent are no ground. No second substantive director while a stayed removal remains
unresolved.

Ordinary examiners, counsel and custodians use C04 career recruitment, qualification and
protected discipline. The director designates a qualified career deputy through that
process before vacancy, not for a disputed case. At vacancy/suspension, at most 180
elapsed days of existing routine management; no new policy or discretionary senior
appointments. If absent/conflicted, or afterwards, statutory examiners/custodians
continue only their own lawful functions, while chief-reserved acts remain
unperformed/disclosed. Cabinet must begin a new competition within 14 days; relabeling
cannot renew acting power. These choices expressly replace any generic C04 acting
discretion for this service, not V10's separate regulator panels.

**KR23 — Assignment and money.** Publish application requirements, assistance,
priority/assignment rules, conflict exclusions and reviewable estimates before filing.
Career assignment staff rotate competent examiners under those rules; minister/director
cannot choose an examiner to obtain one result. Complex cases receive an attributed
second technical/legal check outside the first decision; genuine qualification absence
stays a delay, never presumed grant/refusal. Preserve actual useful-time urgent access
and B16 challenge to repeated irrelevant requests/delay. Assembly funds examination,
accessible prior-art evidence, defense and courts; enacted bounded filing/renewal fees
enter elected accounts, not examiner pay. No grant/refusal-volume bonuses, fee-retention
expansion or patents deemed granted by silence.

**KR24 — Challenge and capture.** Applicants receive reasoned claim-by-claim decisions
and a chance to answer adverse evidence; materially affected third parties can submit
grounded validity/title objections, with protected evidence and direct courts without
compulsory internal exhaustion. Correct procedure, review validity and enforce properly
defined rights under B; no automatic injunction merely because a certificate exists.
Disclose controllers, aggregate grants/refusals/reversals/delay and external advice,
with C04 risk/random audit of source and actual examination. B38's certification-loss
rule is not a guarantee that an issued patent survives validity challenge; a competent
Act must specify any new assurance liability before fees imply insurance. Capture of
staff, Treasury, records and courts can still defeat the chain.
