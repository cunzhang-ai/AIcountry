# Three councils and one catchment

Design357 · Constructed comparison under
[CLX01–CLX06](../../country/C04/01/02-cross-local-effects-and-residual-decisions.md).

Three councils share a catchment. North and Central each propose a
new draw of 3 water units, to be approved as separate applications.
South does not belong to their joint compact and depends on 2
units of the 5 already assigned to continuing lawful uses. The
reliable gross water is 14 units. A separately protected ecological
flow of 4 leaves 10 for allocation. The scenario assumes compatible
sites and timing and stipulates no right to draw from the protected flow.

| Option | Existing and new uses | Result |
|---|---:|---|
| Assess North alone or Central alone | 5 + 3 = 8 | Each appears within the 10-unit limit |
| Approve both at the requested size | 5 + 3 + 3 = 11 | One unit exceeds the limit |
| Approve a smaller joint package | 5 + 2 + 2 = 9 | One unit remains available under the stated conditions |
| Hold both unbound additions after a new low-flow finding | 5 out of a revised 8 | Continuing uses fit; the two-part package would require 9 |

A South resident's local office receives the shared-effects report on
day 0; the national office receives the file on day 8. Both notices
use the day-0 clock. The forum is due by day 45 and the Assembly
disposition by day 90. Assume the forum receives an independently
checked flow account and hears South before proposing the smaller
package. This neither gives South unilateral control nor counts its
silence as assent. The case makes no change to any protected consent.

The smaller package requires 3 cash units from North, 2 from
Central and a separately appropriated 2 for independent monitoring
and affected receiving. The latter has a named payer and must be
usable by the first draw. South has no new unpaid duty. A late
monitoring transfer leaves that package unready. A narrower
earlier start would need its own proved conditions and funding.

If reliable gross water falls from 14 to 12 before release, the
allocation limit falls from 10 to 8 under the same protected flow.
Holding the optional package leaves the original uses in place
within the stated quantities. If any part has already become
legally binding, use the existing lawful water and contract
correction routes. Preserve original claims, record any unmet
receipt and identify who funds relief.

Ordinary local decisions are cheaper where effects are genuinely local.
A permanent elected basin or regional authority could hear durable
cases sooner if it has legitimate boundaries, money and practical
opposition. Neither model resolves a live dispute by itself. This
exercise establishes no actual hydrology, consent, prices or staffing.
