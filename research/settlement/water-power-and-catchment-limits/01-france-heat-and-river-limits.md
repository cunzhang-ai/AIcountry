# France 2022. Distinguish exceptional authority, use and observed effects

The French nuclear regulator's retrospective describes low river flow
and higher water temperatures during heat and drought. At several
sites, prior rules set a normal operating band and another band
for exceptional weather and a grid reliability need. There was
also a separate temporary process beyond those existing bands.
In summer at Bugey the prior bands reported in the regulator's
table use these two simultaneous constraints.

| Prior band for Bugey | Maximum downstream temperature after mixing | Maximum rise from upstream |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ordinary summer band | 26 °C | 5 °C |
| Exceptional weather and grid need | 27 °C | 1 °C |

The second band raises one ceiling and lowers the other. Treating
it as uniformly more permissive would lose a material constraint.
The values are historical source entries for that site and period.
They do not define AIcountry's protections or an operating procedure.
[ASN technical retrospective, pp. 2–3](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-water-power-and-catchment-limits/original-sources/asn-thermal-discharge-review-2022.pdf#page=2)

The retrospective identifies four temporary decisions covering five
plants in the period from 15 July to 11 September 2022. It records
use at four plants for **24 site-days** in total. Tricastin used the
temporary provisions on nine days, Bugey on eight, Golfech on six
and Saint-Alban on one. Blayais maintained production without using
them. Site-days count concurrent days separately. RTE highlights
Bugey's use on 19–20 July under the 15 July decision. The five-site
full-period totals here use the regulator's later site-by-site account.
[ASN retrospective, pp. 4–5](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-water-power-and-catchment-limits/original-sources/asn-thermal-discharge-review-2022.pdf#page=4)
· [RTE production review](https://analysesetdonnees.rte-france.com/bilan-electrique-production)

Monitoring results differed by site. The regulator reports no detected
upstream-to-downstream effects in the monitored parameters at Golfech
and Tricastin, and describes differences in biological observations at
Bugey and Saint-Alban. The report leaves causation unresolved where the
plant effect could not be distinguished from other ecological factors.
A recorded operating authorization, an inspection finding on compliance,
and absence or presence of ecological harm are different findings.
This evidence supports separate records for each. It does not show
which protected household or service received the additional power,
or what an alternative would have delivered at the same cost.
