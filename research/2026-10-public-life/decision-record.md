# Public life without state-managed belonging

4 October 2026 · Integrated design 33 research selection; not enacted law

Prepared by an AI assistant. No affected residents, cultural groups, local
officials, independent reviewers or authorizing electorate participated. The
[design-32 predecessor](predecessor-design-32/README.md) is preserved with
checksums. This record selects a working-country proposal, not a legal right
or an operating service.

## Bounded design question and diagnosis

C01 protects plural ways of living and private relationships. C05 protects
usable time; C09 plans land; C11 mentions diverse public spaces; C14 reports
social connection. The country did not assign a public duty to provide,
maintain and fairly allocate places where people can gather without buying
entry or joining an official group. The causal problem may be scarce time,
unsafe or inaccessible places, unaffordable venues, discrimination,
displacement, social distrust or unwanted isolation. A place alone cannot
resolve all of them. The selected rule addresses a public condition and
leaves relationships voluntary.

The [WHO Commission on Social Connection report](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/978240112360)
establishes the importance of social disconnection for health and wellbeing.
The [OECD's 2025 chapter on social infrastructure](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/social-connections-and-loneliness-in-oecd-countries_6df2d6a0-en/full-report/social-infrastructure-and-digitalisation-as-drivers-of-dis-connection-implications-for-measurement-and-policy_dd12ad00.html)
describes physical and community conditions that can support connection while
stating that evidence on population-level interventions remains limited.
[UN-Habitat's public-space programme](https://unhabitat.org/programme/global-public-space-programme)
offers a comparator for inclusive, accessible and maintained public-space
networks. None proves that this country's proposed institutional chain would
improve lives, or that every person's preferred association is public business.

## Alternative institutional packages

| Package | Benefit | Material failure or value conflict |
|---|---|---|
| Retain time, association rights and private or charitable places without a public-space duty | Preserves spontaneity and low state involvement | Money, land ownership, disability barriers and local exclusion can make formal freedom unusable |
| National cohesion service with official events and belonging targets | Can focus funds and identify isolated people | Turns a plural life into a state objective; creates surveillance, compelled participation and capture of group voice |
| Individual venue vouchers or digital matching | Allows personal choice and plural suppliers | Does not create safe nearby capacity, maintenance or transport; platform can gate social life and harvest relationship data |
| **Selected statutory access floor with locally chosen plural places** | Names a delivery authority, resource path, fair-use rule and remedy while keeping relationships voluntary | Local patrons can still control access; land and maintenance compete with essential needs; use may not yield connection |

The selected package adds [C11's shared-place rule](../country/C11/04-shared-places-and-association.md)
and connects it to local administration, spatial planning, usable time, lived
experience and outcome review. Elected law defines the floor and funding;
local councils choose sites and providers; named officers secure upkeep;
independent review handles discrimination, unlawful closure and procedural
claims. Ordinary civic groups retain their own voice, without a state registry
of relationships or a favored association claiming consent for everyone.

## Dependencies, objections and dissent

National equalization, local taxation, sites, accessibility works, staff,
maintenance, travel and energy must fit C06's actual shared account. Existing
land, cultural and ecological rights, leases, contracts and safe public use
cannot be replaced by an invented vacant parcel. During a change, lawful
ongoing access and claims continue under their current authority until a
funded successor exists. A council may close an unsafe site after fair
process and a real interim response; opposition cannot freeze every plan by
calling a place communal. Conversely, a token alternative across a dangerous
road is not effective continuity.

The strongest objection is that a public duty can formalize informal life and
make cultural survival dependent on local approval. The design therefore
limits the duty to access conditions, allows independent association and
private reflection, bars ideological loyalty tests and gives excluded users a
direct challenge. Another objection is that scarce funds should go first to
housing, care or food. The Assembly must compare the actual marginal package
and long-term maintenance under one account; this document does not assert
that a venue always outranks an unmet essential claim. A third objection is
that venue availability may be irrelevant to a person's isolation. C14 must
report mixed outcomes and dissent, not count openings as proof of connection.

## Status and next bounded test

Evidence qualification: the cited sources support concern and a plausible
public-space mechanism, with material uncertainty about population effects.
Research selection: the plural local network under a statutory access floor.
Legal authorization: none. Operational readiness: no territory, inventory,
budget, staff, provider or appeal capacity. Observed outcomes: none. AI and
human reviewers were not independent of this authored decision, and there was
no actual affected-party participation.

Next, in a real locality, compare the chosen floor with a serious private and
voucher alternative using original observations of reach, hours, exclusion,
travel, maintenance, displacement, informal use and resident effort. Include
people who avoid official venues. Test whether an unpopular lawful group can
book and retain access against a hostile council, and whether a closed site
has a usable successor. Reopen this selection if that comparison finds worse
access, greater capture, unmet higher-priority duties or a less intrusive way
to secure the same public conditions. Do not claim readiness until lawful
rights, finance, capacity and delivered remedies are verified separately.
