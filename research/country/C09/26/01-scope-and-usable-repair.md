# LCY01–LCY04. Scope and usable repair

[Product repair and material responsibility](../26-product-repair-and-material-responsibility.md)

**LCY01 — Attach responsibility to actual market placement.** Begin
with prospective statutory schedules for material household electrical
and general communications products. Each schedule states the class,
risks, reasonable support, actual tests, reporting and dates. Cover
the domestic manufacturer or commercial first placer of an imported
product within applicable jurisdiction. Require a reachable liable
domestic operator before covered commercial sale. A marketplace must
verify that contact when it controls the covered commercial listing
and use its actual statutory correction duties on credible notice.
It assumes first-placer duties if it actually supplies or imports
in that capacity. Preserve defenses and proportionate help for small
operators. Ordinary private resale and informal repair do not create
a second placement fee or a general licensing requirement. Existing
safety and civil liability continue.

**LCY02 — Disclose and deliver a repairable service life.** For initial
scheduled ordinary classes, propose a minimum seven calendar years
after the model's last first placement in the domestic market. The
schedule names actual available parts, instructions, diagnostics,
necessary firmware, safe security support and compatibility. Give the
buyer a conservative, enforceable availability date at sale, accessible
without an account. Update the public date when the last placement
extends it. Provide qualified repair within reasonable time and
documented fair terms. Keep warranty, recall and covered defect remedies
on their own stronger terms. Ordinary repair outside those terms can
carry a lawful price. Different classes need evidence and prospective
law for different duties. A label or supplier promise is no proof that
parts and paid repair are available.

**LCY03 — Preserve independent safe work and user choice.** Provide
timely, non-discriminatory access for owners and independent repairers
to parts and scoped diagnostic and pairing means, with competency
conditions only where justified by the actual risk. Accept lawful
compatible and refurbished parts that meet the applicable test. A
manufacturer restriction needs a specific safety, security or rights
reason, a less restrictive alternative assessment and usable independent
review. Protect legitimate intellectual property and security under
existing law. No release of a master key, customer records or unrelated
secrets follows. Preserve the user's option to repair, replace, share
or retire the product without losing stronger existing remedies.

**LCY04 — Make continuation independent of a vanished supplier.**
For covered classes where a controlled tool or online service is
material, require a tested independent repair or functional continuity
route at entry. Examples include supported local operation, limited
transferable tools or safeguarded release of the necessary capability
on defined cessation or default. Name lawful rights, recovery custody,
qualified operator, real staff and funding. A second reseller with
the same unavailable service key is not a substitute. Upon a proved
unsafe configuration use applicable TS and consumer remedies, with a
qualified alternative for independently owed essential service. No
continuity claim authorizes unsafe operation or irreversible disclosure.
