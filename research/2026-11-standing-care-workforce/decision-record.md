# Design490 decision record

**Scope and baseline.** Apply the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md)
to the Design489 [baseline](baseline.json). Compare staffing for a
bounded standing service on the same original and proposed care.
The fixture uses constructed dates, assignments and budgets.

**Selection and objections.** Select paid voluntary return and
independently assessed training for the first window when the
origin's paid cover, actual deployment and resident receiving
qualify. Separate later replacement from nominal qualification.
Where funded retention, safe redesign or a genuinely independent
ordinary staff pool supplies the capability, compare that option.
A better job may lawfully attract an existing worker. The staffing
plan must count the real origin loss without restricting mobility.

**Compatibility.** Continue ordinary care, paid work, binding teaching,
original dates, user choice and independent challenge. An optional
start needs its own enacted source and receiving conditions. A missed
start or unavailable skilled worker supplies no authority for coerced
care or unpaid replacement. Check shared rostering and competence
evidence without depending on one controller.

**Evidence and reopening.** The [case](../settlement/standing-care-workforce/01-pay-for-net-new-care-capacity.md)
and [sources](sources.md) qualify dates and scope conditionally.
Reopen on retirement, worker departure, actual user receipt or a better
retention route. The wider unresolved choice is how to fund shared
workforce formation when several essential sectors need the same
supervision, assessors and replacement shifts. Compare care and
school expansion alongside independent scrutiny on the same first
budget, with exact staged and competing elected motions. Actual participation,
adoption and effects remain unverified.
