# 212. Earnings records and portable correction

[Design304](../../2026-11-earnings-records-and-portable-correction/decision-record.md)
selects assisted proof and verified handoff across existing work,
insurance and income functions. [Y25–Y29](../../country/C07/04/07-earnings-records-and-portable-correction.md)
preserves separate authority, original time, earned rights and actual
receipt. It adds no cross-office power to decide all entitlements.

The [constructed comparison](../../settlement/earnings-records-and-portable-correction/README.md)
checks omitted covered work, failed employer remittance, correct
care-credit backing and a matched wage advance. It establishes no
actual review capacity, social insurance financing or benefit receipt.
