# Authored case. More responses and fewer timely remedies

Compare three mutually exclusive motions in one district. Keep
the same existing rights, original receipt law, urgent authority
and funded services. The cohorts, budget units and outcomes below
are constructed and do not use Copenhagen's volumes.

Forty distinct journeys start during one operating interval.
They include twelve general enquiries, twenty ordinary filings
and eight urgent requests. Group membership is verified through
the protected evaluation route. Repeat attempts belong to the same
journey. The outcome for a general enquiry is usable correct advice,
for an ordinary filing it is legally recognized timely receipt,
and for urgent protection it is actual timely receiving of the
required assistance. None is inferred from a line going quiet.

A standard compare uses the same affected scope and counts each
journey once. C completes 37 across the defined outcomes versus
36 under A, yet fails the requirement to protect all eight urgent
requests in this fixture. B improves general information without
losing a filing or urgent result in the ordinary path. Under a loss
of the common model and routing platform, B only matches A if
its independently staffed route, receipt proof and evidence are
actually usable. With a shared credential on the alternate route
the reference result does not transfer. Preserve the original
receipts and report the failed cases.

The incremental envelope is two early units, four cumulatively
and one qualified review slot. A can proceed on exact authority.
B requires a fresh, fully funded choice even with its expected
information gain. C cannot qualify as the same protection. A
wider funded B trial remains a real rival if ordinary service and
independent capacity hold. The ordinary route must stay useful
whether a person uses, declines or cannot access the assistant.
No forecast saving from B or C pays for any option.

For one urgent case, a timely valid request reaches the authorized
intake at minute 2. The model proposes a transfer at minute 5.
The receiver does not accept it until minute 28 and cannot deliver
before minute 35. The last useful time is minute 30. An independent
paid route accepting at minute 10 and delivering at minute 25
protects the person. Keep the minute-2 receipt in either branch.
If the public system loses the receipt, the ordinary independent
review assesses usable evidence and any lawful restoration. It
does not turn every anonymous enquiry into a claim.

A missed useful time remains a failure even if later assistance
arrives. Report the continuing duty and actual remedy. Later cuts,
changes of evidence retention or a new shared service need their own
qualification and competent decision.
