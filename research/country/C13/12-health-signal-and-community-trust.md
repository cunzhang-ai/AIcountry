# C13. A health signal in an excluded neighborhood

*Part of [C13 lived experience](../C13-lived-experience.md); integrated design 55 authored stress, not an observed event.*

A local wastewater sample and two clinical reports suggest a possible
infectious cluster. The signal may instead reflect sampling error or a
contaminated supply. Many nearby workers cannot take unpaid time off; some
residents fear that a health record will be used for immigration enforcement.
A national AI tool marks the neighborhood high risk, a minister asks for a
large movement restriction, and another locality requests the same laboratory
and cold-chain staff for its routine prevention programme.

The local public-health team verifies the original samples, obtains clinical
judgment and gives people accessible information, voluntary testing and a
safe route to care. The national service supplies bounded laboratory and
cross-place help, publishes uncertainty and protects source records. C11
keeps the changed method and missing-population evidence visible. The health
service cannot announce a compulsory order from the AI score. Any testing,
isolation or movement restriction needs C10's enacted ground, scope, support,
expiry and independent review; broad continuing effects return to elected
control. The same worker, van or cold store enters C06 once.

The public-health route cannot quietly send identifiable case data to an
immigration or police database. The clinical network still owes individual
care, the sanitation steward investigates a possible water fault, and the
employer owes applicable work and leave protections. The Assembly can fund
additional capacity or alter general law; neither it nor a technical expert
may falsely label a referral as a received service. Residents must be able
to report incorrect data, stigma, denied pay or care and obtain a usable
remedy without entering a single mandatory app.

The exercise fails if unverified data becomes coercion, fear hides cases,
prevention workers are double-booked, a minister suppresses a warning, or
an emergency is extended by renaming orders. It proves only that distinct
authorities are named; there is no real population or observed benefit.

adds a failed operator and a late realization of assets. It keeps early
water protection, repair, estate priority and residents' claims separate.
