# Design303. Common import permissions and continuity

10 November 2026 · Working selection · No actual enactment

## Baseline and decision

[Design302](baseline.json) requires actual currency and local receiving
for essential imports. Multiple bids can still count a single upstream
permission twice. When permission fails after prepayment, the original
claim and finance for timely replacement are different problems. A
supplier's additional terms may also exceed the buyer's authority.
Apply the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).
Codex assisted. No affected person, foreign producer, regulator or
independent reviewer participated.

## Alternatives and selection

- **Retain the normal order and count every distinct seller as a spare.**
  Low standing cost where inputs and permissions are actually diverse.
  In the comparison both named sellers depend on the failed permit.
  A separately funded spot purchase can work where cash arrives in time.
- **Buy real continuity through both windows.** Select a qualified
  rotating stock and independently executable reserved capacity in the
  example. Keep licensed receiving, gross payments and foreign cash
  by due date. Stage future payments or secure their advance where the
  cost and terms qualify. Preserve small supplier liquidity needs and
  existing contractual rights.
- **Bring production home or rely on regional reciprocal provision.**
  Both are genuine alternatives when labor, inputs, permissions and
  accepting operators qualify. The domestic surge needs more domestic
  cash in the example. A regional supplier needs a real commitment and
  sufficient donor cover under concurrent loss. Ownership or a promise
  cannot replace those conditions.

Evidence requirements could entrench large firms or expose sensitive
supplier information. Use scope and materiality, protected verification
and competition among actually usable alternatives. A stockpile or
reserved line can waste scarce cash or capacity. Show rotation,
foregone work, expiry and the next call. Political pressure remains
a fact to establish. Do not let purchasing act as diplomacy, legislation
or a licence exemption. Preserve narrow outside review and report
what remains unavailable under each decision.

## Authority, migration and next test

[NS26–NS30](../country/C06/54/07-common-import-permissions-and-continuity.md)
applies prospectively through existing budget, contract, regulatory and
treaty competence with any necessary express legislation. Preserve
due wages, earlier payment terms, independent release, remedies and
foreign claims. The [primary sources](sources.md) show why a supplier
name does not establish permission. The [scenario](../settlement/common-import-permissions/README.md)
tests assumed exposure rather than a real bidder.

For one essential item, compare two actual upstream permission paths
and a qualified domestic or cooperative alternative on the same two
useful dates. Establish own currency, original contract milestones,
safety and a concurrent donor call where relevant. Reopen after a
joint restriction, unusable compensation, stock expiry, withheld
source or better paid alternative. Actual commitments, readiness,
delivery and outcomes remain unverified.
