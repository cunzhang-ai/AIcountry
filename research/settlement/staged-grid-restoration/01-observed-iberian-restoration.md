# Observed restoration in Iberia after 28 April 2025

The ENTSO-E expert panel's final report dates the mainland blackout to
12:33 CEST on 28 April. Its Portugal chronology records early failed
starts, a trip after restarting and a new start in each of two separate
electrical areas. At 18:36 CEST an interconnection with Spain provided
a third restoration area connected synchronously to continental Europe.
The two local islands joined this synchronous area at 20:01 and 20:22.
The interconnection schedule was 0 MW when automatic generation control
was activated at 20:25. This illustrates the distinction between
the electrical function of a connection and a booked delivery of energy.
[Final report, §§5.3.1 and 5.5.2, pp. 364–369 and 386](../../2026-11-staged-grid-restoration-and-essential-receipt/original-sources/entso-e-iberian-blackout-final-report.pdf#page=364)

| Area | Transmission restoration | Distribution restoration | Interval between reported endpoints |
|---|---|---|---|
| Portugal | 00:22 on 29 April | 04:00 on 29 April | 3 h 38 min |
| Spain | 04:00 on 29 April | 07:00 on 29 April | 3 h |

The entries follow the report's time basis. The intervals use same-country
pairs and require no local-time conversion. In Portugal the transmission
endpoint marks reconnection of the last delivery point, Divor substation.
For Spain the report also describes early support through France at 12:43
and through Morocco at 13:04, alongside autonomous hydroelectric starts.
[Final report, §5.3.1 and §§5.5.1–2, pp. 371, 381 and 386](../../2026-11-staged-grid-restoration-and-essential-receipt/original-sources/entso-e-iberian-blackout-final-report.pdf#page=381)

The report measures electricity restoration stages. It does not give a
matched record of water quality at each service boundary, clinical access
or communications for specific residents. These observations support
tracking distinct handoffs and testing early and late paths. They do not
establish the cost, clinical effect or superiority of the proposed portfolio.
The event record is one evidence family assembled from operator records
by an expert panel. [Source and inference scope](../../2026-11-staged-grid-restoration-and-essential-receipt/sources.md)
