# C03. Forming and replacing a government when no party wins

*Part of [C03 democratic government](../C03-democratic-government.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

An Assembly election chooses lawmakers, not a prime minister by inference. The
reference election has no legal party threshold and can produce several groups
without a majority. A current cabinet cannot turn failed coalition talks into
a renewed electoral mandate. The first formation rule must be enacted by the
legitimate founding or predecessor authority **before** the Assembly election;
later lawmakers may change only future formation rules within constitutional
competition and anti-self-dealing limits.

## An Assembly choice with a visible failure route

The outgoing cabinet enters the limited caretaker role at the start of the
ordinary election period under a date fixed by prior law. The new Assembly
has a convening date set before polling. For a lawfully constituted
Assembly, that date starts formation even if the sitting is obstructed.
An unresolved or annulled Assembly mandate follows the applicable election
law and supplies no second formation trigger. The old cabinet stays within
its caretaker powers when its successor is contested. Elected groups and independents may nominate
qualified candidates through objective, accessible
member sponsorship. The Speaker consults every group, publishes candidates,
programmes, proposed coalition and support declarations, and schedules open
votes. The Speaker cannot suppress a lawful nomination, invent a candidate or
extend a clock by refusing the first ballot. Exact sponsorship, vote and
challenge rules are fixed in advance and reviewed independently. A
preauthorized deputy conducts a mandatory ballot if the Speaker refuses,
with urgent court review of any obstruction.

For the first **21 days** from the applicable convening date or an actual
constitutional vacancy under HT28, a candidate receiving more than half of
the Assembly's full membership is elected prime minister. Use the operative
event's legal date. A late record provides evidence and cannot move the
start. Use the independent alternate when the ordinary custodian is blocked.
Disclose any obstructed act on its original date and seek timely review.
If no candidate wins a majority, the fallback tests a minority cabinet
that the Assembly can tolerate:
members each name one candidate in an open ballot; the highest-supported
candidate must have at least one quarter of the full Assembly and is then
tested in a separate vote. That candidate is elected unless **more than half
of all members vote against**. If rejected, the next highest-supported
candidate is tested; a rejected candidate cannot be recycled in the same
formation round. Four actual lawful rejections trigger the first repeat
earlier. Otherwise, if there is no effective lawful investiture by
**day 30**, an automatic first repeat is called then under prior law and
any operative court order under that law. Record separately whether
nominations failed, nominations were rejected or a required vote was
obstructed. Preserve the substitute sitting and direct challenge.
The proposed ER01 calendar starts voting 76 calendar days after the
trigger and closes at 90, with protected entry and preparation. The
judgment-based ER17 rule retains its separate trigger. The Speaker and
electoral authority perform their specified duties, with independently
reachable alternate publication and scrutiny. The caretaker has no
discretion to call or waive the required poll. Tied candidate support requires
a public runoff; an unresolved tie cannot be awarded by the Speaker.
Withdrawals, absences, failed quorum, dispute stays and accessible voting
need pre-enacted treatment; a clock alone cannot elect someone. The
21/30/90-day periods and one-quarter
floor are hypotheses requiring a real timetable, staff and public choice.
The recorded investiture vote itself confers office under the valid
constitution; no ceremonial appointer may veto the Assembly's choice.

This fallback gives no minority cabinet a blank cheque. It must obtain the
Assembly's votes for new law, tax, appropriation, treaty and material shared
choices. A majority of the full Assembly may replace a prime minister at once
by electing a successor. The same majority may also dismiss a prime minister
without a successor; the cabinet then becomes caretaker and the public
formation process restarts. The latter right prevents an unpopular minority
cabinet from surviving solely because its opponents disagree on a replacement.
Recorded member sponsorship and a short prior notice guard against a surprise
vote, without barring urgent legal accountability. Resignation, incapacity
and an ordinary election also start the same lawful succession route.

## Caretaker powers and continuing obligations

A caretaker may carry out already enacted law, due benefits and judgments,
essential existing contracts, ordinary merit-service continuity and narrowly
preauthorized immediate safety duties. It may provide factual estimates and
proposals to the Assembly. It may not initiate new general policy, grant itself
new delegated power, make discretionary senior appointments, dispose of major
public assets, bind a material treaty, or redirect security forces to prolong
its tenure. Existing emergency law can still authorize a necessary bounded
act, with its ordinary expiry and review; the caretaker label creates no new
emergency power. Named career officers and courts have their own duties and
review routes. The elected Assembly remains able to legislate and supervise
until a lawful successor Assembly convenes; a repeat-poll call does not give
the old cabinet control of parliamentary records or opposition access.

The [C06 continuing-obligations schedule](../C06/04-shortfall-and-continuity.md)
funds enacted essential duties; a separate, nonrenewable bridge covers only
specified existing indispensable operations for its lawful term. Fiscal and
formation clocks are distinct. Treasury publishes actual cash, arrears and
unmet claims, while Assembly members can propose a lawful budget or limited
continuation. Neither a caretaker nor a court can create a tax, renew a bridge
or treat a missing appropriation as cancellation of accrued rights. A lawful
new cabinet receives command, records, treasury access and contracts through
named civil and security successors after the recorded result; outgoing
refusal is reviewable but no delay invents a different winner.

One automatic repeat applies per formation impasse. A lawful investiture
ends that impasse. If formation fails again after its repeat, report the
constitutional crisis and put the exact unresolved questions before
the lawful Assembly and public review. A further early election needs
separate lawful authority. [GF01–GF05](06/01-national-recovery-after-a-failed-repeat.md)
proposes a prospective voter-triggered choice and conditional proportional
replacement. It requires protected constitutional adoption and independent
funding before any future qualifying contest. Regular election and office clocks continue. The narrow
caretaker and existing lawful claims continue while the public can use the
protected amendment and opposition routes; no rule can guarantee agreement
without removing people's power to disagree. The [decision
record](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-government-formation/decision-record.md) compares
other formation models and names what would reopen this hypothesis.

[ER16–ER17](10/07-result-validity-and-repeat-poll.md) now prospectively defines new-national-contest annulment after formation: affected mandates cease, a cabinet formed on the fully annulled investiture becomes limited caretaker, fixed N/severable lawful seats and particular old-act remedies persist, and a lawful repeat closes90days after final judgment with at least60days before first voting. No old Assembly, guessed winner or judicial government is revived.

[DB ordinary appropriation](../C06/60-democratic-budget-and-execution.md)
retains member proposals during formation, actual standing duties/90-day bridge
and separate confidence votes. A failed budget is no automatic dismissal or
renewed term, and new method clocks do not reset existing claims.

[HT01–HT12](19-lawful-handover-and-actual-control.md) implements actual
access at the legally operative act. Preparation, review, failed credentials
and late receipt do not extend or confer office. Ordinary caretaker and
standing powers retain their existing limits.

[GP01–GP08](20-government-programme-and-public-choices.md) connects published formation terms to a reconciled programme within 30 days of lawful appointment. Existing formation, replacement, caretaker and budget rules continue.

[BLC01–BLC05](../C06/66-budget-lapse-and-paid-continuity.md) specifies the
standing schedule and a separately enacted closeout at budget lapse.
A formation result, missed vote or caretaker action cannot restart its
clock or alter eligible payments. Independent execution and a late elected
appropriation keep their own authority.

[HT13–HT17](19/04-caretaker-commitments-and-public-choice.md)
reconciles old liabilities, unmade proposals and narrowly authorized
continuity. A long future effect does not become routine through
an early announcement or later effective date.

[HT24–HT31](19/06-executive-incapacity-and-bounded-succession.md)
separates temporary cover from a real vacancy. Keep the ordinary
formation and confidence powers, and start their clocks only on
their own lawful events.
