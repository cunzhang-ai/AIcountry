# Universal cash and mutual delivery in the compound comparison

*Design536 · Scoped finding*

The [five-arrangement comparison](../../settlement/first-round-mandate/07-cash-and-mutual-rivals-on-the-same-week.md)
prices universal cash and voluntary mutual delivery on the same useful
deadlines as the other three routes. Universal cash can avoid a missed
renewal when proof fails and a lawful provisional payment cannot reach the
person. Mutual delivery can tie the chosen budget with independent paid
support and a nonmember route. Actual tax capacity, staff, rights protection
and comparative resident outcomes require independent evidence.
