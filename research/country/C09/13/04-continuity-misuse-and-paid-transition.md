# LA16–LA20. Continuing obligations, misuse and paid change

*Design145 · [Public acquisition](../13-public-acquisition-and-secure-relocation.md) · No actual project, receiving capacity or superiority established.*

**LA16 — Preserve the economic and personal-capital boundaries.** Compelled title
exchange/sale is not tax-free custody transfer. Actual acquired consideration,
old indexed basis, actual costs and new actually paid asset basis follow TA/PA/NF;
credit source tax once. Private property converted to cash already recognized in
I05 assets A is not new income O merely because its form changed. Actually newly
received replacement/loss-of-income components need their source-law classification,
with no same-period O/A duplication or invented receipt from blocked court funds.
Inaccessible/disputed assets remain inaccessible pending actual authority/access;
missing records call for AP help/review, not default0basis or family pooling.
Separate resource capital from taxable real gain and from continuing earned Y
rights; an indemnity label creates no new pension accrual, income disregard or
exemption. Replacing an asset is not new national output of the same amount.

**LA17 — After possession, the duties still exist.** The actual public owner carries
approved purpose, maintenance, ecological restoration and safe records. Contracted
operators/workers/creditors keep actual source rights and paid OP/T successors;
a new project title or territorial authority does not novate their claims. Revisit
replacement access and livelihood restoration with affected people, including
those absent from the original register, until actual due obligations are met or
lawfully changed. Resale, rent collection or a signed handoff does not prove this.
Meaning/consent and claims survive changed communication media and provider exit.

**LA18 — Unused land and changed purpose.** On material abandonment, surplus land or
proposed private onward sale, publish the actual reason and map, notify original
holders/tenants/collective claimants and examine restoration, return, reuse and
continuing harm. A different compulsory public use needs a new elected/rights/
source decision; a favorable developer offer is not authorization. Where disposal
is lawful and no superior actual collective/title claim bars it, offer the former
lawful holder a90-calendar-day first opportunity after usable lawful notice to reacquire at independently
assessed current lawful value with actual condition/restoration costs disclosed.
Credit only proved unpaid amounts due to that holder, not previously paid
compensation a second time. Existing tenants/other interests remain; a discounted
sale cannot erase public cleanup or wrongful-taking duties. The offer waives no wrongful-taking claim and
creates no free windfall or compulsory repurchase. Other actual contract/public-
asset disposal and open-award rules remain; no secret transfer to a sponsor.

Wrongful original purpose/procedure retains B restoration and actual compensation
routes with third-party safeguards; this prospective resale rule cannot legitimize
an unlawful taking. A later legitimate change is not automatically proof the
original decision was fraudulent. Fault, title, available restoration and source
require actual findings, not an AI project-success score.

**LA19 — Pay the whole independent chain.** Existing local/national land and service
owners, independent qualified valuers, ordinary courts/counsel, Treasury/project
payers and civil executors need actual concurrent staff/time, source records,
translations/offline/other-provider access, safe replacement sites, host services,
physical inputs, maintenance, compensation/deposit liquidity and long-tail remedies.
An owner cannot be forced to finance the state by an unpaid award; general sector
funds/old37 bids are not spare capacity. Distinguish project capital purchases,
transfers, ordinary operating loss and real new work/FX; internal public tax does
not pay twice. A court order creates neither site nor money. If protected source/
receiving fails, redesign, delay or stop the taking while earlier service/rights
claims continue; no emergency or proponent's readiness label cures the failure.

**LA20 — Dates, compatibility and reopening.** The ordinary reference starts180calendar
days after a competent coordinated Act, with actual jurisdiction, source inventory,
component dates, ongoing orders/claims, title/creditor rights, budgets/workers and
accepted successors identified. Applicable constitutional/RR holds require the
later explicit lawful date for affected components; no presumed restoration or
readiness certificate bypasses them. Existing stronger/earlier rights and due
compensation continue; new/mutated acquisitions after commencement use LA, with
an express valid transition for pending cases. No research repeal, retrospective
offense, erased liability or software/contribution permission follows.

Reopen on qualified displacement/host harm, exclusion, false purpose, unaffordable
replacement, source/court overload or a better actual cooperative/public/market
rival. Replacement-first may make worthwhile projects expensive or impossible;
acquisition can still dominate vulnerable people. No consulted public, independent
review, complete national cost/source or observed better lives is claimed. Next
repair the separately ranked public-media control/finance gap unless new evidence
makes another whole-country omission more consequential.
