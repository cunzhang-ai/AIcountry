# Design112: local law and public control

2 November2026 · Research choice, no municipal or national authority granted

Read [the current country comparison](../settlement/local-law/README.md) and
[LR01–LR24](../country/C03/14-local-law-and-public-control.md). The five canonical
parts cover powers/fixed criteria, input/vote/effect, agenda/urgency/continuity,
actual courts/payers/execution and capacity/rivals/transition. This package is
support for that country decision, not another national blueprint.

- [Decision](decision-record.md): actual baseline, competing diagnoses, selection,
  material objections, compatibility/continuing duties and bounded next action.
- [Model](model.py) and [results](results.json): exact authored consultation/final
  dates, full-n votes, maker/publication, NF19 timing, source/72h urgency, fixed
  formula boundary, petition counts and workload failure. No real semantic or
  legal classifier, local public opinion or source acceptance is inferred.
- [Primary source scope](sources.json): Charter Articles4/8/9 and official OECD
  checklist for limited questions, not our dates, law adoption or compliance.
- [Scope](scope-record.json), [exact manifest](source-manifest.json), [checks](validate.py)
  and [validation](validation.json): bounded identity/timing/semantic hypotheses,
  not independent evidence qualification, funded readiness or wellbeing.
- [Baseline](baseline.json):807 exact111 artifacts,806 current Markdown and the
  commitment inventory, copied before consequential changes. Dated editions
  remain immutable; old final reviews/quoted capacity do not transfer.

Run model.py then validate.py. Scenario dates/actors/authority/effects and all
workload hours/allotments are hypotheses, not actual cases or judgments. Draft
and input dates are checked against declared elapsed days rather than accepted
as a label. Unknown/unlawful/changed scope, missing final packet, insufficient
full-n vote, unmade/late signature, notice or NF deadline never becomes an active
norm because a clock passed. A mayor does not get an assent veto on the council's
own ordinance. Required separate statutory making remains real, not a fake act.
The14-day disposition duty is not a perpetual official veto: late real reasons
can precede a prospective complete L, retaining delay liability and unchanged
harder source deadlines. Valid input is not restarted solely to excuse a delay;
changed effects/evidence/objections still need the affected renewed process.

This deliberately rejects copying national DR's routine rule-by-notice path
locally, with its genuine lower-vote/capacity advantage retained as a rival.
Fixed enacted criteria can update proved facts; changing weights/objectives/
fallbacks is a rule, even when a quoted result is cheaper. Workload can defeat
the selected procedure. It grants no claim-expiry quota or new executive power.

A current-state semantic drift was found:111 C03/01 still used a compensatory-
count sentence despite111 EL single national PR selection.112 corrects that
sentence;111 checks were not a semantic proof. The exact old artifact remains
in the snapshot. This is no assertion of exhaustive full-country consistency.

Actual local public/jurisdiction, enactment, independent affected assessment,
payer cash, qualified simultaneous staff, safe actual remedy and improved lives
remain unknown. Next design the nonbank financial perimeter/consumer protection;
full production/source/credit/VAT/price/FX/legacy/pension joins remain substantive.
