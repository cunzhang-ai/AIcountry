# KR25–KR28: effective civil relief and a bounded commercial offence

*Design 95 research candidate; not enacted law, a funded offer or demonstrated
superiority.*

**KR25 — Civil enforcement.** Ordinary courts, not the granting office or ministry,
decide infringement, validity defenses and proportional remedies with accessible
small-claim routes, affordable representation, proof disclosure, protective
confidentiality and appeal. A process-patent identical-product presumption is limited to
a new product or proved substantial likelihood plus failed reasonable efforts to
determine the process; protect the defendant's manufacturing secrets while allowing
rebuttal. Relief may include correction/attribution, reasonable license payment, proved
loss or accountable profits without double recovery, time-limited injunction and
disposal of infringing stock. A provisional block needs urgency, evidence, proportionate
security that does not exclude urgent protection, and rapid challenge; wrongful measures
trigger compensation. Courts consider essential care, food, repair, competition and
third-party dependence before an injunction and may use a lawful paid substitute where
the specific law permits; they cannot create a general free license or cancel rights to
solve an unfunded service promise. Civil infringement alone is not a crime; KR27 is a
separate closed offense. Customs may temporarily hold suspected counterfeit-mark or
pirated-copy goods only under an express application/security, notice, short release
clock, independent review and wrongful-hold compensation; an unexamined patent assertion
is no automatic border veto. The markets authority may address anti-competitive
licensing under its own mandate, not invalidate patents by informal request. The
registry is not a compulsory internal appeal before direct courts. Generic correctly
labelled products, lawful quotation and actual exceptions are not infringement merely
because a powerful holder demands takedown. Privately issued automated notices cannot
decide title or authorize a general site/server/communications shutdown.

**KR26 — Safe evidence and meaningful answer.** Trade secrets protect genuinely
confidential information under the selected substantive law, not facts/skills generally
known or a universal concealment privilege. B14 courts can require necessary
proportionate protected disclosure to the parties' qualified advisers/experts, narrowly
scoped safe testing and an intelligible answerable gist; use closed custody/redaction
where justified. An unanswerable secret or proprietary AI conclusion cannot decide an
adverse civil finding. Protect privilege, self-incrimination, personal data and safe
witnesses. Publishing the reason for a legal decision is different from unrestricted
publication of source code, clinical records or security exploits. Confidential safety
evidence remains available to the competent TS authority within its lawful mandate;
secrecy creates no safety exemption.

**KR27 — Closed commercial counterfeiting/piracy offence, candidate K25.** Only after
prospective enactment, prohibit deliberately making/importing/supplying goods under an
identical or essentially indistinguishable protected mark for the same registered
goods/services, knowing the right and intending false source; or knowingly unauthorized
reproduction/distribution of protected works/phonograms as an organized supply operation
principally for material commercial gain from those infringing copies. Prove a protected
domestic right, lack of licence or valid exception, the actor’s conduct/knowledge/intent
and commercial scale beyond reasonable doubt. Scale means at least 100 distinct infringing physical articles/copies made for
sale or supplied, counted once rather than again at sale, or gross received/binding
customer-contracted value directly attributable to the prohibited operation of one
annual Y05 median full-time wage W in a rolling 12 months. For purely digital
operations use only the proved value limb, including attributable advertising;
storage, availability, downloads, repeated access or account counts do not become
100 physical units. Prove the underlying reproduction/distribution and knowledge
separately; aggregate only the proved related operation.
W is not PA’s separately indexed tax scale M. These thresholds are normative, not a
proven treaty interpretation. Ordinary unpaid sharing, repair, research, uncertain
title, a patent dispute, an AI flag and correctly labelled generics supply no such
offence. Good-faith legally available exceptions remain a defence; mere advertising
elsewhere does not prove the operation’s prohibited principal purpose.

**KR28 — Finite response and execution.** Select no IP-only custody. K25 expressly
extends P/Q fair process, territorial/foreign-finality rules and personal/corporate
attribution to this offence: a natural person’s same-course principal response is zero
to 60 Q02 means-tested units, or a genuinely chosen Q04 community alternative within its
existing bounds, never both full penalties. Q03 protects needs, caps collection and ends
penal residue after 24 months; nonpayment creates no prison or compelled labour. Legal
bodies require Q07 proof and Q08’s lower band: a proportionate fine no greater than the
larger of 2% verified prior-year defendant revenue or twice proved net illicit gain.
Q09–Q12 narrow correction, proved proceeds/implements, no duplicate recovery, record
limits and a three-year charge/usable-notice period expressly apply, including their
bounded concealment/child/finality protections. Civil cessation and actual victim
compensation remain separate. No automatic whole-group revenue, arbitrary asset
destruction, broad internet ban or unproved worker/director guilt. Safety fraud, bodily
harm or coercion retains its distinct offence and no double punishment. The fine-only
choice can underdeter an impecunious organizer; tailored cessation, proved proceeds and
proportionate activity restriction need actual executors, not disguised debt-to-custody.
Compatibility/deterrence and numeric thresholds need qualified review; no fine is
forecast national income.
