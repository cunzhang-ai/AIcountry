# Adversarial reasoning: authority and aid must both be real

Design144 · Authored situations, not observations, eligible persons or legal orders

| Situation | Candidate disposition and unresolved cost |
|---|---|
| Flood destroys water/roads | Named civilian rescue and technical isolation use actual sector grounds; qualified reserves under scarcity law, paid shelter/income/medicine. Declaration cannot invent an appropriation, worker or new priority. Unmet protection remains actual failure. |
| A dangerous disease prompts a worship-site closure | Right-specific prior health law, original evidence, necessity, alternatives and timely review are required. Inner belief remains protected; nonderogability alone does not prohibit every physical access restriction. A closure grants no forced testing/treatment power. |
| Cabinet renews an urgent general rule on emergency day8 | DR13's earlier source/calendar-day7 expiry controls.14elapsed declaration days and three-fifths renewal are neither DR approval nor an Act. Continued general effect needs the lawful normal route or prospective primary law; earlier breach is not cured. |
| Ministers serially rename one emergency | Count the union of related operative intervals and actual initial wall-clock; overlapping regions do not double-count or reset. Timely actual full-N votes and residual90-day budget apply; unfamiliar factual linkage remains reviewable, not an AI-certified new threat. |
| Parliament wants a new hazard power on day2 | It can enact prospective rights-compatible primary legislation through its valid procedure. No need to wait90days or call a declaration first. It cannot suspend constitutional rights, create retrospective guilt or evade protected electoral succession. |
| Government closes all opposition forums for health | Protect actual political function through usable lawful alternatives and independent review. A formally neutral instrument can cumulatively extinguish opposition and therefore fail. State slogans or aggregate health benefit supply no constitutional override. |
| A population movement restriction persists beyond90calendar days | The new ordinary RX08 sunset requires fresh primary law with actual public reasons/source, independent relief and weekly reassessment; serial executive instruments cannot continue it. Repeated law can still harm people; sunset is no social-progress proof. |
| A detainee's court/connection fails | P09 actual24continuous-hour ground/hearing/release rule remains. Its source may require release absent a timely lawful order; declaration gives no holding extension. Preauthorized independent alternate hearing/counsel needs actual equivalents, never fictitious review. Safety cost survives. |
| A dependent patient's UA order expires | UA16's independently bounded minimum dependent continuity prevents expiry-triggered unsafe withdrawal. It is not an emergency renewal, present-refusal override or new elective course. Its missed-review/prolonged-unconsented-care objection remains visible. |
| Whole court or Assembly loses lawful capacity | Existing ordinary preauthorized individual protection/safety may continue in actual scope. No substitute judge, lower quorum, new term or sovereign AI. A society may be unable to deliver protection: record failed control, claims and legitimate recovery need. |

These judgments use assumed facts. Real source law, protected evidence, affected
accounts and independent judicial decisions can change disposition. Common causes
of failure across nominally separate offices require actual independent source,
records, funding and execution; multiplying offices/accounts supplies none.
The no-derogation choice is not proved superior in every catastrophe. Its rejected
narrow derogation rival and simpler all-ordinary-law rival remain in the decision.
