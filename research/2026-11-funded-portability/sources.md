# Evidence for funded portable essential support

- Sweden's [Social Services Act, SFS 2025:400](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/socialtjanstlag-2025400_sfs-2025-400/), chapter 29 sections 4–8, is the current legal comparison. The survey was conducted under the predecessor act. The [minister's parliamentary answer of 17 June 2026](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svar-pa-skriftlig-fraga/ersattning-i-samband-med-tillfallig-hemtjanst_hd12878/) describes voluntary full-cost arrangements as lawful and already used in some municipalities. It does not measure coverage, receivable timing or resident outcome.
- The Swedish National Board of Health and Welfare's [2025 report](https://www.socialstyrelsen.se/contentassets/b687a7ca75c84711b36335bd767045ea/2025-4-9562.pdf), article 2025-4-9562, is preserved as the [original PDF](original-sources/sweden-temporary-home-care-2025.pdf). Main report page 13 and Table 1 give 155 of 186 respondents, and 1,509 people reported by 146 municipalities. Appendix 1 page 32 identifies five additional partial respondents and missing holiday municipalities. Appendix 2 page 34 uses 191 and the same numerator of 155 for the initial host question. Appendix 2 page 36 reports 24 of 150 answering yes to at least one wholly or partly unfulfilled request and 42 of 150 reporting increased net costs. Table 9 and the appendix record the cost categories as multiple responses from those 42. These are separate denominators and municipal reports. Neither the number of unfulfilled people nor the proportion of all eligible residents without care is identified. Sweden's effect after the new act is outside the survey window.
- The Australian [NDIS guidance on changes of circumstances](https://ndis.gov.au/participants/changing-your-plan/ways-change-your-plan/how-tell-us-about-change-situation) describes notice on moving, including interstate, and gives a move to a different city as an example that does not by itself affect the plan. Material changes in needs can change the plan. This is an example of portability of individual funding, with no measure here of a worker or service actually received.

Reported costs and survey cells are descriptive. The worked monetary quantities
are constructed and neither price the Swedish system nor imply a national service
failure rate. The report's checksum is in [the source manifest](source-manifest.json).
