# Copenhagen. The same year of housing activity and public accounts

## Read the municipal gross lines first

The Social Committee's March 2026 record approved the Social
Administration's 2025 accounts and carry-forward request at that stage.
Use the explanatory annex and its corrections, in million DKK. In the
table below revenue is displayed as a positive receipt, the reverse of
the source's revenue sign.

| 2025 accounting line | Adjusted budget | Reported outturn | Difference at displayed precision |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Demand-driven transfers, total revenue | 835.3 | 808.0 | 27.3 less revenue |
| Demand-driven transfers, total expense | 729.1 | 723.0 | 6.1 less expense |
| Central reimbursement, revenue within the first row | 563.0 | 535.8 | 27.2 less revenue |
| Demand-driven transfers, net expense | −106.2 | −85.0 | 21.2 more net expense by subtraction |

The report publishes 21.3 more net expense. Preserve that figure and a
0.1 difference from displayed arithmetic. The administration links the
reimbursement shortfall primarily to lower eligible activity, including
Housing First, and reports accounting placement effects across product
lines. The annex also reports 19.7 of Housing First underspending on the
expense side of a different social services appropriation, linked to
difficulty obtaining suitable homes. The two differences do not produce
a matched Housing First net balance. The wider transfer account includes
other activities. Its subline is already counted in its total.
[Committee decision and annexes](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Socialudvalget/m%C3%B8de-18032026/referat/punkt-2)

## Compare stages with their own denominators

A municipal September 2026 annex reports 1,206 units received for
social allocation in 2025, excluding the stated municipal-owned
housing route. It records 249 returned, about 20.6%. The remaining
units cannot be treated as observed moves. The same annex separately
reports 180 Housing First support starts in 2025 versus 68 in 2024
in its allocation-support figures. The meeting record reports 328
allocations from homelessness in 2025. These counts describe different
scopes. Neither ratio nor subtraction produces a 2025 support coverage
rate for the 328, actual occupancy or sustained tenancy.

A separate September annex records the first signed private allocation
agreement, with CPH Village. It creates the possibility of allocating
student accommodation to up to ten vulnerable young people over two
years, with first allocations expected in November 2026. Use the scope
and ceiling of this specific agreement. The reviewed September record
does not report actual placements or prove a generally available
permanent option. The procurement annex also describes the supply of
new homes as a separate route with a different calendar.
[September committee record and annexes 2.b and 3](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Socialudvalget/m%C3%B8de-16092026/referat/punkt-4)

This case qualifies the need to test supply, gross local finance and
actual receipt together. It does not disclose the payable date of the
specific cohort advance, a unit-level accepted affordability schedule
or a matched long-run cost for a new cohort. Keep the historical
accounting and these open operating facts separate.
