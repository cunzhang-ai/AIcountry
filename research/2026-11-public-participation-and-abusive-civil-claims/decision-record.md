# Design377 decision on public participation and abusive civil claims

## Baseline and selected route

Pin Design376, the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md), B17–B20,
AX, CI, OA and OJ. These protect open expression, lawful records,
privacy, remedies and useful-time review of restrictions. They do
not yet establish an early civil path when the litigation burden
itself threatens lawful participation.

[APS01–APS07](../country/C08/13/08-public-participation-and-abusive-civil-claims.md)
selects early ordinary court review, paid independent response,
severance of mixed claims and actual execution. The Assembly sets
civil scope and resources. Ordinary courts retain merits and
relief. Underlying public decisions, court appointments, evidence,
cost and remedies keep their competent owners.

## Alternatives and objections

Ordinary case management and legal assistance can preserve valid
claims but may leave the civic opportunity lost. A targeted early
route requires fresh paid capacity and disciplined proof. Broader
civil process reform, or a public-defense support fund alone,
can reduce burdens across disputes. Each has a different cost,
power and missed-opportunity effect. Compare existing protection,
the proposed court route and process reform under the same facts,
dates and valid rights.

An early motion can itself be used to exhaust a genuine claimant
or suppress evidence. Require a concrete participation nexus, safe
limited proof, meaningful reply and appeal. A protected topic
does not confer immunity. A pleading that fails early does not
establish abusive intent. Keep privacy, safety and fair trial
remedies independently available. Protect low-resource claimants
and defendants and fund counsel before a later costs decision.
The proposed 2- and 15-working-day limits and single extension
are policy choices requiring actual workload and fairness tests.

## Evidence, migration and next action

[Primary records](sources.md) show distinct outcomes under one
existing procedure and another regulatory model. The
[constructed case](../settlement/public-participation-and-abusive-civil-claims/01-two-claims-before-a-public-hearing.md)
tests independent review, actual delivery and claim severance.
Actual public participation, authority, court capacity and
improvements remain unknown.

Preserve pending cases, legal aid, confidential records, valid
privilege, original receipt dates and judgments through a
prospective change. A bounded next assessment would run one
synthetic mixed case through an independent court, legal aid and
a forum's receiving path. End at actual time and attributed cost
for each disposition and delivery. Reopen if genuine claimants
are deterred, relief arrives after civic deadlines, or qualified
ordinary reform provides the same protection at lower burden.
