# Two care rosters and one pay correction

*Design384 · Constructed comparison with [primary records](../../2026-11-workplace-ai-and-effective-worker-control/sources.md).*

The ordinary qualified roster uses 84 paid hours of care and
16 paid hours of scheduling each week. A proposed scheduler claims
10 hours saved because its interface shows 6 scheduling hours.
In this comparison it also shifts 6 hours of schedule checking to
workers outside paid time and leaves 4 hours of follow-up with
residents. It has neither a valid deployment agreement nor the
alternative independent authorization. Counting the total time gives
100 hours, with a worse distribution and unqualified authority.

The workers propose a narrower, reversible tool. It leaves the
same 84 paid care hours, with 8 scheduling and 4 qualified paid
review hours. Its real four-hour release is a test assumption.
On valid agreement and proof of equal actual care coverage, the
proposal may convert those hours into paid time off at retained
regular pay. Reverting after tool loss needs the original four
hours back in the operating schedule, backed by available paid
cover and lawful terms. No time already earned is taken back.

One worker is removed from a paid shift after two systems repeat
the same mistaken absence inference. The original work record
shows the worker was present. The urgent route examines those
records independently. A valid corrective decision is made on
day 3. Restored roster access must be received before the next
shift on day 4 and due pay before day 5. Both require distinct
verification. A correction reaching payroll on day 6 leaves a
late payment visible even if access has been restored.

| Path | Result |
|---|---|
| Retain ordinary work | Keeps the 100-hour plan and avoids the new inference path. It needs the original competent and funded staff. |
| Accept the vendor headline | Counts 10 hours that were transferred to workers and residents. The hours and approval do not qualify. |
| Qualified worker proposal | Uses 96 task hours while retaining service and testing four paid hours released, subject to actual independent approval or agreement and receipt. |
| Nominal second review or late correction | Same-source scores do not verify the worker's status. A valid decision alone does not restore the shift and payroll in useful time. |

No figures estimate an actual deployment effect. The comparison
keeps safety, pay, care receipt, representative authority and
real time separate. A worker's otherwise valid claim does not
depend on showing the system fails on average.
