# Two tests of court delay

The cases and resource numbers below are authored. They do not
estimate actual Danish justice costs or appropriate waiting times.

## Equal closure counts and opposite effects on old cases

Assume ten open matters can be validly decided and each requires
one equivalent unit of available time. Their ages at the start of
the exercise are 2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4, 9, 10, 11 and 12 months.
No case has an earlier legal or urgent priority. The court can
complete four matters in this period. Use starting ages to
isolate the choice, without adding the same elapsed period to both.

| Choice | Mean age of closed cases | Oldest remaining case |
|---|---:|---:|
| Close the four youngest | 2.5 months | 12 months at the start |
| Close the four oldest | 10.5 months | 4 months at the start |

Both end with six pending cases. The lower mean can be achieved
while leaving every older matter waiting. Real judges still apply
the actual law and urgency and consider preparation, complexity,
safe receipt and existing next steps. This example establishes
why an administrator needs multiple measures. It cannot rank
real people or establish the cause of the observed Danish changes.

## An upstream expansion without matching hearing and defense time

One court has 12 paid hearing blocks that fit the relevant work
this week. Its accepted calendar includes two liberty reviews,
two urgent civil protection matters, four older civil hearings
and four other ordinary hearings. Independent counsel has four
qualified defense blocks at the needed times. Two are booked
for the liberty reviews. Other legal representation and resources
needed for the booked hearings are separately available.

An upstream programme adds three criminal hearings. Assume
each requires one court block and one of these qualified defense
blocks, with other resources available. Their actual urgency
must be assessed individually. The programme pays no court
or defense increment by itself.

| Proposed response | Joint capacity under stated assumptions |
|---|---|
| Promise all three in the existing calendar | Requires 15 court blocks against 12 and five defense blocks against four |
| Buy three matching court blocks only | Two additional hearings fit if actually scheduled. The third lacks counsel |
| Buy three matching court blocks and one additional qualified defense block | All three fit if the sources and times are lawful and the parties can participate |
| Delay a still-unadopted part of the upstream programme or fund the missing chain first | Retains accepted work this week. Pending filed cases and lawful dates continue |

Choose the joint funded option where the added case work is
justified and resources can be secured. If they cannot, bring
alternatives for future policy to the Assembly and report what
will remain unmet. When a new case has an earlier statutory or
serious-harm priority, the independent judge applies it. The
registry records any displacement, safe interim remedy and real
replacement date. The executive cannot prioritize its own cases.
The court executes the existing effect of any expired liberty order.
A cleared calendar entry supplies no proof of payment or protection.

Reopen the plan if simultaneous loss of a clerk, counsel, records
or an execution route defeats the nominal schedule. Secure qualified
independent staff and reachable alternatives before claiming repair.
