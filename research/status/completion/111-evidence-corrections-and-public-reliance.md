# Scoped finding. A corrected record has to reach the decision it informed

Design203 selects a duty to trace material public reliance, decide the legal
effect, protect continuing claims and follow relief to actual receipt.
Retain the applicable release where law fixes it. Recalculate where law
requires it. A finite elected bridge can cover verified reliance or keep a
due service running when lawful funding and receiving are available.
