# NB29–NB32: preserve old claims and make an actual independent order executable

*Part of [C06/44](../44-nonbank-finance-and-client-protection.md); design113 candidate.*

**NB29 — Nonbank failure is a court case with specific sources.** On actual
inability, material endangered title/claims or failed recovery, the affected
person, fiduciary or competent career supervisor may seek ordinary court
protective administration, U restructuring or liquidation under B notice/reply/
protected evidence. Urgent B17/V04 useful-time safeguards apply. Court selects
an unconflicted qualified administrator from the C04 judiciary's preassigned
open professional roster, with actual custody/actuarial/market skill and paid
source; no provider/chief selects their final judge. The order identifies entity,
pools/contracts, power, duration, costs, distribution and review/appeal. U governs
general estate loss; NB05–08/16/19–20/28 govern their expressly separate property/
backing pools and loss commitments. Bank panel M09/BR/bail-in and deposit insurer
powers do not extend by similarity. A nonbank cannot be placed outside any forum
just because its instrument is unfamiliar.

**NB30 — Transfer needs a lawful receiver, not a spreadsheet.** A continuing
contract/portfolio moves only with genuinely funded willing licensed reception,
valid consent or court-confirmed specific restructuring law, valuation/class/
creditor-minority process and protection against a worse lawful liquidation
outcome under U. No forced new insurer/guarantee, private liability novation or
foreign recognition by domestic decree. Preserve valid security, beneficiary
identity and all partial unpaid claims; do not declare old parties released
without the actual source-law consequence. Compare runoff, consensual transfer,
U rescue/closure and new elected support; no automatic Treasury bridge or SF/
S/client/FX pooling. A useful-time essential claim travels separately to E/I/Y
or sector service; it is support, not duplicate financial-loss compensation.

**NB31 — A remedy is verified at the receiving end.** Provider's named career/
contractually accountable correction officer sends material corrections to known
receivers within24elapsed hours/earlier useful time and verifies actual revised
holdings/access/payment; never let a model's notice serve as restoration. Ordinary
independent court determines source/title/shortfall/damages with aid, safe evidence,
collective claims under B and direct appeal. Private final awards use B18/U actual
estate/security/ordinary civil enforcement, not the State judgment budget. State
supervisor/registrar own wrongs use MC28 protected Treasury payer,30working days/
earlier applicable right and express B19/B18 external case execution. Actual
fault/causation/source, paid credentials and separate custody remain; no official
chooses their own outside executor. Empty accounts, inaccessible FX, refused
receiver and actual unlawful refusal are different failures; due claims survive.

**NB32 — Explicit narrow enforcement, not punishment by analogy.** Add VD13 only
through coordinated primary law: actual knowing/reckless materially unlicensed
NB02 commercial activity; NB05–06 unauthorized client use/pledge; deliberate material
falsification of NB09/14/19/21/25 accounts or NB26's precise misconduct; or knowing/
reckless material violation of NB10/14/19/21/28 enacted capital/funding/exposure
requirements within actual control/ability after usable notice and reasonable
correction except intentional concealment. Only V06 B ceiling/court/P proof,
V07 attribution/defenses, V08 one course and Q means/proportionality apply. No
classA, custody, new turnover band, strict guilt from loss or general penalty
for a delayed claim/report. Civil repair, staged restriction, prosecution and
market losses are different. The exact source schedules must be operative;
missing calibration cannot create an offense or a compliant license by analogy.
