refer to generic local civilian incident leads and Cabinet coordinator. Those
labels do not establish an employer, appointment, replacement, qualified duty
assignment or actual received rescue. This is a material ordinary public-service
actor gap, not evidence that all prior emergency powers or clinical duties were
missing. Fourteen entrance headers/ownership and selected canonical HP/CP/RX/
LF/PO/OJ/C04/EL11/HG/ED/DP/DF/work/source/transition dependencies were inspected;
no new full fourteen-chapter/all-clause or original local-casualty audit occurred.

Equal standing, effective opposition, useful protection, privacy/consent and
peaceful replacement are values/requirements. Simultaneous qualified labor,
physical access and controlled stock are constraints. Better incident outcomes
from this organizational form are an untested causal hypothesis, not a fact.
