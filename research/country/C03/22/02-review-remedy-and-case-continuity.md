# Useful-time review and actual repair

*Part of [PX open contests](../22-open-contests-and-neutral-enforcement.md).*

**PX04 — Reach an independent duty court before the opportunity is
lost.** A directly affected person can seek assistance and urgent
review at the first protected receipt, including against cumulative
measures or a material refusal. Use preassigned unconflicted
ordinary judges and actual alternates under OJ, EA16 and the
applicable forum. The proposed initial interim decision must occur
within six elapsed hours of first protected receipt of a credible
urgent filing and sooner if the opportunity or liberty requires it.
An evidence request does not restart that clock. This is an initial remedy
or reasoned refusal, with protected evidence and prompt adversarial
review, rather than a deadline for final criminal findings. Keep
earlier P and E clocks. Provide paid independent counsel, interpreting,
safe notice and a human route during an outage. Preserve good-faith
reporting and actual victim and witness protection. A filing does
not itself stay a lawful act or remove a political opponent.

**PX05 — Repair the loss without stopping lawful justice.** An
independent court may stay the harmful part of an unlawful measure,
limit its timing or scope, order safe return or access, preserve
evidence, require independent reconsideration or enforce a lawful
release. Apply the least restrictive remedy that can work. Where
lawful necessity is proved, protect the investigation and the people
at risk and preserve compatible participation through actual lawful
alternatives. The court does not decide whom to charge, direct
clinical or investigative judgment, forgive misconduct or give a
candidate an unsafe privilege. Failure to meet a proposed deadline
does not confer permission, immunity or an election delay. Existing
limits on arrest and remand still require actual release when the
lawful ground or clock ends. New labels cannot reset those limits.

**PX06 — Keep candidacy, custody and result separate.** An allegation,
investigation, charge, interim restraint, incomplete record or model
flag is no automatic finding of ineligibility or loss of office.
Any restriction of candidacy or office needs its separate exact
prior law, competent decision, timely defense and independent
review. The electoral career officer preserves the timely filing,
valid records and ordinary support. Restore only a lawful opportunity
that can actually be received. Before voting, use funded correction
of access or print where applicable. After a lost opportunity,
preserve the claim and actual loss. ER16–ER17 alone governs an
applicable postpoll contest and repeat under its existing materiality,
proof and narrower-remedy tests. No judicially selected winner,
automatic voiding of votes, electoral-clock reset or term extension.

**PX07 — Apply the rule to selective inaction and the protector.**
A victim, witness or directly affected person retains existing
rights to assistance, protection and challenge of unlawful
non-action. A materially suppressed case against a government
supporter receives the same original receipt, neutral timing and
unconflicted review as a case against a challenger. Use existing
OJ26–OJ30 and PO outside-command routes on their actual activation
grounds. A court can require lawful independent consideration
and preserve evidence without ordering a charge. Where a
reviewer or prosecutor is implicated, use the preassigned eligible
alternative and protected direct court route. An accusation alone
cannot recuse all reviewers or activate an external prosecutor.
