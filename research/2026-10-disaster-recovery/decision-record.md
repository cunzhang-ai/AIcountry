# Decision record: democratic disaster recovery after the rescue phase

5 October 2026 · Integrated design 45 research selection; not enacted law

## Baseline, question and affected people

The [design-44 predecessor](predecessor-design-44/README.md) preserves the
current files before this change. C10 assigned rescue and lawful emergency
restrictions; C07 protected safe shelter and services; C09 addressed land,
hazards and relocation; C06 counted contingent risk. None joined these to
a multiyear recovery decision after homes, public assets and the local tax
base fail together. A government could thereby pay the insured owners,
ignore renters, keep a community in temporary shelter, or let an emergency
administrator choose its permanent land map. The bounded question is who
chooses a durable settlement, under which claims and finance, when one
locality cannot recover with its own budget.

Affected people include residents who stayed or moved, tenants and owners,
children, workers, customary and collective rights-holders, receiving
communities, creditors and insurers, local taxpayers, future residents and
people downstream. No AIcountry affected public or insurer has participated.
Values include immediate safety, home and cultural continuity, equal standing,
contract and property rights, ecological limits, future risk, fiscal fairness
and the ability to replace decision-makers. They cannot be reduced to a
property-loss total or a modelled hazard score.

## Competing diagnoses and whole packages

The problem may chiefly be uninsured household loss, a failed local tax
base, unsafe land, absent building labor, one collapsed insurer, unequal
political influence or a public wrong before the event. Source observations
that distinguish them include actual damage, titles and tenancies, customary
claims, insurance contracts and capital, risk data, water and power assets,
local receipts, contractor capacity, displacement and receiving-place load.
One AI estimate repeated by several agents is one evidence family.

| Package | Mechanism and possible benefit | Material failure or objection |
|---|---|---|
| Retain emergency response plus ordinary property, insurance and planning law | Uses existing powers and avoids a new recovery office | No joined local/national recovery plan, local fiscal bridge or durable comparison; powerful claimants can recover first |
| Full public replacement and rebuild in the same place | Simple promise of restitution and community continuity | Can be fiscally and physically impossible, privilege valuable titled property, and reproduce unsafe exposure |
| Insurance-led market recovery | Prices some risks and may mobilize private capital | Uninsured people, exclusions, unaffordable cover, insurer failure and weak local tax base can leave essential life unrepaired |
| **Selected: universal immediate protection, conditional public reconstruction and risk-layered finance under elected local/national choices** | Keeps distinct claims visible, permits safe return or supported movement, funds a local bridge within national law | Slow deliberation, difficult claimant proof, possible central funding pressure, correlated losses and scarce materials can defeat delivery |
| Central expert-led relocation and reconstruction | May move quickly across fragmented property interests | Can displace dissenters and customary users, convert emergency power into permanent land control and fail on livelihoods or legitimacy |

The selected [C09 recovery route](../country/C09/07-disaster-recovery-and-return.md)
and [C06 finance route](../country/C06/07-disaster-risk-finance.md) are conditional
working choices, not a command to rebuild every site or relocate every
high-risk place. They keep emergency safety, private insurance, legal
compensation, social assistance and public capital choices legally distinct.
The [C13 stress](../country/C13/10-flood-recovery-and-displacement.md) tests
the chain when land records and local revenue fail together.

## Evidence and what it can support

The [UNDRR Sendai Framework](https://www.undrr.org/publication/sendai-framework-disaster-risk-reduction-2015-2030)
identifies risk understanding, governance, investment, preparedness and
recovery as linked priorities; its [recovery guidance](https://www.undrr.org/publication/words-action-guidelines-build-back-better-recovery-rehabilitation-and-reconstruction)
emphasizes pre-event relationships and planning. The [World Bank disaster-risk
finance program](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/disaster-risk-financing-and-insurance-program)
and [risk-finance overview](https://www.worldbank.org/ext/en/topic/financial-sector/disaster-risk-finance-and-insurance)
describe reserves, contingent credit and risk transfer as different possible
instruments, while acknowledging insurance cannot cover all loss. The
[World Bank recovery framework guide](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/692141603785003050/pdf/Disaster-Recovery-FrameworkGuide.pdf)
addresses land-use and community participation. [OHCHR's climate-mobility
messages](https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/Documents/Issues/ClimateChange/Key_Messages_HR_CC_Migration.pdf)
treat planned relocation generally as a last resort with meaningful affected
participation. These are primary comparator frameworks and guidance, not
AIcountry law, local hazard measurements or proof of this package's effects.
No current disaster, insurer balance sheet, budget or affected-person account
has been inspected for an actual adopting jurisdiction.

## Authority, compatibility and migration

Prior law sets safety powers, minimum social access, insurance/contract
rights, property process, eligible public assistance, borrowing ceilings,
local/national competence and court review. A local elected council chooses
a local durable plan within those boundaries; the Assembly owns national
rights, finance, cross-boundary works and a material unresolved common
burden. Cabinet and a temporary receiver execute limited law. Displaced and
receiving people need practical voice; a sponsor cannot select only those
who can attend a damaged town hall. An AI map may inform, but cannot decide
who may return or whose property passes to a developer. Courts correct
specific rights and legal claims; they do not create an appropriation.

The proposal does not retroactively alter a policy, mortgage, lease, title,
customary claim, court order or treaty. A future adopter must reconcile
existing emergency, land, housing, civil, insurance, tax, budget, local-
government and election law. It must preserve pending claims, record custody,
insurance payouts, public wrongdoing claims and lawful interim services.
A preauthorized receiver needs paid staff, records, cash, place-based access
and an elected restoration path; naming one cannot keep a school open. The
[C06 debt route](../country/C06/06-sovereign-debt-and-distress.md) still governs
any new borrowing or a debt crisis. Damage in one locality cannot be used
to hide an unfunded national guarantee or to discharge an old one.

## Objections, dissent and result

The case for fast national command is strongest where a local council and
records are destroyed; the selected route could be too slow. Its response
is prior-law receivers for continuing services and a separately funded
alternate elected decision path, not permanent national land control.
The case for full restitution is strongest for people who cannot bear loss;
selective aid can be arbitrary. The selected package protects basic needs
regardless of title while making broader compensation and grants explicit
law and budget choices. Insurers may argue public relief weakens risk
pricing; residents may argue risk-based premiums or withdrawal make cover
unusable. No average premium or hazard score resolves that distributional
conflict. A rights-respecting local vote can still impose flooding on a
neighbor, so cross-boundary effects remain an Assembly responsibility.
Affected people may reject the package after real comparison.

**Evidence:** official comparator guidance and authored institutional logic,
not AIcountry original observations. **Research selection:** conditional
universal immediate protection, separated claim types, elected recovery
choice and prearranged but bounded finance. **Legal authorization:** none.
**Operational readiness:** no real jurisdiction, people, hazard, insurer,
workforce, reserve or tested service receiver. **Observed outcomes:** none;
no claim of superiority over existing countries follows.

The next bounded test is one actual locality-and-receiving-area recovery
scenario with original hazard, title/tenancy, insurance, local revenue,
service, workforce and cross-boundary data. Compare safe repair, adaptation
and movement with affected people, a rival finance package and a failed
ordinary council. Reopen this selection if essential users remain excluded,
continued service lacks a lawful receiver, insurance or public debt defeats
the budget, or a rival yields better rights, delivery and public control.
