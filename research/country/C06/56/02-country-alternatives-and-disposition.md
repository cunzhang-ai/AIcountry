# FP05–FP08: retain the guarantees, reject an inadequate financial basis

*Part of [C06/56](../56-national-fiscal-settlement-and-source-boundaries.md); design 138 candidate.*

**FP05 — Retain/repair is a whole-country alternative.** CX's retrofit
preference starts with actual people, homes, institutions, sources, productive
relationships and old law. A real retained state has benefits and failures;
it is neither free capacity nor a deliberately weak comparator. Compare
repairing gaps, restoring rights and maintaining current qualified supply
against new ownership, taxes and delivery. No claim is waived because its
price or record is unknown. All state functions remain in the comparison.

**FP06 — Prefer duty-specific paid changes over the old dated rollout basis.**
Retain SG's individual guarantee/services/earned rights, NS's service-first
diversified retrofit and SC's critical-input competence. For prospective new
coverage/investment, specify the complete receiving/source/transition package
and alternatives through existing elected law. Keep affordable support and
stable informal cooperation where adequate; redesign speed is no social gain.

Retire 130/131's scale/date/quoted aggregate as the selected *national financial
reference*. Its adequacy was never established. Preserve its calculations as historical diagnostic support
for compatible formulas, ownership, period and failure interfaces. This is
not a new claim that any smaller population is affordable, a pilot completes
the country, or taxpayers/private employers must meet model-chosen quotas.
No phased proposal can defer an existing or enacted new due right by declaring
its receiving stage unready. Actual law/rights and scope-specific remedies continue.

**FP07 — Keep substantive rivals without inherited source claims.** A
rights-preserving U+C2 package keeps the same essential services, extra needs,
earned/source-year rights and paid transition; its added burdens may be worth
choosing. Direct-public, cooperative/private and regional shared provision
retain real title/work/capital/quality/exit costs. Their fiscal and political
tradeoffs cannot be resolved by subtracting supplier margins alone.

The finite fiscal-issue rival remains a separate unadopted financial regime:
show lawful authority, liability, price/resource/foreign consequences and public
control. No ordinary Treasury/central-bank discretion supplies it. Retain RF's
recurring-tax/insurance and bounded-debt choice meanwhile; no proof here that
tax funding, debt or issue alone guarantees supply or national superiority.

**FP08 — Finish with the actual disposition.** Retain these institutional
choices; reject an adequate national scale/source conclusion from the old
partial book. Do not choose a residual tax, invented income or invisible
service cut. The four diagnostic A/U/provider cases do not establish a funded
winner, complete recurring gap or minimum tax burden. Keep the missing
context-specific full package explicitly unfinished, with a bounded empirical
next step, rather than indefinitely adding local clauses or pretending the
current symbols already describe a launchable country.

Material dissent remains: targeted assessment can exclude/intrude; U can
improve independence; service-first priorities can be captured; bounded debt
can shift costs forward; a different currency/ownership settlement may be
better. Affected people have not chosen the complete bargain. Greater funding,
fewer agencies or a positive accounting identity alone does not resolve it.

[CS14's clarified common boundary](../../C01/03/06-current-country-and-strong-alternatives.md)
protects fundamental rights/needs and actual due/accrued claims without forcing
every rival to copy every unadopted TW/NC/AL/EB/CL number. Compare actual lost/
gained time, quality, agency, source and transition; unknown adequacy stays unknown.
[CL](../../C05/17-care-leave-and-return.md) is a newly specified prospective
component, not already-funded leave or an excuse to weaken a due service.
