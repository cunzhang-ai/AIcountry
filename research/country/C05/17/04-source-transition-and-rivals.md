# CL19–CL24. Choose the whole time-and-care bargain openly

**CL19 — Explicit G liability, no phantom contribution.** Select general-
revenue public financing through actual elected DB/RF appropriation/standing
source law for the CL13 component and administration, not an unspecified future
employer/worker insurance levy, Y assets, private reserves, fines, mineral rents
or forecast AI savings. Actual ordinary lawful borrowing retains its bounded
source; research creates no money/issue authority. Name due gross/net/currency/
dates and paid independent receiving/execution. Fiscal means still need actual
full assessment; an explicit payer is not money already available or readiness.
Better employer salary/top-ups and job continuity keep their actual employers.

**CL20 — Carry the joint cost and simultaneous work.** In the same country
account include replacement cash, retained salary/top-up/annual-rest-accrual counterparts, qualified
cover, recruitment/training/overlap, premises/materials, actual private/business/
foreign work displaced, tax/I05 changes, Y06 backing and administration/aid/
courts/execution. Count each once, separately by owner/currency/due date.
Cash replacement and production of the substitute service are different uses;
a tax on new pay, forgone old pay, internal G transfers and an unpaid recovery
cannot all be new outside finance. No assumed nursery saving or public job yields
free national resources. Retain TW's BOTH36/32 stages, regular pay/rest/learning,
Y and care/clinical/democratic/legal duties; repricing CL cannot silently cancel
them. A whole different offer needs an explicit legitimate prospective choice.

**CL21 — Meaningful country rivals may choose different prospective numbers.**
Strong retain/repair keeps actual working leave/income/job arrangements and fixes
located access/gender/receiving gaps, with real old duties/costs retained.
More public care with less earnings-replacing leave can preserve professional
capacity but lose relationship/discretionary time; ordinary I05 or a nursery
place is not an equal substitute for protected absence. Longer/more transferable
leave can expand choice but reinforce household bargaining asymmetry, gendered
withdrawal and qualified-work/source loss. A different capped replacement,
individual-only rights, child-only pool or mixed insurance source is a genuine
rival with its own exclusions/incidence/administration. No free strong comparator
or automatic winner follows. Common rights/needs and actual accrued claims must
be respected; every unadopted candidate parameter need not be copied as a floor.

**CL22 — Select provisionally and retain the hardest dissent.** Choose CL's
52 arrival weeks, reserved shares of min(16,52/n), six temporary weeks,26week episode window,
two compassionate weeks and100%-to-W public replacement as normative country
hypotheses. They support independent time without assigning care to women or
relatives, at real employment/source/service-replacement costs. The exact numbers
are not proved optimal; other societies' rules are mechanism examples, not causal
approval. Caps can exclude high/irregular earnings, reserved time can go unused,
credit/accrual differs from salary and public payroll source can be captured.
Long-term chosen relationships may need more support; no formal equality proves
usable access or equal care. Actual affected family/recipient/worker/employer/
public-service accounts and qualified fiscal/legal review remain absent.

1January at least12calendar months after valid coordinated ordinary enactment
of CL, tax/I05/EB/Y source joins and paid receiving duties; state the actual date
and pending-arrival/rolling-period treatment in the Act. This is not a readiness
certificate, minister's deferral or activation by research. Existing more
protective lawful leave/pay, medical/care/office rights and earlier dates remain.
No retroactive duplicate claim, extinction of old rights or resetting TW stages.
For an arrival before commencement but within its24month window, preserve actual
used/committed/earned old rights and allow only a prospectively defined unmatched
CL remainder; no full fresh52 on top of matching old leave. Actual old/new
source differences require named treatment, not an invented double subtraction.

OP/T must accept paid assets/staff/pensions/contracts/creditors/records/cases/
claims and source/continuity obligations through lawful actual successors.
Existing institutions receive bounded new work; no new care sovereign, platform
monopoly, family licence or constitutional office replacement. Actual software/
constitution/contribution permissions and all protected holds retain their own
revision law. Missing source/cover is failed delivery with existing remedies and
competent elected correction, not forced family work or silent clock extension.
