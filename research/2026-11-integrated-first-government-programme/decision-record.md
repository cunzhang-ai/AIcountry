# Design281. Integrated first government programme

10 November 2026 · Research selection · No actual enactment

## Baseline and decision

[Design280](baseline.json) already selects a service-first plural country,
a protected first transition, separate elected choices and several
promising sector starts. Its worked budgets price partial groups on
different calendars. Their capacities, sources and feasibility passes
cannot be stacked. Under the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md), choose an explicit first
sequence on one budget with real narrower alternatives. Codex assisted
drafting and comparison. No independent reviewer, public authority,
service provider or affected participant chose these measures.

## Alternatives and selection

- **Retain and repair.** Reserve and operate the same prior duties
  and scheduled repairs. Avoid extra commitments, but lose an early
  housing and heating improvement and a bounded training opportunity.
- **Select repair plus E + P + H on the declared reference path.**
  Deliver an additional finite energy bridge, fund care workforce
  preparation and open three secure tenancies with voluntary support.
  Keep ordinary routes available. Hold the parallel intake pilot. On
  the weaker path, use the separately authorized E + H where funding,
  local acceptance and useful support remain. Waiting households get
  priority over preparation in this branch because the fixture assumes
  an immediate suitable opportunity. A stronger foreseeable care
  constraint could change that preference. If the housing advance,
  local vote or operator is absent before commitment, use the distinctly
  authorized E + P when it qualifies.
- **Accelerate with the additional intake pilot.** Use the same protected
  duties and preserve independent non-digital access. Its fit on national
  cash alone hides a missing local unit and qualified review slot. It can
  return with actual qualified capacity or a genuine displacement.
  An accountable elected body could also choose the intake measure ahead
  of a different discretionary improvement if that better repairs access.

## Authority, failure and continuing rights

The competent elected bodies choose their own budgets, the housing payer
verifies actual incoming funds, the currency holder checks permitted
settlement and the service owners accept real work. Independent budget
scrutiny and courts have separate access and do not vote or operate a
service. Count the common work and national transfer once. A payer or
supplier can fail after legally binding an agreement. Carry all accrued
and lawful obligations, original case dates, records and independent
remedies. A new release condition cannot withdraw an already effective
right. Use prior authority for a bridge and seek lawful correction when
it fails. No model can select who loses existing protection.

The [evidence record](sources.md) qualifies separate cash and service
scopes, with no transfer of country figures. The
[authored comparison](../settlement/integrated-first-programme/01-shared-sources-and-votes.md)
reproduces exact cash and resource constraints, missing assent and
unaccepted delivery. It does not estimate public costs or resident
effects. The [country scope account](../settlement/integrated-first-programme/03-country-scope-and-remaining-choices.md)
retains all fourteen chapters without qualifying the cost or delivery
of any assumed baseline.

## Next bounded action

Before a real vote, price the inherited duties in one governing context
and obtain source and acceptance records for the same two competing
starts in that context. Start with one national payer, one local housing
payer and the independent reviewer serving both routes. Ask whether the
advance arrives before binding the lease and whether the same reviewer
has already been assigned. Preserve a real option to delay any
uncommitted addition. Obtain affected-party evidence and independent
qualified review. Reopen on material differences in actual need, public
authority, lawful source, delivery or a better complete alternative.
