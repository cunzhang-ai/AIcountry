# NB17–NB20: a private retirement product cannot replace social solidarity

*Part of [C06/44](../44-nonbank-finance-and-client-protection.md); design113 candidate.*

**NB17 — Keep public and private promises distinct.** C07 Y/RF/NF statutory
basic/earned/care/survivor units, S restricted cash and due dates remain unchanged.
No compelled private account, privatization of accrued units or compulsory
investment of public reserves finances this reform. Voluntary additional DC
(asset-value) and funded DB (defined promise) arrangements may exist. Name their
actual member/beneficiary, sponsor, trustee, payer, custody, fees and guarantee;
private failure does not erase public rights or make private contributions a
new personal claim to S. Actual receipts enter I/tax tests under existing law,
not imagined pension wealth or another household member's unavailable balance.

**NB18 — Beneficiaries control a separate fiduciary estate.** Private pension
assets/withheld contributions are legally separate from employer, manager and
other plans, with actual tracing/records and independent custodian. A withheld
amount becomes a trust obligation when actually withheld; missing employer cash
is an unpaid claim, not an invented held asset. Pay due employer/member amounts
to custody within5working days after payroll/earlier contract, individually
report missing contributions and permit direct recovery. Sponsor deficit debts
retain U04 unless valid separate security applies; no silent new priority over
workers or other old claims. No investment in sponsoring employer or controlled
affiliate, and no compelled domestic/political investment; this costs finance
and some diversification choices but prevents employment/retirement double risk.

Select five fiduciary trustees: three elected by actual plan members/beneficiaries,
one person one vote including retirees, and two qualified independent trustees
appointed by at least two of those three elected trustees after an open shortlist.
No sponsor appointment/removal veto; the three elected seats have staggered
three-year terms (initial1/2/3-year slots drawn before nomination) and recall
by majority of actual valid member votes in an
accessible plan-wide ballot, with30days' notice and no altered membership roll.
Independent seats have staggered three-year terms (initial18/36months), proved
fiduciary competence and no sponsor/manager control; disclose shared paid
relationships and fund available outside review. Removal is only for proved
fiduciary cause with court review. A substantive investment/funding/provider
choice needs three actual unconflicted yes and three present of the statutory
five; no vacancy denominator reset. Courts can remedy an unlawful election or
appoint only necessary temporary fiduciary administration, not invent members'
policy consent. Pay competent trustees/training from disclosed plan costs.

**NB19 — Fund the promise actually made.** DC units equal attributable assets
less actual lawful fees/liabilities; market loss is borne by those units, no
fake guaranteed return. DB liabilities use the larger prudent accrued ongoing
and lawful termination values, including promised indexation/survivors and
run-off costs; discount reflects matched enforceable cashflows, not hoped-for
asset return. Independently assess annually and on material change, with monthly
funding/liquidity warning. Assets must cover100% of that accrued liability plus
NB14 risk capital for guarantees borne by the plan without an enforceable solvent
sponsor backstop. A sponsor covenant is a separate risk, not cash or automatic
replacement for100% funding.

For an emerged deficit, report promptly; within90calendar days of the first
established deficit submit an actual funded contribution/recovery or court-runoff
plan to the prudential pension case officer and members, at most five years
from that same event to restore100% funding, with annual independently assessed progress and actual sponsor
capacity. Preserve accrual/payments/old claims; new service must be paid at full
current cost, no surplus distribution or improving unfunded promises. A recovery
period creates no solvency or automatic benefit cut; worsening/no committed source
requires earlier NB29 court action. Courts can establish the actual first
finding; acknowledgment/new report cannot reset the same carried deficit.
Compare shorter recovery against recession/
employer failure and intergenerational losses, not universal optimality.

**NB20 — Exit must preserve both movers and stayers.** Immediate vesting of
actually earned/contributed new rights; no employer retention condition. An
accepted DC transfer uses verified actual units/value with no punitive exit fee,
normally within10working days after complete authorized receiving, earlier source
rights prevailing; missing evidence gets prompt help/reasons, not invented
completeness. DB transfer uses independently evidenced accrued value, discloses
guarantees lost and needs actual informed choice. An underfunded DB plan cannot
pay a mover100% while exporting their deficit to stayers: use the same funded
fraction now and preserve their exact remaining sponsor/plan claim, or a lawful
court-protected collective settlement. No forced conversion of vestedDB toDC.
Death/beneficiary/support disputes keep B/Y/RP status and interim receiving;
private trustees cannot select a new civil family definition or public pension.

[Design139 FP19](../56/06-owned-principal-and-country-disposition.md) distinguishes
verified already-taxed private capital from actual new taxable income and public
Y solidarity claims. NB permission grants no contribution deduction, deferral,
asset-return tax or blanket exemption. Detailed prospective plan tax/source/basis
rules need coordinated law and independent review; they remain unqualified.
Existing rights, paid claims and I05 usable-resource matching continue.

[PF01–PF16](../57-private-retirement-tax-and-personal-capital.md) now supplies
the previously missing written tax interface. New work current-service funding
uses actually paid ordinary remuneration with a net-cash choice; existing
contract/guarantee rights retain PF15's source-law transition. Own paid capital,
indexable DC disposal and nominal capital-first nonmarketable payments stay
separate from employer recovery/capital and public Y. No regulatory licence
creates relief, a new actuarial cash receipt or a taxpayer-funded private loss.
