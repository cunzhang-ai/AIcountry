# Humane life, actual agency and no productive-work detention economy

*CD07–CD08; part of [C08/21](../21-conflict-detention-and-release.md).*

**CD07 — Care is a duty, not a reward for information.** Supply safe water,
food, shelter, sanitation, clothing, clinical/mental-health care, disability
access, interpreters, protected contact, religion, learning, ordinary exercise
and usable private complaints, without charging a detention debt. Protect
against sexual violence, public humiliation, coercive media and retaliation.
Choose safe accommodation from actual needs/preferences and age/family safety,
not a model's dangerousness, sex marker or diagnosis alone. Retain civil agency,
will/asset/care communication and rights to confidential independent advice.

No torture, degrading treatment, forced confession, medical/reproductive
experiment, forced nontherapeutic intervention, coercive behavioral treatment
or punishment through withheld essentials. Clinical consent and the narrow
general emergency law remain; captivity is not incapacity. Immediate necessary
safety restraint is minimum, recorded, independently reviewable and ends when
actual danger ends; it cannot become isolation, therapy or a hidden sentence.

Identity/urgent medical questions do not authorize a wider compelled account.
POW identity obligations, where applicable, are explained without coercion;
no merits/intelligence questioning before actual private advice. Voluntary
participation is specific, revocable, source-recorded and never a waiver of
basic protections. Silence, requested counsel or refusal to cooperate with
propaganda does not establish guilt or extend custody. AI/source convergence
cannot replace actual evidence, human interpretation or another person's will.

**CD08 — A closed, noncustodial discipline choice.** Prior published accessible
camp rules may regulate immediate safety, personal space and orderly shared
facilities within actual authority, not beliefs or political allegiance.
For an intentional material safety/order breach proved by the state beyond
reasonable doubt, with notice, source access, a meaningful defense and an
impartial hearing, restrict at most one optional organized leisure activity
for seven days, subject to direct independent court challenge. Necessity,
self-defense and inability to comply remain defenses; this is not a criminal
conviction or a public criminal record.
No repeat labels stack the same-course period. A person may use a freely
chosen representative and actual interpretation. The implicated guard is
not final adjudicator; serious abuse goes outside command.

No disciplinary imprisonment, solitary confinement, collective punishment,
fine, forced work, loss of legal/clinical/family contact, religion, reading,
necessary exercise, property taking or extension of the status term. Mere
escape is not a new criminal offense; protect applicable GCIII Article 91–93 escape/
nonviolent incident rules. Actual violence or an independently established
ordinary offense needs its own full P/K/G case, not punishment for affiliation.
No conviction/discipline quota finances or measures the institution.

No forced productive labor or use of captives to fund detention, public works,
weapons or missing civilian services. Reasonable personal hygiene with actual
support is not an unpaid national workforce. Any lawful voluntary paid activity
requires real safe refusal, disclosed conditions, ordinary safety/remuneration
and review, without military-objective production or reduced rights for refusal.
This is a more protective national choice than merely copying every treaty-
permitted labor/disciplinary power; its actual staffing/cost is not established.
