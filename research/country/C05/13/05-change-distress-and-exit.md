# OE21–OE25: change must carry actual rights, not leave them in an empty shell

*Part of [C05/13](../13-enterprise-and-association-constitutions.md); design118 candidate.*

**OE21 — New capital without concealed dilution.** Share issues disclose actual
consideration, rights, control and source. Existing voting holders get a pro-rata
cash-issue opportunity unless two-thirds of all disinterested voting rights
expressly waive it for a specific fair purpose/price. Related controlling votes
cannot approve their own favorable allotment. Protected class rights need actual
class consent. Noncash source is separately valued, not assumed money or identical
to productive capital. Transfers record real title/encumbrances; an agent cannot
sell an unavailable asset or use each replica's fictional whole subscription.

**OE22 — Reserved transactions.** Charter/purpose changes, merger/split, form
conversion, dissolution or disposal of substantially the whole undertaking need
notice/reasons/actual accounts/alternatives, two-thirds of all entitled voting
rights or members, and two-thirds of each materially adversely affected class.
Ordinary board authority alone is insufficient. Board's own valid approval,
worker/WL consultation/representation, sector/MP gates, source tax and property/
creditor law stay separate; one approval cannot vote for another constituency.
A change cannot override nonwaivable rights, manufacture political franchise or
turn a public asset/risk limit into private discretion. Stronger actual terms survive.

**OE23 — Dissent and actual funding.** A materially affected dissenting member
can seek fair independent valuation, direct court review and a funded buyout or
other adequate protection for a compulsory fundamental alteration. Paid title,
legitimate purpose, equal treatment and actual minority loss matter; neither book
stock nor a desired national cash balance is the valuation. No guaranteed ordinary
market exit at every price. If adequate legally required protection cannot be
funded without violating creditors/rights, the transaction cannot proceed on a
fictional buyout; a court needs a lawful concretely adequate alternative, not
permission to invent cash or confiscate title. Parties may retain/no change.

**OE24 — Distress changes duties, not history.** When inability to meet dues is
reasonably evident, directors preserve assets/records, avoid unlawful distributions/
new misleading commitments and promptly seek actual creditor/work/service plans
or U court process. Reasonable honest rescue can be lawful; insolvent trading
is not automatic personal guilt or a new crime by label. Proved negligent/deliberate
harm or stripping has B/U/precise V consequences. Current wages/accepted work need
real sources; neither a debt stay nor new board compels unfunded labor/supply.
Merger successor takes actual predecessor obligations; a split leaves predecessor/
successors jointly liable for existing obligations unless affected creditors
actually consent or a competent court lawfully preserves no-worse protection.
Security/pension/client/custody and statutory public duties keep their own priority.

**OE25 — Dissolution with actual successors.** Solvent closure requires actual
claims account, notice to known creditors/members/workers and at least90 calendar
days' public claim opportunity, receiver/record custodian and lawful residual
allocation after debts. This clock cannot extinguish unknown, latent, child or
longer B37 claims. Insolvent closure uses U, sector failure actual M/NB law.
A provider's disappearance does not end public S/AS/E receiving or worker claims.
Authorized dissolution is no automatic personal debt for every member or permanent
ban on new enterprise. Restoration/receiver claims remain within source periods;
a shell name change, AI deletion or register strike-off cannot erase actual dues.

A court may arrange proportionate missing-organ/abandoned-entity representation
and records to hear claims, not invent assets or waive fair third-party defense.
Preserve necessary originals at least six years after closure and longer where
actual source claims/law require, with funded safe purpose-limited custody and
proportionate deletion, not universal permanent personal testimony. Missing records
need assisted evidence/correction and fair proof, never automatic loss of rights.
