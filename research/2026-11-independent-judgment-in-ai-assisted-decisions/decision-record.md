# Design358 decision on independent judgment in AI-assisted decisions

## Baseline, proposed rule and authority

Pin Design357, DP17–DP24, DP38–DP42, EV, LX, AP and the
[working method](../FRAMEWORK.md). DP19 already distinguishes
investigative leads from findings and allows shielding ranking labels
when fair. Specify when independent first consideration is required,
how to check advice-driven changes, and what happens when review cannot
finish in time. The project assistant proposes this for legally named
high-consequence classes. The adopting Assembly sets scope, due authority
and funds. Qualified sector officials decide cases under existing law.
Independent review and remedies keep their existing lawful owners.

## Alternatives and objections

A prompt plus human sign-off can be fast but shares upstream framing
and may omit the same source twice. An initial view from originals
protects a chance to notice omissions, and sometimes costs extra time
or introduces anchoring on the first view. Preserve explicit revision
and material contradictory evidence. An ordinary route can keep
verified routine processing and use qualified officials directly for
disputes. It avoids reconciling irrelevant recommendations. Preserve
this rival, which is selected in the constructed case.

A required record could become an employee surveillance measure or a
ritual. Limit it to the material grounds and exposure. A government
could withhold a relevant original, constrain reviewer access or put
both routes under one queue. Count actual controllers and provide
outside challenge, independent source access and an authorized reserve.
Give urgent protection its own clock. Retain open cases if staff or
evidence cannot support the act. An independent review process cannot
decide facts merely by being labeled independent.

## Qualified result, compatibility and next action

The [primary evidence](sources.md) motivates comparative tests and
independent oversight. The [constructed comparison](../settlement/independent-judgment-in-ai-assisted-decisions/01-one-summary-two-signatures.md)
checks omitted original evidence, repeated signatures, real time and
a competent ordinary route. No real effect, staffing gain, qualified
external review, legal adoption or actual service receipt is established.
Preserve earlier case dates, original sources, lawful records, existing
awards, review and correction duties. Apply DP and LX to identified
downstream uses instead of assuming a new procedure corrects old acts.

Compare three routes on one matched shadow set under an authorized
sector test budget, with original evidence, independent qualified
adjudicators and safe input from affected people. Include enough
difficult and ordinarily excluded cases to expose material omissions.
Record wrong proposed acts and missed useful times together. Stop before
any change in live rights. Reopen if the first-view record delays due
work, the ordinary route performs as well, the advice changes, or the
alternate shares the disputed source or controller.
