# Design346 decision on the first programme through recovery

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design345, T01–T30, JC, ordinary sector deadlines and the working
framework. The first programme already prices shared sources and restoring
service. Add a separate operating test for accumulated missed work
when ordinary service is restored but demand continues to arrive.
Select a funded recovery schedule joined to ordinary capacity, with
separate actual receiving and protection of elections and review.

## Alternatives and objections

Return immediately to the standard budget. This is simple and can
leave accumulated duties untouched. Clear the old queue first. This
can improve a visible metric while pushing new needs past their own
deadlines. Buy finite additional capacity and verify its paid
succession. Select the third route in the declared case, and retain
different schedules where actual law, need or availability differs.

The [primary comparisons](sources.md) show competing recovery pressures,
continuity duties and the need to preserve rights records. The ten-
working-day disposition is a design parameter. More staffing may
be unavailable, and a separate recovery programme can drain normal
services. Compare who supplies it, whether it displaces duties and
what happens when it expires.

## Authority, transition and reopening

Existing service owners and payers deliver and record actual work.
Elected bodies determine appropriations and optional starts, and
independent reviewers retain their mandates. The project assistant
authored this choice. Actual legal adoption, qualified independent
review and affected participation remain open. Preserve original dates,
old claims, independent review and worker obligations through migration.

Test two consecutive service cycles with an interruption and a later
loss. Include affected users, service workers, independent reviewers
and the elected funders. Compare actual receipt and who remains exposed
across locations and cohorts. Reopen on hidden cases, stalled flow,
temporary spending without a successor, harm or a simpler route
that clears obligations while maintaining ordinary services.
