# Commitment 116. Account for people affected by a public evidence correction

[EV17–EV24](../../country/C11/11-evidence-corrections-and-public-reliance.md)
assigns material-use review to existing programme owners. Evidence custodians
publish and warn. The governing law determines each due amount or operative
decision. Elected authorities authorize new bridges with named sources.
Existing outside review and courts address delay, wrong effect or unexecuted
relief. Programme owners verify actual receiving and retain unresolved uses.

Keep EV, WB, DP, PD, DB, FB, MF, SR and source-specific rights, finality and
protected records. Paid discovery, review, delivery, actual loss and actual
independence remain open. [Comparison](../evidence-corrections/README.md)
