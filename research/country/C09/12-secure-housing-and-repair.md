# C09. Secure homes, cost rental and repair

1 November 2026 · Design332 · H01–H31 research selection, not enacted law

**Select a permanent public/community cost-rental option alongside private,
cooperative and owner-occupied housing, with secure ordinary tenancies and
repair before replacement where it actually preserves adequate homes.** Housing
is private living space, a durable asset, a service and a source of power;
neither a cash transfer nor a democratic landlord alone settles all four.

The mechanism is durable use and an outside option: a home need not be lost
with a job, relationship, higher earnings or criticism of government. The
public option can limit dependence on a dominant landlord without buying all
private homes. Its cost and bureaucratic power are real disadvantages. Resident
control does not authorize exclusion, capture public assets or replace public
law; ordinary courts, elected funding and alternative providers remain distinct.

**Rivals retained:** current targeted cash/private provision with stronger
tenancy rules; paid dispersed acquisitions; new cost-rental construction; and
repair of effective existing provision without conversion. Private provision
can retain flexibility, dispersed control and lower transition cost. New supply
can relieve actual shortage better than buying occupied homes. The selection
does not impose a national public-stock percentage, a single density, shared
households or relocation to optimize an aggregate account.
