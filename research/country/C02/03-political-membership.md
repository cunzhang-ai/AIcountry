# C02. Political membership

*Part of [C02](../C02-membership-and-rights.md); Design319 research, not enacted law.*

The reference ordinary national franchise gives one vote and eligibility to stand
to adult citizens from 18 and to adult noncitizens from 18 after [one continuous
year of actual habitual residence](03/01-resident-franchise-and-secure-status.md).
This residence period is a provisional democratic choice, not a proven optimum. Local voting and candidacy begin after a
six cumulative months of national habitual residence and current actual municipal residence;
a later genuine municipal move does not restart that six-month waiting period. Time as a refugee, asylum seeker or
under disputed administrative status counts when habitual residence can be shown;
eligibility is not controlled by an employer, landlord or AI account. [MB01–MB20](06-citizenship-and-secure-belonging.md) now specifies territorial
birth, overseas descent, free voluntary naturalization and stable home after
four continuous habitual years, with temporary/coerced absence safeguards and
no involuntary citizenship deprivation. Citizenship is not the only route to
national political standing; previously vested residence need not requalify after return. Citizens abroad retain a usable ballot under adopted
registration and duplicate-vote controls. All eligibility and exclusion decisions
need timely independent remedies and safe alternatives to a single document or
biometric system. No one can lose a vote through arbitrary denaturalization or
politically motivated removal. The [membership comparison](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-membership-review/analysis.md)
records the rival citizenship-only model and the unresolved value tradeoffs.
Core franchise and candidacy rights belong in the protected constitutional settlement;
registration law may administer them but cannot narrow the electorate for the
incumbent's benefit. Changes to electoral eligibility cannot govern the current
officeholders' next contest, apart from the closed prior-law [RR restoration rule](../C14/08-democratic-repair-without-self-authorization.md).
The proposed expanded electorate cannot approve itself; existing lawful rights
remedies and the full amendment route remain distinct.

This ordinary electorate does not decide its own founding authority. A legitimate
founding population and ratification process must be established beforehand with
the affected people; this project's author cannot choose it. Actual residence,
citizenship and diaspora rules would need lawful adoption in a real jurisdiction.


[EL01–EL04](../C03/13-equal-franchise-and-usable-elections.md) now selects an
explicit prospective P-date age/vesting test for called votes/candidacy/entry/
public credits, not civil majority or annual/private PC receipt eligibility.
It protects one real local electoral-home choice, including forced displacement,
and a full national ballot without an artificial overseas territory. Local-only
residents and permanently abroad citizens have different actual local connections;
no identity account supplies them. Exact prior law/MB rights and RR protected
succession persist until coordinated adoption. Supported expression is not a
capacity test or a caregiver/AI proxy vote; source/custody and real relief remain.

**Design143 territorial join.** [TS](../C04/02-territorial-self-government.md)
uses existing local standing/genuine home for its distinct area public, including
forced-displacement protections, while citizens abroad without an area home keep
national standing. Final constitutional/status eligibility is actual ballot-day,
not ordinary EL future-P anticipation. TS's D-date area petition denominator does
not change RR06's national initiative-opening denominator or confer identity/title.

[AR01–AR05](../C03/16/05-hearing-people-without-a-national-vote.md)
protects affected evidence and a committee response before an ordinary
national vote. Earlier national standing remains separate from this
protected hearing. Actual lawful adoption and prior elections retain
their own procedures.
