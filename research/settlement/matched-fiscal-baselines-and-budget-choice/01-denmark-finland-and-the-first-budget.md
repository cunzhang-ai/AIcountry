# Denmark, Finland and the first-budget baseline

Observed 2024 general-government figures from pinned Eurostat
publication and API snapshots. Figures are percentages of each
country's GDP. Differences are percentage points. Expenditure by
function uses COFOG, with its total shown separately.

| Measure | Denmark | Finland |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Fiscal notification total expenditure | 46.5 | 57.9 |
| Fiscal notification total revenue | 50.6 | 53.4 |
| Reported net lending or borrowing | +4.1 | −4.4 |
| Reported year-end gross debt | 29.9 | 82.5 |
| Functional expenditure total | 47.3 | 57.7 |
| Functional social protection | 19.6 | 26.5 |
| Functional health | 8.3 | 7.7 |

The fiscal balances differ by 8.5 points. In the functional data,
social protection is 6.9 points higher in Finland and health is
0.6 points higher in Denmark. These comparisons show the fiscal
scope and recorded allocation. They do not compare service
quality, coverage, health outcomes or democratic control.

Denmark's functional total is 0.8 points above its fiscal
notification expenditure. Finland's is 0.2 points below. The ten
displayed functional headings sum to 47.3 and 57.8, respectively.
The separate 0.1-point functional subtotal difference for Finland
is consistent with one-decimal publication rounding. Revenue less
expenditure in Finland's fiscal notification is −4.5 at displayed
precision, versus reported net borrowing of −4.4. Keep the
published balance. Do not treat the cross-table differences as
rounding or invent an accounting explanation. Substituting the
functional totals would produce 3.3 and −4.3, which are invalid
mixed-source balances.

The selected Danish cells for revenue and net lending are absent
from the pinned main-aggregates API response. The fiscal notification
publishes 2024 revenue and the separate deficit and debt API provides
reported net lending. Retain both the documented source and the
missing values. The fiscal release's note on Danish revenue relates
to 2025 and does not explain the 2024 cross-table residual.

**First-budget disposition.** Retain the standing duties and consider
only independently financed repairs or paid preparation before the
actual domestic baseline is qualified. A national expansion cannot
use the Danish surplus, the Finnish spending mix or either
cross-table discrepancy as source. Put the full spending, recurring
finance, worker availability and lawful service receiving of a
broader proposal to a separate elected choice. This leaves genuine
retain-and-repair and other funded alternatives open.
