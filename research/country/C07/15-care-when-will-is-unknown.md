# C07. Actual care when the person's will remains unknown

*Integrated design82 · Consumer of [UA01–UA20](../C08/23-necessary-care-and-unknown-will.md); research, not enacted law.*

S01–S12 remain the entitlement/receiving floor; they do not create substitute
consent. UA is an express proposed narrow residual authority after serious
communication support, actual prior direction and defensible interpretation fail.
A service assessment never removes legal capacity. Current will/refusal and
credible opposition cannot be relabeled unknown because care seems beneficial.

The clinical/local care authority obtains real support, independent advocacy and
an accepted funded delivery plan before needed court authorization. Perform a
consequential act only after that authorization and actual safe receiving by the
qualified operator, paid shifts, place, medicine/equipment and next handoff. No family
member is the compulsory reserve; no pension, budget voucher or AI companion
replaces a necessary human/social or clinical task. Authority and actual provision
are distinct; unknown preferences do not let a purchaser reduce entitled care.

UA05's explicit minimum noncoercive basic-care duty avoids waiting for a court
for each drink or hygiene act. It does not include tube feeding, new drugs,
restraints, covert medication or closed placement. A new risky/irreversible act,
necessary therapy with unavoidable reproductive effects or life-support decision
needs UA's own heightened act-specific conditions and prior judicial review.
These legal conditions are not clinical safety standards; actual qualified
professionals must apply justified current evidence and safe alternatives.

A safe nonclosed move preserves actual ability to leave with help, relationships
and tenure/property; no unavailable home-support list proves the person prefers
an institution. The public authority contracts/pays in its own role, not under
an invented private signature or loan. Actual shortages trigger C06's lawful
source/provision choices and unmet-right remedies, not coerced treatment or a
quality-of-life denial. Continued dependence is not futility by itself.

Actual choice, objection or adverse change prompts pause/reassessment and
correction. Expired order authority does not renew by silence. UA16's separate minimum
dependent-treatment continuity prevents expiry-triggered serious harm while
urgent review is pursued; no escalation or objection override follows. Statutory basics
and other independently lawful clinical/consent duties remain precisely within
their own grounds. Safe transition must be arranged before expiry. The
[C06 chain](../C06/31-necessary-care-and-independent-review-capacity.md) counts
independent review and actual receiving alongside ordinary service/election/liberty
duties once. No actual staff/cost/source, lawful adoption or outcome is established.
