# Protect the same basket after payment access returns

*Design484 · Constructed comparison through temporary and recurring provision of household choice, food access and public cost.*

Continue the [bank and currency stress case](../bank-and-currency-stress/01-one-month-under-three-regimes.md)
after payments recover. Hold the price of one necessary weekly food
basket at 5 units, up from 4, across two periods. The six residents
can spend 4 units each after any reachable standing hardship support.
Four can use ordinary shops. Two lack a timely suitable shop, even
if they receive another unit in cash. A suitable basket for them must
be delivered with usable preparation or ready-to-eat access. Assume
all six would choose to obtain this basket if reachable. The fixture
does not infer need from transactions or require restricted spending.
Other lawful pay, income and food claims remain funded on their own
terms. For this comparison define the two periods as consecutive
decision and delivery windows before and after a regular monthly
update, with the same six basket needs in each window.

## Compare the first and second periods on the same sources

Authorize up to 8 incremental units for this component per period
in the full-source comparison. In the first period the ordinary
price update has yet to apply. In the second it already requires
one additional unit for each of the same six recipients, at a gross
cost of 6. The fixture assumes the enacted formula and individual
award produce those amounts. The routine award remains due if an
optional route fails. Assume the temporary first-period cash supplement
is lawfully additional for that period only, with no later set-off.
Do not use the same observation as proof that the second-period
increment was received.

| Option | First period | Second period |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary update only | No added cost. All six have an unmet basket under the assumed resources. Existing urgent routes remain applicable. | The six ordinary increments cost 6. Four can shop. Two still lack a timely, suitable route. |
| Uniform cash bridge before the update | Six gross increments cost 6. All retain control. Four obtain the basket, two cannot obtain it. | No additional uniform bridge. The ordinary 6 remains due and the two access gaps remain. |
| Voluntary local fulfillment alongside cash | Four individual cash increments cost 4. Two separately bought service slots cost 2 each including dispatch, paid work and acceptance. Gross 8 enables all six baskets on the stated assumptions. | The six ordinary increments cost 6. Two paid domestic delivery slots now cost 1 each after a contracted route becomes operational. Gross 8 enables all six baskets. |

For the two who opt in, the delivering provider offers a suitable
basket at the ordinary pre-shock price of 4 in the first period and
at 5 in the second, with the public payments buying the service
slots and covering the first-period price gap. First period provider
cost is 6 per slot, financed by 4 voluntary purchase and 2 public
support. Second period provider cost is still 6 per slot, financed
by 5 voluntary purchase and 1 separate public delivery cost. No
provider absorbs the difference. Residents pay the vendor directly
and government owes only its priced share. A public buyer liable
for the gross invoice before copays would need 16 and 18. Both periods
require suitable stock, paid staff and an accepting receiver.
No I17 credit is authorized for this contract. Cash protection continues.
Declining the route leaves ordinary rights and review intact.
Do not infer that a merchant referral or transfer credit secured
an actual basket.

## What can reverse the choice

If the two excluded residents gain ordinary suitable retail access
at the same price, the simpler uniform first-period bridge and
later ordinary update suffice at 6 per period. If the supplier
cannot reload, or a supposedly local source shares the affected
transport or only offers unsuitable food, the two delivery slots
do not qualify. Counting receipts must reflect any refusal and
actual substitution, not just dispatch. If only 7 units are
actually available in the second period, all 6 in ordinary awards
remain due. At most one 1-unit delivery slot can be funded on
these terms. Apply applicable lawful urgency and equal-priority
process, make the other unmet need visible, and seek a competent
elected source or separately funded provider. No unspent first-period
authority, aging reserve or forecast refund fills the gap.

Check price paid and actual supply for other shoppers, including
nonrecipients and local producers. A high nominal take-up rate
could coexist with smaller quantities or another household losing
a slot. Keep the unit of evaluation the person and period, while
measuring provider and local market effects. Test transport, cooking,
safety, resident preference, uncompensated work, overhead and
capture risks. Do not carry a market-effect estimate from another
jurisdiction into these numbers.

## Make an ordinary service choice while prices stay high

The third period begins after the temporary delivery contract ends.
The ordinary award remains 6 and the two people still lack suitable
stores. Compare two recurring budgets with the same resident rights.
Renewal needs 2 recurring units to carry two verified solo deliveries.
A joint local route can serve both for 1 recurring unit, including
paid work and ordinary replenishment, if an independent qualified
provider accepts both and each resident chooses and can pay the same
5 directly. The public 1 buys delivery, with no automatic award credit.
Its 3-unit startup
and contingency handoff are due before the temporary contract ends
and come from a separate source. Three periods at 1 less per period
recover the startup cost against fully funded renewal. With 7 recurring
units, renewal already leaves a slot uncovered. Select the joint route only when starting
cash, separate staffing, dietary and offline access, closure and
a paid interim route qualify. If the claimed joint route draws the
same staff or stock away from other people already due service,
reprice it on genuinely additional capacity or reject it. If the
start misses its cutoff, record uncovered slots and preserve correction.
Replacing a source may change who controls access. The alternate
must be able to accept a complaint without the dominant supplier.

Keep lower prices distinct from a revised basket. A cheaper offer of
the same specification belongs in the lawful price evidence and
does not automatically reduce the nominal floor. A different food,
quality or preparation requirement needs the competent prospective
standards decision if it changes the basket itself. A resident can
freely accept an equivalent alternative under existing standards.
That person's receipt can affect an award only where applicable
law allows matching and protects the cash minimum. The provider's
funding and household's paid share alone establish no credit.

## Count the separate financial claim linked to the basket

In a separate scale check, an enacted national adult B increases from
100 to 120. An eligible live deposit of 1,500 raises its maximum coverage
from 1,200 to 1,440 under M07. An equally large deposit whose insurance
claim already crystallized retains the original failure-date cap. With
no usable insurer cash and only 200 of unused funding above the previously
covered exposure, the 240 increase creates a 40 legal headroom gap
before any other account changes. Actual cash at payout would still
need its own check. Disclose the gap, pause the applicable new
protected growth and seek elected funding. Do not delay the lawful
income update or reprice the old failure. A local delivery subsidy
is outside the national basket parameter. These authored figures
are separate from the six-person budget. At the same B change,
I05's earnings disregard moves from 20 to 24. With earnings 80,
a half withdrawal and no other components, the ordinary award
moves from 70 to 92. Cost the actual formula rather than adding
the headline basket change to each cheque.

## Decide at the funded boundary

Select a person-controlled bridge where suitable supply works, and
add voluntary paid receiving only where shortage or access excludes.
Protect the ordinary statutory update separately. Before the last
funded cutoff, the Assembly must see a standing-law baseline,
an exact funded continuation motion and a qualified supply rival.
If prices remain high, fixed-duration relief cannot simply renew
itself. A competent legislature funds a time-bounded continuation
or makes a prospective structural choice with the opportunity cost
visible. No failure to vote cancels original entitlements, pays an
unbound order or makes an expiring bridge permanent. Review delays
and nonreceipt under the original authority. AI can compare lawful
price and stock observations, flag overlapping claims and expose
forecast errors. It cannot decide individual receipt or compel a
household to disclose transactions.
