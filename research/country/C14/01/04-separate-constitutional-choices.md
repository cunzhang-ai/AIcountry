# Let electors decide distinct constitutional changes separately

*Design333 · CRV01–CRV05 · Proposed A9 procedure for partial amendments.*

**CRV01 — Ask separately about materially independent constitutional changes.**
Before a partial amendment is authorized for the ballot, state each material
change in rights, power, accountability, resources or protected timing.
Give each independently operable choice its own question and exact legal
text. Several clauses can be one question where they are objectively
necessary to an intelligible, lawful common operation. A shared sponsor,
policy theme, bargaining bargain or title does not establish necessity.
Identify what would happen if each material change were absent.
Related implementation and safeguards may be kept together where
separation would leave a legally or operationally incomplete choice.
Apply the rule equally to Assembly and petition routes. Legitimate whole
founding or replacement remains under its separate constitutional authority.

**CRV02 — Make dependencies explicit without transferring assent.** Before
authorization publish the effect of each possible combination, its resources
and commencement. Where one separately operable question is lawfully
conditional on another, state the exact prerequisite, fallback and latest
date in the authorized text and in the accessible ballot explanation.
A condition needs a real legal or operating dependency. It cannot force a
choice about unrelated policy. Each question must satisfy its own initiation,
approval and referendum threshold. Do not pool votes or substitute a sponsor's
preferred result. If an approved dependent change lacks its prerequisite at
the stated date, it has no operative effect. Existing law governs conflicting
proposals within its scope. No automatic preference for the higher vote
total, incumbent text or later announcement is created by this procedure.

**CRV03 — Settle scope before commitment to the ballot.** Publish proposed
scope and dependencies before the relevant signature or Assembly approval
stage and offer timely protected preflight review. After exact authorization,
the competent predecessor-law constitutional forum decides a material
separation or ballot challenge with public reasons and actual rights of
reply. Use existing jurisdiction, composition, recusal and votes. The
sponsor must show a concrete dependency for disputed grouping. Courts can
reject unlawful grouping and refer the question to the lawful sponsors.
They may not write a new bargain, treat old signatures as support for
changed text or revive an unapproved part. Independently authorized and
unchanged questions keep their valid dates and opportunity. Materially
revised or newly separated text needs its own lawful authorization and
full final-text consideration. Preserve direct remedies for official
delay without inferring signatures, consent or extra jurisdiction.

**CRV04 — Protect the voter and the predecessor procedure through effect.**
The election authority provides equal neutral instructions, accessible
separate choices, actual campaign opportunity and question-specific
results and remedies. Leaving one question blank does not spoil another
lawful choice. Each operative change uses the actual predecessor
electorate and A9 timetable, thresholds and rules. Apply RR independently
to each protected subject and any effect of a dependency. A ballot
grouping cannot accelerate protected succession, weaken review or turn
an ordinary improvement into a lawful restoration. These proposed
safeguards themselves require legitimate predecessor adoption before use.

**CRV05 — Keep the choice explainable and reviewable.** Preserve exact
texts, authorized versions, safe evidence, independent reasons,
separate outcomes and how each effective text reached the public.
Price qualified review and timely ballot production. AI may identify
possible dependencies and compare legal versions from lawful evidence.
It cannot certify question scope, infer consent or declare adoption.
Reopen on evasive grouping, needless fragmentation, exclusion by delay,
or a better mechanism preserving genuinely separable choice and a
workable lawful transition.

tests distinct approval and a lawful dependency. Actual voter access,
qualified authority and adoption remain open.
