# A lawful total is not every permission and no permission creates cash

*Part of [budget government](README.md); all quantities below are authored.*

A100fixed project with20first cash needs actual authority for its full binding
scope, not just20. Completion100 versus mutually exclusive cancellation40 is a

Indefinite teachers/care workers retain actual authorized positions, current

An appropriation permits maximum lawful use; an implementation duty or standing
right separately requires action. Genuine changed facts can end a discretionary
project with reasons, but programme-wide abandonment returns to elected review
and cannot redirect source or stay due rights. No spending race is required.

Cabinet-envelope budgeting can simplify work but concentrate reallocations;
detailed member votes can improve control but overload scrutiny or enable narrow
bargains. Local/participatory allocation can disclose needs but exclude quieter
people or impose costs elsewhere. The selected limited-input-discretion model
is rejectable. A full-N vote and a calendar do not establish public understanding,
actual opposition influence, independent receiving or a funded national offer.
Positive founding sources remain unresolved; this procedure is no substitute.
