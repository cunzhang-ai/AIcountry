# C03. Keep unfinished public acts within lawful authority during handover

*Design413 · DPA01–DPA08 · Proposed ordinary implementation of lawful handover.*

An office can change hands while a signature, scheduled instruction or delegated
workflow is still moving. Determine the legal effect of each stage and who
may perform it. Continue established duties without carrying expired
political discretion into the next government.

**DPA01 — Identify the act that changes legal position.** Before material
reliance on a deferred or delegated workflow, name the accountable public
owner, applicable law, exact operative content, reserved decisions and
events required for legal effect. Separate drafting, authentication,
dispatch, required receipt or acceptance, creation of an obligation,
commencement, execution and usable receipt. Different laws can put legal
effect at different stages. A software timestamp or delivery convention
cannot supply a missing condition. Preserve material changes in terms
and independent decisions as separate acts.

**DPA02 — Check authority at each reserved stage.** At the lawful change of
office, the political holder's expired authority and mandates dependent
solely on it cannot support a later reserved act. An earlier signature,
queued instruction, delayed notice or retained credential does not extend
that authority. A delegation expressly continuing under law may operate
within its own limits. An established obligation can be performed later by
an authorized payer or operator without reopening its lawful merits. Its
future effective date is distinct from when it became binding. A genuinely
new discretionary act requires current competent authority and actual
conditions at the stage the law specifies.

**DPA03 — Carry evidence and unresolved status through the boundary.**
Each service owner maintains independently usable originals for material
pending acts and their authority, receipt, obligations and expiry. Distinguish
verified completed, independently continuing, still proposed and disputed
acts. Check the authentic source and chronology, including delayed delivery
and the applicable local or independent authority. Preserve the person's
original request, testimony, notices, objections and remedies. Protect
scope, confidentiality and retention. Where the legal effect is uncertain,
hold only the unsupported consequential step and seek a useful-time
determination. Unknown status establishes neither a new obligation nor its
cancellation.

**DPA04 — Protect existing duties through a partition.** Predesignate
separate funded operators for independently authorized continuing work,
with nonoverlapping assignments, own evidence, defined amounts, expiry
and usable review. A disconnected service can perform only the verified
scope it already lawfully holds. Confirm its own source and actual capacity.
It cannot exercise reserved discretion from a stale copy. Where proof or
authority is unavailable, retain lawful urgent support and unaffected work.
Record the specific missed duty. No universal credential, automatic
emergency mandate or vendor approval of political succession follows.

**DPA05 — Put real pending choices before their competent successor.** Give
the current lawful office the actual proposal, material evidence, pending
conditions, available alternatives, existing commitments and last useful
time. Where a new decision is required, the successor chooses within the
ordinary legal, budget, caretaker and review limits. Preserve independent
offices' separate decisions. Any fresh approval takes effect on its own
lawful date. It does not backdate authority or cure an earlier breach by
renaming it a handover. A missing or disputed successor does not extend an
expired mandate.

**DPA06 — Reconcile deferred execution and protect lawful reliance.** When
routes reconnect, verify original instructions, state changes, settlements
and actual receipt before resuming affected work. Use [TPE](../../C06/60/11-public-cash-and-independent-execution.md)
for payment finality and exclusive release. Do not reissue a final or uncertain
payment merely because a second record appears. Where a premature act
caused harm, use the competent remedy, preserve third-party protections
and distinguish valid accrued rights, disputed reliance and restitution.
A technical rollback cannot reverse legal effect or actual loss.

**DPA07 — Give independent review a real receiver.** Affected people,
counterparties and lawful opposition may challenge withheld evidence,
expired authority or failure to execute a valid duty through the competent
independent route. Identify the act, original deadline, record custodian,
actual payer or executor and proposed narrow remedy. Fund independent
records, review and execution without permission from the challenged
officeholder or provider. Courts and independent bodies retain their
own jurisdiction. AI may compare records and flag unresolved sequences.
It cannot recognize an officeholder, determine legal effect or authorize
an act.

**DPA08 — Test cross-boundary acts and keep the process replaceable.**
Before reliance, test a valid prior obligation with future execution,
a signed proposal lacking a reserved act, delayed notice, a divided
record, an attempted repeat and loss of the ordinary operator. Test
actual receiving and the next useful deadline. Compare a blanket stop,
ordinary scoped operation and independently funded continuity. Adopt
prospectively with original clocks, contracts, claims, lawfully retained
data and paid successors. Reopen on stale authority, blocked lawful
continuity, unsupported reliance or a simpler qualified route.
