# MO05–MO08: measured time, booking and safe return

*Part of proposed C07/17; design 94 research candidate.*

**MO05 — Select local parameters inside a national bound.** The default local
standard is at most60 minutes from the person's usable departure point to the
usable destination entrance, including walking/wheeling, waiting, transfers
and access support, with at most one transfer. The existing elected local
council may select a published sparse-area band above60 and at most90 minutes
on a reasoned geography/access/safety comparison with network, demand-responsive,
closer-service and supported-housing options. It must hear nonusers, disabled
people, children/carers, workers and neighboring receiving areas; publish costs,
burdens, objections and a twelve-month review. Independent courts review lawful
criteria, evidence and unequal exclusion. Cost preference or current refusal
to procure is not geography. A longer individual trip needs actual agreement;
it does not redefine the floor or suspend a claim. Shorter sector deadlines,
existing stronger rights and actual clinical safety prevail.

The default service area reaches the nearest actually usable hub and essential
connections even across a local border. Within it offer ordinary departures
between06:00 and23:00 daily under MO03. Essential shift/care/public duties outside
those hours require an assessed paid plan, not automatic exclusion. Councils may
improve these defaults; changing the national ceiling, ordinary minimum or
coverage requires prospective elected national law and rights transition.
The area/band determination precedes individual allocation and is publicly
challengeable, not a dispatcher's unreviewable exception. Missing local rules
leave these defaults, not zero coverage. Actual inability is non-delivery.

**MO06 — Confirm a real plan.** Accept assisted/offline requests and preserve
the first authorized attempted contact. Twenty-four hours' notice secures the
ordinary booking process; planned essential journeys should be filed as soon
as known, without losing a right because a provider/court gave shorter notice.
Confirm or give a reasoned failure/alternative within four elapsed hours of a
request received06:00–23:00; an overnight ordinary request is considered by10:00.
These are outer response bounds, not transport permission to miss useful time.
The existing first receiver routes urgent/out-of-hours sector need immediately
and retains responsibility until an actual competent receiver accepts.

A confirmation names outward and return windows, destination acceptance where
relevant, accessible capacity/assistance, operator, safe contact and fallback.
A pickup window is no wider than20 minutes; the complete journey still meets
MO05 and must arrive before an actual clinical/legal/work safe deadline with
the required access margin. A vehicle or driver not actually available cannot
be confirmed. Routine booking limits cannot defeat urgent source-law protection.
Provide same-day flexible travel where available without falsely guaranteeing
unlimited spontaneous or simultaneous journeys.

Councils publish an accessible booking-allocation rule: first protect each
person's two minimum opportunities before discretionary ordinary extras, offering
compatible alternatives in the same week and preserving access/sector duties.
Within lawfully available ordinary slots, use published rotation or a witnessed
draw for materially equivalent unavoidable conflicts, not fastest app, wealth,
party, a new account or private priority purchase. Do not consume a minimum for
a cancelled, unsuitable or undelivered journey. A draw allocates a scarce slot;
it does not discharge the losing claim or establish adequate promised capacity.
Record and correct unmet opportunities; this creates no authority to divert
protected sector resources or extend an enacted start.

**MO07 — Reach the person, destination and return.** Identify actual usable
boarding/drop-off points, safe walking/wheeling distance, transfers, assistance,
rest, weather exposure, luggage/equipment and child's safeguarding needs. Do not
require an inaccessible stop, unsafe roadside wait, smartphone location feed or
public disclosure of a refuge. Record arrival and accepted handoff proportionately;
an operator may not claim receipt from GPS or ticket validation alone.
Return is part of the offered journey. A cancellation, breakdown or last-service
failure requires safe contact and paid accepting return/overnight protection as
actually necessary; no abandonment, forced unsafe lift or default closed placement.
Tracking/assistance consent is separate from consent to the destination's service.

**MO08 — Network first, real fallback.** Fund compatible scheduled buses,
walking/wheeling links and interlocal connections first where they meet need.
Where they fail, secure paid qualified accessible demand-responsive or contracted
capacity at the same protected price, including sparse places and unusual needs.
No retainer qualifies until actual vehicle/access, driver/assistance, hours,
maintenance, independent route/control and receiving are matched. Keep driver
TW rest/pay/learning, commercial passenger safety and sector competence intact.
Capacity is attributed once across ordinary travel, school, clinical, care and
reserve tasks. A sector vehicle is not spare merely because the user is absent
at the instant of dispatch. No forced private vehicle, worker or relative fills
a gap. Failure uses existing useful-time relief and elected correction.
