# Design254 decision record. Public payment and independent receipt

10 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned Design253](baseline.json)

## Decision and authority

Select [TU13–TU17](../country/C04/12/04-independent-payment-and-receipt.md)
for established public subsistence and pension cash. Extend the current
TU08 alternate into a no-account option on first contact, a continuing
first-payer duty and a separate funded national route. Preserve the
original amount and clock. Access to the cash right belongs to the
person. The public payer remains responsible through a bank, contractor
or voluntary representative, including a promptly honored revocation
within its lawful control. This decision adds no money or governing power.

The decision owner for adoption would be the elected legislature under
existing procedures. The programme retains the award and payment owner.
Any backup execution and separately funded advances require express
scope, sources and valid appointment. Use the existing ordinary court
and oversight authorities, protected originals and paid execution.
Neither a payment contractor nor a new AI route can approve itself.
Use the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

## Competing diagnoses and the choice

Copenhagen's observed failure had an established award and no usable
method of receipt. A general entitlement gap or an inability to assess
need does not explain that case. The bank-account barrier and official
workflow each mattered. In the later municipal review, wording and
placement in the workflow still favored the bank route. A reported
third-party loss identifies a different risk in apparently successful
settlement. Treat the two individual cases and the administrative
counts within their reported scope. [Original evidence and limits](sources.md)

The strongest simple rival reforms the existing payer's workflow and
contracts for an on-time direct point, with no new universal account.
Retain it where it can provide the same reliable coverage, independent
review and common-loss receiving. A national retail network buys scale
and reach and can leave a single redemption root and gaps in cash,
identity or travel. A universal public account can reduce private
gatekeeping but can centralize visibility and exclusion and still needs
an independent receiving route. The selected model combines qualified
ordinary settlement, plural physical receiving and a named residual
public payer without requiring everyone to enroll in a new account.
The [comparison](../settlement/public-payment-receipt/01-observed-routes-and-the-budget-choice.md)
records each alternative and its remaining burden.

A persistent no-account option may cost more, attract theft or shift
work to local frontline staff. Compare travel, household coercion,
cash stock, security and accessibility, including paid safe support
for homebound recipients. Protect legitimate account and settlement
controls, minimal identity proof and exclusive disbursement. A
credible revocation cannot move another person's legal title by
itself. An ambiguous already-settled instruction may require a court
or review decision. Pay the undisputed balance and use authorized
urgent help rather than choosing between abandonment and duplication.
The one-working-day response is a candidate correction limit,
subject to earlier deadlines and harm and actual staffed testing.
[Authored release and receiving comparison](../settlement/public-payment-receipt/02-revocation-and-exclusive-release.md)

## Evidence, succession and the next national test

Retain TU, AP, CRD, C06 finance, JC, PCO, PD, binding private mandates
and civil remedies. Correct each existing award under the law that
created it and carry stopped instructions, unsettled mandates, paid
and unpaid amounts, safe contacts, liability disputes and review rights
into migration. Trace final payment and access without tracking purchases.
Payment methods cannot change who is entitled, or sweep unrelated
funds. Qualify the backup against shared supplier and credential loss.

The observed audit and service terms qualify the existence of the
problem and credible institutional options. The authored ledger
qualifies a bounded authority and settlement reconciliation. Actual
participation by affected people, independent review of this proposal,
legitimate adoption, full pricing, readiness and improved lives are
unverified. The external sources do not price the candidate.

Use one real protected payment run in the next joint first-budget
comparison. Record a failed ordinary rail, earlier and later due
dates, concurrent local and independent duties, actual reserve
ownership, and who can pay when a representative is withdrawn.
Require accepted evidence, paid staff, safe refusal or actual
receiving at the original deadline and an independent reviewer.
If protected access is unavailable, use redacted records and leave
receiving unresolved. Reopen for actual late payments, dependency
loss, recipient or staff harm, unsafe redirection, altered public
authority, new source evidence or a more effective simpler route.
