# NR05–NR08. An exploration result cannot grant its own production licence

*Part of [mineral rights](../15-finite-resources-and-mineral-rights.md).*

**NR05 — Separate exploration, access and knowledge.** Each exploration grant
names the actual mineral/right, area/depth, specified survey/disturbance, final
date, rehabilitation/security, qualified operator, data/cost terms and review.
Intrusive access needs actual lawful land/collective permission or a competent
paid LA route, not an exploration badge or an owner's general map. Discovery
creates no automatic production, perpetual finder priority, surface possession
or compensation for an unpromised mine. Any agreed data purchase/cost recovery
must be defined, lawfully funded and disclosed before reliance; private risk
is not automatically public debt. Require timely protected delivery of original
geological/quantity/method/uncertainty data and safe sample custody under grant
terms, with defined public release, actual lawful limited confidentiality and
PD/KR rights. No perpetual secret public-resource contract, forced disclosure
of unrelated know-how/private testimony or automatic model-training permission.

**NR06 — A finite production right is an exact accountable instrument.** Before
award publish resource/area/depth, permitted quantity and method corridor, exact
final date, staged work/closure obligations, public price/payment formula and
currency, cost/price risk, operating/source prerequisites, monitoring, defaults,
transfer/change-of-control, remedy and end conditions. No grant lasts 'until
exhaustion' without a finite final date, automatic renewal or implied enlargement.
Set a term no longer than the disclosed reasonably necessary project/investment
case within the actual elected depletion corridor; exact wording cannot hide
an effectively perpetual right. No new clause freezes future lawful rights,
ecological/tax law or secretly guarantees price/FX/revenue. Existing contracts/
external commitments need their actual lawful revision, not unilateral erasure.
A fresh renewal/expansion needs fresh valid public choice/award and source/
ecology/security review; an expired right cannot self-renew through sunk cost.
Select no universal mine-life maximum or royalty percentage without actual
geology/rights/investment evidence. Exact dates/quantities before any grant are
mandatory; unknown is not permission for an evergreen concession. Missed work
milestones may trigger only specified proportionate notice/cure/termination
with fair AP/V review, not guilt, property forfeiture or a minister's new penalty.

**NR07 — Open qualified whole-life award, with no-award allowed.** Use published
competitive proposals for the identified public right and comparable obligations,
verified actual controllers/conflicts, technical/financial capability, work and
closure/security source, evaluation method and reasons for the chosen award route before bids. Highest
nominal payment alone cannot win an unsafe/unfunded bid. Public/cooperative/
nonprofit/private operators compete on actual comparable paid costs/mandates;
public title or common state ownership proves no independence/capability. Reject
all offers rather than promise absent guarantees or secretly lower duties.
Select10working days from usable publication/affected notice of the intended
recipient/controllers, full operative rights/price/closure terms and reasons to
executing the new exclusive grant. A materially changed award needs fresh affected
notice/standstill; protected publication is no proof all claimants were reached.
Ordinary AP/V/B challenge periods, direct interim relief and actual stays remain,
with no mandatory internal exhaustion. A bare
petition does not itself create an indefinite veto, and a missing court is not
a fictional rejection/approval. No clock authorizes extraction before actual
title/consent/operating/security prerequisites. Lawful urgent safety powers
remain separate; a bid or missed clock never approves extraction. Publish actual full grant/amendments,
controllers, quantities/payment/closure terms and reasoned noncompetitive
exceptions; protect narrowly justified private/security facts with ordinary
review, not secret operative burdens. A public owner selling a right is not
ordinary SC purchasing by analogy. Its source Act must enact this award route;
SC applies when the state actually buys services/equipment.

**NR08 — Entry, transfer and small operations do not erase safeguards.** Define
commercial extraction and material physical disturbance by actual prior law;
ordinary lawful minor domestic/cultural uses retain their actual rights and
access limits. Select a proportionate small/cooperative-operation permission
route with AP assistance, objective accessible criteria and actual qualified
paid pooled/support options where lawfully available; scarce comparable limited
rights use a prepublished neutral draw rather than wealth/party preference.
No blanket auction-only gate, automatic first-finder title, ecological/worker
exemption or unlimited state guarantee. This does not compel a private owner
to admit anyone. Corporate/beneficial-control transfer needs prior source-law
approval, disclosed actual transferee competence/closure acceptance and MP's
separate actual merger rules; it restarts neither term nor liability. No shell
sale, token or agent copy transfers a public mandate or unpaid old obligations.
Unlicensed status alone creates no new imprisonment/arrest offense; actual
P/K/TS source, fault and fair process remain. Dangerous work gets its actual
safe cessation/protection without inventing a police or forced-work power.
