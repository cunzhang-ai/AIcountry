investigator hours before hour 8 and two in the next staffed window.

contacts and independent duties below are available only as stated.

actual priority and uses the next two hours for the shop case.
All three next steps are timely, without changing any proof standard.
A missing suspect is still possible after every reasonable step.

## Shared loss and a real alternate

If a common failure removes two early investigator hours, prioritize
the harassment step on its earlier lawful deadline and credible safety
risk. The burglary still needs two independent hours. An affected
person can report the inaction directly. Assume the paid independent
reviewer accepts that request in time and has its own one-hour
review slot. It can check the recorded lead and specify a lawful
step, but cannot perform the lost investigation with that hour.
With two separately funded alternate investigator hours available
before hour 8, actual acceptance by the camera holder can keep the
recording. If the alternate shares the failed root or the request
arrives after the useful time, report the loss and any remaining
lawful routes. A later review cannot recover the overwritten record.

A genuinely exhausted case can close with reasons even when the
reporter disagrees. If two AI summaries cite the same original
image, they supply one source family. Neither refusal to participate
nor a lost lead justifies guilt, compulsory disclosure or withdrawal
of the person's independent safety and support route.
