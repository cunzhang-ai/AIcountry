# Design248 decision record. Territorial capital and basic access

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned Design247](baseline.json)

## Decision and diagnosis

Select [IV13–IV17](../country/C06/63/04-territorial-capital-and-basic-access.md).
Use actual people, service duties and received access to compare national
or cross-local capital choices. Bring unavoidable local capacity and
operating costs into the same elected decision. Do not require a uniform
local matching percentage to fund investment needed for the common
statutory floor. Use MF's attributed sources and legitimate national
responsibility, while keeping local elected delivery choices.
Apply the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

Existing IV evaluates projects with ordinary and adverse schedules. MF
accounts for recurrent local need and source capacity. Together they
still leave open whether the bid's upfront local money determines which
place can finance an enforceable duty. A single fixed share can give
the fiscally stronger area access to funds while a cheaper adequate
route elsewhere remains unavailable. Nor does repairing geographic
inequality justify assuming every small place needs a duplicate facility.
The design protects usable access for people with different needs
within any area, including underserved parts of dense regions.

## Compare coherent alternatives

| Choice | Strong reason | Material exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Allocate capital by a fixed match and expected aggregate use | Simple cost sharing and a useful signal of local demand. | Cash capacity and bid readiness can screen out real needs. Higher total use can coexist with breached duties. |
| Concentrate service at a hub with scheduled accessible transport | Can use scarce professionals and capital more effectively. | Needs actual arrival, a safe return and an independently functioning route through foreseeable disruption. |
| Fund the common floor with source-adjusted contributions and choose a mixed portfolio | Permits adequate local repair and independent outreach where they work, without ruling out shared hubs. | Requires original need, capacity and lifecycle evidence. Bad benchmarks can reward deferral or obscure hidden exclusions. |
| Guarantee a comparable facility in every locality | Gives a clear visible commitment and reduces some travel. | Large fixed costs, thin staffing and duplicated capabilities can reduce useful access. Local autonomy and substantive receiving require more than a building. |

Select the third design. In the [authored portfolio](../settlement/territorial-capital-access/01-two-places-and-one-budget.md)
the smaller dense repair and protected remote route use the full combined
capital and recurrent margins. The selected case forgoes some elective
expansion and maintains due service at both locations under one road
closure. A better funded independent hub can win. If real dense need or
common failure is larger, the selected case must be reopened. Priority
belongs to legal duties and the elected comparison, not a category
of locality. Actual sources or enforceable duties cannot be traded away
by a spreadsheet rule.

## Evidence, authority and migration

The [public comparators](sources.md) support distinguishing modelled
reach from actual use, matching need and fiscal capacity and considering
who bears investment effects. Their methods do not determine the
candidate's actual capital and running costs, lawful grants or outcomes.
The authored reproduction separates protected service, national and
local cash, commissioning and route risk.

The competent Assembly or local elected body chooses each source,
appropriation and project. Service and transport owners accept the work
within applicable duties. Audit and courts review original evidence and
lawful remedies without selecting the general budget. Preserve legally
restricted balances, contracted work, worker rights, pending cases and
pay for existing services through phased change. No recommendation
creates a new tax, public debt, land acquisition or payable grant.
Independent qualified review, affected participation, legal enactment,
funded delivery and observed welfare remain open.

## Next bounded decision

Under an actual approved planning ceiling, compare one existing access
gap, one high-volume improvement and an independently staffed fallback
across two service areas. Observe eligible demand, paid journeys and
first useful receipt, including people who cannot use a car or a
digital route. Cost both funding shares and crew dates under a real
common disruption. Finish with lawful accepting owners and documented
actual choices or a precise list of uncovered obligations. Reopen on
changed demand, lower local receipts, shared failure, residents'
evidence or a cheaper equally usable route. A missing audit leaves the
assurance unresolved and the existing claims intact.
