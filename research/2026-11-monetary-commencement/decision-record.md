# Design482 decision record

**Baseline and procedure.** Compare Design481 under the [working
method](../FRAMEWORK.md), with its [baseline hashes](baseline.json).
The question is how the first elected budget can pay actual claims
during a monetary change. Separate legal authority, release, access
and outcome. Actual affected groups and offices have not participated.

**Selection and rivals.** For the retrofit reference, retain the
lawful predecessor where available. Require separately authorized
monetary policy and appropriation for a new regime, with a timed
public review of parallel routes. An external regime may reduce
some FX exposure while shifting settlement power and requiring
usable foreign balances and local bank-loss arrangements. A staged
own-currency package may improve domestic policy discretion, but
new nominal balances do not pay foreign claims or assure usable prices.
Faster domestic adoption remains a rival if evidence supports it.

**Compatibility and remedies.** Apply the schedule to the adoption
register, reference opening motions and C06/C14 launch rules. Preserve
active claims and due dates, separately verify foreign source ownership
and recipient access, and preserve local courts' ordinary remedies.
Distinguish a failed public prefunding route, a final deposit credit
that the worker cannot access and an uncertain instruction. An existing
urgent advance or meal is not a second wage payment. The supplier,
worker, importer and creditor retain their applicable terms.

**Evidence and reopening.** The [comparison](../settlement/monetary-commencement/01-one-budget-three-currency-arrangements.md)
and its reproduction test conditional choices, not an actual macroeconomic
transition. [Sources](sources.md) distinguish observations and constructed
inputs. Reopen upon actual settlement access, competing price and burden
evidence, a changed FX cutoff, or insufficient protection. At the next
bounded scale compare one month of bank and exchange stress across each
complete package, with one shared adverse timeline, usable paid remedies
and no double allocation of liquid assets. No actual adoption, readiness
or advantage for affected people is established.
