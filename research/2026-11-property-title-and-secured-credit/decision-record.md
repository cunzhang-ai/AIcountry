## Coherent alternatives and research selection

Retain/repair source-specific law minimizes new state records and transition
burden but leaves this candidate without positive ordinary conflict outcomes.
Strong buyer finality/title insurance/private realization reduces some market
uncertainty at potential original-holder displacement and insurer/capture cost.
Public/collective tenure and relationship/public credit reduce some private
market dependence but need actual acquisition/capital, source and democratic
control; not free land or a permission to erase old debts. Purchase-money
priority with advance senior notice remains a genuine finance rival.

Select PT01–PT24: retain true holder; identify real goods/claims/authority;
land conveyance completes on lawful registration with valid protected lodgment
position; shared/tenant/collective rights remain. Functional financed-acquisition
security requires actual attachment/ceiling and public notice or possession;
earliest continuous effective time ranks within scope, not label or lender size.
No purchase-money leap, automatic consumer perfection or old-rank ceiling jump.
Qualifying ordinary-goods buyers are free of seller-created security, not stolen
ownership. Actual proceeds/debt credit once. Real default/cure, court-controlled
nonconsensual possession/sale and received residual replace implied private force.
Future-claim assignments reserve only actual arising rights, subject to prior
binding security; later grants cannot pledge already assigned portions. A
prefiled notice alone cannot cure missing disposition power. Private assignment
history still requires proof; uniform receivable filing is a retained rival.

Public record function/head has explicit C04 career appointment/tenure/source,
separate judicial dispute judgment, protected targeted/offline access and public
challenge. Neither an official receipt nor an AI/vendor settles private truth.
Land filing priority is not backdated ownership/tax days. Movable notice is
effective when actually publicly queryable, not a hidden offline receipt;
outage cannot be misrepresented as an empty index. Existing registered rights
do not lapse just because a search fails. This creates timing/operational risk,
not a fictitious public guarantee that every security is already perfected.

PT21 expressly adds Treasury net direct reasonable reliance responsibility for
wrong official notice/time certification or attributable publication-order
failure. It does not expand B38 by analogy or insure private statements, disclosed
outage, market loss or all fraud. Preserve actual priority of intervening lawful
holders; route remaining proved office loss to public claims, not secret priming.
Public G must pay real free-at-use access/custody/courts/compensation. No fee,
expected recovery, actual capital ratio or old bid supplies qualified source.
