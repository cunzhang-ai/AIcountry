# WF05–WF08. Shorter working time needs paid service capacity

*Part of [WF](../58-country-source-and-capacity-frontier.md); preserve TW's
existing36/32-hour stages, dates, rest, leave, learning, pay and remedies.*

**WF05 — Compare matched time.** Official Danish guidance describes37 normal
hours in most areas by agreement, not a universal statute, public roster or
observed average. Compare37 with TW36 and32 as explicit scenarios. The
weekly-only proxy gives37/32−1=15.625% extra capacity requirement without
task gains. A second authored five-day calendar holds44 scheduled weeks,
110 paid break hours,64 paid learning hours and10% unexpected loss constant
on both sides: `a(h)=(44h−110−64)×0.9`. Task availability is1,308.6 at37,
1,269 at36 and1,110.6 at32. These are TW-like matched assumptions, not actual
Danish leave, absence, breaks or public availability; they do not price the
separate change in leave/learning from any real baseline. Learning can be
removed from matched demand or capacity once, not both.

**WF06 — Preserve existing pay and qualify actual gain.** For affected share
`p` of reference compensation `C=396.633bnDKK`, unchanged annual compensation
per equivalent and same task volume/quality, conditional extra compensation
expense is `C×p×(max(1,a(old)/(a(new)×(1+g)))−1)`. `g` is net validated
same-quality task gain after review/correction/fallback, not a vendor claim,
AI benchmark or fewer staff on paper. Base stress uses zero gain. Unknown
actual `p/g` is not estimated from account counts, GDP or aggregate hours.
No factor below1 funds a dismissal or cuts retained workers' annual pay.

Neither threshold is an achievable-productivity finding or service standard.

**WF07 — Money and annual hours do not supply a roster.** NAN1's4,384.6million
actual hours and3,194.2thousand employed persons describe the whole reference
economy. Their mean is not a full-time contract, public-sector capacity or
new recruit source. A national hire estimate needs actual affected contracts,
specialty, location, training, retention, wages/contributions and accepted
receiving. Distinct courts, review, night/weekend cover, travel, handover,
worker leave and DP simultaneous fallback retain paid real capacity. A
fractional annual equivalent cannot fill a missing licensed shift. Hiring
from private or foreign services has costs/counterparts, not free labor.

**WF08 — Treat AI as a task option.** Compare useful bounded DP support with
repair/simpler work, retained staff, smaller tools, alternative providers and
negotiated sharing. Count actual support, energy, capital, independence and
correlated failure once across sectors. A positive `g` sensitivity is no
forecast of an AI source; actual evidence can reject it. Preserve protected
human judgment and earlier service/claim dates. Neither a spending frontier
nor a benchmark authorizes unsafe automation or unpaid household substitution.
