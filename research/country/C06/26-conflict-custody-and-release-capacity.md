# C06. Conflict custody, independent review and safe-release resources

15 October 2026 · Design 77 · Unqualified proposed increment, not actual funds

[CD01–CD16](../C08/21-conflict-detention-and-release.md) narrows custody
powers and requires actual humane treatment, independent review and release.
[C10/08](../C10/08-conflict-custody-and-civilian-control.md) assigns command/
legislative accountability. Lawful reception is a delivery chain with costs,
not an administrative disappearance or a reserve of captive labor.

| Due obligation | Actual resource/owner and remaining gap |
|---|---|
| First custody/status record and notice | Forces/civilian justice need original safe evidence, independent register custody, chosen contact and accepted tracing; no unverified shared database or algorithmic match suffices |
|24-hour judicial access/ongoing review | Judiciary funds qualified independent duty judges, counsel, interpreters, accessible participation, private channels and actual alternate coverage. Annual hours do not establish a night/overseas hearing |
| Humane detention and child care | Civilian/military custodians fund safe actual accommodation, sanitation, food/water, clinical/mental-health teams, disability/gender/age access and supported child receiving, not forced work |
| Neutral/independent protection | Treasury and competent services fund national independent visits/source access and actual logistics; an ICRC invitation or foreign agreement is not an accepted staffed resource |
| Separate criminal case | Courts/prosecution/defense preserve source/medical evidence, expert time, actual treaty notices, clock credit and timely fair trial without pretrial extensions for backlog |
| Release, transport and documents | Named justice/clinical/child/residence receivers fund actual open safe places, due support, legal identity, clinical continuity and lawful movement; no transfer until actual acceptance/safety |
| Unlawful custody, injury, missing/dead | Existing B compensation/execution, K/G investigation and independent tracing/death/body-care duties need actual payers/assets, staff, original records and received remedies |

Trace date, place, skills, protected communications, private suppliers,
independent controllers, medical/bed/court/transport throughput and physical
consumables alongside existing 37 functions. One judge, medic, reservist,
translator, vehicle, generator or safe place cannot be counted twice during
simultaneous war, elections, disaster, ordinary care and civilian remedies.
Pay preparation, replacement, overlap, maintenance and exit as well as capture.
A narrower detention choice can need more quick review/safe-receiving capacity;
that is a real tradeoff, not a demonstrated net saving.

No actual number/type/location of detainees, conflict/geography, treaty access,
clinical/legal roster, safe foreign/domestic receiver or full cost exists.
Old country bids, banking liquidity and SF standby do not qualify this new
increment. Future enemy reimbursement, confiscation, donor aid and fines are
not today's cash; care cannot wait for peace or a foreign state to pay.
Keep lawful claims, appropriated sums, due cash, staffed service and actual
receipt separate. Missing capacity cannot create preventive powers, cancel
release or silently divert a civilian entitlement. Competent elected action,
direct court remedy and accepted receiving retain their actual roles.
