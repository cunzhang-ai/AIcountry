2 November 2026 · Scoped country-design selection, not a funded service launch

[AS01–AS24](../../country/C07/18-person-directed-assistance.md) now specifies a
usable non-employer route, optional supported direct employment, actual employer/
custody/source, work/safety, fair exit, scoped public ordinary-dues backing and
continuing paid receiving under S09–S12. This adds material law/source exposure,
not just a new service label or generic care right.

**Next bounded national question:** complete ordinary employment law beyond TW
hours/pay/leave and MP competition/bargaining protections. The current country
has substantial labor principles, but ordinary worker/employer classification,
contract formation/variation and fixed terms, fair dismissal/redundancy,
collective representation/action and actual independent remedies remain
underdefined. The fresh country search found continuity/notice and protected
strike references, not that complete positive settlement. Recheck relevant
sources and actual country files before choosing details; no exhaustive audit
of every possible future form is claimed.

Finish a coherent proposed law/control/source/transition package, comparing
worker protection, business freedom, democratic bargaining and distinct social
models with explicit losers and failures. Do not simply copy the assistance
notice rule to every job or call normative numbers scientific optima. Full local
provider/working-cash, current national source/insurance/tax/actuarial/legacy/FX,
affected judgment, legal authorization, safe funded operation and observed lives
remain independently unfinished. Urgent actual harm, stronger original evidence
or a larger whole-country contradiction can reprioritize this next research.
