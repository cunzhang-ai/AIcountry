# Income must meet actual demand without inventing a buyer

*Part of [C06 operating reference](../21-demand-consistent-operating-reference.md); design 64.*

The design-59 comparisons declared GDP and residual household consumption
as planned uses. Those identities were valid under their assumptions; they
never proved a household could or would pay. A government surplus with a
household financing gap may transfer the burden instead of deliver security.
Do not reuse a residual as realized demand or force household borrowing to
make a chosen GDP true. Keep those dated results unchanged with their scope.

The new comparator derives disposable cash from each person's declared
wages, needs, support, pension, dividend and taxes, then derives output
required for their chosen reference consumption plus public consumption,
replacement and net exports. Compare that requirement to evidence of actual
supply, prices and safe goods. Household saving/borrowing preferences remain
free; consuming all available cash is a simplifying hypothesis, not a law
or behavioral forecast. Demand and supply must be tested separately.

The earned-credit rival retains 25%, 35% or 45% of added gross work earnings,
plus a bounded earnings bonus up to 100 monthly to preserve low-earnings
comparison. A needs allowance is available against positive work tax;
capital is separately taxed at 40% and actual nonwork reduces a negative
cash topup. That differs from I05's net-income taper and design-59's integrated
nonwork withdrawal. No silent refactoring, combined-account mandate or
automatic cross-claim netting is authorized. Publish positive tax, due
payment and actual resources separately; clinical/family facts need not
become a tax office's unrestricted file.

The full-payout assumption distributes after-tax market profit after paying
replacement; it is no general command to firms. Retention, cooperative
ownership, borrower finance and different distributions remain real models.
The current numerical capital allocation is concentrated among 5,000 high
earners and is not prescribed national ownership. A different opening title
or payout requires cost, rights and fresh incidence review.

In the 45% fixture, low/zero-earner ordinary cash is preserved against the
same-input I05 comparator; middle singles/parents gain 81.25 monthly,
high-earning singles lose 158.33, high-earning parents gain 362.50 and the
high-earning owner group loses 683.33. Equal aggregate consumption hides
those differences. Work/time, investment, privacy, voice and future pension
wealth must also be compared. Do not declare all people better off.

Higher required nominal GDP is not itself a productive gain or better life.
This reference retains a simplified legacy nonmarket cost convention;
it is not certification of 2025 SNA compilation or an equivalence between
cost and received quality. AI forecasts cannot supply the private productivity,
qualified care, ecological limits or legitimate public choice required.
