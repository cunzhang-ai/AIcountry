GDP here is required final-use value under fixed prices and the declared
cost convention. At the old supplied output 3,000, the 45% case needs about
739.79 more. Private value added required is about 2,902.59; per remaining
market worker it is 33.06% above the pre-move reference. Public cost/GDP
addition is not proof of that private productivity or of received care.
The model cannot fund an unfinished project from its later required output.

In the 45% rival, positive work tax uses an ordinary needs allowance and
bounded work bonus; capital is separately taxed and reduces an actually
negative topup. It is substantively different from I05 and the earlier
integrated capital-credit withdrawal. The legacy-only contribution funds
known 45 with 40 receipts/5 interest; unpriced full rights are not erased.
Full profit payout is a voluntary reference, not a private-firm mandate.

Against I05 at the same new production/capital schedule, monthly ordinary
cash changes: low/zero earners and older recipients unchanged; middle
singles/parents +81.25; high singles −158.33; high parents +362.50;
high owner group −683.33. Aggregate household consumption is equal here
while future accrual/wealth differs and is unknown. This is no Pareto gain.
Every sampled person meets the *declared* ordinary need under the proposed
45% formula; that does not establish actual adequate prices, live access,
work quality or future social security. Real affected participation is absent.
