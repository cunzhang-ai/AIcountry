# EL09–EL12: an actual local electorate, council and replaceable executive

*Part of [C03/13](../13-equal-franchise-and-usable-elections.md); design111 candidate.*

**EL09 — Ordinary local reference.** Select one municipality-wide proportional
council ballot under EL06–08, with n in place of N and its actual local electorate.
Party/civic/one-person lists have identical entry/consent/support/count and paid
PC minimum rights. No mayoral personal-seat exception or mandatory ward track.
Existing protected autonomy/minority arrangements need their own affected-public/
constitutional source; this default cannot abolish them by a map update.

The candidate initial charter's size schedule is9 seats for E_ref≤10,000,15 for
10,000<E_ref≤100,000,25 above100,000; if positive E_ref is smaller, n=E_ref.
E_ref is the verified positive local reference electorate fixed in the founding/
lawful charter process before call, with source, public representation/workload/
cost reasons and court correction. This normative schedule does not prove adequate
oversight in a large city or legitimate staffing in a tiny place. Once called,
no roll correction/turnout changes n or the current majority/quorum. Missing
source or too few willing nominees is nonreadiness, not permission to fabricate
members or disfranchise residents. Later size revisions need prospective law,
public reasons/rights review and RR protected succession, not an office's convenience.

**EL10 — Boundaries are authority, not a database operation.** Ordinary local
boundaries/competence follow C04's affected proposal/evidence/negotiation and
Assembly statutory decision with local participation, shared tax/service/land/
rights/creditor incidence and direct ordinary/CJ review in their actual scope.
Protected status/sovereignty follows C14 and distinct rights-holder assent, not
an ordinary national majority or this election chapter. Publish a proposed
ordinary map at least12months before the first affected call; new arrangements
cannot serve incumbent self-dealing under RR. Continue existing lawful areas
when a proposal is unadopted. No ministry, EA officer, vendor or AI can redraw a
municipality to change likely votes or move people to establish a majority.

Before call, every genuine domestic home has one actual applicable council
assignment or a disclosed lawful joint/self-government arrangement with its
representation/source rights. A gap has direct remedy; it is not consent to no
local vote. New housing, shelter and displacement cannot be left off a database
map. One municipal vote is not a claim to a national territorial seat. Loss of
ward guarantees under this reference is a real representation tradeoff, not
proof that national PR or an efficient service geography protects local identity.

**EL11 — Four-year local mandates and a lawful chair.** Prior law fixes local
ordinary P at four-year intervals and V=P−14, with PC10's call/entry/support
calendar. Joint election dates may save proved cost but cannot be changed after
call or chosen by an incumbent for advantage. New council first sitting is P+14;
without proven lawful mandates no provisional total creates members. Actual
EA unresolved-result/ordinary-court duties continue, not a timer-elected council.
The law preassigns a qualified career clerk to convene and record; the clerk
cannot legislate, choose a chair or lower full n/quorum.

Council elects its chair/executive lead by more than half full n within21days
from first sitting/vacancy. Then use C03/06's bounded minority-toleration method:
open member nominations, named candidate ballot, highest support at least ceil(n/4),
then separate rejection test; more than half full n rejects. Public runoff for
a support tie, no chair's casting vote or repeated rejected nominee. Four lawful
rejections or no lawful candidate by day30 automatically triggers a whole-council
repeat closing within90days under EL20. Same qualified prior-law deputy/clerk
conducts a withheld required vote, with direct useful-time court/execution.
An actual recorded investiture confers office without a minister's appointment.

Council may replace the chair at once by electing another with more than half
full n, or dismiss by that majority and reopen formation. Ordinary legislative
quorum is more than half full n; ordinary decisions need more than half those
present, no casting vote, and tax/appropriation/material new commitments require
more than half full n and their existing substantive law. Absence/abstention
is not assent. Chair cannot dissolve the council at will or create local tax/
coercion powers. Its accountable career administration implements actual law.

**EL12 — Expiry/annulment does not revive an old legislature.** Outgoing/local
invalidated chair is a limited caretaker for due law, judgments, existing essential
contracts and narrowly prior-authorized safety duties only, with no legislative
vote from a vacated mandate, new policy, senior patronage or new emergency power.
If no lawful chair/deputy exists, career officers perform their own continuing
acts, not an invented political investiture. Full annulment vacates affected
new mandates prospectively; no predecessor council returns. An actual partial
annulment preserves only lawfully severable members under fixed n and courts.

Repeat/early/supplemental local members serve only the original remaining term;
no failure restarts four years or extends a caretaker's substantive powers.
If fixed term expires before a new lawful result, expose absence of democratic
legislative authority; source law's bounded essential-career duties continue.
Courts can enforce exact source/record/payment/handovers, not become the council
or invent cash/force. Existing local obligations and individual claims survive.


[LR09](../14-local-law-and-public-control.md) expressly adds full statutory-n
approval for every externally binding local general rule, including ordinance
and delegated criterion, with its own input/making/commencement. EL11’s ordinary
internal procedure remains otherwise. This is a special prospective source-law
choice, not a changed count, actual rule or vote supplied by the clerk.

[LRC01–LRC05](08-local-council-recall.md) gives the local electorate
a bounded route separate from EL11 council confidence. A successful
recall ends the old mandates, with a conditional proportional choice
for the original remainder and lawful continuity if a count fails.
