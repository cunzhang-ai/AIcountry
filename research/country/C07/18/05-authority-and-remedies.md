# AS17–AS20: choice needs independent public correction

*Part of [C07/18](../18-person-directed-assistance.md); design 116 candidate.*

**AS17 — Existing authorities, distinct roles.** National Assembly law sets
coverage, employer-dues backing and sources; national Treasury funds the enacted
protection/equalization obligation, including high-need/sparse-area and wage/exit
exposure. Elected local councils choose actual plural delivery/contracts under
that floor. C04 merit-appointed career care receivers assess, arrange choices,
administer funded accounts and correct mistakes, with actual independent review;
no new sovereign AI, discretionary lifestyle board or self-financing levy.

Publish which public legal entity employs staff, who controls its appointments,
budget/contracts, protected information and replacement. Existing C04 public
procurement/control/conflict rules, independent inspection and ordinary courts
apply equally to public, private and cooperative employers. Recipients and
workers choose their own representatives, peer/union assistance and alternative
communication; neither provider management nor a minister appoints their voice.
Paid accessible challenge must include unrepresented people, without treating
an invited group, funding recipient or copied accounts as everyone's mandate.
For this Act's independent choice/rights help, the person or worker chooses
qualified conflict-free assistance through the funded aid/advice route. The
challenged assessor/employer neither selects nor removes that adviser; eligibility,
conflict or payment disputes have direct independent review, not a care veto.

One public authority being both assessor and provider is a capture risk, not
proof of neutral advice. Offer independent advice and a real alternative route;
separate challenged decision from final review and disclose connected control.
Plural providers can also share a controller, labor pool, payment system or
landlord. Test practical exit, opposition and usable replacement, not a logo count.

**AS18 — Direct relief for either person.** A recipient can challenge need,
route restriction, costs, coercion, missed shifts or unsafe transfer through
S/I/E/B independent routes. A worker can claim pay, time, equality, safety and
fair termination through TW/B, with confidential inspection and independent aid.
Neither must complete internal grievances, accept joint mediation, obtain a
provider signature or prove a whole technical dossier first. The other party
retains notice, reply, fair evidence and separate representation where interests
conflict. Do not make the recipient the necessary defendant for every provider
or Treasury wrong, or erase their own independently proved responsibility.

Use existing earlier useful-time relief and B17 duty-judge consideration within
24 hours at the latest, not a guaranteed favorable order or a safe wait. Ordinary
casework cannot change constitutional judicial appointments, source law or binding
coverage. Money dispute, unwanted contact, unlawful refusal and absent workforce
need different actual responses; an administrative correction is not delivery.

**AS19 — Trace the order to actual receiving.** An established pay/care order
names public or employer debtor, lawful Treasury/payment channel, amount/task,
due time, receiver and proof of actual safe credit/support. For private debt use
B18/U within actual means; for state refusal use only expressly adopted B19
bounded appropriation and independent determined-act execution, not a research
analogy to another guarantee. No bailiff can hire an unwilling worker, create a
tax or take protected funds. Competent elected bodies supply new burdens/sources.

An employer/custodian cannot block undisputed AS12 pay by withholding a timesheet;
use assisted original evidence/provisional treatment and fair later correction.
A paid worker is not proof that the recipient got care; a received care visit
is not proof that the worker was paid. Record both and remaining unmet duties.
Recovery or damages claims remain visible if every executor/funder fails; no
judgment, provider closure or account migration turns them into zero needs.

**AS20 — Privacy and replaceable mediation.** Collect minimal purpose-specific
support directions, hours/pay and safety records under PD/EV sources, with safe
custody, accessible correction and lawful successors. No continuous intimate
video, shared family dossier, public disability testimony or device subscription
is compulsory proof. Staff get necessary instructions, not every private record.
A person can safely report a household/controller without notifying that actor.

Assessors/providers cannot infer consent, political loyalty, workforce discipline
or resource authority from message convergence, synthetic engagement or delegated
AI management. Preserve unfamiliar expressions and interpretation loss; future
interfaces need actual scope, time/resource limits, revocation and usable offline/
other-provider alternatives. Communication, understanding, consent, clinical
permission, employment authority and payment remain distinct. Make any change
in mediator/framing power contestable without interrupting ongoing protection.
