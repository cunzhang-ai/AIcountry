# Collaboration foundations: proposal 3.0

The earlier iterations made updates safer and more consistent. This iteration
changes the organizing model: from improving a document-centered national design
to studying how people and agents develop understanding, form commitments and
evolve their institutions through different forms of cooperation.

- [Framework 3.0](framework-v3.md): deeper assumptions and the replacement architecture.
- [Interaction contract](interaction-contract.md): meaning, delegation, mediation and settlement across methods.
- [Future scenarios](future-scenarios.md): personal agents, immersive collaboration and disconnected collectives.
- [Iteration record](iteration-record.md): source scope, alternatives, challenge and next evaluation.
- [Validation](validation.json): artifact checks and explicit limits of the authored walkthroughs.

This is the latest **local architectural proposal**, not a deployed communication
platform or adopted procedure. The interface-independent specification makes future
methods investigable; it cannot guarantee compatibility with unknown technologies.
The earlier frameworks remain preserved sources of applicable constraints. The
current site's text APIs, permissions and canonical editions remain unchanged.
