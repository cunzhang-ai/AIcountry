# RT26–RT31. Make it possible to leave a job without losing a home immediately

*Part of [private rents and housing entry](../18-private-rents-tenure-and-housing-entry.md). Design344 research selection.*

**RT26 — Classify the home by actual use and necessity.** Apply ordinary
residential tenure when an employer, recruiter, platform or linked owner
provides a worker's ordinary home. Treat a genuine service occupancy as a
narrow exception where the employer proves that this particular dwelling
is necessary for specified on-site duties and no reasonably usable
less restrictive staffing arrangement exists. Record the grounds before
occupation, with accessible independent challenge. Seasonal employment,
a lower rent, a tied contract, common ownership or a short nominal term
cannot establish necessity. Voluntary overnight facilities that do not
replace an accessible ordinary home retain their own safety and privacy
duties. Existing stronger tenure and remedies continue.

**RT27 — Keep pay, housing and migration status independent.** Give the
worker understandable separate statements of wages, housing terms,
lawful charges, who may occupy, exit rights and independent assistance.
Pay the cash wage and established arrears through the worker's chosen
route. Any housing charge needs its own lawful basis and accounting.
A housing dispute cannot justify withholding a wage, passport, personal
documents, essential utility or immigration application. A worker can
refuse optional housing, change jobs, organize or report harm. Apply MB
portable permission and any independent status bridge on their own
terms. Employers and their contractors hold no authority to decide status.
Confidential contact and housing assistance remain reachable without
the employer's consent or an employer-controlled account.

A covered operator or affiliate may not intentionally make payment,
housing or an independently due essential service for the worker or
their household conditional on surrendering lawful exit, refusal or
a claim under these rules. The competent civil court tests the actual
link, original records and independently justified legal grounds under
B13–B14. Shared ownership or timing alone establishes no violation.
Keep wage, possession, service and status decisions on their own
grounds. Use narrowly scoped B17 relief where its requirements hold.
A court order still needs an accepting safe route and lawful funding.

**RT28 — Require a court and a real alternative for service occupancy.**
Leaving the job alone creates no possession ground for an ordinary home.
For a proved service occupancy, the ordinary court can order possession
when the work connection has genuinely ended, an actual operational need
persists, and the order is proportionate. Give usable notice, reasons,
defense and paid independent advice where needed. Ordinary possession
under this ground may occur no earlier than 30 calendar days after both
the work ended and valid notice became usable. Before execution, verify
a safe accessible alternative available to the actual occupants, or
their voluntary move to verified safe housing. Account for household,
necessary journeys, tenure and affordability. Give the employer and
occupants timely review. A genuine imminent danger can require earlier
lawful safety action with actual receiving. It does not confer permanent
possession. No self-help eviction or automated decision is allowed.

**RT29 — Fund transition before the housing and job fail together.**
The employer or named service-housing operator pays the duties imposed
by its law and agreement from job exit until the earliest execution date
under RT28, including safe continued occupancy or necessary substitute
accommodation and transfer where the current dwelling cannot serve.
Before offering material service accommodation, demonstrate a funded
transition plan or enforceable security for simultaneous exits. If a
source fails, the local housing duty owner assesses usable receiving
under H and HL, advances lawfully funded protection when available and
pursues recovery separately. After the initial employer-paid period,
any supported hold or alternative must name a lawful public or other
payer for its attributable costs. The court reviews an outstanding hold
at least every 14 calendar days, with a dated payment and receiving plan.
Missing cash or stock remains an exposed duty and goes to the competent
elected body. It supplies no ground for automatic possession, forced
unpaid private provision or continued employment.

Before a worker decides to leave, the local housing duty owner must
offer a confidential route under SCN and AP to assess an independent
ordinary tenancy or plan a suitable transition. Use existing paid and
lawful routes first. Where an actual service or place cannot be
confirmed without a finite reservation, an expressly authorized
local schedule may pay for a short, independently controlled option.
Name its source, price, purpose, eligibility, unused cost and expiry.
Check a compatible home, paid care and transportation as needed with
the actual receivers. Apply H07 and keep existing priority and
accepted claims. Count option and activation separately. Prefer
independent ordinary tenure where available under RT26.

**RT30 — Make the handoff work in the resident's ordinary life.** The
housing office names an accepting provider, due date, transport and safe
handover before recording receipt. Arrange protected access to belongings,
medicines and needed devices and preserve existing school, clinical and
benefit duties. Housing, employment, immigration and property reviewers
retain their distinct judgments. Asking for a plan supplies no notice of
resignation, benefit trigger, abuse finding or consent to move.
The worker controls scheduling and safe contact. Necessary later
notice follows its own law. Share only evidence the receiving party
needs and preserve private option and address information. Test
actual cash and the next safe visit before claiming a usable exit.
A failed placement or a lost wage stays
open in its own account. AI may compare availability and flag shared
capacity, with lawful limited inputs. Qualified officers verify safe
fit and receipt and hear challenges. Publish safe aggregate outcomes
without exposing a person's location or migration position.

**RT31 — Migrate prospectively and revisit real access.** Keep existing
tenancies, remedies, wage and creditor claims and stronger rights.
Review shared ownership, genuine on-site need, funded regional stock
and independently staffed review before commencement. Assess delayed
moves, coercion, employment entry, costs borne by employers and users,
and whether affordable ordinary housing can meet the same need.
Reopen on evasion, repeat unfunded holdings, loss of useful jobs or a
simpler arrangement with equal protection.
