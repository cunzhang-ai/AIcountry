# Receiving, independence and continuity across changes

*B39–B40; part of [C08/16](../16-family-agency-property-and-estates.md).*

**B39 — Separate decisions, accepted delivery.** Civil-status officials
record; child/family and ordinary property/probate courts adjudicate contested
rights within assigned competence; public clinical/care/income/housing
services receive actual needs. Existing national/local court allocation and
appeal apply. No registrar, social worker, clinician, family council or AI
controls all status, support, evidence and enforcement. Fund independent
child/adult advice, interpretation, disability support and safe source access.
Investigate protector abuse through the outside-command route.

Urgent orders name a payer/executor, due useful time, receiving place/person,
interim support and direct independent challenge. Uncontested essential
support continues through a dispute. No forced mediation, reconciliation,
therapy, confession or household labor finances a settlement. A protection
finding is not criminal guilt, and a criminal acquittal is not a finding of
safe contact or valid title. Sensitive evidence stays purpose-limited; explain
meaning and decisions safely without making family life permanently public.

The cabinet answers to the replaceable Assembly for general appropriations
and actual capacity; independent courts control individual legality and
remedy. Appointment/removal, source custody, advice funding and execution
must be assessed for cumulative common control. A nominal second office or
several agreeing AI agents is no independence. General law can be revised
democratically; no named case is decided by a political instruction.

Count judges, child advocates, independent counsel/experts, registrars,
means/estate assessors, safe carers/transport, clinics, custodians, records,
claim payments and substitute housing once in C06 by actual period/skill.
Neither a family label, plural compact nor a larger court docket supplies
those people. C07 shared-outcome delivery may count actual resources once
without merging personal claims. Existing 37-function bids are not qualified
for these incremental duties; no inheritance/fine/AI saving is booked.

**B40 — Migration and future challenge.** Before lawful commencement, map
existing marriage/partnership and customary claims, child status/orders,
guardianships/mandates, benefits/support arrears, property/security, wills,
estate disputes, limitation bars and foreign judgments to lawful successors.
No automatic conversion to a plural compact, retroactive new debt, annulled
family, orphaned status or revived expired bar. Retain due rights/support and
stronger protections while independent process resolves incompatible clauses.
Public certification liability applies prospectively; old custodial wrongs
retain their actually applicable remedies rather than a retroactive guarantee.

New media can express/support a choice, but communication, interpretation,
assent, delegation and execution remain distinct. Preserve original meaning,
scope, unknown semantics and contradictory evidence safely; a migrated account
or latest synchronized summary cannot silently waive a child's claim or make
a will. Agent replicas share budgets/authority. Offline notice and continuing
commitments need B03's declared limits; there is no instant universal revocation.

Reopen on coerced compact formation, lost child voice, disguised guardianship,
unsafe placement, inaccessible claim, unfair property loss, captured executor,
unfunded liability or a less harmful model. The default remains contestable;
state recognition does not prove informal life improved. Next complete
sector/monetary and jurisdiction/international boundaries, then audit the
whole ordinary-law package and shared country capacity. No adopted law,
operational readiness, independent review or observed superiority is claimed.
