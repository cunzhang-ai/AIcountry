# C05. Time to live, fair pay and the people who keep services running

9 November 2026 · Design222 · TW01–TW28 candidate, not enacted law

**Select a staged normal paid week of 36 hours, then 32 hours, six weeks of paid annual leave, two
normal working weeks of paid public/flexible holidays, protected rest and
learning, and an individual cash minimum wage linked to median hourly pay.**
The purpose is usable time and independence alongside reliable care and public
services. Shorter hours must not become compulsory unpaid family work, concealed
availability or an automatic cut in existing regular weekly pay.

The old design selected bounded time/rest but left numerical rules provisional.
This candidate now makes that country choice explicit. It does not infer law
from QE's 1,080 task-hours, enact the September labor draft or qualify a funded
national launch. Thirty-two hours and the pay/leave amounts are normative
hypotheses; no study identifies them as universally optimal.

Select a staged 36-hour then 32-hour normal week, with no automatic reduction
of existing regular weekly/annual pay. Bargaining may improve terms and make
lawful genuine changes; it cannot waive the common floor. Public procurement
and services budget actual replacements, paid training and absence. Private
employers bear their lawful labor obligations; there is no public guarantee
of unchanged profit or every existing job.

Select employer-paid initial sickness protection and a bounded general-revenue
wage-replacement entitlement thereafter, with I05/long-term protection continuing.
It is a new explicitly sourced requirement, not money taken from Y contributions
or an unexplained zero-cost leave rule. Its actual claims and source are unpriced.

The [reference](../../settlement/work-time/README.md) holds the children's
40-hour service offer fixed. With explicitly paid breaks, the 32-hour five-day
pattern needs 1,937 frontline posts versus 1,507 under the 40-hour rival:
430 additional posts and 12.900 million CU annual pay at the same 30,000 salary.
The old 1080-hour recipe is neither rival's proven workweek. Local care routing
can reduce travel in an authored block, but retention/redeployment creates
capacity rather than automatically releasing cash. No GDP or AI gain is assumed.

I05/IC, Y/RF, service rights, actual accrued pay and constitutional office limits
continue under their own source rules. The minimum wage is no substitute for
the independent ordinary foundation or extra needs. Existing more protective
terms remain. This is research selection, not legal adoption, qualified worker
participation, safe rosters, actual financing or proof of better lives.
