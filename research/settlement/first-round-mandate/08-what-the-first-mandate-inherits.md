# What the first mandate inherits

*Design538 · Historical failure mechanisms and a constructed opening decision*

Use the same inherited people, legal claims and available first-week
resources for the chosen retrofit and its strongest repaired rival.
Two historical cases identify different mistakes to test.

| Original evidence | Failure to test in either candidate |
|---|---|
| In Australia, the Robodebt Royal Commission examined how first-tier review decisions with broader implications were handled. It recommended ways to identify significant decisions and publish those with significant legal or policy implications. [Royal Commission report, chapter 20](https://robodebt.royalcommission.gov.au/publications/report) | An individual case can close while an identifiable common defect continues to affect others. Test whether qualified independent review can reach the next actual adverse act before its cutoff. |
| In England, the National Audit Office concluded in 2023 that plans would fall short of 40 completed hospitals by 2030 under the original definition. The department’s January 2025 plan used phased construction-start windows. [2023 audit](https://www.nao.org.uk/reports/progress-with-the-new-hospital-programme/) · [2025 implementation plan](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/new-hospital-programme-review-outcome/new-hospital-programme-plan-for-implementation) | A changed cohort, spending period or milestone can make two claims incomparable. Keep original and revised commitments visible. Verify funded interim care and clinical opening separately from construction start. |

These observations concern distinct programmes and dated evidence. Review
the evidence available at the relevant decision date. The cases establish
neither a result for the proposed country nor a general estimate of a
mechanism's frequency.

## Run the first-week decision

In this constructed case, ordinary revenue, independently confirmed debts,
approved support and signed clinic works remain legally operative. A separate
inherited power permits material deductions with a contested proof shortcut.
A closed individual case identifies that shortcut. The next collection file
locks at noon on Day 3. It includes one deduction based only on the shortcut
and a separate debt supported by independently verified evidence. The clinic's
replacement is a published ambition outside the signed works. Its planned
start moved later and its current safe service date is unchanged. A qualified
substitute clinic slot at that date requires a reserved roster.

| Opening choice | What can continue or change before the original deadline |
|---|---|
| General legal continuity alone | Preserve valid dues, but the registry supplies no direct warning. The ordinary forum can intervene if independently alerted in time. If it learns only after the file locks, count the missed stop even if a later refund arrives. A construction start proves no substitute clinical slot. |
| Strong ordinary repair under inherited competent law | Fund the registry, independent reviewer and available lawful stay; confirm a scoped instruction reaches the collection operator before noon. Keep the verified debt and approved support running. Preserve signed works and reserve interim care through the original clinical deadline. If the existing law permits all of this reliably, give repair full credit and prefer it for this scope. |
| Targeted opening law where ordinary repair cannot bind the relevant actors | The competent legislature expressly removes the shortcut for prospective adverse acts and preserves proof-based collection and review of earlier acts. The registry sends the protected warning directly. The competent payer and operator acknowledge the lawful stop in time. Use the same interim care and keep the original and revised project scopes visible. Choose this route if a duty, power or independent path needed for timely protection genuinely cannot be secured under ordinary repair. |

If the instruction is signed but the collection operator receives it after
noon, record the adverse action, review and actual refund separately. If both
legal arrangements lack the reserved clinical roster, record the missed
service in both. Service repair cannot be financed by counting a disputed
collection or a promised future building as an available resource.

For the company holding the signed clinic contract, require the existing or
expressly enacted continuity, payment and handover terms that protect the
patients and workers actually affected. Newly specified board proportions
for unrelated enterprises solve neither a missed legal instruction nor an
unfilled clinical roster. Defer them to an ordinary later legislative
decision while preserving existing worker rights and civic review.

The first-round national result is a limited opening instrument, targeted
repair where ordinary law qualifies and express lawful removal or narrowing
of any dangerous carryover power that repair cannot control. A real rehearsal
needs named competent actors, available evidence at the decision date,
executed acts and a funded second call. This constructed exercise supplies
a decision test and establishes no independent participation, actual
authorization, staffing or lived outcome.
