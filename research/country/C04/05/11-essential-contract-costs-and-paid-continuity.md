# SC44–SC49. Allocate cost risk before essential contracts bind

*Design381 · Proposed ordinary procurement law within [public buying and continuous supply](../05-public-buying-and-continuous-supply.md).*

**SC44 — Choose who can bear each risk before seeking bids.** For a
purchase whose failure would materially interrupt an essential duty,
the competent buyer compares a firm price, bounded adjustment of
specific external costs and qualified direct or alternative supply.
Use the same service, term, wage obligations, useful deadlines and
failure assumptions. Compare the price of transferring risk, the
supplier's ability to control or absorb it, working cash, independent
continuity and the burden on residents and workers. Keep controllable
performance risk with the actor able to manage it. Prefer an
independently verifiable component formula where a material,
uncontrollable cost and credible source qualify. Select a firm
price where that option demonstrably protects supply at lower
whole cost. Explain the choice in the tender and elected budget.

**SC45 — Fix the adjustment and its limits before award.** Publish
each eligible component, base amount, quantity basis, independent
series and vintage, currency, lag, lawful fallback, adjustment
date and contractual bounds. Specify symmetric rises and falls
where comparable, with reasons for any asymmetry. Separate
quantity, rate and quality changes. Identify how known support,
rebates and covered hedge benefits or costs affect the formula.
A single cost can be covered only once, including through FX or
sector-rate rules. A published formula does not guarantee margins
or reimburse a supplier's actual costs. Require specific approval
for exposure beyond the disclosed terms. A failed series triggers
the published fallback or lawful reconsideration, with no
unilateral retrospective rewrite.

**SC46 — Budget for correlated claims before committing the margin.**
The buyer and Treasury put contract adjustments, approved wage and
care rates, household hardship and independently funded alternatives
on one dated gross cash and capacity account. Identify common inputs
and controllers across agencies and localities. Include the working
cash due before delivery, competing eligible draws and any common
credit or pledged assets on which multiple supply offers rely.
Independently verify usable concurrent amounts at the relevant
supplier pay dates. Publish safe exposure and scope while protecting
commercial and personal evidence. Compare actual early milestones
on prior lawful terms and distinct financed supply. Preserve
competition, settled credit against the later invoice and rights
of workers and service users. Price simultaneous
lawful adjustments, available cash on each date, source restrictions,
physical supply, next delivery and the work that an alternate
would displace. Test adverse index, cash and delivery paths before
awarding or renewing. A margin can support an optional start only
where the applicable elected release and actual funding survive
that test. Escalate a credible projected limit breach before
commitment or the next service deadline. A fiscal limit does not
erase an accrued contractual, worker or resident claim.

**SC47 — Follow the payment, payroll and service separately.** Verify
the effective contract terms, original index, covered delivery and
nonduplicate claims. Pay undisputed amounts under SC14 and any
earlier source-law deadlines. Use an advance only on prior lawful
terms with a dated source, risk protections and independent review.
Give bidders and oversight independent access to material evidence
with protected commercial and personal information. Independently
verify wages through labor law and service receipt through sector
law. Neither an indexed invoice nor cash to a contractor proves
that workers were paid or residents received their service.
Keep earned pay, ordinary charges and service rights on their
own authority. An adjustment creates no permission to charge users.

**SC48 — Act when the formula cannot preserve delivery.** On credible
risk to the next due service, compare the already authorized
adjustment and lawful payment correction, an independent substitute
and any bounded incumbent bridge under SC12 and SC33–SC37.
Check actual ownership, workers, stock, permissions, receiving,
gross cost and exit in time. A change of quantity, quality, term,
control or pricing risk remains subject to SC13 and competition
under its actual substance. Escalate any exhausted source or
material prospective change to the competent elected body.
Preserve accrued amounts and actual worker and resident remedies.
Where no route qualifies, disclose the exact unpaid or unserved
duty and keep seeking existing lawful protection. No provider
has an automatic right to rescue or material renegotiation.

**SC49 — Review actual incidence and keep adoption prospective.**
Use competent elected adoption for new and renewed instruments.
Retain existing terms and disputes unless lawfully varied. Name
the buyer, evidence custodian, payroll and service verifiers,
independent review, payer and alternate before relying on the
scheme. Review bid participation, paid input risk, public cash,
worker receipts, service continuity and actual costs borne by
people. AI may recompute disclosed terms and flag correlated
exposure from lawful records. It cannot determine entitlement,
raise a tariff, release funds or verify its own delivery.
Reopen on routine renegotiation, distorted incentives, wrong
indices, inadequate working cash, shared failure or a better
qualified direct or competitive route.
