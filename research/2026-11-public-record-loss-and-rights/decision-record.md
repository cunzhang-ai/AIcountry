# Design322 decision on public record loss and essential support

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design321, AP, LRT, TU, LX and DP29–DP32, and the current
framework. Existing law candidates assign public evidence duties,
preserve dates and require reconstruction. This revision allocates
procedural proof risk where the required record has been lost and
gives a scheduled essential-support programme a finite, independently
reviewable interim payment. The project assistant authors the proposal.
Legitimate lawmakers control adoption and appropriation.

## Alternatives and evidence

Keep ordinary case-by-case reconstruction alone, accept disputed
substantive benefits at the claimed rate, or use a rebuttable procedural
rule and verified-needs interim support. Ordinary reconstruction avoids
a new expenditure but can miss an essential deadline and favor people
who kept copies. Full claimed payments could protect some people earlier
while financing unverified amounts and leaving poor incentives for
timely resolution. Select RLW01–RLW05. A credible account can establish
a procedural fact subject to reliable contrary evidence. Merits continue
under their own law. The public budget bears verified interim need,
including when a properly granted interim payment has no later award
to credit. Independent review protects against false receipt findings,
agency neglect and unnecessary retention.

The [primary sources](sources.md) show different elements of evidence,
explanation, continuation and restoration. None establishes this
candidate's proof rule, interim class or optimal duration. The
constructed comparison stipulates staff and funding rather than
inferring national capacity.

## Authority, succession and reopening

The adopting Act specifies covered programmes, evidence scope, prior
records, amount limits, interim authority, appeal and separate budgets.
A successor accepts duties and dates before optional migration. When
a transition is already lawful and effective, the successor remains
answerable for actual protection. A provider has no claim over case
authority. A review officer determines the original act, a separate
authorized payer executes relief, and existing independent courts
review and enforce within their lawful scope. Preserve competing
records, prior decisions, third-party rights and original clocks.
Report uncovered cases without equating a missing record with fraud
or requiring retention of all transcripts.

Test one real recurring payment programme with consenting affected
people, independent reviewers, offline proof, a deliberately unavailable
mock record source and a paid disbursement exercise. Compare time to
receipt, unaided access, privacy, wrongful losses, erroneous findings
and displaced ordinary reviews. Price full incident exposure and renewal
before the next useful date. Reopen on repeated shortfalls, divergent
credibility findings, source manipulation or an equally effective simpler
route. Actual participation, adoption, paid operation and outcomes remain open.
