# SC05–SC08: capable entrants and an award people can inspect

**SC05 — Aggregate the real need.** Proposed material threshold is **5M**,
using the scale expressly authorized and published for procurement.
During retrofit retain actual lawful procurement thresholds until an Act
changes them. An adopting procurement Act may use the TA01 measurement
method independently, with its own published opening value and update
rule, without adopting PA10 taxation. If no lawful new threshold applies,
use the actual predecessor method. Where no predecessor or authorized
scale exists, a buyer cannot rely on a new simplified tier. Use only
an independently lawful procurement route and refer the missing scale
to the competent legislature.
Count expected gross payments including nonrecoverable tax, options, foreseeable
renewals and related lots for the same project or continuing need over a rolling
12 months. Do not aggregate unrelated buyers merely for administrative control;
do aggregate a coordinated split. The threshold is a contestable national-law
hypothesis, not an empirical optimum or a cap on actual service rights.

For material buying, default open competition with at least **30 calendar days**
after usable full notice before closing. For 0.5M to below 5M, publish accessible
specifications and allow at least **five working days** for quotations. Below
0.5M, a recorded market-price comparison or existing lawful catalog may suffice;
do not fabricate three bids in a thin market. Compare usable alternatives where
available. All tiers retain conflicts, records, quality, source and challenge.
Existing competed framework calls follow SC08; emergency buying follows SC12.
Longer actual preparation/translation/safety needs require longer notice, not
fictional compliance with the minimum. Material changes reopen adequate time.

**SC06 — Separate public specifications from vendor design.** Publish outcomes,
quality/accessibility, whole-life cost/risk, criteria/weights, lots, contract terms,
source/acceptance conditions and correction routes before bids. Permit equivalent
solutions and proportionate safety/security proof, including unfamiliar methods.
No mandatory political loyalty, AI vendor, portal account, prior government
contract, avoidable proprietary format or irrelevant turnover hurdle. Accessible
offline/assisted submission has the same deadline and protected receipt. Ordinary
bid access is free; exceptional performance security needs disclosed proportionate
risk/reasons and practicable noncash alternatives. Do not price safe participation
or meaningful competition out of reach. Legitimate clinical/security competence
remains necessary and cannot be certified by an AI score.

**SC07 — Award on prior terms with answerable reasons.** Keep sealed bid content
protected until authorized opening; publish later outcomes/ownership/conflicts
without exposing personal testimony or enabling cartel coordination. Evaluate
responsive offers using prior criteria. Seek explanation of a suspiciously low
offer's safe/pay/input feasibility before rejection; cheapness is not misconduct.
One bid neither proves collusion nor compels award: compare actual alternatives,
price/source and recompetition delay. Material changes in criteria require a new
call. Give each bidder its evaluation, selected price/relative merits and scoped
reasons; protect justified secrets through independent confidential review.

**SC08 — Keep frameworks bounded and contestable.** A framework has a maximum
**24-month** term, named aggregate ceiling, scope, unit/price-update rules,
admission/reopening method and call-off allocation. Publish whether calls use
ranked terms or renewed competition; no secret favored-provider discretion.
It promises no minimum demand unless expressly priced, sourced and authorized.
Multiple providers count as alternatives only when actually independent and
capable, not related shells. Planned renewal competes before expiry; no evergreen
extension, new essential function or unpriced guaranteed volume by call-off.
Retain direct public/cooperative/repair alternatives where competition is thin;
their paid capacity is not created by the failed tender.

[SC38–SC43](10-independent-bids-and-protected-supply.md) provides the
targeted independence and response checks. Related offers keep their
lawful award route and common delivery capacity is counted once.
