# C08. Fair licensing and portable competence

*Design354 · PQ01–PQ06 · Proposed common licensing rules.*

Reserve specific acts when their risks justify prior permission. A person
who proves the required competence can carry it between places, work under
a safely separated scope, or qualify through a limited assessment. Give
people and clients a funded way to challenge exclusion and unsafe admission.

| Part | Candidate rules |
|---|---|
| [What may be reserved](32/01-reserved-acts-and-independent-control.md) | PQ01–PQ03: graded restrictions, independent review, and protection from capture |
