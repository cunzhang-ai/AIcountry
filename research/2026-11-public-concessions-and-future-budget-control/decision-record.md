# Design226 decision record. Public concessions and future budget control

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned design 225](baseline.json)

## Gap and decision

DB07 and DB16 already count full commitments, guarantees and tails.
SC requires open competition, accepted delivery and continuity.
They leave a consequential choice open. A concession can keep a
small immediate capital line while fixing future service payments,
raising bills or moving risks to future elected bodies. Separate
the financing decision from the service decision and put material
long-term commitments and exceptions to a usable recorded vote.
Select [PL01–PL12](../country/C06/64-public-concessions-and-future-budget-control.md)
as the prospective specialization of DB, RF, SC and IV. Use the
[working method](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).
No current approval or amendment follows from this record.

Democratic reversibility, affordable access and real service
continuity are design requirements. Whether long bundles improve
maintenance or transfer collectible risk is an empirical question.
The 20-day public scrutiny window is a proposed rule for material
awards. Its burden on competition and decision time needs review.
The candidate applies functionally across private, public and
mixed legal vehicles at the actual competent scope.

## Alternatives and objections

| Alternative | Strong case | Material objection |
|---|---|---|
| Retain ordinary repair and procurement | Fast protection at low transition cost where existing performance can be repaired. Existing contractors and public skills remain. | Can prolong service fragility and defer necessary renewal. Test actual safe delivery and maintenance before retaining it. |
| Public financing and separate competitive works or services | Keeps finance, policy and operations separable. Can retain replacement options and more visible public authority. | Actual borrowing or capital is scarce. Public managers keep integration and cost-overrun risks. Works and service scopes can fragment. |
| Long privately financed service bundle | Can align design, maintenance and operation, offer earlier delivery and put collectible equity at risk. | Often narrows future operating choices. Guarantees, concentrated lenders, locked evidence and late handback may leave the public with the risks. |
| Bar new long-term concessions outright | Removes one route to fiscal concealment and weak public bargaining. | Forfeits credible risk sharing and may move similar commitments into leases and public companies unless coverage follows the real obligation. |

Select a rebuttable preference for separable finance and works
where they can provide the same service. A long bundle may proceed
with a reasoned elected advantage, named risk, legal caps and a
funded fallback. Reject unresolved material exposure at the award
stage. Genuine urgent prior powers retain their narrower function.
The [water budget case](../settlement/public-concessions/02-water-renewal-and-shared-exposure.md)
selects lawful retained repair on its assumptions. It shows why a
low initial charge and an annual balance can both be true while
termination is unpayable. Documentary evidence qualifies the
mechanism without establishing a universal ownership winner.

## Control, custody and transition

The sector authority owns the need, performance and fallback.
Treasury holds the commitment and payment records. The Assembly
or elected council votes within its actual authority. Independently
appointed auditors and courts need protected access and separate
sources. Opposition members can obtain the original terms through
PE. Independent tariff and safety authorities retain their own
mandates. Test shared lender, supplier, purchaser and source
control through a hostile handover. Neither an auditor nor an AI
model is an award authority.

Before prospective commencement, fund appraisal, scrutiny, record
custody, outside inspection and the accepting operator. Map old
contract and security terms, title, current bills, pay, claims and
relevant treaty commitments. A late or refused new decision leaves
old obligations live and an explicitly lawful continuity route.
Use separate authority for acquisition, liability change or
termination of a contract. Court correction and
current creditor rights continue. Preserve records without
publicizing protected personal evidence.

## Bounded next comparison

For one jurisdiction, compare one real service concession and one
separately financed project on the same access and asset condition.
Inspect their original terms, contingency and guarantee records,
one annual cash window and documented successor readiness. Seek
affected users and workers, independent review and current source
evidence before a substantive recommendation. Reopen for material
renegotiation, uncovered handback, common shocks, a working
separable alternative or loss of effective control. Actual
participation, enactment, funding, field delivery and better lives
remain unestablished.
