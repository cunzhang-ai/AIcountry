# Two public payment chains and what can be matched

## Manchester. Central grant and local payments

For April 2024 through March 2025 the Department for Work and
Pensions circular lists a **GBP 1,396,135** Discretionary Housing
Payment allocation to Manchester and a GBP 3,490,338 total
expenditure limit. The council identifies the same annual grant
and reports that it financed payments above the grant from its
General Fund. This is a matched allocation and local funding
account, without the dates of bank settlement of the grant.
[Department circular, Annex A](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/housing-benefit-subsidy-circulars-2024/s22024-discretionary-housing-payment-government-contribution-for-english-and-welsh-local-authorities-for-the-financial-year-ending-march-2025)

| Quarter ending | Reported cumulative net spend, GBP | Change from previous quarter, GBP |
|---|---:|---:|
| June 2024 | 756,351 | 756,351 |
| September 2024 | 1,363,978 | 607,627 |
| December 2024 | 1,909,160 | 545,182 |
| March 2025 | 2,637,985 | 728,825 |

The reported net spend exceeds the annual grant by **GBP 1,241,850**.
By December it exceeded the grant's full-year amount by GBP 513,025.
This comparison does not establish the dates or usable cash of
individual fund transfers. Net spend includes prior-year payments
and recovered overpayments within its reported scope. The council
separately labels its March ward table as awards rather than
expenditure, since some 2024/25 awards would be paid from April
2025 and some of the year's spending relates to the previous year.
That is a timing distinction, not evidence that a resident was
paid late. Actual household bank receipt or housing outcomes
are unobserved. [Manchester payment data](https://www.manchester.gov.uk/benefits-and-support/housing-benefit/how-the-benefits-service-is-performing/discretionary-housing-payments-welfare-reform-and-caseload-information)
· [March award table](https://www.manchester.gov.uk/download/downloads/id/29561/dhp_entitlement_by_tenure_type_and_ward_march2025.pdf)

The Department's separate year-end financial returns table gives
Manchester expenditure of **GBP 2,635,700** and the same original
allocation. That is GBP 2,285 below the council's public cumulative
series. Keep the difference open. The material inspected does not
identify a complete reconciliation between the two reports.
[Financial Information table](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6874bd7681dd8f70f5de3f12/data-tables-use-of-discretionary-housing-payments-2024-to-2025.ods)

## Copenhagen. Resident payment and disputed regional reimbursement

The historical 2024 Danish order describes qualifying group 2
medical reimbursement paid by the municipality on behalf of the
region under section 9, with the region bearing treatment costs
under section 11. The public service page describes a receipt-based
route when the provider did not deduct the subsidy. Copenhagen's
2025 balance follow-up for one former administering office lists
**DKK 5,142,875** in items requiring action at 31 December. Of
this, **DKK 4,666,195** was more than one year old. The city
attributes these open amounts to unsettled regional invoices,
with disagreement and processing constraints. It does not
establish the final legal value of each invoice. [Historical
Danish order 605](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2024/605/pdf)
· [Copenhagen balance report, pages 9–10](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/agenda/a80a4de7-fdbb-4fa5-99e0-167c1b4eda43/691635c2-185f-4910-b666-b2725bed71d3-bilag-2_0.pdf)

Copenhagen reports that another office took over processing and
payment on 1 January 2025. Its August 2026 committee report says
regional review had not yet reached invoices for payments by the
new office. This is reported resident-side payment activity and
unsettled regional reimbursement, with no matched bank-by-bank
record or finding on each resident. The older balance belongs to
the stated former-office scope and cannot price the total city
exposure. [Audit follow-up](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Besk%C3%A6ftigelses-%2C%20Integrations-%20og%20Erhvervsudvalget/m%C3%B8de-17082026/referat/punkt-4)

The two examples concern different currencies, duties and fiscal
periods. Each supplies a separate check on the proposed model.
Neither estimates the effect of an advance rule, qualifies a
payment calendar for AIcountry or establishes effects on lives.
