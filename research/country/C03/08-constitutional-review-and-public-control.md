# C03. Constitutional review and public control

*Integrated design 80 · CJ01–CJ18 · English working reference, not enacted law.*

Select a separate nine-seat constitutional court, with ordinary courts retaining
ordinary cases and urgent individual protection. This completes a reference
institution; it neither appoints anyone nor establishes independence, capacity,
legitimate founding or superiority. The supreme-court-bench alternative remains
substantively available. No AI holds a judicial vote or sovereign mandate.

The numbers are normative reference choices, not empirical optima. A source can
fail to appoint; a court can lack five lawful judges or five agreeing votes.
The design specifies that failure without creating an executive replacement,
lower threshold, renewed term, fictional judgment or extended custody power.
Continued legal effects can favor an incumbent despite this restraint; that
objection is retained, rather than calling nondecision neutral.
