# W15–W29: methods, property and listed prohibited means

*Closed act schedule under [G04](../03-war-crimes.md); definitions, not instructions for violence.*

IAC-only rows require an international conflict/occupation and applicable
protected status. Other rows use G04's express domestic IAC/NIAC scope.

| ID | Prohibited act and boundary |
|---|---|
| W15 | Intentionally kill/wound an adversary who has genuinely surrendered or is hors de combat and no longer lawfully targetable; prove actual circumstances/knowledge and any properly raised justification |
| W16 | As a commander or effective issuer, intentionally order/threaten that survivors will not be spared; a direct no-quarter command is the act, not a prediction that fighting will be lethal |
| W17 | Launch an attack knowing expected incidental civilian death/injury or civilian-object damage will be clearly excessive relative to the concrete/direct overall military advantage anticipated; assess the information actually available and expected harm, not hindsight or a casualty count alone |
| W18 | Launch an attack knowing expected environmental damage will be widespread, long-term and severe and clearly excessive to that military advantage; prove all those threshold facts. Lesser/uncertain ecological harm retains other actual law, not guilt by analogy |
| W19 | IAC/occupation: intentionally transfer the occupying power's own civilian population into occupied territory, directly/indirectly, or unlawfully deport/transfer the occupied population; prove occupation, act and applicable IHL, not a border slogan |
| W20 | IAC: compel a prisoner of war/other protected person to serve a hostile power's forces contrary to their protection; custody or a job label cannot erase coercion |
| W21 | IAC: compel adversary nationals to take part in war operations against their own country, by force/threat and without applicable lawful grounds; distinguish actual operations from safe voluntary civilian assistance |
| W22 | IAC: intentionally declare abolished/suspended/inadmissible adversary nationals' legal rights/actions solely as a prohibited war measure; ordinary lawful forum rules are not a license to erase standing |
| W23 | Intentionally pillage or appropriate property for private/unauthorized use without the lawful holder's genuinely voluntary consent; distinguish specific lawful requisition with applicable protections from seizure by a label |
| W24 | Intentionally destroy/seize adversary/protected property without imperative military necessity under IHL; extensive unlawful/wanton destruction is covered, with actual property, necessity and fault proved |
| W25 | Intentionally misuse a truce flag, protected medical emblem, UN or adversary flag/insignia/uniform in a prohibited manner, causing death/serious injury with the required intention/knowledge as to consequence; a mere symbol error is insufficient |
| W26 | Intentionally kill/wound an adversary through perfidy: inviting reliance on legally protected status with intent to betray it for that harm; lawful concealment/deception not claiming such protection is not this offense |
| W27 | Knowingly employ poison/poisoned weapons or biological/chemical weapons as prohibited means of warfare; prove the actual prohibited category/use and awareness, not a dual-use label or possession inference |
| W28 | Knowingly employ bullets prohibited for their designed easy expansion/flattening in the human body; prove the actual prohibited category and conflict use, not a general ban on ordinary civilian ownership/work |
| W29 | Knowingly employ a weapon whose primary effect is injury through fragments not detectable by X-ray, or a laser weapon specifically designed to cause permanent blindness to unenhanced vision; prove category/use/knowledge. No unnamed future system becomes criminal by resemblance alone |

Weapon categories are legal boundaries, not technical construction or use
guidance. Additional prohibited means require a prospectively enacted exact
schedule and actual source-law review; a technical office/model cannot append
them as an automatic update. Actual harm, other K/G offenses and civil relief
may apply independently on their own facts. New legal classification does
not approve procurement, reliability, targeting, detention or deployment.
