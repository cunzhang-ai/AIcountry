# EG01–EG04. An independent grid and a usable connection

*Part of [ordinary electricity law](../19-electricity-service-and-grid-access.md).*

**EG01 — Separate public rule, dispatch, wires and sale.** The Assembly
enacts reliability, network-charge, source, consumer and emergency allocation
rules. The state employs a qualified career Power Steward through C04 to
prepare the supply and funding plan. A distinct public system operator,
with a qualified career head and protected real-time duty officers, balances
the system, operates its control path and applies nondiscriminatory access.
It cannot own or control a market participant or set its own tariffs.
Network owners and authorized distribution operators maintain wires, connect
and deliver to users. Distribution maintains its own safe switching authority
within coordinated limits. Existing lawful public, community and private
assets and plural sellers retain their own title and obligations. New publicly
financed core network assets retain PS public/common control. No operator
gains planning, acquisition, retail or police authority from a dispatch role.

The independent technical reviewer has separate C04 appointments, protected
funding and lawful access to controller and network evidence. An outside
own-case route and ordinary court review remain. The incumbent grid or retail
owner cannot select the final reviewer of its case. Councils retain ordinary
land-use and service powers. Publish appointments, removal, source and
execution authority, including who can replace the Steward or operator head.
Existing lawful hiring and judicial roles apply.

**EG02 — A first request opens a real connection path.** The distribution
operator accepts the protected first request for a safe connection or
restoration, including from an occupant behind a shared meter. It supplies
reasons, an attributable works quote and timeline, or the exact limiting
constraint and a lawful route to review. Publish safely aggregated hosting
capacity, committed versus available MW and connection stages. Protect
security-sensitive details and independently disclose them when necessary
for review. A deposit, landlord's arrears or digital account cannot block
essential household receiving. Where a safe connection is not yet possible,
the public service receiver arranges a safe, funded interim route to the
protected tasks. An offer of electricity on another network does not
discharge an unavailable home connection.

**EG03 — Allocate scarce new firm capacity by known stages.** Honor actual
valid connection rights and protected reliance. For uncommitted capacity,
apply elected essential-access and infrastructure classes first. Match
each candidate to a usable node, start, direction, time profile and funded
works. Publish the evidence required for readiness, with accessible
alternatives and proportionate security. Among materially equivalent ready
claims use the protected application order, with a public draw for a genuine
tie. Hold a contingent reservation only for its named milestones and
dates. Give notice and a useful chance to show compliance or unavoidable
delay before releasing capacity. Actual late grid works are not treated
as the applicant's failure. An interim independent order may preserve a
disputed slot. A rejected or expired request receives reasons and a remedy.
A politically connected project, shell application or speculative reservation
cannot consume a priority reserved for a different purpose.

**EG04 — Permit explicitly limited connections by choice.** A customer may
accept a firm tranche plus a separately metered flexible tranche. The
contract identifies maximum import/export, activation conditions, usable
notice, duration and frequency bounds, meter proof, settlement and review.
Model concurrent activation, real ability to interrupt, recovery load and
the effect on neighbours. The flexible tranche receives the agreed
connection or tariff value, with correction if its terms are exceeded.
It is not firm capacity, a free reserve or a promise of later firm access
without identified works. A high-compute, industrial or storage applicant
can take the same route under published terms. Protect clinical, heating,
water, care and electoral tasks as applicable. Never deem essential household
load voluntarily interruptible because a customer is unable to pay or cannot
use another supplier.
