# C08. Animal protection, enforcement and lawful remedies

*Part of [C08 everyday law and justice](../C08-everyday-law-and-justice.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

The Assembly must prospectively define the animal-protection floor, its
species and life-stage scope, prohibited acts, justified uses and exceptional
powers. The reference floor bars intentional or reckless severe unnecessary
suffering and abandonment and requires proportionate care by those who keep,
transport, use or kill covered animals. Detailed species and setting rules
need evidence on behavior and harm; farming, fishery, research, companion,
working and wildlife contexts cannot all be scored by one measure. Freedom
from pain alone is not a complete account of living well. An exception for
food, research, safety or conservation needs a stated lawful purpose and a
least harmful feasible method, not a private assertion of economic necessity.
The law must distinguish grave culpable abuse from correctable conditions
and avoid criminalizing a person for a service or income failure the state
could remedy. Human defendants retain legality, counsel, evidence and appeal.

## Responsibility and control of the protector

The veterinary service advises and treats; a separate public inspector
verifies compliance. The Assembly appoints its chief from a published merit
shortlist after an open hearing; field staff enter through C04's career-service
route. Law fixes conflicts, for-cause removal with court review, protected
source access and a budget covering rural travel, laboratories, interpreters,
safe animal custody and court execution. The Assembly likewise appoints
a public animal-welfare advocate after an open hearing for a bounded term;
its nomination, removal, funding and source access are public and reviewable.
An implicated minister cannot stop an inspector or advocate by cutting a
named case's funds. Ministers remain answerable for policy and appropriation;
they cannot direct the outcome of a named cruelty case. Inspectors disclose
relevant former employers and funding. Independent audit and courts can
correct selective enforcement against small farms, minority practices or
political opponents. Local officers can enforce within enacted competence,
but a national floor and funded receiver prevent one locality from exporting
unwanted animals or unsafe facilities to another.

Inspection, entry, sampling, record access, corrective orders, licensing,
temporary protective custody, closure and emergency humane destruction each
need distinct statutory grounds and proportional limits. Urgency may justify
interim action before a full hearing only when waiting risks serious harm;
recorded reasons, safe custody, prompt independent review and a feasible
return or permanent-care decision follow. An agency may not keep animals in
worse conditions while contesting a case. Confiscation is not a general
forfeiture of a person's land or livelihood. A disease-control order adds
sector evidence and compensation questions, while its welfare method remains
reviewable. Public reports can start a low-burden investigation; confidential
worker and neighbor reporting needs retaliation protection and fair checks
against false or mistaken claims.

Animals cannot file a claim themselves. A designated independent public
advocate and qualified civil organizations may seek enforcement or review of
serious public-law failures under enacted standing and accountability rules;
affected owners, workers and communities may challenge action against them.
Neither the advocate nor a private campaign group can legislate by lawsuit.
Courts can require lawful inspection, stop an unlawful act, order suitable
care, uphold or overturn sanctions and require execution; the Assembly chooses
general standards and resources. Remedies include verified correction,
training, humane transfer, license conditions or loss, proportionate civil
penalties and criminal prosecution for serious culpable harm. Fine revenue
does not determine inspection budgets. An order counts only after animals
receive safe care and affected humans receive required process.

Research-use review follows [C09's separate account](../C09/10-animal-welfare-and-one-health.md):
scientific validity and animal burden are both examined before work starts,
with outside review of conflicts and adverse findings. Consumer labels and
private certification may add information but cannot replace the public
floor. Existing lawful holdings, research protocols, employment, contracts,
food supply and animals already in care need an inventory and funded,
prospective transition; no rule may abandon living animals on commencement.
No original law, cases, authorized inspectors or safe-care capacity exists
for AIcountry. Reopen if the protection mandate becomes a tool for arbitrary
entry, selective punishment, industry capture or unenforceable symbolism.
