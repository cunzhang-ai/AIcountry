# Legacy conflicts and continuing obligations

Integrated design 68 · Unreconciled research issues, not adopted legal changes

Older manuscripts and the current reference design sometimes assign different
authorities, guarantees or transition duties. The seventy-two identified
conflicts and gaps are grouped by their primary system:

| System | Conflict register |
|---|---|
| Democratic and legal power | [Membership, executive, courts, coercion, local authority and legal rank](conflicts/01-democratic-and-legal-power.md) |
| Livelihoods and services | [Delivery, work, income, banking, pensions, housing and market exit](conflicts/02-livelihoods-and-services.md) |
| Resources and external dependence | [Tax, debt, physical systems, external obligations and common scarcity](conflicts/03-resources-and-external-dependence.md) |
| Knowledge, AI and change | [Evidence, digital control, useful AI and agentic communication](conflicts/04-knowledge-ai-and-change.md) |

Each conflict retains the prior duty, the current working answer and the
missing lawful or operational migration. An issue may affect several systems;
its placement does not limit who must be heard or which obligations survive.
A full source-clause inventory, affected-party review and lawful adoption are
still required. These registers cannot certify democratic readiness.
