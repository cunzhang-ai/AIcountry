# Design378 decision. Separate an inaccessible hearing from knowing evasion

## Baseline and selection

The pinned Design377 criminal core prohibits money bail and gives P09
and P10 actual hearing and remand deadlines. SC and OJ supply safe
contact and general access. PO costs lawful custody and alternatives.
The gap is a distinct process for a person who misses a mandatory
appearance, including proof for an absence warrant and actual removal
of a corrected warrant where it might still be executed.

Select [CA01–CA07](../country/C08/12/06-court-appearance-and-pretrial-liberty.md)
for the ordinary criminal process. The value choice is equal practical
access to court with presumption of innocence, individualized safety and
real accountability for knowing evasion. Actual release requires the
applicable liberty order and execution. Existing P limits keep their
original dates. Lawful witness and survivor protection remains distinct.

## Alternatives and objections

Retain and repair using clearer summons and reminders. Two primary
studies justify these tools in their observed settings. This approach
does not resolve transport, coercive inference or stale copies. Retain
plain notices and optional reminders within the selected path.

An intensive supervision model may enable some people to be released
under a specific finding of necessity, but using it by default burdens
people who would otherwise be released unconditionally. Compare any
proposed condition with that baseline and test safety separately from
attendance. The selected path preserves narrow independently justified
conditions under existing P rules.

Funding, repeat court dates and victim safety are material concerns.
A right to correct unusable notice can impose delay and paid work.
Allow representation for suitable matters and preserve prompt
independent action for concrete threats. A person should not be
returned to an unsafe relationship to make attendance feasible.
Do not assume detention savings cover the initial support appropriation.
No affected-party deliberation or independent legal review occurred
for this edition.

A competent lawful adopter would need court-led review of active
absence warrants, independently staffed verification at execution
desks, a voted source and a confidential correction route. No current

For an initial feasibility check, court administration and independent
