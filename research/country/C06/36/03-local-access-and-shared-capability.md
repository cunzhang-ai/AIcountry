# RF10–RF12: share scarce capability without exporting access costs

*Part of [C06/36](../36-recurring-finance-and-pension-commitments.md); design 89 candidate.*

**RF10 — Choose a differentiated service network.** Retain accessible local
first contact and ordinary provision; prefer shared specialist, diagnostic,
purchasing and suitable administrative capability where the full individual
access/task comparison supports it. Do not duplicate every infrequent specialist
in every locality or make an online vendor the sole gateway. Local/sector
authorities propose the shared service within C03/C04 competence; elected
councils can use lawful interlocal compacts. Common national commitments beyond
those powers need the Assembly. A shared service creates no new sovereign region.

The commissioning authority remains answerable for its person's actual service.
Identify the accepting provider, qualified shifts, records scope, referral/receipt
owner, due time and funded alternative before moving an appointment or closing
a local function. The original receiver owns continuity until the next provider
actually accepts; an electronic referral is insufficient. Receiving/public
fallback and independent remedies retain their separate statutory owners.

**RF11 — Compare full access, not the cheapest invoice.** Include paid travel,
transport, interpretation, disability assistance, overnight/dependant needs,
private records, professional consultation, standby, teaching/supervision and
resident waiting/time. Keep an actual local or individually workable alternative
when sharing cannot meet useful-time access, consent, safety or privacy. Do not
shift work into unpaid families, compulsory travel, unsafe waiting or weakened
appeals. Provider preference is subject to the selected guarantee and lawful
choice, not a promise that every possible operator exists locally.

Keep S13 joint learning/care episodes only when one actual staffing/place/time
combination satisfies both duties; count it once. Shared buildings, digital
records or AI outputs never merge clinical consent, educational choice, care
eligibility, regulatory inspection and rights review into one decision maker.
No universal dossier or mandatory mediation platform is required. Maintain
supported/offline communication and lawful minimum necessary evidence exchange.

AI may assist appointment/shift comparison, source reconciliation, accessible
translation and demand scenarios within existing mandates. Keep uncertain
meaning, qualified human judgment, rejection/correction routes, privacy and
retained human capability. Neither a model nor a service vendor may ration
an entitlement by secret criteria, force technology use or choose public burdens.

**RF12 — Pay for reorganization and prove any saving.** Compare retain/repair,
shared services, direct public delivery and credible private/cooperative offers
over the same horizon and received service. Price double running, staff choice,
safe record transfer, contract/title/creditor duties, transport and repair when
a hub fails. IC08–IC12 applies to public investment and its closure risk.

The reference's optimistic elimination of the entire6% commissioned-provider
operating margin is only a bound. It supplies no willing zero-margin provider,
free acquisition, reserve, qualified extra staff or actual source. Lost public
tax/dividend-related receipts and changed income claims must be recalculated;
the headline14.013 million margin is not a net Treasury saving. It cannot by
itself close the hundreds-of-millions recurring gap. No numerical shared-service
saving is credited before a complete qualified offer and accepted access plan.

Preserve present service hours and material coverage until competent prospective
law changes them. Correct or reject the shared arrangement if access, quality,
power or total cost is worse; a funded retain/no-change result is legitimate.
More centralized purchasing or faster communication alone is not a better life.
