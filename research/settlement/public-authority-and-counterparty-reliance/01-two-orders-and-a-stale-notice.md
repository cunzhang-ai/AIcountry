# Two public orders and a stale authority notice

At noon a lawful successor takes office. The ordinary authority
notice channel remains stale. An essential supplier holds a valid
order placed before noon, due for delivery that afternoon. A
different instruction seeks additional services after noon under
the former holder's expired discretionary mandate. The original
service order has its own funded source and sector receiver.

| Receiving rule | Valid order | New direction with expired authority |
|---|---|---|
| Trust the ordinary channel alone | Executes | Wrongly appears acceptable |
| Stop every order pending fresh notice | Stops and misses the service deadline | Holds |
| Verify each authority through a separate route | Execute under existing law | Hold and refer promptly |

A resident with a reply due that afternoon submits it through
the authorized alternate intake and receives a dated receipt.
Their request for verification does not itself count as filing.
The competent office assesses whether any disputed notice was
usable under the existing notice law. The case keeps its date.

A second receiver has already incurred 6 units of reasonable
mobilization expense after receiving an erroneous confirmation
from the designated public verification office. Stipulate
that an independent forum finds the adopting law covers the
error and the supplier acted reasonably without contrary
notice. It confirms 2 units of actual same-loss recovery.
The residual award is 4. If the supplier relied only on the
sender's assurance, the same narrow claim would fail on these
facts. The invalid instruction never gains authority. Any
retained goods or services go to their own lawful return or
restitution determination, with actual same-loss recovery
reconciled before further payment.

In the worked payment window ordinary service needs 8, independent
review 1 and the separate remedy 4. A lawfully payable remedy
source of 3 leaves 1 outstanding if partial execution is permitted.
At 4 with actual receipt, the residual award is paid. Keep the
original service and the next ordinary call protected either way.
This is a constructed case without actual authority, appointments,
suppliers, review, funding or receipt.
