# C09. Food systems, safe diets and public control

*Part of [C09 land and ecology](../C09-land-and-ecology.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

Food security means more than stored calories. The working country must test
whether people can obtain safe and adequate food in ordinary time, use it with
clean water and care, and continue doing so through seasonal and economic
shocks. Diet and production differ by culture, age, disability, ecology and
place. The state protects practical choice and sufficient access; it does not
assign an official diet, require an identity-linked food app or treat one
national average as proof that every household eats well.

## An ordinary chain with distinct decisions

The Assembly enacts an essential food-access floor, sector safety standards,
the food-security authority's bounded remit, trade and reserve powers,
funding, source-data limits and review. The C09 food-security authority
maintains a source-backed account of production, processing, storage,
distribution, imports, price and access gaps, including seasonal and local
variation. It can propose supply and reserve plans, publish warnings and
support local authorities, but cannot set taxes, requisition farms, ration
households or change ecological floors by calling a forecast an emergency.
Its leadership follows open qualification, conflict and elected scrutiny,
reviewable removal and independent source access. Producer or retailer
representation informs evidence but cannot veto public policy; the authority
must show its funders, contractors and common controllers.

Farmers, fishers, processors, traders and retailers may operate through
private, cooperative, public or community forms under labor, competition,
food-safety, animal-welfare, land, water and ecological law. The
[animal-welfare route](10-animal-welfare-and-one-health.md) asks about
individual animal suffering as well as human diet, producer livelihoods and
species conservation; none is inferred from a safe product. C05 protects real market entry,
worker bargaining and producer exit from a dominant buyer or input supplier.
C09 land and water planning preserves tenure, downstream claims, soil and
habitat capacity; a food target does not license uncompensated displacement
or depletion. C10 records critical import, payment and foreign-harm
dependencies rather than equating resilience with total self-sufficiency.
Public procurement may support plural suppliers and predictable demand only
within enacted purposes, fair access and disclosed fiscal and ecological cost.

The C07 social floor owns the person's route to food: adequate purchasing
power, reachable outlets and a lawful in-kind or delivered alternative when
money cannot buy food. Schools, care and clinical services retain their own
meal or nutrition duties where enacted and funded; neither a food authority
nor a retailer can count a warehouse release as a meal received. People
with dietary, disability, cultural or language needs have a usable request
and appeal without a compulsory disclosure of intimate health information.
C07 public health supplies evidence-based nutrition advice and identifies
population harm, but clinical nutrition and individual consent stay with
the patient-care route. Education may teach food knowledge without scoring
families' political or moral worth.

Food safety inspection remains a separate sector power under C08's common
consumer floor. It traces hazards from farm or import through processing,
retail and public meals, can use a specific lawful hold or recall, and gives
producers fair process and buyers timely notice and remedy. The food-security
authority cannot hide contamination to protect a reserve target; the
inspector cannot decide national farm or trade policy through a recall.
Original laboratory, environmental, price and household evidence stays
with lawful custodians; C11 preserves methods and corrections. Missing
people, informal markets and self-provision cannot be inferred away from
retailer scans or AI estimates.

## Plans, shocks and correction

Before shortage, Cabinet compares whole food-system packages for Assembly
choice: production and import diversity, land and water use, storage and
rotation, cold chain, transport, market concentration, household support,
food safety, labor and ecological costs. The account tests affordability
and nutrition alongside physical availability and price, including people
without kitchens, safe water, transport or stable income. It records the
same worker, fuel, foreign currency and reserve once across health, energy,
care and food. Reserve size, release, replacement and loss bearers need
prior law and an elected budget; a high stock figure does not prove that a
safe item can reach a person before it spoils.

An ordinary price rise first uses enacted income, competition, trade,
procurement and supply tools with reasons and appeal. A serious physical
shortage follows C06's preauthorized scarcity stages and C10's narrow
emergency powers only where their distinct statutory grounds apply. A
technical office cannot silently choose who goes hungry or waive habitat
protection; an elected material burden decision must expose unmet claims,
foreign effects and lawful interim relief. No declared shortage creates
new coercive powers, cancels tenure or erases a food-safety recall.

C14 reports separate trends in food availability, affordability, dietary
adequacy, safety, stability, farm and food-worker livelihoods, ecological
condition and actually received remedies by place and group. The account
shows uncertainty and excluded nonusers; a lower average price can coexist
with worse nutrition, exhausted water or an impossible journey to a shop.
No AIcountry territory, producer map, diet observations, stock, law, budget
or outcomes exist. Reopen this choice if an integrated authority suppresses
bad evidence, in-kind support humiliates or excludes people, ecological
burdens rise, or another institutional package protects food and freedom
better under actual democratic control.
