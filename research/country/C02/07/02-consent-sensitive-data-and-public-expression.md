# PD05–PD08: real choice, sensitive uses, children and expression

*Design 96 ordinary-data research candidate; not enacted law, funded readiness or
demonstrated superiority.*

**PD05 — Consent and dependence.** A valid choice is granular by purpose and as easy to
withdraw as to give; refusal of optional processing cannot cost an essential benefit,
ordinary employment, care, school, housing or payment access. A worker, patient,
student, tenant or dependent user may still freely choose an actually separable optional
use with a usable equal-quality alternative. On receipt of withdrawal stop new optional
consent-based collection/disclosure; suspend that optional pipeline while completing
implementation across known processors within five working days, sooner where useful
protection requires. The five days never authorize an opportunistic final dump; it does
not erase a separate legal record, prior lawful act or other valid base. The controller
must identify an independently existing base, not fabricate one after withdrawal.
Silence, bundled terms, supporter status or copied agents do not multiply or transfer
consent. Bodily/research participation consent and agent mandate retain their distinct
laws.

**PD06 — Sensitive and inferred data.** Health/disability, genetic/neural data, unique
biometrics, intimate/sexual life, race/descent, belief, political opinion, union
activity, criminal allegation/history and a person's precise movement trace receive
heightened protection, including an inference that reveals them even if its inputs look
ordinary. Process only with PD04 plus a specific additional ground: genuine express
optional choice; confidential necessary care or supported service under sector duty; a
precisely enacted equality, public-health, electoral or safety function with safeguards;
an answerable legal claim/court order; or protected public-interest journalism/research
under PD08. Keep access separate and log consequential use. No behavioral advertising
based on sensitive information and no routine biometric or neural identification of a
general population. An ordinary service request may use necessary transient location
locally without building a lasting route history. Coercive surveillance, interception
and state identification still need their separate P/C10 power; this data law creates
none. This list is functional, covering a future sensor or proxy revealing the same
intimate characteristics. Anonymization needs a reasoned likely-means/reidentification
assessment, not simply removing names or claiming a trained model is anonymous.

**PD07 — Children and consequential AI.** A person under 18 retains their own rights,
supported voice and safe confidential route; a parent or institution does not own the
child's data or automatically inspect clinical, refuge, intimate or complaint records.
From 16, a young person can directly exercise ordinary optional-service data consent and
subject requests; below 16, assess their actual supported understanding and obtain a
safe lawful supporter or independent child route where needed. This age does not change
B21 civil majority, clinical choice, work/pay control or child protection. Necessary
care/school/identity processing uses its actual duty and safeguards, not a household
checkbox. No targeted behavioral advertising to people a service knows or reasonably
knows are under 18, or household/school sale of their profiles; this does not require a
universal age-ID registry. For a materially consequential housing, work, credit,
insurance, care, payment or civic decision, disclose material data sources and logic in
understandable form and give a qualified human with actual power to change the result
before a final adverse effect; a reversible urgent hold needs its separate sector law,
useful-time review and continuing essentials. No general social-worth score or final
decision by unattended AI. A human review must have actual time, source access and power
to change the result, not a ceremonial click. This does not ban routine lawful
positive/reversible automation or require a fresh human signature for every uncontested
payment; relevant law, independent challenge and protected essentials remain.

**PD08 — Speech, knowledge and shared evidence.** Privacy is not an executive truth
licence. Public-interest journalism, criticism, research and archives may process
necessary information with source protection, minimization, safe access and a specific
enacted rights balance; a journalist needs no state accreditation to invoke the
protection. Political finance, operative law, public spending, court reasons and
official source records retain C03/C11 public-inspection duties, with
redaction/protected access for vulnerable people. A person's data request cannot erase a
lawful public decision, falsify an original observation, suppress another person's
testimony or hide material official misconduct. Conversely, being a public figure or
appearing in an archive does not make an address, clinical file, child's identity or
unrelated intimate fact freely republishable. A court, not the data supervisor or
platform, decides a contested narrow disclosure/erasure conflict under prior law and
timely challenge; no general prepublication permission is required.

For protected journalism, necessary omission/delay of individual notice or
source-identifying access is permitted where notice would defeat the actual
public-interest inquiry or source safety. Record the necessity safely, minimize
unrelated data and retain direct independent court challenge; no routine prior
judicial approval or serial secrecy renewal is needed merely to investigate/report.
This narrow notice/access rule does not waive a lawful base, proportionate privacy,
research-participant consent, search rules or accountability for unlawful conduct.
For lawful public-interest archives/research, a proportionate public notice may
replace individual notice only where individual notice is genuinely impossible or
disproportionate and safeguards preserve meaningful subject rights; this is no
permission for covert clinical experimentation or an unrelated private dossier.
Ordinary opposition canvassing, voluntary association and public-interest research
may use the actual PD04/PD06 grounds, including genuinely chosen oral/assisted
permission; none needs state journalist/party accreditation or a general profiling
licence. Separate political-finance/publication duties still apply.

[DP03/DP13](../../C11/09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md) keeps this
positive/reversible automation distinction in the public operating charter.
Useful clerical assistance/payment needs actual prior lawful rule/mandate,
source records, ceiling/correction and independent direct challenge; no blanket
per-payment human click. Protected final adverse effects still need actual
qualified human power before effect, and separate bodily/political/force acts
retain their own nondelegable sources. No common host/incident creates a new
lawful training, intimate-data collection or national identity-graph basis.
