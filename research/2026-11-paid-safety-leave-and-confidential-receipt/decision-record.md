# Design386 decision. Protect necessary safety time and confidential pay

## Baseline and selection

Design385 has confidential clinical, housing and advocacy receiving,
general paid leave and worker rights, but no distinct time-and-pay
route for a person addressing violence or coercion during scheduled
work. An employee may have a safe place or appointment and still
lose income, employment or confidentiality by using it.

Select [WL54–WL60](../country/C05/12/10-paid-safety-leave-and-confidential-receipt.md)
with two normal workweek equivalents per rolling 52 weeks. The
eligible work loss and separate privacy and safety rules matter
more than the label. Pay usual regular pay via the employer in
ordinary cases, with direct outside assessment and a separately
funded advance if that route is unsafe or unavailable. The cap is
a design choice, with separate hardship and ordinary rights retained.
Genuine independent workers remain within ordinary protection unless
additional replacement is enacted.

## Alternatives and objections

Retain existing annual, medical and unpaid leave. This is simple
but can consume rest, require irrelevant incapacity proof or make
safety unaffordable. A single public payment route could improve
privacy and spread cost but can create a new gate and delay payroll.
An employer-only route is simpler where safe but fails if the
controller is implicated. Select an ordinary wage duty and a
limited independently reachable advance, with no duplicate pay.

Privacy procedures can still reveal a person's absence, and some
workers may lack predictable rosters or trusted contacts. Separate
limited evidence, pay and cover. Permit safe evidence from irregular
work and enforce anti-retaliation. Neither a staffing shortage nor
a claim of fraud gives automatic denial. Reconcile only on lawfully
verified facts with independent challenge. Price the first and later
payments, worker cover and actual services together.

## Evidence, migration and next action

[Primary records](sources.md) distinguish Australia’s legal model
and actual implementation concerns from the domestic proposal. The
[constructed comparison](../settlement/paid-safety-leave-and-confidential-receipt/01-one-shift-two-pay-routes.md)
tests a compromised route, the actual deadline and a separate service.
No affected-worker participation, independent local review or proof
of actual local efficacy qualifies this proposal.

Adopt with the labor authority, independent payer, courts and the
existing service owners. Define the leave period, ordinary wage
contribution and tax treatment, separate advance accounting and
payroll protection before commencement. Preserve stronger terms,
pension and credit rules, existing due wages and all safety remedies
on their own authority. This edition changes no actual law or payment.

Next inspect a small employer, an irregular worker and a compromised
pay route with lawful protected evidence and independent affected input.
Follow one shift, first pay and the next call, including staffing and
shelter or other actual receiving. Reopen on disclosure, retaliation,
late payment, lost access for precarious workers or a simpler equally
effective source. Actual authorization, readiness and outcomes remain open.
