# National strategy: all obligations remain owned and due

Design 133 · [NS01–NS20](../../country/C06/54-national-production-and-source-strategy.md)

All 37 original complete function/bid/actual-null objects, 61 selections and 72
inherited reference objects remain exact. A new strategy reference neither
reweights old promises nor gives a source to unpriced functions/later duties.

The five productive priorities join existing sector/C04/C05 N/IC/SC/Treasury/
MP/OE/WL/AA/EV/OJ duties. Elected national/local bodies choose purposes/risk within
competence; qualified independent receipt, audit, direct courts and actual paid
executors remain distinct. New market inquiry, source/distribution analysis,
instruction, maintenance, records, remedy and transition work is not free.

R04/R05/R09/R03 and livelihood/service functions retain actual title, foreign
seller/route/time, public/private/monetary currency, gross limits and floors.
The independent u/f circuit creates no country wealth, tax, export, reserve,
private job, public monetary facility or universal retail payment account.
Private saving/retained profit/owned FX is not Treasury property. G/S/local,
client/pension/renewal/political custody cannot be pooled by a macro diagram.
New issue/debt requires competent actual authority; asset disposal is not recurring
revenue. No fewer-source or no-bond-need figure proves that people live better.

Existing wages, pensions/earned units, benefits/services, homes/title, creditor/
deposit/client property, contracts, licenses, cases, records and external duties
survive through paid OP/T successors. Changed monetary, tax, treaty or investment
architecture needs its own law, consent where required, price/source/legacy
migration and actual receiving. No readiness office or missing forecast postpones
due claims, called elections or peaceful replacement. Sensitive history is kept
safely without making testimony perpetually public.

Actual constitution/software/contribution permissions/holds and common proposed
method remain unchanged. No current public expenditure/tax/FX rule changes here.
Qualified evidence, research selection, legitimate authority, safe paid capacity
and observed better lives remain separate; the last three stay unverified.
New events/objections/changed meaning require fresh final decisions, not model
copying, a matched identity or transferred approval.
