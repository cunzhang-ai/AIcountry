# VR01–VR04. Record the event and give the person usable proof

*Part of [civil registration](../29-civil-registration-and-identity.md).*

**VR01 — Name the accountable public service.** The state employs a Civil
Registrar and qualified career officers. Cabinet appoints the Registrar
from an open qualified shortlist under C04 merit and protected tenure.
Publish appointment and removal reasons under the applicable law. The Assembly
sets legal scope, resources and lawful sharing purposes. Provide accessible
local and mobile counters, including through a lawfully funded municipal
agreement. A named officer accepts the first reachable filing, preserves
its original date and follows it until an authorized receiver accepts.
Cabinet cannot direct an individual finding, block unfavorable correction
or remove a record by policy instruction. OJ courts retain direct merits
and interim relief, PD retains information-power review and AA audits
public funds. The accused office cannot choose its own final reviewer.

**VR02 — Register birth immediately once established, even during dispute.**
A qualified birth attendant or facility sends the minimum authenticated
notice to the receiving registrar promptly once safe. A parent, carer,
person born or knowledgeable witness can report a birth outside a facility,
late or without institutional records. The officer obtains state-held
records, checks credible attestations and explains any remaining uncertainty.
Register the established birth without waiting for parentage, nationality,
name disagreement or a fee. Keep unknown or contested fields explicitly
qualified. If occurrence or link to this person still needs proof, provide
usable provisional evidence to the supported extent, marking its scope.
A temporary identifier may track this claim without conferring status.
No automatic birth-to-police or immigration watchlist follows.
A foundling or child with incomplete proof retains actual MB and RP
protection, including immediate C07 receiving and lawful assistance.
Neither a parent nor a clinic holds a veto over the child's birth record.

**VR03 — Make proof available in the same transaction where possible.**
Issue the first authorized extract and an essential replacement free.
Give paper, in-person assisted and equivalent optional digital proof.
Fund safe delivery to the person or duly authorized representative.
Offer a limited extract when only an event, age or other relevant fact is
needed. The proof states the issuer, source fact, verification scope and
correction route. A credential is evidence within its scope. It does not
establish another status or prove who controls it without proportionate
authentication. Give urgent provisional proof within 24 elapsed hours,
earlier where actual safety or a shorter legal duty requires it. Decide
routine extracts and reissues as soon as possible and within AP or any
shorter source-law period. Accepted proof and correction must actually
reach the receiver. An uncollected certificate does not establish access.

**VR04 — Record a chosen name and independently lawful status acts.**
A person with capacity for the particular decision may make a free,
supported authenticated declaration of their ordinary civil name.
The registrar accepts it without requiring marriage, diagnosis or a
court hearing. Any refusal needs a specific lawful ground and independent
review. No mandatory publication of former names. Preserve an authorized
link only for actual continuity, lawful claims and correction. Apply B
capacity, child voice and dispute law when a child's representative
requests a change. Record partnership, dissolution, parentage and other
civil events when their actual legal act is established. The record
neither manufactures the act nor overrides any constitutive requirements
of its source law. Foreign records receive proportionate authenticity and
recognition checks under B/MB. Preserve original meaning and uncertainty.
No unsafe origin-state contact, mandatory passport or invented local
equivalence follows from an unfamiliar record.
