## Baseline and purpose

This AI-assisted selection uses [Design413](predecessor-design-413/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md),
the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).
T18–T22 already joins concurrent losses with protected duties. JC09–JC12
and BCV16–BCV20 already protect exact release and fallback authority.
The unresolved design choice is how to compare programmes when several
scenarios look identical before the last useful start. Testing each
scenario with its own earlier ideal decision can produce a false claim
of whole-country readiness. Affected parties include due recipients,
care users, workers, water users, independent institutions and payers.

## Alternatives and selected programme path

Wait for confirmation and then buy the exact response. This avoids unused
preparation but can miss the service deadline. Prepare one fixed response.
This is cheaper if the selected risk materializes but leaves the other
exposure. Prepare both fixed responses. This would cover both on the
stipulated staffing, but the early and total finance do not cover it.
Pay to retain two qualified provider options and authorize one call.
The constructed scenario selects this last path to protect either
single loss on time while retaining protected ordinary services.
It spends standby funds even if neither loss occurs. A compound loss,
failed signal or late activation funds remains an uncovered exposure.

This is an exact conditional programme choice under the authored cash,
lead and response assumptions. It is open to an earlier preventive or
independent capacity alternative. There is no asserted probability or
general priority between water and care. No independent qualified review
or actual affected-party participation was secured.

## Authority and reconciliation

The elected bodies control preparation, sources, scope and binding choices.
Qualified service officers verify evidence and accept the authorized acts.
Existing independent review and ordinary sector priorities continue.
Funded standby cannot be booked into both concurrent responses. Protect
the due benefits, ordinary care and independent public functions against
the same early cash constraint. Use current source, scope and accepted
providers before any branch is exercised. Opposition and affected people
can inspect the dates and challenge an omitted effect. Verify appointments,
custody, payer control and independent execution before reliance.

Existing rights, contracts, original clocks, separate local powers and
pending remedies continue. Carry used and unused options, paid staff,
replenishment and actual delivery through ordinary succession. AI can
compare chronologies. It cannot classify the legal trigger, activate
unvoted spending or treat an outcome known later as earlier evidence.
