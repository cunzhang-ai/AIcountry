# Four payers through year two

Continue the repaired, stressed path of the [Design440 case](../joined-opening-budget-and-release/01-four-payers-two-windows.md).
At day 90 Treasury has 1 and the locality, pension fund and rail
operator each have zero usable units. The old 5-unit standby is
already repaid. Restricted capital stays excluded. The new figures
below are constructed. All early and late flows are assigned to
the stated useful window and only count when legally settled.

| Treasury and recipient calendar | Rest of year one | Year two early | Year two later |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Opening Treasury balance, ordinary path | 1 | 2 | 1 |
| New settled external receipts | 18 | 12 | 18 |
| Treasury external payments | 9 | 8 | 8 |
| Local, pension and operator transfers | 3 + 3 + 2 | 2 + 2 + 1 | 3 + 2 + 2 |
| Closing Treasury balance | 2 | 1 | 4 |
| Treasury minimum | 1 | 1 | 1 |

The Treasury external payments include protected administration
of elections, courts, remedies and debt. In the early year-two
window these are respectively 2, 2 and 4, counting courts and
remedies together. The three service payers use the transfers
for separately due care, pensions and rail in each window, and
close each with zero usable cash. The first-year contribution
to the opening reserve is 1 above the minimum. No historical
balance is imported.

## Timing, reform and a second delay

Under ordinary settlement the year-two total leaves 3 above
the minimum at the end. A phased reform needs 1 for early
preparation and 2 for late delivery. It crosses the early
minimum by 1 despite balancing at year end. The faster path
needs 4 early and 2 late. It fails both windows. Retain and
repair fits the ordinary case. Select it unless the phased
path receives a qualified earlier source and independent
capacity, including a separate recurrent source for any
continuing obligation.

Under stress, 3 of the 12 early receipts moves to the late
window. The early balance falls to −2. It is short by 2 for
due payments and by 3 including the minimum. If lawfully
prefunded before the first deadline, a 3-unit standby restores
the minimum. Assume it costs 1 in the late window and that
its 3 of principal and cost are then due. If the delayed
receipt clears, the late balance ends at 3. If it fails again,
the late balance ends at zero. The repair path would need
1 of new lawful cover to retain the minimum. The early draw
therefore needs a qualified next-window response or different
repayment terms before it can support a feasible stress plan.

A separate maturity restructuring could, if lawfully agreed
in advance, shift 3 of early debt principal into the later
window with a cost of 1. On the stated figures it produces
the same balances as the standby. Compare price, counterparty,
conditions and expiry before choosing. Neither alternative is
an observed facility. Report any remaining payment or buffer
gap and preserve original legal claims.

The cash comparison assumes the qualified staffing and existing
receiving of the ordinary services. It gives no evidence of
extra staff or of actual outcomes. The simulated second-year
result does not close unpriced national duties.
