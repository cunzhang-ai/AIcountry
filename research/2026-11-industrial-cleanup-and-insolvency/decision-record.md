# Design221. Fund environmental protection through industrial failure

## Baseline and decision

Design220 is the pinned baseline. Use the current framework and decision
template. NR already requires secured closure for new mining exposure.
TS and TD carry industrial and environmental duties. U04 determines
ordinary insolvency distribution. The unresolved choice is how a covered
site carries usable early finance and later verified cleanup through a
weak transfer, operator insolvency or absent owner without implying a
blanket preference or waiting for final liability before protecting people.

Select CLN01–CLN08. Require defined scheduled exposure, collectible
assurance and accepted funded transfers. Preserve urgent water and
health duties during disputes. Select a prospective registered charge
on designated site interests after earlier perfected rights, within its
published ceiling, and ahead of later consensual security. For proceeds
otherwise unencumbered, preserve U04's protected ranks first. Establish
a prospective residual sector account by elected law. Use separate
voted source for unmatched historic sites and finite urgent advances.
Actual fees and priorities require lawful coordinated adoption.

## Evidence and competing choices

The Redwater docket shows one legal resolution about sale proceeds.
The Auditor General's report identifies security, fund sustainability
and closure timing concerns. Later regulator reports distinguish spend,
quotas, certification, inactive inventory and updated estimated stock.
These institutional records qualify separate mechanisms within one
jurisdiction. They establish neither enough cash at every deadline
nor a causal advantage for this design.

Stronger ordinary enforcement and up-front individual security can
avoid changes in credit priority, but leave historical and extreme
residual risk. A broader estate-wide environmental priority can
reduce residual public or industry costs if assets exist. It can
impose wider credit losses, discourage restructuring and compete
with vulnerable workers or unrelated creditors. A fully pooled
fund can share risk and help small operators enter, but may reward
poor prevention and fail in a sector-wide downturn. General public
cleanup can protect people fast with elected finance while shifting
some costs away from those creating the risk. A narrow prior-notice
site charge and separately funded residual path preserves explicit
credit tradeoffs. Retain independent exposure assessment and review
to stop an operator or regulator understating or overstating the charge.

## Compatibility, paid transition and reopening

CLN05 is an express prospective exception alongside U04 and PT for
specified site interests only. Enact exact registration, ceiling,
release, eligible uses, proof, accounting and ordinary court jurisdiction.
Do not change existing perfected rank, creditor protections, employer
rights or bankruptcy avoidance by implication. Site duties, worker
claims, private damages and insurer claims remain distinct. TS/TD and
NR continue their stronger actual scope. WS, HP and RC govern immediate
protection. Existing lawful powers and consent rules apply. FB and DB
govern the separate source and budget choice. Protect opposition access
and ordinary independent review through PE and AA.

Map original permits, records, prior liens, assurances and affected
claims to lawful funded officers and actual accepting providers. No new
royalty, blanket fine or guarantee follows from this record. Price
prospective participation and credit effects, including concentration
among small firms and real financing needs. Actual funding and lived
outcomes are unverified.

Next compare a defined site and an existing historic exposure with
actual operator, security terms, creditor notice, independent residents,
treatment capacity and dated authorized funds. Run loss of a surety,
delayed realization and downstream water sampling in a protected
exercise. Reopen for duplicated collateral, unavailable source,
credit exclusion, harm after certification or a stronger qualified
ordinary route. Actual affected participation, qualified review,
enactment, funding and better lives remain open.
