# Evidence for common exposure in the first budget

Irish Revenue's [Corporation Tax – 2025 Payments and 2024 Returns](https://www.revenue.ie/en/corporate/documents/research/ct-analysis-2026.pdf)
is preserved in the [original published PDF](original-sources/ireland-ct-payments-2025.pdf).
Table 1 and the report text distinguish the €32.9 billion of net receipts
excluding receipts arising from the Court of Justice of the European Union
ruling from €34.7 billion total. Table 3's row and column for 2025 show
€19,526 million and a published 59% for that year's top ten groups. The top
ten individual companies are a different grouping. Table 4 gives €5,106
million in PAYE employment income and 43,066 employments for the top ten
groups. Income is distinct from receipts of tax withheld, and employments
are distinct from unique workers. Figure 6 shows other tax and contribution
channels. These observations identify shared exposure across bases. The
report provides no joint shock elasticity used in the authored comparison.

Ireland's [Future Ireland Fund and Infrastructure, Climate and Nature Fund Act](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2024/act/16/enacted/en/print)
provides distinct long-term and stabilization purposes. The
[National Treasury Management Agency](https://www.ntma.ie/business-areas/future-ireland-funds/infrastructure-climate-and-nature-fund)
describes support in significant actual or likely fiscal deterioration with
the instrument's separate environmental scope. This is an existing
alternative to relying on a single year's cash. Neither source qualifies a
draw for the hypothetical purposes or proves delivery.
