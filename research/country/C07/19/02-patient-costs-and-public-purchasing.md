# HN05–HN08: specialist access must be usable without private wealth

*Part of [C07/19](../19-clinical-networks-and-specialist-access.md); design 123 candidate.*

**HN05 — Fund the necessary journey as part of access.** For covered indicated
care unavailable on a usable local route, the public purchaser funds necessary
safe transport, accommodation/subsistence, communication/access support and an
actually necessary companion or substitute support under applicable need rules.
Assess dependent-care and lost-earnings consequences through their proper I/TW/
AS/S sources without double payment. No assumed relative supplies unpaid escort,
care at home or cash. This adds unpriced public support exposure beyond the named
route quote, not a universal wage/profit guarantee for anyone travelling.

**HN06 — Do not require poor patients to finance public care first.** Provide
lawful direct booking/payment or an accessible advance for necessary approved
journey/support costs, with correction of actual unused/duplicate amounts and
ordinary due process. Do not condition covered necessary treatment or travel on
employment, insurance contributions, citizenship or private bridge cash under S01.
Independent urgency/coverage challenge must be usable before the safe time; a
purchasing panel's meeting cycle cannot authorize waiting past it. Patient and
clinical consent remain separate from a public payment authorization.

**HN07 — Make high-cost and contingency burdens explicit.** Keep national
pooled public health finance, actual appropriations/standby and separate source
for due emergency substitutions, unusual case mix, FX and provider insolvency.
Report reservation, unused capacity, paid treatment, travel, staff and continuing
liability separately. A contract/insurer ceiling does not narrow an already due
S right. New material general coverage or rationing still needs elected law/
rights review; a purchaser, treasury analyst or AI cannot silently select people
by productivity, identity, political views or profitability. An underfunded public
promise remains visible, not a fabricated unlimited funded insurance pool.

**HN08 — Let procurement be replaced and publicly challenged.** Publish safe
service/price/quality/conflict and capacity information, preserve protected
patient/source data, actual alternatives and paid failure access. Do not grant
a single broker, platform, insurer or hospital exclusive power to decide lawful
coverage, appeals and whether its own service was received. Separate purchaser,
provider, independent clinical judgment and existing court powers. Sharing support
is bounded by OP/PD; common controllers remain a capture/availability risk even
with several contract names. No new regulator or blanket public bailout is created.

[T105–T108](../../C14/06/23-patient-access-and-paid-roster.md) tests
ordinary funded transport and an independent alternate against the roster
change, payroll window and actual receiving clinic.
