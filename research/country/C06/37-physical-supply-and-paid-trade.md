# C06. Physical supply and paid trade

3 November2026 · Design166 (physical baseline92) · PS01–PS32 proposed country choice, not enacted law

**Select resource-fit plural domestic production, repair/reuse, diversified paid
imports and publicly accountable essential networks.** Reject autarky, an assumed
AI productivity dividend and the old nominal-output/export residual as supply
plans. A domestic asset may still depend on foreign fuel, components and skills;
an import may be the less harmful choice without being a dependable contract.

This is an operating architecture with discriminating choices, not central
direction of private consumption. Producers and households retain lawful choice;
elected bodies set public burdens, rights, ecological limits and supply mandates.
Existing stewards/operators, local authorities, independent inspection and courts
retain their distinct powers. AI forecasts and technically balanced data confer
no tax, rationing, acquisition or personal-surveillance authority.

The [joined reference](../../settlement/physical-supply/README.md) compares three
food/land/water recipes with domestic-source and imported-water variants, then
links their energy and named foreign purchases. More irrigation can save land
and labor while defeating source capacity. Import-heavy food reduces farming,
but also reduces usable wastewater demand and increases external dependence.
An annual electricity balance can coexist with an immediate outage failure.
The old 500m export assumption supplies no verified finance to this choice.

**Limits:** recipes, quotes and stocks are authored, not actual terrain, adequate
diets, accepted plants or funded purchases. Manufacturing, private goods,
mobility, full grid/capital chains and household preparation remain additional
requirements where unmeasured; the named subset is not national feasibility.
The [decision](../../2026-11-physical-supply-and-trade/decision-record.md)
preserves the predecessor, live rivals, received objections and scope.

competence and CP/NP lawful scarcity continue. Next integrate physical recipes,

country allocation, retaining failed cases and continuing cohorts. A better
structural alternative or a rejected physical hypothesis can displace this one;
neither a new index nor a passed calculation adopts it.
