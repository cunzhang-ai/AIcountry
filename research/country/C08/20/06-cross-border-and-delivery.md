# Preserve people across borders and make the remedy arrive

*RP11–RP12; part of [C08/20](../20-reproductive-law-and-parentage.md).*

**RP11 — Child status is not a foreign contract's enforcement clause.**
Apply B41–B52's actual connection, competent authority, original evidence,
notice and fair review. Recognize severable lawful child status, support,
identity and family effects where their proper grounds are established;
a prohibited surrender/payment term can be refused without erasing the child.
A different family form, foreign origin or adult mistake alone justifies
neither automatic recognition nor blanket refusal. A domestic order does
not rewrite a foreign registry, citizenship rule or immune state's law.

The competent child court examines actual authority, consent, safety, source
material/records, established relationships and child voice/representation.
Protect provisional care/identity/residence access while conflicts proceed.
No removal/return or surrender before a credible serious protection risk
can be heard under applicable B45/B31 safeguards. No detention of a pregnant
person or child to secure a fee, discourage travel or punish an adult's act.
Domestic enforcement of foreign offenses/requests retains actual P/G treaty,
finality and protected-transfer grounds; a foreign alert is not authority.

No uniform global surrogacy permission or new Hague parentage convention is
assumed. The inspected HCCH project update records a2026 decision not to
advance to convention drafting at that stage; actual future instruments
need fresh checks. Donor distribution/records cannot be called global merely
because a platform has foreign users. Preserve unknown source meanings and
actual limitations rather than certify consent through data convergence.

**RP12 — A private adult dispute does not suspend public receiving.** C07/S
funds lawful clinically indicated care, urgent aftercare and newborn needs;
I/S income/care and B child services receive on actual due grounds. Public
coverage of particular infertility cycles, storage, compensation or new
technology needs S02's explicit elected opportunity-cost choice, reasons and
appeal. No entitlement to another's material/body or guaranteed child follows. Birth/recovery
care and lawful medical leave follow the person's bodily needs regardless
of transferring legal parenthood; other carers' leave follows actual lawful
care roles, not genetics. Reconcile C05/C07 due rights rather than create
an unpaid recovery/child-care obligation.

Direct B13/B16/B17 clinical/civil/child review is accessible without exhausting
clinic appeals or paying for a full technical case. Evidence, private advice,
interpretation, independent expertise, child representation, source custody,
usable urgent time and accepted execution are actually funded roles, not
one family member or AI labelled independent. Serious reproductive mistreatment
receives outside-provider investigation; regulatory protection/fines use V,
private/civil loss B/U and criminal guilt RP15/P on distinct grounds.

Compensation names actual payer/assets, useful deadline and receipt. A failed
broker, insufficient escrow, foreign law or uninsured injury leaves a real
unmet claim; no automatic unlimited Treasury indemnity or invented insurance.
C06/12 and/23 count shared capacity once and preserve public urgent protection.
An existing ledger share, birth count or booked appointment is not received
care, public consent or superiority over another country's lived outcomes.
