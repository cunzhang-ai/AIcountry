# WS01–WS05. An actual public duty without a water sovereign

*Part of [water allocation and service](../16-water-allocation-and-service.md).*

**WS01 — Name the public employers and heads.** State water stewardship employs
one qualified career Chief Water Steward through Cabinet/C04 open competition/
public shortlist, published mandate/reasons/conflicts and protected employment.
It maintains the connected-resource account, issues covered abstraction
permissions within actual national legal jurisdiction under enacted criteria and arranges the existing national essential-
service guarantee. Assembly chooses material resource pools, priorities, source
and cross-local conflicts. The Steward cannot tax, change ecological floors,
acquire another's title or issue general emergency commands. No new board/police.

The local public employer appoints a qualified Water Service Director under
EL11/C04 merit, responsible for service, public assets/contracts and receiving.
Council chooses its budget and lawful LR tariffs, not privileged pumping rights.
Lawful joint public service may share a Director while retaining each council's
rights/duties, downstream representation, funded dependence and practical exit.
A basin map does not create a sovereign tier or bypass TS territorial consent.

**WS02 — A direct public default with replaceable management.** PS08 public/
common control of new publicly financed core assets remains. For a new publicly
arranged drinking-water system and residual guaranteed service choose a
noncommercial direct public operating unit under the Director, transparent
service/capital accounts and no private equity claim on monopoly revenue.
Qualified construction/testing/bounded operations can be bought through SC;
public eligibility/allocation/tariff authority cannot be contracted away.
Existing lawful licensed, cooperative, public and self-supply arrangements are
not forcibly converted; WS19/OP/T retain their actual source duties and claims.
This refines one utility default, not ownership of the whole plural economy.

Council/Assembly may replace lawful management assignments with reasons and
qualified accepted succession. C04 protects employment/dissent and due process,
not perpetual command or a source/implementation veto. Applicable finite acting
rules cannot be serially bypassed. Workers/users, including tenants and those
without a connection, have accessible supported complaints/consultation and
published responses before material changes; consumption, wealth or AI copies
do not multiply political voice. A new cabinet cannot purge lawful dissenters.

**WS03 — Review outside supplier and allocator.** A separate state career Chief
Water Reviewer has a distinct C04 competition, Cabinet appointment, protected
case judgment, published conflicts, identified source and direct Assembly/AA
reporting. It reviews allowed service costs, allocation compliance and consumer
performance, including public operators, within exact enacted powers. It neither
sets tariffs nor issues its own allocation, and does not absorb TD07 public-
health sampling, TS safety investigation, AA audit or OJ prosecutions/courts.
AP/V ordinary courts supply applicable merits/source/omission and interim relief.
Own-office abuse goes to outside OJ/court/AA roles, never its own final judgment.

Shared Cabinet/lab/model/payment-key/source control can capture all three roles.
Distinct labels are no independence: fund actual unimplicated people/source
access, assistance, court judgment payer and scoped execution. Court/AA/OJ
appointments/status remain under their own law, not C04 managerial discretion.

**WS04 — A connected resource, with original rights.** Assembly/actual delegated
plan defines basin/aquifer and seasonal locations; long-term sustainable
withdrawal/depletion and shorter-period envelopes; quality/temperature,
ecological/downstream/foreign duties, uncertainty and essential reservations.
Affected upstream/downstream/collective communities and original observations
must be represented before boundaries/priorities settle. Scarcity is no new
water. Historic use, land/mineral title or an incomplete registry is not proof
of owned pumping. Preserve actual public/private/collective/customary permits,
subsistence, habitat and treaty claims with their source, including disputes.

A plan exposes over-allocation; it does not revoke old rights. Actual acquisition/
variation needs source authority, LA/compensation where due and paid successors.
Foreign inflow/contract needs actual agreement, source capacity and owned payment;
a domestic plan binds no foreign manager. No approval follows from silence.

**WS05 — Location, timing and returns matter.** Keep gross abstraction,
consumption, groundwater drawdown/delayed river capture, usable return, storage/
recharge, evaporation, conveyance losses and discharge distinct. Credit only
evidenced lawful return at compatible place/time/quality and only once; unknown
return is not guaranteed supply. Smaller diversion/reuse may reduce downstream
availability when returns fall. Never count one reused litre as both a new
source and the original downstream return. Brine/unsafe discharge/paper recharge
is no restored ecological flow. Use bounded causal/lag scenarios and original
readings, not annual totals to license a dry-season peak or delayed aquifer harm.

Unresolved evidence needs proportionate assistance and bounded reasons, not
invented capacity, automatic denial of unrelated essential access or universal
home surveillance. Preserve meter/sample/model uncertainty and corrected source
history; one numerical proxy cannot settle collective rights or distribution.
