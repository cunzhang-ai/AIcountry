# Y17–Y20: common longevity risk, personal agency and equal standing

*Part of [Y retirement](../04-retirement-security.md), design157 candidate.*

**Y17 — A common public conversion basis.** For Y04's new early/deferred share
elections, use one publicly specified survival/annuity basis for persons in the
same birth-year cohort and at the same chosen claim age. Do not split that
public basis by sex/gender, ancestry, income/wealth, disability/diagnosis,
occupation, household status or a predicted individual lifespan. Statistical
binary categories do not define the genders or persons entitled to protection.
This is a normative pooling choice, not individual actuarial equivalence or a
claim that longevity inequalities disappear. The common factor changes only
the elected earned share's start; no factor reduces the ordinary age65 basic
or alters already accrued bases. Y03 price/index and RF old units persist.

**Y18 — Make the common method publicly answerable.** Assembly law sets the
common cohort/method, source revisions, update rule and prospective election
dates. Existing evidence administration commissions qualified actuarial work,
publishes the computed schedule/assumptions/uncertainty and preserves safe
correction; independent challenge and courts retain existing powers. A model,
actuary, insurer, Treasury or software vendor cannot choose a new risk class,
age or accrued-right haircut. Proper factors still require the SAME earlier
valuation age, survival to deferred start, actual monthly advance timing,
discount/tail and revaluation rules under Y04. Assess voluntary take-up/selection
and survivor/new-service effects separately; no Y09 pot cut or old-unit recap.
No adequate basis means no valid
early/deferred quote, not lost age65 entitlement. Published period life
expectancy or an arithmetic average of male/female factors is no qualified
cohort schedule. A new method cannot approve its own legal authority.

**Y19 — Do not make equality require an unsafe retirement choice.** Keep Y04's
funded health/disability/hazardous-work bridge, I05, accessible work/paid leave,
care and accommodation as separate rights. A person need not accept a reduced
annuity, submit genetic/lifespan evidence, perform unsafe work or provide
compulsory family care to obtain ordinary protection. Common factors can still
favor longer-lived groups and hurt a shorter-lived person's expected lifetime
receipt. Preserve that objection and compare targeted support, stronger basic,
different accrual, differentiated-factor and simpler standard-age alternatives;
do not claim one ratio settles distributive justice. Actual funded bridge/access
and a voluntary decision remain essential, not a favorable form alone.

**Y20 — A person is not a fiscal profitability score.** Public authorities and
their AI/contractors must not use modeled lifetime net fiscal contribution,
expected care cost or predicted lifespan to rank political standing or deny
ordinary subsistence, basic age protection, necessary care or legal remedy.
Keep actual statutory need/service/earned eligibility and equal standing
distinct. Aggregate lawful cohort/source planning remains possible with safe
data, uncertainty and plural challenge; an optional personal explanation is
neither an eligibility score nor consent to a cross-sector life dossier.
Necessary credit/claim evidence remains proportionate under Y/I/PD law. This
public-Y rule does not silently rewrite voluntary private NB/PF insurance law.
