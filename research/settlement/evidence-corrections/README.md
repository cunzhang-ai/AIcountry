# A corrected observation and a continuing public obligation

[Canonical rules](../../country/C11/11-evidence-corrections-and-public-reliance.md)
· [Worked decisions](01-allocations-and-indexation.md)
· [Original observations](../../2026-11-evidence-corrections-and-public-reliance/sources.md)

Compare three responses to the same discovery. Publishing corrected evidence and
relying on ordinary appeals is simple and protects legal stability. It is a strong
option for ordinary updates and known users who can act in time. It leaves people
to discover material mistakes behind adverse payments or services. Blanket
preservation of every published allocation reduces immediate planning shock. It
shifts the full cost to a new payer or to others within the existing budget, even
where no cost was incurred. Publication and preservation serve different purposes.

**Select mandatory review for material public reliance and a bounded option for
funded continuity.** Keep accurate records and the applicable legal vintage.
Recompute when the controlling law requires it. Honor continuing claims and make
new relief an explicit elected choice with its own cost, source and eligibility.
Prioritize useful-time relief and check actual receipt. Retain ordinary appeals
and source correction for other cases.

The hard tradeoff is finding excluded people without building a central record
of everyone's encounters with the state. Decentralized traces may miss an office
or person and require added work and funding. Compare missed harms, timeliness,
privacy costs and staffing on the same services before expanding the duty.
