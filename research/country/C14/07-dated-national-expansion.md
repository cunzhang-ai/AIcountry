# C14. Dated national expansion without a readiness veto

15 October 2026 · Design 79 · Proposed research, not a commencement instrument

[C06 CP03–CP04](../C06/28/02-dated-commitments-and-equality.md) now owns the
national new-commitment/cohort choice. C14 T01–T12 continues to own source-law
rights, paid preparation, explicit start dates, missed delivery and handover.
S16/Y15 no longer select one unsupported common second-calendar-year start for
all new services and new pension-service accrual. Actual cohort dates require
express elected law in the adopting schedule, with source/interim/remedy duties.

This is a substantive sequencing choice. It can delay proposed improvements and
preserve an unequal baseline; it must explain need/place/disability incidence
and fund actual interim protection. A comprehensive earlier guarantee financed
through actual recruitment, partnerships and redistribution remains a real rival.
No mythical seven-year national construction rule or universal engineering lead
time is inferred from the old fictional pipeline.

Separate existing/accrued rights, proposed future additions, adopted future-dated
rights and already overdue duties. Readiness evidence cannot suspend existing
rights or activate indefinite ministerial postponement. Contributions and their
accrual/credit treatment must be coherent; records conversion cannot erase an
earned claim, indexation or pending remedy. Changed taxes/coverage/burdens need
their own competent prospective law, source map and independent challenge.

C06's corrected accounting bridge does not create production, available workers
or safe imports. A larger nominal balance or new bank owner cannot pay physical
preparation twice. Proposed cohorts need a single cash/people/material/foreign
schedule including displaced market production and ordinary democratic/court
functions. Elected approval is distinct from qualified technical evidence;
Treasury and the inspector can expose failure but acquire no policy veto.

If an enacted date is missed, T03/T04/E retain non-delivery, interim protection,
responsible receiving and funded correction. Elections and peaceful replacement
continue; no project can protect its cabinet until completion. Custody, consent,
scarcity, clinical triage and emergency powers remain under their own laws.

Full current-duty quantity/source estimates and actual participation/adoption/
