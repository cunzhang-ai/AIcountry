# Who may decide, pay, act and correct the state

29 September 2026 · Integrated candidate for C02–C04/C06–C08/C10–C14

This is the legal operating design for [AIcountry's country proposal](COUNTRY-DESIGN.md).
It allocates public authority through a complete decision chain. It also specifies
what must happen when power changes hands. The earlier manuscript is a research
edition with AI-exclusive drafting permissions, not an operating constitution for
a known territory. These clauses are replacement candidates, not a change to that
edition, the software's permissions or any real person's legal rights.

## 1. Authority begins with the people, under continuing rights

A legitimately established constitution grants and limits each public office.
The Assembly is the ordinary national lawmaker; people and members can introduce
proposals and organize opposition. A cabinet depends on Assembly confidence and
administers existing law. Elected local councils make local policy within national
rights and expressly delegated tax and service powers. Courts decide legal disputes
and protect rights; they do not acquire general legislative competence when the
Assembly is slow. Public officers and AI tools have only lawful delegated tasks.

Publication in the project research library is not promulgation of a statute. A
ballot, authorized vote, effective date and authenticated public legal register
have separate functions. No developer key, owner account, model verification or
research majority is sovereign. A valid enacted text needs a lawful authorizing
decision by the competent body, an exact final text, a public record, rights review
where applicable and an operative date. An independent publication office authenticates
the record; it cannot withhold an enacted law for policy reasons. A refusal or altered
publication is challengeable, with the original vote and signed text preserved.

The public can replace government and propose institutional change without AI approval.
Protected constitutional functions—equal political standing, meaningful opposition,
effective rights remedies and peaceful transfer—cannot be abolished through an
ordinary law, budget rider, procurement contract or emergency order. Their legal
forms may change through a legitimate constitutional route that preserves the
functions. A court may enforce that constraint under reviewable reasons; no court,
AI assurance office or political party becomes an irremovable guardian.

## 2. An end-to-end public decision

| Stage | Competent actor | Binding output | Independent check or correction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agenda and analysis | Citizens, elected members, cabinet, local councils, experts and AI within mandates | Proposal and evidence, not law | Public alternatives, opposition agenda rights, source/interest disclosure and accessible participation |
| General law, tax and appropriation | Elected Assembly under constitutional voting rules | Exact enacted text and funding authority | Courts examine legality/rights; public and opposition can replace lawmakers |
| Executive and local policy | Cabinet and elected local councils within enacted law | Regulations, plans and lawful contracts | Assembly scrutiny, local elections, independent review and fiscal audit |
| Individual decisions | Competent agency officer or lawful adjudicator | Reasoned, reviewable case decision | Fair notice, access to material, timely independent remedy; automation is contestable |
| Commitment and payment | Authorized service officer, separate verifier, treasury and accountant | Recorded obligation, actual lawful disbursement and reconciliation | Audit of authority, cash, recipient receipt and delivered service |
| Legal remedy | Independent competent court or tribunal | Order with identified obligation and executor | Appeal and public compliance reporting with protected data |
| Rule correction | Assembly or constitutionally authorized people | Amended law, prospectively effective unless lawfully specified otherwise | No administrative or AI self-amendment; pending claims and dissent receive explicit treatment |

A claimant may seek urgent court protection directly where delay would defeat the
right. The court cannot conjure missing workers or lender funds. It can identify
breach, order action within lawful competence, preserve a claim, award lawful relief
and require the state to disclose and correct a resource failure. Treasury and the
responsible provider must report actual execution; a judgment unimplemented because
their controller blocks funds is a constitutional failure, not a completed remedy.

## 3. Taxation, budget and the route from rights to delivery

The Assembly alone enacts taxes, rates and exemptions within the constitution.
A local council may use a tax or rate only under clear national authorization and
local democratic procedure; a regulator cannot create a tax by changing a model
parameter. The revenue authority assesses and collects under published law, with
independent taxpayer challenge. Currency and debt powers need distinct enacted
mandates, financing limits and public risk reporting. An external settlement
currency remains a conservative planning assumption, not a claim of access.

Cabinet prepares a consolidated budget and a funded guarantee-and-delivery schedule.
The Assembly adopts the annual appropriation and any material tax or spending change.
It must see obligations, local funding gaps, cash timing, staffing, maintenance,
debt service, contingencies and alternatives. A treasury forecast is evidence,
not legal authority. Treasury executes the enacted schedule and releases funds
against lawful commitments; it cannot disqualify a person, delay an inconvenient
court order or reallocate another agency's ceiling by software rule.

Keep the treasury's four distinct controls: a valid appropriation, authorized
commitment, independent confirmation of delivery or eligibility, and payment plus
reconciliation. For urgent provisional assistance, the authorizing law may permit
a documented payment on stated provisional evidence before full later verification.
The payment is still within a delegated ceiling and audited after delivery; requiring
complete conventional documents before the need's due time would defeat that law.
One person, agent or contractor cannot create a supplier, approve, verify and pay
the same claim. The independent reviewer must be able to inspect protected evidence
without treasury or the challenged provider dictating the finding.

National government enacts essential guarantees and their funding responsibilities.
Local elected authorities choose lawful delivery methods and receive need-based
equalization; the national authority remains accountable for an unfilled legal floor.
An inadequate transfer is a challengeable public funding decision. It does not
authorize the locality to revoke a resident's right, or a court to compel an
uncontracted private supplier without law. Pending claims and accrued contract
payments remain liabilities when a ministry or provider changes.

## 4. Budget lapse: lawful power on day 91

Distinguish a standing authorization from the annual budget. The proposed general
law establishes a **continuing essential-obligations schedule** for specified
statutory benefits, staffed essential services, due judgments, existing lawful
contracts needed for continuity, and minimum election/court/audit operations. It
states eligibility, cost rules, responsible payer, due dates, permitted renewals,
public accounts and review. The Assembly may amend it through ordinary law subject
to constitutional rights. It cannot be silently cancelled by failing to vote on
an annual budget. It must be reviewed and forecast each year so continuation does
not become an invisible permanent budget.

A separately preauthorized bridge covers other **existing** indispensable operations
for up to the candidate's 90 days, using actual obligations due rather than a
monthly fraction of the prior budget. It cannot finance new programs, political
appointments or permanent contracts. By the first 30 days cabinet discloses the
gap and a successor proposal; by 60 days Assembly holds a recorded vote on a normal
budget or a distinct lawful continuation. The caretaker executive cannot renew the
bridge by relabeling it or declaring an emergency. Courts review rights-related
shortfalls and procedural abuse without acquiring a general tax power.

At day 91 the temporary bridge expires. **The previously enacted standing schedule
continues** for its defined obligations, while other activity requires a new lawful
appropriation. Accrued obligations do not vanish. This resolves the earlier missing
legal authority for core day-91 activity in the proposed design; it does **not**
prove enough cash, qualified staff, suppliers or fair service levels exist. If
financing fails, publish arrears and interruption before they occur, preserve claims
and the independent remedy, and require an Assembly decision on actual options.
No deadline converts silence into a new government or law. Standing entitlements
themselves are open to democratic amendment with applicable rights and transition
protections; they are not an untouchable fiscal veto over future voters.

## 5. Courts, appointments and the enforcement chain

Ordinary courts retain their lawful jurisdiction, including urgent individual-rights
remedies. A constitutional court or chamber can review law and institutional
competence, with public reasons, appeal/reconsideration as specified, protected
staffing and direct lawful treasury payments. A binding order names who must act
and what evidence of completion is required. The cabinet may propose an alternative
lawful remedy; it cannot erase an order by withholding its only access credential.

Staggered appointment sources, disclosed nominations and nonretaliatory removal are
reference safeguards. For every office, check who controls the candidate pool,
selection body, budget, data, case assignment, payment operator and enforcement.
Opposition support and a citizen panel do not prove independence if their sponsors
or data channels are shared. A proposed vacancy safeguard retains ordinary courts'
preexisting individual-rights jurisdiction and allows carefully limited interim
relief while a constitutional bench is nonfunctional; final constitutional questions
await a lawfully competent court. That route needs explicit prior jurisdiction,
appeal and conflict rules before adoption. It is not an invitation to invent a
loyal emergency court after appointments fail.

No actor gains a replacement appointment power merely because the intended process
is late. At the same time, government may not hide a rights denial behind a vacant
specialist court if ordinary lawful remedies remain. A simultaneous failure of
elections, courts, treasury, records and lawful enforcement remains a critical
breakdown; additional paper institutions cannot guarantee recovery from common
capture.

## 6. Elections, emergencies and external commitments

Published election dates, reporting duties and caretaker limits become observable
before polling. A certification delay triggers disclosure and an evidence-based
court remedy; it does not certify an AI estimate or give the incumbent a new term.
When lawful results establish a successor, records, payment credentials, premises
and security command transfer to that successor. Civil servants owe their duties
to office and law, not a defeated person or vendor. Neither a party militia nor a
model chooses the winner.

Use the democratic candidate's proposed emergency architecture: an initial executive
period of at most 14 days; Assembly renewal, independent review and a cumulative
limit before ordinary legislation is required. Exact thresholds remain provisional.
Emergency acts identify the concrete threat, place, power, funding, expiry and remedy.
They cannot amend succession, election rules, core rights or ordinary tax law, and
cannot postpone elections by executive declaration alone. Essential services and
judicial opposition scrutiny continue through the lawful routes available. A true
catastrophic loss of institutions is a public legitimacy crisis, not a license for
the project owner or AI to rule.

Major treaties, defense commitments and guarantees need an Assembly mandate, public
fiscal and rights analysis, and the competent constitutional ratification procedure.
The cabinet negotiates within that mandate. AI may translate, model and compare
terms; it has no independent treaty veto or signature on behalf of the people.
Existing treaty duties cannot be cancelled by publishing a new research edition.

## 8. Proposed core replacement provisions for legal review

The following is reviewable candidate wording for the conflicting nucleus above.
It is original drafting, not a patch to the manuscript or an adopted constitution.
Retained text still needs an article-by-article conflict and rights review against
the then-current edition. Detailed election and appointment parameters in the prior
democratic candidate remain provisional and are not silently enacted by this core.

**A1 — Public source and legal hierarchy.** Public authority arises from people
under a legitimately adopted constitution. Constitution, statutes, lawful regulations
and individual decisions have that order of precedence. Each binding instrument
has an exact public text, competent adopting body, effective date and challenge
route. An unpublished instruction, model output or software change is not law.

**A2 — Legislative and local competence.** Elected Assembly members and the people
through enacted proposal routes may originate general laws. The Assembly alone
enacts national statutes, taxes and appropriations. Local elected councils legislate
only within express constitutional or statutory competence. Neither an AI system,
minister, publication officer nor court may replace the required legislative vote.
Courts retain lawful constitutional and rights review; local variation cannot
remove the national rights floor.

**A3 — Publication and AI assistance.** The authenticated public register publishes
the exact enacted text and history after the lawful decision. A technical assurance
failure may be reported and corrected; it does not create an unaccountable veto over
enactment. AI tools can draft, compare, translate and advise within revocable mandates.
People can challenge their framing and use effective non-AI routes. Research versions
and four-stage model reviews are labelled as research, not promulgated law.

**A4 — Administration and individual remedies.** Cabinet and accountable local agencies
implement published law within appropriations. Adverse decisions identify authority,
facts, evidence, reasons, decision-maker and independent remedy. Urgent protection
and basic-service duties begin when a competent receiving service learns of the need;
a late completeness notice cannot restart their clock. A court can order lawful
relief and require evidence of actual delivery. Agency error, provider action or
AI output cannot silently rewrite general entitlement.

**A5 — Tax, money and expenditure.** Only enacted law authorizes taxes, borrowing
limits, currency arrangements, essential entitlements and general expenditure.
Assembly approves a consolidated annual budget and explicit standing essential-
obligations schedule. Treasury keeps separate commitment, verification, payment
and reconciliation controls; temporary provisional payment is allowed only within
a specific law and ceiling. Revenue, debt, contingent liabilities, arrears and
local transfers are disclosed without counting internal transfers twice.

**A6 — Budget lapse.** A preadopted temporary bridge for specified existing operations
expires after its limited term and cannot authorize new policy. Enumerated standing
statutory obligations continue unless lawfully amended; their cash and material
shortfalls remain publicly reported and challengeable. No executive or treasury
action may extend the bridge or cancel an entitlement solely because the annual
budget is late. Assembly must deliberate on a funded successor, with opposition
access and rights review.

**A7 — Independent review and replacement.** Appointments, removal, case access,
funding, order execution and transition records must be structured so the challenged
office lacks effective control of every remedy path. Opposition, lawful media and
civic association can organize alternatives. Valid electoral defeat transfers
office and resources. Missing appointments or an AI outage cannot create a new
acting judge, sovereign AI or renewed mandate by implication.

**A8 — Emergencies and external commitments.** Exceptional powers have a stated
threat, territory, necessity, funding, review and expiry. They cannot alter election
rules, succession, core rights or ordinary tax law. Renewals require the applicable
Assembly vote and independent review. Major treaties, defense commitments and
guarantees require democratic authorization; cabinet negotiates within its mandate.
AI review is advice and never ratification.

**A9 — Protected change and commencement.** A constitutional amendment follows a
legitimately adopted public route that preserves equal political standing, practical
opposition, enforceable remedies and peaceful replacement. The predecessor procedure
governs its own replacement until validly changed. Founding needs an actual affected
public, lawful jurisdiction, clear membership, rights-preserving transition,
opening accounts, staffed institutions and ratification. No research edition,
developer key or project owner may declare commencement alone.

These core provisions allocate authority but do not define every competence,
election rule, fiscal rate, local map or private right. Their subordinate schedules
need exact text, current-source comparison, public participation and legitimate
review. A9 cannot bootstrap its own adoption from the proposed new rule.

## 9. Establishment and preservation of existing lives

The detailed transition and full-country stress tests are in
[founding and failure assessment](FOUNDING-AND-STRESS.md). The key legal rule is that
ordinary rights and obligations continue through a change of institutions unless
a competent new law expressly and lawfully changes them with due process, transition
rules and remedies. Neither a new state, a new AI model nor a new document version
starts society from zero.

There are two different settings: an existing jurisdiction lawfully adopting this
design, or a new constitutional order with a legitimate territorial and membership
settlement. A third setting—this website's research library—has no state authority.
The same proposed institutional architecture can inform the first two, but their
lawful adoption routes, initial electorate, assets and external obligations differ.

International IDEA's [participation paper](https://www.idea.int/publications/catalogue/practical-considerations-public-participation-constitution-building)
supports examining who participates, when and with what influence; it does not
certify this design or identify its founding people. The Venice Commission's
[Rule of Law Checklist](https://www.venice.coe.int/images/SITE%20IMAGES/Publications/Rule_of_Law_Check_List.pdf)
is a comparison for legality, legal certainty, prevention of abuse and effective
justice. Its existence does not fill missing enforcement capacity or give AIcountry
jurisdiction. Sources inspected 29 September 2026; local wording is original.
