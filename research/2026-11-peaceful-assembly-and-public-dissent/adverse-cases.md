# Practical opposition can fail despite rights on paper

Design 189 · Authored challenges, not actual cases, consent or legal judgments

| Situation | Required written response |
|---|---|
| One-hour spontaneous protest before an imminent vote | No notice/permit prerequisite; review before opportunity lost. Six/24-hour maxima are no waiting allowance or late remedy claimed as full restoration |
| No leader or safe named contact | Protect participation, accept minimal anonymous help request, no invented organizer/identity list; actual claim/notice/mandate safeguards remain |
| Fairly notified people decline a restriction hearing | Actual independent merits reconsideration after usable opportunity; nonappearance is neither consent nor veto, sham/inaccessible notice insufficient |
| Police issue road order at0h then rename it security at 4h | Same underlying episode's six-hour unilateral ceiling does not become 10h; seek court immediately, earlier actual grounds/event end still ends it |
| Court refuses/expires restriction, commander repeats it | AX09 cannot extend/replace it on materially same facts. Actual distinct imminent individual rescue retains its own predicate, not recreated crowd ban |
| Provisional report takedown is labeled postpublication | Heightened advance/provisional restraint safeguards apply to continuing/republication before final merits; no timing escape by label |
| Final specific civil remedy after actual merits | Full substantive proof/defenses, proportionate correction alternatives and useful appeal; not a general court political-truth certification |
| Planned blockade of sole clinical access has not started | Concrete clearly imminent serious rights impairment permits proportionate protective court action; do not await actual injury or invent zero-disruption standard |
| Brief delay to commuters or a shop's ordinary losses | No automatic serious sustained rights harm, violence or dispersal; assess real alternatives/people/actual gravity |
| One participant throws objects | Target actual violence where feasible; no collective guilt or automatic whole-event ban; LF necessity/life/device rules remain |
| Countercrowd threatens violence against unpopular participants | Protect threatened people and act against actual violent threat first. Hostility alone is no veto; actual unavoidable immediate danger can require narrow protection, with official-failure remedy |
| Officer escorts protesters but barriers prevent all departure | AX event clock creates no custody/containment power; actual liberty law/P clocks needed, safe exits/help and minimum LF contact remain |
| First booker reserves every square for a month | No permanent political ownership; actual competing use and rights need proportionate sharing/court grounds, not a forced agreement or fake organizer |
| Public land is sold to remove every usable protest forum | Preserve actual public-access rights and practical forums through lawful LA/PT/LU source/transition; no automatic free acquisition or private mall takeover |
| Government calls for private platform to hide critics | Attribute actual coercion/control; AX cannot be evaded by contract threat/secret downranking. Genuine independent moderation keeps its own law |
| Privacy commissioner corrects a private billing record | Actual ordinary nonpublication PD correction remains; no universal publication-hearing gateway. Political/reporting suppression cannot use that label to escape AX |
| Child/intimate safety information in a public-interest report | Specific real legal ground/court, grave direct harm/irreparable intimate protection, least restrictive redaction, actual reply/useful challenge; public-interest label creates no immunity |
| AI replicas flood space/attention | Actual physical risk is not political mandate; copies do not multiply people/consent or make an account the crowd representative |
| No duty judge/facilitation staff available | No planned restriction by silence; actual narrowly grounded urgent protection keeps its own power. Admit unmet duty/source and protect existing rights, not automatic new authority |
| Police/medical/cleaning charge excludes poor participants | Basic protection cannot require a fee/bond/warranty; actual public source needed. Separate proved B damage/optional ordinary service remains distinct |

Clock arithmetic is illustrative: repeated paper/actor changes do not restart the
original episode, and a genuinely ended ground ends power before every maximum.
These are written checks, not an operational scheduling/rights engine. Actual
law, facts, safety, paid receiving and useful-time independent judgment remain
unverified; a complete country cannot be certified by this matrix.
