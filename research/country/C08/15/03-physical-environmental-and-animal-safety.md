# Physical danger, ecosystems and animals: distinct culpable harms

*K17–K21; part of [C08/15](../15-special-crimes-and-bounded-sanctions.md).*

**K17 — Dangerous use or supply of weapons.** Intentionally use a firearm,
explosive or other dangerous means while consciously creating a specific,
substantial unjustifiable risk of death/serious bodily injury: maximum five
years. Knowingly supply such means to facilitate another's specified violent
crime: maximum five, subject to P03's separate proved complicity where
applicable. Ownership, lawful sport/work, an expired permit or a dual-use
article alone is insufficient. Licensing, storage and import restrictions
need separate prospective law; this clause gives no general search power.
Actual injury/death follows the relevant core with no duplicated punishment.

**K18 — Dangerous transport.** Operate or direct a road, water or air vehicle
with conscious disregard of a specific substantial unjustifiable risk of
death/serious injury, creating actual danger to a person: maximum three
years. Recklessly cause serious bodily injury: five. Grossly negligent
breach of a specific published transport-safety duty with actual ability,
causing serious bodily injury: three. Death uses K02. Prove causal fault;
a crash, disability, model alert, technical permit breach or operator status
alone is insufficient. Ordinary traffic restrictions and administrative
penalties require their own schedule and meaningful challenge.

**K19 — Serious environmental harm.** Knowingly release a substance or
destroy a protected habitat contrary to a specific prior published handling/
protection duty, consciously disregarding the substantial risk and actually
causing loss of drinking-water safety or lasting essential habitat function
above a prospectively legislated material threshold: maximum five years.
Without that published threshold and duty, this limb is unavailable; an
agency cannot invent them after harm. Gross negligence is not an unstated
alternative fault. Prove source, causation and scope with contestable original
sampling; a model's regional attribution alone is insufficient. Permits do
not excuse knowing violation of an applicable overriding statutory duty.
Prevention, safe water and restoration retain their own nonpenal grounds.

**K20 — Serious animal cruelty.** Intentionally inflict severe unnecessary
suffering on a sentient animal; or cause such suffering through conscious
disregard of a specific prior care duty with actual ability: maximum three
years. Prior legislation must identify covered animals, material standards
and necessary treatment, husbandry/slaughter, disease-control and research
justifications; prosecutors disprove a properly raised justification. Neither
a cultural label nor an activist's objection supplies the test. A medical
adverse outcome, poverty or unavoidable shortage alone is not guilt.
Inspection, humane receiving and C09 animal-welfare duties persist; seizure
cannot leave animals in worse care or confiscate an entire livelihood by
default. No ecological finding automatically establishes animal suffering.

**K21 — Culpable safety exposure in production and work.** Knowingly supply
a product or direct work in breach of a specific prior statutory safety
duty, consciously disregarding a substantial unjustifiable risk and actually
exposing people to a concrete danger of death/serious injury: maximum five
years. Express gross-negligence liability applies only to an actual serious
injury caused by breach of that specific duty with actual ability: maximum
three. Death retains K02. Knowledge of a defect and the risk, unsafe control
and causal exposure need proof; no hindsight pooling of unrelated workers'
knowledge or automatic liability for every clinician/AI operator.

Across these rows, gross negligence and organizational attribution apply
only where expressly provided. Specific duties/thresholds need elected law
and bounded lawful technical specification; an executive cannot create a
new crime by circular. No illness, diagnosis, help-seeking, vaccination status,
personal drug use or breach of a public-health order alone becomes a crime
under this extension. Independently proved assault, deception, exploitation
or culpable dangerous supply retains its own route. Tax/penal enforcement
does not fund regulators; full standards, capacity and regulatory monetary
penalties remain separate unfinished schedules. Values and thresholds are
unvalidated hypotheses, not evidence of safe national implementation.
