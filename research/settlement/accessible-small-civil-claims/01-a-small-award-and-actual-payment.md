# A refund, failed notice and two payments

A resident pays a 60-unit deposit for an ordinary service that the
business cancels. The business disputes whether it received the
deposit. Use a stipulated personal basket of 100 units, making
the test route limit 200 units. All amounts and the history below
are constructed. Assume jurisdiction, competent staff, a valid
claim and 60 units proven after a fair opportunity to answer.

| Same monetary claim | Ordinary route without repair | Small-claims candidate |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Resident's direct cost through collection | 70 | 5 |
| Principal ordered after proof | 60 | 60 |
| Principal received at first lawful window | 20 | 20 |
| Principal remaining after first window | 40 | 40 |
| Principal actually received by second window | 60 | 60 |
| Total principal received less direct cost | −10 | 55 |

The example assumes no separate expense award. Hold payment and
execution constant to isolate the effect of the direct cost. The
ordinary route can be repaired instead if it achieves the same
effective access with lower public and household burden. Separately
price court time, representation, accommodations and execution.
Private outlay in this table cannot stand in for public expenditure.
If the business cannot make the second payment, 40 stays unpaid
subject to ordinary law. The resident has then received 20 and has
15 after the stipulated costs.

**Failed notice.** A portal marks the notice delivered to an obsolete
address. Hold any adverse merits decision, keep the first receipt
and obtain lawful service with a real response window. If service
later succeeds and the business stays silent, an authorized judge
still needs reliable transaction and payment evidence. A payment
record naming a different business requires resolution of identity
before an award. If a faulty notice is discovered after an order,
use direct judicial review and verify the actual return of any
wrongful collection that the court orders.

**Collection.** Assume an applicable protected balance and 20 units
lawfully reachable after verified prior obligations. The service
can receive 20 under lawful authority and credit it once. A second
remittance of 40 completes payment only when it reaches the resident.
A stay stops compulsory action. A submission labelled paid or a
repeated copy of the same receipt leaves the unpaid balance intact.

**Other stakes.** A possession counterclaim changes suitability,
even if the associated money claim is within the cap. Transfer to a
competent route with the original date and independently considered
urgent protection. If a repeat claimant files separate demands from
the same transaction, test their combined exposure and look for
other affected parties. The facts in one file do not decide another
person's consent, liability or remedy.
