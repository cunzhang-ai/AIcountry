# MS17–MS21. Protect essential energy through a price and supply shock

*Design280 · Proposed extension to [employment, prices and household security](../61-employment-prices-and-household-security.md).*

Select a finite person-side bridge and funded essential supply together
when both affordability and real availability are at risk. Count safe
use by the affected person or service. Preserve cost signals for optional
use. Keep the ordinary duties and authority of each public body.

**MS17 — Diagnose price, power and usable access separately.** When the
grid, thermal protection, household income, water or care owner verifies
a material threat to a protected energy-dependent function before its
last safe time, it notifies the other affected owners and Treasury under
[JC](../65-joint-continuity-and-first-budget.md). Keep the first report
and any earlier individual deadline. Treasury publishes the near-term
scenario and unresolved claims using the owners' verified records and
[DB](../60-democratic-budget-and-execution.md) scrutiny. Separate
unaffordable bills, a physical shortage, blocked settlement and an unsafe
dwelling. Show place, time, cold or heat conditions, usable quantities,
price, currency, transport, import and personnel needs, and alternative
routes. Include residents who lack a direct account or cannot reach a
payment or top-up point. Low observed use can signal deprivation.

**MS18 — Reach the person before the next safe use is lost.** Use enacted
[I](../../C05/06-income-floor-and-safe-resources.md), [EG](../../C09/19-electricity-service-and-grid-access.md)
and [TH](../../C09/23-safe-home-temperatures-and-continuity.md) protection
first. For a material additional bridge the Assembly sets eligible scope,
lawful evidence, maximum gross commitment, source, duration and the date
of a recorded decision on ending or renewal. Use automatic payment where
lawful, reliable evidence establishes eligibility. Fund direct assisted
claims and independently reviewable provisional help where the ordinary
proof would arrive too late. Cover prepaid, shared-meter, intermediary
and off-grid residents on equivalent need terms. Protect safe access
while a material claim is checked. Credit an invoice only where the
resident can use it or the reduction reaches them. Keep accessible cash
or authorized in-kind service where credit cannot work. Reconcile
duplicate aid for the same cost and interval under applicable law.
A lawful provisional payment and later correction follow the actual
award terms and ordinary review. Preserve basic support during disputes.

**MS19 — Hold the protected uses together when power is scarce.** The grid,
fuel and service owners name accepted supply and a paid alternate by
location and useful time. Check essential household use, qualified home
and clinical care, water and any dependent remedy or electoral function
on one calendar, under their respective law. Count shared crews, fuel,
connections, vehicles, review, site access and cash once. Verify the
actual converter, connection and receiver before counting any substitute.
Keep the due protections and safety duties during loss. Existing dispatch
and lawful contingency priorities govern any residual scarcity. Neither a
benefit award, a supplier nor this programme creates a new rationing power.
Where lawful routes cannot meet the protected requirement, identify the
remaining failure and activate existing remedy and budget correction.

**MS20 — Elect and fund the package, with a smaller viable choice.** Compare
ordinary funded repair, essential supply with a targeted bridge, and a
broad temporary tariff intervention with the same receiving protections.
Price who carries remaining costs, the range of usage and wholesale
exposure, payable dates, retained service and independent review. For a
quantity shortage, compare physically usable supply and substitution as
well as money. A budget may pair compatible measures with separate
votes and specify an exact, already-authorized narrower release under
[DB24](../60/07-independent-fiscal-forecast-and-budget-release.md).
Use it only when all its delivery and authority conditions hold. The
Assembly owns unresolved allocation choices. Keep local acceptance,
ordinary dispatch, independent monetary policy and statutory remedies
with their competent actors. No new automatic tax, overdraft, cutoff
authority or recurring promise follows from a temporary bridge.

**MS21 — Prove receipt, restore reserve and return for an elected decision.**
At the dated review, report actual safe household use, water and care
delivery, burden after relief, missed or delayed cases and restoration.
Show gross public and supplier exposure, stock consumed and funded
readiness for another call. Audit against original records and preserve
safe direct reporting and independent useful-time review. AI can check
inconsistent dates and reconcile sources with traceable assumptions.
Human officers authorize payments and dispatch and remain answerable.
End new temporary commitments at the elected boundary unless lawfully
renewed. Carry payments due, pending corrections, service and earned
rights under their own law. Seek a fresh prospective vote if the shock
persists. A bridge's expiry does not extinguish an earned claim.
