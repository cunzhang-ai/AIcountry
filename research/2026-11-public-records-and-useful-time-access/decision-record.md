# Design375 decision on public records and useful-time access

## Baseline and selection

Pin Design374, the [research method](../FRAMEWORK.md), AP, B17–B19,
AA, PE, PD and EV. The current design provides published sources,
personal record correction and specific parliamentary, audit and
protected disclosures. Those powers do not give ordinary people
a complete general request, decision, delivery and remedy route.

[OA01–OA07](../country/C04/03/04-public-records-and-useful-time-access.md)
selects a general right with narrow lawful exceptions, existing
agency officers and direct ordinary courts. The Assembly and local
bodies fund their own scope. Protected court administration allocates
judges and case work. Records officers implement exact release duties.
An independent statutory executor and separate authorized source
custody must make court relief operable. No new central information
guardian, policy veto or automatic postponement is selected.

## Rivals and objections

Routine proactive release costs less per request where records are
regularly sought. It cannot anticipate every disputed record. Ordinary
filing with a generic deadline is cheap and can miss an imminent
public opportunity. A specialist information commissioner could
supply expertise and consistency, and could also add an appeal queue
or become a common political dependency. Price independent review,
paid release and displaced work on the same dates before preferring
a different institutional owner.

Privacy, commercial secrets, fair proceedings and deliberation can
require protected treatment. Record the legal basis, concrete harm,
limits and review, and give releasable portions independently.
Allow narrow relief against demonstrated workload abuse, with
assistance and neutral reasons. Protect anonymous request channels
without making third-party data visible. Ordinary officials and
contractors must retain official source versions without collecting
unrelated private communications. AI can help find originals and
suggest reviewable redactions. It cannot decide disclosure.

## Evidence, migration and next action

[Official data and legal comparators](sources.md) support access,
independent review and distinct timing measures. The
[matched case](../settlement/public-records-and-useful-time-access/01-a-record-before-a-public-hearing.md)
checks early partial release, shared control, insufficient funded work
and late ordinary delivery. It supports a scoped choice. Actual
independent review, authorizations, staffing, participation and better
public outcomes remain unverified.

Preserve prior requests, stronger clocks, protected records, existing
privilege, individual claims and accepted successors. No public
disclosure becomes lawful through a repository update. Use an
authorized dry run with synthetic records, one ordinary request and
one imminent hearing. Verify original retrieval, independent review,
safe release, actual receipt and paid workload by date. Stop with
matched costs and an evidenced missed route where any. Reopen on
systematic delay, unsafe release, third-party control or demonstrably
better review or proactive publication.
