# RT11–RT15: no economic eviction by evasion and no fictional receiver

*Part of [private rent/entry](../18-private-rents-tenure-and-housing-entry.md).*

**RT11 — Exceptional essential costs need a bounded real answer.** An owner can
seek ordinary-court temporary relief for proved unavoidable exceptional net
costs necessary to sustain a safe home that cannot reasonably be met from
lawful receipts after reasonable management. Prove actual cost, existing cover,
non-self-created cause, feasible safer/cheaper alternatives and affected tenant
needs; a lost speculative return, chosen overpayment/refinancing, normal risk
or overdue repair is not hardship by itself. Any cost-only allowance needs
notice/response, exact receiver/source/dates and at most12months, outside the
indexed base. No automatic renewal, duplicated recovery or permanent market
reset. The separate allowance ends at genuine full vacancy/new RT02 letting;
protected continuation/succession keeps only its actual remaining lawful term.
Further necessity needs a fresh ordinary decision, not relabeling dates.

Consider funded assistance, workable finance/operation change, voluntary sale/
lease to an actual capable provider and lawful supported transition. No court
can invent public money or compel an indefinite unpaid private shelter. If
source/receiving fails, H10/H14/H15 preserve tenant unmet claims, proved owner
loss/debt and bounded interim/court/elected scarcity protection. A missing grant
is neither a safe dwelling nor an automatic eviction/market-rent exemption.

**RT12 — Substance governs evasion.** No retaliatory termination, fake owner
occupation, sham holiday/employment label, forced paid bundle, coerced tenant
swap or repair pretext avoids H/RT. H02's actual owner-occupation need/notice/
12-month use and independent possession review remain; do not forbid a proved
lawful ground merely because the rent is regulated. A genuine voluntary
surrender/settlement retains B's consent and actual claim protections, not a
blanket presumption that every buyout is coercion. Concealed linked controllers
and payment recipients are relevant evidence, not automatic guilt. No new
search power, VD offence, punitive fine or landlord-licence scheme is selected.

**RT13 — A usable price challenge.** Landlord and tenant keep the lease, actual
rent/receipts, notices, index calculation and any allowance order; either can
seek direct ordinary housing/civil review, reasons, interim relief and actual
refund/credit of proved excess. Need-based public assistance and independent
counsel/evidence access must be paid; no compulsory mediation or finished expert
dossier. A tenant need not disclose a life graph or waive private testimony.
Lower rent does not refund an unreceived public payment to the landlord twice.
I03/public recovery and the person's corrected assessment need source-period
matching, fair notice and protected needs under existing law.

**RT14 — A dispute is not permission to withhold everything.** Pay the actually
undisputed lawful rent; a court can order proper deposit/escrow/interim amounts
and determine genuinely disputed differences with fair proof and earlier useful
time. A claimant's mere assertion neither cancels due landlord receipts nor
establishes arrears, fraud or possession. Only lawfully due proved differences
can become arrears, with protected correction/payment plans under H14. No
self-help lockout, utility cutoff, unlawful distraint or automated eviction.
Ordinary false statements/crimes need all their own actual prior elements.

**RT15 — Rent correction and a received home are different obligations.** An
RT order does not build a unit, finance a worker or secure an accessible outside
option. Local housing/benefit roles, lawful landlord/receiver and courts retain
separate allocation, I03/repair, money/tenure and execution powers. Actual H
possession/temporary safety duties, LA compensation, PT creditor priority and
paid OP/T successors continue. A safe place, actual payer and accepting repair/
service owner are needed; neither a queue, rent cap nor expected recovery
counts as delivery. Source/stock failure remains visible and reaches actual
lawful elected correction; no new sovereign rent office or scarcity power.
