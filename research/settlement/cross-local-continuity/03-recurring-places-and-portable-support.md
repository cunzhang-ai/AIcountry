# Recurring services across two places and one late change

*Design311 · Constructed common assistance and access comparison.*

A person lives across areas A and B during a regular week. One
operative assistance award covers distinct Monday and Thursday
care episodes. A separate access decision provides an eligible
Thursday journey to an existing public course. Providers have
confirmed capacity and the public owners have funds and accepting
payers. The person uses the same service rights and independent
review in each alternative. The case does not change residence,
tax, voting, eligibility or any education-place decision.

The accepted schedule reserves care in A on Monday for 40 and
in B on Thursday for 50. The B journey costs 20. The actual
ordinary gross bill is 110 if both care visits and the journey
are received. If a change to area C arrives after care staff in
B become entitled to 15 for paid cancellation, keep that claim.
In this fixture the B transport booking can be cancelled free.
C's accepting provider can deliver the care for 65, and a
separately qualified transport operator can reach the course
for 25. None of the mobile capacity is assigned to a concurrent
protected booking. In this fixture, the authorities lawfully
protect the care payment first during shortage. All amounts are
authored planning units.

| Choice or event | Accepted provision | Gross due and remaining gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single-address service with no funded cross-local substitute | A care only on the relevant dates | 40. Thursday care and access remain uncovered. |
| A and B agree one recurring schedule | A and B care and B journey | 110. No extra claim or return journey implied. |
| Late change with available staff, transport and a 35 reserve above the ordinary schedule | A and C care and C journey | 145, including 15 for B cancellation. One Thursday care visit received. |
| Late change with 120 usable and no further source | A and C care; no C journey | 120, including 15 for B cancellation. The missed covered journey remains a separate breach. |
| Cash available but C care worker booked on another protected task | A care and the separately confirmed C journey | 80, including 15 for B cancellation. C care remains uncovered in this fixture. |

In the last row the journey remains independently safe and usable.
Where the uncovered care makes travel unsafe, qualify the route
again and keep the loss of course access visible. The source laws
determine any further costs, protection and remedy. A duplicate B
full-visit invoice after confirmed cancellation does not establish
another service. A missing receipt with disputed status requires
verification and any authorized interim protection, not automatic
cancellation or duplication.

A per-visit agreement can be simpler for a single exceptional trip.
A recurring schedule reduces repeated proof and lets services book
paid standby, with a risk of stale assignments. A single national
service organization is a stronger structural alternative where
compatible staff and sources can be organized nationally. It still
needs actual places, consent, transport and correction. Select the
recurring person-directed schedule for this scope, with existing
local and sector responsibility and an independently financed
fallback. Compare real workloads and frequency before requiring
a formal plan.
