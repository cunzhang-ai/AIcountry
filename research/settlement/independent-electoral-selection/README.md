# Keep electoral selection usable through a constitutional vacancy

*Design288 · Matched constructed comparison with a fixed election date.*

The ordinary formation already stands apart. A simultaneous
constitutional appointment and two election legal-source vacancies
expose the remaining shared dependency. In the example both L seats
and their commissioner reserves are unavailable. Of the other
five commissioners, one is independently recused with no usable
reserve. Four members remain. The scheduled election and its
statutory service clocks run through this period.

| Choice | What happens if every CJ06 lay selector and alternate is unavailable | Added obligations and residuals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shared CJ06, with separate records and independent pay | Six professional votes are possible, but the required lay category is absent. Both legal-source paths are blocked. Four commissioners cannot convene a decision. | Lowest recurring selector count. Independent records and pay can cure an administrative interruption, while an unavailable category still blocks both sources. |
| Separate CJ, OJ and EA formations | CJ06 remains blocked. The electoral formation with six timely votes including all three categories can make a lawful appointment. One timely accession restores five lawful commissioners, subject to an actual four-vote decision. | Thirty principal and thirty alternate places across the three formations if filled, compared with twenty-one of each in the baseline. Also fund standby, hearing cover, records and usable external review. |
| Replace the two election legal seats with separately drawn public-panel seats | A properly staffed, independent panel can also appoint despite the CJ06 category loss. No additional standing legal selectors are required. | Requires extra paid public panels, independently run draws, qualified nominees, information and review. Shifts a majority of commission seats to one selection method. A common recruitment or mediation failure can therefore affect more seats at once. |

In the declared window, the prospective legal-source process opened
180 days before the scheduled term expiry. Notice for a qualified
nominee began 48 days before expiry, the vote took place 30 days
before expiry, and appointment notice was available for 30 days.
An unexpected second vacancy remains open. A funded independent
electoral process can seat one commissioner at the scheduled
expiry, in time for a decision seven days later. If source records,
lawful acceptance or the review route are still missing, the
additional formation earns no timely appointment credit. A valid
refusal leaves the seat vacant. Four votes are still needed for
the actual commission act after five can attend.

Count resource use before selection. On the illustrative staffing
calendar the recurring reserve needs one separate qualified hearing
slot for the challenge and one backfill slot for a released judge.
Funding the selector while using the same slot for both leaves an
ordinary hearing uncovered. Keep case access and potential
certification distinct. If four commissioners can lawfully sit and
the board cannot decide, only EA12's existing career and judicial
routes remain available. If lawful facts, jurisdiction, timely hearing
or execution are missing, record the unresolved result or lost remedy.
