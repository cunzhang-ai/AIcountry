# C13. A debt, medicine and pension payment crisis

*Part of [C13 lived experience](../C13-lived-experience.md); integrated design 55 authored stress, not an observed event.*

Suppose tax cash arrives late, foreign-currency debt service and imported
medicine fall due together, and domestic banks and a public pension fund
hold government bonds. Treasury cannot treat this as one generic deficit.
It must show the exact currency and dates, original creditor claims,
available settlement balances, import contracts, medicine stocks, pension
payments, bank capital and continuing service obligations. The monetary
authority can maintain payments only within its prior law; printing domestic
money neither pays the foreign creditor nor creates a drug shipment.

The Assembly hears a tax/temporary-finance package, delayed discretionary
projects, a creditor extension, and a debt workout. Each exposes who loses
cash or purchasing power, whether lenders actually agree, whether the bank
and pension claims then need separately funded protection, and whether
patients can receive an alternative medicine safely. Treasury cannot pay a
connected lender quietly while reporting a patient's missed treatment as
“covered”. Clinicians handle patient-level safety within enacted health law;
new nationwide scarcity priorities remain an elected choice with rights
review. The claimant's legal right and actual medicine receipt are reported
separately.

If a foreign creditor refuses and reserves run out, a domestic statute does
not change that contract's foreign legal forum. Record arrears, interest,
court exposure, lost trade access and any agreed settlement. Keep due pension
and essential-service claims visible even if cash is missing; the standing
schedule authorizes only what its law states and supplies no medicines by
itself. A lawful workout may reduce debt service yet damage the same banks
needed for wages and payments. The Assembly cannot count gross debt relief
without the recapitalization, pension and import costs. A court may correct
an unlawful preference or denial but cannot negotiate with outsiders or
issue foreign currency. The exercise proves only that the decision chain is
specified, not that the country survives this shock.

[The MQ two-region case](../../settlement/critical-medicines/02-two-regions-and-two-deadlines.md)
separates a matched national stock count, actual local treatment and the next
replacement window. The existing protected pension and source boundaries remain.
