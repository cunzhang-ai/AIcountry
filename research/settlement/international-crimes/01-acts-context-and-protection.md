# Acts and context: names never substitute for criminal proof

*Part of [international-crime journeys](README.md); fictional IC01–IC12.*

| Case | Candidate boundary and continuing protection |
|---|---|
| IC01 A single unlawful detention is called a crime against humanity | Prove G03's widespread/systematic civilian attack, organizational/state policy and knowledge; the label and one event alone do not supply them. K05, liberty review and civil protection retain their own actual grounds. |
| IC02 A reproductive restriction changes population forecasts | G02 needs the specific intent to destroy a protected group and a listed act, not a demographic prediction. Actual coercion, bodily harm or deliberate attack can engage G03/K/civil protection separately. Lawful freely chosen reproductive care is not criminalized. |
| IC03 A political opposition group is persecuted | Absence of a G02 protected-group category does not erase G03 persecution or ordinary/equality protection. Prove actual severe rights deprivation and attack/context, not merely a political dispute. Support/safe opposition continues without a G conviction. |
| IC04 A direct public call to destroy a protected group is compared with a quoted historical passage | G06 requires actual direct public meaning and specific genocidal incitement purpose, with original linguistic/social context and contrary evidence. No subsequent genocide is needed, but quotation/model-generated wording alone proves no intent. No new truth-score tribunal. |
| IC05 A hospital is described as either automatically protected or automatically hostile | W13/W14 asks actual protected status, material awareness and lawful military-objective/precaution facts; neither the sign nor a population profile settles them. Preserve independent clinical/civilian receiving and usable proof before a court. |
| IC06 A cabinet labels riots and peaceful autonomy advocacy an armed conflict | G04 needs real organization/intensity or IAC facts and offense nexus. A label does not create war powers or war-crime context. Ordinary force limits, opposition rights and independent public review remain. |
| IC07 Civilian movement during occupation is described as voluntary | W04/W09/W19 distinguishes actual coercion, occupation, necessary protective movement and applicable safeguards. A paper agreement or emergency label is not free choice; no court verdict is required before safe receiving. |
| IC08 A supply failure and deliberately withheld food both produce hunger | W10/G03 requires the specific intentional deprivation/context/control; P02 examines real capacity and causation. Neither shortage nor budget title alone proves/excuses the act. Food/clinical and continuity remedies remain due regardless of guilt. |
| IC09 A software update changes targeting and a human merely clicks approval | C10/03 and C10/07 require prior material legal/reliability review, usable information and actual control. That failure does not alone prove every G element; an opaque classifier neither licenses harm nor decides guilt. No weapon-building or deployment authority follows. |
| IC10 Adults recruit people aged 14, 16 and 19 | W08 under 15, G08 national 18 service/recruitment and G01 accused-age rules differ. Prove each age/act/fault; ordinary civilian aid is not armed recruitment. Children receive safe disengagement, not adult prosecution or indefinite locked treatment. |
| IC11 A surrendering person is still classified as a predicted future threat | W15 requires actual surrender/hors-de-combat facts; prediction cannot remove protection. Lawful necessary protection and any custody need their own authority; a completed sentence or future-risk score creates no execution power. |
| IC12 The Assembly voted for a campaign later alleged to be aggression | G05 needs a manifest Charter-violating state armed act, actual leadership control, personal participation and fault. A vote does not legalize unlawful force or make every voter/soldier guilty. Self-defense and contrary evidence require independent examination. |

These are proposed legal distinctions, not judgments on any real conflict.
The source/variation note preserves limits and disagreements; no government,
victim, service member, clinician or accused has participated in these cases.
