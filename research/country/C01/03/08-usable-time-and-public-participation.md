# Give people time they can actually use

*Design296 · CS19–CS23 · Proposed cross-government access and evaluation rules.*

**CS19 — Compare time, control and available opportunities separately.**
For a material change in work, care, public access or democratic
participation, show who must do what, when, for how long and with
what notice, help and opportunity to choose. Compare ordinary
and adverse windows. Distinguish paid work, unpaid care,
administration, travel, waiting that ties someone to a place,
rest and time they can direct. Keep incompatible uses separate
even if they overlap in a diary. Equal daily totals can leave
different usable periods. Keep participation optional and any
specific essential or legal deadline intact.

**CS20 — Compare public designs on the same people's feasible routes.**
The competent service owners and elected bodies assess the materially
affected groups, including residents who cannot use the ordinary route.
Compare a competent ordinary arrangement, flexible support with
independent judgments, and a structural rival that moves costs or
duties between institutions. Count staffing, interpretation, paid
relief, accommodation and lost opportunities. A policy that shortens
official handling time but requires more unpaid work needs to disclose
who bears the change. Show time compatibility, money, useful service
and political opportunity separately. A large average improvement
cannot settle loss of a due right or valid challenge.

**CS21 — Repair a material collision that a public procedure creates.**
The existing first receiver preserves the original date and offers
reachable choices under the applicable access, urgency and review
rules. Where two authorities' mandatory steps are incompatible,
each retains its decision and appoints a contact for an accepted
handoff. Prefer a timely simpler route, flexible appointment or
paid support where it works. Under an expressly adopted rule,
a routine adverse finding of noncooperation cannot rest solely on
missing a step for which the authority supplied no usable route.
Apply any existing urgent protective grounds separately. Preserve
appeals, first receipt, prior lawful protection and lawful time
limits. A timing objection creates no entitlement on the merits,
no automatic postponement of a poll and no veto over another person's
right. Explain a genuine inability to offer timely receiving and
retain the independent remedy.

**CS22 — Make public participation workable without one gatekeeper.**
Where law permits multiple formats, keep a funded direct route
and enable accessible contributions and replies before the
competent decision. A paid human facilitator can relay original
views with permission. Preserve the speaker's meaning, material
objections and ability to correct them. Label missing exchange,
context and disagreements. A form or recording with no useful
response path cannot count as effective deliberation. Do not
substitute a message for a secret ballot, a lawful hearing or a
required independent decision. Offer compensated respite or release
where it preserves a better joint route and the worker, recipient,
source and timing actually qualify. AI may help compare schedules
or translate. It cannot interpret silence as consent or act as
the compulsory voice of a resident.

**CS23 — Let elected bodies see the trade and the next failure.**
Use representative voluntary time-use and access evidence,
qualified administrative observations and protected testimony.
Show excluded groups and what could not be measured. No resident
needs to disclose a full calendar to receive a public right.
Publish the actual public cost, the added or removed resident
burden, whether people received services, and the independent
review and ordinary fallback. Compare after a material disruption,
including loss of a shared mediator or a care arrangement. Use
PCO and DP rules for evidence and optional AI. Preserve rights,
paid responsibilities and original dates through adoption and
handover. Reopen on hidden unpaid work, lost reply or due service,
an unusable alternative or a better whole design.
