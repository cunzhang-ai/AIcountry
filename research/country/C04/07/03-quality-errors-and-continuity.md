# AH09–AH12. Advice is useful only when responsibility and receiving are real

*Part of [person-directed assistance](../07-person-directed-public-assistance.md).*

**AH09 — Competent people can use qualified tools.** Existing public/provider
employers recruit/supervise actual competent advisers under C04/WL, with public-
service/access/source/mandate/privacy skills and actual specialty boundaries.
DP supports replaceable tools and authorised data/actions, not a new all-purpose
advice licence or vendor certificate. Human help, accessible plain sources and
other/simpler qualified tools remain usable without an AI account or training
consent. No mandatory national personal-agent product or free unlimited compute.

Source-based explanation identifies relevant current enacted rule/guidance,
version/date, uncertainty and distinction from the helper's interpretation.
Official guidance itself may be wrong or challengeable; do not make one
approved corpus the sole imaginable argument. Optional AI can summarise,
translate, compare supplied documents, prepare draft forms and track chosen
steps within scope. DP/HC require actual review where relied on, source access,
refusal/correction power and paid time; a label or disclaimer cannot replace
competence. An AI answer never becomes final adverse eligibility or a licence.

**AH10 — Reach the actual professional and service.** When the matter requires
reserved legal, clinical, financial, safeguarding or other professional judgment,
arrange accepting qualified receiving with necessary access/cost support under
actual B13/P/sector law. The core helper can stay with the chosen practical
coordination, not substitute its own judgment or force a single provider.
Legal aid/defense, clinical consent, financial licences and service eligibility
keep their own scopes; no blanket full-litigation/private-premium entitlement.
Actual independent conflict handling may require another provider/paid specialist;
shared brand/model/host alone supplies no independence. A directory is not receipt.

**AH11 — Errors and withdrawal preserve actual duties.** Preserve material source/
advice/mandate versions safely, explain a discovered error promptly and arrange
useful correction, actual claim/record repair and accepted receiving. Correct
errors without hiding earlier loss or asking the person to abandon a valid claim.
Actual B09–B12 duty/control/fault/causation/state nondelegable liability and source
remedies apply; contracting to an insolvent tool supplier cannot erase the public
assistance duty. A disclaimer cannot waive it. No automatic unlimited indemnity,
benefit from a false answer or compensation without its actual legal grounds.

Delay/supplier failure, adviser conflict, negligent advice, genuine uncertainty
and a lawful unfavorable decision differ. If a missed special filing needs court/
source-law extension, seek it with actual evidence rather than claim the date
was magically restored. On helper/provider exit, name paid accepted successor,
necessary safe records, urgent tasks and time/source overlap under OP/T. Deleting
a personal agent or changing vendor does not discharge already made obligations.
Public receiver withdrawal has usable source notice and actual paid accepted
replacement/forwarding, with safe records and exact old/new scope. AH06's limited
official holding-out date protection survives a hidden contract/credential expiry;
actual special-source filing correction still needs its competent legal route.

**AH12 — Outside remedy with a real executor.** The person can challenge access,
conflicts, scope/fees, disclosure, poor advice, failed filing or continuity through
actual AP/PD/OJ and relevant professional routes, with competent independent
assistance. The implicated helper/funder/agency is not final judge of its own
conduct. Existing B/E urgent access, courts/counsel/source and private/public
execution retain actual law; AH creates no new tribunal or arrest/search power.

A remedy identifies actual person/body, act/amount/date, lawful payer/executor
and accepting adviser/service receiver. Source shortage or failure of every
alternate remains unmet protection requiring lawful elected response, not a
completed referral or a new sovereign guardian. A person seeking help needs no
population study, signature threshold, general career profile or prior internal
exhaustion to protect an existing urgent right. Qualified real outside receiving,
not additional agent accounts, is the measure of independence.
