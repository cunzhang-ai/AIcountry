# Design359. Fund an alternate sitting, scrutiny and continuing payments

[LCQ01–LCQ06](../../country/C03/05/01-legislative-continuity-and-quorum.md)
requires accessible alternate sites, actual presiders, paid evidence custody,
independent review and the next usable sitting. Reserve these separately from
an electoral trigger and the existing standing service or fiscal bridge.

The [comparison](../legislative-continuity-and-quorum/01-two-sittings-and-the-original-clock.md)
keeps attendance and assent distinct and ends bridge operations at their original
date. Actual funding, participation and delivery remain open.
