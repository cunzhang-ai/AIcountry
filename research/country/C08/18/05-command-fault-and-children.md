# Actual command, defined fault and children as rights holders

*G07–G08; part of [C08/18](../18-international-crimes-and-accountability.md).*

**G07 — Effective control is a fact, not a rank.** A military commander/person
actually acting as one answers for subordinate G crimes caused by failure
to exercise actual control only on proof of effective command/control,
knowledge or circumstances in which they should have known of the impending/
ongoing crimes, actual ability and failure to take necessary reasonable
preventive/repressive measures within their power, and causal contribution.
Identify available information, time, communications, orders and feasible
action. A title, casualty report or later AI reconstruction is insufficient.
This is an express command-duty knowledge rule, not general strict liability.

A civilian superior requires actual effective authority/control, knowledge
or conscious disregard of information clearly indicating the crimes,
activities within that responsibility, actual power/ability and the same
proved culpable causal omission. No chain-of-command diagram alone proves
those elements. Contractors, civilian leaders and AI-mediated controllers
are assessed on facts; neither automation nor delegating to a nominal officer
removes the actual controller's proved duty or excuses another's crime.

Failure after a completed unforeseen crime cannot retroactively cause it.
As a separate prospective duty offense, an actual superior who knowingly
and without lawful excuse fails to take reasonably available steps to
submit credible known G crime information to a competent independent
investigation authority commits a bounded reporting breach. Prove actual
duty, control, knowledge, ability and unjustified omission; dangerous or
impossible transmission is not presumed feasible. It has G11's five-year
ceiling, not automatic guilt for the underlying completed atrocity.
K12 obstruction/retaliation retains its own elements; no doubled punishment.

This causal/reporting separation is an explicit national choice informed
by different primary approaches, not a claim of word-for-word Rome Article 28
implementation. Actual treaty/custom compatibility and command practice
need qualified review. No executive/manufacturer can certify the issue finally.

**G08 — Fault, excuses and age.** Unless a clause expressly differs, require
intention for conduct/consequences or awareness consequences are practically
certain, and knowledge of material circumstances. Context does not replace
personal fault. P04 factual error, necessity, proportionate defense and
actual inability apply; genocide/torture/slavery and deliberate killing of
an uninvolved person are not justified by state interest or superior order.
No defense for an order known or manifestly unlawful; ordinary choice must
be assessed on actual information/pressure, with prosecution's burden.
No compulsory treatment/confinement follows an excuse or diagnosis.

For G conduct the accused must have been 18; below 18 use only independently
applicable ordinary child law, never adult transfer. A recruited child is
not guilty merely for association or victimization. Receiving includes safe
disengagement, identity, health, school/income, chosen support and independent
advice, with no forced confession/reconciliation or indefinite locked care.

The proposed national armed forces/reserves accept service only from 18.
An adult who knowingly recruits/uses 15–17-year-olds for armed service or
active hostilities commits a separate domestic protective offense, maximum
five years, with P fault/defenses and exact conduct proved. W08's under 15
war-crime minimum remains distinct. Training/learning is not a disguised
armed-service exception; no extension to all voluntary civilian assistance.
This broader national rule is not asserted universally criminal everywhere.
