# A private lease needs a price rule as well as protection against eviction

Design186 · [Canonical RT01–RT20](../../country/C09/18-private-rents-tenure-and-housing-entry.md)

The selected private-rent bargain now has three explicit stages:

| Stage | Country choice |
|---|---|
| Genuine new first letting/full lawful turnover | Actual agreed initial rent, transparent charges and B protection; no initial comparables ceiling |
| Continuing tenancy/renewal/lawful succession | Annual signed-CPI ceiling, valid source/90-day notice for rises, no banked unused increases, sham reset or disguised recurring fee |
| Actual improvement/unavoidable essential-cost exception | Limited prior independent ordinary-court permission, net proved cost, finite recovery/term and actual source/receiving |

CPI decreases can lower the ceiling. Temporary repair abatements do not become
permanent lower undamaged bases; permanent cuts/recurring discounts do. Genuine
one-off credits need clear prior evidence. Improvement finance ends with the
actual authorized capital/service boundary; old separate allowances end at a
new market letting instead of riding on top of a newly negotiated base.

H11–H13 keeps public/community audited cost rent; RT does not turn private
acquisition/mortgage/appreciation into an automatic tenant bill. Actual I03
need, safe supply, LU permission, H possession and PT priority remain separate.
The public housing option is not assumed immediately available or funded.

[Decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-private-rents-tenure-and-housing-entry/decision-record.md)
retains market review with real assistance/supply, dwelling-linked controls and
funded public rent insurance as genuine alternatives. Stayers gain more than
entrants; higher initial prices, conversion, withdrawal, maintenance and owner
loss can defeat the preference. No actual effect, source, new stock, authority
or superiority is established by a cap or a successful formula check.
