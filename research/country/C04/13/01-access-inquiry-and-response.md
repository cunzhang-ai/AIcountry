# OM01–OM06. Find the problem, protect the person and obtain a reasoned response

*Part of [independent public Ombuds oversight](../13-public-ombuds-and-systemic-correction.md).*

**OM01 — Open a direct, free route.** The public Ombuds receives reports
about national and local administration, including the delegated public
functions of contractors and joint bodies. Anyone can report a concern,
including through an authorized representative, protected staff channel
or supported oral or offline contact. Neither citizenship, a sponsor,
an account nor a finished application is required. Anonymous reports
can inform inquiry. They do not by themselves establish facts, consent
or authority to obtain a personal remedy. Keep courts' merits, private
conduct outside public duties and separate constitutional, electoral,
clinical and criminal jurisdiction with their competent owners.

**OM02 — Protect the person's useful deadline.** Record first receipt,
give the person a safe confirmation and identify any earlier service,
appeal or evidence deadline. Triage apparent imminent harm on receipt.
With lawful consent or separate prior authority, send only the minimum
needed for the competent duty owner or urgent review to accept the
matter before its deadline. When the first referral fails, escalate
under the applicable sector or AP authority and tell the person how to
reach immediate independent relief. A referral is complete when a
qualified receiver accepts it. A complaint does not pause a court,
service or appeal clock. The office gives usable deadline information,
assisted filing through separately authorized help and a reasoned
routing or inquiry decision within five working days, subject to
earlier urgent duties. Independent review remains directly accessible.

**OM03 — Investigate a credible systemic signal on the office's own
initiative.** Open a dated, bounded inquiry on a reasoned indication
of serious or repeated administrative failure. An individual complaint
or prior court order is unnecessary. Explain jurisdiction, materiality,
source limits, planned work and the inquiry's next decision date.
Investigate both failure to deliver law and unfair practice that law
may still permit. Use protected original evidence and assess people
missing from the agency's account. A report count, repeated AI summary
or public campaign cannot establish prevalence or decide whether an
inquiry proceeds. Coordinate with competent reviewers. Explain a
deferral, refusal or duplicate referral with safe reasons.

**OM04 — Obtain scoped evidence under fair process.** Ask the public
owner and covered operator to preserve and supply necessary originals
within a dated and reasoned scope. The contract must carry this
bounded evidence duty for the delegated public function. Protect
privilege, source confidentiality, safety and personal data. A court
resolves contested preservation and disclosure with protective terms.
No access to unrelated customer data, continuous surveillance,
compelled self-incrimination or entry into a home follows. Give each
materially criticized party a fair chance to answer, ordinarily at
least 20 working days for adverse findings unless existing law gives
more. Publish only safe findings, material reply, limits and reasons.
Keep urgent individual protection available while findings are tested.

**OM05 — Require a response without transferring the merits.** A final
recommendation triggers a lawful duty on the responsible authority to
respond within 20 working days, with earlier existing duties intact.
The response states what it accepts, rejects or disputes with reasons,
and identifies actual authority, named owners, dated source and
receiving evidence for accepted action. It may identify a lawful
alternative or an unresolved resource or policy choice. On a timely
evidenced application the Ombuds may grant one public reasoned
extension of up to 20 working days for the response alone. Courts may
enforce the duties to preserve, disclose or respond within their
jurisdiction on application by the Ombuds or a person with standing.
The recommendation itself grants no money, reverses no decision and
creates no authority to direct a minister, clinician or court.

**OM06 — Track action to actual results.** At each accepted action's
due date check actual implementation and a proportionate sample of
receipt and exclusion. Separate administrative repair, existing due
individual remedies and prospective policy or budget change. A proved
common implementation error goes to the competent duty owner or
reviewer under LX and ordinary law. Keep collective claims, direct
urgent relief and SR court repair available on their own grounds.
Publish a safe account of accepted, rejected, delivered, late and
unverified action. A press release, new queue label or partial sample
cannot close unresolved cases. If capacity or money fails, expose
the unmet duty and identify the competent lawful escalation.

A complaint may also concern direct loss after a missed legal deadline.
[SR28–SR32](../../C08/30/07-remedy-after-lost-service-time.md) provides
the separate proposed route for an independent award. Ombuds assistance
can refer the person and preserve the original chronology.
