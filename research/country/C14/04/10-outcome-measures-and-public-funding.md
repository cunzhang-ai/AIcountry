# PCO36–PCO40. Protect public funding from self-reported success

*Design542 · Proposed public finance and evaluation duties under [public conditions](../04-public-conditions.md).*

**PCO36 — State what the measure can change before adopting it.**
When a material indicator can automatically increase payment, reduce
resources, change a provider's mandate or release an expansion, the
adopting instrument identifies the exact consequence and controlling
authority. Distinguish a public signal, a contractual payment predicate
and evidence for an elected renewal. Record what is claimed, the reference
population, group definitions, original event and useful time, eligible
source, reporting authority, independent evidence and the party that
benefits from reaching the threshold. Record how the measure could change
admission, classification, effort, records or treatment of difficult cases.
Ordinary due pay and payment for verified authorized work continue on their
own sources. A measure of case throughput alone may pay for specified
verified work where lawful. It cannot qualify a claim of better lives.

**PCO37 — Start with the assigned duty and test both routes.**
For the first mandate, fund indispensable service through its protected
ordinary source, with qualified staff and independent remedy. Prefer
ordinary service funding with independent comparison and repair over
automatic reliance on an outcome incentive. Consider a limited incentive
only with exact prior law or contract, bounded exposure, independently
verifiable benefit and stated protection against omission or harm. Compare
ordinary funding, a genuinely different method and the incentive on the
same people, duties, resources and next call under PCO22–PCO26.
Test the funded roster, cash and actual access when no optional reward
comes due, including a stalled verification and the next operating call.
If standing service depends on the expected reward, provide a qualified
ordinary source or change the prospective instrument.
For a covered programme test how an authority could improve its reported
score while excluding harder cases, moving costs or narrowing its work.
Budget proportionate independent checks on original referrals, actual
receipt, need groups and an appropriate outcome horizon. Specify one
separately reviewable protection for a material deterioration among
worse-served people. A protected right follows its own law.

**PCO38 — Put independent evidence at the consequential decision.**
Before an optional reward, expansion or reduction that depends on a
measure, the independent reviewer checks material definitions, missing
cases, early closures and original receipt. Have a protected route
for affected people and staff to challenge the record without the
operator's assent. Preserve missing or disputed observations on the
original denominator and qualify certainty. Label inferred or imputed
results separately. Several tools using the same event record provide
one underlying observation, and a closure code establishes only its
verified meaning. Model estimates can guide verification and comparison.
They do not satisfy a release condition that requires observed receipt.
Oversight uses the existing
audit and evidence roles. Apply the law or contract's stated fallback
where the required proof is missing or materially contested. A pause
or correction reaches only the dependent authorization and only under
competent law. Preserve enforceable payments already earned, ordinary
service finance, individual rights and direct remedy. Give an affected
provider notice, original evidence and timely independent review.

**PCO39 — Reopen the actual policy and payment uses.**
When credible evidence shows that control of a measure changes who is
seen, served or funded, identify each consequential use under EV and
reach the responsible authority before the next material act. Reconcile
the original and revised series at their true scope. Separate lawful
payment recovery, individual restoration, aggregate comparison and
the elected continuation decision. The elected body uses the original
review date, sees a qualified ordinary repair and an exact changed
instrument within existing budget rules, and gives reasons about
remaining tradeoffs. Pay due support and corrective work from existing
appropriations or a separate competent vote. A statistical disagreement
outside a legal release condition remains evidence for public judgment.

**PCO40 — Verify through the next call and limit AI's role.**
Show full funding cost, administrative burden, observed service,
material effects outside intake and actual remedies after a correction.
AI may reproduce scoped counts, expose sensitivity and surface possible
omissions. Independently verify signals against protected originals.
Keep a direct human route and an inspectable ordinary calculation.
An assisting system cannot declare actual delivery, determine eligibility,
change a denominator or trigger payment on its own. Apply prospectively
with lawful treatment of prior contracts, awards and rights. Reopen on
persistent suppression, shared control of sources, recurrent unverified
reward, damaged care or an equally effective simpler rule.
