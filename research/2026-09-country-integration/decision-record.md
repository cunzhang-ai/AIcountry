# Integrating the project into a country design

28 September 2026 · country-integration-1 · Working design, not legal adoption

## Request and decision

The user identified the central failure: material was poorly organized, and repeated
local research had displaced the project's purpose of designing a future country.
This is a substantive correction to work selection as well as navigation. The response
creates one [country design](../COUNTRY-DESIGN.md) and one [status register](../DESIGN-STATUS.md),
places them first in the project README, and clarifies their role in the common method
and contributor instructions. The country design remains in English.

The assistant authored this integration. There was no delegation, independent review,
affected-party consultation, public vote or appointment. The user's direction authorizes
project design and organization, not enactment for a real population or changes to the
platform's publication permissions. Existing software, hosted material and canonical
manuscripts are not modified.

## Baseline and observed problem

The [manifest](source-manifest.json) pins the local uncommitted inputs and prior dated
research. The working method remains consolidation-3-democratic-continuity, with the
existing decision template; snapshots preserve the README, framework and contributor
instructions before this integration. Newly completed resource-access work is preserved
as a supporting study. Its proposed next standalone review is deprioritized explicitly,
not silently erased or described as completed.

The main README led with platform workflow. Subsequent work accumulated democratic,
legal and emergency-service candidates without one substantive integrated entry point.
The older manuscript still assigns lawmaking and important fiscal/treaty decisions to
AI while the democratic candidate assigns them to the public and elected institutions.
Emergency durations and budget-bridge assumptions also differ. These are textual
findings about the local artifacts, not claims about an operating state's behavior.

## Alternative responses and selected package

| Option | What it changes | Reason for selection or rejection |
|---|---|---|
| Keep following local study questions | More detailed specifications | Does not correct the user's identified loss of the national design; reject as the default next action |
| Add an index only | Makes the same documents easier to find | Useful but leaves incompatible authority and disconnected social systems; insufficient alone |
| Consolidate a substantive reference country and expose unresolved choices | One coherent political/social/economic design, with supporting studies and a national work sequence | Selected for the working design, with known limits |
| Rewrite the canonical legal edition and deployment immediately | Would make the platform appear aligned | Current request is framework design; lawful adoption, complete dependency review and operational authorization are absent |

The selected package is a parliamentary constitutional democracy, decentralized within
common rights, with a plural mixed economy, universal essential services, an income
floor and revocable AI assistance. These are explicit design choices synthesized from
prior proposals, not newly demonstrated optimal institutions. Currency planning retains
the no-assumed-issuance baseline. Unconditional cash, ownership choices by sector and
founding territorial/membership arrangements remain open.

New synthesis connects job change, care, learning, housing, health and political voice
to the same budget and capacity. It does not claim those links have been costed or
shown to work. Universal essential provision and democratic standing are normative
commitments; fiscal identities are accounting constraints; beneficial effects remain
hypotheses. No claim that the model outperforms every country is made.

## Material objections and limits

- A master document may conceal disagreement or impose one ideology. The status
  register preserves substantive competitors and identifies selected choices as
  revisable; current democracy and rights requirements remain explicit.
- A clear reference design can look more complete than it is. The main document
  discloses missing population, fiscal settlement, capacity, code coverage and founding
  authority. It does not infer readiness from chapter coverage or file checks.
- National integration can flatten local institutions and unfamiliar cooperation.
  C04/C12 retain local discretion, informal life, alternative media and practical
  routes around dominant mediators, within common obligations and remedies.
- Expanding the state may concentrate power despite rights language. C03/C06/C11
  connect appointments, appropriations, information, enforcement and provider control.
  Existing independence and court-vacancy failures remain unresolved; no new guardian
  is assumed to solve them.
- Universal promises may outstrip supply and be used to ration secretly. The next
  work item requires one shared fiscal/material scenario and visible shortfalls.
  Monetary flexibility, automation gains and a second use of the same worker cannot
  be invented to balance it.
- Further integration could still become an endless writing exercise. Completion
  conditions now attach to national packages and conflict resolution. Evidence work
  must change or qualify an identified design choice, and no-change remains valid.

These objections were retained by the author, not independently resolved. Participation
by actual affected people and qualified legal/economic review remain absent. No sensitive
testimony was collected, and no claim of beneficiary consent is made.

## Dependencies and compatibility

The main proposal maps C01–C14 to all sixteen subject areas and the relevant earlier
studies. Supporting artifacts retain their original status and limits. The current
source manuscript is the September 16 local snapshot; there was no fresh verification
of the live legal edition. External references within prior work were not treated as
new effectiveness evidence or comprehensively re-reviewed for this synthesis.

The status register names known authority conflicts in the constitution, budget,
taxation, currency choice, treaty authorization and emergency rules. It also carries
forward stronger proposed criminal evidence safeguards and the emergency resource-
access distinction. It does not silently transfer prior approvals to this combined
package. Full legal dependency coverage and review must precede any real adoption.

Existing claims, public contracts, debts, employment, care, pending proceedings and
privacy obligations survive any eventual institutional transition under explicit
lawful rules. A new master document grants no right to cancel them. The research's
organizational priority changes now; country procedure and current application
permissions do not. AGENTS instructions add the user's integrated-design preference
without replacing the existing method or constitutional holds.

## Next action

Develop the national social and fiscal settlement specified in the status register.
Use one declared reference scenario and disclose its invented inputs until empirical
calibration exists. Compare coherent service/income/tax packages over a shared multiyear
resource boundary; identify distribution, shortfalls, transition and democratic decision
responsibility. Integrate the selected or unresolved result into the main design.
This takes priority over the preceding local study's next research suggestion.

The present output is bounded to the country design, status register, integration
record and necessary entry-point changes. Staff/compute cost was unmeasured; there
is no continuing monitoring, fieldwork budget or appointed implementation team.
Reopen the synthesis when a material conflict, infeasible resource commitment, excluded
group, failed remedy or a superior democratic alternative changes a country choice.
