# Design375 commitment. Deliver public records by the time they can be used

[OA01–OA07](../../country/C04/03/04-public-records-and-useful-time-access.md)
requires dated intake, actual source custody, independent retrieval
and review, qualified redaction and usable receipt. Finance duty
work, alternatives and actual enforcement under competent budgets.
Preserve open claims, protected originals and first dates during
custodian, media or government change.

The [worked hearing comparison](../public-records-and-useful-time-access/01-a-record-before-a-public-hearing.md)
distinguishes a lawful response window from a usable civic opportunity.
It supplies no authority or actual staffing.
