# Equal persons, real agreement and bounded agents

*B01–B04; part of [C08 civil core](../13-civil-obligations-and-executed-remedies.md).*

**B01 — Persons and supported decisions.** Every human has equal legal
personality; adults retain legal capacity. Disability, diagnosis, age or a
care address does not remove it. Make information and chosen support usable,
protect against coercion and permit direct independent challenge. Supporters
act within limited revocable mandates and disclose conflicts; they do not
silently acquire property or guardianship. [B21–B23](../16/01-age-decisions-and-support.md)
now specifies candidate majority, young-person acts and narrow asset preservation;
criminal age, clinical choice and voting remain distinct. A legal person has identifiable responsible
organs and assets under law; registration is evidence, not blanket immunity.
Future nonhuman personhood is not settled by assigning today's AI output to
its responsible deployer; any revision needs explicit rights and migration.

**B02 — Agreement across media.** A contract needs voluntary communicated
assent to sufficiently determinate lawful obligations. Speech, conduct,
drawing, simulation or future media can express assent; only specifically
enacted form requirements limit this. Preserve operative meaning, source,
material qualifications and known translation loss without requiring all
private relationships to enter a central ledger. A signature proves only
what its authentication method actually establishes. It does not prove
understanding, authority or noncoercion. Silence, passive platform use or an
agent's agreement cannot supply a reserved personal waiver. Material terms
must be available in a usable form before assent; routine agreements need no
mandatory comprehension examination or continuous human reapproval when
validly delegated. Courts resolve disputed meaning from actual context and
source evidence; a mediator's preferred summary is not definitive.

**B03 — Authority, reliance and revocation.** Identify the principal, scope,
time, resource ceiling, reserved decisions and allowed subdelegation. All
replicas share that ceiling; they cannot each spend it or multiply consent.
Within actual authority, an automated act can bind without a human reviewing
every transaction. Outside it, private apparent authority exists only where
the principal's own attributable representation justifies the other party's
reasonable good-faith reliance; an agent's assertion/credential alone cannot
create it. Prove that representation, reliance and relevant notice. Public
bodies cannot gain tax, spending, force or rights-waiver powers by apparent
authority. A private principal may expressly ratify a lawful disputed act
with usable knowledge and voluntary assent; retained benefits/restitution and
separate negligent misrepresentation need their own findings, not automatic
ratification. Unlawful or nonwaivable terms cannot be cured this way.

Revocation takes effect against the agent on receipt through an agreed
reachable lawful channel; against a relying party when actually received
or when that party knows authority ended. The principal cannot hide notice
in an unusable vendor channel and call it received. Clearly communicated
expiry/limits also bind a party with notice. Offline mandates require a
specified expiry and nonoverlapping resource reservation or other declared
prior allocation. Reserved high-consequence acts need their current assent/
competent authority. Platform notice failure, duplicated spending and innocent
reliance require fair allocation of actual duties/losses, not automatic debt
for the person with least bargaining power. A valid pre-revocation obligation
survives; deletion or the last synchronized message cannot erase it.

**B04 — Nonwaivable terms and usable exit.** Private terms cannot exclude
liability for deliberate harm, erase basic work/consumer/safety/civil equality
rights, waive political standing or authorize public coercion. Disclose total
price, material risks, renewal, cancellation, sponsorship and actual responsible
supplier before agreement. Hidden recurring charges, deceptive design and
fabricated performance claims are not assent. Waiving protected rights is
unenforceable; ordinary lawful allocation of a negotiated commercial risk
remains possible with actual disclosure and agency. Dependent workers,
tenants and consumers cannot be forced before a dispute into exclusive
private/foreign arbitration that removes protected court access. After a
dispute, voluntary advised settlement/arbitration can resolve lawful private
claims with meaningful refusal and independent enforceability review.
Clinical consent, public status and statutory duties retain separate grounds.

[MP21–MP26](../../C05/11/10-usable-switching-and-continuity.md) supplies
a scoped sector duty for actual transfer and separate billing correction.
Preserve valid mandates, ordinary cancellation and accrued claims.
