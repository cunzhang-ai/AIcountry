# Design512 decision record

**Basis and selection.** Use the [Design511 baseline](baseline.json) and
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md). Select independent long-horizon
challenge in existing funded evidence and elected institutions, backed
by a separate material and rights account through the last credible
exposure. Attach the original renewal windows and remaining options to
a new commitment before relying on its early price. The
[canonical choice](../country/C09/04/02-long-horizon-capacity-and-public-choice.md)
reuses ecological thresholds, phased release, investment renewal and
ordinary independent review. It leaves accrued claims and dates intact.

**Real alternatives and objections.** Compare sponsor appraisal with
existing independent financial, ecological and service challenge, and
a dedicated future generations office with review and response authority.
A dedicated office could concentrate attention on neglected horizons.
A single guardian or a model could also impose an unaccountable view of
future preferences and crowd out present people. Prefer existing roles
where they can jointly produce timely, independently funded challenge
without losing their other protected work. Respond to the loss of a later choice even when no new permit is sought. Secure the independent observations before changing any essential measurement method. Test fixed costs and real receiving when successful service transitions shrink the legacy customer base. Reopen if actual suppression
or persistent capacity loss defeats this design. A more expensive stage
can defer useful present protection. Require current affordability and
test a narrower or different path. Do not manufacture a reserve or
commitment by using a favorable valuation rate.

**Evidence and handoff.** The [sources](sources.md) distinguish legal and
appraisal comparators from authored sensitivity. Existing commitments and
safety and judicial roles retain their scope. No real successor, assets,
budget, lawfulness or affected consent has been verified. Parallel internal
analysis does not supply public assent or authority. Preserve the option
to revise methods or reject the project within lawful rights and limits.

**Next decision.** Use actual independently observed asset condition,
paying sources and service and ecological limits for one adopting
portfolio. Put one linked critical renewal and a different feasible option
to the competent elected forum with useful dates and actual receivers.
Reopen on earlier deterioration, a binding rights issue, a loss of shared
staff or a better funded and independently operable option.
