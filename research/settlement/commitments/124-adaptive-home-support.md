# Commitment extension 124. Adaptive home support

Design211 specifies [HS01–HS08](../../country/C07/25-adaptive-home-support.md).
The existing public care guarantee has a protected individual floor,
person-led day-to-day changes, a public determination before material
reduction, actual paid interim receiving and a recurring source test.
Clinical authority, consent, paid work and independent challenge remain
separate. Existing assistance choices continue.

verified improvement.
