# An integrated first government programme

This comparison puts selected proposals from the national design into one
common budget. It distinguishes the continuing country, a narrower motion
and a faster reform rival. A common date, currency and qualified work pool
apply to each alternative.

- [Shared sources and exact choices](01-shared-sources-and-votes.md)
  shows gross advances, owned money, capacity, effective votes and conflicts.
- [Country scope and remaining choices](03-country-scope-and-remaining-choices.md)
  states the first-programme disposition across all fourteen chapters.
- [Canonical programme selection](../../country/C14/06/05-integrated-first-government-programme.md)
  explains the order and continuing rights.
- [Historical evidence and decision](../../2026-11-integrated-first-government-programme/decision-record.md)
  distinguishes historical evidence, designed choices and open qualifications.

Run `python3 research/settlement/integrated-first-programme/verify-programme.py`
to reproduce the declared branches. The numbers are constructed and the
coverage ledger is not a national cost estimate.

The [Copenhagen housing check](../affordable-housing-and-refunds/README.md)
qualifies the real-world start. The numerical case still assumes
accepted affordable units and timely gross funding.

The [care placement check](../care-training-and-mentor-cover/README.md)
qualifies P's actual staffing and timing without reusing the budget
from the separate placement exercise.

The [Copenhagen energy check](../energy-claims-and-useful-time/README.md) qualifies
E's gross local source, indirect accounts and accepted household
heat without transferring the separate case's budget.

The [public intake check](../public-intake-without-a-digital-gate/README.md) retains
the hold on accelerated A. It separately tests funded ordinary repair
and keeps the resident's first receipt and handoff verifiable.

The [first release register](04-first-release-register.md) joins these
checks on the same budget after a declared shared-system loss. The
original case remains a separate comparison.

The [protected correction comparison](05-protected-corrections-and-options.md)
charges separate due repair on the same calendar and compares the remaining
exact options before and after a legal commitment.

The [concurrent-loss comparison](06-concurrent-losses-and-binding-duties.md)
tests two additional due costs on that same protected calendar. It
prices an already binding tenancy and its qualified substitute before
recomputing optional starts.

The [Copenhagen care and water benchmark](07-copenhagen-care-and-water-budget.md)
matches the same 2025 accounting year across the municipality and
its water company, then traces the distinct 2026 decisions. It
records which proposed starts remain open on the observed evidence.

The [Copenhagen reserve comparison](08-copenhagen-emergency-reserve.md)
follows the subsequent approved regional release, distinct older
people's funding and the proposed water deadline. It tests the
additional protection required when restoration takes longer.

The [majority preference comparison](09-majority-preference-and-budget-choice.md)
puts preparation, housing and repair-only options to the same members
under two ordering rules. Each exact budget still requires a binding vote.

The [conditional housing and care comparison](10-conditional-housing-and-fallback.md)
reserves a real alternative vote through a separate local decision and
distinguishes failed authority from obligations already incurred.

The [national expansion comparison](11-expansion-and-origin-capacity.md)
tests whether a later care start can keep the first service and the
workforce origin operating. It uses a separate illustrative budget.

[Recovery after reopening](../first-programme-through-recovery/01-restoration-and-new-demand.md)
extends the operating comparison to accumulated duties, new demand,
paid review and the first cycle after temporary cover ends.

[First-programme timing](12-first-programme-before-outcomes-are-known.md)
compares one early decision across water and care losses, with a separate
constructed budget and a paid single-call reserve.

The [reference opening motions](13-reference-opening-motions.md) select
ordinary repair on the Denmark–Copenhagen calendar and identify the
evidence required for a separate funded stage or faster integration.

The [two-year budget and decision](../opening-budget-and-public-choice/01-two-year-budget-and-decision.md)
tests separately authorized care and water alternatives on a compatible
constructed budget through renewal.
