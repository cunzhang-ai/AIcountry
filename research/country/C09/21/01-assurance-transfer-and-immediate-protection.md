# CLN01–CLN04. Prepare closure before exposure and protect people through failure

*Design221 · Part of [industrial closure](../21-industrial-cleanup-and-insolvency.md).*

**CLN01 — Schedule the sites, duties and applicable actors.** Apply this
candidate to industrial discharge, hazardous-waste treatment, mining
and comparable consequential facilities expressly scheduled in prior law.
Set published substance, scale, containment and receptor criteria using
actual geography and cumulative risk. Keep minor ordinary activity under
its own law. The competent environmental authority identifies the actual
operator, permit, site boundaries, off-site exposure, existing contamination,
water and waste receivers, and closure and monitoring duties. Preserve
collective and customary rights and actual local and downstream voice.
Different physical sites, phases or affiliates cannot conceal a common
source. Distinct owners do not become liable by association. TS/TD governs
technical permissions and lawful restrictions. No certificate authorizes
otherwise prohibited exposure.

**CLN02 — Secure work and cash by the time each is needed.** Before new
or materially expanded exposure, require independent assessment of the
cost for a competent third party to protect, contain, clean up and monitor
the covered site. Use a credible abrupt-failure path as well as orderly
closure. Price emergency and later work, cash and currency, actual
disposal and receiving capacity, residual risk and post-closure duration.
Identify enforceable dedicated funds, guarantees or funded public
arrangements, with protected purpose, draw terms, limits, provider risk
and dates. No self-bond based solely on the operator or affiliate's
future solvency. Do not double pledge property or count a disputed
insurance payment, future sale or inaccessible surety amount as
spendable cash. Test correlated provider and industry failure. Review
at least annually and after material exposure, source, cost or control
change. Require adequate added cover before additional exposure. Limit
or repair an uncovered existing activity through its actual law and
safe receiving route.

**CLN03 — A transfer requires an accepted and secured successor.** The
permit authority tests the proposed operator, asset and record scope,
actual source, paid staffing and work to date. State which duties
remain with earlier actors and which successor expressly assumes.
A licence transfer or asset sale cannot move profitable work and
leave required care without a funded owner. Require actual acceptance,
continuing security and valid assignment before releasing old assurance.
A change in site use or land title alone creates neither approval nor
blanket inherited personal liability. Preserve existing liable parties
and claims under their own terms. Courts decide disputed title,
avoidance, attribution and contract terms. Publish scoped reasons
and allow affected people and counterparties to challenge capture,
underfunding or arbitrary exclusion.

**CLN04 — Keep urgent protection separate from the liability dispute.**
Under WS, HP, RC, TS and existing civil law, named local and sector
officers arrange safe water, hazard notice, interim containment,
health access and other due protection through paid, accepting
providers. Do this in useful time while attribution, insurance or
insolvency is pending. Use prior lawful powers and protect private
addresses and testimony. Support residents and workers with usable
information, original evidence, independent testing where warranted
and direct remedies. Land access, displacement, clinical consent
and individual compensation retain their own grounds. The licensed
site owner, environmental custodian and household-facing service
each keeps its actual duty and source. A sample, a cash transfer
or an order alone is no proof of safe water or received help.
