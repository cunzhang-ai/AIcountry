# Design308 decision on exact competing budgets

## Baseline and decision

Pin Design307, BCV01–BCV10, DB04, BLC and the same first-programme
scope. Keep the existing research method. Select a prospective
ordering rule within the budget design. The proposer is the project
research assistant. Enactment belongs to a legitimate Assembly and
the competent constitutional process where required.

## Diagnosis and alternatives

In the authored example the cabinet could accept full opposition access
and still benefit from sequence. Approval signals answer which budgets a
member can accept. They need not reveal which budget most members prefer
to each other exact option. Separately ask comparative preference,
retain binding enactment, and retain the existing order when no text
wins every comparison by a full-seat majority. The current approval
rule is simpler. Direct enactment from the comparative result would
save a vote but could convert willingness to prefer into consent.
A negotiated consensus is valuable and retains ordinary opportunities
before exact voting. Compulsory consensus gives a holdout more power.

## Selection and objections

Adopt BCV11–BCV15 as the research selection. The recovered text loses
unconditional first priority if an alternative defeats it and every
other qualified text by a full-seat majority. It still obtains access
and first fallback. This is a real reduction in the priority benefit.
A qualified proposal excluded from the first budget still needs a
usable ordinary reply. Parties can withhold rankings or shape the
docket. A cycle can return the choice to the former order. Keep those
limitations visible and budget the independent tabulation and remedy.
No actual affected participation or independent qualified review
occurred in this comparison.

## Continuing duties and next question

A vote completed under prior procedure stays subject to that procedure
and its existing remedies. Use the proposed rule only after competent
prospective enactment and with a paid Clerk, alternate presider,
independent custody, inclusive ballot and challenge time. Give any
pending eligible text fresh scrutiny and a fresh ordering ballot under
applicable transition rules. Keep accrued claims, standing service,
cash restrictions and elected offices on their prior authority.
The older vote checker continues to reproduce the predecessor, while
the new checker addresses this change in priority. Reopen on verified
strategic omission, lack of useful access, or a paid rehearsal showing
that correction cannot finish before the vote. Ask whether the
existing scrutiny staffing can run all eleven alternatives, including
a non-digital challenge, within the same calendar. No staffing rate,
schedule or pilot authority is assumed. The bounded exercise here
establishes vote consequences. Law, operational readiness and lived
effects remain open.
