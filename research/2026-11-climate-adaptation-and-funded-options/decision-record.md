# Design399 decision. Make climate adaptation choices while they are still feasible

## Baseline and choice

[Design398](predecessor-design-398/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md) governs climate
budgets, ecological limits, public investment, service continuity
and disaster recovery. Adaptation still needs an explicit account of
when a viable option disappears, the cost of keeping it available,
and the operator and source for protection while a transition occurs.

Select [ADP01–ADP08](../country/C09/08/01-climate-adaptation-and-funded-options.md)
under the [working research method](../FRAMEWORK.md). Use place-specific
paths, early evidence, a latest useful decision derived from actual lead
times, paid reversible preparation and a timely elected switch. Account
for interim and long-term service separately. Keep ownership, consent,
common legal duties and independently protected ecological limits intact.

## Alternatives, objections and authority

Retain ordinary repair and decide only after the hazard is clearer.
This preserves immediate cash but can lose land, supplier capacity or
decision time. Commit now to a single permanent solution. This can
be justified where long lead times and high irreversible harm leave
little room to wait, but can overbuild or shift exposure on weak
evidence. Choose staged protection and paid options where a meaningful
choice can be kept open. Compare early construction on the same
hazard ranges. Compare demand and exposure reduction, not only larger
structures. A plan can choose early protective action if no safe
delay qualifies.

Options cost money and can restrict future land use. Pay for and bound
their actual scope, listen to affected and host residents and retain
ordinary review. Do not select a durable project merely because
preparation was paid for. Disclose unequal exposure and missing
evidence, including renters, people unable to travel and ecological
effects beyond the project. Elected bodies choose funding and
material changes. Qualified independent reviewers verify the evidence
and rights through their own sources. A sensor or AI model cannot
activate spending, compulsory acquisition or relocation.

## Evidence, migration and next action

[Primary records](sources.md) distinguish long decision lead times,
periodic monitoring, future options and estimated funding shortfalls.
The [constructed comparison](../settlement/climate-adaptation-and-funded-options/01-one-clinic-and-two-flood-dates.md)
tests a faster branch in which a regular review loses a viable path.
It does not establish actual hazards, funding, authority, participation
or outcomes for AIcountry.

Adopt prospectively with dated funding, named local and national owners,
qualified independent assessment, affected participation and continuing
service routes. Preserve ordinary planning, acquisition, tenancy, water,
ecological and emergency law. Carry prior works, debt, protected spending,
liability and appeals on their own dates. Do not hide maintenance or
eventual replacement outside a chosen budget horizon.

Next trace one real essential service through two plausible exposure
dates and two qualified pathways. Reconcile actual approvals, land,
construction, staff and settlement lead times with resident and
worker evidence and the next budget. Reopen on earlier exposure,
cost or land changes, repeated missed receiving, shifted harm or a
more protective feasible path.
