# Design203 decision. Carry material evidence correction into actual public decisions

9 November 2026 · Research selection from Design202

## Choice, evidence and values

EV13 preserves vintages and applicable legal rules. WB09 directs verified
corrections to their competent owners. Design202 leaves the consequential work
between delivery of a warning and an executed decision largely implicit.
Select EV17–EV24 to name who checks material reliance, which powers apply,
how people reach review and what counts as delivery. The Assembly still
controls new fiscal authority. Courts retain their lawful jurisdiction.

The England school formula case shows a common data dependency can invalidate

## Alternatives, power and delivery

Publishing a correction and repairing ordinary appeals is cheaper when users
already know their exposure and can act. It leaves omitted people to discover a
problem even when an office can find them. Preserving every forecast protects
reliance at the cost of paying nominal differences regardless of actual loss or
moving the loss to other users. Prefer a duty to identify material use,
individual and service protection under existing law, and an elected option
for a separately financed bridge. A qualified claimant can still dispute the
body's account. Illustrative units 100, 110 and 103 separate the programme
envelope, blanket preservation and the targeted bridge.
[Matched cases](../settlement/evidence-corrections/01-allocations-and-indexation.md)

An executive can suppress a warning, classify a recurring loss as immaterial
or leave a plan open until a claim becomes useless. A service, vendor and
reviewer can depend on the same staff, contract or upstream data. Use EV release
independence, named programme ownership, equal opposition access, ordinary
Assembly budget scrutiny, direct claims and existing outside audit and courts.
Use separate protected custody where review needs the material. Existing EV,
career administration, Assembly and judicial appointment, removal and funding
rules continue. These routes need actual independence and paid capacity.
A record or automated notice creates no new decision-maker. Hearing directly
from affected pupils, families, schools, recipients and frontline staff remains
necessary before claiming an evaluated result.

The strongest objection is that a comprehensive duty could delay remedies,
overstate precision and create a record useful for surveillance. Cover material
uses with programme-level traces where possible, retain restricted data at
its lawful source and test actual omissions and burden. Reuse original
observations only for the named circumstances. No transferred review or
affected-party assent is claimed. Design203 is research with AI assistance.
No actual independent reviewer, ratifying public, funded operation or
delivered outcome was established.

## Continuing duties and bounded next decision

Keep EV01–EV16, WB, DP, PD, SR, DB, FB, MF and relevant sector, election and
court law. A prospective law would name scope, owners, earlier cases, funded
successors, independent review and exit from any temporary bridge. Existing
law, restrictions on sensitive material and accrued claims continue while a
correction is disputed. A deadline triggers review without implying permission,
an adverse finding or the end of existing rights.

Test one funding formula and one recurring payment under ordinary release and
appeal versus this duty. Include a shared source failure and a missed outside
receiver. Require an actual warning time, independently traced users including
omitted groups, named paid roles, appeals and receipt before the next useful
time. The budget and setting remain unknown. If comparable records or capacity
are missing, record unresolved reach and harms. Retain simpler repair when it
achieves equal timely coverage with less burden. Reopen on privacy breaches,
strategic delays, source costs or failure to reach previously omitted people.
