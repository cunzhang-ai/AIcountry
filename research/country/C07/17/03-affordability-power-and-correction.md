# MO09–MO12: affordability, public control and remedies

*Part of proposed C07/17; design 94 research candidate.*

**MO09 — A usable free floor.** Covered MO02 necessary journeys and MO03's two
return journeys carry no point-of-use fare, booking fee, deposit or access-
assistance charge. National equalization funds the enacted scope and relevant
high-need/place costs; poorer localities cannot make inability to pay a denial.
This new scope is a real public cost, including higher-income users, financed
through an express elected package rather than assumed fare savings. Additional
ordinary travel and private vehicle use may retain published lawful fares/prices
and I rules for actual necessary expenses. No unlimited taxi entitlement follows.

**MO10 — Match costs without pricing a person as income.** Treat the protected
mobility service as a public service, not privately cashable income. Its use
does not create a per-trip tax or automatic reduction of U, Y basic, personal
cash discretion or another person's entitlement. This expressly retains ordinary
personal cash alongside the new service. Reconcile a specific actual paid work/
access expense once: an actually delivered trip cannot also generate a fictional
fare reimbursement for that trip. Preserve necessary uncovered costs, actual
support transfers and I03 housing protection. Choosing a private car need not
entitle its whole purchase/finance cost to public reimbursement; decisions need
actual necessity, alternatives, reasons and independent correction. A cash-only
mobility budget cannot discharge a journey that cannot actually be bought.

**MO11 — Existing accountable institutions.** Local mobility authorities own
ordinary delivery and handoffs; sector owners retain their own duties. Elected
councils choose routes/contracts within national rights and interlocal competence.
National equalization/stewards join cross-boundary funding and publish safe
access/capacity accounts. Treasury pays actual authority, not eligibility by
budget veto. Procurement, appointments, beneficial control, worker voice and
independent transport inspection follow C04/TS; public operators face the same
review. A political office, public landlord or vendor cannot condition journeys
on support, supply an opposition travel list or determine a clinical/court case.
Collect minimum booking information; ordinary outings need destination/routing
information, not an intimate purpose dossier. Protect location, correction and
manual access without requiring indefinite public journey histories.

**MO12 — Correction before the trip becomes useless.** First receiver provides
safe reasons, a responsible officer, timely alternative and direct independent
administrative/civil review. Do not require serial internal appeals or mediation
before B17 urgent courts or a sector's earlier route. A missed booking is not
delivery. Preserve the opportunity or an equivalent without displacing an
innocent rider unlawfully. For provider-caused MO03 loss, offer a suitable makeup
within14 days if useful and wanted, additional to the next week's minimum
unless the person freely chooses another valid remedy; that date is not expiry of the original
breach, loss claim or remedy. It is not an unlimited bank of voluntary unused trips.
Wrongful refusal, private liability, actual compensation and public continuity
have their separate grounds and payers. Inspect actual vehicle/driver/assistance
and arrival; an order or refund alone has not supplied the missed journey.

Where all lawful alternatives fail, retain the individual's claim, record the
displaced receiving duty and use CP/NP/E19 within their actual scope. No new
general diversion, private requisition, unsafe driving, election delay or forced
remote participation arises. Courts correct lawful duties and withholding; they
cannot manufacture vehicles, drivers, cash or a new national allocation.

Design115 [I17](../../C05/06/06-received-services-time-and-replacement.md)
retains MO10: a separate protected mobility duty is no automatic ordinary-basket
credit. A retail ticket is not all journeys or the network's full public cost;
only applicable source law and actually avoided matching expense permit a
specific reconciliation. Preserve necessary uncovered travel and private cash.

[MO17–MO22](05-joined-journeys-and-service-access.md) names the funded journey owner and receiving service in a failed protected connection. Earlier deadlines, MO09–MO12 prices and independent review continue.

[MO23–MO29](06-integrated-fares-and-operator-settlement.md) adds common transfer pricing and
equivalent cash access for covered paid journeys. MO09 remains free at use.
