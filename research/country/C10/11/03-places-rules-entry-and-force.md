# HP13–HP18. Physical hazards, public settings and distinct enforcement

*Design147 · [Ordinary protection](../11-ordinary-health-and-hazard-protection.md) · No emergency prerogative, permanent land plan or treatment power.*

**HP13 — Immediate physical danger and specific evacuation.** A named qualified local
civilian incident lead may order departure/exclusion from a precisely bounded place
on credible original facts of imminent death/serious injury from fire, structural
failure, flood, toxic release or comparably concrete physical hazard, only where
warning/safe voluntary exit cannot adequately protect in useful time. Explain scope,
actual exits/accessible transport, need, safe receiving and remedy. This express
limited nonclinical evacuation may require departure from the active dangerous
place despite refusal; it grants no medical examination/treatment, closed placement
or control of later private choices. That paternalism/safety tradeoff is retained.
It is not a mental-health/self-harm intervention by another name; those keep their
actual independent law. Any actual confinement invokes HP07's shorter liberty clock.

Reach independent meaningful ordinary-court review within72continuous hours under
HP14, earlier if useful protection/actual liberty requires. A surprise immediate
rescue cannot wait for a fully built replacement, but the lead owes immediate safe
transport/first receiving and continuing HP19 protection. Foreseeable planned
removal must have actual usable receiving before action. Neither delay nor a model
creates an unsafe camp, forced family care or new permanent land/title allocation.

**HP14 — Identified unsafe places and closure.** A competent HP01 officer for grave
transmission, or the existing qualified sector officer/incident lead for HP13 danger,
may order the minimum specific temporary closure/exclusion/capacity correction with
reasons, actual hazard/remedy, people affected, support and executor. Without prior
hearing it lasts at most **72continuous hours**, no officer renewal, and must reach
an independent meaningful hearing/operative order no later than expiry. This is an express shorter
HP source: V04/B17's possible further72-hour extension cannot extend it. A housing/
liberty measure needing earlier review keeps that earlier protection.

The judge may continue an established necessary specific-place measure for at most
28elapsed days per fresh order, with source reassessment at least weekly and on
material change, affected reply and paid access/receiving. End earlier when corrected.
This can persist while a real unsafe structure remains: no public-use takeover or
permanent land plan follows. An expired HP order does not make an independently
unfit plant safe; actual distinct technical-safety duties retain their own source,
not an expired order disguised as routine operation. Broad effects invoke HP16/RX.

**HP15 — Entry is not a bodily test or a fishing search.** Ordinary nonconsensual health/
hazard inspection entry needs an express person/place/purpose-specific ordinary
civil warrant, actual evidence/necessity, scope/method, source protection and a
single visit within48elapsed hours; further entry needs fresh actual authority.
Licensed-workplace inspection within V03 remains its own source. There is no HP
warrantless home inspection, private-message/biological-data search or surveillance.
A separate immediate rescue entry by authorized civilian responders is permitted
only where concrete imminent death/serious injury cannot await a warrant; record
minimum action, necessity and actual time, with independent review within24elapsed
hours and earlier useful relief. It cannot become post-rescue evidence gathering,
asset requisition, compelled labor, forced treatment or continuing occupation.

**HP16 — General measures need elected law.** This Act enables the accountable national
health minister, on public HP02 evidence and a lawful source/support account, to
propose bounded ventilation/protective-face-covering/capacity rules in specified
indoor shared public/work settings, or temporary closure of identified nonessential
high-risk gathering categories. Medical/disability/child access and safe effective
alternatives must be provided. Normal DR material-rule consultation, final notice,
actual affirmative approval and making apply. A genuinely necessary prior-Act DR13
urgent rule has the earlier actual-source/day7 expiry, not HP14's specific-place
clock, constitutional14days or a ministerial renewal. Local general rules use their
actual LR/national competence; a local official cannot assume general ordinance power.

No blanket population home-confinement, geographic cordon functioning as detention,
forced vaccination/test passport for essential rights or power to erase opposition,
courts, elections or food/clinical access is created. New classes require fresh
primary law within RX, not a catch-all “other necessary measure”. RX08 ordinary
90-calendar-day segment/weekly reassessment/fresh-primary-law continuation applies
to materially broad movement/assembly/participation restrictions. An individual
order cannot fake a national rule, nor a general label erase actual liberty.

**HP17 — Physical enforcement is express and narrow.** The Act authorizes sworn civilian
police, on the competent public officer's documented urgent direction or actual
court order above, to use only necessary minimum contact/escort and safe entry
within the specific authorized HP task, after usable warning/opportunity where
feasible. Qualified lawfully designated public civilian rescue responders may
perform only minimum hands-on HP13 extraction from active physical danger to the
first safe point, under LF; not infection confinement or onward closed transport.
Clinicians, labs, private guards and AI gain no arrest/force power by
referral or contract. B18 civil executors may secure a court-closed site only in
their actual authorized property scope, no bodily detention. LF's second necessity/
proportionality and life thresholds bind every act; a health order supplies no
weapon, coercive interview, chemical restraint, sedation or diagnosis-based force.
No force may overcome a medically valid refusal under cover of sample collection.

**HP18 — No punishment by status or clock manipulation.** This Act creates no new
status/noncompliance crime, punitive health fine or civil-contempt imprisonment.
Civil restriction/enforcement needs its own actual grounds. Independently proved
assault, culpable harm, corruption or other actual offense uses P/K and source-law
fault/defenses, never test refusal, poverty or suspected infection as guilt. P09's
original criminal24-hour hearing, remand and defense remain distinct; health/
criminal labels cannot reset the same actual restraint or defeat either applicable
review. Protected protest/journalism is not an epidemiological offense. Workers
may report unsafe practice without forfeiting pay, care or ordinary legal standing.
