# Design337 decision on recurring school staffing loss

## Baseline and decision

Pin Design336, ED01–ED38, WF85–WF89, public budget release and the
working framework. Equal educational entitlement requires actual qualified
teaching in the learner's school or a usable equivalent. Individual remedies
can protect a learner, while repeated losses across a cohort require a public
response by an identifiable office. Select a school-level correction trigger
with elected funding, voluntary staffing arrangements and review of the
receiving and originating schools.

## Alternatives and open objections

A school-led staffing supplement preserves local discretion and can retain
experienced teachers quickly. If the additional appointments all come from
nearby schools, it can leave the same total unmet need. A national placement
service could pool more capacity, with risks to staff autonomy, local
language and professional continuity. Select local ownership with national
need-adjusted financing and voluntary shared appointments where origin
coverage is real. A school may use a simpler retention package or local
recruitment when it can restore the offer in time.

The school-day thresholds and first-term review are design choices. They
could produce excess paperwork at schools where staff already have little
time. Pay administration time, preserve immediate individual remedies and
test the effect of excluding suppressed or shortened sessions from the
denominator. Remote teaching, training and pay supplements carry different
costs and uncertainties. Their merit depends on the binding local bottleneck.
The [primary comparisons](sources.md) support testing staffing categories
and geographic spillovers.

## Authority, migration and reopening

Local education directors hold the delivery duty. Elected bodies control
lawful appropriations and an outside inspector can inspect, hear challenges
and invoke established remedies. Maintain stronger prior deadlines,
appointments, pay, individual awards and access during migration. The project
assistant authored the selection. No actual learners, staff, councils or
lawmakers have made this decision.

Before any broader claim, compare a full term of paid provision in two
connected localities with different language and travel needs. Seek safe
direct evidence from pupils and workers in both, including those missed by
normal reporting, and an independent costing and review. Reopen if losses
move between schools, competent staff leave, the grant fails, learners lose
continuity, or an alternative provides equal access with less burden.
