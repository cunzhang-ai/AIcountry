# C11. Public digital operation that cannot become the government

*DP01–DP103 · [C11 knowledge/AI](../C11-knowledge-culture-and-ai.md) · Proposed research law.*

Retain useful plural task-mandated AI and sector authority. **Concretize the
existing C04 shared digital-support function and C10 technical coordinator,
with actual responsible appointments, admission/reliance, stop/restore duties
and independent receiving.** Neither a new AI sovereign nor a national command
platform is selected. A working application is useful only if its human/legal/
physical service and challenge can continue through relevant failures.

No national master credential, mandatory AI account/model, universal life log,
central truth service, automatic new tax/benefit rule, cross-sector kill switch
or novel cyber punishment. Support sharing never transfers an institution's
case, publication, budget or court power. The selected arrangements can still
fail through cost, common physical dependence, captured protectors or absent
independent workers. Diversity is relevant only to the actual failure/control.

[The finite country review](../../settlement/country-review-150/README.md) locates
this positive gap within14 chapters and12 whole-goal requirements. It retains
adequate current choices rather than declaring every topic missing. The largest
remaining feasibility gap is the current whole-country source/capacity account,
not another automatic digital subquestion. Actual country/source/adoption,
qualified independence, useful service gains and better lives remain unverified.

[WF's conditional frontier](../C06/58-country-source-and-capacity-frontier.md)
now follows through that research priority. It treats net task gains as unproved,
retains actual paid review/fallback/peak costs and selects no DP-funded saving.
The next source comparison qualifies income/tax/retirement and service increments.

[DF civilian succession](../C10/12/03-voluntary-service-rights-and-handover.md)
retains actual sector command/record/pay/credential duties without a national
master key or vendor/Chief election veto. Bounded technical support and actual
lawful accepted receivers remain; an AI order/copy supplies no military mandate.

[GA upstream safety](10-general-ai-capability-and-public-control.md) is a distinct
proposed civilian technical-safety remit. The DP Director gains no private-AI
licensing, GA decision or universal shutdown power. Public model controllers
meet the same GA floor; sector restoration and existing due claims remain.

[HT practical handover](../C03/19-lawful-handover-and-actual-control.md)
separates expired access, newly issued scoped authority, tested recovery
and actual service. The DP support role receives no master credential
or authority to recognize a government.

[Design220](../../settlement/public-ai-exit/README.md) tests exit without supplier
cooperation. A lawful independent service core preserves first receipt, payment
and human review even when the optional supplier can no longer operate.

[LX public rules](12-public-rules-and-verifiable-implementation.md)
adds independently derived legal tests, the case trace and affected-case
correction. A safe exit path still needs the correct law and proof.

[CRD scoped credentials](13-scoped-credentials-and-public-access.md)
specifies shared-issuer suspension and original proof under continued
sector authority. Keep DP reporting, restoration and service duties.

[HC05–HC10](05/01-ai-and-succession-of-public-competence.md) conditions
material workforce or learning-capacity reductions on a usable
successor path. Use DP for the task-specific gain and ordinary reliance
decision, and carry paid mentor and review capacity into its actual cost.

[ASI01–ASI08](09/16-public-scoring-and-purpose-limits.md) applies purpose limits to
cross-office labels and checks separate lawful evidence and simultaneous relief.
