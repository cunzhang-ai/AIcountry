# From a custodial sentence back to ordinary life

Design 190 gives effect to the halfway review already selected under P19.
The court schedules review, the state proves any need to continue custody,
and the corrections service executes the lawful date. Local services and
victim protection have named duties before and after that date.
[Canonical provisions](../../country/C08/28-sentence-review-and-release.md)

| Situation | Proposed result |
|---|---|
| State cannot establish the continued-custody test at halfway | Release with the least justified restriction |
| The court misses the decision deadline | The sentence loses its authority to hold at halfway unless a valid continuation order exists |
| A limited contact condition sufficiently protects an identified person | Conditional release with a specific end and direct review |
| A missed appointment reflects transport or care problems | Help or adjustment through the existing receiving route |
| New material facts establish serious risk during conditional release | Independent hearing and tightly bounded judicial recall where lesser measures fail |
| The recall window expires while the person is back in custody | Release under this sentence at the same endpoint, with independently applicable protection assessed separately |
| Shelter, treatment or paperwork falls through | Execute release and pursue the responsible public receiver |

The difficult choice is the endpoint for the original sentence after release.
Capping recall, including recalled custody, prevents a long suspended prison
term from following the person. It shifts responsibility for later harm to
ordinary protection and new lawful cases. Actual public safety, equitable
decisions and paid care remain open questions. [Decision and cases](../../2026-11-sentence-review-and-release/decision-record.md)
