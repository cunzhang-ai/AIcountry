# A small civil claim people can bring, answer and collect

12 November 2026 · Integrated design 430 · Candidate procedure

Use the existing independent civil court to resolve straightforward
low-value monetary claims. The design limits the cost and form of the
proceeding while keeping the ordinary standards for evidence, reasons
and review. The legislature must enact jurisdiction and fund access.
The court allocates and decides cases. The statutory execution service
acts within the order and the ordinary protected balance.

**SCL01 — Suitability and transfer.** For the first comparison, use
two monthly published ordinary adult personal baskets under
[I02](../../C05/06/01-coverage-needs-and-housing.md), before housing
and in-kind offsets, at the original receipt date. This proposed
amount measures a route limit, with no change to substantive remedies.
Count related monetary relief from the same transaction together
without counting alternative measures of the same loss twice. A
linked counterclaim over the limit moves with the claim unless a
judge finds severance fair and safe against inconsistent findings.
Neither party can force the route by splitting a demand. An
independent judge considers disputed identity, evidence needs,
dependence, complexity and related cases. Possession, status,
liberty, bodily care and fundamental-rights issues receive the
competent ordinary or special route, even at low value. Urgent
protection under B17 can run while allocation is decided. A transfer
retains the original receipt date, relevant usable evidence,
protective orders and pending review. Explain and allow challenge
to allocation before imposing new steps or financial exposure.

**SCL02 — Staffed access for either party.** Accept an ordinary
account in person, by phone or through an accessible remote channel
with a receipt and a named clerk. Explain what is disputed, what
can be ordered and how to answer or ask for help. Fund interpretation,
accommodations and evidence assistance for either side. Provide
conflict-free advice and representation where stakes, complexity
or dependence require them, without requiring a lawyer to file or
answer. A hearing remains available on request or when necessary for
fairness. Use a decision on written submissions only with informed
agreement and a judge's finding of fairness. Offer voluntary settlement
with safe refusal and independent advice where needed. AI can help
transcribe, translate or organize, with a human correction route and
access to the source. It has no power to certify service, infer assent,
admit disputed evidence, enter judgment or trigger enforcement.

**SCL03 — Proof after a real chance to answer.** The judge verifies
usable service of the claim, supporting material and response route,
with sufficient time to respond. An alternative form of service
requires a reasoned judicial finding under ordinary service law.
A delivery flag or stale contact record alone is insufficient.
Pause an adverse decision on failed service or inaccessible
participation, fund a further lawful attempt and retain the filing
date and access to urgent relief. Give both parties an accessible
status and a judicial review date for continued delay. Where lawful service and a real
opportunity are established, a judge can decide in absence on
sufficient evidence of identity, liability and amount under B14.
Silence supplies none of those facts. Give accessible reasons and
notice of review. An independent judicial route promptly hears an
objection to failed notice or a material inability to participate.
Provide interim suspension where needed, a rehearing when fair
participation was denied, and reviewable restitution of wrongful
collections through the actual receiver.

**SCL04 — Predictable cost for meaningful participation.** Set
published modest fees and an accessible waiver before collection
when fees would block access. The court may award scheduled,
reasonable direct expenses with notice and reasons. Each side
ordinarily bears its elective lawyer costs. A narrow exception
requires prior warning, a hearing and a judicial finding of abuse
and necessary incremental cost. Losing or declining settlement
does not establish abuse. A transfer applies any new cost
exposure prospectively after notice and funded assistance where
required. Price clerks, judges, interpreters, advice, review and
execution through ordinary court and legal-aid appropriations.
Evaluate total household cost, including time, care, travel and
collection, on both sides. Fee receipts, seizure proceeds and
case-closure bonuses may not fund or reward the decision-maker.

**SCL05 — Execution and actual receipt.** A monetary order states
the parties, amount, credit for prior payment, due date and review
route. After the lawful voluntary window the claimant can request
a staffed handoff with the existing record. The competent service
verifies the remaining mature balance, stay status, insolvency
priority, third-party rights and the protected balance under
[B18](05-orders-execution-and-collective-claims.md) and
[DCR01–DCR06](07-combined-debt-recovery-and-essential-income.md).
Only ordinary lawful means are available. Instalments require
authority and an ability-to-pay finding before compulsory use.
Credit each confirmed transfer once and show what is due, paid,
unpaid or stayed. An open balance can continue subject to ordinary
insolvency and limitations. The court can correct or stay the
execution and verify any ordered return. Where a common practice
harms many people, offer the
[representative route](06-representative-claims-and-delivered-redress.md)
while preserving applicable individual deadlines, relief and choice.

**SCL06 — A testable route with a receiving fallback.** Compare with
funded ordinary-court simplification and voluntary settlement on the
same rights, caseload and total budget. Give the legislature and
independent reviewer privacy-protected totals from first contact
through corrected collection, with estimates of missed intake
reported separately. Check household time and expense, contested
service, transfers, repeat filers, unpaid awards and displacement
of urgent and ordinary cases. Examine disparities by access needs
and resources where lawful and safe. If added intake lacks paid
staff or shifts delay to another route, provide an accepted ordinary
receiver with original dates, urgent protection and no second filing
fee. Restrict expansion and reconsider the threshold or route.
Preserve open cases, lawful review, payments and protected records
through a later change in procedure or technology.
