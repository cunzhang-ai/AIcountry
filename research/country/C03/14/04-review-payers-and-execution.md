# LR16–LR20: a lawful rule needs an independent way to correct it

*Part of [C03/14](../14-local-law-and-public-control.md); design112 candidate.*

**LR16 — Council records do not belong to the chair's case office.** Assign a
Council Clerk/local rule registrar through C04 open merit/equivalent competence,
qualified public shortlist and reasoned full-n appointment. Career tenure/pay,
source custody, conflicts and discipline use proved job-related grounds, usable
reply and direct ordinary courts, not named-case removal by chair or issuer.
The council has a separate readable clerk/records/counsel appropriation and actual
career payer; this grants no office tax/debt/self-appropriation. Preassigned
qualified career substitutes retain exact existing duties on conflict/vacancy.
Share services lawfully if useful, with actual employer/time/funds and external
conflict rotation; one provider/name is not proof of independence.

Registrar and implementing authority maintain separately usable originals of
source/text/input versions, L, calls, actual votes, making, notice C, effect/
expiry and corrections, with paid free paper/offline access. Council workers,
affected residents and ordinary courts have necessary protected evidence access;
no public private-testimony/health/political-choice graph. Unknown future media
must disclose operative meaning/loss and preserve challenge rather than disappear
from the record. No minister/provider controls the only copy and its appeal.

**LR17 — Direct courts, including pre-enforcement review.** Affected people,
workers/providers, authorized collective claimants, neighboring councils and
members with blocked scrutiny rights may approach unconflicted ordinary courts
without pointless internal exhaustion. Credible imminent application can challenge
source/scope/cumulative effects, mandatory process/version, vote/making, notice/
expiry and rights. Individual B16/15 notice/period/tolling and B14 protected
answerable disclosure apply; broad publication does not consume an unknown injury
claim. Urgent E13/B17 consideration≤24h/earlier useful time, aid/interpretation,
third-party notice and bounded ex parte hearing rules remain. Filing alone does
not stay law. Constitutional issues retain CJ's scope, no new municipal court.

**LR18 — Stop the wrong act, do not appoint a policy.** Court may restrain/
annul an unlawful rule/effect, require exact lawful consideration/publication,
correct records, restore a proved owed service/payment or order source-law
refund/loss with version/class/stop time and residual duties. It cannot set a tax,
benefit, technical coefficient, missing appropriation, national/local boundary,
substantive council vote or a preferred legal policy. Full-n approval cures no
ultra vires rights violation. A harmful but valid policy needs elected change;
rights/fault/actual loss remain independent cases, not automatic policy insurance.
A good-faith benefit/permit/worker recipient does not automatically repay or lose
all past lawful acts because an official used a defective norm; competent source
forums protect ongoing rights, reliance and third parties without continuing
unlawful coercion. No unpublished/expired norm supports punishment or denial.

**LR19 — Local payer, external bounded civil execution.** Each local source
names its accountable C04 implementing and records-correction officers; they
send material correction to known receivers within24h/earlier useful time and
verify actual reconsideration/restoration, not just message receipt. Local ordinary
Treasury's protected career judgment payer, assigned by C04 local finance careers,
pays finally determined municipal refund/compensation within30working days or
earlier applicable right, with actual local lawful source and grounds/attribution/
fault/causation. No automatic national indemnity, fine funding or municipal raid
on client/pension/insurance/foreign property. National authority/own wrongs use
their own actual payer. Missing municipal money remains an arrear and C04/C06
lawful national/local financing/receiving duty, not canceled entitlement.

The adopting Act expressly extends B19 bounded continuing appropriation and
ER/B18 professional civil-enforcement execution to determined LR record/correction/
notice/verified-payment acts, with separately protected credentials, pay and
preassigned external court-service conflict rotation. Ordinary duty court directs
the exact proved withheld duty; chair/issuer/payer cannot choose its own executor
or erase credentials. No substitute legislates, searches, taxes, borrows, grants
new eligibility or commands a foreign actor. Independently proved refusal/ability/
fault has its own fair process; an empty account is not automatic personal guilt.
If the substitute also fails, record actual nonperformance and continuing claim,
not an invented superior office, returned democracy or a successful paper remedy.

**LR20 — Supervision respects local authority and common dependence.** National
rights/legality/courts and C04's exact prior-law essential-service fallback remain.
No national policy-merits clearance, new inspector veto or automatic municipal
takeover follows from LR delay, a report, model warning or funding dispute.
Review distinguishes unlawful refusal, absent resources, policy disagreement and
missing authority, with actual successors/duties. Shared land/water/services and
external people receive C04's competent scope/participation route; affected input
is not unilateral sovereignty, and data convergence does not settle the resource
conflict. Preserve stable lawful informal cooperation and practical alternatives;
more committees, registries or faster redesign are not evidence of better lives.
