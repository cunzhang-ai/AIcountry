# WF90–WF94. Make the first budget payable on its actual dates

*Design274 · Proposed national financing and operating rules.*

**WF90 — Publish three reconcilable views.** Treasury submits a
continuing-law budget and each exact proposed alternative with
a fiscal flow account, a gross financing schedule and an owned
payment account. Keep recurring sources, accrual, one-time uses,
repayments, borrowing proceeds, restricted cash and the operating
balance visible separately. Report each authority, payer, currency,
applicable restriction and actual settlement date. Reconcile views
on stated perimeters and conventions. Preserve unknown opening
balances, liabilities, data revisions and residual differences.
Independent analysts disclose a material discrepancy with its
effect on the choice. A favourable aggregate cannot fill an
unreconciled cell.

**WF91 — Bring maturities and operating duties into the same dated
choice.** Carry principal and interest due, funded replacement or
safe closeout, binding payroll and contracts, protected resident
services, electoral work and independent remedy execution. Add
the local and foreign receiving routes and the first year after
the policy change. Trace each loan, guarantee or transfer to its
actual legal authority and record gross outflow, repayments
and residual risk. An on-loan remains a cash use even where
it creates an asset. Do not treat a municipal or pension account
as Treasury liquidity, or count the same tax receipt on several
dates. A covered amount must reach the correct payer and recipient
in usable time. Keep qualified staff, independent access and
actual service receipt as separate checks.

**WF92 — Choose the minimum balance and contingency in advance.**
In each material currency and payer account, qualify a funded
liquidity minimum against the timing of due work, settlement
failure, known common exposures and later calls. Include carrying
costs and which optional work is displaced. The Assembly
authorizes any binding draw, prefinancing, maturity change or
lawful fallback under DB and [FP](../56-national-fiscal-settlement-and-source-boundaries.md).
Specify release conditions, permitted purpose, replenishment,
expiry, price and repayable tail. Show remaining capacity on each
date after drawdown. An expected auction, quoted facility,
short-term refinancing or one-time balance cannot count as
recurring income. Never relax an applicable statutory or
contractual obligation through the financing schedule.

**WF93 — Return a material failure to a useful-time public choice.**
Compare ordinary financing, delayed refinancing or revenue,
reduced yield and failure of a payment channel under the same
due dates. A later receipt may settle a bridge without curing
earlier missed work. If a prospectively authorized start needs
funds or capacity that fail, follow the exact lawful option or
return a narrowed proposal to the Assembly. Preserve all due
rights, accrued pay, independent remedy and democratic duties
through the existing lawful routes. State which original tasks
remain exposed, the actual competent officer and the next
dated decision. No fallback creates a general Treasury power
to pick who loses a lawful claim or to move an election date.

**WF94 — Verify the fiscal claim and the real delivery separately.**
Treasury records projected and actual flows. The Assembly's
independent analysts and audit bodies retain their own access,
source history and reasons. Publish safe aggregate comparisons
and protect sensitive operational and personal evidence through
independent lawful custody. A separate payer or service owner
verifies actual payment, staffing and receipt within its remit.
AI may reconcile figures and detect duplicated finance, a
missing date or a forecast dependency. It cannot reclassify
restricted cash, raise debt authority, issue a payment or
certify real service. Reopen on material source conflict,
refinancing failure, loss of usable capacity or a better whole
programme. Apply [T13–T17](../../C14/06/04-linked-choices-and-lawful-start.md)
to the resulting dates and effective parts.
