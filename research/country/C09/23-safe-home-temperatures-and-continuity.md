# C09. Safe home temperatures and continuity

*Design225 core and Design287 household protection · TH01–TH17 · Proposed housing, energy and protection duties.*

**Protect residents from harmful indoor heat and cold.** The building,
equipment, energy bill, grid and accessible fallback each affect whether
someone can stay safe. Assign their duties separately and require a
named public officer to arrange interim protection even when the
cause or final payer is disputed. Use safe building improvements where
they can reduce exposure and peak energy demand. Keep affordable
heating and cooling and an accessible alternative for the conditions
that repairs cannot cover.

1. [A usable standard and a remedy](23/01-standard-and-ordinary-repair.md)
   sets the outcome, ordinary responsibilities, evidence and urgent relief.
2. [Housing improvement and affordable thermal service](23/02-retrofit-energy-and-source.md)
   joins building performance, protected bills and paid renewal.
3. [Extreme events and continuing public duties](23/03-events-and-continuity.md)
   specifies activation, capacity limits, independent review and handback.
4. [Household protection and usable temporary receiving](23/04-household-protection-and-usable-receiving.md)
   joins same-place services, authorized funds and safe return.
