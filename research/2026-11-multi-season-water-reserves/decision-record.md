# Design406 decision. Carry usable water into the next protected season

## Baseline and public purpose

This AI-assisted selection uses [Design405](predecessor-design-405/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md),
the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and the [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).
WS04–WS09 already accounts for connected sources and allocates a compatible
seasonal envelope. WS21–WS25 joins water and electricity in one dry window.
The missing operating choice is how to limit optional releases now when
their lawful effects would use up the next window's essential receiving.
Affected parties include households with and without network connections,
farms and seasonal workers, care providers, ecological and collective
rights-holders, neighboring localities and downstream users.

## Alternatives and retained objections

Allocate against current-season availability and seek relief only if the
next season fails. This permits more immediate production but leaves later
users exposed if refill or procurement fails. Reserve for a specified dry
sequence and preserve a successor window. This limits some immediate uses
and can impose needless losses if the dry assumption is excessive. Procure
an independent additional source or fund demand and production changes.
These can protect current and later uses together but need accepted capacity,
lawful external impacts, dated cash and time. Select a public rolling
carryover rule for optional allocations, leaving qualified substitution
and earlier adjustment open. Compare all three on the same protected needs.

Reserve size, risk tolerance and social burdens cannot be decided by
hydrology alone. An overly conservative rule can destroy livelihoods.
An optimistic rule or inaccessible store can leave households unserved.
Evaluate both errors, provide separately lawful adjustment support and
publish missed cases. No independent review or actual affected-party
participation was obtained.

## Authority, finance and migration

The competent elected body sets the risk and priority law, material rights
and funded scope. The Steward verifies facts and implements the adopted
bounds. Directors receive and operate service; the separate Reviewer
and courts examine legality, original evidence, execution and remedies.
Opposition, users and collective rights-holders can raise alternatives and
challenge a deficient assumption or discriminatory release. Before reliance,
check appointment and replacement, funding, access to independent evidence,
and actual lawful execution even during a shared operational failure.

No existing rights, protected ecological limits, foreign duties or court
orders are silently varied. Move live permits, work, contracts, accrued
claims, operating records and funded responsibilities under WS19 and
ordinary succession. A balance can expose unavoidable loss without creating
a new emergency command. Price actual transport, treatment, power, support
and paid verification in the same operating budget. An independent supply
draws on its own lawful source and actual receiving.
