# Whole-package incidence and real institutional rivals

*Design 84; causal hypotheses, not observed distribution or consent.*

| People or interest | What the selected choice can improve | Burden/failure to keep visible |
|---|---|---|
| Excluded/dependent person | Prior paid accepting care/housing/payment and independent legal access rather than another list/referral | Scarce staff, unsuitable public provision, retained assessment power or delayed additional coverage |
| Unpaid/family carer | Paid alternatives/respite, voluntary withdrawal and independent recipient choice | Hiring/learning/transport may fail; a cash award alone does not release actual time |
| Low-income worker | Safe I05 resources and paired service receipt with tax/withdrawal/work costs visible | Nominal higher wage can coexist with lost care, more travel, less time or prices/rents; no automatic “every job pays” claim |
| Existing worker/pensioner/creditor | Preserved debt, indexation/accrual and review | Lawfully adopted shortage priority can delay some cash; unpaid sums and real financing/distributional harm remain |
| High-income/asset holder and firm | Stable essentials, functioning law and productive capability | Progressive burden, displaced discretionary use, reduced payout/returns or changed private investment; behavior/exit isn't assumed away |
| Poorer/sparse locality | Equalization and national residual responsibility | May face hardest staffing/transport costs; rich-area readiness cannot repeatedly consume all expansion |
| Opposition/nonuser/outsider | Protected scrutiny/replacement, original evidence, safe private/offline routes | Source/controller capture and imported/environmental harms can remain despite nominal public benefit |
| Future person | Maintenance, learning/replacement and separate ecology/claim accounts | Debt, damage and unfinished renewal persist beyond the study horizon; no assumed births/migration/AI gain |

Retain/repair can avoid destructive reorganization and preserve informal
cooperation. Comprehensive earlier guarantees can remove deprivation faster with
actual idle/recruited supply and redistribution. Direct public provision changes
ownership, bargaining and source control; prepaid plural capacity changes payment
and continuity without the same ownership. All have actual transition costs and
can be preferable in a real context. Hold a new programme to the same completion/
operation/renewal period as its rival, not a cheap unfinished snapshot.

Protection-first is selected for this conditional design because present severe
exclusion and usable democratic remedies matter before optional expansion, and
paid capacity is more concrete than a purchase promise. This is a value and
institutional choice, not empirical proof that gradualism or public employment
is always best. Repeated no-expansion can entrench deprivation; NP15 makes it
an explicit replaceable public choice rather than an indefinite technical hold.

Original workforce/service/household data, voluntary choices, prices, actual
payers/receivers, tax incidence, private saving/investment and ecology/FX remain
unknown. Actual affected participation and independent qualified evaluation are
needed before a real adoption/readiness/outcome claim. A desk selection can
specify responsibilities without pretending to have obtained those facts.
