# Eight inspections and four independent final decisions

Design354 · Constructed comparison under
[PQ01–PQ06](../../country/C08/32-fair-licensing-and-portable-competence.md).

A recovering district needs eight limited inspections and four final
occupancy determinations in the same week. The example assumes the
inspection tasks can be separated safely under a future sector Act.
Only a qualified officer with the necessary local competence can make
a final determination. That officer still checks the underlying
inspection evidence. Staff are on paid, non-overlapping assignments.

| Available team | Limited inspections | Final determinations |
|---|---:|---:|
| Existing team with valid local authority | 4 | 2 |
| Visiting team with the same domestic standard and valid scope | 2 | 2 |
| Two applicants whose foreign work shows equivalent inspection skills, with local decision competence unproved | 2 | 0 |

A blanket local relicensing gate leaves the visiting team and the
two applicants waiting. A blanket recognition based on titles lets
all eight inspections occur but would also assign two extra final
determinations to people without established authority for them.
The selected route recognizes the domestic scope, and admits the
two inspection-only applicants after independent verification and
express limited authorizations. It delivers eight inspections and
four final determinations under the stated assumptions.

The recognition decisions require two paid case-officer slots. A
qualified alternate needs separate availability. If both assessment
routes fail and no substitute workers are funded, the district receives
six inspections and the same four final determinations on independently
supported cases. Two inspections remain due. Pending files retain
their first-receipt dates. Delay cannot add two final authorizations,
and the district must handle any unsafe housing through the existing
safe-receiving duty without counting unsupported approvals.

A structural rival licenses the responsible operator and relies on
open entry for inspection work, using independent checks and liability.
It could use fewer individual applications when mistakes can be
detected and corrected before harm. In this case no operator has
offered a proved check for the two extra final decisions. Keep
individual authority for that act pending evidence of an alternative.
Neither model proves actual staffing, sector safety or national costs.
