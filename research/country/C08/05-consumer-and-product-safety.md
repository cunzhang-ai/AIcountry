# C08. Consumer rights, product safety and effective repair

*Part of [C08 everyday law and justice](../C08-everyday-law-and-justice.md); integrated design 62 research, not enacted law.*

A person must be able to understand a material offer, receive what was
promised and obtain a safe repair or remedy when the seller fails. Prospective
Assembly law sets clear terms, fair dealing, nonwaivable safety duties,
reasonable records, accessible complaints and proportionate collective
redress. Private contract cannot waive protection against serious hidden
hazards or make an AI screen the only place to contest a charge. Sector laws
retain stronger duties for food, medicines, vehicles, buildings, financial
products and essential utilities; the common floor fills gaps between them.
For food, the [C09 system route](../C09/09-food-systems-and-nutrition.md)
joins supply and access while sector inspectors keep traceable safety holds,
recalls and buyer remedies under this chapter's fair-process floor.
Food safety and [animal welfare](07-animal-protection-and-remedies.md)
have separate legal tests: a safe product does not establish humane care.

A producer, importer or responsible domestic supplier must identify itself,
keep traceable versions and report a material hazard. A marketplace or
intermediary that controls listing, payment or delivery has defined duties to
act on credible safety notices and preserve evidence; it cannot disown every
transaction as someone else's product while controlling the only buyer route.
Duties must match actual control and allow small sellers a usable compliance
path. Safety inspectors under the relevant sector law can test, require
warnings, isolate or recall an unsafe product on evidence and a specific
statutory ground, with time limits, reasons, prompt independent challenge and
recorded remedy. Serious immediate hazards permit narrow interim action; a
recall is not a license to punish disfavored speech or shield an incumbent
firm from a safer rival.

Affected buyers receive accessible notice, a practical repair, replacement,
refund or compensation route as law permits, and help for consequential loss.
A merchant's bankruptcy or foreign location cannot turn an announced recall
into a completed remedy. Courts decide disputed liability with fair defenses;
a consumer office can assist claims and investigate patterns but cannot be
the final judge of its own enforcement. Collective proceedings are available
where many small harms would otherwise be impossible to bring, with judicial
control of representation, evidence, settlement and distribution. Public
services that bought the product retain a separate continuity duty toward
their users while supplier claims proceed.

For connected or AI-enabled products, safety evidence identifies the operative
hardware, software and model version, update path, vulnerabilities and
instructions actually supplied. A vendor update cannot silently rewrite the
legal safety floor or erase a previous failure record. C11 preserves sources
and correction history; C10 customs and external cooperation address imported
goods; C09 logistics traces physical recalls. Cross-border action uses actual
treaty and jurisdiction powers rather than asserting control over a foreign
seller. C06 accounts for laboratories, inspectors, legal aid, replacement
stock and safe disposal, not only fine revenue.

The [decision record](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-market-power/decision-record.md) compares
this common floor with contract-only, unified-super-regulator and sector-only
routes. Actual product classes, tests, claims, inspector capacity and lawful
adoption remain unknown. Reopen if the common floor overwhelms small sellers,
sector gaps persist, or recall orders cannot reach harmed people in time.

[B09–B12](13-civil-obligations-and-executed-remedies.md) adds civil defect
responsibility without proving producer/importer knowledge, actual control
and causal defenses, employer/state responsibility and indivisible-harm
contribution. A claimant cannot recover the same item twice; a recall or
refund does not automatically repair bodily loss. Liability and loss finance
need competent adoption and actual delivery, not a supplier-name inference.

[V01–V12/VD04](19-regulation-and-bounded-enforcement.md) now specifies prior
duties, bounded inspection/urgent stop, nonpunitive correction and independent
monetary proof. Technical product limits still need actual enacted instruments;
no recall, fine or announced remedy proves safe replacement was received.

[TS/TD](22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md) now adds explicit
technical/product entry, safe operation, configuration/evidence recognition,
independent safety learning and VD10's prospective predicate. Real recall/
replacement and continued essential care remain duties; missing local annexes
supply no inferred threshold, guilt or actual safety certificate.

[LCY product lifecycle](../C09/26-product-repair-and-material-responsibility.md)
adds prospective support, independent repair access and take-back
for specified goods. Existing warranty, defect, safety and recall
remedies keep their own terms.

[DMR01–DMR08](../C04/01/03-domestic-recognition-and-local-protection.md) carries qualified product claims
across localities and provides scoped warnings and review. Ordinary product safety,
recall and consumer remedies continue.
