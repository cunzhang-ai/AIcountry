## Competing models and selection

Compare familiar household/status control, private-contract-only ordering,
and an individual floor with scoped optional ties. The first can preserve
stable expectations but centralize power over dependants; the second can
accommodate variety but neglect coercion, children and third-party duties.
Select the third as a research candidate, preserving informal cooperation,
ordinary trust and substantive rival models rather than registering all life.

B21–B40 specifies 18 majority; supported young-person/clinical choices;
bounded asset preservation without substitute legal personhood; optional
plural care/property compacts and no-fault unilateral exit; care contributions,
support, ordinary parentage, child arrangements/protection/adoption; wills,
dependent/default estates, missing people, civil periods and title loss.
Limited support/property compacts are distinct from a shared-life tie with
intestacy consequences. Shared assets, insurance pots and mandates never
multiply through extra accounts/relations. Ordinary genetic support duty,
reproductive-coercion exception and nonbiological lawful responsibility need
protected adjudication; special donor/gestational law remains unselected.

C06/23 assigns incremental receiving and prospective public operational
registry risk. A named Treasury payer for proved official-certification loss
replaces an unspecified compensation promise; private fraud is not generally
guaranteed. No actual reserve, tax receipts, appropriation or cash is invented.
Default title restoration, innocent buyer claims and interim housing remain
separate. Courts cannot enact tax or cancel the claim when a budget fails.

## Values, evidence and objections

real care/property contributions, stable claims and independent correction.

are normative hypotheses. Arithmetic examples use invented quantities;

Primary CRPD General Comment 1 distinguishes legal capacity and decision
skills and emphasizes will/preferences; it does not certify B23's procedure.
The 1996 HCCH text supplies scoped child-protection jurisdiction/cooperation,
not adoption/parentage/maintenance/succession recognition or automatic
membership. [Source note](../settlement/family-civil/03-selection-sources-and-limits.md)
limits claims. This is a bounded source/objection inspection, not a comprehensive
2026 comparative family-law or policy-event review. Shared older drafts
remain one upstream family, not independent corroboration.

Twenty-four reasoned journeys and independent arithmetic of the authored
fixtures expose child/adult conflation, compact power, double property,
multiple survivor claims, impossible support, finite estate and court shortage.
They are not verdict tests, observations, actual consent or independent review.
No sensitive actual clinical/child evidence is collected or made public.

Material dissent remains: plural structures can hide dominance; stable
traditional defaults may be more usable; clinical autonomy in grave child
cases may protect or harm; a removal deadline without a judge risks incompatible
safety/authority duties; inherited wealth and strict restoration distribute
loss unevenly; new public compensation may be unaffordable. Thresholds and
periods can be unfair. The chosen responses are advice, narrow scopes,
actual authority, safe independent receiving, child voice, explicit budgets
and visible unmet claims. Their effectiveness is unproved; a better social
model can displace this hypothesis rather than be absorbed into more clauses.
