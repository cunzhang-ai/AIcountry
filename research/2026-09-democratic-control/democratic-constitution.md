# Democratic control that can be exercised

21 September 2026 · Candidate constitutional architecture · Not enacted

**Design commitment:** AIcountry should be a constitutional democracy in which
people can organize an alternative, influence public decisions, remove decision-makers
peacefully and obtain effective remedies. A constitution's label, a favorable model
score or an incumbent's account of popular support cannot establish those conditions.

This proposal changes the intended allocation of power. AI-exclusive legislative
authorship is not the democratic endpoint: citizens and elected representatives must
be able to originate, amend, reject and enact legislation without an AI's permission.
AI may assist under revocable mandates and cannot have an independent sovereign veto.
This conflicts with parts of the older research design and requires a fundamental
transition before canonical adoption. Existing software permissions remain unchanged.

The articles below are candidate provisions and institutional choices. Exact legal
drafting against a verified current edition remains separate work. Numerical choices
are explicit starting hypotheses, not scientific optima or borrowed authority.

## D1. Public authority and political equality

Public authority derives from the people under a constitution they can meaningfully
contest and legitimately amend. No founder, party, security body, vendor or AI owns
the state or its constitutional future. Equal political standing cannot depend on
wealth, intelligence tests, ideological conformity, social-credit ratings or compute.

The proposed ordinary national franchise is all adult citizens from age 18, on equal
terms. Citizenship administration must not be used to remove political opponents;
registration and exclusion decisions have prompt independent remedies. Political
views, disability and need for assistance are not grounds to deny a vote. Local
participation of long-term residents needs an explicit inclusive settlement rather
than being silently equated with national citizenship. All people retain basic
rights and standing to challenge harms, whether or not they vote nationally.

The founding electorate cannot be chosen unilaterally by this project's owner.
Its territorial basis, membership and legitimate ratification arrangements are
currently unestablished. No population or consent is invented in this proposal.

## D2. A replaceable government with a public legislative mandate

The reference candidate is a parliamentary constitutional system: an elected
Assembly enacts legislation and appropriations; a prime minister and cabinet
administer under its confidence; independent courts decide legal challenges.
The executive has no separate mandate to override the Assembly or suspend elections.
Courts constrain legality and rights, not ordinary policy preference.

Proposed Assembly elections occur every four years under a compensatory proportional
system with a 3% national party threshold and an accessible independent-candidate
route. The seat count, districts and minority accommodations require a population
and electoral-design assessment before adoption; the threshold is not permission
to exclude otherwise protected representation. There is no winner's bonus. Votes,
seat allocation and effective representation must be publicly reproducible.

The Assembly elects the prime minister by a majority of its full membership. The
same majority may replace the prime minister by electing a successor in a constructive
no-confidence vote. The executive cannot dismiss the Assembly to evade that vote.
An early election requires a two-thirds Assembly vote or a constitutionally specified
government-formation failure, administered by the electoral authority. The prime
minister may serve no more than eight cumulative years; changing titles or declaring
a constitutional restart does not reset this candidate limit. Terms and turnover
alone do not prevent control through proxies, which remains subject to the power audit.

Citizen and member proposals need a guaranteed consideration route, not guaranteed
passage. Ordinary laws remain subject to debate, reasons, recorded voting and rights
review. A research publication or AI verification cannot enact them.

## D3. Opposition can become government

People may form parties, stand as independents, organize peaceful opposition,
assemble, publish, investigate and advocate replacement of the government and its
AI arrangements. Incumbents cannot approve their own opponents' ideological fitness.
Candidate registration uses objective, proportionate requirements with timely judicial
appeal. Claims of disinformation, disloyalty or foreign influence are not general
powers to prohibit criticism. Restrictions on unlawful conduct need evidence,
individualized reasons, due process and independent review.

Reserve at least one quarter of ordinary Assembly agenda time for non-government
business, allocated among opposition groups under a public rule. Every represented
opposition group receives minimum questioning opportunities and access to relevant
legislative information. One fifth of members can require an investigative hearing;
the government cannot erase the request from the docket. Opposition members chair
the public-accounts committee. These are proposed functional rights, not gifts
revocable by the governing majority's procedural convenience.

Public funding, impartial public-media access and accessible participation must
make lawful competition practicable. Publish beneficial ownership of campaign
funders, report donations and coordinated spending, and apply enforceable expenditure
rules equally. Exact financial limits need local cost evidence and prior adoption;
until they exist, the design cannot claim equal practical campaigning conditions.
Neither corporate wealth nor unlimited AI-generated activity creates additional votes.

## D4. Elections are independently administered and independently contestable

A permanent electoral commission controls registration administration, polling,
counting and certification within law. It does not choose acceptable political
opinions. Candidate agents, observers and independent media can inspect the process
subject to voter privacy. Publish count-level results and reconciliation evidence;
maintain an independently auditable record and usable non-digital voting channel.
The incumbent cannot certify its own victory or substitute model confidence for a count.

Proposed commission: seven members, seven-year nonrenewable terms, one scheduled
replacement annually. Two seats derive from an independently selected judicial
appointment process, two from opposition parliamentary groups, and three from a
temporary stratified citizen-selection panel operating a public competence and
conflict assessment. Seat sources are fixed and rotated within their categories;
a new government cannot reappoint the whole commission. These appointment sources
also require scrutiny for common control; their names alone establish no independence.

Publish nominations, conflicts and reasons before appointment. Removal requires a
reasoned independent judicial finding of specified misconduct or incapacity, with
appeal; disagreement with government is not cause. Qualified alternates selected
in advance through the same source serve only the remaining vacancy term. The
executive cannot fill delays with loyal acting appointments.

Electoral disputes go to an independent judicial panel, with urgent interim relief
available. Proposed initial review of an exclusion or counting complaint: within
72 hours where delay could defeat the right, without pretending every merits case
can be resolved in that time. A missed deadline does not establish a claimant's
victory or permit official inaction without an explained escalation.

Fundamental election rules are fixed before competition. Proposed protection:
no incumbent-benefiting change to the formula, district allocation or commission
structure during the year before an election. A narrowly justified rights-restoring
correction needs independent review and must not become a pretext for rewriting
the contest. Official communications, security bodies and state resources cannot
be used as the governing party's campaign machinery.

## D5. Independent review has people, money, information and enforceable powers

Create constitutional review with authority to hear rights, electoral and
institutional-competence cases, issue interim orders and require compliance.
Public-interest standing and funded assistance must make serious challenges possible
without personal wealth. Case assignment follows published rules with auditable
random allocation where appropriate; no leader assigns politically sensitive cases
to a preferred judge. Judgments give reasons, dissent and appeal/reconsideration
routes; privacy and justified confidentiality do not conceal the operative order.

Reference constitutional court: nine judges, twelve-year nonrenewable terms,
three replacements every four years. Three appointment sources each select three
seats: a plural judicial appointments body, a three-fifths Assembly process including
meaningful non-government support, and a stratified citizen panel choosing from an
independently assessed qualified pool. No office chooses the entire pool, all judges
or its own review panel. Publish qualification grounds and conflicts; neither wealth
nor political loyalty is a qualification. No automatic lowering of appointment
requirements after deadlock. Preselected qualified alternates prevent a vacancy
from becoming an executive appointment power.

For this candidate, meaningful non-government support means approval by at least
one third of non-government Assembly members as well as the overall three-fifths
threshold. A nominal opposition that shares the incumbent's effective control cannot
be cited as independent support without disclosure and examination. Citizen panels
use a publicly auditable draw from an inclusive roll, predetermined replacement rules,
compensation and accessibility support. Independent judicial and multiparty observers
inspect the selection; the platform provider may not rank loyalty or quietly alter
the pool. These arrangements must exist in practice before claiming independence.

Bootstrap staggered terms and initial selections require a separately legitimate
founding procedure. This cannot be supplied by appointing the founder's nominees
and labeling them independent. Professional, parliamentary and citizen sources can
all be captured; demonstrate actual selection independence and review their failures.

The Assembly adopts transparent budgets. Elections, adjudication and audit receive
direct scheduled treasury payments under their appropriations, not discretionary
monthly executive release. Pending a budget dispute, essential functions continue
at the prior lawful rate under a limited published continuity rule, with court
review of necessity, revenue constraints and proposed cuts. No body sets an unlimited
budget for itself. A fiscal shortfall is disclosed, not hidden by a promise that a
constitutional sentence pays staff. Access to records, personnel and enforcement
must be demonstrated as well as appropriation on paper.

## D6. The public can set questions and challenge framing between elections

Citizens do not participate only on election day. A verified petition from 1% of the
electorate triggers a public hearing and reasoned Assembly response within 90 days;
assisted and offline signatures are available, subject to privacy-preserving checks.
This is a candidate agenda threshold, not automatic enactment or a barrier to
individual complaints and rights claims.

Stratified, compensated and rotating citizen panels can investigate major choices,
question competing experts, issue recommendations and require a recorded response.
They cannot silently displace elected legislative authority or personal voting
rights. Selection, exclusions, facilitator control and source access are open to
challenge. Referendums require fair alternatives, deliberation, independent
administration and rights scrutiny; a government-written yes/no slogan does not
resolve every constitutional question.

Protect journalism, independent research, lawful whistleblowing and association.
Government cannot monopolize public-interest information or secretly use personal
data to manipulate political choices. Civic education should enable criticism and
institutional understanding, not test loyalty. Care time, accessibility, language
and the material cost of participation belong in the operating budget.

## D7. AI is accountable assistance, with an independent route around it

Humans may introduce and amend laws without an AI sponsor. An elected institution
must be able to reject an AI proposal, replace a provider, inspect decisive reasons
and continue lawful work during an outage. AI cannot determine who deserves the
franchise, censor opposition by itself, certify its own mandate or hold a permanent
veto over constitutional change.

Personal agents may translate, explain and explore choices within revocable mandates.
Agent copies do not multiply political standing. A prediction of voting preference
or collective agent agreement is not a ballot. Any future voting assistance must
preserve the individual's deliberate authorization, secrecy, equal vote and usable
alternative; it needs independent evaluation before deployment.

Publicly funded deliberation tools expose sponsorship, ranking rules, changes in
models and material omissions, with protected access where necessary for audit.
Different interfaces are not independent alternatives if one operator controls
their identity, data, priorities and appeal route. No single entity should control
political eligibility, opinion mediation, lawmaking, enforcement and the evidence
used to audit itself. Personal data and internal security credentials remain protected.

## D8. Defeat produces an actual transfer of office

Before each election, publish the applicable certification, appeal and handover
timetable. Once the result and any judicially ordered remedy establish the incoming
mandate, the outgoing cabinet has no veto over transfer. For this parliamentary
candidate, the new Assembly meets within 14 days of final certification and the
cabinet hands over within 7 days of election of its successor.

During transition, the outgoing administration conducts essential caretaker business
only: no opportunistic long-term appointments, destruction of records or exceptional
contracts binding the successor without the required independent authorization.
Legally specified succession and removal cover obstruction; the losing leader
cannot certify whether those rules apply to them.

Civil servants, treasury officers and lawful security command owe duties to the
constitutional office and valid orders, not personal loyalty. They must preserve
records, implement valid court and transfer orders, and use protected channels to
challenge manifestly unlawful instructions. Enforcement and any sanctions follow
independent legal process. Military or police bodies cannot choose the political
winner. No autonomous AI system may exercise political coercion.

Use separately controlled custody and recovery arrangements for public records,
payment authorization and administrative access. A private operator's refusal to
release a password must not be a constitutional veto. Test handover and recovery
with actual responsible institutions before claiming readiness. Paper entitlements
cannot guarantee transfer if courts, treasury, security command and records are
already jointly captured; that is a critical democratic failure, not a passed test.

## D9. Emergencies cannot silently change the regime

An emergency declaration names the threat, evidence, limited powers, territory,
funding and expiry. Proposed executive duration: at most 14 days without Assembly
renewal; each renewal lasts at most 30 days and needs three-fifths of the full
Assembly, public reasons and independent judicial necessity review. Courts and
opposition oversight remain available throughout. After 90 cumulative days, the
same measures require ordinary, fully reviewed legislation or lapse; relabeling
the same emergency does not restart the clock. Ordinary legislation cannot acquire
the forbidden constitutional effects by changing the label either.

Emergency power cannot amend succession, remove term limits, reconstitute oversight
bodies or prohibit peaceful opposition. Elections cannot be postponed by unilateral
executive declaration. Any genuine physical impossibility needs a separate electoral
authority finding, independent court order, geographically narrow remedy and a
public recovery timetable reviewed at least weekly. It cannot award the incumbent
a fresh term. Catastrophic loss of constitutional continuity remains a real failure
requiring a legitimate recovery settlement, not an indefinite self-renewing mandate.

## D10. A current majority cannot remove future democratic choice

Equal political standing, meaningful opposition, genuine periodic competition,
effective rights remedies and peaceful replacement are protected foundations of
this democratic design. An ordinary majority, referendum slogan or AI forecast
cannot convert their abolition into a compatible amendment. Changes to their
institutional form must preserve or strengthen their practical function.

Proposed constitutional amendments require two-thirds of the full Assembly, at
least 90 days of public consideration, independent rights/competence review and
ratification by a majority of valid votes in a genuinely administered referendum.
No turnout quorum is proposed because its incentives require separate evaluation;
participation quality and equal access remain obligations. A special rule applies
to incumbent-benefiting changes to tenure, election rules and appointments: they
cannot take effect for the current officeholders or the contest selecting their
immediate successors. The predecessor procedure governs its own replacement.

An inherited procedure is not democratic merely because it is inherited. If it
structurally prevents meaningful public control or peaceful constitutional change,
record a legitimacy crisis rather than treating the incumbent's veto as permanently
justified. A legitimate constitutional settlement requires actual public standing
and participation; neither this project nor an AI can manufacture it. Respecting
current research-publication credentials does not establish the legitimacy of an
analogous political monopoly or give its holder ownership of a future constitution.

Review the cumulative effect of linked budget, appointment, media, identity and
procedural changes. Each can appear ordinary while together eliminating opposition
or enforcement. Conversely, constitutional review cannot become a permanent
unaccountable political veto: publish reasons, hear opposing parties and retain
lawful accountability and replacement of reviewers without retaliatory removal.

The doctrine of democratic protection must not become a general power to outlaw
peaceful critics. Restrictions on violence, coercion or specific unlawful conduct
need individualized evidence and due process. People can argue for constitutional
alternatives; this research project need not endorse proposals that eliminate equal
standing or meaningful replacement as compatible with its democratic commitment.

## D11. Independence is an operating relationship, not an institution count

For each safeguard identify who appoints, removes, funds, controls records for,
supplies technology to and executes orders for it. Examine joint and indirect
control, including coalition partners, common sponsors and vendor dependence.
Separate logos, human/AI labels or departments do not establish independence.

At least one effective challenge and enforcement path must lie outside the control
of the actor whose action is challenged. Test that path under noncooperation:
information withheld, appropriation delayed, appointments blocked, hostile rankings,
result disputed and an incumbent refusing to leave. Include the risk that the
reviewer, anticorruption body or citizen panel abuses its own powers.

There is no compensating total democracy score: actual exclusion from competition,
no enforceable remedy or an incumbent's effective veto over lawful replacement
blocks a claim of functioning democratic control even if service outcomes improve.

## D12. Establish progress through evidence, not a superiority claim

Compare this candidate with credible democratic alternatives, including a
presidential separation-of-powers design and an elected system with stronger direct
participation, under the same population assumptions, resources and attack cases.
The parliamentary choice provides a concrete starting design, not a proven winner.

Assess practical agenda access, unequal political influence, opposition viability,
time and access to remedy, implementation of judgments, truthful public information,
handover reliability and rights under pressure. Use distributions and actual records,
not only constitutional promises. Support healthy civic institutions and professional
norms as well as formal checks. No design can guarantee immunity to coordinated capture.

For now, all institutions and exercises above are proposed. Founding legitimacy,
membership, budgets, actual appointments, enforcement capacity and independent
evaluation remain unestablished. AIcountry may claim to be researching a stronger
democracy; it cannot yet claim to have achieved one or outperformed existing societies.

## Source boundaries

The Human Rights Committee's General Comment 25 emphasizes effective participation,
genuine elections, independent administration, scrutiny and implementation of results.
It supplies a rights reference, not the particular institutional design above.
[OHCHR compilation, General Comment 25, paragraphs 1, 19–25](https://docstore.ohchr.org/SelfServices/FilesHandler.ashx?enc=Kp8jq1gjGDCNYl6YF%2FWUSXXCtILteaVJWQfRU32X8OuDMF2JIq8WOcTGbbtZDvDxvmRnkwupTTjJgR6%2BqMt6sT8kJSU5NrEgWPWLVOIvsOs%3D).

The Venice Commission's code addresses electoral rights, impartial administration,
appeals and stability of fundamental electoral rules. It informs those subjects;
its recommendations are not all reproduced or claimed universally binding here.
[Code of Good Practice in Electoral Matters](https://www.venice.coe.int/webforms/documents/default.aspx?pdffile=CDL-AD(2002)023rev2-cor-e).

International IDEA documents attacks on courts, electoral competition, opposition
and civic space, and notes that defensive institutions can themselves be abused.
This supports examining interacting routes to capture, not treating a label or
single veto as sufficient. The attack scenarios and candidate rules are our design.
[Designing Resilient Institutions](https://www.idea.int/publications/catalogue/html/designing-resilient-institutions-countering-democratic-backsliding-asia).
