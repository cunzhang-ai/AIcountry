# Design287. Household protection and usable receiving

10 November 2026 · Research selection · No actual enactment

## Baseline and question

[Design286](baseline.json) brings the first programme onto a common
dated budget with independent release and remedy checks. TH02 names
the arranging officer and TH10 and TH12 already cover the whole
journey and a safe handback. Apply the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md)
and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md) to the remaining
question. When each provider can complete its task independently,
what gives a resident usable protection through the interruption?
Codex assisted with drafting and the comparison. Actual affected-party
participation, independent qualification and democratic authority remain open.

## Alternatives and selection

- **Keep repair and care in place.** Prefer it when safe independent
  interim heat and ordinary care can be verified by the relevant times.
  It avoids displacement and uses fewer resources in the authored
  case. It fails under that case's shared control loss.
- **Fund joined temporary receiving.** Keep the dwelling and underlying
  service claims while the existing housing officer arranges an
  accepted temporary place with accessible travel and essential care
  at the receiving site. Select it for the authored reference case
  and retain it as a conditional choice in weaker funding. The paid
  extension uses scarce rooms, care and travel that cannot also serve
  other households. A room-only option saves money and can be useful
  where care can safely stay separate or travel with the household.
  In the declared case it leaves an essential need uncovered.
- **Pursue a voluntary secure move.** Prefer this as a competing
  long-run route if the home is persistently unsafe and the resident
  wants it. Include the effect on tenure, care, rent and household
  commitments. The assumed later offer leaves both first deadlines
  unmet, so it still needs lawful interim protection.

Select [TH13–TH17](../country/C09/23/04-household-protection-and-usable-receiving.md)
as the working rule. [Observed cases](sources.md) motivate the
suitability and handback checks. The same-place test, resources and
deadlines in the exercise are authored. A supported move requires
consent or a distinct pre-existing legal basis when applicable. The
design grants no new compulsory authority.

## Compatibility, objection and migration

Apply the joined check to existing housing and first-budget receiving
records only where the components genuinely depend on one another.
Keep ordinary report channels and offer independently safe services
without waiting for the combined check. The housing case owner carries
open repairs, protected payments, original dates and unresolved remedies
into any amended record. Care providers retain their duties. Existing
authorities must separately approve additional spending, reserve access
or any change in rights. A previous referral supplies only evidence of
the referral. Recover evidence or mark actual receipt unknown, without
withdrawing valid decisions or entitlements.

A combined gate could slow urgent aid, enable broad data sharing or
favor households that are easy to serve. Keep the rule proportionate
and support ordinary human contact. Audit actual useful receiving
and unresolved gaps separately from department counts, with protected
resident evidence and independent urgent review. Keep already due
services and rights on their original calendars. A genuine inability
to join the services requires disclosure and correction on the actual
clock. It provides no discretion to rewrite that clock.

## Next bounded decision

With consent and the necessary independent access, follow one local
case from first report through payment, destination care, renewal
and handback. Compare resident experience, original service and
an authorized repair alternative. Reopen if coordination itself delays
protection, the ordinary route works more reliably, an extension
displaces another due service or no independently usable local
provider can accept. A successful trace would qualify only that
delivery path and context.
