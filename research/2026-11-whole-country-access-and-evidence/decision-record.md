## Choices, rivals and consequences

**MO01–MO16:** select an accessible scheduled-network backbone with paid
qualified demand-responsive fallback through existing local authorities and
national equalization. Add free necessary journeys and two flexible returns
weekly to an actually usable hub/equivalent; protect ordinary life independently
of commuting/care.60-minute default, local reasoned sparse band up to90,
booking/arrival/return and direct correction are explicit normative hypotheses.
Old sector safety, useful times and claims continue; proposed new ordinary
components have a candidate prospective18-month notice/date, not present dues.
Actual enactment fixes dates; local refusal or readiness cannot restart them.

Retain existing-route repair, public/plural network, paid demand response and
cash/private-car support as substantive rivals. Network packing can reduce work
but impose delay; response routing may improve access but costs drivers/vehicles.
Cash cannot buy an absent accessible operator and may exclude non-drivers.
A free floor subsidizes richer users and adds public cost; no U/cash cut funds
it silently. Actual necessary expenses are matched once, not priced as service
income. No compelled relative, unsafe seat, forced move or presumed remote assent.
Fresh review added fair ordinary-slot allocation, one service-week/no reset,
full first partial-week scope and makeup additional to the next minimum.

**EV01–EV16:** federated sources plus one publicly appointed six-year,
nonrenewable chief; public open qualification and three-fifths full Assembly
single-name appointment, no lower-threshold or executive fallback. Courts prove
for-cause removal with appeal; a qualified career deputy has at most180elapsed
days, after which officers retain only their own statutory routine preservation,
existing-method correction/release duties. No acting-policy mandate renews itself.
Ordinary ministry leadership reduces appointment friction but risks direct
political release pressure. A self-perpetuating expert board closes public
replacement. The selection accepts parliamentary deadlock; it does not prove
independence or eliminate hostile-source/court/majority/vendor capture.

Assembly law controls legal-series scope/vintage/revision, tax/benefit rules and
funds; professional methods do not amend rights. Statistical correction is not
automatic tax/benefit repricing; source-law claims and correction remain.
Protected publication and plural outside inquiry coexist with statistical
confidentiality, safe originals and independent correction. No truth office,
private-life directory or service veto follows. Chief/recruitment/privacy/
fieldwork/publication/remedy costs are not supplied by old K01 subsets.

**CX01–CX06:** primary research context is existing-community retrofit, keeping
paid greenfield/direct provision a serious rival. Compare retained/repairedI05,
the same opening with paid protected-U transition, and a separately sourced
new settlement. Actual title, condition, legal authority and land remain unknown.
No free retained assets combined with absence of inherited duties. Preserve
wage/tenancy/pension/net/index/creditor/currency/foreign obligations and cases.
Retain NP's120-day proposal/180-day public disposition and reasoned no-expansion
option, annual review and actual statutory entitlement dates. No new founding
or appropriating authority arises. Separate recurring G/S, initial/transition,
due-date liquidity and owned foreign sources; restricted assets stay restricted.
