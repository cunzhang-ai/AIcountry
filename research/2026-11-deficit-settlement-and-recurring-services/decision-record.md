# Design269 decision record

**Decision and baseline.** Design268 preserves paid urgent execution
where an ordinary source fails. FB protects continuing services and
separates finite and recurring sources. Specify an explicit joint
decision when a historic deficit is settled while recurring ordinary
support stays underfunded. Use the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md)
and [decision handoff](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

**Evidence and rival diagnoses.** The English evidence separates an
accounting treatment, net cumulative deficit, in-year cost and later
restricted settlement. An older balance may be the immediate risk to
a locality, while current unmet support, high unit costs, increasing
need, or shortage of qualified providers may drive a continuing
gap. Each requires different evidence and action. Neither a grant
award nor an audited balance confirms children's daily receipt.
No affected people or legitimate decision-makers have participated
in this proposed country's choice. [Source scope](sources.md)

**Selection and rivals.** Compare a standalone settlement, finite
bridge and a linked ordinary and source decision with verified
receipt. Retain a genuine lower-cost route with the same actual
support as a strong rival. In the authored case select a lawful
ordinary reallocation of three units with a separately repayable
one-unit bridge for the first deadline. That means foregoing an
unbound optional programme. A three-unit finite source alone
covers only the first year and part of the second. A lower
recurring yield or unavailable staff reopens the result.
Keep already existing rights and all other protected budgets.
[Authored comparison](../settlement/deficit-settlement-and-recurring-services/02-authored-continuing-support-comparison.md)

**Authority and objections.** The Assembly and the competent local
elected body choose their own source and service scope. A grant
payer cannot use a historic condition to impose an unrelated
policy or extinguish a current learner's lawful right. The
public duty owner and independent reviewer need qualified access,
paid execution and actual receiving. Local officials can hide
cost shifting, queue exclusion or unmet need in a favourable
account. A new recurring grant can mask missing workers or
wasteful provision. Publish actual staff and receipt scope and
material objections. Preserve protected personal and case evidence.

**Transition and next decision.** Preserve old liabilities, original
service and review dates, qualified staff, local ceilings and actual
authority at each date. Count historic grants, local contributions
and later repayment once. Qualify one current support cohort with
actual source, payment dates, staffed places and independent
recipient review over the budget and following years. Set actual
financial and time limits and affected participation before a live
test. Reopen if a payment, qualified route or later source fails,
or if a better service model protects the same people. Enactment,
paid operation and better lives remain open.
