# Design history: foundations through design 8

*Part of [Design history](../DESIGN-HISTORY.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

## Completed reference-design work and real-world gates

1. **Social/fiscal design: first integrated pass completed, readiness open.** The
   [settlement](../SOCIAL-FISCAL-SETTLEMENT.md) specifies guarantee, delivery, financing
   and democratic shortfall duties. One invented national scenario compares retention,
   service expansion and an unconditional supplement over three years. All fit
   narrow reference financing limits *within that earlier scenario*;
   retention/cash leave assumed service gaps, and expansion fails adverse
   financing or workforce conditions. Its assumptions predate and do not cost
   the current election, banking, production, utility, disaster, time/leave,
   housing/mobility and evidence institutions together. Real benefit adequacy,
   tax incidence, subannual cash, geography, land/material/ecological limits
   and lender commitments remain unqualified. No national feasibility
   certificate follows. A lawful adopter would need one updated account using
   original assets and obligations; another invented case is not calibration.
2. **Authority and legal package: drafted, adoption open.** The [authority candidate](../AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION.md)
   allocates legislation, taxation, spending, treaty approval, service decisions,
   independent review and execution. It provides A1–A15 proposed wording and maps
   material older clauses, including scattered AI lawmaking references. A new lawmaking
   procedure cannot ratify itself; the actual then-current edition requires full review
   and a legitimate fundamental route before any enactment.
3. **Founding and operating transition: designed conditionally.** Two lawful adoption
   settings, an interim path, phased commencement and a map for existing rights, cases,
   contracts, staff, assets and debts are specified in [founding](../FOUNDING-AND-STRESS.md).
   No territory, affected electorate, actual constitutional vote or institutional
   capacity exists here. Those are missing real-world inputs, not text to invent.
4. **Whole-country failures: eleven authored exercises completed.** Election/handover,
   day-91 budget, fiscal/workforce, local/AI-service, emergency/opposition,
   official-violence/evidence-control, housing-credit/payment, armed attack
   during contested succession and payment failure, and child identity/family
   safety across localities, and a vacant court with executive refusal trace
   actors and remaining failure conditions.
   The eleventh joins territorial-status claims, shared water, service continuity
   and rival national/regional assertions. They are arguments, not evidence
   of observed resilience. Common capture or missing cash can still defeat the design.
5. **Civic-power correction: selected for the working design, readiness open.** A
   further authored case joins private control over jobs, political finance, news
   reach and public payment access. The [decision record](../2026-10-civic-power/decision-record.md)
   compares limited political-finance rules, public consolidation and a plural-power
   response. The main design now requires usable independence for lawful opposition
   across these systems. Actual beneficial control, legal powers, privacy limits,
   provider exit, costs and enforcement capacity require a real jurisdiction and
   affected-party scrutiny. No stress-case narration proves those safeguards work.
6. **Integrated design 5: four structural corrections selected, all conditional.**
   [Membership](../2026-10-membership-review/analysis.md) closes the indefinite
   settled-resident exclusion path while keeping founding consent separate.
   [Political economy](../2026-10-political-economy-review/analysis.md) connects public
   promises to banking, imports, production and physical accounts under the selected
   external-currency reference, later displaced by design 18. [Security](../2026-10-security-review/analysis.md)
   carries the rights floor across all coercive actors and allocates bounded local
   and national protective capacity. [National learning](../2026-10-integrated-review/learning-analysis.md)
   separates this project's revision practice from a future country's authorized
   inquiry, trial, elected decision and continuity. Each selection has rival designs,
   known objections and untested operating dependencies. The
   [integration decision](../2026-10-integrated-review/decision-record.md) states their
   compatibility limits and the review still required.
7. **Integrated design 6: everyday life and shared capacity connected, readiness
   open.** [Social provision](../2026-10-social-provision-review/analysis.md) adds a
   usable cross-service claim without creating one compulsory agency.
   [Spatial planning](../2026-10-land-ecology-review/analysis.md) specifies which
   elected body settles cross-boundary resource conflicts before project permits.
   [Ordinary law](../2026-10-civil-law-review/analysis.md) joins common legal rights
   to intake, help, temporary relief and executed judgments.
   [External relations](../2026-10-life-systems-integration/external-relations-analysis.md)
   connects commitments, imports, refuge and exported harms. These are proposed
   national choices, not staffed services, measured land or water, a reconciled
   civil code, signed treaties or observed outcomes. Their shared labor, cash,
   data, sites and authority remain to be costed and authorized in a real setting.
   The [design 6 decision](../2026-10-life-systems-integration/decision-record.md)
   records the comparisons, conflicts and continuity obligations.
8. **Integrated design 7: operating institutions selected, readiness open.**
   [Health, education and care](../2026-10-core-institutions/health-education-care.md)
   now distinguish democratic funding, responsible purchasers, public delivery
   capacity, professional practice, safety regulation and rights review.
   [Digital public power](../2026-10-core-institutions/digital-ai-power.md) separates
   law, records, technical operation, consequential decisions and appeals while
   requiring usable assisted routes and practiced provider succession.
   [Criminal justice](../2026-10-core-institutions/crime-justice.md) selects a bounded
   penal strategy from prevention and immediate protection through fair proof,
   proportionate disposition, lawful custody and reintegration. An additional
   integration check required a law-triggered, reviewable national service fallback
   and a named civilian corrections/release authority. The three choices
   must share the same workforce, physical, digital and public budget account;
   none creates staff, a prison, a regulator or independent remedy by writing it.
   The [design 7 decision](../2026-10-core-institutions/decision-record.md) records
   alternatives, shared objections, affected law and reopening conditions.
9. **Integrated design 8: residual decision authority made explicit, readiness open.**
   The [democratic-institutions review](../2026-10-operating-settlement/democratic-institutions.md)
   adds a distributed election-evidence and rights-remedy circuit and a real
   contested-transfer readiness gate, without inventing a new guardian or winner.
   The [territorial-authority review](../2026-10-operating-settlement/territorial-authority.md)
   gives the Assembly the final material unresolved interlocal allocation after
   bounded joint preparation, retaining an elected regional tier as an actual
   jurisdiction-dependent rival. The [economic-control review](../2026-10-operating-settlement/economic-control.md)
   gives bank insolvency, payment outage and Treasury shortfall different actors,
   funds and legal defaults. None of these paper chains proves an independent
   court, clearing provider, public cash, land/water account or obedient officer.
   The [design 8 decision](../2026-10-operating-settlement/decision-record.md)
   records shared dependencies, objections and migration.
