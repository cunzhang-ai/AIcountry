# APS01–APS07. Protect public participation from abusive civil claims

*Design377 · Part of [civil obligations and executed remedies](../13-civil-obligations-and-executed-remedies.md) · Proposed ordinary law.*

**APS01 — Give a direct application on public participation.** A
defendant may seek early review where a civil claim, counterclaim or
requested interim order arises materially from public-interest
participation. This includes reporting, research, testimony, a
public records request, peaceful organizing and necessary lawful
assistance. The defendant identifies the connection and the issue
of public concern. Entry depends on the actual acts and issue,
without a press licence, public position or official agreement
with the views expressed. This route does not create immunity
for a proved wrong or decide criminal liability.

**APS02 — Protect the opportunity while the court assesses the claim.**
Use accessible ordinary court intake, prompt notice to both sides,
independent assignment and available legal assistance. A judge may
hold only the costly or coercive steps whose delay is necessary for
fair early review. Preserve necessary evidence, protected sources,
independently justified urgent safety and fair trial measures.
The proposed initial case direction is within two working days
of protected receipt, sooner under B17 or AX where needed. Set the
earliest safe hearing and decide early disposition within 15
working days of that receipt where parties have had a meaningful
opportunity to answer. Allow one reasoned extension of up to ten
working days where necessary for fairness. Preserve earlier
applicable safeguards and act before an identified civic
opportunity is lost where possible. Filing creates no automatic
gag, merits ruling or postponement of a public event.

**APS03 — Require an evidence-based claim and weigh the actual harm.**
After the participation nexus is shown, require the claimant to
identify the actionable act, legal elements, causal harm and
proportionate relief, with a substantial basis in admissible or
reasonably obtainable evidence and an answer to material defenses.
Allow narrow, necessary proof or amendments where fairness requires.
If that basis is absent after a meaningful opportunity to respond,
dismiss the unsupported part with reasons and ordinary review.
For a supported part, weigh likely serious harm and the interest
in genuine redress against the burden on participation and
available narrower relief. Continue the part whose adjudication
is justified under this test. The court can sever mixed claims
and narrow unnecessary disclosure. An early survival decision
does not establish liability. An early dismissal does not prove
malicious intent.

**APS04 — Keep privacy, testimony and genuine redress usable.** Apply
existing privileges, source protection, confidentiality, fair
defense and separately lawful urgent protection. A request to
identify a confidential source or compel a bulk search needs its
own precise lawful basis and proportionate order. A specific
supported claim for private harm can proceed even where it
appears in the same complaint as an unsupported speech claim.
An interim restriction on expression needs the stronger AX
and B safeguards where applicable. Use scoped relief that
addresses the proved risk and preserves unrelated participation.
Protect claimants and defendants from cost or disclosure demands
that would defeat a fair opportunity to be heard.

**APS05 — Make defense and relief financially usable.** Fund accessible
intake, interpretation, independent counsel where justified by
need and stakes, protected preservation and actual court
execution under OJ and DB. Do not rely on a later costs award
to finance the first response. After an independently supported
abuse finding and fair hearing, the court can order reasonably
incurred defense costs, documented compensatory relief within
existing law and narrowly tailored procedural protection against
a proved abusive repeat. Ordinary cost law governs otherwise.
Security requires specific necessity, proportionality and
attention to the parties' means. No charge can bar an ordinary
claim solely because its claimant lacks money. Wealth, loss of
a motion or an opponent's allegation alone proves no abuse.

**APS06 — Verify that protection reached the actual forum in time.**
The court gives reasons and names the lawful executor, exact
measure and useful date. Verify delivery to parties and any
affected forum under existing authority. The court can preserve
a lawful opportunity and remedy an unlawful restriction without
deciding the underlying elected or regulatory issue. A late
award or nominal stay does not restore a missed hearing. Record
the lost opportunity and preserve appropriate relief and review.
Repeated or parallel demands use ordinary coordination and
cross-border recognition rules with original dates and defenses.

**APS07 — Keep the safeguard independent and review its effects.**
Separate the appointments, paid counsel, records, decision and
execution from the implicated public or private litigant. Publish
safe aggregate timing, outcomes and unresolved access gaps,
including cases where legitimate claims proceeded. AI may aid
search, scheduling and case comparison. It cannot infer motive
as proof or decide abuse, credibility or liability. Preserve
pending cases, privileges, original claims and final judgments
under prospective transition. Reopen on abuse of expedited review,
chilled genuine claims, missed opportunities or a better qualified
less burdensome route.
