# Design388. Keep shared public interfaces replaceable and legally accountable

[LX17–LX23](../../country/C11/12/05-public-standards-and-replaceable-implementation.md)
requires accountable adoption, independently implementable terms,
preserved meaning and funded alternative receiving. Change versions
without transferring authority to a vendor or losing original rights.

[Primary sources and the worked transfer](../../settlement/public-standards-and-replaceable-implementation/01-one-interface-two-real-routes.md)
test controller independence, undisputed payment and a disputed
adjustment. Actual local users, legal authority and readiness remain open.
