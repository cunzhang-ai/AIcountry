# Design361. Fund common proof, local access and actual distribution

[RCR01–RCR06](../../country/C08/13/06-representative-claims-and-delivered-redress.md)
joins common and individual hearings, notice, counsel, independent execution
and protected receiving. Keep the original local remedy when the lead court
cannot accept. Keep returned payments and unpaid balances open.

The [comparison](../representative-claims-and-delivered-redress/01-one-common-issue-separate-receipts.md)
distinguishes promised, sent and received compensation and preserves opt-in
scope. Actual staff, lawful sources and outcomes remain unverified.
