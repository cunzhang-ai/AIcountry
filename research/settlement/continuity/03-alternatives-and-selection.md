# Continuity packages: legal powers, resources and losses

*Integrated design 57 research choice, not adopted statute or a proof of country superiority.*

| Package | Legal and delivery mechanism | Resource and power consequences | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: existing general access/continuity promises | Preserve current chains and generic readiness/shortfall duties; ordinary offices set service timing inside their mandates | Least new statutory administration; leaves safe-time, provisional-payment, qualification and substitute-execution gaps open, and generic reserves unallocated | Reject as a sufficient operating specification; preserve useful informal/sector cooperation |
| B: targeted due-time continuity with attributed capacity | E01–E20 fixes receipt, standing schedule, provisional hardship formula, earlier safe time, direct urgent review, distinct narrow substitute, timely contest support and elected shortfall response | Select existing officers and separate final judges; publish cash/shift/control dependencies and displaced duties; existing rights survive. Core attribution and path alternatives consume capacity rather than create it | Select for working research; refuse a full-feasibility or operational-readiness claim |
| C: national direct provision with dedicated reserves | National public clinical/care, payment and receiving networks operate the core directly under Assembly law, with ring-fenced qualified reserves, protected local access and separate courts | Could reduce fragmented purchasing and local fiscal dependence, while creating national appointment, funding and supply concentration; requires explicit transfers of powers, added standby/transition cost and lawful alternatives when the national operator fails | Keep as a substantive competitor; its full resources and capture safeguards are untested, so this exercise cannot reject it or claim B is superior |

C is substantively different, not just another deadline: it changes the
operating level, provider mix, reserves and source of funding dependence.
Both B and C require elected general priorities and independent final review;
neither gives an AI or executive autonomous allocation power. A cash/private-supply country model remains a separate legitimate
competitor under the [design-56 comparison](../commitments/06-combined-results.md).
Neither this service-oriented test nor a zero new-office count resolves
its distribution, supply or autonomy claims.

## What is selected now

Select the **proposed clauses** and the rule that due/useful protection,
independent execution and displaced duties must be tested together. Retain
the present sector/Assembly/court division. Do not select the authored core
FTE shares, average costs or controller labels as actual staffing, national
priorities or a passed reserve. Clocks and the seven-day bridge are explicit
hypotheses; benefit/health/campaign amounts and actual institutional mapping
remain source-statute choices. No real law or current software is changed.

Record continuity law, source-law migration, cash, qualified capacity,
protected data, review and received remedy as separate states. Failure
requires interim lawful protection and a recorded elected response, not
automatic eligibility, coercion, a delayed election or cancelled claim.
An office can be repaired or replaced by lawful public decision; a narrow
payment substitute cannot inherit general sovereign discretion.

The next integrated decision is the **income-floor specification**: one
proposed eligible scope, needs basket/update formula, tax/benefit interaction,
payment calendar and provisional/accrued-claim transition, compared across
households and places with the same service/cash/resource constraints.
This sets the missing statutory content that E05–E08 deliberately cannot
invent. The country-wide ordinary civil/criminal code and other 37-function
parameters remain named gaps; completing this package does not complete them.
