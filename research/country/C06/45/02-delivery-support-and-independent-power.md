# OP05–OP08: simplify support while keeping power challengeable

*Part of [C06/45](../45-country-operation-and-handover.md); design 122 candidate.*

**OP05 — Select a public backbone with usable plural routes.** Retain elected
local/national public operators for their actual assigned essential functions
and funded fallback; permit qualified public, cooperative, nonprofit and private
providers and voluntary person-directed routes within S/AS/H/MO/IC law. Do not
make a person become an employer or navigate competing contractors to obtain a
due service. A public fallback needs staff, stock, premises, working cash and
accepted receiving, not just a contractual right to call another provider.
Public ownership alone proves neither care quality nor democratic control.

**OP06 — Pool genuine support tasks, not independent decision authority.**
Default new administrative support to an existing compatible C04/local service
rather than automatically creating another agency: payroll processing, ordinary
procurement logistics, accessible receiving and records infrastructure may be
shared under lawful scoped service agreements. Existing statutory appointment,
budget proposal, protected access, publication, case and complaint powers remain
with their actual office. Separate staff, permissions, purchasing and records
where conflicts require it; shared hosting/payroll cannot permit Cabinet or a
vendor to read defense/source files, block an opposition payment or suppress a
report. Full PD/private life protections remain; no universal person database.

**OP07 — Independent remedies cannot be their own service manager.** OJ/CJ,
prosecution, EA/EL, AA, EV, PD and sector roles keep their exact qualifications,
selectors, sources, fixed terms, recusal, review and enforcement routes. Sharing
meeting rooms or accountants never makes judges, carers or independent assessors
interchangeable. A common contractor/controller is a shared failure/capture risk.
Preserve funded alternate access, exportable lawful records and a change route;
price these and specialist external capacity. No new national coordinator,
mandatory AI platform, inspector's policy veto or constitutional shortcut.

**OP08 — Retain a genuinely simpler whole alternative.** Compare territorial
direct-public provision with fewer provider interfaces and collective management,
still preserving voluntary consent, actual personal cash rights, AS non-employer
access, courts/opposition/elections and lawful private enterprise. It can reduce
contract/choice burdens but needs paid acquisition or formation, management,
worker cover and a usable response to monopoly failure. It may displace the
selected plural route if better qualified; do not assume its payroll is larger
or its quality worse. A residual cash-and-contract system is another distinct
rival: price private premiums/fees, public fallback and exit/nonclaim risk, not
just a smaller Treasury. Removing rights is a separate openly contested choice,
never an administrative simplification silently built into this comparison.

[CS16–CS17](../../C01/03/07-compulsory-burdens-and-country-disposition.md)
extends the comparison to new compulsory steps as well as offices. Lawful
simplification may remove duplicated navigation/proof, never independent
judgment by convenience. Retained human/other-provider access needs actual paid
source and cover; forecast digital savings cannot supply it. Existing competent
law, clocks, final-review independence and paid OP/T succession remain.

[DP](../../C11/09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md) now concretizes
C04 shared digital support and C10's existing technical-coordination hypothesis,
not a new national allocation or AI sovereign under OP07. No compulsory host,
master credential, sole appeal gate or new cross-sector stop power. Actual
institution-specific support/keys/judgment remain separately sourced where
common control would defeat a remedy. Count the actual shared job once.
