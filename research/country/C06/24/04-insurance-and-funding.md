# Defined deposit protection with an explicit public risk account

*M07–M08; part of [C06/24](../24-financial-law-and-loss-allocation.md).*

**M07 — Coverage is a limited legal claim.** The candidate covers ordinary natural persons and
nonfinancial enterprises/charities, prospectively per actual beneficiary per licensed legal
deposit-taking bank, up to twelve times B. B is the published national monthly ordinary adult
essential cash basket under I02, excluding individual extras and the separately reconciled housing
bill; it is not a guarantee of twelve months of every living cost. The legislature approves B's
method and revisions. The same standard limit applies to a legal entity/group; nominee/sole-trader
accounts do not multiply it. BF04 expressly replaces the earlier connected-bank-group unit:
different licensed banks can generate distinct capped insured claims, increasing maximum public
exposure; healthy sisters are not automatically failed. BF04 also states a six-month nonadditive
merger-coverage/usable-exit rule. No company-name change splits one legal bank debt or resets its
original event. Public treasuries, deposit-taking/financial intermediaries, investment/pension funds
and their own-account deposits are excluded; beneficial clients' separately proved property remains
their own.

Aggregate principal and accrued lawful interest once. Joint owners have
proved shares, equal only absent contrary evidence; trusts/omnibus accounts
look through to actual beneficiaries with protected usable records. Foreign
currency deposits use independently published failure-day conversion into
the domestic unit; no guaranteed foreign currency or old exchange rate.
Investments, own capital, bearer/anonymous unproved claims and token market
losses are not deposits. A fraud suspicion is not confiscation; disputed
ownership receives rapid advice/review and undisputed portions are paid.
No automatic netting against a loan not yet due or disputed tax debt.

The [BF01–BF04/RA07–RA09 reference](../39-bank-failure-and-protected-payment.md)
now expressly assigns the reasoned unavailability/coverage finding to a separate
statutory insurer claims officer, with direct ordinary-court determination on
omission. It does not require M09 exceptional critical-function entry; ordinary
U bank liquidation still pays covered claims. BF02 has a two-hour bank report
and six-hour officer decision; BF03 pins the earliest objective qualifying event itself,
with reasoned insurer finding and independent court correction. An effective
closure/liquidation order finding inability also crystallizes eligible insurance
claims, including undemanded term deposits, without accelerating estate
contracts or promising unearned interest. M07 payout clocks run from that
T0, with one-B/undisputed advance≤24actual hours and the remaining undisputed
covered balance as soon as possible/≤seven working days, crediting once.
Missing identity/share facts leave a protected claim and do not block an
undisputed portion. No new government rescue vote or readiness reset.

**M08 — Insurance does not make the risk disappear.** Mandatory member banks
pay an annual 0.4% of their average actually covered balances into a separate
fund, toward a 2% covered-balance target within five years. Publish actual
fund costs, liquid assets, exposures and shortfalls; collection and hoped-for
investment return are not existing reserves. No automatic premium remission
while a lawful public seed/advance remains unpaid. Changes in premium/target
require legislation, incidence review and actual bank credit/service effects.

Before protected deposit-taking/growth, the Assembly authorizes a Treasury
liquidity commitment covering the maximum current insured liability less
already usable fund assets; no recovery forecast is deducted. It records the
full contingent exposure, financing source, maturity, pricing, repayment and
loss bearer. Draws need that enacted ceiling and actual due cash, not a new
central-bank overdraft or a receiver's use of the service budget. Growth
beyond funded lawful headroom cannot be marketed as newly insured capacity.
The protection claim survives a delivery failure; publish the arrear and seek
lawful elected funding, never silently lower coverage for existing claims.

The insurer subrogates only actual payments and has M10's depositor rank.
Recoveries and future member levies repay public advances where lawful;
any unrecovered public loss is explicit, not assumed repayable or free money.
A 2% fund is not enough for every systemic loss. This substantial contingent
public commitment can displace other uses; the narrow-payment rival avoids
some bank credit insurance while changing lending costs and access. Actual
source financing and a whole-country choice remain necessary before launch.

[BF05–BF08](../39/02-ownership-backstop-and-drawn-debt.md) now defines M08's maximum still-unpaid exposure and total outstanding facility ceiling: usable undrawn headroom L−D must cover max(0,C−F), with actual due-date cash separately. Already drawn/unrepaid D and interest persist; future recoveries do not free the ceiling. SF social-insurance/public standby and M11 compensation are not deposit-insurer F.
