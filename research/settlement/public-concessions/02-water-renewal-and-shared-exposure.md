# A water asset, two delivery modes and one shared shock

*Authored counterfactual. All costs, contracts, dates and capacities are
assumptions. Monetary units are nominal, with no inflation or
discounting in the table. This is a cash and authority screen,
not a full economic appraisal or an actual budget.*

## Match the service and the opening state

A public water authority must preserve the existing statutory safe
service. Assume that an inspected repair can sustain it during the
period, with fewer operational options than the proposed renewal.
Two renewal options target the same accepted treatment capacity,
outage coverage, user tariff, commissioning date and asset condition
at the end of year 5. They use the same defined physical inputs.
Assume the local public works bid is denominated in domestic currency
and its specified price and security hold in this particular shock.
Neither option changes the user's tariff or the public authority's
legal duty. The initial capital window is 20 units. The relevant
annual payment window is 20 units in each of the following five
years before the other payments described below. The windows have
distinct authorizations and no automatic transfer.

| Choice | Year 0 | Annual ordinary project payment, years 1–5 | Five-year ordinary total including year 0 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Retain and repair | 7 | 7 | 42 |
| Publicly financed renewal and separate competitively bought work | 24 | 8 | 64 |
| Privately financed renewal and maintenance bundle | 4 | 11 | 59 |

The repair route does not buy the improved outage performance.
Its actual access and maintenance duties are already protected.
Direct renewal needs four more units at year 0. The private bundle
requires four units at year 0, but the supplier also seeks an
exceptional exchange-rate guarantee with a legally payable maximum
of three units in each of years 2, 3 and 4. If its conditions are met
and all three caps are called, the five-year continuing-service total
becomes 68. The elected body can refuse the guarantee, ask for a
new bid or consider it on its own terms with a paid source. The
supplier's original price cannot be assumed after the guarantee
is removed.

## Trace the simultaneous cash demand

Continuing unrelated obligations already use seven units annually
in the same payment window. They include a contract exposed to the
same exchange-rate movement through an existing capped guarantee.
In the tested year 3 shock that older guarantee requires another
three units. The new bundle plus the existing obligations need
18 units in the ordinary year 3 and 24 when both guarantees are
called. The extra four units cannot come from the capital window
by assumption or from a separately protected service.
On the stated prices and sources the direct renewal would require
18 in the shocked year 3, with its unresolved year 0 gap unchanged.

In a separate branch, the new provider has already received the
11-unit payments for years 1 and 2 and the year 2 guarantee of
three units. Default at the start of year 3 terminates its future
operating and guarantee payments. The independently reviewable
maximum compensation for this branch is 18 at termination.
An accepting public replacement needs seven more units in year 3
and eight in each of years 4 and 5. The same currency shock calls
the unrelated older guarantee in year 3. The year 3 cash need
is then 18 compensation plus seven for actual continuity plus
ten in old obligations, or 35. The cash gap is 15. Across the
new project path the termination branch costs 70 if the full
compensation cap is payable, including replacement and prior
payments. Do not add the ended future
operating and guarantee stream to the termination amount.

These are two stated event paths. A real authorization must also
bound other permitted triggers, years and combinations and name
what can be verified, what remains uncertain and who accepts delivery.
A disputed cost to complete requires independent review. Neither
an investor's claim nor a provisional estimate certifies safe
handback or supplies replacement money.

## Make the decision available without stopping the service

The capital and recurring screens expose different constraints.
Choose verified retained repair for the stated opening budget,
while the elected body considers the capital gap, a reworked
concession or a different funded operator. The condition is that
the old route actually meets the existing duty. If it fails, use
its earlier safe continuity and remedy routes. Bring the unpaid
exposure and applicable operator evidence to members and the
independent reviewer before commitment. The extra source,
different proposal or explicit retained repair is the elected
decision. There is no automatic tariff surcharge, bridge renewal
or displacement of an unrelated standing duty.
