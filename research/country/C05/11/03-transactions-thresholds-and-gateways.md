# Transactions, measurable gates and essential routes

Design 98 · Proposed research, not enacted law or verified operation.

**MP11 — Merger merits.** A concentration is acquisition of effective control of an independent
undertaking, asset business or durable joint control. Prohibit or condition it only on evidence
that, against a documented no-transaction counterfactual, it is likely to substantially and durably
reduce independent buyer, seller, worker or potential-entry constraint in a defined domestic
activity. Prove likely price/wage, quality, innovation, output or access harm persisting at least 12
months within the following 36, and assess entry, verifiable transaction-specific efficiencies,
small-firm survival and less restrictive remedies. A high share, foreign owner, political viewpoint,
small target, data stock or merger filing alone is not enough. Benefits to one group are not
silently netted against a distinct harmed group.

**MP12 — Measurable reportability.** The adopting Act must name a lawful initial `B`; thereafter use
the lawfully published M07/I02 `B` on **1 January of the year of the first binding agreement,
legally operative public takeover offer or control-conferring acquisition step**, or the latest
prior lawful value if that year's update is missing. For first-year benchmark events before the
first 1 January with a lawful published B, use the Act's initial B instead. Freeze it for that
transaction and set `T = 12B` (one ordinary adult annual essential-cash basket). A missing update
neither makes the threshold zero nor delays another due right; no first lawful `B` means this
numerical gate has not lawfully commenced. Domestic nexus means either party actually sells to
domestic recipients, buys labor from domestic workers, operates a domestic facility, holds a
domestic activity licence or serves a domestic due essential route at that first step or in the
previous 12 months. A concentration with that nexus is reportable before completion when the
**combined independent-party control groups' domestic attributed turnover in the preceding completed
12 calendar months before that first step is at least `10,000T` and the acquired target/business has
at least `500T`**. The target's turnover is counted once, including acquired business lines; current
internal group transfers are eliminated. Domestic turnover follows actual domestic sales/earned
financial income under MP13, **not the amount of labor purchased**; a foreign parent is not counted
by worldwide sales alone. No annualization of a startup's few months or value of unpaid users. No
agency invents B or retroactively flips a transaction after revision. Interdependent steps already
required by one binding control arrangement are one concentration; count the entire planned
target/business once before its first control transfer, not each instalment. A genuinely new later
acquisition is assessed prospectively at its own first binding step under its then-applicable gate;
it cannot retroactively make a lawfully completed earlier step gun-jumping. Actual decisive control,
not an arbitrary minority-percentage label, decides whether a minority purchase is reportable. A
binding deal predating MP commencement but not completed uses commencement as the first benchmark
date under MP20; no pre-commencement consummation is retrospectively reportable.

**MP13 — Financial and foreign measurement.** For ordinary firms, turnover is third-party gross
earned sales attributable to domestic delivery, net of legally charged sales tax, actual returns and
intra-group double count, not profit, asset price or global revenue. For deposit takers/payment
firms use earned domestic interest plus fees/commissions, **not deposits, client property, payment
flows or central-bank liquidity**; for insurance use earned premiums on domestic risks, not reserves
or claims liabilities. Mixed groups sum each distinct stream once. Foreign-denominated sales are
valued only for the legal trigger at independently published transaction-date rates; this neither
makes FX available nor changes a creditor's currency right. Disclose accounting period and group
changes; necessary source estimates are answerable in court, and an unknown number is not zero. For
multisided intermediation/advertising, attribute earned sales to underlying domestic transaction or
audience delivery through lawful aggregate accounts; neither fictitious prices for unpaid users nor
new personal tracking is authorized.

**MP14 — Narrow prospective gateway gate.** Independently of turnover, acquisition of a
**pre-designated** operator's effective control is reportable if, for the prior 12 months, it
controlled the sole or one of at most two actually usable domestic routes to an already legally due
essential payment, communications, energy, water or transport service in a defined service area, and
another route cannot be built/used within 30 days at lawful funded capacity. Markets authority gives
source evidence, affected-user/worker input, notice, reply and ordinary-court review; designation
first takes effect 90 calendar days after final notice, lasts at most 12 calendar months from that
effective date and is reviewed at six calendar months from that date. The independent gateway gate
applies only if that designation was effective at the transaction's MP12 first binding event. A
genuinely binding private contract before effectiveness is not retroactively reportable through
later announcement/closing; a loose nonbinding announcement reserves no earlier benchmark. A deal
reportable at that event keeps that original filing/clock duty even if designation later expires;
renewal needs fresh reasons and prospective notice, never a revived clock on the same deal. A
designation proposed after a transaction is publicly announced does **not** retroactively make that
transaction reportable; no viewpoint/content or all-platform designation is allowed. Assembly may
revise the enumerated service scope by law. The designation itself gives no tariff, ownership,
censorship or tax power. A qualified preassigned statutory career decision/hearing officer, separate
from investigators and including an actually qualified MA05 inaugural case officer, makes the
reasoned designation without a chief or senior-manager signature; MA ordinary review applies. Keep
designation and its grounds in a safely inspectable public register before effectiveness.

*Part of [the competition statute](../11-competition-and-economic-power.md); normative thresholds and institutions, no authority from publication.*
