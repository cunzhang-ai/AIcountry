# Design470 continuing commitments

Keep the [source service and original assessment access](../opening-budget-and-public-choice/02-source-service-before-release.md)
through a proposed cash reallocation. Preserve water and care during
[start-up and restoration](../essential-inputs-and-operating-release/02-commissioning-during-ordinary-service.md).
Keep each [currency obligation and actual return](../opening-debt-and-cash-window/02-collateral-before-service-release.md)
on its date. Actual authority continues to follow prior law. Record
independent review separately from performance. Preserve accrued claims,
original service dates and lawful replacement if a source fails.
