# C06. Financing interpersonal safety

*Part of [C06 public finance](../C06-public-finance.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

The Assembly funds C07's confidential first receiving, clinical care,
independent advocacy, safe communication, accessible shelter, transport and
continuing housing and payment routes. C08's legal powers also need police
response, evidence storage, prosecutors, courts, respondent and survivor
counsel, interpretation, order service, monitoring, independent inspection
and executable remedies. A statute that creates an order without evening
staff, safe location, judicial time and enforcement is not a safety system.

The consolidated account distinguishes ongoing care and housing from
time-sensitive surge capacity, and the person helped from the person
restricted. It counts the same clinician, vehicle, safe bed, advocate, court
slot and school or care worker only once across disaster, mental-health and
interpersonal-safety commitments. Sparse places and cross-boundary moves need
an equalized receiving and reimbursement rule. A shelter contract must cover
safe staffing, accessibility, confidential records, child and disability
support and an alternate receiver if the operator closes; cheap empty beds
are not delivered protection. Public and nonprofit providers may coexist,
but a contracted advocate does not gain police or judicial authority.

Cost and incidence analysis includes interrupted work, replacement income,
relocation, legal aid, property and debt claims, child arrangements and the
burden on people whose household controller holds all liquid funds. Safety
cannot be financed by a fee charged at first contact or by counting unpaid
family care as reserve capacity. Report actual timely access, safe placement,
order service and execution, repeat harm, wrongful restrictions and complaint
repair by place and affected group with strict privacy. No invented number
certifies readiness; existing contracts, staff and legal claims need a lawful
transition before commencement.
