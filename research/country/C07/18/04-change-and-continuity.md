# AS13–AS16: the support relationship can end without either person losing rights

*Part of [C07/18](../18-person-directed-assistance.md); design 116 candidate.*

**AS13 — Refusing a worker is not dismissing an employee.** A recipient can end
unwanted intimate contact or home access immediately and request a replacement,
without first proving misconduct, accepting mediation or facing a loss of care.
The employer separates that assignment change from any employment decision.
Keep allegations, safe receiving and pay distinct. Temporary non-contact is not
a guilty finding, permanent blacklist or automatic summary dismissal. No order
compels a specific worker to provide intimate care or the person to admit them.
An employer cannot avoid equality/fair-work duties by attributing all decisions
to the customer. Review independently proved recipient wrongdoing separately.

**AS14 — A funded fair exit.** Employer reassignment, agreement or lawful
termination handles a broken assignment. For these assistance contracts,
employer termination requires a stated genuine reason, accessible notice, the
worker's opportunity to respond and independent B/TW challenge. Protect union
activity, lawful complaint, unsafe-work refusal and civil equality. Assess
reasonable accommodation and feasible reassignment; do not invent another job
or indefinite guaranteed employment. Actual support need ending, genuine role
redundancy or a broken direct-employment relationship can justify fair termination
without declaring either person blameworthy.

Select at least 28 calendar days' ordinary-pay notice or pay instead for employer
termination, with stronger existing/statutory/contract terms preserved. Summary
termination without that notice requires proved serious misconduct, fair grounds
and process; actual earned wages and other accrued rights remain due. A disputed
allegation alone is not proof. The source package includes lawful covered exit
costs under AS12; known accepted longer terms remain, not a new 28-day cap on old
claims. A worker may resign under lawful terms; unsafe/impossible notice cannot
be specifically enforced as forced care. Safe public receiving persists.

Plan paid handover/redeployment/recruitment before switching funding; maintain
lawful wages and necessary overlap. No recipient is forced to keep the assignment
until the labor dispute ends; no worker is forced to waive dues to release care
money. New optional terms above an unaccepted source commitment are not silently
guaranteed by AS12, but an actual employer's lawful contract remains enforceable.

**AS15 — Hospital, moves and death do not erase contracts.** On hospital admission
review actual continuing communication/personal-support tasks, clinical boundaries,
pay/leave and discharge continuity rather than cancel all assistance. Do not
invoice the same supplied task twice. On an internal move the originating public
care payer/receiver keeps due protection until an actual lawful funded handover
is accepted; authority boundaries do not reset assessment or make the person wait
for a new diagnosis. Host authority supplies urgent receiving under S/E while
interauthority cost disputes are settled separately. This creates no general
foreign-export, immigration or free-housing entitlement.

On death or an ended need, separate remaining support, actual wage/notice/contribution
and supplier obligations, lawful estate claims, restricted service balances and
safe record succession. Public AS12 backing covers only its defined dues, not
every estate/provider debt. Settle/retain known actual commitments before returning
an uncommitted balance; do not make relatives employers, debtors or compulsory
administrators. The estate's separate source-law liabilities and actual employer
claims remain, without duplicate recovery or a silent funding transfer.

**AS16 — Backup needs an available human and a safe place.** The care authority
arranges accepted paid shifts, necessary tasks/qualifications/equipment, transport,
contact, records and ordinary/asymmetric provider/payment failures. Plan withdrawal
of informal help, employee absence/strike, night/two-person tasks, sparse places
and common-control/vendor failure. Respect lawful labor action and bargaining;
this statute grants no standing compulsory-service exception. Actual safe
alternatives need willing qualified workers and prior lawful means, not requisition.

Use E09–E12's earlier safety time and two-/six-hour outer receiving clocks, not
new waiting allowances. Shared standby is usable only if concurrent commitments,
travel/rest and actual acceptance permit. No duplicate worker/bed, free relative,
AI companion or paid invoice fills a missed shift. If all lawful options fail,
continue available protection and independent correction, preserve the unmet claim
and seek an elected source/capacity choice. No care-office order creates cash,
staff, confinement, generic property diversion or a lawful delay of an election.

[XR](../../C04/09-cross-local-service-continuity.md) assigns the wider
cross-local route and a separately enacted advance. AS15's continuing
duties and AS16's earlier receiving protections apply.

[XR11–XR15](../../C04/09/03-recurring-places-and-portable-support.md)
supports recurring care across more than one place under the active
award. Pay the ordinary and changed delivery and verify each episode
without treating a new service location as a new residence decision.
