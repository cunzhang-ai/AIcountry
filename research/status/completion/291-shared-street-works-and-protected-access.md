# Design383. Keep a neighborhood accessible during interacting public works

[SW01–SW06](../../country/C09/11/01-shared-street-works-and-protected-access.md)
assigns a joined works calendar, accessible receiving conditions,
attributable costs and qualified independent acceptance. Shared
work does not imply shared critical infrastructure.

[The constructed schedule and original records](../../settlement/shared-street-works-and-protected-access/01-two-routes-and-three-works.md)
expose an overlap, an unstaffed inspection and an unfunded alternative.
Actual geometry, bids, affected input and staffed delivery remain open.
