# DP29–DP32. Switch operators without losing decisions, payment or review

*Design220 · Part of [public digital operation](../09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md).*

**DP29 — Transfer the operating role and the pending obligations.**
The lawful sector owner activates the accepted ordinary or successor
route by the last safe start, using DP09–DP11 and FT. Identify the
responsible writer, decision-maker, payment officer and review receiver
for each task and time. Retain scoped access to prior evidence,
continuing payments and pending notices. Revoke expired credentials
when authority ends. Parallel observation or safe assistance can
continue. Parallel binding decisions or repeated payments for the same
act need prevention through nonoverlapping authority and actual
settlement reconciliation. A failed supplier has no right to retain
public power. A public operator has no power to seize private systems
because a handover is late.

**DP30 — Reconcile old and new events under their governing law.**
Use original events, purpose-specific identifiers and authoritative
status to distinguish a duplicate message, a distinct claim, a
pending obligation and an actually completed act. An acknowledgement,
last timestamp or inference from one model is insufficient proof.
Do not reset first receipt, replay a payment merely because the
receipt is missing, or erase a correction that was lawfully executed.
Route irreconcilable events and unsupported meaning to a competent
human with the protected original and the earlier useful time.
Keep urgent protection and undisputed help available through their
existing authority. Verify actual paid or received relief. Close the
transfer only after access, exceptions, privacy and remedy are accounted
for. Handback or rollback needs the same checks on intervening rights.

**DP31 — Pay for acceptance and keep the budget answerable.** The
responsible procurer distinguishes contract readiness, actual exercise,
paid overlap, activation and real service. A separately contracted
exit-readiness milestone is earned at its stated verified scope.
Follow SC14 for accepted undisputed invoices and ordinary remedies
for defects. Price retained public expertise, licenses, hardware,
data movement, power, independent receiving and courts in the
matched service budget. Show what the ordinary work loses when
assigned to transfer. Under AA and PE, opposition and independent
review can challenge material costs and control using protected
records. No assumed future saving, supplier damages or unpaid help
funds fallback. A failed check prompts actual lawful replacement,
repair or budget choice, with current due claims visible.

**DP32 — Keep the exit architecture itself replaceable.** Sector owners
may use common tools, plural hosted support, local providers or
in-house operations if real lawful exit and review survive. Sharing
a tool does not merge public decision or evidence custody. AI may
compare records, flag semantics at risk and assist accessible notice.
A person can use the ordinary route. A model or vendor cannot
approve a new policy, its own successor or its own readiness. Preserve
actual public authority, private and worker rights and paid transfer
under OP/T. Reopen when dependency, scope, harm, source or supplier
control changes, an affected person cannot get relief, or a simpler
route serves better. This proposal creates no new enforcement office
or general takeover power.
