*Design524 · National accountability choice and constructed comparison*

The [existing exit rules](../../country/C10/01/05-expiry-and-paid-handoff.md)
stop expired instructions and preserve lawful care, pending claims and useful

## Credit working inquiries in existing systems

Canada's Emergencies Act section 63 requires the Governor in Council to
cause an inquiry within 60 days of expiry or revocation and requires the
report to be laid before both Houses within 360 days. Canada used this
mechanism in 2022 and later published a government response to the inquiry's
recommendations. It gives a real comparator for timed public inquiry and
response. These records do not establish that any individual claim was
remedied or that a comparable event would call for the same decision here.
[Canadian statute](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/E-4.5/section-63.html)
· [Government response](https://www.publicsafety.gc.ca/cnt/rsrcs/pblctns/2024-pblc-rdr-mrgncy-rcmmndtns/index-en.aspx).

Australia's Royal Commission on National Natural Disaster Arrangements
used public submissions and hearings to examine cross-government
preparedness, response and recovery. The national agency publishes
implementation reporting. Credit a wide independent inquiry where the
needed powers, knowledge and work cannot be provided by existing review.
Its reporting scope is distinct from a particular resident's received
support or the legal validity of a particular act.
[Commission report](https://www.royalcommission.gov.au/natural-disasters/report)
· [Implementation reporting](https://www.nema.gov.au/about-us/governance-and-reporting/reviews).

The UK Commons committee found that the Coronavirus Act's section 98
review motion could not require particular temporary provisions to end
separately. It also identified restrictions authorized by other legislation
outside that review. This gives a comparator for reviewing cumulative
effects and recording separate decisions on measures that can operate alone.
[UK Commons committee report, paragraphs 79–87](https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/cm5801/cmselect/cmpubadm/377/37707.htm).

## A deadline passes while an emergency restriction still affects people

Use a constructed regional access case. A temporary order expires at hour
zero. Voluntary transport and care retain ordinary authority. The state
publishes a valid expiry notice. The old case marker is still used by a
local contractor, one ordinary payment office and an accessible transport
desk. A replacement AI summary treats that marker as a still-current
restriction. An existing application and court hearing remain on their own
time. A qualified independent review verifies the original order and old
marker and identifies people who could not enter the main route. Keep
protected sources with their lawful custodians. Test the same people with
and without the affected model and compare a qualified manual route.

| Test on the original clock | Valid result |

| Hour 2 direct review | A resident reaches an independent receiver with the original date. Opening a file has no effect on a downstream restriction. |
| Hour 4 transport and payment | With independent authenticated scope, the transport desk accepts the resident's lawful trip. The named payment payer separately determines and executes the original lawful claim. |
| Hour 6 judicial protection | A usable order reaches each executor who can still apply the expired marker. The court verifies execution through its applicable process. The review body neither casts a vote nor decides a merits dispute. |
| First later demand | The independent fallback accepts the next eligible person despite losing the original platform. Track other overdue cases and repeat effects separately. |
| Days 30, 120 and 150 | The preliminary account, independent findings and elected response describe each live gap and funded remedy. An earlier missed trip remains a missed trip. |

An official notice and published report can coexist with failed receiving.
Suppose three originally affected people have lawful and independently
verified relief. Two receive it by their useful time and the third is
confirmed late. A hearing report can correctly say all three orders were
issued. Its resident-side report must still say only two timely remedies.
If the third person's timing is unknown rather than known late, publish a
supported range of two to three. Do not estimate how many additional people
were affected when the denominator is incomplete.

If a new law would retain one part of the earlier restriction while ordinary
transport funding continues, show the restrictive effect across both Acts
before the new vote. Debate that part separately from the independently
operable transport line. The after-action report's later deadline supplies
no reason to postpone evidence at the new decision or broader valid aid.

A separate recovery choice concerns households that lost schooling or paid
work during a lawful restriction. They may have no existing damages claim.
After funding two independently ordered reimbursements on their original
dates, compare additional learning or care sessions, a finite needs-based
payment and extra ordinary provision on the same remaining funds and staff.
Track which residents each route reaches and whether it displaces the next
due service. Approval of a new funded offer has its own lawful terms.

## Choose the independently executable arrangement

Compare strong ordinary expiry, audit, court and sector repair, the
precommitted after-action branch and a broader temporary inquiry. Give
ordinary repair full credit where it preserves originals, reaches the same
missed people, executes correction, protects independent civic opposition
and gives the legislature a genuine follow-up decision on time. Select the
preassigned branch on these assumptions if the implicated executive could
otherwise suppress the review record or refuse a funded docket and where
these protections can operate. A wider inquiry can win where independent
staff, authority and evidence scope actually qualify and urgent relief
remains paid separately. A superficially independent reviewer that depends
on the disputed model for evidence or the same official for access leaves
the affected scope unverified. Follow the original claims and lawful
substitute. AI may find stale references and help compare accounts after
lawful access, with restored status and field action checked independently.

An adopter still needs the lawful authority, real usable evidence,
affected participation, paying and receiving staff, funding and actual
follow-through. This case exercises the design, with no estimate of
comparative national performance.
