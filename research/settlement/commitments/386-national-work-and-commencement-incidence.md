# Design473 continuing commitments

Carry the [public payers, work and service clocks](../matched-national-work/03-who-pays-for-the-shorter-week.md)
through each proposed national labour standard. Price the second
stage against the funded first. Reconcile actual local transfers
and the source service before reassigning work. Retain valid
pay, time, due services and remedies through a fiscal loss or
handoff. A historical comparison grants no commencement authority.
