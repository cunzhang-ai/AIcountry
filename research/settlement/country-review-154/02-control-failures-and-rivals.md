Design154 · Authored legal-design counterexamples, not observed incidents or automated law

| Authored situation | Required distinction and continuing duty |
|---|---|
| Minister asks for better response to repeated violent crime | Lawful public resource/priority choice remains possible; enacted powers, equality and contrary evidence still govern each case. Operational independence is no general policy veto. |
| Minister privately orders arrest of an opposition organizer | No political case instruction; actual P09 grounds/necessity and LF still required. Preserve instruction/refusal source and direct external protection; civic dissent is no offense. |
| Chief has an operation plan but no search ground | Plan/office supplies no P07 warrant or broader surveillance. Independent judge/existing narrow urgent exception retain exact scope/time. |
| Prosecutor requests a search or a charge quota | Evidence direction cannot become judicial authority or conviction control; OJ/P insufficiency, independent defense/courts and no punishment bounty remain. |
| Case moves between regions or into custody | Preserve original complaint/custody clock, qualification and accepted receiver. A new commander, clinician or software identifier restarts no period. |
| Chief accused; different region offers to investigate under that Chief | Different geography is no outside-command independence. Civilian Investigation Service needs actual separate employment/source/control; Chief cannot choose or close its own inquiry. |
| Investigation Director or relevant outside reviewer implicated | Prior neutral accepted outside-command allocation and unimplicated court/prosecutor route, not a post-incident ministerial friend or Director credibility gate. An allegation alone is no guilt/suspension finding. |
| Both chiefs, all actual alternates or sole source controllers implicated/unavailable | No imaginary fourth officer, foreign power or private force. Preserve urgent records/liberty/claims, record failed receiving and use only actual lawful routes/prospective replacements. No immunity arises. |
| Investigator has a different letterhead but accused owns sole payroll/key/forensic access | Written separation fails its actual source/delivery condition. Direct court/AA/PD and authorized outside-conflict execution need paid usable access; a model graph is not real access. |
| New government wants lawful priorities changed | Follow elected law/appropriation; protected heads/staff cannot veto. A party purge or demand to conceal an inconvenient lawful case remains impermissible. |
| Accused officer says worker protection forbids inquiry | Fair career/defense rights stay, but do not block lawful independent evidence/prosecution. Immediate evidenced safety removal is bounded, paid and reviewable, no presumed guilt. |
| Head's personal term ends with cases open | Reserved head acts expire, lawful officers keep their actual own case powers and original dates. No consultant/credential/deputy title extends the head. |
| Acting deputy passes180 elapsed days | Routine substitute head authority ends without reset; officers/record/pay/custody duties continue only under their own enacted roles. No actor inherits all head powers. |
| Custody sentence/order expires while housing or clinic has not accepted | Actual release is due under original P/CD/HP law; funded safe support remains an unmet separate claim, never an extra prison term. |
| Shared AI dispatch labels a crisis as dangerous | No new offense, force ground, clinical consent or competent worker. P06 accepted triage and separate actual public/clinical/peer powers remain. |
| Police refuse a court order and Cabinet calls for soldiers to take over | PO grants no military domestic fallback. Use actual authorized unimplicated civilian executor and independent source access; if none exists record failed execution, continuing rights and public responsibility. |
| One specialist promised for simultaneous abuse inquiry, urgent protection and custody | Actual skills/window/nonoverlap source must fit; a job label or annual FTE cannot establish all three. Do not increase force, extend custody or delete a complaint to balance staffing. |
| Both approval thresholds met by nominal opponents sharing patrons | Conditional votes can pass while actual independence is unproved; retain lawful opposition votes and investigate shared control rather than ideologically disqualifying critics. |

## Competing institutional choices

Strong retain/repair with C04 protected careers could cost less and may be
adequate in context. Local police can know communities and entrench local
patrons/unequal resources; national administration can equalize and concentrate
force. Elected chiefs improve a direct vote link but can chase punitive case
incentives; Cabinet-only chiefs simplify responsiveness and expose case control.
Separate inquiry reduces one direct hierarchy and adds scarce skill/source/
handoff burdens, while all roots can still share patrons or money. Unarmed
civilian/peer/clinical pathways can reduce police dependence but need accepted
paid safe receiving and their own authority. No scalar proves PO superior.

No actual authority, independent reviewer, source, victim, accused official,
opposition behavior or public judgment is claimed. These counterexamples
test distinctions and failures; they do not demonstrate a staffed state or
better lives. Whole country/source/transition remains the unit of next work.
