# Design507 decision record

**Basis and choice.** Continue the [Design506 baseline](baseline.json)
under the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md). Select an ordinary
working legislative, court and election route with independently
controlled alternatives for exposed functions. Prove protected
draft production and filing, timely review and performed remedy,
and actual authorized handover through the next call. The
[rehearsal and decision](../settlement/democratic-rehearsal/01-prove-the-right-to-oppose-and-replace.md)
compare shared recovery and scoped paid independent production
without creating a new certificate or veto.

**Objections and proof.** Separate routes may be more expensive
and can still share control, skills or appointment dependencies.
Use a cheaper qualified ordinary repair when it covers the same
protected endpoints. A witnessed exercise does not prove that an
actual election is fair, that individual rights are secure or
that government can always be replaced. Parallel source analysis
supplies neither affected participation nor independent final review.
The [sources](sources.md) preserve the separate empirical scopes.

**Compatibility and reopening.** Preserve original mandates, votes,
legal deadlines, private deliberation, evidence and live service
claims. Use the applicable relief and lawful predecessor where
authority is unresolved. Reopen on controller concentration,
late or unauthenticated receipt, lost remedies, a real complaint
or an equivalent lower-cost route. An actual competent adopter
must fund staffed independent rehearsal and receive evidence.
No real mandate, legal rule or practical performance was created.

**First-round national selection.** The [first settlement](../FIRST-ROUND-SETTLEMENT.md)
and [mandate and supply decisions](../settlement/first-round-mandate/01-decisions-and-conditions.md)
consolidate the constitutional, fiscal and service choices for public review.
Select lawful retrofit, a replaceable national parliament and elected
local bodies, ordinary repair at opening, and exact separable votes where
authority, approved sources and receiving actually qualify. A stronger cash
foundation, direct public provision or mutual provision may displace the
material choice on credible evidence and the same rights floor. National
list proportionality trades territorial-person mandates for equal access
to the same allocation. Preserve candidate pluralism and reopen the form
on evidence of recurring territorial exclusion or exhausted legislative capacity.

**Handoff and participation.** Three agent workstreams reviewed the
democratic chain, legal transition and operating constraints. They used
shared project evidence and supplied no real constituency or affected-party
decision. The first instruments need a defined lawful public, jurisdiction,
actual constitutional routes, locations and appointments, dated financial
sources and receiving partners. No public or legal disposition or real
test of service performance was available. On a real adopter, the next
bounded output is a scope and continuity schedule with protected first-contest
texts, funded independent capacity and a decision by the competent prior
forum. Reopen the working selection if those inputs conflict, the adversarial
test fails or a stronger feasible alternative changes the opening choice.
