# A production plan needs buyers, inputs and a bearer of failure

**PW05 — Compare the same serviceable output before selecting a supply form.**
Use PB124's age-weighted **1,213,200 garment items/year** only as a reference
quantity, not paid orders or a shopping command. The new authored recipes have
explicit work, imported materials/finished goods, power/water, sites, equipment,
paid learning, renewal, gross opening finance and full annual payroll. They do
not infer unit recipes from DOM24's unmatched historical hours/sales. Garment
quality, sizes, accessibility, repairability, ecological effects and actual
buyer choices remain unresolved; a generic item cannot certify equivalence.

At CU/FU=1 and full paid reference demand:

| Named offer | Local making and retail | Finished import and retail |
|---|---:|---:|
| Worker posts / additional instructors | 874 / 5 | 132 / 1 |
| Annual gross pay due, million CU | 26.52 | 4.02 |
| Consumer unit offer including VAT, CU | 36.01 | 26.26 |
| Normal imported inputs/finished goods, million FU | 9.7056 | 24.264 |
| Gross named startup finance before refunds, million CU | 11.89968 | 3.6592 |

These alternative offers neither displace actual firms nor prove these distinct
workers are available. Protected task capacity is 1,110.6 hours per paid year;
actual rosters, worker choice, instructor competence and minimum-pay compatibility
need qualification. Imported finished goods also embody foreign work and harms;
their exclusion from domestic payroll is no evidence of lower worldwide cost.

**PW06 — A target return is not a purchase commitment.** Price the authored
full-capacity offer with a 4% pre-company-tax target on named opening equity;
this is a contestable return target, not deductible interest or guaranteed profit.
All-equipment five-year depreciation is an explicit tax/renewal hypothesis.
Capital imports are included once in asset quotes and reprice with FX. Include
gross initial inventory/VAT finance; actual credits/refunds and capital title
remain unknown. No prior-year company receipts are invented: conditional annual
assessments fall due next April, and retained profit is not received worker income.

At 60% paid demand and unchanged offer/pay obligations, named annual accounting
losses are about **11.4424m CU local / 1.7202m CU import**. Depreciation is not
a cash payment: the result separately exposes operating cash before stock,
capital, VAT and distributions. Opening finance is not a proved loss buffer.
At 120% demand, unserved quantity remains; no automatic overtime or new factory.
The 1.5 CU/FU sensitivity reprices named direct imports/capital only. It is not a
full FX-price/utility/lender equilibrium. Extra process meters are not qualified
against grid/source peaks, ecology or old “other” meters; do not book a net gain.

**PW07 — Retain plural supply; reject a job-count or cheap-price default.**
The domestic offer uses more local work/capital, costs more at these inputs and
needs less recurring FU. The import offer is cheaper here and externally more
dependent. Neither is selected as an actual national garment contract. Existing
PS/N/MP/WL/OE institutions support voluntary entry, safe bargaining and fair
competition. No production quota, forced import ban, guaranteed state purchase,
private-care labor reserve or automatic public loss guarantee follows.
