## The bounded omission and alternative diagnoses

The [design-38 coverage audit](predecessor-design-38/COVERAGE-AUDIT.md)
identified three real assignment gaps: no full sanitation/solid-waste
service chain or failure receiver, no bounded cyber incident command and
recovery chain, and no customs clearance/appeal split. These gaps concern
public responsibility, not an observation that a real operator failed.
Poor service in an actual place might instead result from money, land,
corruption, common suppliers, geography, law, user exclusion or weak
inspection. A new named office cannot by itself solve those causes.

[WHO's sanitation-safety planning](https://www.who.int/teams/environment-climate-change-and-health/water-sanitation-and-health/sanitation-safety/sanitation-safety-planning/)
traces risk from containment through conveyance, treatment and disposal;
[WHO's solid-waste account](https://www.who.int/tools/compendium-on-health-and-environment/solid-waste)
identifies health, worker and downstream harms, while [UNEP's 2024 waste
outlook](https://www.unep.org/resources/global-waste-management-outlook-2024)
compares waste-reduction and management pathways. [NIST's CSF 2.0](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework)
separates governance, detection, response and recovery across technology
contexts. The [WCO Revised Kyoto Convention](https://www.wcoomd.org/en/Topics/Facilitation/Instrument%20and%20Tools/Conventions/pf_revised_kyoto_conv)
provides customs risk-management and appeal comparators; its
[appeal chapter](https://www.wcoomd.org/en/topics/facilitation/instrument-and-tools/conventions/pf_revised_kyoto_conv/kyoto_new/gach10.aspx)
requires a path beyond customs itself. These are independent official
function descriptions, not original AIcountry field observations, treaty
membership, optimal institutional form or proof of readiness.

## Coherent institutional choices

| Function and package | Possible advantage | Material failure or conflict |
|---|---|---|
| Sanitation: retain water and health references without named full-chain steward | Low new structure | Sewers, on-site systems, waste handling and downstream disposal can each be someone else's problem |
| Sanitation: one national combined water/waste utility | A single funded command and possibly equalization | One provider can hide failures or crowd out local geography and worker knowledge; network and waste hazards still differ |
| **Selected: local sanitation and solid-waste stewards under national floors, equalization and separate inspection** | Complete service chains with local delivery and enforceable common protection | Weak places may lack technical capacity; shared contractors and funders can capture inspection |
| Cyber: each service responds alone | Clear local ownership | A common vendor, election/payment dependency or attack crosses sector boundaries before warnings travel |
| Cyber: central commander controls all public systems in incident | Rapid unified response | The incumbent or compromised center can suspend civic systems, suppress evidence or keep emergency power |
| **Selected: service ownership plus bounded national coordination and independent review** | Shares technical capacity while preserving each legal duty | Could be too slow if thresholds, staff and tested mutual aid are missing |
| Customs: dispersed border queues without shared clearance owner | Separate safety and revenue judgment | Nobody owns the total shipment clock, perishable custody or cross-agency contradiction |
| Customs: unified border command deciding tax, safety and protection | One release channel | A single command can convert a revenue or security hold into unreviewable exclusion, coercion or trade favoritism |
| **Selected: separate statutory decisions with coordinated clearance and independent appeal** | Reduces repeated filing while keeping legal powers and remedies distinct | Multiple offices may impose delay; a common digital window can still be a choke point |

The selected [C09 sanitation](../country/C09/06-sanitation-and-waste.md),
[C10 cyber](../country/C10/04-cyber-incident-and-recovery.md) and
[C10 customs](../country/C10/05-customs-and-border-clearance.md) rules are
separate choices. They should not be bundled into a new super-ministry.
Existing competent institutions may carry the proposed roles if their
actual law, independence and capacity satisfy the duty; the research does
not create offices or displace an incumbent statute.

## Democratic control, shared resources and continuity

The Assembly sets scope, floors, finance, inspection powers and appeal under
prospective law. Local elected bodies choose ordinary sanitation routes and
operators within those floors. A cabinet can replace accountable executives
under law but cannot direct inspectors to certify its own failed plant,
cyber staff to erase election evidence or customs to target political foes.
For each service, review appointment, protected removal, funding, access to
source records, enforcement and whether one party or vendor controls the
supposedly separate reviewer. Opposition, workers, downstream residents,
traders and excluded users must be able to present original evidence and
obtain a remedy that can actually be delivered. A court cannot create an
unfunded treatment plant, forensic team or inspection bay.

C06 must count shared staff, fuel, trucks, generators, sites, chemicals,
cloud dependencies, backup capacity, import duties, service subsidy and
appeal capacity once. C07 keeps health/essential-access duties; C08 keeps
lawful investigation and courts; C09 keeps network and logistics delivery;
C11 preserves evidence and data rights; C14 must assign pending duties,
workers, licenses, treatment liabilities, cyber records and held shipments
to funded lawful successors. The [C13 cross-system failure](../country/C13/04-boundary-service-failure.md)
tests whether the links deliver in the same physical event. A research
selection cannot change existing tariffs, waste permits, privacy law,
contracts, treaties, worker rights, claims or appeal deadlines.

The strongest sanitation objection is that national operation might better
protect weak localities. Equalization, national floors and intervention for
proved failure are proposed answers, but a real comparison may reject local
stewardship. The strongest cyber objection is that split legal authority may
waste hours during attack; the answer is preauthorized narrow technical
steps and drills, not broad political command. The strongest customs
objection is that appeal and multiple inspectors can strand food or
medicine; the answer is transparent time limits, security for disputed
revenue where lawful, manual records and a named clearance coordinator,
without overriding a real safety or protection claim. These objections are
retained; no affected-party deliberation occurred here.

C01–C14 files. Research selection: three bounded stewardship and review
routes. Legal authorization: none. Operational readiness: no mapped staff,
