# C09. Climate budgets and essential continuity

*Design239 · CBG01–CBG12 · [C09 land and ecology](../C09-land-and-ecology.md) · Proposed research law.*

Set enforceable emissions limits before investment and retirement
decisions. Keep gross emissions, verified removals, other gases
and external burdens distinguishable. An essential-service emergency
can require an immediate lawful response and a later correction.
Its emissions still enter the cumulative account.
