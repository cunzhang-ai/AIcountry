# Design441. Continue the same payment comparison through year two

Design440 is the pinned predecessor. Its day-90 test closes with
Treasury at the minimum and the three service payers at zero.
T85–T90 require a second-year tail, and FB42–FB47 already govern
the forward budget. This edition applies them to one continued
four-payer baseline. This revision used AI assistance and a
parallel design review.

The [worked comparison](../settlement/second-year-financing-and-service/01-four-payers-through-year-two.md)
selects retain and repair where cash fits on the original dates.
Phased preparation balances at year end but breaches the early
minimum. Accelerating requires more cash at both dates. A lawful
standby or a changed debt maturity can close the early timing
gap. Either must preserve the next minimum after its cost and
repayment. A repeated delay defeats the worked terms. Price both
options on actual terms before selecting either. Delaying a reform
can leave existing unmet needs, so record those effects and compare
a separately funded repair.

The [source record](sources.md) identifies the budget reference
and constructed scope. There is no actual year-one outturn for
this exercise, enacted reserve or appropriation, affected-party
participation, or independent qualified review. Keep existing
legal claims, account restrictions and review routes through
renewal and handover. Elected bodies choose sources and policy.
Independent analysis and lawful execution retain their own roles.

Use a real common closing ledger and disaggregate the first
recurring payment and repayment dates. Match staff and physical
receiving, independently verify a protected duty, and show the
earliest feasible optional release under a repeated shock.
Reopen on a different outturn, financing cost, missing
receiving, or a better funded alternative.
