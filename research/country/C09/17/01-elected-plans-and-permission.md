# LU01–LU05. Public rules govern development, not a private favor

*Part of [development permission](../17-development-permission-and-serviced-land.md).*

**LU01 — Elected plans and actual competence.** Existing local planning authority
prepares and Council adopts land-use plans/rules under LR, national rights and
C03–C04 scope. Actual material shared/territorial/foreign questions keep their
own competent route; no map makes a regional sovereign. Existing qualified C04
planning officers apply rules, give reasons and preserve records; no new board,
ministerial individual rezoning or public/private AI allocator is created.
This route applies where exact prior published law requires planning consent
for new building/subdivision/material use or intensity change. It adds no
universal certificate for existing lawful use, ordinary noncritical repair or
already-permitted minor activity; actual source exemptions remain.
Council controls general purposes, lawful budgets and plan amendments, not a
compliant applicant's political affiliation or bargaining payment.

**LU02 — Plan usable space, source and access together.** Publish actual allowed
uses/intensity/envelopes, protected nature/heritage/hazards, streets/access,
water/sanitation/power, housing/service needs, cumulative effects and feasible
repair/infill/expansion/no-change alternatives before adoption. Distinguish land
capacity on paper, titled usable land, financially/source-accepted infrastructure
and delivered safe occupation. Actual geometry/engineering/access standards need
lawful place-specific instruments; no universal lot size or height is invented.
Restrictions need a legitimate purpose, proportionate evidence, distributional
reasons and less-burden options, not an incumbent property-price objective.

Tenants, unconnected/new applicants, workers, downstream/receiving communities
and materially affected outsiders can contribute/challenge with accessible safe
source information. Existing residents retain actual local votes; affectedness
is no fictitious extra franchise, property-weighted poll or automatic consent.
Public evidence/meaning and conflicting values remain visible, not settled by
an AI's density/supply score. No compulsory shared household or relocation.

**LU03 — A conforming project has an enforceable applying entitlement.** Under
operative exact rules, grant the planning component when its defined tests are
met, with only LU07's lawful necessary conditions. A case officer has bounded
qualified judgment about actual facts, not an extra undefined 'public interest'
veto, ministerial favor or a fresh Council vote for every conforming project.
State each operative provision, source fact and permitted condition; publish
reasoned refusals and uncertainty. AP proof/help/reply and direct review apply;
missing facts/official silence are not automatic approval or invented compliance.

This entitlement covers public/private/cooperative proposals equally under actual
law. It does not compel an owner to build, promise market demand/finance, exempt
a public project from rules or guarantee rent/land value. A plan can itself be
exclusionary: applicants/affected people can challenge its lawfulness and seek
actual elected revision, not merely ask the same officer for a political waiver.

**LU04 — Limit exclusion, retain actual neighbor and access rights.** Ownership,
prior local residence, expected tax contribution or a private neighbor's approval
is no additional planning test for otherwise lawful ordinary homes. Protected
housing allocation and equality law retain their own scope. Actual title,
easement/covenant, nuisance, collective, heritage and safety claims remain;
planning approval does not extinguish them or guarantee an unchanged view,
property price or free public kerb space. A complaint needs a legal/source issue,
not a private general veto; genuine harm and required notice remain reviewable.

For new ordinary residential planning permissions under this Act, select no
mandatory minimum GENERAL private car-parking quota. Voluntary lawful parking
remains. Necessary accessible parking/drop-off, emergency/servicing and safe
journeys have independently applicable needs/standards, not a fraction of whatever
ordinary parking a developer chooses. No quota removal waives actual access,
creates a free curb entitlement or confiscates existing private parking rights.
Council still manages lawful public streets/parking under its proper authority;
public alternative journeys require actual capacity/source under MO, not a wish.

**LU05 — Separate the permissions before physical reliance.** The planning
decision identifies site/use/envelope/phase/conditions and the applicant's claimed
authority; no title or capacity reservation follows by application alone. If not
the actual holder, give required safe owner notice; a filing cannot encumber or
freeze the owner's lawful rival proposal. Actual ownership/access/collective
consent and LA acquisition where needed must precede work affecting those rights.
TD05 independent building plan/checks, ecological/WS/other source permissions
and safe occupation keep their own requirements, not an extra universal combined
licence. Planning suitability alone is no demolition, tenancy termination,
structural approval, utility connection, public appropriation or foreign right.
