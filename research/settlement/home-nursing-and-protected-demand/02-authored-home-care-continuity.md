# Compare care when demand grows at the handover

This authored exercise concerns a six-hour window and a real change
of legal service owner at its start. Numbers represent accepted
comparable task slots with their clinical and support qualifications
and feasible travel already allowed for in the case. This is a
planning comparison rather than a rule for an actual clinical visit.

The existing plan owes six nursing visits and ten daily support visits
in the window. A qualified clinician identifies three additional
nursing tasks that cannot safely wait beyond the same window.
The continuing teams offer seven nursing and eleven support slots.
The successor can activate an independently qualified external
reserve for up to two nursing visits. Its paid readiness is already
in the fixture's baseline. Each activation visit costs two separate
units and four units of cash are available at the right time.
Additional operating integration within the same window would
take one otherwise usable nursing slot for safe handover and
training. All variants keep the same protected support and require
actual consent, receiving and independent review.

| Route | Nursing by the safe time | Ordinary support by the safe time | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Continuing teams and no additional reserve | 7 of 9 | 10 of 10 | Two nursing visits exposed. |
| Immediate integration and two activated reserve slots | 8 of 9 | 10 of 10 | One nursing visit exposed even with four cash units spent. |
| Successor accepts the duty, continues qualified teams and activates both reserve slots | 9 of 9 | 10 of 10 | The stated duties fit if the handoff and reserve actually deliver. |
| Phased route after a donor constraint permits only one reserve visit | 8 of 9 | 10 of 10 | One nursing visit exposed despite unused cash. |

The modeled staged route keeps the legal transfer date and moves
the optional operating integration to a separately staffed and
funded window. It cannot count the available support slot as a
nursing visit. A separately funded receiving clinical site could
cover the remaining visit under donor loss if qualified, accessible
and timely, and acceptable to the person. No such site is supplied
by the case. Record the open duty, protect the person and invoke
the actual independent route. Any unused activation money remains
within the appropriation and is different from paid standby.

Reopen the ordinary budget if the higher demand persists.
Temporary reserve use and cheaper accounting do not establish
new recurring capacity. A fully staffed earlier integration or
a competent permanent split with a better accepting route may
outperform the staged candidate under different local facts.
