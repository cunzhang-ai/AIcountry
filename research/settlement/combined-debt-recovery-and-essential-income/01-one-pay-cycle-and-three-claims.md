# One pay cycle and three claims

*Design370 · Constructed household and enforcement comparison.*

A worker has 60 units of net pay received before essential bills of 55
fall due. A second 40 is expected before the end of the period. A further
25 of protected costs is then due. A separate 20-unit restricted award
covers 20 of disability costs and supplies no recovery capacity. None
of the same 20 costs is counted again in the 80 of ordinary needs. A
child in another household is owed 8 of current support by the later
date. Two confirmed old claims, 12 for tax arrears and 12 for a civil
judgment, have equal rank for this example. The priority assumption
describes the example and does not assert actual tax or judgment rank.

| Comparison | Actual result |
|---|---|
| Separate channel limits | With both payments received, each old collector sees 20 above the person's own needs and takes 12. If both take that amount and 8 of support is paid, the person keeps 68 against needs of 80. |
| One verified limit | The opening 60 cannot cover the period's protected needs of 80, so no early debt instruction is released. After the additional 40 arrives, 20 can be allocated. Current support receives 8 and each equally ranked old claim receives 6. The person keeps 80. |
| Later pay falls to 15 | The person receives 75 against protected costs of 80. Collection releases zero. The person's 5-unit gap and child's 8-unit unmet support remain distinct. Actual bridge eligibility, authority and money must be checked separately for each. |
| Judgment claim is subject to a stay | With both payments received, current support still receives 8. The tax claimant can receive its lawful 12. The stayed claim receives zero. |
| Earlier secured collection route | This case covers no seizure of a separate nonexempt asset. A lawful recovery or restraint against such an asset remains subject to its own property rights, priorities and judicial review. |

Suppose the matched collection paid 8, 6 and 6, but a bank additionally
executes a stale copy of the civil judgment instruction for 6. The
resident has 74 until the duplicate is returned. A proved return before
the bill deadline restores access to 80. A return after the deadline
restores the balance but leaves a missed essential payment. Treat the
duplicate as an overcollection, with claim credits corrected once and
no additional final entitlement for the creditor. A new worker,
bank or collector cannot restart the limitation period.

The [executable comparison](verify-combined-recovery.py) checks declared
cash, relative priority, equal-rank allocations, delayed receipts,
a stayed claim and return of a duplicate. It assumes accepted lawful
instructions except where expressly varied. It supplies no actual
household costs, tax rank, funded assistance or borrowing forecast.
