# Design507 sources and scope

- The [UK House of Commons Procedure Committee's published response report](https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/cm5801/cmselect/cmproced/565/56503.htm)
  documents different capabilities available under temporary hybrid
  proceedings, later limits on virtual participation and scrutiny
  concerns. It establishes a bounded institutional comparison.
  It supplies neither the candidate rules nor proof of an executable
  alternate in the proposed legislature.
- The [first sitting without its shared drafting system](../settlement/democratic-control-through-service-continuity/03-a-sitting-without-the-shared-workbench.md),
  [government handover and care](../settlement/government-handover-and-essential-service/01-one-handover-three-protected-deadlines.md)
  and [early electoral discovery](../settlement/electoral-discovery-and-direct-access/01-common-index-and-early-voting.md)
  are authored cases with distinct scopes and resources. They
  motivate the selected exercise. They do not supply real resource,
  election, remedy or handover observations.
- The [country resource account](../settlement/first-country-resource-account/01-the-first-budget-on-one-perimeter.md)
  identifies the original Danish reference series and preserves the
  separate authored commissioning conditions. The [election and supply
  case](../settlement/opening-resilience-and-public-choice/01-govern-and-deliver-without-the-common-channel.md)
  uses a constructed shared start day. Neither supplies an actual
  adopting country, payable budget, legal finding or court execution.
