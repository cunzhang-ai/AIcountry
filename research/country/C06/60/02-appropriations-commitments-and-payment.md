# DB06–DB10. A spending ceiling cannot conceal a different permission

*Part of [democratic budgets](../60-democratic-budget-and-execution.md).*

**DB06 — Vote named programmes and distinct permissions.** Annual schedules name
responsible legal authority, public purpose/affected scope, beneficiaries/service
floor, actual source, currency, amount/rules, release conditions, expiry and
reserved subceilings. State which provisions impose actual implementation
duties and which permit discretionary projects within an envelope; an ordinary
appropriation alone does not require spending its maximum. Separate new commitments, current payment permissions,
continuing statutory dues, capital/financial uses, guarantees and actual cash
needs; never treat them as interchangeable balances. Treasury executes, service
owner commits, separate qualified receiver verifies eligibility/delivery and
payer/accountant pays/reconciles under existing four controls/provisional law.

A giant miscellaneous envelope cannot hide a different programme, independent
opposition/court/audit task or changed eligibility/geographic obligation. Tie
public programme units to inspectable actual service/source duties; preserve
separate powers even where support is shared. A lawful output target guides
performance, not a condition that erases a person's standing claim. Forecast
volume/price may fail without changing legal duties; costed results are not cash.

**DB07 — Count the obligation when it is made.** A new multiyear contract/project
needs actual aggregate gross commitment authority for its full binding payment
and legally possible termination/exposure under the authorized terms, with dated
cash, currency, operating/maintenance/renewal and tail. Do not approve a100project
against a20first-instalment ceiling. Record actual lawful index/variation/risk
limits; an uncapped promise or side letter cannot evade a nominal project cap.
Mutually exclusive performance/termination paths are alternative maxima, not
automatically added together; actual concurrently possible liabilities still
count together. Preserve gross face commitments and conditional risks separately.
Guarantees/indemnities have separate gross contractual exposure and RF/financial
law mandate; expected loss is not their authorization or spendable reserve.

Show existing commitments against future schedules before further optional
awards; no contract, public enterprise, special vehicle, loan or lease supplies
an off-budget policy power. Private/foreign acceptance and qualified capacity
remain real. Actual full binding exposure, contingent exposure and annual cash
are reconciled lenses, not three copies of production or automatically additive
promises. A quoted unaccepted bid is not a binding public obligation.

Indefinite statutory/career employment uses authorized positions/conditions,
current paid periods and actual accrued wages/leave/notice/severance/pension
obligations plus longer projections, not capitalization of all possible lifetime
service as already purchased. WL/C04 employment/earned rights survive; DB cannot
force fixed-term hiring to obtain a nominal ceiling. For renewable service state
the actual minimum/notice/uncancellable term and any binding renewal tail. An
optional renewal is not yet a binding commitment, but necessary continuity/
replacement and its cost still need the actual source/delivery plan.

**DB08 — A commitment, due debt and payment authority differ.** Each multiyear
Act/schedule states whether/where it supplies continuing dated payment authority
or instead requires identified future appropriations; neither a commitment cap
nor a forecast creates unrestricted future cash authority. Applicable standing
law/valid court execution and protected provisional source remain. A lawful
contract/debt can survive expiry or failure of annual cash authority: record
and remedy it, not silently void it or declare the missing cash received.
Never solicit an unconditional binding commitment while concealing that its
specified lawful payment authority/source is absent; state actual risks and
seek competent funding before new discretionary awards, preserving older dues.

Standing benefits, staffed essentials, protected democratic/remedy duties and
other actual standing-schedule claims keep their exact law, eligibility and
payments; annual estimates are not hidden caps. If an estimated100standing
formula lawfully incurs110, report110due and its source gap, not deny the last10
or count a100annual line as repeal. Existing stronger law/contracts and actual
NP/E priorities remain. DB neither expands every old contract into essential
standing authority nor lets a new minister determine constitutional priorities.

**DB09 — Limited input discretion, no interprogramme transfer by executive.**
Within an enacted programme, a service authority may adjust input timing/mix
only inside its actual purpose, current/commitment/source/subceilings, legal
worker/procurement/safety conditions and reserved service/geographic duties.
No delegated transfer to another programme, purpose or legal authority, however
small, unless the specific prior primary Act already enacted that bounded
allocation formula. No changed beneficiary/rights, independent source, restricted
fund, future exposure or material access effect by calling it an input change.
Prior specific RF/CP/sector permissions retain their own resources/limits; DB
adds no general reserve, virement percentage or cross-sector command.

Treasury checks legality and records the adjustment/reasons and actual effect;
it does not allocate another agency's headroom. Repeated small changes are
assessed cumulatively; software, an AI forecast or a giant programme label
cannot conceal a material transfer. Actual public title/revenue is no authority
to divert pension/security/custody/private or earmarked funds. A lower-cost input
cannot be a secret service cut or free family/worker replacement.

**DB10 — New purposes or ceilings need a prior supplement.** Cabinet or any
member can propose an exact supplementary Act/schedule before a new commitment/
transfer exceeds its actual authority, with changed source, burdens, full-year/
future cash and foregone alternatives. Use DB03–05 actual elected vote and
existing law, not a discretionary Treasury approval or retrospective AI edit.
A lawful pre-enacted contingency may be drawn only for its specified triggers,
purpose, ceiling/source and reporting; it is neither a pot of cash nor authority
to create a new programme. No otherwise unauthorized emergency spend follows.

If unlawful spending already occurred, preserve source records, actual rights,
liabilities and fault/recovery process; later appropriation cannot retroactively
make the original action lawful or erase victims' remedies. Lawful funding of
continuing care/restitution is possible through the proper route. Actual valid
old urgency, provisional aid and standing payments do not wait for DB to invent
new permission. No pending supplemental proposal silently suspends those duties.
