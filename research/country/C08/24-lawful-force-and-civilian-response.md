# C08. Lawful public force and civilian response

1 November 2026 · Integrated design 97 research selection · LF01–LF16, not enacted law

Ordinary domestic public force needs an affirmative ceiling before harm, not only
an investigation after it. Select a separate lawful task plus immediate necessity,
least harmful feasible means, a strict person-protection threshold for potentially
lethal force, prompt care and independent correction. Public force remains
subordinate to a civilian democracy and to actual human choice in care.

An arrest, warrant, civil judgment, protective order or emergency declaration
supplies only its own underlying authority. Neither this ceiling nor a lawful
order grants a new search, detention, seizure, sedation, deportation, protest
dispersal or shooting entitlement. Unsafe acts may have to stop while a lawful
receiving service protects people; urgency does not make a missing worker or
clinic available. P04 private defense remains separate and does not license
private policing. A person harmed by force can seek immediate care and B/P
remedies without obtaining a criminal conviction.

All terms, staffing and 12-hour ordinary reporting number are normative
candidates. No territory, source law, public consent, professional review,
qualified roster, equipment inventory, budget or observed safety result is
established. Existing lawful duties, pending claims and independent official-
abuse investigations survive any authorized transition.
