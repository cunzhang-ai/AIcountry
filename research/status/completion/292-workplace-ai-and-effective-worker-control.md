# Design384. Give workers an effective decision before consequential automation

[WL47–WL53](../../country/C05/12/09-workplace-ai-and-effective-worker-control.md)
requires paid worker participation, exact collective agreement or
independent prior permission, and timely individual remedy. It
keeps private data, existing wages and separate consent protected.

[The worked comparison and primary records](../../settlement/workplace-ai-and-effective-worker-control/01-two-rosters-and-one-payday.md)
test claimed and actual hours, independent evidence, restored
access and payroll. Actual representation, lawful adoption,
qualified staffing and results remain open.
