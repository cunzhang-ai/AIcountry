# Design463 completion finding

The [first sitting](../../settlement/first-assembly-legislative-programme/01-first-sitting-and-candidate-instruments.md)
puts standing authority, a limited new appropriation, a coupled
substantive Act and their independent scrutiny into one reference
choice. A material amendment can postpone a funded option without
stopping a lawful narrower measure or extinguishing a claim.

The reference decision and constructed capacity branches are
specified. Actual statutory terms, qualified Assembly resources,
legitimate decisions and received public benefits remain open.
