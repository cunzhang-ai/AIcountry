# Binding public confirmation for one defined withdrawal

*Design519 · Proposed national institution*

[NI17–NI22](../../country/C03/16/06-public-confirmation-of-service-withdrawal.md)
select a separately authorized, prospective referendum on an ordinary national
statute that would remove the last independently usable essential service
or payment route. Ordinary agenda access, original rights, individual remedies,
judicial review and elected control of budgets retain their own roles. Compare the
funded strong incumbent, a binding confirmation and broader referendum authority
on the same access, costs and successive clocks.

The [comparison](../../settlement/public-reconsideration/01-who-can-stop-a-withdrawal.md)
records primary observations from existing parliamentary and referendum systems.
Its polling thresholds, timing, and procurement and staffing branch are
constructed. Existing independent channels could sometimes deliver the same
protection with less work. Actual jurisdiction, law, affected participation,
qualified adjudication, paid readiness and experienced protection remain open.
