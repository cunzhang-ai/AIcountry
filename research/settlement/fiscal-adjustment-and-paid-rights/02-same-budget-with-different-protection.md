## Contrast an execution block with cash and physical shortages

Re-run with 94 available and the same authorized standing scope.
An officer withholds the authorized two-unit benefit payment route
under a general instruction with no operative basis. A pre-authorized,
independently funded and credentialed payer can release those two
within purpose if the receiver accepts. The remedy replaces the blocked
execution rather than a shortage. A different signer on the same
inaccessible system leaves the benefit route exposed. Preserve the
original missed date and loss if the remedy comes late.

A verified supplier invoice discrepancy under existing law may
instead warrant a narrowly scoped hold with reasons and independent
review. Continue the undisputed work and original remedy. A lawful
seasonal grant schedule with accessible cash and no due earlier act
does not become a violation solely because the month has low
spending. If the care team is physically unavailable, funds and a
second payer do not fill a shift. Verify and pay the accepting route
or expose the care shortage and protect the people affected.
