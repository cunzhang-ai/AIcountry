# C08. Special harms and bounded sanctions

15 October 2026 · Design 74 · Proposed K13–K24/Q01–Q12, not enacted law

Select a differentiated extension of the criminal core: precise culpable
harms, a usable noncustodial schedule, attributable organizational liability
and finite consequences. Prevention, civil repair and safe receiving remain
separate duties. Criminal power cannot substitute for missing service capacity.

| Canonical part | Proposed scope |
|---|---|
| [Child protection and sexual exploitation](15/01-child-protection-and-material.md) | K13–K14; age, peer exceptions, actual exploitation and safe evidence |
| [Stalking, privacy and property damage](15/02-privacy-stalking-and-damage.md) | K15–K16; harmful conduct, not dissent or vague offensiveness |
| [Physical, environmental and animal safety](15/03-physical-environmental-and-animal-safety.md) | K17–K21; specific risk/duty, causation and fault |

| [Proceeds, records and limitation](15/07-proceeds-records-and-limitation.md) | Q10–Q12; ownership, bounded disclosure and prosecution clocks |

All P01–P20 protections apply. Prosecution proves every element and excludes
properly raised defenses beyond reasonable doubt; new rows confer no search,
surveillance, licensing or emergency powers. Maxima are upper limits for
natural persons, subject to P16's same-course 25-year adult/five-year child
ceiling. Under-16 conduct cannot produce conviction. No mandatory minimum,
death, indefinite predictive detention or punishment by AI is introduced.

Every new number is a provisional value choice. The country chooses bounded
plural responses over either automatic custody for regulatory breaches or
abolition of accountability for serious culpable harm. It does not establish
optimal deterrence, a staffed enforcement system or superior safety.
The [boundary exercises](../../settlement/special-crimes/README.md) test
contradictions and receiving duties; they are not verdicts or participant evidence.

This extends the ordinary draft; it is **not a complete code**. B21–B52,
P21–P24 and G now supply family/civil, connected cooperation and atrocity
candidates. [V01–V12](19-regulation-and-bounded-enforcement.md) adds common
regulatory monetary rules, with [M](../C06/24-financial-law-and-loss-allocation.md)
financial predicates. Technical sector, reproductive and conflict-detention
instruments remain incomplete. No analogy fills an omitted crime or penalty. Actual applicable
law and accrued claims continue until competent lawful revision; this text
neither enacts new offenses nor decriminalizes conduct under existing law.

Adoption needs published legislation, independent defense/courts, real safe
receivers and fresh affected/practitioner review of ages, maxima and burdens.
No fine, confiscation, unpaid work or automation saving finances the capacity
assumed here. The current book has no qualified incremental cost for this
extension. Next close the remaining specific statutes/technical instruments
and source-finance conflict, then audit the whole-country objective.

[KR27–KR28 candidate K25](../C11/07/07-civil-remedies-and-commercial-offence.md)
adds only knowing commercial-scale counterfeiting/piracy with fine-only sanctions
and express P/Q extension; patent/civil disputes and ordinary sharing are not
new crimes. This addition requires prospective enactment and actual legal review.
