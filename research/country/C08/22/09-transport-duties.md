# TD01–TD04: transport duties without automatic extraterritorial power

*Application of TS01–TS16; [technical safety](../22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md).*

The named national transport-safety role sets the common lawful floor; local
road/infrastructure owners implement their assigned duties. Mode-qualified
assessors and independent investigation remain distinct from operators/promoters.
No certificate supplies foreign operating rights, admission or military authority.

| ID | Defined entry and substantive operating duty | Conditions still requiring a lawful instrument |
|---|---|---|
| TD01 | Public-road operation of motor cars, buses, powered freight vehicles and motorcycles requires a competent licensed driver aged at least 18, registered vehicle, maintained brakes/lights and fitted passenger restraints. Necessary licensing accommodates demonstrated functional ability. No materially impaired operation or handheld device use while moving; yield to pedestrians at crossings and obey actual traffic directions. The operator must use safe speed/visibility and actual maintenance, not merely hold a paper certificate. | Vehicle/class tests, accessible assessment, inspection periods, approved restraints and detailed road signs need published exact annexes. No missing technical annex creates a numerical offense. |
| TD02 | Shared/public railway operation requires separately authorized infrastructure/control and train operators, qualified safety-critical staff, maintained rolling stock/track and demonstrated route/train compatibility. Do not dispatch into an unprotected conflicting movement or knowingly defeat a critical protection without an approved equally protective degraded mode. | Signalling, braking/clearance, safe working and rest requirements need mode/site instruments, actual competent rosters and accepted emergency routes. A timetable cannot override a known unsafe condition. |
| TD03 | Carrying people by powered aircraft, commercial air carriage, powered flights over an assembly of people or beyond the operator's direct visual observation requires actual aircraft/operator authorization and competent operational control. Aerodrome/air-navigation operation requires its own authorization; maintain airworthiness and operate only within the approved domain/clearances. | Aircraft/flight categories, separated airspace, weight/height/separation, crew and environmental limits require published instruments and actual international competence. Ordinary low-risk model flight is not silently licensed by the commercial category. |
| TD04 | Commercial passenger carriage on navigable water and merchant operation under our flag require competent operator/vessel authorization, maintained safe vessel, qualified crew and real lifesaving/pollution controls. Do not exceed approved load, route or weather limits; provide lawful distress assistance without avoidable grave danger to those already aboard. | Vessel/route, capacity, pilotage, navigation and discharge standards need exact instruments. Flag, port, coastal and high-seas authority differ; no domestic text grants unlimited foreign boarding or command. |

TD01 does not require a driving license for walking, mobility aids or ordinary
cycles; pedal-assisted cycles whose assistance cuts out at 25 km/h are exempt
from its driver-license requirement. Other applicable safe-use/product law still
applies. These age/assistance choices are national hypotheses, not WHO standards.
Default maxima: 30 km/h on built-up streets with pedestrian/cyclist mixing,
50 on other built-up roads with physically separated vulnerable access,
80 on other roads, 100 on physically separated limited-access roads. Lower
necessary lawful local limits prevail; road owners publish classification,
accessible notice and reasons. A higher category needs actual separation and
safe design, not a sign or new AI map alone. Slow further for actual conditions.

A specifically authorized automated road system may perform the driving function
only within its approved domain under TS05. The licensed operating entity retains
safe operation, fallback, maintenance and response; a mere passenger need not hold
a driving license or accept an impossible takeover duty. No vehicle self-enlarges
its road/category permission. Public vehicles and emergency services retain duties;
a strictly necessary proportionate rescue departure needs recorded actual grounds,
not blanket immunity. Foreign license/qualification recognition needs actual law
and equivalent competence, not nationality or a proprietary examination monopoly.

For each row only precisely adopted conduct/conditions engage V/VD10; ordinary
negligent accident or paperwork mismatch alone is not B guilt. Road/other traffic
sanctions use actual V/P proof and proportionate means, no imprisonment by this
schedule, automated conviction, private bounty or new penalty points by software.
These four rows are not complete global transport conventions or engineering codes.
