# OE11–OE15: voice must have a real constituency and an effective decision rule

*Part of [C05/13](../13-enterprise-and-association-constitutions.md); design118 candidate.*

**Design544 current choice.** Compatible law, charters and collective
agreements govern ordinary composition and process. Keep existing worker
and minority rights and independent remedies. The mandatory governance
formula and its supporting procedures below describe a specified alternative.
A later sector Act must select its exact scope before any dependent election,
threshold, voting formula or public registrar duty can start.

**OE11 — Owner/member meetings.** Give all actual entitled holders at least14
calendar days' usable notice, material terms/source and accessible/direct or
properly mandated participation. Ordinary share-company decisions use more than
half valid votes cast, weighted by actual voting shares, no turnout quorum.
New ordinary voting equity is one share/one vote; disclosed nonvoting investment
may have protected class rights but no disguised permanent supervote. Existing
special rights need OE31 lawful transition, not deletion by a filing. Coops and
member associations use actual one-member/one-vote rules. No duplicate proxy or
agent vote; abstention is not assent. Exact tie means no new decision, with
existing lawful duties continuing. Charter may require stronger protections.

**OE12 — Workforce in enterprise government.** At a13-week average of at least50
actual covered workers, reserve at least one-third of ordinary enterprise-board
seats to the workforce; at1000, one-half. Standard board is six:2 worker/4 owner,
then3/3. A charter can choose a multiple of six preserving or improving the worker
fraction and exact actual seats through OE22 lawful charter/class approval;
below threshold ordinary charter can be simpler. Stronger prior rights survive.
At one-third, four owner votes can still pass an ordinary six-seat board act;
there is no hidden worker veto. At parity a winning coalition must cross the
classes, not win every individual director. No owner chair has a second vote. Scope includes the domestic
actual controlled operating group; artificial subsidiaries/contracts do not evade
it. MP control proof, actual work links and worker choice matter, not server/account
count. Public and cooperative labels are no automatic exceptions.

For non-distributing civic/religious/member associations, the member/purpose organ
retains doctrine, affiliation and membership authority; the substantial employed
undertaking's economic/work operating board has the worker fraction. Purpose
cannot evade WL or safe receiving; operating seats do not enroll workers in a
religion/party or seize member purpose/asset authority. No new control over sovereign
Assembly/council/court/monetary/police offices or private informal association.
Sector-specific statutory organs/fit-and-proper rules prevail unless competently
amended with actual protected succession; no board reform by analogy.

**OE13 — Actual elections and removal.** Covered workers, including part-time,
qualified continuing temporary/dependent workers and disputed retaliation cases,
have one ballot per actual enterprise constituency, without citizenship, union,
share purchase or membership conditions. Directors must be adults; workers may
elect willing qualified worker or independent nominees without a manager/guild
veto. Work Relations Registrar receives an explicit OE mandate to administer the
WL proportional-list/person-preference/candidate-capacity method for these seats,
with original roll/control evidence, safe conflict handling and direct courts.
A list cannot fill nonexistent people, nor does a user-controlled agent make
its principal several voters. Different actual employment constituencies remain
separate; do not count their same time twice.

Terms are four years. Electing owner/member/workforce class may remove its own
director by its actual ordinary majority after notice/reply, subject to OE20
public-owner appointment/removal limits and stricter sector law; worker recall uses
20% petition and majority valid secret votes, no ordinary repeat within six months,
without blocking urgent court correction. Capital votes cannot dismiss worker
seats or employment in retaliation. Vacancies use the original class/list's next
willing eligible person, otherwise an assisted election. Paid director time,
training, independent advice and task replacement are entity costs under TW/WL,
not extra unpaid duty or proof of professional independence. Voluntary ownership
membership and worker representation remain distinct mandates.

**OE14 — No phantom majority during deadlock.** Board act needs affirmative votes
from more than half its full statutory seats; vacancies/recusals/agent replicas
do not lower that denominator or acquire extra votes. Particular conflicted
transactions need actual disinterested source approval and direct court relief,
not an invented unconflicted quorum. At3/3 a tied vote fails; lawful prior mandates
and actual ordinary due obligations continue, not a chair's new policy.

Use negotiation, mutually accepted mediation or lawful owner/member change with
rights intact. Existing officers may execute only actual prior delegated/funded
routine duties. For an actual missing-organ threat to rights/assets, an independent
court may appoint a qualified conflict-free temporary actor for specified necessary
acts at most90 days, no serial same-reason reset. It cannot invent capital,
approve a disputed merger, waive worker votes, grant new public powers or replace
lawful reconstitution indefinitely. If the firm cannot continue lawfully, U/closure
and actual service receiving apply. More blocking/funding cost is a retained risk.

**OE15 — Minority and affected remedies.** Actual members can request records,
challenge unequal/unfair-prejudice or conflicted acts and seek proportionate B17
relief. Any member may request court leave for a genuine entity claim where its
competent organ wrongly refuses/conflictedly controls it; no mandatory share-buy
threshold excludes a small holder. Court checks material prima-facie grounds,
fair representation/conflicts, entity interest and cost, with safe assistance and
respondent reply. It can order lawful correction/restitution, constrained buyout,
loss compensation or dissolution, not a free guaranteed investment exit. Workers,
users and victims retain their own B/WL/sector rights without owning a share.
No state/union/entity panel is the sole final judge of its own wrong.
