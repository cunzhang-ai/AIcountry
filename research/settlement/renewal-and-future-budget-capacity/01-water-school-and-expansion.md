# Water, school and an optional expansion across two budgets

Design324 · Authored comparison for [IV28–IV32](../../country/C06/63/07-renewal-and-future-budget-capacity.md)

Two authorities share a qualified civil works crew. The public envelopes
are 120 and 100 illustrative units, with 20 of binding claims in each.
There are six qualified crew-weeks each year. The second-year envelope
is a planning limit, not a future appropriation. All claims use their
own lawful sources. The comparison assumes qualified safety advice,
accepted receiving and indicated dates where stated.

commitment. Select water repair, the school bridge and bounded expansion
preparation. The owner verifies accessible classes and transport before
closing any school area. Reserve the school and pump weeks and present
their funding before the later work is due. Year-1 headroom of 30 cannot
be presumed transferable or recurring. If lawmakers separately make

full commitment source, service path and safety advice are qualified,

If the school bridge fails before the safe start, reopen the choice.
Use the separately staffed repair only after acceptance within 117.
