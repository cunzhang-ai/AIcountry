# Independent selection for the electoral commission's legal-source seats

*Design288 · EA21–EA25 · Prospective change to EA05.*

**EA21 — Assign legal-source election appointments separately.**
Establish a nine-person electoral selection formation for L1/L2
and their reserves only. Use three judges elected by judges,
three legal practitioners elected by practitioners and three paid
lay electors selected by the inclusive draw. Apply the substantive
CJ06 eligibility, direct entry, term-matched alternates and
six-year nonrenewable terms with two-year rotations. Elect the
professional categories on distinct ballots and draw lay terms
separately. A principal or alternate in any of this formation,
CJ06 or OJ06 may hold only one of those slots. Use independent,
prior-law assignment for any resulting conflicts. Leave CJ06
constitutional appointments and OJ06 ordinary appointments with
their respective formations.

**EA22 — Keep the limited authority and the fixed vote.** For each
EA03-qualified candidate or reserve, at least six of the fixed
nine must attend and six must vote for the appointment, with at
least one affirmative vote in each category. Keep EA08 notice,
objections, reasons, candidate and reserve votes and term rules.
This formation appoints only the specified two legal-source seats
and their reserves. The opposition and public sources keep their
own selections. No selector may review their own appointment act,
choose a reviewing judge, exercise an election power, appropriate
money or acquire another source's vote on failure. The commission
retains the EA10 five-present and four-affirmative rule. AI may
check conflict, cost and timing evidence under accountable human
review. It cannot decide eligibility, rank the pool for exclusion,
supply a vote or fill a vacancy.

**EA23 — Buy the independent path before the vacancy.** The
Assembly prices scheduled recruitment, paid alternates, lay
access, separate receipt and recovery of records, hearings,
review and civil execution on the same calendar as ordinary
and constitutional duties. Price actual backfill for displaced
judicial and practitioner work. The statutory recruiting
custodian must accept direct applications, preserve authenticated
originals, provide reasons and maintain a funded alternative
under an independent failure path. The appointers cannot make
candidacies depend on a single ministry, court president, guild,
vendor or discretionary payment. Keep the original records
available for competent independent review. Common professional
electorates, funders or suppliers can still produce common
control. Verify distinct staff, access and ability to pay and
deliver on the required dates before crediting separation.
Routine shared administration may remain where a funded,
accepted alternative survives the declared common loss.

**EA24 — Preserve election duties under selection failure.**
Count the three formations' actual timely, lawful members after
only eligible preselected alternates. Distinguish a lawful refusal
to appoint from an unlawful procedural block, a captured channel
or missing capacity. Under EA16 the ordinary court may require
lawful process, protect a record and enforce a particular duty.
Neither review nor a vacancy creates consent. Continue EA12
local filing, polling, preservation and mechanical counting, with
the original deadlines and a paid route to useful relief. An
unresolved commission decision remains unresolved if the lawful
board or competent court cannot supply the required act. An early
poll neither shortens EA08 safeguards nor extends an expired
term. Pay for the actual remedy as well as the hearing. Account
for the claimant's lost opportunity if relief arrives too late.

**EA25 — Change authority at a prospective statutory boundary.**
Competent prior-law adoption fixes and publishes the first
ordinary process-opening date for this source, funds its
qualified readiness and assigns original records and liabilities.
Retain the EA09 public founding route where legitimate predecessor
formations do not exist. Existing appointments and reserves keep
their expiry and lawful powers. A process validly opened before
the boundary keeps its original owner and review unless express
transition law provides a fair transfer with renewed notice,
consideration and votes. Earlier endorsements supply evidence
only. A new replenishment opens with the new formation under the
same legal-source label and remaining term. Unlawfully stalled
cases receive existing review. Neither formation may choose the
boundary for an individual case. Preserve called polls, standing
career duties, protected pay and all original claims and challenge
dates. Reopen on common upstream control, impossible recruitment,
displaced hearing capacity or a stronger democratic alternative.
