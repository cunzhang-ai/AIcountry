# Stop an unlawful collection route even when the budget expects the money

*Design537 · Historical failure mechanism and separate constructed budget comparison*

Australia's Royal Commission into Robodebt identified failures in the budget
process. Its Chapter 15 recommendations distinguish whether legislative change
is needed to implement a proposal from whether legislation authorizes
expenditure. They call for circulation of relevant legal advice with proposals
and disclosure of the assumptions and data underlying compliance costings.
Chapter 17 calls for independent scrutiny, and Chapters 18 and 19 address
collection protections and unresolved draft advice. The findings concern one
program. They support tracing how an unlawful mechanism can survive review.
They do not prove a particular AIcountry safeguard would have prevented it.

## Hold the same people, law and original deadlines

Assume a public payer has eight settled ordinary units. Six fund already due
income awards. Two fund independent review, accessible help and routine
execution. A budget forecast estimates another four from newly claimed
debts. Officials have annual totals but the enacted test requires evidence
for individual pay periods. There is an unresolved original legal objection.
The affected people include a resident who has no surviving local payslip
and another who did not receive a usable notice. Unaffected benefits and
properly established debts keep their own scope. All figures are illustrative.

| Budget and enforcement choice | If all four expected units fail to qualify |
|---|---|
| Finance a binding four-unit expansion from expected collections | Eight units can still fund the standing schedule if kept separate. The new liability leaves a four-unit gap. Officials gain an incentive to let weak assessments stand, but the binding commitments create no collection power. |
| Keep the expansion expressly conditional and use ordinary assessment with independent payment and review | Eight units cover standing delivery and review. Hold the additional four-unit scope. No established statutory debt is released by this decision. Count only independently lawful receipts before any later voted spending. |
| Fund a narrower improvement from an independently verified two-unit source | Ten units can protect standing work and fund the two-unit improvement if an independent operator accepts it. Any foregone purpose and actual ongoing source must be part of that separate vote. The remainder waits. |

Choose the second route on these facts when there is no separately qualified
source. This choice respects the ordinary budget decision and leaves the
same lawful casework available. If a source for the narrower route qualifies,
consider it on its own costs and receiving. A wider lawful enforcement reform
can still win if evidence supports both fair process and a net benefit under
the actual cost of operating, refunding and reviewing it. Using staff in place
of the original tool does not cure the same unsupported assumption.

## Stop the dependency and follow the people

Suppose 2 units have already been collected from one resident and are proved
refundable. The resident needs the money while a still-due award is being paid.
Another resident's collection was scheduled but had not run. An independent
reviewer can inspect the original facts and obtain available scope-specific
relief. Stop the disputed route under its applicable authority rather than
waiting for recovery figures. Give the reviewer both original outcomes
even if the agency withdraws the second case before a ruling. An
individual withdrawal does not answer whether the same unsupported
inference still underlies the national budget. Preserve the original decision times and
follow the common defect to both cases and to other people who never applied
for redress. The refund is a payable claim when established. In this example
the eight units are already assigned, so it needs a separate eligible two
or must be recorded as unpaid with its deadline and lawful correction.
Neither freezing later recoveries nor correcting the software pays it.

Accept period-specific evidence or the applicable lawful evidentiary
alternative. Do not infer that everyone is owed the same amount or that no
one owes any lawful debt. Give the resident accessible review, reasons and
actual payment; protect the missing notice and any separate hardship remedy.
An AI tool may trace shared assumptions or flag missing evidence. Budget and
service staff retain their respective authority and can continue without it.
