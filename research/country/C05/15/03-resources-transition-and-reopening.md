# SG09–SG12: continuing claims, paid delivery and an open democratic choice

*Part of [C05/15](../15-service-first-personal-security.md), design 136 candidate.*

**SG09 — Name source and scope before claiming affordability.** Keep dated G
payments/receipts, restricted S, local grants, employer/creditor claims, client
money, reserves, lawful borrowing and actual owned foreign settlement separate.
An annual tax assessment is not due-day cash; a new pension unit is not a
current benefit payment. Do not add earmarked money twice or fund a recurring
guarantee with a one-time asset sale, imputed cash, debt rollover or invented
AI/output/export/take-up assumption. RF/MC and their separately authorized
finite issue rival retain actual limits; no new financial power arises here.

The four reused authored country cases hold their service/input/needs/earned
rights meanings, not a complete actual national denominator. Their named
service-plus-personal net-assessment subtotal excludes other taxes, source
dates, G/S backing, public debt, transition, private production/capital and
unpriced later duties. It is neither total expenditure nor deficit. Some
direct-public rows are lower; qualification, physical inputs and paid transfer
remain required. All named G stresses still lack funds at their first due day.
Select no rate, beneficiary exclusion or service cut to turn that result green.

**SG10 — Retain due rights and fund the handover.** This preference change
does not cancel an existing I05/U payment, source-period arrear, Y unit/credit/
survivor/index right, worker/wage/remittance claim, lease/title, contract,
deposit/creditor interest, case, foreign obligation or constitutional hold.
Existing I/Y/sector offices keep their lawful duties until paid OP/T successors
actually receive each one. No compulsory wind-down of commenced U or retrospective
tax recovery follows. Any later law must distinguish prospective new coverage
from actual accrued/due rights, with express notice, matching and remedies.

Use protected records, lawful evidence custody, qualified accepting staff,
accessible notice and paid overlap. Additional assessment/assistance, claims,
independent review, service capacity and correction have real costs. Missing
new-launch finance does not extend old-law deadlines or grant a Treasury veto.
Do not promise permanent hold-harmless benefits from an unpriced transition.

**SG11 — Existing democratic authority chooses changes.** Assembly law and
appropriation, replaceable government and accountable sector/local officers
retain their scopes. Independent evidence, AA spending scrutiny, courts and
funded execution allow challenge; none chooses sovereign burdens alone. The
public must be able to organize opposition, propose U/ownership/service rivals,
inspect material sponsorship and peacefully replace decision-makers. Protectors'
own appointment, funding, information and enforcement can be captured; actual
independence has not been demonstrated. AI assistance is revocable and cannot
qualify its own decision, multiply a mandate or make the preference binding.

**SG12 — Complete finite comparison, then resolve the next larger gap.** The
present two-layer comparison ends in a research **revision** and a **rejection
of imported affordability/superiority claims**, with unmatched old-age/service/
transition evidence disclosed. It does not leave the destination question
permanently waiting for one more local tax/credit vignette.
