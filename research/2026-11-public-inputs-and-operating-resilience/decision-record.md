## Alternatives and disposition

1. **Retain/repair supply:** preserve actual useful capacity and relationships;
   keep scope/need/quality/source checks, rather than forcing replacement.
2. **Single-source/just-in-time:** may save inventory/qualification effort, but
   a delayed/common upstream failure can defeat useful-time service. Retain
   only where the protected risk/cost/continuity bargain justifies it.
3. **Public critical-input competence plus plural routes/usable buffers:**
   select the primary conditional architecture, with nonexclusive pooled
   buying where actually usable and existing paid sector/local continuity.
4. **Domestic manufacture:** retain explicit public/cooperative/private
   investment options; reject assumed national self-sufficiency and free assets.
5. **Regional shared purchase/reserve:** retain a substantive cooperative option,
   requiring real accepted title, source, release, contribution, exit and remedies.
