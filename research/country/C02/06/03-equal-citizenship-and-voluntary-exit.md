# Belonging: stable citizenship and voluntary exit

*Canonical MB07–MB08; part of [C02/06](../06-citizenship-and-secure-belonging.md).*

**MB07 — One stable civic status.** No involuntary citizenship deprivation,
including for fraud, crime, foreign nationality, ancestry, marriage/divorce,
adoption, nonregistration, overseas residence, disloyalty or peaceful opposition.
Birth and naturalized citizenship have equal political standing and protection.
Dual/multiple nationality is permitted; no mandatory choice at majority. Actual
foreign conflicts and recognition remain disclosed, not erased by this rule.

Protect valid acquisition by operation of law, actual official grants and actual
official declaratory recognition to an identifiable person. 'Competent' means
the designated office has authority over this kind of act, not that every
substantive qualification was correctly found. Otherwise 'never eligible' could
recreate deprivation under another label. A genuine grant to an applicant using
a false name is not mere theft of another person's existing record.

A counterfeit credential, forged official signature or mere use/copying of a
victim's record is no actual grant to an impersonator. A civil court fairly
determines whether acquisition or a genuine official act occurred. To displace
an identifiable person's established official status, the state must prove by
clear and convincing evidence that no qualifying acquisition/genuine recognition
occurred or the claimant is not its actual subject. Missing archives, a failed
biometric match or a substantive eligibility error alone is insufficient. Use shared
proof, interim protection and appeal before adverse irreversible effects. Correct
names, duplicates and stolen records without transferring the victim's identity,
stripping a genuinely recognized person or erasing the safe correction history.
The executive cannot decide this distinction by calling an opponent a fraud.

Independently prove any personal deception, corruption, property loss or crime
under the separate B/P/K rules; no automatic guilt, penalty or duplicate recovery.
Do not invent a new offense or harsher sanction by analogy. Closed entitlements,
separate evidence assessment and final conferral, authenticated source records
and audit before effect constrain corrupt grants. They do not eliminate them:
irreversible mass grants could change the electorate. Preserve that objection
and the bounded judicial-fraud-revocation rival in the comparison; do not cure
it covertly through a second-class naturalized franchise or citizenship stripping.

**MB08 — Personal voluntary exit and correction.** Only an adult's specific,
informed, uncoerced act can renounce citizenship; verify actual possession or
simultaneous effective acquisition of another nationality, not a future promise.
No parent, partner, employer, supporter or agent may renounce for someone else.
Provide independent advice, accessible communication and a thirty-day withdrawal
period after acknowledged usable notice, with a personal request to shorten it
only where an independent court verifies necessity and free informed choice.
If another nationality never takes effect, this citizenship never lapses.
A foreign demand for earlier loss may remain incompatible; seek a safe conditional
route without pretending to control the other state. No tax-clearance, debt payment
or military-service completion is an exit condition;
actual lawful duties remain enforceable through their own ordinary routes.

Voluntary relinquishment of secure residence likewise requires personal informed
choice, independent advice and thirty-day revocation; departure alone is not it.
Neither act automatically releases accrued duties, private claims or a child's
status. Persons may decline naturalization without losing resident standing.
A person wrongly deprived or subjected to forged/coerced renunciation has a
direct status-restoration and consequential-remedy route; a past valid voluntary
exit is different. A former voluntary citizen may recover citizenship on supported
application and identity proof, without another four-year wait or wealth test;
no loss of another nationality is required. Record the actual effective date,
retain prior lawful claims and do not invent foreign retroactive recognition.
