# Expand through elected dated cohorts without making rights wait for a certificate

*CP03–CP04; part of [national commitments](../28-national-commitments-and-residual-scarcity.md).*

**CP03 — New commitments need an express statutory schedule.** Replace the
single second-calendar-year start for the entire new S service/Y new-service
schedule. An adopting Act must enumerate each service/coverage/new-accrual cohort,
its exact legal date/event, responsible operator, prior-law comparator, interim
protection, preparation/overlap and source/remedy duties. Cohorts can be services,
activities or justified need groups; a jurisdiction cannot choose dates merely
by copying a fictional six-year asset or seven-year training reference.

Dates are enacted choices, not floating conditions such as 'when AI, Treasury,
the provider or a reviewer declares readiness'. Technical evidence informs the
choice but cannot activate, suspend or indefinitely postpone a right. If a proposed
whole new regime has no lawful dated schedule, no fictional common research date
supplies one. Source-law duties continue; a missing new schedule creates neither
new criminal guilt nor a power to erase existing claims. A legitimate legislature
can choose a comprehensive earlier guarantee with actual qualified recruitment,
partnerships and financed redistribution; this remains a substantive rival.

Before new dates, compare existing due protection, urgent receiving, direct-provider,
partnership and paid preparation options in one actual national account. Evidence,
public value choice and enacted authority remain distinct. Publish unresolved
source/capacity risks and actual fallback rather than pretend all new duties cost
zero. At least annual public legislative review and an earlier material-failure
response are required; they do not automatically revise an enacted date.

**CP04 — Sequence must not select only the easiest people to serve.** Explain
incidence across needs, disability, age, place, income, existing deprivation and
future people. Prefer removing severe avoidable exclusion and securing dependent
essentials over prestigious expansion where comparable protection is feasible.
Readiness in a rich area alone is no justification for indefinitely excluding a
poorer area. Any differentiation needs published relevant grounds, funded interim
access, equal-standing review and direct individual remedy under actual law.

Preserve stronger existing services, accrued pension/care credits, indexation,
pending claims and due dates. Match contribution collection to actual lawful
accrual/credit treatment; no new payment extraction without its corresponding
rights. Do not call an already overdue entitlement an uncommenced future cohort.
After a date passes, failure remains non-delivery with E/T correction; only lawful
prospective legislation can revise future coverage while preserving due obligations.

No staged service can delay an election, reset an incumbent term, remove political
standing, authorize coercive treatment or make a claimant furnish a full economic
book. Legal equality and professional/clinical authority remain independently
reviewable even while the material package is incomplete.

[NP14–NP18](../33/05-dates-preparation-and-migration.md) now joins new I income/
tax/contribution dates with S/Y and their indispensable receiving, records,
independent review and execution dependencies. The funded preparation-enabling
Act fixes first 120-day proposal/180-day disposition and annual choice; a later
adopted expansion Act fixes actual service/accrual cohorts. No process deadline
launches benefits, postpones elections or invalidates a lawful budget by itself.
