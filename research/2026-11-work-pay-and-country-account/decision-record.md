## Genuine alternatives and substantive choice

- Retain I05 plus services: lower universal gross outlay, ordinary means assessment
  and nonclaim/reporting exposure; Ybasic/earned claims still continue.
- Protected ordinary U plus progressive other income from its first unit:
  independent ordinary access, different tax burden, residual needs and funding.
- U plus an additional general personal-income allowance: more net relief on
  wages/pensions/capital, with real public cost, not the same bargain relabeled.
- Taxable gross U plus equal exclusive offset: exactly equivalent to the selected
  exclusion, not a separate funding source or an empirically superior arrangement.
- More public/direct versus plural/import provision: compare actual receiving,
  control, paid acquisition and full costs; neither ownership removes a margin
  for free nor requires every potential worker to be assigned full-time employment.
