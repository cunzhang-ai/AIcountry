# TN09–TN12: make the operating state survive a source and power change

*Part of [TN](../59-tax-sources-and-country-operation.md); OP/T and sector law apply.*

**TN09 — Join the ordinary year once.** Keep WF's baseline expenses separate
from replaced, incremental, capital/renewal, contingent and old-claim costs.
Include I05/Y, service and NC peak-qualified receiving, TW paid non-task time,
election/opposition, courts/prosecution/PO outside inquiry/custody, PM buffer,
DP restoration, maintained material supply and actual independent execution.
Shared people, cash, buildings and standby can be allocated once only within
their real scope and simultaneous availability. A function label or public FTE
is no priced qualified worker. No extra new office is needed to name this work.

**TN10 — Trace the due date and independent controller.** For each ordinary
and adverse handover, identify the legally liable payer/receiver, permitted
source owner/currency, due date, actual accepted qualified work and independent
review/execution. Distinguish assessment from receipt, annual expense from
usable cash, G from restricted S/local/foreign/private title, and a generic
vendor credential from a lawful mandate. A public annual surplus cannot cure
an earlier payroll/care/election gap; political defeat cannot authorize an
outgoing police Chief or technical vendor to retain command keys or sources.
Existing independent legal orders and paid execution apply; no new emergency
tax, central master credential, military safety sovereign or invented alternate.

**TN11 — Carry liabilities even when both routes fail.** OP17–OP20 retain
obligation-specific old obligor/successor, paid lawful overlap and pending
claims/records/contracts/worker/pension rights at original dates. In source
shortfall or hostile withholding, preserve due personal/service/custody and
democratic claims and their existing urgent remedies. Report actual missing
cash, competence, control or acceptance distinctly; neither a favorable model,
expired acting term, unpaid family, private asset sweep nor a paper judgment
supplies the missing execution. Lawful elected correction and actual accepted
finance are necessary where available; claims survive even where delivery is
unresolved. An old source remains legally due until its lawful repeal/period
transition, not until an administrator predicts a replacement will work.

**TN12 — Finish the discriminating comparison before another tax invention.**
Next complete a same-context source/base and distribution bridge for the
benchmark and strong retain/repair: positive/negative business and labor,
basic/earned/private pension, realized gains/noncash, coverage, measured W/M,
source periods and ordinary tax versus restricted Y. Join the largest matched
service/TW/NC and democratic/PO paid increments, due-date sources and transition
under ordinary and hostile/shortfall paths. Deliver a bounded feasible, failed
or unresolved package comparison with explicit remaining unknowns; no fabricated
microdata, consent, recruitment, asset access or AI saving. Research budget and
independent affected reviewers remain unknown. Reopen this selection on a
qualified full-base/source match, corrected observations, changed offer/old
claims, actual hostile control failure, material objection or a better whole
alternative. Final decisions need fresh review; this edition certifies none.
