# PS09–PS12: electricity in time, with public control

*Part of [C06/37](../37-physical-supply-and-paid-trade.md); design92 research.*

**PS09 — Derive demand and preserve missing loads.** Distinguish delivered
kWh, network/auxiliary losses, coincident MW, ramping, voltage/frequency, storage
power/usable energy, charging, fuel and outages. The joined recipe identifies
homes, existing service-building inputs, food and water operations. Undefined
manufacturing, private goods, construction and transport remain additional
requirements, not zero or a GDP-dependent residual. Neither75,000 structures nor
an authored energized-home count proves actual occupancy, heating or adequacy.

**PS10 — Test a lost source and its common causes.** Before crediting a supply
plan, test the largest relevant single-source loss, correlated weather/fuel/
corridor/substation failures, heat/cold demand, duration, recharge, startup,
network access and protected dependants. Set actual reliability standards by
elected law with independent engineering evidence, not by annual surplus alone.
Contracts/accounts sharing a corridor or fuel source do not establish independence.

The separate110MW stress uses net capacity at the same load bus. Two40MW links,
two25MW domestic blocks and zero stress wind leave90MW after one link loss.
A20MW/80usable-AC-MWh battery lasts four hours ideally; a six-hour loss fails.
Adding one25MW qualified block yields115MW after a single link loss, but both
links lost leave75MW: the35MW gap exceeds the20MW battery immediately.
A40MW instantaneous trip also needs accepted fast response beyond that battery;
spare nameplate capacity alone proves none. This is not a whole-country load map.

**PS11 — A mixed and revisable supply choice.** Prefer lower-harm demand
efficiency, distributed clean generation, diversified interconnection, paid
qualified firm reserves and local critical-service capability over one dominant
plant, seller or automatic fossil build. Compare real domestic-firm, import-
heavy and storage/flexibility packages under the same duties/ecological law.
Do not retire an existing source before accepted replacement and continuity.
Safety-required cessation/isolation remains lawful without a ready replacement.
New publicly financed core network assets retain public/common control; generation,
retail and voluntary local systems may remain plural under nondiscriminatory access.

Solar50MW/60GWh and wind40MW/100GWh are authored conditional annual outputs,
with no guaranteed stress-wind credit. Domestic fueled generation still needs
imported fuel, spares, expertise, permits and a compatible emissions path; it is
not an autonomous or zero-carbon solution. A battery needs actual charge above
reserved floors and energy to recharge. Repeating an empty battery is no supply.

**PS12 — Dispatch is bounded execution.** The Assembly chooses the programme,
exposure, tariffs/assistance, public expenditure, ecological limits and lawful
shortage priorities; councils retain lawful siting. Existing C04 appointments,
integrity/procurement, independent safety/evidence access and ordinary courts
constrain stewards and operators. Operators/AI implement accepted dispatch and
narrow necessary safety isolation, not new political rationing or a tariff.

An interruptible contract needs actual assent, named load/duration/compensation
and the protected dependent task's continued supply. CP's residual pool cannot
silently absorb already-due clinical, water, election or remedy power.
[PS21–PS32](06-critical-public-power-and-actual-priority.md) now selects a separate
prior ordinary allocation for expressly scheduled public backup power, including
an unavoidable critical collision; this is not CP power or grid-wide authority.
Elsewhere retain the failed claim/elected correction route. A blackout itself
never grants new executive or AI authority or supplies absent physical capacity.

[EG ordinary electricity law](../../C09/19-electricity-service-and-grid-access.md)
specifies public dispatch, distribution, consumer and seller-exit roles.
It keeps these PS physical tests and special backup powers at their existing
scope. Firm and flexible contracts need usable capacity at the same node
and time, and the fallback supplier needs an independent financial path.

[WS21–WS25](../../C09/16/05-water-power-and-catchment-limits.md)
qualifies firm power against contemporaneous withdrawals, returns
and downstream limits. Price and accept a separate route where a
common hot or dry interval constrains expected generation.
