# MF01–MF04. Assign and finance the common floor

*Part of [funded local mandates](../08-funded-local-mandates-and-equalization.md).*

**MF01 — State what the country owes before distributing its costs.**
For every material common-floor service delivered by a local authority,
identify the law, eligible people, useful time, national funder, local
duty holder, receiving provider and remedy. The same service can have
both a general equalization source and a sector appropriation. Keep
optional local additions, national direct tasks and interlocal services
distinct. Record shared resources once with their competing obligations.
A move of responsibility preserves the original resident claim,
employment and supplier terms until the competent lawful transition.
A national requirement that changes actual tasks or minimum service
counts by its effect, including delegated rules within their lawful scope.

**MF02 — Measure the cost of adequate receiving and lawful fiscal capacity.**
Use publicly adopted rules for comparable efficient costs, need and
representative assigned revenue capacity. Cost unavoidable geography,
accessible travel, competent staffing, existing obligations, paid review,
normal and adverse capacity, and needed maintenance at matched scope.
Include eligible people who cannot reach a service, not just current use.
When prices or capacity vary across places, record a justified benchmark
and the materially different local evidence. State which costs are finite
and which continue. Present the source vintages, uncertainty and sensitivity.
Use protected necessary evidence. Give local authorities and residents
a usable way to contest a missed need or wrongly assigned input.

Do not make the transfer a refund of any local spending or reward deliberate
undercollection, exclusion or deferral. Recognize actual legal restrictions,
mandatory holds and separate cash shortfalls at their proper scope. Price
local optional choices independently. No new representative rate, tax base,
marginal retention rate or proxy grants itself authority. Use the adopted
source method. TN's current-law and benchmark distinction governs.

**MF03 — Allocate each source to its actual obligations once.**
Treasury and the affected council reconcile, at a common scope and period,
the standard cost, allocated lawful local contribution, attributed part
of general equalization, and eligible dedicated receipts. Keep a distinct
line for gross cost, any part already netted under source law, and any
remaining funded national sector amount. The aggregate of sector
attributions cannot exceed the legally usable source. Reconcile cash
availability and prior commitments by due date separately. A claim,
forecast, unreceived grant or restricted balance cannot pay an invoice.

For a sector gap, credit only the general grant and own-source share
expressly allocated to that same cost and period and usable for that duty.
Never credit the same general grant again elsewhere or subtract a receipt
already netted in the cost. An overdue valid grant stays owed under its
original route. Separate the need for interim finance from permanent
cost. Show any separately enacted sector addition and its payer.
If a duty moves into the ordinary grant, specify the old sector authority,
the replacement appropriation, first usable receipt, overlaps and actual
handback. Preserve accrued payments and remedy rights. The transfer
within public government is counted once in consolidated finance.

**MF04 — Put the new mandate and its financing to an elected decision.**
For a material new or expanded mandatory local task, the proposing body
lays out the incremental and continuing cost against the existing duties,
local distribution, source incidence, lower-capacity case, paid preparation
and the named payment and delivery paths. Use FB and DB for the source
and four-year assessment, including later obligations. A lower cost
requires the FB09–FB12 actual release test. A nationwide average or
announcement of efficiency cannot settle a locality's cost.

The proposed adopting law or linked competent instruments specify the
new duty, funding authority, payer, prospective date and initial cohorts
or scope. Require an expressly financed start under prior-law limits
for new mandatory local assignments. If source remains unresolved before
adoption, the Assembly can fund, narrow, stage, retain the current scope
or decline the proposal. Only a competent lawful act sets the date.
Missing paperwork, a technical certificate, delay, repeal of an
appropriation or an executive funding hold cannot suspend an existing
or commenced right. An actual funding failure goes to correction with
its original dates and parties.

[IV13–IV17](../../C06/63/04-territorial-capital-and-basic-access.md)
uses these attributed sources for national and cross-local investment.
Do not require a uniform local match for projects needed for common
duties. Keep capital, recurring finance and dated usable cash distinct.

[MF14–MF18](04-funding-through-population-change.md) applies these distinctions
when population, the revenue base or statistical coverage changes. Price
fixed network and access duties separately from variable work. Annual
assessment and cash at each useful deadline need distinct support.
