# C05. Technology transition and shared gains

*Design329–521 · AT01–AT39 · [C05 economy and work](../C05-economy-and-work.md) · Proposed research law.*

When new production methods increase useful output or alter work,
people can still lose income, service access and bargaining power.
Act on those losses and provider dependence at their actual scope.
Keep due rights working while elected members decide durable
sources, transition and the distribution of realized gains.
