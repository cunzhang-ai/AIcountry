# JC33–JC38. Restore interdependent public functions in a lawful order

*Design367 · Part of [joint continuity](../65-joint-continuity-and-first-budget.md).*

**JC33 — Prove how recovery starts.** Before relying on a joint continuity
plan, each responsible service owner identifies which other functions
its recovery needs, the exact useful window and the accepting operator.
Include payments, independent review and the actual recovery inputs.
Distinguish operating a service once restored from the act that enables
its first restoration. If two paths require each other's restored output,
treat the pair as unqualified until at least one usable starting route
can act independently of that loop. A proposed restart, signed plan or
parallel list of backups is not an available input.

**JC34 — Compare independent starting routes before failure.** Treasury
and the competent elected bodies compare direct temporary receiving,
a separately staffed and funded starting route, and a structural change
that reduces the shared dependency. Match service, rights, deadline,
staffing, legal source, independence and replenishment in the comparison.
Reserve only the resources that can actually be assigned, with a
qualified receiver and expiry. Keep currently due water, clinical,
remedy and democratic duties on their own authorities. No enabling
project can spend their protected resources merely because it might
eventually help them. Compare the cost of waiting and residual losses.

**JC35 — Use the preauthorized sequence with separate decisions.** The
existing sector and incident leads use only the adopted scope, actual
evidence and source. Name the first qualified act, necessary accepting
party, earliest receipt, next act and condition for stopping. Each
sector officer retains the applicable safety, supply or service decision.
The coordinator can reconcile the schedule and relay a conflict.
It cannot transfer control, allocate private resources or waive a
sector or court requirement. A failed step preserves earlier urgent
protection and goes to the lawful substitute, or remains an exposed duty.

**JC36 — Protect independent receipt of urgent challenges.** Account for
usable records, lawful hearing, safe notice and actual execution during
the same interval. Keep opposition access and any scheduled democratic
duty reachable through the established independent routes. A review
procedure depending entirely on the same unavailable first act does
not qualify. Pay for an independently controlled alternate within
actual appropriations. If an elected decision is needed, use the
ordinary or lawful alternate sitting under the original rules. Failure
of both routes confers no additional power or extended emergency.

**JC37 — Check the next call before crediting recovery.** Verify that the
starting act produced accepted output on time and that the next
service obtained the needed inputs. Keep use, remaining protection,
ordinary demand, paid replenishment and the next due act on one
account. Record interruptions and missed duties at their original
dates. A recovered facility, first delivery or nominal refill cannot
certify durable receipt. Treat a late, partial or failed chain as
its actual result. Use JC and T for the correction and handback.

**JC38 — Exercise the order and preserve open limits.** Use an authorized
exercise or qualified equivalent evidence to test one blocked starting
route, continuing service, independent control and the next call.
Publish comparable results, missed duties and scope without exposing
sensitive infrastructure or personal details. Preserve records, actual
responsibilities, dissent, dates and paid successors through a change
of provider or government. AI may compare timing and find unsupported
loops. It cannot authorize the first act or certify a restored service.
Reopen on hidden common control, an earlier deadline, failed
replenishment or a more reliable qualified alternative.
