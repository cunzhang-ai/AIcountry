# Constitutional review: source evidence and actual execution

*Canonical CJ15–CJ16; part of [C03/08](../08-constitutional-review-and-public-control.md).*

**CJ15 — Evidence, understanding and consent stay distinct.** Preserve the exact
operative law, relevant original record, provenance, competing interpretations,
material objections, reasons, votes and correction history. Parties can challenge
the government's or court's summary through usable protected access to sources.
Distributed custody includes physically usable authenticated copies and lawful
successors outside a single provider's exclusive credentials. Public reasons
must not expose protected testimony, children, addresses or classified sources
beyond what an effective fair hearing and lawful scrutiny require.

Communication may use speech, meetings, translated sign, remote presence,
simulation or future methods. A shared immersive model is an interpretation,
not agreement on facts, law, delegation or consent. Preserve a party's ability
to correct representation, obtain independent assistance and use another usable
route. No unfamiliar concept is discarded merely because a platform schema
cannot encode it. Authentication verifies a particular person's legal act;
accounts, biometric access and an AI model's paraphrase do not supply authority.

AI may search, translate, check references and organize disclosed schedules,
with source inspection, protected data and accountable human correction.
It cannot vote, silently rank admissibility, assign a preferred judge, turn
model agreement into five votes or issue a binding disposition. A party may
challenge the framing and replace its assistant without losing standing.
Automation controllers, sponsors, shared evidence and material translation
loss must be inspectable; thousands of replicas remain one delegated scope.

**CJ16 — From judgment to received protection.** Each operative order identifies
the obligated office/person, exact lawful act, deadline, available appropriation
or asset source, responsible civilian executor, appeal/stay and evidence of
actual receipt. Final nationwide effects and the treatment of pending cases
must be explicit. A delayed declaration cannot leave a successful claimant
without necessary interim protection; criminal reopening preserves P protections
and no retrospective new offense or harsher penalty follows invalidation.

Career service, Treasury, records custodians and civilian enforcement execute
their existing duties. The C06 E15 alternate has preauthorized independent
credentials and source access for an exact due payment/record act, not a general
power to tax, allocate, appoint staff or use military force. An appeal is not
an automatic stay. Refusal consequences require individually proved enacted
grounds and fair hearing; a court cannot imprison a minister indefinitely as
a substitute for a legally impossible fiscal act.

Distinguish deliberate refusal from missing cash, receiving staff or physical
capacity. Verify usable receipt and continuing service, not just an instruction
or bank acknowledgment. Report unresolved orders, responsible offices and
lawful elected funding choices. If courts, Treasury, source custodians and
civilian execution all fail, constitutional control has failed in practice;
this text supplies neither an automatic sovereign successor nor proof of rescue.
