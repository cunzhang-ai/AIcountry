# AA31–AA35: lawful independent work has a bounded public source

*Part of [C04/04](../04-public-audit-and-accountable-oversight.md); design121 candidate.*

**AA31 — Present a separate budget, do not appropriate it.** AG submits a gross
staff/work/records/report/court/source/transition budget directly to Assembly;
Cabinet may publish its competing reasons but cannot quietly replace the request
or condition release on favorable findings. Assembly sets law/appropriation and
bounded standing core duties. Treasury pays due lawful scope through protected
ordinary credentials, with source access/audit and specific B19 substitute;
no head/committee can create tax, debt, a permanent blank cheque or an automatic
new levy. Audit-office external examiner/source is separately authorized.
General lawful public resource choices remain reviewable for actual retaliation,
not a judicial/AG power to choose unlimited spending. Genuine shortage remains
unpaid/unperformed scope, preserving claims and elected source duties.

**AA32 — Count the same actual dependence once.** D07 career/audit/procurement,
D04/D05 Assembly/opposition, D11 aid/courts/execution, D10 civil/property, R04
finance/source and K01 evidence/AI join actual affected bodies and all37/later
duties. Price auditor/quality/report/admin shifts, selection/examiner, safe data,
working replies, field/travel/technical competence, court/aid/execution and
transition once. Shared source/record/paid staff is not multiple independent
capacity. No free trainee/volunteer/AI labor, hoped recovery or ordinary fine
receipt funds the office; no old D07/D11 bid prices every new AA duty.
Private/local/G/S/client/insurance/pension/CCP/FX remain different owners/sources.

**AA33 — Independence is the full chain, not an opinion label.** Inspect appointing
patrons, paid work/career pressure, plan/frame, original information/credentials,
report release, court access and actual execution together. Public lawful criticism
and minority requests do not let an audited minister pick a case; statutory
auditor autonomy does not remove public reasons, legal review or source limits.
Same Assembly support for AG/DPP/examiner and common Treasury/vendor dependence
are retained capture risks. Do not certify independence from nominal opposition,
professional membership, peer consensus or model agreement. Independent performance
and affected experience still need actual qualified observation.

**AA34 — Methods can evolve, authority cannot hide in them.** Publish operative
methods/units/scope, uncertainty, original observations, transformations and
material objections/reasons in safe forms. Technical manuals can guide staff;
new binding accounting/report duties need actual enabling law and DR/LR scope.
No foreign standard or AI version enacts itself. AI may search/reconcile, identify
questions and support consented future communication; it cannot create a source,
statutory finding, automatic target ban, guilt, binding publication or extra vote.
No unfamiliar cooperative/financial/social form is silently excluded from public
resource scope or private rights. Preserve unsupported meanings/translation loss
and allow actual mediation/assistant replacement. Understanding, participation,
delegation and lawful consent remain separate across speech, text, models and
future media. A shared ledger settles neither value choice nor resource conflict.

**AA35 — Correction and remedies need a real lawful executor.** Final ordinary
orders name liable office/person, act/amount/deadline, actual source, competent
independent executor, protected notice/appeal and receipt verification. Private
B18/U and only expressly adopted B19 public due/state-order scope apply; the
coordinated AA Act expressly includes its established public orders, not a
judicial new appropriation. No audit report executes a private seizure or invokes
LF force without an independently lawful act. Distinguish refusal/ability from
cash or physical shortage; actual personal liability needs law/fault/proof.
Protect due care/pay/political access during an audit or budget lapse. If all
routes fail, retain unpaid claims/continuing work and disclose failed public
control, not compliance by a report or a new sovereign guardian.
