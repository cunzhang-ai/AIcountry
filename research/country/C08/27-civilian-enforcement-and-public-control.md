# C08. Civilian police, independent investigation and custody administration

9 November 2026 · Design214 · PO01–PO32 proposed institutions

**Select civilian police under elected general law, case decisions protected
from political direction, and a separately led public investigation service
for serious official abuse.** Police, prosecutors, courts, corrections, care,
defense and intelligence retain distinct duties. A professional chief is
accountable; operational independence is no personal sovereign franchise.

Police Chief: Cabinet nomination from open qualified applicants, full-N
majority plus protected non-government approval; five personal years.
Civilian Investigation Director: any Assembly member may nominate a qualified
applicant,3N/5 plus protected non-government approval; six personal years.
Both terms are nonrenewable, with no holdover. Numbers/form are normative
hypotheses, not proof of independent judgment, actual people or better policing.

P/K/Q/B/LF/OJ/PD/RX/HP and actual source-law limits remain. This adds no
offense, search/arrest/force power, broad intelligence command, political
policing, private coercive franchise or military fallback. [The finite
whole-country review](../../settlement/country-review-154/README.md) identifies
the positive command/employer gap; full national sources, actual independence,
qualified response/receiving and better lives remain unverified.
