# 209. Conflicting public orders and timely execution

[Design301](../../2026-11-conflicting-public-orders/decision-record.md)
proposes a limited ordinary appellate route when two enforceable
first-instance civil or administrative public orders cannot both be
performed on their exact terms. [SR23–SR27](../../country/C08/30/06-conflicting-public-orders.md)
preserves original deadlines, affected participation and compatible
execution. Use lawful independent capacity or timely originating court
action first. No conflict filing stays an order.

The [matched exercise](../../settlement/conflicting-public-orders/README.md)
checks one actual shared interval, an independently accepted vehicle
and an authorized accessible hearing route under authored assumptions.
Missing authority or capacity leaves unmet protection and claims visible.
Actual court capacity, adoption and useful receipt remain unverified.
