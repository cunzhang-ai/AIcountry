# Critical inputs: a purchase quotation is not a functioning supply path

Design 137 · [SC21–SC32](../../country/C04/05/06-need-and-upstream-capability.md)

**Country disposition:** retain public buyer/quality/fallback competence,
accountable plural procurement, conditional nonexclusive pooling, proportionate
qualified usable buffers and paid alternate routes. Compare retaining/repairing
supply, just-in-time contracts, domestic investment and regional shared provision.
No foreign access, several independent factories or self-sufficient industry is
assumed. Actual services and existing rights continue while new sources fail.

The [full66-row register](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-public-inputs-and-operating-resilience/input-register.json)
preserves the existing firm/plural/A purchase boundary and every owner/quote.
It has 12 primary inquiry families: clinical products; specialist care/employer
support; fuel; equipment/materials/repair; sanitation/testing; knowledge/digital/
protected sources; independent expertise; democratic/legal access; care/custody/
external support; and the three partially linked food/utility/housing interfaces.
These are not fungible skills, actual recipes or an exhaustive national catalog.

Total named quotes remain 123.986m fictional CU, of which 94.182m lack matched
current recipes; four partial links total 29.804m and are not certified either.
The six legacy clinical quotes 43.52m, including 30.72m medicines/devices, are
retired as adequate-price/source inputs. They remain historical placeholders,
not zero costs, spending caps, minimum costs or grounds for denying care.
All quantities, actual upstream/quality/source/receiving/transition fields in
the new register remain unknown. No row is promoted merely by its new category.

## The resource choice that the country must make

| Arrangement | Useful capability and unresolved burden |
|---|---|
| Retain/repair actual supply | Preserves relationships/skills; still verify useful-time capacity and obligations |
| Single-source/just-in-time | Lower inventory/qualification overhead possible; common-node and arrival failures remain |
| Pooled plural buying with buffers | Selected conditional direction; pays for qualified storage/rotation/backup and can still share upstream failures |
| Domestic public/cooperative/private production | Real control option; requires inputs, know-how, staff, safe scale, capital and transition |
| Regional shared purchase/reserve | Real structural rival/partner route; requires accepted contributions, title/release/exit, foreign law and paid continuity |

Critical specifications must include actual item/service, period/uncertainty,
source and owner/currency/date, shared upstream nodes, qualified labor,
storage/maintenance/lifecycle and useful-time fallback. Existing sector/C04
staff do that work; it is not a patient or ordinary reporter's paperwork gate.
Retain C07/TD09 quality/clinical roles, independent verification, ordinary courts,
AA scrutiny and elected burden choices. A stock signal is no appropriation,
order, quality release, political consent or permission to bypass a court hold.

## Abstract failure cases, not a new supplier economy

Opening physical stock 19 units contains 8 short-lived, 4 longer-lived, 4 already
expired and 3 recalled. Demand is 5/day for 3 days. Qualifying use removes stock
by earliest expiry; unusable items remain held, not assumed destroyed. Arrival
and qualification below are fixture assumptions, not actual product evidence.

| Counterexample | Units issued | Unmet units | Distinct source issue |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Retained stock, no new delivery | 9 | 6 | A budget or contract supplies no missing units |
| One 6-unit delivery, no disruption | 15 | 0 | Conditional quantity success is not actual readiness |
| Two labels, one failed factory | 9 | 6 | Both routes fail together |
| Two factories, backup only 3 | 12 | 3 | Independence alone is insufficient capacity |
| Backup 6, source shortfall | 15 | 0 | 40 CU of an independent 120 CU due call remains missing |
| Backup 6, source in wrong currency | 15 | 0 | No unreceived/unauthorized currency conversion |
| Additional 100-unit pending lot | 9 | 6 | Physical presence does not establish released quality |

All cases retain 7 expired and 3 recalled units after the horizon; the pending
case has 100 additional held units. Physical receipt=issued+held, with no silent
disposal. Failed nodes affect only the declared incoming routes; whole-site
quality failures, competing orders, future demand/expiry, unknown quantities,
clinical use and a full source/lifecycle cost are not simulated. The 120CU call
is a separate authored financing illustration, not a price for these lots.
Actual patient receipt, actual source and safe funded operation remain unknown.
