# RL06–RL10. Release conditions have a hard endpoint

*Design 190 candidate. Part of [C08/28](../28-sentence-review-and-release.md).*

**RL06 — Choose the least restriction that protects people.** Release without
conditions when no specific safety restriction is justified. Where actual
findings establish the risk component of RL03 under its proof standard
and show that a practicable condition can address it, use a narrow order specifying its purpose, affected acts and people,
receiver, review and end. Options include particular contact or place limits
and proportionate accessible reporting. The court explains why voluntary
support or a less restrictive condition would be insufficient. A public
service can offer care, learning, housing and employment help without making
receipt or acceptance a test of deserving freedom.

Q05 restrictions apply to the content of conditions. Compulsory therapy,
medical testing as a release condition, blanket internet bans, tracking,
home detention, forced work and poverty penalties are outside RL authority.
Independent protective orders use their own proof and periods. The same
course retains one principal penal response under Q01. RL is execution of
that custodial sentence with narrower liberty restrictions.

**RL07 — Fix a single endpoint.** Specify one recall endpoint at the earliest of the
conditional release order's stated end, original sentence expiry and
twelve calendar months from the first ordered conditional release date or
actual conditional release, whichever is earlier. Individual
conditions can end sooner. The order sets its recall endpoint no later than
the last condition's stated expiry. If the last condition ends early while
the person is at liberty, discharge remaining custody authority then.
Temporary suspension of community obligations during a valid recall keeps
the existing endpoint. That endpoint bounds all conditions, recall and
recalled custody under the order. For conduct
before 18 use six months. Any earlier running same-course Q05 supervision
clock also limits the endpoint. This is one calendar window. Transfer,
temporary recall, revised conditions or further release cannot restart it.
Time at liberty under RL cannot extend the original expiry. Credit actual
same-case custody in full. Unconditional release or the endpoint discharges
remaining custodial authority under that sentence. A different lawful case
or independent protective order keeps only its actual source and limits.

**RL08 — Correct problems in the community first.** A named qualified
corrections officer offers reachable assistance and promptly records actual
obstacles, including unsafe contact, care, illness, travel, housing and
service failure. The person can seek adjustment or removal directly through
court. A missed appointment, inability to comply or withdrawal from
voluntary care supplies no recall ground. A change that increases a
restriction needs fresh necessity and fair hearing within the original
window. Q06 continues for punitive variations within its own scope.

**RL09 — Recall requires new material facts and judicial necessity.** During
an active conditional release window, the prosecution may request a recall
decision from an independent ordinary court. Corrections and affected people
can report actual facts or seek independent protection. Recall requires
material facts arising or reliably becoming known after the operative
release decision, and the same clear and convincing RL03 proof that
continued liberty under feasible narrower conditions cannot protect others.
A breach allegation, charge, probation report or adverse score alone is
insufficient. Give usable notice, counsel, disclosure and a real hearing.

A final recall order identifies the receiving corrections officer and the
precise return act. When physical enforcement is necessary, the court issues
a specific named warrant with the order's expiry and RL07 limit. The Act
authorizes only sworn civilian police to execute it subject to LF.

A justified return to custody runs only within the remaining RL07 window
and original lawful sentence limit. Give a finite written order and a
further review under RL04, earlier if the ground changes. A recall order
cannot convert remaining years into a revived prison term beyond RL07.
Account for that protection limit when considering release. Prosecution of
fresh offenses and independent protective orders remain available on their
own facts. They cannot supply a disguised extension of this sentence.

**RL10 — Limit urgent recall and specify execution.** An ordinary duty judge
may issue an interim recall warrant before hearing only on specific evidence
of imminent grave physical danger that cannot safely await a hearing or be
managed by lesser means. Name the person, factual ground, narrow act and
original RL07 endpoint. The warrant expires unexecuted within 24 elapsed
hours of issue or earlier when its ground or authority ends. Sworn civilian
police may execute that specific warrant subject to LF's separate necessity
and force tests. Corrections receives the person and original clocks.

Custody on that warrant ends within 24 elapsed hours of actual deprivation
unless an independent merits hearing establishes RL09 and issues an order.
Provide immediate counsel and earlier review where required. Each earlier
expiry still prevails. No repeated warrants, orders or custody labels reset
the same episode. Police, service providers, a prosecutor and an AI have no
independent RL recall authority. Genuine distinct P arrest or protective
powers retain their own predicates and review.
