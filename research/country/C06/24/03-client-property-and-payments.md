# Client property remains the client's and payment errors get corrected

*M05–M06; part of [C06/24](../24-financial-law-and-loss-allocation.md).*

**M05 — Segregation is actual ownership, not a reassuring label.** A payment/
investment custodian holds attributable client cash or assets separately
from its own estate, with daily reconciliation and independent source access.
No own lending, security pledge or investment of that property without a
separate specific lawful informed mandate naming actual risks. A claimed
segregation without valid title, accessible assets and tested records is not
safeguarding. State any host bank's actual claim and M07 look-through coverage;
one beneficiary cannot collect twice through platform and direct accounts.
No protection of investment market losses is implied.

A fully safeguarded payment issuer keeps 100% of corresponding client cash
in protected accessible monetary reserves or separately held cash, with
no maturity transformation. Qualified issuers have a statutory route to a
segregated monetary reserve account under published objective access,
security and operating conditions, with V02 reasons and independent challenge;
an incumbent bank cannot veto that route. It grants no overdraft or credit.
Actual usable settlement capacity still needs demonstration before operation.
The issuer charges disclosed lawful fees or has separately
funded public support; it does not finance itself from undisclosed client
losses. Noncash client investments retain their actual risks and return-in-kind
route. Fraud or a shortfall leaves attributable claims/repair and U/M process,
not a presumed equivalent pot or automatic Treasury guarantee.

**M06 — Actual authorization, finality and correction.** A valid instruction
requires B02/B03 actual authority for amount, recipient, purpose and scope;
a credential or copied agent alone proves none. The operator publishes the
precise acceptance/irrevocability point before use, records actual receipt
and offers a cancel route until that point. A domestic retail instruction
accepted before its published cutoff is executed/credited by the end of the
next working day, or an evidenced shorter promised time. A lawful rejection
returns funds promptly and identifies reasons and challenge. Final settlement
is not unilaterally reversed by a risk score; an erroneous/unauthorized act
creates a correction/recovery claim with innocent-recipient process.

On credible disputed authorization/error notice, the provider investigates
and normally provisionally restores the disputed retail debit by the end
of the next working day. It may withhold provisional credit only for
specific evidenced claimant fraud, giving immediate reasons and independent
urgent review; a device login or disputed carelessness is not that finding.
Final reasons/correction are due within 15 working days, with one exceptional
reasoned extension to 35 total. The provider proves actual authorization
and its own lawful performance; claimant fraud/deliberate authorization
requires actual evidence, not a contractual automatic liability transfer.
No duplicate provisional/final payment; later recovery needs notice/review.

Report promptly when reasonably able; ordinary B periods and access tolling
apply, not a secret 24-hour forfeiture term. Offer assisted/offline reporting,
a lawful basic-payment route and urgent essential access under existing E/I
rights. A genuine outage preserves claims, duplicates neither tax payments
nor withdrawals and requires accepted alternative operation. No software
availability statistic proves usable food, wages or opposition payments.

[SF11–SF17](../25/06-cash-access-and-essential-payment-continuity.md)
joins actual retail acceptance, funded alternatives and nonduplicate
settlement across an outage under existing M06 correction rights.
