# Due-time resources, concentrated power and fair credit

*M03–M04; part of [C06/24](../24-financial-law-and-loss-allocation.md).*

**M03 — Liquidity and concentration are not capital.** Require unencumbered
usable same-currency liquid value L to cover a defined 30-calendar-day stress
outflow O. L counts cash and accessible monetary settlement reserves at face,
and readily saleable sovereign debt at 80% of an independently evidenced
market value. No double pledge, assumed sale market, new rescue facility or
uncommitted FX conversion counts. O is 20% of actually insured household
sight/short deposits, 40% of other withdrawable deposits, plus 100% of other
contractual cash outflows and committed drawdowns due within 30 days; items
are counted once in their actual category. Do not subtract hoped-for inflows.
In a realized stress L may be used, with immediate reporting and a reasoned
restoration/restriction plan; forced fire sale to preserve a paper ratio is
not automatically required. Existing M09 loss/viability tests still apply.

For maturity beyond a year, E plus noncallable funding remaining at least
a year plus 80% of insured household deposits and 50% of other deposits
must cover fixed/illiquid assets at 100%, performing loans remaining beyond
a year at 85%, other loans at 50%, and committed draws beyond a year at
100%. Do not count the same funding/asset twice; category and maturity
changes require actual evidence. Bad loans use their prudent net realizable
value and loss-adjusted E, not a claim that loss cures funding risk.
Design 75 closes previously unmapped categories: eligible cash/settlement
reserves require no stable-funding allocation; actually readily marketable
sovereign securities require 50%, other securities/unmapped funded assets
100%; committed draws within a year require 50%, beyond it 100%. Existing
categories still govern; no asset or commitment disappears from the scope.
These new candidate weights need fresh lawful adoption/review, not an old
bank approval or an agency's retroactive reclassification.

Net legally enforceable exposure to one actual connected borrower group
cannot exceed 25% of E; domestic sovereign exposure at most 100% of E;
related controllers at most 10% each and 20% combined. Central settlement
reserves are separately disclosed, excluded from these borrower ceilings,
and never presumed risk-free in every currency/regime. Related-party terms
are independently scrutinized and no better than evidenced comparable terms.
Collateral reduces concentration only at independently realizable net value,
without double pledge; an unsupported own-state guarantee is no exclusion.
Withdrawable deposits owed to a borrower cannot offset its concentration;
only an actually irrevocably pledged, enforceable cash-security arrangement
can qualify as collateral. No such pledge is disclosed in the old bank book.
These are national hypotheses, not a copied international minimum. Combined
risk, opaque groups and foreseeable liquidity loss still require assessment.

**M04 — A usable choice, not a compulsory loan.** Before an agreement, the
provider gives understandable total borrowing cost, fees, rate-change rule,
currency, security, default and early-payment effects with a durable copy.
No unilateral undisclosed fee, backdated rate or deceptive guaranteed return.
Assess affordability from actual accessible resources/needs, not a forced
waiver of essentials or the presumption that another person's wealth is
available. A lender may refuse evidenced unsafe credit but must give material
reasons and a human challenge; no general credit entitlement or AI score veto.

No political loyalty, lawful opposition, protected status or AI-account gate
to basic payments. Actual lawful anti-fraud/AML restrictions identify legal
power, proportionate scope, notice unless specifically delayed by court/law,
and independent challenge; no general account freeze follows a suspicion.
Collection preserves B/U needs, housing process and direct remedies. A
material departure from M duties uses VD01/VD02 and V proof, not instant fines.

[PFR01–PFR05](../67-payment-scam-recovery-and-protected-funds.md) proposes
express prior authority for narrow temporary scam preservation and a
separate paid consumer claim. M04's proportionate scope, notice,
protected basic access and independent challenge still apply.
