# AL21–AL27. Carry an apprenticeship through a move or an employer's exit

*Design396 · Proposed ordinary learning and work duties within [lifelong learning](../22-lifelong-learning-and-renewal.md).*

**AL21 — Give work, learning and assessment separate accountable owners.**
For a publicly accepted apprenticeship, the local learning authority
records the consenting learner, legal employer, host if separate,
qualified instruction and supervision, independent assessment, wages,
protected learning time, and funded continuity. Give the learner a
usable agreement and direct access to evidence of completed work.
Offer direct employment or a qualified group employer with replaceable
hosts where the learner freely accepts. The employer pays actual
wages and remains responsible for employment duties. A host has its
own workplace safety and supervision duties. Applicable child safeguarding, school and age-specific work
protections continue. The public learning authority retains responsibility for the funded qualifying route.

**AL22 — Preserve the claim at first contact and offer a dated response.**
A move, impending employer or provider failure, loss of safe mentoring
or exit from unsafe work can trigger a continuity request from the
learner or a lawful supporter. Preserve AP first receipt and use XR
for cross-local responsibility. For a planned move, seek acceptance
before the next due episode where practicable. Within five working
days of credible receipt, or earlier where an existing duty requires,
the competent owner gives a reasoned disposition with a named
receiver, safe interim instruction, the first useful date, any
pay or access gaps and an independent challenge route. Keep ordinary
learning that can lawfully continue while transfer is unresolved.
A payroll exit or provider code does not establish that the person
withdrew from learning. No approval is required for the person to move.

**AL23 — Transfer demonstrated learning without lowering standards.**
The responsible providers transfer necessary protected evidence and
independently accepted credits at the learner's direction or under
separate lawful authority. Distinguish teaching received, supervised
practice, assessment, qualifications and any independently required
licence. The competent assessor credits valid equivalent work and
identifies only the remaining tasks and changed safety requirements.
Where records are missing, use lawful alternative evidence and
proportionate paid assessment. Keep unresolved evidence visible.
A missing employer, new locality or unfamiliar training tool cannot
by itself require starting over. An AI summary or hours worked alone
cannot certify skill or confer a licence.

**AL24 — Fund a voluntary bridge with the right wage or benefit payer.**
Before admitting a new publicly funded cohort, the elected authority
funds dated interim instruction, access, independent assessment and
genuine alternate placements with compatible mentors. When the route
breaks, offer a finite receiving plan that can include public or
partner workshops, an accepting group employer or another paid
host. An already due wage, employer insolvency claim, ordinary
income entitlement and new apprenticeship wage have separate payers,
dates and rights. While no new employment exists, make any covered
learning and living support available through actual applicable
authority. Require a lawful paid employment arrangement before
productive work. Protected learning time, accommodation, travel and
care keep their own rules. No assumed grant, unpaid trial, arbitrary
repayment or household advance fills the gap.

**AL25 — Release a receiving place only with real consent and capacity.**
Verify the learner's informed choice, employer or host acceptance,
first pay, qualified supervision, accessible travel and instruction
at each relevant deadline. Separate a job opening from an accepted
place. Protect prior learners and existing mentor duties in both
sending and receiving areas. One teacher or workshop hour can
count for distinct tasks only within actual simultaneous scope.
If coverage fails, name the exposed task and keep lawful interim
instruction or supported pause available. The national learning
owner takes up unresolved essential continuity under enacted
funding and receiving arrangements. Neither an empty slot nor a
new authority label supplies a qualified mentor.

**AL26 — Review the bridge before it expires.** The elected decision
sets the funded period and any separate later source. Use an
eight-week initial planning reference with an outside review by
week four, preserving earlier rights and stronger requirements.
Before expiry, state which learning can finish, what qualified
practice remains, and the next accepted place or funded alternative.
The competent body may choose continued paid provision, another
equivalent route accepted by the learner, or a voluntary pause
with documented credit and a dated re-entry decision. An expiry
cannot cancel accrued learning or wage claims, spend another
programme's allocation or treat an exposed duty as fulfilled.
Give direct review of unsuitable placements, wrong credits,
retaliation and undelivered support. Distinguish a resolved
education case from a pending labor or insolvency claim.

**AL27 — Verify actual resumption and keep the route replaceable.**
Local elected bodies fund the ordinary offer and may replace
operators through lawful contracts. Independent academic, labor,
safety and judicial bodies retain their own powers. Count prior
learners, shared exposure, wage and provider settlement, accepted
teaching and supervised hours separately. Publish safe performance
and missed coverage. AI may compare schedules and flag possibly
equivalent evidence. Learners can use a different channel, and
competent people verify consent, credit, safety and receipt. Adopt
prospectively with paid succession and preserve valid contracts,
earlier duties and original claim dates. Reopen on repeated
nonreceipt, common employer loss, unpaid work, disputed credit or
a better qualified arrangement.
