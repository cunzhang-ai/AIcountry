# 208. Independent public statistics and lawful reliance

[Design300](../../2026-11-independent-public-statistics/decision-record.md)
selects a proposed statutory producer for recurring common series,
with plural sources, professional release and independent challenge.
[PCO06–PCO10](../../country/C14/04/04-independent-public-statistics.md)
defines bounded collection, versioned release or quality notice, safe
replication and lawful review of statistical reliance. Statistical
absence cannot deny an individual service.

The [constructed comparison](../../settlement/independent-public-statistics/README.md)
separates a 6% observed-channel rate from the all-eligible rate. A
qualified and timely independent check changes what the body can
infer. It supplies neither authority nor money. Actual participation,
coverage, appointment independence and paid operation remain unknown.
