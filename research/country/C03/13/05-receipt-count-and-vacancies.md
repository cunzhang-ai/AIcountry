# EL17–EL20: a received vote, a known count and a lawful successor

*Part of [C03/13](../13-equal-franchise-and-usable-elections.md); design111 candidate.*

**EL17 — One lawful vote, no disappearance at a counter.** Cast/return by actual
P under prior fixed hours/zone law, at funded accessible receivers. Late delivery
caused by source failure is harm with direct remedy, not a clerk's invented
extra polling day or presumed valid vote. Identity/eligibility/home disputes at
first timely receipt get a sealed conditional ballot with protected outer proof,
private inner choice, exact reasons/help and urgent independent source review.
Never publicly display the disputed person's marked vote to decide eligibility.

Keep absent inner ballots sealed/pending until P. A verified later personal
secret in-person/mobile cast may replace the same person's pending absent return:
mark the outer source/cancel status, never inspect its choice; reject duplicate
later absent envelopes. Two personal casts are not allowed. Unlink eligibility
and choice before opening/mixing the eligible inner ballots, with observers and
independent originals. If an earlier impersonation caused a false voted flag,
protect the true person's conditional timely return and investigate original
facts; no automatic rejection or guessing/removal of an anonymous first choice.
An unidentifiable fraudulent vote and its possible result effect remain visible
for ER16/EL20, not cured by an identity ledger declaring perfection.

Source checks ordinarily finish P+7days and proved certification by P+10, with
E13/B17 earlier consideration and timely pending claims preserved. A source/court
finding of actual initial entitlement can validate a timely sealed vote; no new
post-P preference. Missing material proof does not auto-validate or erase a person.
If unresolved ballots could change seats, disclose incomplete proof and use EA12/
ordinary courts rather than certify a guessed final mandate. A missed clock does
not elect a member or restore a caretaker's ordinary legislative power.

**EL18 — Evidence and counting survive the sole vendor.** Paid career teams
reconcile issued, spoiled, canceled/replaced, returned, conditional, blank,
invalid and valid ballots against exact source/custody totals, with qualified
independent observers and distinct original copies. Publish aggregate tally,
quotient/capacity/person-order trace and source uncertainty, not voter-linked
ballots, turnout identity lists or refuge locations. Small batches may need
combining within their lawful electorate before publication; a one-person local
result cannot promise mathematical anonymity. Do not suppress a lawful vote or
invent a new electorate to conceal that limit.

Machines/AI may assist reproducible arithmetic or locating discrepancies, not
infer a missing choice, decide membership, create a source or suppress a dissenting
original. Originals/alternative counting and ordinary independent review remain
usable if a vendor, EA leader or cloud refuses. Exact equality of a model ledger
is not actual custody, free choice or evidence of no intimidation. Court powers
and EA certification/votes remain their defined source, not an extra result by AI.

**EL19 — Frozen succession, not post-poll party appointment.** Determine the
initial count's capacity/person order from the frozen actual nominees. A lawful
pre-V withdrawal removes that name and its capacity; its preference cannot erase
another valid list vote. Voluntary refusal/death after first voting does not
retroactively alter already cast votes or the initial count capacity: if selected,
apply frozen unused preference/fallback succession for that list. Proved substantive
ineligibility/state error follows ER07/16 on its own facts, not this refusal rule.
Party expulsion or a later coalition alone does not vacate the person.

For later list vacancies, offer the mandate to the next willing currently eligible
unused nominee from that election's frozen ranked pool, in recorded order. No new
name, reordered loyalty list or runner-up from another list. If exhausted, leave
that actual seat vacant pending one whole-electorate supplementary poll for the
vacancy block under the same PR/entry formula with seats=m; full governing N/n is
unchanged. Existing members' mandates and original list votes are not rerun.
Supplemental winners serve only the remaining original term. A majority could
win a minority's exhausted seat at this new vote; that is a real departure from
original proportionality, preferred here to an unelected appointment/indefinite
empty seat, not proof of superiority. Court review/rights of affected lists remain.

**EL20 — Exact repeat/supplement clocks and no new ruler.** A court-established
vacancy/exhausted offer, lawful formation-failure trigger or final result annulment
has an automatic call under prior law, independent of a refusing clerk. The
needed supplementary/local repeat closes at trigger+90days, V=trigger+76; call
begins at the trigger and preserves V−60 entry and every V-based support offset.
Apply the original full N/n to governing quorum/confidence; vacant or disputed
seats never lower it. Whole national annulment/formation follows national ER17/
C03/06; local whole/partial effects follow EL12. Referendum PC14 remains distinct.

ER16–17 clear-and-convincing material free-choice/result harm,30day contest/
proved-concealment180day outer, urgent merits and ordinary appeal stay; no fine,
technical error or party accusation alone voids votes. Court cannot award a seat
to a petitioner. First/supplement/repeat plus formation source needs actual paid
staff, guides/media, ballots, receiving, custody, counsel/courts and B18/B19 civil
execution. Preauthorize one necessary repeat/supplement source, not unlimited
cash or self-renewed emergency. Another failure needs elected lawful source action;
old dues/elections/claims do not expire or acquire fake finance by a deadline.

clocks/body size/mandate rules above persist. National/local/question actual
