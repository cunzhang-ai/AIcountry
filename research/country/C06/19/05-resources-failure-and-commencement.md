# Candidate Act E17–E20: resources, failure and commencement

*Design 57 proposed clauses; [Act entrance](../19-continuity-law-and-delivery-package.md).*

**E17 — One attributed budget and roster.** Before proposing commencement,
Treasury and each delivery authority shall reconcile E04 duties to existing
budget lines, incremental obligations, overlaps and unknowns. Count each
qualified shift, cash receipt, site, supplier and reserve once. Publish safe
aggregates for normal duty, leave, standby, surge, maintenance, due-date cash,
imports and independent review/execution, including the duties displaced
by a proposed protected allocation. Shared clerical support is permitted
only where conflicting decisions, private records and direct challenge
remain separate. A table or average FTE shall not certify a safe night shift.

**E18 — Breach and remedial response.** A missed due time shall be recorded
as non-delivery or unfulfilled procedure, with responsible office, reason,
continuing duty and actual interim protection. No benefit, care episode,
court order or political opportunity shall be marked completed by referral,
dispatch, unavailable voucher, annual cash balance or expired timer. Protect
the person's direct review route; the claimant need not diagnose the
national failure. Officers shall notify affected people through safe usable
channels and disclose systemic shortfall to audit, opposition and the Assembly.
An allegation of misconduct shall receive independent fair investigation;
inability and deliberate refusal shall not be equated without evidence.
This Act creates no automatic criminal offense, fine or compensation tariff.
Existing lawful relief and individual responsibility remain applicable.

**E19 — Return the new burden to elected decision.** Cabinet shall present
a consolidated resource response at the next lawful Assembly sitting and
no later than seven calendar days after a documented material cross-sector
shortfall. If no lawful sitting is reachable, preserve attempted notice and
report that missed duty; no timer creates a new executive mandate. Opposition
and affected local bodies retain their prior-law agenda route. The Assembly
shall compare collection, lawful borrowing, feasible added capacity, permitted
reallocation and genuinely deferrable new work, with incidence, remaining
claims, local displacement, maintenance and future cost. A protected core
allocation shall state which other work loses capacity and why under rights;
the executive shall not infer it from an optimization result. Due claims
survive until valid prospective law and transition change their source rule.

**E20 — Commencement, transfer and prospective revision.** A legitimate
enacting authority shall specify the source statutes and standing lines,
exact institutional designations, eligible scope, benefit/clinical/contest
parameters, operational date and transition. Test simultaneous receiving,
qualified shifts, payment, original records, independent review and execution
before reporting this package as operating. Preserve inherited claims,
due dates, contracts, accrued benefits and pending orders with funded lawful
successors. New provisions cannot govern an incumbent's own contest to its
advantage or validate the procedure that selects them. If a future start
is missed, an elected response must state interim protection, outstanding
duty and revised lawful date; a minister cannot extend it indefinitely.
Review the clocks, safety, demand, privacy, burden and actual remedies at
six months after lawful commencement and annually thereafter, with
affected people and opposition. Those reports neither repeal rights nor
prove readiness by absence of complaints.

The [reference package](../../../settlement/continuity/README.md) exposes
conditional inclusion and displacement; its numbers are authored and cannot
populate an actual national appropriation. A court or treasury refusal,
new hazard, altered controller or missed safe time reopens the design.

[NP10–NP15](../33-protection-production-and-national-expansion.md) distinguishes
claim survival from immediate payment priority. Only its prospectively compatible
adopted law can give the core-first/comparable-unsecured pro-rata rule authority;
existing orders/priorities and restricted property otherwise remain. Core cash
insufficiency is no Treasury ranking power. E19's seven-day response, individual
useful-time remedies and public decision remain; no default enactment or repeal.
