# Design214. Lawful custody capacity and shortage response

The design now joins the demand created by justice policy to actual
occupied places, compatible staffing, due releases and timely funded
relief. Where a shortage persists, direct rights and independent
review continue. General policy and budget choices go to elected
authority. The individual order retains its own judicial grounds.
