# Upstream AI safety without licensing ordinary inquiry

Design183 · [Canonical GA01–GA24](../../country/C11/10-general-ai-capability-and-public-control.md)

Existing public DP/sector rules leave a distinct upstream question: a lawful
research controller can expose nonusers through general-model tests or releases
before a product sale or TD scheduled use. Select a narrow prior-exposure duty
across public/private/community control, including equivalently hazardous
specialized capability with no equivalent sector gate.

| Stage | Selected rule |
|---|---|
| Ordinary inquiry and truly contained evaluation | Free under actual applicable laws; no general model/training licence |
| Planned/discovered training above10^25FLOP | Ten-working-day notice,30-day screening; not a danger presumption or permission |
| Credible severe capability plus an external pathway | Proportionate inquiry, evidence and GA12 permission before the specified exposure |
| API, live testing, autonomous external operation or executable weights | Decide actual tools/access/irreversibility and feasible narrower alternatives |
| Incident or failed external barrier | Safe immediate action, at most24-hour initial notice, bounded actual stop/courts and accepted paid successors |

The General AI Safety Office has qualified protected appointment and own-case
staff; external assessors do not grant permits, DP does not license private
models, and ordinary courts review scope, merits, evidence and delay. Elected
law controls material scope. No new VD crime, punitive fine, official ideology,
master shutdown, assumed global recall or presumed paid capacity is selected.

[Decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-general-ai-capability-and-democratic-control/decision-record.md)
retains meaningful ex-post/sector, shared voluntary/insurance, broad-licence and
moratorium alternatives. Capability evidence, effectiveness, capture, open-
research cost, qualified independence and full source remain unproved. This is
one positive institutional correction, not a claim of overall AI/country safety.
