# WF23–WF28. Public staffing and the next country choice

3November2026 · Design152 · Part of [WF](../58-country-source-and-capacity-frontier.md)
· Source/capacity interpretation, not recruitment, enactment or achieved welfare

**WF23 — Retain actual public workforce scale.** Unadjusted OBESK1 reports
2023 quarterly general-government paid full-time equivalents760,671,
766,801,757,562 and765,836. Their four-quarter mean is762,717.5; health,
education and social-protection purposes together account for581,324.5.
These are jobs converted to paid full-time units, not distinct available
professionals or the whole health/care workforce. A purpose includes many
occupations and excludes relevant private/contracted work. A teaching post
cannot be relabeled a nurse, independent judge or DP incident specialist.

**WF24 — Do not turn statistical FTE into task supply.** Applying WF's
authored matched37→32 ratio to all762,717.5 reference FTE gives about135,979
additional equivalent units. This is a conditional scale illustration,
not a recruitment forecast or proof all existing employees work37 hours.
Paid hours, actual task hours, protected leave/learning, staffing mix, shift/
travel relief, trainee/supervisor needs and accepted simultaneous professional
cover need matched evidence. Do not multiply this headcount by a guessed
salary and add it to WF's compensation expense as a second labor bill.
Existing pay remains; no displacement, overtime or foreign recruit is free.

**WF25 — Compare the whole net change.** Keep separate the personal transfer/
tax/contribution distribution, actual service expansion/replacement, paid
capacity/time, capital/maintenance/imports, shared support/independent peaks,
legacy/arrears and net lawful source. Published population/income/FTE anchors
now constrain scale, but establish none of their complete candidate matches.
Reference national accounts, income statistics and quarterly employment have
different definitions/vintages; shared administrative origins supply no
independent causal confirmation. Rounded means cannot force reconciliation.
Actual uncertainty may be bounded rather than filled with invented records.

**WF26 — Retain the selected country with the cost disagreement intact.**
Keep service-first individual protection, practical human/AI-assisted access,
TW36/32, earned Y, plural production and mixed sources. Strong retain/repair,
rights-preserving U+C2, longer-hours and direct/plural/mutual/regional provision
remain materially different alternatives. U can reduce assessment dependence
while costing more or displacing other uses; targeting can consume privacy,
time and political independence. Public ownership or more AI does not resolve
these conflicts. No aggregate cash surplus justifies lost opposition or care.

**WF27 — Revise estimates without changing old law by implication.** Existing
Assembly burdens/source choices, career receiving, independent evidence/audit/
courts and useful-time execution remain responsible. WF adds no statistical
eligibility test, global identity dossier, regulator, financial-reference
adequacy, annual-balance rule or automatic timetable extension. Source data
exclusion, changed labels or new models cannot confiscate private capital,
cancel pensions, cut a child's protection or restart service/electoral dates.
Changed support needs fresh scoped review, paid lawful successors and safe
correction; public research navigation cannot approve itself.

**WF28 — Finish a bounded material comparison, not a census fantasy.** Next
country-design work should qualify representative ordinary-needs/housing and
essential-service workforce bundles against one matched price/access context,
with coherent person/source histories and preserved extra/old claims. Price
included/replaced/new work and compare meaningful service-first/U/provider
alternatives under declared conservative source/capacity ranges. Deliver a
reasoned feasible/failed/unknown disposition, not another aggregate table or
an exhaustive directory of future private life. Actual operating/research
budget and independent affected reviewers remain unknown. Reopen on changed
source definitions, excluded-person evidence, qualified B/W/service recipes,
rosters, old claims or a better whole rival; urgent harm can reprioritize.
