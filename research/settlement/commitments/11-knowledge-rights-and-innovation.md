Public grant, product purchase, background licence, royalty, prize, equity,
private creator receipt, tax and public return are different claims. A valid
previously paid foreground permission removes only its matched repeated fee,
not manufacturing/QA, foreign inputs or every later creator use. Partial/moved
scope needs fresh rights review. Open code is not a staffed compatible receiver;
nonvoluntary patent use is not trade-secret transfer, forced work or foreign law.

Preserve due I/U/Y, prior units/index/net claims, H/TW, NP/CP and actual service/
worker/property/privacy duties. New awards require an IC source/cap; existing
claims do not wait for that annex. New rights/crimes need express prospective
scope/dates and paid preparation, with stronger/old law preserved through lawful
migration. A certificate, fee, deadline or AI cannot adopt its own regime.
