# T49–T54. Fund the work of changing government services

13 November 2026 · Integrated design 434 · Candidate first-programme rules

**T49 — Show transition work beside continuing and new service.**
For each material first-programme change, the responsible agency
names the work needed before, during and after release. Include
reconciling old cases and source records, training and supervision,
manual exceptions, public assistance, operating both routes during
overlap, independent acceptance and restoring the old or an
alternative route if needed. Identify the legally competent payer,
qualified roles, location, deadlines, dependencies and next service
window. Price the work against existing cash and planned service
under T43–T48. Include burdens on residents, local bodies, courts,
workers and suppliers. A lower future staffing forecast is not
a source for earlier transition.

**T50 — Make net available work visible at each useful date.**
Start from actually available qualified people and paid time. Keep
ordinary obligations, lawful working limits and rest, scheduled
leave, independent review and contingency work visible. When work
moves to a transition team, name who accepts displaced work and
who funds it. An alternate must be qualified and available before
the deadline. Show the interfaces between departments and the
common staff, vendors and records on which their projects rely.
Never assign the same hour to two duties or count someone as
independent reviewer of their own work. Show proposed recruitment
separately from accepted assignments. Estimate overlapping pools
by role, place and date, and report credible ranges when overlap
is uncertain. Check paid cover for duties at sending services
before counting a net gain. This does not restrict worker choice.
Use aggregate public accounts and give authorized scrutiny the
necessary records without a compulsory shared applicant dossier.

**T51 — Choose a sequence through democratic budget and law.**
Before an optional material release, give the elected body the
unchanged baseline with funded repairs, a sequenced reform, and
parallel starts where qualified cover is credible. Compare scope,
dates, expected outcomes, who waits, gross overlap and failure
cost, and the next lawful service obligation. The legislature
chooses material programme scope and sources. Competent owners
execute within those decisions and applicable start dates.
A central scheduling office can assemble and challenge estimates
within existing powers. It cannot postpone enacted duties, replace
independent review, or select reforms for elected bodies. No
provider may reserve public capacity by its own filing or contract.

**T52 — Preserve legal deadlines and usable alternatives.**
If one shared role cannot cover all commitments, protect actual
due obligations under their own law. Report each exposed duty
and who needs a timely alternative, including those who cannot
use the new route. Offer a qualified, paid alternate or a
prospectively authorized narrower optional start. Where an enacted
start or individual duty still cannot be met, invoke existing
protection and independent review, preserve the claim and seek
lawful correction before the last useful date. Retain the original
deadline and actual service loss. A technical stop or restart
cannot amend legislation or undo an already accepted act.

**T53 — Release transition capacity after actual handback.**
A competent receiver verifies that the new route handles normal and
exception cases, that open claims and records are accessible and
that the qualified alternative remains available through the next
material window. Reconcile old and new accounts, access and duties.
Remove a reserve, contract or overlap shift only after its lawful
end and accepted handoff. Retain lawful pay, protection, data
custody and independently executable remedy. Test the first
released hour against continuing need before assigning it to
another reform.

**T54 — Keep evidence, decisions and automation accountable.**
Use existing budget, Assembly scrutiny and independent remedy.
Publish an accessible account of competing demands, conflicts,
unknowns and actual receiving. Affected people, workers, localities
and opposition can challenge omissions with usable time before
material choices. AI may compare calendars, expose a shared
dependency and run scenarios. Its suggested saving becomes
available only when the underlying qualified capacity is actually
released. Its output does not authorize a change, certify its own
work or substitute for paid scrutiny. Reopen on lost receiving,
unexpected duplicate work, excessive transition burden, a changed
binding date or a stronger complete alternative.
