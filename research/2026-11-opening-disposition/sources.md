# Design493 sources and scope

- [Copenhagen committee status on the municipal transfer](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Sundheds-%20og%20Omsorgsudvalget/m%C3%B8de-20052026/referat/punkt-13)
  reports the 3 May 2027 operating target. [Copenhagen's reform overview](https://sundudvalg.kk.dk/om-forvaltningen/forebyggelse-sundhedstilbud-og-behandling/sundhedsreformen)
  identifies the distinct January reform and potential agreements.
- [Copenhagen committee submission, 5 November 2026, table 1 and recommendation](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Sundheds-%20og%20Omsorgsudvalget/m%C3%B8de-05112026/dagsorden/punkt-5)
  gives the allocation by purpose and the separate municipal recommendation.
  This evidence does not establish final local acceptance or dated funds
  for this backfill.
- The [historical perimeter](../settlement/usable-public-service-guarantees/02-reference-envelope-and-open-budget-lines.md)
  and [opening obligations](../settlement/first-year-obligations-and-receiving/01-common-opening-and-overlapping-care.md)
  keep the observed starting points and payer boundaries. The visit,
  common loss and alternative releases are candidate scenarios.
