# MS05–MS08: distinguish lack of demand from loss of real supply

*Part of [national stabilization](../61-employment-prices-and-household-security.md).*

**MS05 — Demand slack is not proof people are unnecessary.** Where usable
workers/capacity and lawful unmet needs coexist with deficient orders/spending,
compare monetary conditions, protected automatic personal claims and elected
maintenance/repair/learning/service/investment with real accepted tasks and
sources. A fiscal deficit is not automatically a failed policy or a reason to
cut current rights during falling receipts. Equally, an idle aggregate does
not establish the required nurse, safe site, supervisor, imported part or useful
project. Evidence can reject the slack diagnosis. No automatic spend-to-output-
gap rule, permanent public-job promise or fictional AI production is selected.

**MS06 — A supply loss requires an honest real-income choice.** When essential
energy, food, qualified care or imports become scarce, cash alone cannot restore
all previous use. Prefer protection of actual basic access and targeted person-
side support through existing I05/SD/service mechanisms, alongside feasible
repair/substitution/qualified capacity and ordinary competition enforcement.
Show who absorbs remaining price, consumption, time and income losses, including
nonparticipants and foreign communities. Do not promise full compensation to
every consumer/firm, book an unreceived import or force unpaid household care.

General price caps, indiscriminate consumption subsidy, mandatory wage freeze
or a generic forced-work programme are not the default selected response.
A particular elected exception needs its actual law, supply/ownership/source,
duration, distribution and exit; this adds no such power. Actual public tariffs,
contract prices, minimum pay, S rights and existing urgent CP/NP/clinical law
retain their own duties. Preserving price signals never authorizes essential
exclusion, arbitrary rationing or disregard of actual need.

**MS07 — Persistence and mixed shocks remain empirical questions.** Separate
first-round imported/relative-price changes from spreading persistent pressure,
weak orders, financial failure and market power; mixed causes can coexist.
Use original price/wage/order/vacancy/input/household evidence, alternative
explanations, revisions and material dissent. A sector price increase proves
neither aggregate excess demand nor monopoly; a low jobless rate proves neither
adequate work nor overheating. The selected policy can tighten, ease or retain
within existing instruments with reasons; a supply label does not mandate
inaction, nor does above-band inflation mechanically mandate contraction.

**MS08 — Do not export or hide the adjustment.** Assess households after actual
housing/food/care/work costs, debt repricing, saver/borrower and employer/worker
incidence, region, currency and import access. More domestic nominal income is
not owned FX or restored foreign supply. A temporary tax deferral is later
liability, not free income; a loan is not compensation, and insolvency relief
retains actual creditor/worker/client law. Progressive sharing of discretionary
adjustment is preferred over subsistence cuts or captured-firm protection;
specific taxes/transfers/reallocations still need elected law and real sources.
Existing rights, private consent and protected restricted money remain.
