# Scoped finding. A government must reconcile its promises in public

Design207 links optional campaign costing, actual support for government,
a first reconciled programme and later lawful budget and sector decisions.
It preserves political bargaining, minority and opposition alternatives,
independent scrutiny, actual legal duties and continuity.
[Canonical rules](../../country/C03/20-government-programme-and-public-choices.md)

first budget tests 100 seats, a protected minority formation and a shared
capacity limit. Neither establishes a financed country, independent
