# Design392. Give recurring administrative failures an independent route to correction

[OM01–OM12](../../country/C04/13-public-ombuds-and-systemic-correction.md)
gives direct free access, scoped own-initiative inquiry and a mandatory
reasoned response. Actual care and payment remain with competent owners.
Courts retain binding relief and elected bodies retain policy and funding.

The [constructed comparison](../../settlement/public-ombuds-and-systemic-correction/01-hidden-intake-and-a-missed-care-deadline.md)
separates a missing intake record, urgent receiving, a reasoned response
and funded action. Actual appointment, costs, independent operation
and results remain open.
