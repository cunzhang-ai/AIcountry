# FB66–FB70. Fund continuing protection when several sources can fall together

*Design523 · Proposed first and forward budget rule*

**FB66 — Identify the real dependencies of each public payer.** For a material
permanent commitment or a material change in funding, Treasury and responsible
local, insurance and sector payers place ordinary obligations and proposed
additions on the four-year account. Group materially exposed receipts by actual
underlying employer, group, sector, geography, counterparty and ability to
settle. Trace receipts, refunds, credits, intergovernmental transfers and
relevant restricted balances to each paying body's actual legal perimeter and
deadline. Separately identify shared dependence in the qualified workforce,
essential supplies, external settlement and independent execution. Link revenue
and operating evidence when a shock could affect both. Publish methods and
aggregate exposure, with independently accessible original records under lawful
custody. Unknown dependency remains visible. Distinct tax names, agencies or
bank accounts alone supply no evidence of independence.

**FB67 — Use dated net sources under the same plausible adverse path.** For
each covered purpose compare the ordinary account with a documented combined
shock, using the same information at the decision date. Estimate net receipts
by useful time after refunds, shocks in demand and collection, and delay or
loss under the same dependency. Preserve coherent paths, gross flows, opening
cash and payment cutoff order through the next due window. Count overlapping
receipts and reserves once. Size authority and usable resources for each payer.
For a prospective permanent commitment, give recurring sources credit only for
the scoped amounts collectible in time on the adopted adverse path. Show
uncertainty, an independent challenge and a worse branch where it would alter
a vote. Verify each scenario with the same early commitments under JC40.
Do not add the worst values from mutually exclusive paths. Recalculate the
receipts, demand and available work induced by each exact fiscal or operating
choice before comparing it. A one-time reserve or loan can cover an authorized timing gap where its
repayment and replenishment qualify. It cannot erase the recurring residual.
Keep an independently authorized durable draw from an owned asset as an
alternative under its actual loss, payout and title rules.

**FB68 — Put the residual with the exact first-budget alternatives.** When
the combined path exposes a material recurring gap, put a separate elected
choice on the recurring source, its incidence and how it reaches the intended
payer. Compare strongest repair, a distinct recurring tax or lawful
reprioritization, and qualified use of a separate durable public asset or a
different service model. Existing payer and local powers remain. A prospective
new permanent duty proceeds only with the required source and qualified
receiving at its enacted commencement. Where a duty already applies, use
competent immediate protection and the earliest applicable disclosure, relief
and elected correction. Preserve all original obligations, restricted sources,
independent democratic and judicial functions and direct challenge. Publish
each unresolved duty and verify who bears it. An analyst, fiscal officer or
AI system cannot change individual rights, set a new tax or prioritize services.

**FB69 — Show the people and inputs that still make the budget executable.**
Price each cash alternative beside the qualified worker or assigned independent
officer, site, inputs, maintenance, restocking and actual receiving. Check
origin duties, travel and physically available essential imports where
material. Compare one ordinary disruption and the shared failure with a later
call or sustained loss. Use a truly separate route where a shared operator
would miss the protected deadline. A new supplier or national pool can count
only after showing a paid uncommitted alternative independently able to accept.
Preserve qualified competent repair if it achieves the same rights and
delivery with less burden. In a genuine tradeoff, publish different winners,
losses and costs for elected choice, including combined household tax,
essential prices and actual receipts. A successful money account cannot
certify a roster, vote, hearing or delivered service.

**FB70 — Keep the rule revisable and accountable.** The independent fiscal
reviewer tests exposure and timing alongside the competent service owners'
actual delivery evidence. Give opposition and affected residents the same
definitions, dates, adverse assumptions and review route. AI may compute and
compare scoped alternatives. The elected bodies authorize sources and the
statutory payers execute them. Reopen when a shared dependency becomes
material, a refund or shortfall crosses a usable date, a reserve is used
repeatedly, a source imposes a materially different burden or a qualified
rival can deliver more securely. Keep live claims, their clocks and paying
successors through any change. The research account creates no tax, limit
substitution or spending authority.
