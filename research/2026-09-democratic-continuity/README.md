# Democracy when institutions stop cooperating

22 September 2026 · Proposed research correction · Not enacted

The previous democratic candidate leaves important transitions underspecified.
A deadline after certification does not bound certification; a replacement list
does not resolve a blocked replacement process; formal opposition votes do not
prove independent support. These are weaknesses in the research design itself.

The [continuity candidate](continuity-candidate.md) makes the allocation of power
during delay explicit. It separates contested authority, continuing services and
enforceable remedies, and compares alternative ways of handling deadlock.
The [failure traces](failure-traces.md) expose four concrete counterexamples and
the limits of the corrections. They are authored reasoning, not performed trials.

Use the [decision record](decision-record.md) for scope, migration, objections and
the next bounded question. The [source manifest](source-manifest.json) pins the
local baseline; [validation](validation.json) records artifact checks only.

This is a scoped successor to the [21 September candidate](../2026-09-democratic-control/democratic-constitution.md).
It qualifies its D2–D5 and D8 continuity claims and adds a common research check.
It does not replace the whole institutional design, enact a fallback procedure,
alter application permissions or establish that any country is operationally ready.
