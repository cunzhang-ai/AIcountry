# Design326 decision on income shifts and public finance

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design325, TN, AT, FB, DB, Y and the working framework. Prior design
keeps current lawful sources until express replacement and selects no
technology-specific levy. Add a joined response to sustained changes in
the actual source of incomes. Preserve earned claims and reconcile
general backing before testing the recurring fiscal replacement.
Prefer enforceable ordinary realized capital-income reform before
raising burdens on low earnings or essential consumption. The project
assistant authors the proposal. Competent elected institutions retain
law, tax and appropriation authority.

## Alternatives and evidence

Compare ordinary sources with temporary continuity, a wider ordinary
realized-income base with qualified collection, higher labor or
consumption burdens, and duly owned public returns. Temporary backing
protects immediate rights and uses a finite source. Capital income can
be volatile, mobile, deferred or costly to assess. A higher labor or
consumption burden may collect earlier but can worsen material security
for those already losing income. Public returns need lawful ownership,
capital, distribution and governance and can be concentrated. Compare
these packages using effective incidence and dated net collections.
The selected preference carries no revenue forecast or automatic tax
increase.

The [primary evidence and scenarios](sources.md) establish the variety
of source structures, an observed change in measured labor share and
plausible distinct fiscal scenarios. They do not establish the cause
of a particular shift or the yield of the selected reform.

## Authority, migration and reopening

Assign the tax and contribution accounts, dated backing, finite reserve
and permanent choice to their actual instruments. Preserve applicable
assessment years, source and attribution rules, appeals, existing
benefits and funded administrators. Existing funding decisions and
claims survive political change. The adopted law must name any
continuing general backing rather than assuming Treasury transfers.
Use independent fiscal review and protected taxpayer access.

Compare the retained system and a prospective reform for one lawful
jurisdiction, with matched actual assessment and cash cohorts, relevant
refunds, contribution and benefit schedules, household incidence,
lawful cross-border attribution and paid review. Test timing under
a weaker case and show the least exposed lawful short-term route.
Reopen when receipts persistently underperform, lower-income
households bear material loss or a stronger source package qualifies.
Actual funding, legal adoption, administration and outcomes remain open.
