# Design431. Give eligible contestants practical preparation for replacement

## Decision and scope

Design430 is the pinned predecessor. HT01 already calls for comparable
preparation. HT18–HT23 provide a common fiscal baseline and GP01–GP02
provide optional independent costing. HT32–HT38 assign general
portfolio information, protected factual preparation and a useful-time
remedy before lawful appointment. The Assembly can enact and fund
this ordinary procedure within existing constitutional limits. This
revision used AI assistance.

## Rivals and costs

A standing public pack alone is inexpensive and protects private
exploration, but leaves questions that require factual help or
unpublished source verification. Confidential meetings approved by
the serving executive can be fast and keep questions private, but
their access can be withheld from a credible alternative. A new
independent agency could improve separation at the price of new
appointments, funding and records interfaces. Select a published
common pack and bounded factual service in the existing independent
Assembly administration with named career suppliers. Keep ordinary
ministerial work and independent party costing distinct.

Broader access can displace lawful work, expose private political
options or favor people who formulate technical requests more easily.
Retain staff and translation for a first useful answer, keep protected
custody, and compare equalized useful receipt across late and early
entrants. Providing current operational facts can alert a party to a
real constraint. Allow each party to contest the framing and publish
its own interpretation. Do not treat an officer's view of feasibility
as a condition of political legitimacy.

## Authority, evidence and continuity

The legislature sets the published scope and funds it. Existing
independent Assembly and career appointment, removal and assignment
rules apply. Political contestants cannot select their reviewer.
Departmental source duties, independent inspection, a staffed alternate
release and prompt judicial routes address coordinated withholding.
Incumbents retain their lawful statutory access and scope of action.
A reviewer can remedy access without setting the election calendar,
qualifying a campaign or deciding formation. Test the common
funding, people, custody and vendor dependencies of every alternate.

[Sources](sources.md) distinguish the historical UK election guidance
and the New Zealand formation process. They show different allocation
and confidentiality mechanisms. No real staffing, partisan advantage
or voter outcome is estimated. The constructed case checks separated
hours and useful-time receipt. There was no recorded affected-party
participation, independent qualified review, appropriation or enactment.

Apply prospectively. Preserve electoral dates, existing opposition and
parliamentary access, open scrutiny, protected custody, continuing
services and legal cutover. Transfer only authorized open work and
necessary records to paid lawful successors. Record expiry and any
lawful preservation hold for private material.

## Bounded test and reopening

Compare both designs during one actual scheduled or simulated contest
using the same roster, late eligibility, withheld evidence, common
correction and two formation groups. Obtain evidence from incumbent
and opposition participants, new entrants, civil servants and
independent reviewers about actual time, costs, privacy and usable
answers. Include the next budget and service deadline. Reopen on
selective access, unsafe disclosure, a displaced statutory duty, or
evidence that the common pack with simpler assistance meets the same
need. Lawful readiness and actual political effects remain unverified.
