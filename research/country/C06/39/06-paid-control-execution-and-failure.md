# Paid control, execution and four hostile failures

Design 99 · Proposed research, not enacted law or funded bank protection.

**RA12 — Sources are legally different.** Assembly appropriates panel/reserve standby and actual
activation, paid bank/insurer case teams, skilled night/weekend coverage, independent valuation,
defense/courts, secure records, alternate settlement, receiver preparation and safe closure once in
C06. Preserve separate M08 insurer assets/premiums/authorized liquidity commitment, M11 public
compensation account, M09 bridge capital, monetary eligible collateralized facility, NP unrestricted
public cash and SF safeguarded public-payment stock. No same asset supports all of them
simultaneously. Existing enacted M08 ceiling draws need no new Cabinet/Assembly case permission, but
require actual lawful payer/source cash and payment execution; increasing risk beyond it needs the
prior M15 elected statute/appropriation. Panel or insurer cannot amend coverage/premium,
borrow/create sovereign debt, issue money, guarantee losses or treat anticipated recoveries/bank
write-down as new public liquidity. Restricted insurer/client/SF assets stay within their lawful
purposes; NP cannot raid them by a general shortage label.

**RA13 — Control the evidence and receive an actual remedy.** Named bank/prudential/payment
custodians retain originals, counterpart claims, valuations/uncertainty, lien/netting packages,
current balances, coverage/beneficiary shares and every action's actual time/authority. Necessary
separate protected access/copy survives the reviewed bank/vendor; PD/KR, privilege, safe witnesses
and answerable disclosure limit collection/release. Panel case team, prudential source, independent
compensation valuer, insurer entitlement, Treasury/monetary source and payout/receiver verification
are attributed functions, not automatically independent because titles differ. Common
adviser/cloud/industry controller is disclosed and addressed through actual conflicts/alternatives.
Reuse personnel only with demonstrated simultaneous role/time/skill and consent, never counting one
person as two panel votes, court/valuer/receiver and payer at once.

M09 powers identify exact assets/eligible claims, M10 ranking and exclusions, actual source/payer,
licensed willing receiver, independent control, usable staff/systems/IP, own cash/FX and the
claims/dates assumed. Receiver appointment/contract follows panel's two-vote lawful disposition and
funded acceptance, not compulsory private rescue. Court-ordered ordinary U receiver remains that
court's different process. Preserve M11 compensation, client property, security/netting,
wages/pension/merchant/political payments and foreign-law rights. M09's termination-only stay
stays≤two working days from actual entry; bridge≤two years from actual creation/sixmonth review,
further existence requires a fresh Assembly statute and explicit losses. Renaming, sale to another
vehicle or staffing delay grants no new stay/term. Existing claims do not expire with the bridge. A
domestic order cannot compel foreign recognition/conversion, export assets or confiscate private
know-how/labor.

**RA14 — Missing delivery is not a sovereign rescue mandate.** Every payable award/order names
insurer/public/estate/receiver owner, existing authorized payment or B18/B19 executor, actual due
amount/source, time, recipient and received verification. Unavailable cash, receiver or qualified
judge is an unmet obligation, not a lawful waiver or proof that a digital notice delivered food.
Distinguish inability from willful refusal; personal penalties need actual duty/ability/fault and
independent process. Use actual M06/M07/SF routes without double payment; keep any distinct
bodily/contract/compensation claim. Already lawful safe service/settlement may continue, necessary
mandatory stops/isolation need not await replacement, and responsible owners must seek lawful
receiving/source action. No deposit holiday, withheld opposition payment, broader liquidity rescue
or authority extension follows from shortage. If no panel/ordinary U/M07/SF route can protect
critical functions, disclose financial/constitutional failure rather than invent an emergency voter
or an unlicensed/unfunded bank.

**RA16 — Compatible adoption and finite next action.** A competent Act under prior
constitutional/C14 law completes only the M09 panel and M07/M08 insurer chain, exact terms/seat
dates, case powers/source/independent court and first-staffing schedule, while preserving
prudential/monetary/markets/electoral bodies in their actual scope. No panel/self-selector/receiver
approves its own authority. Map existing deposits/coverage, pending applications/cases, valid old
appointments/terms, liens/netting, contracts, foreign law, staff/pay, public exposures and due
remedies to lawful successors; adverse new predicates prospectively only and actual stronger
rights/current deadlines continue. Fixed new dates are not contingent on a manager's
inventory/readiness certificate. Actual authority, source settlements, paid specialist pools, public
review and observed lives remain unverified.

Integrate the financial agent's actual M07 trigger/clocks/funding and M09/M10/M11/M12 constraints,
review this selected compact panel against chief/court-led alternatives and run these four bounded
cases with one attributed resource map. Fresh review must inspect the actual changed text,
especially insurer head/case separation, quorum exhaustion, direct M07 versus unchosen judicial M09
powers, compensation valuation and no doubled public source. Then return to whole-country
material/democratic priorities, not an automatic new financial board. Reopen on insider preferential
loss, absent useful-time review, unpaid insurance/compensation, forced unsafe receivers, persistent
selection blockage or a stronger complete simpler model.
