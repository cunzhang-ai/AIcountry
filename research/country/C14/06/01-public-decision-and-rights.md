# Public choice, continuing rights and exact commencement

*Candidate T01–T04; part of [C14 protected stages](../06-staged-capacity-and-protection.md).*

**T01 — Scope and successors.** A proposed transition Act identifies the
actual jurisdiction, affected residents and outsiders, powers being amended,
old and new duty owners, dates and surviving claims. Publish an obligation
map for wages, pensions, benefits, pending cases, contracts, guarantees,
property, protected records and remedy execution. Unknown claims are not
zero. No research entry or AI instruction enacts an amendment. Sensitive
case material remains protected; public reasons do not require public testimony.

**T02 — Elected choice across whole alternatives.** Cabinet presents retain/
repair, staged plural supply and acquisition/direct provision with the same
rights, cash, physical, private-finance and time scope. Workers, local elected
bodies, residents and opposition can introduce alternatives through C03
routes. Assembly decides taxes, borrowing, general burdens and the legal
programme; ordinary elections, dismissal and courts continue throughout.
A technical office advises on evidence, not whether political opposition
may replace a government. No implementation council obtains a protected term
until its project finishes. Lack of perfect service readiness is not a new
reason to suspend an already required election or obstruct founding consent.

**T03 — Rights survive transition.** Carry every valid accrued obligation and
pending remedy to a competent lawful receiver. Pay and protect under source
law during overlap; contest duplicate or incompatible claims through due
process, not automated deletion. A proposed new floor awaiting adoption is
not an existing entitlement; an adopted floor with a passed due date is owed
even if delivery failed. Research readiness reporting cannot convert that
breach into lawful waiting. E01–E20's safe-time protection, independent review
and narrow execution route apply only following competent adoption.

**T04 — Exact new start and interim protection.** Each proposed statutory
start states who is covered, the exact legal event/date, source appropriations,
interim protection, receiving and remedy duties, and a public review date.
Capacity statements and lawful commencement instruments must have distinct
names and appeal routes. Any delegated commencement discretion is expressly
bounded by prior law and contestable; an operator cannot keep a right dormant
by withholding its own certification. A missed enacted date records the breach
and requires funded elected correction. It neither repeals the entitlement
nor enlarges emergency, caretaker or provider powers. A harmful programme
can be lawfully changed; continuing liabilities and rights need explicit
successors, compensation and review rather than an impossible immutability rule.

New institutions cannot inspect or approve their own creation as independent
review. An affected public, lawful authority and delivered remedies remain
absent in this research. Actual participation must include people who cannot
use the dominant platform or safely disclose to a patron; AI-generated
objections are not a substitute for their accounts.

[CP03–CP04](../../C06/28/02-dated-commitments-and-equality.md) now selects
express elected cohort schedules for new S/Y coverage/accrual rather than a
blanket start; [C14/07](../07-dated-national-expansion.md) joins preparation,
equality and interim protection. Actual existing/overdue rights cannot be
reclassified as future cohorts; no readiness certificate suspends their dates.

[NP/C14/09](../09-national-preparation-and-expansion-choice.md) now requires

public choice. These process dates neither enact the coverage/tax programme
nor invalidate an otherwise lawful budget when missed. A separately adopted
repair/expansion Act supplies linked I/S/Y and safeguard dates, preserving T03.

[LRT01–LRT05](../02/04-award-transfer-and-linked-rights.md) carries operative
awards, their pending reviews and separately authorized linked services.
Keep the competent bodies, original dates, due amounts and sources distinct.

[T13–T17](04-linked-choices-and-lawful-start.md) specifies which new
parts need simultaneous commencement and what returns to elected
decision after partial approval. Existing starts and rights continue.
