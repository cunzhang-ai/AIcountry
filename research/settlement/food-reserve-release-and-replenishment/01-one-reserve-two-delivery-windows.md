# One reserve and two delivery windows

Constructed comparison in suitable meal packages. The affected group
needs six packages in the first window and seven in the second. The
ordinary market and delivery routes can actually supply two and one
respectively at the stated useful times. The remaining needs are four
and six. Ten safe, suitable reserve packages are available. The
protection budget has separately paid capacity for up to six reserve
deliveries per window and ten cash units in total. Each reserve
delivery costs one cash unit, including lawful receiving. Replacement
purchase has its own source and cannot arrive before either deadline.

| Response | Received in the protected group in window one | Received in window two |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Increase purchasing power without adding available food | 2 of 6 | 1 of 7 |
| Release six packages to a general market in window one | 6 of 6 if four of the released packages reach the group. Two reach other buyers. | 5 of 7 at most. Four reserve packages remain and the ordinary route supplies one. |
| Release four to the qualified receiving route and keep six for the next protected window | 6 of 6 | 7 of 7 if the remaining packages and route stay usable. |

Select the timed protected release under these conditions. It preserves
the usual purchasing power route and adds the missing quantity with
verified receipt. The selected deliveries use four cash units and then six. The general release could be justified if ordinary
access and subsequent protected supply were independently established.
The comparison does not estimate its effect on prices or other buyers'
welfare. No stock held for a later recipient is a second early offer.

If an independent safety hold removes two packages before window two,
only four remain. The affected group receives at most five of seven
unless another qualified source can arrive. A vehicle or receiver loss
can leave the same shortfall even with all six packages in the warehouse.
Record the original unmet need and funded correction attempt. Replacing
the two packages after the deadline can restore the reserve, but it
cannot make the missed delivery timely. Do not release a held lot.
