# Health spending and unmet care in a matched 2024 comparison

The financial figures use the 2024 general-government COFOG health
function from Design428. The access figures use Eurostat EU-SILC
for people aged 16 or over in private households. They are percentages
of the relevant population group, including people who report no need.
The question concerns self-reported medical examination or treatment
over the previous 12 months, excluding dental care. The survey
period is not an exact match to a fiscal-year service log.

| Observed measure | Denmark | Finland |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| General-government health function expenditure, percent of GDP | 8.3 | 7.7 |
| Unmet need citing cost, distance or waiting, percent of population | 3.1 | 8.5 |
| Waiting as main reason, percent of population | 2.3 | 8.3 |
| Waiting, lowest income fifth | 3.7 | 12.3 |
| Waiting, highest income fifth | 2.0 | 4.7 |
| Cost as main reason, percent of population | 0.3 | 0.2 |
| Distance as main reason, percent of population | 0.5 | 0.1 |

The lowest-to-highest income waiting gaps are **1.7** and **7.6**
percentage points respectively. The reason categories and combined
published value may differ at displayed precision. The estimates
describe main reported barriers, not all barriers experienced by an
individual. Excluded institutional populations, children, people
outside the survey frame and people with unreported need require
other evidence. The fiscal health heading covers different services
and populations from the survey. These figures provide neither a
causal explanation of the gaps nor a price per case.

## An illustrative equal-budget choice

This constructed comparison applies only if local evidence confirms a
similar waiting constraint. Existing duties and qualified resources
are already protected. An accepting site can provide one uncommitted
treatment slot. There is an authorized six-unit increment. Assume that
all people considered for these extra slots need the same covered
path, and that each completed assessment must be followed by one
qualified treatment on time.

| Exact use of the six units | Assessment capacity | Accepting treatment capacity | Maximum complete new paths |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Buy eight assessment slots only | 8 | 1 | 1 |
| Spend three on four assessments and three on three additional treatment slots | 4 | 4 | 4 |

The paired choice is preferred in this example. It is not a staffing
estimate from EU-SILC. Losing one of the new treatment slots drops the
maximum to three even when every assessment is completed. Actual
consent, safe priority and receipt are separate from the upper bound.
If cost, transport or communication is what prevents care, bring the
corresponding independently funded alternative to the same decision.
Existing point-of-service protection remains in force in all variants.
