# PT13–PT18: a household purchase and a lender's loss need different rules

*Part of [C08/26](../26-property-transfers-and-secured-credit.md); candidate execution law.*

**PT13 — Ordinary goods buyers.** A real buyer for value in the seller's
ordinary business of selling goods of that kind takes actual lawfully delivered
goods free of security created by that seller, unless by actual delivery the
buyer actually knows this particular sale violates the creditor's security rights. Knowledge of a
filing or the seller having a loan alone is insufficient; no retail obligation
to read hidden loan terms/search every household item. The secured creditor
has its actual debtor/proceeds claim. Actual creditor authorization can also
release an item. A later purchaser of this freed item does not revive the old
security. This is an express security exception, **not good title to stolen
goods** or a way to sell someone else's NB custody property. B38/PT02 original
ownership survives, and fraud/collusion needs actual proof. A bulk disposal of
the business, creditor taking goods against antecedent debt, sham sale or private
occasional sale is not an ordinary-course retail sale by assertion.

Outside that exception, a purchaser takes subject to perfected actual security.
A buyer for value with actual lawful delivery before perfection, without actual
knowledge of the existing security, takes free of that unperfected security;
the creditor keeps its debtor claim. No first-click contest on missing facts.
This Act creates no new first-in-time override of H tenancy or KR licensing,
NB securities or foreign/title categories. A seller must disclose known material
title/security defects and give usable proof; B/consumer remedies do not mean
an insolvent seller has actually paid a wronged buyer.

**PT14 — Default, cure and neutral preservation.** Enforcement needs an
actual contractually/statutorily due material failure, an accurate debt/credit/
interest account and the correct liable party. Price decline, refusal of AI,
political action, a prediction or an invalid acceleration alone is no default.
Give at least twenty working days after usable default/account/remedy notice to
cure or seek B/U restructuring before nonconsensual realization; earlier ordinary
due dates do not remove this opportunity. An actual dispute receives independent
review, not forced private arbitration. B17 can preserve specifically threatened
collateral earlier, with necessity, fair hearing and no premature distribution.
Perishable goods can receive a prompt court-authorized safe sale preserving
net proceeds and disputes; no lender-created urgency bypasses the court.
No pre-default waiver of these process/subsistence protections.

**PT15 — Possession and sale need an actual independent order.** A willing
holder may separately and freely surrender a specified item after default, with
safe advice and retained third-party rights. Otherwise ordinary civilian courts
alone authorize possession, diversion/collection of a security-assigned debt or
realization; a registry notice is not a writ. Even voluntary surrender does not
authorize disputed sale/distribution without court approval or all materially
affected holders' actual post-default agreement within nonwaivable law.
No private entry, lockout, remote immobilization, essential-service cutoff,
credential seizure or forced handover by lender/AI. Court-appointed civil
executors use B18 and only actual LF task/force sources. Humane physical safety,
protected equipment, U needs, safe H/C07 receiving, actual leases/collective
rights and appeals remain; a title dispute cannot erase them. Ordinary public
acquisition needs LA, not an invented creditor/court public-purpose power.

**PT16 — Fair disposal and a complete account.** The independent court
approves identified assets, actual enforceable debt, priorities, method,
reasonable costs, notice and receiving. Use independent proportionate valuation
and an openly notified competitive sale; a reasoned alternative needs actual
better net outcome/safety within rights, disclosed connected buyers and affected
reply. No lender self-purchase at its own untested appraisal or automatic asset
forfeiture for a small arrear. A creditor may bid only under the same approved
terms and independently verified priority; a credit bid offsets only its own
actually distributable secured claim, never senior claims/costs. Required senior
payments and sale costs need actual cash. Maintain a protected account of gross
receipts, actual reasonable necessary realization/preservation costs, priority
payments, residual and remaining debt. Disputed funds alone can be reserved;
undisputed balance goes to the actual entitled receiver, not the operator's use.

**PT17 — Credit every receipt; preserve actual special priority.** Pay actual
secured claims from lawful net collateral realization in PT10 order, up to debt/
ceiling, then residual to the actual holder/estate. Outside a U opening, lower-
ranked claims receive only what remains; unencumbered assets are not silently
appropriated. A deficiency has only its lawful personal/U unsecured route; no
imprisonment, forced labor, family debt or revival of discharged principal.
U stay/avoidance/ranking/discharge remains exact. Genuine non-estate client or
shared property is excluded first, not treated as junior collateral. NF21 home-
levy deferral keeps its limited-recourse actual-maturity ledger, prior perfected
claims and necessary receiving/moving deduction; no PT shortcut adds a tax
superpriority or collectible personal deficit. H15 existing-security/non-
disturbance migration remains; PT does not retrospectively bind an old lender
to a new coverage promise. In the prospective H regime a later lender takes
the valid existing protected tenancy, not vacant-title pricing by wish.

**PT18 — Remedies against wrongful enforcement.** B aid, direct court,
urgent consideration and independent execution apply without exhausting a
registry/vendor complaint. Stop an unsupported act; correct accounts/title,
restore property where lawful and compensate proved attributable loss under
actual B/consumer/public rules. State execution error, private creditor breach
and official notice error have distinct causes/payers; no universal public
indemnity for private insolvency or a truthful negative search. Preserve safe
housing/care while claims are heard without declaring the creditor paid.
Existing K fraud/theft/official abuse require their actual elements and P proof;
late paperwork, disputed ownership or poverty alone is not a new crime. Actual
received redress, not a corrected record or a successful simulation, is the end
of the remedy chain. Missing cash/executor stays an unmet enforceable claim.
