# 213. Independent public statistics and lawful reliance

| Owner | Distinct commitment | Acceptance and failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assembly and Treasury | Prior statutory scope, distinct paid line and explicit uses | Track actual cash, published capacity and lawful contingency. Preserve claims on their own source. |
| Statistical Service and local source producers | Scheduled release or quality notice with original series and usable scope | Verify actual publication, separate custody and safe access. A shared failure or late estimate limits its use. |
| Independent reviewers and court | Safe replication and enforceable statistical process | Publish independent scope and result. Order exact lawful procedure without setting the estimate. |
| Policy owners and affected people | Trace statutory use and preserve appeal, rights and actual receipt | No silent series swap. Revisit material reliance under EV and fund any valid continuity or repair. |
