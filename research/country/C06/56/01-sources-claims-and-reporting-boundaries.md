# FP01–FP04: know which money, whose claim and what real supply

*Part of [C06/56](../56-national-fiscal-settlement-and-source-boundaries.md); design 138 candidate.*

**FP01 — One country choice needs every material source interface.** Retain
the defined personal/company/VAT/site/received-transfer source interfaces,
actual public rents/distributable returns and Y's restricted insurance sources,
with their exemptions, offsets, losses, dates, owners and enforcement.
[TN05/TN17–TN18](../59/05-country-package-and-source-continuity.md) governs current
selection: PA/SD and NF numerical packages remain explicit benchmarks, not
default replacement rates. The preferred retrofit retains actual lawful sources
until express prospective replacement; a new settlement needs its own positive
founding sources. This is not restoration of withdrawn rates or adoption of
foreign taxes; retained architecture/source definitions do not set a rate.
An enacted rate is not a measured base or a collected receipt. Missing tax
events are unknown, never zero or a residual amount chosen to close the book.
Do not tax fictional profits, imputed public surplus, unpaid wages or future
AI gains into existence. Actual lawful collection/remedies remain due.

Own public payroll taxes may already appear as net cash cost; adding the same
withholding as independent new revenue counts it twice. Capital/company/personal
overlap, VAT inputs/refunds, site deferrals/local shares and transfer periods
need matching rather than gross-base addition. Contributions and restricted S
returns are not general G receipts. Public returns need paid title, lawful
distribution, actual income, maintenance and retained-capital scope.

**FP02 — Separate expense, investment, finance and liquidity.** Retain RF's
current uses, replacement/new capital, inventory, loans/equity, interest,
principal, arrears and opening cash. A matured principal needs cash without
being a new service expense. A paid reserve needs its lawful source without
being another supplier delivery. Missing capital/expense splits keep the
operating/recurring result unqualified; do not label every prepayment a current
expense or treat a financial subscription as GDP. Pension annual units and
future liability are not current pensions or spendable personal assets.

Liquidity can fail before an otherwise sourced annual programme; a recurring
shortfall can persist despite an opening bridge. Neither case is resolved by
renaming tax, debt or reserves. Existing cash and new borrowing carry titles,
restrictions, currencies, maturities, source/price risks and displaced uses.
RF remains a planning selection, not a constitutional annual-balance rule or
a claim that all sovereign spending requires prior private saving.

**FP03 — A reporting boundary confers no ownership.** Keep gross G/S/local,
public/private/foreign and fiduciary obligations visible. Matched internal
flows cancel only inside an explicitly declared reporting set; this cannot
change that same set's net position. Reporting a larger set is a different
question, not a saving or permission to sweep restricted pensions, renewal,
withholding or political funds. Statistical public-sector membership and
social-contribution rerouting require actual institutional classification;
the diagnostic account sets are not certified general-government accounts.

**FP04 — Finance and productive capacity constrain each other.** A public
payment is not proof of qualified staff, safe useful-time supply or owned FX.
Private consumption/investment/working capital, wages, rents, taxes, saving,
credit and external counterparts must remain mutually consistent; income
transfers alone supply no missing production. Do not infer a national output
target from a desired tax yield or fill residual demand with invented companies.
SC's retired clinical quotes, all other unpriced duties and maintenance stay
unknown. Foreign currency, permitted imports, actual sellers and later foreign
claims retain their own acceptance and ownership requirements.

[Design177 production/source bridge](../58/13-public-offer-production-and-source.md) joins public production,
consumption, fiscal spending and one declared service/capital change. Direct
and upstream requirements are not extra invoices or fiscal sources; same-
service TW wage/time cost is not proportionally more real service. Own-account
R&D adjustments need paired capital/revenue treatment, not a cash inference.
Original import/duty/ownership boundaries and unpriced actual sources persist.
