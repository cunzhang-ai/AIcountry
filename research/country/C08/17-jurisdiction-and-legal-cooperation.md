# C08. Jurisdiction, applicable law and legal cooperation

15 October 2026 · Design 73 · Proposed B41–B52/P21–P24, not enacted law

Select **connected, rights-protecting legal cooperation**. Separate forum,
applicable law, recognition, evidence powers and actual execution. Assess
actual laws, treaty scope and usable foreign cooperation independently.
Foreign people retain legal standing; jurisdiction needs an actual lawful
connection and cannot be generated by a server, account or accusation alone.

The [C10 cooperation route](../C10/06-legal-cooperation-and-protected-transfer.md)
assigns diplomatic/justice receiving without letting a minister select the
result. Domestic human courts retain independent individual decisions,
funded defense, source evidence and review; the Assembly enacts general
law and external commitments. AI assists translation/search, not jurisdiction,
conviction, political-target selection or final transfer decisions.

The [boundary journeys](../../settlement/jurisdiction/README.md) distinguish
closed borders, broad extraterritorial reach and this connected alternative.
They are authored reasoning, not cases, consent or proof of better lives.
An enforceable order and a paid/received remedy remain distinct. Foreign
acceptance, actual treaty status and independent capacity are not assumed.

These rules do not enact universal jurisdiction, classify every future asset,
waive actual immunities, determine treaty membership or import new international
crimes by a live link. [G01–G14](18-international-crimes-and-accountability.md) now defines a national
international-crime candidate; actual treaty implementation and sector/
reproductive/conflict-detention rules remain open. P16 caps stay: the Rome Statute's own
penalty provisions do not by themselves mandate national thirty-year/life terms.
Source-law reconciliation and real qualified review remain prerequisites to
adoption. Existing actual duties survive; no software or constitutional change.
