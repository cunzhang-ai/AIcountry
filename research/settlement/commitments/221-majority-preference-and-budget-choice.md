# Design308. Comparative budget priority and separate enactment

[BCV11–BCV15](../../country/C06/60/08-majority-comparison-and-final-budget.md)
uses recorded individual comparative preferences and the full-seat
majority. The same Clerk and elected Assembly carry the decision,
with actual time and records for correction. The recovered text
qualifies for comparison and first fallback when another text goes
first. A final vote authorizes only its exact budget; separately
required source and legal instruments retain their own votes. A
funding or release gap remains open after political agreement.

Prior votes, financial and programme obligations, standing payments,
independent remedies and pending cases continue under applicable law.
Fresh ordering never authorizes fiscal lapse spending on its own.
No authority migrates from an actual government or to an AI by
publication of this proposal. See the [decision record](../../2026-11-budget-majority-and-final-choice/decision-record.md)
for evidence, objections and the paid readiness question.
