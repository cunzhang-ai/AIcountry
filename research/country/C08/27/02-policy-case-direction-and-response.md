# PO05–PO08. Political control is not control of an opponent's case

*Part of [PO](../27-civilian-enforcement-and-public-control.md); retain P/LF
grounds, personal responsibility, independent charging and judicial authority.*

**PO05 — Publish lawful priorities through the budget.** Cabinet proposes
ordinary national policing priorities/resources with the annual budget;
Assembly sets law, appropriations and political accountability. Territorial
commanders receive local council/resident/worker input on actual danger,
access and unserved need, publish safe priorities and resource reasons, and
remain bound by national rights/guarantees. No closed interest group owns
priority, no mandatory incident-by-incident public consultation, and no local
leader may license harassment or veto a lawful case outside the locality.
Priorities cannot immunize allies, suppress evidence or equate dissent with crime.

**PO06 — Investigation and prosecution have different signatures.** The
assigned competent investigator holds actual P statutory evidence duties,
collects contrary material and follows lawful reasoned prosecution/court
directions within their scope. DPP/prosecutors make OJ/P charge/noncharge
decisions; judges decide warrants/liberty/trial/remedy. A prosecutor's request,
Chief's plan or minister's risk warning is not a P07 warrant, P09 arrest
ground or LF permission. Preserve the original factual episode and custody/
hearing clocks; a renamed team or transfer restarts none. Own-abuse routes
are outside the implicated command, not an optional ordinary allocation.

**PO07 — Own the operational instruction, preserve dissent.** Chief and
qualified territorial/incident commanders allocate lawful operations, staff
and methods. Material directions/reassignments affecting a case have an
identified author, lawful source, original reasons, conflicts and contrary
material; no secret oral political order, deleted dissent or sham officer
signature. A changed allocation preserves the original file/receipt/dates
and qualified receiver. A Chief may correct unlawful or unsafe work but cannot
dictate an arrest/closure for political advantage, command a prosecutor/judge,
conceal official abuse or create a new offense by plan. Protected refusal of
manifestly unlawful instructions and direct useful-time review remain.

**PO08 — Respond through the competent service.** Public first contact offers
reachable human/phone/assisted/offline routes under P06, with police, clinical,
care/peer, fire/rescue and other actual statutory receivers. A common intake/
dispatcher does not merge authority, private records or budgets. First receiver
retains triage/protection until a competent successor accepts; an unanswered
referral is no response. LF16's plural civilian/noncoercive options remain
actually paid alternatives, not forced unarmed exposure or compulsory clinician
police work. Genuine immediate danger receives existing lawful necessary
protection; absent staff neither creates coercive powers nor erases the duty.
AI can assist routing/evidence work under DP/PD, never invent consent, grounds,
qualification, a charge or a person available at two simultaneous incidents.

[AX04/AX09–AX10](../../C03/18-peaceful-assembly-and-public-dissent.md)
now proposes actual facilitation/public-space and PO command interfaces for
ordinary assembly. Existing qualified predesignated civilian commanders alone
hold its bounded on-scene function; no political case direction, new arrest,
search, weapon, custody or contractor/defense AX physical power follows.
Courts and unconflicted actual source/receiving remain independently necessary.
