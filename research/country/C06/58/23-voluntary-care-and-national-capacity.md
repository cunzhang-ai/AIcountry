# Count public care capacity when unpaid support may change

*Design289 · WF100–WF104 · Proposed whole-country comparison rules.*

**WF100 — Publish linked need, delivery and budget accounts.** For
adult home support use the same eligible needs and time windows
across alternatives. Keep assessed need, accepted informal help,
paid service, bought standby, actual receipt and uncovered need
distinct. Count paid workers once across delivery, training and
reserves. Preserve actual scope of national-account worked hours.
Measure unpaid support and household time separately rather than
turning it into public revenue or recruitable paid hours.

**WF101 — Fund the same obligation through a changed household.**
Cost ordinary shifts and respite, paid availability, activation,
transport, lawful replacements, review and administration on
their applicable dates. Treat the amount required on activation
as part of the authorized exposure even when ordinary cash
outlay is lower. The finance authority attributes standby and
actual draw to their named payers and replaces any used capacity
on the funded schedule. Intergovernmental advances affect owned
payment capacity. Consolidate their transfer only once. National
equalization under MF considers need, access costs and physically
usable cover, without treating local family availability as a
permanent deduction or assigning help to a specific person.

**WF102 — Compare paid ordinary cover with retained and pooled
alternatives.** Use [HS14–HS18](../../C07/25/04-voluntary-care-and-paid-continuity.md)
for consent, withdrawal and useful time. The local authority
proposes own staffed provision, genuinely usable shared coverage
or a mix. Compare on the same accepted assignments, ordinary
duties, shared losses, second call and safe repair period.
Check low-density routes and protected worker time. The Assembly
and elected local authorities decide their lawful sources and the
actual opportunity cost. A contracted payment, average caseload
or optional training place supplies no current shift.

**WF103 — Expose household effects beside fiscal outlay.** Track
actual respite and the affected groups' time, safety and practical
choices. Keep changed paid work, income, earnings credits and
tax in their own scopes. Avoid inferring that each hour of
public replacement creates an hour of market work or a new
tax receipt. Likewise distinguish real avoided admissions from
a cash release that a competent payer can actually use. Publish
ranges or unknowns for material missing demand and disaggregate
where safe. A fiscal comparison must show any service difference
on the same page as its savings.

**WF104 — Correct a funding gap without erasing the claim.**
Before a national rollout, validate local shift acceptance,
dispatch and standby payment, rest and travel, independent review
and the replenishment date. A payer's missing funds or a failed
contract remain visible on the original time and rights. Continue
prior-law care and eligible independent payments. Pursue the
lawful repair and funded correction under MF, NP and FB.
Household reports can trigger review without entering a public
workforce dataset. Reopen if pooled cover loses timeliness,
standby crowds out ordinary care or permanently staffed
provision performs better at a comparable cost.
