# DF06–DF10: armed institutions cannot own their political or legal escape

*Part of [DF](../12-civilian-defense-and-service.md); existing equal-standing law applies.*

**DF06 — Neutral force, citizens with rights.** Service members keep individual
vote, lawful private belief/expression, association, complaint and ordinary
legal protection. Do not collect votes by unit, inspect ballots or require a
party oath. Forces as institutions cannot endorse/sponsor parties, interfere
with peaceful opposition, claim reserved Assembly seats or certify results.
Serving commanders cannot simultaneously hold party executive or elected
governing office; lawful leave/relinquishment of command preserves access to
candidacy and actual career rights under general law. This is no blanket
private political-speech ban or permission to suppress unions/complaints by label.

**DF07 — All force sources answer to elected public finance.** Defense pay,
procurement, donations received by the institution, grants, asset receipts,
foreign assistance and guarantees enter the ordinary disclosed C06/SC public
account, with justified protected detail and AA/Assembly/court access. No
off-budget payroll, punishment/forfeiture bounty, patron donation command,
private sponsor veto or self-created levy. Gross sale, internal transfer,
future recovery and forecast AI gain are not recurring national sources.
Protected Y/client/pension/private property remains separately owned.

**DF08 — No military commercial estate.** Armed-force commands/units may
not own/control profit-seeking businesses or investment vehicles, use coercive
office to win commercial privileges, or retain business income as an autonomous
military budget. Needed repair/manufacture/logistics can remain a paid public
service or separately civilian-owned public/cooperative/private supplier with
ordinary liability, SC competition/source and no command-controlled commercial
veto. This does not confiscate members' lawful private savings, pensions,
veteran associations or employee property merely by affiliation. Actual
conflicts/beneficial control are proved, not inferred from a surname or rank.

**DF09 — Ordinary criminal courts, bounded professional discipline.** In the
selected future system, ordinary OJ courts/prosecutors have criminal, civil
and rights jurisdiction over service members, including service-related
offenses actually defined in primary law. Create no separate command criminal
court or Chief-controlled trial. Professional discipline can address proved
lawful-duty breaches with notice, reply, reasons, proportionate employment
measures and independent ordinary review. It cannot impose confinement,
criminal punishment, forced unpaid work or detention for contract debt.
Liberty requires its actual P/CD/other ordinary-law ground and court clock.
No new desertion/insubordination crime is enacted by this research text.

**DF10 — Investigate our own command through actual civilian powers.** Serious
force/custody/corruption/retaliation/evidence abuse uses existing PO outside-
command receiving/investigators and unimplicated OJ prosecution/courts, with
actual DF statutory military-case assignment and source access. No new warrant,
secret intrusion or guilt presumption. Implicated Chief/minister cannot choose
the sole investigator, pay gate or closure; PO own-director conflict rules also
apply. Military boards may learn operational lessons but cannot replace
criminal inquiry or suppress original evidence. Actual qualified access and
safe independent execution, not a different office/model name, is required.
