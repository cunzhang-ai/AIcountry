# Evidence for parallel local trials and shared obligations

| Primary source | Observation | Use and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Norway, Pilot Schemes in Public Administration Act, sections 4 and 5](https://lovdata.no/NL/lov/1992-06-26-87) | The published statute distinguishes trial authority and approved trial-specific rules from limits on changing individuals' rights and duties. It assigns adoption of municipal trial regulations to the relevant municipal or county council within the statute's process. | Distinguish varying administration from modifying rights and separate local choice from the authority to make a legal exception. It supplies no law or central approval model for AIcountry. |
| [Murray–Darling Basin Authority, surface water diversion limit resource units](https://library.mdba.gov.au/data_product/surface-water-sdl-resource-units) | The official dataset describes the boundaries of surface-water resource areas and a long-term average diversion limit. | Use a common mapped source and a stated measurement window. A long-term average is not the water actually available in any particular trial window. The Australian boundaries and quantities are not used here. |

The [worked comparison](../settlement/parallel-local-trials/README.md)
uses authored councils, permissions, residual water and budget.
The sources establish no actual overlap, participant consent,
trial result, constitutional choice or authority for this project.
