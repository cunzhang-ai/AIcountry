# T100–T104. Make the national sequence a real budget choice

13 November 2026 · Integrated design 447 · Candidate first-budget rules

**T100 — Carry the complete reference and identify the changed uses.**
Build on [T85–T90](19-joined-opening-budget-and-release.md).
For each national motion give the full retained purpose perimeter
and identify the actual affected lines by legal payer, recipient,
expense type and time. Publish which links are observed, inferred or
missing. Keep staff compensation, provider invoices, household
transfers and capital distinct. Purpose, industry and ownership
describe different dimensions. A statistical total cannot be moved
across them to produce a vacant position or available cash.

**T101 — Count the receiving work and its displaced use.**
For each reform give ordinary, conversion and successor work by
qualified role and useful date, including independent review. When
direct hiring, a purchased provider or reassignment supplies a
role, verify acceptance, supervision and the originating service or
production it leaves behind. Record any real replacement and its
payer. Record household time, expenses and lost service where a
public offer transfers work to households. Distinguish one-off
conversion and recurring expense. Credit lawful realized releases
once and preserve gross commitments until they actually change.

**T102 — Compare dates and ways of delivering the same protection.**
Submit strong retain-and-repair, phased reform and the fastest
proposed reform on one continuing baseline and the same interruption.
Where the result depends on delivery form, compare a credible
direct, contracted or other route on actual control and receiving.
Show the useful date when cash, accepted resources or independent
review first fail and the smallest evidenced change that could
reverse the result. Publish the human cost of waiting and the
fallback if a proposed start cannot operate. A higher annual
total or more unqualified hours alone cannot reverse the result.

**T103 — Give the elected choice funded alternatives.**
The Assembly chooses commencement, funded repair and any changed
burdens under the existing budget and legal process. Pay for
opposition analysis and independent correction of shared assumptions.
Treasury and sector officers give auditable cash and capacity evidence.
Reviewers can expose a missing condition without acquiring a power
to delay enacted rights. A staged motion specifies its funded early
uses and the subsequent decision or conditions lawfully voted for
later starts. An accelerated motion can qualify with its own prior
authority, independently usable receiving and qualified sources.
Missing evidence leaves the affected new commitment open. Retained
duties, appeals, original dates and actual claims remain enforceable
through their own law.

**T104 — Reconcile implementation before expansion or renewal.**
Show actual receipt, first unmet protection, paying and losing
parties, released worker time and next-period cash. Review original
supplier and staff dependencies before treating a later window as
independent. AI can compare assumptions and locate inconsistent
claims. It cannot appropriate funds, certify actual service, rank
legal claims or approve its own savings. Carry all binding duties
and counterpart losses through election, agency or provider change.
Reopen when received service, accepted staff, household burden,
source availability or a stronger rival changes the comparison.
