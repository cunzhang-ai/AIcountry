## England. A permission to use capital is a distinct fiscal instrument

An official direction dated 4 August 2026 permitted Peterborough to
treat up to GBP 4.039 million of specified properly incurred 2025/26
revenue expenses as capital expenditure. Where the resulting capital
financing requirement increases, the direction requires minimum revenue
provision under a proxy life no longer than 20 years. It does not
establish a national cash transfer, the actual financing chosen or a
recurring receipt. The council's actual source, later repayment and
service receipt remain separate tests.
[Peterborough capitalisation direction, paragraphs 2–5](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a7ee8ef0ad4bc475be7de90/Peterborough_Capitalisation_Direction_2025-26.pdf)

## United States. A court-supervised adjustment is distinct from government

Federal Chapter 9, subject to its authorization and eligibility rules,
permits municipal adjustment initiated by the municipality rather than
ordinary private liquidation. Section 904 limits judicial interference
with government, property and revenues, with statutory exceptions.
Section 943 includes feasibility, creditor interests and applicable
regulatory or electoral approvals in confirmation. The broader federal
stay, eligibility, protected claims and confirmation rules are their own
law. This comparison does not adopt them or assert their superiority.
[United States Courts, Chapter 9 basics](https://www.uscourts.gov/court-programs/bankruptcy/bankruptcy-basics/chapter-9-bankruptcy-basics)
· [11 USC 904](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title11-section904)
· [11 USC 943](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-134/t11/s943/)

## Decision

Keep ordinary budget and grant arrangements as the observed strong
baseline. Identify each timing, recurring and debt gap in the first
country budget. A finite permission, a cash bridge, capital borrowing
and a permanent change in service funding require different votes and
sources. Retain local elected authority, separate paid service delivery
and actual independent challenge through a narrow prospective workout.
No cited amount prices that option or transfers resources to it.
