# Design420. Make the evidence for public AI replaceable and contestable

## Decision and baseline

Design419 requires a service owner, meaningful exit and continuing
rights, tests ordinary routes, and protects legal judgment from a
common inference. It does not assign who controls the final
cross-supplier qualification sample or how to respond when ostensibly
independent alternatives share a blind spot. This revision proposes
a bounded evidence process for material reliance on public-service
AI. It was drafted with AI assistance. Elected law and qualified
sector decision-makers retain actual authority.

## Alternatives and working choice

A public fixed benchmark is cheap to repeat and easy for people and
suppliers to inspect. Keep it for transparency and known regressions.
Over time its cases or scoring proxies can become part of optimization,
and its published sample can miss affected people. Vendor testing
allows rapid diagnostics but leaves the same controller specifying,
running and grading the qualification claim. A single national
certifier could consolidate skills but would concentrate the power
to define acceptable behavior across different legal sectors.
The working choice pairs open examples with scoped independent fresh
checks under existing sector and review structures. Keep procurement,
lawful reliance and actual delivery separate.

Protected cases and rotating samples can make assessments less
transparent, more expensive and less stable over time. Publish
methods, uncertainty and safe retired examples, fund protected
replication and keep a route to contest changed criteria. Qualification
follows the exact use and dependencies. Reuse stable evidence only
after checking source validity and new events. Fresh samples are
especially important after material exposure, incompatible cases or
changes to interpretation. Where no protected fresh set can safely
be obtained or replicated, show the gap and narrow reliance, rather
than treating private test scores as evidence of coverage.

## Evidence, migration and authority

[Primary comparators](sources.md) identify independently assessed,
context-specific evaluation and the use of fresh cases in general
benchmark research. They do not qualify benefit casework or establish
a required numerical error rate. The worked exercise stipulates
its cases and time. No actual beneficiaries, vendor, sector authority
or independent reviewer participated in this revision.

Apply prospectively to the covered new purchase, material renewal or
reliance change. Keep contract rights and previously due public work
on their original authority. The adopting law names the review office
for each sector, its appointment and removal route, source and paid
alternate. The Assembly controls finance. Existing accountable sector
officers set lawful requirements. Review staff control assessment
materials and can publish safe findings outside the buyer's control.
The buyer issues the award under ordinary procurement. Sector owners
accept actual operation. Courts and competent reviewers retain direct
challenge and independent remedy, with protected records and funding.
Keep prior tests, privacy restrictions, original claims and a paid
operator through replacement. Findings alone confer no new authority.

## Scoped result and next test

The constructed comparison supports a narrow research choice where the
paid ordinary route works. No actual deployment, sample adequacy,
qualification, service improvement or acquisition has been established.

For one ordinary benefits assistance task, the competent owner and an
independent assessor would compare the exact incumbent, an alternate
and an ordinary route on a protected fresh sample. Complete when each
has a reproducible assessment of errors, uncertainty, ordinary-route
capacity, receipt and consequences of the first shared failure. Give
affected groups and workers a safe opportunity to challenge the test.
No assessment budget, supplier or service owner has committed to this
exercise. Reopen on a common data or grading source, affected-group
exclusion, blocked independent inspection or cheaper ordinary delivery.
