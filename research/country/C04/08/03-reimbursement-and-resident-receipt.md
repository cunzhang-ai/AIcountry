# MF09–MF13. Keep resident payments ahead of public reimbursement disputes

*Design275 · Proposed national and local payment rules.*

**MF09 — Give the resident duty and the public settlement their
own clocks.** For a material common duty delivered by a locality,
the adopting law identifies the payer to the person or service,
the ultimate funder, the legal basis and evidence for each claim,
the last useful time and the dispute forum. The first budget
prices both calendars. Record eligibility, award or accrued
obligation, authorized transfer, local usable cash, actual
recipient payment and later intergovernmental settlement as
distinct facts. A grant notice, accepted invoice or published
annual expenditure does not prove the next fact in the chain.
Use [WF90–WF94](../../C06/58/21-national-cash-and-first-budget.md)
and [independent receipt](../12/04-independent-payment-and-receipt.md).

**MF10 — Finance the duty before requiring another body to perform it.**
For mandatory common provision, prefer a lawful dated advance
of the assessed near-term undisputed cost where a locality
cannot safely carry payment in arrears. Compare a reimbursement
route only with an identified, paid and lawfully usable local
bridge, protected other work, an end date and a real bearer of
denial or delay risk. The Assembly specifies advance conditions,
any local share, recurring cost, repayment risk and ceilings.
The local elected body retains its budget and lawful delivery
choice. Neither a forecast claim nor presumed local reserves
finance the work. Do not require eligible people to fund a
public payment delay or submit a duplicate claim because two
public offices cannot settle.

**MF11 — Split undisputed release, contested evidence and provisional
protection.** Pay or release the undisputed amount when due under
its own law. Keep the narrower contested amount, reason, original
date and qualified review route visible. If a delay threatens a
resident deadline, use any separately authorized advance or paid
alternate within scope and cap. A receiving officer verifies
funds and the person-side payer checks actual receipt. Keep an
independent route when a common payment rail or office fails.
If the advance or a substitute cannot reach the required amount,
report the original unpaid duty and request actual elected
correction. The funder gains no power to hold unrelated transfers
or use a pending dispute to direct local political choices.

**MF12 — Reconcile the final public account without disturbing valid
resident payments.** Match the original award, public transfer,
provisional funds, returned payments, final allowed amount and
any repayment to their own dates. Credit prior payment against the
same liability once. A denied reimbursement is a public loss or
claim against the proper actor as determined under source law.
It does not itself prove resident overpayment. Price a lawful
reserve, assign residual risk and keep actual underfunding visible.
Only the competent authority may correct a resident award under
its ordinary substantive, notice and review rules. Preserve both
authority and evidence when the administering office changes.

Where prospective law permits, offer an optional reciprocal settlement
between the exact public obligors. Both competent payers authorize the
same pair of due, undisputed obligations in the same currency and
with compatible purposes and restrictions. Verify no assignment, legal
stay, higher-priority claim or competing pending or final payment covers the matched
amount. Preserve both gross records. A legally indivisible discharge
reduces both outstanding amounts only upon its verified effect. Identify
the residual cash, prefunded protected service and next call separately.
Use a separate residual payment only where the binding terms allow it.
Retain ordinary payment without a penalty for refusing to match.
Do not withhold an earlier obligation for an incoming promise or use
private funds, funds restricted to another purpose or independently
due protected funds to close the
pair. Adoption supplies no automatic set-off or new priority.

**MF13 — Test actual payments and democratic correction.** The
first-budget comparison follows an exact national or regional
payer, a local receiving and paying body, and separately verified
recipient payment or service. Publish safely aggregated outstanding
claims by age, scope, undisputed and contested amount, last useful
date and corrective action. Keep confidential claim evidence
available to qualified independent reviewers through lawful
custody. Give elected bodies and opposition usable evidence
of delay or persistent local self-financing. AI can match
authorizations, invoices and reported receipt within lawful
scope. It cannot declare an amount payable, close a dispute or
certify receiving. Reopen on repeated bridging, uncorrected arrears,
denial of actual service or a more workable direct payment route.
