# Design407. Preserve essential household electricity when payment controls fail

[EPC01–EPC08](../../country/C09/19/05-essential-electricity-and-payment-controls.md)
applies the current guarantee to balance-based, shared and intermediary
controls. Keep ordinary billing usable, protect necessary use before the
deadline and reconcile current charges and prior claims separately.

[Worked case](../../settlement/essential-electricity-and-payment-controls/01-two-billing-windows-and-a-live-line.md)
checks late credit, old debt, a live line and actual alternate receipt.
Real costs, safety, operating capacity and outcomes remain open.
