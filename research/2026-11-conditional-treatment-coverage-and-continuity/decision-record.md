# Design376 decision on conditional coverage and continuity

## Baseline and selected route

Pin Design375, the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md), S01–S04,
HG, TD, MQ and OA. These separate existing clinical coverage,
product authorization, independent review and shortage response.
They do not yet specify how a finite evidence-limited offer starts
and ends without exposing people already treated to a funding cliff.

[CTC01–CTC07](../country/C07/14/01-conditional-treatment-coverage-and-continuity.md)
selects bounded conditional access under elected law, independent
appraisal, gross dated funding and a safe funded exit. Clinical,
purchasing, review, marketing and budget authorities stay distinct.

## Rival choices and objections

Keep competent ordinary care while evidence develops, adopt supported
ordinary coverage when the evidence and capacity qualify, or use
a bounded conditional offer where additional evidence can resolve
a material uncertainty. Compare waiting harm, access barriers,
staffing, opportunity cost, privacy and practical alternative care.
A conditional fund can invite supplier influence, repeated
extensions, displaced care or an evidence burden on vulnerable
people. Independent reviewers and patient and worker input must
be usable. Keep final criteria and original dates visible. Preserve
ordinary care and rights for people declining optional participation
or waiting for a finite place. Any research requires its own authority.

A dedicated fund can simplify cash and procurement while separating
one clinical condition from competing needs. Use the existing NCS
and elected budget system with an identified source for each
conditional offer. New broad coverage or hidden displaced duties
cannot enter through a commercial agreement or model score.

## Evidence, migration and next action

[Primary records](sources.md) show a real distinction between
notifications, reported drug costs and reappraisal. The
[worked comparison](../settlement/conditional-treatment-coverage-and-continuity/01-two-patients-and-a-funded-exit.md)
shows where later money or an accepting slot defeats an apparently
funded start. Actual affected participation, independent review,
authorization, source and improved outcomes remain unknown.

Preserve original awards, consent, records, ordinary claims, paid
operators and remedy dates. A bounded next exercise would cost
one proposed indication using independent clinical review, actual
comparator care, safe original aggregate evidence and a later
capacity check. Stop at a costed finite choice or documented
reason to retain ordinary care. Reopen on unsafe continuation,
unresolved evidence, changed prices, lost access or a better
supported simpler route.
