This is a substantive distributional choice: the independent foundation is
protected, while other ordinary personal income starts contributing immediately.
An additional personal-income allowance or retained I05 destination is a live rival
with different gains, assessment burdens and public cost. Taxable U with an
exclusive matching offset is merely an equivalent representation, not new money.
The selected rates/widths are contestable hypotheses, not empirically optimal
numbers or authority supplied by a model. Full national financing is not selected.
