# IV18–IV22. Keep services working after public assets open

*Part of [public investment](../63-public-investment-and-asset-renewal.md).
Design249 research selection.*

**IV18 — Separate condition, money and service obligations.** Each material
asset owner keeps a dated account of future preventive work, overdue tasks,
actual contract or legal liabilities, available cash and unmet service.
Name the service supported, maintenance owner, observed condition, source
method, applicable standard, last supported safe date or unknown, and effect
on residents and workers. Record safety advice and incidents alongside
estimated costs, without equating either with delivered service. An overdue
task estimate is not a booked financial debt or an appropriation. A valid
contractual claim keeps its own amount, due date and source. Include risks
at contracted sites and on handback without double counting the same work.
Protect security-sensitive records and independent access.

**IV19 — Explain movement in the backlog on a comparable basis.** In each
annual budget, reconcile the opening estimate with new tasks that became
overdue, earlier defects newly found, changed measurement or price, and
independently accepted completion. Keep lawful exits, transfers and changes
of scope explicit. A transferred task stays in the consolidated account
until actual lawful closure. Confirm any successor service, continuing
liability and site restoration before taking credit for an exit. Show
unexplained differences and non-comparable records, including survey gaps.
Preserve the original useful dates and inspection history. An invoice,
purchase order or revised spreadsheet cannot prove completed work or
restored access. Identify when changes are measured in money rather than
physical deterioration. Use the same basis before treating work as removed.

**IV20 — Authorize upkeep and continuity alongside a material new asset.**
The competent elected body approves the purpose and capital ceiling and
a separately identified maintenance and continuity schedule. Name the
funded opening and first full operating cycle, qualified inspection and
crew windows, protected service during work, and a credible recurring
source for later renewal and lawful exit. Use dated finance and sources
under DB, FB, MF and JC. Keep actual restricted funds in their lawful
purposes. Do not create a permanent charge or an all-assets funding
percentage by adopting this schedule. Preserve ordinary proportionate
maintenance within existing authority. A new project may prepare before
it can safely open. It cannot rely on hypothetical future savings or
an unfunded successor. Where a current service already has a legal start
or deadline, report and remedy the actual gap instead of moving that date.

**IV21 — Make material deferral a public choice within safety and law.**
Distinguish safe rescheduling within the supported window from material
delay. For the latter, the responsible owner presents early repair,
funded temporary receiving, replacement or exit where plausible, and the
consequences of deferral. Identify the next inspection, last supported
safe date, qualified independent advice, available crew and actual money
by date, remaining risk and residents who would lose access. Return a
material change to the elected body under IV05 and DB. An urgent safety
measure follows prior law immediately. A vote or allocation cannot make
an unsafe asset safe. If continued use is unsafe or the date is unknown,
use actual competent protective action and a lawful funded alternate.
Where the alternate fails, disclose the unmet duty and seek correction
before the useful deadline where possible. A deferred payment or repair
is no recurring source for expansion merely because this year's cash
outflow is smaller.

**IV22 — Leave a usable handover and a way to correct mistakes.** Before
closing an annual disposition or transferring ownership, name who will
do each outstanding repair, pay lawful dues and receive displaced users.
Keep actual contract, worker, access and remedy duties in force. Give
affected people and opposition protected routes to challenge unsafe
assumptions, concealed condition or lost receiving. Publish safe records
with the funded response or precise remaining gap. AI may flag condition
changes, compare windows and trace estimate revisions. Independent qualified
people inspect safety and actual completion; elected bodies retain money
and policy. Reopen on lost capacity, new evidence, credible incidents,
changed need or a safer lawful route. An unavailable audit leaves uncertainty
visible and prior duties intact.
