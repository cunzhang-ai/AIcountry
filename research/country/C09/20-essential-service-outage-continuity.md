# C09. Keep essential services working when the grid fails

*Design259 · OC01–OC13 · Proposed continuity duties.*

**Buy and verify continuity for a named service and time. Protect the
energy, people and access it needs from conflicting commitments.**
[EG](19-electricity-service-and-grid-access.md) keeps the electricity
network and customer route accountable. OC governs how the receiving
service prepares for a loss of electricity, and when it must switch to
another way of delivering the duty. Drinking water, wastewater, home
care, civil rescue, courts and elections keep their own powers and
standards. No additional emergency declaration or cross-sector
requisition power is selected.

**OC01 — Start with the service deadline.** Each covered essential
operator and the public guarantor jointly identify the smallest useful
service that must continue when grid supply fails. The competent sector
body determines interruption tolerance, duration, quality and receiving
requirements from actual needs, including exposed homes and less
accessible places. Identify the last safe time to start a substitute
after allowing for travel, connection, startup and verification. Record
which requirements come from prior law and which are planning assumptions.
Assembly law and local budgets choose lawful resilience classes and their
funding. A facility name, average uptime or annual consumption is not a
deadline, a clinical judgment or permission to suspend a duty.

**OC02 — Test primary and alternate routes under one failure.** Trace
usable energy, discharge and startup power, transfer path, islanding,
reconnection, safe control, water or treatment inputs, trained staff,
transport, communications, site access and due cash. Check what fails
together in the relevant storm, flood, equipment, supplier or credential
loss, and which other service has already committed each resource.
Test a safe local control mode without the ordinary cloud or backhaul.
Include restoration and replenishment, and record the earliest next
exhaustion. Physical connection and independent technical acceptance
must match the actual load. A distributed asset with the same disabled
switch or software is not an independent source. Funds, title, reserves
and standby staff retain their own scopes.

**OC03 — Protect the useful reserve during ordinary operations.** For each
paid readiness commitment set an accepted, time- and site-specific lower
bound for the energy and other resources available to the named tasks.
Meter and report usable state, derating and available recharging within
the lawful plan. Discretionary peak shaving, export or other sales may
use only the margin that remains after that commitment. Do not promise
the same usable discharge, crew or logistics to concurrent customers.
A warning with a credible recharge risk can reduce that margin under
the accepted contract. The floor is a contracted preparation duty, with
actual lawful activation and PS rules applying during use. It is not a
rule against using reserve for its purpose or a change to existing
valid contracts by implication.

**OC04 — Pay for verified performance and lifecycle capacity.** Price
reinforcement, fixed on-site sources, protected demand reduction, mobile
mutual aid and alternate physical delivery against the same service and
deadline. Separate construction, available reserve, actual event delivery,
fuel, staffing, debt, replacement and eventual handback. The commissioning
sector buys attributable service-specific resilience. Assembly or council
law explicitly allocates shared network and public-guarantee costs.
Match any support, grants, loan and charge once under EG/WS/FB. Revenue
from a usable commercial margin is separate from a funded guarantee and
cannot finance the same capacity twice.

A provider earns the reliability portion of payment only at the
demonstrated scope. Record limits or suspension when tests or delivery
fail, and remedy faults under the actual contract and law. Recheck before
first reliance, after material changes or an adverse event, and at least
annually by safe witnessed test or accepted evidence of equivalent
operation. Include shared dependencies and actual receiving. No mandatory
unsafe live test, withholding of wages or loss of an existing personal
right follows from a failed readiness check.

**OC05 — Switch while the substitute can still work.** The existing service
duty officer starts the funded alternate plan when observations or credible
limits show that restoration cannot be relied on before the last safe
start time. Seek technical and sector acceptance for the actual path.
Existing independently authorized urgent acts do not wait for an
unavailable second signature. RC coordinates available assistance. EG
operates the grid, WS and sanitation officers secure safe water and
treatment, clinicians direct actual care, and election and court officers
keep their own protected opportunities and records. Use PS/CP/RX only
where their separate terms apply. Moving a generator or sharing an alert
does not move statutory powers, personal consent or the duty itself.

**OC06 — Reach the person and verify what actually arrived.** Local
receivers keep reachable offline and supported contact for people who
cannot use an app, travel or safely identify themselves in public.
Provide staffed accessible contact points and lawful outreach in affected
areas when the normal channels fail. Use volunteered or otherwise lawfully
limited contact details only for
the protective purpose. A person without a prior list entry can seek
help on the same need grounds. Make receiving workable for homes with
medical devices, water pumps or unsafe temperatures, including accepted
in-place support or safe offered transfer where applicable. No new
home-entry, treatment, data-retention or relocation power follows.
Preserve first claim dates. Log received safe water, treatment, essential
care, notice or usable public access separately from energization. A
message sent or a grid restored does not close an unmet duty.

**OC07 — Preserve control and useful remedies when both paths fail.**
Report the uncovered task, realistic next decision, responsible officer
and actual available help. Keep protected original records and a usable
independent route to challenge unsafe denial, hidden diversion or exclusion.
Notify the Assembly and affected local bodies under existing law where
a material allocation or funding decision remains. Apply actual poll,
court, compensation and remedy rules to the loss. No missed clock,
silent court or inaccessible platform grants new power or certifies
delivery. An AI tool may compare dependency and timing records and flag
contradictions. Qualified operators verify them against original evidence
and can work offline. No score selects people for rescue, power or review.

**OC08 — Return responsibility after service is safely restored.**
Hand back control only to a named lawful operator that accepts the tasks,
staff, records and usable source. Check receiving quality and outstanding
claims and replenish the reserve. Preserve ecological and worker duties,
payments, title and privacy under paid OP/T succession. New obligations
need competent prospective law, actual resources and independently
qualified receivers. Reopen on repeated shared failure, a changed
deadline, unpayable upkeep, a better physically feasible arrangement
or loss of independent review.
