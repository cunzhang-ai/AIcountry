# Commitment 106. Keep an electricity promise through physical or seller failure

[EG01–EG12](../../country/C09/19-electricity-service-and-grid-access.md) joins
public dispatch, authorized distribution, plural sellers and independently
enforceable access. Identify actual firm and flexible tranches by node and
time. Fund protected household use, maintenance, timely correction and an
accepting default supplier. A seller transfer and physical restoration have
different executors and source requirements.

Existing ownership, accrued and legal claims, engineering limits, actual
emergency and election authorities and paid OP/T successors continue. The
illustrative node case distinguishes constrained supply from full feeder
loss. Actual reserve, hedge, cash, staffed control and service remain unknown.
