# Release, individual trace and correction

*Part of [LX public rules](../12-public-rules-and-verifiable-implementation.md).*

**LX04 — Review changes in legal effect, including from a supplier.**
Before a material release, compare each affected legal rule, data
transform, source version, local scope, fallback and receiving duty.
Test safely without imposing experimental adverse decisions. Identify
who authorizes actual deployment, when it begins, what changed and
which cases remain under earlier terms. Changes in evidence meanings,
thresholds, court effect or default behavior need new scoped review.
One approval for a previous service or build cannot authorize a
different effect. A technical rollback must still apply the legally
correct rules for each period. An unverified route cannot hold a
statutory commencement or condition an already due right.

**LX05 — Explain the actual case and preserve a verifiable trace.**
Record the legal provision and its applicable date, procedure version,
material evidence and any transformation, decision owner, reasons,
actual result and receipt or challenge path. Distinguish authoritative
facts from technical flags, later reconstructions and unverified model
outputs. Give the person accessible meaningful reasons and access to
their material information under applicable law, with a human and
non-digital route. A generic description of the service is insufficient.
Where a model contribution cannot be faithfully reproduced, preserve
the actual output and material effect or do not use it as the basis
of a consequential finding. Protect unrelated data, witnesses and
security. Use scoped retention and independently reachable originals,
with no new universal case repository.

**LX06 — Stop the affected route when a material error is credible.**
The duty owner records and promptly reviews a credible material
challenge or applicable legal order. Identify the affected rule,
versions, periods, services, original case dates, consequences and
which orders are already binding within their lawful scope. Preserve
the failing version and actual records securely. Suspend the
questioned behavior when continued reliance risks material unlawful
harm and switch the affected cases to an independently lawful,
funded ordinary or interim route. Keep unaffected duties and earlier
remedy clocks live. A report does not decide merits, and a sector
can isolate only its own authorized task. The ordinary court and
independent reviewers retain useful-time relief.

**LX07 — Find and remedy the affected cases in both directions.**
Under controlling case law and EV, trace every materially affected
use within the verified scope, including people who never filed
a challenge. Reconstruct what actually happened and independently
reconsider the outcome using applicable law and admissible facts.
Preserve disputed records and the actual scope of legal finality.
Separate established additional dues, possible excess, unresolved
cases and third-party interests. Pay or otherwise execute lawful
correction through the named receiver and verify receipt. There is
no automatic collection, deduction or netting across people, claims
or periods. A recovery needs its own authority, evidence, process
and applicable protections. Expose unpayable or unreconstructible
cases and their independent remedies. A fixed release does not
close a person's unresolved loss.

[LX11–LX16](04-proactive-redress-and-missing-cases.md) specifies the
search outside the operational roster, dated payment, distinct recovery
and verified receipt for a material common error.
