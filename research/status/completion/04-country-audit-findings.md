# Whole-country audit: coherent obligations before more local design

*Design-79 scoped audit; design-80 C03, design-81 C02 and design-82 agency corrections below. Not an independent review or complete code census.*

The requirement is a complete, revisable country design for better human lives,
meaningful democracy and accountable AI assistance. Fourteen chapter headings,
37 function bids, numerical closure and preserved editions do not establish that
requirement. This audit asks whether the proposed powers, material obligations,
decision routes and transitions can coexist under their own stated conditions.

## Inspection and evidence scope

The integrated overview, status, chapter entrances, completion assessment and
relevant current canonical topics were inspected. Closer reading covered C01–C04,
C11–C12 and C14 democratic, membership, communication and transition rules; C08
supported agency; C06 scarcity and operating accounts; and C07 service/retirement
starts. The joined numerical review checks the identified accounting comparison.
This is not line-by-line revalidation of every current file, a complete legal or
engineering census, fresh qualified external review, or affected-party participation.
Unchanged chapter labels retain their own earlier support limits.

## Consequential findings and corrections

**1. Reconcile material support before claiming a common package.** The earlier
market value-added comparison used a baseline of 2,202 million where the joined
account requires **2,311 million**. The conditional SF hybrid requires market GVA
**3,294.733891963 million** and **50.186584718%** more GVA per remaining market FTE
than the corrected baseline. This correction changes the productivity comparison,
not GDP, cash or available goods. Older numerical outputs and claims derived from
the wrong baseline remain historical false support; do not edit them into apparent
past correctness. The [numerical bridge](../../2026-10-whole-country-reconciliation/results.json)
and [current operating book](../../country/C06/22-country-operating-resource-book.md)
must distinguish corrected required output from achieved productive capacity.
Unknown later duties and unresolved water/FX/finance failures remain binding.

**2. Supply the missing residual scarcity choice.** [C06/05](../../country/C06/05-cross-system-scarcity.md)
requires an enacted residual rule, but requiring a future statute is not itself
that rule. Design 79 adds a [CP candidate](../../country/C06/28-national-commitments-and-residual-scarcity.md)
for unforeseen cross-sector collisions:
protected stock/duties, evidence, named actor, equal-claim treatment, expiry,
review and prompt elected reconsideration. Compare retain/no new diversion,
immediate serious-harm protection and other legitimate priority packages. Neither
an AI aggregate score nor a vague life-saving instruction authorizes allocation;
the unmet and displaced claims remain visible. Actual sufficient stock is separate.

**3. Reconcile blanket starts with staged protection.** S16 and Y15 prescribe
1 January of the second calendar year after enactment in the audited predecessor.
The [T01–T04 rule](../../country/C14/06/01-public-decision-and-rights.md)
instead requires exact lawful starts, paid overlap and competent successors for
each stage. References with delayed skill/grid capacity do not support a universal
start. Design 79 selects expressly enacted component/cohort starts with interim
protection, retaining a funded common start as a rival. Preserve already
accrued rights and passed due dates; do not turn an operator's readiness statement
into indefinite legal postponement. Pension accrual and cash-service dates differ.

**4. Finish necessary agency without inventing consent.** The audit found B23's
asset-only authority did not settle necessary nonemergency personal care. Design 82
now selects [UA01–UA20](../../country/C08/23-necessary-care-and-unknown-will.md): real
support/actual will first, express basic-care duty and prior heightened court
review for consequential therapy, reproductive effects and life-support choices.
It excludes actual-opposition override/general guardianship, names the CRPD
interpretive objection and separately authorizes minimum dependent continuity
when order expiry would harm. This closes a proposed-choice gap, not actual
participation, lawful adoption, funded independent review or received protection.

**5. Identify genuine pending democratic choices.** The audit found a missing C03
final-forum package. Design 80 now selects [CJ](../../country/C03/08-constitutional-review-and-public-control.md),
including qualification, appointments, fixed votes, finite reserves, external
own-court jurisdiction and execution. This closes that reference-choice gap,
not actual independence or universal remedy. Separately, C02's four-year residence
franchise did not supply citizenship acquisition/loss, statelessness prevention
or durable residence. [Design 81 MB](../../country/C02/06-citizenship-and-secure-belonging.md)
now closes that reference-choice gap. Stable status, independent review and actual
receiving remain separate requirements; no real authority or operation is inferred.

**6. Resolve the amendment exception openly.** C14 delays all specified changes
to offices/review through current rulers and their immediate successors' contest;
C02 already recognizes a narrowly reviewed rights-restoring exception. Blanket
delay prevents self-dealing but can also freeze exclusion or unusable review.
Retain it with that cost stated, target incumbent benefit, or select a tightly
bounded restoration exception under existing lawful procedure. No exception may
become executive court packing, removal for an adverse ruling or self-approval.

**7. Bound consultation without suppressing new meanings.** C12 preserves distinct
material arguments and requires answers, but unlimited AI novelty can exceed finite
hearing capacity even after duplicates are grouped. Separate receipt/preservation
from the bounded examination owed for a decision; publish coverage, unresolved
objections, grouping and lawful closure reasons. A material new rights/safety/legal
fact can reopen; the mere existence of another argument need not suspend every
decision. Preserve unassisted access, independent challenge, unfamiliar semantics
and immediate legal remedies. Volume quotas or a mediator's unexplained summary
must not buy a sponsor priority or give it a procedural veto.

## What this audit establishes

These findings locate omissions, compatibility defects and value choices; they do
not certify all remaining law. Correcting an account does not supply productivity;
selecting a candidate rule does not enact it; a lawful enactment does not prove
staffed capacity; a completed exercise does not prove better lives. The design
requires a single reconciled material, legal and transition package before a
whole-country readiness claim. Then revisit the pending choices above and test
the full objective again. Reopen on new excluded duties, changed source evidence,
unusable remedies, actual affected objections or a substantively better model.
