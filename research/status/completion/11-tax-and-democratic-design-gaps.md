## Selected changes and visible burdens

PA10 rates remain20/35/45/55% with protected ordinary usable U and no additional
personal zero band. TA adds calendar-year receipts, a source/residence test,
actual noncash household remuneration with safe-cash/employer-funded tax-inclusive gross-up,
five/25-year personal-business capital recovery, five-year ringfenced losses,
indexed realized gains and capped foreign-tax credit. No special home gain
exemption is selected; mobility/liquidity and wage-substitution conflicts remain.
Later actually paid foreign tax can justify a bounded refund of domestic tax
already paid, even after the state's ordinary assessment bar; it creates a
long-tail cash obligation, not new yield or unrelated reopened assessments.

30April annual reconciliation and15th-next-month payer remittance are separate.
Proved withholding credits the worker despite payer default. Authorized-channel
irrevocable acceptance **plus actual debit** discharges direct payment even if
the state collector fails; neither event proves settled Treasury cash. Replacing
already owned/taxed bank principal is not new income; first genuinely untaxed
interest/earnings retain their character and are taxed once. Unpaid U/refund
cannot be counted as available household cash.

Cabinet separately appoints/disciplines protected C04 professional Revenue Director
and Merits Review Head, without a new board/fixed political term. Each career
case officer owns their exact statutory competence. Review Head controls separate
staff/source allocation; Treasury's named career refund payer implements lawful
entitlement without minister/director merits veto. This is functional separation,
not proof of institutional independence: Cabinet/Treasury/common vendor capture
and career entrenchment remain. Political identity is no audit ground; bounded
risk/random selection and externally inspectable original records remain required.

Serve a usable **final** additional assessment within four years of statutory
return/remittance due; court-proved deliberate concealment adds only actual time
≤two/absolute six. An inquiry/proposal cannot reserve an expired period. Give
ordinary20working days to answer and optional60working-day review or direct
ordinary court for full facts/law/amount, with no pay-first/bond/exhaustion.
No compulsory collection of a changed increment before the60-working-day challenge
window expires; timely disputed increments stay through ordinary merits.
Proved undisputed refund reaches payment≤ten working days from first sufficient
receipt; disputed remainder gets30working-day decision plus one necessary30,
and favorable correction is paid≤ten working days. Delay does not imply rejection.
Symmetric capped simple interest distinguishes late refund, unlawful debit and
upheld additional tax; good-faith review pauses accrual, not principal liability.

Court-specific preservation needs actual imminent dissipation proof and one90-day
period plus one90-day extension; it is not Treasury collection or criminal guilt.
Compulsory property collection needs an independent court and protected needs/
ownership/priority. A **single six-calendar-year outer bar includes execution**,
with actual court-proved evasion toll≤two/absolute eight, from the original due
event. Judgment, review, installment or reminder does not restart it. Reject
six-years-to-sue plus B37 judgment/renewal stacking; ten-year single bar remains a
serious rival. Court backlog can forfeit public recovery under this selection.
