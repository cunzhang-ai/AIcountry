# D73: defined international crimes and constrained public force

15 October 2026 · Research selection of G01–G14/W01–W29 and C10/07

## Identity, baseline and consequential question

Design 72 expressly left full international-crime definitions, command modes,
special reach/finality, penalties and cooperation unresolved. Preserve 362
current Markdown files plus inventory as [363 hashed artifacts](predecessor-design-72/SNAPSHOT.md).
One AI author/controller; no qualified independent reviewer, affected people,
verified sponsor/interest, founding public or actual authority. The user
authorizes national-design research, not a trial, source-law amendment,
international commitment or sensitive testimony collection/publication.
[Framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md)
remain unchanged proposed method, not an adopting procedure.

The problem concerns our own organized/AI-mediated power as well as external
atrocities. Generic ordinary crimes do not fully specify context, superior
responsibility, sham foreign adjudication or cross-border actual competence.
A treaty link or vague label leaves both impunity and abusive prosecution
possible. This is a bounded national code/force decision, not a new local case.

## Alternatives and selected design

Compare treaty-minimum implementation, a predominantly ordinary/restorative
model and an expressly differentiated national statute. Shared definitions
can improve foreseeability; ordinary routes reduce exceptional penal growth;
a separate national schedule can specify context/control and protect broader
domestic interests. Each can be captured, costly or ineffective. Select
the third provisionally, retaining ordinary proof, support and correction.
No evidence establishes it superior or every detail universally criminal.

G01–G14 defines four families, closed war acts, specific genocidal intent,
civilian-attack context, actual leadership aggression, personal participation,
causal command/differentiated knowledge and a separate reporting duty. New
domestic protection includes inclusive persecution/reproductive violence,
selected common IAC/NIAC coverage, adult G scope and national 18 armed service.
These are explicit choices, not a claim of exact latest-ICC reproduction.

Jurisdiction is connected or qualified presence-based for recognized core
crimes, with a narrower aggression route, actual immunity assessment and
a narrowly proved foreign-sham exception before an independent formation.
No absent-person/world-police mandate, victim-nationality veto or presumed
collective guilt. Domestic acquittal is not generically reopened. Actual
international-court competence and implementing duties remain distinct.

Main ceiling 25 is retained. Causal command branches 25/15/10 distinguish
actual knowledge, civilian conscious disregard and military constructive
knowledge; incitement 7/reporting and separate recruitment 5. Attempt is capped
at half. No mandatory minimum, life/death/torture or predictive extension.
A finite 30-year alternative remains live; category naming/ICC's own penalties
does not alone justify raising the existing national ceiling. This retention
is a value judgment, not deterrence calibration or proof of equal outcomes.

## Evidence, primary-source limits and material objections

UN corrected 1999 Rome Parts2/3/5/9 and an official introductory account are
saved with exact versions/hashes. The German official English CCAIL covers
amendments through 30 July 2024; it supplies national comparisons, not ICC law
or evidence that its life/minimum sanctions improve lives. Raw and separately
derived files stay intact, including original source-language headers.

Current ICC PDF/ICRC/government-route attempts did not verify a latest full
consolidation. The draft therefore **does not incorporate such a text**;
it sets out independently authored national definitions and variation reasons.
Fresh complete actual treaty/amendment/jurisprudence review is still required.
Primary legal facts are original context; original human case/outcome
observations are absent. Related Rome/national texts and AI summaries are
not independent empirical corroboration. This is no comprehensive2026 audit.

Twenty-four authored journeys inspect context, source, minority, control,
causation, command duty, finality, real competence and receiving. Structural
checks do not test verdicts, contextual legal interpretation or safety.
No actual child, survivor, accused, military member, clinician, lawyer or
foreign court has participated; no sensitive testimony/image is collected.

Objections remain: residual ambiguity/context inference, uncertain international
recognition of domestic extensions, narrow/broad command responsibility,
historical proof/immunity, possible sham exceptions used for harassment,
G age 18 versus accountability, inadequate/excessive ceilings and high costs.
The proposal answers with closed/contextual elements, independent burdens,
actual control/ability, no retro-causality, narrow routes, original evidence,
finite penalties and visible unpaid/unreceived remedies. Their adequacy is
unproved; better models may replace this design rather than become more clauses.

## Authority, controls and continuing duties

Assembly enacts general law/funding and actual external commitments; cabinet
commands only within lawful mandates. Independent civilian prosecutors/
courts with funded defense determine cases; command/security/vendor officials
cannot select a favorable judge or retain sole originals. Appointment,
removal, source access, Treasury, expertise and actual execution need a
common-controller/capture assessment. Child/clinical/food/shelter services
receive needs independently of guilt; victims' support is not an admission.

C10/07 adds actual-information/control/stop requirements beyond a nominal
human click, prior weapon/software legal review and national 18 service.
None supplies new targeting, search, emergency or battlefield internment
powers. Distinct conflict-detention/status/release law remains unfinished.
No criminal allegation alone legalizes international intervention.

Changed consumers: C08 core/special cooperation and G chapter; C10 defense/
cooperation/prevention; C12 mandates; country overview/status/history,
selections/conflict/audit, completion and inventory. Current software, source
manuscripts, constitutional holds and old dated validators remain unchanged.
Map past acts/age/law, actual treaty/amendments, immunity, pending cases,
sentences/custody, periods and victim obligations before authorized migration.
No retroactive national extension, automatic amnesty or revived bar. A
past international offense requires its actual then-applicable lawful basis.

Specialist investigations, prosecutors/defense, courts/translation, original
forensics/clinical sources, safe witnesses/custody, child receiving and
executed claims need uniquely attributed C06 resources. Existing bids and
unmeasured AI gains do not qualify the increment; international assistance,
fines/confiscation and unpaid relatives are not committed current funds.
