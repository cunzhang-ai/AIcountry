# A successor needs a service route it can actually use

*Design539 · Constructed national handover exercise.*

The outgoing government maintains home support. The two successor budgets
each have 12 eligible cash units after independent court, electoral and
other common dues. The eligible amounts are already provided under lawful
sources in the exercise. Twelve residents have the same due care under all
options. An existing agreement delivers the standing visits until the planned
cutover. During overlap its costs remain on the common line. The authorized
incumbent route can continue throughout if chosen under its actual terms. A
qualified local team and a voluntary mutual team with a paid nonmember
route can each serve all twelve at the original deadlines if their necessary
inputs remain. The figures, residents and bids are assumptions.

| Decision the successor could make | First / second window, including transition | Essential dependency |
|---|---|---|
| Retain and repair incumbent delivery | 9 / 9 | The incumbent retains an accepting paid roster. |
| Use the directly staffed local team | 12 / 10 | The local accessible depot must be usable by the start of qualified overlap. |
| Use the mutual team with independent nonmember access | 10 / 12 | A distinct roster must be reservable by the same date. Within this bid its coverage is required in the second window. |

Before the election a public asset owner proposes an irrevocable sale of the
local depot. A separate public operator proposes to commit the only qualified
mutual-route relief roster to other work for both windows. Assume both
acts can lawfully proceed under existing sector rules and serve different
public purposes. Selling alone leaves the mutual route. Reassigning alone
leaves the directly staffed route. Together they leave only the incumbent,
with three unallocated units in each window. There is no other depot or
qualified roster in useful time. Neither a larger cash balance on paper
nor recovery of a file replaces either input.

The earliest useful deadline is the last date to reserve the relief roster
or to retain access to the depot through the planned cutover, whichever comes
first for the alternative being preserved. Before both losses bind, voters'
representatives must see the combined closure and the exact protection that
keeps a choice. Under this case's terms the asset owner can retain a six-month
use right for one eligible **outgoing** unit while transferring title. The
qualified owner accepts that arrangement and the local team confirms the
site. The depot is required only for the paid overlap in the first window.
The separately funded operating costs in the table already cover the second
window. The outgoing unit is available and can alternatively pay for an
identified discretionary repair. The relief roster can then move to the
other work, leaving the direct route and incumbent both executable. Where
the owner's proceeds cannot absorb the cost, count it against the actual
outgoing payer and reconsider the offer.

If the purported right of use lacks enforceable access or the necessary
safety acceptance, disclose that loss and compare keeping the roster with
an agreed alternative shift. If none can receive in time, the elected
body must expressly choose the prospective closure before binding it.
Do not cancel a valid sale or roster contract after the fact. If only one
department releases its proposed act, trace the other act's real authority
and remaining option. A claim on an independent court's budget cannot fill
any gap.

In a separate branch a valid late debt uses two units in the second successor
window. Ten there still funds direct delivery if the depot was retained.
The mutual route would now fail at its second call. If the debt instead
uses two in the first window, the direct start fails and the mutual route
fits only if the roster was kept. Reprice before commitment if those calls
are credible. If both calls are plausible and no affordable reservation
covers them, publish which risk remains. Preserve the incumbent care,
the debt and original deadlines. Do not report a path as resilient
by switching between two options after their respective cutoffs.

England's National Audit Office found that some public bodies preparing for
PFI expiry lacked parts of the original agreements and that contract terms
and missing information could hamper understanding the condition of the
assets. This is evidence that asset return and operating control must be
verified early. It gives no estimate of this case's costs or the effect
of the proposed elected decision. [National Audit Office report, part three](https://www.nao.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/Managing-PFI-assets-and-services-as-contracts-end.pdf)

Prefer keeping the depot right in the stated base case if the elected body
finds the lost discretionary repair acceptable. A cheaper ordinary method
that retains equivalent choice wins on the same evidence. Where choosing
the longer commitment protects service materially better, make that decision
in the open and state when choice can return. The exercise tests the
institutional rule using constructed facts. An actual result requires
qualified receiving, affected-party evidence and lawful public decisions.
