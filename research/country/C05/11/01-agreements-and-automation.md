# Scope, agreements and lawful collaboration

Design 98 · Proposed research, not enacted law or verified operation.

**MP01 — Object and scope.** Protect independent rivalry and usable consumer, worker, supplier and
civic exit against proved private, cooperative or public-enterprise market power. Competition law
does not decide speech fitness, an election result, tax, asylum, the national monetary unit or a
bank's M09 resolution. Parliament chooses any public-service duty and funding; the markets authority
cannot create one by case order.

**MP02 — Economic units and control.** An undertaking is a person or group actually offering or
buying goods, services or labor. Distinct legal names under one ultimate decisive controller are one
economic unit for this Act, not automatic independent rivals. Effective control means presently
enforceable voting, appointment, veto, contract, key-asset or finance rights that permit decisive
direction of commercial strategy; ordinary minority investment without those rights is not control.
Prove ultimate beneficial/controller links and common vendors with original source and reply, not
names or AI scores alone. Internal restructurings leaving ultimate control unchanged are not
concentrations.

**MP03 — Relevant constraint.** Define the domestic product/service/input or labor-buying activity
and geographic reach by actually usable substitution: price, quality, time/travel,
disability/language access, legal permission, data portability, network effects, capacity, switching
cost, worker mobility and credible entry. Test public and foreign options by received availability
and payable FX, not an assumed theoretical substitute. State the counterfactual, source dates,
contrary evidence and uncertainty. Share and concentration are clues only; no share alone proves
agreement, dominance or merger harm.

**MP04 — Naked horizontal restraint.** Two independent actual or reasonably capable rival
sellers/buyers may not intentionally agree, implement or cause an association to implement a common
transaction price/wage or material term, output/capacity restriction, bid winner/cover bid, or
customer/territory/worker allocation. A communication or common pricing tool is evidence only if
agreement/implementation is proved; parallel conduct and public price observation alone are
insufficient. This core includes no-poach/wage-fixing between competing purchasers of labor, not
collective worker bargaining. A party's unilateral refusal is not a horizontal agreement.

**MP05 — Genuine integration and bargaining boundary.** A demonstrably integrated joint venture,
purchasing cooperative, quality/safety standard or limited shared research may use a restraint only
if it is directly necessary to specified joint production or lawful standard, no wider in
people/products/duration than needed, leaves members independent outside that scope and has a
credible lower-restriction comparison. A naked price/output/bid/customer pact cannot be relabeled a
venture. Labor unions and associations of employees or economically dependent solo workers may
bargain collectively over their own pay/conditions and pursue protected strikes; that does not
license member businesses to rig downstream bids. Defendant produces venture/necessity evidence; the
authority retains the ultimate burden to prove unlawful scope.

**MP06 — Shared algorithms and signals.** Common vendor/code, correlated prices or model output is
no cartel finding. Prove an agreement, purposeful common instruction, knowing exchange of
competitively sensitive future terms through a hub, or actual controller coordination under MP04.
V03/PD protected source access and rebuttal apply; no mandated source-code publication, permanent
behavioral surveillance or guilt by similarity. Remove a proved coordinating instruction without
banning otherwise lawful automation.

*Part of [the competition statute](../11-competition-and-economic-power.md); normative thresholds and institutions, no authority from publication.*

[WL21–WL30](../12-work-contracts-and-collective-power.md) now supplies positive
labor representation/bargaining/action rules behind this exception. Genuine
protected worker collective activity is not downstream business price fixing;
actual controller/economic-unit evidence and MP civil duties remain. A collective
agreement, cooperative label or agent does not acquire sovereign authority.
