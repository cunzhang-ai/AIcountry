# Eight authored boundaries, including our own protector's failure

*Support for [V/M](README.md); these are reasons, not observations or verdicts.*

| Case | Candidate treatment and remaining failure |
|---|---|
| RF01 Inspector fines an AI flag without showing the actual duty | V01/V06/V11 require a prior precise scheduled duty, original evidence, fault and an independent court. No administrative label avoids P safeguards. Urgent hazard protection retains distinct grounds and expiry. |
| RF02 A laboratory outage prevents a required return | V class A requires a precise duty, usable notice, correction opportunity and attributable negligent ability. Missing public/technical capacity is not automatic guilt. Correct risk and fund a receiver independently; no daily stacked fine. |
| RF03 Sole water-part supplier must stop a dangerous batch | V04 permits the narrow lawful urgent stop with B17 hearing; V09 names actual safe water/replacement, transport, executor and money. A missing replacement is an unmet public duty, not immunity to keep harming or evidence of completed protection. |
| RF04 Opposition account closed for a model's risk score | M04 requires actual legal restriction grounds and independent challenge, not political/AI-account conditions. M12 preserves usable campaign payment through a funded alternative. Independent supervision fails if the same vendor controls records, access and review. |
| RF05 A platform mixes client balances with its own lending | M01 distinguishes a bank claim from M05 custody, actual title/reconciliation and 100% safeguarded cash. No fictional segregated pot. Trace ownership/shortfall, M07 look-through and one beneficiary cap; license words do not create cash. |
| RF06 Bank fails; couple and child share multiple accounts | M07 proves shares/beneficial ownership and aggregates once per person/group; child's actual property remains separate. Disputed identity does not block undisputed payout/essential advance. Actual 24-hour and seven-day receivers/funding still need demonstration. |
| RF07 Resolution favors a politically connected creditor | M10 prior hierarchy, independent source valuation and M11 compensation/review apply. A systemic-risk label alone excuses neither unequal loss nor hidden public funding. Treasury compensation is a real liability, not the insurer's imaginary excess cash. |
| RF08 Our national book finances Treasury through its only modeled bank | M03 sovereign ceiling exposes 250 against E100. Retain the boundary and hold the transaction incompatible; at least 150 needs another funded holder or actual additional equity. The old cash balance cannot establish legality; do not exempt our own state merely to pass. |

All cases need actual competent law, original evidence, funded advice, safe
source custody, independent adjudication and accepted execution before any
real-world conclusion. Neither the same AI's prose nor arithmetic supplies
participation, review independence, deterrence, solvency or public consent.
