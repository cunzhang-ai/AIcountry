# Living together without giving a manager power over private life

Design149 · Authored routes, not observed residents, legal advice or adjudicated cases

| Conflict | Selected rule and remaining failure |
|---|---|
| Two owners cannot agree while a shared pipe fails | CB12 existing duty continues without a fresh majority. Qualified minimum making-safe, H04 source clock/receiving and direct court/paid receiver. No order creates cash or a plumber; actual gap stays visible. |
| Developer holds most unsold units and appoints an affiliate | All units owe actual shares; owner money decisions use full entitlement/share denominators. Special conflicts cannot vote themselves an award; independent necessary-contract court source is distinct. Concentration can still control lawful budgets, so do not claim democratized ownership. |
| Renters pay rent but cannot influence common-room rules | Actual adult residents have equal use ballots within real funded/lawful scope, irrespective of owner/rent/AI status; children/other users retain rights/input. Not a vote to spend another unit's money or remove its title. |
| A small active resident group passes a disliked rule | One-third participation and majority of participants permits some minority-of-all outcomes; notice, reserved rights, direct challenge and replacement remain. Stronger full-resident consent is a genuine participation-versus-deadlock rival. |
| Wealthy majority describes a luxury rebuild as repair | Prove actual necessary safety/renewal or use CB08 optional threshold; neither permits taking private units/old creditor rights. LA/ordinary source/H safe receiving remain. Court/expert capture can still misclassify necessity. |
| A unit subdivision manufactures extra owner votes | Replacement subunits share old aggregate entitlement/cost pending lawful heard reallocation. AI accounts/delegations create no units or resident mandates. No private rewriting of the denominator. |
| A ground-floor owner refuses roof/lift costs | Necessary shared systems use actual source shares; no-use assertion alone cancels no common duty. Actual separate service groups/metered use and lawful alternative shares can change the fair schedule; floor area is not an empirically perfect proxy. |
| Low-income owner cannot pay a qualified safety levy | Actual I03/I05 qualifying personal need can reach a verified due share directly without advance personal financing; no depreciation/luxury/entire-association guarantee. Public cash/source and receiving unknown; unpaid claims cannot become private force. |
| Manager cuts lift/door credentials for arrears | CB06/CB16 forbid essential-service cutoff, private fines and vote suspension. Actual unsecured claim can use court/U with safe housing; nobody declares the manager/contractor already paid. |
| Seller leaves arrears or conceals a large approved job | Ownership-day operation and actual levy-date liability, disclosure before binding sale/update before completion. Buyer doesn't automatically inherit seller debt; future valid bills remain. B seller remedy may be uncollectible. |
| Common renewal money sits in a manager affiliate's account | Purpose/title safeguards, exact M/NB custody, protected accounts/reconciliation and actual creditor/source chain. No use as working capital/loan security; a bank balance/insurance label does not prove usable protection. |
| Repair needs access through a refusing resident's home | Actual consent or narrow ordinary-court access order with reasons, dates, safe work/restoration and B/LF execution. Ordinary notice alone isn't a key; hazard rescue has its own source. Privacy and necessary repair can still conflict. |
| Neighboring business/spectacle repeatedly intrudes into homes | CB19–CB21 require substantial specified source interference, reciprocal context and actual control. Direct lawful occupier/child claims, abatement and attributed compensation; permit/public benefit alone doesn't erase a remedy. No blanket right to silence or immunity for all innovative uses. |
| Neighbors use noise rules to expel a disabled child or a minority | No identity/belief/ordinary-child nuisance finding. Prove source effect, real threshold, feasible reciprocal accommodation; privacy/safe representation and direct equality/H/B remedies. Majority preference is no harm evidence. |
| Receiver or council is captured too | Trace actual owner/council/court/expert/funding/enforcement controllers; task/time/source constraints, independent challenge and real replacement. Naming another protector proves no independence or received outcome. |

## Coherent rivals, rather than more machinery by default

| Arrangement | Mechanism, burden and what could favor it |
|---|---|
| Retain familiar source law/informal agreement | Preserves trust, voluntary cooperation and low participation cost. Could be best where real law already supplies effective safe repair/voice/redress; absent authority or cash isn't repaired by adding meetings. Candidate's missing default decisions still need explicit disposition. |
| Owner-only rule with tenant consultation | Aligns asset financing with owners and can lower split-authority delay. Leaves renter life strongly dependent on landlord/manager; enforceable H/equality/exit and actual outside housing must be credible. Consultation isn't resident consent. |
| Selected bounded owner finance/resident use + mandatory safety | Keeps funding/property and everyday life distinct, protects individual rights and gives deadlock a direct lawful endpoint. Small active groups, owner financial veto, experts/courts and unpaid gaps remain. No proven universal winner. |
| Strong resident dual consent over spending/management | May limit landlord power more directly. Needs legitimate source/ownership rights, burdens, nonresident creditor/work/neighbor voice and a real necessary-repair endpoint; a resident chamber cannot wish new cash into existence. |
| Cooperative/public ownership or shared service | Can align use and ownership/remove some investor conflict. Requires actual paid acquisition/equity/loss and workable alternatives; political landlord, exclusionary cooperative or centralized vendor capture remain possible. |
