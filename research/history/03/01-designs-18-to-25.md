# Designs 18–25

19. **Integrated design 18: monetary architecture revised, readiness open.**
    The [monetary decision](../../2026-10-monetary-architecture/decision-record.md)
    displaces the prior external-currency reference with a conditional domestic
    unit, public monetary mandate and independent operations within elected law.
    It keeps bank liquidity separate from bank-loss resolution and Treasury
    finance, and names inflation, exchange, foreign-debt and import constraints.
    The complete external-currency package remains a rival and stress branch.
    No jurisdiction has enacted the unit, established credible operators or
    shown that either package improves lives under actual conditions.
20. **Integrated design 19: tax and local-finance architecture selected,
    adequacy open.** The [tax/local-finance decision](../../2026-10-tax-and-fiscal-federalism/decision-record.md)
    compares the old list of taxes, a narrower direct-tax alternative, a
    wider local consumption/land-tax model and the selected plural-base settlement.
    It assigns national and local powers, integrates household relief and
    taxable capital transfers, protects valuation/assessment appeals and
    connects equalization to potential capacity rather than actual effort.
    No rate, base yield, incidence, filing capacity or local service budget
    has been verified in a real adopting jurisdiction.
21. **Integrated design 20: state execution and procurement chain selected,
    capacity open.** The [state-capability decision](../../2026-10-state-capability/decision-record.md)
    compares patronage, a central integrity gate, a contractor-led state and
    the selected elected-policy/professional-execution package. It links
    appointments, procurement, service delivery, payment, audit, complaint,
    exit and handover without giving experts or AI a sovereign veto. No
    staffing, supplier market, conflict review, funds or tested successor
    capacity exists for a real jurisdiction.
22. **Integrated design 21: accountability for serious abuse of public power,
    operation open.** The [public-power abuse decision](../../2026-10-public-power-abuse/decision-record.md)
    compares internal review, a single protective authority, automatic political
    control and preassigned plural investigation/prosecution. The selected
    research route preserves evidence outside an implicated command, maintains
    fair process for accused staff and separates urgent care, criminal judgment
    and delivered civil remedy. An authored sixth stress case tests shared
    control during an opposition gathering. No independent source custody,
    investigators, prosecutors, courts, funds or observed results exist here.
23. **Integrated design 22: normal credit and financial risk selected,
    operation open.** The [credit-and-finance decision](../../2026-10-credit-and-finance/decision-record.md)
    compares deferred design, lightly regulated credit, directed public
    finance, narrow public payments and regulated plural intermediation.
    The chosen route separates basic payment claims from lending and public
    subsidy, includes linked nonbank risk and household/small-firm debt
    remedies, and bounds a statutory systemic-risk council. A seventh
    authored exercise tests a joint housing, funding and payment shock.
    No actual lenders, exposures, protection funds, courts or outcomes were
    verified; this does not establish financial safety or fair credit access.
24. **Integrated design 23: defense and war-power boundary selected,
    authority and readiness open.** The [defense decision](../../2026-10-defense-and-war/decision-record.md)
    compares executive war, standing defense with Assembly renewal,
    parliamentary operational control and smaller-force dependence. The
    selected research route places immediate defense in prior law, sustained
    foreign force under an affirmative bounded elected mandate, operations
    under accountable professional command, and classified review, civilian
    justice and humanitarian limits outside one command. Its proposed
    24-hour, seven-day and 90-day periods are hypotheses distinct from D9's
    constitutional emergency period. An eighth authored exercise tests an
    attack during a disputed election and payment outage. No jurisdiction,
    applicable treaty settlement, force, legislative fallback, affected-party
    participation or observed outcomes establish security or legitimacy.
25. **Integrated design 24: personal status, family duties and care authority
    selected, operation open.** The [personal-status decision](../../2026-10-personal-status-and-care/decision-record.md)
    compares modular law, household-head control, family-agency discretion,
    maximal nonintervention and the selected person-level rights and relational
    duty chain. Registration, urgent services, child protection, supported adult
    decisions, protective orders and estates now have distinct powers and review.
    A ninth authored case tests a child's disputed records and a carer's flight
    from violence across localities. Source laws, affected people's choices,
    actual registrars, courts, shelter, placements and safe payment remain
    unverified; the selection proves neither family safety nor fair intervention.
26. **Integrated design 25: judicial independence and compliance chain selected,
    authority and readiness open.** The [judicial-power decision](../../2026-10-judicial-power/decision-record.md)
    compares retaining broad safeguards, elected judges, a self-governing
    judicial guild, one exclusive constitutional guardian and the selected
    distributed ordinary-court/constitutional-review route. The proposal
    connects plural appointment and case allocation to vacancy, fair discipline,
    bounded core funding and a named lawful executor for orders against the
    state. A tenth authored case tests court vacancy, budget lapse and cabinet
    defiance. D5's numerical court design remains a hypothesis, and no actual
    judge, clerk, counsel, treasury, enforcement or observed remedy is verified.
