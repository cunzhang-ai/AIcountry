# Can a favourable budget variance pay for better lives?

*Design198 · Copenhagen's 2025 accounts and a separate 2026 care phase-in.*

The whole-country alternatives face the same decision. Can lower recorded
spending support a continuing service, and who still needs the work
behind that balance? Compare AIcountry's service-first design with strong
retain-and-repair in the same real setting. This tests the budget and
delivery join, without inventing a national tax, production path or adopter.

[Copenhagen evidence and account reconciliation](01-copenhagen-service-and-budget.md)

## Compare the strongest local repair and the proposed rule

| Choice | What it can achieve | What the weaker case exposes |
|---|---|---|
| Strong retain/repair | Keep the real elected budget, local employers, carry-forward and BU30 monitoring. Use existing competent bodies to fix hiring, premises or late payment. Choose this for the local comparison where it can achieve the same timely and contestable service | Forecast payroll can improve without staffed opening hours for a child or time the family can actually use. A late supplier payment may persist. Actual existing remedy, receipt and costs still need evidence |
| AIcountry service-first plural, with FB09–FB12 | Make material recurring reallocations conditional on demonstrated released spending and test actual receiving alongside budget execution. Preserve local delivery and independent recourse under the selected country rules | Adds verification cost and the risk of rigid comparisons. Reopening needs a named paying and receiving path. Same-provider monitoring can miss non-applicants and shared failures. The design changes the test for a qualified source, while elected authorities retain lawful choice |
| Centralize the service and payroll | Could remove local contracting or payment interfaces with a capable public employer and direct user control | Title, pay, staff and supervision move only through paid handover. A common payroll or uniform recruitment bottleneck can make failure more widespread. A lower quoted budget supplies no staffed delivery |
| Recycle the favourable variance into a continuing promise | Gives a visible new offer quickly and can use an actual uncommitted balance for finite action | The published figures establish no positive recurring source for that promise. Delay, restricted balances and displaced duties can recur, and a real second year requires a real source |

For this Copenhagen decision, **retain and repair first**. Nothing in the
inspected data establishes that changing the employer, transfer architecture
or whole country would fix the observed barriers more effectively. For the
AIcountry national design, add the recurring-source and receiving test
because a fiscal balance alone leaves the causal distinction unresolved.
Do not infer that Copenhagen lacks particular legal rights or comparison
tools from this limited inspection.

## Run one ordinary case and two adverse switches

Use authored amounts in million DKK per year, separate from all published
figures. Two providers owe the same qualified care and assisted access.
A proposed redesign claims a 1.00 recurring gross release. Independent
records are assumed to confirm actually avoided recurring spend, comparable
case mix and adequate delivery. New recurring public operation and
oversight cost 0.35 and paid fallback costs 0.25. At most 0.40 can fund
another continuing service after scope and lawful disposition are established.
A new 1.00 offer therefore still needs at least 0.60 annually, plus any
separate transition cash. If the staff remain on payroll and 1.00 was merely
an hours valuation, the calculation establishes no recurring fiscal release.

Under adverse conditions, the same apparent saving comes from missing
qualified cover at closing time or unpaid supplier invoices. Keep missed
hours and amounts due visible, with original dates, separate from cash
remaining. A reallocation might still lawfully use a distinct finite
uncommitted balance. It does not cure the pending care or payment. Test
the claimed fallback during the same holiday or staffing failure.
If only observed ordinary daytime care passes, preserve funded peak
cover and do not transfer the result to peak hours or absent applicants.

For a cutover, name the incumbent and accepted successor, maintain wages,
supplier claims, protected family choices and lawful remedies, and fund
parallel coverage until usable receipt is confirmed. If a controller
changes during implementation, confirm actual authorizations and alternate
payments under [HT](../../country/C03/19-lawful-handover-and-actual-control.md).
AI can assist reconciliation or schedule testing with source and human
fallback. Treat new software, review, correction and exit as costs.
No procurement, appointment, authorizing vote or national comparison
is proved by these cases.

## Decide the extended-hours question before scaling

Keep the 2.7 purpose undecided within the actual elected process. The
least disruptive candidate is assisted access to existing late-hour
places where they are safe, reachable and staffed. Check whether families
can use them on their actual schedules, including those needing fewer
than ten flexible hours or unable to find a private carer. If the
barrier is unavailable slots at useful hours, compare a geographically
targeted paid extension with wider restoration on the same qualified
staff and journey assumptions. If improved access still shows little
need and existing obligations are met, propose lawful finite
reallocation with earlier claims and a funded restoration path specified.
A budget line alone neither allocates a childcare place nor supplies the
people who would staff it. The local committee's evidence-first response
is compatible with the candidate and is the first path in this setting.

The next test is a matched provider-period account for one practical
opening window, joining paid qualified hours, missed or rejected care,
provider receipt, family schedules and journeys, and an accepted
alternative. Include shift-working and single-carer households and
people whose need falls below an existing route's threshold. Test
ordinary and holiday staffing and bring the proposed disposition to the
competent elected body. If existing arrangements can deliver the same
access and accountability, retain/repair wins this choice without a
new administrative layer. A specific gap that persists after repair
supports a targeted change; actual source, effect and public decision
remain separate.
