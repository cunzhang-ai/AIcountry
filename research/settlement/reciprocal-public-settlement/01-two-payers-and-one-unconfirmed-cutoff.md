# Settle two public claims without changing residents' rights

*Design478 · Constructed interlocal settlement comparison.*

Two local authorities owe one another verified reimbursements for
eligible care expenses. A owes B 60 and B owes A 50. Both claims
are payable at 08:00 with a last useful cutoff of 09:00, use the
same currency and have compatible
legal purposes and funding restrictions. Each has 15 usable
before the cutoff, after funding standing duties except where
separately stated. The match and residual are severable under the
authored legal terms. The amounts, authority and timing are
constructed. The [historical reimbursement account](../intergovernmental-reimbursement/01-observed-funder-locality-and-receipt.md)
documents unsettled public balances, with no evidence of the
reciprocal arrangement assumed here.

| Option | Actual funding requirement at the cutoff |
|---|---|
| Two independently prefunded gross payments | A needs another 45 and B another 35 if neither can use an incoming payment first. |
| Sequential gross settlement | A qualified 35-unit bridge lets B pay 50 first. A actually receives 50, then pays 60. B repays the bridge from received money on the assumed terms. A closes with 5 and B with 25, before any fee. Both payments must complete by the common cutoff. |
| One authorized reciprocal discharge followed by one residual payment | Each competent payer assents on exact terms. An independently verifiable legally indivisible act discharges 50 on each side. A pays the remaining 10 before cutoff. A closes with 5 and B with 25. The discharge reduces gross cash turnover by 100. It creates no extra source. |
| Standing separately funded care, with any interlocal settlement delayed or otherwise corrected | A protected source can fund a qualified provider when ordinary reimbursement remains blocked. Public arrears or other shortfalls still require separate resolution. |

Use reciprocal discharge in this constructed eligible branch only
where the adopting law, terms, exclusivity, evidence and due-date
cash actually qualify. Price fees, review, safety and accessibility
against ordinary gross payment and a separately funded bridge.
If B's 50 is not yet due, disputed, subject to a conflicting
payment instruction or restricted, do not match it. A still
owes 60. Its 15 is available only within existing priorities.
An independent lawful payment or funded bridge can cover the
remaining 45. Otherwise record the actual outstanding amount.
No unilateral withholding, retroactive match or new repayment
priority follows.

## When the record fails before or after the match

Use original authorization and settlement evidence under
[TPE03–TPE06](../../country/C06/60/11-public-cash-and-independent-execution.md).
The responsible payers retain independently reachable original
records and a funded manual route. An AI or other mediator can
prepare exact terms, compare records and mark discrepancies.
A proposal, summary or acknowledgement of receipt supplies
neither assent nor proof that obligations ended.

| Independently verified state | Disposition |
|---|---|
| No reciprocal discharge, with competing instructions excluded | Keep the remaining gross obligations and use a qualified ordinary payment or existing bridge. Any already final or pending direct send keeps its own verified state. |
| Discharge is uncertain | Keep both original claims visible as unresolved. Verify through the independent record. Do not replay either disputed amount or book a residual as payable under the match. Continue all independently verified unaffected duties. |
| Reciprocal discharge is final but A's residual has not settled | Only the matched 50 on each side is extinguished. Track A's 10 under its own settlement state and retry only after lawful exclusion of duplication. |
| Both the match and residual settle | Reconcile both books and verify provider and resident receipt separately. |

Suppose instead that A's 15 includes a separately protected 6-unit
readiness payment already committed for an accepted staffed care
block at the same cutoff. Preserve it under the stated source
terms. Only 9 is available for the 10 residual. The exact proposed
complete route is one unit short even with much less cash turnover.
If the match validly becomes final before that shortfall is found,
only the uncovered residual remains for lawful correction. If
the financial condition is discovered before execution, qualify
a separate lawful source or preserve already funded care and
report the unsettled public obligations. Keep any existing
individual deadlines and the next funded shift. The loss of a
payment record cannot justify sending a person to the old area,
charging them for the shortfall or requiring them to disclose
their location to settle the two public accounts.
