# PM13–PM18. Money and distribution must not purchase obedience

*Design146 · [Public media](../08-public-media-and-editorial-independence.md) · Amounts require actual context/source; formula is a selected statutory rule.*

**PM13 — A costed standing schedule, not an annual favor.** The adopting Act fixes
an actual base annual envelope K0 and base official consumer-price index I0,
with the PM01 service/need/language/coverage plan and paid staff/leave/standby,
original reporting, safety/legal defense, accessible distribution, rights, correction,
archives, premises/energy and independent governance. K0 is selected from that
qualified whole duty/capacity/source account by the Assembly, not an arbitrary GDP
share or last model's bid. Missing actual K0/context/source is unqualified operation,
not permission to insert zero, promise affordability or let the board tax people.
The positive selection rule is this service-cost/rights-source account; actual
country parameters must be enacted before authority/operation is claimed.

For year y, use the latest lawful official index vintage actually published by
1November(y−1): J_y. On a missing release retain the last actually published lawful
index and record the failure; never suspend payments waiting for EV. Select
**K_y = max(K_(y−1), K0 × J_y/I0)**, with K0 in the first schedule year. Round the annual due upward only to the
actual statutory currency minor unit; first11monthly installments round down
to that unit, with the exact annual remainder in the twelfth. No annual haircut
or invented currency unit. For partial opening/closing years, covered full months
use K_y/12 and a partial month uses that amount times legally covered days divided
by that calendar month's days. Round the resulting legally due yearly total up
once; round earlier installments down and put only its remainder in the final
covered installment. This follows lawful component dates, not actual newsroom
delay or a backdated claim for uncovered months. Initial asset acquisition/preparation costs remain
separate from the annual operating envelope and actual buffer stock. At least
one actual independent correction route can contest a material source error;
a valid correction yields only the lawfully due prospective/arrears adjustment,
not a Treasury reinterpretation or fictitious earlier cash. Actual unpaid costs
or higher real requirements are reported for elected source/remit decision;
the formula is neither an adequacy certificate nor a cap cancelling due claims.

**PM14 — Pay and review without a fiscal hostage.** Treasury pays K_y/12 monthly
in advance under express standing authority, with separation of verification and
execution and existing B19 remedies. The statute requires a public source/remit/
alternative review every four years; missing that review leaves the actual standing
schedule, not editorial approval or an expired general budget bridge. Assembly may
prospectively amend/replace the schedule and service by express primary law within
rights, actual notice/transition and applicable A9/RR dates. Annual appropriation,
a procurement rider, bad ratings or disliked stories cannot silently repeal it.
No broadcaster, accountant, auditor or court acquires a fiscal-policy veto.

Budget a separately sourced three-month operating buffer **K0/4** as an opening
cash stock, with lawful custody/replenishment and actual source, not a second
annual expense or an automatically real fund. Shared Treasury cash cannot back
several simultaneous reserves. Existing essential/democratic claims across the
country retain prior-law scarcity/source protections; no institution prints its
own rescue money, sweeps insurance/CF funds or declares itself always first.
Missing cash despite authority is failed payment, not optional political withholding
or permission for a court to create money. Preserve actual unpaid claims/received
execution evidence, including actual independent records/server/source custody.

**PM15 — No advertising or patron's purse.** This Service takes no commercial/political
advertising, sponsored placement, earmarked or general private donations, household
device/licence fee or personal-data-sale revenue. Ordinary private/community media
retain their different lawful models; no economy-wide advertising ban follows.
Commissioned journalism/rights/contracts are paid under SC/KR with published actual
control/conflicts and editorial independence, not gifts buying favorable coverage.
Actual ancillary receipts/property have their ordinary TA/NF/public-account scope;
a public-media label gives no blanket commercial-tax exemption or budget offset
chosen secretly by a minister. Counting public payments/tax internally creates no
new national real resource, trained journalist, source or foreign capacity.

**PM16 — News is not a campaign account or government press office.** Keep ordinary
journalism, independently lawful PC/ER guides/forums and official service/alert
communications separately labeled, sourced and paid, with shared actual work
counted once. PM's remit creates no new election access/eligibility or paid party
message right; existing campaign-source/guide/forum/first-vote dates remain exact.
Official warnings require competent actual source/authentication and correction;
urgency is no partisan speech slot or forced editorial endorsement. Critical
journalism about a politician is not automatically paid campaigning. Actual
coordinated express-electoral spending/public misuse keeps PC's substance test.
All national/local official advertising and notice purchases outside this Service
use SC's objective actual audience/access/cost criteria, published awards and
quarterly issuer/recipient/control/gross-expenditure records published within
30calendar days after quarter end, with lawful privacy
limits. Criticism/favor cannot be an award criterion or editorial condition; this
limits the public buyer, not private speech or every outlet's lawful funding model.

**PM17 — Plural reach and future forms.** Maintain usable open archives/feeds,
accessible source/correction links and actual non-internet alternatives; nobody
must use a preferred assistant to receive news. Need and access evidence determines
formats and languages, not assumed universal broadband, legacy licence labels or
forced cultural identity. Radio, print, speech, text, audio, audiovisual, immersive
or future representations can meet equivalent functions with disclosed loss/limits.
Preserve original evidence versus reconstruction, editorial/translation attribution
and disputed meanings; unfamiliar concepts are not automatically false or illegal.

If the Service uses recommendation/summaries, disclose material selection purposes/
criteria and offer a usable nonprofiled alternative. No covert audience political
profiling, engagement-only target or automatic ranking that silently defines who
was heard. Lawful independent private/community distribution remains; the Service
cannot grant itself preferred carriage, suppress competitors or command every
platform. Actual access/concentration disputes use MP/PD/general courts, not
editorial board permission. Outlets may criticize this institution or the country.

**PM18 — Useful AI without replicated authority.** Task-bound AI may transcribe,
translate, retrieve records, compare claims and deliver authorized low-risk routine
formats under C11/HC/PD. Consequential journalism needs competent accountable
editorial judgment, time/source access and real power to reject; not a compulsory
single click for every routine operation. Disclose synthetic reconstructions/
material uncertainty. Repeating one source through models is no corroboration;
agents/copies supply no extra trustee, audience mandate or source independence.
No model/vendor certifies political truth or privately retires the paid human/
other-provider route. Actually acquired public-owned publication rights may be
made openly reusable with attribution/no implied endorsement under KR; protect
third-party IP, human creator pay/credit, confidential sources and personal safety.
