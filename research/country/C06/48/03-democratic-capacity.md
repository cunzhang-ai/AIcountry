# Democracy must work with a full lawful field

Use an explicitly hypothetical 80,000 qualified national electorate and 40 seats,
plus four 20,000-person local electorates with nine seats each. These are not
actual membership, geography or eligibility findings. Existing EL/PC law remains
the design owner; the operating model neither changes entry nor delays duties.

EL06 gives a national endorsement threshold of 500 and at most 320 qualified
lists/25,600 nominees. Each local case permits 200 lists/3,600 nominees. Replace
the old two-track source fixture rather than reuse its staff count. A maximum
field is a planning boundary, not a prediction, target or budget admission cap.

The national source case requires 19,680 task hours. With five declared available
task days at 5.9 task hours/day, it needs **668 distinct released source workers**,
plus training; paying 33.4 annual-role equivalents does not provide these people.
Four available days would need 834, not 668. The available-day assumption cannot
extend legal clocks. Local source/ballot/formation peaks still need separate
current qualification; national staffing must not be presented as all-scope proof.

An authored 10% nominee/list challenge stress gives 2,592 cases. Urgent work in
one task day needs **220 distinct judges and 220 counsel** under the specified
tasks; annual-role funding cannot create competence, legal appointment or
unconflicted simultaneous availability. Neither nine constitutional judges,
16 ordinary trial judges nor a status-peer pool supplies 220 free judges.
This exposes missing capacity, not a demonstrated real incidence or permission
to bar challengers, wait beyond useful time or postpone an election.

At the declared monthly B=1,104 and minor unit .01, each PC credit is 5.52.
Annual national/local maximum source envelope is 883,200; one national contest,
four local contests and one national question add 1,324,800: **2,208,000 CU**.
This is maximum eligible source capacity, not assumed assigned/received spend.
A national repeat's 441,600 cash exposure is separate and excludes rerun staff,
printing and formation. Eligibility, principal/source control and actual receipt
must be proved; an account or copied agent does not multiply political mandate.

The full-paper request sensitivity quotes 30.24m pages before accessibility,
1.451520m CU for pages, and 1,320 national/local/question forums. A national guide
alone is 269 pages and national list presentations total 37.33 hours. Printed
availability is no evidence of usable understanding. Neutral navigation, framing,
disability/language access, attention and mediator power remain material limits.
AI summaries or future media can assist interpretation, not silently choose
the lawful field, infer consent or replace public control. Practical opposition
requires timely receiving and enforced remedy, not just a paid directory.
