# OJ31–OJ35: usable independent justice needs real sources and paid time

*Part of [C08/25](../25-ordinary-justice-and-public-prosecution.md); design120 candidate.*

**OJ31 — Public source, no self-appropriation.** Assembly enacts funded court/
prosecution/case-officer/selection/aid/records/execution posts and bounded standing
core operations, with transparent gross recurrent/transition budgets. Judicial
and prosecution management propose actual workload and separate own budgets;
Treasury pays verified lawfully due items without the litigating official's
selective release. Prior-law continuing core scope can outlast the distinct
90-day budget bridge, not fund unlimited new programs. Courts/Council/DPP cannot
levy, borrow, expand pay or create appropriations themselves. All actual source
and public-risk losses remain unpriced, not funded by old D11 or fines/recovery.

**OJ32 — Exact claim and institutional budget review.** Personal judicial/prosecutor/
case-officer pay/status, selectors' ordinary acts and ordinary court/prosecution
budget legality/retaliation go to unimplicated OJ16 formations, with B17 useful-
time relief. Genuine constitutional questions use CJ01–CJ12, including CJ12's
unchanged closed exception for the constitutional court's own budget/status.
OJ does not turn ordinary status into that seven-person jurisdiction. Direct
personal-interest rules apply to all formations; OJ19 visiting judges need prior
lawful appointment and actual independent terms. Compel available authorized
dues/records or lawful reconsideration, not a new tax/general allocation. Genuine
cash/physical shortage preserves the unpaid claim and elected source duty;
refusal needs proved actual ability/fault before personal consequences.

**OJ33 — Paid calendars, reserves and legal help.** C06 counts trial/appeal/status,
CJ12 external and correction formations, recusal/reserves, prosecutors/independent
case officers, clerks/aid/translation/source custody and actual executors once.
One person cannot be a simultaneous trial judge, selector, substitute, external
appellant reviewer and paid worker elsewhere. No FD07 annual constitutional spare
hour is ordinary staffed capacity. Establish actual duty coverage and independent
source/record/payment paths, including ordinary night liberty/urgent services,
political access, worker/recipient cases and a hostile outgoing executive.
Missing qualified people remains failure, never a lower quorum, longer custody,
unpaid family role or assumed AI saving. Shared buildings/payroll can serve
different powers only with actual access/conflict/source separation.

**OJ34 — Sources and future methods are contestable.** Preserve original evidence,
case allocation/selection/career/instruction reasons, dissent, applicable rules
and safe correction history with purpose limits, actual access and successor
custody. Public reasons protect children, witnesses, diagnosis and safe locations;
no universal public litigation/person dossier. Speech, signing, remote presence,
simulation and future media may support understanding without creating consent
or votes. Disclose framing/translation loss, sponsors, vendor/source dependence
and unsupported meanings. A party can replace its assistant, challenge a summary
and use a real human/offline route without loss of standing or original date.
AI may assist disclosed search/translation/admin; no secret judge ranking,
appointment, charge/merits gate, conviction or extra mandate from replication.

**OJ35 — A judgment is not actual protection.** Orders specify lawful duty owner,
act/amount/time, available payer/asset/source, independent civil executor, notice/
review and evidence of receipt. B18/U private and only expressly adopted B19
public execution apply; OJ coordinated law expressly includes established OJ
public due/state orders within that actual B19 source. No new bailiff tax,
sovereign force, wage/firm/investor guarantee or body-created appropriation.
Protected counterpart/worker/client/foreign rights remain. If every decision,
record/payment or receiver fails, preserve the unmet remedy/urgent relief and
report constitutional failure in practice; neither a court order nor a corrected
AI output creates missing staff, foreign consent, dignity or democratic control.

[UCL01–UCL08](13-urgent-civil-legal-assistance.md) names the covered
civil assistance roles and paid alternates within the same OJ33 calendar.
