# Evidence for household protection and temporary receiving

Two historical Housing Ombudsman decisions supply evidence of specific
failure mechanisms. They are adjudicated accounts of individual complaints.
They do not establish national prevalence, project readiness or the cost
of delivering the proposed route.

| Primary decision | Documented finding or limit | Use in the design |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Notting Hill Genesis, 202117181](https://www.housing-ombudsman.org.uk/decisions/notting-hill-genesis-202117181/) | Paragraphs 11–19 find delays in temporary heating and alternative accommodation and insufficient assessment of interim measures. The resident had raised concerns about the practicability of using boiling water. The decision leaves acceptance of the eventual hotel offer unclear. | Verify whether a resident can use an interim measure and distinguish a booking or offer from actual receiving. |
| [Peabody Trust, 202221669](https://www.housing-ombudsman.org.uk/decisions/peabody-trust-202221669/) | Paragraphs 32–39 document five temporary moves during prolonged repairs. The landlord acknowledged that the repair overrun should have been communicated sooner to the accommodation team. The resident returned to a home with failed heating that had gone undetected before return. | Recheck accommodation before its renewal deadline, and verify the home's practical safety before handback. |

The same-site care condition is a design inference for a resident whose
care and safe accommodation must coincide. Neither decision directly
tests that condition or the proposed duty officer, funding schedule or
appeal route. The [constructed comparison](../settlement/household-protection/README.md)
adds those assumptions explicitly. Neither complainant took part in this
design decision. The source links identify the full primary records.
