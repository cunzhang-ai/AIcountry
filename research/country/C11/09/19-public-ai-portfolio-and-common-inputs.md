# DP89–DP94. Choose public AI uses on common service and physical capacity

*Design465 · Part of [public digital operation](../09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md).*

**DP89 — Compare tasks before choosing common infrastructure.** The
existing digital support office publishes proposed uses, purposes,
costs and common dependencies in the joint budget and grid account.
Compare competent ordinary service, nonexclusive shared public
capacity and independently qualified procurement. The elected budget
decides material public allocation. Each competent sector retains
the authority to choose and stop its own task. A local government's
decision, independent court function or resident's choice cannot
be taken over through a shared purchase. A public option supplies
no compulsory national model or common resident dossier.

**DP90 — Admit only the physically usable scope.** Show ordinary,
peak and recovery demand on paid compute, electricity, cooling,
water and communications where material. Join declared connections
and actual availability under EG and C06, including shared upstream
dependence. Count each useful slot and energy allocation once.
Verify reserved essential loads and existing obligations before
optional AI use. Place any flexible load on previously lawful and
tested terms. Designate its exact queue, stopping conditions, pending
work and next feasible start. For material deferral, qualify the
restarted load and unfinished work with the connection operator under
EG14–EG18. Separately reconcile bound purchases, applicable cancellation
or imbalance charges, replacement power and timely usable settlements
by payer. A lower future spot price supplies no budget authority or
proof of avoided cost. If the later window or its funds fail, retain
the qualified earlier block where available or use staffed ordinary
service; disclose deferred optional work and carry already due cases.
Any protected power treatment follows the underlying duty's existing
classification and actual minimum supply. A municipal or sovereign customer has
no automatic priority or power to change grid rules.

**DP91 — Fund the work revealed by better access.** Distinguish an
optional access tool from the underlying right to file and be heard.
Estimate the additional eligible and unresolved work generated by
easier entry and voluntary take-up. Fund actual downstream merits,
professional supervision, ordinary intake, remedies and independent
appeal before optional widening. A legitimate intake surge must
still receive lawful receipt and retain its original clock.
Preserve the ordinary and urgent routes. Do not suppress new
claims, charge unpaid household effort or reset case dates to
match the capacity forecast.

**DP92 — Choose between competing uses with explicit reasons.**
Compare the best feasible uses of the same money, peak capacity
and independent staff on a common ordinary and adverse calendar.
State whose receipt improves, who still waits and which improvement
is forgone. Preserve existing priorities and make a separate elected
choice when legitimate requirements compete. Keep safe routine
work and processing that can proceed independently. A verified
task gain carries no appropriation, entitlement to a shared slot
or standing authority to redirect a court's capacity.

**DP93 — Keep the common layer replaceable and purpose-limited.**
Where supported, purchase usable public specifications, reusable
nonpersonal test material and lawful tools separately from live
records and binding decisions. Use authority, protected licensing
and ordinary procurement under LX and DP. Shared public funding
does not require a single private vendor. Test loss of the common
physical route and a separate absence of the human receiver.
Preserve timely fallback and qualified successor training. An
unfunded local alternative or an inaccessible language path stays
an exposed limit, including in the next operating window.

**DP94 — Verify ordinary and adverse receiving before scale.** The
service owner records actual receipt, open cases, additional
eligibility, priced resources, shared use and capacity loss. An
independent reviewer tests costs, alternate arrangements, affected
people and distribution. Reopen on new need, physical constraint,
lost qualified staffing, a new model dependency or stronger
ordinary delivery. Courts, elected bodies and service authorities
keep their own powers. AI may reconcile the ledger and compare
options. It cannot admit its own use, assign legal priority,
release a budget or certify continuity.
