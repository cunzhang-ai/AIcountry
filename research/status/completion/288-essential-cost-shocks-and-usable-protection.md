# Design380. Protect the purchasing power and access of the income floor

[I36–I41](../../country/C05/06/10-essential-cost-shocks-and-usable-protection.md)
joins monthly indexation, early hardship, actual price and availability,
received provision and the next separately funded delivery. It preserves
the individual cash floor, separate services and original rights.

[Observed price evidence and the two-place comparison](../../settlement/essential-cost-shocks-and-usable-protection/01-two-places-and-one-shock.md)
test why a price-only response misses supply loss. Actual local
evidence, independent review, authority, funded capacity and receipt
remain unverified.
