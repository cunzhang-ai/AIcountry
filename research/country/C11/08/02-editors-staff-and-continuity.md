# PM07–PM12. Editors answer for work without taking orders on conclusions

*Design146 · [Public media](../08-public-media-and-editorial-independence.md) · No actual staff or authorized publishing established.*

**PM07 — One accountable editor/director.** Four actual board votes with five present,
after open merits applications, hearings/conflict review and21-day proposed-publication,
appoint an editor/director for **five years, nonrenewable**. This officer is not a
trustee and has no casting vote. Criteria concern actual source judgment, editorial/
financial responsibility, safe management, access and ability to protect inquiry;
legacy credentials/party loyalty or a model score cannot be exclusive gates.
The officer manages the enacted remit and funded plan, qualified staff, independent
desks and professional standards. Board approves general budget/policies but no
named story, omission or conclusion. Normal assignments require competent editorial
judgment, not a ceremonial human click on an AI's political narrative.

**PM08 — Professional decisions and protected staff.** Recruit and promote through
C04 job-related open methods under WL, with real worker voice, fair discipline,
protected outside reporting and independent challenge to retaliation. The chief
may delegate ordinary editing to named qualified desk editors within actual scope;
no all-headlines approval bottleneck. New evidence and fresh investigations of
selectors, unions, government or this institution remain normal lawful work.
Interests, corrections and serious factual allegations require professional
verification and a meaningful timely opportunity to answer; refusal to comment
is no veto. Distinguish observation, allegation, inference, commentary and uncertainty.
Criminal presumption of innocence does not prohibit responsible investigation.
No quotas for flattering, hostile or mechanically equal airtime; public-service
fairness does not require false equivalence between evidence and fabrication.

**PM09 — A separate complaints editor with publication power.** The same open board
vote appoints a nontrustee complaints editor for five nonrenewable years, distinct
from management and stories they review. The role has its own explicit funded line,
records access with source/privacy safeguards, independent advice and public findings.
It can require publication of a reasoned complaint finding and editorial response
under PM19, not order political views, select the news agenda, punish other media,
license journalism or conclusively adjudicate a person's civil/criminal claim.
It cannot be transferred to EV's statistician or an official AI truth service.
Private independent media need no approval from this role.

**PM10 — Cause and external review, not retaliatory dismissal.** Chief and complaints
editor have PM06's external-court removal/appeal protection for serious actual
misconduct, persistent material duty breach, corrupt conflict or accommodated
inability. Board four votes may initiate proceedings, but actual affected-standing/
integrity routes remain without that gate. B17 judicial suspension preserves reply/
shorter urgency limits; mere allegations, ratings or a minister's demand do not
suspend an investigation or put another editor in charge. Ordinary employment
responsibility remains; public office is no personal immunity or guaranteed second
term. Court orders removal/correction within law, not a desired editorial line.

**PM11 — Continuing journalism during vacancies.** Before a vacancy, appoint a
qualified career deputy through actual open recruitment, with board's normal
four-vote approval of that senior appointment. If chief is absent/suspended, the
deputy may act for at most180elapsed days, with existing funds/remit and fresh
ordinary journalism, necessary safety/correction and service contracts under the
existing plan. No enlarged remit, discretionary permanent senior appointments,
major long-term commitments or partisan staff purge. New scandals are not banned
as “new policy”. No serial acting title resets the same vacancy.

After180days no one has acting-chief powers. Already lawfully delegated desk
editors/finance custodians retain their own bounded statutory/funded work,
including new reporting, not powers reserved to the missing chief/board. If no
actual deputy exists, those actual desk duties continue immediately; no first
appointment/deputy is invented. A missing complaints editor leaves existing career
intake/correction duties and direct courts; it grants the chief no power to issue
an independent finding on their own work. Unperformed roles and useful-time harm
stay public failures, not fictionally approved choices or extended terms.

**PM12 — Bootstrap without a founder's newsroom.** A valid founding/implementing
instrument names actual C04 recruitment custodians, paid initial career posts,
open job/source safeguards and lawful roll/draw access before any appointment.
Retrofitting an actual service uses real protected transferring staff/rights.
Where no actual workforce exists, custodians may fill only those enacted genuine
paid initial work posts through open recruitment; no nominal employees hired solely
to manufacture a voting class, political appointments or presumed volunteer capacity.
That actual workforce elects its three trustees under the unchanged rule.

Three public trustees alone have no five-person quorum. Failure to constitute a
real board, appoint competent editors, fund staff or reach audiences is a launch
failure; a project owner, minister, AI or improvised “acting” organ supplies none.
Preparation mandates permit actual investigation/training/source preservation only
within their legal scope. Private speech remains free. Existing services/claims
continue until lawful receiving; an exact service due date is no proof of delivery.
