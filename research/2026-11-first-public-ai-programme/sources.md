# Design497 evidence and limits

- The [UK Government Digital Service report](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/microsoft-365-copilot-experiment-cross-government-findings-report/microsoft-365-copilot-experiment-cross-government-findings-report-html)
  describes a cross-government 2024 trial with 20,000 participants across
  12 organizations. Participant-reported time savings are observations
  about perceived utility. The report supplies no transferable project
  engineering, beneficial case disposition, freed roster or budget saving.
- [Brynjolfsson, Li and Raymond, Generative AI at Work](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjae044)
  reports a staggered introduction involving 5,172 customer-support
  agents and a 15 percent average result measured in issues resolved
  per hour. One private operation and a distinct task cannot
  establish the effect on public casework or capital works.
- The [construction sequencing comparison](../settlement/construction-capacity-and-sequencing/01-two-windows-and-one-inspection-market.md),
  [public AI gain comparison](../settlement/public-ai-service-gains/01-casework-time-access-and-funded-alternates.md),
  [independent review funding test](../settlement/ai-capacity-and-budget-release/01-two-offices-one-independent-roster.md)
  and [future civic media case](../settlement/civic-media-and-democratic-replacement/01-two-media-and-one-floor-decision.md)
  supply authored earlier constraints. Their calendars and amounts
  remain separate. The Design497 portfolio figures are constructed.
- The [physical portfolio rules](../country/C11/09/19-public-ai-portfolio-and-common-inputs.md)
  keep procurement, power, actors, lawful demand and exit distinct.
  Actual owners must produce new operating evidence for the first offer.
