# OA01–OA07. Give the public an enforceable right to official records

*Design375 · Part of [accountable administration](../03-administration-and-services.md) · Proposed ordinary law.*

**OA01 — Give everyone access to public records within defined limits.**
A person may request existing records of public action held or lawfully
controlled by national or local authorities, public administrative
functions of courts and legislatures, and others acting under delegated
public authority. Include records held on the authority's behalf by
vendors, advisers or other custodians. The responsible public office
must obtain these under its existing rights or authorized contracts.
Separate private work, judicial case records, legislative privilege
and other special protected material under their applicable laws.
No citizenship, press accreditation, stated motive or mandatory
digital identity is required. Use a minimal safe return channel.
Give accessible assistance to identify existing recorded information.
Include an ordinary extraction from recorded data where feasible.
There is no duty to invent an analysis or generate an answer as a
substitute for an original record. Personal-record, party, member,
audit and whistleblower access retain their own scopes and safeguards.

**OA02 — Give the request an owner and preserve the source.** Each
covered authority names a paid career records officer, substitute and
secure retrieval path. Receipt by a designated accessible public route
starts the original clock. Within three working days the officer
offers necessary scope help, gives a tracking reference and names the
office responsible. A mistaken address or vendor handoff within
covered government preserves the original date. Secure relevant
versions, messages and records under lawful scoped retention,
including material official acts in a vendor or informal system.
A missing record requires a reasoned account of search and custody,
including any applicable duty to hold it. Preserve independent
correction and AA examination where a required record is missing.
Neither a vendor summary nor a formal filing system's silence is
proof that the public record does not exist.

**OA03 — Disclose usable portions and justify each withholding.** Publish
or deliver the actual releasable record in a reasonably usable format,
with source date, versions and visible omission markers. Apply only
precisely enacted exceptions protecting privacy and confidential
testimony, safety, legitimate security and investigations, fair
proceedings and privilege, defined commercial interests or narrowly
protected live deliberation. For qualified grounds, demonstrate
foreseeable specific harm, necessity and proportionality and consider
the overriding public interest. Follow express stronger protections
for absolute grounds. Embarrassment or a supplier's confidentiality
label cannot alone justify refusal. Give itemized reasons, applicable
authority, duration or reconsideration event and direct review.
Protect where even confirmation would cause the specified harm.
The reviewer can inspect the real original under lawful protection.
Limit fees to authorized marginal copying costs with a free accessible
inspection route. Handle evidenced unreasonable burden through
assistance, narrowing, rolling release or reviewable limited refusal.
Criticism, repeated official errors or an anonymous return channel
do not make a request abusive.

**OA04 — Deliver promptly and protect an impending public opportunity.**
The proposed outer ordinary decision and delivery window is 20 working
days from first receipt. Give a decision on available parts and first
usable release within that period. One reasoned extension of at most
ten working days for genuinely complex review may cover only identified
unresolved portions, with notice before the original date. Staffing
shortage or a handoff cannot reset the clock. For an identifiable
imminent hearing, consultation, public decision or risk of serious
irreversible harm, decide and deliver safe priority material by the
last useful time where possible, and decide its priority within two
working days or earlier if necessary. Give a prompt partial release,
protect the remaining records and name the independent route for any
gap. An expedited request neither enlarges disclosure powers nor
automatically delays a public decision, hearing or election.

**OA05 — Make refusal, silence and failed delivery independently reviewable.**
A requester or protected third party can go directly to a competent
ordinary court under B17 and the adopting law. Internal review is
optional. Fund accessible filing, unconflicted duty review and an
independent retrieval and release route. The court can inspect protected
originals, order preservation, search, partial disclosure, corrected
reasons or actual delivery, and guard against unsafe release. Verify
appointment, conflict substitutes, source, evidence and actual court
execution outside the office refusing access. Use a named authorized
custodian or executor and actual receipt by the relevant date. The
court cannot take over an elected policy decision. Where a missed
disclosure prejudiced a statutory opportunity, the lawful decision
owner and court apply their existing reconsideration or relief powers.
Record actual loss even if the file arrives later.

**OA06 — Fund access and publish the difference between reply and receipt.**
Competent elected budgets fund intake, indexing, independent
retrieval, protected redaction, accessible delivery and courts alongside
due work. Publish safely held recurrent records, material decision
documents and their effective dates in usable formats under existing
law. Publish safe aggregate counts by clock, extension, full and partial
release, actual useful-time receipt and unexecuted remedy. Do not create
a list of requesters or make protected personal records public to
improve a metric. AI may search lawfully accessible records or propose
redactions for qualified review. It cannot settle the scope or refuse,
disclose or reconstruct an official original on its own.

**OA07 — Preserve rights through a change of custodian.** Adopt
prospectively with paid source and lawful authority. Keep previous
receipts, scoped holds, records, deadlines and pending challenges
with a named qualified successor through contract, media or
government change. Stronger sector publication and disclosure,
including environmental information, stay operative. Reopen on
delayed delivery, requester retaliation, unsafe disclosure, shared
vendor control or a simpler qualified route with equal access.
