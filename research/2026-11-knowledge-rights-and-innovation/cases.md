## A. A paid research licence is not received medicine

A prior research grant 12 buys a defined foreground licence for the designated
supplier, territory, acts, public purpose, period and conditions. A later
supply quote 12 contains processing 2, foreign ingredients/packaging 4, quality 2,
distribution/clinical handoff 1 and the exact same foreground permission 3.
Only if the complete matched permission remains valid is the repeated 3 removed:
current distinct uses 9, past-plus-current uses 21, no public return 21.
An altered recipient, acts, territory, purpose, period, conditions or actual
holder authority requires fresh review, not automatic reuse or a deemed new
fee payable. Background rights and all other costs remain distinct/unpriced.

The fixture has nominal quote cash 9 and zero owned foreign quote-equivalent
against the named foreign 4. A nominal budget test passes but foreign settlement
fails; local cash has no presumed conversion guarantee. Actual currencies,
exchange and safe ingredients remain unknown. Licence, quality authorization,
patient consent, safe manufacture, stocked care and money are separate.

## C. A commission does not buy all future creative receipts

## D. A cheaper unit can cost more during a paid transition

A separate authored 120,000-unit/year case retains a 10CU incumbent quote. A new
path quotes inputs 4, quality 1 and a hypothetical negotiated royalty 0.5 per unit,
plus initial know-how 250,000 and validation 150,000. These omit other upstream,
capital, finance/insurance, later maintenance and full supplier costs;0.5 is

Assume the new path could begin only after four months. Keeping 40,000 incumbent
units costs 400,000;80,000 new units cost 440,000; initial distinct uses 400,000.
First-year named quote 1,240,000 exceeds retained incumbent 1,200,000 by 40,000.
A later full-year named quote 660,000 excludes additional costs and does not
establish comparative effectiveness, readiness, safe supply or a public saving.
At zero delay the first-year subtotal is 1,060,000; at twelve months 1,600,000,
with no new supply that year. Paid bridge/time matters; a licence removes none
of these distinct delivery uses or creates the required source.

## E. The licensed operator owes a royalty before its invoice arrives

An operator owes 0.5 on 5 January but has 0.1usable cash; expected invoice 1.5 on 10 January
is unreceived at due time. Gap 0.4 remains. No automatic Treasury guarantee arises
from public procurement or the patent order; source failure needs lawful service
continuity and a disclosed unpaid claim, not fictional payment or production.
If a valid public instrument also assumes payment, it creates only that stated
public duty. It does not release the operator without valid creditor-approved
or statutory novation. Both payers may answer for one 0.5 claim, not a total 1.0;
credit actual payments once. A release without an identified replacement basis
is unsupported by this fixture and is not silently treated as valid authority.
