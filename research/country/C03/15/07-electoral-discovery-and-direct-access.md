# EDA01–EDA06. Give voters a direct route to election information

13 November 2026 · Integrated design 442 · Candidate ordinary law

**EDA01 — Fund the independent public route first.** The electoral
authority publishes operative voting information and the current lawful
candidate field with source, scope and date. Offer accessible direct,
assisted and offline paths and an authenticated alternate. Give each
qualified contestant the same opportunity to link or provide their own
materials in the public directory. Show whether a contestant supplied
materials and preserve routes to correct omission or stale status.
Candidate qualification and ballot validity retain their own law.

**EDA02 — Designate only a demonstrated bottleneck.** Prior ordinary
law specifies the independent regulator, evidence, remedies and
bounded election period. Designation requires evidence that a material
affected group depends on the service for election discovery, practical
substitutes fail before its deadline, and public-route repair alone
cannot close the gap in time. Assess shared suppliers, indices and
controllers. Give the provider, contestants and affected voters an
opportunity to challenge evidence. Publish reasoned scope, expiry and
ordinary court review before reliance. The service, not a particular
viewpoint or contestant, is the subject. No ad hoc ministerial or
election-officer designation during a contest.

**EDA03 — Make a direct and nonprofiled choice usable.** On a designated
service's user-requested election discovery surface, give a visible,
accessible route to authenticated official voting information, the
complete lawful field and contestant-supplied materials. The route
must be available without submitting a political preference or
accepting personalized ranking. Use the user's explicit contest,
language and accessibility choices within that request. Keep original
materials inspectable and distinguish election administration from
contestant claims. The provider may meet the duty through a
dependable direct link with independent fallback. General editorial
ranking, commentary, candidate speech and choice of assistant keep
their ordinary protections. No duty to equalize audience or endorse
any claim follows.

**EDA04 — Test actual retrieval and its failure roots.** Use voluntary
task-based accessibility tests, aggregate results, include late
qualified candidates and affected groups, and count each participant
once across routes. Test by the original postal, early and ordinary
voting windows. A different interface drawing on the same stale
source does not supply independent recovery. Preserve accurate and
accessible correction notices. Reviewers receive necessary evidence
under lawful custody. Do not build voter-query files or disclose
political interests to campaigns or funders.

**EDA05 — Correct access before the voter loses it.** Give voters and
contestants a reachable first desk and direct independent review.
A reasoned remedy can restore the covered access path, correct a
broken link or stale directory, or use a paid public alternate.
Where the official source is disputed, preserve the challenge and
use the competent electoral and evidence correction routes.
Keep paid campaigning, false voting instructions, public-media
editorial duties and election validity under their separate rules.
A delayed remedy records a lost opportunity. It supplies no
automatic change in voting dates, qualification or result.

**EDA06 — Fund oversight, compare rivals and allow expiry.** Keep
existing election and independent review costs funded alongside
limited provider compliance. Use ordinary public appropriations for
official paths, and specify any provider duty under competent law.
Test a stronger public route alone, the scoped duty and wider
mandatory ranking on access, privacy, speech, time and cost.
Select the scoped duty only while it adds timely access on the
evidence. Retain independent and assisted public access if a
provider exits. Preserve pending complaints and evidence through
lawful custody and keep powers replaceable. AI may check routes
and stale references. It cannot select a lawful field, decide an
electoral dispute or certify its own accessibility.
