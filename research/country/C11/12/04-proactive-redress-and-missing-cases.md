# LX11–LX16. Make a common administrative error lead to proactive redress

*Part of [public rules and verifiable implementation](../12-public-rules-and-verifiable-implementation.md). Design342 research selection.*

**LX11 — Start a public duty to find and remedy affected cases.** When a
competent duty owner or independent reviewer establishes a material common
error with possible missed payments or wrongfully imposed charges, name the
accountable sector officer and executing payer within two working days of
the finding. Existing LX06 stops, urgent relief, ordinary duties and court
deadlines apply from their own earlier grounds. Within ten working days
publish a protected scope and a dated search, notice, review and execution
plan. Give the independent reviewer access to necessary originals and a way
to test exclusions. Report uncertainty and actual staffing and funding.
The programme starts on the common finding. An individual need not file
a new application or relitigate an already binding common issue.

**LX12 — Search beyond the system that made the error.** Bound the search by
law, rule versions, periods, affected decisions and functional dependencies.
Compare the operational list with lawful independent records of protected
intake, awards, payments, collections and people dropped before a final
decision. Check safe evidence from affected people and a relevant sample
of excluded records. Give the reviewer a search route outside the
failing roster, including failed intake and absent confirmations.
Separate the known lower bound from any defensible coverage estimate.
For people without records, offer an assisted and offline route
without requiring a repeat application. A credible omission triggers
a documented scope check with a named owner and date. Continue
separately verified unaffected payments during that check. Search for former recipients and estates where the governing law
allows payment. Deduplicate persons, decisions, periods and obligations
only to the supported extent. Preserve unmatched and disputed links
without merging identities. Record the identified scope, open groups and
unknown coverage. Publish safe totals and methods. A matched roster is
a minimum search result while material gaps remain. Use bounded links,
purpose limits and lawful retention. Create no universal claimant register.

**LX13 — Pay established amounts and give useful decisions on the rest.**
An authorized career officer may approve payment of an independently
verified undisputed amount under an inspectable rule, including an
authorized batch under TU. Establish the lawful recipient, applicable
period, amount and settlement status before release. Use a batch only
under TU06 for independently checked common grounds and individually established
payee, amount and safe receiving. Keep safe outreach, unsettled
instructions and discretionary decisions on separately paid routes.
Never treat silence, a roster match or a familiar address as proof
of authority or consent. An established favorable payment may
continue on a lawfully verified standing route. A changed or disputed
route requires its own proof and any consent required by law.
Use SCN01–SCN05 for safe contact. Pay the established
amount within five working days after it is verified and lawfully payable,
or by any earlier existing duty. Do this while unresolved amounts are
reviewed. Where estate authority, identity, settlement or other material
facts remain disputed, assign a human reviewer, dated next action and a
safe contact route. Missing public records use RLW and ordinary lawful
evidence rules. Keep original claims, protective advances where available
and distinct compensation or interest under their own law.

**LX14 — Separate favorable redress from adverse recovery.** Stop disputed
collections within the applicable authority and cancel or refund unlawful
charges once established. Reconcile amounts actually received for the same
obligation so they are paid once. A suspected overpayment or separately
claimed debt needs its own authority, proof, notice, reply, hardship
protection and independent review. A common correction cannot use a
different person's, period's or programme's alleged debt to withhold an
undisputed amount. Any lawful same-obligation credit is separately
explained. Preserve valid final orders and third-party rights and use
their lawful correction route. A payment, silence or receipt acknowledgment
waives no other claim or appeal.

**LX15 — Verify usable receipt before closing the work.** Report identified
people and obligations separately, including those reviewed, amounts due,
sent, received, returned, unclaimed, disputed or still outside the search.
A paid status requires settlement to the lawful recipient through a usable
route. Use protected alternate contact and an assigned payer for failed
delivery. Where restoration needs another public service, send only lawful
necessary correction evidence and follow the authorized receiver to actual
relief. The independent reviewer checks a sample of exclusions from a
separately justified frame and a sample of receipts, can require scope or process correction within its
powers and records residual uncertainty. Set a useful independent exclusion and
delivery checkpoint while corrections can still arrive. An incomplete
search or omitted group remains open with a funded responsible owner.
Close only the resolved scope.
Keep each residual case and original remedy with a funded ordinary
successor. Where an operator closes, test paid receiving, original
filing, safe payee, finality and direct challenge under FT05–FT08
and LPC02–LPC07. Keep unresolved amounts and exclusion leads funded
where legally due and record their continuing owners. A fixed system
release, high review count or closure date
cannot discharge an undelivered obligation.

**LX16 — Pay for the remedy and keep authority with accountable people.**
Programme budgets identify standing obligations, dated redress, protected
review and any paid alternate separately. A cash or staff shortfall
remains a breach or unpaid obligation where already due. Expose it for
independent review and an elected source decision. Use AI for bounded
search, discrepancy detection and reproducible comparisons under verified
sources. Qualified officers decide disputed facts and release lawful funds.
Adopt prospectively with paid migration and preserve accrued rights,
earlier deadlines and stronger remedies. Review after the first complete
remedy cycle and reopen on omitted groups, repeated failed receipts,
unsafe linkage, excessive delay or a simpler effective route.
