# PCO17–PCO21. Count changes where a reform moves capacity or burdens

13 November 2026 · Integrated design 449 · Candidate evaluation and response

**PCO17 — Follow the effect beyond the participating area.** For a
material expansion or claim of comparative improvement, identify
where the changed route obtains staff, money, physical capacity and
unpaid help, and where it sends costs, delays or harm. Include
affected nonparticipants, service origins and shared systems across
jurisdictional boundaries. Use the original dates and reasonable
evidence about timing, common causes and affected scope. Preserve
the published boundary and explain any change. Limit records to
what is needed for verification, with protected testimony and
safe publication. Keep proportionate inquiry where no material
cross-boundary effect is indicated.

**PCO18 — Treat an affected comparison as exposed to the reform.**
If a material dependency can change outcomes in the comparison
population, treat its use as an unchanged comparator as unresolved
until independently qualified. If the effect is established, carry
that population's result as an exposed outcome. A before-and-after
change or simple relative difference may still describe the record.
It cannot be presented as the effect relative to unchanged conditions.
Seek a genuinely unaffected comparison with comparable trends and
conditions, or a design that explicitly estimates shared effects.
State the assumptions, uncertainty and limits. When the causal
comparison cannot be supported, report observed changes and credible
bounds separately. No missing observation supplies a zero or a gain.

**PCO19 — Separate transferred capacity from added provision.**
Verify which resources moved, the service or activity they left,
and what actually reached both ends by the relevant deadlines.
Where evidence supports it, report changes from improved use,
added resources and displaced provision separately. A transfer
need not be harmful. Where it exposes an existing duty, pursue
lawful urgent protection on its original grounds. Preserve each
person's missed date, access to independent review and actual
remedy. A successful intervention at the receiving end cannot
close the source case.

**PCO20 — Give the elected decision a qualified rival.** Before
an optional expansion relies on the affected comparison, show
the ordinary repair path, a narrower scope if feasible, and an
option that funds any necessary replacement at the source. Test
the later window and the effect when several places attempt the
same change. Show the people still waiting, the lawful payer
and who must accept the work. Submit a qualified serious
finding under PCO04 and use the existing LT and budget
decisions. The Assembly or other competent elected body chooses
among lawfully funded options. Evidence alone does not alter
entitlements, extend a trial or direct another body's budget.

**PCO21 — Correct downstream claims when the boundary changes.**
An independent reviewer uses original source and receiving evidence
and has a lawful way to hear affected people outside the pilot.
Give opposition and affected authorities the same timely access
to safe findings. Correct later assessments that materially rely
on the affected comparator under the applicable evidence rules.
Keep previous results, reasons, dissent and continuing obligations.
AI can trace dependencies and compare explicit assumptions. It
cannot decide whether a lost service is acceptable or certify its
own comparative result. Reopen if unobserved exposure, an accepted
replacement, a new comparator or actual receiving changes the result.
