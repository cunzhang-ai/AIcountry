# Design494 completion finding

The [first-round settlement](../../FIRST-ROUND-SETTLEMENT.md) covers
national form, democratic authority and remedies, the social and
material floor, future collaboration and the first programme. It
selects a prospective fixed-cycle repeat rule and reconciles the first
mandate and budget clocks. The [opening funding example](../../settlement/opening-funding-and-delivery/01-which-source-can-pay-for-the-first-visit.md)
tests the legal purpose of a source and each binding delivery path.
Actual territory, adoption, financing, provider acceptance and lived
outcomes remain open.
