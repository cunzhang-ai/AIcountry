## Housing is an actual bill and an ownership/credit chain

45,000 owner groups, 26,000 private-rental groups and 4,000 supported-rental groups
map to 9.750bn housing/site assets, .600bn private mortgage debt and 9.150bn equity.
They are hypothetical pre-period paid titles, not free public acquisition. The
private 156m rent book funds repairs, renewal, site dues, management, 30m mortgage
interest, 15m principal, 5.125m conditional company tax and .375m contingency.
Principal is an asset return, not income; no shareholder dividend is invented.
A separate first-year creditor sensitivity names its companion rental debtor;
actual ownership, collection, tax basis and creditor liquidity remain unknown.

The old uniform 6,000-CU bill included owner utilities but omitted tenant
utilities. Adding 30m tenant utilities and separating owner repairs, paid site
charges and qualified restricted renewal gives 480m annual bills, versus 450m.
Owner aggregate cost is unchanged, but 40,500 non-deferred and 4,500 deferred
owners have different bills. It does not add electricity twice to the grid.
At the same full wages, A personal G rises 22.669m and C1 10.164m, rather than
mechanically reimbursing the whole 30m. Excluding an **unproved** owner renewal
reserve is a disclosed alternative, not a new rule cutting existing benefits.
[I03](../../country/C05/06/01-coverage-needs-and-housing.md) now requires the actual
necessary cost and legitimate restriction, not a grant for depreciation, mortgage
principal or unrestricted owner wealth. Missing proof does not erase old claims;
pay undisputed amounts and preserve provisional hardship relief and review.

## Finance is a real institutional choice, not a disguised residual

Retain current RF tax/insurance/bounded debt and MC monetary boundaries. Also
retain distinctly authorized fiscal monetary issue as a serious structural rival.
The [100-CU paired-stock example](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-production-title-and-source/monetary-results.json)
shows a household deposit, a bank reserve and central-bank liability/negative
equity; it creates no goods or owned foreign currency by itself. Domestic nominal
payment capacity is not identical to real scarcity or external-payment capacity.
This does not prove that monetary issue is either free or inevitably inflationary.

Bank credit can create deposits ([Bank of England, 2014](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/quarterly-bulletin/2014/q1/money-creation-in-the-modern-economy));
that fact does not establish a lawful fiscal grant. A model of monetary financing
finds effects depend on circumstances and credible commitments
([Federal Reserve research, 2017](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/feds/money-financed-fiscal-programs-a-cautionary-tale.htm)).
Fiscal/monetary interdependence also requires credible limits and public trust
([BIS, 2023](https://www.bis.org/publications/aer-2023/monetary-fiscal-policy-safeguarding-stability-trust)).
These sources inform distinctions, not this country's output or inflation calibration.
New primary law and democratic control would be needed; MC19 market operations
cannot silently supply fiscal power. AI, ministers and monetary experts cannot
adopt the rival for themselves or use source tests to postpone elections/remedies.
