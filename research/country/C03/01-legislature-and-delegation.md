# C03. Democratic government and the ability to remove it: Assembly and delegated law

*Part of [C03. Democratic government and the ability to remove it](../C03-democratic-government.md); integrated design 112 research, not enacted law.*

An elected Assembly legislates and controls taxation and appropriations. Elections
use the four-year cycle and [EL’s fixed-size single national proportional-list
reference](13-equal-franchise-and-usable-elections.md), with optional person preference.
The [formation rule](06-government-formation-and-caretaker.md) first seeks a
full-Assembly majority and then tests a publicly supported minority candidate
against majority rejection. Four actual rejections or the day-30 endpoint
without lawful investiture triggers the first prior-law repeat poll. A full majority can replace the prime minister with a
successor or dismiss one into limited caretaker status. No formation failure
renews the old cabinet's ordinary policy mandate. Courts adjudicate legality
and rights. Ordinary policy remains contestable through elections and
legislation, not permanently delegated to experts.

[DR01–DR24](11-delegated-rules-and-effective-public-control.md) now specifies
national delegation: precise enabling Act, reserved primary-law choices,
effect/cumulative materiality and separate mandatory consultation. Complete
final publication L opens30calendar days of one-member call-in. Material or
called rules need floor(N/2)+1 actual yes/present on days31–45, with protected
agenda; every ordinary proposal needs lawful issuer making by day45 or lapses.
Routine uncalled detail may be made after day30 within its actual Act; silence
is no Assembly approval. Urgency needs existing exact source and earliest
source/seven-day expiry, not a new generic Cabinet power. Old duties/claims,
direct courts and paid received correction continue. [LR01–LR24](14-local-law-and-public-control.md) now gives local ordinances/
delegated criteria their own full-n approval/input/agenda/source/remedy route;
no national agency seizes local lawmaking.
The [earlier general selection](../../2026-10-delegated-rulemaking/decision-record.md)
is preserved as history, not a parallel current calendar. Neither ministerial
guidance nor an AI update acquires sovereign authority by default.

[MC07–MC08](../C06/41/02-joined-votes-careers-and-operational-exception.md)
selects a narrow primary-law own-rate/transaction route, not a DR vote on every
monetary operation; general bank rules and fiscal risk still follow their
proper DR/statute. The Assembly retains general mandate and outer risk control.

[DB ordinary budgets](../C06/60-democratic-budget-and-execution.md) proposes
a specific full-N national appropriation/supplement rule and member alternatives,
not a new rule for every primary law or DR30/45 process. Actual higher-law/holds,
local full-n powers, confidence and current software permissions remain.

[NI01–NI16 public national initiatives](16-public-national-initiatives.md)
now implements A2 ordinary public origination without an MP sponsor: actual
qualified support earns hearing/recorded disposition, not law, appropriation
or referendum. Actual primary-law/DB/DR votes, rights and dates remain.

[TR treaty scrutiny](../C10/15-democratic-treaty-commitments.md) assigns
exact material votes and usable time for opposition and independent
review. Negotiation, domestic legislation, appropriations and actual
external acts retain their distinct legal sources.

[PWR01–PWR06](01/01-review-and-renewal-of-material-powers.md) limits
new material delegations with express expiry and evidence-based Assembly
review. A fresh primary Act is needed to continue the power. Underlying
rights and standing obligations continue through the funded handover.

[PCR01–PCR08](01/02-public-commitments-before-effective-review.md) preserves useful review before scheduled
material execution. Legislative, budget and sector decisions retain their authority.
