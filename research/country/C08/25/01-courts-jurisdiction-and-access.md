# OJ01–OJ05: ordinary justice is a public service with defined jurisdiction

*Part of [C08/25](../25-ordinary-justice-and-public-prosecution.md); design120 candidate.*

**OJ01 — Two ordinary levels, separate constitutional authority.** Select public
first-instance courts for ordinary civil, family, labor, administrative and
criminal matters, and an ordinary Court of Appeal with legally specialized
formations. Enacted territory/access and workload rules set court sites/posts;
no mayor, minister, provider or AI creates jurisdiction. Civil/public protection,
P criminal proof and sector-specific competent routes remain distinct. CJ keeps
its exact national constitutional competence and CJ12 own-court exception.
There is no new supreme policy guardian or prosecutor's general veto over courts.

**OJ02 — Lawful formations, actual people.** The prospective ordinary default is
one qualified trial judge and three different qualified appellate judges, with
two affirmative votes of the full three for an appellate disposition. Express
stronger/different P/G/sector or transitional source-law formations prevail.
OJ16–OJ18 supplies the distinct three/five judicial-status formations; CJ12's
seven/five-present/four-vote exception remains unchanged. No acting title, empty
seat, recusal or replicated agent lowers a fixed formation. Assignment cannot
create extra qualified people; lack of a lawful formation is a disclosed failure,
not approval, guilt or an automatic extension of detention. Professional judging
is selected here; lay juries/assessors remain a substantive rival, not compulsory
unpaid public service or a proof of inferior justice.

**OJ03 — Accessible ordinary appeal.** Civil/administrative appeal lies to a
different competent three-person formation within 30 working days of usable
notice, with extension for inaccessible records/counsel, defective notice or
serious unfairness; preserve longer/stronger actual source terms and pending
cases. P15 retains its exact criminal appeal and innocence/acquittal rules.
Urgent relief never waits for this window. The appeal can correct facts/law and
order a fair rehearing where needed, not invent sovereign tax or constitutional
jurisdiction. CJ referral is not an automatic stay; particular protection continues.

**OJ04 — No appointment before legal power and competence.** Judges are actual
consenting adults appointed to an enacted funded court/grade, not hired for a
named dispute. First-instance applicants need eight years' relevant legal work
and demonstrated fair-process/evidential competence; appellate applicants need
12 years and demonstrated complex appellate/equivalent legal work. Prior judging
is not the only qualifying path. Actual defense, practice, teaching, legislative
or customary work may qualify on relevant merits, with paid bridging/training.
No single guild, wealth, party agreement, diagnosis or AI score owns eligibility.
Lawful noncitizens may qualify; actual work permission, language/interpretation,
security/privacy, source-law compatibility and paid availability remain necessary.
These years are authored hypotheses, not scientific measures of merit.

**OJ05 — Distinct counsel and public powers.** Independent defense/aid, victim
support, civil enforcement and prosecution remain different mandates. Criminal
prosecution does not control court filing, aid or a person's ordinary civil
claim; State civil counsel follows lawful client/C04 duties without a criminal
Director's general superintendence of private life, civil courts or public truth.
P06/P08/P11, B intake/execution and direct sector routes continue. A complaint
needs assistance, not a technical dossier, a prosecutor's permission or a platform
identity. Informal voluntary resolution cannot waive nonwaivable rights by default.

**Design147 civil-health source.** [HP07–HP12](../../C10/11/02-separation-courts-and-bodily-choice.md)
expressly assigns ordinary person-specific health liberty/hearing, original24-hour
release and7-day orders, with OJ's actual three-judge/two-assent appellate formation
for any effect beyond28elapsed days. No new court/judge, lowered vote or30/180-day
constitutional target supplies a continuation. Actual assignment/recusal, paid
counsel/expertise, original evidence and received freedom remain required.

[DF09](../../C10/12/02-political-neutrality-money-and-ordinary-justice.md) now
selects ordinary criminal jurisdiction for actual service offenses as well as
civil/rights matters; professional discipline cannot confine or replace a
criminal trial. Actual adopting law must resolve incompatible old forums and
pay pending-case/counsel/record successors; no jurisdiction changes by indexing.

[SR23–SR27](../30/06-conflicting-public-orders.md) proposes express
OJ02 appellate competence limited to the overlap between enforceable
ordinary public orders. It preserves original cases, deadlines, votes,
ordinary appeal and all specially assigned powers.
