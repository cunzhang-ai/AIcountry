# Candidate Act E09–E12: urgent health and paid care

*Design 57 proposed clauses; [Act entrance](../19-continuity-law-and-delivery-package.md).*

**E09 — Safety time governs actual care.** A credible threat from delayed
essential treatment or a missed essential support task shall receive
immediate routing to a qualified clinical or care receiver. Acceptance or
a recorded escalation shall occur within two elapsed hours of E02 receipt,
and sooner whenever delay could cause serious harm. That outer limit is
not a clinical waiting allowance. A qualified practitioner shall identify
the latest safe time, lawful interim measures, accepting team, transport
and supplies for the specific need. The state shall record any actual
failure to meet that time even if administrative routing met its clock.

**E10 — Paid-care interruption.** Where failure of a paid carer, provider
or safe household arrangement threatens essential personal support, the
local care authority shall name an actually accepting worker or safe place
and an interim arrangement within six elapsed hours of receipt, sooner
where the latest safe time requires it. Include travel, rest, supervision,
equipment and next shift. A relative shall not become a compulsory worker,
guardian or confidential contact. Chosen support, informed consent, home
and community options and independent safeguarding continue. An unusable
voucher or unaccepted referral shall not discharge the duty.

**E11 — Scarcity and separate powers.** Qualified professionals shall apply
published clinical/safety criteria and the Assembly's resource-specific
scarcity law. They shall record the available resource, relevant urgency,
alternatives, actual allocation, displaced duty and remaining unmet need.
Receipt date preserves a claim but shall not override lawful clinical
urgency or become a universal first-come priority. Need, diagnosis or an
AI risk score shall not itself authorize force, confinement or treatment.
Rescue, ordinary medical emergency consent, child protection, population
restriction and criminal powers retain their separate grounds and limits.
The Act grants no general requisition, private-worker conscription or power
to transfer resources from a nonconsenting locality outside prior law.

**E12 — Accepting fallback and independent correction.** The public clinical
network or local care residual remains responsible after contractor failure.
A fallback shall identify qualified available shifts, usable site, medicine,
power, transport, records and lawful payer, and be tested against simultaneous
ordinary duties. An alternate sharing a failed key, supplier, supervisor
or unavailable worker shall disclose that exposure. Where no lawful safe
receiver exists, continue available protection, record the unfulfilled
duty and seek prompt independent correction and an elected resource decision;
do not close the claim as transferred or discharge a person into a
nonexistent service. Clinicians determine individual safety, courts legality
and remedies, and elected bodies general standards and new material burdens.

These clauses apply within existing essential coverage and E01 boundaries.
They do not promise an unbuilt facility or let a care director suspend a
clinical consent rule. Safety intervals and outer routing clocks need
professional and affected-person assessment before authorized operation.

[CP05–CP08](../28-national-commitments-and-residual-scarcity.md) now supplies
the proposed residual public-stock scope/priority, now with [CP13–CP16](../28/07-committed-logistics-and-continuing-claims.md)'s
strict scheduled public-logistics slice, unchanged24/72-hour expiry and direct
remedy missing from the earlier E11 reference. It applies only after competent
adoption; actual clinical triage, source-law rights, consent and separate powers
remain. A protected resource conflict outside its pool has no invented fallback.

[AS13–AS19](../../C07/18-person-directed-assistance.md) now applies these earlier
receiving clocks to assistance changes/absence. Actual willing qualified backup,
worker pay/rest, recipient consent and private/public dues remain distinct; a
route change, withheld payroll or nominal reserve cannot reset useful-time duties.
Its new source/pay exposure needs an express coordinated law, not E power by analogy.

[PS21–PS32](../37/06-critical-public-power-and-actual-priority.md) now proposes
E11's missing prior allocation only for scheduled public backup power. Actual
useful-time/body/source/record tradeoffs, separate clinical/UA10 permission and
ER/RX/RR protections remain; the rule proves no accepted supply or fulfilled care.
