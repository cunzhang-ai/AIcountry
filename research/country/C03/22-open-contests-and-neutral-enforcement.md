# C03. Open contests and neutral enforcement

*Design229 · PX01–PX10 · Proposed constitutional floor and ordinary implementation.*

**Protect the public's ability to choose, organize and challenge government
while enforcing the same law against every person.** A state action can
deny practical competition without removing anyone from the ballot. Review
material effects across agencies before a real opportunity is lost. Keep
lawful investigation, victim protection and genuine urgent safety available.
Nomination or office supplies no personal immunity.
