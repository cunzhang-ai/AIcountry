# OJ06–OJ10: a selected appointer, not an unspecified independent council

*Part of [C08/25](../25-ordinary-justice-and-public-prosecution.md); design120 candidate.*

**OJ06 — A distinct ordinary selection body.** Express coordinated law creates
a twelve-person ordinary judicial appointment formation with six judges,
three practitioners and three paid lay electors. Its eligible professional
rolls and open direct entry follow CJ06's substantive rules. Separate
elections and a separate lay draw select its members and term-matched
alternates. CJ06 retains its own nine people and exact votes for its
constitutional and authorized electoral work. No principal or alternate
may serve in both formations at once. Implement separate usable receipt,
funded sitting and protected review under [OJ55–OJ59](12-appointment-independence-and-concurrent-vacancies.md).

**OJ07 — Exact seats and votes.** Use CJ06's published term assignments,
open professional ballots, candidate capacity, tie draws and same-category
alternates, with six judicial seats and three places in each other category.
Ordinary selector terms are six years, nonrenewable, with two judges, one
practitioner and one lay member renewing every two years. Set the ordinary
cycle apart from scheduled CJ06 cohort turnover under the commencing law.
Inaugural term slots are drawn before nominations and establish the ordinary
cycle. When both bodies start together with CJ06's two/four/six-year slots,
the ordinary inaugural slots are one/three/five years in each renewal group.
Replacements fill the original remainder. Service across the two
formations remains one personal nonrenewable selector tenure. A move cannot
extend it. An ordinary appointment
or promotion needs eight affirmative votes of the fixed twelve, including at
least one from each category, with at least eight lawful members present.
Conflicts use preselected eligible same-source alternates. Unavailable members
leave vacancies with the same denominator and no executive substitute.

**OJ08 — Open criteria and actual choice.** The body publishes each funded post,
OJ04 criteria, sources/conflicts, application and reasons; keep applications
open at least 30 calendar days. Any qualified person
may apply directly, without a minister/association nomination. Registry staff
preserve every actual application and assist correction, not an exclusive AI
shortlist. Qualification exclusions get source/evidence and B16 reply/direct
review; a 21-day appointment notice never shortens its ordinary 20-working-day
adverse-reply protection. Complete material ordinary qualification reply/
disposition before the final vote; changed candidate/grounds require fresh
necessary reasons, vote and notice, never transferred old approvals.
Publish proposed appointment/reasons at least 21 calendar days before
entry; B courts can correct criteria/procedure, never choose their favorite judge.
Each candidate must independently meet OJ07. If several do for one post, a further
single-choice round requires that same threshold; no plurality appointment.
Cabinet has no confirmation veto. No age stereotype, reversal/conviction quota,
patron contact or agreement with a selector is merit. Lawful criticism stays lawful.

**OJ09 — Vacancy, challenge and existing judges.** Advertise planned vacancies
180 days before expiry/retirement, unexpected vacancies within 14 days; repeated
rounds keep actual notice, conflicts and votes. A failed selection creates no
holdover, new acting judge or power to recruit a case-targeted substitute.
Appointment challenges normally within 30 calendar days of usable publication go to OJ16's competent
status route; later concealed fraud/material conflict remains hearable. A challenge
is no automatic stay. Invalid appointment does not automatically void every old
judgment: court states material-bias/process correction, criminal finality and
protected third-party rights. Existing lawful appointments are not reselected
merely because this candidate changes future selection.

**OJ10 — Explicit inaugural route, no self-bootstrap.** Retrofit uses the actual
lawful judiciary/CJ06 sources under competent predecessor/founding law. For a
joint founding process where no domestic judicial electorate exists, the founding
Act must expressly authorize a one-time judicial-category roll/candidacy exception:
consenting current or recently retired judges (within five years) of specifically
recognized source courts, with public equivalent competence, actual service/
conflict proof and independent challenge. The legitimate founding law fixes the
recognized courts and roll criteria before invitations; founders cannot handpick
three friends and label them peers. Eligible peers elect judicial-category places
through the separate formation ballots. Actual practitioner/lay sources and
fixed votes remain. Normal domestic lawful judicial rolls govern later elections;
inaugural incumbents retain only their assigned original term. This is an express
OJ/CJ06 founding exception requiring lawful constitutional adoption, not an
interpretation already in CJ06. No qualified consenting pool or legitimate prior
review means no staffed launch, not invented consent, temporary AI judges or
approval by the first appointees. Prior-law Assembly Clerk recruitment staff receive initial
applications/source records until the lawful Court Service Registrar accepts
exact custody and protected originals; this supplies no appointment power.
Existing competent forums/rights continue.
