## Situation, values and competing explanations

C05 proposes a plural economy and practical exit, C03 protects opposition
from economic retaliation, C04 examines procurement dependence, C06 protects
payment access, C08 names consumer rights, and C09 permits safety recalls.
But the current design does not assign the ordinary cross-market case,
product-harm and remedy chain. A resident can formally have several sellers
while one controller owns their jobs, housing, payment channel and complaint
path. Alternatively, low choice could come from geography, scarce skills,
regulatory entry barriers or an efficient network rather than unlawful
conduct. A safety injury can come from a product defect, bad maintenance,
missing warning or a public operator's misuse. Those diagnoses require
different evidence and remedies.

The value conflict is real: scale and innovation may improve service, while
concentrated exit costs and political dependence can make legal freedom
unusable. Protecting safety may require rapid removal, while wrongful recall
can injure a firm and its workers. A price index cannot decide those values.
No market data, hazard observation or affected-party participation is
available here.

The [OECD competition-assessment work](https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/sub-issues/competitive-and-fair-markets/competition-assessment.html)
shows that public rules themselves can restrict entry. Its [competition and
democracy paper](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/the-interaction-between-competition-and-democracy_8b3a575f-en.html)
identifies a possible conversion of economic into political power but says
the link is complex. The [UN consumer guidelines](https://unctad.org/publication/united-nations-guidelines-consumer-protection)
identify legislation, enforcement and redress functions, and the [UN product
safety principles](https://unctad.org/topic/competition-and-consumer-protection/united-nations-principles-consumer-product-safety)
cover online and offline goods. These are official comparative inputs, not
AIcountry observations or proof that one regulator model is superior.

## Coherent institutional alternatives

| Package | Potential gain | Material failure or value conflict |
|---|---|---|
| Retain broad rights, private suits and scattered sector references | Small new administrative burden and case-specific courts | Poor people and small firms may lack evidence, time or money; common control across sectors and diffuse product harms can remain invisible |
| Create one central economic authority for competition, product safety, consumer claims and public procurement | A single intake and possibly faster cross-sector evidence | One office could choose favored firms, control speech gateways, judge its own safety orders and become hard to replace |
| Put most essential gateways under direct public ownership | Clear service duty and some relief from private choke points | Incumbents can control jobs, contracts and civic access through the state; capacity, finance and lawful transfer are not supplied by ownership |
| **Selected: statutory competition cases, sector safety duties and an accessible common consumer floor with independent courts and funded exits** | Names cross-market dependence, products and remedies while keeping distinct legal powers | Multiple bodies can delay action and share one controller; actual exit and inspection capacity may be expensive or unavailable |

The selected [C05 market-power route](../country/C05/05-market-power-and-real-exit.md)
asks whether exit is usable across work, housing, payments and civic gateways,
not merely whether several firms appear on a list. The [C08 consumer and
product route](../country/C08/05-consumer-and-product-safety.md) sets a
common floor, sector inspection, traceable recall and legal redress. C03
retains political-opposition protection, C04 procurement, C06 finance and
payments, C09 physical networks, C10 customs, and C11 evidence and digital
rights. No new superagency is assumed, and no product-safety notice grants
political censorship or a general market veto.

## Power, resources, obligations and dissent

The Assembly chooses general prohibitions, jurisdiction, investigation
powers, safety standards, merger-review scope, appropriations and remedies
prospectively. The markets authority's case and decision functions have
plural public appointment, bounded continuing funding, reviewable removal,
source access and independent judicial correction. Inspectors of unsafe
goods need independent technical evidence and prompt review, including when
the supplier is a public enterprise or official favorite. Trace appointment,
funding, vendor, laboratory, data, court and order-execution controllers
jointly. Formal separation cannot rescue a review chain controlled by one
platform, ministry or donor. Opposition, workers, consumers and small firms
need safe evidence routes; defendants need notice, defense and lawful appeal.
A regulator cannot legislate tax, dictate a named loan, choose party speech
or use an interim order to take over an essential service indefinitely.

C06 counts investigators, laboratories, judges, claimant assistance,
replacement products, switching infrastructure, transition staff and lost
service. C14's opening inventory needs existing mergers, licenses,
contracts, product liability, claims, records and court orders; a new regime
cannot erase valid rights or retroactively punish lawful conduct. C13's
[dominant-gateway failure](../country/C13/05-market-exit-and-product-failure.md)
asks whether a recall, structural remedy and alternate service can actually
operate during a common-controller failure. Harms abroad and imported goods
use C10's jurisdiction and treaty routes, not an imagined power over foreign
factories. A past violation may need restitution, but a fine is not an
appropriation until collected.

The public-ownership rival has a strong objection: fragmented competition
can leave an essential bottleneck intact for years. The selected design
answers with fair-access duties, funded public options and structural
remedies where feasible, yet that answer fails if the state cannot operate
a replacement. The litigation-only rival warns that regulators can be
captured or overreach; independent courts and reasons limit that danger but
cannot supply universal legal aid. Small sellers may be crushed by generic
compliance mandates; duties must follow actual control and sector hazard,
with proportional administration, while serious safety floors remain.
This AI record cannot dispose of those value conflicts for affected people.

## Result and bounded continuation

Evidence qualification: the official sources identify competition,
consumer and product-safety functions; the institutional gap is an internal
current-design audit. Research selection: layered market-power and
consumer-safety routes. Legal authorization: none. Operational readiness:
no actual firms, investigators, testing sites, courts, budgets or receiver.
Observed outcomes: none, including no evidence of superiority over existing
countries. Current law, pending rights and software permissions remain as
they are until validly changed.

In an actual jurisdiction, a competent public sponsor should select one
essential gateway and one product class with real original observations.
Map ownership and dependence across jobs, housing, payments and politics;
compare lawful entry, public operation and structural relief; test one
unsafe-product trace from warning to physical removal and claimant remedy.
Include affected workers, buyers, firms and nonusers, defense rights, shared
controllers, transition costs and a court able to execute the result. The
sponsor, lawful procedure and resource ceiling remain unassigned. Reopen
this choice if tested alternative institutions deliver greater real exit,
safety and democratic control, or if the selected panels cannot be kept
independent and effective. A retain/no-change outcome remains possible.
