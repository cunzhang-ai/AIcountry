# Two waste streams and a closed receiving plant

*Design394 · Constructed comparison using abstract mass and cash units.*

A district collects four units of food waste and six units of dry
recyclables by its first collection deadline. Some flats cannot store
food waste safely through a missed round. The primary food facility
cannot receive either of the next two rounds. Dry sorting remains
available on a separate contract, paid from its ordinary budget.

An independent food route can receive three units for treatment in
window 1. It can also take one unit into a separately permitted
containment site with a fixed removal deadline in window 2.
The four-unit first window costs six settled units. The containment
unit has been collected and is held safely, but treatment remains due.
A four-unit restricted closure provision sits with an independent
custodian and cannot pay for this alternate route.

| Route | Result before the next deadline |
|---|---|
| Send loads to the nominal primary destination | Zero confirmed food treatment receipts. Collection alone cannot certify safe custody or discharge the remaining duty. |
| Fund the independent route for window 1 | Three units accepted for treatment and one under temporary lawful containment. The earlier household accumulation is avoided on the stated facts. |
| Rely on the same alternate in window 2 | Four new units and one carried unit need treatment. Only four treatment slots and four settled units for four places are available. One unit still lacks both funded and permitted capacity. |
| Buy an additional timely slot | All five can be accepted only if separate lawful funding, a qualified operator and actual receiving are confirmed before the deadline. Late payment or a higher nominal total does not achieve this. |

For the dry stream, six units arrive at sorting in window 1. By the
reporting date, three units have independently verified material
recovery, one rejected unit reaches lawful disposal, and two are
still held in recorded stock. The dry mass balance closes at six
units, but only three count as verified material recovery. If the
two stored units lack verified custody, report an unresolved
difference of two while retaining the three units of proved recovery.
Do not add the six sorting receipts to the final outcomes.

The case selects a paid independent first-window route with a
separate earlier search for one more lawful second-window slot.
It does not infer priority among loads, certify a site or estimate
actual costs. If the shortfall persists, the Steward retains the
duty and the designated national sector owner must address any
unresolved essential need within lawful authority and funded capacity.
