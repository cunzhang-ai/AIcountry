# FP13–FP16: source qualification and a finite next whole-country comparison

*Part of [C06/56](../56-national-fiscal-settlement-and-source-boundaries.md); design 138 candidate.*

**FP13 — Keep support scopes explicit.** The
[fiscal disposition](../../../settlement/national-fiscal/README.md) classifies
all 41 event types in four unchanged historical A/U/provider cases, reports
the full 2030 year separately from known-only 2031 tail events, and preserves
sixteen source interfaces whose actual full amounts remain unknown. It does
not requalify old producer/price/work/clinical assumptions or derive a national
tax requirement. Bookkeeping checks are neither independent economic review
nor qualified affected participation.

**FP14 — Show what remains unresolved instead of forcing closure.** Personal
cash needs and paid capital/worker/service duties remain; SC's clinical quote
adequacy is retired and other inputs are unmatched. Company/net-VAT/transfer/
capital-return bases and dates are incomplete. Supplier prepayments lack a
complete expense/capital/inventory split. Private labor/income/care, ecological
inputs, actual FX and complete debt/pension tails remain unqualified. Do not
collapse any of these to zero, positive income or guaranteed future borrowing.

ABS's original cash/consolidation definitions inform distinctions, not this
country's policy or a certified GFS account. The diagnostic account sets have
no inferred statistical institutional membership, rerouted social transactions
or authority to use bound assets. Current original-source/event checks,
limitations and failed retrievals are in the dated record. Final decisions
need fresh review, even if a supporting input has not changed.

**FP15 — Next use one published fiscal context to discriminate source menus.**
Build a bounded current-vintage/source-year comparison joining a published
whole-country tax/source composition and protected spending/claims, then map
which retain/repair and candidate changes can actually be compared. Keep public
consumption versus all expenditure, personal/company/social bases, source
ownership, actual legal/monetary context, cash/accrual and distributions distinct.
Do not divide aggregate GDP/tax by population to finance the candidate or
transplant foreign wealth, salaries, law or permissions. A real contemporary
baseline can reveal missing interfaces without serving as invented new-country
receipts. Candidate incremental costs/bases may legitimately remain unmatched.

Deliver one finite source alternatives/scale disposition with retain/repair and a
genuine whole rival, losses, old claims, capital/FX and paid transition explicit.
Reject unsupported totals or narrow claims with reasons; do not automatically
follow a local tax/product's next question. Research contributors prepare it;
actual fiscal/legal/engineering qualification and affected authority require
real resources/budget not supplied here. Urgent harm or a larger contradiction
can reprioritize. This is a next evidence task, not an adopted governing procedure.

**FP16 — Reopening is part of the design.** New original source/need/price/
tax-base/ownership/collection/receiving facts, political objections or stronger
whole alternatives can displace the current preference and diagnostic scope.
Preserve safe reasons, old editions and correction without making sensitive
testimony permanently public. Evidence qualification, research selection,
lawful authority, funded readiness and lived outcomes remain independently
reported; the latter three remain unverified. No monitoring means uncertainty,
not proof that a better country has been designed or delivered.

**Design139 follow-through:** [FP17–FP20](06-owned-principal-and-country-disposition.md)
closes FP15's bounded published-context comparison with retained architecture,
qualified source-risk/basis reasoning and no imported numerical winner. FP15
records the original task, not a command to repeat fiscal studies. Next audit
the integrated country; actual full costs/sources/authority/receiving stay unknown.
