# HL23–HL27. Protect a safe placement from a delayed or refused payment

*Design540 · Proposed housing and income law within [secure receiving](../25-housing-loss-and-secure-receiving.md).*

**HL23 — Establish what the resident would owe before an urgent placement.**
For an emergency or transitional housing placement the public housing steward
arranges under HL, state the actual charge, whoever will owe it and the dates
before the resident is asked to accept any charge-bearing instrument. Apply
the existing affordability floor using money the person can safely control
on each date, after due care, travel and essential living costs. Check the
first known change or expiry, including one ordinary bill period after an
interim arrangement. Expected allowances, unreceived wages and inaccessible
household funds supply no usable cash. The programme payer distinguishes an
existing verified award with a transfer problem from an unresolved claim.
A quick check can support a same-day safe offer. A full merits decision on
an income claim need not precede independently lawful temporary protection.

**HL24 — Assign the uncovered risk to the public arrangement.** First use a
suitable available offer affordable on confirmed resources, or correct
an established payment in time under its own law. When the steward relies
on a temporary placement that would otherwise expose a resident to an
unaffordable charge, use a directly procured place or a public payment
undertaking under a separately enacted, funded housing schedule. A qualified
provider must accept the arrangement in advance, including the exact
covered charge and the resident's independently affordable share, if any.
The public housing payer bears the covered charge directly through its
agreed date, even if the expected benefit is late or denied. The provider
cannot bill the resident, create arrears in their name or recover possession
for the public share, including when the public payer defaults. The
resident can enforce this protection directly. The provider's claim for
the public share runs against the public payer. General income support remains under the
person's control. Apply to public provision, contracted rooms and covered
private placements alike. Existing private liabilities keep their own law.

**HL25 — Fund the gross commitment and limit its scope.** The adopting Act
and appropriation identify eligible urgent placements, maximum rate, period
and aggregate concurrent exposure, the responsible housing payer and its
backstop under existing local and national finance law. Commit only the
covered shortfall necessary for safe receipt. Keep the entire guaranteed
amount available, including where the anticipated public reimbursement
never qualifies. Match payables by recipient, provider, charge and period.
Any adjustment between public programmes requires its own lawful basis and
actual settlement. A late award paid to the person follows its governing
law. It neither automatically belongs to the housing payer nor permits the
provider to collect a covered charge a second time. Preserve the resident's
ordinary cash and recovery rights. This is a finite public housing purchase
or obligation. It supplies
no general state guarantee of rent, tenancy insurance or landlord income.

**HL26 — Keep the terms of consent and safe receiving separate.** The steward
explains available destinations, charges, care, practical travel and the
date protection ends. Confirm care and transport with qualified receivers
where needed. When more than one accepted route can meet the person's
needs on equivalent terms, offer the choice. If the person asks to change
one part, retain the original compatible bookings until the lawful hold
ends or a qualified replacement is accepted, subject to lawful priority,
safety and the resident's own decision to release them. Check actual cash receipt
alongside location and arrival times. Assent to one part does not bind
the others or attach another occupant's income or care. Protect an
independently owed payment, existing right and safe
contact if someone refuses a place, data disclosure or optional service.
Give direct access to existing urgent review and funded independent
assistance. Refusal of an unsuitable or unaffordable option does not
satisfy the housing duty. If money or capacity runs out, record the unmet
need at its original time and act within existing lawful emergency and
priority rules. Deliver safe severable support. A referral or pending claim
does not transfer the uncovered cost to the resident.

**HL27 — Decide before the end of protection.** Identify the last date a
successor can be booked or the arrangement lawfully extended. Before
that date, verify an affordable next placement or terms, signed
undertaking where needed, care and actual accepted receiving. If benefit
support is still absent, compare a lower-cost appropriate placement,
an independently funded extension and correction under the ordinary
income law. A lapse ends prospective commitment authority under the
instrument. It neither makes prior covered charges the resident's debt
nor closes an unmet housing duty. Report charges, timely receipt,
affordable retention, private debt imposed and displaced housing and
care capacity. AI can compare dates, audit duplicate charges and explain
scoped terms. Qualified people establish safety, eligibility, liability
and assent. Preserve existing claims and protect private evidence.
Reopen on a repeated unfunded extension, displacement or a simpler,
equally reliable ordinary payment or direct service route.
