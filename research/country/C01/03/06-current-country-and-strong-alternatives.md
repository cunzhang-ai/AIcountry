# CS13–CS15: choose a whole country, with real competitors

*Design 141 · Part of [the country case](../03-country-case-and-competing-designs.md); research selection, not enacted law.*

**CS13 — State the present national bargain.** Retain a replaceable
parliamentary democracy; enforceable personal/collective rights; competent
ordinary justice; individual I05/SD05 security, universal essential services
and continuing Y earned rights plus168 finite EB transition; plural production and worker/user voice;
public delivery responsibility with usable plural routes; national/local
self-government; ecological/external duties; and bounded, replaceable AI.
SG prefers service-first continuing protection, not a staged promise of U.
FP retires the old national financial-reference status, not a single due duty.
AP makes ordinary receipt/decision/omission reachable without replacing special
clocks. All fourteen chapters and all 37 original functions/later duties remain.

The hypothesis is practical independence: people can sustain ordinary life,
leave private dependence, oppose rulers and obtain correction without one
employer, family, provider, official or mediator controlling every route.
Means assessment, common controllers, professional power, taxes and complexity
can defeat it. More institutions, public ownership or AI do not prove the gain.

**CS14 — Keep substantively different national alternatives.** These compare
institutional choices under the same protected rights/needs and paid continuing
claims. They are not calibrated countries, supplier offers or an equal-cost
menu. An actual inherited state may fail the rights requirement; do not credit
its repair with free democratic transition or call it already compliant.

**What is common, and what can actually differ?** Every alternative must protect
equal political standing/opposition/replacement, bodily/personal agency, civil
fairness, safe essential access and continuing actual due/accrued rights.
These fundamental commitments are not permission to copy every unadopted
candidate number into the comparison floor. Different prospective TW hours,
NC group models, AL time/source, EB/CL duration/replacement and delivery/ownership
can be genuine country choices. State exact differences and actual time, safety,
quality, independence, income, source and transition consequences; assess whether
human needs and the common protections really remain met. Unknown adequacy is
unknown, not a pass or a semantic claim that fewer services meet the same needs.

A rival cannot be dismissed merely for declining a proposed increment, nor
credited with that increment's benefit while omitting its cost. It cannot waive
existing vested/due legal claims by relabeling them proposed; actual amendments
and continuing obligations retain their own lawful safeguards. A rights-destroying
arrangement fails the common requirements; incompatible value choices need
legitimate public judgment rather than automatic equivalence or an AI score.

| Arrangement | Different diagnosis and selected organizing response | Important costs and power that stay in the comparison |
|---|---|---|
| Strong retain/repair | Preserve lawful working institutions, services and informal cooperation; correct located exclusion/capture without general replacement | Inherited harms, required democratic repair, deficient resources and old claims; no assumed zero transition |
| Current service-first plural country | Meet actual individual needs and universal service/earned duties, with public responsibility and voluntary provider/ownership choice | Means/privacy/nonclaim burden, contractor/common-node risk, public fallback and paid control; no assumed cheap targeting |
| Territorial direct-public service country | Reduce contracting/interface dependence through directly accountable public provision of the defined essential-service scope | Paid assets/workforce/management, monopoly or patronage, user/worker choice and independent review; ordinary lawful enterprise stays plural |
| Rights-preserving U+C2 country | Replace ordinary means-tested cash with an individual universal foundation while retaining essential services, additional needs and earned rights | Complete tax/source/incidence, residual assessment, prices, distribution and paid overlap; no cancelled disability/pension/service claim |
| Decentralized mutual/common provision | Give voluntary local/worker/user arrangements more practical control, with national equalization, rights and enforceable common duties | Unequal local resources, informal domination, exit, receiving, national residual workload and external obligations; no compulsory community membership |

The alternatives can combine compatible elements, but a combination must state
whose decision, title, tax base and guarantee change. Do not absorb a rival's
advantages while ignoring its conflicting authority or resources. A fully
specified rival may displace the current preference. The comparator's limited
specification is not proof of its inferiority.

**CS15 — Compare the same whole lives and continuing country.** Each option
must carry law/justice, politics, defense/external relations, infrastructure/
ecology, knowledge/culture, care/learning, ordinary private production and
future revision as well as its headline income/service bargain. Changing a
provider does not nationalize all activity or remove an old function. The
[common comparison](../../../settlement/whole-country-bargain/README.md) keeps
ordinary freedom, hostile-government correction and paid transition together.

For each claimed institutional gain, distinguish what the inherited arrangement
actually delivers, what its strongest lawful funded repair can deliver,
and what requires the exact new authority. Attribute improvements available
under both arrangements to both, using each route's actual costs and
dependencies. Test whether equivalent ordinary protection survives loss of the
provider, a weaker budget and a successor government before claiming that
single-window receipt establishes equivalent durable power. Credit a narrower
lawful lasting protection as an alternative to wider constitutional reform.
The [public-confirmation comparison](../../../settlement/public-reconsideration/01-who-can-stop-a-withdrawal.md)
applies this distinction to independent essential access and who can stop its withdrawal.

Count personal/worker time, safe proof, nonclaimants, received service quality,
ownership/control, actual source and dates, capital/renewal, private/foreign
burdens, legacy claims and simultaneous independent capacity. Missing totals
stay unknown, not zero or a residual tax/export/AI gain. A cheaper quote, fewer
desks, more uptake or an aggregate welfare gain cannot waive political standing,
bodily agency or useful-time essentials. Actual resource conflicts and value
disagreements remain visible; a score cannot settle them.

[EB01–EB16](../../C05/16-portable-earnings-and-work-transition.md) is a contestable
new finite-buffer element, not a universal social minimum every unadopted rival
must copy. Compare its actual source, privacy/history/insider and mobility effects
against I05/WL-only, employer retention and real paid public work/learning; any
actually accrued old EB obligations survive a later lawful change of preference.

[Design179 PI](../../C03/17-public-decision-influence-and-interests.md) adds
the narrow ordinary-influence/interests and proposed constitutional self-benefit/
public-interest-remedy choice within this retained bargain. It creates no
empirical or source winner over these whole rivals. The [current register](../../../DESIGN-STATUS.md)
now separates active decisions from preserved chronological support; consolidation
neither adopts a governing method nor revalidates all previous choices.

[CL care-time choice](../../C05/17-care-leave-and-return.md) now specifies the
previously deferred arrival/temporary-care leave, portable income and return
promise. Its numerical schedule remains a contestable candidate, with stronger
public care/less leave and actual retain/repair rivals; it is not a universal
numerical condition every unadopted country must copy.

[Design188 whole-demand/work comparison](../../C06/58/17-whole-country-demand-and-work.md)
now supplies a matched industry-dependency probe alongside the retained whole
rivals. Equal price-valued demand and aggregate employee balance can still
leave industry needs unmatched; model identity is no source or welfare proof.
Needs-protecting production/time rebalancing retains private agency and public
choice, not uniform household/export cuts or automatic numerical TW retreat.

[PCO01–PCO05](../../C14/04/03-comparable-outcomes-and-hidden-regression.md)
makes each comparative outcome claim explicit about scope, observed
composition, missing people and causal limits. No rival is defeated
by one favorable national average or an unresolved protection duty.

[Concurrent first-programme losses](../../C14/06/07-first-programme-under-concurrent-losses.md)
compares exact starts and retain-and-repair under the same continuing
water, care and housing duties. Use its amounts only in the constructed
context. Wider ownership and income alternatives need matched evidence.

[The observed Copenhagen budget comparison](../../C14/06/08-first-programme-with-observed-budgets.md)
uses a real retain-and-repair comparator. Its approved purposes and
continuity plan give a starting point for a matched reserve offer.
An actual launch still needs verified receipt and source on both dates.

[The dated cash comparison](../../C05/15/04-cash-alternatives-and-actual-receipt.md) tests
repaired ordinary support and the universal base on common households,
with continuing services and independent cash receipt.

[PCO22–PCO26](../../C14/04/07-better-lives-and-country-choices.md) tests the original population,
changed outcomes and exact rival uses of scarce paid work across renewal.

[Observed distribution and budget choice](../../../settlement/comparative-country-validation/01-observed-gaps-and-first-budget.md) lets ordinary repair, independent delivery and direct provision displace the working choice when real scale, actual receipt and sources justify it.
