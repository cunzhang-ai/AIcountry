# Design327 decision on tax preferences and subsidy renewal

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design326, TN, DB, public revenue, AT and the working framework.
Current budgeting includes tax expenditures and current purchasing
requires enforceable results. Select purpose-based combined scrutiny
and express expiry for new targeted discretionary business support.
A separate elected renewal weighs additional effects, alternatives
and usable fiscal exposure. The project assistant authors the proposal.
Actual lawmakers retain competence to adopt, amend or fund a successor.

## Alternatives and evidence

Compare current reporting and review, mandatory expiry for targeted
support, and replacing targeted tax preferences with appropriated
spending by default. Reporting preserves predictability but a measure
may continue despite weak effects. A default spending route makes cash
more visible but can sacrifice a useful tax-administered instrument.
Select expiry and a cross-instrument comparison, with explicit prior
eligibility, lawful continuity for valid claims and a four-year ceiling
for new schemes. The duration is a normative selection. Delays can
reduce take-up or deter useful long-horizon investments. Compare
bounded cohorts and lawful stable terms rather than stripping reliance.

The [primary sources](sources.md) show reporting and review practices
and methods for evaluating what a subsidy adds. The constructed case
keeps assumed effects separate from observed evidence.

## Authority, migration and reopening

The adopting body states the tax baseline, qualifying acts, exposure,
scope and date. The tax or award authority administers legal claims.
Independent fiscal and sector reviewers check sources and performance.
The elected body decides any successor. Keep existing rights and
unmodified tax years under their own law. Reconcile valid claims,
carryforwards, refunds and guarantees at expiry. No new award or recovery
comes from a prediction, an evaluator's report or a review delay.

Compare one tax preference and one direct programme for the same goal
in a real jurisdiction. Obtain original tax and payroll records under
protected access, an independent comparison of additional outcomes,
and affected worker and small-provider participation. Price review,
exit, and alternative financing without assuming the repeal estimate.
Reopen on capture, evasive relabeling, delayed claims, lost access,
systematic displacement or a better instrument. Actual adoption,
qualified fiscal exposure, collected money and outcomes remain open.
