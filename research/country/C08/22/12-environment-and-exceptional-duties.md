# TD11–TD12: cumulative exposure, source custody and exceptional activities

*Application of TS01–TS16; [technical safety](../22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md).*

| ID / competent role | Defined entry and substantive duty | Boundary and missing prerequisites |
|---|---|---|
| TD11 / environmental, water/extraction and waste roles within their actual jurisdictions | Commercial extraction from a public/shared water source, industrial environmental discharge, hazardous-waste treatment/disposal and irreversible conversion of legally protected habitat require the actual site/activity permit before commencement. Under the coordinated NR Act, new commercial mineral extraction, materially disturbing exploration and new/enlarged mine phases also need their qualified independent site/activity permission and NR10 closure security regardless of public/private/collective mineral title; actual prior grants, minor-use/small-operator definitions and paid transition retain NR08/NR15. A public concession is not an operating permit. Under the coordinated WS Act, genuinely new/enlarged commercial or material noncommercial self-supply surface or groundwater abstraction connected to a shared basin/aquifer needs WS permission regardless of public/private/collective land, source or operator title within actual national jurisdiction; existing rights, required consent, WS exact source-specific material/minor/collective scope and paid lawful transition remain. A connected-source permit transfers no title or foreign authority. Meet enacted cumulative extraction/emission/habitat conditions, representative monitoring, lawful warning and secured restoration/closure duties. | Pollutants, quantities, ecological floors, exemptions for minor domestic uses and transboundary reach need exact prior law and actual place evidence. A carbon credit, fee or private offset cannot cancel a distinct water/toxicity/habitat duty. |
| TD12 / civilian radiation-safety role; Assembly for specified exceptional new activities | Manufacture/commercial supply or use of ionizing-radiation generators or radioactive sources above enacted exemption limits requires authorization, justified purpose, optimized protection, qualified operation, source custody, monitoring and lawful return/disposal. National nuclear-fission operation/fuel-cycle facilities, deliberate open environmental release of self-propagating engineered organisms and spacecraft launch/reentry require a particular prior Act and qualified legal/operational regime before new authorization. | Exemption/dose/source limits, medical/research exposure distinctions and transport need exact instruments. A smaller operator label or distributed AI cannot avoid actual source aggregation. This row supplies no nuclear/biological/space engineering instructions, military authority, territory claim or foreign consent. |

TD11 protects actual downstream/neighbor/future interests, hearing and legal
standing under C09. Cumulative small permissions share a declared physical
budget; duplicate paper entitlements cannot turn the same water, habitat or
restoration stock into multiple available resources. Existing lawful rights,
private claims and nonwaivable actual duties need TS15 reconciliation. No
technical permit chooses an unfunded national scarcity priority or expropriates
land; C03/C04/C06 retain those decisions and remedies.

TD12 distinguishes a justified practice, a design constraint, an enforceable
limit, a monitoring trigger and an emergency reference level. Do not copy one
public/worker dose limit to patients, research volunteers, emergencies and
natural background indiscriminately, or call a planning target breach a proved
penal violation. Actual science, law, purpose, scope and qualified review are
needed. No numerical radiation annex is claimed complete here.

Clinical imaging/treatment and medical devices retain TD09/TD10 and patient
consent; technical approval does not authorize forced screening, immigration
age testing, experiment or a criminal search. Direct liberty, bodily and privacy
rights remain under B/P/RP, with the actual warrant/consent route. Nonmedical
ionizing screening of people requires its own express legitimate statutory
purpose and necessity, with safer alternatives and independent review; a machine
purchase or security preference alone creates no such authority.

The three exceptional activity families are not a ban on all research, ordinary
closed laboratory work, astronomy or new technology. The particular Act must
decide public purpose, actual international obligations/jurisdiction, accountable
operator, safety evidence, independent sources/review, affected participation,
liability, capacity, long-term duties and lawful cessation. No open-ended cabinet
exemption, lab label or insurer substitutes for that decision. An actual existing
lawful operation maps its continuing obligations under TS15 rather than being
automatically stopped or reapproved by this proposed rule.

Unknown future activity needs a reasoned ordinary-law classification and a
competent decision on material new risk, not a silent analogy to these three
families. This schedule is bounded technical law, not a complete current treaty
implementation or a grant to conduct any weapons activity.

[NR01–NR16](../../C09/15-finite-resources-and-mineral-rights.md) assigns finite
mineral title/owner award separately from this title-neutral operating remit.
The Act must designate actually qualified mine/environment/safety roles and
prerequisites; a water official's title alone licenses no mine. Existing TD12
exceptional, TS source/record/safety, ordinary permits and stronger duties remain;
no new implicit arrest/crime, public taking or authority by an unspecified annex.

[GA's separate prior-law proposal](../../C11/10-general-ai-capability-and-public-control.md)
now addresses severe-risk general-AI exposure explicitly. It does not add AI to
TD12's nuclear/organism/space list or waive any particular prior Act/sector duty.
Only valid actual enactment can create its new subject and civil powers.

[AQ01–AQ10](../../C09/24-clean-air-and-cumulative-exposure.md) specifies
binding ambient, cumulative approval and funded correction duties under
prospective air law. An ambient breach does not by itself establish a
source violation or individual fault.
