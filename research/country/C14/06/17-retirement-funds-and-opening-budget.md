# T73–T78. Protect pension payments at the opening of government

13 November 2026 · Integrated design 438 · Candidate first-budget rules

**T73 — Assign the right to its legal payer.** At opening, list the
tax-financed basic pension, earned and survivor insurance, public
employer pensions, occupational and private promises, cross-border
claims and local care under their respective law. Name the obligor,
administrator, payer, receiving account and ultimate source.
Income statistics and institutional sector accounts remain useful
comparisons. Neither assigns a beneficiary's legal claim or cash.
A common administrator cannot pool restricted funds.

**T74 — Separate benefit funding, collection and administration.**
Match contributions, legally voted backing, employer and local
obligations, investment cash and administration by purpose and date.
Do not count a public reimbursement as fresh national resources
when consolidating within the relevant perimeter. Keep both sides
for cash. Separate insurance assets and collateral from general
cash and administration funds. Track the underlying pension and
any care-credit transfer once. Reconcile cash receipts and accrual
accounts without treating their difference as an appropriation.

**T75 — Carry due payments and restricted reserves into the dated
budget.** On the first-90-day calendar and the second-year tail,
show each fund's owned available cash, binding payments, material
backing, known delays, custody, and the next payment window.
Retain the earned scheme's existing proposed post-payment
protection under [RF08](../../C06/36/02-pension-units-cash-and-generations.md).
Separate the shortfall in actually payable benefits from any
remaining gap in reserve restoration. Keep long-term liability
and adequacy assessments on their own horizon. A late contribution
or a buffer target cannot lawfully move a due benefit.

**T76 — Qualify a bridge before the pension date.** Compare paid
ordinary financing, lawfully retimed backing, funded standby and
a lawful independently executable alternate. Reserve the
specified amount, expiry, settlement method and replenishment in
advance. Where liquidity fails, protect payments under existing
rights and use only a separately authorized purpose-eligible
source. Expose any remaining shortfall in a dated elected decision.
Avoid substituting local care money or another scheme's assets.
Use existing correction and recovery for failed or duplicate
payments. Do not treat a reserve draw as new recurring revenue.

**T77 — Verify receipt and preserve review.** Keep original
entitlement dates and review clocks. Identify which pensioners
and survivors received payment in usable form and which remain
unreached, including assisted and cross-border cases. Independently
verify the original receipt before counting a replacement. Record
funding defects separately from eligibility disputes. AI can
identify duplicate flows and mismatched dates. It cannot adjudicate
rights, redirect reserves or declare settlement.

**T78 — Carry existing rights through any institutional change.**
Retain old earned units, survivor rights, accrued amounts,
foreign claims, restricted funds and pending appeals through
paid succession. Allocate contributions and public backing once
under the governing law. Test fund cash, care services and
independent remedies together before optional commencement.
Reopen on repeated bridge use, missing recipients, double-counted
flows or stronger funding or delivery alternatives.
