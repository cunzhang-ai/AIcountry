probe fixed parliamentary authorization/actual source capacity, unresolved lot,
declared origin families/unknown dependencies, actual cash-owner separation,
nonfungible language/legal shifts and original Gregorian/UTC clocks.

A model returns no truth, genuine opposition, lawful target, public authority,
qualified original evidence, permission, funded source or received remedy.
All actual qualifications remain null. Distinct declared family identifiers are
not proof of independence; several AI summaries can still have one originating
claim. Unknown source/skill/cash is not silently zero. Unfamiliar semantics require
new scoped reasoning, not silent reuse. The model is not a legal/intelligence engine.

[Dependencies](../commitments/18-civilian-assessment-and-control.md) keep ordinary
care/election/cyber/justice and source/FX claims alongside the new workload. The
no-unit/external/noncriminal-warrant alternatives, concealed-threat warning loss,
shared-controller capture and source/correction limitations stay in the
[decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-civilian-intelligence-and-democratic-control/decision-record.md)
and [fresh text review](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-civilian-intelligence-and-democratic-control/received-review.json).
No old validator, green metadata or recorded warning proves a complete or superior
country; actual context/public/qualified comparisons and delivered outcomes remain open.
