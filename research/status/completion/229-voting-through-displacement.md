# Voting through displacement

Design321 · Scoped result for [country completion](../../DESIGN-COMPLETENESS.md)

[VP01–VP05](../../country/C03/13/07-voting-through-displacement.md)
selects prior-law host and mobile electoral receipt for residents
involuntarily away from their ordinary polls. Keep the original lawful
ballot, actual deadline, housing and rescue obligations, and independent
eligibility, custody and remedy.

The [flood exercise](../../settlement/voting-through-displacement/01-evacuation-shelter-and-vote.md)
checks combined funding, overlapping crews, transfer after timely receipt
and a failed alternate. Actual safe access, staffing, costs, authority
and independent legal execution remain open.
