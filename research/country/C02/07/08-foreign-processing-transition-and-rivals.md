# PD29–PD32: foreign reach, continuing claims and a revisable choice

*Design 96 ordinary-data research candidate; not enacted law, funded readiness or
demonstrated superiority.*

**PD29 — Determine actual reach and usable control.** Apply the substantive Act's
territorial/controller/processor scope, C08/17 forum/law/recognition and actual treaty
duties; a foreign server, domestic access request or contract label alone creates
neither foreign compulsion nor immunity. The local responsible authority/controller
remains accountable for the processing it actually determines or controls, with
attributed processor/recipient duties, purpose limits, rights access and lawful
continuity. Before consequential outsourcing/transfer, map destination,
controller/processor/recipient, accessible copies/keys, onward use, government-access
exposure, retention, meaningful subject remedies, exit and actual qualified successor.
Do not label encrypted or locally copied data safe if the same provider still holds
controlling keys or exclusive lawful access. No requirement to preapprove every ordinary
cross-border message or a presumed universal localization rule is created.

A transfer/remote-access arrangement needs an actual domestic lawful basis plus the
specific adopted cross-border safeguards/exception; a contract, copyright licence,
data-subject click or EU-style label cannot override a mandatory ban, source
confidentiality or another person's rights. No EU adequacy status, membership, foreign
recognition or shared-law jurisdiction is assumed. A compelled foreign request must
receive necessary lawful notice, independent review/challenge where realistically
available, minimum disclosure and retained safe reasons; restrict notice only on a
specific lawful necessity with independent review and expiry. If actual foreign and
domestic law cannot both be met, seek competent court/authority resolution and lawful
suspension/replacement or elected treaty/policy choice within rights; do not invent
authority to ignore either, seize a foreign server or coerce a worker to supply keys.
The supervisor may stop an identified unlawful transfer within PD23/PD24, not declare a
general national foreign-policy embargo or close the Internet. Preserve safe lawful
service and unmet claims while replacing a forbidden route; foreign reliance and actual
exit capacity may fail despite contract text. Transfer safeguards are enacted minimum
equivalent purpose/security/rights/remedy/onward limits actually enforceable against the
named recipient. Verify real foreign-law/government-access exposure; if safeguards
cannot be met use a compliant alternative or a particular lawful exception, never an
invented adequacy label. Routine optional informed transfer cannot waive another
person’s rights or restricted clinical/source duties.

**PD30 — Prospective start and continuing claims.** Inventory old public/private
records, bases, active consent, vendor/subcontractor copies, legal holds,
clinical/child/election/source duties, pending access/correction claims, transfers,
remedies and successor custodians before commencement. The candidate Act starts new
processing and the new ordinary subject-request floor 180 calendar days after actual
enactment. Existing processing has an express dated, funded migration no later than 18
calendar months after that Act, those specified migration tasks must then be completed or the incompatible
route stopped, with safe lawful receiving. For all
controllers, subject requests, lawful bases, optional-consent withdrawal, sensitive-
purpose limits, breach notice and independent remedies start on day180, subject to
stronger already-effective law. The bridge covers only identified necessary legacy
technical/storage/record changes with safe restricted use; it is not an
amnesty for unlawful old use, a new sensitive purpose, optional onward sale, denied
already-effective subject rights or avoidable serious harm. A new model/contract label
cannot reset legacy dates or enlarge old permission. New consent cannot be retroactively
invented, old lawful claims and deadlines are not paused, and an official's missing
inventory is not authority to keep a harmful use running. Rights already due under
actual source law and urgent B/P protections continue. C06 prices counsel, examiners,
interpreters, secure custody, alternative channels, supervisor/court cases, vendor
changes, deletion/continuity and foreign services without assuming fines or data sales
finance them. Actual jurisdiction, old law, treaties, contracts, staff and affected
consent are unverified. All institutional preparation, first appointment, source and
delegated case functions need a funded pre-start schedule; missing chief/staff means
disclosed non-delivery and direct courts, not authority to delay an enacted date. No
privacy text auto-extends intelligence permissions, tax/benefit deadlines or court
holds. Preserve constitutional/software/contribution holds, EV/KR
confidentiality/access, U/Y/H/TW, lawful worker/contract and foreign obligations;
adverse punitive changes remain prospective. The new ordinary floor does not silently
authorize personal processing that its actual source law forbids.

**PD31 — Paid shared capacity and observable correction.** C06 counts controller
mapping, qualified privacy/sector/cyber staff, procurement and subprocessor audit,
correction propagation, secure storage/deletion, privacy-preserving verification,
translation, assisted/offline receipt, backup, independent counsel/courts and dual
operation during transfer. Reuse a staffed task only when its actual hours/skills are
released; no AI saving or data sale funds the floor in advance. Prospective law
inventories existing files, vendors, purposes, old consent, litigation holds, contracts
and foreign duties; named successors preserve lawful claims and correction history
without bulk publishing private life. No model, account identity or statutory text
proves actual receiving capacity. Publish safe annual source-backed answered/denied
requests, corrections reaching actual decision owners, lawful erasure exceptions,
breaches/proxies, excluded nonusers, false findings and delivered remedies, with costs
and controllers. Counts measure a stated scope; they do not prove harmless processing or
better lives. Common vendor forensics is counted once, sector duties and simultaneous
skill/shift requirements remain distinct. Expected fines, data sales, AI savings or a
lower call count fund no current claim.

**PD32 — A replaceable architecture.** Select purpose-limited plural custody as the
allocation of legal responsibility, preserving consent/property-style voluntary
bargaining only for genuinely optional rights-respecting reuse. A property/consent
market can reward participation and reduce official approval but dependent people may
sell under pressure, copies/inferences affect nonconsenting others and rich buyers
accumulate predictive/civic control. Local processing/selective proof/personal mediators
can reduce disclosure but may exclude device-less users, hide issuer/vendor
concentration or destroy proof needed for earned claims. That architecture can displace
this choice if it preserves practical access, correction, authority and public control
at lower whole-life burden; it is not automatically absorbed as a free feature. A single
chief is simpler than a board, yet three-fifths deadlock and cumulative
Assembly/recruitment/Treasury/vendor/court capture remain real failures; court-only or
ministerial routes remain rivals. Actual independent review needs paid competent staff,
protected sources, usable hearings and execution, not a different institutional name.
Reopen on coerced profiling, lost care/proof, censorship, unreviewable secrecy,
inaccessible own-office remedy, false unlearning/erasure claims, unsafe transfer or a
stronger complete alternative. After bounded tests, recheck the whole country’s highest
remaining substantive gap rather than automatically extending privacy research. Actual
legal adoption, affected participation, safe operation and improved lives are separately
unproved.

**Design147 HP source.** HP04 supplies only necessary grave-hazard clinician/lab
notification to the qualified health receiver; HP22 specifies minimum case-record
retention/claims holds. PD purpose, safe original/correction access and independent
review remain; no all-patient dossier, routine employer/immigration/party/police
feed or training licence. Distinct actual clinical-record/foreign sources survive.
