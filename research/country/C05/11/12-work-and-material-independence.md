# Give workers an independently usable choice where jobs are concentrated

*Design511 · Proposed labour-market assessment and elected remedy within [competition and economic power](../11-competition-and-economic-power.md).*

**Test the power to worsen work.** For a merger or exclusion inquiry where hiring or labour intermediation is materially affected, examine the work a person with the relevant skills can actually take under its real wage, shifts, care, travel, accessibility, housing and status constraints. Trace actual control of recruiters, wage and hiring approvals, credential verification and payroll. A minority funder or shared intermediary can block a specific function even where employers remain generally independent. Distinguish an exercisable veto from an ordinary service contract with independently usable routes. Test independence again against a common buyer, production site or funding failure through the next roster. Consider work people would take with usable support, qualifications they can lawfully carry, independent job entry and voice inside the current firm. Test whether pay or conditions could be made worse without workers having a practical alternative. Record different results for shift, occupation, place and affected group using privacy-preserving evidence. Employer numbers, concentration estimates, vacancies and job advertisements are evidence to examine. They do not establish feasible offers, liability or a blanket case for separation. The existing transaction, conduct, proof, deadlines and remedy tests apply.

**Decide what to do when market structure is lawful.** A credible persistent failure of practical independence is grounds for a funded market and service assessment under ordinary public powers. It does not create a violation or a stop order. The markets authority publishes the source-qualified diagnosis. Labour and sector officers jointly identify which entry barriers and affected protections they can actually remedy. The Assembly and competent local bodies compare exact funded alternatives: ordinary labour and benefit enforcement, independent bargaining and portable qualifications, neutral access to care, travel, housing and payroll, genuinely new independent capacity, and a public or cooperative option. Use collective bargaining for conditions that people cannot individually negotiate. Use directly available protection when exit conditions would otherwise make that voice ineffective. Where ordinary repair yields real usable alternatives, prefer it to maintaining a protected new provider. Where a key constraint cannot be repaired, price a different qualified service or production route. Report any remaining dependence before relying on the national promise of choice.

**Prove the bundle for the same people and dates.** A worker can privately inquire or decline an offer without notice to the current employer unless a separate lawful act requires it. Verify actual options with safe assisted intake, voluntary acceptance and paid receiving. Need for income protection or an urgent remedy does not depend on accepting a job, leaving a home, joining a union or receiving a matching score. Protect independent representatives, members, nonmembers and lawful informal cooperation. Keep the wage, existing individual rights and the practical opportunity to remain and contest distinct from a new employment offer. No offer authorizes a transfer of records across housing, immigration, recruitment or benefit systems. Existing independent exit and sector routes retain their legal sources.

**Choose and review a specific intervention.** A publicly financed intervention states its accepted capacity, admission and exit rules, provider independence, funding, source-service effect, transition, useful-time review and next elected decision. Do not count a protected worker's current employer twice, accessible transport as care, catalogued projects as confirmed jobs, or an assumed economy-wide AI gain as public funds. An open, competitive route and a directly staffed public or worker-cooperative option need the same receiving and affordability check. In an individual or competition case use existing forums, lawful relief and actual enforcement. In a market study involving lawful power, bring structural public choices to elected decision. AI may compare job conditions, resource schedules and reported outcomes in scoped data. It cannot set worker eligibility, infer assent, decide merger legality or require relocation.
