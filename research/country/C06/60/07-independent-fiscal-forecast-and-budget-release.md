# DB21–DB25. Give the Assembly independent fiscal evidence before a commitment

*Design264 · Proposed budgeting and release duties.*

**DB21 — Start material public fiscal analysis without a minister's
request.** The Assembly's existing independent budget analysts start
a scoped review when the first annual national budget or a material
fiscal proposal is publicly announced or formally filed, whichever
happens first. Apply the same rule to Cabinet and member proposals.
Use previously enacted cumulative materiality across related tax,
spending, guarantees and contingent liabilities, or an identified
material effect on protected services, remedies or independent
democratic functions. Assess related changes together even where
announced on separate dates. The initiating office records the exact
scope or published description, applicable baseline, materiality
reasons, responsible analyst and ordinary useful date under DB02–DB03.
This preserves voluntary confidential campaign and exploratory work.
No preliminary analysis conditions proposal, formation or voting rights.

**DB22 — Give the analysts independent access and a route to publish.**
Protect the existing career analysts' funding, access to original
Treasury and service evidence, and capacity for minority and member
alternatives. Analysts issue their own evidence and revisions to the
Assembly through the Clerk without approval from the fiscal proponent,
with a safe public summary and protected independent record access.
Use PE for withheld or delayed existing records and its useful-time
review. Apply the DB02 response clock, earlier where required. Where
a full estimate cannot arrive in time, publish the affected question,
known bounds and dependencies, reason for failure, who can supply the
missing work and a realistic date. Assign backup work through prior
lawful Assembly staffing and access. Preserve lawful confidentiality
and publish an identifiable independent dissent. External audit tests
later actual performance within its own mandate. Neither an auditor
nor an analyst approves the policy or its appropriation.

**DB23 — Put versions and assumptions side by side before the ordinary vote.**
For a material exact proposal show the continuing-law baseline,
proponent estimate, independent estimate and any material unresolved
alternative on the same population, dates and accounting perimeter.
Separate enacted measures, macro assumptions and ranges, financing
authority, contingent receipts, restricted balances and usable cash,
capacity and lived effects. Trace disagreement to its source and keep
unqualified assumptions visible. Compare the source and protection
on common market, behavior and timing assumptions. Justify differences
specific to an instrument and show where changing an assumption
reverses the outcome. Include nonrecipients exposed to shared bills
or markets. Keep statutory liability, actual receipts, final
burdens and lawful changes in benefit costs distinct.
A shared data source or AI tool does
not produce two independent findings. The existing ordinary vote
receives a useful opportunity to examine the record or stated failure
and a material amendment needs its own affected scrutiny under DB and
BCV. If a forecast or response is absent, keep that failure on the
record and use existing useful-time review. Preserve the Assembly's
power to vote, the rights of individual members and lawful deadlines.

**DB24 — Separate adoption from a release that depends on uncertain
facts.** For a material optional commitment the elected law specifies
the actual conditions for release, the official who verifies them,
earliest and latest dates, and any authorized smaller tranche or
alternate. Qualify dated cash, authorized purpose, owned capacity,
independent public control and accepted receiving under JC. A
material contested receipt or efficiency gain cannot count as a
qualified condition merely because one forecast includes it.
Where conditions fail, use the enacted qualifying tranche or seek
an exact lawful correction. Existing lawful standing services,
urgent action, binding contracts and actual accrued claims continue
under their own law. Where multiple optional releases share capacity,
review them together after protecting existing independent functions
and ordinary services. Compare exact different sequences and the
next window under the same paid calendar. Earlier scrutiny of one
release does not qualify a changed joint package. Existing urgency can alter the ordinary sequence
only within its prior authority. Preserve independent scrutiny and
a dated follow-up. No emergency report extends its powers or authorizes
an otherwise unavailable release.

**DB25 — Reconcile the forecast to actual decisions and receipt.** Keep
the proposal and forecast that members saw, publish the adopted text
and release conditions, and reconcile actual source dates, staffing,
accepted services and shortfalls on the existing budget and audit
calendar. Reconcile actual essential costs and compensation dates
against the same voted incidence cases, including affected nonrecipients. Distinguish a revised law, source correction, forecast error,
withholding and a failure to deliver. A wrong estimate is evidence for
correction and learning, with separate fair review for actual misconduct.
The elected body hears material divergence and decides a lawful correction
with independently reviewable evidence. For a common error affecting
several owners, keep independent origin evidence and open a protected
urgent route for each affected duty under JC29 and EV25–EV30. Give
nonrecipients direct assisted reporting without enrolment under OM01–OM06
and independent challenge under applicable law. Use protected originals
and a safe account for Assembly members under PE01–PE04. Schedule
verified relief, corrected legal decisions and new elected sources separately.
Use LX11–LX16 if its administrative error trigger is met. A forecast
error alone neither invalidates a lawful charge nor creates a refund.
Concurrent systemic review preserves earlier urgent rights and clocks.
Keep continuing cases and
original deadlines through personnel, provider and model changes.
AI may check totals and compare versions. Analysts remain accountable
for conclusions and officers and elected members retain their own
distinct decisions.
