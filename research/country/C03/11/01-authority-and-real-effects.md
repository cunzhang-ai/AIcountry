# Delegated law: scope, reserved choices and cumulative effects

*DR01–DR04; part of [C03/11](../11-delegated-rules-and-effective-public-control.md).
Design103 research, not enacted law.*

**DR01 — Binding effects determine the route.** This package governs general
rules made by national executive/administrative issuers under Assembly statute.
A rule is a prospective criterion for a class of people, activities or places
that binds legally or practically controls an entitlement, public charge,
inspection, permission, penalty, public-service access or challenge. Forms,
tariffs, mandatory manuals, model thresholds, procurement conditions and
incorporated private standards qualify when they have that effect. Genuine
advice and fact-specific professional judgments remain under their own law.
A recurring pattern prompts inquiry; it does not itself establish an imposed
rule, personal guilt or grounds to override a clinician or teacher.

An enabling Act identifies purpose, persons/territory, named public issuer,
permitted instruments/effects and limits, subdelegation if any, classification,
finance, scrutiny, court route and duration. A minister, vendor, foreign body
or AI gets no extra power from a contract. Elected local councils retain their
own competences and procedures; this national route does not turn their own
lawful choices into national regulations. Local execution of a national rule
cannot add an unauthorized condition. [LR01–LR24](../14-local-law-and-public-control.md) now supplies local delegated/
ordinance procedures separately; national guarantees/direct courts remain.

**DR02 — A vote cannot replace the necessary statute.** Franchise/count and
viable opposition, crime/maximal punishment, national tax base/rates, core
service eligibility, general force/search/surveillance powers, public debt or
guarantees and transfers of elected competence belong in legitimate primary
law under C14. Technical measurements inside a specified right/risk corridor
may be delegated; redefining an annex or input cannot abolish that right.
An affirmative instrument vote cures neither a missing delegation nor a reserved
or unconstitutional choice. Change primary/higher law through its proper route.

**DR03 — A positive materiality test.** Within valid delegated scope, a rule
is material if it reasonably may change protected access/equal standing;
essential-service quality or practical availability; exposure to serious bodily
or environmental harm; inspection/coercive burdens or usable review; a duty
across local borders; a hard-to-reverse physical/data commitment; or introduce
or increase a compulsory public charge already permitted within primary law.
A new unfunded national duty imposed on a locality is outside this route,
not legitimized by calling it material. Higher sector triggers prevail.

Also material: substantiated forecast annual incremental public spending or
mandatory aggregate compliance cost reaching **1% of the programme's enacted
annual appropriation named in the enabling Act**. Pin the fiscal year/value
in force at L; use full12-month equivalent effects, not a convenient partial
implementation month. Reassess changed sources/effects before actual making. No issuer may substitute or alter the Act's named programme line or fiscal-year
denominator, aggregate unrelated lines to dilute the trigger, or net away
losers' costs with others' gains. Without a positive named line,
any new net public spending or mandatory private compliance cost is material.
Missing substantiated cost/scope evidence cannot establish the below-threshold
routine class: use affirmative scrutiny or narrow/withdraw the new commitment. These are normative gates,
not estimates of actual effects or proof that spending below1% is harmless.
Routine means reversible detail inside the enacted corridor with justified
absence of every material limb. Credible unresolved serious effects go to the
affirmative route or a narrower proposal, rather than presumed insignificance.

**DR04 — Keep the original comparison.** Count all live and announced related
changes in the scheme since its last statute or affirmative baseline; geographical
batches, vendors, model versions, renewals and renamed forms cannot split a
material change. A routine update does not reset that baseline. A later
baseline cannot conceal still-live burdens: disclose the whole current scheme,
its distribution and the incremental comparison. The issuer justifies source,
tier and estimates publicly; an affected person/council or member can challenge
classification directly under DR17. Courts decide legality, not preferred policy.
