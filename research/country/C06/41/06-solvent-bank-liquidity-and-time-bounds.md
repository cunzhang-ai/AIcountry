# Money: solvent-bank liquidity needs evidence, a receiver and an end

*MC21–MC24; part of [C06/41](../41-money-and-financial-public-control.md).*

**MC21 — Neither a ratio nor collateral proves solvency.** A preassigned
qualified prudential case officer independently evaluates original realizable
assets/liabilities, viability and losses, including contrary information; a
separate monetary credit officer verifies that finding, charter eligibility,
gross group/aggregate headroom and pledged-title/haircut capacity for the actual
loan. No head/committee case signature gate, self-selected bank/AI certificate,
expected taxpayer bailout or uncertain rescue equity establishes solvency.
Doubt means prompt actual assessment and separate lawful M07/M09/ordinary
protection, not a fabricated loan. A solvent eligible bank has access to a
reasoned lawful decision, not an unlimited credit guarantee. Direct courts
review criteria/process/rights without inventing eligibility or new finance.

**MC22 — Own clocks, no pause of depositor rights.** The monetary first
receiver records the request and within two elapsed hours gives precise
necessary missing-item/help notice, earlier useful time where required. The
credit officer gives a reasoned decision within six elapsed hours of the first
objectively sufficient receipt, which an ordinary court may independently
identify; artificial incompleteness/serial notices cannot reset it. Even without
sufficient facts, report reasoned grant/decline/unresolved necessities and lawful
referral within24elapsed hours of original receipt; no silent endless queue.
A separate settlement officer verifies actual control/source and effects an
approved transaction within two elapsed hours of valid decision or records
actual failure. These require paid qualified24-hour coverage, not a promise
that solvency can always be proved in six hours. Useful protection can be earlier.
M07/claim/outage/court clocks retain their original actual events throughout.

**MC23 — Separate normal markets from emergency accommodation.** Ordinary
standardized market-wide overnight/up-to-seven-day secured operations may
repeat within the charter with fresh eligibility/title/solvency and actual
gross headroom each commitment. They cannot silently adopt bespoke weak-bank
collateral or terms. A bank/group-specific exceptional facility lasts at most
90calendar days from first draw; one fresh MC05 three-vote extension of at most
90 more requires new prudential/credit findings before original expiry and
within the original180-day outer. Rename, subsidiary, replacement security or
an ordinary-repo label cannot reset the same exceptional accommodation episode.
The charter may be stricter. A genuinely new episode needs independently
established recovery/termination of the old accommodation and new facts, not
merely repayment with another public loan. Existing interest/debt remains owed
after expiry; no automatic forgiveness, credit renewal or shareholder grant.
A nonviable bank uses actual M09 law, not revolving monetary support.

**MC24 — Price and propagate an actual decision.** Publish ordinary eligible
classes, terms and risk bounds before use. An exceptional bank's pricing must
cover the charter's enacted collateral/risk terms without hidden preferential
subsidy; a fiscal subsidy needs M15. No institution buys a case approval with
fees or fear of publication. Give reasoned bank/affected-person notice and
protected access; report refusal, pending evidence and actual acceptance/source
failure distinctly. Preserve correctable original requests/versions/collateral,
known material receivers and first clocks; new contrary facts require immediate
reassessment/correction under actual law, not automatic breach/guilt or an
unlawful freeze. Monetary refusal neither proves an insured event nor cancels
due M07, SF alternative payment, tax finality, customer or worker rights.
