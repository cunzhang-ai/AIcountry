# JC05–JC08. Elected choice, review and continuing claims

[Joint continuity](../65-joint-continuity-and-first-budget.md)

**JC05 — Present the elected choice before a material commitment.**
Treasury publishes the continuing baseline, credible repair and
independent alternatives with dated capital, recurring net source,
cash and qualified staffing. Show each exposure under both ordinary and
stressed source conditions. Price restocking and paid operating overlap
as they fall due. Identify the agency with the duty, the body with the
appropriation and who can make each lawful correction. Assembly and
competent local elected bodies retain their respective budget powers.
A weak source requires an explicit lawful repair or a recorded unresolved
shortfall and timely protection. Any proposal to change an obligation
needs its own legal process. A pass in an authored scenario supplies
neither spending authority nor a licence to withdraw existing service.

**JC06 — Keep the challenge independent and useful.** Treasury owns
reconciliation. Sector owners answer for their own schedules and receipt.
Existing audit, judicial and electoral bodies test the original evidence
or decide disputes within their powers. Preserve their own funded and
independently accessible means during the same adverse interval,
including direct access for affected people and lawful opposition.
Publish assumptions, dissent, costs and exposed classes. Limit disclosure
of protected persons, ballots, credentials and exploitable operational
detail, with safe independent access and a reasoned withholding decision.
Independent findings inform an accountable decision within existing
powers. The schedule adds no budget veto or binding automated decision.

**JC07 — Record actual performance and correct a broken assumption.**
Before first reliance, responsible operators confirm the joint schedule
through an authorized exercise or equivalent qualified evidence. Separate
a model result, exercise result, paid readiness and actual incident
receipt. On loss of a material source, route or protection, preserve
original records and claims and notify the relevant independent recipient
before the remedy loses practical value. Use the applicable earlier legal
clock. Keep original timestamps, adverse evidence and relevant actual
receipt. A substitute counts only within the lawful receiving body's
scope. Use the existing review, remedies and emergency authority for
actual action. A missed proof requirement leaves the exposure visible
and does not suspend an already commenced right.

**JC08 — Transfer duties with an accepted successor.** Apply the
existing commencement, migration and protected political rules to
each changed commitment. Existing contracts, lawful orders, remedies,
original records and due dates survive to the extent of applicable law.
Secure lawful source, paid overlap, qualified acceptance and usable
challenge before retiring a working route. Reopen the package on a new
shared failure, unpayable recurring cost, unsafe donor diversion, loss
of independent control or a better complete alternative. Keep a public
summary of changed assumptions, scope and remaining exposures, with
private details held by the lawful custodian.

[SC44–SC49](../../C04/05/11-essential-contract-costs-and-paid-continuity.md)
puts coincident essential contract adjustments on the same due-date account
as household protection, sector rates and existing obligations. Count any
conditional source or optional start only on its actual lawful dates.
