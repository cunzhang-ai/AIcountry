# Design275 evidence and custody

- The [DWP contribution circular, Annex A](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/housing-benefit-subsidy-circulars-2024/s22024-discretionary-housing-payment-government-contribution-for-english-and-welsh-local-authorities-for-the-financial-year-ending-march-2025)
  gives Manchester's 2024/25 allocation and limit. The department's
  [end-of-year returns workbook](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6874bd7681dd8f70f5de3f12/data-tables-use-of-discretionary-housing-payments-2024-to-2025.ods)
  gives the same allocation and another expenditure observation in
  Financial_Information. [Archived circular](original-sources/dwp-dhp-2024-25.html)
  · [Archived workbook](original-sources/dwp-dhp-outturn-2024-25.ods)
- The [Manchester City Council payment series](https://www.manchester.gov.uk/benefits-and-support/housing-benefit/how-the-benefits-service-is-performing/discretionary-housing-payments-welfare-reform-and-caseload-information)
  gives the same annual grant, the council's own spending and quarterly
  cumulative values. The [award table](https://www.manchester.gov.uk/download/downloads/id/29561/dhp_entitlement_by_tenure_type_and_ward_march2025.pdf)
  explicitly separates awards from payment timing. Both were inspected
  online. Do not count the two financial series as independent proof
  of bank settlement.
- [Historical Danish order 605 of 2024, sections 9 and 11](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2024/605/pdf)
  describes the regional and municipal roles in the inspected law.
  The [Copenhagen balance report, pages 9–10](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/agenda/a80a4de7-fdbb-4fa5-99e0-167c1b4eda43/691635c2-185f-4910-b666-b2725bed71d3-bilag-2.pdf)
  gives one former office's follow-up stock. The [17 August 2026
  committee report](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Besk%C3%A6ftigelses-%2C%20Integrations-%20og%20Erhvervsudvalget/m%C3%B8de-17082026/referat/punkt-4)
  gives the reported handoff and regional processing delay.
  [Archived balance report](original-sources/copenhagen-balances-2025.pdf)
  · [Archived committee report](original-sources/copenhagen-audit-followup.html)

The [scoped observations](observations.json) distinguish allocation,
published local expense, aged balances and unobserved bank settlement.
The Danish order was inspected online. Download access did not supply
the order or Manchester pages, so those sources remain externally
linked. Different countries, currencies and duties are not consolidated.
There was no actual affected participation in the research choice.
