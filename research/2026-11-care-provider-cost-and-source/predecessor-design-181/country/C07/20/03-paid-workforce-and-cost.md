# NC11–NC15. Small groups require real paid capacity

*Part of [NC](../20-early-care-relationships-and-staffing.md); no free professional,
family worker, foreign recruit, provider asset or hypothetical AI gain.*

**NC11 — Pay the whole job.** Existing TW/WL/OE guarantees govern pay, actual
hours, rest, leave, bargaining, preparation, learning, travel, supervision and
notice. Staff participate through their own worker/peer routes; management
does not appoint their voice. Qualification/induction/training, retention and
substitute capacity need paid source. Salary at a foreign statutory minimum
is no qualified recruitment price or compliance with this country's separate
median-linked wage floor. Do not count trainees as an already staffed future.

**NC12 — Test the room and the day as well as the year.** For the illustrative
16 two-year-olds, two groups8 require4 present competent adults under NC02.
40 hours/48 weeks mean7,680 contact staff-hours; authored10% preparation adds
768. At TW-like1,110.6 available task hours, eight annual equivalents cover
that scalar total. An ordinary two-block day can use8 distinct adults for
4 contact hours plus0.4 preparation each, within32-hour norms. Neither check
certifies leave/absence coverage over48 weeks or a real year's safe roster.

If3 of8 are absent together, even the generous upper32 paid hours available
from5 people in a6.4-hour normal day cannot cover35.2 contact/preparation hours.
Breaks, learning and qualifications can worsen that shortfall. Three actually
accepted healthy qualified substitutes would restore this particular8-person
day template; they are a priced example, not a universal minimum or proof
that every more efficient7-person rota is impossible. Other absences or
common failures require their own scope. Unserved children retain protection.

**NC13 — Keep the source distinctions visible.** In the separate April2026
GBP wage sensitivity,32×52 paid hours at the official UK12.71 cash rate gives
169,195.52 annually for8 staff, or232,643.84 for11 including3 full-paid backup
posts. This is not a provider quote, actual local wage or full cost: employer
dues/pensions, experienced pay, rooms, food, equipment, utilities, safeguarding,
management, training/transition and transport remain unpriced. Annual pay
is retained; available task hours do not imply paying only hours of attendance.
The same backup cannot be promised elsewhere at the same peak. No foreign-
currency or national fiscal conversion is made from this external sensitivity.

**NC14 — Parent fees are not total resources.** Published MIS2026 childcare
fees already include country-specific free-hours support and regional averages.
They cover the household's stated two-child mix, not this16-child nursery's
recipe or a matched local invoice. Source public funding, actual parent
payment, provider cost/receipts and reference transfer avoided are distinct.
Do not subtract a subsidy twice, count a grant as reduced real cost or treat
the difference between unmatched fees and payroll as profit/source. Universal
40/48 care also differs from a work-tested38-week entitlement.

**NC15 — Compare full models and their transition.** Retain private/cooperative/
public qualified provision, simpler administration, appropriately safeguarded
home-based alternatives and genuinely negotiated sharing. Compare stable
relationships, inclusion, competent staff, child/parent/worker time, source,
space and failure, not only ratios.1:4 and1:5 can need the same4 present staff
for16 children in the illustrated grouping; this is not a universal no-cost
tightening result. Smaller groups or stricter ratios can consume scarce skill,
increase cost and restrict rural choice. More AI or public ownership proves
neither safety nor efficiency. A real rival can displace this candidate.
