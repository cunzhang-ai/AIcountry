# Choose a first budget that can keep the country working

9 November 2026 · Design212 · [Canonical choice](../../country/C06/62-lasting-services-and-fiscal-shocks.md)

Retain the integrated democratic and service-first design. On the first
budget under uncertainty, select funded repair of binding duties and
paid preparation for demonstrable capacity gaps. Give the Assembly a
real immediate-reform alternative with all sources, sacrifices and
receiving shown. A positive aggregate budget margin cannot determine
the first programme. The country must carry old claims and independent
control through either choice.

| Comparison | What it distinguishes |

| [One published national fiscal plan](01-danish-budget-bridge.md) | From apparent room through demographic and other reservations, planned priorities and a scenario for expiring appropriations |
| [Common case and whole-country scope](02-first-budget-and-later-cohorts.md) | Shared repair, paid training and optional starts under four years of budget and actual staffing constraints, with a standing register of unpriced duties |

The Danish comparator is a dated plan. The authored case is a
counterfactual with independent inputs and no currency conversion.
Neither supplies a complete priced launch or claims that every existing
public service delivers safely.

[The observed 2025 baseline](../observed-government-baseline/README.md)
adds historical outturn and employment for the same national year, with
audit examples that qualify actual capacity. It remains separate from the
DK2035 planning figures and the authored case.
