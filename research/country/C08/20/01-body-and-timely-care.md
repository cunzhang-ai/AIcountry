# The body, supported choice and an accepting provider

*RP01–RP02; part of [C08/20](../20-reproductive-law-and-parentage.md).*

**RP01 — The person's actual choice governs their body.** Protect continuing
or ending pregnancy, contraception/removal, fertility assessment/preservation,
birth and refusal of a particular intervention. No compulsory pregnancy,
abortion, sterilization, gamete retrieval, genetic testing or embryo selection
for demographic, racial, fiscal, employment, family or political purposes.
Marriage, a support contract, donor role, guardianship, public benefit or an
AI prediction gives no authority over another's body. Embryo disposition
requires RP03/RP09 consent, not a claim that it is ordinary saleable property.

Preserve B21/B22 supported agency: decision-specific understanding presumed
from 16, with younger supported choice possible; no blanket adult veto or
forced disclosure to an unsafe household. Under 18 donation/third-party
pregnancy assistance is excluded by RP03/RP05, not ordinary confidential
contraception, pregnancy care or the young person's own clinically indicated
fertility care. Age, disability or an unwise choice alone cannot displace a
valid refusal. Elective reproductive use cannot be authorized by a substitute
agent or a court interpreting a general welfare mandate.

For an immediate medical emergency with no communicable choice, only the
existing narrow general clinical emergency law applies, with actual necessity,
least interference, known will and review. No exception to an ascertainable
valid refusal; irreversible effects require real clinical necessity, not a
sterilization/demographic objective. No pregnancy-specific detention or forced
extraction follows. A newborn receives independent care once born; that does
not retroactively authorize an intervention on the pregnant person's body.
A live-born child's independent care/protection cannot be waived by an earlier
abortion or parenting instruction.

[UA09](../23/03-consequential-and-therapeutic-acts.md) adds an express prospective
nonemergency therapeutic exception: prior heightened court review of this person's
serious actual medical condition may authorize unavoidable fertility loss or
pregnancy-ending effects, absent actual refusal/credible opposition. Necessary
therapeutic tissue/material removal and proportionate clinical pathology differ
from retrieval/preservation/use for reproduction, donation, research or another
person, which still need actual specific authority. No reproductive-policy goal,
consent fiction, elective use or permission from this research alone follows.

**RP02 — A right includes timely, usable care.** Safe abortion on the person's
request remains lawful under evidence-based professional standards, without
a penal gestational cutoff, partner authorization, mandatory ideological
counseling or a waiting period created to defeat access. Patient pregnancy
outcome, miscarriage, stillbirth or obtaining post-abortion care is not itself
a crime. A requested end of pregnancy does not require a clinician to perform
an unsafe particular technique; explain safe options, actual uncertainty and
an accepting timely alternative. Fetal-development/ethical disagreement is
real but cannot become a hidden official consent requirement in this candidate.

C07/S03's assessment and earlier useful-time deadlines cover the complete
referral/treatment path. No clock resets at a closed clinic or jurisdiction
boundary. An individual professional may declare prospective conscientious
nonparticipation in an elective service, with notice for a funded alternative;
no institutional veto over a legally owed service, obstruction, false information, record withholding
or denial of necessary emergency care. Competent clinical limits still apply.
The network accepts the person promptly without making them negotiate a
refusal; an individual objection cannot end its lawful delivery duty.
No AI or agency can require an unsafe act; human clinical/court review must
remain prompt enough to be useful, without compulsory court approval of
ordinary lawful care. Safe privacy and accurate source accounts remain;
a pregnancy outcome alone authorizes neither disclosure nor a police search.
