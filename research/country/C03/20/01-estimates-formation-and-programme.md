# GP01–GP04. Estimates, formation and the first programme

**GP01 — Give contestants an optional fair route to independent estimates.**
The Assembly's DB analysts, under its nonpartisan administration, offer
qualified and accessible campaign costing to lawfully qualified contestants,
including new contestants and one-person civic lists in the national contest. The published election
service rules set a funded ordinary request window, a fair first pass and
further capacity limits, using equal substantive criteria. Allocate a
proportionate first pass across contestants before repeated discretionary work
for any one group. Account or agent replication adds no priority. Reserve
capacity for lawful legislative and continuing service work. Treasury and
relevant bodies supply the same safely accessible baseline and timely original
inputs to analysts through existing lawful channels. The government has
no access to a rival's confidential request. Ballot access follows electoral
law. A skipped, limited or unresolved estimate remains permissible and
cannot invalidate candidacy, speech or a vote.

**GP02 — Publish only the estimate that was actually made.** Keep private
exploratory requests within safe confidential custody. A contestant who
publicly claims an official estimate for a proposal must identify the exact
proposal version and allow publication of the matched estimate and its
material limits. Otherwise publish a commissioned estimate only with lawful
authorization. Keep public methodological criticism and the Assembly's
ordinary independent analysis of public proposals available. State current-law
baseline, dates, payer, tax and distribution assumptions, restricted funds,
implementation and longer liabilities, evidence limits and uncertain physical
or workforce constraints. An official estimate has no endorsement status.
A revised proposal needs its own scope check and material recomputation.
Label partial estimates and incompatible baseline versions. Several positive
individual estimates cannot validate a common package.

**GP03 — Make governing support intelligible before the vote.** Under
existing formation rules, the candidate or sponsors give the Clerk the
programme and actual coalition or support terms on which investiture is
sought. Publish known material commitments for public powers, money and
services, including whether support is for formation, a named measure or
a wider relationship. State duration or review terms when agreed. Identify
major unresolved choices and indicate what has no committed support. Keep
ordinary negotiations and legally protected material appropriately private.
A secret undertaking cannot authorize public spending or office. The Clerk
makes records accessible, accepts corrections and preserves lawful nominations
and vote schedules. Failure to obtain costing or agreement on every topic
does not obstruct a required ballot or change the formation clock. Use the
existing independent route for deliberately obstructed notice or disclosure.

**GP04 — Reconcile the programme soon after lawful appointment.** Within
30 calendar days of the operative appointment, the Cabinet publishes a
single programme reconciled to current law, received public commitments,
the actual baseline and the competing budget paths. Existing earlier budget
and legal clocks continue. Name material promises kept, materially changed,
deferred or withdrawn, and important new measures. Separate desired change,
draft bill, appropriated authority, usable resources and received results.
Identify who pays, changes in access and incidence, qualified people,
sites and external inputs, which programme claims each shared resource,
delivery dates, continuing obligations and independent review capacity.
Keep statutory and public-operator funding, votes and acceptance distinct.
Give material changes and continuing duties a named owner. Use known evidence
and publish open dependencies without waiting for an invented complete
forecast. Failure to publish is a remediable procedural breach. The lawful
Cabinet's term, standing services and Assembly deadlines continue.

**Keep the opening decisions on their own clocks.** An already competent Assembly may consider exact existing-duty supply during formation. A later appointment starts GP04 and carries the lawful dispositions; it does not change or excuse statutory budget dates. Cross-body promises require their own decisions, matched conditions where necessary and separately verified receipt.
