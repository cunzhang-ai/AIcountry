# Design400. Receive displaced people with complete and compatible local service

[FR26–FR33](../../country/C09/07/06-joint-displacement-and-host-receiving.md)
joins actual homes, needed services, transport and paid receiving
without counting a displaced tenant or a committed worker as new supply.

The [worked case](../../settlement/joint-displacement-and-host-receiving/01-two-origins-two-hosts-and-one-care-route.md)
tests overlapping origins, a common care constraint and second-window
cash. Actual local choice, costs, readiness and outcomes remain open.
