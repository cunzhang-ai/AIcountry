The bounded decision is **whether the integrated design's many justified
separations can remain usable as one country**, rather than turning each new
right into a new form, gate, council, queue and appeal. C06 already requires
one account for qualified people, cash, physical resources and remedy capacity.
C07 protects effective access and C08 preserves first receipt and execution.
Neither yet gives a rule for the *cumulative cost of the institutions
themselves*: resident time and repeated testimony; frontline and reviewer
hours; slow serial permissions; shared vendors and data; and the number of
distinct bodies an ordinary or urgent problem must traverse. A document can
state many independent safeguards while all are staffed by the same few people
or cannot finish a claim before harm occurs.

**Values:** rights, practical democracy, dignity, stable informal cooperation,
public competence, speed where harm is imminent, review of coercive decisions
and real time for life outside government. **Constraints:** existing legal
obligations, no waiver of protected rights by convenience, necessary technical
specialization, impartial case review, privacy and finite staff and attention.
**Unknown empirical claims:** actual caseload, waiting and handoff times,
informal-help patterns, review quality, common-controller risks and the cost
of either consolidation or separation in a real polity. **Design hypothesis:**
separating conflicting *decisions* while sharing safe *capabilities* can give
people fewer compulsory steps and still preserve opposition, independent
challenge and professional judgment. It can fail when a shared back office
holds every credential or when simplified intake quietly becomes a sovereign
mediator deciding what a person's problem means.

## Different diagnoses and coherent packages

| Package | Mechanism and possible gain | Failure or tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Retain design 25 as written | Sector protections can mature separately; no new meta-rule or transition cost. | Each sector may count its own reviewer, portal, file and time as free; the whole journey remains unusable even if every clause looks protective. |
| Add an independent office for every risk and a universal prior approval | Makes conflicts visible and gives each right a named defender. | The same scarce specialists may sit on multiple boards; sequential vetoes delay urgent care, housing, infrastructure or justice; an approval bureau becomes the new choke point. |
| One central case manager or AI mediator decides all cross-sector issues | Fewer apparent doors, consistent records and fast routing. | The manager can redefine a person's claim, decide who is affected, combine sensitive data and silently ration legal rights; a single outage or patron blocks all remedies. |
| Broad ministerial consolidation and deregulation | Faster political command and fewer formal steps. | The service provider judges its own safety, evidence and appeal; an elected majority can disable opposition and individual remedies through cumulative efficiency reforms. |
| **Selected working design: accountable receipt, distinct legal decisions, proportionate review and shared support** | An authorized first receiver preserves the lawful claim date and follows a person-level journey; legally conflicting judgments stay separated; translation, clerks, secure exchange and other support can be shared with access boundaries and exit. A new institution must displace or justify old work and show its full load. | Shared support may itself become a vendor monopoly; differentiated reviewers and alternate routes still cost money; proof of usability needs actual journeys, not a diagram. |

The selection is a **country-design rule**, not a new national efficiency
ministry, algorithmic gate or single case file. It does not conclude that
fewer offices always produce better lives. A small public system may need a
shared regional professional service; a large system may need more local
decision capacity. Some judicial, electoral, safety and abuse investigations
must remain independent of the actor they review even when sharing a building
or payroll service. A no-change finding is valid where existing arrangements
already provide a usable, independently enforceable path.

## Selected reference operating rule

1. **Name one receiver and the real decision owners.** A person may report a
   problem through a lawful local office, provider, professional, court or
   assisted channel without first knowing its classification. The first
   authorized receiver gives a durable receipt, preserves the earliest legally
   effective date under the applicable statute and arranges urgent interim
   protection within existing competence. Referral is complete only when
   the competent recipient accepts it, and the person
   can see who owns each outstanding duty. A competence dispute goes to a
   preassigned statutory or court route without closing urgent protection.
   The receiver does not become a universal adjudicator; medical, benefit,
   housing, tax, police, family and election decisions remain with their
   named authorities and appeal routes.
   Voluntary mutual aid or informal resolution remains available without
   making a coercive household, employer or community leader the only gate.
2. **Separate conflicts of judgment, not every piece of infrastructure.**
   The following functions cannot be made one unreviewable decision: policy
   author and legality reviewer; service provider and final complaint or
   safety reviewer; investigator/prosecutor and judge; military/police command
   and serious-abuse investigator; election contestant and result adjudicator;
   treasury payer and sole verification of its own entitlement decision.
   Clerks, interpreters, professional training, accessible premises, secure
   message exchange, procurement expertise and some payroll or records tools
   may be shared under purpose-specific access, original-source custody,
   visible conflicts, alternate operation and independent audit. Shared
   support cannot be the only key to both a challenged act and its remedy.
3. **Choose review according to harm and reversibility.** Prior independent
   authorization remains necessary where law assigns it to coercion, loss of
   liberty, intrusive surveillance, irreversible harm or high-impact public
   commitments. An ordinary correctable service error may use fast internal
   correction plus a direct independent route; a claimant is not forced
   through several serial mandatory offices that cannot change the result.
   Urgent interim protection and access to a court remain available under
   their legal grounds. Review cost and delay are recorded as part of the
   decision, never hidden as a resident's duty to wait. Silence does not
   approve force, dangerous development, fiscal commitments or removal of a
   right. Low-risk routine activity may use ex-post audit only when a real
   remedy and tested detection exist.
4. **Budget the institution's own work.** Every material new office, mandatory
   step or oversight power enters the existing C06 commitment account with
   its proposed displaced function or reason to add capacity: annual case
   volume, qualified staff hours, hiring and training lead time, hearing and
   evidence work, cash, accessible sites, translation, travel, resident and
   carer time, data/security and independent appeal, backlog, maintenance and
   a failure-mode substitute. Report different burdens and distribution
   separately rather than converting autonomy or time into a single price.
   Count the same legal professional or provider
   once across all mandates by time and location. The Assembly receives a
   small set of coherent alternatives: reuse and repair an existing body,
   share support while separating decisions, create a new body, or retain
   the current system. It decides policy; a workload estimate is evidence,
   not a technical veto or pretext to cancel an existing right.
5. **Track a complete journey and non-users.** For representative and adverse
   cases, publish protected measures of first contact, repeated submissions,
   required separate decisions, elapsed time, cost, travel, care and paid-work
   disruption, abandonment, actual service or judgment received, correction,
   and unequal effects. People who never reached an intake or refused a
   compulsory agent are part of the denominator. Do not compress lost privacy,
   autonomy, time and legal protection into one satisfaction score. Preserve
   original observations and dissent; never expose private case files to
   make a dashboard appear transparent.
6. **Simplify without retroactive erasure.** A periodic elected review may
   consolidate duplicative forms, clarify competence, retire an ineffective
   program or fund a better route, after affected users and workers can
   contest the actual effects. It must name successors for pending claims,
   evidence, staff, contracts, judgments, data and remedies. An automatic
   sunset of basic rights, an agency's self-certified success, or an AI
   optimization update is not lawful simplification. A streamlined process
   that removes the only safe reporting, independent review or opposition
   route fails even if it saves administrative hours.

The [OECD Recommendation on Human-Centred Public Administrative Services](https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/api/print?ids=729&lang=en)
is a primary institutional comparator for portability across locations and
channels, clear responsibility, accessibility, coordinated life-event
services, procedural guarantees, capacity and resource planning. It is a
recommendation to adherents, not AIcountry law, evidence that one-stop
systems work here or authority to share all personal data. The [WHO practice
brief on continuity and coordination of care](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241514033)
is a sector comparator for continuity of care; it does not decide national
institutional structure. These sources do not supply local case volumes,
safe separation boundaries or a numerical staffing ratio.
Their support for usable journeys and coordination is not evidence that this
particular plural-receipt and shared-support package will work; that package
is this project's contestable inference.

## Interfaces, objections and continuity

This qualifies C01's treatment of scarce human attention, C04's administrative
execution, C06's commitment account, C07/C08's intake and remedy, C11/C12's
interoperability and human–AI collaboration, and C14's commencement proof.
It also qualifies the apparent creation of separate institutions in designs 6–25:
the **decision responsibility** remains distinct where legally or practically
necessary, while shared staffing or support is a contextual choice requiring
conflict, privacy and fallback tests. The legal rank of rights, courts,
elections, local discretion, taxes and public budgets is unchanged. This
record does not merge any current agency or alter any source manuscript.

Examples of material handoffs already present in the design are health/care/
housing provision, household safety and child protection, election evidence
and courts, lender/payment/benefit disputes, water/power scarcity, and
defense/civilian emergency logistics. The revised
[F1–F10 assessment](../FOUNDING-AND-STRESS.md) adds a cross-case burden and
shared-capacity check; it does not create an eleventh sector scenario. A real
adopter would need original case journeys, staff rosters, appointment
controllers, vendor keys,
budgets, waiting lists and lawful service/appeal records before merging or
starting anything. Even a shared regional interpreter or records platform
needs a lawful employer, payer, backup, privacy rule and receiving service.

Objections remain. “One receipt” can become a powerful classification gate;
shared infrastructure can bind every supposedly separate reviewer to one
vendor or politician. Reusing an overburdened court or inspector can worsen
delay and conceal under-protection. Proportionate review can be manipulated
by labeling a harmful process low-risk, especially when many small decisions
accumulate. Measuring administrative burden can itself demand intrusive data
or make officials optimize the published metric. Residents may reasonably
prefer a trusted local relationship over the shortest transaction. The design
therefore compares total real effects and protects multiple lawful entry
routes, original evidence, alternate operation, cumulation review and
non-quantified objections. It permits more institutional separation when
tested capture risks warrant its cost; it never trades away equal political
standing for a faster service flow.

**Evidence qualification:** local text architecture and two primary
institutional publications; no observed AIcountry cases, staff capacity,
affected-party participation or measured burden. **Research selection:** one
accountable receipt, distinct decisions, proportionate review, shareable
support and an explicit cumulative institutional-load check inside the
existing C06/A4 process. **Legal authorization:** none; no research index
adopts a governing rule. **Operational readiness:** no staffed intake, secure
exchange, independence test, alternate provider or appropriation verified.
**Observed outcomes:** none; F1–F10 remain authored cases.

The next bounded adopting-public test is one ordinary and one adverse journey
through existing health/care/housing and election/court systems, including a
failed first handoff and a common-vendor outage. The deliverable is an original
case-flow and staff-time map showing what protection each step provides, which
could safely share support, what delays or excludes people, and how an actual
remedy is delivered. Compare no change, shared support and fuller separation;
record dissent and non-users. This project has no lawful jurisdiction or field
budget. Reopen the design if simplified routing increases capture or exclusion,
or if an existing arrangement already meets the same rights with less burden.
Absent that evidence, no higher document count proves a better country.
