# SC29–SC32: source choice, preserved claims and the next national gap

*Part of [SC](../05-public-buying-and-continuous-supply.md); design 137 candidate.*

**SC29 — Select resilience with public accountability, keep the rivals real.**
Retain existing paid supply/repair where it meets actual needs. For critical
new commitments prefer retained public buyer/quality/fallback competence,
qualified plural routes and proportionate usable buffers, with nonexclusive
pooling where actual parties accept it. This is a conditional institutional
choice, not proof of cheaper prices, independent factories or adequate delivery.

Single-source/just-in-time procurement can reduce stock/qualification overhead;
several routes/reserves cost more and may still share a failed node. Domestic
manufacture can improve control but needs scale, know-how and imported inputs;
regional shared provision can widen capacity but introduces joint-decision,
foreign-source and exit dependence. Direct public production has no free assets
or automatically qualified workers. Compare the whole bargain; choose retain,
repair or a simpler arrangement when it better meets the protected purpose.

**SC30 — Retire budget amounts as adequate clinical source prices.** The six
legacy clinical quotations total 43.52m fictional CU, including 30.72m bundled
medicines/devices. Neither is a qualified demand, minimum cost, offer, source
or adequate country price. Retain them only as historical diagnostic/planning
placeholders with their limits; do not use them to certify an operating scale.
No spending cap, beneficiary exclusion, presumption of zero cost or cancellation
of a clinical duty follows. Replacing them requires the quantities/quality,
staff/lifecycle, source and receiving path, not another invented supplier recipe.

The 66-row account retains all identifiers/owners/quotes and four partial linked
interfaces without promotion to actual supply. Other 62 remain unmatched; their
94.182m quotations are not a measured gap or a floor for total current costs.
All later duties, private inputs, function costs and transition remain in scope.
The [bounded counterexamples](../../../settlement/operating-inputs/README.md)
test expiry, quarantine, common nodes, insufficient backup and separate source
currency; they are not clinical doses, new companies or a production forecast.

**SC31 — Preserve rights through a paid handover.** SC's proposed primary-law
commencement/notice and existing law continue; this extension enacts nothing.
Existing buyers/operators keep responsibility until qualified paid OP/T
successors accept actual duties, staff, stock/title, records, licenses, contracts,
wage/remittance/pension claims, creditors, foreign terms, pending recalls/cases
and useful-time remedies. A contract change or joint-purchase exit cannot
abandon care, an election or another existing protected service.

New storage/inspection/maintenance/standby, staff and source work need funding;
the old tender-publication quote does not price them. Shared support must not
merge buyer, verifier, product authority, independent court or public mandate.
Assembly/local law, replaceable government, funded opposition/source scrutiny,
AA and courts retain their scopes; their actual independence/capacity remains
unverified. No new protector or AI may award its own authority or delay elections.

**SC32 — End the bounded source decision without inventing readiness.** This
selection disposes the remaining purchase-scope question: retain/account for
the existing 66-row reference boundary, select the positive critical-input architecture,
and reject adequate-price/source/ready-supply claims. It does not pretend to
complete a priced country or make patients await a perfect supplier register.
Evidence qualification, research choice, lawful authority, funded qualified
receiving and observed lives remain independent; the latter three are unknown.

Next reconcile the **service-first country's recurring and transition source
alternatives** in one current whole-function/provider context, including the
retired-price boundary, individual protection/earned rights, all tax/source
owners and dates, private income/work/care, capital/FX and continuing claims.
Compare retain/repair, scaled prospective expansion and a substantive whole
rival; close with a reasoned retain/revise/reject conclusion on the offered
scale, not a residual output/tax or a new isolated product. Actual fiscal/legal/
engineering and affected qualification requires real resources not supplied
here. Urgent harm or larger contradiction can reprioritize. Reopen on real
supplier/source/need/stock/capture evidence or better whole alternatives;
without monitoring, successful arithmetic is not observed better lives.
