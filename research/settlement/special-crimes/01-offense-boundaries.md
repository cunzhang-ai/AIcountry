# Offense boundaries: hypotheses that a fair trial must still prove

*Part of [special harms](README.md); fictional accounts, not legal findings.*

| Case | Candidate application and independent receiving duty |
|---|---|
| H1 A freely choosing 15-year-old and a 17-year-old are 23 months apart | K13's peer exception excludes the age-only offense absent coercion, dependency or authority; credible exception evidence must be disproved by prosecution. At 25 months the exception is unavailable, but age/fault/acts still need proof, P05 still governs the older child's case and no mandatory custody follows. This threshold cliff is a declared value objection, not a scientific fact. |
| H2 A supervisor uses housing/security power to obtain a sexual act from a 17-year-old | The under-18 authority-abuse limb needs proof of actual power, use and fault; an adult consent-age label is no answer. Free clinical support/protection does not await charge. A nominal former teacher relationship alone cannot establish the offense. |
| H3 A young person seeks care after making a private image; a third party circulates it | Do not prosecute the protected young person merely for own voluntary creation. Identify actual exploitation under K14 or unauthorized identifiable image sharing under K15; peer sharing is not permission for distribution. Preserve minimal restricted originals, arrange safe removal/help and defense access; do not publish image evidence. |
| H4 A fabricated intimate image is presented as proof of a child's abuse | K15 may cover knowing unauthorized distribution; K14 actual-child production needs its own proof. Synthetic depiction does not establish a real act or offender. Preserve transformations and contrary evidence, challenge provenance and receive the depicted person's privacy claim. |
| H5 Public-interest reporting upsets an official; a stalker calls surveillance journalism | Neither a label nor dislike settles K15. Prove unwanted course, fault, fear/disruption and absence of lawful justification; test necessary reporting independently. Safe support and narrow interim protection retain their own evidence and prompt challenge. |
| H6 A lawful strike interrupts water service; a person intentionally destroys a safety valve | Labor withdrawal alone is not K16. The valve case needs actual damage, ownership/authority, fault and concrete disruption or danger. A technical essential-service label does not prove them. Safe supply/repair must be received even if no conviction follows. |
| H7 A driver crashes after a model warning that was unreliable and incomprehensible | Prove actual specific risk knowledge or express gross-negligence duty/ability/causation. The warning, license or crash alone cannot establish K18. Fund independent technical challenge, casualty care and road repair; civil liability has its own burden. |
| H8 A plant pollutes water before a new agency invents a threshold | K19 cannot apply its missing published predicate retrospectively; an actually applicable K02/K21 or civil/sector law still needs its own elements. Preserve source samples, arrange safe water and lawful prevention/repair; a criminal gap does not eliminate those duties. |
| H9 Animals suffer during an unresourced evacuation; a business intentionally withholds available care | Distinguish inability/necessity from K20's actual duty/ability and intentional or reckless suffering. Humane receiving cannot wait for guilt and cannot leave animals in worse custody. Independently examine who controlled usable resources; poverty itself proves no cruelty. |
| H10 A company obscures a known product hazard through changing AI reports | K21 needs an actual prior duty, specific danger, fault and exposure; death/injury retains the relevant result offense. Q07 requires an attributable control structure, not pooled guesses. A recall/service remedy remains necessary independently of criminal proof. |
| H11 A taxpayer cannot pay and later disputes classification | No K22 merely for debt/dispute. A knowingly false material declaration with evasion purpose needs separate proof. Preserve accessible assessment challenge, actual subsistence and tax collection's own lawful bounds. |
| H12 A cabinet alleges false campaign speech while an official alters counting records | Speech alone is not K23. Prove actual falsification/force/conditional personal benefit and fault; protected challenge remains. Election access/correction runs through C03 before the vote/handover, not a prosecutor's unilateral opponent ban. |
| H13 An expired custody order is hidden by the implicated commander | K05/K24/K12 need personal act, knowledge and applicable fault, without multiplied punishment. Direct liberty review, original clocks, outside-command source custody and accepted release services are immediate. A later acquittal cannot validate the expired hold. |

Every row is a reasoned proposed disposition, **not an executable guilt rule**.
Unknown facts, fair trial, original evidence and actual authority remain.
Clinical, support, election, environmental and animal receiving have distinct
grounds; the claimant is not required to prove an entire criminal dossier.
