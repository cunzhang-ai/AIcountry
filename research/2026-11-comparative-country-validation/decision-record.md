# Design516 decision record

**Basis and decision.** Use the [Design515 baseline](baseline.json) and
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md). Keep service-first plural provision
as a working country choice. Replace an average-only first-budget screen
with a same-period, same-population comparison that preserves material
gains and losses, including independently checkable access and burden.
Where suitable evidence shows competing barriers, bring a bundled repair,
credible ordinary repair and direct capacity alternative to the same
budget decision. A sharper source does not substitute for actual
qualifications, authority or affected people's choice.

**Rivals and objections.** Averages remain useful for scope and trend.
Conditioning can change who enters a comparison. Different measures can
show apparently contrary trends without a calculation error. Compare
mechanisms rather than picking the favorable denominator. A program that
claims broad improvement needs timely receiving and workable alternatives
for the people who bear its costs. A narrower supported claim can proceed
on its scope. Excess measurement can intrude on privacy and delay relief.
Use safe aggregates or scoped local evidence and preserve original remedies.

**Evidence, dependencies and handoff.** The [original sources](sources.md)
support scoped descriptive differences. The [worked choice](../settlement/comparative-country-validation/01-observed-gaps-and-first-budget.md)
uses separately authored cash and service limits. Do not combine survey
vintages, infer a clinical decision or transfer an outcome from an
unresolved comparison. The three internal review scopes provide no
adoption, affected consent or independent final review.

**Next decision.** At the responsible local or adopting authority, verify
one exact ordinary access path and a funded alternative through fee or
travel support, clinical receiving, actual receipt and the next visit.
Disaggregate by the predeclared constraint and include people outside the
first queue. Reopen on verified harm, changed scope or a stronger plausible
alternative. Neither funding nor comparative superiority is established.
