# Public audit: independent findings, public choice and actual correction

2 November2026 · Design121 research selection, not enacted law

[AA01–AA40](../../country/C04/04-public-audit-and-accountable-oversight.md)
answers A1's positive institutional question. Its eight canonical parts own the
rules. [Dependencies](../commitments/34-public-audit-and-accountable-oversight.md)
joins actual sources/people; [completion](../../status/completion/29-public-audit-and-current-operating-reference.md)
separates the candidate from actual adoption/qualified independence/outcomes.

The issue is both suppression and abuse: a Cabinet-dependent head may bury public
loss, while an unconstrained auditor can target opponents, expose private people
or substitute its priorities for elected law. Audit staff, head, original source,
reply, report release, outside examination, courts and real response need their
own accountable acts. More institutional names are not independent control.

| Whole arrangement | Benefit and retained loss | Current disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Cabinet-qualified protected career chief | Simple existing C04 accountability and competence; executive career/funding pressure can survive formal protection. | Serious simpler rival, staff safeguards retained; prospective head source replaced only through AA36 law. |
| Assembly-qualified nonrenewable chief with statutory report duties | Visible elected source plus protected case/report work, court and external examination; shared patrons/blockade/long-term entrenchment remain. | Selected hypothesis, not actual independence or a new sovereign guardian. |
| Plural collegial board | More deliberation/dispersed signature can create a cartel, deadlock and fixed staffing burden. | Real alternative, not another advisory body added by default. |
| Audit court with binding recovery | Can link findings to consequences, but inquiry/merits/proof/defense/appeal must be separated and funded. | Retained structural rival; this candidate leaves liability/punishment to actual ordinary courts. |
| Peer/public/market audit | Can broaden scrutiny; wealth, hidden client dependence, source access and unperformed voluntary work can limit it. | Genuine supporting/alternative arrangements, no substitute for a legally accountable funded public function by label. |

AG has an open Assembly3/5fullN plus1/3frozenO appointment and eight personal
nonrenewable years. Statutory audit/report staff have protected competence/career
and actual duties; completed supported reporting cannot await an audited minister
or a vacant head. Necessary record/preservation powers have precise scope, reply,
privacy and independent review, not private searches/forced confession/freezes.
Recommendations cannot veto policy, spend or convict. No VD15 or new fine source.

A separate three-year examiner is selected by open procurement/fullN majority
plus the protected O condition. No consecutive same-controlled-provider renewal;
three years' separation before another fresh award, no renamed-firm reset. It
audits office accounts and selection/quality/control and reports without AG
approval. Exact OJ status/ordinary/prosecution routes review the head and staff;
no auditor decides its own final liability or demands a guilty verdict.

Risk-directed cases do not yield a national corruption rate. Original frames,
probabilities, missed units, contrary sources and actual receipt matter; one
clean sample is not a clean country. Future methods can change understanding,
not multiply mandates, erase unfamiliar public/private ties or enact standards.
Source dependence, framing and mediator replacement remain challengeable.
