# 217. Earnings records and portable correction

| Owner | Bounded obligation | Acceptance and failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First competent office and lawful record custodian | Preserve original receipt and assist with alternate proof | Keep person-side access and verify the receiving owner. A failed handoff stays open. |
| Work, insurance and income decision owners | Apply the legally relevant facts to their distinct decisions | State reasons, correction dates and remedies. Respect privacy and preserve earlier due rights. |
| Revenue, insurance and Treasury payers | Reconcile owed wages, contribution cash, credit backing and matched advances | A corrected fact does not constitute receipt. Count actual recovery once under its own source. |
| Independent review and successors | Check due performance and disputed classification | Preserve pending cases, aid and actual alternative access. |
