# Design302. Local service funding through population change

10 November 2026 · Working selection · No actual enactment

## Baseline and problem

[Design301](baseline.json) requires funding for nationally assigned
local duties and distinguishes grant assessment, payments and receiving.
It leaves the choice between population adjustment, fixed service costs,
revenue capacity and a broken series open. The problem can come from
increased need, a stranded route, base loss, a change in method or late
cash. Those explanations need different responses. Apply the [working
framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).
Codex assisted. No affected resident, local authority or independent
reviewer qualified the proposal.

## Alternatives and selection

- **Freeze prior shares or smooth all changes.** Useful when estimate
  noise and staffing commitments dominate. Can favor places with low
  actual demand and leave a growing host short. Preserve as a
  prospective smoothing alternative only on actual comparative costs.
- **Move the old grant immediately with the headcount.** Legible and
  responsive but insensitive to minimum staffing and networks, changes
  in assessed capacity, and the timing of need. The authored case
  underfunds Ridge annually and leaves both short at the payment date.
- **Assess gross floor cost and representative capacity, with dated
  continuity.** Select this reference. Attribute funding once,
  revise durable changes when qualified, and separately authorize
  temporary continuity where the next payable duty is at risk.
  Retain ordinary retiming when valid and funded. If the evidence
  remains contested, hold only the affected change and preserve
  the undisputed and actual correction paths.
- **Change the delivery structure.** A regional or shared operator
  may cut a fixed burden. It can also shift travel, staffing, risk
  and control. Require elected scope, paid overlap and acceptance
  before claiming savings. Keep it a genuine rival where access is
  preserved rather than assuming every current site must remain.

The selected method could reward a locality that overstates its costs
or make the statistical body a practical gatekeeper. Publish comparable
benchmarks and challenges, use independent original evidence, separate
verification and payment, and keep a real route during disputed data.
A lower tax effort does not raise the assessed grant. Cap the bridge
under actual authority and expose long-term shortages to elected choice.
The 60-day publication window and 90-day outer advance limit are
reference choices, not empirically calibrated periods.

## Authority, compatibility and bounded test

[MF14–MF18](../country/C04/08/04-funding-through-population-change.md)
requires prospective legislation and voted sources. Preserve earlier
valid entitlements, restricted funds, staff, local decision powers,
accepted contracts and timely remedies. Keep statistical release under
PCO, distinct sector funding under MF03 and revised amounts under
their proper source. The existing NF benchmark remains a comparator.
Use [primary evidence](sources.md) only for institutional distinctions.
The [constructed case](../settlement/local-funding-and-population-change/README.md)
does not price an actual authority or prove better delivery.

Test one common service in two actual contrasting localities on the
same annual scope and first due date. Match fixed and avoidable costs,
standard effort, unserved need and actual receipts. Compare using the
existing distribution, annual recalculation and an independently funded
continuity route. Reopen after a failed series, missed service,
persistent advances, strategic base manipulation or a better shared
provider. Adoption, cash, staffing, review and outcomes remain open.
