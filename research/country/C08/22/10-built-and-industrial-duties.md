Temporary, mobile and emergency accommodation is not automatically a permanent-
building permit category. Actual exit/fire/sanitation duties and protected tenure
remain; no paperwork shortcut licenses displacement without lawful grounds.

A TD05 approval distinguishes planning permission, technical conformity and actual
safe occupation. An assessor cannot promise structural safety without real site,
material, load and inspection evidence. A new public code cannot simply close
all existing homes; TS15 requires phased lawful reconciliation, actual hazard
review and funded alternatives. A dangerous structure may need urgent evacuation,
but occupation/tenancy/compensation and safe receiving need their own powers;
no inspector may create homelessness or a police custody ground by paperwork.

TD06 reviews cumulative off-site effects, contractor controls, power/cooling
loss, maintenance, foreseeable extreme events and interaction with nearby sites.
Do not split one actual controlled installation into paper companies to evade an
applicable threshold. A genuinely distinct operator is not liable by association;
actual control and fault remain matters for independent proof.

Plans require actual transport, clinical and safe-place capacity alongside
ordinary services. A siren or simulated evacuation does not prove everyone can
leave; a warning cannot become an unfunded duty for residents to rescue themselves.

Closure, tailings, contamination, abandoned workings and dam maintenance remain
obligations through insolvency or sale under B/U/TS11. State ownership does not
erase them; private insurance does not transfer them to the public by default.
No closure bond or technical forecast is spendable public cash twice. Radiation,
nuclear operations and deliberate open biological release also require TD12;
TD06 is no generic license for those exceptional new activities.
