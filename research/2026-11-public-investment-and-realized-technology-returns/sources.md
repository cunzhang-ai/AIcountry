# Evidence for public investment and realized technology returns

| Primary source | What the record establishes | Use and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [U.S. Government Accountability Office, GAO-26-107882](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-107882/index.html) | In the reviewed CHIPS portfolio as of July 2025, Commerce negotiated upside-sharing agreements for 27 of 40 awarded projects. The report describes profit thresholds, varied funding and milestone schedules. | Separate an award, accepted milestone and contingent return. The observation does not establish how much cash was collected or that the terms improved the underlying industry outcomes. |
| [U.S. Government Accountability Office, GAO-26-109121](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-109121/index.html) | A subsequent audit describes amended awards involving equity or a warrant, and removal or revision of some upside-sharing terms. It reports actual program disbursements separately. | Preserve the effect of amended terms and distinguish a valuation, title, paid public outflow and received return. The follow-up covers part of the same programme and is not independent replication. |

The [comparison](../settlement/public-technology-returns/README.md)
uses authored costs, accepted scope and a contingent-return formula.
Neither audit supplies those parameters or demonstrates that a
similar instrument improves AIcountry's service, labor outcomes
or public finance. Actual authorization and affected participation
remain open.
