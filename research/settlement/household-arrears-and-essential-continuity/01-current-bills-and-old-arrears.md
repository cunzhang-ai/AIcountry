# Keep essential service during a missed payday

Design330 · Constructed comparison for [HL18–HL22](../../country/C09/25/05-household-arrears-and-essential-continuity.md)

A household's wage is late. Usable household cash is 5 illustrative
units. Current essential charges total 10: energy 2 by day 2, water 1
by day 3, rent 7 by day 4. Old rent arrears are 6 and old energy
arrears 3, separate from those charges. The home is safe and the
resident wants to stay. The landlord intends to initiate a new
arrears-based proceeding on day 4. No court order exists. A separately
authorized continuity source can pay 5 before day 4, with actual
receiving officers and the person's consent. A corrected benefit
payment of 6 is expected but cannot settle before day 6.

| Arrangement | Result by day 4 on the authored facts |
|---|---|
| Wait for the corrected benefit | The household can pay current energy, water and 2 of rent. The current rent gap is 5. |
| Apply the available 10 to old arrears first | Prior arrears consume 9 and leave 1 for current bills. The current gap is 9. The allocation is illustrative, not an assumed creditor agreement. |
| Protect current receiving and coordinate a bounded arrears pause | Pay energy 2 and water 1, and use the remaining 2 and bridge 5 for rent. The 9 of old arrears remains separately owed. Apply the proposed prior-law pause to new covered proceedings only. |

The authored selection is the third route. The competent steward issues
a supported seven-day notice before the planned new proceeding. The
tenant and claimant can use direct review. An existing order would
instead require timely independent court action. The provider receipts
and ability to remain safely housed are checked separately. If the
bridge can supply only 4 by day 4, the current rent gap is 1. Apply
any stronger protections and review without calling the pause a payment.
Existing essential utility limits remain applicable in every arrangement.

A late benefit does not pay day 4 retroactively. Reconcile it when
actually received, under its own period and lawful advance rules.
No automatic debit or recovery from unrelated support follows.
For the next cycle, suppose verified ordinary resources available for
these charges are 8 and the same charges cost 10. The next budget
has a real 2-unit gap before arrears repayment. An agreement that
takes any amount for old debt without financing this gap is not an
affordable current plan. Seek actual eligible support, an agreed
affordable restructuring or other lawful housing provision. Preserve
an existing urgent safe-receiving duty if staying becomes impossible.
