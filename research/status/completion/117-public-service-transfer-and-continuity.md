# Scoped finding. Legal transfer and service delivery have distinct tests

Design209 specifies transfer by function, legal owner, payer, operator
and actual service receiver. It joins continuing rights and obligations,
staff, facilities, funded overlap and independent remedies without a
readiness veto or a general takeover.
[Canonical clauses](../../country/C04/11-public-service-transfer-and-continuity.md)

A Danish transition compares direct and retained operation, conditional
costs and separate fiscal adjustments. An authored case tests midnight
transfer, a shared worker and first payroll. Actual paid transition,
authority and better lives remain open.
