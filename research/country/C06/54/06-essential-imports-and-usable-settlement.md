# NS21–NS25. Pay for essential imports and verify that they arrive in time

*Part of [national production and source strategy](../54-national-production-and-source-strategy.md).
Design258 research selection.*

**NS21 — Put foreign settlement and physical receiving in the same
public choice.** For material imported dependencies in the first
budget and recurring offers, name the protected service, required
quantity and specification, accepted supplier, settlement currency,
lawful owner and payer, last safe settlement date, transport,
clearance, local stock and actually accepting receiver. Include
continued work, power, storage and independent review. Trace
direct and upstream dependencies across care, water, food, energy,
communications, justice and elections without counting a shared
input or cost twice. Keep gross assets, usable owned currency,
contractual drawing room, actual release, cargo and final use
separate. A valuation or credit ceiling does not pay a supplier.
A settlement or border release does not prove useful receipt.

**NS22 — Keep title, purpose and currency at the point of release.**
Treasury executes within actual voted public authority and the
monetary authority retains its own source limits. Require an accepted
seller and intermediary, confirmed permissions and timing, lawful
conversion if needed, settlement evidence, remaining caps and existing
reserve floors. A private exporter or household can voluntarily sell
its own available currency under the applicable law and terms. No
public claim follows from aggregate export receipts, deposits or
remittances. Treat a conditional swap, tied foreign credit, pledged
asset and potential refinancing by their actual terms. Record fees,
collateral, currency, repayment and competing use. A supplier-specific
facility cannot finance another import or a foreign debt payment
without expressly applicable authority and counterparty acceptance.

**NS23 — Choose a resilient portfolio before a shortfall.** Compare
ordinary trade with genuine route and supplier diversity, paid
regional assistance and qualified domestic repair, production,
stock or demand reduction. Hold the protected service and deadline
fixed. Count what each option requires from workers, households,
producing regions, debtors, donors and affected ecosystems. Require
actual paid shifts and local receiving before treating a planned
substitute as usable. A mutual-aid option must preserve the
supplying place's obligations and agreed reserve under a concurrent
call. Test loss of the principal corridor, a seller's or lender's
refusal, incompatible settlement currency and a second call before
replenishment. Retain effective trade and choose only verified
replacement of the exposed part. Do not treat emergency imports,
a larger reserve or a domestic production target as sufficient on
its own.

**NS24 — Apply elected priority without commandeering exchange or
border control.** The Assembly names the covered public uses, lawful
release ceiling, urgency grounds, non-discrimination, protected
floors, expiry and independently accessible challenge under CP and
DB. Treasury and authorized service officers use the verified route
only within their actual mandates. Monetary, customs, safety and
clinical decisions keep their distinct jurisdiction and evidence.
Priority access to public currency, a scarce slot or a subsidized
source needs prospective criteria, documented scope and actual
delivery verification. Disclose public price support, who receives
it and what remains exposed. Preserve paid independent legal help
and usable outside review before avoidable loss. No model, finance
officer or logistics coordinator may take private exchange, compel
foreign dispatch, cancel debt or substitute for a safety decision.
A failed conversion, refusal or port hold triggers applicable
protection and an elected correction where new authority is needed.

**NS25 — Confirm local use and the next protected window.** Record
actual accepted goods and service at the relevant deadline, losses,
unmet cases, and a paid replenishment and alternate route. Preserve
existing supplier and worker claims, clinical consent, trade and
debt obligations, environmental limits and independent remedies
under their own law. Keep domestic liquidity, foreign settlement,
physical handling and licensed use visible as separate exposures.
After a port, vendor or currency change, retest the affected
sources, successor and previously uncounted dependencies. AI can
trace dependencies, reconcile times and compare scoped alternatives.
Competent people verify release, lawful receiving and public
choice. A successful short window qualifies only that window.
Reopen on changed foreign conditions, shifted harm, short receipt,
lost independence or a stronger complete alternative.
