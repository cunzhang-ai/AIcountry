# C13. Animal harm, food access and disease response

*Part of [C13 lived experience](../C13-lived-experience.md); integrated design 55 authored stress, not an observed event.*

A small fish farm and nearby food-processing cooperative employ much of a
coastal district. Workers report distress and injuries during handling. A
possible animal disease appears, the veterinary laboratory is far away,
and the district imports much of its affordable protein. A minister wants
immediate mass destruction and a national product announcement; a producer
offers only its private welfare certificate. A storm threatens power and
oxygen supply while residents need the same vehicles for evacuation.

The C09 veterinary service seeks original animal and disease observations;
the independent C08 inspector investigates conditions without waiting for a
consumer complaint. A time-limited hold, treatment or humane disease order
uses the precise prior-law ground and a less harmful feasible comparison.
If immediate action is necessary, it records uncertainty, methods, people
responsible, animal care and prompt court review. C07 human health, C08 food
safety and C09 food supply each make their own lawful decisions; the minister
cannot turn a disease suspicion into an unlimited cull or suppress a warning
to preserve jobs. A welfare advocate, producer and workers have usable
routes to challenge distinct failures without a veto over emergency care.

C06 counts the same laboratory, vehicle, energy, fish feed, safe receiving
site and replacement food once. The Assembly decides any new burden that
exceeds preauthorized disease and scarcity rules, protects household food
access and states lawful compensation and work support. C10's storm lead
coordinates human evacuation with realistic animal care; no planning app
assumes one truck can complete both journeys. C14 later compares actual
animal injury, disease control, human safety, food received, jobs and
ecological release, including unobserved losses.

This exercise fails if a welfare certificate replaces observation, delay
leaves animals in severe suffering, emergency action becomes unreviewable,
safe care has no funds, imported substitutes move harm elsewhere, or human
food and evacuation claims disappear. No actual fish farm, disease,
resource account, community participation or outcome has been tested.
