# Build health and care capacity without exporting the shortage

*WF111–WF116. Candidate rules for publicly funded international recruitment.*

**WF111 — Separate public recruitment from personal mobility.** The
national health or care authority identifies the employer, occupation,
duties, locations, first useful dates and recruited scope. Include
active sourcing through public contracts, agencies and intermediaries.
Ordinary independent applications, protection, family and admission
claims follow membership and employment law. An origin assessment
cannot veto an individual's departure, lawful application or right to
change jobs. A public recruitment plan cannot count foreign personnel
as already available. Compare lawful retention, supported return,
training, portable recognition and redesigned delivery under WF85–WF89
on the same service dates.

**WF112 — Use evidence from the actual source service.** Before launching
a material targeted campaign, the recruiting authority and its
qualified independent reviewer assess likely additional departures,
verified staffing, protected tasks and available cover by place,
qualification and date. Seek evidence and meaningful input from
source-country authorities, affected providers, workers and
communities through safe routes. Use relevant international
safeguard evidence as a signal for scrutiny. National headcount
does not settle a specific rural or specialty gap. Publish the
limits and reasoned disposition with safe aggregates. Where a
credible material shortage may be worsened, hold new targeted
activity unless WF113 establishes a supported joint pathway.
Keep unknown coverage visible. No private employer or AI can
declare a foreign service adequately staffed.

**WF113 — Fund and verify the paired commitments.** A programme that
would recruit from an exposed source service requires a lawful
agreement with the competent foreign authority and an accepted local
receiving plan. Specify scope, ceilings, dates, actual funded
additional capacity or other qualified measures that preserve the
affected source duties, workers' protections and who can review
and verify delivery. Identify one-time and recurring payers on
both sides. A payment to a foreign institution, a training place
or a promised future hire is insufficient to demonstrate cover.
Before releasing any public recruitment stage that depends on
source protection, verify the needed service by its actual useful
date. Stage or hold the new campaign if the evidence fails.
Preserve independently lawful hiring, existing contracts, travel
and employment claims, and remedies. No repayment or forced return
obligation falls on a worker because a public agreement fails.

**WF114 — Keep recruitment costs and exit off the worker.** The
responsible public buyer and employer fund recruitment and related
required costs across the authorized chain. Prohibit direct and
indirect recovery from workers, including fees through agents or
deductions disguised as charges. Give written, intelligible terms
before acceptance, with equal labor protection, actual wages and
independent advice. Require protection against contract substitution
and retention of identity documents. Provide direct inspection,
wage or fee recovery, applicable interim support and the existing
sponsor-exit route under membership law. Payment, residence,
work authorization and accommodation keep their own duties and
remedies. A worker can leave or complain without a recruiter's
approval. No new immigration status or guarantee arises here.

**WF115 — Count only qualified work that reaches residents.** On the
destination schedule, separate lawful admission, recognition of
competence, paid supervision, accepted work and retained delivery.
Protect existing shifts and count supervisors, bridge placements,
housing, travel and review once. Apply the ordinary portable
qualification rules. No approval target overrides a safety
assessment. Use authorized interim service or separately financed
local cover for any late or absent arrival. If both fail, report
the original unmet duty and seek competent correction. AI may
compare qualifications or schedules under scoped authority with
human verification. It cannot score personal mobility, decide
status or licence an unproved act.

**WF116 — Review both sides and carry the obligations forward.**
Independent reviewers and ordinary auditors trace origin cover,
destination receipt, actual worker pay, free exit, any fees,
unfilled service and source spending across each stage. Give
workers and affected communities useful direct challenge and
privacy. Publish safe results, including unmet original duties.
Pause or redesign new activity if retention, actual cover, labor
rights or independent evidence fails. Carry wages, lawful admissions,
recognized qualifications, committed support and valid remedies
through expiry or successor change. Reopen if a stronger domestic
or cooperative option delivers the needed care, or evidence shows
the campaign is displacing the shortage.
