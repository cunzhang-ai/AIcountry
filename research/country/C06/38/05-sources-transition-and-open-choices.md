# PA17–PA20: sources, transition and the remaining country choice

*Part of [C06/38](../38-work-pay-prices-and-country-income.md); design 93 research.*

**PA17 — Count a wage rise and a new tax on every affected side.** Show gross
employer cost, worker net/resources, changed I05/IC, restricted Y contributions,
G credit transfer, future pension units and producer price/profit/hiring effects.
Neither 9m extra S contributions nor an eligibility-dependent credit reduction is
unrestricted new G revenue. A tax on a public payment is not independent productive
income; equivalent net schedules have equivalent source requirements. Do not fund
new wages by silently reducing an unrelated modeled F payroll or median.

RF recurring sources, capital/debt, liquidity and long pension tails remain;
PS foreign ownership/receipt and H paid title/tenancy obligations remain. An
opening device, bus, plant or portfolio requires included actual capacity or a
paid source. SF, client/pension property, bank redemptions, supplier profits and
expected export orders cannot become one national spending pot.

**PA18 — Forecast uncertainty without inventing acceptance.** The Assembly
can budget and authorize borrowing on disclosed credible forecasts; it need not
preapprove every private market contract. Operators and Treasury execute within
actual law and financing. A ready/funded/delivered claim, however, requires
appropriate dated source, skills, receivers and acceptance evidence. Forecasts,
orders and remaining working-age people are not cleared cash or qualified output.
Missing prices or ownership block a national feasibility claim, not elections,
already due rights or an independent court's existing power. No new Treasury veto.

**PA19 — Migrate through an express coordinated Act.** The Act names exact
ordinary-payment, tax, allowance/offset, wage and record periods, funded overlap,
notices, correction and prior-law successors. The candidate PA start is the
first 1 January at least 12 months after enactment; existing TW wage/week and RF
pension notice clocks retain their own scope. The earlier C0 no-allowance rival cannot remove an existing personal-income
allowance before matching ordinary U is usable. Design 106 SD05 supplies a distinct
I05/positive 0.4M-allowance package under express prospective law and protected
prior-net matching; it does not claim full-U first-unit revenue. Involuntary nonreceipt requires provisional correction/continuity;
nominal access or an AI certificate is not receipt. Freely declined usable
payments, inaccessible routes and unlawful refusal need distinct reasoned treatment.
No work/party/AI condition attaches to ordinary or urgent protection.

Prior accrued/indexed, net-tax-protected, contract, foreign and overdue claims
keep source dates until lawful matched revision/actual replacement. A technical
office cannot adopt this schedule, withhold dues to force reform or permanently
postpone an enacted claim on readiness grounds. No research edition changes
current software, constitution, contribution permission or actual tax law.

**PA20 — Let the full economic choice reject the reference.** Retain I05 plus
services, U with an additional personal-income allowance, lower-rate/higher-allowance
packages and different plural supply/ownership forms as substantive rivals.
They need the same needs/quality/old-claim horizon and explicit incidence,
administration, autonomy and source costs. The current partial price book cannot
select full national financing or prove a superior country by green checks.

Next finish the matched supplier/price/job/asset/source case, including 37-function
substitutions, additional/private discretionary needs, Y cohorts, actual paid
transition and owned foreign settlement, then compare the full objective/rivals.
Do not follow another isolated tax curve or commodity question by default. Reopen
on unsafe work, unreceived needs, distorted prices, exclusion, capture, loss of
political control or a stronger whole alternative. Actual public participation,
professional qualification, safe operation and better lives remain unproved.
