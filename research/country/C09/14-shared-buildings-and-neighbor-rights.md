# C09. Shared buildings without private government

*Design149 · CB01–CB24 · [C09 places](../C09-land-and-ecology.md) · Candidate law, not adopted building rules.*

Select **nonwaivable maintenance, bounded owner financial authority, resident
control of funded everyday common use and direct independent remedies**. A
shared roof/pipe creates dependence without granting a manager sovereignty over
private lives. Retain H's repair/tenure/access, PT's actual title/creditor limits
and B's executed remedies; supply the missing common-property decision rules.

Necessary shared repair is not an optional improvement requiring unanimous
agreement. Conversely, calling a luxury redevelopment a repair does not let a
majority seize units, revise shares, compel loans or evade H/LA. Ownership,
residence, affected scope, maintenance responsibility and public power differ.
Public/nonprofit/cooperative and private housing retain their actual titles
and stronger source protections; no universal new landlord or compulsory club.

Owner concentration, resident turnout and professional/court dependence can
still produce domination, delay or exclusion. Protected necessary owner repair
can benefit private wealth through I03, and has a real public source/incidence
cost; it is not free capital renewal. [Rivals and adversarial routes](../../settlement/shared-buildings/README.md)
preserve simpler contracts, owner-only management, stronger resident co-control,
cooperative/public provision and actual no-change alternatives.

No association crime/fine, public tax, eviction, forced repossession, clinical
power or behavior-scoring authority. AI can assist inspection schedules/accounts
and express alternatives within a real mandate; it cannot supply consent,
qualifications, original evidence or legal judgment. Actual buildings, people,
titles, safe workers, independent reviews, funds and better lives remain unknown.
