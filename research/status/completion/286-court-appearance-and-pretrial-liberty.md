# Design378. Protect pretrial liberty through a usable court process

[CA01–CA07](../../country/C08/12/06-court-appearance-and-pretrial-liberty.md)
selects usable notice, paid attendance support and a separate,
evidence-based review of absence. Actual warrant correction and
continued witness protection have named execution responsibilities.

[Two studies and a constructed docket](../../settlement/court-appearance-and-pretrial-liberty/01-two-missed-court-dates.md)
separate communication, physical access, deliberate evasion, and
whether a correction reaches both desks. Actual authorization,
participation, capacity, safety and life outcomes remain unverified.
