# Observed sources. Ireland's biodiversity deliberation

Inspected on 10 November 2026. These are different stages of one case and
share the original panel recommendations. The stage records establish who
did what and what they reported. They are no independent estimates of effect.

- **Opening, invitation and selection.** Resolutions in February 2022
  established the biodiversity assembly with a chair and 99 members of the
  public. The recruitment announcement records 20,000 randomly addressed
  household invitations for this assembly and 14,000 for the separate Dublin
  assembly. One adult per invited household could apply. Stratification
  operated on those who registered, with eligibility extending beyond the
  electoral register. This establishes a two-stage route and a source of
  nonresponse, not achieved coverage of everyone affected. The official
  selection pages and report were inspected online and could not be locally
  retrieved. [Recruitment announcement](https://citizensassembly.ie/citizens-assembly-broadens-participation-for-upcoming-assemblies-2/)
  · [Selection record](https://citizensassembly.ie/previous-assemblies/citizens-assembly-on-biodiversity-loss/selection-of-members/)
  · [Assembly report, section 2.1](https://citizensassembly.ie/wp-content/uploads/ReportonBiodiversityLoss.pdf)
- **Panel and parliamentary action.** The adult assembly reported 159
  recommendations. The Dáil referred the report on 20 June 2023. A
  December 2023 joint committee report records hearings and its own 86
  recommendations, with a focus on perceived implementation gaps. The
  committee's number has its own scope and denominator. It is neither a
  floor vote count nor the number of panel items implemented. [Assembly
  launch record](https://citizensassembly.ie/report-of-the-citizens-assembly-on-biodiversity-loss-report-launches/)
  · [Committee report, foreword and introduction](https://data.oireachtas.ie/ie/oireachtas/committee/dail/33/joint_committee_on_environment_and_climate_action/reports/2023/2023-12-14_report-on-the-examination-of-recommendations-of-the-citizens-assembly-report-on-biodiversity-loss_en.pdf)
  · [Archived committee original](original-sources/ireland-committee.pdf)
- **Executive status.** The National Parks and Wildlife Service reports that
  the government adopted its response on 30 October 2024, covering the
  adult assembly's 159 recommendations and the separate children and young
  people's assembly's 58 calls to action. It describes over 90% as
  implemented or in progress and explains the status in terms of elements
  incorporated into existing policies and strategies. This is a reported
  classification. Specific completion, changes due to the panel and
  attributable ecological or human outcomes remain unverified.
  [Official release](https://www.npws.ie/news/government-publishes-response-recommendations-citizens%E2%80%99-assembly-biodiversity-loss)
  · [Archived release](original-sources/ireland-executive-status.html)

We infer that a recommendation record, an elected decision, funding and
subsequent work should be traced separately. The case supplies no direct
comparison with a well-supported ordinary hearing and no estimate for the
proposed frequency, support budget or deadlines.
