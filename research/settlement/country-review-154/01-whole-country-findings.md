## Priorities and rejected shortcuts

1. **Positive repair now:** PO ordinary civilian police, actual head selection/
   removal, lawful case/operation separation, distinct external inquiry employer,
   director-own-case path and named corrections administration. A force ceiling
   alone cannot identify who hires/fires/controllers evidence/pays an investigator.
2. **Quantitative/source unfinished:** join current country obligations and paid
   independent capacity/source/old claims in a credible bounded operating/handover
   comparison. Earlier reference statistics and consumer fees cannot price all
   duties. New PO source is unknown, not assumed absorbed by old37 bids.
3. **Actual context/authority/operation/outcomes unverified:** actual public,
   independence, qualifications, usable legal/currency sources and received
   remedies must be qualified for implementation. Their absence is not proof
   that no country design can be completed without already building a state.
4. **Value/structure conflict:** retain simpler protected careers, locally
   controlled police, direct election, Cabinet-only leadership, unarmed civilian
   alternatives and different source/provider/income choices. PO's confirmations
   and separate employer have real cost/deadlock/capture risks and can lose.

Neither14 entrances nor37 original function rows close the universe of needs.
This finite inspection does not certify every clause/code annex, future social
form or a superior country. Material new evidence/urgent harm can reprioritize.
