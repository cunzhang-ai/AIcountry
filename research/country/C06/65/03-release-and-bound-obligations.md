# JC09–JC12. Release choices and binding obligations

[Joint continuity](../65-joint-continuity-and-first-budget.md)

**JC09 — Vote on the baseline, first releases and alternatives by
their own authority.** The elected budget distinguishes due and
accrued work, binding contracts, scoped initial releases and options
that remain available for a later decision. Where a material optional
release depends on a joint scenario, record its exact eligible
scope, decision maker, financial cap, evidence trigger, last useful
start and expiry. Specify the alternative for a documented loss
or require a new vote. Distinguish mutually exclusive authority
from approvals that can be exercised together. Commitments to
multiple possible routes need their actual option and standby costs.
An option is no permission to commit each underlying route.

**JC10 — Show a schedule that can operate under the same conditions.**
Treasury and responsible service owners reconcile cumulative cash
before payments, authorized purposes and counterparties, protected
balance, qualified work and acceptance in each dated window.
Trace dependencies at the actual source, place and controller,
including a paid alternate. Count simultaneous claims once.
Preserve each local, independent and sector obligation under its
own law. Record post-window recurring costs and commitments with
their lawful sources. Before a discretionary act becomes binding,
show funded continuation and lawful exit costs if the declared
material stress follows the act. If the adopted release conditions
are unmet, seek a funded alternative or a fresh lawful choice. If one measure fits only by spending a
later receipt, using a worker in two roles or assuming a shared
backup survives, publish the actual gap. Separate uncertain
inputs and binding conditions from modelled feasibility.

**JC11 — Respond to loss before and after commitment.** The named
responsible officer verifies the observed trigger and records the
action within the prior elected limits. An unstarted optional
measure can remain unstarted or use the exact funded alternative
approved in advance. The officer cannot change the elected
priority, redirect protected resources or cancel a binding act
without its own lawful authority. Once a worker, counterparty
or recipient holds a binding claim, carry it and any lawful
exit costs in the continuing account. Use actual standing
protection and seek timely elected correction of the remaining
gap. Unpaid claims remain visible. A delayed receipt, adverse
forecast or model update does not undo a prior lawful commitment.

**JC12 — Make the comparison reviewable and revisable.** Present
what each real alternative can deliver, what people would wait
for, which duties fail and which earlier approvals and rights
continue. Preserve the prior schedule and factual correction
history. Use existing independent scrutiny and direct review,
including protected access to originals and paid execution
before remedies lose value. AI or simple calculation can expose
conflicts but cannot choose a budget, verify its own authority
or settle a claim. Use existing DB, FB, IV and GP scope, lawful
migration and FT/OP/T. Reopen on a material evidence, cost,
source, capacity or authority change or a better qualified rival.
