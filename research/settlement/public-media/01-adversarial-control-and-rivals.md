# Adversarial public-media situations and unresolved costs

Design146 · Authored reasoning, not observed cases or legal decisions

| Situation | Candidate response and retained failure |
|---|---|
| Government wants flattering coverage | One Assembly-appointed seat supplies no formal board majority or news-desk instruction. Contacts/interests and actual retaliation remain reviewable. Public panels/staff can still share its controllers. |
| Workforce wants to protect a corrupt newsroom | Three seats cannot pass a four-vote act alone; independent complaints/courts and actual public sources remain. Collusion across classes can still capture the institution. |
| All public trustees agree while workers object | Three votes alone fail; parity is real, not a chair's casting vote. This can block a useful reform, an explicit cost rather than “consensus” by silence. |
| One board seat is vacant/recused | Five lawful participants can still give four assents; no source-specific veto. Four present with four yes cannot act. Existing actual editor/desk/source duties continue; no invented trustee. |
| First institution has no workforce | Only the enacted lawful recruitment/source mandate may fill genuine paid jobs, whose actual workers elect trustees. Public trustees alone cannot form a quorum or let a founder act as editor. Actual failure can prevent launch. |
| A chief's term ends during a new scandal | Bounded career continuity expressly permits fresh normal investigations; after acting expiry actual desks retain their own lawful funded journalism. New senior/whole-policy powers stay unfilled, no eternal acting chief. |
| A minister withholds the monthly installment | Standing law gives an actual due claim and B19 source/execution remedies. Index release delay does not veto payment. Missing real cash/joint capture still prevents delivery; a court cannot print it. |
| Public media's funding harms plural competitors | No ads/donations or privileged mandatory carriage; general MP/PD/SC rules apply. Crowding, source cost and dominant agenda power remain real objections; compare a no-central-outlet arrangement at review. |
| Government buys ads only from friendly private outlets | Objective public-purpose audience/access/cost procurement and quarterly original spend/control disclosure, outside independent review, no editorial condition. This constrains public buyers without making private journalism a licensed activity. |
| The complaints editor calls a disputed inference false | Finding, limits and chief response are published; no sovereign truth verdict or automatic takedown. Actual harmed parties retain direct ordinary rights review; privacy/source duties survive. |
| An AI feed omits a minority's framing | Preserve original meaning, attribution and objection, disclose losses and offer a nonprofiled/direct route. Copies agreeing are not corroboration or consent. No requirement that every private assistant share one narrative. |
| Parliament chooses another public-information architecture | Genuine prospective law may replace the body while preserving protected functions, actual terms/holds and paid successors. Renaming to remove critics or changing a budget rider is not lawful replacement. |

A temporary panel, editor or court can itself be captured. Real records, source,
appointment/removal independence, alternative reach, actual execution and safe
participant accounts are required. No authored journey or successful numerical
case demonstrates any of those exist. The intended country remains unfinished.
