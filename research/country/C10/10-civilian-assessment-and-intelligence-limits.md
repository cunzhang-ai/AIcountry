# C10. Civilian assessment, warning and the limits of intelligence power

1 November2026 · Integrated design102 · Research selection, not enacted law

Select a civilian National Assessment Unit inside ordinary government for lawful
strategic/open-source/diplomatic analysis and actual warning. Do **not** establish
a separate domestic covert service or new noncriminal intrusive collection power.
This is a substantive security/liberty choice with a lost hidden-threat capability,
not a claim that all threats are public or that powers are merely awaiting an appendix.

A warning neither authorizes a search nor proves guilt. Existing independently
lawful police/criminal, defense, diplomatic and cyber functions remain in their
own actor/ground/source scope. Foreign location, a contract or PD notice delay
creates no extra power. The proposed adopting Act must actually sunset/repeal
incompatible predecessor covert authorities; merely renaming an old case cannot
preserve the rejected architecture indefinitely. Present actual law/software and
constitutional/contribution holds are not amended by this research text.
