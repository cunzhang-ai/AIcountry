# C02. Personal and collective rights

*Part of [C02](../C02-membership-and-rights.md); integrated design 96 research, not enacted law.*

[PD01–PD32](07-private-life-and-personal-data.md) now specifies ordinary personal-
data bases, controller duties, rights and independent remedies across media;
sector powers and collective rights retain their own scope.

All persons have bodily autonomy, due process, privacy, expression, association,
religious freedom, protection against discrimination and standing to challenge harm.
Children have independent interests and supported voice. Disability and language
differences require access, not lower civic status. Care and dependency do not reduce
political worth. Families and voluntary communities may take different lawful forms.
The [equal-standing topic](05-equal-standing-and-differentiated-needs.md)
specifies compounded exclusion, accommodation, lawful distinctions and
privacy; [C08](../C08/11-equality-law-and-effective-remedies.md) assigns
enforcement rather than leaving this guarantee as an aspiration.
The [C08 reproductive-autonomy route](../C08/06-reproductive-autonomy-and-parentage.md)
applies bodily integrity to pregnancy, contraception and fertility care
while preserving each born child's independent status and remedy.
The [end-of-life route](../C08/08-end-of-life-decisions-and-survivors.md)
also applies bodily integrity, supported choice and equal standing during
serious illness and after a death; a prognosis or dependant status does not
turn one person's will into another person's property.
The [mental-health crisis rule](../C08/09-mental-health-crisis-liberty-and-consent.md)
keeps liberty, legal capacity and political standing independent of diagnosis,
distress or an AI risk score. Immediate safety powers need their own law.
The [interpersonal-safety rule](../C08/10-interpersonal-violence-protection-and-fair-process.md)
protects bodily and family life against private coercion while preserving
respondent fair process and each child's independent standing.

People can use, teach and sustain their language, culture and religion together,
and obtain practical language access to courts, political information and essential
services. Protection follows the person even outside a concentrated home region.
Voluntary cultural and educational institutions may receive public support under
fair, reviewable rules without becoming the compulsory representative of everyone
with a similar ancestry, language or belief. Self-identification is protected;
neither a group officer nor the state may impose membership, silence internal
dissent or make a person's ordinary rights depend on loyalty to a community body.
Distinct Indigenous and other historically grounded collective land, governance
and treaty claims require their own lawful recognition, participation and remedy
path. They are not reduced to an ethnic-service grant or resolved by an ordinary
majority vote. The actual holders, territory and applicable law cannot be named
by this research project.

[AX01–AX20](../C03/18-peaceful-assembly-and-public-dissent.md) now specifies
ordinary peaceful assembly/public dissent across physical, remote and future
forms: no permit or compulsory notice, actual accessible civic forums, safe
recording/direct review and bounded restrictions. Nationality/voting eligibility
and missing organizer do not erase basic participation; actual private rights,
property/safety, specific source law and constitutional holds retain scope.
