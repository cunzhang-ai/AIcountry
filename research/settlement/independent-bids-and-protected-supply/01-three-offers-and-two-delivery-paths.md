# Three offers and two delivery paths

Design366 · Constructed comparison under
[SC38–SC43](../../country/C04/05/10-independent-bids-and-protected-supply.md).

A public buyer needs four qualified service shifts in the first
window and two standby shifts in the next if the primary team
fails. The published call permits distinct primary and reserve
awards and requires the reserve to withstand the loss of the
primary controller and its team. A and B share decisive control
and one actual crew. C has a separately accepting qualified team.
C and A use the same accountant. The buyer verifies from primary
records that this does not give the accountant power over
commercial decisions or delivery in the declared case. None of
these facts alone establishes unlawful bidding conduct.

| Option | Gross cost | Window one shifts | Window two if A's team is unavailable |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A primary and B reserve | 4 + 2 = 6 | 4 | 0; B offers the same team |
| A primary and C reserve | 4 + 3 = 7 | 4 | 2 under the declared qualified acceptance |
| A primary only | 4 | 4 | 0 |

Three responsive labels yield two verified economic units in the
case. A's award is possible if otherwise qualified on the prior
terms. A and B cannot satisfy the independent-reserve requirement
together. A blanket rule excluding every bidder with a shared
business service could remove C. Fair reply and verified original
facts prevent that mistake. The second row is preferred when
seven lawful units, actual contracts and accepting staff are
available.

If only six units are usable, the cheaper dependent arrangement
still fails the requirement. Use a qualified, separately authorized
and funded bridge only within SC12, or return the price, narrower
lawful choices and lost service to the competent elected body.
The example assumes no additional bridge is available. The
scenario makes no claim about an earlier failure before the
reserve's agreed window. Preserve service and bids under their
actual lawful terms; an unenforced guarantee or later recovery
does not pay for a replacement.

The comparison establishes no real price, unlawful coordination,
availability, award, receipt or remedy.
