# Design433. Carry corrections across material uses

[EV25–EV30](../../country/C11/11/03-scoped-evidence-correction-across-decisions.md)
assigns discovery, scoped reassessment, independent challenge and
receipt without cancelling unrelated work or continuing obligations.
The [working method](../../FRAMEWORK.md#6-maintain-valid-support-and-continuing-duties)
uses the same distinction for human and AI research.

[Worked comparison](../../settlement/scoped-evidence-correction-across-decisions/01-one-source-and-four-decisions.md)
tests four distinct effects of one correction. Actual participation,
authority, staff capacity and outcomes remain open.
