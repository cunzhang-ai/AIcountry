# Design473 completion finding

The [shorter-week comparison](../../settlement/matched-national-work/03-who-pays-for-the-shorter-week.md)
uses original matched 2025 statistics and replays two stages across
eleven historical years using a distinct fixed-calendar mechanism.
A consolidated positive balance can coexist with negative local
balances. A funded earlier stage cannot supply the later increment.
Retain exact local sources, actual qualified work and elected
commencement. Actual costs, national delivery and better lives
remain open.
