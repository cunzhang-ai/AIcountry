# Democratic revision: choices that can displace the reference

*Design 83 alternatives, not automatically combined layers.*

| Choice | Causal case and cost | Selection / what would change it |
|---|---|---|
| Blanket structural delay | Simple categorical anti-self-dealing limit, but may enforce another exclusionary contest and freeze unusable remedies | Retained as rival, displaced as default for narrow already protected restoration only; prefer it if exception classification is consistently captured. |
| Narrow RR exception | Removes a defined barrier under old-law review and unchanged public vote, with no packing/self-extension | Selected conditional reference; can fail under captured old reviewers or an exclusionary old electorate. Actual effective restoration versus gaming must discriminate. |
| Broad constituent repair | Can replace an already captured structure rather than ask it to judge itself | No standing incumbent shortcut. A legitimate affected constituent settlement is a different authority question; its absence is not cured by a repair label. |
| Exhaustive reason review | Every genuinely distinct accepted reason assured substantive attention | Rejected default under unlimited novelty because completion may never occur; bounded scientific/specialist inquiries may still justify deeper scope. |
| Elected representative scrutiny | Fewer extra structures, clearer elected responsibility and lower participation burden | Remains ordinary default outside mandatory FA scope and a live national rival; concentrated staff/party agenda and source access must be compared. |
| FA finite portfolio | Mixes consequence-led, opposition/affected and human-drawn agenda access; finite time and honest unexamined issues | Selected for major national consultation only. Small draws, intake limits, mediator and sponsor framing can still exclude important people. |
| Distributed situation-based forums | Better local context, practical relationships and multiple communication forms; no single global consensus required | Lawful local alternative and substantive national rival. Requires actual settlement of shared resources/rights rather than pretending data convergence does it. |

The choice is value-laden: who bears delay, uncertainty and exclusion cannot be
settled by response speed, token counts, a welfare average or a clean file check.
FA's numbers are hypotheses with explicit lawful amendment routes; neither OECD
guidance nor Venice principles prescribe this country's thresholds or establish
public consent. Different models may be preferable at different scale/context.

Reopen on actual restoration denied, disguised incumbent benefit, manipulated
sampling, lost meaning, controlled sources, excessive effort, inaccessible legal
correction or a better complete rival. Compare real original observations of
access, understanding, influence, time/care burden, rights, opposition and peaceful
replacement, with affected people and qualified independent review. Those actual
observations and legitimate authority are currently absent. No extra committee
is the automatic answer to a committee's failure.
