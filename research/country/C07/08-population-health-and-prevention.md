# C07. Population health, prevention and public trust

*Part of [C07 social floor](../C07-social-floor.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

A country cannot rely on clinical treatment after preventable harm or on
emergency restrictions after an outbreak. The Assembly therefore establishes
an ordinary **public-health duty** alongside, but distinct from, each
person's clinical entitlement. Its purpose is to identify and reduce avoidable
population harm while preserving equal standing, privacy, voluntary life and
accountable local action. A lower disease count can mean better prevention,
missing people or suppressed reporting; a healthy average cannot erase an
excluded neighborhood. No health office gets a general power to govern work,
housing, speech or movement by naming a risk.

## Ordinary roles before an emergency

Prior Assembly law defines the public-health service's functions, legal data
bases, powers, funding and independent challenge. A national civilian
public-health service maintains laboratory reference capacity, common
methods, cross-place analysis, supply and professional support, and any
international notification required by applicable law. Its leadership is
chosen through published qualifications, conflict review, recorded elected
scrutiny and reasoned, reviewable removal; it answers to elected health
policy and budget, but may publish source-backed technical findings without
cabinet clearance. Local elected authorities maintain reachable public-health
teams for outreach, community assessment, prevention and place-specific
response under national rights and equalization. Their professionals cannot
be dismissed for a lawful warning or made immune from correction. C11's
separately governed evidence service protects original data and methods;
this is not one national personal-health dossier or an unelected health veto.

The health service offers a lawful, evidence-reviewed prevention programme
across the life course: immunization access, early detection where benefit
and harm justify it, infection prevention, injury and mental-health
promotion, and help to reduce avoidable risks. The Assembly chooses material
coverage, payer, eligibility and revision procedure; qualified clinicians
judge individual benefit, informed consent and contraindications under C07.
An offer recorded in a registry is not a received vaccine, a completed test
or improved health. Publicly funded outreach needs interpreters, accessible
sites, time away from work, safe transport and non-digital routes. Consent
and the rights of children and supported adults retain their C02/C08 rules.
A [mental-health promotion programme](11-mental-health-substance-use-and-crisis-care.md)
can address isolation, violence and harmful environments and offer help;
population surveillance does not grant a diagnosis, detention or forced
treatment power over an individual.
A general compulsory intervention requires separate enacted grounds and
rights review; a programme budget or model recommendation grants no mandate.

Public health also depends on air, water, food, housing, work and sanitation.
The [C09 food-system route](../C09/09-food-systems-and-nutrition.md)
connects safe and adequate diets to production and access; public-health
advice does not grant an office power to assign what a household eats.
Sector stewards and inspectors keep their own legal standards, source evidence
and enforcement duties; the public-health service can identify cross-sector
patterns and recommend correction but cannot secretly waive a utility limit,
close a workplace or order a household search. The [C09 sanitation
chain](../C09/06-sanitation-and-waste.md) and environmental duties remain
separately accountable. When a landlord, employer or supplier controls access
to prevention or reporting, residents and workers need a direct, protected
public route. Public communication gives the evidence, uncertainty,
alternatives and correction history in usable languages and formats. It does
not make scientific disagreement an offense or treat a state slogan as trust.

## Observation with bounded data and powers

Law names the observations needed for population surveillance, including
laboratory signals, deaths, service gaps, environmental exposure and original
community accounts. Collect the least identifiable data able to answer a
specified public-health question; record lawful source, purpose, access,
retention, sharing and deletion or archive rule. Identifiable records stay
with their lawful clinical, local or sector custodian where possible. Linkage
needs a defined legal basis, necessity, independent privacy review and a
route to correct material error. Routine data cannot be repurposed for
immigration, political policing, employment exclusion or commercial scoring
by an informal data-sharing request. Publish methods, missing groups and
uncertainty without exposing a person or a small stigmatized community.
AI may help detect a signal or translate advice; it cannot declare a disease,
coerce a person, change a right or turn uncertain correlation into guilt.

An unusual signal prompts verification with original observations and
alternative explanations. Local teams offer proportionate help and clinical
referral; the national service assists with laboratories, stock and
cross-place tracing. **Observation, technical risk judgment, individual
clinical care, regulatory enforcement and compulsory restriction are
different acts.** An official who proposes isolation, testing, closure or
movement limits must use the specific [C10 enacted route](../C10/01-public-safety-and-emergencies.md):
evidence of a concrete serious threat, necessity, affected scope, minimum
duration, essential support, reasons, independent remedy and continuing
elected review when effects become broad. A data alert does not extend an
emergency clock. Ordinary voluntary prevention and sector safety orders can
continue after extraordinary powers expire. Cross-border notice and
cooperation follow actual treaty and legal obligations; neither WHO guidance
nor this research file establishes AIcountry's Party status.

The [C06 public-health account](../C06/09-prevention-and-health-security-finance.md)
funds local teams, laboratories, safe data stewardship, outreach, vaccines
and stock separately from clinical visits and emergency command. Staff,
transport, cold chain, test capacity and communications are counted once
across routine work and surge plans. An apparently cheap intervention that
pushes unpaid time, stigma or an unfunded mandate onto people is not free.
The public-conditions account reports actual reach, avoidable harm, time to
warning and correction, unequal exposure, consent and coercion complaints,
and delivered remedies with known uncertainty. A high test count or rapid
AI warning alone is not better health. This route has no observed AIcountry
population, law, staff, budget or outcome; reopen it if surveillance harms
exceed benefit, care is displaced, excluded groups remain invisible or an
alternative governance form works better under actual public control.

**Design144 rights join.** [RX01–RX16](../C10/01-public-safety-and-emergencies.md)
selects no domestic constitutional derogation. Prior-law health restrictions need
right-specific justification and their own actual order/treatment/data authority;
a declaration extends neither them nor DR13's urgent calendar-day7 ceiling.
New broad population movement/participation restrictions require the express RX08
ordinary sunset/reassessment/primary-law continuation rule. Prevention and due
clinical/support duties continue after exceptional powers end; paid capacity is
not established by this join.

**Design147 positive powers/data join.** [HP01–HP24](../C10/11-ordinary-health-and-hazard-protection.md)
now supplies the grave-hazard notification duty, qualified career officer/court
source, supported voluntary protection, bounded individual/place restrictions and
express no-forced-body/no-general-home-confinement endpoints. HP does not turn
routine prevention/data into coercion; notification has minimal PD purpose and
safe retention. Actual professional capacity/source and independent receiving
remain necessary; an alert/WHO recommendation/AI label authorizes none.

[AQ07](../C09/24/02-correction-and-acute-protection.md) gives smoke
and other acute episodes a funded receiving route, independent of how
the episode is counted for legal compliance. Existing clinical and
coercive powers retain their distinct scope.
