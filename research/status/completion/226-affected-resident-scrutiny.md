# Affected residents and national scrutiny

Design318 · Scoped result for [country completion](../../DESIGN-COMPLETENESS.md)

[AR01–AR05](../../country/C03/16/05-hearing-people-without-a-national-vote.md)
protects affected testimony and a timely response when an ordinary
national proposal directly changes the legal position of people
without a national vote. Individual claims and the elected decision
remain separate.

The [comparison](../../settlement/affected-residents-and-national-decisions/01-voice-before-national-voting.md)
reproduces the authored interpreting and response limits. The real
petition and electoral examples establish distinct models. Actual
adoption, safe access, paid participation and outcomes remain open.
