# FP09–FP12: no fiscal gap may silently choose the country's politics

*Part of [C06/56](../56-national-fiscal-settlement-and-source-boundaries.md); design 138 candidate.*

**FP09 — Existing elected authority chooses burdens.** Cabinet presents
current needs, sources, uncertainty and genuine tax/spending/investment/debt/
external alternatives to the Assembly. Local elected bodies retain LR scope
and NF source ownership. Existing primary law, appropriations, confidence,
caretaker, notice and borrowing rules apply; this creates no second fiscal
legislature or technical launch licence. Disclose affected losers, excluded
needs and unexamined alternatives without exposing private testimony.

Opposition must have funded access, source scrutiny, useful-time independent
remedy and peaceful replacement. No finance office, central bank, actuarial
adviser, public provider, creditor or AI may turn a diagnostic threshold into
a sovereign tax/cut, new money power, opponent ban or election delay. Their
appointment, budget, information and enforcement independence remain actual
questions, including capture of supposed protectors. No model supplies public assent.

**FP10 — Rights and actual execution remain visible during fiscal failure.**
Keep actual U/I05/Y, services, worker wages/remittances/pensions, contracts,
refunds, creditors and court duties due under their own law. Disputed amounts
and missing source preserve independent claims and interim useful-time duties.
Existing E/NP/CP lawful protection/scarcity rules and court/execution routes
retain exact scope. No office may rewrite priorities because a spreadsheet
fails; no text or court record can manufacture unavailable money or staff.

Disclose the shortfall and execute actually authorized available protection,
with unfulfilled/displaced duties recorded. Use existing elected fiscal
resolution rather than automatic nominal benefit cuts, forced family care,
unpaid suppliers, covert arrears or an unreceived loan. Unlawful refusal,
legitimate disagreement and absent material capacity remain distinct problems.
Neither missing facts nor a clock creates consent to a new policy.

**FP11 — Carry the financial and human transition.** A proposed expansion
needs opening lawful source/title, gross advances/refunds, paid overlap,
qualified receiving and future debt/maintenance/claim tails. Restriction,
currency, counterparty acceptance and timing survive aggregation. A longer
planning horizon is not permission to omit later earned units or change a
claim's source year. New retirement restraints remain prospective under Y/RF;
all prior units/indexation/survivors and stronger source-law rights continue.

Existing OP/T authorities map people, records, cases, title/leases/licenses,
stock, contracts, workers, deposits/creditors and foreign claims to paid lawful
successors. A preferred architecture or renamed model does not extinguish
commenced U, arrears or a supplier loss. Do not promise costless or permanently
unpriced compensation. Missing new-launch resources do not postpone old due dates.

**FP12 — Keep assistance, evidence and consent separate.** Existing Treasury,
Revenue, sector/local bodies, EV, AA and courts retain their distinct mandates;
source/payment, observation, audit, legal judgment and execution are different
work. Bounded agents and future communication may improve understanding and
coordination, without multiplying mandates, merging custody or transferring
old reviews to changed financial choices. No mandatory global public family/
patient/source graph, central AI fiscal decision or technical-dossier condition
on ordinary reports is introduced. Actual independent people, protected
original evidence and paid receiving—not model/account counts—are required.

[DB01–DB20](../60-democratic-budget-and-execution.md) now supplies ordinary
elected amendment/commitment/payment/execution mechanics using existing actual
roles. FP boundaries, actual qualified source/old rights and paid successors
remain; a better procedure is not the missing full-country source package.
