# Transition must preserve lives while whole choices remain real

Use CX's existing-community retrofit: people already hold homes, jobs, claims,
relationships and institutions. A new-settlement rival must pay for absent assets,
settlement, services and obligations, not combine free retained property with no
old creditors. No real territory, residents, sovereign permission or partner treaty
is supplied by this reference. Its 100,000 people/50,000 occupied homes are authored
planning assumptions, not authority to register or relocate anyone.

| Whole choice | Practical gains to investigate | Costs/power/disagreement that remain |
|---|---|---|
| Repair existing rights with I05/positive allowance | Direct resources toward actual gaps while retaining essential services and old claims | Assessment burden, privacy, nonclaimants and receipt mistakes; no presumption that targeting is more legitimate |
| Protected full U plus extra needs | Stable individual ordinary resources and less repeated means assessment | Complete tax/price/source incidence and transition; SG retains U as a substantive rival, no presumed destination |

| Paid new settlement or a regionally organized country | Different spatial/territorial accountability and construction choices | New title/capital, consent, ecology, inherited/external claims and actual geography; no free sovereignty or universal design winner |

Compare the same people's rights/needs, ordinary/private purchases, service quality,
work/rest, political powers, physical exposures and continuing liabilities. Do not
credit a competitor with cheaper demand, free acquisitions or cancelled pensions.
Compatible differences stay visible; a value dispute over ownership or universality
cannot be settled by averaging a fiscal score. Retain a justified no-change choice.

Before replacing an AI/provider or opening a new service, OP/Tnames the old and
successor duty, actual payer/source/date, custody, workers, wages, pensions, contracts,
creditors, pending remedies, overlap and safe acceptance. Paid instructor/release/
maintenance/fallback work enters once. A certificate cannot suspend an existing
or commenced right. New legal coverage starts only on a competent exact Act/date;
source tax receipts beginning later cannot be presumed to pay earlier wages.
Old accrued and stronger actual rights survive; lawful future change has express
process and continuing claims, rather than impossible institutional immutability.

The 127 portfolio's food/water/power bills, new staff/capital/owned foreign calls
and 128 democratic peaks must reach the same current provider/personal/tax/source
account before a country-year claim. A CUloan is not the supplier's owned foreign
currency; public wages recycled through tax are not independent output or first-day
cash. A larger reserve list is not accepted safe capacity. Normal operating cost,
renewal, capital purchase, principal, interest, custody and arrears stay distinct.

During an outage, use prior lawful offline allocations, original receipts and
nonoverlapping reservations. On reconnection preserve meanings, revocations,
individual objections and valid earlier commitments; last-arriving data cannot
decide who loses a right. Replacing a mediator or collaboration method is possible
without erasing obligations. Informal cooperation and stable institutions may be
better than further redesign; neither AI novelty nor formalization proves progress.
