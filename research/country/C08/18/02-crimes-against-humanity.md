# Attack context and crimes against humanity

*G03; part of [C08/18](../18-international-crimes-and-accountability.md).*

**G03 — Context plus an actual prohibited act.** Intentionally commit one
of the following acts as part of a widespread **or** systematic attack
directed against a civilian population, knowing the attack and the act's
connection. Attack means multiple prohibited acts pursuant to/furthering
a state or organizational policy; it need not be a military assault or occur
during war. Prove scope/organization/policy and knowledge with original,
challengeable facts. A lone ordinary wrong, a named organization, poverty
or a government policy one opposes does not establish this context.

| Prohibited act | Required material boundary |
|---|---|
| Intentional killing | Actual intentional causing of a person's death without lawful justification |
| Extermination | Intentionally impose lethal conditions, including deprivation of food/medicine, calculated to destroy part of a population; prove conduct and the required population-scale result/context |
| Enslavement | Exercise powers of ownership over people, including trafficking/control imposing slavery or servitude; ordinary voluntary work/debt alone is not it |
| Deportation/forcible transfer | Coercively displace people lawfully present without an applicable lawful ground; coercion includes grave threats/abuse of actual power, not freely chosen movement |
| Severe deprivation of liberty | Imprisonment or comparable confinement contrary to fundamental applicable law; identify the actual liberty loss and absence/abuse of authority |
| Torture | Intentionally inflict severe physical/mental suffering on a person in custody/control without a lawful rights-compatible justification; a disciplinary or lawful-sanction label never legalizes torture |
| Sexual/reproductive violence | Rape, sexual slavery, forced prostitution, forced sterilization, comparably grave sexual violence; unlawfully confine a forcibly impregnated person intending to affect a population's ethnic composition or commit another defined grave G crime; or intentionally terminate a pregnancy against the person's valid will without a rights-compatible medical justification |
| Persecution | Intentionally and severely deprive fundamental rights because of political, racial, national, ethnic, cultural, religious, sex, gender, gender-identity, sexual-orientation status or another ground expressly protected against such deprivation by prior enacted equality law, connected to this attack/prohibited act; disagreement or criticism alone is insufficient |
| Enforced disappearance | State/political-organization-authorized abduction/detention followed by intentional refusal/false information about fate/whereabouts to remove a person from legal protection for a prolonged period; no registry deletion or hospital transfer erases the original act |
| Apartheid | Inhumane acts within institutionalized systematic racial domination/oppression, intentionally maintaining that regime; prove acts, structure and purpose, not a label alone |
| Severe intentional injury | Intentionally cause serious bodily/mental injury or great suffering of comparable gravity, with an actual causal act and this attack context; P02's serious harm and fault boundaries apply, not a residual authority to criminalize unpopular policy or invent a lower harm threshold |

Gender protections are inclusive national choices under C02; the older UN
Article 7(3) wording is not silently imported to narrow them. The forced-
abortion and expanded persecution formulation also reflects explicit national
selection, not a claim that every detail is universally recognized for every
foreign-jurisdiction purpose. G09 requires a separate actual international
basis where presence-only jurisdiction relies on that recognition.
Lawful freely chosen reproductive care retains C08/06's protections; neither
this definition nor an attack label creates a partner/state veto over it.

An actual inability to deliver resources is not intentional extermination
or policy knowledge by itself. Deliberate deprivation/selection with the
required control, intent, attack and consequences is not excused by calling
it a budget/AI error. P02/P03 identify genuine choices, usable capacity,
knowledge and contribution; unrelated employees' information is not combined
into fictional intent. Civil relief, urgent care, food and liberty receiving
do not require a G03 conviction or surrender of the victim's private evidence.
