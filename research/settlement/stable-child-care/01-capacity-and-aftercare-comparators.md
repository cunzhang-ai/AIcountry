# Observed capacity and a transition comparator

[Stable child care](README.md)

England's Department for Education reports simultaneously that many
children's homes have unfilled places and that some children are far
from home or have placements that fit poorly. In Table 3 the estimated
mean journey from the previous home to the children's home was
50 minutes for children of North West home authorities and 157 for
South West home authorities on 31 March 2025. These means exclude
outliers under the study method. They cover children in children's
homes, not all children in care. The report does not determine
the right placement for each child or measure how many local places
were displaced by another authority's purchases. The evidence
supports distinguishing suitability and workforce from bed counts.
[Department for Education sufficiency report, introduction and Table 3](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/understanding-childrens-social-care-sufficiency-in-england/understanding-childrens-social-care-sufficiency-in-england)

England's statutory guidance for local authorities describes the local
offer, accommodation duties and access to an adviser on request for
eligible young adults up to age 25. This gives a real institutional
comparison for support after legal adulthood. It does not qualify
the proposed housing guarantee, its age limits or its outcomes.
[Care leaver local offer, accommodation and adviser duties](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/local-offer-guidance/care-leaver-local-offer-guidance-for-local-authorities-effective-from-30-september-2026)

The proposed breadth of eligibility, voluntary continuation and
local public residual are design choices. The case's ages and costs
are declared inputs. Use original protected receipt, qualified staff,
child and care-leaver experience, and actual cost before a real
appropriation or effectiveness finding.
