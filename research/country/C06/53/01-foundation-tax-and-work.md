# One allowance, with the cost of the choice visible

**PW01 — Revise the preferred conditional U tax counterpart.** C2 keeps U
outside ordinary personal tax and worked-wage Y. For other TA-defined annual
receipts Q after lawful worked-income Y, use **T(max(0,Q−0.4M))**. Retain PA10's
20/35/45/55% rates, 0.5M/0.5M/1M/unlimited widths and protected definition/update
of M. One individual allowance, nonrefundable, nontransferable and not multiplied
by employers, accounts, agents or marital status. U is not another I05 resource.
Y basic, existing pension accrual, residual extra needs and tax attribution
still match once. This replaces C1's preferred status, not its historical cases.

[Design155 TN](../59/02-source-choice-and-real-alternatives.md) retains this
conditional C2-versus-taper value preference and exact benchmark arithmetic,
but withdraws PA/SD's default numerical replacement preference for the country.
A complete source/paid-transition choice, not C1's yield or R=0, is required.

The reason is a value choice: prefer one transparent progressive schedule to
an additional allowance withdrawal that creates a high marginal-rate region.
C1 raises more from higher receipts; C2 gives those recipients more and requires
more source. Neither is optimal by arithmetic. C1, C0, a revised ordinary scale,
and different service/income mixes remain genuine alternatives for public choice.

**PW02 — Do not describe the change as self-financing.** Fresh calculation on
the four current 130 U cases reduces assessed PA by **17.1504–17.24544 million
fictional CU/year**, with unchanged modeled I05 and U. The same amount increases
net annual G personal incidence in this fixed-price/received-wage comparison.
Every named G stress still first lacks funds on 1 January 2030. No behavioral
growth, new tax, saving, issue, export or reduced old entitlement fills that call.
This is not an actual national deficit, revenue forecast or launch judgment.

**PW03 — Retain I10's cumulative-incidence duty and make this failure concrete.**
At 60,000 gross, M=30,000, worked Y=10%, active I05=50%, and fixed recognized
necessary costs high enough to keep I05 payable, C1 retains 0.1665 of the next
gross CU; C2 retains 0.2475. This is a residual necessary-cost stress after
separate payments, not withdrawal of independently protected disability support.
Ordinary recipients whose I05 has ended do not face that combined withdrawal.
At other wages C2 with active I05 can still retain only 0.2025: the basic burden
has not disappeared. These are local cash slopes, not observed work responses,
all-in consumption incidence, benefit adequacy or proof that more paid work is
always the right outcome. Paid rest, care, learning and voluntary nonwork matter.

Keep the existing I05 0.5 withdrawal/0.2B disregard pending a whole cost/receipt/
extra-needs comparison; do not add a nominal “maximum rate” promise without a
funded correction rule. Address necessary work/care costs before treating extra
gross pay as usable freedom. No forced household pooling or compulsory work.

**PW04 — Prospective migration only.** Express coordinated primary law and
TA15/PA19 notice/source-year protections remain prerequisites. If U commences,
the existing eligibility date and first-year A protection remain. C2 uses the
same allowance function as A, so no following-year allowance withdrawal or C1
transition tax saving is carried over. Recurring cohorts still need actual
eligibility/payment and old-net-right matching. No retroactive C1 recovery or
fictitious prior allowance. NF30/TA04 gross-up
uses that same source-year T once; C2's allowance is not exempt noncash pay.
Collection, provisional protection, actual refunds, independent TX review and
useful-time court/execution routes keep their existing assignments and costs.
Existing actual greater net protections survive through lawful paid succession.
