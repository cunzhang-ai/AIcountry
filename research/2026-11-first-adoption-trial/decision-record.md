# Design506 decision record

**Basis.** Continue the [Design505 baseline](baseline.json) under the
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md). Translate one national receipt
claim into an independently reviewable first decision without inferring
operating authority from the project.

**Choice.** Offer a bounded established-payment trial through one
competent payer, with improved ordinary service as the strong rival,
preserved rights and funded independent review. Compare actual full
money receipt by the original date. Qualify gross resources and the
accepting path through a second call. Keep unsafe, unproved and
non-answering cases visible. Prefer repair when it achieves the
same effects at no greater burden. The [trial decision protocol](../settlement/first-adoption-trial/01-test-payment-access-before-expansion.md)
defines expiry, stop, renewal and reopening.

**Objection and proof.** Staggering may exclude or shift help to relatives.
Missing receiving records and crossover can distort comparisons.
Equal lawful protection and timely urgent relief prevail.
Review only needed records and price comparative burdens. Parallel
agent analysis can share originals and is not independent final
review or affected participation. [Evidence and scope](sources.md).

**Compatibility and next decision.** Preserve original payer authority,
finality, protected sources, dates, complaints and handback. Limit live
reliance to actual legal and financial scope. Submit exact terms,
authorized local routes and paid comparison to a competent adopter.
Reopen on adverse access, an equivalent cheaper route, material source
change or verified next-window capacity. No actual trial, enactment,
national readiness or improvement has been established.
