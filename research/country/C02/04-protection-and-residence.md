# C02. Protection and residence

*Part of [C02](../C02-membership-and-rights.md); integrated design 58 research, not enacted law.*

The state must not make essential protection conditional on ideological loyalty,
employment, a digital identity or acceptance of an AI intermediary. Residence and
citizenship serve different purposes and cannot silently determine every entitlement.
At a border or other control point under this country's authority, a person's
protection claim has an individual, reviewable route before an irreversible return.
Neither lack of documents nor a contractor's screening result settles the claim.
Admission and protection do not themselves confer a national vote; actual habitual
residence can later count under the ordinary franchise rule. Work-residence rules
need a reviewable change-of-employer path so a sponsor cannot control legal status,
wages, housing and access to complaint at once. C10 specifies the receiving and
review duties; C07 specifies the funded service path.

The [candidate income scope](../C05/06/01-coverage-needs-and-housing.md)
separates ordinary factual residence from citizenship, admission and the
political residence threshold. Actual need and interim protection survive
status/document dispute; these proposed rules do not enact a new migration mandate.

After [CD conflict-custody release](../C08/21/06-release-and-transfer.md), missing
papers, an unsafe country of return or a housing vacancy cannot become default
closed immigration custody. Fund usable interim identity, safe nonclosed
accommodation and lawful review; actual freedom and accepted receiving matter.
Interim protection alone grants neither citizenship nor a national vote.

[MB01–MB20](06-citizenship-and-secure-belonging.md) now supplies the selected
citizenship/statelessness, stable-residence and ordinary-admission reference,
express provisional stay/work, sponsor exit and suspensive removal review.
These powers come from the proposed MB statute, not an income or E01 claim.
[C10 receiving](../C10/09-membership-admission-and-reception.md) and
[C06 capacity](../C06/30-membership-and-reception-capacity.md) expose actual
source/places/staff gaps; no digital credential or political label resolves them.
