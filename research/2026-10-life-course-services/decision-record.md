# Decision record: a usable health, education and care settlement

5 October 2026 · Integrated design 43 research selection; not enacted law

## Problem, predecessor and different diagnoses

The [design-42 predecessor](predecessor-design-42/README.md) is preserved.
Its C07 specified a national effective-access guarantee and broad health,
school and care operating model. Yet one shared topic left material decisions
inside the word “access”: who judges a clinical shortage, who guarantees a
school place and challengeable curriculum, who supplies a missed care shift,
and who pays when all three fail for one household. This affects patients,
learners, care recipients and families, clinicians, teachers, carers, public
budgets and lawful private providers. No such people have participated in
AIcountry. This is one AI-assisted analysis, not their consent.

Possible causes of failure include too little cash, too few qualified staff,
provider selection of easier people, remote travel, discriminatory admission,
unsafe professional practice, unpaid family substitution, fragmented
handoffs or a central purchasing monopoly. One generic agency or more
computer coordination cannot solve all causes. The values include equal
access, personal choice, professional independence, local democratic power,
safe quality, fair worker time and affordable continuing obligations. A
child's education, a patient's consent and an adult's legal capacity are
different claims, not three cases to close on one dashboard.

## Coherent whole-service alternatives

| Package | Possible gain | Material risk and cost |
|---|---|---|
| Retain a general C07 guarantee without sector operating chains | Simpler law and local flexibility | Responsibility for triage, school exclusion and missed care remains ambiguous; a referral can falsely count as delivery |
| Central public provision of all three services | Clear public ownership and direct staffing control | A single state employer or record controller can dominate professional judgment and user exit; local fit and transition capacity may fail |
| **Selected: national rights/finance, distinct public residuals and regulated plural providers** | Common protection with separate clinical, school and care duties; a public receiver remains when a contractor fails | Many accountable bodies need trained staff, workable handoff and distinct reviewers; plural provision can select easy cases or become a private oligopoly |
| Insurance/provider-led choice with subsidies | Some users may have more provider options and faster adaptation | Price, geography, information and risk selection can make formal choice unusable; public payer still bears catastrophic need |

The selection keeps the existing public-backbone reference but gives the
three services distinct canonical duties: [health](../country/C07/05-health-service-and-patient-control.md),
[education](../country/C07/06-education-and-learning-power.md) and
[care](../country/C07/07-care-and-support-through-life.md). Their [common
contract](../country/C07/03-health-education-and-care.md) preserves an
optional coordinated access path, not one compulsory family dossier.
Clinical regions are functional service networks, not a newly elected
political tier. A future C14 national-form choice may assign some delivery
competence differently, but must preserve rights, claims, staff, finance,
review and lawful successors. A contract, grant or digital standard cannot
quietly move these duties.

[WHO's service-delivery brief](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/organizing-health-care-services-to-ensure-universal-health-coverage)
separates service prioritization and organization from simply buying more
inputs; its [universal-coverage account](https://www.who.int/health-topics/universal-health-coverage/)
describes needed quality care without financial hardship. [UNESCO's right-to-
education materials](https://www.unesco.org/en/right-education/legal-instruments)
distinguish legal commitments and actual implementation, while the [ILO's
care-economy resolution](https://www.ilo.org/resource/record-decisions/resolution-concerning-decent-work-and-care-economy)
links care access with decent paid work. These are primary normative and
comparative sources. They do not show that the chosen AIcountry package has
better outcomes, establish applicable treaties or supply country-specific
workers and money.

## Authority, funding, correction and continuity

The Assembly enacts each essential package, financing duty, lawful priority
method and revision route. Health purchasers maintain first-contact and
receiving networks; clinically competent staff decide individual urgency;
separate safety and patient-rights bodies review error. Elected local school
authorities guarantee a place and travel under national finance and rights;
teachers and independent inspectors have different roles. National care
finance supports local assessment, public residual capacity and regulated
additional providers; recipients retain a direct review route and paid
workers retain labor rights. Courts enforce legal boundaries and individual
remedies without choosing a national clinical package or curriculum. AI may
support options, translation and scheduling but cannot make unattended
denials, treat its summaries as independent observations, or substitute a
screen for a reachable person.

The C06 account must reconcile health staff, teachers, carers, interpreters,
transport, buildings, medicine, power, water, safe waste, supplies,
inspection and appeal time with other public commitments. Public budgets
cover high needs, fixed sparse-area capacity, provider exit and receiving
staff; a low unit price with no accepting provider is a false saving. The
[C13 linked-failure exercise](../country/C13/08-linked-health-school-and-care-failure.md)
tests whether one household receives all three services when contractors,
transport and records fail together. The person may coordinate claims but
need not consent to a single shared file or surrender separate appeal.
Actual delivery, not referral, utilization or enrolment alone, is the result
to observe.

Strong objections remain. A national package may lag new treatment or
diverse learning methods; local discretion may perpetuate inequality;
public backbones may crowd out community initiative; plural providers may
select easy users; an inspector may suppress professional or cultural
freedom; a person-directed care budget may shift wage and fraud risk to the
recipient. The selected texts bound these risks but cannot eliminate them
without real evidence and affected-party judgment. Equal rights do not imply
identical services everywhere; geography and scarcity still require
transparent, challengeable priority choices. A full public-only alternative
or a different finance model remains available for legitimate comparison.

Existing health, education, child-protection, professional, labor, privacy,
contract and fiscal law would need exact authorized reconciliation. Current
treaties and accrued claims cannot be inferred away. A new provider cannot
commence until there is a funded accepting predecessor/successor path,
source-record custody, independent review and a safe contingency. Earlier
support for the general C07 proposal is not automatically support for these
new sector provisions. The predecessor remains available to inspect changes.
Its clinical-waste, border-reception and pension/care distinctions continue
in C09's sanitation, C10's reception and C07's retirement topics; their
obligations were not repealed by splitting the service chapter.

**Evidence:** the original official sources above and the logical C13 test;
no AIcountry observations. **Research selection:** distinct health,
education and care operating chains under the existing public-backbone
reference. **Legal authorization:** none. **Operational readiness:** no real
population, service map, professionals, patients, pupils, carers, cash or
receiving facilities. **Observed outcomes:** none. No claim of superiority
over existing countries is warranted by this desk design.

The next bounded question is whether one real possible jurisdiction could
fund and deliver a representative high-need health-school-care journey under
this package and the public-only and insurance-led rivals without losing
independent remedy or forcing unpaid family labor. It requires original
service, worker, price, travel and claim observations; affected residents
and workers must contest the packages. Reopen if any sector's public
residual cannot take a failed provider, if privacy or professional freedom
is dominated, or if a rival produces better actual access and control.
