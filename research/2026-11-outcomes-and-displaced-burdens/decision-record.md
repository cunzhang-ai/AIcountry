# Design449. Correct comparisons when the reform changes both sides

**Baseline.** Design448 prices parts of an access route and separates
the common dispatch failure. PCO01–PCO16 already govern measurement,
regression response and elected review. LT06–LT10 already protect
origin and receiving capacity. The open question is whether an area
whose outcomes are affected can represent what would have happened
without the intervention. AI assistance and parallel scope review
contributed to the research selection.

**Choice.** An affected comparator remains in the public outcome
account. Qualify any causal use with a credible unaffected
comparison or a design that estimates shared effects under stated
assumptions. Otherwise report the combined and local observations,
coverage limits and bounded sensitivities. Continue qualified
ordinary repair. Select a narrower or funded expansion only through
the competent decision with independently accepted source and
receiving capacity. Neither a relative gain nor its withdrawal
changes the original protected claims.

**Rivals and objection.** Keeping the original comparison alone is
cheap and preserves continuity of reporting, but may overstate
improvement when the reform weakens the comparator. Requiring a new
causal study for every change can delay low-cost repairs and suppress
credible accounts of harm. Use proportionate checks for material
exposure and allow elected consideration of qualified descriptive
evidence. A wider account may omit hidden transfers or threaten
privacy. Give independent reviewers scoped original access, with a
safe route for excluded people and a record of unknowns.

**Continuity and next evidence.** Preserve published versions and
correct their affected downstream uses. Keep the original remedy and
review clocks. Examine one actual shared dispatch or staffing change
with preexisting rosters, assignments and service receipt on both
sides. Assess demand, timing, origin cover, later recovery and people
outside the main record. Reopen if the comparator was independent, if
the exposure is immaterial, or if an accepted replacement changes the
available decision. Actual participation, independent local review,
lawful authority, funds and outcome improvements remain unverified.
