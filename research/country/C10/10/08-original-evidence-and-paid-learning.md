# IE01–IE04: source meaning, scarce capacity and correction that reaches people

*Part of [C10/10](../10-civilian-assessment-and-intelligence-limits.md); design102 research, not actual qualified intelligence.*

**IE01 — An observation is not its repetitions.** Preserve each material
claim's original observation or explicit unknown, date/context, actual collector/
controller, source purpose/authority, translation/version, copying/partner links,
contrary material and uncertainty. A witness repeated by diplomatic notes, sector
alerts and AI summaries remains one declared origin family; different models or
accounts do not prove independence. A partner may quote this country's own output
back to it. Distinct origin identifiers do not prove independent methods/controllers
or truth. Mark corroboration established/shared/unresolved with reasons, never an
unsupported numeric confidence standing in for judgment. A credible uncertain
imminent-life warning can go to its lawful receiver without inventing a second
source; coercive/personal decisions retain their independent law and proof.

**IE02 — Expression, interpretation, tasking and authority differ.** Paper,
spoken reports, supported communication, simulation and future media can convey
observations. Preserve disputed meaning, attribution, scope, uncertainty and safe
correction without forcing private testimony into a public database. A model can
assist translation/synthesis within a revocable bounded processing mandate, not
create an intelligence subject, factual origin, political command or extra reviewer.
Do not pool private provider consent into a public-task licence. Every material
inference remains answerable to protected independent review; genuine unresolved
value disagreements and source objections are retained, not averaged away.

**IE03 — Source quality and receiving need paid capability.** Price area/language/
science and source-law competence, translation checks, separate original custody,
foreign-law/licensing/FX, recipient coverage, on-call warnings, quarter/year outputs,
committee counsel/records/audit, PD inspection, ordinary court/qualified counsel and
actual record/compensation execution. No agency rank, contract, AI subscription,
foreign confidence or committee seat supplies an available skilled shift. A shared
worker/site/key/vendor remains a shared dependency, not independent capacity by
label. Procurement keeps the actual beneficial controller, field/deliverable,
source limits, ordinary competitive or justified exception, paid duration, safe
exit and source-audit right. NAU cannot buy an unlawful file through a licensed
reseller, or finance itself from a promised recovery/security saving/fine.

[Dependencies](../../../settlement/commitments/18-civilian-assessment-and-control.md)
retain actual Treasury G, S, insurer F, SF standby, client/worker/pension property,
prior election/care/court/refund claims and separately owned FX. The authored
cash/FX/shift probes are a stress case, not a national budget, observed cost or
source supply. No claim of equal safety or full-country feasibility follows.

**IE04 — Learning must reach the decision owner.** A consequential correction
traces source→analysis/model/version→each known material recipient→its actual
reconsideration/notice/execution. Preserve safe reasons/history and minimum lawful
sealed abuse proof, not an active false-person profile or permanent public
witness file. A revised assessment does not itself reverse a court, benefit, tax,
nomination or foreign decision. Record received correction, refusal, source loss
and any actual harmful use separately. Scheduled programme/data/notice review
and new material objections are not waived by an unchanged dataset/model hash.

The [decision](../../../2026-11-civilian-intelligence-and-democratic-control/decision-record.md)
retains concealed-threat losses and the no-unit/external/noncriminal-warrant rivals.
Reopening needs actual original threat/failure/misuse evidence or a prospectively
authorized, safe discriminating exercise, not a tragedy requirement or a desire
for a more sophisticated service. No real threat, recipient, independent public/
professional qualification, actual authority, funded roster or better lives is
established by this proposed learning method; FRAMEWORK itself is unchanged.
