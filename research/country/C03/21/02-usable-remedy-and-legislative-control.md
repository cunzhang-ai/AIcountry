# PE05–PE08. Make the information useful while the decision is still open

*Part of [parliamentary scrutiny](../21-parliamentary-evidence-and-independent-access.md).*

**PE05 — Order a specific act and check receipt.** Use ordinary B17
for timely preservation and protection, including earlier statutory
deadlines where they apply. The court may order an identified lawful
search of records by their custodian, production, a safer inspection
mode, a usable gist or reconsideration of a refusal. It states the
source, act, competent custodian, protected receiver and useful time.
The adopting Act expressly extends B19's bounded record execution
only to already determined parliamentary access and preservation duties.
Use prior conflict-free assignment of paid B18 civilian executors and
independently usable lawful credentials for the exact ordered records.
No general entry, unrelated search, witness detention or new offence
follows. Keep classified originals in authorized custody. Verify
qualified access and actual usable receipt, with a continuing route
for refusal, missing records or lack of capacity. Use court and SR
routes within their own scopes. The judge cannot select the vote or
order a general merits inquiry.

**PE06 — Give contrary or missing evidence an actual pre-vote hearing.**
A member who timely identifies a material omission linked to a
scheduled ordinary vote can put a scoped item into the existing
protected agenda without majority committee permission. For this
agenda right, a request lodged at least five calendar days before the
announced vote is timely. A newly revealed material fact can justify
a later item where a meaningful hearing can still take place. Moving
a vote earlier cannot defeat a timely request. This is a proposed
agenda threshold and leaves DB's deadlines for offering alternatives
and prior-law urgency in their own scope. The Clerk and lawful
presider provide a meaningful opportunity before that decision to
present competing reasons and evidence and hear the custodian's
usable response. Document what members received and
could examine, what remains disputed or unavailable, and the costs
of proceeding or waiting. The Assembly decides lawful postponement,
amendment or disposition by its own votes and deadlines. The court
can enforce the specific access and hearing duty within jurisdiction.
Neither an application nor an incomplete response creates an automatic
stay, changes an election or budget clock, or authorizes a new measure.
Genuine urgency uses existing prior law and prompt follow-up. Earlier
service, electoral, DR, DB and independent remedy duties continue.
Keep missing material visible if a lawful decision must proceed.

**PE07 — Protect people and prevent scrutiny from becoming harassment.**
Use necessity, specific purpose, proportional scope and secure custody.
Related or repeated requests can share a source response when that
preserves separate material concerns and dates. One member's request
supplies no authority to compel private testimony or open a personal
dossier. A prompt review is available for excess scope, improper motive
shown by evidence, unsafe access and biased refusal. A plausible
dissenting position is no ground to refuse. Protect lawful source
confidentiality, anti-retaliation and respondent rights. Secret records
stay purpose-limited, with inspectable access logs, lawful expiry and
protected appeal. No liability or adverse individual decision is
established merely by a parliamentary request, refusal or AI summary.

**PE08 — Fund the whole route and preserve it across governments.**
Use the Assembly Clerk's protected budget and career staff, lawful
agency custodians, separate ordinary courts/counsel and independent
execution. Budget secure reading and accessible interpretation,
qualified review, ordinary case cover and continuing source custody
under DB, FB and OP/T. Neither the investigated executive nor one
vendor should control all usable copies, staff or keys. Handle speech,
text, simulations and later formats through lawful provenance and
safe human inspection. AI can locate and compare material under DP
and PD. It has no independent standing, final privilege decision or
official evidentiary authority. Keep original dates, custody, open
rights and orders through dissolution or transfer. Incoming members
use their own lawful mandate. If all independent routes fail, record
the breach and unresolved effects through the safe elected and judicial
routes still available. Reopen for delay, abuse, source loss, capture or
a demonstrably stronger ordinary arrangement.
