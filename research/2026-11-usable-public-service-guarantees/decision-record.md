# Design446. Put the access path inside a designated guarantee

**Baseline and remit.** Design445 remains pinned. MO17–MO22 already
require direct payment on covered journeys. AP13–AP18 and SR13–SR17
fund narrow routes in disputed responsibility and execution. SR28–SR32
protect a limited claim for proved loss. AI-assisted drafting and
parallel design review are research contributions. The new decision
concerns the ordinary offer for designated access supports when the
owner is known and an order, interagency dispute or prior expense
need not exist.

**Alternatives and selection.** Keep reimbursement as an optional
route. Select an ordinary, directly payable offer with a funded,
independent alternate where one can deliver. Test service redesign
that removes the need to purchase access. Local or home delivery may
be preferable if the same lawful service can actually be received.
A new lost-opportunity award remains open. It raises distinct
questions about causal proof, severity and fiscal incidence that an
access duty cannot resolve by assumption.

**Tradeoffs and power.** A payer-controlled supplier list can narrow
choice, delay provider payment or fail through one controller. Use
published terms, accept qualified voluntary providers, preserve direct
provision and accessible independent challenge. Standby costs and
bookings reduce capacity available elsewhere and must be priced on
the common budget. Review should have separate access to protected
original dates, capacity and payment records. No broader merits,
priority or emergency power changes. Direct delivery needs genuine
agreement and should disclose only information needed for that act.

**Continuity and next evidence.** The legislature defines covered
supports and votes funding. The service owner remains accountable
for the actual offer through a change of payer or provider. Carry
valid awards, supplier commitments, missed dates and remedies forward.
Compare real paired service schedules with a common failure using
provider offers, accepted alternatives, independent case review and
receipt with and without advance funds. Reopen if provider concentration,
displacement, recurring rejection or an in-place option materially
changes the comparison. There is no actual affected-party review,
adoption or demonstrated service improvement in this edition.
