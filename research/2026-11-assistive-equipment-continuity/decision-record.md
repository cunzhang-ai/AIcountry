# Design339 decision on essential assistive equipment continuity

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design338, S health entitlement, HG clinical governance, HN receiving,
TD conformity, LCY independent repair and the working framework. The
existing design treats repairability and received care as duties. Neither
alone assigns a qualified team to keep a specific person's functions
available during a repair, platform failure or supplier exit.

Select a named NCS equipment team with a useful-time duty, interim provision
and a lifecycle procurement gate. Treat the person's essential functions
as the outcome, including mobility and private communication in ordinary
settings. The person controls choice and consent, professionals determine
safe fit and use, the purchasing team pays for continuity, and independent
review can enforce existing duties. The project assistant authored this
choice.

## Alternatives and evidence

A warranty and general repair right can preserve more supplier competition
and cost less up front. That approach can leave a person waiting through
a gap no company is able to cover. An all-public equipment workshop could
retain expertise but may lack access to niche parts or specialist fitting.
Select public responsibility with qualified plural repair and replacement
routes and independently tested fallback. Keep public workshop provision a
live alternative where local use and skills make it effective.

The [primary comparators](sources.md) support individualized fit and
follow-up, independent repair and replacement after a particular market
exit. The 24-hour and five-working-day targets are candidate standards.
Backup stock, specialist travel, secure data transfer and staffed
independent review have costs. A substitute that restores indoor mobility
may leave other activity inaccessible. Validate safety and real function
individually, including use of non-digital support if chosen.

## Migration and reopening

Enact prospectively with elected funding, named clinical and purchasing
owners, lawful review and paid legacy support. Preserve earlier due dates,
valid warranties, independent safety powers and accrued claims. Place
existing devices on a funded repair or replacement pathway as contracts
change, without removing a functioning device by default.

Before wider adoption, test a full support period with consenting users,
local repairers, qualified fitters and an independent reviewer. Include
a simultaneous supplier outage and a loan that only partly restores
function. Measure actual time to fit and use, missing functions, worker
and family burdens, privacy, cash and repeat failures. Reopen if
inaccessible loans remain outstanding, substitution reduces choice,
common dependencies persist or a rival serves users better.
