# Primary records on public interoperability and open standards

1. Regulation (EU) 2024/903, *Interoperable Europe Act*, Article 2
   on binding requirements, Article 3 and the Annex on assessment,
   and Article 4 on sharing solutions and exceptions. Within its
   cross-border public-service scope the law includes legal,
   organizational, semantic and technical requirements. It requires
   assessment for relevant new or substantially modified binding
   requirements with stated exceptions.
   [Official regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/903).
2. UK Cabinet Office, *Open Standards principles*, updated April 2018,
   definition and principles on competition, specifications and
   exemptions. It specifies accessible documentation, fair support
   for implementers and royalty-free licensing as part of the
   policy definition. It is a government technology policy, with
   its own scope and assurance procedure.
   [Official policy](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/open-standards-principles/open-standards-principles).

The records describe real institutional choices. They do not
establish independent service operation or legal compatibility
for the proposed country. The worked case is authored and uses
no actual resident records.
