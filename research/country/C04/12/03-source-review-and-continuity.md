# Finance, outside control and change

*Part of [TU effective take-up](../12-effective-take-up-and-early-benefit-delivery.md).*

**TU09 — Fund the benefit and the receiving work.** The programme
owner and Treasury show the underlying lawful entitlement and
sources, the cost of increased receipt, due-date cash and any
new staffing, non-digital access, source correction, urgent
advances and independent review. Distinguish staff time
released in one task from time actually available to another.
Buy and protect a manual and urgent response floor by service,
skill and useful time. Never count an unclaimed benefit, failed
transfer or an unstaffed slot as savings that can fund a new
promise. When cash or capacity fails, apply existing interim
and scarcity law and expose the outstanding claims. Limit
discretionary outreach that would displace current duties.
A budget gap is not an eligibility decision or permission to
take another programme's reserved funds.

**TU10 — Test whom the route misses.** Compare a simpler competent
ordinary route, assisted contact and early delivery on the
same legal offer and need. Measure practical reach,
unsupported cases, false matches, time to actual receipt,
lost useful opportunities, independent corrections, claimant
and worker burden, and privacy harms. Distinguish lawful
estimated eligibility, uncertainty and the actual unknown
population outside administrative records. Use safe sampling,
accessible feedback and affected-person participation without
building a population surveillance register. Pay particular
attention to people who cannot safely receive notice,
people whose records disagree and those whose urgency
is obscured by easy-to-complete cases. Publish outcomes
and remaining gaps in safely aggregated form.

**TU11 — Keep the route accountable through a hostile handover.**
Use existing public appointments, payment, privacy and external
audit and court roles. The programme owner answers for timely
delivery. The custodian answers for source accuracy and access.
The Treasury payer answers for lawful actual payments. The
independently funded reviewer can inspect original evidence,
require a reasoned correction and pursue enforceable relief
within existing powers. Trace shared control of appointment,
access credentials, staff and audit sources. Ensure a direct
route outside an implicated official, contractor or shared
vendor. An executive cannot start or stop individual awards
by changing an inaccessible database. Independently owned
records and an accepting ordinary route must work in the
scoped failure. AI does not appoint, pay or decide cases.

**TU12 — Commence and revise by lawful, testable scope.** Start
with an enacted class of event or already established award
and a dated, funded receiving schedule. Publish the exact
programme, version, permitted evidence, exception ownership
and independent review. Preserve the earlier rights, choices,
source law, active payments, appeal dates and safe contacts
when systems or operators change. When an evidence root is
compromised, stop reliance on the affected route and use the
funded ordinary and urgent routes. Keep existing lawful dues
live. Expand or replace the method only after fresh scope,
safety, burden and receiving checks. Reopen on concealed
exclusion, privacy harm, a failed payment or human route,
changed circumstances or a simpler way to reach the same right.
