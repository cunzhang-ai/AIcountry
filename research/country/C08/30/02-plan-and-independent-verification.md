# SR05–SR08. Correct the cause through a bounded public plan

*Part of [public remedies](../30-repeated-public-failure-and-remedy.md).*

**SR05 — Require an executable plan with a named public owner.** Where
a lawful shared repair is ordered, the duty body presents a reasoned
implementation plan within 14 calendar days of usable service of the
order, or sooner where useful protection requires. Set a finite schedule
of dates, expected receipt, lawful source, actual capacity, shortfall,
interim protection and a route to challenge. Identify known missed and
excluded people without assuming a registry is a complete census.
Where a plan cannot lawfully be funded or staffed, identify the precise
gap, what can be done now, and the competent elected choice. The
responsible minister or local executive brings needed options to its
elected body under DB/FB and LR. A proposal or appropriation is not
a received remedy. The plan cannot reset earlier due dates.

The court can require changes needed to satisfy the established duty,
stop unlawful practice, set outcome and evidence milestones within
its jurisdiction and review a lesser lawful route. It cannot enact
eligibility, set taxes or new appropriations, take over an agency,
award a contract or choose a discretionary programme. Protected
decision-makers retain authority over lawful means and actual
resource allocation within rights and source law.

**SR06 — Check both records and people's actual access.** The court may
use a qualified, conflict-checked independent verifier under express
law and a funded finite assignment. Choose through published prior-law
selection, with party challenge and outside review. Give scoped access
to necessary originals, safe interviews and actual provider, Treasury
and claimant evidence. Do not accept a contractor's assertion or an
agency's aggregate as sole proof. Disclose method, limitations, missing
groups and material dissent. Verification is evidence, with independent
judicial decision, and grants no instruction, appointment, spending
or case authority. AA audit and sector inspection retain their own scopes.
If no independent verifier can actually work, expose the evidence gap
and use available lawful court evidence and protection.

**SR07 — Reach people outside the first case.** Provide usable notice and
safe assisted intake to people potentially subject to the common wrong,
including those who could not file. Correct a generally applicable
unlawful rule within the lawful scope of the order. Give each person a
practical way to seek application, reconsideration, dues or urgent
protection without re-proving an already binding common issue.
Individual facts, defenses, remedy eligibility and third-party rights
remain separately determined. B20 governs class effects and the
settlement or waiver of claims. No absent person is treated as consenting,
identified publicly or discharged merely because a plan exists.
Review and pay individually undisputed amounts where already authorized.
Maintain offline routes and allow affected people to contest the plan.

**SR08 — Protect continuing service and its source.** Name actual
interim care, support, payment, notice and replacement-provider paths.
Use only prior-law authority, accepted contracts, owned funds and
qualified receivers. A court's repair order cannot itself extend an
invalid contract, appropriate money or compel unpaid care. Show actual
cash dates, preserved evidence, safe transfer and any displaced duty.
An unfunded option returns to the responsible elected body within
the available lawful route. Record open judgments and unmet needs.
Preserve existing emergency, equality, custody and useful-time clocks,
even where the broader work needs more time.

[SC33–SC37](../../C04/05/09-essential-service-exit-and-recovery.md)
separates a verified service exit from financial accountability.
The plan funds its independent verifier and reconciles actual
recovery without changing creditor priority or original remedies.

[LX11–LX16](../../C11/12/04-proactive-redress-and-missing-cases.md)
provides an ordinary proactive correction route for common payment and
charge errors. Keep earlier orders, independent verification and actual
delivery under SR. No individual needs to re-prove a binding common issue.
