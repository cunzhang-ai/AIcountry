# DP100–DP103. Keep independent public work in ordinary operation

*Design525 · Part of [public digital operation](../09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md) · Proposed ordinary law.*

**DP100 — Put the minimum required capability in the institution that owes the duty.**
When a shared digital or AI service can interrupt a protected act, delegate
support while keeping the minimum independent operation under its lawful owner.
The judicial administration controls court intake, scheduling, records and
authentic orders under judicial authority. The election authority controls its
own lawful receipt, instructions and contest records. Each essential-service
authority controls timely intake, lawful decisions and actual delivery or
payment, including direct access to challenge. Each must be able to obtain
the necessary original evidence, qualified staff and accessible receiving
without the cooperation of the implicated support provider. A duty involving
another institution still requires an agreed, independently usable receipt at
that end. Authorized local or contracted support can form part of the minimum
when the duty owner can replace and operate through it on the necessary dates.
Apply the same test to dependencies on executive-controlled services.

**DP101 — Pay for minimum operation as a recurring public duty.** The Assembly
and elected councils set their respective ordinary appropriations and lawful
payment routes under the applicable national and local finance rules.
Each responsible institution operates its minimum through its own lawful
appointment, supervision, records and budget arrangements. Finance the court
and election paths under RR18–RR21 with independent execution of paid capacity
and their original receipts. Apply existing equalization and payment law to
qualified local costs. On each forward budget show the permanent roles, paid
practice and qualified successors, operation and renewal costs, cash dates and
concurrent receiving commitments. Continue them in ordinary operation at a
frequency sufficient to demonstrate current capability. Book optional shared
tools and genuinely spare common help separately. A shared-service charge or
release decision cannot control access to the independently necessary funds or
staff. Any proposed retirement or reduction of the minimum returns to the
competent budget and sector authorities with equivalent funded coverage or an
explicit unresolved gap. This does not itself authorize spending in a lapse.

**DP102 — Share support when the minimum can continue without it.** The existing
shared support office may provide replaceable tools, evaluation, standards and
exchange, including useful AI assistance. Keep authority and custody subject to
the purpose, disclosure and independent review rules of the responsible body.
Qualified accessible technology and assisted channels may serve either route.
Learning from a shared source does not excuse independently producing and
checking the underlying official work. Providers, including public operators,
have no claim on additional tasks merely because a common purchase is cheaper.
The Assembly can choose an integrated operator or separate services on a fresh
comparison if the protected powers, capacity and remedy remain genuinely usable.

**DP103 — Show ordinary delivery, the next call and succession.** Before
material reliance or a reduction in the ordinary route, each owner demonstrates
receiving, lawful decisions and execution through withdrawal of the shared
service and, separately, denial of cooperation by its controller. Use a safe
exercise and qualified ordinary evidence. Test concurrent duties and corrections
at the next binding dates and after the first finite cover expires. Show a
qualified successor can perform the protected work with independently held
originals, accessible interfaces and financed renewal. Count each person, site,
specialist and payer once where commitments overlap. Measure the number of
people who actually receive a timely act and the burden on people requiring
assistance. Record late repair separately from timely receipt. When a route
misses, keep original dates and continuing claims,
use existing lawful relief and return the visible gap for funded correction.
Restrict implicated new reliance while repair remains unverified. Resume optional
use under its own scope and fresh tests after any material change.
