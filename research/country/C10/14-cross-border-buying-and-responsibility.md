# C10. Commercial buying and responsibility across borders

5 November 2026 · Design187 · Proposed BC01–BC20, not enacted law

A better life here cannot be justified by making serious harm elsewhere
invisible. C10 already selects prevention, mitigation and accessible external
remedies; B41/B44 already supply connected forums and applicable domestic
mandatory duties. This choice gives independent commercial buying an explicit
substantive duty, without declaring every buyer an employer or guarantor.

Select proportionate **own-conduct prevention and reasonable linked-risk
response**, enforceable against connected buyers. Apply the same serious-harm
floor to domestic sourcing: outsourcing across either boundary is no escape.
Ordinary purchases need no licence, prior court order, universal chain register
or certification. A genuinely low-risk small purchase needs proportionately
little inquiry; grave known harm cannot be ignored by splitting orders.

An audit badge, AI score or supplier promise does not discharge a buyer's own
duty. Conversely, abuse in a chain is not proof of this defendant's breach or
causal contribution. Preventive action, compensatory damages, employment,
product safety, border exclusion and criminal guilt need their distinct bases.
BC changes no B11 indivisible-loss rule after actual contribution is proved.
