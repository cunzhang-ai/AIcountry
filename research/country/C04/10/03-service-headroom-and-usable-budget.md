# LD09–LD13. Distinguish spending room from money and service

*Design265 · Proposed local fiscal and national budget duties.*

**LD09 — Reconcile each constraint in its own account.** Before a
material local correction, the council and Treasury show the enacted
appropriation and purpose, applicable service and capital limits,
restricted funds, actually spendable cash by date, qualified staffed
capacity and the protected person or service that must receive it.
Identify whether an applicable ceiling uses gross or net spending,
which receipts or offsets its law recognizes, which other places or
duties already use the same allowance and which body can change it.
Show when a reported balance is a forecast or reserve classification.
A transfer of ceiling room has no cash effect by itself. Cash in an
account cannot be used beyond its scope or against a binding ceiling.
A financed vacancy or accounting reduction has no delivered capacity
by itself.

**LD10 — Reserve matched capacity for continuing duties.** When
national law limits aggregate local service expenditure, the Assembly
and local elected bodies budget the common legal duties before optional
starts. Compare a paid continuity route within the ordinary envelope
with a separately voted contingency allowance and source where
necessary. Assign the actual limit, payer, payable dates, releasing
officer and eligible local duties. Keep available allowance and dated
cash separate even when approved in one package. Use existing
[MF](../08-funded-local-mandates-and-equalization.md) authority for
allocations and independent correction. A centrally held ceiling
or reserve cannot also be pledged to each locality separately.
Respect local delivery choice and existing independent review.
Price the ordinary protected capacity that must remain available
while the contingency is held.

**LD11 — Put an exact joint correction to the competent bodies.** Compare
a legally available earlier transfer, budget or ceiling change, qualified
local or jointly paid capacity, and a smaller optional programme on the
same due dates. Show both accounting effects of every material change,
including a receipt reduction offset by a separate payment adjustment.
Name who gives up cash, service allowance or real capacity. A national
aggregate limit stays with the competent national law and the local
appropriation stays with the elected local body. Neither office can
authorize the other's action by implication. A paper transfer, internal
loan or relabelled capital project cannot create service room by itself. Carry
an approved advance once and show its later repayment and displaced uses.
Use the [DB independent evidence route](../../C06/60/07-independent-fiscal-forecast-and-budget-release.md)
and [JC release rules](../../C06/65/03-release-and-bound-obligations.md).

**LD12 — Protect residents during the decision and check actual receipt.**
Keep prior claims and the first due date. Apply existing MF and LD interim
duties and useful-time remedies when capacity or authority is absent.
Use a separately authorized service route only within its real legal
scope, without double charging the original payer or exporting an unmet
duty to another place. A targeted hiring or purchasing restriction must
identify which accepted work and qualified staff remain by the useful date.
Protect the care, access, electoral and review work needed for the actual
right, including its shared dependencies. When a joint correction still
cannot meet the duty, name the affected people or functions, remaining
lawful protection, cost, competent decision and earlier remedy. Do not
report a balance or a committee decision as actual service.

**LD13 — Reconcile proposals, votes, cash and people after the window.**
Publish the original baseline, amounts and changes by scope, exact elected
decisions, dated payment, staffed service and actual receiving or remaining
shortfall. Protect personal and commercially sensitive evidence with
independent access and usable reasons. Reopen on a lost receipt, changed
limit, hidden displacement, unaffordable recurring cost or better qualified
route. Reconcile old claims, standing limits and pending decisions through
lawful handover. AI may identify mismatched classifications and double
counted allowances. It cannot vary a cap, approve a payment or determine
that residents received a service.
