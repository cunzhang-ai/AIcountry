# Necessary care: adversarial journeys and failure costs

*Design82; authored legal counterexamples, not clinical recommendations or observed cases.*

| Situation | Selected UA response | Limit or retained cost |
|---|---|---|
| Person communicates 'no' through a nonstandard gesture | Investigate/support meaning; ascertainable refusal controls. No cognition-score override | A perceived unwise refusal is not unknown will |
| Person reliably refuses one component, while another essential choice stays unknown | Treat known refusal as a constraint, assess other genuinely unresolved act separately | No all-or-nothing capacity status or averaging away the refused component |
| Provider repeatedly uses an inaccessible interview, then declares no preferences | Correct support failure, use appropriate communication expertise and safe contacts | Lack of support cannot create authority |
| Never-communicated preferences, serious support supplied, no personal interpretation exists | Record true uncertainty; use only UA's explicit limited authority | It can act contrary to unknown wishes; no fictional universal desire for treatment |
| Unknown-will person needs clinically safe oral water, warmth or hygiene | UA05 noncoercive statutory basics, observe response, independent advocacy and direct review | No fresh court for each drink; silence still is not consent |
| Worker calls a new feeding tube 'ordinary food' | Consequential invasive intervention requires UA07/08 and, if sustaining life, UA10 | Clinically unsafe oral feeding is not compelled by the basics duty either |
| Necessary surgery cannot safely await restored communication | Court reviews actual serious condition, two relevant opinions/independence, alternatives, irreversibility and accepted delivery | Order is not a clinical license or a safe surgical standard; actual refusal/opposition bars UA |
| Clinically necessary procedure needs anesthesia | Explicit act-specific anesthesia/recovery scope may be authorized | No induction over opposition, forced transport, postoperative confinement or unrelated restraint |
| Cancer therapy unavoidably removes reproductive tissue or ends pregnancy | UA09's express heightened therapeutic case plus reconciled RP permission may apply | Real bodily consequence remains; no reproductive-policy purpose or implied consent |
| Removed tissue might later serve fertility storage, donation or research | Therapeutic removal/pathology is distinct; later use still needs actual specific authority | No useful-material opportunity expands the court order |
| New effective intervention has uncertain or insufficient proportionate benefit | Failure of the positive starting test gives no new intrusion; preserve safest existing care and reassess in useful time | No duty to start every effective treatment or automatic conclusion that all benefit is absent |
| Existing ventilation achieves gas exchange but will not restore cognition | Cognition/dependency cannot redefine the therapeutic purpose as failed | Comfortable dependent life is not a withdrawal indication |
| Existing effective intervention itself causes severe refractory bodily suffering | Prior heightened court/independent clinical+palliative review tests actual burdens, alternatives and benefit under UA10 | Cost, inheritance and communication prognosis are excluded; unknown actual wishes remain a moral risk |
| A valid applicable advance refusal becomes known | Apply actual choice/source scope, stop relying on unknown will at earliest safe point | Court/payer preference cannot overrule it |
| Provider labels fiscal rationing 'clinical futility' | Examine actual therapeutic purpose, source evidence and independent challenge; scarcity uses S/C06 law | Court cannot invent a national budget; unmet claims stay visible |
| Person objects after an invasive act begins | Pause/modify at earliest clinically safe point with support and explanation | Safe transition is not permission to complete an unwanted course |
| Original fourteen-day UA order alone authorized life support; court misses renewal | UA16's expressly separate minimum function-preserving statute applies while unknown and unobjectioned, with immediate request and24/72-hour review duties | No expiry unplugging, new escalation or renewed course; missed review may prolong unconsented support and remains breach |
| Provider changes case number to reset that review | Original actual resort to dependent continuity anchors its24/72 clocks; relabeling adds no authority | Earlier useful time/E13 receipt limit still controls |
| No independent advocate or second assessor is available | Pursue a lawful qualified alternate; maintain only independently lawful basics/limited continuity | No treating provider self-certification, family unpaid reserve or invented consent |
| Only a closed institution offers a bed | Compare real supported alternatives and unmet public duty; UA supplies no closed-placement power | Unlocked door with prevented assisted departure is still restrictive in substance |
| Public authority needs a new essential nonclosed care service | Obtain accepted funded plan, then court authority; public authority contracts in its own role | No forged patient signature, private loan, home sale or open-ended personal bill |
| Order granted, next-shift worker never accepts | Public receiver retains duty and verifies actual safe handoff; seek remedy and funded substitute | Referral/payment is not actual care |
| Family stops voluntary help | Arrange public paid receiving, preserve separate worker/recipient rights | No kinship-based forced labor or inherited body authority |
| Request to renounce nationality/vote/write a will is made by helper during unknown will | Excluded from UA; respective personal/legal rules govern | No general guardian or political mandate appears |
| A future interface confidently reconstructs a person's desired life | Preserve actual source meaning, uncertainty and alternative communication; verify the person's own act | Simulation and replicated AI agreement do not create consent |
| Concerned party asks for assisted dying on behalf of an unrepresented adult | No UA or current reference assisted-dying authorization | Known voluntary requests, refusal, relief and future lawful reform are distinct; no automatic crime from a care conversation |

The cases exercise coherent proposed boundaries. They do not establish an actual
clinical indication, rightful court order, safe provider or received remedy.
