# One worker and two service rosters

This constructed case tests [SSR01–SSR08](../../country/C05/10/08-shared-staffing-and-protected-rest.md).
A qualified nurse already worked a paid shift from 06:00 to 14:00, including
the required paid break. Assume weekly limits and all other safety conditions
are met for one additional two-hour shift starting at 01:00 the next day.
Each of two providers needs a separately qualified person from 01:00 to 03:00.
They both receive an availability reply from the same nurse.

| Claim or route | Result under the stated conditions |
|---|---|
| A further two-hour shift from 20:00 to 22:00 | Six hours since the earlier shift and a 16-hour combined span. Incompatible with the existing duty-period rules. |
| One accepted 01:00–03:00 assignment | Eleven hours' rest and ten total paid work hours within the relevant 24 hours. One usable assignment after the receiving check. |
| Both providers claim that same 01:00–03:00 window | At most one assignment. The availability replies supply no additional nurse. |
| An adjacent 03:00–05:00 shift after the nurse accepts 01:00–03:00 | Twelve paid hours in the relevant 24 hours. The further shift is incompatible. |
| A qualified independent standby worker receives and delivers the other 01:00–03:00 task | Two services if authorized costs settle and both tasks are actually received. |

The second worker costs three units that must settle by 23:00 before the
night shift. A branch with two units by that time and one unit later
leaves one service uncovered. A branch with three timely units but no
qualified handover also leaves one service uncovered. In each branch,
record both the staffing situation and actual receiving. The example
does not decide which provider takes the first nurse. Applicable service
priority and worker agreement remain separate.

The two employers need only evidence that the actual proposed intervals
are compatible. They do not need the nurse's full work history or the
identity of the other employer to compare these routes. The example tests
one elapsed-time window and funding, not a full work-time or clinical
assessment. [Reproduction](verify-shared-staffing.py).
