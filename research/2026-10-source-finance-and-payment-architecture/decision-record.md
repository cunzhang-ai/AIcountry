## Diagnosis and alternatives

The core dependence is material: public payments are the creditor claim of
the same bank that finances the Treasury and future private payroll. More
procedural review cannot produce cash outside that bank. The old narrative
also uses a commitment as working cash and a global 25% public share after
new equity would change actual ownership. Merely reducing sovereign lending
leaves the safe-payment asset and future claims unspecified.

Compare: retain the old source or waive its limit; reduce borrowing alone;
a hybrid independent public standby with paid common bank equity and drawn
working stock; and fully reserve all payments while funding credit with
common equity. These differ in ownership, monetary asset allocation, public
cash, private risk and who can refuse the source transaction. No universal
AI/coordination office chooses or settles these conflicts.

Reject the old 250/E100 source and own-state exemption. Smaller-only borrowing
95 cannot transfer 179.3625 public cash into safe reserves 175; even a balanced
book does not create missing settlement assets. Select the hybrid as a
conditional working arrangement, not as an operationally ready country.
Keep a full-reserve architecture able to displace it on public/source evidence.
