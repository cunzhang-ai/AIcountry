# A public proposal can reach the Assembly without a political patron

Design174 · [NI01–NI16](../../country/C03/16-public-national-initiatives.md)

**Public origination needs a usable institutional route.** Existing member,
opposition, local and protected constitutional routes remain; NI supplies the
ordinary national route without requiring an MP sponsor or a selected panel.

| Stage | Actual right and boundary |
|---|---|
| Open a coherent proposal | Plain-language assistance, exact supported meaning, reasoned competence decision and outside remedy |
| Collect support | k=ceil(E0/1000) actual opening national franchise,180days, real dated assent/revocation and safe plural channels |
| Verify qualification | Express EA career duty under existing protected roles; actual source/reply/count and usable noticeQ |
| Hear the public question | Protected hearing byQ+60 with paid access, organisers, affected dissent and relevant opponents |
| Decide publicly | Recorded Assembly disposition byQ+180; only one funded exact drafting mandate to final enactment/rejection byQ+360 |
| Carry the duty | Qualified agenda survives succession; actual bills/votes retain source law, no substituted consent or phantom sitting |

[Examples and real rivals](01-support-agenda-and-rivals.md) compares the current
representative/panel gate, a hearing/disposition initiative and a binding popular
legislative vote. No initiative enacts itself, creates funds, changes the franchise
or delays an existing urgent remedy/election. [Decision and evidence](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-public-national-initiatives/decision-record.md)
distinguishes source guidance, authored cases and same-controller critique from
actual independence, funding, legitimate adoption or improved democratic lives.
