# IV05–IV08. Change, accept and renew

**IV05 — Return material changes before incurring new exposure.** The project
owner tracks actual scope, cost to complete, expiry, critical dependencies and
availability of the service. Preserve the original approved baseline and add
later changes and reasons. Escalate a credible breach of the binding ceiling,
an authorized return trigger, loss of a critical source or capacity, or a
material change in approved purpose, service, harm or opening commitment.
Review related changes cumulatively across delivery vehicles. While a decision
is pending, place no new discretionary obligation in the affected scope that
would foreclose viable alternatives or breach existing authority. Pay lawful
dues, keep the site and service safe and complete genuinely separable work
within valid authority. Seek a recorded choice by the competent body before
adding exposure. Use the applicable earlier safety, payment or service clock,
and DB11's 30-calendar-day report where its material programme abandonment rule
applies. Silence supplies no authority. No project hold stays a statutory
service duty, payroll, protection, appeal, election or independent review.

**IV06 — Compare completion with safe redesign or exit at the decision date.**
Show avoidable remaining costs and harm for continuing, changing, sequencing
or stopping. State actual incurred liabilities and expected termination, worker,
creditor, restoration and service continuity obligations. Sunk expenditure
remains visible for learning and accountability. It does not count as a future
saving from continuation. Both the service sponsor and the body paying for
a change show displaced work and remaining service gaps. Only the applicable
elected authority can add purpose or exposure, subject to existing safety,
procurement, consent and remedies. A reviewer can expose unreliable facts and
a court can enforce lawful limits without selecting the project.

**IV07 — Accept a usable asset and fund a responsible successor.** Keep safety
commissioning, contractual payment checks, acceptance into service and
benefits evaluation distinct. Before reliance, the receiving service owner
checks actual access, operating staff, inspection, maintenance funding,
handover records, supplier exit or spare supply and safe continuity. Name the
legal owner and the officer responsible for remedy. Preserve worker pay,
contractual warranties, redress and pending orders through transfer. Publish
whether the asset opened, who can actually use it and which needs remain
unmet. An unopened facility or an unavailable service is an unmet result
even after certified construction. Renew, repurpose or close under the
applicable law with an accepted and financed successor or a disclosed gap.

**IV08 — Adopt proportionately and keep oversight replaceable.** A prospective
law sets materiality thresholds for gross exposure, rights/service disruption
and irreversible effects, with simplified reviews for routine work and
existing emergency safety powers. Use current elected budget authorities,
sector officers, procurement, outside audit, independent review and courts.
No new project veto or national project permission for ordinary local work.
Publish testable forecasts and compare realized service, interruptions, cost
and effects across affected groups, including delay from the review itself.
AI may reconcile dates or flag inconsistent assumptions. A named officer checks
the original evidence and remains responsible for decisions. Keep independent
routes that work without the same vendor, model or project file. Migrate live
commitments, guarantees, known maintenance, source records and recipients with
paid staff and preserve applicable contracts and appeal clocks. Reopen on
evasion through staging, repeated condition failures or a simpler process that
delivers equivalent accountability and timely service.
