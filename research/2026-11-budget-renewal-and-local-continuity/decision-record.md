# Decision record for Design464

**Baseline and procedure.** Use Design463 and the [research method](../FRAMEWORK.md).
[Baseline hashes](baseline.json) retain its exact scope. BLC, BCV, DB,
MF and ordinary source law retain their separate authority.

**Selection.** In an expressly enacted separate continuation, prefer a
qualified duty-by-duty early handback with an unchanged fixed outer
expiry. Keep successor legal effect, actually usable delivery and old
debts on their original accounts. Compare ending on passage and a
single fixed end. [Selected rules](../country/C06/66/01-budget-renewal-and-local-continuity.md).

**Reasons and objection.** Ending on passage is simple but can expose
timely services during a real funding or supplier gap. A single fixed
date reduces verification work but can tie up people and resources
after an independently qualified successor starts. Duty-by-duty
handover adds evidence and reconciliation costs. Retain a single date
where genuinely simultaneous acceptance is qualified.

**Compatibility and consumers.** The conditional exit applies only to
its enacted continuation. Keep ordinary bridge and closeout expiry,
independent institutions, valid earned claims and local authorities.
Clarify the separate tax procedural reform and conditional benchmark
source choice under current TN. No rate or taxable event is adopted.
Earlier wording remains in the predecessor.

**Limits and reopening.** Dates, amounts, approvals and operators are
constructed. Actual political approval, independent review, paid
capacity and receipt remain open. Reopen on failed succession,
dependent suppliers, repeated temporary finance or a better funded
source. Compare the next real budget using actual local acceptance,
original debts and protected independent execution.
