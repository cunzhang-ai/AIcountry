# Renewal and future budget capacity

Design324 · Scoped result for [country completion](../../DESIGN-COMPLETENESS.md)

[IV28–IV32](../../country/C06/63/07-renewal-and-future-budget-capacity.md)
protects material renewal windows across budget years and requires a
separate recorded elected decision to displace them for an optional
start. Safety, lawful cash, staff and received alternatives remain
independent conditions.

The [two-budget case](../../settlement/renewal-and-future-budget-capacity/01-water-school-and-expansion.md)
checks later cash, concurrent crews and lawful alternatives to delay.
Actual inspection, operating capacity, authority and benefits remain open.
