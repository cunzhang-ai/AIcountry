# Design381. Keep essential contracts funded through common cost shocks

[SC44–SC49](../../country/C04/05/11-essential-contract-costs-and-paid-continuity.md)
requires the buyer to compare risk allocations before award and carry
material common cost exposure into one dated budget. It separates
payment to suppliers, actual wages and essential service receipt.

[The worked comparison](../../settlement/essential-contract-costs-and-paid-continuity/01-care-and-meals-share-an-input.md)
exposes simultaneous draws, late cash and the next call. Actual
bids, legal authority, independent review and delivery remain open.
