# Secure belonging: source scope and review limits

*Design81; selected primary texts, not full treaty conformity or outcome evidence.*

The [1961 Convention](https://www.refworld.org/sites/default/files/2023-12/3ae6b39620_0.pdf)
was inspected through Articles1–9 and13, with a separate official
[UN text](https://legal.un.org/ilc/texts/instruments/english/conventions/6_1_1961.pdf)
checked by a task agent. It addresses otherwise-stateless territorial births,
foundlings, ships/aircraft and nationality loss. Article8 permits specified
exceptions including fraud; it does not require fraud deprivation. MB's universal
territorial birth, equal no-deprivation and secure residence are stronger domestic
choices, not treaty mandates. The two URLs are one treaty, not independent evidence.

The [1954 Convention](https://treaties-prod.un.org/doc/Publication/UNTS/Volume%20360/volume-360-I-5158-English.pdf)
was inspected by a task agent in Articles1,5,31–32 and in the annex to the
[UNHCR handbook](https://www.refworld.org/sites/default/files/2023-12/53b676aa4.pdf).
Its factual definition and benefit exclusions are separate. It does not itself
supply a general non-return rule or an express residence-permit requirement.
MB expressly chooses broader national protection and an actual secure-home route.

The 2014 handbook's selected paragraphs78–99,127–150 and164–168 inform shared
proof, relevant nationality links, confidentiality, residence and in-situ
exclusion. [UNHCR Guidelines4](https://www.refworld.org/sites/default/files/legacy-pdf/en/2012-12/50d460c72.pdf)
(21 December2012), selected paragraphs20–27 and57–63, inform child nationality
uncertainty, safe registration and foundling/vessel treatment. These interpretive
texts do not authorize this country or prove actual foreign nationality.
[Guidelines5](https://www.refworld.org/sites/default/files/legacy-pdf/en/2020-5/5ec5640c4.pdf)
(May2020) was inspected for fraud causation, renunciation, review and correction.
No broader comparative case-law, contemporary nationality survey or legal
qualification is claimed. UN treaties and UNHCR guidance share the UN system;
five documentary texts are not five independent observations of better lives.

The [source register](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-citizenship-and-secure-belonging/sources.json)
records scope, failures and duplicate locations. Direct OHCHR/UNHCR retrievals
failed403/429 and a definition page returned a robot/script shell; the listed
Refworld PDFs were readable. No publisher-byte archive or actual treaty-party,
reservation, state-recognition or foreign-law determination was established.

Root and three agents share one AI controller. Fresh read-only review exposed
continuity/succession, derivative-child, removal-filing-window, actual-control
custody, provisional-status gate, direct judicial-conferral and receipt/deadline
defects; the current draft corrects them. No resident, displaced person, child,
consular official, registrar, employer or independent qualified lawyer took part.
Agents did not represent their consent or interests.

Values favor stable belonging and equal standing; numbers and institutions remain
normative hypotheses. Document/parameter checks verify only declared artifacts,
not rightful citizenship, credibility, safe transfer, funded reception, legitimate
adoption, actual received remedy or superiority over an existing country.
