# Liberty and remedy: protection cannot become an endless hold

*Authored boundary cases for the [criminal-core draft](README.md); not observations.*

| Situation | Required boundary and actual executor |
|---|---|
| C7 Police transfer a detainee after 20 hours and rename custody clinical observation | The clock runs from original actual restraint. P09 requires a defended judicial hearing before 24 hours; a new site/label cannot restart it. If no lawful timely order exists, the custodian executes release. Urgent medical help retains ordinary consent/general rescue grounds and cannot serially evade liberty limits. No availability of a duty judge is proved. |
| C8 A 15-year-old causes serious harm and is sent to a locked care unit | P05 bars criminal conviction, even for a grave category. Specific current child-protection/general rescue grounds, minimum safe intervention and prompt independent review may protect people; the allegation or a maturity score cannot authorize a disguised penal term. Voluntary support and a safe receiving service are still owed on their own grounds. |
| C9 A trial is delayed to day 181 in an alleged killing case | The exceptional aggregate remand cap is 180 after individualized reasons, not a sentence or entitlement to hold until then. P10 requires release at expiry; summons and lawful narrow protection may continue. The offense case does not automatically disappear, but repeated civil/clinical orders cannot continue the same remand. Court capacity failure and endangered people need an elected/funded response, not a new police power. |
| C10 A person fails an unaffordable fine or impossible community condition | P17 protects subsistence and needs a lawful resource/installment reassessment. Poverty alone cannot become imprisonment; an alleged deliberate evasion or material escalation needs its own lawful proof/hearing. Do not impose a condition dependent on an unfunded carer, inaccessible address or compulsory therapy. Fines/community parameters are not yet enacted. |
| C11 Prison term expires but the housing provider refuses receipt | P19 requires corrections to execute release. The provider's separate unmet housing/health/income duty remains with a named receiver and E01–E20 remedies if adopted; a completed program or offered bed is not actual receipt. A failed receiving chain does not extend the sentence. No safe placement or payment has been demonstrated. |
| C12 An acquitted person still needs compensation for an unlawful detention | Criminal finality and independent civil repair are distinct. Correct records, identify the state payer/executor and preserve third-party rights. The claimant need not first convict the arresting officer; an acquittal does not itself prove the arrest unlawful or determine compensation amount. Trace the judgment to delivered release/payment, not case closure. |
| C13 An enforcement office budgets for more fines and rewards staff for charges | P20 bars pursuing-office fine income and punishment-count rewards; receipts use elected Treasury rules. Appointment, staffing, evidence, prosecution and court review need independent actual control. Removing a quota alone proves neither less crime nor a usable remedy; inspect actual harms, case errors and delivered protection. |

All proposed numbers are ethical/procedural hypotheses. These cases show the
questions that must be answered; they demonstrate neither that the selected
caps are optimal nor that violent harm will be prevented. Qualified rosters,
independent control, source law, humane sites, courtroom time and safe actual
receivers remain unknown. More formal rules do not supply those capacities.
