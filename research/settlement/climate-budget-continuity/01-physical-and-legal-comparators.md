# Physical basis and legal comparators

[Climate budgets](README.md)

IPCC Working Group I assesses the relationship between cumulative
global anthropogenic net CO2 emissions and warming. It gives
conditional remaining-budget estimates with uncertainties,
including the response to non-CO2 emissions. That physical
relationship supports tracking actual cumulative emissions.
The national gross limit, treatment of removals and fair share
are separate institutional choices. The assessment does not
assign this country's share or give a guaranteed temperature.
[IPCC AR6 Working Group I, Chapter 5](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/chapter/chapter-5/)

The UK Climate Change Act 2008 as enacted uses statutory carbon
budget periods. Section 17 allows specified carry-back up to
1% of the later period's budget and allows carry-forward of
specified unused budget. Section 19 requires proposals for
compensating excess emissions. This is a concrete legal rival
to the selected tighter ordinary no-banking and no-advance
rule. The measures differ in scope, including the use of a net
UK carbon account. [UK Act as enacted, sections 17–19](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/27/pdfs/ukpga_20080027_en.pdf)

In a 2024 UK High Court judgment reviewing the Carbon Budget
Delivery Plan, the court allowed challenges concerning the
decision process, including delivery risk. It dismissed the
separate challenge that the statutory parliamentary report
omitted required detail about individual risks. The decision
provides a concrete comparator for reviewing the evidence
behind a delivery plan without equating that review with
judicial selection of the plan. It establishes no present
emissions outcome or authority for this proposal.
[Friends of the Earth and others, 2024, paragraphs 95–119 and 171–172](https://www.judiciary.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Friends-of-the-Earth-v-Secretary-of-State-for-Energy-Security-and-Net-Zero-003.pdf)

The illustrative budgets, prices, timing, service and qualified
routes are authored inputs. Actual permits, emissions vintages,
work, siting and original recipient evidence are needed before
any operational claim.
