The bounded question is how the **current C03–C14 country package** makes a
new national promise without counting the same cash, qualified worker, site,
water, electricity, imported input or remedy staff twice. The earlier
[three-year social/fiscal scenario](../SOCIAL-FISCAL-SETTLEMENT.md) predates
later election, banking, production, utility, hazard, time/leave, housing,
evidence and AI commitments. It cannot certify this package affordable.
Inventing another population and prices would not answer that missing
real-world question. The design choice is a truthful obligation-and-capacity
rule for future elected decisions, not a fiscal estimate.

The value judgment is that lawful claims, democratic replacement and a
life-sustaining floor cannot be made invisible by a neat balanced budget.
The accounting constraint is that resources and due payments cannot be used
twice. Whether a proposed service yields better lives, what tax burden is
fair and how to rank competing lawful uses are contested empirical and
political questions, not outputs of this record.

## Alternatives

| Coherent package | Benefit | Material failure |
|---|---|---|
| Retain sector budgets with C06's general consolidation instruction | Low administrative burden; ministers and local bodies keep familiar control. | The same clinician, grid connection, housing site, emergency reserve or foreign-currency receipt can appear as available in several plans, while a national total hides local impossibility. |
| A central technical affordability authority that approves or vetoes all commitments | Could enforce uniform estimates and reject openly impossible projections. | Whoever controls models, categories, data and appointments could veto an elected policy or quietly rank lives. Cash sufficiency alone still misses physical and rights failures. |
| **Selected: elected package decision with a federated, reconciled commitment account** | Assembly sees one national exposure, source-backed sector capacity and explicit collisions; disagreement and shortfalls remain visible without a technical sovereign. | Producing it costs skilled staff and may delay needed action or invite strategic estimates; independent audit and usable opposition access need real money and protection. |
| Automatic statutory priority ladder for every shortage | Reduces discretion and may speed some urgent decisions. | A single permanent ranking cannot fairly determine unlike claims, local conditions, prevention and future maintenance; it could conceal value choices and exclude the people harmed. Narrow preadopted emergency priorities remain useful. |

The [IMF Fiscal Transparency Code](https://www.imf.org/external/np/fad/trans/Code2019.pdf)

addresses coordination across levels of government. These are primary
institutional sources, not tests of AIcountry's new rule or endorsements of
its currency, rights or political choices.

## Selected national commitment rule

The Assembly's annual budget and each material midyear or framework-law change
are accompanied by one **public commitment account**, assembled from lawful
source custodians. It is a set of interoperable, signed and versioned schedules,
not a compulsory central dossier about every resident. The schedule records
program-level commitments and capacity; protected individual claims stay with
competent service and court custodians. A resident may report an urgent problem
without completing the schedule.

For each material **existing duty, proposed expansion, maintenance obligation,
guarantee or contingent exposure**, the responsible public proposer supplies:

| Entry | Required distinction |
|---|---|
| Legal standing and people affected | Current constitutional/statutory duty, accrued claim, lawful contract, proposed law or conditional future start; affected groups and territory; protected exceptions and appeal. A forecast cannot change the legal standing. |
| Cash and financing | Due dates, payer, currency, usable receipts, transfers net of internal flows, authorized borrowing, opening liabilities, payment-channel and refinancing dependencies; gross commitment and net public exposure separately. |
| Delivery | Named provider and lawful successor; qualified roles, hours, location, leave, training lead time, current roster, recruitment and receiving capacity; ordinary and failure-mode service. |
| Physical and external inputs | Site/land title, accessible route, materials, water, power, food, equipment, import settlement, supplier and ecological limits; stocks separated from flows and from reserves already promised elsewhere. |
| Life-cycle and correction | Capital, maintenance, renewal, decommissioning, insurance/guarantee risk, independent inspection, legal aid, appeal, remedy executor, safe interim route and funded vendor/provider exit. |
| Evidence and uncertainty | Original source custodian, date, assumptions, ranges, known exclusions, rival interpretation, changes since last account and independently challengeable omissions. Unknown is not zero or available capacity. |

Treasury reconciles the cash, liabilities and currency schedule, including
national, local and controlled public entities. A sector authority attests
the actual professional and physical service path; a local government
attests its lawfully controlled resources and may challenge an unfunded
transfer. C11's evidence service preserves comparable methods and old
vintages without changing entitlements. Independent audit tests duplicate
claims and fiscal risk; professional and safety reviewers test their domains.
Opposition, affected users/workers and courts receive timely lawful access
to source evidence and can identify common controllers. The Assembly, not
Treasury, an auditor, a dashboard or AI, chooses taxes, debt, standards,
allocation and tradeoffs by valid law.

There is **one debit per genuinely scarce capability** across the selected
package. The account reconciles shared inputs by time and location, including
the following consequential collisions:

| Shared input | Competing commitments whose simultaneous delivery must be shown |
|---|---|
| Qualified people and usable time | Health, care, schools, justice, inspections, utilities, disaster response, defense and election/court operations; paid leave and training remove hours from a current roster until replaced. |
| Serviced land, buildings, routes, construction and repair | Safe housing, schools, clinics, care, shelters, depots, water/grid works and ecological restoration; a planned site is not an occupied safe place. |
| Energy, water, food, equipment and ecological flows | Households, essential networks, industry, public AI/compute, emergency reserves and future supply; a reserve's location, rotation and release rule matter. |
| Usable cash and external settlement | Due benefits, wages, debt, imports, bank/deposit protection, construction, maintenance, foreign commitments and emergency response; a guarantee is risk exposure, not free insurance. |
| Independent remedy and information capacity | Election evidence, courts, defense/legal aid, service appeals, audit, abuse investigation, records/archives and provider exit; one vendor or challenged cabinet cannot be their only operator. |

An item is reported with separate states: **lawfully owed**, **authorized
spending**, **cash available when due**, **physical delivery capacity**,
**service actually received**, and **remedy executed**. No one state implies
another. In particular, enacted-but-uncommenced expansion is labelled with
its statutory start condition, accountable authority, public review date
and funded interim protection. A start condition cannot be a minister's
unbounded declaration that it is “ready.” If the lawful due date passes
without delivery, record the unfulfilled right and the responsible failure;
do not move the date by changing a database field. A court may order lawful
protection, correction and reasons, but not invent a tax, worker or external
currency.

For a **new discretionary expansion**, the Assembly must see a feasible
path through each material cash and physical account, maintenance, adverse
case, effects on existing duties, and a fundable remedy/exit. It may instead
enact a bounded preparation phase or trial with a future commencement
decision; it may not report the intended final service as already delivered.
This is a truth-in-commitment requirement, not a technical veto. The
Assembly remains able to enact an openly risky or redistributive policy
within rights and law, with the risk, alternatives and dissent recorded.
An **existing right** never loses force because its ledger input is missing;
the responsible authority supplies feasible urgent protection, exposes the
shortfall and seeks lawful funding or revision. A signed “balanced” account
does not extinguish a person's claim.

The government publishes a **decision table** with at least: maintain and
repair existing commitments; a targeted rights/access correction; the
proposed expansion; and a meaningfully different arrangement if one is
available. It shows who gains, waits or loses, which resource is released
and which duty still remains. Interlocal transfers are netted nationally but
remain visible to affected places. No arbitrary numerical welfare score
settles the choices. Finance, disability, care, ecological and democratic
standing cannot be collapsed into one favorable aggregate.

## Shortfall, transition and capture

When an input fails, its custodian reports the date, location and affected
claims before avoidable interruption. Cabinet presents revised lawful
financing, reallocation, repair, delay or changed future standard options.
The Assembly makes a recorded choice; standing duties and the limited
budget-lapse bridge retain only their previously enacted effect. Providers
use authorized interim routes; courts preserve and enforce claims where
law and actual capacity allow. If elected institutions do not resolve the
conflict, the backlog, harm and power of the acting caretaker stay public.
Delay supplies neither consent nor a new mandate. A legal change must name
successors for pending cases, benefits, contracts, workers and ecological
liabilities; bank reserves, pension funds and local grants cannot be silently
spent for another purpose.

At founding, the resource account starts with the audited **opening
obligations and assets**, rather than a blank spreadsheet. The first election,
court, treasury, payment, legal-aid, records, safety and basic service
continuity need their own lawful staff and cash paths before any launch
claim. The new government cannot book speculative AI productivity as cash or
qualified labor. A phased service law must distinguish immediate protection,
current inherited entitlement, newly enacted but future-start standard and
observed access. Areas with greatest unmet need cannot be postponed merely
because serving easy places gives a better headline; the Assembly must state
and defend any geographic sequence, interim support and deadline. Failure
to meet a milestone requires a fresh public decision and preserves accrued
claims; a minister cannot self-extend a launch hold forever.

This account itself can become a gatekeeper. Treasury might suppress a
liability, a provider might inflate scarcity to block an alternative, an
auditor might share a sponsor, or a common vendor might control every
source. Therefore original records remain federated; audit and opposition
have protected access; methods and dissent survive revision; courts can
review material falsification, withheld evidence and unlawful deprivation.
Sensitive personal or security information may need protected inspection
instead of total publication. A reviewer may correct the record and enforce
lawful procedure, not acquire general budget or service-ranking authority.
The budget must fund this review, records continuity and manual/alternate
operation as part of the same account.

## Effect, limits and next bounded test

The change strengthens C06's countrywide budget obligation, C14's
commencement sequence, proposed A5 and the founding phase-F check. It
affects all C03–C14 consumers, especially the social floor, local
equalization, care leave, housing/mobility, essential networks, bank
protection, defense/hazards and AI. Existing statutes and claims would
continue until legitimate change; this research does not impose a new
filing burden on an actual government. No amount from the older invented
scenario is carried forward as present headroom. Both unrestricted
ministerial budgets and a central affordability veto remain live rivals
if evidence or democratic choice shows this account delays protection
or hides power.

**Evidence:** document-to-document reconciliation and the cited primary
institutional guidance; no local original observations or quantified
capacity. **Research selection:** federated source-backed account with
elected, explicit whole-package decisions and truthful commencement
states. **Legal authorization:** none. **Operational readiness:** unknown
and untested; no real staff, payment access, archives, audited opening
ledger or institutional independence. **Observed outcomes:** none.

The next bounded action is a **real-jurisdiction opening-resource
assessment**, if a lawful adopting community exists. Its deliverable is
one period-by-period account of current obligations and options, original
source references, disagreements, a collision table, independently
inspectable cash and physical constraints, an affected-public hearing
and the Assembly's prospective decision. Completion means every material
new commitment has a named payer/provider and every known shared bottleneck
or unpaid right is visible; it does not mean all shortages disappear.
The actual budget and staff available to this project for such fieldwork
are zero. Reopen this design if the account becomes a technical veto,
if its cost diverts more help than it enables, if people cannot challenge
source errors safely, or if a simpler public budget yields more honest and
usable control. Without actual observations, retain “unverified” rather
than fabricating a national feasibility certificate.
