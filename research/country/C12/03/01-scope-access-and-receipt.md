# Public attention: scope, access and receipt

*Canonical FA01–FA03; part of [C12/03](../03-finite-attention-and-lawful-closure.md).*

**FA01 — A prior public charter, not manufactured consent.** Before intake, the
competent issuing authority publishes the exact proposal/version, enabling law,
question, affected scope, baseline/no-change and materially different rivals;
who will select, advise, decide and execute; actual source and spending ceilings;
formal examination capacity and dates; privacy/custody; independent challenges;
and what can reopen the decision. A public consultation supplies arguments and
evidence, not a vote or permission to enact. The Assembly retains reserved policy;
material delegated rules retain affirmative elected approval under C03.

Use at least 30 calendar days of open intake, then publish the selected portfolio
and allow 10 calendar days for selection/framing correction before its substantive
hearing closes. Complete supported examination and the reasoned closure report
within 90 calendar days from opening. The competent elected body may authorize
one reasoned extension of at most 30 calendar days before expiry. Longer controlling
legal periods prevail; this package cannot shorten the 90-day amendment minimum
or extend its 180-day ballot limit. An ordinary urgent exception requires its
separate prior-law grounds/expiry; FA supplies no emergency power.

**FA02 — Legal claims have separate receivers.** A report suggesting immediate
liberty, clinical safety, status, due benefit, electoral or other individual
legal injury is assisted to the competent legal/service receiver with a receipt
and its actual useful-time deadline. The first public office retains referral
responsibility until acceptance; the person may also approach that receiver
directly. A claim need not arrive as a technical dossier or be true to deserve
lawful triage. Consultation staff do not decide its merits through labeling.
Mandatory hearings, protected petitions and valid signatures do not compete for
FA slots. Closing a consultation closes none of those claims or court routes.

**FA03 — Finite intake without a speech license.** Offer staffed, assisted,
private, collective, spoken, demonstrated and future-media routes to a concise
issue/source reference. No mandatory text conversion, universal public identity,
paid agent or public life log. Preserve source meaning, translation losses and
unsupported semantics; provide a second interpretation when meaning is disputed.
A person can identify the situation and why it matters in ordinary language.

Before opening, publish comparable formal intake allowances, actual storage,
source-inspection and translation limits in the charter, with free assistance
and an accessible exception for disability, language or unfamiliar media.
These limits govern state-funded processing, not lawful public speech. Give a
receipt for an accepted formal unit; rejected/oversized payloads receive a safe
reason and a usable assisted way to identify the issue, not a false substantive
review. Retain the bounded reference, original-source location and custody/retention
terms, not an unlimited obligation to archive every generated byte. Safely record
intake overload, lost material and coverage unknowns. Shared sponsorship and agent
mandates are disclosed proportionately; private testimony uses protected custody.

The charter fixes allowances in relation to funded receiver hours, risk and media
access, with published reasons and challenge, not an arbitrary global byte limit.
A purportedly adequate allowance with no usable alternative is a defect. Repeated
agents share their principal's allowance; rejecting a copy says nothing about the
merit of a genuinely new reason. Open discussion and later ordinary proposals
continue outside this finite funded docket. No first-come vendor flood purchases
all the slots or creates cost-plus public reimbursement.
