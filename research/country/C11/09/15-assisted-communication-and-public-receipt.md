# DP70–DP77. Preserve what people mean when public services use mediated communication

*Design395 · Proposed ordinary operating duties within [public digital operation](../09-public-digital-operation-and-continuity.md).*

**DP70 — Accept the person's communication through a usable route.**
A covered service owner provides free assistance needed for effective
access to a consequential public act. Include interpreted speech,
sign language, supported communication, accessible text and other
lawful ways the person chooses to express meaning. Offer direct human
and offline contact without a tool, account or intermediary. Record
protected first receipt under AP even when meaning needs clarification.
An unsupported medium triggers help, not a finding of abandonment or
lack of capacity. Give a usable receipt distinguishing what was
received, what remains uncertain and which original deadline applies.

**DP71 — Permit bounded AI help where the task can support it.** A sector
owner may qualify optional automated navigation, captions, translation
drafts and explanations under DP33–DP37. Specify language, variety,
medium, task, version, consequential errors and routes outside the
model's demonstrated scope. Mark generated or edited renditions and
give a quick way to question them. Compare with professionally prepared
standard materials and direct qualified service. Permit a reviewed
accessible template to operate at its exact lawful scope. AI can help
find a route or prepare a draft. It cannot authoritatively reinterpret a
person's request, invent a missing response or decide what was meant.

**DP72 — Qualify the material meaning before relying on it.** Where
mediated meaning matters to an entitlement, binding direction, care
choice, formal notice, refusal, withdrawal, consent or independent
challenge, the competent officer must obtain qualified interpretation
or equivalent evidence under applicable sector law. A directly
communicating qualified bilingual worker may establish meaning within
their actual scope. Provide an independent interpreter where required
by conflict, privacy, coercion or sector law. Preserve distinct
language, disability and communication support needs. Let the person
correct the interpretation using a usable medium. Where appropriate,
check the intended next action in the person's own terms without
requiring a scripted statement. A fluency score, silence, model
agreement, click or interpreter's presence does not establish assent.

**DP73 — Keep urgent action available while meaning is unsettled.**
Identify the original service, protective and filing deadlines.
Where misunderstanding or missing interpretation could expose someone
to harm, the receiving owner escalates promptly to a qualified route.
Use already lawful provisional protection or safe independent action
where available. A request in need of clarification remains open.
No adverse inference or new compulsory act can rest on an unsupported
rendition. For a funded change of intake medium,
confirm comparable use under the actual cash, staffing and privacy
conditions before relying on it. Separate refusal of a tool or
account from refusal of the underlying service. Preserve the
original filing, safe alternative and independent correction
through the authorized receivers. Preserve clinical, judicial, safety and electoral authority
and their useful-time review. An unavailable interpreter can expose
a delivery failure. It cannot authorize a model to supply consent
or create a general rule that urgent action must wait.

**DP74 — Retain only the evidence needed to resolve the act.** Identify
the person's original contribution, a derived rendition, material
uncertainty, any correction, its scope and the responsible recipient.
Where keeping original speech, sign or other sensitive media is
unsafe or unnecessary, use a lawfully agreed attributable record,
with an independent review route and visible limits. Follow
applicable evidence and preservation duties. No standing duty to
record a conversation, store biometrics or send private testimony
to a model vendor follows. Protect confidentiality, the person's
access, purpose-limited reuse, lawful retention and deletion.
Do not reuse a supporting person's wording as the resident's consent.

**DP75 — Fund separate qualified receiving across shared failures.**
The sector owner purchases accessible ordinary and urgent interpreting
with actual language, medium, skill, location, availability and
acceptable waiting windows. Shared pools can reduce waiting. Count
each booked interval once and test common staffing, communications,
credentials and supplier control across services. Fund an independently
usable route before relying on it, with local or otherwise accessible
receiving and coverage for infrequent needs. Preserve a resident's
choice of a safe supporter and offer a qualified independent service.
No child, relative, worker or volunteer is presumed available.
Claim a gain only when verified released time can reach its intended
task without weakening existing and emergency routes.

**DP76 — Follow a material correction to every authorized receiver.**
On credible disputed meaning, preserve safe evidence and start prompt
independent reconsideration. Check any award, denial, notice, payment
or service changed by the rendition. Keep original protected filing
dates and deadlines for due public action. A materially defective
required notice cannot start an adverse reply or appeal period under
this proposed notice rule. Use urgent relief or existing provisional
authority when waiting would cause harm. Give the correction to each
lawful dependent receiver, verify usable receipt and restore what is
owed. Preserve other parties' applicable rights, defense and lawful
finality. Do not silently overwrite a disputed original or treat
corrected text as actual restoration.

**DP77 — Review service, power and succession together.** Elected
bodies set funding and access duties. Qualified sector officials own
decisions, independent reviewers and courts hear challenges, and
the digital support role supplies replaceable tools. Test receipt,
qualified availability, rework and error patterns by actual scope,
including people who gave up or could not enter. Publish safe
coverage gaps and corrective action. Adopt prospectively with paid
staff and transfer routes, original duties and stronger rights intact.
Reopen on lost meaning, missed protection, forced dependence,
a version change affecting the task or an independently verified
simpler route.
