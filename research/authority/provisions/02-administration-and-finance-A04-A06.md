# Candidate provisions A4–A6: Administration, finance and budget lapse

*Part of [Authority and legal transition](../../AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION.md); A4 ordinary-clock/standing-source join revised in design 143; other provisions retain scoped earlier research, not enacted law.*

**A4 — Administration and individual remedies.** Cabinet and accountable local agencies
implement published law under annual or standing lawful spending authority.
Ordinary statutory decision duties run from protected first receipt, with actual
help, a finite reasoned disposition, usable notice and independent omission
review under AP/B16. Completeness, referral or software queues do not restart
the clock; silence grants neither approval nor final denial. Special/earlier
rights and fair adverse reply retain their actual law.
Adverse decisions identify authority,
facts, evidence, reasons, decision-maker and independent remedy. Urgent protection
and basic-service duties begin when a competent receiving service learns of the need;
a late completeness notice cannot restart their clock. A court can order lawful
relief and require evidence of actual delivery. Agency error, provider action or
AI output cannot silently rewrite general entitlement.
Statute defines which authorized receivers may preserve a first claim date, their
handoff and urgent-protection duties, and when a competent recipient has accepted
the matter; it preserves direct sector and independent-review routes. Receipt does
not let an intake operator decide another body's entitlement or require a common
AI account. Shared support remains subject to purpose limits, alternate operation
and separation of a challenged decision from its final reviewer.
Political appointments have enacted scope; operational recruitment and promotion
use open, job-related criteria and review, with lawful discipline and protection
against partisan purge.
Procurement law distinguishes need, competitive award or justified exception,
contract management, independent delivery verification, payment and audit.
Supplier ownership, conflicts, amendments and performance remain reviewable.
No contract grants taxation, coercion, general entitlement or lawmaking power.
An essential-service contract names records custody, receiving operator,
funded exit and an effective public remedy when the supplier fails.

An Assembly statute must identify the issuer, purpose, outer content and scope,
affected people, permitted subdelegation and scrutiny class of a generally
binding rule. The rule's effect, including cumulative changes and operative
AI criteria, controls its class rather than its title. Material delegated
instruments require an affirmative Assembly vote; narrower routine rules
need accessible notice, reasons, authenticated publication and an opposition
call-in route. A narrowly necessary urgent instrument has a prior-law trigger,
fixed expiry and prompt independent and elected review. No regulation,
internal guidance, private standard or model update can create an offense,
tax, general force power, new core eligibility restriction or durable
constitutional change. Courts may set aside an unlawful instrument and
protect pending claims but do not write its replacement policy.

**A5 — Tax, money and expenditure.** Only enacted law authorizes taxes, borrowing
limits, currency arrangements, essential entitlements and general expenditure.
Tax law fixes the base, rates, relief, local delegation, effective date and
review route. Equalization compares standardized service need with potential
own-source capacity, funds enacted national minimum duties and preserves a
reviewable transition when the formula changes; a locality retains lawful
rate choice but cannot extinguish a national right by under-collection.
Assembly approves a consolidated annual budget and explicit standing essential-
obligations schedule. Treasury keeps separate commitment, verification, payment
and reconciliation controls; temporary provisional payment is allowed only within
a specific law and ceiling. Revenue, debt, contingent liabilities, arrears and
local transfers are disclosed without counting internal transfers twice.
Each budget and material change presents a source-backed, whole-country
commitment account that separates legal duty, authorized spending, cash when
due, physical delivery, actual receipt and executed remedy. It reconciles
shared qualified staff, sites, water, energy, materials, imports, maintenance
and domestic-liquidity, inflation, foreign-currency and exchange exposure
across national and local duties. Missing
capacity remains unknown or unmet, not zero. Sector and local authorities
attest their inputs; Treasury reconciles cash; audit, opposition and affected
people can challenge material omissions. The account informs the Assembly's
public vote but gives no accountant, auditor, vendor or AI a general veto over
lawful policy. A future-start service names an objective commencement rule,
interim protection and review date; a delayed start cannot erase an accrued
claim or be extended by an undefined executive readiness label.
Financial law distinguishes insured deposits, safeguarded payment assets,
investment risk and debt; it protects a usable basic payment route without
compelling a lender to extend credit. Banks and materially connected nonbanks
face function- and risk-based supervision, with separate conduct complaints,
personal/small-firm insolvency and financial-firm resolution. A statutory
financial-stability council may use only enumerated, bounded, reviewable
system-wide tools on a recorded systemic-risk finding; competent supervisors
apply them. Neither council, supervisor, payment operator nor AI may direct
a named loan, create a public guarantee, change tax or benefit law, or use
financial access to control lawful political activity. Exceptional public
support and industrial credit need distinct Assembly authorization and a
visible loss account.

**A6 — Budget lapse.** A preadopted temporary bridge for specified existing operations
expires after its limited term and cannot authorize new policy. Enumerated standing
statutory obligations continue unless lawfully amended; their cash and material
shortfalls remain publicly reported and challengeable. No executive or treasury
action may extend the bridge or cancel an entitlement solely because the annual
budget is late. Assembly must deliberate on a funded successor, with opposition
access and rights review.

The [candidate Continuity and Effective Access Act](../../country/C06/19-continuity-law-and-delivery-package.md)
provides E01–E20 ordinary-law implementation hypotheses for A4–A6 and
independent review: receipt, due income, urgent receiving, narrow substitute,
timely contest access and recorded shortfall. Its exact clocks and scope
remain proposed; it neither adopts these candidate provisions nor expands
an actual statutory mandate or the separate budget bridge.
