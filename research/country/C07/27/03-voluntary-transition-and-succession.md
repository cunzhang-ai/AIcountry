# YC09–YC12. Voluntary support into adulthood

[Stable child care](../27-stable-child-care-and-adult-transition.md)

**YC09 — Prepare an exit the person can actually use.** Offer a
youth-led plan six calendar months before an expected end of official
out-of-home care or at first lawful receipt if that date is closer.
For an unexpected exit, address needs at their useful time and offer
the complete plan within 14 calendar days. Name the still responsible
service, an independent adviser chosen from qualified routes, actual
housing, safe contact, education, income, health and paid support
receivers. Verify keys or other lawful access, safe transport, usable
funds and actual contact. A nominal referral, unaccepted voucher or
promised future apartment cannot discharge the housing duty. A late
plan does not authorize a hold, eviction or unsafe compulsory return.

**YC10 — Make the eighteenth birthday a change in authority.**
At 18, B21 adult agency governs. Offer voluntary continuation in an
existing safe placement through the twenty-first birthday when the
person and carer or provider both freely agree and the arrangement
meets adult and child safeguarding requirements. Provide a funded
supported housing alternative if continuation is unsuitable, refused
or ends. The local duty service arranges and pays for safe interim
receiving at the useful time if a planned option fails. Respect
withdrawal, ordinary tenancy and employment rights and existing child
occupants' needs. Neither the former placement nor the fallback
extends a child order, gives the provider adult decision authority or
requires a former carer to work without consent or pay.

**YC11 — Preserve a route back to transition support.** Until the
twenty-fifth birthday, a person who was in official out-of-home care
before 18 and has transition needs may return directly for an adviser
and assessed bridging help, regardless of the length of care or an
earlier refusal. Access uses minimum necessary evidence with agency
help to recover missing records. It does not require a family sponsor,
attendance in training or a clinical diagnosis. A former placement
is not a guaranteed address. General housing, personal security,
clinical care and S/AS rights remain available on their own grounds.
Confirm actual paid adult successors before ending transition
components. Age or change of payer cannot erase a matured payment,
existing tenancy, pending claim, or separately owed adult support.

**YC12 — Fund transition before changing the service.** The Assembly
sets the floor, lawful source and actual commencement. Local and
joint providers retain accountable delivery under that floor. Apply
FT/OP/T to continued cases, legal dates, records, staffing, contract
and housing claims, confidentiality and funded accepted successors.
Price the retained system and the additional recurring duty separately,
including cases near 18 and young adults seeking to return. Preserve
stronger actual protections. Reopen on unsafe disruption, inaccessible
review, selective placement, forced dependence, unpayable capacity
or a better complete rival. The ages and plan dates are proposed
parameters, open to legitimate choice and evidence.

[HL06](../../C09/25/02-secure-home-and-accountable-handover.md) joins
the general housing duty to the actual YC sending service and adult
receiver. The voluntary support terms and original due dates continue.
