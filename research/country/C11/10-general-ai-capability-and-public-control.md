# C11. General AI capability: protect people before external exposure

*Design183 · GA01–GA24 · Proposed ordinary law, not actual permission or a deployment.*

The country should enable useful research and plural AI while governing severe
upstream risks that product-sale and sector-use rules can miss, including equally
hazardous specialized capability without an equivalent sector gate. Select a **bounded
pre-exposure safety regime**, with a separate civilian technical-safety office,
ordinary courts and elected control of its scope. Do not license every model,
training run, publication or private conversation. No AI acquires sovereign power.

| Canonical part | Positive choice |
|---|---|
| [GA01–GA06: scope and stages](10/01-scope-training-and-exposure.md) | Actual control, three severe-harm grounds, limited compute notice, contained research, separately assessed exposure |
| [GA07–GA12: responsible judgment](10/02-office-evidence-and-decisions.md) | Qualified protected office, distinct assessors, proportionate evidence, finite decisions and direct courts |
| [GA13–GA18: controls and failure](10/03-controls-incidents-and-remedies.md) | Configured access, irreversible distribution, incidents, bounded stop and actual relief |
| [GA19–GA24: freedom, source and renewal](10/04-freedom-source-and-transition.md) | Protected inquiry, democratic scope, paid capacity, lawful legacy receiving, genuine alternatives and reopening |

Existing DP governs public digital operation; it gains no private-model licence.
TS/TD scheduled activities, PD privacy, KR knowledge, MP competition, WL work,
ordinary civil/criminal law and C12 mandates continue independently. Passing GA
never licenses a clinical act, weapons activity, data theft, election interference
or an unauthorized agent transaction. C08's VD offence list is not expanded.

The research choice closes a specific missing upstream duty, not all AI risk.
Thresholds, staffing, performance and regulatory independence remain hypotheses.
The [country decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-general-ai-capability-and-democratic-control/decision-record.md)
compares sector/ex-post protection, narrow prevention, broad licensing and a
moratorium; uncertainty and loss-of-control disagreement remain explicit.
No actual public, legislature, qualified assessor or funded safety service is claimed.
