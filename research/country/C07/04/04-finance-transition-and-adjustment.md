# Y13–Y16: financing rights rather than one year's margin

*Part of [C07 retirement candidate](../04-retirement-security.md); design79 with design157 pooled-source comparison.*

[Y21–Y24](06-pooled-source-and-life-course-comparison.md) retains the public
architecture/parameters and makes credit/internal G-to-S versus remaining
support explicit. Its36 stationary scenarios are no actual lifetime/national
source or closed transition valuation. New common-factor elections have their
own prospective12-month notice; old units/quotes and RF/CP/other clocks persist.

**Y13 — Pooled finance and a payment reserve.** General revenue funds basic
pension, credit transfers, explicit subsidies and health/care under distinct
appropriations. Insurance contributions pool current risk and pay due earned/
survivor benefits; they are not individual investment balances. Legal public
backing remains explicit; it is no proof of available cash or an automatic
tax, money issue, asset seizure or diversion from another protected service.
Treasury must fund a disclosed shortage through the ordinary elected route,
with interim protection and urgent court access continuing under E/I.

Keep a normal usable-cash reserve of four months of scheduled earned,
survivor and administrative payments **after ordinary due payments**, with
prior-law shock use and a funded replenishment plan. Identify currency,
bank/custodian access and collateral; a long bond is not day-1 cash. Existing
legacy reserves remain assigned to their obligations, not swept as apparent
surplus. Separate reserve investments, other legacy assets and accrued
liabilities. No compelled purchase/rollover of sovereign or industrial-policy
debt; actual risk-limited investment authority and willing counterparties
are needed. Diversification cannot be asserted from a public-owner label.

**Y14 — Full cohort and national accounts.** Each budget contains qualified
long-horizon projections for current and future cohorts, accrued old-age/
survivor claims, credits, administration, basic/support interactions, receipts,
assets, cash due and uncertainty. Compare at least 75 years plus a disclosed
remaining-claims tail; this horizon is a research default, not full valuation.
Stress longevity, prices, labor earnings, caregiving and import/production
capacity. Reproductive/migration choices and AI productivity are not guaranteed
inputs. Publish methods and dissent through the separately governed evidence
service; missing original data means liability unknown, not zero. Assembly
uses the evidence but an actuarial calculation cannot change rights.

The [bounded finance comparison](../../../settlement/retirement/02-finance-and-comparison.md)
shows why the old 188 margin cannot fund the general floor indefinitely.
Current retirement histories, future population, all-duty costs and actual
productivity remain unknown. A higher nominal GDP or restricted surplus
does not produce care workers, goods, willing loans or a legal appropriation.

**Y15 — Explicit continuity and commencement.** Map every predecessor basic,
earned, care, disability, survivor and pending claim by person/period/source
and lawful successor. Preserve vested amounts and source-law indexation;
use a proven component match to prevent double payment, not an aggregate
subtraction. [WF46–WF48](../../C06/58/10-paired-net-source-and-legacy-bridge.md)
selects same-basic-component top-up to B, retaining stronger eligible old-law
amounts/conditions without earned/private offset. Preserve old annual units;
only prospective new service gets new Y units. Unclassified source labels,
unreceived remittances and a mature40-year illustration supply no replacement
credit or retroactive accrual. [CP03–CP04](../../C06/28/02-dated-commitments-and-equality.md) now
requires an express Assembly-enacted new-coverage/new-service-accrual schedule,
with exact cohort dates, contribution/accrual/credit matching, source and
interim protection. No blanket second-year start or ministerial readiness
condition supplies it. Prior service, vested amounts/indexation and passed
due dates retain preserved law and explicit credit recognition. Fund record
conversion, paid overlap, staff, review, reserves and payment channels. A missed enacted start is
non-delivery requiring elected response, not automatic cancellation or a
minister's indefinite readiness delay. Existing software/source law is unchanged.

**Y16 — Democratic prospective adjustment.** A statute, with public reasons,
cohort incidence and affected participation, may change future contribution,
new-service accrual or basic/age rules subject to rights and courts. An age
increase cannot affect anyone reaching the current age within ten years
after enactment. No automatic longevity/asset trigger cuts accrued claims,
raises age or shifts care to relatives. Retain/repair, a stronger universal
basic, alternative accrual and genuine funded models remain contestable.
If the chosen package cannot be financed, disclose the actual burden and
propose a lawful whole-country revision; do not let this procedure approve
its own evidence, source finance or institutional replacement.

[NP](../../C06/33-protection-production-and-national-expansion.md) joins actual
I/S/Y cohorts and paid receiving/review/production sources. The225 fixture is no
inherited liability map; restricted insurance remains separate. A general claim's
survival is not automatically first access to every public cash unit; any new
payment priority needs compatible law and independent review, never cancellation
of accrued rights, indexation or corresponding contribution credits.

[RF05–RF09](../../C06/36/02-pension-units-cash-and-generations.md) now confirms
pooled tax-backed finance, rejects default mandatory funded conversion, caps
only future service after notice and specifies next-due cash plus four-month
post-due protection. Active prior units, actual care-credit bases and survivor
stocks/expiry must continue through75years and the accrued tail; old constant
15,000/225/6 lines are frozen examples, not a national demographic forecast.

[LPC01–LPC08](../../C14/02/06-legacy-claims-and-public-closure.md) assigns
unclaimed and later pension obligations to a funded successor with review.
