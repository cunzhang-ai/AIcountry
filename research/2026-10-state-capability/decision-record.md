The bounded question is how C03's elected government can implement C04–C09's
rights, services and public investments when people who appoint, tender,
inspect, pay and hear complaints may share a patron or vendor. Design 19
called for a professional civil service and reviewable procurement, but did
not say who makes each consequential decision, how a defeated cabinet
relinquishes control, or what happens when a supplier fails. Better statutes
and budgets cannot improve lives if their delivery chain is captured.

**Values:** democratic policy direction, equal service access, competent
administration, decent public work, public economy, fair supplier entry,
individual rights and peaceful replacement. **Constraints:** enacted law,
appropriation, labor and property rights, current claims, finite skilled
staff, procurement markets and continuing services. **Empirical claims
not established:** that open recruitment prevents capture, that more tendering
improves a given service, that an integrity reviewer is independent, or
that an exit operator can actually take over. **Design hypothesis:** a
replaceable elected policy-maker plus a professional, reviewable execution
chain can preserve both responsiveness and continuity if appointment,
budget, records and remedies do not converge under one controller.

## Competing diagnoses and coherent packages

| Package | Diagnosis and mechanism | Failure or tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Retain design 19's general merit and procurement language | The main gap is legal detail; future ordinary statutes can allocate it. | Until then, a minister can call patronage “confidence,” a contractor can certify its own work, and a formal appeal can lack records or a receiving operator. This is honest as an incomplete reference, not an operating chain. |
| Cabinet appointment and contract freedom after each election | Bureaucratic obstruction is the main problem; elected winners replace managers and suppliers to ensure responsiveness. | Job and contract dependence can make opposition, audit and professional advice unsafe. Purges lose capability and existing claims; incumbents can use the same power before losing. Electoral victory does not authorize violating individual rights. |
| Central independent appointment/procurement/integrity gate | Corruption is the main problem; a protected specialist body pre-approves senior staff and material contracts. | Its own appointments, budget, evidence and appeal path can be captured, and its delays can veto elected policy or urgent service. One approval label does not verify delivery or independence. |
| Outsource most operations to competitive firms and platforms | Public employment is the main source of rigidity; contracts and customer choice replace administrative hierarchy. | In concentrated or essential markets, exit may be fictional; vendors can control records, eligibility, workers and payment. The state still owes rights and needs enough capacity to commission, inspect, step in and remedy. |
| **Selected: elected policy with professional, separable execution** | Political and administrative powers need different mandates: cabinet sets lawful priorities; merit-based service and plural providers deliver; procurement, finance, audit and remedies leave distinct evidence trails and actual exit capacity. | More people, time and funding are required. Procedural complexity may deter small suppliers or delay protection, and professional officials may still form an unaccountable bloc. Exceptions and review must remain bounded. |

The selection is conditional. A direct public provider may be better than
tendering in a thin market; a short urgent purchase may be better than a
full competition during danger; the elected cabinet may legitimately
replace a policy leader within law. None of these exceptions gives a
general patronage, emergency or contractor-sovereignty power. A later
affected public may choose a different allocation after comparing actual
delivery, corruption, costs, rights and control.

## The selected chain from mandate to received service

1. **Policy and staff.** Assembly enacts duties, funding and review; cabinet
   sets policy priorities within that law. A limited set of political advisers
   has published positions, duties and end dates. Ordinary and senior career
   jobs use open, role-related competition and accessible recruitment. A
   cabinet may select a senior manager from a qualified shortlist with
   reasons, conflict disclosure and fixed lawful mandate. Temporary acting
   posts cannot be repeatedly renewed to bypass this route. Staff can offer
   candid contrary advice without retaliation, then must execute lawful
   decisions. Appointment protection is paired with performance and
   misconduct procedures, not lifetime immunity. A new cabinet cannot
   purge staff for prior lawful service or political opinion.
2. **Delivery responsibility.** Each essential guarantee has a named public
   authority and service leader, budget, workforce, assets, suppliers and
   complaint/receiving plan. Professional clinical, teaching, legal and
   inspection judgments are respected; ministers cannot direct an
   individual result through a contract. A vacancy, delayed tender or
   platform outage remains a public shortfall, not an efficiency gain.
   Direct, cooperative, private or mixed delivery is compared against
   full-life cost, rights, access, worker conditions, market concentration,
   maintenance and viable replacement, not only bid price.
3. **Purchase and payment.** A lawful commissioner documents need and
   functional requirements before tender. Competition is the default,
   with accessible proportionate bids and predetermined criteria. Material
   beneficial owners, official interests, subcontractors, exceptions,
   awards, amendments, payments and outcomes are recorded. A narrow urgent
   or sole-source exception has a prior legal ground, ceiling, duration,
   alternatives and prompt review. Splitting purchases or modifying a
   contract to avoid those limits is not permitted. Need-setter, evaluator,
   contract manager, delivery verifier, Treasury payer and auditor hold
   distinguishable roles and records. A small locality can obtain shared
   professional support without ceding its elected policy choice.
4. **Challenge and repair.** Residents, workers and bidders have routes
   proportionate to their affected interests: service complaint and urgent
   protection, labor remedy, procurement challenge, protected reporting,
   audit access and court review. A public authority cannot outsource the
   only appeal to the same supplier. Protected records allow an independent
   reviewer to find what happened. Review identifies a person or body
   capable of suspending, repairing, recompeting, compensating or taking
   over; success is assessed by received service and executed remedy.
5. **Exit and handover.** Essential contracts prearrange transferable
   records, credentials, equipment access, labor and claim continuity,
   funded overlap, receiving staff and a time-bounded step-in route.
   Contractor insolvency, political defeat or an AI outage does not cancel
   the underlying right. A paper exit clause without lawful assets and
   people is reported as unavailable. The outgoing cabinet and supplier
   must preserve evidence and enable lawful successor access; the incoming
   cabinet receives authority, not a right to erase pending claims.

Public enterprises have a disclosed ownership mandate, open competency-based
board selection, professional management, worker voice, separate commercial
and funded noncommercial accounts, public audit and lawful removal. The
Assembly sets their duties and risk through law and budget; ministers cannot
issue secret credit, employment or procurement instructions. An enterprise
may compete or supply an essential fallback under its mandate, but public
ownership cannot make its service self-certifying or immune to residents'
complaints. Guarantees and losses enter C06's common account. Privatizing
or closing one requires successor duties for accrued wages, pensions,
contracts, care and ecological liabilities.

Integrity rules cover political officials, career managers, public-enterprise
leaders, bidders and significant subcontractors. Material interests and
beneficial control are disclosed to a competent reviewer with secure access;
recusal, reassignment, divestment or a bounded cooling-off period addresses
the actual conflict. Protected reporting includes interim anti-retaliation
relief and safe evidence custody. An affected person can reach audit, an
ombud, a competent prosecutor or a court within their distinct mandates,
so the implicated manager is not the only intake. Audit, investigation,
prosecution and judgment are separated so an integrity office cannot itself
blacklist, convict and confiscate. A complaint triggers assessment, not guilt; accused
people have reasons, defense and challenge. Agencies report proved losses,
recoveries and service consequences without publishing sensitive witnesses.
The anti-corruption institution's own appointments, funds, data access and
case selection must be contestable; an incumbent cannot starve it, and it
cannot indefinitely freeze a lawful opponent's work through unresolved
allegations. Narrow interim preservation or suspension needs reasons, a
time limit, review and a funded service-continuity route. AI can detect
anomalies and help compare bids, but it is not an awarding, disqualifying or
final investigative authority.

The [OECD public-service leadership recommendation](https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/public/doc/641/641.en.pdf)
informs merit, impartial advice and senior appointments. The
[OECD procurement recommendation](https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/public/doc/320/body-text.en.html)
covers the full need-to-payment cycle, competition, exceptions, supplier
access and professional capacity. The
[OECD public-integrity recommendation](https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/public/doc/353/353.en.pdf)
extends integrity beyond one ministry to public enterprises and contracted
services. The [UN Convention against Corruption](https://www.unodc.org/res/ji/import/international_standards/united_nations_convention_against_corruption/united_nations_convention_against_corruption.pdf)
offers primary comparative provisions on public appointments and purchasing.
These norms explain possible mechanisms; none tests this project's
appointments, markets, budgets, independence or actual delivery.

## Compatibility, objections and transition

The selection revises C04 and proposed A4, qualifies C05 public enterprises,
C06 spending/verification, C07 service commissioning, C08 remedies, C11 AI
and records, C12 vendor power and C14 founding. It preserves C03's elected
policy authority, C04's funded local autonomy and service fallback, C06's
separate commitment/verification/payment controls, and prior worker,
contractor, creditor and resident claims. It does not enact a universal
staffing commission, force every service into a tender or import a new
constitutional veto. Old procurement, labor, public-enterprise and digital
clauses require exact lawful migration; no earlier review automatically
approves this new appointment/contract chain.

At founding or transfer, a valid predecessor or legitimately enacted law
must inventory posts, political/career designations, payroll, qualifications,
discipline and grievances; live procurement and amendments; supplier owners,
subcontractors, assets and data; public-enterprise mandates and liabilities;
and pending complaints, litigation and service claims. The first budget
must fund professional procurement, audit, secure reporting, legal aid,
records and actual receiving teams alongside the service itself. Staff
retain accrued wages and process rights; contractors retain lawful payment
and challenge rights; affected people retain their service claims. A new
cabinet may revise future policy through law, not by purging staff or
withholding a supplier's due payment. Emergency continuity uses existing
bounded authority with reasons and review, not a self-renewing exception.

Important objections remain. Merit tests can encode privilege or exclude
experienced community workers; protected officials can obstruct change;
cabinet shortlist choice can still reward allies; publication can enable
bid collusion or expose trade secrets; conflict declarations can invade
privacy; and multiple review bodies can delay urgent care. A public backup
may be too expensive or scarce, and a shared procurement service can
become a new local-government veto. The design therefore needs accessible
recruitment, protected but reviewable performance, calibrated publication,
strict exception grounds and actual capacity. These are not claims that
the safeguards will work in an unknown society.

**Evidence qualification:** local predecessor texts and cited primary
comparative sources; no original jurisdictional observations or affected-
party participation. **Research selection:** the separable elected-policy/
professional-execution chain. **Legal authorization:** none. **Operational
readiness:** no established staff, procurement market, independent reviewer,
receiving operator, funding or live transfer test. **Observed outcomes:**
none; no claim of superiority to any current country is supported.

The next bounded test for a real adopting jurisdiction is a **combined
handover and supplier-failure exercise**: an election changes the cabinet
while the largest essential-service vendor fails and a conflict allegation
targets the proposed receiver. Use one audited opening set of staff,
contracts, records, funds and due claims to compare this package, a cabinet-
appointment package and direct public provision. Record who may lawfully
appoint, pay, access evidence, operate the service, challenge and execute
remedies on each day, including what remains undelivered. Completion is a
published dependency and loss map with affected-worker, user, supplier,
opposition and independent-review objections, not merely a passed simulation.
This project has no lawful field mandate or budget. Reopen the selection if
actual service continuity, capture, procedural delay, cost or public
participation favors a rival; when monitoring is absent, retain the
unverified status rather than reporting institutional readiness.
