# Two terminals and one inspection team

Design365 · Constructed comparison under
[BCR01–BCR06](../../country/C10/05/01-border-corridor-continuity-and-physical-receipt.md).

The principal port closes. The public buyer already has lawful title,
the necessary foreign release and adequate onward transport in the
declared case. Four water-repair cargo units must reach their operator
by hour 12. A food receiver has an existing lawful reservation for two
units in the first window and needs one more by hour 18. All counts
are constructed comparable handling slots, with no product-specific
clearance rule implied.

Alternate East can handle five and North four in the first window.
Both rely on the same six-slot inspection team. Assume compatible
cargo, legal custody and travel between the sites are verified. The
team can serve the six combined slots in time under these assumptions.
The second window has four separate slots, two for previously booked
ordinary cargo and one for the remaining food unit.

| First-window case | Qualified slots | Water received by hour 12 | Food received in the first window |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Treat both terminals as full standalone capacity | 6 usable out of 9 advertised | At most 4 | At most 2 under the stated shared allocation |
| Reconcile the routes and reserve together | 6 | 4 | 2 |
| Shared inspection capacity drops to 4 | 4 | 2 | 2 |
| Drop plus independent 2-slot supplement, paid by hour 6 | 6 | 4 | 2 |
| Supplement active; separate lawful hold affects 2 water units | 6 staff slots | 2 | 2 |

The independent supplement requires 3 public cash units by hour 6
and a genuinely separate, competent team. With only 2 available,
it cannot be activated. That leaves two water units outstanding at
hour 12. The case assumes no timely qualified local substitute;
available lawful protection continues and the original shortfall
stays visible.

A staffing supplement cannot release cargo subject to a safety hold.
Use its verified remaining capacity only for eligible work. The later
window can still accommodate the remaining food and both booked
ordinary units under the given assumptions. Its unassigned slot
cannot satisfy the earlier missed water deadline. Record the cost
and delay separately.

A structural alternative is a qualified two-unit inland reserve
owned before the outage. Assume it can be received by hour 12,
uses a separate route, and costs 5 to establish and 2 to replenish
after this use. Under the four-slot inspection case it covers the
remaining water need without the supplement. Compare its ongoing
quality and carrying cost, dependence and replenishment against
repeated surge staffing before adopting it. The emergency example
has no such verified stock available. The model does not claim a real port, carrier,
quality finding, law, budget or service improvement.
