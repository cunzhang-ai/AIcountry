# 214. Conflicting public orders and timely execution

| Owner | Bounded commitment | Receipt or failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Responsible executor and affected parties | Report exact incompatible requirements with original evidence and carry compatible acts | Both courts and affected people have a protected route. A filing has no stay effect. |
| Originating courts or lawfully assigned duty formation | Clarify or vary only necessary implementation within their respective powers | Check actual formation, notice, original clocks, exact scope and lawful acceptance. |
| Treasury, elected source owner and operators | Price independent capacity, accepted services and the next call | Actual dispatch, staff, funds and receiving are distinct. No guessed alternative or court-created appropriation. |
| Independent review and lawful successors | Preserve missed duties, original cases, evidence and relief | Record obstruction and real shortage separately. A late remedy cannot backdate receipt. |
