# Design222. Deliver time people can use and protect the whole service

## Baseline and choice

Design221 is the pinned baseline. Use the working framework and decision
template. TW already selects 36 and 32 normal paid hours with retained
regular pay, rest, paid learning, qualified cover and elected funding.
WF49–WF52 accounts for joint staffing and sources. WL06–WL07 covers
guaranteed hours and real agreement. The gap is a usable rule for placing
released time, protecting the weekly pay of existing hourly workers, and
showing the same accepted service through the resulting real schedules.

Select TW21–TW28. Workers can request a lawful usable pattern. The
employer must show actual constraints for refusing it. Use independent
bargaining and a predictable same-days, same-start default when no
agreement is reached. Protect ordinary weekly pay across pay methods
for the same existing participation. Preserve stronger and genuinely
chosen terms. Use a bounded alternate placement only within the shorter
limit and existing safeguards. Four weeks is a provisional outer limit
for an unagreed temporary placement. It does not permit repeat exemption.

## Evidence, alternatives and real tradeoffs

Chile's timetable, pay protection and no-agreement rule show how the
placement issue can matter after a national vote. The Labour Directorate
chooses larger discrete releases in the observed 44-to-42 example.
That is a genuine rival to a proportional daily fallback. The proposed
default protects predictable existing starts and days. Small daily
reductions can still be unusable. Give actual access to alternatives
and review with worker choice and continued service. French public
rules illustrate distinct hours and continuity scopes. These sources
supply no outcome estimate for the proposed country or optimum calendar.

Retain the strong longer-week repair alternative and the staged
alternative with a later separate 32-hour vote. A simple statutory
week with employer discretion is easier to state and can leave time
fragmented or capture it through standby. Requiring the same four-day
pattern everywhere gives some people larger blocks and can exclude
others or leave vital windows empty. Voluntary shortened schedules
may work well where employers and employees have bargaining power and
funded cover. Retain the national candidate floor, with varied lawful
patterns and actual allocation of disputed time. A deeper funded
comparison can displace the working preference.

Preserving ordinary cash pay can increase hourly costs and affect
hiring, prices or closure. Optional overtime income may fall. Separately
trace those changes, the value of rest or care time and actual service
use. Do not use the worker's released time as unpaid cover. The
authored roster shows a timing problem even with enough total hours.

## Compatibility and bounded next decision

Keep TW18's exact candidate adoption and proposed staged dates, TW01–TW20
protections, WL, CL, S, Y, WF, PE, FB/DB and OP/T. Earlier enacted and
stronger rights survive. A new stage needs a real elected source and
authority. A missed roster is failure to deliver and gives no permission
to postpone enacted rights. Ordinary labour and sector remedies remain
independent. No new tax, work hours inspector, employer subsidy or AI
allocation power is selected. The legislation must make the pay protection and narrow TW22
exception to WL07 explicit. All other material contract changes
retain their ordinary agreement and protection rules.

For an authorized start preserve accrued pay and protected schedule and
case dates, and verify cover, contracts and workers through accepted
paid succession. Exercise an actual service calendar with affected
workers, providers and users. Compare hours placed on different days,
real pay, available skills, leave, one absent worker and retained service.
Price both stages on the same scope and budget. Reopen on time that
cannot be used, hidden standby, reduced ordinary pay, unsafe gaps or a
stronger funded rival. Actual affected participation, independent
review, adoption, paid readiness and observed better lives remain open.
