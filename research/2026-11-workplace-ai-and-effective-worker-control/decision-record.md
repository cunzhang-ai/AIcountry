# Design384 decision. Give workers a real decision before material automated control

## Baseline and selected rule

Design383 WL10 protects reasons, privacy and contestable human
decision. AT18–AT22 supports paid bargaining over material changes
and gains. DP64–DP69 addresses adverse public decisions within its
own scope. The gap is a prior deployment decision for workplace
control, including tools that silently allocate fewer paid offers
or leave a human to sign repeated inferences.

Select [WL47–WL53](../country/C05/12/09-workplace-ai-and-effective-worker-control.md).
Require exact agreement or bounded independent permission for covered
uses. Keep workers' collective and individual power separate, labor
and privacy oversight independent, and elected authority over public
sources. Withhold unsupported adverse use and trace lawful correction
through actual pay and work access. Material changes reopen permission.

## Alternatives and objections

Retain consultation and after-the-fact remedies. This costs less
upfront but employees can face irreversible loss before challenge.
A full worker veto could prevent more intrusive designs, while
blocking a necessary, independently justified improvement or
incentivizing circumvention. Select scoped co-decision and a narrow
independent route. Preserve existing collective and individual rights
and safe ordinary work. A general ban would also block qualified
coordination tools that reduce actual paid burden or improve care.

Representation can miss contractors, applicants and nonstandard
shifts. A new gate may burden small firms or congest labor forums.
Use paid participation, proportionate procedure and actual coverage
and authority. Keep a worker-chosen route, confidential independent
input and remedies. An agreement cannot trade away private life.
Financial or social gains are hypotheses until the work, pay and
receiving evidence qualify. Neither a signature nor a cheaper roster
establishes safety, consent or productivity.

## Evidence, migration and next action

[Primary comparators](sources.md) distinguish existing co-determination,
platform safeguards and qualitative workplace experience. The [constructed roster](../settlement/workplace-ai-and-effective-worker-control/01-two-rosters-and-one-payday.md)
tests actual hours, fallback, payroll and access. No affected-worker
participation, independent legal review or local evidence qualification
has occurred for this proposed law.

Adopt prospectively with existing labor, privacy and ordinary courts
or other competent existing labor forums. Identify coverage, financing,
docket, interim powers and execution before reliance. Keep WL07,
WL10, AT, PD and DP within their own scopes. Protect existing terms,
original claims, lawful records and paid ordinary service. No actual
government, employer, public-budget or software authority changes here.

Next compare a normal roster and reversible paid trial over two
representative cycles under actual worker representation. Include
different shifts and directly affected users. Audit work moved
outside payroll, loss of the tool, original evidence and whether
a correction reaches both roster and payroll. Reopen if approval
becomes nominal, worker voice excludes a group, the ordinary route
cannot be staffed or a simpler design works equally well.
