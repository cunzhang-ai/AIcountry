# Follow the actual payer and the residents a transfer can miss

The Capital Health Council agenda paper EMN-2026-03103 reports
an annual DKK 574 million appropriation for 2027–2030 covering
four transferred tasks, including an additional 56 million. The
hospitals reported 55.2 million of financing needs and Copenhagen
and Frederiksberg a preliminary 25.4 million. Against the 56
million increment, the displayed comparison leaves 24.6 million
uncovered. The two groups' figures carry different service
purposes and include preliminary or disputed assumptions.
The paper identifies reprioritization within the frame, adjusting
the scope of municipal operating agreements and drawing on other
hospital budgets as possible responses. It requests discussion
before a later decision. The arithmetic supplies neither a final
funding gap nor a paid agreement. [Agenda paper, pp. 1–4](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-transferred-duties-and-targeted-grants/original-sources/capital-health-council-funding-paper.pdf).

The Copenhagen November committee agenda separately reports
DKK 54.4 million of 2027 local health investment funding across
Copenhagen, Frederiksberg and Bornholm. Its table attributes
33.3 million to Copenhagen, split 15.3 and 18.0 for designated
municipal purposes. The formal recommendation uses 33 million.
Keep both reported figures. The proposal calls for city approval
before acceptance and identifies the city administration as the
proposed invoice owner against East Denmark Region. Unspent
investment funds must be returned under the stated terms. The
inspection establishes no actual invoice settlement or delivered
service. This grant cannot offset the transferred-task estimate.
[City committee agenda and table 1](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Sundheds-%20og%20Omsorgsudvalget/m%C3%B8de-05112026/dagsorden/punkt-5).
