# IV13–IV17. Make basic access a funded investment obligation

*Part of [public investment](../63-public-investment-and-asset-renewal.md).
Design248 research selection.*

**IV13 — Begin with people who cannot use the service.** For each material
national or cross-local investment, identify applicable legal duties and
current gaps by place and affected group. Follow the journey from a realistic
point of access through a lawful, staffed service in useful time, including
safe return where applicable. Check cost to residents, opening and operating
hours, assistance, disability access, weather, connectivity and receiving
capacity. Include unmet or suppressed need and people who cannot use a car,
device or private help. A proximity model, a register of facilities and
current visits are different evidence. Describe reach that remains unknown.
A national or local average cannot extinguish an individual duty or hide
systematic exclusion inside an otherwise well-served area.

**IV14 — Fund the common floor without a uniform local match.** For an
investment needed to deliver a common statutory duty, do not make a uniform
local matching percentage an eligibility condition. Use MF's lawful cost and
revenue capacity assessment and reconcile attributed local and general funds
once. Separately record any authorized national share, available cash by
milestone and the payer of operating, renewal and fallback costs. A region's
low assigned capacity or lack of a mature bid does not reduce the common
duty. Neither actual undercollection nor deferred maintenance creates an
automatic larger payment. Publish the capacity benchmark and reasons for
allocation, with direct challenge to a material exclusion. Eligibility
cannot depend on a locality's political support for the national government.
Use a bounded funded preparation route where comparable evidence or
design capacity is missing. If lawful usable funding
is absent, record the uncovered duty and bring a funded correction to the
competent elected body. Ordinary grants, existing claims and optional local
enhancements keep their own source and approval rules.

**IV15 — Compare service designs and actual failure, then decide.** Consider
local repair or co-location, staffed outreach, hub plus accessible transport,
and direct or shared provision as real alternatives where applicable. Use the
same relevant needs and service quality, recurring and capital sources,
qualified crews, actual receiving and independent review. Test an adverse
event shared by projects and any cost or work transferred to another place.
Within lawful obligations and resource limits, choose the portfolio by a
recorded elected vote, with unserved people, foregone benefits and dissent
visible. An aggregate increase in visits or a high benefit-cost ratio cannot
discharge a known basic duty. A duty can be met by a different genuinely
usable route. No automatic geographic priority, uniform facility count,
compelled relocation, court project selection or AI capital budget follows.

**IV16 — Bind the service, not just the construction.** The chosen decision
names the local operating owner, national funder where applicable, dated
sources, first useful service and paid accessible fallback through opening
and failure. Separate future discretionary improvements from existing dues.
A fixed capital commitment needs a lawful plan for ongoing service and
handback, with costed access for residents who cannot use the apparent
alternate. Phase only stages that have their own lawful receiving or clearly
bounded preparatory purpose. Keep local elected delivery discretion under
MF and actual national residual duties. Challenge an unavailable route or
unfunded allocation early through current review. Source and safety failure
leave a disclosed duty and remedial action within existing authority.

**IV17 — Verify received access before counting it.** Maintain a named
responsible owner and safe evidence of the first required service under
ordinary and declared adverse conditions. Compare actual eligible demand,
access barriers, waiting and real service against the adopted plan.
Give affected residents and opposition a usable way to challenge missing
scope or displaced burden. Publish source uncertainty, uncovered duties
and the lawful correction or elected disposition. AI can compare maps,
time windows and competing schedules. It cannot decide entitlement, allocate
public capital or certify delivery. Reopen on route loss, new distributional
evidence, material cost, unserved need or a simpler lawful alternative.
Carry original dates, commitments and paid successors under IV, JC and T.
