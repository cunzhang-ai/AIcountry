# One missed connection and two continuing services

This case is authored. Times, funds and operators do not describe an
observed journey or apply Danish terms to a clinic. The person consents
to an accessible alternative and has a confirmed joined trip to a due
clinic service with check-in by 08:50. An interchange lift fails and the
journey owner receives a confirmed alert at 08:10. An independently available vehicle needs 22 minutes
from pickup, and the person needs eight minutes to enter and check in.
Its last useful pickup is 08:20. The dispatcher needs six minutes to
book it, so the latest useful start of that arrangement is 08:14.

| Response | Transport and public-service result |
|---|---|
| Give a reimbursement route after a 30-minute wait | At 08:40 the useful pickup time has passed, even if the expense is later repaid. |
| Treat a listed accessible standby vehicle as spare | That vehicle and driver are already reserved for a due home-care visit. Reassignment would displace another actual duty. |
| Dispatch the independent alternate at the first warning | On the stated terms the confirmed pickup is 08:16 and check-in is 08:46. Existing clinic and home-care authority continue. |
| Neither route is physically available | The clinic must determine a lawful acceptable alternative or retain the missed duty. A paid fare or closed ticket cannot certify care. |

The authorized journey contingency has 60 units available now. The
independent trip costs 55 and is paid directly by the responsible
journey owner. The earlier home-care reservation is a distinct funded
commitment, and is neither a travel saving nor a second source. The
remaining five cannot reserve a second equivalent vehicle. The return
is separately booked under the ordinary journey plan and requires actual
verification. If the alternate shares the failed lift, driver or
dispatcher, its independent capacity falls out of the comparison.
Fault, interoperator settlement and any compensation are decided later
under existing law. A refund helps a passenger who can purchase a timely
alternative but does not discharge the duty in this case.

Use the same test for a child travelling to school or for a person going
to court. The receiving body supplies the relevant deadline and records
actual usable participation. The journey owner works back from that
time. An election, court, care or school duty cannot be transferred to
the dispatcher's rules by booking it as a trip.
