# A real standby reserve without double counting or omitted assets

*SF05–SF06; part of [C06/25](../25-source-finance-and-payment-continuity.md).*

**SF05 — Prefund an independent public-payment alternative.** Statutory
budget/payment owners maintain actual separately safeguarded settlement
assets with qualified public or regulated M05 custodians outside a lender's
estate and sole control. Choose the protected horizon by due lawful claims,
useful receipt times, plausible outage and accepted replacement capacity;
one funded ordinary public-payment period is the reference minimum, not a
ceiling on rights, crisis needs or private access to safe payment accounts.
No custodian decides eligibility, new priorities, bank rescue or political
loyalty. Independent court access, usable assistance, offline routes, distinct
vendors/source custody and mandate revocation need actual staff and proof.

The numerical hybrid keeps this stock as a **standby** while normal payments
use separately available cash; it does not assume a spent reserve instantly
replenishes. The required reference stock is the largest month's gross
Treasury bill, including the monetary coupon before any remittance, plus the
insurance body's first 20 payment: total 179.695833. It is transferred from
existing G/S deposits into actual segregated monetary reserves, not new
spending or an additional claim on the same bank's reserves. Treasury still
has 265.304167 in ordinary bank cash, alongside 159.695833 safeguarded; S has
80 ordinary and 20 safeguarded. Both normal due cash and untouched standby
are checked after the separate legacy 80 payment and zero annual remittance.

On an independently verified interruption, the competent payer uses the
alternative for actual unpaid due items, with original instruction, finality,
recipient choice, no duplicate tax/benefit/wage payment and verified receipt.
Record actual outflow and residual protected stock. A second period requires
actual replenishment/accepted finance; no constant-reserve assumption or
forced return of a recipient's money. If safe-account demand or lawful needs
exceed the source envelope, fund the additional assets or change the whole
architecture lawfully; do not block withdrawals or shrink due rights to fit.

**SF06 — Every funding-relevant asset has an explicit category.** The
[M03 candidate](../24/02-liquidity-exposures-and-conduct.md) now expressly
assigns stable-funding treatment to cash/reserves, marketable sovereign
securities, other/unmapped funded assets and commitments within/beyond a
year. The old text left some categories unspecified; they cannot silently
count as zero. New 50%/100% classifications are national hypotheses requiring
fresh lawful review/adoption, not an existing bank's grandfathered approval.

The source comparison assumes existing producer loans beyond a year and
the actually drawn twelve-month working stock within it. All withdrawable
bank balances are conservatively in the 40% outflow branch; new household
insurance eligibility is not assumed. Government securities count as liquid
only with actual usable independent market evidence; otherwise that branch
fails. Counterparty/related control, collateral, derivatives, currencies,
insurance/compensation and actual accounting remain separate incomplete checks.

[BF05–BF08](../39/02-ownership-backstop-and-drawn-debt.md) confirms G159.695833 and social-insurance S20 standby are different owners from the bank-deposit insurer. They are not insurer F, M11 compensation or bridge capital; any actually authorized transfer must expose source depletion and continuing due claims.
