# C03. Elections and representation

2 November2026 · Integrated design111 · Working selection, not enacted law

Select [EL01–EL24 equal franchise and usable elections](13-equal-franchise-and-usable-elections.md).
Every national elector, including lawful citizens abroad, chooses one nationwide
party/civic list, with an optional person preference. Independents may run as
one-person lists. Exact capped Sainte-Laguë allocates fixed N seats among all
positive valid list votes, without a legal vote-percentage threshold or a special
independent personal-seat exception. A nominee may appear for one list only.
Within each list, preference totals then frozen order determine persons and
unused succession. Party expulsion cannot alone remove a member.

The predecessor two-vote mixed count, N−I deduction and territorial-first mandates
are now a retained rival, not the current working reference. It gave personal
geographic mandates and a smaller organizing area to some independents, but made
a backing/control classification affect national seat allocation and left an
unclosed diaspora territorial track. Single national PR does not promise perfect
proportionality, eliminate party power or prove better government. It sacrifices
those territorial-person guarantees and still has rounding/splitting incentives.
The [comparison](../../settlement/campaign-access/02-franchise-ballot-and-local-elections.md)
shows both the structural example and the selected rule's remaining counterexample.

[EL06](13/02-national-vote-entry-and-count.md) fixes the new national gate at
min(ceil(E_call/100),ceil(E_call/(4N))), with two active endorsements per person,
1…2N actual consenting nominees/list, common V−40 freeze and original-proof cure.
The field is floor(2R/s), not automatically200; actual distinct nominee supply
also constrains total names. A one-person list gets the same gate/count/access,
not a territorial bypass. A clear list vote survives a missing or invalid preference.
Candidate-only clear indications can imply their list under EL14's exact rule.

Ordinary local councils use whole-area PR, their actual C02 electorate and
[EL09–EL12](13/03-local-councils-and-continuity.md) size/formation/continuity rules.
Local democratic power and protected territorial status are not erased by the
national count. N/actual founding public and lawful municipal map/source must
be fixed before call; a platform address or minister cannot supply them.

[EL17–EL20](13/05-receipt-count-and-vacancies.md) supplies actual receiving,
conditional/absent ballots, count evidence, frozen succession, exhausted-pool
supplemental polls and independent useful-time remedies. It appoints no winner
by timeout and does not lower full N/n when seats are empty. National C03/06
formation remains; council formation is separately defined, not a judge's policy.

[EN/CF/ER](10-democratic-entry-and-campaign-resources.md) preserves compatible
entry/access/source/court protections with EL's express one-track/gate changes.
[PC](12-continuous-political-finance.md) continuous source/spend, annual actual-
local/national and referendum duties remain. Called national cash is E v, not
(E+E_T)v; no old due grant is retrospectively canceled or old quote called saving.

All changes require coordinated prospective primary/constitutional law and
C14/RR protected succession. Earlier-called contests, actual count/term/vacancy
law, candidate/voter/grant/contract claims and software permissions retain source.
[Historical count decision](../../2026-10-election-rule/decision-record.md) and
dated101/110 studies remain unchanged; their final judgments do not transfer.
No actual voter usability, independent affected consent, legal adoption, paid
capacity, free competition or superior lives is established by exact arithmetic.

[NM contestable nominations](23-contestable-list-nominations.md) gives
collective selectors control of the consenting pool and fallback order
before the freeze. Keep the existing public preference, independent entry,
seat allocation and elected member's independent mandate.

### Preserve the ordinary electoral cycle through a repeat

For a prospective founding election act, select remainder-only national
mandates after a formation repeat or an annulment repeat. Publish the
ordinary cycle and the commencement and end of each mandate before
the initial call. A repeat never restarts the four-year cycle. If the
next ordinary Assembly lawfully takes office before a full repeat can
confer a mandate, the repeat no longer supplies that previous mandate.
The founding act must give an independent court authority to confirm
that effect and preserve evidence, timely claims, remedies and any
legally distinct contest. Before the next ordinary Assembly takes
office, retain the applicable fixed repeat rule and funded proceedings.
The act must settle the coexisting calendars before first call.
Neither a pending challenge nor a repeat by itself postpones the
ordinary election, extends a mandate or revives a predecessor.
Already called contests keep their applicable law. This is a
prospective addition to be coordinated with [formation](06-government-formation-and-caretaker.md)
and [ER16–ER17](10/07-result-validity-and-repeat-poll.md).
