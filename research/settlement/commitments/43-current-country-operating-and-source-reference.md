# Current country reference and surviving commitments

Design 130 · [C06](../../country/C06/52-current-country-operating-and-source-reference.md)
· [Comparison](../current-country/README.md)

Original 37 complete function/bid/actual-null objects, 61 selections and 69 inherited
reference objects remain exact; a current sidecar records the new reference.
Every actual full function cost, qualified simultaneous staff/source and received
remedy remains unknown. Dated editions and common proposed method stay unchanged.

D04/D05/D11 democratic work, D08 local representatives, L06 personal/pension flows,
L07 ingredients/instruction, L10 housing, R04 sources, R05 physical supply, R09 freight
and K03 secure operational equipment now have explicit current interfaces. Other
functions retain their due scope and resources; this is not a closed universe.
Changes never move independent judgment or actual consent merely because a task
can share a source record, room or payroll operator.

Replace old 125 electoral rows/paper, thirty-six-place representative fixture,
public ingredient/utility prices, housing upkeep/renewal attribution and station/
ordinary-instruction scope once. Keep old 44 utility support people/repair inputs
in G; credit them once in utility revenue requirements. New physical/housing/
seasonal instruction is distinct. Held unmatched provision is a source reservation,
not a proven saving, paid invoice, permanent spending right or extra second bill.

The **new candidate local working advance** adds bounded principal exposure:
45,000/85,000/150,000/180,000 CU, total 460,000 in this context. It requires express
competent authorization/appropriation, fixed gross annual disbursement limits, no repayment-based replenishment, voluntary lawful local
council acceptance, actual Treasury source, no policy conditions and independent
review. Repayment is a financial transaction, not tax/grant revenue; loss,
source/opportunity/administration costs and changed actual needs remain explicit.
No forced borrowing, floor-defeating recovery, automatic overdraft or general
municipal bailout is created. NF24 monthly grants/formula remain unchanged.

Public housing reserve formation/draw, private programme calls, new/retained
assets, old claims, capital-import/initial-stock/normal foreign calls, interest,
principal, renewal and working cash remain distinct. The mature year requires
prestart accepted capacity; the assumed dates do not enact commencement or
cancel old service/benefit/election duties if construction fails.

G/local/S, withholding/client/political custody, utility/private/insurer/lender
sources keep their title/currency/due dates. Actual eligible B/other means,
private profits, VAT/refund/company/transfer bases, source and legacy actuarial
scope remain unqualified, not zero. Actual stronger tenant, worker, pension,
creditor, contract, pending case, deposit, record and foreign rights carry through
paid OP/Tsuccessors. Sensitive testimony need not become permanently public.

Actual constitution/software/contribution permissions and current-called law
remain unchanged. No monetary office/model/source register approves its own
creation or larger fiscal power. Retain the separately authorized finite issue
rival and whole ownership/income alternatives; no pseudo-residual closes a source.
New events, objections or dependency changes require fresh scoped and final review.
