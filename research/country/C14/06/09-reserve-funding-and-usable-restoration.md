# T27–T30. Fund the interval before a restored service reaches people

*Design307 · Proposed first-programme rules with a Copenhagen reference.*

**T27 — Trace the payment through every public owner.** The
finance owner identifies the original source, municipal decision,
receiving agency, permitted purpose and date of actual usable
receipt. Separate a national allocation, the local share and the
same amount received by a joint operator. Assign each contribution
once. Reconcile capital, organization and operating costs, including
staff, rotation and replenishment. The same announced source may
appear again in a subsequent budget. Check the original reference
and its purpose before counting any increase. A multi-year budget
gives the exact scope it authorizes. An order, actual payment,
qualified equipment and accepting staff need their own evidence.

**T28 — Price the whole interval to independently accepted service.**
The utility establishes a network recovery objective and the
conditions for safe restoration. Care operators and local authorities
establish what each facility and resident route needs before and
through that restoration. Include drinking water, water for daily
use, sanitation, qualified care and accessible delivery on their
actual clocks. Separate firefighting water from drinking water.
Nominate an authorized payer and receiving operator for each duty
and verify safe handoff. A household preparedness recommendation
supplies no available stock for someone who cannot obtain, carry
or safely use it. Keep the existing public duty, service-user
choice and paid support through the expected and extended window.

**T29 — Check the first allocation, replenishment and late return.**
A shared reserve states who can call it, which needs it can cover,
and how it protects the supplying area and other accepted calls.
Record an earlier and a later accepted delivery within the same
physical and fiscal limits. Check delay beyond the network objective
before relying on the reserve. A delivery after a deadline leaves
the earlier gap visible. Verify the competence, actual availability
and consent required for any temporary care or location change.
Under prior law, identify which authority must correct a gap and
give people a usable route for challenge. New requirements and
cross-body commitments go to their competent elected decision.

**T30 — Carry actual appropriations and seek a priced service offer.**
In the Copenhagen reference, carry the approved emergency
appropriation and earmarked care amount on their actual scopes.
Use the reported water objective in a conditional exercise while
its public decision and implementation are open. Prepare a priced
local reserve or a funded shared alternative with known care staffing,
water and sanitation through accepted restoration. Give independent
reviewers enough protected evidence to verify performance. Publish
safe totals and unmet service by route. AI may reconcile duplicated
funding lines and flag incompatible calls. Public officers and
operators verify authority, acceptance and receipt. Reopen on an
effective change in the water objective, an exact allocation,
paid capacity, a failed handoff or a stronger ordinary alternative.
