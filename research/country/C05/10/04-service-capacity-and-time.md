# TW14–TW17: shorter working time must not reduce received care

*Part of [C05/10](../10-work-rest-and-paid-capability.md); design90 candidate.*

**TW14 — Derive paid positions from tasks and the actual calendar.** Start
with service need, received hours, safe grouping, preparation, records, handover,
travel, breaks, learning and qualified absence/relief. Keep those hours in
demand or subtract their matched allocation from availability once. One person's
several jobs/agents do not multiply available hours. Annual paid FTE, distinct
people, qualified shifts and actual local access are different requirements.

The authored five-day comparator has 52 weeks minus six leave weeks and two
public/flexible weeks. At 32 hours, paid scheduled time is 1,408; subtract 110
paid-break hours and 64 protected-learning hours, then a 10% unexpected-loss
stress:1,110.6 task hours. At 40 hours the corresponding amount is 1,427.4.
A four-day 32-hour pattern has different break incidence; actual schedules,
qualification and duties decide, not which spreadsheet denominator is larger.
The 10% stress is no observed illness rate or public sickness-fund estimate.

QE's old 1,080 already excluded unspecified ordinary non-task time. None of
these new calendars proves it was a 40-hour legal week or authorizes a staff
cut against that baseline. Pay, source, qualifications and transition need
explicit new matches. CJ's monthly judicial cap, elected-office mandates,
clinical quality and statutory response clocks do not enlarge with paid hours.
A work-limit breach does not automatically invalidate a lawful vote, judgment
or public act, or change an officeholder’s removal procedure. Correct actual
staffing/rest/pay without transferring constitutional power to an inspector.

**TW15 — Keep the child offer unchanged.** The existing authored under-three
block is 500 groups ×two carers ×40 offered hours ×48 weeks ×1.12 preparation:
2,150,400 annual task hours. At the new paired calendar it needs 1,937 versus
1,507 frontline posts:430 extra,12.900 million CU annual gross pay at unchanged
30,000 each. This is a paid-position bound, not a certified roster or full bill.
More instruction/sites/materials, professional care, taxes, recruitment and
accepted shifts remain separate. Do not assign the difference to parents or
reduce the children's 40-hour offer to the employees'32 hours.

**TW16 — Choose better organization when it preserves the same service.**
RF's local-access/shared-specialist choice can reduce real travel and repeated
handoffs. The authored 400-person weekday home-care block preserves 104,000
annual contact hours and 12% documentation. Eight local bases change central
156,000 task hours to 128,266.667, including paid additional worker commuting.
At 32 hours this is 141 central posts versus 116 task posts plus eight local
reserve posts. Add three paid dispatch/stock posts and 0.3 qualified independent
review FTE; the latter must cover its separate 300-hour task, not disappear
inside a service manager's judgment.

The annual-task-only named comparison differs by 130,000 CU after added local premises,
renewal and upkeep, with 640,000 initial fit-out. It is not a complete actual
offer: vehicle/fuel, common retained depot costs, learning/overlap, tax effects,
geography, acceptance and sources remain unqualified. More directly, a 32-hour
five-day pattern has only 5.9 daily task hours after break, while the intact
central/local routes need 6/6.1667. Neither fits that normal day. Split routes,
nonuniform/four-day patterns and added handovers need separate paid evidence;
the 130,000 is not an executable saving. Even a four-day route fit needs enough
whole route-days, not only annual hours. The 400-person block
explicitly has the stated weekday need; it does not replace anyone's required
night/weekend or higher-intensity care.

If 141 existing frontline staff are retained/redeployed rather than vacancies
actually avoided, their pay remains; this branch adds 380,000 named recurring
cost while releasing 18,880.2 qualified task hours only where a receiving duty
and safe roster exist. No automatic dismissal or fictional cash saving follows.
Compare retain/repair and qualified alternative provision; reject local routing
if it exports travel, reduces consent/privacy or worsens actual access.

**TW17 — Count learning and shift failures honestly.** The old clinical practice
fixture contains 8,000 participant hours and 1,000 distinct independent-review
hours. Only an actually matched employee learning activity can remove the
same 8,000 from demand when 64 learning hours are excluded from capacity;
the 1,000 review/teaching work remains. Unspecified teacher preparation and care
documentation cannot be silently removed. A single continuous post needs
at least eight annual equivalents at 32 versus seven at 40 before local roster,
handover, rest, break relief and professional limits. Even a favorable annual
sum can fail the first shift; record non-delivery rather than relax protections.
