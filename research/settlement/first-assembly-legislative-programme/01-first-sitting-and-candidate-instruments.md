and one peak review slot above the baseline. Preserve pending
applications and lawful old awards until their paid lawful
successors accept them. If a pre-commencement condition fails,
hold only the dependent new scheme. Standing duties and original
remedies continue. A changed award, verification rule or start
requires exact scrutiny and decision. A separately priced public
delivery rival using existing verification can compete when its
complete receiving and review qualify.

These texts state the decision for the constructed scope. Real bills
