# Opening motions on one national transition calendar

*Design455 · Proposed decisions for a parliamentary retrofit · Denmark as a reference.*

Use 31 December 2025 for the observed opening positions and the
2026–27 overlap for the operating comparison. The reference includes
national Treasury, Copenhagen's municipal government, the regional
health authority and the separately funded water operator. Apply
ordinary taxes, legal obligations and protection to all places. The
published local evidence is concentrated in Copenhagen. Missing
local operations elsewhere remain visible as missing. The reference
sets no actual AIcountry territory, budget or authority.

## Alternative motions

**R — Continue ordinary operation and correct evidenced failures.**
Direct the competent existing public offices to carry lawful services,
contracts, staffing and standing remedies on their current authority
and sources. Preserve separate receiving at every binding transfer.
Execute undisputed lawful payments on their due dates. Bring any
unfunded standing duty and an exact lawful correction to the first
competent budget decision before its deadline where practicable.
Record a missed service and maintain the affected person's remedy
when the correction cannot arrive in time. This motion makes no
incremental appropriation or change to a binding statutory date.
**Select R for this reference.** Use the [opening disposition](../opening-disposition/01-first-budget-and-continuity-choice.md) to price a finite backup for a due home visit against ordinary repair and the drinking-water rival. Keep all already effective duties on their original authority.

**P — Authorize a defined early continuity stage.** Preserve R. Offer
one separately priced care backfill or drinking-water protection
package for a named facility and assisted home route. The Assembly
and council vote only the applicable source and local contribution.
The competent operators must accept and demonstrate usable delivery
in the overlapping window. A future motion needs gross cost, payer,
deadline, paid roles, suitable physical supplies, closeout and any
displaced uses. Hold P until those terms and independent receiving
are established. If its condition fails before commitment, continue R.
After commitment, carry all binding obligations and remedies.

**F — Authorize earlier integration and an additional service cohort.**
Preserve R and test both handovers jointly, with a separately paid
cohort and independent review. Require a voted recurring source,
territorial funding reconciliation, origin and destination acceptance,
actual usable water and the alternate route for each essential duty.
Hold F on the current reference. No P or R authorization implies a
vote for F. A binding transfer continues under applicable law
if the acceleration fails. A separable protection needs its own
lawful approval and verified delivery.

## Decision record and next release

For each line of a proposed release, record the duty and jurisdiction,
who decides, who pays and receives, gross amount and expiry, legal
authority, cash cutoff and settlement, paid role and acceptance,
physical input, the protected alternative and material displacement.
A vacancy, unresolved budget or missed delivery stays explicit.
Publish a safe version and preserve individual review. Reopen the
selection when independently reviewable evidence qualifies an exact
P or F motion, or exposes material harm under R. Any change to a
binding date, allocation or remedy follows its competent legal route.

The opening choice is specified. P and F have no qualified amounts
or operating commitments on this reference. Wider budget and service
evidence, actual adoption and experienced outcomes remain open.
