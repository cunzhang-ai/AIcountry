# Design371 commitment. Fund coverage and usable protection on the same dates

[WF105–WF110](../../country/C06/58/24-housing-warmth-and-budget-coverage.md)
requires a common covered population, actual reach and overlap bounds,
separate finance and service capacity, paid independent review and
accepted housing or warmth protection through the next bill. Preserve
earlier awards, claims and emergency duties on their own terms.

The [coverage comparison](../housing-warmth-and-budget-coverage/01-evidence-and-first-budget-choice.md)
uses public observations for exposure and an illustrative intake limit.
It supplies no cash, real stock or new expenditure authority.
