## Diagnose and compare

Housing is durable private space, an asset, a service and bargaining power.
Separate occupancy/space from income grouping, landlord title from tenant use,
participation from authority, and cost recovery from affordability. These are
conceptual/value commitments; the numerical inputs are authored hypotheses,
not observations of a population. Existing source-law constraints are unknown.

- Retain current plural provision/I03: dispersed control, fewer acquisition
  costs and flexibility; cash can miss scarce safe units and landlord dependence.
- Strengthen tenure/repair without conversion: protects existing residents at
  lower public capital cost; does not itself provide an independent supply option.
- Paid dispersed public/community acquisitions: durable tenure/control changes
  and potential outside option; buying occupied stock adds no homes and carries
  valuation, debt, landlord capture and operating risk.
- New cost-rental construction or accepted repair: can add usable supply or
  retain communities, but consumes land, skilled labor, materials, temporary
  homes and time. Unsafe/short-lived repair can lose to rebuilding.

Select a permanent option, not blanket conversion, public-stock quota or
compulsory shared living. Local/elected authorities compare those choices at
actual sites; national law fixes rights, funding responsibility and remedies.
No newer institutional form is assumed socially superior by definition.

## Objections, dissent and limits

- Permanent use reduces vacancies/turnover and can privilege incumbents. Keep
  published need bands, equivalent-claim treatment, newcomer access and funded
  voluntary transfer; reject resident allocation veto and hereditary asset sales.
- Strong tenure can restrict private supply/owner use. Permit proved narrow
  owner occupation/redevelopment and actual unresolved arrears with independent
  process; retain current contracts/security until lawful migration. Effects
  require real evidence; neither aggregate welfare nor a court erases losses.
- Cost rental can be expensive or a patronage landlord. Disclose full costs,
  binding new security, plural provision, organizing, replaceable operators,
  independent courts/inspection and appropriated assistance. A no-conversion
  or new-supply choice remains valid. Public title is no proof of control.
- Strong private space can cost more than forced sharing; retain that value
  choice and actual household consent. Household autonomy and equal access are
  not averaged into an optimization score. Refusal, suitability, provider
  acceptance and judicial authority remain different facts.
- Repair can fail safety/life or need more professional skills and repeat work.
  Condition it on independent acceptance and retain rebuilding/retention rivals.
  New jobs are requirements, not relabelled unallocated national workers.
