# IA09–IA12: fixed parliamentary places and individual protected scrutiny

*Part of [C10/10](../10-civilian-assessment-and-intelligence-limits.md); design102 research selection, not enacted law.*

**IA09 — Five ordinary elected members, with a disclosed opposition floor.** Complete C10's existing opposition-inclusive proportionally constituted Assembly security committee. Authorize **K=min(5, full Assembly N) seats**, fixed for the whole Assembly term; current ministers cannot serve. Let A be occupied eligible nonminister seats, divided into G (the actual publicly recorded governing/confidence-and-supply cohort) and O (all other seats), using CJ05's closed cohort meaning but **not its appointment approval or fixed-vote jurisdiction**. Allocate only **k=min(K,A)** actual places; the remaining K−k places are vacant. No fabricated O, ideology test or unoccupied seat supplies a representative. If A=0 no member can act; lawful external complaint/court/audit/PD routes continue, and K does not fall to zero. Publish positions, affiliation/cohort, arithmetic and each source vacancy; a new member may challenge source classification in an unconflicted ordinary court without a minister authenticating political sincerity.

For A>0, allocate the nearest whole number to k·O/A (half rounds up), adjusted to the following bounds: minimum nongovernment seats is max(k−G, min(2,O,k−min(1,G))); maximum is min(O,k−min(1,G)). The result kO is the nearest allocation clamped between those bounds; kG=k−kO. Thus a normal five-member committee has at least two genuine nongovernment members when available, at least one governing member when available, and otherwise follows cohort proportions/capacity. If O=0 none is invented; if G=0 every allocated place is nongovernment, with any unfilled authorized seats still vacant. The two-person floor intentionally departs from exact proportionality for a very small opposition, rather than disguising a 3–2 preference as mathematical necessity. The committee has no sovereign case power, so that adjustment does not give the minority a veto on government policy.

Within each cohort, allocate its seats among actual parliamentary party/civic-list groups in proportion to occupied eligible membership by largest remainders; independently elected members are each a one-person group. A group cannot obtain more places than its actual eligible members; redistribution among remaining groups uses the same rule. Published lot resolves an exact final allocation tie, not the Speaker's discretion. Each source group chooses its named consenting representative(s) by a recorded majority of its fixed occupied eligible members; no chair or minister chooses for that group. A source refusing to choose leaves its place unfilled; it does not transfer to Cabinet or another source. A single independent chooses themselves if their allocated place exists. Deliberate broad nonparticipation can leave scrutiny weaker; ordinary courts cannot cast the missing political votes.

**IA10 — Term, replacement, chair and meeting rules.** Committee membership lasts only within the member's actual Assembly term and eligible group/cohort allocation; loss of seat/ministry appointment ends committee eligibility. Within14calendar days of a new Assembly or genuinely changed governing agreement/membership that changes allocation, the Clerk publishes the recalculation and sources fill their actual places under IA09. Classification allegations alone do not remove a member. Sources may replace their own representative prospectively by the same recorded majority with published reasons; no party expulsion vacates an Assembly seat, destroys evidence/dissent or blocks an existing court order. This ordinary representative role is replaceable, not a new protected seven-year office. A lost committee place does not remove a person's protected testimony/court claim; necessary protected originals stay with the lawful receiver and former members retain confidentiality obligations.

Draw and publish the nongovernment members' annual chair rotation at formation; if O=0 use the whole committee's drawn rotation, disclosing the absent opposition rather than claiming plural control. The chair organizes only. Any two members can call a meeting; any one may initiate an urgent access/complaint/preservation request under their own statutory right. No chair's refusal prevents those acts. A collective ordinary report/recommendation needs **floor(K/2)+1 affirmative votes and that many present** (normally3of5); vacancies, ministry appointments, recusals, absences or abstention do not shrink K or this threshold. Only a new Assembly term sets its new authorized K. If fewer actual members can serve, no collective report is possible; individual lawful access/dissent remains, rather than pretending a smaller denominator approved it. No casting vote, source-presence veto or proxy AI vote. Each member may separately submit a safely redacted dissent or confidential source-based minority report to the Assembly's protected channel; publication obeys the specific legal privacy/source rules and direct court route, not a committee-majority truth test. A common patron can still capture both cohorts, and replacement power can discipline a critic: preserve safe originals, individual access and reasons rather than claiming the floor proves opposition independence.

**IA11 — Staff/employer and budget have an owner.** The existing Assembly Clerk recruits, assigns and disciplines a dedicated committee counsel, technical/audit analyst and secure records clerk through C04 open protected careers, with actually competent externally commissioned advice when needed. These are funded proposed roles, not three already available polymaths or free support. The committee states its lawful scrutiny work/program within the enacted budget; the Clerk administers employment/pay, cannot suppress an adverse source claim and is not a new security commissioner. Job-related discipline requires usable grounds/reply and direct court review; minister/NAU/party group cannot pick or dismiss a named investigation's adviser. Member direct conflicts use ordinary published parliamentary recusal and court legality routes; a chair cannot eliminate an opponent by alleging a conflict. No security service or foreign partner clears or selects the committee's own counsel. Committee-source inspection is not a police investigation or another prosecution office.

Assembly funds oversight as a separately readable line within its own appropriation, distinct from NAU's Cabinet line; the executive/Director cannot transfer it away or require a NAU invoice approval. Treasury pays actual lawful staff/supplier invoices through separately assigned parliamentary career payer/source records. Assembly may openly change future scope/resource policy within rights; no committee, Clerk or court can self-appropriate an unlimited inspection budget. Source failure is reported with what scrutiny cannot occur, direct court/elected appropriation action and NP's existing protected duties, not paper independence. Count secure sites/credentials, ordinary judges/appeals, actual technical skill, interpretation and staff concurrency against other duties. No fines, private donor/vendor gifts, foreign security subsidy or secret off-book reserve funds this oversight.

**IA12 — Information cannot depend on the investigated service's permission.** Each lawful committee member and role-authorized committee staff has statutory protected access to necessary original NAU tasking, source provenance/limits, contrary evidence, analysis versions, access/dissemination logs, contracts, expenditure and complaints. They need no majority vote, political promise or NAU clearance to request it. The NAU records officer provides an existing lawful item or specific protected-access/remainder response within five working days and earlier for actual urgent harm; receipt/preservation are immediate. Specific source/safety/privilege limitations require the companion law's necessity/minimization, safe inspection or protected gist; source identity is not automatically circulated to every aide. The implicated service cannot finally judge withholding, permanently refuse by foreign caveat or make a public disclosure the only way to challenge it.

An unconflicted ordinary court decides contested necessary access/safe mode with relevant originals and an answerable case, E13/B17 urgency where delay defeats protection, and existing direct appeal; CJ12 is not borrowed. The court may protect a source with a narrower inspection mode/qualified counsel while retaining genuinely meaningful review; if no safe qualified receiver exists, disclose the failed inspection rather than deem unseen material checked. Ordinary PD25 **subject** notice delay is not a committee/PD inspection veto; classification review, privacy confidentiality and a subject-notice order are distinct decisions with distinct expiry. Oversight copies minimum authority/tasking/access-audit evidence under separate Assembly custody and legal holds, not a standing mirror of all personal dossiers. The unit's sole vendor cannot be both original custodian and only complaint key. Own-Clerk/committee corruption or unlawful disclosure goes to existing audit/integrity/prosecutor/PD/courts under their actual mandates; the committee is subject to the same information/privacy duties and cannot finally exonerate itself. Court orders name an actual lawful receiver/executor and source; if that chain fails, preserve the breach and claim, not invent a superior security board.

**Changed research selection:** the authorized seat denominator is fixed by fullN for the Assembly term, while the actual allocation uses only eligible occupied people. This replaces the draft’s K=min(5,A), which could shrink a later collective threshold through vacancies/ministry changes. It is an explicit proposed committee rule, not inherited current law or prior approval. The retained cost is an unavailable collective report when too few eligible members serve; no one invents a replacement member or removes direct individual/external review rights to cure it.
