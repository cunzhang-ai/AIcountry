# Authentic versions and public law

Design315 · Scoped result for [country completion](../../DESIGN-COMPLETENESS.md)

[ML01–ML05](../../country/C12/04-authentic-versions-and-public-law.md)
keeps one exact elected decision across designated authentic
versions and gives people accessible publication and review.
It distinguishes a publication mistake from a difference in what
the legislature actually adopted.

The [notice comparison](../../settlement/authentic-language-and-public-law/01-notice-and-equal-versions.md)
reproduces different case deadlines and preserved receipt under an
authored rule. It does not establish legal timeliness or actual
court and service capacity. Legitimate language designation, funding,
enactment, independent review and outcomes remain open.
