# Test universal cash and mutual provision through the same handover

*Design536 · Extension of the constructed Design535 exercise*

Carry the [same adverse week](06-three-countries-on-the-same-week.md):
lawful replacement of a government, loss of a shared digital operator and
a late transfer to a host locality. Keep the same required care visits,
opposition evidence and executed court remedy. Use the same inherited
rights, original deadlines and qualified service staff. Test both rival
arrangements alongside strong repair, service-first plural and direct-public
provision. None of the numbers in the comparison describes an actual adopter.

## Price the cash choice on its own side of the budget

Unpack the original income line of four units in each window. The visiting
resident has three units of ordinary income support and one additional unit
of established disability support. The other resident already has adequate
resources and has no ordinary support award in the base case. Previously
earned awards and any other due obligations continue on their original funds
outside these incremental lines. The two residents have the same eligible
status under the universal base. The three-unit universal amount is assumed
for this comparison alone. Both residents would qualify and could receive it
once funded. For the same person and period the universal three replaces the
assessed three by prospective law. It does not replace earlier arrears. The
additional unit to the visitor continues. This costs seven at the income payer,
three more than the established line. Assume unchanged payment and review
operating costs on their already funded common lines, with no administrative
saving credited. The independent civic budget remains three.

| Exact route in both windows | Income due and eligible funds | At the first payment deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Retain and repair assessed income | Four due and four funded | The visitor can control all four through the independent ordinary route under the main case's established proof. The host resident has no due ordinary award. |
| Universal base with the same independently usable payment route | The proposed law would make seven due. The original four leave a gap of three on the income side. | Release requires an additional qualified three on that payer's own lawful source before the due dates. If obligations have already become due by law, any missed payment retains its claim and remedy. Existing disability support and earned claims retain their terms. |

The funder cannot reduce the three civic units or the seven and six care-side
ceilings to make up the gap. The universal rival using the same plural care
route costs 16 and 14 before the switch, with budgets of 14 and 13. If a new
income-side source of three settles on time in both windows, the same protected
care and two-unit switch fit within the enlarged authorized budget. The
financing proposal must identify who pays,
ordinary and adverse collections, cash timing and interaction with other
duties. No later tax receipt, disability allocation, anticipated saving or
unpaid family work supplies the gap. A wider national base would require its
own full tax, take-up, recurring and transition account.

For a separate proof-failure branch, both proposals carry the exact same old
award without reassessment. Instead test the next ordinary renewal, where
the operative law requires renewed proof for the new period. Assume the old
ordinary award has lawfully ended and the separate disability entitlement
continues. The visitor's lawful basis for the next ordinary award cannot be
determined before the same useful cutoff. Compare an independently funded
provisional payment where enough evidence exists and the universal base.
When provisional payment lawfully reaches the full three on time, the universal
route avoids ordinary means proof and expands coverage. It has no punctual
payment advantage for the visitor. If neither provisional proof nor timely
correction can qualify, the fully funded universal base protects those three
units by the original cutoff. Record the unverified assessed award and its
review separately. A common payment failure can still stop both systems.
Judge the receipt and dignity gains independently of the net taxpayer and
household burden. A revised adjudication never pays a past bill retroactively
on time.

## Give mutual delivery an actual funded alternative

Under the mutual rival, a voluntary resident and worker cooperative offers
both visits on a legally accepted roster. Member-elected governance controls
ordinary methods, staffing decisions and retained surplus within the funded
contract and workers' legal protections. The local authority retains its
commissioning, lawful access and paying duties. The ordinary national floor,
its residual funding and an independent hearing remain available on the same
scope. Nonmembers can use the funded alternative. Local leaders cannot decide
a resident's rights or see protected records merely because they control
membership. Workers can leave or refuse unsafe work under ordinary law.
Require paid management, travel, qualified shifts, absence cover, ordinary
employment protection and an accepting provider for exit or refusal.

Stipulate 13 and 12 units for standing care, income and political work in the
two windows. Relative to the selected plural route, the second-window extra
unit pays shared supervision and an independently staffed nonmember route.
An additional one-unit change to a separately qualified mutual team fits at
13 if members, workers, residents and the competent council make the exact
decisions within their respective authority. Confirm actual receipt through
the handoff. In the qualified branch the host resident declines membership
and still receives the visit by hour 4 through the separately staffed
route. The ready rival now ties the plural route on its two whole budgets
with the switch and can win if worker and resident control work better without loss
of choice or safety. The same improvement earns the same credit if a charged
and independently accepted mutual route already fits under ordinary plural
procurement.

For example, if the members choose and can replace the management, both
residents can receive an acceptable visit time the incumbent cannot offer,
and a worker's refusal is covered by a distinct paid shift, prefer mutual
delivery for this scope at the same cost. A nonmember still uses the
public rights and review route and has no duty to join the internal vote.

If the same cooperative manager controls both intake and the apparent backup,
the alternate does not qualify against that controller's refusal. If qualified
distinct cover adds one unit on the first call and one on the next, the
protected schedule becomes 14 and 13. The one-unit switch then lacks funds.
If the supposedly separate cover is already due in another locality, those
prices supply no staff. Keep both original care duties, provide only accepted
lawful relief and name the remaining missed service. Test a qualified ordinary
or direct-public route before relying on the impaired mutual bid. National
funding cannot replace worker consent or actual coverage.

## Compare the five on one restricted budget

The unchanged ceilings are 14 and 13. The independent civic line is three
in each window, income is four, and eligible care and transition funding
is seven and six. The total is a cross-check. Each purpose must fit its
separate lawful source and date.

| Arrangement | Standing work, first / second | With second-window switch | Result under unchanged sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strong repair | 12 / 12 | 14 in the second window | Standing work fits. Switch needs one more care-side unit or a qualified lower cost. |
| Service-first plural | 13 / 11 | 13 in the second window | Standing work and authorized switch fit. |
| Direct public | 14 / 11 | 13 in the second window | Standing work and public-team switch fit. |
| Universal cash with plural care | 16 / 14 | 16 in the second window | Each income cutoff needs three additional eligible units. Care and transition stay within their separate ceilings. |
| Voluntary mutual provision | 13 / 12 | 13 in the second window | Both fit if the separately accepted nonmember route and paid roster remain usable. |

Keep the current plural arrangement as a working selection under the stated
scope. Strong repair can win where its qualified switch costs less.
Direct-public provision can win where it retains equivalent choice and avoids
a shared provider or licensing constraint. A funded universal base can win
where it prevents otherwise unavoidable missed income with an acceptable full
burden. Qualified mutual provision can win where it improves control and
receipt without assuming free labor or exclusive membership. Fund and compare
each exact alternative again before extending that judgement beyond the two
windows. A useful element can carry into another arrangement if its authority,
cost and protection all carry with it.
