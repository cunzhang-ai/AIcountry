# EV09–EV12: evidence can correct government without becoming government

**EV09 — Preserve sources without compulsory total visibility.** Named sector
custodians retain authenticated originals, proportionate retention and lawful
successors; a separately controlled protected copy/access route survives a vendor
refusal where needed. The service uses purpose-limited records, samples and field
observations, preserving missingness and local meanings. Source families, shared
controllers and AI transformations are disclosed. Copies are not corroboration.

**EV10 — A calendar the minister cannot edit.** Publish series/methods, source
coverage and release dates at least annually in advance. Routine results go to
the public and lawful opposition access on equal published terms; no private
ministerial clearance or election-period suppression. A missed release states what
is unavailable and why, preserving originals and remedy; it neither certifies a
favorable result nor suspends an election. No chief can compel public belief.

**EV11 — Methods and legal definitions are distinct.** Professional staff choose
technical methods within enacted scope, with reasons, plural challenge and old/new
bridges for material change. Assembly law defines legally operative W/CPI series,
vintage, population/pay scope, fallback and revision treatment; M is the statutory
tax scale, not automatically the current median. No changed sample, AI version or
statistical label silently changes a legal definition, rate, floor or franchise.

**EV12 — Access has privacy limits and an independent route.** Publish useful
aggregates/methods; sensitive material uses necessary lawful inspection, safe summary
or restricted court access. Individual data collected for statistical compilation
remain confidential and confined to statistical use; this does not erase the
source office's separate lawful operational duties. No public life log, refuge address or permanent testimony
archive is required. Staff, subjects, opposition and outside inquiry can contest
source error, suppression, method or privacy through applicable ordinary review/courts
without the chief's permission; disagreement alone supplies no deletion or censorship.
