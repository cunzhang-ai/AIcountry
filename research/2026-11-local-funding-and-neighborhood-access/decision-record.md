# Design360 decision on neighborhood access after local equalization

## Baseline, proposed choice and authority

Pin Design359, MF01–MF18, LD, PCO, the actual local-law route and the
[working method](../FRAMEWORK.md). MF already assesses standard costs,
representative capacity and cash across localities. Select a second
check on who can use each common service within a funded locality,
with direct standing and a dated elected response to substantiated
systemic failure. The project assistant proposes the ten working day
response and 30 calendar day disposition parameters. Adopting law
sets duties and powers; councils retain lawful method and budget
choice. Sector owners and independent courts retain their own work.

## Alternatives and objections

An authority-wide grant leaves local flexibility and can miss a
concentrated access failure. A uniform ring fence is easy to inspect
but treats unequal costs as equal and can strand lawful capacity.
A sector-by-sector centrally prescribed distribution may enforce
some duties more directly and reduce local discretion. Compare
both with the selected local method, which protects common rights,
makes alternatives contestable and verifies usable delivery.
Scope reporting to material differences so it does not create a
new small-area burden for every routine choice.

Disaggregation can expose identifiable residents or misclassify
people who travel or cannot apply. Use protected originals, safe
aggregation and affected evidence. National correction can become
an indirect takeover, while a local majority can ignore the same
neighborhood repeatedly. Keep existing backstop thresholds, time
limits, direct rights review and actual funding. Binding optional
contracts can constrain the available remedy; keep their costs and
lawful choices visible. A reserve cannot stand in for scarce staff.

## Scoped result and next action

The [sources](sources.md) illustrate distinct internal allocations and
smaller-area measures. The [constructed case](../settlement/local-funding-and-neighborhood-access/01-one-council-two-neighborhoods.md)
compares equal money, different cost and a separately funded fallback.
It establishes no actual participation, authority, price, staffing
or received result. Preserve original due dates, cases, local sources
and claims through any boundary or operator change. Existing health,
learning and local service authorities retain their duties.

Use an authorized local review budget to compare one real service
catchment and one route with credible omitted need. Verify usable
arrival, missing cases, source restrictions and staffing by actual
time, with protected affected-party participation. Stop at a
matched diagnosis and costed lawful options. Reopen if the smaller
scope excludes important users, the fallback displaces another
duty, or ordinary review is equally timely and less burdensome.
