# Twelve households and twenty homes

Constructed example. Twelve affordable homes are occupied. The
locality has two suitable vacant affordable homes elsewhere that
would otherwise be offered to new entrants. Eight suitable temporary
homes can be reserved for this project without displacing other
protected bookings. Independent inspection finds that long-term
adequate repair of the old block is infeasible. It can remain
safely occupied through the proposed first stage with funded
maintenance and independent inspection. A separate parcel can be
serviced, built on and safely occupied without removing the old
block. The two project parcels together would eventually have
twenty suitable affordable homes.

| Choice on the same housing baseline | First receiving test | Net entrant offers at completion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demolish and rebuild immediately | Two households choose permanent homes elsewhere. Eight can use temporary homes. Two still have no suitable option. Hold the demolition. | A claim of eight additional offers cannot cure a failed move. |
| Build on the separate parcel first, then replace the old block | Accept 12 new homes with independent services. Ten households accept direct on-site replacement. Two freely choose secure off-site alternatives. Verify all moves before releasing the old site. | Of the final 20 on site, 10 house the original households and 10 are available to entrants. The two off-site moves consumed existing offers. Net additional entrant offers are eight. |
| Retain the old block while pursuing other sites or protection | Preserve occupation for the safe interim period. Seek separate lawful receiving before that period ends. | No added offer is assumed. A funded alternative or changed inspection can change the choice. |

Select build-first under the stated conditions. The two unused new
units in the first phase offset the two existing off-site vacancies
used for replacement. At that cutover, the net increase in available
entrant offers is zero. The next eight on-site completions count
only when safely occupied or actually ready for lawful allocation.
Over the complete project, the physical stock increases by eight
and the net entrant offer also increases by eight in this fixture.
The two measures have different dependencies.

The first stage needs nine usable units for construction and two
separately reserved units for move and occupancy overlap. Assume
both sources are settled in the selected branch. The later eight
homes need a separate accepted release. A late grant cannot release work requiring all nine. If only nine of the
ten on-site replacements pass inspection, one affected household
still lacks its chosen receiving. Hold the affected demolition
and preserve lawful occupancy and support. If the original block
becomes immediately unsafe, use the applicable urgent duty and
report any missing receiving. An emergency move cannot close the
tenancy or return claim by itself.
