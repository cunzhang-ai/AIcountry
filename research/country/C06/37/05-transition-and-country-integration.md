# PS17–PS20: a paid transition and a country account that can change

*Part of [C06/37](../37-physical-supply-and-paid-trade.md); design92 research.*

**PS17 — Match the old obligation to the new receiver.** Before a consequential
supply commitment, map assets/title/security, past and pending contracts, grants,
operating duties, price/source law, worker and local revenue obligations, permits,
case records, source evidence and actual receiving to lawful successors.
New public/common assets do not confiscate old concessions or revalue land/debt.
Public ownership can improve accountability and still fail supply or create a
dominant landlord/utility; inspection, opposition, alternative provision and
direct enforceable remedy remain necessary against public protectors too.

Retain lawful safe operations where continuation is permitted until accepted
replacement, with paid overlap,
construction/commissioning, stock fill, foreign purchases, training and source
timing. A leak project's capital classification needs accepted improvement and
matching old-component treatment; ordinary maintenance is no new asset by label.
Depreciation, renewal cash, debt principal and actual replacement remain separate.
Safety-required stops/isolation do not wait for replacement; protect available
service, record unmet claims and retain remedies. A delayed plant leaves claims intact,
while any substitute import/receiving needs its own lawful paid source.

**PS18 — Human work and seasonality remain real.** Use TW pay/rest/leave/
learning and distinct qualifications/locations/shifts; do not deduct the same
break or learning twice. Producer quality, professional supervision and
independent statutory inspection are separate roles. New source/reuse operators
cannot be taken free from existing network/sewer control. Farm/retail annual
hours do not prove harvest staffing, reachable shops or household free time.
Retain paid worker transition obligations and count actual accepted reassignment;
do not capitalize idle pay or treat unallocated people as already skilled output.

The favorable mixed harvest test at32 normal hours needs more people in eight scheduled peak
weeks than its annual field-work bound, even assuming leave/learning elsewhere.
Lawful voluntary paid overtime can alter that bound; its premiums, fatigue and
maximum-hour/consent requirements are additional, not free standing capacity.
Seasonal hiring, lawful migration, equipment or accepted crop/import changes
need actual pay, consent and source. No compulsory family work, population
assignment, food substitution or private saving may silently balance the model.

**PS19 — Replace technologies and communication without copying mandates.**
Future foods, distributed grids, autonomous equipment and new collaboration
media may change costs and feasible institutions. Require valid product/output
meaning, actual dependencies, consent and lawful control rather than today’s
text-only interface or one AI planner. Preserve unfamiliar concepts, material
objections, original observations and scoped commitments. A replicated agent,
shared forecast or matching data does not multiply a public mandate, settle
resource conflict or authorize action. Method/proposal changes get fresh review;
new procedures cannot adopt themselves or override current constitutional holds.

**PS20 — Finish the country integration, not a chain of plant studies.** Use
existing NP public120/180-day choice/preparation duties and C06 budget scrutiny
for joined supply alternatives; they confer no new technical-office power or
extension of already due claims. Prepare named quantities, quality/receivers,
paid skill/time calendars, assets, transition and owned cash/FX sources together.
Where they cannot jointly support a commitment, disclose failure and present a
lawful funded narrower stage or genuine structural rival for elected choice;
do not narrow existing rights by a research certificate.

Next reconcile these physical paths with country prices, income/tax, wages/jobs,
initial assets, recurring/foreign sources and continuing pension/housing duties,
then audit the full objective and strongest rivals. Unknown manufacturing and
private goods cannot remain zero in a national completion claim. Reopen on
source/quality failure, unsafe work, excluded nonusers, ecological harm,
common-controller capture or a better whole alternative. This conditional desk
selection proves neither engineering, actual funds, public consent nor better lives.

[PA01–PA20](../38-work-pay-prices-and-country-income.md) now supplies the
five-table/private-goods/pay/tax reference for that integration. Missing supplier
prices, actual old/new job/asset matches, transition, household choices and owned
sources remain explicit; no partial compiler output is a full national solution.
