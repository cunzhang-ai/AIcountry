# C08. Reproductive agency, assisted parenthood and the person born

15 October 2026 · Design 76 · Proposed RP01–RP16/RPO01–RPO03, not enacted law

Select a differentiated reproductive statute: bodily consent is continuing;
origin information is not a parenting mandate; parentage and support require
their own lawful grounds; a person born never loses care because adults,
a clinic, a state or a new technology dispute their status. AI cannot supply
consent, select a socially preferred child or make a contested civil judgment.

The 18-year donor/third-party-assistance threshold, ten-family donor limit,
30-day post-birth consent revocation, ten-year storage terms and 36-month
posthumous estate window are contested national hypotheses, not scientific
constants or automatically imported international law. B22's own clinical
agency and B31/B32 child protection remain; ordinary conception is not licensed.
