# Design398. Separate temporary executive cover and lawful government replacement

[HT24–HT31](../../country/C03/19/06-executive-incapacity-and-bounded-succession.md)
sets a confirmed roster, evidenced activation, short expiry and
independent review. Existing standing work continues, and an actual
vacancy starts its own formation process.

The [worked case](../../settlement/executive-incapacity-and-bounded-succession/01-two-signatures-and-a-due-payment.md)
tests a due payment, timely receiving and an unresolved reserved act.
Actual staffing, authority, independence and outcomes remain open.
