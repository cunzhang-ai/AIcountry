# Money: losses are public facts, not free income or automatic austerity

*MC25–MC28; part of [C06/41](../41-money-and-financial-public-control.md).*

**MC25 — Paired accounts and honest remittance.** Publish assets, issued
reserve/note liabilities, actual paid capital, provisions, encumbrances, expected
and realized losses, reserve-interest costs and maturity/FX risks. A purchased
asset and its new reserve liability are not two fiscal resources. Safeguarded
client property, G/S/SF standby and insurer F retain their own titles. Remit to
Treasury only audited realized legally distributable income after due costs,
provisions, carried losses and enacted capital retention. No unrealized markup,
forecast recovery, new reserves or artificial claim on future Treasury tax is
distributable profit. Treasury-paid coupons have their actual counterpart;
consolidation never authorizes spending or hides who bears loss.

**MC26 — Negative equity is not commercial-bank failure.** Recognize actual
losses against provisions/retained income and capital without hiding the excess.
The monetary authority is not an M02 deposit-taking commercial bank; negative
equity alone does not order its insolvency or forbid lawful domestic-unit
settlement. Nor does issuance repair owned FX, confidence, real resources or
make the country's commitments affordable. The preassigned qualified statutory accounting officer records/reports a
below-charter capital finding from verified interim original accounts or an
independent audit, whichever establishes it first; committee/head acknowledgment
cannot postpone that event. Credible unresolved material loss gets a prompt
source-qualified warning, not a false completed finding. Within30calendar days
of that first established finding, publish a medium-term loss/policy-continuity
plan, risk and distribution to the Assembly. Courts can identify the actual
first finding; no annual-audit wait or renamed report resets it. A loss-review threshold
is a trigger to report/reconsider new risk, never a permission to conceal larger
actual losses or cancel already due interest/payment claims.

Any recapitalization, indemnity, asset transfer or sovereign debt for the monetary
authority needs a separate finite Assembly authorization, actual payer/source,
terms and incidence under M15. No automatic charge on social services, pension/
client property, insurer or SF. If no recap is enacted, disclose actual negative
equity and obey the continuing charter; do not force austerity, invent a Treasury
receivable or suppress rights. Policy judgment can still be constrained by
credibility/fiscal pressure; this design does not prove costless independence.

**MC27 — Who pays the protectors.** Assembly enacts separate readable monetary,
prudential/conduct and independent oversight operating lines, actual lawful
Treasury channels and any statutory fee base/cap. Director/each chief employs
staff; preassigned career finance officers pay due lawful salary/contracts,
with own-office ordinary review and bounded C06/B19 continuity. No ministerial
case-release veto, self-tax/debt, fine-funded office, regulated-firm/foreign/
vendor gifts or free AI labor. Actual domestic operating pay is distinct from
a central-bank trade/reserve issuance. Price primary/reserve availability,
source/risk staff, affected access, two hearings, custody/audit/PD, ordinary
counsel/courts and actual execution once with elections/care/service duties.
Missing appropriation/cash/qualified people is disclosed nonperformance, not
new monetary credit or an office's veto of an elected budget.

**MC28 — Direct review and a real executor.** Financial authority/Clerk own
appointment, staff, data, procurement, source/access and case wrongs go to
unconflicted ordinary courts under B14–B17, with protected answerable evidence,
legal aid and ordinary appeal. Courts can correct an unlawful exclusion,
record, owed payment or actual B/PD/sector loss; they cannot choose a policy
rate, invent a solvent bank or grant new indemnity. Each office has a named
records/correction officer; known material receivers get actual correction
within24elapsed hours/earlier useful time and verify reconsideration/receipt.
Finally determined State refunds/compensation use the protected Treasury
judgment payer within30working days or earlier applicable right, actual fault/
attribution/causation and source, not insurance against every policy loss.

The adopting Act expressly adds MC-scope B19 bounded continuing appropriation
and B18 civil-service execution of specified determined records/correction/
notice and verified-payment acts, with C04 external conflict rotation, separate
credentials and audited pay. It cannot set rates/ratios, search, appropriate,
command a foreign actor or create lending policy. Actual refusal, cash/FX/
collateral/skill shortage and received remedy remain distinct; an empty account
is not automatic personal guilt. Unpaid claims and elected finance duties survive.


Design109's [financing comparison](../../../settlement/country-delivery/06-financing-and-public-control.md)
distinguishes paid reserves, term bills and zero-rate operation. Moving cost from
G coupons to CB interest does not erase consolidated exposure; bank interest is
not automatically paid to depositors. A zero-rate sensitivity is not proof that
an unchanged positive-rate operating framework works. Preserve actually accrued
interest and lawful rate terms; no model retroactively cancels them. The distinct
finite fiscal-grant rival remains unadopted. MC25–28 disclosure, elected source
choice and legal remedies continue, without automatic recapitalization/austerity.
