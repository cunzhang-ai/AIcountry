# Making the care, shift and journey fit in one day

*Design199 · Decision comparison for [CW01–CW08](../../country/C07/24-usable-care-time-and-shift-continuity.md).*

The design198 comparison left a live choice about a useful late window.
An institution can have space, an employer can have a lawful roster and
a family can hold a childcare place while the three schedules still
leave the child without a safe receiver. Decide which source of that
conflict can be changed, with the same people and duties in every option.
[Original policy and price boundaries](01-copenhagen-and-helsinki.md)
· [Worked receiving, workforce and budget case](02-one-household-and-one-shift.md)

| Arrangement | What has to work in this case | Material tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Strong retain/repair using an existing place | A reachable compatible place, accepted pickup, fee support and real assistance with short or irregular requests | Least disruptive when it works. Published opening, late admission or a voucher cannot supply an absent place or affordable journey |
| Change the originating schedule | A voluntary shift swap or properly paid relief, or a lawfully retimed public obligation, without displacing its own required service | Keeps the child's known group and avoids a second service. May move inconvenient hours to another worker or fail when the originating service needs the shift |
| Targeted extension or an intermittent shared session, selected where repair leaves a gap | An accepted compatible late window with named paid workers, safe transfer, family fee protection and funded substitute | Lower spare-capacity need than automatic full restoration. Additional handoffs, uncertain local demand and joint holiday shortages can make a hub unsuitable |
| Wider public evening or overnight provision | Stable teams, separate sleep and safety arrangements, all-year posts, actual admission and supported journeys | Can protect repeated substantial need or places a hub cannot reach. Higher fixed obligations and exposure to common staff shortages need real source and public choice |
| Portable reimbursement or leave as the main solution | A safe accepting carer the family can actually use, or genuinely available chosen leave with protected pay and return | Can preserve child relationships where freely chosen. A price allowance alone can make families their own broker or employer, or leave a late gap. CL retains its own scope |

**Country decision.** Use scheduled ordinary care or repair the source of
the conflict first where that produces an equivalent, chosen result.
Offer a funded short window when a necessary timing gap remains. Keep a
stable placement for repeated need, without a minimum weekly purchase
or a formal-work eligibility test. Use the ordinary assessed fee basis
for covered equivalent care, with actual national/local finance under FC.
Reject automatic blanket restoration and standalone cash as defaults.
A real local extension, shared session or another arrangement can win the
matched decision. Copenhagen's particular earmark remains a decision
of its competent bodies.

The stronger objection is that irregular requests consume spare staff and
can destabilize relationships or shift inconvenient hours onto care workers.
The selection protects the child, not a last-minute employer demand, and
requires real paid coverage and independent challenge. It remains possible
that a negotiated roster, rather than additional capacity, solves most of
the observed problem. An employer change that is unnecessary or unlawful
remains a work issue. No unsafe staffing or compelled household cover fills
the residual. Test clustered and dispersed requests before buying capacity.

The paired exercise narrows a national choice about time, actual receiving,
household cost and who bears volatility. Its scheduled data are authored
and its fee and access facts are local. It does not show a national net
source, a clinic or employer rota, a real place or a change in any life.
