# DCR01–DCR06. Protect essential income from combined collection

*Design370 · Part of [civil obligations and executed remedies](../13-civil-obligations-and-executed-remedies.md) · Proposed civil law.*

**DCR01 — Apply the limit to the person across collection channels.**
For natural persons, bring compulsory collection from the same usable
income or its proceeds under one limit. Cover execution of monetary
judgments, tax arrears, public overpayment recovery and compelled payment
from payroll or deposit accounts. Apply to statutory offsets or creditor
set-off where they serve the same compulsory collection function. A second
channel, assignee, joint account or transfer from wages to a deposit cannot
create additional collectible income. Keep ordinary current tax and
contributions, property title, real security against other nonexempt
assets, and independently voluntary payment under their own law. A
boilerplate waiver or required direct debit supplies no voluntary exception.
More protective exemptions and operative insolvency stays and caps control.

**DCR02 — Protect actual essential costs and restricted funds.** The
Assembly adopts the ordinary needs schedule, covered periods, evidence and
review with existing I, B18 and U safeguards. Use the liable person's
net, safely controlled, timely receipts after ordinary current taxes and
contributions. Reserve needs due in the covered interval and account for
timing before release. Include verified unavoidable housing, food, care,
health, dependants and necessary work costs within applicable law.
Exclude separately exempt, earmarked or third-party funds, with their
covered needs and actual payments reconciled once. Preserve protection
through traceable transfer or mixing of funds. Accept alternative proof
where a payer has failed to mark a deposit. Do not count a partner's
unavailable income, future wages or an unpaid award. An official applying
the schedule may use lawful standard amounts and correct atypical needs
on timely evidence. A collection gap creates no new tax, benefit or debt.

**DCR03 — Allocate an available balance once under a reviewable plan.**
Before a covered compulsory instruction is executed, the B18 civil
enforcement service reconciles lawful claims, stays, actual receipts and
live reservations in a period-limited account for the person. It can
administer an undisputed schedule under prior statute and court authority.
The ordinary court decides contested means, title, priority or execution.
Give current child or dependant support determined under B30 first call
on available amounts before old debt. Never invade the person's protected
essential resources. For older claims apply expressly enacted priority.
Among claims at equal rank, distribute proportionately to their lawful
currently collectible amounts. A creditor cannot gain rank by issuing
a faster instruction. Keep any uncovered current support visible and
invoke the independently funded I/S or B30 route if it qualifies. Credit
payments for the same obligation once. A valid unpaid claim keeps its
applicable remedies, review and original limitation clock.

**DCR04 — Authorize bounded amounts at each payment point.** The
enforcement officer gives each employer, payer or bank a distinct
instruction identifying only its lawful target, amount, source, dates
and safe contact. Reserve amounts before dispatch, require accepted or
failed receipts and return unused authority only after verified
cancellation or expiry and reconciliation. Old and substitute
instructions cannot run concurrently against the same balance. Renew
only after verifying the next period's needs, remaining obligations and
current funds. During stale records, dispute or coordination failure,
continue lawful ordinary pay and exempt transfers. Hold the unsupported
part of collection. Separate lawful court preservation remains available
for identified nonexempt assets on its own grounds. There is no authority
for a blanket account freeze or a general debtor registry.

**DCR05 — Restore protected access before the resident's deadline.**
Provide a dated account and protected direct B17 review for the resident,
support recipient, creditor and affected owner. The court or other
competent owner can cancel a deduction, unfreeze protected funds, correct
receipt, and require repayment or ordinary interim relief. Use the first
protected receipt and any earlier statutory deadline. The executor names
who can actually return or release funds and verifies safe access before
the threatened loss. Seek a separately authorized, funded bridge when
return is too late, without presuming the person's new liability.
Provide prompt reply and preserve the claim and actual loss if the bridge
also fails. Authorities do not buy compliance by stopping unrelated
wages, benefits or ordinary services.

**DCR06 — Budget and migrate the protection across institutions.**
Before commencement, fund civil execution, assistance, duty court
review, secure evidence and paid bank and payer interfaces under DB.
The elected source account separates deferred recovery, eventual loss,
cash needed for current support, and any restoration bridge. Trace
appointment, removal, funding, evidence and execution outside the
implicated collector. Publish aggregate rates of excess recovery,
late returns and recipient nonreceipt with safe omissions. AI may
check sums and dates using lawfully limited inputs. It cannot decide
means, priority or authority to seize. Preserve original claims,
orders, stronger exemptions and limitation dates during migration,
with review of existing instructions before the common limit applies.
Reopen on recurring underprotection, abusive hiding of assets,
unreachable child support, or a simpler equally protective route.
