# Design206 decision. A service appointment must survive a broken interchange

9 November 2026 · Research selection from Design205

## Gap and decision

MO already assigns access, bookings, staffed alternatives and useful-time
relief. TD defines safe modes. XR governs handover after a move, and IV
connects projects to service continuity. These do not settle ordinary
continuation responsibility when a protected trip fails between transport
operators or when the destination remains open but the rider cannot reach it.

Select MO17–MO22. Confirmed protected journeys have a designated continuity
owner, the actual receiving service retains its duty and the first contact
keeps the case until accepted. Work backward from useful arrival and arrange
a paid accessible substitute without requiring the rider to advance money.
The affected person can use existing remedies directly. Service law retains
consent, exceptions and legal deadlines. Ordinary fare integration, on-site
assistance and independent journeys retain their proper boundaries.

## Evidence, alternative and known loss

One observed municipality and budget year supplies a public capital decision,
a school transport rule and the existing care settlement. The capital
account links municipal and regional financing. The school rule changes bus
access because travel itself was consuming class time. These are real
retained mechanisms. The published Metro terms and the EU rail regulation
give different scopes of existing access and compensation. Neither proves
how a named patient or pupil fared. The worked example verifies why the
cutoff at the destination can be earlier than a refund trigger. It also
exposes a second lawful trip that would be lost if shared standby is
claimed twice. No price, worker or asset is transferred from the baseline.

Retain existing ticketing, operator replacement and refunds when they
produce timely accessible journeys. A common authority could reduce
dispatch disputes but concentrates refusal and may make destination
providers subordinate to a transport operator. Improve ordinary routes and
consider valid closer services before paying for extensive standby.
Select a bounded protected-journey duty alongside these rivals. It incurs
contract and dispatch cost, and may consume scarce capacity needed elsewhere.
Do not extend it to every coincidental interchange or infer an unlimited
taxi allowance. Exact operating thresholds, affected participation, paid
capacity and outcomes are unqualified.

## Power, successors and bounded comparison

Elected authorities and service laws set the floor and its real source.
Route owners act within that scope. Payer, receiving provider, independent
inspection, outside review and order execution remain distinct. Provide
original evidence and service impacts through existing public channels and
safe protected review. Neither a common platform nor an AI optimizes away
a court hearing, clinical choice or opposition trip. An ostensible backup
that shares an unavailable person, route or controller fails independently
of its app or contract name. Review actual ownership, staff, money and
appeal channels. No sovereign coordinator or new diversion power follows.

Keep MO, TD, IV, XR, CW, OC, AP, SR, CP and other prior-law limits. Prepare
paid reserves, real contractors, route notice, receiving, privacy, support
and review. Preserve fares and accrued obligations through any change.
Use direct public funding or lawful commercial terms with a defined
covered scope. A failure of paid readiness cannot erase enacted claims.

Apply the compared routes in a single service corridor, with consent and
legitimate authorization, or use compatible existing evidence. Pair the
same people, shifts, windows and lawful alternatives. Count independent
capacity, actual door-to-service and return times, authorized payment,
lost care or learning and the work displaced by each path. Include a
planned closure and a shared failure. Require one real paid contingency
quote and an accepting provider before claiming feasibility. Retain the
simpler existing path if it reaches the same people in time. Reopen on
changed network terms, failed accessibility, shared failure, cost or
evidence of unnecessary coordination burden. This AI-assisted research
does not establish affected participation, independent review, adoption
or better lives.
