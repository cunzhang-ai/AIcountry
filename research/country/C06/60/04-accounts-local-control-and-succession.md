# DB16–DB20. Source, locality and accountability survive a new budget

*Part of [democratic budgets](../60-democratic-budget-and-execution.md).*

**DB16 — Reconcile the selected account, do not invent a new observation.**
Retain the pinned manuscript's monthly budget/revision/commitment/expense/cash/
arrears comparison, quarterly commitment report and rolling13-week cash forecast
updated weekly, now under actual elected/competent authority rather than AI law.
Trace authority, source, payee, currency, due/receipt dates, programme/function,
commitment and correction; publish material deviations and truthful unresolved
amounts. Preserve original safe records and reasons, not deleted errors. AA23
six-/nine-month account/opinion dates remain, not replaced by a fresh DB clock.

Consolidation eliminates internal G/local/S transfers only within the actual
boundary while retaining final services/household payments and restricted-source
needs. Display wider public enterprise, bank, guarantee/PPP, tax-expenditure,
legacy and foreign exposure without inventing consolidated ownership. Cash,
expense, production, capital, debt principal, arrears and actual received service
have distinct units. Model/template results or netting are not collected revenue.

**DB17 — Public control at actual local and institutional scope.** National
consolidation supplies no appropriation over a council's own lawful source or
an independent institution's cases. National Assembly votes its legal transfers/
guarantees; Council adopts local budgets/changes under actual LR/full-n rules
and source competence. NF standardized needs/capacity and national guarantee
claims remain; a reduced grant cannot silently change a resident's right or
impose an unpaid new task. DB chooses no new local tax/equalization formula.

Assembly/Council scrutiny cannot make a court judgment, clinical fact, audit
finding or protected election contingent on budget agreement. Actual separately
funded opposition/court/AA/legal-aid/records/payer/executor and complaint routes
need source independence across appointment/pay/information/control. Cabinet
and parliamentary majorities can both be abusive. Annual review and lawful
rights-respecting change remain possible; no institution self-appropriates or
uses an invented fiscal mandate to govern the rest.

**DB18 — Remedies and learning must reach actual people.** Treasury/issuer
provides reasoned decisions and lawful source access; AP/V/B19 and applicable
AA/OJ roles address illegal release holds, hidden commitments, records and
executed relief with actual unimplicated staff/payer/court powers. Individual
needs/provisional/shortfall remedies retain their earlier clocks. Courts can
enforce lawful duties, access, agenda and remedies, not choose tax rates, cast
member votes or manufacture cash/workers. Public adverse signals may reopen a
budget/source choice; metrics never turn a missing visit or election into success.

Pay the actual additional work: legislative/counterproposal analysis, hearings,
private source custody, Treasury/sector reconciliation, outside review, judgment
payment/execution, accessible non-AI channels, old records and transition.
Original37functions and later duties continue; old D/R accounting labels do not
price this complete increment. No free duplicated expert/service shift, new
reserve, AI saving, pension/mineral/water sweep or compulsory family contribution.
Useful AI may compare scoped sources/options under DP/HC, with unfamiliar media/
meaning/objections preserved and actual delegated scope, consent and authority.

**DB20 — Different budget countries remain possible.** Cabinet-led broader
service envelopes can reduce voting/input burden but concentrate allocation/
withholding power. More detailed Assembly allocations strengthen control while
risking micromanagement, bargaining, delay and obscured whole costs. Local/
participatory budget choice can reveal needs while favoring organized insiders
or moving shared obligations onto nonparticipants. A separate advisory fiscal
body may add critique but also cost/common-patron/gatekeeping; do not assume it
must exist or can decide policy. These are real rivals, not free additions.

Retain the chosen programme/limited-input-discretion/member-amendable model
conditionally. The full-N vote,150/120/14-day timetable and carry-forward method
are normative hypotheses, not empirical optimality, actual independence or
complete source. Reopen capture, overload, missing services, hidden multiyear/
local burdens, recurring deficits or better complete paid alternatives. A positive
founding source/public-offer comparison remains unresolved, not supplied by this
procedure or reinstated benchmark tax rates. Next integrated ordinary/adverse/
paid-country largest-gap choice, not an automatic budget-calendar/case series.
