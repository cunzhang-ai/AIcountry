# Commitment 121. Protect continuing local duties when finance fails

[LD01–LD08](../../country/C04/10-local-fiscal-recovery-and-debt.md)
assigns a dated source and service account, ongoing payments, elected
recovery and a narrow court-supervised future debt path. Keep actual
claims, conditions, creditor losses and common-service money separate.
Local elections, worker rights and ordinary review survive.

No automatic grant, cash advance or authority is created by the design.
Actual payment, staff and safe recovery remain open.
