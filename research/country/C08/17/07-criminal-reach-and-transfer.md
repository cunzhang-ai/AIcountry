# Criminal reach, concurrent cases and protected transfer

*P21–P24; part of [C08/17](../17-jurisdiction-and-legal-cooperation.md).*

**P21 — Defined criminal reach.** K01–K24 ordinarily requires a substantial
material act/omission here, or a direct intended prohibited result here.
Incidental data transit, criticism from abroad, a victim's nationality or
an agent account alone supplies neither jurisdiction nor guilt. Actual
domestic control/duty and causal conduct can qualify; position, AI ownership
or corporate group membership cannot replace P02/P03 proof.

For a citizen or actual habitual resident at the act, a separate personal
basis covers abroad conduct constituting K01–K06, K11, K13 or K14 here,
with criminality at the place/time of conduct ordinarily also required.
K13/K14's actual child-protection offenses do not require that second state's
criminalization; this is a prospective normative choice, not universal reach.
Its age/fault/peer exceptions and all P defenses remain. Public entrusted
acts directed under this country's authority also remain within their actual
domestic duties; no delegation offshore immunizes them. No new in-absence
trial, abduction, rendition or foreign enforcement power arises. Actual
presence/transfer/trial and applicable immunity still require lawful grounds.

**P22 — Concurrent prosecution and actual credit.** Independent prosecutors/
courts compare real proceedings, competent forum, proof, defense, victim
access and timely lawful execution; a minister cannot choose a favorable
named court. A genuine competent final foreign acquittal/conviction for the
same factual episode bars duplicate ordinary prosecution in this candidate.
Different labels, nationality or a less severe foreign outcome do not evade
it. A fabricated document or body lacking judicial authority is not such
a judgment, but proving that defect is not a retrial on disliked merits.
No new general fraud/sham exception is created here; any distinct actual
international-crime exception needs its own precise law and independent review.

Where a fresh lawful case is actually permitted, give full credit for all
same-case custody/relabelled holds and actual penal payments/restrictions,
with P16 proportionality/aggregate limits and no invented money-to-prison
conversion. Civil/support remedies keep distinct proof/grounds. Jurisdiction
alone authorizes neither coercion nor imported foreign guilt.

**P23 — Extradition is not deportation by another name.** A request needs
actual prior statutory/treaty authority, authenticated identity/particular
conduct and credible supporting evidence, ordinarily dual criminality at the
relevant time. A foreign alert is not an arrest order. An independent court
hears counsel, source objections, age, specialty, onward-transfer risk and
conditions; a foreign-policy officer cannot overrule a protected-rights refusal.
Guarantee no prosecution/onward surrender beyond the lawful authorized scope
without fresh competent review; informal assurances are not a general power.

Do not transfer for conduct below 16 or to evade P05's child process and
age-specific protections. For 16–17 conduct, require genuinely enforceable
child proceedings/conditions consistent with that floor. Credible risk of
torture, prohibited ill-treatment, execution, irreducible life, political
persecution or flagrant fair-trial denial requires an effective independent
determination before irreversible transfer; no security/reciprocity balance
licenses prohibited treatment. A paper promise does not cure a persisting
substantial risk. Adult domestic ordinary maxima are not automatically a
foreign sentence ceiling; precise rights and actual applicable treaty govern.

A credible irreversible-protection challenge suspends transfer through an
effective independent appeal. Repetitive requests without new grounds need
a reasoned court decision, never executive removal while review is useless.
Any restraint under this country's control uses P08–P10 counsel and 24-hour
first hearing. Count relevant earlier same-episode pretrial deprivation
across states/offices toward the applicable aggregate 90/180-day ceiling.
The complete usable request must arrive within 30 days of first restraint
for this transfer process, including its earlier offshore/contractor holds,
or release follows. This additional candidate limit does not reset or
extend P clocks; proceedings may continue without custody. No successive
foreign request/immigration label evades expiry. Safe actual receiving,
clinical continuity and protected records accompany any lawful handover.
Clocks are uncalibrated proposals, not proof of staff or foreign performance.

**P24 — Assistance and international accountability.** Evidence/asset/witness
assistance needs B51 and its actual legal basis. Torture/secret-source limits,
independent defense and proportional powers survive secrecy, diplomacy or
foreign accusation. Actual international duties, immunities and competent
international-court requests need a distinct documented assessment, not
automatic treatment as an ordinary civil judgment or extradition request.

[G01–G14](../18-international-crimes-and-accountability.md) now supplies an
independently authored domestic genocide/humanity/war/aggression candidate,
with effective control, bounded presence reach and a narrow foreign sham-
process exception. It does not invent treaty membership or automatically
settle actual source/immunity/transfer compatibility; ordinary P routes
and applicable actual international obligations retain their scope. Rome Statute Article 80 leaves national penalty
law unaffected by its own penalty Part; it does not force this candidate
to replace P16's ordinary 25/five-year limits with thirty years/life. Separate
international-crime choices need fresh reasons rather than that false premise.
No updated external text or procedural rule can approve its own adoption.

[CD12](../21/06-release-and-transfer.md) separately governs actual conflict-status
transfer, protected receiving and continuing duty. P23's ordinary extradition
file clock is not automatic authority or the universal clock for POW transfer
or an actual G13 ICC request; each needs its own competent instrument and order.
