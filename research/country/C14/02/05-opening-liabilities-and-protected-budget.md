# Opening liabilities and protected budget choices

*Design292 · OL01–OL05 · Proposed fiscal transition rules.*

**OL01 — Keep the opening public balance sheet open to correction.**
At an authorized transition or material budget revision, Treasury
publishes a dated opening account with each relevant public body's
source returns and gaps. Include controlled public enterprises,
local bodies, restricted funds and potential guarantees as applicable
to the actual accounting and legal boundaries. Keep commitments,
verified payables, disputes, contingencies and missing information
distinct. State who incurred the claim, who owes or guarantees it,
the source instrument, due or plausible call date, payment currency,
restriction and original proof. Reconcile records without counting
one payment twice or merging distinct legal obligations. An estimate,
missing return or disputed claim remains visible with its scope and
an accountable next review. It cannot establish another payer's
liability or a spendable public balance.

**OL02 — Test how a plausible call changes each exact budget.**
With the same lawful baseline and protected duties for every option,
show the known due schedule, jointly plausible calls by payer and
date, and material exposures without a defensible bound. Distinguish
a legal ceiling, an assessed range and a scenario. Include late
confirmation, accelerated maturities and applicable currency or
settlement risk where supported. Reconcile gross commitments and
usable balances under WF90–WF94. Price any contingency reserve with
its actual authorization, source, cost and restrictions. Use no
unconfirmed recovery, guarantee counterclaim, unreceived advance
or separate account as available cash. Where a material unknown can
change feasibility, retain it in the decision and do not certify
the affected discretionary release from a favorable central estimate.

**OL03 — Preserve claims without prejudging contested liability.**
The assigned payer continues lawful due and verified payments, using
SC14 and other existing clocks. A competent authority records the
specific disputed part and protects evidence, claimant notice and
timely independent review. Preserve wages, pensions, benefits, court
and election duties and essential service on their own authority.
When necessary, fund an existing lawful service bridge separately
from the claimant or supplier dispute. Treasury can propose reserved
capacity to pay. Only the competent payer or adjudicator, under the
governing law, establishes whether payment is owed, stayed, made under
reservation or recoverable. An auditor supplies fiscal evidence without
determining civil liability. A fiscal register cannot freeze a creditor,
acknowledge a disputed debt, guarantee payment or create a haircut.

**OL04 — Give the elected body a real revised choice.** Before a material
commitment, attach the affected owner, cash date, actual service scope,
lawful source and any exact contingent alternative to the motion.
If material information arrives later, publish the changed exposure
and reconcile it against the prior version. Use only an already lawful
and independently verified alternative or return the changed proposal
under DB and T. Hold the affected optional release when it no longer
qualifies. An already binding obligation keeps its original status.
Continue the prior protected programme and pursue authorized urgent
repair where it is short. Give members, opposition and competent
independent review timely original evidence with lawful protection.
No outgoing or incoming officeholder, accounting label, forecast
or model gets to make a fiscal choice by default.

**OL05 — Follow each exposure through payment, remedy and handback.**
Assign source and evidence custody, actual execution, review and any
lawful successor separately. Record revisions, recognized amounts,
gross payments, creditor or resident receipt, remaining exposure and
actual recovery. Recheck at the next payment or policy decision
affected by the uncertainty and on material new evidence. Confidential
information stays purpose-limited. Unaffected spending and services
remain answerable on their own dates. AI may reconcile duplicate
references and test budget scenarios under human oversight. It has
no authority to admit or deny a claim, appropriate money or close
an audit. Reopen if evidence is withheld, a reserve hides ordinary
underfunding, or a more reliable paid ordinary route protects the
same duties with less loss.
