# Parentage, child voice and actually received maintenance

*B28–B30; part of [C08/16](../16-family-agency-property-and-estates.md).*

**B28 — A child is registered before adult disputes end.** Record birth and
immediate identity, with a safe accepting carer and independent access to
health, care, education and subsistence. Ordinary initial parentage records
the person giving birth and an undisputed lawful acknowledgement, existing
adjudication or adoption; marriage alone is no conclusive finding. Disputes
go to a child court with independent child representation, protected original
records, notice and hearing for materially affected adults. A registry, genetic
score, donor contract or social platform cannot decide contested parentage.

For ordinary conception outside an applicable donor arrangement, proved
genetic parenthood presumptively creates financial support responsibility;
it does not automatically displace a safe parent or force contact. A proved
nonconsensual use of a person's body/genetic material has a protected civil
exception; it does not leave the child's essential public receiving unpaid.
Contested proof needs proportionate court-controlled evidence, not a public
genomic register or a mandatory criminal conviction. Lawful existing donor
rights and their separate prospective statute remain distinct.
Intentional lawful parenting commitments and actual care
are relevant to responsibility under a reasoned order. There is no automatic
administrative two-parent ceiling, but adding a legal parent needs an actual
lawful basis and court findings, not signatures that sell or assign a child.
Assign each duty and protected relationship explicitly; no transitive powers.

No person is forced into contact/cohabitation with an abuser; sexual or
reproductive coercion, donor/gestational status and contested use of genetic
material require separate protected evidence and lawful adjudication. A
criminal conviction is not a prerequisite to civil safety. [RP01–RP16](../20-reproductive-law-and-parentage.md)
now proposes the distinct donor/origin, pregnancy-assistance, intentional
support, storage/death and future/foreign rules; no actual adoption is implied.
The child's services and existing lawful claims continue.

**B29 — Care arrangements without a custody formula.** Legal carers owe
safe care, not ownership. Hear the child with accessible independent support
and give their evolving views actual weight. Decide residence, contact,
education and ordinary decision roles from safety, continuity, relationships,
practical schedules and the child's needs; no automatic equal-time or one-
parent preference. Poverty, disability, family form or an AI attachment score
alone decides none of them. Resource support is considered before separation.

Care orders specify ordinary decision roles, major consultation, urgent
action, safe contact and review. They cannot override B22 bodily choice,
create a parental veto over complaint, or require dangerous repeated retelling.
Protected communication may use future media without compulsory tracking.
Review on material change, promptly on danger, and at least every six months
for a state-directed continuing out-of-home arrangement. A completed contact
session is not evidence of safety; a protective allegation alone is not guilt.

**B30 — Child support and safe budgets.** Legal obligors share verified
reasonable child costs, including the child's actual housing/access share,
after actual non-means-tested child resources and services already delivered.
Do not double-count a housing bill or public school/clinical service. A
means-tested payment triggered by missing support does not erase the private
duty; money merely owed is not an actual child resource. An assessed child's
ordinary needs are not financed by compulsory liquidation of their assets.

Allocate the remaining need among all actual obligors in proportion to their
usable resources after their own protected essentials, reconciling other
dependants/orders in the same account. Cap each amount at actual ability;
do not assume a relative's earnings, inaccessible assets or a hypothetical job.
Credit actual safe in-kind/cash provision toward the same assigned amount,
with no private authority to invent extra offsetting services. If combined
ability is insufficient, expose the shortfall and receive I/S protection
immediately; no fictional arrears are created for impossible prospective pay.
Existing accrued valid arrears need lawful review, not automatic deletion.

Ordinary duty lasts to 18; needs-based continuation during foundational
learning can last to 21 with actual need/ability and review. These ages do
not end disability/care, adult individual income, Y survivor or voluntarily
assumed valid rights. No lifelong unpaid family-care workforce is created.
Reassess on changed need/means; enforce without employer/partner coercion or
debt imprisonment. C07's receiver pays legally due provisional support while
claims are resolved; later recovery uses actual lawful resources and protects
the child's continuing needs. Its funding is a public duty, not proven cash.
