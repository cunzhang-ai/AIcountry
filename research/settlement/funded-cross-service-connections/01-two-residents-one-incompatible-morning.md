# Two residents and one incompatible morning

*Design541 · Constructed exercise for the first local service budget.*

Resident A accepts an accessible temporary flat. The only ordinary
accessible ride leaves at 09:00 and reaches it at 09:30. A qualified worker
must provide A's due care there by 10:00. A separately established cash
award must be usable by 10:30. A's free staffed cash pickup at 09:10 is
at the original site. Assume these individual offers all qualify and have
their own funding. The accepted flat and care can proceed together. The
current cash pickup and ride cannot. Resident B has a binding booking for
the only later accessible vehicle at 09:20 to meet a critical care cutoff
at 09:50. In the counterfactual A could finish pickup by 09:15 and use
that same vehicle to reach the flat by 09:50.
The same payers, times, legal protections and needs apply to each alternative.
All figures and arrangements are assumptions.

| Take the later vehicle booked for B | A may avoid the cash conflict if the later journey still meets A's own cutoff. B would lose a protected earlier route. On these facts there is no applicable priority change or qualified substitute for B. | Reject this route. Counting A's services together cannot turn B's loss into a success. |

The local schedule has three first-window units. The two-unit delivery uses
one qualified worker outside existing care and vehicle rosters, and leaves
one unit. Confirm actual cash receipt, not dispatch. If the pickup instruction
has already settled, keep the original payment record and use the applicable
correction process. If its status prevents lawful exclusive reassignment,
hold the duplicate principal and report the unmet cash duty. Existing
independently authorized interim assistance still follows its own law.

If A declines delivery to keep the address private, preserve the accepted
flat, ride and care and the underlying cash award. Offer another qualified
channel when available. Release only the uncommitted delivery hold and record
the missed receipt separately from refusal of the channel.

In a separate concurrent branch resident C needs the same two-unit
connection by the same scheduling cutoff. Assume additional qualified
staff could accept. Three units cover one, with one unit remaining.
The competent authority applies the actual lawful priority and gives
reasons and direct review. The number of other services attached to a
case confers no priority. With four units, test the two distinct workers
and accepting receivers before counting two complete routes. If they
share one worker unavailable for a second delivery, the second gap remains.

The US Government Accountability Office reported separate difficulty
reaching safe shelter, accessing medicine and obtaining recovery assistance
for older people and people with disabilities after three 2017 hurricanes.
It also identified potential underreporting from confusing application
questions. This supports checking lived receiving and missing people during
a coupled disruption. The evidence concerns disaster response. It supplies
no ordinary-service costs or measured effect of the proposed local schedule.
[GAO report](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-19-318)

For a real first budget, price ordinary repair and the exact eligible
connection tasks with the competent council, national funder and affected
people. Verify who reaches all applicable services at the original times,
what happens to people outside the coordinated offer, and whether recurrent
conflicts justify changing the ordinary delivery method.
