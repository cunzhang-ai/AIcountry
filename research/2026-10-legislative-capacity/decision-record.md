## The bounded defect and rival explanations

C03 reserves general law and material delegated rules for the Assembly.
C04/C09 reserve unresolved material interlocal allocations; C06 adds budget
and compound-scarcity choices; C10 includes war, emergency and external
commitments. Each reservation has a democratic reason. Together they draw
on the same plenary, committees, analysts, opposition time, public hearings
and original evidence. The earlier [territorial-authority analysis](../2026-10-operating-settlement/territorial-authority.md)
already identified national overload as a risk and selected Assembly finality
only conditionally because no real region, geography or electorate existed.
The later additions make cumulative capacity more consequential, but do not
prove that a new regional tier is preferable everywhere.

Backlog may reflect too many nationally reserved decisions, insufficient
legislative staff, strategic agenda suppression, poor source information,
real disagreement, over-formalized consultation or external shocks. These
causes have different remedies. No actual AIcountry docket, workload, case
delay or affected person's account exists. An authored list of duties is
evidence of possible collision, not an observed parliamentary failure.

The [European Charter of Local Self-Government](https://rm.coe.int/european-charter-of-local-self-government-/1680b0fbec)
is an official comparator for legally defined local competence, proximity,
consultation and adequate resources. [OECD analysis of inter-municipal
cooperation](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/how-to-make-inter-municipal-co-operation-work_44d03db6-en.html)
describes flexible forms and warns that more formal cooperation can add
complexity and accountability risk. Neither source binds AIcountry or
establishes the correct scope for its unformed territory.

## Whole institutional packages

| Package | Benefit | Failure or value conflict |
|---|---|---|
| Retain all current reservations without a combined load check | Preserves direct national authority and avoids a new procedure | Each good safeguard can consume the same limited hearing and review capacity; delay may reward the powerful |
| Move material interlocal and regulatory decisions to cabinet or technical agencies | Fast execution | Creates an executive shadow legislature and makes classification a route around voters |
| Establish permanent elected regions for all shared systems now | Directly replaceable decision-makers closer to repeated cross-local effects | No actual map, electorate, fiscal capacity or common geography; another tier may fragment rights and budgets |
| Require national direct votes or referendums for each material conflict | Visible popular choice | Repeated complex ballots can exhaust attention, reward sponsors and fail to represent nonvoting rights-holders |
| **Selected prior-law scope routing plus a public decision-capacity account** | Keeps local choice, Assembly-reserved national duties and the ability to replace a demonstrably overloaded route | Reporting can itself consume time, majority may ignore backlog, and without real geography it cannot certify the chosen territorial architecture |

The selected [C03 rule](../country/C03/05-assembly-capacity-and-scope.md) does not
transfer sovereign power. Prior law classifies national, local, voluntary
interlocal, effect-sensitive delegated and temporary emergency decisions. The
Assembly remains the residual final authority for unresolved material shared
allocations until a legitimately established regional competence displaces
it. Its own administration publishes one combined queue and resource account;
opposition and affected local bodies can trigger publication and a protected
agenda item without majority committee permission. A court may enforce that
process, but not dictate the vote. The Assembly can then change prospective routing
by valid law, or start a separate regional founding process. An indicator,
missed deadline or committee decision cannot itself transfer authority.

## Democratic chain, continuing obligations and objections

The electorate can remove Assembly members; Assembly leaders arrange the
agenda under protected opposition rules; professional parliamentary staff
produce the load account from authenticated dockets and committee records.
C11 preserves source versions, while opposition, local bodies and independent
inquiry can challenge omissions. A court can compel a lawfully required
hearing or information access and preserve existing rights; it cannot choose
a region, tax rate or water allocation. C06 funds staff, translation,
participation and remedy execution. These offices can still share a party,
paymaster, vendor or information controller. A capture test must trace the
complete appointment, funding, evidence, review and enforcement chain,
including when the majority refuses to schedule its own accountability.

The strongest objection is that another official account and hearing consume
the capacity they measure. Reuse the existing C06 commitment account and
Assembly docket rather than creating a new planning commission, and scale
the response to material effects. A contrary objection is that the Assembly
can always explain delay and still do nothing. Keep direct local competence,
protected opposition agenda access, legal remedies and public disclosure; repeated
unexecuted duties reopen the choice of territorial architecture. The research
selection cannot guarantee a noncooperating majority will fund a regional
alternative or obey a court.

An overbroad materiality threshold can centralize routine local decisions;
an unduly narrow one lets councils shift costs downstream. Prior law must
state affected-scope and cumulative-effect criteria before the dispute, with
review of competence but no judicial policy substitution. Existing permits,
service duties, debts and pending claims survive any new routing rule until
lawfully assigned funded successors. A temporary compact or regional body
cannot acquire taxation or sovereign competence through a delayed national
vote. Revised election or opposition rules take prospective effect under C14.

## Status and next bounded test

Evidence qualification: the cited official comparators identify assignment
and cooperation risks; the cumulative collision here is a documented design
inference from current C03–C10 duties. Research selection: prior-law routing
with combined workload disclosure and elected reconsideration. Legal
authorization, staffed readiness and observed outcomes: none. No real
legislature, public docket, boundaries, shared resources or affected public
has been supplied; authored analysis is not an independent review.

For an actual jurisdiction, trace one ordinary legislative term: all mandatory
budgets, delegated-rule votes, treaty authorizations, territorial petitions,
interlocal residuals, emergency renewals and opposition hearings, with their
time, staff, due dates and resulting decisions. Compare the retained national
route, an elected region for a durable shared area, and narrower prospective
delegation using original dockets and affected people's accounts. Completion
requires a lawful competence map, credible cost and timing, objections from
excluded and downstream people, and evidence of executed remedies. The
responsible role, financing ceiling and authority to conduct such a real
study remain unassigned. Reopen this choice if national backlog, strategic
agenda control, local exclusion or cumulative cost makes the selected route
less democratic or less usable than a coherent rival.
