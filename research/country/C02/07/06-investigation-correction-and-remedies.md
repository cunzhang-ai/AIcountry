# PD21–PD24: protected inquiry, received correction and independent remedies

*Design 96 ordinary-data research candidate; not enacted law, funded readiness or
demonstrated superiority.*

**PD21 — One usable request and controlled inquiry.** Accept individual, representative
and grounded public-interest accounts through B13 assisted/offline/remote routes without
a paid account or complete legal dossier. Preserve first receipt and urgency, give a
reasoned competence/referral decision and retain routing responsibility until a lawful
receiver accepts. B16's help/ordinary decision/one extension and direct delay challenge
apply; useful-time urgent protection is earlier, not a30day waiting permission. The
chief sets public risk/complaint plus bounded random compliance work within the enacted
plan; case assignments use prepublished qualification, rotation and conflict rules. An
agency/AI flag is an inquiry lead, not proof. Reasons must disclose examined/unexamined
scope; investigation cannot secretly become a blacklist. The controller’s PD09
calendar-day access clock is not replaced by this B16 supervisor proceeding. A referral
does not grant another wait before urgent care or a vote.

**PD22 — Inspect a controller's conduct, not everybody's life.** On an attributed case
or a lawfully scoped compliance audit, statutory inspectors may require necessary
relevant processing/authority records, data-flow/security logs, named processor
contracts and minimised identified samples within expressly enacted record powers,
purpose, period and lawful custody. Give usable reasons and an urgent court route
against excess; protect privilege, self-incrimination, complainants and unrelated
subjects. Nonconsensual entry, forced seizure, private-message interception or direct
account/server access requires prior specific independent judicial authorization under
applicable C08/C10 safeguards; this charter supplies no urgent warrant exception. It
does not require every controller to send entire databases, passwords or decryption keys
to the office. Preserve relevant exculpatory evidence and a separately controlled
protected access/copy route where needed; protect the regulator's own source custody
from its suppliers. A criminal case cannot disguise itself as an inspection to defeat P
protections.

**PD23 — Correction needs a ground and a reachable executor.** After B16 notice,
evidence/reply and reasons, a designated statutory decision officer may order a
specified controller/processor to give established access/explanation, correct
inaccurate consequential records, stop identified unlawful processing/transfer, restrict
use pending lawful resolution, or delete data no longer lawfully retained. Identify
lawful duty, affected copies/recipients, safe date, responsible officer and actual
verification. An inaccurate operational entry may need an appended correction and
restricted old evidence, not destruction of legally required source history. Preserve
necessary protected litigation/archive material under its own lawful basis and retention
limit; 'audit trail' grants no permanent universal archive. No order compels publication
of private sources, destroys relevant defense evidence or removes a legally operative
public record by analogy. Correcting a source record does not itself change a
tax/benefit/election rule, W/CPI/M definition or final judgment: the competent
revenue/service/election/court owner reviews each consequential decision under its own
law, with direct review and useful-time interim protection. A court can review
law/facts/necessity, stay, annul or require lawful correction; no mandatory internal
exhaustion. Appeals are not automatic stays. Civil compensation needs proved wrong,
causation and recoverable harm under B, not every disagreement or data breach as
presumed damages; punishment never pays the claimant by itself.

**PD24 — Urgency, sanctions and execution remain different.** A competent statutory
officer may use V04's necessary temporary stop/isolation/preservation only on documented
credible imminent serious harm: exact act, safe receiving, notice and B17 independent
hearing within72actual hours, at most one necessary72hour extension, no serial reset.
Urgent court consideration remains within B17's24hour outer limit and earlier useful
time. Later bounded protection follows V04 review; ordinary nonpunitive conditions
follow V05 necessity/six-month and material-change review. Unlawful processing may have
to cease before a replacement exists; protect actual remaining lawful service, retain
the person's claims and expose unserved need, rather than compel unlawful continuation
or call interruption a completed remedy. VD08/V06 alone authorizes proposed penalties:
existing A/B fault, ability, notice, P proof/defenses and ceilings; inspectors
investigate/propose, an independent civilian court determines contested punishment. No
new administrative turnover fine, custody, seizure reward or penalty-funded budget.
B18/B19 supply only their lawfully adopted specific execution scope, never a new force
unit, universal server takeover, tax or worker. Public bodies have the same substantive
duties; internal state fines create no national resource, and personal
discipline/liability requires separately proved duty/fault. Courts may order actual
access, correction, restriction, erasure, safe disclosure, alternative service, narrowly
justified cessation and compensation for proved material or nonmaterial harm, with
privacy-protected proof, proportional costs and receiving executor. A credible claimant
identifies the act and harm; the controller supplies its retained base, purpose and
source decisions under B14 without making the person prove secret systems. Preserve
third-party speech, records and defense. VD08/V06 monetary penalties apply only to its
already scheduled material processing/disclosure outside enacted basis or scoped
delegation and deliberate denial of established access/correction, plus precise
access/security decision-record duties where enacted; erasure, portability, objection,
breach notification and other new duties have civil correction unless the Assembly
expressly extends the closed VD schedule prospectively. No mere model error is guilt, no
fine pays the office, and no civil claim becomes a new crime. B15's ordinary civil clock
and urgent B17 relief remain.
