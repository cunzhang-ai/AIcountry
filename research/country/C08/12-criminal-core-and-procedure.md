# C08. A bounded criminal core with an enforceable defense

15 October 2026 · Integrated design 73 · Candidate code, not enacted law

The country uses criminal power for defined culpable harm, while prevention,
victim support and civil restoration keep their own grounds. Select the
following general part, core schedule and procedure as the working draft.
It supplies concrete boundaries rather than a promise that police or AI will
choose fairly. It does not claim a complete national criminal code.

| Part | Candidate provisions |
|---|---|
| [Responsibility and defenses](12/01-responsibility-and-defenses.md) | P01–P05: legality, fault, causation, participation, excuses and children |

The draft raises minimum criminal responsibility from the predecessor's 14
to 16, retains child proceedings for conduct before 18, removes the proposed
extra police-custody day and adds aggregate pretrial limits. These are explicit
normative research choices, not adopted law, estimates of optimal deterrence
or a change in medical consent, civil capacity or political membership.
Most core maxima preserve predecessor numbers; new grave-negligence,
coercion, exploitation, torture and obstruction limits are provisional
values requiring public comparison and practitioner review.

[Boundary cases](../../settlement/criminal-core/README.md) expose mistaken
reports, corporate/AI blame, disappearing custody time, economic compulsion,
child punishment disguised as care and inability to pay. They are reasoned
hypotheticals, not judgments or observed performance. An accurate article
map cannot supply independent investigators, defenders, courts or safe custody.

Retain/repair, broader incapacitation and community-first responses remain
substantive competing models. Select differentiated protection and bounded
penal power, with no-conviction/no-sanction alternatives and voluntary
restoration; reject broad public-order criminalization, indefinite predicted-
risk detention and obligatory reconciliation. Serious violence still has a
lawful criminal route. The [existing penal architecture](03-criminal-justice-and-custody.md),
[courts](02-courts-and-effective-orders.md), [official-abuse route](04-coercive-power-and-official-abuse.md)
and [protected transition](../C14/06-staged-capacity-and-protection.md) remain
cross-cutting duties. No statutory adoption or software change occurs here.

The [civil core](13-civil-obligations-and-executed-remedies.md) now supplies
B01–B20. [K13–K24/Q01–Q12](15-special-crimes-and-bounded-sanctions.md) now adds
special harms, finite alternative/body sanctions, records and prosecution periods.
[P21–P24](17/07-criminal-reach-and-transfer.md) now adds criminal reach, foreign
finality and transfer; [G01–G14](18-international-crimes-and-accountability.md)
adds a defined national international-crime candidate. Regulatory/conflict-
detention law, actual treaty compatibility and costs remain open. Reopen on an ambiguous element,
disparate coercion, unreliable proof, failed remedy or a better social model.
