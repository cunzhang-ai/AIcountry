# A nonviable bank: bounded intervention and explicit losses

*M09–M10; part of [C06/24](../24-financial-law-and-loss-allocation.md).*

The [RA01–RA06 panel and BF/BR chain](../39-bank-failure-and-protected-payment.md) now completes the distinct authority: fixed three seats, two actual votes, separate M07 insurer and automatic meaningful court review. These are research rules, not actual appointees.

**M09 — Entry is a reasoned finding, not a ratio alarm.** The prudential
supervisor supplies source accounts/valuations. A distinct resolution authority
finds actual/likely near-term inability to meet due obligations or nonpositive
net realizable assets after liabilities, no credible timely private cure, and
why ordinary U liquidation cannot preserve critical lawful functions with
less interference. Near-term means the next 30 calendar days, using evidenced
due obligations, realizable values and uncertainty, not an indefinite forecast.
For a solvent viable bank, assess already lawful available M15 liquidity
before treating a temporary cash gap as resolution necessity; do not assume
a new rescue or use liquidity to hide an actual capital loss.
Ratio breach, an AI forecast or sovereign political instruction alone is
insufficient. Independent reviewers see material assumptions and alternatives;
actual control/funding/source conflicts require alternate qualified assignment.
Notify parties and court immediately; urgent action has a meaningful judicial
hearing within 72 hours without serial reset. No merits immunity by urgency.

Within enacted scope it may transfer identified assets/liabilities to an
actually funded willing licensed receiver, establish a bounded bridge, or
write down/convert eligible claims according to M10. Preserve linked collateral,
netting and client property; no cherry-picking away a lawful security package.
A bridge has a published maximum two-year term, reviewed every six months;
continuation beyond that needs a fresh Assembly statute and explicit losses,
not an authority's automatic renewal. No forced funding by healthy firms.

A narrow stay on termination triggered solely by resolution may run for at
most two working days from actual entry, with immediate review. It does not
stop performance/termination for separate actual nonperformance, client
withdrawal of segregated property or essential due access. No blanket deposit
holiday, tax, new guarantee, pension raid or foreign-law rewrite follows.
If the bounded transfer/bridge fails, move to lawful winding down with M12
receivers and existing claims, not an indefinite technical protectorate.

**M10 — A bank-specific prior loss hierarchy.** Return proved client/trust
property outside the estate. Valid security receives only its net collateral
value under fair realization; deficiency joins its lawful unsecured class.
For the unencumbered estate, distribute assets in this order:

1. Reasonable court-reviewed preservation/administration costs.
2. U04's protected recent wage and due dependant claims on the same capped basis.
3. Covered deposits and insurer claims for actual paid covered amounts, equal rank.
4. Uninsured ordinary deposits of natural persons/nonfinancial businesses/charities.
5. Other ordinary unsecured claims, including ordinary tax and public-deposit claims.
6. Expressly subordinated controlling-insider/instrument claims; then owner residual.

Within each class use pro rata equal treatment. Loss absorption is the reverse
order: exhaust owner equity before subordinate claims, then lower-ranking
ordinary claims, before more senior eligible classes. Do not write down a
covered depositor's M07 payment right, client property, secured collateral
value or the protected wage tier. Insolvent-estate recovery and funded insured
payout are distinct; the insurer cannot claim more than it actually paid.
Uninsured depositor losses remain visible and get no invented guarantee.

Prospective bank statute expressly displaces only U04's ordinary unsecured
ranking, preserving other U protections. Equal-rank departures require proved
necessary critical-function preservation, reasons and M11 full compensation;
no political friend exemption. A Treasury/monetary advance has only its enacted
security/rank; an emergency label cannot jump prior creditors. No new general
levy, criminal guilt or release of a borrower from an otherwise valid loan.
