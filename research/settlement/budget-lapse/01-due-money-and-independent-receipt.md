# Day 91. Due money and independent receipt

An Assembly has not passed a new annual budget or a separate continuation.
The fiscal bridge ends after day 90. The standing schedule and an express
closeout were validly enacted beforehand in this authored case. All amounts
are abstract units. The payees can accept settlement when a tested rail is
available. Water has a qualified provider for the fixture. The account
models payment, with actual water and remedy delivery still to be checked.

| Line | Due | Obligation | Cash in its lawful purpose | Lawful payment scope |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Earned disability payment | 91 | 4 | 4 | Continuing schedule |
| Urgent election-appeal judgment | 91 | 3 | 3 | Continuing schedule with a tested B19 substitute for this exact judgment |
| Accepted essential water work | 91 | 2 | 1 | Continuing schedule with a permitted partial payment that leaves the rest due |
| Existing bridge equipment hire returned on day 90 | 92 | 2 | 2 | Express closeout with its own source and ceiling of 2 |
| Proposed new hire starting day 91 | None | 0 | 0 for this purpose | Requires actual new authority and a separate commitment |

The new hire requests two units. Matching the price of the old hire does
not give it a claim. The old hire was lawfully committed during the bridge,
fully earned by day 90 and verified at settlement. Its two units cannot
fund water or a replacement hire. The first four lines total 11 due.

| Base, with the narrow closeout | 10 | 1 | Water cash is one unit short |
| No Act carrying closeout permission | 8 | 3 | Water cash is short, old hire has two units of debt without payment authority |

| Both routes for the appeal depend on a disabled rail | 7 | 4 | Funded appeal misses its deadline and water remains short |

| Hire verification arrives on day 121 | 8 | 3 | The balance is held, but the closeout is expired and the debt is still due |

Where timely water is essential to the recipient's safety, a partial
payment is not equivalent to full water service. Verify safe interim
receiving and record harm, even if a later payment settles the invoice.
A separate legislative source could pay the full water line on day 91
if enacted and usable before the deadline. A vote alone has no such effect.
A missed appeal opportunity also needs its own timely remedy beyond cash.
