## Restoration and receiving

The published agenda for 17 November asks Copenhagen's Finance
Committee to recommend a three-day restoration objective for
HOFOR collective drinking-water and wastewater service. The agenda
describes an implementation plan for HOFOR's board in March 2027,
expects an unpriced tariff increase and places financing for
municipal critical institutions outside the recommendation.
It describes reliance on customers' own arrangements in the
interval. As of 11 November this is an agenda proposal. It
qualifies a concrete interface to test. Independent acceptance
at a care facility or home route and the extended interval beyond
the target require a paid receiving plan.
