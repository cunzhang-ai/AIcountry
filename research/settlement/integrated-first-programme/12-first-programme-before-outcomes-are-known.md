# The first budget when the useful warning comes late

This constructed comparison tests [T37–T42](../../country/C14/06/11-first-programme-before-outcomes-are-known.md).
It is a new matched set of assumptions within the first-programme design.
Its amounts cannot be combined with earlier constructed or observed budgets.

The government has 10 settled units by day 1 and two further lawful units
by day 4. Eight of the opening units are already due for benefits (4),
ordinary care (2) and separate court and election operations (2). Assume
their actual, independent routes are staffed and paid. The response
envelope has two units on day 1 and two additional units by day 4. No
protected allocation can be shifted into it.

The government must protect day 5 service against an additional loss of
water delivery or of qualified home-care cover. Both possibilities have
the same usable evidence on day 1. A confirmed distinction can first
be independently received on day 4. Without prior preparation, each
route needs four days from decision to delivery. A fixed route costs
two units on day 1 and one by day 4. A second fixed route requires
distinct staff and funds. Preparation for both costs four on day 1
and six in total, above both available limits.

A single-call reserve costs two units on day 1 to keep both distinct,
qualified provider options actually available. Activation of one costs
two on day 4 and can deliver on day 5. The terms authorize only one
activation for this period. Both providers voluntarily accept their
respective standby and delivery terms. The costs, qualifications,
independence and timing are assumptions.

| Day 1 decision | Water loss only | Care loss only | No loss |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wait and contract only after confirmed evidence | Cannot deliver by day 5 | Cannot deliver by day 5 | No option cost |
| Prepare only the fixed water route | Water delivered | Care gap | Preparation remains paid |
| Prepare only the fixed care route | Water gap | Care delivered | Preparation remains paid |
| Pay to keep the two single-call options open | Water delivered with timely verification and activation | Care delivered with timely verification and activation | Two units used on standby |

The final row is the selected conditional first-programme path for
this case. Taking the better fixed row separately after seeing each
outcome would hide the single choice that had to be made on day 1.
The reserve costs more in an activated single-loss case than the fixed
response that happened to be right. Its advantage is the ability to
choose later while still meeting the deadline.

If both losses occur, the prepared options still allow at most one call.
The model does not choose between protected services. Use only existing
competent authority and show the remaining gap. If verification or the
activation funds arrive on day 5, the paid option cannot deliver on time.
The original eight units remain separately protected. A fresh call
after the covered period needs actual funds, accepted staff and source.
An unused option or successful first dispatch is no replenishment.

The exercise compares dated authority and resources under assumed
evidence. It supplies no actual warning time, contract, cost, emergency
authority or lived outcome. [Reproduction](verify-first-programme-late-evidence.py).
