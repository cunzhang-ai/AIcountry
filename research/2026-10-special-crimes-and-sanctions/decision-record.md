## Alternatives, values and selection

Compare broad custodial/strict regulatory enforcement, mainly civil/voluntary
restoration and differentiated bounded criminal power. Civil repair alone
may fail serious coercion or unpayable harms; broad criminality can punish
status/error and expand custody. Select the third as a conditional draft,
with specific culpable harms, separate care/protection, noncustodial/no-sanction
comparison and independent proof. No observed causal superiority is claimed.

K13–K24 specifies child/exploitation, privacy, physical/sector and public-power
harms. Q01–Q12 adds usable needs-based units, finite chosen community work,
proved body responsibility, bounded fines/orders, confiscation, sealing and
prosecution periods. Existing P burdens, child age/process, liberty caps,
no coercion-derived proof and no AI conviction/sentence remain. No association
crime, new search power, political truth tribunal or generic misconduct
catchall arises. Published sector predicates remain necessary and unadopted.

Values: effective protection, individual agency, equal standing, dependable
process, proportionality and practical exit from state supervision. New
ages, peer months, maxima, units, percentages, hours and periods are candidate
value choices, not fitted science. Every accused/victim/child retains their
separate standing; voting or averaging their harms cannot settle guilt.

## Support, objections and actual inspection

Primary ICCPR Articles 9/14/15 support liberty, fair trial/finality and
legality/leniency context. Article 11's contractual-debt rule is not a treaty
mandate for our penal-fine cancellation. CRC General Comment 24 addresses
child status criminalization, consistent responsibility and under-18 process;
it does not select the new sexual-protection/peer or fine parameters.
Exact sources/consultation scope are in the [source note](../settlement/special-crimes/03-selection-sources-and-limits.md).
No numeric input is an original observation; shared old drafts are one
source family. This bounded check is not a global current-law/events review.

Twenty-four authored boundary cases examine value cliffs, mistaken/synthetic
proof, hidden income/work coercion, organizations, receiving and finality.
A separate arithmetic exercise exposes a 148-hour staffing gap before
omissions. It neither tests verdict correctness nor supplies safe ratios,
whole-country costs or capacity. Independent review is not fabricated by
running code or naming a second model.

Material objections persist: threshold cliffs can treat close peers
differently; strict ceilings/expiry may be too lenient or reward concealment;
assessment and community consent can be coercive; corporate costs pass to
innocent workers/users; elastic duties/old records can perpetuate control.
Retain these disagreements. Explicit elements, safe evidence, independent
burdens, needs floors, short clocks, narrow tolling and accepted successors
are proposed responses, not demonstrated solutions. A less harmful effective
model can replace this selection; extension is not automatically improvement.

## Power, compatibility and continuing obligations

Elected general law fixes offenses, limits and funding. Ordinary independent
prosecutors decide charges; human courts with funded defense decide guilt
and sentence. Outside-command routes apply to implicated protectors, with
predesignated alternate assignment and independently accessible evidence.
Cabinet, Assembly, vendors and AI cannot select a favorable named result.
Courts can enforce limits; a nominally separate reviewer sharing funds,
records and executor may still be captured. No actual independence is proved.

Consumers changed: C08 entry/core, current country overview/status,
selection/conflict/audit, completion priorities, commitment inventory and
history. This is a new review scope; no old validator is refreshed. Current
software, constitution and source manuscripts are unchanged. Migration maps
each old offense, period, sentence, custody credit, fine, property, record
and support claim to a lawful receiver, with leniency and no revived bar.
No retroactive criminalization, bulk deletion or executive release follows.

C06 must cost investigators, advocates, counsel, technical evidence, courts,
means assessment, safe paid supervision/transport, custody/receiving, monitors,
record correction and claims once by due period. A 37-function bid is not
qualified for this increment. No penalty/proceeds forecast, unpaid-work saving,
AI gain or public/pension guarantee supplies missing funds. Care, voter access,
safe water, humane animal care and civil victims' claims keep distinct routes.
Sensitive actual testimony/images are not collected or reproduced here.
