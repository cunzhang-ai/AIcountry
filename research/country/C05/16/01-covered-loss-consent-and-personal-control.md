# EB01–EB04. The support belongs to the person, not the old job

*Design168 · Part of [earnings transition](../16-portable-earnings-and-work-transition.md).*

**EB01 — Add bounded adjustment time, keep basic rights independent.** Existing
I07 assesses current usable resources/earlier24-hour shock protection, I02 actual
basket prices, I03 housing, SG/service/earned rights, WL dues and Y06 qualified
credits remain. Choose an additional individual, non-household/non-asset-tested
finite bridge for covered personal labor-income loss. It is not a new ordinary
universal-income destination, a privatized insurance account or a promise every
prior standard of consumption survives. Basic I05/care/housing/clinical rights
never wait for this application, employer proof, work search or a recession label.
People with no/short history retain those rights even if the bridge is small/zero.

**EB02 — Identify the actual covered event and source.** Cover involuntary job
end/redundancy, actual fixed-term/seasonal expiry causing joblessness, or genuinely
agreed temporary reduction of future paid hours for a documented operational
loss. A protected necessary exit from proved serious danger/abuse or unavoidable
unaccommodated necessary care is assessed on actual evidence and safe alternatives,
not treated as leisure by an employer label or made a duty to provide family care.
Ordinary planned nonwork, vacation, a pay cut with unchanged actual hours, asset
loss or an ordinary voluntary resignation alone is not a new qualifying event.
Self-employment needs evidenced cessation/loss of actual personal labor activity,
not business turnover, passive profit, speculative orders or an unpaid invoice alone.

Actual work under national labor/source jurisdiction or expressly funded lawful
cross-border coordination supplies the reference; nationality/account count or
an extrapolated foreign salary does not. Proved covered work is not excluded
merely because an employer failed registration/remittance or status is disputed.
No new citizenship waiting period or permission to work follows. Prior foreign/
inherited insurance and MB rights keep their actual source; no world wage guarantee.
A credible report can start assisted receipt without a complete payroll dossier.

**EB03 — Preserve genuine short-time choice and existing pay.** WL06 guaranteed
hours/WL07 individual or actually binding collective agreement apply. Before
agreement explain reduced future hours/pay, expected end, return to original
guaranteed hours, the person's bridge formula/calendar limits and later job-loss
implications. Silence, hunger, private-platform/AI assent or public payment is
no consent. Employer still pays all actual work/hourly floors, leave and notice;
refusal retains lawful WL bargaining/termination/redundancy rights, not a new job
guarantee. Hidden worked hours, already owed wages/notice or TW's retained-pay
statutory shorter week cannot become publicly funded idle time. A benefit decision
neither permits dismissal nor makes an unlawful contract change valid.

**EB04 — Portable time for a real choice, no workfare.** Select no additional
job-search quota, employer-viability certificate, compulsory training or required
acceptance of a particular job for this finite transition buffer. Offer actual
voluntary work/learning/placement help under N/WL with competent paid receiving,
not a fictitious post or AI career allocation. Actual new work earnings reduce
the insured gap rather than ending support merely on re-employment. Safe paid
trials and choices within the original spell do not renew its clock; an ordinary
voluntary quit cannot create a new spell or extra days by itself. The original
covered event must be genuine; fraud needs actual fault/process, not a political
or personality score. This autonomy choice has real source/behavioral costs and
is contestable against suitable-work conditions/stronger basic-only protection.
Basic rights/Y06 qualification remain separate, not sanctioned by this choice.
