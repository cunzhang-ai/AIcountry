# CB13–CB18: a lawful contribution is not yet funded repair

*Part of [C09/14](../14-shared-buildings-and-neighbor-rights.md); paid common dependence.*

**CB13 — Allocate actual costs once.** The registered source schedule states
fixed ordinary common-cost shares summing to one, distinct from ownership value,
household income and number of AI accounts. For new units without an agreed
lawful alternative, use each unit's independently measured internal floor area
over the sum of such areas; auxiliary stores/parking remain attached to their
principal unit. Missing/contested areas do not become zero or guessed shares;
preserve actual prior rights and seek source correction. Allocate minor currency
units by largest fractional remainders, ties by prepublished stable unit IDs,
so charges sum exactly to the actual bill; reverse matched refunds without
inventing a second charge. Separate metered individual use and actual separable service-
group costs go to their actual users/beneficiaries, with no double charge.
Necessary structure/fire/accessible entry is shared even if an owner says they
do not personally use the roof/lift. Actual caused damage has B contribution
on proof, not an automatic landlord/tenant blame fine. Permanent reallocations
need CB08/PT protection; existing lawful shares are not rewritten by this
default. Size is an imperfect proxy: heritage, commercial load, access and
lower-floor objections can favor a different lawful prospective schedule.

**CB14 — Budget, renewal and real protected money.** Show current operation,
actual repair, qualified planned renewal, reasonable management/insurance,
tax, contingencies, opening reserve and due invoices, using actual cost shares.
Fund necessary current work and dated renewal contributions under the accepted
plan; no majority opts out of needed protection by voting away a reserve.
A forecast establishes neither a contractor nor adequate reserve; no uniform
national reserve fraction or automatic full insurance is selected. Keep separately
accounted operating and restricted renewal money in actually safeguarded/custodial
arrangements under M/NB with exact title/coverage and independent reconciliation.
No manager's operating account, affiliate loan, speculative trade or pledge of
restricted funds. Resident/owner safe inspection of accounts, actual creditors
and independent review continue. Collection, reserve accumulation and actual
repair are different flows; a reserve draw is no second expense/GDP repair and
debt principal no duplicate depreciation. Uncollected bills are not bank cash.

**CB15 — Who owes, due date and sale.** Ordinary current operating liability
follows actual ownership days. A lawful special repair/renewal levy is owed by
the actual owner on its levy date, within the approved lawful scope; identify
which rule/bill applies and credit earlier same-item receipts. Give a usable
itemized bill at least30calendar days before due payment. Necessary emergency
work may begin earlier with actual lawful source, not a fiction of collected
cash or private seizure. A new buyer owes their actual subsequent liabilities,
not an automatic inheritance of seller arrears or undisclosed private guarantees.
Sellers disclose unpaid bills, approved/pending material work, plan/reserve,
defects, disputes and source restrictions before binding sale and update before
completion; B/consumer remedies and PT title remain distinct. No dues certificate
insures hidden physical defects or acquires B38 assurance by analogy.

**CB16 — Arrears are not a private punishment system.** Verify correct owner,
share, actual work/budget/authority, due amount, receipts and challenge. Give
at least20working days of usable payment/plan/defense opportunity; compulsory
collection starts only after both this opportunity and the lawful due date.
Only independent B/PT/U courts can authorize actual garnishment, levy or sale;
no automatic statutory lien, creditor superpriority, percentage collection bounty,
late-payment fine, compounding charge, vote suspension or essential-service
cutoff. Actual justified loss/ordinary contract interest requires B lawful
terms/proof; a manager cannot invent a rate. Association debt ranks ordinarily
unsecured unless a holder separately actually grants valid PT security. U needs,
stay/discharge and H/C07 safe receiving persist; others' shares are not joint
guarantees. A short fund still owes the contractor under its real contract;
report unpaid repair/source loss rather than assume a court or other owners
will supply the gap without lawful liability.

**CB17 — Existing necessary-need protection has to reach the bill.** Retain
I03's actual necessary owner-occupier running cost and specifically legally
due protected safety/renewal item, with I05 personal actual-resource assessment,
separate protected needs, earlier hardship and direct review. An actual verified
necessary share to keep an adequate primary home can qualify; nominal property
depreciation, mortgage principal, speculative improvement, avoidable luxury,
another owner's bill or an entire association debt does not qualify by label.
This applies the existing need guarantee; no new all-owner repair subsidy,
public loan/lien, insurance, separate wealth grant or source-free public takeover.
Use proportionate actual defect/work/source proof with provisional urgent help,
not a requirement for a poor resident to finance the job before claiming.
Where lawful, the housing/income authority pays the verified due item directly
to an actual accepted contractor/protected receiver. Count that housing receipt
and matched expense once; do not reduce unrelated person-controlled cash or
report the same invoice paid by tenant, owner, reserve and G. An actually received
same-loss insurer/developer payment is credited; expected litigation recovery
cannot delay due safety/need protection. Private landlords pay their own CB
shares; H rent/security rules forbid an automatic capital pass-through to tenants.
Actual public incidence, owner-wealth benefit and source cost remain contested.

**CB18 — Independent execution, access and a bounded receiver.** Ordinary
courts can order the association/actual liable owner to perform a proved duty,
approve its lawful proportionate necessary cost, and appoint a qualified paid
unconflicted task receiver where management is absent/captured or refuses.
Name work, budget/source, records, affected hearing, report and at most90calendar
days per appointment, renewal only on fresh necessity; it cannot become a new
landlord/government or alter title/priority. Do not demand new unanimous consent
to a duty already owed. Unaccepted/unfunded receiver is disclosed failure.
Planned private-unit entry needs actual occupant consent with ordinarily48hours'
notice or a specific independent civil access order: necessary shared task,
least intrusive route, qualified workers, safe time, restoration/payment and a
date window no longer than30calendar days. Fresh proof for extension; no blanket
key, home camera, private labor, eviction or search power. Immediate life/hazard
rescue retains its actual HP/technical/LF source and safe receiving, not CB
authority to break in. Court execution uses actual B18/LF task/force safeguards.
Access, the contractor's acceptance, completion and people's received protection
are separate; a judge cannot create workers, taxes or an unsafe forced move.

[RT09–RT10 private rents](../18/02-review-index-and-improvements.md) keeps CB's
lawful common-work mandates and owner obligations distinct from tenant pricing.
No individual RT veto or automatic capital pass-through follows; an exceptional
private-rent allowance needs its actual new-law scope, independent permission,
net eligible cost/useful-service proof and finite recovery. H cost rent remains.
