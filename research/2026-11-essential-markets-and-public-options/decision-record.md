# Design498 decision record

**Basis.** Continue from the [Design497 baseline](baseline.json)
and [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md). Decide how to keep effective
exit when a commercially or publicly controlled bottleneck stops
essential access. Compare with enforceable private switching, an
independent mutual or contracted route, a scoped directly staffed
service and broader government ownership.

**Choice.** Keep plural ownership. Use the least burdensome qualified
independent route for each essential duty. The elected locality
chooses qualified provision under common rights and ordinary
equalization. Escalate a repeated exposed gap to a finite-capital,
recurring elected public or cooperative service decision. Require
operator-neutral terms for ordinary equalization where compatible
with the protected duty. Prospectively include narrowly designated legally due home support in the existing turnover-independent gateway. Keep antitrust and public service authority
separate, including merger filing, legal merits, emergency receipt
and actual funded succession. Use the [comparison](../settlement/essential-markets-and-public-options/01-two-real-routes-to-an-essential-service.md)
and [sources](sources.md) within their scopes.

**Objection and authority.** Public providers can capture the same
records and decision paths as private owners. Upfront costs can
crowd out existing duties. Conversely, portability cannot create a
new worker or a physical network. Evidence from parallel analyses
shares sources and does not supply affected-party participation or
qualified independent final review. Neither the designation, a
market allegation nor a local financial gap authorizes compulsory
transfer, takeover or a new funding source.

**Migration and reopening.** Preserve prior transactions' legal
effects, existing lawful service, cases, payment claims, local
competence and independent remedies. Reopen after demonstrably
usable switching, a more effective compared public or cooperative
route, repeat origin disruption, selective funding or failure of
the remaining alternate. A competent adopter needs the actual
control evidence, timely accepted roster and owned settlement
for the specific scope. Readiness and comparative outcomes
remain separate from the research choice.
