# Design271 evidence and custody

- The [2024 NICC annual report](https://www.nifc.gov/sites/default/files/NICC/2-Predictive%20Services/Intelligence/Annual%20Reports/2024/annual_report_2025.pdf),
  printed page 24, records the crew request status counts for 2024.
  [Archived original](original-sources/nicc-2024.pdf)
- The [2025 NICC annual report](https://www.nifc.gov/sites/default/files/NICC/2-Predictive%20Services/Intelligence/Annual%20Reports/2025/annual_report_2025_1.pdf),
  printed pages 17 and 25, records preparedness days and crew request
  status counts for 2025. [Archived original](original-sources/nicc-2025.pdf)
- The [2025 NMAC operating plan](https://www.nifc.gov/sites/default/files/NICC/5-NMAC/Ops%20Plan/Current%20NMAC%20Ops%20Plan.pdf),
  printed pages 1–4, describes delegated national allocation, priority
  and retained initial response capacity. It is inspected as a dated
  plan. [Archived original](original-sources/nmac-plan.pdf)

The [NICC annual-report catalogue](https://www.nifc.gov/nicc/predictive-services/intelligence)
qualifies its statistics as tactical. The two report years share a
source family. The [observation fields](observations.json) preserve
the totals and unknowns. The reports do not provide a joined series
of safe arrival, unreported demand or effects of contested priorities.
The fixture uses independent authored times and costs. Local and
Indigenous community views, responder experience and independent
affected-party review have not been obtained for this design.
