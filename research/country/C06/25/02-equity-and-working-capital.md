# Real paid equity and real disbursement carry different risks

*SF03–SF04; part of [C06/25](../25-source-finance-and-payment-continuity.md).*

**SF03 — Equity has an identified prior asset and consenting owner.** The
conditional source reference exchanges 100 of the household sector's existing
425 sovereign-bond holdings for cash through a bounded eligible secondary-
market purchase by the monetary authority, then voluntarily subscribes that
cash for new bank common equity. The bank neither lends its own capital
subscription nor guarantees the buyer's stock value. No circular self-loan,
relabelled public guarantee, assumed new saver or compelled pension investment.

An actually authorized general monetary operation requires published eligibility,
pricing/risk bounds and operationally independent decisions under M14/M15;
Treasury cannot direct a named household purchase or a disguised gift. A
matched transaction requires binding lawful offers, safe conditional settlement,
actual ownership, delivered securities, paid subscription and final account
entries. Preserve M06 finality: a failed later step does not magically reverse
an already final trade. If the complete source cannot execute safely, do not
launch on an assumed subscription. Human/agent offers require B actual scope
and assent; shared records or AI agreement are not the owner's consent.

The reference adds 100 to monetary assets and reserve liabilities, and 100
to the bank's cash/equity, not to national net wealth or output. Household
sovereign holdings become 325, bank common equity becomes 200, and the public's
unchanged bank stake 25 is 12.5%, not 25%. Recalculate producer/bank returns
separately; ownership dilution is a real public-income/control consequence.
Actual fair share price, independent investor choice and all sources remain
unverified; book-price arithmetic cannot establish fair terms or mandate.

**SF04 — Pay the working loan before treating it as usable.** Replace the
old undrawn payroll promise with a proposed twelve-month actually disbursed
working loan. The reference stock 149.0925 equals the peak monthly market
payroll, not all firms' input, tax, import or settlement needs. The bank's
loan asset and the producer's debt both rise; the credited producer balance
is a bank obligation, not extra monetary reserves or a second public resource.
A private producer may use another lawful payment route; no lock-in clause
can excuse missing settlement liquidity or political control of wages.

Interest, total price, affordability, security, draw authority and bank risk
must be evidenced under M/V. The reference uses 6% and assigns bank cost 5
within existing market costs; neither is observed capacity or a new funded
budget. Internal producer-to-bank interest is not national output. Separate
bank/producer profit and actual equity stakes determine taxes/dividends.

Actual closing receipts must repay or lawfully refinance the working principal;
no automatic yearly renewal, debt forgiveness or wage-financing promise.
An orderly funded run-down/accepted alternate producer is required where
credit ends. Do not force unpaid work, cancel accrued wages or charge the
social floor for shareholder loss. The arithmetic does not qualify full
private cash cycles, independent borrower groups or actual credit delivery.
