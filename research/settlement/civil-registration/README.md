# Follow civil proof from a recorded event to a person who can use it

*Design195 · Evidence and cases for [VR01–VR12](../../country/C08/29-civil-registration-and-identity.md).*

## Two different failures in the evidence

UNICEF's 2024 synthesis estimates about 150 million children under five
whose births were unregistered and a further 55 million reported as
registered but without a birth certificate. The second group identifies
an independent delivery problem. Counting entries cannot show who has
usable proof. The global figures are rounded survey-based estimates.
They do not estimate local staffing, a future country's coverage or the
effect of a particular registration architecture. The Convention on the
Rights of the Child connects prompt birth registration to a child's
identity and its restoration after wrongful deprivation. The UN Legal
Identity Agenda distinguishes records and proof. None prescribes a
universal database key for this proposal.
[UNICEF estimates](https://data.unicef.org/resources/the-right-start-in-life-2024-update/)
· [Child rights convention, articles 7–8](https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/crc.pdf)
· [UN legal identity definitions](https://unstats.un.org/legal-identity-agenda)

A June 2026 audit by the U.S. Social Security Administration's Office of
Inspector General examined a random sample of 175 beneficiaries within
its identified population of incorrect death records from 2020 through
2024. For 78 the required reason for recording or removing the death was
missing or inadequate. For two, payment records were not updated to
reinstate benefits as required. These are findings in the sampled cases,
not an error rate among all death reports or a measured outcome for every
downstream institution. They motivate both a traceable correction and a
separate check of restored access. The affected individual should not
have to carry the entire correction from office to office.
[Inspector General audit, findings and scope](https://oig.ssa.gov/~assets/uploads/032311.pdf)

A separate OIG review identifies the inverse risk. Valid reports of
death can remain unposted after automated verification fails. A rejection
or mismatch needs staffed investigation and cannot be treated as a
definitive conclusion that the person is alive. Timely survivor support,
proper payment and the identity protections of living people need
distinct paths. [OIG review of rejected reports](https://oig.ssa.gov/assets/uploads/state-death-rejection-records.pdf)

## Cases that can fail even with a working registry

| Authored case | Decisions and real endpoint |
|---|---|
| Child born at home with no agreed parentage and an inaccessible official document | Accept the parent's or witness's report and preserve receipt. Check the birth separately. Record an established birth with only supported fields, give usable proof and maintain actual C07 care. MB determines nationality, B/RP determines contested parentage. A referral is incomplete until the receiving offices accept. |
| Living person linked to another person's verified death | Keep the actual death record and suspend adverse reliance on the false identity link. Give urgent offline proof and available support. Seek an unconflicted second officer's review. Notify the actual authorized payer and other affected users. Restore the due payment and access, with court execution where needed. Mark unresolved effects separately. |
| Authentic death notification with conflicting identification | Record established event facts without choosing a person by automated similarity. Preserve independent identification and statutory notice. Protect living claimants while qualified staff resolve the mismatch. Receive survivors' claims without letting an unconfirmed match distribute assets. |
| Agency refreshes its records but the copied adverse flag still blocks care | Require the downstream controller to inspect the affected case and derived flag, then provide lawful restoration or a reasoned refusal and remedy. Acknowledging the correction does not itself close the original case. |
| Two offices use the same vendor during an outage, and a resident has no phone | Use staffed offline intake and protected receipt. Find independent usable proof or record uncertainty, with urgent service under its own law. Reconcile without overwriting the first date or replaying a withdrawn credential. A copied login does not make two paths independent. |
| Person changes name while fleeing an abuser | Verify the actual declaration and offer the minimum extract. Keep necessary continuity evidence under controlled access. The former name and safe contact are unavailable through general search. Existing lawful claims and fair proof stay usable through protected review. |

## Compare the institutions, including the cost of error

| Arrangement | Benefit | Material objection |
|---|---|---|
| Repair existing local or paper registration | Retains familiar trusted places and makes immediate forms and delivery easier | Fragmented scope, destruction of sole copies and slow correction across borders and agencies |
| One accountable statutory service with local intake, scoped records and bounded proofs, selected | A public debtor has to issue and repair proof, while limiting the reach of a wrong entry or an intrusive query | Human review, offline counters and secure correction cost money. Local discretion can exclude. Interoperability and false matches need real paid testing. |
| Common national number and central real-time verification | Simpler routine matching and timely status updates where it works | A universal key and instant reliance make misuse and shared failure more consequential. Equal service and meaningful correction still need alternatives. |
| User-held attestations alone | Can limit unnecessary disclosure and support plural providers | An issuer, real offline assistance and recovery are still needed when a person has no device, sole witness or surviving credential. |

The selection provides a common accountable civil service without a common
compulsory cross-service identifier. A failed record match may need
investigation, while existing rights continue through usable alternative
proof. Actual access, funded review, safe identifiers, protected notices
and live downstream repair remain open. Publish counts of records,
usable proof and completed remedies separately.
