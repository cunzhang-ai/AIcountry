## Put the three possible dispositions on one schedule

1. **Retain and repair.** Use the current lawful payer, assisted verification,
   an actually accessible alternate when needed and an independently
   reachable remedy. Inspect safe contact, usable funds and failed or revoked
   payment authority at the original deadlines. Give this route full
   credit for existing improvements including a genuinely independent
   ordinary alternate.
2. **Release a bounded alternate only when it qualifies.** First compare
   the same awarded population, dates, rights, public and household costs
   and paid roster, including cases without safe account access. Use two
   complete cycles plus a witnessed no-harm common-provider failure
   exercise. An exact proposal needs a legal authority, named payer,
   accepting receiving route, paid independent review and term. Supplies
   and cash controls must prevent duplicate principal. Protect everyone
   eligible from overdue payment regardless of offer, consent or enrollment.
3. **Keep the next vote open if evidence is insufficient.** Publish
   known timely receipt, supported bounds for unknown receipts and
   continuing claims with protected evidence. Compare money in hand,
   safety, time, burden and receipt after remedy. A small trial can support
   a local decision only. If ordinary repair matches the verified benefit,
   keep it. If an alternate demonstrably improves receipt on the same
   safeguards and full budget, bring its exact scope to a separate vote.

Commit no incremental rollout on these public records. Before any
live comparison, independently confirm legal scope and the accepted
ordinary route, identify existing and displaced staff work, fund both
cycles and reserve the next timely correction. If no voluntary payer
participates, a protected retrospective comparison or witnessed exercise
can establish a narrower result. Keep actual participation and live
outcomes open. Neither a successful task nor this published docket
authorizes changes to the national first mandate.
