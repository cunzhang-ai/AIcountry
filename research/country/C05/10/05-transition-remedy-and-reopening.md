# TW18–TW20: workers and residents retain control during change

*Part of [C05/10](../10-work-rest-and-paid-capability.md); design90 candidate.*

**TW18 — Use exact prospective law and paid transition.** The Assembly enacts
hours, pay, leave, source, liability, coverage and commencement through ordinary
law after usable worker/employer/affected-service participation. The candidate
default is the first 1January at least 12 months after enactment for the 36-hour
stage and new pay/rest/leave/learning duties; the first 1January at least 24 months
for 32 hours. The Act states exact dates and any protected split periods.
For an Act choosing both stages, its source/delivery schedule must identify both
dated stages and the continuing joint service/retirement/old-claim/transition
burden. First-stage money or an assumed productivity gain does not fund the
second by implication. [WF49–WF52](../../C06/58/11-paid-time-joint-capacity-and-net-source.md)
clarifies joint capacity/net-source matching; no new readiness certificate,
separate second-Act prerequisite or change to the selected dates follows.
Earlier due or more protective source-law/contract rights remain; this notice
does not suspend them or automatically enact the research candidate.

Price actual public replacement posts, recruitment/learning, premises, medical
income, records, labor inspection, independent remedies and paid overlap. Show
private wage/price/closure effects and any lawful transitional support; IC
investment authority does not guarantee employers' profit. Preserve wages,
pensions, contracts, debt/title and pending disputes with lawful successors.
Keep I05/IC actual income and S/Y rights visible until received lawful replacement.
New public sickness payments and Y credits need explicit source-law matching;
an annual salary/absence reserve does not also fund another public payment.

Cabinet proposes the funded calendar; Treasury reconciles actual dates/sources;
service owners secure qualified cover; independent inspectors/courts enforce
their assigned law. No provider or Treasury readiness certificate may postpone
an enacted date. A failed source/roster is non-delivery requiring existing E19/
NP protection and a competent elected response, not an automatic leave cut,
unpaid relative, election delay or technical emergency exemption.

**TW19 — Remedies must reach the job and the due paycheck.** Existing labor
inspection, independent adjudication and C08 courts can correct underpayment,
records, retaliation, denied leave and unsafe schedules, with proportionate
evidence access, interim protection and actual enforcement. Worker assistance,
inspector replacement, source custody and execution need paid independent capacity;
an employer-controlled reviewer or a national AI score is not independence.
Do not require a full technical dossier from a worker reporting unpaid hours.
Respect ordinary proof/defense, privacy and bounded record retention.

Worker and employer organizations may propose different hours/pay/sector patterns;
unorganized workers, carers, service users and future entrants also have voice.
They cannot waive others' rights or choose the electorate. New numbers or an
inspection register do not grant governing authority. Current software,
constitutional holds, contribution permissions and research method remain.

**TW20 — Preserve the actual tradeoff and the next task.** Select 32 hours
for more scope to care, participate, associate, learn and rest, with paid
replacement and retained regular pay. The 40-hour rival can deliver more paid
coverage at the same headcount/wage bill; shorter hours can raise prices,
staffing shortages, informality or job losses. More leisure on paper need not
be usable time. Minimum pay may improve independence and can also change hiring
and classification. These are distributional choices and uncertain effects,
not conclusions settled by AI agreement or a green aggregate.

No actual affected public has chosen these terms; no qualified national labor/
care/medical-insurance study or full source package exists. Reopen on actual
time/pay/access, safety, behavior, sources, care burden, capture or stronger
alternatives. Monitoring absence leaves operation and superiority unproved.

Next finish the national material operating alternative under this time/pay
choice: independent household/space/tenure, repair and qualified replacement,
water/energy/food/trade tasks, wage/job matches, existing claims and paid
sources. The unallocated 55,000-worker remainder, fixed 2.28 bn wage bill,
assessed resource groups, nominal GVA and forecast exports are not physical
capacity. Context parameters need bounds, lawful selectors and transition;
they must not postpone every desk choice or be invented as observation.

Design116 [AS](../../C07/18-person-directed-assistance.md) now adds assistance
employer/recipient control, paid exit and prospective public ordinary-dues
backing. It retains TW's hours/pay/rest and actual worker consent. Neither a
care-route change nor refusal of home contact proves worker fault; source and
nonduplicate wage receipt remain explicit. This is not general wage insurance
or completed ordinary employment-contract/dismissal/collective-action law.

[WL](../12-work-contracts-and-collective-power.md) now supplies ordinary status,
contracts, exit and collective power, two protected work careers and direct courts.
Its180-day new-law date does not restart TW stages. New public wage advances,
paid representation/cover and inspection/ballot/aid source remain explicit costs;
WL is no automatic expansion of AS backing or change to constitutional offices.
