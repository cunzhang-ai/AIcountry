# Adverse paths: the duty is delivery with contestable public power

Authored reasoning cases, not actual participation, adjudication or operation.

| Situation | Candidate consequence; unresolved capacity is not success |
|---|---|
| Four related 40,000 CU lots with M=30,000 | Aggregate 160,000 exceeds 5M; material notice/standstill applies, not four micro exceptions. Unrelated needs are not forcibly centralized. |
| Notice exists online but a capable entrant's only usable route fails | Preserve original access evidence, correct notice/time and court relief; a portal timestamp does not establish meaningful opportunity. |
| Medicine award is challenged before contract force | Main award holds for independent interim attention; optional buying-office review cannot run out the clock. Patient need may require a separately sourced/qualified narrow temporary purchase, not validation of the challenged award. |
| No competent judge can hear the urgent motion | Protection is unmet, not deemed lawful award. Existing OJ conflict-qualified assignment/aid/reserve and paid executor sources must be supplied; no invented surge judge or powerless “external reviewer.” |
| Continuity order exists but no medicine/source/qualified receiving | The order alone does not treat a patient. Existing sector authority retains urgent protection and traceable claims; procurement cannot create stock or override the court. |
| Same executive renews a 30-day exception for the same need | Aggregate window does not reset. New finite authority/source must come from the competent elected body; incumbent tenure/elections are unaffected. |
| Award changes 8%, then another 8% | Cumulative discretionary 16% exceeds original 10% trigger; re-compete or prove a separate applicable bounded exception. Even a smaller new function can be material. |
| Bidder submits lowest price but cannot fund safe pay/inputs | Seek a supported explanation and judge prior disclosed feasibility criteria, with review; low price is neither automatic winner nor proof of fraud. |
| Three bids have one controller or a coordinated boycott produces none | No fabricated independent competition. Disclosed controller evidence and MP inquiry differ from proof of unlawful cartel; compare lawful public/cooperative/repair alternatives and source. |
| Procurement AI marks a critic high risk | No automatic disqualification/blacklist. Accountable reasons, task-relevant evidence, independent challenge and existing criminal/civil proof remain. |
| Supplier delivers accepted work, public payer delays | Pay undisputed accepted dues under source law; preserve contract/default-interest/court/execution claims. A missing appropriation is no erasure of wages or completed work. |
| Supplier's customer data/keys are controlled by its failed foreign host | Contractual transfer clause alone is insufficient. Actual safe access/title, qualified successor and foreign recognition remain necessary; do not claim a received remedy. |

These cases distinguish procurement illegality, legitimate disagreement, missing
source, absent skills and material shortage. No central AI, common vendor,
forced worker transfer, foreign sovereign compliance or actual participant
consent is inferred. Every corrected notice or changed proposal needs its own
fresh final decision; source/arithmetic checks cannot issue that decision.
