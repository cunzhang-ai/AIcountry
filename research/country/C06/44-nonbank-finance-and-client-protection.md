# Nonbank finance: protect ownership, price promises and keep losses answerable

2 November2026 · Integrated design113 · NB01–NB36 candidate, not enacted financial law

**Select plural licensed nonbank finance with actual client title, distinct
investment/insurance/pension risks, independent court-controlled failure and
separate public essential protection.** Neither a private risk product nor a
reassuring AI interface is insured money. No new public investor/insurer guarantee
is selected, and publicY/S social-insurance rights remain intact.
