# JC13–JC17. Put common failure and paid public control in the first budget

*Part of [joint continuity and first-budget commitments](../65-joint-continuity-and-first-budget.md).
Design253 research selection.*

**JC13 — Make separate service plans one lawful operating choice.** For
each material first-budget alternative, assemble one common calendar of
protected receiving and binding claims. At each useful deadline name the
duty, lawful actor and payer, restricted purpose, actual receipt of money,
necessary staff and dependencies, and what people must actually receive.
Use each underlying obligation once when consolidating local, national,
contract and review accounts. Compare real alternatives with the same
protected baseline and loss assumptions. Prior exercises may identify a
bottleneck or useful design. Their assumed margins do not become a new
available source or staffing pool merely because their conclusions are
compatible.

**JC14 — Test shared failure by dependency, not by supplier label.** Trace
the principal and fallback to their actual signing and identity roots,
source data, staff, payment rail, communications, equipment, location and
control. Verify that the alternate's receiving and review survive the same
loss, including a lawfully bounded hostile withholding case. Identify
who can interrupt access or payments and whether independent relief can
restore them in time. A different
provider using the same failed authority or operator cannot deliver that
act. A common interface can remain where it does not control both routes.
Price a simpler shared ordinary route, distinct protected alternates and
decentralized provision on the same statutory and service scope. Distinct
sources and independence are required where a common dependency could
otherwise remove practical control or due receiving. Blanket duplication
of every routine task is not a selected objective.

**JC15 — Give the elected budget a real choice of reserve and exposure.**
Use existing DB and BCV to vote the exact funded core, source restrictions,
applicable standby and any conditional additions. Distinguish ordinary
delivery, authorized response, when the reserve can be used and who
can lawfully release it. A timely change in the date of one verified
receipt can cure a cash timing gap without supplying new annual money.
Count it once and keep restrictions and any donor obligations. Recurrent
standby needs its own continuing source. Use an accepted alternate and
qualification before naming a path feasible. If no option protects the
whole due set, record each exposed duty and use existing urgency and
elected correction. A safety officer, fiscal model or court cannot
appropriate a missing source or give someone else's task a new deadline.

**JC16 — Keep lawful continuity through a lost source or delayed budget.**
Match standing payment, binding claims, operating bridge and separate
closeout authority under BLC, including actual amounts and date limits.
Funds still held do not prove payment authority. No unstated renewal,
guarantee or transfer follows from a common loss. Withhold a new optional
start when its funded continuation would compete with protected duties
in the declared case. If the obligation already binds, keep the original
claim, payer and amount in the schedule and seek lawful repair. Report
ordinary access, urgent relief, notice, payment and physical receiving
separately. Use a qualified alternate before the applicable time without
letting that office inherit authority outside its scope.

**JC17 — Verify the joined result and leave a useful successor.** Rehearse
one combined loss in the same window with actual receiving, source evidence
and independent challenge where authorized and safe. Measure which people
received which acts and by what deadline. Record uncovered services,
staff and funds, whose burden moved and the owner of each remedy.
Check the fallback's usable access and independence at both ends.
Use PCO's population and coverage checks before reporting improvement.
AI may compare schedules and identify contested or shared dependencies.
It cannot certify its own independence, choose the allocation or turn
an untested claim into a payable obligation. Keep actual deadlines,
sources, accepted actors and affected people's remedies through
political or provider succession. Reopen on changed scope, dependence,
source timing, material harm or a stronger whole rival.
