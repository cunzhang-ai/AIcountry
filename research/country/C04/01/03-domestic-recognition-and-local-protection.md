# C04. Domestic recognition and local protection

*Design401 research candidate for prospective legislation. Apply the existing
[allocation of authority](../01-national-local-authority.md) until lawful commencement.*

A product test answers a defined question about a product. A site decision answers
questions about a particular use, including risks to people who did not buy the
product. Separate those decisions so a common domestic market can coexist with
elected local control of actual local effects.

**DMR01 — Define the common scope by law.** The Assembly specifies the product
classes, covered claims, applicable test methods and versions, equivalence criteria,
qualified issuers, exclusions, transition dates and which authority bears each
cost. Use public, open-to-challenge qualification criteria and admit equivalent
methods on evidence. Decisions about tolerated harm, coverage or enforcement powers
remain subject to elected lawmaking and rights review. A private standard-setter,
procurement specification, agreement among councils or automated match cannot
extend coverage. Local public procurement may require performance for its documented
use on equal terms.

**DMR02 — Give qualified evidence portable effect.** Within the declared scope,
an authenticated and current result has the effect specified in law in every
locality without a second permission for the same product claim. The evidence
identifies the item or traceable batch, configuration, use, controls, issuer,
method and version, and limitations or current corrective orders. Any authority
requiring additional evidence must identify the unresolved factual claim. For a
new material configuration or use, verify the difference and reuse unaffected
findings where their dependencies still hold. The same rules apply to local and
incoming products. Product findings confer no personal practice entitlement,
site approval, business establishment right or right to public funding.

**DMR03 — Keep locally accountable decisions about place and use.** Receiving
authorities apply published, lawful siting, installation, operation and environmental
conditions to comparable uses regardless of origin. A stricter local measure
requires a defined legal objective, evidence of the relevant exposure or cumulative
burden, and reasons a less restrictive measure would give inadequate protection.
Disclose the expected effects, costs and review date. Give affected residents and
workers a practical route to challenge both underprotection and exclusion.
Use [CLX01–CLX06](02-cross-local-effects-and-residual-decisions.md) for material
unresolved cross-local effects, with ordinary local decisions proceeding within law.
A site officer may inspect the actual installation even when the product claim
travels, and a favorable installation finding cannot cure a product defect.

**DMR04 — Contain credible failures across borders.** Suppliers retain ordinary
safety, traceability, repair and recall duties. Origin, receiving and qualified
sector authorities exchange actionable warnings by product scope, evidence,
time and responsible contact. Under existing statutory powers, an accountable
officer can impose a reasoned, time-bounded hold on the implicated batch or use
when credible harm requires it. Give prompt notice, access to reasons consistent
with privacy, and independent interim review before an avoidable service or rights
loss. Identify unaffected units and lawful uses explicitly. Confirm warnings,
corrections and restorations at each receiving authority. Where an unresolved
risk persists, seek ordinary authorized relief before the interim order expires.

**DMR05 — Make recognition usable with competing systems.** Fund accessible
verification and dispute intake with an authenticated offline or direct-issuer
path, recorded provenance and human responsibility. Publish recognition criteria,
fees, restrictions and meaningful deadlines. Exchange the minimum facts needed
to verify a claim and its current limitations. Protect complaint sources and
confidential details under ordinary law. An inaccessible registry or a commercial
platform cannot become the only route to exercise a valid right. An outage triggers
assisted verification and, where authorized, an evidence-based bounded decision.
Missing facts stay unresolved and silence cannot establish a new permission.

**DMR06 — Assign the risk of delay.** A single named receiving contact records
the first useful filing and keeps the original clock through referrals. Provide a
reasoned scope determination, restriction or correction before the relevant
installation, market or service window, with funded expedited review and an
independent officer or court able to order actual correction. A new legal test
applies prospectively under published transition terms. Existing lawful permissions
and service duties continue under their own terms, subject to lawful safety action.
For essential public service, the duty holder must name a lawful alternative,
confirm capacity and receipt, and identify payment due before dispatch. Keep any
unserved need visible when the alternative fails.

**DMR07 — Preserve democratic control of evidence and enforcement.** Elected
bodies set scope, appropriations and delegations and can amend them prospectively.
Fund independent qualification and review outside the supplier's control.
Publish aggregated denial, repeated-test, fee, inspection and harm patterns with
safe disaggregation and a channel for residents, workers and small suppliers to
challenge omissions. An independent reviewer can inspect original evidence,
including shared testing providers and common AI dependencies. Repeated failure
triggers a reasoned elected response about capacity, narrowed scope or lawful
replacement. Individual findings and enforceable orders stay with authorized
officers and independent review.

**DMR08 — Start within verified capacity.** Before a class commences, name
qualification, verification, inspection, warning, appeal and payment owners.
Run matched local and incoming examples across different sites and concurrent
failures. Check elapsed time, total costs, supplier entry, unequal denial, actual
risk and completed remedies. Preserve inspection capacity for new hazards and
ordinary duties. If any receiving or review path remains unworkable, hold the new
scope and preserve prior lawful paths while the Assembly or competent authority
repairs it.
