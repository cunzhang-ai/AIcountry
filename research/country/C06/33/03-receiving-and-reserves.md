# National package: dependable receiving before reliance

*Canonical NP08–NP09; part of [C06/33](../33-protection-production-and-national-expansion.md).*

**NP08 — Pay for a usable residual before a provider fails.** The responsible
local/sector public authority maintains the capacity to receive nondeferrable
personal essentials that ordinary supply cannot dependably meet. Public/local
retained delivery is the reference where people cannot safely exit or wait and
no independently usable prepaid substitute exists. A regulated private,
cooperative or shared public provider may instead supply an actually accepted
capacity contract with equal access, paid readiness, records, replacement and
safe receiving. Compare its real cost, control and continuity against direct
provision. No ownership form is an automatic proof of competence/independence.

A mere provider directory, purchase promise or penalty clause is insufficient.
Specify ordinary workload, the actual reserved roles/places/stock, activation,
beneficiary access, price/retainer, maximum exposure, ordinary source, useful
time and continuing operator. Separate an unoccupied safe place from an available
competent shift and an independently usable payment/record route. Existing care
payroll and the old last-resort duty may supply an included part only after its
actual task/time is released or another duty is explicitly replaced. Count it
once; rebranding 3448 clinical or3560 care fixture FTE creates no new capacity.

Public delivery uses existing C04 merit appointments/procurement, worker rights,
local democratic control, national equalization and independent inspection.
Personal choice, clinical consent, UA/RP limits and safe nonclosed alternatives
remain. The public residual does not become a general custodial institution or
force people to accept only a government provider. A claimant can reach an
independent advocate/court without provider permission; independent legal-aid
capacity is funded separately, not managed by the accused provider's team.

**NP09 — Reserve the actual bottleneck, including renewal.** For each resource,
place and due window, derive concurrent demand and dependable included supply
under the same specified ordinary/adverse scenario. Count supply lost in that
event as unavailable; add only genuinely concurrent incremental demands. Choose
a justified resource-specific envelope across those scenarios, not the sum of
mutually exclusive disasters or a maximum that merges professions/shifts.
Its selection rule uses actual
case/shift/service observations, travel/geography, useful time, provider/control
failure, qualification and maintenance; publish uncertainty and excluded hazards.
There is no universal reserve percentage or open-ended authorized invoice.
Assembly approves exact bounded commitments and sources before reliance; future
material changes need elected budget/coverage review and professional safety.

Keep frontline, teaching, trainee, leave, repair, standby, independent review
and household participation time separate. N's paid learning, instructors,
turnover and accepted receivers cover both startup and renewal; neither a course
place nor immigration assumption supplies a worker. Voluntary time released by
an actual service/AI improvement can move only to a compatible chosen task after
verification/rework/exit burdens. Cost paid idle capacity and false alarms, not
only activation; protect ordinary service and independent review simultaneously.
A spare hour in July cannot meet a custody or election claim due tonight.
