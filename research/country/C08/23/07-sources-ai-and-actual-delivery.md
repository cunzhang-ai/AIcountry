# Necessary care: sources, future assistance and actual delivery

*Canonical UA17–UA18; part of [C08/23](../23-necessary-care-and-unknown-will.md).*

**UA17 — Preserve meaning and privacy, not an exhaustive public biography.**
Keep relevant actual communication, prior directions, observations, clinical
uncertainty, support attempts, objections, reasons, precise scope and corrections
with purpose/access/retention limits. A court's public reasoning protects intimate
records; sensitive testimony does not become permanently public training data.
Collect only what is needed and safely held. No mandatory complete family/medical
file, immersive reconstruction or continuous biometric surveillance to earn care.

Speech, sign, eye movement, translated gesture, meetings, assistive/future media
may express actual choice; investigate unfamiliar meaning instead of deleting it.
An interface's interpretation is not the person's authorization. Allow independent
correction and another usable route without forfeiting support. AI may help search,
translate and compare disclosed records, with accountable human source review;
no AI can determine unknown will, prescribe a binding care act, sign consent or
vote in place of the person. Copies share one actual assistance mandate and no
new political or bodily authority. Agents' consensus is not independent evidence.

**UA18 — Authority, skill, resources and receipt are separate.** The accountable
clinical/local care authority retains receiving responsibility through an actual
accepted qualified provider, paid shifts, safe transport/equipment, monitoring
and next handoff. Public service contracts/payments do not create the person's
private signature, new loan or unlimited bill. Existing B23 asset preservation
keeps its own grounds, advocate/custodian and time limits, without treatment power.

The [C06 chain](../../C06/31-necessary-care-and-independent-review-capacity.md)
counts communication expertise, proposing/independent clinicians, advocate/counsel,
ordinary/appeal judge, actual care/clinical shifts, housing, records and payment
once. Neither a historical gross bid, available constitutional reserve nor the
old SF protected stock automatically supplies these concurrent new duties.
Missing capacity remains non-delivery and an elected source/provision question,
not reason for coercion, unpaid family labor or declaration of a low-value life.

Every remedy names exact lawful act/source/executor, deadline, review and actual
received protection. E15 may execute an already due record/payment duty, not
supply treatment consent, choose a court or authorize a new appropriation. If
all alternates fail, continue only independently lawful essentials, expose harm/
unmet duty and seek actual remedies. An order or payment alone is not delivered care.
