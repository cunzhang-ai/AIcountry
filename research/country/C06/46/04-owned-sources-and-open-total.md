# LC13–LC16: named allocations cannot close the national source account

*Part of [C06/46](../46-life-needs-and-paid-capacity.md); design 123 reference.*

**LC13 — Select the open national account and preserve all other functions.**
Keep OP's 37-function/current-duty source and transition register. The health/care/
additional-route named direct allocation is 355.231333 m CU under the declared
nonduplicate component scopes; the mixed counterpart is 363.563493 m. Neither is
the national budget, full health/care requirement, lower bound on a complete
deficit or a proposed tax yield. Education, housing/private production, income/
pensions, democratic/justice/source/cyber/physical/ecological/foreign duties and
all current increments remain in the same open account. Unknown is not zero,
and a historic general bid is neither a free matching service nor an extra bill.

**LC14 — Capital and renewal need separate cash/source dates.** Health 120 m
and care 67 m are all-new quote stocks in this case, not actual retrofit acquisition
or G title. Show actual retained/new/replaced/acquired assets, maintenance, safe
remaining life, financing/interest, depreciation and paid renewal. A reference
renewal allocation is future funding, not already purchased replacement or
another initial asset. Care adaptations and housing renewal may overlap; retain
unknown scope rather than charge both full totals or silently drop one. Actual
household/private bills and public acquisition/subsidy keep their counterparties.

**LC15 — Join gross payments to actual owned sources.** National health finance,
local receiving/grants, private/contract provider funds and distinct restricted
S/Y/client/insurer/standby/FX remain under their own law. Gross wages/treatment/
travel/stock/advance need dated cash before tax/remittance or recovery. Do not
solve national wages/GDP/company profit, borrow restricted savings, assume
private export surrender or spend a later company assessment to make the account
balance. RF ordinary finance and SD's separately unadopted finite-issue rival
keep their own bounds; neither creates staff, medicines or foreign acceptance.
Pay HN's additions only through actual authorized sources, not simulated capacity.

**LC16 — Keep private labor and continuing claims in national feasibility.**
Private suppliers need real orders, materials, qualified paid time and working
cash; public payroll alone cannot forecast all personal tax/I05/Y or output.
Keep actual worker pay/benefits, board/learning cover, old pension units/index,
care credits, creditor/service tails, due refunds, imported inputs and accepted
foreign settlement. Retain larger actual rights, old legal due periods and
useful-time remedies. The two-year OP bridge is a cash-only example, not funding
for these allocations or evidence this larger package is real-economically feasible.
