# CL13–CL18. A portable payment must reach the actual person

**CL13 — Choose an income rule, not an unspecified insurance promise.** G's
existing social-protection authority pays100% of actual unreplaced usual gross
personal labor earnings lost for qualifying CL time, with the public annual
rate capped at independently defined Y05 full-time median W times
`min(1,combined usual covered normal hours/enacted normal week)`.
For a weekly fraction f, public maximum is that annual rate times f/52;
pay no more than actual qualified same-hour loss. Aggregate all jobs/businesses
for one person. This caps the public component, not stronger lawful employer
pay or genuinely additional top-ups. W is not the separate hourly minimum,
withdrawn numerical tax benchmark, family income or personal fiscal-worth score.
The100%/W choice prioritizes ordinary earners' usable time; its cost/distribution
and high-earner loss remain open, not evidence of an optimal replacement rate.

**CL14 — Portable proof, no short-service hole.** Use TW's13 preceding paid
weeks of usual normal earnings, excluding exceptional overtime; actual approved
absence/temporary gaps are treated fairly, not zeros invented by an employer.
New/short employment uses actual agreed ordinary pay and active lawful loss,
not a waiting period. Independent work uses evidenced personal labor earnings
after genuine business expenses/before personal tax, not turnover, passive
returns or an invented salary. Existing source limits/MB/foreign coordination
apply. No fixed contribution-tenure qualification, employer remittance failure
or missing private form cancels proved coverage; ordinary evidence/fraud law
can correct a sham arrangement with fair reply, no automatic new-parent suspicion.
Historical earnings without current actual lost work create no replacement.

**CL15 — Existing payer, due instalments and safe correction.** Claim directly
from the existing social-protection authority through accessible human/paper/
oral-assisted/remote routes. First protected receipt is not postponed until an
employer files. Determine within14calendar days, earlier where actual AP/E/I
protection requires; provisional undisputed payment needs no complete private
dossier. Pay weekly in arrears; first due instalment within seven calendar days
after both actual qualifying leave and the received claim have begun, then at
least weekly. Earlier due support and urgent subsistence advance remain.
State gross, actual withholding, usable net, period, used balance and safe
challenge. Unreceived award/recovery is no cash. Material doubts need reasons,
reply and useful-time independent review, not suspension of all undisputed need.
Same-component actual later receipts reconcile under I08/B without gross-from-net
recovery or punishment for honest error, preserving subsistence/finality law.

**CL16 — One actual replacement; preserve salary and better rights.** Where
an employer lawfully continues salary, pay the worker that due salary and use
only the actual due CL component for a lawful matched reimbursement, with actual
payment/withholding proof; no duplicate worker payment or employer windfall.
Do not deduct a merely anticipated CL award from currently due salary. A missing
employer does not cancel a direct public claim. Better salary/top-up/old rights
retain their actual debtor/source, not a new unlimited G guarantee. Public
employers show both payroll and G transfer counterparts; reimbursement creates
no independent national saving. The worker's wage/accrual claims survive unpaid
or merely promised reimbursement/recovery with actual source priority stated.

**CL17 — Tax, I05, EB and Y retain their actual meanings.** New direct CL
replacement is ordinary taxable personal replacement income under coordinated
TA law. Net actually received enters I05 E once under its existing aggregate
disregard/taper; no second allowance, household pooling, worked hours or offset
of an unreceived award. Actual tax withholding/credit discharge stays separate
from G cash receipt. Direct public replacement is not actual labor E/13 recovery-
work weeks or paid-employment leave for EB history, nor a reset of its182day
spell. For EB same-period M match only comparable gross discharged against usable
net plus actual lawful withholding/credit discharge; no gross/net mismatch.
Actual continued employment salary retains its true payroll/EB source once.

CL cash alone is not worked wages for Y's10% levy or earned units. Actual covered
salary/paid-employment earnings retain their original contribution/accrual law;
qualified Y06 needed care/illness credits assess real periods and same-period
covered earnings once, independently of CL receipt, with separate G-to-S backing.
No automatic full-wage care credit, duplicate care/illness/work unit or change
to prior pension units/stronger targets is selected. Paid leave and a pension
credit protect different losses; neither supplies missing cash, staff or consent.

**CL18 — Keep actual services and different needs.** S09/S10/NC/AS, clinical,
housing, additional needs and child/recipient rights remain. A willing carer
may use actual leave alongside complementary professional care; attendance is
not automatic evidence of no relationship/support need. Do not cancel an optional
place, charge a recipient or deduct care expenditure merely because CL is paid.
No assumed reduction of nursery/clinical/public support use finances CL. Paid
professional tasks and family support are distinct; match any actual same-task
public payment by source/purpose, without counting complementary help as fraud.
Neither a person on leave nor an unused group-care place is free qualified staff.
