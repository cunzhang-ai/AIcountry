# Original sources, shared controllers and bounded correction

15 October 2026 · Part of [country reconciliation](README.md)

The original numerical observations are authored local fixtures, not a real nation.
The new bridge pins 29 direct/transitive local dependencies, reads preserved
results and separately reconciles their income components. Its counterfactual posts
new transactions explicitly rather than rewriting old flows. Numerical invariants
verify only those declared identities; none measures safe supply or public consent.

The external accounting checks are one institutional source family:

- [OECD: value added by activity](https://www.oecd.org/en/data/indicators/value-added-by-activity.html)
  distinguishes output from intermediate consumption and industry value added.
- [OECD national-accounts questions, 18 May 2026](https://www.oecd.org/en/data/insights/data-explainers/2024/03/annual-national-accounts-frequently-asked-questions.html)
  distinguishes market-price GDP, basic-price industry value added, product taxes
  and expenditure/income approaches. It also records the SNA2025 transition;
  this bridge deliberately does not silently migrate the old fixture's concepts.
- [OECD government production costs](https://www.oecd.org/en/data/indicators/government-production-costs.html)
  distinguishes government employee and purchased-input costs. No OECD source
  endorses our quantities, tax choices, resource scaling or national institutions.

The attempted UN SNA2008 PDF retrieval failed. The basic accounting correction is
supported by the actually opened OECD material and the original local cost/flow
identities; no complete UN manual inspection or publisher-byte archive is claimed.
Nor is the simplified bank/FISIM treatment or full statistical conformity established.

Three task agents examined democracy/future collaboration, material accounting/
transition and everyday life/law. They shared the root AI/controller and original
project sources. Agreement is not independent qualified legal, economic, clinical
or affected-party review. One initial sensitivity used the wrong inherited GVA
measure and was rejected before publication; only the corrected bridge is current.
This safe reasoning correction creates no witness testimony or real court finding.

The scope covers the identified cross-country contradictions and actual inspected
topics; it does not freshly validate every current paragraph or every potential
national function. Subsequent events, valid objections, actual operator/source
changes and new context can reopen support even if hashes match. Preserve private
accounts safely when real participation later occurs; no dossier/account gate is
required to report a problem or challenge a concrete exclusion.

CP03–CP08 dates/powers, equal-claim handling and protected pool are normative
proposals. Qualified analysis, participation, legal authority, available sources,
actual delivery and better-life outcomes remain separate unknowns. Earlier model
passes retain only their unaffected complete scope; a repaired percentage never
reapproves the whole national package.
