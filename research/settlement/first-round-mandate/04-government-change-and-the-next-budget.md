# A government changes, and the country keeps working

*Design529 · Current integration of the first-round settlement and later proposals · Constructed exercise.*

Follow a government through an ordinary term, parliamentary defeat and a
successor's first budget. Assume the country has adopted the first-round
institutions lawfully. Use the post-freeze choices only in branches with
their own prior enactment and funding. Separate legal status from what the
resident, court, election authority and successor actually receive.

## Decide who can govern and pay

| Situation at the useful cutoff | Transfer and spending authority | What must arrive independently |
|---|---|---|
| Lawful Assembly, no effective investiture | The Assembly can vote ordinary or limited supply. Caretakers execute authorized standing duties. The ordinary bridge and any closeout retain their own scope and expiry. | Supply alternatives, opposition access, original service receipt and timely hearing. A failed vote cannot be booked as money. |
| First formation repeat fails and the additional recovery law had separately commenced | Electors may qualify the paired GF ballot. A lawful Assembly retains competence while a necessary result or legal transfer is pending. If the extra poll misses its fixed boundary, the ordinary election continues. | Original results on both ballots, direct review, actual lawful convening, successor credentials and an ordinary service path. A timely unresolved petition remains live after transfer. An operative stay blocks transfer. |
| An operative electoral rule or judgment leaves no Assembly legally competent to vote supply | Use prior standing authority. A pre-enacted BLC supplement can finance its eligible scope after ordinary cover ends and only where no equivalent timely source exists. Test exact ceilings and separate cash routes. | Independent confirmation of the legal event, payer's accepted execution, actual care, benefit or remedy, and the next funded call. Former members acquire no renewed mandate. |
| A lawful successor Assembly convenes and appoints a government | It can reset prospective priorities within its authority. Carry binding contracts, wages, judgments, earned claims and local protections. Apply inherited expiry and independent review schedules. | Accepted credentials and source originals, actual control at each receiving institution, an exact vote and lawful next funding. A public announcement alone changes no payable instruction. |

The [paired-ballot example](../national-formation-deadlock/01-restore-choice-without-creating-a-vacancy.md)
sets usable certification on day 167 and handover on day 185. The ordinary filing
period runs to day 197. A timely petition without a stay can remain live while
the successor convenes. A stay through day 195 instead defeats the extra handover
deadline of day 190. That stay of transfer alone leaves a separately lawful
Assembly able to vote. An operative annulment that actually removes every
statutory basis for legislative competence produces the distinct third branch.
If the new Assembly has already taken office, later lawful annulment applies
prospectively under ER17, with valid completed acts and individual remedies
treated on their own authority. The previous Assembly does not revive.

## Test whether the election can change policy in practice

After common funded duties, assume the elected successor controls eight
eligible units in each of two windows. Independent court, electoral, care and
benefit funding is outside those units and performs on both dates. Both local
operators can keep the same people served on time in the ordinary branch.
Existing elected local price relief remains on its valid terms and separate
source. The old cabinet used a mixed provider with optional shared AI support.
The support term ends under its ordinary contract. Its independently funded
court and service paths remain usable during retirement or replacement.

| Exact successor motion | First / second window | Resident effect and displaced choice |
|---|---|---|
| Continue mixed delivery and consider renewing AI assistance | Existing provider 4 / 4. Optional qualified evaluation 2 / 2. Total with evaluation 6 / 6. | Delivers the same standing work. Two units each window remain for a separately lawful decision. No assumed AI saving enters the budget. |
| Fund the qualified locally staffed successor without the outgoing optional tool | First call 1 exit + 2 paid overlap + 4 ordinary delivery. Second call 4 ordinary delivery. Total 7 / 4. | Enables the elected change if original records, qualified workers and actual receiving are accepted in time. Costs an extra unit now and leaves two more next time relative to the first motion with evaluation. |
| Preserve standing work and wait for qualified evidence | Existing provider 4 / 4. An evidence and reservation motion requires its own priced option, useful deadline and later accepting route. | Useful when an ownership or delivery change is not yet executable. Report lost service improvements and opportunities if the reservation buys no real future choice. |

On these initial assumptions the first two motions are feasible. Only the
successor's exact competent vote chooses between them. If an independently
verified inherited debt of two falls due in the first window on this same
eligible source, the first costs eight and the second nine. The outgoing act
and governing contract must actually support the debt. If it binds, preserve
it and the ordinary route. The incoming government can choose ordinary repair,
hold optional evaluation, or return with a separate scope and funded date.
It cannot count a pending bank transfer or seize an independent court line.
If instead the predecessor withholds original service records, test the existing
independently controlled copies and lawful order execution. Hold only the
unqualified transfer where the originals or accepted alternative remain absent.

Write down the change voters cannot obtain on the first date, why, which duties
still reach people and the next feasible elected choice. Do the same in the
forward budget before approving any discretionary outgoing commitment that may
foreclose a credible alternative. Compare real retained costs, termination
rights, exit and unpaid household work under existing GP, HT and budget law.
An inflexible but valid inherited contract retains its force. Funds remaining
under either motion create no general right to a particular level of headroom.
[The joined care comparison](09-a-successor-cannot-spend-a-roster.md) extends
this budget to a prospective decision where distinct commitments can remove
the available route even while cash remains.
