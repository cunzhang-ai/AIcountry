# LR21–LR24: pay for democratic judgment and keep the architecture rejectable

*Part of [C03/14](../14-local-law-and-public-control.md); design112 candidate.*

**LR21 — Do not classify a work queue into consent.** Council's clerk publishes
its annual forward rule/budget/election/casework/legacy/petition docket with actual
member prep/sitting, qualified legal/source/translation/record staff, affected
people's paid time/access/care, alternate venue/provider, source-law checks, courts
and execution. Count the same people/source once across simultaneous tasks;
collecting a public report is work, not free AI output. No expected fine, tax not
yet collected, protected client/S/FX property or unaccepted volunteer pays it.

Pre-enacted parameter functions can reduce repeated rulemaking only inside LR04,
not by calling changed learned weights a fact. Batching related source hearings
preserves each distinct burden/motion. Actual backlog can justify funding/time,
fewer rules, simpler direct floors or a prospective different delegation, not an
operative update by silence or a late rights/participation cut. A duty to list/
consider does not prove enough lawful meetings occur. The [authored probes](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-local-law-and-public-control/README.md)
include full-n/timing/NF, urgency/reset, semantic version and workload failure;
no actual capacity, safe receiver or net national incremental price is established.

**LR22 — A real alternative can displace this selection.** Copying national DR's
routine notice/one-member call-in reduces mandatory votes but retains a contested
routine/material boundary and burdens a small opposition. Broad local executive
discretion can respond quickly but moves burdens before review. Full council
control selected here removes that ordinary rule-by-silence path at substantial
meeting/preparation cost and status-quo delay. Direct statutory floors/fewer
binding rules/plural voluntary practice may protect life with less process but
can leave private gatekeepers dominant. Local assemblies/referenda can give
direct public voice with their own membership, agenda, source, time and minority
protections; a petition or AI-community poll cannot secretly adopt that rival.
No invented clock or passed fixture proves LR superior; reject or change it on
whole effects, including informal cooperation, participation fatigue and power.

**LR23 — Actual prospective adoption, not a self-waiver.** Competent coordinated
national Local Government/sector/B/E/C04/NF legislation establishes LR at180days
after enactment; no readiness certificate moves this date or an actual election.
C14/RR protected succession bars incumbent-benefiting election/term/review changes
from this label. Before commencement inventory every actual old source/version/
sunset, proposal/case, funds/worker/provider, records/license and lawful paid
successor. New operative rules/amendments after commencement use LR; old unmade
drafts need final L and missing input. Existing valid rules/claims retain source,
unlawful legacy is not cured. National DR/MC own-source routes remain national.

EL11 is expressly supplemented: full-n for all externally binding local general
rules, ordinary internal voting otherwise unchanged. NF19 full-n/120/30/14/60
stays with the joined LR final window; no deadline extension or hidden new tax.
LR procedure itself can be replaced only by its competent higher-law route, not
a municipal standing order declaring an exception or its own AI review. Actual
software/constitution/contribution permissions remain. Preserve predecessor and
new source/version/effect/migration in safe records; no vote/review silently
transfers to changed consumers or declares current country law already amended.

**LR24 — Report five different facts and a bounded next question.** Evidence:
qualified original effects or just authored example. Research: retain/reject/
revise LR. Authority: actual public and legal adoption. Readiness: paid independent
people/source/received useful-time remedies. Outcomes: observed agency/safety/
wellbeing and peaceful control, not speed or filing volume. They are independent;
currently only scoped root research/probes are supplied, not affected consent.
Keep unknown semantics/participation, source limitations and dissent visible;
do not gather sensitive testimony without safe purpose/custody just to fill a map.

Reopen on shadow criteria/secret software, unsafe expiry or legacy harm, exclusion,
captured clerk/payer/court, repeated harmful delay, impossible simultaneous work
or a stronger whole rival. Without monitoring, disclose uncertainty/preserve
claims, not presumed public approval. Next design the nonbank financial perimeter:
pension/investment/client-money/payment/insurance activities, custody, prudential
and conduct responsibility, loss/payment/exit and real consumer correction. Existing
bank/MC rules do not automatically supply that technical law. The full producer/
source/credit/VAT/price/FX/legacy account remains; urgent actual harm/larger
country contradictions can reprioritize. More local detail is not the default.
