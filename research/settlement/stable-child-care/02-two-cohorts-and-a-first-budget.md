# Two siblings and one eighteenth birthday

[Stable child care](README.md) · Authored comparison

The protected interval starts on day 0. Two siblings need urgent
care that night. Assume the lawful B31 grounds and actual first
hearing are established separately. Both children want to stay
together and that is assessed safe. Their district lists two nominal
vacancies in separate homes. One is staffed and neither is a
qualified paired placement. Leaving them at home is unsafe on
the declared facts. Independently qualified safe family help
would change the comparison.

A neighbouring district accepts the pair from day 0 through day 2
for 3 budget units, including safe transport, receiving and shared
school support in the declared scope. Its same paired place has a
protected prior commitment starting day 2. Extending this visit
would displace that duty. The sending service remains the payer and
keeps the original dates. The time intervals below include the start
day and exclude the end day.

Ada, already safely placed in the sending district, turns 18 on
day 2. She and her carer freely agree to continue for 14 days, at
an incremental 5 units including adult support. The adult and child
safeguarding requirements are met. A separately funded tenancy and
adult successor are ready and accepted on day 16. At age 18 her
continuing care is voluntary. Her old place, if vacated, is one
place and would still fail the two-child receiving requirement.
No other qualified adult option is available before day 16 in this
case. Ada may refuse continuation; that changes the required response.

| Day 2 to day 16 route for the siblings | Added units | Receiving |
|---|---:|---|
| Use the listed vacancies or vacate Ada's place | 0 | No qualified paired receiving. |
| Keep the neighbouring paired place | Unavailable | Displaces the host's previously protected duty. Excluded as a valid option. |
| Fund a local paired placement and reserved staff | 8 | Qualified paired receiving, safe contact, school and clinical access. |
| Fund an independently controlled out-of-area pair | 9 | Qualified paired receiving with school transport, contact and clinical access paid and accepted. The journey is longer. |
| Return home with paid support if fresh evidence changes the court finding | 4 in a separate scenario | Usable only on the separately assumed lawful safety finding. |

All figures use the same 16-day window. Continuing placement before
day 2, original ordinary budget and the lawful day-16 successors
are assumed funded. The comparison requires the 3-unit bridge and
5-unit continuation for both cohorts. The local package costs
3 + 8 + 5 = 16. The qualified distant rival costs 17. At 16
select the local package. At 15 neither complete package is funded.
This exposes both sets of claims for immediate lawful receiving,
elected correction and independent review. It establishes no priority
between a child and a young adult.

If the local route is known unavailable before committing the 8,
the independent route needs one additional unit above the 16-unit
budget. If the 8 has already been paid and is unrecoverable in the
useful window when the local operator fails, replacing it raises
the payable total to 25. An invoice dispute or possible later refund
cannot fund the immediate replacement. Pay for the alternative's
actual availability before counting it as a backup. If fresh
evidence establishes the safe family plan, the package can cost 12
and the case no longer justifies an out-of-home route after day 2.

The package neither verifies an annual national source nor makes
a clinical, custody or housing disposition. Check the person who
receives each child, original hearing time, actual transport,
unavailable staff and safe service past day 16 independently.
