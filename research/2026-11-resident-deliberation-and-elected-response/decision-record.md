# Design278. Resident deliberation and elected response

10 November 2026 · Research selection · No actual enactment

## Baseline and decision

[Design277](baseline.json) leaves paid resident panels at a general promise in
C03 and separately protects qualified public initiatives. Apply the
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md)
to choose whether and how a panel can put a useful alternative before elected
representatives while there is still a choice. Codex assisted the research
and drafting in this workspace. No panel, independent reviewer or public
authority took part in the selection.

## Diagnosis and rivals

An ordinary consultation may fail because the decision is already fixed,
officials did not encounter lived constraints, or representatives can ignore
an answer without a vote. More submissions cannot solve those failures
alone. Affected residents need usable time, independent evidence and control
of their account. Organized minorities need a way to put disagreement on
the record. Elected representatives need room to choose and responsibility
for doing so.

- **Retain an ordinary hearing with funded affected access.** Use it when
  an ordinary elected or sector route can uncover the evidence and test live
  alternatives without placing a larger burden on residents. It may better
  accommodate urgent or highly local questions. It has fewer opportunities
  for residents to develop and test their own alternative over time.
- **Select one paid temporary panel as a bounded experiment.** Reserve a real
  Assembly choice before inviting anyone, give the panel drafting support,
  and require exact recorded disposition. Compare both procedures at the
  same decision boundary and preserve existing hearings and due services.
- **Give agenda control to a permanent randomly selected body.** A standing
  agenda can sustain attention beyond a legislative cycle and resist
  incumbent topic selection. It also needs continuing funded selection,
  information and review, and a defensible relationship to equal electoral
  standing. This reference retains elected control of the agenda and tests
  resident-authored alternatives before adding a permanent body. Reopen if
  useful proposals repeatedly fail to get a commissioning vote.

## Capture, qualification and continuity

The Assembly authorizes the question and money, its service runs the draw
and facilitation contract, and elected members decide in public. Publish
declines and framing objections, separate original evidence and facilitators,
and fund a substitute and independent timely review. A majority can still
turn down a commission, the Assembly service can still be captured and a
reviewer can lack time or capacity. Use independent NI access where its
criteria are met, keep opposition agenda rights and record actual failure.
The selected design does not guarantee topic access or repair. Test the
joint appointment, procurement, information and funding chain in context.

[Observed evidence](sources.md) qualifies a bounded historical process and
published status. It does not establish a causal effect or validate a
design parameter. The [authored case](../settlement/resident-deliberation/02-authored-panel-and-disposition.md)
checks different coverage, calendar, appropriation and delivery results.
One panel per legislative year and the two time limits are initial
normative choices. Actual affected participation, independent assessment,
legal authority, paid receiving and changes in daily life remain open.

Prospective law and an appropriation would be needed before opening a case.
Specify a usable reviewer, backup custodian, accessibility staff and
reserved Assembly time. Carry all existing deadlines, accrued compensation,
protected testimony, objections, and uncompleted decisions and remedies.
Do not convert earlier consultations or initiative assent into panel
support. Preserve original meanings across media and require fresh review
of materially changed proposals.

## Next bounded action

If a real decision remains open, compare a paid hearing and a temporary
panel on that same question before reserving the slot. The Assembly service
would need to show the same eligible costs and calendar, likely coverage,
participant burden, and whether residents could surface a different feasible
choice. Actual elected authority must set and fund the maximum before opening.
If the cheaper hearing obtains the same useful information and response,
retain it. Reopen on systematic topic refusal, material exclusion, changed
decision text, missed deadline or an unfulfilled funded commitment.
