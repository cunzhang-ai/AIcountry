# Design486 decision record

**Scope and baseline.** Use Design485 and the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md),
with [baseline hashes](baseline.json). Test whether a shared defect
can be corrected before personally significant deadlines while case
decisions and independent review remain effective. The three residents,
staff blocks and costs are authored.

**Comparison and selection.** Separate sector verification preserves
distinct contexts but may waste scarce review time. Reuse a qualified
factual verification when the material premise, scope and date match,
and budget each independent case decision and remedy. A blanket action
requires its own lawful scope and original evidence. Prefer separate
checks if evidence, custodian, meaning or challenge differs materially.
A common controller can negate the shared route's advantage.

**Authority and continuity.** Preserve original filing and due events,
direct reporting outside the recipient list and protected source custody.
Each competent office owns claims and funded execution. Separate the
elected correction of a proposed policy or source from administrative
or court action under prior law. Preserve unresolved losses and the
ordinary remedy even after a later correction arrives.

**Evidence and reopening.** The [case](../settlement/shared-fiscal-error-and-remedy/01-one-error-three-deadlines.md)
tests matched conditions and failures. [Sources](sources.md) identify
the legal design and external practice limits. Reopen on new affected
people, authority, displaced capacity or a failed receiver. The next
bounded choice is whether to extend a verified correction to other
people through targeted proactive notice or a legally scoped batch,
while protecting unheard cases, deadlines, privacy and review.
Actual participation, enactment, capacity and welfare remain unverified.
