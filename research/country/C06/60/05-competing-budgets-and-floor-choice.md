# BCV01–BCV05. Competing budgets and the right to a decision

*Part of [democratic budgeting](../60-democratic-budget-and-execution.md).
Design243 research selection.*

**BCV01 — Make alternatives comparable and amendable.** An exact whole
alternative identifies the statutory baseline, provisions it replaces, which
appropriation and other source instruments are required, uses of continuing
and new money, fiscal ceilings, and the timing of actual payments and
delivery. Reconcile shared cash, staff and equipment once. A common table
shows protected dues, incidence, unmet needs, material unknowns and
transition effects on the same dates. Any member retains DB02–DB03 access
to submit, receive help and present or contest an alternative. A lawful
proposal can enter despite a disputed estimate, with the dispute visible.
An analyst, minister or software supplier cannot certify it into or out of
political consideration. A documented source or staffing gap remains a
gap even when members choose to vote.

**BCV02 — Keep linked finance together and separate unrelated laws.** Put
mutually dependent tax, debt and spending changes in the same comparison.
Where a companion change needs a separate legislative act, name its exact
text and competent procedure and make the affected spending conditional
on the operative authority. An appropriation vote cannot waive another
vote or give access to restricted cash. An independent policy that is
unnecessary to the proposed finances or delivery goes through its ordinary
legislative procedure and cannot be made a condition of funding standing
duties. An amendment to divide, combine or remove provisions identifies the
resulting whole text and shows the residual money, work and duties.
Disclose material objections to the division. The Assembly can resolve
disputed procedural scope on a recorded motion under its existing rules,
with expedited review of binding duties within actual court jurisdiction.

**BCV03 — Secure floor access before the government can force a binary choice.**
Use the ordinary DB03 notice and submission clocks. Each actual member
can designate one timely exact whole proposal for the protected voting
sequence, including one introduced by the Cabinet. Designations remain
changeable until a published lock at five calendar days before the
announced vote. A package with designations from at least ceil(N/10)
distinct members qualifies, where N is the full statutory Assembly.
A designation reserves time and carries no assent. At most ten packages
can qualify through this route. The Cabinet has no separate priority.
Members who cannot meet the gate retain DB03 hearing, amendment and ordinary
floor rights. Record why a proposal is outside the protected sequence and
provide a timely challenge to the count, scope or version. Publish the list,
exact texts and voting method before voting. A material substitution needs
fresh affected scrutiny and designation. Changes to a common baseline
reopen every materially affected comparison. A missing analysis is not itself
a material substitution. No chair may bring the sitting forward to displace
a timely proposal. Existing urgency rules retain their own conditions.

**BCV04 — Establish a public order, then approve an exact law.** In the
same scheduled voting sitting, members cast a recorded indicative yes,
no or abstention on each protected alternative, with indications collected
together before announcing any tally. Publish member positions and tally
against each exact version. Schedule the exact final votes in descending
order of affirmative indications, using a prior-law publicly witnessed
draw solely to order tied packages. Include zero-indication packages
if qualified. Ordinary lawful amendments can be considered before the
sequence. A materially revised final text must be rescrutinised and
cannot borrow an indication, designation or vote from its predecessor.
If all affected procedures cannot be completed in time, defer the affected
text and retain unaffected eligible alternatives. Reasons, open objections
and revised timings remain public.

At final disposition apply the DB04 full-Assembly majority and presence
rule to each exact alternative in order, stopping only when one passes
or the list is exhausted. Indicative positions are no approvals and
members can change their vote. A failed final vote advances the next
eligible alternative under the same agenda. A missing quorum is an
unheld vote. Retain the pending order for a lawful resumed sitting and
report the delay. Record any proposal displaced
by a successful incompatible vote. Members remain free to pursue its
severable parts later under ordinary law. If none passes, publish the
non-decision and its consequences. BLC protects the earlier lawful
continuity routes. No indication, draw, withheld vote or Speaker action
can authorize expenditure, dismiss a cabinet or extend office.

**BCV05 — Keep the procedure usable under obstruction and handover.** The
Assembly's protected administration maintains independently accessible
proposal versions, sponsors and authenticated vote records. Fund ordinary
staff, a lawful alternate presider and equivalent paper or accessible
recording at the same sitting. Correct a proved omitted proposal, tally
or version through the Assembly's procedural route and independent review
within existing powers. Restore the exact vote before adoption where
legally possible. A court can enforce a specific access or record duty,
with remedies for a defective enacted instrument under ordinary jurisdiction.
It cannot choose an alternative or record votes members did not cast.
Document unavailable time, presider or custody and any limits on correction.
During a transition keep original dues, held authority, open challenges and
the lawful clock. Reopen the gate and ordering method if they systematically
exclude small groups, favor tactical cloning or exceed paid scrutiny capacity.
