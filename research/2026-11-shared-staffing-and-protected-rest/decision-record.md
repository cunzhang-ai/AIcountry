# Design409 decision. Match shared paid staffing to actual people and rest

## Baseline and affected scope

This AI-assisted selection uses [Design408](predecessor-design-408/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md),
the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).
TW02–TW05 already counts actual paid work and rest per person across jobs.
WF85–WF89 and JC23–JC27 already protect origin services and limit a shared
reserve. The missing operating choice is how independent providers can
verify compatible shifts and confirm who actually accepted without a
universal employment record or an unpaid reservation of workers. Workers
with multiple jobs, service users, families, providers and public payers
are affected.

## Rival arrangements and selection

Separate provider staffing with dedicated paid relief reduces coordination
and shared failure, while it can consume scarce skills or money and leave
shortages at small providers. A central labor exchange could reconcile
every schedule and expose conflicts, while concentrating evidence, allocation
and bargaining power. A shared qualified reserve using a worker-controlled
availability route and limited accepted assignments can reduce duplication
where paid administration, privacy and receiving work in time. Select scoped
verification and explicit acceptance for any such reserve. Retain ordinary
provider-owned relief and better work design as alternatives, and apply the
same time, rest and consent constraints in all options.

Private evidence may be incomplete, workforce brokers can make implied holds
effectively compulsory, and strict limits can expose services that previously
ran on unsafe work. Retain confidential verification, paid reserves and
independent remedies. Report any exposed public duty rather than treating
overwork or a family member as available capacity. No independent reviewer
or affected participant was secured for the selection.

## Authority, money and migration

Elected bodies set public service funding, work protections and eligible
use of reserves. The worker retains lawful control of optional commitments.
The employer retains pay and safety, and the receiving provider verifies
real service. Labor inspection and courts can access necessary lawful
evidence and issue remedies. Existing duty officers allocate any residual
scarcity on their prior authority. Test staff, payment and evidence
independence, worker organization, and remedies through changes of operator.

Ordinary duties, pay, existing contracts, stronger sector rules, actual
reservations and review clocks retain their sources. No financial budget,
emergency label or model authorizes a shift. Carry lawful records and paid
handover to named successors. AI may compare compatibilities without
authority to assign work, infer agreement or decide a complaint.

## Evidence and continuation

[Primary comparators](sources.md) inform the legal and operational risks.
The [constructed case](../settlement/shared-staffing-and-protected-rest/01-one-worker-two-service-rosters.md)
checks a specific daily work window, overlapping and adjacent assignments,
and paid independent standby. [SSR01–SSR08](../country/C05/10/08-shared-staffing-and-protected-rest.md)
records the proposed choice. Actual adoption, paid capacity, worker
participation, safe service and observed outcomes remain unverified.

A competent labor inspector and two willing providers could next compare a
lawfully inspectable shared-roster interval against separate reserve staffing.
Record compatible time, accepted work, paid standby, direct service receipt,
worker-reported burdens and actual correction. Budget and protected access
are unresolved. Reopen on repeat double booking, gatekeeper control, rising
unpaid time, exclusion or an equally protective simpler arrangement.
