# Fund dated common duties without changing the grant formula

Keep NF24's twelve first-day monthly grant advances and NF22's actual 80/20 site
receipt ownership/five-working-day distribution. Add local representative pay to
the named common teacher-duty scope, with actual local payroll and contracted
purchases distinct. Annual N+.25 C can still miss January payroll before collected
site cash arrives. The reference retains that failure without an added source.

Compare these genuine arrangements:

| Arrangement | Benefit and cost |
|---|---|
| Existing lawful local opening reserves/no new credit | Simplest if actual usable funds exist; the zero-opening fixture has none, and restricted/private assets are not automatically available |
| Bounded voluntary working advance | Small temporary source, actual public receivable and local debt; requires national/local lawful approval, paid source, fair access and safe recovery |
| Frontload the existing annual grant | Avoids new local debt but changes NF24 timing and moves more G cash earlier; needs explicit prospective law/source and preserves common duties |
| National direct payroll | Can remove this local payment interface but transfers control/employer responsibilities; must remove matching local costs/grants and compare local self-government |

**Select a candidate bounded local working-capital schedule for this context,
not an automatic loan power.** An express competent Act and appropriation must
set the source, fixed annual/per-local exposure, permitted standard common duties,
zero-interest terms, loss/administration treatment and review before payment.
The local elected council must lawfully accept the exact debt terms. The existing
Treasury career payer executes; no new bank, ministry veto, tax or standing bailout.
An unaccepted proposal or model check supplies no authority or credit.
The reference cap covers **gross annual disbursements**, not a revolving limit
replenished by repayment. New draws end 31 December 2030 in this schedule; no
automatic rollover. Original receivables/claims and common duties continue.

The existing C04 grant-calculation officer prepares source-grounded proposed
amounts from Revenue/EV records; local elected approval and the competent
national Act/budget remain separate. The separate career Treasury payer sends
lawfully available money; local finance/payroll records actual acceptance and
payment. Neither lender nor debtor finally judges its own dispute. Councils and
people with concrete affected claims can use unconflicted ordinary B/OJ courts,
existing appeal and earlier useful-time relief for unlawful denial, source/cap,
records or recovery. Tax-assessment disputes keep their distinct TX route.
Existing lawful Treasury/local transfer and B19 public execution carry orders
within their adopted scope; no court creates an appropriation, extra loan or
unavailable money. AA audits legality/source, not local political loyalty.
Publish safe reasons, fixed limits, actual arrears and receipts, with protected
personal source access. If source or execution fails, retain the claim/national
receiving duty and disclose failure; a record is not a delivered wage.

Use only the evidenced peak dated shortage, not optional overspending, as the
research sizing input. The four authored advances are 45,000/85,000/150,000/180,000 CU:

The fixed cap requires a new authorized decision if scope changes; the model
cannot enlarge it. Actual G cash must exist before transfer. Source shortage
preserves national common-duty/sector receiving responsibilities, never user fees,
political conditions, forced local borrowing or an election delay.

Recovery needs actually received owned local cash and a fresh check of common
obligations and protections; forecast receipts, held taxes or a future grant are
not repayment. In this conditional holiday-free calendar, 6 February 2030 is the
first safe model recovery date, with equal principal returned once. A new hold,
late receipt or newly due protection can invalidate it; retain the receivable/
loss and competent review instead of automatic collection that defeats the floor.
There is no secret overdraft, general national guarantee or creditor priming.
Voluntary refusal leaves ordinary national protection duties, not abandonment.

No-advance local failures and the proposed advance both remain inspectable.
With the advance, this fixture removes the local timing gap only **if** G funds
arrive; the required G path itself fails in January. Do not claim payroll delivered.
Principal loss exposure is bounded by 460,000 in this case; source/opportunity cost,
administration, later guarantees and actual recovery remain unqualified additions.
Do not use “not expense” to imply costless public risk.
