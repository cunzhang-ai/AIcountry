# National package: a dated duty to choose and prepare

*Canonical NP14–NP18; part of [C06/33](../33-protection-production-and-national-expansion.md).*

**NP14 — Schedule the whole selected bundle, not only S/Y.** A legitimate
adopting authority sets exact separate dates/cohorts for new I income/tax/insurance,
S services, Y coverage/accrual, capacity/continuity payments, necessary records,
legal aid/courts and actual execution. Include CJ/MB/UA/RR/FA dependencies where
used; their own substantive/constitutional boundaries remain. No general common
launch date or safe-world assumption is inferred. A new coercive/licensing power
cannot operate before its required independent safeguards; an uncommenced new
power is not permission to suspend an already due right. Contributions and
corresponding accrual/credits start coherently under Y; no collect-now/rights-later.

**NP15 — The public gets an actual improvement decision.** The proposed enabling
Act funds accountable preparation and requires Cabinet's first whole-package
alternatives within 120 calendar days of its commencement. Assembly records a
public disposition by day 180: adopt a specified repair/expansion programme,
require a stated revision with a date, or expressly retain the baseline with
reasons, affected incidence and the next annual review. No-expansion is a valid
contestable political choice; no answer or expert report is not a disposition.
Neither deadline creates a benefit, tax, appropriation or favorable vote.

Provide a protected opposition listing route under existing C03 competence when
Cabinet withholds the proposal; record material inability, ordinary replacements
and safe original evidence. Courts may enforce due publication/access/listing
within actual powers, never vote for members or deem adoption. Caretaker,
confidence and election rules remain. At least annually, publish updated full
alternatives in time for meaningful budget scrutiny and record disposition
before approving the next ordinary annual budget. This does not grant a new
veto over lawful emergency/continuity spending or extend a parliamentary term.
Breach alone neither prevents nor invalidates an otherwise lawful ordinary
budget; the publication/listing/reasons duty and remedy continue separately.
Actual constitutional/budget deadlines prevail; missed duties remain remediable.

**NP16 — Preparation is a funded deliverable.** An adopted programme states
appropriation/source, paid people/instructors, purchase/build/repair decisions,
interim access, milestones, acceptance, ordinary service during overlap and
cancellation/replacement/bridge obligations. Supply plans name competent receiving
sites and contracted sources; independent safety/clinical acceptance is distinct
from elected authority. Compare the same preparation, completion, full operating
and replacement horizon for each model, including what falls beyond an arbitrary
study window. No growth forecast, paper vacancy or unreceived grant is payment.

**NP17 — Dates are choices; delivery is a fact.** Existing/accrued and already
overdue source-law claims keep their dates/remedies. Proposed additions are not
yet due merely because research selects them. Enacted future rights take effect
on their lawful dates; missed dates are breaches, not floating readiness holds.
An engineer/clinician cannot certify unsafe supply, and Treasury cannot postpone
a right. E/T duties require actual interim receiving, truthful notice and lawful
correction; a subsequent delivery does not erase earlier harm. Future revision
needs competent prospective law preserving accrued claims and actual successors.

**NP18 — No unpriced migration or authority transfer.** Map duties, contracts,
wages, earned credits, pension liabilities, safe records, pending cases/orders,
claim dates, borrower/creditor/currency exposure and restricted assets from each
predecessor to a named competent receiver. Included existing tasks are replaced
once, not added to their own payroll; displaced tasks/people get an explicit
lawful path. Actual controlled-source review, participant consent where relevant,
independent judgment and delivery require fresh assessment. New procedural rules,
a new court or this programme cannot approve their own creation. Actual software,
canonical law, contribution permissions and constitutional holds stay unchanged
until authorized migration; research authors appoint, tax and commandeer nobody.

[DB ordinary calendar](../60/01-proposal-members-and-budget-choice.md) joins
this annual improvement duty to the proposed150/120-day preparation timetable,
without changing NP15 non-invalidation, actual120/180 preparation/disposition
clocks, due services/holds or source-law commencement. No readiness veto.
