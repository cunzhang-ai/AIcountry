# C05. Automation, income and economic power

*Part of [C05 economy and work](../C05-economy-and-work.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

AI may complement workers, displace tasks, lower prices, concentrate profits or
fail to improve actual production. Exposure scores do not say which path a
country will take. The settlement therefore keeps essential services, basic
income protection and political standing independent of a current employer,
while retaining a plural productive economy and worker voice. It does not
require people to prove that their work remains valuable to receive rights.

## One distribution and capacity account

For a material automation transition, C06's common account joins changes in
paid hours, wages and job quality; household income and unpaid care; corporate
profit, capital gains, land and consumption bases; contribution receipts and
accrued pension and care-leave promises; prices and essential-service demand;
qualified service workers; compute, energy, water, imports and local effects.
It separates observed changes from modelled exposure and private forecasts.
The same claimed productivity gain cannot appear as lower prices, higher wages,
more public revenue and a new dividend without accounting for who receives it.
C11 preserves source definitions and firm-level confidentiality where lawful;
a public aggregate cannot erase excluded workers or a loss concentrated in
one town, age group or occupation.

If earned income or contribution receipts fall, lawful essential access,
needs-based income protection, basic old-age payment and accrued earnings
benefits continue under their own rules. C06's shortfall route must expose
cash and delivery gaps and bring a funded choice to the Assembly. It cannot
reduce a due payment, change eligibility or assume unpaid family care by
calling displaced work an AI transition. New insurance accrual and future
contribution rules can change only prospectively through law with affected
people's participation and fair treatment of existing claims.

## Share capability without making a single owner sovereign

Workers have information, consultation and bargaining over consequential
workplace automation, safety, task quality, paid transition and time. People
outside employment retain civic voice, access to services and the income
floor; a union cannot alone authorize a national distribution choice. Firms
and cooperatives may invest at ordinary risk within labor, competition,
consumer and ecological law. The state may fund open public research,
interoperable infrastructure and bounded public equity or enterprises when
an enacted mandate, cost and usable alternative justify them. Publicly funded
assets name any actual public return and license conditions in advance; an
imagined future dividend cannot finance today's entitlement.

Competition and procurement scrutiny follow common control across model,
compute, cloud, data, distribution, payments and public-service interfaces,
including dependence through contracts. Lawful remedies may include open
access, switching, interoperability, collective bargaining, public options or
structural separation, with challenge and a continuity plan. No ministerial
platform monopoly is presumed safer than a private one. A provider cannot
buy political reach with control of jobs, essential software and the sole
appeal route; C03's electoral and civic safeguards still apply.

The revenue design taxes lawful economic substance through its broad income,
profit, land, consumption-with-relief and resource bases. It checks labor-to-
capital relabelling, related-party shifting, tax concessions favoring one
technology and actual collection across borders. A special charge on an
undefined “AI job replacement” is not the default; nor is tax exemption for
AI capital. Any change in bases or rates requires elected law, a distribution
and production account, taxpayer remedies and treaty compatibility. A public
asset return, robot levy or universal cash dividend is counted only when its
funding and incidence can be shown.

[KR public knowledge](../C11/07-knowledge-rights-and-innovation.md) specifies
creator/worker terms, grant/prize/purchase distinctions, public foreground
permissions and paid essential continuity under N/IC. Hypothetical patent value
or future royalties remain no current public source.

## Elected response to a structural shift

[AT01–AT12](18-technology-transition-and-shared-gains.md) specifies
quarterly reporting and material or urgent triggers for income loss,
due protection and essential control. Opposition can challenge a
suppressed trigger using original records. A
qualifying change calls for a public joint assessment by the labor, revenue,
social-protection, competition and service authorities, using C11's protected
sources. Opposition and affected workers, nonworkers, localities, consumers
and firms can challenge the diagnosis. The Assembly compares coherent
packages: repair of existing protection and tax collection; broader general-
revenue support; negotiated time and ownership changes; a justified public
option; and an unconditional cash component where feasible. It publishes
who pays, who receives, delivery staff, local effects, transition timing and
what remains unfunded. No indicator automatically imposes a robot tax, cuts
benefits, nationalizes a firm or creates a new AI authority.

Immediate individual claims and employment protections continue while that
choice is made. If the Assembly's queue blocks a due funding decision, C03's
protected agenda and C06's lawful continuity route apply. This is a research
selection, not a forecast that AIcountry will become a post-work society or
proof that one income model is superior. The [decision record](../../2026-10-automation-settlement/decision-record.md)
sets out rival packages and the real-world test. Reopen this design if actual
income, service or power data show that plural ownership with general taxation
cannot distribute gains or maintain democratic independence.

[AT13–AT17](18/04-public-investment-and-realized-technology-returns.md)
compares paid public outputs, contingent returns and ownership where
public funding is discretionary. It preserves ordinary work and resident
rights and keeps projected returns out of the current cash source.

[AT28–AT33](18/07-realized-technology-gains-and-public-use.md) compares
voted uses of cash a public body actually receives and discloses
any replacement of ordinary appropriations.
