# Design411 decision. Give a qualified resident alternative a separate elected disposition

## Baseline and purpose

This AI-assisted selection uses [Design410](predecessor-design-410/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md),
the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).
RD04 already requires a vote on the exact main recommendation and preservation
of minority reasons. AR and FA preserve materially different affected views
and procedural correction. The missing choice is whether a competing resident
text can have its own protected disposition when the sponsor or a panel
majority can otherwise keep it off the elected agenda. Participants, people
outside the draw, affected users, representatives and staff are affected.

## Competing procedures and selection

Keep the main disposition and publish dissent. This is lighter but leaves
even a well supported and feasible alternative dependent on member sponsorship.
Give every material resident alternative a protected floor vote. This reduces
agenda discretion but can exceed finite drafting and hearing time and invite
strategic fragmentation. Select one bounded slot for a materially distinct
exact text with verified support from one quarter of commissioned seats.
Keep ordinary member and affected-person routes for others. Provide equivalent
independent support and keep the original RD clock. The quarter threshold
and one additional slot are explicit design hypotheses.

The main objections are a small affected group's exclusion, a captured
qualifier, staff shortage and an incentive to overstate support. Preserve
material reasons regardless of the threshold. Use original expressions and
independent correction with an actual alternate presider. Keep the threshold
fixed through vacancies and distinguish requesting consideration from assent.
No independent qualified review or affected-party participation was obtained.

## Decision authority, continuity and implementation

The Assembly commissions and pays for the process and retains the elected
choice, legislation and appropriations. The career Assembly service verifies
scope and support. The lawful presider executes the disposition, and the
independent forum reviews breached duties within RD05. The panel or reviewer
cannot select the policy. Verify actual staff, evidence access, appointment,
funding and the ability to restore a vote against the sponsor's resistance.
Opposition and authors may challenge without relinquishing their own views.

The proposal preserves RD's main text, report and floor deadlines, actual
participant pay and source custody. It adds a procedural vote only for a
qualifying version. It changes no ordinary voting, initiative, finance,
existing service, urgent action or constitutional rules. AI can assist
drafting and comparison with author correction. It cannot count presumed
agreement or merge reasons. Adopting arrangements must carry live records,
earned payments and review to lawful funded successors.
