# Three paid versions and two actual voting windows

*Design387 · Constructed comparison with [primary records](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-political-advertising-and-private-choice/sources.md).*

One list buys distribution of three materially different versions
of a lawful political argument. Version A runs beside a relevant
publication and uses no personal profile. Version B is delivered
using privately inferred beliefs even though the publisher's form
says the campaign buys a broad audience. Version C uses directly
supplied language preferences with separate valid permission, but
its material variation is not inspectable when first delivered.
Unpaid versions of the same argument remain lawful. A separate
editorial discusses the campaign without paid coordination.

A complaint reaches an independent receiver on day 2. In the
constructed case, protected original delivery evidence is obtained
on day 3. Meaningful reply and competent review finish on day 4.
Independent execution can correct or stop only the disputed paid
delivery by day 5. An early voter has their only usable opportunity
on day 4. Another group can use the corrected route on day 6.
Evidence work costs 2 units, independent review 2 and actual
execution and correction 1. Both voters' choices are secret.

| Path | Result |
|---|---|
| Disclosure alone | A broad label can coexist with unlawful or hidden actual targeting. The report may expose a missing version but supplies no privacy constraint. |
| Scope-specific correction with five units | A can continue. End B's prohibited targeting while allowing lawful contextual placement. Make C's original variant inspectable safely before continued paid use. Leave lawful arguments and editorial speech available. |
| Four units, missing original evidence or shared execution control | A complete independent timely remedy cannot be confirmed. Preserve individual review and disclose the specific failure. |
| Timely correction on day 5 | It is useful for the day 6 group. It does not restore the lost day 4 opportunity. |
| General ban on paid electoral messages | It avoids covered targeting but also removes lawful A and permissioned C, with distinct access and expression costs. |

No ballot outcome, harm prevalence or actual privacy can be
inferred from the modeled timing. The case separates sponsorship,
actual paid delivery, consent, the rule for creative versions and
lawful public argument.
