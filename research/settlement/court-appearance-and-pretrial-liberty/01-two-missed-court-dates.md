# Two missed court dates on the same docket

*Design378 · Constructed comparison with separate [primary evidence](../../2026-11-court-appearance-and-pretrial-liberty/sources.md).*

Two people miss required appearances on day 2. The first received
notice but an accessible route failed. Defense verifies the barrier
and a fair new hearing is available on day 5. The second received
usable notice, had an accessible route and knowingly evaded a
material hearing. The court has specific admissible evidence beyond
absence and finds a new summons inadequate. A separate, valid
witness protection order remains in force in the second case.
Neither person is initially confined.

On day 3, a competent court withdraws a preexisting absence warrant
in the first case. The registry has to reach two execution desks
before the next planned enforcement check on day 4. Each
acknowledgment costs one unit in this model. The corrected hearing
needs one unit each for assistance, interpretation and independent
defense/registry review. Units are additive only within these
specified, staffed work packages. The second case uses its already
funded independent judge and defender, and their time is separate
from these five units. Transport requires actual booking and
acceptance. The court's disposition alone pays for neither transport
nor correction at an execution desk.

| Path | Result |
|---|---|
| Plain notice and optional reminders alone | The first person already understood the date. A reminder supplies neither a route nor a warrant correction. Retain reminders for people who choose them. |
| Five units, both desks confirm on day 3 | Correct the first warrant and provide a feasible day 5 hearing. The second warrant may issue under the separate findings. It supplies no automatic remand. |
| Four units with the funded hearing intact | One execution copy is unconfirmed on day 4. The executor checks the authoritative order or seeks verification before acting. The incident stays open even if arrest is prevented. |
| Five units with one desk late or assistance unaccepted | Report incomplete execution or attendance support separately. Preserve a fair reset where needed, witness protection and existing liberty limits. |
| Uniform release supervision | Adds a burden to the first person without addressing the lost route, and supplies no automatic finding of necessity in the second. Test any narrow individual condition on its own merits. |

With both people simply absent and no independent evidence of
evasion, neither absence warrant qualifies. If the second person
is later arrested on a lawful warrant, the original actual arrest
time governs P09 and a fresh decision governs P10. The modeled
conditions evaluate authority and operational completion separately.
The case assumes no prosecution, safety or public savings from
the reform.
