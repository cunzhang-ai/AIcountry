# Design104: specify monetary and supervisory power, retain its real losses

1 November2026 · Scoped design/research choice, not adopted or observed progress

[MC01–MC32](../../country/C06/41-money-and-financial-public-control.md) now gives
the missing positive monetary/prudential/conduct leadership and operating chain.
Five fixed monetary seats/three actual votes, separate six-year supervisory
heads, full-N three-fifths appointments/finite reserves, outside court removal
and case officers' own duties replace “qualified staggered leaders” alone.
The three-source stability council needs two actual votes, not Treasury assent,
a career Deputy or reduced vacancy denominator. Shared appointments can still
capture all three; no labels prove independent power or successful replacement.

| Actual selection | Retained cost / failure |
|---|---|
| Primary-law own-rate/typed-transaction exception | Avoids a parliamentary vote per trade but delegates consequential debtor/saver/worker effects to specialists; general bank rules remain DR. |
| Finite continuing risk charter with annual audit/hearings/June30 response | Avoids an annual authorization cliff but can preserve stale expert-shaped limits and a majority's nonresponse; no silent expansion follows. |
| Gross commitments/group limits, independent solvency/title and separate settlement | Unknown/bad sources or unavailable qualified roles can stop eligible help; collateral/ratios alone do not establish bank viability. |
| Seven-day M13 urgent outer and six+one-six ordinary duration | Fast systemic harm can outlast temporary authority before DR finishes; a separate valid primary Act or protection may be required, not an invented bridge. |
| Recognized losses, paired reserves and separate recap law | Credibility/fiscal pressure can still constrain policy; negative equity creates neither free FX nor compulsory austerity. |
| Finite safe bank/risk disclosure plus protected granular sources | Both disclosure-induced run harm and secrecy abuse remain possible; actual source/affected experience must assess the choice. |

[The received review/decision](../../2026-11-money-and-financial-public-control/decision-record.md)
records actual source-clock, FX-unit, interim-loss, reconsideration and model
repairs; same-controller helpers are not independent professional/public review.
[Paid dependencies](../../settlement/commitments/20-money-and-financial-public-control.md)
and authored arithmetic expose credit/shift/cash/FX/ledger bounds only. They
cannot prove original risk, actual solvency, authority, currency acceptance,
owned source, staffed operation, received remedy or better country outcomes.

**Next substantive national choice:** positive nonpersonal tax bases and
collection/source rules, with noncash Y interaction. Existing TA/RG/TX specify
personal income, not the whole recurring revenue package. Compare corporate/
consumption/land-resource/property and simpler alternatives, effects on actual
people/jobs/prices/investment, mobility/imports, incidence, compliance, transition
and joint public duties. Do not assume a corporate label bears the ultimate
cost or count profit remittance, credit, fines and asset sales as recurring tax.
Select a coherent source package, not just another revenue table that leaves
unpaid U/I05/Y/H/TW/care/election/remedy obligations outside its scope.

Exact local delegation, year-round/pre-call/local/referendum finance, complete
ballot/map usability, detailed nonbank-sector technical law and one viable
whole-country production/source/service/FX package remain. Actual urgent harm
or a larger joined contradiction can reprioritize. Founding, competent law,
qualified independent and affected participation, operational capacity and
observed superiority are separate later facts, not substitutes for finished
positive design or claims supplied by a green artifact check.
