The bounded question is **how the country recognizes a person and their chosen
or dependent relationships without making a household head, guardian, registry,
family agency or AI a gatekeeper over all of that person's rights**. The current
C02/C07/C08 name rights, care and a civil-law floor, but do not yet give birth,
change of status, parenting, protective intervention, supported adult decisions,
separation and death one accountable path across services and courts. A false
family record can deny school, money, housing, medical consent, a vote or an
inheritance. A protective service can rescue someone; it can also remove a
child because a family is poor or treat a disabled adult as having no voice.

**Values:** independent legal personality, care and belonging, bodily autonomy,
child safety and evolving voice, disability equality, freedom from violence,
fairness to respondents, privacy and continuity of valid duties. **Constraints:**
applicable rights and existing family orders, actual shelter and care capacity,
safe evidence and prompt independent review. **Unknown empirical claims:**
prevalence and patterns of harm, registration exclusion, discriminatory removal,
court delay, available placements, safe alternatives and whether the selected
authority chain works under common control. **Design hypothesis:** a portable
individual-status floor, plural lawful relationships, support-first care and
bounded protective intervention can improve lives while preserving usable
private cooperation. It may fail if courts are slow, registries control service
entry, or a nominally independent child protector shares political and vendor
control with the agency it reviews.

## Rival diagnoses and packages

| Package | Mechanism and reason to consider it | Critical failure or cost |
|---|---|---|
| Retain modular family and civil manuscripts, add service funding | Preserves existing proposals and avoids a new status architecture. | A child can remain undocumented and unserved while registry, school, welfare and court each refer the case elsewhere; old capacity and protection clauses can conflict. |
| Household-head or registered-marriage gateway | One verified representative simplifies benefits, consent, housing and record access. | A controlling partner or guardian can block a person's money, safety, vote, health care and exit. Unregistered and diverse families are excluded; one corrupt record propagates everywhere. |
| Family-agency discretion with rapid removal and placement | May act quickly where danger is real and adults cannot protect a child. | Poverty, disability, migration or dissent can be misread as danger; the agency may select evidence, placement and appeal without independent testing or enough safe homes. |
| Maximum family privacy with court action only after conviction | Limits arbitrary surveillance and coercive removal. | Urgent harm may continue before a criminal case is proved; survivors and children may lack independent access to shelter, money and legal help. |
| **Selected: individual status, relational duties and independently reviewable protection** | Services use a portable person-level claim, family and support ties are legally legible without one household sovereign, care is offered before coercion where safe, urgent protection has narrow grounds and prompt review. | Requires registrars, interpreters, safe accommodation, care workers, courts, separate evidence custody and funded relief; privacy and emergency action can conflict. |

The selected package does not merge every critique into a single compulsory
family form. A family may be formed, changed or dissolved under law; care networks
can be recognized for specific purposes without automatically transferring
parental or property powers. A real adopting public must settle parentage,
reproductive, adoption and inheritance rules through legitimate law, especially
where values cannot be averaged. The project does not choose one cultural ideal
of family or infer that a blood relationship is always safe.

## Selected operating chain

1. **Status and identity belong to the person.** A public civil-status service
   offers accessible, free registration of birth, death and legally significant
   relationship changes, with original evidence, reasons for refusal, protected
   correction and an independent court route. A provisional, reviewable identity
   path permits urgent health, school, safety and subsistence access when papers
   are missing or disputed; it does not settle nationality, parentage or electoral
   eligibility by administrative guess. Records separate the legal fact,
   contested claim and source provenance. No family member, landlord, employer,
   biometric vendor or AI account owns the person's identity. Data sharing is
   purpose-limited; a universal household dossier is rejected.
2. **Relationships confer specified duties, not ownership.** Consenting adults
   can enter and leave lawful partnerships with equal status and fair process.
   The legislature specifies property, maintenance, child and survivor duties,
   including obligations after dissolution; neither marriage nor co-residence
   is an automatic condition for each person's essential benefits or vote.
   People may name trusted supporters for information, visits or decision
   assistance under a limited revocable mandate. A support designation does not
   silently transfer property, parental authority or medical consent. Disputed
   parentage and succession require evidence, representation of affected people
   and an independent decision. A child's rights do not depend on the adults'
   marital status or papers.
3. **Children have a route independent of adults and agencies.** Parents and
   lawful carers owe safety, care and education while the child has supported,
   age-appropriate participation and privacy. The local child-and-family
   service funds practical support, safe housing and respite before treating
   a material shortage as parental unfitness. Specific serious harm or a
   demonstrated immediate danger, not poverty, disability, migration status,
   family form or political belief alone, grounds compulsory separation.
   Emergency action has recorded reasons, minimum necessary scope, immediate
   safe placement and prompt independent judicial hearing with the child and
   affected carers heard through suitable support. Any continuing order has
   review, contact where safe, a reunification or stable-care plan, and a
   funded placement; neither a shelter bed nor a court form is a caregiver.
4. **Adult support preserves legal agency.** Adults retain legal capacity on
   an equal basis. Accessible explanations, communication assistance and a
   chosen, revocable supporter or agent come first. Supporters disclose
   conflicts, account for property and can be replaced. Disability, age,
   diagnosis or residence in care never removes legal capacity, including for
   a single decision. When a person cannot communicate a current choice even
   with support, prior wishes and preferences guide any narrowly authorized
   protective act. An emergency medical act uses the same necessity and consent
   safeguards applicable to anyone; a temporary property custodian may preserve
   assets but not become a general substitute decision-maker. Such acts need
   a stated legal ground, minimum scope, independent review, safe challenge
   and a way back to the person's direct control. Care services and banks
   preserve direct access to contest a supporter or provider. Relatives are
   not assumed to supply unpaid care indefinitely.
5. **Protection is separate from criminal guilt.** A person facing domestic
   or other intimate harm can reach confidential advice, safe accommodation,
   urgent health care, independent payment and a temporary protective order
   without an offender's permission or a prior conviction. A named court or
   legally authorized officer uses a stated risk ground and the least
   restrictive effective measure, records reasons and gives a prompt hearing
   and challenge with safe disclosure. A temporary order does not determine
   criminal guilt, child custody, title or immigration status automatically;
   those require their own lawful procedures. Police enforce only lawful orders.
   Misuse, retaliation, discriminatory under-protection and false accusation
   are reviewable without presuming that every unproved report was malicious.
6. **Death and succession do not erase claims.** The registry records death
   with medical and legal safeguards. A lawful estate administrator inventories
   assets, debts, dependants and valid wills; creditors and beneficiaries have
   notice, protected subsistence where enacted, dispute and appeal routes.
   Pensions, care, housing, data access and pending proceedings have named
   successors. A disputed record, algorithm or family claim cannot privately
   transfer property or cancel a dependent person's protection.

The CRC's [full treaty text](https://www.unicef.org/child-rights-convention/convention-text)
is a primary comparator for birth/identity (Articles 7–8), reviewable separation
(Article 9), the child's voice (Article 12), protection from violence (Article 19)
and alternative care (Article 20). The CRPD's [Article 12](https://www.un.org/development/desa/disabilities/convention-on-the-rights-of-persons-with-disabilities/article-12-equal-recognition-before-the-law.html)
and [Article 23](https://www.un.org/development/desa/disabilities/convention-on-the-rights-of-persons-with-disabilities/article-23-respect-for-home-and-the-family.html)
inform legal capacity, support and equal family rights. The CRPD Committee's
[General Comment 1](https://documents.un.org/doc/undoc/gen/g14/031/20/pdf/g1403120.pdf)
calls for replacing substituted decision-making with supported decision-making;
the older manuscript's restrictive-guardianship clause therefore cannot be
carried forward without fresh rights review. The Committee interpretation is
authoritative guidance, not a claim that this research has resolved every
hard emergency or chosen the law for a real jurisdiction. CEDAW Committee
[General Recommendation 35](https://docstore.ohchr.org/SelfServices/FilesHandler.ashx?enc=mdnxIPKfpXr0kaBuhkSZBE60Jj395tBuVQNr22I61CYuHGpRAY4ZmER3Y02lqUqhY2zevx3M8DtWjPOwVWioYQ%3D%3D)
informs survivor access and non-stereotyping. These instruments and guidance
do not adopt AIcountry law, supply its workforce or determine local treaty
applicability. The selected registry, care and court divisions and any hearing
clocks are domestic design choices, not conclusions compelled by those texts.

## Dependencies, dissent and transition

The Assembly sets the common civil-status, family, child-protection and
supported-decision floor under A3/A7; elected local bodies organize accessible
family and care services within it. Registrars record and correct facts but
do not adjudicate contested parentage or confer sovereign rights. Qualified
care and protection staff can act only within enacted grounds; judges decide
coercive continuation and disputed status, while separate inspectors and
criminal investigators review agency harm. Treasury funds shelters, legal
help, interpreters, foster or community care, adult support, courts and
enforcement in the same C06 account as schools, health and housing. A child
or survivor can reach review without the challenged carer or agency's login.
The appointment, funding, original evidence and execution paths of reviewer
and provider must be checked for common control.

This revises the working interpretation of C02, C07, C08 and proposed A7, and
adds a civil-status and family-duty row to founding transition. It does not
amend the immutable source manuscripts, law, service contracts, family orders,
custody arrangements, inheritance rights or tax definitions. In particular,
the proposed old clause allowing restrictive guardianship is preserved as a
historical artifact but is displaced in the working design by equal capacity
and supported decision-making; emergency protective custody of property is
not permission to remove the person's legal agency. Existing claims,
records and lawful orders need named receiving institutions, privacy controls
and review before any registry or agency migration. The old draft's 48-hour
and seven-day hearing periods stay visible as candidates to test against real
court and safety capacity; silence or a missed hearing cannot grant permanent
custody, erase the child's claim or prove a respondent guilty.

Material objections remain. State documentation can become surveillance;
supporters may dominate a person's expressed wishes; a family agency may
mistake material deprivation for neglect; a court may return a person to danger;
universal individual benefits may duplicate or miss actual household support;
multiple lawful carers may dispute authority. Court review itself can be slow,
expensive or captured. These are not settled by calling every actor independent.
The selected route requires an original-record and controller map, safe
participation, alternatives to removal, and data on both under-protection and
wrongful intervention. An affected community may prefer more local family
governance or a simpler national system if it can preserve each person's
standing and practical exit. Value conflicts over reproductive and kinship
law remain for legitimate public decision, not AI consensus.

**Evidence qualification:** primary treaty comparisons, original project
manuscript and logical failure analysis; no affected-party testimony, local
law, birth or case files, caseload, placement inventory or real cost model.
**Research selection:** person-level identity and claims, plural lawful ties,
support-first care, bounded protection and independent review. **Legal
authorization:** none. **Operational readiness:** no trained staff, secure
registry, independent court, shelter, alternate payment or placement is
demonstrated. **Observed outcomes:** none; F9 is an authored exercise.

The next bounded test is **a child and noncitizen carer displaced across
localities during intimate violence while their identity record is disputed**.
An actual adopting jurisdiction would trace the first safe night, school and
care entry, benefit payment, protective hearing, record correction and any
parentage or immigration dispute under its current law, with child, survivor,
respondent, disability, registrar, shelter and court perspectives heard safely.
The deliverable is a source-backed timeline of authority, placement, money,
privacy, review and actual receipt, comparing the selected route with the
modular and agency-discretion packages. No jurisdiction or field budget is
available here. Reopen this selection if original records, affected testimony,
capacity or credible alternatives show either exclusion or coercive capture.
Until then, no paper pathway warrants a claim of family safety or superior lives.
