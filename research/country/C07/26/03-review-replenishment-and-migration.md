# Keep independent oversight and restore capacity

*Part of [MQ critical medicines](../26-critical-medicines-and-safe-continuity.md).*

**MQ08 — Give patients and reviewers a separate route to evidence.**
The existing clinical-rights reviewer can examine useful-time access,
receive a complaint directly and order an available lawful correction
within its remit. The TD09 authority keeps independent quality
judgment and the courts keep rights, supplier fairness and enforcement.
Audit can inspect purchasing, lots, protected source evidence and
ongoing payments under its own powers. Trace appointment, protected
pay, information, credentials and execution for each body. Preserve
source access outside an implicated purchaser, provider or vendor.
Publish safe aggregate shortages, affected routes, delayed treatments,
uncovered people, paid costs and actual remedies. AI may flag changes
or dependency conflicts for accountable staff. It cannot waive quality,
change an allocation rule, authorize a release or choose a treatment.

**MQ09 — Replenish through the next shortfall before closing.** Recheck
actual delivery, remaining usable stocks by place and time, new demand
for substitutes, reserve protection and dated replenishment. Count one
batch, trained shift, transport window and currency source once. Before
declaring recovery, confirm that each affected route can meet its
next known windows and that open cases have a named lawful receiver.
Where shortages persist, disclose the remaining duty and obtain
competent budget, supply or scarcity action under existing law.
Restore the donor's readiness and follow any delayed treatment and
compensation through the original claim. The end of a protocol or
national alert does not close a patient's unresolved case.

**MQ10 — Pay for the capability and preserve lawful successors.**
Budget procurement, stock, rotation and disposal, quality and
dispensing staff, substitute capacity, patient transport, urgent
review and safe regional or foreign settlement. Separate current
delivery, paid standby and additional stocks. Before a change,
map title, permissions, batch evidence, protected patient records,
contracts, staff and pending treatment or claims to funded lawful
successors under FT/OP/T. Phase out an old route only when the new
one receives safely, or keep the exposed cases visible with lawful
interim protection. Reopen on repeated common failure, serious
exclusion, substitute depletion, unpayable readiness or a demonstrably
better supply and clinical route.
