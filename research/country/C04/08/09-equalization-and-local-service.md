# MF37–MF40. Preserve local services through national reallocation

*Design455 · Part of [funded local mandates](../08-funded-local-mandates-and-equalization.md).*

**MF37 — Reconcile the same reform at every responsible payer.** Before
a material transfer, equalization change or national standard with
material consequences for standing local duties takes effect, Treasury
and the affected councils and sector authorities reconcile the affected
public schedule. Before the competent prospective vote, publish each
payer and protected service in its actual scope. Show gross duties and costs
that leave, arrive or remain, the ordinary formula and source change,
separately authorized temporary compensation, and the first and post-expiry
budgets. Identify the first cash and service deadlines and the paid
receiving path. Keep unknown or disputed lines visible with their owner
and consequence for commencement. Publish safe aggregates and methods.
Use restricted access for individual service records. A neutral total,
an offset at a different payer and a conditional allocation each leave
the local funding question open. For material recurring host
demand under XR, reconcile separately the lawful cost of keeping
qualified capacity available, actual visits, protected local work
and earlier advances. The source may pay for readiness before
usage where the competent budget authorizes an accepted block.
Exclude duplicate funding for the same cost under ordinary
equalization or another service payment. Test the second call against the remaining cash after unsettled
advances, and test the loss of another host, with no forced change of residence
or final payer from a provisional allocation.

**MF38 — Decide which kind of gap needs a response.** Establish a local
claim through matched costs, continuing protected duties and sources
usable at their own due dates. An accounting loss by itself creates no
right to reimbursement. Pay each undisputed amount by its original due
date. For a proven timing gap, use an already authorized MF advance or LD
backstop only up to the least of the eligible uncovered duty, the lawful
limit and actually accessible funds. Confirm the accepted operator and
test simultaneous claims under MF27. An advance against a final transfer
requires lawful reconciliation and may not assume a draft appropriation
as repayment. Where settlement is late, restore the revolving capacity
only after actual reconciliation. Record an unpaid or unserved duty and
its remedy when no funded route delivers.

**MF39 — Vote the durable settlement and preserve local choice.** If
matched duties remain underfunded after temporary compensation or before
any lawful source exists, the Assembly must explicitly decide a funded
recurring correction or a lawful redesign with a funded successor.
The elected local council chooses lawful delivery and remains answerable
for its decisions. Independently review disputed inputs, late payments
and selective enforcement. Optional integration waits when necessary
funds, capacity or approval are missing. Existing legal duties,
deadlines, remedies and binding transfers survive loss of the bridge.
Where a commencement date already binds, use only a competent legal
change or an already funded continuity route, and report unmet duties
rather than deeming the transfer complete.

**MF40 — Check both sides of the handover.** Treasury verifies lawful
settlement by payer. The sector owner verifies independently sampled
resident receipt and eligible people missed by either roster. Keep
receiving on both sides distinct from net financing. Give councils
and residents their respective review routes under MF08. AI may
check attribution and flag discrepancies, with a paid manual route.
Appropriations, release, coverage and findings stay with the competent
public actors. Reopen if the formula repeatedly produces unserved
local duties, payment timing becomes politically selective, or a
simpler rights-preserving funding rule performs better.
