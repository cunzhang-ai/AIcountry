# Design414. Test a first programme with evidence available at the time

[T37–T42](../../country/C14/06/11-first-programme-before-outcomes-are-known.md)
requires common earlier commitments across indistinguishable scenarios,
dated evidence and funded activation. Protect existing obligations and
show concurrent or unobserved risks separately.

[Worked case](../../settlement/integrated-first-programme/12-first-programme-before-outcomes-are-known.md)
selects a single-call reserve for a stipulated joint budget and records its
limits. Actual warning, capacity, sources and outcomes remain open.
