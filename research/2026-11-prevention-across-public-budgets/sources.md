# Observed source. Housing First and the cost of other services

Latimer and colleagues' 2019 secondary economic analysis of At Home/Chez
Soi studied adults who were homeless, had a current mental disorder and
met the trial's moderate-need criteria. In four Canadian cities, 1,198
people were randomized to scattered-site Housing First with rent
supplements and off-site intensive case management or to usual services.
Of those, 1,160 contributed economic data. Service use was collected
through questionnaires. The analysis used a modified societal perspective
that included social assistance and disability benefits as costs. Use the
original population, accounting basis and comparator. The broader trial
summary already used in C09 is from the same evidence family.
[Article, methods and Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6707012/)
· [Publisher](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2748596)
· [Archived open-access article](original-sources/latimer-2019-pmc.html)

Across the two-year analysis, the authors report a mean cost of about
14,496 Canadian dollars per participant per year for the intervention and
6,629 in lower estimated costs across other services, in 2016 Canadian
dollars. They report a net increase of 7,868 per year after offsets
(95% confidence interval 4,409 to 11,405). The inputs and result are
rounded independently. The authors estimate 140.34 additional days of
stable housing on the study's analysis basis (95% confidence interval
128.14 to 153.31) and 56.08 Canadian dollars per additional day. These
are comparisons against usual services, not a cash ledger for a public
payer. The authors' categories include shelter and emergency and ambulatory
care use. [Article, results, discussion and Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6707012/)

The trial supplies bounded evidence that a combined offer can improve
housing stability even when estimated offsets leave a positive net cost.
It does not identify which service could cut a payment, when that cash
would be available, or how to assign any payment change to a later budget.
It did not test the separate contribution votes, proposed rights or an
intake-repair comparator in this design. The present selection is an
institutional response to that financing uncertainty. No trial number
funds or prices the proposed country.
