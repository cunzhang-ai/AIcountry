# Secure belonging: competing models and the choice's cost

*Design81; values and mechanisms, not a universal institutional ranking.*

Membership organizes whose home can be conditional and who can replace government.
An identity registry is only one mechanism. Inherited bonds, lived dependence,
property/work power, public resources and control of entry all affect belonging.
Citizenship and election policy cannot be reduced to message coordination or an
aggregate happiness score. No ancestry is more human; universal rights precede
the selected boundary of a particular political community.

| Coherent choice | Why it could be preferable | Cost/failure and present disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Previous resident franchise with citizenship/secure residence unfinished | Keeps national votes separate from discretionary naturalization and avoids prematurely fixing nationality | Leaves a government able to threaten a resident voter's home and undefined child/stateless routes. Reject leaving these design gaps open as an endpoint |
| Descent/citizenship-only political membership | Nationality expresses inherited continuity and external representation; entry remains controlled | Can create durable governed nonmembers and inherited exclusion; executive naturalization/removal influences the electorate. Retain as a genuine rejected-default rival |
| Guaranteed naturalization, citizen-only vote | Transparent entitlement can open membership while retaining one nationality boundary | Still requires people to change nationality and clear records/application gates before governing their shared life. Not selected over independent resident franchise |
| Immediate resident membership/vote | Minimizes governed outsiders and waiting-time coercion | A visitor/very new arrival may lack durable shared dependence; actual residence and reception still contested. Retain; four years is not a proven optimum |
| Selected birth/lived-membership package | Universal territorial birth, bounded overseas descent, free four-year voluntary naturalization and permanent secure home weaken sponsor/executive leverage | Broad birth/family duties, four-year exclusion, continuous-clock costs, irreversible corrupt grants and durable return rights consume resources and can be contested |
| Narrow judicial fraud revocation, never stateless | Can correct a corrupt or personally fraudulent formal grant within a finite window and rigorous proof | Naturalized membership remains more conditional and government can weaponize allegations. Not selected; no-deprivation treats ordinary accountability separately |
| Renewable permits and serious-crime expulsion of settled residents | Retains stronger entry/expulsion policy control and a route to remove a perceived threat | Makes an established member's home conditional on administrative status and adds banishment to ordinary punishment; not selected after secure belonging |
| Cumulative rather than continuous four-year residence | Respects mobile lives and prevents genuinely ended periods being lost | Can attach full membership without a recent continuous shared-life period. Retain as a real alternative; temporary/coerced absence protection does not eliminate the difference |

The selected causal hypothesis is that durable status plus independent political
standing makes government, sponsor and identity-provider retaliation harder.
Separate officers and original evidence reduce some control chains; a common
patron, corrupt conferral or shared social prejudice can defeat them. Actual
public rejection, impossible reception or better protection through a rival can
displace this model. More generous prose does not prove material delivery.

At commencement, preserve established citizenship/stronger rights and actual
prior residence. Include resident territory-born minors expressly, without
recalculating past elections or inventing founding authority. Existing laws,
rights, source records, contracts and pending claims need funded lawful successors.
The actual number/cost of new admissions and cohort rights remains unknown;
an Assembly capacity schedule must expose those effects rather than shift them
to unfunded local receivers. No model grants itself a public mandate.

Reopen on exclusion, sponsor coercion, demographic manipulation, unsafe return,
irreversible corrupt grants, missing sources, blocked review or impossible
paid receiving. Independent qualified review and actual affected people are
missing; same-controller agents cannot supply them. Next revision/consultation closure and the joined material country choice,
not another automatically generated nationality study.
