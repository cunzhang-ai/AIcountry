# EN01–EN04: actual persons and proportional-list entry

*Part of [C03/10](../10-democratic-entry-and-campaign-resources.md); design111 EL revision, not enacted law.*

**EN01 — Equal actual eligibility.** Preserve C02/MB and EL01–04 citizen/resident/
diaspora standing, no higher candidacy age, citizenship-only, wealth, language/
loyalty, employer/landlord approval, diagnosis or AI-account test. New called
contest voter/candidate/endorsement/credit eligibility uses independently provable
P-date age/vesting under EL01; no predicted future status or speculative move.
National list entry needs no territorial domicile. Local nominations need the
actual EL02 local connection and six cumulative national habitual months.
The one-year national rule has its own scope. Old lawful
individual incompatibility has its exact source/review; no guilt by allegation.

**EN02 — One common party/civic list gate.** For the particular national ballot,
a party or nonparty civic list submits distinguishable name/identifier, actual
nomination method/charter, records/finance contact, effective control, actual
candidate consents and frozen fallback order. [NM01–NM06](../23-contestable-list-nominations.md)
supplies the required selection and proof for collective nomination. One person may form a civic list;
no fee, incorporation, prior votes/seats, compulsory bank account or ideological
certificate. Each list has1…2N distinct P-eligible consenting people; each person
appears on one list only. EL06 supplies s and two-active national endorsements.
There is no territorial candidacy/independent-seat bypass in the new national count.

Publish names/chosen names, accepted affiliations, eligibility outcome, pool/order
and nomination/control process, not addresses, private status/birth files or
supporter identities. Similar names receive neutral distinguishing identifiers,
notice and direct ordinary review, not ownership of an idea or exclusion of both.
Courts can correct an unlawful process, not appoint the preferred ideology.

After actual filing assent/identity check, a neutral preliminary directory gives
the pool/order, lawful contact/endorsement methods and pending/correction status,
clearly unqualified. Free assisted original-record access, requested paper/audio/
language formats and actual source/worker privacy are paid duties. It is no
pre-freeze final broadcast/cash grant or promise to mass-mail every unqualified
filing; a useful newcomer-discovery route remains unproved. Do not price only
100-word messages or duplicate a single person across several pools.

**EN03 — Independents face the same seat rule.** A consenting person may enter
as a one-person list or join one collective list, with same qualification,
public baseline and national credit cap under EL22’s proved-controller rule. One-person status neither grants
a personal exception seat nor an additional voter/cash entitlement. Material
sponsor/control/coordination still needs PC evidence and direct review for money;
shared views/vendors or a later coalition alone are no fraud or forced affiliation.
No officer may attach an independent to a favored list to change the count.
Assistance transmits the candidate's own personal assent; it cannot choose them.

**EN04 — Frozen nominees and personal refusal.** At V−40 freeze consenting pool,
fallback and endorsements; before that a list may revise its lawful pool/order
with actual consent and accessible changes, using NM09 for collective results.
A supporter may reassign until the cutoff. After
cutoff no party convenience or expulsion inserts/reorders a new name. A lawful
pre-V withdrawal removes the name/capacity, not another valid list vote. After
V begins, refusal/death does not erase cast votes or initial capacity: apply
EL19's frozen unused succession, then actual exhausted-seat supplemental poll.
Proved substantive ineligibility/state error has ER07/16/EL19 source review,
not withdrawal by analogy. Voluntary association/ordinary speech remains free;
no office chooses an opponent, a proxy mandate or a replacement winner.
