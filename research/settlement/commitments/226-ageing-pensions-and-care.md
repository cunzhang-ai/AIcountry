# Design313. Ageing, pensions and care

[RF17–RF21](../../country/C06/36/05-ageing-finance-and-care-capacity.md)
reconciles pension contributions and general backing with long-term
care and actual qualified work. Keep money and staff on their lawful
dates, independent of projections. Old claims, pension reserves,
care access and separate sources retain their own authority.

A financed near-term bridge addresses timing. A permanent gap
requires a lasting and democratically authorized choice. An
existing duty or original date persists through a shortfall.
See the [decision record](../../2026-11-ageing-pensions-care-and-sources/decision-record.md)
for scope, objections and the real-world test.
