# Learning time, actual teaching and a meaningful next route

Design173 · [Country choice](README.md)

An unpaid carer with an old qualification can apply for meaningful renewal
without first proving unemployment or obtaining an employer's sponsorship.
Assess actual usable capability and proportionate necessary learning, including
care, disability, location and the freely chosen aim. A credential still being
formally valid is not evidence it is practically usable. Equally, a sequence
of unrelated new preferred degrees is not automatically proportionate renewal.
Specific refusal/alternatives need reasons, evidence and outside challenge.

An adult requiring language or preparatory learning is ready to begin that
appropriate funded first step; they need not already meet the final programme's
prerequisites. The plan names actual qualified continuation and dates. Generic
orientation or missing public accommodation cannot count as fulfilled access.
A promised place in another unwanted field does not fulfil the accepted aim.

For100 adults choosing64 hours each,6400 learner hours in suitable groups of20
requires320 scheduled group-teaching hours before preparation, assessment,
individual support, actual qualified cover and rooms. Offering every adult
one-to-one teaching would require6400 contact hours instead. Neither authored
example proves that groups of20 are appropriate, actual take-up or a full budget.
A public offer needs credible capacity/expansion, not automatic multiplication
of learners into teachers or assuming every voluntary place is always used.

A genuine64-hour episode may satisfy both AL instruction and compatible TW13
learning activity, with actual voluntary purpose and correct payer. It is not
128 learner hours or free employer release/replacement. A2000-hour qualifying
pathway is not capped by the64-hour continuing floor; costs and paid leave still
need actual lawful owners and sources. Necessary living income is not tuition.

At a large covered public advanced institution, OE12 workforce parity persists:
the default AL economic board has6staff/3learner/3public seats. At smaller ones,
2/2/2. No constituency can pass a full-membership-majority decision alone. These
are governance hypotheses, not evidence of independence or a popular mandate;
qualified academic judgment and licensing remain distinct from board votes.

A smaller public continuing offer plus project/need-funded N renewal can limit
new cost and focus instructors but retains portfolio/employer gatekeeping.
Money accounts can improve purchasing choice but can leave costly inaccessible
courses and weak usable alternatives. Unlimited repeated-degree provision gives
more formal freedom but needs substantially different source/priority choices.
AL retains person direction with proportionate meaningful support and paid
continuity. It can be displaced by a better complete paid/rights-compatible rival.
