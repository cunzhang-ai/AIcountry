## Problem, competing explanations and selection

The previous integrated design assigned ordinary law to an elected Assembly, yet
the local September 16 manuscript still assigns general law, tax parameters,
appropriations, treaty review and emergencies to AI. Dependency inspection found
material AI-law references in administration, digital service, energy, health,
justice and government-capacity schedules as well. Replacing one constitutional
sentence would leave effective power elsewhere. This is a textual finding about
the local source; the hosted edition was not verified.

Values: equal political standing, practical opposition, rights and peaceable
replacement, alongside service continuity, legal certainty and public candor.
Constraints: valid existing permissions and law, real finance, competent staff,
contracts and enforcement. Hypotheses: parliamentary authority can align the
general rules with democratic choice; standing essential obligations can preserve
legal payment authority during a budget lapse; explicit founding maps may reduce
displacement of prior claims. None is proved effective in a real jurisdiction.

Compare (1) retaining the AI-exclusive research arrangement as a future sovereign
model, (2) a parliamentary candidate with revocable AI help and explicit rights
review, and (3) stronger direct democratic legislation with a less central cabinet.
The user's clarified requirement excludes the first as this project's desired
sovereign endpoint. The parliamentary choice supplies a coherent reference without
claiming superiority over the third; detailed institutional/number choices remain
open to public comparison. The website's *current* research permissions stay as they
are because they are not national sovereignty.

For day 91, compare automatic cessation, an executive-renewed bridge, and a
preenacted standing essential-obligations schedule plus a limited bridge. Select
the third as proposed law: it preserves defined legal authority without giving
the incumbent self-renewal power. It can entrench an old allocation and can still
run out of cash; the design therefore includes Assembly amendment, public accounts,
independent rights review and an explicit unresolved capacity qualification.

For founding, an existing polity's lawful reform and a new legitimate territorial
settlement have different sources of authority. Neither is selected without an actual
affected public. The research platform is a third, non-sovereign setting, not a
founding route. Five compound authored cases expose where protections depend on
independent appointment, records, finance, workforce and enforcement.

## Material objections and retained disagreements

- Courts could acquire a budget veto by declaring every need a right. The candidate
  limits them to lawful rights remedies and reasoned orders. The Assembly decides
  general taxes and appropriations; a court cannot create currency or staff. Exact
  rights-floor disputes require real adjudication and the applicable constitution.
- Standing obligations could indefinitely privilege old programs. The schedule
  covers specified rights and existing indispensable commitments, is publicly
  accounted for, and can be amended by ordinary law subject to rights. This still
  distributes power during deadlock; the value conflict is not settled by arithmetic.
- A formally independent reviewer may share controllers with the accused office.
  The design traces appointments, funding, data, execution and provider control and
  discloses common-capture failure. It does not certify any existing institution.
- Parliamentary decision might exclude residents without national votes. All
  persons retain basic rights and remedies; local political standing and founding
  membership require an actual inclusive settlement. Consultation is not a vote.
- An interim charter could become permanent rule by its caretakers. It needs an
  independently valid source, narrow mandate, actual public route and limited
  duration; the project cannot appoint an interim ruler.
- Emergency continuity can hide coercion. The proposed emergency branch cannot
  alter elections, ordinary taxes, succession or core rights, and every renewal
  remains challengeable. This is paper protection until independent capability exists.
- A complete-looking national design might be mistaken for an operating country.
  The main documents plainly separate research design, adoption, operational
  readiness and observed results. No population or superior outcome is invented.

No objection was resolved by affected-party participation or independent expertise.
Future communities may reject the parliamentary form, a particular social settlement
or the entire project while preserving peaceful political equality.

The complete local `research/2026-09-readiness/source-documents.json` is hash-pinned.
Specific predecessor clauses and their revision identifiers are in the manifest.
A scoped search examined AI legislative terms across all 29 local source documents,

[International IDEA's constitution-making participation paper](https://www.idea.int/publications/catalogue/practical-considerations-public-participation-constitution-building)

[interim-constitution paper](https://www.idea.int/publications/catalogue/interim-constitutions-peacekeeping-and-democracy-building-tools)
informs the comparison between interim enforceability and permanent settlement.
These authored institutional analyses share a source organization and are not
independent observations of AIcountry. The Venice Commission's
[Rule of Law Checklist](https://www.venice.coe.int/images/SITE%20IMAGES/Publications/Rule_of_Law_Check_List.pdf)
provides a legal-certainty and remedies comparison, not an endorsement of this
design. These primary references were inspected on 29 September 2026; only the
described scope is used. The project has no legal opinion on any actual territory.

The [authority package](../AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION.md) maps constitutional,
budget, tax, treasury, monetary, service, emergency and external-affairs clauses.
It proposes A1–A9 wording and distinguishes research publication from enacted law.
Affected consumers include residents, claimants, companies, local authorities,
Assembly, cabinet, judges, treasury, security command, providers, lenders and
future research contributors. Earlier lawful entitlements, judgments, contracts,
staff claims, debt and treaty duties require explicit successor treatment. A
technical rollback cannot undo a missed benefit, wrongful detention or lost home.

The [founding assessment](../FOUNDING-AND-STRESS.md) supplies a conditional adoption
sequence and maps those continuing obligations. Project URLs and file navigation
grant no authority. The actual old publication gate and any higher constitutional
holds retain their force until legitimately changed; review cannot transfer from
old wording to the new combined package. AI and human contributions receive the
same substantive scrutiny, but neither model counts nor account labels create
independent reviewers or a public mandate.

## Completion and bounded reopening

The requested **reference country design** is now complete enough to be reviewed
as a single constitutional, social, economic and operational proposal. This does
not close the genuine questions only a real affected public and empirical work can
answer. The next bounded external question is which lawful jurisdiction and people,
if any, may choose among this and other designs. A real sponsor must establish
standing and a lawful public decision process before any implementation claim.

Reopen on a concrete jurisdiction, contrary participation evidence, an omitted
right or prior obligation, a new source-edition conflict, a demonstrated capture
path, an unfinanceable service or a substantively better democratic alternative.
Without actual observation channels, a lack of new complaints is not evidence
that the design succeeds. Do not keep manufacturing local research iterations
merely to demonstrate activity.
