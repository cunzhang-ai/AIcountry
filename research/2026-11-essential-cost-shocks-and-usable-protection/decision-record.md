# Design380 decision. Protect what the income floor can buy

## Baseline and selected route

Design379 already has I02 monthly updates and a last lawful nominal
fallback, E06 seven-day protection, and FM and MS sector continuity.
The gap is applying them together when a necessary cost rises before
the next ordinary payment or the cheapest offer stops being usable.
People without transport, a reachable supplier or a suitable
substitute can bear a different burden from the published average.

Select [I36–I41](../country/C05/06/10-essential-cost-shocks-and-usable-protection.md).
Use priced reachable components, ordinary and short-window authority,
person-controlled cash where purchasing works and funded actual
receiving where a price-only response cannot deliver. Retain the
existing individual cash and service boundaries. The proposed
implementation needs an enacted bounded source for additional
interim reprice and the Assembly retains future content choices.

## Alternatives and objections

Retain the nominal floor and ordinary adjustment with generic hardship.
This is simpler, but stale prices and missing local offers can defeat
early receipt. A uniform early supplement reduces proof burdens and
may work where most people can purchase suitable necessities. It is
more expensive for the same matched cash need in the worked example,
and it cannot deliver the missing food. Direct provision for everyone
can protect against stock loss but risks dependence, unsuitable choices
and hidden work. Select the mixed route, retaining cash where usable.
It needs assisted access so a person does not have to prove an entire
market failure. A uniform route remains a rival on the same outcomes.

Rapid local prices can be noisy, affected by powerful suppliers or
disclose sensitive needs. Independent source checks and safe challenges
are material, and recipient-level decisions remain human. A broader
demand injection may compete for scarce supply. The example assumes
accepted stock and staffing, without predicting inflation, fraud,
administrative cost or national savings. A benefit cap cannot become
a finding that people have enough.

## Evidence, migration and next action

[Official observations](sources.md) distinguish household exposure and
limited low-price offers. The [constructed comparison](../settlement/essential-cost-shocks-and-usable-protection/01-two-places-and-one-shock.md)
has declared units, timing, ordinary stocks and cash. No affected-party
participation or independent local evidence qualification occurred.

The income authority keeps current claims and dates; independent
evidence publishes components; Treasury and the Assembly identify
lawful gross source and expiry; sector owners verify delivery. Preserve
I05, E06, I02 nominal protection, separate rent and service rights and
the actual rights of recipients and workers. Migration uses current
lawful price tables until a competent successor has lawfully begun.
No new software, procurement, payment or statutory permission is
exercised by this edition.

Next test two places against one actual seven-day window, including
suitable food, necessary travel and an unusually high access cost.
Reconcile accepted stock, staff and paid cash through the next ordinary
payment. Include people who lack ordinary market access and verify
receipt independently. Reopen on costly exclusion, supplier distortion,
repeated bridge dependence, loss of physical receiving or an equally
protective simpler route. Actual authorization, readiness and outcomes
remain open.
