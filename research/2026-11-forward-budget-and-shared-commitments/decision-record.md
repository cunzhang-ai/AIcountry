# Design362 decision on forward budgets and shared commitments

## Baseline, proposed choice and authority

Pin Design361, FB01–FB41, DB, JC, the first-programme rules and the
[working method](../FRAMEWORK.md). Existing FB asks for a four-year
comparison and durable sources. This proposal requires a prior elected
common path, a published margin and a single account of what additions
consume. The current commitment limit uses budget law. The rest of
the path supplies the public baseline for choice and later correction,
with future appropriations subject to their own law. The project
assistant proposes this allocation procedure. No numerical margin,
borrowing, expenditure or release has been authorized.

## Alternatives and objections

Annual and proposal-by-proposal scrutiny leaves flexibility but may
count the same later-year capacity repeatedly. A permanently fixed
ceiling is easy to explain but can cut through stabilizers, rights or
changes in prices and population. Use a voted and openly revisable
forward path with protected margins, separate laws for new commitments,
and actual rights and fiscal sources carried through changes. A weaker
case can delay valuable services. Show who waits and let members put
an exact better funded option to a vote.

An accounting ceiling can invite reclassification and apparent
privatization of risk, and a conservative estimate can become a
technocratic veto. Report comparable fiscal, cash, guarantee and
material effects, protect opposition access and accept lawful elected
revisions. Distinguish planning exposure, unavailable cash and
unavailable staff. Correct none through a model's unilateral judgment.

## Scoped result, migration and next action

The [sources](sources.md) document forward ceilings and revisions.
The [constructed case](../settlement/forward-budget-and-shared-commitments/01-two-reforms-one-forward-budget.md)
compares separate reforms, their combined tails, a weaker source and
one missed payroll date. It selects the staged candidate only in that
case. It does not establish a real budget, appointment, payer, affected
mandate or improvement. Preserve exact prior commitments, original
obligations, separate sources and funded execution through revision.

Within authorized research resources, take two live recurring reform
options and one already binding service in a matched jurisdiction.
Build their comparable four-year path, the relevant later tails, first
payroll, local acceptance and independent review. Compare both with
a common weaker path, actual source and a no-change baseline. Stop
at a qualified choice with explicit remaining shortfalls, without
commitment. Reopen on persistent margin calls, forecast error,
disguised obligations or a better qualified funded offer.
