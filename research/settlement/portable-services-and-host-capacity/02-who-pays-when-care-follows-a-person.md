# Who pays when essential care follows a person?

*Design520 · Observed institutional comparator and constructed budget*

## Existing systems already carry some care across boundaries

Under chapter 29 sections 4–6 of Sweden's current Social Services Act,
the host has emergency responsibilities and must assist a home municipality
and execute its decision on request for specified shorter stays. The home
municipality pays under its equivalent-service reimbursement level. The
responsible minister said in June 2026 that municipalities may already pay
full costs voluntarily. Sections 7–8 also give certain people who need
extensive care or must move because of violence a route to apply in advance.
A realistic comparator includes timely requests, full-cost agreements and
actual staffed receiving when those powers can provide them.
[Swedish legislation](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/socialtjanstlag-2025400_sfs-2025-400/)
· [Minister's written answer](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svar-pa-skriftlig-fraga/ersattning-i-samband-med-tillfallig-hemtjanst_hd12878/).

The National Board of Health and Welfare examined October 2023–September 2024
under the predecessor law. The main report counts 155 of 186 responding
municipalities that performed temporary home care. Among 146 reporting people
served, the reported total is 1,509. In the separate 150-municipality host
item, 24 reported at least one request they could not deliver wholly or in
part, and 42 reported higher net costs. Of those 42, 33 selected travel, 31
additional recruitment and 27 working conditions or inaccessible homes among
uncovered costs. Multiple choices were possible. Missing municipalities and
nonresponses stay outside these denominators. These are municipal reports,
not person-level delays, national incidence or causal attribution. The appendix
uses 191 for its first item after five partial responses. The source record
keeps both denominators. [Original agency report](https://www.socialstyrelsen.se/contentassets/b687a7ca75c84711b36335bd767045ea/2025-4-9562.pdf)

## Decide the smallest funded arrangement that carries both calls

Extend the [earlier host-capacity comparison](01-pay-for-receiving-before-visits.md).
Use fictional units. A host has a qualified three-visit block paid six before
each window. Each visit needs two additional units. Ordinary equalization
already pays two of the six at each cutoff. Protected home and local resident
services are outside these incremental costs and keep their own staff and
funds. One resident with a payable origin needs a visit in week one. In week
two, two visits have a liable origin and one covered visit has no liable
origin. Assume each patient can accept the offered route and the ordinary
national fiscal source may fund the defined additional expenses.

| Viable ordinary branch | Week one | Week two | Disposition |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Gross cost of host block and use | 8 | 12 | Twenty over the two weeks, including already paid equalization. |
| Existing equalization | 2 | 2 | Four already committed to this capacity. No second claim for these costs. |
| Established origin contribution on actual visits | 2 | 4 | Six in total if paid by their cutoffs. |
| Additional ordinary funding needed | 4 | 6 | Eight for the uncovered part of the two blocks, plus two for the covered visit with no liable origin. |

A qualified compact can buy the same coverage for the same dates and full
cost if it has both legal spending authority and funded solidarity for the
unassigned visit. Prefer it where it can preserve equally durable receiving
and local accountability. A voluntary arrangement limited to invoices to an
identified origin leaves the week-two visit without a lawful source. In that
branch, the targeted national appropriation pays the verified host readiness
and bears the uncovered eligible visit after review. It pays an established
origin's invoice early only if XR06 authority and cash qualify, and books its
actual later settlement separately. A general reserve cannot count unpaid
recovery as available. A fully funded regional mobile team competes if it
has qualified staff, travel, local safety and access, and can be called
without depleting protected rosters elsewhere. A cash allowance or digital
award that travels with the person can help pay an independently available
worker. It cannot create one.

If the week-two origin contribution of four is payable but arrives late,
the ordinary budget remains twelve gross. Four more units must actually be
available at the relevant cutoff as a separate authorized advance if all
visits are to proceed. The national payer's week-two exposure would be ten
before repayment and six after it. If cash later settles, record four once
against the actual public liability. On the ordinary branch with timely
origin payments, a week-two national cap of five can pay the four-unit
readiness balance but leaves only one for the two-unit unassigned visit.
Unless a lawful additional unit or a safe lower-cost offer reaches the
provider in useful time, one whole visit remains uncovered in that branch.
If the shared qualified team fails,
neither the six nor the ten buys a visit from that team. Protect unrelated
and already due work on its own clocks, use an independently accepted
alternate or record the missed service. Remedy follows the original person
and deadline. The eligible home's later return visit remains a separate act.

## Carry one household through the boundary

Check a resident who uses planned care in two regions and receives an
established payment from a separate payer. A host can give a protected
first receipt for a payment access problem and arrange competent assistance.
The payment payer retains original amount, clock and independent lawful
payout. No revised residence status follows from the care booking. Verify
care at the destination, unchanged protection on return, independent access
to the due payment and any harm that a paid correction did not restore.
Where the agreed care, payment and safe journey together make the trip
possible, report whether this same person could use all three by their
respective deadlines. Keep each duty and remedy independently visible.
Count protected commitments of both rosters. The first budget can price a
home-support scope and the independent payment path without turning
portable financing into one combined entitlement or an identity register.

Proceed only within an actual enacted scope and paid alternatives.
A scoped two-window comparison can reveal whether national residual funding
provides access beyond strong local repair. To justify wider adoption,
observe timely receipts, who was excluded, unused capacity, worker and
household burdens, cash at both ends and next-season availability. Preserve
a well-funded simpler arrangement if it secures the same receipt and
durable accountability. [Selected law](../../country/C04/09/04-funding-portable-essential-support.md).
