# Correct the account before claiming production

*CP01–CP02; part of [national commitments](../28-national-commitments-and-residual-scarcity.md).*

**CP01 — Output, value added and cash are distinct.** The country's accounts
separate nonmarket public output from its own value added, intermediate purchases,
product taxes, depreciation, investment and financial/property-income transfers.
Under the declared fixture convention, public own GVA is employee compensation
plus public consumption of fixed capital; purchased inputs enter output/cost but
are not added a second time to that sector's value added. GDP at market prices
is not a basic-price industry measure: remove net product taxes before its split.
Actual accounting classifications, including financial services and later standards,
need their own source review; this simplified bridge is not full SNA conformity.

The preserved models' GDP minus total public output was mislabeled market GVA,
and the original 2202 comparison had the same defect. The [new bridge](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-whole-country-reconciliation/results.json)
uses original basic-price market GVA 2311. In the unchanged selected hybrid,
corrected GVA is about 3294.734, requiring about 50.19% more GVA per remaining
market FTE at the declared prices/mix. Neither the old 52.33% nor the corrected
ratio is an observed productivity gain. Do not add purchases to GDP to repair
this reporting error; the separate GDP/cash identities need not change.

**CP02 — Independent supply must constrain the joint package.** Explain how
actual skills/time, maintenance, capital, energy/water/materials, imports, production
mix and willing work produce the promised goods/services. Required demand derived
from tax/transfer/dividend flows is not a productive capability. A larger nominal
GDP, equity conversion, bank loan or compulsory full payout does not create supply.
Do not make lower household needs, involuntary saving or unpaid care the unexplained
residual. Preserve actual voluntary saving, retained investment and plural ownership
as legitimate choices with explicit counterpart claims and distribution.

A corrected GVA-index energy/water comparison remains only a proportional authored
proxy. Sector outputs, real quantities, locations, seasons, ecological floors and
new legal duties can invalidate it. Neither a green proxy nor an old source bid
qualifies actual delivery. Trace common components once and expose both unknown
requirements and displaced existing tasks. All 37 current function costs/quantities
remain unknown; no new law's incremental workload is priced by the old book.

The no-growth counterfactual holds the old fixture's corrected GVA/FTE, wages,
service bids and financial rules fixed to expose incompatible planned uses and
sources. It is not a consumption cap, inflation forecast or claim that nobody
will save. Separate actual willingness and funding from a possible matching
financial balance; no household's surplus is automatically Treasury property.

[NP](../33-protection-production-and-national-expansion.md) now selects actual
supply/use, paid receiving and explicit burdens as the prospective architecture.
The corrected historical required GVA ratio,55,000-worker universe and proportional
energy/water proxy are not a current production/staffing/engineering plan.
