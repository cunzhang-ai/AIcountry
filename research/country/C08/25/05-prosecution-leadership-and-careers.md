# OJ21–OJ25: a prosecution service under law, not a government's private advocate

*Part of [C08/25](../25-ordinary-justice-and-public-prosecution.md); design120 candidate.*

**OJ21 — Public prosecution's actual scope.** A Director of Public Prosecutions
(DPP) leads lawful ordinary criminal charging and administration; prosecutors
apply P13 sufficiency/public-interest rules, seek contrary evidence and disclose
under P11. No criminal policy maker creates offenses, punishments, detention,
search or civil-supervision power by circular. Assembly sets general law/source;
Cabinet and Assembly cannot direct a named charge, acquittal or noncharge.
Published lawful general priorities disclose scarcity/rights and cannot secretly
immunize allies or require a conviction quota. Civil counsel/aid/courts and
outside-command investigators retain their distinct powers and employers.

**OJ22 — An open, different leadership source.** Protected Assembly recruitment
staff receive direct consenting applications and publish objective criteria,
qualified names/exclusion reasons and conflicts, with at least 30 calendar
days for applications and B16 evidence/reply/direct review of exclusions;
21-day leadership notice never shortens ordinary adverse-reply rights.
DPP candidates need 12 relevant
legal years and actual criminal-evidence/fair-process/leadership competence;
ordinary career prosecutors need five years and demonstrated criminal-law skills,
with equivalent relevant experience and paid supported entry. No sole guild,
wealth, proprietary certificate, political loyalty or AI ranking controls entry.
Candidates cannot hold minister/legislator/senior party/campaign/lobby leadership
roles; four years after those leadership roles is the proposed cooling period.
Ordinary lawful civic views/advocacy are not a loyalty exclusion. Judges share
that incompatibility/cooling rule under OJ39; actual protected old rights survive.

**OJ23 — Fixed full Assembly approval, finite DPP term.** Any member may nominate
an eligible DPP applicant; after at least 21 calendar days' usable public notice/
hearing, a named single-candidate ballot needs ceil(3N/5) of full statutory N and
ceil(O/3) from the protected non-government cohort. OJ expressly incorporates
CJ05's exact governing-cohort definition, frozen seat positions/denominators and
new-government fresh-vote rules, not CJ12's constitutional-only forum: OJ16
reviews DPP selection/classification. O=0 cannot appoint. No failed vote/clock
creates a plurality winner, lower denominator or Council-controlled pool.
Term: seven personal years from lawful assumption, nonrenewable with no holdover
or return as acting DPP. A vacancy starts a new appointee's own seven years, not
extension of a predecessor's personal term. Open recruitment 180 days before
expiry, within 14 after unexpected vacancy. Deadlock and common-controller
opposition remain real risks, not proof of independence from two labels.

**OJ24 — Ordinary staff and vacancy are not a policy succession.** DPP appoints
qualified prosecutors/deputies through C04 open merit, reasons/conflicts and
protected career due process. Every prosecutor has actual statutory case powers;
absence of a chief cannot cancel a charge route or remand clock. A predesignated
qualified unconflicted career deputy may do funded routine management for at
most 180 elapsed days from the vacancy, no serial same-vacancy reset or new
general policy/discretionary senior appointment. Existing lawful case officers
continue their own decisions after that expires. No acting chief's label supplies
all DPP reserved powers. Individual urgent/abuse cases use OJ26–OJ30, not an
indefinite acting leader. Keep source/court/paid cover and direct access usable.

**OJ25 — No retaliatory prosecution career.** Staff promotion/transfer/discipline
needs actual competence/duty/performance grounds, source, notice/reply and OJ16
independent status review; never refusal to conceal contrary evidence, lawful
objection, an acquittal, politically inconvenient charge or noncharge alone.
Provide accommodation, training and real caseload resources before claiming
persistent inability. DPP removal uses OJ17 closed grounds/proof and OJ16/OJ18
three/five process, paid finite suspension and appeal, expressly for that office;
Assembly cannot dismiss the DPP by a policy-confidence vote. Substantive misconduct
proof and P criminal process stay distinct; a changed government inherits lawful
cases/staff, not a purge or power to direct a prosecutor's signature.
