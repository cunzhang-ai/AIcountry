# Two learners and one support slot

This is an authored two-week exercise. Each learner needs five usable
three-hour sessions a week on a separately agreed interim plan. Both can
use a stable, accessible setting. Each due session needs a teacher and
one trained support worker present for the same three hours. The plans
are individually suitable assumptions for this example. Other statutory
teaching, rights and professional judgments remain outside its arithmetic.

A school removes five scheduled sessions each week for one learner
after a support worker leaves. Another learner temporarily cannot use
their ordinary setting while recovering from illness and can use short
local instruction with support. Both can only use the same morning
windows for independently evidenced reasons. The local authority already
pays for two distinct teachers, and only one trained support worker is
free in those windows. Existing staffing and room bookings allow two
parallel sessions each morning. The first worker can support one.
No plan uses the learner's household as unpaid cover.

| Two weeks | Teacher hours | Supported learner hours | Missing supported hours |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Due across the two learners | 60 | 60 | 0 |
| One worker across ten morning windows | 60 available | 30 deliverable | 30 |
| Second qualified worker present for the same windows | 60 available | 60 deliverable | 0 |

A generic tutoring voucher would still leave the support gap. A purchased
second slot could cover it if it includes the worker and actual compatible
hours. Repair in the school is feasible for the first learner with paid
cover. The second can use a local interim site with independent review of
the recovery plan. A second worker adds 30 contact hours. For this
exercise add one paid planning hour each week, so the incremental
staffing source is 32 hours at the contracted full hourly cost. Travel,
access and review are separately booked under existing compatible
capacity by assumption. If those assumptions fail, their missing
resources must be added before the second row can become the third.

If a second worker starts only in week two, 15 hours remain unmet in
week one. Balance immediate risk under prior lawful criteria with neither
learner disappearing from the account. Keep the distinct lost sessions
and arrange catch-up where useful. The week-two gain alone does not settle
week-one harm. A coerced timetable reduction or a declared home placement
does not change the original denominator. The outside reviewer can order
correction under ED15 but cannot make the second worker exist. Ask the
learner separately whether support actually reached them and whether the
setting could be used. Record useful receipt even if national attendance
systems would code the two routes differently.
