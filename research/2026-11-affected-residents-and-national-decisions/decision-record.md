# Design318 decision on affected residents and national decisions

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design317, C02 political membership, MB, NI, RD, FA and the
working method. Select a useful hearing and answer for people
directly governed by a live ordinary national measure during the
period before national enfranchisement. The project assistant authors
the proposal. Legitimate institutions retain every adoption and vote.

## Alternatives and objections

The present franchise and elector initiative leave a bounded gap.
An elected member can speak for a concern but cannot supply the
affected person's evidence. Scottish petitioning illustrates standing
beyond voters. New Zealand's earlier enfranchisement of some residents
is a substantive institutional rival, with its own narrower legal
status boundary. The sources do not validate the four-year national
reference or this hearing. Retain the shorter-franchise option for
a legitimate comparative and constitutional choice.

A protected hearing adds real time, safety, interpretation and work.
It can be captured by a well-resourced association claiming to speak
for others. Keep separate accounts, protected originals, independent
review, ordinary hearing scope and no entitlement to repeated copies.
A paid committee response can expose neglect and make a change possible
while leaving policy with the Assembly. It may still fail to influence
a hostile majority. Do not claim a hearing supplies political equality.

## Selection, continuity and next decision

Select AR01–AR05 for exact materially affected live proposals.
Preserve all existing statutory protections, pending cases, evidence,
lawful political standing and original deadlines. Use the competent
prospective procedure with paid independent receiving. The proposal
neither adopts a new franchise nor changes current constitutional
holds. Provide support in the existing paid scrutiny calendar and
record what happens when that capacity fails.

Test a real ordinary bill window, separate affected reasons, safe
evidence and an effective committee response. Reopen on retaliation,
systematic exclusion, ineffective review or stronger evidence for
a different franchise or participation route. Actual participation,
independent review, authorization, paid operation and observed
outcomes remain open.
