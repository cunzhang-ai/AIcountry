# C08. Reproductive autonomy, parentage and the child's status

*Part of [C08 everyday law and justice](../C08-everyday-law-and-justice.md); integrated design 76 research, not enacted law.*

The working country's bodily-autonomy floor protects a person's decision
whether to become or remain pregnant, seek contraception or fertility care,
continue or end a pregnancy, and accept or refuse a clinical intervention.
No government, partner, parent, employer, clinician, insurer or AI system
may compel pregnancy, abortion, contraception, sterilization or genetic
testing for a demographic, fiscal or political target. The state must also
protect a child born from any lawful or disputed arrangement as an
independent person; adult contracts cannot make that child's identity,
care or essential services disappear.

## Decisions over the body and access to care

Prior Assembly law protects confidential, informed, timely access to the
[C07 reproductive-health route](../C07/09-reproductive-health-and-early-life.md),
including contraception, pregnancy and birth care, miscarriage and abortion
care, and proportionate infertility services. The reference choice permits
safe abortion on the pregnant person's request under evidence-based clinical
standards, without a partner or family veto, criminal punishment of the
patient for a pregnancy outcome, or administrative barriers designed to
make the service unavailable. The Assembly may set clinically justified
delivery, professional and safety rules, but cannot replace consent with
an official's moral approval or deny urgent life- or health-preserving
care. This is an explicit normative research choice, not a claim that all
affected people agree; a lawful founding public must examine the competing
views about developing fetal interests and the bodily rights of the person
who is pregnant without treating either as a laboratory measurement.

An individual receives understandable options, privacy and a direct route
to independent review when care is delayed or refused. An emergency
exception is specific, necessary, time-limited and reviewable under the
same patient-rights safeguards as other care. Conscientious refusal, if
recognized by law for an individual professional, does not belong to a
public institution, cannot obstruct emergency care or hide the referral
and funded accepting provider the health service still owes. A provider
may not make marital status, disability, migration status, income, sexual
identity, genetic social-worth ranking or political views an exclusion gate.
Actual clinically relevant genetic risk can inform a specific safe choice,
with reasons/review, never compulsory selection or denial of essential care. Adolescents need age-appropriate confidential access,
supported understanding and safeguarding; their voice and evolving
capacity are assessed under C02/C08 law, not replaced by a blanket adult
veto or automatic exposure to an abusive household.

Coercion and exploitation are distinct from ordinary disagreement.
Pressuring someone to terminate or continue a pregnancy, withholding
contraception, forced sterilization, reproductive violence and false
clinical information have usable protective and civil remedies, with
specific criminal law where enacted. The respondent has evidence and
fair-process rights. Care records stay with lawful custodians under narrow
purpose and retention rules; an AI fertility score, pregnancy registry or
population forecast cannot become an eligibility, policing or benefits
command. Demographic ageing or pension stress is addressed through C06's
honest resource and worker account, not compulsory births.

## Parentage, assisted reproduction and the person born

Birth registration records the child's existence and immediate identity
without waiting for adults to settle genetic, gestational, intended or
social parentage. The child has provisional health, care, education and
subsistence access, a safe responsible carer and an independent route to
protect identity and family relations. A disputed parentage claim goes to
an authorized civil process with evidence, privacy, representation for
the child and fair hearing for adults; a clinic, donor contract, household
head or algorithm cannot make the final contested judgment. Maintenance,
contact and caregiving duties continue provisionally under valid law and
are corrected without retroactively declaring the child unserved.

Assisted reproduction may be offered through licensed clinical services
with informed consent from each person whose body or gametes are used,
independent counseling where conflict exists, safety and outcome reporting,
and protected records needed for the future person's health and identity.
No contract can prospectively waive a pregnant person's medical consent,
sell a child, erase a child's claim to identity or make genetic origin the
only basis for care. Donor anonymity, third-party gestation, embryo storage,
new genetic interventions and cross-border arrangements raise distinct
value and jurisdiction questions. Until a legitimate legislature enacts a
prospective rule with child, donor, gestational-person and intended-parent
rights and funded oversight, an official or clinic may not invent a new
binding parentage category by contract or software update. Existing lawful
claims and the child's immediate protections continue. Commercial or
cross-border arrangements require examination of labor, exploitation,
foreign law, consent, records and a remedy before endorsement; a technology
being possible does not make its social terms acceptable.

Adoption and nonbiological caregiving follow C08's child-protection and
family-status procedures: consent where valid, independent assessment,
child voice, original-record protection and review of placement and
continuing duties. The state cannot treat infertility as a reason to take
a child from another family, or poverty as proof a parent must surrender
care. C14 must compare real service and court capacity before calling
this route ready. Reopen the design if access or privacy harms are
unequal, child identity remains unresolved, a health provider is forced
to perform an unsafe act, or a rival rights package better preserves
bodily agency and children under actual democratic choice.

[B28–B32](16-family-agency-property-and-estates.md) now adds ordinary
parentage/support and child-care/protection processes, with independent child
voice and no automatic two-parent administrative ceiling. It does not adopt
the still-contested donor, third-party gestation or foreign-arrangement
statutes; no genetic relationship or compact alone settles every duty.

[RP01–RP16/RPO01–RPO03](20-reproductive-law-and-parentage.md) now supplies
the proposed specific statute: donor/origin, noncontingent pregnancy-assistance
payments, pre-use support and post-birth status, material storage/death,
foreign child protection and future-method boundaries. This replaces the
unspecified research choices above, not actual source law. Adoption, safe
capacity and legitimate affected/clinical/legal review remain unestablished.
