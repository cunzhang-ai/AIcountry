when linked receiving is unready. The 30-day review interval is a
candidate accountability period. It supplies neither legal authority

applicable. Preserve prior valid decisions, loss dates, privacy choices,
protected income, case files and staff rights. A payment error is not by
itself a lawful offset against protected income. Actual affected
participation, independent review, enactment, source and delivered results
