The original sixteen subject areas remain covered: governance (C02–C04), economy
(C05–C06), work (C05/C07), care, health and education (C07), housing (C07/C09), justice
(C08), energy and infrastructure (C09), food/water (C07/C09), security and diplomacy
(C10), science and culture (C11), and digital systems (C11–C12). Coverage in a chapter
is not completeness of its law or proof of delivery.

inspected documentary families and authored cases are not independent legal
review, affected participation, actual treaty conformity or observed safety.

future operation, independent sources/investigation and repair, with twelve
authored cases, real rivals and an explicit missing-instrument register. Eight
partial documentary entries/six institutional families are not qualified
independent review, actual engineering conformity or observed outcomes.
