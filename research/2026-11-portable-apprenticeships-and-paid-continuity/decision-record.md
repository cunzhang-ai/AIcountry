# Design396 decision. Keep an apprenticeship portable beyond a single employer

## Baseline and selected mechanism

[Design395](predecessor-design-395/COUNTRY-DESIGN.md) gives people a
public qualifying route, paid work safeguards and cross-local service
continuity. A placement can still lose the linked employer, practical
supervision or receiving funds. General portability alone does not
provide an actual bridge or give a new host authority over old wages.

Select [AL21–AL27](../country/C07/22/05-portable-apprenticeships-and-paid-continuity.md)
under the [working research method](../FRAMEWORK.md). Make verified
learning portable, keep wages and public support separately owed, and
fund a named qualified alternate before relying on a new cohort. Give
people a dated choice to continue, move or pause with an accessible
route back. The same competence and safety standards continue.

## Alternatives and objections

Keep direct employers and ordinary education transfers. This is simple
where workplace capacity is reliable. Employer closure can still
remove employment, mentoring and instruction at once. Require every
apprentice to join a public or group employer instead. This could
pool workplace risk and keep employment stable when a host leaves.
It would add a common employer dependency, cost and a compulsory
intermediary. Retain direct and group routes on comparable worker
protections and offer a common funded fallback. Compare actual
independent paid workshop capacity with both. A public workshop can
teach safely within its scope without automatically supplying all
workplace practice or a future job.

Funding reserve staff and placements can take capacity from current
learners. Protect their existing commitments and count only available
compatible blocks. Require clear terms and qualified assessment to
avoid subsidizing free productive labor or lowering qualifications.
Elected authorities fund and replace providers. Independent assessors,
labor and safety reviewers, and courts hear distinct matters.
A worker can leave unsafe work and pursue unpaid wages separately.
A group employer's legal name alone does not establish an independent
alternate. The five-day disposition and bridge planning dates require
costing and affected review.

## Evidence, migration and next action

[Primary records](sources.md) document finite training continuity,
partial completion evidence and plural host employment in other
jurisdictions. The [constructed comparison](../settlement/portable-apprenticeships-and-paid-continuity/01-two-apprentices-and-one-mentor.md)
separates record transfer, accepted instruction, settlement, old wages
and the next call. It does not establish costs, actual participation,
legitimate adoption, operational capacity or improved outcomes.

Adopt prospectively with funded local and national owners, actual
independent receiving, lawful record custody and named employers.
Preserve current contracts, accrued wages, existing learning, claim
dates and applicable safeguards. Phase new cohorts if standby is
unavailable. Existing rights and appeals keep their governing law.

Next trace two actual transfers, including employer exit and a
cross-local move. Follow the original wage date, credited practice,
mentor roster, next actual learning and review with learners and
workers. Compare direct, group and workshop options at the same
deadline. Reopen on lost credits, unpaid production, displaced
learners, uncovered repeat demand or a better qualified route.
