An innocent purchaser can still lose an asset to its true holder; compensation
against a fraudster may be uncollectible. First-notice priority can entrench an
incumbent lender and reduce new equipment finance. Public records expose some
financial relationships; court-only nonconsensual realization consumes paid time
and may increase credit prices. These are retained costs, not solved by AI.
More secure creditor recovery is not itself a better-life outcome.

No general bearer-token title, land nationalization, retrospective creditor
priming, private forced repossession, new status crime or all-fraud public
insurance. Special M/NB/KR, family/collective, housing, tax, public-acquisition,
foreign and existing constitutional law keep their exact scope. Unfamiliar
assets need actual interpretation rather than silent deletion or fabricated
negotiability. [Review and rival arrangements](../../settlement/property-title/README.md)
separate written choices, missing actual title/source and value conflicts.

The property function is a public custodian, not a sovereign allocator. Actual
parcels, instruments, independent staff, safe records, paid compensation, public
adoption and better lives remain unknown. No software or current law changes.
