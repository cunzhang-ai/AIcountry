# Design220. A public service must survive replacement of its digital supplier

## Baseline and institutional choice

Design219 is the pinned baseline. Use the working framework and decision
template. DP05–DP08 require paid alternatives, usable exit and scoped
review. DP12 preserves legal events. SC14 and FT protect accepted
delivery and continuing duties. The unresolved operating question is
how a public buyer proves that the named alternative can do the work
without a cooperating supplier and how duties continue after activation.

Select DP25–DP32. Require a defined minimum service, lawful custody or
independent usable access, explicit commercial exit terms and an
acceptance exercise. Use actual qualified sector owners and their own
payment and review paths. Preserve the original effect, reasons and
dates of existing acts. Separate the source record from interpretation
and genuinely unsupported meaning. Publish safe outcomes and remaining
risks. There is no requirement to clone a supplier's model.

## Observed obstacles, alternatives and objections

The GAO report identifies agency-reported licensing costs and limits on
choice. The CMA inquiry distinguishes skills, technical differences and
transfer costs. The NAO report documents a public buyer capability and
spending-visibility gap. They support pricing exit and verifying it
with qualified staff. They do not provide a sectoral counterfactual
or prove better public outcomes from a particular architecture.

Keep competent ordinary provision without the optional tool as a real
baseline and an available choice. It may be cheaper or more accessible.
A single integrated contract reduces interface and procurement work and
can offer better specialist assistance. It leaves a risk of weak leverage
if records, licences and staff are controlled by the outgoing supplier.
Two contracted hosts can share resilience costs but still share a root
dependency. Full public operation can retain skills and records with
higher workforce, hardware and maintenance burdens and concentrated
public control. Select an independently usable service core, with
qualified optional supplier assistance where it provides net benefit.
A sector may choose other delivery structures if the same rights,
useful-time continuity, privacy and paid independence can be verified.

Independent copies and credentials add attack and disclosure risks.
Limit their data, purpose, access and tested retention. Protect source
and defence rights. Escrow, open formats and competition do not replace
actual permission, runtime, skills or statutory authority. Tests and
milestones can deter smaller suppliers if made larger than the material
risk. Scale to the consequence, use shared competent support where
safe, and leave disclosed defects open without cutting earned dues.

## Compatibility, authority and further proof

Keep SC award, variation and payment rules, KR intellectual property,
PD access and correction, DP safety and original event rules, FT/T
acceptance, PE scrutiny, B/SR remedies and actual bank settlement.
This prospective design uses existing budget and sector powers.
No new licence exception, universal platform or takeover power follows.

For lawful implementation map legacy contracts, workers, evidence,
mandates, licenses, pending services and expiry to named successors.
Reissue only scoped access. Protect original expiry and due dates.
Pay for overlapping obligations and explicitly accept each task before
relying on it. Record an existing exposure or inability to pay without
turning that fact into power to stop an entitlement.

Next compare one authorized service exercise with a competent ordinary
route and a qualified supplier path that loses the original provider
and a shared credential. Test one payment, new intake, a pending review
and unsupported legacy meaning under actual paid cover. Verify deadlines
and received outcomes and price the overlap. Use no private test evidence
without safe authority and storage. Reopen when a provider or dependency
changes, the alternate is unstaffed, someone cannot use the route or
a simpler alternative is demonstrably better. Actual participation,
independent qualified review, adoption, readiness and outcomes remain open.
