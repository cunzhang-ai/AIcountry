# Actual responsibility and a bounded total burden

*V07–V08; part of [C08/19](../19-regulation-and-bounded-enforcement.md).*

**V07 — Prove whose conduct and whose fault.** A natural person answers only
for their own scheduled act/omission, prior duty, actual control, ability and
specified fault. Corporate office, kinship, public employment, AI deployment
or worker presence is insufficient. A body requires Q07's proved authorized
policy or delegated controller's act within the function and the relevant
fault; unrelated people's information cannot be pooled into fictional intent.
For class A, establish an attributable failure of reasonable organizational
care, actual ability, the precise duty and the V06 notice/opportunity. A
policy manual alone neither proves nor defeats the actual case.

Directors are not convicted automatically with their firm; a firm is not
exempt because the actual responsible human cannot yet be named. Public
providers meet the same substantive duties; an internal state fine creates
no national resources and cannot replace repair or individual accountability.
A controller's own culpable policy remains attributable across subsidiaries,
but ownership alone does not authorize whole-group fines or asset seizure.

**V08 — One proportionate punishment and actual needs protection.** Apply
Q02's actual resources/essential-cost formula and zero minimum, Q03's 25%
surplus collection limit and fixed 24-month adult/12-month child expiry.
Poverty never becomes prison, forced work, interest or a larger fine. Means
review is reachable without a fee. Class A/B same-course totals use the
highest applicable band, not multiplied duties/days/accounts; a child's total
cannot exceed 90 units. V and K punishment for the same factual course is
jointly assessed under Q01/P15 and existing Q ceilings, with full credit;
no second prosecutor evades finality by renaming an offense administrative.

Body fines use Q08's verified revenue/gain, same-course cap, proportionality
and worker/user/repair effects. Fines rank no higher than ordinary unsecured
claims in insolvency; victim property and secured rights retain B/U/M law.
No guarantee, license fee or compulsory monitor disguises extra punishment.
Actual repair and traced net proceeds have separate proof and no duplicate
recovery; inability to fund them is an unmet claim, not a fictional payment.

A usable V charge/notice must occur within three years of completed conduct
for A, six for B, with only Q12's proved concealment/evasion toll of at most
three extra years. Continuing conduct is not an endless old effect. Apply
P15 finality, Q11 limited adverse disclosure, and prospective lawful migration/
leniency; no revival of an expired case or public blacklist by model update.

[ER19’s VD12-only body alternative](../../C03/10/08-precise-fines-and-continuing-duties.md) expressly adds twice proved unlawful campaign value to the possible upper ceiling. One traceable flow/course is not counted as both receipt and spending; late return does not legalize knowing used finance. All other V/Q maxima, source proof, attribution, zero minimum, restoration and same-course protections remain.
