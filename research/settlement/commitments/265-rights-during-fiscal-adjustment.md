# Design352. Protect minimum services and independent remedies in fiscal adjustment

[FB36–FB41](../../country/C06/62/07-rights-and-distribution-during-adjustment.md)
keeps dated funds, paid review and service receipt in the same budget.
An unresolved shortfall keeps its original duty and remedy.

The [decision](../../2026-11-rights-during-fiscal-adjustment/decision-record.md)
compares uniform cuts, broader prohibitions and targeted adjustment.
Actual authority, funded mitigation and affected outcomes remain open.
