# TR01–TR04. Give elected members a real choice before commitment

*Design223 · Part of [treaty commitments](../15-democratic-treaty-commitments.md).*

**TR01 — Classify by legal and practical effect.** Prior Assembly law
sets materiality by rights, jurisdiction, durable expenditure, guarantees,
tax, force, territorial or collective claims, critical service and the
difficulty of reversal. Consider cumulative related acts. Material entry,
provisional application, withdrawal, suspension, material renewal
requiring fresh consent and changes to a material commitment require
the elected route. A side letter, nonbinding instrument
or public-enterprise contract has its own legal nature. It cannot implement
a material domestic commitment outside the required source, vote and
rights process. Prior statute may authorize routine technical cooperation
within fixed powers and funding, with reasons, published limits and
challenge. No new power follows from a description or translated label.

**TR02 — Negotiate within an actual public mandate.** Cabinet may consult
and explore options under existing authority. Before negotiating a major
commitment, put public objectives, authority, affected scope, red lines and
expected costs before the Assembly under prior law. The Assembly authorizes a bounded
negotiating mandate with update and expiration requirements. Protect
negotiating positions only for a specific lawful reason and time. Use
protected opposition access and independent review under PE, with paid
translation and expertise. Hear materially affected residents, workers,
local authorities, collective rights holders and people abroad through
usable means. Applicable consent duties remain distinct from consultation.
Negotiation supplies no permission to bind another public, exercise force,
spend unappropriated money or accept a final text.

**TR03 — Present the whole operative package in time to examine it.**
The Clerk records the complete publicly accessible text, authentic
languages, related instruments, authorized reservations, changes, legal
effects, reciprocal conditions, implementation, funding and exit account.
Where a lawful restricted annex is necessary, provide exact protected
access to voting members and qualified support, with safe public reasons
and a usable public account. Public legal powers, generally applicable
rights, material fiscal ceilings and affected scope must be public.
Under PE, identify which evidence remains unavailable and whose judgment
or implementation could be affected. Compare retain, decline, conditional
acceptance if externally permitted and renegotiation. Track actual local
and foreign receiving, exposure and dissent. A public explanatory
translation is separate from the authentic text. Preserve material
unresolved language and require its scope to be understood before consent.

**TR04 — Require an affirmative vote on exact terms.** The ordinary
earliest vote is after 30 elapsed calendar days from complete public and
required protected receipt. Completeness refers to the specified package
and lawful access, with uncertainties recorded. The Clerk records actual
receipt and has no general merits or readiness veto. Give a meaningful protected hearing, opposition
time and access to distinct alternatives before the vote. A material
change requires fresh receipt and review for the changed commitment.
Select at least floor(N/2)+1 actual yes votes and that many present for
ordinary material approval, with N the full statutory Assembly including
vacancies. Preserve stronger constitutional, territorial, collective,
force, budget and rights procedures. A separate recorded procedural
vote with at least ceil(2N/3) actual yes votes and that many present
may shorten the interval to no less than seven elapsed calendar days
on exact reasons of real urgency, with usable opposition hearing and
protected access. Record actual harm from waiting. Late administrative
preparation alone is insufficient. Neither vote can waive a higher-law
safeguard.
Silence, a procedural vote, absent staff or an unavailable sitting
does not authorize the commitment. Prior lawful routine and protective
activity continues within its own scope.
