# Design508 decision record

**Basis and choice.** The [Design507 baseline](baseline.json) and
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) distinguish actual authority from
research selection. Strengthen proposed A12 from a lawful sphere of choice
to an enforceable effective sphere. National law fixes common protections
and can require a common method only for a demonstrable need unavailable
through equivalent funded methods. Provide individually claimable remedies
for national coercion and local denial. Amend the statutory or protected
charter allocation through its own authority. Retain national residual
decision where qualified and put regional or negotiated constituent forms
to their competent decision where the comparison supports them.

**Mechanism and objections.** The [comparison](../settlement/territorial-competence/01-common-protection-local-choice.md)
uses the same service duty across unequal locations, with alternatives for
national uniformity, equivalent local provision and a weak local-only route.
A common method can protect interoperability. The stricter justification
may add decision time. Separate a minimal interoperable interface from
authority over procurement, workflows and receiving. Local discretion can
exclude vulnerable people, and funded substitutes with review impose real
costs. Independent review can enforce rights and scope while placed,
elected decision-makers retain material political choices. Federal and
asymmetric structures need their own real appointment, revenue, oversight
and shared-resource authority. They may be justified even where the ordinary
Assembly has more than enough docket time.

**Evidence and continued duties.** Three parallel agent workstreams covered
territorial authority, finance and overlapping rights. Their shared sources
and constructed cases establish no affected consent or qualified final review.
Keep valid duties, old dates, local lawful choices and separately required
collective dispositions. Carry due transfers, records, paid continuity and
remedies through a limited backstop or successor. Recheck permanent fiscal
restrictions separately from late settlement. The [sources](sources.md)
distinguish written comparator rules from outcomes.

**Next decision.** For an actual adopter's first territorial docket, compare
the existing map with one exact transfer of a service or power. Record the
original office and effective date, electorate, affected nonvoters, rights
holders, financed service catchments, accepting successor and paid review.
Test a due service, a challenge and a next call with adverse funds or staff.
Reopen on unusable local choice, common-floor failure, externally displaced
harm, a credible narrower interoperable method or a legitimate distinct
constituent claim. No charter or transfer has been enacted or operated.
