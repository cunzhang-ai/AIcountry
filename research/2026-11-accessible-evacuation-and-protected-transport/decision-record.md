# Design290. Accessible evacuation and protected transport

10 November 2026 · Research selection · No actual enactment

## Baseline and decision

[Design289](baseline.json) retains civil rescue and its published command,
reserved capacities, the CP protected-source exclusions and PS standing law
limited to scheduled backup power. RC already requires actual receiving
and safe return. The open case concerns a transport reserve whose two
legitimate uses are incompatible despite separate service promises. Apply
the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md). Codex assisted research and
comparison. Actual affected input and independent review remain open.

## Alternatives and selection

- **Use only existing sector routes and general sharing.** This avoids a
  new dedicated cost where separate services can really use the same
  transport in sequence. In the declared closure the shared route
  cannot meet both useful times. A different commitment name or
  departmental contract does not provide a second vehicle or receiver.
- **Protect independent critical civic transport and buy usable
  shared rescue readiness.** Select this mixed architecture where it
  can meet concurrent requirements. Keep only explicitly scheduled and
  actually uncommitted public reserve within the proposed shared
  priority. Give feasible imminent grave bodily protection priority
  inside that pool. Preserve the unmet civic access claim if no lawful
  usable route remains. This can still leave a consequential election
  failure. It is a value choice with real capacity and review costs.
- **Maintain dedicated staffed public routes for both functions.**
  This limits exposure to common loss and competition for the standby
  vehicle. Compare it when protected calls recur or a region cannot
  fund a genuinely independent contract. It needs more regular
  staff and capital in the authored case.

[RC29–RC33](../country/C10/13/07-accessible-evacuation-and-protected-transport.md)
selects this transport-specific rule. The
[comparison](../settlement/accessible-evacuation/README.md) tests a
genuine missed civic ride, a compatible sequence, missing destination,
dated funding and a failed second call. The
[primary evidence](sources.md) qualifies the same-fleet and
inaccessible-receiving failures. The priority and unit costs are
authored. Separate existing CP and PS dispositions retain their scope.

## Public control and migration

Before relying on the design, competent elected bodies must pass a
prospective ordinary source schedule and exact financing, consistent
with protected electoral succession and applicable holds. The dispatch
actor uses its narrow source and ordinary appointment chain. Rescue,
clinical, electoral and court actors retain their own decisions. In an
actual loss, preserve existing safe actions, report missing access and
provide useful-time independent review. Later remedies or election
determinations remain governed by their own existing law.

Preserve old contracts, terms, booked duties, worker rights, consent
and evidence. Move open claims and physical assets only through actual
lawful accepted custody with a paid route for review and enforcement.
Recurring dependence on an undersupplied vehicle invites manufactured
scarcity and selective loss of access. Compare readiness across
communities, including non-digital and unregistered residents. Protect
private testimony and independent access to dispatch evidence.
Prior approval of a reserve supplies no decision about its later use.

## Next bounded decision

Price and test one route with willing local authorities and participants,
including a real compatible vehicle, named relief driver, accepting site,
one blocked corridor and one time-critical independent civic trip.
Verify cash, private-safe evidence, accessible direct intake, genuine
independence and the actual second turnaround. Reopen if a protected
route was displaced, if reliance repeatedly denies access, or if a
dedicated or distributed rival provides better protection on the same
scope. No live readiness, cost or better outcome is established.
