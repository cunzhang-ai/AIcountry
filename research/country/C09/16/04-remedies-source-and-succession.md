# WS16–WS20. Duties survive failure, disagreement and renewal

*Part of [water allocation and service](../16-water-allocation-and-service.md).*

**WS16 — Distinct remedies and execution.** Failed supply/connection, allocation,
tariff and unsafe water involve different facts/law. Director/Steward give
reasoned receipt/correction under AP and earlier E/sector clocks. Outside
Reviewer acts within its own delegation; ordinary V/OJ courts review actual
cases, grant interim relief and require lawful execution. TD07/TS health/safety,
AA audit, PD privacy and ordinary prosecution remain distinct. Public ownership
is no exemption; a favorable model or order is not received safe water. Fund
unimplicated source/sample/records access, assistance, actual judgment payer and
scoped executor outside supplier final control. Own-case review stays external.

**WS17 — Whole-country source and failure.** Local service charges, express
elected G/local equalization/public-purpose payments and only actual lawful
financing fund this choice. Resource permission, tariff, NF tax, private
collateral, restoration security, capital and owned FX differ; no new water tax,
unrestricted water rent, autonomous fund or implicit G guarantee. RF/FP/NF/NP
source/borrowing/priorities remain. Join public/private source/extraction, home/
farm/industry prices, actual shifts/lab/reviewer independence, treatment/power/
chemicals, repair/capital/stock, foreign inputs, private records/non-AI receiving,
old liabilities and paid transition once. Original37 functions/later duties
continue; old bids do not price this complete scope. Full costs/sources unknown.

Exit uses actual lawful assets/contracts/worker arrangements and preauthorized
C04 bounded essential-service fallback/OP succession. No Director seizes private
pipes/crew, guarantees every lender or extends emergency command. Name paid
accepted receiving, records/stock/continuity source and return to local control;
absence is an unmet guarantee. A national shortfall neither removes personal
water claims nor shifts them unpaid to another council. Actual NP/E/elected
correction and rights persist, not executive tariff shock or readiness veto.

**WS18 — Future evidence and AI under public control.** Users/communities may
communicate shared needs through meetings, observations, supported speech,
simulations and bounded agents. Expression, hydrological interpretation,
delegation, consent and authority differ; convergent AI/efficiency predictions
cannot resolve distribution conflicts. Disclose/examine unfamiliar customary/
future meanings with help rather than erase them. Keep safe original readings/
corrections, qualified worker knowledge, purpose-limited private evidence and
independent usable channels. Household routines/testimony/complete-life logs are
not permanently public/model-training data. DP/HC limited tasks, paid human/
other-provider receiving and sector stop/restore remain; no AI water life score.

**WS19 — Prospective method and exact paid successors.** Proposed start is first
1January at least12calendar months after coordinated WS/TD/source law, naming
actual date/pools/rights/source, qualified Steward/Director/Reviewer and exact
paid OP/T old permit/title/collective/user/worker/pension/contract/concession/
security/creditor/record/foreign successors and overlap. Finite-grant/default
rules apply only to genuinely new future permissions/systems. Actual existing
grants/tariffs/conditions and accrued claims retain their law until expressly
authorized prospective transition; no retroactive expiry/nationalization,
automatic compensation formula or reset of AP/TD/E/CP/constitutional clocks.
Existing essentials continue. Actual A8/RR/other holds retain their own scoped
procedure; research changes no software, constitution or contribution permission.

**WS20 — Real rival countries, not institutional inevitability.** Retain/repair
licensed plural service can avoid reorganization/preserve skills while leaving
owner/allocator dominance. Transferable allocations with protected floors can
allow adjustment but risk concentration, dry rights and changed returns. Elected
catchment government represents a shared basin but adds a tier/boundary/transition
burden. Community/cooperative management can retain relationships/context while
excluding outsiders or depending on unequal capacity. Direct public default
concentrates employer/budget/source power and may suppress maintenance/criticism;
a career Reviewer may share its patron. Labels establish no winner.

National/local form, ten-year new-grant ceiling, notice and cost method are
normative hypotheses, not optimal hydrology or causal public-owner superiority.
Reopen/reject on exclusion, failed ecological/essential receiving, wrong return/
lag accounting, regressive fixed charges, capture, unpayable sources or a better
whole-cost/control rival. Actual affected participation, hydrology, qualified
legal/engineering/independent review, authority and better lives remain unknown.
Next inspect integrated ordinary/adverse/paid-country largest positive/source/
control gap, not an automatic water-engineering, tariff or drought study series.
