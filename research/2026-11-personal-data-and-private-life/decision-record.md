## Diagnosis, values and distinct architectures

Information supports care, proof, coordination and correction; it also enables
prediction, exclusion and concentrated political/economic power. Erasing all
records can destroy earned claims/defence; universal retention can expose a life.
Dependence can make a purchased consent coerced. Technical proofs can reduce
fields while preserving correlation or excluding device-less people. These are
mechanisms/risks to test, not measured national causal rankings.
Values include agency, private relationships, safe care, truthfully limited proof,
public criticism, equality, usable remedies and future communication diversity.
Conflicts are not settled by a privacy score, output volume or an AI majority.

Retain 95/sector-only avoids new administration but leaves common bases and the
supervisor undefined. Data-property/consent markets can reward optional reuse,
but dependants may sell under pressure and cannot consent for other people.
Local-proof/personal-mediator primacy can reduce disclosure but creates device,
issuer, revocation and proof-of-claims dependencies. Court-only enforcement avoids
a new chief but can leave hidden systemic/small harms undiscovered. A ministry
has clearer majority replacement but can suppress cases against government; a
board adds views/continuity and also quorum/capture/expert cost.
Select **purpose-limited plural custody** as legal responsibility, with optional
proof/local methods and genuine optional bargaining, not a universal wallet or
state life directory. Those rivals may displace the choice with stronger complete
access/control/cost evidence; they are not presumed free features of this model.
