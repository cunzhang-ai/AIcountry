# Design264 decision record

**Baseline and choice.** Design263 assigns independent career
budget analysis under the Assembly, member costing and timely
existing-record access. Most rights activate through a request
or an ordinary budget step. The design does not specify standing
independent review when a material package is publicly announced
before a member request or formal vote. Select DB21–DB25 within
the [working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision handoff](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

**Evidence and rival diagnoses.** The historical UK case separates
preparation, an omitted accompanying forecast, provisional
government costings and a later forecast of changed policy. A
forecast can be missing because a controller withholds its start,
because inputs are late or because staff cannot complete it.
These need different remedies. A real source or physical shortage
still exists when officials agree about the estimate. Original
facts and dissent need a usable scrutiny date. The UK records are
not affected-party participation in AIcountry's selected design.
[Sources](sources.md)

**Alternatives.** Retain Treasury costing and member requests as
the baseline. The 2024 UK commission-and-fallback form gives an
existing forecaster a way to act after an omitted request. A
separate fiscal institution could provide resilience or add
appointment, funding and reporting costs. Select standing
initiation by already separated Assembly analysts and cumulative
materiality under prior law. Preserve protected minority work,
publish unresolved evidence and keep elected voting authority.
Do not give forecasters a veto. Use the same enacted objective
conditions for an optional release, with smaller alternatives if
expressly voted. The authored window favors a preparation tranche
and a smaller lawful fallback after common loss. It makes no
country-wide causal or affordability claim.
[Authored comparison](../settlement/independent-fiscal-forecast/02-authored-forecast-and-release-comparison.md)

**Control and objections.** Automatic workload can crowd out
individual members or invite nuisance submissions. Apply declared
materiality and a scoped first response, coordinate work without
merging proposals and protect minority capacity. Trace who
appoints, pays and controls access and dissemination. Parliamentary
staff can also be captured. Preserve independent record access,
external audit, protection for dissent and direct review. Assess
public cost and private records with proportionate disclosure.
An expert forecast can systematically overstate or understate
risk. Publish matched assumptions and actual later evidence.
Existing urgent action and lawful standing duties keep their own
authority and clocks. An emergency label earns no open-ended release.

**Compatibility and transition.** The new duty begins on a fixed
prospective date under competent law with actual funded staff and
data access. Carry earlier valid decisions, appropriation limits,
pending scrutiny, protected records, contract rights and dates.
Only changed assumptions within their proven scope can be reused.
No money, appointing role or substantive vote is transferred by
a review. No later evidence reverses an earlier lawful obligation.
A proposed fiscal option and an authorized payment remain separate.

**Next bounded decision.** Have Assembly analysts and the Treasury
compare one actual first-budget package with a real opposition
alternative on the same deadlines. Reconcile a contested revenue
date, staffing claim, standing services, household receiving, an
executable court order and election access. Deliver independent
scoped reports and exact release choices with staffed review.
Set the analyst time and budget, actual authority and protective
limits before use. Reopen on common capture, chronic uncosted
work, emergency splitting, a failed real receiving route or a
simpler qualified alternative. Observed national superiority,
adoption and paid implementation remain open.
