# Two regions, one flexible team and a second call

This is a hypothetical flood-response comparison. Region A reports
first and needs a qualified field team by hour 6. Region B reports
before the same safe decision time and needs one by hour 8. Both
tasks are in the same protected class. The asset custodian and local
incident leads confirm compatibility and arrival times. Region C
retains its own single minimum team throughout. That team is outside
the national call list. Assume both calls are known before hour 1,
when the two paid national assignments must be made.

| Team | A ready to work | B ready to work | Later availability |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Flexible | Hour 3 | Hour 5 | B second task no earlier than hour 24 |
| Western | Hour 4 | Hour 9 | No second B task by hour 20 |
| Independent second shift | After hour 8 | After hour 8 | Can start B's second task at hour 18 |

Each early assignment uses one unit of already usable cash. The
combined budget funds at most two early assignments. A later B task
needs a qualified team by hour 20. Only the second shift can reach it
in time. Keeping that shift paid and available costs one additional
unit through an independently executable source by hour 18.
In this fixture the elected source is a one-unit reallocation from
an unbound optional activity. The cost is inclusive for the assumed
interval. No expected reimbursement is available before hour 24.

| Route on the same calls | A by hour 6 | B by hour 8 | B second by hour 20 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Request-order routing gives A the flexible team | Yes | No | No |
| Compare joint assignments within two units | Yes, western | Yes, flexible | No |
| Same assignments plus paid second shift | Yes, western | Yes, flexible | Yes |
| Same budget and loss of flexible team | Yes, western | No | Yes |
| Same teams but blocked release of third unit | Yes, western | Yes, flexible | No |

The lost flexible team leaves an earlier failure even if the later
shift works. In all cases, C retains its minimum. An area is never
required to classify a late resource as timely to keep its claims.
The comparison isolates the value of matching and a real independent
shift. Permanently stationing adequate independent teams in each
region is a structural rival. Its full costs, proximity and common
exposures require a separate price and may beat a pooled arrangement
where routes are slow or shocks recur. None of the fixture's times
or costs are observations from the United States.
