# A separate criminal case and independently counted deprivation

*CD09–CD10; part of [C08/21](../21-conflict-detention-and-release.md).*

**CD09 — Same ordinary civilian justice for our personnel and captives.**
The proposed national law expressly gives ordinary independent civilian
criminal courts jurisdiction over our service members and protected foreign
persons for the same actual offenses/procedure, with published assignment,
qualified defense, original evidence, funded expertise and outside-command
investigation. No victor's special judge, executive military commission or
AI decides guilt. Actual territorial/personal/international competence and
immunity remain P21/G09/G10 questions, not effects of wearing a uniform.

Recognize lawful combatant privilege where the actual applicable law grants
it: lawful enemy acts of war are not ordinary murder merely because they
oppose us. Protected POW treatment does not itself give every person combatant
privilege or excuse a war crime. Specific K/G criminal conduct needs actual
act/context, fault, defenses and legal basis at the time. NIAC has no new
blanket status immunity or membership-guilt rule in this Act. A peace process
may seek lawful participation amnesty through competent law, with original
victim/actual international obligations, not a research self-pardon or
impunity for crimes which the applicable law requires be addressed.

Keep P disclosure, exclusion, presumption, counsel, public reasons/safely
protected sources and appeal. Relevant GCIII protective trial notices and
defense preparation must be honored where applicable; if they require delay,
that never creates additional pretrial custody time. No adverse defense
from silence, nationality, calling our own conduct unlawful or refusing an
oath of allegiance. POW protections continue despite charge/conviction.

**CD10 — Concurrent grounds do not reset each other's clocks.** Every order
states status custody, any actual criminal remand and each separate starting
event, factual purpose, expiry, evidence and actual place/custodian. P08–P10
criminal counsel/24-hour hearing and first/renewal limits continue. For POW
criminal pretrial confinement choose the shortest applicable P aggregate,
90 continuous days or three calendar months from actual relevant confinement;
three months is not automatically 90 days. This implements a stricter national
comparison with the inspected GCIII Article 103 limit, not a claim of complete treaty
conformity. Applicable earlier/stronger protections prevail.

Criminal time starts when restraint is actually used for that case, including
a dual-purpose or relabelled investigative hold; the state cannot conceal it
as POW custody. Independently lawful status-only captivity is not automatically
all criminal remand, and criminal expiry does not itself decide status.
Each continuing basis must independently survive review; no new claim label
adds another detention term after the true ground ends. Give full credit
for all case-attributable deprivation and penal burdens under P16/P22; no
double counting/omission because it occurred abroad, before a new charge,
through a contractor or under another recorded purpose.

When hostilities/status custody ends, a real criminal remand or sentence may
continue only within its own actual lawful grounds/remaining limits. GCIII Article 119
is no automatic new hold until a prosecutor chooses to finish. Without such
a valid remaining ground, actual release follows. Courts decide disputed
credit and delay with source evidence; software time arithmetic cannot prove
purpose, custody necessity, guilt, treaty scope or a superior justice system.
