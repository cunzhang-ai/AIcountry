# C09. Safe infrastructure and mobility as usable public goods

15 October 2026 · Design 78 · Proposed research, not operating permission

[C08 TS/TD](../C08/22-technical-safety-and-accountable-operation.md) now owns
technical entry, safe operating duties, independent evidence and bounded
correction. C09 stewards remain responsible for actual safe access, maintenance
and accepted alternatives; a ministry's certificate is not a delivered service.
Planning, ownership, tariffs, ecology, professional competence and emergency
force retain their separate lawful owners.

For roads, choose streets/transport that tolerate foreseeable human mistakes:
usable walking/wheeling/cycling routes, safe crossings, separation where higher
speed is actually allowed, accessible public transport, safe maintenance and
credible rescue. TD01's 30/50/80/100 km/h maxima are proposed choices; 30 on
mixed built-up streets is informed by WHO guidance, while the remaining numbers
need actual geometry, mobility, disability, emergency and harm evidence. No
speed-limit sign or enforcement revenue substitutes for safer street design.

Rail, aircraft, water transport and logistics stewards demonstrate actual safe
route, personnel, maintenance, communications, supply and receiving, with the
relevant TD02–TD04 authority. A future automated system can meet a proved
functional standard without a nominal powerless human monitor. No model can
create a foreign corridor, port consent or new military flight mandate.

Building, dam, industrial and public-water owners map old assets, known hazards,
repair priorities, worker/tenant rights, actual alternative shelter/water and
qualified inspections. A new code cannot simply make ordinary homes unlawful
or use a closure ticket to erase the public receiving duty. A real imminent
hazard may still require stopping use before alternatives arrive; the resulting
unmet duty and harm stay visible for lawful remedy and elected funding.

Sector operators preserve original readings, repairs, accidents, uncertainty
and independent source access, with correction history and appropriate privacy.
The same inspector, sample, truck, spare part or paid shift counts once across
water, food, hospitals, elections, defenses and technical investigation. C06/27
names incremental assessment/repair duties; actual sources/costs remain unknown.

Regional/shared laboratories, all-public operation or a different ownership model
remain real alternatives if the selected plural regime cannot provide safe access
without excessive entry costs/capture. Neither technological novelty nor more
regulators proves better lives. Next join this candidate to the whole-country
law/resource/public-control audit rather than presume sector safety completes it.

[NSE01–NSE06](28-sleep-and-cumulative-noise.md) requires a material
route change to account for sleep exposure and event patterns in the
receiving area. Preserve lawful essential journeys and verified mitigation.

[SW01–SW06](11/01-shared-street-works-and-protected-access.md) assigns
coordinated street works, paid accessible routes, independent checks
and received reopening. Sector and emergency authority remain separate.
