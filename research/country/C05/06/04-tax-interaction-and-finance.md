# Income clauses I10–I12: tax interaction and democratic finance

*Design 58 proposed clauses; [income package](../06-income-floor-and-safe-resources.md).*

**I10 — Joint incidence rather than separate good-looking schedules.** Tax
and income authorities shall publish a joint reference and source-backed
assessment of personal/corporate/consumption/land taxes, insurance
contributions, ordinary top-ups, pensions, cost support, housing, care fees
and actual payment timing. Compare individuals, safe/unsafe shared
arrangements, children, disability, different places and irregular work.
For each wage increase show actual after-cost disposable resources, benefit
withdrawal and expense/eligibility thresholds. Continuous ordinary taper
cannot excuse a childcare, housing or subsidy cliff in another program.
Expose combined losses and return a material correction to elected law;
an agency cannot secretly revise another statute or an individual's award.

The candidate ordinary taper protects some net earned gains for fixed
needs; it is not proof that every job, cost jump or in-kind loss makes a
person better off. Examine participation, quality of work, time, choice
and public cost alongside marginal rates. Income need shall not depend
on accepting unsafe employment or an ideological work test.

**I11 — Elected parameters and independent source control.** The Assembly
sets the basket method, protected cash content, disregard, taper, asset
buffer/draw, tax/contribution schedules and prospective transition.
Officers administer them; separate evidence custody, fiscal audit,
independent courts and opposition challenge suppressions and errors.
Neither Treasury nor a benefits model gets a policy veto. Use protected
data access and publish safe aggregates, not claimant bank histories.
No contributor or model count establishes affected-party assent or reviewer
independence. New appointments and providers use the existing public-control
and court chains, with costed alternate payment and records.

**I12 — One funding and delivery package.** Reconcile recurring gross
transfer payments, actual taxes and contributions, old/new overlap,
administration, private work/support transfers, qualified service staff,
housing/supply, imports, ecology and executed remedies. Insurance
contributions remain assigned to their lawful insurance obligations;
an unpriced reserve liability or unused forecast is not general floor revenue.
Private parent support is a transfer of existing resources, not national GDP
or a second public receipt. Formula entitlement and annual appropriation
remain separate from due-date cash and physical delivery.

The [reference comparison](../../../settlement/income-floor/README.md)
holds explicit prices and wages and finds unfunded launches; it does not
estimate demand, inflation, work responses or national wellbeing. No chosen
rate, lower priced basket, hypothetical extra tax or assumed AI productivity
can certify adequacy and feasibility without the complete new package.
Where funding fails use the elected shortfall/continuity route, preserve
existing claims and record non-delivery; do not silently shrink the basket
to fit an attractive annual total. Compare meaningful pooled/universal
alternatives and rights-preserving prospective changes openly.

The [demand-consistent comparator](../../C06/21-demand-consistent-operating-reference.md)
compares I05 with materially different earned-credit, legacy-accrual and
payout schedules. It does not amend I05 or release restricted pension
claims. Normal annual closure, timely payment, productive supply and
intergenerational security are separate tests; assess incidence and prior
funding before selecting an alternative.

[FD joint incidence](../../C06/35/01-prices-tax-and-personal-resources.md) now
attributes work/pension/capital tax once before I05 and tests price-adjusted
needs, basic Y, earned-pension indexation and consumption. Attribution/rates
are proposed hypotheses, not authority to reset actual history or tax gross
floor payments. Four source bundles fail; no old receipt envelope fills them.

[I21–I24](07-source-control-context-and-transition.md) now joins component,
20% ordinary cash-need, received-service, replacement and time duties to the
same elected finance/career/remedy chain. Price included/incremental/overlap/
replaced/unknown people and stock once; old bids do not fund these additions.
No new revenue, self-appropriation or readiness veto arises from this research.

[WF17–WF28](../../C06/58/05-observed-population-income-and-retirement.md)
now supplies published population/income/workforce scales and separate nonlinear
incidence/receipt counterexamples. Same total wages do not determine tax/I05;
imputed owner rent, family equivalisation, missing basic and provider costs
cannot create received personal cash or a national source. Existing I10–I12
rates, remedies and source-law obligations remain; full matched costs are open.

[I30–I35](09-coordinated-benefit-withdrawal.md) gives scheduled income-linked
changes a coordinated rule, separately funded temporary correction and
independent review. Existing taxes, duties and award rules continue.
