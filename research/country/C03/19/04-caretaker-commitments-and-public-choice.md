# HT13–HT17. Preserve the successor's real choices

*Design277 · Proposed rules for commitments before and during handover.*

**HT13 — Record when a decision really binds.** For a material rule,
appointment, contract, grant, guarantee or other commitment crossing
a political handover, the competent issuer records its source,
exact text, status, authorizing act, relevant signatures, legal
effect, first payment or enforcement, and continuation and exit
terms. Distinguish announcement, proposal, signed obligation and
future effective date. Record lawful optional renewal deadlines and
the risk of becoming bound through inaction. Route completed and
pending matters to their lawful receivers. A press release, queued
agent instruction or earlier political approval does not prove a
binding act. Preserve accrued rights and statutory duties.

**HT14 — Expose commitments made before the caretaker boundary.**
Use ordinary DB, DR and PE scrutiny for material related decisions
that constrain a later government. In the regular budget and when
the existing statutory caretaker trigger occurs, publish the scoped
inventory of commitments, unexercised options, legal dates, costs,
unfunded tails and unresolved challenges. Aggregate materially linked
acts even when spread among entities or instruments. Include major
commitments with no first-year payment, automatic renewals and
uncommenced instruments. Opposition and independent reviewers receive
usable facts and alternatives before the original useful deadline.
The inventory creates no new pre-election freeze or third-party veto.
A competent government may still make lawful choices through their
ordinary procedures.

**HT15 — During caretaker, use the least binding lawful route that
protects due work.** Classify an act by what it would do and when,
including a material amendment, a renewal, an affiliate transaction
and a later effective date. Existing lawful payments, standing
obligations, ordinary appointments and independently authorized
urgent action follow their actual scopes. Hold a still optional
major commitment that the caretaker lacks authority to make.
When necessary under prior law, compare a short usable bridge,
continuation on existing terms and any narrower qualified option.
Record the earliest loss if action waits, why a less binding route
would fail, resources, exact expiry, and actual review. If a new
material commitment is needed beyond existing power, seek competent
elected law. Consultation supplies scrutiny within its own role.
A missing response or opposition agreement cannot create legal power.

**HT16 — Protect review before rights or options are lost.** A member,
affected person or eligible counterparty may use existing evidence,
procurement or court routes within their lawful standing. A competent
court can grant scoped relief under its own law. The reviewer sees
the original source, disputed scope, proposed alternatives and
affected dates. Pay for independently reachable review during
transition. A challenged action does not automatically stay lawful
public protection or void a third party's rights. Record any actual
stay, its end and independently lawful interim protection. Preserve
the original first notice and deadlines across handoff. Apply
[DP38–DP42](../../C11/09/09-shared-dependencies-and-safe-changes.md)
to stale or disputed digital instructions.

**HT17 — Carry a real choice and the true liability into the first
budget.** The successor records what remains enforceable, what can
lawfully be changed, and which material prospective items require a
new vote, approval or source. Price accrued liabilities, paid exit,
replacement and ongoing service as separate uses. Preserve earned
wages, residents' claims and independent functions. A new majority
may change future policy through ordinary law. It cannot cancel a
valid prior obligation merely by classifying it as an outgoing act.
Verify execution and missed service under the original deadlines.
AI may reconcile dates, compare the same decision through affiliated
entities and flag a hidden renewal. It cannot determine legal effect
or choose whether an elected government takes office. Reopen if
timing permits circumvention, urgent action repeatedly fails, or a
better ordinary procurement route protects the same duties.
