# Design351 decision on coordinated benefit withdrawal

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design350, I01–I29, I10 joint incidence, existing care and housing
duties, elected appropriations and the working framework. Existing
design recognizes combined withdrawals but leaves correction to
later elected action. Specify an income-only no-loss rule for scheduled
programmes, a bounded funded bridge and a separate actual service test.

Select prospective joint calibration with sector-owned decisions.
Broader universal or less assessment-dependent provision remains a
real rival. The project assistant authored this choice. Competent
elected bodies enact the schedule and sources. Named officers
administer it. Existing review and court authority remain independent.

## Alternatives and objections

A warning calculator costs less and can reveal a cliff without
resolving it. A permanent case-by-case compensator can be precise
and add stigma, disputes and complex administration. A universal
base may reduce withdrawal while requiring a different fiscal
settlement. Select a transparent coordinated schedule and a short
bridge when expressly enacted and needed. Compare flat and positive
gains, service access, recurring cost and distribution.

The [primary sources](sources.md) illustrate combined calculations
and their scope. They do not establish the candidate's cap, selected
periods or actual cost. A cash result can conceal a lost care place
or private work costs. Use matched facts and report them separately.

## Transition and reopening

Adopt prospectively with dated funds, secure evidence, staffed
administration, independent review and actual receiving. Preserve
earlier awards, claims and urgent rights. No qualified independent
review, affected participation, adoption or actual delivery has occurred.

Exercise a threshold crossing with willing workers, carers and service
owners. Test a late wage, a lost place and the first month after
the bridge. Compare net usable resources, care access, burdens,
government cost and take-up. Reopen on a shifted cliff, repeated
bridge reliance, unequal access, excessive cost or a stronger rival.
