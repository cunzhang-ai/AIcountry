## Diagnosis and genuine alternatives

Design100 has a two-vote/count and electoral-administration candidate, but leaves
objective entry, support allocation and exact pre-vote payment unfinished.
Equal formal votes without entry/reach/remedy can protect an incumbent monopoly.
Unlimited low-gate entry can also overwhelm the ballot, print, media and courts.
Both problems are structural; a central AI deciding which parties are serious is
not a solution. Values: equal standing, plural speech/association, meaningful
replacement, private agency, finite public commitments and useful-time relief.
Incidence, coercion, attention, cost, behavior and actual better lives are unknown.

[Whole choices](../country/C03/10/09-whole-choices-migration-and-open-design.md)
compare open filing/unlimited low support, one exclusive1%, selected two active1%,
two2%, equal cash, past-vote/donation matching, pure vouchers and no-cash public
forums. **Reject the initial unlimited nonexclusive0.1% hypothesis** as a complete
guaranteed paid-access architecture: it can approach one list per eligible person.
One exclusive signature bounds field more but restricts minority permission; two
active slots permit two different entrants, with tougher organizing/hoarding costs
than0.1%. No average score resolves that disagreement or proves this compromise.
