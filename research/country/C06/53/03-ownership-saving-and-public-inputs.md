# Ownership changes power and losses, not physical productivity by itself

**PW08 — Compare ownership on the same material recipe.** Keep OE/WL/IC rather
than creating another economic planning office. For any qualified productive
investment, identify the actual owner, loss bearer and decision replacement:

| Form | Practical control and failure obligation |
|---|---|
| Private firm | Actual capital providers and OE worker powers; investors bear lawful residual loss, not workers' unpaid due wages; competition/insolvency and direct remedies remain |
| Voluntary cooperative | Actual members elect/replace organs; outside nonvoting capital does not buy member votes; disclosed member risk and WL employment survive; no forced membership or personal guarantee as the price of basic rights |
| Commercial public owner | Assembly/local elected owner approves mandate, gross capital and loss exposure; board/career execution, public accounts, opposition and independent review remain; company tax within consolidated G is no outside source |

The same recipe can have the same price/pay/output under all three forms. A
public or cooperative label supplies neither costless capital nor extra demand.
If a social mandate provides lower prices, safer standby or underserved access,
show its paid incremental duty and source separately from trading performance.
Private ownership may exclude people without wealth; cooperative gatekeeping
and captured public boards can also exclude. IC's fairly accessible finance,
funded learning, public residual supply and practical alternatives must be tested,
not presumed available from legal registration. No new universal startup grant
or compulsory nationalization is promised without its own elected source choice.

**PW09 — Preserve demand uncertainty and the right to save.** Minimum-needs
quantities, private wishes, capacity offers, accepted orders, delivered output,
invoices, paid receipts and taxes are different stages. Purchasers may save,
repair, share, use public services or choose alternatives. Do not force all U
or I05 into purchases, multiply the unassigned choice envelope into production,
or treat the 29,556 unprovided-resource adults as an unemployed hiring reserve.
Private saving can coexist with public net financing; it does not create an
owned Treasury or foreign-currency asset. Lack of demand is not automatically
lack of need. Distribution, prices and safe access can prevent a purchase.

**PW10 — Public input revenue cannot fund its own gross purchase twice.** For
each remaining major public purchase, existing spending/delivery officers must
separate the buyer's due appropriation and payment from supplier labor, actual
upstream inputs, capital, margin, VAT/refunds and later tax. Replace an old row
only once when scopes match. If G buys 100 and receives an actually settled 20
tax back, gross delivery still needs the 100 payment source/date; only the
qualified net source account may credit the 20 once. Client withholding,
unreceived recoveries, supplier deposits and next-April assessments are not
current G cash. No new approval is required for every private job or order.

Accountable sector buyers choose lawful specifications and accept delivery;
Treasury pays authorized obligations; TA/RG administer tax; AA audits rather
than chooses policy; independent TX/ordinary courts and existing executors
correct unlawful rejection/nonpayment in useful time. A common controller of
buyer, supplier, lender or reviewer defeats claimed independence. New supplier
work, oversight, procurement failure and paid continuity add costs, not free
capacity in old bids. If the supplier fails, due wage/user/creditor claims
continue through WL/OE/insolvency/OP routes; an invoice alone is no remedy.

**PW11 — Finance alternatives carry the same obligations.** Retain RF ordinary
tax/insurance/bounded debt and the separately authorized finite fiscal-issue
rival. Compare interest/ownership/private saving, CU price pressure, FX and
paid transition on the same actual output. Issue can change domestic settlement
and distribution; it cannot supply an accepting foreign seller or erase a real
input shortfall. Domestic producers' private FX holdings are not G's property.
Voluntary conversion, asset sale or authorized borrowing needs an actual
counterparty and continuing liability. No residual export or issue closes a gap.
