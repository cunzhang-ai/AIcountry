# C07. Care and support through life without compulsory family labor

*Part of [C07 social floor](../C07-social-floor.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

National law finances an individual, needs-based support floor for children,
disabled adults, older people and others needing sustained help. Local care
authorities assess and organize delivery with the person, not only a household
head. The assessment identifies tasks, hours, safe timing, access, housing and
transport links, respite, communication, a responsible provider and a backup.
The person receives a written decision, a usable interim response when delay
threatens safety, and independent review. A diagnosis, age, family form or AI
score alone cannot decide entitlement. Claims to health treatment, income,
education and legal capacity remain separate even when one plan connects them.

The reference offers a real choice among lawful home, community and
residential support where safely feasible. A person can use an accountable
public/local provider or a regulated additional provider; a bounded
person-directed budget may fund chosen support only with transparent wages,
safety checks and help that the person can refuse. The local authority remains
the provider of last resort and must accept a claim after a contractor's
failure. Choice does not mean a list of providers who will not accept the
person. No essential access is conditional on a relative quitting work,
sharing private records or agreeing to unpaid care. Relatives who freely
provide care can seek respite, income support and training without acquiring
the recipient's vote, property or decision authority.

Paid care workers need fair pay, predictable time, training, safe staffing,
collective voice and a protected report route. A funding formula must cover
travel, handover, night cover, supervision and replacement, not only minutes
recorded inside a home. Public and private providers face independent safety
inspection and a proportionate correction or closure route. A worker's
misconduct is investigated fairly; a complaint by a recipient cannot be
handled solely by the provider whose contract is at stake. The review system
tracks missed visits, actual accepted replacements, preventable harm,
recipient control and worker conditions, with privacy for intimate care.

Adults retain legal capacity and chosen support under [C08's personal-status
rule](../C08/01-civil-law-and-personal-status.md). A care plan cannot confer
guardianship or justify confinement. Restrictions for immediate danger need a
specific lawful ground, minimum scope, recorded reasons, safe alternative,
prompt independent review and a route back to chosen arrangements. Child
protection uses its distinct harm and court tests; poverty or unavailable
services are not parental wrongdoing. A person can contact an advocate,
court, health service or safe shelter without the provider's permission.

If a facility closes, a caregiver is ill, a payment route fails or a household
becomes unsafe, the local authority arranges an actual accepting worker or
safe place and coordinates health, housing and transport. It preserves the
person's claim and lawful records, with consent and minimum necessary sharing.
A family member is not the default emergency reserve. National finance and
equalization must cover high-need and sparse-area costs, while C06 counts
qualified workers, rest time, vehicles, safe places, inspection, backup and
future obligations once. An unmet shift remains visible as non-delivery,
even when a budget ledger balances. The [decision
record](../../2026-10-life-course-services/decision-record.md) compares this
public residual with more centralized or market-led arrangements.

Child and family support includes reachable respite, housing and income help
before material poverty is treated as neglect. An immediate child danger
needs an actual safe placement and competent carer under C08's distinct
court rule. A recipient whose partner or supporter controls the household
account keeps a safe direct claim and independent payment route. [Retirement
income](04-retirement-security.md) may support a life but cannot be counted
as a delivered care visit.
The [reproductive and early-life service](09-reproductive-health-and-early-life.md)
keeps pregnancy, postpartum and newborn care with the clinical network;
local care support and C08 child status join it without assuming a
relative will supply unpaid replacement staff.
The [end-of-life clinical route](10-serious-illness-and-end-of-life-care.md)
keeps symptom treatment with the health network, while this care authority
still owes actual paid support, safe housing and respite where needed.
The [mental-health route](11-mental-health-substance-use-and-crisis-care.md)
may connect chosen community support and clinical care to this authority's
housing and paid-care duties. A care placement is not psychiatric custody,
and a relative is not the unbudgeted crisis service.
The [interpersonal-safety route](12-interpersonal-safety-and-survivor-support.md)
also needs an accepting care worker and safe place when the usual carer or
household controller is the source of harm; the same person cannot serve as
both mandatory contact and confidential fallback.

[S09–S12](13/03-care-childhood-and-agency.md) specifies candidate assessment, childcare offers, direct-budget safeguards and safe receiving. Average workload hours are not individual care caps; a declined learning offer does not remove childcare.

[NP/C07/16](16-paid-receiving-and-personal-essentials.md) now makes the last-resort
commitment an ex-ante allocation/retainer choice: fund actual accepting shifts,
places and independent remedies before reliance, using included capacity once.
Cash awards/direct budgets remain separate from delivered support; no family
member becomes an unpriced reserve. Actual paid new receiving remains unverified.

[AS01–AS24](18-person-directed-assistance.md) now supplies the assistance
employment/control settlement: usable person-directed non-employer provision,
optional supported direct employment, source/custody, paid fair exit, scoped
public dues backing and actual fallback. A refused assignment does not prove
misconduct or terminate employment. New source/insurance/roster costs need fresh
qualification; public choice is no permission to transfer risk to the recipient.

[NC organized early care](20-early-care-relationships-and-staffing.md) now adds
under6 group/present-staff/relationship/source/correction candidates through
these existing local-care/inspection/court chains. It neither sets all care
ratios nor turns family life or voluntary childminding into this group regime.
Actual independent staff/source/receiving remain unverified; additional needs persist.

[HS01–HS08](25-adaptive-home-support.md) specifies who can change an adult
home-support plan and who pays for actual receiving. The person may keep a
specified plan or choose AS, with clinical duties and consent preserved.

[YC01–YC12](27-stable-child-care-and-adult-transition.md) assigns a
qualified, funded placement path and accepted voluntary adulthood
support. Keep existing S/AS care, B31 grounds and legal clocks.

[HS14–HS18](25/04-voluntary-care-and-paid-continuity.md) gives both people
direct confidential access to change a plan and prices overlapping
withdrawals while keeping booked respite and original care needs visible.

[YCR01–YCR07](07/01-young-carers-and-funded-family-support.md) gives
children direct help, qualified replacement and a separate learning
route. Keep consensual family life and adult care rights intact.
