# Design371 decision on housing, warmth and budget coverage

## Baseline and choice

Pin Design370, the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md), WF17–WF22,
WF65–WF68, HL01–HL22 and TH. The retained whole-country bargain
separates income, housing and useful service. The question is whether
a first common budget can use one income threshold or one estimated
caseload to stand for housing and heat exposure.

[WF105–WF110](../country/C06/58/24-housing-warmth-and-budget-coverage.md)
selects actual-need entry under existing duties, a common population
with honest overlap limits and separately funded receipts. The
Assembly, competent local bodies and sector authorities keep their
source, lawful allocation and review powers. The research changes no
eligibility threshold, payment level, tax or actual duty.

## Alternatives and objections

An income-only gate is cheap to implement, while the observed
above-threshold groups can be large. Broad discretionary assessment
could increase proof burdens or make access arbitrary. Retain a
simple reachable report, actual household-resource checks only within
applicable law and independent review. Compare competent ordinary
repair, bounded broader support, and durable retrofit or net affordable
housing supply. Survey poverty is neither safe command over money
nor physical need. Housing and warmth can also reflect different
conditions of tenure, building quality, prices or supply.

A broad intake can promise more than the service or budget delivers.
Use exact funded scope and preserve due access. A narrower pilot
discloses the excluded people and ongoing claims. Cover costs for
missing people and actual staffing separately. Keep upper and lower
exposure bounds distinct from the amount or kind of support each
person needs. An evidence gap cannot authorize collection of whole-life
records or displace an urgent right.

## Evidence and migration

[Official inputs](sources.md) and the [matched comparison](../settlement/housing-warmth-and-budget-coverage/01-evidence-and-first-budget-choice.md)
support the income-gate diagnostic and reject a single inferred
coverage count. The two populations differ in risk structure. No
sampling confidence interval, programme take-up, control of resources,
actual costs, lawful authorization or experienced improvement is established.
No affected resident or independent qualified reviewer has participated.

Preserve existing housing and support claims, emergency receiving,
provider pay, review and protected records. Before a real allocation,
the public analyst and qualified sector owners would use an authorized
aggregate joint tabulation or bounded lawful sample to compare actual
assistance, current repair and durable provision in one district.
Count staffed assessments and available accepting dwellings, funded
work and bills at two due dates. Stop with an attributed source and
capacity range and the first exposed duty. Reopen when overlap evidence
resolves the choice, stock or prices change, or excluded people show
an earlier material need.
