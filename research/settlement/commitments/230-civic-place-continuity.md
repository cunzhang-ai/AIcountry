# Design317. Civic-place continuity

[CPV01–CPV05](../../country/C11/04/01-open-access-and-usable-succession.md)
preserves free ordinary access and fair use across maintenance,
operator exit and other material changes. Actual local and national
sources, qualified staff, useful hours and direct review govern.
Existing legal claims, contracts and lawful assembly protections
survive the change through paid successors. No public power over
relationships follows.

The [decision record](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-civic-places-and-usable-continuity/decision-record.md)
keeps repair, community provision and less formal alternatives open.
