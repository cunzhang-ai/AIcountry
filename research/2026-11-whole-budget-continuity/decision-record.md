# Design212. A budget choice after the cost of continuing

## Baseline and question

Design211 is the pinned baseline. Use the current framework and decision
template. GP requires one public programme; DB separates authorization
from delivery; FB sets four-year source and shock rules; T preserves
existing duties and exact starts. WF's industry comparison rules out
assuming that total hours or spending establish compatible capacity.
The open choice is how to distinguish maintaining current rights from
an expansion and sequence the first budget when several reforms claim
the same resources. This decision grants no actual authority.

## Evidence and diagnosis

The February 2026 Danish DK2035 plan separates a projected operating
space, demographic and other provisions, named new priorities and a
mechanical scenario for expiring appropriations and operating pressures.
It is strong evidence of how an existing state frames the decision.
It is neither evidence of paid delivery nor a price book for AIcountry.
The same document's demographic calculations are not a measure of
unmet needs or the payer's settled cash. The constructed comparison
shows what further questions matter when the legal budget fits but
people, providers or independent capacity do not. [Sources](sources.md)

## Disposition and objections

Select FB13–FB20. Show the existing legal and voted path and a separate
continuation account. Keep state and local owners, actual receipt and
independent capacity distinct. Under the case assumptions choose funded
repair and paid preparation over immediate common expansion. It protects
binding duties and keeps a path open to improvement. Extra authorization
can reverse that disposition when it buys usable qualified capacity
and recurring source, with paid transition and no displaced duties.
A current spending freeze is a real comparator, but may let lawful
access deteriorate when need rises. A strong existing system can already
project operating pressure and retain services. If a staged option adds
cost without relieving the bottleneck, retain the competent ordinary
route. If delay leaves avoidable harm, an earlier legally funded change
can be preferable. Keep affected people waiting for improved access and
workers who bear training or transition costs in the comparison.

Estimated continuation can hide inflated provider bids, undercount
people excluded from current use, or create a finance ministry veto.
Sector owners supply evidence and alternatives. Members can challenge
the scope and bring a competing budget. Treasury reports; elected
authority chooses; independent reviewers and courts retain their
own evidence, budgets and remedy powers. No blanket new entitlement,
tax, borrowing power or fiscal ceiling follows from this comparison.

capital, WF resources, DP source validation and T commencement and
succession. Preserve actual claims, adopted dates, stronger rights,
existing law and earlier scopes. The quantified case covers two skill

unpriced. Affected-party participation, independent review, enactment,
funded independent execution and observed lives are unestablished.

The next whole-country decision needs a dated authoritative enacted
baseline for a named setting. Assemble one priced continuation account
and one funded alternative using legal obligations, actual receipts,
qualified shifts and independent budgets on compatible dates. A
Treasury team and sector owners would need lawful, paid access to that
material, independent costing and affected review. If an input is
unavailable, show where the comparison remains unresolved. Reopen on
