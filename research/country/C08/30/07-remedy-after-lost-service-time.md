# SR28–SR32. Remedy the loss left after a public service arrives late

13 November 2026 · Integrated design 445 · Candidate ordinary law

**SR28 — Give a bounded claim against the responsible public body.**
For a service class expressly covered by the adopting law, an
independent adjudicator may award redress when an essential public
service was legally due by a useful deadline, breach is established,
and material residual loss is proved to have resulted. Select
reasonable replacement costs for already established time-sensitive
essential assistance as the first class to test. The person need not
identify an officer who was negligent. Apply the governing duty's
actual exceptions and fair proof to the breach and causation. Agency
shortage alone supplies no defense unless the applicable law gives
it effect. A service aspiration, mere delay or an unsupported
claim of a favorable outcome cannot establish an award.

**SR29 — Protect and restore before measuring residual loss.**
Use the existing urgent route, paid equivalent assistance and
ordinary enforcement as soon as time is threatened. A person who
cannot advance money retains the same funded assistance and may
request direct booking where existing law and capacity allow it.
Record if that route fails. Offer a
genuinely equivalent rescheduled opportunity where feasible, with
the person's choice and the affected third party's lawful rights.
The agency must give the original deadline, failure and available
options in accessible form. Proceed with established service or
payment while a compensation dispute is open. Keep the missed
date in the record even when later delivery succeeds.

**SR30 — Decide each head of loss and credit actual recovery once.**
Use the position supported by evidence had timely lawful service
occurred. The adopting law specifies material eligible losses and
any limits. In the initial class reimburse reasonable proved
replacement expenditure and other direct monetary loss only within
express law. An irreversible nonfinancial loss or uncertain
opportunity can warrant acknowledgement, restored access where
possible, or an existing remedy under its own grounds. Do not
presume the desired outcome would have occurred. Credit actual
recovery for the same loss once, preserving other awards and
the underlying benefit. Give reasons, access to relevant evidence,
fair response and independent challenge. No standard settlement
waives future rights by default.

**SR31 — Give the order its own funded path.** The adopting law
names the adjudicator, liability-bearing body, authorized payment
source and paid independent remedy staff. Schedule source,
payment, verified receipt and the next due service together.
Keep a qualified reserve or other lawful source separate from
money already needed for ordinary service. No court order creates
an appropriation. If funds fail, preserve the order, original date
and ordinary enforcement while seeking lawful budget correction.
An Ombuds recommendation retains its own effect.

**SR32 — Make repeated loss visible and test simpler alternatives.**
Retain distinct evidence of timely service, late service, residual
loss, compensation and systemic repair. Offer accessible intake,
including oral and offline reports. Provide independent review
with protected records and safe aggregate reporting. AI may help
match dates and credits. It cannot decide causation or close an
award. Compare this limited claim with ordinary tort and a
flat credit on cost, missed people and real receipt. Preserve
accrued obligations and separate remedies on handover. Reopen
on repeated harm, intrusive proof, funding loss or a better
lawful response.
