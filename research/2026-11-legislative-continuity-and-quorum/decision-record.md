# Design359 decision on legislative continuity and quorum

## Baseline, proposed choice and authority

Pin Design358, full-seat EL19–EL20, ordinary formation, PE, HT, BLC,
election readiness and the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md).
The existing design keeps thresholds fixed, recognizes true vacancy
and retains fiscal continuity. Select a paid alternate sitting, a
route to distinguish causes and a protected docket when no binding
decision can be made. The project assistant proposes the 24-hour
notice, 48-hour rescheduling target and seven-day reassessment. The
adopting constitution and ordinary law control participation and
powers. No current chamber, mandate or permission changes.

## Alternatives and objections

The ordinary physical chamber has simple custody and supports informal
deliberation. It can exclude members when travel or access is lost.
Supervised linked sites keep actual members and unchanged vote rules
but require comparable debate, independent custody and extra staff.
A temporary smaller denominator can allow quicker legislation after
catastrophe. It can make selective exclusion or manipulation of
incapacity declarations decisive. Retain fixed thresholds for this
design, accepting that genuine deadlock may leave business unmade.

Site control can become a majority or vendor veto. Fund separate
custody, alternative presiders and access, protect member challenge
and keep the right to return to an ordinary sitting. Do not mistake
abstention, boycott or a contested credential for a vacancy. No vote
can be inferred from a model, party office, proxy or a message.
Continuous protection and electoral readiness cost resources in their
own right. A reported alternate that cannot host opposition and
affected members does not supply usable legislative continuity.

## Qualified result, transition and next step

The [sources](sources.md) describe actual temporary arrangements
and the provisional-denominator alternative. The [constructed case](../settlement/legislative-continuity-and-quorum/01-two-sittings-and-the-original-clock.md)
checks attendance, consent, a disputed vote, frozen succession and
the fixed fiscal clock. No actual participation, legal adoption,
recoverable chamber or source is established. Preserve exact pending
texts, dates, lawful mandates and continuing payments through a
change of site or government. A supplementary poll, a fiscal vote
and a restoration of access retain separate legal triggers.

Within the legislature's authorized research and drill budget, compare
one interrupted sitting, one disputed ballot and the next due payment.
Use separately controlled sites and protected representation from
government and opposition. Stop at witnessed usable participation,
an independently reconciled tally and a separately priced continuing
payment schedule, without actual use for a binding vote. Reopen on
selective exclusion, failed custody, recurring blockade or a simpler
route that preserves equivalent control.
