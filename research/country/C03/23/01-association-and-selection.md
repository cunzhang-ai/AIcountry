# NM01–NM04. Association and meaningful selection

[Contestable nominations](../23-contestable-list-nominations.md)

**NM01 — Scope follows collective control of the public filing.** Apply
this minimum to a party or civic group selecting one or more nominees
for an ordinary national or municipal list. A genuinely self-nominating
individual remains eligible for the one-person route with the same EL
entry and count. A party-sponsored single name selected by the party
remains a collective selection. Determine control from actual power over
the filing, with notice and answerable evidence. Shared views, endorsements
or use of a common service alone establish no control. Leave ordinary
association, informal support and policy choice with the participants.
Protected territorial or other electoral arrangements need their own
authority and affected assent to change.

**NM02 — Set the selectors and terms before competition.** A standing
organization publishes the nomination rules in force at call, including
eligibility and membership or delegate dates, notice, conflicts, tally,
lawful withdrawal and whether parts of the outcome can stand separately.
Members choose directly, or through delegates elected by the eligible
members with equal votes and secret ballots under a prior representative
rule. No appointed leadership bloc has a separate veto. A newly formed
civic slate may use its declared consenting organizing members as the
selection body, with equal votes, a privately verifiable roster and
prospective rules published before notice. Only persons eligible to
endorse for that contest are selectors. Groups may use prior,
lawful eligibility and association criteria, including political purpose.
A selective expulsion, blocked enrollment or roster alteration to
manipulate a pending choice is reviewable. No organization must accept
every applicant or defend every policy disagreement to the state.

**NM03 — Give eligible proposals a route to the selectors.** Any eligible
selector can put themselves forward; a consenting candidate who meets
the prior lawful terms may also be proposed by any eligible selector.
The candidate's own qualified proposal suffices. Verify consent, ordinary
candidacy and the published terms with reasons for refusal and a protected
independent challenge. Give competing proposals comparable access to
the selectors, an accessible statement and usable response through a
protected relay. No executive approval, monetary fee, compulsory device
or sponsor-only communications gate. A group may advocate a slate,
publish its principles and argue for its preferred candidates. The
process must give selectors a meaningful choice on contested nominees
and rank, including a way to reject an unopposed proposed slate.

**NM04 — Let selectors determine the pool and frozen fallback order.**
Each qualified selector casts their own private choice, with accessible
assistance that conveys the person's act and a usable non-digital path.
Use the disclosed prospective counting rule and verify actual receipt.
The valid selection determines consenting nominees and fallback order.
It needs no political countersignature or ratification. The group can
publish a slate endorsement, but cannot revise the selectors' result
by leadership instruction. A model may assist with formats, translation
or a checkable count. Neither its ranking nor an agent copy can cast
a selection vote, add a selector or supply personal consent.
