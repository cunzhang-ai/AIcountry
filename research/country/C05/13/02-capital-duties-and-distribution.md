# OE06–OE10: capital and profit must not be invented by legal form

*Part of [C05/13](../13-enterprise-and-association-constitutions.md); design118 candidate.*

**OE06 — Actually contributed capital.** A subscription states consideration,
rights, timing, conditions and actual receiver. Paid cash, enforceable receivable,
valued transferred asset and expected future work are separate. Record received
and unpaid commitments distinctly; unperformed labor/AI output is not cash or
paid equipment. Noncash title/value needs reasons, original source and conflicts;
no fictitious subscribed-capital assurance. No universal nominal minimum creates
solvency; sector risk/capital/bond/insurance law still applies. An actual creditor
can request security/information lawfully without acquiring all governance.

**OE07 — Separate liability without a shell escape.** Entity owes its actual
debts; passive members ordinarily risk committed contributions, not every entity
loss. Personal guarantees, unpaid subscriptions, own wrongdoing, unlawful receipt
and B10/11 actual control/undertakings retain their proved source. Common ownership,
consolidated reports or MP economic-unit treatment alone does not make all group
debts joint. WL labor-hire/host and public nondelegable duties remain. A controller
that deliberately strips assets, fraudulently misleads or negligently directs a
harmful act cannot use a shell to erase its own responsibility; prove conduct,
duty/causation and fair defenses. No automatic criminal guilt for business failure.

**OE08 — Loyal, careful and accountable directors.** Directors act within lawful
entity purpose, with reasonable care, honest information, actual conflicts and
regard to worker/creditor/consumer/ecological rights, not a duty to maximize today's
share price at any cost. Protect informed good-faith unconflicted commercial
judgment from hindsight liability; unlawful acts/fund diversion remain reviewable.
All director classes have duties and protected source/advice access. General
worker pay/conditions or ordinary owner return policy does not automatically
recuse the entire corresponding class; own special pay/deal needs actual conflict
handling. Material related-party deals need fair terms, disclosed control and
approval by unconflicted directors and, for controllers, disinterested member
votes after usable information. No self-approval by splitting a controlled account.

**OE09 — Accounts and an independent check.** Keep original assets/claims,
liabilities, cash, wages/contributions, control, protected property, guarantees
and actual commitments; preserve entity and group accounts separately. Annual
accounts/return due within six months of year end, with proportional assistance,
member/worker lawful access and safe public summaries. Ordinary external audit is
required at50 covered workers,100M assets or200M annual revenue; M is the actual
scale expressly authorized for the audit period. Retain actual lawful
audit thresholds through retrofit. A competent corporate Act may set
an opening scale and apply the TA01 measurement method separately
from tax adoption. An unavailable new scale gives no exemption from
existing audit duties and does not authorize a novel numeric trigger.
Apply the actual predecessor standard and independently operative audit
grounds. Refer any unclassified exposure to the competent legislature.
Sector/public issuer and public
enterprise audit can be stricter. Smaller entities keep meaningful accounts,
not mandatory full corporate bureaucracy for every informal gathering.

Auditor must be qualified, independent in actual control/interests and selected
by ordinary member vote under OE11 and affirmative votes of more than half
the full statutory workforce-director seats where OE12 applies. Vacancies do not
reduce that latter denominator; a conflicted auditor cannot vote on appointment.
The same approvals govern removal/replacement with reasons/reply and no audit
gap; unjustified obstruction or unavailable qualified candidates permits direct
court appointment of a paid independent interim auditor, not management
self-certification or cancellation of an owed audit. Interim authority covers
only the next owed audit; it conveys no continuing appointment power and leaves
the constituencies their lawful subsequent selection/removal rights. Neither management's preferred AI label nor an owner's fee proves
independence. Disclose fees, related work, qualifications and removal reasons;
rotate the responsible lead after six years, retaining continuity/custody. Audits
and registry receipt do not certify all facts, future solvency, sector safety or
a government guarantee. No compelled public sensitive testimony or trade-secret dump.

**OE10 — Distribution needs assets and actual liquidity.** Before dividends,
buybacks, capital return or disguised related-party benefit, show actual net
assets after the payment cover liabilities and nondistributable capital/reserves,
and a reasonable supported12-month cash/claim view with adverse cases. Do not
ignore wages, pensions, tax, known contingent loss or actually restricted assets;
undisputed overdue essential dues must be paid/provided before owner extraction.
A current cash surplus is not distributable if future due claims are unsupported.
No model ratio automatically authorizes a distribution. Wages/real fair-value
trade are not dividends merely by recipient relationship.

An unlawful distribution is returnable up to the actual improperly received
benefit, with B08 actual change-of-position/other fair defenses and protected needs;
there is no blanket immunity just from passive-owner status. Knowing/negligent
authorizers retain their own proved responsibility. Preserve B/U periods, notice,
causation and source-law priorities; no new retrospective strict liability for
old lawful receipts. A record of return is not actual recovered cash. Lawful
capital losses/creditor shortfalls stay visible, not shifted automatically to G.
