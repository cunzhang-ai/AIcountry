# Primary records on cumulative impairment and judicial remedy

1. Venice Commission, *Opinion on Amendments to the Act of 25 June
   2015 on the Constitutional Tribunal of Poland*, CDL-AD(2016)001,
   adopted 11–12 March 2016, especially paragraphs 53–56 and 88–90.
   It assesses quorum, voting majority, case sequencing and other
   delays together. It records the public justification for sequencing
   and concludes that the combined amendments could make the tribunal
   ineffective. This is the Commission's own expert assessment.
   [Official opinion](https://docs-venice.coe.int/api/Document?pdffile=CDL-AD%282016%29001-e).
2. European Commission Recommendation (EU) 2016/1374 of 27 July 2016,
   section 3. Its contemporary record distinguishes the Tribunal's
   9 March judgment on the December amendments from the separate
   dispute about publication and implementation. It is the
   Commission's recommendation and account of the dispute.
   [Official record](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reco/2016/1374/oj/eng).
3. European Court of Human Rights, *Xero Flor w Polsce sp. z o.o.
   v. Poland*, no. 4907/18, 7 May 2021, paragraphs 243–291. The Court
   separately found a violation of Article 6 § 1 regarding a tribunal
   established by law in the applicant's case, after examining the
   judicial appointment irregularity and domestic review. This is an
   individual adjudication, with distinct scope from the 2016 opinion.
   [Official judgment](https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/app/conversion/docx/html/body?id=001-210065&library=ECHR).

These records distinguish procedural effects, lawful composition and
execution of judgments. The first two are overlapping evidence on the
same episode. The case below uses independently authored tasks and
dates and supplies no prediction for Poland or AIcountry.
