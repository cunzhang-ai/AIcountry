# Primary evidence on small drinking-water supplies

1. Ireland Environmental Protection Agency, *Drinking Water Quality
   in Private Group Schemes and Small Private Supplies 2025* and
   official release of 29 July 2026. The release reports 1,623 small
   private supplies registered with local authorities, of which 1,526
   were monitored in 2025. It reports E. coli failures in 14 private
   group schemes in 2025, down from 24 in 2024, with six repeat
   failures. Its scope excludes household wells. The total number
   of small private supplies is unknown. None of the reported
   denominators covers every person or supply outside the public network.
   [Official results and report link](https://www.epa.ie/news-releases/news-releases-2026/drinking-water-quality-in-private-supplies-continues-to-present-public-health-risks.php).
2. World Health Organization, *Guidelines for drinking-water quality:
   small water supplies*, February 2024. The guideline includes
   household, community and professional systems, recommends proactive
   risk management, and distinguishes independent surveillance.
   [Official guideline](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240088740).

The monitoring and guidance have distinct scopes. They do not
establish AIcountry's standards, contamination, costs or outcome.
The worked case uses independently authored assumptions.
