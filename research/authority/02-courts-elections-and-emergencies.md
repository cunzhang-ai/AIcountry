# Courts, elections and emergencies

*Part of [Authority and legal transition](../AUTHORITY-AND-TRANSITION.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

## 5. Courts, appointments and the enforcement chain

Ordinary courts retain their lawful jurisdiction, including urgent individual-rights
remedies. A constitutional court or chamber can review law and institutional
competence, with public reasons, appeal/reconsideration as specified, protected
staffing and direct lawful treasury payments. A binding order names who must act
and what evidence of completion is required. The cabinet may propose an alternative
lawful remedy; it cannot erase an order by withholding its only access credential.

Staggered appointment sources, disclosed nominations and nonretaliatory removal are
reference safeguards. For every office, check who controls the candidate pool,
selection body, budget, data, case assignment, payment operator and enforcement.
Opposition support and a citizen panel do not prove independence if their sponsors
or data channels are shared. A proposed vacancy safeguard retains ordinary courts'
preexisting individual-rights jurisdiction and allows carefully limited interim
relief while a constitutional bench is nonfunctional; final constitutional questions
await a lawfully competent court. That route needs explicit prior jurisdiction,
appeal and conflict rules before adoption. It is not an invitation to invent a
loyal emergency court after appointments fail.

No actor gains a replacement appointment power merely because the intended process
is late. At the same time, government may not hide a rights denial behind a vacant
specialist court if ordinary lawful remedies remain. A simultaneous failure of
elections, courts, treasury, records and lawful enforcement remains a critical
breakdown; additional paper institutions cannot guarantee recovery from common
capture.

## 6. Elections, emergencies and external commitments

Published election dates, reporting duties and caretaker limits become observable
before polling. A certification delay triggers disclosure and an evidence-based
court remedy; it does not certify an AI estimate or give the incumbent a new term.
When lawful results establish a successor, records, payment credentials, premises
and security command transfer to that successor. Civil servants owe their duties
to office and law, not a defeated person or vendor. Neither a party militia nor a
model chooses the winner.

[A8/RX01–RX16](../country/C10/01-public-safety-and-emergencies.md) now selects
no domestic constitutional derogation. Ordinary right-specific lawful restrictions
remain possible; a declaration creates no new coercive, fiscal or foreign power.
The selected declared-authority ceilings are14elapsed days initially,30per timely
three-fifths full-Assembly renewal and90aggregate operative days for the same
emergency/material continuation. The shorter actual source clock controls,
including DR13's calendar-day7 urgent-rule expiry. Ordinary legislation after
expiry remains within the full constitutional ceiling; it cannot inherit a
suspension power or restart the Cabinet's initial period. Existing custody,
clinical, electoral, court-vote and RR succession protections remain distinct.
Actual catastrophic loss is a failed constitutional condition requiring legitimate
recovery, not a license for Cabinet, a project owner or AI to rule.

Major treaties, defense commitments and guarantees need an Assembly mandate, public
fiscal and rights analysis, and the competent constitutional ratification procedure.
Material provisional application and withdrawal also need prior elected authority,
the instrument's lawful external route and a transition account for continuing
claims and essential supply; a domestic vote alone does not erase treaty duties.
The cabinet negotiates within that mandate. AI may translate, model and compare
terms; it has no independent treaty veto or signature on behalf of the people.
Existing treaty duties cannot be cancelled by publishing a new research edition.

The [defense decision](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-10-defense-and-war/decision-record.md) proposes a
different clock for armed force, separate from constitutional emergency expiry.
Prior law permits immediately necessary territorial defense; the cabinet records
the basis and notifies the Assembly's protected channel within a proposed 24 hours.
An urgent cross-border action cannot mature into one continuing foreign campaign
beyond a proposed seven days without an affirmative Assembly mandate. The mandate
defines mission, area, force class, civilian protection, fiscal ceiling, reporting
and exit, and expires within a proposed 90 days absent affirmative renewal. An
alternate protected sitting and opposition-inclusive classified scrutiny must be
arranged before a crisis. Inability to convene leaves standing defense and safe
withdrawal under prior law, not an executive power to widen the campaign. These
specific periods remain unadopted hypotheses; domestic approval cannot cure a
breach of applicable international law.

The current [CJ reference](../country/C03/08-constitutional-review-and-public-control.md)
selects the previously open final forum, fixed votes and external own-court cases.
Its adoption/transfer still follows prior law; it cannot certify its own creation.
Existing ordinary jurisdiction and emergency clocks are unchanged.
