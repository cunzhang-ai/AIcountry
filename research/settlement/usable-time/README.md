# Compare equal totals and unequal usable time

*Design296 · Authored design comparison.*

| Resident | Periods available | Total |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| A, fragmented | 09–10, 12–13, 17–18 and 20–21 | 4 hours |
| B, continuous | 09–11 and 17–19 | 4 hours |

The examples include only two tasks within this day. Each resident
can take their separately staffed independent review by 13:00,
with one-hour slots offered at 09:00 or 12:00. The voluntary
deliberation closes at 19:00. The live meeting needs both hours
from 17:00. The additional contribution and reply route takes
12:00–13:00 and 17:00–18:00, with actual moderated exchange.
The format is assumed lawful for this consultation. It is not a
substitute for a ballot or required independent hearing.

| Choice | Public cost | Timely benefit review | Practical deliberation opportunity |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Staffed review and a common live meeting | 3 | A and B | B |
| Live meeting plus staffed contribution and reply | 4 | A and B | A and B, using different lawful formats |
| Live meeting plus paid relief for A from 18:00 to 19:00 | 5 | A and B | A and B, live if A chooses and the qualified care route is accepted |

The cost units include only the declared public offer. They are
not wages or money values for anyone's time. For A, review at
12:00 conflicts with the contribution path. The 09:00 review
and the later exchange fit in sequence. For B, the 09:00 review
and live evening meeting fit. An unstaffed inbox, inaccessible
translation or reply after the decision does not serve A. The
paid relief case fails if that hour or consent is missing. Both
alternatives preserve the ordinary independent benefit review.

With four units, select the additional contribution and reply
route in this case. At three units the live route still
supports B, but A's missed opportunity remains visible. Report
the gap and seek a lawful funded way to improve it. At five
units the accepted relief route is a real rival. It offers
richer live exchange and uses more care resources. This comparison
does not assign a price to deliberation or settle the wider
relative value of optional time, improved services or more pay.
