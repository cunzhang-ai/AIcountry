# A dated, paid enabling schedule

*Part of [protected-stage reference](README.md); fictional units, not actual costs.*

The bounded example has six construction years and two operating years.
No larger-output tax receipt enters its sources. Opening programme cash is
hypothetically granted/appropriated without new debt; **the real source is
unknown**. Actual loans would add carry, repayment and creditor exposures.
All full-country services, income, pensions, private finance and future
replacement remain separate and unresolved. Attribution to the earlier
capital line of 60 is unknown; this envelope is not automatically added to it.

| Item | Declared schedule and liability |
|---|---|
| Energy | Capital 60 over years 1–6; target adds 500 firm GWh only after paid, safe acceptance at end of year 6 |
| Water | Capital 30 over years 1–6; target verifies 1 independent usable supply and 1.5 actual savings, in million cubic metres; neither is an unexplained rise in catchment |
| Records/receiving route | Capital 18 over years 1–6; physical commissioning does not establish independent review or usable claimant receipt |
| Training | Current expense 2 annually during construction, total 12; teaching/supervision capacity is not yet priced separately |
| Added salaries | 420 voluntarily reassigned workers at total 16.8 annually, including training years and continuing operation; eight-year total 134.4 |
| Maintenance | Annual energy 10, water 4, records 2 in years 7–8, total 32; ordinary replacement and later years still need finance |
| Cash buffer | 10 separate from payable costs; no actual basis for its adequacy |

Capital 108 + training 12 + salaries 134.4 + maintenance 32 = **286.4**
through year 8. Keeping buffer 10 requires opening cash 296.4 if there are
no later programme receipts. A 120 construction/training allocation omits
166.4 of these costs and lacks the separate buffer. That is not savings.

Workers move from the assumed private pool 44,910 to public pool 10,090:
private becomes 44,490; public 10,510; total remains 55,000. Added care 200,
justice 100, administration 100 and technical 20 are distinct positions.
Qualifications take respectively 2, 3, 1 and 2 years; full shifts are only
credited in subsequent years under the hypothetical qualification assumption.
Pay never becomes conditional on pretending a trainee is already qualified.
Private foregone work/output, training supervisors, licenses and independent
appointment are unverified; the national tax base is not held proven unchanged.

The currency subaccount traces programme imports 8 per construction year
and operating imports 3 later, **already inside** the domestic cost components.
Existing foreign service 6.5 is also due; baseline trade is assumed balanced.
Exports 12 are assumed actually received and voluntarily converted, not
forecast or compelled. With reserves 100 and floor 75, the declared reference
ends year 6 at 85 and year 8 at 90. Prices, bank access, imports and exporter
consent are hypothetical. These are annual checks; monthly payment deadlines
and a missed safe time remain untested.

If paid and safely accepted, the illustrative larger-demand vector has
energy 1,220 against 1,450 ordinary firm availability; after 10% firm loss,
1,300 remains. Water demand becomes 6.5 through separately verified saving;
ordinary availability is 7.5 and raw-catchment loss of 10% leaves 6.6.
This is a physical target, not a claim of growth, safety, ecology or catchment
rights. Qualified care/justice/admin additions would leave 2,025/1,008/1,008
FTE after 10% loss against 2,000/1,000/1,000 assumed requirements. Role mix,
shifts and independence are unverified; aggregates cannot qualify a court.
