# Commitment 122. Keep public duties funded when the legal owner changes

[FT01–FT08](../../country/C04/11-public-service-transfer-and-continuity.md)
assigns each transferred task, operator and payer separately. Reconcile
shared resources, existing obligations, lawful labour and actual live
acceptance. Preserve the legal date and a funded fallback. Keep actual
service receipt, worker pay, title, remedies and public choice separate.

No new region, authority, payment or accepting staff is supplied by this
research. [Comparison](../public-service-transfer/README.md)
