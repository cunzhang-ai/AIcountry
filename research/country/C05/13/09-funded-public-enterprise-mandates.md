# OE41–OE46. Fund the public service that government asks an enterprise to deliver

*Part of [enterprise and association constitutions](../13-enterprise-and-association-constitutions.md). Design341 research selection.*

**OE41 — Put the public instruction, payer and service on the record.**
When a national or local authority asks a controlled enterprise to run an
otherwise uneconomic route, supply at a protected price or maintain special
standby, identify the statutory duty or lawfully agreed service mandate.
Publish the covered people and service, tariff, required capacity, condition
of assets at handover, term, cost method, lawful sponsor and dated source.
Assess the effect across controlled companies and linked instructions.
An ordinary rule applicable to all suppliers creates no compensation by
itself. A ministerial letter, shareholder direction or budget estimate
alone gives no appropriation. Apply established procurement and competition
law to the provider choice. Keep existing rights and earlier deadlines.

**OE42 — Pay the attributable public service and expose other costs.** Before
authorizing a material mandate, compare the same access, quality, timing and
maintained capacity under direct provision, eligible open contracting and
an enterprise assignment. Base compensation on evidenced efficient net
cost for that scope. Include justified shared costs, capital use and
readiness once, and subtract attributable user receipts, other public
support and evidenced benefits of any associated exclusive right. Any
explicit return needs separate authority and disclosure. Publish the
method and reconcile later against verified delivery and attributable
costs. Show commercial losses, shortfalls in compensation and service
failures separately. Reclaim excess under lawful terms with hearing
and review. A tariff cross-subsidy needs an express lawful tariff choice,
published incidence on paying users and protection of essential access.

**OE43 — Distinguish ownership from commissioning and execution.** The
competent elected body authorizes the mandate, ceiling and funds. Its named
sector sponsor commissions the service and the authorized career payer
releases the money. The ownership representative sets the lawful ownership
mandate. The board retains professional management and worker
representation. Use distinct decision-makers for commissioning, ownership,
tariff and quality regulation, and independent review, with protected access
to originals. The board must flag informal instructions and unfunded
requirements and protect staff who report them. Publish any lawful public
loan, guarantee or equity separately from payment for a service. Avoid
counting an internal public transfer as additional consolidated revenue.
Enterprise debt remains with its legal obligor unless separately assumed.

**OE44 — Expose a funding gap before it reaches users.** When usable cash,
qualified staff or protected maintenance will fall short, the board gives
the sponsor and lawful oversight bodies a dated notice promptly and within
two working days of identifying the gap. The sector sponsor records what
will be missed, the immediate paid continuity route and the elected source
decision required. Preserve existing minimum service and rights under their
law. Use any available prior-law bridge within its own cap and dates. Within
20 calendar days of the notice the competent elected body holds a recorded
decision on a lawful funded correction or alternative, or gives reasons for
refusal and states the remaining breach. Earlier service or remedy clocks
continue. A board cannot charge a new fee, divert restricted funds, defer
essential maintenance or borrow on presumed state backing to close the gap.
If the sponsor refuses or a source fails, affected users can seek direct
review and useful-time relief under existing law. A budget breach does not
itself establish an enterprise compensation claim.

**OE45 — Test the same cash and service window across enterprises.** Include
actual availability of dated payments, arrears, commercial liquidity,
maintenance, staff, common lenders and other contemporaneous calls.
Match the shared reserve to verified simultaneous uses and show unfunded
duties. Distinguish an actual guarantee call, a lawful but unpaid sponsor
obligation and a possible elected service rescue. Fund a qualified
successor when an operator cannot deliver, preserving lawful worker,
creditor and user claims. A separately authorized service rescue buys
usable provision. It creates no blanket guarantee for commercial creditors
or shareholders. AI may reconcile accounts and flag shared dependencies.
Qualified officers verify sources and independent reviewers can inspect
originals and resolve disputes within their scope.

**OE46 — Renew from actual delivery and publish the remaining exposure.**
Report original promises, services actually received, full costs, cash
arrears, asset condition and who bore each loss. Schedule a review before
the term ends and prepare a funded successor for any continuing duty.
Apply prospectively with costed audit, elected scrutiny and safe reporting.
Retain valid old debt, pay, contracts, sector independence and review.
Reopen if costs migrate into user bills, debt or maintenance, compensation
rewards weak performance, appointments compromise review or simpler
direct provision or open contracting performs better.
