# Design365 decision on border corridor continuity and physical receipt

## Baseline, choice and authority

Pin Design364, NS21–NS30, customs, SC, sector receiving and the
[working method](../FRAMEWORK.md). The design already separates
foreign payment and permission from physical supply. Select a
qualified alternate border schedule, lawful slot assignment,
separate release and onward receipt, and a funded second window.
The project assistant proposes the coordination and release
procedure. The adopting Assembly sets scope, priorities and
sources. Each border, sector and review authority decides only
its own legal act. No actual new site, inspection, tariff or
freight priority is authorized.

## Alternatives and objections

A priority lane with ordinary inspection capacity may ease access
where delay is clerical. Two advertised corridors still fail if
they rely on the same inspector. A paid independent alternate
expands useful scope but can be expensive. Prepositioned qualified
stock or durable domestic capacity can avoid a shared clearance
delay at a carrying, quality and replenishment cost. Compare these
on the same needs and dates. The constructed case uses a qualified
alternate and a paid supplement only where the source is timely.

Priority rules can exclude small importers or give a carrier
market power. Reserve only necessary lawful slots, release unused
discretionary capacity by a published rule and preserve challenges.
A faster route cannot override regulatory requirements. Separate
actual and disputed physical dependencies, and protect commercial
information without putting all verification under one operator.
Reserve alternatives through the next call without shifting
undisclosed costs to another place.

## Scoped result, transition and next action

The [sources](sources.md) support lawful facilitation and coordination.
The [constructed case](../settlement/border-corridor-continuity/01-two-terminals-and-one-inspection-team.md)
checks shared inspections, an existing reservation, dated funds,
a lawful hold and later receiving. It establishes no actual
authority, price, staffing, cargo qualification or improved life.
Preserve declarations, claims, costs and original service dates
through a change of route, provider or government.

Within an authorized logistics research budget, compare one real
critical corridor, one valid reservation and one independent fallback
on the same first and second receipt windows. Obtain affected
operator, staff and receiver evidence without changing live priority.
Stop at a costed and independently checked set of options. Reopen on
selective access, common inspector loss, missed delivery or a stronger
qualified inland alternative.
