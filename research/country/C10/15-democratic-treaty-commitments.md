# Democratic control of treaty commitments and their end

Design223 · TR01–TR12 · Proposed country rules

The Assembly authorizes material external commitments through a recorded
vote on the exact terms. Cabinet negotiates and carries out the authorized
acts. Domestic authority, external effect, lawful public spending and
actual service remain separate through entry, change and exit.

| Canonical part | Working choice |
|---|---|
| [TR01–TR04. Mandate, disclosure and vote](15/01-mandate-disclosure-and-vote.md) | Effect-based scope, prior negotiating mandate, complete text and affirmative elected approval |
| [TR05–TR08. Signature, provisional use and changes](15/02-entry-provisional-use-and-living-terms.md) | Separate external acts, bounded temporary application, controlled joint-body decisions and authenticated status |
| [TR09–TR12. Exit, rights and ongoing performance](15/03-exit-continuity-and-accountability.md) | Valid notice, funded transition, distinct remedies and lawful succession |
