# OM07–OM12. Keep administrative oversight independent and publicly answerable

*Part of [independent public Ombuds oversight](../13-public-ombuds-and-systemic-correction.md).*

**OM07 — Docket unresolved systemic failures.** The office publishes
material final findings, agency replies and follow-up status directly,
with lawful protected omissions and no permission from the investigated
body. Send the safe record to the competent elected body's clerk. The
clerk places a material missed response, unresolved recurring failure
or proposed change in law or finance on the public scrutiny docket.
Under the proposed national standing order, one third of the full
oversight committee membership can require a hearing within 20 sitting
days of a valid request. The clerk reserves paid time and publishes
any missed date with reasons and the next opening. During dissolution
the safe record remains public, and the new committee takes the open
item at its first lawful scheduling opportunity. The national Act
requires an equivalent reachable scrutiny route under local law.
Affected people may offer protected evidence and minority members may
publish a reasoned view. A hearing cannot decide an individual's
entitlement, approve funding or alter a court order.

**OM08 — Give leadership an independent and replaceable source.**
Protected Assembly staff run a public merit call and accessible
hearing. Disclose relevant professional, party, supplier and affected
agency ties. A recorded appointment needs three fifths of the full
statutory Assembly and one third of the applicable protected
non-government cohort under the adopted AA07 denominator rules.
An empty protected cohort cannot appoint. The term is seven
personal years, once only. No cabinet appointment, extension or
vacancy lowers the threshold. Select the chief independently of
the Auditor General and stagger ordinary expiry where lawfully
possible. Bar simultaneous elected office, party management, paid
lobbying and management of a body within jurisdiction. Ordinary
views and lawful advocacy do not disqualify a candidate.

**OM09 — Keep paid staff and sources outside the subject's control.**
The Assembly votes a distinct appropriation on a directly submitted
plan. Publish agreed funding, releases, coverage and unsupported work.
Case choices cannot be conditions of release. Fund accessible intake,
investigators, secure evidence, protected reporting, court access and
the scrutiny docket. Staff exercise expressly assigned routine powers
under public criteria, with protected merit employment and no reliance
on an investigated body's sole systems. An unconflicted merit deputy
may cover chief management for up to 120 elapsed days from a vacancy,
without serial reset, senior appointments or mandate expansion.
Existing officers retain their assigned intake, urgent referral and
completed-report functions after the limit. Publish work that cannot
lawfully proceed and start a fresh open appointment.

**OM10 — Make abuse by the office independently reviewable.** Courts
review jurisdiction, material procedural failure, unlawful refusal,
privacy and evidence demands. Give affected people a direct route
without first complaining to the same office. The Auditor General
examines funds and actual operational controls within AA without
deciding investigative priorities. The enacting Act assigns chief
removal and appeal to separate ordinary judicial formations under
OJ16, with three judges and two votes at first instance and five
different judges and three votes on appeal. Require fair notice, full reply and narrowly stated grounds of serious
misconduct, deliberate abuse, material qualification fraud, persistent
material unlawful failure within actual control or established
inability after reasonable accommodation. Lawful dissent, a
disputed finding or an unfunded workload is insufficient. There is
no confidence dismissal or immunity for the office's own abuse.

**OM11 — Keep evidence and participation independent of software.**
Accept testimony and records through several channels and retain safe
source and correction history. Distinguish actual affected witnesses,
lawful representatives and generated material. AI can help locate
potential overlaps or compare records under bounded, reviewable use.
It cannot close or prioritize away an urgent report, decide whom to
believe, compel disclosure or publish a finding. Examine unknown
coverage, common source and vendor failure before relying on a pattern.
Maintain direct human access and protected originals through tool or
provider replacement.

**OM12 — Adopt with paid migration and test independence in practice.**
Enact the mandate and standing orders prospectively with actual
appointed leadership, separate funds, local acceptance and paid review.
Keep existing complaints, original appeal periods, stronger oversight,
court remedies and testimony protections with lawful successors.
Review after the first complete public follow-up cycle. Test the
government's ability to block appointment, cash, records and hearing
time in combination, and the office's ability to misuse confidential
records. Reopen on displacement of timely relief, exclusion,
unsupported investigations, obstruction, duplicated oversight or a
better funded alternative that preserves independent scrutiny.
