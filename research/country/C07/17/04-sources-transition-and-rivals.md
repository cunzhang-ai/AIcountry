# MO13–MO16: offered capacity, paid transition and a choice that can lose

*Part of proposed C07/17; design 94 research candidate.*

**MO13 — Plan the offer, usage and simultaneous failure separately.** Price
the actual ordinary minimum and assessed essential paths by locality/time,
safe passenger/access spaces, vehicle/driver/assistance skills, work/paid waiting,
deadhead, depots, land, fuel/power, parts, maintenance, renewal, reserve and
dispatch/review. Include fares forgone and resident travel/waiting burdens.
Expected uptake informs operation; it cannot finance guaranteed capacity by
presuming that people refuse. Unused annual seat-kilometres cannot fill an
already full wheelchair bay or a missed time-critical departure.

For100,000 people, full use of two ordinary return journeys weekly is10.4m
return journeys over a52-week reference. At10km per complete return it is104m
covered-user passenger-km; at20km it is208m, before necessary work, school,
clinical or other journeys and additional companion/assistant occupancy/legs.
These are reference quantities, not an exact calendar-year entitlement count.
PA's old150m passenger-km reference therefore proves neither case adequate.
Combine actual chosen purposes/vehicle legs once, without assigning ordinary
life to compulsory commuting or promising the same driver/vehicle twice.
The accompanying cases are authored bounds, not geography, a complete demand
forecast, accepted rosters, qualified prices or a net public saving.

**MO14 — Pay people and identify assets before relying on them.** Distinguish
existing included vehicle/contract capacity from actual new paid acquisition,
lease, retrofit and operating source. Retain titles, concession/security, worker
wages/pensions, tickets, claims and records through lawful successors. TW hours,
breaks, leave/learning and safe commercial/clinical/child qualifications apply;
annual FTE is not a shift. Paid overlap, instruction, recruitment and a failed
operator's accepting replacement need resources. No invented AI gain, free
private car, compulsory household sharing or assumed land supplies the package.
Any assessed cash alternative keeps its legal limits; it cannot waive safety.

**MO15 — Separate old duties from the new ordinary minimum.** An expressly
enacted Act starts the new MO02 ordinary work/care component, MO03 flexible
minimum and associated free-price extension on the first1January at least
18 months after enactment as the candidate default, with funded preparation,
exact source/eligibility dates and notice. Existing school/clinical/safety,
legal/electoral, disability/access, necessary-expense and stronger source-law
journeys retain their actual earlier dates and remedies. No common research
date recommences or narrows them. A paid local staged addition can start earlier
on lawful equal terms without buying a waiver from later applicants.

National law may enact explicit additional cohorts/dates with reasons and paid
bridges; an official may not silently phase an already commenced entitlement
by postcode, queue or readiness certificate. Failure on an enacted date is a
breach requiring existing useful-time protection and elected correction, not
automatic extension. Core insufficiency cannot be solved by cancelling existing
sector trips, forcing family labor or treating newly uncommenced flexibility as
already due. NP's public preparation/disposition duties remain separate.

**MO16 — Compare the real alternatives.** Retain repair of effective current
routes, a scheduled public/plural network, paid demand-responsive provision,
person-controlled journey budgets and private-car-based support as rivals.
The network can share staff/vehicles, lower cost and permit spontaneous contact,
but strand seats and impose waiting/transfers. Demand response can protect sparse
or exceptional access but require more driving, dispatch and vehicles. Cash/car
support can preserve choice where actual roads, safe cars/drivers and maintenance
exist; it excludes non-drivers and does not supply a missing accessible operator.

Show gains and losses for nonusers/non-drivers, disabled people, night workers,
carers, rural residents, high-income subsidized riders, operators/drivers and
taxpayers, including displacement from other services and environmental harm.
Keep landlord/employer exit and informal cooperation usable; no mode is compulsory
merely because its aggregate is cheaper. Reopen on actual unmet arrival, burdens,
unsafe work, control/capture, source failure or a better full alternative. Actual
affected-party choice, authority, safe operation and better lives are unproved.
