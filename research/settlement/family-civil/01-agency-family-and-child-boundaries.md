# Agency, exit and children's independent standing

*Part of [family/civil journeys](README.md); fictional boundary cases.*

| Case | Required proposed distinction and receiving |
|---|---|
| J1 A 17-year-old freely chooses work, seeks confidential care and clicks a long-term loan | B22 permits protected work/direct personal accounts and actual supported bodily choice; it does not bind open-ended child debt. Sexual protection, criminal responsibility and voting ages retain their own rules. Provider/court must support understanding and safety, not let one capacity label decide all acts. |
| J2 A disabled adult's family calls an unwanted choice irrational | No loss of legal capacity or general guardian. Support accessible direct communication, actual B03 mandate and independent challenge. A relative's material interest cannot decide the person's will; a diagnosis or model score is no substitute. |
| J3 A person cannot presently communicate and an expiring bill threatens their home | B23 may preserve the specific asset/payment on best interpretation of their will, with narrow scope, accounts and court review. It cannot authorize medical treatment, make a new will or freeze every resource. A 48-hour hold cannot self-renew; unavailable lawful support remains a service failure. |
| J4 Three adults enter a shared property/care compact; one later leaves | Registration does not create parentage, whole-property ownership or political votes. Identify shared assets and actual shares once, permit unilateral status exit, preserve vested property/support/third-party duties and the others' separable ties. An AI copy is no fourth partner or extra asset share. |
| J5 An abusive partner blocks notice and demands joint mediation before exit | B25's valid unilateral declaration and safe substituted-notice court route prevent the veto. No refuge address disclosure or compelled reconciliation. Notice and B03 mandate revocation have their own effects; ending status does not automatically empty all shared bank accounts. |
| J6 A long-term unpaid carer lacks registry/title entries | Hear the actual common acquisition undertaking and care contribution under B26/B27. Missing paperwork alone cannot erase rights; simple cohabitation alone cannot appropriate a roommate's assets. Independent evidence, safe housing and a reasoned settlement are needed. |
| J7 Adults dispute a child's genetic/intended parentage and demand a DNA verdict from a clinic | Birth identity/services and safe responsible care are received now. A competent child court hears actual legal basis, existing relations and independent child voice. Genetics, marriage and a donor contract each have limited evidential scope; no automatic two-parent ceiling or contract sale of the child. |
| J8 Adults demand automatic equal custody or a blanket no-contact order from an allegation | B29 asks actual safety, continuity, relationships, wishes and workable schedules. A protective order has its own grounds; criminal guilt and child arrangements are distinct. Fund safe contact/receiving rather than make the child repeatedly retell harm. |
| J9 The state withholds housing support, then calls resulting poverty neglect | Receive owed help; poverty is not the removal ground. B31 requires specific serious harm not safely addressable by available less restrictive support. A real emergency needs actual safe care and independent 24-hour hearing, not a new status offense. |
| J10 The placement is unsafe and the duty judge is unavailable at the deadline | No administrative self-extension, abandonment or forced dangerous return is authorized. Obtain immediate independent court access and safe consensual alternatives; disclose the unmet protection/capacity failure. The draft cannot declare that incompatible missing resources were magically supplied. |
| J11 A birth parent is pressured to sign an irrevocable prenatal surrender | B32 rejects binding prenatal consent, sale and per-placement rewards; post-birth independent advice and 30-day withdrawal precede final order. Receive safe care in the meantime. Poverty, disability or an agency's supply target cannot establish grounds to terminate parental status. |
| J12 A candidate parent wants adoption to erase every prior relationship | Stable care need not erase safe lawful ties. A court states actual responsibilities, origin-record protection and supported child voice. Required consent or strictly proved serious persistent abuse/abandonment and less restrictive options need independent findings, not a contract or model prediction. |

These proposed answers preserve value disputes: strong adolescent bodily
autonomy, plural compacts, threshold ages, urgent separation and permanence
can affect people differently. No family, child, court, clinician or advocate
has participated in these examples, and no empirical safety claim follows.
