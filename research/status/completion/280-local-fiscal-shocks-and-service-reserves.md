# Design372. Protect local services through simultaneous fiscal shocks

[MF25–MF30](../../country/C04/08/06-local-fiscal-shocks-and-service-reserves.md)
selects a dated common reserve, prior statutory priority, complete
service packages and a separate later call. It retains local elected
choice and gives residents and councils independently executed review.

[Historical transfers and the constructed case](../../settlement/local-fiscal-shocks-and-service-reserves/01-two-localities-and-one-national-reserve.md)
separate provincial forecasts, reported cash and actual service.
The case tests simultaneous calls, a late supplement, shared supplier
loss and weaker later revenue. Actual national sources, authority,
staffing and outcomes remain open.
