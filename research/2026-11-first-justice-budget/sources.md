# Design500 sources and scope

- England's [2024–25 safe accommodation management information, sections 8 and 11](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/support-in-domestic-abuse-safe-accommodation-2024-to-2025/support-in-domestic-abuse-safe-accommodation)
  records 28,190 referred households that could not be supported and
  identifies capacity constraints for 8,580 of them. Reasons are not
  mutually exclusive and some reporting is voluntary. These are reported
  households in that service dataset, not a population prevalence,
  a case-matched comparison or a candidate service forecast.
- [Domestic Abuse Act 2021, Part 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/part/4/2025-11-07)
  supplies the distinct English statutory framework for accommodation-based
  support. No equivalent candidate authority transfers with the comparison.
- The [European Court of Human Rights Article 5 guide, first appearance and release](https://ks.echr.coe.int/documents/37821/7237693/Guide_Art_5_ENG.pdf/2035ed60-a47f-b12f-db77-a839dfc3d879?download=true&t=1776172172877&version=7.0)
  explains the Convention's requirement for prompt judicial control,
  speedy challenge and release where detention is unlawful. This
  source does not set the candidate's 24-hour ceiling.
- The separately scoped [independent remedy execution comparison](../settlement/independent-remedy-execution/02-matched-remedy-and-second-call.md)
  and [criminal appearance rules](../country/C08/12/06-court-appearance-and-pretrial-liberty.md)
  support the project decision and its boundaries. The timing,
  resources and events in the joined example are constructed.
