# OE01–OE05: an organization needs actual organs, assets and authority

*Part of [C05/13](../13-enterprise-and-association-constitutions.md); design118 candidate.*

**OE01 — Association is free; personality is a defined option.** People may
cooperate, speak, organize, form informal groups or trade individually without
first incorporating or passing a political/AI test. A registered legal person
has separate assets, organs, capacity and service/claim routes under this Act,
not a natural person's franchise or a sovereign mandate. Future personhood forms
remain open to explicit revision; a software process is not silently made a
citizen, director or an independent voter. B01–B04 rights and supported decisions
continue; no universal identity/family ledger is required for ordinary cooperation.

**OE02 — Forms with different consequences.** Offer sole trading; genuine
consensual general partnership with disclosed joint business obligations;
limited-liability share company; cooperative; registered non-distributing member
association; and asset-locked purpose foundation. A public enterprise uses a
specified competent public charter plus compatible company rules. Trust/custody
is a fiduciary asset relationship, not separate personality by label; name actual
trustees, purpose/beneficiaries, assets, duties and direct B/NB remedies.
Marriage, friendship, a shared platform or jointly editing a proposal does not
create a partnership. Unincorporated groups may sue/be sued through authorized
representatives/common funds under B; mere membership is no personal guarantee.
Those who actually contract/guarantee or cause a wrong keep their own liability.

**OE03 — Registration by objective law.** One actual capable founder can form a
share company; a cooperative or registered association needs at least three
consenting real members. A foundation needs lawful contributed purpose assets
and at least three trustees. Under18 property/debt and unsupported agency retain
B21–B23; no account/co-signature creates a child's open-ended liability. Publish
name, form, lawful purpose, actual service address/secure agent, constitution,
responsible natural-person organs and required control/capital records. Do not
publish an unsafe home address instead of a usable lawful service route.

Basic formation/annual filing is free, with paid accessible assistance. Registrar
decides a usable complete application within five working days, giving specific
correctable deficiencies/refusal and direct court access; no serial clock reset
by unreasonable demands. Delay supplies no fictitious certificate or consent.
A court can order lawful registration on actual criteria. Pre-entry contracts
bind only their actual contracting/authorized parties until a lawful assumption/
novation with required assent; no fake retroactive corporate immunity or all-member
debt. Registration is no sector license, investment approval or solvency assurance.

**OE04 — Natural responsibility and bounded automation.** Directors/trustees
are identifiable adults with legal capacity and actual source-specific competence;
diagnosis is no exclusion. A legal-person owner acts through actual representatives,
not an unaccountable corporate director replacing every responsible human.
B03 mandates can authorize routine automated transactions within shared ceilings,
including useful offline allocations; replicas do not each own the whole limit.
Reserved constitutional/member/board acts need their actual competent decision,
not a model's best-interest score or an agent's invented vote. Delegation does
not extinguish entity/operator/vendor B duties or authorize public coercion.

**OE05 — Know actual control without total exposure.** Entity records trace
legal title, actual ultimate decisive control and persons with at least10% economic
or voting rights aggregated through their actual controlled nominees. Smaller
rights with decisive control still count; matching models/vendors/views alone do
not prove it. Keep accurate original chains, source dates and protected challenge.
Public register shows material controllers/rights bands and actual organ authority,
with specific safety redaction/substitute service reasons independently reviewable.
Protected beneficial detail is accessible to competent courts/authorities under PD,
not a public membership/donor/belief list. For non-distributing
associations, ordinary one-member votes alone do not trigger public controller
listing by percentage, even in a small group; actual special decisive control or
private asset entitlement must be established separately. Necessary protected
member/authority evidence stays available under lawful PD safeguards, not a
routine public belief map. Counts/disclosure do
not establish independence, approval of a purpose, asset title or lawful funding.
