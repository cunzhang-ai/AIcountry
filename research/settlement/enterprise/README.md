# Enterprise constitutions: actual power, liability and lawful exit

2 November 2026 · Design118 research selection, not adopted national law

[OE01–OE40](../../country/C05/13-enterprise-and-association-constitutions.md) is
the canonical topic. Design544 selects lawful charter choice as the ordinary
default. The numerical design below preserves the Design118 alternative. [Dependencies](../commitments/31-enterprise-and-association-constitutions.md)
joins paid work/source/court duties; [completion](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/status/completion/26-enterprise-and-country-integration.md)
separates design from actual authority/capacity/lives.

The problem is organizational dependence, not just access to corporate filing.
An owner may control work, information and exit without owning a country; a
cooperative or public label can also conceal concentrated power. Conversely,
forcing every friendship/commons into formal government can destroy useful
association. Keep forms voluntary and protect actual persons/rights across them.

| Whole arrangement | Control, benefit and retained loss | Current disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Investor boards plus labor floors | Owners choose strategy, bearing capital risk; worker bargaining/courts can constrain abuse. Faster decisions/financing are possible, not assured; work dependence remains. | Real narrower rival; not sufficient default for large covered enterprises. |
| Floors/councils plus workforce board shares/parity | Capital and labor retain separate constituencies, original title and continuing dues. Minority seats give information/voice, not a veto; parity requires a cross-class winning coalition. | Selected hypothesis from50/1000 actual workers, subject to paid capacity and lawful migration. |
| Worker ownership/parity at every size | Can change residual ownership and voice more deeply; can concentrate job/savings risk, exclude nonmember labor/users and constrain capital entry. | Real structural rival, voluntary entry allowed; not compulsory free acquisition. |
| Wider public/common ownership | Can integrate access and service obligations; party/manager/vendor capture, gross public risk and ineffective exit remain possible. | Elected funded public mandates permitted, not a universal ownership assumption. |
| Voluntary member association/foundation | Protect purpose and cooperation beyond investment/work; insiders, donors or trustees may entrench themselves. | Distinct rights/duties and courts; no claim that a private foundation is democratic government. |

The six-seat standard has2 workforce/4 owner seats at50,3/3 at1000, with no
chair's extra vote. Four united owners can pass an ordinary first-stage decision;
at parity neither three-owner nor three-worker bloc alone can. Worker members
retain pay/union rights; a cooperative substitutes its board only if all covered
workers, including nonmembers, actually have the required equal franchise/seats.
Public sovereign and special financial organs keep their actual statutory scopes.

Registration is free basic entry with assisted correction, protected original
control evidence and safe public authority records, not a public donor/belief
map. Private capital statements are not government guarantees. OE27 expressly
adds a limited office-error entity/authority-certificate reliance exposure;
OE29 adds only a precise prospective court-decided B-only VD14. Neither is an
extension of every old assurance or regulatory penalty by analogy.

No book profit, unpaid subscription, internal group loan, AI copy or hoped
recovery supplies cash. Separate actual assets/title, capital losses, paid board/
cover/audit/advice, registry/ballots/courts/custody, public assurance and transition.
NF legal-unit tax and protected S/Y/client/insurance/pension/FX accounts remain.
Ordinary new duties start180 calendar days after competent enactment, board
restructuring12 calendar months; stronger old rights and WL/TW/AS dates continue.
