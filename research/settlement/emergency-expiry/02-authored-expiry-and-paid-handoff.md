# Separate an expiring restriction from necessary work

The hypothetical declaration reaches its RX06 initial ceiling at
hour 336 with no timely renewal. An emergency-source road
restriction must end at the boundary. Assume ordinary law
authorizes traffic protection on a much narrower site only with
an independent decision, verified necessity and accepted ordinary
operator. A pump can continue moving water from a lawful worksite
under an independently valid ordinary rescue source. If there is
no qualified takeover, an existing limited authority permits a
safe shutdown by hour 336. The authority to pay each accrued
claim survives under the fixture's standing law.

| Work and clock | Timely ordinary handoff | Late receiver or blocked money |
|---|---|---|
| Exceptional road restriction at hour 336 | Ends | Ends |
| Narrower ordinary protection starting at hour 336 | Independent decision and receiver in place at hour 335, starts on its own source | Receiver arrives at hour 338. No ordinary protection for hours 336–338. Report residual harm and use lawful warnings and voluntary routing. |
| Pump and staffed receiving through hour 348 | Receiver accepts at 335 with two eligible paid units, then operates through 348 | If qualified receipt or two units are missing at 336, safe shutdown occurs under its separate source. Ongoing water removal is exposed. |
| Accrued wage and supplier claim, three units due at hour 340 | Standing payer delivers three independently authorized units | If the ordinary payer is blocked, an independent payer delivers the same eligible amount if separately authorized and usable. Otherwise the original claim and deadline remain. |
| Independent petition and hearing at hour 337 | Separate reviewer, support and lawful record access available | Outage of the former command portal uses an already staffed offline route. Without a live receiver the hearing is exposed. |

The two pump units and the three accrued units have different
scopes. The shutdown is staffed and funded in every tested path.
No routing plan can use the three accrued units for operating the
pump. Each protected clock is independent. A cached dispatch token
and a purported renewal received at hour 338 have no effect on the
expired source. A narrower independent traffic order can take
effect prospectively at hour 338 if its actual source, actor and
acceptance then support it.

A standalone statutory recovery mandate is a rival. It can enable
useful new tasks, including narrow compulsory powers in some
countries, at the cost of another activation, control and expiry
chain. An integrated public service with stronger ordinary
preparedness could reduce the need for any declaration. Compare
both on sustained protection, concentrated authority, cost and
civil rights. The scenario asserts neither the existence of
actual capacity nor the benefits of stopping a real operation.
