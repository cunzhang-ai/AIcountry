# Primary records for public access and useful-time disclosure

- [Council of Europe Convention on Access to Official Documents](https://rm.coe.int/tromso-convention-eng/1680a2a0f2),
  Articles 1–8 and 10, supplies comparators for standing, record scope,
  necessary limits, handling, partial disclosure, review and publication.
  Its explanatory report distinguishes government records held on behalf
  of an authority from unrelated private information. The proposed
  clocks, domestic institutions and execution rule here are independent.
- [UK Cabinet Office, revised annual 2025 bulletin](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/freedom-of-information-statistics-annual-2025/freedom-of-information-statistics-annual-2025-bulletin),
  sections 2, 3 and 7, documents covered institutions, volume, statutory
  and extended timing, and the National Archives workload change.
  These statistics cover monitored central-government bodies and include
  applicable environmental-information requests within that scope.
- [Official published dataset](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6aa2c4a5c5796a7a179c6691/foi-statistics-annual-2025-published-data.csv)
  is the source for the [local annual extract](annual-source-extract.csv).
  Select rows with Quarter exactly 2024 or 2025, retain the body and the
  six source count fields, and preserve the original values. Do not add
  quarterly rows to annual rows. The original file fetched for this
  edition had SHA-256
  `4a5fe06429a693815ea30c4bb01b13924f1c57b4593864edfa469c1c52bafe91`.
- [UK Information Commissioner's Office, response timescales](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/foi/guide-to-managing-an-foi-request/timescales/)
  explains ordinary first receipt and the base response window within
  its actual legal framework. It does not prescribe this proposal's
  urgent delivery or ordinary court route.

The derived timing rates are arithmetic using those source categories.
They are not causal performance estimates or measures of useful-time
receipt. The hearing, work costs and release conditions are constructed.
