# F10. The final court is vacant and the cabinet refuses urgent relief

*Part of [Whole-country stress](../../FOUNDING-AND-STRESS.md); integrated design 55 research, not a real-world result.*

**Events:** A statutory benefit is unlawfully stopped during a budget lapse.
The claimant needs medicine and files an urgent case. The constitutional
forum lacks a lawful quorum after appointments fail; cabinet says no judge
may act. A vendor withholds the original service record, legal-aid payment
is delayed, and a minister orders treasury not to honor an interim award.
The agency also claims it has no medicine or worker to deliver the service.

**Candidate response:** A preassigned ordinary court retains narrow urgent
individual-rights competence and preserves the first filing date without
claiming a final nationwide constitutional ruling. Published assignment and
conflict rules select the judge; funded counsel, the original evidence route
and an accessible hearing allow both sides to be heard. The court distinguishes
an unlawful refusal from a real lack of cash or medicine and makes a specific
reviewable order naming the agency, treasury channel, due time and evidence
of delivery. A prior statutory substitute can release records or due payment
if the responsible officer refuses, within the same appropriation and audit.
Any personal sanction for defiance follows enacted elements and fair process.
The Assembly faces the disclosed real shortage and the unfilled claim; neither
the court nor treasury invents a tax, supplier, constitutional judge or winner
of an appointment dispute. A lawful eventual final forum reviews the reserved
constitutional question under its own jurisdiction.

**What remains unproved:** the ordinary bench, clerk, counsel, original record,
medicines, payment rail and substitute executor may be absent or controlled
by one coalition. A true resource shortage cannot be cured by a paper writ;
an official's refusal is not proven merely because delivery failed. Parallel
ordinary-court orders might conflict. Test staff, money, law and receipt under
a noncooperating government before claiming effective judicial independence.
