# Design474 decision record

**Baseline and procedure.** Use Design473 and the [working method](../FRAMEWORK.md).
The [baseline hashes](baseline.json) preserve the previous edition.
Existing utility, service, budget, procurement and court law retain
their respective authority.

**Selection.** For material flexible commitments count interruption,
deferred work and actual restarts on an operator-approved common
connection schedule. Keep standing protected loads, funded ordinary
service and delivery through replenishment. Check the finance
separately, including existing payable purchases and adjustments
actually released by the due date. This governs prospective release,
budget comparison and reporting of avoided cost.

**Rivals and risk.** An earlier firm block can be expensive but
deliver on time. A fully accepted split can cost less under new
purchase terms. It depends on additional qualified receiving
windows and safe continuation. Paid ordinary delivery can protect
the underlying service with a deferred optional improvement.
A material workload may require a separate public reserve.
Report the forgone work, actual cost and people who bear any transfer.

**Compatibility and reopening.** Preserve original grid,
supplier, worker, public-service and review rights. AI can compare
operation and source evidence within lawful scope. It cannot
reserve capacity or declare savings usable. Reopen when validated
electrical headroom, safe receiving, qualifying contractual cash,
a stronger ordinary route or material harms change the decision.
The [case and sources](sources.md) establish a bounded comparison.
No actual participation, operating authority or measured improvement
follows from it.
