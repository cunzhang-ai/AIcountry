# Design442. Preserve access through electoral discovery services

Design441 is the pinned predecessor. The existing C03 voter guide,
paid distribution, incorrect-instruction remedies and public media
leave a possible gap when an unpaid search, feed or assistant becomes
a practical gateway. This revision used AI assistance and parallel
review. Select a narrowly designated, user-requested discovery path
with a competent independent reviewer and a continuing public route.

A stronger public route alone protects editorial autonomy and can
serve people without a commercial account. It can miss voters who
cannot reach it before the deadline. A scoped direct-source path can
reach them while preserving editorial choice, at the cost of oversight
and possible designation abuse. Wider ranking control would give
public bodies undue influence and is not selected. Require credible
evidence of dependence and failure of practical substitutes. Protect
queries and political privacy. Expire a designation when necessity
ends. Preserve the voter and contestant's direct review and actual
access across changes in provider, index or format.

The [sources](sources.md) describe a bounded legal comparator and
an open consultation. The worked case tests common-source failure
and overlapping access. Neither establishes real reliance, costs,
election outcomes, independent qualification or affected participation.
No new law, legal authority or appropriation is in force here.

Next compare independent, assisted and provider routes for one
actual voting task before the earliest relevant deadline. Include
affected voters and contestants, test shared dependencies and
measure qualified cost and receipt. Reopen on timely public
substitution, misuse of designation, privacy loss or late correction.
