# EV13–EV16: correction without automatic retroactive law

**EV13 — Correct a series; determine a claim under its law.** Append corrected
observations, methods and vintages with reasons rather than erase history. Statistical
revision alone neither retroactively reprices tax/benefits/contracts nor freezes a
lawfully owed correction. Responsible payment/tax offices apply their source-period,
indexation, refund/recovery and provisional-protection rules; courts hear disputes.
Missing evidence is not zero liability or authority to deem an unpaid claim received.

**EV14 — Protect against the protector.** Independent source custody, ordinary
audit, protected reporting and courts can examine the service's own classifications,
contracts, conflicts, chief selection and access decisions. It cannot control the
only record/key, complaint intake or executor of an order against itself. Publish
safe control/funding accounts and correction failures. A hostile majority, captured
court or unavailable alternate can still defeat protection; labels promise no cure.

**EV15 — Prospective adoption and continuing obligations.** A legitimate Act names
the start, first appointment, paid staff/continuity designations, sources, archives,
pending cases and legal-series successors. Initial career designations use
C04 competition, never bypass EV04 to choose a permanent chief. It cannot approve itself or validate an
incumbent's own protected institutional change; C14/RR holds remain. Preserve old
lawful claims, privacy and releases until authorized migration. Vacancy or budget
failure is no readiness extension of already enacted duties or a new acting mandate.

**EV16 — A choice that can be displaced.** Retain this simple parliamentary-chief
reference over an ordinary ministry head, whose direct Cabinet accountability can
reduce appointment friction but increases release-pressure risk; reject a self-perpetuating expert
board's closed public authority. Three-fifths selection can deadlock and parliamentary
capture remains possible. Reopen on suppression, exclusion, ineffective correction or
a better funded plural arrangement. Actual independence/public assent is unknown.
Finish this governance choice; no new office claims omniscient forecasts or makes
every private transaction a prerequisite to completing the reference country design.
