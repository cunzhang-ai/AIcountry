## Diagnosis and noninterchangeable reasons

A visible balance may represent owned cash/security, a unit of market assets,
a private contingent insurance debt, a DB promise, a lender/issuer obligation,
a clearing commitment or a public statutory benefit. Labeling everything a
bank, applying bank8% capital or giving all products deposit insurance would
misallocate title, liquidity risk, market risk and public loss. Conversely,
blanket prevention of intermediated finance can restrict risk-sharing, credit,
choice and independent enterprise, or concentrate power in one public provider.

Values: ordinary people's security/agency, meaningful democratic control of
public risk, fair private choice, continuity and actual correction. Definitions:
actual title, legal claim/issuer, readily usable same-currency cash, accrued
promise, material information, defined fault and distinct public/private source.
Hypotheses: retail10% issuer/110% gross exposure,20% redemption stress,90day
illiquid interval minimum,72h exceptional suspension,99.5% one-year capital,
20% lender equity, five-year pension restoration and universal CCP cover-two.
These are selected normative/method parameters, not estimated local safety.

The problem may be deceptive title/mediator power, mismatch/leverage, sponsor
underfunding, concentrated ownership, missing consumer recourse or insufficient
basic public protection. More examinations alone do not distinguish these or
prove improved lives. Preserve disagreement on how much private risk a society
should permit; aggregate stability cannot replace effective public control.

## Genuine whole alternatives and selection

Compare no-change generic bank/conduct language, licensed plural finance,
public safe-payment/public-pension provision with fewer private promises,
broad disclosed private contracting/later civil suit, member/cooperative
intermediation and levy-funded investor/insurer guarantee. Public provision can
reduce runs while concentrating dependence; disclosure lowers entry cost while
shifting proof/tail burdens to customers. Cooperation changes voice but not
correlated local loss. A guarantee improves continuity but needs funded coverage,
fees, headroom, exclusions and public incidence; it is not free trust.

Select NB01–NB36 plural licensed finance with separate title/promise/failure and
no new automatic investor/insurer guarantee. Essential public protection and
Y/S solidarity remain outside product purchase and private investment returns.
This can lose to a stronger whole alternative; no obligation to retain NB by
absorbing all objections into a more complicated regulator.
