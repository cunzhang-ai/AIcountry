# Four services, two deadlines and a new call on the reserve

This authored first-budget case uses an election 26 days after the
call and a household payment deadline on day 33. The dates reflect
the calendar shape of the observed comparator. Money, tasks,
staffing, authority, due clocks and common failure in the case
are invented planning assumptions, with no conversion into DKK
and no claim that the actual city experienced the outage.

| Protected function | Day 26 core cash due | Day 33 additional core cash due | Actual acceptance required in the fixture |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Home nursing or necessary care | 5 | 5 | Qualified staff, access, consent and usable receipt on both dates |
| Household support | 0 | 6 | An authorized benefit payer and accessible timely receipt on day 33 |
| Urgent independent court remedy | 3 | 2 | Lawful court and separately authorized execution by each original deadline |
| Poll access and subsequent election evidence | 4 | 2 | Properly staffed access on day 26, protected records and timely challenge on day 33 |
| Total due by each date, incremental | 12 | 15 | 27 units cumulative, reserved separately by lawful purpose |

All 27 ordinary units are on the correct protected lines and cannot
pay the incremental reserve. The ordinary court, election and
benefit routes share one identity and signing dependency. Declare
its loss on day 26 and persistence through day 33. The ordinary
care route remains independent and accepted. An alternate's
records, payer, receiving site, staff and authority are separately
verified only in rows with that explicit assumption.

Flexible cash for the additional operations is two units by day 26
and six units cumulatively by day 33. Recurring funded standby is
limited to two units. The competent budget can permit one actual
later receipt to be accelerated. If it does, the cumulative six
stays six.

|---|---:|---:|---|
| A. Retain the shared stack | 0 / 0 | 0 | Care continues. Both electoral and court deadlines and the later benefit are exposed. |
| B. Contract qualified emergency cover as each event occurs | 3 / 7 | 0 | The first external roster can cover the poll and court if financed. The second uses one clerk for two simultaneous duties, so the day 33 pair of court and benefit cannot both be verified. |

| D. Use independently staffed local intake and direct ordinary delivery | 2 / 4 | 3 | Can cover all duties with an added recurring source. Judicial, payment and electoral authority retain their separate lawful owners. |

C spends two units by day 26 on the separate electoral route and
one on lawful court execution. On day 33 one unit each pays for
the separate benefit receiving, court execution and election
evidence routes. These amounts include their distinct paid staff
and custody in the fixture. The ordinary care line funds its own
accepted staff on both dates.
The earlier receipt raises day 26 availability to three. It leaves
only three additional units available on day 33. B still fails
cumulative cash and independent staffing. D is a genuine structural
rival. It has a lower early cash need and higher continuing cost.

Before replenishment on day 33, suppose a second urgent case
needs another separately qualified, accepted execution route with a
one-unit activation cost. The necessary person is available under
the assumptions. C now needs seven cumulative flexible units.
The existing six cannot pay that additional route. None of the
protected core balances is lawfully transferable in the case.
Record the exposed court execution and use existing prior-law
protection and elected correction. Funding the first two dates
gives no authority or source for the extra case. A discretionary
new project also fails the remaining finance test.

The selected C route is conditional on the exact authority,
qualified receipt and independent workers. If the earlier source is
late or the separate record route fails, record the affected duties
and preserve lawful substitutes and remedies. No test here proves
actual staffing, cash, operational outcomes or coverage of the
whole national budget.
