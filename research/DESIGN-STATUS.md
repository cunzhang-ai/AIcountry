# Country design: current choices, conflicts and priorities

Integrated design548 · Research proposal, not enacted law

**The selected country is a replaceable parliamentary democracy with individual
need-based security, universal essential services, pooled earned rights, plural
production/worker voice, national/local public responsibility and bounded AI.**
Its intended gain is practical independence: people can sustain ordinary life,
leave dependence, oppose government and receive correction. Neither prosperity
nor an AI's performance substitutes for equal political standing.

## Five whole arrangements remain in the comparison

[CS13–CS15](country/C01/03/06-current-country-and-strong-alternatives.md) owns this
choice. Options carry common fundamental democratic/bodily/essential protections and
actual valid due/accrued claims. Unadopted candidate numbers may differ with
explicit human/source/transition consequences; unknown adequacy stays unknown.
They are not equal-cost calibrated countries or a menu of convenient features.

| Arrangement | Current disposition / real tradeoff |
|---|---|
| Service-first plural country | Selected working bargain; need assessment and common providers can still intrude, exclude or capture |
| Strong retain/repair | Live alternative: less unnecessary replacement, but inherited harms, democratic repair and transition are not free |
| Territorial direct-public essential provision | Live alternative: fewer contracting dependencies, with actual title/work/management, monopoly and user/worker-control costs |
| Rights-preserving U+C2 | Live alternative: greater cash independence, with full tax and transition consequences. [Matched receipt and financing](settlement/cash-alternatives-and-actual-receipt/01-proof-payment-and-the-same-deadline.md) tests delayed proof and failed payment while preserving earned and additional rights. |
| Decentralized mutual/common provision | Live alternative: local/worker/user control, with unequal resources, informal domination, practical exit and national residual duties |

Different employment/stabilization, ecological, monetary and ownership choices
can also challenge the bargain. No score averages away incompatible public powers,
rights or value choices. A justified better whole rival may displace the preference. [Lived outcomes and budget choices](settlement/better-lives-and-country-choices/01-common-budget-different-lives.md) tests when a narrower delivery rival can displace an early stage after an observed dependency changes.
