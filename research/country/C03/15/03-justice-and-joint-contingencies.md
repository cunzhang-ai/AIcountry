# Independent useful-time justice and complete contingencies

Use assigned career qualification under ER and direct unconflicted ordinary
courts under EA16/E13/B17/OJ, not a new electoral tribunal or AI appeals office.
The urgent duty is consideration within twenty-four hours and earlier useful
time where needed, not a promise that every merits trial ends within one day.
Notice, answerable original proof, individual assistance, counsel, hearing,
reasoned disposition, service and received execution remain separate tasks.
An allegation alone neither excludes a contestant nor suspends the opponent's baseline.

B20 permits opt-in common claims. Offer common-issue scheduling only where a
court finds the same material legal/factual question with adequate notice,
representation, conflict protection and actual consent within lawful scope.
Keep personal assent, residence, coercion, source discrepancies and relief
individual. No party, database cluster or AI may bind nonconsenting people in
status cases. A participant may use an individual claim; shared evidence is not
proof of common interests. Group size or model-assumed consent is not a judgment.
The 80-percent/64-family sensitivity is an unobserved hypothesis, not a budget saving.

Under ER07, provisional nomination still requires a strong likelihood, fair
notice, actual with/without count testing and actual qualification before any
affected certification. **Test all outcome-relevant combinations of unresolved
facts**, or use a qualified method proving equivalent complete coverage. Compare
actual member identity, filled seats and lawful preference/ballot interpretation,
not just one party's total. Do not silently transfer a test of one changed fact
to several changes or assume disputed ballot meaning is fixed by a software fixture.

Counterexample: two seats, A100/B90 votes; A has A1/A2 and B has B1/B2.
Removing either A nominee alone preserves one A seat, while removing both gives
both seats to B. Even the one-removal cases elect different A members.
The executable fixture enumerates all four binary worlds using already interpreted
votes and frozen orders. It supplies no actual qualification or ballot semantics.
Its eight-unknown computation bound is not a legal candidate limit; above that,
representation is unresolved and a different qualified proof is required.
No incomplete model can certify a result, exclude candidates or extend a lawful clock.

Appointments must already satisfy OJ funded posts, open selection, secure careers,
conflict/assignment rules and lawful accepted deployment with paid release from
other duties. A short election drill creates no judicial qualification or acting
judge. External personnel require actual lawful appointment/permission, not a
list of foreign names. If timely lawful capacity fails, retain claims and seek
existing direct relief; record the failure rather than create default qualification,
default exclusion, a new emergency sovereign or an extended incumbent term.
