# WL06–WL12: daily work does not place the whole person under the employer

*Part of [C05/12](../12-work-contracts-and-collective-power.md); design 117 candidate.*

**WL06 — Predictable paid hours.** State guaranteed ordinary hours and actual
availability limits. After13 complete weeks of a sustained ordinary pattern,
guaranteed hours are no less than the higher of agreed ordinary hours and the
representative13-week ordinary-hours average, with actual leave/illness and
seasonal pattern treated fairly rather than as zero. Do not convert overtime
into unsafe required hours or exceed TW. A genuinely worker-chosen intermittent
arrangement can remain variable with a real right to refuse, no punishment and
periodic assisted review; an employer-written waiver alone proves no such choice.
TW's seven-day material-change notice and earlier/stronger terms remain.

**WL07 — Real changes, not deemed consent.** Material pay/hours/place/task
changes need actual individual agreement or a lawfully binding collective
mechanism within its scope, usable reasons/time and protected old claims. Continued
work to avoid hunger, silence or agent approval outside mandate is no consent.
Do not fire and rehire solely to evade this protection. A genuine operational
need may use fair redundancy/termination with its costs, never fictitious agreement.
Collective terms cannot erase a better individual contract without valid lawful
assent or waive common floors. Necessary short safety action retains its separate
prior law, paid time, minimal scope and independent review.

**WL08 — Equality and portable standing.** Recruitment, pay, promotion, training,
work allocation and exit follow civil equality law, reasonable accommodation and
proved job requirements. No politics, pregnancy, lawful family role, disability,
union choice, lawful complaint or nationality proxy may replace actual grounds.
Migrant/irregular-status workers retain due wages and safe report/claim access;
reporting unpaid work is no new basis to collect unrelated immigration data.
Residence/admission and lawful employment permissions retain their separate law.

**WL09 — Safe work and protected voice.** Employer organizes actual training,
equipment, risk control, rest and qualified safe cover. A worker can stop/refuse
specifically reasonably believed imminent serious unsafe work and report with
protection from retaliation, prompt independent assessment and actual safe
alternative. A good-faith report need not ultimately prove the hazard; intentional
false accusations retain fair proof. Prior accepted immediate safety/handover
duties continue without an indefinite work or strike exemption. Inspection has
lawful access limits; homes are not open public workplaces for all searches.

**WL10 — Consequential AI and surveillance.** Actual employer remains responsible
for its delegated hiring/pay/rating/scheduling/discipline. Give intelligible
reasons, relevant original facts, protected challenge and a competent accountable
human decision-maker before final contested adverse action, with necessary
interim safety steps separately bounded. Vendor secrecy does not bar protected
court/inspector evidence. PD purpose/necessity and individual privacy protections
cannot be waived by collective vote, performance target or automated consent.
No whole-life tracking, mandatory intimate recording or political-belief scoring.

**WL11 — Practical mobility and knowledge.** Post-employment noncompete and
no-poach restraints on covered workers are unenforceable for future work after
this law's prospective start. Genuine confidentiality, nonmisuse of protected
source material, intellectual-property title and independently proved wrongdoing
retain KR/PD/B law, not a disguised employment ban. Ordinary skill/experience is
not an employer-owned person. No recruitment/passport debt or mandatory-training
charge buys labor control. A separate genuinely chosen portable qualification
can have a fair actual-cost agreement, never punitive repayment, forced labor
or a deduction below protected needs without actual source-law process.

**WL12 — Voluntary ties, not compulsory unpaid production.** Off-duty speech,
association, private cooperation and informal care do not become the employer's
resource. Genuine volunteers retain freedom to stop; required productive work,
moderation, training or AI monitoring cannot be renamed free community contribution.
Different media, async tasks and copied agents keep the same actual person/time
and mandate limits. Worker co-owners retain the common floor where they actually
perform dependent work; voting shares do not by themselves prove free control
or waive employment. True independent/self-managed arrangements need facts,
not automatic employee status or an exemption from all protections.

[EB01–EB04](../16/01-covered-loss-consent-and-personal-control.md) allows actual
prospectively agreed short-time within WL06/07, with disclosed personal spell/
quota cost, end/return terms and no employer-owned claim. Public money neither
supplies consent nor pays hidden worked hours, already owed wages or TW's
retained-pay shorter week. Refusal keeps actual WL/needs rights, not a new job.

[CL07–CL12](../17/02-choice-absence-and-return.md) now specifies qualifying
care absence/actual same-or-equivalent return and continuity, with no employer
eligibility veto. Genuine independently proved WL redundancy/closure, fixed-term
expiry and cover-worker dues retain their actual grounds, not an automatic job
for life or permission to penalize leave. CL gives no proxy constitutional office.

[CW05–CW06](../../C07/24/02-work-time-and-public-delivery.md) joins compatible
early care with a work schedule. TW05 and actual agreement, justified
refusal, due pay and independent correction govern. A childcare offer
supplies no authority to impose a shift or track a family.

[WB protected reporting](../../C08/31-protected-disclosure-and-evidence.md)
adds direct outside access, a civil retaliation test and timely remedies.
Keep existing stronger labour duties, safe-work refusal and other
contract and employment claims on their own terms.

[TW21–TW28](../10/06-usable-time-and-pay-at-commencement.md) adds a
no-agreement roster default, actual released time and pay protection at
each statutory hours reduction. TW22 proposes a narrow temporary
placement exception to WL07, limited to the shorter schedule. WL06
and the other WL07 protections keep their scope.

[WL47–WL53](09-workplace-ai-and-effective-worker-control.md) qualifies
material workplace automation before reliance and verifies independent
correction. Preserve separate agreement, safety, pay and privacy duties.
