# Shared capacity and unresolved legal specifications

*Design 56 reconciliation; dependencies from current topics, no measured overuse or enacted allocation.*

These collisions identify where joint allocation is necessary. They do not
justify multiplying the old budget by the number of functions or inventing
workers. Distinct resources inside a class remain non-interchangeable.

| Shared dependency | Competing or overlapping duties | Required reconciliation |
|---|---|---|
| Clinical shifts, medicines and receiving sites | L01 prevention, L02 crisis, L03 violence response, L07 general health/care, L08 relief, L09 reproductive care and R01 emergency care | Assign specialty and hours, actual baseline inclusion, lawful urgency, continuing appointments, qualified cover and receipt; no assumed spare clinician |
| Paid care and safe accommodation | D10 family protection, L02 crisis, L03 safety, L07 disability/later-life care, R02 displacement and R03 reception | Distinguish clinically suitable places, voluntary consent, household support and lawful custody; one bed or carer cannot accept simultaneous incompatible uses |
| Independent legal work and execution | D03 equality, D05 campaign repair, D10 civil claims, D11 courts, D12 criminal safeguards, L02/L03 liberty/protection and R11 border appeal | Count distinct case roles, recusal, clerks, counsel, notice and execution before due time; the old justice envelope has no function-level allocations |
| Elected attention and source records | D04/D06 ordinary law/budget and handover, D08 shared allocations, R01 emergency renewal, R03 treaties, R08 transition and K02 correction | Separate ordinary workload, emergency sittings and opposition access; shared hearings must retain distinct burdens and reasons |
| Domestic cash, Treasury and clearing | Every duty; L06 transfers, D05 campaign payments, D11 judgment execution and R04 debt/payment liabilities | Assign due-date cash once, preserve claim status and trace failed payment; a common operator can disable service and review together |
| Foreign settlement, freight and safe stocks | Clinical medicines, L11 food, R01 safety, R05 power, R06 animal care, R08 grid replacement, R09 logistics and R11 border custody | Separate contracts, currency, shipping, lawful release, cold chain and last-mile acceptance; no domestic cash model establishes imports |
| Land, water, grids and construction | L10 housing/mobility, R02 recovery, R05 essentials, R07 planning, R08 ecology and R10 sanitation | Assign location, ecological floor, crews, connection dates, maintenance and downstream duties; a common site cannot satisfy conflicting projects |
| Records, connectivity and resident time | D02 status, D03 accommodation, D04 vote evidence, D11 courts and K01/K03 AI/cyber routes | Test common credentials/vendors, privacy, notice receipt, nonusers and alternate records; an extra form shifts work to people without creating capacity |

## Clauses that a numerical model cannot supply

| Specification still needed | Why it changes the whole package | Owning current design |
|---|---|---|
| Exact standing schedule, payment dates and allocation limits | A budget lapse preserves only duties actually authorized in prior law; cash allocation cannot be an office's new ranking of rights | [C06 continuity](../../country/C06/04-shortfall-and-continuity.md), [authority proposal](../../authority/01-public-authority-and-budget.md) |
| Entitlement content, need/quality/time rules and interim service | “Effective access” has different urgency and staffing consequences for health, school, care and cash; first receipt protects a date, not universal priority | [C07 access](../../country/C07/01-effective-access.md), [distinct chains](../../country/C07/03-health-education-and-care.md) |
| Competent court, independent case allocation and execution powers | A new right creates workload only a lawful forum and actual executor can discharge; civil inference is not criminal guilt | [C08 courts](../../country/C08/02-courts-and-effective-orders.md), [criminal chain](../../country/C08/03-criminal-justice-and-custody.md) |
| Scarcity triggers, protected uses, materially equal claims and renewal | One reserve cannot serve every emergency; comparing expected harms does not create allocation authority | [C06 scarcity](../../country/C06/05-cross-system-scarcity.md) |
| Shared-place authority, local funding and successor duties | A local or territorial redesign can move costs and leave a national guarantee unreceived | [C04 authority](../../country/C04/01-national-local-authority.md), [C14 transfer](../../country/C14/02/01-opening-inventory.md) |
| Safe data sharing, refusal and correction during joint service | One family account or controller could make access faster while removing consent and independent evidence | [C11 digital power](../../country/C11/03-digital-power-and-contributions.md), [C07 separate decisions](../../country/C07/03-health-education-and-care.md) |
| Provisional decisions, future starts and missed milestones | A phased new service cannot suspend an inherited claim or leave “readiness” under indefinite ministerial control | [C06 shortfall](../../country/C06/04-shortfall-and-continuity.md), [C14 opening](../../country/C14/02/01-opening-inventory.md) |

These are known design dependencies, not findings that a particular current
national law is invalid. The [54-selection mapping](inventory.json) ties
every choice to at least one function; it does not settle value disagreements,
unreconciled source clauses or unfamiliar concepts. The bounded next package
starts with the first four rows because their interaction determines who
can act and obtain correction during the same cash/staff shortfall.
