## Selected canonical changes and competing models

[RR01–RR10](../country/C14/08-democratic-repair-without-self-authorization.md)
retains ordinary delay, including recount/rerun/formation-failure repeat polls,
with a closed exception for necessary repair of an already protected democratic
function. Existing remedies come first. No court packing, term extension,
appointment shortcut, changed ratifying electorate, retroactive claim erasure
or procedure approving itself. Prior-law competent review uses existing judges,
thresholds and conflicts; no approval by silence, absence or split. Publish an
early-effect determination before the final90-day consideration period and use
the exact supported commencement/fallback. A called election permits only the
necessary execution of actual binding existing-rights judgments, not a new
amendment shortcut or automatic postponement. The exception's own introduction
obeys the predecessor law; legitimate original founding is a separate route.

Keep1% hearing/5% petition, Assembly one-third initiation/two-thirds full approval,
majority referendum on that route or three-fifths on direct petition, no turnout
quorum, one-year collection and90/180-day safeguards. Initiative opening fixes
the corrected lawful denominator; eligibility at signature/ballot uses old law
then in force. Rounding never invents signatures. This is a research reference;
actual predecessor law may require different treatment before lawful adoption.

Blanket delay remains a simpler rival with real exclusion cost. Delay only when
incumbent benefit is predicted asks courts to guess votes and can obstruct fair
access; the selected test instead examines entitlement/mechanism. Broad immediate
constituent repair can address a captured order but a manipulated referendum
can entrench it. RR cannot repair every captured predecessor court/electorate.

[FA01–FA12](../country/C12/03-finite-attention-and-lawful-closure.md)
selects a30-issue reference for major ordinary national consultation:12 consequence-
led, four opposition, five affected-agenda and nine public-drawn agenda issues.
The latter14 agenda setters are actual humans from safe affected-population
sampling/outreach, not a draw among agent messages or representative national
assembly. Protected/legal claims, mandatory hearings and constitutional votes
keep independent routes/clocks. Publish finite intake/source limits, assistance,
paid capacity, unexamined arguments and coverage failures. Preserving a bounded
issue reference is not a promise to archive every generated byte or inspect it.

Use 30 days intake, ten selection-correction days, report by day 90 and one elected
extension at most 30 days; actual higher-law periods prevail. A missed process
produces acknowledged failure, not law or endless default consultation. A team
appointed through existing merit administration controls lawful operational
process but cannot close/enact policy; the competent issuer owns reasoned closure
and elected bodies reserved policy. Another career supervisor hears reassignment
requests, with direct ordinary courts and original-source access; no new guardian
selects its own reviewer. Vendor exit preserves claims, sources and due pay.

Exhaustive review is a real rival but vulnerable to endless novel generation.
Ordinary elected scrutiny is cheaper and remains available outside mandatory
FA scope, with concentrated agenda cost. Distributed situated forums preserve
context and relationships but cannot duplicate shared resources or sovereignty.
These remain alternative models; FA need not absorb every institutional critique.
