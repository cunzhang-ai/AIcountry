# RDA09–RDA13. Restore a resident's choice when a proposal was misrepresented

13 November 2026 · Integrated design 452 · Candidate ordinary deliberation rules

**RDA09 — Reserve time to correct the presentation.** At commissioning,
set the initial support cutoff and reserve a short, dated correction
window within RD's original outer deadlines. Fund an independent
reviewer, accessible interpretation, protected intake and a usable
floor slot. Keep the exact authored proposal, the presentation each
affected participant was offered and any reported difference. Record
only the evidence needed to establish the discrepancy and support.
Speech, interpretation and other usable media retain equal standing.
No participant has to use the original facilitator or AI provider to
report an error.

**RDA10 — Determine whether the difference could change the choice.**
A separately accountable reviewer checks whether a required public
presentation changed an operative term or omitted a material
condition, or whether a failed channel deprived someone of usable
access. Obtain the authored text or verified original expression
and give affected authors and participants a fair chance to answer.
When the difference cannot affect operative meaning, preserve
verified support and record the correction. A proposer changing
their policy after cutoff uses the ordinary revision rules. The
correction window restores access to a text already eligible for
the original process. It does not accept a newly authored substitute.

**RDA11 — Restore only the affected opportunity.** Reopen the support
choice for participants whose opportunity to choose between the
original eligible texts was materially impaired by the verified
error. Use pre-cutoff evidence of the affected presentation or
failed access, with an outside challenge for an omitted participant.
Give them the verified terms in a usable form and allow an explicit
choice, replacement or abstention by the correction deadline.
Preserve timely support if independent evidence shows the person
knew and supported the actual terms. Otherwise support based on
the materially defective presentation needs fresh confirmation. Silence supplies no support. Retain other valid
choices and the original denominator, one live designation per
seat and the original qualification and tie rules. Keep reasoning
and safe aggregate results without publishing individual choices.

**RDA12 — Keep the exact elected disposition.** Calculate the
verified result after correction. If an unresolved material error
could change which text qualifies or leads, record the precise
failure at the reserved slot and use any lawful correction or
resumption still available within the original calendar. Do not
assign the disputed slot on an assumed result. Dispose of the
unaffected main text on its own terms when lawful. Ordinary
sponsorship and existing remedies remain available. After the
window, later evidence can support ordinary review or a separately
authorized decision. An error report neither extends authority
nor creates an automatic stay or a public vote.

**RDA13 — Verify the correction at the receiving end.** Test whether
participants actually received the corrected terms and had a usable
chance to choose. The Assembly service carries the costs, records
and original claims through replacement of a facilitator or provider.
AI can flag a discrepancy and offer a draft. An independent person
judges materiality and each participant decides what they support.
Reopen on recurring distortion, failure of the alternate route,
unfair exclusion or a simpler process with equivalent influence.
