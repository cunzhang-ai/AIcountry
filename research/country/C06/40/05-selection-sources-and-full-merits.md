# RG06–RG10: fair selection, answerable sources and full merits

*Part of [C06/40](../40-tax-law-and-accountable-revenue.md); design100 research candidate, not enacted law.*

**RG06 — Public priorities, bounded risk and real random selection.** Within elected law/appropriation Cabinet approves prospective general service/compliance priorities and the director publishes a reasoned annual operational plan. It states the lawful in-scope population/frame, relevant material-error/evasion indicators, expected burden, risk/complaint-directed work, a genuinely random bounded component, assignment/conflict rules and audit trail. Publish methods/selection probabilities and lawful scope; protect only details whose early disclosure would demonstrably enable evasion or expose a source, with necessary opposition/audit/court access to the actual method. After a lawful frame is fixed, an auditable draw/seed and source checks prevent removal of allies or insertion of enemies. Related/split entities/source families are attributed, not counted as corroboration. Unregistered, paper/offline or unfamiliar lawful activity must not disappear merely because the platform has no account.

Political opinion, donor identity, journalism, lawful union/association, religion, ethnicity, disability, benefit receipt or refusal of optional AI is no audit/merits ground by itself. Actual relevant economic facts or a grounded protected report can support lawful inquiry; public officials/connected firms receive no exemption, and similar views do not prove common control or evasion. Risk scores lead to accountable inquiry, never determine liability/guilt. Selection officers use prior job-related case rotation/conflict rules; Cabinet/director cannot privately choose an examiner or reviewer for one outcome. General quality/caseload supervision is lawful, but recovered-amount, denial or conviction quotas, percentage bonuses, bounties and private tax farming are forbidden. Contractors may provide bounded support, not choose targets, assess disputed liability or wield collection force. A delayed new annual plan cannot cancel already enacted case/refund duties; continuing work uses its actual standing statutory scope and funds, not a new hidden policy.

**RG07 — Necessary information, not a universal financial dossier.** An examiner may require only identified relevant records/third-party information under an actual enacted tax duty/power, with purpose, period, material connection, usable notice, reasonable assistance, privilege/confidentiality and review. PD governs personal data; KR protects legitimate secrets while B14 supplies answerable necessary protected evidence. No general access to homes, personal messages, devices, bank/resolution files, foreign servers or private accounts follows from being a tax officer; nonconsensual entry/search/seizure/interception needs its specific independent judicial authority and P/LF limits. A tax inspection cannot evade criminal silence/counsel/exclusion through its label. Ordinary statutory compulsory reporting is not fictitious consent, and a data-sharing contract grants no wider power.

Original tax, employer, bank/payment, benefit and independent-source custodians retain their own facts/versions/authority and protected correction history; share only lawfully necessary fields, not one joint tax/benefit/family life log. Store necessary separately controlled lawful evidence/access so a reviewed vendor/director cannot erase the only appeal record. Taxpayers/reviewers/courts can obtain an intelligible answerable factual/legal case and appropriate protected counsel/expert access even where software is proprietary; an inaccessible AI conclusion is not evidence sufficient for an adverse finding. EV methods/W/CPI sources remain contestable evidence, not tax definitions, and source correction reaches competent benefit/payment/assessment owners under their own laws rather than automatically repricing every past obligation.

**RG08 — Assess, explain and permit correction.** A qualified statutory assessor gives lawful source-period/base calculation, credited withholding/payments, necessary evidence, proposed adverse result and the actual TA/TX notice/reply/assessment limitation rules. Preserve B16 ordinarily20working-day adverse reply, but do not invent another tax limitation, rate, default debt or interest schedule here. Assessment is an administrative finding of liability under actual law, not proof of K22 knowledge/intent. Payer nonremittance does not remove a worker's credit for proven withheld tax under the TA10; other payment discharge follows actual authorized irrevocable acceptance/debit, not a mere unexecuted instruction. Revenue records a Treasury receipt only when cash settles; a collectible tax/intermediary receivable is not spendable money. Source, office or model errors can be challenged with provisional useful-time protection. All penalties, anti-dissipation actions, collection restrictions and interest need the separate selected legal predicate; no 'compliance charge' fills a missing sanction.

**RG09 — Optional administrative full-merits review.** A person may file an ordinary assisted objection directly with the separately staffed review function, preserving first usable receipt and safe records. A preassigned qualified reviewer independent of that assessment's investigation/advice/employment chain examines the actual statutory facts, source period, law, amount/credits and procedure, hears both sides and issues a reasoned **binding administrative merits decision**: confirm, reduce/cancel, correct credit, require lawful reassessment or certify an owed refund within enacted scope. They cannot create a new tax/base, settle for patronage, invent exemptions or override a final court ruling. A proposed increase beyond the notified assessment needs the actual adverse-notice/limitation/reply route, not a surprise appeal penalty; the precise liability/remedies code controls it. Director/minister cannot veto a favorable result. Neither acceptance of a refund nor use of assisted review waives other valid claims. Reviewer is administrative, not the final independent court or a sovereign policy referee.

For a disputed administrative merits matter use TX03/TX07's decision within 30 working days of protected first receipt, with at most one timely notified and reasoned extension of up to 30 working days, without serial irrelevant completeness demands or loss of earlier urgent rights; the initial statutorily proved undisputed refund instead uses RG12's10working-day first-sufficient-receipt payment route. Source/identity uncertainty preserves undisputed parts and direct court access. These are proposed outer duties, not evidence of actual paid capacity. Internal review is optional; no exhaustion, pay-first condition, account credential or compulsory settlement bars direct ordinary courts. Timely disputed-principal collection stays, permitted preservation, refund deadlines and interest are exactly the separately selected remedies rules, not staff discretion or a duplicate clock from this charter.

**RG10 — Full ordinary courts and own-office misconduct.** Affected taxpayers may go directly to a competent unconflicted ordinary court for **statutory merits review of facts/law/amount**, not only procedural error, plus source disclosure, useful-time relief, assessment/collection correction and due refund execution. Use the TX07’s60working-day usable-notice/blocked-access filing protection and ordinary distinct appeal; B17 urgent consideration≤24actual hours/earlier useful time remains. No examiner, director, reviewer, minister or tax software chooses its judge. A court may correct actual liability/due credit or order lawful reassessment/refund; it cannot write the missing tax policy or fabricate cash/foreign jurisdiction. CJ01 retains only a necessary constitutional component; CJ12 own-court exception, EA or bank-resolution panels supply no tax tribunal.

Revenue/review service's own corruption, procurement, records or personal leadership wrongdoing has direct external court/PD and existing independent audit/integrity/prosecution routes, with protected original access without a head's permission. Internal merits officers do not finally judge their own employer's misconduct claim or criminal case. K22 uses its actual specific disclosure duty, knowledge/intent, capacity and P proof/defense; honest classification disputes, inaccessible filing, poverty or a failed business do not become evasion by an assessment. K07/K08/K09/K12/K24 retain their own distinct elements, no generic official-misconduct catchall. Unavailable competent courts/sources/receiving remains disclosed unmet protection, not institutionally guaranteed independence.
