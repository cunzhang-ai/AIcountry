# Night exposure across two neighborhoods

This constructed comparison exercises
[NSE01–NSE06](../../country/C09/28-sleep-and-cumulative-noise.md).
Two neighborhoods receive road and rail noise in the same night period.
For the example only, use a combined limit of 50 dB and no more than
four distinct loud events in the stipulated observation night. The
events are already verified against a common illustrative event threshold.
These are test inputs. A real rule needs qualified evidence, lawful
metrics and affected participation.

| Option | First neighborhood road and rail | First total and events | Second neighborhood road and rail | Second total and events |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Existing operation | 48 and 48 dB | 51.01 dB and 9 events | 47 and 44 dB | 48.76 dB and 3 events |
| Move road freight to the second neighborhood | 43 and 48 dB | 49.19 dB and 7 events | 50 and 44 dB | 50.97 dB and 9 events |
| Retime road freight to the day without moving it | 43 and 48 dB | 49.19 dB and 7 events | 47 and 44 dB | 48.76 dB and 3 events |
| Quieter operation at source and a targeted schedule | 44 and 47 dB | 48.76 dB and 3 events | 46 and 44 dB | 48.12 dB and 3 events |

The two source levels in each place are like-period equivalent levels
under matching metrics and measurement conditions. Add their acoustic
energy, not their decibel values. Event counts represent distinct
observed events, including any coincidence, rather than the sum of
unmatched event logs. Adding an illustrative 0.5 dB uncertainty allowance
to the last option still keeps both places below 50. Real decisions
need measured uncertainty and the applicable evidentiary rule.

Retiming alone brings the first average below the test limit and leaves
seven events. Diversion creates a second exceedance while leaving a
breach at the first location. Insulating homes cannot erase either
outdoor breach. For one household in the example, keeping windows
closed without safe ventilation or cooling prevents counting the
offered insulation as usable indoor protection.

The last option needs six monetary units for actual source work and
two for independent measurement and verification. Only six units are
initially available. Record the verification gap and seek an elected
two-unit supplement. Necessary interim measures retain their own law.
The example assumes the final option has separately verified staff and
allows a scheduled essential night journey without a general exemption.
A genuine rescue event would be recorded and governed by its own law.
The [arithmetic check](verify-night-exposure.py) tests both places,
separate event counts and full funding. Real effects on sleep,
nighttime work, access and operating costs remain open.
