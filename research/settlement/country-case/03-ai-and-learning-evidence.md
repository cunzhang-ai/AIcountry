# Original-study reports: useful AI and independent human capability

*Design 85; two study families, not a common national productivity coefficient.*

[Brynjolfsson–Li–Raymond, QJE 2025](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjae044)
reports a staggered customer-support rollout analyzed with difference-in-differences:
5,172 workers and about 15% more issues resolved/hour on average, with substantial
skill heterogeneity. The agent inspected setting, strategy and results; root DOI
fetch failed. The older NBER working version has different sample/effect figures;
it is not an independent replication and its 14% is not the published 15% figure.
Single-firm medium-run productivity does not identify national wages/labor demand,
healthcare/justice competence or a fifty-percent macro supply gain. Confidential
raw data and replication were not inspected.

[Bastani et al., author-hosted manuscript](https://hamsabastani.github.io/education_llm.pdf)
for the [PNAS 2025 paper](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122) was read for main
methods/results: randomized access variants in one Turkish high-school math
setting. Easier assisted practice can accompany worse subsequent unaided exam
performance; guarded tutor design mitigates the short-run loss without proving
long-term learning gain. This is not measured adult professional deskilling or
a universal AI harm. Publisher/PMC routes failed; author copy's whole68-page
appendix, raw data and replication were not examined. Published affiliation
correction was located; no effect reanalysis is claimed.

The correction is a proportionate design safeguard, openly extending beyond
the study population: when the promised benefit needs learning or independent
review, fund and assess retained/transfer/fallback competence alongside assisted
output. Safe independent assessment preserves accommodations and essential
assistive tools; no dangerous withdrawal in a live service is authorized.
Protect teacher/worker/learner voice, lawful research consent and safe receiving.
This is a falsifiable deployment condition, not a new national AI authority,
mandatory prohibition, fixed test frequency or claimed optimal pedagogy.
