# BLC06–BLC10. Hand back each continuing service to a funded successor

*Design464 · Part of [budget lapse and paid continuity](../66-budget-lapse-and-paid-continuity.md).*

**BLC06 — Specify what ends each temporary line.** When an elected
limited continuation provides for earlier successor cutover, the
enacted text names the relevant duty, legal effect, funding and
qualified receiving conditions and a fixed outer end. Check actual
local decisions where required. Use an already lawful fixed expiry
on its own terms. A budget vote does not itself supply settled cash,
an available provider or a replacement appropriation. A new condition
cannot extend the ordinary bridge or revive expired authority.

**BLC07 — Reconcile work and payment on each original calendar.**
For a material rollover, separate annual obligations earned before
year end, lawfully incurred and earned bridge obligations, already
binding multiyear work and prospective new work. Record authority,
source, actual earned or delivered date, due date and receipt for
each line. Split a bundled invoice by lawful purpose and verify
it with the original receiving evidence. Use any valid accrued-only
closeout solely for its designated period and scope. Expiry of unused
payment permission leaves the underlying debt and review intact.

**BLC08 — Hand back only the qualified scope.** The competent sector
owner and payer verify independent accessible funds, accepted staff,
supplier and safe receiving, with applicable local consent and source.
An independent reviewer checks evidence where the source law requires
it. End each conditional line at its lawful boundary and reconcile
overlap so the same act or expense is covered once. Where a line
cannot hand back before its fixed outer end, use separately authorized
protection if available and bring forward an exact elected correction.
Keep original obligations and actual missed dates visible. For an
applicable material cross-sector shortage, follow E19's earlier
elected response clock as well as the BLC calendar.

**BLC09 — Keep independent execution through the same rollover.**
Include elections, opposition, legal aid, court remedies and their
actual execution beside essential services. Neither ordinary annual
supply nor a change of Cabinet diverts a distinct protected source
or creates confidence. Record the earliest service or appeal at risk
and whether the missing step is authority, cash, professional or
supplier acceptance, independent execution or actual receipt. Use
existing timely review and accountable alternatives. No official
acquires power to renew the continuation by reporting a shortage.

**BLC10 — Reconcile the later budget and preserve losses already borne.**
The succeeding payers show the remaining and committed balances,
source restrictions and original accrued claims. A late receipt
can pay a valid debt once. Record any late service, lost review
opportunity and outstanding remedy separately. AI may compare dates
and original records. It cannot approve spending, extend authority
or certify that an unpaid or unserved obligation was delivered.
Reopen on repeated late handbacks, coercive supplier dependence,
loss of independent execution or a simpler funded successor.
