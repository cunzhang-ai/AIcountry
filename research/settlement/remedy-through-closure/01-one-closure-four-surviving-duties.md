# Keep redress payable when the original provider closes

*Design488 · Constructed cost and receiving comparison.*

The public service provider from the [proactive redress
comparison](../proactive-redress-and-safe-receipt/01-repair-without-a-new-application.md)
ceases operation at hour 0. Its legal closure date is already fixed.
The applicable law assigns surviving public duties to a named authority.
It does not make a new contractor liable for the provider's private debts.
Ordinary creditor, wage and insolvency routes continue on their own terms.
The source and receiving assumptions below are authored.

| Item at closure | What the successor must actually do |
|---|---|
| A, essential home visit by hour 6 | An outside team confirms the worker, safe handover, necessary information and acceptance. The paid visit costs 1. A travel voucher or a worker still committed elsewhere supplies no visit. |
| B, unpaid redress of 2 due by hour 12 | The original payer's instruction was returned without settling. B has moved and ended their old agent's authority. The public payer verifies the return, exclusive release authority and a safe new receiving route. The move changes the destination of the payment, not the debtor, claim or date. |
| C, redress of 2 with uncertain settlement | The last instruction from the old provider lacks independently verified finality. Keep 2 reserved until reconciliation. Use a separate, authorized 1-unit emergency provision if necessary before hour 12. The old principal is not replayed. |
| D, pending appeal lodged before closure | Carry prior award, filing date and the scope of the appeal. At hour 18 an independent hearing needs one paid support block, protected access to originals and a competent executor for any order. Closure is not a decision against D. |

There are four separately sourced cash purposes: A's 1 for service,
B's 2 for payable redress, C's restricted 2 for contingent release,
and 1 for urgent provisional provision. Independent review funding
is separate. A transition agreement priced at 2 covers paid handoff,
specialist team availability, payer setup and records support. The
respective workers are assigned at their own deadlines. The 2 is additional to the
service, benefit and review obligations. All three alternatives below
inherit the same applicable legal duties and deadlines.

| Arrangement | What reaches people before the deadlines |
|---|---|
| Successor with a bulk export at the legal date | It holds legal responsibility. Two successor portals use the same expired custodian credential. The export does not show B's revocation or independently prove settlement. A team sharing the old unavailable roster cannot perform A's visit. Preserve affected claims and review, and record nonreceipt. |
| Stand-ready handoff under the named public owner | The paid team accepts the visit, payer verifies B and makes one transfer after the return, and independent staff accept D's originals. Execute the separate 1 for C when authorized and usable without determining the principal. The handoff changes no underlying award. |
| Continue via a narrowly authorized public fallback | An eligible public team can handle B and D using independent access and assigned paid slots. It cannot perform A's specialist visit unless it has the necessary qualification and a free shift. Prefer this route where it supplies the same outcome with lower funded overlap or a more independent controller. |

If the 2-unit transition plan has only 1 usable by closure, the full
handoff does not qualify. Preserve independently executable tasks and
use the lawful fallback. Disclose each unmet deadline and seek the
competent source or enforcement decision. If the closure were instead
discretionary and conditioned on receipt, the empowered authority would
hold only the closure or asset release governed by that condition.
An enacted date cannot be moved by a readiness report. The organization
that retains legal responsibility remains answerable when nobody can
take the first shift.

## The next window and independent control

When C's old instruction is checked, distinguish lawful discharge
from actual access. If the money reached the lawful recipient and is
usable, reconcile it once and account for the separate urgent provision
under its own law. If it is final but inaccessible, use the ordinary
access remedy. If nonpayment is established, pay the 2 once after
verifying the lawful receiving route. Keep any distinct loss claim.
If the old instruction used an agent whose authority was already
revoked, verify the original notice and release times independently.
A settled transfer to a disputed receiver requires its own liability
decision. It cannot be counted automatically as lawful discharge or
replayed automatically. Keep urgent protection available separately.
Unknown finality continues as an open case with a named owner and
next check. No silence, missing archive or institutional move
authorizes another principal payment.

Before ending paid overlap, repeat the exercise after withdrawing the
old vendor's cooperation. The independent record holder and reviewer
must still read the minimum necessary originals. A change of receiver,
scope or purpose needs the applicable authority and proof. A model
can help find unresolved cases and compare dates. It cannot certify
acceptance, revive old mandates, transfer a claim or approve payment.
An unused amount held for C remains restricted until the legal
exposure and release conditions are reconciled. Review a second due
visit, an appeal missed by the export, and unpaid staff time before
reporting closure savings.
