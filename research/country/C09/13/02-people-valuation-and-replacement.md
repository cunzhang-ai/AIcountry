# LA06–LA10. Compensation belongs to people with different claims

*Design145 · [Public acquisition](../13-public-acquisition-and-secure-relocation.md) · No actual title or asset price established.*

**LA06 — Find affected rights without inventing owners.** Identify actual owners,
possessors, tenants/subtenants, secured creditors, workers, informal users,
collective holders and affected service/ecological dependencies. Publish a safe
claims route; registry entries and AI matches are evidence, not conclusive title.
Use AP alternative proof/help and protected objections; no title paper or nationality
is needed to seek urgent housing or prove a genuine non-title loss. A census/date
records actual pre-project rights/use, with correction for excluded/absent people,
birth, succession, lawful transactions and forced absence. It cannot erase a
preexisting right or ordinary later essential need. Prove opportunistic fabricated
loss individually; no collective penalty or family-head-only payment.

**LA07 — Select replacement, not only depreciated book value.** Independently qualified
valuation establishes the actual existing lawful right as of intended possession,
excluding value created solely by the announced scheme and its own depressed
blight. Include equivalent safe asset replacement, necessary transaction/moving/
reinstallation costs, actual severance and directly caused transition loss.
An existing compensation quote must be updated before possession if stale; missing
market data calls for inspectable replacement/input evidence, not zero. No payment
for an unlawful use or speculative ungranted permission. Do not capitalize the
same lease, business income, improvement or land interest twice across holders.

The claimant gets the valuation/method and funded proportionate independent advice,
not the acquiring office as sole valuer/reviewer. Protect adverse valuation
findings and client-directed independent advice from the proponent's funding
pressure; ordinary B15 proof/merits apply, no conclusive official estimate. Land-based livelihoods need a
real equivalent productive-land option where actually feasible, with qualified
receiving/access/support. Cash is not equivalent to unavailable land. Applicable
collective/Indigenous consent, sacred-site and other nonreplaceable protections
cannot be converted to compulsory monetary acceptance. LA does not decide an
actual historical ownership dispute or buy the right to disregard its outcome.

**LA08 — Housing and livelihood are separate duties.** Offer a lawful replacement
property/tenure route or an actually informed cash choice. Before removal, provide
safe accepting housing (or the person's genuinely available informed own
alternative), usable work/school/care journeys, disability/language
support and lawful tenure no weaker than the actual protected position. Paying
an owner's land value does not pay a tenant's move, a worker's lost wages or a
household's actual new care/journey cost. Informal occupiers without recognized
land title do not receive invented ownership, but retain property/livelihood loss,
adequate safe rehousing and essential-access duties. No displacement into an
unsafe camp, abusive household or a promised later vacancy.

Compensate evidenced transition net-business loss, actual moving/re-establishment
and necessary wage/learning protection under their own source; no guaranteed
turnover/profit forever. Paid support must allow restoration, with actual review
when it fails rather than automatic closure at the offered course's end. Affected
people retain choices and cannot be directed to a job, family or community. Host
communities keep funded services and rights; their lost capacity is not spare.

**LA09 — Taxes and old debt are not free resources.** TA/PA/NF apply to actual sale,
exchange, income-replacement and title on their own scope. No special tax-free
rollover, home exclusion, duplicated basis or gift label arises. The project
replacement package includes **actually unavoidable incremental tax/transaction
cost caused by its compelled transaction**, calculated through the ordinary schedule
with review, so a gross headline does not conceal an unaffordable replacement.
Only the attributable additional tax, including tax on a taxable gross-up, is
covered; not unrelated income tax, old tax principal or every future voluntary tax.
Publish gross payment, actual credits/remittance and net received/source once.
Internal public tax recirculation is not new national real capacity or FX.

Secured debt, NF21 old deferred-tax principal, leases and creditor priorities
retain actual law. Pay the same underlying property interest once, allocate to
actual rights-holders and state actual net equity. No forced creditor novation,
debt forgiveness or public loan guarantee. A replacement plan cannot presume a
new affordable lender, force worse debt merely to comply or treat compensation
paid to a creditor as the displaced person's usable cash. Budget and lawfully
source any necessary financing/tenure bridge before possession; absent consent/
capacity can require redesign/no taking. Existing debt is not erased by the plan.

**LA10 — Compensate real timing loss, not a second asset.** A taking/temporary use
ledger separates title value, lost use, actual damage, relocation, lost net income,
source tax and interim support. If an asset returns repaired/intact, do not also
pay its full freehold value. Compensation for the same loss received elsewhere
is credited once, without treating an unreceived/refused service as money.
Pay undisputed amounts promptly; no rights waiver is a condition of receiving them.
Actual final compensation shortfall remaining after sums actually paid or made
unconditionally available to the lawful recipient carries CPI protection from
its due date (no later than actual possession) with a nominal floor plus2% annual simple interest
on original unpaid principal, actual elapsed days/365.2425, no compounding. Do not
add the same inflation/delay loss again; independently proved additional physical/
consequential loss keeps its separate source. This rate is a normative choice,
not a market return or proof of adequate delay compensation in every context.
