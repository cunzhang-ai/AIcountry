# Design196 decision. Transfer practical government at the lawful boundary

*9 November 2026 · Research selection · Exact design195 predecessor.*

## Public problem and selection

Design195 made civil proof and correction operational duties. This
edition addresses a different cross-country condition. Existing C03
ends or changes office by law. DP, OP/T and sector law require records,
pay and real authority to pass. Generic instructions to transfer keys
do not distinguish old authority, stale credentials, actual receiving
or the incumbent's ability to veto a handover. Select prior preparation,
a verified operative source, separate revocation and issue, institutional
recovery and actual service and control checks. The recorded investiture
still confers office under actual prior law. [Canonical choice](../country/C03/19-lawful-handover-and-actual-control.md)
· [Exact predecessor](baseline.json)

The Assembly, courts or other actual authority authenticate their own
legal acts. Cabinet and ministers do not control court, election, audit
or local administration. Career custodians and independent alternates
implement scoped access and have separately accountable review. The
paired recovery arrangement is a material choice. It prevents unilateral
recovery within its actual scope and can add delay or fail through
collusion. Set an initial 24-hour report and a separate maximum
24-hour temporary recovery grant, with fresh bounded court authority
for any exceptional continuation. These are hypotheses and preserve
earlier actual due times. The political act has no technical timer.

## Alternatives, objections and observed support

Ordinary departmental transfer preserves existing skills and may cost
less. A common override can be easier to run but makes cross-institution
control and recovery depend on one center. Separate, enforceable
recovery supports independent offices at the cost of staffed alternate
paths, careful evidence and testing. Keep these real rivals and reopen
where actual authority, technology or material costs favor a better one.

A blocked actor might manufacture a competing source, or a new
government might use recovery to capture the courts. The authorization
is limited to a particular act, tied to original law and subject to
outside review. Refuse unsupported political access while keeping
lawful standing duties available. A real command, cash, court or staff
failure must be reported even if the new holder is legally recognized.
A budgeted reserve can fail if a shared contractor or administrator
controls both paths. Protect public scrutiny and affected persons
without exposing keys or confidential evidence.

## Continuation and reopening

Retain C03/RR office effects and stays, existing constitutional and
contribution holds, DP sector authority, SF payment scope and actual
B19/E15 execution limits, DF civilian command and PO enforcement.
Adoption must name lawful scope and paid OP/T staff, assets, records,
contracts, notices, pending acts, old claims and successors. A test of
the new path cannot change legal effect or cancel an existing duty.
Counsel, physical enforcement, cash, communication, review and
technical skill need actual separately funded delivery.

Evidence and desk comparisons support the research choice. No affected
public or qualified independent reviewer participated. No actual
authorization, safe paid operation or better lives are verified.
Reopen on an actual failure, captured recovery, unresolved legal conflict,
unpayable preparation or a better complete arrangement. In a whole
country comparison, jointly test a lawful succession and the next due
payment or service with actual source, original records, independent
control and a person receiving help. Retain meaningful objections
without transferring a prior approval.
