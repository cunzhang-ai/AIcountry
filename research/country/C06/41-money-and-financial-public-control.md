# C06. Money and financial power under effective public control

*Integrated design104 · Proposed MC01–MC32 · English research, not enacted law.*

Choose a five-person monetary committee, separate prudential/conduct chiefs,
a finite three-source stability council and protected career execution. Public
law sets the general objective and outer risk; no expert vetoes an elected
budget or obtains a secret bailout mandate. This completes the missing positive
leadership/operating chain, while preserving meaningful rivals and failure.

Primary law expressly distinguishes authority-own rate/transaction operations
from general banking rules: the former use actual monetary votes/charter; the
latter retain DR consultation/affirmative scrutiny. M13's older14-day urgent
route is explicitly displaced prospectively by the source/seven-day cap.
A continuing finite charter with annual audit/hearings avoids an annual payment
cliff, but can preserve stale/captured limits. Domestic issuance creates no
Treasury revenue, privately owned FX, actual imports or funded welfare by itself.
