# C02. Identity and supported agency

*Part of [C02](../C02-membership-and-rights.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

Legal identity belongs to the person, not a household representative or digital
platform. Birth and later status changes need accessible registration, evidence,
privacy, correction and independent review. Missing or disputed papers must not
bar urgent safety, health, basic education or subsistence; a provisional access
route preserves the claim while nationality, parentage and electoral eligibility
are decided under their distinct laws. Children retain their own identity and
voice even when adults disagree. Chosen supporters may assist an adult with
specified decisions, but support does not silently transfer the person's legal
capacity, property or vote. The [personal-status decision](../../2026-10-personal-status-and-care/decision-record.md)
records the competing family and state-power risks and the unresolved lawful
adoption questions.
The [advance-choice and death-record route](../C08/08-end-of-life-decisions-and-survivors.md)
uses the same supported-agency and correction principles when a person
cannot communicate easily or a survivor's status is disputed.
A [crisis support plan](../C07/11-mental-health-substance-use-and-crisis-care.md)
may record preferences and chosen helpers, but cannot itself transfer
legal capacity or authorize detention.

[C08 B21–B40](../C08/16-family-agency-property-and-estates.md) now supplies
candidate ages, bounded support, plural scoped family ties and child/estate
processes. Registration does not make a household or supporter the person;
existing identity, supported will and political rules retain their scope.

[MB](06-citizenship-and-secure-belonging.md) now distinguishes automatic/genuine
official nationality from proof, residence and electoral recording, with safe
shared inquiry and direct judicial determination. Missing/compromised credentials
do not cancel an established person or transfer another person's identity.

[VR01–VR12 civil registration](../C08/29-civil-registration-and-identity.md)
now specifies the registrar, accessible civil event recording, proof and
actual repair. A mistake, lost certificate or disputed link does not cancel
personhood, legal capacity or access under C02. Status decisions remain
separate.
