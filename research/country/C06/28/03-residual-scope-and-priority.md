# A selected residual rule for genuinely available public resources

*CP05–CP06; design162 revision, part of [national commitments](../28-national-commitments-and-residual-scarcity.md).*

**CP05 — Use only the enacted available pool.** When a sudden serious resource
conflict lacks a timely applicable sector allocation, the existing legally assigned
local civilian lead may temporarily direct actually controlled uncommitted
public flexible stocks and aid voluntarily accepted with lawful source/release
permission. [CP13–CP16](07-committed-logistics-and-continuing-claims.md) now adds
only the expressly enacted, condition-limited public-logistics category; it does
not make every committed asset flexible. A pre-enacted preparedness reserve is available within its own release
purpose. Record actual quantity, location, time/shift, condition, controller,
release authority, donor locality's displaced duty and responsible receiver.

A department's nominal earmark cannot immunize spare stock; a real due commitment
cannot be reclassified as flexible. A CP13 committed-logistics order must name
its displaced due duty and continuing claims. Exclude resources actually required for already
due life-sustaining care, bodily-safety-critical dependent tasks, ecological safety and the
functioning of independent election/remedy records and access, unless a verified
lawful safe substitute is actually accepted first. Restricted pension/client money
and the SF standby retain their own purposes; this rule cannot sweep them.

No forced private property transfer, worker/relative conscription, compulsory
care, new debt/tax, privacy search, emergency franchise change or military command.
Existing separate lawful powers keep their grounds; the residual rule does not
expand them. No resource becomes available because an AI or office promises it.
If no pool is usable, provide available existing lawful protection and seek an
elected choice; record the unmet claim rather than invent residual authority.

**CP06 — Prioritize the declared pool, not every public value.** Within this
pool, first prevent imminent irreversible serious bodily harm on actual credible
facts and feasible protective effect; next prevent imminent failure of an essential
dependent service that would cause such harm. Use the minimum sufficient resource,
less irreversible displacement and safe alternatives. Give usable reasons and
consider people least able to avoid the harm; wealth, political loyalty, account,
sponsor, model rank and first arrival are not priority grounds.

Apply actual clinical triage to clinical cases under its separate lawful standards.
Only materially comparable urgency, expected protective effect and displacement
use transparent random selection, or safe rotation where interruption cannot harm
an indivisible/continuous task. Preserve an independently checkable protected draw
record. Do not lottery unlike constitutional, ecological and bodily claims into
one metric. A waiting person's claim and unmet harm remain visible.

A single protected power source already needed by clinic and election functions,
with no safe alternative, is not in this residual pool. The lead must preserve
what can safely continue and urgently seek the competent elected decision; this
clause supplies no concealed tie-breaking general diversion power. The162
logistics slice does not resolve that protected critical-resource collision.
[PS21–PS32](../37/06-critical-public-power-and-actual-priority.md) now proposes
separate standing ordinary law for expressly scheduled public backup power,
including its bodily-priority/democratic-loss choice. Only that independently
adopted scope supplies such an allocation; CP exclusions/pool/clocks remain.
The original uncommitted-only rationale remains a real rival; broader diversion
and actual paid redundancy retain distinct coercion/source/distribution costs.
Unlawful prior exclusion and actual downstream harm remain challengeable.

[RC03/RC04](../../C10/13/01-public-employer-and-bounded-leadership.md) now
concretizes CP's local civilian lead and national coordinator through qualified
public employment/assignment. This changes no CP05/06 pool, exclusion, priority,
protected critical-resource collision or actual source-law requirement.
