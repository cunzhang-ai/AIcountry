# C03. Peaceful assembly and practical public dissent

5 November 2026 · Design 189 · Proposed AX01–AX20, not enacted law

Democracy includes the practical ability to appear together, contest power and
reach an audience between elections. C02 already protects expression/association;
LF07 already restricts assembly force and demands a distinct enacted dispersal
ground. RX03, B17, PM21 and PD27 already constrain restrictions and suppression.
This statute supplies that missing ordinary assembly/public-space interface,
not a claim that peaceful dissent previously had no protection.

Select assembly as a right **without prior permission or compulsory notice**.
Voluntary facilitation, spontaneous/leaderless action, actual shared public use
and useful-time courts coexist. Planned material nonconsensual assembly-specific
restrictions require an ordinary court; exceptional actual on-scene harm has
strictly bounded civilian authority. A restriction is no weapon, custody or
speech-licensing permission. Protected dissent does not excuse actual injury.

The choice is stronger than merely permitting a notification regime. It is a
normative hypothesis about practical opposition and risks, not a proven optimum
or an inference that more judicial steps automatically improve democracy.
Prior permission, compulsory notice with real exceptions, and administrative
restrictions with genuinely prompt review are meaningful rivals. Compare
missed protection, excluded people, command/court capture and actual paid capacity.
