# Design379. Protect democratic functions against cumulative impairment

[RR11–RR17](../../country/C14/08/04-cumulative-impairment-of-democratic-control.md)
requires joined effects evidence, bounded challenge and actual independent
execution for material threats to protected democratic functions. A forum
cannot gain jurisdiction or set the rules for review of its own changes.

[Primary records and the worked comparison](../../settlement/cumulative-impairment-of-democratic-control/01-three-changes-before-a-contest.md)
distinguish case access, decision and receipt. Actual adoption, independent
capacity, affected participation and public outcomes remain open.
