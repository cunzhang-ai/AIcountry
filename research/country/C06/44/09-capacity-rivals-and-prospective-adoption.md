# NB33–NB36: count paid specialists, retain genuine rivals and preserve transition

*Part of [C06/44](../44-nonbank-finance-and-client-protection.md); design113 candidate.*

**NB33 — Pay the full chain once.** Extend the37-function account's R04/D07/D05/
D11/K01 obligations: professional supervisors/alternates, custodian evidence,
actuary/valuation/market/credit expertise, protected customer assistance/time,
merit hiring, courts/aid, administrators, ordinary external execution and actual
accepted alternate settlement. MC27 separate elected operating lines and enacted
objective fee base/caps apply; no fines, industry gifts, free AI, forecast recovery
or client/S/pension/FX raid pays regulation. Fees can exclude small providers;
compare shared professional services without pretending one person can be both
primary valuation source, inspector, independent reviewer and duty judge.

Operator capital, fund market assets, insurance backing, pension property, CCP
margin and public operating cash are separate sources. A paid reserve for one
purpose is not simultaneously spare fiscal resources. The authored probes test
available custody fractions/recovery, fund redemptions, capital/DB/CCP shortfalls,
loss classification and simultaneous professional work. No actual licensed
providers, tail distribution, funded roster, return channel or national source
book exists. More records or an AI label does not supply missing cash/authority.

**NB34 — Compare institutional alternatives, not just thresholds.** Select plural
licensed intermediation with segregated property, bounded risk, no automatic
investment/insurer guarantee and separate public essential protection. A universal
public safe-payment/public-pension model with fewer private promises can reduce
runs/capture but concentrates provision and may constrain choice/risk capital.
Broad disclosure/private contracting with later civil redress lowers entry cost
but moves tail/record/power burdens to customers. A levy-funded industry guarantee
could socialize custody/insurer losses and improve continuity but needs explicit
coverage/exclusions, headroom, premiums and public backstop incidence; it is not
selected implicitly. Cooperative/relationship finance may improve voice while
sharing correlated local losses. Actual outcomes can displace NB rather than
merely add another guardian. Unknown evidence is not a value compromise.

Nonbank/Insurance/Pension/Securities/FMI Acts expressly add NB/MC case scope,
VD13, court property/priority/administrator jurisdiction and B19 State execution;
commencement is180calendar days after enactment. No readiness label extends an
elected term or suspends a due public/private right. Before commencement publish
actual institutions/contracts, member/beneficiary units, pool title/security,
funding/fees/guarantees, pending claims, professional rosters and paid successors.
New offers/pledges/funding use NB after that date; existing lawful contracts,
accruedDB/Y/S, foreign/security/insurance rights and old periods remain unless
competent prospective law specifically changes them with remedies. No retroactive
new penalty or silent subordination of an old creditor.

A deficient legacy institution needs a case-specific paid compliant migration,
no new harmful risk and court-supervised old obligations, not automatic license,
forced distressed sale, rescission or indefinite unexamined exemption. National
DR and actual constitution/software/contribution permissions remain. Required
line/product valuations/scenarios are explicit bounded lawmaking tasks, not
self-approved private models; their absence means an unfinished technical table,
not adoption, preparedness or social superiority.
