Inspected on 9 November 2026. These sources have separate institutional origins.
The departmental letter and later review concern the same episode. Repeated

## England's 2024–25 schools national funding formula

The December 2023 review by Peter Wyman found a mismatch between forecast pupil
records for the former Cumbria authority and affordability code expecting its two
successors. The mismatch excluded the pupils of Cumberland and Westmorland &
Furness from the affordability calculation. With corrected pupil numbers, the
original indicative rates would have exceeded the core schools budget settlement
by £370 million, or 0.62%. The review describes the scope and checks that failed.
It builds on departmental work and tests it with officials and expert assistance.
[Original report, pp. 3–5](original-sources/england-school-funding-independent-review.pdf)
· [Publisher copy](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/65819c6f23b70a000d234c08/Independent_review_to_assess_the_error_made_in_the_production_of_the_schools_block_NFF.pdf)

The 6 October 2023 Permanent Secretary's letter announced corrected indicative
allocations with a continuing planned core budget of £59.6 billion and stated
schools had yet to receive their 2024–25 funding. The case shows exposure at
the planning stage, without measuring actual reliance losses, costs incurred or
the effects of a targeted bridge. It concerns England's programme and period.
[Original letter](original-sources/england-school-funding-correction-letter.pdf)
· [Department of Education publication](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/dfe-permanent-secretary-nff-letter-to-the-education-select-committee)

## ONS consumer price statistics

The 23 December 2025 policy separates revisions and error corrections. For
errors in headline CPIH, CPI and RPI annual growth rates it describes when a
prominent correction notice is issued, including a discrepancy exceeding 0.1
percentage points. It leaves the original headline data unamended and uses
corrected information in the next index. It links prospective correction to
indexation stability. The notice threshold is a publication practice for that
series. The design uses effect on a particular lawful decision to assess
materiality, and does not infer a UK legal rule for every contract or benefit.
[Original ONS policy, sections 3–4](original-sources/ons-consumer-price-revisions-and-errors-2025.pdf)
· [Publisher copy](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/inflationandpriceindices/articles/revisionsandcorrectionoferrorspoliciesforconsumerpriceinflationstatistics/december2025)

The [worked cases](../settlement/evidence-corrections/01-allocations-and-indexation.md)
are authored comparisons, not measured losses, actual fiscal powers or an
estimate of superiority. No confidential records, affected-party participation,
