# A national economy cannot be financed by residual labels

14 October 2026 · Integrated design 59 · Qualified authored comparison

## Decision, baseline and procedure

Retain plural production and person-level protection; require a joined
production/distribution/finance/ownership/external/physical account for a
proposed operating package. Hold all six reference cases from readiness.
The prior fiscal gap cannot be closed by GDP residuals, pre-growth revenue,
uncommitted savings, free public equity or a green Treasury balance that
shifts debt and non-delivery onto people. This serves the user's full
future-country goal and explicit rejection of today's harmful practices.

The [design-58 predecessor](predecessor-design-58/README.md) preserves 236
artifacts. Current C01–C14 choices, relevant C05/C06/C09 and income/continuity
dependencies were inspected. The existing framework and decision template
remain the proposed research method; no procedure adopts itself here.
One AI assistant authored and checked the work. No affected population,
independent economist, practitioner, creditor or adopting authority participated.

## Competing mechanisms and research selection

Compare the existing income/tax candidate, a stronger profit tax, a paid
public portfolio, an integrated refundable work-charge/needs-credit rival,
and two larger-economy retention/tax sensitivities. These are whole declared
numerical changes, not interchangeable favorable lines. The portfolio case
prices a prior stock transfer and carry; the credit case changes distribution
and cannot be introduced through administrative netting. Larger GDP/wages
with unchanged basket prices is a hypothesis, not an observed productive gain.

Select the joined account and transition condition in canonical C06/20.
The integrated credit is investigated as a substantive rival, not selected
law or a superior social model. Its 25% and 45% earned increments, and
the 75% corporate sensitivity, are uncalibrated value/behavior hypotheses.
Current I05 and actual software/source-law boundaries remain unchanged.
Public ownership may serve reliable access, bargaining and civic control
even where its dividend cannot finance all protection. Cash/private-supply,
safely pooled and other democratic ownership alternatives remain legitimate.

## What the new account actually shows

The baseline records GDP 3,000 through both expenditure and income. Wages
2,280, product tax 265, other production taxes 155 and gross surplus 300
sum to it. Public cash services 774 plus noncash depreciation 24 make
nonmarket consumption 798. Enterprise taxable profit after private capital
consumption is 180, not the 720 residual after subtracting wages alone.
Compensatory relief 40 is paid and credited against need once. Earned
insurance 45 is separately paid, with restricted contributions and future
liabilities retained rather than converted into general spending.

The [full planned flow ledger](results.json) matches household, enterprise,
government, insurance and foreign counterparties. Baseline sector balances
sum to zero although government still lacks 721.85 after assumed financing.
Household planned saving includes unpaid public obligations and cannot be
called actual lending cash. The design-58 764.7 gap remains its own immutable
partial exercise; changed tax bases, pension/relief and returns are not
observed improvement or a retroactive correction of that result.

A 50% profit tax reduces the gap but does not fund the reference. Raising
public portfolio share to 60% requires a prior proposed debt/equity bridge
of 672; lender agreement is unknown. Operating carry 33.6 exceeds the
added net budget return and increases the operating gap. Scope-sensitive
public consolidation cancels matching internal taxes/dividends/purchases;
ownership percentage alone does not establish real control or a second
public resource. Producers still need private investment finance.

The ordinary-size integrated credit gap is 159.5. The larger 25% case has
government surplus but household planned financing need 607; closing one
budget can shift burdens instead of improve lives. A 45% retention/75%
corporate case fits the first year's assumed public cash limit but requires
enterprise financing and exhausts most cash room. Repeating unchanged
flows/limits leaves about 47.30 unfunded before later debt/price/investment
effects. None of these implied loans is committed or socially qualified.

Both larger cases require energy 1,220 GWh against ordinary firm availability
950 and water 8 million cubic metres against 6.5 after ecological/standby
allocations. Their gaps are 270 GWh and 1.5 million cubic metres. The inherited
care and institutional staff-loss cases remain unfilled. An export-loss
test also needs 53.5 more foreign currency beyond declared reserve room.
Actual land, emissions, material inputs, prices, actors and service/remedy
receipt remain unknown. Larger-GDP tax receipts cannot fund a project that
is required before that larger output is possible.

## Sources, qualification and objections

Original numerical support is the preserved September family and design-58
cohort/input/model family, explicitly pinned. New macro tax classifications,
capital, consumption and physical vectors are authored, not measured data.
[Validation](validation.json) checks paired flows, GDP approaches, cash versus
depreciation, conditional consolidation, debt/cash/unfunded identity, stock
price, resource/FX and private-finance counterexamples and predecessor scope.
It establishes neither lender consent, actual control, lawful adoption nor
independent review. A new source manifest does not transfer old approval.

The [UN SNA description](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/Nationalaccount/sna.asp)
is a methodological comparator for coherent production/income/use accounts;
its official page was inspected on 14 October and links both 2008 and 2025
editions. The [IMF GFSM 2014 overview](https://www.imf.org/external/np/sta/gfsm/index.htm)
and official indexed consolidation/dividend material support distinction of
counterpart flows and appropriate public envelopes. The direct chapter
retrieval returned 403; no full chapter inspection is claimed. These support
accounting comparison only, not our classifications, prices, taxes or welfare.
No new original social observation or actual jurisdiction is available.

Material objections: coordinated accounts can centralize framing and burden
ordinary life; government assembles bounded relevant evidence and preserves
private/informal cooperation, plural interpretation and challenge. High tax
or credit withdrawal may impair entry, work/time or private independence;
numerical balance cannot dispose of those value/behavior questions. Public
capital can concentrate patronage; the whole appointment/funding/information/
remedy chain remains subject to C03–C05/C08 safeguards. Inability is not
deliberate refusal; qualified interim protection and actual failed remedy
must remain visible. These are authored objections, not affected-party assent.
