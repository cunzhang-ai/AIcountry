# F8. Armed attack during a disputed election and payment outage

*Part of [Whole-country stress](../../FOUNDING-AND-STRESS.md); integrated design 55 research, not a real-world result.*

**Events:** An armed attack strikes the border while an election result is
contested. The outgoing cabinet holds armory credentials and secret threat
reports, the Assembly's usual chamber is unavailable, a payment operator fails,
and an ally requests cross-border operations. A targeting model proposes a
person as a hostile combatant. Civilians need shelter and fuel that the defense
plan also assumes.

**Candidate response:** Commanders use only preauthorized force needed to repel
the attack and protect forces. The cabinet records orders and legal grounds,
notifies a protected Assembly channel within the proposed 24 hours, and arranges
an alternate authenticated sitting with opposition-inclusive classified review.
No disputed count renews the cabinet's mandate or transfers command before a
lawful result; neither the model nor the ally authorizes an attack. A distinct
legal assessment precedes cross-border action, and a continuing campaign
beyond the proposed seven-day limit needs an affirmative Assembly mandate.
Without that vote, standing territorial defense and safe withdrawal continue
under prior law. The Treasury and defense ministry expose competing fuel,
medical, shelter and foreign-payment claims; lawful emergency payments preserve
original records and due claims. An independently preserved order and casualty
record allows investigation and remedy during the conflict. Upon lawful
certification, command, armories, intelligence access and payment credentials
move to successor offices under protected custody.

**What remains unproved:** the force, alternate Assembly route, classified
reviewer, courts, independent investigators, communications, reserves and fuel
may share one compromised controller or simply lack capacity. An external
threat might demand action beyond the proposed limit; a vote might endorse an
unlawful campaign. No simulation here establishes attack facts, international
legality, civilian safety, reliable AI, supply or legitimate succession.
