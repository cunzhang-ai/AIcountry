# SD21–SD24: real rivals, retained losses and finite revision

*Part of [C06/43](../43-country-source-and-delivery.md); design 106 candidate.*

**SD21 — Compare complete social choices.** A is the selected next access-first
reference: I05+0.4M allowance, universal essential duties, paid plural provision,
public residual capacity and mixed physical repair. B keeps the same social
package with import-heavy/bulk supply. C2 is preferred conditional full U+extra needs with SD06 untapered 0.4M allowance; C1 remains a rival;
C0 first-unit tax remains a comparator. D now fairly retains the same current
essential offer and A personal rights, while prepaid plural capacity replaces
public residual delivery. It cannot win by deleting a promised service or
shifting its cost to unpaid households. E is direct-public capacity with actually paid acquisition,
management, qualification and renewal. Retain/no-change and a paid alternative
founding context remain admissible. No default averaging resolves these choices.

**SD22 — Hold comparisons fair without hiding different promises.** Use one
population/need/old-claim calendar, tax law, prices/exposure assumptions, quality,
independent review and paid transition. For genuine policy differences publish
the resulting changed cash, workload, demand, ownership and access; do not force
identical outcomes or invent output to close budgets. Compare new commitments
separately from due old rights, which every rival must carry. All 37 rows and
later duties, private production, capital/inventories, G/S/local and owned-FX
sources, simultaneous shifts, ten-year renewal/debt and the Y tail remain in
scope. Current calculations demonstrate selected subsets, not that whole comparison.

**SD23 — Retain disagreement and reject on relevant evidence.** I05 may preserve
stigma/nonclaim and dependence; U may displace urgently needed capacity and
tax/investment; prepaid private capacity may capture rents or fail thin places;
direct public concentration may block exit or hide maintenance. More imported
supply reduces local exposures and increases external ones. These are different
losses, not a single welfare score. If actual same-scope evidence defeats a
proposed core's cash/skills/FX/receipt, reject its scale or new commencement and
reopen the design; do not lower existing rights to pass. Reject a fiscally cheap
rival if it defeats practical equal standing, adequate access or peaceful control.
No current result establishes that either social model wins.

**SD24 — Make a scoped judgment and keep the real gap visible.** The design 107
[comparison](../../../settlement/country-delivery/04-matched-country-comparison.md)
now joins current function/person/physical cases. Retain A as the next access-first
reference; its C1 preference is superseded by design 131 C2 for the conditional universal goal; reject choosing D
for assumed savings or E solely for deleting supplier margin. D costs more in
this offer and requires a quarterly prepayment/performance source; E moves paid
capital/management risk to G. None is selected as a sourced operating launch.
The independent-producer/private-demand/price/source and old-claim book remains
unfinished. A required external-profit threshold is not evidence of output or
permission to select that missing profit. Finish that material case rather than
claim the priced public subset proves a complete country.
Its written deliverable must state exactly which obligations/supply/source choices
it selects, rejects or leaves conditional, with missing original observations and
value disagreement separate. Work may prioritize an actual urgent harm or a larger
contradiction. Do not append a local detail simply because its predecessor ended
with that question. Reopen on material collection, price, title, source/worker/
receiver offers, exclusion, ecology, capture or stronger original objection.
When monitoring is absent, no readiness or superiority claim accrues by time.

Design108 now provides the named domestic/traded/title and receipt tests. It
**rejects the old fixed wage economy as a revenue forecast**, not the social
objective. Smaller books leave major wages unreceived in the sensitivity;
even a deliberately invented workforce-saturating export book is no buyer or
financing proof. Retain A/C2 as current conditional choices; C1 remains a scoped rival. Do not repair the case by
selecting order volume, IP rents, a public buyout or money issue to fit the gap.

The next whole-country comparison must distinguish monetary settlement, actual
production/acceptance and owned foreign payment. Test a distinctly authorized
fiscal-money alternative against current tax/debt at the same output/private
saving and price/FX conditions; retain or reject either on those conditions,
public control and actual received lives. Household needs, safe professional
capacity and private input/VAT/credit sources remain joined obligations. A new
financial label or extra office does not resolve them. Urgent harm/larger gaps
can reprioritize; final decisions require fresh substantive review.
