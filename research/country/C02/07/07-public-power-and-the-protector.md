# PD25–PD28: intelligence, courts, public control and the supervisor’s own conduct

*Design 96 ordinary-data research candidate; not enacted law, funded readiness or
demonstrated superiority.*

**PD25 — Police and intelligence remain covered.** Supervisory
records/access/security/retention/correction duties apply to public and private
controllers, including police, defense and intelligence; no ministry can exempt itself
by classification or contract. C10/P define any actual collection/search authority: the
Commissioner cannot authorize interception, cancel an investigation's lawful warrant or
invent a national-security data basis. Inspectors receive necessary protected original
grounds, authorization, purpose, dissemination and retention evidence under the statute,
with privilege and source safety, not automatic public disclosure. The implicated
service does not decide the final legality of withholding from supervision; a competent
unconflicted court determines contested lawful access with protected hearing and an
answerable case. The Assembly's opposition-inclusive security committee retains separate
protected scrutiny; it is no substitute judge or intelligence commander. Chief
appointments and staff access must not depend on a discretionary clearance veto by the
service being reviewed: an independent court reviews specific necessity/exclusion with
safe alternatives. An absence of safe qualified staff is disclosed, not deemed
independent inspection.

Where notifying a subject/access to their data would concretely prejudice an ongoing
lawful operation or another person's safety, only a specific enacted ground permits
necessary scoped delay/redaction with recorded reasons, expiry and independent judicial
review. The service must seek renewed justification before expiry and notify/provide the
lawful remainder as soon as the ground ends; no permanent 'neither confirm nor deny' by
institutional label. Delay does not block a confidential complaint or direct court
scrutiny, nor turn a warrant into a general research/training permission. Publication of
sensitive operational detail and meaningful protected review are distinct acts. An
operational/investigative delayed-subject-notice order under this data floor, excluding
PD08’s narrow protected journalism/archive/research rule, states a review/expiry no
later than 30 calendar days; renewal needs fresh specific judicial necessity before
expiry, not a service label. When the ground ends, give the lawful notice/remainder
without undue delay and within seven calendar days, subject only to another specifically
reasoned judicial ground. Earlier sector duties prevail. This bounds privacy
notice/review, creates no surveillance power and does not restart or extend any
underlying warrant or authorization.

**PD26 — Court administration is covered; judgments are not executive data orders.**
Routine court employment, procurement, access control, cyber security and administrative
records remain subject to the ordinary data duties and supervisory correction. The
Commissioner cannot direct a judge's evidentiary rulings, adjudication, assignment,
judgment/publication/redaction or legally required case-file retention. Privacy in those
judicial functions is enforced through the competent court's
procedural/application/appeal routes with ordinary assignment, direct-conflict recusal
and protected evidence, not a blanket judicial exemption. A technical vendor's
processing or police collection does not acquire judicial immunity because its output
enters a case: supervise that distinct conduct within lawful privilege/source
protection, while the court decides admissibility, judicial use and correction of its
own decision. Court-administration inspection cannot relabel a merits challenge or
obtain protected deliberations. A complaint against a supervising court/judge goes to a
competent different unconflicted formation under existing B/CJ competence; CJ's special
own-court route applies only within its closed scope, not as a universal new tribunal.
Disclose a genuine lack of lawful competent review rather than appoint an ad hoc
friendly judge or let the Commissioner decide a judgment.

**PD27 — Privacy cannot suppress public control.** Apply the substantive law's actual
expression, journalism, research, lawful source protection, information-access and
public-interest disclosure rules. Target unlawful data practices, not political
viewpoint, disputed truth or reputational discomfort. Privacy does not erase lawfully
required procurement/beneficial-control/campaign records, evidence of official
corruption, protected testimony to investigators, or necessary opposition/audit access;
law defines redaction, safe inspection, recipient limits and challenge rather than
automatic public bulk release. Contested orders whose effect would restrain a specific
journalistic/political publication require an ordinary court's specific necessary
proportionate order under substantive law and B17 if urgent; the supervisor cannot order
a broad network/site shutdown, editorial change or advance truth licence. An access
requester or affected subject may take a contested privacy/information-access balance
directly to a competent independent court without first obtaining the Commissioner's
consent; neither the source office nor Commissioner is the final judge of that conflict.
Public facts are not automatically free for any profiling purpose, and investigative
publication is not automatically a licence to sell unrelated private dossiers. Resolve
the actual conflict with reasoned protected evidence, not a universal right to be
forgotten or an executive secrecy veto.

**PD28 — Supervise the protector through external routes.** The office is itself a
controller under the same data duties. Its own access/correction/processing complaint
may be received by ordinary intake, but contested legality, compelled disclosure,
coercive correction or penalty against itself goes directly to an unconflicted ordinary
court; no chief finally adjudicates their own office's breach. Existing independent
audit/integrity channels can inspect their lawful records and refer proved misconduct;
courts decide remedies/removal and P/VD08 fault. Preserve minimum separately controlled
protected records/access, subject notice and correction, not the chief's sole keys,
vendor, counsel or intake. Use B19 only within its enacted substitute scope. Publish
safe aggregate own-office complaints, reversals, unmet orders and retention failures.
Staff/subjects may reach courts, audit, ombud or prosecutor within distinct lawful
competences without the chief's permission; whistleblower protection does not make
allegations guilt. Simultaneous capture of Assembly, staff, Treasury, vendors, courts
and execution remains a possible failure, not cured by calling a director independent.
