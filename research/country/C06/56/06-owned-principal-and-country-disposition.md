*Part of [FP](../56-national-fiscal-settlement-and-source-boundaries.md); design139 proposed clarification, no current-law change.*

**FP19 — An asset return is not automatically fresh pension income.** A
voluntary funded-plan withdrawal must distinguish verified already-taxed own
capital, income/gains previously recognized, actual untaxed income and a separate
defined employer/insurance promise. Preserve source-period evidence and lawful
prior tax relief/terms; restore recognized basis only once. Do not tax an
already-taxed capital return again merely because the payment says “pension”,
or exempt all actual income under that label. Missing evidence is disputed
classification with protected correction, not a universal zero basis.

This is an extension of PA/TA's ownership/receipt distinction, not a new funded-
plan exemption, contribution deduction, tax deferral or pension-yield levy.
Detailed prospective contribution/return/withdrawal treatment and compatible
basis allocation require coordinated source law, records and independent review;
an NB licence does not supply them. Preserve stronger actual source-law rights,
notice, old units and existing payment/refund/court deadlines. No retrospective
recovery, custodian asset sweep or regulator-created tax by foreign analogy.

Y statutory solidarity contributions and earned annual units are not a privately
owned personal capital account; this rule creates no principal withdrawal or
new tax deduction from public Y. Worked wages, pensions, compensation, basis
recovery and refunds retain actual source character. Private beneficiary/client
property is not the manager's company profit or unrestricted Treasury cash.

I05 independently counts safely usable resources under I04–I06. Excluding
returned basis from income tax does not exempt accessible assets from its
lawful asset-draw rule; matching actual income/principal/resource use occurs
once, with protected buffer, pension disregard and no double O/A credit.
Blocked/locked/imagined balances and another person's assets remain unavailable.
No one must liquidate a private plan merely to resolve a new fiscal comparison.
