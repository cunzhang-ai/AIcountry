# Commitment 118. Preserve useful service when investment dates move

[IV09–IV12](../../country/C06/63/03-timing-options-and-usable-service.md)
joins the capital payer and service owner for material retiming. The elected
body sets bounded release and return authority. Sector owners protect actual
access and use existing independent review. Record payment and service dates
separately. Count concurrent obligations and keep accepted interim receiving.

Keep DB, FB, MF and sector-law obligations, restricted funds and prior claims.
Actual procurement capacity, access, costs and lawful enactment remain open.
