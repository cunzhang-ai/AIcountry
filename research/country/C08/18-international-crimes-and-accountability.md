# C08. International crimes and accountable public force

15 October 2026 · Design 73 · Proposed G01–G14, not enacted law

Select a domestic international-crime schedule with defined acts/context,
personal fault, actual control, independent civilian trial and finite punishment.
Government, military, commercial and AI-mediated power face the same proof
discipline. A commander, opposition movement, company, nationality or model
label is not itself an offender. Immediate protection/repair has separate grounds.

This is an **independently authored national candidate**, using explicitly
dated UN and official comparative-law texts. It is not a live incorporation
or certified reproduction of the latest consolidated Rome Statute. The
[source/variation account](../../settlement/international-crimes/03-sources-choices-and-limits.md)
preserves what was inspected, unavailable and deliberately changed.
Gender/sexual-orientation protections, common IAC/NIAC coverage, age 18 and
25-year domestic maxima are proposed values, not universal treaty prescriptions.

[C10/07](../C10/07-atrocity-prevention-and-legal-control.md) connects prevention,
command records, independent investigation and a replaceable civilian state.
The [boundary cases](../../settlement/international-crimes/README.md) are
authored reasons, not verdicts, affected participation or safety evidence.
Actual source-law/treaty compatibility, immune persons/assets, capacity and
received remedies need qualified review before operation. No statehood,
territory, treaty membership, foreign consent or actual adoption is assumed.

Remaining sector/monetary, special reproductive and conflict-detention rules
still require specification; the ordinary code/country is not complete.
Next close those grouped powers and rights, then audit the entire country's
simultaneous obligations/resources and comparative life/public-control case.
More articles or a valid index are not proof of a superior operating state.
