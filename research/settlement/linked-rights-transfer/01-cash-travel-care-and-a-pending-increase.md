# An unchanged award, linked access and a pending increase

The lawful payer changes at the start of day 1. A person holds a valid
benefit award, accessible transport entitlement and an agreed home-care
visit. The transport entitlement uses proof of the award. Care has its
own assessment. The person requested an increase on day −12 and has a
review appointment by day 2. The change of payer does not reopen the
application or move the review deadline. All units below are authored.
For the service and review lines, payment and actual useful receipt are
both assumed only in the stated successful routes.

| Existing duty | Useful deadline | Units |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ordinary benefit at the last valid rate | Day 1 | 4 |
| Accepted accessible travel | Day 1 | 2 |
| Received home-care visit | Day 1 | 3 |
| An independent review appointment that actually occurs | Day 2 | 1 |

The ten units represent four differently scoped duties. A later valid
decision in the fixture authorizes two more units for the period including
day 1. That decision is made on day 4 and the increase is paid on day 5.
The original filing date and any legally determined entitlement period
remain visible. The additional amount was not independently confirmed
as payable on day 1.

| Transition or failure | Obligations met by original deadlines, valued at their stated units | Consequence |
|---|---:|---|
| Preserve payment, travel proof, care and the pending review | 10 | No listed deadline missed. Later increase is recorded separately. |
| Transfer the ordinary payment but omit travel proof | 8 | The transport provider cannot verify entitlement by day 1. |
| Transfer current payment and care but omit travel proof and pending review | 7 | Travel and review miss their useful dates. |
| Ordinary payment status is unknown and no advance is available | 6 | The person misses the day-1 payment even if later settlement proves possible. |
| Expressly funded advance arrives on day 1 and ordinary transfer later arrives on day 2 | 10 | Four benefit units were usable in time. Gross spending for these duties is 14 before any lawful reconciliation. |

For the advance, assume a separate lawful source and a cap of four.
The ordinary instruction remains unresolved at the deadline. Its later
receipt does not justify representing both cash transfers as two
entitlements or ignoring the four units of gross overlap. Any later
recovery requires a distinct decision. If nothing can lawfully be
recovered, the public account retains the overlap cost. Where the
alternate instead verifies failure of the ordinary transfer, it can
use the one ordinary payment authority and settle the same four
once. Sending another instruction without resolving the first
does not supply that proof.

The linked transport provider needs a valid eligibility confirmation
and period, without the diagnosis or full award history. If transport
eligibility itself lawfully ended before day 1, changing payers cannot
revive it. If the record only reports a technical status error,
there must be a protected route to examine the original decision and
reach an actual transport provider. A later journey cannot replace
a missed necessary appointment.
