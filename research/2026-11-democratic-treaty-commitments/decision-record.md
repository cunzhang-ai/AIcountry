# Design223. Make external commitments subject to democratic choice

## Baseline and decision

Design222 is the pinned baseline. Use the working framework and decision
template. A8 and the external-relations chapter already require prior
elected authority for material treaties, provisional application and
withdrawal. The gap is an operating procedure for exact text, hearings,
protected evidence, later joint decisions, valid external notice and the
services that depend on the instrument.

Select TR01–TR12. The Assembly gives bounded negotiating authority and
votes on the exact material commitment. Cabinet carries out the authorized
external act. Set a 30-elapsed-day ordinary minimum after complete accessible
receipt and actual scrutiny, and a separate two-thirds urgency route with
at least seven elapsed days and actual scrutiny. These are candidate
choices and do not measure adequate deliberation in every case. Keep
lower-impact cooperation within prior law. Reserve material provisional
use, amendment, and withdrawal for actual prior elected approval.
A treaty vote does not supply external acceptance, statutory powers or money.

## Evidence and alternatives

The Vienna Convention helps separate signature, consent, application,
entry, termination and their effects. The UK and New Zealand comparators
show real scrutiny with different executive and parliamentary roles.
The UK committee reports an exceptional case of post-ratification laying
and practical problems with access, time and amendments. This does not
establish that all comparable agreements suffer the same problems or
that 30 calendar days can replace actual evidence and hearing.

Retain executive negotiation with a review window as a substantive
alternative. It can offer flexibility and speed for ordinary cooperation.
For material commitments, a legal act or practical exposure can precede
a usable opportunity to prevent it. Requiring affirmative approval for
every technical exchange would consume scarce legislative time and can
make sound cooperation harder. Keep the explicit technical delegation,
while testing cumulative scope and actual ability to object. A full
standing ban on any provisional application protects oversight and
can sacrifice a useful lawful bridge. Select finite expressly approved
provisional use with an external end mechanism. A majority could still
suppress opposition or invoke urgency strategically. Use access, fixed
votes, actual hearing and stronger constitutional rights.

## Compatibility, transition and further evidence

Keep A8, A2, PE, DR, DB/FB, HT, FT and OP/T. Constitutional amendment,
territorial choice, actual use of force, migration and individual
protection, money, taxes and domestic implementing law retain their
own authority. Do not treat a departmental classification as a general
veto. Explicitly map prior foreign obligations, existing legal positions,
appropriations, guaranteed debt, workers and claims during adoption.
Domestic invalidity or a missed vote does not decide external effect.
An unresolved external duty needs competent international legal review
and lawful correction. No new power to break an existing obligation is
selected.

Test one authorized material treaty choice with actual qualified
legislative and diplomatic staff, lawful members' access, affected
domestic and foreign perspectives and an authenticated counterparty
calendar. Compare retention, valid exit and a bounded bridge through
one missed deadline and unavailable source. Verify actual notice,
appropriation, recognized claims and received service. Reopen for lost
scrutiny, secrecy capture, late notice, new reliance or a superior
simpler arrangement. Actual affected participation, independent legal
review, enactment, funds, foreign assent and observed outcomes remain open.
