# Commitment 119. Keep protected journeys and their services reachable

[MO17–MO22](../../country/C07/17/05-joined-journeys-and-service-access.md)
assigns a funded continuity owner for covered confirmed journeys. The
first contact retains responsibility until accepted. The transport owner
acts by the useful time, without requiring advance passenger payment.
The service owner checks access and actual receiving under its own law.

Retain safety, consent, due journeys, paid reserves and independent
remedies. Count vehicles, drivers and onward receiving only once.
Actual source, paid independent execution and outcomes remain open.
