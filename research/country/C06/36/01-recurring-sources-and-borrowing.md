# RF01–RF04: income, assets and borrowing are different sources

*Part of [C06/36](../36-recurring-finance-and-pension-commitments.md); design 89 candidate.*

**RF01 — Select recurring funding for recurring protection.** The elected
budget assigns collectable recurring taxes, lawful pooled contributions and
actual distributable public returns to ordinary personal protection, services,
maintenance and pensions. Contributions and restricted assets keep their legal
owners/purposes. No expected asset sale, reserve depletion, investment gain,
new debt issue or unrealized wealth is renamed recurring revenue. Declining
pollution receipts cannot permanently back growing rights without replacement.
Publish collection timing and personal/service incidence after tax, benefit,
price and producer responses, not a gross headline yield.

Treasury maintains separate current uses, actual capital replacement, net new
formation, inventory, acquisitions, equity/loans, coupon, principal, arrears
and liquidity accounts. A financial subscription is not another public service
or new GDP. Replacing a worn asset is capital spending but a continuing cost;
removing it is not a sustainable saving. A cash balance supported by unpaid
wages, depleted private working capital or a missing remedy is not full funding.

**RF02 — Borrow through explicit elected authority.** C06/06 remains controlling.
The annual borrowing mandate identifies purpose, gross/net issuance, guarantees,
currency, instruments, maturity, maximum exposure, permitted counterparty classes,
genuinely precommitted offers if any, and servicing sources. An authorized auction
need not know its purchasers in advance; execution records the actual parties
and settled sources with protected scrutiny. Treasury executes within it.
New authority expires on its
stated date; existing debt, servicing and standing essential dues do not.
An existing lawful multiyear debt contract is not annually voidable.

For refinancing, show each binding issuance and actual discharge in its
settlement sequence and the resulting gross outstanding peak. Use the
existing mandate where it covers that peak and its uses. Otherwise
authorize a named, time-limited overlap by an exact prior elected vote
with an exposure ceiling, currency, maturities, restricted proceeds
and failed-settlement disposition. An offer supplies no usable cash
until settlement. An old claim reduces exposure only on actual lawful
discharge. Accept simultaneous exchange only where legal effect,
counterparties and settlement can support it. If a planned discharge
fails, stop further use of the overlap authority and preserve gross
claims and proceeds under their terms. Escalate on the voted clock
without automatic renewal or diversion from standing duties.

Investment or stabilization borrowing may be justified without a current-year
surplus. A temporary exception for recurring provision requires an express
Assembly vote with duration, displaced alternatives, household/creditor effects
and a dated next recurring-source/coverage decision. There is no automatic
permanent rollover, willing lender, sovereign overdraft, constitutional creditor
policy veto or automatic balanced-budget cut. Failure to meet the plan triggers
existing E19 disclosure/protection and an elected response; it neither enacts a
tax nor authorizes default, care cancellation or an election delay.

**RF03 — Examine the continuing national burden.** Show monthly useful-time
cash and a rolling ten-year current/capital/debt path, plus Y's75-year cohort
account and remaining-claims tail. Ten years is a candidate planning window,
not proof that later obligations vanish. Separate debt currency/maturities,
gross redemptions/rollovers, cash buffers, lenders and restricted/public-bank
exposures. An internal G-to-S coupon is a G expense/S receipt, not a new source
for consolidated public protection. A redeemed bank principal is the bank's
asset, not unrestricted money for another firm's investment or wages.

Interest, wages, tax collection, prices, work, imports, assets, behavior and
new claims can change together. A constant-primary cash stress is a diagnostic
only. Debt/GDP and solvency signals inform public choice; neither an AI threshold
nor a fiscal/actuarial office can impose new burdens or override elected law.
Record the actual voted decision, refusal or unresolved disagreement and the
continuing unpaid duties. A model cannot claim approval from a missed deadline.

**RF04 — Reject superficial cash closure.** In the frozen selective-tax I05
reference, the814.853 million CU G cash deficit contains36.150 interest,
4.000 financial equity subscription,50.689 own capital/inventory and 724.013
current nonfinancial primary gap. The recurring full-cost primary gap is768.577
after excluding only new retrofit6.000 and initial inventory0.126, while retaining
44.563 replacement. The G+S version is5.000 larger because its internal coupon
cancels. These are authored subsets, not a complete national expenditure floor.

A deliberately extreme 82% in all bands universal case matches offered first-year
loans but still has233.153 cash deficit and186.877 recurring full-cost primary
gap. Repeating its noninterest cash need exhausts the frozen new-offer cap in
year 2 in the declared stress; no future lender is promised. More tax is no
solution when it makes nonbank production loss-making under the same fixed
wage/input assumptions. Nor does renaming universal transfers as tax credits
change their net source requirement. Retain actual needs and disclose the
failed assumption; do not invent GDP, coerced saving or free provider conversion.
