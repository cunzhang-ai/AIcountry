## What was wrong with the prior whole

Design 4 linked constitutional authority, services and a public budget, but four
cross-system boundaries remained too thin. A citizen-only ordinary franchise plus
an unspecified naturalization route could leave settled residents governed but
unable to replace lawmakers. A three-year *public* cash/staff example did not
specify bank, payment, import or productive paths for the social floor. Police and
emergency rules did not bind every actor able to surveil, detain or use force.
Finally, project-document updates stood in for a future state's lawful route from
new knowledge to policy. These are findings about proposal coverage. They do not
show that the selected corrections will work or outperform existing countries.

The value choices are explicit: political standing should track durable subjection
to common institutions; security should be protective but not a single command able
to decide elections; economic promises should distinguish legal entitlement from
real supply and external payments; knowledge should inform, not govern in place of,
the public. The contested values are also real: nationality versus residence,
monetary autonomy versus finite external-currency discipline, concentrated response
speed versus distributed coercive power, and stability versus institutional change.
No shared outcome score resolves them.

## Selected reference choices and alternatives retained

| Country decision | Selected for design 5 | Alternative and material reason to retain it for comparison |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary political membership | Adult citizens and adult settled residents after one ordinary four-year Assembly term of habitual residence have one national vote and candidacy; local residence threshold is shorter. Founding membership is decided separately. | Citizen-only franchise with guaranteed naturalization may better express a nationality bond and can be simpler, but can still be controlled through admission or paperwork. Immediate universal-presence voting reduces exclusion but weakens the durable-community boundary. Four years is provisional, not empirically optimal. |
| Money and production | Conditional external-currency reference: lawful clearing, finite liquidity, supervised/resolvable banks, essential-payment continuity, critical import paths and linked production/physical accounts. | A domestic-currency system could absorb some nominal shocks but creates inflation, exchange, foreign-input and political-credit risks. Retaining a cash-only sketch is simpler but cannot support a completeness claim. No actual external currency access is presumed. |
| Protection and force | One enacted rights floor across coercive actors, local civilian protection, bounded national specialist/rights-protection capacity, independent prosecution/investigation, separate intelligence and external defense, and no force powers through procurement. | A single national chain may mobilize rapidly but can concentrate election-defeating command. Local autonomy may preserve plural centers but can abandon people under local capture. The divided choice needs real personnel, money and reviewed triggers. |
| National learning | Distributed accessible agenda and inquiry, bounded lawful trial, qualified evaluation and a fresh elected decision with continuing duties. Research-project revision remains separate. | Ordinary ad hoc change is less costly; a central expert renewal body may have more specialist continuity. Neither should silently acquire a veto over elected lawmaking, and the distributed path also needs capacity and protection against captured framing. |

The choices form a conditional reference package. Settled-resident standing must
reach the bodies that enact tax, security and membership rules; border, identity
and security offices cannot change the electorate through administrative labels.
The security architecture protects that electorate, including during handover, but
its national power is bounded so it cannot become an incumbent's substitute army.
The external-currency package constrains what the Assembly may honestly promise;
it supplies no secret monetary rescue for social or security costs. The learning
route can investigate and recommend changes to any of these choices, but cannot
approve its own authority or make trials scale automatically. Separate media and
AI providers from the prior civic-power correction remain applicable.

## Objections, dependencies and continuing obligations

The membership rule may give votes to people who remain tied to another polity;
the opposing objection is that four years still excludes governed residents. One
vote in this country's election, a stable constitutional franchise, early local
standing and independent status review are candidate constraints, not resolution
of the value dispute. [UN Human Rights Committee guidance](https://docstore.ohchr.org/SelfServices/FilesHandler.ashx?enc=NDHbUvPo0H0e6ReM%2BeoASfvbY3aH6JttLpsRvkmL87wD80W4xnAiRpmKA6ZLwTp9xzCLNUYwTBP35uH1Fw6yqQ%3D%3D)
protects citizens' votes; it does not itself command this broader franchise.
Old democratic candidate D1 remains a conflicting predecessor, not silently edited.

The monetary choice can make creditors safer than patients when reserves are scarce.
Payment and deposit priorities need adopted law, reasoned distribution, appeal and
funding; courts cannot create foreign currency or imported medicine. The cited UN
and IMF accounting/monetary frameworks in the supporting analysis establish useful
categories, not AIcountry's reserves, trade balance or bank solvency. The source
three-year service model remains an authored public-sector comparison, not a
whole-economy feasibility certificate. The new security capacities also compete
for staff, cash, facilities and data; no budget line has been measured for them.

National intervention against local violence may become an occupation; functional
separation can delay rescue. Law must define an urgent but reviewable trigger,
command, funded mutual aid, appeal and return to local control. Investigation of
protectors must have actual evidence access and enforcement independent of their
command. Blanket staff purges may violate due process and destroy protective
capacity; blind inheritance can preserve abuse. Existing cases, custody clocks,
rights, contracts, salaries, pensions, debts, treaty duties, essential services and
ecological duties persist through lawful transition.

The inquiry system can become another gatekeeper if one funder chooses problems,
data and success measures. The proposal permits alternative media and rival
interpretations, discloses control, funds qualified review and reserves binding
choices for elected institutions. It has not specified a real staff complement,
budget or lawful implementation body. Sensitive testimony is not permanently
public merely because reasons are recorded. Ordinary reports need not be technical
dossiers. A no-change outcome can be justified; more trials or faster updates
are not evidence of better lives.

This revision affects C01–C14 and the consumers named in the four supporting
analyses: constitutional franchise and D1, elections, migration, local government,
banking, budget, social guarantees, procurement, criminal procedure, intelligence,
security and emergency schedules, public data, and constitutional change. The
candidate A1–A9 authority package remains the earlier legal reference but does not
already encode these new choices. Exact current-edition reconciliation and fresh
review must precede any claim of an adoptable code. No reviewer approval or
operational certification transfers from design 4 to design 5.

## Result, actual checks and next gate

**Evidence qualification:** a local textual consistency review, authored accounting
sensitivity and official conceptual comparisons were completed; no observed national
outcomes or affected-party accounts were obtained. **Research selection:** the
four arrangements above now form integrated design 5, with rivals and objections
preserved. **Legal authorization:** none. **Operational readiness:** unknown; no
territory, electoral register, payment clearing, bank resolution, protective
command, qualified evaluator, budget or remedy executor was demonstrated.
**Observed outcomes:** none, including no proof of democratic resilience or better
lives. The project's checks establish file integrity and internal linkage only.

The next consequential question is whether this package, or a rival, can be lawfully
and practically evaluated in a **specific jurisdiction by an affected public**.
The first bounded deliverable there would be an applicability dossier: current
membership and electoral rules, public/private opening balances and critical
imports, the complete coercive-actor and command map, and existing agenda,
evaluation and amendment routes. It must identify participants and dissent,
applicable authority, indispensable continued services, payment/people/material
capacity and an independent review path before suggesting adoption. There is no
resource budget or responsible real-world sponsor in this project, so this remains
a gate, not a scheduled intervention. Credible jurisdictional evidence, affected
opposition, failed delivery or a better coherent alternative reopens any selection.
