# Y01–Y04: basic protection, age and retirement choice

*Part of [C07 retirement candidate](../04-retirement-security.md); design65 with design157 factor clarification.*

[Y17–Y23](05-common-factors-and-equal-standing.md) adds a common birth-year/
claim-age public factor basis and prospective method date: no personal sex/
gender/health/income/lifespan pricing. Preserve normal age65, funded bridge,
earlier elected/binding shares and all old units. Period expectancy alone
is no valid quote; monthly/survival/tail/source methods remain necessary.

**Y01 — Individual residence-based basic protection.** A person ordinarily
resident at standard pension age receives the basic payment individually,
without a work, marriage, asset or fixed residence-duration test. Ordinary
residence means an actual stable center of life, with multiple forms of
evidence, supported identity and appeal; a missing document is not conclusive.
The rule grants no immigration power or citizenship. A visitor and a person
with disputed residence retain the applicable needs/urgent floor while
status is determined. Earned rights are not lost on moving; export and
foreign coordination require applicable law, not an assumed foreign treaty.

**Y02 — Age and work are distinct.** The candidate standard age is 65 for
all genders. It does not require leaving work, surrendering political rights
or proving incapacity. The monthly basic equals the I01 adult ordinary
needs-basket component; separately assessed housing, disability and care
remain separate. General revenue pays it. I05 credits its actual same-period
receipt once, rather than paying it again or eliminating an earned pension.
Entitlement starts when age and ordinary residence both hold, prorated for
the initial calendar month. A person becoming resident at 70 starts then,
not five years before residence; a late application does not itself cancel
an already accrued claim or independent review of the protected start date.

**Y03 — Price protection.** Revalue accrued annual earning bases and pensions
each January by the verified general consumer-price ratio; do not reduce
nominal accrued amounts on deflation. A cumulative price rise of at least
5% since the current payment basis triggers provisional adjustment the next
month, with source correction and needs-protected recovery under I08.
Basket/basic adjustments and immediate hardship protection retain their
own earlier duties. Delayed statistics are not evidence that prices stayed
constant. The evidence service publishes source/uncertainty and a provisional
index under the preenacted method, open to independent challenge. If no
supportable estimate exists, disclose the unquantified adjustment liability
and seek an elected interim-payment decision, with immediate needs protection
continuing. Neither a minister nor an AI may quietly set missing inflation to zero.

**Y04 — Supported choice.** Earned pension may be claimed in 25%, 50%, 75%
or 100% shares at standard age. An unclaimed share may start between 60 and
70 only with a preenacted transparent lifetime annuity-factor schedule:
the expected standard-start payment value divided by the chosen-start value,
both measured at the same earlier age, including survival to a deferred start.
Use a default zero real discount rate, disclosed survival and bounded tail
treatment. The factor applies only to that elected share; prior shares
cannot be repriced. No adequate survival/tail basis means no valid early/
deferred quote, not loss of the ordinary age-65 claim or a compelled delay.
Explain lifetime reductions, health inequality, taxes and needs interactions
through accessible independent assistance. Continuing contributions create
only the additional lawful accrual, never a second payment of the same right.
Health/disability and hazardous-work bridge support is a separate funded
needs/compensation route before 65; it cannot require accepting a reduced
annuity, dangerous work or compulsory family care. The exact occupational
schedule remains a special-law task, not an authority created by a model.

[SD27/114](../../C06/43/07-production-prices-and-paid-work.md) distinguishes ordinary-basket changes from general CPI and earned/S/index liabilities. Its explicit January B-ratio proxy is an authored sensitivity, not the verified Y03 general index or a new statutory method. Missing index evidence remains an unquantified adjustment claim, not zero inflation.
