# National package: production, households and financing

*Canonical NP04–NP07; part of [C06/33](../33-protection-production-and-national-expansion.md).*

**NP04 — Supply and use constrain the proposal together.** Derive each selected
service/production path from qualified voluntary time, usable assets, tasks,
materials, maintenance, turnover, imports, actual demand and ecological limits.
Separate cash prices, volume, location and due shifts. A green aggregate
energy/water proxy, bank credit or higher nominal GDP is no engineering/care
supply. Relocating work into public service exposes the market output/private
income it displaces; existing public work cannot be renamed idle spare time.

The55,000-worker/100,000-person universe,23 components, old fixed shares and
535.725 preparation envelope are not national limits or available sources.
The hybrid's corrected +50.19% declared-price GVA/FTE requirement is a condition
of that frozen demand model, not a plan or a growth target for this country.
Use a conservative evidenced range of supply/price/voluntary behavior and show
failure cases, including zero unverified AI gain, not a forecast dividend that
pays today's bill. No lower household basket or forced saving repairs a gap.

**NP05 — Choose progressive burdens and real sources.** Continuing basic access
is principally financed through recurrent lawful revenue, national/local
redistribution and explicit paid public duties. Compare a broader progressive
income/capital/rent base, effective lawful collection and removal of unjustified
subsidies with the actual incidence of broad consumption/labor taxation; protect
necessities and personal minimum resources. This is a preference for ability to
contribute, not a claim that taxing capital has no behavioral or external cost.
Amounts/bases/rates require elected law, evidence, equal treatment and appeal.

For an expansion vote state the net household/public-service/producer burden,
collection timing, avoidance/price risks, voluntary investment and displaced
uses. Present recurrent-cost finance separately from borrowing for preparation,
productive assets or an explicit temporary bridge. Debt, repayment, rollover,
currency and contingency needs remain visible; there is no rule that every
annual sector balance must be zero or that all borrowing is bad. Refuse a bundle
whose chosen obligations/sources are internally incompatible, or narrow/repair
it through public law. An estimate cannot levy tax or make a lender participate.

**NP06 — People need resources and actual receipt.** Show net usable cash after
tax/contributions/I05 withdrawal, rent, work travel, paid care and actual in-kind
access for materially different households, including disabled, separated,
undocumented, sparse-area and nonworking people. An average gain can conceal
an intolerable loss. Separate wages/credits, cost compensation, received support,
blocked wealth and unpaid due sums under existing I/Y/RP rules. Preserve the
personal cash component and free refusal of unwanted care/work/AI mediation.

A top-up, pension, voucher or direct budget is not a qualified care shift,
clinical treatment or a home available to rent. Extra cash under scarce housing
can reach landlords; compare actual public/shared supply, purchase and lawful
market correction rather than hiding that exposure in a benefit cap. Voluntary
family care is respected and supported, not a required balancing workforce.
Cash/received service and autonomy/time/relationships are independent outcomes.

**NP07 — Private and future counterparts remain real.** Saving, retained profit,
private investment and borrowing are legitimate voluntary choices, not errors to
force out of the account. Match financial assets/liabilities, taxes, transfers,
restricted insurance and foreign settlement by owner/period. No automatic full
profit payout, household full consumption or mandatory purchase of public debt.
The old 225 pension fixture is not inherited liability data; ongoing accrual,
indexation, survivor claims and promised credits need their own full horizons.
Do not spend pension/client property, expected recovery or exporter FX as public
cash. SF's safe standby needs its own actual source; more protected adopted dues
require recomputation. Do not also count the same stock as ordinary training/
payroll finance while claiming it remains untouched standby; lawful outage
activation may pay its protected unpaid dues, including payroll, under SF05.
