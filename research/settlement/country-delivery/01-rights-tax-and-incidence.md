# Two income bargains, with their real distributional differences

*Design 106 authored fixed-price cases, not national fiscal or transition results.*

A uses I05 individual needs/work retention and T(max(0,Q−0.4M)). C uses full
received ordinary U and T(Q) on other receipts. U-plus-A is a live additional
rival and explicit first-partial-U-year transition. PA10 rates/M and TA source/
receipt definitions continue; actual old allowances/net and earned rights cannot
be silently removed. Universal services are obligations, not taxable private cash.

The frozen PA fixture uses M30,000, ordinary basket 13,248, housing 6,000 and
worked-wage Y10%. It assumes perfect eligible receipt and fixed facts.

| Person | A: tax / total personal cash | C: tax / total personal cash |
|---|---:|---:|
| No work/resources |0 /19,248|0 /19,248|
| Wage 12,000 |0 /25,972.80|2,160 /24,892.80|
| Wage 30,000 |3,000 /32,572.80|7,200 /33,048|
| Older, earned pension 15,000 plus matching basic once |600 /27,648|3,000 /25,248|

These are **before** any required old-net transition top-up. An actual pension
net guarantee forbids reading the 2,400 difference as a permissible cut or saved
G source; its compensation has its own payer and cost. The low-wage A case uses
1,080 more G net cash than C despite zero personal tax; the median case uses
475.20 less. Hence less universal cash is not automatically less net G use for
every person. The cases establish neither national saving nor complete adequate
resources, household prices, actual take-up, autonomy or work response.

I05 can burden people with recurrent assessment and nonclaim. U can strengthen
independence even where cash is identical. Provider rents, tax incidence and
effective service receipt may dominate either arithmetic advantage. Actual
additional needs, net pension disregard, inaccessible assets and safe personal
control remain protected. Different persons' cases are not a sample-weighted
population and must not be summed into national revenue.

The actual noncash employer formula uses its source-year tax function: with
O0,V100,M1000, I05 A400 gives g0 and c100/9; full U gives g25 and c125/9 before
minor-unit adjustment. Apply A once to the annual base, never per employer or
again after gross-up. G employer tax and S contribution remain separate, actual
remittance not assumed. NF's minor-unit allocation and old/new Y dates remain.

Every later midyear-U entrant also retains the first-year A. The dated model
shows separate July-entry examples in2028–2030 with time-proportional U and full
annual allowance, plus a short-source taxpayer without U. These authored annual
tax/U examples do not price within-year I05 reconciliation or actual immigration;
the overlap is recurring exposure, not a founding-only item or verified saving.

the preferred U tax counterpart to C1, prices current duties/paid physical repair,
and retains this earlier subset only within its declared scope. These figures
are not transferred to changed tax/source/receiver assumptions.
