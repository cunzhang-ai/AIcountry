# Public attention: selecting a substantive portfolio

*Canonical FA04–FA06; part of [C12/03](../03-finite-attention-and-lawful-closure.md).*

**FA04 — Thirty issue slots by three different routes.** The reference funds
thirty comparable substantive issue examinations, including reasoned response,
source inspection and supported discussion time, rather than thirty favored
messages. A slot states its concrete question and allotted work; it cannot hide
hundreds of incompatible questions behind one general label. Selected people
may use their own advocates/agents within the common resource ceiling.

| Route | Slots | Selection and challenge |
|---|---:|---|
| Consequence-led scrutiny |12| Process team selects from the received issue references by rights/irreversibility, actual affected dependence, urgency and cross-system consequences; publish comparative reasons and remaining serious risks. |
| Adversarial/affected agenda |9| Four issues chosen by non-governing Assembly members through their protected agenda procedure; five by affected-person agenda setters recruited as below. |
| Public agenda draw |9| Nine agenda setters drawn as below each choose an eligible question from the docket or their own affected situation; not a lottery among agent messages. |

Choose14 consenting human agenda setters by auditable draw from the affected
public, with five designated to the prior charter's materially burdened/poorly
heard scopes and nine to the full affected scope. Actual scope, coverage gaps,
recruitment, confidential one-person checking, strata and reserve draw order are
published before opening. Use lawful population sampling and staffed outreach,
not only a platform's opt-in accounts; absence of a complete safe frame is a
stated limit and may require narrowing the decision's actual imposed effects
or deferral, not dropping a hard-to-reach group while imposing the same burden.
A limited participation claim cannot assert that missing people were represented.
Stratification ensures
coverage, not a claim that fourteen people statistically represent a nation.
No citizenship prerequisite where the affected scope includes other people.

Verify an actual human once solely for the draw/mandate through proportionate
confidential evidence and assisted alternatives, not a universal identity to
speak. AI contributions remain eligible arguments but agent copies have no draw
entries. Sponsorship/control is disclosed; different affected people sharing
one source remain different people. A person cannot occupy both draws. A
selected person declining is replaced in the prepublished reserve order.

The four opposition slots are allocated proportionally among occupied
non-governing seats, largest remainders and public draw for ties; they do not
require government assent. If there is no non-governing member, four further
affected-public agenda setters are drawn in advance under the same method.
Unused slots/declined choices pass to pre-drawn alternates, never the minister.
A chosen duplicate is jointly examined; the chooser may nominate another distinct
issue within that route's allocation. No source-family count is political weight.

**FA05 — Notice what was omitted.** Publish the selected questions, safe issue
references, selection/grouping reasons, common source families, sponsorship,
unexamined received issues and known coverage gaps. A novel argument excluded
for capacity is “not substantively examined,” not “refuted,” “duplicate” or proof
of consensus. Unread intake may contain unknown risks; say so. A lone unfamiliar
meaning can be consequential even without fluent advocacy or many supporters.
Correction may replace a selected issue within its route; a demonstrated legal
problem may require narrowing/deferral, not merely relabeling it low priority.

**FA06 — Competing accounts remain possible.** Participants can question original
sources, choose advocates, propose rival frames and request a different mediator
or interpretation. Protect private small discussions and publish safely agreed
reasons/dissent rather than secretly inferring consent. Shared situations,
embodied demonstrations and ongoing cooperation can generate understanding not
reducible to an argument list. Provide actual paid time, care/access support and
reflection; thirty slots do not prove any of these resources exist. Preserve
incompatible values instead of averaging them into a score or machine summary.
If the funded portfolio cannot cover the indispensable work for a lawful choice,
the authority must narrow, defer or withdraw; a filled quota does not cure that.
