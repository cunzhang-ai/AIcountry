# A real opportunity to claim, and who bears a registry mistake

*B37–B38; part of [C08/16](../16-family-agency-property-and-estates.md).*

**B37 — Civil claim periods.** B15's ordinary three years from actual or
reasonably obtainable knowledge of harm/material facts and responsible party
continues, with reasoned tolling for concealment, minority, serious threats
or an actually inaccessible competent route. Diagnosis alone neither removes
capacity nor tolls time. Failure of the responsible office's notice/handoff
cannot consume an impossible filing opportunity. Apply this proposed table:

| Claim | Candidate special period and protected boundary |
|---|---|
| Wages and support installments | Six years from each due installment; child's clock no earlier than 18. Due arrears differ from prospective reassessment; no phantom receipt |
| Bodily/latent injury | Three years from discoverable material harm/responsible party, with B15 tolling and no absolute injury-date cutoff |
| Child sexual abuse, torture or intentional killing/disappearance | No civil limitation; still prove a valid civil claim fairly. This is not a new criminal rule or automatic state liability |
| Estate/will challenge | One year after usable individual notice of the operative will/full account and reasonably knowable grounds; fraud/undisclosed rights retain ordinary discovery protection. Minority/access tolling survives |
| Recovery of land/title | Twelve years from actual discoverable adverse interference and a usable route; no automatic title transfer by occupancy, database expiry or a missing claimant |
| Judgment execution | Six years from usable final judgment, with one reasoned six-year renewal on proved remaining balance before expiry; B15 obstruction/access tolling, U discharge/exceptions and periodic support installments remain separate |

A completed wrong does not restart merely because its effects persist;
new due installments/acts have their own periods. A filed usable claim
preserves that claim during court processing; court backlog does not bar it.
No contracting out shortens protected work, child, consumer or personal-harm
rights. Reconcile shorter valid legacy limits, third-party reliance and
later protective law through competent migration, not bulk revival or
deletion by this text. Stale proof remains contestable; a long clock is
no substitute for actual evidence or unlimited public retention of testimony.

**B38 — Title restoration and official certification loss.** Default to
restoring the true holder after a void/fraudulent property transfer; a registry
edit alone never cures missing authority. Hear the holder, innocent buyer,
secured/collective/tenant interests and actual occupiers with independent
evidence. A specifically enacted money/negotiable-instrument or public-
acquisition rule may differ; no analogy creates an exception. No quiet-title
order without meaningful notice, lawful grounds and challenge, and no eviction
from an unresolved match. Provisional safe housing remains C07's own duty.

An innocent buyer first has actual seller/wrongdoer restitution and other
proved responsible-party claims. **For proved net direct reliance loss caused
by a materially incorrect official title certification, the registry authority
owes statutory compensation regardless of personal officer fault.** Prove
the certification, reasonable reliance, causal loss and actual ownership;
exclude hypothetical profits and duplicate seller/insurer recovery. A mere
unendorsed third-party upload is not an official title assurance. Private
fraud without official certification/custodian fault is not automatically
socialized. Claimant wrongdoing and B08 change-of-position issues need
separate evidence and fair allocation, not a no-digital-record penalty.

The Treasury is payer for this official operational risk through C06's
public-claims account; it may recover actual wrongdoer/insurer receipts later.
No new fully capitalized assurance fund or guaranteed transaction-fee yield
is assumed. Cost contingent exposures, independent adjudication, actual due
cash and accepted housing/claim remedies before readiness. A budget shortage
does not erase the judgment or let a clerk cut it; it requires elected
funding/continuity action with the unpaid claim visible. No judge can create
new tax, appropriate land or guarantee every market loss by record correction.

[OE27](../../C05/13/06-registry-courts-and-enforcement.md) expressly proposes a
separate prospective Treasury assurance for office-attributable materially wrong
operative certification of entity existence/identity or recorded decision authority:
proved net direct reasonable reliance loss, no personal officer fault prerequisite.
It does not extend B38 title assurance by analogy, guarantee private uploaded
fraud/value/solvency or compensate all investment loss. B defenses/no duplicate
recovery and OE31 express adopted B19 funding/execution apply; actual source is
unpriced and the public risk must be assessed before any readiness claim.

[PT01–PT24](../26-property-transfers-and-secured-credit.md) retains this true-holder
restoration and title-certification source. Its PT13 ordinary-goods exception
removes seller-created security only, not someone else's ownership. PT21
expressly adds a separate Treasury exposure for attributable security-index
search/effective-time error or wrongful publication-order failure, with actual
causation/reliance/net loss and no duplicate recovery. This is new prospective
law, not a B38 analogy, blanket private-fraud insurance or a funded reserve.
Land lodgment priority and actual completed ownership/tax dates remain distinct.
