# Design355. Fund evidence, elected scrutiny and continuing repair

[PCO11–PCO16](../../country/C14/04/05-review-major-laws-in-operation.md)
assigns a due decision to an elected body and separately prices independent
evidence, opposition drafting, protected witness access, continuing rights
and later disposition. Shared hearings can cover two compatible duties
when both receive actual deliberation and decision.

The [comparison](../reviewing-major-laws/01-a-review-that-survives-implementation-delay.md)
keeps review, remediation and staff overlap inside the same dated funds.
Unheld scrutiny is neither a saving nor a completed public choice.
Actual source, independent staffing and outcomes remain unknown.
