# C09. Product repair and material responsibility

*Design237 · LCY01–LCY12 · [C09 land and ecology](../C09-land-and-ecology.md) · Proposed research law.*

People should be able to keep using a safe product through repair,
including when the original supplier leaves. Assign the cost of safe
return and treatment before covered goods reach the market. Verify
actual service, receiving and recovery without exporting harm or
making producers the gatekeepers of independent repair.

|---|---|
