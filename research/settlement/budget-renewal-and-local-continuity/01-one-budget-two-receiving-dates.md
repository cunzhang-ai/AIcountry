# One successor budget and two different handback dates

*Design464 · Constructed first rollover under the proposed BLC dates.*

A valid annual budget is approved on lapse day 85 and takes legal
effect on day 91. The standing essential-obligations schedule and
the separately enacted limited continuation remain distinct. The
continuation authorizes named care and water work beginning on
day 91. The successor budget retains its independent legal effect.
The limited continuation ends each covered line at the earlier of independently verified funded
successor acceptance or the start of day 101. Actual authorizers and
paid routes are assumed only for the scopes shown. All amounts
are authored units, with no national cash inferred.

| Original item | Due or operating date | Amount and eligible scope |
|---|---|---|
| Water provider's prior-year work, earned before the year ended | Day 93 | 2 owed. Old annual payment permission expired. Needs a lawful successor settlement or express carry-forward. |
| Water provider's bridge work, lawfully committed and earned before bridge expiry | Day 94 | 2 owed. The separate authorized closeout can pay it through day 120. |
| Standing payment and independent election remedy | Day 92 | Restricted standing funds and an accepted independent executor cover their own scope. |
| Care work under the successor | Day 91 onward | New payer and qualified operator accept before day 91 with settled source and actual receiving. |
| Essential local water work in the later window | Days 92 and 98 | Two per call. The distinct continuation has four accessible units and an accepted interim operator. The successor's local appropriation and paid operator are ready on day 97 if the local route qualifies. |
| Optional later service cohort | Prospective | It uses none of these funds or workers by default. It needs its own approval, eligible source, qualified staff and receiving. |

A single supplier bill may describe the first two lines together.
Split by work earned and its original authority. The two units of
closeout cannot pay the prior-year invoice or new work after day 90.
Neither period creates a second debt for an already paid act.
If no successor authority covers the old invoice by day 93, report
the outstanding debt and original date. If the interim supplier
lawfully declines an unbound renewal because of that debt, verify
a separate alternate. Paying a future invoice cannot establish the
supplier's acceptance or a retroactive service.

## Compare three cutoff designs

| Hypothetical cutoff in the limited continuation | Care | Water |
|---|---|---|
| End on passage of the annual budget | The voted continuation ends before its day-91 work can begin. Existing law protects only what it actually covers before day 91. | No continuation on day 92. Later approval and budget cash do not restore missed service. |
| Keep all lines to the fixed outer end | Successor care can begin under its own authority, with explicit allocation to avoid duplicating payment or tying up both rosters. | Interim water can serve day 92 and, if needed, day 98 within the four-unit cap. |
| Apply early handback by qualified duty, with fixed outer end | Confirm accepted successor care from day 91. Release unused care authority only on its lawful terms. | Interim operator uses two by day 92. If the local handback qualifies on day 97, successor delivers day 98. The remaining two are unspent, subject to the original source law. |

Select the third design in the constructed comparison. Handback
applies to the next uncommitted service unit. A prior signed or
earned obligation stays with its lawful payer unless independently
transferred under applicable law. It protects
actual access without leaving two available authorities for the same
call. If local approval or receiving fails on day 97, use the
separately authorized second call on day 98. If it remains unresolved
on day 101, the continuation still ends. An independently due
day-102 service stays due, with an exposed delivery gap and an
urgent funded correction for the competent bodies. No assent can be
inferred from the earlier vote. Carry the authorized independent
payment and review throughout. If its rail fails, use its own
lawful alternate. If the successor fails after a valid handback, the old authority
does not revive. Any remaining missed opportunity retains its
original clock and remedy. An unused care balance or an overdue
invoice's settlement reserve cannot cure that failure by relabeling.
