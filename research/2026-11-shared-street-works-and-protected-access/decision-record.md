# Design383 decision. Plan street works around usable access

## Baseline and selected mechanism

Design382 gives separate street, utility, safety and service
responsibilities, paid continuity for confirmed journeys and
recognition of cumulative route noise. It lacks a common approval
test for interacting street works across owners. A permit can
be reasonable in isolation while its closure overlaps the only
alternative and the combined schedule defeats an existing service.

Select [SW01–SW06](../country/C09/11/01-shared-street-works-and-protected-access.md).
Use existing street authorities, joint notice and challenge, and
verified access as a planned-work condition. Prefer compatible joint
work where the whole cost and access qualify. Keep asset ownership,
technical sign-off and network independence separate. Emergency
occupation uses its own lawful powers and shortest justified scope.

## Alternatives and objections

Retain individual permits and improve notices. This costs less to
administer but leaves the risk of conflicting routes. Apply lane
charges to make disruption costly. That might improve incentives
in some places but can encourage night work, deferral or payment
in place of access. A single integrated network owner can arrange
some work more readily but can concentrate control and create
common failures. Keep these as rivals. Select joint conditions
without a new universal fee or a compulsory joint trench.

The permit authority can favor its own project, or a large utility
can use coordination to obstruct smaller rivals. Apply the same
criteria to the authority's projects and keep direct outside review.
Price paid inspectors, alternative mobility and ordinary operation.
A sole suitable route should not be treated as expendable merely
because most journeys are unaffected. Case review remains distinct
from a government promise to eliminate every inconvenience.

## Evidence, transition and next action

The [original sources](sources.md) supply a legal coordination
precedent and a limited comparative evaluation. The [authored case](../settlement/shared-street-works-and-protected-access/01-two-routes-and-three-works.md)
checks routes, cash, inspection and an alternative deadline. No
independent local review, affected participation or verified actual
operation occurred.

Adopt prospectively with competent local assignment, sector owners,
sources, safe record handling, the original worker and liability
rules and access to independent relief. Preserve prior permissions
and claims unless changed under their actual law. Any conflict
across authority borders follows existing national/local and
cross-local jurisdiction. No new property, spending or emergency
permission follows from this edition.

Next test one real two-route area from original permits, bookings,
local schedules and qualified accessible journeys. Compare safe
phasing, a shared window and independent works. Include a real
late closure and an original emergency route without counting
the same inspector or accessible vehicle twice. Reopen on displaced
harm, missed access, deferred maintenance or stronger alternatives.
