# RR11–RR17. Protect democratic functions across separate measures

*Design379 · Proposed charter safeguards and implementing duties under [democratic repair](../08-democratic-repair-without-self-authorization.md).*

**RR11 — Protect the actual democratic function.** Review a public
measure in combination with other operative measures and sufficiently
specified changes proposed to operate with it when their effects
materially depend on the same authority, information, finance, staff,
review or execution path. Measures include statutes, appropriations,
delegated rules, contracts, administrative actions and proved omissions
of legal duties. An effects claim identifies a protected function,
affected public and actual opportunity, a concrete causal connection
and an injury or imminent substantial risk. Require no common
sponsor or proof of improper motive. Consider election participation,
opposition, independent review, enforcement and lawful handover.
Ordinary policy disagreement, an abstract loss of convenience or
merely sharing an agency does not establish impairment.

**RR12 — Make the public sponsor show how the function survives.** For
a material change to the operation of a protected path, before final
adoption give the Assembly and affected public a joined statement
of controlling measures, necessary steps, holders of authority and
records, paid staff, alternatives, deadlines and downstream execution.
Show what remains usable under the change and a reasonably evidenced
shared failure. Disclose unresolved dependencies and affected access.
Give independent reviewers protected original evidence and opposition
a usable chance to challenge. The review duty follows the effect across
instruments and legislative sessions. Treat an unknown fallback as
unverified. Give reasons for any immediate existing-law safety act and
preserve review. Avoid repeating analysis for an unchanged dependency
whose continued validity has been checked.

**RR13 — Give a direct, bounded challenge.** A person whose protected
opportunity is materially threatened, an affected eligible contestant,
a recognized opposition group or the legally responsible independent
institution can seek review without first exhausting the challenged
chain. Identify the measure and threatened link as far as reasonably
possible. Once a credible specific risk is shown, the authority must
produce facts on the links it controls and justify its change against
an effective less impairing lawful alternative. Use existing evidence,
safe disclosure, recusal and review standards. A missing report alone
does not void an enactment. Nonproduction of evidence within the
authority's control is reviewable and cannot substantiate a claimed
alternate. Protected actual deadlines govern scheduling. There is no
automatic stay, default merits ruling or new certification veto.

**RR14 — Keep adjudication on prior law and existing forums.** Use the
competent predecessor-law forum for amendment timing. After lawful
commencement, use CJ for general constitutional relief, CJ12 for its
closed own-court subjects and ordinary courts for existing individual
and urgent remedies. A wider joined factual record supplies no wider
jurisdiction. Use existing timely case coordination where claims span
forums. The challenged change cannot choose its reviewer, raise the
quorum or lengthen the clock governing review of its own validity.
Use a timely lawful unconflicted reserve only under prior rules.
Loss of both competent paths remains a reported loss of effective
control. It neither validates the change nor empowers another actor
to invent an adjudicator or confer a sovereign role on AI.

**RR15 — Preserve the smallest workable independent route.** Under
existing lawful interim powers, stop or suspend only the acts needed
to avoid imminent loss while hearing the case. An urgent order needs
specific reasons, defined scope and expiry and timely adversarial
review. At merits, identify the causal measures, reject unnecessary
impairment and apply only remedies within the forum's jurisdiction.
Preserve separable lawful changes, accrued claims and ordinary
services. A reviewer can restore existing authority, compel existing
duties or invalidate an unlawful restriction within its powers.
It cannot vote a tax, create an appropriation, install a reviewer or
replace a lawful election with its preferred result. Ordinary
policy and financing alternatives return to the elected Assembly.
Material changes to constitutionally protected structure must
follow A9 and the applicable RR succession rules. Genuine
routine improvements can proceed within ordinary authority.

**RR16 — Verify continued public control by actual delivery.** Name
the operative order, independent holder of evidence, lawful payer,
executor, recipient and useful deadline. Verify timely receipt
through an actor outside the challenged controller where that
controller could block execution. Preserve original votes, claims,
notices and due dates. Record and disclose gaps safely when staffing,
money or independence fails. A lawful ruling delivered too late
does not recover a lost contest. Existing correction, repeat or
handover rules decide further action on their own proof. No
office gains an indefinite term or emergency budget because an
independent remedy failed.

**RR17 — Permit repair and test the safeguard itself.** Apply the
same standard to measures of judges, election officials, opposition
bodies and purported protectors within their legal powers. An
institution need not retain its existing supplier, staffing model
or discretionary spending when a qualified independent alternative
works in time. Compare narrow safeguards in each Act and a
broader independent preclearance model. Select joined effects
review with case-specific relief. Price ordinary intake and real
execution before reliance. AI may trace dependencies and compare
versions against original records. It cannot decide materiality,
standing, impairment, legality or authorization. Lawful adoption
assigns prospective operation and review for ongoing effects,
preserving final judgments and accrued claims under existing law.
Reopen on obstruction through speculative litigation, unsupported
delay, strategic disaggregation or a better less burdensome route.
