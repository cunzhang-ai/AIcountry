# Resolve an initiative and a competing constitutional proposal

*Design334 · CRV06–CRV10 · Proposed prospective addition to A9.*

**CRV06 — Protect the original public vote and qualify a real alternative.**
A valid direct initiative keeps its exact text and original timetable.
The Assembly can submit one direct counterproposal for paired treatment
only if its exact alternative independently meets the Assembly route,
is lawful on its own and cannot operate together with the initiative
on the specified constitutional choice. The competent predecessor-law
forum decides disputed incompatibility and scope in useful time, with
independent challenge and reasons. Apply CRV01–CRV05 to unrelated
clauses and each authorization. A recommendation, ordinary statute,
forecast conflict or late proposal has no effect on the initiative's
validity or timetable. This route does not decide collisions among
other kinds of proposals.

**CRV07 — Freeze the two texts before the public consideration period.**
Set the paired close at the original lawful initiative close. This is
an express exception to the counterproposal's standalone close, not
a change to the initiative's date. Pair only if the two exact texts,
the comparative question, all material legal findings and accessible
balanced information can be public at least 90 calendar days before
the first vote. Give each side and opponents of both actual campaign
opportunity. Publish the finite conflict and repeat rules before
voting. If pairing misses the deadline or fails, proceed on the
initiative's original timetable. Any valid Assembly text follows its
ordinary separate route with applicable law and dates; it cannot
obtain a paired place by executive direction or later ballot editing.

**CRV08 — Ask for approval twice and for a preference once.** The elector
may vote yes or no separately on each exact text, and may indicate
which should take effect if both pass. Every otherwise eligible
elector may answer the preference question regardless of their two
approval answers. Count the three questions separately. A blank,
invalid or missing response to one does not spoil a valid answer to
another. Apply the direct initiative's three-fifths and the Assembly
proposal's majority of valid yes/no votes after their respective
authorization. If only one passes, its own lawful effective date
controls. If neither passes, predecessor law continues. If both pass,
the one with more valid preference votes prevails within this pair.
If a verified preference count is exactly tied or contains no valid
preference votes, the initiative prevails. No preference vote can
supply missing approval. The losing approved alternative has no
operative effect from this vote.

**CRV09 — Correct actual defects without offering a veto by delay.**
Preserve original records and independently verified unaffected
results. A material defect in qualification, voting or count goes to
the competent independent court under existing procedural protections.
The court first seeks exact lawful correction and narrower remedies.
An unascertainable preference is not a verified tie. Where a final
judgment annuls only a decisive preference count and both approval
results are lawfully severable and final, use an expressly authorized
preference-only repeat with the exact same alternatives. The proposed
close is 120 days after final judgment, with the prior-law voting
interval and funded publication and review. This repeat timing is a
narrow exception to the original certification timetable. No disputed
alternative takes effect pending the required valid preference result.
Apply only the operative date or delayed fallback authorized in advance.
A missed date supplies no retroactive effect. A defect in an approval question uses its own lawful result and repeat
route. No ordinary petition automatically stays a proved result.

**CRV10 — Keep authority, implementation and access separate.** Use the
predecessor electorate and all applicable A9 and RR conditions for each
original or repeated act. No preference result overrides rights review,
territorial consent or protected succession. Fund the separate ballot,
original custody, review and any actual repeat. AI can check version
and tally arithmetic under human oversight. It cannot choose pairing,
interpret a missing vote or certify commencement. Adopt prospectively
under valid predecessor authority and preserve pending proposals and
contests under their own law. Reopen on manipulation of timing, weak
comprehension, unequal campaign access or a better lawful mechanism.

The [matched voting case](../../../settlement/constitutional-counterproposals/01-two-approvals-and-a-preference.md)
tests the result, the deadlines and a lost preference count. Actual
adoption, voter access and lawful execution remain open.
