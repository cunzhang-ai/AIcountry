# Keep essential services replaceable

*Design498 · National operating choice and constructed market comparison.*

**Keep plural production and establish a paid alternative where a single controller can stop an essential service.** Start with enforceable switching, transparent ownership and portable records. Compare open access to shared infrastructure, an independent purchase or mutual arrangement, and ordinary public operation. For a locally delivered duty, the elected local council chooses and funds the delivery route under national rights and equalization rules. A public route has the same duties of continuity, fair access and independent review. At an indivisible physical facility, preserve independently enforceable access terms and the ability to replace the operator. Treat loss of the physical facility as a separate continuity test. A common accountant without control over service or remedy does not make two operators dependent.

Avoid a mandate that reserves every market to a public operator. The body running a service cannot set the conditions of entry for a rival, control the record used against itself or investigate its own takeover as a competition authority. Keep the operator, funding decision and complaint authority separately usable, including if they share public ownership. This applies when a national funding body also offers to operate the service. Attribute any public provider compensation to its authorized service and maintenance, with unrelated commercial losses on their own account. A transfer follows the eligible duty and actual cost. The elected locality may choose any lawful qualified route, with published reasons and independent appeal.

## Review the last usable provider before control changes

The series gate can miss the last route in a thin market. In a variant of the three-deal case, overlapping targets of 180T, 170T and 140T total 490T. With the combined group threshold met and no other gate, the last deal escapes mandatory series filing even though it would leave a single provider. Use the evidence-based MP16 route where it qualifies. Relying only on case-specific discovery can leave control already transferred before a timely hearing. Requiring all small acquisitions to file would burden useful exits and ordinary investment. Select a prospective statutory extension of the existing MP14 gateway for already legally due home support only. The usual evidence of at most two usable routes over the preceding twelve months and no funded alternative within thirty days still applies. Designation needs source evidence and review, and must be effective before the deal's first binding event. Preserve the existing ninety-day notice interval, finite decision clocks and bounded relief.

Designation assesses effective control, including control of indispensable access to delivering the covered service where the evidence supports it. A contract dependency without a transfer of effective control remains a continuity or exclusion question under its own law. When an operative provider changes control or a critical subcontract, recheck the actual contractually required alternate. Review the deal, a likely failed sale or closure and any accepting independent successor on the same evidence. If a qualified reserve or a failing seller changes the likely outcome, a valid designation still opens review, while the merits may support clearance. A contested block or forced transfer stays with the competent court. The service owner carries live protection under its own authority throughout. ## How ownership changes the answer on the same obligations

Use an authored home-support case after the relevant control transfers were lawfully completed. Ownership review is a separate counterfactual for any next deal. A region owes two calls after ordinary work, each with two high-dependency visits. Ordinary entitlements, other providers' dues and independent complaints stay funded outside the incremental comparison. Scope includes one shared dispatch or records failure and one simultaneous origin-area call. The local budget has 2 startup units and 3 recurring units per cycle. Each qualified slot includes consent, travel, pay, protected rest and independently usable records.

| Exact motion | Startup / recurring | First and following call |
|---|---:|---|
| Renew current brands with a shared broker | 0 / 1 | One usable slot after the first failure. The second branded backup shares its dispatch and contributes zero. An expired first emergency order cannot supply an ordinary second call. |
| Ordinary local standby plus a worker cooperative with independent dispatch | 1 / 2 | Two slots if the cooperative can accept without displacing a due visit at origin. A simultaneous origin-area call removes that slot unless separately staffed. |
| Limited directly staffed local service with independent oversight | 2 / 3 | Two slots in each window if accepted workers, lawful records, funds and distinct oversight survive the stress. The service is limited to this protected scope. Other eligible routes remain usable. |

With a proven independent cooperative slot in both windows, use the second motion. If origin conflict is persistent and no other qualified alternate can fit, select the third on the authored capacity and cost assumptions. If startup falls to 1, or staff cannot lawfully and voluntarily accept by the deadline, public provision cannot start as claimed. Preserve already due work, fund whatever independent lawful correction fits and record the specific unserved claim. If private switching plus a repaired scheduler meets the same conditions at lower lifetime cost, keep it. A temporary backstop cannot cover indefinite procurement delay.

If the forecast later drops to 2 recurring units after the pilot, a temporary grant or conditional new vote supplies no durable third unit. Where legally available, use capped timely relief against the affected duty, with named source and actual settlement. The Assembly considers a general recurring equalization correction or lawful service redesign with a funded successor. Use optional eligible startup support only for its named purpose. A local decision to reject optional restricted aid keeps the original statutory funding, rights and ordinary powers. When a protected sending call causes material spillovers beyond council or compact capacity, refer the specific allocation through the lawful elected route. The service choice remains local within its actual competence. If no lawful source qualifies, disclose the gap and seek the existing remedy. An operator's exit or acquisition is not itself a finding that local democracy has failed.

## Test ordinary replacement as hard as the original takeover

Every supported ownership change or contested remedy has to identify a funded receiving owner, staff, independent access to records, applicable worker and resident choices, actual delivery and the next funded call. Compare promised volume with completed work, missed people, worker mobility or wage effects and harms in the supplying locality. Use the same out-of-hours request and one independent complaint against each arrangement. Test whether residents can still receive care or a binding correction when the incumbent or public fallback fails. A paper transfer of control earns no credit until the receiving route works.

The 2022 UK Competition and Markets Authority study found an inadequate supply of suitable children's placements and highlighted possible disorderly exit by heavily indebted large providers. This is a real analogue for matching financial resilience to suitable receiving. It cannot pick an ownership form for adult home care. The FTC's 2025 Welsh Carson consent order supplies a separate example of targeted prospective acquisition notification in healthcare. A settlement resolving agency allegations is different evidence from an adjudicated market finding. Neither source supplies local prices, thresholds, authority or a roster for AIcountry.
