## Decision and diagnosis

The requested work is further country design. A bounded A1–A15 comparison found
A15/C04/C14 still promise an objective territorial route while deferring actual
thresholds, electorate and final consent. This is a missing research design,
distinct from legitimately unknown actual geography, public authority and source
law. An executive could exploit undefined initiation to avoid even a hearing.
A4 ordinary receipt/standing-source wording and A10 generic P-date also needed
explicit alignment with AP and RR06. The review does not revalidate every law.

Values: equal political standing, dissent, shared national obligations, minority/
collective rights, usable remedies and peaceful change. Selected numeric gates,
local standing, times and two-public approval are design hypotheses, not original
empirical observations or optimal/legal rules derived from foreign examples.

Select TS01–TS16 as the candidate charter/framework route. Reject a second first
permission ballot and compulsory mediator: neither adds final authority; each can
add delay or capture. No new permanent office. Existing EA gets limited express
jurisdiction only through valid prospective law, with fixed actual votes. Ordinary
courts enforce process/rights without choosing map or political outcome.

## Continuity, control and objections

Trace receivers/EA/map evidence → Assembly mandate/Cabinet talks → independent
rights-holder/opposition voices → separate public campaigns/results → lawful
courts and OP/T execution. No actor controls the whole chain. Existing appointment,
removal, information, funding and execution protections remain necessary and can
still fail together; same-controller reviewers are not independent. Missing paid
receivers/resources remain unknown, not presumed supplied by a new deadline.

Retain serious objections: national majorities may persistently frustrate justified
regional wishes; local-home gates may exclude affected diaspora/service users;
whole-municipality maps can preserve old injustice; fixed 5%/times can be onerous
or manipulable. Hearing other affected groups does not pretend to give them area
votes. Existing collective/international sources retain their separate force.
Reopen on qualified exclusion/obstruction/capacity evidence or a justified rival,
not by changing rules after a live claim reveals likely results. No score averages
away competing public authority or protected rights.

A request controls protected receipt; D fixes area support independently of clerk
delay; K starts bounded negotiation and only the full Assembly may grant one
bounded extension. C alone starts final-text vote calendar. Refusal, disagreement,
disputed authority and shortage remain distinct. Silence supplies neither consent
nor denial, and no failed fallback invents a sovereign. Paid current services,
workers, debt, claims and ecological duties persist with named accepting successors.
Material new terms need new support/consents; ordinary election/RR succession,
actual source law/software/permissions and earlier claims remain unchanged.
