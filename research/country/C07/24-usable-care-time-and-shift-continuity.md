# C07. Early care that fits the child's and family's real day

*Design199 · CW01–CW08 · Proposed under-six implementation of S10, NC and FC.*

**Offer a safe care window that covers the actual need and the journey.
Preserve the child's ordinary relationships while the timing changes.**
The local care authority owns the usable offer, the provider owns safe
delivery, and the national authority funds and provides the existing FC
fallback. Parents may choose an available lawful alternative, including
care at home. An employer's roster neither determines eligibility nor
creates authority over a child.

addition specifies organized early care. School-age coverage retains
its own applicable standards and needs a distinct costing.
