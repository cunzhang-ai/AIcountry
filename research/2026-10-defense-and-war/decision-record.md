The bounded decision is **who may order, continue, constrain and account for
defensive force and intelligence when an attack is real, disputed or used as
a pretext during a political handover**. C10 already divides police,
intelligence and external defense and requires Assembly mandates for sustained
force abroad. It does not distinguish standing defense from an enlarged
campaign, set the authorization clock, assign classified scrutiny or protect
against a commander, vendor or model controlling weapons and records. A
phrase such as “civilian control” could describe both a replaceable democratic
government and an executive able to wage indefinite war while declaring
opponents security threats.

**Values:** life and territorial safety, peace, lawful international relations,
equal political standing, service-member rights, civilian protection,
independent truth and the ability to replace government during a conflict.
**Constraints:** applicable domestic and international law, existing treaty
duties, finite personnel, logistics and cash, operational secrecy where
necessary, real-time self-defense, and no military right to settle elections.
**Unknown empirical claims:** the actual threat, allied commitments, feasible
force size, command reliability, whether the reference clocks are workable,
and whether independent oversight has people and secure evidence access.
**Design hypothesis:** a narrow standing defensive mandate plus elected
authorization of enlarged hostilities and divided review can protect people
without making a cabinet or general the state's alternate legislature. It
can fail if the Assembly cannot convene, executive and military share all
information, or a supposedly independent reviewer is captured.

## Competing packages and diagnosis

| Package | Mechanism and attraction | Failure or cost |
|---|---|---|
| Keep C10's broad civilian-control wording | Avoids arbitrary war clocks and permits context-specific future law. | Cabinet can repeatedly label an expanding campaign “immediate defense”; the Assembly's mandate, budget and review can arrive only after facts become irreversible. |
| Executive strategic command with a general emergency declaration | One political center can react quickly and preserve operational secrecy. | The same declaration can fund prolonged foreign force, mobilize civilians, censor opposition and delay elections. Cabinet can hide military losses or use intelligence against lawful rivals. |
| Assembly approval of every use of force or target | Maximizes visible elected control over weapons. | A legislative chamber cannot make timely tactical judgments, may leak sensitive operations, and can take responsibility away from accountable commanders. Deliberation delay may leave people exposed. |
| Small force, primarily diplomatic/civil resilience and external guarantees | May reduce militarization, cost and the temptation to govern through a security apparatus. | A partner may not defend the country; a wholly unstaffed territorial defense cannot be created during attack. Diplomacy and civilian resilience remain essential even if a defensive force exists. |
| **Selected: standing territorial defense, bounded external force, civilian policy and professional command** | The cabinet can repel attack under prior law; the Assembly authorizes sustained or enlarged operations, reserves, money and alliances; professional command executes within law; independent review and evidence custody survive handover. | More interfaces, time and money; renewal can politicize operations or expose secrets. A cabinet can misdescribe facts, an Assembly majority can approve abuse, and a security committee can be captured. The clocks and force structure need real threat testing. |

This is a choice of **authority architecture**, not the size or ownership of an
actual force. No territory, hostile actor or ratifying public has been
identified. If a real adopting community can obtain credible security with a
smaller force, alliance or demilitarized arrangement, this working choice may
be displaced. AI forecasts cannot settle the value tradeoff between risk,
liberty, public spending and lives abroad.

## Selected authority and protection chain

1. **Separate legality and domestic mandate.** The [UN Charter](https://www.un.org/en/node/125814)
   is a baseline for peaceful settlement and the prohibition on unlawful
   interstate force; an Assembly vote cannot by itself make an external use
   lawful. A founding public must identify applicable treaties and lawful
   self-defense obligations. Domestic law may preauthorize immediate actions
   necessary to repel an actual armed attack on the country's territory and
   protect its forces. This standing power does not authorize conquest, a
   permanent foreign campaign or domestic suppression. The cabinet records
   the trigger, legal basis, orders, geographic scope, civilian risk and
   resources and notifies the Assembly's protected security channel within
   24 hours. That proposed clock is an accountability hypothesis, not an
   international-law deadline. For a UN member invoking [Charter Article 51](https://www.un.org/en/node/125430),
   the Charter's Security Council reporting duty is a distinct external
   obligation; a domestic Assembly notice does not satisfy it by itself.
2. **Make escalation a new decision.** A cross-border action urgently needed
   to stop an ongoing attack has its own legal assessment and notice. In this
   reference it cannot be turned into a sustained foreign campaign beyond
   seven days without an affirmative Assembly mandate; repeated small actions
   pursuing one campaign count cumulatively. The Assembly must be able to
   convene at an alternate protected site or lawful remote channel, inspect
   a classified annex and vote a public mandate stating purpose, theater,
   categories of force, maximum duration and fiscal exposure, civilian and
   humanitarian duties, reporting, review and termination. The reference
   mandate expires within 90 days unless renewed by an affirmative vote.
   An expired mandate allows safe withdrawal and immediate repulsion of a
   new attack under standing law, not an executive extension of the campaign.
   These numbers are provisional choices, not proof of strategic adequacy.
   Silence, a withheld report or inability to assemble is not consent.
3. **Keep policy, command and adjudication distinct.** The elected cabinet
   proposes lawful objectives and the defense minister issues a recorded
   mission order within the mandate. A qualified professional commander
   directs operations, logistics and troop safety under law and may be
   replaced for lawful reasons with a preserved chain of orders. The
   military serves constitutional offices, not a person, party or its own
   corporate interest; it cannot certify elections, veto a lawful civilian
   change of government or invent an emergency cabinet. Service members
   receive training, legal advice, safe reporting and fair discipline.
   A superior order does not automatically excuse a manifestly unlawful act;
   exact liability belongs in enacted law. Refusal and reporting need
   a protected route, but a commander cannot invoke conscience as a general
   policy veto. Civilian courts decide legality and crimes; ordinary tactical
   decisions are not assigned to judges or legislators.
4. **Review secret power without making secrecy a veto.** Intelligence has
   specific statutory purposes, collection thresholds, retention and
   dissemination limits. Domestic intrusive surveillance generally needs
   prior independent judicial authorization, with narrowly enacted urgent
   preservation and prompt review when waiting would defeat protection.
   Foreign collection, cyber operations and shared data need distinct lawful
   mandates and review; changing the server or calling an action “cyber”
   does not erase its real effects or domestic rights. A proportionally
   constituted Assembly security committee including opposition members
   has protected access to mission, spending and intelligence legality,
   supported by independent legal and technical staff. It can report
   redacted findings and challenge unjustified withholding before a court;
   it cannot itself authorize war, reveal protected sources or direct a
   named operation. Judges, auditors and outside investigators retain
   different evidence and remedy functions. When secrecy ceases to be
   justified, an affected person has a lawful route to notice and remedy.
5. **Protect civilians and investigate violations.** Mission rules and
   training incorporate applicable humanitarian law, including distinction,
   proportionality, feasible precautions, humane detention and protection of
   medical and other civilian services. A new weapon or material software
   change receives a documented legal review before fielding; the review is
   revisited when the mission or environment changes. Death, torture,
   disappearance, serious unlawful attack or evidence suppression by forces
   triggers C08's outside-command preservation, independent investigation
   and prosecutor path. Civilian crimes and serious humanitarian violations
   reach independent civilian courts under enacted jurisdiction and fair
   trial rules; service discipline handles bounded service breaches and
   cannot erase a criminal case. Responsibility of a superior requires
   proven knowledge or legally defined duty and feasible power to prevent
   or repress, not rank alone. Harmed people need care, safe contact and
   executable civil remedies without waiting for conviction.
6. **Limit AI and vendors in force.** AI can help with logistics, translation,
   threat analysis and lawful decision support, but a prediction or target
   profile is not a legal determination of combatant status or permission
   to attack. This reference prohibits autonomous selection and engagement
   of persons without a human decision on the particular target. A narrowly
   bounded defensive interception of clearly specified weapon objects can
   be separately authorized only after legal review of the system and use
   context, demonstrated reliability, confined area/time/target class,
   named human responsibility, feasible stop controls and preserved logs.
   An unpredictable or unreviewable system is not deployed. A software
   update, contractor or classified procurement cannot expand authority
   silently; technical and operational reviewers must be able to contest
   the provider's claims.
7. **Account for people and resources.** The reference uses a professional
   volunteer force and trained voluntary reserves. Any compulsory service
   requires a separate prospective statute, Assembly vote, clear necessity,
   finite duration, fair selection, pay, care and employment protections,
   civilian review, and nonpunitive alternative service for conscientious
   objectors. Family care or disability cannot become an unrecorded manpower
   source. Defense planning funds readiness, maintenance, medical care,
   veterans' obligations, independent investigation, cybersecurity and
   actual replacement supplies, not just equipment purchases. Classified
   procurement has narrow documented exceptions and protected independent
   audit under C04. The C06 account shows its draw on clinicians, fuel,
   imports, foreign currency, power and civilian shelters; neither a war
   order nor an AI logistics estimate creates those resources. Treaty,
   creditor, worker, pension and affected civilian claims continue across
   mobilization and after demobilization.

The [OSCE Code of Conduct](https://www.osce.org/fsc/41355) is a primary
comparison for democratic control, political neutrality and service-member
rights, not a claim that AIcountry is an OSCE participant. The
[Additional Protocol I text](https://ihl-databases.icrc.org/assets/treaties/470-AP-I-EN.pdf)
informs the review of new weapons (Article 36), distinction (Article 48),
precautions (Article 57) and a superior's failure to act (Article 86);
actual treaty participation and applicable customary law must be checked
for a real jurisdiction. The [ICRC's autonomous-weapons position](https://www.icrc.org/en/document/icrc-position-autonomous-weapon-systems)
is an institutional recommendation about human control, not an enacted ban.
The [OHCHR account of conscientious objection](https://www.ohchr.org/Documents/Publications/ConscientiousObjection_en.pdf)
informs the rights question; domestic scope and remedies require legitimate
law. None of these sources supplies the reference's 24-hour, seven-day or
90-day clocks, an actual force, an alliance or evidence that this package
will protect a real public.

## Delay, transition and remaining objections

If an Assembly cannot convene during attack, the pre-enacted territorial
defense mandate and narrowly necessary self-defense continue under law;
the cabinet cannot use absence to widen a foreign campaign, suspend the
franchise or renew its term. The predesignated alternate meeting method,
published session trigger, authenticated votes, protected original records
and temporary classified route must be arranged before crisis. A court can
review legality and protect records but cannot substitute itself for an
Assembly vote. If all lawful elected and judicial routes fail, this is a
legitimacy breakdown, not an AI or military succession rule. Difficulty
convening cannot retroactively legalize a forbidden target or erase a
service member's or civilian's remedy.

The old security manuscript's Article 5 AI-controlled 30-day emergency
scheme remains an unreconciled predecessor. Design 23 develops C10 and
proposed A8 without enacting either; D9's constitutional emergency clock
is **separate** from this war-mandate clock. Armed conflict does not
automatically suspend ordinary elections, courts, labor rights, social
claims or independent abuse investigation. Existing service orders,
contracts, veterans' duties, evidence, treaty commitments and pending
cases need named successors before any force reorganization. During a
founding or cabinet transition, both commanders and procurement vendors
must hand over armories, authorizations, credentials, sealed records and
payment authority to lawful successor offices without exposing secrets to
unentitled people. An automatic purge may destroy protection; automatic
inheritance of an abusive command may destroy accountability.

Serious objections remain. A seven-day external limit may constrain a
necessary defense; a parliamentary majority may rubber-stamp an unlawful
war; an opposition committee may be starved of evidence or leak it;
classification may hide waste or civilian harm; a legal duty to refuse an
unlawful order can expose a junior member to retaliation; compulsory service
can load burden on poor families; a narrow autonomous-interception exception
can expand by changing labels. The selected package uses bounded authority,
independent source custody, public reasons, protected classified scrutiny,
fair process and tested alternatives, but no paper clause proves those
institutions will resist capture or improve security. A smaller-force or
different authorization model remains open if original threat, rights,
resource and affected-party evidence supports it.

**Evidence qualification:** conceptual use of the primary texts above and
local predecessor documents; no real threat assessment, force inventory,
budget, neighboring-state response or affected-party testimony. **Research
selection:** bounded standing defense plus Assembly authorization for
expanded/sustained force, divided command and review, rights-protecting
intelligence, independent abuse route and constrained AI weapons.
**Legal authorization:** none. **Operational readiness:** no staffed force,
trained reserves, Assembly alternate session, classified reviewer,
investigator, weapon-review body, payment capacity or treaty position
demonstrated. **Observed outcomes:** none; F8 is an authored stress case.

The next bounded adopting-public test is an **armed attack during a disputed
election and payment outage**. Use real current law, orders, armory and
credential custody, force readiness, civilian shelter and import needs to
trace the first day, day seven and day 90: who can defend, who can see
evidence, who may enlarge the mission, what happens if the Assembly lacks a
quorum, and how a lawful successor receives command. Compare the selected
route with a more executive mandate and a smaller-force/ally-dependent
route. The deliverable is a timed authority, resource, civilian-harm and
remedy account with testimony and dissent from residents, troops,
opposition, lawyers and affected outsiders. This project has no jurisdiction
or field budget. Reopen design 23 if actual threat, response delay,
international obligation, civilian harm, capture, AI reliability or a
credible alternative changes the choice. If the evidence cannot be
obtained, do not claim readiness or superior protection.
