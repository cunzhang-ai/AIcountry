# SC09–SC12: prevent an unlawful award without abandoning people

**SC09 — Give a real precontract opportunity.** Material awards and material
new framework entries require an intended-award notice with usable reasons to
all bidders and public notice for excluded would-be entrants. No contract enters
force until **ten full working days after usable notice**: exclude the notice
day, end at the close of the tenth working day, and permit entry only afterward
and after any applicable court hold is lifted. Defective notice or blocked
meaningful access does not start the affected person's clock; publish corrected
notice and preserve no-fault late challenge. A lawful previously competed
framework call within unchanged terms needs no second full standstill, but
unlawful allocation remains challengeable. A buyer cannot award first and make
every remedy a damages claim. Nonmaterial buying retains prompt direct review
and protective relief, without a universal precontract waiting period.

**SC10 — Use independent ordinary courts, not a buyer-controlled tribunal.**
A bidder or unlawfully excluded potential entrant with a plausible affected
interest can apply directly to the competent ordinary civil court under C08/OJ;
internal reconsideration is optional and cannot consume the outside clock.
Known specification wrongs should be raised before closing; award applications
use the ten-working-day standstill. A hidden wrong or inaccessible lawful route
allows application within ten working days of reasonably usable discovery/access,
with no-fault late relief and existing C08 claims preserved. The court decides
timeliness and good cause; a buyer cannot certify its own notice as conclusive.
Residents/workers keep their distinct service, equality, labor and public-wrong
routes without pretending they submitted a bid. A brief account/receipt can
initiate assistance and protection; no complete technical dossier or security
deposit is the price of access. An authorized receiver preserves the original
date and promptly gives protected notice to the buyer; its routing failure
cannot reward a buyer who knows a timely court challenge was lodged.

A timely court application identifying a plausible precontract wrong holds the main award pending the
court's interim decision, not until the buyer declares itself satisfied. Courts
alone screen the claimed wrong; the buyer cannot refuse notice as “implausible.”
must give initial protective attention within **two working days and sooner
where harm requires**, hearing other parties as feasible. This is a duty to
act, not a promise that every dispute is resolved then. No assigned competent
judge means unmet protection, not deemed approval. OJ ordinary assignment and
outside-status conflict rules, aid, interpreters, paid clerks/reserves and actual
execution keep their sources; procurement creates no new judges by name.

**SC11 — Match the remedy to the wrong.** Courts may preserve evidence, suspend
or permit the award with conditions, correct unlawful criteria/evaluation,
require a fresh competition, declare an unlawfully concluded contract ineffective
prospectively, and order proved civil loss/payment under C08. Protect good-faith
third parties and paid safe service succession; automatic lost-profit awards
or public guarantees do not follow. Seek a merits disposition within **20
working days**; a complex case needs reasons and a public-safe case plan, not
lost rights. Review continuing interim restrictions at least every ten working
days. Only a competent court may lift its hold, balancing credible illegality,
irreversibility, bidder/user harm and a funded alternative. Buyer delay, mere
accusation or replicated complaints confer neither consent nor automatic guilt.

**SC12 — Keep essential continuity separate from the disputed main contract.**
Where delay risks concrete imminent serious service harm, the existing responsible
sector executive may authorize the strictly necessary temporary purchase within
existing lawful purpose/appropriation, capped at **30 calendar days** and a
published gross amount. Document actual stock, alternatives, price, qualified
receiving, source and why ordinary time fails. Notify the court immediately if
a hold exists; this provision never overrides that court's order. An urgent
court may permit a bounded continuity arrangement without validating the main
award. Publish within two working days with narrowly protected annexes; missing
publication is a breach, not retrospective erasure of a user's received service.
Same-need renewals cannot restart the 30-day aggregate window. More temporary
authority needs an express competent elected decision with a new finite ceiling,
sources, alternatives and review; no executive self-extension or election delay.

[SC33–SC37](09-essential-service-exit-and-recovery.md) narrows a
permitted bridge to the residual service, prices independently
accepted transfer and separates actual recovery from funding.
The aggregate SC12 limit and the court's order still govern.
