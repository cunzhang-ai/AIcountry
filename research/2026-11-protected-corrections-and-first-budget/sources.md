# Evidence for protected corrections and first-budget choices

- The [UK National Audit Office's 2025 local government financial
  sustainability report](https://www.nao.org.uk/reports/local-government-financial-sustainability-2025/)
  finds that real funding growth did not keep pace with population,
  demand, complexity or delivery cost. It identifies pressure on
  social care and homelessness services and distinguishes immediate
  fiscal support from structural repair. The observation motivates
  a protected-baseline stress. It does not price the proposed case.
- The [matched first programme](../settlement/integrated-first-programme/01-shared-sources-and-votes.md)
  and [release register](../settlement/integrated-first-programme/04-first-release-register.md)
  provide the unchanged constructed input prices and earlier scope.
  The new protected correction and extra review reservation are
  authored. No observed cost is treated as their source.
- [Usable food](../2026-11-usable-food-and-local-meal-continuity/sources.md)
  and [voluntary care](../2026-11-voluntary-care-and-paid-continuity/sources.md)
  motivate separate first and next receipts and paid replacements.
  The previous evidence does not show that the two losses occur
  together or that the assumed correction can actually be bought.
  Their scenario amounts are not summed into the new budget.

A material dependency can be described without establishing its
frequency, a qualified alternative or a legal change. Actual prices,
newly accepted resources, enactment and outcomes remain open.
