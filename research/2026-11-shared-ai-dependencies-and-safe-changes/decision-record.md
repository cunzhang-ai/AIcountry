# Design276 decision record

**Baseline and question.** DP05–DP08 already requires actual
dependencies, DP25–DP32 prices supplier exit and LX separates
a release from legal authority and correction. Specify who can
change and restore shared dependencies and what happens to
already distributed outputs. Use the
[working method](../FRAMEWORK.md) and [decision handoff](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

**Evidence and diagnosis.** A routine shared upstream configuration
release affected distinct public applications. NHS England reported
technical recovery while warning about appointments needing rebooking.
A missing operator, an unsafe release, an unqualified standby and
a persistent cached effect are different failures. The case supplies
no causal evidence for the proposed deployment control.
[Sources and limits](sources.md)

**Alternatives and selection.** Compare simultaneous managed
updates, separately controlled release and recovery with a paid
alternate, and a competent ordinary service with fewer common
technical dependencies. Choose the independently recoverable
route in the authored comparison. One shared reserve cannot
cover the simultaneous exposure. Retain simple ordinary service
as a strong rival. A single rapid security update may shorten
exposure to an active vulnerability. Separate the cost of secure
staging, training, retained ordinary access and actual restoring
work. A dangerous old version cannot protect continuity.
[Authored comparison](../settlement/shared-ai-change/02-authored-release-and-queued-effects.md)

**Control and objections.** Providers disclose changes and bounded
proof of control through lawful contracts. Public duty owners decide
reliance within existing authority. Independent sector review,
Assembly sources, opposition, audit access and case remedies remain
separate. A change coordinator could use dependency information to
create an unofficial national command. Limit its view and decision
power to the actual support mandate. Technical custody cannot
qualify evidence outside its purpose. Preserve the earlier case,
permission and independent review on exit. No law or safety
procedure adopts itself by changing the configuration.

**Next bounded decision.** Exercise two services with the same
updater and recovery login, a disconnection and a previously
distributed suggestion. Require one independent staffed recovery
path, preserved case dates and no replay of valid past acts.
Set the authorized test duration and resource cap in advance.
Reopen on a missed duty, stale effect, slower unsafe patching
or a stronger ordinary route. Actual legal authority, preparedness
and public outcomes remain open.
