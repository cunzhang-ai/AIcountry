# Original evidence for local funding through population change

| Primary source | What it establishes | Scope and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Commonwealth Grants Commission, GST distribution occasional paper, September 2025](https://www.cgc.gov.au/sites/default/files/2025-09/Occasional%20Paper%20No.12%20-%20GST%20distribution%20-%20addressing%20some%20misconceptions_Final.pdf) | The Commission distinguishes representative fiscal capacity from actual spending or collection choices. It describes annual assessment and a three-year moving average used to reduce volatility and phase changes in. | Supports separate choices about effort and update speed. It describes Australian state transfers within a defined pool, not a costed municipal service floor or a particular advance rule. |
| [England local government settlement, final core spending power explanatory note](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/explanatory-note-on-core-spending-power-final-local-government-finance-settlement-2026-27-to-2028-29/explanatory-note-on-core-spending-power-final-local-government-finance-settlement-2026-27-to-2028-29) | The final note distinguishes recurring allocations, transition protections and a one-off adjustment support grant. | A precedent for reporting transition separately from the ordinary funding formula. It does not show that a blanket grant floor targets actual service need or that a listed amount was paid or received. |
