# Shared resources, continuity and external dependence

*Integrated design 68 research map; the linked topic files contain the current detailed choices. This map does not enact law.*

| Public question | Selected operating answer | Detailed design |
|---|---|---|
| How does the country reach its new design? | Ordinary elected authority chooses protected stages with exact starts, continuing rights, paid overlap, available sources, safe acceptance and lawful handover; delays keep claims and post-horizon costs visible. No project extends its executive mandate. | [protected transition](../C14/06-staged-capacity-and-protection.md) |
| How are the country's responsibilities budgeted without counting shared resources twice? | One operating book allocates unique components across all currently audited functions while separating a proposed bid, estimated requirement, actual source, qualified capacity and receipt. Missing needs and continuing claims remain visible; Assembly law chooses actual burdens. | [country book](../C06/22-country-operating-resource-book.md) · [joint schedule](../C06/18-joint-commitment-and-delivery-schedule.md) · [commitment inventory](../../settlement/commitments/README.md) |
| Can the proposed economy support those promises? | Compare household disposable cash, required output, paired opening claims, paid replacement, continuing pensions, due-date domestic/foreign finance and distinct skills together. Normal ledger closure does not establish productive supply or decide the income/ownership burden. | [national package](../C06/20-national-economic-package.md) · [demand, stocks and timing](../C06/21-demand-consistent-operating-reference.md) |
| Who bears a bank failure, and can the state exempt itself? | M defines client property, insured claims, prior loss order, compensation and elected contingent risk. Its sovereign ceiling rejects the old bank source 250/E100; a balanced fiscal book is not compliant finance. | [Financial law](../C06/24-financial-law-and-loss-allocation.md) · [source conflict](../../settlement/regulatory-finance/02-financing-and-alternatives.md) |
| What if debt service, essential services and imports cannot all be paid? | The Assembly distinguishes cash timing, debt unsustainability, foreign-currency failure and physical shortage; it compares complete burden-sharing packages and alone authorizes any bounded negotiation and exact binding debt terms. Creditor claims, domestic bank and pension losses, service rights and foreign legal limits remain visible. | [Debt distress](../C06/06-sovereign-debt-and-distress.md) · [combined exercise](../C13/09-debt-medicine-and-pension-crisis.md) |
| How does a community recover when disaster destroys homes, records and its tax base? | Basic shelter and services continue without title or insurance as a gate. An elected local plan compares safe repair, adaptation and supported movement; the Assembly authorizes national finance and cross-boundary burdens. Insurance, legal compensation and public reconstruction retain distinct claims. | [Recovery and return](../C09/07-disaster-recovery-and-return.md) · [risk finance](../C06/07-disaster-risk-finance.md) · [flood exercise](../C13/10-flood-recovery-and-displacement.md) |
| How does today's majority avoid taking choices away from people who cannot yet vote? | Prior law identifies material hard-to-reverse losses; the competent elected body compares less damaging paths, respects enforceable floors, funds maintenance or repair and gives reasons before commitment. No guardian or AI claims future consent. | [future options](../C09/04-future-options-and-irreversibility.md) |
| Who chooses a climate and nature transition without sacrificing reliable services or excluded people? | The Assembly sets a measured multiyear emissions path and separate habitat, water and air floors, then chooses a whole sector and finance package. Operators demonstrate actual replacement capacity; worker, household, local and external burdens stay visible. A missed path requires public correction, not technical self-authorization. | [Ecological transition](../C09/08-climate-and-ecological-transition.md) · [finance](../C06/08-ecological-transition-finance.md) · [grid-delay exercise](../C13/11-transition-with-grid-delay.md) |
| How do food supply, safety, access and land/water limits survive together? | The Assembly compares whole food-system packages and funds person-level access, safe reserves and workable supply. A technical food authority coordinates evidence, while safety, markets, trade and ecological decisions retain distinct owners and remedies. | [food system](../C09/09-food-systems-and-nutrition.md) · [finance](../C06/10-food-system-finance.md) · [shock exercise](../C13/13-drought-price-and-food-safety.md) |
| Who protects animals when food, disease control, research and ecology pull in different directions? | The Assembly defines a species-sensitive serious-harm floor and its exceptions; veterinary expertise, separate inspection, safe care, courts and a shared cost account test welfare directly. Human health, food safety, conservation and welfare keep distinct evidence and powers. | [animal welfare](../C09/10-animal-welfare-and-one-health.md) · [enforcement](../C08/07-animal-protection-and-remedies.md) · [finance](../C06/13-animal-care-and-transition-finance.md) |
| Who allocates essentials when several systems fail at once? | Prior elected law bounds first-hour safety and use of nonoverlapping reserves; operators and a named coordinator record actual diversions, while any new material priority returns to the Assembly with rights review and unmet claims visible. | [compound scarcity](../C06/05-cross-system-scarcity.md) |
| How do rights and essential goods survive a broken network or delivery route? | National law assigns physical connectivity and freight duties to separate accountable stewards, regulates plural operators, funds usable offline and alternate paths, and keeps cross-sector scarcity with elected authority. A digital platform or dispatch record is not delivery. | [connections and freight](../C09/05-communications-and-logistics.md) |
| How do sanitation, cyber and border services keep their distinct duties during failure? | Local stewards own safe sanitation and waste chains under national floors; service authorities retain legal responsibility while a bounded cyber coordinator shares technical response; customs, safety and protection decisions remain distinct with coordinated clearance and appeal. Shared reserves and new material priorities return to elected law. | [sanitation](../C09/06-sanitation-and-waste.md) · [cyber](../C10/04-cyber-incident-and-recovery.md) · [customs](../C10/05-customs-and-border-clearance.md) |
| How does the country bind itself and protect people across borders? | Elected authority controls material treaty entry, provisional application and exit; an individually reviewable protection claim precedes irreversible return; critical imports and foreseeable harms abroad enter the public account. | [treaty commitments](../C10/15-democratic-treaty-commitments.md) · [external relations](../C10/02-external-relations-and-migration.md) · [foreign payments](../C06/02/03-foreign-payments.md) |

[Private insolvency](../C08/14-private-insolvency-and-debt-recovery.md)
allocates lawful estate/creditor losses and ordinary discharge under court
control. Bank/pension/private finance and foreign payment effects remain
in the joined account; a write-off does not fund a public obligation.

[SF01–SF10](../C06/25-source-finance-and-payment-continuity.md) now selects
a conditional source/equity/standby package and preserves full-reserve credit
as a different rival. Reserve stock, normal spendable cash, private risk and
actual executed payment remain separate; no source or superiority is proven.

[C06/26](../C06/26-conflict-custody-and-release-capacity.md) now attributes
conflict custody/release duties: paid counsel/courts/care, protected records,
safe receiving and continuing injury/missing claims. Count ordinary/election
staff and transport once; old bids, captive labor and anticipated foreign
repayment supply no actual new qualified capacity or cash.

[C06/27](../C06/27-technical-safety-and-repair-capacity.md) now joins TS/TD
assessment, calibration, maintenance, investigation and actual replacement.
Private insurance, public self-insurance and long-term closure claims have
actual counterparties and costs; no bond, laboratory or engineer counts twice.

[CP national reconciliation](../C06/28-national-commitments-and-residual-scarcity.md)
now joins corrected productive support, enacted new-service/accrual dates and
narrow residual public-stock powers. Existing rights/elections/remedies retain
their own sources; expert/AI evidence cannot authorize an unproved resource,
indefinite readiness delay or self-renewed emergency mandate.

[NP](../C06/33-protection-production-and-national-expansion.md) distinguishes
resource/shift-specific supply, private voluntary uses, source-law payment
classes and physically impossible collisions. Restricted assets/SF keep their
purposes. Core insufficiency triggers relief/elected correction, not a new
Treasury priority, enlarged CP power or cancelled election.

[SD country delivery](../C06/43-country-source-and-delivery.md) now pairs the
historical I05 access-first comparison with one 0.4M allowance. SD06 C2 is a universal cash alternative. [SG](../C05/15-service-first-personal-security.md) selects service-first protection. The37+later dependency map, dated G/S/
local/owned-FX sources and simultaneous paid shifts govern comparison. The current
mixed/desal launch is rejected; neither physical branch is certified ready.

[NB nonbank finance](../C06/44-nonbank-finance-and-client-protection.md) separates custody shortage, fund market loss, insurer debt, private-pension promise and CCP loss. PublicY/S, M deposits, private general estates and elected new public risk cannot be interchanged; actuarial tables/full source evidence remain unfinished.

[SD25–SD32/114](../C06/43-country-source-and-delivery.md) now joins same-person retail price/work/needs and pension sensitivities, private inventory/credit and paid-invoice/foreign-owner consequences. Local manufacture and cheaper imports have different losses; neither alone funds G/S or proves a country ready. Basket/upstream/current-duty/legacy source evidence remains missing.

[Whole-demand/work](../C06/58/17-whole-country-demand-and-work.md) makes
public-service supplier chains and competing household/export/investment work
visible together. Neither a modeled import decrease nor industry surplus is
owned public FX or an accepted qualified worker. Actual needs, domestic/unpaid
work, BC external duties, due claims and paid OP/T succession remain.
