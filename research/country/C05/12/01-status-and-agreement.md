# WL01–WL05: actual work creates rights, not a convenient label

*Part of [C05/12](../12-work-contracts-and-collective-power.md); design 117 candidate.*

**WL01 — Protected scope.** Cover natural persons doing paid personal work in
an employment or substantively dependent relationship, including public, domestic,
agency, temporary, migrant and platform work. Fundamental equality, safety and
civil protections also apply within their actual scope to independent enterprise;
not every customer contract creates employee leave/pay. Care, ordinary income,
residence and franchise are not conditional on paid work. Child-work restrictions,
clinical consent, constitutional office mandates and actual foreign-law limits
remain separate. No new general compulsory labor or immigration enforcement power.
Unfamiliar future work/personhood claims remain visible for interpretation, not
silently erased; replicated agents do not multiply a natural person's time or vote.

**WL02 — Facts and a rebuttable presumption.** Once a claimant shows paid
personal work for another undertaking and plausible direction/control, integration
or economic dependence, presume protected employment unless the respondent proves
actual independent enterprise on the balance of evidence. Examine real choice
of work/price, organization, delegation, equipment/risk, continuity, integration,
market access and freedom to serve others together. One client, flexible hours,
a registered business or use of an agent alone decides nothing. An actual trade
with its own independent business can rebut; a nominal substitution clause,
platform account or consent form cannot waive the floor. Courts decide disputed
status with original evidence, reply and assisted access, not an AI score.

**WL03 — Several parties need attributable duties.** Identify actual employer,
labor-hire agency, host, payroll custodian and work director separately. A host
undertaking that actually directs supplied labor is jointly responsible with
the labor supplier for its due wages/time and site safety within actual control;
worker receipt is reconciled once. Ordinary purchasing of a genuinely independent
service does not make every customer a wage guarantor. Parent/group responsibility
requires proved actual control, undertaking, evasion or other law, not a logo.
A shell transfer cannot restart service or erase a real debt. AS person-directed
service does not alone make a recipient an employer; its specific election,
public duty and backing rules prevail. Source-law agency/contract liability and
B10 duties remain even when employment status is contested.

**WL04 — Agreement with usable evidence.** Give accessible durable essential
terms before work where feasible and no later than seven calendar days after
start: parties, work/place, regular/payable hours, pay and deductions, leave,
variation/termination, relevant collective terms and direct remedies. Provide
translation/understanding support; oral or future-media agreements can prove work.
Missing paper or a worker's copy does not defeat performed work or due wages.
Recruitment/required trial work is paid where productive work/control is real;
no fictitious volunteer label. Delegation needs actual scope and revocation;
logging in, a generated reply or a helper's signature is not general assent.

**WL05 — Stable default, bounded trials and finite purpose.** Employment is
indefinite unless an actual objective finite task, absence replacement, training
or season justifies a disclosed term. Normal same-function fixed-term service
converts to indefinite at24 cumulative months or on the third renewal, whichever
occurs first. A genuinely specified finite project, actual named absence or
bounded training program can justify a longer term only on proved facts and
an initially disclosed finite end; recurring ordinary demand, annual grant
uncertainty or changing accounts is insufficient. No exception avoids fair reason,
notice, accrued rights or challenge at termination. Artificial gaps within30 days
and actual continuity through a controlled labor supplier count as service;
longer sham breaks can also be proved. Necessary business closure remains possible.

Probation is at most three calendar months for the actual role, with paid work,
support/feedback and no reset by a new label or owner. Valid-reason, equality,
union, wage and fair-hearing protection applies from entry, not only after a
qualification period. A genuine new role can have a supported assessment without
restarting the person's accrued service or stripping existing protection.
