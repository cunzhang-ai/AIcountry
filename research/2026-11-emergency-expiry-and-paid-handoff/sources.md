# Design272 evidence and custody

- [Gazette issue 118, page 139](https://gazette.govt.nz/issues/31765)
  publishes notices 2016-go7087 and 2016-go7088. The individual
  instruments supply the stated cutover time and different scopes.
- [2016 amending Act, section 27](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2016/88/en/2016-11-29.pdf)
  supplies the contemporaneous source for sections 94A, 94C, 94D,
  94L and 94M. The 8 August 2017 reprint from the Otago Civil Defence
  Emergency Management site corroborates the historical framework.
  [Archived reprint](original-sources/otago-reprint-2017.pdf)
- [Gazette issue 33, pages 57–58](https://gazette.govt.nz/issues/31876)
  publishes notice 2017-go1408, with a narrowed geographic scope and
  a separate endpoint. The [agency transition table](https://www.civildefence.govt.nz/emergency-events/previous-emergencies/transition-periods)
  records the overall duration and change in Marlborough scope.

The Gazette issues and amending Act were inspected online. Direct
retrieval of the initial Gazette and amending Act returned an access
page or empty content, so the archive includes the separately
retrieved historical reprint only. The [checked fields](observations.json)
capture local civil times, notice identity, territory and unknowns.
No underlying service records or actual affected participation were
obtained. The case distinguishes valid authority and field delivery.
It does not establish the merits of that law or the effects on people.
