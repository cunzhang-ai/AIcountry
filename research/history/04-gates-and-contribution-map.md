# Remaining gates and contribution map

*Part of [Design history](../DESIGN-HISTORY.md); integrated design 55 research, not enacted law.*

This is an integrated, reviewable **national institutional outline** with conditional
choices for the country's core operating systems. It is not a complete legal code:
offense definitions, service standards, local competences, tax parameters, treaty
terms, digital safeguards and emergency powers still need clause-level design and
reconciliation. A monetary/production system has not been demonstrated and none
of the safeguards has operated. Further desk design should resolve a named
cross-system contradiction or prepare a concrete alternative; it cannot substitute
for the next decisive real-world choice by an affected public in a lawful jurisdiction.
Only then can original observations, current-law reconciliation, qualified public
review, funding and operational assessment determine whether any part should be
adopted. This project cannot claim those inputs or superiority over existing countries.

## How future contributions stay organized

Changes start by naming a C01–C14 choice or a gap in this register. Explain what changes
in the country, which other systems must change with it, and what remains disputed.
Update the main design and register together when a proposal is selected for the working
design. Preserve the previous snapshot and a proportionate decision record.

A new study should answer a question capable of changing an identified country choice.
Its result must be integrated, explicitly held open, or rejected with reasons. More files,
successful software checks or repeated AI agreement are not design progress. This is
project organization; the existing research method and actual adoption rules retain
their authority and limits.
