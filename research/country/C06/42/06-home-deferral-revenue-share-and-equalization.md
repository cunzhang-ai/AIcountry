# Recurring taxes: protect homes and fund the local floor with actual cash

*NF21–NF24; part of [C06/42](../42-recurring-tax-and-noncash-work.md).*

**NF21 — A real primary-home liquidity deferral.** An individual actual primary-
home holder may defer the unaffordable levy after protected essential/support
costs if accessible liquid assets are no more than the greater of I04's three-
month buffer and six months ordinary basket plus evidenced imminent necessary
costs. No spouse pooling, hypothetical loan, home sale or unsafe support counted
as cash. Assisted annual source review/full merits; actually affordable portion
is paid. Companies, second homes and speculative vacancies have no such personal
relief. Age/disability alone neither qualifies nor excludes a person.

Deferred principal bears 2% annual simple noncompounding carrying interest and a
prospective limited-recourse parcel charge. It matures on actual disposal/death/
cessation of protected home; a qualifying co-owner/dependant successor may assume
the same dated ledger, no nominee-reset/forgiveness. Recover only actual net
realization after prior perfected security/mandatory priorities and necessary
safe receiving/moving cost, no retroactive creditor priming or personal deficit.
Unrecoverable remainder is a disclosed public loss. Continuing protected need
alone cannot force possession. For this express statutory deferral only, TX12
first collectible due event is actual maturation plus 30 working days; six/evasion-
only-eight-year execution outer then runs once, no new office/installment reset.
Timely annual assessment/review remains. The 2% carrying rate applies only while principal is lawfully deferred; stop
it on maturity and use applicable TX neutral late-interest rules thereafter,
no simultaneous double interest/interest-on-interest. No fabricated deferral
revives an already barred ordinary debt; nondeferred arrears keep original TX clocks. Public carrying
cash/loss and owner-versus-renter equity costs remain visible.

**NF22 — Split one actual receipt 80/20.** Revenue records received land tax
once: 80% site locality, 20% national G, with same-share refunds/corrections.
Actually collected NF21 carrying interest has the same 80/20 ownership split
and same-share correction/refund, separately identified; accrued interest is
not cash and never enters representative capacity C or held-principal releases. Named
Treasury distribution payer transfers the local share within five working days
of actual receipt, earlier due law where applicable, no ministerial loyalty gate.
Assessed/deferred/stayed/unpaid claims are not received national/local funds;
the Treasury-to-local transfer is no second national tax/output. Councils budget
within actual competences; no new base, guarantee or municipal bailout authority.

**NF23 — Need, lawful cash capacity and marginal retention.** N_j is enacted
standard common local-duty cost from actual service quantities/population/access
geography/additional needs×evidenced qualified unit/fixed-place cost under C06/
C07, not discretionary underspending. Let B_j be lawful taxable site value;
D_rep/J_rep are nonoverlapping legally mandatory deferral/court-stay portions
of the annual levy at representative 1% (proportionate from the actual in-range
rate for the same held share). Set **C_j=.80×max(0,.01 B_j−D_rep−J_rep)** plus
only another expressly assigned base's representative capacity. Do not subtract
optional exemptions, deliberate undercollection, private bad forecasts or the
extra burden of a locality's higher chosen rate. Unknown N/B/holds are not zero.

Maintain each held amount's source-year/representative-share ledger. When
previously deducted held principal actually settles, add its 80%-local 1%-rate
-equivalent release once to the current receipt-year C before grant calculation;
not before actual settlement, not again each year, and not the higher-rate
increment or carrying interest. Refund/cancelled liability corrects that same
source item rather than a fictional receipt. No retroactive clawback of an
unreceived grant; a release too large to offset that year's positive grant
creates no negative grant or permanent recovery lien. The unrecouped excess
is an explicit solidarity cost, not a hidden second tax source.

The national grant is **max(0,N_j−.75 C_j)**. At actual floor-rate collection net
those lawful holds, local source C plus grant is N+.25 C in the positive-grant
branch;25% of additional representative capacity survives grant withdrawal.
Higher-rate actual cash retains its 80% local share. This buys discretion/incentive
above the common floor, costs G extra and leaves unequal optional resources.
Neither 20% national land receipts nor potential capacity is assumed to fund grants.

**NF24 — Grant has a lawful payer and failure route.** Treasury's C04 grant
calculation officer uses enacted annual N/C schedules, protected inspectable
Revenue/EV sources, reasons and direct correction/court access; neither office
changes legal need/rates. Pay annual grants in 12 first-day monthly advances
through a separate career payer, actual G source and verified local acceptance.
Adjust mandated-hold schedules prospectively in monthly reconciliation with
source reasons, no clawback of an unreceived installment or duplicate duty cost.
Formula/cash shocks retain national responsibility for common essential delivery:
report immediately and use actual NP/CP/sector funding/accepted receiving. No
automatic user fee, vote delay or cancellation of rights. Undercollection remains
auditable debt/failure, not extra formula grant; urgent national protection needs
an explicit source/recovery decision, no secret unlimited local overdraft.

Design 130 [the bounded local working-advance candidate](../52/04-dated-sources-and-local-cash.md)
compares an express, funded, voluntary source/recovery schedule for a dated
common-duty mismatch. It does not amend NF24 monthly grants/formula or authorize
a loan by itself. Actual G source, local lawful acceptance, fixed limits, fair
access, safe recovery and independent review remain required.

**Design145 compelled disposal.** LA's compensated taking is actual disposal
under NF21 where its source applies; principal/limited-recourse priorities and
actual due/collection dates remain. [LA09](../../C09/13/02-people-valuation-and-replacement.md)
does not forgive old deferred-tax or mortgage principal, invent creditor consent,
count inaccessible escrow as cash or promise a new lender. Any necessary lawful
paid housing/financing bridge is a project cost, not assumed source or title.

[PT17/PT23](../../C08/26-property-transfers-and-secured-credit.md) preserves
NF21's actual limited-recourse parcel claim, prior perfected security and
necessary safe receiving/moving costs. Record the actual statutory charge/
ledger with protected notice; do not create a new top rank, personal deficit,
new maturity date or retroactive debt by an ordinary-security index entry.

[MF](../../C04/08-funded-local-mandates-and-equalization.md) reconciles
general and sector funding and actual cash before a service falls due.
It preserves this benchmark's exact grant and advance rules, TN's
current-source disposition and already accrued local claims.
