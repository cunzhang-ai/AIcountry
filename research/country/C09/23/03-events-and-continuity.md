# Extreme events and continuing public duties

*Part of [TH safe home temperatures](../23-safe-home-temperatures-and-continuity.md).*

**TH09 — Prepare for when ordinary protection runs out.** The
local public-health authority issues an actionable warning from
credible hazard evidence, including persistent night exposure and
outage or smoke interaction. The local housing and civil-rescue
duty officers use the existing sector and incident powers to
activate the paid plan by its last safe start. Occupant reports,
high-risk sites and inaccessible places can trigger action
before an outdoor warning threshold. No prior registry entry
is needed for immediate help. Care teams offer supported contact
with consent or other strictly limited prior-law grounds.
Use CN independent communication and equivalent offline contact.
Separate a warning sent, a person reached, an offer accepted
and actual protected receiving.

**TH10 — Buy fallback for the whole journey and period.** Compare
safe in-home repair and temporary equipment, islanded power,
and nearby safe places that can accept the actual people.
Before claiming shelter capacity, check consent, accessible travel
both ways, opening hours, sleep, power and air quality, care,
medication and dependent household needs. Deduct already committed
seats, staff and vehicles. Test power and energy at the receiving
site and the date of exhaustion or next safe handoff. Meet CN,
OC and RC independence and donor protections when a feeder,
controller, communications path or reserve is shared. Where
people cannot leave, verify a separate in-home route or a
specific supported transfer. An open building without reachable,
staffed, safe places earns no continuity credit.

**TH11 — Expose the gap and preserve lawful review.** Existing
clinicians and sector officers apply lawful need and time
criteria to actual rescue, clinical care and energy scarcity.
If resources cannot cover everyone, record the uncovered people
or tasks, decision authority, safe available action and next
decision time. Recheck through simultaneous demands and keep
discretionary loads separate from proved essential uses.
Apply existing C06/OC/RC and PS/CP rules. No added rationing,
home-entry, treatment, surveillance or eviction power follows.
A resident can reach a separately staffed reviewer or court
under existing urgent procedures. A network or landlord
dashboard cannot close the case. AI may help check weather,
physical dependencies and conflicting commitments. An authorized
human decides priorities, verifies evidence and remains answerable
for delivery.

**TH12 — Measure actual protection and carry obligations forward.**
Publish recurring counts of harmful exposure where observed,
restoration times, received interim help, uncovered places and
groups, net household bills, rent or displacement effects and
matched peak demand. Preserve separately the actual outcome,
reported evidence and model estimate. Compare ownership and
operating choices under the same duty. Keep protected testimony
purpose-limited, with an independent reporting channel against
a captured landlord, council or regulator. Preserve inspection,
funding, open claims and paid receiving through sale, insolvency,
plant replacement, operator transfer or a revised standard.
Hand back only when the receiving actor and resident's safe
service are verified. Review the floor, installation assumptions
and sources after an adverse event or material change. Any
lowered protection needs its own lawful and prospective decision.

[AQ07](../24/02-correction-and-acute-protection.md) joins the smoke
response to safe indoor heat and cold protection. Verify actual receiving,
power, air quality and accessibility through the same window.
