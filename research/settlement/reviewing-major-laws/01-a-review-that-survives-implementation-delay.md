# A first review that survives delay and rebranding

Design355 · Constructed comparison under
[PCO11–PCO16](../../country/C14/04/05-review-major-laws-in-operation.md).

An Act changes access to an essential housing repair service for 100
eligible households. First legal commencement is day 0. Delivery starts
on day 140. The operating agency is renamed on day 600. For this example,
assume the 24-month anniversary of scheduled commencement falls on
day 730 and precedes the 36-month enactment limit. The review remains
due on day 730. A proposed expansion is separately votable.

In the matched baseline, 90 of 100 households received timely repair.
The ministry's new channel shows 76 of 80 served on time, or 95%.
The independent review preserves the same denominator. The 20 omitted
households are still eligible. Independent case receipts establish that
12 missed their deadline; 8 results remain unknown. Actual timely
receipt is between 76 and 84 of 100. Even the upper bound is six
percentage points below the baseline. This bounds a regression in
receipt in this case, with no causal attribution to the Act as a whole.

The independent comparison is due on day 670. Assume the verified
late-case receipts arrive afterward and a qualified finding is
independently received on day 700. The existing
PCO04 response remains due on day 730 and the hearing and disposition
on day 760. If the scheduled hearing on day 730 covers the finding
with usable notice, evidence and an actual disposition, one process
can satisfy both sets of duties. A vote only on future verification
for the Act, with no disposition of the finding, leaves the day-760
duty outstanding. The 12 households keep their original remedies
regardless of the parliamentary timetable.

| Alternative | Consequence in the declared case |
|---|---|
| Departmental review alone | A new name and successful-channel figures can leave omitted households and the original schedule out of the decision. |
| Automatic expiry of the whole Act on day 730 | The authority for a stipulated continuing repair service would lapse unless a separately effective successor were provided. |
| Dated independent review and a separate elected choice | Keep lawful services and remedies. The Assembly can repair access and verify the eight unresolved cases before deciding on expansion. |

In a simplified forward budget, 10 units are available after base
services. The repair and remedy schedule needs 6, independent review
and paid access need 2, and continuing staff overlap needs 2.
An additional 4-unit optional expansion does not fit. Even though
expansion might improve access over time, it cannot spend the same
10 units. If an alternate paid review team is unavailable, record
the missing review, carry the due repair and keep the unresolved
comparison in the elected record. The model assigns no cost saving
to late, missing or unused scrutiny. Any reallocation requires
lawful authority.
