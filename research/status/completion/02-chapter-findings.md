A chapter with specified hypotheses is not “empty.” A missing real person,
territory or enactment is not necessarily a missing design rule. Conversely,
conditional labels cannot conceal unspecified powers, material tradeoffs or
finance. Keep prior valid support only within its complete scope.

Context-specific air/sea/rail/space, tax administration, products and external
commitments require a bounded enabling/special-law schedule if relevant;
they cannot be silently treated as complete by a general safety paragraph.
A new function goes to a declared owner with cross-system duties, not another
copy of all fourteen chapters. Current office/audit classifications can change
without granting new sovereign authority.
