# A change of government and two public deadlines

Constructed example. The outgoing service unit has eight qualified and
paid staff-days for existing housing assistance decisions due on Friday.
An incoming elected minister lawfully directs a simpler application
process for new cases from the following Monday. Its tested rollout
takes four additional qualified staff-days during the same window.
The Assembly has already funded four separately available days for
training and rollout. Existing claims keep their former effective dates
and proof rules. The change uses a common eligible records source
with lawful access for each team and a separate review route.

| Response in the same window | Existing casework by Friday | New intake preparation by Monday |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Announce a new unit and immediately remove the old unit's access. Only the rollout team is qualified and authorized to use the new queue at opening. | Eight days of due casework go uncovered. | Four days available if the rollout team's source and access work. |
| Retain the old unit and let its head defer the lawful change. | Eight days of existing casework available. | Four days of preparation go unused. The valid Monday instruction is missed. |
| Give each team a separately authorized scope and a deadline. | Eight days of work in the old queue with the original rights and clocks. | Four other days of preparation with tested access. Begin under the lawful Monday direction if the work is received. |

The third response is the working choice under the stated budget.
It does not guarantee a job in the old unit after work moves. Any later
abolition or reassignment follows ordinary employment and service law.

Test loss of the alternate credentials and common funding. If the old
team has its paid shifts but cannot access the live queue, keep lawful
manual or alternate access only where it has actually been tested.
If an ordinary court finds an imminent unlawful interruption, it can
order the authorized alternate to restore limited access for the Friday
queue, while leaving the Monday work and its lawful scope in place.
Check that the team receives usable access before Friday. An unexecuted
order leaves the missed casework visible. If the four additional days
were assumed from the same eight already needed for claims, keeping
both Friday and Monday is infeasible. Continue the
original duties and return the unfunded rollout to the responsible
minister and Assembly for a lawful correction. Record a missed lawful
date if no authorized change arrives. A court order and a Cabinet
announcement each leave the staffing arithmetic unchanged.
