# KR05–KR08a: inventions, seeds, signs and confidential knowledge

*Design 95 research candidate; not enacted law, a funded offer or demonstrated
superiority.*

**KR05 — Patents.** A patent covers only claims to a new, inventive, industrially
applicable product or process that is disclosed clearly enough for a skilled person to
perform. A discovery, abstract principle, mathematical idea or program expression alone
is no invention; assess a computer-implemented technical invention under the same
criteria as other fields, not a blanket software or medicine exclusion. The recorded
human inventor is credited; lawful employers/assignees may hold economic title under
disclosed agreements and worker law. Rights to make/use/offer/sell/import claimed
products or use claimed processes run **20 years from filing**, subject to published
lawful fees and prior-right challenges; later trivial formulations do not extend the old
claim. Limited experiments on the claimed invention, not ordinary commercial production
merely called research, and acts necessary for lawful regulatory preparation receive the
KR09 exceptions; any wider non-voluntary public/essential use requires individualized
grounds, scope, notice, adequate remuneration and independent review under applicable
law. A patent grants no clinical, environmental or market authorization. The claim scope
is public and opposition can test genuine novelty and inventive contribution; a separate
genuine improvement cannot restart or enlarge an expired earlier claim. Disclose
applications 18 months after earliest valid priority, or filing if none, with protected
personal details and only separately lawful security limits; publication is not grant.
Rights commence on actual grant, with no automatic grant or provisional infringement
debt from filing/publication. A late decision does not restart the 20-year term. Human
clinical treatment/diagnostic acts and naturally occurring discoveries as such are
excluded; eligible technical products, microorganisms and processes face the ordinary
tests. Excluding a patent never waives safety or another valid right.

**KR06 — Plant varieties and chip layouts.** A plant-variety certificate protects a
novel, distinct, uniform and stable identified variety against commercial
production/multiplication, conditioning, offering, sale, export/import and stocking
propagating material for those acts for **20 years from grant, 25 for trees/vines**; the
generic variety name stays usable. Private noncommercial use, experiments and breeding
other varieties remain free. A farmer may retain and replant harvested seed of a
protected **annual food crop on the same holding** for that holding's production, but
may not sell/exchange it as seed; a commercial crop pays **half the arm’s-length,
published comparable per-area licensed propagation royalty each sowing**, with price
review, while genuinely household-consumed production pays none. This limited
own-holding choice is normative and its UPOV compatibility requires actual review.
Public-interest third-party use needs equitable remuneration and review. Public
breeding, seed banks, food continuity and Indigenous/community seed duties are
separately funded and cannot be extinguished by certificate. If no credible comparable
exists or terms are disputed, an ordinary court sets a reasoned reasonable base and
interim payment/protection, not a self-set infinite tariff or presumed zero. Essentially
derived commercial varieties need the relevant original breeder licence while that right
lasts; the experiment/breeding act remains free. Publicly used/common varieties and
uncaptured wild resources cannot be enclosed merely by describing them. Evolving
heterogeneous populations need not become uniform to remain usable; a certificate does
not erase their lawful collective/ordinary interests. Actual local seed law and treaty
compatibility remain unverified.

**KR07 — Marks, designs and origin.** A distinctive registered mark protects against
materially confusing commercial source use, not ordinary description, criticism,
interoperability naming or truthful repair identification. Initial registration and
renewals each last **10 years**; renewal requires recorded fee and genuine use, with
cancellation after **three years of unjustified nonuse** on notice/hearing. A
new/original registered industrial design protects commercial copies of appearance, not
features dictated essentially by function, for **10 years from filing**. A geographic
indication is protected for qualifying origin-linked goods against misleading origin
claims and unfair competition, including protection of qualifying wines/spirits origin
names against false-origin use even labelled as a type or imitation, subject to proved
prior/generic and actual treaty exceptions; a producer group or community, not a single
firm's false ownership claim, can invoke it. A public origin register aids proof but
cannot erase eligible unregistered claims. Protection ends when the
origin-quality/reputation connection or lawful designation ends after hearing; no
perpetual monopoly over generic or truthful names.

**KR08 — Secrets and submitted tests.** A trade secret has protection against dishonest
acquisition, disclosure or use only while it is not generally known/readily accessible,
has commercial value because secret and is reasonably protected. Independent discovery
and lawful reverse engineering are not appropriation. Protective court handling
preserves proof without a sealed private court; safety reporting, worker rights, lawful
oversight and disclosure necessary to protect the public retain their specific grounds.
Undisclosed pharmaceutical/agrochemical regulatory test data receive protection against
unfair commercial use and disclosure within the applicable legal test, with
public-protection exceptions; a public filing is not an automatic perpetual data
monopoly. No new fixed regulatory-data exclusivity term is selected in addition to that
protection; lawful evidence reliance, participant privacy, product quality and actual
surviving legal/treaty duties require their own decisions. No worker is barred from
using general skills or forced to teach proprietary know-how.

**KR08a — Chip layouts.** An independently created original chip layout registered
within two years of first commercial use is protected against commercial
copying/distribution for **10 years from the earlier of registration or first commercial
exploitation worldwide**, with a **15-year outer limit from creation**; innocent stock
after notice may continue on reasonable royalty. These are candidates to be checked
against any actual TRIPS/UPOV/IPIC obligations, not assertions of membership.
