# Evidence for voluntary care and paid continuity

| Primary source | Observed scope | Use and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman, 23 016 335](https://www.lgo.org.uk/decisions/adult-care-services/assessment-and-care-plan/23-016-335) | Paragraphs 40–45 record unchanged assessed needs and a reduced care plan that assumed more support from a spouse. The Ombudsman found fault in the authority's lack of consultation and failure to reassess after learning he was unwilling or unable to provide that level of support. | Preserve underlying need and distinguish actual willingness, capability and receipt. One adjudicated case supplies no estimate of prevalence or required reserve size. |
| [Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman, 20 009 009](https://www.lgo.org.uk/decisions/adult-care-services/assessment-and-care-plan/20-009-009) | Paragraphs 34–40 distinguish eligibility from delivery and record the recipient's objection to intimate care by her spouse. The Ombudsman found a loss to both people when that preference was not properly considered. | Check the recipient's and helper's choices separately, including for each intimate task. The case does not test the proposed fiscal options. |
| [Office for National Statistics, England and Wales Census 2021 unpaid care](https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/healthandsocialcare/healthandwellbeing/bulletins/unpaidcareenglandandwales/census2021) | The historical bulletin reports about 5 million usual residents aged five or older providing unpaid care. The age-standardized share providing 50 or more hours per week was 2.8 percent. The question concerns help related to health conditions, illness or old age and excludes care provided as paid employment. The pandemic and wording change qualify comparisons with 2011. | Keep household time visible beside paid work and public spend. These are reported activities and broad time bands, not forecasts, clinical skills, actual shift availability or figures for the Danish fiscal reference. |

The scenario's units, demand overlap, rota and cash ceilings are
authored. The historical records qualify particular failure mechanisms
and the need to count household time. They do not authorize local action
or establish comparative effects. No complainant participated in this design.
