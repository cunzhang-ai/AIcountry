# Allocate public technology returns by their actual effect

*AT28–AT33. Candidate public finance rules within the technology transition.*

The rule covers proceeds from technology investments already owned by a
public body and returns due under authorized public agreements. Use ordinary
tax, worker, competition and ownership law for other gains.

**AT28 — Establish the amount and lawful owner before allocating it.**
Separate settled contractual receipts and distributable earnings from
returned principal, restricted capital, refunds, unrealized gains and
uncertain receivables. Identify the public owner and actual account.
Reconcile the existing charges and commitments of that account, including
collection, earned worker claims, maintenance, liabilities and funded
exit. Cost later enforceable calls at their own dates and hold any lawful
reserve independently of current spending. Preserve local and other
owners' title. Count the same return and public transfer once. Net
available cash is the remainder that may lawfully be put to a vote.

**AT29 — Show the public what each exact use would change.** At an ordinary
budget decision, present a common continuing-law baseline and compare
the available choices. Include retaining the balance, a bounded service or
time improvement, and a finite payment when legally and practically
feasible. Include debt reduction or an authorized investment where its
scope materially changes the result. State the cost, timing, people reached
and residual balance. For a payment, show gross receipt, taxes, benefit
interaction, delivery and household net effects by group. For service or
time, show actual access, affected workers, paid cover, operating costs
and time people can use. Compare who is left out of each option. Public
AI can reconcile accounts and compare evidence. It cannot choose a
distribution or decide that a service is worth a particular amount to a person.

**AT30 — Expose replacement of ordinary funding.** Show the voted service
and benefit baseline before the new receipt, all linked funding changes,
and the resulting aggregate and local delivery. Replacing an ordinary
appropriation can be a lawful elected choice with reasons. Record
what the original money now buys or where it is withdrawn. Label
baseline replacement and actual improvements separately. A new funding
label, reimbursement or budget transfer is insufficient proof that residents
received more service, more income or more free time. Keep local votes
and claim remedies where relevant.

**AT31 — Choose finite commitments from finite cash.** An unusual receipt
can finance an exact one-off payment, a bounded transition or a
lasting improvement with independently financed upkeep. Record the
last service or payment date and the balance after all obligations.
A continuing entitlement or staffing commitment requires a separately
qualified recurring source under FB. A future dividend, sale or
valuation increase cannot be booked as replenishment. A voted finite
payment has enforceable terms, delivery and an appeal under its own
law. Its expiry does not claw back earned payments or change existing
income, care, pension or worker rights. A different benefit interaction
must be enacted and costed explicitly.

**AT32 — Give saving and distribution separate mandates.** Cash may
be retained under ordinary budget law with a disclosed purpose and
review date. A long-term pooled investment alternative requires its
own vote on contributed title, eligible assets, gross loss limits,
stewardship, expenses, draw rules and liquidation. Model inflation,
correlated losses and years with weak or unavailable returns. Keep
management, draw authorization and audit institutionally distinct.
An investment manager has no automatic control of enterprises or
authority to promise a household payment. Under either route, the
Assembly decides the national use by recorded appropriation. Local
returns follow local authority and funding law. No common fund or
national claim on private wealth is created here.

**AT33 — Verify who received the gain and keep the decision revisable.**
Give opposition and affected workers, recipients and nonrecipients
usable access to the amounts, source rules, rivals and independent
checks. Treasury executes the enacted payment route. Providers and
outside reviewers verify actual service, wages, household net receipt
and accessible correction. Publish safe results with enough scope to
expose substitution, underpayment and concentration. A refused or
missed payment retains its lawful remedy. On office, contractor or
policy change, preserve records, commitments, restricted balances and
their lawful successors. Reopen if the payout is clawed back through
ordinary offsets, upkeep fails, expected gains never reach the public,
or a stronger durable source changes the preferred allocation.
