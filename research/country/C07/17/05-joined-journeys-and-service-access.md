# MO17–MO22. Keep responsibility when a journey changes operator

**MO17 — Identify which journey is protected and who owns continuity.**
A public mobility authority designates an existing journey owner for covered
MO journeys that it confirms across operators. The mobility authority retains
responsibility where it has not obtained an accepted designation. A commercial seller or
operator selling or expressly confirming a protected through-journey must
identify the responsible continuity office under the applicable carriage law
or contract. For a journey needed for a due service, the service body owns
its receiving duty and either arranges the mobility path or obtains actual
acceptance from the transport owner. A common fare or a route suggestion
alone does not establish a confirmed commercial connection. Publish that
boundary plainly. Existing statutory and protected journeys remain covered
without any ticket, platform, purchase or transfer of sector jurisdiction.
Continuity includes necessary boarding, the interchange, safe arrival and
the accepted return under MO07.

**MO18 — Hold responsibility until the next party accepts.** The first
competent contact records a safe means of reply and keeps the case until
the designated owner actually accepts it. This can be through an accessible
desk, phone, intermediary or offline route as well as a platform. Disclose
only information necessary to connect and deliver the trip. The route
owner obtains real capacity, driver and assistance acceptance for any
alternate. The receiving provider confirms that arrival still permits the
lawful service and what lawful recovery is possible. A clinic retains
clinical judgment and consent. A court or election official retains the
applicable deadlines and decision power. No transport dispatcher may move
an adjudication, approve care or substitute remote access unilaterally.

**MO19 — Act before the last usable departure.** For a credible threat to
a covered trip, calculate the last safe and useful time to start an
alternate, including pickup, transfer, accessibility, travel and entry
time. An earlier lawful sector clock controls. For known
closures identify affected paths in advance and confirm an accessible
alternative where duties require one. A generic notice cannot replace an
accepted protected journey. For unexpected failures begin arranging
continuity as soon as useful time is at risk. Let the passenger choose
between genuinely available options and preserve the safe return where
covered. Distinguish a reserved and confirmed alternative from a taxi
suggestion. When timely travel is infeasible, the service owner applies
its lawful in-place, alternate-site or later receiving rules and records
any unmet due service. Nothing authorizes unsafe transport.

**MO20 — Pay for a usable response before allocating liability.** On a
covered trip, the responsible public or commercial owner uses its authorized
direct payment, contract or standby arrangement for necessary continuation
without requiring the passenger to advance money. Keep elective
self-arranged travel and reimbursement where law or terms permit. Before
launch, fund standby, actual use and replenishment against plausible
concurrent events. Attribute each driver, accessible vehicle and place
once. Contractual public duties, including paid reserves, remain protected
from diversion to another route except under their own lawful terms.
Interoperator or interlocal cost recovery follows prior agreements and
actual evidence after response. A disputed invoice, fare share or
operator fault does not transfer the operational duty to the passenger.
Any uncovered cost remains with the lawful owner or returns to the
competent elected body under existing budget law.

**MO21 — Verify the result at both ends.** Track the travel result, receipt
of the destination service and any return separately. Preserve a lost
ordinary opportunity, missed care, school time or public remedy as an
unmet result even if fare or additional expenses are repaid. Affected
people can challenge refusal or delay through existing transport and
sector routes without first resolving which operator caused it. Existing
urgent courts and independent review can require timely lawful acts.
They do not create an unavailable vehicle or an unlawful extension.
Report unmet travel, service delays and barriers in safe aggregates
alongside costs and possible displaced capacity.

**MO22 — Apply proportionately and keep the network replaceable.** Adopt
with express covered classes, named owners, lawful funds, worker and
safety standards and an independent paid backup path. Test scheduled and
responsive transport, usable closer service and a simpler dispatch
arrangement on comparable safety, person-time and cost. Contracted
providers can be replaced without resetting continuing claims or public
duties. AI may help estimate missed connections and compare lawful
routes. It must expose unknown access constraints and leave actual
acceptance and recovery with accountable people. Share only the facts
needed for the route and remedy. Avoid a common passenger dossier or
exclusive dispatch interface. Preserve existing fare, sector, worker,
political and privacy rights in migration. Reopen on missed useful times,
cost diversion, common failure or a cheaper equally usable route.
