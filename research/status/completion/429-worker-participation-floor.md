# Worker participation with a direct cash claim

*Design521 · Scoped national institutional proposal*

[AT34–AT39](../../country/C05/18/08-worker-participation-in-realized-surplus.md)
select a prospective sector trial based on realized group surplus with a
cash floor, independently verified equivalent agreements, and direct coverage
for qualifying workers supplied through another employer. The claim follows
eligible work through departure. Cash, ordinary wages, income effects and
insolvency recovery retain separate tests.

The [comparison](../../settlement/worker-participation-floor/01-a-share-that-survives-employer-change.md)
checks payment offsets, shortfall, priority and competing arrangements on
a shared scope. It supplies no effective employer charge, actual staffing,
tax incidence, affected agreement or improved household outcome.
