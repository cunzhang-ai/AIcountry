# Resident deliberation and elected response

[RD01–RD05](../../country/C03/24-resident-deliberation-and-elected-response.md)
selects one paid, temporary panel for a decision elected representatives can
still change. The standing capacity is in the Assembly service. The panel's
recommendation is public and gets a timely disposition by the elected Assembly.

- [Historical evidence](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-resident-deliberation-and-elected-response/sources.md)
  distinguishes invitations, recommendations, parliamentary review and the
  executive's published status.
- [Authored case](02-authored-panel-and-disposition.md) exercises the cash,
  coverage, timely response and delivery distinctions.
- [Decision and migration](https://github.com/cunzhang-ai/AIcountry/blob/edition-1/research/2026-11-resident-deliberation-and-elected-response/decision-record.md)
  compares an adequately staffed ordinary hearing and a permanent agenda body.

The case is a research exercise. Actual inclusion, independent review,
enactment, staffed delivery and effects on residents remain open.
