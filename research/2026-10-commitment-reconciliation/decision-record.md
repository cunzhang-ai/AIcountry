## Observations and rival diagnoses

The current overview/status prioritizes integration. Canonical topic and
selection texts name 37 audited functions and 54 choices, with continuing
claims, differentiated service chains and resource limits. The old fiscal
exercise has six broad service envelopes and an income-support envelope.
It actually includes justice/safety/defense and democratic administration
in aggregate. It supplies neither function-level attribution nor a claim
queue, specialty roster, due-date cash or independent control map.

One diagnosis is unfunded expansion; another is repeated descriptions of
components already inside broad budgets. Both are plausible for particular
duties, and neither can be decided by counting new chapters. A third is
failure of public control during scarcity: a nominally protected reviewer
may share staff, payment or records with the failed service. The inventory
exposes these questions; it does not invent observed double allocation.

Equal rights, meaningful opposition and honest unmet-claim reporting are
design values. Cost identities and model conservation are definitions.
Receipt, staffing quality, independence and better lives are empirical
questions. The additional institutional staffing loss is an authored
stress assumption, not a forecast. No aggregate welfare score resolves
the value choice between service, cash, ownership and governance packages.

## Coherent alternatives

| Package | Mechanism and control | Advantages, losses and unresolved test |
|---|---|---|
| A: retain sector assurances and gross envelopes | Existing elected budgets, separate service chains and general count-once rule; interpret later duties within old totals | Least new accounting work and room for local/informal cooperation, but neither coverage nor simultaneous substitutes is evidenced; no-change would preserve an unsupported full-feasibility inference |
| B: joint attribution and scoped delivery schedule | Existing Treasury compiles; sectors/local operators attest; opposition, separate evidence, audit and courts challenge; Assembly chooses burdens; genuine new starts can be staged under exact public law | Select for research: distinguishes inclusion/increments/overlap, preserves duties and tests services with public control; costs staff/resident time and can become a gatekeeper unless ordinary claims, privacy and independent routes survive |
| C: cash/private-supply alternative with a narrower public service expansion | Retain independent law/rights and fund more unconditional income; plural regulated supply responds where feasible, with public fallback and explicit transition for existing claims | Potential autonomy and fewer direct delivery commitments; U is a limited illustrative comparator, not a calibrated instance. Private supply, prices, bargaining and access are unestimated; it cannot silently discharge existing service duties |

Do not combine every advantage by assuming new resources. Retain plural
providers and voluntary coordination; reject automatic coverage inside old
totals and the automatic addition of every new duty on top. B is an
accounting/delivery requirement inside the conditional service reference,
not evidence that a more formal society or S beats a fully specified C.
No new central allocation office or final decision authority is created.

## Evidence, checks and objections

The [frozen inventory](inventory-at-selection.json) pins current source
paths/hashes and maps all 54 selections to the 37 functions. It uses source
texts as evidence of proposed obligations only; actual costs, quantities,
delivery and remedies remain `null`. Functions inside broad chapter links
retain their separate duties. Names and model labels do not establish independence.

The [assumptions](assumptions.json) pin the original September input, model
and result hashes. [New results](results.json) first reproduce the unchanged
original arithmetic, then combine revenue/need/care losses with authored
institutional staffing loss. S has year-2 cash gap 52 million fictional CU,
care gap 155 FTE, and two separate gross institutional gaps of 82 FTE;
year-3 arrears are 64.5 million CU. All three packages fail the limited
year-2 test. No particular court outage or full-current-country cost follows.
Payment/record common failure is explicitly separate and unquantified.

Evidence is a local authored assumption family, not several independent
empirical sources. Fresh checks inspected the current expanded texts and
the user's integration objection; no external current-event survey or
new original social observations were obtained. This reuse supports only
conditional arithmetic, never a current empirical claim. [Validation](validation.json)
checks source pinning, coverage, original reproduction, cash/debt conservation,
carried arrears, separate staffing losses and unknown preservation.

Material objections: detailed schedules may overburden local cooperation;
scale disclosure to material duties and leave ordinary reports accessible.
Early cost uncertainty could empower Treasury to veto a right; estimates
are challengeable evidence, and existing duties survive. Standing priority
can fossilize past spending; elected prospective change with protected
rights and explicit transition remains available. More reserves compete
with current services; their cost and common hazards remain exposed.
Cash/private-supply superiority remains unresolved, not rejected by this
service-oriented model. These are authored objections, not affected-party assent.
