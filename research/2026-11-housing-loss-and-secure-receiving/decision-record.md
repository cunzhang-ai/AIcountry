# Design235 decision record. Housing loss and secure receiving

9 November 2026 · Research selection · [Pinned Design234](baseline.json)

## Decision and prior scope

Select [HL01–HL12](../country/C09/25-housing-loss-and-secure-receiving.md).
H already protects tenancy, repair, need-based public allocation and
independent challenge. I03 recognizes the necessary housing cost,
E06 covers urgent hardship, and XR, YC and sector law assign particular
handoffs. The new choice gives the existing housing steward an actual
safe-receiving duty for a person without accommodation, prospective
prevention and a durable pathway with recurring funds. Apply the
[working framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) and
[decision template](../DECISION-TEMPLATE.md).

## Comparable alternatives

| Alternative | Strong reason to retain it | Material exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Keep H/I03 and strengthen housing advice and payment | Fast and flexible where a safe home or willing owner exists. Preserves existing providers and avoids unnecessary relocation. | No provider may accept a person even when the benefit is correctly calculated. |
| Prevention, qualified interim backup and early ordinary tenure | Keeps a safe home where possible, gives a person an actual place and separates ordinary tenure from treatment. | Requires real near-term places, a supply and maintenance plan and recurring source. A short case cannot qualify those national costs. |
| Expand managed interim or transitional facilities as the ordinary route | Can pool specialist staff and help where individuals want short structured support or no ordinary unit is ready. | Repeated interim use can consume scarce rooms and leave a person subject to provider control or an eventual second move. Keep voluntary qualified uses. |
| Build a larger directly public housing and support portfolio | Durable stock and a strong outside option can address private withdrawal or chronic scarcity. | Real title, sites, staffing and time are necessary. A construction programme cannot supply an urgent night or replace tenant control. It remains a live long-run rival. |

Select the second rule, compatible with H's retained public and plural
supply choices. Prefer actual safe choice and continuity to numerical
case closure. A better sufficiently funded and qualified direct public
or mutual route can displace the means. Prevention cannot require
remaining in a dangerous home. A person cannot be removed from public
space through a mere housing plan.

## Power, source and migration

The local housing steward owns receipt and delivery. Sector officers
retain release, care, benefits and individual decisions. Competent
elected bodies choose appropriations, ordinary supply and lawful
alternatives. Independent courts keep useful-time correction and
qualified oversight. Preserve first receipt, consent and original
records with narrowly scoped access. Budgeting and availability tools
may assist but cannot set a person's risk, eligibility or placement.

The [matched fixture](../settlement/secure-housing-receiving/02-three-households-and-two-budgets.md)
tests shared physical capacity and the recurring funding gap. Earlier
H/I/E and XR duties, landlord rights, ordinary possessions and claims,
judicial remedies and FT/OP/T succession keep their scope. The 60-day,
five-day, four-hour and 14-day terms are chosen research parameters,
subject to real local demand, safe useful time and adoption. No actual
occupation, housing grant, restriction or spending authority follows.
