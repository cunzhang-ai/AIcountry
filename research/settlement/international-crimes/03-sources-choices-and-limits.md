# Original texts, competing choices and deliberate national variations

*Part of [international crimes](README.md); consulted 15 October 2026.*

The accessible primary base is the UN's **corrected 1999** Rome text:
[Part 2](https://legal.un.org/icc/statute/99_corr/2.htm) defines genocide,
humanity/war acts; [Part 3](https://legal.un.org/icc/statute/99_corr/3.htm)
addresses individual/command responsibility and fault;
[Part 5](https://legal.un.org/icc/statute/99_corr/5.htm) includes Article 59;
[Part 9](https://legal.un.org/icc/statute/99_corr/9.htm) addresses actual cooperation,
including immunity/consent issues. These are dated texts, **not a claim of
current complete consolidation**. Later amendments, current jurisprudence,
each actual state's obligations and implementation need fresh qualified review.

The UN's [official Rome introductory account](https://legal.un.org/avl/ha/rsicc/rsicc.html)
describes aggression's leadership/manifest threshold and restricted ICC
jurisdiction. The [German official English CCAIL publication](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/englisch_vstgb/englisch_vstgb.html)
explicitly incorporates amendments through 30 July 2024. It provides a concrete
comparison for broader sexual/persecution protection, IAC/NIAC methods/means
and separate supervision/reporting offenses. It is a national statute,
not international law or an independent empirical demonstration of good lives.
Its life/mandatory-minimum provisions are **not** selected for this country.

Raw source files and separate derived text are preserved in the
[dated source folder](../../2026-10-international-crimes-and-accountability/sources/README.md),
with publisher/version/retrieval/hash records. The current ICC PDF/ICRC
retrieval paths did not yield a verifiable full text here; no latest-version
copy, exact-ICC conformity or invented successful download is claimed.
Historical source artifacts are not translated/edited to satisfy a current UI.

| Material choice | Proposed national position and remaining disagreement |
|---|---|
| Definitions | Self-contained G/closed W schedule, informed by the preserved sources; not live incorporation or every source's penalty/procedure |
| Inclusive protection | Explicit gender/identity/orientation, nonconsensual abortion and serious injury/persecution rules; no silent restrictive import of old Article 7(3). Presence-only reach still needs the particular recognized international basis |
| War context | Selected common IAC/NIAC protection plus expressly IAC-only acts, without claiming all Rome versions/amendment parties are identical |
| Responsibility | Own fault/actual contribution and causal command proof; a separate knowing reporting failure does not retroactively cause a completed crime |
| Child scope | G accused must have been 18; ordinary 16–17 child process remains; national armed recruitment from 18 differs from W08's under 15 war threshold. These are proposed choices, not automatic domestic commands from ICC's age rule |
| Penalty | Retain a finite main maximum of 25 years, with command knowledge/disregard/constructive branches 25/15/10, incitement 7 and reporting/recruitment 5, meaningful review and no mandatory minimum/life |
| Reach | Connected/presence-based core routes with actual recognized international basis; no worldwide absent-person policing, unqualified domestic extension or invented treaty membership |

Treaty-minimum implementation promises shared definitions/foreseeability but
can leave broader domestic protections and distinct penalty/procedure choices
unaddressed. Mechanical copying is no compatibility review. A mainly
national-core/restorative approach can reduce penal expansion but leave
organized contexts, genuine sham proceedings and cross-border control poorly
specified. The selected differentiated national statute makes those choices
explicit. A finite thirty-year ceiling and broader/unrestricted universal
reach remain substantive rivals; no evidence establishes 25 as an optimum.
Retain the existing cap because the newly defined context alone does not
establish a justified higher ceiling; gravity/fault can move a sentence within
it. This is a normative retention, not proof of equal deterrence or welfare.

Qualified practitioners, affected communities, accused/defenders, children,
service members and disabled people have not reviewed the draft. Actual
case evidence, independent controllers, cost, staffing, treaty compatibility
and received remedies are absent. Newer prose or successful hashes cannot
prove safety/superiority. Reopen on a material protection/legality gap or
credible better model; finish remaining sector/reproductive/conflict-detention
rules, then re-audit the full country's coherence rather than endlessly
adding cases to this study. Current law/software and constitutional holds stay.
